At A Gym?

29 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/29/02 A Local Gym Introductiuon: The hour is around 6pm, on the cold, winter Friday, just one day before SLAM! Michael Dame has not been heard of since his altercation within his own mind earlier in the week. A day later from that 'altercation' Gloria went to visit Michael to find he was not there. And left behind was a torn apart house, filled with blood that soaked the floor and made the smell unbearable. Now, with the time gone, and still Michael not found, the case of murder is lingering through the air, although you and I know this is not the reality, the police department, and more importantly Gloria think differently...very differently. At a Gym? The camera gazes the large scale World Class Gym. The parking lot is half full, and the lights are getting darker, as dusk is upon us. Suddenly a red covervette comes spinning into the parking lot coming to a halt directly in front of the gym. The car almost takes out a man, who jumps backwards. Out hops Gloria, with a long black coat on. She beeps the alarm on and walks past the man she almost hit without a word said to him. 'Hey lady, what the fuck do you think you're doing?' The man says, making Gloria stop in her tracks and look over her shoulder. 'What...I know I didn't just here that. I'm MPD..(Minneapolis Police Department).., on official police business. Now....get.' Gloria says, obviously lying to ease the situation at hand. She has something very stern on her mind, and won't let anything get in the way. She walks furiously right past the desk that sits near the enterance. A woman from working asks for her to stop. 'Excuse me mam, you're going to have to sign......' She is cut off by Gloria. 'Didn't you just hear what I said to him..' She says as she keeps walking. 'I know, I heard you loud and clear, although you're still going to need to sign...' once again she is cut off. 'Sign this...' Gloria stops and gives her the finger. The woman is repauled, and doesn't know what to say. Just gives a very hasty face, and turns the other way. Gloria continues on her way, seemingly knowing where she's going. She walks past several men working out, jacked, and handsome. They all look her way... 'Keep Dreamin'..' She says as she walks past them, and down the hall, to the spa area. She fails to see the signs for men and woman, to enter different spas, she enters the men's. And in the back corner....Michael Dame. Sits in a towel, in the corner, wrinkled up, and looking as depressed as ever. 'Michael...Oh my god.' Gloria stunningly says, as he lifts his head up. Looking as if he doesn't even know who Gloria is. 'Gloria?' He says in a low, monotone voice. 'Oh my god, Michael are you alright?' She runs over to Michael and sits down next to him. 'No, I'm fine. But we should leave..' He continues to explain. 'Alright, Lets go. But Michael, let me ask you something... Who hurt you early this week? Did someone attack you?' She asks concernly. 'Attack me? Nobody. I feel...I feel someone attacked me, but that person...was me.' He mumbles. 'Alright, don't worry, no need to explain. Lets get you to a doctors office.' She says as she helps the withered down Dame up. It seems he has not eaten anything all week, and been around the gym/spa for quite sometime. Gloria helps them out, and they walk out of the building....holding hands. Camera fades to black.
