Abortion III

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28-04-02 “Go on then, release your fury, fuck everyone else and get it over with.” The deep sounds of a sadistic voice egged him on. “Your suffering will be put to an end, and to end it, you must end them…” His mind contorted in a twisted look of rage, his bulging eyes, bulging eyes aflame with hatred, eyeing them up. “Nobody loves you. Remember everything that they’ve done this to you, now it’s your turn…rid yourself of the torment…” Tears welled up in those, his eyes of hatred. But he was motionless. The tears began rolling down his cheek, but disappeared, as the poison took over his mind. “You know they have it coming to them. They have asked for this…they wanted this…they made you suffer and now you must show them the consequences…” The same blood flowed through their veins, the same thoughts ran through their minds, however differently he spoke those words into his ear, they were perfectly understood. “There is nothing to fear. There never was. Swallow that little lump in your throat and do it. Take no prisoners. Make daddy proud…” He flinched and looked at his victims. Looking around, the creature that possesses him examines the cold, ripe land, and growls. “I’m starting to hate this place. My family’s legacy began here, yet a virus is sweeping through it that makes me wish I had never been. I know you feel this same pain. The question is…what do you plan to do about it?” More tears. Hidden from the light of day, his pain grew inside. It fuelled him, but at the same time it was destroying him. A Terminal Bloodlust. “It feels like that same Abortion I have seen and felt before. Tear this life from you now. I know it hurts inside. Worry not, the hurt, the pain; it will all leave you shortly. You know what you must do, though do you realise what it is?” He paused, and leaned close to his ear, whispering gently. “It…is true total enlightenment.” The pain left him. It was all clear now. Total enlightenment. The fear he felt had manifested into a virtual poison, seething through his veins, infesting his mind. No remorse. No pain. No fear. “Now go…your Abortion awaits…” With that Hessian watched as the deranged youth, thriving on his influence, cocked his shotgun and made his way into the high school. The screams and gunshots coursed through Hessian’s veins. Like a 1000-hits-at-once adrenaline rush. This Abortion, his ecstasy. Fin
