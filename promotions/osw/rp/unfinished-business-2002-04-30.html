Unfinished Business

30 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-30-02 Ivan Stanislav stayed in his office for a short period of time after the meeting with Alexei. No one knew what happened during the period of time after the exit of Alexei and the return of Praporschik Stanislav to what some would call “active duty.” Just prior to the time when Stanislav exited his office after staying within it’s confines for over a day, a loud roar came from the room, obviously from Stanislav. He then kicked the entire door down in a fit of rage. Praporschik Stanislav was back. For the rest of that day, Stanislav marched through the military base barking orders, destroying equipment, and generally making his presence known. This was in stark contrast to what the denizens of the base were used to as Stanislav had grown increasingly lenient and docile during the past few weeks, but that all had suddenly changed. No one dared to get in the way of the raving Praporschik as he neared them. The look in his eyes was unlike anything they had ever seen. It was a type of look that showed a tremendous amount of thought going through his mind, yet at the same time an air of spontaneity and recklessness was also visible. Stanislav had a lot of work to get done that he had neglected during the period of a few weeks which had been referred to as “The Tempest Ordeal.” One instance in particular, over a week ago, was an incident in which a Private by the name of Grishenko had played a practical joke on a Drill Sergeant and Stanislav was to reprimand him. Stanislav, for a reason he could not seem to fathom now, let him slide with a warning. It was time to pay the piper. ~~ Sergeant Stukov stood in front of his men, a company of about thirty conscripts in which amongst them, Private Grishenko stood. The company stood out in a large open field in which the snow had recently fallen and left it clear and pristine. Stukov brought his hands behind his back and began to talk to them, not knowing what was going on just behind him… From a nearby cabin a door flew open and a massive form exited it, only after first dislodging his fist from the door in which he had punched to open. Everyone knew it was Ivan Stanislav. He had moved constantly the day before after exiting his office and again, first starting at 4:30 in the morning, Stanislav had rumbled around the base issuing orders and generally making the lives of anyone near him unbearable. This time, Stanislav saw what he wanted and headed towards the company. Snow flew behind him as he stomped through the fluffy snow, disturbing it and sending it scattering into the air. Steam literally rose from his shoulders and arms as he marched through the cold air in nothing more than his typical shirt, suspenders, pants, and boots. Lines of steam also exited his nostrils as he zeroed in on the company. The men grew uneasy as they saw the juggernaut nearing. Stukov was not aware of Stanislav’s presence, as his back was facing the raving Praporschik. The sudden shifting in the ranks caught Stukov’s attention. “What are you men doing! Stand still!!” barked Stukov. The men were petrified in fear as Stanislav neared, growing larger and larger with each thunderous step. From the cabin he had exited a crew of men exited carrying what appeared to be an unconscious man…that did not bode well for any of them. One man managed to point over the shoulder of Stukov, and while normally he would have instantly be reprimanded for such an action, Stukov let it slide, for when he looked behind him he too was petrified in fear. “Oh shit…” was all that Stukov could mutter as the growling of Stanislav could be heard as he came within 50 yards of the group. Stukov turned to the men, “Listen to me men! Do what he says, and do it to the best of your ability! Praporschik Stanislav is under much stress!!” Stukov tried to calm himself and regain his composure, but the pallor of his skin had already gone far paler than it had already been. He spoke of various drills as the shadow of Stanislav fell upon him. Ivan stood and breathed down the back of Stukov. The young Sergeant turned and nodded to Ivan. The men prayed that their Sergeant would protect them. “Praporschik Stanislav! What brings you out here?!” Stukov said, trying to appear as happy as he could. Stanislav narrowed his eyes and then glared up at the men, scanning them for a moment. Ivan blinked once and then pointed directly at Grishenko. “HIM! He is why I am here!” Grishenko almost fainted as Stanislav pointed at him and eyed him like a wounded animal. The massive Russian took a step towards him before Stukov got in his way! “Praporschik, I am sorry, but I am busy conducting drills! If you wish to see Private Grishenko, you must come at another time.” Stukov swallowed hard and prayed that Stanislav would go away. Ivan ground his teeth, extremely irritated, and looked to Stukov. “That man has an offense of which I have not yet handled and I will take care of him! Step aside…Sergeant.” Stukov swallowed and furrowed his brow, “that was over a week ago, Praporschik! He got his warning and…” Stanislav’s hand shot out and gripped the throat of Stukov, crushing it like a vice. He gritted his teeth and looked into the huge eyes of the Sergeant, “I do not think you heard me, Sergeant. I believe Ivan said STEP ASIDE!!” With that Stanislav tossed Stukov away, hurling him head over heels and into the snow! The company stood dumbfounded. Should they protect Grishenko? Should they go to the Sergeant Stukov? Grishenko panicked and started to babble, “Praporschik, I am sorry! I did not mean to do anything wrong!!” Ivan growled and pointed at the group. “Unless you wish to be part of this allow that dog to step forward!! Come here….boy.” Stanislav growled, “I think it is time Praporschik Stanislav taught you lesson.” The group parted, giving Grishenko enough opening to walk closer to the massive Stanislav. Ivan paused and stared at him, “I remember the crime you committed some days ago and I believe that I was a little too…lenient before.” Grishenko shook his head ‘no’ as to perhaps it would convince this maniac otherwise. He was wrong. Stukov stood slowly in the background and grabbed his neck but made no attempt to stop Stanislav. The crowd grew far in the background as they watched Praporschik Stanislav loom over this much smaller, and much younger conscript. Ivan looked up at the men, “MEN! What you saw weeks ago was a falsity! It was not real! It was you men that thought perhaps leniency would get the best of Praporschik Stanislav! And it was dogs like young Grishenko here that thought they could take advantage of me!” Ivan looked down at Grishenko, “Isn’t that right, Private?” “N..nyet, Praporschik!” stammered Grishenko. “So you say that Praporschik Stanislav is a liar?!” Ivan grinned. “Behold my little tin soldiers! Behold what happens when you take advantage of Ivan Stanislav! Behold what happens when you take away something that I hold dear to myself! Behold what happens when you break my…..” Ivan paused and blinked, “My…..my…..” He paused and stared at the snow for a moment. His shoulders bucked as he gulped in a breath as a memory of something tried to push through his head. He shook his head and glared at Grishenko. “You will learn a lesson for what you did to me!” Growled Stanislav. “D…did to you? I did not do…” Grishenko’s words were suddenly cut off. The roar of Stanislav drowned him out with ease. Suddenly, Grishenko found his nose thoroughly broken and his face covered with his own blood as the raving Praporschik punched him square in the face. The massive fist of Praporschik Stanislav had eclipsed Grishekno’s face as it smashed the cartilage of his nose! The men gasped as Grishenko grabbed at his face and cried out loudly. Still Stukov did nothing, other than cover his eyes. Ivan looked to the men. “Do you see? Do you see what happens when you defy me?!!” Ivan raised an eyebrow and squinted his eye and laughed loudly, “DYAAHAAHAAHAA!!!” He looked down at Grishenko and walked closer to him, “Boy, perhaps now you will remember your mistake!” Stanislav shot his massive size 19 boot out and caught the young soldier in the ribs, eliciting a loud SNAP from the inside of his body. He rolled over, the snow now thoroughly colored a deep, dark syrupy red. Stanislav took another step and kicked him again, rolling him over. “You are not laughing now…are you Private?” All Grishenko could do was groan. Ivan reached down and grabbed him by the hair and lifted him off the ground, completely off of his feet. Grishenko breathed weakly as he tried to hold his nose with his hand. Stanislav slapped his hand away. “Take it like a man, boy! We must make sure you never make jokes again, da?” Grishenko sobbed…. “ANSWER ME!!” He nodded weakly and tried to respond, “D..da…Pa..Papo….schik….” Stanislav grabbed him by the back of his jaw and held him there, palming his entire bottom half of his chin. Ivan gritted his teeth, “If you ever….EVER try to do something such as what you pulled again…..” He tightened his grip as the jaw compressed…. “….I will not only ensure that I handle you myself….” Grishenko whimpered in the iron grip of Stanislav as he squeezed even harder…. “..but that you never SPEAK again as well!” CRUNCH Grishenko squealed as his jaw snapped and Stanislav tossed him on his back into the snow. The men and Stukov stared petrified as Stanislav whirled around and headed towards Stukov. The smaller man hopped out of the way as Ivan headed back towards the cabin where what appeared to be approximately 100 men stared in disbelief as Stanislav headed towards them… ~~ From the main building of Arkhangelsk, General Leonid Tomifei stared at a television set in his office as he watched the rampage of Ivan Stanislav take place. He leaned back in his leather chair and drummed his fingers together. “Welcome back Praporschik Stanislav. Welcome back to the fold.”
