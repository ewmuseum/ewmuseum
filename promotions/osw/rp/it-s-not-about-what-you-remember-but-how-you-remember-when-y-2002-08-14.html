It's not about what you remember, but HOW you remember when you finally wake up...

14 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 14th, 2002 When you wake up with the feeling that your head is slowly imploding upon itself and the previous evening is a near blur, you get to thinking... ''Aww SHIT, I'm fucking sore...what the hell was I DOING last night?'' Hey, it's profane, but speech requires SOME thought. Amy was just stirring from a half-conscious, fitful sleep on a heavily dented and piss-poor excuse for a bed when she recalled the prior night... She'd been sitting alone in a rather dank little bar somewhere in what she considered to be one of the most hell-bound spots on earth, Long Island. Yes, New York. Of course, she wasn't ENTIRELY alone there that night...the bar tender wasn't finished his shift yet, and the rows of shot glasses in front of Aimz were providing some great company for the hours she'd already been there. When the...okay, she couldn't quite recall how many...but with what seemed like the hundredth shot, or so she wished, of the evening a surprise came walking through the door. A small surprise that Amy didn't even recognize through her blurred vision until the already half-drunken woman staggered over to the bar and called for tequila shooters just before sagging down onto a rotating stool next to Campbell. Her hair was longer than Amy's had ever been, her eyes were VERY deep blue and a dominant feature, almost reminding her of Reno in a way. It wasn't terribly long before, without realizing she was staring or that the woman was staring back, chuckling, Aimz recognized her as Lillian Duran...a co-worker? Yep, and a participant in the Slam main event this week. ''Are you...supposed to...be here?'' Lillith muttered with a half smile between shots. Campbell was equally as drunk, maybe even more so than Ms. Duran...so nothing was exactly processing. ''Huh?'' ''A little young?'' Was all she said, following it up with a choked laugh. Amy knew when she was being made fun of, however subtly. That's when she produced a leather wallet with red flame imprint from the back pocket of her rather distressed, oddly fitting jeans and waved it in front of her. ''All the ID I need.'' ''OSW doeshn't like it's employeesh engaging in illegal activity,'' She was so OBVIOUSLY drunk off her ass that it was funny to Aimz. ''And sinsh you're Missh 'Illicit Substance Search 2002'.......................yeah.'' This was incredibly odd behavior on Lillith's part, to say the least, but Amy replied in perfectly clear English, still having an amazing balance even after so many drinks. ''Does it look like I give a flying fuck what OSW does and doesn't like? Sorry, but after their little clauses saying Corg can do whatever the hell he wants to me, the whole 'Better be good for the company' thing wore really thin.'' Both sat in silence for a moment, each taking a few more shots before Lillith looked back to Aimz and said, ''Ha...they don't have to tre...tr....treat over-glorified valets to ha...has-beensh with any reshpect...'' ''Excuse me?'' And that's when the fight, much to the delight of the bored bar tender who's shift hadn't seen a whole lot of action thus far, ensued... It ended almost as fast as it started, to Amy's recollection, as she soon slid Lillith over the bar, bloodying her face on broken shot glasses when Tempest walked in, finding the stand-offish redhead ready and willing for another fight... Instead of starting trouble, she'd been proud to see the damage and congratulated Amy for eliminating yet another small sliver of competition from their upcoming match with a free round. She soon snapped out of the memory when Reno Davis' voice came calling from the bathroom as he stepped out, towel-drying his hair much to the delight of his monkey companion ZaC, who clung to and swung from the white, fluffy fabric. ''Rough night?'' He'd smiled when he found her propped against the headboard, rubbing her temples. ''I guess...lots of booze, I know that much.'' ''Oh yeah?'' Davis laughed aloud, before flicking the TV on and curling up on the floor next to the bed. ''That's what the officer who carried you back up here said.'' Ah well...at least she's shown ONE girl who deserves the title shot, eh? ((Final Note: Heh, did that seem weird and awfully backwards? Good. Take it away, Noonan...))
