Temper, temper

14 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Feb. 14th 'It's over, it's over now. Move over, it's my turn now. Move over, we can't shut down..sorry.'- Kurupt. The Old School Wrestling TV screen faded to black and suddenly emerged to show the name, 'Drew Hostyle' in bold red lettering and you knew what was coming next. Not some catchy song lyric or a greasy new haircut. Drew Hostyle. Professional wrestling rookie at sight, and veteran at mind. So maybe you know who exactly Drew Hostyle is, you recognize his facial expressions, and the way he talked with that certain drawl in his voice. But you, or anyone else will ever know what was going to happen next. No one did, he liked it better that way. Rule#1 Keep the public on their toes, when they're comfortable, they're boring. And boring was just what Drew's career had been up until this very point. Entering federation after federation, winning and retaining titles until the end of their existance, this was boring. So if a person considered THIS to be boring, then what exactly would be exciting or interesting? Pain, pain was the answer. Maybe he had never felt it, maybe he had never taken the time to notice. But if it was one thing he set out to learn in the OSW, it was this..pain. Finding out about it, perfecting it, inflicting it on others..that was something for a different day. Or was it? With the formation of this new unit within the OSW, 3B or Brooklyn Born and Bred, with the most controversial stars in the history of OSW and probably the whole damn business. And there was no doubt that with Switchblade and Frankie Bones on his team, there had to be some pain involved, on Slam, whether they deserved it or not. Drew'd definately learn a lot about inflicting pain himself that night. But for Rhett Locke and Jesse Ramey..it'd be a different story. This time Drew Hostyle, his face covered by an unitied black and white do rag that's two strings dangled by his chin, sat in the exuecutive chair of a huge office, equipped with a 72 inch TV screen and long brown desk which Drew has taken the liberty of seating his black suede Timberland boots on, and scoff marks are visiable on the desk, along with dirt. As the camera turns to face the screen that Drew is watching, a lost scene from the debut episode of OSW's Slam! can be seen and so can two members of 3B, hismelf and Frankie Bones. Drew continues to watch as the lights dim in the arena, the crowd shoot their glare to the entrance way. 'Who We Be' by DMX booms over the P.A. system as a mix of boos and cheers poor from the crowd. Out steps Frankie Bones, engulfed in various colored spotlights, he almost appears to just be an outline. He glances both directions and starts walking deliberately down the ramp. He gets to the ring and slowly steps through the ropes, encircling the ring he motions for a mic, and is thrown one. 'Well well well, if it isn’t the Legend of Pain himself. That’s right boys. Just ‘cause old Mista Bones wasn’t on the card tonight, you didn’t think that’d stop him from makin’ his impression felt, did ya? No no no, quite the contraray. I have a plan that will make my impression felt by you all, in a big way. I’m sure you’re thinking to yourself, well, here’s another empty promise of future World Championship gold. Well you’re wrong. Titles aren’t on my mind right now. The only thing on my mind right now, is total Domination. Domination Frankie? Yes, domination. Now, not only will I dominate all who cross me, but I will make it a point to dominate the entire federation, and it starts now. Will I do this alone? Oh no, I have something in mind. I’m sure you fans remember a little group by the name of...Black Attack. Quite simply put, we were Black, and we Attacked. And I see a growing need for a rebirth of Black Attack, right here in OSW. And my first step to this happens here, tonight. So, Mister Drew Hostyle, get your ass out here. Now son.' Everyone waits in anticipation as Frankie stares at the entrance way from a couple of seconds. he doesn't come on. Just as Frankie is raising the mic to his lips to speak again, 'Right now, Later on' by Fabulous booms over the P.A. Drew Hostyle emerges from behind the curtain, to applause from the crowd. He then smirks while looking down at the cameras on the steel ramp, and raises his eyebrows, turning his partial attention to the man in the ring. As Drew stands on the entrance way, he raises a mic to his lips. 'What do you want, man?' Drew asks, impatience in his tone. 'I’m offering you a spot kid. A place at the table. A chance to join the team. So let’s hook up, and start Black Attack all over again. You want to be a part of this team, don’t you?' Frankie replies. 'Be apart of the team, you said? What team? Your little..Whack Attack, is about as played out as Sisqo's 'Thong Song', Dog. Don't play me fo no fool, cuz I ain't one. I know all about the Whack Attack's history, just like you said. You formed, you interfered in each other's matches, and then you broke apart. No achievements, no accomplishments. So what you're really tryin to ask me, what you really want Drew Hostyle to do is go ahead and help you do what you can't. Right?' Frankie lowers the mic from his mouth, his glare at Drew becomes more serious, and he looks nearly pissed off. Frankie turns his head fully, popping his neck, the sound can be heard on the mic. He raises the mic again. 'Well, it appears you don’t know who you are talking to son. You speak of the PCW Black Attack as if it would have any bearing on what we would do here. I am giving YOU and opportunity here, I am trying to help YOU out here. Believe me DOG, there is nothing you can do that I can’t. Hell, I’ve already forgotten more about this game then you’ll ever know. I’m gonna say this one more time, and that is it. You want be remembered as one of the greats?' 'Oh, so now I get it. You want Drew Hostyle to tie your shoes, so that you can walk all over me? Hell nah. Hell f*ckin nah, man. What I'm gonna do is this, I'm gonna go on bout ma business, pretend like you never insulted me with this phony ass deal, and continue to go and get mine's by myself, like any real man would.' 'Hmm, you couldn’t have said it worse kid. You’ve got a bad attitude my man, that stuff can get ya hurt.' Frankie sighes. 'Well from what I hear, attitude reflect leadership.' Frankie’s eyes fill with rage, he raises his finger and points sternly at Drew. 'You ungreatful little bitch. I come out here and offer to help you. I just want what’s best for our kind son. But you're just making it tough. I can see there is no common blood here, you’re nothing like me kid.' 'It's all about you and what you want, ain't it Frankie? It's never about no one else. You gotta be in the spotlight, gotta be the star. Well guess what? There's always gonna be a bigger star, a better athlete than you, and in this case and for the rest of your OSW career, that person will be me. So get used to it Frankie. Get used to being on the back burner. Being runner up. Being second..best. Cuz you ain't gettin any closer to me and what I got to offer. Got beef? We can settle it anytime, any place, my brotha.' 'Any time, any place huh? I’ll take you up on that. I’m gonna say time...uhh how bout now? Place? I’m gonna have to say, right here.' With this Frankie drops the mic and slides out of the ring. Drew sees this and sprints down the entrance ramp. The crowd explodes as the two men collide. Frankie spears Drew as they meet, but Drew is quick to get up. He grabs Frankie's head and DDT's him on the relentless steel ramp. Frankie pops up and is bleeding profusely from his head. Before both men can get to their feet, security swarms them. Drew and Frankie fight frantically to get free from the security guards, separting them from every possible way. Although it takes many men, they finally get them seperated. Frankie stops fighting and simply stands there and stares at Drew, with blood pouring down his face. Drew pushes the boldest security guard down with a lot of force, and the rest back off. This particular scene ends with both men starring hard at each other.' Drew takes clutches the remote he's just used to flick off the TV screen and throws it to the ground in a fit of anger, after stomping his feet and trampling more mud on the dirty desk, someone nearby hears the commotion and runs in, slightly perturbed. 'What is it Drew?' asks the man from Drew's last promo, his attorney turned promoter, Jonathan Black. 'What da hell do I pay you fo, shit like dis is still available for the viewing public to watch?!' 'Well that is accurate and DID happen, however I pulled some strings and got it off the air on that episode of Slam!, remember?' Jonathan asks than smiles. 'Nah, nah...I don't remember that, cuz I just saw that shit on the screen and I'm sure my boy rankie wouldn't like it just as much as I didn't if he manages to see it too.' 'Drew I was right there when you got it, you practically killed the audio visual guy, uh what's his face..? Mike Lasandra, that's it. You damn near ripped the eye brow ring off the man, that wasn't exactly normal..' Black explained. 'NORMAL? You mean if any old cracka on da streets came up and did that, then they'd get the tape too?' Drew says, getting closer to the face of Jonathan Black. 'Nobody in their right mind what do what you did to that poor boy..and lately I've getting questionable about whether I want to be on your side in this trial..maybe even plead insanity, once people find out about this pre-teen temper tantrums you've been having.' Jonathan says and turns away from Drew, while running his fingers through his hair. 'YOU SON OF A...' Drew almost grabs Joanthan from behind, but controls himself and pulls the collar up on his black POLO sweat shirt and puts his hands in the pockets of his saggy black POLO jeans. He makes his way all the time back over to the huge chair and plumps into it. 'I'm sorry about the way I been actin, people just take this whole dman thign to far. What 3B have done in the past is just that..the past. We're pretty damn tight now, and we goin dominate the whole damn OSW. Nobody will stop us, not no virgin Russian man who don't own no toothbrush, and no no damn trailor park crackas that need self esteem boasts from a bunch of damn fat ass fans like themselves that watch my every move, every week. So, enough about dat man, let's talk about my case.' 'Well I did say that I had a solution for your case, about a week ago, but now I' just not sure it it'll follow through.' 'What was it? It damn well better be believable, cuz I'll make dis plan work!' 'Well everybody knows that there are different inpretations of the Bible. This lady may have interpreted that there was no forgiveness for her in God's heart for the lack of actual help she had given her patients in the past. Like there wasn't in Genesis when Eve shared her apple with Adam. Or like when it rained for 40 days and nights, or when..' 'I get yo point and I dont' like it one bit. So know I'm supposed to be some kinda prophet ot this chick? I liked her..but not that damn much...like I said she was tight, but...too damn trustworthy wit people. I don't...I don't...we're not doing that defense man.' 'There's not that much time left Drew, unless you want to come up with your own defense, minus the advantages of having a person who's actually passed a Bar and had experience.' 'You know what? I'm sick of you callin em Drew, from now on..to EVERYBODY, it's Mr. Hostyle. And I dont' wanna hear anymore shit about this case except behind this doors I'm about to wlak out of. Matta fact I'm take YOU up on that offer and find my own case. But until then, I'm not worried about that..I'm worrin about findin a damn ass to kick!' Drew exclaims. Jonathan stands in front of the door and begisn to speak again. He begisn to speak as Drew cuts him off. 'Welll if you really.' 'Unless you want that ass to be yours, I suggest you move da hell outta my way, and NOW.' Drew says in Jonathan's face as he gets him a shoove to the side, perhaps persuading his decision with emphasis. As Drew leave leaves the room, Jonathan mutters to himself. 'Now I know why mother told me to never take up that career in wrestling.' Jonathan then grabs his own right arm. 'And it's not because I don't have the body either, it's because she didn't want me to break somebody's limbs! I feel sorry for those freakin idiots who are taking on Drew, Frankie, and you might as well say Switchblade too.. Talk about getting the sense beaten into you..' Jonathna then mumbles some more to himself and the sceen fades to black, only to leave viewers with more questioning to themselves. Those questions? Well..you figure them out this time.
