Abortion

20 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20-04-02 He sat in the darkness, silent tears rolling down his pale cheeks. Thoughts of the past, the present, and the future ran through his head, as he reminisced about the good times with her. Even then, pictured fresh in his mind were thoughts of her long, golden hair. That fresh, enticing scent lured him closer, the silky smoothness caressing his face and fingers, as they embraced in the warm depths of his memories. As her beautiful smile graced him, more tears welled up, and he looked down at his hand and arm. “Beautiful…” That word plagued him. Her gentle touch relaxed him. He felt wanted. Whilst the world passed him by, his parents drank, smoked, and wasted their lives, his friends watched on as he deteriorated mentally before them. No one could comfort him. Only her. And she was well without his reach. Rocking slowly on his bed, fighting back a flood of tears, she infested his mind like a virus. His infatuation with her had grown from crush to love, and too many complications had risen for him to declare it to her. Now it was over and he had nothing to live for. Did he? First love. Now it was gone. That word rang through his mind again. Beautiful. His love for her was summed up into that one word. Every time he whispered it into her ear, his stomach would tense and her face would flush, and deep down they both knew they would be together. Wrong. His friend Stobo had warned him of this. “There’s more people playin’ the game than ye think son!” He remembered Stobo’s words, all he could think and even whisper was, “Fuck off”. More tears. Then he thought of her beauty again. Never leaving her without reminding her of it. No matter how she took it, he would always leave her assured she was loved by someone, yet she couldn’t acknowledge it… “Night, night beautiful…” Again he looked at his hands, as the pain inside slowly grew worse, he winced then sighed as his only release shot up his arm, sharp pinpricks stabbing at his skin, flowing through his veins and painting a disturbing picture for him. Disturbing, because he actually liked it. Sitting there for hours, in the darkness, just glancing at remnants of her existence, her lipstick marks, jewellery and other such items, he cried. He cried because he had lost the one important thing to him, and the only way to forget her was to forget himself. It was hell as he remembered the fun times, the intimate times and the serious times. Every moment with her had been a treasured memory. Now he was alone. A complex situation that was so straightforward it became impossible, his situation worsened with every stinging release of the pain inside, hidden by the pain outside. Looking at his hand and wrist, he smiled through tears as the thick, red blotches painted a picture of sorrow on a canvas of pale skin. Tiring of the pain, he threw the penknife to one side, then put his head in his hands and let it all out. All the while the uncaring world shunned him, as the feelings he had built up for so long towards her became of no use any more, and seeped out, draining him in the form of little clear droplets. “Fuck it all. I wish I’d neve-…” His cries became muffled in his own sorrow. All the love he had for her was diminished in the knowledge that her first love rested with another, and he was pushed out into the cold, into a world forlorn and uncaring. All the while the confusion in his mind became clear, then unclear, increasingly breaking him apart slowly and painfully. He could only come to one conclusion. “I can’t go on any more…” From the shadows, he was being watched. The looming tower of hatred gazed at the lost soul, feeling no remorse for his situation. Moving as though stalking this pitiful being, the Hessian manoeuvred around the room, lingering behind the young man. Quietly and quickly throwing his cape back, Hessian grinned as the sleek black glimmered in the intense green of his eyes. Hessian sat the Beretta M92FS down beside him… “In many ways I understand. Though, one whom I loathe brought me into this world. The Soul Stalker. I respected, listened and obeyed it. However I see clearly now, that our paths have finally crossed. All my ‘love’ for this being has gone; all that remains is the instinct to survive, and to do so I must destroy it. I want to rip all meaning of it from my soul, like an Abortion I will rid myself of every ounce of its being. All the feelings or emotions I harboured for it have been taken over by the will to condemn, and so I let go, and now. Fucked if I know. Damned if I care…Vampir will perish…” Hessian’s eyes grew wider, more intense, glowing maniacally as he turned to the gun, unbeknownst of Hessian’s presence, and picked up the gun. As the feelings of hate, love and pain left him; he smiled again, and looked at the gun, down the tunnel of light to his own fate, to his own destiny…and dreamt of that Abortion. Click…Click…Boom… Fin
