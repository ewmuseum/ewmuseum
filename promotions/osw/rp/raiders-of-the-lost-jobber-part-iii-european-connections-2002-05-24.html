Raiders of the lost jobber Part III: European connections

24 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 24th 2002 Date: 5 days ago **The scene opens in a small river in the middle of Venice. It's a clean spring night, and the city lights reflect in the water mirror, making a beautiful scene for the young couples, who are finding about the beauty of pos-marry life. The camera is over a small brick bridge, one of the thousands that cross the rivers, and it shows a few gondols, coming and going around the city. After a few moments, the camera zooms on a coming gondol, and the camera shows a man, sitting on it, wearing a trenchcoat and a big backpack to his side. The man has a phone on his hand, which he turns off when the gondols comes closer. The camera gets a good glimpse of his face, and it's finally revealed he's Andrew 'Ace' Hawk, the OSW superstar. The shot switches to inside the gondol, where the driver, a tall italian dude, about 6' tall, wearing a stripped white and red T-shirt and white pants, tells Ace:** 'The street you're looking for is there.' 'Thanks', says Ace, before handing the man a 2000 lires bill. The man looks at it and curses, but Ace doesn't pay attention, getting out of the boat. **Walking around the small medieval aisles in the old Venice area is a fun thing to do on spring, as Ace realizes. Several small family business, going back to the 1200s, pack the streets, making the place ideal for tourism. Little crafty shops, little family restaurants serving pizzas with the same recipe as 800 years ago, Ace realizes how big this world is. He remembers about his home in Brazil, the beaches, the hill house with the look to the beach, and his Hollywood hills mansion, and he notices how much cultures there are around. After walking for a while, he stops at a small bakery shop, across a short building that Ace can't stop but looking. He walks in the shop, a very rich and traditional bakery with several kinda of candy and cakes. He goes straight to an old fat man finishing a wedding cake. He calls for him:** 'Hey...' 'Hi, how can I help you?', answers the big italian, without taking his eyes off the cake. 'I'm looking for the lost jobber...', asks Ace, before realizing that was a stupid question... 'Who???', asks the baker, visibly clueless. 'Hank, HANK!!!', yells Ace, rolling his eyes. 'Hank... Hank... OOOOOOOOOH, you mean the all-mighty Hankster???', the man asks, letting the cake go and turning to Ace, visibly interested. 'The all-mighty... WHO?', asks Ace, not believing his ears. 'The Hankster. The biggest star in ICW', the man says, sparks coming from his eyes. 'The Hankster... ICW???', asks Ace, completely clueless. 'Yeah, the Italian Champioship Wrestling. It's our biggest fed here. Hank has been dominating it since he arrived, 2 months ago. He's even the Italian Champion!!!!' 'Italian... CHAMPION?', asks Ace, completely disoriented... 'Yeah, TWICE!!!', the man says. **Ace, now completely shocked, tries to find a place to lean himself into. The big man helps him, walking away from behind the counter and helping Ace sit down in a small tasting table nearby. Ace sits down, letting himself throw completely in the chair. The fat baker sits in front of him and asks:** 'Are you ok, sir?' 'Yes, I'm ok. Just shocked. Champion? Hank, a champion? This is insane... Where is him? Where's the ICW's next show?', Ace asks, grabbing the man by the neck of his shirt, shaking him back and forward, making the guy's head bounce around. 'I... I don't... I think...' 'TELL ME, DAMN IT' 'Stop... shaking me!!!', the man yells, before shoving Ace's hands off him. He looks at Ace and finally answers. 'Look, I don't know, people in ICW don't know either. He's disappeared about 2 weeks ago. People are saying he went to Japan to practice for a match he has, for the ICW Title. That's all I know.' 'Japan, right? Hmmmm I think I can track the bitch down... By the way, is this the building he lived on?', asks Ace pointing 'Yes' 'Thanks for the help, man' 'No problem...' **Ace zooms out of the scene, flying to the building across the street, going upstairs. The scene shows one of the buildings' floors, where Ace starts looking for a specific apartment. He finally finds it and grabs a skeleton key, used to open any kind of door locks. After a few seconds struggling against the lock, Ace finally opens it. Entering the apartment, Ace realizes it's a very rich place, with several paintings on the walls, new, modern furniture around, and a new-modern look to it. Ace turns the lights on and immediately sees a group of flyers on a table. Checking them, Ace finally finds three flyers for fighting dojos in Japan. He looks at them, as they have informations on how go reach it, get classes and stuff like that. Ace finally looks satisfied, grabbing them and smiling. He knows his quest is near the end. He gets out of the apartment and starts running down the streets, trying to save some time. His satellite phone rings and he answers it:** 'Hello...' 'It's Dick, Ace... Where the hell are you?', answers Dick Strang, Ace's manager, visibly upset with him. 'I'm in Venice, I'm going to Japan now.' 'Japan? Are you insane? You have a match on Slam this Saturday, you need to come back NOW!!!' 'No, I can't. I'm almost finding Hank. I need to find him. I have to!' 'Ok, fine, do whatever you want. But if you miss this match, you'll miss the chance of starting at the top of the cage at Ascention...' 'Whatever, dude. I mean, that person should be an easy target, right?' 'It might be, but it'll certainly give you an advantage.', explains Dick, trying to put some senses in Ace's mind. 'Don't worry, I'll be back in time. Bye now...', says Ace, before taking the phone down. 'WAIT, YOU DON'T KNOW THE CATCH...', tries to yell Dick, but to no avail, as Ace turns the phone out and throws it on his backpack, as he jumps in a nearby cab and leaves the area, as the scene fades to black**
