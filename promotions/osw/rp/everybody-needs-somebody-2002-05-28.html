Everybody Needs Somebody...

28 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Tuesday, May 28, 2002 Our Scene once again is the Toronto Local Hospital, unlike last time we aren’t in the waiting room but a patient’s room. Several cards line the side of a bed; the rest of the room is plain and dull. In the bed lies Pablo Rucha, a semi glazed over look in his eyes. Surgery to repair the damage in his knee took place only hours early and the drugs are only now starting to wear off. His eyes flicker as he slowly wakes, glancing at his heavily bandaged leg being elevated by an elaborate contraption. A creak is heard as the door opens… Of all the people to enter the room, Alexei Ruslan, the proponent of Rucha's injury, slinks into the room and shuts the door. Alexei smiles at Rucha and turns the lock on the door. 'Pablo Rucha.....how did the surgery go?' questions Ruslan, a sly grin on his face. Rucha blinks twice as he tries to lift his head upward to see the face, but he can't muster the strength. It barely matters, as the voice is enough for Pablo to know who is there. 'You have a lot of nerve...' Pablo starts, '...What do you want?' Alexei grins wickedly and walks over to the damaged leg, which is held by a sling suspended from the ceiling. He leans on the bed and eyes the damaged knee. 'Why,' comments Alexei, 'just here to see how the surgery went? I am concerned for your safety..after all....' Pablo tries again to pull himself up but the strength just isn't there and he falls back down in a huff. He starts to fumble a small remote which Alexei eyes, 'Here, let me.' He says in a convincing tone. Rucha hesitates before handing the device over to the Russian. Alexei fidgets with the remote for a second, 'Here Mexican, allow me to show you how to handle machinery. After all, Russians are the industrial people and scientific geniuses! You wish your head to be raised?' He grins and hits a button..which raises the lower portion of the bed. He bites his lip and hits another button, which emits a quickly alarm, but he quickly shuts it off. Alexei grumbles in Russian as he stares at all the strange buttons. He sticks his tongue out and hits another button, finally getting the right one and slowly elevates Rucha's head. 'THERE!!' Alexei exclaims triumphantly. Pablo stares into the proud eyes of Alexei a sense of tension fills the air. 'Thanks...' Is the word that breaks the silence as Rucha glances past Ruslan and at the closed door. Alexei looks at the door and smiles, 'Oh, no one will be bothering us for a while.' He grins and motions to a nearby security camera, the red light, indicating that it is 'on' is not lit. 'Something The Russian Federation has. I thought I would test it out on that camera too.' Alexei walks over to Rucha, right by his head, and whispers, 'I could put you under for a long time..if you want, Pablo.' Pablo stares into the serious eyes of Ruslan and decides it's best to play along. 'I think I'm pretty good with the drugs in me already, but thanks for the offer. I appreciate it.' Alexei grins back and stands up straight, 'Just a thought, that is all. I got you this far, after all. Perhaps now, you can understand the severity of the situation, da?' Alexei walks over to the heart monitor, 'After all....your precious El Drago is quite alone at the moment..he could use some friends, could he not? More so than..a cripple...' Alexei fools with a wire, not really paying much attention to Rucha. 'Everyone needs a friend sometime...' Pablo chimes in. Alexei turns, grinning ear to ear. 'Very true Rucha! Very true! That being said, are you willing to join the glorious Red Army?' Pablo ignoring Alexei starts to hum the tune to 'Everybody needs somebody sometime' and then upon Alexei's last word he starts to sing 'Cause everybody needs somebody sometime, Everybody needs someone, can't you see. Everybody needs somebody sometime...' 'Shut up you fool!!' Screams Alexei, not appreciating the ignorance of Rucha. 'Everybody needs some drugs sometime...' Rucha sputters out before comprehending Ruslan's scream. He turns to the Russian and just stares at him blankly with a giant goofy smile on his face. Alexei runs his hand under his hat and through his hair, 'Do you understand the severity of situation, fool?' Alexei looks over at the damaged knee, 'I could ruin you, right now, you understand this?' 'You shouldn't do that.' Pablo says with sudden urgency. 'Do what?' Alexei says with an evil smirk. 'Wearing hats all the time has been known to cause baldness.' Rucha says before breaking out into a stoned laughter. Alexei breathes in deeply and takes a step closer and points a finger at Rucha. 'You listen to me, Pablo Rucha. You can laugh now..but you weren't laughing when you were squirming on the ground, begging for mercy in that dog language of yours...do you remember that? Huh?' Alexei puts his hand just above Rucha's damaged knee. 'You're touching my special place,' Rucha responds with much laughter 'It gives me the no feeling.' Pablo starts to laugh hysterically as Alexei seethes and fixes his hat. 'Fine! You wish to laugh?! You wish to carry on!? We will see who gets last laugh Pablo Rucha! You will not outsmart Alexei Ruslan! You will not outfox him!! You will not out maneuver him!!' Ruslan turns and trips over a cord as he tries to leave, he stumbles into the door and spins, fixing his tie. 'Das vidanya, Pablo Rucha! You have not seen the last of Alexei Gregorovich Ruslan!!!' He grits his teeth and unlocks the door and exits the room. 'Toodle Ooo!' Rucha gets out between laughs as the red light blinks back on the video camera and the OSW feed cuts out.
