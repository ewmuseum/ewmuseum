The Spotlight

10 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 10, 2002 Type, type, type away. '' Slight pause. 'Hoooooooooorse shit!' Well, Nick Casey certainly sounded happy. 'Christy, have you seen this shit?' Nick's face became redder, as the light off the computer screen cut through the dark night. He was obviously angered at the contents of the screen. He ran his finger back and forth in a horizontal line. Obviously on Old School Wrestling's site, as indicated by the address previously read, but what could possibly be wrong? '....Doug Troy, Frankie Bones, and Andrew Hawk. Which leaves one very important question, WHERE AM I?' Christy read across the horizontal lines. 'I don't know what to tell you, Nick.' 'Well, someone's gonna hear about this! I bust my ass for this company, taking blood and sweat and whatnot and this is the thanks I get!?' ANC took a deep breath. 'I can't even think about this right now. I'm going to my room.' Nick shut the laptop and rolled onto his hotel bed. Soon after, he got up. There was a loud crashing noise coming out from around the ice machine. The only people on this level were OSW superstars, and Nick wanted to see who was making a disturbance this late at night. He opened the door, and saw a surprising figure standing out in the hallway. It was Andrew 'Ace' Hawk. Nick became infuriated, seeing the CCW Legend trying to take his spot. It may have been completely unintentional, but as of late, Nick hadn't been so rational. Andrew never actually saw Nick, but Angeldust tapped him on the shoulder, and superkicked him directly in the mouth. Andrew flailed back, and Nick speared him. He repeatedly punched him in the face until he started spewing out blood. The doors started to open, as Andrew began to scream. Graphic Violence stepped out of his room, and was greeted with another spear by Casey. Nick just slammed Graphic V's head against the ground until he stopped moving, and felt an arm around his neck. He flipped him over to discover it was Jason Winters, and he stepped on his throat, crushing his windpipe. Propaganda was next, but Angeldust just kicked him square in the balls, and kneed him right in the nose. Kid Wonder flew at him, but Nick ducked and dropped KW on his head. Doug Troy ran up, but Casey just booted him in the face. Civil Disobedience came from both sides, but Nick hit them with double elbows and punches to the face. What a fucking roll. More superstars came out, Reno Davis, Kimbusa, Clay Vendetta, and Corg all found there ways to being thrown down the flight of stairs at the end of the hallway. Next, the man who they call World Champion came out of his room. Switchblade saw Nick, even though he had no clue who he was, and ran at him. He got Nick good in the face, however Nick used his own move against him; The Greco Roman Ballshot. Needless to say, he was out. However, that was not the last of the superstars. Next, Ivan Stanislav walked groggily out of his room, wearing a bathrobe, and a blindfold. 'How dare you disturb my sleep? I'll kill you!' The Russian Bear ran wildly at Nick Casey, however, one punch from Casey and Stanislav hit the floor. One last door opened, however, Nick didn't quite see it. He continued to walk down the hallway, and soon heard the footsteps creaking behind him. 'Who are you?' He turned around and saw none other than President George W. Bush. George W. Bush? Nick turned around again, and suddenly Jack Miller hit him in the face with a crowbar. And even more suddenly, it was light outside, and Nick was in his bed. 'Wow...that was pretty intense.' But how much of it was a dream? He went over to the computer again, and began typing. 'www.oldschoolwrestling.com' he said aloud, since Christy had not been there at the time. Probably getting breakfast or putting her face on. Women. But there was still no 'Angeldust' on the front page of OSW. 'Pssh. That's what you get when Russians are on the top of a federation.' Racist? Kinda.... 'Lousy Commies.' Racist? Yes.
