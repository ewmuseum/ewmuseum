Interview... WITH A JACKASS!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After a good-natured urban comedy on some nameless affilate station ends, the OSW logo appears as Nashville Pussy's song 'I'm The Man' begins playing. We see Robbie Decker at poolside, having lotion rubbed into his skin by beautiful models, and Decker is wearing a 3 sizes too-small speedo, his package straining against the material. He looks at the camera and grins as he points to his sack. Some short clips of OSW shows begin playing, featuring clips of Sebastian Toomes, Meanstreak, Drew Hostyle and Frankie Bones, Corg, and other OSW heels beating on 'the good guys' as 'I'm The Man' continues to play. Finally, the camera opens up to Robbie Decker in a studio, similar to what you would see if you were watching WWF Excess. Yeah, I'm going there. Robbie looks smug and grins widely, showing his freshly cleaned teeth. 'Hello and welcome to OSW Frontline with Robbie Decker. Tonight on the show I'm gonna go over the events of last Slam! and shamelessly hype the Pay Per View, Head On Collision... Because I get paid to. In addition, I will also be my lovable self, pointing a shitty pointed stick at anyone I want... Because I can. You got a problem with that, change the channel.' Decker turns to a video-screen. An image of Sebastian Toomes bull-dogging Tempest's face off the steel ringsteps is shown, and Decker smiles. 'Our guest tonight is someone I personally admire. He's the OSW Champion, and probably will continue to be until the very end of time, when God decides he's had enough of you morons running around breathing the same air as superior beings such as myself... And Sebastian Toomes, as well. But before we bring him out, why don't we go back to last Saturday's Slam! and check out what happened?' The image begins to move and the last Slam! match between Tempest and Sebastian Toomes begins to play. It starts off with Toomes and Jane Reagan coming down to the ring, Jane holding Toomes back in mock-concern against Tempest. 'Here we are with the Champion coming down to the ring with the always lovely Jane Reagan...' Decker says in a voice-over. The angle quickly changes to Toomes bull-dogging Tempest's face off the steel-ringsteps. 'No sooner as the match started, Toomes began taking it to the challenger! Hell, as soon as he got down to the ring, he was kicking her ass, baby!' Once again, the tape cuts to Toomes kicking Tempest in the side, from an angle where Jane Reagan (who came in to interfere) is totally obscured... Tempest is lifted off her stomach and moves to the side, clutching her flank. Decker's voice again. 'After administering a beatdown on the challenger, Toomes went for the pin...' Finally, the tape cuts to an in image of Toomes pinning someone... but someone's Photoshop'ed Tempest lying on the mat. The ringbell sounds, and we cut to a picture of Toomes holding the OSW title... A image of Toomes from Crowning Glory, however, when he won the title in a match against Timo Bolamba. 'And another successful title defense for the OSW Champion! Tempest never stood a chance againt the creme de la creme of OSW, Sebastian Toomes... And now, with no further ado... SEBASTIAN TOOMES!!!' Toomes' entrance music, 'Get To The Gone' by Static-X hits, and Toomes entrance video plays on the monitors in the studio. Toomes appears from backstage, with a friend in tow... PCW Champion Chris Chase! They both hold up thier title belts, obviously having had them shined for the show, and they look at each other and congratulate each other by shaking each other's hand. 'Good job, fellow Champion!' Toomes says cheerily. 'Why thank you, OSW Champion Sebastian Toomes! I, The Messiah Chris Chase, PCW World Champion, thank you!' Chase says, putting his cheek against the title. Suddenly, Chase looks at Toomes seriously. 'Wait, a hair is out of place on the OSW Champion's head!' Toomes puts a hand up to his mouth in shock. 'Perish the thought! Where?' Chase holds up his shiny title. 'Here, use my highly-polished PCW World Title as a mirror, it's highly reflective surface should certainly prove to be sufficient to your needs, fellow Champion!' Toomes fixes a stray strand of hair on his ponytail, then turns around and gives Chase a thumbs-up. 'Why, thank you, fellow Champion! And you know what, Chris?' 'What's that, Sebastian?' 'I noticed something. I look DAMN good in the reflection of your...DAMN good looking title!' Toomes says, pointing to his face and then to Chase's PCW World Title. Chase smiles and points to Toomes' title. 'Hey, your title is pretty damn good-looking if I, The Messiah Chris Chase, PCW World Champion, say so myself!' Both World Champions walk up to the screen where Robbie Decker stands, and all three men shake hands for the next 45 seconds. 'It's great to see both World Champions of the PTC... The Messiah Chris Chase, PCW Champion, and Sebastian Toomes, OSW World Champion. What a treat it is to see you both!' Decker says. 'Why thank you, Robbie! It's great to be here on YOUR show!' Chase says. 'It's not often that I appear in clean studios. I know that the PCW Champion deserves more dilligence on the cleaning staff of PCW, something you people here in OSW do FINELY!' Toomes nods in affirmation. 'But I must make a point of congratulating your towel-boy, Snow, for shining that title. It's quite shiny. And Decker! Thank... Well, thank Messiah here for having the strength and courage to show the match between me and that skanky ho, Tempest the way it really happened!' Decker nods and continues. 'Well, Sebastian... May I call you Sebastian?' Toomes nods, brushing some stray dust off his title. 'Right. Well, Sebastian, this Saturday you face Ivan Stanislav, Doug Troy, Timo Bolamba, and Michael Dame. What are your thoughts on the matter?' The camera shifts to Toomes and Toomes hefts the title up on his shoulder. 'Well, Robbie, this match is nothing to a Champion of MY caliber, right Chris?' Chris nods, hefting his title onto his shoulder as well, grinning wickedly. 'Yes, if I, the PCW World Champion, were in such a match, I wouldn't sweat it either.' Robbie nods, pleased. 'So, Champion of OSW and in this case, Defender of the American Way of life, liberty, and the pursuit of fine ass owners of the company you're employed with, do you have any words to your opponents for Saturday?' 'Why, I'm glad you asked, Robbie! I do, in fact, have words. Several words, some of which I have to say to my opponents!' Toomes says, placing his title belt around his waist. Chris Chase helpfully fits it snug around Toomes. 'First of all,' Toomes begins, 'I don't understand why Timo Bolamba is even in this - more Ed trickery, I reckon! I defeated Timo single-handedly at Crowning Glory, he shouldn't even be in this match. Second, this Michael Dame character... Shouldn't he be in prison for child molestation and drug trafficking? Ivan, I can understand - he's a little pissy about me defeating his valet in a title match. Well, BOO HOO, Ivan! She wanted a shot at THE STAR! Well, she got one, and she just couldn't handle all this. Am I right, Chris?' Chris nods and points to Toomes title. 'Hey, with that around your waist, nothing more needs to be said. A World Champion like myself and Sebastian here... We're just too much!' 'Well said!' Decker interjects. 'Isn't there some other guy involved?' Chase asks wonderingly. 'Hmmm... I do seem to recall there was a washed-up has-been loser involved as well... And I don't mean Boda, either!' Toomes laughs heartily at his own joke. Chase points to Toomes and guffaws. 'Ain't that the truth! He sullied this title, and only the cleansing light of The Messiah could wash the filth his Slavic body emitted upon this wonderous thing I wear around my waist!' 'Oh, yes...Ed! Ah, well, I'll let Meanstreak to take care of Ed. And Bones and Hostyle can take care of the other trash. Ivan, I'm not worried about. After all, I'm the Champion of these people. Ivan and his brood should pay me the respect I deserve - after all, I AM the Champ!' Decker turns to the camera. 'Well, as much as I despise commericals, we must go to one know. When we come back, we'll be taking e-mails and callers, and The Two Greatest Champions Ever will tell us all about the way the belts feel and how much better they are than all of you. See ya soon, suckers!' The camera pulls out to a commercial, as Toomes and Chase polish thier belts and Decker claps appreciativly of thier shining metallic gleam. We, fortunatly, fade to black!
