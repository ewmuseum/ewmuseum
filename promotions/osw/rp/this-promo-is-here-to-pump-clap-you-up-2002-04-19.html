This promo is here to pump *clap* you up!

19 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-19-2002 The unmistakable ‘Theme of 2001: A Space Odyssey’ welcomes you, the viewer, to the latest Doug Troy promo. The music begins its gentle fade to a black background. The background starts to brighten as the music builds slowly. Buuum, buuuuuuuum, buuuuuuuuuuuuuum… Suddenly, the background quickly changes into a picture of two muscle-bound men, their physiques covered with a grey sweatsuit and sweatpants. Everything about them is nearly identical, from the short black hair to the ugly smiles. BA-DUM! BUM BUM BUM BUM BUM BUM BUM BUM! The music continues as the men flex their pecs in time to the music. When they finish flexing, the music fades out and the short one speaks. “Ja, ja, that was gut!” The short one smiles as he flexes his arms. “Hallo, I am Franz!” “And I am Hans!” says the taller one, flexing as well. “And we are here to pump,” both men clap their hands and point at the camera. “You up!” “Ja, that was goot. Hallo and welcome to Hans and Franz. Now, before we start ze show, we would like to say a little something at first.” Says Hans. “Ja. First of all, do not adjust your TV sets. This is our actual size.” Franz continues. “Und secondly, we will not do little girlie things like Tae-Bo on this show. Me and Franz care not for karate!” “Ja, Bruce Lee was a girlie man! All I would have to do to beat him is flex my beautiful muscles at him and he’d run away!” Franz says, smiling widely in the knowledge of his superiority. “Now that dat is out of the way, we would like to welcome our special guest to the show! He is American pro-wrestling for the Old School Wrestling federation! His name is Doug Troy, und he has brought a friend mit him today! Frauliens und gentlemen, welcome Doug Troy and Nibbles!” Hans says, finishing his introduction by flexing some more. Doug Troy steps out onto the stage with Nibbles perched on his shoulder. “Welcome Doug Troy! It is pleasure to have you and little furry friend here! Now, I understand that you want us to train your koala for big fight this week?” Doug nods in reply. “That’s right, Franz. Nibbles has a big match for $100,000 against a monkey with an afro, and I think that you guys are the only two who can get him into shape.” “Haw haw, well he is a little girlie koala. Look at how skinny he is. What are you feeding koala?” Hans ask while offering his finger for Nibbles to shake. Instead, Nibbles tries to take a bite out of it. “Eucalyptus leaves is all, maybe the occasional SUUUUUUUUUURGE!” “Oh, no wonder he is skinny! He needs to sample the might of German food!” Franz nods knowingly. “Yeah, anyway, how do you guys wanna do this? Do I just leave him here with you?” “Nein, nein. You stay here too. You’ve got some girlie man in you too. I can see it in your imperfect muscles!” says Hans, crossing his arms in front of his chest. “Oh, okay. Uh, before we start, I just have a quick question. Are you two Communists?” Both Hans and Franz share some hearty German laughter. “Dyaaaahahahahahahahahaha!” They both break the laughter at the same time, and Franz says what’s on both of their minds. “If we were Communists, we would not be as perfect as we are today!” “Ja, Communists tell someone else to be perfect for them, but me and Franz do not which to do a girlie man thing like that!” “Ja, we’re the ones that killed Communism in Germany! This one day, Hans was standing on the Berlin Wall, flexing at the girlie man guards and making them wet themselves in fear and envy, and this lady on the east side of the wall was so overcome by Hans’ perfection that she started to tear the Berlin Wall down mit her bare hands! Is this not true, Hans?” “Ja, it is true. I remember it too, this is the exact flex I did too!” Hans strikes a pose. “Warning to all female women watching this show! Do not allow yourself to be overcome with lust at seeing this perfect body!” Meanwhile, Doug is watching this with wide eyes, wondering just what the hell he’s gotten himself into this time. Franz continues the monologue. “Ja, I understand dat you are fighting Communists in wrestling, are you not Herr Troy?” “Uh, yeah.” “What you should do to beat them is flex at them like this!” Franz strikes a pose. “The mere sight of a genuine Hans and Franz Flex is enough to scare any of those Communist girlie men!” “Ja, but now it is time to stop the talking and start the working, because me and Franz are here to pump…” *CLAP* “You up!” Doug takes Nibbles off his shoulders and sets him on the ground in front of Hans and Franz. They glare at him as menacingly as they can, causing Nibbles to cower. Both of them speak at the same time. “Welcome to girlie koala hell, Nibbles!” Before anything can happen, though, the feed cuts to a black screen filled with text. A narrator speaks the words out loud. “The following segment has been cut from this promo, as it exhibits animal cruelty, angry Germans and lots of gratuitous flexing. Due to author laziness, the scene has been cut from the broadcast of this promo. We now return you to the conclusion of this promo.” The feed cuts back to Hans, Franz, Doug Troy and a beefed up Nibbles, who is now much larger and is wearing a grey sweatshirt and sweatpants just like Hans and Franz’s. “Ha ha, that is all for Hans and Franz’s television show! We would like to thank Doug Troy for allowing us to turn his girlie koala into a koala worthy of being a German!” says Hans. “Ja, und what we did for Nibbles we can also do to you! So if you have a little girlie dog or a flabby tabby, just bring them to Hans and Franz! We will take care of them, because that is what we do! I’m Franz.” “And I’m Hans.” “And we are here to pump…” *CLAP* “You up!” Hans, Franz and Nibbles flex for the camera while Doug stands still, wondering what the hell he’s doing there as the promo comes to a close.
