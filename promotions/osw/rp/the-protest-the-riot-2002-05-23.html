The Protest, The Riot

23 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 23, 2002 The protest had already begun as Reno Davis, Mikey Thomas and Frank Swallows approached the large group. Reno immediately noticed the bandanas around each protestor’s neck and felt a shiver run down his spine. They had parked the car a few blocks away for safety, but Reno felt that a getaway might prove difficult if things got sticky. “Well, should be go join the crowd?” Frank asked, looking on. “Nah, we’ll just sit back and watch. If we’re needed, we’ll join. I wanna see where this thing is heading first.” One of the protestors, presumably the leader, stood up on an upside down trashcan and began to shout: “America has proven their hypocrisy once again with their acts of revenge towards innocents in Afghanistan!” An ovation from the crowd followed, and Reno began to be disappointed. “We demand peace by any means necessary!” The leader screamed once more. After the ovation, sirens blared and police began to surround the protestors. Reno, Frank and Mikey were all pushed towards the protestors by incoming police cruisers. The protestors began to holler and Reno realized that this protest was about to get rowdy. He checked his watch and shook his head in disappointment; they had only been there 5 minutes. “Go home pigs!” The leader screamed whipping a Molotov cocktail at a nearby cruiser. Flame spilled like water all over the place and the police officers began shooting tear gas canisters at the crowd. “Shit Reno!” Frank shouted, “What a great idea you had!” “I fucking hate protests!” Reno replied. Mikey looked around in confusion and then crawled into a little ball on the ground like Frank had shown him to do when he got scared. Frank and Reno’s eyes met and they each nodded, pulling Mikey by the arms trying to move him from danger. He didn’t budge at first, but slowly realized it was his friends who were pulling him, in which he began to move away from the riot. A police officer blocked the three men, but a protestor jumped on the cop’s back and began beating him savagely. Reno steered them all in a different direction, only to be faced with an oncoming SWAT team. “Drop to the ground!” One yelled to them. “We’re not protestors!” Frank screamed, but was overshadowed by a Molotov cocktail splashing onto a SWAT team member. The officer shrieked and in reflex unleashed a barrage of bullets from his M16. The firing was followed by the out crying of protestors and police alike as the bullets shred through their clothing and flesh. “Reno!” Mikey said as he held his massive forearm with his right hand, blood streaming down from a gaping wound. Reno’s knees began to wobble and he stepped back, close to fainting. Frank took his shirt off and wrapped Mikey’s arm with it, comforting the big guy. Nearby, the perpetrator stood with a wide smile on his face. Davis didn’t recognize the blonde haired mask man until he was the blood soaked pair of scissors in his hands. The man waved and charged at Reno, lunging the scissors at The Unwanted Angel’s throat. Reno dodged and flipped the man over onto his back, sending the scissors sliding across the ground. The man kicked Reno in the face and then tackled Reno to the ground. “Remember me Reno?” He asked, then shot a fist at Reno’s nose, which erupted with blood. Reno yelped and threw a rabid punch to the man’s throat, making him gag. With a flip and a few kicks and punches, Reno and the man stood face to face in a mercy fight. “Who are you?” Reno demanded, through clenched teeth. “Think back young padawan learner!” The man replied, laughing insanely at his joke. Reno’s eyes grew wide and he froze, allowing the man to flip backwards and kick Reno under the chin. Davis’ eyes went blurry and all he could make out was the sight of the yellow, brown and red masked man charging at him again. A monstrous black figure shot by Reno’s field of vision, knocking the attacker away. Mikey Thomas picked the masked man up by the skull and began to squeeze, much to the expense of the man. “Leave him alone!” Mikey shrieked, slamming the man’s face against the road. Surprisingly, it didn’t knock the man out, who shot back up and struck his foot into Freight’s groin. The 7’5 monster of a man keeled over allowing the masked man to send both thumbs up into Mikey’s eyes, sending the big guy onto his ass holding his eyes. Reno’s vision cleared and saw Mikey fall backward. “Holy shit...” Reno said to himself in amazement. This skinny blonde dude just took out Mikey! Reno thought to himself. “Well Reno, fancy seeing me here huh?” The man said, laughing again. He had a bad sense of humor. “Who the hell are you?” Reno’s memory hadn’t been jogged as to who he was. “Reno! Get the hell away from him!” Frank yelled at Reno as he ran to tackle the masked man. The man kicked at Frank, who dodged and sent an uppercut into his nose. A crescent-kick, sidekick and pump-kick later, the masked man had toppled over backwards. Frank and Reno grabbed Mikey and ran away with him. The three took off further into the field of protestors as the masked man recovered. His got up to his feet and wiped some blood from his nose before licking it off his fingers. He picked up his scissors and smiled, He wasn’t done with Reno yet. A rioter ran past and the man stuck his foot out, tripping him onto his own Molotov cocktail, igniting him into flames. The masked man grinned and winked at the boy. “Hot out today isn’t it?” He joked, badly at that. His smile disappeared when something he saw caught his interest. Oh, was he ever not finished with Reno Davis. “What’s his name?” Reno asked Frank as they exited the crowd and stood at the opposite corner of the blockade from what had just happened. Mikey whimpered over his hurt arm and the fact that both of his eyes were practically poked out. “His name is Kasp. I fought against him in CLUB last summer. He’s a dangerous guy Reno, what did you do to piss him off?” Frank looked genuinely scared. “I don’t know...I’ve never even heard of the guy! Kasp was it?” Reno replied. “Yeah, he always spoke to himself. I think his real name was Noiles...” Frank shook his head, thinking. Reno’s heart stopped. “Kyle Noiles?” Reno asked monotonously. Frank eyed him in surprise and nodded. “Yeah, that’s it. How’d you know?” “He trained me...”
