'Snap.'

15 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 15th, 2002 'I don't want to die here.' Six words, one contracted, summing up how you see your future. 'Yer not gonna die, ya dumbass.' Your eyes are swollen shut. Not from any beating he's given you, but from all those tears you've let roll down your cheeks. It must have been hours now. You haven't been drugged or harmed...at least physically. Every word he's said to you since he dragged you off those dirty streets last night has done nothing but make you want to crawl into a hole too small for your body to fit into. It's made you want to lay there with your eyes open and let everyone around you fill in your shallow grave with dirt...paying special attention to get the proper amount into your eyes to soak up your tears. You screech, a sound so distorted and muffled from repetition, it barely seems to bounce off any of the walls in the cheap motel room. Earlier, you'd wondered why he would be staying in a place like this on his salary. He's told you about everything in his life he remembered, giving enough information and sickening description it's forced your mind to toy with itself just to attempt any righteous clutch to your sanity. The burning around your wrists continues as he yanks at them and growls obscenities under his breath in defiance. As he pulls away, you try to move your arms up, a foolish choice that only brings restraint and shooting pain to your dislocated shoulder. You can see the blurred outline of his head as he looks up at you. He must know how much agony you are experiencing at that very moment...at least it seems he does. His head stays in the same position for an everlasting second. Your tears have ceased shortly, for the moment, but your mind has been swept under the carpet ages ago. You know he's not going to do anything but your mind keeps fearing the worst. The worst was at the magazine rack. The first time you'd seen him in years...seven to be exact...almost to the day. You remember what the two of you were like in high school...how he held your hand, the exact amount of pressure he applied. And, ironically, how gentle he was with you. You'd been looking at Time magazine at a comical little article defacing Bill Gates' public image and how he saw the world...each word in the text sending signals of pleasure to your brain. Then, as if eclipsed, Ethan's body loomed over you as he browsed the selections on the display before him. You looked up to him and back down, merely acknowledging his presence. Never, not in a million years, would you think he'd step so nonchalantly back into your life. Your eyes perked up and began welling with what are now torturous tears. Your ears reddened in anticipation and your skin rose in goosebumps as old thoughts wakened once more within your body. You were excited. Beyond bounds. You wanted to touch him, but something inside you told you that it wasn't proper, an old habit you had picked up a millenium ago. Instead, a vote for an adressing of his name came to mind and shot out through your lips almost involuntarily...almost as if your body was being taken over just by the sight of your first real love. You watched him turn towards you and give you an almost dumbfounded look that sent more tears free. You always cried...even at your niece's birthday parties...and you see her twice a year. When he said your name, your knees nearly left their foundation and crumbled beneath your broken torso. You clutched at your breast, as if you could actually stop your heart from its acceleration, and stepped toward him. You were reliving the past. It seemed to take him a moment, which confused you, but when that smile finally managed to light up his face, your confusion had gone. You were home with Ethan Gabriel Giroux in Louisiana, arms thrown about eachother and speaking words strewn out through broken poetry. You were 16 again...and today was your birthday. You choke back another cry as the thoughts bring to light once more that today, March 14th, is the day of your birth. Always had you looked upon these as days of excitement and joy...but you assure yourself no more joy or excitement will accompany the 14th...nothing will EVER make this day joyous again. 'God yer lippy...shut the f*ck up, can ya? Jus fer a second?' You glance back to him as he pulls at your ankles like he were hanging a slab of dead meat on its hook. 'If ya don't, I'ma stuff yer airway...ya don't want that, do ya?' You shaked your head warily as your head falls back, the fatigue finally beginning to get to you. The smallest of tasks, even the raising of your head, seem to be arduous feats of stamina now. You need rest. Suddenly, your head shoots up, muscles in your neck pumping with adrenaline, the moment his hand rests upon your bare thigh. Your mouth involuntarily props open as he looks up to you. Your eyes blink and your tongue creeps over your dried lips, moistening them as he wipes away your latest tear with his masive thumb...just as he did last night in the dim, fading sunlight. It brings a calm over you along with clearing, sharper vision. You smile...he almost looks the same to you. 'Yer a piece of ass...why the hell'd they take me away fore I banged ya once more?' This is not your Ethan. Your smile fades slowly upon his crude words as you stare down at him. You remember him referring to himself as Meanstreak now. God, what had they done to him? Meanstreak? Never do you remember him using a less than civil tone with you...even when you had your lover's quarrels. He used to be so gentle, so kind, so consumed...with you. Not himself. Why would they drag him into some sanitarium as if he were foaming at the mouth and spouting threats of releasing a revised plague on the entire populace? You must have asked yourself varied questions of the same nature thousands of times now. No answers were coming to you, but you were begging for some explanation. His hand remains on your thigh as he props his other arm on his knee and places his chin in his hand. He cocks his head a couple times, blinking through both of his eyes, seeming as if he were pondering something deep within your own 23 year old body. 'How long were we together, Clara?' His question catches you off guard. That adrenaline the single touch he'd aniministered upon you seems to flow from your neck and you groan as your head slowly falls back against your constant disapproval. You keep your head propped back for a moment and think, finiding the answer nearly instantaneous with his hand as it rises higher up your thigh. The adrenaline returns, as do your eyes' lock with his. 'Three and a half years...' You stop there, fear driving you away from revealing anything more unsettling to him. 'Damn...three and a half years...' His hand leaves its act as the perch for his head and becomes a blunt instrument being driven down to that tiny 3/4 inch space of wood that's free from your arm's safety. You clench your eyes tight in response, even after the blow is delivered, and turn your head just slightly to the side. You hear him mumbles something under his breath then elect to open your eyes. The pain of such an instinctual response shoots through your battered and tired eyes as you bring them back to the light. Tears begin surfacing and try to persuade you to let them be with their brethren at the base of your face, but you refuse and shake your head. Your mind suddenly begins to throb, you setting the blame on that of the verbal abuse and his nauseating stories involuntarily clutching your sympathy rather than your swiftly diminishing energy. 'I woulda membered that had they not taken me there...hell...I could even be married ta ya and fatherin' our children with ya...' His eyes creep back to yours as he shakes his head, anger and contempt beginning to seep through him. 'I could be just another normal guy right now...another person in this world livin' their life without any f*ckin' catch...think our children woulda been somethin' special, Clara?' His new string of words delivers your thoughts to your two twin children most likely sleeping away the night at your sister's house in a makeshift crib, unbeknownst to the location of their poor mother. Those tears bully around your mind again, but you manage to fend them off and remained focused on his question. With the utmost honesty and more meaning than you've ever wielded before in your life, you answer him with four simple words. 'Absolutely...they'd be ours.' Ethan, once a man, now this...thing...perks up a quaint smile as he looks down from your eyes, his own seeming to fall upon a study of your body. You know he's not surveying your body one bit...you know what you said is sinking into him and maybe even warming one of those countless chilled caverns within his body. His eyes slowly rise back to yours as his lips spread wider in a more jubilant smile. 'We woulda had beautiful kids, doll...how old are your children, Clara?' He's getting nosey. You wonder if it's another trap he's beginning to set...the same as he did yesterday. You wonder if he's trying to lure your mind in first and lash out for your body later. All these precautionary warning lights shooting to life in your head, yet, you still answer. 'Becca and Jenna...they're twins...just turned one.' The monster before you tilts his head. He brings his hand free of your thigh as his brow furrows downward. 'Becca...Jenna...?' His eyes narrow and his upper lip contorts into a mediocre snarl. You know what you did and you're stabbing yourself in the heart with every single bad thing you can remember in your head. You curse at yourself, demean, berate, slander...every horrid thing you can conjure up in three seconds time within your own mind as a means of punishment for telling him. One of those memories of his is beginning to stir...and his brain is not taking it lightly. You somehow manage to stammer out a weak response, both in terms of delivery and context. 'Yuh...yes...' Ethan stands from his crouching position, your entire body now filling with more anxiety and fear as you remember just how large he's become. You are deathly afraid of him...and for good reason. 'Why...didja name one Becca?' You swallow, one of relief for that question or statement you thought would arise being postponed. 'The father and I...we just liked it.' Then, you realize you have two children...and you can't very well name them both the same. '...and Jenna?' He bares his teeth, a new projection of one of the three emotions you've seen him show. There it is. That question you didn't want to be obligated to answer. You know why you chose that name...you know exactly why. You also know what this beast's actions will be in response. And it frightens you...more than any moment in this 24 hour period has. '...it was my way of remembering you...your mother's name...' You answer again, regardless of your fears. Ethan stays still as he glares down upon you. His lips slowly cover up his flawless teeth again but his eyes remain narrowed. He leans down and places both his hands atop your rope bound wrists, his head and chest lowering down to you. Your neck aches and cranes back in an almost painful position as he closes in on you. 'Why...do ya...insist on...mindf*ckin' me...ever since ya mentioned my ma...my brain's been screamin' at me like a dyin' puppy...ya woke somethin' up, Clara...it don't wanna go back ta sleep, but I want it to. Make it sleep again, Clara...make it sleep again fore I...snap.' His face, only a distraction. Your mind shoots from the present pain and the past of the few seconds since gone. You remember his pause. Dramatic and profound. You feel his hot breath shoot over your chin as he speaks that single word again. You keep yo-yoing back and forth between past and present, until you can't ignore the present anymore. 'Snap.' He says it again. No build up this time...no hidden meanings. Just an accompaniement to the action he unfolds. 'Snap.' God...another repetition? 'Snap.' Another and your mind begins attempting to block out the pain. Endorphins rush to heightened levels and accelerated numbers, trying to dull the pain and keep your head occupied on other things. 'Snap.' However, that word keeps interrupting their noble stand. Your eyes, since jammed as tightly shut as they could possibly be, slowly open upon the realization of his ceased actions...and that word that will haunt your nightmares till the day your body expells its last breath. 'Snap.' That one, your body replays in crisp quality for your own displeasure. You look up to him for a moment, shocked at his head set still in the same place. He blinks out of his scarred eye and then, quickly following, out of his unaltered. 'Ya prolly should jus stay quiet the next time...ya still have fifteen more fingers and toes for me ta destroy.' Your eyes manage to trek about his barracading face and look down to your mangled hand. With one sweep of your knuckles, you notice none of them are where they should be. Your thumb tilts to the side and points up towards your nose. Your index finger, no longer standing true to its infamous straightforward reputation, instead rests in a twisted deformity that ends in a swaying nub directing towards your big toe. Middle finger and ring turned away from eachother, each bone in the knuckle readily visible to the naked eye. And your pinky...it's not even there anymore. He clears his throat and that glance is quickly blocked as he turns your face so your eyes are level with his again. 'I warned ya once, Clara...next time...,' he sways your missing digit before your eyes, ironically clutched tightly with his own, '...I'll rip every finger from yer right hand, every toe from yer left foot...and yer right...and if ya still insist ta f*ck with my head some more...you'll lose larger body parts.' Your mind is done fighting off those tears. They come in a rush as you gather your hopes and thoughts and let the pain get to you, your maddened cries having no sound to them now...no ears to fall on. Rendering no one capable of phoning in your help, your freedom from a man you once loved. You try harder to yelp out some form of warning and cry for help, but still, nothing exits your lungs but air that dries those once moistened lips up once more. You realized earlier that he'd left your mouth free from any restraint. But, now you know why he did. He knew your voice had been taken for hours now, so why cover up the tool to bring that out? No point...it was simple logic. You continue your useless cries into the morning air as he pulls himself from you. He knows you won't last much longer...you need to renenergize. You need to rest and gain back the strength just to hold your head up straight. Had he broken your fingers just a half hour earlier, the sheer pain of it could have kept you conscious and awake for a couple hours at best. You feel him push off your body and leave you there, his heavy footsteps echoing clearly in your mind as your eyes begin to shut from the fatigue. Without your sight, your sense of hearing, touch, taste, and smell are all that remain. You try to listen harder, but to no avail, the energy needed to do so, quickly depleting itself like a punctured gas tank. Your right hand feels at the wood of the chair and, for a moment, you are satisfied. It's when you try to move that missing pinky on your left hand that your sense of touch leaves in even more agonizing pain. Your tongue is bathed in the air at the moment, nearly drying it to the same texture as your chapping lips. You close your mouth to try and garner what little strength it takes to salivate, but even that is a task to difficult to partake in. Darkness pulls you in as you try to sniff for some distinguishing odor that may lie evident within the air. With the aggrivated sinuses from your shameless crying, your nostrils have no way to bring pleasure and grant consciousness to you with the excess muchus clogging their path. You simply sit there like an energy starved puppet, letting your head lighten and your muscles slow. The physical world failed to bring any temporary pleasure from this twisted moment that is your reality. Perhaps the dream world can relieve you with an oasis of euphoria as its timed substitute.
