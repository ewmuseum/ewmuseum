Deportation: Part II

18 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/18/02 A police car pulls up in front of the Entrance to John F. Kennedy International Airport. A police officer steps out, and opens the side door for Tom Walczak. Walczak gets out, and slowly follows the officer into the airport. They bypass baggage check, and all the check-in counters, and quickly make their way over to the designated gate at which Walczak’s plane is waiting. When they arrive at the gate the officer takes the handcuffs off of Walczak, and hands him over to a customs officer. “Mr. Walczak, as you know you are being deported based on the facts that we do not have any record of your Citizenship in this country. There is a court date that is being set as we speak, and you will be contacted as to when it is. Now, we would not do this for most deportees. They have only a couple of days to sort out their matters before they get deported, but because of your somewhat celebrity status you will be given more time to gather all your information.” “Why can I not just go to my house, and collect it there. I have everything that you need there. I have never been angered to a greater extent in all my life then at this very moment, and I would very much like an explanation as to why I must leave at this instant?” “Well, it is by government order that you be sent back to your native country because they fear that a man with your resources could make a run for Mexico or for Canada if he was given the chance to stay here, and then they would not be able to pursue your… Well your deportation.” “Why in the hell would I run to Canada or Mexico? If I were to run to anywhere it would be to Poland the place where you are fucking sending me to. That explanation just proves the stupidity that surrounds this case. I would like to call a lawyer or at least my employer at OSW.” “I have been given strict orders not to let you call or contact anyone whatsoever. You are to be placed directly on the plane and there you will be contacted by the government, and they will explain to you what will be going on in the next couple of days. Now, all that I have to do is ask you a couple of questions.” “Well if you are going to ask then go the fuck ahead I can’t win anyway.” “Ok, I understand that you own a home in Poland. Will this be the residence at which you will be staying in during your tenure at Poland?” “Tak ty gupia baba.” “Ja rozumnie po Polsku.” “Myslisz zie to cos do mnie znaczy?” “Wiez co, jak ty bedzies tak do mnie odzywacz sie to ja moga to bardzo ciesko zrobicz.” “Dobrze to spiesz sie nobo jak mam jechacz do Polski to niech bedzie szybko.” “Ok, another question is do you plan to visit any relatives outside of Poland.” “I have no relatives in Poland let alone outside of Poland, but I will be visiting friends in Poland.” “Ok, well than in that case you must be home between the hours of 8-11 PM because these are the hours in which you will be contacted concerning your case. You will be given a call each day, so you should be at home.” “I have a Polish cell phone, so why can I not just be contacted on that?” “Well, all those type of questions will have to be directed towards the government official whom you will be speaking with during your flight.” “This is the last question which I will be asking. Do you have any bags that you will be bringing along with you on your flight?” “Are you shitting me? I was dragged off of the fucking street before I came here of course I have no fucking bags. It is people like you that make Polish people seem so fucking dumb.” “Ok, well you have been quite possibly the worst person whom I have had the displeasure of working with, so if you could just get on the plane it would make my day a whole hell of a lot better.” “Well, my day has been just fine and dandy, so I wouldn’t want to incur any gloominess onto yours sunshine.” Walczak heads over to the boarding area, where he is placed into OSW’s private jet. The plane takes off as the scene fades out…
