Elements of Life: Blood to Blue

14 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 14, 2002 He lays on the bed, wondering, pondering. “What,” he sobs. “What was her name?” Corg cannot remember the name of his deceased lover. He can remember her appearance, her voice, her personality, her touch, but not her idenity. Corg is spread out on his Satin fabric bed, looking up at his black ceiling. He is… crying. Not hysterically, but very much emotionally. He feels that he is nothing without her, and he maybe rightfully so. She contained my soul within her heart, soothing… Corg’s worst fear in life is his depression, and after his girlfriend died, the depression rightfully came back. And wih the depression, comes the murders, and everything that makes humanity seem so dispute. Recombinning my thoughts into oblivious pieces of insanity… “I’m dead to this world.” Corg barges, as he engulfs a swab of tears. I am nothing… I still love her… He is nothing without her. He is everything to her. Corg’s tears stain his maroon bed spread, soaking in a mob of liquid which separates itself in color. Corg has never really cried like this before. Corg has never really loved anyone like this before. He will never love anyone like this again. This will never end… It can’t. fin
