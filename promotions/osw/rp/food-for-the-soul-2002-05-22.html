Food for the soul

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 22 'I'm sorry.. what did you say again?' Kanaida asked as he lowered his glass of red wine. 'I'm afraid I didn't hear you.' The man across the table held up one finger, and continued to chew his steak. Kanaida grinned, and took another look around the unusual restaurant setting. Charlie Trotters was Chicago's best restaurant, and they were seated at easily the most sought-after table in the house.. the kitchen table. Set in the center of the restaurant's bustling kitchen, the table enjoyed a unique menu, created for the guests by the head chef, only a few feet away. But one drawback was that if there was any commotion in the kitchen, it was easy to lose track of a conversation. 'I said you need a change, my friend. You can't keep doing things the way you used to.. and you know it.' Kanaida sighed, and looked away. He'd love to argue with the statement that had just been made.. but it was true. Because if there was anyone who knew him, it was the man sitting across the table from him. His best, and oldest.. sometimes only.. friend. The man only known as Justin Sane. He sipped his wine as he considered his awnser. And as he did, he also considered Sane. One of Chicago's favorite adopted sons, 'The Rage' had risen to prominence as a no-holds fighter. His still-handsome face was a testament to his skill in the arts, after years of competition. And he'd been the Northern Wonder's greatest ally in the UWA, not only watching his back, but co-founding Highly Unstable. So when Justin Sane spoke, Kanaida listened... even when he didn't like what he was hearing. He put his glass down again, and leaned forward. 'You know as well as I do that I'm a power wrestler' he said in a low voice. 'I'm not going to start flitting around the ring like some Jason Winters alike idiot flyweight. I'd rather retire again.' Sane slapped one big hand down on the table. 'You're not listening, K.' He shook his head. 'You aren't six foot six anymore, damnit. You don't weigh two hundred seventy five pounds of freakish muscle. You have to make changes to the way you do things! You're not cut out to be a pansy like that gimp Winters, but you can't do the same things you could two years ago. If you can't see that, you SHOULD retire again.' Kanaida's face turned red, and he glanced towards the kitchen staff. Sane chuckled. 'Forget them. I pay very well for deaf ears when I dine here.. that's one of the benefits of Trotters. And one of the benefits of being a minor celeb in Chi-town.. you can get this table when you need it, and not get pestered. In return, I tip double the amount of the meal. So we can talk freely here.. besides, it's not like they'd understand your situation anyway. I personally still don't, really... could you explain it again?' Kanaida rolled his eyes. 'What am I, Basil Exposition?' Sane snickered, and raised his wine glass in salute. With a shrug, and another suspicious glance at the kitchen staff, who were studiously ignoring them, Kanaida lowered his voice, and leaned in across the table. 'Well, you remember what I told you about the procedures they performed when I was...' A server drops a tray, and sends it's contents across the floor. The head chef begins screaming and throwing objects, and chases the hapless waiter out a side door. When the commotion settles... 'And over time, things have returned to normal. The biggest changes were in the six months that I stopped working out.' Kanaida stops, and finishes his glass of wine. Sane shakes his head. 'Six months to go from six foot six to six foot two? Incredible.' He lifts the bottle of red from the table, and fills his friend's glass. Kanaida shrugs. 'Yes, but from my original size to six foot six is even more unbelievable..' he chuckles. 'I've found that now that I've started to work out again, the reversion has stopped. I'm regaining muscle mass again, and staying at this height.. and I can live with that. My biggest problem is getting my wind again.. as you remember, conditioning is a tricky thing for me now.' Sane nodded sagely. 'Ok. And while you work on that, maybe a change in style is in order? You don't have the leverage to military press DDT everyone you meet anymore, no matter HOW strong you are when you get back into shape. Think of it this way.. what are your best attributes in the ring?' 'Toughness, size, strength, and stamina.' Kanaida paused, and sighed. 'Only thing is, now I'm not as big, out of shape, and still a concussion risk. So every strength, everything that made me what I was is gone.' 'You are an idiot.' Justin's nostrils flared as he spoke. His eyes were lit from within with his legendary intensity, and Kanaida felt the full force of his friend's fury in the coldness of his voice. And this, he thought to himself, is why they call him 'The Rage'! 'You didn't succeed because you were big.. or strong.. or tough. Those things helped, yes. But that's not where your true strength lay. Damn it, think for a minute. Before you were Kanaida, before you were a huge monster, you still had the same mind. You're a quick thinker. You're inventive, able to make up new ways to take somebody down. Able to re-invent yourself. Your wrestling was always technically perfect, crisp and clean. And you've got the heart of a lion. It was not so much the physical toughness that you had going for you.. it was a never-say-die attitude... too much of one sometimes. That's how you got yourself hurt, you know.. you never did know when to quit.' Kanaida's eyes now burn with the same intensity as The Rage's. He opens his mouth to reply, but Sane cuts him off angrily. 'Shut UP. Now, you listen to me. You can take those strengths, and build something new. And why not? Remember, twice now you've started fresh under a mask, with a whole new look, moveset, and style in the ring. Why not now? Why not as yourself? Hit the gym, and while you're at it, hit the ring, and re-invent yourself. And when you're ready, your opponents will never know what hit them.' He stops, and an uncomfortable silence falls over the table. Kanaida stood, with an angry expression on his face. He dropped his napkin on his chair, and took a step towards his friend. Sane sat in his seat, not rising to any challenge, real or imagined, from his best friend. 'You're... right.' he said, his voice burning with intensity. 'I've done it before, I can do it again. But there's one more thing that is a major difference that you didn't touch on, Justin. And that's this...' He waved a hand at the kitchen. Sane's brows furrowed. 'You're in a restaurant?' he asked, not understanding what his friend was getting at. Kanaida snickered. 'Sort of. Easy living, Rage. Back in the day, I lived a more simple life.. no rich foods, no big pocketbook to fall back on. Well, that's another change. Until I get my shit back together, there's no more living in luxury.' He stuck out his hand, and after a moment, Sane took it. Kanaida held the handshake as he went on. 'Thanks, old friend. From here, I've got to take this on my own.. I'll be in touch.' Kanaida let go, and grabbed his coat. Sane stood, and gestured at the table. 'Hold on.. at least finish supper?' he said with a laugh. 'You don't need to start right away.. start tomorrow morning.' Kanaida put on his black leather coat, and turned. Sane raised an eyebrow at the burning intensity he'd seen in his friend's eyes. 'No, Justin. I'm starting now... I've waited too long already. I'm going home today, and changing everything for the better. Now, how much do I owe you for..' The Northern Wonder reached for his wallet. Sane again cut off his friend with a wave of his hand. 'Chicago's my town.. it's on me. The advice was free too. Just don't make me go all Dr Phil on you again, hmm?' Kanaida laughed, and waved. Then turned, and walked out of the kitchen. Sane moved to pour the last of the wine into his own glass.. then put the bottle down instead. 'Maybe Kanaida's not the only one who needs to get the fire back..' he mused. 'Maybe... ' He stood, and motioned to the head waiter, who came running. 'Yes, Mr Sane?' 'My bill, Alex.' Justin reached for his own jacket, and put it on. 'But sir... you haven't completed all the courses! What about..' Alex stopped talking as Sane fixed him with an intense glare. He turned, and scuttled off to tally up the night's bill. Justin Sane chuckled softly to himself. 'Maybe...' he breathed, as he pondered his own future.
