A chance encounter, but one she'd been searching for...stupid irony and it's being a bitch.

8 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 8th, 2002 ((Note: Title makes little sense, just sounded kinda neat. Thanks to Alex for being him so I didn't screw things up too badly!)) Corg was definitely trying to make Amy's life a living hell... People, basically shades of the past, kept coming back to haunt her throughout her travels, and it was REALLY becoming a pain in the ass...not only that, but it wasn't exactly fun to be tormented by these creeps. So the goal was to find Corg's big brother...yep, Rhett Locke. Amy'd honestly never encountered Locke in the least, through these last couple of months in OSW they had kept pretty seperated from one another, which was odd to say the least...but now, she HAD to encounter him, it was for her own well-being.......or so she hoped. She'd been searching all the major stoped between BC and Seattle, but found him nowhere...BUT, she had finally come into contact with one of the roadies she frequently brushed away the time backstage with by hacking (You'd BETTER know that means hacky sack.). He'd informed her that, through the night, Rhett had arrived in Seattle and taken his designated room in the Holiday Inn they'd set up. So he was there, in Seattle...but that was one BIG city, and she was just one insignificant girl...this could prove difficult. As she drove along a small street, mainly in hopes of getting coffee, she was thinking of various ways to beat Corg, her younger, yet larger and tougher opponent, not only at Ascension, but on Slam the entire was there... Sure, that whole Bethany Yamato factor was still there...but she'd disposed of Yamato several times already, so what the hell did she matter? Corg would probably go all animal again and take her out early, that much was basic psychology...so there wasn't a whole lot of point to fretting over the third party. Aha....Starbucks! She'd been up most of the night trying to get to the city, she'd been searching for Locke the rest and all morning so far...it was around noon when things perked up. Leaning against the counter in exhaustion, Amy got just simple, regular blend coffee...dumping about four packets of sugar inside the large cup before she went to sit down...landing herself right next to a very white man wearing a Saves the Day t-shirt. His hair was jet black, his septum pier----wait just a frickin' minute! ''Rhett Locke?'' He didn't seem to recognize her at all, just peering up from whatever book he'd been flipping through. ''Yeah?'' ''You're a hard guy to find!!! I've been looking EVERYWHERE...'' 'Wait, wait. You're lookin' for me? Who are--Oh, I know you.'' Locke's eyes became knowing for a moment. ''You're that one girl, fuckin...Firesnatch, right?' UGH! He did NOT just call her that...Aimz wasn't particularly fond of the nickname she'd gotten for being a natural redhead.... ''Well...uhhh....yyyyeah......but I prefer Amy. We work together. You've probably never seen me, but we do...'' 'Yeah...are you stupid? Of course I've fuckin' seen you, that's how I knew what people called you.' Rhett chuckled, 'Dumb shit.' Well, at least he had a semblance of a sense of humor! ''Yep...people sure love calling me that,'' She cringed, ''...but anyway...I'm fighting that brother of yours this week AND at the pay-per-view. See, he's been causing me a LOT of grief lately, most recently by carving an anarchy symbol into my hand with a jack knife and then sending various people from my past around to mess me up...'' 'Isiic? Fuckin' Isiic eh?' Locke's left hand suddenly took a sharp delve into his baggy pocket, and he revealed the trigger of a glock. 'Take this. You'll most likely need it.' He laughed again, amused at his own joke. 'Anyway, yeah, if I were you, I'd forget about him...stupid fucktard, needs to learn to relax.' ''You're not one for resolving issues via a simple ass-kicking, are you?'' She laughed, ''But yeah...I guess that COULD help...seeing as it's basically the only thing people I know would listen to. As for him, yeah...he's definitely one huge fucking prick.'' 'Well...how about I arm you with some advice instead of a fuckin' gun, okay?' ''That could certainly help! Heh...'' 'Alright, look at it from this perspective. Appearently, he's giving you and your life hell....so....' He paused a moment, sorting out how he'd word things, when Amy interrupted with a little pun she THOUGHT was amusing. ''..........I...make things so that the cops mysteriously find him hung in his hotel room the morning of the match?'' 'Sure, if you want. Though, if I were you, Fire--'' Locke just barely managed to correct himself in time, ''Amy, I'd return the favor. Give him hell back.' Campbell was rather puzzled... ''How, though? He seems like a pretty hard guy to get to...'' 'That's for you to decide.' The redhead was quite disappointed, to say the least. ''Well...alright, I guess. Thanks, man.'' She was about to stand, but Rhett chuckled... ''What? No sex?'' Amy laughed, ''I sure do seem to get THAT question tossed at me a lot.'' 'Come on!' ''Maybe some other time, 'kay?'' She chuckled again, winking sarcastically at Locke. What a tease. 'Yeah, whatever! Get the fuck out of here, firesnatch!' Rhett laughed when Amy stood, stepping outside of the little coffee shop and laughing all the while. Hey, maybe he wasn't such a bad guy after all! Well, that remained to be seen...this WAS Rhett Locke, but right now it didn't really matter who's personality was like what...she had a match to win at Slam.
