Pursuit

9 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-9-02 OOC: This takes place directly after Saturday Slam. Erratic footsteps echoed through the hallway backstage of Saturday Slam as the sound of someone running could easily be heard. From around the corner, Rachel Leigh Cook, still dressed in her “Tempest” garb, bolted into view and ran down the hallway. Even at a closer glance, Cook could be a passable Tempest. Perhaps lacking the Asian build to her face and not nearly as toned as Tempest, only at closer inspection could one tell that she indeed was not Tempest. This was probably used to the advantage of Davis, while keeping her a decent distance from Ivan Stanislav in the ring, he was unable to discern her features. Fatigue, blood, sweat, and chaos were probably also key factors in the ploy to throw The Russian Bear. But now, while Reno Davis has succeeded, Cook had bigger problems. She had known about pretending to be Tempest, but not the situation completely. First off, what were these Russians doing chasing her? And who was the smaller one that blindsided her? Davis had said NOTHING about her being in danger! She remembered hearing that ‘the fans would love her’ and that ‘everyone would accept her’ and ‘it’d be fun.’ Getting chased by Soviet throwbacks was NOT fun. Just as that thought entered her mind, the smaller Russian rounded the corner behind her. His dress shoes slapped against the concrete slightly softer than her black boots, but he was moving FAST! “Come back here you vixen!!” screamed the smaller Russian as he mustered up more speed. Cook screamed and rounded another corner. The area opened up into a storage area of some sort in which stacks of boxes, crates, and merchandise from old venues were piled everywhere. She dove behind a stack of boxes just in time as her assailant rounded and entered into the room. At the far end of the room was another door, but she would have had to move extremely fast to have made it. Cook stares between two boxes, her breathing quick and gulping as she spotted the smaller man enter into the room. He glared right at her for a moment and then passed her up, scanning the room. She could tell from his expression that his entire being was focused on finding her. For two minutes, the Russian scanned the room, studying it, and then he moved out of her view. Cook listened, hearing his footsteps get farther and farther away. She moved slightly… ..which was all she needed to do. Her foot squeaked on a small puddle that was created by an overhead heating pipe. She froze, listening for any sort of movement, but none came. She closed her eyes and exhaled silently. “I GOT YOU, IMPOSTER!” the high pitched voice of the Russian cut into her ears as his brown clad arm shot between the boxes and grabbed her halter top. She squealed and tried to wriggle away, but it was futile. The Russian pulled her to the side, knocking over a stack of boxes and dragging her into view. He wasn’t a terribly big man, just a few inches taller than Cook as a matter of fact, but the look in his eyes was what really bothered her. A crazy, almost, sadistic look in his eyes. She had seen a uniform much like the one that he wore before, in an old picture of Soviet soldiers rushing to attack Berlin in their brown trench coats. Her attention was suddenly snapped back to reality as he dragged her across the room, roughly pulling her, and throwing her up against a wall. “WHAT do you think you were doing?!” screamed the Russian, bringing his arms up in an overly dramatic fashion and fixing his brown hat with red band and gold star. “I…I didn’t know..I didn’t know that was going to happen!” screamed Cook back at him, trying to be as honest as she could. Tears formed in her eyes as the Russian moved in closer. “What did you think was going to happen?!” screamed Alexei at her, “you..you are nothing more than succubus that comes in and tears men’s hearts from their bodies! You are nothing more than..than a devious deceiver brought to punish Ivan for what?! For caring for somebody!!!” Cook shook her head, Ruslan’s words cutting into her, “He just said it was a promotional stunt, that is all!” “WHO SAID?!” “Reno!” Ruslan ran his tongue across the front of his teeth and fixed the waist of his pants. He reached into his inside pocket and pulled out a large .45 caliber pistol and pointed it at her. She screamed loudly. “I should blow your insides into the wall behind you for what you did! You are nothing more than another capitalist, at the highest bid of the enemy and with no conscience whatsoever!” Alexei lowered the gun and moved closer to her, grabbing her by the neck. “You think, because you are a woman, that I wont’ be harsh with you, Miss? You are wrong. I have killed men far more honorable than you…” She screamed again, but Ruslan merely laughed. “You are in storage, my dear. No one can hear you!” He paused and took a step backwards and crossed his arms, “What is your name?” “R….Rachel Leigh Cook.” She answered. “And what is your occupation?” “I…I’m an actress!” “Yes, you certainly are. And what did he tell you? I want to know everything, otherwise, my revolver might get a better response!” Ruslan patted his sidearm in his hand as Cook nodded and swallowed. “He..he just said to dress like this, and to head down during his match! He said the fans’d love it, I swear, that’s all he said! When..when I saw the..the big guy..I froze. His stare kinda…it just pierced me.” She shivered and bit a nail. Ruslan nodded, she was telling the truth, he could tell. This girl couldn’t lie without him picking it up. Alexei shook his head and put his gun away. This girl was exactly malicious, more so she was misinformed. He was still angry, however. He glared up at her, “You do not understand the pain you have caused my friend, Rachel Leigh Cook.” Just as things began to calm down and Cook actually was getting comfortable, heavy, loud, and angry footsteps began to thunder in their direction. Ruslan and Cook, at the same time, looked over in the direction of the footsteps. Just as they did, Ivan Stanislav thundered around the corner, carrying the steel post from the ring on his shoulder. “WHERE IS SHE ALEXEI!?” bellowed Stanislav. The objects rattled in the room as Stanislav rumbled into the room and saw her. Ruslan stepped to the side as Stanislav brought the huge, heavy post into his hands. “You..you….you BITCH!” Ruslan’s jaw dropped as Stanislav swore. She screamed and slinked into a corner as Stanislav walked closer to her. “You are not laughing now, are you wench? You are not laughing now that the man you thought you could twist is looming over you!” Stanislav stood over her, his shadow covering her, and raised the post high over his head, prepared to pound her into the concrete floor. “I DIDN’T KNOW!!!” she screeched, fearing for her life, “I’M SORRY!! I’M SORRY!!” Stanislav froze and looked over his shoulder at Ruslan. Alexei nodded in agreement with her. “I…I was just playing a character! I was just pretending…” she babbled, tears streaming down her face. Ivan dropped the huge steel post, taller than him, and let it land on the floor behind him and roll away. He brought his hand down and grabbed her by the throat, lifting her to his level. “She is not a character! She is more than that! She is more than anything you could understand! And you..you posed as her! You…you…..” Ivan trailed off and breathed heavier as he looked at the floor, “You…” He stared at her, his face twisting, and dropped her. “You don’t understand….” Was all he could mutter. Ruslan walked over to Ivan, “Praporschik…” but Ivan brushed him off. “Get away from me, Ruslan. You don’t understand either.” Ivan turned and walked away, his shoulders suddenly appearing very heavy on his back. He walked slowly and left the area as suddenly as he had come. “I..I’m sorry…I…” Cook began, but Ruslan shushed her. “Yes, I understand. It is not your fault. You will find that Reno Davis is wily man…and after tonight, he is also a dead man.” Ruslan started to walk away, but Cook yelled after him. “I feel bad! I..I wanna make it up to him!” Alexei turned, his face going slightly pale, “There is nothing you can do, my dear. You are lucky to be alive. Be glad you did not break his heart completely….that is what saved you.” Ruslan turned and left, leaving Rachel Leigh Cook alone in the room
