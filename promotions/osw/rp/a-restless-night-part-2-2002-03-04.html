A Restless Night (Part 2)

4 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/4/02 The scene becomes visible and we see Tom Walczak in his younger form lying on his bed. His face is dirtied and wet from the tears that he was shedding. His eyes are shut, as the remnants of his weeping still exist. His hair is even more unkempt than it was before, and his shirt is wrinkled. After a couple more minutes of lying on the bed Walczak sits up, and begins to talk to himself. “That Bitch thinks that nothing will become of me. She thinks that I will be nothing more than just some hobo on a street corner. Does she think that what she says matters to me? Does she think that she has any influence in my life whatsoever? She is no more important to me than the shit that comes out of my ass. She is nothing but a mere obstacle in the great game of life. I will show her that I will become something. Before my life is over I will be the greatest thing to come out of this orphanage, and if she is still alive than I will be sure to let her know it.” Walczak begins to stomp up and down on the floor uttering the words, “I will show you…” over and over again. As the kids eat dinner downstairs they here this rampant noise protruding through the ceiling. The nun automatically knows that it is Thomas and she rushes upstairs. Walczak, oblivious to the nun’s presence drawing nearer and nearer, just keeps stomping away even as the nun rushes into the room. “What is the meaning of this Thomas? Do you feel that you need to be even more severely punished than you already are?” “You call this a punishment? This is more of a pleasure than it is a punishment, and the best part is that I don’t have to see your pathetic and disgusting face while I am in here.” “Did you just say what I think you did, or are my ears deceiving me?” “You heard exactly what I said, and you know that I meant it. I mean everything that I say sister, and I also mean that when I become rich and famous your old saggy ass will not get a piece of shit from me.” The nun walks over to Walczak and slaps him in the face without saying a word, but Walczak only moves his head back, but looks straight back into her eyes. She once again slaps him, and once again gets the same result. The nun infuriated with Walczak takes out a yardstick, and tells Walczak to turn around. She proceeds to beat Walczak across the back with the stick, and every time she opens a new gash, but Walczak only flinches, and does not let a sound escape from his mouth. Finally when his back is covered with blood the nun leaves the room although visibly dissatisfied. As soon as she leaves Walczak falls on his bed, but this time does not weep, he just lies down and looks up at the ceiling as the scene fades to black…
