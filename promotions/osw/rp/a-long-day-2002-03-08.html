A long day

8 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-8-02 “You know, I can’t believe all of this crap that people start about you when you make it big.” Jesse says as the dark canvas turns into a gym. “I mean for crying out loud, I’m dating you.” Switching around to Chass, “ Yeah, but you knew this kind of stuff would happen to you in the long run. I mean think about it.” She thinks to her self for a moment. “Wait, why in the world did you run that stupid angle in the first place?” Jesse begins punching the punching bag, “I thought I told you already. I was forced into running the angle. If I hadn’t have done it, I would have been fired.” Jesse stops for a moment. “I had a child to take care of at the time, so, I really didn’t want to lose my job.” Chass’ eyes light up in amazement, “You had a child? What do you mean by that?” “I mean just what I said. I had a child. Her name was Michelle-Marie Ramey. She was around eighteen years old and she was a foster child before she came to stay with me. I had her when she was seventeen, just so that she would get a taste of what it would be like when she got out on her own. She actually wrestled a few matches, but shortly after was going to be sent to the developmental federation. So, she went and is staying with some of her blood relatives now and is going to college. I still keep in touch with her and I send her money every month or so.” Jesse rambles on. Chass begins to think to her self for a moment and then looks up at Jesse, “Do you think that I could maybe meet her one of these days?” Jesse stops punching completely and looks at Chass in a bleak stare, “Are you being serious?” Chass looks up at Jesse from the ground, “Yes, I am very serious!” “Well, I guess maybe one of these days I’ll send her tickets to a show and have her come watch. You two could meet after the show. The only thing I’m afraid of is the fact one of these idiots in the backstage area will say something to make her made or offend her. You know how the guys are in when they are off camera. Completely pricks and idiots.” Jesse says in an anger tone. “Come on, Jess. You know that isn’t true. Take a look at some of those guys. Trevor and Seth? She would love to meet them. She would be nothing but laughs around them. I can hardly keep from laughing my head off when I’m around them. Then you’ve got Naket, who is like your best friend. I’m sure she would love to meet them. All you need to do is keep her away from Ivan Stanislav. I hate that guy.” She acts as if she is gagging. “You’re not the only one. I hate it even more when those damn interviewers ask you things like; Sir, are you sure you want to be making those type of statements about this person? Well, of course I do you dumb asses. If I didn’t want to then I never would have said it to begin with. I don’t see what everyone find so scary about Ivan anyway. What is it, honestly?” Jesse questions. “Well, his height and weight. He makes up two or three of you. Which, you aren’t the smallest person in OSW. So, you have to think about the smaller guys. They should really be shaking in their boots.” Chass states. Jesse thinks to him self for a moment, “What about Doug Troy? He isn’t afraid of them and look at him. He’s smaller than I am and weighs a whole lot less than I do. So, what is it that everyone finds so scary about him? Cause I sure don’t see it in him.” “Speaking of the OSW, you need to be getting back to your trainings. You have a match tomorrow against Vampir Nosferatu and then you have that match against Tom Walczak on Crowning Glory.” Chass says. “Bah, I’m ready to take on them both. I could take them both on in a handicap match if I wanted to. I’m at my physical peak and I’m a freaking monster for crying out loud! Just look at me, Chass!” Jesse begins flexing his muscles. “I am a monster and I can beat anyone in OSW that I want to. I put Matt Blaze out of a job and he knows that. He wanted to get out of that match before he had to step into the ring with me. He failed and he lost not only his pride, but his dignity, to me in the ring!” Jesse slams his fist into a mirror and it burst into a million tiny pieces and falls to the ground. Chass screams, “Jesse, what in the world do you think you are doing? I want you to stop this right now. You could hurt yourself!” “No, Chass. I am a monster!” Jesse grins widely. “I don’t have to worry about anything else in life. I know what I want. I know what I’m going for in life and I know what I’m going for in OSW. I know who I’m going to take out and I know why I’m going to do it. Hell, I think I might just beat the shit out of someone for the sheer fun of it.” “Jesse, we seriously need to talk about this. I don’t like the way you are acting and I don’t like what you are doing to yourself. This isn’t funny.” Tears begin to fill Chass’ eyes. “Come on we can go talk about this over dinner, but let me mend to that hand first.” Jesse looks into the shattered mirror and then looks at the blood as it drips from his hand, “Okay, let’s go get this fixed up.” “Thank you so much, Jesse.” Chass wipes the tears from her eyes and the two walk off. ~Thirty Minute Time Break~ The scene reopens on a little coffee shop, somewhere outside Roanoke, Virginia. Not to far from the gym where Jesse was training a little bit earlier. The two, Jesse and Chass, are seen sitting in a side booth of the shop. Jesse wearing his training shorts and a pull over muscle shirt. While Chass is wearing a work out bra and a part of thigh high shorts. Jesse’s hand is now bandaged up and he is slowly taking drinks out of his cup of soda. “You know, it may sound strange, but what happened earlier. It felt so good, Chass. I don’t know what’s happening to me, but it felt good to let that rage out for once. I mean,” Jesse looks down at his hand, “It did hurt, but it felt good to let out that aggression that I had built up inside of me. You know what I mean?” “Yes, Jesse. I totally understand what you are talking about. You and I have talked about certain things in your past and I know how much you do hold back sometimes, which you really shouldn’t do. It’s not good for you to keep those type of things bottled up inside. It only makes things worse. Trust me, I know from experience as well.” Chass glares at Jesse. “Listen, if this is about what happened before, when I asked you to stay behind and wait for me. I’m sorry. I know it was nearly a year before I came back, but did I or did I not come back for you?” Jesse asks and Chass doesn’t respond, “Of course I did. I love you Chass and I would never do anything to hurt you. Never have and never will.” “Yes, but you see, that did hurt me. It hurt me more than you will ever know or understand. I know it’s not your fault that you don’t understand how I feel sometimes, but I do hurt.” Chass says, “Like those times when Toomes, Bones, and Hostyle were attacking you. You have no idea how much that hurt me deep down inside.” She places her hand over her heart. “Baby, you know that nothing is ever going to happen to me. You saw what happened to those three when they can to the point of actually going to hurt me. Didn’t you? I’ve got people on my side as well. People like Locke and Dame. You have nothing to worry about. I’ve got people watching my back as much as any other person in this federation. Then again, that is also a problem.” Jesse says. “Yes, I know. You’ve got both Brooklyn Born and Bred and The Red Army to worry about this weekend. They could easily interfere in both of your matches on Slam and Crowning Glory.” Chass tells Jesse. “Yes, I know and this is a chance that I have to take. Think about it. A construction worker has things to worry about at his work site. As do I. The only difference is that I’m not going to get hurt as much as he would and I’ve got people who are willing to help me out when the time comes for this kind of stuff.” Jesse says. Chass begins to think to her self, “Well, I’m only going to tell you this once. If things begin to look bad and you have the chance to get out of the ring and make it to the back safely, I want you to do that.” Jesse begins to laugh, “Have I ever been the type to ever run from a fight?” “No, you haven’t. I want you to though. I don’t want to see you get hurt. Please, Jesse, do this for me. Not for you.” Chass pleads. “It’s not in me to go and do something like that though, baby. I’m not the type of person to just up and run from a fight. If someone is looking to pick a fight with me, then that’s what he or she is going to get. I’m “Bad Attitude” Jesse Ramey and I’m not afraid of anyone. Nor do I feel the need to run and hide from anyone because I’ve got the ATTITUDE!” Jesse begins to make a ruckus amongst the coffee shop. “Jesse, calm down. People are beginning to stare at you.” Chass says quietly. “Don’t be like that Chass. These people know who I am and they all love me!” Jesse shouts into the crowd. The bunch of people shouts back and cheer for Jesse. A small group at one table even begins an “Attitude” chant. “See, don’t worry. Everyone loves someone who has the Attitude! Now let’s get out of this place and get back to that hotel room. I’ve got a little surprise for you.” Jesse grins at Chass. The two leave the coffee shop to the cheers of the fans. ~5 Hour Time Break~ Another one? Dang this is getting quite consistent. (OOC: Actually the time is 6:54 and my phones are down, so, I’m just going to continue writing until I can post this thing.) Back at the hotel, the night air is crisp and the sun has just gone down over the horizon. A car pulls into a parking space and has shut off its engine. The people have exited and made it up to their room door. There we see, Jesse and Chass, standing together. Embracing in a kiss. “I don’t really know what this surprise is, but I’m sure it’s going to be good, if it’s what I think it is.” Chass says. “Well, you’ll just have to wait and see. I don’t really want to give away any secrets right off the bat.” Jesse continues as he pulls the key out of his pocket. As Jesse is trying to get the door open, “Well, hurry up. I’m starting to think that there isn’t a surprise.” The door slowly opens and into the pitch-black room walks the two. “May we please turn on the light, Jesse?” Chass asks. “Hang on a second. Come right over here.” Jesse pulls Chass over. “That’s it hit the lights!” Jesse says in an excited tone. The lights suddenly flip on, but who turned them on? Chass stands with a strange look on her face as slowly we turn to see the person who turned on the light. This person is a young looking female who is very pretty, but who is she? “Chass, this is my daughter, Michelle.” Jesse says. Is this the truth, could this be true? Chass stands with her mouth gaped open, still not words can reach her tongue. “Chas, say something. Don’t just sit there like that. It’s not like this should make you made. You did say you wanted to meet her sometime.” Jesse continues. “Well, I did want to meet her, but this has come as such a surprise to me.” Chass finally speaks. “I told it was going to be a surprise. So, surprise!” Jesse says. “Hey, I’m here you know. I didn’t fly in to hear you two fight with each other. Please.” Words come from Michelle’s mouth. Jesse looks over at Michelle, “Oh, I’m sorry. I wanted you to come in, Michelle. I missed you so much.” Jesse runs over and grabs his daughter and gives her a huge hug. “I missed you too, dad.” Michelle says as Jesse lifts her into the air. Chass just watches the as the moment of love continues. “Well, I guess we could make this out to be a good weekend. I just wish you had told me, Jesse. I would have gotten things all planned out for Michelle and we could have had an even better weekend.” Chass sighs. “You really need to inform me of these things, Jesse.” A smile comes across Michelle’s face as she walks over to Chass, “Don’t worry about it. We can still have a good time. Dad got us both tickets to both the shows this weekend. So we can spend time together there and then go into the backstage area and meet some of those hunky guys.” “Michelle! What did I tell you about those guys?” Jesse butts in angrily. “Yes, dad.” Michelle sighs. “I know what you said about everything and I believe everything you have told me.” “Great! Now, let’s all go out for pizza.” Jesse says. “Good idea, sweetie. Let’s do that.” Chass adds. “Sounds good to me. I haven’t had anything to eat in I don’t know how long.” Michelle says. The three walk out the door and as it shuts everything goes dark. The words “More To Come Later” appear and everything goes dark. ~The End~
