Secuirty...Locks? Security...Number? Maybe Security Guards is the trick...

27 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27.06.02 “Axtell,” shouted Krieg, “Who’s he” Sitting in his room by himself, I don’t think he really expected an answer, but it was worth a shot. Looking round to his right, Krieg caught a glimpse of a small bird sitting on his windowsill and looked at it pitifully. As he stood up to look at it more closely, it flapped it’s wings and took off into the morning sky. Watching it go, Krieg’s eyes followed it closely, but he reverted his eyes to something off in the distance, behind the numerous houses and apartments scattered on the horizon when looking out of his Florida-based home... Walmart... Thinking to himself, Krieg’s mind seemed to click suddenly... “Axtell...security guard....security guards....Walmart.....Got it,” he roared, Why don’t I go down there and find out what makes these security guys tick, and maybe I’ll get an insight into why that chump Axtell managed beat Storm... Standing up tall in the air, Krieg raised his right hand in the air, pointing his index finger to the sky, “I’m a genius,” he bellowed, stomping around the flour in delight, “SHUT UP MICHEL,” screams his mother,” “Sorry mother,” replies a scornful Michel Krieg, Pulling a sweatshirt over his massive head, Krieg then pulls on a pair of heavy boots, a light grey bobble hat and heads out of his room, lumbering down the stairs to the front door and exiting the Krieg residence after a brief but detailed explanation of where he was going to his ever-caring mother. The engine of Krieg’s motorbike roars to life, and the Kawasaki 1200 Ninja screams out of The Krieg’s drive and disappears off down the road, him and his powerful motorbike dissolving into the sunset... The scene reopens outside some sort of supermarket or grocery store, Krieg appearing from the right hand side of the screen and grinding to a halt outside the door of the store. He steps off of his cycle and parks it in one of the designated spaces near the door for push bikes and motorcycles before removing his helmet, shoving it into the compartment under the seat and heading into the supermarket. As he steps through the sliding doors, he instantly spots the typical supermarket security guard. You know the ones. The ones who stand at the door and look at you suspiciously even when you try your best to look as innocent and uncorrupted as possible. He eyes Krieg as the massive German takes a few steps further into the store, and then reaches down and grabs his radio, speaking quietly into it. Presuming he’s asking for something from one of his other co-workers, Krieg continues into the store and heads towards what appears to be the staffs section of the store, a huddle of lockers verifying Krieg’s guess. Suddenly, a huddle of security guards come bundling out of a set of double doors and stop in front of Michel. The 5 guards stand, arms crossed, a metre or so, in front of Krieg as the German War Machine looks them up and down, the group of men comprising of the most oddly shaped security guards you’ve ever seen... A short skinny kid. A fat dumpy middle-aged man. A tall lanky 20 year old. A medium sized feminine looking fella. And to cap it off, who looks to be the leader of the team, a bald-headed, tall male, with massive biceps, tiny shoulders, a skinny chest and not enough meat on his legs to feed an Ethiopian. Krieg chuckles to himself at the bunch of ‘rejects’ and remembers the common fact that most security guards think they’re invincible, or think they’re destined for something bigger, such as stewards at rock events or bodyguards for the rich and famous...It was also a common fact that they thought they were bigger, tougher, stronger and generally cooler than anyone else on the planet. This fact couldn’t be more correct... “Hey you...punk,” said the ‘leader’ of the group, his cronies chuckling to themselves behind him, “Who the hell do you think you are,” “Emm...It’s pretty obvious you know who I am, because you called me punk...” Confusing the guard, the right side of Krieg’s mouth grins slightly, “Ugh...Emm...You just shut up, right now,” “Please don’t use any of your menial security guard tactics on me,” chuckles Michel, “I’m not some five year old reading a comic without buying it,” “I think you should just shut you mouth now son,” shouts the short skinny guard, stepping forward and pointing his finger at Krieg, Holding him back with his right arm, the head security guard silences him, “It’s Ok, Bob. This piece of European trash isn’t gonna do anything, ‘cause he’s just some immigrant coming to our country to try and steal our women, jobs and money,” A small Mexican, who was putting some cheese and milk up his jumper just down the aisle looks round as if he’s being addressed before scuttling off towards the main entrance, “I see they train you well in situation defusing here,” suggests Krieg, Another one of the oddly shaped security guards takes a step forward, this time the head guard doesn’t restrain him, “I think it’s time we made a situation here, bhoys,” says the hillbilly guard, ”Let’s get him,” cackles the feminine styled guard, The others look at him with concern, there being a good chance his intentions aren’t quite the same as theirs. With a sneer, he reassures his team-mates of his intended actions, “No, beat him...or something,” With that said, the group looms towards Krieg as the German looks down at them, a look of confused and sympathy struck across his face, ”What are you doing,” Krieg asks as they start to pull at his arms to no avail, “Right,” shouts the lanky 20 year old, “Let’s use the heavy stuff,” Suddenly, the chief guard produces a foam baton from his waist belt and starts to attack Krieg’s joints with it, once again to no avail, “Bhoys, he’s a tough’en,” Shaking his head, Krieg turns round and heads back towards the entrance, the guards continuing the situation neutralising tactics for another 30 seconds before giving up and watching Krieg walk off. As he leaves the supermarket, Krieg climbs back onto his bike and thinks to himself, I can see that security guards like Axtell don’t have much in the way of intelligence. Maybe if I bring a foam baton and some plastic handcuffs to the ring on Saturday, he’ll be in his element... The engine once again roars to life, and Krieg screams out of the car park, the security guards walking to the door as he does, “Bhoys, that’s a code red...A real shopper,”
