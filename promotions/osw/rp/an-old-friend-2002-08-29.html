An old friend

29 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/29/02 We start off where we last left off... in Wal Mart. The Trashcan Man spins, fists clenched in front of him, a wild hate in his eyes that quickly fade into confusion. 'Ed?' Before the Trashcan Man stand the wavering, almost transparent figure of a figure we all know and love... Ed. Trademark cocky grin on his face, Ed leans against a shelf full of Barbies, arms crossed nonchalantly. 'Heylo, Trashy. How's things been?' Ed asks. Trashy's face widens into a huge grin as he lunges forward, arms spread to embrace the other hardcore legend. He flies right through Ed and slams into the shelf with enough force to send it teetering, falling over until it hits the next shelf over, knocking it over in turn with a thunderous crash. Screams of fear and shock are heard as the shelves continue to domino throughout the store. Trashy frowns and rises back to his feet, looking over his shoulder at the laughing figure. 'Trashy, you moron, I'm dead. You can't hug the dead...' A grin spreads over the faces of both men as they complete the sentence at the same time. 'Unless you're vegita.' They chuckle as Trashy dusts himself off, oblivious as always to the carnage he's inadvertantly caused. 'So, what, you're a ghost?' Trashcan Man asks. Ed's grin widens. 'I'm the ghost with 'it', baby!' 'How'd you die, anyways?' Ed's face grows solemn. 'You killed me, Trashy. You and Kriegman.' Trashy hangs his head for a moment. 'Oh yeah, I forgot.' He looks up at Ed. 'I'm sorry about that, Ed. But the master told me that it had to be done. Do you forgive me?' Ed chuckles as he shakes his head. 'Do I forgive you? Trash, you asshole, you friggin' KILLED ME! I know you're messed up in the head, but to expect me to forgive you, well, that's asking a lot.' The Trashcan Man nods. 'I understand, Ed. I don't blame you at all. But it's your own fault. The master said so. You shouldn't have gotten in his way. MY LIFE FOR HIM!' Ed holds up his hands in front of him, waving defensively. 'We won't get into that now, man. I've came back for a more important purpose.' 'Purpose?' exclaims the Trashcan Man. 'What kind of purpose?' 'To show you a few truths, Trash. First of all, you've turned your back on your fans.' 'You've GOT to be kidding me! C'mon, my fans have ALWAYS loved me! SEE!' Trashy points all around at the mass group of security guards around him. We pull back and see Trashy waving his hands and talking to himself, as there's nothing but empty space where Ed stood. 'These guys are just begging me for autographs, Ed.' Trashy turns towards the tense circle of uniformed rent-a-cops all around him. 'Just a few minutes, guys, I'm talking to my dead friend here.' The guards all look at each other nervously as Trashy turns back to Ed, whose transparent form is once again apparent to us. In the background, we hear mutterings of 'Who the hell is he talking to?' and 'He's crazy, be careful...' 'Trash, maybe you don't understand. You're not a good guy anymore.' says the unusually serious ghost of Ed. 'What do you mean? Of course I'm a good guy. I'm always a good guy.' 'You're working for Alan Kriegman and the Geek Tank, Trash.' Trashy looks perplexed. 'Yeah, so?' 'So... they're bad guys.' 'No... no way! The master would NEVER work with bad guys. He's so wise and merciful.' 'So merciful he pulled the plug on me.' 'You put it in your will you wanted to die. We were just following your wishes.' Ed points down at the Trashcan Man's official EVIL RULES! t-shirt. 'You're SHIRT says evil rules, Trash! Don't you get it?!' The Trashcan Man clutches his orange gloved hands to the side of his head. 'NO! THE MASTER IS NOT EVIL! MY LIFE FOR HIM! MY LIFE FOR HIM! MY LIFE FOR HIIIIIIIIIIIIM!!!!!!' Trashy suddenly turns and barrels through the tight circle of security guards, knocking several of them sprawling on their underpaid, undertrained asses and runs out the door, screaming, 'TRASHY'S NOT BAD! YOU'LL SEE! I'LL SHOW YOU! I'LL SHOW YOU ALL! MY LIFE FOR HIM! MY LIFE FOR HIM!!!!' One of the guards picks another up. 'Whaddya make of that?' The other security guard shrugs. 'Nuttier than a jar of extra crunchy Jiff.' We fade to black
