Capon, Kimbusa, what's the difference?

25 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/25/02 Capon (kay-pän): noun A castrated male chicken ---- The sound of a knife slamming against a board greets us. We’re in Silica, Ohio, just outside of Toledo. Sounds of clucking, grunting, and chopping are heard in the background. Meanwhile, standing at a counter of sorts is a man. He’s a rather large (fat) working class man. There’s some stubble on his chine, but aside from that, he’s rather clean shaved. The man is also wearing a white apron with what appears to be blood on the front. “Would you stay still you little cock,” shouts a man from the back. Those words are suddenly followed up by a chop and silence. Some bells start to jingle. A door opens. A fairly small man walks in wearing a black “In Like Flynn” shirt and some dark red, baggy, genie sequin pants. As you can guess by now, it’s OSW wrestler Kid Wonder. “Can I help you, sir?” asks the man from behind the counter. Slowly, Wonder walks up to the counter. “Yeah,” Wonder says as he comes to a stop. “I was told that you’re the only place within 20 miles that has chickens.” “That’s right,” answers the worker. “Good,” says K-Dub with a smirk. He then starts to laugh a little for no apparent reason. “You know,” the man says as he puts on a pair of rubber gloves, “I wouldn’t expect someone like you to ever come in here.” KW looks at the guy. “Oh,” snaps K-Dub with an offended tone of voice. “Just because I’m a celebrity doesn’t mean I can’t come in here and mingle with you plebes.” The man behind the counter seems a bit confused as to what the little guy is going on about. “What?” he questions. “I don’t care if you’re a celebawhatever. You just don’t look like someone who’d come in here. I mean,” the man snickers, “hell, you barely have any meat on you.” Kid Wonder stares at the guy for a moment. He then mockingly laughs at/with him for a few seconds. “Shut up,” Wonder tells the guy as he stops laughing. “What can I do for you?” asks the man behind the counter. “I want a kimbusa,” Wonder tells him. “I’m sorry. A…a kimbusa?” wonders the guy. “Yes,” KW says with a stern voice. “I want a kimbusa.” “I’m sorry,” the man tells the wrestler, “but we don’t have any kimbusas.” “Look,” says Wonder. “you can’t sit here and tell me you don’t have a kimbusa.” The guy just shakes his head. “Sorry,” he tells the young wrestler. “Perhaps you’re thinking of a coot.” “A what?” “A coot,” the man tells Wonder again. “It’s a…” Wonder cuts the man off. “Yeah, yeah,” Wonder says, almost uninterested. “Look, you can’t stand there and tell me you don’t know what a kimbusa is. Not knowing Kimbusa is understandable. But not knowing what a kimbusa is, especially considering what you do, is unacceptable.” “Sorry little man,” the guy tells him, “but I have no clue what Kimbusa, a kimbusa, or whatever else you might’ve said is.” Kid Wonder sighs. He shakes his head. “But,” the man continues, “if you want, you can have this nice, freshly killed duck. The guy behind the counter walks to his left half a dozen steps and grabs a dead duck with its head cut off. At the sight of it, Kid Wonder grimaces. “No…no thanks,” Wonder tells him. The guy behind the counter takes the duck and puts it onto a hook. He then walks back over to his right and back to the position he was at before. “All I came here for was a kimbusa,” Wonder says. “You know, a kimbusa: the biggest chicken of them all.” Just then, the man looks up at the ceiling and starts to laugh a little. “All you want is a chicken?” he asks Wonder while bringing his head back down. Kid Wonder nods his head. “A kimbusa, capon, or whatever your dumb ass wants to call it.' The guy snickers a little more. “Hey Charlie! Gimmie a chook!” shouts the man, turning his head back so as to shout in the other man’s general direction. “Stay still you little cock! Stay still so I can chop your head off!” shouts Charlie in the back. “Do you want that ‘kimbusa’ dead or alive?” asks the man. Kid Wonder sighs. “Fuck it,” Wonder says. “I’ll go somewhere else and get my kimbusa.” Wonder turns around and starts to walk away. The guy at the counter just shrugs. The door opens and the bells jingle. There’s the sound of a chop. “I did it! I chopped that DAMN cock’s head off!” shouts Charlie with delight. At the door, the sound of the knife hitting the chopping block makes him cringe. He lets go of the door ad allows it to close behind him. The bells on the door jingle as we slowly fade away.
