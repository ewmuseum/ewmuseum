Rising Sun

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The sun is rising, slowly peaking over the mountaintops. The morning dew glistening in the new light. The trees dance as they are given life by the warmth. Birds chatter with the beginning of a new day. A new dawn. The dawn of a new time. Kimbusa sits perched on a large tree stump in his own reclusive valley. The location is still a secret, though it seems to be cool there despite the morning sun. Kimbusa's breath forms a vapor upon its explulsion, but is quickly whisped away by the cool morning breeze. He is dressed in his traditional Oriental silk cloak, clad in black from head to toe. His feet are covered as usual by his usual black sandals, his jet black hair tied back with a small stick. He sits in his usual silence, motionless as the sun rises to greet him. He is unstartled as a new presence emerges. A small hand circles Kimbusa, holding a single red rose. The hand places the rose behind Kimbusa's ear, and Kimbusa turns to face his companion. Bethany Yamoto smiles and crouches next to the massive warrior. Bethany has just woken, but seems radiant in the sun. She pulls her hair back behind her ears, as Kimbusa coldly turns back to stare at the rising sun. Bethany frowns modestly. 'Didn't your mother ever tell you not to stare at the sun?' Kimbusa is silent for a long while, before Bethany sighs. This finally evokes a response. 'My mother died when I was very young.' Bethany, not surprised at this response, simply returns to her previous smile and stands, placing her arm around Kimbusa's massive neck. Kimbusa seems uninterested in her at the moment, but Bethany does not seem bothered by his apathy. 'I suppose this would be one of those times when Master Yoshi would have some sort of wise lesson for you.' Kimbusa, his unmoving form finally cracked, turns slightly and faces Bethany. 'He is done. His ways were antiquated. They were the ways of the old.' Bethany, now slightly frustrated, turns and crouches in front of Kimbusa, looking up to him with wide eyes. 'If you hate the old ways so much, then why do you stay here? Why do you seclude yourself like this? Why are you still tucked away in your old religion home in the middle of the woods? This is a beautiful place, but we're not here to be beautiful. What they saw last week was that we could be brutal. That we could unleash that fury. Yoshi tried to keep it inside you but you're free now! Free to do what you please! And Kimbusa, you can do whatever you want. I am free of my father, and I can do whatever I want. All I want is you... to allow yourself to be who you are. Don't run from your past any more. Don't hide your anger. Let it out, Kimbusa. Let it out on Kid Wonder.' Kimbusa's rock solid face seems to grow even more menacing with each word Bethany speaks. Once she finally grows silent, his face becomes bolder, stronger. Kimbusa stands suddenly, and flashes a rare half-grin down at Bethany. She smiles back, but is startled as Kimbusa suddenly throws his head back, and looks to the sky. After a moment, a horrendously agonized and angry yell escapes Kimbusa's lips, as he screams out into the air. Bethany shivers with awe as Kimbusa's rage finally calms, and he turns back towards the house with a quick movement. 'We must leave this place.' Bethany, confused, stands as Kimbusa begins walking quickly back to the house up the trail. 'What about the house? What about this place?' Kimbusa stops for a moment, and studies the house. 'Burn it.' Bethany seems confused, but does not object. As the two begin walking closer to the house, a smile finally emerges on Bethany's face. Kimbusa moves up the dirt trail towards the house, and finally gathers a quick armfull of branches that he snaps from a tree. Bethany follows as Kimbusa kicks open the front door, which promptly falls to the floor from its hinges. Bethany moves to the kitchen and grabs a match, and upon returning finds Kimbusa in the living room. Kimbusa has found himself a knife, and is slashing away at the couch in anger. His eyes red with fury, Kimbusa finally crams the dry branches into the destroyed upholstry, and turns to deliver a massive kick to the mirror on the wall. The mirror splinters but does not fall, leaving Kimbusa to face only his fragmented reflection on the wall. This image leaves Kimbusa silent for a moment, staring at his blistered reflection. After a moment, the sound of a match lighting is heard. Bethany touches a lit match to an entire pack, and clears her throat. Kimbusa turns as Bethany happily hand him the flames. Kimbusa stares at the fire for a moment, as Bethany smiles. 'This is our first step... into the new world.' An evil smile rises on Kimbusa's face, as he tosses the flames onto the sofa. The torn fabric and leaves immediately burst into flames, sending fire up to lap the ceiling. Kimbusa stands for a moment, warmed by the fire. But a slight tug on the belt from Bethany ushers him out the door, and the two make an exit. Together, Bethany and Kimbusa begin walking down the trail. Neither of them speak, neither of them turn back to watch the burning house. Flames begin escaping the windows on both floors, but neither are startled by the fire's progress. They share a sadistic smile, as Bethany Yamoto and Kimbusa leave the valley forever, seeking a place in the New World......
