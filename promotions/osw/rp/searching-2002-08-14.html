Date: 8-14-02 Silver rain falls from the skies; As godless children, dancing and weeping together in tragic harmony. No loss as great as this, To have fallen short, and displeased creation. TO have fallen behind, the eye and mind of God. Letting the heart escape persecution; in darkend whirling decent, to the bottem of consciousness. In black pitch, dancing and twirling, spiraling into the ground. So consumed by this hostility, and the self destructive logic; that has been given so freely until this day. Time runs through it all, untouced and unsettled. Undisputed anger, poisoning the innocent mind; destroying gardens of beauty and delight. Spreading the seed of deciet inside of it all. Building cities of death and ruin; bringing pain and insolance. Our world, our pain, our child; I appologize for this but I do not apologize for the pain I have delt. Though the worlds veins are but a river for the heroine which is pain and hate..And misunderstanding I wish more damage to rain. I have come in to this establishment apart of something dark and unsuspecting but I fear my road will lead me to wanting more. Do not misinterpret, I do not regret my position within this organization. I am merely losing sight of the true reason they chose me to compete by their side. This place I speak of is within the realm of The Cult. Men and women bent on destruction, mutilation, and rage I find that I fit in nicely, but I am but an outcast among outcasts while at their helm. Perhaps my path is a different one to follow, though dark and at the time unseen to my eyes I can feel a presence pulling me away from my present course. I have much to do, much to prepare for, but my thoughts constantly are somewhere else, roaming the celestial plain for my own exsistance. I need answers, I need to know... I need..Truth in a world of Lies
