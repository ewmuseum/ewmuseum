Date: July 29th, 2002 ((Note: I'm bored and depressed for some reason, so remembering is kinda fun.)) ''Get the fuck back here!'' It was all a dream, a horrible nightmare... Memories of the past, that much she could be thankful for...but it felt like it was happening all over again. ''Get away!'' She heard herself screaming over and over again, like a distressed chant. ''Leave me alone!'' He grabbed her by the arm all over again, shaking her violently and with another swift slap, she felt the metallic flavor of blood washing over her tongue in a burning wave. The teenager screamed at the man again, kneeing him in the stomach to get him to relinquish his grip before she ran behind the table on the other side of the dingy little basement appartment. ''GO!'' ''Why you gotta be such a little bitch?'' Amy knew she was asleep, but his piercing green eyes still stung like it was cruel reality so long ago. ''Everyone said you were a slut, snobby little cunt.'' She'd tossed a practically rotten chair in his direction, praying something would knock his six-foot-something frame to the ground so she could make her escape. ''Fuck you, Adrian! Let me go home...I want to go home...'' ''Well I wanted a little ass,'' He'd spit at her. ''But we don't all get what we want, do we? Ya don't know what's good for you...you shoulda just shut the fuck up and done your work!'' ''Work?! God...I HATE YOU! Just let me go home!'' Silvery laughter filled the room. ''If you go home now, you'll just tell daddy dearest...he'll get his nice tight-ass little lawyers all over me!'' ''Christ! I could've told EVERYBODY when you gave me that concussion last month...'' She'd been cowering behind the table, but her knees were weakening. ''But no, I 'fell off the porch'. Fuck you, you know I'd have plenty to tell the cops and proof to back it but I DON'T.'' '''Cause you know I'd rat your pretty little ass out for the shit you do, huh? Ha! Dumb little whore.'' ''Just let me out! I won't tell anyone, you know...just fucking let me GO!'' ''You want out? Here ya go...'' That was the last thing she heard before the table was flipped onto her. Sixteen years old, a literal junkie and girlfriend, better referred to as victim, of a 22 year old man, Amy Campbell was at the lowest point in her life... The scary part was that, when the memories came back to her, she saw Adriano Paolucci as Isiic Raiyn, Corg himself. Like then, she took his abuse and, like then, she knew she was helpless... She could do absolutely NOTHING about it. At best the memory of the abuser was just that, a memory re-introduced through a nightmare. There weren't any memories of Corg, however... That nightmare was one she presently lived.
