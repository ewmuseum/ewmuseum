Mexicano Monster [Part 1]

5 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7.5.02 The scene fades in from Krieg’s passenger seat, a view similar to these cheesy “Follow The Cop”-type shows you see all the time on the TV. Anyways, Krieg has both hands on the wheel and is battering his paws on it, pretending they are some form of drum sticks, “...And all it was was something beautiful....When tides and dreams don’t seem so tall, at alllll....,” roared Krieg, pounding his hands on the leather coated wheel, American Head Charge’s “The War Of Art” album accompanying him on the CD player, As he drives through some sort of urban town, he passes various shops: a grocery store, a off licence, a drug store, a Lucha Libra gym, a jewe...Wait a minute. A Lucha Libra gym? In sub-urban America? This can’t be true, can it?, “Dah, what is das,” asked Krieg, slowing down and stopping about 10 metres or so past the gym, Lumbering out of his jeep, Krieg slammed the door shut and walked towards the gym, a look of confusion, similar to the one you’re probably got on your face right now, plastered across his face. You must be thinking, “Why the hell is there a Lucha Libra gym in this part of America?”, whereas Krieg is thinking, “Where can I get a frankfurter at this time of the day?”...No, but really, he’s thinking the same as you...duh, “Vat is das,” said Krieg to a local man walking past the gym, “That’s the Mexican wrestling place, son,” replied the old-timer, “Many a wetback has jumped the fence to come here,” “So, do people like, for example, El Drago, come here to train,” questioned Krieg, trying to get a bit more background information on the gym, “I wouldn’t know. I tend to stay as far away from these damn Mexican’s as I can...You see, when I was,” As the man starts to rant, Krieg just pushes him to the sides and whips open the door to the gym. Hoards of men stand around in a circle, a tutor standing in the middle. As they catch a glance of Krieg, after looking up at the ceiling, the men, who are mostly around about the 5’5”-5’8” mark, stand facing him, their arms crossed on their chests, “We know who you are, German monster,” said the tutor, stepping through the crowd of men towards Krieg, “Yes, and who are you, little man,” asked Krieg, “I am El Sorro,” replied the proud Mexican, standing to his full height and pushing his chest out,” “Dwahhhahahh! I have heard of you, Mr. Sorry,” “It is Sorro, you European fool,” “Whatever the case may be, Sorry, I want you to give information for Krieg and Red Army. Where can Krieg find more Lucha-Libra wrestling,” “Well, if you go south of the border, you will find Mexico, the home of Lucha-Libra wrestling,” claimed El Sorro, his students still standing closely behind him,” “That sounds like a good place to start, Comrade,” “I am no Comrade of a German Neanderthal like you, my friend,” shouted Sorro as he started to walk back through the crowd of men, the rest of the Mexican’s all laughing at Krieg, pointing fingers in his direction, “You will pay for insult, Mexican...after I have some lunch. I’m starved,” With that, Krieg walked out of the gym, holding his stomach as it rumbled loudly, and walked down the street, looking for a place to eat. Suddenly, he stumbles across a small Mexican eatery, named “Bello Taco”.... .....TO BE CONTINUED....
