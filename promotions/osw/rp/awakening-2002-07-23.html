Awakening

23 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-23-02 It had been exhilarating. It had been refreshing. And it had been amazing. The energy that coursed through Ivan Stanislav’s veins when he fought Kory Storm and soundly defeated him was something that he had not felt in a long time. He felt young again, much as he did when he fought Switchblade and Jane Reagan months ago. How could it have been that somewhere in his life he had grown null….devoid of such a feeling? For Ivan Stanislav, he remembered why he was doing what he is doing. He loved the fight, to stand up against another combatant and thoroughly defeat them. And then, it happened…. News of Ed’s death hit Ivan Stanislav hard. For most people one would not expect Stanislav to care much of Ed, after all they had fought each other several times and it was Ivan Stanislav that had sent him to the hospital several times. But death? Death is something that the Russian Bear knows all too much about. He’d seen his friends be killed in front of his eyes in Afghanistan. He had seen people being tortured and killed. He had seen the horrors and the permanence of death. Somewhere between Saturday Slam, and Tuesday morning, Ivan Stanislav had an awakening of some sort. Between that brief period of time, he realized what he had to do. The Think Tank was back. Trashcan Man, Alan Kriegman, Howard Porter, and that little twerp PHD were running things, as well as using Armour and that freak John Mammoth as their muscle. Ivan Stanislav is a man that knows no fear. Size means nothing to Ivan Stanislav. Six against one?? Those odds are just perfect for The Russian Bear. Alan Kriegman pulled the plug on Ed, a man who had lived so much in his short life, yet had much more that he could have experienced had it not been for Kriegman’s cowardice. He was overjoyed once again when word hit him that he would fight Kriegman and his lapdog Trashcan Man on Saturday Slam. Again he felt the energy through his body as he thought of bringing them to their knees in the name of not Communism, but of Justice. To repay the people of whom he had terrorized for so long. Ed would get his revenge on Saturday Slam in the form of Ivan Stanislav. And by accepting Stanislav’s match, the Think Tank had just bitten off far more than they could ever chew.
