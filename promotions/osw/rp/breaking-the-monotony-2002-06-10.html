Breaking the Monotony

10 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-10-02 Rolling down Interstate 5 en route to Portland, Oregon in their Chevrolet Celebrity, Trevor Hawke and Seth Masters are wearing their usual goofy grins. No...upon closer examination, the grins aren’t quite as goofy as would be expected. Actually, they’re not very goofy at all. Far from it, in fact. If one had to come up with an adjective to describe them, they would end up being labeled as...superior. At least, that’s the feeling they seem to emit. One of them, at least. There’s a shitload of other emotions that can be read...if you were into reading emotions, that is. This isn’t a sociology course, however. It’s just an image of the team of Civil Disobedience riding on the highway to the next show because the OSW front office was too cheap to spring for plane tickets. Despite the frugality of the situation, Seth and Trevor don’t look too miffed about their current lot. As stated before, they ARE grinning, after all. What reason might they have to be happy? Well, it might have to do with the events that transpired on the last Slam. Then again, it might have something to do with the cigarette Trevor is holding in his hand. You know, the kind of cigarette whose smoke gives you a contact buzz. Wink wink, nudge nudge. Yeah...you know what I’m talking about. The most likely answer, though, is the most obvious one. That is, it’s a combination of both. As if it matters why they’re happy... It doesn’t change the fact that they’re in a fucking good mood, now does it? “Seth...” Trevor asks, reaching down to the side of his passenger seat and pulling a lever that allows him to recline backwards. With the creaky sound that signifies the cheap nature of a vehicle, Trevor leans back, taking a deep inhalation of the joint he holds in his hand. “Is it wrong for me to feel really, really good about the fact that a lot of people are pissed off at us, right now.” Seth glances over at his partner, a small grin crossing his face before he returns his eyes back to the road. “Yeah, I’m pretty sure that’s wrong,” he answers with a nod. “Good,” Trevor states with a smile, closing his eyes as he takes another hit from the joint in his hand. “That’s what I was hoping for.” “Feeling a little self-destructive today, are we?” “A little...” Trevor says mysteriously, exhaling another cloud of smoke as he waves the joint in the air. “I look at it this way. When one has reached the pinnacle of perfection such as we have, self-improvement is impossible. Destruction is all one can hope for to break the monotony.” “Monotony?” Seth states in confusion. “What monotony?” “Well, for one, I can only make so many gay jokes before I lose what sense of humor I had before coming into this place,” Trevor states, a touch of disgust creeping into his voice. “When insinuating that one man unwillingly sucks another man’s cock ceases to become funny, you know that something is wrong. That’s the monotony I’m talking about.” “Oh,” Seth nods, not taking his eyes off the road. “That monotony.” After thinking for a second or two, Trevor rolls his eyes, then moves on to continue his rant. “And when you couple that with the fact that we’ve been stuck in this fucking dipshit role for the past five months, suppressing any negativity we might feel in order to put on a good front for those dicksmack fans, I’d say that’s the damn definition of monotony.” Once again, Seth nods, not looking as if he has any dispute with what Trevor has to say. “Well when you put it that way, I’m hard pressed to argue with you.” “You were actually going to argue with my point?” Trevor asks in borderline disbelief. “No, but these fucking road trips are so boring I have to do something to pass the time.” Seth says as he encompasses the rolling meadows with a flourish of his hand. “This lousy scenery gets old after about ten minutes.” “Here, why don’t you hit this shit?” Trevor asks, his eyebrows lifting in the air as he holds the cigarette out to his partner in an offertory gesture. Seth waves it away, shaking his head in the negative. “I’d like to, but I doubt those front office chumps would really want us toking up.” “Well I want a hand job from Shanda Russell and you don’t see me getting my way,” Trevor says sarcastically, still pushing the joint toward Seth. “Damn, another good point,” Seth admits grudgingly, taking the joint from his partner and lifting it up to his lips. “You’re on fire today.” “That would explain why I’m so fried,” Trevor says with a laugh, before raising a hand to his head. “Whoa...” “What’s the matter?” Seth asks after taking a puff. Trevor smiles back at him. “I just forgot how good it felt to...feel so good!” “I don’t know man. This stuff isn’t that great? I’ve had way better...” Seth states, eyeing the joint in his hand askance. “I’m not talking about the weed, you dumbass,” Trevor shouts, smacking his partner in the back of the head, pointing at himself. “I’m talking about my recently inflated sense of worth. I feel like for the first time since joining the OSW, I’ve actually made a career choice that will pay off big time in the long run. This is going to be huge, man. Joining with Seabass was the best thing we could have done.” “Yeah?” “Yeah,” Trevor says, raising his eyebrow. He makes sure to emphasize his words with hand gestures as he speaks. “You know, it’s going to be nice showing up at the arena next week and not having to put on some fake-ass smile. And it’s going to be great to go out there and know I’m actually doing something instead of just standing still in a frickin rut I’ve created for myself.” “Yeah?” “This Saturday night will be the first step toward our redemption, Seth.” Trevor remarks, resolve filling his voice. “I’m going to step in the ring with Hessian and Riptah...those ignorant fucks...and just like Andrew Ace Hawk last week, I’m going to lay them out flat on their backs in the middle of the ring.” “I’m sure they’re used to being laid out flat on their backs, seeing that they each have nasty ho-bag managers on top of them all of the time,” Seth laughs. “Hessian has a ho-bag manager...?” Trevor’s voice fades out as realization strikes him. “Oh, Minion! Yeah...” “Trev, I’m actually looking forward to going into work Saturday night. This whole turn around we’ve pulled...it’s like we’re finally going to get what we deserve.” “Damn right we’re going to get what we deserve. It’s going to be a friggin’ revolution, Seth,” Trevor says, sounding excited, almost like a teenager anticipating his first fuck. “Us, and Seabass, and Amy...we’re the best the industry has to offer. It’s about time we decided to claim our rightful places at the top. We’re going to make waves. Real waves. No more of that small time Carrot Top bullshit. From now on, it’s going to be everything we’ve ever deserved and more.” “Power,” Seth says, his eyes shining with anticipation. “Glory,” Trevor answers back, laughing eagerly. “Fame.” “Fortune.” “It’s the fucking American dream, man,” Seth says matter-of-factly. “And a fucking nightmare for the rest of the OSW roster,” Trevor says in a mirrored tone. The two men look at each other, shit-eating grins appearing on each of their faces. “Heh heh heh. You know, Trev,” Seth says, looking over at his partner. “I’ve changed my mind.” “About what?” “This weed isn’t so bad, after all,” Seth admits, taking a final puff of the joint before flicking the depleted butt out the window. “Dumbass...”
