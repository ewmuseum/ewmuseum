'Tainted' part 1 of ???

4 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Feb. 4, 2002 'To disarm the people (is) the best and most effectual way to enslave them...' - George Mason Lennox Lanthrop is all alone. His eyes slowly open after a long-needed nap. The sudden piercing sensation comes as a surprise, as his temples throb because of the radiant white light. It takes several moments for his eyeballs to adjust. He rises in bed and wipes his eyes, as he disorientedly searches the room for anything familiar. Where Am I? He looks across the room in a blank stare, the room is boring; generic and bland. This HAS to be a hospital. How Did I get here? Nurse...I'll call the nurse. Lennox searches hastily for the remote. He finds it buried underneath a blanket. He eagerly presses it several times, just as a nurse rushes in, breathing heavily... 'Wh- What is it sir?' The nurse pants. Lennox lets out a muffled chuckle. 'Hey, can you go get the doc? I need to talk to him...its important.' Lennox asks. Look at her go..she's pretty good lookin.... The nurse sees Lennoxes advancing look and embarrased, grins and exits the room. About a minute passes and a chinese doctor enters the room, a look of surprise molds on Lanthrops face... Ahh God...another chinese dude...like I haven't had ENOUGH trouble with that race lately.... Lennox stops and observes his racist comment, as his face turns stern and he looks down...visibly ashamed. 'You called, Mr. Lanthrop?' the cheery doctor inquires. 'Yea...uh..why am I here?' Lennox replies with a question. 'I mean, my schnozz just started bleeding and I couldn't tell why..no one struck me or anything at SLAM.' Lennox confidently adds. 'Yes, well, I was about to come in and talk to you about it, Lennox.' the mans voice softly states. Lennox looks up and sees the doctors emotion changing... Wow, I wonder what's up. 'I'm going to ask you a simple question, I expect an honest answer; can you give me one?' the doctor asks... Oh sh*t, What now. Lennox nods, no verbal answer. 'Lennox, have you been taking drugs in the last 48 hours? Prior to Slam?' WHAT?!?!? 'No, I did not...' Lennox sarcastically hisses. He calms down...the doctor is just trying to perscribe the problem..'I mean, No. Not to my knowledge.' Lennox looks up and sees the doctor nod...'Wh- Why, you think someone drugged my drink?' 'It's quite possible, actually. The pill could have dissolved in your drink, what were you drinking?' 'Oh -- just some ginger ale.' 'Yes, well, we found the drug in your blood stream..its a strong one. Your body fought it quite well...the carbonated beverage reduced the potency...also. It was supposed to knock you out, but you had the common side effect of bloody nose.' Lennoxes blood boils in his veins as he starts breathing heavily. Its then he rises to his feet...just as faint words echo through his head.... 'Great Dragon....Great Dragon....Tainted Win....Tainted Win......Get rid of HIM.....Get rid of HIM'.... 'Wh-What is it?' the chinese man frantically asks to the angry Lanthrop... 'I--I gotta go,' an unusual reply. Lennox charges out of the room, but the doctor shouts at Lennox. Lennox turns... 'Mr. Lanthrop..I know that hospital garb looks good and all, but it might be nice if you changed into your normal clothes.' The chinese man blurts out before laughing out loud. Lennox innocently smirks, looking down at his hospital gown. He hurries back into the room, and he snatches his clothes from a chair.... 'I'll change on my way out.' Lennox softly yet sternly states as he leaves the room. Fade.
