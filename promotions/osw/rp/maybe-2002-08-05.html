Maybe...

5 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-5-02 It was quiet in Ivan Stanislav’s office. It had been quiet for a very long time. He sat in his makeshift workplace somewhere in the United States, but its location at the moment was still unknown. Saturday Slam had taken a lot out of The Russian Bear. The irony of it all was that he did not have a match, but the mental anguish was quite real. His interview, in which Tempest was present throughout the entire ordeal, was not what he had expected, or was prepared for. The room was dark, save for one small lamp on his desk. The desk, a hulking piece of oak furniture, had one small folder sitting atop it. The light cast strange shadows against the chiseled face of The Russian Bear, but his face was devoid of much emotion. He knew that he had made her cry whenever he confronted her on Slam. He knew the second he stormed out of the room that she was going to cry. Half of the time, he did not know why he reacted the way he did every time she came near him. His entire being yearned for her yet when she was near he must push her away. Ivan closed his eyes slowly and breathed in deep, causing his broad shoulders to rise, and then exhaled slowly and opened his eyes again. Maybe it was her way of approaching the situation. Perhaps that was why he reacted the way he did. It was always with a smile, with a somewhat cavalier attitude that just angered him. How could one be so lighthearted and frivolous about something so serious? He shook his head slowly. No, that was not the reason. He knew why she did that. He knew why she acted in such a carefree way. She had told him before whenever they were on better terms. It was her way of fighting her fears. It was her way of handling a difficult situation. It showed that she was concerned. It showed that she was willing to work. Ivan cocked his head to the side slowly and bit his lip. Maybe it was the stress of so much going on so quickly. Russia was not making it easy on him. The fans were expecting an awful lot from him, going up against the Think Tank with allies that he really was unsure of. The political structure of the OSW was also changing, that could all attribute to his somewhat shortness with her. Again, he shook his head and furrowed his brow. That was not it either, and he knew it. The Think Tank was unable to stop him when they tried, and he had allies that he knew would fight to the last moment. As for the political structure within the OSW, he did not care if the President believed he had the power or not, it would not stop Ivan Stanislav. And the fans would appreciate him regardless and Russia… Russia was a world away. He brought his large arm up and laid it upon the desk, absently picking at a fingernail. Maybe he should change his mind. The easy alternative would be to forget what had happened, run up to her the next time and take her in his arms. The easy alternative would be to forget the past and make a better future. The easy alternative would be to do what everyone wanted him to do, even himself. The last time he neared himself to her she was sent to the hospital. The last time that he had closed the proximity she was huddled over his body after a fire extinguisher had ruptured around his skull. The last time that he had touched her… she was running away with tears streaming her face. He closed his eyes again very slowly. His lip quivered and a low, painful growl came from deep within his being. What if he hurt her again? What if he caused her more pain? He knew he was doing such a thing now… but it wasn’t a concussion or a coma or a beat down. The last time that he tried to save her from harm… whenever she laid in a hospital bed and had forgotten… the last time he tried to shield her he merely traumatized her more. If he got close to her again, would those that wished to hurt him try to hurt her once again too? His eyes remained closed, his head jerked to the side just slightly. Maybe he should just forget it. Perhaps it will right itself in the end. No, things such as this don’t go away. Things such as this do not disappear with the time. He knew that. Three months had gone by and still he felt feelings for her. The Russian Bear opened his eyes and stared at the folder in front of him. Enclosed was the card for Saturday Slam, but he had not read it yet. Maybe he should just try to think of something else, anything else. That might calm his nerves and bring him peace. Maybe just looking at the card will ease him. He reached and opened the folder where a computer printout of the card sat and brought it up into the light. His eyes scanned the sheet of paper and read what was on the menu for Saturday Slam. Ivan Stanislav and El Drago vs. John Mammoth and PHD Ivan almost chuckled when he saw his opponents. Dorkiman was a fool, he knew it, and surely he was not a fighter or a wrestler. And John Mammoth… Mammoth, a man who could not even stand up to a 146 pound weakling. He might be bigger than even the great Ivan Stanislav, but he had already proven that he was far inferior in both mind and body. Stanislav actually smiled as he read the card, it appeared that something was going to keep him occupied after all. He read up the card slightly and another sentence caught his eye:                                                             OSW Cruiserweight Title Match                                                                         Tempest vs. Corg © He leaned forward, his chair creaked loudly beneath his weight and read aloud. “Tempest's return to the OSW squared circle sees her fighting for gold against the Silent Assassin, and enemy of women everywhere, Corg. Questions abound around this match, like is Tempest interested in Corg's gold, or is she only out to take his filthy hide? Also, what role, if any will Tempest's jilted ex-boyfriend, Ivan Stanislav play in this matchup?” He cleared his throat, his facial features weakened, and his smile disappeared. Ivan’s eyes darted around the page in uncertainty. Tempest vs. Corg?? A man that had done such vile things to females in the past. Ivan had visited Aimz herself in the hospital and had seen how traumatized she was! His breathing quickened as he became excited. Tempest vs. Corg?? She was a big girl, he knew that. He would never have paid any attention to her if he had not noticed the strength and the fight within Tempest. She could take care of herself. She could deal with it on her own…. Ivan paused in mid thought and bit his lip harder. Maybe Corg’ll try something. Maybe he’ll try to take advantage of her. Hurt her. Ivan's eyes widened as he sat up straight and nodded. Never. Never would he let someone such as Corg hurt her. She was too precious too… innocent. He grabbed his phone and dialed a number, “Alexei? Da, it is Ivan. Get me information on Corg. Yes, Corg. NOW!” He slammed the receiver down and nodded. Ivan leaned back in his chair and crossed his arms. Maybe the cause of it all was Ivan Stanislav. Maybe things wouldn’t ever be the same between himself and Tempest again. Maybe the Think Tank had a trap planned for him on Slam…. …. but never would anyone ever hurt Tempest again. On Saturday Slam, he would make sure of that.
