Two guys, no girls and a vending machine...

7 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/7/02 “There's blood in the streets it's up to my ankles blood in the streets it's up to my knee Blood in the streets, the town of Chicago Blood on the rise, it's following me Just about the break of day Blood on the streets runs a river of sadness Blood in the streets, it's up to my thigh The river runs down the legs of the city The women are crying red rivers of weeping” - Jim Morrison Ain’t that a nice little intro? But I wouldn’t just pick some quote out of no where, would I? Would I just put “Cannon to the left of them, Cannon to the right of them, Canno in front of them, volley’d and thunder’d” just for no reason? Of course not, if I put something, it will have meaning! There must be some significance, even if how small it may be. This is an excerpt from Peace Frog by The Doors, which is also the entrance music of Ritchie Syxx, you know, that jobber that I always talk about? Ritchie has been really MIA since his loss to Corg last Saturday, partly because he was sad that he lost and partly because his handler had a lot of school work this week. But who am I to make excuses? I am just the narrator, sent here from a far away planet to narrate, in 3rd person limited, the lives and tales of Ritchie Syxx. We see a pepsi vending machine. A blue pepsi can is featured on the machine. Many different buttons can be seen on the right of the machine, all Pepsico products of course. Ritchie Syxx can be seen trying to put a dollar in there. I say tried because as he puts the dollar in the machine, it comes out again. It is very crinkled and crimpled as you can infer. Ritchie swears to himself everytime the dollar comes back out over and over. Ritchie wears a pair of dingy jeans and has a black nautica coat on over that, zippered all the way up. Although Ritchie seems to be trying very hard to get the can of pepsi out of the machine, he neglects to notice the Coke machine right next to it, where soda costs 80 cents. He keeps trying and trying, until a man walks past. He has a pair of tight black jeans and a white shirt with horizontal black stripes. He wears a blakc beret to complement his gay European Mustache. This man is none other than - Pierre Gaston - one of OSW’s referees. He is a snotty french man, if you didn’t notice already. “Yo dude, do you have like a crisp dollar bill?” “Moi? I only carry the Franch Franc,” respondes Pierre, who’s french accent is still very noticable. “Do you know if Soda machines take Francs?” “In France they do. I am not sure about L’America.” “Can you just lend me a franc then?” “NON!” “Damn Frenchy, always being so...so...so French!” “Shut up you stupid Americaine!” “I.....I am not a American dude. I’m British, can’t ya tell by the accent?” “You all sound the same to me, stupid Amercaines!” “Don’t mess with America anymore! I’m not even American! I’ll kick your ass!” “Do you know who I am!?” Pierre starts to get very annoyed with Ritchie, and vice versa. “I am Pierre Gaston! I am the referre of the how you say? Ahh yes...OSW!” “Well......I’m in OSW!” “Non, if you were in OSW, I’d already know who you are.” “I’m Ritchie Syxx! Who the hell are you? Un Boucher Russe!?” Ritchie recently bought an english to french/ french to english dictionary. The first thing he looked up was the translation for a russian butcher. After that, he looked up radiator, I don’t exactly know why. Pierre starts to feel stupid and replies with, “I know you were in the OSW, I am not stupid! Tu es stupide! Tu es TRES stupide!” If you don’t know french, there’s a double insult there, he’s calling him stupid, in the feminine form. “Yeah, well.....you smell!” Pierre proceeds to smell his pits, with his big ugly long french nose sticking into pit. “It is my naturale juices!” “Pee is also a natural juice, but do you wanna drink some for breakfast?” Pierre gives Ritchie the evil eye.“I hope I call your match with Corg, stupid Ritchie, you will job worse than Tim Walden on a cold day!” “Go ahead, I am so good that even if you try to make Corg win, I’ll win. I am that good!” “Shut up, before I kick your ass myself!” “If I wasn’t allowed to beat you up in a promo, I would beat you up!” “I don’t want to put up with your nonsense anymore, I am going.” Pierre leaves muttering words like Stupid Brit and get a haircut. Syxx goes back to the soda machines and tries to put his dollar in some more, all in vain. He finally gets the dollar to go in and stay, he presses the pepsi button. It says “sold out” then he proceeds to look at other options, which all say “Sold Out”. He presses the change button, and it says “Out of change” He lost his dollar, what an ironic ending. Wait, that isn’t ironic at all......nevermind.
