Charity, Schmarity...[Filmed Yesterday]

27 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27.03.02 4.28pm, 27th March 2002, Hertha Berlin Stadium, Berlin, Germany Balloons. They are so annoying. A bit like Jesse Ramey, but hey, everyone knows that. But seriously, they really annoy the hell out of me. You touch them, they squeak. You let them go, they blow away. There’s no two ways around it: they’re just plain annoying. And the abundance of yellow, red, blue, green balloons didn’t help as I walked out from the tunnel. The Astroturf covering the trackside area was slightly slippery under my boots, the rain from the previous night not helping the situation a hell of a lot. But when I caught a glance of the cameras, the mass of decorations, the conditions of the stadium didn’t really matter all that much. The thing that really got me was the kids. The small, innocent kids, who were here to learn how to keep fit and to make something of themselves. Just like I had done all those years ago. I mean, giving something back to Berlin, to Germany, was the least I could do for the country that had raised The German War Machine. Cheers erupting didn’t do much to calm my nerves: more like put them on edge. But you’ve got to do what you’ve got to do. And I had to do this. Mother and Father sitting in the crowd, I couldn’t exactly turn back now. And what kind of man would I be if I chickened out of a social event like this? The Krieg name would be dirt around Berlin. Stepping up on the podium, Krieg was met with a mass of cheers from the crowd, his shyness not showing as much on the outside as it did in his eyes. But he kept his composure and forced a smile for the cameras, just like he always did when he was at these kind of events. The microphone stood on its own, looking like an abandoned remnant of times gone by. His voiced echoed around the arena as he spoke harshly into the microphone. “We are all here today to help the youth of Germany, the youth of Berlin, to become the young adults they are destined to be. The reason I am here is to show these youngsters what they can be if they unleash the courage, determination and potential they have inside them,” claimed Krieg, a little bit of Krieg’s mystery being unravelled by the minute. Nervously shifting back and forth on the stage, Krieg looked like a different man from the confident, forward man he usually is, “And with the help of the German government, hopefully not a Capitalist one,” said Krieg with a slight snigger, met with a couple of cheers from the crowd, “I am going to help the youth of Germany make something of themselves and not to held back because of lacking facilities.” A short title appears on the screen along with the time: 4.55pm. After his brief speech, Krieg was bombarded with questions from the paparazzi while sitting in the press conference area. “What are your thought on the government’s plans to reduce funding for sporting facilities and increase the budget in the education and transport sectors,” questioned one reporter. “Well, it just goes to show that the stereotypical image of politicians being fat cats is completely true. I mean, if they can’t even be bothered helping youngsters stay fit and healthy, what’s the point in putting more money into transport and education if kids can’t even catch a bus to school,” responds a pumped up Krieg, his enthusiasm for fitness quite prominent as he speaks. After answering similar questions Krieg takes a brief drink of his water as another reporter asks Krieg a question, although it’s not really connected to the theme of the day, “Michel, what do you think you and Tom Walczak’s chances are against the established tag team of 3B this week on SLAM!,” “Well, 3B are established, and everyone who’s ever watched them in action will know that they work well together and can finish a match of in a seconds notice...But come on. They’re up against 2 of the biggest men ever to grace the OSW...to grace Wrestling...to grace America even! Those 2 street punks from Harlem, Hollywood, Brooklyn, where ever the slum is they come from, better watch their mouths around The Red Army because we’re not like all the other American pussies who walk around backstage, we’re not going to take any more of their crap. As far as 3B are concerned, they are just another 2 jobbers myself and Walczak have to get through the prove we are superior,” roared Krieg, fists clenched as they slammed down on the table. Slightly shocked by Krieg’s reaction, the reporters give him a minute to calm down before asking him another question. “Michel, what do you think about The Red Army’s involvement this past week on SLAM! during the main event, helping out Timo Bolamba,” “Over the past few days, I’ve been asked the very same question, so I’m going to give you the very same answer: we didn’t help out Timo because we like him. We went down to the ring on SLAM! to take out 3B, to show Sebastian Toomes and his cronies just who they were messing with. There wasn’t really much to do with Timo Bolamba or Amy Campbell, except the fact they were in the ring and so were we...No if there isn’t anything else you crows would like to gouge out of me, then I’ll be on my way...” Krieg slowly stands up and gets out of his seat, walking away from the press area, the press reporters slightly pleased at having pissed off Krieg, which seems to be the main objective of most reporters these days....
