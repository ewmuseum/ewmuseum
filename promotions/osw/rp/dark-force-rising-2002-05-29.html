Dark Force Rising

29 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-29-02 Our scene opens to reveal Trevor Hawke sitting in front of the television set in Civil Disobedience’s degenerate suburban estate. His eyes glued to the screen in front of him, Trevor appears to be completely mesmerized by the visuals unfolding in front of him. Judging by the scene unfolding before him, where one Luke Skywalker is watching the Battle of Endor unfold before him on the bridge of the second Death Star, it looks as if there’s no question that Trevor is watching Star Wars: Return of the Jedi...as it unfolds before him. The VHS cassettes of A New Hope and The Empire Strikes Back are laying on the floor next to the television set, leading us to believe that Hawke is wrapping up what looks to be an original Star Wars movie marathon. Ah, Jedi was such a good movie. It was the best one in the series, in fact. A lot of people will tell you that Empire was the best, but they’re all wrong. It was Jedi. And you know I know what I’m talking about, because I refer to the movies as Empire and Jedi. That’s the mark of a true fan. Getting back to the nonexistent plotline, Trevor hasn’t moved an inch from his seat on the floor. I guess we should take it that the tag team title match this Saturday on Slam is the furtherest thing from his mind. Indeed, he appears to be entranced by the eighties special effects, as he should be. So absorbed is he that he doesn’t even notice an ominous shadow approach him from behind. Growing in size, a black silhouette draped over the wall looms over Trevor’s unknowing head. The sound of breathing through a respirator, eerily similar to one Darth Vader, can be heard softly echoing from the direction of the advancing figure. The Vader-esque breathing grows in volume until finally Trevor whirls around to face the oncoming figure, nearly jumping out of his seat at the sight of him. “Fuck, Seth!” Trevor exclaims, taken aback by his partner’s sudden appearance. “You scared the shit out of me.” “Sorry,” apologizes Masters, waving a small device in the air. “I have this inhaler thingy because the doctors say I may have a bit of the black lung.” “You have silicosis?” Trevor asks, disbelievingly. “Since when the fuck have you ever been in the mines?” “Alright, maybe its just a minor phlegm buildup, but The Black Lung just sounds so much more...sinister, don’t you think?” Seth says, before breaking into a creepy fit of laughter. “Muhahahaha!” “Eh, I guess so. It’s not really that funny, though,” Trevor remarks casually, stroking his chin as he eyes his partner askance. “Say...something about you looks different.” Looking Seth up and down, Trevor suddenly jumps back again, this time in shock as he finally realizes just how fucked up Masters really looks. As far as fucked up looks go, this one definitely takes the cake. Clad all in black, Seth has undergone some sort of demonic transformation. His face is painted with all sorts of cosmetics, his eyes outlined darkly by eyeliner (that’s what it’s for, isn’t it?) and black lipstick sticking all over his lips (again what it’s for, right?). His shirt seems to be made out of some fishnet-like material, the sleeves coming down his arms and hooking around his thumbs. A ring is protruding out of his nasal septum. All and all, he looks as if he was the son of the devil himself. If only that role wasn’t already filled by George W. Bush. “Holy shit!” Trevor shouts in astonishment, his hand slapping against his forehead. “What the hell happened to you?” Seth looks down at himself, as if he doesn’t know what Trevor is referring to. After a few seconds, it dawns on him that he wasn’t dressed like the bride of Frankenstein when he left the house. “Oh, this? Well, while I was in town for the doctor, I decided I needed a new look. You like?” “You look like a fucking pussy!” Trevor screams, his hands clenching. “You’ve got more make-up on than your slutty-ass mom.” “Well...it kind of goes with the personality. Mmmm ha ha,” chuckles Seth, much to the dismay of Trevor. “Don’t tell me you’re coming out of the closet,” Trevor mutters, looking away from his partner in disgust. “No!” “Then what...” Trevor starts, turning back toward Seth before his eyes open wide with understanding. His mouth dropping open, Trevor points accusingly at Masters. “Oh shit. Now I remember. You’ve joined the fucking dark side, man! You’re a fucking dark gimmick GOTH!” “It’s about time you remembered. I was a bit distraught at your lack of reaction at first. So are you pleasantly surprised?” Seth asks nonchalantly, a sinister expression painting his face. “Fuck no! Just the sight of you is making me seriously ill,” Trevor says, holding his stomach. “Heh heh heh. That’s the look that I was going for,” Seth answers with a nod. “Oh man...I think I have to sit down,” Trevor mumbles, holding his head as he makes his way back to the chair. Seth nods and walks back toward the doorway he entered the room from. “Good. You do that,” he agrees, moving to stand in front of the door. “I’ve got a lot of work to get done, so it would better if you just got out of my way. Gimmick changing is hard work, you know. I’d better get started.” “Hold on! You’re not changing shit!” Trevor spouts, turning around to face Seth once more. “Oh, but I already have,” Masters says, motioning for Trevor to sit back down. “Now where do I start explaining?” After a few seconds of pondering, Seth nods, having decided on a course of action. “How about I introduce you to our new manager,” Seth offers, gazing over in Trevor’s direction. “A manager?! What the fu...” Trevor begins, starting to stand up before being pushed back down into the seat by Seth. “Shhh. Sit down and shut up.” Seth orders, stepping out into the other room and making an invitational gesture. “Raul, can you come in here for a second?” Stepping back into the room, Seth is followed by the ugliest little thing that you’ve ever set your eyes upon. A big hook nose, covered in warts, protrudes from the thing’s face. His eyes are squinty, the pupils big and black. Big ears lay under a mat of unkempt hair, and dirty rags cover the squat body. “What the shit is that?!” Trevor shouts in dismay. “This is our new manager. I believe I mentioned his existence a few lines back,” says Seth with a nod. “You never answered my question. What-the-shit-is-that?!” Reiterates Trevor. “Oh, what is he? Yeah, Raul here is a beggar troll. See his change cup?” Lifting a crinkled Styrofoam cup, the troll shakes it, causing the sound of clattering coins to fill the room as he smiles a big toothless smile. Trevor does not look amused. Walking over to Seth, Hawke places a hand on his friend’s shoulder, speaking to him with a lecturing tone. “Look, Seth, I don’t care how much make-up you want to put on...there’s no sense in us getting a manager. Especially one like this,” he states, encompassing Raul the Beggar Troll with a disgusted glare. “Couldn’t you get a hot babe instead...or at least a semi-attractive chick...or at least a non-troll being?” “Trev, you have a lot to learn,” Seth says, taking his partner’s same lecturing tone and turning it on him. “If we’re going to work a dark gimmick, we’re going to need a manager that is ludicrous in concept, sinister in nature, and unable to ride any of the fun roller coasters at Six Flags.” “That’s bullshit,” Trevor says, rolling his eyes. Seth shakes his head in disagreement. “Oh, is it? Let’s just think about this a few minutes,” Seth says, ticking off his fingers as he begins to make his points. “Hessian has Minion, a satanic imp. Azrael Cain has Caleb, a demonic hobo. Clyde has Bethany, his pre-pubescent, hate-filled lover. The Undertaker/Kane had Paul Bearer, a fat little funeral home director. Have I made my point yet?” “What about Darkstar and Talena? He’s a goth and he gets a hot chick,” Trevor offers, looking confident he’s proven Seth wrong. “That’s an exception to the rule,” Seth states, raising his eyebrow. “An exception. We’re playing this one by the book.” “Seth,” Trevor says, looking his partner in the face. “It pains me to say this, but you’ve gotten completely out of control. Don’t you think it’s about time that you dropped this little act?” “Oh no, my friend. I’ve barely even begun,” Masters replies with a chuckle. “But...” “No buts. Too much work to do to stand around talking about it,” Seth says, cracking his knuckles. “Next on my agenda is fixing up this promo. We’re going to do this the right way.” “What do you mean by that?” Trevor asks in a confused and frustrated voice. “Doolooloo doolooloo doolooloo doolooloo,” Seth begins making crazy little noises as he gestures toward the screen, wiggling his fingers around wildly. As he does so, the scene fades wildly in and out of focus before finally fading out to black. “What the fuck is this?” Hawke’s voice shouts angrily across the blackness. Seth merely chuckles in reply. “You’ll see soon enough, my good Trevor.” Seth chuckles once more as the promo cuts off mysteriously. Want to know what happens next? Well, just hold on to your pants and wait until the next installment, you impatient person, you. TO BE CONTINUED
