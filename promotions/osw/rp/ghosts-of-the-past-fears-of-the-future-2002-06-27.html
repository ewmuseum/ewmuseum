Ghosts of the Past....Fears of the Future

27 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-27-02 Since the Ascension Pay Per View, Alexei Ruslan had not stood face to face with Ivan Stanislav. While Ruslan had returned to Russia to handle more personal matters regarding his work, Stanislav had voluntarily remained within the United States, allowed himself to be taken care of by American doctors, and had stayed in a small apartment for the duration of the stay. This sort of action was extremely uncharacteristic of The Russian Bear. What was more strange, however, was the urgency of a phone call Ruslan had received from Stanislav, almost a hysterical Stanislav, asking that Ruslan return to America as soon as possible. And here he stood, the diminutive Russian in front of a large door with the number: 558 upon it. This was the room that Ivan had told him he was staying. Alexei stood in his trench coat, brown pants, brown hat, and black shoes and checked the directions again, for this door did not appear to be very clean but rather, the paint upon it, which was once brown, had chipped to reveal the cold black metal beneath it. He brought his hand up to the metal and knocked once and waited, fixing his coat and hat in the process. No reply came from the other side of the door, however. He blinked a few times and knocked again, slightly louder than before, and waited. For what felt like an eternity, there was no answer from within the room. “He said to be here, this is quite strange…” Ruslan muttered as he brought his hand up and knocked a third time upon the door and waited, yet silence filled the dank hallway. He fixed his tie and brought his hand to the doorknob softly and turned, it did not stop, meaning that the door was open. Ruslan swallowed and turned the knob to the right and pushed slightly. The door opened quietly, amazingly and brought Ruslan into a small foyer. He looked around, noticing a large red hat hanging from a hanger on the wall. He nodded, that was Ivan’s hat, he was here. The Russian Agent walked through the foyer, his footsteps completely silent as he was trained, and rounded a corner which brought him into a large living room, the sight that he found shocked him. The floors of the room were covered in open books. Perhaps fifty books covered the floor, almost completely blotting out the brown carpeting. On an end table, more books covered it, on a large dining room table, more books, atop the television, a few magazines that were opened. A small area in the center of the room was bare of books, but across the room, via a small path of carpet, Ivan Stanislav stood with his back to Alexei. The Russian Bear faced a fireplace in which a book rested in his massive hand. He was slightly bent, mumbling to himself as he read the book. Ruslan stood in shock as his massive Comrade continued to read. Stanislav shook his head and flipped through some pages feverishly, before stopping and reading some more. Ruslan swallowed as the sight overtook him and he shook his head. Ivan was still unaware of his presence. “I…Ivan??” Ruslan croaked, his voice suddenly very weak. Stanislav looked up for a moment and spun around. He remained bent over the book. His beard had more gray in it than usual, bags had formed underneath his eyes, and he squinted slightly, his brow wrinkled from what appeared to be an arduous amount of concentration. “Alexei??” Ivan replied, dropping the book and sending it flying to the floor with a thud. Alexei walked deeper into the room, into the area that was clear of periodicals. Stanislav walked up to him and brushed past him, almost knocking him over, “I cannot find it Alexei, I cannot find it!” Alexei blinked, still in shock as Ivan walked over to the dining room table and grabbed a book and looked through it. “Find what…Ivan?” Ivan grumbled to himself and hastily ran his hand through the pages as he searched for something, “I can’t find it. I can’t…it’s nowhere to be seen! It’s not here!!” Alexei swallowed and stooped down, looking at a few of the books. Communism in Russia ; The Russian Federation ; Capitalism in America ; Leninism in Action Alexei stood up again, “What is it you are trying to find?” Ivan walked back to Alexei and pushed past him, very irritated. His breathing was erratic, his movements were not calculated but almost uncoordinated. Alexei staggered as Stanislav grabbed a large book with the American Flag on it and started to read it. Ivan furrowed his brow, “You said that the State controlled my actions. That I worked for the State…you said that Alexei…..” he paused and fumbled through the book again, “It’s not HERE!!” Alexei had had enough. With a stride he walked over to Ivan and slapped the book out of his hand. For a moment, Stanislav stared at the empty spot between his hands. Ruslan grabbed Ivan by the shoulders and forcibly turned, facing him directly. “IVAN!” he screamed, “WHAT IS WRONG!!” Ivan stared at Alexei, the small amount of color exited his face as his breathing slowed. Alexei stared at Ivan desperately as Stanislav looked at him. “I…I cannot…I cannot find it….” “WHAT can you not find, Ivan?” Ivan lowered his gaze, his voice creaked, “What….what do I do..if the Communist…does not have a State?” Alexei blinked and let go of Ivan’s shoulders. Stanislav pushed past Alexei and walked away from him, his head lowered as he stared at the floor. “I have looked through all these books and I cannot find the answer..I..I cannot..it’s not there!” Ivan grabbed another book and slowly looked through the pages. Alexei put his hands in his pockets, “Ivan..I….I do not know the answer..I…” Stanislav threw the book to the floor, splitting the front cover which revealed a picture of Lenin, now shattered from the crack. Stanislav walked to the dining room table and sat down slowly, defeated. His shoulders sunk and bowed forward, no longer was his back straight as it usually was but rather, his entire posture was bent. He stared at the floor, “I do not understand, Alexei.” He shook his head as Alexei looked at his friend, he had never seen Ivan Stanislav like this. Stanislav continued, “I have done what they have asked. I have done my job. I have done everything that they want…” “Yes you have Ivan, you have.” Answered Alexei as he walked closer to Ivan, who’s broad back faced him. Ivan went silent for a moment and brought his hands up to his face, covering it. He breathed heavier, “And I did what I had to do to him…he took her from me….he did the damage….he was responsible..and STILL I am haunted….STILL I am not at ease!!” Alexei thought for a moment and spoke softly, “You still think of her, Ivan?” Ivan nodded, his face still covered, “I think of her all the time Alexei.” “Do you….want her back?” Ivan paused, “I do not think she would have me..but I wish to be at ease with myself. I am haunted, Alexei. Haunted constantly.” “Haunted?” Questioned Ruslan, “By what?” Ivan brought his hands to his hair and grabbed two handfuls, “Ghosts of the Past, Alexei. Words I have uttered. Vows I have given. Promises I have made. Injustices that I have caused.” “Ivan..I….” Alexei was cut off as Ivan continued, “My countrymen shun me! The politicians, the generals, they laugh at me.” “Ivan, things in Russia are changing.” Alexei replied, “Mid year reports are going out, and from what I have heard many people are going to be facing demotion, or termination. Most of them..I am afraid, appear to be communists.” Stanislav closed his eyes and covered his face. “With the coming of NATO, they want to change the image of the country. Even I am afraid I may lose my job.” Alexei whispered. Ivan nodded and looked through his hands, “It is time to look to the future, Alexei Gregorovich. I think the answer to my problems lies within Communism, but outside of it. I am a Communist….without a State. Russia has shunned me…shunned us, it would appear.” Ivan rose slowly and turned to Alexei, he looked horribly tired, “I think I know where the answer lies. I think I know where it is now. It is not in these books..it is..within each of us.” Alexei tilted his head to the side, his face pained, “Ivan, when was the last time you slept?” Ivan growled and swung an arm to the side, “It has rained for two days Alexei! I cannot sleep when it storms, I cannot sleep at all!” Ivan blinked and took a step towards the fireplace, only to stumble and fall into the large wooden frame, busting a hole into it. Stanislav straightened himself as Alexei grabbed his arm, “Come Ivan, lie down…I will clean this up. You need to rest, this is not healthy.” “Alexei…what am I going to do?? What if they fire me? What if I lose Russia!? I will have nothing!!” Ivan grabbed Alexei’s arms and seemed to beg to him, but he also appeared to be slightly delirious. The break from the constant thinking might have given him time to realize how tired he was. “Ivan, do not worry. I do not think you will get fired, come, rest, lie on this couch.” Alexei brought The Russian Bear over to the couch and laid him down. Ivan stared at the ceiling and breathed heavily. “Ivan, think of El Drago. You have important match with him this weekend. Think of the match and your opponent. Let issues in Russia remain in Russia.” Ivan nodded and brought his arms up upon his chest, “Things are going to change Alexei, and I will make sure they are for the best…” Alexei nodded, turned, and walked to a chair and sat. By the time he looked back at Ivan, he was already fast asleep. Alexei Ruslan shuddered and started to clean up the volumes of books.
