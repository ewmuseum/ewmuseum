The beginning of the end

10 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 10 From within the apartment, the sound of footsteps on the stairs was faint but unmistakable. All around the room, large men dressed in black tensed, preparing for the confrontation that they felt was inevitible. As one of them nervously readjusted his balaclava, he glanced at the man seated in the easy chair, in the center of the room.. that man was a pool of calm. 'At least someone here isn't terrified..' he thought to himself, and pressed himself back into the shadows as the handle turned on the door... As he climbed the stairs from his garage to his third floor apartment, Kanaida was lost in thought. He had somehow beaten not only Winters and Wright, but the odds.. and now had himself in position to regain the UWA title. No, scratch that, he thought to himself... HIS UWA title. If he thought of it as anything less than his property, he could fold like Mike Tyson. As he reached the top and walked up to the door, the Northern Wonder let out a satisfied sigh. Getting back into wrestling had been good for him. His body was back in shape, and his mind was occupied. He would much rather spend his time plotting and preparing for his next match than remembering his past life. Either of them. He reached out, and punched in the 7 digit code that would allow him access to his home, and once the green light flashed, he turned the handle. And froze, before even opening the door. Someone was in his apartment.. he could sense it.. could practically TASTE it. His mind raced.. who the hell could get in? He had the best security system in the world! And then it hit him. His past enveloped him like a cloak, and plunged his heart and mind into darkness. With nerveless fingers, he let go of the door, and allowed it to swing open, and as he had suspected, the old man was sitting in an easy chair, facing the door. With rage evident in his pinched voice, Kanaida dropped his bag, and hissed 'Sullivan...' From the easy chair, the old man smiled. 'Agent K... Kanaida, if you prefer. It's been how long since you betrayed your country... three years?'
