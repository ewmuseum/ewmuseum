Making contact

25 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 25, 2002 She smiled to herself as she looked around the well appointed bar.. she was impressed. On the upper level, this place had looked like your typical, if not classy, sports bar and restaurant. But here, on the lower level, it was a different story altogether. There were some great 'sports' elements, sure.. batting cages, pool tables, hoops. But the dominant factor in this huge bar was the wrestling memorabilia. From a Flair robe to an Andre singlet, this place had it all. It was not only a cool bar, but one of the best collections she had ever seen. Taking a deep breath, she walked up to the bar. She glanced one more time at the wall of title belts, and when she turned back, the bartender had appeared out of nowhere. 'Oh.. you surprised me.' she said, batting her eyes at the cute bartender. 'Would you happen to be the manager, by any chance?' He shook his head, and pointed to the other end of the bar. Turning away, she quickly thanked him, and walked down to where a woman with black curly hair was just hanging up a phone. 'Hi. You're the manager?' The woman nodded, and extended her hand. Shandra shook it as the woman introduced herself. 'I'm Laura.. and you are?' 'Shandra Russell. I...' Laura cut her off. 'From OSW, right? Yeah, I though you looked familiar. What takes you to Toronto, Shandra?' Shandra took another deep breath.. then took the plunge. 'Your boss.' The friendly smile fell from Laura's face. A scowl filled it instead, and she took half a step backwards. She raised an accusing finger, and shook it at Shandra as she spoke. 'If you think that being an interviewer in a fed is going to let you score like some damn ring-rat, you've got another think coming sister. The boss doesn't want a parade of floozies and autograph hounds.. he wants to be left alone.' Shandra held up a hand. 'I'm no ringrat, ok? I don't know what you've got going with your boss..' Laura blushed, and looked down. Aha.. thought Shandra. She's either sleeping with him, or wants to... 'but that's not what I'm here for. I'm not just another fan. I'm here on business.' Laura shakes her head. 'He's retired. And one of our main priorities here at Ground Zero is to protect his privacy.. not only is this the best bar in the city to work at, but he takes care of us.' She lowered her voice, and leaned in close. 'One of the waitresses here had a boyfriend that was beating her up, you know? That guy's still in traction, and it's been about a year now. So if the boss wants to be left alone, you're going to leave him alone... nobody here would sell him out to you. Most of these guys don't even know him, and they still love him to death.' Shandra reached into her purse, and pulled out a package. She placed it on the bar.. Laura made no move to touch it. 'That's from Ed. It's an offer, and a video of OSW's highlights. And a second video, with the career highlights of the two guys that OSW just signed.. Jason Winters, and Jesus St Crux. Your boss may have heard of them.' Shandra stood, and closed her purse. 'Look.. I know he gets his mail here. Just give him the package, and let him make up his own mind, would you? At the very least, he'll be interested in that second tape.' She turned, and walked to the stairs that would take her back outside. Then she stopped, and turned back. 'And if it helps my case any.. I know why he chose this particular spot. I understand it's significance.' And with that, she walked up the stairs, a small smile on her face.. she'd seen that last bit strike home with Laura. With a sigh, Laura picked up the large manilla envelope, and carried it to the dumbwaiter. She placed it inside, and pushed the button that would take the package to the top floor. And as she closed the door, she took one last glance at the name on the package.. the name of her employer, who had sequestered himself from the world for the past year. Kanaida.
