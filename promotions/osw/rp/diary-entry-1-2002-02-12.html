Diary entry 1

12 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/12/02 Frankie sits at his dinner table in the dark. Night has crept over Brooklyn and sleep has passed Frankie bye, yet again. He peers down at the notebook in front of him. It appears to be a makeshift diary, opened to the first page. Frankie sits, pen in hand, and starts venting... Imagine kickin' ass in tha street Without cops harassin', Imagine going to court With no trial. Lifestyle, spewing truth like water, No welfare supporters, More conscious of tha way we raise our daughters. Days are shorter, nights are colder, Feelin' like life is over, past bites hard like a cobra. Tha world's hot, my folks got knocked, Evidently, its elementary, They gonna hold us back eventually. Just some thoughts from my mind, I take a glimpse into time, Read the Tron read the world is mine... If I ruled the world, Imagine that. I'd free my mind, And the demons held therein. Black past full of pain, It haunts me like the plague. If I ruled the world, Still livin' for today, just can't get past the days of time. Paradise like relaxin', Black, Latino, and Anglo-Saxon. So many years of struggle make me envision, A better livin', Tha type of place to raise our kids in. Open ya eyes to tha lies history's told foul, But I'm as wise as the old owl, Plus the gold child, Seein' things no kid should see, Bein' places no kid should be. You love to hear tha story, How tha thugs lives no worry. Duck down in cars seats, Guns mandatory. But I transcend stereotypes, Hit tha ring, lookin' for tha big lights. Gettin' caught in tha life I should lead, Better take head, I change my future, and let my past stay deceased. But I could get all tha chips, Be poor or rich, Aint nobody want a nigga havin shit. If I ruled the world and everything in it, Sky's the limit, I'd have a gold strap in a minute. There wouldn't be such thing as jealousy, Or B felonies, Strictly living longevity, It's my destiny, To be atop of those who less them me. Better find out, before your time's out, What the fuck? Frankie drops the pen and leans back in his chair. As he reclines his head turns towards the window. His eyes peruse the cityscape as the camera fades to black...
