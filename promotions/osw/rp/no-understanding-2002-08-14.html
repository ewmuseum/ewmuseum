No Understanding.

14 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-14-02 'I cannot find you..It is impossible!' I scream out in to the night sky as my hands once again slip from their grasp on the wet rocks before me. My energy is leaving me quickly now for I have not eaten since I set out. My body is ground against the rocks jagged edges as I reach upward and pull up once more. With a groan I am finally atop the mountain. I fall on to the wet mossy grass with a great feeling of exhaustion panging inside of my body. The pain in my sore limbs is enough to break me but I know I must endure. Never before have I been so close to knowing..Never before have I been so close to finding out..The truth. My body is battered by the fierce raindrops pounding down from above as I lay. I am motionless against wet mother earth, allowing her to soak me in. I hear a noise from my far left, something loud and heavy but I am too weary to lift my head. I lay still.. A roar is fills the night as does a bolt of beautiful lightening does the same. I cringe, my eyes closing against the light, the sound, and the rain. My scream is deafening as my body is snatched from the ground and upward in to the air. I hear snapping, and I taste the sweet tinge of blood spill inside of my mouth. Air is pressed from my lungs, I can hear it as it passes through my lips. My body is jerked back and forth until I come to rest on the damp earth once again. The beasts are much larger here, and this one was bent on my demise. I feel sharp claws on my back, and I arch as they are drug downward drawing massive amounts of blood. Again I cry out, my screams echoing against the blanket of darkness surrounding me. I fall limp, my body being shattered my this thing upon me. I cannot see for the rain is falling in to my eyes,..But it is not rain falling upon me anymore. It is my own blood raining down on to my body like a shower cool and refreshing. I scream, my hands tugging at my hair. I am being consumed, eaten alive piece by piece until nothing of me is left. Upon the cold ground, thick with mud and moss I can see something from within the gut I now reside. I can hear something as well..And my eyes narrow to the object. My heart lies ripped open, beating on the ground. Thump.. Thump.. Thump.. Slowly though the sound is changing, no longer to I hear the beating of my heart but..Something, loud, screeching. My hand comes slamming down upon the snooze button on top of the alarm clock next to my bed. My body jerks upward from the satin sheets and I open my eyes wide. 'A dream..It was all a dream..' But still yet it feels as if more should come of this. How could something that feels so familiar be merely a dream? As I fall backward on to the bed and rub my sleep laden eyes I ponder this and simply return back to sleep. The gym could wait.
