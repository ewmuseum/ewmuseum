Back to Real Old School?

15 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/15/02 'Sell your soul and sign an autograph. Big bang baby it's a crash, crash, crash. I wanna cry, but I gotta laugh. Orange crush mama, it's a laugh, laugh, laugh.' -Scott Weiland, 1996 A journey in life, or a lifelong journey. Which one of the two fits you best? You might take a journey that lasts for a period of time, and though still a journey, one day that journey comes to an end. And then there are others that have a journey from the minute they exit their first home, inside their mother. And that journey will continue to that very day you end up six feet under. But one thing is for sure, each and every single one of us has a journey sometime in their life. Scott Naket's journey? Oh, it's a lifelong journey, and it's still going on this day. From the first time light hit little Scott's eyes, he looked around this brand new world and proclaimed, 'I'm gonna be champion of the world!'. Then the doctor spanked him and the thought left his mind for the next 20 years. And that one day, 20 years after the red bottom hand whack, the thought came back to Naket's mind. So now, back to the place known as Old School, Naket has that one journey going on to this minute still. Standing outside on a sidewalk during the middle of the day, Naket is looking around. Complete with his 'Too Hott To Handle' tee shirt, blue jeans and a pair of sunglasses, Naket looks around as a few, and when I say a few I mean about one or two, people pass him. Not much going on, as many are stuck in their offices, and this ain't no New York. Hell, there's a bunch of bushes about 20 meters behind the Canadian Icon. Naket looks at the camera. 'So I'm standing outside of this building here,' Naket begins, as the camera shifts over to show the building. It's labeled 'K-Mart' and the camera shifts over to the left to show a empty parking lot. The camera shifts back over to Naket, and he continues, 'and I thought, HEY! What better place to be to show us all a in depth view of the lifestyles of both Clay Vendetta and Mike Marvelous then the local K-Mart. Now, I know K-Mart has been known for it's cheap, crappy and stale merchandise, cause hell, we all know that those three words perfectly fit descriptions of the 'Classic One' and the 'Canadian Hero'. But I figured that we could go inside, and show you some items that these two could use, after I show them both just how stale, crappy and horrible both of them are.' Naket turns around and walks over to the sliding doors. He steps forward, and smacks into the doors. Taking a step back, while rubbing his face, he looks at the door, which just sits there. He looks forward at the door, which reads 'Out of Business' on it. Naket shakes his head and shrugs. 'It seems I can't show you all this, cause it appears that the stronger, more cared for competition of this company survived, while the cheap, no one could give a crap for company just died down and was forgotten about,' Naket begins, trying to force a grin on his pain stricken face. 'The point you ask? It's really simple, as I would be the strong and cared for competition, that people pay good money to see, while Vendetta and Marvelous would be the cheap crap that no one cares about. Marvelous, on Slam, I will show you why you wi...' 'Excuse me, sir?' Naket pauses as he fells a tug on his pant legs. He looks down to see a little kid pulling on his leg. Naket bends down to see the kid, who's probably no older then eight. The kid is wearing a Ed tee shirt back from the UFW days. The kid looks at Naket. 'Are you Scott Naket?' the kid asks, showing no fear in the size difference of the two. Naket grins, and looks at the kid. 'I sure am,' he replies. 'Would you like a autograph little boy?' Naket asks, getting ready to pull out a trusty pen from his pocket. The kid looks at Naket, and shakes his head. 'No, I don't want a autograph. It's not because I don't like you, but,' the kid starts, but pauses, looking at Naket. Naket just stays kneeled down, waiting for the kid to finish. 'Go on,' Naket says, encouraging the kid to continue. 'Well, I liked the old Scott Naket better. The one that thought he knew everything there was too know. The one that thought each and every match, no matter who he would fight, he'd come out on top easily.' Naket just stares at the kid, blank faced. He looks down at the ground, and slowly replies. 'So, what...what your saying is, you liked the cocky, I make the rules Scott Naket better then myself right now?'. Naket just sits there, waiting for the kid to reply. Naket waits again, and waits. The kid looks down at the ground, and looks at Naket. 'Well, yeah,' he begins. 'You won more matches like that, and you were cooler that way'. Naket just looks at the kid and scratches his head. 'Ok, but why are you telling me this kid?' Naket asks him, wondering what kind of answer he will get. The kid looks at Naket and says 'Well, I want you to become OSW Champion one day, but if you're like this all the time, I'm afraid you won't win the title.'. Naket stands up, and looks down at the kid. 'well,' Naket begins, 'I'll try my best to make you happy then kid, but I can't make any promises for you. You understand where I'm coming from?' Naket asks the kid. The kid looks up at Naket, and nods his head. Naket smiles and starts to turn around. 'Take care kid.' he says, as he begins to walk away. The kid stands there, and looks at Naket as he walks away. The kid looks down at a bottle he's holding in his hand that was obscured from the camera before. 'Hey sir!' the kid yells. Naket turns his head around and looks at the kid. The kid tosses him a green bottle. Naket catches it, and looks at it. He reads the label, which says '7-UP' on it. Naket opens the bottle and takes a sip of the drink. He looks at the kid, and holds the bottle out towards the kid in a gesture of thanks to him, and continues to walk away. The kid, kind of disappointed, turns around and slowly starts to walk away. He has moved a total of five steps before a hand is felt on his shoulder. The kid turns around and looks up at Scott Naket, who's now standing behind him. Naket looks down at him. 'Hey kid,' Naket starts. He holds out the bottle to the kid, and he grabs it from Naket. 'Make 7-UP yours!'. Naket turns around and walks away, as the kid looks at him. He quietly whispers through his big smile, 'He's back.'. So will Naket actually complete his lifelong journey? Can he become one of the all-time greats in this sport, and forever silence those who criticize him week after week? Well maybe, and that’s just maybe, he can go back to his old form after all, and show everyone why he was one of the most dominant wrestlers in old school PCW, right here in Old School Wrestling. Fade to black.
