..the simple things

14 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-14-02 'By a blessed dispention, human beings forget physical pain much more quickly than their joyous emotions and experiences.' -Winston Churchill Lennox is in his hotel bathroom crouched down on the tile floor...searching violently through his bag... He pulls out several clothing items and hastily casts them away, before he comes across the item he had been searching so intently for... ...a disposable bic razor. He rises to a straight position, as he gazes blurrily into the mirror. His eyes slowly fall into focus as he sees his reflection. His face looks pale, and in dire need of sleep.He sluggishly squirts some shaving cream into his hand, then spreads it on his face.... ....time to get rid of this thing now. He raises the razor and goes to work. He starts off with the stubbled beard he has started to grow...the razor gos *skretch skretch* as he cuts the hair off. He then moves down to the thick goatee; He probably shouldn't be shaving with a disposable razor, but he persists. He takes hard, careful strokes as he takes out huge hunks of hair from his face. The job is almost complete. On the last stroke, however, Lennox nics himself quite deeply. A sharp pain rises in his chin, and he lets out a cry... 'Goddamn!' The blood is now sliding off his chin and dripping into the sink. He is in a shocked state, but overcomes it after he realizes he's losing some substantial blood amounts. He reaches and grabs a white towel and tightly presses it to his chin. He looks down at the blood and turns on the faucet. The streaming water mixes with the blood turning it a shade of pink; his facial hair is mixed as well. He watches as the water prevails, eliminating the blood and hair from the sinks surface...sliding down the drain.. He leaves the bathroom and sits on the edge of his bed. He lets out a sigh and collapses backwards... fade.
