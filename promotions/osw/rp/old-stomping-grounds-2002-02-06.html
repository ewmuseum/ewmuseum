Old Stomping Grounds

6 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/06/02 A tremendous odor fills the area, a putrid smell. Only the two kinds of things could stand this. Those being, animals and, well, the other is something that must be described. You wouldn’t believe it if you had seen it with your own eyes! These beasts are massive in size, but not all are tall. They can range from around five feet to nearly seven feet tall. The muscle structure on most of them is a feet in itself, but their brains aren’t always the best thing about them. Braun sometimes gets the best of them and they are smelly as they come. These things that I am talking about are what you would call Professional Wrestlers, but we are in the presence of the worst ones. These men haven’t made it big, these men are only working on the Independent circuit. A locker room that really isn’t all that big, with a minimal supply of lockers, and about two showers. The floor seems as if it is cleaned maybe once ever month and the lockers are almost big enough to fit a basketball in. Noises are heard from in another room. As we maneuver our way through the mess of a locker room, we make our way into a small gymnasium looking arena. A badly set-up ring is set in the middle of the building, padding surrounding it and not much anything inside the building. A ton of steel chairs are either set up against the walls or lying on the ground. Three people stand outside the ring, while three more are inside the ring. Two seem to be getting trained, while the other is obviously the trainer. “Try an arm drag.” The words come from the trainer’s mouth. The two men try the arm drag and fail successfully, when a door swings open and the light shines into the building. A figure stands at the door, but we can’t quite tell who or what this thing is. The camera tries to pan in on it, but the light is surrounding the person and engulfing them in it as well. The door squeaks shut and we can finally tell that the man is “Bad Attitude” Jesse Ramey. The people in the ring have all stopped doing what they were doing and are just watching. A smile comes across the trainer’s face as Jesse walks to the ring. Jesse’s attire is almost the same as what he was wearing the day he was at the bar. He is wearing one of the orange OSW T-shirts, (which you can find at the shopzone). Jesse walks to the ropes and leans up against the side of the ring. “So, tell me, how does it feel not having me help out and work around here any longer?” Jesse asks with a smile. Smiling, “Jesse, you know it’s been almost five to six years since you stopped working around here.” Jesse pulls back and hops up onto the apron. He gets into the ring and walks over to the trainer. Jesse crosses his arms and looks at the men in the ring. “Tell me something. Are these men any good?” Asks Jesse “I like to tell them and myself that they are the best I’ve ever had, but then again I don’t want to give them a big head or myself. God only knows that I wouldn’t want to mess up with them.” The trainer says. “Well, you obviously did a good job with at least one person you had ever trained.” Jesse ribs him and smiles. “Yes, that one thing is true there. I never thought you would turn out this good.” He responds. Jesse looks confused, “What do you mean? I was the best student you had from the beginning. You even told me that.” “Come with me.” He grabs Jesse by the arm. The two walk off and come into what seems to be the trainer’s office. “Listen, Jesse I know you are good and I don’t want to just go around and tell that to all of these boys right to their faces. Do you know how down it would make them?” He questions. “Well, I only guessed that they would love to have a man of my stature in their presence and here to maybe give them a hand at doing what they have been doing. I didn’t mean anything by all of this.” Jesse responds. Whipping the sweat of his forehead, “I know you didn’t mean anything by it. Things just haven’t been to well for me around here since you left. You were my best student and the only person who actually knew what he was doing. As soon as you were gone there was nothing left for me here. All of the other members quit because you weren’t around and my new students aren’t worth crap! I need you back, Jesse.” “Don, you know I can’t come back. I’m working for Old School Wrestling now and you know as well as I do that this is big time.” Jesse continues. Sitting down in his chair, “Yes, I know this, but I really could use your help around here. I really do wish you would stick around.” Raising his eyes in excitement, “Why not come with me? I could use a trainer!” “Jesse, you know I can’t just up and leave this place. I’ve been here through thick and through thin. I’ve never stopped once before and I don’t plan on stopping this school now. Thanks, but no thanks.” He regretfully responds. “Okay, well, how about if I give you some money to get this place back in shape. It is the least that I could do. Seeing as how you did help get me off the ground to begin with.” Jesse tries to reason. Thinking, “Okay, but only if you really want to make a donation to this place.” “Well, how does one hundred thousand sound?” Jesse asks. His eyes lighting up, “Are you serious?” Look of confusion, “Yes, why wouldn’t I be?” “You actually have that kind of money on you?” He asks with excitement. “No, I don’t have it on me, but I can write a check for it.” Pulling out a checkbook. He watches on as Jesse writes out a check for $100,000. Ripping the check out, “Here, I hope it will help out some what.” “Thank you, Jesse.” He responds. Coming to his feet, “It’s the least I could do. I really must be on my way though, I’ve got someone waiting outside in the car and I’ve got a show to be getting to this Saturday.” Shaking hands, “Well, be careful and take care of yourself as well.” Jesse walks out of the room and down the hallway. He continues into the room where all of the men are still wrestling, most of them just playing around. Walking back to the exit door, he makes his way through the maze of chairs on the ground, opens the door, and makes his way outside. The door slams behind him as we see a yellow Corvette ZR-1 sitting in front of it. Jesse makes his way to the car and opens the door. We see Chass sitting in the passenger seat and Jesse sticks on leg in the vehicle, puts his shades on, and looks up at the building. “I know I was trained by the best, regardless of what it looks. I know I am the best and that I am better than every man in OSW. Frankie Bones and Switchblade will not be a problem for me. I was taught better and I’ve worked a whole lot more than they have. I deserve this win more than they do and more than anything, people just like me more than they do those two ingrates. OSW, It’s Time For An Attitude Adjustment!” Jesse steps back into the vehicle and closes the door. The car pulls out and drives off into the sun. Slowly fading into nothingness. ~END~
