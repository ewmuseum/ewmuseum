Aftermath (Part 5 of 6 of 'Breaking Benefits')

8 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Mar. 8, 2002 ...Beep... ... ...Beep... ... ...Beep... 'Well fuck, at leas' it aint no flatline.' Dom said, from a chair in the corner, as he scrubbed his bald head. He watched from the cardiogram jump up and down, only to turn to a grinning Rhett Locke. ''ey, turn the sh*t off already would you!?' Rhett called to the rather obese nurse. 'I'm awake, Jesus H. Christ!' The two men chuckled, it was hard to believe a night ago he had been brutally beaten by a gang called the Benefit. And who did Rhett say was behind it all, Drew Hostyle. The fluorescently lit room had egg-shell white walls, a grey tiled floor, and of course, the complete monitoring system. His blue hair was scruffy, nearly brushed down, and his skin was still a bit red from the blood that stained him earlier. This event would become the single thing that'd change his life forever. The single thing that would make his life...better. He'd put a new foot forward. As well as piece a new puzzle together, with his career, his duties, and his obligations. It wouldn't be easy, but he knew the first step. Drew Hostyle. ''Ey Rhett, I'ma go get some cheap ass hospital cigs from tha venda machines. Youz want one?' 'No, not right now bro.' Dom nodded, his stalky 5'9 figure strolling out the door, Rhett was temporarily blinded by the light that reflected off of his bald scalp. Rhett pressed the yellow button with an arrow pointing down on his remote, and the upper part of the hospital bed lowered. He looked up, finally seeing the ceiling, and smiled. He was alive. And for the first time in a long time... 'I f*ckin care.' Funny how that works. Regardless, his thoughts travelled to two days, Slam...the first step of a two-step revenge plan. He'd make sure Drew Hostyle knew who the fuck he was messing with. He'd make sure Hostyle knew exactly what he got himself into setting up that entire fiasco, the whole beat-down...He wasn't sure if Drew was behind it...but he was damn sure part of it. And at that moment, that was enough to beat the sh*t out of him. 'Mr. Locke, I believe your free to go.' Dom soon followed, smacking the new pack of Vantage Ultra-Lights against his palm in a hurried fashion. It was good to be alive... To feel alive. This was the aftermath of what previously happened. The aftermath: Pure bliss.
