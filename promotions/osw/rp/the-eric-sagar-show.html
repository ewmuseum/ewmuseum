The Eric Sagar Show

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8:00am 29th March 2002 The is a purple background with 'GOD TV' written in bold white letters across the front and under is a graphic with a white background and purple writing it says 'Next: The Eric Sagar show' in purple lettering and under it says '8:45am: An audience with Cliff Richard.' But as the backdrop, a lady with a soft voice says 'Next on GOD TV we have The Eric Sagar show followed by an audience with Cliff Richard.' The scene fades to black, then it starts up with Eric Sagar in an empty room. Behind him is a large plotted plant, and a leather couch. This is obviously his dressing room. 'Hi my brothers and sisters and welcome to the Eric Sagar show.' Sagar says, 'Tonight my special guest will be a very close friend of mine, OSW Superstar and dedicated Christian, Atlanta 'Propaganda' Johnson.' This scene fades to black. Then a mans voice says 'The Eric Sagar show is filmed in front of a live studio audience.' The scene fades into the studio, it has a white background with a huge black cross in the centre. In front of that are a bunch of gospel singers all wearing white robes. They begin to sing, as the audience clap and on walks Eric Sagar with his trademark grey suit and a white shirt underneath and a grey tie. He also has quite a big bible under his arm. The gospel singers fade. 'A-Men, my brother and my sisters.' Sagar preaches. 'A-Men' the audience replies. 'Welcome to the pilot edition of the 'Eric Sagar Show' coming to you live thanks to those great people at God TV!' Sagar proclaims. 'But before we go to our special guest let me read to you a few passages from the good book, the bible.' The crowd all start to cheer as Sagar finds his page. 'They came to Capernaum. When he was in the house, he asked them, 'What were you arguing about on the road?' But they kept quiet because on the way they had argued who was the greatest. Sitting down Jesus called the twelve and said. 'If anyone wants to be the first, he must be the very last, and servant of all.'' Sagar turns the page. 'He took a little child and had him stand among them. Taking him in his arm he said to them. 'Whoever welcomes one of the littlest children in my name welcomes me; and whoever welcomes me does not welcome me but the one who sent me.'' Sagar closes the bible and the audience begins to clap. 'A little taking from Mark chapter 9. My sisters and brothers.' By now the gospel singers have moved to reveal two chairs. Both are plastic with black metal stands and the actual seating bit is white. 'So my brothers without further adieu, I present to you, your next OSW Wrestling Champion! Propaganda!' 'Play God' by Spineshank plays as from the curtain comes out the power himself, Propaganda. He is wearing a brown suit, with a brown like green shoulder patches. Under the suit he has a white shirt and a black tie, his usual black thick framed glasses. On his head he is wearing a brown cloche hat. He shakes hands with Eric Sagar and both men take a seat. 'Welcome to the Eric Sagar show my brother Propaganda.' Eric greets the power himself. 'The pleasure is all mine my brother!' Propaganda thanks Sagar. 'All mine!' 'Well' Sagar says, 'Let's get down to business.' 'All right' Propaganda agrees. 'So let's start off by telling the folks at home what your 'mission' is in Jane Reagen's Old School Wrestling promotion.' Sagar suggests. 'Indeed we shall.' Propaganda starts his tale. 'Well I bet you are all wondering why such a dedicated Christian like myself is in a pro-violence organisation like OSW! Well this is how it happened. I grew up rough! I did the drugs, I did the alcohol, and I sinned! Like Satan!' Propaganda shamefully announces. 'I even beat up my father, but my violence and sins would soon be stopped by the good people at Tennessee's law and order department.' The camera cuts to the crowd to see everyone is hanging off Propaganda's every word. 'I was arrested, put in 'the slammer,' 'the big house,' and though I thought this would've ruined my career I was wrong my brothers and sisters! It was a blessing in disguise, for I had no friends, I had no family but I got visited by this man right here.' Propaganda points to Eric. 'He told me about Jesus.' The audience all shout 'Alleluia.' 'He told me about God!' Propaganda yells. The audience all shout 'Alleluia.' 'He told me about the Holy Ghost!' Propaganda yells again. The audience all shout 'Alleluia.' 'He told me about Moses, Samson, Mark, John and all the disciples.' Propaganda Testifies. For the last time the audience all shout 'Alleluia.' Propaganda lowers the volume of his voice and it's like he's leans towards the audience 'And I can tell you my brothers and my sisters, I was hooked! More than any other drug. I knew that from that day forward I would want to spend the rest of my life worshipping god!' The audience all engage in a round of applause. As Propaganda seems really happy, he looks over to Sagar. 'A-men! A-Men!' Sagar preaches. 'It's all true! What a man, but the story is not over is it Propaganda?' 'Oh no my Brother Eric!' Propaganda tells the world 'Oh no, I shall carry on. Well I promised that I will give up Wrestling and being an insider myself I would take down professional wrestling with rallies! So we held rallies, we protested and we held special church services but did this work?' Propaganda asks. 'No.' Sagar answers. 'No it didn't.' 'So we did what the old proverb says 'if you can't beat them join them!' so we did. I re-signed the application and with Eric by my side and God in my heart we over took the federation, it's name was THW. And finally I won the World Title.' Propaganda goes on. 'Eric would you like to finish this?' He asks. 'Indeed I would,' Sagar says 'The Power himself, Propaganda had beaten people to a living pulp! This had the roster in fear. Knowing that if he won the World Title there would be no chance! Then in came this monster equally as tall as Propaganda at seven feet, a bit heavier then Propaganda at four hundred pounds! He was a biker, punk kind of guy! His name was 'Road Rage' everyone thought Propaganda would fall to him! But in a Clarksville street fight with the power of God Propaganda destroyed him in a flawless victory!' Sagar chuckles evilly. 'Some tough guy.' Propaganda comments. 'Indeed my brother!' Sagar agrees still chuckling. 'The owner shut down the company at the hands of Propaganda, Sagar and the power of God, but this had us fooled. We thought because THW did this, that others would shut in fear that Propaganda may come. This is yet to happen so we thought instead of trying to take down a little regional fed why not take down a world-wide fed. So myself and Propaganda are coming here to take down the world of wrestling with one clean swoop as we go for Old School Wrestling!' 'Testify my brother!' Propaganda roars. 'So our first target is this man who claims to be a vampire, a man whose face is mangled, whose heart is black! We want to take down Vampir Nosferatu!' A huge OSW Promotional poster is rolled over the backdrop and this poster has on it Vampir Nosferatu with a wineglass in hand full of what seems to be blood. And under it is has 'God Blood?' on and Vampir seems to have a blood moustache. The crowd boo and hiss at it. 'Take a look at it that Satanist scum my brothers and sisters! For he shall go down!' Propaganda yells. 'Indeed he shall.' Sagar says calmly. 'You've already beaten him twice Propaganda and now shall be the thrice time! And the final time, with the power of God behind us all the way! We shall take him and send him straight to Lucifer in the reddest pit of hell!' 'A-men my brother!' Propaganda preaches! 'A-men. Now onto this 'Angeldust' Nick Casey.' 'Ah Yes!' Sagar is reminded. 'Now my brothers prepare to be amazed. Well Nick Casey was not a wanted sperm cell! So he was a test tube baby. Being a freak of nature and less better than Propaganda and myself we were willing to give him a an easy time because of his troubled past, but oh no! In his latest promo he said this…' A gospel singer wheels a wide screen television on a trolley onstage. He stops it in the middle of the stage and then we see the clip from Nick Casey's latest promo. 'Kids, the moral of this story is don't hate people for being a lot better, stronger, faster, and better-looking than you, like EoE does. Don't blame God for loving someone more than you, like Propaganda does. And just because half of your face may be rotted away and you have no friends, like Vampir, doesn't mean you get to chokeslam the new guy. Or you will pay, just like those jerk-offs did.' The clip ends and Sagar stands in front of the television. 'Now some of you may or may not know that he told that to a group of school children.' Sagar yells in disgust. 'Now his filth and ignorance has been inherited to these poor, poor children! When I find out what school this is at I shall go down personally and clear there mind of these atheist ideas passed down to them.' Sagar gets a round of applause. 'Finally…' says the 7' monster, Propaganda, 'Finally we have Epitome of Evil, he like Vampir Nosferatu and 'Angeldust' Nick Casey is a non believer!' the crowd. 'Oh my brothers it is true.' Sagar backs up Propaganda. 'Not only does he not believe in God and all the bible's scriptures… but he doesn't believe in the power of myself.' Propaganda says with much disgust in his tone, this can also be seen in his face. 'For he is the most ignorant of the bunch my brother Propaganda.' Sagar comments. 'Indeed he is as last Slam I carried our team, for the stupidity of Jane Reagen and Ed had me booked to team with Epitome of Evil and The Magi, both of whom are Satanist scum!' 'Which brings us onto another topic.' Sagar says 'The staff at OSW, I mean take Jane Reagen for Example, if she would stop having sexual relation with the likes of Sebastian Toomes, the tainted World Champion and got back to the job she should be doing then the whole world would be better off.' Sagar mocks. 'But what about Ed, the so called commissioner?' Propaganda asks. 'If he would stop sitting on his rear, drinking beer, and mourning cause a cheap fold-up steel chair got a bit scratched then we'd all be better off.' 'Indeed' Sagar agrees. 'We'll be back with questions after these commercial messages.' Sagar says as the scene fades to black. The Eric Sagar show comes to you in association with Mike's Heating Company: 'Heaters are heaters, and should not be thrown out of a window.' 'Welcome back.' Sagar says as the scene fades in where he is sitting next to Propaganda. He hands Propaganda the bible and himself and his microphone go over to the audience. 'Now Propaganda will answer some of your questions.' He looks over the crowd and heads over to one man who has a checked shirt on and nicely combed back hair. 'Yes you.' He points to the man and the man stands up. 'Hi, I'm Bob from Dallas.' Bob says in an outraged Texan accent. 'And I'm no flaming Christian, I suppose you guys would call me 'Atheist Scum' but I'm a fan of Vamp' and I…' already he has won the disapproval of the other audience members. 'Let him say pass his my brothers and sisters.' Sagar says to prevent the hissing. 'Yeah, what ever.' Bob says carefree. 'And I only came in because I'm an OSW fan! You hear me? And I do not approve one bit of you trying to take down OSW! But the last straw was when you said all that about Ed, man Ed is god! You shouldn’t talk that way about…' Bob is interrupted by Propaganda roaring hatefully. '…LOOK YOU SNIVELLING LITTLE WEASEL!' He yells 'HOW DARE YOU COME ONTO A CHRISTIAN SHOW AND ANNOUNCE YOU ARE ATHIEST SCUM! AND THEN HAVE THE AUDACITY TO SAY YOU'RE A FAN OF VAMPIR NOSFERATU! YOU’RE IGNORANCE EXCELLS…' Sagar interrupts Propaganda. 'Calm down my brother, to quote Jesus himself 'Don't be afraid; just believe and she will be healed.' In other words don't worry about his ignorance, atheist scum like him are not worth it.' This quote and symbolism gets a good reaction from the audience and a few 'Yea's' are heard. 'Well you guys can go fuck yourselves.' There is a lot of gasps and Bob storms out of the studio. 'Oh dear…' Sagar mumbles out in worry. 'Before the broadcasters at God TV take us off air please remember what Jesus himself said 'Forgive them Father for they no not what they…' Sagar is suddenly interrupted by the GOD TV logo with a small caption saying. 'The Following broadcast has been stopped early for unforeseen circumstances.' As gospel music in a ring tone style plays as a musical backdrop.
