Let the Carnage Begin...

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28/02/02 ”The Monster They Call Meanstreak was vanquished and I have attained my revenge…and now the time for fun is upon us!” Hessian leans back in his 8ft stone sarcophagus, situated in the middle of his chamber which he calls home, in Detroit, MI. In place of Minion the Imp on the black throne to the left of the sarcophagus sits The Marked Beast, and fellow Heresian Bloodlust member, Azrael Cain. On the right side of the room, lying on top of a stained mattress, with his head in his hands, is Minion the Imp. Staring at all the fine details of Hessian’s abode, from the fine black carpeting to the faded yellow walls, Cain seems almost forlorn without his homeless companion, Caleb. Minion pulls a bottle of Peppermint Schnapps from an inside pocket in his old, worn cloak. Giggling fiendishly, Minion takes a swig, whilst Hessian looks ahead at the steel door that suits as the entrance in and out of his little boudoir. ”Hessian, everyone realises you beat Meanstreak, be it with the help of………” ”No! I did not request any help of any sort from that Communist scum! Meanstreak may have had the upper hand at some point, but I’ll be damned if anyone thinks I wasn’t going to fight to the end! It doesn’t matter any more…Meanstreak was shamed out of a win, I have my revenge…and I was sure that you were going to win your match as I observed it………” says Hessian gruffly, through his steel mask. ”The Heavyweight title is an honourable prize, but now that we are destined to decimate those two pretty boys, and even Meanstreak…the title can wait…” Azrael replies, the hint of anger in his tone of voice. Minion releases a muffled hiccup, and screws the lid back on his Schnapps and leans over to the right, looking past the sarcophagus to find Caleb lying sleeping next to the black throne that is occupied by Cain. Caleb lies motionless, again cloaked in Cain’s bearskin cloak, motionless, and seemingly peaceful, regardless of the wound to his head. ”What about Ivan Stanislav?” asks Minion, glancing over to Hessian. Hessian adjusts his large black cloak to cushion his large shoulders on the sarcophagus, and turns to Minion. ”What of him? Let him have Meanstreak, it’s apparent that is all he wants at the moment. Cain and I have our pick of the litter, if any four of them decided to step out of line a simple Wrath of the Heretic would set straight their attitude…” Cain looks at Caleb, then to Hessian, and with a muffled grunt, brings his fist forcefully down onto the armrest of the throne. ”Pick of the litter? Look at Caleb………Meanstreak claims to be a Monster…a Beast…a Crazed Lunatic…never in existence have I encountered a self proclaimed Monster and watched as he ravaged the health of an innocent old man. Meanstreak is going to suffer for what he did………” Azrael snarls at the mere thought of Meanstreak, and slumps in the chair. Minion gets to his feet and waddles over to Caleb. Staring down at the helpless hobo, Minion sighs, and waddles over to Hessian, slouching in his sarcophagus. ”So what do you plan to do in the few days before Slam?” asks the 3’11” Imp. ”Do not be so foolish Minion. The few days we have, Caleb must rest, you must drink and Azrael and I will be busy planning the destruction of the Monster and his two “bitches” for the weekend. Stanislav may even be a useful tool in this encounter…we three together can permanently eliminate the Monster and the Suburban pretty boys…but even so Meanstreak is Meanstreak…” ”………And you are Heresian Bloodlust! Remember that…Hessian you’ve already Eternally Damned Meanstreak, and Cain, Cain almost won the OSW Title shot hadn’t it been for that Samoan miscreant! Hessian and Azrael Cain are among the best in OSW! No one can doubt that, not even Streak, Civil Disobedience or Stanislav! Don’t even think about thinking about thinking of thinking you can be beat with your odds! Heresian Bloodlust are being underestimated…and that is just the time when you both need to strike and strike hard!” Minion thrusts his stumpy hand into his cloak pocket and pulls out the bottle of Schnapps. He jerks the cap off and takes a vigorous swig of the drink………and suddenly begins choking. His little black eyes seem to pop out of his head as he slams the bottle on the sarcophagus. Hessian cocks his head at the little Imp, who looks to Hessian choking profusely, and points to his neck. ”It………*COUGH*………went………*COUGH*………down………*COUGH*………the wrong………*COUGH*…TUBE…*COUGH*” Minion begins drooling and coughing insanely. Hessian calmly screws the cap back onto the Schnapps bottle, and reaches out over the sarcophagus; grabs Minion by the head…turns him around…and pulls his other arm back, his right hand silver gauntlet gleaming in the light of the four large black candles in each corner of the room……… THWACK!!! Minion literally flies across the chamber and smacks into the wall, and just like any Imp should, he slides slowly down, groaning all the way. Cain winces at the giant blow to Minion’s back, whilst Hessian tosses the bottle of Schnapps onto Minion’s mattress. As Minion twitches nervously on the floor, trying to utter a simple thank you to Hessian, when suddenly the entire chamber begins pulsing with the sound of heavy thumping. Azrael looks to Caleb and looks around, trying to pinpoint the source of the rumbling and the thumping. ”Hessian……...how many earthquakes befall Detroit annually?” asks Azrael, his voice very jumpy and jittery from the strong vibrations coursing through the chamber. Minion begins to stir, however Caleb seems to be caught in a deep slumber. Hessian climbs out of his chamber…and the noises stop. Hessian looks to Azrael Cain, who returns a surprised glare…and the noises return, this time in the form of hard knocking on the steel door. The constant banging of the steel echoes through the small chamber as Hessian slowly steps forward, a fist drawn back in case of emergency…closer Hessian gets to the steel door, closer and closer, with every step the banging gets louder. As Hessian reaches the door and extends his large hand to open it, the entire steel door bursts wide open and in the bright light of the day, two figure’s silhouettes are outlined in the immense light. Before Heresian Bloodlust can react to the intrusion, a similar thumping sound begins pulsating through the floor of the chamber. As the two figures in the doorway slowly make their way into the chamber, the roof begins cracking, sending little flakes of debris raining down on the four Heretics. The thumping gets more intense, and the cracks turn to little holes, and finally the roof caves in over the sarcophagus. Azrael rushes to cover Caleb, whilst Hessian throws the sarcophagus lid over Minion, and shields himself from the falling rubble. The sunlight shines through the ceiling, and silhouetted in the light is another figure. With an intense roar it jumps through the roof and lands on the floor in front of Hessian and Azrael Cain. The two satanic giants stand side-by-side as the three, silhouetted figures walk into the chamber, sending them backing off to the back wall. As they close in, Hessian and Azrael suddenly feel the floor rumbling, and in all the debris and immense light the floor falls in and out from the basement leaps a massive figure. Hessian looks the dark figure over as he jumps in front of the other three, silhouetted figures to join them in their shadowy state. As they close in on Heresian Bloodlust, the silhouettes suddenly vanish as the light fades, and Heresian Bloodlust stare in awe at who stands before them. A sudden flush of rage passes through their veins and they charge forward, and the chamber is filled with an animalistic roaring. The four figures leap at Heresian Bloodlust, and Hessian and Azrael retaliate similarly, however as they fly through mid-air en route to a collision, a sudden burst of light fills the chamber, and everything goes black. Sound, vision, everything is silenced, and finally, the opening of a pair of eyes… Hessian sits up, and stretches his body, grimacing at the sickening sound of bones and muscle stretching and cracking to life. Hessian looks across the dying flames of a little fire, to the two bodies of Cain and Caleb, lying sleeping comfortably on the warm, concrete floor of Azrael’s dilapidated factory. Seconds later Minion appears from behind Hessian, while Azrael releases a roaring yawn, and sits up, dazedly. Minion the Imp plops down next to the remaining embers of the fire and warms his little stubby Imp hands. Caleb, as always, remains motionless and unconscious. Azrael nips up on his feet, while Hessian uses the more conventional method of pushing himself up. Both members of Heresian Bloodlust stretch the wear and tear of a rough night from their hulking frames, and then look into the flames. ”A fine Nightmare that was………whatever happens………we shan’t trust anyone but ourselves…yes?” says Hessian in a slightly slurred and tired fashion. ”Our master has given us a premonition, you should thank him for not lashing you with an internal pain that cannot be stopped………” replies Azrael, bringing himself to a fully alert consciousness. ”Of course Cain, however before that happens, we should have a little fun decimating the infidels first………let the carnage begin?” ”Let The Carnage Begin………” Fin
