Erik Estrada is Who?

24 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Feb 24th So many times we look inside of ourselves to see what beats in the chest of a man. Is it the courage, the heart, the pure determination? For some that may be all, but for the very few with the deep competitive spirit flowing through them like the very blood that flows in their veins, it is a different story. “Can you believe it? Of all people I expected to win that thing, it was that guy...hell I cant even remember his name!” Timo Bolamba ranted in his living room as he casually flicked the channel changer. “Timo, just let it go, you will get other chances you know” came the voice of Gwenneth Cross as she applied an ice pack to the back of his head. “Damnit Timo, hold still, you are squirming like a six year old!” “It isn’t right!” the Samoan stated. “I can’t go around whomping everyone I see with a wrench and get away with it. I tell you what, that bastard is going to pay, and pay dearly.” Gwenneth leaned over and moved the light a little closer to examine the lump that had grown upon Timo’s head. She sighed a bit and moved his hair around to check for any cuts, he was lucky, there was just one nasty goose egg. The Samoan flipped the channel again and put the clicker down. He had really taken about all he could with sneak attacks and jackasses lately, and Timo finally had just got sick of it. Now he was in OSW and yet these cowardly acts continued. Timo expected this kind of crap from Ivan, but who was this Switchblade guy to attack him? “You know what?” asked Timo, the Samoan turning his head as he spoke. “What now?” was the reply from a slightly annoyed Gwenneth. “I think I can win if I get to that title shot this time.” Timo said. “I have a strong feeling about this one, something like all the people telling me in my heart I can do it. This place is different somehow, maybe its just the atmosphere.” “Maybe it’s just the fans cheering for you.” Timo sat still for a minute and pondered what she had said. Maybe it was just the fans cheering him on. He had the match won, that much was clear. It was merely a point of asking himself whether or not he wanted it bad enough. The Samoan gritted his teeth a bit as Gwenn hit a tender spot on his head. He tried to keep his eyes on the television at this point and let her do what she was doing, pain was no big issue, the scars on his body proved he had received worse. The whole problem is that sometimes the worst pains are that of being beaten unfairly. “Quit squirming Timo, you would think that for a guy who gets beat up every night in a ring you could take a lump to the head,” she scolded. “You need to just watch your show and settle down. Can you do that?” The theme song from CHIPS plays and Timo chuckles. He had recently developed an addiction to these cult classic shows, mostly things that people like Snow and Toomes would never understand. The Samoan laughed as Ponch took a dive into a lake on his bike. “The Ponch, always hot dogging it,” Timo smiled. “Kinda reminds me of me in a way. I bet if they put Poncharello in the ring against Ivan he would kick his commie ass all over the California border. Think of the ratings Gwennie, Ponch vs. Ivan. Its classic.” “Timo, I don’t know if you remember much about Ivan, but he is the guy who destroyed your car, I highly doubt Erik Estrada has a chance.” said Gwenneth. “Who?” She sighs a bit and thwaps him on the melon on his bruised spot for good measure. Timo shrieks a bit as she does and turns around all quick like to stare at her as she laughs. Damned Samoan goofball, that’s what you get for general idiocy. “What the hell was that for?” Asked the enraged Samoan. “Quit making an ass of yourself Timo, Erik Estrada is the guy who plays Ponch,” she sighs. “I thought you knew that.” Timo looks at the TV, then back at her. Was she crazy, this dude was forever branded as Ponch, no matter what anyone says. Besides, who ever said a guy needed to know somebody’s name for them to be cool? Its like an unwritten law! “I think you are crazy Gwenn, look at the guy, he just SCREAMS Ponch!” stated Timo. “Come on, Erik Estrada? Please…that name is for Hawaiians.” Timo turned back to doing what he was doing, watching the tube, and he began to think about his first night in OSW. It really felt good to be in a fed with so much promise again. In PCW, things had been just one bad step after another, and every time he got back up. Timo refused to stay down, even now he had a new enemy on his first night. Bastard uses a pipe wrench, what a coward. They will have a reckoning though in the ring, that is for sure. He flips the channel and it lands on a show about muscle cars. The Samoan smiles and leans back, making Gwenneth realize he has finally held still for more than ten seconds. She applies the ice pack on his head and holds it for him, as he appears to be in a trance like state watching a show about Hemi’s. “So what does the future hold for you dear?” Gwenneth asks nonchalantly. “Do you realize you have a match against Toomes this coming SLAM!?” Timo looks up at her quickly. “Eh?” He looks back down and gets a silly grin as the Cuda’ on the screen peels out and roars down the road. Timo gets a funny look on his face, and a bit of drool running down his chin. Gwenneth shakes her head and laughs. “You know, the guy who waffled you with the wrench?” She sounded concerned. “Timo, you need to watch yourself, you are getting hurt a lot lately. I don’t want to see you lose your career over a guy like Sebastion Toomes.” “Damnit, I told you already, its not a Tumor, its just a lump!” He says as he looks up and realizes she isn’t smiling. “I guess you are right. Maybe I should take this seriously. What I want to know is how he was allowed to get out of the ring in the first place, I don’t remember that rule.” She leans down and shuts the television off. He looks back to her and slides around to pay attention to Gwenn now. She was coming along nicely now, nearly 6 months pregnant, and still as beautiful as ever. Sometimes it was a wonder she put up with him. “There are a lot of things you don’t remember Timo.” She leans in close to him and he grasps her in his arms as she talks. “You were hit by a wrench in the head, if it wasn’t for your four inch…” “Four and three fourths babe, get it right” “Four and three fourths inch thick cranium, you would have lost a lot more than the memory of Toome’s name,” she says to him softly. “Face it Timo, you need to start trying to go that extra mile again, this is OSW, not some rinky dink operation run by people that stand on street corners for a living.” “Babe, are you bashing Tyler Nelson again?” Timo smiles to her. “Actually I was talking about Freddy Ryder, but yeah Tyler has probably done his share of cock sucking.” She sounded almost pleased to say it. “I heard he had AIDS…is that true?” Timo shrugs and looks over to the coffee table next to him. He reaches out and grabs a magazine off of it that is full of old cars. The Samoa looks up into his girls eyes and chuckles. “You know, I never really got the chance to ask him,” he snickered as he spoke. “I am sure Duke had good reason to say it though. You know, I’m going to miss working for that whiskey drinking hell raiser. Did I ever spoke to you about the time we drank a beer on the Malice show?” “About a million times dear.” “Right…well lets brighten the mood and buy something.” He opens the book as he says it, and points to a 1970 Hemi’ Cuda. “What do you think Gwenn? Plum Crazy! They didn’t make a lot of these you know. How would you like to scream down the road in this baby?” “Timo, it’s your money, do what you want dear.” She got up and stretched a bit. “So you remember what you have to do this week?” “Buy a new car…I got it.” “No! You have to get ready for OSW SLAM!” She shook her head and smiled. “ Timo, I don’t think you will ever learn.” “Probably not, but then again, would you have it any other way?” Timo shot off his million dollar smile as he spoke to her. “Never dear, you just keep on being you for as long as you can, sooner or later your going to strike gold.” She walked out of the room. Why later? The sooner was upon OSW, and with a renewed vigor, The Samoan Silencer set out to do what he had never done before, bring home the world title. The only thing standing in his way was Sebastion Toomes, but with a little luck, and a ¾ inch drive torque wrench, the road was clear. The scene fades to black and we all have to wonder one thing… “Who am I facing again on SLAM! Gwennie?”
