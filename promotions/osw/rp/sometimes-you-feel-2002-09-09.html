Sometimes you feel...

9 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 9th, 2002 ...like you want to scream. HONK! Boomer rushed underneath the clothing rack and knocked it over as he made his way towards the shoe section. The little bugger waddled pretty fast for a sea creature. Tom Lexian tripped over himself as he pursued. He looked up only to see shoes flying in every which direction and people huddling for cover. 'Oh god no,' Lexian murmured to himself. A store clerk, a nerdy looking guy with wide brimmed glasses and a tucked in pink shirt, stared in horror. He looked down at Tom and put his hands on his hips in a very gayish fashion. 'Is that your MONSTER roaming around in MY store?' He demanded. Tom bit his lower lip, remembering that you shouldn't start off the week with a fight. 'Uhm, yes, that penguin is my manager. Not monster, but my manager.' 'Well shouldn't he have like...like a leash or something? I mean, this is like...like a store, you know?' Thanks for the info, Captain Obvious. Maybe next time you can describe to me the color of the sky. Tom brushed himself off and helped up an older lady who had fallen over in the collision with the clothes rack. She thanked him and began mumbling something about those crazy porcupines rampaging around the city. Porcupines sort of look like penguins, right? 'Listen bud,' Tom began to say to the clerk. 'I know that my penguin is causing some trouble, but I swear to you I have everything under control. I will handle this.' The clerk looked at the carnage around him, sweat glistening on his forehead. If you looked closely, you could almost see the beginning of a pimple break out on his face. 'What about this mess?' He cried. 'I'm going to have to clean this all up?' SHOVE! The clerk went flying into another clothing rack as Mike Extreme entered the scene wielding nothing less than a steel pipe with a nail taped onto the front of it. 'What the hell is that?' Tom exclaimed, viewing the weapon with open disdain. 'What does it look like?' Mike responded sarcastically. 'I'm going to find Boomer and put an end to the madness.' 'Put an end to the madness? Have you gone mad? That's our manager!' Mike sighed and kicked over a pile of shirts. 'You see what I mean? Look at what he's done? He's a PENGUIN! Not a manager, Tom! But an animal that belongs in the snow playing with polar bears and killer whales.' 'He's ours!' Mike slapped Tom in the back of the head. 'Get it through your skull, Tom. This thing is embarrassing us.' Another scream yelled out as Boomer chased a woman down an aisle who was holding a very expensive looking glass vase. 'GO BOOMIE! GO!' Tom yelled out. 'He's at the forty...the thirty...the twenty...the ten...HE...COULD...GO...ALL..THE...WAY!' CRASH! 'TOUCHDOWN!' Tom exclaimed, throwing his arms in the air and dancing around. He stopped as soon as he saw an unamused Mike Extreme look. 'Would you stop being ridiculous?' Tom stopped. 'You can't hit Boomie with that...that...' Tom was at a loss for words to describe the weapon. '...that pipe with a nail glued onto it.' 'It's taped on, moron.' 'Taped or glued, it still looks stupid.' Mike dropped the weapon and crossed his arms. 'So what do you suggest we do? Try to reason with him? Tom, all he does is break things.' 'So we'll lead him somewhere else.' 'Where else?' Mike asked. 'He seems to be having a great time here.' Tom looked around the store, trying to find something that would catch Boomer's attention. Then in a flash of brilliance, he knew just the thing that would get him... 'Hey Boomie!' Tom yelled out. The penguin stopped his mad spree, if just for a moment. 'Look at that GLASS window!' Zoooooooooooom! CRASH! Boomer sped out from the now shattered window and into the parking lot where a series of car honks and beeps could be heard. Mike fell to the floor in shock as Tom merely shrugged. 'It was better than your nail on a pipe trick.'
