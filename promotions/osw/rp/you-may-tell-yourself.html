CLICK! On grainy 8mm film, we see Sebastian Toomes, with long dark hair and in street clothes, accompanied by Jimmy The Dog Man. Toomes is holding a yarmulke, and is looking at it intensely, jabbering away on the film. “Do you remember this, Sebastian?” a voice calls out. On the film, Sebastian puts on the yarmulke and dances around, screaming “I’M KING OF THE JEWS! I’M KING OF THE JEWS!” Sebastian’s groggy voice mutters a response. “Yeah… Heh… That was good.” CLICK! The film changes to Toomes in a toy store, looking for a copy of his action figure… Indeed, his very first promo in CCW. The film changes to Toomes holding a makeshift Brad Lamen Thumb Wrestler, and Toomes chatters with it incessantly. “Do you remember doing this?” “Yeah…” Toomes says weakly. “That was back when I first broke into CCW…” “It’s good stuff.” The voice intones. “No… It sucks… All my promos in CCW sucked.” “Hrmm… Correct. Nevertheless, they were a far sight better than what you’re doing now.” CLICK! The film changes to Toomes leading a mob of handicapped people picketing a CCW show, aimed at “The Crippler” Chris Shaw. CLICK! Jimmy The Dog Man and Switchblade in a Starsky and Hutch type of setting, walking and jiving to the Shaft theme song, making fun of Black Vader. CLICK! Toomes absently hitting a drunken frat boy while he’s cutting a promo in the French Quarter. CLICK! A segment from Toomes brief and failed attempt to get an angle going where he would be obligated to ‘feed’ the Spanish Announce Table every match. In this segment, Toomes is telling Jimmy about his dream where a legion of Mexican people and Mariachis sent him to see their deity, the Spanish Announce Table. CLICK! A shot of Toomes making Jimmy The Dog Man jump up and down on picnic tables inside of a Home Depot, so that he might find out how suitable the tables would be in a Table Match. “Gaah! That’s enough!” Toomes whines. The projector goes off, and the lights flicker on. Jimmy The Dog Man, wearing a Lacoste t-shirt (with the little alligator on them) and khaki shorts, his coarse black body hair sprouting at every inch of his exposed skin, comes into view. “Sebastian… You need help. Serious help.” We see Toomes, who is bound to a metal folding chair, with Rod Thicke and Diez Nutz, Sebastian’s erstwhile Interracial Porn Star buddies, flanking him so he can’t be crafty and make his escape. “What are you talking about?” Toomes moans. “I’m the OSW World Champion! What could I possibly need help with? Besides the frequency of my promos?” Jimmy sighs, as if trying to explain a concept to a stupid child. “Look. The general public cannot watch your promos without sending their kids into another room, lest they see you perform some sort of obscene act! You know that your mother is ashamed of you, right?” Toomes grins. “But she should be happy for me! I’ve found true love!” Three simulanous sighs fill the room. “Well…At least I’m enjoying myself!” Jimmy continues. “Your first promos in CCW were funny. Humorous, jack-ass in nature, and generally harmless. You talked trash, had a cute little setting or gimmick, and at least I got some recognition out of the deal. And they worked. They were funny and effective. They wouldn’t cut the mustard in the PTC, but who cares? CCW fans liked them. But when you got frustrated and went to PWA, everything changed. You started putting quantity over…well, questionable quality, and you just have been going downhill ever since. The Gay Manager dude? Dude, he’s a fucking blight on your career! Did you notice that he made a promo for the Global Tourney where he was jerking off to your Independent Title match? And marking out for your opponent?” Toomes shrugs. “So?” Jimmy throws his hands up in exasperation. “Dude, he was JERKING OFF in a promo! GAY MASTURBATION IS A BAD THING, SEBASTIAN! Don’t you get it? You’ve gone and did musicals about cumming on your boss’s face…” “Oh, I went ahead and made that fantasy a reality.” Toomes grins guiltily. “You’ve put your crotch on people, you’ve been caught jerking off to pictures of your valet, who might I add is a total bitch and has yet to do anything meaningful on Slam? She shows up in your promos to act as a foil, but that’s it. When she comes out for matches, she’s useless. You have all this extraneous crap now that you’re in OSW, and you need to get back to what made you effective. And funny.” Rod Thicke stretches his arms a bit, making a cracking noise. “Word. And those two other niggas you hang with sometimes… Drew Hostyle and Frankie Bones… What the fuck is up wif dat? I thought we were your token black friends!” “True dat, my White Bitch Fucking Nigga in Arms!” Diez Nutz says in agreement. “And you haven’t used us in a promo other than window dressing since December! There’s a couple of bitches I have yet to stretch out in the PTC, and you haven’t given us any play! Nigga just tryin’ to get his Interracial Groove on, you gotta cockblock a brutha!” he complains. Toomes shakes his head mournfully. “Look guys. It’s been very busy. And The Gimmick Man was very specific that I try a new tack when I signed with PWA. And when I signed with OSW, you guys weren’t part of the contract. Did you ever wonder why I let you just linger at my apartment without having to pay me rent?” Rod wonders aloud. “So we could fuck dippy white bitches who like to piss off their parents that they’re getting split by big black cock?” Diez ponders the question. “So I have a place to stay while the police search my girlfriend’s apartment for coke with the police dogs?” Jimmy nods. “Or is it because you have no use for us at the moment, yet we’re still mentioned in your bio. Is that it?” Toomes nods solemnly. “Well, I couldn’t just convienatly forget you guys when I jumped feds! I had to do something with you guys!” Rod looks indignant. “Nigga, I coulda been fucking white bitches left and right while I was waiting for the call so I could be in an OSW Promo!” Diez taps Rod on the shoulder. “Nigga, you WERE fucking white bitches left and right.” “Oh yeah. Sweet.” “Nevermind that now!” Jimmy yelps impatiently. “We need to get your promos back on track…” Toomes fixes Jimmy with a stare. “Dude… Come on. If you three wanted to be back in my promos, you coulda just said so. You didn’t have to kidnap me and go through this elaborate scheme to make me feel guilty. You coulda sent a porno with the three of you guys fucking some co-ed and I would of felt a little left out. I mean, I like fucking co-eds as much as the next guy, but when you’re fucking some college chick who needs the money for books for the next semester with your friends, it means so much more…” Rod sniffles. Diez smiles sadly. “Those were the days, nigga…” Jimmy The Dog Man sniffles as well, tears filled his eyes. “Really? You really enjoyed it when you three were fucking the girl and I snuck out of the closet and tried to get her to suck my dick and she started crying?” Sebastian nods sincerely. “I wouldn’t of traded it for all the times I came on a woman’s face.” “Ah, you big lug! Untie him, guys!” The two black porn stars rush to loosen Sebastian’s bonds. “Yay!” they both cheer. The effect, coming from two 6’5, 250 lb black men is quite odd. The scene fades to black as the three lock arms and skip down the street into the sunset. (OOC- Hey, I had to do something with them sometime)
