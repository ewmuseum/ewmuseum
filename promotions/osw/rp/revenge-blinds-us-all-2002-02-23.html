Revenge blinds us all...

23 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/23/02 Frankie wakes, rising slowly and groggily. He rubs his bald head as he turns his head to look at the clock, it reads 1:30. 'Sleep the damn day away' mumbles Frankie as he sits up in bed. He sits, looking out of his terrible view of Brooklyn from his shitty apartment. His eyes pierce the horizon and his mind wanders. Straying away from his usual thoughts on wrestling and anger. Straying to thoughts of longing, and again more anger. Thoughts of Hardy, and where the hell he is. Frankie has decided beyond a shadow of a doubt that it all comes back to him. Nobody would just kidnap Hardy, they had to be trying to get to Frankie. Frankie's thoughts are very clear... 'God I hope this works. The sheer number of people who watch OSW has to help the cause. Someone watching has to know something. And knowing the heat is on him will have to worry whoever took the kid. But I can't shake one impending thought. That it is someone associated with PTC. Through my career here I have ran into many a crazy foe. And I have upset them all. Never holding back, never sugar coating, never pulling punches, am I paying for these indiscretions? Have I gotten a kid killed over what I have done? Even if he's not dead, he's definitely been through some shit. But I hope it's not someone in OSW, or PTC for that matter. I will kill them when I find them, and I saw when on purpose. This will not end until there is closure. I cannot stand this weight on my conscience.' Frankie's mind continues with these thoughts all afternoon. As he makes some lunch. As he tries to watch T.V. He cannot shake the thoughts of the kid. And with it, the thoughts of the kidnapper and what will happen to them. He cannot shake the thought of it being someone close to him, someone in the wrestling world. He yearns for this to pan out quickly, for closure, and most importantly, for revenge. There is a man that deserves this revenge, and Frankie will make it happen. Revenge. It sits heavy on Frankie's mind for the rest of the day. Catching up to Frankie much later in the day, it is apparent that his mood has changed. It has gone from somber to almost jovial. But it is also apparent that revenge is all he has thought about all day. His downward spiral is gaining speed. His mind is clouded with thoughts of revenge, and he loves it. Is this all a preconceived reality? He squashes this thought with a quickness. He will not allow his mind to turn inward yet again, that is what has gotten him in trouble in the past. And as Frankie is all to aware, the past is best left alone...
