Waiting Is The Hardest Part

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lights… Camera… FLASH The sound of cheers, the sounds of tires squealing, the sound of metal smashing against concrete… Jane being hauled away, her forehead bloody, the arc-sodium lights reflecting off the shattered safety glass, a sea of diamonds set against the wine-colored upholstery. The rotating lights of the ambulance reflecting off the impacted sheet metal. All the while, he was in the ring, showboating and doing his thing, instead of being where he should have been, namely, her side. I-Cybies, wrestling matches, little brush-fire wars backstage, smelly bikers… these things were minor, ephemeral… He should have been taking care of her… He should have been taking care of her... He waits in the corridors of the Saint Joseph East hospital, anxiously walking up and down the length of the waiting room, his gaze going over the subdued colors and the three-week old People magazines, dog-eared and rumpled from anxious people much like himself. This Vax guy… It had to be The Phantom Booker. It had to be. He hated her for having an affair with Toomes, and for rejecting him for PCW last year. And now he was having his revenge. It had to be like that. Who else would be after Jane? And who else but a spurned husband execute something like that with such murderous precision? He wasn’t even told how badly she was hurt. All he saw was the footage… Toomes wanders over to the reception area, his hands finding the lip of the Formica counter, his hair a mess, falling down over his face in thick strands. The receptionist looks at him, repressing a frown. “Can I help you, sir?” she says, her 25 dollar an hour ass in white linen sitting on a blueberry chair, her ballpoint pen roaming over a form of some sort. Toomes lets out a pent-up breath. “Can you tell me the condition of Jane Reagan? She was admitted about an hour ago…” The nurse flits up her black eyes at him and looks back down at a clipboard, her eyes roaming over some hand-writing. “Are you a relative or the spouse, sir?” Clenched teeth. “No.” She smiles tightly. “I’m sorry, sir, I can’t release that information to non-family members. Can you try back later?” That Southern twang. His hands wrap against the edge of the counter, pressure building. How could this dippy bitch understand? He felt like pistoning his fist out, smashing her face, breaking the skin of his knuckles on her fractured jaw, on her teeth, her blood spewing in a high-pressure jet-stream all over her precious papers and coffee cup, her precious fucking latte she got from Starbucks down at the local fucking strip mall next to the tanning salon and the fucking dry-cleaners FUCK! FUCK! FUCK! “Thank you…” he chokes out, turning away, clenching his fists. Turn, throw the punch, smash her face. How the FUCK can she just sit there and deny him? No, keep walking, walk to the Snapple machine. Get a Snapple. Relax. Look at your reflection in the glossy tile. You’re tired, exhausted… Turn away, slump against the wall. Wait…that’s all you can do but wait… Her beautiful body, contorted and smashed in a car wreck… The body he ran his tongue over just hours ago, now bloody and damaged. GODDAMNIT! His fist hit the white corridor wall, his knuckles impacting along the drywall. Grimacing, he turns away, looking at the people who turned thier heads to stare at him, thier mouths gaping. 'Look away... Look away...' Toomes mutters, turning away and making his way back down the hall. Things were moving fast. The Phantom Booker was making his move. Tonight was proof of this. The biker. CCW coming into OSW and making thier move. Everything was coming to a head. Time to be a little pro-active.
