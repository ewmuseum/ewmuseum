Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/14/02 Frankie lies in his bed, another sleepless night. His mind swells, in need of venting. He reaches over and turns on a lamp. He grabs his new homemade diary and a pen. He opens it, takes a deep breath and starts writing... Hey yo I know you ain't talkin' about me dog. You? What? You've been on my dick nigga, you love my style. I... Fuck with ya soul like either... Will... Teach you to know your place... Not... Pain written across the belly... Lose... I prove you lost already... Brace yo'self, for the main event, Ya'll impatiently waitin, It's like an AIDS test, What's the results? Not positive, Who's the best? Drew, Bones, and Blade. We da best, East, west, north, south, Flossed out. Greedy, I embrace you with napalm. Blows up, no guts left, Chest, face gone. How can Bones be garbage? Got gats aimin' for ya cartlidge. Burner at tha side of ya dome, Step off my throne. I got this locked since day one, I am the trueist, Name a wrestler that, I ain't influenced. Gave ya'll matches, But now I keep my eye out for Judas. Check it. I... Fuck with ya soul like either... Will... Teach you to know your place... Not... Pain written across the belly.. Lose... I prove you lost already. I've been fucked over, Left for dead, Dissed and forgotten. Luck ran out, They hoped that I'd be gone, stiff, and rotten, Y'all just, piss on me, Shit on me, Spit on my grave. Talk about me, Laugh behind my back But in my face, Y'all some well wishin', Two faced, envy hidin' snakes. Heard it in my sleep, That these gay commies, red bitches wanted beef. Started cockin up my weapons, Steady loadin' up this ammo. To explode it, on a commie, And his soldiers I can handle this. But Marx, and his manuscript. Just sound stupid when, Everyone knows russians can't Handle it they stupid. Pain written on tha belly, Prove that you lost already. You seem to be concerned with defending your past with women, Were you abused as a child? Scared to smile, They called you ugly. Well life is harsh hug me, Don't reject me, Or cut promos to disrespect me, Blatant or indirectly. You a fan, A phony, A fake, A pussy, A mark. I'll still whip you ass, You 41 and still in a karate class? Tae-Bo hoe. Tryin' to work it out, You try to get violent, Ask me if I'm tryin' to kick knowledge? Na, I'm kickin' tha shit you need to learn though, That either, tha shit that make your soul burn slow. Are you Ruslan's diddy, Ruslan's daddy, Or Ruslan's dummy? Oh I get it, You're just two gay Ruskies. The Red Army got killed, That was the end of their chapter, And that's the thing you choose to name your cheesy group after? Put it together, I rule the world, You rule cold weather. And now y'all trying take my spot fellas? Brooklyn's hot rock fellas, Put you in a dry spot fellas, In a pine box, With nine shots, From my glock fellas. Tempest got you hot cause the contest was booked, And you think you gettin' chicks now cause of ya looks? Com-mie please. You no mustache havin', With whiskas like a rat, Compared to me you wack. Red Ar-my, Get gunned up in clap quick, Ivan Stanislav, Get gunned up in clap quick, Your whole damn country, GOT gunned up in clap quick, A Russian wrestler? Damn you on Volkoff's dick, How many of his promo's gonna come out your fat lips? Wanted to be in every single one of my classics, You punk shit, apologize nigga just ask Calyx. He view cuts back to Frankie face. A sly smirk curls his lips as he lies back down. He quickly falls asleep... fade...
