Family Matters

17 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-17-02 Ever wonder what the rain can do to a man? I know what it can do. It can make him shiver and it can make him cry but in some men it entices anger, frustration and most of all…violence. Most men feel the first I spoke of but in a few men like myself we feel the latter. To most people I am just a misunderstood minstrel of my own creation. But there are few people, like Graphic Violence, who understand my pain and frustration. Before we make the most fearsome Tag Team in the business we where two guys trying to make a name for himself. GV, doing a better job then I, moved from promotion to promotion and made a huge name for himself. But I was stuck in places like Tornado Wrestling2k fighting men like Marauder. Now I am not saying Marauder wasn’t a force to be reckoned with but there is just so much of Marcus that one can stand. Last Saturday I stepped into a new world, a world of the past, but is it? Old School Wrestling seems to have a few flavors of the past but more often then not they seem like another federation trying to spice up their game. My thoughts come in spurts and this was on of them. The drizzle broke through the night making it more miserable then any night that Jessica Andrews could remember. Jessica had been without her husband for 3 years now and wondered if he would ever return home. After make a trip back to Tibet, to find out more about his past, he just disappeared. Now with control of her maiden name again she could start her life over with her young daughter April. April at this time was 10 years of age and had been many years since her father and mother met on a stormy night just like tonight. As Jessica made her way up her front porch she wondered what that tingle on her neck met. It happened 11 years ago when she met him on a night just like tonight but it couldn’t be, could it? Jessica turned around and peered down the empty street with no soul in sight. She sighed before unlocking the door and entering the house. “Thank god, I don’t know what would happened if he showed up in my life again.” Jessica said as she placed an arm full of groceries on the table. Little April ran off into the living room and turned on the TV to one of those non-important kiddy shoes. Jessica began putting things away when she spotted a picture of her and him on the fridge and a tear rolled from her eye. “Not now, not tonight. Kc stay out of my life.” “It would be a pain if he showed up again wouldn’t it?” a voice from the den said. Jessica’s face turned white and she turned to met his eyes. He was wearing that trench coat he met her in but his face and expressions where different. “You look like you have seen a ghost Jess.” “Kc you disappeared and April and I had to move on in our lives!” Jessica screamed as the tears ran down from her eyes. “We thought you where dead and we had to move on, we never thought you would return! “Tisk, tisk my dear Jessica. I didn’t come back for you or your sympathy and I defiantly didn’t come back for the sex. I only want on thing from you, my life back.” Jessica looked at Kc with a confused look on his face. She had no idea what he wanted but what ever it was it would end her life forever. “What do you want?” A stern look came across Kc’s face and he looked towards the living room and then it became clear on what he wanted. “No, you can’t!” “I can and I will. Someday she will disappear from you life and she will began that a new with her father.” Kc turned towards the door stopping to look back at Jessica’s tears. “And get it right bitch, the name is Silence.” With that Silence turned and slammed the door on his way out leaving his wife to cry and bear the burden of losing her child one day. Spurts, that’s all I can think in. My darling wife will soon see what happens when you piss off the hubby. My sweet April how you long to be with your father. I know your slut of a mother will try and shield you from ever knowing who I am but get ready because I will be bringing you home soon. Ah, my brain is shutting down again but I am certain it will boot up again soon and when it does you will be the first to know.
