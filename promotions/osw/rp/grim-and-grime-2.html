Grim and Grime

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The sun managed to eek its way through the nooks and crannies of the dark alley, filtering down through broken window panes and rotting wood. The alley that had been so forbidding the night before now revealed its true nature. It was a place frightening only in respect to its decrepitude. The real extent of its filthy nature is now revealed by the controlling sun, as the old decaying warehouses creak and settle in the breeze, moaning in agony from their solemn surroundings. Kimbusa awoke in those same surroundings. His eyes slowly open to mere slits, the light from the sun burning Kimbusa's eyes. His hat had fallen from his face hours ago, and was now saturated in a thick black puddle too revolting for even Kimbusa to fetch it to safety. Kimbusa, disoriented from a night spent huddled next to a concrete wall, slowly remembers the man in the newspapers across the alley. Kimbusa looks across the way to find only a bare patch of dark, wet pavement. The man had moved on, and taken his bed with him. Car horns begin to sound in the distance, a welcome assurance to Kimbusa that he is not far from the real world. But for the moment, he is not quite done with his miserable experience. Kimbusa slowly pushes himself upward, managing to rise to his feet after some difficulty. He painfully stretches out the stiffness in his arms and back, noticeably uncomfortable but not minding the pain. After a moment he looks below, and sees for the first time the extent of the filth he had just risen from. He had spent the night hunched in a corner next to a dead bird, its skull crushed from the impact on a window pane above, its carcass already half eaten by insects. This was the smell Kimbusa had experienced the night before, and only now did he realize how close he was to the source. Kimbusa flips back his jacket sleeve to look at his watch, but finds it gone. Slightly dismayed, he turns his head to the sky, finding the sun almost directly overhead now. Noon. Had he really slept through the entire morning? Shaking his head slightly, Kimbusa begins to move out of the alleyway. His usual shuffling steps are a distant memory, now replaced by a confident and perhaps even angry stomp, as the massive dark figure moves out of the old alleyway. A few hundred yards later, Kimbusa emerges from the alley. Again his eyes are struck by the hard pains of bright sunlight. Cars whizz by quickly on the busy street in front of him, huge masses of people weave in and out, attempting to sidestep the massive roadblock that has now made its way onto the sidewalk. Kimbusa glances casually at the faces of the American people as they pass. Most of them turn up their noses and attempt to pass him at as great a distance as possible. At first Kimbusa assumed that the Americans were afraid of him, before coming to the humble conclusion that it was, in fact, the awful stench rising from his coat. Kimbusa unties the leather straps from his chest and pulls off the jacket, revealing a black shirt and a massive pair of jeans below. He tosses the jacket back into the alleyway, revolting to the people on the street but a treasure to those who dwell beyond. Kimbusa looks upward to the skyscrapers above. The buildings encompassing his alleyway are, in fact, taken great care of on the sides that are on display. The building on the corner of two busy streets has been very carefully preserved on the outer walls, proving that the building was not abandoned at all. The occupants had merely been satisfied to be clean and presentable on the outside, and were content to keep their true nature concealed in a dark realm that most will not traverse... Kimbusa looked at the building, and saw everything he hated about this new country. He saw a world where the only beatuy that mattered was the kind you could see. A world where real truths were hidden and only the most courageous of travelers could venture far enough to find them. A world where money ruled all. A world where those who had it walked confidently through the sun, while those who didn't slept in the filth and darkness... Two totally different realms, all just inches apart. It was Kimbusa who now turned up his nose. He had left the stench behind, he was now among the higher class of America. But in retrospect, Kimbusa had already preferred the company of the filthy old man, even if he had stolen his watch while he slept. Kimbusa stepped off the sidewalk and hailed a taxi. Rummaging through his pockets, Kimbusa found a few spare American bills, and waited for someone to stop. Several empty cabs passed him by, perhaps refusing to stop and serve someone of his size. Yet more taxis pass, and Kimbusa finally begins to get the picture. He turns back to the walk, and looks across the street. A subway station was just a few feet away. Perhaps he'd have to go underground to get to the airport....
