A Black Sabbath

24 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/24/02 The night air is completely silent. There is nothing that can be heard in this part of the city at this hour of night. This is a different neighborhood then what is usually described in these tales. There is last trash on the streets. Not just the litter, but also the trash in the kind that describing the people that usually fill the streets of the other part of town. The streets are lit better than the slums of old. This part of town is slightly more upper scale than the old neighborhood. The silent remains only until an all too familiar high pitched gravely voice pierces through the quiet night air. 'You're going to love the place that I got for us! Heh-Heh. I'm so glad that you have decided to get us out of that slum that we have been in for the last six months. Heh-Heh. It's about time that you start living like the warrior that you truly are! Heh-Heh-Heh.' This voice belongs to none other than the tiny hobo known to all of the fans of OSW as Caleb. Of course, when Caleb is around Azrael Cain is not far behind. The small decrepit old man is walking about five feet in front of the marked beast. Cain is wearing his black and tattered cloak. His silky long black hair is down in his face. Over his shoulder his bear skin cloak is folded into a satchel and hanging from some rope. Also, across his broad back is his thirty pound sledge hammer that is hung in place by a piece of rope that is strapped across the front of his upper body. Caleb is wearing his traditional dirty and stained trench coat. His gray hair blows from the breeze of the night air. The two figures continues to move through the streets. As Caleb's thin hair flaps in the breeze, his mouth also continues to flap. 'You see, that place was not worthy of a champion like you. Heh-Heh. No way. That place was a real shit hole...' Caleb is cut short by Cain's massive hand reaches from behind and covers the toothless mouth of the hobo. Cain's massive hand spins the little man around in order to face him. Cain lowers his head to the hobo' s level. 'I hope that you do not think that I have forgotten about what you have been doing? You are by no means in my good favor right now and if it wasn't for Hessian, I would have pounded you into the earth by now! Consider yourself lucky!' Cain removes his hand from the hobo's mouth and then proceeds to walk right past the stunned hobo. Caleb looks on at the massive warrior in shock as he passes by. Caleb simply starts to follow after Cain in typical subservient manor. Both of them continue to walk for a awhile until something finally stops Caleb and causes him to clear his throat very loudly in an attempt to stop Cain from continuing down the street. Cain spins around quickly and looks on as Caleb is bowing in a sarcastic manor as he speaks. 'This is the place master. Heh-Heh-Heh.' Cain's eyes squint with rage as he looks at the hobo start to laugh at his own little attempt at a joke. Cain walks towards the laughing hobo and raises his head up at the building that is to be his new home. Cain's eyes widen with pleasure which is a very rare sight for this being that only knows anger, hatred and rage. His lips slowly part as he looks up at what will be his new domicile. 'And what a home it is.' The new domicile that Caleb has found for the warrior is a giant gothic cathedral that is standing in practical ruins. The high towers climb high into the sky. There is a huge circular window where an impressive stain glass window once was placed in order to glorify the Creator. Cain looks down at Caleb before he sprints up the massive steps that lead up to the giant double wooden doors of the cathedral. Cain's hands press against the heavy wooden doors. With very little effort from the beast the doors are swung open with great force. The wooden doors crash into the stone walls flinging dust up into the air. The loud crash noises causes several doves to fly into the air in fright. The interior is in as terrible shape as the exterior. Many of the pews are toppled over on top of each other. Many of the symbolic statues have been broken or shattered against the ground. Many of the painting on the walls have been covered and defiled with spray paint. 'The perfect sacrilege!' Cain states as he looks down the isle towards the north end of the cathedral where the alter is. Above the alter there is a crucifix that has been hung upside down mocking that sacrifice that the son of the Creator made so many centuries ago. Cain walks towards that alter as many of the doves are cooing in freight. Once Cain arrives in front of the alter, he drops his satchel to the ground as well as his sledgehammer that hits the stone floor with a loud clang noise that echoes throughout the sanctuary. Caleb scurries up beside the beast and lights some old candles that were on the floor with a silver Zippo lighter. The hobo turns to the beast and is disturbed by the look of contempt on Cain's face. The candle in his hand starts to shake as he inquires about Cain's reaction. 'You don't like the place after all? Heh-Heh.' Cain's eyes do not move from the face of the upside down statue of Christ. 'On the contrary my little friend. You have done quit well. This is exactly what I've needed. This is the ideal place for me to focus my energy on my hatred for this wretched plain of existence and the mortals that occupy it.' Caleb breathes a sigh of relief as the candle slowly begins to cease shaking in his hand. The hobo begins to pick up toppled candle sticks in an attempt to start lighting some of the candles. The marked beast reaches down and removes his title from his satchel. Cain's eyes still have not moved from the upside down crucifix even as he climbs the steps that lead up to the alter with his tag title in his right hand. Cain then places the belt on the alter on the alter in front Christ's eyes. Cain stalks over to the upside down crucifix he leans his head into the Christ statue's ear and starts to slowly whisper into its ears. 'Look upon that symbol of a champion. Once again I am an honored warrior in this raging battle, which you and your father are loosing. Your people lavish treasure upon me for destroying my enemies. More people watch the carnage that my partner and I unleash on your mortals each week then flock to worship you. They cheer the violence and each week my Master gets stronger and stronger from the growing bloodlust.' Caleb is now looking on with shocked look on his face as he sees what Cain is doing. The fallen one continues to speak to the crucifix without noticing his manager who is now looking on. 'Your time is running short. This week there will be millions and millions of your people watching me on your Sabbath instead of praising you. They are going to watch on as I destroy those two pitiful creatures Hessian and I are scheduled to face. This week I fight in celebration of your foolish sacrifice for these pathetic mortals. It was all for nothing. Prepare yourself, the end is near and there is nothing that you can do about it.' Cain sticks his tongue out only to lick the side of the statues face. The beast turns to the hobo. Cain crosses down off of the raised area where the crucifix and alter are and slowly approaches Caleb who is looking on in a disturbed manor. Cain's face is that of total determination the likes of which has yet to seen. 'You okay there big fella? Heh-Heh-Heh.' Caleb asks with an extremely tense tone in his voice. The look of determination remains on Cain's face as he replies. 'This is week Hessian and I go to battle for something to more than these belts. We go to war against the Creator himself. This Sabbath will be a day that no fan of OSW will ever forget. Heresian Bloodlust will destroy Krieg and Walczak this Sabbath only to create more bloodlust and hatred in this world. The reign of the Master continues on and this Sabbath will start a very dark reign of terror on all of OSW.' The small hobo is shaken by the way that Cain is behaving. Cain rolls his bear skin cloak out on the cod dank floor of the cathedral. The beast lays himself down on the cloak in order to sleep for the first time in his new domicile. Cain slowly lowers his eyes for a rest without saying a word. Caleb looks on in shock at the massive warrior. He then starts to glance around the cathedral taking in his new surroundings. As the hobo comes across the alter he sees the title belt on the alter glistening in the candlelight. Suddenly, there is an unusually large breeze that bellows through the cathedral. The flames flicker from the breeze. Caleb looks upon the upside down crucifix again, but this time something shocks him. There are tears of blood now flowing from the eyes of desecrated figure of Christ and drip to the floor. Another gust of wind causes all of the candles to go out. The cathedral is now in total darkness again.
