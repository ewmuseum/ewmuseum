Mysterious Happening

4 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-4-02 Injured. That is what “Bad Attitude” Jesse Ramey was after he was so brutally attacked after Slam this past week. After checking in with the doctors outside of Miami, Jesse was hasty to get moving on to Gulfport, Mississippi and what could go down as the greatest match in OSW history. While now we find ourselves inside of a waiting room, in yet another hospital. This one is located in Gulfport. A small little waiting room it is. We make our way out of the waiting room and down one of the halls. The tiles on the floor so slick and cold as we make our way past many people. Some looking deathly ill, while others seem to only be in for visiting. Through the Emergency Door and swerving into one of the rooms, we see a nurse and “Bad Attitude” Jesse Ramey. Jesse wearing his routine workout gear and his head still bandaged up. Jesse looks flustered. “Listen, lady. I need to know when I’m getting out of here.” Jesse says in a forceful manner. “I need to get back to my work out. I’ve got a match this weekend.” “Mr. Ramey, do you even recall what caused you to be put in here?” The nurse asks Jesse looking him right in the eyes. Jesse begins to ponder to himself, “Actually, um, no.” Jesse looks off and then back at the nurse. “It doesn’t matter though. I’m fine now and I’ll be going.” Jesse tries to stand up. The nurse puts her hand down on Jesse’s shoulder, “Oh, no,” she shakes her head, “You won’t be leaving.” She looks at the now very frustrated Jesse. “You’ll be able to leave when the doctor tells you that you’ll be able to go.” She makes Jesse look her in the eyes. “Now is that understood?” “Yes, ma’am.” Jesse says as he twists around on the bed and begins to breathe heavily with his arms crossed. The nurse walks over and sits down, “Now would you like for me to tell you just what happened with you and why you are here?” “Well, it wouldn’t hurt to know what just went wrong.” Jesse says. “Okay, well, then this is what I was told…” The nurse says as everything begins to blur into a past occurrence sequence. “Sixty-six…” Heavy breathing is heard, “Sixty-seven…” More heavy breathing is heard as blackness ends and Jesse can be seen doing pull-ups. “Sixty-eight…” Still, he has the bandage around his head and covering the wound. “Sixty-nine…” “Hey, aren’t you “Bad Attitude” Jesse Ramey?” Comes a voice from far off. Jesse drops from the bar and begins to wipe the sweat off his head, “Yeah, who’s asking?” A person comes in to view and this man is pretty big compared to Jesse who is also very well built. “I’m asking. Is that a problem?” Jesse walks over and tries to stand face to face with the man, but is obviously at least half a foot smaller than he is, so he just looks up. “Actually, it doesn’t bother me one bit.” Jesse says as he walks back over to the pull-up bar and jumps back up. “So, I heard you were only on what? Seventy? That’s pathetic.” The man laughs. “Seventy…” Jesse then begins to laugh. “Why are you laughing?” The man asks with a harsh sounding tone. “Seventy-one…” Jesse begins to breathe heavy, “Because…I’m…Working…On…My…Third…Set…Of…Pull…Ups…” Jesse musters out what he was trying to say. The man gets a strange look on his face, “What do you mean this is your third set of pull-ups?” “Eighty-two…” Jesse still breathing heavily has even more problems getting out what he wants to say, “I’ve…Been…Doing…Sets…Of…One…Hundred…” Jesse gets out. “So, what you are telling me is that you’re on two hundred and eighty-two pull-ups?” The man asks. “Nope…” Jesse says as he drops from the bar. “I just got done with three hundred.” Jesse says as he picks up his towel and wipes his forehead with it. “Take this water boy and clean it for me.” Jesse snickers as he tosses the towel into the chest of the man. The man just stands there in disbelief and then finally reacts to the now leaving Jesse. “Well, I bet you that I could beat you in a push up competition.” The man says with obvious revel. “I don’t sink that low buddy.” Jesse says, “By the way, you just saw how many pull-ups I did. Why in the hell would you want to do something as stupid as embarrass yourself like that?” The man begins to get very steamed as Jesse continues to make his way out of the gym and into another room. The locker room where out of nowhere a door swings open and knocks Jesse to the ground. The man comes storming into the locker room to get Jesse, “You listen here buddy.” The man points his finger at thin air. He looks around the room, “Where’d you go?” The door that was launched into Jesse’s face is now slammed shut, causing the man to jump. He then proceeds to the sound of the door and finds Jesse knocked out in the middle of the floor. “Oh, shit!” The man says in fear. He turns and begins to make his way out of the locker room. “Someone help!” He screams. “And that’s what he told us happened to you, Mr. Ramey.” Everything goes fuzzy until we come back into the hospital room where the nurse has just finished her story. Jesse is now sitting on the edge of his bed, “So, I didn’t like get some kind of blood rush to the head which caused me to pass out?” “No, you didn’t.” She says, “I was guessing you would have figured that out from the huge knot on your head.” “My head has been split open, what’s a knot going to make a difference. It hurts all the same.” Jesse pauses. “None!” The nurse looks at Jesse very unbelievably. “You Professional Wrestlers. I swear.” “What? What?” Jesse asks and comes to his feet, as does the nurse. “You’re all the same. You try to act all big and macho when you know you’re not. So, why don’t you just get over the fact that it does hurt?” She says. Jesse begins to think for a moment. “It doesn’t hurt lady. Trust me, if it hurt I believe I’d be the first person to notice that.” The nurse begins to roll her eyes as she makes her way out of the room, “Sure, Mr. Ramey. The doctor will be in to see you shortly.” Jesse flops back up on the bed and crosses his arms once more and begins to think to himself. “If I didn’t pass out and what she says is true, then maybe, just maybe the people behind all of this were the Masked Men.” Jesse begins to rub his chin. “Why though? Why me?” Jesse begins to question himself. “I’ve done a whole lot more than any of the others in OSW.” Jesse begins to chuckle to himself. “Doesn’t really matter, all these stuck up ass holes think about are what has happened in OSW thus far, not my past achievements. Wait a minute…” Jesse begins to ponder, “Neither does anyone else in OSW…” Just as Jesse begins to get heavily into this subject the doctor walks into the room with Jesse’s papers. “So, how does it look, doc?” Jesse asks as he sits up. The doctor looks over at Jesse, “You’re not going to be able to wrestle this weekend.” Jesse jumps up in astonishment, “What do you mean? I have to wrestle this weekend!” In a very clam tone, “I’m sorry, Jesse, but I’m not going to be able to clear you.” “Why not?” Jesse asks with determination. “Well, you’ve just been hurt way too badly to even be stepping into the ring for the next three to four weeks.” The doctor stands up and begins to try and profess things to Jesse. “You’re head, you see, is way too badly damaged. What was done to you on your last show was way too dramatic of a situation for body to handle.” Jesse looks at the doctor like he is stupid, “What are you talking about doc? This thing…” Jesse points at his head, “It doesn’t bother me in the least bit. I can go out there and do my thing for the rest of my life if I wanted to.” “Jesse, you may feel that you are up to par, but here, I don’t feel that you are in any shape to compete on this weeks Slam. You can always go against my issues and wrestle anyway, but you’ll be chancing yourself big time by doing so.” The doctor says. Jesse looks at the doctor a little weird as he places both hands down on the hospital bed, “What do you mean? What could happen to me if I were to show up and wrestle?” Jesse asks with a curious look on his face. The doctor sighs as he begins to flip through his charts. “Well, the be quite honest with you, if you take one wrong bump out there and injury your head any more than what it is, you could cause permanent brain damage.” The doctor says with a slight hint of remorse. “You could very well do anything out there in this state and condition that could vary from minor brain damage to being totally paralyzed from the neck down.” “So, you’re saying that I could be confined to a wheelchair for the rest of my life if I go into this match and get severally hurt?” Jesse asks. The doctor sighs, “Yes, you could honestly hurt yourself out there. It would not only end your career, but it could very well end your life, Jesse.” The doctor looks at Jesse, “But as I stated before, the decision is yours as to whether or not you will go out and wrestle. I can’t make that decision for you.” Jesse begins to think to himself for a moment, “But why can’t you keep me from doing it. I mean can’t they keep me out of the arena if you just say so?” “No, I can’t unfortunately.” The doctor says. “So the decision is yours and I’ve told you all that I can. You may leave.” “That’s all you have to say?” Jesse asks. “Yes,” the doctor says as he begins to fill out Jesse’s release forms. “Here you are.” The doctor hands Jesse his release forms. “Thanks, doc. I’ll be seeing you around.” Jesse says as he stands up and walks out of the room. After Jesse is gone the doctor mutters to him-self, “I’m sure you will. I’m sure you will.” Into the darkness… ~The End~
