Potential rape is the (Surprisingly enough) the last thing on your mind with Bre...

2002-06-27

Amy 'Aimz' Campbell

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
((Note: I've been sick all week, just a bit of a cough left now...but I figured I'd express my hatred of viruses through a very angry, virtually pointless piece of writing. :) Oh yeah, and look at my white background poser...pretty creepy, eh? Muahahaha.)) ''FUCK!'' Amy Campbell's voice grumbled from underneath a mountain of blankets that were positioned, along with a pillow, on the couch of the 'cheap-ass' motel room she'd been confined to. There was a funny ring to the usual whining, though...it sounded as if she had the worst head cold in the history of human kind. Stuffy sniffling noises were soon followed by a scratchy cough that sounded like she'd just hacked a lung out onto the floor. Soon after that, a tinted brown bottle of purplish liquid was launched across the room, straight in the direction of Breanne LeLievre, who caught it while almost to tears in laughter while Amy just laid there underneath the blankets, cursing... ''What IS that shit?! It tastes HORRIBLE!!!'' Sniffle sniffle, cough cough. ''UUURRRRGH!!! I fuckin' HATE being sick!'' ''Benylin.'' Bre kept laughing, bending forward on the chair by the door as she watched Amy's face peek out from under the blankets on the uncomfortable sofa. ''You KNOW tha...'' She coughed. ''UGH! You KNOW that shit makes me gag. I hate you.'' Wasn't Amy glad Reno Davis wasn't present at the time...her complection was pasty, her lips were white and the dark circles around her eyes weren't helping. He probably wouldn't have enjoyed the frequent barking cough, either. Breanne just smiled at poor Aimz. ''I know...but it'll help that cough. I think I can figure out what'd REALLY help clear those sinuses...unfortunately, neither of us are twenty one yet, and we're stuck here in this fucktard country.'' ''Meh.'' Was all Campbell could really gather to say before she hacked again. ''Anyway...'' The dirty blonde 'punker' continued. ''...what happened between you and that Rent-A-Retard? He called me all upset 'n shit, asking where you were and why you 'left'.'' ''Don't call him a fucking retard, Breanne...'' Amy snapped. ''And I don't want to talk about it...I felt guilty about a lot of fucked up bullshit I've pulled on him lately.'' ''Like Reno, huh?'' ''Fuck off, Bre...it's not as easy as you think, to care so fucking much for two different guys. Renti---'' She corrected herself, pet names were in the past. ''...Rent-A-Hero really struck a chord when he proposed...I can't get into shit like that, I need my head straightened out.'' ''Well what the hell's going on with Davis, then?'' Breanne was pretty damn confused...her best friend had never exactly spoken with a whole lot of clarity. ''As far as I'm concerned, he's a heartless prick...thinks the whole fucking world's against him, so he turns from everybody else. He's a God damned hermit with no company but a monkey.'' She coughed again...at least her lungs weren't quite to her mouth yet, but this continued hacking would certainly launch them there. '' Let's just not talk about this, okay? I've got enough to deal with.'' LeLievre eased back into her chair, watching the syrup in the Benylin bottle swirl around. ''...'kay, sounds good.'' Just then, Amy went into yet another coughing fit. ''Fuu--'' Hack hack. ''--uuuuuuck!!!!'' ''Y'know...'' Breanne laughed. ''...if you'd just take some of the damn medicine...'' The redhead pouted, reminiscent of a five-year-old girl, wanting to experience the taste of the cough syrup about as much as she wanted to see Corg's 'buldging member' again. ''No!'' ''Suit yourself.'' She grinned. ''Sooo...Aerosmith's making a new albu---'' Bre was cut off by another deep-chested coughing fit from Campbell. Finally, Amy reached a pale, shaking hand out. ''Give me the fuckin' purple shit!'' With that, her best friend tossed it back at her, falling down in a fit of hysterical laughter as she watched it smack Aimz square on the forehead... ''GAAAAH!!!'' And thus goes a generally shitty week... ((P.S: RP number 667. Die, Ross...die.))
