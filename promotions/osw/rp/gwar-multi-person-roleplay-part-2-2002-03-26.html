GWAR (Multi-Person Roleplay Part 2)

26 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-26-02 Starring: Jesse Ramey from Old School Wrestling as Rob Van Attitude Brad Lamen from Cyberspace Championship Wrestling as The HurraLamen Meanstreak from Old School Wrestling as “The Big Streak” Paul Mean Blayze from Old School Wrestling as Ghetto Brown Andy M. from Primetime Championship Wrestling as Hunter “F’N” Helmsley Kareem Olson from Primetime Wrestling Academy as Booker KO “Someone get some security in here,” is shouted from an off screen. “Holy shiznittaly, evil doer Attitude is trying to kill the superhero of GWAR!” Is being said from the obvious HurraLamen. “Take it back you dirty bastard! You know none of this frigin stuff is true! Get over it!” Is being shouted. Back into the place of the last show, we see what happened after we last left our dynamic heroes of wrestling. A few new people run into the scene. As the battle of Attitude and HurraLamen continues we find ourselves making our way behind two new competitors. “So, tell me again, what do you think about the new management?” The one wrestler asks the other. “Well, to tell you the truth Hunter, I’m not too pleased with them. I mean for crying out loud. I am a twenty time,” holds hand to face, “GWAR Jobber Champion!” Hunter stops dead in his tracks, “Why do you always hold your hand up to your face when you are doing that, Booker?” “Well, I don’t see so well.” Booker lowers his head, “I don’t reads so well, either sir.” Booker looks down and then looks back up at Hunter with a smile. The two burst into laughter, “Jesus Christ man, that shit always cracks me up.” Twisting around, ever so slightly, we pan back into the heavy quarrel between Attitude and HurraLamen, but now we have another figure that has made his presence known. “Listen hera boys!” Shouts an African-American man. “The boss don’t like him talent fighting like hera. He rather want us back in that there ring. You hera me boyiz?” Attitude backs off, “Yeah, I hear what you are saying Brown.” “Goo, goo. I go let the boss know thing alright now.” Brown leaves the scene and takes off down a long corridor. “You listen to me, Lamen.” Attitude says pointing at Lamen. “If you so much as go in character one more time back here, it’ll take more than Brown to save your ass.” “Whatchamacallit? I know for a fact that I’ve got a match with you, Attitude.” Lamen says with the sour looking face, “Once we get in the ring, I’ll show you just how a real superhero handles his light weight.” Lamen turns and acts as if he is flying out of the scene with a loud “Swoosh!” following him. Mean places a hand down on Attitude’s shoulder. “It isn’t worth it kid. Trust me.” Mean throws down his crumpled paper cup and walks away. “I’ll show his ass.” Attitude says as he begins to walk off in the opposite direction. ~To Be Continued~
