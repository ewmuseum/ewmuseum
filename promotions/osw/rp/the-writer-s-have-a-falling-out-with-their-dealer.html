The Writer's Have A Falling Out With Their Dealer

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cut to inside the famous OSW Creative Department. Inside, the greatest minds of the WrestleTattler community develop and plan for the direction and storylines of the world's fastest rising federation, one that eclipses the famous PCW in popularity. Around a stained oak table, five men sit, a haze of cigarette smoke and body odor filling the room. At one end is Ron, the Head Writer. He sits, bags under his eyes, in an office chair, his crumpled white shirt stained with pasta sauce and his black slacks bunched around the crotch so his sock garters show. His hair is frazzled and already graying at the age of 31. He looks around the room at his most trusted writers. Dan sits off to his left, carefully scraping the resin out of his bowl. The gangly twenty-something intently uses a red Uni-ball pen, which was previously used by Ron to slash up a potential skit that Dan had devised in a hallinogenic frenzy, involving a full grown adult female sow, a sponge, a vat of E-Z Glide and a Swede; to scrape out the resin, which he intends to smoke. Across from Dan sits Hutch. Hutch, a veteran of this craft, is zoned out of his mind on amphetamines to stay awake. He was the creative mastermind over the current Reno Davis - Ivan Stanislav feud, and it was beginning to take its toll on the poor man. A slight formation of drool bubbles forms at the edge of his cracked lips, a look of single-minded obsession in his eyes. To Hutch's left sits the enigmatic man known only as...Wippit. Wippit sits...well, sits isnt exactly the right word. Wipput sort of sprawls out on the office chair, his shirt a modern art masterpiece of drying vomit, vodka, pizza consumed three days before, and NyQuil. The poor man was responsible for some of the upcoming Ascension logistics and the stress seems to have finally gotten to him. On the other side isSam, the dyed blue-haired writer who was Ron's greatest problem child. Sam nervously looks around the conference room, trying not to make eye contact with the Head Writer. But its too late. Ron's hypnotic gaze locks onto Sam, and the weak-willed writer cowers in his chair. 'What the hell is this crap?' Ron barks, the sound of his voice piercing through the cigarette haze. Hutch snarls and whips his head around, a streamer of drool whipping through the air and splattering on his cheek. A stack of papers slides across the refuse strewn table and goes neatly into Sam's grasp. On it, the latest Toomes promo. Something he had written after a marathon of masturbation involving cloned 16 year old nymphomaniacs calling him daddy, and the subsequent struggle to get to sleep in order to show up for work this morning. A political wishlist written in the form of a speech made by the President. 'Uh... Gripping social commentary?' Sam tries, but Ron glares at him, his eyes piercing through the fog of the room. 'Nice try. Why don't you actually do something with that rotted out raisin you call a brain and come up with something funny? Or at least sexually explicits o the guys get into it... Christ, is this what I have to put up with?' Sam tucks the Manifesto in his battered Lamborghini Trapper Keeper. 'Well, I DID come up with that little ditty about Toomes' sister getting molested by that Brad Lamen guy.' Hutch goes off into a gale of high-pitched laughter, sounding like he's laughing hysterically and crying at the same time. 'MORE CRANK, MOMMY! MORE CRANK!' he chatters. Ron sighs. 'Who cares about CCW wrestlers....' Ron begins. Wipput drawls out, a bubble of vomit popping as he opens his mouth. 'Then explain Ace Hawk and Jesse Ramey? Why didja give them programs?' Ron throws a fully stocked ashtray at Wipput, who does a lazy slump to the floor after it rebounds off the coating of stomach contents he had produced. 'Hey, I can't be smart all the time. Anyway, can you get back to writing something funny for this guy? Like those music videos. Those were funny!' Ron says. Sam shrugs. 'Well, I suppose I could, but then they would lose the novelty of the whole...' 'MORE CRANK!' Dan looks up to Ron. 'Got onee of them....whaddya call em....You know, the thing the dentist guy scrapes your teeth with?' 'Dental Pick?' 'Yeah, one of those.' Wipput rumbles up from the floor. 'Why can't you wait till our dealer gets here? I'm sure he's got some real stuff for you...' 'You don't understand, MAN... I need my HERBS, MAN! How am I supposed to write for the PPV if I don't have my HERBS, MAN?' Dan dissertates before grabbing the offered dental pick that Ron produces out of his shirt pocket. 'You actually carry those things around?' Sam begins. 'Nevermind. Arn't you supposed to be writing something funny?' Ron snaps. 'Oh.' Sam mumbles and looks away, doodling away on his legal pad. Ron sighs and holds his head in his hands. Just a few more minutes, then he was gonna go find that dealer and BEAT THE LIVING SHIT OUT OF HIM if he didn't have his smack. Oh, then, oh yes, then heads would roll. How are you supposed to write for the biggest fed....EVER...without your fix, man? And these idiots were killing him! Political statements? Limp dicked CCW wrestlers? Using his dental tool? What next, an Aimz heel turn? Oh. Wait. Ron looked across the table at Sam, who was no doubt, thinking of another stupid promo. Man, that boy sure has gone downhill. Maybe he needs some...persuasion... Yeah, that's it. Get him back to writing the good stuff. Maybe a little beating. Break a few ligaments. He won't be able to run, that's for sure... Yeah, yeah... And then, while Sam is flopping around, crying, I can shoot up with some sweet refreshing China White... Oh yes, sweet, sweet Heroin... That's the ticket, yeah... The knock on the conference room door interrupts Ron from his reverie. A dark haired man in a French Maid's outfit opens the door. 'Yes, Edward?' Ron growls. Ah yes, the woes of those who displeased him... Yes... Ed's squeaky voice cuts through the air, no doubt caused by the extremely tight underwear Ron made him wear as punishment for not getting stuff turned in on time. 'The dealer's here, Master Ron!' Ed squeaks. '...Excellent.' FIN
