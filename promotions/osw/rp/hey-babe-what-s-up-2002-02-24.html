Hey Babe, What's Up?

24 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-24-02 Our scene opens with a shot of the backstage area at the Baltimore Arena, site of the most recent Slam that has rocked the nation’s television sets. And rock it did...no, wait...now that I think about it, it was more like getting hit in the head with a rock over and over again. Why you ask? Perhaps the sheer amount of travesty that took place in one two hour span of time has influenced my take on the whole situation. Then again, maybe it was just the torture of seeing two Clay Vendetta vignettes over the course of the show. Whatever the case, it was a very depressing broadcast on all fronts. Surprisingly however, none of the night’s events seem to have put a damper on Civil Disobedience’s good spirits, as when we hear them approaching from around the corner they seem to be talking rather merrily. “Man, Trev...I don’t know about you, but I feel great!” we hear Seth shout with a joyful tone. “I can’t say I disagree with you on that point, bro.” Trevor answers in an equally pleasant tone. “I mean, did you see Shanda Russell looking at me after our match?” Seth states, sounding proud. “She was so checking me out.” “It seemed like more of a look of disgust, but take it as you will,” responds Trevor, chuckling slightly. “That’s just what I plan on doing,” says an oblivious, but happy, Seth. “Heh heh. I’m gonna get laid.” “Yes, so all things aside, I sure am glad to be an Old School Wrestling superstar,” states Trevor, a bit more clearly as we hear the duo drawing closer to our location. “Right on, bro. Right on!” answers Seth’s voice in reply. A few seconds later the two suburban warriors make their way around the corner with huge grins on their faces. Not only grins, though, but also lots and lots of blood. Seth and Trevor appear to be wearing your typical “crimson masks” as they strut down the hallway, rather cockily. Their clothing is still sweat-stained from their match earlier in the night, as well as splattered with the drops of blood that have trickled down off their heads. It’s amazing that they’re in such good spirits considering their current conditions, but, like always, to try to understand the minds of these two men would be a task too daunting for any psychologist, much less a layman. Pausing for a moment in front of a locker room doorway, Trevor regards Seth with a inquiry. “So now that we’re done here, what do you want to do the kill off the rest of the night?” he asks, with a raise of a blood-caked eyebrow. Stroking his nonexistent beard, Seth escapes into a period of deep thought...well as deep as you can expect from Seth. “I’m up for a little disco. You?” he asks slyly, to which Trevor shakes his head incredulously. “Disco? Why the hell would you want to go to a disco?” “Heh heh. Well, I guess you can say we’ve been ‘Stayin’ Alive’ so far here in OSW. Barely. But still, it would be appropriate for our situation, don’t you think?” chuckles Seth, wiping the blood from his eyes like it is no big deal. “I don’t think you can come up with anything that could really be considered appropriate for our situation,” says Trevor. “In fact, disco is about as far away as you can get from anything pertaining to us at all.” “But...but...‘Stayin’ Alive’!!!” pleads Seth, sticking his hands in front of him. Trevor says nothing in reply, just looking at him like the idiot he is. Seth drops the begging act, crossing his arms in front of him. “Well do you want to go or not, dammit?” Seth asks sternly. “Eh. Why the hell not. I’ll go.” Trevor agrees with a shrug. “But not with you. That’s just gay.” “Fair enough,” nods Seth. “Now all we have to do is find some dates, but that shouldn’t be too hard. I’m a good looking guy, and you’re...not deformed in any noticeable way. Now let’s see...who could we get to go with us? Hmmm.” The locker room door the two men are standing in front of swings open to both of their surprise, and out steps sultry OSW referee, Betty Lovejoy. She’s currently not wearing what passes as her referee’s uniform, instead opting to sport a pair of tight black leather pants with a hot pink halter top. Her blond hair is pulled back, with one of those hairclips keeping it from falling back in her face. She’s smiling ditzily as shuts the door behind her, until she notices Civil Disobedience standing directly in her path. A look of disgust quickly replaces her smile, as she takes a step back from the two bleeding suburbians, trying to get away unnoticed in the opposite direction. The click of her high heels gives her away, though, as both Seth and Trevor’s gazes fall upon her. She winces, while they exchange grins, before walking up to stand beside her. “Hey Betty! Babe! What’s up!” Trevor says, placing an arm around her shoulders. “You’re looking great tonight, as usual.” “Um...thanks,” she replies unenthusiastically, as she pushes Trevor’s arm away and slips away from him. In doing so, he almost runs smack dab into Seth, who has moved to block her path. She recoils from the grotesque appearance of his bloody face, turning away to face Trevor once more, who places his arm around her again, despite her obvious uncomfort. “So...what are your plans for tonight.” Trevor says, shooting her an attractive smile...well it, would be attractive if blood wasn’t dripping from his nose down onto his bottom lip, at least. “Uh...” she struggles, looking back at Seth, who isn’t looking much prettier than Trevor. “Actually I was...” “Because we were wondering if you wanted to go disco dancing with us,” Seth interrupts. “We know you’ve had your eyes on us studs for weeks now, and despite your shoddy refereeing we think we’ll make your dreams come true tonight.” “Who knows...you might even get lucky,” says Trevor with a grin. Usually this would be enough to cause any woman to swoon. When his lips curl back this time, however, it causes a few drops of blood to splatter on Betty’s arm, thus countering the near orgasmic effect it normally has. Betty is so repulsed by this that she lets out a high pitched scream at the top of her lungs, pushing Trevor away forcefully and running past Seth. Trevor shoots a confused look at Seth, who shrugs in response, as the two stare at Betty’s swaying hips as she runs away. “What’s the matter, honey?” Seth calls after her mournfully. “You guys are, like, icky!” she shouts back, before disappearing behind a corner, leaving Civil Disobedience alone again. “Well that’s never happened before,” Trevor says, scratching his head in confusion. “Do you think maybe I’m losing my touch?” “Nah, it happens to me all the time. I think when stuff like that happens, they’re just too intimidated,” Seth says, placing a comforting hand on Trevor’s shoulder. Spying a hot brunette walking with her friend, Seth points a finger in their direction. “Well, as they say, if at first you don’t succeed, try, try again. I see two foxes over their, Trev. The night is still young.” Seth leads Trevor in the direction of the two women, but as soon as their eyes fall upon our two protagonists, they have a similar reaction to Betty’s. I guess chicks must not find the sight of flowing blood very attractive. Go figure. Anyway, Trevor and Seth each place an arm around the prospective repulsed dates, as we do a quick fade out into a dissolve. When we dissolve back in, we find Seth and Trevor sitting on a bench in the same back hallway they were in earlier. The words ‘Two hours later’ appear on the bottom of the screen. The two partners are looking rather forlorn, and seeing that they’re not currently at a disco, one would think that their lack of dates is the cause of this depression. “I just don’t get it,” says Trevor, shaking his head. When he does so, little beads of blood fly from his face, collecting in a growing pool at his feet. “Why doesn’t anyone want to go out with us?” “I don’t know what to tell you, Trev,” answers Seth. “Maybe this isn’t our night after all. Which is shame, considering it started off so good.” As they talk, a short woman pulls a mop and bucket into the hallway. To say she is homely would be an understatement. Standing about five feet tall, she’s about half of that distance wide. Her hair sticks up in all directions in a rather unkempt fashion, though it compliments her grungy janitorial jumpsuit nicely. A fat wart juts out from her nose, and another one from her chin. Her body odor makes even Azrael Cain’s in-ring work seem not to reek as much. If there ever was a woman to match Danny Devito’s natural unattractiveness, this was her. As Trevor and Seth see her enter the hall, their frowns drop even deeper, then, as they exchange glances, shrug to each other and walk over to her. “My, how far our standards have fallen,” Trevor whispers to Seth as they draw near to the wench. You would think she would be happy to have any member of the opposite sex talk to her at all, but instead she looks just as horrified as the dozen other prospective dates approached by Seth and Trevor over the past two hours. She’s not repulsed by the blood covered faces, however, instead waving her hands in the air angrily as she sees the trail of blood following the two competitors as they walk toward her. “So, hey...babe...I guess,” Seth says, unenthusiastically. “You’re looking...um...almost like a human being.” “Ahhh! You! You make big mess!” she shouts in some shrill unrecognizable accent, as she points at the blood on the floor. “It take forever to clean. Ahhh!” “Whoa, settle down, hon,” Trevor says. “I know you’re excited that someone decided to give you the time of day, but there’s no need for all that...screeching.” “Excited? At you? Why I care about you? I got job to do. Job you make harder. Now go!” she screeches angrily, pushing Seth and Trevor aside to start mopping the floor. “Seth...is this broad rejecting us?” Trevor asks, looking like he’s about to cry. “Please tell me she isn’t. I don’t know if my ego could handle this.” “No, Trev. I won’t let her do this to you...to us,” Seth says, with an angry look on his face. He turns to face the haggish woman, pointing at her angrily. “Hey bitch! What the hell’s your problem? You think you’re too fucking good for us or something?” “Yeah, pretty much,” she says, still mopping, not even looking up from the ground. “Ha! The sheer ludicrous nature of that answer makes me laugh. Take a look at yourself and take a look at us. What makes you think you even deserve to be in our company, much less have the right to turn us down?” “Well...” she says, looking up from her job with a serious expression. “For one I not a loser. You got pinned by robots. You poor, poor men.” Seth opens his mouth to give a retort, then shuts it slowly...and sadly. He turns to Trevor, looking for help, but Trevor looks equally speechless. Seth smacks in head in realization, shouting loudly in anger. “Damn it, we lost!” wails Seth mournfully. “We, Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke, LOST! Our undefeated streak is over! And we lost to those stupid ass robots! Of all the teams in the world, why did it have to be them! Argh! I was in such a good mood, too! How could I have forgotten about that! That just ruins my night! It FUCKING ruins my FUCKING night! Damn Hessian and damn Azrael Cain. It’s all their FUCKING fault. DICKSMACKS!!!” “That certainly would explain our excessive bleeding tonight,” Trevor admits thoughtfully. “I was wondering what the deal was with that. How did we forget about losing? That sort of things is a big deal. We were acting so happily, too. Boy is my face red.” “Yes, with blood,” says the cleaning lady as she mops. “Well we can’t go to the disco now,” Seth says, shaking his head. “I’m much too distraught to do such a thing.” “We can always prepare for our match next week,” Trevor offers. “We’re in the frickin’ main event, dude. We can get revenge on those two oversized mongoloids. And Meanstreak is on our team! The Monster! We can’t lose!” “Meanstreak you say? Hmmm, I wonder if he measures up to our standards,” Seth ponders, while Trevor regards him with a look of disbelief. “Our standards of being cool, I mean.” “Ah,” Trevor says. “Well, I guess we’ll find out next week.” “Yes. Next week Heresian Bloodlust will find out that payback is a bitch,” Seth says menacingly, before walking over to the cleaning lady and kicking her over. “Just like this broad. Let’s go, Trev.” The two men walk offscreen, still trailing blood behind them as the scene fades out.
