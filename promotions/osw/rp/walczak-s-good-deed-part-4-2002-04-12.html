Walczak's Good Deed (Part 4)

12 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/12/02 Walczak is seen walking down a street, and he is getting weird looks from people. Most of them have seen that he just saved a man from being burned, but the looks he is receiving is not those of admiring, but of disdain. The looks are usually accompanied by a smirk or a sneer, but Walczak just keeps walking, and is trying to get home without causing any type of conflict. However, there is a man who walks up to Walczak and taps him on the shoulder. The man stands five foot nothing, and is wearing thick black glasses. His hair is slicked back, and he wears a flannel shirt, and khaki shorts held up by suspenders. Although he has shorts on his legs are not visible because the socks he is wearing come all the way up near his thighs. The short man begins to speak. “Where do you think that you are going you big bully? You think that just because a little bit of silence came over your area that your admonishment has ended? Well, you are wrong, and I am going to show you.” “Listen, I am not in the mood. I understand that what I did was wrong, but why must people like you still badger me? I admit that what I did was wrong, is that good enough?” “No, it is not, because I know that all that you are trying to do is avoid my verbal thrashing. I know your type. You guys are big dumb mejungas who thinks that their poop don’t stink.” “Did you just say poop?” “Why, yes I did. Do you have a problem with that?” “No, I don’t have a problem per say it is just that I haven’t used the word poop since I was like two.” “Well, what do you use?” “Shit, Crap, Ass Waste, Brown Stuff etc.” “Oh, the vulgarities, I must shut my virgin ears!!!” “What are you like six?” “As a matter of fact I just turned twenty one, and I would appreciate it if you stopped with your degrading, and sarcastic remarks.” “Twenty one huh? How about I take you out for a drink?” “Alcohol? Are you serious, if my mother found out it would just break her heart.” “Ok, listen you can continue with your verbal thrashing another time, but I really have to get going, so take care.” “Hey wait, I am not through with you yet.” “Take care buddy.” Walczak hurries off, and the man does not follow. Once Walczak sees that the man has left completely he goes back on the road, and heads back home. “Christ, that could have been the most annoying man that I have ever met in my life. He may be even more annoying than that little punk Midget the Impotent. Seriously, I cannot wait to face Hessian. He goes around talking about how he is the biggest baddest man in the OSW, when in fact if he was to be in the Red Army he would rank after the two Round faced women in terms of strength and power. That is how weak he really is. Anyway enough trash talk. I must make hast because the past couple of days has been weird everywhere I go trouble follows me, and I am the man that usually puts an end to it.” Walczak walks off and the scene fades to black…
