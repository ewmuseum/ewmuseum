Forgetting...

26 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26-06-02 Hessian sat, pondering silently on what had happened. Running his hand along the stitches on his face, Hessian glared at the once-wrapped-in-barbed-wire gauntlet before him. The blood and indents of the wire were still there, as fresh as the moment when Hessian put his foot down on Azrael Cain. In the shadows to his left, Hessian hears the clanking of some worn old chains hitting against each other. Careless, Hessian removes his skull mask and checks it over for any cracks he may have missed, and places it carefully back on his worn head, as the clanking grows louder, and closer to Hessian. “Remember my teachings…” Vampir Nosferatu sauntered across to Hessian, stopping abruptly before the Mercenary of Satan, he himself seated on the cold dirt and ash, which used to be the floor. “Time has passed, much has changed. Though remember your roots, remember the beginning. Who it was that made you who you are today. As one, we share the knowledge and power, enough to overcome any and all. Remember all of this Hessian…but that which you must forget…is the mistakes of the past…forge-…” “Forget Azrael Cain? Is it he that has caused me such grief? A few mistakes at a fight that meant as much as everything has in turn dismantled the one thing I have put so much of my mind and body through! For months Cain and I have dominated the tag team division of OSW, and now it is all forgotten in a flurry of barbed wire and blood. Forget Azrael Cain? Forget that which has assisted me in the destruction of every single tag team in this, the place where we can be violent and barbaric, legally? Forget…” Vampir Nosferatu growls and paces up and down the cool earth, leaving deep impressions in the ground. The chains embracing his frame clank together eerily as the Soul Stalker tops again before Hessian, standing defiantly before that which he created. “There is much you must be taught…much you must learn, for your mind will soon be corrupted if you persist in letting this eat away at you. I will, as I have done before, mould you into the sheer brutality that you once were Hessian…for now; this chapter in your life is closed…you will move on.” Hessian, fitting his gauntlet back on his massive hand shoots up and stands looking down at Vampir, staring into his creator’s eyes with his own, burning embers, the windows to his soul, if the Goliath truly ever had one. They exchange looks of rage, before Vampir rests his hand on Hessian’s shoulder, and forces the Mercenary of Satan back to his seat, without much effort at all. “You will forget…you will learn…and together we will conquer. Do not forget who I am, I can break you just as easily as I made you Von Kelsig, do not defy me…do not defy me. Now, I tire of this place, let us take leave for now, to prepare for future endeavours…and bring that Imp with you…” At the mention of his species, Minion looks up from an ash castle he has made, and hops to his stumpy little feet, and chases after Vampir through the ash and cinders outside into the dazzling moonlight. Hessian gets back to his feet and brushes himself off, and looks up, out past where the roof used to be and stares into the full moon. “ Vampir Nosferatu…the ultimate evil. I have no choice…then again do I really care? If I must I will take his wisdom and use it as I have done in the past. Forget Azrael Cain, Vampir wants me to forget my partner, my fellow Heretic, the one that I have shared the glory with, my partner…the man that lost us the tag team championships…my partner………” Hessian turns from the full moon and wades through the thick blanket of ash and cinders on the ground and walks outside, slamming the large doors shut behind him. Nodding to Vampir Nosferatu, they take their leave, while the light of the full moon shines down on the freshly burnt down remains of Azrael Cain’s Cathedral… “Forget Azrael Cain…forget I shall…” Fin
