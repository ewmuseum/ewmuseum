A Taste Of My Own Medicine

14 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07.14.2002 For the entirety of my useless life, I’ve been tortured by my memories and have desperately tried to walk away from them. However, when I climb up one wall built to stop me, I just end up finding another. Be it a lost match or a cold blooded murder … I just cannot escape. Is it destiny for me to feel pain? I hope not. For the more pain put on my shoulders, the more I inflict on the Old School Wrestling organization as a collective. When I hurt … you hurt. When I bleed … you bleed. When I antagonize … you all become the victims. Yet tonight … the story was rewritten to benefit a hero. One man who’s historic rise to the top has been documented in every medium. Never once could I take control and do something productive. Never once could I emerge a victor. I’ve prayed to a God before … and none of it came true. I’ve aligned with those superior … and they have just used me. Trying to make a mark … I combated a animal. In the end I find myself here in an ambulance, eyes closed but my ever rampant thoughts providing a fallen scapegoat, at the world who hates him, with a vision. Insanity. Violence. Lack In Self-Worth. Now you see what I see. My opinions. My history. My rise. My fall. “Christopher … come here.” The familiar voice of Kory Storm speaks, surprisingly calm. Now the colors brighten and the images are seen clearly. Where has he taken us? It couldn’t be any recent place in time … for we’ve seen him. The body draped in a sea of wreckage after the Red Scare, courtesy of Ivan Stanislav. No. He has chosen to disturb us even further. We are inside the memory of Kory Storm. ][Kory Storm is standing in some sort of field, atop a fresh plot of dirt and a mass of foliage around him. In the background, the gray skies provide a slight outline, that of a limousine. Picking up a handful of the ground soil, the wind blows spreading further from Storm’s hands, as a small man in a pressed suit approaches from behind the massive build of the controversial wrestler. Almost as if having a sick sixth sense, Kory obviously feels the presence of his agent, Christopher Kingsley.][ “I witness the sights of this dirt … fresh in nature and just part of this environment.” Kory Storm speaks in an outlandish tone. “Yes … a newborn plot of soil.” Christopher Kingsley replies with a hint of sarcasm in his voice, a look on his face which “Perfection Personified” clearly ignores. “I know what runs through your head Kingsley.” “W-W-What do you mean?” “Please you insolent waste of flesh…” Kory Storm screams into the air, “I see your stares. You think I’m crazy. You’ve never seen this side.” ][Kingsley feels sadly about his blatant disrespect, approaching Kory Storm with no hesitation and taking his side.][ “I apologize.” Speaks the now timid agent. “I know you’re going through a tough time Kory …but you’ve been through life before… why choose now to stop in the middle of Ohio and have a nervous breakdown on some agricultural station?” “Ha!” Laughs Storm. “You know nothing about my feelings, only about my pockets. You’ve never bothered to ask about the human side of me Kingsley … you’ve never cared about anything but the vile character who bleeds to make you money. Not that I blame you. A disgusting little man like yourself, obviously insecure sexually and in every other facet of life, feeding off of my success. But what happens when the mother runs out of food for the baby? Death…” Now you begin to see it fall into place. Everyone knew that Kory Storm provoked … out of frustration. He felt, and probably still feels, held back. Unappreciated. Never, at least in the short time we’ve seen the complex star, has he reached this amount of depth with his reasoning. “For there is no remnant of success within my career, Kingsley.” Storm manages to look his companion into the eyes. “I have lost. Now I give up.” “Not…” Christopher struggles to mumble, “the end of your career?” “No.” Storm reassures him. “Not yet, at least. I still feel, more or less I know, that I can make an impact. I thought I could make a rise to the top by defeating a man who did the very same. Earning respect through actions and not through words. All Kory Storm can be is talk. I strut around with a high held head, and inside my head I’m the best. Why can’t anyone catch on?” Because… “Maybe Christopher,” He laughs to himself, “it’s because I’m made to be a joke. I work, no matter how ruthless I do so, very hard. Does anyone care? No, for they’ve taken a page out of my book. And why the fuck shouldn’t they? Why should anyone care about what I do, when for so long I’ve spit on everything else? Silence. “I don’t know.” “For once I feel on the same level as you.” Storm speaks. “For our attitudes aren’t needed here, and they never have been. We don’t belong. In my opinion, it’s because humanity is afraid of what we can do once in power. But what does my opinion matter anymore?” It’s never mattered. And admitting your wrongs won’t change that, you selfish bastard. As Kory Storm, you’ve terrorized. You then claim to be human, yet humans could never be like you. Why are you hated? Because you’ve made it that way. “Will you awaken yourself and change?” Kingsley asks. “And admit that I was wrong?” Storm screams. “You must be thick, Christopher! Can I compromise what everyone hates now and start anew? Of course not. They’ll never let go of the beatings and the verbal obscenities. No matter what they do, wrestler or fan, I’ll always be the only one who isn’t right. Forgiveness is erased.” You can’t teach an old dog new tricks. Even in a memory that is what is resembled. “Even if I will forever be the thorn stuck in the proverbial side … I’ll walk into Slam! tonight as the same person. If I can finally convince the people of what I’ve known for so long, I’ll have accomplished my goals. I will now know that I’m the only one who matters. Kory Storm. Two wrongs will never make a right in my eyes. Thus, you’ll all still be fucked. Then I’ll see each of you in hell.” ][Storm shovels a small amount of dirt into his pocket, walking through the tall grasses of the field, Kingsley screaming after him.][ “What is that for?” ][Kory Storm turns around and gazes into the skies, right past the curious friend who is the only other concerned.][ “A reminder…” Storm whispers, “For the fans view me as dirt. But the dirt belongs here. Maybe that means I belong after all.” Now, after many confrontations and a brutal attack, I’m back to where you first saw me tonight. Laying on my back, blood pouring from my mouth and a light head. Blame Ivan Stanislav? Hardly. For it is myself. I acted in spite and tried to make something of myself which has never been there … and in a way … I was brought back down to Earth. Unfortunately for my enemies, I’m now more dangerous. Kory Storm has found a purpose in life, and I will earn each jeer. It won’t even take my words or the constant conceit. It will just take your fears, and a stare into the eyes of the one who will bring them out. -------------- ][The scene opens at exactly 3:38 AM … Sunday, July 14th. Hunched over, a white room the setting, the face of Christopher Kingsley is seen. Under his glare lays the awoken, frail body of Kory Storm. “Bad news.” Kory Storm whispers, a glint in his brown eyes. “How bad?” “You’re out of Old School Wrestling for a week … they want to run some tests.” ][Kory Storm just sighs and closes his eyes, falling deeply asleep. The scene fading to a view of his clenched fist.][ If any good can come of this … we’ve seen that one thing can be proven. You truly can mess with perfection.
