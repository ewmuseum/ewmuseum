A Carnal Promise

13 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/13/02 The sun is beginning to set over the city. The amber light is shining in through the shattered windows of the factory that Azrael Cain calls his home. There is complete silence in the factory. Only the noise from the streets can faintly be heard in the distance. The shrill voice of Azrael Cain’s manager, Caleb, breaks the silence in the Beast’s decrepit factory. “Cain! Cain!” The dirty hobo screams as he runs waving a piece of paper over his head. Caleb is wearing his usual dirty and stained trench coat. His old Velcro Nikes move the waddling hobo quickly through the rubble and debris that cover the floor. Azrael Cain can be seen sitting on the floor with his knees up to his shoulder height. His arms are resting on the top of his knees with his head buried into his forearms. Cain is wearing his traditional wrestling attire, black tight pants with red stripes going up the side, black leather boots, and his high black fingerless gloves. His long black hair is down hanging so that his loyal friend cannot see his face. Caleb continues to get the warrior’s attention. “Cain! Come on you big lummox! Heh-Heh-Heh. Snap out of it already! Heh. I got something that might lighten your spirits. Heh-Heh-Heh.” There is no response from Cain who keeps his head down. The hobo scratches at his greasy gray comb over and tries again. “Cain? You have been like this Sunday night! Heh. Will you just snap out of it? Heh-Heh-Heh.” Cain’s left arm extends from its crossed position and grips Caleb by the collar. The beast then pulls the hobo close to him without raising his head. He begins to address the hobo without looking at him. “I have failed! Those little whelps beat us again! Hessian and I failed to destroy them, but instead we handed them the titles! How do you expect me to act, you worm?” Cain then releases his manager’s collar, which causes him to fall on his ass hard. Caleb starts to squirm as he wines. “Oh shit! Charlie Horse! Heh. Dammit it all to Hell! Heh! Damn! Damn! Damn!” Caleb gets up and starts hopping around trying to shake the pain out. He still holds the piece of paper in his hand though. After a second the pain begins to subside. Caleb then tries to get the warrior’s attention again. “Are you going to lay around here like a wounded animal the rest of your damn day on this rock? Heh-Heh. What the hell is your problem? Heh? You are acting like a damn woman! Heh-Heh-Heh.” Cain’s head rises from his forearms and shoots a glare at the hobo that could stop a herd of stampeding Rhinos in their tracks. Cain’s teeth grind and his eyes squint as he snarls at the nuisance that is his manager. Caleb slowly backs off a little and finally just comes out with the information that is on the paper. “Well, it seems that you and Hessian are going to have a match on the next Slam, you big ogre! Heh-Heh-Heh. And this match couldn’t be anymore perfect! Heh-Heh. It seems that you guys are taking on none other than Ivan Stanislav and that freak, Meanstreak! Heh-Heh. You two should be able to maul the two those goons! Heh. Especially because most of the time they will be at each other’s throats instead of worrying about you and Hess! Heh-Heh-Heh!” Cain rises to his feet. The massive beast lumbers over Caleb. Cain reaches down and removes the piece of paper from Caleb’s small and grubby hand. He brings the paper up to his face and begins to read. As his dark eyes peruse the paper his facial expression begins to change for the first time all week. His face is returning to its battle worn and hate-filled usual expression. He lowers the paper and looks off into the distance for a moment before saying anything. As he speaks he continues to stair into nothingness. There is and uncomfortable coldness in the fallen angel’s voice. “The front office of OSW is in shambles right now. The President is nothing more than a whore who has played the people for the fools that they are. The commissioner cares about nothing more than his own agenda to stop the one they call Meanstreak. The head of security is nothing more than a pawn. The voices of OSW don’t know who to side with and as a result they are lost in limbo. They keep allowing worthless excuses for warriors to enter into the ring with likes of Hessian and myself. This is a perfect opportunity.” Cain’s words are confusing the decrepit hobo. Caleb removes a small flask from his trench coat and takes a large swig before addressing his large friend. “What the hell are you talking about? Heh-Heh. I was just excited about the fact that you would get to slap that overgrown bitch, Meanstreak around again. Heh. But of course you have to go out on one of your damn tangents again. Heh-Heh. Will you just get to the point already? Heh-Heh-Heh” Cain drops the paper that holds this week’s Slam schedule of matches. The paper carelessly floats to the ground. Cain looks at the confused old man and attempts to make himself a little clearer. “What you don’t understand is that now is a perfect time for Heresian Bloodlust to destroy all of those that continue to get in our way. It’s time to bring our enemies to their knees.” Caleb’s mouth opens as he begins to practically drool at the thought of the carnage that Cain is thinking about. His mouth opens to reveal his toothless gums as his tongue actually runs across his lips. His dirty hands begin to rub together. Caleb begins to talk a little boy waiting to open his Christmas gifts. “And what pray tell is going through that dark mind of yours? Heh-Heh-Heh.” A devilish smirk stretches across the warrior’s face that exposes his enlarged fang like canines. Cain’s eyes flair with rage as he continues on. “It is about time for Heresian Bloodlust to make their presence felt on OSW. And now is the time! Those in charge are too caught up in there own little dramas to stop us.” Caleb chimes in playing the devil’s advocate. “What about Ivan and Meanstreak? Heh-Heh. Those two goons aren’t the kinda fellas you take lightly. Heh-Heh-Heh.” Cain bends over to pick up his sledgehammer. Cain’s smile grows as he hoists the weapon up. He is almost laughing while he talks about his opponents. “We don’t have to worry about the likes of the big Russian Bear! The dog has let a woman into his heart. He is weak. The moment that a warrior opens his heart to anything, they are doomed to fall. And Meanstreak…Ha! It is time that he learns that he is no longer the monster that everyone once feared! This week the monster is going to fall and fall hard. Besides, the two of them will be at each other’s throats. They will be too busy pounding away at one another to notice their impending destruction looming in the darkness.” Cain slams the sledgehammer down hard on a pile of twisted rusty metal. Caleb begins to howl widely in anticipation of the chaos that is to come on Slam. Cain’s smile grows even wider as he speaks again. “This week, Heresian Bloodlust is going unleash the kind of pain and suffering the likes of which have never been seen. All of our enemies will wail for the mercy of the Creator to take them away from the pain. This week…the carnage is going to be unleashed on OSW!”
