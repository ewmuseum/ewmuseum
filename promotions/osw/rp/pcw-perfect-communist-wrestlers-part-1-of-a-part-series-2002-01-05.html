PCW: Perfect Communist Wrestlers [Part 1 of a ? Part Series]

5 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1.05.02 The jeep crunched out of gear as Krieg slammed the jeep to a halt and Walczak jolted forward slightly, casting a annoyed glare at Krieg, “Comrade, these damn American vehicles are nothing compared to automobiles in the Motherland, I can tell you that,” “I understand, Michel, but maybe learn to drive before you take me out,” responded Walczak, with a slightly harsh tone, “Dah, no need to get frustrated, Comrade Walczak,” giggled Krieg, slowly opening the door to the jeep, Ah, what can you do? He’s loveably clumsy thought Walczak, Both men stood in front of a 3 or 4 story building as the sign above the main door stated their location, PCW: Primetime Championship Wrestling “Ah, the embodiment of Jobber-ism,” said Krieg, “Don’t be so tough, Krieg. I used to wrestle here, as did Ivan,” replied the Pollack, a look of slight anger, but also one of an insulted man, Seeing Walczak’s mixed feelings, Krieg backtracks, “Ah, I’m sure it is a good place, Tom. Just some of the wrestler’s here are, you know, jobbers,” “I see. Let us go inside,” Both men lumbered up the steps and Krieg opened the heavy steel door of the PCW Headquarters, allowing his Polish comrade to walk in before he closed the door after stepping through the threshold, “I shall give them some credit, Comrade. They have nice interior,” said a sarcastic Krieg, “Oh, give it up Michel. Let’s find those you recommended,” growled Walczak, walking off towards a set of double doors, Striding to catch up with him, Krieg slammed open the doors and walked after Walczak as his Polish comrade stopped outside a locker room, the name tag on the door reading only one word: BODA “So this is the great Boda,” asked Krieg, “Yes, former member of Army. I thought he would be good first target, him being former World Champion, Communist and close personal friend of Walczak,” said the grinning Pollack, “Tom, you know friendship is only useful when a person is fighting the same battle as you. We shall see if this ‘Boda’ character still has what it takes to be a Red Army member...Some of these former PCW Red Army members have went a bi...” finished Krieg as he saw the look on Tom’s face changing to one of profound anger, “I’ll shut up now,” “I suggest you should,” ”Anyway, let’s see if your ‘comrade’ is in,” said Krieg as he knocked the door of Boda’s dressing room, There is silence for about 30 seconds before Krieg pounds on the door again, once again leading to a moment of silence. He raises his hand one more time, and as he brings it down to knock on the door, it swings open to reveal the giant Boda, Krieg’s paw stopping a few inches from Boda’s face, the former PCW World Champion standing with a look of confusion plastered across his face as he looked at Krieg, who shot right back at him with a matching look. But when Boda caught a glimpse of Walczak, he smiled and extended his hand to Tom, the two men shaking hands for a second or so, “Chris, how are you,” asked Walczak, looking quite pleased at having met up with his former Red Army cohort, “Ah, as well as can be expected...How’s the OSW? And who’s this giant you’ve brought with you,” questioned Boda, casting his eyes up to Krieg’s face, cracking a small smile at Krieg, the OSW’s only German entertaining him with a cheap smile back, “Oh, this is Michel Krieg. Michel, Boda. Boda, Michel,” “Nice to meet you, Mr. Krieg,” said Boda, once again extending his hand, this time in Krieg’s direction, With his arms across his chest, Krieg looked down at Boda, “We have no time for greetings...Tom tells me you are former member of Army...” “Well, yes, I use...” “That is all well and good. We are looking for new members of Army. Do you want to leave this dungheap of a federation and come work with real talent, real wrestlers, alongside real Communists in the OSW as part of Army,” asked Krieg, his expression still featureless, “Well, I’m not sure if I want to leave here guys. I’m doing quite well, ye know,” reasoned Boda, looking at Walczak as the Pollack gave him a sympathetic gesture, “Oh. I see. Money in pocket, Boda gets push...Tom, let us move on. This man is not of our taste,” claimed Krieg, turning his back to both men and moving off down the corridor, Giving Boda a shrug of the shoulders and a “I don’t know” kind of look, Walczak stood next to Boda as the PCW’s answer to Sebastian Toomes shouts after Krieg, “Hey, German! How about you find what you’re looking for? I think Walden carries some spare vibrators and lubrication!” Continuing to walk down the corridor, Krieg headed towards another set of double doors, completely disregarding Boda’s comments as Walczak made his excuses, said bye to Boda and strode off after Michel. Finally, Tom catches up with him, “Hey Comrade! No need to insult my friends,” “Tom, we will not get anywhere being nice. And anyway, that Boda character was too cocky for my liking. Bigger ego than Seabass Toomes,” claimed Krieg, Once again, Krieg spots Walczak’s look of distaste and makes sure not to offend his Polish friend, “But, emm, let’s go find these other fellows, shall we,” said Krieg with a smile, “Let’s. Maybe you won’t insult someone this time,” “We’ll see...” Both men continued to walk down the corridor and went to turn the corner when they suddenly bumped into two smaller men...Jack Valentine and Johnny Heart, otherwise known as The Heartbreakers, PCW’s most popular tag team, “Well if it isn’t...” “Michel Krieg and...” “Tom Walczak,” said the two men, both taking turns to fill in the sentence, “Yah, you are correct, Breakers of Hearts,” replied Krieg, “It’s Heart...” “Breakers, Mr. Krieg,” “Dah, whatever boys. Why do you stand in front of us,” “Ah, we heard you...” “Guys were here and we...” “Wanted to see what the competition...” “Looked like.” “I don’t suppose you two fellows would like to come work with us in the Red Army, now would you,” asked Walczak, presenting himself a bit better than Krieg, “Well, first of all, if we were to join the Red Army,” “We’d need to do something about the colours,” “Because you know, It’s All,” “About The PINK!” Both Krieg and Walczak look at each other as both Heartbreakers continue, “Secondly, we’d need to,” “Work on how...” Krieg’s frustration rages on again as he interrupts them, “Listen Breakers, we don’t have all day to listen to your half-assed rhymes, so are you with us or against us,” roared Krieg, “Well if you’re going...” “To be like that, we...” “Wouldn’t join the Brown Army if...” “In a million years, unless...” “You had Jay Leno wrestling in a pink cat suit, “And yellow shoes. Now, that’d rock!” As the two Heartbreakers discussed their dreams of stars in flamboyant outfits similar to theirs, Krieg just gave up again and stormed off down the corridor, Walczak walking proudly next to him, “We are not having much success here, Comrade,” said Walczak to his less-than-pleassed German comrade, Krieg, ”Yah, Tom. This has not been as successful as Krieg hoped, but the day shall get better, believe me. Let us find this next piece of talent,” “Good idea, Michel,” replied Walczak, the two men continuing to walk down the corridor and suddenly, Krieg stops outside of a dressing room, Walczak walking right into the back of him, “Hey Comrade! No need to walk so fast! You should not have been following so closely! Slow Down In Town, that’s what I always say,” chuckled Krieg, Walczak just sniggered, realising Krieg wasn’t always the angry man he looked to be, and still kept his sense of humour even on a day like this. Both men looked at the door as the sign read: JAY SYLO Knocking the door, Krieg and Walczak stand patiently as there is no response. Waiting for about 30 seconds, Krieg hammers the door again when suddenly, a dark shadow looms over the men, Walczak turning to see Jay Sylo standing behind them, but Krieg stands facing the door, oblivious to Sylo’s presence, “I can not believe some of these PCW wrestlers,” huffed Krieg, slamming the door again, “They’re never in their dressing rooms and when they are, it takes them half an hour to answer,” “Michel,” said Walczak, flashing a smile at Sylo, who gave him one back, “Tom, this is no time to talk. This man is too ignorant to even answer...” “Michel,” “Walczak, please do not interrupt me while I am speaking. Now, this ma...” “Michel,” said Walczak, the tone of his voice getting a bit sharper, Turning round, Krieg goes to shout back at Walczak and then notices Sylo standing there, “Tom, it is ve....Ah, you must be Comrade Sylo,” “I am no Comrade of a rude fellow like you, Krieg,” replied Sylo, “Ah, I see the PCW attitude problem is prominent in you also,” shouted back Krieg, Sylo just an inch or two taller than Krieg, “Maybe it’s the way you address people, man,” “There is nothi...I am not going to get into a conversation about addressing people. Myself and Comrade Walczak are here today to ask you if you would like to ally yourself with the Communists of The Red Army and really show the wrestling world what you are made of, instead of being stuck in this ‘scuzpit’ of a federation,” replied Krieg as he looked around in distaste, “Krieg,” “Yes, Comrade Sylo,” “The next time I want to join the Red Army, I think I’ll sabotage my career, belittle my name and offend everyone in wrestling before I do,” “Ah, so that is your attitude, Ameri-kun,” roared Krieg, “Yeah, ‘Ameri-kun’ and proud of it, Nazi,” “Why I never....Walczak, it is time to leave,” “Bu...” “Now Walczak. Let’s leave,” said Krieg as he walked off down the corridor, “But we’ve not recruited anyone yet...” questioned Walczak, “It does not matter Walczak, there are better wrestlers out there,” replied the German monster, whipping open the main doors and climbing down the steps to his jeep, “Ah, whatever Comrade,” Both men climbed into the jeep and Krieg reversed it in a hurry, the camera just catching him as he shifted it into first gear, “I believe RUSH has some talent...” The jeep roared out of the car park as the Red Army Communists left, no better off than they’d started...
