Best Buddies? (Michel/Reno series 3/3)

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 28, 2002 The screen pictures Reno Davis and Michel Krieg as they come down separate corridors towards the rear entrance to the arena, the screen split into two sections as both cameramen follow their consecutive wrestlers. Suddenly, both sides of the screen picture the exit sign of the rear entrance as both Krieg and Davis make their way to the door. As both men try to walk through at the same time, they bump into each other. Stopping, Krieg motions for Davis to go through first, “Age before beauty, Ameri-kun,” Chuckling as he walks through the door, Davis fires back, “Pit you lack the latter, and I’m Canadian you ignorant fascist.” Slightly annoyed, Krieg thinks to himself about what Shanda Russell told him about Ed’s Non-Violence Stipulation, Better let this cocky Capitalist have his fun…Wouldn’t want to miss our title shots tonight, would I? Ivan thinks to himself. Better annoy this idiot to all extremes...It’d be funny to watch him get suspended. Reno thinks to himself. Davis bounds ahead of Krieg and steps into the mini-bus awaiting them. Striding across the dimly lit car park, Krieg power-walks towards the mini-bus and suddenly, ZaC, Davis’ furry sidekick, bounds past him, nearly causing Krieg to fall as he tries to step around the little Neanderthal, “Why can’t he keep that hairy beast under control? Toomes manages to do it most of the time with Reagan,” Eventually, Michel reaches the mini-bus and spots Davis sitting a couple of seats from the back of the bus, playing with his cell phone. But as Krieg goes to step into the bus, ZaC once again skitters past his giant feet with a handful of bananas and this time, Krieg isn’t so lucky and stumbles into the bus, landing on a double seat at the front of the bus, “Watch your step there fella,” commentated Davis, a pair of Oakley shades covering his unusual eyes as well as his flamboyant red hair, “Ha ha, Capitalist,” shouted Krieg, and then spoke quietly to himself, “You shall not be laughing when Ivan and the Army crush you,” “You say something there, Michel?” asked Reno as the mini-bus began to move off, “Emm…Yes. I was just…uhh…singing a nursery rhyme…” “You’re becoming quite a well-known singer, Krieg. Maybe you should give up wrestling and start up a band,” joked the former j0lt star, “You could call yourself Michel and Swazis. Or how about the Clannies? Because supporting Hitler is basically like supporting the KKK...” Reno said, solid faced. Just count to ten, Krieg Michel huffed to himself. “Eins…Zwei…Drei…Vier…Funf…Sechs…Sieben,” Davis pondered to himself as ZaC sat on his knee and tapped his little paws on the window at other drivers, Almost at breaking point Reno!! Passing some houses on their right, Krieg, Davis and ZaC kept quiet until Reno broke the silence with what he thought was a reasonable question, “So what are you and Walczak gonna do when you lose tonight? Go after some other minority?” Krieg suddenly sat up fully up and looked round at Davis, his eyes were wide with anger, “And who says we’ll lose tonight, Ameri-kun?” “I told you that I’m Canadian. Do I look like some propaganda totting, arrogant, imperialist, cry baby to you?” Reno shot off. “Takes one to know one...Crap-head” Michel snickered as Reno squinted, missing the insult. The big bus began to drive off and both men said nothing during the entire ride. Krieg started to sing along to a song on the radio, but heard Reno laugh and he stopped. Both men unloaded at the local Blockbuster Video, who hoped to profit off of both men. Fans began to gather around the entrance and gave off a chorus of boos when Krieg exited the bus. He shrugged them off at first, but the boos turned to cheers when Reno and ZaC leaped onto Krieg’s shoulders and began to holler at the crowd. Reno didn’t notice this time, but Michel was counting to twenty for this one. “Come on Krieggy, my fans await.” Reno said with a playful punch to Krieg’s arm. Michel swore in German and slowly began to follow the tiny Canadian into the Blockbuster Video. “And this goes out to?” Reno asked the fan in front of him. Krieg and Davis both sat at the same table, signing off random items that associated with each wrestler. Reno never noticed that he had a fanbase until he joined OSW, since he never had a great deal of success in any other federation. Whereas Michel signed a lot of autographs for the sake of signing them, because if you meet a pro-wrestler signing autographs for free, you might as well get one. Just to be funny, when a fan asked for both Krieg and Davis to sign something, Reno would draw a little swastika underneath Krieg’s name along with a heart encircling the signature as well. Reno thought it looked cute. ‘Hey sweetheart, would you mind passing me another pen please? Mine is dry.” Reno asked Michel, who rolled his eyes and pulled a pen out of his pant’s pocket. It was an expensive fountain pen, which Reno broke immediately and handed back to Krieg. “Must have been made in ‘Ameri-kuh’ huh?” Reno joked. Michel didn’t laugh. “You have nerve Cahndian! Lots of it!” Krieg exclaimed. “My name is Reno, I come from Ca-na-da. Let’s say it together.” “I will not be mo-“ “Ca...” “What?” “Ca...” “Enough of the childishness!” Krieg yelled, slamming his fist into the table. A few fans laughed. “Now Krieggy, you’re going to have to cooperate here.” “I will not-” “Ca...” Reno repeated. Krieg stood up and raised his fist as if to punch Reno, who flashed a 5-dollar bill, reminding Krieg of the consequences. Michel sat back down. “You are indeed lucky today Ameri-” Krieg started. “Canadi-” Reno began, only to be interrupted in turn. “I DO NOT GIVE A CARE!” Reno laughed at Krieg and signed another autograph, it was going to be a long day for Krieg...
