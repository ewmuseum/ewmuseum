The Thoughts of a Heretic

20 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/20/02 There are twelve men sitting around a large rectangular table. There is something wrong though with the picture. The men’s garments are not what one would be used to these days. The men are not wearing your traditional pants, shirts, and maybe even a necktie. Rather, they are wearing flowing robes and garments. It appears that this is the past, about two thousand years ago to be precise. This isn’t even the United States, this is some foreign land. The men all share the same concerned look on their faces. The men look around the room at one another all asking why he has called them together on this evening for dinner. “This is unusual for him to behave this way” or “He has been acting funny lately. I hope that everything is okay.” These are some of things that the men are asking one another. Many of them are talking amongst themselves, while others remain silent wrapped up in their own thoughts. There is one man in particular that seems out of place compare to the others. He sits among all of the other men, but he seems more relaxed then the others. He seems complacent, calm, and collected. He wears a smug expression on his face. He begins to drink his wine without a thought of waiting for their leader. The others shoot dirty looks at him as he indulges himself in the delightful drink. As he lowers the mug from his face, he sees the looks that he is receiving from the others. The man gets completely wrapped up in his own thoughts as he stares back at those that would persecute him. “Look at all of them, they think that they are better then me. They believe that they are more worthy to sit in his presence. I will show them all. I am the one that will save all of their lives. If it wasn’t for me, they would all share the same fate that he is going to.” A heavy wooden door opens in the back of the room. The man that enters through the door is the one that they have all been waiting for. He is the type of man that consumes everyone’s attention when he enters a room. No one can help but to focus on him. There is a certain presence about him that would be unsettling if it were not for his gentle nature. All of the men rise to their feet in order to greet their leader. He greets them with a warm and welcoming smile. The outcast is filling with anger and jealousy. “Look at the way that he pulls the wool over these fools eyes. It is I that they should be welcoming with that much warmth. I am the one that is sparing them from their lives of futile death. Look at the way that they grovel at his feet and praise him as if he is the lord. This man is leading them to their death. He is not the savior that they are all making him to be.” The leader of the group finally makes his way through everyone else and arrives in front of the doubting man. The gentle man looks upon his doubting follower and only lowers his eyes in pity as if to say “I know what you did and someday you will be forgiven.” He then just passes by the traitor and sits at the head of the table. That look just enrages the man further. He is the first one sit of all the followers and does so proving a point. His mind is filled with raging thoughts. “How dare he look at me that way! He acts as if I am not worthy to sit in the presence of these men. As if they all have something more than I do! As if they are more deserving than I am! Little do they all know that I will be the one that is the most remembered for what I have done! I will be the one that walks away from this evening with dry eyes and a smile in his face. This is the end of the road for this man that that has lied to us for so long. He brought us together this evening and I will be the one that destroys all of their dreams and hopes for the future. I will be remembered by everyone for what I have done and all of them will be forgotten. I am bringing him closer to what he wants anyway.” The leader of the group begins to pray at the head of the table. All of the men lower their heads in meditation that is except the one traitor that looks around at the men who do everything that this man says. He continues to brood wild and crazy thoughts. “They all behave like sheep and he is their shepherd. Go ahead and follow him! More lambs to be slaughtered! This is how it has to be! There is nothing else that can be done now. The wheels of fate are in motion. This man will fall and then all of his pathetic followers will be hunted down and slain. This is the end of the line for many of these men. Their dreams crushed. Their hopes destroyed. Their lives threatened. Their nerves searing in pain.” As the prayer continues, something strange comes over the man. He begins to breath heavier and his face begins to contort. It appears as if something evil is now taking over his entire body. Not changing it, just taking control. A disturbing smile stretches across his lips as he stares around the room at those that he loathes. His thoughts seem to be consumed by something else now. “Death! That is the only thing that they all deserve! They will al perish at my hands on this memorable evening that will plague the minds of mortals for all of eternity. I will live in their minds! I will always and forever be associated with the downfall of these men. And who’s name will pop into everyone’s mind? Not Paul! Not Luke! Not Peter! Mine! My name will be the one that they always associate with this evening. It is my night! Not theirs! MINE!” The man at the head of the table is now talking to his loyal sheep and preaching to them about what they need to do as his followers. There are ears in all of their eyes as they look on and listen to his teachings. All of them are so wrapped up in the preaching of their leader that none of them notice that the traitor has exited the room. He is outside of the inn now and walking the dark streets alone. His thoughts are consumed with these evil visions depicting the death of several of the followers. He is in a frenzy now and the evil has completely consumed him. He walks the streets in a complete daze, paying no attention to anyone that he passes. When he bumps into people, he simply continues walking past them as they scream obscenities at him. His thoughts are even more deranged then before. “Death! They all deserve it! And that is what they will get! I will be the only one that will come out of this evening with anything to gain. They will all be left in the rubble that I have created for them. They will be left to pick up the pieces and try to continue on, while they are being hunted down like vermin in the streets no matter where they flea to.” The disturbed man turns a corner and finds himself standing in front of two guards. The men look at him waiting some sort of answer. The man looks at both of them. His sinister smile grows even larger than before. He finally addresses them. “He will be in the garden behind the inn shortly. Allow me to lead you to them. And please, don’t spare any of them.” The scene begins to change violently. It fades into black. Suddenly, a sleeping Azrael Cain is seen. He is in his old abandoned factory sleeping by a roaring fire and wrapped up in his bearskin cloak. He awakens quickly. He looks a little confused for the moment, but he gets himself in control quickly. A smile grows on his face. His small friend, Caleb is close by and sees that he is awakened. Caleb looks confused about the situation, but finally checks on his friend. “You okay ther ebig guy? Heh-Heh. Looks like you had yourself one hell of a nightmare> Heh-Heh-Heh.” Cain looks at his manager for a second then decides to answer. “Nightmares? I know no nightmares. This was a sweet vision of some of my more handy work..” The hobo looks extremely confused as the beast rolls over to get more comfortable on the old decrepit factory floor. Cain continues to talk though. “I am the nightmare. I am every one of my opponents’ nightmares this week. They will know what true fear and evil is after Saturday.” Cain drifts back to sleep as the old hobo continues to stare at him with a smile and a very confused look. The scene fades to black.
