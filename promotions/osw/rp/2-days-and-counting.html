Date: Jan. 31 The date is closing in: February 2, 2002, or 2-2-02, if you like numerology. On this date is the opening of OSW, Old School Wrestling. With the announcement of most staff in OSW, more and more seems to be revealed with the passing of each day - except for who the ship jumpers are. The people who will leave PCW for OSW, should Ritchie Syxx be happy that more people are coming to OSW? Of course not. If people are going to jump ship, they won’t be just jobbers, but Mid-Card and Upper-card type of guys, which means, Syxx’ll be thrown into the shuffle that is the Mid-Card. Of course Syxx likes getting attention, so he doesn’t want this to happen. And with two (2) days left until the opening, Syxx better be preparing for the ship-jumping to happen. We open up at a Gym, pretty big place with red brick walls and a small parking lot. The outside says ‘POWERHOUSE’ with a bulky muscular arm pumping a very large dumbel, and the sounds of pumping iron echo from the building’s inside. And then, we zoom to the building next to it. A Shop Rite, or for our non-American readers who aren‘t aware of American stores, a supermarket. It’s basically a large rectangular building with stone gray walls, The only non-plain looking feature is the shopping cart that serves as a sign, with the words Shop Rite next to it in neon red. The parking lot is disturbingly large. So which one is Ritchie Syxx in? Neither! Ok, I’m lying, he’s in............the supermarket. How anti-climatic, everyone knew he was going to be in the supermarket. But what can I do? We see Ritchie step into the building, pushing a cart on his way in. He wears black leather pants, a shirt with a black Porsche on it and black leather boots. Did I mention he looks like a jackass in the aforementioned clothes? The automatic doors open and Ritchie enters the Shop Rite, the first thing he sees is the fruit section. Almost every kind of fruit can be seen in brown wood displays, Ritchie walks up to the banana section, picks it up and smells it. “Smells good,” he says. Ritchie begins to gently stroke the banana. God knows why. He looks at thge other displays, still stroking the banana. At this point, he has put in about 100 to many strokes to the banana, and looks like a pervert to passers by. They look at him and wonder why the hell a blue mohawk-ed man strokes a banana in front of people with a smile on his face. He finally puts the banana into a bag and into the cart. He moves open to the watermellon stand. Many big juicy watermellons are piled together on top of one another. Ritchie looks at the mellons and immediately calls out something. “LOOK HOW BIG THOSE MELLONS ARE!” Basically, the whole god damn place hears him. His voice echoes throughout the place, making him seem like a even bigger pervert. “I...I’m sorry people,” he says. He turns around to slowly walk away, but he trips and falls onto the mellons. They fall everywhere, mellons scatter amongst the floor. “I’m sorry for that as well.” Ritchie picks himself up, and pushes his cart as fast as he can out of that section and into the aisle parts. He moves onto Aisle 2, the Cereal Aisle. All imagineable cereals and cereal rip offs lie here. Syxx humms to himself, while pushing the cart up the aisle and looking at each cereal. “Oh my god!......Lucky Charms!!!! Hearts, Stars, and Horseshoes, Clovers and Ballons, something something something!” Syxx picks up a box of Lucky Charms and puts it into his cart. He then takes 3 more boxs and puts those into his cart. So far he has: 4 boxes of Lucky Charms and a banana. Big spendin’. He moves into Aisle 4, the Breakfast Food Aisle. He picks up a bottle of Aunt Jemima. “I’d ask you something stupid pretending that you were real, but Barney Gumble beat me to the punch on that one Simpson’s edisode.” “Yeah, the one with the monstro mart,” replies Aunt Jemima. “Yeah and where Apu left the Kwiky mart, who needs the Kwiky mart?” “Not meeeeeeeee,” sings Aunt Jemima. “Excuse me...sir,” says an emplyee of the store. This kid is freckly, about 5”7’ and wearing the uniform that consists of green pants and a vest. He has dirty ugly brown hair and speaks with a rather high pitched voice. “Yeah? Man, I can’t believe how rude some people are. I’m having a friendly conversation with a bottle of Syrup and you just barge right in on us.” “That’s exactly the point, you’re talking to a bottle of syrup!” “I am??” “Yeah.” “Ahh!!” Ritchie drops the bottle of Aunt Jemima and the glass shatters. “I’m dying...heeeeeeeeeelp,” says Aunt Jemima, who’s syrup pours out. “Sir, are you ok?” “No, I’m kool.” “Are you sure? Talking to syrup bottles I think can qualify as psychophrenia.” “That’s a big word.” The employee sighs. “Should I just kick you out of here and end the promo or should I say something like ‘hey, you’re Ritchie Syxx, how do you feel about your match with Corg’.” “I’ll go with the match with Corg........I am so totally going to kick his ass!” “That’s all you got?! Now we have to fade to black with a bad memorable statement!!!” We fade to black as the employee shows Ritchie how to write a proper rp, proper dialogue and ambiance and so on. We see a black screen with a circle in the center. The circle has a white outline and a red center. We see Ritchie Syxx who pops up from the center and waves his arm at us. “Beeeeeebadee......that’s...LINE...folks”. We fade out for real after that.
