The Winner is in the Eye of the Beholder

14 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2002-08-14 The first thing Lillith was aware of was the feeling of thousands of needles sticking into her face and neck. The second was a large sledgehamer slamming into her head over and over. With that thought came the realization that she was dead, and this was hell. Lillith cracked an eye open, and sunlight from her hotel window seared her retina, causing her to yelp in pain and squint her eye shut. 'Not dead... worse. Hung over...' Her body shuddered for a moment, and she debating just laying on the cheap rug under her all day, but that wouldn't accomplish much. With a herculean effort, she hauled herself to her hands and knees. Grabbing the coffee table for support (she evidentally passed out in the living room of the two-room suite), she managed to get to her feet long enough to collapse into a nearby arm chair. She put her hand to her head to try to ease the pounding headache, when she felt a large lump on her head and a few cuts. 'What did happen last night?' she wondered. Closing her eyes, she tried to remeber the events of last night.... She received a phone call from the OSW booking department, informing her of the big main event match she was in. Rather than feel excited about the match, and the potential to have a title shot, she was rather upset - pissed would be a better word actually. it was just another 'women's match', and the title possibility was just a way to make her feel better about it, she was sure. OSW had screwed her over again. Lillith looked over at the in-room bar, seeing the door now half open, and empty liquor miniatures scattered around it. 'Oh yeah...' she muttered... With the mini-fridge emptied, she was tipsy, but just more frustrated. In a huff, she wobbled down to the lobby and hailed a cab. She told the cabbie to take her to a quiet bar where she could get plastered and forget the state of her life, or some such nonsense, and ended up at a place called... 'It was called... uhh... meh, doesn't matter...' She walked into the bar, the only people being the bartender and a woman slumpled over the counter. She stumbled over to the stool next to her, calling loudly for a bottle of tequila and a shotglass, and sat/fell onto the stool. The bartender put down 3 shotglasses, and filled them. Then filled the three of Lillith's neighbor. Lillith gunned down the first two one after the other, but stopped before the third as she got a better glimpse of the other woman. Red hair, eccentric clothing... she knew her, it was... umm... 'That's right,' she said, 'It was Amy Campbell.' Wasn't she underaged? 'Hey!' muttered Lillith, her voice sounding a little shrill in her own years, 'Are you supposed to be in here?' Amy turned her head drunkenly, her bloodshot eyes looking at Lillith... and giggled! 'Wha?' Lillith downed the third shot, and called for more, before talking to Amy again. 'Aren't you... umm... too young... hehehe', Lillith broke into giggles. A chuckle escaped Lillith, 'She looked like shit, too...' Amy struggles to remove the wallet from her back pocket, and waved it in front of Lillith, almost dropping it. 'All the ID I need, right here,' exclaimed Amy, as she downed another shot. Lillith struggled to keep her gigling in check, and with a big 'shitfaced' grin, said 'I'm telling!' Amy looked back, 'Wha?' and started to giggle - it's contaguious! Lillith took anoher shot before continuing, 'OSW doesn't like employess engagenining in illegal activities. And what with you being Miss Illicit Substance Seach 2002...' Lillith let the thought trail off and took her 6th shot in 10 minutes. 'yeah...' 'Wait a second,' said Lillith to herself, 'Did I actually say 'engagenining?'' Amy turned to face Lillith fully, tequila-smelling breath blowing lightly into her face, 'Does it look like I give a ffffflying ffffFFuck what OSW doesn't and doesn't like?' Lillith giggled at the double negative, 'Soooo Sorrrryyy, but after the clauses they included about Corg doing whatever the hell he likes, The whole 'better be good for the company' thing is a big joke!' Lillith remembers conversation broke off at that point, and both her and Aimz went back to drinking in silence. Then... it was hazy... didn't Amy say something...? Amy giggled and said out loud, 'Besides, I've been hearing you and Reagan have been getting it on. Trying to sleep your way up like Seabass?' Lillith looked at Amy in shock, and said 'Excuse me?' before grabbing an empty tequila bottle and smashing it over Aimz head. 'And that's when the fight started,' muttered Lillith, trying to remeber the blurry details. She knew the bartender was enjoying himself, and then someone else came in... 'Who was that..?' The fight didn't last long, ending with Amy's head met the bar with a loud thunk. Lillith quickly turned at the sound of applause, to see Tempest standing in the doorway.... 'And she bought me another shot to contadulate me,' Lillith smiled. 'One less person to worry about, she said.' Now that the night's events came back to her, Lillith nodded to congradulate herself... and her head exploded in renewed pain. Groaning, she managed to get off the bed, and stumbled into the bathroom for several aspirin. (Aimz's story's a bit different: /roleplays/messages/859.shtml)
