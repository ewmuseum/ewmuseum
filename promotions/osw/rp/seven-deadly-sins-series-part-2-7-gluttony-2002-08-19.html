'Seven Deadly Sins' series; Part 2/7; 'Gluttony'

19 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19th August 2002 'Bing Bong' 'Clear up on aisle 10.' Says a voice of an unknown female. The scene fades in from black, as we see a busy super market. The camera spins round catching a glimpse of a few aisles, a few shoppers going about their everyday business and the tills. Then it stops when it sees a double door. Out through it is Eric Sagar and Propaganda hobble in pushing a shopping trolley. 'My Brother…' Propaganda calls Sagar. 'What worries thou brother Propaganda?' Sagar queries. 'What's… a Kellogs?' Propaganda asks. 'Don't worry about that my brother. Kellogs is purely an atheist franchise that sells breakfast cereals.' Sagar comments. 'Oh.' Propaganda loses interest after Eric's ramblings. 'Well, my brother. No Matter as all we need is a water melon for tonight's 'Protestants in Science and Sport' party. 'Is that what they're called?' Propaganda asks dumfounded. 'Yes.' Sagar confirms. 'PISS!' Propaganda states 'BROTHER PROPAGANDA I HARDLY THINK THAT LANGUAGE IS ACCEP--' Propaganda interrupts Sagar's rant. 'No, their initials are P-I-S-S. Piss.' Propaganda still quite shocked. 'Oh… Ugh… I suppose they are.' Sagar stutters rather embarrassed. After a long spell of silence the men head off with their shopping cart. 'Aisle 1, eh?' Sagar says scanning the aisles. 'Mostly alcohol.' Propaganda says with much disgust. 'Bah.' Sagar shows his disgust. The pair move to aisle two. 'Aisle 2 is…' Sagar checks the shelves. 'Moronic confectioneries, processed foods and ready made meals.' The Power Himself comments. 'Let's move on.' Sagar suggests. 'And over here we have?' 'Toiletries.' Propaganda scoffs. 'Oh Dear.' Sagar sighs. 'Finally Aisle 4.' Sagar proclaims. 'Fruit, Vegetables and Herbs.' Propaganda says proudly. 'Here's the place.' Sagar says rather relieved. 'Water Melons are situated over yonder!' Propaganda says pointing to a green box with a few Water Melons in. The two head over too it and start rummaging in it. 'Which one to choose?' Sagar asks. 'THIS ONE!' Propaganda roars holding up a melon. 'YOU SEE THIS FOR IT IS A SIGN! A MIRACLE FROM GOD! IT HAS A CRUCIFIX INDENTED IN IT!!!!' Sagar looks at the melon squinting. 'It looks like a Rabbit.' Sagar comments. 'You know Blasphemy is wrong my brother!!!' Propaganda condemns Sagar's comment. 'No, Look there's one ear, there's the other…' 'Oh Yeah…' Propaganda says 'It does kind of look like a rabbit, but turn it round.' 'Nope… no Crucifix my brother.' 'BLABBER!' Propaganda dismisses Sagar's comment. 'Purchase it anyway.' 'I don't see why not.' Sagar comments. Propaganda drops the water melon in the shopping trolley and they head over to the nearest till. By it sits an attractive young lady with long blond hair and a white and blue striped dress. She insolently is chewing gum and twiddling it around her finger. 'Excuse me.' Sagar says as Propaganda drops the water melon in front of her. 'What!?' She says rather annoyed. Eric looks rather mad. 'Could I possible purchase this water melon.' Sagar says with a somewhat mock politeness. 'Jesus Christ! What did your last slave die of?' the girl rhetorically asks. 'BLASPHEMY IS A SIN! THE DEVIL SHALT FRY YOUR SOUL! FRY YOUR SOUL LIKE A CHICKEN WING AND THE HANDS OF COLNEL FLANDERS YOUNG LADY! YOU SHALT PAY FOR YOUR IGNOR-' Propaganda's very loud rant has the whole super market is silence and staring at the prophet they call 'Propaganda.' 'Calm down!' Sagar requests. The girl looks at Propaganda like some he's got a rainbow coloured lizard stuck on his face. 'That'll be… um… $2.95 please.' She says after running the melon threw the cash machine. 'Coming right up.' Sagar says whilst rummaging in through his pockets. 'Um… Urgh… Arh… Awww…. Eeeer.' Sagar proceeds to pull the whole contents of his pockets onto the counter in front of him. 'That's $1.37. You're umm.' The lady counts her fingers. 'You're $1.52 short.' 'It's $1.5--' Sagar is about to correct the check out girl. 'SHUT UP!' Propaganda roars. 'Oh… Ummm… Yeah. $1.52' Sagar blurts. 'Well… how you gonna pay for it then?' The girl asks. 'I'm going to… PREACH!' Sagar says. 'YOU WHAT!?' The girl is gob smacked. 'Yeah Preach.' Sagar backs up his statement. 'What?' Propaganda says, who like the checkout girl is unsure. 'MY BROTHERS AND SISTERS LISTEN TO MY WORDS! FOR I COME TO YOU WITH A MESSAGE, LIKE AN ANGEL! I COME WITH A MESSAGE FROM GOD! JOHN 3:16 - 'GOD GAVE HIS ONLY--' 'Uhr, Would Security please escort this man out of the building please.' 'BEGOTTEN SON SO THAT ALL WHO BELIEVE IN HIM MAY HAVE ETERNAL LIFE!' MY BROTHERS IF YOU BELIEVE THEN WHEN THE TIME COMES FOR YOUR LIFE TO END YOU WILL BE…' Two rather bulky security guards approach the desk. 'We must ask you to leave the store, sir.' Says one. 'WHAT IS THIS!?' Sagar shouts. 'Please sir, we don't want to use force.' The other says. 'YOU BAFOONS! YOU WILL PAY FOR--' Sagar is interrupted. 'We're just leaving.' Propaganda says has he sternly nudges Sagar forward who is still rambling on as they leave the store and the scene slowly fades to black.
