Going Home (1/2)

21 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 21, 2002 From Ottawa, Reno decided to stop by his home town of Moncton, New Brunswick, Canada and see how his old friends were doing. He hitchhiked onward and on Tuesday, May 21, 2002, Reno Davis was home. Oak Park was Reno’s real home. The small park was really just an oversized alley off of Main Street. 11 months out of the year it was also run down due to some drunk-punks feeling like breaking everything that the city worked so hard at making nice. Reno always shook his head at such stupidity. If you’re going to hang around somewhere, you might as well have it nice to look at. May was one of the 11 months. “Reno!” A foreign voice called from the far end of Oak Park at Reno, who had just entered one of the two entrances. He looked up and noticed that it was Brent calling at him. Brent was one of his good friends at the park; it was a pity that Reno never knew him as a child, or any of the “punks” from Oak Park during his childhood. It seemed that they accepted who he was. “Brent?” Reno asked, although he knew the answer. He opened his arms and hugged his slim friend who wore a tattered mo-hawk on his head. Brent’s mo-hawks were always tattered due to the fact that he always did stupid things to his hair like put house paint in it or industrial glue. Brent was a nice guy, but lacked in some of the intelligence departments. Brent planted a kiss on Reno’s forehead, (a friendly kiss, not a homosexual one for all you homophobes out there) and called out to his friends who were sitting around in the side alley. Oak Park, which was an alley in itself, had it’s own alley. Various individuals appeared out from the alley and wandered over towards Reno and Brent. Brent wore red bondage pants and a simple black t-shirt, made into a muscle shirt, that read “Democracy, We Deliver” with a picture of a plane dropping bombs. Reno realized how badass Brent looked in bondage pants at that moment, and also realized how grimy he looked with his long unwashed red hair, big hobo coat and now ripped up jeans. He was lucky that after the run-in with the creepy and familiar masked dude, that urine stains weren’t now a part of the faded jeans. Most of the people who arrived seemed to look the same. Each either had liberty-spiked hair, or colored mo-hawks and wore ripped up jeans and big boots among there fairly new band t-shirts. Oak Park had changed a lot since Reno had last been there. Some held brown paper bags with undoubtedly Colt 45 inside; Maybe Oak Park hadn’t changed THAT much. “Everybody, this is Reno Davis. THE Reno Davis of like those pro wrestling places! He’s cool shit and he’s been hanging out here longer than any of you poser combined! He’s cool shit.” Brent stressed. A few nodded their heads and a few just stared in either amazement, or drunken stupor. It was so hard to tell these days. “Hi,” The Unwanted Angel said simply, “How’s it going?” “So you were in Survivor right? The fWo one I mean,” Said the one with green liberty-spikes and an old ‘Crass’ shirt that read: “Jesus Died For His Own Sins, Not Mine”. Reno smiled at the shirt and knew damn well that this kid couldn’t back up a word of his hatred for religion. That’s the way things are these days though. “Yeah, that was...Like in 99. I’ve been in a lot more companies since then,” Reno replied. A few muttered and still some stared, the ones that stared really drove Reno nuts. He hated stares, but kept calm. He realized how hungry he was and looked at Brent. “Wanna grab something to eat?” He asked. Brent nodded and the two waved goodbye to the ‘punks’ and went off to grab some food. “So...what happened to all the old guys? I mean these new kids are so...” Reno trailed off as he sucked down some water from a cup. He and Brent sat facing each other at a nearby Burger King in downtown Moncton. “Bah,” Brent intervened as he stuffed part of a hamburger into his mouth, “These guys are good kids. Just trying too hard. The old kids all..grew up.” “Ah,” Reno nodded. He’d seen many people just magically grow up and become adults. You’d think that being an adult meant to stop having any fun at all and dress in suits all the time. Reno was grown up and he only dressed in a suit during his ska trip during his old Asylum days...But Reno never really spoke of the Asylum much. “So what have you been doing? It’s been months...” Brent nodded again, cramming the rest of his burger into his mouth along with a few fries. Brent was a friggin’ pig sometimes. “I’ve...I’ve been trying to finish school. I’ve been straight for 2 months now too.” “Bullshit.” “Okay, 3 weeks.” “Bullshit” “Fine...fuck...I’ve been drinking once a week for 3 weeks.” “That’s better...Damn you were getting drunk everyday when I last spoke to ya. It’s about time it caught with you.” Reno shook his head smiling. “Yeah...I was getting headaches all the time and I went to rehab. I think I’m going to fail this year again though.” “Brent...you only need one credit to graduate. How many classes did you take?” “5 each semester.” “And you past how many in the first?” “None...I got real sick.” “You mean real drunk?” “Yeah...something like that.” “Awww....Brent...fuck man...” Reno rubbed his eyes in disappointment. “Well dammit man. I figured it wouldn’t be so hard to pass ONE course!” “You’re twenty fucking three! You should be halfway through university or college. Are you working?” “I panhandle.” “You have a home.” “No,” Brent chomped on a fry, “My folks booted me out.” He finished, and Reno sighed. “You have to start taking life more seriously.” “HAH! This coming from the pro-wrestler! What are you going to do when that big guy you pissed off breaks your damn legs? Huh? You have nothing to go back on! You’re fucking screwed man! Look at the size of you ya runt! This Stanmanilaman dude is twice your size and you tried to get in the sack with his chick.” “It wasn’t like that!” “Bah! Who’ve you got set up now for Reno’s row?” Brent asked, bringing up the fact that anyone who Reno’s become close with has been hurt severely. “Nobody...” “You do realize I watch you on tv right? Who’s the hot red head?” “Amy. She’s cool shit.” “Hot shit.” “She’s a nice girl.” “She’s got a nice ass.” “Fuck off Brent.” Reno whipped a fry at Brent, ricocheting it off his face and on the floor. Brent picked it up and ate it. Brent always did stuff like that. “You’re a good guy Reno.” “Thanks Brent...Hey, have you seen Mikey lately?” “Freight? Naw...Hasn’t been around for a while. That fucking thing you have watching him is very protective.” Brent shuddered. “You mean Frank? Haha, he’s got his eye on you I see?” Reno laughed hard. Brent nodded and hung his head. “I thought you weren’t into that whole homophobe thing Brent.” Reno poked Brent’s forehead. “Yeah, but when he wants you out of his house he does his stupid over-exaggerated gay guy routine.” “That over-exaggerated gay guy routine was what made him the top heel in POW you know. I heard he didn’t do too bad in that underground fight club thing last summer either...But the cops busted that.” “Yeah, he was let down. He’d found a really nice guy up there or something named like Wippit Guud or something. Once the place closed, the guy took off without Frank. He cried for weeks.” “You didn’t comfort him?” Reno grinned. “Fuck off!” Brent smiled and looked at his watch, “Aww, I have to go dude.” “For what? You have no life remember?” Reno joked. “I have a girlfriend now. I have to help her do some stuff.” “Stuff...gotcha.” Reno made the ‘whipped’ noise and stood up, “I’ll see ya later then. I’m gonna head over and see Mikey and Frank. I miss them a lot as much as it pains me to say.” Brent smirked and scratched his hand through his mo-hawk, strands of hair falling out into his palm. Reno cocked his head and then shook it. The two men exited from the restaurant together.
