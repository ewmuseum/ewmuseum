Talk about tough love. Why is Kid Wonder such a wuss?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sunday SLAM was an eventful night. Sure, Kid Wonder wasn’t on the card – again – but that doesn’t put a damper on what happened. Saturday night, Kimbusa went down in defeat. And he didn’t just go down in defeat. Kimbusa went down in defeat…alone. He lost on his own. He lost to a relatively untalented newcomer named Griff Truxell. That proved something K-Dub pretty much knew the second he met Kimbusa. It proved that Kimbusa couldn’t win on his own. And if that wasn’t enough, Bethany Yamato got totally punked out by Stormbringer’s half-sister or whatever she is today. Aimz beat Beth senseless and left her retreating to the back. Why is this so good? Why is it so important? It gives Kid Wonder hope. With the fact that Kimbusa pinned him at Head on Collision still lingering over his head, Wonder needed something that could possibly take the wind out of their sails. And what better timing than now. Both of the Japs suffered down moments this past Saturday. Wonder can keep them on their downward spiral. He can do that by pinning the same woman that caused her to lose to Kimbusa at the PPV. Kid Wonder can go and beat Bethany Yamato. Easier said than done. Unless Yamato suffers a heart attack, or she goes through some sort of womanly thing, Kid Wonder is gonna have to beat the crap out of Bethany. And while she may be a little bitch, and while he may have hit her a couple weeks ago on SLAM, Kid Wonder has always had a hard time hitting a lady. In fact, except for beating Sassy to a pulp in his last days of PCW, K-Dub has never fought a woman outside of inter-gender matches. And even then he was never in the ring against the girl. So while beating Bethany would be easy for the teen idol to do, it’s all a matter of bringing himself to doing it. That brings us to today. Six days before Wonder’s semi-final round match against Yamato. Thanks to Amanda, Kid Wonder is no longer learning from former Real World: Seattle cast member Stephen Williams. Amanda has now taken it upon herself to get Wonder into female brutality. She’s gonna get him ready to beat Bethany Yamato within an inch of her pointless, miserable life. Or she’s gonna try to. “Hit me,” we hear Amanda say as we fade into a hotel room. “I can’t,” Wonder tells her. Amanda, wearing some gray sweats, stands over her boyfriend as he sits in a chair. “Do it!” she shouts. Wonder, wearing an “In Like Flynn” shirt and some basketball shorts, looks up at Amanda. “I can’t.” Amanda has an intense look in her eyes as Wonder lowers his head. Just then… SMACK! Amanda smacks him across the face. “Hit me!” she shouts again. “You’ll never learn any other way!” Wonder sighs. “I just can’t do it, babe,” he says softly to her. “I can’t hurt you like that.” SMACK! “I’m gonna keep hitting you until you hit me back,” she tells him. Wonder, in response, shakes his head. SMACK! “You’re turning into a wuss,” she says to her boyfriend. “If you go in there with a wussy attitude, Bethany Yamato is gonna eat you alive.” “I’m not a wuss,” he says. “Then prove it,” says Amanda. “Hit me!” “I won’t do it,” Wonder says again. SMACK! “Hit me all you want,” says Wonder. “I’m not gonna hit you.” SMACK! “You’re nothing but a little pussy,” Amanda tells her man. “You’re becoming a girl yourself.” Wonder sighs again. “Any man can hit a lady,” Wonder says to Amanda. “But it takes a real man not to.” SMACK! SMACK! SMACK! You can see an angered look starting to come on Wonder’s face. He slowly starts to clinch a fist. SMACK! “Hit me!” she shouts once again. SMACK! “Hit me, damn it!” Wonder swings his fist, but, fortunately, he stops it about halfway. He then lowers his fist and lowers his head again. “I just can’t do it,” Wonder depressingly says. “I don’t wanna do anything I might regret.” “You’re hopeless,” Amanda says with a sigh. She turns around and slowly starts to walk away. “I’m getting a shower.” In the background, Amanda opens the door to the bathroom and walks in. Meanwhile, in the chair, Kid Wonder sighs. He’s left to sit there. He sits there alone with his thoughts. Thinking about how to get around this predicament he’s in. Thinking about Kimbusa and getting his revenge. Just thinking. Thinking as we fade to black.
