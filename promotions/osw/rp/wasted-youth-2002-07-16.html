Date: Tuesday, July 16, 2002 }----I guess there's once good thing to being a preacherman... free boy sex. Way to be mature Lillith. Let's show off for everyone. }----I wonder if Steven Smith should take up a new line of work. Boy, I can't wait for the HBO 'Killer-Psycho-Whore Comedy Hour! }----True, but I have to get up for... church... And you wonder why people don't like you? Hermit motherfucker.... you want to know why i'm singling you out? have you really sat down and thought about it? what else could i possibly want. i know you can figure this out lillith. want to know what it is? but you can't understand. you haven't been around long enough. can't begin to contemplate what it feels like. get some experience before you try. it takes blood, sweat and tears. I can't let you win. Why you might ask? You can't begin to contemplate what it feels like. To get passed over time and again. To give your life to a company and have them promptly turn around, give you the finger, and tell you to fuck off. I'm not gonna let Reagan or anyone in Old School do that to me. They don't know what I go through. They don't know the pain I feel. They don't know what it's like to wake up in a hospital like me. They don't know what it's like to get passed over. They don't know how it feels to see a flashy midgit win a title. I'll get them. I'll get them all. You started the war, Propaganda. You threw fuel to it, Lillith. You got your nose in the wrong place, Vampir. And I won end it until the belt is mine. I need it to exist. It's becomming a part of me that I never had before. Just knowing that all of the hard work I've put into the wrestling world has lead to something tangable in gold and leather. I need this vindication. First it was stopped by madBLACK, one of the best wrestlers ever, and Gangstar, no slouch in his own right. Then it was ended by a millionare, Alex Herr, and a cocky rich-kid, Ethan Ryan Alexander. Now, I'm not going to let a freak, a zealot, and some Communist vampire destroy my hopes and dreams. I can't have it. I won't have it. But I will have it.
