'Lovefurypassionenergy'

2002-03-27

Amy 'Aimz' Campbell

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Note: Sorry for poor workrate this week, been busy and not booked...just un-inspired basically. This RP was a little rushed because I need to finish a few things for a friend of mine, expect better tomorrow.) So Slam is over and done with for this week...Amy isn't even booked for the next, is this a good thing? Well, she has a tendancy to get edgy without focus, but as long as she has someone around to keep her occupied, all will hopefully be fine. On the bright side, she had certainly seemed perkier than usual...a bounce in her step as she walked down a fairly snow-covered, lengthy driveway. What really tipped it off? Her pants were tighter than usual...a shining leather with red flames coming from the ankles, along with an equally restricting, black long-sleeved Boy Hits Car band shirt. She had stepped into the forest green home she shared with Jason Dexter, Byron Calver and Breanne Lilievre with a degree of nervousness, though...it seemed that her little sparks with Rent-A-Hero were beginning to show through, and Jason couldn't be allowed to see that. When Aimz stepped into her home, there was nobody to be found in the immediate area, which was unusual. But, on closer inspection, she found her friend with the lovely ring through the center of her nose, Breanne Lilievre, huddled up on the black futon reading a 'Johnny The Homicidal Maniac' (HUGE props to that book!) comic... She peered around for a moment, realizing nobody else was there. ''Bre...where'd the guys go?'' ''Whaaa...uhhh...huh?'' Amy had caught Breanne off-guard, her dirty blonde pony tail bobbing around as her head quickly shot up. ''Oh! They went to some pool hall...I asked if you were going, but Jay said it was 'Guy Stuff'.'' ''He would.'' Aimz snuffed, slinging a little book bag from her back, discarding it to the floor with little concern for the contents. ''Mmmhmm...but to happier things...some reporter called today and wanted to know what it's like to be your best friend.'' ''...the hell?'' Was all Amy said as she opened the door to the small fridge they had in the attatched kitchen area. ''He was just for a local paper or something...when he asked what you were like in general, I just said ''She tackles everyday life with a perverted innocence that just makes you want to give her a big hug...and then stand back very quickly while checking to see if everything is still attached.''...I think it was a decent description.'' She laughed, peering back down at the demented comic for a moment. ''Heh...sounds alright, might make 'em afraid.'' Aimz chuckled back, tucking a few strands of hair behind an oddly pierced ear. ''But yeah...I'm gonna see if I can call Rentie tonight...'' ''Rent-A-Hero? Damn, Amy...you've got it bad .'' ''Shut up...'' Uh oh, back to the girliness. ''...I do not!'' ''Oh, so that's why you go all red and cheerleader-ish at any little mention of his name, right? Yeah...'' Bre continued, sarcastically poking fun at her hero-smitten friend. ''...of course, why didn't I see it before! You SO obviously can't stand the guy!'' When Amy went to sit down next to Breanne on the futon, she smacked her on the arm a bit for mentioning the object of her current REAL affections, only with the same smile a Catholic schoolgirl has at mass. ''Bre...fuck you,'' The smile held, both laughing. ''...it's your fault I'm in this mess as it is.'' ''How?'' ''Don't ask questions...'' She glanced nervously from side to side, this sort of craziness was a running joke between the two since high school. ''...it just is.'' They both paused a moment in the mock argument, leaving them each time to think of something more to say. Bre was, as usual, the first to speak...speaking, also as usual, so quickly that she could only be understood by somebody like Aimz, who had known her throughout childhood. ''Meh...you've got a week off, go spend it with hero-boy instead of harassing me.'' Breanne stuck a bare tongue out to contrast the pierced one Amy had flashed her earlier at the ribbing. The redhead pouted, folding her arms while she leaned heavily against the black cushion. ''Okay...so what if I like him? It's not like the universe doesn't know already!'' ''There you go,'' Bre smiled at Amy, standing up from the seat to pat her on the back. ''Don't you feel so much better?'' ''Not really...'' ''What? Why?!'' ''Because now, as soon as he gets home, Jason's gonna want to have me shot.'' ''Jason can kiss my ass...'' Breanne was rather matter-of-fact here! ''...if he likes you so fucking much, he'd want you to be happy. So go be happy, with Rent-A-Homewrecker.'' She laughed a bit at the nickname RAH had earned, though Amy didn't. Even though she was less-than-impressed by Breanne's RAH comment, Aimz didn't get angry at all, she was too happy for that right now...even though she refused to show it. ''Meh...beleive me, I'm gonna be enjoying myself. But what're you gonna do when I move out?'' ''Tag along?'' ''Ummm, Bre...'' Amy was a little skeptical, not completely sure of what her friend really wanted to do in this situation. ''...what about Byron?'' Aimz quickly received a rather surprising answer from her longtime companion in an unusually cheering tone. ''He and Jason are a single unit...he's only a guy, I'm not gonna risk hurting my closest friendship for a chance to get laid more often.'' ''That's why you're my best friend...you'd do shit like this in a minute, nobody else would make sacrifices like that...'' Awww...cute conversation continued as we faded away...
