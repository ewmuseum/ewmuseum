Lunch with Jane

9 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/09/2002 'I don't know who's got it worse Tempest,' said Jane Reagan as she sipped at her Chardonnay delicately, licking a drop from her lip. 'You have the Russian reject and I have the slimiest of the slime balls after me.' She shook her head, tossing her red locks a little while Tempest shook her head and said, 'Well, I dunno, I don't dislike Ivan. I just... I don't understand him. We're so different...' Reagan laughed and said, 'Tempest everyone's different from Ivan. Come to think of it I don't think anyone's like you either. But Ivan's backward, conniving, in some ways a coward. Nothing he does is without a calculated gain. So I find it hard to believe you lasted this long in his company.' Tempest nodded absently and ran her finger around the rim of her own wine glass, lost in thought. After a moment she said, 'Yeah I suppose. There's something about him I like, a sort of weird genuineness under all that though. But even he's not gonna own up to it. And mostly...' She shook her head vigorously, tossing her dark pony tails and the little pink beaded ties holding them. 'He just doesn't know how to have fun and I can't handle that. I mean, I could admire his purpose...' 'His purpose!' Jane spat. 'Tempest his purpose is to take over my federation as some sort of prize for his glorious homeland!' She sighed and then took a deep breath. 'I'm sorry, I know you're a very forgiving person. I shouldn't be surprised that if anyone can see something good in him it would be you. But believe me, everyone and everything is a tool to him. He would use you as surely as anyone else to get ahead.' She chuckled a bit and added, 'My god, I never even expected you to actually go out with him after that Dating Game promo. I mean it was a great promotional piece, but I never meant it to be more. And then you actually spent time with him beyond that? I was stunned. You've gotta have better prospects than that.' Tempest shrugged and picked at a plateful of pasta as she said, 'Well, I guess. But that's not really the point. I guess you're right though, I'm not gonna be able to get past him being such a stick in the mud. Could be worse though.' 'How's that?' Reagan asked. 'I could have Sebastian Toomes after me,' Tempest said and broke up in a fit of giggling. Reagan rolled her eyes and said, 'Ug, if only it was so easy to joke about. That man is a plague, I don't know why I hired him. He's... he's crude and revolting and hardly a talent.' She huffed and shook her head as Tempest looked on sympathetically and said, 'Well, I know it was tough to fill out the roster of a brand new fed. Don't worry about him though. I mean he doesn't impress me as someone who could actually do anything to hurt you. And besides I bet Timo sends him packing tomorrow night.' She giggled and added, 'I just love that name, don't you?' 'What name?' Reagan asked quizzically. 'Well, say it,' Tempest said, with a small gesture, 'Timo Bo-LAM-ba... it sounds like a dance or something.' She grinned and grooved in her seat and Reagan laughed. 'You can find a joke or something fun in anything. That's why I wanted so badly for you to come here,' Reagan said with a smile. 'I'm glad you did.' Tempest's smile softened a bit as she said, 'I know. You need some fun, something to just enjoy amid all those guys looking to further their careers, get back at Tyler Nelson, or whatever their agendas may be. And I know it's been hard alone. How are you really doing Jane?' There was a long silence in which Jane examined the leaves of her salad and seemed to notice little else. At last she sighed and said, 'Ok, I guess. But you're right, it's been hard... very hard. I don't doubt that I can ruin Tyler Nelson and destroy the PCW, but then I have this, the OSW. This is my fed and my legacy and now I have the title to go with the power, but all the responsibility as well.' She took a deep breath and added, 'Right now I'm running on hatred though. It's all revenge, but one day... one day that will be over with and what I want the OSW to be then...' Her voice trailed off and she shook her head. 'I don't know yet. I signed people, people like Ivan, people like Meanstreak, because I knew they'd get me as far as the ruin of PCW, but whether they will eventually cause the place to implode is another story. I know I can't trust them, not like I can you.' Tempest nodded and said, 'Just remember that you can.' Reagan smiled and said, 'I know. Even if you did spend some time in the company of one of the most ruthless back stabbers in the fed, bucking for his new Communist regime.' She smiled coyly as Tempest tilted her head and said, 'If you really don't trust Ivan, why did you hire him?' Reagan shook her head and said, 'I told you, I knew he'd get the job done. He'd draw fans, even if just to scream obscenities at him.' 'So you're using him,' Tempest said levelly, but with a smile, almost as unreadable as Reagan's. Reagan paused and answered, 'Of course, but then he's using me too. And I'm using you... It's the nature of the business. We all use each other, it's just a matter of whether everyone gets what they need or not. I think you'll find you do.' Tempest nodded and said, 'So... speaking of that, are you ever gonna let me wrestle? I'm over the rib thing, really.' She chuckled and balled up her fists as Reagan laughed and reached into her sleek black briefcase. 'As a matter of fact...' she said, with a sly smile, 'I think I have the perfect opportunity for you.' She opened a folder and slid a legal size document across the table. Tempest looked at it a moment and then grinned. 'You really are wicked, you know that?' she said. Reagan grinned as well and handed her a pen. 'So are you, that's why we work well together.' Tempest laughed aloud and signed at the bottom of the sheet as Reagan looked on with a satisfied smile, and just a hint of malice smoldering in her eyes.
