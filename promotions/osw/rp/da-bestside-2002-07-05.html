Da Bestside?

5 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07-05-02 Rats festered in a pool of blood. Picking away at chunks of flesh from a rotting corpse nearby, the disease-ridden creatures soiled their meals whilst eating away at it, picking at whatever morsel of nourishment was available, caring not as the lifeless expression of horror on the little girl’s face stared back at them as slowly, they consumed the life from her. As the rats fed in the gutters, screams rang out from a nearby alley, as a group of youths ravaged two women. Ripping at their chic clothing, tearing at their hair, the women screamed as the youths continued to beat them, pistol whipping them and kicking them. And then the rape began. It seemed like forever as the women writhed and whimpered on the cold concrete, scraping at their flesh, taking some of the pain away from the physical and mental pain of the rape. It seemed to lag on forever, but ended as if in an instant, as the youths ran away, and the women lay in the alley, beaten and battered. A small river of blood flowed down the alley, leaking from the wounds of the women and dripping into a drain in the gutter. The youths ran along the street, the adrenaline running through their veins as the intensity of the rape closed in on them, the faster they ran. The screams of the now silent women lying battered in the alley remained with them, not phasing them in the slightest as they ran away from a dastardly crime, to say the least. Across the front lawns they kept running, their hearts beating as in the distance sounds of police sirens rang out. Those that had pistols hid them in their pants pockets, and continued running. Across town the alarm of a grocery store hollered into the night, as the getaway car sped past, chased by three squad cars, adding to the noise pollution. The sirens wailed through the starry night, and various lights in the neighbourhood shone onto the street, as a few angered husbands began making a fuss about the noise. As they open their doors the blurred forms of the cars whiz by, and with a simple grunt, the doors are shut and locked and peace soon follows again. Overdosed and Shot to Shit, the criminals in the car laugh insanely, guzzling Jim Beam out of a paper bag, and taking the occasional draw from a fat joint. The driver squints past the windscreen, struggling to see past the fog of Marie J and alcohol-stained glass. The Pontiac Trans Am began veering across the road, as the front seat passenger drunkenly pulls a pp7 from his jacket pocket, and leans out of the window. The three squad cars veer to the other side of the road, as shots are fired, the gunshots echo through the neighbourhood, but this time however no lights come on…except one. As more bullets fire into the streets, a silhouette stands by one of the windows, watching this horror unfold. More bullets. The figure jerks. Glass breaks. The 7-year old boy dies within minutes. The car continues to rampage through the neighbourhood streets, closely tailed by the three squad cars, their sirens shrieking across town. A couple of blocks away, the rapists are still running. Almost out of breath they stop, as the sound of the sirens cascading towards them send a nightmarish sensation through their bodies. Looking for a way to escape, the youths give up, and turn back, sprinting down the streets. The getaway car, now swaying constantly across the entire street, again veers around a corner, and speeds down the same street the youths are running down. All four of the intoxicated criminals rummage around the car, looking for spilt booze and more drugs. The booty they have is sprawled all over the back seats with two of the criminals, consisting of cigarettes, pornographic magazines, candy and other miniscule items. The driver of the car becomes frustrated. Fumbling around under the drivers seat, he pulls out a sawn off shotgun, and holds it out the window. Taking aim through the wing mirror, he pulls the trigger, and jerks as the kickback of the shotgun forces his arm back, and his elbow smacks against the door, jolting the nerves. The criminals in the car all laugh insanely as the shots successfully hit the driver of the squad car in front, instantly killing the driver, who in falling limp against the wheel turns the car, sending it flying over the curb, and through the wall of a house. No sooner have all the lights in the block shone brightly onto the streets than the squad car bursts into flames, then explodes, creating a shower of debris, smoke and flesh. One of the squad cars comes to a halt outside the house, the officers within rushing to the aid of their already dead comrades. The other squad car chases after the Pontiac. The driver tries to fire another shot, but forgets he has to cock the gun, and throws it out of the window. Driving close to the sidewalk, the driver occasionally hits the curb, sparks flying from the metallic hubcaps and fizzing into darkness again. Ahead, the youths seem to ignore all the sounds, running on instinct. The wailing of the sirens fills their ears, but still they ignore it. The screams of the women penetrate their minds and all at once reality hits them like a bitchslap from Azrael Cain. Breathing heavily, all kinds of fear ravage their bodies as they once ravaged their victims. The driver sticks to the curb, and in a desperation move, the police car veers onto the sidewalk, and speeds towards the car. Slowly reaching the getaway car, the officer in the passenger seat cocks his shotgun, and rolls down the window. Taking careful aim the officer tries to hold his position, cussing in frustration as the driver dodges streetlights and newspaper stands in front of him. Glancing back and forth at his fellow officer, the driver is drawn into the suspense as he keeps aim, holding his position, with his finger of the trigger. The driver glances from the sidewalk to the other officer, screaming for him to pull the trigger. Glancing back and forth, skidding around obstacles as they appear in the headlights. BOOM! The officer fires, and the Pontiac immediately spins out of control, as the remnants of the driver’s skull splatter against the windscreen and the others in the car, as it veers to the right. “Oh SHIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIT!!!!!!!!!” The driver of the squad car yells out as the gang of youths appear suddenly in the headlights, just as the Pontiac crashes onto the sidewalk, colliding with the police car. Blood and metal and smoke fly into the air, and come crashing down again, painting a beautiful picture of horror on the sidewalk. The driver of the squad car lies lifeless on the hood of the car, shards of metal and glass jutting from his skull, whilst the officer in the passenger seat lies twitching, as his head and torso are trapped between the two cars. The bodies of the youths lie broken and twisted on the sidewalk, actual limbs still hanging from the wreckage. One of the youths opens his eyes. The stinging electric sensation shoots through his mid-section, and he looks down to find his legs twitching 3ft away. He can’t scream. He begins gasping, the pain pulsating through his body like razor wire. Without knowing it he moans out. The sound draws the attention of the whole street, as the lights flood the street, and a barrage of people run towards the wreckage. More sirens echo throughout the area as more squad cars and ambulances race towards the scene. Police and doctors and neighbours almost immediately surround the house decimated by the squad car further up the road, whilst more squad cars and ambulances skid to a halt before the wreckage down the street. As more and more people flood to the scene, onlookers notice a slumped figure up a dark alley, highlighted in the intense light of the police helicopters. Rushing to see what it is, the bodies of the rape victims stare back at their discoverers, filling the alley with a shrill scream. The only survivor of the incident, the legless youth’s vision becomes blurred, but still he recognises the blue figures of the officers. Taking a deep breath, he reaches into what is left of his pants pocket, and removes his pistol. Quickly pulling it up and taking bad aim, an officer before him turns one second too late, and falls back to the ground, his face gushing with blood as the bullet drives itself into the pit of his cerebellum. Turning the gun to hi sown head, the youth’s eyes fill with tears, as all the pain and horror the night has brought writhes in his mind like a virus. Closing his eyes, he breathes out, and pulls the trigger. Freedom. Meanwhile, watching from the shadows of another nearby alley, those same old gleaming, green eyes watch on, wide with interest, as the squad cars suddenly burst into flames. The crowds surrounding have no time to escape as the cars explode, sending yet another wave of death to the entire street. Those eyes watch on. Unmoving. The screams and gore enlighten him as he watches death reap its way through the streets. From the rat-infested corpse in the gutter to the burning bodies lying in the street, those eyes light up with some kind of twisted joy, and Hessian utters but few words. “It truly is Da Bestside after all.........” Fin
