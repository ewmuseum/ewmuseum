Car trouble

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Wednesday Saturday night, during the course of OSW Saturday SLAM, a giant Russian who will go unnamed for safety purposes took it upon himself to destroy the car of Reno Davis. During this occurrence, a rental car of OSW wrestler Kid Wonder was totally destroyed. After the show, as you might’ve guessed, Kid Wonder was furious. Not only was the car beaten up, but it wouldn’t start. The red Omni P.O.S would not start! This would make anyone mad. It was bad enough some cheap Canadian rental car was in shambles, but now Kid Wonder would have to take a Canadian taxi back to the hotel. For the next few days, Kid Wonder, along with his girlfriend, stayed away from the car mess. But, as is always the case, it caught up to the teen idol. Shift to Wednesday afternoon. We’re at a Hertz…eh rental car shop in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. The owner of the shop has beckoned the four-time PCW Cruiserweight champion to come and face the music. “For the last time, I didn’t do anything,” we hear Kid Wonder say inside the shop. Move inside the store. Kid Wonder, wearing his usual black “In Like Flynn” T-shirt and red genie sequin pants, stands face to face with the owner of the shop. Amanda, in her pink halter top and leather pants, stands behind her boyfriend, not saying anything. “I am not responsible for what happened,” Wonder tells the owner. The shop owner, whose name is Phil according to the gold plated nametag he’s wearing, sports a blue, button-down shirt and beige colored khaki pants. He’s a middle-aged man, roughly three inches taller than the wrestling star and appears to have a small beer belly of sorts. “You’re not responsible for the damage to one of my cars, eh,” says the owner. “The car was in your name, little boy. That makes you responsible in me eyes, eh.” Kid Wonder sighs. “I know you’re Canadian and all,” Wonder says to Phil with a smirk, “but I figured you’d still understand me. For the last time…” Wonder pauses. Then, real slowly, K-Dub says, “I did not damage your cheap rental. I am not responsible for your car’s destruction.” Wonder sighs again. He turns and glances at Amanda. “Stupid Canadians,” the cruiserweight mutters in his girlfriend’s general direction. Amanda lets out a little giggle. Phil, however, is less than amused. “OK hot shot,” Phil says with a straight face, “if you’re not responsible, how aboot you tell me who is, eh.” Phil stands there and looks down at Wonder with a straight face. Wonder smirks and snickers. “You wanna know who’s responsible?” Wonder asks Phil. Phil, of course, nods his head. “It was this ugly, fat, sorry excuse for a human Jap,” claims the teen idol. “The guy, who can’t do squat except for squatting over a toilet after eating one of his 10 meals a day, is upset because I can beat him up. So instead of coming at me and getting smacked down, he decides to damage the already damaged piece of garbage that you rented out to me. He set me up for this!” shouts Kid Wonder. “It’s true,” Amanda blurts out. Phil just laughs at the story. “A fat Japanese man, looking for vengeance against a little guy like yourself, destroys your car for no reason, eh?” asks Phil. “Is that what you’re saying?” “That’s exactly what I’m saying,” Wonder tells him. “Heh.” Phil just laughs. “Nice try, kid,” the owner tells Kid Wonder. “You’re the third guy to use that excuse this week. Next time you decide to wreck one of my automobiles, come up with an original excuse, eh.” Kid Wonder starts seething. He looks ready to destroy the car owner. Amanda goes from being in back of him to being at his side. She makes sure he does nothing stupid. “Now,” Phil says with a smirk, “someone owes me my money for the car or else you’ll be in a lot of trouble, eh.” “I didn’t do this!” furiously shouts Wonder. “How much?” asks Amanda in a calm manner. “Ten thousand dollars,” Phil tells her. Kid Wonder, upon hearing this, is infuriated. “Ten grand!” he shouts out in anger. “That’s preposterous!” “Calm down,” Amanda tells him. “Jesus! I only paid 500 to rent the damn thing!” he shouts at Phil. “There’s no way in hell I’m paying 10 grand for that piece of shit!” Phil cracks his knuckles. “Then I guess you’ll have to pay another way, eh,” Phil tells K-Dub. “I’d like to see you try,” Wonder tells Phil. Phil makes a movement. Wonder tries lunging at him, but Amanda holds her man back. Phil goes to a desk where a telephone sits. He picks it up and dials a number. “Yes, Mountie,” Phil says into the phone, “I have a lunatic in my store…” Phil pauses. “U-huh. That’s correct, sir. I was aboot to take care of him, but decided to call you, eh.” “Lemme attem!” Wonder shouts out. Amanda, however, continues holding him back. “That you, sir.” Phil hangs the phone up. He then turns and looks at Wonder. Amanda is still holding him back. “You bastard!” Wonder shouts at Phil. “I didn’t do anything! I’m not paying for anything! You can bring your friend Mountie, or whatever his name is, here and I’ll kick his ass, too!” “The Mounties will be here any moment,” Phil tells the outraged customer. “I wouldn’t try anything if I were you.” “Let’s just go,” Amanda says to her boyfriend. Wonder, without saying anything, obliges to her request. The couple starts to go when through the window of the door, we see a car pull up. The driver side door opens and out steps a man in official Canadian Mountie getup. The Mountie heads towards the door from the outside. The OSW couple head towards the door from the inside. And before a confrontation happens, we fade out.
