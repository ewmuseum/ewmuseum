Insanity Persists The following is a joint roleplay written by Rhett Locke and Graphic Violence. Step. Step. Step. Each step down the fluorescent, tiled hallway was embraced. Every time he heard a door shut, or watched a doctor pass him, it was revenge. The echoes of screams that fled into the hallway from padded walls was sheer ecstacy. Every word spoken was retribution. He had gotten what he wanted. Rhett did have everything he wanted. The doctor led him down the hallway to a small, secluded office. The same four walls in every office was no different from this one, other than the fact that the aura in the room didn't feel the same. Perhaps it was Rhett's Joy that he had defeated the monster, or his nerves giving him a slight, 'on the edge of your seat' feeling. Regardless...something was different. Something was different as he heard a man's voice, claiming 'We'll suit you right back up and put you right back in that room if you try anything, bud!' Something was very different as the door slowly creaked open, and he entered. Something was very different, as Rhett smiled. 'How're you feeling, Steven?' **** Bumb rushed by guys in white scrubs. They're quick to put me in this straight jacket, but they don't need to try that hard. This place has me so doped up on medication a ten year old can kick my ass. Where are they taking me now? It's only been two days and I've been through about fifty tests, numerous pschiatric visits and go knows what else. Half the time I've been knocked out. I can hardly stand. These pricks are practically dragging me down the hallway. My vision is glazed over, I have a pounding fuckin' headache. I play it cool and I'll be out soon. And when I'm out Rhett Locke will fuckin' die for what he's putting me through right now. 'We'll suit you right back up and put you right back in that room if you try anything, bud!' A man is in front of me. My vision is blurred. Must be the doctor. These fucks are s- 'How're you feeling, Steven?' I know that fuckin' voice. **** 'Go to hell, prick.' GV replied, the two ward doctors merely shook their heads and exited the room. The door slamming behind them in a violent manner, echoing through the office as if the earth shook. 'Steven, please don't speak to me in such a manner. I've waited to see you for over two hours. Are you not happy to see me, friend?' Graphic Violence scratched the side of his bald head, and grunted. His eyes squinted, a single bushy eyebrow lifted. 'What are you trying to pull, fuckwad.' Rhett sighed, and slouched back in the chair, Graphic Violence finally took a seat. Rhett smiled, 'Are they treating you well pal?' 'WHAT THE FUCK ARE YOU DOING HERE, RHETT!?! WHY ARE YOU HERE!?! WHY THE HELL ARE YOU HERE!?!' Rhett knew he was putting on quite a show for the men that would be watching the videotape of the confrontation later. 'My friend, you must still be dillusional...' Graphic Violence flipped the desk over with the little strength that he had. A loud, deafening crush as it toppled and hit the floor. Rhett had apparently dived out of the way just in time not to get taken over with it. 'Fuck! I was hoping your ass would be dead!' The two men charged in, with the straight jacket and tazers in hands. Rhett slowly stood up, continuing his act. 'Steven! Steven! He didn't mean to! Let him go! Guys, please! Let him goooo!' **** So much for playing it cool. I can't stand Rhett Locke. He must be crazy thinking he can come in here and act like he's my friend. Acting as if he's concerned about my well being. Bumb rushed again. No more energy. AGH! Stuck in the neck with a needle. Getting more tired as the minutes go by. Fuck, the white jacket again. It's only been two days, but it feels like ten years. Rhett Locke Rhett Locke Rhett Locke I'll….fuckin'….kill….you….when….I'm…out **** Fuck him. Fuck that god damn...FUCK HIM! I can't believe GV did that shit! Trying to fucking kill my ass in that office! That fucker! I got his ass back, that stupid, stupid piece of shit! Oh hell yeah I did! I told that fuckin' shrink what happened...he said he'd up the medicine. Take that Graphic Violence, bastard!
