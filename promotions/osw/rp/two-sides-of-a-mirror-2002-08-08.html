Two Sides of a Mirror

8 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/8/02 His bald head leaned against the wall, mere inches from the numbers, 212. tick tock, tick tock. What to do? What to do? Indecision clouds his vision. As he thinks, he fiddles with a hair pin. His thoughts are deep and directed, but the next thing he knows, he's standing in the darkness of the hotel room. He slides silently into the darkness and sits. His eyes never leaving the two occupants of the bed, just feet from where he sits. Sleep, slumber, dream. It must be nice, they look so peaceful. Frankie stands and walks slowly towards the bed. As he approaches, a floorboard under the carpet produces the slightest creek. Jesus St Crux, asleep in his hotel room, quickly perks up. Ha, so I guess he's not so peaceful after all. While Jesus was jerked back to the real world, his female compadre still sleeps, unstirred. The look of anger and anticipation on St Crux's face shown through the darkness. Satisfied, Frankie sat back down. Frankie chuckled, 'Don't worry boy, I'm not here to hurt you.' These words had no influence on St Crux's demeanor, so Frankie continued, 'Keep your ass in that bed and listen to me. My motives are not so simple as you may believe. The things I do may seem strange at this time. But in the future, people will look back and study me. Only then, will they understand the true scope of my work.' Jesus was not paying attention, he kept looking at his chick. Frankie cleared his throat. Jesus looked up and whispered, 'Que passa? What are you doing here?' Frankie smiled wide, 'I am here to check up on you. That's all.' There was an itching urge to yell, to wake his partner up. He was visibly hoping I would not. Frankie chuckled, 'Are you angry?' Jesus returned with whispered Spanish. Frankie did not understand all of it, but he closed with 'Si, estoy enfadado! Estoy muy enfadado!' Frankie DID know what that meant, and it pleased him beyond belief. He needed to know more. 'Do you hate me?' questioned Bones. Again St Crux returned in spanish that Bones did not fully comprehend. Yet again, he ended with, 'Si... si, chico...' Something Frankie definitely understood. It's sometimes funny how anger and hate can transcend all language barriers. Frankie's confusion and self doubt is long gone. In light of recent events, he is now happy. The proverbial pig in shit. When he is happy, he rambles... 'Good, I'm glad you hate me. Bring all that anger... all that hate; you are going to need it.' Frankie instructed as he starred at Jesus. He continued, 'Bring all these things, and you'll have the best match of your career. Consider this a gift.' Frankie was satisfied, he said what he had come to say...done what he came to do. Jesus was not so pleased, or impressed. He rebutted Frankie's comments in Spanish that Frankie cared not translate. He wasn't there to listen, he was there to talk. He stood, leaving St Crux with a simple, 'I'll show myself the door.' Frankie stood and left. He smiled from ear to ear. Thoughts bounced around and raced in that bald dome of his. He was giddy, man he loved this shit. A grown man, a man people fear, a man who's seriousness knows no bounds, was almost skipping down the hall. Damn, this is gonna be fun...
