Autograph Session

Date: 2-19-01 Late last night it was released that Michael Dame would be appearing for an autograph session, in the city of Minneapolis, his original hometown. With the time approaching to the session, all feelings are at a tight tense. Not only for Michael, but the fans outside. His comments and feelings were all let out on SLAM! On OSW, what he calls his new home. Michael waits inside Tower Records in the “star” room, where other have stood before signing for the screaming fans. He stood for seconds, no longer. Kept walking back and fourth, he couldn’t stand still. He then stops to look up at the door, as it swings open. Gloria- Mike, you almost ready? I know you must be full of tension, but compare this to other points in your life… this is peanuts. -It was his former MCW Manager, and best friend, Gloria who gave him inspiration on this day. Michael needed it. Indeed, he needed it.- Michael Dame- Yah.. I’m all ready. Just give me the cue.. Gloria- CUE! .. –laughs- .. No, really, it is time to go. Michael Dame- Alright. -Michael grabbed his leather jacket, and took a deep breathe. Slid his massive arms into the sleeves, and fixed the jacket for comfort. He walked out and shut the door behind him. Instant cheers from the crowd he sees in front of him… Michael gives them a wave and sits down at his chair. A long line of kids stood in front of him. On either side were aisles of CD’s, DVD’s, and Videos. The store was rather magnificent. Gloria waves up the first child, that stands no more then 10 feet away, she walks up rather scared- Michael Dame- It’s alright, you can come up. There is no reason to be scared. -The child was no older then 10 years old. Her Mother waited for her with a keen eye on Micheal. Dame simply looked up and smiled at the mother. At the same point the child turns around to her mom. Her mom smiles and,…- Mother- It’s alright baby, no need to be scared. -She makes a signal with her hands to go…and she does. The child turns around with a massive smile on her face. She hands her picture to Michael- Girl- My name is Lilian. I know all about you, Mr. Dame. My mom would tell me the best of stories. Michael Dame- Ohh… your mom is a fan of mine, well then she deserves a autograph also. –Michael calls the mother up… She points to her self-... Girl (turning around)- Come on Mom, Mr Dame says you’ll get one too! Come on! -the mother blushes and then walks up.- Mother- It really isn’t necessary. Michael Dame- No…No… You’re a fa…. -Michael cuts himself off by his key thought….could it be…is this someone Michael knows?- Michael Dame- Linda? -He pauses as she cracks a smile and nods her head, yes- Linda- It’s me, Michael. How have you been?...-She turns around-…Actually, you’ve got this whole line of people to tend to, you can't be dealing with my small talk… Michael Dame- Oh no you're worth it, although you're right. We'll talk some other time.. Maybe we can meet for coffee later this week? Linda- Sure. I’d love too! Michael Dame- So, then it’s a date. -Dame drops his head to sign two autographs and hands it back- Linda- Thanks, Michael. We’ll be in touch. And also, off the record. –Holds the childs ears-.. I knew it wasn’t you. Michael Smiles and nods. Waves as they turn and head towards the exit. The next person is waved up, and two kids come up at the same time. Young teenagers. Obviously, OSW Fans as they have the Tshirts on… Kid #1- Dame! You’re the man! MCW Days baby, you were my hero! Kid #2- You’re still my hero man! Kid #1- Me too man…We knew it wasn’t you with all that drug talk. We just knew it! Kid #2- And now…OSW! Conquor and reign supreme! Kid #1- That’s right! Ask for the title shot! But what about Bones, Dame? What are you going to do to shut his mouth…? Michael Dame (Standing up, and takes off his jacket)- If Frankie Bones wants to go on live television and threaten me, and disrespect me, then I’m going to show him why he SHOULDN’T! I’m make him squeel like a pig, and realize that my years in prison were the best years of his life! He didn't fear Dame! Kid #1- You're gonna make him feel like the scum of the Earth! Kid #2- If he doesn't already! (All three laugh as Michael Walks over, grabbing a clipboard on the way) Michael Dame- I’m not really digging the sitting and signing thing, I think I’m gonna walk through the people, starting with you kids. -He takes the kids pictures and signs them, and hands them back, they both give a very enthused handshake and huge smiles as they turn and head towards the door. Michael continues on his way down the row of people. Signing each and every picture of his the people hold. It’s obvious that Dame’s actions have been forgotten, and he is a forgiven man. Or is he? He comes to the next man in line. He would have some choice comments to say to Mr. Dame- Man #1- Bullshit! Michael Dame- Excuse me? Man #1- You did it! You piece of garbage, I know you did. How you pulled that interview on OSW…and turned your back on your wife, your kids, and your fans…day in and day out you commit a deadly sin…how the fuck do you live with yourself? -Michael quickly looks around for security as this is going in the wrong direction. Suddenly a man standing next to all this intervenes- Man#2- Why don’t you lay off his back… He’s not the man he was made out to be. You can tell by his sincerity. Dame was never looked at in that way during MCW, and he isn’t now…so get over your notch with him… Cause you’re fighting a losing battle. Man#1- Fuck you dipshit… (He takes a swing at the man who ducks and tackles him. They roll around on the ground for 2 to 3 seconds before Dame lifts off the peacemaker, and pins the adsurd man to the ground) Michael Dame- Will somebody get some security in here. (The security races in, and take the man away. Michael shakes the man’s hand that stood up for him) Jack- That wasn’t necessary, but thank you. And don’t get down about him… He’s still in the “Hate Dame” era. It’ll pass...And the name’s Jack. Michael Dame- Well, thank you Jack for saying what you said. It is appreciated. Here is your autograph. (He hands him his autograph, along with a handshake and continues on down the line. About 3 hours pass, and all have been signed, his job is through with Tower Records, right on time as well. He shakes the store managers hand, and leaves with Gloria. They exit through the front of the building. All is well….or is it?) Michael Dame- So where to now? Gloria- Well, are you interested in meeting up with that woman, Linda? I can get ahold of her, if you'd like… Michael Dame- Sure… Why don’t you do that. Thanks, Glo… (As they exit through the building they see a large group of picketers. Holding signs and screaming such things like… “Don’t let him into your hearts, he’ll just break it again” and “A liar can never be trusted” and as far as “Michael should be hung”… They were an angry bunch, although they haven’t looked at Michael….yet!) Michael Dame- Hurry up, lets avoid this group. (They hurry into the limo, but the picketers see them, and start to dismantle their barrage of fruits and vegetables. They could be arrested for this, very clearly, although didn’t seem to care. They get in without anything hitting them, when…SMASH!) Michael Dame- A fucking pumpkin! These kids are maniacs! (A pumpkin had been thrown, and landed on the sunroof which shattered. Dame gives the signal to drive, and the driver does so.
