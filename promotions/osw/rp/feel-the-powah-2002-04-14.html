FEEL THE POWAH!!!

14 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14-04-02 ♫♪…Hessian and Azrael, a disaster untold, The holders of Tag Team Championship Gold, Come Bitches or Commies or Vampires too, The Bloodlust will tear them through and through, So fuck all the ones that think they’re the best, The Bloodlust will beat them then destroy all the rest, Minion, Caleb, Azrael and Hess, The ones you should fear, The time’s come to kill: Proppa-G and Vampir………♪♫ Minion stops humming to himself as Hessian finishes cracking his back, and then picks up his title belt from atop his 8ft stone sarcophagus. A draught blows into the chamber from under the large steel door, causing the large flaming candles in each corner of the room to flitter, shaking silhouettes and shadows within. “Tom Walczak and Michel Krieg, OSW Tag Champions? HA! I told you last week Minion, that hard-headed Pole is no match for me, and I’ll prove with Azrael this week that Propaganda and Vampir Nosferatu himself are nothing to me…” “But Hessian…”, Minion replies, “Vampir was the one you trained under for those five years. If it weren’t for him, you wouldn’t exist as you do today. Anyway, shouldn’t you savour the ‘Mentor vs. Student’ encounter for the Global Title Final?” “There is no guarantee I shall advance into that final, Akiyama, Seth and Mexi are as talented as I am at this sport, therefore Vampir, Propaganda, Azrael and I shall clash next week. The mere thought of that pathetic excuse for a Soul Stalker curses my mind, as does that preaching fuck. We warned him, Minion, we told him to leave or be subject to a war, and now they’re going to die by the hand of the true Heretics…” Hessian walks around his chamber, rubbing his gauntleted hands together and thinking to himself. “You know…Vampir Nosferatu, Global Champion, very good, and Propaganda, another slave to a false God. Perhaps our young Christian friend should look back to old NTICW tapes to learn of The Josh’ crusade against Satanism and how that crumpled, much like his neck after that Eternal Damnation from the roof of a Hell In A Cell all the way to the ground below. Anyway you look at this match it’s in our favour, either I can dismantle a 7’0” reject of society, or I can decimate my own creator. I will meet my maker, and then I will personally send him straight back to hell………Now come Minion, methinks me has a plan.” ------------------------------------ The sun shines brightly in the April skies. As birds chirp and people converse, the scent of Death begins to linger around the suburban haven. A perfect world, filled with white picket fences and Sunday family BBQs. As the kiddies play in the quiet streets, free from crime, the distinct stench of something wicked walks this way. Striding along the neat little sidewalk, Hessian looms over the children and pet doggies playing in the streets. Sneering as he walks by, parents begin to worry, and slowly inch towards the street as Hessian walks by, his belt and indeed entire front hidden behind his large, black cape. Behind him, Minion the Imp strides along, fully cloaked, and only a shadowy smile peeking out from his hood. The children instinctively drop their toys and run for their mothers and fathers, as the stench of death lingers where the 7’2” Satanist once walked. Passing through streets almost identical to the last, Hessian begins growling, frustrated at something, as he passes the final row of houses and turns a street corner to find a little white church staring back at him, nestled in a little nook on the edge of a large forest. As though on cue, the church bells begin ringing, and Minion looks up to the sky. “Hmm………10:30am. We’re just in time…” squeaks the 3’11” torrent of rage and pain the people dare to call Minion the Imp. “Then let’s have some fun…” replies Hessian. As the families flock from the streets, dressed in their finest, Hessian and Minion take cover behind a hedgerow, and as the townsfolk enter the church, Minion throws off his cloak to reveal something very interesting… ------------------------------------ “Welcome one and all, to the House of God…” the Reverend says calmly. The final few children take their seats, as the collection plates are passed around, and the sermon begins. From outside in the bushes, Hessian and Minion listen as the Reverend starts preaching about hatred and revenge, the do’s and don’ts of being a Christian and how sexually active English Catholic Bishops are these days. Minion begins giggling as he thinks on his plan, whilst Hessian peers into a stained glass window of Jesus on the cross, complete with Stigmata. “Ok Minion…are you ready?” whispers Hessian. “I was born ready bay-bee! Come on, bring it on!” replies the Imp, somewhat perversely. “Actually you were born upside down, with the umbilical cord around your neck…for some strange reason. Ok, now just do what you want in there, but remember to be yourself!” Hessian whispers, then chuckles evilly. Minion waltzes around to the front of the Church, in front of the big doors, pulls them open, and rips off his cloak. Inside, the Reverend and the people gasp as a bright flood of light engulfs the entire church, and a little silhouette appears in the aisle. Cockily walking down, the light fades slightly and the silhouette fades, to a 3’11” Imp clad in a mock Angel’s outfit, complete with halo… “BY-THE-HOLY-POWER-OF-LORD-JESUS-CHRIST-I-HAVE-COME-TO-SAVE-YOU-ALL-FROM-IMPENDING DOOM-AH!!! MY-PEOPLE-YOU-HAVE-BEEN-BLINDED-BY-YOUR-OWN-FEAR-OF-LIFE-AH!!! YOU-MUST-EMBRACE-THE-POWER-OF-THE-TRUE-LORD-HIMSELF, THE-GREAT-DIVINITY-OF-LUCIFER, THE-GREAT-ONE-HIMSELF-THE-LORD SATAN-AHH!!!!!!!!! YOU-LIVE-IN-A-WORLD-OF-OPPRESSED-RACIST-COCONUTS, FAT-MOTHERFUCKER-PORN-STARS-AND-FUTILE-LEADERS, JOIN-ME-NOW-MY-BROTHERS-AND-SISTERS-AND-EMBRACE-THE-POWER-OF-THE-UNHOLY-LORD, EMBRACE-THE-POWER-OF HELL-AHHHH!!!!!!!!!” Minion stops to take a breath, and jumps up onto the pulpit, in front of the Reverend, who remains gob smacked. “THERE-IS-ONLY-ONE-POWER-IN-THIS-WORLD-AND-YOU-AIN’T-FOLLOWING-IT-YOU MOTHERFUCKERS-AAAAHHHHH!!!!!!!!! HOW-MANY-OF-YOU-HEATHEN-BASTARDS-WATCH-THE-HOLY-DIVINITY-THAT-BE-THE OLD-SCHOOL-WRESTLING-AHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!” A couple of children and fathers raise their hands, but people look unsure of this gibbering monkey. “WELL-HEAR-ME-NOW-MY-BROTHERS-AND-SISTERS, THERE-IS-AN-UNHOLY-DOING-IN-THAT-FEDERATION-THAT-IS-A-CANCER-TO-YOUR SOUL-AAAAAHHHH!!!!!!!!! THOSE-WHO-WATCH-SHALT-KNOW-OF-A-POWAH-CALLED-HERESIAN-BLOODLUST, AND-NOW-THEY-MUST-DESTROY-THE-SCUM-THAT-IS-YOUR-OWN-BROTHER-PROPAGANDA-AND-HIS-HEATHEN-MOTHERFUCKER-OF-A-PARTNER, VAMPIR NOSFERATU-AAAAAAAAHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! I-IMPLORE-YOU-PEOPLE, DO-NOT-FOLLOW-THIS MAN-AH!!!!!!! FOLLOW-THE-WAY-OF-SATAN-AND-HIS-SERVANTS-SUCH-AS-HESSIAN-AND-AZRAEL-CAIN! DO-NOT-SUCCUMB-TO-THE-CANCER-THAT-IS-CHRISTIANITY! ARE-YOU-WITH ME-AHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! The people laugh a little, as the Reverend pushes Minion off the pulpit, and he lands hard on the aisle. “We shall not follow such a vile religion you little heathen devil. Now leave this place before God condemns you forever!!!” “So…you’re not interested in joining the army of Satan?” asks Minion, slightly saddened. “NO! LEAVE THIS PLACE YOU LITTLE HEATHEN BASTARD!!! LEAVE! LEAVE! LEAVE! WE WANT NOTHING OF SATAN! LEAVE US! I CAST YE INTO ETERNAL DAMNATION YOU LITTLE HEATHEN BASTARD!!! LEEEAAAVE THE HOUSE OF GOD NOW!!!” “Well…fuck you. As for Eternal Damnation…” Minion replies, and flips off the Reverend. A vein appears on the Reverend’s head, as Minion exits the church, and as the doors swing shut, Minion lifts his arm and pushes a little red button on a small handheld device in his palm. Inside the church, the pulpit bursts into flames, as the Reverend and some of the people become engulfed in a cloud of Napalm. Hessian’s green eyes glow intensely, as the flames rise into the sky, turning the sky into a sea of red, black and death. Minion rips off his Angel’s outfit and slips into his cloak. The two Heretics turn and calmly walk away from the church, and back through the suburban wonderland. Hessian looks to the beauty of the sky filled with the shrill screams of Christians, and inhales some of the sweet tasting air. “I love the smell of burning Christians in the morning…” “Nothing like some Eternal Damnation on the Sabbath!” Minion adds, giggling hysterically, as they walk off into the sunrise. ------------------------------------ It has been foretold that an apocalypse will wipe out all of you dirty, inbred rats…and that apocalypse begins with the destruction of The Red Army!!! ………That Apocalypse will continue with the complete destruction of Propaganda and Vampir Nosferatu, the Christian will burn…and the weak shall perish…Let The Carnage Begin!
