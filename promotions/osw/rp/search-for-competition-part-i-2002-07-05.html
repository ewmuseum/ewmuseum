Search for Competition: Part I

5 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 5th, 2002 North Hollywood Clinic. It’s a place Riptah’s been more than a few times during his wrestling career and Dr. Melrose Highland is someone he’s seen many times as well. Actually, Dr. Highland is the man that caused Da Westsidaz’ retirement in ‘that other promotion’. This time it’s much less severe, but Mel has concerns about Riptah competing in a match tomorrow night, as any doctor would. The scene cuts to a room, where Riptah is sitting with his shirt off on a little small bed used for inspecting. The doctor is flipping through a rather unkept medical record while Riptah picks up his “Los Angeles Gym XXXL” muscle shirt and stands up. “Alright doc, you gotta let me go now. I got a match tomorrow night,” Riptah pleads with the doctor who is also Riptah’s physiotherapist. “It’s not like it’s going to be tough, it’s only Twisted Metal. There not even competition…” “Andrew, Andrew, Andrew, Andrew… It doesn’t matter if there going to be a ‘tough fight’ or not. You are not 100%. You know I put my reputation on the line everytime I clear you to wrestle?” Mel shake his head and strokes his gray goatee. “You know your back is shot. You’ve had surgery on it twice in the last two years and yet you still do these high spots and the like. You have to tell your superiors that you can’t take these bumps anymore.” Riptah moans in disappointment and looks his friend and advisor in the eyes. “Mel, am I able to compete? Cut the b.s. and just give me the truth, straight up.” Mel looks at his ceiling with his genuine concerned face. His gaze returns to Riptah’s eyes and he lets out a long, reluctant sigh. “Yes, you’re able to wrestle tomorrow night. But I want you back Tuesday next week for a check-up. Just try and take it easy.” Riptah laughs heartedly at the doctor, “Doc, you know I can’t do that. I ask for these spots, it’s what makes me who I am. Besides, I love opening eyes. Maybe management will see that I’m legit and actually give me some competition.” He takes out his sunglasses and whips them around his forehead. “Well doc, call my agent. I got some stuff we can send over to your house for all the fine work you’ve been doing. I appreciate it and I like to show my appreciation, catch my drift dawg?” “Alright Andrew, take care.” The doctor opens the door as a courtesy as Riptah takes his leave. Riptah makes his way down the corridor quickly and travels into the lobby. He yawns for a minute, smiling to the secretaries who wish him luck Saturday. As soon as he reaches the outside of the facility he pulls out his cellphone and mashes the keypad. It rings for a moment or two, then a voice crackles through the line. “Marcus.” Answers the voice on the other line. “SC, it’s Rip. I’m heading to the airport and going to Tulsa. I’ll meet you at the arena, a’ight?” Riptah quickly shoots into the cellular phone. In the same breath he whips out his car keys and hits the unlock button on his keychain, unlocking a large blue HumVee. “No problemo dawg. I’m already here and just enjoying the sights. Hopefully after we send this trash buckets to the scrap-heap we’ll get some real competition around here. Not many people around that are quite our caliber, if you know what I mean.” Short Circuit replies over the phone. “Okay, peace out bro. I’ll catch you later.” Riptah hangs up and jumps into the vehicle. He starts it up and heads out of the parking lot, driving the large vehicle with the utmost ease as the scene fades to black.
