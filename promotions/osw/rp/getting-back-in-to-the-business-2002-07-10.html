Getting back in to the business

10 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2002-07-10 His name is Bob. He's all of 16 years old, stand just under 5'8', and 150 lbs soaking wet. Since he was old enough to watch TV, all he's wanted to do was be a professional wrestler. And he's started on his way... by being an OSW delivrey boy. Face it, people, you gotta start somewhere. Bob is in Twilight, PA, one state over from where the next SLAM! will take place, walking down a apartment building hallway. After glancing down at a piece of paper on his clipboard, he stops at apartment 113 and knocks. After waiting a few moments, the door opens, revealing the Queen of Extreme, Lillith. She looks Bob up aand down quickly, and says, 'Bob.' leaving the door open, she walks back into the apartment. Bob follows her and closes the door, getting his first look at Lillith's apartment, which is not quite what one would expect. Rather than black as the predominant color, the walls are a light blue, and a lot of the tables and shelves have plants on them. A few pictures adorne the walls, including a large Chinese dragon above the navy blue sofa. Bob stood in the doorway in a sort of awe - this looked almost normal! 'Beer?' asked Lillith. Bob returned his attention to Lillith, who was standing behing the counter that separated the living room from the kitchen. Bob game a somewhat sheepish grin and answered 'Umm... I'm only 16...' Lillith narrowed her eyes at Bob, and asked, 'So?' 'Ok... sure... I guess,' stammered Bob, as he tentatively sat down in a chair that matched the couch. Lillith came into the room with a couple of Miller Genuine Drafts in long-necks and handed one to Bob. Sitting on the sofa, she took a pull, and then turned to Bob. 'So...?' she asked, trailing off in a question. 'Oh!' said Bob in realization, pulling an envelope freom the clipboard and handing it to her, 'This is from Ms. Reagan, about SLAM! this Saturday. She wanted it hand delivered to you. Taking the letter, Lillith's eyes darkened visibly, and she quietly said, 'It better be Corg.' before opening the letter. She took a minute to read it through, and her eyes widened a bit. 'International contendors match?' she asked herself. 'A change to get at the church boy? It's not Corg, but...' A small smile crept onto her face, and Bob didn't know where to be relieved or scared. As Lillith stood up, he raised the bottle to his lips for the first time, and as the first drop touched his tongue, Lillith snatched it away. 'You can't have this, you're underaged,' she said, moving back into the kitchen. 'But...' stutterd Bob, 'but... but you gave me...' 'The Lord giveth, and the Lord taketh away, Bob,' Lillith explained, 'If there was ever anything worth reading in religion, that would be the best lesson to learn. Thank you for the letter, you can leave now.' Bob fairly lept out of the chair - Lillith was starting to freak him out - and flew towards the door, lunging to catch it just before he slammed it shut - he didn't want to piss her off. With the door closed, he took a deep breath in relief, and then ran down the hall and out the building.
