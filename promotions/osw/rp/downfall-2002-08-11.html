Downfall

11 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 11th, 2002 I sat alone in the dark, clutching a pad and paper in my lap as I dozed off to sleep. The quiet sound of a fan swirling in the background kept me awake, but not for long. The screaming of a man from the television I was watching caused me to sit upright in my chair, finally coming back to reality for a second. Looking about myself, I realized that I was in my apartment. The lights were all turned off, with only the glimmer from the television showing. I eased in my chair and listened as the CWL announcers screamed and shouted at the top of his lungs as NC-17 was victorious in yet another match. “NC-17 must be the greatest tag team in the history of professional wrestling!” The CWL announcer shouted. He sounded like he really meant it. I shut off the TV as the face of Graphic Violence and Silence came on again, shaking my head. Only a fool would try to take NC-17 lightly, but if you asked anyone, I was pretty foolish. I looked down at my pad and saw the notes I had written down… …arrogant… …cocky… …dangerous. I glanced at the last word for the longest time. There were few men in this sport that I would consider dangerous. Crazy, demonic, idiotic…these were all words I was familiar with. But dangerous? Throwing the pad down to the floor, I rubbed my face with my hands and contemplated going to bed. The clock said it was almost two in the morning. I was hungry. Within a few moments, I had a tuna sandwich in my hands and a glass of milk in the other. Munching on the soggy bread, I hit the play button on the answering machine. That was odd. I hadn’t even heard the phone ring. The recorder blinked the number three, reminding me that I had three messages to listen to, not just one. How convenient. Beep. “Mr. Lexian, how are you? This is Raymond Burris. I am sure you remember who I am. We recently made the announcement that we had inked a contract with you and I am happy to say it has gone over very well with the media.” I made a hissing noise under my breath. “The media. Like I care what the media thinks.” “We have also been discussing possible new looks. We see that you have graying hair, which is a bit of a shock for us, considering that you’re only twenty-six years old. Perhaps we could begin looking into different dyes in the near future.” “My ass you will,” I replied. “Finally, we have a program for you to work this week. I can’t give all of the details over the phone, but let’s meet some time tomorrow and flesh out the details. I promise you, you’ll enjoy it. Until then, enjoy your day off and I’ll see you in my office at eight o’clock prompt.” End of message. “Prompt, huh?” I said laughing. “Fat chance of that happening.” I knew it would piss him off that I would be late yet again for another meeting, but pissing off Burris was only the beginning of the hilarity. I pressed the next button on the answering machine. Beep. “Hey Tom, this is Colin…” My interest was peaked. “…listen, you have to get this damn penguin out of my apartment. It’s driving me crazy. The damn thing runs into every piece of furniture I have and even knocked over this priceless vase.” “You don’t own any vases, Colin,” I remarked. “…ok, I was just joking about the vase, but the damn thing is still driving me up the wall. And it barks at me sometimes too. Yeah, that’s right, it barks. I didn’t think penguins could, but apparently this one is pretty high on the annoying meter. You have to help me, Tom. Call me back and we’ll take him back to the zoo. Later.” I chuckled, knowing that I’d never let him take the penguin back. The damn thing was just too cute to return. Besides, I figured I could still train it. Feeling slightly amused, I pressed the next button to listen to the final message. Beep. There was nothing for a moment, except for some heavy breathing. I listened closely. “…Lexian, you have to get out. You have to get out now. You don’t know what you’ve gotten yourself into…” The sound of a door crashing open and a scuffle ensued. There was nothing for a few seconds, and then the mysterious male voice came on again. “…gotta…go…SHIT!” The line clicked dead, leaving me to wonder what the hell was going on. Who would leave me a message like that? Who was this person? I looked up to see Natalie coming out of the bedroom in her bathrobe. She looked tired. “Honey, what’s wrong? I heard some weird noise…” “Nothing, sweetie. I was just coming to bed.” I hurriedly pressed the delete button and ran over to Natalie to change the subject. She wouldn’t know about this. She didn’t need to. Message deleted.
