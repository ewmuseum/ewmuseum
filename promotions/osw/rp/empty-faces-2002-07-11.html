Empty Faces

11 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07.11.2002 'Remember yesterday ... walking hand in hand ... love letters in the sand ... I remember you ... Through the sleepless nights ... and every endless day ... I'd want to hear you say ... I remember you.' - Skid Row I still fall asleep at night with a clear mind and wake up in darkness, a cold sweat running down my face because in a dream which could surely be a nightmare, I see her cold blue eyes peering down at me. Even in death she evaluates me ... and I'm sick of it now just as I was then. Her voice, high pitched and pure, still echoes through my mind whenever I fail. 'I will not tolerate this isolence, Cory!' She often screamed. 'How can you still manage to be proud of yourself when you act this pathetic?' My mother. She was always the one with the iron fist and the discipline. I used to feel the sting of her belt when I acted out in usual teenage angst, my father too busy with business or just sitting in a quiet corner. Observing. Trying to draw a line between tough love and child abuse. Everytime he tried, he failed. Never was he punished ... for she still had her furs and her gold. He was there out of convience ... I was made for the sole purpose of a fucking trust fund for her to steal. She never cared. So why did I? Young Cory Rutherford reached out for a hug and there was dirt kicked in his innocent face. That may have been one of the key reasons I changed so quickly. Kindness and love were gone long before the trigger was pulled, contrary to mine and popular belief. Yet I still reflect back to that night to use as an example for the hatred which runs through my unknown soul at this very moment. 'Say goodbye to daddy, young one!' 'Mother! No!' Gunshot. 'Now where is that old woman...' Cries. 'Not Nana ... please not Nana.' 'Shut up you filthy little excuse for a mistake.' She slapped me, then looked my grandmother into the same blue eyes she had inherited from her. Just like I hated my mother, she hated hers. I, unfortunatly, didn't get the chance to kill mine. She did it herself. A second shot from the smoking gun. 'And for the endpoint...' Phrases from the mouth of the beast. And then... One last bullet through the head of the shooter. Flashback to this exact where I see my face, bleeding and mixed with tears, sitting on my bed and wondering what could have been. Would I be the way I am, which ever way that is, if I had a family. Would Kory Storm exist? No. I could never exist as the sadistic man I'm shown as on television and to the masses had it not been for my pain. Without the bleeding holes in the bodies of my parents or the vicious remarks from the beast ... the very woman who gave birth to me ... there is no Kory Storm. Does humanity give a fuck? The question is, do I give a fuck about humanity? There is no way I can. For I cannot care for something or someone if I'm unable to identify with it or them. Thus the ideals of a man like Jim Axtell, hard work and family, could never succeed in a mind like me. I work hard at one thing, and it is systematically destroying ideas like that. Much like one Ivan Stanislav tries to bring down capitalism, the one thing I am familiar with. Try and bring me down Ivan ... make a man who already bleeds on the inside for his identity become hurt on the exterior. You will be the only one who will have to cope with your pain in the end, much like I already do. Each person I associate with is just like me ... yet no one accepts the true characteristics of the human mind. Mankind has an urge to be on top, and I am still the only fucking one who's left behind. Fuck authority. I'll claw my own way. Don't book me ... see if I don't bounce back. Kory Storm ... Cory Rutherford ... my personality will always get the last word. Then I'll be a painful memory in the minds of everyone else.
