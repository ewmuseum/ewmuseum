Journey of a Trashcan Man (Training time!)

16 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/16/02 'Ah, the Pepsi Arena. His last known whereabouts.' Trashy pulls his trash hauler to a stop, parking lengthwise across two handicapped spaces and turns off the engine. He opens the door and hops out, gazing over the empty concrete wasteland that is the Pepsi Arena's parking lot. 'Well, this is where the last show was supposed to be. The wrestling federation that was going to end all the others. The ABF. Home of some of the greatest legends of our sport. Names like the Beserker and Ludwig Borga graced their cards. Masters of the mat like 'Hacksaw' Jim Duggan and 'The Road Dogg' Jesse James. And of course... HIM.' The Trashcan Man walks up to the double doors leading to the arena. He tries the door, and of course it's securely locked. He frowns in frustration. 'How the hell am I going to get in to see if he's here... guess I'll have to try brute force.' Trashy takes a couple of steps back and gives the steel doors a KICK OF FEAR! A loud boom is heard throughout the parking lot as the door shudders beneath the awesome impact. The hardcore legend shakes his head. 'Damn doors think they're Meanstreak or something. No selling bastards...' Suddenly, a deep voice is heard behind the Trashcan Man. 'You're not going to be able to get in that way.' Trashy grins as he hears the voice of the very man he's came to see. He turns to see a figure all in black, with a white skeleton painted on his outfit. To fight an overweight luchadore, one must train against the greatest of all overweight luchadores... 'La Parka, just the man I wanted to see!' exclaims the Trashcan Man. 'I've been looking for you, man, I need your help.' 'Well, the chairman of the ABF is needing some help, too. He needs some green so he can catch a bus back to Mexico.' Trashy nods to the most powerful of all luchadores. 'I'm willing to pay.' If we could see La Parka's face, we'd probably see him smiling. So we're going to assume that's what he's doing. 'Then the chairman is here to help. Whatcha need?' 'I need you to train me. You're the greatest of all the oversized luchadores in the world. And I've got a match with a big, almost 300 pound lucha by the name of El Drago. I figure if I can see what YOU have, I'll be plenty prepared for anything he's got.' La Parka nods. 'Makes sense to me. Well, what I've got is pretty simple.' Suddenly La Parka whips out a chair and just SLAMS it into the Trashcan Man's skull with a sickening thud. Trashy staggers back, putting his back up against the doors. 'That's pretty much it,' says La Parka. 'Really? You mean, no high flying? No crazy super Mexican technical moves? Overweight luchadores are just hardcore wrestlers?! ALL RIGHT! No one can beat me at that, that's MY game!' Trashy tosses a few bills to La Parka as he runs and leaps into his trash truck. He fires it up and rockets out of there as La Parka sputters. The Mexican legend shakes his head as he pockets the money. 'Man, what kind of an idiot pays you to hit him in the head with a chair? He's gonna get creamed.' La Parka shrugs. 'Ah well, time to go find the Kazmaster and see if we can get a job down in NWA-TNA, I could use a new gig...' The camera fades to black
