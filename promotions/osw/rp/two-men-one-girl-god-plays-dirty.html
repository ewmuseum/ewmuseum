Two Men, One Girl: God Plays Dirty

2002-06-24

Reno Davis

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I barely get time to relax anymore. It’s always one thing after another after another these days. Sometimes the buildup in the emotions starts to seep through and turn into physical pain, and when you are constantly being put through physical pain anyway, you tend to burn out. I broke my skateboard last night at Ascension trying to save Amy from that fucker Corg. Too bad I was too late anyway and she got her face chewed on and almost raped. I ollied over a 60 foot drop, heh it’s almost too bad that I didn’t land on my skateboard. I was too afraid I’d hit Amy though, so I heel-flipped and then kicked the board away. Corg didn’t do much to break 60 feet of momentum though, and here I am now. I’m in a mall in Dallas. I didn’t catch the name, as focusing my vision tends to cause a migraine followed by shooting pain up my spine. Cuts scatter across my body, combining with welts and bruises of various colors. I betcha I could find an image of Jesus Christ somewhere on my body if I looked hard enough. For the first time in awhile, I smiled. The feeling was foreign, almost like my face was sunburned. Well, I suppose it’s close enough to a sunburn anyway, my doctor gave me a list of injuries this morning that I’m suffering from. I pulled it out of my pocket and looked at it. On the list was: Bruises and torn muscles, cuts spreading all over my body, bruised spine, separated jaw (He popped it in for me), fractured shin, mild concussion... and the list went on. Luckily there wasn’t going to have to be any cosmetic surgery. The doctor also told me that I had a high amount of stress that’s needed to be dealt with. I can’t walk without a crutch either. God I hate crutches. The mall is pretty crowded, as I’m in the food court. It’s nice though because I think Ivan popped my eardrums and everything seems so far away. It’s like I’m in my own little bubble amongst a riot. Riots...I hate those too. I’m eating pizza I think too. I’m having trouble remembering or realizing what I’m doing today. It’s like I’ll slip into a subconscious world but still another part of me goes on with my normal life. Half of the pizza is eaten, but I’m still hungry. A nice aroma sneaks through my blood-clogged nose, a scent I recognized, but couldn’t figure out. I looked around and saw the source; it was a Cinna-Bon store nearby. Those things are damn good. I observed what was going on in the store. Sometimes I like to do that, I just watch someone and wonder what he or she’re feeling, what there thinking, what they’re hiding underneath their silence. I looked through the line up and it became apparent to me. I’d gone crazy. Spider-Man was standing in line for a Cinna-Bon. I swear to God that Spider-Man was right there. He gave his order to the lady at the cash register, grabbed a Cinna-Bon and walked towards the food court where I sat. He stopped and peered around, almost stupidly, even under the full body suit. This was a first. I’ve never had a hallucination before. I’ve spaced out totally, which was how I spent most of my day today, but this was nuts. Then he looked at me, right in my eyes. Even under the eye things, I could tell he was looking right at me. What happened next sort of caught me off guard. The Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man waved at me. I didn’t know what to do, so I waved back and he started to walk towards me. Looked for some non-bruised skin to pinch, but couldn’t find any so I slapped a bruise. Ouch, seriously, it hurt. But Spidey was still there, or here that it, as he had just sat down, much to my own amazement. “Uh...Hi.” I’ve read Spider-Man comics since I was a little kid. What would you say to Spider-Man? 'GREAT RENO'S OF LUNAR LAS VEGAS, its Reno Davis! State your condition Mr. Davis' Spider-Man was excited to see me... Alright then. Through his mask I could see he was smiling, happy to see me. I took a bit from my pizza, looking away from him for a moment, and when I looked up again he was still there. “I’m...I’m fine. How about you there...Spider-Man?” I was in a bit of disbelief, but hey I was having a conversation with Spider-Man. I don’t even think Stan Lee does that.... well he is getting old and kind of creepy at comic conventions so maybe I should double check on that. 'Spider-Man. You truly have been around monkeys too long...sure it doesn’t have intergalactic brain sucker fleas? I'M RENT-A-HERO...CHAMPION OF PCW...ALL AROUND GOOD GUY...you do know me right?' His tone dwindled towards the end into almost a whine with a hopeful pleading. Rent-A-Hero? I’d heard of him before, I think. He must be a wrestler. “Ah yeah! The wrestler?” I hoped I was right, “Man It’s a pleasure to meet you! How’s it going?” I lied, but the guy who of sent himself to superhero heaven if I told him otherwise. That poor guy... Walking around in Spider-Man outfits and stuff. The wrestling business does strange things to people. 'AHHH YES, it is nice to meet a fellow fan favorite and 'superhero' of sorts.... But where's your costume? Don't all heroes have a costume, or are you in your 'secret identity' mode Reno?' He eyed me suspiciously and pulled my t-shirt’s neck down a little bit to probably see if there was a costume under there. All I felt was searing pain as the t-shirt moving up by back burned the bruises and cuts. I was holding back from slamming my crutch over this guy’s face. Then I realized that if would be wrong to hit someone who...thinks he’s a superhero. Then again, I’m the guy who carries a lightsaber around with me In my kitbag wherever I go. Poor fella. “Yeah, you got me! I’m in my secret...identity mode right now. Heh, but let’s not talk about that stuff. You know,” I winked at him, “Don’t want a villain in his secret identity mode overhearing us and attacking me now do we?” 'Your wisdom is far beyond your years...and I must say, stellar performance last night at Ascension, I must say. You’re bound to be OSW Champ at this rate. Sadly I was watching the pay-per-view for another purpose, I'm sure you know...' What the hell was he talking about now? Maybe he meant like one of his friends or something. “Ah, your friend in OSW huh? Live up to your expectations?” I tried not to say he or she, as I still didn’t know what he was talking about. This was getting a little weird. Wait, I’m talking to a guy dressed as Spider-Man, it was already weird. 'Things could have been better...but all is well. Tell me Mr.Davis...you look down or confuzzeled, how about placing your problems upon the mighty shoulders of...” I swear to God he struck a fucking pose right in front of me, “RENT-A-HERO!!” He yelled his name out with a deep, booming voice, causing people to look over at us. He sat down and under the goggles I knew he wasn’t blinking. He was just watching me. Whatever, what’s the harm in telling him? “Ah...just girl trouble. I’m sure a big superhero like you would know about all that though.” 'Well at a time...but allow my Rent-A-Senses to help you...tell me, what is it about the girl your having trouble with my blue-eyed compatriot?' Well this was new. A mental kid in a spandex superhero costume was giving me women therapy. Next I’ll be jobbing to Griff Truxell on Slam. “Uh, I just kinda didn’t want to hurt this girl I know and told her I couldn’t be with her... But now I realize my mistake and I mean I saved her last night from some freak and she’s still avoiding me. I can’t apologize or tell her how I really feel because of my decision.” 'Oh yes you can...be delicate with her, pour out what it is you truly want and feel. If she truly cares about you...your true and utterly complete feelings will send her into your arms Reno. Love rights many wrongs, it's rights more wrongs then a fist or a costume could ever do.” That answer threw me off too. He was almost normal, and he made sense. “Thanks man,” I felt that I needed to try and give him some advice too, “You said you had a girl at a time, what happened?” 'Well I still do have her...and actually...I've been thinking about...well...marrying her, but I just don't know how to come out and ask her if she wants to be miss 'A-Hero'. Well, whoever this chick was must have been pretty weird. I mean who would dig a guy in spandex superhero costumes? Then again who would dig a freaky little dude with bright blue eyes and no pupils? It’d have to be one unique girl on both parts. “I hear ya man. Just don’t let anything stand in your way of proposing to her,” I smiled, “And let’s wish some good luck for the both of us huh?” This guy was growing on me 'THE POWER OF GREYSKULL IS WITH US FRIEND!! Hey, how about me and you go to the local graphic literature shop, I here Elektra #325 is out.... with Titanium foil pages and...3-D glasses...” He melted into his hands and looked like he was going to have an orgasm. Seriously. “Uh, I’m not an Elektra fan, but the new Daredevil is out and I’d like to look at that and...OH! Is the new Agent X out? Deadpool is so bad ass.” A comic fan is a comic fan, and Deadpool is bad ass 'WIZZER!! LET US BE ON OUR WAY!! TO THE GRAPHIC LITERTAURE SHOP RENO MAN!!' He stood up dramatically, and I pushed myself slowly to my feet, pain sweltering everywhere inside me. This guy was weird, but alright. I grabbed my crutch and together we went to the comic shop to read up on the latest in crime fighting action. All in all, I hoped he got his girl, and I hoped I got mine. Just once, it’d be nice is life did have a bit of a happy ending for everyone...
