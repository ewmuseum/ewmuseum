Jane Says...

3 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-3-02 “Knock knock,” Trevor Hawke says as he bangs his fist against the office doorway, a hopeful look painted across his face. Seth Masters is standing next to him, rocking back and forth on his heels, his hands clasped behind his back as he whistles a small tune. “Come in,” a feminine voice answers from the other side of the door. The two members of Civil Disobedience look relieved upon hearing this, and swinging the door open, they walk into the room happily. Sitting at a desk across the room, OSW owner Jane Reagan, sifting through a series of papers, is waving them further in the room as her glance shifts from them to her paperwork. Back and forth. Back and forth. “Janie!” Seth shouts excitedly, raising his arms in the air to show just how elated he really his. Finally setting her papers down with a small smile on her face, seemingly pleased for the break in her monotonous-looking work, she nods at both men. “Hi Seth...Trevor...what’s up, guys?” “Oh, we just figured you wouldn't mind a visit from your favorite OSW tag team...” Seth remarks with a sly smile. “Not to mention the most-talented and best-looking, of course.” “Oh really, now,” she says back, returning Seth’s smile. “That’s funny, because I don’t see the Throwbacks anywhere.” Seth’s smile fades to a shocked expression, only to come again slowly as realization dawns on him. “Oh, you’re just messing with my head. Yanking my chain. Um...joking. Yeah, I get it. Very funny.” “I thought so...” Jane says, chuckling to herself. “Anyway, you know you wouldn’t be a part of the Elite if you weren’t the top of line as far as talent is concerned.” “And looks,” Seth corrects, pointing a scolding finger at her. “You mustn’t forget the looks.” “I’ll try not to do so in the future,” she replies with a laugh. “As fun as this small talk is looking to be,” Trevor interrupts, clearing his throat, “I believe that despite what my partner says, we have indeed come here on business.” “Oh really?” Jane asks, raising a questioning eyebrow. Trevor takes a more serious pose, lifting his hand as if to make a point. As it looks like he’s about to begin, he suddenly drops his posture, turning to face Reagan in a more casual manner. “On a side note, do you know how frickin’ hard it is to get a hold of you? We’ve been trying to track you down all week. Do you have any idea how hard it is to climb up these umpteen flights of stairs five different times in as many days?” “I’m sorry I’ve been so busy, guys,” Jane states apologetically, “But why didn’t you just take the elevator?” “There was an elevator?” rings out Seth’s shocked voice. “Well I figured that the stairs would be a better form of exercise for us, seeing that the tag team tournament is coming up. I want us to be in tip-top shape for this thing, you know,” Trevor explains to Reagan, not acknowledging his partner’s question. “Why didn’t you tell me there was an elevator?” Seth protests, seeming not to want to drop the topic. “Will you shut up about the damn elevator!” Trevor shouts in an annoyed tone, turning to face his partner. Seth shakes his head in disbelief, not looking too happy, but he says nothing in reply. Satisfied, Trevor returns his case to Jane. “Speaking of the tag team tournament, though, that’s part of the reason we came here today.” “Part of the reason?” Jane asks skeptically. “Ok, well it was all of the reason,” Trevor says. “Well that and we figured you wouldn’t mind a visit from your famous OSW tag team,” chimes in Seth with another sly smile. Jane chuckles again at Seth, before turning back to Trevor with a more serious...and more curious gaze. “The reason we’ve been looking for you all week,” Trevor continues, “is because we were wondering if you could possibly change around these bogus brackets Howard Porter has set up in the tag tournament.” “Hell yeah, they’re bogus,” Seth agrees with an angered nod. “How is it fair that he gets to face some fucked up team like Wonder and Taylor, and even worse, his stable mates get to wrestle...Disorderly Conduct?!?! Yet we’re stuck wrestling Da Westsidaz in the first round, a fairly competent team if not entirely at our level, but still head and shoulders above the stuff Porter has lined up for his clique.” “Well I have to agree with you there,” Jane says, nodding. “The talent pool does not seem evenly distributed at all. It seems as if he hasn’t bothered with the seeding system at all and is just tossing pairs together however he sees fit.” “And that’s not even the worst part,” Trevor says, sounding more disgusted the more he dwells on the topic. “We wouldn’t mind facing Da Westsidaz at all, considering they owe us a fair match after all that shit that went on last month. But the fact that the only reason we’re facing them is that so Porter can knock one of our teams out of the tournament early is complete bullshit.” “I know I sure as hell don’t want to be part of one of the Think Tank’s fucked-up schemes,” Seth adds. “If we just kept our mouth shut and did what these bitches wanted us to for even a minute, we’d be contributing to the eventually downfall of the OSW. I, for one, don’t want to play a part in that shit at all.” “That’s why you, Jane, need to veto Porter’s match making propensities and book both us and Da Westsidaz against Think Tank opponents in the first round,” Trevor says, pleading his case as he cups both of his hands together in a praying gesture. “Please! We need to start taking the fight back to those dirty fucks. We’ll take Da Westsidaz out later in the tournament. Just let us take out the Think Tank now. You have to do this, for the betterment of the federation.” There’s silence in the office as Trevor finishes. Jane’s stare focuses on Trevor for a few seconds, flickers to Seth, then back to Trevor again. Sighing deeply, she starts explaining the situation to the two men. “Guys, there’s nothing I’d like to do more so than give you two what you want here. I completely agree with all your points, and you’re right, it is bullshit that Porter is allowed to do this. However, I no longer have the power to manipulate booking in the OSW. At least not as far as this tag tournament goes. I can’t break the pairings Porter has already announced.” “But...you were the only person we felt could talk to...” Seth moans, disappointment heavy in his voice. “No, there’s still someone else that may be able to help you,” Jane says, putting a finger up to show that all hope is not lost. “The new OSW president...he has the power to grant you guys your wish.” “Why do I feel like I’m stuck in the Wizard of Oz,” Trevor mutters, looking at the ceiling as he rubs his temples. Bringing his head back down, he looks across the desk at Reagan. “Alright, Jane, we’ll talk to the president. Though who is this guy anyway? I mean, you must trust him pretty well to have awarded him with that position.” “Well guys...it wouldn’t be a secret if I told you,” Jane says, the grin returning to her face. “What?!” Seth shouts indignantly. “We’re your trusted accomplices and you can’t tell us who it is! That’s an outrage! That’s worse than an outrage! That’s...” “Let’s just go, Seth,” Trevor says, pulling his partner toward the doorway as he waves goodbye to Jane. “Thanks for your time, Jane.” “No problem, boys,” Jane answers back, trying not to laugh at Seth’s childish outburst. “And I’m sorry I couldn’t help you out. Good luck tonight!” Seth is still muttering to himself as Trevor drags him out into the hallway and shuts the door behind them. “Come on, Seth,” Trevor remarks, walking toward the staircase. “We’ve got to get to the arena early tonight so we can find this mysterious president character and get him to change our match.” “Fuck that,” Seth says, garnering a surprised reaction from Trevor...which turns to a smile as he finishes his sentence. “I’m not doing that shit. I’m taking the damned elevator.” Fade to black.
