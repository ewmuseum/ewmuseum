No Drinking in Public...

5 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/5/02 We see Tom Walczak walking down the street with a six-pack in one of his hands and inn the other an opened bottle of Vodka. The scene is most common for Walczak and all those who know him, but to the people around him now he comes off as nothing, but a drunkard. You see since Walczak’s childhood in Poland there have been many changes to the laws of the country, and one of them is that a man cannot walk down the street with an open bottle of liquor. Stupid as this may seem, rules are rules and everyone, including Walczak must abide by them. However, Walczak is oblivious to the rule change, and no one feels the need to walk up to him, and tell him that what he is doing is illegal. That is until two cops come up on opposite sides of Walczak and ask him to go over to the side of the street. “What the fuck did I do now? I assure you that there is nothing illegal about what I am doing now, or is it illegal for a man to have a drink in public?” “Well sir, actually it is illegal for a person to drink in public, so we are going to have to ask you to come with us, so that we can write up a ticket.” “You have to be joking? So, you are saying that me walking down the street, towards my own home sipping some vodka along the way is illegal? I cannot even indulge in the pleasures of life anymore, what is it that my dear Poland is becoming?” “Sir, please we do not want any theatrics, we just want you to come over to the side of the road, and we can than just give you your ticket without any trouble.” “Yeah sure no problem, it just amazes me that this capitalistic country will not even let me drink in public. If we were still under the communist regime than I would not have to put up with this. I still do not see why you sacrificed all that was good for that which is petty and capitalistic.” “What did you say? Did you just say that Communism was better than that what we have now? Well I oughta…” “I thought that I knew this guy from somewhere. Yeah he is that radical communist guy in OSW. He has a match with that punk ass commie Stanislav, against Doug Troy, and a mystery partner.” “What did you just call comrade Stanislav? I hope that you know what kind of power I wield and if you knew what was good for you than you would realize that shutting your mouth is what would be best for you.” “Oh, yeah well you forget that I have the ability to arrest you causing you to miss the PPV, and thus your number one contenders match with Jesse Ramey, which I foresee you losing anyway.” Walczak does not reply to thus only gets his ticket and makes haste away from the two capitalistic pigs which as soon as he is out of view curses off with a barrage of verbal onslaught that causes mothers to cover the ears of their children. As he gets farther and farther away the scene fades out…
