The Big Time

12 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/12/02 Gloria has just picked up Dame, and they're heading somewhere unknown. Gloria refuses to tell Michael, as she drives down the streets, on the cold, wet, rainy night in Minneapolis. 'Come on Glor, you gotta tell me where we're going' Michael asks with a confuses tone to his voice. He strokes his hand through his hair... 'Well?' He looks her way, this time sounding a bit more assured. 'Oh Mike, I told you from the get go, that it was going to be a surprise.' She says as she looks over and smiles. 'The get go was 10 seconds ago, just tell me, so I can relax.' Michael still confuses, and holds his hand upon his head. 'Relax? What do you think I'm going to do, drive the car off the bridge? Please..' 'I wouldn't be surprised...' Dame mumbles. 'What?!' Gloria intensifies her tone. 'Well, With the way you've been acting lately, I don't know...(he pauses)..I obviously know you won't drive the car off the bridge, but who knows, you could be taking me on some wild goose chase, and I'm just not down for that. Or you may corner me into spending a lot of cash, and to tell you the truth, that's the last thing I want to do. Now anyways.' 'So, now you don't want to spend cash? So tomorrow I can pick you up and you'll buy me a diamond necklace!?' She asks eagerly awaiting the answer, although still the feeling of sarcasim lingers the air. 'Nope. No diamonds tomorrow.' Dame says, also with a sarcastic tone. 'Then you'll buy one now' Gloria slams on the breaks, as she pulls into a paralell parking spot, right outside, 'Willy's Jewlers' 'Ohhh, you have got to be kidding me.' Michael says as he throws the door open. The door swings open and almost hits an old lady walking across the sidealk. 'What in the world are you doing?' the old lady screams. 'Ohhh mam, I'm so sorry.' Dame gets out of the car to see if she needs any assistance. 'Is there anything I can do to help you?' Dame asks with a concerned look. 'Oh shaddup' the lady says as she pulls her large purse over her head and slams it across Dame's face. He lifts his hand to block, but is too late, and ends up just massaging his smacked face. The lady begins to walk down the street and you hear blurbs, but nothing clearly, from the lady. 'Just your luck, you get beat up by a grandma.... Come on Michael, lets go inside. ' She pulls on his arm, dragging him inside. The Jewelry store is nothing of eye catching. Just a couple of rows of showcases, and some tall vertical areas. Only one person is in the store. She's being helped as another jewler walks over to Dame and Gloria. 'Can I help you two?' 'No..' Dame quickly replies. 'Actually, pay not attention to him, he's not having the best of days. And yes, I would like some help. I'm interested in looking at your diamond necklaces. 'Just over this way mam, we have an exceptional selection.' The store worker says as he walks towards the showcase displaying the diamon necklaces. 'Gloria, I really hope you're kidding. It's not like we're married, I'm not buying you jack' 'Calm down there killer, I'm buying it for myself. ' She says as she follows the salesman. 'But Glor, what is the occasion? Why go out and spend 2 thosand dollars?' 'For tomorrow Michael. I'm going to be in your corner for the main event match. You're in the spotlight, and it's only right that I'm in your corner, just like your days in MCW' 'Whoa, I know it's a big night, but why are you going to be there. I mean, I love you Gloria, you know I would do anything for you, but I think I want to be out there on my own.' 'Too late, I already have a meeting schedule for tomorrow with Jane Reegan, where she'll give me my manager license. And we can be the team we were meant to be. ' 'But why now, why didn't you want to be in my corner at the beginning of my OSW career?' 'Oh Michael, you'll never learn. I was waiting for the right time, and with you main eventing tomorrow night, I feel it's the right time. The sky is the limit Michael, and I'll guide you straight there, once again, just like MCW days.' Gloria says as she looks over at the saleman. 'Yes, I'll take that one. ' She points to one of the exquisite necklaces out of the three the man laid out on the table. 'Alright mam, how are you going to be paying for that?' The salesman asks. 'Cash' Gloria says as she reaches into her purse. Michael leans over to see the price of the loaded Diamond necklace. 'Gloria, that necklace is 4,000 dollars!!' 'Mochael, money is no object when you're in the big. Tomorrow night, we, me and you, re-enter the big leagues. You're main eventing Michael. Like I said, The sky is the limit...(she pauses) I gotta stop, my ego may pop.' 'If it didn't already' Dame mumbles as he turns the other way. 'What did you say Michael?' 'I said, I may buy a ring...nahhh, forget it. I'll meet you in the car Gloria.' He turns and exits the store, as Gloria pays. The camera fades to black.
