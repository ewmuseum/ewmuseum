Hurrying to the airport...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/6/01 Walczak is seen in his bedroom, and on his bed is a suitcase, which Walczak is in the process of packing. He puts in a couple of shirts, some pants, shorts, and other miscellaneous objects. He eventually zips up the suitcase, and heads into the kitchen. Walczak grabs a beer from the refrigerator, and leans on a nearby counter and sips the beer slowly. He begins to speak out loud even though there is no one else in the room. “I think that this is a good idea going back to Poland. I mean it will give me some new scenery, and I can always come back here. Plus it will help me get back to my roots, and realize the hatred that burns deep inside of me. The hatred will help me as a wrestler and will help me in my plight for the Red Army to reign supreme once again. What time is it?’ Walczak looks over at the clock on a nearby wall. He notices that he only has an hour and a half before his plane leaves. He unleashes a barrage of vulgar language as he rushes to get dressed. He puts on a pair of jeans and a plain white shirt. Hurriedly he calls a cab, and gets his last few things ready. Within minutes the cab is there and Walczak rushes to get in. He tells the cabby to make his way over to John F. Kennedy Airport, and the driver leaves, but as he is going he keeps his eyes on Walczak. “Shouldn’t you keep your eyes on the road? I think that we would get there quicker if you did.” “Yes, of course sir, but I was just wondering, are you Tom Walczak?” “Of course I am. Why would you want to know?” “Well, I guess it is because my son watches wrestling, and well he really likes The Red Army.” “Is that so? Well, than that is good to hear because it shows that the fight for Communism is already reaching the younger ones.” “No he really isn’t a communist he just likes you and Ivan.” “Well than what the hell would you mention him for? He is not worthy of being spoken of if he is not true communist.” “Hey, you can stop with the act now. I realize that you are not a true communist. You just think that acting like one will help you in life.” “Is that what you truly think? How could they send me a piece of crap driver that thinks like this? You actually believe that I one of the few true communists left in this rotted and decayed world is a fake?” “Yeah I mean you don’t seem like the kind of person that would turn to communism as an outlet for frustration.” “You have been misinformed my friend for I do not turn to communism from frustration, but because it is the one true government. There is no other philosophy such as that of Karl Marx, and there was no leader stronger than Comrade Lenin.” “Ok there buddy, you don’t have to get all pissed off. I understand that you take this crap seriously.” “That is good. Now shut your no good capitalistic mouth and get me to the airport I do not want to be late.” The cab enters onto the Long Island Expressway, and starts its journey to the airport as the scene fades to black…
