Pretending

18 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/18/02 A good deal of the human species is constantly pretending to be something it is not. Whether it is something as subtle as pretending to like your signifacant other's new clothes, when in fact said clothes are extremley ugly, or whether its pretending to be something you are not, man as a species are natural pretenders. No one knows this better than Micheal Luke Elliott, the man all of you know better as Enforcer, the so-called 'anti-hero'. Micheal has found himself in a deep depression lately, and he refuses to tell even his own wife why. Micheal looked out towards the slowly setting sun, a nondescript look on his face. It was 5:45 p.m., and of course the sun sets early during the wintertime. Micheal was wearing his trademark Enforcer black pants and cut-off jeans, and was trying his best to get into the Enforcer persona, despite his depression. He was seated outside a large sports collectibles store, signing autographs of his new special-edition OSW magazine, Enforcer: Epitome of Extreme to commemorate his entrance into OSW. A young boy walked up to the desk Micheal was using to sign autographs, couldn't have been older than seven or so. 'Hi Mr. Enforcer!' the young boy said with his childish innocence. 'Will you sign my magzine, pretty please?' One would think a young boy's happiness would bring a grin to Micheal's face, but they would think wrong. Micheal did not even reply as he simply signed 'Enforcer' on the other side of the front cover, then motioned for the next person to come up. The boy, however, would not give up that easily. 'Won't you at least say hi to me, Mr. Enforcer?' the boy asked. Micheal didn't say a word. 'I'm not moving until you say hi!' the boy said in a huff. Finally, Micheal spoke. 'Someone have this kid removed.' Micheal turned his head back towards the sunset as a pair of security guards led the boy back to his father. 'Mr. Enforcer! Wait!' the boy yelled, but his pleadings were not even registered in Micheal's mind. His eyes remained transfixed on the beauty of the sunset. As the next person in line walked up, a teenager wearing an OSW t-shirt, Micheal called two of the security guards over. 'I'm done signing autographs.' Micheal said. 'But Enforcer, sir....' one of the security guards began to say. 'What the f*ck are you, a wrestling mark?' Micheal said angrily. 'Call me Micheal, jerk-off. No objections, I'm just out of here.' The security guard went to speak again but Micheal got up from his seat and walked away from the table and to his car, parked nearby. 'HEY!' the teenager called to him. Micheal did not even acknowledge him, just continued walking. 'WHY DON'T YOU SHOW US FANS SOME MORE RESPECT?' the teenager called at him. Again, Micheal ignored him, and continued walking. 'WE'RE THE ONES WHO MADE YOU'RE UNGRATEFUL ASS!' Micheal finally stopped in his tracks. For a few moments, he just stood there, then he turned around. He walked back towards the teenager, and stopped a few inches from his face. 'Yes, you did make me what I am today.' Micheal said in an angry, hushed whisper. 'And that is why I will never forgive any one of you. Not a damn one of you.' Micheal turned around and walked back to his car, leaving the stunned fan to dissect exactly what Micheal's words meant. Micheal climbed into his car and drove off, into the sunset, the beautiful sunset. I'm nothing more than a pretender. A pathetic person pretending to be something he is not. Someday, that will change, and everyone will pay for what they have done to me.
