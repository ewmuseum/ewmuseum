The confession booth

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24th March 2002 - 28th March 2002 2-0-0 is his streak so far! Is it true? With the power Jesus, God and the Holy Spirit by his side Propaganda can bring OSW wailing to it's knees? He has already beat Vampir Nosferatu in singles action, where supposedly God gave Propaganda help by blinding him for a few seconds. But at this weeks Slam Propaganda won the 6-man tag match for his team! But now comes his toughest task yet. To defeat Epitome of Evil, 'Angeldust' Nick Casey and Vampir Nosferatu all in one go. An every man for himself four way match. ooOoOoo 'Father, for I have sinned…' We hear these words spoken from a camera secretly hidden inside the pinewood of a confession booth, a man thin as a rake. With a brown scrag of hair, a green woolly jumper and a pair of grey jogging pants. He is speaking into the mesh screen. 'Last night, my wife was out.' He says with much depression, 'And I… I went to the farm in a state of sexual need.' 'Please go on.' Who's voice could it be? It is extremely familiar 'Okay, you promise you will not tell anyone?' The man asks for reassurance. 'Please, I'm a preacher. If you can't trust me who can you trust?' with the ironic fact he is being filmed and broadcast on live OSW television is not to reassuring is it folks? 'Yeah, yeah, I believe you. All right I went down and I saw my favourite pheasant, Pickles. She looked so beautiful, so I kind of took part in sexual relations with the pheasant!' 'WHAT!' The preacher says full of outrage. 'You had sexual intercourse with a pheasant!' Then he calms down… 'I take it you were not married to the pheasant.' 'No.' The man says humbly. 'Well my brother as you know you have sinned that's why you came to me.' The preacher continues 'So my brother join in prayer with me.' 'Okay.' The man clasps his hands together and closes his eyes, as the preacher says the lords prayer. 'Our Father, You art in Heaven, Hallowed be thy name, Thy kingdom come, Thy will be done, On earth as it is in heaven, Give us this day our daily bread, And forgive us our trespasses, As we forgive those who trespass against us, Deliver us from evil, And not into temptation, For thine is the kingdom, The power and the glory, Forever and ever, A-men.' They pray in unison. 'Your sins are forgiven my brother.' The preacher tells the man. 'Thank you father.' The man walks out of the booth. As the scene changes to the other half of the booth, it is identical, the dark brown pinewood, same window. But on the bench sits OSW Manager, Eric Sagar. He was the familiar voice. 'I like helping out the less fortunate…' Sagar tells us, 'I mean me being a better person them they obviously look up to me. ' Sagar gets out of the booth as he sees the man who he was just talking to walk past. He over looks him as he leaves threw the massive brown doors of the church entranceway. He leaves into the sunny realms of outside. 'Freak' Sagar comments on the man, he walks into the main section of the church. It is massive there are pews upon pews that look like they are illumines as the stained glass windows shines its many sections of little colour. At the top is the altar and the choir pews on either side over looking the stand. Sagar walks up the aisle, over the cold stone floor of the church up the steps and behind the stand. 'That was a day in the life of Preacher slash wrestling manager, Eric Sagar.' He echoes through the empty church, as it bounces from the grey stone walls all the way up to the steeple. 'But what about a day in the life of the power himself, Propaganda.' Sagar sighs. 'Well let me show you what we have to put up with.' Eric Sagar walks over to the dark brown wooden choir pews and goes behind them, he then rolls a small television above a VCR on a kind or trolley thing. He rolls it out and stops it next to the stand and takes a black VHS with a label 'Casey Promo #1' on it he slides it in the VHS. 'Now this is a clip from 'Angeldust' Nick Casey's roleplay.' He gets a control and this clips plays. 'Kids, the moral of this story is don't hate people for being a lot better, stronger, faster, and better-looking than you, like EoE does. Don't blame God for loving someone more than you, like Propaganda does. And just because half of your face may be rotted away and you have no friends, like Vampir, doesn't mean you get to chokeslam the new guy. Or you will pay, just like those jerk-offs did.' 'Did you see that?' Sagar asks, 'Let me show you again.' Sagar rewinds the tape, then he presses play. ' Don't blame God for loving someone more than you, like Propaganda does.' 'Oh my Brother' Sagar cries. 'You have sinned! And made one big mistake, for you shall join the likes of Lucifer for your ignorance! If god loves you why did we find out you were a test tube baby? My Brother Saturday Slam Propaganda, with the support of me, every fellow Christian, God, Jesus and of course the holy spirit! Shall take your greasy blonde locks and throw you to a doom!' Sagar chuckles evilly 'Now if you think God loves you more than the Power Himself, then why will Propaganda and I look up to the heavens and Propaganda will be given the power to strike down upon yee with anger and furious vengeance!' Sagar screams. 'You shall pay your price!' 'Now onto Epitome of Evil,' Sagar's voice trembles through the church. 'Now last week on Slam you were incompetent! You and The Magi, both Satanist scum. Were useless it was virtually a three-on-one handicap match against the Power Himself.' He preaches. 'I knew Propaganda could beat all three of them, but he needed a slight bit of help as you and The Magi were both useless. So I looked up to the heavens and like he did to Samson, he gave me a few seconds of ultimate strength, so I put down my bible climbed in the ring and speared the Great Dragon like only Propaganda would.' Sagar brags. 'And the finally entrant in this so-called four-way match 'every man for himself.' Match-up Vampir Nosferatu! I mean Propaganda has kicked his butt twice now, it is getting very repetitive. He is such a loser, he thinks because he is some kind of Vampire he can take down THE POWER HIMSELF! Balderdash! That's all it is. Hopefully Slam, Propaganda shall get you and Exodus you to the reddest pit of heck! RIGHT… WHERE YOU…. BELONG!' He growls 'Testify my brother!' A voice roars from the other end of the church, the camera turns to see standing there in a brown suite that looks kind of old with lighter brown patches on the elbows is Propaganda, 'Testify.' 'Ah, My Brother Propaganda, I'm so glad you could make it!' Eric tells the Power himself as he strides up the long aisle to Sagar. Making a loud clapping noise on the stone. 'Are you telling the Atheist scum at home about what I shall do at Slam?' Propaganda asks. 'Indeed I am.' Says the southern preacher. 'Did you tell them that 'Angeldust' Nick Casey will be sorry for his foolish ignorance?' 'Yes I did my brother.' Sagar assures him. 'Did you tell them that Epitome of Evil's uselessness will not go unpunished when I crush his skull?' Propaganda enquires. 'Indeed.' 'Well did you tell them that Vampir Nosferatu has his last chance against me, for God and I shall send him straight to Lucifer?' The Power himself climbs up the steps to the stand where Eric Sagar is. 'Yes,' Sagar says 'Yes I did! Have faith in me, Mark 9:24 'I do believe, help me overcome…' '…my disbelief'' Propaganda finishes the quote. 'I believe you my brother.' 'Good, good. For myself…' Sagar is interrupted as a cell phone goes off. 'Ah, That would be me.' Sagar takes the cell phone out of his jacket pocket and puts it to his ear. 'Hello Eric Sagar speaking, yes, yes, what… on the God channel? Really? Amazing! My brother you are in my prayers, may god be with you always. A-men.' Sagar hangs up the cell phone and puts it back in his top pocket. 'What was that?' Propaganda asks. 'It was the kind people at 'GOD TV' my brother.' Eric Sagar informs Propaganda. 'What did they want?' He asks. 'Well my brother.' Sagar says, 'Friday night they will give me my own Television chat show.' 'Alleluia!' Propaganda testifies. 'And if it goes well I could very well have a weekly slot. But my brother may I ask a favour of you?' 'Anything my Brother.' Says the 7' monster 'Anything.' 'I want you to be my guest.' Eric requests. 'I shall' Propaganda proclaims. 'I want you to tell the world your story, how you came to be.' Says the Preacher. 'I see.' 'So you shall?' Sagar queries. 'I shall.' Propaganda again proclaims. 'A-men.' Sagar 'A-men my brother.' Propaganda says for the ending line as the scene fades to black then in bold white letters it says… To be continued….
