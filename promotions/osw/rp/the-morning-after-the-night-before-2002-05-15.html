The Morning After The Night Before

15 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15.05.02 The curtains flutter as the giant slowly stirs in his bed, rubbing the side of his head with his massive hand, trying to get rid of the aching pounding at the back of his cranium. A crack of sunlight sneaks into the room through a gap in the curtains, the wind changing its location as it catches the blinds. The curtains flicks the sun onto the face of the beast, forcing him to squint his eyes, shielding his face with his forearms, “Ahhhh…,” yawns the German, slowly pushing the covers down from his chest to closer to his abdomen, Rubbing his chin and cheeks, Krieg starts to sit up but slumps back down after realising how tired he is. Making another attempt at bringing himself together, Krieg pushes himself up with his elbows and slowly but surely sits up, rubbing the sleep out of his eyes. Looking over at his desk, the green light on his phone is flashing quickly twice, indicating he has two messages. Krieg swings his legs round the side of the bed, one by one, and begins to stand up. Walking extremely slowly, Michel walks over to his desk and places his left hand down on the desk to support him as he presses the ‘Playback’ button. The machine clicks and a computer-styled voice begins to speak, “First Message. Message received today at 10.07am,” Krieg looks at his clock on the wall, the hands indicating it is 12.14, “Oh no…” “….Krieg, this is Ed, from the OSW. I’ve just got word that the bar you destroyed the other night is suing you for a considerable sum. I wouldn’t go around busting anymore bars or barmen for the moment, because this is coming out of your paycheck. Just remember Baby, you’re not got ‘It’ so just keep it cool, and I’ll see you on SLAM!,” The machine clicks again, the light only flashing once now, as Krieg rubs his head again. Pressing the Playback button once more, Krieg listens to the next message, “Next Message. Message received today at 11.28am,” “…Michel, it is Ivan. Did you get a good sleep, Comrade? I hope you do not try any stunts like that again, my friend. I can not come and find you every time you want to go out on a binge. And also, remember what I said about Communism and The Red Army. I want you to make the right decision on SLAM! That is all. Ivan,” Krieg rubs his chin again and sighs, What will I do? Who is the better victor? Me? Ivan? The Army? Walking over to the window, Krieg pulls the curtain open slightly but shuts it quickly as the light gets in his eyes again, “Ahhh! I really need to watch what I drink in future,” said Krieg, regretting his drinking binge, What is more important? A victory for Krieg? A victory for Ivan? For The Red Army? For Communism? Who will prosper more from the win? What will happen if I choose to win? Thoughts ran wild through Krieg’s head, the German not so sure what he was aiming for anymore. I need time to think. Time to get my thoughts together…But Slam is only 4 days away. That’s four days to choose between myself and boosting my career or Ivan and bettering the Army.That’s not enough time.
