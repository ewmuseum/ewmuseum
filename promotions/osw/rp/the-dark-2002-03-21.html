The Dark

21 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 21st 'I will dedicate And sacrifice my everything for just a second's worth Of how my story's ending And I wish I could know if the directions that I take And all the choices that I make won't end up all for nothing Show me what it's for Make me understand it I've been crawling in the dark looking for the answer Is there something more than what i've been handed? I've been crawling in the dark looking for the answer Help me carry on Assure me it's ok to use my heart and not my eyes To navigate the darkness Will the ending be ever coming suddenly? Will I ever get to see the ending to my story? Show me what it's for Make me understand it I've been crawling in the dark looking for the answer Is there something more than what i've been handed? I've been crawling in the dark looking for the answer So when and how will I know? How much further do I have to go? How much longer until I finally know? Because I'm looking and I just can't see what's in front of me In front of me Show me what it's for Make me understand it I've been crawling in the dark looking for the answer Is there something more than what i've been handed? I've been crawling in the dark looking for the answer' - Hoobastank 'Crawling in the Dark' Crawling in the dark, in a sense this whole Tag Team Table match was about just that. Michael Dame was crawling in the dark after finding out about the 3B's and Hardy's involvment in their planned kipnapping. Jesse Ramey was in the dark about..well what wasn't he in the dark about? I'd say he's in a current state of confusion with..himself. And then there were Azreal Cain and Hessian, Heresian Bloodlust who were just dark, in a gothic sense of the word. Twisted Metal though, they were the exception. They weren't particularly dark in their mindsets about anything, so it seemed. After all the closet thing those two got to a brainstorm was a light drizzle, so it wasn't really their faults but their programmers or whoever was responsible for assembling them, but that was an issue for a different day. Fact was 3B had faced most of these men before, they'd beaten Twisted Metal and they'd beaten Dame and Locke in tag team compation and Drew'd faced Ramey himself; and man did they beat em bad. Poor Chico was taken apart and given the Hostyle Takeover not long ago, which is still probably inplanted in his artifacial intelligence of he's even been repaired. Meanwhile Michael Dame and Jesse Ramey, they just got beat into obvillion. Yet they all came back for second helpings, Heresian Bloodlust would be served first though. And all for a shot at Civil Disobedience, the tag team champions. Those guys at the very top of the Tag Team mountain and they'd earn their place by beating the huge monsters of Heresian Bloodlust. So the two were bound to be out for blood. But 3B were beaten to by Civil Disobedience and were out for the very same thing, the Tag team Titles. Heresian Bloodlust? Michael Dame? Jesse Ramey? Twisted Metal? They wouldn't be an issue. The scene opened to show Drew Hostyle standing near a park in Orlando, Florida where the next Slam! event would be held. Drew is wearing a pair of long black Lugz jean shorts and a shirt with the Lugz logo on it. He has a Switchblade T-shirt in his hand and is pacing around near the swings of the playground. We pick up on audio and can hear Drew mumbling to himself about the person he's about to meet. 'I hope he likes this shirt, his ass is so hard to please half the time. Pffft, you'd think I'm doing this for HIM.' Drew than spat on the ground beside him and shook his head as he sat down on the wooden bench behind him. Drew then puts his elbows on his knees and as he raises his head to look up again, there is a boy small white boy beside him. The boy has brown eyes and short bleached blonde hair. He's also wearing a pair of casual khaki pants and a royal blue Polo shirt. He's also wearing a big pair of Christian-esque sunglasses that cover his eyes and much more of his face. He's also wearing a hat that covers his ears and has dark and light blue designs on it's side. As he puts his hand on Drew's back, he speaks in a calm but not overbearing tone. 'Ready for the match?' The boy asked. 'Well..' Drew starts. 'What's wrong too many chicks wanna Do the Drew or something? No time to train?' The boy inquires. 'Ya damn straight, I'm ready.' Drew answers simply to the man. 'I don't know Heresian Bloodlust look like pretty stiff competition.' The boy inquired. 'They were anything but stiff when they fought Civil Disobedience, those seven foot monsters fell like four ounce feathers when they went for the title. Us on the other hand, 3B we stood tall and those crackas resorted to cheap tactics to win the match. You know, they can say this and that about deserving it more or about how we're not a challenge because we think we're being oppressed and using racism as a crutch nowadays. But when it comes down to it, I don't need no crutches to whoop their asses, and I sure as hell don't need an excuse. But by saying all this they'll crippling eachother from right under their feet and they dont' even know it. Hell, if it gives them more ways to feel better for losing then heres' to em. But when they find that losing is losing no matter what direction it may come from.' 'By hook or by crook it's all in the book, right?' The boy smirks. 'Heh, heh. Yeah, you right.' Drew smiles back. Drew then realizes that the T-shirt he's had in his hands is still restign there, so he raises it and stretches it out in front of the boy's eyes. 'Wow, thanks dude. I really appreciate this.' The boy said in awe as he looked in the Switchblade T-shirt he'd just received from Drew. 'Well, I'm glad you like it. It's the least I could do after what you did.' 'That's true enough.' said the boy, still gawking at his brand new shirt. 'You could really offend someone with your modesty, you better lay back on it.' Drew said using sarcasm as he said the word 'modesty'. 'Don't you speak to me as if I'm some kind of kid, you know how we cut the deal and if you dont' like it that way then you're out.' 'Whoa, whoa, whoa. i never said nothin bout that man. I just think that..' 'That's just it, you don't think things through Drew. You don't plan, you don't have patience, you dont' get the big picture.' 'What the f*ck is dis therapy with Doctor Hardy?' Drew asked outraged. 'If you see it that way. I'm simply saying that you need to follow through with ideas. My ideas in particular.' Hardy declares. 'And what have your ideas ever got me?' Drew queried, still not thinking through the question before he asked it. 'Hmm..I see what you mean.' 'You and the Benefit could take a few lessons from me on how to run another man's life. I do it quite well I think.' 'We'll see the effects of that first hand on Slam! But speaking of first hand, I managed to throw my weight around with da Boss and..' Drew pauses and pulls a tiny piece of paper out of his pocket. '..got you a ticket.' Hardy's reaction is pure silence. 'And here I go thinkin that you'd be grateful, you know you really is a evil lil kid, that..' Hardy gives Drew 'Don't give me no shit' look and Drew stops talking dead in his tracks. 'You want me to be there? Once again not planning things through, you expect me to be in the same arena as..Shim?' 'Shim?? Well I know that Ramey's a fruit, but he won't try and pull nothin on you man.' Drew assured Hardy. 'No. Not that Shim, I'm talking about Dame. You already know why he ended up in jail once, and he managed to get parole for that, he'd probably slither his way through being innocent for killing a child.' 'You sure didn't sound like no child a couple of minutes ago. Don't worry 3B got ya back lil man. Michael Dame won't be able to think straight, so forgot about remembin names and sh*t.' 'Somehow you're ebonics and muscles dont' comfort me now.' 'They will. After you see how I deal with this match, you'll see. I'm not just looking out for you Hardy, I'm watching myself. Ever think that I wanted to hold some gold for once. F*ck being a guardian or some shit, I wanna win too. Crackas, crackas everywhere talkin shit about how damn thug like or bad I TRY to be. Well I'll show em just how thugged out I AM. At least I don't deny who I am. So I’ll tell you exactly who the men I’m fighting are. There’s Hessian, the seven foot monster who can't pull half of that...where it counts. Azreal Cain, the 375 pound beast who couldn’t lift five with his inflated, steroid pumpin’ arms. Then Jesse Ramey, the insecure man, who no matter how much his buddies pull him, won’t step out of the closet. But last and certainly least der’s Michael Dame..a guy you’re nervous about? Who sold dat dro and got the block hot without even admittin it? He don’t even know who he is, himself or for that matter the people around him. We’ll see who earns some “respect” Saturday. All those years, and months of tryign to regain his oh so great reputation that he maintained before being in jail will seem like a waste to the de-moraling and chopping him down to the levels that I play at this Saturday. Ya feel me?” Drew finished. “I’m not touching you, Drew.” Hardy says as he snatches the tickets out of Drew’s hand and he extends his hand to slap him but Hardy turns around and says, “And make sure to introduce me to the guys.” “Heh..will do. Maybe Frankie can handle you‘re grown ass then.” Drew laughes as the two walk off the playground and the scene fades to black.
