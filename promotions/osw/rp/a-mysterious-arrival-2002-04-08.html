A Mysterious Arrival

8 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-8-02 Last Saturday night, a tremor was felt all across the nation of America. A powerful, torrentious rumbling that shook the very foundation of the professional wrestling world. A tremor caused by the wake of a thousand souls all stampeding away for cover following an explosion of epic proportions. The face of the Queen of Extreme contest has been shaped and distorted and significantly altered by one man. The newest and perhaps greatest threat this company has ever seen. One man alone. This man is the yokozuna warrior Kimbusa. At an average six feet, Kimbusa is perhaps taller than most of his Japanese bretheren. But at an incredible five hundred pounds, perhaps more, Kimbusa is a formidable sight. Dressed in his traditional black silk robe, Kimbusa stands in all his glory amid a frothy bath of golden sunlight. The light of the setting sun bathes his face in an angelic glow, the ultimate of all ironies. For below this heavenly glow lies a black storm brewing. Kimbusa is standing on the balcony of his new home. Having moved from Japan, Kimbusa was seeking a remote house of oriental construction, and found an old home once owned by a recluse in a mountain range in the Rockies. Its exact location is difficult to determine, but is inconsequential anyway. Kimbusa bathes in the sunlight as is sets beyond the horizon, shedding darkness and chill on the opposite side of the valley. The Japanese house has been painstakingly constructed, and matches the ancient designs of Kimbusa's old home in Hiroshima. But his eyes tell the tale of malcontent, as he stares out at the valley below. The balcony overlooks the river flowing below, the furthest point out from the home which rests firmly on a green cliff. His jet black hair flows freely, dancing lightly in the breeze, but remaining clear of his face. The mighty warrior stands in total silence and stillness, his eyes only half open, his chest not moving despite his slow, deep breathing. The screen door behind him slowly slides open. A small, aging man steps onto the wooden balcony, which moans under the added weight. The door is slid closed, and the man takes a position next to the warrior. This is Yoshi Kimawa. Yoshi has been Kimbusa's manager, trainer, guide, and friend for many years. Together, they are the only men either of them trust. Kimbusa acknowledges his mentor's presence with a raised hand, not turning away from the sunset beyond. Yoshi is clad also in his traditional Japanese robe, grey and black and made of silk. Yoshi has a thin layer of grey hair receding on the back of his scalp, and a bushy grey beard hanging from his chin. With soft, careful strokes, Yoshi runs his fingers over his beard, remaining silent for several moments. Finally, after a few moments of silent reflection, Yoshi breaks the silence. 'There is much to be done. They fear our arrival.' This statement earns only a light nod from Kimbusa, whose attention is not diverted. 'I spoke with her father. He is pleased.' Kimbusa nods once more, now turning his head slightly to glance at his mentor. 'And what of Bethany?' 'We will obey her father's wishes. That is all.' Kimbusa nods once more in agreement, before turning his head back to the sun. Yoshi smiles thinly and turns to Kimbusa, studying him carefully. 'Is this place satisfactory? I have found it to be quite peaceful, myself.' '...It will do.' Yoshi, not satisfied with Kimbusa's answer, now presses him further. 'Something troubles you.' Kimbusa again turns his head slightly so that he might make eye contact with his mentor. He holds a stare for only a moment, before turning his head back to the valley, and looking downward. 'Kid Wonder deserves our retribution.' Yoshi's face tightens with dissatisfaction. 'To what end? He gave you what he had on the first night of our presence, and already you have proven your might. Now you must prove your enlightenment. Let it go, Kimbusa-san. Your energy is best spent elsewhere.' Kimbusa seems angered by this, and now finally turns fully to his left to face his mentor. He crosses his arms over his mighty chest and cocks his head slightly to the left, impatient with his mentor's lack of understanding. 'So I should allow this American to get the better of me after my first night? I have come here to find an outlet for those emotions you have so long wished to keep locked away. I would not have afforded this Kid Wonder a second glance before he chose to do what he did. But he sent me a message, and it is my responsiblity to send him a message back.' Kimbusa quickly turns away from his master, placing his arms on the balcony railing and resting his weight on the steel, placing a great deal of trust into their integrity. Yoshi sighs and turns his head downward. 'You must follow your own path. But when you have followed my guidance you have found the right path, son. Instead of your glorious victories in Japan, you could have found yourself fading into obscurity, a mere relic of a once feirce warrior. But because of the peace I taught you, Kimbusa, you now have the opportunity to become the feircest combatant in American history. But this is a new realm for both of us, Kimbusa-san. I beg your patience.' As Yoshi speaks, Kimbusa's face melts from its stern glare to a submissive frown. He closes his eyes for a moment, still motionless in the breeze. 'I will be patient as you wish. But Kid Wonder will not escape my wrath. We knew from the start that someone would have to be the first example. Kid Wonder named himself for that position last week. I will heed his own request to be first. But I shall let him come to me. I shall let him continue as he has, until he sees the futility of his actions. Only then will I make my move.' Yoshi sighs again, and reaches into his cloak. He emerges with a long wooden pipe, which he places in his mouth. In his other hand he produces a match, which he strikes against the railing and raises to the pipe. After a moment a large puff of smoke osmosizes from his mouth to the cool air beyond, and Yoshi drops the match to the floor. He takes a few puffs from his pipe, as Kimbusa presses for an answer. 'Will this be acceptable, sensai?' Yoshi turns to the fading sun, which has now fallen below the horizon. With the few last rays of light bouncing amid his black eyes, Yoshi nods slowly. As he opens his mouth to speak, another large cloud of smoke is ejected, covering his face in its entirity. 'Yes, Kimbusa-san. You shall have your revenge.......'
