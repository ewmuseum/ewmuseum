Damnation for Walzak

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02Sept02 Even the spoken words of a madman do not account for his actions. A broken man but his pride not damaged is the only thing that matters in life. But when that bond is broken then the man becomes mad and dangerous. People say that no man has ever fallen to this fate but few actually have. Losing something of great importance to you will snap this bond like a twig and force you to new lows that you never thought you would sink to. Titles are one thing but when a man loses his daughter for 5 years he will force revenge on all of those who stand in his way. One man seems to haunt the dreams of Silence now, Tom. “There a problem Mr. Hight?” a sheepish lawyer asks as he slides a stack of papers towards the sulking Silence. “Problem? Sure my life is full of problems and these papers only add to my problem.” “Kc, these papers demand your signature by the courts, there is nothing you can do about it. We have tried to get your daughter back to you but the judge deems that you are on the road to much and that she is in better hands with your wife.” It is painfully obvious that the lawyer is trying to keep Silence calm because he knows that his daughter means everything to him and is the only thing keeping him from complete and utter insanity. “That fuckin’ bitch can go straight to hell. I proved that she is a fuckin’ drug addict that can’t take care of a damn cockroach let alone my daughter.” Silence groans and begins to sign the papers. “But all is not lost my friend because for the first time in 1 year I have a job and I am ready to prove to everyone that I am ready to get my daughter back.” The scene fades and opens back up a few hours later to an airport where cameras and reporters standby, hoping to catch an OSW superstar as he or she gets on the plane. Stars like Rhett Locke stop and chat and Aimz does her famous tease fest to the cameras before getting on the plane. One camera catches Silence as he is getting on plane. “Silence, Yo Silence, George Hutch from ESPN news can we have a word with you?” cries out a very sluggish looking reporter. He grabs Silence by the shoulder and whips him around smiling the entire time. “If you don’t want to find out just how educated my fuckin’ feat are I suggest you let go.” says the OSW superstar who is just a few hours from two loses of the day. “ R R Right away Mr. Silence.” Replies a not so confident reporter. “I just wanted to get your thoughts on your lose last night and you blowing you chances of getting Tag Team gold.” “Blow my chances? Let you fill you in you fuckin’ punk! Last night I took a 10 foot dive out of the ring and landed on a damn board of barbed wire! After that I still got up and put on a fuckin’ fight and why did I do that? Because I can! After tonight I am going after a man who I owe a lot of fuckin’ pain to.” “Who would that be?” “Tom Walczak.” “Walczak? Why him?” “That fuckin’ has been has been haunting my dreams ever since I took his ass from pillar to post in our tag match a few weeks ago. Walczak needs to be taught a lesson, he needs to find out what happens when you cross that line from wrestling into homicidal destruction! Tom needs to find out what its like to lose something that you love and revere. I am going to take his pride, he honor and he fuckin’ career and flush it straight down the fuckin’ drain. As soon as I am done with him then I will throw his body into a pit of hell and watch it burn in damnation. Walczak will then realize that not even a Lazarus pit will be able to save him when he is done serving his time in my living hell.” “Strong words for a man who is not so strong!” laughs the reporter. “Fuck you too!” Silence screams right before he places a well placed roundhouse kick upside George’s head. Silence then lifts him up and throws him head first into a lavatory sign. Silence then picks up his bags and heads towards the plane as he leaves the bloody reporter on the ground. “Interview over bitch!” Insanity, such a strong word to describe yet a strong man.
