To: 'Amy Campbell'; ([detail withheld]) From: 'C.L.I.T'; ([detail withheld]) Received: 8:51PM 09/04/02 Text: Miss Campbell, The Christian Ladies International Tribunal fell witness to an absolutely disgusting and utterly offensive sight earlier this morning when a representative in our council happened upon a document that had your name stamped on it as the author. We are deeply offended by this so-called 'guide', which contents directed young people to not only become vegan, a very damaging eating habit of sorts, but also told them to take their parents' clothing with and/or without permission, act disrespectfully towards any and all people as well as listen to explicit music in hopes of meeting your standards of being 'cool'. Documents of this sort should not be accessable to our impressionable youth, and it is to this point that we request for you to denounce the paper in question's content, meaning and any possible interpretations. In short, we here at CLIT wish for you to respect our requests, which would mean completely obliterating traces of this document and, we regret to inform you that if our wishes are not met to our satisfaction, we will take whatever immediate measures are necessary to have our needs fufilled.
