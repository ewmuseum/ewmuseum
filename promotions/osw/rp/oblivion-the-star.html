Oblivion the Star

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'THIS IS THE SONG THAT NEVER EEEEEENDS, AND IT GOES- OOOON AND ON MY FRIENDS! SOME PEOPLE-STAAARTED SINGIN' IT NOT KNOWING WHAT IT WAS, AND THEY'LL JUST KEEP ON SINGING IT FOREVER JUST BECAUSE IT IS THE SOOOOOONG THAT NEVER ENDS!' The young children were squealing the song at the top of their lungs in the back of the SUV, the mother in the front of the car was just hardly tolerating their unneeded screams and shouts. 'Children, please quie-' 'NINETY NINE BOTTLES OF SODA ON THE WALL! NINETY NINE BOTTLES OF SODAAAA! TAKE ONE DOWN, PASS IT AROUND, NINETY NINE BOTTLES OF SODA ON THE WALL!' 'SHUT UP!' The mother yelled, interupting the rest of their ''singing'', as it were. The oldest, a boy, with a buzz cut, replied. 'You silly goose.' His mother replied, sternly. 'I'm not a silly goose. Be quiet, I'm trying to drive.' 'Silly goose.' The youngest chuckled at her older brother's name calling. It was all innocent fun to the young blonde haired girl. 'I'm not the silly goose, Kyle, you are,' Their mother once again replied, trying her hardest to tolerate her two ''bundles of joy''. The little girl said, 'Silly goose.' 'Janet, I am NOT the silly goose!' They continued to argue over who infact the silly goose was, all the way down that interstate highway. Alas, they would meet their end to an eighteen wheel truck. Kyle, age ten, would be the only survivor of that seven car pile up, excluding the eighteen wheeler's driver- he would be paralyzed from the neck down, and mentally retarded. Oblivion shined bright. . . *** .Shortly After. Blood was spread across the asphault like paint on canvas as the paramedics pulled dead body after dead body out of their vehicles. It was dusk, indeed, and the stars gleamed brighter than the sirens and the flashlights that seemed to shroud the unfortunate incident. The local channel news program vans covered the perimeter, while the choppers hovered over the seven car pile-up. The smell of fresh death loomed over the apparent accident, and investigators were speculating over exactly how this accident happened. 'It appeared the owner of this now unidentifyable grey SUV went on a complete road rage of sorts, and was speeding over the speed limit excessively by the time she slammed into the Honda Civic, and shortly after the Pepsi truck had nowhere to go. It was somewhat like a domono effect, if you will. . .only this was no game. . . I'm afraid not.' Others believe that it was a complete accident. . . 'I have no idea how this event occured, but I can make a hypothesis in stating that this was an unfortunate accident created by the Honda Civic. My guess is the driver switched to that lane, prematurely. . .that is what caused the accident' Oblivion shined brighter. . . *** 'GOD DAMNIT KYLE! SHUT UP! SHUT YOUR MOUTH!' 'MOMMY, STOP! IT HURTS!' 'I'LL SHOW YOU WHAT HURTS KYLE!' The mother continued to slap her son around, completely turned around in that driver's seat. The younger daughter cried, weeped, begged for her mom to stop. But unfortunately, she never did, until it was too late. 'NOW, STOP YELLING! I DON'T WANT TO HEAR ANOTHER WORD!' 'PLEASE MO-' 'SHUT-UP!' As the abusive mother turned around, she became the reason, and the victim of this seven car pile up. How delightful this was for Oblivion. . .Another victim of his will, another victim of his god forsaken massacres. Not just 1, but fifteen more. And Oblivion's favorite part of it all? As Oblivion shined while shrouded in the dark, a child survived to be tormented. . . The child of an abusive mother. . . The victim of a car accident. . . The unfortunate Kyle whom survived. . . Oblivion would have another chance to play. ~
