I hate Scott Taylor. Oh yes I do. I hate him in that HATEFUL sort of way.

11 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 11th There are two kinds of hate in this world. One is a love/hate. The other is a hate/hate, or pure and utter hate. Love/hate is a friendly sort of hatred for someone or something. Kind of like a rivalry in sports. The New York Yankees and Boston Red Sox hate one another on the field, but never would they wish serious harm upon one another. Same for two English football teams that hate one another. They might hate each other on the pitch, but they don’t actually hate each other. Love/hate could also be when you like someone one minute, hate them the next minute, and then like them the minute after that. Sort of like a relationship and/or friendship. You love them…you hate them. It’s all good. Then there’s that other kind of hate…the hate/hate. That’s the bad hate. You never want to hate/hate someone, but sometimes it happens. Sometimes certain things happen that lead you to absolutely hating someone. In Old School Wrestling, the hate/hate form of hate is a common thing. Corg and Amy Campbell have a hate/hate relationship. He hates her…she hates him. It wasn’t always like that until Corg took it upon himself to rape the young lady. A feud is fine, but lowering yourself to the point of raping someone is not only wrong, it’s completely indecent and unnecessary. What else is Aimz supposed to do? Is she supposed to pretend it never happened? Of course not! She hates Corg’s frickin guts and wants him to suffer as much as he made her suffer. Then there’s the relationship between Lillith and Bethany Yamato. These two really hate one another. Well, actually, I dunno how Beth feels about Lillith in reality. But one thing is for sure: Lillith loathes Bethany. She hates her to the point of breaking script in a wrestling match and actually beating the crap out of her. OSW is a professional wrestling organization. But when someone like Lillith gets personal, you know it’s real hate. Lately, the relationship between Scott Taylor and Kid Wonder has crossed that line to the hate/hate side. Taylor hates Wonder for being successful on his own and making a real name for himself. Wonder hates Taylor for being a jealous douchebag. Hate/hate all around. In fact, it’s gotten to the point where kayfabe is becoming less and less important in the workplace. Now James Spinks, better known as Kid Wonder to the wrestling world, is not a hateful person. So what exactly would make him hate Scott Taylor so much? Maybe we should examine some facts. First, Kid Wonder makes a great impression at the last OSW Pay-Per-View, Ascension. The next show, OSW SLAM!, all Kid Wonder asks for as a reward is to be booked in one match. What happened instead? Scott Taylor, in typical newbie fashion, sneak attacks the proverbial big name in an attempt to make a name for himself. The next week, Kid Wonder soundly beats Scott Taylor in a singles match for a shot at aforementioned Corg’s Cruiserweight title. But Wonder didn’t get the win. Instead, the referee, the lousy communist Speedy Riggs, gets knocked out just long enough for Taylor to cheat his way to a cheap win. A couple weeks later, before his title match, Taylor hires this extremely large jackass named Randolph to be his bodyguard. Nothing happened early, but the jackass would play a role in events later to come. Some time passes. Things cool off a little, but remain heated as Wonder gets the best of Taylor on a couple occasions. Things seem to be cooling down just in time for the “Quintessential Teen Idol” to move on to bigger things. Then, a couple weeks ago, Scott does the stupidest thing and barges into Howard Porter’s locker room. In return, Porter punishes Taylor by tagging him with Kid Wonder for the tag team tournament. Then comes last week…the week that really turned this rivalry ugly. First, Taylor refuses to tag in leaving K-Dub to take the majority of the abuse in the tag match. When Taylor does get tagged in, he promptly jobs. Next, not long after Taylor jobs away a chance at the tag straps, Scott takes it out on KW by going after he and Amanda. Amanda is not a wrestler. Sure, she competed in the Queen of Extreme tournament, but that doesn’t mean anything. She is not a wrestler. Repeat: Amanda Sampson is not a wrestler. So Taylor, along with the previously mentioned jackass, decide to go after her. And to do so, Scott cowardly takes out K-Dub from behind. Words are said, kayfabe is broken, and the end result is Amanda left in the back lot in a trashcan. Scott might find it funny now, but does he realize what he actually did? He angered a man who, despite his appearance, is not a man you want to be around when angry. And Jimmy has reason to be angry. Imagine if he took that foreign lover of Scott’s, Tomas or whatever his name is, and did to him what Scott did to Amanda. How would he like that? Money says he doesn’t. Money says he comes out looking for revenge. On August 25th, that’s exactly what the wrestler known as Kid Wonder is gonna get. Revenge. It’s not longer a wrestling thing. It’s a personal thing. Scott Taylor, whether he realized it or not, crossed that imaginary line. You know, the one that you just don’t cross no matter how much you hate someone. And to get his revenge, James is putting Amanda on the line. Either he’s that confident or he’s that hell bent on vengeance. Whatever the case may be, Wonder has put the love of his life on the line just to get another shot at the bitch. If he wins, not only does he get revenge, not only does he keep Amanda, but he also gets one of Scotty’s loves…Randolph. But if he loses, he loses everything and goes on that DAMN path towards drugs and alcohol. August 25th is make or break. And Wonder isn’t quite ready for that big “break” to come yet. He’ll do whatever it takes. He’ll win by any means necessary even if that means the death of him. It doesn’t even matter to him. That’s because it’s no longer about him. It’s about Scott Taylor and Amanda Sampson. Hurt Scott to help Amanda. That’s all there is to it. That is hatred.
