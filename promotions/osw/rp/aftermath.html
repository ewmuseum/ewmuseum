Date: Pretaped on May 11, 2002 It’s times like this that I wish I’d of stayed a boxer. Reno Davis observed the pile of twisted metal that was his 1994 Pontiac Grand Prix SE. Ivan Stanislav decided that my blowing up Reno’s car, it would make sense. Dumbass. ZaC hopped about and looked at the aftermath, touching the car randomly. Since 1994, Reno drove the car across Canada and the United States, but tonight, all of his history with the car had ended. Blown to pieces thanks to some stupid Russian. “Reno! Oh my god...Reno!” Aimz called as she ran up to Davis, staring at his car in shock. “Hey Aimz...” “Hey, how’s it...uh, never mind.” Aimz looked away for a moment, then continued, “What a jerk huh?” “Fuck...This really pisses me off. Why won’t he just attack me?” Reno shook his head, “How’s your bruises and stuff?” Aimz rubbed her arm and shrugged, “Okay I guess, I was lucky that I only had to fight a pussy tonight.” She said with a smile. Reno laughed and playfully shoved her before walking closer to the heap. The trunk of the car seemed to be in relatively decent shape.... at least compared to the rest of the car. With a steady kick, the trunk opened, allowing Reno to see that his skateboard and clothing remained untouched by the fire and destruction. “Ah, at least something good came of this.” Reno said as he picked up his skateboard and dropped it onto the ground. Reno rolled around a little as Aimz continued to look at the car. ZaC climbed up her leg and body and onto his shoulder. She scratched behind his ears and suddenly a look of concern struck. “You know...maybe you should start leaving ZaC at home.” Reno tried to Heelflip, but the statement distracted him and he just let the board drop as he stopped at looked at Aimz. “Why...Why would I do that?” Reno asked, a peculiar look on his face. “Well...Ivan attacked me, put me in the hospital. Now he’s blown up your car...He seems capable of anything. Besides, doesn’t Wang want to eat ZaC to begin with?” Aimz looked at the ground the whole time that she spoke. The Unwanted Angel got back on his skateboard and landed an Inward Heelflip. He got into a nose manual and Kickflipped out sloppily, he seemed very disturbed with everything “I don’t know... I’ve been bringing ZaC to the ring with me since my very first match in 1999. He’s never been seriously injured, and besides, who would I leave him with?” Aimz shook her head, “I’m sure you could find someone...ZaC has already been seriously hurt in OSW on your first Slam, remember? That was Krieg, this is Ivan. No, this isn’t just Ivan, this is Ivan Stanislav more seriously pissed off than he’s ever been before and that, Reno, is not something you can take chances with.” “Nah, ZaC’s tough,” ZaC leaped onto Reno’s shoulder and crawled through his hair, “Right buddy?” Reno said with a smile at ZaC, who chirped and jumped back onto the ground, “Aimz, you have to remember...I’ve been doing this for 3 years now. I know my way around.” “I guess you’re right...I’m just worried.” Aimz said as she kicked a stone away. “Pfft!” Reno said as he approached her and put his arm over her shoulder, “Come on, let’s go.”
