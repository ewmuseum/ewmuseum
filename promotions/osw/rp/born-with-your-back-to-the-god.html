OSW Old School Promos — #259

Born with your back to the God that picked you up by your puppy-scruff. Angry at who, me? You'd better back up...

2002-03-31

Amy 'Aimz' Campbell

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Guess who's got strep throat?! YAY! Time to go lay down and get my voice back.) Aimz had every single intention of confronting Jason about a few issues this evening. The only uncertainties were how she'd go about it... Breanne had offered to provide any backup necessary, but Amy immediately refused it, letting her friend know that she could handle her own...though it was painfully obvious she couldn't, or she wouldn't have gotten herself into this awkward situation to begin with. So what was going to be done about this? She had heard Jason was cheating on her, that much could be confirmed by various sources. Amy was, once again, being used because of the small amount of fame she's mustered for herself. That was obviously quite upsetting...but did it matter? She had plans to drop Jason...plans that were created the moment she saw Rent-A-Hero again. Ah...so where did good ol' Rentie fit into things? She was going to just instantly dump the man she's with now...that's really something for this girl to do! Amy honestly thought she loved PTC's resident hero, that basically says it all. The real question is, has she been dipping into Breanne's cocaine supply? After all, she loves Rent-A-Hero...the COMPLETE opposite to her. To be with him, she gives up getting drunk almost constantly, occasional dabbling into drugs with friends like Bre, any hope of sex and so much more...where's the gain? With Rent-A-Hero, everything became a challenge...that's what Amy loved about this. She had to work with him and teach him anything that should be done...things took time, time that could be used simply to savor his company. That was the greatest thing...Aimz didn't have to worry about sex, and life was so much simpler that way; Just sit, cuddle and enjoy the company. Who did she have for company at the moment? Nobody...Amy was just sitting in the lobby to Sammy Kain's tattoo studio. Warm wood walls, similar flooring and a fireplace lit as a centrepiece...it was quite a nice place, and gave her plenty of time to admire Kain's work, which was strewn all over the walls and just about any other bare place...including the skin of the shop's employees. She was dressed down in a pair of men's camo-pattern cargo pants, a studded leather belt and some sort of long-sleeved shirt that sported a red pentagram of some sort on the chest and down each arm. So what was she here for? When approached by Sammy, an old, rather intimidating, friend and tattoo artist, Amy quickly made her intentions known... ''Tattoo...that little Superman sign thingy...about this big,'' She made a triangle gesture with her thumb and index fingers that was about the size of a pop can top. ''...on my hip.'' ''Color?'' ''Can you do it black and red? That would kick ass!'' ''Niiiiice,'' Kain, not a man of many words, quickly complied, scratching a long red goatee that matched, nearly surpassed, the length of his hair. ''I'll set up, Rebecca and Sarah went off shopping...just c'mon back in a couple minutes.'' She smiled at Sammy as he walked into the back room she had walked into so many times...always going through a degree of pain when within but coming out with another piece of art added, be it a piercing or one of her various small tattoos. Then everything went away...Amy had heard the door of the shop creak open, and expected Rebecca or the apprentice piercer, Sarah, to walk in and greet her...but neither of them were there. When she looked up the figure that was approaching Aimz first saw the shins, then the waist, then the stomach then finally the head and face of none other than Rent-A-Hero. Wait a minute...RAH, in a tattoo shop?! Something was amiss and Amy intended to find out... ''Ummm...'' She kept a smile on her face while she spoke...after all, the sight of that man, no matter where, was always welcome. ''...Rentie? What're you doing here?'' ''Fair Amy!!!!!'' The moment he laid eyes upon her, curled up on a green armchair near the fire, the spandex-clad 'hero' sprinted over...only taking about three big steps before he reached Aimz, and scooped her up into his arms. She struggled a bit at his grip, not completely comfortable of her feet suddenly leaving the ground. ''...and hello to you too. But what're you doing here?'' ''That girl...where you live...that one with the unwholesome ring in her nose!!!'' Amy straightened out a few loose strands of Rent's hair that the gel seemed to have missed. ''Huh?'' ''She said you were coming here for one of those...those...ink things!!!'' ''You mean a tattoo?'' She smiled to him again, with kinder eyes than she had ever let anybody else see. ''You mean, a tattoo?'' ''Yes!!! I apologize, but I forbid this! If mortals were meant to have these designs and patterns on their skin...'' He gazed at Amy a moment, trying hard to resist the pouting, pleading glance she was casting up at him and eventually becoming successful... ''...well...by the power of Greyskull, you would be born with them!!!!!'' Obviously her not doing this would mean something to the hero, and Aimz was actually willing to comply with his requests...maybe she really did love him, after all! ''Alright, no tattoo today.'' She hadn't said that quite loud enough, that much was obviously when the six-foot-eight frame of Sammy Kain appeared in the small doorway at the back of the lobby. He looked rather surprised to find the girl he had practically raised in the arms of the spandex-toting Rent-A-Hero. ''Uhh, Amy...'' Kain cast RAH a momentary, rather cold glance. ''...who's this?'' ''Oh...this is Rent-A-Hero, the guy I was telling you about. And sorry for getting you all prepared...but no ink for me today...'' ''Umm...'' Sammy couldn't quite understand why she had changed her mind...he was way too confused already by the sight of Rent. ''Why?'' ''A request was made...I kinda chose to follow it.'' ''Okay...'' The big biker grunted back, a little annoyed by the silly grin that seemed almost constantly stretched across RAH's face. ''...but I'm gonna have to give you a little talking-to about men later.'' Kain discarded the rubber gloves he had put on and reclined in the computer chair behind the large wooden desk in the corner of the room, clicking away at some Solitaire came on the computer while Amy sat down with Rent-A-Hero, who was uncharacteristically silent. Much to Sammy's discontent, Aimz was nipping at Rent's lips playfully...but it annoyed the biker further that it appeared as if the ''spandex psycho'' didn't even notice what this pretty little girl was doing. Unknowingly taking that as if RAH didn't appreciate Amy, instead of realizing that Rentie was just a complete prude, Kain was growing quite angry...but that rage soon shifted when another man walked through the shop door. ''Jason!!!'' Amy immediately shouted as she saw Dexter coming through, her red hair going every which way in a flurry as she darted up from the couch. ''I...uhhh....I....what're you doing here? I thought you had to...work?'' ''I did...but my shift ended early...'' He was slowly inching towards Amy with an unbridled anger, the glare he was casting her proved that much. ''...Breanne told me I'd find you down here.'' Jason continued toward Aimz, but stopped dead in his tracks when Sammy Kain stood up, rather suggestively, from his seat. ''Bre told you I'd be here?'' Finally she realized it, Breanne knew Amy wouldn't get around to breaking up with Jason herself, so, being the best friend she is, Little Miss Lilievre decided to push things along for her. ''Yes, yes she did. Obviously she wanted me to...to see...'' His face flushed with anger, Dexter gestured toward Amy, who was once again near Rent-A-Hero. ''...THIS!'' Seeing Jason's anger building, Sammy stood up again, soon followed by Rent-A-Hero. ''See here, this is no way to treat a lady!!!'' RAH was about to get into Dexter's face, but he was pushed back my Kain... ''Let me handle the boy...'' The big man actually SMILED at Rentie and then turned back to Jason, who wasn't looking quite so brave anymore. ''See, kid...in here we don't like people raising their voices. So I'm gonna have to ask you to leave, otherwise I might be forced to quiet you down.'' Sammy got a little closer to Jay, making the young man practically look like a midget compared to his size and bulk. Dexter quickly turned on his heels and left, slamming the door without saying a word...obviously he had attempted to scrape a bit of his dignity from the ceiling before he went. ''I don't think poor little Jay likes it when the tables are turned.'' Amy had smiled, a hint of her mischievous nature showing through in it when she sat back down next to Rent-A-Hero, who was still nervous and confused even by her presence. ''Neither do I,'' Since Sammy was up, he headed toward the door. ''But hey, can you and the nutcase watch the shop for me? I'm just gonna go get some coffee...if that jackass comes back, there's a nice, sharp switchblade in the top drawer of the desk.'' ''Yeah, no problem.'' ''Good stuff...see ya in a minute!'' Was all he said before stepped out the same door that was slammed by Dexter mere moments before. So Amy and Rent-A-Hero just sat there, feet warmed by the fire. They were silent for so long that Aimz finally couldn't stand it, and she spoke up to him... ''What's wrong?'' She made sure to pout, knowing he couldn't refuse to answer. ''And don't say 'Nothing'...'' ''That Drew Hostyle...he's almost twice your weight!'' ''So what?'' Amy never really understood why everyone was so concerned about size in a wrestling match, but that was all part of her usual blind confidence. ''So...'' RAH continued, standing up to place his hands on his hips in that usual uber-heroic fashion. ''...he might hurt you...badly!!'' ''Ummm...Rentie...this is Drew Hostyle we're talking about.'' She chuckled, grabbing Rent's hand to guide him back to the seat. ''...he wears just about as much spandex as you do, only in a much more revealing manner. Don't worry...if he seems to be getting any sort of advantage, I'll just snap his thong.'' ''But...he's so much bigger than you!'' ''Size means jack if you can keep to the ropes.'' ''Who's Jack?'' Amy rubbed her temples for a moment, only now remembering how stupidly innocent Rent-A-Hero had always been. ''Nevermind...'' She managed a weak smile. ''...but trust me, I can take him out.'' ''Well, you just rest up for now...you've been limping around an awful lot since that Meanstreak creature hurt you. When you get Hostyle, give him the spankering his mommy never could!!!'' ''Ummm...right, then.'' Nobody but Sammy entered, and nobody left for a long while after that. They sat, they spoke and Amy pondered any sort of news manoeuvres she could level Andrew Copeland with.
