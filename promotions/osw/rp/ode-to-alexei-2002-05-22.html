Ode to Alexei

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-22-02 Imagine a life in which you were completely alone. A life in which all the people that you came in contact with were expendable. In which the people that you knew, you only knew because you wanted to take advantage of them, with the knowledge that they too…wanted to exploit and take advantage of you. To live a life where you were in complete control of your actions, and yet, controlled by an invisible hand at the same time, like a puppet. The people that you know, fear you, and the people that you do not know, wish you to be dead. Every time you smile, someone shivers, and every time you frown, someone’s life ends. To sign the dotted line on the lives of countless people, and to know that with every dotted ‘i’ that you make, a gunshot will be fired. Such is the life of Alexei Ruslan. When he walks down a hallway, the others that pass him look a bit lower, regardless of their stature. Whenever he scratches the back of his neck, terror is felt throughout those nearby. His voice may be feeble, but his meaning is all powerful. To some, work is nothing more than a way of life, to him, it is a favorite past time. The sacrifices that he has made are few that others are capable of. To not be related to or associated with virtually any other human being, and to know that when he dies, he will be forgotten, is not easy to be taken. Years of work has turned him into a killing machine of another sort. A silent machine. One that cannot be detected or defended from, and whose effects are sorely felt. Many, MANY people, those of whom he has not even noticed, have been effected by his mere whim of paranoia. A paranoia that keeps him alert at all times….a paranoia that keeps him, in a way, in complete fear totally. At night, he does not sleep with both eyes closed, but with a gun close to his side and an ear wide open. Such is the life of Alexei Ruslan. While once capable of a multitude of emotions, many of them are now dead. Love, compassion, and hope has given way to duty, prejudice, and hatred. To make a sound is forbidden, but to revel in the silence is encouraged. Surviving the hand of fate and climbing through the ranks in a most dangerous fashion, he has become one of the most powerful men in the Russian Federation. With his great power, comes the ability to destroy virtually anything within it’s sphere..a power that few men have ever had. To live to kill for years on end can turn a mind towards something horrifying. To know that you are a reaper of sorts, to know that you, at any moment as well, could die, is maddening. To find solace in your office, only to know that everyone can hear you. To know that right now, someone is watching you, and yet, you are watching them. Such is the life of Alexei Ruslan. One man has only won his trust. One man has only earned his respect. An ally, a communist…. …..a friend. A friend unlike any other. One that is not out to get him, but rather, to help him. Ivan Sergeiovich Stanislav…the Mighty Russian Bear….one of the most watched men in Russia…but everybody. After the past situations, to hear that he may have to eliminate his friend was something he could not tolerate. Something came in front of the work…something…..he himself could not explain. Was it…compassion? Love for a friend? And thus, he is stuck in a quandary as well. He must watch his comrade and do his job..and watch his comrade and live his friendship. The screaming at night during the rain storms…the mood swings….the quiet hours in his office has worried Ruslan, for Ivan has never reacted like this before. He squeaked by the last time with Tempest leaving and did not need to take any action…but perhaps now if the attitude continues, Ruslan may need to. To do his duty, or to help a friend? That is the question that he cannot find an answer for. He lives surrounded by Comrades…and yet he is completely alone. He sits in his office with the world at his fingertips, and yet at the same time it’s in someone else’s hand…. Such…is the life……of Alexei…..Ruslan.
