Week in Review...

15 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/15/02 We see Tom Walczak making his way over to his television set. He turns the television on, and he sees that some kind of wrestling recap show is on. They are talking about OSW, so Walczak is intrigued. They come to an interview with Jane Reagan and Walczak raises the volume. “Mrs. Reagan, do you have any comments on Tom Walczak’s recent behavior. This is his second bar incident in as many months?” “Well, all that I have to say is that if he does not clean up his act, that he will have to take a look at where he wants to go in the future.” The new personnel continue talking to Reagan, but Walczak pays no attention. He slams his fist on the table, and goes into a fit. “Who does that bitch think that she is? Does she think that she can tell me what to do? If there is one person in the entirety of OSW that I can’t stand more than Doug Troy, it would have to be that bitch Reagan.” Walczak once again turns his attention to the program. He hears them talking about Poland, and wonders if it has anything to do with the vandalization that had taken place earlier this week. “We reported earlier this week that the Baranski Palace in Poland had been vandalized, and although all fingers pointed to Doug Troy no charges could be made. However, we have an old lady who said that she saw Doug destroy the historic building. Let’s show the tape.” “Yes, I saw a man with a black Sobe t-shirt do the terrible act, and there was a second man with him, but I could not identify him.” “That is all the news that we have this week, and until next time good night.” Walczak turns the television set off, and shakes his head in disbelief. “That good for nothing bastard. He has taken this to another level. I could see that he was happy with the act, and knew that he had some part in it, but that he was actually the man to do it. That is just not right. He will get his this week, and I will make sure of it, and than at Ascension, well I am going to bring my all he can be assured of that.” Walczak gets off of the couch and heads out of his home, as the scene fades to black…
