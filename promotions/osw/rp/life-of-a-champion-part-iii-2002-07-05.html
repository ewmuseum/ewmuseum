Life of a Champion: Part III

5 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-5-02 Tom Walczak is seen sleeping on his sofa, and it seems that he is calm, and not very distracted. Then the doorbell rings. Walczak’s look of serenity changes to a look of agitation. He goes to answer the door, and there he is, Mr. Dick Nibbler. “What the hell do you want?” “Well, we are filming you are we not.” “Yeah, but don’t you have enough footage?” “Normally for a day it is enough, but we only have one day to film you, so in this case it is not enough.” “Ok, what do you need?” “Well, first we need to look at your ordinary life, and then after we film that I would like to get an on camera interview with yourself.” “Ok, and then you will leave me the fuck alone.” “Yes.” Walczak goes to sit on his couch as the camera crew sets up. He goes to the fridge and grabs a couple of beers, and then makes his way back to the couch turning the television on. “You want a beer?” “No, I prefer to stay sober while filming these things.” “Ok, that’s fine with me.” The camera crew finishes setup, and Mr. Nibbler once again does his opening for the show. “We are back in the home of OSW World Champion Tom Walczak, and we will see what it is that Champions like him do in their off time.” They observe Walczak, but he doesn’t do anything out of the ordinary. He just sits there watching television. “So, this is what you do all day?” “Most of the time, I take a nap occasionally, and who knows sometimes I even go out for a night on the town.” “Well, could you show us around your house?” “Fuck No, I don’t wanna get of my ass to show you asses around, Scurwy Siny” The reporter looks bewildered but nonetheless doesn’t talk back. “You know what let us just go to the interview.” The scene fades out as they get ready for the interview… Life of a Champion: Part IV Location: Walczak’s living room We see the camera crew setting up for the interview, and Walczak even seems excited. So much so that he has changed into a nice polo shirt and a pair of khakis something never before seen from the champion. “We are here with Tom Walczak, and we will get a deeper look into the mind of this wondrous man. Tom, were you surprised when you won the belt?” “No.” “You were the underdog heading into the match, how could you not be surprised?” “I was the underdog in your mind, and just about everyone else’s, but not in mine. I knew I was going to win.” “Well, do you feel the same way about Rhett Locke?” “I feel better.” “Better?” “Yeah, what has he accomplished, at least Doug Troy was World Champion, Locke is just another punk.” “He was International Champion, and won Ascension, and if I remember correctly he beat you in your previous encounter.” “The past means nothing all that counts is now, and now is when I will tear him a new asshole.” “Ok, well I think we have all that we need.” “Do you wanna know what the best part of being Champ is?” “Sure.” “Respect.” “Respect?” “Yeah, before this no one cared about what I did. They thought of me as the Polish wrestler, or Stanislav’s crony, but now they all watch my next move. What will I do? Also, I get service whenever I want, and no one talks back to me. That is what Respect is.” The reporter looks on in amazement as the scene fades to black…
