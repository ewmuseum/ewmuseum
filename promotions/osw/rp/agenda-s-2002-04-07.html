Date: 4-7-02 Its not really that Alexei Ruslan wanted to, but he appeared to be stuck between a rock and a hard place for the first time. Generally, when something like this happens to the small Russian Communist, he blows the rock up and decides where to go from there…. ..but this rock..is Ivan Stanislav. Alexei has always had the luxury of being devoted to two completely different things, that were greatly similar: Russia, and Ivan Stanislav. Both have paid him back from his duties with great rewards, as well as a strong friendship between himself and The Russian Bear, who could be one of the few people in the world that the Director of the FSB truly trusts. After all, Alexei has seen things in his life that would horrify most people, particularly perpetrated by his home country against his own countrymen, he knows what longevity is, and that does not come easily within the Russian Federation. “The Tempest Question” as he reverently referred to it, was something that he did not expect. Ivan Stanislav becoming emotionally attached to an attractive, ambitious, independent woman? Unheard of. But lo and behold, Ivan Stanislav appears to be doing just that. And while Alexei Ruslan is quite happy that his friend is finally beginning to live a little, there are others within the upper echelon that do not share his enthusiasm. And those people are powerful people indeed. Leonid Tomifei, supreme head of the project at which they are a part of, is one of these people in particular. While Tomifei may not have direct authority over Ruslan, he could talk to those that do, and make sure that Ruslan does not have a job for a long time, and Stanislav as well. Ruslan walked down the hallway backstage of Slam, shortly after the show had gone off the air with words echoing in his mind… ‘Do not fail again Ruslan! I want rid of her. Or else I will find someone that can get the job done themselves! Ivan Stanislav is a soldier, and nothing more! He can be replaced, and so can you.’ Those were the words of Leonid Tomifei, a stern warning to say the least and something that Ruslan would much rather not have to deal with. But perhaps if he could rationalize and justify the reasoning behind having Tempest so close to Ivan, it would fly for a while, if not for good. Ruslan pushed open Ivan Stanislav’s locker room door to see The Russian Bear standing in front of a mirror with his hands on his hips. Stanislav narrowed his eyes as he stared at his massive head and turned to Alexei. “What is the word, Comrade?” Questioned Ivan as he walked to a locker and pulled out a large duffle bag. “Nothing, Praporschik,” replied Alexei, “what of Tempest?” “She will be fine. I have Wang attending to her now, she suffered some trauma, but nothing that some medicine and rest cannot fix.” Ivan growled as he thought about the happenings of that night, “I will be sure that retribution is in order.” Alexei nodded and walked deeper into the room, closer to Ivan. He turned his back to Alexei and began to take down a large Soviet Flag that hung along the wall. Alexei thought for a few moments and then spoke again, “It appears that you two are becoming very, close to one another, eh?” Ivan shook his head and tossed his reply over his shoulder, “I suppose you could say that. We may have more in common than Ivan at once thought.” He turned with the flag and began to fold it carefully, “Is there problem with that, my friend?” Alexei put his hands in his pockets, “Oh no, Comrade, there is nothing wrong with that….” He paused for a long time while Ivan put the flag in the bag, “I just want to be sure that you are sure as to what you are doing.” Ivan paused and fixed a twisted suspender strap, “I always know what I am doing, you know that Alexei.” “Do you think that she can be trusted?” Ivan chuckled and shook his head, “Of course I believe she can be trusted. Yes, I was skeptical at first, but I believe she has proven that she is trustworthy.” He stopped and fixed the waist of his pants, “She came to the Motherland to make sure that I was alright, and she has fought alongside me….” He paused and thought for a moment while Alexei kicked some dust off of his shoe. “I was thinking, Praporschik,” continued Ruslan, unsure exactly how to word the next statement, “as to how we should use and take advantage of this situation, and Tempest….” Stanislav, who had now turned his back on Alexei, paused and tilted his head to the side, “I do not like the tone of your voice, Alex.” He turned around and stood up straight, looking down at his friend, “What do you mean by, ‘use and take advantage of?’” Under the gaze of Ivan Stanislav, Alexei Ruslan shifted his weight and shrugged, “I was merely speaking upon the opportunity that had presented itself, that is all.” Alexei paused, realizing that the response he had given to Stanislav was not satisfactory, “If she comes closer into the fold of Red Army, she will be harder to sway in any other way. Also, she is female, which is an area that we have lacked in influence, and she can help us in such regard!” Ivan exhaled deeply and ground his teeth together, “So you wish to bring her deeper within Red Army? I do not see much problem with that, currently.” Ivan thought for a moment, “But that is not exactly, ‘using’ her, eh?” Alexei fixed his tie under his trench coat, “You know that relations between you two have increased, however, there are many contentious issues that could be, unearthed, if the need be, and that could be very damaging….” Ivan rubbed the back of his neck uncomfortably and nodded, “Da…da.” “And therefore, I had thought that if we not only deepen our ties with her, but also cut ties off with rest of federation to a point, leaving us will not be option.” Alexei folded his hands together and shrugged. Ivan pointed at Ruslan, “So, you are telling me that you wish to turn federation against Tempest, so that if anything happens between us she will not be able to leave?” “That is the long and short of it, Comrade.” Ivan rubbed his chin for a moment, “I do not like that, Alexei. I do not like that at all. We may have fouled up something in past, but I wish to make amends now.” Ivan paused and thought again, “She has had much pain in her life, and she trusts us, we should not do something like this and be so manipulative.” Alexei muttered under his breath, “…..you would not have said that a month ago…” Stanislav had turned to head back to the wall and undo the flag of the Russian Federation, but by the remarks made by Ruslan, he wheeled around quickly and roared loudly, “DO NOT TALK OF ME LIKE THAT, RUSLAN!!” Alexei yelped and took a step back, shocked and surprised by the sudden anger showed by his nearest and dearest friend. “Praporschik, I…” Ivan took three steps towards Ruslan, seemingly growing in size at his sudden anger, as if he was about to burst through the ceiling, “What sort of conclusion are you coming to, Ruslan?” Alexei’s back hit the wall and he yelped suddenly, “Nothing, Praporschik! Calm yourself!!” Ivan growled and seemed to shrink to his normal, still massive, size. He lost the angry look on his face, “Good….good Alexei.” “Comrade,” whispered Alexei, “you have never treated me in such a way before.” Ivan took a step back, “I am sorry, Alexei, I did not know what came over me.” Ivan thought for a moment, very confused, “I felt as if I was being punched…I felt a great…..anger inside of me when you said that…I do not understand it…” Ivan shook his head and rubbed his forehead. Alexei cleared his throat, “It is alright, Comrade, I was out of line.” Alexei paused and licked his lips for a moment, “However, you may wish to consider it. I mean, she has been great asset already,” he looked at Ivan and paced back and forth, “she has increased relations with Aimz and Timo Bolamba, perhaps if only counteracting the stigma that we have of being some sort of….communist barbarians. And the people like her, and therefore in a way, they like you.” Ivan nodded and took a step back, “Yes, perhaps you are correct.” He thought for a long while and looked at Alexei, “I think she may have to be slightly more integrated into Red Army, but not ‘used’ as you have put it.” “We just have to recognize our objectives, Praporschik, that is all.” Alexei warned. ~~~ The scene shifted to another locker room where Tempest sat with her arms folded and a pout on her face. Loyal Communist Party Member Wang bustled in with a towel wrapped around something. 'Hewe you awe Tempest. This will make you feew better.' he said and opened the towel to reveal ice. He bound it up in a long coil and placed it around her neck and said, 'Thewe you awe.' He grinned, satisfied with himself as Tempest nodded. Then she looked up and said, 'Wang, do you think they like me?' Wang paused and exhaled, 'Why..whyeva do you ask that??' She shifted a little and said, 'The other members of the Red Army I mean. I know Ivan likes me. It just seems like... I dunno, maybe they don't trust me.' Wang sat across from her on another bench and looked at her, 'I do not believe that they do not twust you,' he thought for a moment, 'but I think they awe hesitant to accept you, that is all...you awe...diffwent, yes?' She nodded and said, 'Yeah. And I know that. I guess... I just don't wanna be someone they don't like. I mean, I hear commentators saying I ruined Ivan or whatever...' she huffed and shook her head, rolling her eyes, 'But that's the sort of thing commentators do. His friends... I guess I just don't wanna be the enemy in their eyes.' Wang chuckled and grabbed at his stomach, 'Oh ho ho! If you were enemy, you would be broken into many little, itty bitty pieces, Tempest!' He leaned closer to Tempest and smiled warmly, always having a slight affinity to a member of his home country, 'I can assure, you, Ivan and Red Awmy have best intentions when dealing with you. It takes time, that is all.' He smiled and gave her a wink. She smiled a little and said, 'Maybe so. Maybe I should find a way to work with Krieg and Walczak. I mean if they saw I can do good things first hand, it might help.' Wang nodded, 'That could be in order, just to let them know that you are twue to your contwibutions.' He thought for another moment, 'It may not be easy though, they are vewy involved in what they do.' She shrugged and said, 'So am I. It's not easy to think about fitting into a group so much. I mean I have lotsa friends, but I generally let the doubters go. But I don't feel like I can do that this time, for Ivan's sake.' He grinned and nodded, 'Ivan does not doubt you, Tempest. I am sure of this. Othawise he would have..abandoned...you...' He paused and stood up, 'You obviously are something special, Tempest, thewe awe few people that get this close to him.' She smiled and was silent a moment, lost in thought. 'I'm happy about that. I like Ivan a lot. I know people think it's odd, but... well so am I, so they should know better.' She giggled and added, 'Ivan's been great to me, so why should I care about the people who hate him for reasons that have nothing to do with me?' Wang clapped, very happy with her response, 'That is cowwect! Ivan would be gwad to hear that fwom you, Tempest! It is far too often that people jump to concwusions about Ivan and Wed Awmy!!' Tempest chuckled and said, 'I'll be sure and let him know. I kinda think he does, but I will anyway. Let's see if they're ready to go yet.' She hopped up and started toward the door. ~~~ Stanislav nodded, “Ivan understands our objectives. Perhaps yes, we will bring her deeper into the fold just to ensure stability.” Ruslan nodded, “I did not mean ‘use’ her in conventional sense, Ivan, but you must understand that she has to be….learned of the Red Army’s ways.” Alexei smiled, somewhat wickedly, “Besides, what she does not know, cannot hurt her….” “HEY!!” The door flew open as Tempest bounded into the room and jumped up to Ivan and wrapped her arms around his neck, literally hanging from him, “SURPRISE!” Stanislav chuckled, somewhat nervously, and stared at the floor with a blank look, as the camera faded out…
