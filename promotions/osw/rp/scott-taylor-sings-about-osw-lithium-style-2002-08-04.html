Scott Taylor Sings About OSW, Lithium Style.

4 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/4/02 The last time we saw Scott Taylor and his personal bodyguard, Randolph, the two were in a music shop known as 'Stan's Music Center.' Scott purchased a rather nice looking accoustic guitar, was mistaken for a PCW wrestler by the shop owner, and then Randolph nearly ran over an old lady. All in all it was a semi-bad day. I mean, getting mistaken for being in PCW?! Oh...there was that old lady too. As the scene opens we find ourselves in a parking lot, the view focuses in on the front of a Black 2002 Chevrolet Blazer, the familiar vehicle of Scott Taylor. The view pans around to the side of Blazer showing the silver racing stripe running down the side, and stopping behind the vehicle. The back hatch is up and the trunk is down, Scott is seen reaches into the back of the Blazer, he produces a large bag which houses the newly accquired guitar. Scott turns to Randolph who is standing about a yard away from him. 'Randolph, go ahead and unlock the door. I'll meet you there to finish setting up.' orders the employer of the overweight bodyguard. Randolph merely nods his head and turns to leave. Scott shuts the trunk of the Blazer and then pulls the hatch down, a clicking sound informs him that it has instantly locked upon closing. Scott slings the large bag over his shoulder and begins to walk away from the vehicle. As he reaches the stairs that lead up to the small hotel complex he's staying in, his left hand slips into the pocket on the black jeans he is wearing and produces a folded piece of paper. Written on the paper are the song lyrics that he was writing earlier. He slides the lyrics back into his pocket and begins to jog up the steps towards his room, after circling two flights of winding stairs he fianlly arrives on the top floor of the hotel, an immediate look around reveals about six rooms and a wonderful view from a balcony. However, before he can enjoy this to it's fullest extent Randolph appears again, he points towards an open door about ten feet away. 'There's your room. Everything should be set up if you're ready.' 'Alright, let's go ahead and get this over with.' Scott says with a small sigh. The two of them walk away from the balcony and begin the walk towards the room. Scott enters first and has a look around. Nice sized room, about thirty feet from wall to wall, two double beds, a 19 inch television, and one of those neat little refridarators that you have to open with a key. The walls are painted a light shade of brown, a sort of a tan color, and the carpet is brown. It's a nice contrast. Scott walks over to the double bed farthest from the door and sits the large down on it. He opens it up and produces the accoustic guitar. As Randolph closes the door Scott sits down on the bed and begins to strum a few chords on the guitar. Low and behold, the man obsessed with 'stroke' can actually play a guitar. He plays a few more notes and then looks up at Randolph who is standing right over him by this point. 'It seems to be working in order. I got a nice sound out of it. Now, are you ready Randolph?' Randolph looks down at Scott with a look of confusion spread over his face. He blinks and then takes the guitar from Scott's hands. 'If you can play this thing, then why do I have to?' Scott looks back up to Randolph and shakes his head. He points to his pocket and then reaches into it, producing the paper with the lyrics on it again. He points to the paper and nods his head, as if he has just proved a point. 'Because I have to do the singing. You play the song while I sing. Come on, I already gave you the sheet music, just play alright? This won't take so long, or be that hard for that matter. Now, start playing!' Randolph backs away from Scott, stopping when he bumps into the other bed. He has a seat and situates the guitar on his lap. It takes him a moment until he can hold it right, once that part is done he moves his fingers over the strings, nothing but noise is produced. Scott grinds his teeth at the horrible song and then looks at Randolph. 'What was that?! I'd expect to hear a country song on Malice that sounded better than that! Come on, play the music that I gave you!' Randolph shakes his head at the mistake, nods at Scott and then starts to play again. A few off chords, but he soon finds the accoustic strumming to be not so hard, and the song starts to sound correct. Scott stands from the bed, paper in hand, and starts to sing. 'I'm so happy. Because yesterday I put that bitch in the can. He's still in my head, damn that teeny bopper is ugly, but that's ok. Cause Randolph is too, and we've broken each other's manager. By Any Means Necessary is all I care, that match will be everything for all that I care. And I'm not scared, put you through a table, hit you with a chair, In a daze because I'll have Amanda! Yeah! (X a bunch of times.) I can't wait, but that's ok. I lost the tag team match, and I'm not sad. And just maybe I'm to blame for all I've done in OSW, and I'm not sure. I'm so excited, I can't wait to wrestle you there! And I don't care, I'm so angry, but that's ok. My intentions are good. Yeah! (X a bunch of times, again.) At this point Scott begins to sing the chorus as Randolph starts to jam on the guitar, falling onto his knees circa 1985 Hair Metal Bands. I like it! I'm not going to crack But I'm sure your back will. I'm not going to crack And I'm sure OSW loves you. I'm not gong to crack And I'm sure OSW will miss you I'm not going to crack But I will kill you At By Any Means Necessary! (Two Times) I'm so happy. Because yesterday I put that bitch in the can. He's still in my head, damn that teeny bopper is ugly, but that's ok. Cause Randolph is too, and we've broken each other's manager. By Any Means Necessary is all I care, that match will be everything for all that I care. And I'm not scared, put you through a table, hit you with a chair, In a daze because I'll have Amanda! Yeah! (X a bunch of times, once more.) I like it! I'm not going to crack But I'm sure your back will. I'm not going to crack And I'm sure OSW loves you. I'm not gong to crack And I'm sure OSW will miss you I'm not going to crack But I will kill you At By Any Means Necessary! (Two Times)' As the song comes to an end, Scott looks at Randolph who is still jamming out on the guitar, Scott walks over to him and looks down. Randolph is still jamming, until Scott kicks him in the shin. He jumps up and in the process drops the guitar. Scott leans down to pick up the guitar. 'That right there was a number one hit that will never happen.' 'True Mr Taylor, but that book said that venting your frustration through art is the best thing to do.' 'Really? That's where you got that from?' Randolph nods as the scene begins to fade to black. Fin~ (The song was 'Lithium' by Nirvana)
