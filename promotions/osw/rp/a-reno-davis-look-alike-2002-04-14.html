A Reno Davis Look Alike

14 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/14/02 We zoom in and we are in a hair salon. It is not elegant, and there is nothing special about it, but there is a whole bunch of kids waiting in line. There is literally a line out the door of kids waiting for some kind of hair treatment. Down the street we see Walczak come into view. He looks at the long line of kids, and is somewhat dumbfounded. Walczak is supposed to meet a lady for lunch at this salon. Walczak had met her as he was leaving the Rupp Arena, and they had decided to go out on a lunch date. Walczak went into the salon, and asked the receptionist where she was. “Ohh, Elaine, well she is in the back. You see all these kids, they are waiting to get their hair done so it is just like one of those crazy Wrestlers.” Walczak smiles at the receptionist, and heads to the back. Walczak would be lying to say that he is not intrigued to find out who this superstar is that all these children are emulating. Realizing that he has no hair he rules him self out from the start, and then he gets to thinking. Could it possibly be Ivan Stanislav? If he was a child, than no doubt would the Russian bear would be the superstar whom he would emulate. Or, could it possibly be Civil Disobedience, they are adored by many of today’s children. Of course Walczak would never be seen in a trend started by either Trevor Hawke or Seth Masters. Of course today’s children have very different minds than that of Walczak’s. Than what Walczak sees shocks him, and his mouth drops to the floor. A kid walks out of the room, and has his long hair died a bright red much like the color Reno Davis has, but what is even more surprising is the fact that his eyes are the same color that Reno Davis has. Walczak grabs the kid and starts to talk to him. “Hey, what are you doing? Get the hell off of me you dumb Pole.” “How did you know I was Polish? You must be related to Reno Davis.” “I wish I was. He is like a God to me, and he kicked that dumbass German’s ass, and this week he is gonna kick yours.” “Let’s not get verbally abusive here you little shit. If you are not related than how do you look so much like him?” “Well, that lady in there just died my hair, and I think that she pretty good job.” “Yeah, but the eyes.” “That’s nothing, they are just contact lenses that give the effect of Blue Eyes.” “So you wear glasses?” “Hell no!” “You know that wearing contacts without a prescription can severely damage your eyes?” “Does it look like I give a damn? As long as I look like the one the only, Reno Davis. Now leave me alone you piece of Polish Shit.” Walczak grabs for the kid, but grabs nothing but air as the kid scurries away. Walczak turns back around and heads off to look for Elaine, and as he does so she comes out of the room. “Hey Tom how are you doing? Listen, I am going to have to cancel our date, you see all of these kids want their hair died, and I am the only one that can do it. They really love this Reno Davis guy.” “Yeah well I don’t, and to tell you the truth anyone that helps kids look like that no good sun of a bitch Reno Davis isn’t worthy of my presence anyway.” Walczak leaves in a flurry, and Elaine just stands there staring as the scene fades out…
