Legna Austere-Self-Destruct

24 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-24-02 The smoke passes from my nose in to the room and then runs over my body as I release the drug. The smell of opium is heavy in the enclosed confines, but the smoke calms the pain throbbing in the side of my face. You can see it, can't you? The bruises covering me, my welcome home gift among many others. I can't feel them anymore though, the pain is numbed from what is running through her veins. I am laid up against the wall behind the side of my bed, my head lolled to one side. My eyes too far gone to show any emotion, not even a slight hint. You can see more can't you? The needle hanging out of my out stretched and limp arm. My gift in another form, my savior filling me up with the substance I craved. The mans head who keeps me intact is laid atop my thigh. He was far gone, breathing in the smoke that pollutes his lungs in the most happy of manners. He was fine, I was fine..We were all fine. Because I see you, looking to me and thinking 'what a whore' but what would you have me do? Get better? Be stronger? What right do you have to tell me who I should be? Take it all away..Take it all away..NO! Sights of horror, pain is struck. The life is cast and interrupted. Who could have built a shield to deflect it? This world is crazy sin reflected. This horror is a house we roam with meager dreams while our family moans. This world is a vacant slate, this world is obscene. I am broken and battered by what I have seen. There is no reason for this murderous wish of indecision. Who might decide to play the judge in the lives of man? My tolerance is slipping, my mind's will is slipping, my cognizance dripping like blood, from a cut never closing. This world is imposing its sickness and I would see it destroyed. This world has overstepped its own bounds. My hands tied as alibis in the criminal court of repent. I want more than this for the blood I have spent. Two thousand wet angers filled with suffering-mine. I want this cancerous deviation to be extinct in my time. I want to strangle the cruelty of the world 'til it begs. Then I'd blind its eyes and I'd break its legs. I'd feed it its belly, bloated full with despise. I'd feed it its words 'til it burst from the lies. Gangreneous fingers, dripping pus as they type on into the nightmare which might also be called night. My words are fool visions, draped in cowls of deceit. No NOTHING IS RIGHTEOUS!!, and for this do I bleed? So, my heart is forsaken as an offering lacking to feed the hungry lips of the world, cruel and stalking. I will not accept this! Bitter world, angels live! I demand, send them saviours with better wisdom to give. I demand, end this sickness, better yet end yourself. For you, Life, are a place, where only evil now dwells. And I have no more left to give of myself. I'm sitting in my room.. With a needle in my arm.. Waiting for the tomb of some old dying man..
