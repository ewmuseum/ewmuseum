Taking anger out on the nearest available target

21 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/21/2002 The slow-paced intro beats of The Crystal Method’s ‘Trip Like I Do’ fade up amidst a black background to start the latest Doug Troy promo. The black slowly transitions to the large open room of MTV Studios, a room usually reserved for employee break that has been converted into a training room of sorts. Various audio/visual equipment is stored along the walls off the room while training equipment litters the floor. The centerpiece of this room is a large mat, like those seen in an amateur wrestling competition. At the present, the mat is occupied by Doug Troy and Richard Blackthorne. Doug is wearing a wife beater and khaki shorts while Richard is dressed in the typical kickboxer garb. Both of them are wearing boxing gloves and sparring helmets. It seems that they’ve just started the sparring match, as both of them have yet to break a sweat. They circle around each other for a moment, neither taking a punch. The music continues it’s steady backbeat as they circle. A pause… And Richard throws the first punch. Doug easily dodges and answers with a punch of his own. Richard dodges and counters with a swift punch to Doug’s midsection. Doug’s arm blocks it, but Richard continues the attack with several more body blows. Doug blocks them all and throws a punch at Richard’s face in return. It connects and Richard falls back. They start circling each other once again, only the camera does a closeup on Doug’s face. Meanstreak gives Doug At Death’s Door on the railing Doug’s eyes widen as the fighting continues. The camera stays on Doug’s face as some audio distortion starts to fade up under the scene. The sound of the punches get more and more muted, with Doug’s face being the only indication of what’s happening. Ivan Stanislav gives Doug the Red Scare Doug’s eyes widen again and a sneer starts to develop. Meanstreak ADD’s a Dr. Pepper machine through the PCW stage and onto Doug, Reaper and Ryan Knox. Doug grimaces. Ivan Stanislav snaps Doug’s leg in an arena chair. Doug’s sneer gets larger and his actions get much more forceful. Meanstreak and Ivan battle on top of the Kremlin building Doug suddenly roars with anger, but the roar is cut off by a stiff punch to Doug’s face. Doug reels back, surprised, as the camera pans outward. The look of surprise turns into a look of shock as Doug looks at Richard, who is seething with anger. A small stream of blood runs from Richard’s nose past the silent snarl that his mouth has curled into. The music, which has slowed to a quiet rumble in the background, chasing the audio distortion away. With a yell, Richard throws a kick at Doug’s face. Doug, not expecting this, takes it whirls around. He quickly recovers and roars with anger as he runs at Richard. The music quickly goes into high gear, the beats thumping in a faster and faster tempo. Richard tries to intercept Doug with a swift punch, but Doug dodges and aims a kick at Richard’s midsection. Richard slides out of the way and throws a kick at Doug’s face, but Doug gets his forearm in the way just in time to block it. With Richard’s leg still in the air, Doug heaves forward and attempts to throw Richard off-balance. Richard flips backward to compensate, but misses Doug with his other leg. While Richard recovers, Doug goes on the offense with a swift flurry of punches and kicks. The attack drive Richard backward as he dodges each one, off the mat and close to a pile of A/V equipment. With a yell, Doug throws a hard kick at Richard’s midsection. Richard sees it coming, though, and catches the leg right as it makes contact. With Doug’s leg trapped, he swings Doug around and throws him right into the equipment. Video playback machines and speakers clatter to the ground around Doug as he recovers from the flight. With a groan, he sees Richard lying in wait with a kung-fu stance. Doug groans as he starts to get up… and surprises Richard by throwing a VCR at him. Richard blocks it, but it gives Doug enough time to get to his feet. More punches are thrown, this time with Richard taking the offensive. Swift punches and kicks drive Doug closer and closer to some mounted cameras. He barely blocks each attack as it flies at him. As Richard recovers from a side kick, Doug throws a quick punch that catches Richard off-guard. Richard winds back for a second, then whirls around with a hard roundhouse kick that would’ve decapitated Doug… if Doug hadn’t ducked out of the way just in time. The camera right behind Doug isn’t so lucky, however. It gets knocked right off its perch atop the tripod. The lens of the camera shatters as it hits the ground. Doug grabs the tripod that it used to be mounted on and prepares to strike Richard with it right as Pgod runs into the room with the Angry Panda in tow. Pgod grabs the tripod out of Doug’s hands while the Panda restrains Richard from attacking again. “Hey hey HEY! STOP IT!” Pgod yells at Doug. “Settle down, right now!” Doug turns to Pgod, a bonfire burning in his eyes. Meanstreak pins Stanislav at last week’s Slam “CALM DOWN!” Ivan and the rest of the Red Army beat Doug and Trashcan Man down “DOUG!” Suddenly, the fire in Doug’s eyes is snuffed out. The music stops. He looks around the room, confused, as Pgod gets in his face. “Just what in the F*CK do you think you’re doing?!” Pgod shakes Doug. “You have a match in a few days that would make you #1 contender for the World Title, and here you are smashing up all of our equipment! Are you TRYING to get yourself hurt or something?!” Doug blinks a couple times before turning to Pgod. “I’m sorry, man. I dunno what came over me…” Meanwhile, Richard has simmered down. The Panda sees this and lets go of Richard. Without a word, Richard grabs his gear and calmly walks out of the room. The Panda starts to follow him out of the room, but looks at Pgod on the way out. Pgod gives Doug a look of indignation before letting go of his arm. As he walks out of the room, he mumbles “How do they expect me to run this damn station with all this bullsh*t?” Inside the room, Doug stands completely still as everything sinks in. With a heavy sigh, he walks over to his gear. After rummaging through his duffel bag, he pulls his cell phone out. While he dials the number, the camera starts to pull away and out of the room. As it pulls out, we can hear Doug saying “Yes, this is Doug Troy. I’d like to make an appointment to talk with Jane Reagen. When’s she available? Before Slam? Great.” We can hear the sound of the phone being flipped shut as the camera pulls farther and farther away. As the scene fades to black, we can hear Doug say “Not again. I’m not gonna let this happen again. It stops right now…” Fade to black
