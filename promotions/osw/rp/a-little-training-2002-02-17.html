A little training....

17 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/17/02 Tom Walczak is seen running up and down the hill that leads up to his home. As he runs he carries a tree trunk on his back, and wears a sweat suit. The suit drips of perspiration and the marks are visible as Walczak’s entire suit is visibly darker than it was when he started. Walczak runs up and down two more times bringing his total to ten sets, and he then puts the tree down, and almost collapses from exhaustion. Just as he sits down his limo driver comes towards him. “Tom, are you ok? I was watching you run, and well you look pretty tired I just came over here to make sure that everything is ok.” Walczak tries to get his breath back before he speaks, but can’t do so, and therefore has trouble answering his driver. “Yeah…..It…..is all…..just…..part…...of the…..training….I need……..to…….get…… ready…… for…… my…….match……on…….Saturday…….It……..is…….for…….the …….World…….Championship…….. number……. one…….. contendership.” “Oh, ok. You know I carry a tree trunk on my back every once in a while as well. In fact I would have joined you, but the thing is that my back is killing me from all those power squats.” Walczak just looks up and smiles as the driver clenches his back in pain. Walczak lies down on the grass and closes his eyes. He is still breathing heavily, in an almost wheezing type of manner. After a couple minutes of lying there he sits back up, and has almost all of his breath back. “That was a great work out that I just had, and after a little break, and some food I will get back to training. I have to be in peak physical condition for this match. I am one of twelve men that enter the match, but I want to have the condition to be the only one standing at the end of the match.” Walczak stands up and goes towards his home. He goes inside and sees that his driver has his feet up on the table in the living room, and has a sandwich in his hand, and a beer in his other. “You really should go shopping. Your refrigerator is nearly empty, I could barely pull some things together to make this sandwich, but you do have a hell of a lot of beer in there.” “Did you just make that sandwich? You just took the last of my food, and you didn’t even ask me. I really should kick your ass right now. I am starving and yet I have no food to eat, and you had the nerve to tell me that I should go to the store to buy some. If you don’t get out of here right now, and go get me some food then I swear to hell that you will not leave this house except on a stretcher.” Without a word the driver rushes out of the house, and hops into the limo. He drives away with a tremendous amount of speed. Walczak sits down on the couch, and rubs the sweat off of his forehead. “What was that guy thinking? Did he think that I wasn’t going to get angry at him if he ate my food? It doesn’t matter now though, all that matters is the #1 Contender match, and you can rest assured that I will put my best effort forward, and no matter how tired I am I will not stop my drive towards the championship.” Walczak lies his head back and nods off to sleep, and as he begins to snore the scene fades to black…
