What Lies Beneath

17 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-17-02 Amid the greening leaves of the forest in the valley, the sun is barely able to penetrate through the treetops. As the light filters through, the understory is given life, while a comfortable shade protects Kimbusa as he strolls. It is warm this sunny afternoon, the springtime air brisk following a good April storm. A few fallen leaves remaining from the wintertime crunch beneath Kimbusa's weight, adding to the naturesque sound of springtime. Birds are chirping, singing happily in the perfect weather. A squirrel scrambles across Kimbusa's path, taking care not to be crushed beneath the mighty sumo's girth. Kimbusa, however, keeps his focus on the path immediately ahead of him, not stopping to take in any of the scenery. His traditional silk gown flutters lightly in the breeze, its plain black design causing Kimbusa to be darkened in the shadow. His hair is loose and meandering through the air as Kimbusa walks, his sandals flipping lightly with each step. A small puff of smoke rises above Kimbusa's shoulder. Yoshi walks a few feet behind him, smiling in reflection of a beautiful day. He raises his wooden pipe to his mouth to take another puff, as he looks downward to be certain not to trip on the many obstacles. Luckily for the small, old man, Kimbusa easily tramples a clear path ahead. Yoshi clears his throat, and Kimbusa stops abruptly. Yoshi moves towards a pine tree to Kimbusa's left, his pupil still facing onward down the forested path. Yoshi inverts his pipe and gives it a firm shake, emptying it of the rest of his tobacco. With another grin he looks up through a hole in the canopy, glancing upward towards the clear blue sky. Kimbusa's eyes narrow, leaving only darkness to be seen. 'Why do we stop here?' Yoshi ignores Kimbusa for a moment, still taking in the scenery. After a moment, without looking back to Kimbusa, he speaks. 'I am tired. My knees are weak. Rest a moment.' Kimbusa grumbles at the delay. With a low, thunderous sigh he turns and dusts off an old tree stump. With great care he turns again and seats himself on the stump, though his massive rear remains a bit too large for its perch. With great discomfort, both of the seat and the situation, Kimbusa glares back at his master. 'There is something else. Why have you brought me here to the woods? What have you to say?' Yoshi finally turns back and faces his warrior, his face growing grim with acceptance. Kimbusa has discovered his alterior motive, and the time has come to speak. 'I have felt the tremors that your past has left you. These are tumultuous times for you, Kimbusa-san. Dangerous times. I do not want to see you make another mistake as you did so many years ago.' As Yoshi speaks, Kimbusa begins to grow furious with rage. His anger at his master's mentioning of the past becomes almost volcanic, but as his master's voice fades, Kimbusa inhales deeply and turns away to the forest, doing his best to calm his fury. After a heated silence, Yoshi moves in and raises his hand to Kimbusa's shoulder. Kimbusa closes his eyes in an attempt to further bury his emotion, as Yoshi speaks softly, comforting his pupil. 'It is time, Kimbusa-san. It is time to show them why we are here. It is time to show them why we have come to this place. It is time to bury the past forever, and leave it to decay in the furthest reaches of our minds. You have a chance to release that anger, Kimbusa-san. But when the time comes, will you be able to focus your anger on your adversary, Kid Wonder, and not...' Kimbusa shudders, knowing what is coming. '...the girl?' Kimbusa's face noticeably begins to tremble, and his eyes open. His large black eyes now seem plagued with an enraged red glow, his face becoming menacing despite the tears that have welled up in his eyes. Kimbusa finally breaks his silence, speaking with a voice trembling from both sadness and rage. 'It is time. And I will show them why I am here.' Yoshi nods with a satisfied smile, looking upward to his monstrous student. 'Finally, we shall get what we came for...'
