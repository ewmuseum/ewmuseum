A Canadian on MTV, eh? Worse. Amy Campbell on MTV...this is the apocalypse we always hoped for. (Pt.1)

12 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 12th, 2002 ((Note: I'm kinda dealing with a mixture of pain killers *Just got an eyebrow ring* and nausea medication *Stomach flu*...so bare [Is that how you spell it?] with me, here.)) Usually when someone walked into the MTV studios, well-aware that they were to be a guest on Total Request Live within an hour, they'd be excited. Well, Amy Campbell had agreed to participate in the show, to stand next to Carson, answer a few questions and make a video request... She was pissed. Why? Basically, the only reason she agreed was because she had to, it was an 'extra-curricular' activity of sorts that made up the section of her salary dubbed 'Public Appearances'. Aimz had an agenda against MTV since Britney Spears 'burst onto the scene'...that certainly wouldn't change. She considered it just another media mogul there to twist the minds of impressionable youth to love what they want to sell. Guess what? Amy was going to twist them HER OWN way...she'd be happy if she could convince just one kid to think for themselves. It was asking for a miracle, but it would hopefully pull through. Of course, she'd deliberately not bothered to 'fix herself up': Stepping into the studio with hair pulled into a loose, scruffy pony tail and a little Hatebreed t-shirt on over baggy, torn Army cargo pants that were held up with a leather studded belt, all along with a pair of black leather buckle boots that had fire imprints at the toes. Basically, she was looking to be the definition of the 'Anti-MTV' movement. As expected, Campbell had little on her mind aside from ZaC, Reno's monkey, interrupting the two at a 'pinacle' moment of sorts.........and let's be truthful, here...questions about Civil Disobedience's assistance in kicking Corg and Bethany Yamato's asses last Slam were floating around in there too, closely tailed by plots for this week's match, tagging with an ancient enemy. Ah well, everything was hopefully going to work out, the match at Ascension was still lurking at the fore-front of her head...but it would be over soon, only a few days left now. Her thoughts were soon interrupted, right after she sat down on a little cushioned chair in the lobby, a representative stepping up to the redhead and tapping her on the shoulder as she twirled her tongue ring around rows of her teeth, enjoying the irritating 'Click click click' sound. ''Ahem...Miss Campbell?'' The woman, wearing a business suit with a knee-length skirt and thick, tacky looking glasses, frowned. ''You're an hour late!'' ''Uhh...yeah...I figured we could afford to skip the whole 'Pre-production pretty-up time'.'' ''You mean...hair, costume and makeup?!'' She looked Amy over, shaking her head violently with wide eyes to match. ''My Heavens, no! Follow me, we must get you up there!'' Amy rolled her eyes as she followed the middle-aged woman into an elevator, humming a tune all the way up. Was this woman REALLY working for MTV? Unbelievable...no WONDER people like Britney Spears and Mandy Moore get all the TV time. As if an old coot like this is really 'in touch' with her Norwich Union life insurance broker, let alone the youth of the world! When the elevator door opened, the woman led Aimz to a small, folding chair. She quickly darted off as a man with short, gelled black hair, a tight black turtle-neck top and leathery pants came up to stand behind her, flipping the back of her hair with the light touch of a few fingers. ''Oh my! Thith hair ith jutht THUPER!'' He screamed, bending back on his knees whilst he stroked his chest with open palms. ''GirlFRIEND, I can do WONDERTH with thith hair!!'' Amy didn't respond, she simply shuddered as the guy flicked his wrist at her in the mirror. ''Ith juth GORGEOTH! Ith it your natural color!?'' ''Uhhh...yeah...........'' ''THUPER! I with I could get hair like that! Ith juth the beeth kneeth!!!'' He grabbed a pair of scissors. Amy leapt up from the chair. ''You're not TOUCHING those to my head.'' She growled. ''Fuck this...I'm gonna go find that Carson freak...'' ''But Mith CAAAAMPBELL...'' The man whined, pouting as he stomped a foot on the floor and folded his arms, sticking a hip out. ''We HAVE to get thith hair thtyled and then thend you to makeup, then you'll get your new clotheth! Promith!'' ''Eeww...just....ugggh.........'' With that, Aimz walked off, eventually landing herself across the large, black backstage area, back in front of the woman she'd originally met. ''Miss Campbell! You look horrid! Are you feeling alright?! Gah, we should fire those makeup fools...they do NOTHING for a person.'' She tossed her arms in the air. Amy rolled her eyes again. ''Remind me to hurt you for that later.'' ''Bah!'' She pulled a few cards out of her pocket. ''Here's your script. In bold, there are the questions you'll be asked, in italics are your answers. For a video, you've been told to request 'Slave For You' by Britney Spears.'' ''What...the...FUCK?!'' Campbell's eyes went wide, the woman's did too...but only after Amy uttered 'That awful F word'. ''Errr...'' Muahaha! She'd show them... ''...alright, just write that down for me so I don't forget. I'm sure to be nervous, I mean, Carson's SUCH a cutie.'' Nice act. The lady fell for it, laughing along with Amy's well-faked giggle. ''Oh, I KNOW! Anywho...I suppose you'll want to look those over. I'll let the hair and makeup slide this time, you're pretty enough...but DO change those pants, and take the eyebrow ring out by the time the assistant escorts you to the set...they make you look like some...some...homeless individual!'' As the prude strode off, high heels clicking, Amy laughed and muttered to herself... ''Heh...wouldn't want somebody going on MTV looking relatively realistic, now would we?'' The antics had only begun...soon enough, she'd be nationwide.
