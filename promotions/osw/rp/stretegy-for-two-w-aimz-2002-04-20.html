Stretegy for Two (w/ Aimz)

20 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/20/02 (recorded, pre-Slam) Tempest hopped on one foot, trying to squeeze the raised one into a tiny high heeled purple suede sandal and said, 'Dammit, I'm gonna be late!' She huffed and finally accomplished the feat, then stomped and said, 'And Aimz doesn't even know I'm coming yet.' She paused and her eyebrows knit together as she added, 'So technically, I'm not really late.' She chuckled and adjusted her short lavender dress, made of some shimmering synthetic and said, 'This is gonna be great!' She bustled out the door, with a flourish and was heard whistling down the hall. ~~~ Amy Campbell flopped back on her couch and groaned. 'God what a day...' Suddenly a knock came at the door and she pulled a pillow over her face as she moaned, 'Nooooo.' After it came again she stood and plodded to the door, her hair falling forward. 'Whaaaa...Tempest? Ummm...hi....' Amy mumbled, looking through narrowed eyes at the small figure before her, who almost seemed to be bouncing happily... or was it a trick of the light? 'Can people really be that happy?' She gasped jokingly, 'You're on LSD, AREN'T YOU?!' Tempest snickered and shook her head, her jet black pony tails bobbing. 'Of course not!' she said. 'Drugs might make me normal. But I was thinking we should go over some strategy for Saturday night!' Amy yawned and said, 'Yeah, sounds like a plan. Any ideas so far? I'm not really much for strategy...I usually just throw myself around and hope I don't get my ass kicked.' She laughed a little with a hint of a smile. Tempest laughed and said, 'Well, I can do strategy, but not here! The clubs are calling. Let's go!' She grabbed Amy's hand and started to haul her out the door. Startled, Amy backpedaled and stammered, 'Ummm....clubs? As in techno...and dancing...and eeew... Why would I go there?' Tempest rolled her eyes and sighed as she said, 'Because that's the pest place to relax and strategize! I think best with a beat to dance too and a cool drink.' 'But it'll be all poppy and dancy and gross with the preps and their shiny clo-----------did you say DRINKS?!' Amy's eyes suddenly lit up. Tempest grinned and said, 'Yes.' 'Like...alcohol kinda drinks?' Tempest nodded and said, 'If you like.' 'Forget looking decent...let's go NOW! But, just in case I actually hear the music somewhere above the sound of booze spilling into glasses...........we'll pick up some ear plugs on the way!' Amy said as she barreled out the door. Tempest laughed and said, 'I can live with that! C'mon!' And the mismatched pair darted down the hallway. ~~~ Amy looked suspiciously at the bubbling pink concoction before her and said to Tempest, 'Are you sure this has alcohol?' Tempest sipped something similar and said, 'Yeah of course! Actually they're quite deceptive.' She winked and added, 'But they're not all heavy and stuff. You can still dance after a few of them.' She grinned and grooved in her seat to the techno throb reverberating in the room. '...and I would want to dance...why? Something to knock me out of my head would be welcome at the moment.' Amy said, grimacing at the bouncy rhythm. Tempest frowned jokingly at her and said, 'Aw c'mon, don't tell me those fast feet never grace a dance floor.' 'Are you kidding me? Not to this music...not to ANY music....eeeeeew...' Amy said wrinkling her nose. 'Well...' Tempest said, and paused a moment in thought, 'How do ya dance to Metallica?' 'You don't, that's the whole point...you just stand and clap at the end of a song, no dancing involved,' Amy said, talking to her drink between sips. 'Well that's boring!' Tempest exclaimed and jumped of the purple leopard spotted barstool. 'How can you stand that? I mean, I gotta move, I dunno about you. Speaking of which, we've been sitting entirely too long.' 'Is there any possible way we could sit a little longer? I mean, you can go have fun and all...but I don't totally see the point of...' 'Phooey!' Tempest blurted and grabbed her hand. She whisked Amy off the barstool, leaving it spinning in her wake and scurried to the dance floor. Amy stood still amid the whirling bodies and stared at them. 'What the Hell?' she said as she was bumped by a couple in the middle of a lively groove. Tempest shook her head with a laugh as she swung and a swayed and yelled over the noise, 'Come on Amy! Pick up one foot, then the other... you can do it!' 'Oh yeah...I'm just itching to try the new jive,' she said, standing with he hands on her hips. 'Let's see here...no? Yeah, that's about right.' Tempest rolled her eyes and hopped in irritation as she said, 'But Amy... we gotta work together and be a team and all that! Dancing is a great way to do that.' 'Whoooooa whoa whoa...just stand back for a second here,' Amy said, looking at Tempest warily, 'dancing...team...dancing...............sorry, but I don't go for that.' 'Oh all right! Fine, no dancing. I'll dance on my own, helps me think,' Tempest said with a shrug. 'But I figure between the two of us, we could embarrass Toomes pretty good on Slam! Whaddya say?' She asked this in mid shimmy. 'Well yeah! What else would I be doing? He thinks he's the big man and all that...technically he is, but even the biggest can cry when two little ladies take them down,' Amy said with a smirk. 'Yeah!' Tempest burbled and hopped up and down, with a little right jab. 'Exactly what I was thinking. I could give him a Rotweiler and you could hit him with a Dead Aim and then we could grab him and throw him upside down at the turnbuckle and...' 'Okay okay...I get where this goes. It all ends with Toomesie's ego getting shot down a few notches...as long as we can achieve that much, I'm a happy little metalhead.' Tempest grinned and nodded, then shook her head at Amy's still planted feet. 'Aren't you dating some hero guy? Don't you know you'll have to dance with him when he defeats the bad guys and celebrates? It's all in his contract I'm sure.' Amy snickered and said, 'I don't call it dancing...errr...umm...yeah, sure. And RAH? He's just a little crazy...there's no official hero thing, and a little crazy is VERY good.' Tempest chuckled and said, 'Glad we're agreed.' The music ended and as the crowd cheered, Tempest fanned herself and motioned Amy to follow her as she left the dance floor. 'We're leaving? Can we go somewhere remotely tasteful?' Amy said sulking. Tempest looked her up and down with a raised eyebrow and said, 'How would you know when we got there?' AMy stopped for a second and said, 'I'm not entirely sure what THAT was intending...buuuut I'm up for anything involving food, alcohol or...you know...non-blinky lighting and decent music.' Tempest smiled and said, 'Well, there's food and non-blinky lighting upstairs. And the music is a dull roar, howzat sound?' 'Dull roar, eh? I say we hear this roar out...' Tempest smiled and wound her way up a spiral staircase, leaving the din of the club behind as the lighting gave way to softer luminescence uncluttered by the rage of color of the dance floor. Tempest plopped down at a table, black enamel with dark red glitter interspersed, and onto one of the fuzzy black wing chairs before it. 'Lots of black...looks slightly more to my liking, good stuff,' Amy said with a nod. Tempest smiled and said, 'I thought so. And they make a great sandwich here too. I just love their French Dip.' She nabbed a menu and offered it to Amy. 'So what do you think we should do after we totally embarrass Toomes? Hang Jane up by her garters maybe?' 'Garters? The hell? I was thinking more along the lines of THROAT...but it's all good, I'm willing to do just about anything as long as they get hurt or embarrassed in the process.' 'Oh good!' Tempest said beaming. 'So you won't have a problem with the matching pink tights I got for us for the match?' 'Please...PLEASE tell me you're joking!!!!! I'm afraid I might need to purchase a gun before the night's out.........' Any said, leveling her gaze at Tempest. Tempest laughed and clapped her hands delightedly. 'Of course! But the look on your face is worth it,' she said with a devilish grin. 'Anyway, we beat Toomes, we run Jane herself up the flag pole instead of her bra, and we grab out respective sweeties and go close down another club. Sound good?' 'I'll drink to that! Sounds good...as long as we're still walking.' Amy said and raised her glass. Tempest clinked her little glass against Amy's and the two laughed. All in all, it was a flawless strategy.
