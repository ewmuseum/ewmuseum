Aimz of a Russian Bear (Ivan/Aimz rp)

14 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-14-02 The injuries that Ivan Stanislav and El Drago inflicted upon each other were grievous indeed. Even Stanislav, the more difficult to bring down of the two, had suffered injuries that required more than one day in the hospital, but actually several. When Stanislav awoke, he had recovered remarkably well. El Drago, covered by a thick sheet to hide his identity, was another story. The Russian Bear’s first instinct was to go to Arkhangelsk, back to his base, and relax there. But yet..he didn’t find relaxation in Russia. Not now, at least. Ruslan had told him that the politicians were anxious to hear from him. Rumor that Karpov would even be coming to see Stanislav had arisen. Rather than see those people, let them come and see him, he thought. So Stanislav took advantage of some much needed rest and contemplated his future. And then, he remembered that there was something that he had to do. Something that was far overdue….. Ivan Stanislav stood in the quiet hospital hallway wearing his typical attire, including his black pants, boots, shirt minus the hammer and sickle, and suspenders. He stood tall as he stared at the door and removed a chart beside the door itself, reading it. He shook his head and replaced the chart, once again staring at the door. He brought his hand up to the door and knocked loudly three times, shaking the entire door with every thunderous knock. He paused for a moment and grabbed the doorknob..and opened the door…. Inside the hospital room, Amy Campbell, better known to the world as Aimz, laid in her hospital bed. The trauma caused by Corg, particularly the stab wound in her shoulder, had landed her in this room. As the door opened slowly, the hulking for of Ivan Stanislav walked into the room. She looked over at him groggily, but quickly snapped back to attention when she realized just who had entered. The man who alone had sent her to the hospital in the past. “'Ho..ly.....hell...”' was all she could muster as the realization hit her that The Russian Bear had entered her room. She shivered and jumped out of bed, grabbing the nearest object that she could, a flower vase, and backed up slowly. Stanislav put his hands out in front of him and squinted an eye, perhaps making himself look even more threatening, “Hold on Campbell, Ivan is not here to hurt you!” “A phone call would be to not hurt me...you don't show up when you don't want to hurt me....get the hell out!!” yelped Aimz in response as Stanislav took one heavy step into the room and stopped. She backed into the way and raised the vase up in defense. Stanislav nodded and took a step back, “You are correct. Listen…I do not wish to hurt you regardless of what you think..I..I wanted to tell you something….” Stanislav paused and took another step backwards until he reached the door. “Look, Ivan is at door, I will remain here if you wish, just calm down.” Aimz shivered again, fear still in her eyes, and took a step closer to her bed. “You'll forgive me if I don't run over and give you a hug. But..uhh...what's there to say to me? You might preach all you want to the people in the arenas, and they may believe you, but I don’t!” Aimz walked to her bed and slowly put the vase down, but her hand rested on it, “Last time you were ‘friendly’ you decided to play catch with my skull. I won’t let it happen again.” Stanislav nodded, “That is correct, I did, Ms. Campbell. I…I do not know how to prove to you that I am not threatening, since I am threatening anywhere that I go. However, please, take my word for it. I will not move from this area if you wish, but I must speak with you.” “I don't exactly think a Russian that could have bench-pressed K-19 really needs to ask for my permission to speak...feel free to say what you want, just keep the beatings to a minimum, or my hand can 'slip' to that Help button pretty quickly...” she warned and leaned against the bed, her shoulder taped from the stab wound. Stanislav nodded and fidgeted slightly, “You know, Amy Campbell….I have done many things that I can say Ivan is not proud of. Though, I must say that there are many things that I have done of which I am proud of, and do not regret doing such things.” He stopped and thought for a moment, before continuing. Aimz watched on, confused. “I know many people do not believe that Ivan Sergeiovich Stanislav is seriously fighting for them. And that is understandable. However, I must say that I am trying to do what is right. To do what is right for me….not for Russia..but for myself.” He paused again and looked down at the floor. “The last time that we met….we were on different terms….” Campbell is was quick to respond, her high pitched voice a stark contrast to Stanislav’s rumbling Russian dialect, “Yeah, I guess you could call bashing someone's skull in each time you laid eyes upon them 'different' as far as terms go.” Ivan cleared his throat and took one tiny step into the room, careful not to spook Aimz, “That was appropriate..and needed. Yes, I have said that there are things that I am not proud of, that is one of them. I…I wanted to tell you, Amy Campbell, that Ivan is not blind to what is going on with you. I have seen the vile things that have happened to you, and whether you believe me or not…I sympathize for you.” “You probably expected this, but there's no way in hell I'm believing that. In a list of people least likely to ever honestly feel bad about me, I'm pretty sure Communism's poster boy is way up there.” Fired back Aimz. Ivan paused. He gritted his teeth for just a moment and looked at the door behind him, as if to contemplate leaving, and then thought otherwise and faced her again. “Yes, I can understand why you would think that. My reason for coming here is twofold, Amy Campbell. First, I wanted…I wanted to apologize in turn for my actions of the past.” He paused again and thought for a moment, and then looked down to the ground, “I was mistaken for what I did. I was not myself, obviously. When I lost her…..I…..I did not know what to do..and I pointed the fingers at all around me..when in reality..I was to blame…for it all.” He stared at the floor. Aimz stared at Stanislav for a long time. He did not look up, he did not move, almost as if he was paralyzed by her sight, and then slowly, she responded. “Okay...well THAT I can understand. It's definitely hard to lose someone you care about, even harder when you know they're still out there continuing their lives...but...well yeah, you don't need to be told that, I guess.” He did not look up, “Nyet, I do not. I know of war, I know of death, and I know of loss. But I did not know of that…sort of loss.” He slowly looked up at her and took another step into the room. “I was wrong, and now I realize it. I took my anger..my hatred..out on you…” He looked away suddenly, “How I hated you..Amy Campbell….how I despised you because I thought you had sabotaged me. I do not…expect you to accept my apology, but I wished to tell you this.” Again, she took time to digest what he had said. Her voice lightened just slightly, “You really mean this stuff, don't you?” Ivan nodded slowly, “Yes. I come not here as a member of Red Army or as wrestler of OSW, or as Russian soldier. I come here, as Ivan Stanislav. What I did was wrong. My reasoning was wrong. And I am trying to fix that from which I had destroyed.” He looked up at her and straightened his back, “Yes, I mean all that I say. One cannot lie about something like this….do you think I would? If I wished to come in here and dash you against floor…would I not have already?” ''To tell you the truth, the main reason I didn't believe you was...well...yeah, I didn't exactly expect Ivan Stanislav just to prance into my room to apologize and talk about love. I was kinda expecting a beating any minute...but I'm starting to think you might actually be real about this. If you're truthful about this, there's no need to apologize...I would've done the same. Sure,'' she paused and chuckled to herself, ''...I probably wouldn't have been capable of dropping trucks on people or tossing girls ten feet, but I can actually almost relate to the feeling. If I ever lost Reno like you lost...you know who, I'd just go nuts, even if I DIDN'T think other people had something to do with it. You were wrong in what you did, yeah...but I understand the motivation. I'm not sure if that made sense or anything, but there you go.'' Ivan looked up at her and almost, smiled. As much of a smile as The Russian Bear could muster, “Yes, it makes perfect sense Amy Campbell. I….I know I caused you pain, and I know that the terror I must have caused was great. And while I know that there is not much I can do….I…” he paused for just a moment, “I have something for you.” He reached into his back pocket and produced a small black box, “If..I may?” She smiled and hopped onto the bed, “Ummm...yeah, absolutely! But..err.....damn...this is honestly one of those instances where I wonder whether I actually STOPPED doing drugs or not.” Ivan thought for a moment, trying to figure out her sentence, and then shrugged and slowly lumbered toward her. He got down on a knee so that he was about eye level with her and handed her the box. “I…I did not know what to get..uh..young girl like you. But I knew that money was not enough…..money is not way to happiness, of course. It may not mean much to you..but..it is all I could think of.” Aimz looked at the small box and opened it slowly. She removed a small sheer piece of white fabric and stared at an ornate silver cross of some kind, connected by a chain. “What's....this?” she asked, staring at the cross. Stanislav thought for a moment, his breath exited through his nose heavily, “When I was young boy, my father disappeared. It left Ivan Sergeiovich with just himself and his mother. However, when I became a man, I too had to leave my house, and my mother.” He swallowed loudly, “As a Communist, Amy, we do not believe in a God. We are atheist. However, my mother..she was of Russian Orthodox faith. That, is a Russian Orthodox cross. I do not know if it will help you, or mean anything to you, but when she was alone, my mother said she could find……strength…in her cross.” Ivan breathed heavily as Aimz continued to stare at it, “Perhaps it will help you in these trying times, Amy Campbell. Perhaps if you feel all alone…you can look at that and feel better.” Ivan looked away and stared at the floor, slightly embarrassed. She stared at it for a moment and noticed Ivan looking away, “This sounds corny, and isn't something you'd normally catch me saying...but a gift coming from the most unlikely person means a lot more than any other.” He looked up and smiled crookedly, “You can have it. Wear it if you like, or just keep it close to your side. However, I had said that my coming here was twofold. I wished to tell you something else.” He looked at her now, “I know that I was wrong for what I did. And I hope that this will ease your anxiety regarding Ivan Sergeiovich, but I had a few questions to ask you.” Ivan swallowed and stared straight at her, his head dwarfing her, “Whenever Corg…did such things to you, did you feel helpless? As if no one could help you? As if the world meant nothing to you and all you wished was for that wrong to be righted? Did you feel yourself break from within?” She stared into Ivan’s eyes, shaking slightly, “Look...it doesn't feel good to always be the victim..it....it just never will, but I'm living with that. These contract clauses, everything...it's like my luck is running against me. I don't mean this to offend you, but you'll NEVER know what it's like to be absolutely helpless. It's like being lost in the ocean without a life preserver...you can only stay afloat so long before you weaken...any hope of surviving is bleak. Eventually you stop fighting the tide and just wait for it to overcome you...I'm on the edge of that stage, fighting this gets me nowhere.” Ivan nodded and stood up slowly, “Do not be so quick to presume, Amy Campbell, that Ivan Stanislav does not know what that feels like. I wish to tell you of something. While I am apologetic towards you for what I did, I am NOT sorry for what I did to Reno Davis.” He gritted his teeth, “Understand that the feelings that you felt when that happen, are EXACTLY what I felt when he took her from me. You may care for him, but I wish to let you know that if the time comes when Ivan Stanislav and Reno Davis must fight again, if you stand in my way, I will have no choice but to go through you, to him.” Ivan swallowed, “That is not a threat, my dear….that is just warning. He reached into my heart and took away my soul….he is no knight in shining armor…..he did such an act because he ‘felt something’ for Tempest. And therefore, he punished me.” Ivan’s brow furrowed and he blinked, calming himself, and actually sat on the bed lurching it in his direction. “I am sorry, I just….wished for you to understand.” He looked over at her, “That is all that I wished to do, Amy Campbell. I am trying, understand that.” “I know, I know...it's okay. Warn me all you want, just DON'T mention what he thinks of Tempest...I don't even need that to cross my mind right now. You probably don't understand, but it kind of hurts...I'm a bit possessive, I guess. I know how you feel, and believe me, I don't agree with what he did in the least...I just try not to think about it, because I know what his motivation was and, like I said, that really leaves a sour taste in my mouth.” She paused and then continued, “If he provokes you again, I won't stand in your way...just please leave him be unless he does, he's been punished...hurting him right now would only hurt me, you know? But stop apologizing, man...I know what you feel, and I've gotta say that I really do believe you might not be such a monster after all.” She chuckled to herself, somewhat surprised at her words, ''...but don't worry, I won't tell anybody.” Ivan smiled warmly this time, “Well thank you Amy, I am glad that you gave me an audience to speak. I will not bother you, nor will I bother Davis so long as he remains out of my way.” He reached into his back pocket and produced a small steel flask, “Here, take that, Vodka always numbs the pain for Ivan.” He grinned, “I will be going now, Amy Campbell. Thank you.” She grinned and stood up as Ivan did, “You don't have to thank me...I should probably be thanking YOU. You've kind of changed my entire method of judgment here this afternoon.” She gingerly brought her arms up and hugged the massive Russian Bear around the waist. Stanislav stood there, somewhat thrown off by the hug, and patted her on the back of the head. “Yes, perhaps all of us are too judgmental at times.” He answered. She released him and sat back in the bed as Ivan walked to the door and turned, “Keep the flask, my dear, sneak more Vodka if you want, I have been in this hospital, and they never caught me. DYAAHAAHAA!!!” Ivan laughed loudly and exited the room, leaving Aimz to stare at the flask and the cross.
