Date: April 13, 2002 'Nick, you fucking maniac!' The black Porsche zoomed up the Kentucky Turnpike, speeding in and out of traffic. The speedometer read about ninety, however everyone knows that speedometers run slow. Nick Casey sat behind the wheel, one elbow out the window and the other hand on the wheel in traditional truck-driver fashion, since the car was alreayd in fifth. After all, Nick was from a long line of truck drivers. Wait, no he wasn't. The breeze from the window blew Angeldust's blonde hair back onto the seat. His face had no real expression, he just weaved through traffic with ease. He did have a spotless driving record, minus...well...you know. On the other side of the car, sat Christy Zeller. I suppose that sat isn't the right word; hannging on for dear life was Christy Zeller sounds like a more appropriate term. She apparently wasn't quite so confident as Nick in these sorts of cases. Past exit after exit, radio blaring some of Nick's favorite Nirvana, becoming one with the whir of the engine and the passing white lines. Right now, Nick's mind was clear of thought. 'Lithium' played almost ironically at the time, Angeldust just melded with the music. He didn't worry about Epitome of Evil. The seven-foot monster had been his achilles heel since he entered Old School Wrestling; he had haunted him from the day he set foot in the ring. The route to a Head On Collision would have to be through Epitome of Evil on Slam. The meaning of this match would give one person a supreme advantage. As EoE so eloquently put it, it's like putting your life in Satan's hand. This was a battle of complete opposites. The Big versus The Small. The Power Wrestler vs. The High Flyer. The Slow vs. The Fast. The Strong vs. The Weak. Most importantly though, this was a battle of Good versus Evil. The classic story, seen depicted a thousand times in books, stories, and movies since biblical times. Throughout all of those encounters, there was one fact that always remained true; Good always wins. However, Nick's path to this supreme advantage in the proverbial 'Good vs. Evil' Battle required the help of another man, Rhett Locke. The Old School Wrestling International Champion. The Anti-Paladin. The Anti-Hero. Possibly one of the greatest offensive weapons in the world, and Nick had him in his corner to go against evil. EoE wasn't by himself, there is the small matter of Corg as well. What did Nick give a shit? He would get what he wanted. He spent his whole life being neglected of what he wanted in MWW, he would not get it taken away from him in OSW as well. If he had to fight someone eight feel tall and nine-hudred pounds, he would do it. Epitome of Evil is nothing but a face that will be counting the lights, just like everyone in MMW. Except this time, OSW is not going to go bankrupt. It's not going to get personal like with Ethan Ryan Alexander. No, it's just business time. Business that has to be done. 'Nick, what are thinking about?' Christy chimed in. Angeldust sighed. 'Nothing Christy. Nothing.'
