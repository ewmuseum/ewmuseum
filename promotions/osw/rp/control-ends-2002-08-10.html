Control Ends.

10 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-10-02 'Harder..' I can feel my strength wanting to exit my body but I refuse to stop my motions. My legs are on fire, the muscles inside panging with a deep hurt. I can no longer keep my eyes open as I can only close them and hope I continue doing as I was before they shut. I hear his voice ringing out in to the stark, stale air and it sickens me. He pushes me harder, raves me about it when I falter. 'Harder..' His words make me want to lash out, but I bite my tongue and keep all of the rage inside. A few more hours and this would be over only to be able to vent all I would ever need inside of the ring. I ache, I am in pain and I can feel it surging through my body. Yet he continues on, knowing full well I can handle more than what he is putting me through. 'Harder..' I continue to leap upward in to the heights of the air and spin kick the top of the wooden post. It has cracked, splitered but no where near breaking with such a thickness as it holds. I land in a low crouch, sweat dripping from my brow as I push onward. Once more I leap up and land my heel in to the same place. The pain is becoming erotic to me. 'Stop.' 'Listen to me.' I turn my head slowly to the left and look upward to the robed man before me. He stares me down from under his hood like a child. I know he is disappointed in me, but I care to reserve my strength. The time is rapidly approaching for an insect to become disected. His eyes bore in to mine and I listen while he begins to speak. 'You are not concentrating. Callisto, Look at yourself. Show me what you are truly capable of.' I growl almost too softly to be heard and tear my eyes from his. I feel a hot rage building up inside of me. He does underestimate my skills..My power, but I will show him. I will have him pressed under my thumb soon enough and then I will be rid of his banter once and for all. I will show him alright, and I do.. From my feet I spring upward as I have done a million times over this night. But it is not the post I come in to contact with. Instead my bulk catches him off guard and down we topple. I land atop him, my forearm pressed to his throat. He gasps and coughs, quickly losing air from my pressure. I can feel my silver laces stare boring in to him and I see him cower under me. He knows all too well what I can do, and just what I am made from and he understands that when he pushes he I can break him. I have matters to attend to before I head directly toward the ring, and tonight will be my night to make sure my name is known. As I witness his face turning to resemble a grape I release my hold and stand quickly. Spinning around I grab hold of my leather jacket and pull it over my Gi top. The black leather mends well with the silk blackness of my Gi of choice. With that my bag is also lifted on to my shoulder and I leave with my teacher on the ground still trying to fight to retrieve his breath. 'No more..' Is all that exits my lips while I leave the place I once new as my only home. I have answers to find, but not tonight..No tonight I have revenge on the brain. A certain someone who attacked me with a crowbar needs to be paid back and tonight..Tonight is the night.
