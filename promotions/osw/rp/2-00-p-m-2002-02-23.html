2:00 P.M.

23 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-23-02 Our scene fades in to reveal Trevor Hawke, who is sleeping on the same couch he sleeps on every night. Well, at least that’s how it starts out. It’s only seconds before a rustling noise in the other room brings him out of his slumber. Glancing at the clock on the wall, he sees that it’s 2:00 P.M., meaning someone (most likely Seth) has felt like cheating him out of three hours of sleep by waking him up this frickin’ early. Naturally, he’s not too happy about this, and, after rubbing the sleep from his eyes, makes a point to investigate the disturbance. Trudging into the makeshift TV room (which is currently functioning as more of a closet), Trevor encounters Seth, who is currently digging through a large cardboard box. Trevor opens his mouth to yell at his partner, then realizes that the urge to bitch Seth out has already faded. Shrugging his shoulders at the thought, Trevor instead elects to take a seat along the wall and watch Seth sift through the box’s contents. “You know you woke me up, right.” Trevor says, more of a statement than a question. He knows by now that too many questions mixed with Seth Masters was a vile combination. The sheer idiocy of his answers would drive even the sanest man off the deep end. That made it all the more surprising that he still had his wits. Or did he...? Seth looks up from the box, gives an affirming nod toward Trevor, then immediately returns his attention to his task at hand. He tries carrying on a conversation and looking at the same time, actually pulling it off rather well, which is amazing for a man who has trouble walking and chewing bubble gum simultaneously. “Sorry ‘bout that, dude. I didn’t even realize how early it was. I just figured that since I wasn’t tired, nobody would be.” “Not tired at two o’clock?!” shouts Trevor. “Are you frickin’ crazy, man?!” “Um...I don’t think so,” Seth says with a shrug. “I just woke up about an hour ago and I’ve never felt so refreshed in my life. It’s like I’ve been born again, or some shit.” “That might have something to do with the fact that you’ve slept through the past five days. What kind of crazy medication did they give you in that hospital, anyway?” inquires Trevor. “I don’t know, but now that you mention it, it sure made things look pretty. Mmmmmm. I wonder where they buy that stuff...” “Forget about it, bro,” Trevor states, waving his index finger in the air. “I’m not letting my partner become the next Road Dogg.” “You mean the charismatic one of the team?” “No, I mean the dopehead that gets his cracked out ass fired.” “Ah,” says Seth, obviously not caring enough about the exchange to add any more than that. A lull in the conversation pops up, causing Trevor to twiddle his thumbs impatiently. Eventually the silence gets to him, causing him to be the one who breaks the monotony. “So what the hell are you looking for, anyway?” “You’ll see when I find it,” Seth responds unenthusiastically. “Well are you going to find it anytime soon? I’ve got things to do, you know.” “No you don’t,” Seth says in monotone. “Alright, I’ll give you that one,” Trevor says with a reluctant nod. “But I COULD have things to do if I wanted to.” “No you couldn’t,” Seth says, sounding even more monotone than the first time, if that’s possible, considering the prefix mono means one and if there’s only one tone how could there be variation in...I’ll shut up now. “Shut the fuck up, Seth.” Trevor responds, obviously wanting his partner to follow my lead. “Yes sir,” is all Seth has to say in reply, as he continues digging through the box, ignoring Trevor. After about two more minutes of watching Seth dig through that stupid box, then thinking about just how frickin’ hard could it possibly be to find something in a box that size, and then thinking of the stupidity of Seth to have been looking for over five minutes and still persisting in his search when it is obvious that if he hasn’t found it already then it isn’t in the box, Trevor stands up, deciding that better entertainment could be found elsewhere. “This blows,” he states eloquently, as he walks to the front door. “I’ll be at the gym watching the aerobics class if you need me.” “Eureka, I’ve found it!” shouts Seth, just as Trevor’s hand reaches the doorknob. Of course, this causes Trevor to retreat back to the TV room, sighing heavily as he realizes that he’s wasting valuable time that could be spent staring at tight, trim, female ass. “Well what the fuck is it?” asks Trevor, crossing his arms and expecting quite a lot out of this discovery of Seth’s. In other words, he knows he’s setting himself up for disappointment. Seth reaches down into the box and lifts up his finding with a huge grin on his face. “ROCK ‘EM SOCK ‘EM ROBOTS!!!” he shouts excitedly, as he wipes the dust off of the contraption. He grabs both of the controlling sticks and starts pressing the buttons rapidly. Punches fly madly from each robot, until the unfortunate one on the right gets struck in the chest, causing it’s head to fly up from the body. Seth grins again, looking up from his toy to nod appreciatively at Trevor. “Seth...” Trevor sighs. “If you’re going to tell me that you think that stupid toy is going to help us in our match with Twisted Metal this Saturday, or that it’ll show us how to find their weak spot, or that we can possibly hold the toy hostage and give it to them in exchange for their forfeiture of the match, then I don’t want to hear any of it. You hear me? I’m not participating in any of your stupid ideas this week. We’re going to have a nice normal match for once...well, as normal a match as is possible against two human sized robot wrestlers. You got me?” “Twisted Metal? Who said anything about Twisted Metal? Hell, I didn’t even know we had a match with them.” Seth says, as he presses the robot’s head back down on its body. “Then why did you get that damn toy out?” Trevor asks, raising an eyebrow in confusion. “Because! It’s primo!” Seth shouts happily, as he goes back to tapping the buttons again. He becomes so engrossed in his activity that he doesn’t even notice Trevor leave the room, nor does he hear the sound of the car pulling out of the driveway. He doesn’t even notice the scene fading to black, which it shall do right about...now.
