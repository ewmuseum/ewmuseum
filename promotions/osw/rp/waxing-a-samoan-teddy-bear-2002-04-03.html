Waxing a Samoan Teddy Bear...

3 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 3rd, 2002. ((Note: This is what happens when you watch Nair infomercials while heavily medicated.)) ''Tiiiiiiiimooo...'' Amy Campbell's voice rang out over the sound of some of her favorite music, ''Phantom Of The Opera Song'' by Me First and the Gimmie Gimmies blaring through large headphones that were hanging from her neck. Her chosen style was unusually exotic today; she had a pair of uncharacteristically tight vinyl bondage-style zipper pants slung with a small fiery leather belt rather low around her hips, topped with a pair of leather calve-height buckle boots (Very Nny-esque, for those who'd read the comics.) and a rather snug black t-shirt that was sporting an interesting, yet pointless white logo on the chest in thick print that read: 'Real friends don't let friends screw fat people.'. A mocking response courtesy of the Samoan Silencer quickly came from outside the small Jetta Aimz was sitting in the passenger seat of. ''Aaaaaaamy...'' She was rummaging through a little black backpack that had come from 'down home'. It's contents? CDs, hair elastics, a knife or two, her discman and some little white plastic box that had 'For Timo' written in permanent ink on the lid. While Amy fiddled through that, the big Samoan left his post fixing the flat tire at the back of the car to knock at her half-opened window... ''Is there something you wanted,'' He smiled, however weak. ''...or did I just need to be distracted?'' Amy instinctively smiled back. ''Nah...a little of both. Is the tire fixed yet? 'Cause when we get back to the hotel, there's fun to be had...Bre sent me something interesting.'' ''Ummm...I don't want to know, do I?'' The radical Samoan chuckled, shaking his head at Amy's almost constant antics that made his life just that much harder. ''And as for the tire...if you want it fixed in such a hurry, do it yourself!'' ''Baaaaah...not my car!'' She teased, pinching Bolamba's lower back as he walked away to repair the flat that had caught them just outside of the practical nowhere city of Gulfport, Mississippi. Boo to being stuck in a churchie town! Sure, it was fun to freak them out...but without a boyfriend, that was hard to do...it just wasn't interesting if you couldn't grope somebody in the middle of a crowded mall that was filled to the brim with elderly, church-going men and women. None-the-less, walking through Gulfport with a perma-fried Samoan and his retarded giant manchild might gather the same general reaction... Speaking of the manchild, thank God Timo had locked him in the hotel room with various Zoomboomafoo tapes...Amy wasn't exactly fond of that big, slobbering mountain of flesh. Thankfully, The Samoan Silencer quickly fixed the tire issues...and they were off... BACK AT THE HOTEL... Aimz' voice called out like a small child's again, this time from the bathroom of her Holiday Inn 'suite'... ''Tiiiiimoooo...'' ''Not again...'' Bolamba was just sorting through a stack of CDs he wanted to borrow when he remembered Amy saying she had something interesting to try on him. ''...err...whaaaaat?'' She stepped out into the open with several strips of what looked to be wax paper with some sort of green gel on them. Timo looked slightly alarmed. ''What's that?'' ''Dude,'' Amy had that same mischievous, sinister smile that she usually had before doing something her partner would hate. ''...we're gonna wax your legs!'' ''What the hell?!'' ''You wanna beat Meanstreak, don't you?'' ''Yeah...'' ''Well...'' She pondered a moment, thinking up some excuse that would let her do this. ''...ummm...extra hair will only give him something to pull...he can't grip a silky-smooth Samoan though, can he?'' Bolamba inched back a few steps. ''Uhhh...I don't like the look in your eye...'' ''What look?'' Aimz tried to wipe her face back to a look of divine innocence. ''C'mon, Timo...if you're scared of a little wax, I'm gonna call you The Samoan Sissy for the rest of my days!'' He gulped. ''It's not the waxing I'm worried about...it's the woman on the other end of those little strips!'' ''Awww...don't worry, I'll make it quick. Now sit down and spread 'em.'' ''Never say that again, for the love of God...please...never again.'' ''Sorry...'' Amy smiled, rather amused over how easily she could disturb this monster of a lutz. ''Now, let's get to it!'' She nodded towards a little wooden chair set next to a coffee table. ''Sit there.'' Bolamba reluctantly obeyed the little redhead. After all, this couldn't be THAT bad if women put themselves through it so often. ''And if you're wondering why I'm doing this...yes, it's a bet with Bre...errr...uhhh...it'll help you.'' As Aimz watched the big man, who was afraid to even look as she applied a single strip to a patch on his right calve, she snickered to herself...thinking of the look that was about to streak across his face. She put some pressure on the paper, rubbing down so it'd pull the most hair possible. After moments of this, Amy quickly tore it off, along with what looked like hundreds of thick, black hairs... ''YOOOOOWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWW!!!! SONOFAMOTHERFUCKINGBITCHTHATHURTS!!!!!!!'' Sure enough, the look on his face was funny enough to get Amy rolling on the ground, laughing so hard that she was near to tears. When the laughing fit settled a little, she sat back against the wall and watched the Samoan Silencer hop around the room on one foot, rubbing the now-hairless patch on his other leg. ''Timo...'' She was still chuckling. ''...man, that was a beautiful thing.'' ''I...could...'' Bolamba was now sitting on the bed, blowing cool streams of air across his calve. ''...kill you...for that.'' ''Oh bah...I did the world a slight favor, maybe you'll consider waxing more...'' ''NEVER!'' He let himself fall back on the bed, wrapping every exposed piece of his dark skin under the covers...including his partially bearded face. ''And just for putting me through that...lock your door, because the minute you go to bed tonight, I'm gonna be running in here with one of those little doohicky strip things to put on the top of your head!'' To get air, Timo uncovered his face...the poor man was visibly pouting. ''Hehe...'' Amy practically ignored him, she was still looking at the strip in her hand, laughing. ''I still can't get over how much fun that was!'' She thought a moment, her tag partner still pouting on the bed. ''But...it would've been MUCH better if you had cried like Hostyle will when a girl half his height cuts him down to size, I'm gonna aim for that next time.'' ''Wax his bikini line, then...that's guaranteed to make the jackass sob!'' ''He probably waxes it himself...plus I'm going nowhere NEAR his groin, that'd be like touching...Glen Pierce.'' ''The big bad bi you used to date?'' ''Yep...'' They actually began to laugh TOGETHER as everything faded away...though Timo was still paying the most attention to his now semi-bare right leg.
