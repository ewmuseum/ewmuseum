Journey of a Trashcan Man, Part II (Picking up the pimpest ride in the world)

16 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/16/02 'That'll be eighteen twenty, Mac.' 'Keep the change. MY LIFE FOR HIM!' 'Whatever...' The Trashcan Man climbs out of a yellow cab, grinning wildly as your stereotypical Bronx Italian cab driver, complete with hat and cigar shakes his head and puts his cab into drive, kicking up dust and gravel as he peels out. Trashy pays no attention as he starts to walk towards the front door of 'Old Tom's Auto Storage', as the old, faded, rusty sign over the door proclaims. He walks up to the entrance and pushes a button on the side. A sad, pathetic buzzing noise is heard, followed by a hacking cough and slow footsteps leading up to the door. Trashy is almost hopping up and down in excitement. The door opens, and an old man, sun glinting off of his leathery bald head, hobbles out. He squints up at the Trashcan Man, and suddenly breaks into a blacktoothed, nasty grin. 'Oh, it's you. Guess yer back for yer automobile,' says Old Tom. Trashy, suddenly solemn, nods to the old propietor. The old man spits a wad of brown tobacco juice on the ground to the side of the door and nods. 'Reckon ya should follow me, den.' He moves into the office, and Trashcan Man follows, We flash into the interior of the garage, where Old Tom is slowly fiddling with a key, sliding it into a padlock on a garage door. 'Kept it oiled up an' fueled up for ya. Started it every now and den so da battery wouldn't die, too. She should be runnin' fine.' He finally gets the padlock off and slowly lifts the door up and over his head, revealing... a garbage truck? No, that's not just any garbage truck, as we can see from the rapt expression on Trashy's face. Suddenly, accompanied by the sound of a thunderclap, we see various scenes of this beast in action. The trash hauler slams into Johnny Raindance's infamous yellow humvee, the Trashcan Man grinning behind the wheel Thunderclap The garbage truck busts through the wooden fence of Kriegman's palatial home in Pleasantville, machine guns from the home's security system blazing away. Thunderclap The trash hauler slamming through the wrought iron gates that provided entrance to the Danger Yard, Trashy's junkyard hardcore combat simulator/dojo. A huge explosion erupts, and a fireball washes over it as it hits a corner and skids, almost tipping over before it regains control and takes off down the road Thunderclap And we're back as the Trashcan Man suddenly shakes his head while Old Tom looks at him, a worried expression creasing his old, wrinkly face. 'You OK, son?' Trashy waves Old Tom away, not even looking at him as he walks up to the truck, running his hand along the caved in front left fender, the headlight being held in by bailing wire and duct tape, where it had slammed into that yellow humvee, saving Meanstreak's life. He walks down the driver's side and stops, gazing at the spray painted words 'I LOVE YOU OPTIMUS JUNIOR!' which had been painted there by Anguish... while Anguish was travelling with Ed. Trashy looks down at the ground for a moment. Ed... He looks back up at the scrawlings of that mental midget. Where are you now, Ed? Come back... He can't. He's dead. The master set you on him and you ended him. He deserved it. All glory to the master. My life for him! Trashy shakes out of his reverie and opens the driver's side door. He steps up into the truck and shuts the door. He gives a slight smile as he pulls out the keyring that Alan Kriegman gave him and slides one into the ignition. He turns it over and grins at the rumbling sounds of the motor. 'Denver, here I come!'
