A Harder Side- Part 1

9 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/9/02 A Local Diner: A Harder Side- Part 1 Camera comes in to Michael Dame and Gloria, having lunch at a local diner. Dame is dress as usual, and Gloria in a short black skirt, and tight red top. Her hair is also done to perfection. The two exchanged conversation.. 'So, How do you feel about your first Main event booking this week, Michael?' Gloria asks as she bends down to take a sip of her soup. Her beautiful cleavage now exposed. 'It's a chance to really get in the spotlight Gloria. The main spotlight. It's also a chance to finish this Bones thing once and for all' .. He takes a piece of bread to his mouth. 'Bones is finished' Glroria Replies. 'You taught him his lesson Saturday night, I doubt he'll cross your path again, he if he has any brains he would do as I say' 'Bones...have brains? Please, the guy will probally try and kidnap me. But, this is what to expect from the wrestling world. It pisses me off to no end knowing that I want the 'storylines' to be circulated around the matches. Not scams, lyes, and cheats to help you gain the advantage, but what can we expect from a stable such as 3B.' Dame concludes. 'Yah, you hit it on the button, but do you have any gameplans for this week. You're going to be in the ring with all 3 members of 3B, and if you're lucky, Frankie will be crippled still.' Dame cuts her off. 'Yeah, I threw him with no remorse... The bastard deserved it.' 'Yeah, and Like I was saying, you should have a plan.' 'Well, as much as I hate to say it, I'll rely on my team. I believe, and this is a very thin thought, but I believe all 3 of my partners are going to be in there for a reason, and that is to take out the opposition, so have the plate stacked as you will. I will finish this 3B fiascal forever! ' 'And what should we do about Hardy?' 'Oh god this kid cracks me up. I swear, I really felt torn when they screwed me the first time, then I felt like I was going to pop when they screwed me the second time, now my feelings towards the kid are simple. If he were to jump off a bridge, I wouldnt drop my video recorded to go save him. I'd rather watch through my zoom lens and laugh my ass off. The kid deserves to be...well, hurt. I'll let 3B take care of that when they realize they don't need them.' 'You know Michael, this attitude of yours is really shaping up well. I like the way you're presenting your ways.' Gloria exclaims. 'Oh no, Gloria, that is where you're wrong. My ways have not changed a bit, although my feelings towards a certain select group of people have and always will be the same. And that is 3B deserves to rot in hell.' Gloria looks up into Michael's eyes with an agressive look. A determined look. A look that can be explained as... 'Hard'...as the camera fades to black.
