Friends & Family (Part 1)

1 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 1st 'Deep in the abyss where the waters run, As deep as the land of the Rising Sun, you know I'm down. 'Cause even when the odds are against us, it doesn't even matter, nothin else matters.'- Trik Turner 'Friends & Family' A family should be a very important thing to a man..or anyone else's heart. Money and friends may come and go but family was forever whether you liked it or not. But for a cold and unpassionate man like Drew Hostyle, who knows no other family besides his cult, the Benefit or maybe even with his fellow members of Brooklyn Born and Bred, who gives a damn about family? He'd already shared to the world his feelings about his mother, hadn't he? Spitting on her grave wasn't an everyday token of appreciation or something. He hated her, and his Dad, wherever he may be, Drew hated him too. So there was no one else to hate, besides the Red Army and maybe Ed No sac, who pretended everyweek to run a muck in the OSW..or were there? Well, there was one other person who was apart of Drew's immediate friends and family. His little brother, Jordan. He hadn't been around in Drew's life though for what we've seen here on OSW TV in...forever. But Jordan'd created his own life in the PTC, out of scratch. He'd been apart of the New True Internet Championship Wrestling for awhile now, and he didn't show any signs of interest of meeting his big brother, or..did we in TV land miss this? I mean if we don't see it then it doesn't happen, riiight? Pffft, we don't see a lot of things here and I'm guessing 15% is a big exaggeration on what we ever will continue to see that happens behind closed doors in the Old School Wrestling federation. However, one fan thinks they can see the whole deal they know what's going on 24/7 and share it with you and your impressionable youth..every other day. This girl is Amy 'Aimz' Campbell. Now, who knows what could've started this flare backstage or between the two, Aimz and Drew? But whatever it was had landed them a match this coming Saturday at Slam! Maybe a little lover's quarrel? Perhaps a small misunderstanding? Or even..try and imagine this..maybe the two never had good intentions to start with. Aimz was..well she was the exact opposite of everything that Drew was, and it was destined to be that way from the start. Amy Campbell was a twenty year old Canadian rebell-ish chick, with more appeal than those baggy red pants and lame ass insults let on..but that's off the subject. Drew was exactly the contrary, he was black. Heh, there was no doubt about THAT. He was a geniune hoodrat from the place with the majority of the minority, Brooklyn, New York. But enough with the obvious. Here's something not so oblivious to everyone. Drew wasn't a man to hold grudges against others, nope, not at all. He was a man of forgiveness to everyone. But the terms of forgiveness were...grave to say the least. Maybe it wasn't even worth forgiveness once you had earned it from the man, but Amy Campbell? She was begging for it. Let's see..thinking about the man in her sleep? There's a sign in there somewhere. Shouldn't she be thinking about Rent-A-Homie more intently? Oh no..wait her boyfriend was the homie, who's not treating her right but RAH is...no wait she likes RAH but..ah, f*ck it. The b*tch went mental a long time ago when she accepted the match. Besides the misunderstandings and insults I'm sure there's a whole bunch more layers to her bitchiness, but those aren't layers I'm willing expose. Or..heh, maybe I am. You know what they say, when opportunity comes a knockin.. What? Hell, the chick is basically giving herself an ego booster day by day through insisting that she has some completeness in her life after taking multiple gang bangings that I'm sure she enjoyed, because I haven't seen her fret over them yet. Her insults to 3B and her assumptions that Switchblade carries me? They're nothing, her words are clean. Not pure..but clean. They've been filtered through that bad girl type image and her obsession with looking at Drew's lower portion on average to measure the length of his..shorts. Clean. You know kind of like that song, 'Hands Clean' by Alanis Morisette. Haven't heard of it? Well I heard she likes it, almost as much as she likes being squeezed by some ape or whatever the hell else she squeezes in her spare time. But enough of her, back to OUR hero. OUR proven hero by not beating down just some trick. But by beating THE trick. Not in some official match, type sh*t, but when he felt like it or when he felt the need too. There was no leash on him, literally or figuratively. He could break free anytime he wanted for the OSW, from the Jane and Sebastion show or..Slam as you so vaguely call it. But he stayed for the perks. The perks, shuting up loud mouthed s-him's whose words and insults can't back their size or worth. The perks, having a part in controlling how things happened in the show who got bloodied next, who'd be crying next. So many choices, and oh, so many perks. When would they stop? The scene opens to show Drew Hostyle sitting on a black leather couch in a completely black room, holding in his hand a tiny black remote with red buttons that were flashing continuously. Drew had an intense but unwaivering look on his face that suggested that he'd been sitting there for awhile. The slump in the couch that had him sitting almost utterly on the floor suggested the very same thing. Timo points at Aimz, her arm hanging out for the tag, and the crowd go nuts. Timo slaps Aimz palm, and Aimz enters the ring, ducking a clothesline from Sebastian Toomes who has neatly stepped around referee Betty Lovejoy, and goes into the rising Drew Hostyle with a flurry of punches and forearms. Despite this, Hostyle gets to his feet. Aimz reaches out and whips Hostyle to the ropes and quicker than lightning takes him over with a hurricanrana. Aimz backflips to her feet, pumps herself up, then rushes towards Toomes corner and knocks him face first off the apron into the guardrail. Aimz bounces off the ropes again but is brought up short by the slithering arm of Frankie Bones coming in under the bottom rope and tripping her up. Nate: COME ON BETTY, DO YOU DAMN JOB!!! Robbie: She gives good jobs each and every time!!!! Drew Hostyle gets to his feet again, a seriously pissed off look on his face. Seeing Aimz lying motionless face down, his look changes from pissed off to sadistic, and he yanks Aimz to her feet by the hair. 'She'd probably never been man handled before...ah, there's a first time for everything. And I thought she'd had enough, always begging for more though. But then again what white girls don't beg, stereotypical tricks..' Drew grunted while curling his face more in the direction of the TV screen. Nate: Does he think he’s a tough guy?!?! She’s all of 140 lbs!!! 'And I'm all of 265, ya f*ck with me I'll use it to the best of ma ability.' He retorted with satisfaction to Nate, to which he obviously couldn't answer back. Hostyle ignores the protests of referee Betty Lovejoy to let go of the hair as he reels Aimz in and plants her with a Last Breath powerbomb before Lovejoy can reach the mandatory 5 count. Aimz clutches her belly, fighting for air. With a few well placed kicks that send Aimz body a few feet off the mat with each blow, Hostyle positions Aimz' head under the bottom rope, then springs up to the top rope and lands Mo' Money (Gullotine Leg Drop) across Aimz exposed neck. He then leaps to his feet and poses for the crowd. Nate: This is a travesty!!! Look at Hostyle celebrating like he’s won the superbowl!!! What a jackass!!! 'More like leading the game with 2:00 minutes to go, Nathaniel. Heh, who'd have thunk she had some fight left in her, not to mention the bodily fluids from that Samoan probably kept her on a high. Whore.' Drew commented. Hostyle tags in Frankie Bones, who sneers over at the enraged Timo Bolamba momentarily before picking up the almost comatose Aimz and lining her up for a suplex. Aimz up.................. Aimz DOWN! A cruel smile crosses Bones' face as he picks up Aimz and snap suplexes her over five times in quick succession. Bones covers Aimz and Betty Lovejoy drops to make the count...1.........2.........Bones pulls Aimz up by the hair, then to her feet, then throws her to the corner, following up with a Trouble Past spear, then tags in the Heavyweight Champion, Sebastian 'Switchblade' Toomes. Nate: (solemnly) I think this match is over. Aimz can’t take anymore, and she is not moving after being hit with that nasty spear. 'She looks more lively now then she ever does or will after Saturday. Take a Kodiak or somethin, those were the good times.' Drew declared to the Telvision again, matter of factly. Toomes climbs through the ropes and immediately flips off Timo, who is going ballistic in his corner trying to inspire Aimz into some form of movement. Toomes poses to the crowd, completely ignoring the crumpled Aimz. Timo, enraged, comes through the ropes and attacks Toomes, but is sent back to his corner by Lovejoy, allowing Hostyle and Bones to pull Aimz up by the hair to her feet and hold her out to Toomes. Toomes continues to taunt Timo, who is being held in the corner by Lovejoy. Bones and Hostyle let go of Aimz hair, allowing her to stagger out of the corner and try to make the tag. Nate: Oh man, Aimz is out on her feet. Robbie: Welcome to the big leagues, Miss Campbell. 'I'll be sure to give her a more proper welcome to the OSW this at Slam! Hell this is her first singles match up, I might as well make it worth her time to show up, if she even does that much to help her sinking career.' Drew says. Toomes sees the dazed Aimz wandering past him towards Timo, and allows her to get one foot away from Timo's outstretched arm before yanking her backward by the hair, sending her crashing to the mat, butt first. The crowd boos. Nate sighs in disgust. Betty Lovejoy again lectures Toomes on the hair pulling, allowing Timo to encourage Aimz to try to get to her feet. The loud roar from the crowd sees Aimz to her knees, and she starts to crawl, but in the wrong direction. Toomes ignoring her protests, shoves Betty Lovejoy aside and reaches for Aimz, but Aimz crawls between his legs and launches herself through the air and lands a tag on Timo's outstretched arm! The crowd explodes. Nate: TAG OUT TO TIMO!!! TAG OUT TO TIMO!!!! Robbie: WHAT?!?! THAT WASN’T A CLEAN TAG!!! Timo comes into the ring like a house of fire, sending Switchblade to the mat with a flurry of lefts and rights. Timo ducks a lariat by Frankie Bones and dropkicks Drew Hostyle outside the ring, then turns and hits Bones with a German Suplex which sends him rolling out the ring after Hostyle. Nate: TIMO JUST TOOK OUT BONES AND HOSTYLE!!! NOW ITS SWITCHBLADE’S TURN TO FEEL THE PAIN!!! Timo turns towards Toomes but is greeted by a boot to the face. The Crowd: Ooooooooo!!! Timo goes down hard, but gets to his feet immediately and with presence of mind ducks another boot of similar force. Timo and Toomes turn at the same time, but Timo is the quicker of the two and lands the Cave In on Toomes, sending him gasping to the mat. Robbie: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!! Nate: THE CAVE IN!!!! THE CAVE IN!!! TOOMES IS DOWN!!!! Timo climbs to the top rope and signals for the Fist of the Heavens. Nate: TIMO IS UP TOP!!! Robbie: GET UP SEA-BASS!!!! Nate: WE COULD HAVE A NEW CHAMPION!!! RIGHT HERE!!! RIGHT NOW!!!! 'Heh, fat chance' Drew answered already knowing the outcome from this match a mere two weeks ago. Jane Reagan jumps up onto the apron in order to dislodge Timo, but is in turn taken out by the recovering Aimz. Both women catfight up the aisle, clawing and screeching at each other, much to the delight of the crowd. In the ring, Timo launches himself into the air and lands FIST FROM THE HEAVENS on Toomes! Timo covers Switchblade and calls for Lovejoy to make the count. The crowd explodes. Nate: WE ARE GOING TO HAVE A NEW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION!!! Betty Lovejoy drops awkwardly to her knees and starts counting out Toomes....1...... 2......Betty Lovejoy is yanked from position by Drew Hostyle before being able to make the third count, and at the same time Frankie Bones drops an elbow across the back of Timo's head. Nate: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!! Betty Lovejoy gets to her feet and calls for the bell. Nate: BETTY JUST DISQUALIFIED SWITCHBLADE!!!! TIMO WINS!!! TIMO WINS!!!! Robbie: Heh…Unless the rules have changed, the title is not changing hands on a Disqualification. 'Is Decker always this knowledable? Someone give this guy a raise. Not only is he a good resource of information to greats like me, but he helped the dumbass fans put it together too. They had no effect that night just like they wouldn't the next week or the next. It wasn't commentary or crowd reaction that kept the matches moving, it was the sheer brillance of the men ionvolved. Me, my boys Frankie B and Sea Bass and of course the Boss keep the show on it's two feet, what would it look like if I, a founder of Slam! at it's finest were to lose to Aimz of all women? It's ain't hap'n.' Hostyle and Bones help the wheezing Toomes to his feet and all three men start stomping on Timo Bolamba. The crowd is outraged. So is Nate. Nate: THIS IS JUST MORE CRAP FROM A CRAP WEASEL OF A CHAMPION!!! THIS IS A JOKE!!!! Outside the ring, Aimz is taking it to Jane Reagan with punches and kicks. Suddenly, out of a moment of desperation, Jane rakes Aimz with her nails, right in the face, momentarily blinding her. Aimz falls back, but Jane wastes no time, suddenly producing a pair of handcuffs. Nate: WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON?!?! Robbie: Bondage?!?! Jane wields the cuffs like and old pro, handcuffing Aimz to a nearby guardrail. Aimz screams out in frustration, kicking and screaming at Jane, who gives the camera her trademark thumbs up signal. 'The prime example of a kind and moral woman. Now wouldn't all be right with the world if Amy were to give her a teenty bit of credit for keepin all da hoes and crackas in check? It's not an easy job you know, so when she gets the chance to get rid of one problem without getting her hands dirty..you bet your ass I'd be happy to. Not cuz I'm her lil' puppy and do as I'm told, but because I like dat.' Drew smirks. Nate: This is a travesty… In the ring, Toomes makes a breaking motion with his hands and points down at Timo, upon which Bones and Hostyle pick him up, set him up and then drop him hard in a brutal double neck breaker. Timo offers no resistance while the three men continue to perform various combination moves on him designed to damage as many moveable body parts as possible. The crowd is enraged. Police and security officials are shown stopping several fans from jumping the guardrails. Debris is peppering the ring. Nate: 3B AND SWITCHBLADE ARE DISMANTLING TIMO BOLAMBA IN THE RING!!! THIS IS DISGUSTING!!!! Jane Reagan re-enters the ring, upon which the camera pans up the rampway and spies Aimz handcuffed to a guardrail, unable to mount any kind of rescue attempt for her partner Timo Bolamba. Reagan flies into Toomes arms at the exact moment the match result is announced... Earl: Your winner, as a result of a disqualification, the team of Aimz and the Samoan Silencer, Timo Bolamba! Still OSW Heavyweight Champion, Sebastian 'Switchblade' Toomes! The crowd who hadn’t already realized the ultimate outcome, now do, causing more debris and trash to be hurled at the ring in outrage. Upon hearing that he had retained the Heavyweight Title, Toomes celebrates by picking up Timo's legs and locking him in the Controlled Burn while Hostyle, Bones and Jane Reagan celebrate around him. Nate: SOMEONE!!! ANYONE STOP THIS!!!! Click. 'That's enough, enough with the calling for help, for your heroes, while you overlook the biggest one der is. Help is HERE. Help isn't Bob, it's not the Red Army. Help is ME, Drew Hostyle. 3B is help. I'll help to rid of the fish we smell everyday, and I'm taking a new approach to this one. Straight beating didn't work, neight did showing her all the advantages I have over her last Saturday. But embarassment will work. It will stop her in her tracks, make her open her eyes even more so then they do when I kicked her ass the first time.' Drew comtemplated. 'That'll do it...that'll work.' Drew said to himself. Ring, ring, ring Ugh. More tapes to review..who the f*ck is this..Drew looks on the Caller ID icon that's been built into the phone. Drew's face brightens from the frown it's been in and he slightly cracks a smile as he reads the name. 'Yo' Drew says with the most enthusiasm we've heard all day as the scene fades to black.
