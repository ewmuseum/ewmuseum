My First Rebellion [My Life - Part 2]

14 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 14th, 2002 Rebel 1.) To refuse allegiance to and oppose by force an established government or ruling authority. 2.) To resist or defy an authority or a generally accepted convention. 3.) To feel or express strong unwillingness or repugnance. --------------------- I remember the eyes. The eyes of my boss, a man who owned my battlefield, a man who believed he owned me. This man was a man filled with controversy and a man filled with an ego the size of a full planet. He was a man who was blinded by his arrogance and his greed, and a man whose eyes went wide open...once he peered into mine own eyes. I remember his face, a face of horror and shock, as he looked into my face. A face of rage and murder. He realized what he got himself into, but knew there was no way out. He looked over at his tag team partners at the opposite end of the ring, and quickly raced towards his corner. I smirked in joy, for the fear of this man...made my own ego grow...made my intentions even more cruel. I allowed him to get within two feet of his partner's hand...before I grabbed him and hurled him across the mat. I smiled widely as he sat in the center of the battlefield, slowly backing away from me. I shot a look at my partners, who began bickering....worthless. I took my eyes of my partners and starred into the eyes of the frightened dictator. He begged for forgiveness, saying he didn't wish to give me such a hard time...but his begs were ignored by myself...as I slowly advanced towards him. Every step closer I took, another tear of fear rolled down his cheek. A usual person would feel mercy and remorse for this man. A usual person would allow this man to be free.... But I'm not a usual person. I grabbed him by his throat, lifted him up high off the ground, and dropped him down with all the force I had left in me. I saw his entire body compress like an accordian, and his eyes glaze over. I was having fun. I grabbed him by his head once more and lifted him up. I remember looking at the audience, and watching as each and every one of them cheered wildly. It was the first time I was cheered and liked... And I hated it. I didn't want to be loved. Love is a mental weakness within human beings, and I would NOT be weak. I would NOT be one of them. I refused to be! And thus I took it out...on this dictator. I threw him down with a standing clothesline, watching his head jerk back from impact. I watched as he fell to the mat, and remained there...motionless. His partners rushed to the ring, but I was prepared. I grabbed the one who considered himself an authority, and hurled him out of MY ring. The last partner charged at me with a heavy amount of rage, but rage can also be blinding....he found that out the hard way...after I planted him with a powerslam, almost cracking his back in half. I was having fun. I looked away from the two partners, watching as the owner began to escape...walking up the rampway. I wouldn't let him get away...I wouldn't let him take away my fun. I quickly exited the battle and grabbed him before he could reach the backstage area. I held him in a front face lock and drove his thick skull into the steel mesh of the stage. I watched as crimson began to slowly drool down his forehead and onto the steel mesh. I smiled. I was having fun. 'You are weak,' I remember yelling out, 'You had NO chance against me...what made you think you had ANY sort of a chance against a warrior like me?' I remember the look in his face. He was horrified and scared. He feared his life. He was so filled with fear, he couldn't mutter out an answer, or a plea for forgiveness. Maybe because I brought my fist down into his face before he even had a chance. But why not? I was having fun. I grabbed him by his scrawny little neck and hurled him down the rampway. I watched as his body rolled down like a ragdoll. It pleased me to see he was enduring so much pain, and not giving up....because it made it more fun. And I was having fun. I grabbed this weakling by his head and drove his skull into the steel steps, leaving a crimson stain on the steps. A crimson liquid slowly dripped from his forehead as he begged me off. He went on his knees and actually begged....and that...made me mad. He was showing weakness...and that is something I do not tolerate. I answered his begging with a stiff kick to his face, knocking him flat on his back and into a pool of his own crimson and sweat. I looked down at this fallen foe, and grinned sadistically...this is what my mission was about. Destroying the weak, and making the battlefield stronger. Than suddenly, the bell had rung. I shifted my attention to the ring, to watch one of my partners get pinned by the law making bastard. I stared into the ring, but for some reason I wasn't angry. I wasn't sad. I wasn't insane. I was pleased. Not because I lost. But because I won. I accomplished what I wanted. I was a rebel. I rebelled against the weak dictator of this battle...and beat him to oblivion. I looked down at him once more, watching as a pool of blood was coughed up from his insides. And I smiled. I was having fun. --------------------- 'That was the first rebellion I ever took place in. I destroyed an owner of my first big federation, I showed him exactly what I was about. I showed the world what I was about. I wasn't about pleasing anyone else, but myself. I had a mission...to destroy anything that was weak...to make the battlefield stronger...and more exciting. Because that's what I am. I am the strongest of men on that battlefield. I fight because it is my life. It is what I was born for...what I breathe for...and what I shall die for. It's my life... Because it makes life...all that more exciting. Will I rebel once more? Will you see me rebel once more? Only fate has that answer. But does OSW control that fate? Time Will Tell.' You Live, You Breathe, You Die...if only life was that simple
