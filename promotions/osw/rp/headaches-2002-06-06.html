Headaches

6 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/06/02 A bright blinding light moves from side to side. Back and forth. Slow at first, then switching to fast, snap movements. “Keep your eyes focused on the wall over there,” orders the doctor as he looks deep into Jim’s eyes. Axtell sits motionless, back straight as a board, staring at the wall across the room as he endures the doctor’s examination. A bandage surrounds Jim’s head and bruises adorn his face under each eye, a result of the heinous and cowardly attack from Kory Storm on SLAM! “Damn that’s bright,” complains Jim, squinting somewhat. “Just…about….done,” says the doctor as he turns the pen-light off. He spins around on his swivel chair and jots some notes down in the file that is setting on the counter behind him. When he is finished he swivels back around to face Jim. “You have a slight concussion, nothing to get too worried about. The lacerations and bruising on your head could have been a lot worse. You got pretty lucky, Mr. Axtell. What exactly were you doing getting into a fight with a sledgehammer anyway?” The doctor smirks slightly as his joke, which causes Jim to do the same. “I wish it were that easy, doc,” replies Jim. He grimaces somewhat from the pounding headache that he still has from Saturday. “Some coward blindsided me with it, and then proceeded to give me an up close and personal view of it several times over.” The doctor purses his lips and cocks his head slightly. “Gotta be careful in those bar fights, Mr. Axtell.” “Oh, it wasn’t a bar fight,” explains Jim. “I work for Old School Wrestling as a security guard and just recently got added to the roster. One of the guys didn’t take too kindly to the attention I was getting and he wasn’t, so he decided to try and do something about it. Too bad for him he didn’t finish the job.” The doctor nods his head knowingly. “Ahhh, a professional wrestler. I’ve seen many of you before. Concussions, separations, contusions, lacerations…you name it and I’ve probably seen it. Are you sure that’s what you want to do?” Jim looks the doctor dead in his eyes. “I’m as sure as I’ve ever been about anything. When I decided to do this, leaving my wife at home and pursuing this career, I made the decision to go all the way with it. I’m not going to go half-assed or let some punk with an ego problem deter me from my goals. When I start something I don’t quit, and I came to OSW to be the best I can. Kory Storm or no Kory Storm, that’s exactly what I plan to do,” says Jim defiantly. “Well, I wish you all the best and odds are I will see you again sometime,” the doctor says as he stands from his seat. Jim stands as well, his examination over. The two men shake hands. “Thanks, doc,” Jim says appreciatively. “No problem. The nurse at the front counter will have some paperwork for you to fill out before you leave.” The doctor grabs the file from the counter and leaves the room. Jim gathers his coat from a chair in the corner and follows him out. As he walks down the hallway Jim suddenly clutches his head and staggers a bit. This goes unnoticed by the doctor and Jim doesn’t say anything to bring it to his attention. The two men continue on down the hallway and to the waiting area. The doctor drops Jim’s file on the counter and shoots him a cordial smile as he leaves to the back once again. “All done Mr. Axtell?” asks the older female receptionist. Her standard white uniform is filled to the max with her large frame. Her gray hair is permed tightly and her grandma-ish looks fit her to a “T”. “Yes ma’am,” replies Jim with a smile. “Just sign this last form and we’ll get you on your way.” The woman shoves a document in front of Jim which he immediately signs. “Thanks sweetie!” “Thank you, ma’am,” Jim says kindly before he turns and walks out of the office.
