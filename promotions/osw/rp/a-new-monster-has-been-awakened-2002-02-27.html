A new Monster Has Been Awakened!

27 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-27-02 The voices of panicked paramedics are echoing off of the cedar black walls that line the hallway of the arena. “Jesus Christ, is that a lot of blood! Someone stabilize his head! Get the ambulance around back immediately! This guys face looks really bad!” All of the paramedics are gathered around a small figure that is bleeding profusely and barely moving at all. Equipment is being handed back and forth between the EMTs in an attempt to stabilize the fallen figure so that they can move him. People are in a frenzy in the hallway trying to figure out exactly what had just happened. All of the paramedics’ gloves are covered in blood from the victim of a vicious attack. A massive figure is moving down the hall towards the mass of people. The figure is Azrael Cain. He is wearing his black and tattered cloak. He himself is in a frenzy as a result of Meanstreak's attack on him and his manager Caleb. As Cain gets closer to the congregation of EMTs that are taking care of the small-injured hobo, a security guard tries to stop him. “Hold on a just one second there, buddy.” the security guard orders. Unfortunately though, Cain pays no attention to the overweight man. The enraged beast sprints past the guard towards Caleb. Cain finally gets to the mass of paramedics that have the injured hobo surrounded and begins tossing people aside trying get to his fallen comrade. One of the EMTs grab Cain by the arm though trying to get his attention long enough to see how he is doing. “And how are you feeling? That was one hell of a nasty lick that you just took there!” The paramedic asks. The furious Cain releases a feral growl at the curious EMT before he answers the ridiculous question. “You honestly believe that I could be hurt by a that spineless whelp who inflicts his strength on those that are weaker than him!” Cain then shoves the EMT to the ground. The EMT hits the pavement floor with a loud thud. Cain continues to throw people aside until he finally sees Caleb lying on the pavement floor in a pool of his own blood. Cain’s anger consumes him completely and a look emerges on his face that sends chills down the spine of all those looking on. Cain reaches down and grabs his manager by the back of his head and begins to talk to him. “Caleb! Get up!” There is no response from the hobo that lays there with his toothless mouth hanging open. “Caleb!” Cain screams again. A paramedic reaches down and grabs the warrior’s shoulder as he tries to console him. “Listen pal, he isn’t good. We need to take him to the hospital and get him checked out.” “No!” Cain screams at the top of his lungs as he grabs the EMTs hand and twists it around. “I don’t need consoling form the likes of you! And he will not be going anywhere with anyone, but me!” The EMT is no screaming in pain form the pain that is being inflicted upon him by the frothing beast that is now Azrael Cain. “He doesn’t need your medicines or your care! He will come home with me!” There is a moment of silence that fills the hallway for the first time since the attack. The paramedics are looking at one another with a confused look on their faces and are not sure what to say next. Finally, one of the men breaks the silence. “I’m sorry, but we can’t let you…” The foolish paramedic is interrupted by Cain who has risen and is now screaming in his face. “And who are you to say what I ca and can not do?” The paramedic is speechless. Cain shoves the paramedic to the concrete floor just like the other one. Azrael is now over top of Caleb. Cain removes the black cloak that he is wearing and begins to wrap it around the hobo’s small and fragile frame. The blood from Caleb is immediately being soaked up in the woven cloth of the cloak. Cain picks his manager up and begins to carry him down the dark hallway towards the exit. As he progresses down the hallway people are clearing a way for him to exit. Cain eventually disappears into the darkness carrying his injured manager in his arms like a mother carrying a dying child. The scene changes suddenly. Cain is now lying on the floor of the dirty and dilapidated factory that he calls home, sleeping. He is wearing his traditional wrestling outfit, but he is using his cloak that is stand with Caleb’s blood over him as a blanket. Cain arises abruptly from his slumber. His long black hair is hanging down in his face. He begins to stare at something. Across a burning fire he sees the figure of his manager, Caleb, asleep. The hobo is wrapped in Cain’s bearskin cloak. The hobo’s face is covered in rags that are wrapped like bandages. Some of them show traces of blood seeping through. The hobo lies motionless on the floor amongst the dirt amongst the debris. Cain sits up, but now stares blankly at the flickering fire wrapped up in his own thoughts. “The impudent dog! What kind of man is it that hides his vicious ways behind a mask of righteousness? He has all of the mindless masses fooled! He is actually cut from a similar mold as I, but yet the ignorant people love him and praise the carnage that he unleashes! Those words that were said were mine, not Caleb’s! So what does that coward do? He takes his anger out on defenseless old man because he knows that he can’t hurt me!” Caleb begins to move a little bit, which causes Cain to leave his thoughts for a moment and direct his focus to his injured manager. There is an unsettling silence as Cain hopes to see some sort of movement from the small figure, but all of his hopes are shot down. Caleb remains motionless again except for the bobbing of his chest as he breathes in his sleep. Cain’s anger is growing as his thoughts about Meanstreak and this week’s match begin to flood his already dark mind. His expression turns sinister and menacing as his thoughts fill his head again. “This week will be a different story! This week I not only fight Meanstreak and those insistent annoyances, Civil Disobedience, but I also fight to unmask the cretin for what he really is! The fools in the front office thought that it would be smart to schedule Heresian Bloodlust and that loud-mouthed Ivan Stanislav together in the same match! Meanstreak couldn’t handle Hessian alone! When his nemesis and OSW’s most fierce and powerful duo collide this week, his screams of pain will reach the heavens! And there will be no one there to show him mercy, but me, the Dark Angel! And I will personally see to it that this spectacle will be on that no one will ever forget!” Cain’s anger erupts in a fit of rage. The frothing beast reaches over and hoists his thirty pound sledgehammer up into the air only to bring it crashing down onto some of the larger pieces of metal that are scattered around him. A loud clang sound echoes throughout the factory. Cain’s breaths are wild and sporadic. His eyes are filled with hatred. His thoughts are filled with carnage and are ripe with mischief. “This week is personal! I want nothing more than put that dog in his place and no one will get in my way from accomplishing my goal! Not even that Russian nuisance, Ivan Stanislav better get in the way of my destruction of Meanstreak and his lap dogs, Civil Disobedience! This week, Meanstreak is going to learn that there is a new monster in OSW and his name his Azrael Cain!”
