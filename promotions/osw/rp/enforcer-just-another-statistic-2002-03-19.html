Enforcer: Just Another Statistic....

19 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19.03.02 Europe. United Kingdom. Scotland. Glasgow. That was the directions he was given. He knew fine well where Europe was: he lived there. He'd visited the United Kingdom before. He'd wrestled in some town called London, or something like that. Scotland was a different territory though. Never before had he ventured into the country known for its wild nights, crazy men and sexually active sheep. He'd heard things about Glasgow too. Mad drug dealers, easy prostitutes, hotheaded youths all roaming the streets. I'm not sure about this. What if these crazy Scots people hate Communism...Don't be so soft Krieg! You are Communist! You stand up for Communism! They can not crumble the foundations of Communism! If anything, you reinforce the power of Communism and show these uncivilised people why Communism is the political standpoint of the future! As he thought about the consquences of his presence, Krieg walked down a dimly lit corridor behind two security guards, a couple of policemen also walking behind him. Approaching an entryway, light burst through the opening, presumably the way out of the tunnel. The two security guards walked ahead, checking that everything was Ok before allowing Krieg to enter whatever kind of building they were in. Walking out of the darkness and into the light, Krieg's eyes adjusted to the light change, catching the various signs around what looked to be a music store. Banners proclaiming, 'The German War Machine, Michel Krieg, is here!' and 'Communism's figurehead, right here in Virgin Megastores, Glasgow!'. As he approached a table, he noticed a hoard of fans gathering around record stands about 50ft away from the table. Sitting down on a metal chair, Krieg shifts his neck, cracking it a couple of times as he does, and begins to take his 'OSW: SLAM!' sweatshirt off, sitting in on the back of his chair. As Virgin employees pull back a small fence sepating the fans from Krieg, a couple of young males shuffle towards the table, wearing OSW T-shirts bearing names like 'The Red Army', 'Sebastian Toomes is OSW!' and 'Aimz = HOT!' Suddenly, an OSW magazine, with the face of Sebastian Toomes on the cover, flies down onto the table, a young boy with his hand still holding it. “Here, maun. Kin you sign this fir’ me, maun,” asked a tracksuit wearing 15 year-old, looking a bit rough and slightly ‘hard’ as Glaswegian’s say. “Emm. I was expecting a please somewhere in there, boy, but since,” Krieg is suddenly cut off, “Haw man! Don’t mess me aboot! I don’t think ye know who am are, day ye? Am Davie Bell! Fae Duke Street, might a’ add,” squeals the youth. “Am I supposed to be impressed by that? I mean, if Duke Street some sort of infamous gangland,” questions Krieg. Slightly confused by a higher English language, the Ned* reverts back to laymen English, “No, ma-nn. Don’t mess me aroon, au’right? Jist sign the magazine an’ al’ be on ma’ way,” *Ned – Glasgow scumbag, known for wearing cheap tracksuits, baseball caps at a 45 degree angle from their head, lots of cheap jewellery and sports socks pulled up over their tracksuit bottoms. Doing as the Ned says, Krieg signs the magazine: “Nice to meet you, you uncivilised little scumbag. When Communism rules Scotland, you will be the first on my list to be dealt with. Yours sincerely, Michel Krieg” On looking at the magazine, the youth’s face goes red with anger, and as he goes to lunge at Krieg, security guards are quick to drag him away, telling him he’s going to have his magazine taken off of him as if it’s some sort of mortal punishment. Sniggering, Krieg sits back in the chair and signs some more of the magazines as fans approach, giving fake smiles to the camera as caring parents capture an image of their son or daughter with their favourite OSW superstar on film. After ploughing through a mass of fans, still with about the same number remaining, Krieg calls for a break and walks over to the cafe, picking up a cold soda and sitting down in a private seating area of the cafe, reflecting on the “Enforce This!” tour so far. “I’ve been to Wales, I’ve been to Ireland, I’ve been to some country called England (Apparently a Queen lives there), and now I’m here in Scotland. I guess what I heard was true....No, not that Scottish women have more hair on their chests than Ivan Stanislav! That The Enforcer is stepping up to the plate with Michel Krieg this week on SLAM!,” says Krieg, sipping some of his cola before placing it back down on the glass covered table. “It seems that he thinks he can push his weight around, trying to muscle in on my good friend, Tom Walczak. Well, I can tell you one thing, Enforcer, Robocop, Judge Dredd, whatever the hell your name is. You will fall victim to a strike from the Red Army’s biggest juggernaut, Michel Krieg. As I always say, “There Will Be No Foreplay, There Will Be No Games” and that statement couldn’t be truer for this coming week on SLAM! Because when I walk down that ramp, you standing in the ring, I won’t be looking at my opponent. I’ll be looking at another statistic on the Red Army’s lists. Just one more man that the Red Army had to destroy to show their sheer power.” “It’s as simple as that, Robocop. You bear no meaning to me or to the Red Army. It will just be another practice match for me, just another test to see how long it takes me to demolish a up-and-comer. I’m not going to be wrestling at my best in our match, oh no. There is no need for Krieg to tire himself on someone who models himself around some fiction superhero. So, if you want to align yourself with some of the other jobbers around the OSW, go ahead, because if you even try to even the odds with Krieg by bring in another superstar, there will be hell to pay, because Krieg isn’t gonna get “All Hulked Up”, he isn’t gonna “Layeth The Smack Down”, he’s not gonna “Open A Can Of Whoop-Ass”...Krieg is going to simply show you why The Red Army Are Law, And You Are Crime,” roars Krieg, slamming his massive fist down onto the glass table, slightly cracking it. As he goes to get up to return to the autograph signing, Krieg remembers something and sits back down, looking intensely into the camera. “There’s one more thing. One more person I want to address before I go back to this rabid fans. Ramey. Jesse. Bad Attitude. You wrestled Walczak and I in a tag match with that imbecile, Rhett Locke. You barely touched me and as always, The Red Army was victorious over its foes. But you better remember: I’ve not forgotten about you, not by a long shot. I had my reasons for attacking you at Crowning Glory. It wasn’t a simple “Interference for the Red Army” as it usually is. I saw you, parading around the backstage area when I came to visit the OSW for the first time a week or so before Crowning Glory. You walked around as if you were something special, as if you had something no one else had. But you seem to constantly forget that you don’t have it all. You don’t have anything on The Red Army. The Two Round-Faced Women have more wrestling ability than you, and they’re women. I mean women, for Heaven’s sake! Women are lower than Capitali....Well, maybe not. But they are reasonably low in the greater picture. So Ramey, if you want to walk down that ramp, or hurdle that crowd barrier, on SLAM!, by all means do so, but whatever you do, don’t step into the ring with me because I will do things to you that have been banned in 17 countries. I will tear you apart, limb from limb, if you fancy your chances with Krieg. But Ramey, that isn’t a threat, oh no...That’s the Communist Truth, and you better remember it,” claims Michel, his face now slightly red and the veins in his arms bulging slightly from his clenched fists. Slowly standing up, Krieg walks away from the seating area and begins to walk back over to the table, the fans still cheering as Krieg sits down on the seat and flips off to them. The scene begins to fade as fans surge over to the table, Krieg’s face looking rather grim as he takes in the size of the crowd still waiting for autographs.
