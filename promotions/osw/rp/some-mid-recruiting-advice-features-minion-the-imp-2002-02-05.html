Some Mid-Recruiting Advice...[Features Minion the Imp!]

5 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2.5.02 Loneliness...Krieg was feeling the effects of being a genetic outcast as of late. Even though his antics with Walczak and the rest of the Army had lifted his spirits, the Berlin native was feeling down and there was one thing causing it....women. Being a German stud-muffin, Krieg was always expected to have the best women, but that was not always the case. You see, when Krieg got a date, he always ended up confusing the young lady by indulging in politics and Communism, frazzling the brain of any half-witted bimbo, 'I think it is time to seek advice from a pro,' said Krieg, getting up out of his chair in the middle of his hotel room. Grabbing the handle of the door, Krieg yanked it open and strode down the corridor, passing a good 20 or so doors before he reached number 666. Hammering his large paw on the door, a few seconds past before the door creaked open and the room's participant looked up at Krieg....Minion was dwarfed by the monster, looking confused at Krieg's presence, 'Can I help you, German,' asked Minion, reaching behind him for Cain's sledgehammer, 'Yes, you can, my friend...Krieg needs women help,' said the scornful German, 'Oh, come in, come in, ‘Michelle’,' replied Minion, motioning for Krieg to come into the room, Minion led Krieg into his lavish suite, a strange occurrence since Minion normally sticks with Caleb, Hessian and Cain. In any case, Krieg's mood was leading him to the room of an enemy's friend...and only god knows why, 'Minion, I wish to put aside our differences for a while...' 'I don't got no beef with you any more German...Hessian and Cain already beat you, so what ails ya?' replies Minion, jumping up into a big, pink furry chair, I appreciate your honesty, Heretic, and I desire help,' gawked Krieg, 'Right, the basics of women are simple: you treat them badly, they love you. It is simple,' answered a smiling Minion, leaning back in his seat, 'Emm...I am not as stupid and dumb on girls as I may look Minion...I know how to handle them...I just need to get one, that's all, and I thought you may be able to help me,' asked Krieg, 'Well ya know...ok...lemme level with ya ‘Michelle’. You look like a desperate man, so here's the low down. Women want a hulking brute of a man, you look pretty big yourself, so you got the physical thing down...' Minion squeaks at the big German, Krieg moves forward a little, strangely intrigued by the Imp's words, 'Now...the mental thing...and how to hook a bitch...erm...woman' Minion added, before cracking his knuckles and smiling slyly, 'Minion,' said Krieg, patting his hand on Minion's shoulder, 'I am ready to be your student if you will teach me the ways of a male lover,' Grinning, Minion stood up out of his chair and moved closer to Krieg, ''Michelle', it is time for you to learn the ways of a true lover, of a true sex god...Of Minion,' 'The Krieg-miester is ready,' roared Michel, standing to attention in front of Minion, Giving the first of his many tips, Minion squeaked to Krieg, 'Michel, drop the cheesy lines,' 'Yah, I see,' said Krieg, grinning down at Minion 'Let us hit the town, my pupil,' replied Minion, donning his dark cape and quickly skittering across the floor and swinging the door open as Krieg followed closely behind, The scene suddenly fades out and fades back in after a few seconds to a bar, somewhere in downtown Montreal, 'Ok, now basically the only way to do this is...erm...leadership by example, so sit here, look cool, and watch the master at work.' Minion whispered, jumping off of his bar stool and strutting across the bar, to a table of lovely ladies drinking and laughing, Krieg sits, and cocks his head in confusion, as Minion struts across to the table of ladies.........and trips and falls over, directly in front of them, Sauntering back over to Krieg, who is still watching intensely, Minion explains, 'That was...emmm...just an example of...what not to do,' Minion informed Krieg, 'You see, watch this, Grasshopp'a,' Minion hopped off his stool again and hobbled over to another group of girls, putting on a slight swagger, trying to look Pimp-like. But as he reaches the table and makes verbal contact with the ladies, two 'females' walk over to Minion and start to grope his chest and body. Looking both complimented and confused, Minion excuses himself and walks back over to Krieg, smiling slightly, 'Now, Krieg. This is how to really score with ladies,' said Minion, 'See these two fine ladies,' motioning to the two 'females', 'these are the kind of women you really want to hook up with....Now let Minion show you some techniques to use when you do get a lady,' Strutting back over to the two 'things', Minion escorts them over to a small booth and the two 'females' start to kiss him on the cheek. Looking over to Krieg, Minion slowly passes his hand down their bodies, towards their groins and as he reaches towards their....well...you know what, he finds something else, hardly ever associated with a female...a SCHLONG! Both 'females' have male genitals! The look on Minion's face is a Kodac classic, as he jumps up in the air and just about flies over the table, grabbing Krieg by the arm and hauling him towards the door, 'Minion,' said a confused Krieg, 'Aren't we staying for your two lady friends,' the two 'shemales' crashing out of the doors in pursuit of their Heretic lover, Minion, Running for his life, Minion sprints away from the bar and from Krieg, calling 'They're shemales!
