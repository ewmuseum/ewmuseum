Adventures in Hershey Pt. 2 'Nature Calls'

25 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-25-02 The Panda Palace restaurant was always a restaurant that Major Wang, more commonly known as Loyal Communist Party Member Wang, loved to visit. When he had heard that there was a Panda Palace near Hershey, Pennsylvania, he knew that he had to indulge in it’s beautiful Chinese buffet. Little did Ivan Stanislav and Loyal Communist Party Member Wang, the two men heading out into the countryside of Pennsylvania, realize what they were about to experience….. ~~ Ivan Stanislav and Loyal Communist Party Member Wang drove along the country road towards The Panda Palace Restaurant. Pennsylvania, the state with the worst roads in the country, proved to be a challenge for the aging Chinese officer. Between twisting roads, road kill, and back road hillbillies driving recklessly, Stanislav felt like he would puke up his borsch at any moment. Wang continued to drive the white knuckle gauntlet of wildlife and automobiles before disaster struck in the form of a… ….groundhog?? A groundhog, a rather harmless and elusive critter to say the least, rushed out into the twisting, pothole filled road! “DOG!!” Screamed Stanislav as the large, fat animal scurried out into the way of the large, red jeep. “AIEEEE!!” Wang screamed and jerked the wheel. With a screech he flew off the road, got a good amount of airtime, and crashed headlong into a tree branching out from a stream! Silence. The kind of stunned silence that accompanies death. For the groundhog, once full of life and vibrant, lie upside down in the middle of the road. As for the jeep, the front end was successfully turned into scrap metal. The drivers side airbag had deployed, stopping the smallish Major of the People Republic of China from flying through the windshield. And as for Stanislav? His hands had punched through the glove compartment as he braced himself, but remained unscathed. “We…we are….awive?? WE ARE AWIVE!” Screamed Wang insanely! Stanislav looked over his shoulder at the groundhog, unmoving, “At least that damned dog is dead.” And suddenly, the groundhog rolled over, and scurried away. Stanislav cursed and dislodged himself from the jeep, staggering into the stream. Wang undid his seatbelt and crawled out as well and walked over to Ivan, who stood right beside the stream. “Now what, Wang? Our Jeep is not going to work, what can we do? We are in middle of nowhere, without any way to contact our Comrades, and the Panda Palace is probably miles away!” muttered Ivan, wiping mud off the top of his shined boot. “Well..I bewieve that pahaps Panda Pawace is just a few miles away. Maybe we can just walk there.” Wang stood and looked around, “After all, it is nice day out, let us walk, eh Pwapowschik?” Ivan rubbed the back of his neck, “I do not wish to go on the road. Those people appear to be radicals, I do not wish to deal with them. Let us walk through this field.” Stanislav nodded towards the field ahead of him and began to walk with Wang beside him…… ~~ TWO HOURS LATER Wang and Stanislav continued to walk through the Pennsylvania countryside. They had vaulted a barbed wire fence about half an hour ago and walked through what seemed like farmland. Ahead appeared to be cows. “Pwapowchik…” Wang rasped, perspiring heavily. He had since unbuttoned his military jacket, revealing his white dress shirt underneath, “what is that?” He pointed ahead at a cow that had separated from the herd. “That, Wang,” Ivan muttered as he wiped his brow, “is a cow.” The two men continued to walk towards the cow. Stanislav continued to stand tall and straight, as per his years of rigorous training. But Wang toddled along beside The Russian Bear, dragging his feet slightly. Dirt caked both men’s faces as they approached the cow. Moooooo! Stanislav and Wang approached the cow as it sullenly turned and eyed the two men, not paying much attention to them, however. It swished its tail at a few flies and blinked. Wang’s eyes lit up suddenly. “I forgot!!” He yelped! “What?” Ivan groaned, fixing a twisted suspender. Wang fumbled in his pocket, “I have cellular phone!!” Wang grinned widely and pulled out a cell phone. Stanislav slapped his forehead. “You JUST remembered?” “I was thinking of the Panda.” Wang answered matter of factly. He started to fumble through his phone numbers and propped an elbow on the behind of the cow while Ivan turned and walked a few feet away. “I cannot believe this..what is Ivan doing here? I should be out fighting, I should be….” ~SQUISH~ Ivan closed his eyes slowly and growled before opening them and realizing what had happened. A cow pie, as they are known, is a name for a cows offal. Needless to say, Ivan’s foot just punctured the pie. Stanislav glared at his boot, now heavily caked in a cow crap. He growled and turned to look at Wang, who still fumbled with his phone. Stanislav kicked his foot angrily to free himself of the excrement, when it flew off the heel. The resulting incident was almost surreal, almost in slow motion. The piece of cow turd flew through the air, past Wang, and hit the cow in the side! MOOOO!!! Editors Note: This was not a normal, docile “moo” that a cow makes. But a moo that means she’s gonna make your life a helluva lot harder. The cow, spooked by the sudden assault of its own creation, grunted angrily and kicked backwards hitting the phone out of Wang’s hand!! Wang yelped and retracted his hand, barely missing the hoof of the cow. The phone flew through the air and landed on a rock feet away. Ivan and Wang turned and stared, stunned, shocked, and saddened that now, the phone was probably destroyed. Ivan, however, held on to hope and after a moment of silence, he looked at Wang, “Perhaps…..it still works.” FWOOSH As if God himself willed it, the phone, somewhat biblically, burst into flames. Stanislav and Wang gasped in astonishment as the phone spontaneously combusted before their eyes and their hopes of being rescued from the hell that was Southern Pennsylvanian farmland was dashed before their eyes. Wang blinked, still shocked by the sight, but Stanislav had since moved on to the culprit of the maiming of the phone: The cow. Ivan gritted his teeth and walked over beside the cow. In the background the herd of cattle behind him grazes stupidly, a few glancing over at Ivan, or perhaps just deciding to move their eyes in that direction. “Very funny, stupid cow!” With a roar, Stanislav pulled his fist back and decked the cow in the side of the head. MOOO!! Editors Note: This is the scream of a cow losing consciousness. Ivan stood with his hands on his hips and laughed as the cow collapsed to the ground, unconscious. Wang stood behind Ivan and stared at the background as the herd ran away in fear of the violent outburst. “Pwapowschik??” Wang muttered nervously and palmed behind him, trying to get Ivan’s attention. “Who is laughing now, hamburger? DYAAHAAHAA!!” “Pwa…Pwa…PWAPOWSCHIK!!!” Wang screamed and snapped Stanislav’s suspender. Ivan turned quickly as a bull, with his head lowered, growled at the two men. “It is okay Pwapowschik, only if they see wed…” Wang muttered, before staring at Ivan’s red suspenders…… ~~ THREE MINUTES LATER Ivan Stanislav and Loyal Communist Party Member Wang ran for their lives across the Pennsylvanian Farmland as an enraged bull charged headlong behind them. The two men screamed crazily. “You had to wear wed suspenders!!!” “You had to crash Jeep!!!” “You had to kick cow poop!!” “You had to eat at the Panda Palace!” “AHHHHH!!!!” MOOOOOOOO!!!!! The bull charged behind Stanislav and Wang, slowly gaining speed. The two Asians noticed the edge of the fence in the distance. “Ivan..we are..awmost…there!!” “Keep running and shut up Wang!!” Wang lagged behind as the red faced bull bulleted towards them. Stanislav grabbed Wang under his arm and trudged through a swampy area of the farm, pulling dirt, mud, and probably excrement up with him. As he neared the fence, Stanislav lifted Wang over his head and literally threw him through the air, over the fence, to the other side safely! Wang hit and rolled as Stanislav diverted his course, knowing that the bull would burst through the rickety fence. He paused and dove to the right, narrowly being missed by the angry bull. It turned and growled as Ivan stood up and the two, for one moment, had a stare down. ~Close up to Ivan’s eyes~ ~Close up to the Bull’s eyes~ Editors Note: Slow Cowboy Music Plays in the background The cow snorted and pawed at the ground as a tumbleweed blew between the two… ..yes..a tumbleweed. With a burst of speed, the bull charged straight at The Russian Bear. And Ivan Stanislav knew what to do: He turned around and ran away screaming. Ivan lowered a shoulder and charged headlong into the fence, shattering the wooden fence in the process. He stopped and yanked the post holding a portion of the fence up out of the ground and swung at the bull. CRASH!!! MOOO!!! The post, about the size of a railroad tie, shattered instantly over the iron hard skull of the estimated 1400 lb bull. It charged past Stanislav came about, and stared at Stanislav and Wang, who glanced at one another and then at the bull. “Pwapowschik..I’m scawed….” The bull lowered it’s head, pawed at the ground and then….. ….fell over in a heap. Ivan Stanislav and Loyal Communist Party Member Wang stared at one another, and then at their fallen adversary, and laughed. “DYAAHAAHAA!!!” “OHHHH HO HO HO HO HO!!!” ~~ But the travels of Ivan Stanislav and Major Wang were hardly over. Little did they know what trials were before them as they noticed a sprawling corn field ahead of them.
