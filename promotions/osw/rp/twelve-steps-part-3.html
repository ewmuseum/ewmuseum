'Hey, buddy, is that yer girlfriend over there?' He points behind him. I motion him to move and sure enough, Paris is being physically restrained by three more truckers. A black, glossy semi rig is parked nearby and a crowd of people are surrounding a guy clutching his crotch. A dark maroon stain is spreading. What the fuck, I wonder aloud. What the fuck are you talking about, bit his dick off, I ask. The asshole kinda skitters over to the others, and Paris snarls as one of the truckers slaps the shit out of her with a backhand. 'Fucking asshole!' she growls. Sure enough, there's blood around her mouth. 'Oh my fucking God! Sweet Jesus, fuck!' the trucker with the penile wound moans as a thirty-something woman is trying to calm him down. Probably a RN, from the bedside manner, more aptly truckside, but why split hairs? 'Just calm down and let me look at it.' she tries. I look at this scene in dumb shock. My mind is still on last night, the sex, and then the dreamless sleep. From the light present, it's probably about 6 or seven in the morning. I can barely focus. What the hell is going on? I ask. 'Fucker tried to rape me, that's what!' Paris snarls. 'Bullshit, you and Tommy were all over each other, and when he tried to get you to swallow, you bit him!' one of the truckers, most likely his buddy, states, and Paris shrugs. Whoa, is all that I get out before the probable RN manages to get the wounded trucker to pull down his blood-stained pants and reveal the horribly wounded prick. Everyone recoils in shock and digust and turns away, but I can see it clearly - the tip and the mushroom-like glans had been savagely ripped, dangly by a few sinewy scraps of tissue. That's all it takes for one of the truckers to send a ham-sized fist into her jaw, and she sags down, screaming. I move forward, not thinking, and dropkick him into the silvery air cleaner on the side of the cab. My feet connect at his shoulder, and he stumbles away, his head rebounding off the metal with a dull muted clang. The others unhand Paris, who prompty kicks one of them in the balls. That woman has penis issues. The other trucker lunges for me, but it's all too easy to DDT him into the pavement. When I roll away, textbook as always, he is rolling over, clutching his head. The crowd scatters a bit, and I grab Paris, who is stomping her thick leather shoeheel into the crotch of the man she previously kicked. I think we should leave now, I say. 'FUCKER!' she screams at the wounded truckers, and I pull her away towards the Camaro. Get in the fucking car, I scream, as I twist the ignition and put the Camaro in reverse. She opens the heavy door and throws herself in as the tires squeal and I throw it in first gear. We peal out of the rest stop as more people come streaming from RVs and minivans, parked for the night. I look over at her as merge into the highway lane, doing about 90. What the fuck did you do that for, I ask. 'Hey, guy tried coming on to me, and I decided to have a little fun with him. He tried getting physical and I bit his dick off. No biggie.' Um, yeah. 'Don't worry, I'll brush my teeth.' she says, spitting out the window. After she wipes the blood away from her lips, she reaches in her purse to get a pack of gum. This is surreal. Well, I say, this is the first time I've seen someone suffer severe trauma to thier dick. 'First time for everything, I guess. Isn't that what they say?' Yeah, I guess. The Camaro thunders on down the highway, away from the rest stop. -FIN-
