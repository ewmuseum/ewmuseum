Doug Troy: Legend?...Yeah, used to be...

16 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16.04.02 Looking back to SLAM!, it was a success as well as a failure. I mean, I was beaten by Davis, but that is no big deal. I know he cheated to do so. But Walczak being beaten by Hessian? Now that I did not see coming. I know it must have been an off night for Walczak, ‘cause any other night, he would have beaten Hessian into smithereens. But why all this fuss over Doug Troy? Yeah, he is great PCW legend, I know that. But who does he think he is blasting off on the Army? It is fine for someone to show opinions on people like me, Walczak, etcetera, but you do not bring it into a State Of Federation Address. It just isn’t right. The darkness previously on the screen disappears to reveal Michel Krieg sitting at a desk of some sort, sitting back in an old leather chair, his head slightly down. Obviously he’s still thinking about SLAM! and a certain individual. Raising his head slightly, Krieg’s eyes catch the camera like eagle’s eyes do as the expression on his face shows the conviction of his thinking, “It is not very often that the German War Machine gets a little angry at someone for disrespecting him or what he stands for. It is usually just the case of jealousy, so it can be fobbed off easily. But this time, it is obviously not jealousy. This man has pretty much done it all in the way of wrestling, and then he comes out, during a State Of The Federation Address might I add, and completely disrespects the ethics and standings of the Red Army and its members,” “I appreciate you may have some sort of rivalry between yourself and Ivan, most probably down to the fact that he managed to get a title shot at Toomes and you couldn’t. But if there is a rivalry between you and our fearless Russian, Ivan, then take it to the ring. Don’t dishonour and disrespect the federation, the Red Army, and may I say, yourself. For being such a legend, you don’t seem to have the dignity that you should,” says Krieg, as he slowly stands up out of his seat, Walking over to the window, Krieg looks out with his hands grasped behind his back, continuing to reflect, “It is a sad state of affairs when we see one of the PTC’s biggest names letting himself be drawn down into a “shouting-fest” in the middle of a ring where most of the crowd couldn’t even tell you where he used to wrestle. Doug, you just don’t know how much respect you have until it’s gone, do you? You had some much respect from fans, from wrestling pundits...from wrestlers. And you just go and throw it down the drain by letting something get on top of you,” Slowly turning round again, Krieg looks into the camera deeply, “As I said previously, it is a sad state of affairs when a legend like Troy gets an ego the size of Canada and becomes a glory-hunting lunatic. But it has to be put to a stop. That’s why we’re facing off on Saturday, Doug. A PCW Legend against a man who could well be an OSW Legend. Look at it this way Troy: it’s like your time to prove you’ve still got it. I mean, if you can beat me, then obviously you’ve still got the thing that made you great back in the days of PCW. But if you crumble at the feet of me, then I think you should really consider your position here in the federation. To be quite honest, if you can’t beat me, you’ve got no chance in hell of beating Ivan when he’s angry, have you? Maybe the OSW should take note of the result of our match, just to see how much of a legend you are, and how much of a legend you think you are,” “I’m not going to stand here and trash talk you, Mr. Troy, because I think we are both adult enough to know that all the trash talk in the world isn’t going to win anyone a match. So Troy, I’m just here to tell you what will happen if you even turn up on Saturday. You will be meeting me in the ring. A 7 foot monster. You will no doubt see Tom Walczak at ringside. The Polish Punisher. And I suspect you might even see Ivan if he thinks your head’s getting too big for your body. So Doug, it will be a pleasure to meet you in the ring on SLAM!, but be warned: I will not be going lightly on you because you are a man I have some respect for. You will be just like everyone else who steps into the ring with me. Just another statistic on the Red Army’s tally, my friend,” With that, Krieg returns to the window with his arms crossed over, looking out onto a park next to his Berlin home. Fade.
