Prison Thought

8 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-8-02 Introduction- What the hell happened? Dame was finally beginning to gain trust from those who once supported him. The drugs have been forgotten, and he has been forgiven... Forgiven for something he never did. But now, the fans and family hate him more then ever. He is portrayed as a a drug dealer, an even bigger liar, and to top it all off, a kidnapper. Your father would refer to him as a 'Sick Fuck' If ever in conversation on the topic. But...what the hell happened? Prison Thought- Part 1 Michael Dame lays in his prison bed, inside a small correctional facility where they keep the 'accused'.. Awaiting their trials. He lays there, with a thousand thoughts racing through his head. He feels like he is running a marathon, sprinting his fastest, due to his state of mind. '(In thought) I am not a kidnapper, and I don't know why I'm being accused. I assume it's for the drug convictions that shadow me, it lead an unstable man..Frankie..to point the finger. But what evedince is there? None, there can't be. Unless It's happening again...Unless, I'm being framed. ' Michael reaches over to his night-table and picks up a book. Appears to be the bible, but it can not be made out due to the bad lighting and angle of view. '(In thought) My beautiful children (obviously looking at pictures he has placed inside the book) So sweet, and so young... Taken away from me. But...why? Why does this happen to me? First and foremost is the question. What is the answer? My life is full of questions. Of dismay, of utter insults and disbelief. And it just keeps piling up. When something looks bright, simultaneously the clouds close in, and it's dark and gloomy. Evertime. EVERYTIME!' Michael quickly closes the book, and clenches it across his chest... 'Oh man, my thought is driving me up a wall.'.. Michael Stands up, book still in hand, clenching it hard.. '(in thought)I will not let anything keep me down, I will once again have the same attitude coming out of jail for the first time. I will hold my head high. If people will believe me, let them, it's their choice, but if people will not, then I will try harder. I will take years to make them believe. I will take decades. I don't care, I'm 28 years old, and all I want is forgiveness. Forgiveness, once again for something I didn't do......and to see my children.' Dame turns around towards his desk, a small wooden desk, painted blue to match the walls, bolted down at the bottom of the legs. He looks down at a piece of paper, he has written something. It reads.. Frankie Bones, Last Man Standing... Over and Over he jotted and wrote it..'(In thought).. And Frankie Bones. If I can talk to you, I would. I am not the kidnapper of Hardy. If I were, I would not want to help you find him. When I am released, no matter when it is, I will help you find him. That is a promise. But, I doubt you'll take my words and promises as anything other then lies..so, so be it.... I doubt it, but If I do appear on the pay-per-view, I will give you everything you can handle. I will be the last man standing, and then our feud ends there. I will help you find Hardy, becuase, I would want you to do the same for me. I would want you to help me find my children.' Michael stops and gazes out of his prison cell. A cold, concrete wall sits in front of him, no farther then 7-8 feet. He holds his book tight, as he walks towards the front of his cell. 'Officer, I would like my phone call now..' Dame obviously has someone he wants to call. You hear a clanking of black, officer shoes walking closer and closer, gaining sound of echo. 'What is it, Dame?' The officer says rudely. 'I would like to make my phone call now, I have not yet done so..' Michael replies. 'Wait here..' the officer says as he walks down the hall... '...and I would wait anywhere else..' Dame cracks a joke to himself, lower then a mouse's peep. A laugh also slides out. The cell front slides open and Dame is instructed to step out. He is patted from head to toe, and told to put the book down. He clenches it, as he slides it upon the top of his desk. The officer locks Dame in a pair of handcuffs and begins to walk him down the hall... Camera Fade to black.
