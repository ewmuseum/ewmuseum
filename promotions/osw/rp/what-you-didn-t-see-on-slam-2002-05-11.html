What You Didn't See On SLAM

11 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 11th 2002 The show was ending and the fans began to file out of the arena. The OSW wrestlers were in their locker rooms changing and taking showers in preparation to hit the road. But one man wasn’t in his locker room; one man was still on a mission. “Locke! I know you’re back here somewhere. COME ON OUT! LOCKE!!” Graphic Violence stormed the backstage area with vicious intent. “Locke you sissy little bitch. Come out here and fight me like a man. Don’t run and hide.” GV continues his search but to no avail. He sees an OSW crew member packing up the semi truck with various show props. GV glares, grinds his teeth and grabs the man by the back of his collar. He swings the man around and grabs him by his throat with his right hand and shows his massive strength by lifting him off the ground a good six inches. “Where the fuck is Rhett Locke’s locker room?” The crew member can’t speak because GV has his massive hand around his neck, so he just points in the direction. GV smiles and drops the man to the ground. The OSW crew member rubs his neck and gasps for air and manages to flick GV off behind his back. Graphic Violence walks down the hallway and comes to a door that reads “Rhett Locke” but there is a note attached to the door. “Dear GV, I kicked your ass. You suck and I hope you die 100 deaths. -Rhett” Graphic Violence grabs the note and crumples it up into a ball and throws it in a fit of rage. He then punches the door while screaming “Locke! Locke!” He stops, takes a deep breath and lifts his hand to his mouth. He notices some blood streaming down his hand from the numerous punches to the door. He licks the blood up, grabs the camera and gets his face real close. “Locke, you have just started the end of the life. Your fate is in my hands now, and what I do to you next week will put you out of commission for a long time. All you had to do was be a man and face me right now, but it’s clear that you have a pussy and Lillith has a set of balls. Because at least she’d be here and stand up for herself…but you run and hide. That’s fine Rhett, because next week on SLAM there will be nowhere for you to hide. See you then.” GV lets out his trademark grin as he pushes the camera away from his face. He walks away as the camera fades to an OSW logo.
