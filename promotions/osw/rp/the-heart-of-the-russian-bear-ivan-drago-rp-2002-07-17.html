The Heart of The Russian Bear (Ivan/Drago rp)

17 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-17-02 Whenever Ivan Stanislav had seen the feather within his desk in Arkhangelsk, something changed inside him. The feather was a symbol, if you will. A feather of something that he once was. Something that he had longed to be once again. To Ivan Stanislav, he knew that Tempest was gone. She had made it a point to remain unknown in the eyes of the Russian Bear. Immediately following the falling out that they had, she had moved from her apartment, and he knew it was for the sole reason that he would return there to confront her. And try as he did, she was gone. However, Ivan Stanislav wanted to regain as much of what he once was. Russia had gotten rid of him for the time being. The fans were once again beginning to recognize him as a positive force. The Red Army was gone and unable to sway his thinking. And yet, the only force that really did recognize Ivan as nothing except for an individual, was gone. The feather was the last thread that seemed to keep that belief alive within himself. Pride was perhaps the most powerful force that drove Ivan Stanislav. While he was physically superior to virtually anyone that dared to get in his way, and far more intelligent than most individuals, it was his pride that remained the driving force for his actions. His pride spurred him to the military. It drove him to wrestling. And led him to Tempest, even. And at the same time, the great, unshakable pride of Ivan Stanislav was a great burden as well. To admit a wrong, or to speak against something you have upheld for so long, is a sign of weakness, and a rebuttal of one’s pride. To speak your mind about subjects that might not appear to apply to someone, or to admit a great pain within the body of an immovable force, can damage that pride. He could not sleep, however, knowing the pain inside of him. He could not seem to rest knowing what had welled up inside of his heart. He had pushed the memory of Tempest, the memory of his happiness, happiness caused not by politics or governments, but from another person, deep into the recesses of his heart. Now, with the feather had a great influx of pain been brought upon him. Ivan Stanislav knew of one person that might be able to help him. Someone that would listen to him admit things he would be unable to tell others, and yet still a person that would remain in the dark completely: El Drago. While Stanislav was unsure if he could call El Drago a friend…El Drago was a man that did not speak English. While he claimed in the past htat he knew Drago was actually an imposter…he was still skeptical. Now, he had a chance to voice his frustrations fully, and Drago wouldn’t even understand it. Ivan Stanislav walked into the Tulsa Hospital room that housed El Drago. El Drago lied in his bed, an oxygen mask over his mouth which helped to strengthen his injured lung. Upon Ivan's arrival the Mexican Dragon sat up slightly, a little weary of the Russian Bear's return. Stanislav closed the door behind him and realized that Drago was aware of his presence. The Russian Bear straightened and swallowed, somewhat uncomfortable knowing that he came back to see someone outside of his Motherland. “You are awake?” said Stanislav, blatantly stating the obvious. Drago did not move, staring a hole through Ivan. Stanislav shrugged and muttered to himself, “He cannot understand what I say anyway.” He walked into the room and brought his arms up in a nonthreatening way and spoke very slowly, “Ivan…is….not…here….to…hurt…you.” Ivan paused and bit his lip, eyeing Drago again, “Ivan…wanted….to….talk.” 'Talk?' Responded Drago questioningly. Ivan slowly nodded in response and walked deeper into the room, “Drago, I have been suspended from Russian Military for my actions. You have to understand that by such an event occurring, I have lost a tremendous amount of support from my Motherland.” Ivan paused and reached into his pocket and pulled out his fist, clenched tightly. “I returned to my base to clean out some books..and this….this is what I found.” He opened his fist, revealing the small red feather that came from Tempests boa. “Do you see it, Drago?” Drago reached out slowly to grab the feather but with a mighty roar, Ivan slapped his hand away, “NYET!” El Drago quickly pulled his hand back, a shocked look on his face. Ivan cupped the feather in his hand and looked at Drago, “You see it, I take it. A feather, El Drago. A feather. A piece of fluff! Something one looks at and does not give another thought. An afterthought if you will! But this feather….this feather is capable….of stunning….a Russian Bear.” Ivan paused and stared at it. He looked up slowly, “You probably do not understand, El Drago. But…whenever you were new in OSW, Stanislav had met someone. Her name….was Tempest. Perhaps you know her? Does that name register? Tempest?” El Drago simply nodded recognition of the name. Ivan looked down and stared at the feather again, “She meant a lot to me, El Drago. How could one person, one individual, mean so much to a Communist? How could one solitary woman mean so much to Ivan Sergeiovich Stanislav?” He sighed and leaned back against the wall, staring at an unknown portion of the wall ahead of him, “When I first met her, I thought she was nothing but child. I went on that vacation with her because it would make Russia look good. I did not care for her. I did not care about stupid contest!” Stanislav paused and shook his head, “And yet…I felt…..alive.” Ivan looked at Drago again to see if he was paying any attention to him. Speaking to someone for an extended period of time in a foreign language concerned him. But El Drago was listening, understanding was another thing but for some reason the man that just a few weeks ago was a sworn enemy listened to every word. Ivan sighed and breathed heavily through his nose, “You know, El Drago, she was first person to like me….for me. Even Alexei first got to know Ivan Stanislav because of his reputation, but Tempest….nyet…she ignored all the negatives about me and enjoyed my company.” He stood up straight again, “And she defended me when no one else would. She stood by my side whenever I needed her…and did I?” Ivan choked back a few words, “Did I?” The Russian Bear paused and walked to a window and stared out of it, “El Drago, I never meant to hurt her. They say that you can see the past perfectly, the present only halfway, and the future is blind. As I look at the past, I see my error…..I see my mistake and if I could change it, I would.” He paused and looked at the feather, his back to Drago, “I would do anything to change it.” A low growl exited Ivan’s huge frame, “I am forty years old, El Drago. Forty years of service to Russian Military, and I am suspended. I have no country, I have no Comrades, I have no military. My life, for all intents and purposes, has been wasted. And there is truly one person that could have still not cared and been beside me, and I was stupid…and I hurt her!” Ivan’s volume quickly grew as he knocked the windowsill to the floor with his huge fist. He breathed heavily and rubbed the back of his neck. The huge, muscular back of The Russian Bear hulked up and down. He spoke softer, almost in a whisper, still gazing outside at the sky, “Whenever she left…El Drago…and I returned to my base….I found that feather. I…I found it in my pocket. And do you know what I did when I got to my base and locked myself in the room?” Ivan paused for a long time as Drago watched on. “I cried, Drago.” He shook his head slowly, “I cried in my office as I stared at that feather. ME! The Russian Bear!! When I was shot in Afghanistan, I did not cry! When Soviet Union collapsed, I did not cry!” “But when she left….I did.” Ivan turned slowly and gazed at El Drago, pain coursing through his face, “My mother told me once that she ‘loved’ me. I never understood what that meant. What is love? It cannot be described! It cannot be seen! And for my entire life, for forty years, I ignored love and yet, questioned what it was.” He lowered his eyes to the floor, almost shamefully. “I know what love is now, El Drago. Love is something that could bring grown man to cry. Love is something that could open the eyes of a man that had forever been blind.” He paused and took a large breath. “I loved her, El Drago.” He gritted his teeth, “I loved her for her personality. I loved her for her positive outlook! I loved her for her understanding and her compassion and her joyfulness and her determination and…..” He swallowed and raised his gaze, “I still love her, El Drago. While she may hate me, I love her with all of my heart and will forever love her.” He shook his head and lowered himself into a chair, defeated. “And I hurt her, of all people. Her life was so difficult, so many horrors did she have to live through, and when I tried to comfort her, I am just another shattered dream. A memory gone bad. A nightmare.” Ivan chuckled haphazardly, “She is afraid of thunderstorms, do you know that?” He looked at Drago, not necessarily expecting a response. And no response was given. El Drago just stared, lost in the words of the Russian Bear and not willing to interrupt. “I swore, El Drago, that I would protect her. I swore that she would never have anything to fear from the storm…” he trailed off, his eyes darting around a small pinpoint on the floor, “….and I can not sleep at all when it rains. When I close my eyes...I see her. I see her afraid, alone, and fearful. My greatest wish is to comfort her, to tell her that it is all right, to do something to let her rest…and then I realize that it is not the storm that she hates…but it is ME!” Ivan stands up almost angrily, still holding the feather. “And all of this stems from a memory. A memory that cannot be forgotten. A memory that will NEVER be forgotten!” Ivan brought his arms out to his side, “I am nothing, El Drago! I am not a Russian! I am not soldier! I am not success! I am nothing but coward! That is what I am! I am lonely coward, who’s cowardice cost him the greatest thing of all: love.” He growled, “I live in world where everyone despises me, no one trusts me, and they all hate me. And the only person that I could truly believe was faithful in me, I have scarred for life.” Ivan moved closer to Drago, “I thought Davis was to blame, but it was my actions that caused her the pain. I thought it was Aimz that was to blame, but she was an innocent. And who was to blame, El Drago?” He slowly pointed to himself, “I was. The greatest irony of it all. I am nothing but a monster, El Drago, and I have proven it.” 'Love conquers all, Amigo...' Drago interrupted as he placed a hand on Ivan's shoulder. Ivan backed away quickly like the touch was an electric shock, almost not realizing what Drago said, “Love does conquer all, El Drago! IT DOES CONQUER ALL!!” Ivan roared, shaking the walls, “For love has defeated Ivan Stanislav! I am nothing but tormented man! I am a shattered form of what I once was!” Ivan gingerly put the feather in his pocket, “The only memory of have of the one person that ever meant a thing to me, I will keep. A feather. A woman that changed my life….can only be represented by feather.” Ivan snorted and shook his head, “I am going to go..I do not know why I came here! I am doing nothing but wasting my time, and wasting yours in process.” He turned and headed to the door and stopped, not looking at Drago, “A moment with her means more to me than my entire lifetime of Communism…El Drago.” Ivan paused and opened the door slowly and left. A few seconds later, a loud roar and a crash was heard down the hallway. El Drago lowered his head as the camera stayed on the Mexican, 'Worry not my friend...'
