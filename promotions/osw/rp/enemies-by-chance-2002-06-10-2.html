Enemies By Chance

10 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.10.2002 ][Everyone has to sit down at one point in their lives and just think. Think about the past. Think about the present. Think about the future. In doing this, one can find one's self and therefore become a better person. When you know who you are, no longer can those who are negative tell you how to be. Going through this process allows a man who had never been able to deal with his life, to take control of what he can't deal with. This is the story of Kory Storm, who to this day struggles to reflect on his life, for he has so long has put on a mask of being 'Perfection Personified'. Yet when he sits alone at night, no one there to console him because he's hurt everyone who has ever tried mentally and physically, staring in the mirror ... he views his own insecurities. He sees the murder. He sees the addiction. He sees the sneering face of his nemesis Jim Axtell staring at him, who now has control of his life, driving Kory to every sadistic move he makes. While he has no control, he realizes that as a man he is broken. He also comes to the conclusion that if he continues to let this happen, he will soon be just as broken in his competition. Finally, he chooses to reflect. Sitting in a plush leather waiting room chair, blood gushing from his face and silence surrounding his ears, it's the perfect time.][ 'I constantly ask myself how the fuck I got into the situation I'm in.' The voice, along with the face, of Kory Storm is clear with this phrase. 'I ask myself about Jim Axtell, and why we hate each other. Is it my fame? Is it my glory? Or is simply because I'm an asshole?' ][Kory Storm pauses for a minute, before speaking, as if to answer his own question.][ 'If you feel that the answer to my current question is the latter, than you people are certainly more stupid than I thought.' Speaks Kory Storm sneering at his surroundings, still confused sounding, trying to evaluate what is going on both in his life and inside his mind. 'Because whether you love or hate my attitude, I gave this very blood which pours from me for you people! And that is why you ignoramuses, including Jim Axtell, hate me. The answer to all the negative attitude directed towards me is my emmense talent. Why? You all can't stand to see someone like me in the spotlight! You can't stand to see someone who deserves to be on top in that very position. Instead you cheer for those with a huge lack of experience, men like Axtell, which causes a true hero like myself to protest. This creates rivalry. Rivalry creates violence. And violence creates injury.' ][Pausing once again, amazed that it has taken him so long to come to this conclusion. He now knows the answer, at least the answer which he himself feels is true. Some might beg to differ. For they have seen the sledgehammers driven into their hero's heart. They have seen the blood which Kory Storm claims to give for them, spilled from the head of everyone else but him. They have seen what 'Perfection Personified' has done since he arrived here, and that is make it a living hell for anyone who crossed him. He can't ignore it. He can't subside it. Instead he feeds it with more rage until the man known as Kory Storm explodes. And just like in any war, when the explosion happens, we all must take shelter.][ 'And so it all leads back to Jim Axtell and the reasoning behind his jealousy for me. My injury. My pain. My defeat. All due to one man's loathing for my misfortune. Now I wait anxiously to clear my blood from my face and start freshly in Oakland this Saturday. For I no longer feel that it should be spilled for the hopeless. In my genius, I now will never question my actions. For I truly know that I am the Superstar! I am the Icon! I am 'Your Paragon of Perfection'! Hell, I'm simply 'Perfection Personified'!' ][Is Kory Storm wrong in where he places the blame? Yes. But is he willing to accept that fact? No. He'll keep thinking that until his own ego destroys himself and the OSW. Then he will no longer be able to run from the hell he will have created. It won't be the people. It won't be Jim Axtell. It will be Kory Storm ... alone as usual ... blood only willing to be given for himself.][
