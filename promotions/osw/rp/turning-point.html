Turning Point

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1998 Almost in spite of his massive body, he cut through the night like a cat, slipping across the rooftop of the hotel. Pulling a device out of his pocket, the man known as Agent K spliced it into the hotel's security system, and shunted the cameras in the penthouse elsewhere.. effectively allowing him access. Without a smile on his now changed face, the massive man slipped through a skylight and into the hotel room. He checked over his assortment of knives, assuring that they would easily slide from their sheathes when the time came. His preference of blades over bullets was one reason that he the CSIS favorite for wetwork, and had been ever since he had survived what they termed the 'upgrade' progress. If any spark of humor or humanity had remained in the now huge body of this man, it would have noted the similarity to the old seventies show 'The Six Million Dollar Man'. We can make him better, the shows tagline had proclaimed. But in the case of CSIS, the subject had not been badly broken to start with.. there were no silly bionics involved, but genetic mutations.. and it cost considerably more than six million dollars. Agent K, or Kanaida as his handlers referred to the best of the so-called super-agents, pressed his ear to the door of the room, listening for a certain man's voice in the living area of the huge suite. Tonight's mission, his latest in the year since he had been changed and programmed, involved a terrorist working out of Washington. He planned on importing a nuclear weapon into the US through Canada, and had managed to bribe just the right people to do so.. all that remained was for him to place one final phone call, setting a chain reaction in motion. Kanaida's assignment? To make sure that the phone call was never placed, by terminating the man. Jack O'Shea had protected himself well, ensuring himself a suite with bullet-proof windows, and he never went out into public long enough to make a hit from a distance, or to arrange an accident.. so it was time for a wetwork specialist. Wetwork was getting close enough to kill while getting your hands wet in the blood.. and Agent K was a natural. Hearing a mans voice in the next room, he opened the door a crack. It was O'Shea, and he was reaching for the phone. Throwing the door open, Kanaida sprang into action. A throwing knife glittered through the air, severing the cord of the phone. O'Shea spun around, and stared in shock at the black-clad giant looming over him with a huge knife in it's paw. 'For your crimes against Canada and the world, I hereby sentence you, Jack O'Shea, to death.' With that, he drove forward with the big blade. But Jack had grown up in Dublin as a killer himself, and was no stranger to a fight. He spun inside the blade, and began hammering punches into his attacker's ribs. Kanaida shrugged them off, and slashed at the smaller man. Whistling in pain, O'Shea managed to trap the hand with the knife, lock the wrist, and hit it across his knee, forcing the blade to drop. With a frown, Kanaida headbutted the irishman, and threw him against the table, which shattered. They locked up, but the money spent by CSIS to improve it's super agents was well spent.. Agent K was stronger and faster than he had any right to be. And with his brain wiped out, he had no regret, no doubt, no remorse, no pity. He was as close to 'The Terminator' as money could buy.. and that made CSIS very happy. Quickly, Kanaida pummeled his foe, and drove him spinning to the ground. Wasting no time, Agent K took two big steps across the room, picking up the knife once more.. he preferred the big blade to the throwing knifes for it's finality. But when he turned, O'Shea had two bodies blocking him from harm. A young boy and a younger girl.. they pleaded for their father's life. The first seeds of doubt planted and exploded into life in Agent K's mind.. why did these children haunt him so, with their blonde hair and their blue eyes? And why did their faces constantly change to the faces of two other children, over and over? The more the children pleaded, the more they unlocked doors in his heart... and crushed barriers erected in his mind. He backed up a step, unsure of what to do next. And that step saved him from being shot through the heart, as a woman ran out of the bedroom shooting wildly with a sub-machine gun. Kanaida took a round through his left lung instead, and staggered backwards. Then, even with his newly awakened mind reeling, his training took over, and his deadly hands busied themselves with the throwing knives. They appeared like magic in her belly, chest, then throat. As her eyes bulged and her life's blood poured out, her finger tightened on the trigger. The spray of bullets went wild, and silenced the children and the husband forever. Kanaida dropped to his knees, with his memories of the present and the past mixed into a confused jumble. He held the children and cried over them as if they were his own.. and in a way, they were. For his own were now as dead to him as these poor innocents.
