I Dream of an Angel..

24 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-24-02 I must have passed at least one hundred white doors, and there... That one. It looks like all the rest; plain and white, yet it seemed to be calling out to me. I put my hand on the doorknob. It feels warm. I turn it slowly and push in. I walk to just inside the door, and let it slam behind me. I feel warm. I hear the faint sound of water running, replacing the sweet melody I had been accompanied by before. Suddenly I don't want to move. I feel safe in this place. 'Cally, come to us. Come to us now...' Sweet whispers of a voice beckon me. I have to obey. I follow the voices and am led into a huge room. I look around. I am surrounded by hundreds of shower stalls. No curtains, all white. In each small shower stall stood a beautiful angel. Clean white hair, white robe, and beautiful white wings spread out from behind her. Water was pouring from the showerheads down onto the angels, yet they did not get wet. Not only did each shower stall hold an angel, it was the same angel in each one. Same beautiful face, same beautiful aura surrounding her. They all smile at me. Wait,.. there's one different. I look at her and she speaks: 'Welcome, my child.' 'What is this?' I ask. 'This is your home, my child.' I felt the impulse to enter into one of the shower stalls. 'No you must wait until the others clean off their coats...' Coats? They weren't wearing coats and they weren't getting wet? The angel swept her hand from one end of the room to the other, seemingly removing a clear blanket of deceit. I focused my eyes to the shower stalls. Each angel was now drenched, their hair stringy and ratty. Each had a different color hair; each had a different, distinct face. 'Oh my god...' I mumbled. I watched as the water poured slowly down their blood-covered trench coats. It didn't look like the water was helping any. I hear the head angel laugh a wicked laugh. I look to her. Blood dripping from her teeth, her halo, her black wings. She holds out her hand to me; I look down. I cringe as I see where her hand used to be, now just a bloody stump with strips of skin dangling down. 'Step into the water, my child.' I look back at the other angels. I gasped. They were all holding their heads, trying to rip out their matted hair, shaking their heads violently back and forth, back and forth, pounding on imaginary shower doors. Screams. Loud piercing screams... I wail, my body convulsing as I am ripped from this horrible nightmare, my life force pounding from inside of my soul. The fear covers me, flashbacks entering my mind and throwing me in to a spiraling tunnel of needles, drugs, alcohol, and sex. My body is drenched from sweat and rain water, both mixing now inside of my mouth. A foul taste comes from the pit of my stomach upward and spews from my mouth. I hear the blades of grass as they catch the bile and it sickens me. My eyes are glossy, my skin pasty and stail..Why am I not dead? So much of that crank was pushed inside of my body..I should be dead. I roll from my back on to my stomach and allowed the rest of my upheavel tograce the dirt below me. So much pain, my hands now clutching my stomach. I cannot take it, I callapse in my own vomit, crying..Letting it all out in to the night air. I can't stay out here, I will surely die. I should be already, but for some reason I am still alive. My legs are sore, stiff..I try to stand slowly but I fall, bashing my already battered body off of the side of a tree before hitting the grounf once more. With a grimace I push myself up once more, tears streaming down my face. This time with a soft cry exiting my lips I manage to stand upright. I am freezing, my lips had to be colored blue, it is the beginning of morning and the air has a chill running below zero. I had to make it, I had to survive. For some reason I am given a second chance at a life not ruined and I have to take it. But as I stumble through the woods, naked and half dead I can only think of one thing. I need it, I crave it, I have to have it. My world spins away from me, and the place I find myself is on the front steps of that house, my hands banging on the door before me. With a creak my destiny opens up in front of me..Cameron looks down upon my form, a smirk appeared on his lips. 'You can't just die can you?' 'I..I..need it..' I can feel his strong arms lift me from the porch and as he carries me inside the coma embraces me. From somewhere my Angel speaks to me..Singing to me softly. 'It's all going to be alright..'
