Touching the Spark...

1 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 1, 2002 Looking up at the skyscraper that less than a year ago was his employer, Jesus St Crux slowly nods his head. 'Si, Dadi, I do miss those times. Even Douglas...' He shakes his head to dismiss the thoughts and studies the building now. The former UWA Towers had been converted into office space; an insurance company, a travel agency, and an up-and-coming Internet DSL provider now used the upper floors, and the Eastern Sunset Dojo had taken up residence in the lower floors where the training and ringside areas had been. 'Eastern Sunset... I wonder if Sun-Tzu is responsible...' 'Hey Latino!' Jesus St Crux and Caridad Mariposa are brought out of their reverie by the chipper female voice. They turn to see the speaker, and smile at the young woman standing in the doorway of a restaurant. 'Ola, chica! Que passa, Brandi?' Brandi Brown smiles as she waves Jesus and Caridad into Mohammed's Tap and Grill, made infamous for its numerous appearances on UWA television. 'Oh, things have been going pretty good. Mohammed was looking to expand after all the publicity he got from the exposure on Turmoil and Fury, so he hired me on as a business manager. It's a far cry from chasing you wrestlers down for questions, but I enjoy the work.' Brandi and Caridad continue towards the bar, but Jesus pauses after he steps inside the door. He hasn't been in here in almost a year, not since he, Thunderhawk, Black Tiger, Mariko, Pike, and Wippit had one final dinner together after Scott Douglas had announced the closure of the UWA. The Mexican wrestler looks around Mohammed's... it was a veritable gold mine of UWA memorabilia; pictures of all champions, signed posters, even replicas of all the title belts. His gaze rests the longest on the UWA World Heavyweight Championship... the belt Jesus had finally won back on what turned out to be the very last UWA card by finally defeating his former friend, Jason Winters, cleanly. Brandi and Caridad leave St Crux to relive his memories, and turn to each other as they sit at the bar. 'So tell me, girl, what are you guys doing now?' Caridad smiles, 'We hit a rough spot for a while. Ended up back in Mexico doing a few short stints in various promotions down there. But things are looking good, Jesus signed with Old School Wrestling just a few weeks ago, so things seem to be on the rise.' Brandi cocks her head slightly, as if trying to remember something. 'Old School? Didn't they sign Jason Winters just a while ago?' Hearing the name of his former friend, Jesus drops his gaze down from the replica UWA belt. 'Si, just as they signed me. There are rumors around that they're looking at other former UWA talent, but I haven't kept in touch with many other than Wippit, and he's off somewhere else, althoguh I imagine he's out of the ventilation shaft by now...' Brandi looks like she's going to question about the ventilation shaft, but sighs as she remembers Wippit Guud's antics. 'So are you going to run into Jason anytime soon, or are you two keeping your distance?' Jesus takes a breath of resolution as he sits next to Caridad and slips an arm around her. 'I haven't been avoiding him, intentionally anyways. I was backstage at the last Slam and at Head on Collison and I didn't see him. But I just found this morning that he and I are set to meet Saturday night. I guess OSW wants to play up the grudge match.' Brandi looks at Jesus a bit seriously. 'You sound like you don't want to fight him. Aren't you still mad about him betraying you and joining Kanaida and Ian Wright as a new Highly Unstable?' Jesus shakes his head with a slight smile. 'You can take the reporter out of the UWA...' Brandi blushes a bit, as she apologizes, 'Sorry, I didn't mean to.. I'm acting like my old self again.' 'De nada, mi amiga. I have no problem facing Jason again. In fact, I think it'll be good to start off things again with someone I know.' Jesus nods his head a bit, with a slight smile of anticipation. 'Si.. Jason and I are going touch things off with a bang.' Jesus waves his hands to change the topics, a bigger smile on his face. 'Now, where's Mohammed? I want our usual table. Come, join us for dinner, Ms. Brown.
