Inseperable...

2 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 2nd, 2002 So you come to life in a bed that had much more the day before... You grab a shirt, pull it over your head... Slip on some huge camouflage pants for tiny legs to swim in... And then you wrap a few fingers around the tea tree oil from your nightstand to drip it onto stretched earlobe piercings... Not quite the typical day-after-breakup routine, but hey...Amy Campbell was never someone who found any comfort in a system. The only upset after THIS breakup was turning to her side instinctually and finding herself alone. It hadn't been Reno she honestly could say she missed...he'd pissed her off the night before, he'd gone crazy and he'd been practically ignoring her, off in his own little world though standing beside her the entire duration of the brief little relationship between them. It was ZaC she missed, the same as someone would have missed a cuddle from their tabby cat every morning when they stirred...it just felt weird, and Amy felt terrible not only that the little creature's life had ended, but that it ended at the hands of someone no life should be in. ''Alone again,'' She groaned to herself, arching her body up and putting enough strain into it to make her tired muscles come to life. ''...stay that way this time, crazy bitch.'' Amy couldn't help but smile. It was a horrible thing to do on a morning like that, but it was a truthful emotion prying it's way to the surface for once. Unfortunately enough, the pleasant little moment was cut short by a high-pitched and annoying cell phone ringing to the tune of Orgy's 'Blue Monday' cover. Campbell sighed when she saw that the LCD read 'LeLievre' and followed with her best friend's phone number. She just chuckled as she put it to her head. ''How did I know you'd call?'' ''Because you're Amy Campbell...'' Tinkling laughter sounded on the other line. ''...and you know it all.'' ''Funny, Breanne. So let me guess...the order of the day is a nice, big serving of un-necessary love and comfort?'' The voice on the other end began again, still giggling but going serious after a moment. ''Right you are! So how you doin', anyway? You okay?'' ''Fine!'' ''...are you sure?'' ''Yep.'' ''....REALLY sure?'' ''Absolutely!'' ''One hundred and ten perce---'' Aimz snarled. ''Don't push.'' ''Alright...well guess who's flying out to Chicago this week to see her favorite person in the world?'' ''Uggggh........'' ''Thanks, but I wanna see you! You never come home anymore...'' ''Are you trying to push your bisexuality upon me now that I'm single?'' Amy laughed, only drawing a growl from her friend. ''No! I'm gonna find you a man...'' ''Grrr...'' ''A DECENT man...not one of these losers you always fall for. Look at your last few boyfriends...a thug, a guy convinced he's a superhero and now chases small girls on playgrounds and then this Reno Davis...he was sorta hot, but the guy had no pupils and a fucking monkey for a best friend, Amy...NOT a turn-on.'' ''Hey!'' She whimpered. ''I happen to like the guys I date...'' ''You and Reno never HAD a 'date'...you just kinda started having sex one day!'' ''Ugh...'' Amy sighed to herself again, then decided to cut things short before she said anything further. ''I'll see you in Chicago, Bre.'' ''Wh---'' BEEP! Bye bye phone call,CLICK! Bye bye batteries, THUD! No more interruptions.
