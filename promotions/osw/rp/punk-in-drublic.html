I don't have the time This is how I live my life I haven't got much pride And it's ok if I live like A slob cause this is my Life, and I will waste It if I want, 'A wasted Life ain't no crime -NOFX, ''The Punk Song'' (Album: ''The PMRC Can Suck On This''.) After an eventful weekend of general non-stop action for Amy Campbell, it was time to relax... Slam had been a great one, indeed...as was PTC Intervention, the inter-federation pay-per-view extravaganza...that night, in fact, had been one of the best of her life. Spent entirely with Rent-A-Hero...it wasn't exactly normal by his virgin, now very much so NON-virgin, standars... None-the-less, the redheaded super starlet was still walking...straight into a hotel room shared by she and Breanne Lilievre, who had a night just as eventful, possibly more painful, in a locker-room with NTICW Producer Mat. Sweaty, exhausted, Amy was trotting into the room with an unusual bounce in her step...dressed in the same thing she was the previous evening: her favorite 'Guess?' brand cargo khakis and a little black halter top that had been ripped a little on the left side, either in the Water Park match...or the little match that was had between she and PTC's resident hero later in the night. Bre, of course, had absolutely no clue...having spent the ENTIRE night of kink with Producer Mat doing only God knows what...so her response to seeing Campbell step in, looking like she did, was loud as usual. ''Jesus! What the hell happened to you?!'' Amy put her wide, beaming smile, which was a rarity, on display. She said nothing, just kept walking until she sat on what had been designated as her bed, slipping a pair of buckle-down leather boots off. Breanne just looked at her, chuckling a moment. ''You smile like you got laid or something!'' The redhead just looked up, smiled again and continued unbuckling her boots while Bre grasped for some sort of confirmation... ''But we all know you were with Rent-A-Hero...heh, God knows screwing a freak like that's impossible...'' She paused a moment to look to Amy, who was still just smiling. ''...right?.......RIGHT!? Oh please...tell me you didn't....'' As Aimz' dirty blonde, well-pierced best friend, Bre had to gasp at this point... ''Amy! My GOD...even I wouldn't stoop so low as to rape a virgin hero!'' Campbell cocked her head, staring at Breanne with disbeleif before laughing and simply stating, ''You can't rape the willing.'' ''Please...'' Bre paused a moment, processing a mock shudder. ''...PLEASE...tell me you didn't...'' Amy smirked, tossing her boots off near a luggage carrier while saying, rather smugly, ''Oh, we did...we did indeed. Quite a few times, in fact...'' ''GAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! GET THE PICTURE OUT!!!! GET THE MENTAL IMAGES AWAAAAAAAAAAAAY!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!'' At that point, Breanne hit herself repeatedly...leaving Amy to laugh. ''God, you're so sensitive...'' ''Yeah...well...........picture...ME.............naked....with........Producer Mat...............'' She paused another moment, waiting to see if that caught any reaction from Aimz. The redhead merely cringed, so Bre continued... ''.......on YOUR bed!'' Amy immediately shot up from the bed, brushing the seat of her pants off as she did so. ''NEVER tell me these things!'' The dirty blonde 'devil' smiled. ''You deserved it for lying about RAH just to sicken me.'' ''I didn't lie...if you want proof, I'll let you see the stains in his rental...'' ''AAAAAAACK!!!!!!!'' Half-jokingly, Aimz stuck a pierced tongue out at her best friend, knowing she had won the little battle. ''Mehhhhhh..boo to this,'' Bre was still disturbed. ''I'm gonna go see if Mat checked out yet...'' With that, she was out the door before Amy could say another word. ''Finally...'' Campbell muttered to herself. ''...a little silence.'' For some reason, Aimz was stepping over to her discman, which had been sitting idle on the nightstand... She picked it up and, contradicting herself, slid the headphones on...flicking the 'Forward' button until 'The Unforgiven II' by Metallica began to blast, audible even outside of the headphones. Sagging down into a little arm chair across the room, her thoughts were wandering every which way...mostly on Rent-A-Hero and the activities of the previous night until she was torn back to reality... While Amy had sat there, Timo Bolamba entered the room quietly...slipping the headphones away from his tag partner's red locks and screaming 'BOO!'. Campbell wasn't pleased that she'd been caught off guard... ''Timo! Jesus...don't TRY to give me a heart-attack!'' ''Muahahahahahaa....'' The big Samoan was in a seemingly wonderful mood after the triumph at Slam. ''I'll just stop your heart with my dashing good looks!'' ''Bad Samoan! Bad!'' She smacked him on the arm, joking as usual. ''Don't get a big head...hehe.'' ''I saw you and that Reno Davis guy in the parking lot...RAH'll be jealous.'' He chuckled. ''...not that he already isn't, what with this hot and steamy love affair you and I supposedly have.'' ''Who the hell started that rumor, anyway?'' Bolamba paused a moment, thinking before growling, ''I dunno...but I'd like to get my hands around their neck sometime.'' ''Fucking closed-minded retards...guy and girl team, apparently that means they're screwing backstage or some shit.'' ''I like the language you speak.'' Timo laughed, tossing his leather coat over onto the floor. Amy grunted. ''Boo to speech without expletives...swearing rocks almost as much as I do. It's all a part of being naturally cool, my friend.'' She smiled. ''...besides, I speak with proper gammar most of the time...why not have a slight flaw? Flaws are cool...'' He laughed again, this time watching Aimz as she rummaged through her luggage case...finding something that caught his eye... ''Is that a bong?'' The curiously-shaped glass object caught his eye. ''Huh? Ohhhhhh...yeah, but I don't do that shit or anything.'' Though she looked it, Amy wasn't quite THAT fried. ''...in high school I was really good at making them when we used to steal big beakers from the science labs. My buddy Chad just got me to make him one to bring home on the road...I've gotta take it apart to get it past customs...'' Amy giggled, mischievious as ever. ''Riiiiiiiiiight...possession and distribution of drug-related products, that'll be a nice fat charge.'' ''Boooooooo to those dumbass laws...for all they know, this could be a vase for flowers or something,'' She was fidgeting a bit. ''And besides...I'm not USING it, and I'm not SELLING it...I'm giving it to Chad, that's all.'' The big Samoan sighed, mumbling to himself... ''There's no use even trying...'' ''Huh?'' ''Nothing...but what the hell're you gonna do about this match coming up? You're teaming with the Red Army's lady-friend...'' ''Meh...'' Amy shrugged, a few strands of hair falling from the loose pony tail she was just finishing off. ''Tempest and I are two VERY different people...but she's definitely alright by me, so it'll all work out. I'd say we all did a pretty good job on Slam...'' ''You're just saying that 'cause you got to knock 'Streak out.'' He teased. ''Oh blah, at least it didn't end in ADD or a gang-beating by Brooklyn Bi and Bred again!'' ''True...but there's no doubt they'll be there,'' Timo was rather unenthusiastic. ''...they just can't seem to keep their hands in Hostyle's pants...instead they're touching us clean folk with those filthy paws.'' ''They'll learn...don't worry, they'll learn.'' She smiled, her confidence growing. ''We taught them enough of a lesson this past week...'' Bolamba nodded to Aimz, and she continued... ''Buuuuut I wanna get to see Rentie before we head for the airport...so can we leave now?'' ''I guess...'' He shrugged, the same as Amy before...still unenthusiatic.
