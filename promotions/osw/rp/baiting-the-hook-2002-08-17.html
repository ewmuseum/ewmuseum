Baiting the hook

17 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-17-02 She would take the bait. Provide her with an easy target, however not to easy, otherwise she would grow suspicious. It had to be tempting enough, savory enough she could taste it. She's a simple creature really, single minded in their pursuit of rank and glory, so eager to appease that comatose martyr she'd elevated to deity status. 'Know your enemy'… a clichéd line perhaps, however the advice should be heeded, if not with a grain of salt. Pardon me, I'm off topic. Yes, provide her with an easy target. Abandon a note just off the borders of her locker room, batter it up enough to display the illusion of a derelict one. Then, when she takes the bait, and ever willing to pin another medal to her chest, the real predator slips out from hiding and claims its prey. And this is what I was to do, and this is what I did. Write a note, pin it to the door and wait..So now I was waiting. And do you know what for? I will tell you. I was waiting for Amy Campbell to take my bait. It would only be a matter of time now and then she would be mine for the taking. Games are something I do not play, and this little baited note would be the end of it. She'll come to me..
