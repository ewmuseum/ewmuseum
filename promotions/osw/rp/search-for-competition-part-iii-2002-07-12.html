Search for Competition: Part III

12 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 12th, 2002 Holy shit, Twisted Metal didn’t even resemble competition! But boy, it was sure fine to crush their little tin-heads! Heh… Oh man, will this woman ever move! I’ve been waiting in line for about twenty minutes now!! The scene fades in to a McDonald’s in downtown Anaheim, where one-half of the Tag Champions is waiting impatiently in line. The McDonald’s is rather busy and one would expect someone to recognize a celebrity like Short Circuit, but that’s the problem. SC doesn’t want to be recognized. So instead of the usual dressing, he’s decked out in a Fubu Sweatshirt, baggy jeans and sunglasses. The sweatshirt also has that little handy thing called a hood, which is over Shorty’s head. The woman in front of him is really disgusting looking, hence why she’s taking so long in the line. She looks about three hundred pounds, with a flower dress on. Real keeper this one… She looks to be buying about enough beef to stock for nuclear winter. Short Circuit shakes his head in disgust. Dammit, why is taking her so long! Damn this is boring. Well, it is better than what’s happening in frickin’ OS-Dub. I hate it. I’m a tag champion and nobody has stepped up and challenged us! God, I wish Twisted Metal would have put up a better fight. This boredom is eating away at me!! Short Circuit shakes in frustration, almost twitching. He looks at the ceiling and starts tapping his foot. C’mon fatty just move it! What the hell is your problem?! I’ve never seen a stupider fat bitch in my life! MOVE! MOVE!!! MOOOOOOVE!!! “MOVE!” Short Circuit screams out aloud, accidentally. Everyone in the fast-food restaurant slowly turns and stares at him, adding to his discomfort. He giggles nervously, looking around. “Uh… sorry, day-dreaming…” Everyone shrugs and gets back to munching down on their fattening burgers. Dammit, this boredom is eating away at me! Oh well, hopefully next week I’ll get something to look forward too! The Throwbacks or the Cult, huh? Well, it’ll be fun either way. Cult’s got that whole gothic thing that pisses Rip off, so he’ll be psyched. Plus I owe Hess a few whippin’s after the fucka mashed my face at Ascension… Short Circuit reaches into his pocket and accidentally drops his wallet. He reaches down to snatch up the wallet and returns to his standing pose, only to see that yet another whale of a woman has cut infront of him. Fuck! Could this get any worse? I hate these goddamn places. Should have had a pizza or something delivered. Some Taliban-wannabe dropping off a pizza is better than some Jenny Craig prospect. God, boring lives of the rich and famous. That’d be classic. Actually I wouldn’t mind going a few rounds with the Throwbacks. Those boys are tough sumbitches. Dave Gibson has gotta have the most true line in alla wrestling. Shut Up and Wrestle. God I wish someone would do that with us. Clay Vendetta ain’t no push over either, even though last time we locked horns it was for about two seconds before sending him for a ride offa Mount St. Hell’s End. Heh… Short Circuit grins to himself after the happy memories. The first pig-woman leaves the line with her mountain of food as the second steps up. SC taps his feet, looking around until he hears a soft, quiet whistle. He looks around, but then the source of the whistle becomes apparent. Oh GOD! Did she just… do what… GOD IT STINKS! The woman giggles, muttering an excuse me as she returns to order. Short Circuit almost turns green with disgust before snapping. “WOMAN! GET THE FUCK OUTTA THIS LINE!” Short Circuit grabs the woman by her sweatpants and tosses her quickly to the side. He then walks to the counter, ignoring all the stares and whispers directed at him. He looks to the cashier and spits on the floor. “I’d like a small fry.”
