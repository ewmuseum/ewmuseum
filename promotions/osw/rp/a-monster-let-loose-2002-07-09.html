A Monster Let Loose

9 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 9th, 2002 Same asshole place. Same asshole town. Same asshole people. It was the usual at the pub, nothing new. It was the same thing every damn day. Scotty and Rex would start to bicker over a stupid pool game, TJ and Russ would whistle at any good lookin' woman that passed their way, and of course...the usual drunks at the bar itself. Who am I? My name is Roger Harris, I'm the owner and bartender of this pub. I'm very picky about money, so I mainly do all the work 'round these parts. If anyone gets rowdy in MY pub...heh...well, I got a Shotgun right underneath this here bar thats just WAITIN' to shoot off. 'Hey Rog', TJ says. Yep, it's good ol' TJ. He may be pervert to the women, but he's a helluva guy to talk ta'. Never gave me any trouble, always cool and always comin' back...and givin' me money. Thas' what I like about em most. 'How ya doin' TJ? How's life treatin' ya?' I asked em back. Hell, ya gotta be friendly when you're in my line of work...it's the only way to keep em comin' back. 'It's ok, little good, little bad. Ya know how it is. But hey Rog, can ya get me a double shot of Tequila...I'm alittle dry...' 'Sure thing TJ, comin' right up' and in a matter of seconds...it was in front of him. And man, did I mention TJ could drink? If I had more customers like him, I could retire at the age of 39 years old. Shot. Now while TJ's in his drunken slumber, I'm just waitin' for them god damned trouble makers Scotty and Rex to come down to this bar and start bickering about who owes who a drink. It's so damn stupid...in the end, it's always Scotty that ends up buyin' em. What the hells the point of a fight if you know what's gunna happen? I never understood these people, let alone this business much...but for some reason...I just love runnin' this here pub. It never really occured to me why...I just do... Just than...as I'm just hearin' Scotty and Rex startin' at the pool tables...I see my pub door swing open and almost break off the damn hinges. It scared the hell outta me...especially when I saw this big sonuvabitch come from the outside. Hell, this guy got everyone's attention...the guys...the girls...and me. He was a MEAN lookin' bastard too. His brown hair was hoverin' over his pale face...and his face looked like he was ready to kill someone. I'm surprised his damn shoulders fit through the door. His muscles were damn near rippin' through his leather jacket...I swallowed a wad of spit down my throat...cause this fella' was comin' toward me. But suddenly, I got alittle more at ease...cause all he did...was sit down...damn near broke the chair when he did...and sure as hell took two spots with his muscles...but nonetheless..he didn't look like he meant no harm. I looked over at TJ, who just stared at the big fella'. TJ's eyes were wide with shock, as he looked over at me slowly and asked in a low whisper, 'Who is THAT guy?' I shrugged and answered the best I could...'A customer'. I walked over to the big guy, and placed my hands on the counter. I looked him in the face, but he didn't look back...he just looked at the counter. I smiled the best I could, fightin' off alittle shock, 'What can I do ya for stranga'?' The guy didn't speak, all he did was shoot up a huge hand, and pointed out in back of me to the bottle of tequila. I looked at the bottle and back at the big fella', 'Want a regular shot or a double?' The guy looked up from behind the shadows that formed from his hair. His eyes were still friggin' mean, and looked like he was gunna hit me or somethin...but all he did was answer, 'I want the whole thing...' I never really got an order for the whole thing, but I didn't ask no question when he slapped down a few tens on the counter and directed at me to hand it over. I didn't waste no time and gladly gave em the bottle. And that sucker drank that down. I looked at em in amazement and shock, and finally asked, 'So fella' what's your name? I don't see ya 'round these parts' But before he could answer... 'FUCK YOU REX, you know you lost the damn game!' 'The hell I did. Rog, would you tell this idiot that I won the damn game?' In came Rex and Scotty. TJ slowly walked away from the bar, and nodded at me, as Rex and Scotty approached the bar and continued their damn bickerin. 'Hey Rog, Rex here is buyin, two Coronas!' yelled out Scotty. Rex didn't appreciate that much and pushed Scotty in the shoulder lightly. 'Fuck you Scotty. Rog, this cheatin' bastard lost...so HE'S payin...' yelled back Rex. 'What are you talkin' about, I beat your ass!' Scotty yelled out, this time gettin' in Rex's face. 'You betta' back up jackass!' ok, now Rex pushed Scotty in the chest with a damn pool cue. 'Now fellaz, easy now' I tried to stop it...but they wasn't budgin'. They just kept pushin and pushin and pushin. Rex finally broke out and punched Scotty in the chest, than I see Scotty smashin Rex in the head with his elbow. This is the point I know I gotta stop it...I reached for the shotgun...when I see Rex grab his pool cue and lift it in the air... 'Oh Shit...' Crack. Scotty dodged the shot...and in no second later...Rex cracked the cue over...the big guy. The big guy's head went down on the bar...and I looked at Rex in a damn fury. 'REX! WHO THE FUCK DO YOU...' Than...I heard a loud gruntin'...like a wild beast. I looked at Rex and Scotty's eyes...they were wide...with fear. I looked where they were...and I saw it...the big sonuvabitch was on his feet...not a damn scratch on him...he looked down at the floor... Where a shattered Tequila bottle lay... 'You..fuckin'...made...me...drop...my...drink!' the big guy yelled out in anger. Rex and Scotty looked a bit scared, but Scotty still approached, like a jackass, and got in the big guy's face... 'YAH? And what you gun' dew 'bout it ya faggot?', god lord does Scotty got a damn big mouth. He even pushed the guy a bit... And than...it happened. I saw the big guy grunt at Scotty, and in a split second...The big guy had his hand wrapped around Scotty's neck and hurled him over my bar and into a full wall of liquor! I was damn near shocked...he threw Scotty like he was a damn rag doll... Rex looked at the scene in horror, as the big fella' looked right at Rex...with his broken pool cue... 'I...paid...good...money...for...that...FUCKING...DRINK!', the big guy yelled out...Rex, for the first time I ever seen, backed off... But he didn't get far...cuz the jackass tried to hit the big guy again...with the rest of the pool cue. But the big sonuvabitch grabbed Rex's arm and twisted it the wrong way...ya know...that way the arm ain't supposed to go...yah. I saw Rex yelp like a little puppy, as the big guy grabbed Rex by the waist and flip him THROUGH the damn pool table...ya know...I had just about enough of that... This big fucker just ruined my liquor AND my pool table...I reached under my bar...I was gunna let this fucker have it...but when I looked for the gun...it wasn't there... It was in the hand of the big bastard! How the hell did he get it? I have no idea. The big guy just looked at me and grinned, when he crushed the barrel of my shotgun like a can of beer. He than handed me the broken gun, and slapped down a few hundred dollar bills on my counter... He was payin' for the damages... The big fella' turned away from the scene, but not without snatchin' an extra bottle 'a' liquor...but before he left...he stopped at the door he nearly broke when he got in, and looked over his shoulder at me... 'You asked what my name was...right?' I didn't answer, I just looked in complete and utter shock...and all he did, was smile as if nothin' ever happened... 'The name's Armour...thanks for the drink,' he had no emotion in his face...it was like any other day for him. I just hope I never see that man again... It's bad for business, don't ya know.
