Refocusing

26 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 26, 2002 What is it that makes a man great? Jesus St Crux stands in front of his bathroom sink, with one hand palm up and open, letting the pouring tap water pool there. Somewhere off in another room, he can hear Caridad packing her bags, and beyond that, Steppenwolf plays on his stereo. The cold water in his hand numbs the feeling, but not his memories. Determination? His eyes whince a bit as the memory from nearly two weeks ago... Frankie Bones, cruelly branding Jesus' abdomen. The searing pain had made Jesus collapse at the time, but somehow, despite the pleas of Caridad, he had still gone out to participate in his scheduled match. Jesus' free hand gingerly touches the bandage covering the damaged skin. Thanks to Frankie's adept ministrations, the burn had been small, although extremely painful. The doctors had insisted Jesus take a few weeks off to let the wound heal properly, but he would have none of it. The doctors gained a small victory by making sure St Crux wasn't cleared to wrestle last week, but had resigned themselves to the fact they'd probably have to care for the wrestler again soon, since he had been booked in a six-man, cage match for the number one contendership to the cruiserweight title. Passion? He raises his gaze to look at himself in the mirror. His spirit was almost broken these last few weeks. He had signed with OSW for the competition... a roster of men and women who just possibly loved this sport as much as himself. Instead... rape... assault... possibly even murder... No... Almost broken... but not quite. These vile acts burned him, worst that any branding Frankie Bones could ever inflict. Jesus clenches his fist, squirting the water resting there into the sink. What is it that makes a man great? Jesus never backed down from any challenge, and he wouldn't back down from this one. And it was definately a challenge. He could fall, and join masses of the Frankie Bones, the Graphic Violences, the Cults, the Think Tanks... But he won't. Jesus stares at his reflection. He'll stand against them and do what he always has and always will. The fact that one man can make a difference.
