Inclination

9 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 9th, 2002 You gasp for air as he pulls your body free from its chilled grave. The warmth of room temperature enters your lungs in a lump of excess oxygen, shoving itself down your throat like some unneeded digestive. That air you so desparately needed is coming to you, but too much of something is not necessarily a good thing. You pry at your eyes to open them, yet they already are. Clouds of blurred vision skulk past your eyes and rest upon his brow. You choke again on the air swiftly filling your insides and manage to spit up some constraining water still lodged in your larynx. Your vision is slowly coming back to you, but your constant notice of it seems to bring it to a stunted pace. No time is passing...you're worrying too much about your condition...and why a child is pulling you from the tub. His hair is slightly matted over his eyes, black in color, or so you think. Your eyes haven't fully adjusted without that water that was just recently bathing them. They struggle to focus on the boy as he grunts, using his thin arms to pull you towards him. Your body falls limply against his as if you're embracing, setting him off balance for only a moment of which he quickly recovers his footing. The boy pats your back a few times and you feel more water start to rise in your throat. The muscles in your neck seem to spasm as the fluid exits your mouth and falls back to rest with the remaining ice cold water you're sitting in. This is the first time you notice how badly you're shaking. You're body is heating up and your nerves are beginning to regain their prestige. 'Are you finished, Antony?' The words are deciphered as if they were being barked forth from a twisted megaphone. You feel the boy rotate his body and look away from you. Somehow you know his eyes don't fall upon your body, now a blue and purple contortion. 'Nearly.' That gaze of his falls upon you as you hear the heavy thuds of steel toed boots turn away from the doorway of the room. You blink a few times and think, for once, about moving. Your muscles react slowly and require the utmost concentration to allow them movement. Your arms grip to your sides, searching desparately and drunkenly for the sides of the tub. The boy's arms tighten around you and with another grunt, he pulls you further towards him. 'You don't need to move, mister...you're alright for now.' 'for now' Two words ringing through your ears repeatedly and in a blaring voluminous mass. The two words that have freed your tunneled hearing and seemingly pumped the bloods through the proper arteries and veins. You grip at the sides of the tub and push yourself skyward. The boys arms leave you as you manage to reach your feet. Finally, you turn your head toward him, looking down at him. With your assumption proving itself to be correct, the boy looks up at you and pushes aside a trussle of hair from his eyes with his wet hand. He blinks once and you breathe in deeply. 'Mister...you should really stay down.' 'Wha...why?' You speak your first words since your new birth. Moments ago, you could have sworn your life was at its end. You'd left your family and friends behind you unwillingly and were residing somewhere you did not know you'd end up. That cold water had seized you and taken you for nearly ten minutes and it had taken it's toll. 'He'll be back. You heard him, I saw it in the muscles in your back. They shook when he spoke. It's okay...it's okay to be afraid.' You haven't a clue what this little child is talking about. He must be at least ten years old and he's speaking such things...unsettling to say the least. 'I used to be. But now, he takes care of me.' 'Who are you talking about?' You turn your body toward him as he begins to walk away from you. You watch the boy exit the room and bring a hand to your face to wipe the free drops of water that still rest there. Crouching down, your lower abdomen suddenly feels as if it were back in the water. You gasp in reaction to the reminder and look down at the culprit. Your belt buckle, shiny silver in color and cold as the water you're standing in. You immediately unbuckle your belt and pull it from the waist of your khakis, tossing it aside just as the boy comes back into the room. He rests a towel, blue collared shirt, and blue slacks over the seat of the toilet and looks up to you. 'Change. He will see you after.' The boy exits just as soon as he entered, closing the door behind him delicately, treating it like it was about to shatter. You hesitantly step free from the tub and onto the floormat, wiggling your toes to rush blood to them, an informal invite to share the same warmth your body has been gathering all this time. You unbutton your pants, looking around at the room. Nothing but white walls and white linoleum tile serve as a setting for your confused state. A hand reaches out and clutches the towel from the toilet, bringing it up and drying your face first. It's coarse textures slides across your skin roughly, but manages to serve its purpose and dry every pore. As you continue to ready yourself for this man Antony spoke of, your mind wanders to another place, trying to remember how you ventured to an ice cold tub in the first place. You remember you were in there and for how long, but anything previous to that is something that escapes your reach. You bite your lower lip and look up, as if your attempting to scour the insides of your head for the answer. For a moment you do nothing but think. A very soft knock to the door breaks your thought process and you drop the towel down lower to cover yourself from the knocker's eyes. Instead, the door ceases to open, and Antony's tiny, confident, and diminutive voice floats through. 'He's ready for you, mister. Just proceed down the hall to your left when you're finished. You have three minutes or he will come in and speak with you there.' Your mind begins to race as you towel your lower body and begin to pull the clothes onto your body in the appropriated hurried fashion. You have no idea as to who or what that child is referring to, but something inside you tells you that you best rush. You button your new change of pants and tuck in that blue collared shirt, stepping forward and onto the white linoleum. The hair on your arms raises in reaction, that cold coming back to you yet again. You know from that moment that the temperature of the water within that tub will forever serve as the nemesis of all your fantasies' protagonists. You reach for the doorknob and turn, your mind stopping your hand and turning your gaze to your forgotten belt on the floor, that hideous buckle resting against the wall. You shake your head and decide to leave it be. Turning back to the door, you grip the knob harder than you had at first and swing it open. Instead of allowing it to rest against yourself, you release your hold upon the knob, the door swinging back and hitting the wall instead of your body. You step into the hallway and look out to your right. More white walls. Barren like an apartment vacated by a daughter trying to escape her persistent brute of a father. You blink and look back to your left: the road Antony had informed you to take. You begin walking, each footstep multiplying twenty fold into almost spasmatic heartbeats. You don't know what you're going to but your insides apparently do. They know something you don't. As you reach the end of the narrow hallway, you reach another bare white wall and a door to your west. 'Turn the knob. He's waiting. You still have...seventeen seconds, mister.' You turn upon his words, heart pounding even faster from the surprise. Antony now sports a new watch to which he holds up to his face, in his line of vision. Odd way to view a timepiece, but after all, children are odd. They do grow out of certain things. 'Thirteen. Open the door, mister.' Antony's wrist moves away after his insistence, giving you a clear view of his narrow, hazel eyes. 'Go on.' He motions his arms as if he were a polite host. Your hand reacts before the rest of you and grips the knob. You turn it and take a breath, pushing forward and stepping with the new open entryway. Empty. One lounge chair, upholstered in a gleaming white material, faintly resembling leather, sits in the middle of the room. You look down. More white tiled linoleum. You turn around to scout Antony and, much to your surprise, he's there, closing the door behind you. 'Where is he, Antony?' The child turns back to you and motions his head forward to the chair. You move your head back towards your original observation view. Obviously, nothing has changed. 'Is he coming?' You speak as you notice the large french doors to the right of the room, painted white as well. Curtains are draped over the clear glass windows, obstructing any view to the inside. 'Is he in there, Antony?' He whispers in retort, just loud enough for your ears to register what he says. 'He doesn't like me to speak too loud, mister...' Antony tilts his head down, his eyes still fixed upon that chair. You blink and shift your eyes about the room, conducting a quick study of the room. White walls. Plaster. Bare. You look back to Antony who still stares intently forward at the chair. He clears his throat, noticeably doubling his efforts to muffle any unwanted noise. 'Is he your father, Antony?' 'No, mister...' You look away from him and back over to the french doors, almost mindlessly stepping towards them. 'Mister...he doesn't like to be ignored.' Your feet stop you and hold you in place. 'Antony, there's no one here.' You sigh and shake your head. His eyes dart over to you and then back to the chair. Another whisper slides over his lips, gliding upon the tiled floor and grabbing at your feet. 'He sees you.' They clutch at the cuffs of your pants and begin to climb higher as you stare at the chair. Ascending they go, grasping at your waistband and tugging at the bottom of your new collared shirt. Further they trek, clutching at the material around your collar, using each button for leverage to leap upon your chin and scurry into your mouth. You feel those words inside you, using your brainstem as a rope and climbing towards the heavens. 'No one's there...' They reach their destination and claim residence upon your brain. You know what's going on. It took too long to free yourself from your forgotten memories robbed by that bathtub full of icy water, but with a few spoken words, they were free to burst to realization. 'Mister?' You tumble the thoughts inside your head, trying to find the quickest reasoning behind the boy's imagination. You stare at the couch. 'Mister?' Your eyes don't move. The tendons wrapped behind them stiff as a corpse, your retina blinded, not allowing you to see the ghost Antony speaks of. You stare at wasted space, breath slowing and heart beating slower now. You weren't afraid of anyone. 'He' was nothing but an empty chair, void of any human form or animal. 'Mister???' The raised volume allows the words to seep in, yet you ignore them and toggle through your thoughts. You try as hard as you can to remember what happened before the tub. Your eyes shoot to the french doors randomly, maybe trying to visually forcefeed you a clue. 'MISTER?!' Slowly, they creep down the glass and stop upon the handles. 'MISTER?!! ARE YOU ALRIGHT?!?!' Like a Magic Eye image, you stare at the handles, hoping for something to show up before your eyes. You lick your lips and breathe in quickly, your vision shooting forth in a closed tunnel, the exit blocked by those damned door handles. You elect to step forward and do so. A hand suddenly grips your wrist and the handles begin twisting. 'MISTER!!!!!' Your eyes shoot back towards Antony and everything comes back to you. 'Are you okay?' The boy looks up at you and blinks, flooding worry filling his dilated pupils. You nod slowly and begin turning your head back towards the french doors. Antony again yanks at your wrist, but this time you aren't letting him steal your eyes from you. The doors are open now. You see nothing but boxes stacked high upon eachother, some resting on shelving units, others pushed off into corners of that room behind the door. Another yank comes to your arm, your confusion of the unexpected scene behind the door jarring your earlier refusal to look back at him. Your eyes once again make their way down to Antony as he stares up at you. 'Mister? Are you sick, Mister?' You speak for the first time in what seems like forever, a piece of you hoping it quells Antony's rude habit for the moment so you can look back to the doors. 'It's probably just from that freezing tub.' The thickening stench of a Russian accent leaps through the air. 'Ethan, what on earth are you going on about?' Your head nearly spins off your neck to look back to that supply room. A thin man, tall, with silver hair that stretches past the shoulders of his silver trenchcoat, stares back at you, those doors you so intently wanted to see, closed behind him. His arms are folded behind his back as he takes a few steps forward, the light of the room shooting off his silver lensed glasses, and his hair rocking in motion with his movement at his hips. 'Ethan...are you feeling well today? I hope the operation isn't tooling with your mind again.' Thick Russian descent fills his words as a slight grin protrudes upon his face. Your hopes haven't been met. He's pulling at you again. Hopes leaving you at the greatest instant are something you do not take well. You turn back to him, grinding your teeth together and glare at him. 'WHAT?!!?!?!' Your voice seems to deepen, soaked in rage and littered with ill regard. 'WHAT IN THE F*CK DO YA WANT, YA LIL C*NT!?!?!' 'Don't speak to my son like that!!' Too many voices. You turn back towards the supply room, that silver Russian gone and replaced by a short woman, racing towards you at full speed. She comes within five feet of you, nearly diving to your side. Something inside you rockets out of the atmosphere and propels out of control. Your left arm shoots out and catches her tiny skull in mid air. You clasp your fingers about her cranium and slowly lift her into the air, staring at your arm instead of her flailing form. You tilt your head and study the markings upon it. Before you can think, the child tugs at your arm impatiently again. You drop that woman and swing towards him, twisting your right arm quickly and gripping him by that matted hair upon his head. Just as you begin lifting, you notice your surroundings have changed. Despite the boys high pitched squealing, panicked kicking, and those random cries from all around you, the eyes that had shown you nothing before now point you inside short shelves. Potato chips in front of you, ice cream behind you. You stand on the tips of your toes and peer over at the beef jerky in the aisle before you. Suddenly, you remember it all. And, again, that one emotion that has controlled you, that one emotion that has molded you at will and made you do so many things that now came as a normality and pleasure to you, seizes every enormous muscle of your colossal body. Your fist clenches tighter to the boys hair as you walk around the aisle, shoving a gaping mouthed man out of your way and over a counter into the clerk behind it. Several tobaccos rain down upon both men as they hit the opposite side of the wraparound counter and you turn down the aisle. You stop, shaking the boy in your palm as a means of warning, and allow your eyes to dart around the sheleves before you. 'Where in the f*ck is it?!' You growl and stomp to your right, the boy still squeaking out in fright and terror, and grin, a gruff laugh involuntarily wandering out through your mouth. Your hand shoots forward and grips your prize as you drop the boy. You tear the packaging free and push him back against the shelf with your foot. The boy cries aloud again as you tear a bit of silver lining from the treasure. It's dull color manages to glint a small bit of light to your eye, reminding you of what birthed this emotion again. You hated the hallucinations that came along with that operation. But, as certain as you were that they had rid you, your last few moments tell you differently. Bending down, you put that prize you just brutally pulled from the shelve between your teeth, pulling the boy back and pressing him to your right arm. You step forward and push him facefirst into the shelf, stepping away and pinning him up with your bent arm. He cries louder now, ignoring your quick snarl and growl at him as you pull that piece out of your mouth and push it partially against your decorated skin and his chest. Moving your hand, you glance back to the sticky material and grab the entire treasure from the shelf. 'LEMME GO!!!' 'Antony' flails wildly, trying to free himself desparately like an animal caught in a trap. Your mind grins at the thought of him gnawing a body part off to have his taste of freedom again. You quickly move your left hand around your right arm and the boy, treasure screeching in all its wonderous glory. You bring his legs to a stop. 'The tape hurts!!! IT'S TOO TIGHT, TOO TIGHT!!!' You growl again, a six year habit of yours. A few more quick movements around your right arm and you allow it to rest at your side, the boy coming with it. You begin walking away from the aisle, glaring at the few onlookers inside and shooting a glance over in the direction of that crazy diving woman. She still lay still, body calm as a snow drift, eyes clasped shut. They say no words as you walk out of the gas station. You don't even remember how you arrived here. But you were leaving now. A hallucination had visited you again without warning, but you were leaving with a child. Not one born of your own power but one strapped to your right arm, nearly mummified in your prize: duct tape. 'Antony' can no longer move. You left his crying mouth open to breathe, but he's greedily spent most of his cries already. You know he won't be much trouble. After all, Clara emptied her cries early. You're just working off your experience.
