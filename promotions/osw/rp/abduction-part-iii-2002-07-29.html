Abduction: Part III

29 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29-07-02 He had had a long time to think about it. Dwell on it. Worry, stress and sicken himself over it. The plane had barely touched down before he had leaped out; at least that’s what it felt like. His face showed rage beyond no other. That voice rung throughout his head, agitating him and plaguing him. … D-D-David? Please... Help me... They’re HERE... The veins popped in his head and his knuckles whitened as he jumped into his nearest mode of transportation in a beat-up old cab. Clutching his sports bag in one hand, Gibson’s anger shrouded over his worry as the 12-gauge lay beneath his clothes and equipment at the bottom of the bag. Far from a few miles away, the Gibson residence sat as if almost in silence. The front door lay open, though none of the passing people cared much as to even look in. In the den, the doilies and covers, sewing machines and family treasures lay strewn around. On the mantle adjacent to the door, there sat perhaps Gibson’s most prized possessions. The IOW Television Championship, and a framed picture of Gibson holding the jOlt Revolutionary Championship next to his mother, with a little message inscribed at the base of the frame. The thoughts of his dearly beloved mother ran through his head, soiled and raped by the sudden intrusion of those glowing red eyes. Gibson saw his mother, then saw his evil glare staring back at him. He was barely four blocks away from the residential area when the little old cab erupted in a ball of black billowing smoke and nuts and bolts. Gibson threw the driver a few dollars more than he should have and bolted from the cab. The sounds of struggle and moaning emitted from the house, while Gibson ran for his life, or rather hers, along the streets towards her home. Seeing the house just ahead of him, he unzipped the sports bag, and pulled out the shotgun. The rage was flushing through his veins, turning him deep purple. Sprinting towards the house with the shotgun in hand, the worry of being arrested completely subsides as the voices swim around in his head. Leaping over the picket fence and rolling, Gibson hops back to his feet and presses himself again the wall of the house, before the door. In silence, he peeps around, and then walks into the doorway. “Help………meee………please…please…help…” Gibson recognised the voice immediately and dropped the shotgun in shock. Looking into the den the crumpled pile of flesh, lying in a pool of blood sent shivers down his spine. His mouth filled with a taste so sour he vomited there in the hall. His knees were as jelly, his nerves shaky, and his voice filled with horror as he entered the den, the door wide open. “M-mom…mom…are you ok?” Gibson collapsed to his knees in the blood before the pile. A blanket was covering the pile, with only a pair of old slippers and her hair in view. Gibson embraced the body, crying, and slowly, with tears rolling down his eyes, pulled back the blanket………and recoiled in horror. Before him, clad in his mother’s clothes, was the little purple face of a demon Gibson loathed. To his uttermost horror, the little Imp’s mouth opened, but it was not the gruff, squeaky voice that sounded, but rather a voice that Gibson held dear. The voice that gave him hope in his darkest hours away from her…his mother’s voice. “David what did I tell you about talking to strangers! HAHAHAHAAAAA!!!!!!!!!” Gibson’s eyes widen and he grabs Minion around the throat, choking the Imp as the incantation wears off and Minion’s voice returns to normal. As Gibson chokes the Imp, the door to the den creaks shut, and from behind it emerges the large frame of Hessian. Gibson fails to notice as Hessian saunters behind him, and pulling back a right hand, drives his gauntleted fist straight into the back of Gibson’s skull, sending him falling to the ground. Gibson looks up dazed, as Hessian hovers over him and grabs him by the hair, pointing him to the other side of the room as Vampir and Sepiroth Du Luc emerge from behind a sofa, clutching Dave Gibson’s mother, who they have tied down and gagged. Gibson lets out a weak yell for his mother, but barely finished it as Hessian brings another hard right down on the back of Gibson’s head, knocking him out. Vampir turns to Gibson’s mother and whispers in her ear. “This predicament may seem a little extreme…but if you tell anyone about this incident…you’ll find your son mailed back to you, piece by piece…understand?” Gibson’s mother’s eyes filled with tears as she nodded regretfully. Vampir and SDL threw her over and onto the cushiony sofa. As Minion picked himself up off of the ground and cleaned himself off, Vampir and SDL walked over to Gibson’s motionless body, while from in the hallway footsteps could be heard…and as the den door opened, in walked Bethany Yamato and the Cultist, clad as always in black robes. They all stood around Gibson, in silence. “Where were you two?” Hessian asked, nodding at Bethany and the Cultist, who simply drew its long, twisted dagger across its throat, motioning towards Gibson’s mother, while Bethany nodded to the door and the hallway. “Just checking out the house…she’s just a lonely old woman with a loving son…” replied Bethany, sneering at Gibson’s mother. Hessian pulled Gibson up and threw him onto his shoulder, turning to the door he pointed at the shotgun. “Minion…hide that…” then he pointed to Gibson’s mother, while looking at the Cultist. “Untie her and remove the gag, and if she moves before we leave, kill her…” The Cultist gladly flipped Gibson’s mother, and used the twisted blade of the dagger to cut through the ropes holding Gibson’s mother’s hands and legs together. Barely missing the skin, the Cultist cut away, and then ripped the gag off of her mouth. She sat up as the Cultist got up off of the sofa, turning to see the rest of The Cult leaving the house with Gibson still on Hessian’s shoulder, unconscious. Hissing at Gibson’s mother, the Cultist drew the dagger lightly across her throat, leaving a shallow wound in the shocked woman’s neck. As blood surfaced, the Cultist hid the dagger in its robes and backed off, pointing at Gibson’s mother. The front door of the Gibson house closed with a might SLAM!, and as Gibson’s mother broke into a flurry of tears and blood…her son was taken from her…carried away into the darkness……… Fin
