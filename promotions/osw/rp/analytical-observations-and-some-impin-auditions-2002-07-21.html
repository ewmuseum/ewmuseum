Analytical Observations...and some Impin' Auditions

21 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21-07-02 “SLAM! WAS A TRIUMPH!!!” Pacing back and forth in the locker room, Hessian pumps his fist in the air, his eyes glowing fiercely, fresh off of one of the most prolific nights of Slam! in The Cult’s history thus far. While the fans pile out of The Igloo and the other wrestlers head for their hotels or other post-show destinations, The Cult are nestled in their locker room. While Hessian paces back and forth, Vampir stands with his back against one of the lockers, arms crossed with a stare fixed on the door. The two Cultists are sat on a long, wooden bench. The first Cultist sits rubbing one of its sores, attained from the Cafeteria Brawl earlier in the night, while the newly acquired second Cultist picks bits of dirt from under its nails using the long, lethal dagger it had threatened Axtell with, the sharp, twisting blade gleaming in the light. “That it was, Hessian…that it was…however-…” Vampir starts, but is cut off as Hessian interrupts. “There’s no however about it Vampir! The Cult are entered into that Tag Tournament, we slaughtered those damn women, have two possible recruits, and gained ourselves a second Cultist! Albeit we were beaten down at the hands of those bottom-feeding rats, but our Cultists, us, will have our vengeance next week!” Hessian paces back and forth as the Cultists watch on, and Vampir maintains his stare towards the door. The second Cultist glances at Vampir, and lowers its dagger. Whistling at Vampir, the Cultist nods as if to ask what the matter is. “Strategy. The Cult now has four, to the Throwbacks four…with a scheduled 8-man match next week…the war is fast approaching, we must decide on a game plan.” The second Cultist chuckles, and then draws the dagger across its throat. A smile appears on Vampir’s grotesque face, and as Hessian comes to rest by one of the lockers next to Vampir; the Soul Stalker nods his head slowly. “When the time is right…however we must-…wait!” “Yes?” Hessian asks. “If we are to destroy The Throwbacks, there are two ways in which to do it…one isn’t possible this far into the game, however the other is-…” SLAM! Vampir is cut off suddenly as the locker room door slams open suddenly, and the 3’11” frame of Minion appears in the doorway. With a cheeky wee smile on his face, Minion the Imp waves to The Cult, and then heads back out into the hallway. The Cultists cock their heads in confusion as Hessian chuckles and Vampir groans… “As I was saying, the only way to destroy The Throwbacks is to-…” SCREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEECH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Vampir is cut off again, and The Cult cover their ears as a horrifying screech echoes throughout the hallways…and Minion reappears at the door, trying to pull a large, heavy table into the locker room. Hessian roars out, forcing Minion to stop, then heads to the doorway, grabbing the table and pulling it in with little effort. Minion nods satisfactorily, then opens the door fully and again grins, from ear-to-ear. “Hessian, stick that in the middle of the room, near the back, just before them hooded chaps, then come over here.” Hessian obliges, and walks over to Minion. The Imp pulls a large sign from out of his black cloak and hands it to The Goliath. “Do us a favour and stick that to the door, then we can let the show begin!” As Hessian tapes the sign to the door, he takes a quick moment to read it first: - “CULT AUDITIONS INSIDE!” Hessian sighs, then closes the door, to find Minion sitting at the table on one of the steel chairs, a folder thick with documents before him, and the usual desktop items in front of him; pens, pencils, lamp, ruler, notepad, pictures of Aimz from Ascension………and a happy little smile on his face. “What IS all of this for, Minion?” The Titanic Satanic Insaniac asks. “Tonight The Cult became a quintet, and I figured why not hold auditions for other members instead of wasting time recruiting?” The first Cultist shrugs its shoulders, while the second Cultist and Vampir stare at Minion. Hessian slams a fist down on the table, when suddenly someone knocks at the door. Minion gasps and waves his arms wildly. “Places! Places! Cultists, you’re good there…Vampir, move to the side of the room…and Hessian, you stand beside me at the desk!” The Cult chuckle and oblige, and clearing his throat, Minion looks over at the door. “Enter!” The door to the locker room slowly creaks open, and the atmosphere overcomes The Cult as the first person to audition enters the room…Trevor Morgan from popular the British soap opera – Eastenders. Minion nods as Trevor makes his way to the desk and stands before The Cult, and hands a piece of paper to Minion. “THAT’S MA RÉSUMÉ FUR YE A SILLY CUNT!!!” “Ah…Mr. Trevor Morgan…and this is the name you wish to go under should you be accepted?” “AYE MIN! NAEB’DY WAANTS TAE SEE THON WEE GOTHIC BASTERT WI’ A NAME LIKE ALEX FERNS MIN!!!” The Cultists chuckle, as Hessian leans over to Minion, with a slightly sarcastic tone in his voice. “You sure this isn’t one of Andy M.’s posse?” “No…just shut up and go with this…AHEM! Ok, Mr. Morgan, it says here on your résumé that you have experience in violence…care to expand on that?” “AYE MIN! AH PURE BEAT THE SHIT OOT MA WIFE FUR SAYIN’ SHE LOVED ME!!!” “Excellent…now the first-…OH WAIT! I almost forgot…we do have a second judge!” Suddenly, Minion opens his cloak and pulls out none other than Pop Idol and American Idol Co-Judge, Simon Cowell. Clad in a grey sweater and black pants pulled up past his stomach as usual, Cowell takes a steel chair and sits next to Minion. The British atmosphere fills the room as Simon stares Trevor down. “First impression are not good here Trevor. Uhhh, you do have the appearance of a violent man; the nationality accentuates that, although the overall look is something to be desired. A Kilt in July? Please!” “If you will Mr. Cowell, the appearance will come into play later, however, Mr. Morgan, as I was saying…the first category you must prove your worthiness in is dialogue…if you please?” “AYE MIN, GIES A MINIT YA SLEEKIT WEE BAWBAGS, BUT IF THON WEE BASTERT GETS ON MA TITS AH’M GAWNAE HAMMER ‘IM! AWRIGHT? NOO………………………Aye, thon wee Satan wis pure chibbin’ bricks through yer maw’s windaes…an’…an’ ah pure fucked yer maw and crucified some wee Bawbag cuz he didnae gies me a pickled onion wi’ ma burger supper………………HOO WIS THAT YA BASTERTS?!” Simon pulls his pants up to his nipples, and crosses his arms, marking down something on a piece of paper as The Cult watch on in awe, and Minion strokes his chin. “Yes…the accent and the dialogue is ok, but the pronunciation and abbreviation of all these words will not do, Simon?” “I shan’t even comment…the result would be me reminding all these Yanks how pathetic their country is, and how the poor education system is what leads to all of these unwanted pregnancies and obese, white trash people living in caravans, as you say and how good Britain is because I’m from there and I have had sex which prevents any insulting to be thrown my way…instead let’s skip to the second category, shall we, Minion?” “Uhhh…yeah…ok, Mr. Morgan, appearance. Now, your attire of a skirt and a shirt shall not do, you have 20 seconds to either tell us what a Cult should be wearing, or change into some attire yourself…” Trevor stands and thinks for a minute, then suddenly grabs an iron from atop one of the lockers and brandishes it towards Minion…” “ARE YOO’S SAYIN’ AH’M NO’ GID ENOUGH TAE BE WAN O’ YOO WEE GOTHY BASTERTS??? AH’LL KILL YEES!!!” “We simply asked you to produce some form of attire for us Mr. Morgan, calm down please…” “Your abuse of the apostrophe offends me…” Simon adds. Trevor’s eyes widen to the size of dinner plates, and he lobs the iron across the room, running out of the door as it crashes down next to the second Cultist, who jumps as the iron clatters next to it. Looking up, the second Cultist holds up its knife and growls. “Be right back…” “Send the next one in on your way!!!” Minion adds. With that the second Cultists exits the room in pursuit of Trevor Morgan, and as the screams of a disgruntled Scotsman echo out through the hallways, another figure enters the room, just as the second Cultist re-enters, with Trevor Morgan hanging from its knife by his spine. The second tryout stands before the table, glancing in shock as Trevor’s corpse twitches and shakes, hanging onto the Cultist’s dagger. “Ok…résumé? Name?” The second tryout hands over the résumé and clears his throat. “Ahem! My name is…Duckula…Count Duckula!” Simon and Minion glance at each other as the 4’6” cloak wearing Duck looks blankly back at them, and then they continue. “Ok Mr. Duckula…” “Please, call me David Jason-…I mean! Count Duckula…” “Ok…” Simon continues. “Mr. Duckula it says here you living a spooky castle above a small peasant’s village…you have a Nanny and a Butler and a Castle which can use matter transportation to…switch location?” Count Duckula nods. “So be it. Ok, well Minion…take it away…” “Ok, Count Duckula…well since we have one here we’re going to examine your reaction to a corpse. As we all know murder and mutilation is fun, but can you stomach this?” Minion points to the second Cultist, who removes the dagger from Trevor’s corpses spine and drives it through his face, the sound of the eye bursting and eye socket scratching causes Duckula to squint, but he puts a hand to his beak in horror as th Cultist pulls back on the dagger and grins as the blade snaps back up through the face, sending a large and sickening CRACK! throughout the locker room. Count Duckula watches as the thick, Scottish blood oozes down the face and stains into the clothes. “Oh my…is that? Is that Ketchup?” “Not this time around…that’s blood and eye jelly and boogers and stuff…” says Minion, grinning as Count Duckula turns from Green, to a slightly darker green, and runs out of the room, the sound of the Duck vomiting causing The Cult to laugh. “This is entertainment…” Hessian whispers. “NEXT!” Minion screams at the top of his voice as the door opens and who should enter, but the man himself…Rolf Harris-… “NEXT!!!” Minion shouts. A little sad frown comes over Rolf, who skulks back out of the room as Prince Harry enters the room. “Hello Harry…” Simon says. Harry waves at Simon then hands his résumé to Minion. As Minion reads it over, Harry pulls a joint and a cigarette out of his pocket and begins puffing on them simultaneously. “God I love smoking on a fag first thing in the morning…” “It’s almost midnight…” “Fags at midnight? Even better! You know if I keep smoking these little blighters, my teeth will become all British and then I can get Lung Cancer and die because the NHS is a pile of piss and if they gave the money to me for some Crack Cocaine and heavy alcohol it’d have been better spent…” Suddenly, before Minion can even ask the first question, the door slams open again, and in walks someone with a very happy smile on her face…Aimz. “D-…did you say Drugs and Alcohol?” “Yes ma’am…” “And you’re rich?” “Well…I’m the future King of England…” Aimz’ eyes widen and her tongue flops out, and the Firesnatch grumbles. “Wanna fuck?” she asks, trying to flaunt the flatness towards Harry. “Sure…you’ll be just another person to be royally fucked over by The Royal Family, just look at Britain!” With that, Harry and Aimz take off to the Janitor’s Closet, a trail of smoke burning up their tracks. Minion and Simon stare wide-eyed at the door, while Hessian and Vampir shake their heads in frustration, and the Cultists sit picking away at Trevor Morgan’s corpse. “Ok, Imp. You’ve have your fun now leave! Next week calls for planning and preparation and we can do neither unless we have-…” “ONE! VAMPIRE!!! AH! AH! AHHH!!!” The door to the locker room bursts open and the final tryout enters. Doting towards the table, the burly looking figure slams his résumé onto the table and nods his head. “I am here to tryout, yes?” “Um yes Mr…….The Count…let me take a look here…working with children, acknowledgement of bestiality and two homosexual puppets one with monobrow one without…what does “This résumé brought to you by the letter Fuck You!” mean exactly?” “It means I am your man!” says The Count, flailing his arms as though he was a puppet of some kind……… “Ok, Mr. Count…let’s begin, um…” “ONE! WANKER!!! AH! AH! AHHH!!!” The Count points at Simon Cowell laughing, and though we can withstand any insult, Cowell cannot underestimate the power of a Henson creation…and he explodes. The Cult laugh on as Minion continues the interview. “Ok Count…how familiar are you with the workings of the Cult?” The Count stares blankly at Minion, then points and laughs. “ONE! IMP!!! AH! AH! AHHH!!!” “Please stop that Count…answer the question now…” The Count turns to the Cultists. “ONE! CULTIST!!! AH! AH! AHHH!!!” Minion watches on, slightly frustrated as The Count carries on regardless. “TWO! CULTIST!!! AH! AH! AHHH!!!” Hessian cocks his head as Vampir sighs and the Cultists growl under their breath. A Janitor passes by the locker room, and stares inside, his eyes widening in horror as he continues on his cleaning duties. “ONE! HESSIAN!!! AH! AH! AHHH!!! TWO! CULTISTS!!! AH! AH! AHHH! I HAVE THREE! FINGERS!!! AH! AHHH! AHHH!!!” “Stop…please stop…Goddamnit will you just stop…” Minion pleads, getting really frustrated. “FOUR LOCKERS!!! AH! AH! AHHH!!! FIVE SATANISTS!!! AH! AH! AHHH!!! SIX!…SIX!…SIX! MINUTES UNTIL THE ARENA CLOSES!!! AH! AH! AHHH!!! SEVEN!-…OOOOAAAARGH!!!!!!!!!” The Count suddenly recoils and convulses in terror, and slumps in a pile to the ground, as whoever had their hand up The Count’s ass pulls out a bloodied stump, and Minion stands with fire burning in his eyes, and The Count’s dismembered head hanging out of his sharp little mouth. The Count and his puppeteer exit the room, leaving The Cult and Minion in complete silence… “……………………………So what was your plan to destroy The Throwbacks?” Hessian asks. “Well…if we want to destroy The Throwbacks…we have to use their weaknesses against them. And there is one major weakness I know of…” Vampir replies. “And that is…” “Let’s just say…Mother Knows Best…” Fin
