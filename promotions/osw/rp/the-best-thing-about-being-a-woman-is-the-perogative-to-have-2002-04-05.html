The best thing about being a woman is the perogative to have a little fun...

5 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 5th, 2002 (Note: PLEASE SHOOT ME!!! I USED SHANIA TWAIN LYRICS AS A TITLE!!! GAAAAAAAAH!!! Too...many...drugs.) ''Wakey wakey, sunshine!'' Timo Bolamba was standing over his redheaded counterpart's bed with fireballs of mock enthusiasm, mostly sarcasm... ''Unnnngh.....'' Campbell moaned, stuffing her face into the solid white pillow. ''...no...wake.......need.....sleep.'' ''And why do you need sleep?'' The Samoan put a finger to his stubbled chin. ''Oh...that's right, because SOMEONE didn't take my advice, instead she went out and drank her ass off!'' Sure, she had consumed a tad too much alcohol the night before, but that was to be expected...her best friend, one of the hardest partiers you could ever imagine, Breanne Lilievre had shown up the night before with a ticket to Slam; She was there for support, and let Amy have a little bit of fun in the meantime. ''Timo......piss off, I'm sick.'' So what if she got a little drunk? She was safe, she didn't drive...instead, she had to be dragged from the sidewalk outside by Bolamba and Bre, up to the room and put into bed, still clad in street clothes. Speaking of her clothes, when Aimz rolled over, all she could smell was the alcohol on her shirt, obviously spilled from the night before. It was on her breath, everywhere and, remembering the night before, she was instantly sick of the scent on her. Oh well, she might be in the 'Big leagues' now, but it didn't kill her to break loose once in a while. She had, thankfully enough, let her career take over her drug use, so that aspect of straight partying was no longer an issue...but the booze was still there, and Amy'd never turn that down. It's not like she was an alcoholic, she didn't crave it, didn't suffer from withdraw...she'd just go on a slight bender when she was let loose to party with friends...and that was a rare occasion at this point. ''Sick?'' He laughed in a kind way, almost a complete change of heart...now he went from being such a naggy big brother figure to the 'I can relate' sort. ''That's called paying the price of admission, Amy...you chose to drink, you chose to pay.'' Alright, so her occasional heavy partying was okay...but the night before, like Timo had said, Amy shouldn't have gone out. It was Thursday night, this was Friday morning...one day before Slam, Aimz couldn't afford to be so hungover but did she listen? Of course not! All she saw was her best friend/drinking buddy, a bottle of Crown Royal and a proverbial night on the town. She chose not to respond, instead rolling over again to face the back wall. So Timo poked her in the back again. ''Come on...I'll get you some Advil.'' Amy didn't exactly feel like getting up, she didn't exactly feel like being conscious at the moment. There was a headache the size of the Empire State Building pounding itself at her skull repeatedly...she hated headaches and this was the worst, one induced by a little too much pleasure in the beverage department. Face it, her head hurt too much to even let her THINK about heavy music...for the love of God, Britney Spears tunes were nearly running through her head! ''Okay...no Advil...maybe some Bacardi?'' Bolamba chuckled. ''That seems to heal you...'' ''GAAAAH!'' Amy groaned at the mere thought of more alcohol, this time stuffing her face UNDER the pillow. Yep, this was the hangover to top all hangovers...and only a day before Slam! She was going to have to toppled Drew Hostyle in THIS condition...the thought was sickening, even more so than the image of his face. That 'man' called himself what? ''OSW's hoodrat Hugh Hefner'', right? She knew he liked to speak in his gang slang, ebonics or what-the-fuck-ever...but did 'hoodrat' mean wiener fest? Because all Amy had ever seen around Hostyle was an overabundance of sausage, and judging by the disturbing view his spandex booty shorts gave, none of the bratwurst was attached. Uh oh, she was thinking again... The mention of Hostyle's spandex short led her to think of, to no surprise, the hero she didn't have...RAH, who wore his spandex in a considerably less-Mariah Carey-sense than Drew every did, could or would. That, of course, hurt. Every second she couldn't be near Rentie hurt...thinking about him being seen by others hurt, this was all entirely new to Amy...so much pain over things so trivial, it was definitely odd but oh well, she had to concern herself with a certain hangover... So finally, she moaned again through a veil of scruffy red locks... ''...Advil...'' ''Good girl...'' He smiled. ''I've gotta sneak past sleeping Eugene to get it, but I'll be right back.'' She didn't respond, just groaned as expected. Thank God she wouldn't have to try very hard tomorrow night...size meant nothing if the opponent had actual talent, so thank the good Lord she was loaded with the good shit (Talent, so stop your semen jokes.), otherwise she might actually have a challenge ahead of her! Yeah, facing Drew Hostyle...a challenge? Maybe for an alcoholic midget, and Amy wasn't that small, or that hard a drinker yet. The only thing she really had to worry about would be interference by the two men who, for some God damned reason, let Hostyle share a little bit of their limelight...the one that shone just behind them, a little under their elbows. She wondered if Drew had a problem with being a walking joke, a curtain-jerker that clung to better men in hopes that people may eventually see him as a wrestler...but it just wasn't working, and it wouldn't be getting any better for the Master of Sausage after a hundred and forty something pound woman beat him this week, right in front of the 'brothers' that never let him play marbles with them, however hard he tried. After a few moments of blissful silence that nursed her headache well, Timo came stampeding back in...a few pills and a glass of water in hand. ''Advil!'' The Samoan obviously had to work for it, he used such a triumphant tone. Amy managed to prop herself up against the headboard of the bed, groaning again as she grabbed the glass then the pills and downed everything in one big gulp before sinking back down to the cushy mattress. For some reason, Timo laughed. ''I invited Breanne up...I knew if I couldn't get you to drag your lazy ass out of bed, she would.'' ''Hrrrmmm...'' Another groan from Amy as she peered at the light streaming through little cracks in the closed blinds of the bay window. ''What time is it?'' ''Noon.'' ''What?!'' ''Yeah...'' He flung the blinds open, laughing when Aimz flung herself under the covers of the bed, sheltered from the blinding glare of the midday sun. ''..Ha! That gotcha' up!'' ''Not quite...'' She was tired, so very hungover...why couldn't he understand that? It really was bothersome to always have people who cared about you around, but the redhead could live with it...after all, at least they meant well. However many times Timo had tried to haul her out of bed, it only happened when the little whirlwind Amy had grown up beside strode into the room... ''Wake up call!'' Ah yes, Breanne...long time friend, constant motivation to do anything. How? Well, she had her ways...none of which were very pleasant. This time, Miss Lilievre decided to grab Amy by the arm and LITERALLY drag her out of bed, no fear of igniting her temper whatsoever...those two had an understanding and, though it confused Timo, he looked upon the scene with a quiet admiration...they were practically twins personality-wise. Amy laid on the floor a moment, rubbing the sleep from her eyes and eventually, after quite a struggle, standing up. ''Jeez...'' The dirty blonde, who's hair was left shoulder-length, left fairly unkempt this morning, looked her friend up and down with near disgust for a moment. ''...you look like hell!'' Normally, Amy would've killed someone for commenting like that...but this was Bre, this is what she was SUPPOSED to say. So she continued. ''You haven't been hungover in a while, I can tell. Shit,'' The slightly French Canadian half-chuckled, half-giggled. ''...I've gotta get you out more! Remember high school? You and I were straight partyin' all the fuckin' time! Man...a little hangover like this would've been like a runny nose!'' The Samoan Silencer rolled his eyes. ''Don't encourage her...I've got matches to fight with that girl's help!'' Aimz still hadn't said a word, she just leaned against the wall rubbing her temples until Breanne turned to her again. ''Awww c'mon...go take a shower, relax and try to feel better...we'll be waiting when you come out.'' ''Sounds good...'' Amy's voice still sounded sickly, weak. ''...maybe the Advil'll kick in by the time I'm done.'' At least she'd rid herself of the alcoholic stench... 36 MINUTES LATER Amy stepped back out into the room, soaking hair tied back with a weak black scrunchie...an interesting sight for her bisexual best friend, and male tag partner's eyes...why? She was wearing what she considered to be pyjamas...a black sport's bra and a pair of flannel pants laden with a Canadian flag patch on the back. ''Amy...'' Timo started, then chuckled a moment. ''...you're gonna have to start wearing more clothing around me. Especially with her here...God knows what she might try.'' ''Stupid...Samoan.'' Okay, so Bre wasn't the best with insults. That's where Amy stepped in... ''Right now, I really don't care who's looking at what...I just wanna relax all day, rest up for the match or something.'' ''Boo to rest!'' Breanne snorted, grabbing her arm. ''Let's go hang around McDonald's for hours and scare the old people!'' The smaller girl tried to drag Amy off, but quickly got out-powered. ''Sorry Bre, I'm all funned out...'' ''Alright,'' She pouted. ''...if you need me, I'll be at McDonald's. See ya tonight...'' That's all she said before giving a disappointed wave and walking out. Timo was too busy watching, and laughing at, a 'Marry Me Mom' episode of Jerry Springer...he only snapped out of the fits when Amy sat on the floor next to him. ''You know what'd be fucked up...'' He glanced at the screen for a moment. ''If you ballooned to like three hundred pounds, I lost like a hundred, we had a twisted drunken fling and you ended up carrying my interracial love child.'' Okay, Amy was beginning to think Timo had dipped into the Crown himself... ''Uhhh...why?'' ''Because we could make millions by appearing on like every episode of Jerry! Think about it, ESPECIALLY if you ended up having some weird lesbian attraction to the ki----'' She had to stop the slightly radical Samoan before he got ahead of himself. ''Think about what you're saying.'' Amy laughed. ''Well...I figured the best way to get your head off of being so hungover was to disturb you beyond human belief...'' Aimz could sense the coming rib by the tone of Timo's voice... ''I certainly found the thought of you carrying my child disturbing beyond belief, not to mention the rest of that crazy shit!'' ''That was almost as disgusting as thinking of Drew Hostyle with a man...'' ''It'd be even more outlandish to picture him with a woman.'' Bolamba laughed again, catching the course of the conversation. ''True...getting beaten by a woman just isn't feasible for him...but getting beaten in a rusty ring sure as hell isn't...I'm gonna prove that tomorrow night!'' ''Should I be standing up, swinging a bra around screaming 'You go girl!' by now?'' Amy smacked him half in earnest on the leg. ''Not funny...I hate that girl power bull...'' An almost evil grin crept across her face for a moment. ''I just think it's fun to be a girl WITH power.'' Timo reacted to the smack a little late, putting on a comically over-exaggerated pout. ''...owwie...'' Aimz laughed, but after a moment of silence the Samoan retorted. ''Go hit hero-boy...I'm an innocent bystander who makes the occasional comment!'' He stuck his tongue out in jest. ''Buuuut...I'm also a bystander with a manchild to care for, see ya!'' Gah...there went Timo. Shutting the TV off, thankful for the peaceful silence...Amy was exquisitely aware of being alone. She laid across the bed again, just letting her head wander wherever... Again, Hostyle and tomorrow's match came to mind...she still had 3Bi to think about, but that could be saved for later...right now, she had other, more important things than tearing a wannabe thug apart to think about. What would that be? Rent-A-Hero, of course! The PTC inter-fed Pay-per-view event was coming up soon...April fourteenth, to be exact. She HAD to tell Rentie how she felt by them, though Amy considered herself a lousy fool to even be admitting to herself that she had feelings for him. Meh...she'd admit it when she next saw him, that much was certain. She'd score a pinfall over Drew Hostyle on Slam, that was also certain...what wasn't certain is how much she was getting herself into by screwing around with the lap dog of 3Bi, his friends might come after her...Meanstreak might even.
