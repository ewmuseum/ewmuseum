Peace (Ivan/Tempest rp)

24 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-24-02 Of all the places, this is last place that I should be. The heavy thud of Ivan Stanislav’s massive leather boots echoed through the stairwell as he ascended the large staircase…. I have disobeyed an order…what is going on? How could I have angered the Motherland? Stanislav made it to the top of the stairwell and opened the door slowly and began to walk down the apartment hallway…. Tomifei is bound to find out what I am doing..should I be doing this? Is this best course of action? Ivan walked slowly down the hallway and looked to the left and right, almost surreally. He ground his jaw…. I will see what happens….and take it from there. And if things do not work out…I will take the action that is required…. Ivan swallowed and turned to a door. His face was more ragged than normal, his beard grown out and the gray hairs that he had eliminated days before had returned. Even his demeanor, a twisted suspender over his right shoulder and a wrinkle in his shirt had shown that something was amiss. He breathed out heavily and raised his hand to knock, a look of worry on his face… Suddenly the door opened and he found himself looking down on a very worried looking Tempest. Her coat was bundled around her shoulders and over her shoulder was a bag too hefty to be a purse. More likely it was packed for a journey. 'Ivan?' she said, almost in disbelief. 'Yes Tempest, it is Ivan.' He looked down at her, slightly deflated. 'You appear to be going somewhere. I thought I would stop by to talk to you, but I will give you your space...' Ivan swallowed and shifted his weight. 'I... I was going to go and find you,' she said with a little smile. 'Didn't think it would be such a short trip.' Ivan smiled, 'Oh, well here I am.' Ivan stood awkwardly, 'What did you wish to talk to me about? Why are you packed so heavily?' She looked at her bag and turned around, hefting it through the door and onto the floor as she walked back inside, leaving the door open. 'Well, like I said, I was gonna try and find you. For all I knew that meant a trip to Russia. I just... needed to talk to you.' She turned and looked up at him nervously, wringing her hands a little. Ivan walked through the doorway and peered back to the hallway, looked left and right, and then shut the door. He turned and locked the door and swallowed, 'You were going to The Motherland to speak to me?' Ivan rushed closer to her, trying to look as if nothing was wrong, but it was a futile effort, 'What would be bothering you so my dear?' Ivan grasped her shaking hands. Tempest looked up at him questioningly and paused a moment. She took a deep breath and said, 'I've heard you've gotten into some trouble because of me.' Ivan's jaw shook suddenly as he let go of her hands and turned away, 'What have you heard, Tempest?' Tempest shook her head and said, 'That's just it I'm not sure. I mean I've heard the news reports, saying that you're losing face with some of the military, but...' she shook her head in frustration. 'I don't understand it. It's too far from my world to try and figure out.' Ivan brought his hands up to his hips slowly and raised his head. He turned and faced Tempest again, 'I will not lie to you, Tempest, the Russian High Command is concerned with my affiliation with you.' Ivan walked closer to Tempest, 'I do not understand it myself, to be honest. I think they fear that I have...changed...somehow...' Ivan paused and looked back up at Tempest with more worry in his eyes than he wished to show, 'perhaps I have.' Tempest looked up at him and asked quietly, 'Do you think it's been a bad change though?' Quickly Ivan responded, 'Nyet Tempest!' He hurriedly walked over to her, his footsteps shaking the floorboards, 'I do not regret any of it!' Tempest stepped closer and hugged him, huddling close as she said, 'And I don't either but... I don't have anyone whose orders I'm supposed to follow. Ivan... they want you to leave me, don't they?' Tempest stepped closer and hugged him, huddling close as she said, 'And I don't either but... I don't have anyone whose orders I'm supposed to follow. Ivan... they want you to leave me, don't they?' Ivan swallowed and rubbed the back of her head with his hand, 'Yes Tempest,' his voice wavered, 'they wish for me to leave you...' Ivan swallowed and held her tight. 'But... are they right?' Tempest asked, not looking up, trying to avoid his gaze. 'I mean, will you? After all, you've been with me a month... you've had a whole life in the army and Russia and...' 'And it is one of most joyous months of my life, Tempest!' Ivan pulled her back slightly and looked into her eyes, 'They are wrong, Tempest. It pains me to say that but they are wrong.' Ivan tilted his head to the side and smiled slightly. He brought a hand up and ran a finger along her face, 'In the last month things have happened to me that the military could never do....wonderful things that I cannot explain.' He swallowed and looked at the floor, 'They say Ivan Stanislav is changing...they say Ivan Stanislav is losing focus.....they say Ivan Stanislav is not who he used to be...' he looked up at her, 'but I say that Ivan Stanislav is happy...and it could not have been done if he had never met you.' Tempest nodded and looked up at him with a sigh. 'I know. I just don't want you to get in trouble because of me,' she said. 'I wanna be a good thing in your life and not take away from some other part of it.' She bit her lip and added, 'But I don't wanna lose you either.' Ivan smiled, his scraggly hairs on his face crinkled as he did so, 'If Ivan Stanislav is to get in trouble it will because of Ivan Stanislav's actions alone and have nothing to do with you, dear Tempest.' He stood tall again, 'You will not lose me Tempest. Nothing that General Tomifei or any of his men can do to get rid of me.' He lowered his voice and placed his hand on her shoulder, 'You appear worried, Tempest...' Tempest nodded and then jumped as a sudden burst of rain smacked against the windows. 'I.. I am,' she said haltingly. 'I may not know a lot about the army, but those men are dangerous. I know that much.' She squeezed his arm and added, 'I'm worried about you.' Ivan leapt at the sudden jump by Tempest and wrapped his arm around her. 'Yes, those men are dangerous, but they won't risk doing anything to me.' Ivan swallowed, 'Ivan Stanislav cannot just disappear..that would be folly.' Ivan looked down at her, his eyebrows raised, 'Please do not be concerned about me Tempest. If I cause you anxiety, I am being a burden, and I do not wish that to be.' Thunder rolled outside causing Tempest to shiver as he held her closer, 'Do not be afraid, please.' Tempest summoned a smile and said, 'It's ok. I'm fine. I just hate storms.' She chuckled and shook her head in the direction of the window and said, 'Kinda funny huh?' Ivan turned to the window as the water splattered against the pane, 'Why do you hate the storm?' She shrugged and said, 'I'm not sure. I mean they used to be miserable just because I was homeless and having no shelter in one is no fun. But I think....' She shivered and turned away from the window as she said, 'It's just something overwhelming, something I can't stop or control or even effect. I guess I feel like I'll just be swept away by it.' 'You have nothing to fear Tempest. You may not have control over the storm, but you have people around you that will never allow you to be harmed. I wish for you to remember that.' He paused as he peered out the window, 'It pains me to imagine you homeless and....scared. I do not believe I could survive without a home....' he paused and turned to her, 'but I feel that for a very long time, I was very much alone.. much due to my own doing.' Tempest turned from the window and smiled at him a little. 'Well, now you don't have to be. At least, as long as you're willing to take the chance,' she said. 'I just don't know what to do about this whole thing though. I don't wanna cost you everything that was important before you met me. It's not like those things don't count.' She sighed and sat down on the couch and said, 'I talked to Alexei about it.' Ivan did not turn, 'I will not deny that it worries me in the position that I am in. As a communist, one of the things that we believe in is to never be alone. And yet, it appears that if I do one thing, I will not be alone regardless.' Ivan paused, 'Yes, the things in my life count, but perhaps certain things have given way to others that are of more importance to myself....rather than to the state.' He turned to face her, the thought of her talking to Alexei worried him, 'Did you talk to Alexei....or did Alexei...' he swallowed, 'talk to you?' Tempest seemed to think for a moment and said, 'I talked to him. I wanted him to help me understand what was going on.' Ivan walked closer to Tempest slowly, almost apprehensively, 'And did he help you understand?' She shrugged and shifted nervously where she sat. 'Well, sort of. I think he's scared,' she said, looking up at him. 'It's like all those people just want to crush anything between us. And I didn't understand. I mean, you've been doing great, so why should they complain? But...' She shook her head. 'That's not it, is it?' Ivan sighed and walked across the room and turned to face her, 'Nyet Tempest, that is not it. They praise me for my successes...' he contemplated his thoughts for a moment, 'they are critical of my humanity, which they believe you have unlocked.' Ivan swallowed and turned from Tempest, 'I will not lie to you, Tempest, Ivan Stanislav has not always been a hero that some would make him to be. I have done things that I am ashamed of...things that I regret now...and it is these things that they see absent in my demeanor.' He turned to her as his face twisted, 'It is as if my head is.. splitting in a way, Tempest. I do not know what to think.' Tempest nodded and looked at him questioningly. 'What do you mean exactly?' she asked as she scooted a little ways down the couch. Ivan walked to the couch and sat down. His weight caused a shift on the couch that pushed Tempest up a bit on her cushion. He shook his head and stared at the floor, 'Part of me wants to do what they say and then suddenly, another part of me wants to do what I believe is right.' He swallowed loudly and looked to her, 'If they were to tell me to go and attack someone in the OSW, I would do it unquestioningly. But if they tell me to do something against you, I cannot do it. Part of me says that under the communist doctrine, I am being selfish, but another part of me says that I am doing what anyone else would do...' Ivan's eyes darted around as he tried to make sense of what he was saying. Tempest nodded and put her seemingly tiny hand on his as she said, 'I know. But... don't some of those generals have families? Don't they have others that matter to them? I read some of that book and I don’t' remember it saying you couldn't be close to people. It said some stuff about religion, but not people.' Ivan shifted his weight, 'Yes, but the women there...they do whatever is said, they know better. The men can be as stand offish with them as they want because they do not really....' Ivan swallowed and looked at her, '...care what happens to them.' Tempest sat up a bit straighter and said evenly, 'So if I were to just pipe down and do what I'm told, it'd be ok?' Ivan stood up again and took a few steps away from her, 'I believe 'fall in line' would be more of the terminology. Which means to live in Russia, allow them access to your phone lines, send in letters calculating the amount of money you are spending, not associating with anyone else, and more.' Ivan turned and looked at her, 'Tempest, if someone were to say to me two months ago how one should act in situation, I would be on their side!' He shook his head, 'That is what is strangest. I know that I would be with them, but for some reason I am not. I have learned that the State is first, the individual is second..and yet, for some reason, I believe I am putting individual first....and State second...' 'Maybe... it's because you and I both know, I wouldn't survive that,' she said. She shrugged and said, 'I'm can understand what you mean in a way though. Two months ago, if anyone told me I'd want to be with a Russian soldier, many years beyond my age, who's never even seen the inside of club, I wouldn't have believed it either. But...' she tilted her head and looked at him, 'there's more to you than that, just as there's more to me than what those officers can see.' Ivan nodded, 'I do not understand.' He sat next to Tempest again, obviously uneasy, 'The people like it! The people enjoy what they see! But the officers are against it and do not seem to care! Are we not fighting for the people?? Are we not doing this for their benefit??' He paused and thought for a moment, 'While they cannot take any drastic action against me, in a blink of eye they can pull me from this assignment and have me in watch tower in Siberia for the rest of my life if I anger them. It is almost as if...I am questioning....their loyalty..' Ivan's eyes widened at this thought. He turned to Tempest and grabbed her hand, 'If I were to lose Russia I would be nothing! What would I do? Where would I go?' Ivan talked with an urgency few had seen, he truly was upset. Tempest shook her head and said, 'That's just it. I don't want you to lose Russia over me. I... I think you could survive it, but that's just because I have faith in you. Russia's your home in a way that Hong Kong and well most any place, isn't for me.' Outside thunder rolled and Tempest scooted closer to Ivan as it did. Ivan put his huge arm around her and held her tight, 'I believe that I could too. Normally all I ever thought about was mission at hand, measures that needed to be taken, and the thoughts of my superiors. Yet, when Ivan is with you he thinks about Tempest and Ivan and not those men that have always been watching me.' Ivan rubbed her arm with his hand, 'Perhaps when Ivan wins World Title things will change, eh?' Tempest smiled and said, 'I know. I can’t wait. And no matter what happens next I'll be there to celebrate it with you.' She stretched up and kissed his cheek softly. He grinned and kissed her back on the top of her head, 'Da, I had never doubted it.' Ivan thought for a moment, 'I can assure you that Russian Military will not split us up...' he thought for a moment and swallowed, his voice lowered slightly, 'I do not think anything could split us...Tempest.' He blinked twice and contemplated something to himself with a slight tension inside of him. Tempest sighed happily and said, 'I don't think so either. We're too stubborn to be pulled apart.' She chuckled as if quite satisfied with the thought. Ivan shook his head, pushing a thought out of it and laughed loudly, his chest shook Tempest as he did, 'DYAAHAAHAA!! Ivan Stanislav is not stubborn, is he? DYAAHAAHAA!!' Ivan smiled to himself and looked down at Tempest, 'Tempest, would it be too much of problem for Ivan to have drink of some sort?' 'Of course not!' she said, popping up with a grin. 'Lemme go raid the fridge.' She stood and trotted off happily. After a moment she called form the kitchen and said, 'Hey I'm fetching you something. Does that count any toward that whole disciplined Russian thing?' Ivan chuckled and fixed his stray suspender strap, 'DYAAHAA! Yes, perhaps so! I will be sure to tell General Tomifei!!' He paused and grinned, 'Perhaps I should start just referring to you as 'woman' too, he would like that!' Tempest whistled a tune from the kitchen and made no remark for a minute. Then she called, 'Oh, were you talking? Woman is a bit generic, I figured you must mean some other one.' She giggled as glasses clinked in the other room. Ivan nodded and muttered to himself more so than to Tempest, 'Yes...you are anything but generic..which is good thing..' Suddenly a clap of thunder shook the windows. The lights flickered and from the kitchen, Tempest screamed. the sounds of glass shattering followed almost instantly. Instantly, Ivan burst from his seat and rushed to the kitchen, 'TEMPEST? ARE YOU ALL RIGHT??' His voice boomed through the room as he thundered through the kitchen door to find broken glass all over the tiled floor as well as a puddle of liquid surrounding it. On the kitchen counter sat Tempest, shivering slightly as she stared at the floor. Ivan rushed over to her, stamping over the glass as he did and lifted her in his arms, 'Are you all right Tempest??' She nodded meekly and mumbled, 'Dammit, I thought it was done doing that.' She looked at the floor and then at Ivan, from the safe perch in his arms and said, 'I'm sorry.' van carried her out of the kitchen and sat her down on the couch in the living room, 'It is my fault, Tempest, I should not have asked for the drink!' He sat her down and bit his lip, 'It is all my fault...is it not?' He turned and looked at the kitchen door, 'You are anxious..you are afraid..and that is what caused it! I am the reason for this!' Ivan's brow furrowed as he became distraught. He took a step towards the kitchen, and then looked to her, not sure what to do. 'I will clean it, Tempest. I will clean it and then I will go....I fear I am being more of burden than I am a help...' He turned and headed to the kitchen. 'Ivan wait...' Tempest said and reached out toward him as she stood up. 'It's ok. I just... I'm always like that in storms. It's not your fault. It's silly and I shouldn't be that way.' She shook her head and tried to look less nervous. Ivan paused and turned to her. He walked over to her slowly, 'Nyet Tempest,' he said, 'it is not silly. There is nothing wrong with having fears.' He walked over to her and gently pushed her down into the couch. He sat beside her and let her lean up against him. He wrapped his arms around her and held her hands, letting her head rest against his chest, 'Relax Tempest, no storm can hurt you so long as Ivan is here, I can assure you.' He paused, 'But if there is problem that Ivan is causing, I wish for you to tell me, understood? I do not wish there to be anything hidden between us.' She smiled and shook her head very slightly. 'Naw, you're great to me Ivan,' she said. 'I'm a little scared of that Russian military machine but that's all. And you're more important than that and I'm not gonna be scared off by them.' Her words came at a rapid clip, as she huddled against him. Ivan smiled and squeezed her tightly, 'You are shaking like little snow rabbit trapped in Siberia. Relax Tempest...do not think of storm or anything, just relax.' Tempest began to speak but he quickly cut her off, 'Shhh....just relax....' He paused and slowly hummed to himself. His voice rolled through his body as he began to sing softly to Tempest, 'Priviet menya zavut iygor....nazdarovie...' he continued to sing in his native tongue, and while what the words meant were unknown, his thick, rich Russian voice was soothing nonetheless. Ivan smiled at her and continued to sing to her, rocking her just slightly in his huge arms. The lightning flickered in the background but Tempest did not stir as Ivan began to sing another verse, watching her face intently. He brought one huge leg up from the ground and laid it on the couch beside her softly, trying to recline himself. Tempest snuggled against him, slowly relaxing, stretching out and seeming more comfortable. She yawned a little and nuzzled his chin. Ivan stopped singing for a moment and whispered softly, 'Sleep Tempest....no one can harm you. Nothing will scare you...' he hummed and brought his other leg up and leaned back with Tempest, still holding her tightly and securely. 'Mmmhmmm...' Tempest mumbled. She nuzzled against his shoulder and said quietly, 'Thank you...' Her breathing slowed and slowly she began to be still. After a few moments, her breathing was even as she lay comfortably in Ivan's arms. Ivan sighed as she relaxed and slowly fell asleep. He breathed out heavily and furrowed his brow… I would never have thought she would be so calm in my arms. Few people are at ease when the arms of Ivan Stanislav are wrapped around them. I can only hope that she is at ease with my presence…she is far too precious to harm… Ivan swallowed as he thought about the only time he ever abandoned her and shivered slightly. Tempest stirred but calmed again. What am I doing with her in my arms? What is that I feel right now? My mother once told me that she ‘loved’ me. Is this love? Is it love that would cause The Russian Bear to turn his back on an order so that he could help someone outside of the system? Ivan looked down at Tempest and smiled. I do not know…but this woman is special to me in way that I have never known. Perhaps when I win World Title…they will understand and if not…I will live with the consequences. Ivan leaned down and kissed Tempest on the cheek. She sighed and snuggled into him. Ivan leaned back and closed his eyes. I will protect her….and nothing will stop me. Ivan closed his eyes and began to hum the lullaby again until he too slowly drifted off to sleep. The lightning and rain had scared Tempest to death… The Russian Military worried even Ivan Stanislav…. Yet by holding each other they achieved something they rarely had…. …peace.
