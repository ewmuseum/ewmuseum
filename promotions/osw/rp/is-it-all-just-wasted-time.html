Is It All Just Wasted Time?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jennifer watched him sleep, motionless on the hospital bed, the beating given to him by Armour having decimated the man she once thought of as a pompous asshole. Now he's just a hurt asshole, an asshole who she came to like as the days got closer to the first OSW PPV. And just like that, she was given plane tickets back to Chicago. So why is she back? Doug asked her on the way to Tulsa, shuttling down the interstate in the inky blackness that covers the interstate from the rest of the land, obscuring everything under the cover of night, rendering everything into anonymous flat, passing you by at 70 miles an hour. 'I... I don't know, Doug.' she said after a long while, flipping through Doug's CD case. 'Well, you made a show of hating him, didn't you?' Doug grinned, hiding his smile with a hand. Jennifer smiles slighly. 'Yeah, but part of that was playacting. I still think he's an ass, but I don't know... He's good looking, has a fantastic body, and... Shit, I don't know why, Doug.' Doug laughed softly. 'Shut up!' She made a motion of backhanding him. 'Dickhead.' 'Hah hah! You're in love with SeaBass! You're in love with SeaBass!' Doug sang in a high voice. 'You're a putz.' she giggled, and sighed. 'I wouldn't say that I love him, let's just say I don't have to be the bitch anymore, and I'd like to give him a chance, y'know?' Well, maybe not that simple, she sighed. Sebastian and her... It was about the Gimmick Man's gimmick for him. An image and a girl. A girl who didnt like him much. An image of a jackass. And this image got him the OSW World Championship. But where did it leave her? It left her at the curb. ALone, sitting in her studio apartment in Rockford, Illinois. Watching daytime television and reading whatever she could get her hands on. Going on on pointless dates. Her biggest social interactions were with the clerks at the GAP. Pretty pitiful life for the past couple of months. And now, Sebastian gets the crap kicked out of him by some hired thug. Jennifer didn't know much about the whole business other than what The Gimmick Man told her, but she knew what happened to Toomes on Saturday wasn't in the script. She knew that this Armour guy really laid into him. And he wouldn't be in the hospital if it was just 'wrestling'. She sighed, leaning her head on his bed, looking up at his sleeping form. His nose was heavily bandaged, a result of Armour dropkicking a monitor into Sebastian's face. His head and back were bruised heavily, the powerbombs on the steel ramp and the steel steps to his cranium, thank you very much. The residual injuries he's worked through, still working on him. This may be the well-deserved break he needed. God knows she's watched him enough. Heh, how about that, huh? She's watched him... ALmost obsessively. Every match on tape. She bought everything CCW had on him after they collapsed. She'd curl up and watch one of his promos and just feel a little closer to him. Scary, huh? But she had more fun in the month or so they were together than she had with any guy she had ever dated. And now she was here with him. The faithful girl he left behind. God, the histronics... But it was better than clinging onto nothing and just dating one more boring asshole after another, whose main agenda was to fuck her. But Sebastian... This was his life, his career. And like she'd once heard, once you're in the business, you never really get it out of you. So why not get into it, leave all the bullshit behind? Why not... Share it with him? Not marriage or anything like that, but it'd be nice to share something with somebody, share a common interest. And hey, at least she'd have a job again. Jennifer smiles to herself. She must be going soft in her old age, a positively ancient 23. She leans up and kisses his dry lips and nuzzles him for a bit until he opens his eyes after she pulls away. 'Whu...huzzzat?' he mumbles. 'Nothing, Bas... Nothing. Just go back to sleep.' Jennifer smiles and pats his cheek. -FIN-
