Martyrdom

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1 March 2002 As our vision becomes clearer and clearer, we can see hundreds of thousands of red dominoes lined up on a floor of concrete. On the sides of said dominoes, we can see something has been inscribed on them, and after further investigation we see that the inscriptions is actually the names of people. We can hear breathing in the background, followed by the mist of these breathes in this extremely cold environment. A shadow can then be seen from time to time, as it moves around the dimly lit red lights that illuminate this room. A sound suddenly breaks the silence, the sound of laughter; a quiet but menacing laugh, full of content and contempt. We then return our attention to the strategically placed dominoes that adorn the floor. We then realize the names on the first three dominoes in this arrangement, Trashcan Man, Lennox Lanthrop, and finally, Dark Liege. Suddenly, a skeletal finger comes into view and pushes the first domino over, causing it to topple the second, third and each domino thereafter, and in effect, causing a chain reaction. A voice is then heard. “My children, welcome to the new era. In the first few days since my departure from Primetime Championship Wrestling, the Chief Executive Officer of said company, Mr. Tyler Nelson has gone public by saying, and I quote, ‘Screw that punk, we don’t need him anymore…’, interesting.” A pause occurs, followed by a sinister laugh as the voice continues, “I believe, Mr. Nelson, that you need to take a step back and personally confirm the validity of your statements, prior to your opening of your mouth and infecting us all with your unfounded and opinionated insight. Look to the moral of your now beleaguered roster; is that panic that I now sense? So many months you became worrisome about someone or something becoming a threat to your precious federation, do you have your answer yet? It was after all under your nose the entire time. You, Mr. Nelson, are what you have been looking for this entire time, the enemy of PCW. Business is business as they say, would you not agree? ” The dominoes continue to fall as droplets of blood fall from the ceiling with each domino that hits the ground. A huge muscular figure now comes into view, a figure long since scarred from the battles throughout time. Chains rattle as the man moves towards a table where he then snaps his fingers and the black candle that was laying on the table suddenly illuminates, somewhat revealing the face of the individual, Vampir Nosferatu. He turns his head and observes the dominoes as they continue to endlessly topple over and he smiles in approval. He snarls, exposing his blood soaked razor sharp fangs, “With a new body and battlefield, comes a new mission. Dark Liege, since that is what you like to call yourself these days, you seem to believe that you have the right and the authority to teach your lackey of the week my moves and then claim that you will take care of me when the time arose.” Vampir then kneels over and with his fingernail, cuts into the concrete slab, drawing a proverbial line in the sand. After drawing the line, Vampir takes a step for and over the line. Grinning, he continues, “Well, it looks like to me it is now the time for you to take care of it. I’m waiting Dark Liege, act upon your words for I am not going anywhere.” Vampir continues to take steps forward, “With each step I take, the closer to you I am. The time you have among the living evaporates further, your worst fears, becoming more and more an evident reality.” He stops and again looks towards the dominoes, and smiles. It is then at this time we finally see what it is exactly this arrangement of dominoes has spelled on the floor…PTC. Vampir continues, “Ah yes, the PTC, Primetime Central, a conglomerate of federations united on a front, to further enhance the competitive nature of our industry. With this a second global tournament has been enacted to find me an opponent, a number one contender to my Global Title, an opponent worthy enough to be sacrificed to the darkness. I welcome your aggression and challenges with open fangs, who will be the fortunate one to be perished? Only time will reveal the answer my children, time is your only ally now. Unfortunately, it is an ally that with each passing second, leaves you.” A sudden bright flash occurs and the scenery has changed. We now see Vampir standing in a graveyard, as two men positioned behind him are hard at work digging a grave. The tombstone, made of granite and shoal, lies behind them. The name on the stone, inscribed in the old English style font is Lennox Lanthrop. It’s a cold night tonight, a bitter cold, one that just permeates into anything you may be wearing and allowing the comfortable bearing of this weather near impossible. The gravediggers work feverishly as Vampir overlooks them in approval. After a few moments Vampir takes out a silver chalice from his coat pocket and begins to chant an incantation in the ancient Sumerian language. As he tilts his head back and closes his eyes, he begins to sway from side to side, lifting the chalice at arms length and directly over the empty grave. His chanting becomes louder and louder, the sounds of thunder and the flashes of lightning can be heard and observed on the horizon. Vampir then opens his eyes utters a word, ”mortis”; he then turns his hand, spilling the contents of the silver chalice into the grave. “Lennox, well well my little compadre, looks like you are set to become my first official victim here in my new domain. Unfortunately you have enraged your opponent more than you can possibly fathom. Instead of being a true warrior and preparing yourself for your imminent destruction, you have decided to run off and hide behind your so-called master. Have you dropped off the face of the planet little one? Has thy become so scared of the darkness that thou has decided to not even put forth an effort? Well Dark Liege, it is now official, thou has picked up a fine specimen of mortality to become your student. Who shall I wait to hear from in your defense next week, Judas Steele?” Vampir turns and faces the gravediggers, whom he motions to get out of the one. Once they do so, Vampir takes a step in and continues, “A final resting place I have prepared for you out of my generosity. In just a few days time, I shall allow you the opportunity to inhabit this dwelling, where you can mull over in darkness the true meaning of the word…martyrdom. Be weary my friend, the word mercy has no meaning or value anymore, thy sand within this hourglass…”, Vampir kneels over and retrieves a gothic looking hourglass that was resting within the grave and holds it up, “…has almost concluded its journey. On Saturday Night Slam, your journey will conclude, and my first step into the elimination of all those blasphemers will have been taken. Lanthrop, may your pagan god have mercy on thy soul, you have been warned.” Vampir then snaps his fingers and a bright flash occurs, we are now in a pitch-black room. The sounds of metal screeching can be heard as we observe a multi shaded orange globe spinning around on a un-oiled axis. On it however are three letters written in black and outlined in white, the letters are O S and W, Old School Wrestling. Vampir then emerges from behind the globe as he creates a scratch from pressing his fingernail against the spinning globe. “Members of OSW, hear me now, now begins a new dawn upon your lives. My brother Maskimxul has shown me the error of my ways, hence this I will proclaim; a return to my roots and origin I shall relive. You will all experience the true nature of this beast in the form, which led me to notoriety in years gone by. Unfortunately, if you disagree with my methods or in the manner in which I do business, take a number and I shall deal with you accordingly, the rules of this game you all seem to play has now changed.. Martyrs, all will become, deceased, all will be.” The globe continues to spin as the scratch marks Vampir is making on it suddenly begin to bleed a bright red. Vampir smiles as he moves his hand to his face and begins to draw lines of blood upon it, then licking the excess off his fingers as his eyes suddenly begin to glow bright red in nature. “In the days to come, a new front will become established as I am set to meet with the heads of the twelve families and discuss a few much need changes that I plan to evoke. Those who stand in our way shall become perished.” He then takes a few steps forward and in a whisper, concludes, “Lennox, on Saturday night, you shall become the first. Liege, you lie in waiting as my next victim, prepare thee well oh confused peasant of this planet, yours will be a mind much fun to play with. Until then, so it has been said, so it shall be done.” A bright flash then occurs, as we now appear to be inside the Roman Coliseum during the middle of a contest. We see three brown robed individuals with crosses hung around their neck as they are tied to a pole in the center of the arena. Metal then screeches and slams as three Bengal tigers emerge from their pens located under the arena floor. A voice is then heard, whispering however still audible, “Victims, aren’t we all.” A bright flash then occurs again, followed by the blackness of darkness.
