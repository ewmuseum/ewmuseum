I Need To Puke....And Shit

9 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/09/02 'ARRRRRRGGGHHHHHHHHH!' That's all I could remember screaming during the painful and ever lasting ride to the hospital. What the hell just happened to me though? That's the first thing I thought of after blocking out the painful pain in my stomach. Opening my eyes, all I saw around me were EMT's, making sure I was ok. But man, right now I felt like a combination of needing to puke and take a crap. Problem was, I didn't want to do that in the ambulance ride on the way there. That, and I didn't feel like I could to that. But aside from the pain, I was wondering what had just happened to me. Did I black out and get the worst of my confrontation with Clay Vendetta? Maybe someone jumped me from behind. Other then my scar on my forehead, I don't feel any pain anywhere besides inside my stomach. That's odd I thought to myself. 'Wha, what happened?' I asked, trying to fight the painful sharps of sudden pain that felt like a thousand needles inside my stomach. 'It's ok Mr. Naket,' one of the EMT's replied, keeping a close eye on me. 'You just have a case of bad food poisoning,' he said. Food poisoning? That's it? That's the sharp pain, and the cause of me blacking out then? Well, that explains the sudden urges to puke and take a crap, but that doesn't explain why I blacked out. 'You should really consider trying to get some rest on the way to the hospital Scott,' the other EMT replied. I was about to nod my head in agreement with them, when I remembered that I have a match to get to tomorrow. 'But my match!?' I asked the EMT's, wondering if the food poison would be only temporary and if I would be able to compete at the pay-per-view. 'Sorry Scott,' the EMT replied, 'food poisoning usually takes a while to run it's course, and you'll probably be in the hospital for at least the next few days at a minimum.' Dammit was the only word going through my mind. How the hell was I to take a crucial step forward when I've already lost the match I'm in on Sunday? 'So either Mike or Clay win this thing then?' I asked the EMT's, hoping to get a answer like the match was cancelled. Boy, my prayers were just about answered with this response. 'Well Scott,' the first EMT replied, 'Clay is in the ambulance right behind us, and must have eaten the same thing as you did. And Mike is in serious condition, but we have no word on him yet.'. Now that was the answer I was looking for. That probably means no match on Sunday, not confirmed, but pretty much seals the deal. I nodded, and started to look around the ambulance. Some basic and advanced medical supplies in there. One of those breathing masks, some bandages and cloth, and some pills and liquids. To advanced for me to figure out exactly what each thing was. From the position I was in, all I could see outside were the street lamps, passing one at a time every half-second. I guess we were going at a high speed, but I couldn't hear the sirens blaring outside. 'OH NO' I thought to my self. What about Todd, the bum I picked up? I left him in my car at the arena! Now my stomach really feels like crap. Hold on a second, will you? Leaning over the side of the stretcher I was laying on, I looked around for something while the EMT's looked at me with a curious look in their eyes. I grabbed the first thing I could see, which happened to be a paper bag. Opening it up, I just let it all come out. I will spare you from the graphic details of what came out, but I can tell you this. It wasn't a pretty site to say the least. And now my mouth tasted really funny. And those awful little leftover chunks were still stuck to the side of my mouth, as I barred with it to swallow them. 'Well, there goes your meal tonight Earl,' one of the EMT's says, grinning as he looks over at the other EMT, who's jaw has just hit the floor of the moving vehicle. One guys meal was the last thing on my mind though at this time, as all I did was lay back and close my eyes, hoping this long ride of death would soon come to an end. A nice sleep was one thing that I desperately needed right now. ******* When I awoke, the location of where I was, was completely different. Loud noises everywhere, as I was wheeled to the main lobby of the hospital. Looking around, I saw the pain and anguish in many of the peoples faces around me. Some had huge gashes on their arms, but no one was attending to them, as they just had to wait. Others were sucking onto their last breath right there and then, but still, no attention as they had to wait like everyone else. Other people who had major damage done to bones in their bodies were also waiting like everyone else. Hell, some were even reading magazines by now. But here I was, looking like a buffoon laying on my stretcher, and only suffering from food poisoning. I sat up, but then quickly coughed and covered my mouth and stomach at the same time. The attention was now on me, as I leaned over the side again and let it rip. SPLASH! EEEEEWWWWWWWW! The tiles on the hospital floor have been many colours. From it's original white, to red, and even a bright yellow, but now I have officially added a orange and green kind of mixture to the assortment. The thousand needles in my stomach felt like they have just duplicated, as I held the outside of it in pain and leaned back, forcing to hold in an agonizing scream of pain. The staff at the hospital quickly grabbed the end of my stretcher and rolled me down the hall. 'Get a bucket or something!' one of them yells out, trying to make sure I have something to put my mess into. Did I mention the pain was killing me by now? Worse then any suplex, chairshot or submission move I've ever encountered before combined. I felt like I wanted to die right on the spot. Take me now, I asked, hoping the man upstairs would respond to my agonizing pain. I knew he wouldn't, and I knew it wasn't worth it, but that didn't stop me from asking it. I wanted out now, and I wanted out quick. In my mind, I knew I would be feeling better then ever in a week. But deep down inside, I thought that this would never go away, and would create a slow and painful personal hell to me. DING-DONG! Ah yes, the elevator. Going up I'm assuming, but why can't you just go down and push me into the underground parking lot? Leave me for dead. Maybe Rikishi is driving around the area tonight, and boy, wouldn't that be a treat. Or maybe Clay Vendetta could get his wish and his ambulance would hit my prone body. Nah, that won't happen, cause all I'm gonna be treated to is my own personal room where my suffering would just go on for another endless lifetime. Boy, I sound like a broken record tonight. AND ARGH! To add on to my pain, that goddamn Gorillaz song 'Rock The House' is playing inside the elevator. The pain just keeps growing, and growing doesn't it? Looking up above me, I see the numbers of the floors dim and light up as we pass it. Four floors in total, not including the two below the lobby for parking. Floor two, floor three... DING-DONG! To quote a popular phrase, 'I am Jack's Flaming Pile of Shit'. That's what I was thinking about when I left the small compressed space of the elevator. And that was the last thing I was thinking about before these buffoons of hospital staff forgot to bring their brains to work today. SMASH! CRASH! AHHHHHHH! If car accidents are supposed to hurt so bad at just 30 mph, then I don't wanna know how much it really hurts. Simply because we crashed at a slow speed into another stretcher, and low and behold, I fell over and straight to the ground. Now I warn you, this was the only word that I could yell out at the time, because the pain finally got to me with that fall. 'FUCK!' Because it finally hit to me. I can't sit up. I can't fall. I won't be able to wrestle tomorrow. And I won't be able to escape from this sorry shithole of a place they call a medical facility. Just wrap up the rest of the sandwich and leave it to be forgotten until it's discovered all moldy and stale a few weeks later in the back of your fridge. The way this is going, by the time I return to OSW, I'll be that kind of a sandwich. Gee, I hope Clay Vendetta is having as much fun tonight as I am. I think he also wishes he choked on his food as well by this point.
