Back to basics

1 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sept. 1, 2002 At first glance, you think the hotel room is vacant. The bed had not been slept in and every light was out. All except one. On closer glance, you see a slight glow... that of the television, pointed away from the bed and towards a chair in the other corner of the room. Seated in that chair is Dave Gibson. Old School Wrestling's poster boy is wearing only a pair of Adidas shorts. 'Hey, buddy,' you say, dimly hearing your own voice from the television. Gibson does not respond, his entire focus honed in on the television. 'Dave?' Then you hear Gibson's own face. You turn, and see your own tag team partner on the screen, during the memorial service aired on Slam mere hours ago. Gibson: [recorded] I'm gonna step inside the First Union Center this Sunday, and I'm gonna tape this up in the locker room. To remind every single OSW superstar, every staff member, from the announcers to the guy that brings the donuts... that The Cult was, is and always will be the biggest damn waste of space in the HISTORY of Old School Wrestling! And when its members are sitting at home, wearing full body casts, only starting to come back to their senses after the memory-erasing beatdowns they're gonna receive at By Any Means Necessary, they'll remember one thing. One thing to guide them through their sunset years wrestling in bingo halls and showing up at rich kids' birthday parties to support a diet of Spam and Ripple. And that one thing is to Shut Up... And Wrestle!' The tape pauses. 'Do you recognize that man on the screen, Clay?' you hear Gibson speak but don't turn around. His voice sounds almost hollow. 'I don't. He's a memory, a link to my past. Did you follow IOW, Clay?' 'Can't say I did, pal.' 'Ha! No wonder, nobody else did, either. That fuckin' Winters...' 'What's wrong, Dave?' 'Take a look at that man on the screen, Clay.' You lean forward, studying the individual. You see the cocky smirk, the flared nostrils, the arrogant look in the eyes. 'It looks like you... only... more prickish than usual.' 'Exactly!' Suddenly, the TV shuts off. A light clicks on behind you, and you turn to see Gibson sitting in the only comfortable chair in the room. (Damn hotels, with their intolerable furniture...) A small army of empty Coors Light bottles are strewn about the chair. 'See, Clay, that was me! As I used to be, at least,' Gibson runs a hand through his close-cropped hair. 'I used to be the biggest bastard in IOW. Those fans hated me with every fiber of their being. But you know what, Clay? I thrived on it. I only lost a couple of matches in IOW and jOlt combined. Already I've lost my only singles match, and two tag matches. I'm not faulting you, Clay, but... lately, I've felt like something has been missing. 'Saturday night, I felt it coming back.' You pause, almost afraid to ask the obvious question. You ask anyway. 'What is it, Dave? What's coming back?' A slight smile appears on Gibson's face as he leans back in the chair. 'Quite simply, Clay? I felt my old self coming back. Oh sure, I appreciate the support of the OSW fans, but if they're going to love me, they're going to love the OLD 'Old School' Dave Gibson. They're going to see the aggressive Dave Gibson. It's time to get back to basics... starting tomorrow night...' With that, Gibson fades off, having subsided into a drunken slumber. You shake your head, unable to repress a smile. You reach over, turning out the light and leave the room, now pitch black. As you leave, your partner's final words echo through your head. 'They're going to see the aggressive Dave Gibson.' More aggressive? Then the Cult is in trouble. Deep trouble.
