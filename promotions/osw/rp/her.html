Date: August 17, 2000 Reno Davis slowly trotted down the backstage hallway of Madison Square Garden in New York. He had just defeated Ruben Ross in an exhibition match, which in reality only meant that Reno was a better wrestler and would most likely be a better success in the fWo wrestling-wise. The real winner would be decided later on in the evening as all the survivors’ votes would be tallied and either Reno Davis or Ruben Ross would be victorious... “Hi there, I saw you beat Ross. Great job!” A quirky female voice came from directly beside Reno as he marched on. Reno looked over and nodded in appreciation and saw the tiny beauty standing there. Her long black hair with light blue highlight, ice blue eyes, dark purple eye shadow, and VERY shapely figure...She was hot. “Hey, I don’t think we’ve met. I’m Reno Davis, as you know.” Reno replied, trying to sound professional. Chicks digged professionals. The woman snickered and unleashed the most eccentric smile Reno had ever seen. “Yeah, I noticed since I watched your match...as well as you throughout the whole competition.” “Really?” Reno smiled, “What’d you think?” the small man moved closer to her. “Well...you’re a good wrestler but you changed your gimmick how many times?” “3 or 4 times I think...but the management kept telling me to drop everything I was doing and start again!” She giggled, “Okay then.” “So, are you a dev?” “Yep! I’ve been in training since last month. I hope I get in.” “I don’t know...They wouldn’t be having this competition if the dev talent was above average don’t you think?” Reno stated, and watched the petite, but pretty woman’s esteem shrink. Yeah, he was that smooth. “I-I’m sorry, I didn’t mean it that way...I just.” Reno started. “It’s okay, I’ll get over it.” She smiled again, as if nothing happened. “What do you say I bring you out to supper for apology?” “Get your check book out mister.” She smiled. The two began to walk down the hallway, chatting. “Hey, I didn’t catch your name...” Reno said. “Just call me Doe.” “Like the deer?” Reno said, he easily got a wide smile from Doe. “Well, A lot of people call me Tempest too...” Thus began the brief one night relationship between Tempest and Reno Davis. The next night, Reno was informed that he had been beaten by one vote, 5-4. He packed up and was never able to see Tempest again, although he had tried. Tempest was brought up to the farm federation to work her promo skills in front of a live audience and was not able to get in touch with Reno either. After a few months, the magic they shared during their one evening had soon been forgotten, even to this day. The magic instead worked anonymously, as Reno and Tempest were drawn near once again, but wedged away by Ivan Stanislav. The undeveloped love of Reno and Tempest is just another of God’s teases towards Reno Davis’ fate. As he is the Unwanted Angel.
