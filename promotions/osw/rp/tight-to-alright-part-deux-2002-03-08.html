Tight To Alright Part Deux

8 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/08/02 'Couldn't Find Another Way To Win The Race, Couldn't Find A Way' -Scott Weiland The Story So Far: Distraught in what he believes is his new low in the wrestling ring, Scott Naket is now looking for the new way to move up to the top of the game, and stay at that level. The problem though for Naket is that once he gets to the top, he somehow finds a way to fall down below of where he started. Let it be from losing countless matches that shouldn't be lost, or saying the wrong things at the wrong time, he somehow finds a way every single time. So in his search, in the middle of his egotistical anger boasting to a bum, a bunch of sex-craved horny teenagers have knocked Naket silly while stealing a magazine from the local 7-11 store. We pick up where we last left off: Naket out cold, the bum with his wallet, and a dumbfounded 7-11 clerk. We arrive back where we left off. A nice day outside. Not warm, but not freezing. Ya know, you don't need a jacket, but you can't wear a tee shirt. By this time, the teens, who by now have massive hard ones in their pants, are long gone into the forest somewhere. Focus on a dazed Scott Naket. Holding his head, he sits up and looks around the area. He shakes his head, and looks up at the bum, who by now has some sweat running down the side of his face. Guilty as charged is the statement that sums up his predicament. Looking cock-eyed at the bum, Naket slowly raises to his feet, but stumbles back a few steps from the head rush. Shaking it off, he sets his eyes on the bum, who looks down at the black wallet that belongs to Scott Naket in his hands. Taking one step forward, Naket starts his approach on the bum. However, with each step forward that Naket takes, the bum replies with a step back of his own. 'Hey, old man,' Naket begins, 'I want you to give me back my wallet.' Naket stops, and stares down the old man, who looks down at the black wallet in the palm of his left hand. The old man looks back at Naket and forces out a smile, but it's as half-hearted of a smile as you could get. Naket takes another step forward, and points his finger at the old man. His step total is now a whooping 4 steps. 'Now old man,' Naket says, trying to think of a ploy to gain back his money without using physical force, 'I think you should...'. Alas, before Naket could finish his sentence of whatever you'll call it, he forgot to remember that there are two doors at your local 7-11 store. And he forgot to realize that puzzled 7-11 clerks have no idea what to do, and thus, they panic. And when you panic, you don't care what happens, or you just don't see other people in your way. So unless you are in a school for the challenged, I think you know what is coming up next. SMACK! WHAM! KURPLUNK! Pretty much back to the way he started, the door swings open and Naket flies to the window. He smashes into it, and falls in a heap to the ground. Well, very slowly I should add. 'HEY YOU PUNK KIDS!' The angry Asian 7-11 clerk yells, 'YOU COME BACK HERE OR I REPORT YOU TO THE POLICE!'. Despite what the clerk thinks, those kids are long gone, and probably are soiling that magazine they just sold by now somewhere in the woods. The clerk shakes his fist in the air for no apparent reason, as a few people walking by just stare at him like he's crazy. He looks back at them, and smiles. 'Nacho's for $1.49 inside!' He informs the crowd. A bunch of groans are heard, and the people just carry on walking. The clerk goes back inside, not noticing the bum with the evil grin and a now bloody Naket, who has some of the red stuff flowing out of his forehead. Not caring for Naket's wounds, the bum walks by him and enters the store. The cameras don't follow him, but we can hear the Asian clerk yelling at him. 'I TELL YOU NOT TO COME IN UNLESS YOU HAVE MONEY!' he yells at the bum, who just replies by showing him his wallet. The scene then fades with a shot of a bloody Naket slouched over by a garbage can outside the door. **************************** The Fans Want It You Need It But What Is It? Ed Has IT But It's Not That It **************************** Alright Munch, crunch and more munching. That's the sounds we, and Scott Naket, are treated too when he awakens and we fade back in. Naket shakes his head, and touches his forehead. He notices the now sticky blood on his hands, and looks around to see it on his pants and the pavement he is sitting on. Naket leans his head back on the window, and closes his eyes, trying to focus on the situation. More munching is heard, and Naket looks over to the left to see the old bum chowing down on those cheap Nacho's from inside the store. Naket and the old man stare at each other for a bit, and Naket tries to push himself up to his feet. A headrush hits Naket, and he falls right back down on his ass. Naket shakes it off, and looks back over. 'Good?' Naket asks, as the old bum continues to just feed himself. The old bum just nods, keeping one eye on his food, and one eye on Naket. He reaches down and picks up a slurpee, and takes a drink. Naket just sighs, and slowly raises to his feet. He walks over, slowly, to the bum who doesn't move an inch. All he does is continue to feast on his food. Naket, now standing in front of the seated man, looks down at him. The bum just continues to feast on his food. 'Old Man,' Naket says, breaking the short silence, 'I need my wallet back now.' The old man reaches down beside him, taking a break from his eating, and hands Naket the wallet. Naket grabs it, and opens it up. Inside, there are all his credit cards, bank cards, licenses, the whole deal. However, there is no money in the wallet. A puzzled Naket looks down at the old bum, and questions him. 'Why'd you take all my money?' Naket asks him, as a grin forms on the bums face. 'What money?' The bum replies, smirking under his short breaths, considering he is exhausted from his mass eating. Naket turns his head, and starts to scratch his forehead. He stops and shakes it in pain after remembering the cut he has up there. He looks back at the bum. 'You know,' he begins, getting a little urgent now, 'the money that was inside the wallet. I think it was $100 roughly'. The bum licks the cheese off his dirty fingers, and takes a look at Naket. 'Oh that money,' he replies with a grin, 'well, that really isn't money, considering it was Canadian money.' the bum replies, now with a huge smile on his face. Naket sighs, but looks down at the bum. 'You know what, just keep the money,' he replies, and he turns around and starts to walk back towards his car. He opens the door, but pauses before he steps in. He looks over at the old bum, who's now just sucking back the remains of his slurpee. Naket smirks, and looks in the car. 'Hey old man,' Naket yells, as the bum looks up at Naket. 'What would you say if I offered you a free trip to North Carolina, and a free seat to a show?' The bum just stares at Naket, and slowly raises to his feet. A tilt in his head, and a funny look in his eye, the bum walks over to the front of the car. 'What's the catch?' The bum asks Naket, who seems to be amused for some reason. 'No catch,' Naket replies, 'I just need someone to talk over my situation on Sunday, and give me some fresh advice.' The bum looks over at Naket, and then looks behind him. He looks back at Naket, and replies 'Anything to get me out of this s*it-hole.' As he walks over and opens the door to Naket's 87 Accord. Naket opens his door, and climbs in. He starts the car, and drives off, leaving a situation that even baffles me. What kind of advice is Scott Naket looking for from a bum? I think all that deserves for a reaction is this. Sigh. Fade to black.
