Why must I deal with this woman....

6 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-6-02 -It’s not like he wanted it to happen! It’s not like he knew what exactly was going on! But here he is, Ivan Stanislav, roped into some strange date with this woman that he’s never even seen!!- The scene fades up with Ivan Stanislav walking down the steps from the Kremlin into Red Square in Moscow, Russia. The sky behind him is gray and gloomy, much like the sky usually is in Russia. Some Russian’s skitter around the area to their places of work or home, but most of the people have turned in already. Snow falls rather heavily through the sky, pelting and covering the footstep-laden snow underneath The Russian Bear’s boots. Stanislav himself wears a heavy brown trench coat, black gloves, a brown bushy hat that covers his ears, black pants, and boots. Snow clings to his black beard as he looks to his left and his right and rubs his hands together. -Russia is entering its winter and sure enough, it’s getting colder and colder. Actually, they’ve lucked out. Normally, one cannot see much beyond their own hand at this point. As a whole, Russia’s been having a decent winter, but at any moment that can all change….- Stanislav trudges through the snow and tramples forward. The snow crunches under his feet while the camera spins around and over his shoulder ahead of him. Up ahead, a small man in a large green jacket, green hat, green pants, and black leather boots walks over to Ivan. Actually, the jacket is one of those really puffy types and he looks more like a walking vegetable than anything else. Upon closer inspection, when the little man makes it to Ivan huffing and puffing, we find that it is actually Communist Party Member Wang! Wang huffs and puffs while clutching at Ivan’s jacket, “Ivan….it is…so cold!!” Ivan pushes Wang off of him and straightens him up. He looks down at Wang and smiles. “Cheer up my good friend, you are within the bounds of the powerful Russian Federation!” Wang shivers and brushes some snow off of his hat. “I do not care how powafool the Wussian Fedahwation is! It is vewy vewy cold here!!” Wang shivers as Ivan walks past him. Wang follows behind him and continues to talk to Praporschik Stanislav. “Pwapowschik, where is Comwade Wuswan?” Ivan sighs and shrugs as they walk towards Ivan’s large red van. “Alexei is busy in the Kremlin at the moment, trying to get some information on this Tempest girl…” Wang jumps and stops Ivan. “Tempest?! I know who this girl is! She is vewy vewy beautiful! But she can be twouble!” Ivan shrugs. “How do you know who this girl is?” Wang grins wide, obviously very proud of himself. “You see, Pwapowschik, she is fwom Hong Kong, so naturwawwy I would know about her!” Ivan turns and continues to walk again. “I do not care! At the moment I do not need to bother myself with that girl! People’s Republic of the Congo! I cannot believe it! But first, I must get through Doug Troy and Trashcan Man….that should not be very hard. Not at all!” “Pwapowschik, I have not even heawd of Doug Twoy yet! That is vewy stwange! He usuawwy is extwemewy competitive!” Ivan laughs loudly, drawing some attention to himself and Wang. “I would not be surprised if he does not even show up for the venue! After all, he now has gotten in my way and has realized what horrible mistake he has made! Together, Walczak and Stanislav will show OSW why we are most powerful group of fighters within this fledgling organization!! Wang, it will be beautiful indeed!” Wang nods quickly and tries to hurry Ivan towards the van. “Yes, but could we not get to van soon? It is too cold!!!” Ivan chuckles to himself as they get closer to the van. “Comrade, do be patient. We must take our time in the Motherland. We have the advantage of time on our hands!” “How are you sure?” “Because Comrade, Doug Troy and Trashcan Man have already signed their death warrants. It is only matter of time before they succumb to our power. And then, and only then, the OSW will rally behind me!” Ivan laughs and opens the passenger side door, letting Wang in. Ivan climbs into the drivers side, his massive weight causes the entire van to shake. He grins over at Wang. “Major Wang, I must say that this is going to be much fun! The OSW is nothing more than a group of young men that will become conscripts to our cause! We will make it our own little communist federation, and we will bury the PCW in the way we see fit. No matter what Reagan thinks! Dyaaha!” Ivan laughs loudly and turns on the van, driving away quickly as the camera pans over to the Kremlin and fades out….
