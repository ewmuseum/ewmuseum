Seth's World: OSW Episode 2

15 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-15-02 Our scene opens with a black screen, as the following words scroll upward. ‘Viewer discretion is advised. Aurora public access does not claim responsibility for the actions of the two idiots who produce the next show or any other, possibly more stupid idiots that they may bring onto the program. With that said, may God have mercy on your souls.’ We fade in to the sound of a heavy metal guitar rift, revealing Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke once again sitting on their living room couch. The words ‘Seth’s Wurld’ appear in front of them in fluorescent lettering, stretching out and moving in all odd directions in a manner that just screams high school TV production class editing. As the letters fade out, we’re left with the two former tag team champions, staring toward the screen. Trevor looks the same as he ever did, but the usual goofy expression Seth wears is missing, replaced by a somewhat serious look for once. What a swerve. Leaning forward, the none-too-happy Seth cocks his head to side as he addresses the television audience. “Welcome to Seth’s World, everybody. I’m Seth Masters...” “And I’m Trevor Hawke,” Trevor chimes in. “...and if you haven’t heard yet, we’re planning on milking this show for all it’s worth,” Seth announces with an agitated tone in his voice. “Not that we’re scared we actually might lose our broadcasting rights in our match with those annoying fucks Carrot Top and Gilbert Gottfried at OSW’s next pay-per-view Head On Collision,” Trevor says sarcastically. Taking a deep breath he begins speaking really fast as he runs through a ppv promotional vehicle. “Coming-your-way-Sunday-April-twenty-eighth-from-the-Joe-Louis-Arena-in-Detroit-Michigan-to-order-call-your-local-cable-provider-for-billing-instructions-only-$29.99.” Trevor inhales and exhales deeply, trying to catch his breath after that mouthful. Seth gives him one of those WTF looks, before returning his attention to the camera. “No, of course not. There’s no way in hell we’re going to get beat by a couple of snot-nosed bitches like Team Annoying,” he says, an angry expression coming over his face as he reaches his hands out in a strangling gesture. “In fact, the only reason we’re doing this is because that red-headed dicksmack can’t stand knowing that we have the means to demean him and other stupid fucks like him in front of a national audience,” Trevor remarks mischievously. “He’s annoyed a countless number of people of the course of his career. Let’s see how he likes it for a change.” “What’s that shit called again, Trev?” Seth asks, still sounding pissed. “Poetic justice?” his partner offers. “Yeah, that’s the stuff,” Seth states, nodding his head. “Poetic fucking justice, man.” Trevor raises a cautious eyebrow. “We sure are swearing a lot for public television, don’t you think, Seth?” “Eh, the censors can go fuck themselves,” Seth mumbles, spitting off to the side of the couch. The camera pans to focus on a man standing next the wall, reaching down to pull out the plug for the satellite feed the show is currently hooked up to. He pauses upon hearing Seth’s last comment and stands up, revealing a shirt which says ‘Censor of the Year’. He stares at the duo on the couch, his lower lip trembling. A single tear appears in the corner of his eye, and he hangs his head in shame before walking off screen sadly. We pan back to our original shot, where Trevor is now eyeing Seth quizzically. “What’s making you so pissy today, anyway?” “I don’t want to talk about it,” Seth mumbles, looking down at the ground. “It’s not hemorrhoids again, is it?” Trevor asks, and, seeing no reaction from Seth, slaps his own forehead in disbelief. “Oh my god, it IS the hemorrhoids again. Damn it man, we have a match this weekend. I thought you...” “I don’t have any damn hemorrhoids!” Seth snaps, before shifting his eyes back and forth, adding hesitantly “Uh...nor did I ever have them.” “Well then what is it?” Trevor inquires, raising his hands in an expression of just how clueless he is. “Damn it, Trev. Carrot Top is refereeing our match against the mech-heads next week. That red-headed fuck is going to screw us out of our win against Twisted Metal! You know it, I know it, and the American people know it!” Seth shouts, clenching his fist. “We’ve already lost to those lazy, worthless robots once because of interference from Heresian Bloodlust. If I lose to them again, I won’t be able to show my face in public anymore.” Seth is red-faced and breathing heavily when he finishes, leaving a somewhat apprehensive-looking Trevor on the other side of the couch, . “Dude, settle down,” Trevor says, making a calming motion with his hands as he tries to talk Seth into a less hostile mind frame. “It’s getting to your head, man. That’s just what that disgrace to comedians everywhere wanted it to do. He’s getting under your skin. You can’t let him do that.” “Well what are we supposed to do?” Seth asks, a helpless tone in his voice. “Stop acting like a fucking pussy for one thing,” Trevor says, slapping Seth in the face. “Heh. Sorry about that. I just kind of felt like smacking you, there.” Seth is unable to say anything, his mouth hanging open in shock. “Well anyways...” Trevor continues. “I don’t care about this Saturday’s match. If we lose, then so what. Everybody is going to see that it’s a bogus win. There’s no way in hell Twisted Metal would ever be able to beat any team in a fair match.” Seth is still unable to speak, the stunned expression remaining on his face, but he manages to reach down and scribble something on a piece of paper with a pen before holding it up for Trevor to see. Trevor, reading what it says, shakes his head in a negative manner. “Nope, I highly doubt they could beat even ex-PCWers Hank and Ganker, and they were the biggest jobbers since Salvatore Sincere dropped his schtick in the WWF,” Trevor pauses in thought, as the ramifications of his last remark register in his head. “Wow, that’s pretty damn bad now that I think about it.” Seth nods in agreement, though his eyes and mouth are still open wide in astonishment. “It doesn’t matter. We’ll just go with the flow. If we’re lucky, we’ll dominate those fah-king robots so badly that there’s no way in hell Carrot Top will be able to avoid counting the pin,” Trevor states confidently, taking up the cocky air that usually surrounds Seth. “At any rate, I think that’s enough talking about pointless matches, my brother from another mother. I think it’s about time to bring out our first guest. What do you say Seth?” “I...I...can’t believe you fucking slapped me,” Seth remarks in disbelief, the look on his face unchanging. “Oh get over it,” Trevor says, shaking his head, before turning to face the camera with a big cheesy smile. “Well without further ado, here is the guest of the day. You may recognize him from such films as...” “I can’t believe you fucking slapped me, you fucker,” Seth repeats, staring a hole through his partner with his angry glare. Trevor sighs, turning away from the camera to deal with him. “Dude, are you going to let me introduce the guest or not?” he asks Seth calmly, as if addressing a child. “I want an apology,” Seth demands, still sounding stunned, before muttering to himself. “You fucking slapped me.” “Whatever man. Quit making such a big deal out of it. We don’t have time for your immature bickering. I have to introduce Keanu Reeves.” “I’M immature?! You’re the one THAT SLAPPED ME!” Seth shouts indignantly, flailing his arms about. “I...wait, did you say Keanu Reeves was on the show?” “Yes. In fact, I was just about to introduce him before you interrupted me. I thought he would be a nice rebound guest after the Carrot Top fiasco. I mean, he was the star of ‘Bill and Ted’s Excellent Adventure’, not to mention their ‘Bogus Journey’ as well, two of the most well known teen idiot flicks in the history of the movie industry. If not for pioneers like him, characters like us may very well not exist,” Trevor smiles, then catches himself, a serious expression coming over his face as he begins to sweat a little bit. “Not to insinuate we’re characters or anything. We’re definitely real people. R-E-A-L! Phh...the very notion that we were characters is preposterous.” Giving a confident smile that isn’t quite confident, Trevor gives a thumbs up toward the camera. “Yeah, but anyways, Keanu’s on tonight. Ted himself!” “Yeah, those were good movies, I guess...but he was also in the Replacements and Hardball,” Seth comments sadly. “So he made a couple bad role choices, but so what. It doesn’t demean what he did in the past. What about the Matrix. That was fucking cool. And the Watcher. And Speed. Come on, Seth...Speed!” Trevor says, gesturing in a pleading manner with his hands. “Yeah, but still...” Seth sighs deeply. “I’ve lost so much respect for the man. I mean what was he thinking. Hardball? The whole guy leads a team of misfit kids to little league sport success. It’s been done hundreds of times, in every sport imaginable. Just cause this one’s in a ghetto doesn’t make any difference. I mean...damn, it’s just so stupid!” “I just hope you’ll refrain from the insults when Mr. Reeves joins us here,” Trevor instructs his partner sternly, before looking at his watch sadly. “If we ever get around to bringing him out, that is.” “How is it that he hasn’t heard me already?” Seth asks. “We’ve got him in a sound proof booth until he receives his cue,” Trevor states, pointing his thumb backwards. “Sound proof booth? Where the fuck did we get one of those?” “Well...actually we just kind of locked him in the bathroom. It might as well be sound proof,” admits Trevor sheepishly. “I know the only thing I can hear when I’m sitting in there are the screams of the damned.” “Oh, is that what those noises are? I’d been wondering about that...” Seth utters thoughtfully. Trevor sweeps his arms to point in the direction of the bathroom in a flamboyant gesture you would expect from a game show host or equally insane individual. “Anyway, without further ado...release Keanu from his smelly prison and bring that sucker out!” “Should I get my guitar and play him in?” Seth asks, oblivious of the fact that he sucks ass. “Er...I don’t think that’s necessary, Seth,” Trevor says, in an effort to dissuade his partner. “Oh, it will be no problem at all. I’ll just run and get it really quick,” Seth says, starting to get up before Trevor pulls him back down by his arm. “Seth. No,” Trevor authoritatively commands. Seth sighs and settles back in the couch, as Trevor turns to greet the entering Keanu Reeves. His trademark dark hair is cut short, and he is wearing a black leisure suit, as if he thought this might actually be a legitimate talk show. Phh, shows how much research he does. “Hey, Keanu! How are you doing, man?” Trevor says, standing up to shake Keanu’s hand. “Well...I’m feeling a bit nauseous, but other than that I guess I’m ok.” Reeves answers, managing a smile as he takes a seat in between Seth and Trevor. “Well it’s fucking awesome having you on the show,” Trevor exclaims, grinning stupidly. “Oh, it’s no problem. Despite the fact that it’s a big Wayne’s World rip-off, I’d glad to say you ripped off Meyers instead of me,” Keanu says, looking from left to right as he chuckles at his smidgen of wit. “Oh, just you wait,” Trevor chuckles back. “Huh?” Keanu raises an eyebrow in question, but is quickly cut off by Seth. “Never mind him. Alright, I’ve got the first question,” Seth says, rubbing his hands together anxiously. “Shoot,” Keanu says, turning to face him, a cocky smile coming over his face, as happens to most celebrities when they receive the opportunity to speak of their celebrity-dom.; “Can you believe that Trevor slapped me?” Seth asks, spreading his arms in disbelief as the shocked tone returns to his voice. “Will you drop it already, you big baby!” Trevor chides, rolling his eyes. “You know, Trev...” Seth says, raising his fist at his partner in anger. “I’ll beat the shit out of you, literally. Then, we’ll see who is the one crapping their pants.” Trevor rolls his eyes again, before snickering. “Oh, you talk big, alright, but I remember the time you jobbed to Ice Cold Andi in the PWA.” “You always have to bring that up, don’t you?” Seth responds, sounding exasperated. Not that you can blame him after a jab like that. “Well it’s pretty fucking funny, don’t you think?” Trevor asks, laughing out loud. “My foot up your ass would be pretty fucking funny, too, dicksmack!” Seth shouts, leaning over Reeves in a threatening fashion. Reeves pulls back from Seth, apprehensively, before waving his arms in the air to try to get the two bickering OSW stars’ attentions. “Uh, guys. Hello? Hollywood star Keanu Reeves here!” “Hold on a sec, Keanu. I have to teach this crybaby a lesson,” Trevor says, cracking his knuckles in a show of hostility. “Bring it on, you shit-faced shit-eater,” Seth spits back, craning his head as he motions for Trevor to do something. Trevor cocks his head as well, a confident look coming over him. “Oh, I will, trust me. I...” He pauses in mid-sentence, his attention being drawn to something off-camera. He mouths a few words, before a confused expression comes over his face. After mouthing a few more words, he turns to face the camera, looking somber. “Oh crap, it looks as if we’re out of time for this week.” “You’re kidding, right?” Keanu inquires incredulously. “Um...no, not really,” Trevor explains to Keanu, an apologetic expression on his face. “Sorry, dude, but this isn’t no fancy shmancy hour long talk show deal. We’ve got about twenty minutes, and we wasted ten of it trying to get the camera set up.” Keanu doesn’t look very happy about this news, as he is currently massaging his temples in astonishment. “I spent three hours in that stank-ass bathroom for this! You didn’t even interview me?!” “Sorry, man. We can continue this interview next week, if that’s ok,” offers Trevor, sounding a bit unsure. “Yeah, you could hang out with us until then. It’ll be awesome!” Seth exclaims. “You can sleep in Trevor’s bed and he’ll sleep on the...oh wait, he already sleeps on the couch. Well, Trevor could sleep in the bathtub.” “NO!” Trevor shouts, shuddering at the thought of a night in that bathroom. “Alright then...Trev can sleep on the ground for a week. It’s no big deal,” Seth tells Keanu with a nod. “Yeah, the ground is sweet as hell,” Trevor adds. “That’s where marijuana grows. Not that I’d smoke it or anything.” Turning to the camera, Trevor smiles and gives a thumbs up, one of those glinty things appearing on his teeth. “Er...thanks but no thanks, guys,” Keanu says, the tone in his voice screaming that he wants to get out of the hell hole as fast as he can. In fact, he’s already on his way out the door. “I think I’ll just cut my losses and be on my way. Nice...meeting you...or something,” “Not so fast, Mr. Reeves. I don’t think you’re going anywhere,” Trevor says, placing a hand on Keanu’s shoulder, effectively stopping him. “What do you mean?” Keanu asks, beginning to sound a bit worried about his impending fate. “Did you read your contract before you signed it?” asks Trevor. “Not really...it looked like a normal type of commitment to me. What about it?” “Well if you would have read more closely, you would have realized since we didn’t finish your segment you have to live with us for the next week until we can wrap things up on the next show!” Seth shouts happily. “That can’t possibly be what it says,” Keanu says, shaking his head skeptically. “Mr. High-Priced-Lawyer, am I telling the truth?” Seth asks, looking toward the window. An intelligent looking man in a suit and tie, holding a briefcase in his hand, leans in through the opening and directs a nod toward Seth. “Indeed you are, my lad. We wouldn’t want to have to crush the poor boy under my callous lawsuit filing foot, would we?” “I would hope not,” Trevor says, before turning to reach a hand out toward Reeves. “So what do you say, Keanu?” Sighing deeply, Reeves hangs his head sadly and shakes Trevor’s hand. “I hate my life.” “Good answer!” Seth and Trevor say simultaneously, and it is here where the feed cuts off. I can’t wait to see what kind of exciting adventures Keanu and Civil D are going to partake in over the next week. It should be splendor-ific, to say the least. Thank god for celebrities: the endless store of comedy.
