Working on the farm. No reason why. Just wanna be goofy I guess.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/10 We’re in America’s heartland. Well, at least part of it. Omaha, Nebraska to be exact. Omaha isn’t known for much. Hell, Nebraska isn’t known for much. All we know about Omaha is it hosts the College World Series for baseball and plays home to the DAMN Doug Troy. So what brings us to Omaha? Farmland! Omaha, as well as the rest of Nebraska, is loaded with it. At least so we think. But to avoid public humiliation, we’re gonna hang out just south of Omaha in a place called Papillion. We’re still in Nebraska…just not in Omaha. Fade in An aerial shot shows nothing but farmland. Farmland as far as the eye can see. A little movement from tiny figures below. Those would be farmers or someone else of the such. With farmland come these types of people. There’s nothing one can do about it. We switch from an aerial shot to a shot on the ground. With all the cornfields growing really high, our view is obstructed some. “Stupid hoe!” a male voice shouts. The shot moves its way through the cornrows until we reach the source of the voice. The source: Former four-time PCW Cruiserweight champion, Kid Wonder. “Why the fuck can’t you do your job!” again shouts the young man. In the immediate background stands Amanda. She is wearing a flannel farm girl shirt that’s tied in a knot at the bottom thus leaving her midriff bare. Amanda is also wearing some short jean shorts. “Why are you doing this?” asks the woman. “Damn you, hoe!” again shouts K-Dub. “You’ll die for this!” Wonder, wearing a flannel shirt, overalls, and a yellow straw hat, kicks down. Amanda sighs as we hear a snap. The shot looks down to show that K-Dub has a broken piece of wood in his hand. Look down further and we see the rest of a broken hoe. Once Wonder breaks the hoe, he looks back at his girlfriend. “Sorry,” he says to her. “Had to put that bitch in its place.” Amanda doesn’t respond with words. Just another heavy sigh. “Now, I reckon we get this here corn up out the ground,” says the teen idol in a poor hillbilly accent. “I don’t mean this,” Amanda says in reference to getting the corn out of the ground. “Why do we have to be here?” “Because this here is what nobodies who do nothing but dream do,” K-Dub tells her. “And I reckon that Scott Taylor is a nobody who only dreams of being what I am.” “And why must you talk like that?” Amanda asks in a bit of an aggravated tone. “Talk like what? What you talkin’ ‘bout lil’ lady?” asks Wonder in that annoying hillbilly accent. “Like that!” shouts Amanda. Wonder pulls up one ear of corn. “’Cause this is how all dem inbreds like Scotty-boy talk.” “You know,” Amanda says as she points at her boyfriend, “you’re slowly becoming a lost cause. “Aw, honey, now that’s no way to talk to your lover,” he annoyingly tells Amanda. “Lil’ Scotty-boy just happened to accuse me of keeping him out of P…P…P…” Kid Wonder stops for a moment. He hops up and down slightly while holding his hands over his groin. “I gotta pee,” Wonder says in a Forrest Gump type retarded accent. Without thinking twice, the “Quintessential Teen Idol” turns around, unbuckles his overall, and takes a pee right onto the corn in the ground. Amanda looks up at the sky and just sighs. Once relieved, Wonder puts his unit away, buckles the overalls back up, and turns back towards his girlfriend. “That felt gooooood,” relaxingly says K-Dub. “Now help an ol’ buck out, lil’ lady.” “Ugh!” disgusting says Amanda. “I’m going back to the house.” And with that, Amanda turns around and hurriedly walks away. Kid Wonder, on the other hand, just shrugs. He grabs a backup hoe that just happened to be sitting there and gets back to doing some farm work. “Silly hoe,” Wonder says while working as we mercifully fade away. Fade out
