It was only a matter of time, Toomes supposes. With thousands of people crying out for his painful demise, it was only natural that they pass on the title. But it's small consolation for him, lying still in the backseat of his Camaro, parked at a rest stop, the early morning fog being melted away by the burgeoning sunlight. His body a mass of bruises and aches, his knee screaming in agony. He had wrenched it badly in the match, and now he was paying for the abuse he put himself through. Like a title really matters, he thinks. Good for him. Let him be the target of scrutiny, let him be the target of abuse from Internet Smarks, know-nothing fans and idiot marks. Let the sheets nit-pick him apart. He can have it. But for a former indy wrestler like Toomes, for a little while, it meant the world. And now it was gone. For good. Why bother getting it back? He was universally hated. From CCW to all over the PTC, he was despised. And not just in-character. Everyone hated him. Everyone wanted to see him go under. To see him admit that he couldn't hack it. To see him crash and burn. Well, at least he made them happy now. He was approaching bottom. Right now. He was teetering on the edge. He was exhausted, mentally, physically, emotionally. Jane is up and around, but she hasn't contacted him. Jimmy and the guys haven't talked to him in months. Drew and Frankie are doing their own thing. He was cast adrift and floating aimlessly, and spiraling downward. How long before he was burnt up, burnt out, and cast aside? How long before he was living out of his car and working high school gyms to make ends meet? How long before he went crawling back to CCW? Toomes grunts in the backseat, and rights himself into a sitting position. Fuck this self-pity shit. He looks around him, in the cluttered backseat. His ring gear is on the floorboards, still reeking of sweat. He looks down at himself, the dingy grey sweatpants and the slightly damp vintage Guns N Roses shirt sticking to his frame. His hair was everywhere, lusterless and oily. He went out of the arena, not even bothering to take a shower. Might as well avoid the shame of looking the other guys in the eye, the ones you rocketed past on your way to the top. No need to put yourself under any undue pressure, eh? Groaning, he rolls over the seat and falls into the passenger seat, trying to avoid stepping on his CD wallet. 'Where the fuck are my keys?' he mutters, the cotton in his mouth garbling his words. He finds them in the change dish and fits himself into the front seat. Sliding the ignition key in, he flicks the car into life, and the radio comes on, playing a dippy little Linkin Park song. Toomes curls his lip in derision. 'Meh. No wonder this world is going to shit. They got shit bands like this playing.' He hits the CD player, and Axl Rose and company start off 'Welcome To The Jungle'. Toomes smiles and puts the Camaro in gear, sliding out of the parking space and onto the road. If I'm going to hit bottom, he thinks to himself as the music soothes his frazzled nerves, I might as well take as many people down with me. The Camaro shifts into 5th gear, rocketing down the Canadian highway. -FIN-
