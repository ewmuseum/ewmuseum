Eyes on Me.

23 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sunday, June 23, 2002 There he stood. International Title in his hands, standing on the second rope, with a cheering section stretching all the way across the arena. Thousands of fans were on their feet, throwing their hands in the air fror the newly crowned champion. He had overcome the odds, defeating the large man, who apparently did not have God on his side this time. The Staples Center was roaring louder than during the Lakers' Championship run, but here, a different champion stepped forward. And his name was chanted in unison; 'Angeldust....Angeldust...' It was a feeling he had never felt before. All of his life he had been playing second fiddle in a feud. He had been behind the shadows of every great champion in MMW history, from madBLACK to Gangstar. In OSW it was the likes of Propaganda, Doug Troy, and Sebastian Toomes. Now, however, other people would be living in his shadows. There would be an entire federation of people gunning for him night in and night out. It was a feeling he had already begun to love. He already knew Propaganda was back in his locker room, wiping off the stench of defeat in the shower. Eric Sagar was probably bawling his eyes out. Angeldust was the happiest man in the world right now. It didn't matter that it wasn't a World Heavyweight Title, but now the monkey was off his back, and the press would be too. 'Angeldust....Angeldust....Angeldust' He looked at 5 years of his past before his eyes, getting passed over time and again for the younger, better looking superstars. The drug influence, they told him, meant that a person with the nickname 'Angeldust' couldn't hold the World Title in a federation with such prestige and morality among the indy circuit. Now, that has all changed. Now, it was time for a new beginning for an aging superstar. 'Ladies and gentlemen,' the ring announcer cried out, 'your NEW INTERNATIONAL CHAMPION, 'ANGELDUST' NIIIIIIIIIICK CASEYYYYYYYYYYY!' *** And then he woke up. But damn, did the dream feel nice. The heat reached temperatures of ninety degrees at nightfall, and the humidity was enough to make you want to slit your wrists, but everything feels good when you win a title. That's probably why Nick Casey felt like shit as he lay in his hotel room bed. He donned black basketball shorts, and no shirt, the covers thrown to the side. Nick moved over to the side of his bed, and let his legs over the side, as the mesh dropped with them. He looked at the clock. 'Eight-fifteen.' he said to himself, as he yawned out the sleepiness. 'Better start heading over to the arena.' The night before had been long, Nick had taken a long look in the mirror. He was a changed man now. The night before on Slam! he had cleanly beaten Propaganda, the International Champion, and his clear rival in the past few weeks. But the fact that he had cleanly beaten him meant nothing since he was still without the one thing he desired most in the world; a title. He knew his chance would come, but he was tired of waiting. Now he had beaten the current champion twice in a row, and both times he didn't 'earn' the title. But now, he had bigger fish to fry. Now was his real chance to prove himself. No longer would he be the small fish in the big pond, now he was going to show the world what he was made out of. Reno Davis, Ivan Stanislav, Graphic Violence, Rhett Locke, Sebastian Toomes, all of the big shots of Old School Wrestling would be in this match; and then there's Nick Casey. He has to win. Or at least show that he belongs. Christy rolled over in her bed, smiling at Nick. 'Break a leg, babe.' Then, she said the infamous words, that made Nick's spine chill. 'Everyone will be watching you.' Yes, they would be. But for now, his eyes were enough.
