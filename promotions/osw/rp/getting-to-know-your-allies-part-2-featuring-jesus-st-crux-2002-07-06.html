Getting to know your allies, part 2 (featuring Jesus St. Crux)

6 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/06/02 Back at Del Dante’s Italian restaurant, Jim and Kami Axtell check their menus over as they hold hands on the corner of the table. Jesus St. Crux and his girlfriend Caridad Mariposa sit across from the Axtells, also perusing their menus. Jim shakes his head slightly and moans. “This is tough,” says Jim. “Everything looks so good.” Kami agrees. “Sure does. You know how I love Italian food.” Kami lifts her head and looks over to Jesus and Caridad. “Have you two decided yet?” Jesus smiles at Kami. “I think so. We had the opportunity to glance over the menu before you arrived. The choice wasn’t any easier I can assure you.” “We better hurry and decide, hon,” Jim says as he quickly skims over the menu again. Kami does the same. Finally after a few more seconds the couple settle on their choices. “I think we’re rea-“ Jim starts, but is startled by the waiter as he suddenly appears by his side. “-dy.” The waiter has notebook in hand as he peers down at the four people sitting at the table. “Who would like to start?” Jesus, Caridad, Jim, and Kami all look at each other in amazement at the waiters’ uncanny punctuality. Finally Jim motions to Jesus and Caridad to go first. “Thank you, Jim. The lady will have the chicken tortellini in the red wine sauce, and I will have the manicotti with tomato and basil sauce,” Jesus orders. “And to drink?” asks the waiter. “Hmmm……this is a special occasion,” ponders Jesus. He looks to Jim and Kami. “Champagne?” The Axtell’s look at each other, then at Jesus and nod approvingly. “One bottle of your finest champagne,” requests Jesus. The waiter nods and scribbles some notes down on his notepad, then turns to Jim. “Let’s see here, Kami would like the lasagna and I will have the shrimp and pasta with pesto sauce.” says Jim. “Good choices all. Very good and I will have your orders shortly.” With that the waiter disappears in the blink of an eye. Jim takes a sip from his glass of water, then engages Jesus in conversation. “So are you looking forward to Slam!?” asks Jim of his partner. “Si, it’s time for some payback on those two menaces,” replies Jesus with vindication in his voice. “It certainly is. I’ve had just about enough of Kory Storm’s crap, not to mention bringing Frankie Bones and Michel Krieg in on it as well,” says Jim. “I may be new to this business, but I won’t stand for a bunch of thugs running roughshod and thinking they are above everyone else. I won’t let that happen in OSW.” Jesus agrees. “I feel the same, Jim. There is no room in OSW for people such as this. Caridad and I have talked many a time about the lack of respect that these men have for others, a lack of respect that cannot continue. They must be made to respect.” Out of nowhere yet again appears the waiter, a sterling silver bucket filled with ice and a bottle of champagne tucked in his arm. “Man,” exclaims Jim. “Do you just appear out of thin air or what?” The waiter smiles down at Axtell. “You underestimate my sneakiness, sir. Your champagne.” The waiter places the bucket in the center of the table and quickly spreads out four champagne flutes for each person at the table. With grace and ease the waiter pops the cork on the bubbly and pours four perfect glasses of champagne. No sooner is he done then he disappears once again. Jim, Kami, Jesus, and Caridad smile and shake their heads as each of them raises their glass of champagne toward the center of the table. “To victory!” salutes Jesus. “To friendship!” salutes Jim. “Cheers!” The four of them clink their glasses together and then drink to the toast, hoping that their fortunes are good on Slam!
