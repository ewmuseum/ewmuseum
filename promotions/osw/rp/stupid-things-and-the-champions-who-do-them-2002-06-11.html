Stupid things and the champions who do them

11 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-11-2002 “You know, Doug, there are better ways to go about this sorta thing…” “Shh, you’ll break my concentration.” The scene opens up on a lot of darkness. No, it’s not your TV, this promo is being filmed in ‘can’t-see-shit’-ovision aka nighttime camera work minus lights and special filters. Why the lack of special camera equipment? “Well, excuse me all to hell for thinking this is a stupid idea.” “Look, it’s an international incident only if I get caught, okay?” Yep, something illegal’s going down. The lack of camera equipment is part of the cameraman’s self-preservation instinct. You can’t do much running with 50 pounds of camera equipment now, can you? “Well, be sure to not get caught then.” “Oh, I won’t be caught. The Polish couldn’t catch AIDS if they fucked the entire Ream Team.” The confident guy is none other than Doug Troy. It’s hard to see his face due to the darkness and all. The other party, speaking to Doug through an earpiece, is former DAMN associate Yesman. Yesman, clearly not being used to Doug’s offbeat antics, is about one polish police siren away from a coronary. How are we hearing earpiece conversation? Through the MAGIC OF CINEMA. “Dude, if you get caught, I don’t know you.” “You think I’m going to narc on you?” Today’s antic features Doug quietly making his way across a darkened field toward a large and majestic building. What he plans to do when he reaches his destination is unknown, but we can bet that it’s not the smartest thing in the world to do. “Of course you would! You always were jealous of me…” “Jealous?!” Not that Doug has ever been known for the wiseness of his actions. If anything, he’s known for quite a lot of stupid things. Things that usually wind up getting him in trouble. “Yeah, jealous!” “Why would I be jealous of a wash-up like you?” This might be the thing that gets him in the most trouble. Not only for the man he’ll piss off doing this thing, but also for the potential of an international incident directly resulting from the action. “Well, I had elephants and a statue! And 40 some women for my entrance!” “So what? I’m the OSW World Champion!” But does Doug care about the consequences of these actions? Oh no, why would he go and do something logical like that? No, much better to do something foolhardy and stupid than to do something quick and smart. “Pfft, elephants and women are better than any stupid championship belt…” “Look, would you shut up? I’m trying to do something highly illegal here, dammit!” At least he has the ability to distinguish right from wrong. “Fine, buzz me if when you finish up or if you get caught, so I know to tell your parents.” Doug nods his head and turns toward the large wall in front of him. The building is elaborately built, looking like something right out of a Victorian painting. Meticulously designed stone walls shelter the interior from the weather, while large stained glass windows 20 feet off the ground overlook everything in the area. Doug looks around for any guards and aims his flashlight at the wall. Light sharply splashes the wall, dully illuminating Doug and the area around him. There’s a soft rattling sound before Doug points a can at the wall. The can hisses as Doug presses down on the top. Time to make a statement. It had been too long since Doug had made a statement, and his silence was starting to have an adverse effect on the fans and guys in the back. Several were starting to question Doug’s ability, stating his lack of title defenses as evidence. They had a point. By this time two months ago, the former champion Sebastian Toomes had already defended the titles several times from several different comers. Why has Doug yet to defend his title once? Who knows? Maybe it was the fact that Walczak had stepped into the title picture before Doug had even won the damn thing. Maybe it was the fact that most of the other contenders had other feuds to deal with. No cares? All that matters is that Doug was ready. He always had been. But Walczak was already there, making statements left and right, silencing the competition. Statements I should’ve made first. And yet, even with all the statements and beatings, the fans weren’t truly into the feud. That ate at Doug’s mind. But can you blame them? C’mon, a feud over stolen soda? Anyone would regard such petty circumstances as an afterthought. No wonder fans aren’t taking out second mortgages to see the Pay Per View. The whole feud seemed to be lacking something integral. Something that made the fans get into it that much more. Something that drove buy rates through the roof. Soda wasn’t it. Time for some focus. By this time, Walczak had to be frustrated. He’d already lost cleanly to Doug in a non-title tag match. That HAD to be eating away at his insides. Time to pour salt on the wound. Doug switched to another can of spray paint and continued decorating the wall. What exactly he’s writing on the wall is difficult to make out due to the lack of light. However, due to the sweeping strokes he’s starting to make, it seems that he’s almost finished with whatever. With a final pass, he steps back and admires his handiwork. “Now THIS is much better than fighting over soda. It’s always better to feud over art, anyway. It just seems so much more… CLASSY. You like it?” The cameraman nods, despite his inability to see what Doug sprayed. “See, you know good art when you see it.” Doug takes the can and throws it in a small duffel bag hanging off his shoulder. “Time to split, man. Walczak’s about to get My Take, and when he does things are gonna get a lot more interesting around here.” He quickly waves his hand at the cameraman and starts to jog away from the scene of the crime. The camera fades to black as the two jog away into the blackness of the Polish night.
