At Home in a Strange Land

6 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/06/02 ((As the material suggests, this is 'tape delayed' from a few days ago')) ~Some people are lucky. They always know what it is they're supposed to be doing, where they should be going, and what's expected of them. Some people have signposts to guide them and milestones to show where they've been. I wish I had such a life sometimes. It would remove so much doubt. I don't even remember my mother's name. But here in the isolated building, on the isolated military base in Arkhangelsk that Ivan calls home base, I have a vivid view of just such a life, his. It's a little intimidating really. I can't read half the plaques, but I know they must be honors of various sorts. Most are accompanied by a picture, and although I'm not that strong on Russian politics, I do know what the last three presidents of Russia look like. Do they call them presidents? I'll have to ask Ivan. There he is with Gorbachev getting a medal of some kind. He's in military uniform so I guess it's for fighting. Hmm, the date is... right about the time they pulled out of Afghanistan. Ah, I see. Moving down the wall further is an easy step back in time. He looks so young in some of these. I'll have to tease him about that. Well... maybe not this week. I just wish I could see him. Hmm, this one must be special, placed right between the flags of the Russian Federation and the USSR. Yup, must have wanted this one to be seen internationally; it's in English as well as Russian. 'Hero of the Soviet Union, awarded for valor and the saving of countless lives in the Afghan conflict.' I keep looking for something a little more personal, but with Ivan I'm not sure that this isn't as personal as it gets. After all, everything he is is spelled out here in a wood and polished brass, in draped flags and pressed uniforms. I know there's more to him, but it's so hard to get to sometimes. Hey, here's a closet. I winder if there's any skeletons in here? Nope, but there are twelve black shirts, eight pairs of suspenders and one neatly pressed uniform... I should have guessed. Even mice are afraid to hide out in there.~ Tempest laughed at the all too familiar wardrobe before her and smiled as she shut the door. She looked around the office. She sighed nervously and said, 'I dunno if I can take this. Ivan's been in there a long time now. I hope he's all right.' She paced a little and walked around his desk, eyeing it with some trepidation. 'He can't get mad at me for looking at all this stuff if I can't read it can he?' she asked no one. 'I mean, it's not like I can do any damage to national security when everything on those papers is gibberish.' She smiled having relieved herself of the imagined threat and peered over the desk. It was littered with papers and books, none of which Tempest could read. She chuckled at the near obsolete computer in his desk and said, 'I'll have to hotwire him one of my old ones. It'd still be better than that. Of course he'd probably get in trouble when I program it to make chirpy birdy noises every hour on the hour.' She shrugged and continued to poke around, not truly snooping just... looking. It all seemed very official, boring even to Tempest's uneducated eye. Then she stopped as her gaze fell upon the one thing not even remotely related to the Russian Federation or it's army. There in a small silver frame, carefully nestled between some books and the monitor, was a picture... of Tempest. She smiled and sat down slowly in the chair, which nearly swallowed her up. 'I wonder how long that's been there. Half a world away, among all this...' she said trailing off as she looked around a gestured to the room around her. 'Your regimented thoughts turn to me here?' She jumped up form the chair as the door opened, and tried not to look too startled as an officer walked in wearing the drab uniform of an enlisted man. 'You will want to be coming with me. Praporschik Stanislav is most better,' he said with a nervous air, keenly aware of his broken English. Tempest smiled good-naturedly and followed him out of the room and said, 'Thanks.' She hoped he understood it. A little way down the hall he motioned her into a small infirmary. She gulped and stared at the motionless form of Ivan on the bed. In the corner a heart monitor beeped and Tempest's eyes widened at the sight. The officer seemed to notice and said, 'That is not terrible thing. His heart is beat... um... rhythm... uh...' He struggled for the words. 'Not rhythm like...' 'Not regularly?' Tempest offered. 'Da! Not regular. Is not terrible hurt, just ehm... cautionary.' 'Oh,' Tempest said and nodded. 'Thank you. I'll h... just stay with him awhile. Ok?' The officer nodded and backed out of the room shutting the door behind him. Tempest stepped closer and looked down at Ivan. That in itself was rare. She laid her hand on his shoulder and leaned close as said softly, 'Ivan?' A low grumble came from deep within Stanislav's chest as his brow furrowed, and from under his eyelids his eyes moved. 'MMmmm....' Tempest smiled and leaned forward little more, nuzzling his cheek. 'You there?' she said brightly. Slowly, one of Ivan's huge eyes opened groggily and looked over at Tempest. A rush of air exited his nose, 'Tempest...?' 'Hi Ivan, I'm here,' she said and smiled. 'How ya feeling?' Ivan groaned, his heart monitor began to beep slightly faster as his body stirred, 'Head is slightly throbbing, but nothing Praporschik Stanislav.....' Ivan winced as he moved his torso, '...cannot handle.' 'Awww.... don't push yourself,' she said and patted his shoulder. 'I'm just glad to hear your voice again.' Ivan grunted and gritted his teeth, 'What are you doing here??' He looked around groggily, 'Am I not in Russia?' Tempest nodded and said, 'You are, and so am I. I was worried about you. So I came along too.' Ivan blinked, 'Y..you...you came to Russia? For me?!' He stopped for a moment and digested this and looked at her and smiled just slightly, his beard untrimmed, he appeared older than before, 'You want to hear something....strange?' Tempest grinned as she nodded and said, 'Sure! Strange is my middle name.' Ivan thought for a moment, 'I had a dream....that you were...holding my head...you were hurt...blood was on your face...and you were talking to...Alexei..I think...about how you would..' he paused and swallowed, 'watch after me....and I said....' Ivan paused again, 'n...nevermind.' She chuckled softly and said, 'Beautiful Storm, I am sorry?' The heart monitor beeped faster as Ivan raised an eyebrow, 'Y..yes....that is what I....how did you know??' She shook her head and said, 'It was no dream, that was a little after the show. I came in and tried to help you as best I could.' She shrugged a little and said, 'I figured you must be delirious. After all, you don't have anything to apologize for.' Ivan blushed suddenly at the revelation and sat up some more, 'Uh..yes...well...I suppose I feel..somewhat...uh..accountable.' 'For what?' Tempest said curiously, as she watched him sit up. She put her hand on his arm gently as he swayed slightly. Ivan sighed deeply, 'I feel accountable for what has happened to you...Tempest.' He paused and looked at her, 'Ivan understands that you may not like Reagan after her...change, but men such as Doug Troy, Meanstreak, and Toomes want to hurt you now...because of...me. You are guilty by association because of what I do...and that is not fair, Tempest...' He thought for a moment, perhaps the drugs had lowered his guard a bit, 'You have had enough....trials in your life Tempest, let alone to be hated like the rest of us...' Tempest scooted closer to him and wrapped her arms around him. 'Ivan, it's ok. You haven't brought anything on me I wasn't already expecting after I challenged Jane. If I'm hated by people I don't respect to begin with, I don't really feel like I've lost anything.' Ivan nodded, 'I suppose that is valid,' he put his arm around her, 'but come, I said I would show you the wonders of The Motherland, and now that you are here, I will show you!' Ivan grinned and began to get up out of the bed, pulling wires off of his body as he did so. 'Ivan, take it easy!' Tempest said. She tried to steady him a bit, but it was a difficult task. 'You've got a bad concussion. There's a lot of Russia out there to show, but you don't have to right now.' 'BAH! It will take more than head injury to stop me!' Ivan growled and got out of the bed, clad in only a pair of black pants. His massive shoulders rose and fell as he took two steps and then staggered to the side and crashed into the wall, collapsing some plaster. Tempest held his arm and said, 'Ivan, I know you wanna fight this, but please... try and relax? Right now, you could hurt yourself worse trying to do stuff.' Ivan growled and shrugged her off, he took two steps ahead of her and wavered, he put his head down, 'I do not think you should be here....' She stepped forward and to the side, looking up at him as she said, 'Why? I only wanna help.' Ivan turned his head to the side, not looking at her, 'I know, and I understand...you should not see me like this....' Ivan paused, 'I have not been like this since the War...when I was shot...' She reached out and took hold of his hand and said, 'I'm sorry. I didn't think about it that way. But can't you see I'm willing to stick around when things aren't at their best?' He turned to Tempest and cupped her hand in his free hand and smiled. 'It is okay...I am glad that you are here Tempest... it is... unique... in way...if that makes sense.' He took a step closer to her and nodded with a smile. She nodded and said, 'Why's that? I mean besides the fact that I almost set off a security alarm cuz I got bored and opened the wrong door earlier.' Ivan shrugged and tilted his head and looked at her thoughtfully, 'I would never expect a woman to be here, to pay me visit, merely to see if Ivan was well, that is all.' He chuckled and swayed to one side and then caught himself. 'Awww, but this is me you're talking to,' she said with a wink. 'Did you think I'd just go tripping on my way. I even have a plan to avenge you,' she said proudly. Ivan blinked, suddenly much more grim, 'A plan? What kind of plan?' He turned and shuffled over to his bed and sat on it, groaning loudly. Tempest shook her head sympathetically and said, 'I challenged that nasty Toomes! I'm gonna beat him up and embarrass him. Then maybe Jane will see how stupid she's been.' Tempest folded her arms as a flash of anger crossed her face at the thought of the owner. 'WHAT?!' Ivan jumped to his feet and then quickly sat back down, lest he fall over, 'It is a trap, you know this! That is what they want!!' 'Aww, don't be mad,' she said, shuffling nervously. 'I can do it. And besides, Alexei said I could count on the Red Army's support, so it won't be like last time.' Ivan nodded, 'Da...Alexei will take care of you. I will be there then...if that is the case.' Ivan stood up again, 'I should start to get ready....' He took another step, stopped, and grabbed the wall. Tempest stepped forward and looped her arm around his waist, standing close so as to help him stay balanced. 'Ivan, you don't have to do anything right now but rest,' she said. 'It's a few days away and don't worry, we'll be ready for them. She smiled up at him softly. Ivan looked at her and thought long and hard, and then nodded, 'Perhaps... you are correct.' He shook his head and smiled at Tempest, 'I will make sure that you have somewhere to stay, and if you would rather leave, I can arrange a helicopter for your departure.' 'Nope!' she said and shook her head vigorously. 'I gotta stay and keep an eye on you. I bet if I go, you'll just try and do more stuff you shouldn't yet.' She grinned and pointed at the bed and said, 'So lie down and relax or I'll... I'll...' She seemed quite lost looking for a useful threat. Ivan crossed his arms and grinned, 'You'll what?' 'Well...' she paused and scratched her head. Then she brightened and said, 'I'll sit on you! That's it, I'll sit on you and keep you down that way.' Ivan recoiled and laughed, 'Well then, by all means, it would be in my best interest to....' he paused...and grinned, 'Ivan is not sure what outcome is in his best interest... eh?' He burst into laughter, 'DYAAHAA!!' Tempest chuckled and said, 'Oops, I was afraid of that. But c'mon...' she took his hand and smiled, 'Sit down, let's let that injury heal, hmm?' She gazed up at him with an expression that would put puppies to shame. Ivan nodded and sat down. He squeezed her hand and looked at her face for a moment and smiled, 'Thank you for coming Tempest....really.' 'Aw, did you really think I wouldn't?' she said as she stepped behind him slightly and put her arms around his neck gently. 'Being hurt is boring. I wouldn't leave ya to do it alone.' She smiled and kisses his head lightly. Ivan nodded, 'To be lonely can be scary thing...Tempest...I am sure we both know that...' he paused, 'but we will not have to worry about that again, my dear.' He swallowed and rubbed her arm with his free hand and stared at the floor.
