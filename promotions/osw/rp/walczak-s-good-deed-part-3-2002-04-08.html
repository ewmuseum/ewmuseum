Walczak's Good Deed (Part 3)

8 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/8/02 The scene opens up and Walczak is seen running across the grass to chase down the man who is lit on fire. Walczak runs past a man with a long trench coat, and while passing him he grabs the coat and when he catches up to the man pats him down with it, and eventually gets the fire out. The man’s face and arm are severely burned, and Walczak starts to call for help finally someone hears him, and the person calls an ambulance. Walczak covers the man up in the trench coat, but than a hand comes upon the trench coat. “What the hell are you doing? Can’t you see that the man is burned to nearly a crisp?” “I don’t give a damn what happened, all I know is that I won’t let someone who beat the hell out of a helpless monkey take my jacket for any reason whatsoever.’ “What do you mean?” “I saw you beat up ZaC, on SLAM! Yeah, I saw you throw the poor monkey to the ground, and beat upon it with a relentless vigor. Did you not see that the thing weighed a mere ten pounds?” “Well, we had to send a message to that punk bitch Reno Davis.” Then the man who is burned starts to speak. “Wait, the man who saved me is the sick fuck who beat up ZaC?” “You bet your damn ass he is.” “Why didn’t you just let me burn, it would have been better than being saved by a poor bastard such as yourself.” “Are you serious? I just saved your life, and all that you can do is lecture me on the fact that I beat up a stupid monkey.” “There you go again, resorting to name calling.” “I can’t believe this shit.” Just then an ambulance comes on to the grass and takes out a stretcher. They put the man on the stretcher and put him into the ambulance. “Excuse me sir, but before we take you into the hospital, we will just need you to tell us who saved you.” With little energy left he points to Walczak. “Are you kidding me? This sick son of a bitch that manhandled poor little ZaC is the one that saved you?” The man nods “Oh, great so we have a fucking animal abuser that saved a guy, what a bunch of irony. I would thank you for your brave effort in helping this man, but to tell you the truth I don’t think that you deserve any thanks whatsoever.” The ambulance packs the man and drives away with the sirens blasting. Walczak goes over to sit down on a nearby bench. “I seriously don’t know what to do. I mean I help someone and I am regarded as a hero, but then I beat up a pathetic unimportant monkey, and I am looked upon as a villain of villains. No matter, because I will soon show the people my true beliefs.” Walczak gets up and walks away down the path, and as he walks away the scene fades to black…
