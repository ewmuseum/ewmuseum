002. Shadows

10 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 10, 2002 History has the tendency to always come back and bite you in the ass. It really is an unfortunate thing, because every opportunity for reform and change tends to blow up in the face of the reformer. No matter how hard you can try, you can never escape your past. It's always right behind you like a shadow at the setting sun. And like that shadow, it goes back farther than the eye could possibly see. Jason Winters recognized this as he stood at the base of the UWA's main tower, casting a shadow farther than his eye could see. That place had a lot of history, and a lot of his history was defined by that building. He could never forget his tenure there, and it seemed as if no one would ever forget the UWA in return. Kanaida had arrived in the OSW, ready to cause the kind of havoc that only the self-proclaimed Northern Wonder could provide. As well, Jesus Saint Crux was unwilling to forget the past history of the pair. The three of them had one of the most interesting (and intertwined) histories in the soap opera that was the Universal Wrestling Alliance. Kanaida's entrance had predated Jason's by a good fifteen months, and Jason made the decision to catch up up to the icon. He did and became the definition of an underling to the Wonder. When Kanaida betrayed the fans that adored him, Jason took over the spotlight, going so far as to call himself 'The New Northern Wonder,' and recruited Jesus Saint Crux, who came in four months after Jason. They teamed up, and Jason had become the ultimate face, one that eclipsed even Kanaida's at the height of his career. Unfortunately, Jason was stricken with jealousy when Jesus won the thing he strived for his entire career: the heavyweight title. He turned away from everyone that made him, and went down the path of Kanaida. Jason sighed, staring at the former headquarters of his home. He was incredibly stupid to turn his back on the fans, the people he was paid to entertain, and the people that loved him. He focused on something as worthless as a title, and it got the better of him. Of course, he had changed since then. Unfortunately for him, Jesus hadn't come to realize this. A simple plan for peace and mutual respect had fallen through. But, Jason didn't have time to be distraught about his entrance into Old School Wrestling. He had to focus on Jesus Saint Crux's undying determinism to crush his former friend, and Kanaida's will to stop both of them. Nothing could possibly be worse... Or so he thought.
