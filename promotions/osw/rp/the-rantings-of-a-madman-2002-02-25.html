The rantings of a madman....

25 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/25/02 It was a crescent moon. The day was dim with fear, but the night was bright with passion. In a tall, tall tower, whose shadow shrouds the city streets for miles, a young man resides. Ahh - but not any man. This man earns his wages through hurting people, and taking pain, he is Ritchie Syxs. He writes on a piece of paper, as he sits in a gray swivel chair, wearing baggy gray sweat pants and a Donovan McNabb jersey, the piece of paper is situated on a desk, of which the chair is pulled up to. He scribes things on then piece of paper, thinking at some points, but mostly writing. His hair is darker than normal. Famine comes upon his belly, which forces him to rise from the chair and get something to eat. We see the piece of paper. Ritchie Syxx’s Enemies List 10 Fred Durst This bastard is the biggest sell out I have ever heard of. He rises from the underground, could be one of the biggest stars around with a great album in 3 dollar bill y’all, and then he sells out. Not only that, he sold out all the bands he brought into the buisness, and it makes me sick. If I saw him at the supermarket or something, I’d kick his ass. 9 Strom Thurman He was the only human born in the PreCambrian era, I guess that’s a feat. Get his old ass out of Senate, make him and Ted Kennedy play backgammon for days on end instead of falling asleep at meetings. 8 Women’s Clothes Who’s the jackass that let Women wear clothes? They should just walk around naked. 7 The French Language Je deteste français!!! Français est stupide!! I can’t believe I had to take this in high school, it was horrible. Everything has to be all perfect and all, stupid Europeans. 6 Skitzo The first person I had to fight in PWA, in a Hell in a Cell Match, he killed me, he really did. But his stupid antics are what got under my skin within the weeks after that, and now I’d like to see his bloodly body on a stick, being paraded through the streets of a Latin American country. Last I saw him, he was in PCW. Someone told me that he went to Germany, maybe to meet some “Hessians”? 5 Richard Nixon This guy was a true whacko, he’d parade around in strange haircuts, stupid gestures and catchphrases, and he even had an enemies list!!!!!! How pathetic! 4 The Hamburgular For over 45 years this man has reeked havok amongst the children of the world. Stealing, or hurtig their hamburgers. I once ordered a hamburger with no Onions, Pickles or Mayo, it came back with piles of these on it. By then, I knew he was back there, just trying to destroy my hamburger, and destroy it he did. I vow to make a vigilante group to hunt him down, and castrate the hamburgular. The little hamburgular will be no more! Furthermore, it has come to my attention that the Hamburgular is well known throughout police precincts, and his face is well known, yet he has yet to be arrested. I estimate his total hamburgers stolen at 34,518,245. This bastard will pay. 3 Semore Smoke For some reason, he thinks he was the greatest PWA’er in the summer of 2001. It was most definately me, if PWA was said in a word association test, my name would be said. I’d like to face him at the Inter-PPV. 2 Richard Sixpence The old me, the dork that I cannot stand. I remember when I had to run home in 7th grade when the kids chased me home, in hindsight, calling the toughest kid’s mom a fat whore wasn’t very fruitful. But hey, it toughed me up very much so I could become the man that I am now. 1 Corg I had nothing against the guy in PWA, or NTICW. I even talked to him a few times at the vending machines, he didn’t seem as bad as they said. But when I arrived in OSW his true form came into my view. He is a sick, cold hearted son of a bitch, and I will kill him. And I do not mean this as a joke, o piece of papyrus, I will destroy all of Corg’s hopes, dreams......and organs. He shall be no more. He wants to say that I am a jackass? He wants to say that I am a fool? He will see, when I kill him. HE wants to interfere in a match of mine that doesn’t even involve him in any way? He’s just praying to be killed with that one. I have one goal at Crowning Glory, not to win a title of any kind, but to destory him. I will bathe in his blood the night after Crowning Glory. Ritchie returns with a Capri Sun juice packet, he sips it, looking at his list. Hate fills his eyes as we fade to black, but not just any black, a black brought to you by the good people at Capri Sun!
