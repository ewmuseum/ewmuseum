Profound Silence

15 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-15-02 “What does he think he is doing???” “I know what he’s doing, Ivan, he’s trying to derail our war machine.” The camera fades up on Ivan Stanislav and Alexei Ruslan, who both stand outside the entrance gate to the Arkhangelsk Military Base. It appears to be midday at Arkhangelsk, with a deep gray sky above the two Russian’s heads, occasionally littered with dark clouds that slowly spit snow over the rugged Russian landscape. Stanislav and Ruslan both wear heavy brown trench coats and hats, as well as gloves and boots. The two face one another as we cut into their conversation… “He thinks he is doing something to make the fans have more support for him, but all he is doing is halting the inevitable, Praporschik, that is all,” mutters Ruslan, “He is wrestler, not commissioner, he does not know what he is doing!” Ruslan nods and starts to walk along the perimeter of the fence. Stanislav walks beside him as the crunching of the snow underneath their boots serves as the only real sound between the two for a few moments. “It is just such waste, Alexei Gregorovich!” Exclaims Ivan with a general look of disgust on his face, “It is his fault for creating this problem! What a stupid, stupid fool!” Ivan kicks at the snow in front of him, sending some flitting into the electric fence and zapping it into nothing. Alexei shrugs and pulls his hands from his pockets. “So you must fight alongside Meanstreak! So what! We will install Speedy as referee, and he will not participate in wrestling match! You will destroy Azrael Cain and Hessian, and then when you win, dispose of Meanstreak as well!” He smiles over at Ivan, “It is not that hard, Ivan. Besides, I doubt those two fools you must fight against are even prepared for type of fighter that you are!” Ivan stops and puts his hands on his hips, “What is it that possesses individuals to think like they do?” Alexei shrugs while Ivan continues, “To think that they have parents somewhere in the world! Azrael Cain and Hessian are nothing more that children in grown up world! Believing there is some sort of great power possessing them to do the things that they do…” Alexei shakes his head, “When they are only crazy in their little, feeble minds.” Ivan nods and chuckles. White vapor exits his mouth, proof of the cold in the Russian area that the two men are in. “And then, when Heresian Bloodlust falls to the might of Russian Bear, and Meanstreak views the decimation that I have created before him, he will cower in fear. And then, I will personally lay him to waste so that he never stands against the might of Ivan Stanislav.” Alexei nods his head again and looks up at the sky, “And then a large factor in our war will have been finished.” “You are correct,” interjects Ivan, “and then Ed will realize that when he believed he was stopping Ivan Stanislav, he was only spurring our war machine to bigger and better things.” The two share a moment of thought, not looking at one another but walking side by side. Ivan stops and turns to a range of trees in the distance. The two savor the moment, watching a deer suddenly emerge from the trees and stare at them, only to retreat back into the woods a moment later. The wind howls in the background…. “Do you think she believed you, Praporschik?” Mutters Ruslan, not sure if this is the appropriate time to bring up such a subject. Another long pause. “I believe she does, Alexei. I believe she does,” Ivan pauses, “and I hope that she does.” Alexei looks over at Ivan and smiles, “I am glad, Praporschik.” Ivan nods slowly, “Yes, I am too, Alexei.” Ivan shrugs and looks at Ruslan. “Come, let us head back to base, eh?” Alexei nods as Ivan turns and walks away. Seemingly, the two men have exchanged a tremendous amount of words in such little phrases. He watches Ivan walk away and then nods to himself, hurrying to catch up to his much larger friend. The deer exits the woods again and watches…as the camera slowly fades away.
