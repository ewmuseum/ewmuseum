Rebirth of slick...

18 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/18/02 The recliner stretched back, the room sat silent and dark, his eyes stared at nothing... Minutes ticked by like time lapsed photography. How many days has he sat here, not moving? He has no clue, he doesn't care. He remembers sitting here through Slam! on Saturday. Hell, he doesn't even know where they were last week. Motivation? What the hell is that? Desire? Heh, doesn't ring a bell. Determination? Not familiar. Joy? Happiness? Not speakin' my language. His mindless babble of thought is interrupted by a phone ringing. An unfamiliar distraction as of late, but something tells him to answer it. 'Hello' mumbles Frankie. 'Bones, get your shit together, you have a match Saturday' barks the voice on the other end. 'Whatever, against who?' asks Bones, oozing with disinterest. 'Sebastian Toomes. Now get it together Frank, you're on thin ice.' says the disgruntled voice right before hanging up. Frankie sits there, phone in hand, eyes looking at distant objects yet determined. And then, like a shotgun blast into the chest of a young child's parents, it all came back to him... Motivation... Desire... Determination... Hell, even Joy and Happiness. It was all back. With a vigor he was unfamiliar with. Toomes, the man who used Frankie. Who used him to watch his back while he got his gold and cast him aside. The man who promised a quick rise to the top, why had Frankie been so foolish? Nevertheless, Frankie was about to have his chance. His chance at revenge. In Frankie's eyes, Revenge is a virtue, one which he has full grasp on. So it's set... It's on... It's time... Revenge. rebirth...
