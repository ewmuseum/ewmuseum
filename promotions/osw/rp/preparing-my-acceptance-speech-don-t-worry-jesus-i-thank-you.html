Preparing my acceptance speech. Don't worry Jesus, I thank you

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Late Thursday night-early Friday morning Ever since I signed with on the dotted line and sold my soul to OSW, I’ve done nothing but find myself in trouble. Doesn’t matter what I do or say. Anything involving me causes trouble in some way. It really needs to stop. The only trouble I wanna cause is trouble in Kimbusa’s life. He has done nothing but give me problems. I wanna do the same to him. Today, I’ve decided to assure myself of not getting into any trouble. Just like the 1985 Chicago Bears, I’m preparing for my ultimate victory. But mine will be in the form of an acceptance speech rather than a top 40 hit song. Sure, I might not have won yet, but does that matter? Better to be safe than sorry. Better go get my pen and pad. Gotta write this down so I remember it all. First and foremost, I’d like to thank Jesus, my lord and savior. Without him looking down on me and supporting me, I might never have been able to return to greatness. I’d also like to praise Allah, worship Satan, and thank L. Ron Hubbard to show that I’m an equal opportunity supporter. Mom and dad. Instead of bitching at me, you let me go through with this wrestling thing. And like I thought, it paid off in the long run. Amanda, the love of my life. You don’t have any idea how much your love and support is appreciated. I love you. “Who else is there,” I mumble to myself. Thanks to Moose Malone for not putting a leash on me. And thanks to Shanda Russell. Since all the other higher officials are dicksmokes, you get thanked by default. I wanna thank everyone who had the honor of being pinned by me. St. Crux, Vendetta, Mrs. Stormbringer, it was a joy beating you all senseless in order to reclaim my throne. Speaking of Stormbringer, I’d like to thank Amy’s inbred lover, Bobby, for being the ultimate lackey. Keep kissing ass and you might get somewhere. An extra special thank you to Duke Williams and Tyler Nelson. We all already knew, but you guys running PCW into the ground proved once and for all that I made PCW and it will never survive without me. “I hate thanking people,” I mutter quietly. “I always forget someone.” Shout out to all my peeps in Vanillaland. Jerry, Ice, Speevey, and the rest of the clan. Y’all kick ass. I know you know it, but I figured I’d say it again. Mr. Shazbot and the people with Shazbot. We might’ve had our differences lately, but I’m still grateful for the job you gave me and the chance to shill your product to all my fans. Thank you. Anyone else I forgot, I apologize. So many worshippers, it’s hard to name them all. But to all of you…thank you. “Raise my prize, look happy, and leave,” I think to myself. “This won’t be so hard.” Now that wasn’t so hard. Thanked enough people so I sound appreciative, but didn’t thank so many that it becomes overkill. “I should really do this more often,” I say to myself. Now I need a safe place to put my speech. Don’t wanna lose it. In a few weeks, after I win my first OSW Cruiserweight title, it’ll really come in handy. “Hehehe.” I quietly laugh to myself. “OSW gold, here I come.”
