The riot's finish

23 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 23, 2002 Reno’s mentor and trainer, Kyle Noiles was here. This protest, this riot was getting terribly violent as even police officers were going down from the random bullet or Molotov cocktail. Mikey was bleeding; Frank was trying desperately to stop the blood flow. And here was Reno Davis, having his first run in with his former mentor in almost 4 years. It couldn’t be him, there must be more than one Kyle Noiles in the world, but the mask he wore was definitely that of ‘Twisted’ Kyle Noiles’. Davis had forgotten completely about Noiles after their match in POW that decided who’d stay in and who’d leave. Reno was upset after he defeated Kyle, but once fWo Survivor came about, Reno had blocked Kyle from memory. Unfortunately, it seemed that Kyle...or Kasp, hadn’t. A protestor stepped up onto the upside down trashcan and began to holler and scream something about freedom and peace again, but a familiar figure slammed into the garbage can, sending the protestor flying onto his face. “AIMZ!” Reno screamed, noticing that the woman who knocked the can over was she. Why was she here? He had told her specifically not to be in Washington, “Dammit,” was all Reno could spurt out as he rushed towards her. Amy’s body neared as Reno ran closer, but within five feet from her, Kyle pranced in front of her. “Hello again Reno. How’s it going?” He asked. “Kyle?” “OH!” He clapped his hands in joy, “The big superstar remembers us! I’m so delighted!” Kyle yelled. “Us?” Reno was confused in Kyle’s mode of speaking. Kyle smiled and looked into Reno’s blue eyes. Noiles’ emerald green eyes seemed to shift like a kaleidoscope for a moment and Reno had to blink to notice that the green eyes had turned to a radioactive yellow. “What in the hell?” The Unwanted Angel stepped back in shock. The man in front of him merely rolled his yellow eyes. “Like you are one to talk about weird eyes, Reno.” Kyle said, his tone strangely more high pitched than before. “Kyle...whatever’s wrong...” Reno was interrupted. “Not Kyle anymore. Say hello to Kasp.” He smiled, much to Reno’s confusion. “Listen,” Reno began. “No! You listen freak! You took everything Kyle taught you and then forgot him when you got somewhere. Do you know what it’s like to listen to someone bitch about the same thing for 4 years? It’s not exactly a blowjob, Reno” Kasp pulled out his pair of scissors, “...And it’s high time I gave Kyle a reason to shut up.” “Uh oh?” Reno shot out quickly as Kasp charged at him, thrusting the scissors at Reno’s throat. He narrowly dodged the sharp point of the clippers and kicked Kasp in the ass as he flew by. Spinning around, Kasp chucked the scissors at Reno, hitting him in the cheek. Blood began to flow from his cut, which Reno couldn’t even inspect as Kasp kicked him in the stomach and began to slam his knee into Reno’s face repeatedly. After a few strikes, Reno grabbed a knee and flipped Kasp on his back, then backed away to take a breath. This guy was serious, and the whole situation hadn’t quite clarified in Reno’s mind yet. Reno looked at Aimz, who was still not moving, dried blood on her face. The punch to the face caught Reno off-guard, as a rioter decided that beating on Reno would be fun. Davis thought differently and kicked his man in the shin, then slammed a knee into the man’s neck, sending him to the ground gagging. Five tear gas canisters shot out from the launchers and Reno’s eyes grew large at this sight. Kasp didn’t notice, as he speared Reno into the ground and began to punch wildly at his face. He was going to kill Reno fight here and now, or he was at least until Mikey Thomas hadn’t intervened by swiping his forearm into the side of Kasp’s head. The 5’10, 180 lbs Kasp flew off of Reno, rolling and tumbling away for a few feet before stopping in a heap. He began to recover immediately, which Frank Swallows noted, grabbing Aimz and taking off, with Mikey grabbing the bruised Davis and following Frank. The canisters’ gas began to spread as the rioters’ bandanas didn’t do as good a job as they were supposed to and they fell to the ground choking. The police slipped gas masks on and began to arrest the teenage protestors. Frank, Reno, Mikey and Aimz all made it to the police barricade where they had to explain that they were caught in the riot. The police officer recognized Reno and Aimz, and let them slide through with an autograph from Reno, since Aimz was in no condition to piss, let alone write. Kasp watched as they slipped through the barrier, and he began to follow them, but his autograph for the officer would come in the form of a sharp object...
