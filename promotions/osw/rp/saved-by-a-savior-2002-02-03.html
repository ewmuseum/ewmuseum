Saved by a Savior

3 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-3-02 (OOC: I wanted to post this yesterday, but circumstances prevented it. It really has nothing to do with my match next week, but I'm posting this anyway, if for no other reason then to provide closure to my mini-storyline. Just thought I'd make that clear. *Thumbs up*) When we last left Civil Disobedience they were in quite the sticky situation. By sticky I mean they’re about to be torn to pieces by an angry Christian mob. But then, aren’t all Christian mobs angry? Ha ha ha. Seriously though, their current state of affairs is quite serious. Seriously! They’re presently pinned in the center of the church foyer, along with Paul the Jew, as the advancing hordes close on in them from all sides. The crowd itself is divided into two factions: those that are pissed off at Seth and Paul for trying to steal the mystical, magical, mysterious, miraculous holy water and those that are ready to kill Trevor for holding hands with one of their attractive young parishioners. Geez, if they go crazy over holding hands, Trevor should be glad he didn’t decide to cop a feel. At any rate, it’s funny how it doesn’t take much to rouse a mob’s anger, yet it’s so much harder to quell that same anger. Well it’s funny to me, at least. I’m sure Seth and Trevor find no humor in knowing that fact, considering quelling the rabid masses is their only way out of this mess. Either that or cutting a swath of destruction with their laser vision. Of course, that’s not really an option, as it was just something stupid I made up. They won’t be getting laser vision until at least a few weeks from now. That’s enough with the spoilers, though, as this scene will just pass us up if we don’t start paying attention. Look, something is happening!!!!!!!! One of the children in the crowd picks up a rock and chucks it at Seth, bouncing it off his head. Seth screams out in pain before flicking off the kid. Seeing this sign of vulgarity, the crowd gasps in shock, falling back a few steps in disgust. This reprieve doesn’t last very long, though, as they quickly regain their courage and advance once more. Another middle finger care of Seth sends them back once more, but again it doesn’t last. Again and again Seth shoots the bird, but it never drives them totally back. A look of hope comes over Trevor’s face as he sees this. “Ha ha, it’s working. Don’t stop, Seth. With all the working out you’ve done with your hand, I’ll bet you can keep it up all day. I knew all that masturbating would pay off for you some day!” Paul the Jew shakes his head sadly, causing Trevor’s expression to fade. “No, the profane gesture repels them slightly, but how long could it possibly keep working before it loses its effect? My guess is, not long.” Indeed, the mob already seems to be developing a tolerance to it, as each gesture drives them away an increasingly shorter distance. Soon it barely effects them at all and a sort of bloodlust flows through the crowd, as they can smell their impending victory over the trio. The baseball bat wielding, chain swinging throngs begin to shout among each other, trying to decide what to do with the heathens they’ve now cornered. “We’ve got to kill them!” barks one man near the front, getting roars of approval all around. “Yes, let’s stone them to death!” yells another man off to the right, getting an equally great ovation for his idea. “No, let’s feed them to the lions!” chimes in a woman on the right. This sparks a great debate in the crowd. Half the people seem to want to support the woman’s idea about the lions, while the other half want to stone them to death like the man suggested. Personally I don’t see what the difference would be. A public execution is a public execution no matter how you slice it. But, then again, I’ve never had MY holy water stolen, so who am I to judge. As chants of ‘LIONS’ and ‘STONES’ fly across the room, the three men whose fates are hanging in the balance exchange uneasy looks in the center of the room. Suddenly the argument is stopped by the old crone who defrauded Trevor earlier. Climbing up on one of the pews, she screeches out her idea. “Forget them lions and forget them stones. What we needs is an old-fashioned crucy-fixtion!” The crowd ponders this suggestion for a moment, causing complete silence to fall over the room. Then, all at once, thunderous applause deafens everything, as they express unanimous approval for the proposal. You can see Seth and Trevor swallowing deeply in fear; an expression which grows greater as the mob advances on them for the umpteenth time. Seth’s eyes stare wide open, as sweat streams down his face. He flips the bird, but...no effect! They come closer. He starts to shake uncontrollably, before breaking down entirely. “Damn it, you stupid fucks! You can’t kill us! Just back the fuck off! We didn’t even do anything!” Seth screams, closing his eyes and holding his head in agony. The crowd pays no attention to his cries and draws still closer. They’re now only a few feet away from our trio. Now it’s Paul the Jew’s turn to try to stop them. “You can’t do this,” he shouts. “Your bible forbids murder. ‘Thou shall not kill’ and all that jazz. You wouldn’t want to disobey the bible, would you?” “That didn’t stop your people from killing Jesus, now did it, you hypocrite!” shouts an uppity teenaged girl off to the side as she sticks out her palm, suggesting that she wishes Paul to talk to the hand. Paul sighs in defeat as the crowd pulls within arms reach of the three men. Seth and Paul appear to have given up, but Trevor still has hope. He tries a last ditch effort. “Hold on, hold on! Before you kill us at least listen to what I have to say!” screams Trevor at the top of his lungs. Surprisingly the crowd stops coming forward as the people making it up all stand with their arms crossed, tapping their feet impatiently. “Alright, but make it quick. We’ve still got a raffle drawing to get to tonight,” states a woman near the front, as she gives Trevor a questioning look. Trevor stands up and dusts himself off as he clears his throat. After a few seconds of this, he proceeds on with his proposal. “Hear me, fine Christians. We mean you no harm. In fact, we mean you no good either. Basically we mean you nothing. Well maybe that Jewish dude has some issues to settle with you, but myself and my friend Seth are not the bad guys here. There are no bad guys. This is a complete misunderstanding. The only reason Seth and I are here is because there was nothing else to do to celebrate our acceptance onto the roster of Old School Wrestling. We have not come here to undermine your religion, steal your secrets, or...heaven forbid...deflower your women. No, we are just here as indifferent observers. For you to take out your anger on us would be a travesty and against everything you stand for. So please, take mercy on us. Let us go, alive, and we can promise you that we will never interfere in the matters of the church again. Thank you.” As Trevor stops talking, he relaxes his posture, turning to Seth and Paul with a cocky smile on his face. “That was beautiful, man,” Seth says, as a tear streams down his cheek. Unfortunately, the crowd doesn’t seem as touched by the speech as Seth. Another rock flies from the mass, again nailing Seth in the head. Seth falls to a knee, grasping his skull in pain as he screams at the small child who threw the stone. “Why didn’t you throw the fucking rock at Trevor? He’s the one that gave the speech, not me.” The child shrugs as the mob begins to get rowdy again. They surround the three men, raining blows down upon them before dragging them out of the room. They pull them out the large entranceway in the front of the church and throw them in the middle of the empty green field that has been the site of the day’s picnic. The tents from earlier today are still standing, but the people are quick at work taking down the ones in the vicinity. As soon as the tents are gone, they are quickly replaced with what appears to be makeshift crosses; three of them, lined up in a row facing the church. It appears the end is near for our protagonists, as they are moments away from being nailed to the boards like...nails to some boards. No, it wasn’t looking good at the start of the segment, and it’s looking far worse right now. With the crosses erected, the crowd grabs Seth and drags him over to the cross on the far left. He punches, kicks, and flails like a little girl as they do so, but he has basically no effect at all, as they stand him up in position in front of the cross despite his pathetic attempts to get free. A man walks up to the cross with a huge hammer, reaches into his pocket and pulls out a huge nail. He holds it over Seth’s hand and reaches the mallet back to drive the nail into Masters’ palm and...he swings! “STOP!” yells a voice from the crowd. The man stops in mid-swing, causing Seth to sigh in relief, and turns around to see what the big commotion is all about. The rest of the mob follows suit, as we see a path being cut through the crowd as the people gasp and part on both sides, though nobody can be seen coming through. Eventually, the crowd parts near the front and out from them walks none other than the man, the myth, the legend....Jesus Christ!!! “OH MY GOD!!!!” the crowd shouts excitedly, in their best Joey Styles impersonation. Oddly though, Jesus isn’t looking quite as you’d expect him to. Sure he’s got the flowing white robe, the beard, and the flowing brown hair, but there’s something odd about him. Like he’s kind of short...about five foot two. And he’s pale. Really, really pale. In fact, if you look close enough, you’ll notice his face is covered in acne. Somehow nobody seems to pick up on these abnormalities as Jesus walks up to the cross where Seth is currently positioned. He lays a hand on the shoulder of the man with the hammer, who quickly bows to him and backs away. Seth stares incredulously at the spectacle unfolding before him, not believing his eyes. Even more surprised is Paul the Jew, who has now taken his yamica off and thrown it on the ground, before getting on his knees and praying in the direction of Jesus. The crowd is abuzz at the sight of their Savior come back to them, but is silenced with one raise of the man’s hand. There is absolute quiet as the Christ man speaks. “My children...what are you thinking? How can you have strayed from my teachings so far? Did not I teach you to love your neighbors as you love yourself? If this is how you follow my words, then I would think you all must be self-loathers. What have these young men done today? Certainly nothing to deserve crucifixion at your hands. A slap on the wrist, maybe...even a kick in the pants, but definitely not crucifixion. These boys have not done anything that warrants such a punishment. In fact, with the fear you have instilled in them on this day I think that they should be let go. They have been punished enough,” says Jesus, in a sort of high pitched voice. The crowd stares dumfounded at him. Some of the people are staring at the ground, shuffling their feet guiltily. All in all, nobody moves. “I think you’re free to go,” addresses Jesus to our trio. Seth falls to his knees, kissing the ground before Trevor walks over to him and pulls him to his feet. As the two stand up, Seth gives a spiteful look to the crowd. “Man, you fucking fools. Trevor said basically the same shit earlier and you just ignored him. Then this Jesus dude comes along and you’re all like ‘Yes master, whatever you say! Can I stick my nose up your ass crack any further?’ You are some fickle ass-kissing motherfuckers, you know that? Cause you are!” spews Seth, angrily. The crowd, hearing this, gets rather upset and begins advancing upon them again, but a raised hand from Jesus stops them, wiping the angry looks off their faces in the process. Paul the Jew joins the team of Civil Disobedience and together with Jesus they begin walking off the field together. After they take about twenty steps, Jesus turns around and addresses the crowd again. “I really am ashamed of you today, my children. Crucifixion? Have any of you people ever been crucified? Seeing that you are all still alive, I highly doubt it. Let me just tell you it sucks balls. Really, it does. Monkey balls!” he says, shaking his head in disgust. The sideways motions of his head causes one side of his beard to come undone and swing off, causing it to hang from the other side of his face before dropping off entirely. It is now obvious that it hasn’t been Jesus at all, but was really MELVIN all along! (OOC Note: For all of you out there who haven’t been following Civil Disobedience from the beginning...which I’m assuming is all of you...Melvin is a reoccurring character in their promos. He’s the local town nerd: thick thick glasses, acne, stupid haircut, you know the drill. Melvin talks to Seth sometimes at the comic store where he works, but he doesn’t like Trevor at all. That should be sufficient recap for anyone who has ignored us up to this point.) “Christ, it’s Melvin!” shouts Trevor. “Shouldn’t you be saying Christ IS Melvin?” says Seth in reply. Seeing that Jesus was in fact an imposter, Paul the Jew runs back through the stunned crowd, picks up his yamica, plants a kiss on it before sticking it back on his head, then runs back through the still-stunned crowd to join Seth, Trevor, and Melvin. As previously mentioned the crowd is stunned. They are also shocked, surprised, staggered, and speechless...for a few seconds at least. After that, they begin to get rowdy again, setting off to catch the quartet that so thoroughly duped them. “I think this is the part where we run,” says Paul the Jew, before turning around and fleeing as fast as his legs can carry him. “I don’t know, Paul. I think we’re supposed to wait a couple more seconds,” Seth calls after him, before turning to see that Trevor and Melvin have already gotten thirty feet away from him. He shrugs his shoulders nonchalantly and begins to race after them as well, dodging thrown projectiles coming from the mob along the way. About an hour later, Seth, Trevor, and Melvin are walking down Barker Road, the street housing the residence of Civil Disobedience. They look tired, but relieved none the less, as they finally stop on the doorstep of Seth and Trevor’s house. The three men collapse sprawled out in separate heaps as they laugh deliriously at their day’s experience. “Man, that must of been the craziest shit that ever happened to me,” says Trevor with a smile. “I have a question, though. Why did you risk your life to save us, Melvin? That seems totally out of character for you.” “Don’t get any ideas that I’m a friendly guy,” states Melvin with a sneer directed toward Trevor. “The only reason I did it was because without Seth, the comic store would lose sixty percent of our business.” “Ah, makes sense,” replies Trevor. “I can’t believe the people actually thought an little shit like you would actually be Jesus, though. I mean, the height, the acne, the voice...you should of called yourself Dweebus.” Seth finds this extremely funny and starts to crack up laughing. “Fine assholes, laugh it up. We’ll see if I’m around to save your ass next time,” says Melvin with a sigh. Seth and Trevor finish laughing and everyone has a moment of silent contemplation. Melvin thinks about how much he hates Trevor, Seth thinks about...boobs, while Trevor goes over the day’s events again. “We are so going to go to hell,” he says, jokingly. “Trevor...” Seth says calmly. “We’ve already graduated from the PWA. We’ve been through hell and back already. What’s the worst thing that can happen to us after that?” The two share a big laugh over that, while Melvin continues to sulk. The scene fades to black as we close this chapter in the lives of Civil Disobedience with that question. What is the worst thing that can happen to them? If you tuned in to the first Slam you’d probably answer: they’d have to face Hessian and Azrael Cain on a weekly basis. Were that match and the matches to come divine retribution from some power above? Possibly. Possibly...
