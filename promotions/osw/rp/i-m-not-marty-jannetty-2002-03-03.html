I'm Not Marty Jannetty!!!

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-3-02 Our scene opens up backstage at the Parker Field House in Richmond, Virginia, where we are currently met with Trevor Hawke walking down the hallway. It’s a pretty normal looking walk, which is a nice derivation from all of the previous weeks post-Slam promos. He’s not limping or bleeding all over the place or hanging his head in disappointment. He’s just walking; walking like any normal man would walk. Actually, now that I look at him, he’s kind of doing more than just walking. Ha, I think what Trevor Hawke is really doing is strutting down the hallway. He slicks his hair back in a cocky manner as he steps out of the corridor into a large room. Some OSW production personnel are seen, looking to be busy running errands or whatnot as the crew is in the process of pulling out of the arena for the night. As Trevor sweeps his gaze across the room he notices that his tag team partner, Seth Masters, is currently surrounded by a group of about twenty five people. This time, however, it isn’t a bunch of small children. Surprisingly, it appears to be some actual OSW fans, who are busy mobbing Seth for his autograph. To say the least, Trevor looks somewhat shocked to see this unlikely event unfolding before him, but he manages to regain his senses without too much trouble, shrugging and walking over to the crowd of people. Trevor comes up near the back of the group, and sets out to trying to find an opening in the mob where he could weasel his way through to the center. He’s not having too much luck in doing so when he hears Seth’s voice emanate from the middle of the crowd. Trevor sighs to himself, thinking that the crowd will soon dissipate once they hear what a moron Seth is, and also wondering what kind of nonsense his partner has decided to spew forth this time. He’s surprised once more, however, when the people start laughing in hysterics and clapping their hands appreciatively. It is amazing, for these people actually seem to be enjoying Seth’s company. Shaking his head, Trevor decides to push his way through. He gets a few muttered swears thrown his way from the people he had to shove aside, but in a few seconds he breaks through to the middle, where he sees Seth currently in the middle of another one of his stupid anecdotes. “And then I says to the Pope, I says... ‘Yo Pope! Can I borrow your bicycle?’...and he says to me... ‘My bicycle?! I don’t gots no bicycle. I’m Italian, my boy!’” Seth says in a stupid Italian accent, pausing as the crowd laughs in approval...and Trevor frowns in disapproval. He meets Trevor’s gaze with a cocky grin, then continues. “So we had a nice laugh about that, then went out for some mostacholi. And that’s the story of when I became the vice president of the Roman Catholic church.” The people begin to happily clap once more as Seth finishes up, but Trevor quickly walks in front of his partner and tries to quell the crowd. The mob looks confused at this new development, with this new man standing before them telling them to quiet down. They do so, but they look rather unhappy at having to do so. Most of them cross their arms in front of them impatiently as they wait for Trevor to speak. He shakes his head at Seth in disbelief, before turning his attention back to the people, who seem to be shaking their heads at HIM in disbelief. Trevor sighs, then begins to address both the crowd and Seth. “Why are you clapping for this idiot? He’s obviously lying! He’s never met the pope, and there is definitely no such position as vice president of the Roman Catholic church. Stop encouraging him!” Trevor shouts, before smacking Seth in the back of the head. The crowd is silent upon hearing this, but they don’t look happy. A man off to the side breaks the silence with an angry shout. “Why the hell should we listen to you?” he yells, pointing angrily at Trevor. “On that topic, just who the fuck are you, anyway?” “What?!” Trevor utters in disbelief. “You just saw me wrestle less than an hour ago. I’m Trevor Hawke!” The crowd begins to mutter among themselves in confusion at this information. You can catch bits and pieces of what they’re saying. Such statements as ‘Trevor who?’ and ‘What match was this guy in?’ are distinct amongst the whispers. Trevor is beginning to get irritated, as some color appears in his cheeks. “I’m one half of Civil Disobedience!” he shouts, causing the crowd to mutter amongst itself in a confused fashion again. Trevor looks pissed off now, the swagger he had earlier has now completely disappeared. “Damn it! How can you people know Seth but have no fucking clue who I am?!” “You can’t expect us to recognize every nobody on the street, bub!” shouts a lady near the back, causing Trevor’s face to flush an even deeper shade of read. He looks as if he’s about to explode when Seth steps forward and places a comforting hand on his shoulder. “Relax people,” Seth says as he addresses the crowd. “He’s not as big of a loser as he looks. He’s a little cranky today...I think he must have eaten some bad meat or something. Everyone, this is Trev, my tag team partner.” “Hi Trev!” the crowd shouts happily, immediately losing their unfriendly expressions upon hearing Seth’s explanation. The same cannot be said of Trevor Hawke however, as he still has a surly look painted across his face. “Seth, can I have a word with you?” Trevor asks through clenched teeth. Seth nods his head nonchalantly, holding up a finger telling his partner it’ll be just a minute. “Hey everyone,” Seth says, addressing the crowd once more. “I’m going to have a quick conference with Trev right here and I’ll be right back to you. Ok?” “Ok!” the crowd shouts back. They start chattering amongst themselves once more, as Seth turns back to Trevor. Placing an arm around his shoulder, he walks him a few steps away from the crowd and pulls him down into a huddle. “What’s up, bro?” Seth whispers, before Trevor throws his partner’s arm off of him and stands up. “What’s up?! You’re asking me what’s up?” Trevor says angrily. “If anything, I’m the one who needs to know what is up. What the hell’s with the crowd, Seth? Since when did anyone ever want to talk to your stupid ass?” “Ever since I became famous, that’s when!” Seth responds with a laugh. “When you’re famous it doesn’t matter how stupid you are. The people love you just for being on TV.” “Well I’m on TV and those dumb shits don’t crowd around me,” Trevor responds. “Don’t give me that bullshit. What’s the real deal, man? “That is the real deal, Trev. I have no reason to lie,” Seth says defensively, placing his hands in the air and waving them in a sign of innocence. “Anyway, just because you’re on TV doesn’t mean you’re famous. Even Carrot Top has collect calling commercials.” “Who?” “Exactly my point,” Seth says with a nod. “I hate to break it to you, bro, but the fact of the matter is that I’m famous and you’re not.” “What the hell are you talking about?! You’re no more famous than I am!” Trevor shouts, sounding agitated at what his partner is saying. “Oh contraire, bonjour. I know you don’t want to believe it, but I am obviously much more famous than you are,” Seth says, as if it were the most obvious thing in the world. “Bullshit,” spits Trevor, shaking his head. “It’s true. Come on, think about it, man,” Seth says, tapping Trevor on the head. “For one, I’ve been much more successful than you. Not only did I beat Azrael Cain in singles competition, but I also took down Xavier Kannon in the Global Title Tournament. That match in itself has solidified me as the next big star in many people’s eyes.” “Again I say bullshit,” Trevor says, pointing his index finger into Seth’s chest. “I helped you beat Cain, and that GTT match was an upset in every sense of the word. You’re a fluke, my friend.” “Oh yeah, well how about when Ivan Stanislav Red Scared me from the ring to the announce table. Did you hear that fucking crowd chant? Shit, if nothing else made me famous, it was that. It’s what the business calls getting over, and that pushed me far enough over that now I’m on a completely other side from you,” Seth says cockily, sticking his chest out with pride and wagging his head. “Oh shut up, dude,” Trevor says, punching his partner lightly in the stomach to stop his arrogant show. “Just because Ivan kicked your ass doesn’t make you any more popular.” “Explain to me why that crowd is there, then?” Seth asks confidently. “I...uh...I don’t know,” Trevor says, struggling. “It’s not because you’re famous, though. I know that much.” “Suuuuuure it isn’t. People just crowd around unfamous people all the time. Face it, Trev. I’m famous.” Trevor doesn’t know what to say. He opens his mouth, but no words come out, and he has to shut it again with nothing uttered. A grinning Seth slaps his partner on the back in a comforting fashion. “Don’t lose hope, man. Maybe it’ll rub off on you if you spend enough time around me,” Seth says, then lets out a laugh. “I’ll be more than happy to let you carry my bags into the hotel from now on.” Trevor shoots an angry glare at Seth, pushing him away and taking a few steps in the opposite direction. “Hey, I was just joking, bro,” Seth calls after his partner. “I wouldn’t make you do anything worse than shine my shoes.” Trevor turns around and walks back to where Seth is standing. “It’s not fucking funny, man. We’re supposed to be a team and you’re acting like you’re better than me. Fuck that. I’m the intelligent one, here!” he says, jabbing a finger into his friend’s chest. Seth raises his arms innocently once more. “The people have spoken, Trev. I don’t know what to tell you. I guess every team has to have a Shawn Michaels and a Marty Jannetty. It’s just too bad that you’re Jannetty.” “I’m not Marty Jannetty!!!” Trevor shouts in dismay. “Fine, then I’m Booker T and you’re Stevie Ray,” Seth offers, raising an eyebrow. “That’s even worse!” cries Trevor, to which Seth just shrugs in response. “Eh. I don’t have all day to stand here and talk politics. As you can see, my fans are waiting,” Seth says with a flourish of his hand toward the crowd. “Go take a walk, Trev. I should be done shortly. We can continue our conversation later.” Seth turns his attention back to the crowd once more, who immediately cheer when they realize he’s about to speak. “Seth! Seth! Seth! SETH!” they shout, as Seth takes a bow. Trevor shakes his head once more, this time with much more anger and disbelief than he had when he first arrived. Storming his way back through the crowd, he quickly leaves the odd scene with the chanting crowd behind him as he wanders around the Parker Field House in a seething fury. “He’s not more famous than me, the little shit!” Trevor mutters to himself. “The moron is...he’s a moron! How could anyone like that guy! Just because he got his ass kicked by Ivan? No fucking way! It has to be a trick...it has to!” Trevor is concentrating on talking to himself so much that he doesn’t even notice where he’s walking. It’s no wonder that he almost walks into a girl that looks about fourteen years old. She’s about five foot two, with blond hair, and is dressed much more slutty than any fourteen year old girl ought to be dressed. Trevor stops himself from running her over just in time and begins to apologize. “Oh geez, I’m sorry kid. I didn’t even see you coming! I...” “Oh my God! It’s you!” she shouts with a big smile on her face, interrupting Trevor in mid-sentence. “Oh, oh...can I have your autograph? Pleeeeeease!” “Uh...sure, I guess,” Trevor tells her, as he takes a pad of paper and pen from her. He quickly jots down his name and hands it back to her, talking to himself again. “Heh. Not famous my ass. I’m an autograph signing fool. That’ll show Seth.” “What is this?” the girl asks in a confused tone after she reads the paper. She looks up at him with pleading eyes. “Who the heck is Trevor Havlo?” “Trevor Hawke,” he corrects. “It’s me. Duh.” “Quit messing around. You can’t fool me. I know you’re in the Backstreet Boys. I just love you guys’ music! Now sign me an autograph for real.” she says, handing the pen and paper back to him. “Grrrr...” Trevor growls, as he tosses the pen and paper back at her and storms off once more. We’re left with a shot of the girl looking after the leaving OSW superstar in shock, as our scene fades out.
