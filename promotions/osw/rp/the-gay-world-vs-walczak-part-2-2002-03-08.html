The Gay World vs. Walczak (Part 2)

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/8/02 Walczak is seen sleeping on his bed, but all of a sudden he is awakened by a loud noise. It sounds like a group of people shouting, but Walczak thinks that it is impossible seeing that it is only 6:30 in the morning. Walczak dismisses the noise as his head just playing a trick on him, and tries to get back to sleep, but minutes later Walczak is once again awakened and the noise this time is louder. Walczak gets up and he walks over to his front window. What he sees amazes him; it is nothing else than a group of gay protestors parading around his front yard. They carry signs that say, “Gay and Proud”, and than other ones that say, “Ramey and Smith for Presidential Office”, and there are still others that say “Ramey and Smith = Gay Gods”. Walczak makes his way hastily towards the door, but then realizes that he has only a pair of boxer shorts on, so he makes his way back to put a pair of pants on. Once he is certain that his sack is not visible he makes his way out the door. “What in the hell are you flamers doing here? You guys, or gays whatever you prefer, better get the hell off of my lawn before I call the cops, and have them come over here to beat your asses.” “We want them to beat our asses.” “You guys are bigger pervs than I thought you were. You know what, why don’t you guys just find a butt-buddy and make your way back home. If you do it I am willing to forget this ever happened.” “We get rammed every day of the week, this is the one time that we get to stand up for our gay rights.” “Well go stand up somewhere else, I want you to get off…wait that didn’t sound to good, I meant get out of here.” “Fuck you Walczak.” “Please no, I am not gay just get the hell away from here.” “We came prepared for this moment, and we finally have you in a position that we want. Men get set to fire, 1…2…3!!!!!” At the sound of the number three the men rear back and start to heave objects. At first Walczak cannot make out what they are, but as they get closer Walczak realizes that they are dildos!!!! Walczak tries to avoid as many as possible, but cannot avoid all of them and makes his way inside to avoid the onslaught. He shuts the door and catches his breath. “Damn! I can’t believe that just happened. That may have been the most disgusting thing that I have ever witnessed in my life.” Walczak looks outside of his window and notices that the men are leaving his lawn. He makes his way back to his bed, and as he climbs in the scene fades to black…
