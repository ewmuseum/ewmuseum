Missing: Two OSW Tag Team Titles

1 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-01-02 Our scene opens with a hot red Ferrari pulling into the driveway of Civil Disobedience’s Aurora, Illinois suburban residence. At its helm is an attractive woman wearing sunglasses, with blond hair cascading around her shoulders. That’s not too surprising. Lots of hot chicks drive nice cars. The surprising thing about all of this is that Trevor Hawke is seated in the passenger’s seat of the Ferrari. The woman leans over, smiling seductively at Trevor, before kissing him deeply. She pulls away, smiling again, as she waves her fingers at him. Trevor waves back before opening the door on his side and stepping out of the car. He reaches into the backseat and pulls out a duffel bag, which he sets on the ground, then returns his gaze to the woman. The two maintain eye contact until she puts the Ferrari into reverse, pulls out of the driveway, and disappears down the road. Gazing after the now departed babe Trevor grins, nodding his head approvingly. “I sure am glad I was blessed with these dashing good looks,” he says to himself, running his hand through his celebrated head of hair. “Otherwise it would be really hard to get some action.” Trevor yawns, stretching his arms out in the air. From the looks of the bags under his eyes he’s had a loooong night, though if he spent it with that blond like I think he did, it was well worth it. Trev turns, slinging his duffel bag over his shoulder, then makes his way to the front door of the house. Turning the doorknob, he pushes inward...and is met with the screaming voice of Seth Masters. “Where the fuck is it!” wails the second half of Civil Disobedience. From the sound of it, he is off in his room to the right. His distance away doesn’t affect the tone of sheer despair that is carried by his voice. It seems as if something has really distraught him. Trevor sighs deeply. He had been hoping he could get a few hours of rest and relaxation, but from the looks of things the odds of that happening are slim to none. Eh, personally I think that anyone who comes into a place containing Seth Masters expecting to relax is setting up a prime example of a conflict of interests. Trevor sighs again as another frustrated scream echoes from Seth’s room, before walking straight ahead into the living room. Plopping down on the ratty blue couch, Trevor places a pillow over his ears to drown out the yelling, shutting his eyes tightly in hopes he may be able to fade off to sleep before being discovered. Luck is not on his side however, as it is only seconds before Seth runs into the room, racing over to the couch and shaking Trevor vehemently. “You don’t have yours either! Where the fuck are they, Trev?!” Masters shouts, before disappearing into the TV room off the right. Sounds of frantic digging are heard from the direction Seth disappeared into. Trevor sits in silent contemplation for a while, then decides that he can’t hold back his curious urges anymore. Though more than likely, this whole escapade is another creation of his partner’s stupidity, he might as well find out just what the hell the problem is. Standing up, he walks off to the right in the same direction his partner took. As he enters the room, he can see Seth at work digging through boxes of various crap that have been stored in the room. Seth looks as worried as a straight man under assault by the Ream Team as he searches through the containers, sweat dripping from his brow. “Seth...what are you looking for?” Trevor asks, crossing his arms as he regards his partner skeptically. Seth doesn’t answer, suggesting he either didn’t hear his partner’s question, he’s too busy to respond...or some third option that differs from either of the first two. “Seth...what are you looking for?” Trevor asks again, this time a bit louder. “It’s not those damn Rock ‘Em Sock ‘Em Robots again, is it?” Seth looks up from the box he is currently digging in, glaring at his partner. He ignores Trevor’s question and opts to interrogate him instead. “Just where the hell were YOU last night?” “Eh,” Trevor shrugs. “I don’t deal with losing easily, so I decided to let off some steam with this hot chick I met outside the arena. Damn, I love slutty groupies!” “We have slutty groupies?” Seth asks, confused. “Since when?” “Since always, I guess. The OSW doesn’t have the most attractive roster in the world. By default, that makes us the cream of the crop for the slutty groupies to seek out,” Trevor pauses, then corrects himself. “Well it makes you the cream of the crop by default. I’m sure I’d be the sexiest superstar in the federation even if it was made up entirely of male models and Mr. Universe candidates.” “Well then how come I’m not seeing any action?” Seth complains. Trevor shrugs. “I don’t know what to tell you. It’s not like you have any competition for the ladies. Guys like Azrael Cain and Tom Walczak aren’t exactly lookers, you know.” “God damn it! Why the fuck haven’t I gotten any slutty groupies yet!” Seth shouts angrily, before remembering why he was digging through the boxes to begin with. “Trev, quit distracting me! We’re in the middle of a crisis here and I’ve got no time to worry about trivial shit like that!” “What crisis?” Trevor asks, but Seth just ignores him again. After about another minute of searching through the boxes he throws his hands up in frustration. “I give up! They’re not in any of these damn boxes!” Seth shouts, falling to his knees and raising a fist at the sky. “Oh why?! Why, God?! Why?!” “Seth...” Trevor starts in a condescending tone. “Are you ever planning on explaining to me what the hell this is all about?” “I can’t believe you don’t know!” Seth wails back. “As things are, I am much to distraught to get any intelligible thoughts out.” “Since when have you ever...” Trevor begins, before shaking his head. “Nah, too easy,” he mutters to himself. Seth hangs his head sadly and walks into the adjoining kitchen, or what functions as one. The room is almost barren, but at least it appears to be getting added on to. Where before it only had a microwave, it now has a small refrigerator cabinet sitting plugged into the corner as well. Those OSW paychecks really seem to be raising Civil Disobedience’s standard of living. Anyway, Seth opens the door to the little fridge and pulls out a quart of milk, chugging down the rest of it and throwing the empty carton over his back. Milk always makes him feel better, though it doesn’t seem to have any noticeable effect on this day. Seth sighs deeply once more and slumps against the wall. The milk carton clatters at Trevor’s feet. Shaking his head at his partner’s unwillingness to throw his trash in the garbage bins, he leans over and picks it off the ground. His eyes nearly shoot out of his head when he sees the back of the carton. “I don’t believe this!” he shouts at Seth, pointing at the carton with his other hand. “Neither did I,” Seth responds. “I didn’t think they still put those “Missing” reports on the back of milk cartons. Needless to say, I think we’re both lucky that they do.” The camera zooms in to reveal the face of the milk carton. On it is pictured the Old School Wrestling Tag Team Championship belts, with the word MISSING printed underneath it. It goes on to say that The belts were last seen Saturday night in Miami, Florida. Anyone with information can feel free to call Seth and Trevor. The number is in the book. “Seth, you do realize we lost that Tag Team title match against Heresian Bloodlust last week on Slam, don’t you?” Trevor asks, raising an incredulous eyebrow at his partner. “Yeah, you don’t have to rub it in, man,” Seth says morosely. “What does that have to do with our missing tag team titles, though?” “Seth...” Trevor sighs. “We no longer have the OSW Tag Team titles. We defended them against Hessian and Cain, were screwed out of the match by Carrot Top, and then lost. They are not missing, they just don’t belong to us anymore. Heresian Bloodlust own the belts now.” “What!” Seth shouts. “That’s not fair! Why didn’t anyone tell me that if we lost the match we’d lose the belts!?” “That’s kind of what the whole concept of ‘title defense’ is all about,” Trevor says in a lecturing tone. “There’s no way we’re getting the belts back tonight, man. We’ll have to settle things at a later date. Right now there’s nothing to do. “Hmmm...Heresian Bloodlust have the belts...” Seth says, thinking out loud. Suddenly he jumps up from the ground with a look of purpose on his face. “So then let’s get them back, man! We can fucking bust in the window and take that shit!” “That’s illegal, Seth,” explains Trevor, laying a comforting hand on his partner’s shoulder. “And even if we did steal the belts, we wouldn’t be the official champions anyway. They’d just make new belts for Hessian and Cain and arrest us for theft.” “Oh,” Seth says, hanging his head. “I guess you’re right.” “Hey, cheer up buckeroo,” Trevor nudges Seth. “Carrot Top interfered in our match, man!” “What?” Seth asks angrily. “Is that supposed to make me happy?! That stupid little fuck has irritated the shit out of me for years! He stomped me in the back of the head when I was down! He fucking cost me my precious OSW Tag Team title belt! I’ll kill that annoying bastard!” “WE’LL kill that annoying bastard,” Trevor corrects, a grin spreading on his face. “The stupid fuck initiated the use of physical violence upon us. I think that gives us free reign to do what we want with him.” “Ah,” Seth nods back, smiling as well. “I catch your drift.” “Damn right,” Trevor laughs. “I’m sort of glad he interfered now. If there’s anything I...and the rest of the world too, most likely...hate more than a dicksmack it’s an annoying dicksmack, and Carrot Top is at the top of that list. Ending his career is going to not only be sweet revenge for us, but it’ll be an act of charity toward all of humankind. No one will ever have to hear his pre-pubescent voice again!” “Yes!” Seth shouts happily. “And once he’s gone, we can get rid of all the annoying celebrities in the world! Muhahahaha!” “Uh...sure!” Trevor agrees with his partner’s stupid comment. “Muhahahaha!” The two men are laughing evilly, placing their hands together and moving their fingers in imitation of all the diabolical super villains over the years. The scene fades out with this.
