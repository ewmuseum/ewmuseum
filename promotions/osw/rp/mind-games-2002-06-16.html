Mind Games

16 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sunday, June 16, 2002 **Joint RP with Ivan** 'That's it Mr. Rucha, you're almost there.' The screen fades in to reveal Pablo Rucha for the first time in many weeks. He is sweating profusely as he is slowly trying to straighten his injured leg while a female in her late twenties applies resistance. 'Come on Mr. Rucha I'll ease up a bit if I'm going to fast for you.' Encourages the young brunette. Pablo grits his teeth and pushes a bit harder and fully extending his leg. He smiles proudly as he looks up at the woman. 'You can't go fast enough for me Jenelle.' He says with a wink. 'Oh you.' Clapping is heard from a far corner as a figure rounds from a shadow into the light, revealing first a brown trench coat, and then a hat and face to match: Alexei Ruslan. 'Congratulations! I see that all the Kings horses and all the Kings men COULD put Humpty Dumpty together again!' Alexei grins and leans against the wall with his arms crossed. Jenelle looks down at Pablo with a confused face but Rucha is already turned his attention to Alexei. 'What are you doing here?' He says angrily. Alexei walks halfway into the room and nods to the pretty nurse, 'My dear, it is me that you can owe your paycheck to, and with that, if you could please excuse us, I have business to talk of with Rucha. Don't I, Pablo? Or do you now know the news yet?' Alexei grins wider and tips his hat. 'Yes, I am aware... what do you mean she owes you her paycheck?' Pablo asks. Alexei chuckles, 'Without me, she wouldn't have anybody to work on, now would she?' Alexei grins sadistically and nods at his knee. Rucha shakes his head as Jenelle takes her leave. 'For a moment there, I thought you had some decency and was paying to fix the damage you had caused.' Rucha says with a hint of sarcasm. 'HEE HEE HAA!!' Alexei bursts into laughter, 'EEHEE HEE HAA!!! You Mexicans, always so stupid!' Alexei rubs the tears from his eyes and calms himself, 'But yes, business, Mr. Rucha,' Alexei suddenly becomes very matter of fact and sits on a nearby bench, 'You see, Howard Porter, in his infinite wisdom, has put us in a proverbial 'jam', has he not?' Pablo slowly swings his leg off the bench so he is in a sitting position and nods his head. 'Yes, he most definitely has.' Alexei rubs his chin and raises an eyebrow as he sizes up Rucha, 'I think it would be in your best interest, to hear what I have to say, and do what I say. Eh?' Rucha lowers his head and simply mumbles, 'I guess so.' 'You guess? There can be no guesses, only assurances!' Alexei gets up off the bench and takes a few steps and turns, facing Rucha. 'Drago wishes to fight Ivan, da?' 'Why ask questions you already know the answer to Russian?' Replies Pablo. Alexei nods, 'Exactly!' He pauses and nods to himself, making a mental note, and continues. 'So, he wants to fight Ivan, but he has to get through Krieg first....so he obviously would want an advantage....which he gets if he wins tag match with Ivan!' Alexei pauses and talks softer, 'What I am asking for, Rucha, is an agreement from you that we have complete cooperation from El Drago...we of Red Army don't like irregularities and surprises, lest we stamp them out.' There is several moments of silence as Pablo thinks and then he slides off the bench standing on his good leg. 'Pass me my crutches will you?' Alexei looks at the crutches and slowly hands them to Rucha, keeping a wary eye on him. 'Thanks' Pablo says placing the crutches under his arms and smiles at Alexei. 'Now see how easy that was? You scratch my back and I'll scratch yours. You don't want Krieg knowing that you are scheming behind his back, then you will continue to listen what I have planned.' Pablo smirks at Alexei before he starts to head for the door. Alexei blinks, almost as if slapped in the face and rushes towards Rucha, 'Now hold on one moment, Rucha!' Alexei turns in front of Rucha, stopping him. 'Comrade Stanislav is going to talk to Krieg and we are confident he will willingly lay down for Ivan, because of our stalwart and indominatable beliefs! However, we are merely making this gesture to..to......ensure cooperation, not..uh...victory.' Alexei nods, 'But of course! Lead the way! Alexei is in no hurry!' Pablo nods and the two slowly but surely exit the room as the camera fades out.
