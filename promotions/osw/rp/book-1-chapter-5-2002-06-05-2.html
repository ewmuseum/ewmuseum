Book 1 -- Chapter 5

5 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 5th 2002 This is a joint chapter. Rhett Locke is featured. Part 1: Insanity Part 2: Persists Chapter Title: The Who the hell are you to tell me how I act? You, yes, you. Fucker. You think you can just waltz up to me like the dancing fucking prick you are, and tell me that I'm not acting like a good guy anymore? Just because I put GV in a fucking retard institution, you think I'm not gonna do so well with my 'fan base'? Well fuck you, guy. I'll do whatever I want to. Why? WHY? Because I'm fucking Rhett Locke. I have an ego? I have an ego? What about you, Mr. Man? What about you? Thinking you can just come to me and tell me the difference between right and wrong, christ! You think I don't know the difference in right and wrong? No? Well fuck you. You want an explanation? Fine, I'll give you one. The delusional fuckass tried to kill me, that a good enough reason? Except when he was trying to kill me, he wasn't killing me. It was the fucking doctor, but in his eyes, it was me. What? What? Who the hell do you think YOU are, Steve fucking Austin? So I make this big scheme, right? Classic! I must've...I must have had one of those fuckin'... genius moments, as people call them. Anyway, I send his sorry, insane ass to the George Pearson Centre. So his ass is strapped in one of those straight jackets, and he's in Canada! WHAT could be worse then being insane in Canada? Has to be hard to beat that...like fuckin', fuckin' New Zealand or something. So then, I go and pretend to be buddy-buddy, you know, get that Dr. Schmidt to put trust in me. Why? Because then he'd ask me my opinion on how 'Steven Shadows' used to act! His ass could be stuck in that piss-rotten piece of shit place for eternity, if I wanted! Cool, right? Large! What? ... ... Oh, so that's the way you're going to be huh? Just going to turn your back on me like that? Well let me tell you something bitch! I've worked my ass off in OSW, and I'm not about to let him ruin my career! It's about what? What is? NO NO NO NO NO! FUCK YOU, ASS! IT ISN'T ABOUT FEAR! I DON'T FUCKING FEAR HIM! ... I DON'T FEAR ANYBODY, FUCKER! ... You fucking bastard, how can you say that? Go to hell, I'm through talking with you. **** Four walls. No food. No water. Silence. At first this place was hell to me. I wanted to get out right away so that I can rip off Rhett Lockes’ head and shit down his throat. But he actually did me a favor. Yes, a favor. These surroundings give me a vivid image of what Steven called home for quite some time. The constant screaming I hear in this place is just like when his mother used to scream. The abuse I’ve taken from the doctors, getting beat up time after time. It’s just like his father used to do to me. And I sit here in this room, caged like an animal. Just like when Steven was a boy caged in his basement. If it weren’t for me, Steven Shadows would be a dead man. I kept Steven alive. He allowed me to consume his mind body and soul. I’m the one that gave him the strength to stand up to his bastard parents. I’m the one that’s made him famous. And I will be the one to get him out of this hell hole and kick the living crap out of Rhett Locke once and for all. Rhett might think he’s done a great thing committing me in this asylum. He might think that he’s gotten rid of me for awhile and that hopefully I won’t return. But he’s greatly mistaken. All Rhett has done is put me in touch with my roots. This asylum is exactly what Steven’s home life was. This is nothing new to me. I’ve dealt with this all my life. Thank you Rhett Locke. It’s good to finally be home.
