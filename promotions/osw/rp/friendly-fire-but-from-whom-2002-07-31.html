Friendly Fire...but from whom?

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 31st, 2002 ((Note: Don't mind me...just testing the waters.)) ''You don't think I'm going crazy or anything, right?'' It was a lovely question to be asking a primate like little ZaC as he sat perched on a chair right next to Amy's mass of red hair. She was seriously looking for some sort of response from the little monkey, though all she got in return was a clump of her hair getting picked through in his soft little palms. She was alone in the motel room, aside from the company of ZaC and the sounds of 'The Crow: City of Angels' pounding out from the TV in front of her, yet Campbell felt unusually secure...almost as if she'd finally settled in with the knowledge that no matter what Isiic Raiyn did, it was like the apocalypse: There was nothing she could do to stop it, she only had the power to prolong or bring forward the eminent pain. The thought, of course, wasn't comforting in the least...the only comfort she found was in the fact that Corg wasn't there at that very moment, that she was alone with the cute little ape and a good movie. ''Heh,'' She chuckled, scratching ZaC's neck. ''This is stupid, I'm not crazy...just a little paranoid!'' Aimz snuggled up to her knees in the chair nervously, only moving when ZaC hopped forward onto the top of the television. ''.........right?'' ''Of course I'm right! Yeah...'' Reassurance was her friend for the moment. ''I mean, if I was going crazy, I'd know it! I'm just edgy...you know...with a creepy guy hunting you with the intent of some kind of murder or whatever...that can happen!'' ZaC stared blanky and began to gnaw at the cable antennae, Amy could have sworn he shook his puffy little head. ''Damnit, ZaC! I'm not going nuts! CHRIST...you're such a prick.'' The monkey cocked his head only to sniff the air and then leap at the door in an excited haze, sensing the arrival of Reno Davis in the same way an average dog would have and, at the very same time, someone whispered into Aimz' ear... Sooner or later, he'll have you cornered... Sure enough, Davis strode through the door with a McDonald's bag in hand...but what did Amy see? Corg. ''What the FUCK?!'' She'd screamed, lunging forward at the man. When she knocked him onto the floor, she was punching and tearing at him with her nails, an almost psychotic daze having slipped over her. It only ended when, thankfully enough Reno grabbed her arms, rolling over to pin her to the bargain basement carpeted floor. ''Amy! What's wrong?!'' He shook her hard, only then causing her to regain proper vision... Everything had been blurred, but now it was clear...Reno's face hovered above hers, and she felt his hands gripping onto her tightly, painfully. ''Reno?'' ''You fucking bitch...'' He shouted at her, shaking her harder. ''Raiyn shoulda taken you out when Ed first dragged your sorry ass into this federation...'' ''Reno?'' She whispered this time...she wanted to scream, but she whispered. It didn't sound like Reno, but it looked like Reno. She wasn't sure if he was even speaking, though she saw his lips move, mouthing the words. ''Why don't you die?'' He was calm, but the grip still hurt Amy. Someone was knocking urgently at the door that had slammed shut when Campbell pounded his body into it, whoever it was must have heard the struggle...they were there to save her. ''Why can't you just die? For Christ's sake...I can NEVER get my way...you always fuck it up. No wonder everybody turns from you!'' The same man, whoever it had been before though sightless, whispered into her ear again. She didn't feel his breath, but felt his presence looming, like something was about to come up from the floor and skewer her through the back. No wonder everybody turns from you, don't you know why they all betray you? Nobody wants you, only those who don't know you like you...the sight of your face is fine, but the sound of your voice drives these men to ruin you. ''Fuck you!'' She screamed at Reno and the rasping old man who'd been speaking in her ear. ''Why is everybody like this now!?! GAH!'' That's when she'd tossed Davis off of her, an almost superhuman strength taking over... And that's when the true blur lifted. Laying at the bottom of her shower with hot water burning every inch of her uncomfortably...Amy was sobbing, groaning something unclear. The pounding at the door was still there, only Reno's voice came from behind it where only a mumble had been before... ''Amy? What's wrong?'' He was repeating the nightmarish side of him she'd seen just moments prior... A dream, right?
