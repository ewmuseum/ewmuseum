Life of a Champion: Part I

1 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-1-02 A reporter walks up to Tom Walczak’s home and rings the doorbell. He rings it a couple of more times until Walczak finally answers the door. “Oh, its you just you fucking bastards.” “Yes, my name is Richard Nibbler, and I will be covering your life for the next couple of days.” Walczak bursts out into laughter, so much so that he falls to the ground, and is losing his breath. “Mr. Walczak, are you ok?” “HAHAHAHAHAHA!!!! Your name…..Your name is….. It is Dick Nibbler!!!!!! HAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!!!” “Umm, actually I prefer Richard.” “Ok, so I will call you Dick.” “Fine, can we just start filming?” “No problem.” Walczak takes the camera crew inside of his house, and they set up the equipment. Walczak goes to the refrigerator, and takes out a beer. Then Mr. Nibbler calls him over to start the filming. “Mr. Walczak, I think that your image would come off better if you would not drink beer on camera.” “Whose life is this, mine or yours? I thought so.” “Ok, and lets start shooting, 3…2…1…and shoot.” The cameras begin to roll, and Mr. Nibbler does the introduction. “I am here with OSW World Champion Tom Walczak, and you will get to see into his life for the next couple of days. Right now we are in his home. Mr. Walczak what is a typical morning for a man such as yourself?” “Well, when I wake up I immediately go to the bathroom. I usually take a shit, and then I shower up. After that I usually go to the fridge and grab a beer, and then after drinking a couple of beers I head off to the gym.” “That sounds…well…it sounds interesting. So you are heading to the gym right now?” “You bet your ass that I am.” “Ok, good. Cut the cameras, and we will meet you at the gym. Walczak heads out for his jog to the gym, and the camera crew gathers all of their stuff, and packs it into their van. As the van drives towards the gym the scene fades out…
