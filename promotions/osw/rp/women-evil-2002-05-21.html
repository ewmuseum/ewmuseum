Women.... evil?

21 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05-21-2002 Lillith was relaxing on a bed in a hotel room, flipping through the movie channels, and thinking about the issues going on in her career. Graphic Violence was still being a pest, but he was set on the back burner right now. Her primary concern was with the two religious nuts, Eric Sagar and Propaganda. Flick - The Craft. Seen it. 'What was it about people who claim to be of one god or another, that makes them treat women like they're slaves, or pets, or even kitchen appliances? Don't they realize they're not living in the dark ages? This is the goddamned 21st century, they should dig the crud out of their and realize that!' Flick - Bedazzled. Seen it. 'Come on, do they really think that women are like that? That if they're outgoing and independant, then they're evil? That they're witches, or sluts, or demonesses or something?' Flick - Wizard of Oz. Seen it. 'The problem is, they're using a book written in the 5th century, which was out of date in the 6th century, and it' 1500 years later. They're backwards! The gotta realize that not only are women equal, in many cases they are far, far superior. ' Flick - Vamp. Seen it. 'Well, I suppose there's only one thing to do. Prove to them, and to everyone, just what a woman is capable of. And if that means destroying everything they hold dear, then so be it.' Flick - The Exorcist. Seen it, but nothing better on. Lillith set aside the remote for the TV, and enjoyed this classic of horror films.
