Contemplating issues in the office

4 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-4-02 -History has a funny way of showing us what is in store in the future. History could almost be considered a precautionary study by some historians and students, as various events have occurred, sometimes hundreds of years in the past that really is a precursor of things to come if one is not careful. There are many people in the world that could be considered historians, and very few of them can also be considered wrestlers, however, Ivan Stanislav is a man that could very well be considered a man of each faith. Stanislav has devoted much of his life to the study of history to not only understand his past, but also to look to the future to make his country a much better place. Point in fact, Ivan Stanislav has now made his decision to also impose these beliefs on any and all that would get in his way. And those that are his enemies? Well, they either agree with him or they will fall to his mighty strength. To really understand what Ivan is trying to do, one must understand what had happened in the past. If you were to go back far enough to Vladimir Lenin’s time and Josef Stalin, we can see an albeit small, parallel between the past, present, and the future. The current situation in Russia was not what the people had wanted, and therefore Lenin had decided that change was needed. Change that would change the entire world for the rest of time. Lenin’s great revolution was not something that occurred over night, and it was not something that the majority wanted, however, it was something that Lenin himself wanted, and that was enough. Alexander Kerensky, known Russian politician, also wanted change, but not the change that Lenin wanted, the two butted heads with Lenin becoming the ultimate victor. Fast forward to the present… Ivan Stanislav and his Red Army, against Trashcan Man and Doug Troy. Both factions are fighting for an ultimate goal: The betterment of the OSW and to overthrow the PCW. However, both factions believe that it would be better to handle it in different ways. The brutal, communist laden collectiveness is not something easily swallowed by most men, and sometimes one must handle these matters in a far more…..violent way.- The scene fades up on Ivan Stanislav, The Soviet Machine of the Russian Federation, as he stares at a large map of the world in his office in the Arkhangelsk Military Base, Russia. There is nothing unordinary about the map, it shows the continents, elevations, and countries of the world in various colors. Stanislav in particular stares at the United States. His huge barrel chest rises and falls as he breathes, causing a slight sound of tension on his suspenders around the shoulders. For all intents and purposes, Stanislav wears the same clothing that he had worn in his previous appearance on OSW television, and as we will soon learn, due to the life of a Russian soldier, Stanislav usually wears the same clothing anywhere that he goes. The only difference in his attire from before is that a military hat covers his dark brown hair. The hat is pulled forward a bit to his forehead as he casts a downtrodden look on the map. He looks over at the camera slowly. -Troy and Trashcan Man are now the resistance. Stanislav had a beautiful plan. It was all so simple. The men in the OSW were young, naïve, and easily manipulated. Those that could not would just simply be pounded straight into the mat. And then Doug Troy and the Trashcan Man had to come and ruin it all….- Stanislav growls and curls his upper lip as he continues to think…. -They came in with their fan base and their hope and stifled his great terror that he was going to inflict upon the OSW. When the OSW was hopeless and Stanislav was ready to conscript everyone to his great war, those two got in his way…..- “…again….” Mutters Ivan to himself. Ivan rubs his chin and turns to his desk, a large oak structure capable of not only holding the weight of the computer and all the other books on top of it, but handling Ivan’s tirades in which his fists usually rain down upon the desktop. He sits down in his large red chair, again, specially made to handle the four hundred pound frame of Ivan Stanislav. Behind Ivan, above his head and up against the plane metal wall, hangs the flag of the Former Soviet Union, beside it, a smaller flag representing the Russian Federation. Stanislav has received constant requests to put the Federation’s flag in the forefront, but still he refuses to do so. -It’s not like Ivan does not like his country, that could be any further from the truth, but it is the beliefs that his country should embrace that Ivan is so critical of, that is all.- A slight whirring sound is heard and then the sound of a printer printing out a paper is heard. The type of printer is obviously old as the sound is loud, high pitched, and screeching. We can tell that this is something that Stanislav hates as he grits his teeth and looks at his printer/fax machine. As it slowly dies and finishes printing out the fax Ivan tears it and looks, reading it aloud. “Meeting in Moscow, 5 February 2002 regarding “Tempest” incident.” Ivan growls and shakes his head. -How was he to know that the contest on the radio was going to be for the hand of some girl!! And a girl that shows excessive emotion as well! And now he has to spend time with her in the People’s Republic of the Congo?!- Ivan gets up and walks over to the map. He bends at the waist and squints. He mumbles to himself as he finds the Congo. “Africa….bah….” He shakes his head and looks at the camera. “I cannot believe this is happening!” screams Stanislav. “I am dealing with the beginning of new era in OSW and I have to worry about this woman!” Ivan shrugs and stands in front of his desk. He glares at the camera. He raises his arms high over his head and bellows to the camera. “I am Ivan Stanislav of Russian Red Army! I have come to the OSW in order to dominate and control the entire group of wrestlers within her bounds! No girl is going to change that, and nothing will make me move from my resolve!” Ivan roars loudly and takes a step forward closer to the camera. “Trashcan Man, I saw you in front of stupid “Liberty Bell” and how you tried to be upstanding United States citizen! You Americans, back in revolutionary times, were nothing but upstart brats to the British King! Americans were one of the LEAST taxed people in world, and yet you continued to cry to the world for help! Just as you are now!” Ivan roars and kicks backwards into his desk, the desk moves across the room. “And again, you stand in front of me, crying and preaching….” Ivan bellows again and slams his foot off of the ground. Dust falls from the ceiling onto his shoulders. “But Doug Troy, he is my major target. Doug Troy, for the months that we fought in PCW you got in my way yet you were lucky enough to not have a direct effect on my plans. However, Doug Troy, that has now changed!!” Ivan roars at the camera and puts his massive paws on his hips. “For you see Troy, you ARE now a direct obstacle to my plans of dominating the OSW, and I will make sure personally that you will not make it through Saturday SLAM! in wrestling ring!” Ivan nods to the camera and walks around his desk to his chair. He rests a large hand on the top of the chair. “OSW, two veterans from the PCW are trying to stop me and save their old federation! I urge you all to follow my lead and deny them the pleasure of thwarting our revolution!! And Doug Troy and Trashcan Man, I will see you on Saturday, and I will personally make lives personal HELL!!” Ivan laughs at the camera and then goes suddenly very grim. The camera fades out slowly.
