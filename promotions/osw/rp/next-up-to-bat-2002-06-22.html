Next Up To Bat...

22 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/22/02 CRACK! The sound rings out throughout the stadium as a baseball bat collides with a small, stitched baseball. The ball soars through the air, rising slowly. It starts to drop quickly all of a sudden and it’s path is ended as it crashes into the out-field grass, bouncing until it comes three feet from the wall at the end. The camera zooms in on the baseball, which is covered in dirt from it’s crash landing. The ball rolls for a split second, allowing words written in thick, red marker to show up in view. “HAWKE” Back in the in-field, Riptah is standing at home plate with an intimidating look on his face. He is wear blue jeans and a tight, black beater, showing off his chiseled body. He wipes some dust off his wrap-round sunglasses with his free hand, while the other holds a baseball bat at his side. The very baseball bat Riptah will weld in a mere two days at Ascension. Across the gravel Short Circuit stands on the pitcher’s mound, with a much different complexion than his tag partner. He is wearing huge blue baggy jeans, a Los Angeles Lakers jersey, backwards Dodgers hat and of course, his big crazy-ass sunglasses. This time they’re in the color lime-green. He spits a piece of gum into the grass and looks to Riptah, who is preparing for a pitch down the line toward him. Short Circuit pauses for a moment and raises his eyebrow. “You hit like a girl, you couldn’t make it over the wall. Try hitting a home-run for once, a’ight?” Short Circuit laughs at his own comments, enjoying ridiculing his much larger partner. “I’m a wrestler, not a baseball player. Just hurry up and pitch the god-damn ball.” Bluntly states Riptah. Short Circuit pulls a red marker from his pocket and picks a ball out of the basket. He legibly writes the word, “CAIN”, in big bold letters. He returns the marker to it’s place of origin and stares down at his partner. He winds up comically and releases the ball, letting it fly toward the 290lb Beast. Riptah swings, connecting with the ball again. This time it picks up quicker, reaching significantly larger heights. “Not gonna make it,” informs Daisy who is sitting in the empty dug-out by herself, twirling her hair while she reluctantly watched the two practice their baseball skills. However, her skills of prediction are quite right as the ball ricochets off the wall at the end of the field. Riptah curses as Daisy enjoys a good, strong laugh. Riptah moans in disappointment. “C’mon SC, pitch the ball better. I gotta get this swing down-pat. Y’know, soon were going to be the OSW Tag Champions, so the Dodgers might want us for all sorts of promotion and advertisements. They appreciate talent like us. I doubt the Elite is getting any promotion with any Pro-Sports Teams.” He smirks, happy with his fame in the past. But the past is over, Da Westsidaz are now looking toward the future. Short Circuit rubs dust between his hands and pulls another baseball from the basket. He slowly write the word, “SETH”, on it and smiles. He warms up and drills the baseball across the plate. Riptah swings and connects with the ball. It flies up fast but has a bad left turn. It flies into the stands, but in the foul zone. “Goddamn it, nothing is going our way lately.” Mumbles Riptah, “I mean, sure, we got two tag title shots this week, but did you see how badly we got screwed? Civil Disobedience gets a rest tonight so they can have the advantage tomorrow. Then Heresian Bloodlust brings muthafuckin’ barbwire mits to the dance! That’s just not right, they can’t walk around shredding our skin and stuff.” Riptah groans in disgust and takes a few practice swings. “Yeah dawg, I hear you. Just because I’m the goddamn best looking wrestler ever to grace the ring doesn’t give them the right to try and wreck my beautiful face. The Tag Champs are lucky, because in a normal tag match they can’t even compete with Da Westsidaz. That’s why we got those titles in the bag tonight.” Short Circuit smirks arrogantly and reaches into the basket. Riptah takes another practice swing and laughs, “Doesn’t matter though. We don’t have to go out 100% tonight. Actually, I think we shouldn’t try and win, we should just try and hurt them. It don’t matter what we do tonight.” He swings again and grins at the bat, “…Who cares about the barbwire. It’s not like they’re going to get their hands on us in the first place. If they try and get close to us, we’ll just let ‘em swallow their molars after a nice home-run. Not only will they be ugly-ass freaks, they’ll also be toothless ugly-ass freaks.” Short Circuit smiles and writes, “HESSIAN”, on the last ball. “Yeah, but that scary ass homeless dude is the ref again. That fucking pisses me off. Civil Disobedience have the bookers on their side while Heresian Bloodlust have the refs on theirs. We ain’t got nothin’ but our own mad skills.” He winds up and chucks the ball at Riptah. “Nah, we’re untouchable.” Riptah swings and cracks rip through the bat. The wooden club shatters in a fury of woodchips and shards as the ball skyrockets into the outfield. It easily clears the wall and even bounces into the upper-deck, while Riptah chuckles at the mess of wood surronding him. “Damn dawg, even in baseball the Westside is the Bestside.”
