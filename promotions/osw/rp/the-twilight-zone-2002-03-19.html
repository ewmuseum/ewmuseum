The Twilight Zone

19 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-19-02 Black and white, the sound of a familiar Television show plays in the background, but why is everything in black and white? Things floating in the air all around when the title comes across a swirling circle. It reads “The Twilight Zone”, when out of nowhere pops a man and the music comes to a climatic ending. “Problems on top of problems,” The man pauses, “agony on top of agony,” He pauses for another moment, “it doesn’t matter how much you try you’ll never survive the horrible beating that comes in the end.” Finally finishing his sentence and beginning to walk around the set. “Seeking joy from all of the pains that come into our lives, we do seek it, and we do seek it now.” He begins to look around. “No questions answered. Not by those surrounding us.” He brings his arms up motioning around him, showing no one. “Not by those of a greater power.” He points to the sky. “So, who does this leave to answer the questions that lie dormant in the back our minds?” An eerie music begins to pick up in the background. ”Those questions that just linger and cause us the most problems of all. The real question we must stop to ask ourselves is nothing far from the truth.” He pauses once more. “What would we do if these questions could be answered? How would you react with the truth? We may never know the answer to these, but shortly you will find out how one man would react in this situation. This is his tale here, but anything can happen. In the Twilight Zone.” The music goes into its usual sequence. Fading into the still black and white scene, we come into a living room. Where we see Michelle Marie Ramey and Chass Loraine sitting on the couch. Watching what seems to be an old time television show on one of those huge, old televisions. The front door begins to make noises as it slowly squeaks open. In walks Jesse who seems to have just gotten home from work. Wearing a nasty looking set of pants and a button-up white shirt with the little black tie. His coat draped over his arm and what seems to be a briefcase in his hand. He walks slowly over to the table in the kitchen, which is basically connected with the living room. He places his briefcase on the table and looks over at Chass and Michelle. With a very obvious fake smile, “So, tell me how your day was today, girls.” Jesse waits a few moments, but gets no reply. “Girls, I ask how your day was.” Still nothing comes from their mouths. They sit still on the couch with no movement. The television seems to be on, but no sound is coming from it. Jesse slowly makes his way around the couch and looks into the television. The show is on, but it seems to be stuck in one spot. This is so confusing to him, seeing as how this has never happened before. He can understand why the television may have froze, but why aren’t Michelle or Chass speaking to him. He reaches his hands down in front of both Chass and Michelle’s faces and begins to wave. They sit still and make no movement, when all of a sudden Chass burst out into laughter. “CUT!!!” Comes the sound of someone’s voice from off to the side. Quickly jolting around everything comes into color as we see a man who is obviously a director and a very angry one at that. “Cut, cut, cut! I’m sick of you wrestling personalities always coming in here and taking so long to get one shot! Go back to your trailers and study those damn scripts. Don’t come back until you are totally ready to get this thing done.” He yells. The three leave their positions and head back towards their trailers. “See what you did? If you had only been able to hold yourself there for just a few more moments and then it would have all been done and over.” Jesse says, directed towards Chass. She glares at Jesse, “Well, it’s not my fault! I’m sorry if I can’t be perfect like some people!” “Just go to your trailers. I don’t like you two fighting all the time.” Michelle butts in. The two go their separate ways and into their trailers. We still trail behind Jesse, who falls back into a black leather chair and behinds cross his arms. He slowly slides back into the chair when the trailer door is knocked on. “Come in, the door isn’t locked.” Jesse shouts in frustration. The door slowly opens with the face of the director in it. “Jesse, baby, bobala! Don’t be mad. What happened out there was totally none of your fault.” Swinging his arms around in the air trying to get his point out there, “Come back out here and we’ll get someone more talented than her to play that part. You don’t need her.” Jesse jumps up and back the director back up against the wall, lifts him up by the collar of his shirt, and stares him right in the eyes. “You listen to me buddy. I don’t care how much better the next person can be. I don’t care who you could get to play that part and quite frankly it doesn’t matter to me anymore if I do the part that I have. I don’t like having either of my girls, whether it is Chass or Michelle, being harassed. Especially by the likes of someone as low as you. So, you can either go over to their trailers, one by one, and apologize to them.” “But…Um…I…” He tries to blurt out, but can’t as Jesse pulls up against the wall more. “You’ll do as I say or you’ll not have me here. Now, can you understand that or do I have to show you another way?” Jesse brings his hand up into the air, taunting the director. “Yes, I totally understand. Please let me down and I’ll be happy to head over do as you wish.” The director says as Jesse brings him back down to his feet. Jesse takes his hand and begins to dust off the director’s top and straighten him up, “Now, run along.” The director walks out the door and slams it behind him. We can hear him mumbling to himself as he walks off. Jesse slumps back down into the chair as another knock is heard at the door. Jesse stands up in rage, “Can’t a person get a little piece and quiet around this place?” Jesse walks over to the door and begins to shake his head as he turns the knob on the door and pulls it open. Standing on the other side is John Buchanan, Jesse’s old friend and former tag team partner. Jesse begins to blink in amazement, “Well, kick me in the nuts and call me Skippy. How the fuck are you doing, John?” John smiles back at Jesse, “Doing pretty good. I heard you were on the lot and thought I would come see how you were doing.” “Pretty shitty actually, you’ve caught me at a bad time.” Jesse says. “So, you don’t want any company right now?” John asks. “Shit, I didn’t mean it that way man. Come on in. We haven’t spoken in how long now? I’m not just going to close the door on one of my best friends, dude. Take a seat man.” Jesse points at the sofa he was just sitting in. “Thanks man.” John walks over and slides into the chair, “Nice, seems like they are treating someone around here with a little class.” “Yeah,” Jesse begins to laugh. “I guess they do show a little bit of hospitality. You want something to drink?” “Sure man, throw me a soda of any kind.” John says. Jesse reaches into his cooler and tosses John a Mountain Dew and grabs one of his own out of the case. “I like mine hot man. Hope you like yours cold.” Jesse opens the soda can and takes a drink, sits down in the rotating chair and then continues, “So, tell me about this whole thing. What did you mean by “they know how to treat some people”?” “Well, ever since CWA closed down, I’ve been working here and I’ve never had this kind of luxury.” He takes a drink of the soda, “It’s a little weird, seeing as how I’ve been here for nearly two years and I’m still being treated as a rookie. I’ve got nearly seven or eight years of wrestling under my belt. Held major titles and had a lot of recognition and this is what I’ve got.” John begins to laugh. “Its all a little strange.” “John, man. Do you follow OSW?” Jesse asks. “Old School Wrestling, yeah, I’ve been watching what’s been going on. Why you ask?” John asks. “Well, it seems that I have been gaining a sort of entourage in OSW and everyone of the people that are seen with usually get paid some bit. Michelle and Chass both are bringing in a paycheck. Why not come over with me and become my manager.” Jesse says. “Nah, I can’t do that man. I’m a wrestler, not a manager. I’ve never sunk that low in my life.” John says. “Think of it as a business investment. Not a managerial job. You’re looking at the small picture, not the big one. Think about it man, you’ll have all the benefits as before and you’ll be getting paid better than you are here. I’ll not try and force you into it, but it’s just a thought.” Jesse says. “Well, this place sure doesn’t pay very well and I do need the money.” John begins to think for a moment. “I believe I could give it a try for a little while, but it’s not going to be a thing that continues for a long, long time. Okay?” John asks. “Sure thing man. You can stay as long as you want to. That’s really up to you. I just want to help you out man. By the way, you said you’ve been following OSW. What do you think about Michel Kreig?” Jesse asks. After taking a drink of soda, “Well, if you’re talking about that one Germany guy. The one guy who basically cost you your match at Crowning Glory and has been a throne in your side since his arrival.” He pauses and thinks. “Yeah, I know who he is.” “You catch his last promo? It aired on OSW television just a few hours ago.” Jesse says. “Yeah, I was actually off work and on my lunch break when it first aired. To be quite honest, it seems like this guy is obsessed with hurting you man. Not just doing a number on you like he would either way, but it seems he literally wants to kill you man.” John says. Begins to think to him self for a moment, “I don’t understand it all that much, but if he wants to go on about how I’m going to jump the guardrails and beat him up on Slam, then by all means let him think that way. Only I know what I’m going to do. He’ll have to wait to find out anything.” “While we are on the topic of OSW, don’t you have a match this weekend?” John asks. “Yeah, it’s another tag team match, but I’ll at least be tagging with another friend. These past two matches were fine by me. I like working with people that aren’t communists bastards. Dame and Locke are both okay in my book. As for the rest of the people in this match.” Jesse begins to laugh. “You know you really shouldn’t take this match so lightly. You are going up against Hostyle and Bones. They have done a number on you before.” John says. Still laughing, “Yeah, they’ve done a number on me. When it was three on one. If you can look back and tell me that they had me beat cleanly then I’ll pay you five hundred bucks. It took Toomes to keep me down and then that didn’t even work. 3B will be taken out easily. Then we have Hersian Bloodlust,” Jesse begins to laugh more, “Sorry, I could contain myself. These guys just crack me up. They are over sized muscle heads that know nothing about anything. They couldn’t tell you their bicep from your quadriceps. It’s amazing that these guys are even still around.” “Jesse man, speaking to you as a friend, you had better watch what you are saying man. You are talking about some big, bad people here.” John clearly states. “You’ve really got to be kidding me. Look at these people; Hessian, Azreal Cain, Frankie Bones, Drew Hostyle… Who really cares? These guys aren’t going to be a problem for either me or Dame.” Jesse continues with the cockiness. “What about Twisted Metal?” John asks. “Who?” Jesse thinks for a moment, “Oh those two robots that blow-up on their opponents? Who cares? Robots weren’t meant to be in the wrestling ring. They can both pick up their parts and go back to Battle Bots, or that one on TNN. They are going to have to give it their all on Slam. Mark my words, John, Slam is going to be the night that I make my come back. I’m going straight to the top!” Jesse says as he stands up. “Now, if you’ll excuse me. I’ve got a couple things I need to go do. I’ll see you later.” Jesse walks over to his trailer door and exits the room. John sighs, “Might as well just hang around here. Idiot wants me to come work with him and doesn’t even tell me where he is staying. This is just great.” John stands and exits the room as well. The trailer, the chair inside the trailer, covered in black leather; blackness, almost the same color as the leather on the chair. ~The End~
