Why, Civil D? Why?

9 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-09-02 SSSSSS! Though no one knows for sure, it can be speculated that, more likely than not, that was the last sound Corg heard before being blinded with searing mist last night on Slam. CRACK! Though no one knows for sure, it can be speculated that, more likely than not, that was the last sound Andrew Ace Hawk heard before losing consciousness last night on Slam. These two events would usually not be considered a big deal. Hell, Corg and Ace Hawk get their asses kicked on a nightly basis. The fans expect nothing less. It’s so predictable that these two guys will get beat down that many of the people in attendance use their matches as an opportunity to hit the concession stands. So you see, these type of things are really common occurrences. The one thing that sets these particular events apart from any other ruthless ass-kickings is the fact that their perpetrators were none other than Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke. You know, the team of Civil Disobedience. The team that everyone can relate to. The fan favorites. The laid-back goof balls. The grinning idiots. At least, that’s the image everyone else knows and loves. Except for Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke, that is. Until last night, until they had the opportunity to stand in the ring over the fallen body of Andrew Ace Hawk, they felt quite the opposite. They fucking hated it. Before last night, they looked in the mirror every morning after waking up, just loathing themselves for what they were becoming. And now... Relief. Oh, one could just imagine what the mindless masses, aka the OSW fan base, were discussing this day after the fact. Probably shit like ‘What could have possessed those two to team up with Toomes’ or ‘What reasons could they possibly have for turning on the fans?’ or ‘Did you see those two help out Aimz? Do you know what her bra size is?’ Those are some damn stupid questions, especially the last one. For those fools to think that Seth and Trevor owed them shit is just a sign of how moronic the fan base really is. Civil Disobedience said from the beginning that they were in OSW to set the standard and look good doing it. It’s just a shame that as time wore on the standard became less one of excellence and more one of idiocy. In the beginning, things didn’t start out too bad. They were feuding with Heresian Bloodlust, doing respectably well in the ring, making small waves in the industry. It was a hell of a lot more than most indy workers could ever hope for their careers. Seth and Trevor could have just been happy getting over on their ring work alone. But no, they had all these crazy ideas running through their heads. ‘Let’s use comedy to get over’ was what Seth had said. ‘If we can get the fans to buy into us, we’ll make a fortune off the merchandise.’ And it worked. For awhile they were two of the most popular, if not THEE two most popular wrestlers in Old School Wrestling. For awhile, at least. People would come to see the events and laugh their asses off at the crazy shit they saw Seth and Trevor do. It got to the point where it even began to eclipse their in-ring work. The fans began caring more about what stupid-ass promo the two were going to cut during the show than they cared about the actual match itself. It wasn’t that they didn’t want the fans to like them. It was that they didn’t want them to like them for acting like stupid jackasses. I guess everyone loves someone they can relate too, though. Eventually, merchandise sales began to flatline, which coincidentally coincided with Civil Disobedience’s feud with Carrot Top and Gilbert Gottfried. No further mention needs to be made of what a disaster that turned out to be. Sure, it was good for laughs, but the actually wrestling suffered tremendously. They became categorized as joke wrestlers. You know, the ones that never get taken seriously. Like a Jesse Ramey or Hulk Hogan. Never really recovering from that label, the two had no choice but to play along with the ruse they had unknowingly created. It wasn’t as if they had much choice, with the prodding along they received from the front office in the form of a paycheck getting waved in front of their noses. So they had no choice but to play stupid. Play the fools. Play the fucking jokes. The only problem was they were not stupid. They were not fools. They were not fucking jokes. But that was the image the OSW seemed to want to promote, and that’s the image the fans bought into. Well fuck that. For two guys that base a large part of their existence on their image, going down that road was definitely not in their best interest. At the point they were at, a U-turn was a dire necessity, and that is just what they did with one mighty blow to Hawk’s skull. They slammed the foot on the fucking brake, turned the metaphoric wheel as hard to the right as possible, then skidded a bit before burning out down the road to idealistic redemption. A wise man once said that you have to lose everything before you can gain anything. Seeing that all Seth and Trevor had a few days ago was the adoration of the fans, I’d say it wasn’t too much of a sacrifice for the opportunity to get back a little self respect. That’s why they lashed out, not only destroying what precious few brain cells Hawk had left, but also shattering the public’s watered-down, deluded perception of them. If you want a simile, you could say it was like killing two birds with one chair-shaped stone. One chair shot was all it took to free them from the shackles of public adoration. Now that everyone hates them for smashing in Ace Hawk’s face, they’re free to do as they please. They no longer have to cater to the whims of the ignorant masses. They don’t have to work within the limits of their happy-go-lucky facades. The OSW is wide open to them now, and they’re damn well going to seize this opportunity that lays before them. Their emancipation was long overdue, and you can bet that there’s going to be hell to pay for the torture they had to endure because of it. And now the New School has arrived in OSW. Their first act as free men: Join with the most hated man and most loved woman in OSW, Sebastian Toomes and Amy Campbell, to form the trendiest stable in wrestling history. That is why they’re here after all. To make waves. And what better way to do it than by shoving the undeniable truth in all of your faces. Lesson number one: Civil Disobedience is better than you all.
