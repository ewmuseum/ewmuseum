roleplay the first

14 May 2002

Date: 14th May 2002 He stepped through the doorway and the bar froze. Every eye in the place was looking straight at him, a few of the looks were not ones of recognition, they were just shock at seeing someone of that size, at 6'3'' and 285lbs of sheer muscle he was an impressive site. He reflected that for people in this of all places not to know who he was meant that he had been gone far too long. Most of the people obviously recognised him though, and the looks that were in their eyes were very wide ranging. He turned his head to scan the bar and he saw admiration, some fear but the overwhelming reaction of the clientele of Ground Zero was one of hatred. He wasn't surprised, he had been well informed of the esteem that the people who drank here held Kanaida in by Brandi Brown when he had dropped into Crazy Mohammed's the previous day. Ian Wright had seen a lot of Toronto in the last few days. He had not returned to the city since the uWa has folded. He had no need. But seeing as Slam had brought him to the city he decided to stay in town for a few days and revisit some of his old stomping grounds. The old uWatowers building where he had won so many victories, the Doctor's Den training facility where Doc Hardcore, Kanaida and he had made their plans to dominate the uWa. Wright sometimes wondered whatever happened to the band of misfits that used to hang out there, the Iguana and Echo, not to mention LeBlanc, who fell so far from grace he descended into a parody of himself. Wright didn't suppose it mattered that much. He had also visited the nearby waterfront, where his moment of greatest pride occurred, it was also the moment of Kanaida's greatest shame, that made it even better. He reflects on this for a moment. In the past all he had cared about was competing and winning, if someone got hurt then that was an unfortunate consequence, but he had changed these last couple of years, he began taking joy from his opponents pain. Maybe he really was the sadistic bastard that everyone said he was. But he was not here to deal with his own demons, he had come to this bar to exploit Kanaida's What a pussy that guy is, Wright thought to himself; great wrestler he may be but the way he goes on about his tortured past makes it completely impossible for Wright to have even the slightest bit of respect for him. Nothing was ever his fault, it was always someone else. At least St. Crux always faced him like a man, and Winter's is capable of taking responsibility for his own actions. After what seemed to be an eternity but was in reality only ten seconds Wright let the door swing closed and walked across the floor towards the stairway. He descended into the lower level of the bar where all the wrestling memorabilia was kept. He sat down on a stool at the bar and in the soft but deep tone that he spoke in that belied the rage and hatred that he can spew at times simply said uttered one word to the barman. 'Tiger'. The barman looked at him but didn't move. 15 seconds passed before the manager came over, Wright knew her name was Laura. She told the barman 'It's Ok, I'll serve this guy, you said Tiger right?' Wright nodded, he had heard from Brandi that there might be something going on between Laura and the owner of the place, he had planned to use that to his advantage, maybe get inside Kanaida's head a little by upsetting his girl. Laura placed the bottle down in front of him and Wright was just about to say something when an even better opportunity presented itself. Two of the customers came up to him, one was a small skinny guy, he looked the kind who would never have approached Wright were it not for the presence of his companion. His friend was bigger, 6'5'', obviously worked out a lot, he was comparable in size to Wright. He shaved his head and his beard looked about two days old. He was a real tough guy, Wright liked that, tough guys were always the most fun. It was the bigger guy that spoke first. 'Hey, you're not welcome here.' 'That so,' Wright responded not turning around to face the two men 'in that case why am I drinking a beer that was sold to me?' 'You probably intimidated Laura. I know your type, you're just a bully who gets what he wants by scaring those that are weaker then you. Well intimidate me big man.' Wright took a swig from the bottle, turned around and in one movement grabbed the back of the man's head and slammed his face into the top of the bar. He slumped to the ground, blood covering his face. Wright looks down at him and begins to speak, his voice never changing. 'Maybe you're right, maybe I am a bully. Maybe I'm just like Jack Palance was in 'Shane'...in fact that gives me an idea.' Wright turns his attention to the man's companion. He looks him up and down then breaks the beer bottle on the bar and throws it down at the man's feet. 'Pick it up.' 'I don't wanna pick it up mister, you'll kick the hell out of me' 'Pick up the bottle' 'Look, I don't want any trouble.' 'PICK UP THE BOTTLE.' Wright's voice changes now, when he shouts people notice, his voice booms. The man is visibly scared. The man begins to bend his knees and reaches for the bottle. He stops and looks at Wright who has not moved an inch, he glances at his companion and makes a decision, he stoops down and makes a grab for the bottle. WHAM... Wright has just kicked the man in the face. He grabs him before he has the chance to collapse and throws him javelin style into a wall. The man falls in a heap, he won't be getting up for a while. Wright turns towards Laura and drops some cash on the bar. 'Keep the change, nice place you've got here. Tell your boss I dropped by.' With that Wright turns and walks up the stairs towards the exit.
