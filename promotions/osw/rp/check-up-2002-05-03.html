Date: 5-3-02 “You’ve got to be kidding me right?” Jesse shouts in anger at the nurse who stands before him. Waving a little cup around in the air. The nurse begins to shake her head, “No, I’m not. This is what Dr. Sakauisenkoff wants you to do.” “Well, are you going to help me to the bathroom?” Jesse asks the nurse with a little bit of a smirk on his face. “Sure, why not?” The nurse says with somewhat of a little smirk on her face. She begins to help Jesse up to his feet and then all of a sudden, THUD! “Whoops! My hands must have been a little slippery.” The nurse says as she begins to walk out of the room laughing. “Get your own ass off that floor and go fill that cup.” Jesse’s face begins to fill with rage as he tries to get himself out of the floor. “You dirty, bitch! I should have you fired for this!” Jesse crawls his way over to a walker and pulls himself up on it, slowly; ever so slowly he makes his way up. Seeing the anguish on his face he almost begins to break down in tears. “Why me?! Why Me?!” Jesse begins to yell out at the sky. “Out of all the people in this federation, you had to let me be the one who got squashed by a ton of bricks. Why? What have I ever done to you?” Jesse just lays back on the walker and a tear rolls down his face. Through the door walks a man, obviously Dr. Sakauisenkoff. He continues into the room looking through his flip chart when he bumps into to Jesse and a look of surprise comes over his face. “Woo, Jesse. What happened to you?” “That nurse of yours, she did this to me.” Jesse says as Dr. Sakauisenkoff helps him back to his feet. “Thank you.” Dr. Sakauisenkoff just smiles as Jesse takes his place back on the bed, “So, did you get that cup filled for me?” “Tell me again, why do I have to fill this cup for you?” Jesse asks waving the cup around in Dr. Sakauisenkoff’s face. He just looks at Jesse with a bit of disbelief. “It’s not what I want, but it is standard procedure that we must have you go through a drug test. Somewhat like we have been running the other tests to make sure you haven’t become allergic to anything that we may be wanting to give you to take.” Jesse looks on and nods his head, “Aight.” “Are you going to need any help getting to the bathroom this time?” Dr. Sakauisenkoff asks. “No, I believe I’ll be able to make it there this time, thank you.” Jesse says as he grabs his walker and begins to make his way to his feet. “So, when do you think I’ll be able to get back into the gym and train?” The doctor just shakes his head, “I had things planned for next month.” Jesse begins to laugh, “Sound good to me.” ~The End~
