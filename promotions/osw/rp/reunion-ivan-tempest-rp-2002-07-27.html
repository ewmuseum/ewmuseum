Reunion (Ivan/Tempest rp)

27 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-27-02 It was the last thing he could have expected. El Drago was a possibility. For Drago to come down to the ring and save him from The Think Tank was conceivable…. But Tempest?? It was like he had seen a ghost. A ghost that had haunted him almost every night. At the end of the match when the Think Tank was gone, and they were left alone in the ring staring at one another, Ivan Stanislav knew of only one thing he could do. Walk away. Ivan Stanislav walked hurriedly through the backstage area of the Hershey Park Arena. His first destination: His locker room. He barged into his room where Alexei stood and looked up. “Praporschik?” said Alexei. “Come Alex, we are leaving…NOW!” Ivan screamed, agitated. Alexei stuttered for a minute, “But Ivan, Tempest..I..” “Yes Alexei, I am not blind. We are leaving, immediately!” Ivan growled, “Tell Wang to collect our things and meet me at my hotel, I must leave now!” Ivan nodded and exited the room, leaving Alexei. With quick, heavy footsteps The Russian Bear trudged through the backstage area again, heading straight for the exit. He was visibly shaken, sweating, and perhaps even paler than before. Stanislav continued through the hallway, passing a few technicians and workers before coming upon a corner. As he stepped around it, he found himself face to face with the reason for his agitation. Tempest stood in the hallway and stared up at him nervously in a shiny black bodysuit, almost as if she didn't want to stand out for a change. She gulped and said, 'Ivan?' Ivan lurched to a halt, almost knocking into her. He blinked just once and looked down at her, 'Tempest?' He swallowed, 'I did not know that you were heading this way.' He turned quickly, clumsily, and headed in the opposite direction nervously. 'Ivan wait!' she called trotting after him. 'I was looking for you. Listen we need to talk.' He paused and snorted, keeping his back to her. 'Do we, Tempest?' He turned slowly, his head tilted to the side, ''We' need to talk, as in...you and Ivan?' He turned and stood tall, his hands on his hips, 'You were looking....for me??' Tempest nodded, wringing her hands and said, 'Yes. I... I was. I wanted to tell you that I saw the things that happened lately, with you and the Russian Federation and the Red Army. I don’t know how you feel or anything, but I want to know. And well, I came back because... I didn't want you to be alone.” Ivan blinked and ground his jaw, 'You came back, because you did not want me to be alone?' Ivan shook his head, 'I must be going crazy Tempest, I must be.' He puffed out his chest and gritted his teeth, 'Two months ago, you did not care if I was alone, did you Tempest?? Did you??' He shook his head in disgust, 'You did not hesitate to leave me alone when I asked, when I BEGGED you to stay, why should you return now?' Ivan grumbled angrily, 'What do you expect? Do you expect me to smile and give you great big hug and forget what happened?' He breathed heavier and closed his eyes, 'Nyet...nyet nyet nyet Tempest! I do not forget that easily.' Tempest nodded slightly. 'I know,' she said quietly. 'But... well I was wrong. That's what I wanted to tell you. I mean there's more to it than that, but I was wrong. I thought I didn't matter and that your Red Army and your Mother Russia would take care of you and you'd be fine. I didn't think I was that important. And... that was where I didn't give you enough credit.' Ivan winced, “Did not give me enough credit….did not give me enough credit.” He growled and took a step away from her, giving himself some room, “You know Tempest, little girls do not usually return to see the ‘monsters’ that terrorized them. Is that not what I did? Is that not what you said? That what we had was lie?” He paused and swallowed, “Those words, Tempest, echoed in my mind far longer than you could imagine. And most amazing thing of it all? Do you want to know what is most amazing? Do you?” She stood straight, but not terribly tall compared to Ivan and said, 'What?' Ivan squinted an eye and pointed a finger at her, “You were always the one that never judged me. You were always the only one that appreciated Ivan for Ivan! You never listened to what they said, and you never paid attention to the stories about me. And on that night of Pay Per View, at the drop of a hat, at the literal push of a button, you judged me wholly and threw away ANYTHING that we ever had done, for one solitary belief.” Ivan breathed in deeply through his nose, “I do not have luxury of forgetting, Tempest. I do not have advantage of people accepting me. You ran away when I asked you to stay. I even went to your apartment because I wanted to fix things but you had left. I do not believe it was because you could not pay rent but rather you wished to not see me.” Ivan put his hands on his hips and breathed heavier, “I saw a part of you that I did not know existed, Tempest. Would you like to see part of me that you did not think existed??” Ivan growled, seemingly growing in height. He took one heavy step towards her and paused and swallowed, almost shrinking to his normal, albeit extreme enormity. “Nyet, Tempest, you do not need to be shown a monster.” Tempest shook her head and said, 'Ivan, I was wrong about you. The one lie made me question everything. And I didn't know what to believe and what not to. It hurt so bad that I just ran.' Her shoulders drooped as she added, 'I know it wasn't right. I should have been braver.' Ivan nodded and growled, “Yes, you should have been braver Tempest. You should have been braver. But no, you ran and heard me scream behind you and did not bother to turn around. Was it easy for you to do that? To turn your back on me when I needed you and ignore my cries for help?” Ivan swallowed and shook his head slightly, “I do not know, however, it might be a bit easier, for me. Goodbye. I will at least let you know when I am leaving.” He turned slowly and walked in the opposite direction, his shoulders sagging just a bit. 'No it wasn't easy!' Tempest yelled after him, her eyes welling up in tears. Ivan continued to walk away from her, ignoring her words. El Drago rounded the corner, almost bowling into The Russian Bear. Stanislav looked down angrily, “What are you looking at?” Drago paused and stared at Ivan and murmured, “Not who I expected….” Ivan growled and shoved through Drago and rounded the corner. Drago walked over to Tempest slowly and put his hand on her shoulder. She snuffled and looked up, quickly wiping away the tears as she said, 'It's ok. I didn't think he would just welcome me back with open arms. I mean I hoped, but... Aw, what's the use, of course I hoped he would.' Her head drooped and she snuffled again slightly. Ivan Stanislav never made it to the exit. When he rounded the corner he found a small, secluded room and slinked into it. He walked over to the wall and leaned against it, contemplating what he had just done. He put his hand over his brow and breathed heavily. The door to the room opened slowly and Alexei Ruslan slowly walked in, “Ivan…what is wrong?” Ivan shook his head, not looking at Alexei. Ruslan walked over to him and looked at his enormous Comrade. “Alexei…” Ivan whispered, “She is as beautiful as I remember….” Alexei put a hand on Ivan’s shoulder as he continued to whisper, “So beautiful…..”
