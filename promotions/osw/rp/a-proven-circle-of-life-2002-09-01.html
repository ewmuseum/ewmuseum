A proven circle of life.

1 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 1st, 2002 Still giggling about the look on the faces of Propaganda and his reverend manager, Aimz had taken her attention away from the monitor during Reno's bout against Corg for one moment... One horrifying and crucial moment. All she heard was the unmistakable crunch of a broken bone, having shut out the repetative and annoying voices of Nate, Steven and Robbie, when she shot her eyes back to the screen only to find that it wasn't Reno who'd broken something, not Corg either...but the foot high ape she'd grown almost fond of, the monkey who happened to be not only her boyfriend's best friend, but the one thing he'd seemed to value more than Amy herself. She didn't run out, she didn't budge an inch. She heard the crack of his little spine, she knew the poor little thing wouldn't live more than a few painful moments and she was absolutely no good to Reno out there, though the disturbing near-phobia of Corg that she'd never admit also drove her to stay in her seat. Campbell could only flick the monitor off, choosing not to watch Reno launch into hysterics...Corg would get his for that, she knew it. It was perfectly silent, deathly silent until she heard footsteps running fast towards her from outside. Amy had to hop out of her chair in a flash and prepare for a fight behind the door, or a hysterical boyfriend...it'd be selfish to sit there mourning for something she'd only known a few months, Aimz knew that. So instead, she screwed her eyes shut with half disgust, half from sheer upset when Reno ran in, clutching the broken and lifeless little body of his best friend tight to his chest. ''Reno...'' Was all she could manage to say, and even that drew a shiver into her voice. He didn't even know she was in there, he didn't care...that much was obvious. By the time Amy opened her eyes and reached him, he'd set the little monkey down on the bottom shelf of his locker, as if sheltered in a little tome, and sobbed ZaC's name over his lifeless form as if it would bring him back. Reno wasn't praying, he was convinced God wouldn't help him, only harm him. This would only make him hate religion in any form that much more...it would be the ultimate upset, in his mind, that God had let that happen. ''You can't...'' Amy started on instinct, then re-thought the statement. ''Don't...don't be upset...'' There are situations where stupid comments can be laughed at and joked about, then there are the situations where a thoughtless remark in attempted comfort is a brutal mistake... Guess which kind of situation this is? Campbell cringed at her own words during that crucial time and attempted to make up for them by not opening her mouth again, but reaching a hand out to cling onto Reno's shoulder reassuringly... Another mistake. He swung around, twisting her arm painfully before he shoved her against the wall, tears rolling unchecked down both their cheeks at this point. ''Don't be upset, huh?!'' When Amy came forward, he shoved her back again. ''Don't be UPSET?! How the fuck would you like it if Corg snapped my neck out there, you fucking whore?!'' She tried to understand...tried very, very hard, but this was irrational. ''Reno...'' Aimz still tried to sooth him amidst the abusive commotion. ''...he was just a monkey, come on...don't be like this.'' ''He was just a monkey and you're just a fucking woman, get the fuck out of my sight!'' 'Just a woman'... ''Please, don't start this crazy shi--'' Amy was abruptly cut short by a fist colliding with her cheek and a shout that was broken by a sob from Davis as he shoved her out the half-open door into the hallway. ''OUT!'' Falling against the wall across from it, Aimz got a door slammed shut right before her yet again... This was life to her; Get close, get cast away and repeat the process next time you love or dream. Career, relationships...no matter what: When she gets close to something, she gets kicked away from it. ((Note: You'll have to excuse me for how short this was, I just felt like if I made it any longer, I'd bore you since I'm not exactly in much of a mindset to be writing really emotional things right now.:( Anywho, this basically writes Reno out of the 'storyline' as needed.))
