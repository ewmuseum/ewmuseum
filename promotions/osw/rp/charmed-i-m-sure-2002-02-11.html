Charmed, I'm Sure

11 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/11/02 'Will you stop doing that?!' said the sing song voice of Tempest from outside the camera's view. The slender Japanese woman she was speaking to, stopped in the middle of digging through her suitcase and said, 'Tempest I am not letting you go out with that monster in... in this!' She held up a tiny black lace tank top. A sigh came from the nearby closet and Tempest said, 'Yes mother. I'm trying to have a good time on this trip Miyuki, there's gotta be some cool dance clubs down there I can hit.' Miyuki, shook her head vigorously and said, 'Tempest, the Congo is a war zone! There's nothing fun going on there, trust me.' 'But how can that be? I mean they even have a dance named after the place, it's gotta rock!' Tempest said plaintively. Miyuki rolled her eyes and said, 'That's the Conga, not the Congo. And I'm betting you're not gonna find Ivan Stanislav much of a dance partner.' 'You never know,' said Tempest as the face belonging to the voice peeped around the closet door. She smiled brightly, the corners of her delicate mouth turning up with a note of mischief as her crystal blue eyes sparkled from beneath a tumble of coal black locks. 'Take someone one out of their environment and get them to relax, they can be a whole 'nother person. And if anyone can get him to relax, you know I can.' She giggled and returned to rooting through the closet. She tossed something glittery at Miyuki and said, 'How's this?' Miyuki unfolded the slinky short dress and growled slightly as she said, 'No! That is definitely not what you should wear. Tempest you should wear fatigues to this place and boots too! Even if it isn't a war zone, you do not want someone like Stanislav taking a liking to you.' No?' Tempest said and peered out again, this time with a vaguely disappointed expression. 'Why not?' 'Why not? Why not?!' Miyuki squawked, 'Tempest, the man has had the KGB trailing you for a week now. 'Well... maybe he's just curious and ... maybe even a little insecure,' Tempest offered sheepishly. Miyuki leapt in front of her and her arms flailed as she said, 'He's obsessive, out of touch with reality and damn near certifiable! This is not the time for your well known ability to give people the benefit of the doubt. You want the KGB falling out of every tree around you?' Tempest poked her head into view and giggled as she said, 'C'mon Miyu, you gotta admit watching that Ruslan guy cursing in Russian, stuck in all that plastic landscaping was pretty funny.' A smile broke through Miyuki's expression and she shrugged as she said, 'Ok... ok yeah that was pretty funny. But still, you know what I mean. It's not gonna be entertaining for long.' Tempest strode out of the closet in a pair of reflective denim jeans and a tight one shouldered black blouse clinging to her lithe form. She chuckled and tossed another few items into her suitcase and said, 'Miyuki c'mon, I can always find a way to be entertained. I have fun no matter what and I can certainly find ways to in this instance. Be positive about it!' Miyuki shook her head with a wan smile and said, 'Ok, I'm positive that you're out of your mind.' Tempest shrugged and said, 'This is news? You've been managing me for almost a year now and if that tour of Japan didn't show you, then I dunno what will.' 'This is going a long way toward it,' said Miyuki with a smile. She folded her arms and asked, 'What possessed you to agree to this Dating Game thing anyway? It's not like that little black book of yours isn't pretty full already.' Tempest wafted her palms and said, 'Why else? I was bored.' They both laughed and Miyuki said, 'I should have known.' 'Besides,' Tempest said, with a little smile, 'There's something weirdly charming about a guy so committed to his ideals that he hasn't noticed the rest of the world abandoning them.' Miyuki goggled at her companion a moment and then spluttered, 'Charming? You think that walking Berlin Wall is charming?!' She stalked out of the room leaving Tempest looking mystified and then returned with a large photo which she thrust into Tempest's hands. Tempest looked and an expression of dismay came over her face as Miyuki pointed agitatedly and said, 'That is seven foot one, four hundred pounds of everything in the world that is not charming! If he falls on you by accident, you'll be a little smudge on the sidewalk.' Tempest gulped and said quietly, 'I hope he turns out to be a gentleman.' Miyuki rolled her eyes and said, 'Agreed, but why do you mention it?' 'Well,' Tempest said, handing the picture back, 'I don't think I have a purse that I can fit an elephant gun into inconspicuously.' Miyuki's expression instantly cracked and she laughed along with Tempest. Tempest stood and hugged her friend and manager as she said, 'Don't worry I'll be fine. I'm gonna go and have a great time. I always do.' She smirked just a little and added, 'I'll have fun and so will Ivan... even if it kills him.'
