Date: I dunno 'WHAT DO YOU MEAN, I DON'T MAKE A CONVINCING FACE?' The writer cringes, Toomes holding him by his collar, screaming into his face. 'I'm sorry, Sebastian, but you're too typecast as a Heel to be really considered a fan favorite!' the Senior Writer said, going back to his table for a smoke. Toomes sighed and ran his fingers through his own, freshly dyed hair. 'I can't believe this. I get the crap beat out of me by Armour, I come back and do a Face promo, just getting off the ground with this whole Face turn, and already, the smarks are saying I'm 'not a very good face', or some other crap about how I'm a wanna-be Face! This is bunk!' The writer shrugs, taking a drag off a cigarette. 'Well, shit, they're smarks. They think they know it all, and they don't wanna give anyone a chance. What do you want?' 'Yo...' The writer's room opens up to a nightclub set behind itas a familiar song begins over the PA. 'I'll tell you what I want, what I really really want!' Toomes yells. The writer shrugs and says 'So tell me what you want, what you really really want...' Toomes looks at the writer, annoyed, and replies 'I'll tell you what I want, what I really really want!' The writer sighs. 'So tell me what you want, what you really really want!' Toomes, and a troup of dancing girls who inexplicably pop out of nowhere, calls out 'I wanna!' 'I wanna!' 'I wanna!' 'I wanna!' 'I wanna really really really wanna beacceptedbythewrestlingfansasaface ah!' Toomes says, halfway out of breath. Toomes walks further into the nightclub set while the smartly dressed dancing girls follow him. 'If you want my future forget my past!' One of the dancing girls, we'll call her... Amy Spice, adds 'If you wanna accept his Face Turn, better make it fast!' Another dancing girl... we'll call her... Leah Spice, continues 'Now don't go judging... without sufficent time!Wait another couple of weeks and he'll be a Face that's fine!' Toomes gets on a platform in the nightclub, doing a little be-bop dance, ala Bel Biv Devo circa 1992. 'I'll tell you what I want, what I really really want!' Amy Spice questions him intently. 'So tell me what you want, what you really really want!' 'I wanna, I wanna, I wanna, I wanna, I wanna really really really wanna justbeacceptedbythewrestlingfansasaface ah!'. Toomes yells, again almost out of breath as the three dance into the chorus. 'If you wanna watch me wrestle, you gotta let me turn Face, OSW's full of bad guys, but heels get old fast...' The three continue to dance in the nightclub, surrounded by people who look strangely like old Faces from PCW... We see John Hull, Big Alan Steele, Duke Williams, White Mexican, Miyagawa... 'If you wanna watch my program, you have got to give... Don't piss me off and make me hurt you, I'm a Face and that's the way it is...' Amy Spice gets on the dancefloor and busts some crazy 1996 dance moves. 'What do you think about that?' Toomes nods, forlornly., wiping away a tear 'Now you know how I feel...' Leah Spice continues. 'Say you can handle Face Turns... are you for real?' Toomes shakes his groove thing, picking up where Leah Spice left off. 'So don't be hasty, let me give this Face Turn a try... If you really it then I'll...cry!' Toomes breaks down in tears. However, he fights back the tears, wiping them away to continue 'Yo I'll tell you what I want, what I really really want!' The Duke Williams look-a-like in the crowd yells in a redneck voice 'So tell us what you want, what you really really want!' 'I wanna, I wanna, I wanna, I wanna, I wanna really,really really wanna beacceptedbythewrestlingfansasaface ah!' Suddenly, there is a sound of a record scratching and the lights in the little nightclub come on. The camera pans over to see Jane Reagan, fresh out of a meeting. She looks incredulously at the idiocy-in-progress. 'STOP! Goddamnit! What kind of shit is this, Sebastian? First it's dancing dildoes, now you're a Spice Girl?' she screams. Toomes coughs quietly. 'Um, actually, I'm a Spice Face.' 'GAAAH! EVERYONE! OUT! OUT! OUT!' Jane screams. Amy and Leah Spice walk past Toomes, after petting his head affectionatly. 'Bye Kitty...' they murmur quietly as they walk off the set. Soon, after everyone has filed out... Jane looks at Toomes. 'Christ, what do you care if the smarks don't like your Face turn? Like they matter?' 'I know, I know... but still... They didn't even give me a week, Jane!' Toomes starts sniffling. 'Awww, baby...' She takes his head and hugs him, mashing her breasts into his face, and all we hear are muffled cries. 'It'll be okay, Sebastian... I won't let those mean smarks talk bad about your Face turn...' The camera fades to black as we hear Jane soothing Sebastian's feelings.
