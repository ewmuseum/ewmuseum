Requiem

27 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-27-02 My heart beat is pounding profusely in my ears. I hear every pulse, every tick, every rush. Acid tears burning, etching a permanent pain within my soul. Leaving a burdensome scar. I can't find a way out. I feel trapped..I can't see where I'm going, everything's so blank. The elaboration gone, debating on a prolonged death. For I know that it awaits me.. My mind. My being. My eternity. The feeling of descending a never ending, downward spiral takes hold. It grasps my attention. Needle like sensations course through my body, numbing every part of me. I slowly start to lose feeling, I am jaded. Nothing. The sound of a rapid, violent, pulsating heart slowly begins to diminish. everything grows silent and I await for what I know is to come. I had just told Cameron of the news, I'm pregnant. Three tests litter the trash and all remain the same in their verdict. He is silent for a long while, his brows deeply furrowed, hiding his darkened oculars. I know something is brewing inside of him, something that I can feel from across the room where I sit. Things are always the quietest before the storm. I cannot sense his feelings, all I can do is try and shield myself as in a fluid movement he is upon me. The couch almost buckles under his sheer animosity. I cry out as I am thrown to the floor, lifted and then slapped hard across the face. My head jerks to the side, but I am thrown again, this time against the nearest wall. I hit hard, my body actually bucking upward and arching upon impact. I slump down in to the floor, hiding my body from the raining kicks and blows from above. I try to cover my stomach as much as possible but I feel the shots getting past my defensive arms. I can already feel the warm blood pooling between my thighs and dripping on to the floor. I cry, tears flowing down my face as I am jerked in to a standing position. He proceeds to slam his boot in to my stomach, my eyes going wide as the breath is knocked out of me. I feel my knees giving out below me, my world becoming fuzzy, my vision blurring and finally fading. My pain is great as I hit the floor, falling face first in to the wood panel. My stomach slams in to the wood as well, crushing inside of me what was to come. The blood seeps from inside of me to the outside of me and now covers me on the floor. I have passed out and he has left, leaving me to whatever fate has planned. Life. Is. Gone. My eyes flutter open slowly to reveal a bright light. For a moment I believe I am headed toward my final destination but when voices filter in I know I am just simply awake. Doctors hover over me, a helping nurse to his side. 'Good morning Cally..' His tone was that of a sad man, one who bore bad news and couldn't, or rather just didn't want to reveal. I brace myself until I whimper because of the pain in my stomach and sides. 'A friend brought you in last night. You were badly hurt.. Assault was it? I am afraid with all of the injuries.' He stops suddenly, long enough for a voice I did not recognize as mine to break the silence. 'My baby?..What about my baby?' His brows furrow as he bites down on his tongue for a moment and then looks to me with a very serious expression. 'I'm sorry, we were not able to save your baby. We already operated,..' I feel hot tears well up in my dry eyes, but I hold them back and swallow down my hurt. I can hear my heart breaking in to a million pieces, crashing in to the pit of my empty soul. He is silent, the nurse is silent, and I am silent..So silent that my ears want to bleed from it. My one hope, my one chance, shattered along with my entire world. Cameron won, he won since that night I crawled back to him and he knows it. Something inside of me finally breaks down, some chemical is released and I sit upright in that bed. The stitches rip open, blood soaking the clean white sheets. I scream, my strained voice bouncing off of the walls of the confined room. The Doctor pushes me back, holding me down but I fight back this time, sending him reeling in to the IV machine. I feel the needle being jerked from my vein but I continue my struggle. I see more nurses, more Doctors filing into my room. The restraints are strapped around my wrists and my ankles, holding me to that bed. I cry out, arching upward and scream loudly. My voice fades out, their voices fade out..All I hear is the words echoing. Why have you forsaken me? In your arms forsaken me? In your thoughts forsaken me? I cry when Angels deserve to die..
