Out of Control

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The darkened alleyways are filled with steam and smoke rising from the sewers below. A lone siren sounds far in the distance; a dog barks in response. The lucid flow of traffic hums in the background, filling the cool night air with substance. A single flickering lamplight hangs from a broken down warehouse wall, trying its best to stay alive despite its old, neglected body. The windows in this alley have been boarded up. Garbage litters the black, stained pavement below. A cool breeze funnels from the street a few hundred yards behind, channeling itself through rustling paper and creaking wood. Kimbusa had never seen a place like this before. The five hundred pound mammoth stands alone in the center of this alley, now stopped entirely in awe of the alley's decaying walls. Red brick has turned brown from a constant exhaust haze, window panes broken years ago and sealed by cobwebs. Kimbusa's traditional attire is long gone. He now stands mightily in a long black trenchcoat, sealed tightly over his girth. A black fedora hat rests over his jet black hair, completing Kimbusa's new film-noir motif. Unshaven now for several days, Kimbusa slowly begins to stroke the thin beard now resting on his chin. The sumo warrior finally regains his wits and finds the courage to move on, unintimidated by the darkness ahead. Kimbusa reaches the end of a large abandoned warehouse, and turns back to look at the street behind him. Cars rush by in a hurry, whirring lightly to reassure Kimbusa that he is not far from the real world. But this in itself is an entirely new realm to the man from Japan. Kimbusa turns again and looks ahead. To his left sits a lone black dumptster, saturated with dirt and grime and producing an odor so foul that Kimbusa turns up his nose. To his right is another passageway between the buildings; absolute darkness beyond. Kimbusa looks straight ahead and begins to walk, wisely choosing the rout that appears to lead to another populated street. After only a few steps, a loud rustling noise begins behind the big man. Kimbusa calmly turns to look behind him, and sees a large pile of old newspapers next to the dumpster. After a moment, a face appears from beneath the rubbage. An old man appears from beneath the garbage, and rolls over to his side on the filthy pavement. His face is unshaven and dirty, his skin black either from natural pigment or merely the grime he is coated in. His torso is covered by an old dirty tanktop, once white but now yellow and brown from the suit. Kimbusa's eyes begin to tremble slightly. After having been in the company of a beauty like Bethany Yamoto in his quiet mountain home, Kimbusa begins to grow angry at the analysis of his new situation. He now finds himself in the company of one of the lowest cretins he could find, lost in the dark alleys of some unknown metropolis in a confusing new land. 'Spare a dollar?' The old man speaks with a trembling voice, noticeably concerned at the sight of such a mammoth of a man. Kimbusa turns to face the man completely, and pulls the hat from his head. Kimbusa begins to study the man carefully, causing a great deal of discomfort for the old bum. 'Hey, buddy, I don't want any trouble here...' Kimbusa continues to approach the old man, who begins to cower beneath his home of newspapers and old periodicals. Kimbusa finally stops and slides back against the warehouse wall, slowly moving downward until he seats himself right on the disgusting pavement. The old man seems confused at Kimbusa's presence, and yet more confused at Kimbusa's sudden look of satisfaction. 'I have found what I was looking for.' Kimbusa's voice startles the old man even more, and the man begins to creep back towards the back of the dumpster in case he needs a speedy escape. But Kimbusa simply places his hat comfortably over his face, blocking out the light, and smiles beneath its cover. He had, indeed, found what he was looking for... His traditional paradise home was destroyed, Bethany was angry and hurt. He had no place to stay, he had no home to go to. But this place harbored the same anger and resentment that Kimbusa did. He had truly found what he was looking for... An escape......
