Bar Wars?

15 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 15, 2002 Kanaida poured himself another glass of draft, emptying the pitcher. He would have to send the dumbwaiter back down for another.. but it could most certainly wait for a moment. With a sigh, he stood and walked over to the ledge, and sat overlooking the harbor. How had things gotten so out of control so quickly? They had certainly not gone according to plan. He closed his eyes, and soaked up the sun for a moment. Even Ground Zero was no longer a refuge for him. Normally, any wrestling incidents in Toronto naturally gravitated to Mohammed's Bar and Grill, across from the former UWA Towers. But last night, Ian Wright had walked into Kanaida's own bar, and beaten up two of the regulars, putting him in the hospital. From watching the security tapes, Kanaida had been able to study his former partner once more. Kanaida took a slug of his beer, and chuckled in spite of himself.. Wright had actually done him a favor this time. Spike and Jackson had been causing trouble for a few weeks, and usually got at their worst during PPV's.. the idiots thought that they had what it took to wrestle. From what Laura had told him, they weren't quite so cocky anymore. The thought of his bar manager made Kanaida shake his head.. her hero worship of him was getting out of control. It was flattering, to be certain, but she just wasn't his type. The past few years, he preferred his women to be more dangerous, and certainly wasn't looking for the kind of long-term relationship that she was. So it was better to keep her at arm's length, as he kept everyone in his life. Finishing the beer, he got up from his contemplation of the harbor, and began to pace the roof. Even his former friends were kept so far out of his life that they didn't know how to deal with him.. or he with them. Jesus St Crux was a good man. An honorable opponent, and a former friend. But now.. hell. Kanaida shook his head. The St Crux situation had been his own fault, of course. If he'd simply asked him for a match and been up front about it, Jesus would have given him one, of course. But no, not the Northern Wonder. He simply couldn't do things the easy way. And so when he'd found out that he had no match on his first OSW card, and that Jesus was defending the UWA title? Snap. Stopping at the dumbwaiter, he opened the door and put into the small elevator his empty pitcher of beer, but not his glass. That simple yet eloquent statement would quickly net him more Moosehead. That done, he returned to his thoughts. And as a chill wind caught him, his thoughts naturally turned to Winters. Kanaida closed his eyes, and turned his head to the sun.. of course. Winters was the true cause of all this. Back when the kid had joined the UWA, Kanaida had taken him under his wing, shown him the ropes, and gotten him started. He gave him every opportunity to learn and get ahead.. And in thanks, when Kanaida had been forced to retire, Winters not only took over Highly Unstable, but started calling himself the 'New' Northern Wonder. From that day forth, Winters seemed to do everything humanly possible to take the name and legacy of his mentor, and steal it for himself. There was little respect in the pup.. rumor even had it that he considered himself to have surpassed Kanaida's every deed. 'It didn't have to be like this... none of it did.' he breathed into the wind. But then he set his jaw, and opened his eyes. If anyone had been on the roof with him, they would have seen a look of determination on his face. 'No, it didn't have to be like this.. but it is. So to make the best of it, I've got to deal with the minor business that's getting in the way, and get right down to claiming what's mine.. and to hell with anyone who gets in my way.' And from behind him, the dumbwaiter clicked, signaling another pitcher of draft. With a small smile, Kanaida turned and poured himself another glass. 'A toast' he said to himself. 'To getting what's mine, and getting it over with.. and to making a fresh start, on top of OSW. Hell, that's what it's all about.. a toast to ASCENSION...' With a smile, he took another drink, and stared into the sunset.
