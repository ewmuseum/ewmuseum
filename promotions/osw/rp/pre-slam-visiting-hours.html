Pre-Slam visiting hours!

2002-03-24

Amy 'Aimz' Campbell

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Note: It's 'That time of the month'...I felt like writing, so I did.) Hot on the heels after that last crapalicious pimp encounter, OSW's freshly picked female was sulking in her Orlando hotel room... Why? She had spent the better part of the last few days searching for the TD Waterhouse Arena and still hadn't quite found it...she was growing weary, restless in the company of her lively Samoan tag partner, and just wanted to lay back for a while with people she knew a little bit better. Unfortunately enough, none of those people were currently in Orlando. Her half-brother, Bobby Stormbringer, obviously came to mind...but she didn't know him as well as she should have, and wasn't in any kind of mood to 'catch up' with him, so that was temporarily out of the question... She could always call someone...but a jacked-up hotel phone bill wasn't exactly the same as human companionship. Rent-A-Hero was frequently in Florida...maybe she could invite him over? Wait, no...too tempting. Speaking of RAH, his return party was fast approaching, and Amy would be there...so would Jason. This posed a problem... What problem? She had left the tandem of her own will, she didn't want any further part in that...but right after, almost out of rage she found Jason, immediately made a date and...well...the rest is rather colorful history. The only problem is that Aimz, for some reason, had what you could call a 'crush' on Rent-A-Hero, though he probably wasn't aware...and this was a major deal, since Amy was known to be one of the more spontaneous people in this lifetime. Her only fear, aside from an awfully ackward situation when they meet again, is that she'll most-likely jump Rentie the moment she gets him alone again. So what will she do? God only knows...she's gotta show up, though...she promised Jeremy and basically everyone else, for that matter. So, pondering this, and putting the thoughts of a self-imposed chastity belt OUT of her mind, Amy wandered over to the oak night table set beside the double bed...grabbing her little Nokia cell phone, the one with the happy face snap-on casing and dialing a number swiftly with her thumb. After a few moments, a disappointed frown crossed her face...she got nothing more than an answering machine on the other line, obviously. But still, she spoke... ''Uhhhh...hey.......Rentie..........'' She swallowed hard, this telephone tag proved to be quite difficult. ''...this is...umm...well...Amy and...just wondering.....ah fuck it. I'm gonna be at that party thing, I guess I'll talk to you then.'' Well now that she made a fool of her self, and recorded it, Aimz wasn't very pleased with herself...that much went without saying. But enough of Rent-A-Hero, it was Saturday night! Slam was coming up in mere hours...she had MUCH more important things to think about then what was happening between she and a spandex-clad phsyciatric ward reject. Tonight she had promised Timo victory, and she didn't like lying...Toomes might be a challenge, but nothing two people couldn't overcome with a little bit of work... Damnit! Only a few moments into her quiet thoughts, Amy could hear the door creak open. Seconds later, a tall, redheaded man stepped in...he frowned when he caught the sight of her sitting in the corner , arms folded around her knees. Yup, it was Bobby Stormbringer...big HALF-brother extraordinaire. ''Hey...why so sullen?'' He stood over her, towering above.........this is why she didn't like tall people. ''Meh...'' Amy looked up.......up....WAY up to Stormbringer for a moment, her eyes brightening when she realized that this was exactly what she needed...just to have a casual conversation. ''Just a lot on my mind, I guess.'' ''Like...?'' What could she trust him to know? He was her blood, but did she want to give away things that she didn't even admit to herself? Meh...screw that, she'd just tell him...and did. ''Well...you know my best friend, Bre...right?'' ''Yeah...'' ''She's been calling a lot while I've been gone, as expected...but she's basically been reporting.'' Bobby raised his eyebrows, wrinkling his forehead. ''...I don't follow.'' ''Reports on Jason and what he's been doing. His actions lead her to beleive that he's fucking around behind my back...'' She sighed. ''...and I am NOT impressed.'' ''You shouldn't be! Want me to take him out?'' Amy smiled a moment, but quickly had to correct her thoughts. ''What? No!!! No no...we're not sure what he's doing quite yet. But I promise you, if he's cheating...he's gonna have ME to deal with, along with my closest friends...and I don't think Byron's impressed either, so he might find his best buddy joining the fight.'' Stormbringer leaned against the wall, sliding down next to his little sister. ''Alright, but if you need ANY help, just gimmie a call...got that?'' ''Beleive me, I will...but it's like I said: with Jason, I doubt I'd need any assistance.'' They sat in silence for a moment, each pondering what could be a very interesting, and peculiar end to a chapter in Amy's age-old lovelife. ''So...'' He smiled again and teased a bit with his next question, changing the subject... ''What's with you and Rent-A-Hero now?'' And there it was...finally a smile that nobody had seen since tenth grade was drawn from Aimz, Breanne would have been proud. She almost giggled when answering... ''Heh...nothing!'' ''Yeah...but you've still got it for him, I can tell. When I told you he was coming back, you didn't want to look too happy...but I've never seen anybody try so hard to hide a smile, it looked like your face could've fallen off any second!'' He laughed, jostling his younger half-sister a little...though he was rather serious for the most part. ''Shut up !'' So there was a little of that schoolgirl left in the mini menace yet! But Bobby needed to change the subject again, before Amy got defensive...he knew she didn't like showing people much emotion. ''Alright...well what about this match tonight? I'm gonna try to make it to watch you kick ass!'' ''There won't be much ass-kicking if Sebastian's bitches stick their noses into things.'' He laughed. ''You're afraid of Hostyle and Bones?!'' ''Well I'm sure as fuck afraid of Bones, have you seen the size of that guy?'' Aimz gulped, obviously intimidated to a degree. ''But Hostyle isn't a problem...he'll be too busy ''Thuggin' it up'' in his Mariah Carey shorts and cute little halter-top.''' ''Man, that guy's a walking joke...Reagan doesn't even wear shorts that girlie.'' Stormbringer decided to get a few laughs, immitating a rather 'Chuck and Billy' speaker... ''And the top! I th ju th th ooooo hot!'' He flicked his right wrist, letting his hand and fingers dangle in traditional fag style as he watched Amy put her head to the ground, laughing as hard as he's ever seen her. Can't get enough of the cheesy homosexuality jokes! I wonder if Hostyle has a bracelet with 'Glen Pierce' on the inscription............doodeeedooo... Ahem... ''So yeah...Drew's no problem, Bones is pretty fucked up...as far as Toomes goes, Timo's got him...this should work.'' ''If it doesn't, you know I'll be there...'' The plot thickened as we faded from the family scene...
