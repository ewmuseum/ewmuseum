The Bullshit Ends. Insanity Begins.

26 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/26/02 The Bullshit Ends.. Camera comes in to Michael Dame sitting at the end of a dining room table. The room is dark, and only lit by a candle light sitting at the middle of the table. He eats..slowly...coldly...darkly.. Then he reaches for his glass. A tall wine appealing glass, but does not grab it. He hovers his hand around it, no more then a centimeter from touching the cold glass......then.....he clenches.....incredibly hard, breaking the glass in his hand. With a adrenaline rush he throws the same arm through his food and plate, sending it all to the floor. Blood flies in a single strand towards the floor, making a slapping noise as it hits the floor. Michaels hand was hurt. And hurt badly, but he didn't feel it. Instead he went on like nothing happened. Michael walks into the kitchen, and opens the garbage as if he's going to throw something out, but he has nothing in his hand. Suddenly he rips the garbage out, with such velocity it flies straight towards the ceiling ripping in shreds, yet Michael doesn't move. He stands there with no remorse for himself. He continues out of the kitchen and down the hall, heading into a bathroom. He begins the water as he turns and spits in the toilet bowl. A bit of spit sits on his lip, yet he doesn't wipe it away. He takes off his shirt, and bends down towards the water, as if washing his face. Just before hitting his hands into the water, he grasps the side of the sink. He lifts his head slowly , until he can see his face in the mirror.......... 'On Saturday...You will see a side of Dame nobody has ever seen before. A side of human insanity, that has not yet been classified by the most ingenious doctores, a side of someone respected by a T-Rex... you my friend, will see something worth avoiding, and avoiding to save your life...' ...dame pauses.... '...and no, not you Mr. Bones, I'm not talking about what you'll see....I'm talking about my own eyes....I will see a side of me, that I have never seen!...end of.....story.' Camera fades to black
