Locker Room Look-Around [Continued from yesterday]

4 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4.4.02 A door. Made of wood. As usual. It had a sign on it. “Locker Room 2”. Nudge the door. It swings open. Dead ahead, there is some benches, made of steel, with steel pegs above them. On the walls, cupboards sat above the seats. More wood plated the walls of the room as 3 men stood with their backs to the camera, looking into the shower area. “Ah, these facilities will have to do Wang,” pronounced one of the taller men in a broad German accent, “Yes, Qwieg, they seem to be fine. Andweas, what do you think,” replied a short Chinese voice, “They are sufficient for us, I believe,” boomed the tallest of the men, his Turkish roots protruding through his voice. “That is qwuite good, wouldn’t you say Qwieg,” “Yes. I am pleased that at least one of the venues we are set to visit is of German standard. I think I shall need to check out the ring area also. Heresian Bloodlust are going to receive the punishment they so deserve and I don’t think the ringside area is big enough to contain the hurting me and Walczak are going to give them,” said Krieg, wearing a long black jacket, dark jeans, military boots, a black jumper and a skip cap bearing the German flag, looking to be an item of Krieg merchandise, “Krieg, I don’t think beating the living daylights out of Hessian and Azrail Cain is going to get you anywhere besides gaining tag team title shots and a bad reputation,” suggests Andreas Odessa, Krieg’s loyal manager and non-Red Army associate, Looking at him with questionable looks on their faces, Krieg and Wang turn to each other after looking at Odessa and exchange confused glances, “Andreas, my friend: I’m out to gain title shots and a bad reputation, can’t you see that,” “Yes, of course Michel, but why would you want to do that when you could earn title shots by straight wrestling,” questions Odessa, a man untainted by the Red Army’s ways, “Because, I wa...Ah, nevermind Andreas. You don’t need to understand our motives, you just need to recognise our achievements, that’s all, my friend,” says Krieg, flashing a smile at his associate, a rare gesture from Krieg, Wang interupts Krieg and Andreas’ friendly moment, suggesting their next move, “Qwieg, Andreas, how about we check out this wingside area? Walczak will be here shortly and we want to have some ideas weady for him, wight,” “For sure, Wang. Andreas, let us go to the arena area,” requests the German as his Turkish and Chinese counterparts follow him out of the room, Odessa showing his manners by allowing Wang to walk out in front of him with a wave of his massive arm. Ducking under the door frame, Krieg and Odessa emerge from the room, Wang only visible when he pops his head out from behind Krieg to see where they’re going. Heading down the corridor, Krieg approaches a set of double door, a thick chain linking between the two steel handles. Grabbing the chain with his hand and jolting it, Krieg turns to his buddies, “Comrades, it appears the arena is locked down for the evening. What shall we do,” “Ah Qwieg, you are so naive. It is not shut, it is merely not physically open at the moment,” “But doesn’t that mean it’s clo...” says Odessa, quickly cut off by Wang, “No Andreas. Look, Michel, yank the chain off the door,” Odessa quickly chirps in, “Michel, you would be breaking the law doing that,” Flashing him a quick growl, Wang discourages Odessa from interrupting again before turning back to Krieg, “Now Michel, pull,” yells Wang, With one slight tug, the steel chain fires off of the door and lies on the floor as Odessa’s face reflects his disapproval, a look of surprise and shook spread across his face. “That is my man,” says Wang, patting the small of Krieg’s back seeing as he can’t reach up any further, “Ah, it was nothing,” responds Michel with a hint of pride, “Let’s go no, Qwieg,” motions Wang, his hands pushing Krieg’s back towards the arena through the doors, All three men begin to walk into the semi-lit arena, the stand-by lights the only ones left on. Looking around like they did outside, Krieg and Wang observe the massive arena as Odessa twiddles his thumbs, walking further into the arena as he does. “Ah, this is a fine arena, Wang. Not as fine as venues such as the International Stadium in Munich, but it will do for the OSW, do you not think,” “Yes, the Swan Centre is a fine place, Qwieg. I only hope that it lives up to the expectations of Tom. He is a very demanding man, you know. When we were back in PC...” “Ah! And I am not, Wang? What are you trying to say,” questions an Angry Krieg, “Ah...Emm...No, no Qwieg. I’m not saying Walczak has better opinions. I was just saying that he expects highly of places, as do you,” responds Wang, covering his tracks quickly, “Ah, I am sorry Wang. I am still annoyed after Meanstreak and 3B’s attack on the Red Army last week. It is nothing personal, Wang,” says Krieg, extending his hand to Wang in a guesture of apology, Both men shakes hands as they turn back to the arena and talk amongst themselves, awaiting the arrival of their Red Army cohort Tom Walczak. The scene once again fading out to a familiar “TO BE CONTINUED” message.
