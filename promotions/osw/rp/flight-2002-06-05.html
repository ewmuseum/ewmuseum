Flight

5 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-5-02 Totally normal feelings that run through your head as you continuously lie back in your bed and try to sleep. Tossing and turning as you try to find a way to sleep, but the reality of the matter is that you cannot find your peace. The throbbing of your bruises and your pains, they come and they go as you come and go with your sanity. The slow silence of the flight that carries you away after your visit with the trainer, which didn’t turn out to be the most pleasant of news to your ears. The pain that your job brings and the problems that it causes with your family are only a portion of the problems. After having to deal with so much shit you finally realize that you can’t deal with it any longer and then the problems become worse. Not only do you know have to coop with the long traveling by yourself, but it’s a new life. You have to start over from scratch, which is one of the hardest things in the business. Getting acquainted with the management and adapting to the environment. Coming from one league and class of wrestling that may have looked upon you as a God in the ring, while in this new league you have been all, but given your decency. To have to deal with the constant taunts from not only the management, but the ring crew, the announce team, and even the people in the backstage area who call you friend to your face, but feel no harm is made when they too join in on the barrage of on coming taunts. To one’s self esteem and ego this does nothing, but down grade the ability of your talent. As you begin to question yourself on these somewhat and almost oblivious questions. You know the true answer deep down in your heart to all, but no matter how much you try to speak out, you’re only cut down that much more. Still lost and trying to find the true answers to the problems that you don’t have, because they only lie within your mind so long as you let these people keep you down. The only question that should hinder your mind is, how much longer are you going to let these people keep you down? Faith in yourself and an egger determination are all you need to continue on in your long journey. “Mr. Ramey, may I ask what you are doing?” A voice asks from a short distance away as we find ourselves on private jet. A young woman, most likely the personal flight attendant on Jesse’s jet is standing in front of him as he looks up from his laptop. Jesse looks up with a smile as he pushes his glasses atop his head, “Have a seat my dear and I shall tell you just what it is that I am doing.” The women takes a seat in front of Jesse and crosses her legs, then looks up at Jesse with enthusiasm. “Well?” “Well, you see, in my free time I’ve been working on a sort of pamphlet for the introductory level and up and coming wrestling stars out there. Just to give them a sort of feedback on how life really is once you make it into the wrestling business and that it really isn’t a cake walk once you are in the greatest of all wrestling organizations.” Jesse says and pauses for a response. The young women stares on for a moment before opening her mouth, “It’s not all a walk in the park when you’ve made it to the status you’ve achieved?” Jesse rubs his head and chuckles, “Not in the least. When I really go back and think about it not even my days as a wrestling promoter were so easy. Maybe a little harder than they are now, but never the less, the sport of wrestling is a very complex thing and I feel that my work may just open up the eyes of some of these youngsters and hopefully cause them to realize that they need something to fall back on if their wrestling careers don’t take off.” She just sits back and blinks for a moment and finally finds her words, “I really do need to be getting back up front with the pilot.” Jesse just smiles as the woman slowly makes her way out of the chair and back up front. Jesse chuckles as he pulls his glasses back down and looks into his laptop, beginning to punch away. ~The End~
