Anti-Paladin

17 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Mar. 17, 2002 Strewn across the hard wood floor was shards of a shattered glass window, globs of blood spread throughout different areas of the small house. But as we trailblaze across the scene, we find the source of our blood-spill. The horrid image of brain matter dripping from the wall that the slain body slumps up against, his face opened, and nothing but blood covering him entirely. Upon closer inspection, you find several cuts all over his anatomy. Deep gashes, slashes, stabs. A shotgun bullet resting deep inside the brain matter stained white walls. It's surprising the victim's head wasn't entirely detached from his neck. Crime Scene Investigaters swarmed like bees as a squad of the county police squadron put up the yellow tape that this shithole world has made cliche around the scene itself. Curious spectators stand outside the quaint home of a, as of now, John Doe. Four men carried out a stretcher, with a body bag laying on top to deport to the local hospital for an analysis of cause of death, but it was obvious to the men that saw the victim close up exactly what happened. This scene was the apparent aftermath of an Anti-Paladin's beautiful artwork. This was, as it stands, First-Degree Murder. *** Rhett Locke promised himself, as of last night, that he would be cheated no more. If it took dropping what he stands for and fighting dirty, he would. If it meant that he'd have to fight for a cause unholy, he would. If it meant he had to become an anti-paladin, he would. Rhett lay across his apartment's living room couch, his head propped up on the couch's arm, his legs propped on the other, dangling off the end. A cigarette was tucked behind his ear, a pack of Camel's on the Nirvana shirt he wore, and three cigarette's laying around the pack as well. The smoke filled the room from the cigarette that sat snugly between his lips, one of his arms held upward holding a 9mm pistol, firing blanks at the ceiling. Rhett smiled, somewhat, making the sure the cancer stick had no chance of falling while firing those blanks. Using his free arm, he reached down into a clear container, filled to the brim with bullets o' plenty. As he loaded the gun completely, a look of sheer, unimaginable anger charged his facial expression. That mother f*cker. I sware to god, he won't f*ckin' come near me again...and if he does... BANG! Rhett was at his true breaking point, whether Corg would live or die. Deep down, he never wanted to go this far...but it has to be done. At SLAM!, Corg would either leave him be, or feel hot lead piercing his skin. Just like that man on T.V. ...What did those dirty ass media frenzy fucks say again? Multiple stabs and cuts, along with a shot-gun blast to the head? Yeah, I think so. It was do or die. Rhett was slowly becoming something he never though possible. He was slowly becoming an Anti-Paladin.
