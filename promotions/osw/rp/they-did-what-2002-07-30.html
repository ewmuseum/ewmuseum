They Did WHAT?!

30 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/30/02 Sitting at home with his wife is a rare comfort for Jim Axtell these days. The time spent between husband and wife is cut almost unbearably short due to Jim’s participation in Old School Wrestling. They certainly cherish the time they do get to spend together….like it was to be their last. You can never have enough time with loved ones. As they snuggle on the couch in their basement, watching the video of their wedding, Jim and Kami can’t help but feel an enormous amount of love for each other. “That is the greatest day in my life, Jim” says Kami as she turns her head to look up at her husband. “I just can’t imagine anything different than being with you.” Jim looks down at his wife and smiles warmly. He caresses her face with his hand. “I feel the same way, babe” Jim says with a small crackle of emotion in his voice. “Even though we are apart a lot these days, the love continues to grow stronger. I get so scared when you are in the ring, but it’s only because I love you so much.” Kami reaches her arms up and wraps them around Jim’s muscular neck. “I see you are facing that Mammoth fellow this week…are those geeks out to get you for some reason?” Jim shrugs his shoulders. “Who knows what those band nerds are thinking, hon. All I know is that John Mammoth is a big son of a bitch. I plan on putting in some extra gym time with the guys to get ready for him,” Jim says with conviction. Kami brings her arms down and Jim wraps his own arms around his wife’s torso, holding her tight against himself. “How are things going with the Throwbacks, anyway?” Kami inquires. “I couldn’t ask for a better bunch of guys to take me under their wings,” Jim says fondly. “They have been great about everything. I’m going to learn so much from them…” Jim is stopped by the ringing of the phone. He and Kami rustle from their comfortable positions on the couch and Jim reaches for the cordless phone. He presses the button and brings the phone to his ear. “Hello? Hey!” Jim covers the mouthpiece on the phone with his free hand. “It’s Clay,” he says with a smile. Jim takes his hand from the phone. “Kami and I were just talking about you guys.” Jim is quiet for several seconds as Clay is obviously talking. Jim’s face abruptly changes from one of happiness to one of horror. “What?! They did what?!” Jim almost screams into the phone. It’s enough to get Kami’s attention away from the television. “Jim what’s wrong?” she says with concern. Jim holds his index finger up to pause his wife as he continues to listen to the other end of the phone. “I’ll catch a flight as soon as I can and meet you there. Okay. Bye.” Jim hangs up the phone and fire begins to fill his eyes. Kami senses the anger. “Tell me what happened, Jim” she quietly demands. “The Cult has kidnapped Dave.” “Oh my God!”
