Cracking up

3 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/03/2002 The scene opens up on the night time streets of Philadelphia, the three quarters moon shining down, illuminating the words 'LIBERTY BELL PAVILION' on the front of said building. This late at night, traffic is almost nonexistant, and the pavilion, which would normally be surrounded by crowds of tourists, stands empty and silent. We suddenly flash inside, to a wide view of that famous artifact, the Liberty Bell. Out from the shadows steps the Trashcan Man, his gaze locked on the momument of independence. He steps over the velvet ropes surrounding the bell and approaches it, his exression unusually solemn. 'The Liberty Bell was rang many times in it's early years. But the most important time was on July 8, 1776. It rang then to announce the reading of the Declaration of Independence to the general public,' the Trashcan Man declares. 'It's a symbol of freedom that over one and a half million people come to see every year.' The Trashcan Man kneels down next to the Bell. He runs his orange gloved fingers lightly down the two and a half feet long crack. 'The very first time it was rung, this crack appeared in the bell. Instead of recommissioning a new bell, it was decided to hire some workman to patch it up and to continue to use it.' Trashy straightens up and runs his fingers through his hair, looking at the camera for the first time. 'A lot of people have wondered where I've been the past few months. Hell, rumors of my death flew across the internet rumormills. Some said I died in the September 11th attack, some thought I drank myself to death, I think a few thought that Clyde finally got to me.' Trashy sighs and runs his fingers through his hair, gazing off into the distance for a moment, before gazing back into the camera. 'Actually, I left because I needed too. After I won the PCW World title, I just sort of drifted along. I let my success get to my head, and I thought I was better than everyone else. I started showing up for shows drunk, if I showed up at all. I mean, what were they going to do, fire me? I was the World champion, I thought I was untouchable. Then reality came in and bitch slapped me like I was Ed hitting on a cute girl.' A brief grin crosses the Trashcan Man's face before being replaced by that somber expression. 'I lost the World title to Chef, who that night was a MUCH more deserving champion. Even though he retired and abandoned the belt the very next show, he brought more prestige to it that I did my entire reign. So I left. I had to take a long hard look at myself. I came back briefly at Jane Reagan's request to fill in as president of PCW, but we all know how that ended. I was continuing my shoddy work and not doing a damn thing. And I owe all of you out there an apology. Lately I've been wandering around, just drifting from town to town, trying to get some direction back. I don't know how she did it, but Jane Reagan found me once again. She explained that she was starting a new federation. I told her I wasn't interested. She told me it was a fed that was going to be a thorn in the side of Tyler Nelson. THAT got my attention. She said she needed a symbol, one that the wrestlers in the new fed could rally behind as she took the fight to Nelson.' The Trashcan Man clenches a fist in front of his chest. 'And if Ivan Stanislav and Tom Walczak think they can come into MY new home and run roughshod over this federation like they did PCW, then they'll find out why America keeps the Liberty Bell.' Trashy gazes over at the Liberty Bell, placing a gloved hand on it's side. 'Like the Liberty Bell, I may be flawed. I may be cracked. But I still work, and I'll keep on working. The Red Army plans on crushing Old School Wrestling under their Communist heel? Let's see how they deal with a hot foot the likes of which they've never seen.' Trashy stares into the camera somberly, then suddenly busts out laughing. 'Ok, that's the last time I try to be patriotic! Not my style. Ivan, Tommy Boy, you may be bigger than me. You may be stronger than me. Hell, you may be BETTER than me. But there's one thing for certain. You're not crazier than me. And I think being crazy is going to be enough to bring your little parade to a screeching halt!' With that, the Trashcan Man bangs his fist on the Liberty Bell. A loud creaking is heard. Trashy slowly turns his head towards the historical landmark as the creaking sounds give way to splintering. The oak beam that the Bell hangs from starts to break, and the Bell falls, slamming into the stone floor with a deep ring. It rolls down the stairs, emitting a loud DOOOONG for every stair it hits. The Trashcan Man looks around, then walks away, whistling innocently as the scene fades to black.
