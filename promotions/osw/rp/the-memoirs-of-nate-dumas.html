The Memoirs of Nate Dumas

undated

Date: 2-15-01 The Memoirs of Nate Dumas The scene fades up on Nate Dumas, OSW announcer and ex wrestler extraordinaire. He sits at his desk staring at a sheet of paper with a couple large paragraph’s written in heavy black pen, the pen in question sits to the right of the paper itself. Nate stares at the paper that he has written and does not show much emotion at all, unless the word ‘bland’ can be used to show an emotional state. Dumas wears a brown sweater and a pair of black jeans, as well as a pair of tennis shoes. He looks up at the camera for just a moment, not necessarily acknowledging their presence. Nate Dumas is a man that while he may have had a comparatively short wrestling career in the wake of others in the profession, he has still seen a lot during his short tenure. However, even he was not prepared for the power of the Old School Wrestling Federation. The overall attitude of the federation is amazing. The talent is top notch, perhaps better than any other roster in the entire PTC. The men and women in the federation fight and work with a sense of devotion that is quite rare in the profession of wrestling today. However, there are those that act in a slightly different way….. Nate Dumas knows that there are those within the federation that have their agenda’s. They do not really care much about the Old School Wrestling Federation itself, but rather want to exploit it and bend it to their own means. This is something that once again, is not much of a surprise. The piece of paper sitting in front of Dumas contains various thoughts from the young man, something that perhaps he would not say out loud. We all know that Dumas is not afraid of anything and that he has complete and utter confidence in all of his abilities, but there are certain things that even he may keep buried within the recesses of his mind. And furthermore, Nate doesn’t really know why he sat down to write these few paragraphs. He just sat down and had to put his thoughts on paper. And whilst these thoughts are quite true, chances are that no one will see this paper, for Dumas himself will probably destroy it, lest someone gets the wrong impression of him. Below are the thoughts of one Nate Dumas, perhaps one of the few times that we will delve deep into his own thoughts about a subject in the OSW that has been berating his mind for a few weeks now…. “As I drove to my office this morning, various thoughts continued to haunt my mind and I’m not really sure. In retrospect to the OSW since I’ve been here, I’ve gotta tell ya, this place is great! These guys that wrestle in this federation really do have their hearts and souls devoted to this federation, much like I would if I was wrestling again. Not, of course, that I couldn’t go in there and take out virtually anyone in this federation, mind you, but I just choose to call the action instead of being part of it. Guys like Corg, Ritchie Syxx, and Drew Hostyle are really impressing me. These are some of the guys that I was skeptical about, but they have proven that they really do have a place here and are willing to fight tooth and nail to be in this federation. However, there are a few things that bother me, well, in a sense, one thing that bothers me….. The Red Army Since the opening of the OSW, I’ve watched Ivan Stanislav, Alexei Ruslan, and their band of communists run roughshod through this federation with minimal to no reparations for their actions! And I believe, as did the others after his announcement that he was indeed in the OSW, that the severity of the situation was not nearly as bad as Doug Troy and Steven Smith made it out to be. I think, perhaps, I can admit to being wrong. Of course, we have all heard the exploits of Ivan Stanislav in the PCW. We have heard of the numerous injuries that he’s inflicted upon various members of the PCW roster. We have heard of the cars that have been flipped over, the walls that have been knocked over, the bones that have been broken, and the wars that he himself has waged against the management of the Primetime Championship Wrestling Federation. But somehow, perhaps we believed in a certain sense of ‘security’ about the entire situation, with Stanislav signing with the OSW in order to combat the PCW directly. And once again, I can admit to being wrong. Stanislav came in here with an earth shattering roar that shook the very foundation of the OSW, a foundation that was and is still very, very fragile. Not only that, but he brought his entire band of Communist fighters along with him! While this man may be exceedingly powerful, he is even more influential when all of his allies are around him! They should have been broken up, but no, he was signed, and they all came along with him! Wang, Riggs, The Women, all of them! I don’t fear this man, because if I was standing in that ring and he got in my way, I’d show him why I am the man that no wrestling federation can afford, but the other members of the roster, they are a different story. These men are here to fight for belts, fight for recognition, and fight for the competitiveness of the sport. Ivan Stanislav does not give a damn about belts, he does not care about recognition, and he does not fight for the competition. This man fights for a more vile reason, a reason that we are all beginning to see. This man wishes to dominate the entire federation, make it his own, and wield it against any PTC fed that would dare to stand in his way. There was a glimmer of hope, however. After this monster disposed of two of the best athletes in the federation, Corg and Ritchie Syxx, two men that had sworn to defend the OSW from someone such as him arose: Doug Troy and Trashcan Man. And these two men, veterans of the sport and capable of causing Stanislav much physical and ideological pain, have crumbled under the weight of this…war machine bent on collectivization. Doug Troy and Trashcan Man have fought the two fighters of The Red Army, Stanislav and Walczak, and both they have been crushed to nothing and sent packing. Stanislav has sent Troy to the hospital twice before and has beaten Trashcan Man to within inches of his life, how can they stop this man? Enter Commissioner Ed…. I must say, at the moment of his entrance I had high hopes for Ed and I believed that perhaps he would get the best of Stanislav and this Red Army. But I have lost some hope. Ed speaks of a hidden hero to come and defend the OSW from Stanislav, but we do not hear much from him. The roster is questionable of the veterans attempts to defend the OSW. Not that they are afraid of Stanislav, I do not think so, but Ivan is not just fighting them, he is fighting the essence of the OSW. I don’t think Ed and his harebrained scheme will stop Ivan. A secret opponent is not going to stop Ivan Stanislav. You can put sanctions on Ivan, you can punish him, you can withhold pay, and you can bring secret wrestlers from all over the globe, but that will not stop him. He is a fighter unlike any other. He combines might and intelligence with a seemingly magical charisma that seduces people to believe him. Take Speedy Riggs and Wang for example, and even Robbie Decker is more and more one of them as day goes by. Who next? Moose Malone? Steven Smith? Jane Reagan?! Stanislav’s influence is growing slowly, day by day. And who should we attribute this to? Who is at fault? That is questionable. But I constantly ask myself, ‘Why Jane Reagan? Why did you do this? What were you thinking!?’ Jane Reagan brought Stanislav in to head a battle against the PCW, and Ivan gave her his terms and she accepted them. And Ivan indeed is leveling his force against the PCW, but he’s doing it in his own way, a way that we now scramble to stop. Troy has fallen, Trashcan Man can’t hold on much longer, and Ed, no matter what he says, cannot hinder Ivan with some sort of crazy match. The Red Army is here. It has more leverage and more influence in the OSW than it ever had in the PCW. And no matter how hard they try to hide it, I can see the management backpedaling. Do I think Ivan Stanislav can do what he is charged with and destroy the PCW? My answer is yes. Do I think the OSW will survive? My answer…is no.” Nate stares at the paper as he reads it. He thinks for a moment and grabs it, ready to crumble it up. He holds it roughly in his hands and bends it oh so slightly, and then stops, and folds it neatly down the center, and then down the center again and puts it back in his drawer. He sighs and brushes his hair out of his eyes, stands, and slowly walks out of the camera’s view…..
