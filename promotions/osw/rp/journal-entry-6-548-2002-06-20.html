Date: 6-20-02 Journal Entry 6,548 Another monotonous day at Arkhangelsk. Sometimes I wonder why these men even bother doing any work! Wake up in the morning at 4:30, sing our national anthem, march the men, grab breakfast. I have done it for over twenty years, and I am destined to do it for twenty years more. Is this what I was meant for? I went to the Kremlin a few days ago as I heard that President Putin was to be there and may have needed an escort, yet when I arrived, they promptly told me that my presence was not required. I remember seeing a younger soldier head into the building and was directed to Putin at just about that time…he stayed with him the entire day. Why does my country do this to me? Why do they stick me up here in Arkhangelsk? There is so much more Ivan Stanislav can do. So much more Ivan Stanislav can accomplish. Maybe it is because I am a communist. I do not notice my name in the paper as much. I swear it is that dog Karpov’s doing…he is making sure that Ivan’s name is hidden. The bell rings again, as it always rings while I write at my desk, signifying midnight. A new day has begun..a new day of monotony. There once was a time, a time long ago, when the monotony of my life was not apparent. A time when Ivan Stanislav felt different…alive maybe? Actually, that time was not long ago at all, but a mere few months in the past. I often wonder if she watches anymore. She is probably too horrified…too terrified to even look at me, let alone watch me. I wonder what she is doing…when I hear this bell toll I know that it is midday in America. Is she still there? Is she bouncing around and laughing….or is she grim and scared. Reno Davis thinks he’s cheated death. He thinks that he has beaten God? Ha! God is for those that need an intangible being to give them strength. I need none of this. On Slam, I will let him live, and then at Ascension, I will finally get my retribution. When I think of her, I still feel something deep within me. A warmth almost. It hurts, I cannot understand it, it feels good to think about her, and at the same time, it hurts me so. Reno Davis caused that. Reno Davis took her away from me. If I can eliminate Reno Davis…my pain will end. I hope it does. I hope every night that it will. He will pay for what he did to me. He will suffer the consequences for taking away what was so dear to me. Yes, I feel that if he is destroyed, this pain will subside..everything else I have tried to fill this void has failed, it is time to finish off the job. It is getting late…..I must get up in four hours…I should clean out my desk and read my communist books from my one drawer, it has been a while since I listened to their words and on Sunday…my pain will be over. …I hope.
