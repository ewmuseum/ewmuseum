FREEEEEEEEEEEDOOOOOOM!(joint RP with Nibbles the koala)

17 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-17-2002 I’ll kill them all when I get out of here. For a man in captivity, there are a few things that keep them motivated enough to stay alive and sane. One’s hope, another’s courage and the last is the burning desire to gain revenge on your jailors. The food guy I’ll kill last. He gives me yummy things, so he deserves some consideration. For an animal in captivity, it’s much simpler than that. They operate on instinct and instinct alone. That ugly guy is the first to go. I can’t stand ugly. For a little koala named Nibbles, it’s even simpler. Eating and hurting things motivates this particular marsupial. They have been since he was captured and quarantined several weeks ago by United States Customs Officials. I wonder if that koala hoochie over in the other cage needs a sugar daddy… Okay, so something besides hunger and violence motivates Nibbles. But can you blame him? Cooped up in a cage only twice as big as he, taking squats in a kitty litter box, getting fed eucalyptus leaves every single time he was hungry… okay, the last part wasn’t as bad. However, the time’s jumbled itself up for little Nibbles, as it tends to do when you spend several weeks in the same place, doing the same routine over and over again. Right now, it’s night time in the warehouse where Nibbles is being held. By all logic, he should be sleeping right now. Instead, however, he’s pacing his cage, thinking koala thoughts. He thinks back to when they first brought him to his little cage. The men who brought him there, one of whom was the ugly man, had thought him harmless. “Aw, Bob! How can this guy be in quarantine? Lookit him, he’s just so cute. Aren’t you a cute little thing…” The cuteness thing ended when the non-ugly guy had put his finger in between the cage’s bars to give Nibbles a scratch and pulled away his hand minus a couple knuckles on his index finger. “HOLY FUCK MY HAND MY HAND!” Nibbles smiled a koala smile at this recollection, then frowned as he remembered more. After that, they ugly guy had touched Nibbles with something metallic. It really hurt and it knocked Nibbles out. Not only that, but the ugly man took the finger away afterwards. Great, so he had another reason to kill the ugly man. That wasn’t the only finger he got, either. After the incident, other men started placing bets on who could touch Nibbles and not pull back a stump. Nibbles happily obliged their stupidity, leaving some fingers untouched and other minus a knuckle or too. The last time Nibbles got to eat a finger was about a week ago by his estimation. A small group of people, girls with lots of braids and guys with unshaven faces and vacant expressions on their faces, had come in with flashlights and taken some of the animals away. “Hey there little fella. It’s time for the oppressed animals to be free!” One of the unshaven men had come to Nibble’s cage and had gone to pet him before opening the cage up. Right then and there, Nibbles suddenly felt really hungry. *chomp* “AHHHHHH! MY FINGER! MY FINGER!” The group left really fast after that. The next morning, the ugly man and the other guards looked around the place for something. When they came to Nibble’s cage, they smiled and called Nibbles a “good boy”. Nibbles didn’t care what they thought, though. He was too busy gnawing on the finger to care. Except for those incidents, Nibbles really didn’t do much in his cage. Except for this one time where this guy and girl had come over to his cage and started making out. It was right after he was captured, and the girl had said something about a “state of OSW address” and “a lesson”. The guy had something shiny and large on his shoulder, which made Nibbles smile. Shiny things are cool. They made out for a good ten minutes before noticing that Nibbles was masturbating furiously in response. That made them move away, which made Nibbles sad, because he wanted to look at the shiny thing some more. *crash!* “Mother FUCKER!” The sudden noise startled Nibbles out of his reverie, and he turned toward the source: a toppled metal shelf. The cause of the sounds, a man dressed in all black with a black ski mask covering his face, was picking himself up out of the wreckage. He stood up and brushed himself off, then turned to the cage with Nibbles in it. Nibbles pressed himself up to the bars, thinking that this was the food guy again. Good timing, too, as Nibbles was pretty hungry. The figure raised his finger to his lips and made a ‘shushing’ noise in Nibble’s direction, then slowly and quietly walked over to the cage. “Hey there little guy. You ready to go home, Nibbles?” The man pulled something out of his pocket: a small marijuana leaf. He offered it through the bars of the cage, and Nibbles accepted it happily. He sniffed it for a second before eating, recognizing the scent. Was it? Yes! It was his favorite food! Ever since those ugly guys with the goatees and the microphones had given some to him over a year ago, Nibbles had a tremendous craving for these particular leaves. He munched happily as the man opened the cage up. Nibbles started to protest in his own unique way, but the marijuana had already started to take effect. The man scooped Nibbles up and started to walk back the way he came. “I can’t believe I’m saving you instead of getting ready for my big match. Oh well, gotta have everyone there for the celebration party, even little pot-smoking rabid koalas like you, Nibbles.” Nibbles, high as a kite, didn’t understand a single word the man had said. However, he thought it sounded nice, so he smiled a little koala smile. His eyes started dropping, a combination of several restless nights and the marijuana. “That’s right, you take your weed nap. By this time Saturday, I’ll be the OSW World Champion, and you’re gonna need your rest for the party afterwards.” Those were the last words that Nibbles heard before drifting off to sleep.
