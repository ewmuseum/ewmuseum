Venting Out, To The Extreme!

22 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/22/02 'YER ALL WHITE TRASH!!!' Wait a second, hold up, hold up. Yes, pause the whole tape as it is now, so we can give our viewing audience a better idea why some moron is yelling a comment that even degrades himself in the progress. You see, people get angry all the time. They curse. They yell. They say things that they don't really mean to say. Sometimes they say things that they wouldn't be caught saying in front of their own mother. But there are those times that someone gets so angry, so frustrated, that they just can't stop rambling about nothing until something is done. Point case in one Scott Naket, former Canadian Icon, a so-called 'Hott One' and at one time, a dominant force in Primetime Championship Wrestling. But now, that force has fallen, and Naket is finding himself struggling in Old School Wrestling. A brand new chance, and not off to the start you desire can set a man off. And what's happened to Naket is, well, let's say he's venting his rage now. 'THEY JUST WANT ME TO FAIL!!!' Naket yells at the top of his lungs, as he smashes his fist into the wall. Leaning his head back, Naket takes in a deep breath of air, and throws his head forward, exhaling something that he never, or should I say, we thought never would come out from his mouth. 'MOTHERF***ER!!!' yells a pissed off, angry Naket. Inside his locker room, no one can hear him except the people watching this from the comfort of their homes. The locker room used to be a form of a sanctuary for Naket, a place where he could relax and take it easy. A place where he made up a game plan. Today, it's his place of hiding, or getting away from everything. Naket, now looking a little cooler in the face, turns his head to look into the camera that's just planted in the room. He looks into it for a while, and tilts his head. He smiles, and looks at the camera, before letting out his heart filled, warm message. 'You all can suc...' but before Naket can continue, his words are cut off. Actually, the audio is cut off by the OSW production crew. If you can lip read, you can decode all the naughty words coming out of Naket's mouth. On the bottom of the screen, text starts to flow towards the left of the screen, coming from the right. It reads: 'OSW Translation: I love you fans. I had a tough match on Saturday, and lost, but that's ok. I'm a good sportsman and enjoy good competition. Clay Vendetta is my friend. I think Mike Marvelous is the greatest wrestler of all time. They both can go lick a lollipop, cause they both are winners. I wish them both the most luck this Saturday at Slam! when they go for their shot at the World Title with me.' The translation ends there. Well, unless yer a complete moron, you pretty much got the point that Naket wasn't even close to saying that. Naket storms off and slams the door behind him, with the audio kicking back in just to hear the powerful slam. That happened on Saturday. All this week we've tried to get comments from a calmed down Naket, and tried to stay away from the bad influence to children Scott Naket. Here's how it's gone: SUNDAY-7:34 A.M 'S**K S**T CLAY VENDETTA!!!' MONDAY-7:56 A.M 'MIKE MARVELOUS CAN SUCK MY...' TUESDAY-10:02 A.M 'THEY BOTH HAVE HOMOSEXUAL ANAL SE...' WEDNESDAY-9:23 P.M 'GOD DAMN PIECES OF MONKEY SH..' THURSDAY-7:52 A.M 'EAT CO*K YOU CHEATING BA**ARDS!' THURSDAY-7:53 A.M We see Naket trying to catch his breath, as his voice has become scratchy and deep. Naket takes a few deep breaths and looks at the camera that's in the room with him. He smiles, but the anger can still be seen in his eyes. He takes a deep breath and looks around the room, which appears to be a hotel room of some sort. And not a good one might I add. Naket walks over to the double bed that's positioned in the center of the room and falls forward onto it, and starts smashing the bed. 'I hate Clay Vendetta! I hate Mike Marvelous!' Naket says, his voice lower then what we've seen of him so far. 'It's ok though,' Naket starts, talking to no one in particular. 'I will get my revenge on Saturday night. I will show them that messing with me and pissing me off wasn't the greatest thing to do. I'll get my revenge. And little do they know, that I'm gonna do something I never thought I'd have to do ever again on Saturday.' Naket pauses, and looks around the room. He smiles, and looks around, and starts to unzip the jacket he is wearing. And yes, I don't know either why Scott is wearing a jacket at 8 in the morning, nor do I want to know why he is either. He unzips the jacket and reveals an sHs tee shirt. 'I never thought I'd do this again, but,' Naket starts, but we're gonna cut him off in mid sentence. sHs? sHs?! That's the plan? Well by god, why don't we round up the cows and bring em home, cause they sure as hell don't wanna miss this. I know you aren't listening, so I'll point out the sarcasm in the last statement I just made. If sHs is Naket's plan, then if I were Mike Marvelous and Clay Vendetta, I'd just look for how to beat each other, cause this is the absolute lowest threat they could... 'Hey, since when did this turn into a narrative kind of thing?' Naket asks, looking up at the ceiling. Hey, I'm just stating my opinion buddy, and if you don't like it, you can find someone else to describe this for you. 'Well if you wouldn't so rudely interrupt me,' Naket begins, 'You would find out that sHs isn't my plan. I'm only wearing this tee shirt because they're selling them for $2.99 at the OSW Shopzone!' Well sorry Mr. Rodgers, I'll be more polite next time. By the way, from memory I believe the tee shirt is $1.99, so buddy, you got ripped the hell off. 'WHAT?! They told me they gave me the employee discount on this tee shirt!' Naket says, as we now can sense more anger in his voice. 'But you're throwing me off track. My plan for Saturday is to change my game plan. That's right, I'm gonna do a 180 degree turn. What do I mean by that? Well quite simply, I'm gonna stop playing by the rules. I'm gonna stop letting everyone get a fair shot at winning. It's now all about me winning, not them, ME! So right now, if either Clay Vendetta or Mike Marvelous is watching this, you better get ready. Saturday night...IT'S ALL GONNA CHANGE!!!' 'HEY! KEEP YOUR GOD DAMN VOICE DOWN YOU PUNK KID!!!' yells a apparent pissed off man from the room next to Naket's. Naket looks behind him, and shrugs. He then turns his head around and looks up at the ceiling. He shakes his head, and covers his eyes with his hand and groans. 'I can't believe I was talking to a waffle on the ceiling.' Naket says, still groaning. Yes folks, you heard it right. Through the many stupid things Scott Naket has done in the past, from claiming Ryan Knox couldn't out-wit a grade five kid, to taking advice after advice, after endless rounds of advice from Rev Bob, even through to his days of the stupid cow launcher, I think we have a new low for Scott Naket. Talking to a waffle, wait, scratch that. Explaining his plans to a waffle, nay, explaining his actions for Saturday to a waffle on the ceiling of a cheap motel. Sigh, the day that Scott Naket wins something that involves knowledge, brain power or a small dose of common sense is the day that this world in a whole is in danger.
