Date: April 20, 2002 He has nothing to lose... Screams and painful sounds flew from his vibrating vocal cords, there is no doubt in his mind that he was going to die. But yet he doesn’t quite care. The room is dark, with a few specks of light, barely able to be seen by the human eye. This man is chained up against an old wooden wall. As a matter of fast, everything in the entire shack seems to be made out of wood. With the small bit of light that filt the room, blood is seen pouring down from his chest, somewhere within the intestinal region. “And did you actually think that your persuasion would make me believe you? I think not!” a voice says, which came from a rather tall figure standing in front of the screaming man. “I think not…” he steps closer to the man, his body outline becoming more visible. “Aggggghhhhhhhhhhhh!” the man screams, releasing some inner tensions, and all of the pain he is currently going through. “…” Everything that he has, or had, is already taken. He didn’t mind what this shadowy figure was to do to him. The figure watches as the man begins to pant, and breathes heavily. “So, you want to play it like this, eh?” a long pause is found. “So be it.” He whispers. Did you really think you could outplay me… The man suddenly feels pain. Not like before, but real pain. He feels what he has lost, and he absorbs the discomfort subsequently. And somehow, my uniqueness has allowed you to do this to me… His wife, his children, his friends… his life, all flashing before his very eyes. Before all of the luminent colors which swirl endlessly around him. He learns that everything that he thought was gone, was still there. But in a matter of moments, it will be gone… forever. And like you always told me when I needed help… The man’s now naked body begins to shiver and expresses the cold fear within. Fuck off and die… Because that’s what you’re doing now… No more screams. Nothing. The man is dead. He lay there, chained against that slick wooden wall, as the shiny blade withdrew from his chest, taking a lot of his blood with it. The shadowy figure lets loose a middle finger directed at the corpse, which is seen through his faint body outline. As he leaves the submitted area, he takes the blade, or sword with him. All that is seen now, is the figure’s spiky red hair, and the emotionless smile on his face. fin
