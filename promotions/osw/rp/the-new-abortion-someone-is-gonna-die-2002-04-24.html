The New Abortion: Someone IS Gonna Die...

24 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24-04-02 ♫♪…Life, it’s the ultimate sin. A game with no rules that you’re expected to win…♪♫ - Kid Rock “Someone’s gonna diiieee…” He whispered to himself, the excitement of the thought of it causing him to sing the last few words. The torment he had suffered had left his body. He felt pure. Lying on the cool, soft green grass of the front lawn, he sneered at the few people that passed and gave him those judgmental looks. Tears welled up in his eyes, but were wiped away, quickly, with all the regrets he had/would ever feel. The stars began to shine, but he couldn’t see them through the bright, night sky. He got up and retreated to the comfort of his bedroom, where he would spend the rest of the night planning the next day. And maybe scarring himself whilst he was at it. The next day he woke rather promptly. Groggily wakening, he pulled on a new set of clothes and looked in his full-length mirror. Staring back at him was the embodiment of inner pain, ready to be unleashed on the world. He reached under his bed, and pulled out a box, out of which he pulled his tool, a shiny new toy. Today the pain he felt was at its strongest. All that was wrong in his life was swirling in his stomach, he felt like Steve “The Milk Man” Carnage does after ingesting a good healthy bottle of semen. However, he bottled the pain up, tried not to let it affect him too much. Slowly slinking along the silent corridor of his home, he passed different room’s, a crazed yet forlorn look in his youthful eyes. As he passed each occupied room, from bathroom to bedroom, a horrifying whisper came about his throat as he cautiously pointed his toy at his family. “Someone’s gonna diiieee…” He mimicked a gunshot and hid the Colt 45 in his jacket pocket. Passing each room, he would mimic this same action, from his newborn sister to his oldest brother, he didn’t care whom, it was, and all that mattered was: - “Someone’s gonna diiieee…” He made his way downstairs, and passed the den, his mother and father seated, watching an old family holiday video. As he passed them, the same gunshot sound followed by: - “Someone’s gonna diiieee…” As he opened the front door and stepped out, his father turned to the front hall and called out. “Hey son, you going out?” SLAM! His father shrugged and turned back to his brother-in-law and stepson shaving the hair off of his right leg while he lay comatose in their caravan. Obviously a once happy family. Walking down the busy streets, he would turn the Colt to anyone in public, mimicking the gunshot, and thinking over those three words. Friends passed him unknowing of his destiny, ignoring him. That hurt him. But he didn’t care any more. As happy as someone could be in his situation, loveless and lifeless, he reached a busy part of the business district. Thinking to himself for a couple of minutes, thinking over his losses and his gains, and his wastes of those gains, the lost soul begins walking back and forth along the district, his eyes crazy and his speech almost slurred. “Someone’s gonna diiieee…someone’s gonna diiieee…someone’s gonna diiieee…someone’s gonna diiieee………” He became crazed. The pain inside was overwhelming. As the tears came, he strolled into the middle of an overcrowded square, and looked around at the sheep drinking their Cappuccinos and talking on their little sleek mobile phones. He pulled the Colt 45 from his pocket. “Someone’s gonna diiieee…” He turned the gun towards himself… “Someone’s gonna diiieee…” He rests the gun in his mouth and points it up slightly, as the masses of people see him and scream out, running in all directions… “Someone’s gonna diiieee…” Boom……… ------------------------------ From across the street, a familiar face watches as the skull of this poor child almost shatters, and he giggles inhumanely. Staring at the flocking people, listening as they scream and gag, he sighs to himself, turns in the other direction, and starts walking… “All of those people…sheep…lemmings. Careless of that young one’s feelings, now he’s HellBound. Robert DeNiro once said, “Every man... every man has to go through hell to reach paradise.” Now that I think about it, The Red Army will never reach paradise, it will be one continuous journey on the Highway through Hell…hmm…how convenient this young man kills himself as I rant about those Communist fuckers…oh well…I’m Damned if I do and I’m Damned if I don’t…” Fin
