Pre Match Preparations...

25 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/25/02 We see Tom Walczak getting ready for his match. He is packing up his gym bag with all the things that he needs. First go in his shorts, and they are followed by his shirt, and boots. He goes over to a drawer, and gets his cut-off gloves, and puts them into the bag. He has all the necessary things for the match, yet he does not close the bag. He makes his way over to the refrigerator, and pulls out a six pack of soda. “I have to bring this, just in case I run into any more problems. Now I am almost positive that it was the machines fault, but I just can’t trust that the machines in this arena will be any better. Plus with Doug Troy being my partner I just don’t know what type of shit he is going to pull out of his ass. The thing that bothers me the most is not that I will have to team up with a piece of worthless shit such as Doug Troy, but I will also have to team up against my comrade Ivan Stanislav. Now, as much as I respect my comrade I just as much want to win the match, and I know that even though I will have to carry my team. I feel that I have a lighter load to carry than does Ivan. He has that piece of crap Reno Davis to worry about.” Walczak places the soda into the gym bag, and he once again makes his way over to the refrigerator. This time he pulls out a beer, and a bag of Polly-O String Cheese. He opens up the bag, and brings it over with him as he sits on the couch. “You know as much as I hate the capitalist asses that rule this country if I had to commend them for one thing that they came up with it would be string cheese. I mean it just comes off. You don’t need a knife to cut it all you do is pull and it comes off. It is a truly amazing piece of work.” Walczak continues to eat the cheese sipping a beer every now and then. He eventually makes it through the entire bag, and he lies down to take a rest. “I really don’t see how Doug Troy, and myself can lose the match. Other than Comrade Ivan who else is there? There are those two punks Rhett Locke, and Graphic Violence. The nerve of that dumbass son of a bitch Graphic Violence to call me a dog-faced bitch. I swear that if I get in the ring with him than he will hope that he stayed out of the company when he had the chance. Then there is Andrew Hawk, and Sebastian Toomes. These CCW lowlifes should not even be in the match. Toomes should have never been champion in the first place, and now the OSW is giving him a chance at being champion once again. This I will never understand.” Walczak looks at his watch and realizes that he only has a little bit of time before he has to be at the airport. He gets his stuff and makes his way towards his car. He gets into the front seat and as he drives away the scene fades to black…
