Sweet Nightmares Walczak...

4 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08-04-02 “…Communists. Fuck ‘em. Communism. Defined as a concept or system of society in which the major resources and means of production are owned by the community rather than by individuals. THAT’S what Stanislav, Krieg, Balczak and those other German, Russian and Chinese sumbitches are preaching about. Stanislav? A little rat convicted in his beliefs, a powerhouse and a big Russian pussycat. Little? Pffft, ask Tempest. Krieg is a fucking disgrace to my own homeland. A 9th generation Hessian mercenary and I have to share the nationality with that prick that’s currently lacing the ass of Ivan with the scum that is his own saliva…” Hessian looks up, his green eyes glowing intensely, his championship title slung over his shoulder and an aggressive tone in his lungs. “…Walczak. 6’10” of Polish power. Ha, don’t fucking kid ME, son. Minion the Imp has a bigger set of testicles than that communist scum. A battle between two powerhouses? Give me a fucking break. The man is nowhere near my league; I beat Meanstreak of all people, even if that Russian fuck interfered, so what chance does a Red Army PODLIZUCH have…oh the big dumb Satanist isn’t intelligent…yeah that’s what you think. All I get is the big dumb, smelly, poorly endowed fighting machine that’s got nothin on anyone. Really…?” Hessian lifts something up in front of him and stares at it; the crushed skull of one Christopher Partridge. Bits of skull fragment tangled in the loose, brown hair soaked to the shrivelled pale skin by blood, Hessian’s eyes glow ever stronger. “…An impostor. Some little parasite tried to scrounge off my success and succeeded. Or did he? Last Wednesday night I took a little wander on over to the uWi, and as footage shows, I killed the little fucker on screen with the help of Cain. Now I get to carry on my rage into this upcoming match against Walczak. Carry my rage…oh that sounds so intimidating doesn’t it. It’s even more intimidating, I’ve heard, after you listen as your own neck crumples underneath the weight of a 350lbs Crucifix Piledriver, that’s right, the one where your neck is the only thing to cushion your landing…” Hessian sits up, the glinting reflection of a generic camera reflecting in the steel of his mask. “…Tom Walczak…The Polish Punisher. The only thing heading for a punishment is Walczak himself. A lifeless fuck that mooches after Ivan Stanislav, trying to be something he’s not…I can hear him right now shouting out, “That is good coming from petty Satanist!”. Satanist? Believing in an ultimate evil? Doing the stereotypical thing and killing things for blood? Satanism = Anarchy and free kills. I’d rather be this than some petty lackey to the biggest joke in OSW. What? Me? Ivan Stanislav? A Joke? Well what the fuck? Ivan Stanislav, the decorated Russian with a splendiferous wrestling career. Yet one man, capable of so much, has to hide behind two dummies to do his handiwork, or……………no wait, fuck him he has nothing I want. Then again neither does Walczak. But I have something he wants. Ah well. Desire denied. It took long enough to pry the tag belts from Civil D’s grubby paws; I’m not about to degrade them by dropping them to a disgrace to Communism, not that it isn’t already a disgrace, and to Michelle Krieg, the reason I’m ashamed of my own roots…” Hessian adjusts his championship belt, and leans forward, the evil so obviously manifesting in the atmosphere. “…A Global Title Semi Finalist, a 2-Time NTICW U.S. Champion, ½ of the OSW Tag Champions…and no one will take me seriously? No respect. Or is there? The literally big, bad Russians don’t care about anything else if they want something, namely these tag titles which Azrael and I hold oh so dearly to our blackhearts…” “…You don’t think they think you’re droning on about anything, Hessian…?” asks a voice hidden by the dark. Slowly, a pale figure emanates from the blackness surrounding, gold around its waist and muscles abound. Azrael Cain stands by his partner’s side. “…Heresian Bloodlust, Champions. Heretics with a lust for blood. It’s all in the name. Anyway. The two suburban pretty boys have lost their property, and now a pair of pathetic Communists that believe truly they can stop the might of Azrael Cain and Hessian. ‘Tis a lucky thing indeed that Minion the Imp and Caleb are simply managers, one should think two Red Army dolts such as those two would show a little finesse in chasing these………I sense laughter…or is it sarcasm? Ok. Well then. Let’s let the Carnage begin…how say you, Hessian…?” “…I reckon that Tom Walczak is on the Highway to Hell………come Slam! I’m going to Eternally Damn the Polish Punisher…” “…Przyjemne Nocnemary Walczak…” Fin
