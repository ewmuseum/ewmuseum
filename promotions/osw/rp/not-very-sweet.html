OSW Old School Promos — #269

Not very sweet...just thank God it's short.

2002-04-02

Amy 'Aimz' Campbell

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(I didn't have any ideas, so I just wrote...this blows. Thank God I never got desperate enough just to copy and paste a piece of Slam and add my commentary. **Dies**.) With an interesting week had so far, things are only looking to get weirder for OSW's resident redhead during her stay in Mississippi... It was April 1st, 2002...April Fools Day. The one day of the year Amy hated more than even Drew Hostyle. Why? She was horribly gullible, so usually she got tricked every twenty or so seconds...this time around it was no different; Breanne had started quite early with ''Hey, Amy...Metallica just announced that they're coming to Halifax this August!!!'' and, of course, Aimz beleived it...however momentarily. So she was sitting...completely alone on yet another dreary April 1st, dressed well in a pair of baggy black Caffeine cargo pants and that same old flaming skull Metallica hoodie that served as her security blanket since she was 15, though it's quite ratty at this point. Again thinking to the days ahead, specifically Saturday's edition of 'Slam!!', Amy was was less-than-thrilled...knowing she had to actually soil her hands with trash like Hostyle, and would most-likely come face-to-face, or face-to-stomach as it were, with Meanstreak in Timo's defense. But none-the-less, she wasn't going to let this be a bad April Fools Day like so many others had been. Thank God for Rent-A-Hero...he entered only moments after she made the vow to have a happy day... ''AMY!!!'' Hey, at least RAH was enjoying the day. She responded to his greeting, trying desperately to return any semblance of enthusiasm. ''Rentie...'' RAH came to sit beside her on the bed, still looking more out of his own than Hostyle in a straight strip bar. ''What's the matter my Kr----'' No, she liked semi-normal Rentie...had to stray from the pet names. ''Errr...what's wrong? ''That Hostyle...he seems to think I'm insane...I feel like screaming that I have no valid reason to live because I've lost the respect of a person who has the brain power of Eugene and clogs his TV time with clips of my wrestling.'' He laughed a bit. ''No need to fear fighti---'' ''Hey hey hey...I'm not afraid, that much is certain. Hostyle's not the least bit intimidating when his '3B' fucks aren't around. When has the guy come out on top without the help of Sebastian or Bones? Never!'' She had to pause a moment, re-collecting her thoughts while distantly attempting to keep her temper in check. ''Something was said about people like me not being able to back insults half our size's worth...I'm half of HIS size, half of my size is a quarter of his and yet he's making the same generalized insults...what the hell's that telling us about the intellect of little mister thug?'' ''Uhhh...I...'' ''Meh...and he's using his ebonics, ghetto speak...fucking wannabe. Am I cool because I say fuck practically every other word? No...so what's fueling his slaughter of the English language? He's not impressing anybody!'' ''Why, I'm not su---'' Amy was on a proverbial roll now, she just couldn't stop when her mouth got going, as usual... ''He's talking about perks and all of that bullshit, but what control does he really have? That fool's Toomes' lap dog, and Toomes is Reagan's LITERAL lap dog...so what kind of power, exactly, does Hostyle himself have...huh?'' Her own question was quickly answered. ''Exactly! He thinks he's in control, but the jacksmack's too fucking stupid to realize that all he does is follow what Toomes and Reagan decide! He hasn't figured out that it wasn't just his sorry ass beating me down on Slam, it was he and like two other men twice my frigging size...'' Hold up a second here...she was ranting, though only realizing it midway through the speech. Amy almost immediately stopped, not wanting to subject poor Rent-A-Hero to any more of this... ''Sorry...'' She lowered her head, glancing at the floor. ''...kinda got myself worked up. But he really does need to know that 'Hands Clean' by Alanis has nothing to do with sex, or my supposedly being a whore...it's about her having a relationship with a twenty eight year-old when she was twelve. I mean, who does he think he is? He implies I suck Timo off, but the fucking douche KNOWS I'm with you...'Whore'...really God damn creative...isn't that the norm, see a girl who's more popular, more talented and call her a whore by default? Like, what does he do...ask REAL prostitutes out? It's probably the only pu---'' RAH thought a moment, then interrupted timidly. ''I think I'm supposed to stop you before you get going again...plus the terms you use are growing more and more difficult to understand...'' Amy just had to laugh at this point. Rent was always so incredibly innocent...it was probably what she would consider to be the most charming quality about him because...well...let's face it, ladies...the spandex wasn't exactly a turn-on. ''Heh...sorry again.'' ''It's fine, but let's change the subject...'' Rentie was nervous about Amy's rather apparent anger-managment issues. ''...how about those...comic books!'' ''You know, Rentie...I'm not really into comics...'' She smiled for a moment, pondering her next statment, regarding the few she reads... ''Except, of course, Johnny The Homicidal Maniac and the Squee series...'' ''Johnny the WHAT?! Sweet Amy...you and I must wander into my closet of comics some day!'' ''Hehe...'' Aimz absolutely HAD to mutter under her breath. ''I'd definately like to wander somewhere alone with y----'' ''What was that?'' He leaned closer, trying to hear what she was mumbling. ''Oh...nothing! Just...ummm...little...girlie....thoughts. Yeah, that's it.'' Okay...this blows so it's gonna cut off while we're ahead...
