Deep this time. Armour… That guy came out of nowhere. Didn’t see him backstage. Didn’t see him at all. And he murdered me in front of all those Oklahom… Oklahomies, whatever they call themselves. I got killed out there. How the fuck am I supposed to work with someone that Porter just throws in there, and the guy just brutalizes me. Ring steps, ring posts, the fucking monitor in the fucking face. That was a fucking shoot. I can’t feel my face right now, but that’s okay. I really don’t want to. I know he broke my nose before I went out. I’m a mess. And that guy fucked me up. He destroyed me on national television and I didn’t have a clue about it. I knew someone was supposed to come in and I was going to put him over as a bad-ass, but I was expecting some guy named Krow. Some big bad-ass, coming down the ramp, concrete my Face turn. But this guy, Armour. Who the fuck was he? Why didn’t Jane tell me about him? I know I talked to Porter about the match, and he said Krow was supposed to be coming. I know I haven’t worked with the guy, but there was some history I could look in on… He was in fWo or something… Oooo, colors. I’m doped up so much, I can’t feel a thing. Which is probably for the best. Why would they send this Armour guy to rough me up? Sending a message? He didn’t fuck up Seth and Trevor and he only gave Jane a slight powerbomb, and he controlled that one from what I could see. But he was working stiff as fuck with me, like he was trying to hurt me. I’ve never really been beaten up before. I know that sounds fucked up, but seriously, this is professional wrestling. I’ve been badly hurt before, but it was never intentional. Everything was a pulled punch or a controlled fall or a fake table or a chairshot to the head (which really doesn’t hurt). I’ve had my ass kicked, but its all planned out, all rehearsed and we all go home afterwards and put some Ben-Gay on and watch South Park. I’ve only been put in a hospital once before, and that was after the biggest match I ever had in CCW. And it took me out for a month. But once again, that was all rehearsed. This wasn’t. This was an ass-kicking, a message sent from someone. I’d like to know who. Who…….Whoooooooooo….Whoooooooooooooooooooo Imma owl. Owl with shit sticking out of my nose and something down my throat and a catheter, but hey, us Owls… oooo, I’m not feeling well… ….. ….. I wanna go home. Someone’s rubbing my arm… Could be the only part of me that DOESN’T hurt… My eyelids feel glued shut. Feels soft, like a woman. Maybe its Paris, and she’s come back to haunt me and tell me what a psycho I’ve become, and be the virtual symbol of my failure… Maybe its Cassandra, the one that got away, the girl who wrecked me forever. Maybe she saw the match and in some way, she wanted to come and rescue me from pain and suffering and her love will be the healing light that I need… Wait a minute… Don’t wanna laugh and rip my stitches. I… I dunno who it could be… I slowly open my eyes and see someone I have no right to be seeing. Someone who despised me every step of the way, and when she was no longer useful, she was sent home to sit with a measly paycheck because she didn’t fit in anymore… My eyes are open. “Jennifer?”
