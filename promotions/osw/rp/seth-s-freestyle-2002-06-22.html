Seth's Freestyle

22 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-22-02 The scene opens up to reveal Trevor Hawke and Seth Masters standing backstage in the American West Arena in Phoenix, Arizona. It’s just hours before Ascension goes on the air, where they will collide with Da Westsidaz and Heresian Bloodlust in a Weapons of Choice Elimination Match for the OSW Tag Team Titles. It appears as if they have a few things to say before the PPV goes on the air, so apparently they have pulled aside a cameraman to cut a last minute promo. “Ahem...” Seth clears his throat, as he stares into the camera. “It seems to me as if these poetry type promos are becoming all the rage lately. In fact, I saw one from Hessian earlier today.” “I didn’t really like it at all,” Trevor adds. “Not at all.” “So true, Trev. It was SO lame,” “He really knows nothing about rhyming, so I think it’s up to me to show that idiot what it’s all about. Only I’ll be opting to use a form of rhyming that Da Westsidaz will understand. I’m sure Hessian’s poem went straight over their heads, so that’s why I’m about to cut some MAAAAAD freestyle.” “He is,” Trevor says with a nod. “So if you don’t mind, Trev...hit the music!” Seth says, doing an nWo-like point at his partner with both hands. Trevor cuts his hands to his mouth, then starts imitating a bass-kick. Boom *pshh* Boom boom boom *pshh* Boom *pshh* Boom boom boom *pshh* As Seth hears this, he begins to bob his head, flailing his hands as he begins to rap. “Yo dicksmack, we’re gonna break your back. Gonna fuck you up, we ain’t cuttin’ no slack. Trev and me are elite, we just can’t be beat Sole exception is Shanda, when she beats my meat. So back off Hess, don’t you know you’re less...cool, actin’ the fool. Thinking you’re better, but we’re the trendsetters. On this Sunday night, say goodbye to the night. Because we’re shining stars and us stars we shine bright. And I just have to mention, we’ll ascend at Ascension Where we’ll banish your asses, to the lower classes All the way back to Primetime, where mediocrity passes. Weapon of choice? Now my weapon’s my voice. My words make you reel, before I slam you with steel. Our opponents got bats and barbed wire galore, Minion and Caleb and Da Westsidaz’s whore All these fucks on the outside just prove that you sadder Cuz we ain’t bringing shit ‘cept these two fuckin’ ladders No interference is needed, we’ll beat you ourselves We don’t need no damn slut or some fag gothic elves But still, with all this shit contained in one match It’s almost as cluttered as Minion’s man-snatch. Or should I say ass? Either way, you always pumping it, and I don’t mean with gas. Long story short, Trev and Seth Daddy Mack There’s no doubt in my mind that we’ll get our belts back. The poor Westsidaz and Cain will get knocked off the track. And you’ll still be stuck tonguing hairy Imp sack.” As he finishes up, Seth is out of breath. However, this doesn’t stop him from shaking his head at the camera in a cocky fashion. “What up now, Hessian? We bouty bouty and rowdy rowdy! MID-WESTSIDE!!!!” “That was pretty damn fly, Seth,” Trevor says, nodding appreciatively in his partner’s direction. Seth nods back, a confident smile appearing on his face. “Word.” FADE OUT
