Beauty

25 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-25-02 The sound of the rush of wind flowing through your hair and the jets to your side begin to make their ascension into the air. As you look up and watch them just fly over high above you, containing hundreds of people just like you in them. Off on their merry way, maybe in the same situation as you, but you never really can tell. “Sir, sir” A man yells out. “You can’t be just standing there like that. You must board your plane.” Watching the next plane fly through and totally disregarding everything that the airport worker is saying, we find ourselves watching the same thing as Jesse Ramey. As he continues to watch the planes fly above his head. Almost lost motionless in a state of trance. Like the way a child would look in a candy store for the very first time. Lost in the thought of it all. “Sir, I can only ask you this one more time. Please board your plane, you are not authorized for this area.” The worker begins to make his way nearer to Jesse. Shaking his head and finally giving reference to the worker, “Oh, I’m sorry,” Jesse says with a smile on his face. “I was merely lost in the magnificence of these spectacular contraptions.” The worker looks at Jesse and starts to speak in a sort of yelling manner, so as to be heard over the loud sound of the planes in the distance, “I can’t totally understand that, sir. What I was doing was only standard procedure and you must board your plane now.” Jesse begins to make his way back on course and comes nearer to the plane. He slowly makes his way up onto the steps leading into the plane and just turns around and looks out into the greatness of the beautiful blue skies around him. “What a majestic sight this is, not only have mine eyes seen the glory of it all, but I have found inner peace in this moment in time. Take a look around you and see the beauty of it all.” Jesse says as he raises his arms into the air. “What more do we need in this world than the creations that lays around us? Answer this question to yourself and find the true meaning of it all.” Jesse turns and ducks in through the door of the plane. Shortly after the door closes and we are left with the sight of the clouds rolling over the sky. ~The End~
