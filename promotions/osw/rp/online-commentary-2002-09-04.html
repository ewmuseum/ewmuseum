Online Commentary

4 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09-04-02 To Any and All Of My Fans, This is my first and last online commentary. Words coming straight from the mouth of Steven Shadows, not Graphic Violence. I started this life of wrestling five years ago. My very first fed was the IAV, or better known as the International Association of Violence. It was easy for me to get my frustration out in violent hardcore matches. I felt better after I would cut a guy open from ear to ear, just watching him bleed and his life slowly slipping away. It wasn’t long before people started telling me that I was the personification of violence, and shortly after Graphic Violence was born. I’ve lived my life in hate, despise and in darkness. A life that has made me successful, but yet has taken its toll on my personally. Every promotion wanted to hire me. TW2k, Asylum, jOlt, the CWL, the fWo, OSW and the list goes on and on. But they didn’t want me; they wanted the monster I created. The monster that is Graphic Violence. And when you portray the same character for years and years, three hundred and sixty five days a year, you start to believe that you are that character. That you are larger than life and that everyone around you is shit. I’ve done everything in this business. None of you can take that away from me. You can say that my work ethic was poor, that I didn’t “live up to the name”…but I’m personally drained of this life. I can no longer stand to be Graphic Violence. I am a rich man, and have yet to enjoy my wealth. Hell, I live in a broken down home just so I can keep this character connected with his “roots”. It’s time for me to get out of this mold that wrestling has made me. It’s affecting my health, it’s affecting the way I interact with people and I just feel like I’m dying. At this moment in time, 9:02pm EST, Graphic Violence is dead. I, Steven Shadows, am going to step out of the lime light and into the darkness…but this time it’s for another reason. I’m stepping into the darkness where the people can’t see me, where the media can’t report on me, where wrestling is non existent to me. A normal life is something I haven’t had in over five years, and that is something that I am looking for. I have recently met a fantastic woman, someone who has opened my eyes and made me realize that this life isn’t really what I want. It’s time for me to leave, let someone else have my spot. I am going to concentrate on a life I never thought I would have. One with a girl who wants to be my wife, have my children and be happy forever. Fatherhood, which has a nice ring to it. So I thank all of you for your support over the years. And I also want to thank those of you that hated me so much, because if it wasn’t for you I wouldn’t be where I am today in the wrestling business. You have created one of the greatest heels of all time, not me. And when people ask the question down the road “who was one of the greatest of all time”, I’ll be proud to hear someone say “hey, remember Graphic Violence…he was one of the all time greats”. These are my last words for now. Who knows, maybe if I miss the business years down the road you’ll see me again. Just maybe Graphic Violence will come back from the grave, but for now he’s buried six feet deep. Peace, Steve Shadows aka Graphic Violence
