'So...The Beast has a Libido'

21 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/21/02 The sun is sitting low in the sky getting ready to hide itself until the morning. The workday is ending. All of the mindless drones are leaving their cubicles and heading back to their small cramped apartments for another evening of mindless entertainment that will only waste their precious time that they have on this earth. The people walk amongst the streets like mindless cattle being rounded up for slaughter not knowing what is in store for them. Up above the people on top of a building there is a massive figure watching them. The huge silhouette is OSW’s Azrael Cain. The beast is wearing his black and tattered robe. His long black hair is pulled back in a ponytail. His face is stern. He stares down at the people and takes in their activities like an anthropologist studying a new society. His eyes are fixed on the movement below him. It is the raspy voice of his manager, Caleb, which interrupts the silence on the roof of the building. “Cain! Hey, there you are. Heh-Heh. I have been looking for you all over the place. Heh-Heh. I thought that you would be up here. Heh. What the hell are you doing? Heh-Heh-Heh.” Caleb is waddling across the black top roof to his friend. His thin comb over and trench coat are blowing in the wind. There is no response from the Fallen Angel. He just continues to look down at the movement in the street. His eyes are still focused on the strange thing that these people call life. Caleb clears his throat and tries to get his friend’s attention again. “AHEM!!! Heh. I said! What the hell are you doing up here? Heh-Heh-Heh.” Caleb’s obtrusive attitude finally breaks Cain’s concentration. The beast’s eyes slide away from what he was focusing on before and glare at the hobo from the corners of his eyes. He looks up at the dirty old man for a moment without saying a word. After the awkward silence and staring, Cain returns his gaze down upon the streets and movement below. Caleb attempts to get some sort of verbal response out of Cain, so he proceeds to talk to the quite beast. “What’s your problem today? Heh-Heh. Have you forgotten that you have a match this week that will get you and Hessian back near the top? Heh-Heh. I mean come on. You guys win this week and you get to beat down on Civil Disobedience again. Heh-Heh-Heh.” There is no movement from Cain. He eventually responds to the nuisance that is his manager. “Do you honestly believe that I am concerned with this match? Look who Hessian and I will be facing. I have no concerns.” Caleb is even further annoyed with Cain’s over confident response. “Oh sure, so you beat Ivan and Meanstreak last week and all of sudden you think that you can steamroll your way through all of these opponents this week. Heh-Heh. Well, let me let you in on something buddy. That was your first win. Heh. And this week, you are going to have a harder time getting a win than you think. Heh-Heh-Heh.” Cain still doesn’t remove his eyes from what he is concentrating on, but yet he continues the conversation. “This match is a joke. We are facing off against three other tag teams that have no business being in the same ring as Hessian and I. In fact, there is not a single warrior in the lot of them. And besides that, their petty feuds are going to be their downfall. They will destroy each other.” “That might be easy for you to say, but at least the majority of them have more than one win! Heh-Heh-Heh” Cain is growing angry at the persistence of his manager throughout this exchange. Caleb’s constant badgering his breaking whatever it is that Cain is trying to focus on and think about. Cain’s anger is beginning to overcome him again as he snaps at the hobo. “Jesse Ramey and Michael Dame are of no concern in this match. They will not work together well enough to be able to fight as any kind of a threat. Bones and Hostyle are nothing more than a couple of maladjusted thugs that have nothing better to do then take all of their aggression caused by racism out in the ring. And besides, Bones and Dame will be too busy trying to get at each other that they will do our job for us. And if you have forgotten, I have already proven myself to be the better warrior than Bones while I was in PCW!” A smirk grows on Caleb’s face as he realizes that he is getting under the beast’s skin. “That might all be true there, big fella…Heh…But have you forgotten about Twisted Metal?” Cain rolls his eyes as he replies. “No I haven’t forgotten about those…those…what did you call them again?” Caleb chuckles at the time warp that Cain lives in as a result of being imprisoned for so many years. After a second of laughing inside to himself, Caleb finally answers Cain’s question as if he was explaining it to it a child. “You mean Chico and Gunmetal? Heh-Heh. They are man made machines called robots. Heh-Heh-Heh.” Cain’ s teeth begin to grind as he answers due to the condescending tone from the small decrepit old man. “Yes, those…things. They are still made by man. That is there flaw. Hessian and I will dismantle them once again just like we did so man weeks ago. I am not concerned about manmade weapons. They will fall like the rest.” Cain begins to stare back down at the street again and focus on whatever it is that he finds so alluring. Caleb finally shrugs in complete desperation as he gives up trying to rationalize about the importance of the match with the beast. He crosses next to Cain and sits on the ledge of the rooftop and starts to look down with Cain. He attempts to change the topic. “So…what is it that you are doing up here anyway? Heh-Heh.” Cain answers quickly without looking up from the street. “The people, the way they move the way that they interact with one another. I find it fascinating to just sit back and watch. It helps me understand them better. That way I can learn their weaknesses.” Caleb starts to chuckle slightly. “I…uh…see. Heh. So I guess that you have been doing research the entire time that you have been up here the past couple times I have found you? Heh-Heh-Heh.” Cain looks at Caleb with an unsettling look and responds very nervously with the short answer of “yes.” Caleb starts to chuckle even more. “Right. Heh-Heh.” The hobo looks down at something on the street and immediately his toothless smile appears on his face. He then begins to put things together. “So…I guess (snicker) that I can assume that (snicker) you being up here has nothing to do with that gorgeous blonde bombshell working at that flower stand down there? Heh-Heh-Heh.” Cain rises to his feet quickly and retreats away from the hobo. He tries to answer but there is a definite bit of nervousness and loss for words in his voice. “I…eh…was looking at the…at the…people…trying to get a better understanding of them! Do you honestly think that I would bother myself with a mortal woman again after what happened last time?” Caleb begins to roll around the rooftop laughing hysterically. His laughter is so strong that it is hard for him to concentrate on what it is that he is trying to spit out. “You have been …Heh-Heh-Heh…checking out that piece of…Heh-Heh-Heh…eye candy for how long now? So…it appears that the beast has a libido after all! Heh-Heh-Heh-Heh.” Cain gets defensive quickly and shortly there is an argument of “No I wasn’t's” and “Yes you were’s.” Cain finally looses his temper and quickly approaches Caleb quickly. Cain leans down and picks the hobo up off of the ground with one arm. The beast begins to snarl into the hobo’s face. “I was looking at the damn people and the way that they interact with one another! Do you understand me?” Caleb is still laughing at his friend despite the way that Cain is threatening him. Caleb through his snickers continues to push this topic with the enraged Cain. “It’s okay! Heh-Heh. I knew that it was just a matter of time before you would find your sex drive that you lost so many centuries ago! Heh-Heh-Heh!” Cain releases his grasp on Caleb. The hobo falls to the roof violently and begins to walk away from his manager that is laughing like a hyena right now. As Cain exits, Caleb continues to shoot shots at t he massive warrior. “That’s okay buddy! Heh-Heh. If you have any questions about the way “you know what” works, don’t be afraid to ask! Heh-Heh-Heh.” Cain disappears in the darkness of the stairwell that leads down the steps of the building. Caleb remains on the building still laughing at the way that caught Cain checking out some women. The hobo is also taking more shots at his friend before he exits for good.
