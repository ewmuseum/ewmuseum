Locked up and tied down

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-28-02 I hesitated at first. It was probably my natural instincts but I couldn't stop now, I was so close to bliss. The knife touched down, it was cold on my skin. I dragged it across my chest, it was painful ecstacy. The knife came to the end of it's line, I looked down at the cut I had made accross my chest, it didn't seem complete. I wanted more. More pain. More pleasure. More blood to run down my chest. I made another cut accross the one that I had already made. Again I looked at the mark, I had made a X in the middle of my chest. Blood ran down my stomach, its thickening consistancy rippling as I moved. I was satisfied for a short time. I looked in the mirror to admire the 'art' I had made on myself. All of my problems and worries as a human flowed away as a high took over my mind and erased all that was wrong in the world. But now as I sit, huddled up in my bed I listen. I can hear them talking about me, sitting in their little rooms and talking about my 'condition'. Cameron's screams echo in my head, bouncing around and causing me to wince. He had won, hadn't he? I was in this place, this hospital with my baby in a dumpster out back, dead. Death.. Oh how I wish it were my friend, would come and take me away. If only I were so lucky. Tears come to my eyes, but I cannot hold them back like before. Now they flow freely from my ducts, falling on the white pillow and stain it with my pain. I feel the restraints being removed from my wrists and my ankles. I had been bad with the scapel and they produced my shackles once more. But now they were taking me away, but I didn't care where. Right now I was too scared, too weak, and too hurt to do much. That fight against the Doctor and the nurses earlier had left me hurting even more so. My stomach has been stitched back up, tighter than before so if I should burst out like that again the would hold. The sun reflects from my eyes and I close them, I had no use for the light. I hated the sun now..I hated the light. The light had turned me in to an outcast and I wanted out of the brightness. My wish was granted as I was lowered in to the back of a truck, more or less a moving van. I was on a stretcher, latched to the sides by my ankles and wrists. The van begins to move and my world fades away with the drug they inject in to my arm. Go away. Why can't it all go away.. When I wake all I can see above me is padding. But not the white sterile padding, rather the old and musty padding. I sit up in the bed I find myself in, no latches this time. A glass frame is before me and beside it a steel door. I blink slowly, where was I? As I slide off of the bed I make my way to that glass, looking out and seeing nothing but my own reflection. But outside they study me, I hear their whispers...Or were those the ones in my head? I no longer can tell. I slink down to a corner of the room, my knees to my chest while my head rests on them. Rocking.. Back and forth.. Innocence lost. I am Locked up and Tied Down.
