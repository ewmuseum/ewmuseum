Umirat.... (Pt 3. Possession)

19 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-19-02 He was no longer Ivan Stanislav. His regimented lifestyle was gone. His morals had fled. His compassion had dissipated. And his conscience was non-existent. He was a man possessed… Ivan surges to his feet, spurred forth by the pain he has caused Corg. He stomps around his fallen body, noting the blood of Corg stains the canvas, blood that should have been shed long ago. “Get up!!” he screams as he boots Corg roughly in the side of his head, “This is the price you pay, GET UP!” Corg gets to his hands and knees, a sopping trail of blood hangs from his mouth to the mat as Stanislav drives his knee into Corg’s back, knocking him back down to the mat. “GET UP!!” he roars again. Corg tries again, only for the wind to be blasted from his body by a double axe handle from Stanislav, his entire frame behind the blow. Hate consumes Stanislav as he breathes in furiously, works up his phlem, and hacks down on the back of Corg. ~~ Nate: I’m speechless folks. I don’t think I’ve ever witnessed such…such rage….ever before. ~~ The Russian Bear grabs Corg by the back of the head and lifts him over his head in a military press, like an athlete with a trophy, and drops him into a backbreaker! He loves the feeling of his wracked body of his knee, twisting and contorting from the punishment he has received. Three times Stanislav drives his forearm into the face of Corg, ensuring the blood continues to flow, and then slowly bends his body around his knee as if he was bending a piece of steel. “I could break you right now, Corg!” Ivan growls down at the bloody face that he shoves around his knee, “I could break you and kill you right now… but that would be too easy, da? You are not worth killing… not yet! I… have… only… STARTED!” Ivan slams his forearm over the bent neck of Corg which flips him over his knee to the mat. He does not move. Ivan stands as Speedy approaches him. “Ivan,” Riggs pleads, “I know you told me to prolong the match, but I can’t keep this going forever, you have to stop!!” “OUT OF MY WAY RIGGS!!” Ivan bellows, prompting Riggs to bound across the ring away from Stanislav. The Russian Bear rebounds off the ropes and slams his massive boot over Corg’s back, which covers half of it. With a quick snap of his arms, Ivan has Corg’s hands and pulls back in a standing surfboard! He hasn’t used this move for months, perhaps a year and with every ounce of pressure that he pulls, Corg’s body weakens and buckles under the might of Ivan Stanislav. The righteous might of The Russian Bear. Righteousness etched in the blood of The Silent Assassin. “Are you laughing now, Corg? Are you laughing now under the overbearing weight of Ivan Stanislav? DYAAHAAHAA!!” His laugh rocks the arena as Speedy Riggs yells to his Comrade again. “Ivan!! You have to break that! I have to do what the rules state!” screams Speedy. But Ivan ignores him completely, blinded by his hate. He looks down at Corg’s arms, the tendons within them stretching, his shoulders giving way, his arms being torn from their shoulders, “How dare you hurt her, Corg, how dare you cause her fear!!!!” He growls furiously as Corg’s head hangs limply. ~~ Nate: Folks, I am in shock…Corg is….he might be dead. ~~ For a second, Stanislav believes Corg is dead. He breaks the hold and lifts him by the head, surveying his battered and bashed face. He snorts loudly, realizing that indeed weak, raspy breaths still exit the shell of a body he once had. This was the man that disrespected her. This was the man that harmed her. This was the man that caused tears to exit her eyes. He had to pay… he had to pay the price… beating him was no longer enough… Ivan’s eyes bulge suddenly as a feeling of absolute revulsion overcomes him. He wraps both of his hands around his neck and squeezes, feeling the neck of Corg buckle and squirm between his hands. He hears him sputter and groan as he lifts his tiny body effortlessly above him. The lights of the camera’s echo in his sight, the cheers of the fans reverberate in his ears, but all is focused on Corg in order to make…. ….his breathing…. …stop. “You bastard…” Ivan growls between gritted teeth, “You bastard… she did nothing to you, she was innocent and you took advantage of her… she was innocent and you HARMED HER!! GRAAAAARRRGGGHH!!!” He shakes Corg as his body goes completely limp, “YOU MADE HER SUFFER IN FRONT OF THESE PEOPLE… YOU MADE HER LOOK WEAK!!! IT IS YOUR TURN…MONSTER!!!!” Riggs runs over to Ivan again, “Ivan, you have to stop, please stop, I don’t want to do this….” “Struggle, feel yourself let go Corg, panic… I know you can hear me… panic like you made her panic… fear like she feared. You should NEVER have wronged her.” “IVAN!!” Riggs screams as his shoulders fall, “1….2….” Ivan grins sadistically as blood pours from the face of Corg…. “3…4…” “Bleed….bleed it all out….” “5!! RING IT!! RING IT!!” Riggs calls for the bell and walks away from Ivan. ~~ Nate: Riggs called for the bell!!! He just Disqualified Ivan Stanislav!! He just…he just DQ’d his long time Comrade!! Steven: I never thought I’d see the day when Riggs would have a conscience, especially at Ivan’s expense!! ~~ Riggs turns, seeing that Ivan is not breaking the hold. “Oh God…” he mutters under his breath and runs to Ivan, seeing that look in his eyes many times before… but with never as much intensity. He wraps his arms around Ivan’s massive forearm and tugs, “Stop it Ivan, you are going to kill him!!” “GRRRRAAH!!!” Roars Stanislav, swinging reflexively and batting Riggs across the ring. He instantly replaces his hand around Corg’s neck, who’s turns blue. He shakes and breathes heavily, consumed by his emotions. Emotions locked away for years, and slams Corg against the turnbuckle, shaking his body, punishing it in it’s last waking moments. “UMIRAT!!! UMIRAT!!! UMIRAT!!” He screams, speaking in his native tongue… ~~ Nate: What is he saying?? ~~ The answer was simple… “Die….Die…Die.” He had committed to killing Corg. Killing him like the dog he was…
