The Cost of Benefits (Part 1 of 6 “Breaking Benefits” w/Rhett Locke)

5 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 5th Being part of a gang called the Benefit, located in the heart of Brooklyn, New York guaranteed Drew Hostyle everything he'd need in his near future. Money, cars, anything that he even went on a whim and asked for, was his. Of course in return he gave them something. He gave them exposure, he gave them press, he gave them funds and sponsors. Who knew that a major figure in professional wrestling would be able to continue being in this misunderstood, but clearly violent and vicious gang and keep his dedications to the federation at the same time? Not only was he in the Old School Wrestling federation, but he was making an impact just as well. And beginning March 10th, that impact would be made even bigger. This was because of Drew Hostyle's taking part in the historical match up at OSW's debut Pay Per View, Crowning Glory, in a match that would name the OSW's first ever International Champion. He'd be taking on Rhett Locke in the match, and ever since OSW fans and viewers can remember Drew and Rhett had been in the ring on opposite sides. Even last Saturday Night on Slam!, Drew and Frankie Bones, one of his partners in crime, got the best of both Rhett Locke and Michael Dame in a tag team match. However, nobody's even gotten word of why the two feel such anger towards each other. Nobody except the people who matter, Jane Reagan and Ed. The two put together the match, obviously for their own reasons. But their reasons must've stemmed out of something that occurred or is presently occurring here today. Maybe we'll get some insight into what that is soon, but watching them tear each other apart is great fun in itself. With the proper stimulus this match could only be better. But why these two men? The scene opens to show Drew Hostyle lying on a huge bed with thousands of pictures scattered around its dark blue comforters and pillows that were inside of his room in the Norfolk Waterside Marriott Hotel. Drew's wearing a pair of Karl Kani Boxers and instead that infamous do rag on his head, his hair is shown. His hair has small waves that shape on the surface of it all the way back through his head, but is not at all translucent. His thick hair in something of a small bush like that of NBA player Kobe Bryant's 'Freedom Fro' with the exception of a comb hanging out of it. The look seems to please him none the less as he looks towards to pictures and gives various facial expressions, depending on the pictures. The one he has in hands huge hands now looks something like a small, tiny baby black boy in a kitchen sink most likely naked. Only the upper portion on his little body is shown though. And he's smiling, at the camera with a mouth full of baby teeth. His smile's so wide though, it looks as if as if it'd pain someone else to imitate it at any given time. The boy's hair in the picture looks very similar to Drew's own hair right now. Tangled, and flying every which way. Drew's reaction to the picture is an angry stare. He turns the picture around and sees that the picture is labeled as 'First signs of weakness'. The handwriting doesn't look like Hostyle's from what we've seen of him and never do the handwritings on any of the other pictures. The conclusion can be drawn now that Drew has not written on these pictures from his childhood, but someone else has. Maybe various people. Drew doesn't confirm our superstitions though because he has now ripped various pictures with him rejoicing and being happy into parts. He snarls at the pictures of himself, lying on the ground or further away from his spot and arm's lengths on the bed. But then comes across a picture that most would regret seeing or at least put it away quickly. It was relating to the case that had occurred awhile back when Drew Hostyle had been a primary suspect in a twisted game of surprises that started with the death of a psychologist, her boyfriend, and some of her patience. This woman had been keeping a journal of what had been going on and the arrival of a new patient to her coincided with the death of all these people. Now this woman wasn't mentally unstable at any time during her life, as a matter of fact she was very sane during her studies on people and conducted her business very orderly and methodically. This picture however lay in his hands was a picture of a younger black guy getting his mug shoot taken. The man in the mug shot was unambiguously identified Drew Hostyle. The sign he was holding below his chin said so itself. Drew’s expression in the black and white picture though was different from all the others. The person or people who’d been labeling these pictures t their own accord had drawn a halo above Drew’s head to complement Drew’s expression in the picture, which was generally happy to be there. This label had looked as if it were carefully written compared to the others, which meant that more time had been dedicated to this picture then any other he had looked over this day. Drew squinted his eyes, thus making them more narrow and brought the picture closer to his face to read it specifically and the inscription read, “Reaping the Benefits.” Drew’s expression towards this picture wasn’t clear at all. His mouth still formed a straight line the way it did when he’d first began looking at the pictures. But soon after he tilted his head to the side and a smile emerged unwarranted or not. With this Drew set down all the pictures and swept them of his bed with one motion of his hands. When finished he threw on the nearest shirt to him, which happened to be the complimentary new OSW Slam! shirt he’d been given as being a part of it’s roster, the shirt was orange and white. And to coordinate with it he put on a pair of black pants with orange seaming through them, outlining it’s pockets. Forgetting entirely about his hair, he left his hotel room and took the stairs down to the first floor of the hotel. As he got their and stood waiting at the long line that were there before he was at the front desk for reservations and common concerns that they might’ve been having. Drew sighed when he caught first sight of the line and instead of heading to the back of it, cut everyone in line and went behind the desk where the in charge looked at him, shocked from his lack of respect for everyone else that’d been in line before him. Drew paid the woman no attention and grabbed a book for the bottom drawer of her desk that she’d penciled in for the listings of everyone that had a room in the hotel today. He looked through the book and went straight to the ‘S’. Samuel..Sessoms..Siegel..Soussan...Wait, passed it. He flipped back a few more pages and stopped on a page that started with Sessoms and ended with Simms. Drew then moved his finger up and down the rows until he stopped on one last name.. Shivan. Matt Shivan. Room 157. Drew then grabbed the entire book away from the desk and took it along with him up to Room 157. The people in lines looked at him in disgust and one was even bold enough to flip him off behind his back, the others in line laughed. Drew stopped walking. He turned around and looked at the man that everyone was laughing at. Drew then threw the book he was holding right at his chest for him to catch, and he did. After this he keep walking, up the steps and down the hall to the room marked 157. Once he’d finally made it to the room, he looked at the sign on the door that read “Do Not Disturb” and tore it off the handle. He banged he fist on the door once. No answer. He banged it again. “Read the sign on the door, please!” Matt Shivan said to the person on the other side of the door. So Drew knocked on the door again, but this time he kept banging his fist on the door until it was answered. Matt came to the door, his hair flung in a wrap and his face with some facial cream all over it for unknown reasons and to top it off a white Hugh Hefner type robe that the hotel probably provided him with. “I thought I said to read the SIGN ON THE..” Matt wipes the cream that is above his eye sockets and sees Drew Hostyle cowering over his mere 6 feet in height. “You mean this sign?” Drew asks holding the sign in his hands and crumbling it into a ball then dropping it to the ground carelessly. Matt bends down on all fours to pick his sign up, but drew sees him going for it and rests his foot at the tip of the ball of paper, so that Matt couldn’t grab it up. Matt is, by now shaking from head to toe and looks up at Drew from the ground and stands back up on his feet. “Wha..wha..what can I help you with today, meh..meh, Mister Hostyle? Shivan stutters. “Get a camera and follow me.” Drew says, not taking his eyes away from Shivan, nor moving his feet off the crumbled paper. “Bu..but I’m an interviewer..sir. I don’t know any..th..thing about being a cameraman.” Drew looks into Matt Shivan’s eyes and gives off the expression that he better learn today. Matt understands completely and says. “Well Mother ah..always said that I should learn new things everyday!” Drew manages a smile for the struggling man and Matt goes back into his room and in a matter of minutes is back with a camera in hand. “I..I hope I didn’t keep you waiting to long, sir.” Matt stammers. “I happen to borrow this from the hotel’s archive.” Drew nods and then motions Matt over to him. Matt takes a baby stp forward and leans into drew to hear what he has to say. “Pick that piece of paper off the ground. It’s littering, you know.” Matt obediently bends down, picks up the pieces of paper without compliant, and then stuffs them into the pocket of his black leather pants. “Rah..ready to go..sir?” Matt asks, the camera on his back shaking. Drew walks a few steps and Matt catches on to the answer of his question as the both head down the steps of the hotel and out to the underground parking lot, where Drew’s silver 2002 Porche Boxster is waiting. He grabs the keys out of his pocket and gets into the driver’s seta of the car. Matt goes to get into the e of the Porche, but through the tented windows of the car, Drew shakes his head no and motions for him to slide into the back seat. Matt does as he’s told and gets into the car, and the seconds he’s in, Drew pulls off in it. After about fifteen or twenty minutes of driving in silence. Drew drives into a deserted alley way and parks his car. He then exits the car by himself. “Stay in there I call you.” Drew stays, tapping on the outside of his Porche’s window to get Matt’s attention. Matt gives the thumbs up signal and smiles. Drew shakes his head and walks further into the alleyway, where he looks both ways, then crawls into a small grey door that is nearly hidden by the black and brown surroundings of the alley. About five minutes later Drew leaves the door with two other men, who had been mentioned in Drew’s promos before. Both were black. One was the Supremem Benefactor, the founder and person in charge of Drew’s gang, The Benefit. The other was Drew’s personal big brother or mentor, Jamal. “Shivan, get your ass out here.” yells Jamal, a smaller man than the man beside him, The Supreme Benefactor, but he yelled with just as much authority. Matt pondered to himself whether to come out or not, Drew said not to leave unless he called him..and he hadn’t called him, really. He didn’t really want to leave the car and be surrounded by all these men anyways..there were big and intimidating, not even his black pants would hide stains from this encounter. Drew nodded his head towards his car, knowing what Shivan was apparently asking himself. Matt stepped out of the car, slowly and turned on the camera quickly, to have some proof if anything happened to him. “Look at this man, Supreme brotha. He must be outside his mind, coming to see his big Brothas, lookin like this.” “Humpft” The Supreme Benefactor agrees. Drew then realizes that he hadn’t done anything with his hair that day, he’d even been planning on getting it cut, but got too caught up in his pictures. “I was going to go get it cut, right after I talked to ya’ll..” “And look at him now, Supreme Brotha, makin excuses as to why he’s lookin like he just rolled outta bed. You approach us in suits, Hostyle. You ain’t got one, then carry yo ass to a bum on da streets and ask him fo one, but don’t you ever come to us like this again. EVER.” Jamal barked. “Yes sir.” Drew answered, not ashamed of himself. “What was so important that you couldn’t wait to disrespect your brothas because of it, Drew?” The Supreme Benefactor spoke up. “I was in my hotel room.” Drew started. “Looking at some old pictures I had out.” “How old?” The Supreme Benefactor questioned. “Old enough that he saw himself outside the Benefit. You can see it in his eyes.” Jamal answered for Drew. “You know we don’t look back at life before us, Hostyle. There was no life for you before us. That damn woman is probably filling your head with doubt again, doubt in your brothas.” “I WASN’T LOOKIN AT THAT!” Drew hollered, and both men were quiet. Shocked..but quiet. “I was lookin at the labels that ya’ll put up there. On my mug shot from jail, wit da halo over my head.” “Ah..“Reaping the Benefits.” The Supreme Benefactor remembered. “it was a beauttil picture, Drew.” “You think dat shit was beautiful? Those were the worse days of ma life, beautiful my ass!” Drew screamed at the men. “You think I’m reaping some kinda bull shit benefits, but I’m the one ya’ll asses is feedin off of. You need me. I don’t need you.” “Listen here, little bit..” Jamal begins at the moves closer to backing Drew into a corner. The Supreme Benefactor pushes Jamal away from Drew and instead gets in Drew’s face and glares at him. “So what are you saying Drew?” The Supreme Benefactor asks. “I’m sayin I quit, I’m out as of now.” Drew replies, determined. “AFTER ALL THE GOOD TIMES?! AFTER ALL I DID FOR YOU?! YOU’RE JUST GONNA WALK OUT ON ME!?” The Supreme Benefactor roars, then charges towards Drew with Jamal doing the same soon after. Both men start pounding away on Drew’s stomach and body. Throwing things, whatever they could get their hands on, they then stepped back to overview the damage they’d done and were a bit shocked at the job they’d done themselves. “Damn” Jamal sighs. “Just get that cracka with the camera away from here, and everything’s gonna be ok again. We didn’t need his ass anyways. I’ve got an idea for him..” Jamal then looks to Matt Shivan who’s now formed a steady flood on the ground under his pants and drops the camera with his sweating hand and runs off out of the alley and into the sunset..We zoom into where the camera was dropped and the scene fades to black.
