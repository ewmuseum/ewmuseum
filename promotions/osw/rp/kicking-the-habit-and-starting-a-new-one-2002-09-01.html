Kicking The Habit...And Starting a New One

1 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sunday, September 1st, 2002 'So what....exactly hurts?' The doctor at St. Joseph's Medical Center in Philadelphia, PA stood over Nick Casey. Nick was donning a gown, while the portly doctor examined him. Typical old-school style doctor, salt-and-pepper hair, white lab coat, and of course the thick-rimmed specticles, but Nick was confident he knew what he was doing. This was, after all, one of the best hospitals in the world. 'Did you see the match?' Nick questioned. The doctor started to stutter. 'Well, I was kind of busy and....' Nick cracked his arm. 'I got launched into a section of steel chairs. I fell 30 feet onto the ring. I dove 35 feet onto another man.' The doctor still looked puzzled. He scratched his chin, and looked at Nick. Maybe this really wasn't going to be as easy as he thought. 'So, what exactly are you saying?' There was a long, awkward pause. ANC just stared back at the doctor, with a quizzical look in his eyes. 'Everything hurts, buddy. Just give me some pain killers, and let me be on my way.' 'Oh no you don't,' the doctor said, patronizing Nick. 'I watched that part, and read all about it in the papers. You're not one to be trusted with something like morphine or Vicodan.' 'What are you talking about?' The doctor started to flip the papers of Nick's chart, and pulled out a piece of paper on OSW stationary. The doctor began to mumble something incoherantly to himself, as Nick just sat there and stared. 'Hmm...' he said, quietly. 'It appears as though Jane Reagan wants you blood tested?' 'What the FUCK!?' Nick stood up, shouting in the middle of the ER. The doctor flipped through papers, aimlessly. 'Yes, Ms. Reagan wants you tested for performance enhancing drugs, PCP, marijuana, cocaine, and crystal meth.' The doctor reached into the drawers and pulled out a small bottle and a syringe. 'Blood or urine?' 'Give me the piss cup.' He walked down the hall, and slid into the doctors' lounge. He looked around, and picked up a lab coat off the desk. Picking up the urine cup again, he walked into the hallway where an older woman passed by. 'Excuse me, miss!' The woman stopped and turned around. 'Yes, Dr......Prassad?' the woman said, questioningly. 'I'm terribly sorry miss, but I need you to fill this sample for me, it's another test we need to run.' 'Oh, but I was just leaving.' Nick smiled, right at the woman.'It's just to be sure!' The woman took the cup, and a few minutes later she gave it back to Nick. Nick went back into the ER room where the doctor was still filling out insurance forms from Nick's driver's license.'Took you long enough.' 'Yeah, sorry.' 'You're free to go, the test results will go back to Reagan.' Nick smiled and took his ID back. 'Thanks doc,' he said, taking his back with him. A small bag fell out, filled with a small pill of some kind. The doctor picked it up and yelled down the hallway. 'Sir! You dropped your pills!' Nick's heart sank. 'Oh shit. Thanks man, I appreciate it.' 'Take care, stay clean.' He looked in the bag. Angeldust. He smiled, and yelled back. 'Don't worry. I'll stay clean...' The Next Day... Jane Reagan opened up an envelope from the Blood Services at St. Joseph's Medical Center. 'What the fuck!?' She flipped through the papers. 'Casey tested positive as a 59-year-old woman?'
