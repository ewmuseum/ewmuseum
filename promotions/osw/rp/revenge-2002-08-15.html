Revenge

15 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-15-02 It was dark in the office that Ivan Stanislav sat in, far later than he normally was accustomed to. When you typically wake up at 4:30 AM every morning, you tend to go to sleep earlier than normal, but for Ivan Stanislav he had found himself even sleeping in till 5:00 AM!! The large circular clock on the wall ticked loudly in the background as Stanislav sat in a large, hard wooden chair and stared at a television set complete with VCR. The only sources of light came from some sort of lamp outside of the building, but the frosted over windows barely allowed it pale light to enter into the room, the television screen itself, and one lone incandescent light high above Stanislav, hanging from a precariously thin wire. The static played across the front of the television, casting odd and irregular shadows over the face of the Russian Bear, who hulked in his chair and grabbed a remote control. Ivan Stanislav had stayed relatively quiet throughout the week, save for his column that he had submitted to the OSW to have distributed to the applicable mailing lists. For the most part he had weighed what was going on in the OSW and chose to remain in the background, but one glaring fact continued to burn in his mind. Corg had victimized Tempest. Even Reno Davis, who had broken apart Ivan and Tempest, had not defiled and assaulted Tempest like Corg was about to. He could not help himself and had to do something and for once, he did not know why. For his entire life, Stanislav did what the order said, he did what the State said, and never did anything without confirmation and authorization. But whenever he saw the monitor backstage and Corg began to go beyond wrestling and into personal attacks, that was it. Something had possessed Ivan Stanislav, something that threw every thought of his out of his mind and sent him on a collision course with Corg. He had to do something. Not because of the State or because of the order, but because of the person. If Tempest were not present, he would have done far more to Corg than merely thrown him deep into the awaiting fans. He’d have done far more to him for victimizing such a beautiful person such as Tempest. Ivan swallowed and hit a button on the remote as the Tempest/Corg match from last Saturday Slam played before him. He stared angrily, boring into the screen and staring at Corg as Tempest started the match taking it straight to him. He watched her, even in the heat of a battle with a dangerous individual, almost seem to have fun while she wrestled. Every time Corg would land a move or a blow, however, Stanislav would jerk just slightly and suck in a breath. And as the match started to get ugly, The Russian Bear leaned forward and watched, pain in his eyes all the same. The match took the turn when he breathed the red substance into Tempest’s eyes and blinded her. After that it was all Corg. The coward, he had cheated to try to defeat Tempest. Corg landed a punch hard into the gut of Tempest and kicked her to the mat. Ivan began to breath faster and heavier as he put his boot over her neck and choked her blatantly. He sighed as Riggs shoved Corg off of Tempest, Riggs had lived up to his word and asserted himself, much as Stanislav had asked. Riggs was a good man, if not a bit stupid, but he knew what was going on within that ring. It was the bite that Corg landed. The bite on Tempests neck that sent Stanislav running down the hallway towards the ring. Whenever he placed his teeth upon her slender neck and bit, Stanislav knew it was time to intervene. However, while he was running to the ring backstage, he had not seen what happened during the interim. Corg had grabbed Tempest after a mule kick and shoved her into the corner. A quick knee to her gut later, his tongue was snaking out to lick her face……. Suddenly the tape froze as Ivan stared at the still image. It was a close up of Tempest, her face red from the liquid that was spit at her, her face twisted in what appeared to be terror, and tears streamed her face. It was the same face he had seen before… he has seen before when she ran away from him at Head on Collision. It sent a shiver up his spine. The furrowed brow, the closed eyes, and the tears on her face. For ever night he closed his eyes, when he thought of her, it was that face that he had seen. The face of Tempest, horrified, hurt, and betrayed. Ivan closed his eyes and gritted his teeth, and then looked at the television again. A look of rage in his eyes. Corg had caused her such an amount of pain… Corg had done such a thing to her. He had caused her such anguish…. Ivan growled and stood, his eyes never leaving the television screen. And Corg would fight Stanislav at Slam… and finally… Corg would learn what true pain was…Corg would learn the error of his ways. He made The Little Storm cry… it was time for The Russian Bear to fix it…
