The Day The Earth Stood Still

9 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07-09-02 He was sitting motionless in the darkest corner of the darkest room in the darkest corridor of the United Center. His head rested on his clenched fist, his blinking eyes glowing softly a devilish red. Tonight was a mark against the world. Tonight he had done what no one could contemplate; tonight he had sealed his destiny. Watching from another corner of the room, a 3’11” creature watched silently, his eyes glazed over, motionless. He simply stared across at the great Beast, sitting silently, thoughts racing through his head. ………Nate: ETERNAL DAMNATION!!! ETERNAL DAMNATION!!! ETERNAL DAMNATION!!! HESSIAN JUST PLANTED HIS OWN CREATOR WITH HIS FINISHING MOVE!!!!!!!!! Robbie: WHY HESSIAN? WHY? WHY? WHY? WHY?……… ………Nate: Why in the hell is Hessian doing this?! He’s just returned and already he is destroying someone! Robbie: WHY DOES IT HAVE TO BE HIS OWN MENTOR???????……… ……… Nate: HESSIAN HAS SNAPPED!!! THERE’S BEEN NO PROVOCATION HERE WHATSOEVER BUT HESSIAN IS DESTROYING VAMPIR!!!……… ………Nate: HOLY *BEEP!*!!! HESSIAN JUST *BEEP!*ING STABBED VAMPIR NOSFERATU IN THE BACK!!!!!!!!!……… ……… Hessian: MARTYRS, ALL WILL BE COME, DECEASED, ALL WILL BE!!!……… Robbie: …………………………Hessian………is back……………… A lifetime had flashed before Hessian’s eyes in a mere manner of seconds. A burning inferno, lost loved ones, a rebirth and then…nothing. It was as though he had done away with the only creature that made him whole. Hessian groaned. Tonight he had returned a greater, more devastating force than ever before. Tonight Hessian had shown the world a new side of him. A relentless side. A bad side. Hessian’s mind plagued him endlessly at the events that had transpired. Hessian barely moved. He couldn’t move. If he did, another would surely fall to his might. Hessian had to stay put. Vampir Nosferatu is dead at the hands of Hessian. That was the bottom line. A month of frustration and hatred had built up whilst Hessian healed, and on this night it had been vented in the wrong direction. Or had it? ‘Old School’ Dave Gibson had put him on the shelf, and Hessian was powerless, forced to watch as one by one his precious Cult was broken down and destroyed at the hands of his very nemeses…and then Erik Kelly. Bethany Yamato…Sepiroth Du Luc…they were to be nurtured and raised in Hessian’s image, as he was in Vampir’s all those years ago. That was all over now. The dreams and hopes of the destruction of tomorrow had been diminished in a flood of oozing lifeblood from the Ultimate Evil. Hessian’s future of devastation had been partially blinded by the ignorance of those around him…but now there were no others. An Olden Beast and an Imp are all that remain. “Where did it all go wrong, Minion?” Minion daren’t reply, he knew fine well it was rhetorical, and even then if he did reply, in the state Hessian is in he would soon find himself at the bottom of his own shallow grave. The world was in shock, for the biggest threat to the wrestling world had been vanquished. Ritchie Syxx and ZaC had died on OSW television before, but never was someone of this calibre on the other end of the knife…literally. This was Vampir Nosferatu. A beast that could not be killed. A creature which had dominated Primetime Championship Wrestling and gained high awards and respect in wrestling…and now he was dead. A champion was buried in the ashes, a champion was gone. Hessian had warned them all. The letter, the goat’s skull, everything pointed to Vampir Nosferatu, everything pointed to his demise, yet they did nothing. Warnings had been sent, the actions taken out, yet after all the dust settled, fires died and smoke cleared…the thrill of the kill was replaced by a void of thought. The situation was simple, Vampir Nosferatu, a creature of the dark, a Continental and Global Champion is dead at the hands of that which he birthed, Hessian. Hessian blinked once more through his glowing demonic gaze into the darkness, pictures flying around in the black before him. He had destroyed an Evil like no other, and now the new Apocalypse would begin. Vampir hadn’t uttered a single word during Hessian’s return, he had tried to run, tried to fight, all the while his tail between his legs, but never uttered one word. Vampir Nosferatu knew in his mind his doing was wrong, for he had been on a downward spiral ever since, and Hessian was the one to put the final nail in the coffin. But…could Vampir Nosferatu ever really be killed? It certainly seemed so, as evident from the blood of Vampir spilled everywhere. Hessian sighed and stood up slowly. The Death of Vampir Nosferatu was to be remembered as The Day The Earth Stood Still, and the time a New Evil took charge of the reigns. The student had finally succeeded the mentor, the Prince finally overthrown the King. Vampir is dead and Hessian is now. Hessian’s eyes suddenly flashed that demonic, blood red, and he looked at Minion. Minion could easily tell of Hessian’s state from the look in his eyes, what he’s thinking mentally, how he remembers his Mentor, Vampir Nosferatu…but then…Minion knew deep down if Vampir Nosferatu, a creature of Hessian’s own blood, was destroyed for what he had done, then Dave Gibson and Erik Kelly are dead men walking. Hessian growled and closed his eyes for a few seconds, composing each thought. Finally he opened his eyes again, the glare almost filling the room with a dark light, highlighting the sadistic grin that slowly began to spread across his face. “I’m baaaaack…” Fin
