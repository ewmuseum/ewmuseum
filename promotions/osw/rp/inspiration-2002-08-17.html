Date: August 17, 2002 Caridad looks up from the television program she was watching as the doorknob of her hotel room door jiggles. She had been on edge for the past week, ever since Jesus had told her about Frankie Bones breaking into their hotel room. She catches her breath, wondering who is entering the room. A few seconds later, the door swings wide, and Jesus stepped into the room. 'It's me.' He tosses his gymbag to the side against the wall as Caridad lets her held breath slowly escape in relief. He leans back against the wall, and braces his face against his hands as he sighs. Jesus' eye catches sight of Caridad on the bed, and while it may be fading, he notes that she seemed worried. 'Que passa, Dadi?' She looks up at her boyfriend, and forces a smile to her lips. 'Nada... I just... I'm just still a bit shaken about Frankie being in our room.' Jesus sighs and slips onto the bed beside her, draping his arm over her shoulders and pulling her in tight. 'It's ok, Dadi. It's ok. Forget Frankie; he's not going to do that again.' 'How do you know?' A short squeeze of her shoulders precedes his answer. 'Frankie is just trying to get to me. He's not going to do the same thing again; as much as he may seem to be a bit slow, the guy is pretty cunning and he'd know I'd be ready for something like that.' Caridad pulls back a bit from Jesus, the worry creeping back into her expression. 'So... he's going to do something worse?' 'Everything Frankie's done was to get under my skin... and last week he succeeded. I got angry during our match, and that's when I lost it. When he threw Shanda at me, it was like a light switched on. He managed to lower me into playing his game, and he'll win at that every time.' Slowly, Jesus rises from the bed and walks over to the desk on the other side of the room. Almost absentmindedly, he picks up a small stress ball and tosses it in the air a few times. After a few tosses, he catches it in a fist before pausing to look out the window. 'So... I change the game.' Jesus turns his head slightly to look at Caridad, and a mischievous grin spreads across his face as an idea forms in his mind. Caridad's worried expression is exchanged for one of piqued curiosity. 'What are you planning?' She smiles a bit at her boyfriend's contagious smile. Jesus drops the stress ball on the window sill and picks up the phone beside the bed. Quickly, he starts punching a number onto the pad as he grins at Caridad. 'You'll just have to see, I guess.'
