Corg

14 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 14, 2002 (OOC: Download ‘Hurt’ by the Nine Inch Nails and listen to it while reading, it’s the official ‘soundtrack’ for this promo :-P) “Hey, Raiyn!” I turned around, only to receive a massive fist slamming into my face. Blood, it was on my nose, in my eyes. The feelings of that cold floor of the sophomore hallway, oh the feeling will be forever imbedded into my mind. “You’re a faggot… a pussy faggot!” Those were his last words, as he kicked me in the gut, and ran down the halls with his jock friends, whom were laughing at me. School. I hated school. … That was me, two years ago, in high school. I was a geek, a goth, a tall and skinny kid whom everybody made fun of. School… Thurston High School, where Corg was born… … People glared at me as I walked down the narrow hallway. They would stare at my many piercings, my huge bondo pants, and my funny red hair. They would say things like, “What a loser!” and “Eww, get away from me you freak!” But the most common thing to call me was ‘fag’ or ‘faggot’. I was used to getting called names. So it was no big deal. But the one thing I didn’t like was the jocks. The four jocks, Darrel Engram, Chris Powers, Jeff Ryan, and Derek Glenn. Every day, they would track me down, and mess with me. Now sometimes they would just make fun of me and push me around, I didn’t mind that. But usually they would beat the hell out of me, to a point where I could barely move. They were fierce, insecure little preppies, and I hated them. I was only 16. 16 years old… having to put up with so much shit. I would go to the school pool during my free hours, and take a swim. It would relax me, and allow me to forget about all my troubles. I would swim in the pool every day, usually... until one day… I was casually swimming, back-stroking across the pool, and I heard a voice. “Isiic…” It kept calling out my name. Over and over. I pretended not to hear it at first, but I would give in soon enough. “Isiic...” “What?! Who’s there?” I demanded. No answer. The pool room was very silent. I could see the reflection of the water on the walls of the dimly lit room. The water settled down, and all I could hear was myself breathing. Suddenly, I felt something grab my leg… And before I knew it, I was pulled down into the water. I could feel multiple hands grabbing at me, and trying to punch me. I opened my eyes, and just as I suspected, there was Darrel, Chris, Jeff, and Derek, trying to drown me. I fought to rise up to the top, but Jeff grabbed me by the neck, and began to choke me. I fought and fought, trying to survive for at least ten more seconds… I could feel my life slipping away, as my eyes closed. They let go of me, just as I began to engulf water down my lungs. I rose to the top, inhaling heavily and panting rapidly. They sat on the side of the pool, laughing… sadistically, laughing. Why me… Back then, I was still exploring my own spirituality. I had powers, but very little. I could enter my realm whenever I pleased, I could connect with other’s minds, but not all. But I didn’t really care about any of that, I was still growing… I just wanted one thing… I wanted to be loved. I wanted someone to hold me when I cried, someone who could understand everything I was going through. Unfortunately, I had no one. My parents died when I was young, and my brother Rhett was no where to be found. I had myself, only myself to talk to, on the count of everyone in school hated me because I looked ‘different’. Well fuck ‘em, I didn’t need them. What good did they ever do me, anyway? At least I still have my sanity… And even that wouldn’t last me for much longer. Luckily, the pool incident happened on the last day of my sophomore year, so I didn’t have to put up with my ‘bullies’ the next day. Summertime sucked, as it always did. I had no where to go. No home, nothing. I would train in Realm 2 for hours, usually all day. This summer would be the summer in which I was ‘promoted’ to Realm 2, the place where I learned my skills, techniques, and senses of fighting. But at the time, I was only doing it to keep myself away from suicide. I’ve had depression all my life, and it struck me the worst during my school days. I would slit my wrists, occasionally for fun. Yeah, I know it’s sick, but what did I have to lose? During the summer, not only did I train in Realm 2, but I also attended shows for local bands/underground bands on a weekly basis. Hopesfall, Shai Hulud, Most Precious Blood, Poison the Well… only a few of the bands in which I would attend their shows. Heh, I stole TV’s and pick-pocketed people in order to get the money to go to the shows. It was pretty funny, people were so stupid. But none the less, summertime would pass, and I would start another boring and painful school year. But my junior year, thankfully, would be my last. I skipped the first day of my junior year, I was too afraid to go. Yes, I said afraid. First days were always the worst, those were the days that I would get the shit kicked outta me the most. And it was a very good thing that I skipped, because on that very same day, I had the most wonderful thing happen to me… I went to a Nine Inch Nails concert that night, using the money I got from a car I broke into. It was a rainy night, and I was hyped. Rain always gave me rush, especially when I was about to listen to hours of dark industrial, which was my favorite genre of music. The show began, and I joined along with the chorus of cheers as Trent Reznor appeared. They played ‘Terrible Lie’ as their opening song, and it was great. I moshed a bit, and crowd surfed a bit, but for some reason I didn’t really feel like being in the masses of humanity that night, something drew me away… As I neared towards the back of St. Andrews Hall, in Detroit, I saw a girl… She was sitting 20 or 25 feet above me, on the little balcony which leaned over a portion of the crowd. She was beautiful. Icy blue eyes, blue hair, and she had just the cutest look on her face. It was as if my heart had stopped, and time began to pass slowly, like a scene in one of those cheesy teen movies. I knew I had to get on the balcony, I just had to. So I began pushing through the crowd, as I inched closer, and closer, until I finally reached the small stairs which led to the balcony. I climbed them, as if I were some muscular hero, flexing my heroic muscles as I stepped towards glory. Fuck being a hero. The last thing I need is to be fake. The pulsing sound of a heavy beat vibrated my body, as ‘Closer’ began playing. I reached the final step, turned to right, and there she was. Leaning forward, over the balcony, was the girl. The girl. I approached her, as if I had done this a hundred times before, even though I’ve only done it once of twice, this time being the second. I tapped her on the shoudler. “Hi.” I said in a nerdy tone. She turned towards me, and looked into my eyes. I almost fainted. “Hi.” She said. I didn’t know what it was about this girl… She was wearing huge, blue, Caffeine pants, a small Saves the Day shirt, and as many piercings as I had. I felt like a complete dork, as I was already one at my school. “Uh…” I didn’t know what to say, I was mesmerized by her, and I didn’t know why. “I just wanted to say…” “I wanna fuck you like an animal!” Screamed the vocalist of NIN, as he continued singing the lyrics of Closer. Perfect timing… She giggled as she placed her hand over he black lips. “Perfect timing…” Wow, that was weird. Just as I said it in my realm, she said it in reality. She looked just as old as I did, only a lot prettier. I was drawing a blank, I remained quiet, just like I did in school. “My name’s Shyme. Shyme Orien.” She said, as she introduced herself to me. She extended her hand, as I extended mine. We shook hands, and even that little piece of bonding made me like her all the more. Shyme Orien… Shyme Orien. “Quite an original name,” I said. “I like it. My name is Isiic Raiyn.” She smiled at me, as she released her hand from mine. “Your name’s cute.” She giggled, again. “I like it.” From here, I had nothing else to say. I just wanted to stare into her blues eyes, as a source of protection. “So… are you from around here?” “Uh, yeah, actually I am.” I said. “I live in Redford.” She gasped, her eyes widened as she smiled. “Really? I just moved to Redford all the way from Toronto!” I was shocked. This beautiful girl, resides in my town? I couldn’t believe it, I thought I was dreaming. But I stayed cool, keeping calm. “Wow, what a coincidence. That’s great!” “So, anyways, how old are you?” She asked me, this time it was her staring into my eyes, mesmerized. “I’m 16.” “Oh, me too.” This was getting weird. A girl, whom I thought was pretty cool, is my age, AND lives in the same city as me! What the hell… She began walking towards the couch, which was against the very back wall of the balcony. At this point I didn’t really care about the concert, I had my mind set on Shyme. She sat down, as I followed, like the nerd that I was. She began fumbling throughout the pockets of her blue pants, only to reveal a tube, with many colors and designs. It was a bong. A small bong, but still, a bong… used for smoking marijuana, dumbass. The pot was already smoking in the bong, obviously she had already been using it not too long ago. She took a big huff, and inhaled. She turned to me, winked, and exhaled a massive amount of smoke. I had never seen that much of exhaled weed before, never. She rubbed her nose, itched her eyes, and turned towards me. “You want some.” I nodded. I had smoked weed a few times before, so it’s not like this was totally new to me. I usually liked to get high, just not all of the time. I inhaled, and exhaled. We smoked through the entire song of ‘The Wretched’, which was the perfect song to get high to. Many songs had passed, as we sat back, high as fuck, letting the music drift us away. ‘Hurt’ began playing, which was my personal favorite of the Nine Inch Nails. Weird strobe lights flickered throughout St. Andrews, as Shyme leaned over to me, and whispered in my ear. “Are you feeling it, too?” At first I was confused, but when she grabbed onto my face and began to kiss me, I caught her drift. Her black lips touched mine, as we embraced. It was a very passionate kiss, indeed, and I loved it. Sure, we were high, but who cares? I liked this girl a lot, and I was already making out with her on the first night… I love… “...You.” she said, as she kept the ‘lip-lock’ going. What the hell was that? Could she hear what I said, in my realm? All this time, I thought I was the only person in the world who had so much control over their mind that they could form it into one giant sphere of senses, while remaining in reality. And this was known as ‘my realm.’ I tried not to think about it too much. I didn’t want to waste a moment like this. It was all perfect. I loved this girl, and yet I barely knew her. She let go, and sat back down, relaxing… catching her breathe. I still couldn’t believe… “Wow.” I said, exhausted. “Wow,” She replied. “I’m really high. And it made me enjoy that even more.” She smiled at me, and I smiled back. I didn’t know if she liked me the same, and I didn’t care. As long as this moment lasted, I would be perfectly happy. “And you could have it all…” Trent Reznor sang into the microphone, still playing ‘Hurt’. We sat there, practicing at being silent. I didn’t know what to do or say. I couldn’t think. People moshed, songs were played, and soon enough, the concert ended. Shyme and I walked out of St. Andrews Hall togther, my arm around her neck, her hand around my hips. We were still high, and we didn’t talk. We stayed silent. We said our goodbyes, she took one cab, and I took another. The ride home was definitely 'a ride'. I thought about what went on that night… I thought about the making-out, and my realm… and it all traced back to Shyme. I just couldn’t get her out of my mind. She was perfect, absolutely perfect. I was still feeling the ‘high’ as I paid the cab driver his $6.32, and exited the cab. He sped away as I looked upon a large warehouse. From the outside, the warehouse looked like just, well… a warehouse. But on the inside, it was a training center for fighters. Boxers, kick boxers, martial artists, or people like me… straight-up, Ku-Moei fighters. I walked up the long pavement to the warehouse, which I knew so well, and entered. This was my home away from… erm, this was just one of the I stayed when I was homeless. There were rings, wrestling rings, or boxing rings, whichever you’d like to call it. Padded mats, punching bags, weights, and blood stained floors. It didn’t seem like there was anyone there, on the count of it was 2am. I walked past all of the equipment and training objects, until I reached a back room. It was dark, almost ‘too dark’ as some would like to say. I casually walked in, and traveled down the stairs which very few knew about. I took one final step off the final stair, as I looked upon my surroundings. Rusty walls, torches, and pentagrams. This was Realm 2, the place where I trained. My eyes became blurry, and before I knew it, I was asleep. It was a long night, and it would be an even longer day… I awoke, only to find myself drooling on my black, 40’ Caffeine pants. I rubbed the ‘sleep crust’ out of my eyes, and chuckled. I looked around me as I saw people, students, staring at me. I was at school. How I got there, I had no clue. I stood up, stretched out, and gave them all the finger. I must’ve walked to school during my sleep or something. I heard familiar sounds of a bell ringing, and I knew I had to get to class. I was still feeling a bit drowsy, but I made it down the junior hallway. I knew my classes already, I had them mailed to Realm 2 over the summer. I entered classroom 2B, not knowing where I was going or what I was doing. It was like I was being drawn to the classroom… not like the night before, though. I took my seat, just as the teacher was taking attendance. I couldn’t help but to laugh, as this would be my first day, and everyone else’s second. Yep, I was hungover alright. “Rutherford… Raiyn…” “Here!” I exclaimed to the teacher, whom was known to us as Mrs. ‘Bitch’, although her real name was Patty Denton. This was French class, my favorite class of all… and to think, I actually liked it. I hated all the rest of my classes, they were boring and easy, I passed them all with ease. But I enjoyed this one, it was interesting. As Mrs. Denton finished the rollcall, she stepped in front of the class, and began to speak. “Hello class,” She said, as she looked around the room at the brick walls which were covered by French posters. “You all know me, I’m Mrs. Denton, and you’ve all had me last year for French 3.” Yeah, this was French 4. “This year is going to be a bit different than last year… you will have a new classmate.” A new classmate? We didn’t need a new classmate… we were all dorks, and none of us liked new classmates. And new classmates usually meant a band of preps were coming to take over your class. I pulled off my black hood, revealing my fucked up red hair, as I stared at the doorway, jaw dropped and all… She stepped through, like a princess, a dark princess. Huge, shiny pants, a small Nine Inch Nails shirt, medium length blue hair, icy blue eyes, and those familiar black lips… “Class, meet your new classmate, Shyme Orien.” Shyme… It was her, the same girl that I made out with the night before, what the fuck? “Shyme, go take a seat over by, uh…” Mrs. Denton peered around the room, looking for an open seat. “Over by Isiic, the funny looking kid with the red hair.” I ignored Mrs. Bitch’s rude comment, I just stared at Shyme. She smiled at me and took the seat next to me. “So we meet again…” She whispered. I chuckled a shy chuckle. This was all too weird. First, I found out she is my age, then she lives in my town, and she attends my school, and now she sits next to me? All too weird. All through class, I stared at the blank chalkboard in front of me, thinking, pondering… Her… I got high with her, I made out with her, and I loved her. God, it was great. Does she feel it, too… She said she did the night before, but I didn’t think she felt the same thing I did. “I do.” She whispered in my ear, as if she were responding to… My realm. The ball rang, ending class, as we all exited, Shyme and I were walking slowly in the back of the line of hungry pigs whom were known as my classmates. We walked through the halls, together, as she held my hand, and kissed me on the cheek. We stopped, dead in the middle of the hallway, staring into each other’s eyes. “Isiic…” She said to me, as the crowd of kids bumped into us. “I barely even know you, and we just met last night, but…” There was a long pause, and suddenly that long pause was turned into me getting shoved up against a locker by the kids who’ve sworn to make my life a living hell… The jocks, the preps. Darrel, Chris, Jeff, and Derek. They held me up by my AFI sweatshirt, as Darrel slapped me in the face. “What did I tell you, Raiyn?” He scolded at me, as if he were my mom. My dead mom. “You’re gay! That means no girls for you, even if they’re as ugly as that one…” I would’ve fought back, but I couldn’t. Jeff held down my feet as Derek and Chris held my arms. You motherfuckers… I could barely see Shyme past Darrel’s big head, she had her hands cupped over her lips and she looked extremely upset… It hurt me to see her like this. But, it hurt even more when Darrel’s knee was buried deep into my stomach. I just moaned, as they left me there, sitting up against the lockers, in pain… Shyme came rushing over to me, kneeling down to help me. “Yep, my best friends…” I said sarcastically, yet still in pain. Shyme didn’t laugh, though. I could tell she was really concerned. “Well, are… are you okay? My god…” “Yeah, it’s not like that was anything new.” And it wasn’t. I didn’t care much about Darrel kneeing me, what pissed me off the most was that in ruined my moment with Shyme. She was about to say something to me, something… and I never knew what that ‘something’ was. But we continued throughout the day, she had some classes with me, some without me. I was her only friend in this school, and she was mine. After school we hung out under one of the big Oak trees, shading ourselves from the late summer sun. Somehow, I don’t remember exactly how, we ended up kissing, again. This time it was a more romantic kiss, a kiss of love. Neither of us said that we loved each other that day, but we knew what we felt, and it was love. We ex-changed numbers, I gave her the number to a certain phone in Realm 2, and she gave me her's. I was in love… We were in love. … Shyme… my first and only love. She was my ‘high school sweet heart’, I remember… … It was February, now. It was a Tuesday, the 14th of February. That’s right, it was Valentine’s Day. The day of love, bah, fuck that… Months had passed, Shyme and I were definitely a pair, a couple, two peas in a pot, boyfriend and girlfriend, whatever you want to call it. I’d prefer us to be called ‘lovers’, though. Our school had a prom night for every grade, once a year. It just so happened that the junior’s prom was set for Valentine’s Day. I entered the gym, dragging along my 36’ white bondo pants, and my black Poison the Well shirt. As I walked through the doorway, my bondage necklace jingled. Shyme and I had given each other bondage necklaces, which is what us goths do when we’re in love. I held Shyme’s hand as we passed through the masses of humanity which were my classmates. Memories of the NIN concert danced in my head. The DJ was playing some crappy Britney Spears music, and I hated it, of course. Shyme stopped me from going any further, as I turned to look at her. She was beautiful. Her and her Tool sweatshirt, and her big flannel pants… why must I always describe what everyone wears? “Let’s fuck this joint.” Neither of us really wanted to be here, and neither of us knew why we came. We exited the gym, and sat on the steps outside of the gym doors. Us… alone, by ourselves, with no one but each other. “So…” I had nothing to say. This was supposed to be our 'big night', but it didn’t feel like it… not yet, anyway. “Hey, pussy fag!” I sighed, as I recognized the voice. How could I not? It was only the voice of my tormentor, Darrel Engram. I turned around, and saw him, with the top buttons of his tux unbuttoned. He was drunk. “Ha, I can see you’re having your time alone… but I told you, no girls for you! No girls for you…” Yep, he was complete and utterly drunk. “Time to die, shf fayds… erm, Corg…” ‘Corg’? What the hell was he talking about? Now I knew he was drunk. He charged at me, literally. He extended his arm out and clotheslined me off the steps. “I’m gonna kill you, faggot!” Darrel exclaimed as we flew through the air. I landed on my feet, swiftly. But as soon as I arose, I was confronted by Darrel’s forearm, smashing into my nose, followed by another forearm, just like he had done thousands of times before. I stood there, as he held my up by the collar of my sweatshirt against the brick wall. I could tell he was hitting me, but I couldn’t really feel it, for my body was numb. Die fucker… That voice… It wasn’t coming from my realm. All of a sudden, Darrel’s eyes widened, as he feel forward collapsing onto me. I wrapped may arms around him in order to catch his fall… and that was when I felt the blood. I saw her, standing before me, holding her blood stained knife. Sheer beauty. Shyme had stabbed Darrel right in the back; the spine. It was a direct hit, and I loved it. I let go of Darrel, and kissed Shyme. I loved this girl… I loved her so. So we kissed, some stuff happened, and the whole school ended up burning to the ground, thanks to us, with all of my classmates inside of it. Shyme and I walked away, the school in flames behind us, as we smiled, hand-in-hand… Like Bonnie and Clyde. … So now, I stand here in this boring old graveyard. Death filled the air, and I loved it. Shyme reached over to kiss me as I kissed her back. Here lays the Thurston High School class of 2002 I read the large sign as I peered across the open plains, looking at all of the tombstones of my ‘fellow classmates’. Why is it that everyone from my past has to die? Fuck.
