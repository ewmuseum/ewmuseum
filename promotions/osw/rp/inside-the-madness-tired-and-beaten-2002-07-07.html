Inside The Madness: Tired And Beaten

7 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sunday, July 7, 2002 Eric spits at Casey, and Propaganda runs at Casey for a Knee Strike, when Casey moves out of the way, landing a low blow on Propaganda as he hits the turnbuckle, sending the International Champion back across the ring. The choir goon squad rush Casey, but Angeldust fights back. He low blows a goon and plants him with a DDT, leg sweeps two other goons, then nips up and superkicks another goon. Casey is grabbed from behind by one of them, as another begins punching at his ribs. Outside the ring, the DDTed goon gets to his feet and grabs a steel chair and slides back into the ring. The other goon steps aside as the chair-wielding goon pulls back and brings down the chair, only to miss Casey and hit the other goon. One goon falls and another goes ‘D’oh,’ as Casey hits a belly-to-belly suplex on one of the goons. Nate: NICK CASEY IS CLEANING HOUSE!!! Robbie: This was a great waste of 5 minutes! I WANTED TO SEE A MATCH! Steven: You got a fight; all we need now is Nerva and some guy that likes to bite nipples… Casey stands amongst the ring full of downed bodies, each goon moaning in pain, while Sagar stands wide eyed and trembling in the corner. Casey points at him and runs for Sagar, but as Sagar tries to run across the ring to Propaganda, an intercepting Casey clotheslines him out of the ring. Landing like a stuck piggy on the mat, Sagar rolls around in pain, while in the ring Casey props himself up against the ropes, tired out from the battle with Proppa-G’s choirboys. Turning around though, Angeldust runs into a large hand of Propaganda, as God’s Ultimatum hoists Casey up and slams him hard down into the mat with a damned Chokeslam. Nate: Propaganda getting his revenge on Casey after this fast brawl! Propaganda looks over the destruction in the ring and growls, then slides out of the ring, picking up Sagar and heading away up the ramp, leaving Casey in the ring, tired and beaten. Tired and beaten.... *** Tired and beaten...it's all I can think about. Time to get up, time to get up. Clear this shit out of my eyes, what the fuck? Arms don't want to move, legs don't want to bend...massive cut on my face doesn't want to stop hurting. Nick doesn't want to get out of bed today, it's fucking 11:30 on a Sunday. I should be asleep right now. And my head...friggin' Props and his stupid Choir Boys. John 9:62: 'And then the Lord said to get your lazy ass out of bed.' Nobody should be up this early on a Sunday, damn. Look at this, stupid blood on my pillow. There's no way the hotel is going to let that one slip through the cracks. Well, maybe for me they will, after all, I am of course the world-famous Angeldust. I am the rightful International Champion of ever-so-great Old School Wrestling. I didn't need to waste my title shot getting the shit kicked out of me by a bunch of goons. Choir boys my ass. There was no need for Propaganda to bring his little boy-sluts on Slam! only to get the shit kicked out of them by me. Maybe if Propganda cared less about hiring pussies and more about getting it he'd be better off. Hey, that's pretty good; I should use that this week. But god, I feel so tired and beaten. Ezra 2:14: 'Put on some clothes and get some breakfast, you stupid fuck.' This guy is starting to take over my life. He's beating me to every corner, one step ahead on every move. That's alright; I just need to go get something for this fucking headache. I probably got it from that stupid God-lover and his whole routine with Sagar; the whole thing is so tired and beaten. Not only that but he flaunts that fucking title in front of me like he deserves it. Blue shorts or khaki? Fuck it, black basketball shorts. Like anyone in this place it gonna care what shorts I have on, just so long as I'm there. Do I really want to go down and get something to eat in a hotel with 2,000 smarks just waiting downstairs so they can tell the internet wrestling sheets that I was tired and beaten last night so badly that I didn't even get up until noon? Job 6:301: 'Pop a pill, jackass.' Morphine, how it makes the pain dissapear. I'll just have a MetRX bar really quick...there we go. Now the beauty of the day after Slam!; sweet, sweet painkillers. Anyone in wrestling who isn't on them is badly missing out on something in their career. Just get some in the syringe...there we go...ah yeah. A new day, I'm ready. Now I am no longer tired and beaten. Now I can deal with those smarks, I can think of a way to get Propaganda back. He is now my obsession. He is now my enemy. He will now feel the wrath that Miller and EoE both felt. He can bring his choir boys all he wants, but in the end they're all going to feel the same way, tired and beaten. Proverbs 642:1,253: 'Go get revenge, jerkoff. If you don't do something soon, he's just going to keep walking all over you.' He's in my head.... Ezekiel 13,320:1: 'Fuck Propaganda.'
