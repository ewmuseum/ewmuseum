Blood For Stars

27 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Feb. 27 Great, so I'm stuck fighting two gangsters, that couldn't tell a shot-gun barrel from their own ass. Some f*ckin'idiots backstage thinks it's cool to team me with Dame. What's next OSW? Doink the Clown? The clear blue sky was overpowered by shades of grey, as clouds filled the heavens. What once was a vehicle and smoke infested road had metamorphisized into the rain pelted, cleared away, asphault. On the side of the road, empty stores lined up on both sides of the street. Candy shops, ice cream parlors, salons, and cafés sat adjacent to each other. A single man, sporting spiked blue hair and clothes of black strolled down the road. His humble abode lay just around the corner, and unfortunately, he didn't realize it'd be raining. 'Sh*t.' The word escaped Rhett'ss grit teeth as he stepped into a puddle of mud. 'What a lowsy week it's been... ever since that f*ckin' battle royal. Oh sure, I'm able to be Dame and Ramey's knight in shining armor, everything's fine. 'Then there was the damn...damn... '...Who was he? Yeah, damn him. Meanstreak. Come home, pop a couple of depressants, and I find my ass slitting my wrist...' He stopped, and looked above to the shadows of grey hovering over him. Rhett sighed, as the rain pelted his face. 'Who am I?' A question that many and more eventually find themselves asking. Despite all the glory behind it, self-discovery blows. It's easy to find out what you really are. It's easy to figure out what to do to change it. But not many find the courage in themselves to follow through with their course of action. Rhett's course of action? Fighting. What does violence solve? What does tasting blood, smelling open wounds, and wiping the sweat off your brow have to do with self-discovery? It's certainly not the glory that comes with finding yourself. But then again, who needs glory stars? 'I'd take blood for stars anyday...' And as the rain became heavier, and the sky ever so darker... He turned the corner, and entered his home. The warmth of the heaters warming his seemingly frozen body-parts, he repeated faintly...
