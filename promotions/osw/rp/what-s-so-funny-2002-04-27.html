What's so funny?

27 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 27th, 2002 'Men are from Mars, women are from Venus' You've heard of this little figure of speech haven't you? It simply means that no matter how hard they try to find common ground or maintain some kind of homeostasis, men and women just..can't. It simply won't work. Men and women are..different. And those who try to cross the bridge from planet to planet are frankly on a suicide mission. Well the time has come for Drew to build his first block on beginning of this bridge. Question is, what's the point? Will he regret it once he has? Will he doubt midway why he even started? Or will he muscle through it like a pro and end up getting the sweet reward? Heh, most everyone would doubt that he's even fit to build a damn bridge. Why should he be? He's not capable of loving, you say. He's never loved anyone in the past with his ghetto antics, you imply. Why start now? Amy didn't have him wrapped around her finger like..that. Nah, that wasn't possible. And they say he had no comfuckingpassion. But not just in this case, oh no. Andrew...Hostyle? No. Andrew the racist. Andrew the con artist. Andrew the atheist. Andrew the brutality. Andrew the sexist. There's no room for sympathy in there. Not in-between the atheism or brutality. Not in there, period. He'd been labeled already. Categorized as all of the above and not by choice, but association or for lack of a better word, inexperience with his kinds of people. Why so angry Amy? Priorities not straight again? Putting Daddy on the backburner AGAIN?! Heh, heh, heh. WHY do you have so much to prove? WHY the hell are you always doubting me? Can't I be HONEST for once? Isn't ANYTHING good enough for you? Or is it because I'm too ghetto for son of a bitching God that I can't be normal. IS THAT IT?! HUH?! The scene opened to show Drew sitting dressed in some casual attire of baggy khaki pants and a Polo collared blue shirt. He's sitting in his own private box of some rather large building, which we soon find is the Virginia House of Comedy. As we're told by tonight's MC. The Master of Ceremonies is an obese, as in width wise black man with his hair in some form of a weird ass perm and it tied back in a pony tail that looks more like a rat tail compared to his total size. The man goes by the name Bruce-Bruce and is well known from various stand up comedic shows he'd been on as well, including BET's Comicview and some other you don't know about. 'What's poppin in the dirty dirty tonight, Virginia?' He asks excitedly. This would best translate to a Foley-esque cheap pop for those of you who don't follow me. 'Yeah, yeah. Well folks we gotta good show for ya'll here tonight. So damn good I'm gon get my fine ass off the stage and get right to the show. This man says himself he ain't the brightest bulb in the drawer, but he damn well betta shine tonight or his ass is out of a job...TTTTTTTTTTTTTEEEEEEEEEEEEEEDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDYYYYY REED! A much more acute man than scampers his way onto stage, looking a bit fidgety before we even start. The man is white and carries a hand towel over his left shoulder. He's wearing a pair of tight ass jeans and a sweater with a big letter 'H' most likely symbolizing Harvard as his education roots. 'Er..what's up everyone?' He squawks as his voice begins to crack. No response. 'Well I'm doing okay too my bruthas and sistas. But I don't feel like I should be wearing this here shirt because, she see yesterday I was reading the dictionary.' The crowd lets out a barely inaudible laugh. 'Yes. That is what we white people do for fun.' He chuckles. 'Well, you see I was reading the dictionary and I didn't know it was the dictionary until I finished reading it. I thought I was just reading a big poem about everything.' No response. 'Ha, ha. Well when you walk right into my house, in the living room there's this light switch to the right when you first walk in. And everyday for some strange reason, I just flick that light switch. And I don't know why, because it never works but I just flick it.' Someone murmurs something about 'living in the projects' and the comedian continues, not understand the comment. 'So, so then I went into my bedroom and went to sleep, then woke up the next morning and walked into my living room. And I flicked that same switch once, for no reason in particular. And the phone rang, I picked up and it was some lady in Hungary saying 'Quit that.' No response. 'Yeah. Today I walked up to the 7-11 near my house and the sign said 'Open 24 hours', but the doors were locked and after the alarms went off, I fully understood it was closed. So then, I came back later on that same day when someone was there and said..'Uh hey, that sign out there says open 24 hours..' and he says to me, he goes 'Yeah, but not all at the same time.' Still no response. The man in charge of the Comedy house then waves a flash light on the stage to signal that due to the inactivity of the crowd, his time is up. 'Thanks a lot everyone. Good night.' Some people clap, others carry on the conversation they'd started with the person beside them way before his time slot started. The talking continues again until the fat man comes on stage this time with a piece of friend chicken in his greasy hands. He already looks like he's had enough to eat, but carries a drumstick per hand and looks to the audience who are already applauding him and his careless attitude for everyone who's come to watch the show. 'Na..now ain't thata damn shame? Boy couldn't buy no damn laughs and ya'll crazy n*ggas see a brutha tryin to get his grub on and havin yaselves a good ole time.' Bruce-Bruce sighs and then walks to the other side of the stage where people are rolling on the ground laughing at the sad, but true facts. Bruce-Bruce stops in front of one portion of audience and raises his hand with one drumstick still in it. 'Looka dis man here. He's doin all this laughin but ain't gotta damn bit to laugh at. Heady Murphy's bald head was fried wit more grease then this chicken. Denial must be a disease, boy oh boy I tell ya.' The crowd explodes in laughter. 'And look at dis women here. What's yo name, girl?' He asks out into the audience. The woman shouts back. 'Cocoa.' she yells. 'Damn, you can tell the sistas that was made for big boned bruthas like me. They names always gotta do wit food, don't they? Come on over here girl, Coocoo, cluckcoo, whatever your name is. We can go eat s'more chicken and do the dirty.' Bruce-Bruce says holding the piece of chicken out at her seat in the audience. He slowly shakes her head 'no' then sits back down with her hands on her hips, feeling belittled. 'What's wrong you don't like chicken?' Bruce-Bruce asks taking a huge bite out of the chicken and chewing a bit of the bone too. The crowd starts to laugh again as Bruce wipes his mouth and begins to get serious. 'Aight ya'll lemme get serious. We got us a very special guest in the house, OSW pro wrestler Drew Hostyle. And not only is this cat bigger than all the men in the house. But he can beat down all the women by hisself. Now let's give him a hand.' Bruce-Bruce says sarcastically. The crowd responds accordingly by laughing. 'Whatcha talkin bout?' Drew asks as he makes his way down to the front row of tables of the Comedy House with a slightly sadistic look on his face. Bruce-Bruce chuckles while holding his huge stummy *that's stomach and tummy* while it shakes like, yes a bowl full of jelly. 'You mean to tell me you don't even remember that white girl yourself? What is her name..Aim..Aim..Aimz?' 'Heh, yeah that's her name, and?' Drew scoffs. 'And you didn't really hit it and quit it? Ain't dat right?' Bruce-Bruce asks nonchalantly. Giving no regards to Drew's reply possibility. Drew then walks up on stage and grabs Bruce-Bruce by the neck..all three of them and holds him up above the ground for as long as he can stand. While doing this he says something in Bruce-Bruce's ear. The obvious answer to the question and then drops Bruce's body so that he lands in reverse atomic drop position and gets Drew's knees right in the 'nads. We hear Drew mutter something along the lines of 'Betta keep yo mouth shut' towards the ground in Bruce-Bruce's direction as his eyes are still closed tight and he's holding his crotch like it's the last piece of turkey at Thanksgiving dinner. Drew then kicks his half chewed piece of friend chicken off of the stage and jumps off himself. He even makes eye contact with the camera and we freeze the scene right there. With Drew's eyebrows furrowed, his face in some bags under the eyes and wrinkles on his forehead. Worried? No. Paranoid. Sadistic. Psychotic. A little over the edge. But then again, what was so funny? So go on Aimz..keep making me act like this. Keep using your strange little atomic particles to work your magic. It'll wear off soon. It'll..wear off...soon. You, think you have me all figured out don't you? Then, boom I do something unexpected something unpredictable. Heh, heh, heh. I love you, I love you not. I love you.. which one did you pick Amy? You've never really known what you were doing, all along. Asking for matches, signing contracts, spreading lies. Who's life is messed up NOW?! Heh, heh, heh. Oh and I bet your Daddy's been a lot of help in that department. I bet he took control, I bet he gave you stability. But even he's not sure what to think anymore. Will the results of this match really matter this much? Haven't I already done enough to you? Once you know how I feel..once the whole world knows the truth about us, won't you wish they didn't? Won't you wish you never knew how I felt all along? Won't it be funny?
