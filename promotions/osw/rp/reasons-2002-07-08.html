Date: July 8th, 2002 The limo rode smoothly down the streets of the unknown darkness, riding into the next arena...riding into the next fight. It was a smooth ride nonetheless, and a quiet one at that. Everyone in the limo was doing their own thing.... PHD was sitting in the corner, calculating somethings on his scientific calculator. Howard Porter was checking over a few files and contracts of the OSW, and reviewing every single last one of them...making sure he didn't miss a thing. And Alan Kriegman was relaxing, leaning back in his seat, and relaxing for once...since he came back into this world of wrestling. And finally, one final man sat, looking outside, into the darkness of the night, thoughts pouring in his mind. This man was MUCH larger than any other man in that limo, and much more intimidating. He had a hard stone face, with brown hair hovering over it...leaving shadows around his eyes and mouth. This man was Armour. An unknown face to the main stream wrestling world, but a known face to those who did their research. Banned from numerous federations, even No Disqualification federations, Armour had returned into this world...HIS world...of wrestling. And immediately...he destroyed. Destruction. It's something that may be the only thing in this world...that isn't a lie. It's a sense of power and a sense of victory. Destruction is also something that ends wars and battles...and makes peace. Than why do people fear Destruction so much? All I had done was destroy a few people in the ring...nothing too bad. I mean, they all had their reasons for receiving some sort of punishment... Jane Reagan, I hate anything beautiful or lust-full. Civil Disobedience, I hate anything egotistical and full of themselves. Sebastian Toomes, I hate anyone who has been big in any federation. I hate The Elite. They deserved everything they got, every single amount of pain. And eventhough I was first set to come in for the money....I am beginning to enjoy this destruction. It makes me happy, it makes me glad to be in OSW...it makes me want to destroy even more... Suddenly, the large of Armour reached into his pocket and pulled out a wad of money...money given to him by the three men around him...given to him for his destruction... Why do I take the money?....why not? It's good for many things, I might as well keep the money...and gain even more. Working for these men has been something I shall never regret. We have much in common... Sure, the physcality is nowhere near mine...but the mental power they have goes far beyond any normal person...and mental power is dangerous....just as I am. These men are dangerous...just like me. They cause fear to circle across OSW...just like me. And they enjoy their work....just like me. This team was made to happen...and no matter what, I'll make this team something NO ONE will forget...no one. Especially Sebastian Toomes. Especially...my first victim. The limo drove off into the dead of night, and slowly began to turn into a large hotel suite. Inside that limo, were men...who aligned...would destroy anyone and anything. PHD. Kriegman. Porter. Armour.
