Date: Today, f*ckhead. Sometimes I feel the fear of uncertainty stinging clear And I can't help but ask myself How much I'll let the fear take the wheel and steer It's driven me before And it seems to have a vague Haunting mass appeal But lately I am beginning to find that I should be the one behind the wheel Whatever tomorrow brings I'll be there with open arms and open eyes Whatever tomorrow brings I'll be there I'll be there *** The lone highway was covered with souls and deadbeats that early morning. Rhett's eyes were barely held open as the blurred vision of a green street-light flashed before him. His eyes slowly adjusted as he still sat there, not exactly sure of his surroundings. The car behind him didn't mind waking him up, however, as the car horn beeped several times behind him. 'Hey, hurry your ass up! I got a vacation to get too!' The voice behind him yelled, and Rhett gassed the red Honda Civic, peeling away from him. Rhett didn't mind giving him a middle finger salute out the window either. Nausia overwhelmed him that morning, as dawn slowly began to manifest and take life off into the horizon. The sudden urge to puke his guts out quickly became a reality as he leaned over to the passengers side of the car, spewing bile and water all over the leather interior. 'Shit.' His temples ached as though all the archangels and their cornets blasted in his ears. He was still nausiated, and the aches that made his brain feel as though it'd surely thump out of his head didn't exactly help the situation. Early morning hangovers suck. The beanie he wore over his now dyed-black hair was removed, slowly, as he placed it in the glove compartment, black hair fell below his ears as he followed the straight trail this highway gave him. He drove with a heavy foot, not being able to help going 10 mph. over the speed-limit. The drive was certainly going to be long, a good half-a-day longer as he passed a large, white road sign... 'Welcome to Florida- The Orange State' 'Oh boy, Disneyworld here I come...' Sarcasm flowed from his tongue like a raging river, as he continued his pace. Disneyworld he comes, indeed. Orlando was his destination. And Rhett cracked a smile, as he reached into his pocket. Feeling the trigger, he slowly removed it from his pocket. A sadistic sneer raced across his face as he placed the gun on the floorboard, in all of it's glory. Have gun, will travel.
