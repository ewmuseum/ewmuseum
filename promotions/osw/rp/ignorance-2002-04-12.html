Ignorance

12 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 12, 2002 Reno Davis and ZaC. ZaC and Reno Davis. Just Walk Walk Walking down the street with oomph in their step as they jammed to their ‘devil music’. Phil Collins flooded their innocent ears with blasphemy, when a pregnant woman popped out of nowhere! BAM! Reno decked her, and in his surprise, kicked her in the box. He helped up the angry woman, who then said: “You killed my baby!” Reno smiled and shook his head. “You ain’t seen nothing yet!” He said with a smile, and punched the preggy in the stomach. She keeled over, crying or something so Reno told her to stop her complaining, then pulled the baby out of her womb and beat her unconscious with it. “Hey you sick freak! What are you doing?” An old man hollered at Reno as he wobbled over flimsily on his cane, “You leave that poor woman alone!” “You mean the teen mother here? Or ex-teen mother I should say, since her baby is laying over there.” Reno said and shared an over-exaggerated laugh with ZaC. The old man’s jaw dropped, and the preggy cried. “My name is Diane you f*ck!” The preggy shrieked. “Well Unprotected sex Diane, congratulations! It was a boy!” Reno stated with joy, clapping his hands together and kicking the woman in the face. The old man took his cane and hit Reno in his bad knee. “Ouch! You mother f*cking nazi! What the hell are you doing? Violence solves nothing you anal-pie-hole-plate-kicking-tick!” He exclaimed to the stunned geezer. “I fought in World War 2 and watched all my friends die you ignorant...” He started, only to be cut off. “Boo hoo! My friends died in front of my eyes and now I’m soooooo sad! I’ve had a bottle of cherries in my fridge for three years, and I haven’t been able to get the cap off once! You know how much I love cherries? Three years mother f*cker!” With that, Reno shoved the old man down a flight of stairs nearby, then walked down the thirty so steps and rammed the cane up the antique man’s bum hole. “Where’s your precious nuclear bomb now gramps?” Reno asked, while drawing a swastika on the old man’s forehead. Then a police officer came up to Reno and said: “Oink Oink!” so Reno said that the old man was really a skateboarder, and the police officer kindly tipped his hat at Reno and beat the shit out of the old man, saying it was self defense. “Ahhh...What a day huh ZaC?” Reno inquired, but ZaC was mugging some paraplegic and didn’t hear Reno. Abruptly, A rabbi came out of a nearby X-Rated movie theatre and said hello to Reno, he responded by saying: “Hi, I’m Jesus Christ reborn. I think it’s time you died for my sins dirtbag!” The rabbi didn’t understand, and told Reno that he was Jesus Christ reborn. The two shared a laugh and a nice helping of pork. But before Reno could do anything, a few gay guys came by and beat the shit out of The Pope across the street, but not really the pope, just some guy dressed up as the pope that was selling his old P.O.G.S. for crack. “That’ll learn ya!” Reno said as he continued strolling down the street. Osama Bin Laden was playing skip rope with a few of the neighborhood children. He waved and Reno gave a friendly smile followed by a “Keep up the good work champ!”. Charles Manson turned the corner, dragging a bunch of children around their necks with a rope. “Hey Charlie! What are you up to now?” Reno asked. “Bringing the little fellas on a field trip!” He answered. “Awww Charlie!” Reno said with a playful punch, “You’re such a prankster! Take care buddy!”. After that, ZaC came rushing past both of them and took out his magnum, blowing the faces off of the New York Fire Department. “That’s right ZaC! Give it to those lousy cowards! Make them cry like a little girl named Suzy who just lost her mother to cancer and then dropped her lollypop!” Reno yelled, and then kicked a child who was trying to escape from a burning house in the face, knocking her back inside screaming... Inside Reno’s hotel room, Reno watched ZaC sleeping so serenely on his bed. “Such a peaceful little fella he is...I wonder what he dreams of?”
