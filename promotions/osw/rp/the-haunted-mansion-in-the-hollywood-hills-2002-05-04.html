The Haunted Mansion in the Hollywood hills

4 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 4th 2002 **Another day in Ace's life, I thought. It'd be just another day of boring managering work for me, Dick Strang, former great commentator in CCW. Another boring day of catching up his schedule, setting interviews, making room for personal times, the usual Andrew Hawk day after he came to OSW. But I didn't know what was on the way, and frankly, I didn't like it at all.** **The day began as normal. There I was, sitting on Ace's living room at his palace in the Hollywood hills. I was sitting on the poker table, which could also turn into a dinner table, but that, for the moment, was turned up. The day was cloudy, and a storm began showing its fury on the outside, as thunders crashed, as punches from god to earth, showing us all he exists and is watching. A very heavy wind made the trees surrounding the 3-stores house bend over as if they were brand new. That was the eerie soundtrack that accompanied me that day. I remember hearing voices, coming from the next room. I thought to myself that maybe the TV was turned on. I got up and walked past the rich wooden furniture laying around, past the arch that leads to the corridor that connects this part of the house to the back part. Around the walls, the pictures of Ace's past, with his parents and friends, ghosts on the walls from past times. I looked into one of the doors, the door leading to the TV room, and I remember opening it, and a thunder, cracking on the back, making me jump on my feet. Inside, the TV was turned off. I couldn't understand. The big home theater, dead. Nobody inside the room, the laz-E-boy was empty.** **That was about the time I heard steps coming from behind. I felt my blood freezing in fear, as the steps came closer and closer. I was terrifyed, couldn't turn back. Suddenly, the voice clearly called my name:** 'Dick...' 'AAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHH, don't kill me, please, I'm too young, I haven't done enough women, I haven't tried homo sex, there's so much to do...', I yelled, before turning around and realizing it was Danny Jackson, one of Ace's good friends. **Danny was wearing a nice hawaiian shirt and a pair of cut jeans, reaching his knees. His short black straight hair was soaking wet and he looked extremely pissed off, by the rage coming from his dark eyes. I remember to this day the look on his face, as he watched my terrifying face.** 'What are you on, Dick?', asked him, smiling sarcastically... 'Shut up, Danny. You shouldn't sneak out on people like that. Damn...', I answered, releasing some of the tension. 'Ah, whatever. Johnny said you wanted to see me...', he asked, making me wonder for a moment why I needed him, before finally remembering it. 'Oh yeah, I was wondering where Ace is. I ahve to discuss a few things with him.' 'I guess he's upstairs, on his room.', he answered, honestly clueless. 'Ok, thanks anyway. And never, EVER jump on me like that...', I answered, as I walked away from him. **Walking around that house was a feat, I'll tell you that. Ace's house was built according to his own interest. Passing the corridor, you'd reach the party room, where you'd find a huge set of stairs, diving in two at the top. The room was richly built, with marble floors and several light cannons on the ceiling, giving it a night club look. Going upstairs and to the left, you'd reach the DJ booth. Going the opposite way, you'd reach the inner part of the house, where Ace's room was. The stairs led to another corridor, with 4 doors, two on each side, and a glass one, leading to the pool on the outside. From the stairs, looking to the corridor, you could see the raging rain, falling mercilessly on the mansion. That was when the light cannons and the CD player turned on automatically, making me jump on my spot. I didn't know what the hell turned them on. Suddenly, a huge hand reached my shoulder, making me jump up once again.** 'AAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHH, fuck, please don't, I'm too young to die, my mother didn't like me, she made me dress like a woman every day, so I killed her, please, don't do that...', I screamed, before a huge, grave voice called me. 'Damn it, Dick, you need to cut down the cafeine.', the voice said, as I turned on the heels to see Johnny Atlas, Ace's bodyguard. A huge fellow, about 7'4' tall, wearing a nice custom-made navy blue Armani suit, he had his long black hair tied back as well. 'What the hell are you doing? And why did these THINGS turned on like that?', I asked, visibly shaken and nervous. 'We set it for a testing, remotely. Just for when we're on the road, the burglars think we're home having a party.', he answered, pretty serious. He was not a very talkable guy, just a huge locker of a man whose job was to protect the house and its owner. 'Ah, ok. Have you seen Ace, I need to talk to him.', I asked, trying to cool down. 'He's in the garage, practicing.', he answered, without showing any emotions. 'OK, thanks. And PLEASE, don't sneak on me like that.', I told him, before being greeted by a grumpf. 'Sorry, but that's my job.', he answered, promptly walking to the DJ booth to unplug the light cannons. **I walked forward, straight through the creepy hall, fearing one of the doors would open and he'd be sucked by a creature of the other world and devoured by aliens in another dimension...** **And a door opened, right to my left** 'AAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHH, no, please, I can't live in another dimmension, my meat is sour, I'm all fatty, my colesterol will kill you, PLEASE DON'T EAT ME!!!', I yelled, before realizing it was Ace's brazilian housekeeper, finishing her work on the guest room. 'Mas o que diabos voce esta fazendo?', she asked, I guess... I guess it was a question. 'Sorry, ma'am. I was just trying to find Ace...', I told her, knowing she wouldn't know how to explain it to me. 'Ace... Garagem... Garage... Sim... GA-RA-GE!', she struggled to be understood. I nodded to her, as she proudly left the room, finally making herself understood. **I walked through the glass door, into the pool area. By now, the storm was really plummeting. I ran across the area, having a hard time, since it was an olympic pool. Finally, I reached the other side, where a small set of stairs led to the garage, which was located beneath the pool. I went down, and I could hear the sounds of punching and kicking and screaming, coming from it. I knew Ace was there, so I opened the door, but only found a huge skeleton, hanging from a rope, right in front of me.** 'AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHH, NO, the ALIENS ATE ACE!!! YOU BASTARDS!!! NO, NO, PLEASE DON'T DO THAT TO ME!!!', I yelled, before hearing a huge laugh. I turned to my right and saw Ace and Danny, standing there, pointing and laughing at me. 'See??? I told you he was jumpy today', said Danny to a tired but rejuvenated Ace, who grabbed a tall wooden bench nearby and leaned over it. 'Yeah... Man, thank god I keep all the Halloween decoration here... Otherwise, this scene wouldn't be possible!', Ace said, his long blond hair tied back, messed up because of the training, his blue eyes releasing tears of joy, as he kept laughing. 'All right, laugh as hard as you want, I don't care. I came here in a serious mission. I hope you listen to me, Ace...', I answered, visibly trying to change the topic. **Ace tried to make a straight face for a moment, before continuing:** 'Ok, if it isn't about an incoming alien invasion, then we should be fine... WAIT, WHAT'S THAT?', Ace pointed, as I screamed and jumped about 5 feet away, trying to dodge an imaginary enemy who obviously was made from Ace's mind. Ace and Danny laughed hard, making me feel more like a complete idiot. Finally, he said, straighting up. 'Ok, all right. What's the deal?', he asked me, his focus finally back on me. 'Ok, the OSW has a show this weekend, today, in fact. I wanted to know if I should book you a place in a flight there...', I asked him, making him think for a moment, but making a somewhat sarcastic face. 'Hmmm... Let me see. I show up as a dark rider week in and week out, then I beat the crap out of Switchblade, for no reason apparently, and then there's another OSW show... What do you think, brainiac?', he asked, before putting a very pissed-off look. 'Ok, I see your point. So, you're gonna talk to the fans about the attack?', I asked him, just trying to make a conversation. 'No, I'm going there to play a hockey game...', Ace answered. Clueless, I didn't know what he was talking about, so I honestly asked. 'Should... I... book the place for a hockey game??' 'NO, YOU MORON! THAT WAS RETHORICAL!!! DAMN IT!', he shouted, making me realize my dumb mistake. 'I'm sorry, Ace. I'll book a hotel and a flight for you as soon as possible.', I answered. He nodded, as I walked out of the room and upstairs, buried into shame. Suddenly, he called me. 'Hey, Dick, one more thing.' **As I turned around, both Danny and Ace had rubber masks of Bill Clinton.** 'We loooooooooove you...', they said, together, holding hands. I sighed and got upstairs, as they kept laughing hard downstairs. Deep into shame, I climbed all the way to the pool area and into the house to book the flight. I must say that it wasn't funny at all. Walking around that big house, almost empty, can be a torturing experience in a rainy, dark day. And although I realized that they played me all along, something was left in the air... **The scene changes to inside the garage, as Danny holds a gym bag while Ace punches it. Danny asks:** 'Man, I can't get enough of this...', Danny said, smiling and laughing to himself. 'True, man... I mean, damn... We played him very nicely. He was actually believing the house was haunted', Ace said, also smiling, giggling between punches. 'One thing, Ace. How did you make the TV turn off, if you were all the way here?', Danny said, visibly curious. 'What TV?', Ace asked, stopping the workout, also clueless. 'The home theater on the TV room.', Danny asks. 'That TV is broken, it's been that way for weeks now, the tube is dead, there's no way it could be on.', Ace answers, before continuing. 'Then... How did the TV turned on, when I passed through the door, before Dick got up from the table?', Danny asks, looking intrigued. Ace, also clueless, looks at him. The scene changes to the TV room, as it turns on and the characteristic tunes of 'The Twilight Zone' begins playing and the TV turns on to an old episode, as a huge thunder cracks on the back and the scene shuts down to black.
