Co-ed video with no Lesbians?

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'Whoa...' We open up to the control station for OSW's latest stop in the neverending tour of cities. Sebastian Toomes, Jennifer Kale, Hector Lewis, and several technicians are standing ne a bunch of laptops, an array of recording and playback machines, and several monitors. On the screen now is Sebastian Toomes' JumboTron entrance movie. Most people who follow OSW know Toomes entrance movie is as follows: The Tron is black until the pyro, set into the stage itself, goes off, and then a series of video clips of Toomes plays on the JumboTron, 2-5 seconds in length. Then Sebastian's name crosses over the screen, then it's on to more footage. Nothing too inspired. 'So what we were thinking is that we do something a little different.' one techie says, as he puts another DVD into the machine. 'If Jane wants to go with this angle that the writers have going... We wanna do this...' 'Whoa, hold on. What's on the DVD?' Toomes asks. Jennifer files her nails, bored out of her skull. Hector daydreams, presumably about Danny Bryson, Frankie Bones, and Steven Smith in a baby oil filled kiddie pool. The technician smiles. “Well, my girlfriend is studying film in college, so I had her and some friends do up a video for you.” Toomes furrows his brow. “This ain’t gonna be no artsy crap, is it?” The technician sighs. “No… and it doesn’t involve lesbians either.” Toomes stands up, disgusted. “College co-eds filming something and it isn’t lesbian love? That’s it. I’m outta here.” Jennifer, of all people, stops him. “Sit down, jackass! Watch the fucking DVD, alright?” Toomes sneers at her. “Don’t you have an illegally performed abortion to do somewhere, you shrew?” “Meh…” The technician takes this as an affirmation, and pops in the DVD. “Okay, here goes.” The trio settle down into chairs adjacent to the banks of laptops, and watch the monitors. The high-pitched riff of Marilyn Manson’s ‘Suck For Your Solution’ begins, and the first image we are treated to is a grainy black and white segment of a Catholic schoolgirl, no more than 16 years of age, accepting Communion by having the priest place the wafer on her tongue (old veterans of MTV’s Headbanger’s Ball will recognize this). As soon as the drums and bass kick in, the scene changes to shots of Toomes from various angles and from various matches, promos, etc. The neat part is that there is this black strobe effect going on, and the images are all filtered red. The overall effect is quite striking. “Whoa.” Toomes remarks. Even Jennifer seems duly impressed. “Hey Sebastian…” she begins. “What?” “They almost make you look cool!” Jennifer says softly. “Your mouth is like a suicide…Talking like you’d never die…Automatic Charisma for your chemical mind…” At this point in the song, the movie now shows Catholic Schoolgirls kneeling, taking Communion. When Marilyn sings “I’ll suffer for you… I’ll suffer for you…”, a priest runs his hand along the cheek of a cute young brunette girl. “You’re kissing me like Benzicaine with your sleeping pill eyes… Melting me down, and suck-suck-sucking my brain…” At this point, the scene shifts, and the girl is passionately kissing the priest in the confessional. Toomes’ eyes widen. “I’m gonna hate you tomorrow…Because you make me hate you…today….I’m gonna hate you tomorrow…because you make me hate you…TODAY…” More red and black strobe shots of Toomes in the ring. “I can be the one that you believe in…I can be the liar in your bed… I’ll be the suck for your solution…Tell me that your sex is dead…” [withheld — a passage held under the museum's content rule] “DEAD!” Shot of Toomes giving Ramey a chairshot…shot of Toomes giving Timo the wrench. “YOUR SEX IS DEAD!” [withheld — a passage held under the museum's content rule] Toomes holds up his hand. “Stop.” The techie puts the DVD on pause, and looks at Toomes. “Something wrong?’ “Um…yeah. What does a Catholic Schoolgirl being molested by a priest have to do with me?” Toomes asks. The techie thinks for a second. “Well… Your promos and stuff are kinda raunchy. I thought you’d go for something like this.” “I don’t know. It looks okay to me. Although I’m not too hot on Manson… Maybe if you changed the music...” Jennifer says thoughtfully. “I don’t know about you guys, but I’ve always wanted to see a rap video. Except without the slinky ho’s. I’d replace them with bodybuilders from Venice Beach, all wearing speedos. That’d be hot!” Hector exclaims. Everyone in the room looks oddly at him. Toomes squints and shakes his head. “No.” “I’m just trying to help. Besides, that’s something new. You don’t see a guy who can bench 350 doing the Booty Roll.” Hector says cattily. Jennifer puts a finger up to her chin, daydreaming. “Hmmm… It’d be interesting… If they weren’t gay I could get into it!” Toomes sighs disgustedly. “I swear. There are too many gay and cock references being mentioned around me these days. Okay, look, the video is pretty cool, but its just not my gimmick right now. If Jane and Ed want me to be written that way, with a sorta dark gimmick, then that’d be fine. But right now, I need something arrogant, something heelish… Something to annoy people with. You know, get me put over as a self-absorbed prick straight from the get-go…” “So you don’t like?” the techie frowns. “Look, I like it. I like it a lot. Anything where Catholic Schoolgirls are getting molested is A-OKAY with me. It’s just that it doesn’t fit right now. Right now I’m doing this whole asshole thing. Sexist, Racist, Blasphemous… The whole nine yards, you know? I need a movie and a theme that can get me over to reflect that. If I get a new gimmick down the line, something where this video could fit, then I’ll want it. It looks really cool…but I just don’t think it’ll work.” Toomes explains. “Oh.” The techie looks a little downcast. Toomes stands and shakes hands with the production crew. “Thanks guys, but it just isn’t gonna fit right now.” The techies nod their assent, and soon the trio is outside again, walking towards the car. “Do you realize that you’ve almost made it through this promo without making an ass out of yourself?” Jennifer asks. Toomes thinks to himself. “Hmmm. I guess I’m just pressed for time. No time to be a jackass when you’re on a tight schedule!” “Mmmm… Ass…Tight…” Hector smiles dreamily. Jennifer sighs. “There you go.” Toomes shakes his head wearily and continues on to the car. (OOC- Sorry for the shortness… And the lateness… And the shoddy quality.
