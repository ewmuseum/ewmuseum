Date: 2002-08-16 She didn't want to get her hopes up... but the possibility had her on edge. Her head told her that there was no way that they'd give a title shot to a woman. And even if there was a remote chance they did, it wouldn't go to her. She didn't have the 'look', she didn't give then fans what they wanted - a sex symbol, a cute piece of ass. No, those attributes went to Aimz or Tempest. Then there was her 'heat' as the dirtsheets called it. She's wasn't on good terms with OSW. And why should she be? She had the talents, she had the ability, and they held her back. Even now they're holding her back - sure, it's a main event... but they still book her against other women. They were afraid that she would beat a man. But. But her heart was saying something different. It was saying that she had a chance to do this, a chance to pull off the win here, and then go on to beat Rhett for the title. She could do it! She could prove to herslef, to OSW, and to the world the truth - that a woman could be the best in the business! The opportunity was here, she just had to reach out and grab hold! And there Lillith stood, alone in the darkness. Mind vs Heart. Knowledge ve Emotion. Before she would be ready to battle in the biggest match of her career, she had to endure the the biggest battle of her life. The outcome was unknown... but the fight was sure to be painful.
