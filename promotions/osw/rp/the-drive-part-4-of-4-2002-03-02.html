The Drive (Part 4 of 4)

2 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-2-02 Slowly cutting into a vehicle, the sounds of the radio playing can be heard. “Ladies and gentlemen, this next song is one of my favorites from a recent band. They are called Pressure 4-5 and the album is Burning the Process. The song is titled Enough and it’s coming at you, right now!” The song begins to play as hits heavy melody kicks in. We now see that the man in the vehicle is “Bad Attitude” Jesse Ramey as he continues driving down the highway, slowly banging his head to the music. “I would like to believe, That the things that bring me down, And the life I lead, Are one in the same and no different, Pick your mind to conceive, Of a way to pick you up, ‘Cause you might just be, Going down, down, down and so.” Jesse begins to think about his match on Slam. Seeing as how this was tapped, the show is tonight. Thoughts of Matt Blaze runs through his mind as the chorus kicks in. “When your will breaks down, Will you pick you up? Have you had enough? Yes, I’ve had enough.” Toomes kicks in with all the crap that he has been putting Jesse through, alongside his 3B friends. “Getting bored as I speak, On a simple lesson taught, All in one bad week, When it seemed just like we had lost everything, You awake from the night, Sometimes riding golden sun, And the heat rises, Off the ground, ground, ground and so.” Jesse seeking retribution on his enemies and getting it. Taking them all out at one time. “When your will breaks down, Will you pick you up? Have you had enough? Yes, I’ve had enough.” Management seen, getting on Jesse’s case about the recent happenings. “Do you think if you try, then you’ll calm down, If I scream up at the sky, will you come down? If I could, you know that I would turn it around, But the things that pick me up bring me down, down, down and so.” Jesse getting the job done on last Slam. “Have you had enough? Yes, I’ve had enough.” Scenes from the recent promos and from all the happenings within the PTC. “When your will breaks down, Will you pick you up? Have you had enough? Yes, I’ve had enough.” Back into the vehicle we see Jesse sitting with a grimace look on his face. He turns off the radio and just looks dead on at the highway. “Yes, I’ve had enough!” The words come from Jesse’s mouth in an aggressive manner. ~The End~
