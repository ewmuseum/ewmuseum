The Drug Search.

24 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/24/02 The view changes to show Scott Taylor, strangly without Randolph this time, entering the arena. Scott is dressed in a pair of denim shorts and a black Stone Temple Pilots shirt, his hair is pulled back and a pair of thinly framed silver glasses cover his eyes, a red gymbag slung over his shoulder. Scott stares on confused as the guards stop him, he looks to Mr President. Scott: Mr President? What's all this about? I can't come in now or something? Mr President steps forward and places his hand on Scott's shoulder, casually shaking his head at the former NTICW'er. Mr President: Now, don't worry Scott. I know you're scheduled to wrestle on tonight's card, but this is a mandatory check. After the incident with Nick Casey last week we have no choice in the matter. We have to make sure that our wrestlers are safe. You understand don't you? Scott nods wearily as a dog approaches him and begins to sniff him, the dog sniffs Scott's legs and moves down to his feet, back up his legs, his stomach and then his hands. The dog doesn't bark but continues to look at Scott. The officers stand next to Scott going through his bag. Robbie: They're actually checking Taylor? Nate: It's a mandatory check on EVERYONE, Robbie. They checked us when we arrived here too, remember? The officers keep going through Scott's bag, they remove a pair of black wrestling boots, the familiar black pants with purple flames, some elbow pads, and a roll of black electical tape. They put the items back into the bag, zip it up, and give it back to Scott. One of the officer's turned back to Mr President. Officer: He's clean, sir. There's nothing on him. Mr President nods and turns to Scott, he takes a step to the side and extends his right arm outward. Mr President: Sorry about the bother, Scott. You're free to enter now. Scott nods to Mr President and then walks past him heading into the arena as the scene cuts back to ringside. . . . . . . Nate: Looks like Mr. President means business
