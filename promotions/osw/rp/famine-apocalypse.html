Famine. Apocalypse.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'Hey, what do you think you're doing!?!' The homeless man looked up from the garbage bin he was digging through, to find the restaurant chef screaming. 'Get the hell out!' The man uttered something, and continued digging. He was so hungry...so desperately hungry... 'Did you hear what I said!?!' 'Wha-' Punch! 'OOF!' Soon after the punch, the chef grabbed the homeless man by his rags and dragged him out onto the asphault below. With a deep inhale, he hit the ground hard. The chef picked him up by his rags once more, and began to drag the homeless to the street. 'I told you to stop coming around these parts, didn't I?' The homeless man said nothing in response, he was quite unconcious it seemed. Bulky and over-neurished, the chef found the scrawny homeless a bit heavy. Though he perspired and sweat poured from his brow, he made it to the street. 'Right where that street rat belongs!' The chef nodded to himself, reassuring in his mind he was doing the right thing. He soon pivoted on his heel and span around to the opposite side, and began walking back to the restaurant- where he would once again prepare meals. The wry smile that his lips formed soon became no more as his facial expression became one of concern...one of confusion. 'Take care of that street rat?' Rhett Locke. A hoarse chuckle escaped Locke's voicebox, black hair fell over his face and to his shoulders. Clad in baggy black pants and Pink Floyd t-shirt, he leaned up against the backdoor to that restauruant. 'What do you want, fr-' 'Ssh!' Rhett cut him off, with a feral grin. 'Do you hear that? The sound of a man crying...a mother dying? The sound of children-screaming-because they're on the street starving?' 'Listen guy, it was sta-' 'The sound of a man's stomach, roaring like a lion- while he knows that any minute- his heart will stop beating, because he couldn't have a meal due to state policy?' The cook began growing tired of Rhett's talk, and advanced to the door. 'I don't care, okay?' 'Wrong answer!' And the blade entered the mans side. A small, butterfly knife, but it got the job done. The man's eyes widened, a horrifying expression became on the cook's face, and Rhett kicked him as he dropped to his knees. 'Now, I don't think you'll be following too much more state policy...' He only foamed at the mouth for an answer, and Rhett began stepping away. 'Famine can only hurt the hungy...But I can hurt you.' ... 'Fat ass.'
