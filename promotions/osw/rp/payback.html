Date: 7FEB2002 “That asshole!” Jennifer Kale, the beautiful brunette valet of Sebastian Toomes, is rummaging through Toomes’ trunk outside the Easton arena, where at this very moment, Sebastian and SLR are having their match. She holds the trunk open with one hand, and she tosses out bags of Sebastian’s clothing out onto the pavement of the VIP parking lot. Inside, she is seething. Finding out that Sebastian had invaded her privacy was bad enough, but having it filmed and shown on the OSW Documentary show was utterly out of line. And he was masturbating watching her masturbate! If there was anything else to make her feel like a cheap slut, she’d be calling herself Jessica and she’d be screwing everyone in the PTC. Her eyes narrow as she spies his CD booklet. She unzips it, and flips open the cover. “Let’s see… Slayer – Seasons In The Abyss… Slipknot – Iowa… Fear Factory – Digimortal… Sepultura – Chaos A.D. … Static-X – Machine… God, what crap!” she exclaims. She hefts the heavy 200+ CD book onto the hood of the car, and continues flipping through it. Why, she doesn’t know… Haven’t you taken a look at someone’s CD case, trying to figure out a clue about their personality? The more banal and plain the CD, that’s a pretty good indication of what kind of person they are – a boring one. She continues to flip for no good reason, figuring she has the time… until her face lightens up in glee. “HAH HAHA HAHAHAA! WHAT A FAGGOT!” she screams in delight. She clutches the CD book to her ample busom, and runs around to the back of the Camaro, and slams the trunk shut. “I’m going to get him this time…” She runs back into the building, and we can hear the roar of the crowd even outside the arena. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Jennifer bursts into the locker room, where Maverick Manson and Jesse Ramey are watching the monitors. “Heh… Ivan rules…” Maverick grins. Jesse Ramey nods. “Yeah, this is awesome… OSW is gonna kick ass with these guys around… So, are you on the card for next week?” “HEY!” Jennifer yells. Maverick and Ramey turn their heads. “Well… Hello there! What’s a sweet thing like you doing in a place like this?” Maverick adjusts his posture to get the most out of his bare upper torso. Jennifer’s face darkens. “Getting totally turned off by your juiced workout regimen.” “Hey hey hey… There’s no need for that. What do you need?” Ramey intercedes. Jennifer nods to Ramey. “Look what I found!” She offers the CD book to Ramey. Ramey admires the Prong logo on the leather cover. “What’s this?” “Uh, it’s a CD case, dude.” Manson quips, non-chalantly staring at Jennifer’s cleavage. “I could have figured that one out by myself! What’s the point of this?” Jennifer flips the cover. “You know Sebastian is a big metal-head, right?” Ramey nods. “Yeah, we were supposed to go to a Soulfly show in Pittsburgh tomorrow night.” Jennifer flips the ‘pages’ to where Toomes keeps his ‘lighter mood music’, and taps Madonna’s ‘Immaculate Collection’. Ramey’s jaw drops. Manson looks over Ramey’s shoulder and turns away, laughing. “Oh shit! Lookit that! Mister Tough Guy listening to Madonna and shit! What a faggot!” Ramey tries to keep an even keel about him. “Hey, I got Paul Simon’s ‘Graceland’ somewhere at home…” Maverick points with one hand, holding his stomach in the other. “That’s cuz you a faggot, too!” Ramey’s face darkens. “Shut up, dude.” “Haw haw!” Jennifer smiles vindictively. “Well, I trust you boys will spread the word around, won’t you?” Ramey looks at her. “Why are you trying to stir up shit? Aren’t you supposed to be his girlfriend?” - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Ramey and Manson stagger out of the locker room, clutching their crotches. “Goddamn! You…really…know…how…to talk…to women…” Manson wheezes, falling against a cinderblock wall. Jesse holds himself up with one arm against the wall, wincing. “Shut up. It’s not my fault. I didn’t know… I figured ALL valets fuck their wrestlers…” “Dude… So did I… You think…ouch…that Niki Weathers chick is fucking Clay?” Jesse shakes his head. “Who knows… My world as I know it has been turned upside down… Why, God? Why do valets not actually have sex with their wrestlers? Why God?” Jesse holds up his arms and shouts to the heavens… “WHY?!??!?!?!” - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Toomes steps into the locker room, covered in sweat, his silver and blue hair tangled, his body aching and sore. The match was long and arduous – SLR worked with him well, and Ramey and Hostyle came down for the clusterfuck like they were supposed to. All in all, not a bad night’s work. He first sees Maverick Manson and Jesse Ramey, grinning at him broadly. Then, the other guys. Pretty much everyone, actually. They look at him, some stifling barely concealed laughter. “Uh, hi guys…” Toomes says evenly. Ramey is the first to speak. “So, Sea-Bass… Did it really feel like you were getting touched for the very first time?” Toomes looks at him. “What?” Someone begins singing from the back of the gathering crowd. “Like a virgin… HEEE! Touched for the very first time! Like a ver-her-her-her-gin… Feel your heart beat…next to mine!” Toomes looks at them, and something begins to churn inside of him. No, he thinks. No… There is no way they could know… “I made it through the wilderness…. Somehow I made it through-hoo-hoooo…. Didn’t know how lost I was until I found you…” The locker room erupts in laughter. Toomes looks at them, and sees Jennifer in the back, holding his CD case. “No…no…no…” She found it. His secret stash of 80’s bubble-gum pop… Along with his Madonna Greatest Hits CD… Flock of Seagulls… INXS… the compilation CD with ‘Take On Me’ on it… “Maybe you should wear that thing Madonna has with the ice cream cones on it!” somebody guffaws. Toomes turns, filled with deep, overriding shame, and runs. The cat calls and the ‘witty’ zingers follow him until he runs out of the locker room, the door swinging shut on the verbal abuse. What’s wrong with Madonna? He runs down the corridors, and nearly hits Jane Reagan, his boss. “Watch where you’re going, Toomes.” She reprimands him. “Sorry, Mrs. Reagan…” he mumbles. “I know you’re a shining star and all, maybe even a LUCKY STAR…” she grins evily. “AAAAHHHH!!! EVERYBODY KNOWS!” he screams, and he sprints down the corridor to the parking lot. As he runs out, he spots the trunk open on his Camaro. Yes, the bitch went ahead and did it. He steps in the mess she has made of his baggage, his personal effects, and other such stuff he put in the back of his car for these prolonged trips, gingerly prodding the toe of his boot through the mess, lifting up a t-shirt she had flung onto the wet pavement. This means war. She found his secret 80's stash of music. He didn't MEAN to watch her shower and masturbate, and he certainly didn't mean for it to get on TV... Now...now it's personal.
