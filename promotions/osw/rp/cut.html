Date: February, 26. 2002. Cut me out... Cut me out... Cut me out... I threw it all away, as I ran a finger over the steak knife, with a smile of sadistism. Slowly I turn, to the sink, where the blood of this soon to be slaughtered lamb will pour. I hate you. I hate myself. Kill me. Kill you. Those were the thoughts that ran a marathon through my mind. I'm not a fighter...I never was a fighter...not a good one, anyway. What good am I to anybody? Just some knight in shining armor to come to Jesse Ramey and Michael Dame whenever they need it? Well fuck that, I'm not some hero. And trust me, I definately won't die a hero either. I couldn't help but snicker at the terms 'hero' and 'good guy', as I ran my tongue up and down the flat end of the knife. Your not holding me down, you never will. Your not holding me back... I'm just sick of standing still. I'm not this organizations puppet...I don't plan on being one...I'll cut myself out of this situation, yes I will. Death is great, the entire concept of it... Not to sound anti-paladin when I say that I could careless what happens to you, if you live, die, eat or drink...but I don't give a damn. I've stated it before...and for some odd reason, you cheer me. Because of what I stand for? No. But because you know I fight for what I stand for. I couldn't laugh anymore...Though, pussies cry. So I sat on the floor, taking part in a situation that I never dreamed to be part of... Suicide. A random suicide. And as I stood up, I placed the very edge to my skin, and gasped, slightly, and let the crimson mud ooze.
