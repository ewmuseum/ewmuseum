Date: Thursday, April 18, 2002 Where are we? What, you don't remember this? I'd think you would have it memorized by now... Oh wait, I see the banner...the old MWW arena. Hey, give it up for the kid! Friggin' moron... *** The fans are all on their feet, as the major show of MWW is going on, and of course, it's the main even. The highly acclaimed battle in Flint, Michigan, featuring the two men who had been fighting back and forth for over a year; a years worth of blood, sweat, and tears all working back into one match. It was 'Angeldust' Nick Casey in one corner, with The Gangstar in the other. The blonde versus the black. The good versus the evil. The champion, Gangstar, versus the challenger, Angeldust. Oh my god... Hey, it's all coming back to him now! The match had started off as a highly technical duel between the power and the extremist. The two were known for their sick hardcore spots, such as Casey getting piledriven through a Toyota at the last PPV, and Gangstar getting shocked into a coma by a bare electrical wire. Nick was taking control, but then the match started to get more hardcore; much to the chagrin of Nick, just getting back into the swing of things after the concussion from the Toyota. A few tables came into play, a few chairs as well. This all looking familiar, Nicky? The 'I Quit' Match. I remember. You should. Keep watching. The match got much better. Nick was handcuffed and shackled to himself. Chairshot. Chairshot. Chairshot after chairshot. It was like watching a Mankind match, only Nick was bleeding a lot more. About fifteen chairshots later, Nick stopped moving. Chairshot. Chairshot. The referee stepped in, but was met with, you guessed it, a chairshot. Up in the box, the camera cut to a picture of the owner of the corporation; he was watching Nick's lips on the Mountaintron; he wasn't counting the lights. We took a lot of abuse that night, Nick. You had a concussion, jackass. Why would you even consider wrestling? You should have been sleeping at home. I know, bu--- But nothing, Nick. You got put through the winshield of a Toyota... ...countless tables... ...a small fence... And a glass plate wall of a luxury box. Yeah, well--- It's not the only time, Casey. What are you talking about? We don't forget... Whose we? We are the resonance... What?
