Getting to know your allies, Part 1 (featuring Jim Axtell)

5 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 5, 2002 Jesus St Crux and Caridad Mariposa wade through the sea of tables in Del Dante's, one of the finer Italian restaurants in Tulsa, heading towards the table indicated by the smiling hostess. They seem a bit out of place in such a fancy place - Jesus wearing dark dress pants and a light dress shirt, and Caridad is a flowery summer dress - unusual clothes for the normally casual pair. Jesus, ever the gentleman, pulls out a chair for Caridad, and then gently slides it in after she's sat down. The hostess sets some menus in front of them, and continuing her smile, asks, 'Would you like something to drink?' Caridad shakes her head slightly at Jesus, who then turns to the hostess. 'Not right now, thank you. We're waiting for two more of our party.' The hostess nods. 'Very good then, I'll be back in a bit.' She turns away, and seems to disappear, leaving the two alone. Caridad lazily flips through the menu, occasionally throwing a glance over her shoulder to the door. 'Are you sure Jim and his wife got the message? He was expecting a Mexican restaurant I think.' Jesus shrugs his shoulders a bit, his boyish innocence playing on his smile. 'I'm sure they'll get it. I think he was off picking Kami up at the airport, but Moose said he'd find a way to get the message to him.' The Mexican leans back a bit in his seat and stretches his arms above his head, trying to loosen up a bit from an earlier workout. As his right arm is fully extended, a slight whince of pain flashes across his face. 'Are you OK, Hon?' Jesus shakes out his shoulder a bit and nods. 'Si, I'm fine. My shoulder is still a bit sore from my match against Frankie last week, that's all.' Caridad looks around the room a bit nervously, but Jesus smiles to reassure her. 'Easy, Dadi. This isn't the old days, and there aren't hidden cameras perched everywhere we go, taping our impromptu promos.' He chuckles a bit before continuing. 'Heh... I'm surprised they didn't have a way of seeing inside our thoughts back then, to turn them into promos.' Jesus leans in close to Caridad and whispers, 'Besides, if Frankie and Kory go after my shoulder, I've got Jim to help me.' Again, Caridad turns to look at the entrance, where there's no sign of Jim and his wife yet. Jesus looks himself, then shrugs before turning back to his girlfriend. 'Remember, Dadi, unlike me, Jim only gets to see his lady every now and then, now that he's on the road with OSW. Give them a bit of time to catch up on their own.' Caridad smiles knowingly, then takes Jesus' hand and squeezes it affectionately. 'So, any idea how you're being booked next week?' Jesus shakes his head, with a bit of an indifferent expression. 'Not really. I wouldn't mind seeing if the bookers can get me a match with Sebastian - he's mouthed off about me a few times since Ascension, but I expect there'll be more fall-out between Jim and I and Frankie and Storm instead. Speaking of those two, I'm sure Jim will alert Moose, but I'd like you to keep an eye out for Krieg - Don't confront him, but a little warning might be all Jim and I need.' Patting his hand reassuringly, Caridad smiles. 'You know I'm always there for you.' Her attention is then distracted, as if she sees someone she recognizes off to the side. Jesus turns with her, and sees Jim Axtell, and presumably his wife, standing at the hosts' station. Jesus raises his hand to get Jim's attention, and then Jim and his wife make their way through the sea of tables towards the Mexican and his girlfriend. Jesus and Caridad stand as they approach, and Jesus introduces Caridad to Jim. 'Caridad, you've met Jim before. And, if I may be so bold, Caridad, this is Kami.' Jim Axtell nods at Jesus' boldness. 'Indeed. Kami, this is Jesus St Crux, my partner for this week, and his girlfriend, Caridad Mariposa.' The ladies shake hands as the men pull out chairs for them. When everyone is settled in their seats, they're about to start some small talk, when the waiter seems to appear from nowhere. Jesus looks a bit startled for a second. 'That was fast.' The waiter smiles knowingly. 'I am... very, very sneaky, Sir.' Casting his gaze over the entire table, the water asks everyone. 'Would you like me to list the specials?' Jim lifts up a menu. 'Well, we just sat down and haven't gotten to look just yet, maybe in a minute.' 'Very good, Sir.' All four look around, as the waiter has again seemingly disappeared. 'That's very odd,' chuckles Jesus, as the four start looking through their menus.
