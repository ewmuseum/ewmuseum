MUAHAHAHAHAHHAAAAAA!!!!

Date: 08.22.20.02 'Hahahahahahhahah!' 'No, no, you're not doing it correctly. Like this, MUAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAAAA!!!!!' Kriegman responded. Howard gave it another shot. 'Mahahahahhahahahaha!' 'No, no, NO!' Kriegman was getting frustrated now. 'If you're going to be evil, you have to do the evil laugh correctly. First, throw a U in there.' 'Excuse me?' Porter asked quizically. Kriegman dons a girly voice to mock Porter's, which is hereforth denoted with an asterisk. '*Excuse me?' Kriegman shakes his head. 'Jesus, It's Muah, not Mah, Muah. The U is very important for added inflection. Inflection varies directly with level of sinisterness,' Kriegman preached. 'Muah, check.' Porter nodded at the absorption of this piece of knowledge. 'Right, you also need to be more boisterous, like this. MUAHAHHA...etc. That increases the evil level twofold,' Kriegman explained. He obviously had a knack for this stuff. 'So you need me to say it in all capitals?' Porter inquired. *Sigh* Kriegman was not amused. 'Try to say focused.' 'Right, MUAHAHHA, check.' Porter nodded again, letting Alan know he had it down. 'Now, next you need to drag out the last syllable, so it has that ominous echoing effect.' Kriegman grabs his belly. 'MUAHAHAHAHAHHAAAAAAAAAAA!!!!' Porter bites his lower lip. 'Ok, drag the A, how many exclamation points?' 'I prefer four, but the choice is yours. I'd refrain from doing too many, as it just makes you sound corny.' Porter takes a deep breath and rubs his hands together, as if he's getting ready for an Olympic Laugh Trial or something. 'MUAHAHAHAHHAHAHAAAAAAA!!!!!' Porter grins as he stops. 'How was that?' 'Five exclamation points? Pbbttthhhh' Kriegman blows a raspberry. 'Always trying to one-up me aren't you?' Porter grins sheepishly. 'Anyway,' Kriegman continues, 'Let's put it into action. I'll fire forth some things that have happened, and you respond with the laugh. Then you do it back to me. It's been a while since I've done this, so I need practice. Being dead isn't really a laughing matter...Plus it's a good ego boost.' 'Got it,' comments Porter. Kriegman starts off. 'You figuratively hogtied Sebastian Toomes!' MUAHAHAHAHHAHAAAA!!!!! Porter shoots back. 'You killed Ed!' MUAHAHAHAHAHHAHAHAAAAAAA!!!! Kriegman trades back another feat. 'You gave that schmo Mr. President an empty envelope for an apology letter!' MUAHAHAHAHHAAAA!!!!! Porter's turn again. 'Uh...you killed Ed!' Kriegman goes to take a breath to laugh, but then frowns at Porter. He decides to go on laughing at it anyway. MUAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHHAAAAA!!!! Kriegman passes the ball again. 'You gained the trust of every single fan and then stabbed them in the back!' MUAHAHAHAHAHAAA!!!!! Now Porter thinks of something. 'Um....you jobbed to the Extreme Legends!' MU... Kriegman scowls at Porter. 'You should have been a blowjob.' Porter takes his finger and runs it down Kriegman's arm, and then licks it. 'Yep, bitter.' Kriegman turns around and begins walking away. 'I think that's enough for today.' Porter shrugs. 'What? Was it something I said?'
