Size Doesn't Matter...!

5 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-05-02 “Ouch ouch ouch ouch ouch ouch ouch!!!!!” screams Seth Masters as we open with a close up shot of his face. As we pull out we can see that he’s sitting in a hospital room. It’s what you’d expect of a hospital room. You know, if you’ve seen one, you’ve seen them all. A few chairs off to the side, a bed in the middle, a table to the side of it, a TV screen in the corner of the room, you know the drill. With that said, even though Seth is in a hospital, the reason he is screaming is not why you think. No, he’s sitting in a chair in the corner of the room with a bowl of chili on his lap, waving his hand over his mouth in pain. He takes a swig of a glass of milk sitting on a table to the right of him, then sighs in relief. “Damn, that’s some spicy fucking chili!” he says, getting an angry look from Trevor Hawke, who is currently laid out in a hospital bed. “Ow, my chili is too hot,” Trevor says in a mocking tone. “Just shut up, you big baby. I’m getting frickin’ fluid drained from my knee and you don’t hear me bitching.” “I don’t see you trying this chili,” Seth answers in retort. “If you tasted it, I’m sure you’d be screaming in pain; a pain far greater than the one I’m experiencing. It makes a knee draining feel like...um...what’s something that feels good? Oh, I know. It makes a knee draining feel like sex! You don’t know just how potent this chili is. You don’t know, Trevor.” Trevor sighs, not feeling like arguing with his moronic partner. He’s got enough to think about as it is, without having to deal with the sheer idiocy emanating from the other side of the room. 2-2-02 was a rough night for the team of Civil Disobedience, for Trevor more than Seth. It was just hours ago that the team of Heresian Bloodlust, composed of Hessian and Azrael Cain, stormed down to the OSW ring like a bat out of hell...no, more like a bat out of PCW now that I think about it...no, that’s still no good...more like a couple of big-ass motherfuckers out of PCW. Yeah, that’ll work. Anyway, these two monsters completely decimated Seth and Trevor, throwing them around the ring like a couple of Spike Dudleys and eventually drawing the disqualification for their merciless actions. And just because the bell had rung didn’t mean Hessian and Cain were done for the night, as they proved by continuing the beat down after the match. All in all, it was a rather pitiful display for the first night on the job by Civil Disobedience, especially considering that the damage dealt by the demonic duo caused Trevor to sustain a nasty knee injury. Nasty enough to keep him out for a week or two at least. The mere idea of working so hard to get into OSW and getting injured on the first show causes Trevor to cringe. Considering all that happened, you’d think the two suburban warriors would be in rather low spirits, and...well, Trevor is at least. Seth, however, aside from the pained expressions from the chili, appears to be in quite the good mood after the woeful events of earlier. But then again, he’s an idiot, so go figure. Of course, being an idiot has been his excuse for everything lately, and considering that Trevor has had just about all he could take of life in general tonight, he doesn’t feel like standing for it at the moment. Seeing the smirking expression on Seth’s face, Trevor views it as a mockery of all that has happened in the past few hours, causing his anger to build and build until finally he lashes out at his partner. “Just what the fuck are you smiling about, you fucking moron?!” he screams. “I don’t see anything that’s fucking funny right now, so if I’m missing out on something I’d appreciate you telling me what the fuck has caused you to become so fucking amused!” Seth’s smile doesn’t fade at this onslaught. “Trev, just because your knee hurts is no reason to act like a big crankypants. If you’re going to be a big baby, you might as well hop into your little baby mobile and take a tour of St. Babies-burg.” he replies, raising an wry eyebrow at his partner as he does so. “Do you not remember what happened in the ring tonight? How we had our asses stomped into the mat over and over again? How we were completely annihilated by those giant fucking bastards?” screams Trevor in an even angrier tone. “How can I forget?” answers Seth nonchalantly. “In fact, I’m still seeing stars.” “Then why the fuck are you still smiling!?” shouts an angry Trevor. “Because, stupid...we won the match!” Seth shakes his head, responding matter-of-factly. Trevor reaches behind his head to chuck a pillow at Seth, who dodges it easily. “We won by disqualification!” Trevor communicates in another scream. “A win is a win in my book.” says Seth, shrugging. “Disqualification wins don’t count for shit,” rants Trevor, flailing his arms in the air to embellish his words. “Everybody watching us tonight saw us being dominated for a good ten minutes out there. The OSW fans sure as hell don’t consider us winners. In fact, I’m pretty sure we looked like absolute losers.” “Maybe so but the record book says we’re winners,” Seth says with a grin. “And not just any winners, either. Undefeated winners! Heresian Bloodlust, on the other hand, are winless losers. Who looks good now, huh Trev?” Trevor shakes his head, pointing over to the counter top where a large container full of fluid sits. “Um, considering I have a big jar full of KNEE JUICE sitting on the counter over there, I’d say not us.” “Oh come on. That’s barely any knee juice,” remarks Seth, waving his hand in the direction of the jar. “It’s almost a gallon,” Trevor states angrily. “Like I said; barely any!” retorts Seth. “I’m sure after my match with Azrael Cain next week, the medical personnel at the local hospital will be squeezing five times as much juice out of his oversized patella.” “You’re facing Azrael Cain next week?” Trevor asks Seth, his angry expression fading as he tries to hold in a smile. “Yeah, why?” questions Seth in reply. “What are you smirking at? The fact that you don’t think I can win?” “Yeah, pretty much...” says Trevor failing to hold in his laughter, as he busts out in hysterics. Seth smiles back at him, not letting Trevor get to him in the same manner that he gets to Trevor. “Some team mate you are, pulling for my loss,” Seth says, chucking the pillow back at Trevor. “You’re just bitter because you got hurt tonight and I didn’t. You can’t wait to see me in the same condition you are. Well tough shit, Trev, cause it ain’t gonna happen. I’m going to win this Saturday.” Trevor catches the flying pillow and shoves it behind his head again. Now his smile is gone as well, replaced by a stern expression. His voice is equally stern. “No, no, no. You’re right, it’s no laughing matter. Face it, though...this is nothing like we’ve ever encountered before. It’s not that I want you to lose, Seth. It’s just that you can’t win. We can’t win. And your arrogance is really getting to me, because you don’t seem to realize that. We just got beat down...beaten to with in an inch of our lives...yet you blow it off like it was nothing. This isn’t PWA. We’re not facing frickin’ losers like the Music Man or Rent-a-Dweeb anymore. Hessian and Cain...these guys are ass-kicking machines. We’re simply outclassed, dude.” Seth shakes his head, refusing to believe it. “No way, man. You’re not giving up this fucking easy. So what? We got our asses handed to us. Who gives a fuck? I know I don’t. Hell, I’ve gotten my ass kicked countless times over the years, but it hasn’t affected me any...aside from the countless lost brain cells. What I’m saying, though, is that you need to stop being such a fucking pessimist. The only things these guys have over us is size, and we all know that size doesn’t matter.” “Actually,” interrupts Trevor, “That saying is just a bunch of BS your old girlfriend used to feed you to make you feel better about your lack of masculinity. It‘s a complete crock. Size really does matter. Everyone knows that.” “Uh uh,” shouts Seth in protest, “she wasn’t lying to me. All the girls I’ve been with have said that.” “It’s the same line all the girls give,” Trevor shoots back. “It really does bother them a lot. In fact, the only reason they put up with it...or the lack thereof...was because they probably couldn’t do any better.” “No, no!” screams Seth, cupping his hands over his ears. “I refuse to hear it. Size doesn’t matter! Size doesn’t matter! I’ll prove it to you this Saturday! Just watch! I’ll take Cain down, just watch! Size doesn’t matter! Size doesn’t matter!” Seth is still screaming like a young child as he runs out of the room with his hands still over his ears, leaving Trevor in the room alone. Trevor reaches to his side and grabs the remote to flick the TV screen on. He sighs, glad for the silence for once, as he watches a nice porno movie on Skin-a-Max. As the naked female forms dance across the television, his mind floats back to his current situation. Usually his view would be just as deluded as Seth’s, but his knee injury, though minor, had really put things into perspective. He was oh too mortal in a situation that called for him to be anything but. Trevor really didn’t want to sound like a pessimist, but it was only logical to view their situation as them being the great underdogs. Chances were slim to none for victory, and no matter how big Seth’s ego was, nothing would change that. Egos do not match up to physical form. He hoped to hell that his friend could pull it out, but it looked rather unlikely at the time being. There’s no fighting what logic dictates. But there’s always a chance for a miracle...however small... With those thoughts, Trevor fades off to sleep, as our scene does so as well.
