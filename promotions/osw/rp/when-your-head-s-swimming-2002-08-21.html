When your head's swimming...

21 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 21st, 2002 Amy was in the most dangerous possible mood. The effects? She was sitting in bed, her eyes twitching to each side, then together. Her fingers were weaving around a little white pen while she bent her leg into off-the-wall positions... What's so evil about that? Amy Campbell was bored. Boredom almost always leads to thought or spontaneous action. Thought almost always leads to depression and spontaneous action almost always finds her in the back seat of a police cruiser. She was staring blankly at the TV set with absolutely zero interest...it was the usual late-night tripe, some guy with a bad toupee trying to sell his patented microwave for a ridiculous price that was made to look good by saying ''And you can have this with ONLY ten million payments of 19.99!''. Sleeping hadn't done her any good whatsoever...she'd tried it earlier and the only thing coming to mind was a disturbingly realistic nightmare involving Corg-like rape attempts and Callisto Bane...it just wasn't going to be a good night. On top of everything, it was more humid than she could've ever imagined hell to be, too hot to even share a room with Reno Davis that night. Body heat was a horrible thing, something that kept Amy away from crowds and other people in general during the summer months...if she thought SHE was dying of the heat, touching shoulders with another person wouldn't help cool her down, and sharing a bed with Reno definitely wouldn't be a bright idea. An actual good idea soon came, however. So, with a thud, she rolled off of the bed and onto the cool but hard floor and somehow managed to get up on her feet to drag her sore and worn form over to the door, shutting off the TV, lights and mini-fan that had been recycling hot air onto her moist, sweaty skin for hours before she shut and locked it totally. Amy had to tip-toe her way down the hall, she hadn't even bothered putting shoes on...that would've caused way too much noise, considering the way her favorite leather boots sounded clip-clopping against even the softest of carpets. Her destination was just off of the bathrooms in the lobby and nobody would be working the service desk at the hour it was...around 2am or so, according to the clock above the door at the end of that hallway, so she was clear to go straight through, as she did. Finally, she arrived... In an enclosed but glass-roofed room there was a huge, oddly shaped pool that was filled to the brim with absolutely crystal clear water that would have spilled out onto the artistically tiled edges if even five people entered it....that was the destination. Feeling the even slight temperature change just by being near the water, which promised to be a refreshing getaway from the sultry heat, Amy shed her jeans and just dove in, almost full-clothed and disturbing the surface of the water ever-so-slightly, sending out a few gentle ripples that didn't even make it to the edges of the pool. After gliding through near the bottom of the shallower end, she surfaced, flicking soaked hair behind her neck with a slippery forearm before leaning into the water behind her to float on her back. The sight of the full moon above her, dipped a little behind the silhouette of a pine bough with an eerie glow cast around it from the haze of the moisture in the air caught Amy's breath in the very back of her throat and there's where the rapid thoughts began to shoot at her... It's been a long, long time since I've done any decent work in the ring. Cat fighting, bitch-slaps, nut clutches...that's all well and good, but where did my actual ability go? Probably right out the window with the bookers' notion that women could be a driving force in wrestling... She couldn't help but fill the space with a tinkling little chuckle that danced across the pool's surface like her eyes had been. They finally booked me in a main event and I won the damned thing. Did it prove my worth, though? Probably not...just look at who they let get a match with me THIS week, some dyke who little Japanese people run from while screaming bestial names whenever she steps into the sunlight. Her thoughts ran all over Callisto Bane, including the odd dream she'd had depicting her own mutilation at the hands of the she-beast before... ''Don't be afraid of her...'' She never caught herself uttering thoughts aloud. ''...you're one of The Elite, do what you do and take her back to school. Show those bitches you've still got the ability no matter HOW they want to make me look...'' ''That's the spirit.'' Another voice had come, but it was a welcome one... Reno Davis was standing at the edge of the pool with a smile ten miles wide strewn across his stubbled jaw. ''What the...'' Campbell fumbled her words at first, a little upset that her moment of private thought had been interrupted when she'd least thought it could be. ''...what're you doing here?'' ''Well...'' He laughed a little. ''I got out of bed to open the window a little further and, low and behold, there's my girlfriend's near-bare ass skimming along the water in the pool room below! Just thought I'd run down and see why you weren't asleep...anything wrong?'' ''Nothing's wrong...I'm just out here thinking, it's way too hot to sleep. Why didn't you just buzz down and ask me that?'' ''Ummm...well...see....'' Davis began, obviously trying to contain the giggle of a teenaged boy. ''...I noticed you were wearing a white shirt.....'' At that point, Amy made a note to keep her chest BELOW water. ''Ah, shit.'' ''Heh...yeah, makes for a great little show. Anyway...isn't it a little late to go swimming? I mean, is this even allowed?'' She shrugged, momentarily dipping her head below the surface to wet her hair again before coming back up with a slight gasp for air. ''I swim well and I'm properly dressed...can't see why it wouldn't be allowed. Besides, it's better without a crowd, no fat guts looking at me grossly or little kids grabbing my ankles.'' ''Ah...'' Reno's features suddenly widened in an almost sinister smile. ''So.....uhh....why don't you come out of there and adjourn to my room while your hair dries?'' ''Nice try.'' Aimz dipped her head down under again, this time surfacing with a mouth full of water to spray at Davis, who was high and dry on the tiles, playfully as he moved to the other edge, watching her swim over to him. ''But I like it in here,'' she eyed him coyly. ''Well I don't,'' He attempted a pleading pout. ''Because I can't be in there with you.'' ''Why not?'' Davis tugged at his form fitting t-shirt. ''Uhhh...Amy? Dry clothes...don't wanna get 'em wet, obviously.'' ''Then take them off.'' She hadn't meant that to sound sexual, playful or as a challenge of any sort...Campbell was dead serious, never having really bothered to understand modesty. ''Yeah right!'' He laughed aloud, pointing to the row of windows above the glass ceiling. ''I think your thong's giving those people quite the show already, thanks.'' ''Suit yourself.'' Those were the last words she uttered before grabbing Davis' ankle and pulling him, dry clothes, neat hair and all, into the chilly water with a splash and a ''GAHHH!!! COLD!!!!'' from his then blue lips. Hey, at least she knew the ability to relax was still hiding somewhere within her... Now she just had to re-kindle that ability to wrestle, REALLY wrestle, if she had any hope of living up to her number one contendership spot.
