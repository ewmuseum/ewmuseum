Perks

19 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 19th 'Oops, there goes my shirt up over my head Oh my Oops, there goes my skirt droppin' to my feet Oh my Ooh, some kinda touch caressing my face Oh my Ooh I'm turning red Who could this be? I tried and I tried to avoid but this thing was happening Swallow my pride Let it ride and party But this body felt just like mines I got worried I looked over to the left A reflection of myself That's why I couldn't catch my breath' Tweet- 'Oh My' Perks. Yep, being on the Boss' good side for once did give Drew Hostyle a teeny weeny bit of leeway with the woman in charge. Afterall he was one of Switchblade's closer friends, gladly taking the second to his gay manager who held a tight grip on things in Switchy's life. However, all three of the man in Brooklyn Born and Bred had grown pretty close in a non gay sense. So close that they'd actually let one another introduce new obstacles to the group. Okay, maybe it wasn't that serious. I'll put it in simplified terms. Switchblade was going to introduce Drew and Frankie to..the family as they were gently called. Switchblade's immediate family probably wouldn't admit to knowing him in public but the family that Switchblade'd come to know would be a strange cast of characters if you can even call them that. Drew and Frankie already got the displeasure of meeting Toomes' moody sidekick, Jennifer Kale. They'd even had a few run-in's Hector Lewis, as I said before Switchblade's gay manager. But the two men they'd meet today may have even called this meeting for themselves Diez Nutz and Rod Thicke, Sebastian's old high school buddies and also just happen to be turned interracial porn stars. Diez Nuts and Rod Thicke had been appearing threw out several of SeaBass' promos to..make them up to par as they said. Now I'm not one to judge on what makes the ratings, but I can damn sure bet that what they find entertaining..may not exactly be..the general consensus. 'So tell me again why I have to meet these guys man?' Drew asks, rubbing his chin while talking to Switchblade the man to his left. 'Because they said my promos are crappy, and you're having a little problem with workrate so I figured why not share the wealth?!' 'Heh..the wealth, huh? Ha, ha, ha.' Drew laughed. 'I think what Drew's tryin to say is dat he don't wanna share nothin wit these cats..let alone dey wealth, whatever that may be.' Frankie explained to Toomes. 'Well I don't care what anyone says, these guys can work miracles if you let them.' Switchblade assured the two men. 'Uh huh and they'd work a few otha things if you let them too.' Drew replied cautiously. However all three men walked to the inside of a huge building that looked from the outside like a studio or other place that one could record music in. As their walked in we saw what each person was wearing, Drew had on a Phat Farm red sweater that faded to black on the bottom of it and a pair of black and baggy Phat Farm jeans to match his black Timbaland boots. Frankie had on a white wifebeater and a pair of dark blue jeans with no designer's logo visible. Switchblade's is wearing his usual set of street clothes that vastly contradicts what the other two man are wearing. As they enter the building, walk down the hall led by Sebastian, and get to the door that Switchy's friends specified they all stop in front of it. As Drew looks at both men scratching and rubbing their heads, he goes up to the door and give three loud knocks on it with the brim of his fist. There's silence for awhile after the door the knocked upon and sooner or later Rod Thicke and Diez Nutz answer the door with a smile that seems like it's been there for ages. The two black men then open the door wider for all of the men to enter and Frankie, being the last man to enter glares behind himself at both men to mumbles something about not 'tryin nothin funny'. Both of the men, however ignore Bones' subtle warning and skip all the way over to the spot where all of the men have gathered. 'So what do ya'll have to help me out?' Drew Hostyle asked impatiently. 'Yeah, let's get on wit dis.' Frankie interjected. 'Da fuck you talkin bout my nigga?' Rod Thicke asks. 'Fo sho, we ain't her to help ya wit no promos, we just wanted you to take a look at something we done did.' Diez Nuts explained. 'In the meantime nobody can help your lazy ass with a workrate problem, that is unless you need an energy boost, and in dat case..' Rod Thicke ranted on in vain, speaking a few words that we can't fully hear because of the scuffling of the feet of Switchblade's along with Frankie's who are both holding back Drew from dismantling Thicke for making a pass at him. 'Just cool out!' Diez Nutz exclaims. 'Nah! Let him sweat he looks better that way!' Rod Thicke says as he strokes his Nutz, Diez Nutz on the shoulder that is. Drew, is still outraged and continues to try to advances towards the man with a look of fury on his face. Drew finally gives up getting past his equally strong stablemates of Frankie Bones and Switchblade and flicks off both men then pushes the men he came with off of him. 'So what is it we gotta see, man?' Frankie asks, exasperated after holding Drew back all that time. 'I'm glad ya asked, Black.' Diez Nutz smiles. 'Well, see since ya'll got connections in the OSW with on air time, then I figured that maybe you'd donate a little of your time to a worthy cause that some other stars have already donated too.' 'And this cause?' Drew snarls, very close minded to the idea that's about to be put before him. 'Well..see for yourself.' Rod Thicke smiles and then presses a button on the remote control in his hand, which brings down a projector screen out of the ceiling. When it reaches the floor Thicke presses another button and the screen turns static like then settles into an all black setting and we hear some pretty unpleasant voices in the background. Sounds of two men moaning gets louder and louder and the crack of a whip interrupts and brings all of the noises to a halt. Switchblade decides to speak on behalf of the disgust of all of 3B. 'WHAT DA FUCK..' He starts. 'Oppsies!' shouts Rod Thicke. 'This must be the wrong tape.' 3B then wipe the stern expressions off their faces and then turn their attention back to the screen. The next tape begins with similar sounds during the beginning, and Drew looks to either Thicke or Nutz to change the tape but they smile and huge each other, obviously proud of what they'd created. Drew can't help but stare on at the tape and then the words or title of the song flashes across the screen. 'Squeeze on Diez' Frankie and Switchblade both let out a groan as they hear the title and don't look what they're about to see, but Drew just looks to the men again and raises his fist as they tense up. The tape rolls on to show a still of Rod Thicke and Diez Nutz standing together and in the middle of them standing OSW announcer Steven Smith. They kind of look like an Oreo standing together in that formation, but Oreos involved layers on top of each other, so I think I'll stop with the visual aids. But what they heard next was even more disturbing, not only were the men openly exposing to the world the sheer brazen of their sexuality's' but there were about to..to..rap. Diez Nutz starts the song with the chorus that sadly goes to a contorted beat of Doggy Doggy, where's your Boner..ugh, I meant Bone. 'Get down on yo knees and then beg please. I know you want your Big Daddy but he's not eye candy, Just squeeze on Diez, I said squeeze on me.' Steven Smith then raps along making bizarre motions with his hands and holding his hands down to hold up his ultimately baggy pants with his infamous purple thong still showing through the top of it. 'I'm an Old School Worker and a Old School jerker, known far and wide for bein a Butt Hole Surfer. I would give you a lecture, But if I had a pecker, like Robbie Decker's There wouldn't be a 'Bad Touch', but when I get the chance, I'll still squeeze Diez Nutz' They all join in, in singing the chorus again. 'Get down on yo knees and then beg please. I know you want your Big Daddy but he's not eye candy, Just squeeze on Diez, I said squeeze on me.' Rod Thicke, not to be left out, sings a small portion of the hook of the song too, and the scratching that would usually follow a modern rap song is replaced by Steven Smith's and the other various repeating of Smith's quote 'It's not what you know, it's who you know' and 'Squeeze on Diez'. 'I suck on lots of suckers and lollipops, but when it comes to just Diez Nutz I just won't stop.' The rest of the song is just a blur of oh's and ah's by the end of the video when Nutz goes to turn it off, Switchblade's has his mouth covered in awe, Frankie has his ears covered to block all sounds, and Hostyle has his eyes covering to stop from viewing the tape video in general. Thicke Nutz, both of them though look at the men with their hands intertwined and wide smiles on their faces. There are a few more minutes of akward silence between all of them and Drew looks down to his pants then back up at each man. 'You want us to show this to thousands of people and have them go out and by this shit?' Drew asks amazed. 'Well,...yes.' Rod and Diez both agree. 'You outta yo God damned minds' Frankie says without hesitation. 'Whoa..whoa..whoa, wait a minute Frankie B. I think I know a few people who might dig this stuff.' 'Wow you mean really..dig it?' Diez Nutz questions. 'Fo sho. I'll be sure to give it to them on Slam! they'll watch it together and everything, who knows they might even use some quotes and stuff from here, you got guaranteed sells, man!' 'Hooray!' Both men jump up and down and kiss each other on the cheeks then hug again. 'Whatcha talkin bout Hostyle?' Frankie Bones, but spoke for the rest of the people in the room who were asking themselves the same question. 'Don't worry about it, man. Don't worry about it.' Drew assured him and then took the tape that they handed him, that he held in between to fingers because of it's stickiness. 'Um..thanks, I guess.' Drew said as he walked out the door quickly. And the scene fades to black.
