Friends & Family (Part 2)

3 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 3rd “Right now you probably like me, but Later on you’re gonna love me, and Right now you probably want me, but Later on you’re gonna need me, and Right now you don’t like me, but Later on you’re gonna hate me, and I just got to do me. F*ck ya’ll, I’m ma keep doin my thing.”- Fabolous “Right Now, Later On” Our last scene ended with Drew picking up and speaking into the telephone and should begin on addressing more of Aimz’ complaining. I don't think embarrassment comes naturally to her, maybe this will be easier than I originally thought. We can all adjust to new things, right? However, since my game plan is obviously well known and hasn't ever been tried successfully in the past I'll share with you again, this time in terms that even you can understand. She talks the same sh*..wait am I abusing the language again? I gotta start becoming more aware of these things, when worse comes to worse maybe I'll just start 'fueling my slaughter of the English language' since it seems to make her so damn PMSemistic. So more proof that my plan is going as planned, she grabs a camera and tapes on a whim, meanwhile my promos are tactical, and sure to get her steamed. As I’ve done before. Now I'm sure that you were a bit weary from all the insults that she dished out again in different words, ala 'Drew Hostyle is a big black dummy.' On the contrary Amy, I'm very smart, smart enough to make you showcase your shit to the world, no? On another note the weight and height difference isn't as big a factor as she's made it to be. At first she doesn't seem to care and is more worried about her Samoan shithead of a friend, Timo Bolamba. But then, once I 'light her fire' if you please, suddenly it's a major factor that I am 'twice her size and got help with doing this and that.” Well..Amy, keeping crying. Keep bitching. It really does make me a happy person inside, to know I've ruined the life and sanity of one b*tch who wasn’t mentally stable to start with. It gives me pleasure. Truly. Your popularity, makes no difference to me. It’s gotten you to where you are now and you continue to brag. I socialize with all those I need to and no one else. Look where popularity has gotten you? Ass flat on the canvas two Slam's straight. And look what it has gotten me beating some incompetent...yes, WHORE with ease, week after week. Who's gotten the better end of the deal, huh? Who's got the real popularity where it matters? Heh, and I bet you will appre-ciate me even more, once you see the way I'm annun-ciating so well today, does this make me any more intelligent as it does you talented for spitting out 30 seconds worth of the same insults for your so-called boyfriend? I’ll leave that for you to answer. But the final step of my plan *get this* is to counter whatever she may think of next. Now this may seem like a very challenging and difficult task to perform, but, believe it or not I actually put some thought into things when I’m on screen in front of the world. However, when you are greeted by Aimz in..let’s see maybe a day or so, probably tonight if she catches word of this. Then I bet she’ll try and remain even more calm for you, to show she’s not letting me get the best of her. RAH will probably calm her down by performing his usual dumb ass antics and making himself look like an idiot. Or perhaps Amy will be madder than ever and go out on a limb to parade it to you pathetic viewers. And at the very last and least, Aimz will get in front of the camera and show no reaction. She will simply lank around as she has before and “think about the match” yet again, meanwhile demeaning my good name. As if she doesn’t know what’s going down Saturday. It’s apparent even to me that she can sense it in the back of her head, and deep down in her stomach, that she’s going to get embarrassed and embarrassed to the point of no retaliation. Heh, now that’s entertainment. On Thursday, Drew would be more concerned with the entertainment aspect of the match then the actual wrestling if it ever came down to that. IF it ever came down to that. “Yo” Drew said as he picked up the phone’s receiver. “What’s poppin’ Big Brutha?” said the voice on the other end of Drew’s one and only sibling, his brother Jordan Crazy aka Jordan X Shabazz. “Nothin, just watchin’ videos.” Drew answered in monotone. “That’s whatcha been doin’ the past 2 hours man, why don’t you get out of the house?” “Yeah, aight. I’ll get out of the house, but where am I goin’ to?” “Someplace where they don’t end sentences with prepositions, maybe?” Jordan joked. “Aight then, where am I goin’ to, dumbass?” Drew answered, still joking. “I don’t know, you just need to get out. I have the feeling you’re still wearing that same sweatsuit that you were wearing yesterday and images of some guy drooling on the edge of a slumped in couch keep fillin’ my head.” Jordan admits. Drew, then looks to his left and right. The sofa had a huge slump in it, and he did have on the very same clothes he’d been in before. Hmm.. “Well...ah that ain’t true. Just gimme a few minutes I’ll be at over when I’m ready.” Drew stated, clearly offended by Jordan’s presumptions. “Ha, ha, ha. See you then, man. but I‘m not at my house, I‘m with..a friend at the place you saw before.” Jordan says somewhat secretly and then hangs up the phone. Drew puts down the phone and then makes his way to the closet and sets aside a different, but similar outfit that is made up of baggy blue Phat Farm pants and a short sleeved white shirt with the Phat Farm logo on it’s front. He then walks into the bathroom and takes a shower of which I don’t plan to give details on. Once he exits the bathroom he makes his way down the hotel lobby and out the revolving doors to the complex within walking distance from the hotel in some shabby tenements. Drew looks at the place with a look of distress on his face then appears to hold his breath as he steps under the steel steps and into the complex that looks as if it could fall at any moment. As Drew watches the ground to make sure that he didn’t step on any lost fractions of body parts or whatever was kept in these so-called apartment buildings. He hears a bitter chuckle from the corner under the steps, a slight “Hu, hu, hu” if you needed an audio aid. The mans steps seem to creak as he gets nearer and nearer to Drew’s position from under the steps. “Whatcha be doin here, BOI?!” The old man cackles. “Listen here Pops, I’m lookin fo’ Jordan Cra...uh Jordan Shabazz, you seen him around?” Drew says standing up with more posture now that the man, who was a good foot short than himself, has called him a boy. The old rubs his eyebrow in serious thought. “Shabazz..Shabazz, sorry it don’t ring no bells, son. I don’t know a man named Jordan ‘round here and I’m the most predominant member of da neighborhood watch. Maybe if you looked down by the subway, then..” Suddenly Jordan walks through the door of an apartment over head, Drew steps back and then smirks at Jordan. He however, glares at the old man for a few seconds until Jordan relieves some of the tension. “Er..it’s okay Dave he’s cool.” Jordan confirms. The old man cackles and then spits on the ground next to Drew. Drew’s face tenses up as he looks at his shoes to see a wad of spit. “Son of a..” Drew is cut off by Jordan rushing him out of the complex and into the streets of Brooklyn once again. But as Jordan and Drew walk out, another black man walks in and faint mumbling can be heard, something to the extent of the black man asking: “Where’s Hostyle, cracka?” The old man then waits in silence, obviously growing through the same routine of rubbing his eyebrow in thought. It’d become a pattern. “Sorry it don’t ring no bells, mighty sorry to hear it though. But if ya go down to the subway maybe you’ll see him there.” The old man cackles. The black guy looks at the man as if he’s crazy and then exits after muttering “Thanks anyways.” We now look back at Jordan and Drew who’ve just exited the apartment building. The unidentified black man stops while looking at the blacks of Jordan and Drew, but then shrugs as if he wasn’t who he’d been looking for. “What have I told you, man? My friends aren’t dumb enough to upset you. You just have to stay out of there way.” Jordan stayed as he dusted himself off and prepared to hail a cab from the sidewalk. Drew dug deep into the pockets of his baggy jeans that were nearer to the ground then his waist and pulls out his silver Nokia cell phone. Drew begins to dials numbers on the phone and stops as he realizes what Jordan (who’s now in the middle of the street trying to hail the cab), has just said. “Okay, okay. Maybe they should stay out of YOUR way, next time.” Jordan suggests. Drew grunts and nods his head all in one motion. He then puts the phone up to his lips and a quick “Hello” on the other end is heard. “Let’s get a move on, fat man.” Drew says then puts then phone back into his pockets. Jordan is still on the streets trying to hail a taxi cab. Which, if you didn’t already know from movies, is damn near impossible. “Taxi? Taxi?! TAXI!” Jordan shouts in vain. “Now you know damn well..” Drew starts. “Yeah I know, you don’t even have to explain. I know I finished this experiment thing a long time ago, but hell we need a cab.” Jordan starts as he runs back into the house and returns with some khaki pants, a Polo shirt, and tie in hand along side some white makeup. Drew suddenly remembers back to when Jordan had been performing a test to see how differently he’d be treated. The results were drastic, so why not try it again? “Nah, nah man. I got you, dun even worry about dat, I’m ma show you a different side to things.” Drew says as Jordan raises his eyebrows in his direction. Just then a black stretch limo pulls into the streets filled with Black & White taxi cabs and it looks like a rose in a field of dandelions or something else that might be out of the ordinary. The limo comes to a stop right in front of both Jordan and Drew and Jordan just stands there, still. “What? Don‘t tell me you don‘t wanna go no more, I could be watchin’ them damn tapes!” Drew says, assuming that is the reason Jordan’s not entering the limo; when in fact he is in shock. “Da..da..damn, Dawg. I didn’t know you had it like this.” Jordan says, astonished. “Heh, you sound like a broken Fabolous record, man. I’ll prolly show you a few other privileges you didn’t know I had, either.” Drew says and sighs at his younger brother’s lack of knowledge. The two then get in the limousine and go farther down to where the find a shopping complex the size of MacArthur Mall. *sorry, try to relate to my Virginian metaphors* Point being, the mall was huge. Jordan and Drew then leave the limo and enter the mall as they enter the mall’s lobby they stop and share a look at the directory which lists all the stores and their locations in the mall. “Where should we go first, I don’t wanna spend too much of your money, ya know.” Jordan says to his brother. “Like I said don’t worry about it, e’rybody speaks this language.” Drew says as he pulls a wallet oozing with cash through its side pockets. Jordan smiles and the two continue on their way through the mall and by the end of their day, Jordan has 6 newly acquired shirts, 3 pairs of pants, and 2 hats as well as a pair of shades. Drew’s also gotten himself a pair of shoes and a new hat. As you can imagine after the many bags that the men were carrying by themselves, they both were tired. So tired in fact that Jordan was out cold on a bench within the mall, with bags all scattered across it’s floors. As Drew walks by, he pushes in the bags under the bench and on his face, which serves as the other flat surface besides the floor. He laughs at the sight of Jordan covered with assorted colored bags and then walks a few steps away to a nearby pay phone. As he is about to insert 35 cents into it’s slot *that’s a quarter and dime* he hears the sound of someone preparing to do the same as he was about to, except from the other line. BRRRing! BRRRing! BRRRing! “..Da f*ck?” drew manages as he looks again quickly towards the sign above the telephone booth that says “No Incoming Calls.” He begins to speak about the strange occurrence to Jordan, but as he turns he finds him still sleeping, yes like a baby on the mall’s bench. The phone’s still ringing surprising and Drew takes the time out of his busy day out of his courtesy, mind you to answer the phone, just like a gentlemen. “YO, WHO DA DIS IS?!” Drew asks with impatience to the person on the other line. We just *happen to* pick up the audio on the other phone line and hear a scruffy voice on the other end, the voice no doubt belongs to a black man as can be heard in his choice of speech, naturally. “Shut the hell up, and don’t draw attention to ya’ self, cracka.” He says calmly. Who, of all people would call him a cracka? “I’m bout to go off on dis biatch of you don’t tell me da f*ck you is!” Drew threatens. “Shut UP I said, this is ya brutha, dumbass.” Said the man on the other line, still with the same calm tone. “Oh..er, damn..how are you doin brutha? Long time no see, I guess” Draw laughs nervously. “Don’t worry I’ve seen enough of you today to make me sick. How do you think I rigged these phone lines, jackass? you need not make yo plans obvious to the world. A bum on the side of the street knows how bad you want to embarrass this lil’ slut on Slam. Now I know you want to, but how do you plan to do it?” “Heh, doesn’t the world want to know.. Put it like dis. Rumors don’t always tell the truth, but they do have some truth in ‘em. If what people wanna believe went down wit me and Aimz went down. Then so be it. It ain’t like I give a damn about firin up people’s imaginations and sh*t. But I’ll tell ya one thing, if she likes dark meat so much I’ll give her a whole lot more than she bargains for, and I don’t imagine it’ll but how she wants it either. Either way I’m goin all out on her lil ass and I don’t really know how to be exact yet.” Drew smiles, hoping that his brutha and fellow member of the gang, the Benefit buys his spiel. “Hahaha, you’re one funny cat, Hostyle. perhaps you wouldn’t mind saying that to ma face? Not like I think ya lying or anything, I’m just not sure you’re being completely honest. Hell, you never even told me that Jordan was in town. We haven’t meet the youngster yet, tryin’ to keep him away from tradition, huh?” Drew’s brutha asks, as we find out that he is the black man we saw before, but still poking fun at Drew. “Na..nah brutha. It’s just that I was just..” Drew tries to finish, meanwhile amazed by him already knowing the fact. “Don’t trip, brutha. I know what you meant. You meant you were going to invite him and ya’ self over here.” The brutha implies. “Word.” Drew acquiesces. “Good, and don't’ keep ya bruthas waitin, neither. Goin into a match hurt isn’t somethin I want to see happen to you..or Jordan.” The brutha finishes and then hangs up the phone. Drew then hangs up too. He looks over to the bench where Jordan was just sleeping and suddenly he flashes back to his big brutha’s previous words, “isn’t somethin I wanna see happen to you..or Jordan.” He then runs over closer the bench and looks around frantically, and as he steps away he hears a groan. He looks down to the floor to see Jordan lying there, obviously haven been to tired to control his sleep rage. Jordan groans yet again and then his eyes roll back to his head and he falls back asleep. Drew shakes and slaps Jordan on the face lightly to get him up, but Jordan doesn’t budge. Drew then starts to smack him harder on the face and Jordan throws his arms in in the arm, swinging and hitting the nearby air. Drew then gets an idea. “FIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIRRRRRRRRRRRRRRE!!!!” Drew yells with fake urgency at Jordan. “Huh? Ah..uh WHA..? DAMN THOSE WHITE PEOPLE, ALWAYS HOLDIN GRUDGES ON DA BRUTHAS! HELP! HELP!!!” Jordan yells running around the mall’s bench and the surrounding telephone booths like a mad man. Drew meanwhile has taken a seat on the bench himself and is laughing at Jordan. “I’LL GET THOSE SUMBITCHES OR MY NAME ISN’T...”Jordan stops to look at the crowd growing around him. By this point drew is crying with laughter. “Jordan X Shabazz...” he finishes, ashamed with himself for being played a fool and the scene fades to black.
