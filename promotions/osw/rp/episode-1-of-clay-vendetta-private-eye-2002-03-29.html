Episode 1 of Clay Vendetta: Private Eye!

29 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/29/02 [The camera slowly fades into a monochromatic scene; the streets of Miami, dark, abandoned, with the bad weather slowly creeping into the night. Focusing on a building, a voice-over can be heard...] The night was dark and dismal, with a thick midnight fog hanging over the streets outside. It was almost like something out of a detective novel; except the building were all Art Deco instead of '20s retro-chic... still, the camera was black and white, and that was god enough for me. As much as I love this town, I wish they'd get a few architects with a sense of Classic style... in fact, note to self: speak with Mayor about architects. [Entering the building, the camera clims a few sets of dingy, worn-down old stairs, to come upon a door: and upon the door is lettered, in shiny gray writing (one would presume gold were we not still on the monochromatic kick): 'Clay Vendetta: Private Detective'.] That's right, a Private Detective. It's just one of those things that isn't half as dirty as it sounds. My name is 'Classic' Clay Vendetta, and frankly, I didn't give a damn about the mean streets of Miami and protecting and serving and all that crap. What I cared about was getting to the bottom of things. And if a cheezy film noir montage and an office with my name on the door were what it took to get to the bottom of things, then... uh... that's what it takes. [Cutting to inside the office, Vendetta is sitting behind the desk, his fedora hung on a nearby coatrack. A lit cigarette lies idly in the ashtray on his desk, burning away to nothing as Vendetta goes through a sheaf of files, leaving through with a look of deep thought beneath his bleached-blond hair. Lifting a small tumbler from his desk, he takes a sip of his drink.] Rum and Classic Coca-Cola, stiff, just the way I like it. No, no, that's *not* what I meant; Christ, how did Dragnet manage to do their shows without double entendres? This must have been so much easier for Joe Friday. The files were getting deeper as my patience was growing shorter. I needed names, identities, facts, figures. The lightbulb on the lamp was burning into my eyes like cheap contact solution. Note to self: if I'm giong to do film noir, I need better metaphors. KNOCK KNOCK KNOCK! A rapping, tapping on my chamber door. Wait... that's Poe, not Doyle. 'Come in!', I shouted. [The door swings open and in walks...] The dame had a better rack than the World Billiards Championships and legs that could make a man drown in his own drool. Now *here* was a Classic broad if I ever saw one... she said her name was Niki Weathers, and she came in like a tropical storm. 'Clay... when did you get this place?' Nosy broad 'I picked it up cheap and got a painter to re-do the door. Like it? Classic Enterprises spares no expense when there's a task at hand.' 'It's, uh... novel.' 'Novel', she said. Like a book. And all books have a cover, and the things I'd do to her under the covers when I took her back home... 'Clay, are you making lewd comments in your voice-overs again? You know I hate it when you do that.' Argh, busted. 'No, babe, of course not,'I replied. I needed to push the dame for answers, or at the very least, questions. Anything to not get myself in more trouble. 'Any new information?' 'None, and we're only a few days away from Slam. Clay, I'm worried about you, baby. The last thing I need is for those... those *thugs* to come back again.' She had a sensitive side, like the people on the shaving gel commercials. I liked that in a lady. 'Don't you worry,' I said in reply. 'I've made my own arrangements to deal with them. That psycho slut Reagan is forcing me to show up, but she didn't say I couldn't bring... company. Did you get through to Ramey's number?' 'Yeah, I left a message. Listen, Clay, I've gotta head back to the house...' [... and Niki leans over the desk and plants a kiss on Clay.] '... but let me know if you find anything, okay?' The only thing I intended to find was the bottom of this glass... followed by a ride home... followed by a night of wild, wild... 'And no more of the voice-overs! Yeesh.' [Walking out of the office and closing the door behind her, Clay returned to his files...] So who could it be? 4 guys from PCW... given the talent level surrounding Tyler Nelson and his low cashflow, I expected the worst... the Ream Team? The Ideal 4? Nah, either too easily distracted or too incompentent for the job. The trail had gone colder than a penguin in a refrigerator sipping a Slush Puppie... but no answer was too elusive for... 'Classic' Clay Vendetta... Private Eye! [Vendetta smirks and a trumpet wails a closing theme as the camera fades away to black...]
