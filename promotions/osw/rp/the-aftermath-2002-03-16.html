The Aftermath

16 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/16/02 Tempest paced back and forth agitatedly, the swish of her iridescent purple pants keeping a rhythm with her clacking heels. She stopped and took a deep breath, exhaling slowing as she closed her eyes and stretched her neck inside the black shimmery turtleneck chemise that covered her. 'Damn Meanstreak,' she grumbled. 'Nearly breaks my head and now he ruins my fun too. How'd he get out of that tar so fast? He didn't even have any left on him when he hit the ring.' She shook her head and stepped over to the locker room door. Miyuki told her to await for her there, but this was Tempest after all. Staying in any one place for too long constituted potential boredom and therefore was grounds for whatever she felt was needed to spice things up at the time. And this time it was finding out what happened to her perfect prank. She slipped out the door and walked along the hallway, peering in open doors and down side halls until she found the one where she'd left Streak stuck to the walls. Sure enough, there was a pile of tar and feathers and a huge empty butt print. 'Hmm, I should make a cast of that and give it to Meanstreak for posterior... I mean posterity,' she said and giggled, then brightened as the flash of an idea lit her face. 'That's it! I'll make a cast of it and hand him his ass.' She slumped slightly and added, 'May be the only way I'll be able to do it at this rate.' She walked along the hallway following a trail of feathers and black gooey footprints until she reached the door to a different locker room from the one she had found Clara Deveaux in. She shook her head as a little growl escaped her. Clara Deveaux... All the fingers on her left hand were broken and her left shoulder was dislocated. What kind of monster... Why finish that thought? She knew the answer. 'Monsters all around, but how did this particular Tyrannosaur escape the tar pits?' she wondered aloud as she rooted around the locker room, finding bits of clothing and lots of feathers and tar. Suddenly she stopped as she peered into the bathroom. 'Wow...' There strewn across the floor were the remnants of her work, still drenched in the acetone that had removed them. She staggered back; the smell was still overpowering. Amid the drenched bits of fabric, feathers, and melted tar, there were spots of blood thinned to greater volume by the acetone into a unique compound of pain. Tempest folded her arms and said, 'Looks like maybe I hurt you after all... good.' Behind her Miyuki stepped in, her long black hair pulled back in a ponytail, that draped delicately between the tailored shoulders of her suit. 'Tempest I told you to wait for me what are you... ugh!' She blanched as she caught sight of the mess in the bathroom and said, 'What the...' Tempest nodded toward the floor and said, 'It's how Meanstreak got all that stuff off of him. He scrubbed it all off with acetone. Musta ripped half his skin off in doing so. Miyuki shuddered and said, 'Tempest, this is a long way from dropping Jello on people's heads from your apartment window.' Tempest turned to her abruptly and said, 'Don't you think it should be? It's not breaking all of someone's fingers, now is it?' 'No...' Miyuki said quietly. Her eyes darted from the floor to Tempest and back a few times before she cleared her throat and said, 'I'm afraid though... that you got your wish.' Tempest turned and walked with her out of the room as she asked, 'Which one? I have a lot of them you know.' 'The one where you get a match with Meanstreak,' Miyuki said as she stopped walking. 'Tempest, you can't go through with it, it would be suicide.' Her face was etched with worry as Tempest smiled and said, 'I'll be fine. It's exactly what I wanted. This is a chance I need Miyu.' She took Miyuki's hand and skipped a little, but Miyuki stared at her with even more trepidation. 'Tempest, how will you survive, much less win?' Tempest smiled and said, 'I have my ways. I have lotsa ways! You always think the worst of things Miyuki. Don't forget I've fought big guys before.' Miyuki nodded, almost dumbly, still caught up in the looming nightmare of the match. As they reached the door and stepped out to the limo at the curb, she struggled to find something else to think about, something else to turn her attention to. She found it at last and said, 'Oh yeah, there's one other thing. You know that girl who came on tonight, Aimz?' Tempest nodded and sat back in her seat. 'Yeah I know of her,' Tempest said as she peered out the window. 'Picked up at a bargain basement fee I bet after getting the boot from NTICW.' She chuckled with a note of mischief as her eyes flashed a bit in the low light. 'Why?' Miyuki poked at her palm pilot and said, 'She's had a few choice words about you already like...' Tempest held up her hand ands said, 'I could care less. Truly. She trash talked me before I even made my first OSW appearance, knowing I was on the way. Can you say paranoid?' Miyuki shrugged and said, 'I'd rather you were fighting her.' Tempest laughed and said, 'Well I'm not. And if she feels the need to address me, that's her problem. I have real monsters to worry about, not those nasty little toe biters under the bed like her.' She winked and the scene faded, leaving the drone of the engine and the hum of the wheels behind before silence.
