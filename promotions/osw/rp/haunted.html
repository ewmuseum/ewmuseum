Jennifer Kale sits on her couch in Rockford, Illinois, flipping through the newest issue of Playgirl, sighing. “Why the hell didn’t I ever get to play with this?” she laments, sipping on a cup of coffee. She throws the magazine on her coffee table, and it slides on the glass surface to reveal the September 2002 issue, with none other than her… whatever the hell he was to her… Sebastian Toomes, naked from the waist up, with only a pair of female hands coming from behind him to conceal his package, which is revealed on pages 35-42, not including the centerfold. She sets down the coffee mug and puts her feet on the couch. She had been sent home again, by Jane Reagan. Apparently she was getting annoyed with all the attention Jennifer was paying Toomes in the hospital, and when she finally came to visit, she summarily gave Jennifer her walking papers. After all, what good was she to the company if she wasn’t playing a role on TV? And with Jane and Sebastian together, there was no room in OSW for poor Miss Kale. Sent home again. Infuriating. Just when she was all ready to give Toomes a chance as an honest-to-goodness nice guy and possibly a boyfriend, Jane Reagan has to fuck it all up. Jennifer kneads her feet into the armrest cushion of the couch, stewing like she had been for the past two weeks. Out of a job, out of the wrestling business, and out of a potential boyfriend. Not that was a problem, the latter one. She could have a lot of guys, simply by virtue of her having a vagina. But, then again, she was a woman, and held herself to higher standards. That’s why she didn’t answer Kory Storm’s repeated messages on her machines. Where the hell did he get her unlisted number anyway? Weirdo. Shit. She had to get out of the apartment. It was driving her nuts. She could only watch television, masturbate with ‘Surrogate Knight In Shining Armor’ (named ‘Jesus’, since, realistically, that’s the name she called out when she used it…), and organize her closet so many times before she went insane and started collecting her toenails or something. Occasionally, she would go out and catch a movie (Austin Powers 3 was an abomination), but that would be about it. And now that she would have to find a job… You know, a REAL one, it provided her with the perfect excuse to go out and…socialize, or something. Maybe find a good date or something. But why would she want to do that, when the one she was itching for was in Erie right now, probably, getting ready for Slam!? More likely, she thinks bitterly, he’s probably banging the fuck out of that bitch, Jane. When he should be fucking me… She bites her lip with frustration. Look at me, she thinks. Mooning over him like a teenager. Her gaze drops to the Playgirl magazine. Damn. That’s nice. She looks back up at the ceiling. Was he really fucking her for real? Or is it one of those angles Sebastian was always talking about? Real or fake? Well, it must be real enough for Jane to get rid of me, she thinks. What if he was, like, in love with her? Wouldn’t that be a kick in the head, huh? Really fucking the boss for real, and being like, real buddy-buddy and stuff, boyfriend-girlfriend, relationship type crap… The thought was enough to make her rip Jane’s face off. Then again, look who’s talking. When she started in with this whole valet thing, she was adamant about separating fact from fiction – the fiction that valets always fucked their wrestlers. Like Amanda and Kid Wonder – if that wasn’t staged, Jennifer didn’t know the meaning of the word. Sure, maybe they did it out of attraction or convenience, but it was just a part of the show, a part of the act. But that begged the question – where does the act end and reality begin with Toomes and Reagan? Was it just that, an act, a ‘shoot’, as they call it? And if it is… When does the show end? And if Sebastian is in love with Jane… and the show ends… Where will that leave him? I guess that’s the litmus test, then, Jennifer thinks as she snuggles up with a quilt her mother sent her, stuffing her feet underneath the cushion of the armrest. If Jane and Sebastian ever break up on television, then I’ll know. If he keeps seeing her off-camera, then I’ll have my answer… And I can get on my life. These past few months, she felt a weird kind of tether, tying her to Sebastian. After March, when she was given an extended leave of absence, she felt great to have the childish libido of Toomes away from her. But she watched the shows. Taped every one. Read every on-line interview. Even ordered the Pay-Per-Views. She would bitch at him while she watched him cavorting around the ring like a jackass, out loud in her apartment, alone… But still, she was watching. And disgust turned to a kind of… She sighed, thinking about it… A kind of puppy love. It would be nice, considering she was so alone. He was living the life she wanted to live, not be an occasional care-taker to The Gimmick Man’s mother, who lived out here, too old and frail to fend for herself. She wanted the lights, the cameras… But most of all, she would like to have him again, even if it meant her acting offended when he tried to ogle her tits. Jennifer sighs and closes her eyes. Time to dream of their perfect life again, futile as it was. But hey, she can dream, right?
