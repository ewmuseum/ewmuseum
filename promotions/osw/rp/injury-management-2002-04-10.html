Injury management

10 April 2002

Date: 4/10/02 The days and nights have stacked up since Frankie's epiphany. His self realization that people equal shit and the only way to deal with the world is to say 'fuck it.' And the more he thinks about it, the more he realizes it was the best decision he's ever made. No matter what is said or done around him, he simply doesn't care. He has let his emotions take control of his life, something he used to fight, and recently his stock has risen. His quiet, systematic, destruction of his enemies is OSW is taking it's toll. He has not stopped being the introspective ball of second guessing and thought that he has always been. And at this time, in this place, he has more then enough opportunity for thought. The time is 2:00 a.m., the place, Presbyterian Hospital in Brooklyn. The thoughts have been incoherent as of yet, mainly because Frankie is yet to awake. But that is all about to change. Frankie slowly begins stirring from his pain induced comatose state. The beginning is a bit fishy, but he quickly realizes what has brought him to this place. He quickly sits up, pulling the oxygen tubes from his nose, 'get this shit off me.' He glances around the room, getting his bearings. 'Dame' he growls. He quickly rips off the sheets and goes to stand. As he puts his weight on his feet he nearly falls. 'Ahh DAMNIT!' he yells as he sits on the bed. He glances down to notice that his right ankle is simply wrapped in an ace bandage, 'at least it's not broken,' he mutters as he stands again. He quickly hops over to the closet, passing a mirror on the way. He stops to glare at his reflection. The hospital gown is doing a poor job of covering his massive body and he chuckles as he opens the closet door. 'My damn jeans better be in here,' he mutters as the door opens to reveal a pair of blue jeans and a white T-shirt. He hops back to the bed and struggles to slide the clothes on. After minutes of struggling, he stands in front of the mirror, putting minimal weight on his right leg. The lighting in the room is very dark. Some harsh white light from street lamps unseen seeps through the slits in the blinds, leaving eerie lines of light over Frankie's muscular frame. He stands, staring at his battered body. The bruises and scrapes are hidden somewhat by the dark, dramatic lighting, but the pain in his body is enough for Frankie to know where it looks the worst. The pain has no ebb or flow, no peaks or valleys, it is constant, steady. The pain swells and along with it, anger and hatred. Frankie will have his opportunity for redemption soon enough, he will not let it pass by. As the time passes, still staring, his eyes flow downward, stopping on his stomach. Four simple letters that sum up the man he sees looking back at him. P.A.I.N., it seems so simple sometimes. Historians, philosophers, theologians; all devoting their lives throughout time, searching for the answers to life. Why are we here? What makes the world go around? And it's always been right in front of their faces, Pain. What makes us strive to do better? What makes sadness so bad and happiness so good? What causes activism, passivism, wars, anarchism, communism? Where does the best art come from? Pain, pure unadulterated pain. It's the most simple thing on the planet, and Frankie has grasped this fully. He has been exploiting pain to his full advantage. Striving off his own, and relishing in seeing the pain he inflicts. Hell, pain is what is moving him up the ladder, and moving his opponents down it. The tattoo he stares at has been the subject of ridicule by others in the past. 'They don't get it,' he thinks to himself. 'But they will, soon enough' he mutters out loud. He takes a few steps towards the mirror, highly favoring that right leg. He gets his eyes inches from the mirror, staring deep into his own soul. 'Who the fuck are you?' he mutters. Staring long, cocking his head to the right....then back to the left. He turn cocks it extremely in both directions, making his neck pop loudly. A sly smirk curls his lips. The smirk turns into a full smile, and then his question is answered. His smile has revealed his teeth. His newly acquired cold caps, complete with fangs, shine bright. There are five simple letters there that answer his inquiry into who the 'fuck' he is...D...E...M...O...N. 'Fuck it,' he mutters as he quickly buries his hand into the mirror. Shards of mirror shower the floor at his feet and his hand begins bleeding immediately. He limps out of the hospital room into the hall. Looking both ways, he makes his way out of the hospital and into the night... fade...
