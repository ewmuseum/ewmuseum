The Ramen Noodle Blues

8 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/08/02 The rumblings and bumbling of a Samoan waking from hibernation can be heard. Several bed sheets and covers fly from the top of a large king sized Serta mattress. Slowly a wild haired Timo Bolamba sits up in his bed and yawns. The Master of All Things Samoan opens his crust filled eyes and looks around the room. He finds a pile of dirty clothes laying on the floor, and a smell that can’t be distinguished fully. In fact, it looks as though no women were here at all! Timo looked about groggily and finally swung his feet over the edge of the bed. He stood up slowly and stretched out, his muscles tensing up a bit as he does. He walks over to the door and pops his head outside of the room to see what is going on, but nothing can be found. Odd, it was a Saturday, and Gwenn usually didn’t have much to do. “Gwenn” Timo shouted out once into the hallway, hoping to hear a reply, but there was none. The house was very quiet as a matter of fact, seeing as there was usually someone lounging around the pad. Timo walked down the hall in his boxer shorts, scratching his head and yawning. He peeks his head into the living room and looks around but nobody is inside. Damn! Where is she? The Samoan walks further down the hall and peeks down into the basement, hoping that she would be down there. “Gwenn?” Timo’s voice was wrapped in doubt. “Where are you babe?” He walked into the kitchen and looked around, finally seeing a piece of paper on the counter top. He picked it up and looked over it one time, his mouth forming the words as he reads. “Timo, I am going shopping with an old friend today. Please don’t destroy the kitchen, or the living room, or the bathroom. Actually, please don’t touch anything until I get back. If anyone should happen to come over, there is beer in the fridge. See you later, ” Timo read off. Timo scoffed. “Like I really have that much trouble with things around here by myself!” He looked on the bottom of the page and mouthed the words again. “P.S. Don’t get any crazy ideas about not screwing things up around the house.” The Samoan Silencer put the note down and pondered in semi deep just under the topsoil thought. Maybe she was right, look what he did when he had bought the computer! And the Christmas presents…and the times he got pulled over for drag racing a Honda…and the time he and Emeril made strange dishes that tasted horrible. Well actually that was just Timo. Maybe this time she was right. “Man, my stomach is really starting to growl,” Timo stated. He turns and looked around, grabbing the door handle to the refrigerator and opening it. Sure enough there was plenty of beer, but nothing worth really cooking inside. This was going to be a bit tougher than originally intended. The freezer looked much the same, except for the pack of baking soda inside…to preserve freshness or something. After rummaging around for a minute or two, he decided that it wasn’t much use trying to make a dish out of four trays of ice cubes and a block of hamburger meat. “What to do, what to do…there is a rumbly in my tumbly, and I need to eat something!” Timo spoke to the thin air. “I have to get ready for the pay per view, and I don’t see how I can on an empty stomach.” The Samoan turned to the cupboard and opened it slowly, several packets of Smack Ramen noodles falling as he does. Suddenly The Samoan gets a defeated look upon his face and hangs his head. “The Ramen Noodle Blues…” he mutters. “Why did it have to be Ramen Noodles?” He plucked through the flavors that resided in the shelf, pulling out several of the small ‘just add water’ packages. There was chicken, chicken vegetable, chicken and mushroom, spicy chicken, creamy chicken, roasted chicken, cheesy chicken…and beef. “Beef…that one kinda stands alone, a lot like me in a way.” The Samoan mused. “Look, there are all these guys in the world that are just the same thing with a different package but only one Timo. I like that package of Beefy Noodles. It stands out amongst the crowd of chickens!” Timo grabbed the one package, then did his best to cram the rest of the Ramen noodles in the cupboard before turning to the stove and eyeing it evilly. He reached out and jammed a few digital buttons, turning on two burner and singing the hair on his arm. He jumped back and rubbed his arm a bit, blowing on it, as if that would make a difference. Timo looked down at the package and read the instructions carefully. “Bring water to a boil.” He looked up, and there was no pan, nor water, so he immediately rectified the situation. He pulled a pan off of the rack that hung above his countertop, and ran some water into it. “Check.” He reads some more from the package, examining all of the three steps involved before continuing. “Crush Noodles into tiny bits. Hell I can do that!” Timo exclaimed. He rears back and smacks the package, busting the package open, and sending noodles all over the floor, not to mention a beautiful cloud of brown dust as he managed to break the sauce packet. The best part of the deal though is that on the way down, he managed to crack his hand smartly. “Jesus that hurt!!” He jumps around, waving his hand in the air. The Samoan finishes his dance and grabs whatever pieces he can from the package, hoping to salvage what he can. Timo dumps the remaining contents into the pan, and stands back, looking at the package. “Cook three to five minutes or until the noodles are done.” He looked elated. “Well, I think I can handle this, just like I can handle SeaBass. You know now that I think of it, wrestling and Ramen Noodles aren’t all that different.” He looks at the mess on the counter and smiles to himself. “No, in fact, it’s very similar. First you ready your opponent, bring the crowd to a boil,” Timo rambles. “Then you crush them with your fist, hoping to bust them open! Finally you present their defeated body to the crowd, using their feverish cheers and chants as the very boiling water to turn the opponent to mush.” He looks down at the water and the Ramen noodles he had just pulled out with a spoon to check, not realizing amidst his thoughts and ramblings more time had gone by than he thought. “MUSH?!” He yelled. “NO! The Beef has turned to mush!! Come back, come back, I don’t want mushy noodles!” There is a sound behind him and he turns, in his hands, he holds a pile of mushy noodles. Gwenn stands before him, looking at him as if he were crazy. “Gwenn! Please, pray for my noodles, they were so young, and now they are naught but mush!” He says. “Oh Timo, trust me, I pray for your noodle on a daily basis.” She smirks as she says the last comment. “Why don’t you go lay down and I will fix you a sandwich or something. Okay?” Timo glances down at the mushy noodles that reside in his fingertips and tossed them over his shoulder. They stick to the wall, and make Gwenn sigh as he struts out of the room with a smile on his face. “That’s a damned good idea. Watch me some TV, and eat a sandwich. I’m glad you are here Gwenny, what would I do without you?” He asks. “Lord Knows, Timo, what I wonder is what you did before me.” “Well, lets just say that much like SeaBass has very “lonely” nights, I had many hungry nights.” He smiles. Timo strutted out of the room and took his spot in his favorite recliner. This day turned out okay after all, now all that was left was kicking SeaBass in the ass and winning the world title…well it COULD happen…right?
