Making sense of Ivan's brain....

10 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-10-02 “So now what is it that we do, Praporschik?” said Alexei Ruslan as himself and The Russian Bear, Ivan Stanislav, stood outside of their large, nondescript red van that was parked in front of a large office building which also functions as their base of operations in the United States. “I do not know Alexei, I do not know!” roared Stanislav as he fixed a suspender strap which was coming dangerously close to falling off of his shoulder. Stanislav crossed his arms and leaned against the van with a look of confusion on his face. “It was strange, I do not understand what was going through my head!” Alexei fixed his hat atop his head and leaned against the van beside his massive Russian friend. He looked up at Ivan with a small smile on his face, “What did you wish to do, Ivan?” “It is stupid Alexei, forget it.” Alexei shrugged and playfully punched Stanislav in the arm, “I do not think anything you do is stupid, Ivan Sergeiovich. What was your first instinct?” Ivan pushed off the van and began to walk down the sidewalk which was oddly devoid of people, as if himself and Ruslan were the only two people on the face of the planet at the moment. Ruslan ran and caught up with Ivan just in time to hear him start to speak. “First instinct, Alexei, was to leave Meanstreak to his devices. Let those that are not related to Red Army be thrown to wolves, perhaps Doug Troy would make sizeable fight against Meanstreak and injure him, or each other. Something that would allow their stupid fighting to play into our hands, da?” Alexei nodded while Ivan put his hands in his pockets, “But then, I realized that perhaps, there was someone in wrestling ring that may need my help…” Ivan’s voice trailed off as he looked up at the sunny blue sky, Alexei nodded, “Tempest…” “That is correct Alexei. For some strange reason, much like I would if any of my comrades in Red Army were in trouble, I felt tremendous need to run back to ring and protect her.” Ivan shook his head as he tried to get his thoughts straight and kicked a nearby tree imbedded in the sidewalk, “and it was as if I understood exactly what I had to do, which was go down there and stop Meanstreak, not because of Doug Troy, or because I wished to destroy him completely, but because I wanted….” Ivan paused and looked at the ground, “I wanted to help her, I believe.” Alexei paused and ran his tongue along his bottom lip, contemplating this realization. “But then, I realized that she was on opposing team, she was not a comrade in that match,” continued Ivan, “but I still wanted to help her, until you and the others came to talk to me.” “And then, you wanted to leave?” whispered Alexei. “Nyet, I did not, I still wanted to go down, and if you remember, I started to advance again….” “..and then you turned and ran with us, Ivan.” “Da….” Alexei spoke louder, trying to liven the mood, “There is nothing wrong with that, Ivan. She wanted to be in wrestling ring, and she was. You owe nothing to her, you do not have to help her.” “I suppose you are correct, Alexei.” Ivan shrugged and brought his hands up to the sides of his head, “I do not know Alexei, I do not know what is going on up in my brain! Sometimes I feel like I wish to grab that little jaw of hers and crush it shut…and other times, I wish to touch the sides of her face and….” Ivan shook his head violently at the realization what he was saying, “Never mind! It is over!!” He growled and kicked a wall beside him, loosening some bricks, “She does not care anymore! She knows Ivan did not return to help her and she will surely hold this against me!!” Alexei nodded in agreement, “That is correct Ivan, but, let us focus on tonight, we have major Pay Per View tonight, no? If anything, use your anger from last night against Meanstreak and Doug Troy, I mean, after all, Meanstreak inflicted the damage, and it was Doug Troy that gave her opportunity to even BE in match, no?” Ivan nodded to himself and growled, “You are correct Alexei, those two should pay for what they did! And what better time than to do it at Pay Per View, no?” “Exactly, Ivan Sergeiovich!” Ivan grinned suddenly and started to walk again, “Yes, you are correct Alexei. This is more the reason why those two should be destroyed tonight, which is exactly what I will do. Doug Troy is on some stupid crusade to return goodness to the OSW, Meanstreak is a fighter without any sort of cause, and only one of us truly is thinking in the true interest of the OSW: Ivan Stanislav!” Alexei laughed loudly, “That is correct Ivan!” Ivan laughed and walked faster, “Yes, it will be good night tonight, Alex!” “Also Ivan,” whispered Alexei, “I have heard that Tempest is suffering from mild concussion, she may have forgotten certain events as of late, from what I have heard. Perhaps we should go visit her, and make sure she knows what exactly happened…eh?” “You mean, she may not know that I had to leave….perhaps….” Ivan said, and then grinned. Alexei grinned back. “Da, Praporschik…da!” The two men grin and turn around, walking back to the van as the scene slowly fades out….
