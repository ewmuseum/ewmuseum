Too tired to sleep...

2 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/2/02 The night drags on and on. The air is empty and cold, a cheap carbon copy of his soul. Frankie sits alone like the night before and the night before that. He can't remember friendship, he can't remember love, he can't remember shit, just the things that would drive a man insane. The 'fuck it' attitude was fun while it lasted, but it was exhausting. The constant rage and outpouring of emotion takes its toll on a person, Frankie is no different. His mind wanders to thoughts of SOW. Concentration is out of his grasp. He's not even sure who he's pissed at anymore. No idea of who he can and cannot trust. No idea who he hates, pretty sure that everybody hates him. He's pissed, sad, worried, scared, angry, tired, sick, and he's alone. Who is there to tell him it's okay? Who is there to tell him he is overreacting? Who is there to console him? Who is there to smack some sense into him? Who is there? He tries to remember who he is fighting this week on SLAM! He's not even positive he's booked. He doesn't care. He'll show up, he'll do his best, he'll try to snap out of this. But he has doubt in his mind that it will work. His thoughts come and go quickly, never working themselves out, never materializing. It's the kind of blank, direction less thought that just amplifies depression. He thinks of something, gets really upset about it, then five minutes later can't remember why he's so pissed off. You know a man is in for a world of hurt when he pushes everyone away, then can't get over his loneliness. He wants that voice to comfort him, he wants that body to be near. But no one he knows can fill this void. This void died 20 some odd years ago. A void created by a sawed-off shotgun. Everyone is worried about him, he swears he's okay. OSW shrinks say he's just ticking, waiting to explode at the most trivial thing. It was all he could do not to beat their asses. He chuckles as he realizes his thoughts reiterate their point. He is more exhausted then he's every been, but he can't sleep a wink. The night drags on and on, the haze of dawn slowly growing. Another sleepless night. When will it end? When will he be okay?
