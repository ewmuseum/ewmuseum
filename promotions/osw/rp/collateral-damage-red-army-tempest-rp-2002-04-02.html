Collateral Damage (Red Army/Tempest Rp)

2 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-2-02 Tempest was short, too short for her own good in this case. She peeped up over the elbows of the giants that made up the Red Army and tried to see what was going on as they carried Ivan through the hallways in the back of the arena. She heard curses and mutterings in German, Polish, Russian... but couldn't make out any of the words. She wished for once that communist party member Wang would speak, just so she could understand something being said. As they reached their locker room and piled in, albeit carefully with Ivan in hand, the door flopped back in Tempest's face. She swallowed hard and pushed it open. At least they hadn't locked her out. Tom Walczak and Michel Krieg had Ivan's massive shoulders in their hands, while Ruslan, Wang, and Speedy Riggs nervously carried The Russian Bear's enormous legs, and set him in the corner of the locker room. 'Capitalist PIGS!!' Screamed Alexei as he dabbed his brow with a handkerchief and kneeled beside the unmoving, bloody, and powder covered Stanislav. Krieg and Walczak stood grimly and shook their heads, while Wang and Riggs blinked at one another, dumbfounded. Tempest tried to edge her way closer through the forest of bodies and found herself staring down at Ivan's head. She crouched down near Alexei and looked at him briefly. He didn't look back, simply sat working his jaw as his color deepened. 'Has he said anything?' she asked quietly as she leaned over and wiped away some of the dust with her hand. 'Nyet, he has said nothing....' muttered Alexei. 'I know what we do! We go and crush them all right now!!' Screamed Krieg as he turned on his heels and marched to the door. Walczak stood and grabbed his arm. 'Let's wait till we get a word on Ivan first....' Alexei scoffed, 'They will get theirs, they all will.' Two paramedics barged into the room, prompting Ruslan to stand. 'We are here to..' 'Get out of here, American's!! Capitalists! No one will help Ivan Stanislav except for people of Russia!!' Ruslan screamed and pulled out a cell phone and talked into it hurriedly and paced away. Riggs and Wang walked over to Ivan. 'Ivan will not be happy.. not at all...' whispered Wang. Everyone paying no attention to Tempest. She sighed and stood up, then walked into the bathroom and returned with a stack of towels and two soaked ones. She dropped them on the floor with a sloppy thwuck that turned a few heads toward her. 'Mind if I try Alexei?' she asked with a slight smile. 'I may not be Russian, but...' she shook her head and trailed off as concern clouded over her face while she dabbed away the blood. Alexei breathed through his nose heavily, taking a moment from his conversation on the phone, 'Da, go ahead.' He turned to the phone again and screamed in Russian. 'So now what?' Roared Krieg as he punched a hole in the wall, 'We cannot just sit here and do nothing!' 'Leave it to Ivan when he wakes up, Krieg!' scolded Walczak, 'I've known Ivan for a long time, he'd wait and see the condition of us before just diving into a fight.' Walczak paused for a moment, 'And technically, I'm second in rank here.' Riggs kneeled beside Tempest to help, and saw the gash in Ivan's head and ran to the bathroom. Wang stood beside Tempest, 'Perhaps, Tempest, it would be best if you left, eh?' 'No!' Tempest said sharply. She looked up at Wang with a forlorn expression and said, 'I'm not leaving him. He... I... oh just hand me that towel will you?' she said and gulped. She leaned a little closer and said softly, 'Ivan?' Wang jumped and handed her a towel and backed off just as The Two Round Faced Women walked in with first aid kits. Ruslan screamed in the background, 'I WANT A PLANE HERE....WHO'S AIRSPACE! DYAA!!' Ruslan screamed and threw the phone across the room, shattering it. Tempest jumped and then returned to her task. The two round faced women looked at her and then at Ivan trying decide which looked worse. Ruslan grumbled to himself and began loading a gun, 'Dumb bitch is going to get it now....' Wang and Riggs gasped and looked at Ruslan, who's mind is solely focused on his revolver. 'We all want to kill that dumb skank, but we know that that would cost Ivan his shot at Toomes, and we don't want to see what happens then., said Walczak. Ruslan paused and stared at the floor, dropping a bullet. He looked up at the source of the statement, Walczak, and nodded slowly, 'Da...da' 'We must take this slowly, and remember that even though Ivan is down the Red Army is not am I not right?' Tempest looked up and said, 'The main thing is to see that Ivan's all right. And... I suppose you're right.' She looked up at Walzak curiously. Walczak looked over at Tempest and than at Alexei. He called Alexei over in Russian, so Tempest does not understand. Alexei paused, shaking slightly, and turned, 'Da....da.....' He walked over to Walczak and muttered back in Russian, glancing over at Tempest. 'Does she know what she is doing? I do not want her ruining anything, and yes I know what she means to Ivan, but I just want you to keep her under control.' Tempest seemed not to notice as she took a handful of gauze and pressed it gently against the wound on Ivan's head. She leaned over watching for any sign of response. Alexei whispered back to Tom, 'Do not worry.. I think it will be okay.. I will keep eye on her....' Krieg leaned in the background, seething while Riggs sat in the corner, dumbfounded. Walczak nods and gets back to look on Ivan. 'Tempest I understand that you have a match with Toomes this week, and even though the set up is total shit, we will be watching from nearby. This is what our Comrade, the great Ivan would have wanted.' Tempest nodded and looked up as she said, 'I just hope he can say so himself soon,' she said weakly. 'I don't think we've met really. I'm Tempest.' She raised and offered her hand, although it was covered in blood. Walczak received the hand, and even managed to break out a smile. 'As you know I am Tom Walczak, I know that you are here to help, but I just have what you would call trust issues. Just give me time, and I am sure that we will get along just fine.' Ruslan paced back and forth, muttering to himself in Russian. She nodded and said, 'You're Ivan's friends. That can't be bad. I just wish I could talk to him.' She sighed and pressed the gauze against his head; the bleeding finally starting to subside. Wang nodded, 'But what about us?? I have to fight....I don't know what to do....' Wang fixed his collar. Walczak walked over to Wang and looked at him, 'What? Are you worried, we will crush those two satanist bastards as if they were nothing, for you see that is exactly what they are.' Wang shrugged, 'It is not that I am worried....I just do not wish to be hurt....' Walczak just looked at Wang and laughed. 'YOU will not get hurt, just trust me.' Alexei paused and looked at Walczak and Wang, 'That is correct! We will bury them! Those two fools will NEVER stand up against mighty Red Army!! I can assure you this!!' Wang shrugged, 'I just do not wish to be on recieving end of any...pain, that is all.' 'Pain! Pain is in the mind my comrade. Focus and no pain will be felt!” Walczak nodded to Wang and slapped him on the back, almost knocking him over. Wang nodded nervously and shrugged, 'Yes, I suppose that you are right....I will do it for Ivan...I will rise to cause....' Tempest watched the conversatoin ack and forth, but kept looking back at the prone Ivan. Her face was etched with worry as she looked up and said, 'Alexei, did you have somone coming to check on him? He's...' She trailed off, her lower lip quivering as she tried to hold back tears. Alexei shook his head and walked back over to Tempest, 'I am no doctor, and I will not allow anyone other than someone from the Motherland work on him....we will be leaving shortly, as soon as we get helicoptor to Russia....' Alexei paused and stared at Ivan, 'He is strong, Tempest, he will be alright....' Tempest looked between Ivan and Alexei frantically and said, 'But... there's no time to fly all the way to Russia. He needs a doctor now!' Alexei fidgeted, 'When he gets on helicopter, there will be medic there. I refuse to trust any of these Americans, Tempest!' Alexei turned his back and paced away, at that moment a loud groan exited Ivan's body. 'Dyuuhhhh........' 'Ivan?' Tempest said, turning back to him. 'Can ya hear me?' She leaned closer and mopped a little bit of blood form his forehead. Alexei gasped as himself, Wang, Riggs, Krieg, and Walczak gathered around above Tempest and looked. Slowly, Ivan's left eye opened, followed by his right. It was apparent that he wasn't focusing on really anything. 'T..Tempest...?' Mumbled The Russian Bear. She summoned up a smile and said, 'I'm here Ivan,' as she stroked the side of his face gently. Ivan's eyes searched around for a moment as his brow furrowed and he focused on what was in front of him, Tempests' face. He brought his massive hand up to the side of her face and cupped it, not realizing that with that he brought blood as well. Stanislav mumbled a Russian phrase weakly, staring at Tempest, and then groaned as his eyes rolled into the back of his head while Tempest tensed and held Ivan’s head aloft. His hand fell from her cheek as he went limp again and slipped into unconsciousness again. 'Ivan?' Tempest's voice rose as notch a he slumped onto her lap. 'Wha... Alexei, what did he say?' Alexei swallowed and fidgeted as the other members of The Red Army dispersed and grumbled to themselves. 'He said....' Alexei swallowed and lowered his voice softly, 'He said, 'beautiful storm..I am sorry.'' Alexei blinked twice and then ran to his phone which he had thrown and started to dial again, very alarmed now. 'Sorry for what?' Tempest said softly, as she looked down at Ivan with concern. 'GET THAT DAMN HELICOPTER HERE NOW!!' Screamed Ruslan into his phone as Riggs and Wang stared at the unmoving body of Ivan Stanislav. 'THAT IS RIGHT! IMMEDIATELY!' Ruslan hung up his battered phone and walked back over to Ivan and Tempest, dabbing his forehead with a handkerchief. 'The Helicopter will be here as soon as possible....we are using one nearby...it should be here within the hour....' Tempest looked up and said, 'I'm going with you.' Alexei blinked, 'T...To Russia?' She nodded and said, 'I don't wanna leave him. And Miyuki can take care of things here. I already issued my comments to the press and if they want more, they can hitch a plane too.' Alexei fixed his jacket, 'Da, okay, I will let you come along, if you wish.' He fidgeted again and grabbed at his phone, 'I have to make a few more phone calls, you stay with him, Tempest.' Alexei turned and walked out the door, dialing again. Tempest watched after him and then sighed as she rubbed Ivan's shoulder, flicking the dust from the fire extinguisher away. 'Don't worry, I will. Oh Ivan... why'd this have to happen?' She looked around at the other members of the Red army, who still seemed ill at ease with her there and sighed. Krieg grunted in the corner, 'It happened because a male corrupted a female that had power...let us hope that it does not work in the opposite direction..' He snorted and pushed off the wall and walked out of the room. Walczak glared at Krieg and barged out of the room, leaving Wang, The Two Women, and Riggs alone with Tempest in the very uncomfortable room. Tempest stared at the closed door a moment and said, 'Maybe someone should tell him I'm on your side.” Wang shrugged, somewhat concerned it appeared, and pulled out a handkerchief and walked over to Tempest and started to clean up her face, 'Do not wowwy..Tempest....everwything will be okay...I just would not want to be near Pwapowschik Stanislav when he wakes up.' He paused for a moment, 'Perhaps you should west, you appear tired. We will watch after Ivan.' He wiped off more blood from her face. She nodded and leaned back, propping herself against a wall as she held Ivan's head on her lap. 'I'm ok... I'll feel better when's Ivan's better too.' Wang stood up and nodded, 'Yes, well, we will leave you alone.' He looked at Riggs, 'Come Wiggs, let us go make sure Alexei is okay....eh?' Riggs nodded as Wang, Riggs, and the Women turned and left the unconscious Ivan and Tempest alone in the room. Tempest sighed and leaned over, wrapping her arms around Ivan's neck. 'I know you're strong enough to make it through this Ivan. I just hope I am.'
