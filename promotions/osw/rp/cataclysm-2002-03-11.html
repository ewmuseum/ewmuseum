Cataclysm

11 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 11, 2002 Humanity. There are so many definitions for it, each in everyone's own perspective. What's humane and inhumane described accordingly based on social class and where you live. In some countries, humanity is nothing more than life itself. To some people, humane is doing what's right, and inhumane is the exact opposite. I refuse to believe that, infact... F*ck that. You want to know what humanity is? Humanity is loaning a dollar to someone who needs it. Humanity is going to watch your children's ball games, regardless of if they're the best or worst on their team. Humanity is going to your best friends dorm after he just moved in. Humanity, in society now however, is one big jumble of violence. Humanity is Cataclysm. Cataclysm cat·a·clysm 1. A violent upheaval that causes great destruction or brings about a fundamental change. Crowning Glory? One...big...f*cking cataclysm. *** 'Yeah yeah, point is Rhett, youz got da job done. I'm proud of ya!' Dom called from the opposite end of the pub, there wasn't enough people in the bar to even consider the place open. The once dimmed lights slowly turned fluorescent shortly after saying that. Two men were sitting at the bar, heads down, passed out from a night of party. To the far right, Dom sat, and Rhett banged the table with his fists. 'I know that. You think I don't know that man? It's not about that, bro. I got the strap, and retribution...but now, this piece of metal,' Rhett flashes the belt that sits on his table at Dom, and he only shakes his head. 'Is the last thing I'm paying attention to. That bastard Corg is taking up all my thinking time.' 'Heh, seems like things are gonna get a bit more violent.' 'No sh*t.' Rhett sighed, and took a swig of the Corona-filled bottle that set before him. Corg had been on his mind since last night, and he'd find a way to get the guy out. He always does. But right now, now there wasn't a thing he could do but wait untill Saturday. Not a thing he could do, and that's what pissed him off more. Ed had already placed him in a match with Ivan's cronies, and the odds against him were stacked even higher with Corg playing a facor in it. Yeah, like he's going to turn down a match that Ed brings to the table, he's not that popular yet, after all. 'Well, all I know is, that f*cker better be careful, or I'll do the same thing to him that happened to his b*tch.' ... Yep. Cataclysm.
