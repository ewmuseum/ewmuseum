The long week [Prelude: The brawl backstage]

7 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/07/2002 Pain... Endurance... Betrayal... All elements behind a wrestling career. Elements that you must face, day after day, in preparation for a match. Elements that are always present, whenever you have to face the wrath of seven other men, in the same place. Sometimes, there are no survivors. Sometimes, there's no other way. Sometimes, the rage that takes over your soul is so blinding that you go beyond what you should go and does something you regret later. That was not the case. [[Hi, folks, it's Dick Strang again. It's been a while since I haven't talked to you. Well, in all honesty, things have been a little hectic lately, and you must have seen why. It's been a while since the last time we've been together, but now I'm back. You all must be asking yourselves about the 'scuffle' that happened 2 weeks ago. You've seen one side of the story. The thing is, there's another side of it. Another side that hasn't been told. A side that justifies Ace's actions. Something that clarifies why he went rampant backstage and trashed Switchblade, hitting him on his 'bad' knee, and going completely berserk. This took place during that ill-fated Slam.]] **The scene starts showing scenes from that Slam. Hawk gets to the corner to see that Toomes is leaning over the ropes in order to receive the tag! Hawk leans to let his partner in, but Toomes pulls his hand back allowing Hawk to fall to the mat face first. Toomes pulls his right hand back and hits Hawk with a fierce jab to the nose that causes blood to pour out!** [[This, folks, is what started. The man that so well have been trying to change your opinion about that fateful evening, started the chain of events that only ended after the show was long over, during a huge brawl backstage that was long bond to happen. And finally, it all comes to an end. Finally, the pain of having to see the same ugly face day in and day out, having to hear the same kind of trashing come from a completely disgraceful man, a man that couldn't cut in CCW but keeps on trashing the fed that today is the top fed in PTC. The same man that insists on calling OSW HIS fed, when it's widely known that he doesn't have a call on anything on OSW. A man that calls himself a star but can't even hold himself in a ring, having to resort to the boss to maneuver himself to the World Title, a title that at least 10 guys deserve more than him.]] **The scene starts showing Andrew Hawk, walking away from the area when the scuffle happened, visibly upset. Danny Jackson and Dick Strang following him, trying to ask what happened, but Ace simply completely upset, shaking his arms to free himself from the others' holds, walking into the outside parking lot, visibly upset, searching for his black Lamborghini Diablo, which he finds fast.** [[And yet, after all that, all that Ace could think was go to the road and free his mind of any rage. I could see his intentions, I could see his pain, I could see his frustration over a non-dying subject that could leave a stain on his reputation. A reputation of a good friend backstage, someone that would always look out for the others, enjoy the free time and just plain have fun, like any Brazilians do.]] **The scene shows Ace, turning his Lambo on and speeding it in the middle of the night, out of the parking lot and into the open road. The scene follows Ace's Lambo, as he speeds through the roads, the hood lifted, the wind blowing his madly furious face, as he pounds the wheel of his car furiously in frustation.** [[I knew that, from that moment on time, his approach towards this match had changed. It wasn't a professional meeting anymore. Toomes had gone too far. He hoped this brawl would go on as fair as it could, but he never imagined Toomes would pull suck a stunt and make Ace actually get kicked out of the backstage area and be made into the villain. I remember that, for an entire week, Ace ran around, going after Hank, true, but also trying to get his facts together, so he could get off all the desire of truly crippling Toomes so he could focus on the true goal: Beat Toomes once and for all, humiliate him in front of the crowd that he despised and screwed for more than 3 months. The chance of giving back to the fans the kindness they gave Ace, when he showed up on OSW and gave them back a champion, a true champion, in Doug Troy.]] **The scene changes to the border of a cliff, somewhere in a desertic area. The sun starts rising, its purple rays starting to break through the darkness of the night and announcing the day that is coming. Sitting on the cliff, staring at the sun, is Ace, still on his ring attire, looking, thinking, meditating:** [[And then I remember, as he came back a week later, that he told me what he thought on that day. He thought about how untrue every word from Toomes' mouth was, and how could someone be so ungrateful at the entire past that made him what he was. The way he downed CCW as saying it was an indy fed, something so untrue it offended every piece of news page in the world. The way he claimed how he had been ambushed, forgetting the countless times he ran rampant in OSW AND CCW, beating people left and right, no matter how that could be harmful. The way he keeps trying to come up as a poor bastard who's a victim of circunstances, and, most of all, the painful way he despises the influence Ace had on Switchblade's start, back when they were in CCW Power Plant, where the two were friends, but most of all respected each other. Ace told me that after all the attacks and verbal attacks, that Slam was the last straw, and that he just hoped he could hold himself back when match time came. He knew he had to simply beat Toomes, but the fact that he could snap and put everything he built in OSW to hell truly bothered him. He told me he wouldn't let it. He wouldn't. But that long week before the final match was truly not a quiet one... Not at all...]] **The scene keeps showing Ace, sitting, his feet crossed, looking at the sunrise, as the scene slowly fades to black** To continue
