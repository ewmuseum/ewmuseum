The Hunt Continues- Part 1

13 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-13-02 Introduction- On Sunday night, the Last Man Standing match scheduled between Michael Dame and Frankie Bones was up in the air. There was no sign of Dame for a week. Ther rumors were circulating that the match would be cancelled. Well, that is how the atmosphere of the show felt. At the beginning of the timeslot allotted to the Last Man Standing match, Frankie Bones came out and announced that he would be the only man here tonight from the match, that Dame was in the slammer. Then Suddenly, Dame's music started to play as he stepped out on the enteranceway. Bones was shocked, and Dame continued to announce that he was let go, due to NO evedence. That he was not the culprit, and he was here to FIGHT! The match began, and god..what a match it served to be. Two broken tables, a broken monitor, a barb wire baseball bat, a steel chair, and a fire extinguisher. All weapons used during the match, but what stood out the most. The blood! They were covered from head to toe. Eventually the match lead to Dame laying in the middle of the ring, catching his breathe. Suddenly, A man in a ski mask approached the ring, with young Hardy in his grasps. Dame rolled to see who it was, screaming repeatedly 'Bones Bones Bones'.. He saw it was Hardy and did everything he could to get over there, but Frankie was quick to take advantage of the situation. He nailed Dame, beat him to a bloody pulp, and got the victory. The match ended, but Michael's Dame just began. He went on through the backstage area, looking for any sign of Hardy. To no avail, he found nothing.. other then a partying Frankie. This caused a enraged feeling flowing through Dame's body, which led to an all out brawl...but still, to no avail, no hardy... The Hunt Continues- Part 1 A dark night, close to where OSW held Crowning Glory. The sound of night, is load tonight. Camera sits in front of a large building. Looks to be an apartment, although has clear features of a hotel building. A shadow is scene exiting the hotel through the revolving glass doors. The man can not be made out, although as he walks down the road, it's clearly Michael Dame. He wears a pair of jeans, and a hooded sweatshirt, with the hood up, on this cold night in March. Dame walks down the sidewalk along the row of buildings, at a vigerous pace. He obviously has somewhere to go. He turns into a allyway. Pure mystery reads this situation. The camera shot follows him, but at a farther distance then before. Dame suddenly stops, and turns towards a door. He knocks three times, hard. The door is opened, and now the camera angle sits right behind michael looking up. The man's face is hidden behind the shadows of the ally, as well as the baseball bat he wears upon his head. 'I didn't think you were coming, Michael' Says the mysterious man. 'Sorry...I was doing something. I'm a bit stressed out, and not moving so well after the match' Dame replies. 'Well, you're moving, and that's what matters. After that match with Bones on the pay-per-view, any man would be...dead. And that's an understatement. Now, you said you needed some information?' 'Yes, You saw the match. You saw that I wasn't the kidnapper, and you saw Hardy...(pauses)..poor Hardy be carried out in shackles...? I need any sort of information you could give about who that masked man was. I will pay any amounts of money' Dame says with the sound of mental exhaustion running through his voice. 'No need for money. I know little, but what I know, you know.' The man replies as he leans in to whisper in Dame's ear. He looks both ways down the allyway, then speaks. Nothing is heard of other then air. Suddenly Michael lifts his arm, and clenches a hard fist. A softly, but very sternly pushes his fist against the cold, brick wall. 'Thank you, Pete. I appreciate it.' Michael thanks 'Pete' as Pete nods. Michael quickly turns and walks down the alleyway, back towards the street. He pushes a trashcan over as the camera fades to black.
