My New Found Hate

13 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 13, 2002 God, I fucking hate people. What are they good for? They’re always around to make you feel worse than you already are. They’re always in your face, but not looking you in the eye, and telling you lies that seem so much like the honest truth. They glared at me, like I’m not one of ‘them’ as I walked down the grimy street. I viewed billboards upon billboards, and posters upon posters, all containing the same fucking message: ‘Buy our products because you can’t live without them’. This makes me sick. I see so many advertisements and I cannot see but one of them having a sure impact in my life. This is so fucking stupid. I hate ads. I hate the media. “Hey buddy, do you have the…” the man paused, as he looked at me, very strangely, as if I don’t belong. “Uh, never mind.” What stupid shit. I don’t care about things like these, because I get it every day. That guy’s just one of the many people in this world, and just one of the many people that won’t stop avoiding me as I walk down this narrow street. I have no clue where I am. All I want right now is to close my eyes, and get away from everything. I hate my depression. It always seems to come at the worst possible times. But, it does fuel my hatred; My new found hate for Aimz. She is such a pussy. In order to get away from me and find out new ways to bring me down, she goes to my older brother, Rhett. Why couldn’t she just do that on her own, without Rhett? It disgusts me that she would go that low. But whatever, all that matters is that I keep ahead of her, and right now, I’m behind her. And ever since Aimz has taken ‘the lead’, I have been extremely pissed, but I’m not sure whom I pissed off at. Am I angry with Aimz? Yes, more than you could ever imagine. Am I angry with myself? More than likely, but yet if I was that mad with my self, I’d be dead by now. Ah, this is so fucking stupid. I can usually handle the stress on days like these, but not now. I can’t fucking take it! “Agghh, shit!” I screamed, as my fist smacked against a poster that is bolted into the wall of some movie theatre. It was an advertisement for the ‘911’ incident. I looked down upon my knuckles in anguish. They were blood red. As a matter of fact, they were covered in blood, redder than I ever thought possible. Shit. … What the hell is happening to me?
