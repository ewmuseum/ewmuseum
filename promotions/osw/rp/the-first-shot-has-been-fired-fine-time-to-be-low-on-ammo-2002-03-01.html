The first shot has been fired. Fine time to be low on ammo...

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/1/2002 Today’s promo begins where the others have begun: inside the halls of MTV Studios. However, this time it isn’t night time. Currently, it’s mid-day in the den of pop culture, and various people doing various things hustle and bustle around the Studios. Among the stagehands, cameraman and directors making Total Request Live happen in the main studio, one man walks with a purpose: former TRL host Doug Troy. Currently, he’s moving against the flow of bodies toward the talent area, where all the dressing rooms are housed. He passes many doors, each marked with various logos and plaques. Suddenly, he stops as he sees his destination: a room with faint traces of smoke seeping out from the cracks. He inhales deeply and holds it in before walking up to the door and opening. Did you ever see the movie Backdraft? Do you know that scene where the guy opens the door to his house and a huge column of fire smacks into him? Picture that scene right now, only replace the guy with Doug Troy and the fire with enormous amounts of smoke. Several smoke detectors in the hallway go off in response, adding to the noise of the deep bass beats of ‘I Got High’ by Afroman from within the room. Doug steps into the room and tries to wave some of it away as he steps in, but it’s like jumping into the ocean and trying to wave some of the water away. Doug releases his held-in air and starts in a mid-coughing fit as he tries to breath in the air of the small room. “Dude, lookit him coughin’ like a f*ckin’ lightweight.” “Sheeeeeeeeit.” “Damn, Silent Bob. He’s coughing like you after yer first blunt!” “…” Doug keeps coughing violently while the four occupants of the room, B-Real and Sen-Dog of Cypress Hill along with Jay and Silent Bob, joke at his expense. Each one of the occupants has a large joint hanging from their mouth. “C’mon, esse,” Sen-Dog says. “Let that shit out!” Doug’s coughing starts to slow as he begins to regain his composure. After a couple moments, the coughing has subsided enough for him to speak. “Cypress, you guys are on in fifteen.” B-Real and Sen-Dog both speed-puff their joints until they’re gone, then get up from the couch. “Yo, it was cool hangin’ out wit you and Bob, Jay.” B-Real says as he holds his fist out. Jay thumps it, as does Silent Bob. “Next time we come to Jersey, I’ll give ya a buzz. “Hey man, anytime. Make sure to bring that bomb-ass weed with ya.” B-Real and Sen-Dog walk out the door. Doug starts to walk out the door, but… “Whoa, where ya think yer goin’? Man, sit down in the Green room with us! Stick around for a few puffs, man!” Doug turns around to see Jay pointing at the empty couch. With a shrug, he walks over to it and sits down on it. Jay offers him a puff of his blunt. “Nah, I don’t smoke. Besides, there’s enough smoke in here to get Cheech and Chong high.” Jay all too happily pulls the joint back to his lips and puffs again. He lets it out slowly and smiles contentedly. “Good, more for me then. So, what’s on yo’ mind?” Doug leans back and sighs. “Nothing much.” “Good, cause I wasn’t interested in hearin’ that shit anyway. It’s bad enough when Tubzilla here tells ya his life story or some other f*cked up shit.” Silent Bob glares at Jay while he blows a smoke ring. “It’s just that I’m trying to think up a good way to promo against my opponent at Slam this week.” “Who, that ugly Walczak f*ck? Polish jokes, man. Lots of nasty-ass Polish jokes.” Jay leans forward in his seat as he talks. “The nastier the better, man.” Doug thinks it over a little, but… “I dunno. I mean, I’m not against Polish people as a whole, just Walczak.” “Ah, who cares? Those dudes are lazy and shit. They don’t even have any hot chicks, man. This one time, I thought I was f*cking this Polish chick in the brown eye, but it turned out that she was suckin’ my dick the whole time. Snoogans.” “Jesus, man.” Doug cringes. “That’s a little harsh.” “Are you doubtin’ my story? When I figgered it out, I pulled my shit out and ran. No ugly chick is deserving of my nut, that’s for damn sure. BOOOOONG!” “Nah. Any other ideas?” “Not a one.” At that point, Silent Bob raises his hand. Doug looks over at him as he pulls out an issue of OSW magazine. He opens the book up and flips it to a page featuring Ivan Stanislav. Doug nods as he realizes what Bob’s getting at. “So I should concentrate on Walczak’s boss?” Bob shakes his head ‘no’, then points at the picture again. “Oh, I should use it as a way to get to Ivan directly by taking out all of his help?” Silent Bob nods ‘yes’. “Yeah, that’d work. Yeah, I should try to take out the Red Army one by one so Ivan will be at a disadvantage! That’s a good idea, man! Thanks! Now, should I do anything involving Meanstreak?” Bob nods ‘yes’, then pantomimes running away. “Oh no. Nuh-uh. I’m not running away from Meanstreak. It’s because people ran away from him in PCW that he got to run roughshod over it. I am not gonna give up without a fight, no way.” “Yer funeral,” Jay says while finishing up his blunt. “You should let me and Silent Bob go a few rounds with that big bitch. Bob may look like a tubby f*ck, but he’s a mean tubby f*ck. And nobody can stand up to my berserker attack, yo. I’ll be all ‘schnickty schnick, bitch’, and he’ll be running for his mommy like the bitch he is. Snoogans.” Both Bob and Doug give Jay ‘Are you crazy?’ looks. “What? I may be scrawny, but I’ll beat him down like he owes me money! I’ll f*ckin’ take my dick out and slap him with it!” Jay grabs the OSW magazine from Bob and flips it to a picture of Meanstreak. “Dude don’t look so tough, man! I’ll be all over that bitch like Lunchbox here on a ham sammich!” “Riiiiiiight,” Doug says with a smirk on his face. “Man, f*ck you for doubting!” Jay turns the page. “Holy shit!” “What?” Doug says as Jay holds the magazine out for Bob to look at. “Dude, that’s the f*cking chick! The one from our favorite titty movie!” Doug stands up from his seat to get a look at the magazine. The picture Jay is pointing at is that of Jane Reagen. “Her?” “Betcha money it is, man. I’d recognize those titties anywhere!” “In a porno?” “Yep, me and Bob’s favorite! It’s called ‘Wall Teat’ and it’s all about these freak CEO bitches who have these huge lesbian orgies at board meetings. This chick is like this poor secretary who walks in on one of these orgies, jumps right in and biggity bam she becomes this high-powered CEO slut!” It takes Doug a few minutes to absorb this information. When it’s absorbed, all he can say is “Wow.” “I know, man! I’ll bring it next time I see ya. You’ll love it, so long as you ain’t queer or some shit.” “Yeah yeah, bring it. I’d like to see it…” Doug’s mouth shifts to a smirk, the smirk of a person who’s suddenly found a way to get the upper hand in a battle. “In any case, I gotta get back to the set. You two have fun watching the show, but keep the smoke down so the fire department doesn’t show up or something, okay?” Jay and Bob aren’t even paying attention. They’re too busy leering at the picture of Jane Reagen to see Doug walk out the door.
