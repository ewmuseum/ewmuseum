Next to cheap silicone, I look minimal.

12 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 12th, 2002 ((Note: The title has less to do with the RP and more to do with Aimz' outlook on this week's match.:p )) ''WHY did you have to choose tonight of ALL nights to leave early?'' Reno was dozing off on a rock-hard mattress courtesy of the usual cheap hotel when Amy came in the door, toting a bag of gear and pain-killers behind her. She'd driven HERSELF to the hospital after the fight with Corg's girlfriend and she'd been forced to take a Taxi back because she apparently had yet another minor concussion after getting her skull cracked into a chair numerous times. Sixteen stitches and a minor concussion in total, as if her head wasn't already fucked up enough thanks to Shyme and Corg's bullshit. Of course, in the traditionally male way, Davis didn't bother rolling over to look at his girlfriend after his incoherent groan of ''Huh?'' until she continued her griping. ''I have SUCH a headache.'' ''Wha...'' He'd finally rolled over, seeing Aimz' usually bright and beautiful face had been bruised, sliced and swollen. That's when he jumped up from his spot napping, clad only in a pair of black boxers, to survey the damage further. ''What the HELL happened to you?! Did Corg...'' ''No,'' Amy'd interrupted. ''He was busy, I think...so he sent the bitch after me.'' ''What bitch?'' ''Shyme or whatever her name is...that girlfriend of his.'' She growled. ''I'll tell you, she's fucking tough.'' Reno seemed frustrated, mostly with himself for not being there to protect her. ''Damnit, damnit, DAMNIT! How stupid was I to leave you there!'' ''Don't freak.'' Amy soothed. ''I hit her first.'' ''Yeah, but she was there...Corg was there and I totally forgot about that bullshit!'' ''Don't worry.'' Campbell persisted, warming herself up from the rain by embracing the slightly bigger Davis. ''Corg didn't come. I handled her...don't dwell on it.'' ''I guess not.'' He sighed, laying back down on top of the bed covers. It was way too humid in the room, even though the air had an unusual chill to it. ''Well, at least there's SOME good news...'' Amy shed her coat and plunked down beside him, staring at the ceiling. ''And what might that be?'' ''You're in a ladder match for World title contendorship...'' He said, smiling. ''You, Lillith, Tempest and that tall girl.'' Reno'd expected her to react with glee, or even a slight smirk...but Amy's face didn't even look remotely pleased. He rolled onto his side to get a better look at her and noticed that she actually LAUGHED at the prospect of a title shot. ''Uhh...that's supposed to be a good thing, Amy.'' ''Remind me to act excited later,'' She frowned. ''I get to be part of a match that's just there so OSW looks like they give women equal opportunities.'' Campbell continued with a sigh before Reno could say a word. ''Don't get me wrong, it's great to have a title contendorship match but open your eyes a little...do you see women in ANY title contender spot? I certainly don't. Mark my words, it's a one time thing.'' Both had disappointed looks strewn across their faces, but Davis noted that Amy's eyes were darting every which way. She was watching the small window, the doors and every crevice of the room as if some ancient beast would pop out at her like she was on Buffy the Vampire Slayer. ''What's wrong?'' The sudden of Reno's voice startled Aimz pretty badly. She'd ended up falling off of the bed when she jumped a little. ''Owwie.'' She groaned. ''And nothing's wrong...'' ''Hey, I understand a little paranoia.'' Amy was scattered emotionally...the comfort of having Reno right there was an incredible helping hand, but her eyes were still examining the room closely and her voice was weakened greatly when she said, ''Not like this...nothing like this.'' With that, much to Davis' surprise, Amy just grabbed her Discman from the night table and left the room, muttering ''I'll be in the bar...need to relax.'' on her way out the door. She didn't think to see what she was listening to, just glad to find any salvation she could in sound that could blur out the surrounding world. Unfortunately, the sound that came was David Draiman's voice...preaching Isiic Raiyn's message. Another dream that will never come true Just to compliment your sorrow Another life that I've taken from you A gift to add on to your pain and suffering Another truth you can never believe Has crippled you completely All the cries you're beginning to hear Trapped in your mind, and the sound is deafening Let me enlighten you This is the way I pray She continued listening, but the song, ''Prayer'', was painful by the second verse... Another nightmare about to come true Will manifest tomorrow Another love that I've taken from you Lost in time, on the edge of suffering Another taste of the evil I breed Will level you completely Bring to life everything that you fear Live in the dark, and the world is threatening. Music wasn't going to help. Amy tore the headphones from her ears and threw the Discman to the back wall of the elevator she was entering, smashing it to bits and leaving the PC-burned CD to roll on it's edge at her feet. The look on the face of the elderly lady even before she got there was shocked enough, but then the five-foot-eight, fairly muscular and pierced redhead entered...bruises, stitches and all. The black mesh shirt probably wasn't helping enhance her image any in the woman's eyes, considering the looks and disgusted ''Eugh''s she was casting Aimz by the time they reached ground floor. Of course, Campbell just shrugged her off in the same way she appeared to be brushing the world away lately... By the time she reached the bar, ''Tequila...don't care how, don't care what it's in. I've only got a fifty...give me anything.'' Was all she could word... But 'Fuck, I'm going crazy.' was all she could think. The man strung a few shooters in front of her and the 'festivities' in her throat began as the thoughts continued... Amy looked back to what was nearly two weeks ago...she'd just given herself a self-diagnosis of post-traumatic stress disorder while knocking back tequila shots in a hotel bar. She'd been calling herself crazy then, too. Life was in circular motion and who was to blame? She persecuted HERSELF this time...fuck blaming it all on Corg, she was allowing herself to be weak, to let everything and everyone get to her. Time to let life continue in an arrow-straight line... Meaning? The weak, paranoid side of Amy Campbell had to be obliterated by her and her alone... It was almost reminiscent of how Corg brought the once strong side of her to near-death. It was time to reverse the effects like she'd reversed the roles earlier. Amy fucking Campbell would be paranoid no longer...she'd already let Shyme and the world know that she wouldn't be the victim.
