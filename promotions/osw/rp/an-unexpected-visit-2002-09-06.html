An Unexpected Visit

6 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-6-02 Tempest took a deep breath as she rode the elevator in the Chicago Hilton. It was one of the glass type and she stared down watching the floors below disappear beneath her as she clutched a small package to her chest. The elevator stopped and she gulped before getting out, glancing once more at the small package. She stepped off the elevator and strode down the hall, reading room numbers and finally came to a stop at one. She looked at the package once more and said, 'He's gotta believe this if nothing else.' She took a deep breath and knocked on the door to room 1486. “FOR THE LAST TIME! I WANT NOTHING! LEAVE ME BE!!” The unmistakable roar of the Russian Bear reverberated from behind the door. Tempest shuddered and raised her hand again, but then shook her head and called, 'Ivan? It's me Tempest. Can I talk to you?... Please?' There was a long pause on the other side of the door. A low thumping slowly was heard as it grew in strength, obviously the heavy footsteps of the four hundred pound Russian Bear. Suddenly the footsteps stopped just behind the door as a shadow played between the bottom of the door and the floor. After a few excruciatingly long seconds, the doorlock was unlatched and the footsteps thundered away from the door again. “Enter.” Tempest bit her lip and pushed the door open slowly. Stepping in she summoned a smile and said, 'Hi Ivan... How are you...feeling after the other night?' She shot a worried glance at his face and took a step closer. Stanislav stood roughly twenty feet from the door, obviously backing up after unlocking the door. He stood coldly, his hands on his hips clad in his typical attire and didn’t return the smile, “How am I feeling?” He paused and rubbed his cheek where he had received a gash from Mexi at By Any Means Necessary, the gash was red and angry looking, obviously not tended to and healing very slowly. “I am fine Tempest, I am glad you are looking out for my best interest.” She blinked a few times fairly rapidly and said, 'I wish I could be. But you'd have to let me a little closer to do that.' She shook her head and said, 'Ivan, I wish you'd let me try and make up for things. Why... why are you so mad at Mexi? And at me for that matter?' As she spoke, Stanislav swallowed and his face reddened just slightly. He shook his head quickly, almost overemphasizing it, “You… you do not understand Tempest.” He turned quickly and took a few steps away from her, almost hitting his head off of a ceiling fan. She nodded and took a few more steps closer to him and said, 'No, I don't, that's why I'm asking. Ivan, I wanna help, but you have to talk to me. I can't stand feeling like I'm letting you down, but not knowing how or why.' He breathed heavily through his nose and turned slightly, just enough to see her over his large and towering shoulder and said, “You knew this entire time. You knew about Mexi and you did not tell me. You kept it secret from me Tempest you…” Ivan blinked, “Mexi is a fool and a vagabond. A man that wants nothing more than… than a laugh.” Tempest smiled slightly and said, 'Yeah but so am I.' Ivan turned on his heel and faced her, “I know that Tempest, and I have told you that I am not here for fun, I am here to work. That is only reason I am here anymore, to get job done and to get back into Mother Russia’s good graces. Mexi thought it funny to badmouth and berate Stanislav in PCW and once again he did it in the OSW.” Ivan breathed heavier as his shoulders began to rise and fall quicker, “And you smiled at my face while knowing it entire time.” He stared at her, letting the words take their effect, “The entire time, Tempest.” Tempest gulped and looked up at him sheepishly. 'I had to Ivan,' she said softly. 'I gave him my word. He didn't want me to reveal who he was and I agreed. And you wouldn't argue with that if you just understood that... well, that he brought me back here in the first place.' “Bah!” Stanislav roared and turned suddenly, kicking a chair in the process. He wheeled around and pointed, “Do not make him sound like some… some… what do they call them… Saint!” Ivan breathed angrily, his nostrils flared, “That is all I ever hear Tempest. I do not understand, I do not know entire story, I do not comprehend…” he absently rubbed his forehead, “perhaps if people would not hide behind masks and behind smiling faces that would not be problem!” Ivan tried to calm himself, “Let him hide behind a mask if he was afraid of me. I have no use for cowards.” 'He's not a coward Ivan,' said Tempest flatly. 'He was brave enough to come and find me. And Ivan, why is it that you're so mad about me keeping my word? Is it because it was to someone other than you? I thought it still counted.' She folded her arms with a questioning expression. Ivan growled, “If he was so high and mighty, he would understand that perhaps it is more important to tell someone the truth, particularly when they ASK!” He stomped, shaking the floor and probably scaring the tenants below him to death. “Why is he not here? Has he sent you to apologize for him? To apologize to me and the fans that he lied to for five months? And… more angry because it was someone other than me? Do not be absurd Tempest!” She shook her head and said, 'I mean that that whole honor thing only seems to apply, as far as you're concerned, to you. Doesn't it make sense to you that if someone else asked me to keep their secret I would? I owe him Ivan, for even being back here... and so do you, that is if you really wanted me back here.' She looked away sadly, a hint of a pout developing on her lips. “Do not tell me who I owe thanks to Tempest.” Ivan growled, almost a threat. He took a step closer to her as his brow furrowed and his tone softened, “The people here are glad you’ve returned Tempest, so surely I am glad you are here as well.” 'I... I hope so,' she said and looked up at him, her eyes softening. 'But, Ivan you gotta believe I came back because of you, and Mexi helped. We're not your enemies. I just want you to understand that so you can fight the people who should be instead of us.' “I don’t need you telling me who to fight and how to act, Tempest.” Ivan put his hands on his hips, “I think I can take care of myself. If I wanted lecture, I could have walked into Moscow and asked to see Igor Karpov! And it’s nice to see you that you came back for me, particularly to lie to my FACE! 'I wasn't lying!' Tempest snapped. She stomped her foot once and tried to stifle tears that were threatening to well up. The effect was a lot of rapid blinking as she held up her little package and said, 'You want proof of why I kept his secret? You really must know?' Ivan stood up straight, “You are going to show me proof why you lied? Go ahead and show what you have in your bag Tempest.” Ivan crossed his arms in front of his barrel chest, defiant, “Humor me.” She removed the tape from its wrapping, her eyes never leaving Ivan's. 'Just remember,' she said. 'You asked for it.' She turned and popped the tape into the VCR and after a moment of static it came to life. ~Ivan looked down and stared at the feather again, 'She meant a lot to me, El Drago. How could one person, one individual, mean so much to a Communist? How could one solitary woman mean so much to Ivan Sergeiovich Stanislav?' He sighed and leaned back against the wall, staring at an unknown portion of the wall ahead of him, 'When I first met her, I thought she was nothing but child. I went on that vacation with her because it would make Russia look good. I did not care for her. I did not care about stupid contest!'~ Ivan stared at the television, petrified. He blinked and swallowed and looked at Tempest. “What… what is that??” He rasped. 'It's a little tape that Mexi brought to me,' she said. 'I think you know how it goes, but we can watch the rest to refresh your memory if you like.' ~ Ivan sighed and breathed heavily through his nose, 'You know, El Drago, she was first person to like me….for me. Even Alexei first got to know Ivan Stanislav because of his reputation, but Tempest….nyet…she ignored all the negatives about me and enjoyed my company.' He stood up straight again, 'And she defended me when no one else would. She stood by my side whenever I needed her…and did I?'~ “I… I…” he opened his mouth and closed it several times, “That was…I just…” Tempest hit the stop button and stepped in front of him with a small smile. 'That is the reason I came back,' she said softly. 'That is the reason I dared to hope. And Mexi, then still El Drago, brought it to me. So do you see why I had to honor his wishes?' Ivan swallowed and barely nodded, “You… you have made your point… Tempest. I will uh… consider what you’ve… said.” Tempest looked up at him hopefully and said, 'Please? I just...' She shook her head and said, 'If you really wanna hate Mexi, you're gonna have to hate me too and vice versa. We were both trying to help you. Maybe we didn't do it exactly right, but we did the best we could.' She stepped closer and put her hand on his arm. Ivan looked down at her tiny hand as it rested on his arm and looked at her, “Tempest, I will try. That is all I can tell you, and I cannot promise a thing.” He glanced over at the television and looked back at her and nodded. She smiled and said, 'That's all I can ask for.' She turned and started to leave, but looked back a moment and said, 'Oh, and I wanted to thank you for all the help last weekend. I... Thank you for being there for me.' “Tempest,” Ivan mumbled, “thank you for being there… for me as well.” He crookedly grinned and caught himself, changing it to his blank, granite look again. Tempest almost snickered, but caught herself and simply smiled. 'Sure... I'll continue to be,' she said and let herself out quietly. As the door slowly shut, Ivan Stanislav was once again alone in his hotel room. He blinked and stood very still for a few seconds, still in a stupor at what had just happened. Slowly, the Russian Bear turned to the VCR and ejected the tape and stared at it, before putting it in a nearby duffel bag that served to hold most of his private possessions. The Russian Bear reached into his pocket and pulled out his now infamous feather and stared at it. Perhaps she was right. Perhaps he should stop fighting against the tide and give in. Perhaps things were not as bad as he thought. He stared at the smile and just barely… …smiled.
