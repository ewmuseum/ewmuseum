Mobilization

17 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-17-02 It was on Saturday night that Ivan Stanislav participated on Saturday Slam, in which himself and Meanstreak were brutally overtaken by the team of Heresian Bloodlust, which is comprised of Azrael Cain and Hessian. It is something that could have been averted. Should have been averted. But it wasn’t because of their incompatibility with one another. After the match, Stanislav made no attempt to save Meanstreak, who continued to be brutally beaten within the ring. Actually, Stanislav didn’t even stay for the rest of the show that night, instead he stormed out of the building with Alexei Ruslan, Communist Part Member Wang, and The Two Round Faced Women in tow and headed to his van. From there, he went to the nearest airport and hitched a ride directly back to the Kremlin where he could discuss pressing matters. Military matters. The situation in Chechnya, an area within the Russian zone of occupation that was home of a huge revolution, was still a thorn in the side of the Russian Federation. Militarily, if the Russian Federation wished to it could easily crush the Chechnyen rebels straight into the ground….but politics prevents certain things. Humanitarians, activists, and pacifists stop this violence that has been building in Russia for more the six years now. However, after the heated debate that Stanislav had with the rest of his peers, he was allowed time alone to think about what his next move within the United States should be. And therefore, he sat in a small office in the Kremlin and read a book quietly as he awaited a report from Alexei Ruslan regarding what his next weeks schedule would be like. Stanislav sat at the small desk in his military uniform, complete with hat and all his badges of awards that he has won, including old soviet awards that while they may not mean much of anything now, he still wears with pride. A small book, well actually, a relatively large book that is simply small in comparison to Ivan Stanislav, sits open on the desk. Ivan follows a line of the text with a finger. A small mug sits beside Ivan’s book and a coffee pot sits beside that. Ivan sighs to himself and grabs the pot and pours some coffee into the mug. He lifts the mug and looks at it for a moment, before reaching behind the desk and producing a large bottle of vodka. “Liquid heat, best that Russia can produce!” Ivan grins and twists the cap off of the vodka, he pours a generous amount of vodka into the coffee and closes the vodka, setting it beside the coffee pot. He brings the mug to his nose and takes a deep breath of the drink and smiles, before tilting it to he mouth and downing most of the substance down his gullet in one large gulp. He smiles, feeling the warm coffee mixed with vodka run through his body and opens his book again, reading carefully. A door is heard opening as more light is cast into the room and onto Stanislav. He looks up and nods as Alexei Ruslan walks into the room carrying a file folder under his right arm. “What is the news, Alexei Gregorovich?” Asks Stanislav, closing his book and leaning back in his chair. Alexei opens the folder and fishes out a few papers, handing them to Ivan to inspect, “The next card of week is out, Praporschik, it is very interesting to say the least. I believe you will find it quite enlightening….” Ivan takes the paper and holds them in one hand, holding his mug in the other. He looks at the document, humming some sort of tune to himself as he does. He nods to himself, muttering moreso to himself then to Ruslan, “Hmm…Krieg and Enforcer? Never have I heard of this man. He wished to fight Comrade Walczak? I do not think so. There is no way he will get through Krieg….” Ivan pauses and looks up at Alexei, who stands and watches Ivan. “Do you think this man can overcome the might of an East German?” “Not likely, Praporschik. I was, as I am sure you were, thoroughly impressed with his performance tonight. He will not fall to the likes of The Enforcer.” Answers Ruslan. Ivan nods and looks farther down the page. He pauses and nods again. “Walczak does have match, this time against Rhett Locke for International Title. That is good, he will surely win. I have been very impressed with the way in which Walczak has been carrying himself. He is turning into a very devout communist, more and more by the day. He will defeat Locke, because there is not other alternative to the question. When he does, he will bring title into fold of Red Army. That will make nice morale booster, considering other events that have done nothing but cause us problems.” Ivan growls and shifts in his chair. Alexei nods in agreement. “Yes, I have no doubt that Walczak will win International Title. He will be first Communist to bring gold to Red Army. It will be glorious…” Alexei nods to himself and shifts his weight. “MMmmmhhmmm…” replies Stanislav. He brings the mug to his lips and begins to drink, “GGRRAAHhhhhnnnnggg!!!” Ivan coughs suddenly, spewing coffee and vodka all over the desk. He drops the papers. Alexei jumps and produces a handkerchief from his pocket. “Praporschik? Are you okay?!” Yelps Alexei as he dabs the desk. Ivan drops the mug on the floor, shattering it. Ivan rubs his nose and lifts the now soggy paper, “Tempest vs. Meanstreak?? Red Army is not permitted in vicinity??” Alexei pauses and nods, knowing that was what Ivan was upset about. Ivan stands from his seat and carries the paper with him. “What is doing on here?” Screams Stanislav. “Does Jane Reagan think she can stop me from doing something that I wish to do?” Ivan pauses, more thoughts flooding his mind and overlapping one another, “Wait? Forget Reagan! What about this match! I told Ed NOT to sign it!” Stanislav pauses and stares at the floor for a second, digesting this thought, “Ed is the problem. I told her he was nothing more than collaborator!” “But Praporschik, is that not what she wanted?” Whispers Alexei, “If it is what she wanted, there is little that we can do to stop such a match from occurring.” Stanislav turns to Alexei, his massive shoulders rising and falling with every enraged breath, “There are few things we can do, Alexei. Eliminate Ed. Eliminate Reagan. Or….violate the rule that she set up!” Alexei nods to himself and grins, “That is right! This is nothing more than a test!” “Exactly Alexei Gregorovich. She wants to see if Ivan will back off! She wants to see if Ivan will flinch when a blockade is put up in my way!! She should know Ivan better than that. I will not falter just because it costs a match! And during that, I will also destroy Meanstreak!” Ivan thinks for a moment and rubs his chin, running his fingers through his scraggly beard. “This is actually opportunity, Alexei, and we will make it so!” Ivan paces back and forth, “Yes, that is what we will do. It is time to take advantage of this.” Alexei nods and thinks for a moment. He takes his hat off and holds it in his two hands, “What do you think Tempest would think of that, Ivan?” “Of what? Calling Reagan’s bluff?” “Nyet. Of interfering in this match. She was adamant of getting it.” “She will understand, I am sure she will. She will probably appreciate the fact that not only do I crush Meanstreak, but also defy Jane Reagan at same time!” Ivan contemplates this, somewhat unsure if that is truly how it would work out. The two think for a moment and Ivan shrugs, “Nevertheless, I want the people of OSW to know this! Let us have document written up stating our beliefs! Let the OSW know what we stand for!” Alexei nods, “Da! I will draft one up after we are done speaking, Ivan. But there is more to document than you have seen.” Ivan stops and lifts the paper up and reads it. He stops and reads aloud, “Ivan Stanislav vs. Doug Troy for OSW World Title Shot….” Ivan brings the paper down slowly, still staring where it was, “Do you know what this means, Alex?” Alexei grins to himself, “I believe so.” Ivan turns to Alexei and drops the papers on the floor, “It means we will be fulfilling multiple obligations! Personally! Politically! Militarily!! I will defeat Doug Troy and no longer have to deal with his trash mouth….and then get World Title shot which will lead me directly to Toomes and Reagan…and then I will get World Title and bring it back to our Motherland!” Ruslan claps, “That is right!! It is exactly the break that we wanted!!!” Ivan thinks and picks the paper up again and reads, “There is nothing here that appears to be doctored. Nothing that appears to have any strings attached. I believe this is authentic.” Alexei nods, “It is, Ivan!” Ivan drops the paper and claps, “Alright Alexei! We have work to do! Get my statement out regarding this ‘blockade’ of Reagan. And then, I want you to get the word out to every other member in the Red Army, we must silence Ed for what he has done!” Alexei nods, “Da, I will do that.” “And then, I want you to ensure Tom Walczak that victory is the only option and that he has our full support, and inform Krieg that Enforcer will not be a problem! Finally, prepare to mobilize fully so that we are all prepared for Doug Troy vs. Ivan, and we can make sure nothing goes awry on that front!” Ivan thinks for a moment to check if he forgot anything. “Maybe you should contact Tempest, Ivan. After all, you should consult her before you make any other changes.” Whispers Alexei. “BAH! She will understand. Get the statement out, I will get into contact with her tomorrow about all current plans.” Ivan wrings his hands together, “This is golden opportunity, Alexei. This week will mark the end of the OSW….and the rise of The Red Army…..DYAAAAHAAAHAAA!!!” Ivan laughs loudly as the camera fades to black, his laugh echoing in the background.
