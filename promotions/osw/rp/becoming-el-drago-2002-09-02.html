Becoming El Drago

2 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Monday, September 2, 2002 Step 1: The Character… The character of El Drago was a character that I grew up using. Like many young Mexican wrestlers if you didn’t have a mask, you didn’t have an identity. For me that identity came in the form of a Dragon. The man that raised me from a child was my inspiration, he was the Ultimate Dragon and I longed only to be just like him, so the idea for El Drago was born. I would become the Mexican Dragon, a Mexican fighter like none other before him… Step 2: The Costume… The character now figured out I had to come up with a costume that was not only striking to the eye, but one that would not obstruct my wrestling ability. It took a day of brainstorming and sketching before I found the look I wanted. The mask was to look like a Dragon’s head and the body to be dark scales and in this suit I would look just like a Dragon. I sent my drawing off and several weeks later my suit had be crafted. It wasn’t exactly what I had sketched but it was both eye catching and very, lightweight. The mask had tassels that stood up on the sides looking like the fiery flames of a Dragon’s breath. The mask and suit blended beautifully and was a mix of black scales, dragon heads, fire and smoke. When I slipped the costume on I was no longer myself, I was EL DRAGO… Step 3: Developing a Style… Now that I was El Drago, he needed a style that was all his own. He had to be tough and charismatic. Agile and exciting. It took months of training but soon I was happy with the wrestler I had become. Step 4: Starting Out… The time had come for me to step into the ring for my first match. I was young and wide-eyed taking in every shout and cheer from the crowd. The people were quiet at first and it took time for them to understand the Dragon but I didn’t give up. Match after match the people started to get louder and louder and I gradually became better and better. Finally a chance of a lifetime came. The chance to fight a legend of Mexico, as they said. To fight the man that trained me and raised me… To fight the Ultimate Dragon. Step 5: Fighting a Legend… I recall very little from the match as everything happened so quickly. I was living a dream and in the process become a dream to so many others. By the time the bell rung and my hand was raised I had no recollection of how I had beat my mentor but the impact would change my life forever. Step 6: Becoming a Legend… From then on wherever I went the people knew of me. “That is El Drago” They would scream and every match I could spot many fans wearing Dragon masks. Many times I would walk home and down the streets I could see small children playing and pretending to be El Drago, and yet not one of them recognized me. Step 7: Fighting the Demons… El Drago was famous, but I was not… For any Mexican fighter that would be enough but for me it wasn’t. Perhaps it was the Canadian blood of my parents that pumped through my veins but I wanted my face to be seen. It was a constant fight for me not to unmask myself, not to pull of my mask after a victory and say “Look it is me! I am El Drago!” But deep down inside I knew the people would not accept it. I would betray my heritage if I unmasked myself. Step 8: The Opportunity… Then one day it happened, a talent scout from a major federation in America contacted me with interest to bring El Drago to America. I was so excited and yet wondered, if this could be my chance, the chance to become the man behind the mask. And as luck would have it, they were very happy with that decision because masks are viewed much differently in America. Instead of being who you are a mask is nothing more then a disguise that hides who you really are. At least that is the American perspective. Step 9: Leaving Mexico… The time had come for me to leave Mexico, not to face the personal demons I had in the past but this time to face my future. I didn’t want to think about what I was doing and what the consequences were. I just wanted to go and with that I left El Drago behind.
