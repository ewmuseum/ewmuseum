A Not So Pleasent Phone Call. (Featuring RUSH's Teresa Tomas.)

9 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/9/02 As the scene opens we can see a black 2002 Chevrolet Blazer pulling to a stop outside of a small hotel. The break lights dim down as the engine goes off, a few seconds later the door opens and out steps the new number one contender for the OSW Cruiserweight Title, Scott Taylor. It's been about forty-five minutes since Slam! has finished, and Scott could not be in a better mood. The man who once used holding the NTICW Championship as his only credibility now has something else to brag about, pinning Kid Wonder. Scott slings a red gymbag over his left shoulder and slides a small black celluar phone onto the waist of the denim shorts he is wearing. A hand slides into his pocket and he pulls out a keychain, a beeping sound is heard after the button is pressed. With his vehicle now safely locked and the alarm on the keys go back into his pocket and the trek towards the hotel begins. It wasn't a pretty bad debut, he did do what he said he would do. He came in and beat the person who held him down and kept him out of PCW all those months ago. But as much as he'd like to think that it's a thing of the past, it isn't. Kid Wonder isn't going to take being cheated out a title shot lightly and he's probably already plotting revenge. Damn. Scott comes to a stop infront of the door to his room. As he slides in the key to unlock the door the celluar phone clipped onto his waist goes off, playing the theme to Quantum Leap infact. But atleast it isn't as bad as using the 'Live Long and Prosper' sign at every available chance. He pushes the door open and steps in, using his foot to shut the door behind him. 'Alright, alright. Hold on.' The gymbag hits the bed and his hand goes for the phone. He presses the button with the little phone on it and places the phone next to his ear. 'Hello?' 'What the hell was that all about?!' The voice on the other end of the phone belongs to Scott's girlfriend and now RUSH wrestler, Teresa Tomas. The tone of her voice tells Scott that she is not in a very good mood at all. Scott cringes slightly and takes a seat on the small single bed, next to his bag. 'What was what, hun?' 'You know what I'm talking about Scott Adam Taylor. You blatantly cheated to beat Kid Wonder. What happened to the Scott Taylor who earned everything he got in wrestling?' Full name usage. NOT good. Scott grimaces and tries to think of what to say to her. 'What are you bitching at me for? He's the one who's been holding me down. It's not like I really hurt anything by bending the rules a little. Besides, you know I deserve what I'm getting. Remember when we were looked down upon as second rate when we were in NTICW together? Well, people aren't looking down on me anymore. I pinned him and my mark has been made.' 'So? You cheated, Scott. Don't you remember what happened the last time you tried taking the low road? You ended up in the crazy house. I don't want to see you there again. You know how hard it was to get you out of there...' the retaliation of Teresa. 'That wasn't my fault anyhow. You know I was set-up when I was sent there. That's enough about that. I have bigger plans. I have to wrestle that out of control drunkard and former champion, Doug Troy this week. I need to form a strategy...oh wait. Doug will probably show up intoxicated as hell and will trip and knock himself out before he even gets to the ring. Ha.' says a non too serious Taylor. 'Scott, what's wrong wtih you? You sold yourself out to Reagan behind our backs and now you're nothing like your self. I hope that someone reminds you of why you never got into PCW....' With those harsh words the other phone hangs up. Scott sits there stunned for several minutes, not moving or even blinking. Did Teresa really just tell him that? Does she even really know how much wrestling means to him? He pulls the phone away from his ear and presses the 'talk' button to shut if off. Slowly he sits the phone down on the side table next to the bed and stands. 'Why I never got into PCW.......Because of HIM!' Suddenly his foot slams into the side table causing the bottom drawer to cave in. Scott lets out a scream and then kicks the table again, breaking the bottom drawer into pieces. He turns away and storms towards the shower as the scene starts to fade away. 'And the established people like Troy! But how the mighty fave fallen!' The last words spoken by Scott as he disappears into the bathroom. A victory over Kid Wonder is something, but one over Doug Troy is just the break he needs to make a name for himself. Stay sober Doug....
