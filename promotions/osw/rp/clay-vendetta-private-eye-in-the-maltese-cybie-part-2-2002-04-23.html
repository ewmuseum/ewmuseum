Clay Vendetta: Private Eye in... THE MALTESE CYBIE! (Part 2)

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/23/02 ON TODAY's EPISODE OF CLAY VENDETTA: PRIVATE EYE... Clay appears to have gotten a lead from the man at the bar... sending him to a toy store in Miami. But what answers would he find there? Let's find out on today's thrilling episode of Clay Vendetta... PRIVATE EYE! [in black and white...] The mystery man had left me a mystery clue, but the pieces were starting to fit together like some sort of cosmic jigsaw puzzle. What better place to search for a mystery killer robot dog than at a toy store, right? Absolutely. And so I drove, and so I was driven... driven by the pain in my ribs and the one piece of the puzzle that was hidden, missing, like it fell under a sofa cushion or sucked up by the vacuum cleaner or eaten by the cat or... uh... something. With his fedora firmly planted on his head and a steely gaze in his eye, 'Classic' Clay Vendetta swings a tight left turn in his 'Cuda, pulling the vintage car into a cleanly-paved parking lot. The bright glow of the 'Gee-Whiz Toys!' sign flashed in the sky, and as Vendetta parked he could see a pair of children walking out of the store. 'I want the pack with the Pikachu!' said the first boy, grabbing a metallic pack of cars out of the second boys hands. 'Alright, then I get the pack with the super-rare-legendary Gold-Plated Psyduck!' 'No! I want the Psyduck too!!' said the first boy, kicking the second one in the shins. Sigh... the allure of the Pokemon cards. Everything that's wrong in society today, it seems... I dread to think what these children will become... zookeepers and veterinarians... ugggggggggh. Vendetta stepped out of the car and passed as the mother interceded, separating the two boys: 'Now Felix, now Lester, if you keep arguing I'll take you back to Daddy, and you know what Daddy does when you two fight... don't you?' The boys stopped their scuffle and hung their heads despondently: 'He beats us severely,' they mutter in unison. 'That's right. Now, let's go, Mommy's got to get back in time for her book club.' Meanwhile, Vendetta had entered the store and was looking around suspiciously... Wow... how this place has changed since the days of the Atari, and I can't say it's for the better. Now ATARI... man, *that* was a system. Pitfall! Pole Position! God, that was real gaming. Stepping up to the counter, Vendetta addresses the lethargic-looking teen behind: 'Alright, I need an... I-Cybie.' Reaching behind the counter, the cashier produces one, and taps a few buttons on the register... 'That'll be one hundred and twenty-nine dollars and ninety-nine cents.' Vendetta's jaw drops a good four inches. $129.99? Jesus Christ! I can buy a damned chihuahua for that and have enough left over for a collar! No wonder the economy's going to shit if parents are actually buying these things! Sighing, Vendetta puts his credit card down on the counter. 'First medical bills, now this,' he mutters, and the pimply-face cashier kid returns the card back to him. 'Hey,' Vendetta says, leaning on the counter. 'You wouldn't happen to have a copy of Duck Hunt for sale, would you?' 'Huh?' the kid replies. Atrocious. I am shocked and apalled. 'Sigh... never mind,' Vendetta says, taking the i-Cybie and heading towards the door. But before he gets there, he's stopped by a small kid, about twelve years old... 'Hey! Hey mister! You're that wrestler, aren't you? What's his name... C... C... hmmm...' Finally! A child with some taste! I'd be more than willing to grant an autograph... Smirking, Vendetta bends down to talk to the kid: 'Yeah, that's m...' '...Chris Benoit! That's right! He's the short one! You really kick ass, mister.' I hate America's youth. Rolling his eyes, Vendetta returns to his feet and heads back to the door. 'Go smoke some pot, kid.' ON THE NEXT EPISODE OF CLAY VENDETTA: PRIVATE EYE... so now Clay's got his hands on the elusive i-Cybie... but what next? Will he find out the answers to the mysteries that have been plaguing him? Will the toy store ever come to its senses and start restocking 8-bit Nintendos? Is anybody reading this damn thing anyway? All these questions... and more... will (probably) be answered in our next installment of... CLAY VENDETTA: PRIVATE EYE!!!
