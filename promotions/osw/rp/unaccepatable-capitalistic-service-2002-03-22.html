Unaccepatable Capitalistic Service...

22 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/22/02 Walking down the street is Tom Walczak, and he looks like he is a bit angered. He looks around at where to go, and eventually decides to head into a bar. This is the usual place he goes to sulk in his sorrows, but as he enters the bar he looks around as if he is looking for a specific person or place. Finally, he sees what he is looking for. In the distance a large man is seen. He has a black cap on his head, and is wearing a t-shirt with an imprint of the German Flag on it. This is undoubtedly Michael Krieg the newest member of the Red Army. Walczak walks over to Krieg, and they give the custom Eastern European embrace. They proceed to sit down at the bar, and the bartender without even asking hands each man a beer from their respective countries. “Ahh, this is the service that I like comrade Krieg. It is nice to know that in this capitalistic country. They realize that we are men of stature and men that are feared of wherever they go. They know that if they do not obey the communist regime than they will be destroyed. Yes Krieg, slowly but surely this pathetic country is coming around full circle.” As Walczak speaks the bartender who gave the men the drinks comes back over to the table. He goes up to Walczak and Krieg as if he does not know that he gave them the drinks. Then he asks them if they want anything to which Krieg and Walczak begin to laugh. The man looks at them with a strange look, and then Krieg answers back. “My fellow comrade, you are almost to good to us. For now the beers that you have provided will do. We will be sure to summon you if any troubles or needs arise.” “What the hell are you guys talking about? I didn’t get you guys anything, if I did why would I come over here to ask you if you wanted something.” “What are you saying, you came over here and placed two beers directly in front of us, and both were from our respective countries. You obviously meant to give the beers to….” Mid sentence Walczak is interrupted by a couple of guys in the background. The two men behind Walczak yell at the bartender from their table. “Yo, what the hell is keeping our drinks? We ordered them like half a fucking hour ago. I can’t be as patient as this.” The bartender looks at both Walczak and Krieg, and proceeds to yell at them. “Are you guys a bunch of fucking morons? Did you not here me say that you should pass the drinks back and I will be back with you in a minute. Those two guys ordered the beers that are in your hand now, and if I were you I would explain it to them so they aren’t so pissed off at me.” “What if we do not want to apologize, because to tell you the truth I don’t feel like getting off of my big Polish Ass in order to tell those two dipshits that they didn’t get their beers because of a dumbass bartender.” “If you talk to me like that then I think that I will kick your ass out of here, how do you like that?” “Well, if my comrade doesn’t have any objection I think that we will leave this shithole capitalistic bar.” “Objection, why it would be foolish of me to stay Comrade Walczak.” With that the two men leave, but not before they take their beers and splash them in the face of the capitalistic ungrateful bastard of a bartender. They walk out with a smile on their faces as the bartender dries off.
