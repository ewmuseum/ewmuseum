Messages part 1

29 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29th May 2002 In the corner of the living room in a large apartment in Kirkland Washington there sits an answering machine, that’s it really, just an ordinary answering machine. There is nothing special about it, its not magic, this isn’t the Lord of the Rings. The display on the answering machine indicates that there are three messages recorded, beside it lies a fax machine, there is one piece of paper lying in the in tray. The owner of the apartment unlocks the front door and walks through the door, he is carrying several grocery bags, the contents are not really that important, lots of rice and lean meats, the kind of stuff that someone who has concerned with keeping in shape would buy. The owner of the apartment throws his keys into the tray on the table beside the door and walks into the living room, he notices that he has messages and hits the play button before walking past the counter that separates the living room from the kitchen to put away his groceries. BEEP ‘Ian its your dad, how are you son? We haven’t heard from you in a couple of weeks, I suppose you were busy seeing as you just moved in and got back on the road. Anyway give us a call when you get this. Your mother misses you.’ Ian Wright smiled at that. His mother missed him, that was probably true, but he had always been close to his father. The old man probably missed him even more. Even though he was exited when Ian signed with OSW and realised that it was good for his career he was a bit disappointed that his son would be leaving for America again, he wouldn’t get to see him that often. BEEP ‘Hey Ian its Morty, listen I just talked to the MET-RX people, they really want you to do an ad for them, could mean big bucks, call me.’ His agent, that guy was really starting to piss him off, Wright had used him to negotiate his deal with OSW. Admittedly he had done a good job, the deal that Wright had signed meant that at the end of his 4 year contract he would be financially secure for the rest of his life, in part that was the problem, Wright had no desire to do commercial work but his agent was committed to getting every penny that he could, they would have to talk. BEEP ‘Hi Ian its Shanda Russell from the OSW office, we haven’t been formally introduced but I do some of the interviews at Slam, I was hoping to arrange an interview this week before your match to get your comments on it.’ She was quick, Wright hadn’t even been told that he was booked yet, having said that the sheet of paper that was beside the fax bore the OSW letterhead, that must be the booking sheet. ‘Specifically I want your comments of being paired with Jesus St. Crux in a tag match against Jason Winters and Kanaida by commissioner Porter.’ ‘He did what?’ Wright exploded. A tag match? Him? With St. Crux? Surely there was some mistake. Wright replayed the message. No he heard right, he picked up the letter and it confirmed what Russell had said. What the hell was going on?
