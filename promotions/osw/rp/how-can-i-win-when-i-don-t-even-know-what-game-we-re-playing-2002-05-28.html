How can I win when I don't even know what game we're playing?

28 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-28-2002 “I dunno what to do, Frisco…” The scene fades up on a bottle of champagne. It’s been opened already, and judging from the lack of bubbles, it has been opened for a long time. The glass bottle reflects the figure of a man in the room, but it’s hard to see who they are. The voice, however, has already given away that person’s identity as Doug Troy. “I mean, it seems like every time I try to come up with a good promo, it degenerates into a trash-talk fest. I’ve aborted three promos in just the past few days alone because I couldn’t think of anything worth a damn to say.” A hand grabs the bottle of champagne and pulls it out of frame. We don’t see what happens next, but the wet sloshing sound gives away what’s happening to the contents of the bottle. “Damn it, flat.” The hand sets the bottle back down in front of the camera. The label, which reads ‘Cristal’, is now facing the camera. “Sure, I know that it’s natural to be pissed. The man ruined my moment of glory, a moment that’s been in the making for almost a year and a half. But dammit, this shouldn’t be affecting me THIS much. I mean, TRASH TALK. You know how I hate to trash talk. I mean, I know how you’re like the fucking trash talk king, but you know how trash talk is unnatural for me…” The camera pans around to show several empty bottles of Cristal. Someone’s been doing a lot of drinking here, it seems. Five guesses who that someone is, and the first 4 don’t count. “Documentary? How would I do that without bashing all of Poland?” The camera pans again, this time to a TV playing OSW Aftermath. Highlights of last Slam’s main event are being shown. “Oh, stupid me. You’re right, who gives a shit about Poland anyway? Thanks, I’ll do that. So how’s the election going?” More panning. From the window, we can see that a volleyball court has been set up outside. Various scantily-clad women jump and bounce around, trying to hit the soaring volleyball. Male spectators cheer both teams on, but with this many women in bathing suits jumping around, the true winners are the fans. “Heh, no shit? So can I get his cabinet position? Ha ha, just kidding man. So, when you coming to OSW so we can show the fed heads some DAMN respect? Never? Aw c’mon, don’t say that…” The camera pans again, this time to the source of the talking: Doug Troy. Doug’s face is again decorated with Band-Aids, but not nearly as many as he had from his attacks last week. Instead of his standard t-shirt and jeans, he’s clad in an Armani suit and slacks. Doug’s chatting on his cell phone (if you haven’t figured that out already) to his longtime friend and stablemate Frisco, the man people say could be the next El Presidente of Argentina. “Well, you gotta do what ya gotta do. Hey, I gotta get to the party. Yeah, I better go. I mean, I’m throwing the damn thing. Might as well show up at my own party… anyway, good luck with that election thing, man. Bye.” Doug hangs up the phone and stands up. He straightens his tie and grabs the OSW World Heavyweight Title belt that sat beside him during his conversation with Frisco. He flips it over his shoulder and stands tall as he takes a deep breath. He lets it out, then walks to the door and grabs the handle. “Better late than never, I guess.” Doug opens the door and steps out of the room as the feed fades to black.
