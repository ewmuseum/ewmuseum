Book 1 -- Chapter 3

29 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 29th 2002 Chapter Title: Checking Out Beep. Beep. Beep. Beep. That is the sound of life. Life that is given to people by a machine. Beep. Beep. Beep. Beep. This is a familiar scene. Time and time again he finds himself in this place. He might as well call it home. This is the very reason why his career can end at any moment. This is the reason why he suffers from scared brain tissue. It's a double edge sword for him. He has a reduced sensation to pain, but that just makes the things that he does more drastic. Allowing him to further believe he is invincible. Retirement for him wont come with old age, it'll come due to a shortened life. He'll die before he retires. Don't worry. He's not going to shoot himself in the head, then come back to life six months down the road. 'How's our patient doing nurse?' 'All vital signs are good. He's heavily sedated at this moment. So he should be sleeping like a baby for a few more hours.' The doctor looks over the chart. 'These wrestlers are complete idiots. Why would they risk their bodies like this? To be constantly put in a hospital every other week?' 'I don't know doctor. But it's entertainment.' 'Entertainment? I don't know about that. Mr. Shadows here has 3 broken ribs, a severe concussion, slight swelling in the brain and a partially dislocated shoulder. That's not entertainment, that is brutality. He won't be seeing any action for some time.' Beep. Beep. That mother fucker. Beep. Beep. I hear every word you're saying. Beep. Beep. 'Now lets just take a look he--' Suddenly the doctors words are cut off as GV's hands quickly grab him by the throat. He slowly gets up and lets out a moan signifying he's in pain. He rips the wires off of his body and shoves the doctor into the wall hard. The nurse tries to run, but GV grabs her by the back of the head and yanks he down. She flies towards the ground and her head hits the floor, knocking her out. GV turns his attention back to the doctor, who tries to plead with Graphic Violence. 'Please, Mr. Shadows...you need to lie back down on the bed. The medication has made you delirious. Just relax and we'll get you some help.' 'Relax? I'm fine doc. I couldn't help but recall that you just called me an idiot. And that dumb bitch over there saying that it's entertainment. Well let me give you a little insight doc. I play for real and I play for keeps. That's why I'm the best. These other guys you see on TV, they're nothing but puppets. They're told what to do and how to do it. They're told when to eat, sleep, drink, piss, sign autographs and play to the fans. But that's not how I work.' 'I'm..i'm sorry. Just please sit back down on the bed.' GV just sneers at him. 'I don't fuck around. I don't do this for your entertainment...I do it for MY entertainment. Locke thought this was just going to be another little feud to make his career look better. But I'm not going to put Rhett Locke over. I'm not going to see him shine in my spotlight.' The doctor sits there with a confused look on his face. 'Ok, now I'm convinced that your delirious. Let me just give you some of this...' 'You're not going to give me anything Rhett! I'm going to fuckin' end you NOW!' 'But I'm not Rhett, I'm-' GV lifts the doctor off of his feet and throws him into the wall again. He takes the urinal pan and begins to repeatedly hit the doctor over the head with it. He then drags the doctor by his hair over to the window. The doctor can hardly stand as some blood trickles down his face. 'I'm checking out of here Rhett, while you will NEVER leave. I will see to it that your career ends, and that you rot in this hospital till that day that you die.' And with that said GV takes the doctors head and brings it crashing into the window. The window shatters into a thousand pieces and the doctor quickly hits the floor. GV stumbles to the door and looks around before he staggers into the elevator. He rides it down to the basement. The doors open and he looks around before stepping out. He notices the loading area is open. He begins to run, but falls down due to his sedated state. He gets back up and runs outside, he makes a right and we loose sight of him. He's out, and most likely making his way to SLAM!
