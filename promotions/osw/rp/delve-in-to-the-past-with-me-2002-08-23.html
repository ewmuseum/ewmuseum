Delve in to the past with me..

23 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-23-02 Lets take a trip shall we? Right in to the darker past of Callisto Bane. Have you ever wondered what drives her to do the things she does? To say the things that she says? Tonight while the Olden Beast is taken by the slumber we will show you. But keep in mind this was only the beginning of her torment..Remember what you see inside of her dreams and perhaps you will better understand her..Now get ready because here we go. “Cameron! Cameron, you mother fucking...” The girl’s hoarse voice died away as she ran out of breath. It’s just as well, she thought, I’ve already used every vulgarity I can think of. She had been screaming for hours, screaming through her tears, as if he could really hear her. She tried to focus her foggy eyes on her surroundings. She knew that she was in the woods somewhere, that she was cold. Naked and cold. It wasn’t just the weather that was cold, but it was her essence. Everything was cold. She knew this even as she was too numb to physically feel it. Lifting her parched mouth to the sky to catch some of the rain that was falling, she went over the past few days again in her head. “Cam, I found this in your dresser...” With a sharp stare came the reply. “What were you doing fumbling around in my shit!? Cally?!” “I – I was looking for a pair of socks to borrow-“ “Bitch! Liar! Who the hell do you think you are!?” Cameron slapped her hard across the cheek, but his harsh words hurt more than the physical abuse. Cally dropped the syringe she was holding and sank to the floor. “I didn’t mean to invade your privacy... I’m sorry...” She let her words trail off as she slipped her fingers up his pant leg. Cameron smiled slowly and slyly, not offering an apology for his own invasion and making no effort to conceal his raw lust. He knelt at her level, one hand on her hip and another under her shirt. He kissed her neck, moving downward... he never kissed her on the mouth anymore. She let him have his way with her. It was always like this, and sex was the only way she knew to calm his sporadic tempests. When she met him she knew he had a temper and a strong will, and they had their arguments, but he had never abused her before – emotionally or physically. She had always assumed he was only capable of tough love and their relationship was healthy and normal. But lately his storms came more often, and he was much more violent. Then she had found the drugs. The next day at breakfast she approached him again. After last night she thought he might be more open for conversation. “So... so what is it?” “What’s what?” He mumbled through a mouthful of Wheaties, not taking his eyes off of the book he was reading. Surprised at her own confidence, she said, “The drugs I found in your dresser. What are they? Heroin?” She fixed a steady eye on him, but his gaze was more piercing. She let her eyes drop to the table. “I told you it’s none of your fucking business!” he exploded and slammed his book on the table. “None of your concern!” Cally took a breath and said, “Yes, it is.” “What?” Cameron’s cold stare returned to her. “Cam, I care about you. Lately you’ve been... distant, angry... it scares me. I want you to stop whatever it is you’re doing.” His eyes foretold his outrage. Cally squeezed her eyes shut and clamped her hands over her mouth to suppress a scream as he took her by the shoulders and threw her onto the floor. “What fucking right do you have to tell me what to do!?” Cally didn’t answer, but tried to retreat from him, hiding her wet eyes. He stood over her, looking down with condescending anger. “You’d better cower, bitch! You should know your place! My affairs are none of your concern. Get up on your knees and suck like women were made to do!” This last remark cut her to the bone. To her relief, Cameron turned around and put on his jacket. As he made for the door, he turned around and ordered, “Stay in the house. Don’t go anywhere. And don’t you dare use the phone!” Then he was gone, leaving her quivering in a corner of the kitchen. She had her bags packed before he came home. A phone call had already been made to a friend who lived in the next town – she would hitchhike there and try to get a bus somewhere; she would go anywhere that took her away from him. She was going down the front steps when he pulled in the drive. The colour rushed out of her face and she could feel her heartbeat increase as she dropped her bags over the porch railing in a vain effort to conceal them. “Just watering the plants,” her voice was barely audible as he walked toward her. Knowing that he knew her plan, she braced herself for whatever his punishment might be. Stealing a glance at him, she was surprised to find no outrage in his eyes, just apathy. He smelled like sex. Cameron pulled her to him gently, running his fingers through her hair. He picked her up and carried her through the door, through the kitchen, into the living room, then laid her down on the floor. 'Is it over?' Cally silently asked him. 'Are you okay now?' She was soon to find that he wasn’t. In a sudden fit of impatience, he ripped at her clothes, clawing her skin. Cameron cried out. “Do you like it, bitch?” Cameron whispered with a cruel smile. He went inside her violently, hurting her. Suddenly she came to the realization that this wasn’t Cameron, her soul mate – this was a stranger, a venomous rapist. Why always on the floor? She wondered absently. As soon as he withdrew from her, she faded into sleep. Later Cally woke, but it wasn’t on the living room floor. She was in their bed, enveloped warmly in Cameron’s arms. He was awake. The first thing she thought of was yesterday, and his drugs. Seeming to read her thoughts, Cameron asked softly, “Do you want to know what it’s like?” “You know as well as anyone that I just got out of rehab five months ago. I don’t need to get back into crank,” she whispered timidly. He chuckled. “Poor, naive little girl...” Reaching behind him, he retrieved something from his bed table. It was a syringe, already filled with a dosage of the drug. “Cameron...?” The cold hands took her left arm. “Cameron! Stop!” Cally struggled to escape his grip, but he was on top of her, pinning her nude body to the bed. When she felt the needle pierce her skin, she knew it was too late. At first she experienced a flying sensation, like butterflies in her stomach – but the next moment she was falling, and the mutated body parts of nightmarish creatures took shape in the cracks in the walls. It was always a mistake to use any drug when you were in the wrong mood – and somehow she knew that this wasn’t heroin, but something else entirely. She turned wide, haunted eyes on the man who had initiated the dream, the man who was subduing her. As Cameron injected more of the drug, she reeled. “Too much!” was the most she could manage to scream. “Stop! It’s too much!” Her eyes became fuzzy, and she couldn’t tell if she was crying from fear or from pain. Cameron’s face remained completely expressionless, and Cally knew that he wasn’t a man anymore; he was a savage animal. Two more shots and she sank into oblivion. When she came back into consciousness she couldn’t see. She couldn’t feel anything but pain – her mouth tasted old and musty, and her first instinct was to call out for help. When no one came, she commenced to cursing Cameron even though she knew he wasn’t there. Her vision slowly returned to the same haziness from before she passed out. Hearing only distant echoes, her body quickly became numb from the cold, for which she was grateful. Now it was only a matter of waiting for the death she knew was inevitable. She couldn’t save herself with sex this time. It was too late for her, too late for that beast she had once loved. She closed her eyes and screamed, “It’s too late, Cameron! Too late!” Her tired voice wavered between shouting and whispering. “You can have your drugs but you’ll never have me! You’ll never have my soul..” Softly she murmured her last words through a sly smile. “But I have yours. You’re going to die just as I am. I hope you like your new home in hell, mother fucker. Tell the devil I said hello.” As we release our hold on Callisto Bane's dream realm we can see her eyes shut tightly. No longer was she sleeping soundly, but a single tear was now squeezed out of the corner of her eye. Her pillow is clutched tightly to her chest while the scene slowly fades away..
