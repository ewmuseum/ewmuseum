Trip to the roots and the classic 'Peaceful Protest': Looking for a redhead?

23 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 23rd, 2002 We all go back to our roots at one point or another. Some sooner or more often than others, some may only get the chance to once. Either way, we all need to recognize where we come from, what made us the way we are or just simply what made us... You guessed it, Amy's decided to do that. She's back in New Minas, Nova Scotia for two days just to revisit what made her who she is today. First stop? The Kentville town arena for some 'Real Action Wrestling'... So Aimz was limping in through the heavy, steel and glass public entrance doors, her ultra-baggy nylon cargo pants making a 'swish' sound with every step she took. After stepping into the cold, vast rink that had been converted in springs for 'Real Action Wrestling' and Home & Garden exhibits, she was greeted warmly by a bear hug. The man attached? None other than 'Wildman Austin', R.A.W wrestler and all-around local star known to Amy as Gary Williams... He was about six feet tall, a slim but muscular build was concealed by a pair of torn denim shorts and a South Park t-shirt...his long brown hair and goatee left scruffy, to be watered down just before the show began. ''Amy!'' Williams' voice was deep, but kind in this instance. ''You've been gone so long!'' Aimz pulled back from him, letting Gary look at her a moment...not too long, though. ''I know! Since I was hired to OSW it's been pretty hectic...I don't get home often and, when I do, I barely have a spare moment!'' ''Or you're hurt...'' Long brown hair sagged over his shoulders when Gary noted her favoring a leg. ''...like now. Who did that to you, anyway? I haven't seen Slam yet...'' ''You've probably noticed I'm getting my ass kicked by a giant Russian each week...nothing much.'' ''It's more than 'nothing much' if it's got you like this'' He gestured to the bruises and her bandaged forehead. ''...don't try to fool me.'' Williams scorned, acting the same as Amy's father would have. ''And it's only going to get worse...Mike blew a gasket when he heard you were being picked on by that Army thingy.'' She was a little shocked to hear the name of another old friend. ''Mike Hughes? He's still worrying about ME?'' ''We all worry about you, you're a crazy little bitch.'' He smiled, indicating his own dry humor with a wink. ''But it looks like you've got somebody in your own federation to do the worryin' for us!'' Campbell was slightly confused by the statement, Gary had a tendancy to be sarcastic, vague or both. ''...excuse me?'' ''Awww c'mon...what's his face...ummm...Rolo or something...Romeo...I dunno.'' ''Reno?'' ''That's the one!'' He roared triumphantly, as if he'd figured out the name on his own accord. ''Reno Davis! He's awfully...goodie goodie........dontcha' think?'' ''Goodie goodie...Reno? Heh...you must be thinking of the wrong guy, Reno can get pretty crazy if he wants to.'' ''Has he gone 'crazy' on you yet?'' The redhead slapped her older friend on the arm playfully, trying her best to look offended. ''I don't see how that's any of your business!'' ''Well HAS he?'' ''No...'' She lowered her head, muttering, ''...no matter how hard you try with him, it just gets you nowhere. Besides, I'm not concerned about getting him in bed...I'm seriously enjoying his company.'' ''Company, you say?'' Amy could tell by the shifty eyes 'The Wildman' used, he was prepared to deliver yet another rib. ''...company on those cooooold, lonely nights, you mean.'' Yep, same old Gary Williams...he still laughed just as hard, and still took punches just as hard for his efforts. ''Fuck off, Gary...'' She couldn't help but giggle. ''It's nothing like that...yet. Anyway, though...you've got some guys working here.......Frank Swallows and some really freaky thing named Mike...'' ''Ha! Yeah, I know 'em...'' ''Well they know Reno, and I'd REALLY appreciate speaking with them...'' ''Can't, they're off for these few shows.'' ''Damnit!!!!'' ''Jeez...you trying to get Swallows in the sack too?!'' Williams belted out a rather hearty sounding chuckle. ''...good luck! He's not among us, we people here on earth...he prefers Planet Assram......'' ''Oh my God, Gary...you've sunk to a new level of lame humor.'' ''Blow me, I thought it was funny.'' He shoved the smaller redhead, sticking his tongue out as if they were on some playground. ''At least I'm a happy fella, unlike you...Little Miss..........stuff.......'' The two continued their idle conversation...barely paying attention to the show as it progressed. Afterwords, everyone hung out, wrestlers and a few fans alike. Amy actually had fun not drinking, though a few 'ring rat skanks' had deterred her momentarily for a beating. It was the next morning when she headed for Washington, having caught up with everybody worth seeing... THE NEXT DAY...WASHINGTON Appropriately enough, as Amy walked the streets of Washington, D.C, ''Deer Dance'' by System of a Down was blaring, appropriately enough and almost maniacly from her headphones... Batallions of riot police with rubber bullet kisses Baton courtesy, service with a smile Beyond the Staples Center you can see America With it's tired poor, avenging disgrace Peaceful, loving youth against the brutality of plastic existence Pushing little children with their fully automatics They like to push the weak around ''Where the fu------'' About to ask herself where the protest was, the shouting, screaming and general chaos sounded off as she rounded the corner of a brick building. There it was, Riot Police and all.....hmmm... Far be it for her not to join in on the fun! She stepped into the frantic crowd, searching for any sign of Reno Davis...a turqoise eye, the bright red hair, a monkey...but nothing. No Reno, no ZaC...she couldn't even find that super-tall freak guy Davis was supposedly going to be there with. But he was there, he wouldn't have just made it up...here was the riot, and she had to find him...otherwise she was just standing around, getting shoved around for a bunch of bullshit she didn't really even pay attention to. Of course, she wouldn't tell Reno that...if she didn't sound absolutely submerged in worldy events, it might not be his thing...it might deter him from her. War in Afghanistan? Ha! She wasn't terribly worried for the people in Afghanistan...they weren't about to attack her. Horribly self-centered, Amy wasn't...but when she had other things on her mind, things that would ultimately affect her far more than some Afghans dying, nothing was of a whole lot of concern. She'd avoided the pepper spray, thank God. Unfortunately, she wasn't exactly prepared if she'd encountered anything...only wearing a little black tanktop and a pair of white Kikwear cargo pants in the pleasant spring weather. Amy was accustomed to concerts, mosh pits and street fights...but little else. This 'friendly protest' was a little angrier than she'd assumed...rioting was new to her, but she was confident she could do it. But she didn't succeed. The moment Campbell stepped into the inner-most portion of the chaos, a cop, decked out in black riot gear, came at her out of nowhere with a baton. He struck her over the shoulders but, before he could hit again, a masked man came out of the crowd to take him to the ground. ''Tha---'' She went to thank him, but then noticed that the man wasn't masked the same as the rest of the protesters...his was almost a wrestling mask... A twisted laugh as he stared at her, saying nothing more than ''Hello, sweetheart.'' Got Amy more than a little afraid... The same man who had encountered Reno Davis earlier was walking towards here, and that's all you get to see for now...
