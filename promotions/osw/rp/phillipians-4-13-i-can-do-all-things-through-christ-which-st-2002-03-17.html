Phillipians 4:13 - 'I can do all things through Christ which strengthens me.'

17 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17 March 2002 The scene shows white background and in the centre of what can be seen is a circular clock, with a dark brown frame and the numbers in bold dark brown letters, with the hands of the clock in a similar brown. Both hands are smoothly rounded into an oval like shape with a smooth point on each hand. The time says it's half past six, but the small thin line which is known as the second hand is moving round, it moves from the three to the four to the five. Then a calm southern tone, round the region of Tennessee says… 'Time flies when you're having fun…' The camera then zooms out to show looking at the clock is Eric Sagar. He is wearing a snazzy grey Amani suite, with a white shirt underneath and a light grey tie. He is standing there calmly with a wineglass filled to the brim with red wine clutched to his chest. Underneath the clock is an open fireplace it has a dark brown wooden frame, and the fireguard is made of black mesh like metal only it has wider gaps. On the right side of the open fire is a rack for all the open fire tools all made from a fake gold. On the left side is a coal bucket made from a thin fake golden metal, with a handle. The room has dark brown floorboards but in the centre of the ring is a white fluffy rug facing that and the fireplace is a black leather sofa. On the right side of the rug, sofa clock and fireplace is the side opposite to the camera view. On that wall is just a door with a dark brown wooden frame, and the door has the same kind of wood. The man is standing on the carpet clutching a wineglass half full of a red wine to his chest with both hands. He is rocking on his snazzy black leather shoes from heel to toe, making a faint tapping noise. 'But as time draws on my brothers, fate draws near.' Eric Sagar calmly announces, with his eyes fixed on the clock which is now on twelve to one to two to three, 'I am Eric Sagar, I'm the manager and close associate of the man you know as Propaganda.' His voice has a distinct air of gloating in, and his face, which resembles that of a bulldog has a smile raised on it. 'Now Propaganda and myself have a complaint… that we are in Old School Wrestling, yet there is nothing 'Old School' about it.' Sagar brings the wineglass close to his face. 'We have people like Great Dragon, who is a masked Japanese Wrestler.' Sagar takes a sip of the wine, then brings it down to his chest. 'He refers to himself as a mythical creature, a dragon. I mean if he wants to play around nursery rhymes and fairy tales then so is it. But please don't inflict this on the Old School Wrestling organisation.' Sagar's tone has a certain humours tone, as if he is mocking what he has just said. 'We in the Christian religion do not believe it Dragons and Monsters and your acts are a joke to us. Great Dragon we are not your enemies we are to help you' Sagar offers. Sagar holds the wineglass with one hand and holds it in front of him. 'We also have Angeldust Nick Casey. ' Sagar mutters out after that… 'You claim to be an angel. From heaven, for none of us are from heaven my friend. For we are all from the wounds of our mother, but yet being 'Angeldust' you must claim otherwise. 'Sagar draws then hand not holding the glass to his chin. He has a curious look on his face. 'So there for....' he pauses in thought 'with modern science, yee must be a what are they called?' Sagar asks, 'Isn't it 'Test tube baby?' Well therefor you must be labelled as a freak and no one wants to see a freak, for…' The walls begin to shake. Sagar smiles 'Aha,' Sagar pleasingly says. 'He's here.' Sagar draws the hand of his chin and signals to the door with it. And the door is thrown open at great speed, and makes a muffled bang against the white painted flat stone wall. And in stands the massive 7' 375lbs monster Propaganda. He is wearing his usual ring attire, he strides forward and meets Sagar and they stand toe-to-toe. Sagar looks almost like a midget compared to Propaganda, Propaganda looks at Sagar and says in his emotionless southerner accent. 'My Brother Eric, I have been brought here to exterminate a new evil.' Sagar nods his head then says backing up Propaganda 'You are correct.' Propaganda smiles and he says once again without emotion 'This is false advertisement, cause when you see the title of this fed 'Old School Wrestling.' Yet Old School Wrestling did not have freaks like Angeldust Nick Casey, fairy tale stars like Great Dragon and Vampir Nosferatu.' Propaganda's voice gets filled with rage, but is interrupted. 'Ah Yes, I was getting to him.' Eric informs Propaganda 'He disgusts me, he disgusts you and he disgusts the Christian nation and I labelled him my Brother.' Propaganda implies. 'As what?' Sagar asks 'As a Satanist? a Goth? a loser?' Sagar's tone becomes filled with hatred 'I gave him the dubbing of 'the first of many', the Christian Nation humiliated him. As he thought I was just another newbie, just another stone to step on… he was wrong.' Propaganda bellows. 'He is a Satanist Propaganda, a Satanist. With no Morals, no sense of right and wrong nothing! He was so very wrong.' Sagar's sweating, and his eyes are bulging. He looks a scary site, yet Propaganda stands there emotionless to it. 'My Brother Eric, you should be calm, for their wicked ways shall be stopped soon. On the subject of Satanists lets look at my two partners, The Magi and Epitome of Evil. The Magi is a very mystical man And I do not approve nor respect that of him. He is also what I label a Satanist along with Epitome of Evil, for it was just a week ago I took him down and beat him like a petty fool!' Propaganda gloats. 'You need not of there help.' the hatred filled preacher - Sagar tells to Propaganda. 'I shall strike down upon them with anger and furious vengeance!' Propaganda roars. Sagar looks on laughing he draws both hands to the wineglass and nearly squeezes it until it breaks. 'For I need not of there help. To quote Phillipians 4:13 'I can do all thing through Christ which strengthens me' Propaganda preaches, still without emotion. 'True Propaganda, for if they get in your way you shall annihilate them. Tell me how you shall do it. ' Eric requests with an evil chuckle. 'Tell me how you are going to grab him…' Eric looks like he is reaching a peak of ecstasy '…by the head with one hand and catapult him too his death.' Eric gets carried away, he then throws his wineglass at the wall with much anger and hatred it makes a loud smash, bits of shattered glass fly everywhere. And the wine itself slowly drips down like strands of tears… the camera watches this and finally fades to black.
