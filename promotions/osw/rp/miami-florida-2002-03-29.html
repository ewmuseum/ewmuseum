Miami, Florida

29 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-29-02 The product of a natural disaster or that of something that hit so hard that not only everything was destroyed, but those things that we desire most. Miami, Florida today it’s hot and humid. A nice day to be out on the beach, soaking up the run as the rays shine down and hit your face, but instead we are locked inside of a room with the air conditioner blowing at the max. The room isn’t small, however, it is one that is huge. Two beds sit in the room, and several doors for which we do enter. One being the bathroom that is massive and almost as big as the room with the huge beds in it. Back into it all we find several more doors, one leading into another bedroom with two more huge beds and finally we come close to the last door and we hear laughter rising and the sound of balls cracking together. Slowly we make our way through the door, and into the laughter as we see; Jesse, Chass, Michelle, and John playing a game of pool in this room that isn’t quite as big as the others, but big in itself. “Can you believe this place?” Michelle says with enthusiasm. “I’ve never stayed in a hotel room that has had all of these things in it. Not even when we traveled with the CCW, dad.” “Well, they only get the best for the best and trust me, OSW can afford these kind of things a whole lot more than CCW could have ever imagined having that kind of money. Plus, they usually had to spend most of their money on the arenas that we went to.” Jesse says as he stands up and walks over to the table. John walks over and takes a drink out of his Pepsi and sits it back down, “Yeah, I’ll tell you this much though. Compared to some of those other federations that we’ve worked for this one is the best thus far. I think the only one that actually came close to this was JEW.” “Yeah, but working for them has come at a price on our heads. Do you know I can’t really count how many times I’ve been called Jewish because of my stint in JEW.” Jesse begins to laugh. “Does it really matter if people make fun of you over it? You were one of the best they had to offer and if I’m not mistaken, they were considering placing you in the Hall of Fame alongside of Kurt Kangaroo before they changed management.” John says as he raises his soda in the air. “Yeah, and it was Ragnarok, not Kangaroo you dumb nut.” Jesse says as he begins to laugh. “Eight ball corner pocket.” Jesse draws back and brings the pool stick down onto the cue ball. It rolls down the table and the eight ball goes right into the corner pocket. “Jesse, can’t you let me and Michelle at least win one game?” Chass says with a little bit of anger and resentment in her voice. “You and John have won the last five games and you told me that we would have a good chance at winning.” “You did have a good chance at winning. You’ve only got, umm.” Jesse begins to count the balls left on the table. “One, two, three. Five balls left on the table.” “This is total bull crap Jesse! You knew good and well that we didn’t have a chance against either you or John and I’m sick of you always doing this crap to me.” Chass says. “At least you tried. Doesn’t that account for anything?” Jesse asks Chass with a sincere face. “No, it doesn’t. I want to go shopping, but we have to stay here because you’re afraid that you’re going to get out and get attacked. Why can’t you just realize that these people aren’t going to attack you unless it’s on live television?” Chass demands an answer. “I guess you’re right, but I don’t want to take the chance and if I let you take that chance, you’ll go out and I’m afraid if anything happens to you that I’ll never be able to forgive myself.” Jesse answers. “I know and trust me nothing is going to happen to either me or Michelle if we go out for a little while.” Chass pleads. “Yeah, dad. There are a bunch of little stores and places right next to the hotel. It isn’t like we could get lost or anything cause everything is within walking distance.” Michelle adds. “You know, now that I actually think about it, I would actually like to go as well, Jesse. So, I could go and look after the girls.” John says. Jesse looks down at the ground and begins to shakes his head, “As much as I would like to give you all permission to go without me, I can’t.” They all scream out in unison, “Why?!” “Because I’m going with you,” Jesse says as he looks up and smiles at them all. “I’ve decided that since you all want to go that I’m going as well. I don’t want to stay here locked inside of this place all alone by myself. Can you even begin to imagine how horrible it would be just watching television for at least three or four hours?” “Actually, I thought you might come around to your senses. I know how you are and I know good and well that you wouldn’t have wanted to stay up here all alone.” Chass says smiling. Jesse looks at them all a little weird, “So, this was all one big conspiracy to get me out of this place?” “No.” Michelle says in a very sarcastic manner. Jesse eyes Michelle with that look only a father can give his child, “Well, give me a couple of minutes to go and get ready. There is no way I’m going out in what I’m wearing right now.” “Me either.” They all agree. Each of them goes their own way and the room becomes empty. Slowly sinking into the pool table, everything become green and the words “30 Minute Time Elapse” appears on the screen. Picking back up in the down stairs front desk area of the massive hotel that Jesse and his company were staying in. The elevator doors open and out walk the group. Jesse wearing a white wife beater, a pair of long denim shorts, some sandals and a pair of black sunglasses. His hair spiked up and all of his tattoos exposed. The others wearing similar sets of clothing, but different in their own ways. “So, where we going to first?” Jesse asks the group. “I’m going to one of those clothing stores.” Michelle says. “I believe I’m going to go with Michelle on this one.” Chass agrees with Michelle. “I think I’m going to the music store. I need to get me a new CD.” Jesse says. “I’m off to look for something to party with tonight,” John says. “We still having that party tonight, aren’t we?” “Hell yeah.” Jesse says. “Let’s get going then.” Chass says. Jesse looks down at his watch, “We meet back here at five o’clock. That will give us all about two to three hours of shopping. That work for you all?” Everyone agrees and they make their way out of the hotel. Everyone makes his or her way across the street except for Jesse who begins to walk down the street and into this little record store. “Man, I’m telling you, Iron Maiden was so much better than the Bay City Rollers.” A man behind the desk says. Jesse looks on a little awkward towards them as he makes his way back towards the CDs. The one man that was talking turns and looks at Jesse. “Hey, bro. Can we help you out any?” He asks Jesse. “Yeah, well, I was looking for that new Forceable Entry CD.” Jesse says. “Oh yeah. The new WWF one.” The guy says in reply. “We got it in the other day.” He turns and walks down through the CDs until he comes to the CD and picks it up for Jesse when a familiar voice catches his attention. “Hey yo fo! I tol you I wasn’t looking for no damn country bull shit.” Comes the familiar voice as Jesse turns around and looks. Jesse approaches the man as he continues his verbal assault on the store attendant, “Hey, you. Aren’t you Jordan Crazy from NTICW?” “Snap, yo. Why the hell you fo’es keep asking me that question? Damn!” Jordan turns and looks at Jesse. “Yo, I know you ma’n. You that guy from OSW.” Thinking for a moment, “Damn, I can’t think yo name. What was it again?” “Jesse Ramey.” Jesse says. “Yeah, that’s you ma’n. What ya been into ma’n?” Jordan asks. “Nothing much, just out looking around to see what’s new in these places. I wasn’t really wanting to leave the hotel, but you know how women can be.” Jesse says as he begins to laugh. “Yea, ma’n. I know how those women be. Tells me though, why you didn’t want to leave that damn hotel?” Jordan asks again. “Well, to honestly tell you, I was afraid some of Nelson’s men might have been out around here somewhere. Just waiting to pick me off at the right time.” Jesse answers. Jordan looks at Jesse in a funny way, “Come on ma’n. That’s a little bit fooish. You just paranoid man.” “Yeah, I am.” Jesse begins to laugh. “I’m also afraid that Reagan is just placing me and Vendetta in this match on Slam to try and draw those guys out. Either way, they’ll be getting theirs. Cause I’m “Bad Attitude” Jesse Ramey.” Jesse continues laughing. “Ya ma’n. You the gay guy of sports entertainment.” Jordan begins to laugh. “Whatever man. It’s been real talking to you, but I’ve gotta go and look around somewhere else.” Jesse says obviously trying to get out of the conversation. “Aight, ma’n. It been cool. Later.” Jordan says as Jesse begins to walk back up towards the cash register. “I can’t believe he had the nerves to say that to me.” Jesse says. Slowly fading into the CD on the counter and finally into darkness. ~The End~
