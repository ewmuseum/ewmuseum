Two animals and a zoo filled with 'em.

2002-04-04

Amy 'Aimz' Campbell

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(I'm in a weird mood today...I'm not entirely sure if that affected this RP or not...hmmmm...) It had been an eventful week, to say the very least... It wasn't over yet. Amy had been in the company of Timo and the manchild Eugene since around 8am when both came stampeding into her room today with all the force of a buffalo herd. Since then, they had proceeded to torment the sleepy redhead much to the Samoan Silencer's delight. That's what you get for waxing a man's leg. All three were sitting inside a mini-van that Timo had rented and, oddly enough, Aimz had been condemned to the back seat in place of Eugene, who's hulking mass had taken up most of the passenger's side up front. Amy, of course, was rather grumpy this time around...Bolamba was taking she and Eugene to the zoo of all places! Going around a bunch of animals with two humans that had the combined brain power of a racoon...that'd certainly be interesting. ''Timo...'' She was playing around with the leather belt that held up a pair of 'Guess?' brand cargo khakis. ''...do we have to go?'' ''Yup...it'll be a nice day out with Eugene.'' The big Samoan smiled and, upon hearing his name, the manchild beside him roared... ''DERRRRR...I SPECIAL!'' Amy smacked herself on the forehead, rubbing her eyes. This was going to be a long afternoon. After minutes of silence in the van, the three eventually reached some sort of public zoo located just outside of the city. As expected, it was dusty, smelly and way too warm. ''Awww man...'' Aimz had to whine at this point, as would any self-respecting female. ''...dude, this place smells like...well...wet dog!'' ''Maybe it's bath time.'' Was Timo's only slight explanation as he guided Eugene, who was prancing a lot rather innocently, inside the gate. Just then, Eugene spotted the first cage...a thirty square foot 'reserve' that housed two red foxes inside of a chain-link fence. ''AMINALS!!!!'' Amy couldn't help but feel bad for the little canines...surprisingly enough, she had a soft spot for animals. That's why she hated zoos and pet shops...too many of the poor things crowded into cramped little cages or glass enclosures. Timo, of course, was more interesting in filling his gut...which is why he was about to abandon his tag partner and the man child to wander to the cotton candy stand. He mumbled, the stand catching his eye, and the stash of saliva behind his bottom gums. ''Be...right...back...'' ''Ooooooh no you don't!!!'' Amy grabbed the Samoan by the sleeve of his button-down red dragon t-shirt. ''You're staying here with the retard, I'm not taking care of him!'' ''You like animals, right?'' ''Yeah...'' ''Think of him as...I dunno...a really ugly horse.'' She sighed, shaking her head. ''You're just......argh!'' ''Heh...'' Timo smirked. ''I knew you'd see things my way...'' Too late to run, Amy had a hold of him again already. ''I'm still not looking after him...he's YOUR headcheese, not mine!'' God, handling Timo was almost as hard as finding a hooker to take Drew Hostile. Aimz was growing even more annoyed than before when, finally, the tandem both simultaneously wondered aloud. ''WHERE'S EUGENE?!'' Uh oh...they had lost the manchild and in these kind of crowds, that couldn't POSSIBLY be healthy for anybody. ''Shit...'' Amy muttered, half-to herself while she rubbed her temples, resting her head back against the fencing to the fox display. ''...he's gonna cause a riot out there alone...'' Bolamba was frantic, for once. ''We've got find him! You start over by the tarantula showcase in the barn...'' Aimz quickly huffed at Timo, using her usual profanity a wee bit too loud and attracting the attention of a few small children and old ladies alike. ''Yeah fucking right!'' ''Oh for the love of...'' The Samoan cut himself off, then rolled his eyes at the near five-foot-eight redhead. ''...what now?'' ''I'm arachnophobic.'' ''Okay...fine...'' He snorted, too occupied with finding the wildcard of a boy. ''... I'll start with the tarantula display...you go over to the panda bears or kittens...the girly stuff or whatever...'' ''I'm gonna have to kick your ass for that later.'' She smiled weakly to the big man, but this was no time for joking. ''So yeah...alright. I'll try to get him back to this spot if I find him and he doesn't kill me first.'' ''Good.'' Was the only thing Bolamba said before taking off towards a large building to the south of where Aimz had been standing. Oh...she'd get to finding Eugene eventually. Wait, NO SHE WOULDN'T! Amy didn't feel like dying at the hands of a gargantuan retard today, or any day for that matter. So what did she do? The obvious, she got out of the zoo...headed back to the car to sit down for a while. God bless Timo for always leaving the keys to these vehicles in the ignition! The short walk back to the car was, thankfully enough, uneventful. When she got inside, however, poor Amy couldn't help but notice the stench of the manchild wafting all through the vehicle from earlier. She made a mental note: Remind Timo to teach Eugene the fine art of a shower. That'd make an ''America's Funniest Home Videos'' moment in itself. Ah...CDs! When Amy rummaged around on the dashboard, she found one of her favorite albums: My Animal by Boy Hits Car... She slid the disc into the stereo, sitting in the driver's seat and as ''Man Without Skin'' started to play, a mellow 12-string acoustic guitar chord progression kicking it off, Amy sat back and let her mind wander over everything... Somehow, someway she was going to let Rent-A-Hero know how she felt about him...but how to go about it? He was so innocent, such a prude that nothing really worked in the terms she used...he just didn't understand anything involving feelings and certain types of attraction, or so it seemed. She remembered trying to tell him back in PWA...but quite honestly, back then she had no other goal than to have him lose his virginity, there wasn't a whole lot of feelings involved until she got fed up and went to live in the home of another so-called man. Jason Dexter had treated her fine for the first little while...but things seemed to go downhill after the general awe of screwing a star wore off. God only knew how she'd end up doing this...Amy had to be very delicate with RAH because, as was said before, he was a very, VERY ignorant man when it came to matters of the heart. She had to teach him everything from here on in...but first, she had to find time to see him again! Oh well, after she took Drew Hostyle out on Slam, she'd go see Rentie...maybe she'd even let him know exactly how she felt! Speaking of taking Drew Hostyle out...that had yet to happen. But Saturday was the night, the Gulfport Arena was the venue and Slam was the broadcast...that's when she'd topple 3B's resident lackey and all around heat-leech. Sure, Tempest could probably do it just as well...but she was busy doing exactly what Amy would have done if the situation warranted it, defending her boyfriend...and getting a World Title shot in the process. Surely the girl who was even smaller than Aimz wouldn't be able to so much as get a two-count on Toomes without the help of the entire Red Army, but it was worth a try...and maybe they could all get some retaliation on the part of their fallen comrade. Amy was, in the way, pulling a thorn from their side by taking Hostyle out of action...he wouldn't be there to bother them when Bones ran in on the World Title match. Of course, it was accidental...but would Amy help the Red Army even if it hadn't been by booking? Tempest, being a woman, was immediate competition for Campbell...but they both had an on-going score to settle with Jane Reagan before they'd even think about fighting each other, as far as Amy was concerned. There might even be a future teaming in the works if they ended up getting along well enough...but as it stood, Aimz and the 'Little Storm' had yet to have spoken directly, or very positively. Unfortunately enough, her thoughts were cut short by a now angry Samoan knocking at the window, closely followed behind by Eugene. ''...Amy!'' She rolled the window down, smiling innocently. ''Ohh...uhhh...Timo...hey man. I see you found Eugene, that's aw---'' ''Yeah, I found Eugene...'' The big man scoffed. ''No thanks to you!'' ''Heh...sorry about that.'' Awww...who could resist that pouty face? ''...I'm lazy, tired and don't like zoos...'' She lowered her tone a moment so it was just out of rang of the manchild. ''...or him, for that matter.'' ''Well you're just gonna have to put up with him unless you can think of any other way for me to beat that Meanstreak basket case!'' ''God, you sound like a mother sometimes...'' Amy chuckled, it was a little different to have Timo be the one nagging and her the one laughing. ''...just chill out, man. Just because I don't like the big jolly retard doesn't mean I'm gonna whine about it ALL the time.'' ''Yeah...only ninety nine point nine percent of the time, right?'' ''Right!'' She smiled. ''Gah...this is impossible.'' ''TALK ABOUT EUGENE! I SPECIAL!!!!'' For no apparent reason at all, the manchild poked his head down from over Timo's shoulder. ''Uhhh...yeah bud, you're special alright.'' Bolamba glanced at his redheaded comrade a moment. ''You might as well stay here unless you feel like eating ice cream with Eugene and I.'' ''Yeah, that's cool...'' She decided to one again lighten the mood, ''...I'd rather go watch the hogs in the farm displays eat their mud.'' ''I GET ICE CREAM!? YAAAAAAY!!!' Timo didn't have time to retaliate against Amy's joke, instead he was forced to chase after Eugene, who was merrily skipping...also known as stomping holes in the ground...on his way to the concession stands outside of the zoo gates.
