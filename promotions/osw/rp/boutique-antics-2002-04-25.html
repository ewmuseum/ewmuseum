Boutique Antics...

25 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25.04.02 “....New York City Coppps....New York City Coppps....They ain’t too smart...” The Strokes battered out a mean solo as Krieg cruised up to the front door of a boutique in his Ford Pickup in the main street of Flint, “Kill me now, ‘cause I’ll let you down....I swear one day I’m gonna leave this town....Stop....” Lumbering as he always does, Krieg steps out of his pickup and onto the pavement, Jeremy climbing out of the back seat at the same time, “It is time to shop Krieg-style,” shouted Krieg as he walked up to the main door of the boutique, subtly named Large and Mighty. Grabbing the door by it’s steel rod handle, Krieg rips it open, just about pulling the glass door off of its hinges, and steps over the threshold on the fancy shop. Suddenly, a petite man comes zipping over to him, his pink scarf blowing away from him ever so slightly as he appears to defy gravity and physics with his sheer speed, “Hi sir. Oh, you are a very big man,” he said, rubbing his hand gently on Krieg’s chest, Looking slightly bewildered, he looks down at the man, a confused look crossing his face, “Who are you? Jesse Ramey,” roared Michel, slapping the assistant’s hand off of his chest, Folding his hands at his waist, the assistant introduces himself, “No, no sir. I am not that stud-muffin. I am Petr. The one and only. The man who fits the biggest stars...and I mean biggest,” he said, his eyes lighting up as he does, “Well you can just keep your camp hands away from me and show me where the suit section is,” “Certain sir. Follow me,” replied Petr, once again scooting away from Krieg and lightning-quick speed, the German wrestler and his cameraman Jeremy trailing behind, Eventually, they reached the other end of the shop, Petr already waiting with his tape measure and pins in hand. Krieg begins to tell Petr what he’s looking for, “Now, I don’t want something too flashy. I don’t want to look like one of these PCW schmucks. No, something smart, but casual. Do you think you can help me, Frenchy,” “We, we, sir. I have the perfect outfit for you,” Saying that, Petr disappears behind a rack of clothes and rummages about in some sort of stockroom. Clipping, ripping and a few squeals come from the boom as Krieg looks at Jeremy and the cameraman does the same. After a minute or two, Petr reappears from behind the clothes, his forehead rather sweaty, holding some sort of garment in his hands. Smiling from ear to ear, Petr winks at Krieg as the German War Machine suddenly latches on to Petr’s intentions. Eyebrows raised, Krieg stares at the ‘suit’ Petr produced for him. Holding it in the air with delight, Petr shows the full thing to the camera... A light Blue pinstripe suit with yellow pinstripes... Oh my god! What the hell is that? It looks like an accident in a paint factory Thought Jeremy, as Krieg looked on is disbelief, “What the hell is that,” shouted Krieg, looking slightly pissed off that Petr’s wasted his time, ”It is a piece of art, Michel. You will grow to love it,” responded the gay Frenchman as he started to put the jacket on Krieg, Shaking his arms violently, Krieg tossed the jacket in Petr’s face before kicking him in the gut and slamming his two forearms over Petr’s shoulders, knocking the gay Frenchman to the ground, “That’s art for you, my French comrade! Next time you want to take the piss out of Krieg, at least come up with a decent gimmick,” Michel roared, pulling some more garments off of the racks and tossing them down on Petr, A couple of the other assistants, who were also visibly gay, came rushing over to Michel and started to slap his chest with their palms, personifying a gay man’s fight. Laughing at them, the 3 gay men stepped back as if they’re surprised by Krieg’s laughter. All of a sudden, the 3 men charge at Krieg as the German just about flattens the three men at once, extending his massive arm and clotheslining the three men ‘out of their boots’, “Dwahhhhahhh! Sit down, you stupid gays! Just like I did to Jesse Ramey, I’ve shown you that there’s no need for gays in this world. And you can tell Petr I don’t want his services when he wakes up,” shouted Michel as he and Jeremy walked out of the store, “I think we’ll leave shopping for the day, huh Jeremy,” “Yeah, I think that’d be a good idea man,” Both men climb back into Michel’s truck as The Strokes kick off again as they pull away from the curb, “....she said, Oh baby, don’t fell so down, oh it turns me off....” The screen starts to fade.... “....Well, I've been in town for just now fifteen minutes now..... And Baby, I feel so down.....” Fade. I think I'll meet Walczak for a drink during SLAM! on Saturday....
