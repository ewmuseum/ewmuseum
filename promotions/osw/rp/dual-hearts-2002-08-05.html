Dual Hearts

5 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/5/02 (Note: This RP was co-written by Teresa Tomas and takes place the same night as Slam!) The life of a wrestler isn't easy, especially a female, and especially in an extreme federation. Both the physical and mental well being of the wrestler is effected in one way or the other, not to mention emotional. For Teresa, it's all of the above. While the fans cheer and watch in awe how a female can compete with men twice her size and return the next week to perform yet again, Teresa is silently suffering her own personal demon. One of the pros to living most of your life on the road and being a celebrity is the luxury of a fine hotel if you have the extra cash. Tonight Teresa is in such a hotel. All the employees are dressed in fine tailored clothing and eager to receive generous tips. Inside, the hotel sparkles with a crystal chandelier hanging from the ceiling in the lobby. A restaurant is downstairs with a large gym and spa placed on the opposite end of the ground floor. The hotel also has three floors of rooms and usually is busy with celebrity and wealthy customers. Teresa's room is on the second floor. She has a view of the back area of the hotel this way. All that can be seen is the large trees blocking the view of the interstate. Tonight Teresa is in her hotel room. The room itself is a bit larger than the average room with larger closet space and an area for a small table and two chairs, suitable for card playing or a small meal. The bed is King size with feather pillows. A 27 inch television is placed in a solid oak entertainment center which doubles as a dresser. It's been a known fact for several months now that Scott Taylor and Teresa Tomas have been a couple, a 'hot item' if you will. No secrets there. The doorknob jiggles and a clicking sound follows. The knob turns again and the door is pushed open. In steps none other than Teresa herself, she is wearing a pair of black denim shorts which stop a few inches above her knees, and a light blue tee shirt. She glances down at the watch on her wrist, it couldn't have been that long since Slam! had ended. '11:05....' The NTICW female mumbles to herself. She turns to look behind her, speaking in a somewhat softer tone of voice than we are used to hearing her in. 'Are you coming in or not?' 'Yeah, I'm coming. You shouldn't walk so fast if you want me to keep up.' The voice is all too familiar, and soon the body to which that voice belongs steps into view. Former NTICW World Champion, now Kid Wonder's mortal enemy, Scott Taylor. Scott is dressed in a pair of loose fitting denim jeans, the cuffs cut off so the hem hangs loosely around his black boot clad feet, and a plain white tee shirt, nothing special about it. His long black hair is pulld back into a ponytail which hangs a good three inches past his shoulders. Covering his slightly beaten up face is a pair of thin framed silver glasses. Over his shoulder he carries a red gymbag. Upon setting foot in the room, Scott uses his right foot to shut the down behind him and then has a look around, taking the room in and inspecting it a little. 'Nice room. I guess those NTICW checks are worth a little more than they were when I was there. Then again, this GOD person might not be as tight as S. was....' 'No, not really. It's about the same pay that Boda gave me. I've just learned how to conserve the money this time around, and use it when it's needed.' Scott nods to Teresa as he steps further into the room, he stops next to the King Sized Bed and sits his bag down on it, and then takes a seat next to it. It's only been a little over an hour since Slam! went off the air and he's still feeling the effects of the tag team match. Who would have thought that Armour could be so powerful? The only thing that Scott could really remember was being launched over Armour's massive shoulders, taking a nearly ten foot dive considering Armour's height and the elevation, and then it all went blank from there. The next thing he knew Randolph was pulling him out of the ring, and he was able to get a glimpe of Seabass kicking ass with a chair in hand. 'Scott?' He is suddenly jolted back into reality as Teresa's right hand is on his shoulder and shaking him. He looks up at her and blinks, shaking his head. He sighs a little and looks up at her while placing his hands on her hips. 'Sorry, I was just thinking about what happened tonight. I nearly got killed by that huge freaking cave beast. Which is exactly why I hired Randolph. He pulled me out of the ring just in time.' Scott says with a hint of pride in his voice. 'Randolph? But wasn't it Toomes who came in with a chair?' Teresa asks with her eyebrows raised. Scott stares at her quizically for a moment, and then shakes his head before responding. 'No, not at all. Randolph was right there doing his job. He saved me from further injury.' Teresa stares down at Scott in disbelief for a moment. Could that middle aged fat man really have saved Scott from Armour? Ofcourse not, she was there so she knows, and so does everyone else who saw Slam!, Scott was only saved because Seabass came to get him a little piece of Armour. Randolph was just at the right place at the right time. But that's not important anymore. 'Well, that's not important Scott. Hey, where is Randolph anyway?' Scott answers with a casual voice and a dismissive wave. 'Downstairs at the Casino, I think he enjoys those OSW paychecks alot more than I do.' Teresa nods and then takes a seat next to Scott on the bed. She leans over towards him and removes the glasses from his face, smiling at him. Scott returns the favor with a small grin of his own. Teresa leans forward, giving Scott a soft yet quick peck on the lips and then pulls away. Before Scott could even respond it was over, ahh well. Lover's Peck it seems. She slides back on the bed and repositions herself behind Scott, her hands move down and she begins to gently massage his shoulders. Scott lets out a groan as his muscles tense up automatically. 'Still feeling that beat up, huh?' 'Yeah, I think whatever that move was that Armour finished me off with hurt like hell. I'm still feeling it. Ow!' Teresa backs away from Scott and shakes her head gently. 'This isn't going to work, not as tense as you are.' Teresa stops to think for a moment, and then an idea comes to her. She stands up from the bed and begins to walk towards the door. 'I'll be right back, I have an idea.' She reaches for the door and pulls it open, however just as she steps out she steps right back. She points at Scott, and then at the bed. 'Which reminds me, you strip down and lay on the bed. No! Not for that, take that smile off your face Scott Adam!' Scott snickers as Teresa scolds him for having dirty thoughts. She even used his first and middle names in the same sentence, back to back! The door closes and Scott does as she says. He crosses his legs and easily unties the boots, letting them fall to the floor. Two loud clunks are heard and then he stands. His arms cross, each hand grabbing the hem of his shirt and then pulling it over head. That top rope splash from Armour really put a beating on him, and the bruises on his chest and stomach are living proof of that. Another groan of pain is heard as Scott slides the denim jeans down his legs and steps out of that, leaving him only in a pair of dark green boxer shorts. 'Guess I better lay down then...this is going to hurt too....' The Baton Rouge-ian mumbles to himself as he lays back on the bed, his body tensing as the fresh beating kicks in again. He reaches out to take one of the fluffy pillows from the bed and then rolls over onto his stomach, propping his head up on the pillow. Though still in pain, the soft bed and pillow do make him relax a little. A soft sigh passes through his light pink lips as the door opens. Even though he knows who it is he looks up anyway out of instinct. 'Made it back that soon?' Standing before him is Teresa, she has returned indeed. This time she isn't alone however. In her arms she is holding various bottles of liquids and oils, most of them scented to a nice fragrance. She smiles ast Scott as she walks towards the bed. 'I took the elevator. Now lay down on your stomach. Hopefully, you'll feel better after this' Teresa climbs on the bed and drops the various bottles beside her. She climbs over Scott straddling his back and placing her weight to her knees so that she doesn't hurt him if she sits on his back. She hums to herself as Scott feels a soft scented oil drizzle down his back. 'What are you doing Teresa? Oh, nevermind. A little to the left. That's it.' Scott closes his eyes feeling Teresa's hands rub against his shoulder blades. He moans softly as the heels of her hands dig into his tense muscles. He has always loved how her hands felt, especially when she gives him massages. The first massage she gave him only lasted about five minutes and he couldn't take it any longer. Needless to say, it was never completed. 'I'm glad you're enjoying this. I'm sure you'll sleep easy tonight. After Mayhem Tuesday, I'm expecting the same if not better treatment.' Teresa says this half jokingly as her hands move down his back a bit. Since both of them are on the road constantly, what little time they do see of each other, they value. In the next couple of days, she has to leave for Virginia for Mayhem while Scott leaves for Massachusetts for Slam! It'll be at least a week or two before they'll see each other again, depending on their bookings. 'Don't worry Baby, I'll be all over you. Who are you wrestling Tuesday?' 'Marcus and I are wrestling P.W.A. I might as well walk into a suicide trap than to team with that old man.' Teresa says rubbing slightly harder. She and Marcus didn't hit it off too well and their hatred for each other has begun. She is reminded of her and Ariz Ona's feud and how similar it is and yet so different. 'If I was still there, I'd take care of that vampire and jester for you. You know I would, but I've moved on now. Besides, you a big girl and I'm sure you'll do fine.' Scott winces feeling Teresa squeeze his sore back muscles even harder. She has stopped massaging and is just applying pressure on his back. Her weight is no longer on her knees, but on Scott's lower back which gives him more pain. She doesn't respond to his comment either. 'Damn it Teresa! What the hell are you doing? That freaking hurts!' Teresa remains silent and doesn't release any pressure. Scott forces himself to roll over and pushes Teresa off him. He sits up and looks to her, in her eyes he sees a glare in her that seems to burn a hole straight through him. She stares straight at him with her fists clinched. Her breaths are short and sharp. 'Teresa? What's wrong? What did I say? Talk to me damnit!' Scott slides off the bed and steps closer to his girlfriend. She hasn't moved. It is as though she is in a trance. 'TERESA!' Scott screams only to receive still no response. This scares him slightly. Determined to get to the bottom of this, he gives Teresa slap to the face in hopes to wake her from this strange trance. Once his hand makes contact, Teresa's face slings to the side and she screams then lunges forward towards him still screaming. After forcing him on the bed, she begins throwing punches to his head, one to his nose and another to his jaw. Scott is defenseless, he has little time to react as the woman's fists slam into his face. 'Get off!' Quickly thinking, Scott uses one of his legs to knee Teresa in the back, she falls forward and off of the bed. Scott quickly spins his body around on the mattress and meets her as she is standing. Teres is still screaming at him and throws another punch, Scott ducks down letting her first fly over his head. He steps forward and uses his weight advantage to push her up against the wall. Teresa screams still and tries to punch Scott again. An open palm from Scott catches her fist in mid-flight, and then he pushes her into the wall rather hard, and then again. Teresa stops fighting, stops moving. She looks down as her eyes roll up into her head. Scott, still holding her arms, looks down into her eyes, at her face. The fact that she violently attacked him is just now starting to set in. He lets go of her and takes a step back, Teresa just leans against the wall. Scott's voice is full of sadness, and quivering slightly as he speaks. '...I'm going now, Teresa......I....I'll see you next week....maybe.....' Scott continues to back away a few more feet, he then turns on his heels and walks towards the bed. He only pulls his jeans back in and tosses the rest of his clothes into the gymbag, he slings the gymbag over his shoulder and then heads towards the door. The door opens and then closes, nothing more and nothing less. Somewhere, in a lone hotel room in Pennsylvania, two hearts have just been shattered. Fin~
