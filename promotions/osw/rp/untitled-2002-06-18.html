Untitled roleplay

18 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 18th 2002 NOTE: The following is a joint roleplay between GV and Rhett Locke. Conversion, software version 7.0 Looking at life through the eyes of a tired hub Eating seeds as a past time activity The toxicity of our city, of our city New, what do you own the world? How do you own disorder, disorder? Now, somewhere between the sacred silence Sacred silence and sleep... Somewhere between the sacred silence and sleep Disorder, Disorder, Disorder -'Toxicity' System of a down. Vagrant. Desperado. Vagabond. Nomad. I am Jack. Jack-of-all trades. I am homeless. I am Nature's irony. Loser. Failure. Defeated. Conquored. I am slain. Slain by the monster. I am beaten. I am awake. I am Rhett. Homeless, overthrown....Rhett. Brilliant. Fucking...brilliant... Way to go Monster, you tricked me...you pulled one over my already closed eyes. You are great. You are smart. You are genious. You are fucking stupid. I hope you die. I hope you rot in Hell. I will make you rot in hell. 'God damnit....GET OUT OF THE ROAD, JACKASS!' A small case of road rage. We all have it once in a while, you know....when you're so...fucking...traumatized...you just want to fucking cause a little chaos. You know...have a little fun. I'm a driving lunatic to the people on the road, but to me I'm just letting go of a little steam. Vagrant. Desperado. Vagabond. Nomad. I am Jack. Jack-of-all trades. I am homeless. I am Nature's irony. I am driving. I am Rhett... **** Dumb mother fucker. That is exactly what you are Rhett. A dumb mother fucker. From the very beginning I’ve been playing the game with you. And I’m the master of games Rhett. I never lose and I never will. You thought that by sticking me in a twenty one day program at the Asylum would keep me there for good, but that wasn’t the case now was it? That’s right Rhett. I got released. I played the game better than you. You didn’t know how to react when the doctor told you I was actually normal. That I was off to a speedy recovery. I got your number Rhett. I’ve had your number since the day I stepped foot into OSW. You just never realized it. Last week you lost. Not surprising. I couldn’t be at the show, but that’s why they invented telephones Rhett. One phone call and a little money can go a long way. You’re easily distracted, and that’s all it took for you to lose. I’m out Rhett. I’m a free man. So that means I’ll be at Slam! next week. And that also means I’ll be at the OSW PPV the following day. You know what that means for you Rhett? Heh, I don’t want to give away all the fun just yet.
