A Promotional Video

1 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-1-02 “I think it’s every wrestler’s dream to be where we’re at today.” Trevor Hawke’s voice is heard, talking over a shot of an arena jam-packed full of OSW fans filling the screen. On their feet, cheering, the camera scans over the entirety of the crowd. One would estimate no less than thirty thousand fans had amassed in the arena, and judging by the look of things, they are thirty thousand fans having the time of their lives. On the edge of their seats. Screaming their hearts out. Feeling alive. We segue to a shot of Trevor Hawke, his head against a black background. “No, I know it’s every wrestler’s dream. Going into a pay-per-view like By Any Means Necessary with the tag team titles on the line, performing in front of an audience of millions...there’s no other feeling like that in the world.” A shot of Seth Masters replaces the one of Trevor, his face on the opposite side of screen against the same black background. “And to be in a match of this magnitude with opponents of the caliber we’re up against, the scope of it all is almost too much for me to handle. NC-17, John Mammoth, PHD...just months ago, I couldn’t even fathom stepping into the ring with Hank Gunyon, and now I get to compete with these four men. As much as I loathe each and every one of them, it’s still awe-inspiring for me just to think about stepping into the ring with them. It’s like the world’s been turned upside down. In a good way.” Video clip after video clip begins to play. Graphic Violence hitting his Unsuitable For Children reverse DDT on Rhett Locke at Ascension. John Mammoth chokeslamming Clay Vendetta from the ring to the arena floor. PHD, a sadistic grin on his face, with the Vulcan Death Grip locked onto Amy Campbell. NC-17 completely decimating Redemption Song with the steel ring steps. Finally the shot segues back to Trevor. “We’ve been here before. Big match. Titles on the line. This is the kind of shit we live for. The pressure being on, our goal within reaching distance. When that adrenaline starts pumping, that’s when you know you’re truly alive. And tomorrow night, we live again.” Video clips of Civil Disobedience in action begin to grace the screen. Trevor Hawke flying off the top of a ladder to take down Azrael Cain. Seth Masters hitting a German suplex on PHD as Hawke flies over the ropes to catch Dorkiman with a legdrop. The two members of Civil Disobedience hitting the Trendsetter on Drew Hostyle. The last clip finishes as Seth Master’s face comes back onto the screen. “It seems like just yesterday when we were screwed out of our belts the first time. Yes, I say our belts, because they should have never left our waists to begin with. We were the first champs, the best champs, if there is any justice in the world we’d still be champs, and we’ll be champs again after tomorrow night. Mark my word.” Clips of Trevor Hawke clutching the OSW tag title belts, laying in the wreckage of the cage at Crowning Glory. Him and Seth holding the belts tightly in celebration of becoming the first champions ever. Shots of the following weeks then follow, showing Civil Disobedience walking down to the ring with the belts around their waists, talking to Shanda Russell with the gold over their shoulders, signing autographs with the straps sitting on the table. As this montage ceases, Trevor’s face again appears on the screen. “This is a big match for us. Not just because it’s for the titles...hell, this is the third pay-per-view out of four that we’re contending for those belts. No, this is a big one because we have the chance to really prove something out there. That we’re not just gimmicky bastards getting pushed on entertainment value. We worked our way through this tournament. We beat the Westsidaz. We beat Porter and Armour. We earned this. The world already knows that we’re goddamn talented, and on Sunday night, we mean to prove it once again. Why? Because that’s what we do each and every time we step in that ring.” We see clips of Civil Disobedience’s qualifying matches that led them to their spot in the tournament finals. Seth Masters hitting his Primo Plex fisherman buster on Short Circuit and then pinning him for the three. Both suburbanites whipping Howard Porter into the ropes and nailing him with the Trendsetter double flatliner on the rebound. Civil Disobedience standing in the middle of the ring, their arms raised in victory, expressions of absolute confidence painted across their faces. This fades to a shot of Seth Masters once more, his head still against the black background. “Of course, there’s more to this than just friendly competition. Wrestling is hardly ever taken at its face value. There’s always so many deeper levels to sift through. This match definitely has personal aspects to it. I don’t think we’ve made any secret of the fact that we hold feelings of hatred toward anyone in the Think Tank, maybe more so as far as Dorkiman and Mammoth are concerned. Laying us out on two non-consecutive Slams will cause those types of feelings.” Clips of Mammoth’s arrival in OSW are shown. The futile attempts of Civil Disobedience to bring down the monster. A big chokeslam on Trevor Hawke. The tossing of Seth Masters into the third row of seats. Shots just taken yesterday of Mammoth laying both men out with a double chokeslam on the concrete of Philadelphia State Park. Seth’s voice is heard talking over these clips near the end. “So of course that’s some added incentive. Revenge always is, isn’t it?” Now the shot segues to Trevor Hawke once more, a solemn expression upon his face. “Here we are once again, off to do battle, with the fate of our careers and the future of Old School Wrestling on the line. An epic backdrop cast behind a harsh reality. Us, defending what’s right, as we take on the maniacal Think Tank and the embodiment of viciousness, NC-17. Good versus evil versus evil. I never really thought of us as defenders of justice. Hell, six months ago, I would have considered us the farthest thing from it. I still do, in fact. Yet that’s the stereotypical role we’ve been cast in... “Personally, I could care less about “playing the face” or whatever shit those smarks like to toss out of their anal-shaped mouths. This match is about us getting what we deserve...” Trevor raises his index finger, counting it off. “Respect.” Trevor now raises his first two fingers in the air. “Vengeance.” Three fingers now take the place of the two. “And our OSW Tag Team titles.” A small grin turns up the corner of Trevor’s mouth as he focuses his expression on the camera. “We’ve been here before and we’ll be here again, and this time, just like all the others, we’ll do whatever it takes to meet our goals. The much disputed name of this pay-per-view is quite fitting, really, in that respect...because come match time, me and Seth...we’re going to win this thing. By any means necessary...” The scene fades to black, Trevor’s expression dimming and then disappearing. Suddenly a shot of NC-17 flashes on the left of the screen, then disappears. A shot of Civil Disobedience flashes on the opposite side of the screen, then disappears. A shot of PHD standing in front of Mammoth then flashes in the center of the screen. The images of the other two teams fade in on either side of the Think Tankers, as the graphics of the OSW Tag Team titles are displayed underneath them. Underneath the three teams the following words are displayed. Civil Disobedience vs. NC-17 vs. PHD and John Mammoth for the OSW Tag Team Championship...live on pay-per-view.
