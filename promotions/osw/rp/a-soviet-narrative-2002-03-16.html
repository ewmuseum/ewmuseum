A Soviet Narrative

16 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-16-02 For forty years, he has been fighting. Forty long years, Ivan Stanislav has lived in a world in which the west was the enemy, and his Motherland along with the satellite countries around his beloved land, were the allies. It is hard to imagine exactly what sort of a reality Ivan Stanislav lives in. For while he appears to be a very practical and intense individual, he lives in a world that most people nowadays cannot even understand. He is a man that is before his time. A life in which equality is always a driving force, where the collectivization of the people, economy, agriculture, industry, and all other facets of life seem to always be a constant factor in his mind. A world of old that we of the present cannot understand. But more importantly, or devastatingly perhaps, it is a world that day by day, is being forgotten. The children today as they learn their math, their spelling, their language skills in school, continue to learn the same sort of information that we, and those before us, have always learned, and we learn it for the future. However, for Ivan Stanislav, the very drive of movement that is his life is not the drive for virtually anyone else in our contemporary world. And for that, I mourn for Ivan Sergeiovich, for it is in my mind that he is a true hero, regardless what anyone else would dare to say. Some of his superiors scoff at him and believe he is a funny man that is unable to let go of the past, but I do not. I do not think he is funny, I believe he is almost…sympathetic, in a strange and uncharacteristic way. He is an honorable man. He is a devoted man. And so, here he is, fighting in the United States as a mighty wrestler, his third love only to the Party and the State, which in his mind should be one and the same. And here, in the United States of America, he has seen some of his vision come to life, at least in a very limited sense of the idea. The acquisition of friends from his old Communist Bloc states, the winning of titles, the collectivization of a championship, it truly is a great thing for Ivan Stanislav, regardless what anyone will say. And those that are against him, they believe he is some tyrant, some monster that is hell bent on creating something that only he will be able to control, and all those under him will be subservient. To these individuals I tell them that they are completely wrong. For Ivan Stanislav is a patron to the people that are willing to hear him. Look at those around him that has accepted his cause! Men like Walczak, Krieg, Riggs, Wang, and others have all flourished because of what Ivan Stanislav has done to help them, and yet others condemn him. These people fear something that they do not understand. They believe that communism is in the very primitive sense: Something Bad. And what creates that belief? I can tell you. Years of Ameri-Capitalist fear that they cannot understand. From short-sighted men telling them for years that no matter what a communist is something evil. And I can assure you that the men that sowed these beliefs into the heads of others, are no smarter than their contemporary arguers. Within the OSW, a seemingly chaotic war is about to erupt. Between men from the inner city streets to men that believe they are divine creatures. Political miscreants and rebels and renegades. But ultimately, in the forefront, we see Capitalists and Communists fighting for ultimate power. After all, that is the mantra under which Ivan Stanislav entered this federation, correct? Ed does not understand what he is doing and he is paying dearly for his miscalculated beliefs. He thought that bringing in Meanstreak would bring an end to the battle from which we are fighting, but instead Meanstreak has been redirected by his twisted mind towards his own self indulgence, which in the purest sense is another capitalist tendency. And throughout this turmoil, and the turmoil that we will see brewing, one force stands united, strong, and undaunted. The Red Army, the product of what Ivan Stanislav has believed in. There is only one stable force within the OSW, the communists. All others are fighting for their self betterment, a capitalist belief as well. I can tell you that it gives Ivan great pride to see his men fighting and winning. It shows that his life has not been wasted. For sometimes it appears to him that the reasons for which he fights are for naught. But it is these men that give him the want and the drive to continue on. What would we have foreseen when Ivan Stanislav entered into the OSW? We knew he would be dominant. We knew that the OSW would have to try to do something in order to combat the might of The Russian Bear, and we knew that certain individuals would gravitate towards Ivan, and others would gravitate away, or be propelled away in most extreme circumstances. Tempest, that is an interesting thought. A funny little woman that seems to have constant happiness bursting from her in the face of extreme danger. Something that Ivan has never seen, and it does confuse him so. We have watched Ivan deal with this alien entity in the form of Tempest, for that is exactly what she is. She is something that seems to accept Ivan, but accept Ivan from a standpoint that he could never have imagined. She is from America. Sure, she was born in Hong Kong, but ultimately she is an American. An American accepting Ivan Stanislav? Unheard of, unbelievable. But it appears to be so. Intelligence has been kept on her, and her past is indeed a tragedy, the product of anarchists against the great communist country of China. But she survived, and who would expect that these two would run into each other and coexist, if that is what you wish to call it. Many interesting things are going on within the life of Ivan Stanislav, more things than Ivan would probably prefer to have occurring. The rigid and set manner of which he lives his life is speeding up, perhaps his age is catching up with him, or perhaps it would be better to say that ‘time’ is catching up with Ivan Stanislav. Praporschik Stanislav will always be a Communist, that is no question. He will always fight for what he has always believed in. One must understand that Ivan Stanislav is not only a Communist, but he is a Russian first, something that some people seem to always forget. For forty years, Ivan Stanislav has been fighting. He will fight for one hundred more years if need be. When Ivan Stanislav is unable to wrestle inside a wrestling ring and is no longer capable of keeping his duties up within the Russian Army, he will be as much of a fighter then as he is now. That makes me feel secure, knowing that there will always be the mighty driving force of Ivan Stanislav within the land of Russia. And currently, what will happen is a mystery. Jane Reagan, Ed, and Doug Troy are all key players in this massive war that Stanislav has waged. And what part does Tempest play within all of this? Is it for good? Or is it for bad? For once I can assure you that I do not know, but sooner or later, we all will know for sure and in the end, Ivan Stanislav will be victorious. There has never been any other option, and there never will be other option. Hail Mother Russia! Hail the Party! Hail Ivan Stanislav! And woe to those that try to get in his way!! FSB Director of International Affairs Alexei Gregorovich Ruslan
