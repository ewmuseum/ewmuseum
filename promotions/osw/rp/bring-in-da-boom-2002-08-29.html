Bring in DA BOOM!

29 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 29th, 2002 KA BOOM! 'Lord Jesus in the heavens above!' Mike Extreme exclaimed, watching the little penguin crash over a small vase. The glass particles littered the floor as the furry little bird waddled into the bathroom where more collisions were inevitable. Exreme grabbed a broom and tried to shove most of the exposed glass into a corner. 'I swear, I'm going to kill Tom for getting that little piece of...' CRASH! 'My mirror!' Exreme shouted in horror, as the shards of the former full length mirror appeared from the doorway. 'That's it, no more Mr. Nice Guy.' He took the broom in his hands and stalked into the bathroom, finding the little penguin sitting cutely on the toilet. It looked up at Mike and simply made the sound, 'HONK HONK!' 'Don't try to stop me,' Extreme said to the penguin. 'I know you may be some innocent little bird, but the fact of the matter is...' He paused while he said it, '...YOU'RE LIKE THE DEVIL IN A PENGUINS BODY! DYYYYYYARRRRGH!' He lifts the broom over his head to slam it on to the poor bird, whose eyes have widened in absolute fear. He is paralyzed in shock and cannot move... '...NO!' Tom Lexian grabs the broom from behind and takes it away from Mike, who spins around in fury. 'Tom, what are you doing?' He growled angrily. 'Stopping you from making a big mistake,' Lexian replied, placing the broom behind his back. 'Now, calm down.' As his back was turned, the little penguin scurried underneath Extreme's legs and past Lexian. Mike desperately tried to stomp the cute bird into a pancake. STAMP! STAMP! STOMP! STOMP! 'DYYYYYYYYYYARGH!' Extreme cried out in frustration. He sat down at the toilet seat and just stared at the wall. Lexian just shook his head, dropping the broom to the ground. 'Mike, it's over.' 'You're right,' He answered back. 'My career...it's over. I mean, look at us. We're fighting over a penguin.' He stared at the shattered glass on the floor, seeing only a fragment of his former self. 'We used to fight for championships. Now I feel like I'm in a playhouse.' 'Look, I know the little guy can be a handful...' 'It's not only that,' Extreme interrupted. 'I mean, what are we doing with our careers? Why are we up against jobbers like Disorderly Conduct? Have we sunk to an all new low?' Lexian shrugged. He knew that the Extreme Legends, while having a storied past, would be subject to a scrutiny few teams would ever understand. A loss to NC-17 brought them further down the rung of success then they had anticipated. 'I don't know what to tell you, Mike. But I do know that we can go out there and do what we do best.' Mike looked up and smirked. 'Tom, you somehow turned a monster into a penguin babysitter. Now you expect me to go out there and destroy teams on a whim?' Lexian smiled. 'You'll never lose your flavor and touch for the dramatics. Besides, dropping Disorderly Conduct on their head should be good fun for you.' Lexian reached out his hand and shook Mike's, who let himself laugh. 'Maybe things aren't so bleak.' CRASH! BOOM! BOOM! Lexian looked into the living room and returned with a concerned face. 'Say, did you really like that picture of Billy Joel on your wall?' Extreme slapped himself. 'I say we call him Boomer,' Lexian said nodding his head. 'It fits perfectly.' Yeah, just perfectly.
