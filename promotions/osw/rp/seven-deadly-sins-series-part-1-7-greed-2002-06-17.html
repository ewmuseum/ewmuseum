'Seven Deadly Sins' series;Part 1/7; 'Greed'

17 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17th June 2002 Scene i A bunch of school children are huddled round in a group as the soon brightens there blonde, ginger, brown and black locks with rays engulfed with magnified dust floating in the air. They are all entranced with the tales that are being told them by the man sitting at the front with a book - 'The Good Book' - The Bible. It has gold plating on the back and the front and leather cover underneath that. But reading from it is the stubby fingers, the grey suit and the shiny leather shoes of Eric Sagar. He has reading glasses on and telling the children who are all sat crossed legged on the floor as Sagar leans in to preach his bible readings. '…The stalk's beak got stuck…' Sagar is interrupted by an enthusiastic young boy wearing grey shorts, a white polo neck T-shirt and green and grey stripped suspenders who jumps to his feet and raises his hand. '…Yes Jimmy?' sighs Sagar as if it had been going on all day.. 'Mr. Sagar Sir. What's a stalk?' Jimmy enthusiastically queries 'A bird, So then…' Sagar is interrupted again. 'What kind of bird?' Jimmy asks again. 'A tall one.' Sagar growls without much care. 'What colour are they?' He asks with what we now see as mock enthusiasm, he's just asking questions for the sake of it - or perhaps he's an attention seeker. Or maybe he's just 'White.' Sagar blurts out trying to move on. 'Any d… d… d…' Jimmy stutters. 'Distinguishable.' A boy behind Jimmy whispers into his ear. 'Any distinguishable features?' Jimmy says now reassured by the whispering of his classmate. 'They have long beaks.' Sagar sighs. 'What's that smell of wee?' Jimmy sniffs. 'Oh when an imp urinated on my bible.' 'Ewwwww…' The children express their disgust. 'Oh… Ugh…Ugh…' with each 'Ugh' Sagar sits on the edge of his seat in anticipation for the next question ready to 'hit it out of the park.' 'Yes?' Sagar encourages the youth to express his question. 'Oh… Ugh… Nothing.' The boy shrugs, then smiles. 'So where was I?' Sagar groans. There is no reaction from the sea of blank faces. 'Oh I remember…' Sagar is then drowned out by the sound of a bell. 'Recess!' the class joyfully declares in unison. They all run out of the class room barging through the door and within two seconds, before Sagar can close the bible. 'Oh well…' Sagar's bible closes with a thud then he slowly ascends to his feet. 'Mr Sagar…' an attractive young teacher runs in with her large breasts bouncing and her brunette hair with a tinted red shades in. 'I have to go urgently my brother Chad has come down with something. Can you be the gates of hell keeper today?' 'Gates of Hell Keeper?' Sagar gasps. 'Oh a newbie, I mean Recess duty.' The teacher informs him. 'Well I really…' Sagar is interrupted. 'Thanks. You’re a saint!' The teacher thanks Sagar. 'Hey that is blasphemy! I am not a saint but I shall…' before he can finish the teacher has gone he just stops. 'Bah. Oh well.' He growls. 'No one ever takes time out of their day to praise Jesus anymore.' He mumbles to himself while going to his duties as the scene fades to black Scene ii The scene fades back to see Eric Sagar overlooking a screaming, cheering, laughing, playing etc. of children in the yard. Sagar is looking up at the greyish sky which is clashing with the white balls of fluff that is clouds. Then his day dream is quickly disturbed by a little girl tugging on the sleeve of his top. 'Mr. Sergiar, sir.' The little girl refers to him. 'It's Sagar.' He growls. 'Bobby and Timmy are having a fight.' Her high pitched voice informs Sagar with much urgency. 'I'm coming.' Sagar groans, as the little girl leads him by his sleeve until they meet a pair of scrapping boys. One a small geeky boy with curly ginger hair and glasses that are together with the aid of thick duck tape and a boy twice his size 'Ya' little poo head!' screams the little boy. 'Come here and say that too my face!' yells the little boy. 'STOP!' Yells Sagar who makes the unaware youths jump. 'WHAT HAS BEEN GOING ON!?' Roars Sagar in his trademark fashion. 'He stole my lunch money!' whines the smaller boy who is on the verge of tears thanks to the shouting teacher. 'GIVE IT HIM BACK NOW!' Sagar screams as the taller boy quickly hands the smaller boy his money. 'NOW…' Sagar has gone red in the face he takes a few steps away onto a log in the middle of the yard and stands on it overlooking the playground. 'ALL OF YOU STOP BEING SO SELFISH AND GREEDY! THIS BOY HERE TOOK THIS OTHERS LUNCH MONEY OUT OF GREED! YET YOU ARE ALL CULPRITS! EACH AND EVERY SINGLE ONE OF YOU - EVEN YOUR TEACHERS THINK ABOUT THEMSELVES! NEVER THE IMMORTAL BEINGS! THE SON, THE FATHER AND THE HOLY SPIRIT!' He has silence the whole school, even those inside who are hanging out of their windows staring at him… Sagar grins 'A-MEN!' !~The End~!
