Voices of the Past

6 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-6-02 For all that he had done on Saturday Slam, for all the destruction that he had caused, still something lurked inside of him, yearning for release. The violent assault on Aimz, of whom he swore was a collaborator. The arguments with El Drago and the threats that he had delivered. The plotting with Ruslan about Reno Davis. The explosive statements directed to the people of whom he had claimed to have turned away from him when he needed them most… And that was just the surface level. One conscript in particular, an able bodied man that perhaps did not deserve the treatment he had received, now laid in a hospital bed while they tried to keep his nose in place and fuse his jaw back together. Another soldier suffered head trauma from a wild backhand. And a third had suffered a broken collarbone from a slap on the back that was intentionally too hard. Equipment at the base had been ravaged by his raging tendencies. His office had been dismantled once as he thrashed about, leaving on his desk in tact. And for all for of these changes, the man named Ivan Stanislav had not come to terms with the violent subtraction of a soul in his life. Perhaps it was because for the first time, he had come into true contact with another soul and had enjoyed it, only to have it torn away from him so quickly. Perhaps it was the sudden feeling of great loss that was not associated with anything else in his life except for himself. He knew that it was a long flight back to New Jersey. Back to a place that he had once considered to be his second ‘home.’ A home that was outside of his Motherland and actually existed in the United States of America, of all places. It took him a long time to consider what he was going to do. Even after the violent rage at Slam, he sat in his locker room for a long period of time and stared at the floor. Ruslan had tried to rouse him and get a response out of him, but The Russian Bear did not wish to talk about what he was thinking. It would take a lot of courage to go back to New Jersey. It would be walking into a confrontation of which he may not be prepared to fight. It would mean unearthing those feelings that he had still left rooted deep within his being. It would mean facing something that he truly feared. But there was another factor that was present as well: Hope A hope that perhaps the confrontation would not occur. That perhaps things would correct themselves when he saw her and they would revert back to how they were before that fateful night. That fateful night that he could not even attempt to think about. And with that feeling of hope Stanislav went to the first airport that he could find in Canada and got a ticket to go to New Jersey. To the apartment of Tempest. When he arrived outsides of the apartment complex, Stanislav was petrified. The people as they walked by, the ones that pointed and stared at the hulk that gazed up at the building, did not bother him. His mind was consumed with only one though. Only one want. He entered the building and headed up the staircase. The many flights of stairs were a decent consolation to taking the elevator. If anyone else were to get in it with him he’d violate the weight limit and that only caused more problems than was necessary. And as he climbed the stairs towards the floor in which her apartment was he felt an anxiety that he could barely understand. He began to sweat and his knees weakened. It was as if someone had put a sleeper hold on him and was shutting off his brain. He actually stopped at one landing and leaned against the wall and closed his eyes. Thoughts flooded his mind as he rested his head against the cold concrete wall. Half of him screamed to continue to walk up the stairs. The other half told him to turn and head back to the airport and return to the Motherland. He grabbed the side of his head and breathed heavily as he struggled to regain his mind. After a minute of inward fighting, Stanislav pushed himself off of the wall and began the perilous journey up the stairs again. His face was now completely red as his breathing had gotten heavier. Not from the stairs, but from the pain he was enduring. He finally made it to the floor and he stared at the door that would lead to him to her room. He stared at the paint-peeled door and reached for the handle and paused. His hand was shaking. He stared at his hand as it shook before him and closed his eyes and opened the door. With each ponderous step he neared the door that was the entrance to Tempests’ home. He stopped suddenly in front of her door and closed his eyes. He was going to do it. He was going to talk to her after what she had said to him. He raised his hand to the door and knocked once as the door slowly creaked open. “T…Tempest?” questioned Ivan in a hoarse voice as the door creaked open fully, revealing Tempests’ room… …or what was left of it. Stanislav walked into the room, completely stunned as he noticed what appalled him. Gone were the feather boas and the bright shiny lamps. Gone were the slippers and the exotic plants. Replaced were bare walls, baseboards, sawdust, and silence. The silence was the most painful of it all. The deadly silence of a room that had once bustled with such energy pierced Stanislav as he stared in horror. He rushed into the kitchen and looked around as a voice in his head that sounded strangely like hers echoed through his mind…. 'It's not polite to look in a single woman's kitchen.' “DYAA!!” Ivan smacked the side of his head and staggered back out into the dining room. He shook his head as the voice echoed in his head and faded away. He blinked and whirled around the room… “Can't you see I'm willing to stick around when things aren't at their best?” “HYAAAA!!!” Ivan screamed as her voice echoed into the consciousness of his mind again. “NO!! NO I CANNOT!!?!” He grabbed at his forehead and backed into a wall, cracking it. “Yeah, I mean, I can accept if you know it was wrong to do.” “Uuhhhh………” Ivan groaned as the memories of her words pierced his mind. “I’m sorry Tempest!!!” He fell to his knees with a thunderous SLAM. “I’m sorry…” 'Apology accepted. But there's a little more to making up. Holding me is very important.' Ivan held his hands over his face and shook in the center of the room, mumbling to himself, “I cannot….I…I….” ‘As long as you understand that I'm on your side, no matter what, then it's ok.' He leaned forward until his hands, which were covering his face, touched the floor. “No…you..you are not…you…you are not on my side….YOU LEFT ME!!!! WHEN I NEEDED YOU YOU LEFT MEEEEE!!!!” Ivan roared into his hands, shaking the floor with his thunderous voice. He shivered as the silence filled the room, only his quickened breathing could be heard…. “I….I wanted to…I wanted to help you on that night….I…” Ivan stammered into his hands….. “You should have thought of that before you lied to me! How am I supposed to trust you now?” “Just remember that you don't have to be anyone for me but yourself, ok?” “You're great to me Ivan.” He couldn’t remember how long he had laid there on the floor shivering as his mind raced with her words of the past. But slowly he looked up from his hands and across the room he saw only on piece of furniture left… ….a cracked, full length mirror. He got to his knees and then to his feet and walked over to the mirror and saw his reflection, fragmented, split, torn and irregular. He stared at it and looked around the room… The hope that had gotten him here…the hope of a fight that would not occur…held fast. She was not here. She had moved on. She had left him and would never return. He turned back to the mirror and stared at it again and then slowly grabbed the upper edge and threw it to the ground, bursting it into thousands of little shards of glass. Ivan Stanislav turned and looked around the room as the silence enveloped him…. …and then walked out.
