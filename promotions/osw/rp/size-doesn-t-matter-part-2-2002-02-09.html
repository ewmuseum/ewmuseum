Size Doesn't Matter Part 2

9 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-9-02 As the scene fades in, we are met with the sight of Seth Masters, who is sitting in what is most likely his bedroom. How do we know this? Well, the abundance of identical Cincinnati Reds baseball caps sitting on the dresser lining the west wall is the big clue, and the spray paint covered north wall proclaiming ‘Seth Masters Rulz’ only reinforces the notion. The rest of the room is in the typical state of disarray you would expect from Seth’s dwelling area. The floor is covered in a layer of dirty clothing, totally obstructing the actual carpeting from view. On the right of the dresser lies the small alcove that functions as a closet, which is currently running low on clothes and high on pornography magazines by the looks of it, as a stack of them about three feet high rises up from the floor. The focal point of any bedroom, which is of course the bed, lies along the west wall, facing a small TV set that is perched on yet another pile of pornography magazines. Seth himself is presently sitting on the edge of the bed, with a telephone pressed up to his ear. Wearing the same clothes he wore earlier that day, he looks kind of tired, with bags hanging deep under his bloodshot eyes along with his slumping posture. He still manages to show persistence in spite of this. Glancing down on the phone’s keypad, he maneuvers his index finger deftly, quickly typing in the seven numbers before waiting for the other party to pick up their ringing phone. Ring Ring Ring!!! Ring Ring Ring!!! Ring Ring Ri...CLICK! “Hello?” answers a female voice on the other side. “Hello?” Seth breathes in and out into the phone heavily, not saying anything. The girl on the other end begins to get angry. “Listen, you sick fuck! I don’t have to put up with this shit, you know. I have caller ID...Trevor Hawke! I can go to the police with this.” “Whoa, hold on babe. I was just messing with you,” Seth breaks in. “This isn’t Trevor Hawke, it’s me, Seth. Remember? Wait, what am I saying? How could you forget a studly specimen such as myself?” Silence is heard on the other end, followed by a simple, “Who?” “Seth Masters,” Seth repeats. “You know, the man who rocked your world back in our Junior year. The guy you fell so totally in love with, only to be spurned when you caught me with another woman the night of the prom.” “Er, I don’t remember any of that,” the woman responds, sounding confused. Seth sounds offended that the woman does not remember him. “How could you not remem...oh wait, nevermind,” says Seth, as a look of understanding comes over his face. “Now that I think about it, I got a few things mixed up. What actually happened was I stared at your rack every day in history class and you never even talked to me. The rest of the stuff...well I guess I just made it up.” “Oh!” comes a knowing exclamation from over the line. “Yeah, I think I remember you. Boy, you were quite the spaz back then, weren’t you. Heh heh heh heh heh heh heh.” Seth raises his voice. “Anyways, I was wondering if you remembered Jason Anderson’s Halloween party. The one five years ago.” “What about it?” she asks. “Actually, I’d be surprised if you did, as you were rather intoxicated at the time, but by any chance do you remember the guy you performed a bit of sucky-sucky on?” questions Seth hopefully. “You’re going to have to be a bit more specific than that, hon. I was a bit wild back in those days, if you know what I mean.” she says in a ditzy tone. “The guy was dressed as Spawn,” Seth informs her, drumming his fingers impatiently. “Who?” she asks again. “You know, the comic book character created by Todd McFarlane for Image Comics who was killed, went to hell, was drafted to lead Satan’s armies in exchange for his return to Earth, and then turned against the dark lord.” explains Seth matter-of-factly. “Um...” “He was in a black body suit with a large red cape,” Seth states, beginning to sound frustrated. “Ah, I know who you’re talking about, now. You mean the guy with the small dick.” “It’s not that small!” shouts Seth, offended. “Wait, wait. That was you? Ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha!” Seth pulls the phone away from his ear, but the laughter can still be heard coming from the earpiece. His face turns a bit red as he lifts the phone back up to his head again. “What are you laughing at?” demands Seth. “Ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha. Nothing...Pee Wee...Ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha!” “I don’t think it’s that funny,” Seth interjects. “Ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha!” “You can stop now,” Seth says, a bit louder. “Ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha!” “You told me size doesn’t matter, you bitch!” Seth screams, as he slams the phone back down on the hook in a fit of rage. With the phone down, only silence fills the air. Well, not exactly, as if you’re quiet enough, you can still hear the woman Seth was on the phone with laughing her ass off in the distance. Did I mention she lived two towns over. Because she does. Now that’s a loud laugh, if you ask me. Seth makes his way out of his room and opens the front door from the foyer, leaning out and screaming obscenities in the woman’s general direction. After about five minutes of this, a policeman arrives at his door and warns him that disturbing the peace is against the law, so Seth is forced to withdraw back into his room once more. Picking a pen and a pad of paper up off of the dresser, Seth lays back down on his bed and looks over it. On the top line he has written Brick-like Chicks, or Women I’ve Laid. Underneath this, about thirteen names are listed, the first twelve with a line running through them. The thirteenth one joins the other twelve, as Seth takes the pen and crosses it out as well. Taking in the entire list, he begins to shake in anger, then chucks the pad into the wall, where it falls to join the rest of the cluttered mass that had been chucked by Seth in the past. Standing up, Seth begins to pace the floor. “Damn it!” he shouts to no one in particular. “Out of all the women I’ve pleasured over the years, not a damn one has meant it when she told me size doesn’t matter. Those lying bitches! How dare they delude me. I could have had penile implants five times over by now!” As Seth rants to himself, he realizes that he’s not pissed off about his lack of penile implants. No, what he’s really mad about is the fact that as of yet he has found no backing for his claim that size doesn’t matter. In fact, most of his evidence has been pointing in the opposite direction. The bigger the better seems to be the sentiment that the majority has been voicing, at least as far as the people he has questioned are concerned. Of course, he might have received better results had he not asked a bunch of slutty women who just dun love the cock, but what’s been done has been done, and Seth is starting to feel a bit doubtful of his original stance. Maybe size does matter after all. If that is so, he’s going to have quite a hard time this Saturday when he comes face to face to Azrael Cain. And Civil Disobedience is going to have an even harder time struggling to survive in a tag division containing a team the likes of Heresian Bloodlust. Maybe Trevor was right when he said they didn’t stand a chance. “No!” screams Seth, as he drives his fist into the wall, while angry thoughts rush through his head. He couldn’t afford to lose on Saturday in any way, shape, or form. It was a must win situation for him. Hessian and Cain had all the momentum on their side from last weeks massacre. A win over him would only add more fuel to their fire; a fire already too big and dangerous to be allowed to sit. They would become even more confident in their abilities, while both his and Trevor’s small hopes for victory would be crushed even further. He couldn’t let that happen. He had worked too hard and dreamed too much to just give up on his wrestling career. He had to prove that size doesn’t matter, no matter what the naysayers touted. He needed to win to prove to everyone: Hessian, Cain, the fans, Trevor, and hell, even himself...to prove that Heresian Bloodlust will not just have their way with Civil Disobedience and the OSW tag team division. As he felt the anger rise him, Seth realized that there was no other alternative. This was war...and he would be ready to shed some blood, suburban style.
