He teases him further.

9 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 9, 2002 Reno had that terrible feeling today. On Saturday night he had done what very few had done. Reno Davis had defeated Ivan Stanislav in singles competition. Rachel Leigh Cook, an old friend of Reno’s had helped out a bit, by her free choice of course, and Reno managed to talk her into taking SOME money in case her health was to become in danger. Everything had turned out all right though, yet Davis felt incomplete. The Unwanted Angel stopped the Russian Bear, and that struck Reno Davis as odd. If anything, Reno should have been slaughtered and forced into retirement, but in an unusual turn of events, Reno succeeded in something. “You’re teasing me now...” Reno said, as he stood in the parking lot of the Seven Eleven-gas station. The person whom he spoke to, naturally, did not answer him back. Why would he? He didn’t even answer those closest to him, let alone someone that he wants back in Heaven. For a while, Reno thought that he was off the hook, that maybe God had left him alone to live the rest of his life. Then Davis signed with OSW. So many faces of the past have come back. Kyle Noiles, Reno’s old mentor and trainer. Griff Truxell, Reno former friend and partner. Tempest, the love that never was. All have hurt Reno in their respective ways. All have come back and all have hurt Reno. Kyle tried to kill him, Tempest made Reno think irrationally, which has caused Ivan to try to kill him. Then there’s Griff...A ticking time bomb that has a personal hatred for Reno. The only positive things in Reno’s life, Amy and ZaC; are constantly abused. God was teasing him. He was setting him up for a massive fall. God allowed Reno to defeat Ivan, which has no doubt sent the man into a bigger rage than before. God was no fool, and he was playing his cards properly. The Unwanted Angel was on a straight course into a brick wall. And Reno would fight until his last breath. “I won’t let you win.” Reno silently whispered to himself, watching the clouds as they shifted. Unbeknownst to Davis, Aimz had been watching him for a few minutes now, puzzled. She knew that things were not well in Reno’s head even since that Kyle or Kasp or whatever he chose to call himself guy came back. Then when ZaC got hurt, he went off the deep end. Amy hoped that the win over Ivan would ease Reno’s frustration, but it seemed to have not done anything. ''Reno! That was a great win over Ivan...you're starting at the top of the mountain, man!'' She tried to gain response, but failed. Davis merely half turned his head towards her and nodded before looking back up at the sky. ''Wow...thanks for the greeting. Uhh...whatcha' looking at up there?'' She tried again. Reno shrugged and cleared his throat, “Just...nothing.” Reno turned and approached her. He wore a black security guard sweater and bondage pants that fit perfectly. His eyes met hers, and she noticed a slight glimmer of what could be a tear forming. “Amy...Thanks for watching ZaC.” ''It's...umm...no problem. It's the least I cou----okay, look: Something's seriously wrong, Reno...and you've held off on telling me what it is for a long time now...I'm getting really tired of that. If something's happening, I've got every right to know about it...I'm your friend, I lo----I care.'' Reno came within a few inches of her and she could feel his breath as he looked up to her. She’d never noticed, but she was a few inches taller than Reno. “Amy,” He said as they both began to smooth in closer, “You don’t need to worry about me.” ''I worry because I can't stand seeing you like this...in pain. You won't believe it if I tell you how much I care about you...so I'll relate it to me. I want you around, for my happiness...I don't want you sitting up in some hospital just because you couldn't let me do what I could to help. You honestly do mean a lot to me, I don't want to let anybody drive a wrench into that now.'' Their arms wrapped around each other. Their bodies pressed together Their lips neared. ZaC leapt onto Reno’s shoulder. “Ah! ZaC!” Reno screamed, his heart beating dangerously, “You scared the shit out of me buddy!” The little monkey cuddled against Reno’s face and he scratched behind his ears in return. Amy and Reno’s eyes met again, this time they were a few feet apart from each other. “Uhm,” Reno looked over at the blanket containing their food, “Ah, the picnic’s ready? I’m starved. You?” Reno looked at Aimz. '''Uhhh......yeah...haven't eaten since this morning...'' She looked around uncomfortably. Reno nodded and shifted ZaC onto his left shoulder before walking towards the picnic site. “Yeah...” Aimz said, sliding her foot back and forth across the grass, “Real hungry...”
