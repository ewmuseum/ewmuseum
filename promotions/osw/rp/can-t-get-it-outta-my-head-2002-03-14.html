Can't get it outta my head...

14 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/14/02 The scene opens to show Frankie Bones walking down a Brooklyn street late at night, as has been seen so many times before. The night air is warmer these days, so the pea coat has been retired for the time being. He simply wears a pair of faded jeans and a white T-shirt. He walks slowly with his hands in his pockets, apparently deep in thought, as he so often is. He notices little, and cares about less. He has grown distant from the street, nothing seems to penetrate the wall he built around himself long ago. The passers by, the bums, the lost businessmen, they all come and go, and Frankie could care less. His mind is on one thing at the moment, the Tag Team Titles. He walks on and on, not knowing where he's going, not caring. He is suddenly grabbed by a bum. 'Got any change brotha?' asks the bum. 'Get your dirty ass hands off me son,' growls Frankie as he glances at the man. Frankie stops and takes a double take. The bum had a very nice clean cut face for a homeless person. He looked strikingly like Seth Masters from Civil Disobedience. Frankie looks once more. 'No way' he mutters as he continues walking. He glances over his shoulder only to find the bum trailing him. Suddenly a hooded figure walks up beside the bum, the slight details of his face resemble those of Trevor Hawke. 'What the fuck' whispers Frankie as he speeds up slightly. He shakes his head even harder and continues at a quicker pace. From the street corner across the street comes a yell. 'Five dollas baby, anyting you want!' screams the hooker. Wincing, Frankie sends a menacing glare towards the prostitute. He is nearly floored when he sees 'her' face. She looks just like Seth Masters, with a bad wig. 'You gotta be shittin' me' states Frankie as he adds another step to his pace. He glances over his shoulder, and sure enough, there they all are. The bum, the hooded figure, and the hooker, all striking images of the members of Civil Disobedience. Frankie breaks into a job, running from the demons in his head as much as he's running from the apparent clones of his upcoming opponents. He spots a bus at the corner ahead, picking up passengers. Sending one last glance that affirms the fact that the three clones are well behind him, he sprints on the bus. Throwing some change in the slot he mutters, 'Okay man, that's it, let's go.' 'Right away Mister Bones' states the bus driver as he quickly closes the door and floors the accelerator. Mister Bones? Frankie quickly turns to see the sight he least wanted to, Trevor Hawke, driving the bus. He looks back at the bus full of 'passengers.' Each seat is filled with either Trevor Hawke or Seth Masters. Frankie shoots a quick glance to the speedometer, 65 m.p.h. He grabs the door handle, it won't open. 'aaaaaaaaaaaaaaaAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!' Frankie jerks awake in his bed. Standing upright so quickly he sees stars and is lightheaded for a moment. It takes him minutes to get his bearings straight. 'I'm gonna kill those pretty boy bastards' he growls as he lies back down. The boys from Civil D have invaded his dreams, a very personal place. A hatred has been sparked in Frankie Bones, and there is only one cure for this hatred...PAIN.
