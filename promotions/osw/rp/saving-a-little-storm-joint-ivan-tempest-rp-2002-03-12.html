Saving a little storm.... (Joint Ivan/Tempest rp)

12 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-12-02 The events of Crowning Glory were one that Ivan Stanislav, for being a man so attuned to preparation and contingencies, could not even imagine. From the sudden influx of new talent that flooded the area, quickly bolstering the more 'religious' area's of the roster to the first ever OSW Champion: Switchblade, has done nothing more than further his resolve in dominating and controlling the entire OSW. Jane Reagan's sudden switch from 'savior of the people' to 'whore of OSW' has not increased the happiness in his life either, as she has gone from the positive influence of the people to nothing more than a common piece of trash amongst the capitalist regimes in the federation. That, coupled with the fact that Doug Troy barely managed to garner the win against a match that was so heavily weighed in Ivan's favor can only throw more fuel on the fire that is burning within The Russian Bear. After the Pay Per View, after the celebration of the newest communist to join The Red Army: Michel Krieg, Ivan Stanislav promptly returned to a hospital in order to check on a very unlikely individual. Stanislav walked down the hallway of the cold, dark hospital, brushing past doctors and nurses as he did, getting a confused look from all of them as the hulking Russian Bear lumbered through the corridor. He turned and faced a door in which the name “Tempest” hung from a clipboard. He looked to the left and right and slowly grabbed the doorknob, but it was locked. Gritting his teeth, Stanislav turned the knob and snapped it off, easily walking into the room to find a bed with no one in it! Stanislav blinked twice and looked around the room, finally walking to the front of the bed and pulling the chart from the foot of the bed, reading it aloud.. “Estimated time of stay: twelve days!?” He thought for a moment, “it has only been day and a half! Where is she??” Tossing the chart on the bed Stanislav turned to leave just in time for a nurse to walk into the room and let out an ear-shattering scream. “EEEEIIIII!!” Stanislav grabbed his ears and shushed the nurse, “Quiet you! I am not here to hurt you!!” The nurse shook and pointed at the bed, “Where is she??” “I do not know! I was going to ask you same question, woman!” barked Stanislav, not liking the forwardness of the nurse, “I was told she was to be here for extended period of time!” The nurse fidgets and looks around, “She is! She is supposed to be here!” Slowly, both individuals turned and looked at the nearby window, which was open a crack. Stanislav bolted to the window and looked out it, promptly pulling a long line of tied towels together, “I think I have figured out where our little storm has gone.” Ivan looked at the towels while the nurse gasped, “She is not in the correct condition to travel! She is probably still lethargic and suffering from the amnesia!” Ivan nodded to the nurse, “Do not worry, I will find her!” and he quickly pushed past the woman and headed down the hallway... We fade back in on another hallway, this one more of the likes of an apartment building than anything else. Periodically, a loud 'thump' is heard as the camera continues to focus down the length of the hallway, until Ivan Stanislav rounds the corner, jogging slightly and heads down the hallway and stops at a door, turning and knocking on it. The door itself shakes slightly from the force of Ivan's knocking. “Tempest, are you there??” yells Ivan as he puts his ear to the door and listens, “Tempest??” Stanislav puts his hand on his chin and runs his fingers through his beard, thinking for a moment before coming to some sort of a conclusion and nodding. He turns and heads past the camera and walks into a stairwell. Stanislav looks down the stairwell, as he is obviously on one of the top floors, and begins to head down the stairs, his heavy military boots echo throughout the area as his 400 pound frame barrels down the stairs. He rounds two landings and stops suddenly... The camera pans around revealing Tempest with her head heavily bandaged, laying weakly on the stairs in her wrestling attire. Ivan stands stunned for a moment in genuine surprise at what this woman is capable of. Tempest doesn't even notice his presence and reaches for a stair, only to find that it is Ivan's huge hand that she grabs. She looks up with a weary look in her eyes directly at Ivan's..smiling face.. For a moment she starts to shrink away from the hulking form, not entirely clear in her vision. Then she takes a deep breath and weakly manages, “Ivan? Izzat you?” Ivan smiles and drops to a knee, not wanting to startle her, and brings his hand closer to her, “Yes Tempest, it is Ivan. I am here to help you.” Her head lolls to one side, as she blinks up at him and says, “I'mokay... really. I can...” She tries to stand, but teeters backward. Ivan grunts and reaches forward, quickly grabbing her by the shoulder and steadying her, “Do not overwork yourself, Tempest, you have proven that you are strong woman!” Gingerly, he lifts her up in his arms, “Let Ivan take you back to your room, da?” Her head droops a little and she nods a very little bit. “Ok... you're right. You... you're not gonna take me back the hospital are you?” she asks, with a hint of fear. Ivan smiles and brings his huge arms around her closer, trying to let her feel mildly secure, “Nyet Tempest, I will not take you back to that place. Come, I am taking you home.” He smiles down at her for a moment, turns, and walks up the stairwell to her floor and walks her to her room. “Do you have your key, Tempest?” “Key...” Tempest says, as her eyes close. “No. It's in a safe place... like where Miyuki can find it.” Ivan thinks for a moment and shakes his head, deciding to handle this his way. He leans against the door and leans into it, easily pushing the door in and sending it swinging open. He walks into the room and lays her down on her large couch. Ivan goes and shuts the door and returns with his hands on his hips, thinking to himself. He nods after a moment of deliberation and shakes a finger at Tempest, “Heh, do not go anywhere now! Ivan will make you feel better....” He looks around the apartment and then heads towards her kitchen. Ivan fools around in the kitchen for a minute and returns with a cup of hot tea and sets it on the coffee table right next to Tempest. He squats down beside her and hands her the cup, “Here, drink this Tempest.” Tempest sit up slowly, propping herself against the fuzzy white sofa cushions. She winces a little, putting a hand to her head. She looks at the steaming mug offered by Ivan and blinks. “That's not spiked is it?” I crooked smile forms on Ivan's face, “Just drink it, it will make you feel better...” She chuckles very softly and sips daintily. “Mmm, not bad. Maybe I don't care whether it's spiked after all. Thanks.” She smiles at him with a mildly dazed look. Ivan sits for a moment, his eyes cast down on the floor as he ponders something, “Why did you leave hospital?” He doesn't look at her, he just continues to stare at the floor. Tempest scoots further into the cushions and cups her hands under the teacup as she gulps and says, “It's a hospital... people go there to die. I can't stand them.” A low growl rolls through Ivan's body, more like a sigh than anything threatening, “People go there to be helped as well....” Tempest chuckles, almost bitterly, and flinches. She rubs her temple and says, “Not where I come from. Hospitals in the part of Hong Kong I came from were death traps. I think... I think that's why my dad was house call doctor. People trusted him, but not the hospital.” She looks into her teacup as if seeing something in the ripples for a moment. Ivan pauses for a minute and ponders this, and then asks another question, “Did you see any of Pay Per View?” “Sorta,” Tempest says, with a slight shiver, “I mean I saw bits and pieces and I dunno if they all make sense even.” Ivan grunts, “Did you hear about your ‘friend’, Jane Reagan?” “I... I saw her kissing Sebastian Toomes...” Her brow furrows and she looks pained before saying, “Are you gonna tell me I wasn't hallucinating?” Ivan shakes his head slowly, “Nyet, Tempest, you were not hallucinating, what you saw was complete truth.....” Ivan snorts, “Capitalist to the end...” Tempest slumps and answers, “I don't understand... Jane's never been like that. I mean she may be lonely, but I didn't think she was desperate.” She shakes her head. “But nobody's what they seem I think. Ya know, I thought Doug Troy would be a good friend for me.” Ivan nods to himself slowly, “You will learn that, no one is what they seem, sometimes Tempest, you have to look deep within the person to figure out who they really are, and what they really....” Ivan pauses and swallows, “...care about. She appears to be in league with that idiot Switchblade, that is for sure.” Tempest looks at him sadly. “I guess so. I thought I knew her better than that. It's just... so different than how she used to be. But maybe you're right. Maybe I didn't know her as well as I thought.” Ivan fidgets, his hands clasped together, he whispers softly, “Yes well, Ivan can assure you that...that he would not purposely uh...try....to hurt......you.” Ivan's eyes dart around, looking at the floor, and then slowly brings his hand up on the arm of the couch above Tempest. Tempest smiles a little. “I know. Maybe we don't have a lot in common. But here you are.” She shrugs. “Not Jane, not Troy, not anyone who wants me to believe they're my friend.” She finishes off her tea and smiles, “And you're right I do fell better.” Ivan chuckles, “Ah, well, Vodka will do that, eh?” Ivan pulls out a small flask from his pocket and nods in Tempests' direction and then replaces it again. “I am worried to leave you alone, Tempest. I am afraid you will try to go and do things on your own again.” “Why would you think that?” Tempest says, her eyes wide with such perfect innocence as to be totally unbelievable. Ivan chuckles to himself, “Oh, I would not know..maybe after you climbed out of window and tried to climb up flights of stairs....that may be a reason, no?” Tempest smiles wearily, with a little yawn. “Maybe. But I'm home now, and... I'm glad you're here.” She leans forward slightly and wraps her arms around his neck in a gentle hug. Ivan looks over at her and smiles. He pats her on the head carefully, “Little Storm...do not worry. You will learn that Ivan Stanislav is not.....what everyone says he is. You will be fine, and I will not leave so long as you do not wish to be alone, da?” He looks down at Tempest and smiles. “Thanks,” she says, her head drooping a little. “I'll be ok. I just need to rest and ...” she yawns again. “Wow... I'm not gonna ask what proof that was that you put in here.” Her voice slurs mildly and her head drops to his shoulder lightly. Ivan swallows and leans against the couch, letting Tempest rest her head more on his shoulder. “Ah, it is nothing powerful, Tempest, nothing that Ivan Stanislav cannot drink, you should be able to handle it! DYAAHAA!” Ivan thinks and looks over at Tempest, he blinks once and runs his massive hand through her hair, “Get some sleep Tempest, you had very trying day.” Ivan nods and mutters to himself, “Very trying indeed....” Ivan thinks for a second, swallows, and looks over at Tempest, actually, more like the top of her head, “Tempest?” “Hmmmmmm?” she half speaks, half yawns. Ivan grinds his jaw and furrows his brow, “Do you remember one question you had asked at dating game, regarding bad day at work....?” After a moment of silence, Tempest chuckles a little. “Yeah I do... When I said that meant I got my butt kicked, you said you'd take me to the medical facility, beat the crap out of whoever did it, and then...” she snickers a little, “Not baby me cuz I should get back up and fight or something.” She looks at him curiously. “This is not my best 'get back up and fight pose', but I bet you knew that.” Ivan swallows, “Well, I did destroy Meanstreak for you, and I did take you to hospital....” He thinks for a moment again, “But I suppose I may have been wrong about saying you should get right back up and fight....that was selfish of me, some things are easier said than they are done.” Ivan swallows and thinks for a long, long time, digesting perhaps the first time that he has been wrong, “But I am not babying you, because you need this help. To baby one is to care for one who does not require the help.” He thinks for another long time, “Besides, to baby someone means it must be inconvenience, this is not the case, Tempest.” Tempest chuckles a little and settles into his arms with a contented sigh. “I'm glad you decided that. I'd really hate to get up and fight just yet.” She pouts playfully. “I don't even think I could bite anyone's ankles.” “There will be no ankle biting for you, Tempest. The only thing you are allowed to do is get rest, Praporschik Stanislav's orders!” Ivan grins to himself, thoroughly amused at himself. Tempest giggles and says, “Ok, you win. I'm too tired to put up a fight. I'll behave. “ She smiles with a slight hint of mischief. “Just don't get too accustomed to it.” Ivan chuckles to himself, his laugh shaking Tempest, “I will try not too, but I must admit this is the quietest I have ever heard you!” He chuckles again, “It is amazing.” “I'm quiet... sometimes,” she says, just above a whisper. After a moment, her breathing slowed to a regular rhythm, and she was still, a rare sight few were witness to. Ivan nods slowly as he hears a soft snore from her, “Da...Tempest, Ivan will be sure to make others quiet too...” He swallows and pats her head and leans against the couch, closing his own eyes.
