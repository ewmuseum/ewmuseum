Night Bowling

20 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-20-02 Roll, roll, roll, CRASH! Roll, roll, roll, CRASH! The sound of a ball making its way down a slender, yet waxy lane. Once at the end we hear the loud crashing thunder of an impact. The ball comes crashing into the pins. One by one by one, each pin will fall, or maybe it won’t. Depending on just whose hand is placed upon the ball is only one of the major factors in this game. The surrounding is nice, and that is the most that can be said. Men and women alike wondering around in their specially made bowling T-shirts with their teams named wrote on it. Some of those names; Balls of Thunder, The Alley Cats, Hard as a Rock, Shifty Lanes, The Gutter Balls, and Flaming Balls. When down on the seventh lane, we see a familiar face. The face of one “Bad Attitude” Jesse Ramey and sitting behind him is John Buchanan. We slowly come in closer to the two men; right about now, Jesse is standing and picking up a ball. He makes his way onto the lane. Speaking very softly, “Tonight ladies and gentlemen, Jesse Ramey, is up for his twelfth frame and this one could make or brake him. He has had a tough match tonight with his close friend, John Buchanan. Jesse needs a strike to win this game.” Jesse draws back the ball. “Jesse sets up and is getting ready for this.” Jesse goes up to the lane and lets the ball rip. “The ball makes its way down the lane and…” We have a pause. Jesse spins around, “Woo! I told you that shit wouldn’t keep me from beating you! For the sixth time in a row!” “Damn it, Jesse! Can’t you just let me win one frigin game?” John says in an irritated tone. “Come on man, just admit it. You can’t stand losing! It’s totally pathetic!” Jesse says laughing. “Well, at least I don’t lose every single wrestling match that I’m in.” John says and then begins to laugh at Jesse. Jesse shrugs and looks at John with a smile, “Doesn’t matter to me. I know how good I am. I just don’t show it all off in the ring. I’ll step it up a notch in this up coming match.” John stands up and takes a drink out of the beer off to the side, “Come on dude, you’re a pussy. You couldn’t hold your own in a ring if you wanted to.” “Man, you really must have your facts all messed up.” Jesse begins to laugh. “Tell me who it was the led you to gold in UWL?” John gets really quite, “Guess you have me there.” “That’s right I do. If it hadn’t have been for me, you and Blackheart wouldn’t have made it in the UWL.” Jesse says. “Come on man, you know good and well that Blackheart would have made it without you.” John says. “Oh yeah, really now. You know the reasoning behind him getting that World Title match?” Jesse asks. “He earned it, didn’t he?” John asks. Jesse begins to laugh, “So far from the real story. That title was offered to me and I turned it down because I thought I would be able to get another shot after Blackheart had his run. You know good and well that he couldn’t have gotten that match if it hadn’t have been for me.” “Actually, I didn’t know this. Why didn’t you tell me this stuff when it was going on?” John asks. “This stuff was supposed to be kept between me and the boss. If I had told anyone else and he had found out then I would have been fired.” Jesse says as he takes a drink of his soda. “Oh, well. I guess that is enough to make anyone believe you. That could also be the reasoning behind your long Television Title reign.” John suggests. Jesse begins to laugh, “Nope, that was completely because of my talent. I ruled UWL!” “You wish you had ruled the UWL… You’ve never ruled anything man.” John says. Jesse begins to smile, “Believe what you want to believe man. I’ve always been in charge every time we’ve ever worked together. Especially in UWL, EWF, CWA, and JEW! You’ve never done anything that anyone was proud of man.” They begin to laugh, “You know that isn’t true.” John says. “Yeah, I know it wasn’t true. All that crap you were saying about isn’t true either is it?” Jesse asks. “Nah, I was only joking man. So tell me, what are you going to do about your match?” John asks. “What’s to tell? I’m going to keep up with those workouts like today, but I’m going to try and get Michelle and Chass to come and do them with me tomorrow. You’re welcome to come as well.” Jesse says. “Sure thing man. I know good and well that neither of those two will be able to spot you.” John laughs. “The only real reason I’m having them come with us is because of how much damn money they spent on clothes today!” Jesse states. “Yeah, I totally understand that man. I saw that bill. They almost maxed out your card.” John says. “Whoa, look at the clock man. We told the girls that we would be home at nine and it’s almost nine right now, we had better jet.” Jesse says. The two stand up and walk out the door, the sound of bowling balls being flung down the lanes are still heard as darkness encases the surrounding area. ~The End~
