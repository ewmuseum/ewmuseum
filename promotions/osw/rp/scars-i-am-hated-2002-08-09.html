Scars: I Am Hated

9 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 9, 2002 (OOC Note: I’m going to be developing Corg’s bipolar illness deeper and deeper with each promo, as you will see in this one he begins to develop a sense of paranoia. I just thought I’d let whoever’s reading this know ;-)) As I lay sprawled out on the black carpeted floor of my apartment, I listened, as there was a knock on the door. I kept still, making sure I remained quiet, because I never knew… they might be after me. This could all be one big plot to kill me, and I wasn’t about to wave my white flag. One, two, three knocks. I didn’t answer. I waited, and waited, until I thought it was over. I thought that whoever was behind that grey door would be gone right now, I mean, after all, it had been an hour. Slowly, I crept up to a balancing position from where I was laying. I tip toed very carefully, stalking-like to the door, which by the way was only three feet in front of me. I listened. There were no sounds, not even the faint sound of a low-life hall maid. There was nothing. I cupped my hand around the doorknob, placed my ear against the wood door, turned. I turned the knob so slow that you probably wouldn’t notice it was moving. I peaked through the slight two inch crack which I had made after I opened the door. I didn’t see anyone from where I was standing. So I took a deep breathe, and swung the door open. I looked to the left, then to the right. The halls were empty. There was no one on the sixth floor of my apartment. No one. But below my feet, was a small brown box, taped heavily in duct tape. I reached down to pick it up as I saw no label. I didn’t know if it would be safe to bring it inside, it might’ve been some sort of trap. All in one swift motion, I cradled the box in my arms, jumped back through the doorway of my apartment, and locked the door behind me. I was swift alright, but I wasn’t so sure about the box… I kneeled down to the floor, and placed the box on the dirty black carpet which hadn’t been cleaned in weeks. I stared at the box, as if something was going to happen. It was just a box. A dumb, stupid box. I figured I was worrying myself to much, so I began opening the box. I reached down into my black Altered Mind pants pocket, and withdrew an unpolished jagged knife. My wonderful, oh-so beautiful knife. But much like the carpet, it hadn’t been cleaned in weeks. I stabbed it into the box’s cardboard fold, and tore down the duct tape, until I was able to open the box without any struggle. But what I found inside the box wasn’t what I had expected… ‘You Will Die.’ It was written on a large piece of white shiny posterboard in black permanent ink. Followed by that disturbing message was about one thousand signatures of random women around the country, or the globe for that matter. I quickly stabbed it with my knife, and tore is to shreads. “Aggghh, fuck you! Fuck you!” I screamed as pieces of the poster flew into the air. “I’m not going to die! Do you hear me?!” My voice toned down, from screaming to a sorrow-felt scared voice. “I’m not going to die… I don’t want to die.” Tears strolled out of my eyes, as I cupped my hands over my pale face, crying. Crying like a little pussy. “I'm not going to die die.”
