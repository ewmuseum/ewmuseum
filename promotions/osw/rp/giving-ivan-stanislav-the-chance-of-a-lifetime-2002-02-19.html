Giving Ivan Stanislav the chance of a lifetime

19 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-19-02 ‘So now Ivan must fight against eleven other men in order to get a shot at the OSW World Title. Well, it could have been different, it could have been much more difficult, but it’s actually quite easy….’ Thinks Alexei Ruslan as he sits at his plush office underneath The Kremlin, the most famous building in the Russian Federation. Ruslan sits behind his huge, oaken desk, which happens to sit in the middle of the expansive room. The deep, dark red carpet is a stark contrast to the pale yellow walls that seem to be strictly there for Ruslan’s love, and no one else. Various plaques hang on the walls, signifying various awards and accomplishments that Alex has achieved, however, we do not know what exactly the contents of the rewards are due to the fact that they are written in Russian. Also, behind him on the far wall hangs a huge, red hammer and sickle, almost like the reverse of what the Soviet Flag would look like with the yellow walls behind it. As well, pictures hang on the walls, from Lenin to Gorbachev, and various pictures of Alexei shaking hands with other communists. Most notably Gorbachev and Molotov. The wily Russian himself, as said above, sits at his desk on a large, plush red chair who’s back reaches higher than Alexei’s head. The phone on Alexei’s desk begins to ring. He quickly wheels forward and picks it up, hitting a button on the face of the phone and speaks in a hurried Russian tone. He finishes and hangs up a moment later, obviously conveying his entire thought in that brief moment. He folds his hands in front of his face and thinks, pressing his lips to his fingers. His eyes dart to the left and to the right as he thinks longer. “At least it could be worse,” thinks Ruslan, “we could have to fight all of them on our own! And besides,” Ruslan smiles at this thought, “how are they to knock Praporschik Stanislav over the top rope?” He grins upon the realization of how absurd that thought is. Ivan Stanislav being knocked over the top rope? Inconceivable. Yes, to Alexei Ruslan things were actually going very well. While Meanstreak definitely was a damper on their plans on Slam, he isn’t something that Ruslan is extremely concerned about. His fingers dance on his keyboard as he brings up various documents and files. The light from the monitor flickers across his face, casting shadows across his gaunt face. He leans back and looks at something on the computer screen. As he reads, we see his eyes scan the screen… “These men, they are nothing. None of these men can stand up against Ivan. Why even waste my time.” Thinks Ruslan as he shuts his computer off. He looks at the camera and tilts his hat, greeting us. “Greetings OSW from The Kremlin!” Says Ruslan, cheerily. “I have been watching the events unfold as of late, and I must say that I am unimpressed. With Meanstreak coming back and this false sense of security within the roster, it is actually quite interesting.” Ruslan laughs and stands behind his desk. He quickly pulls a knife out of his pocket and picks a fingernail. “You think that Meanstreak will pose a problem? I do not think so. I think that Meanstreak has better things to do in his stupid little mind than try to interfere in The Red Army’s plans. You see, Meanstreak surely does not care what exactly happens to the OSW….so why would he stop us?” Alexei chuckles to himself and flicks a piece of fingernail off. “And besides, Ivan Stanislav is going to win match on Slam and become number one contender for OSW World Title, so why should we even be concerned about anything. Stanislav will become first ever OSW champion, and no one will have the power to stop him.” He laughs and tosses the knife over his shoulder, sticking it into the wall behind him. “OSW, I do not need to harp on the subject. Stanislav is given prime opportunity on Saturday to become most powerful man in the OSW, and The Red Army will be lead to victory, and then the federations of the PTC will fall to our might. Join our cause…or perish in the battles to come….” Alexei laughs loudly to himself and sits in his chair..the camera fades out…
