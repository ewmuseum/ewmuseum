BE! AGGRESSIVE! B-E AGGRESSIVE!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Few hours after Slam! The doc picked the last bits of glass out of his chest. “You know, you weren’t cleared to wrestle…” The doc clucks disapprovingly. Toomes grimaces, his body on fire. Tonight, tonight was sweet. Armour, headfirst through a glass table. Yeah, okay, he himself got suplexed and Armour’s fat ass fell on him, but hey, what’s a poor boy gonna do? He brushes the lanky black hair out of his eyes, his natural color dyed back by Jennifer, who was conspicuously absent tonight. Ever since he got released from the hospital and Jane came back into the picture, Jennifer seemed to evaporate like fog once the sun comes up. No number, no nothing. Jane stands across from him in the locker room, still dressed in tight black leather pants and a tight black Lycra top. She smiles at Sebastian as the doc daubs some alcohol on Toomes’ wounds. “You did great out there tonight, sweetie…” She says, her emerald eyes locking with his, the promise of fantastic sex tonight in those jade orbs. Toomes grits his teeth against the pain. “Thank you… But I gotta wonder… Whose bright idea was it to bring in Armour? Who signed him? You?” Jane shakes her head. “No, it was all that bastard Porter’s idea… But don’t worry, you’ll get ample opportunity to dismantle him later. You just have to worry about getting back into the ring and staying healthy…” Toomes stands, brushing aside the doctor. “Fuck…” Jane smiles. “We’ll get to that later…” Toomes turns to her, his black eyes flashing. “Thanks for the offer, but I’m… I dunno… Feeling aggressive…” Jane smiles uneasily. “Well, we can work out all that aggression at the hotel, honey…” Toomes turns away, and slams his fist into the steel locker, the sound of flesh and bone hitting sheet metal echoing through the locker room. “Yeah… I’ll get back to you there, Jane… I… I’m gonna hit the gym. See you at the hotel, lover…” Jane steps closer to him, slipping her arms around him and crushing her mouth against his, her tongue darting inside his mouth. She breaks the kiss after a minute, holding him close. “I’ll be waiting, Sebastian… Go hurt somebody.” She licks his lips and spins out of his grasp, and walks out of the locker room. Down the bench, Frankie Bones snickers. “Well, well, well… Mister Billy-Bad-Ass is feeling ‘aggressive’! Ooo!” Toomes snarls. “Fuck off, Frank. I ain’t in the mood to get fucked with.” Bones stands, half-dressed in a pair of Dockers Khakis, his muscled frame still glinting with water from the showers. “Well, maybe I AM, motherfucker! You fucking left me buried in the low card when you bailed on our angle! You left me fucking fighting Drew, you fucking cocksucker!” “Fuck you, Bones. You know as well as I do that the writers were running that shit! Don’t fucking blame your lack of fucking talent on me, jerker!” Bones roars and lunges at Toomes, who flings open a locker in Bones’ face to deflect him, but Bones smashes through, bowling over Toomes. The pair fall off the laminated wooden bench and onto the turquoise tile floor. Bones raises a fist to smash into Toomes’ face, but Toomes cracks his fist into Bones’ jaw. Bones staggers off of Toomes, who lunges the big man. The two land on the tile floor again, trading punches and chokeholds. Bones screams as Toomes locks his arm around Bones’ neck, and the big man stands up and slams Toomes into a row of lockers. The sound of flesh on metal reverberates, and Steven Shadows, a.k.a. Graphic Violence, walks to the row of lockers, clad only in a towel, fresh out of the showers. He chuckles as Toomes is thrown into the other side of lockers. On the other side of the bank of lockers, Hessian is rolling on some deodorant. A bottle of Sang De Versace is knocked out of his locker and smashes on the floor. The big Goth looks down at the fragments and snarls. “Who…the…FUCK…did that?” the Goth screams. Hessian stands, clad in only his ring tights, and walks to where Shadows is watching Toomes and Bones stiff the shit out of each other. Bones has a bloody lip and Toomes’ bandages are beginning to produce spots of blood leaking through the gauze. Hessian pushes Shadows to the side, eliciting a “What the fuck?” from Graphic Violence, as Hessian lunges at Bones. Bones ducks and drives a fist into Hessian’s stomach. “Fuck you! Fuck all of you!” Bones screams, right before Toomes tackles him to the bench, the wood creaking underneath their combined weight. Hessian roars and grabs Toomes by the hair, and flings him away, rebounding off the lockers. Hessian gets Bones in a bearhug as Bones slams his fists into the side of Hessian’s head, the big man’s head down to absorb the blows. Toomes tightens his bandages and plows into the both of them, and all three fall to the floor, the bench snapping underneath them. On the other side of the locker room, Ivan Stanislav thunders over to Graphic Violence. “What is going on here?” Ivan roars. Shadows smirks. “SeaBass and Bones got into it, and they musta pissed Hess off… Fucking groovy, man…” Ivan looks down at GV, a look of disgust on his face. “And you do nothing to stop it?” “Why the hell would I?” Ivan sighs and walks over to the fight. He rips Bones off of Hessian, flinging him back into the lockers. Bones, his eyes blazing, lunges for Ivan, but Ivan throws him into the other bank of lockers. The big Russian wrestler wrenches Toomes and Hessian apart. “Enough… Enough… ENOUGH!” Hessian looks at Ivan and grunts, lumbering off. “What is meaning of this?” Ivan quietly asks, the locker room now quiet. “Fucking Bones… Asshole…” Toomes mutters, holding his throat. Bones growls. “That cocksucker, fuck you Toomes! Fuck you! You strutting through here like your shit don’t stink! Like you don’t got to do anything anymore! Fuck you, motherfucker! I work my ass off, what do I get? Not a damn thing! You got everything handed to your motherfucking ass!” “Oh shut the fuck up! Just cause you can’t draw…” Toomes begins, but Ivan pushes him away from Bones. “ENOUGH! Ivan is tired of this locker room fighting! Go to your hotel, Toomes, and go fuck your bitch. Bones, go on, get out of arena. Ivan is tired of looking at both of you!” Bones screams, the cords in his neck standing out, in frustration. “MOTHERFUCKER!” he curses as he grabs his travel bag. “Go on! Leave this place!” Ivan roars, as Bones stomps out of the locker room, slamming the door as he leaves. Toomes spits a wad of blood out on the floor, looking up at Ivan, the man he beat at Head On Collision, right before Toomes’ personal life went to shit. Ivan, still one to hold a grudge, looks down at him impassively. “Go on, Toomes. Ivan is tired of looking at you.” Ivan rumbles, turning away to finish dressing. Toomes stews for a moment, until he realizes how good he felt. He turns to his locker and pulls out his travel bag, throwing it on the ruined remains of the bench. Slipping into a Slayer hoodie, he grabs his bag and takes off. Now for the real fun.
