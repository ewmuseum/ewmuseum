Reuniting The Army, Part 1

4 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4.4.02 Flash. A flash of light aimed in on Krieg and Wang as they stood in confusion, pausing in their conversation to look up to the arena roof. Flash. Yet another spot of light zoomed in on Krieg and Wang and disappeared just as quickly as it arrived. Flash. This time the light stays on and Krieg and Wang squint to look up at the light’s location. Looking up at the celebrity boxes above the standard fan seats, Krieg spots Tom Walczak messing around with the lights, as Walczak grins down at him and calls out, “Поздравления , Comrade Krieg! [Greetings...],” roared Walczak, his voice carrying an exceptionally long distance, “Tom! It is good to have you hear,” replied Krieg, as he walked towards the bottom of the stairwell Walczak was climbing down, Walczak emerged from through a pair of doors and he and Krieg exchanged a brief hand shake and an even briefer hug, as Walczak and Wang settle for just a handshake. “We have been observing arena, Walczak, and it looks sufficient for mass destruction of Heresian Bloodlust, nao,” asked Krieg, Looking around, letting his eyes take in all the angles, corners and lines of the arena, Walczak observes the arena for a second before turning back round to a semi-grinning Krieg, “Yah, yah. This shall do, Michel. When we are finished with Cain and Hessian, there will be plenty space for us to mop the floor with them,” answer Walczak, a bit of his Polish humour cracking him up, although it takes Krieg and Wang a minute to catch on before they start to chuckle. All three men stand at the bottom of the arena stands and look dead ahead, their arms all behind their backs as they look at the venue of what could be one of the biggest nights for these 3 men, as well as The Red Army. Wang breaks the silence as he opens his heart to Walczak and Krieg, “You know something, Walczak, Qwieg? I will be vewy, vewy pwoud of you two this week, you know that? You two will be pushing the bounds of Communism to new wevels, new heights that we could only have dwempt of not one year ago,” Krieg and Walczak look slighty miffed at Wang’s irregular attitude, but try not to spoil the moment, “Ah Wang, you are a proud man, I shall give you that,” replied Krieg, “Yes. You are a credit to the Red Army and a credit to Communism,” chirped in Walczak, both himself and Krieg still slightly worried by Wang’s change of thought, Reaching around the waists of the two giants, Wang continues, “I am pwoud to be associated with a gwoup that is doing so much good for the only powitical bewief there is...Communism. And we will continue to pweach this bewief until everyone, even those damn Capitalist, bewieve in our ways,” Wang takes his arms away from behind Krieg and Walczak’s backs and steps forward, raising his voice as he actually starts to preach, “And one day, one day my brothers, Communism will be the only following. And fweaks like Hessian and Cain, muscle-heads like Meanstweak and Toomes, and even Gangsters wannabes like Fwankie Bones and Dwew Hostyle, will bend under the greater powers of Communism,” Krieg and Walczak look at each other, severely concerned for Wang’s sanity, as the Chinaman with a lisp rants on. “Qwieg, Walczak, we are standing in the very building that will witness the birth of a new era, the start of a new decade of political domination! This Saturday, Hessian and Cain will qwumble at the hands of Qwieg and Walczak and that imbecile Sebastian Toomes will dwop to his knees at the mercy of Tempest! Boys, this Saturday will be remembered for years as the day the Red Army took over the OSW,” said Wang, slightly out of breath with his arms raised in the air, signalling victory, Krieg turned to Walczak and spoke into his ear, “Tom, does Wang have medication? He is very crackers,” claimed Krieg, Nodding his head, Walczak responded, “Yah, Wang is very loony, Krieg. He is Communist fruitcake, not like the rest of the Army though,” As Wang turned round to Krieg and Walczak, they stop talking and force smiles for Wang, “Qwieg, Walczak, let’s go,” “Wang, you are right. This Saturday will be a success,” claimed Krieg as all three men walker towards the door leading to the reception/locker room area, “We will win our match, Krieg, and then, we shall conquer the two freaks at Head On Collision and bring the first gold to the OSW edition of The Red Army,” Stopping and looking at Walczak, Krieg smiles and outstretches his hand as both men shake, “Tom, I am glad to be your partner. We shall get on just fine,” Walczak returned a smile before the continued on walking out of the arena and towards the main entrance. As they reached it, Wang turned round to Krieg and Walczak, “Boys, it is time for you to see the Russia that Ivan loves so much,” With that, they walk out of the arena and towards Tom’s jeep, both Krieg and Walczak climbing into it with ease as Wang tries to jump into the passenger’s seat. The jeep backs away from the building as the three men head off to the airport, soon to be departing for the country that Ivan adores: Russia. Fade. Fin.
