Leaving OSW (OOC)

3 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-3-02 Silence walks out from the night to share these words. Ever wonder what it takes to drive a man off the edge? Murder of his family, rape of his daughter or the messing with his life. All of these factors contribute to a man going off the deep end but very few things can do it at once like when you mess with a man’s career. Everyone at one point in their life realizes when things are not going their way and when they need to shove off and go to fresher waters. Hopefully we can part ways in a way that we do not have bitter feelings and that we can all remain professional. This is my goodbye to OSW and even though our time was short every bit of it was fun and worth it. But when you are offered to start over and begin something new is also something you need. I would like to thank all of you fans who have followed my career and maybe you will follow my new one as I embark on a new quest. Of coarse it would be unprofessional for me to name where I am heading off too but things like this happen and I wouldn’t stoop so low. Fans: Thanks again for making my stay in OSW a remember able one and hopefully you find me in the future because I guarantee my writings will be something that you have never seen before. OSW Roster: You all have talent and this is the first time I worked with a lot of you. In fact I believe this is the first time I have dealt with any of you. You all have great careers ahead of you and I hope you all capitalize on it. Everyone Else: Don’t know how to thank you but…thanks. Remember that I left you on a good note and that I don’t hold grudges. Please feel free to IM me or Email anytime you wish because the lord knows I will be around. Thanks again for the memories. With that Silence walked off into the night.
