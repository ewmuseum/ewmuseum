Revelations

21 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-21-02 The Russian wind blows in the background, cold and unyielding as it skirts across the cold, rugged, and icy landscape that is known to many as Siberia. In this wasteland of permafrost, ice, and wildlife, a small dummy stands…. No, not Doug Troy It is in the form of a man, but obviously this is not a human being, more like a mock creating of a man holding what appears to be some sort of flag. The flag waves in the air as the wind conjures up more strength and whips it around, tearing the flag slightly as it does so. The gray sky is a harbinger of things to come, some unknown event that will bring doom to some, or maybe all. Facing the dummy some 100 yards away is Ivan Stanislav who wields an old rifle of some sort. The Russian Bear stands alone in his heavy black pants, brown jacket, hat, and gloves. Behind him sits a military jeep of some sort, and tracks that lead deep into the background show that this is probably his means of getting to this secluded, quiet location. Stanislav’s hot breath exits his mouth as he levels the gun and fires sending a shockwave of sound around the area. The bullet flies through the air and punctures the head of the dummy, sending it flopping on the stick that it is impaled on. Birds in the background flee from a small forest and take to the air as the sudden sound spooks them out of hiding. Stanislav works the bolt on the rifle again and reaches into his pocket, producing another bullet and loading the rifle. The camera moves closer to The Russian Bear. “You will not take my Motherland,” mutters Ivan as he sights in the dummy again, “we have worked too hard….” He fires again, shooting the flag from the dummies hand and sending it to the ground to be buried underneath the unstoppable Russian snow. Ivan worked the bolt again, kicking the shell from the rifle and into the ground and loads the gun again. He shoulders the gun via a small strap. The gun itself is dwarfed by the massive back and broad shoulders of The Russian Bear. He puts his hands on his hips and looks around. “Such beautiful country I have, no matter what any man says.” He grins and runs his hand through his scraggly, slightly graying beard. The sound of an engine is heard in the distance, prompting Ivan to turn and shield his eyes from the sun. In the distance Stanislav spies a small jeep heading in his direction. “Alexei, it must be, probably back after his little talk,” Ivan grunts, not too pleased, “I cannot wait to hear this.” He sighs, turns, and unshoulders his gun again, aiming at the dummy and firing, taking the right knee out from under him. Methodically, and in no hurry whatsoever, Ivan kicks the bolt out of the gun and loads another. He aims and fires again, destroying the other knee. The jeep closes in distance and stops as Ruslan can clearly be seen in the drivers seat. He hops out of the jeep and into the snow which seem to have accumulated up to about his shins. His trench coat whips in behind him as the wind grows and sends flakes of snow into his eyes. Ivan fires again, not paying any attention to Alexei, and starts to load once more. Alexei nears Ivan and pauses with his hands in his pockets. “Praporschik, how was your meeting?” “It went well Alexei.” mutters Ivan as he levels the gun and fires. The bullet slams into the throat of the dummy. “And how was your meeting?” Questions Ivan as he straps the gun to his back again and look at his diminutive friend. “It was interesting, to say the least.” Replies Alexei. Ivan snorts, “That does not come as surprise to me, Alexei Gregorovich.” Ivan pauses for a moment, “What did she have to say?” Alexei takes a moment to digest this and figure out how to break any news to Ivan, he decides for the installment plan, “Well Ivan, she does not want you to interfere in her match…” Ivan scoffs, “I could have told you that, Alexei!” He turns and walks over to his jeep and tosses the gun in the back. He reaches inside and pulls out a bottle of vodka and unscrews the top, drinking straight from the bottle. “She does not understand, that is all. When it is over, she will. I will not change my mind just because she does not want me to do something. I have agenda too, you know?” Alexei shakes his head, “I do not think that is going to bode well with her, Ivan.” Ivan slams his hand down on the top of the jeep, denting it, “So what?! She has put me into this predicament!!” There is a pause. Ivan takes another healthy gulp of vodka. “What do you mean, Ivan?” Ivan slides the vodka into his pocket and walks from the jeep thoughtfully, “She brought herself into harms way and now Reagan has leverage against us. Reagan knows that I….I….” Ivan shakes his head in disgust. Alexei walks over to Ivan questioningly, but at a safe distance away, “You what, Ivan?” Ivan lets out a deep sigh causing his massive shoulders to rise and fall. He looks at the ground and mutters softly and shrugs, almost like a child, “Reagan knows that I do not want her to be hurt.” Alexei shakes his head, “I do not know what to say Ivan, I really do not.” Ivan whirls around, exasperated, “What is going on Alexei? What is happening?” Alexei shrugs, “What do you mean?” “Why do I think like this? Why do I act like this? I have never behaved in such a way before!!” Ivan kicks the snow in anguish. “Sometimes, I find myself thinking about her while I’m working, as if it really matters! Sometimes I hope that she is not being watched by some Chinese Triad…sometimes…” Ivan scoffs and spits on the ground, at a loss for words, “I do not know Alexei, I do not understand why I am behaving in such a way!” Alexei thinks for a moment with a very small smile on his face. He takes another stop towards Ivan and nods, “Sometimes it is okay to think about other people Ivan.” Ivan shrugs, “I do Alexei! I always think of other people!! Superiors! You, Krieg, Walczak, the other members of the Army! I think about all their betterment! I think about every man, woman, and child in Russia! I think about the past, I think about Russia’s future. And…and…I think about….her….” Ivan stares at the snow and shakes his head. Alexei leans against the jeep and crosses his arms over his chest, “She says she is doing it because she wants to prove to herself and to the people what she is made of.” Ivan turns, “But she does not..” “I know Ivan, I told her that she does not need to be afraid with The Red Army present, but she would not hear any of it.” Alexei tilts his head to the side and thinks to himself, “It is a confusing notion, I suppose. Capitalist, I believe…eh?” Ivan shakes his head, “Nyet, she thinks of other people too much…I hope…” “She said she will confront you Ivan and talk to you. She said she will not take it as an act of friendship if we intervene. This seems to mean a lot to her.” “And what do you think, Alex?” Mutters Ivan as he turns to him. Alexei shrugs and puts his hands in his pockets, “I want to continue to attack, Ivan. I think we lose much stability against Reagan and the rest of the management if we let this chance go. I understand that she wants to help herself, but I also understand that we have to help the people, da?” “Da.” “However, I am also mindful, Ivan, that she is something unlike any other and she works very hard. It is your decision, you are Praporschik, after all. You are the leader. You must make the call as to whether we attack or pull back. I will do whatever you say Ivan, you know what is best in OSW.” Alexei nods to Ivan and smiles. Ivan grunts and fixes the front of his pants, “At this moment, we still have our plan, we are still going to destroy Meanstreak and make sure no harm comes to her, and that Jane Reagan learns that we will call any challenge she creates.” He grins, “If she is so inspired to get me out of the way, she will talk to me personally, eh?” “She will talk to you Ivan, she is not afraid.” Replies Ruslan. Ivan takes another drink from his bottle and passes it to Alexei. “But let us not deal with that, let us focus on Doug Troy, the man that will serve as last possible defense against the Russian Bear and his Red Army!” Alexei drinks from the bottle and winces just slightly, “He will not be a problem Ivan. He is finished. Since you injured him in the PCW his career has been about as successful as Enron. Heh. He will not put up much of a fight.” “Good, because I do not want him spreading any more lies about me…eh?” “Da.” “He will surely put up admirable fight, I will give him that,” scoffs Ivan who begins to button up his jacket, “but it will all be in vain. No one can stop this juggernaut that is The Red Army.” He turns to Alexei just as he buttons around his neck, “Not Jane Reagan, not Doug Troy, not Ed….no one.” Ivan nods to Alexei’s jeep, “Come Alexei, let us head back to base, leave that vehicle, I will have a private pick it up!” Ivan grins and climbs into the drivers side, Alexei to the passenger side. Ivan turns the vehicle around and drives towards the horizon until the jeep is nothing more than a speck on the screen. Alexei’s voice, however, pierces our ears as if he was right beside us… “She is something special, da Praporschik?” “Yes Alexei, I believe that she is….” Fade to black
