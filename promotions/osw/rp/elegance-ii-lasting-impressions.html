Elegance II: Lasting IMPressions.

2002-05-27

Amy 'Aimz' Campbell

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The late afternoon sun scorched down on the populations, further frustrating everyone unfortunate enough to be out in it. Aimz herself was mumbling as she made her way through yet another generic hotel to grab her wallet before heading off to shop a bit. However, as she neared her room, she had no idea of what awaited her... Her mind was drifting off in a relative daze between Reno Davis and Rent-A-Hero, as usual...but when Inside her room, the scent of lavender floated in a light mist. Purple drapes and rose petals covered the drab brown carpeting, and a shuffling sound could be heard on the bed, as more rose petals were laid out, and heart-shaped cushions were set into place. ''If this doesn't work I'm giving up!'' Came an all-too familiar squeaking voice from the bed. ''Oh God...'' Amy stood there in the darkness for a minute, whispering to herself and bumping the back of her head lightly against the door in frusteration as she reached for a light wooden bat she'd left just behind the door in case of this very thing. ''...not this again.'' Before Campbell could reach the main light switch, two small hands pattered together, clapping a small lamp on...sending streams of fair golden light through the off-colored shade. Minion laid himself out on the bed, grinning from ear to ear. Seductively purring, the Imp waited, clad in what were SUPPOSED to be tight silk stockings two sizes too big, a lace garter belt, leather boots, electric nipple clips attached to some form of ungodly ''pleasure-machine'', a cheeky mask, and a furry pink halo. ''Okay baby, it's time to get BEEZAAAY!!!'' Minion yelled out, not noticing when Aimz entered the room and grabbed the bat. ''IMP!'' She shouted, loud enough for the occupants three doors down to hear. ''I swear to GOD, this time I'm gonna tear you apart!!!!'' ''Wha-...I...hey come on baby, this party is just gettin' started, lemme just dim the lights a little!'' Minion exclaimed in a voice not unlike that of Barry White's. Using a sleek little remote control, Minion dimmed the lamp as a love ballad started playing and, seeing the baseball bat, Minion began purring. ''You sick little...........FREAK! What the fuck is this shit?! Get those things off of your damned nipples, I hope you get some kinda Imp cancer from that shit. You've got thirty seconds to make an exit before I either decide to phone the local branch of the SPCA, or take matters into my OWN hands...'' The redhead snarled, a temper as firey as her hair, with a glaze over her eyes to match as she ran a vicious hand down the bat. ''Aw I always knew you'd COME around, Baby Firesnatch~!'' Minion squealed with delight, cuddling into one of the heart-shaped cushions. ''So uh, do you wanna be on top or underneath?'' He added, carressing his electronically hooked up nipples with delight. ''FireWHAT!? Errr......uhhhh....well...'' She raised an eyebrow and changed her tune soon after spotting a bottle of wine on the night table, then glancing to Minion's electric nipple clamps. ''...on top's always nice......but...ummm...uhhh....'' Amy tried not to think of how disgusted with herself she currently was, holding back a vicious spasm of shuddering as she drew closer to Minion, who was sprawled across a pillow, head resting on the stump of his palm. ''...it's whatever you want tonight...b......b...'' Oh come on, was it REALLY that difficult?! ''....baby.'' Minion winked at Aimz and nodded over at the machine hooked up to his nipples. ''If ya want, before we get into anything we can play around a little, you know. I'll be real gentle, like a GentleImp should be! Just mosey on over here sweet thang and I'll show you wonders not even that redheaded gimp can!'' Minion motioned for Aimz to sit on the bed. ''Oh, and please do help yourself to the wine, wet your whistle before...well...help yourself.'' Minion added, smiling cheekily. Amy had to stifle a laugh as she reached for the wine. ''Don't mind if I do...'' When she grabbed it, the wine wasn't in her hand for even two seconds before it was smashed all over the midsection of poor, horny little Minion...the purple tones of his skin marred by the red of the wine mixed with his blood when the glass shards knicked him. The little fella's biggest problem, however, turned out to be the electrocution spasming the moment the liquid touched those raw electric clamps...much to the pleasure of Amy Campbell, needless to say. Minion lay there in his own little pool of his own misjudgement, as Aimz threw the baseball bat to the floor and stormed out of the room, fuming, but satisfied by her own antics. She'd grabbed her luggage case and locked the door, decided to check out when she got downstairs...apparently intending to find it endlessly amusing when some poor, Spanish maid walked in on a crispy little Imp pimp. After Campbell left, and Minion recovered, he sighed, and turned over in the bed...still trembling painfully from the minor electrocution. ''Oh yeah...she wants me...'' And that was the end of that...it'd be the very last time Amy would walk into her hotel room to pick up he wallet un-armed, that much was certain.
