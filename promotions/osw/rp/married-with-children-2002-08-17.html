Married With Children.

17 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/17/02 As the scene fades in we find ourselves staring at a completely blank screen. There is nothingess. White nothingness. An endless vast spanning in every possible direction. Nothing there, no life, no noise, not a single sound. The silence is broken by an unseen person coughing loudly. Still nothing. The sound of footsteps breaks the new silence and a shadow is seen cast upon the white mystic void. A figure steps into view and we see FSW's Jesse Ramey dressed in a pair of black Nike sandals, some khaki shorts, and a blue silk shirt which hangs open. He looks around at the mysterios white void of nothingness and then reaches into the pocket of his khaki's. He removes a piece of folded white paper and opens it, after staring down at it for a moment he looks back up as a very familar tune kicks in. After a two second pause, Jesse begins to sing. 'Love and Marriage, Love and Marriage, Go together like a horse and carriage, This I tell ya brother, you can't have one without the other. Love and Marriage, Love and Marriage, It's an institute you can't disparage, Ask the local gentry, and they will say is element'ry. Try, try, try to separate them, It's an illusion. Try, try, try and you will only come to this conclusion. Love and Marriage, Love and Marriage, Go together like a horse and carriage, Dad was told by mother You can't have one You can't have none You can't have one without the other.' At the end of the lyrics the music stops and Jesse stands there scratching at his beard as the scene fades away. As the scene reopens from blackness we are shown the interior of a house. The first thing shown in the house is a rather tacky couch. An awkward shade of yellow with little brown designs all over it. Sitting on the middle of the couch is Minion The Imp~!, dressed in a pair of black pants and silky red shirt, his legs crossed at the knee and sitting in his lap is a large red bucket with gold letters on the front which read, 'Cheese Filled Crab Puffs.' Minion's right leg is constantly twitching, moving up and down and up and down as his eyes are glued to the television. 'Oprah! Oprah!' Minion begins to chant at the television, his view never moving from it. The infamously popular talk show comes to a close and the commercials commence. Minion reaches to his left and picks up the remote control, aims it at the tv, and changes the channel to another station which is showing, yes, Oprah. Minion once again becomes so attached to the television that he doesn't notice the door opens. 'Damn shoe store! Damn fat women!....Damn Steve Allen!' The view switches to the doorway where a large Samoan man stands, his brown hair pulled back in a ponytail and his green eyes flaring. He is wearing a pair of brown pants and a light blue shirt tucked in with a brown belt. He slams the door behind him forcefully and strides towards the tacky yellow couch. Timo stops infront of Minion and looks down at him, Timo says not a single word he just emits a grunt sound. 'Yeeugh.' Minion, never looking away from Oprah, scoots down the couch allowing Timo to sit down. Once in a sitting position Timo lets out a groan and slides his right hand into the waist of the brown pants. He stares at the television for a few seconds before turning to Minion. Timo stares at Minion but Minion isn't even paying attention. His eyes are starting to form a coat of glaze from his obsessive watching of Oprah. Some canned laughter is heard as Minion continues to pop Crap Puffs into his mouth not even noticing Timo staring at him. Timo finally speaks. 'Hey Minion, how about making me a sandwich?' Minion hears not a word of it. Everything is zoned out except for Oprah who is discussing male chauvinism. Timo speaks again though Minion doesn't hear. Finally growing tired of this Timo speaks rather loud. 'Come on Min! It's not that hard! Bread-Meat-Bread.' Minion looks away from the television and stares at Timo who is only inches away from his face. Minion continues to stare as he chews on one of the crab puffs, more canned laughter is heard. Minion swallows the crab puff before he speaks. 'Yeah? That's amazing coming from a guy who can't figure out that it's, Lift-Pee-Flush.' Timo's eyes narrow and he stares angrily at Minion who has gone back to watching Oprah. With a sigh Timo stands from the couch and walks towards the kitchen, he stops infront of the refridgarator and pulls it open. He turns back around with a bottle of Girly Girl Beer in hand. He closes the door with his foot as he pops the lid off of the bottle and turns it up. The door opens and Brandi Wilson, dressed in a tight fitting red dress, walks into the house, she closes the door and turns to Timo and Minion jumping up and down excitedly. 'Mom,Dad! I got it! I got the part!' Minion looks away from the television, he presses a button to mute it and looks back to Brandi. He sits the bucket of Crab Puffs to the side before he speaks. 'What part?' 'I'm going to be the after model in one of those before and after commercials. Y'know, the ones where they show a fat woman, then an exercise machine, and then the skinny woman? I'm finally living my dream....' Brandi turns to face the camera and looks off into space, a huge smile spread over her face. 'I have become a lesbian.....' Minion's eyes go wide at the comment from his daughter. He stares at Brandi for a moment before clearing his throat. 'I think you mean a thespian, hunny.' 'I surely hope to God....' chimes in Timo from across the room. 'I've got to go call Karen and tell her. No, actually I'm just going to call her so I can rub it in her face that I got the part and she didn't.' Brandi laughs again and runs up the stairs located behind the couch. As he runs up the steps she runs smack dab into her younger brother, Tyler Valentine, dressed in a pair of khaki pants and a red tee shirt, knocking him into the wall and causing him to drop a large object which was in his hands. Tyler stares at Brandi. 'Watch where you're going. What is it Friday night already?' Brandi smirks and looks down at the object which Tyler dropped, a life sized inflatable woman. Her smirk grows larger as she smiles at Tyler. 'It must be. I see you have your date.' Tyler sneers as Brandi disappears up the stairs. He bends over to pick up the inflatable woman and walks down the stairs. As soon as he reaches the bottom of the stairs Timo meets him. The Samoan Silencer looks down at the inflatable woman and then back up to his son. 'Tyler, we need to have a talk, son.' Timo wraps an arm around Tyler's shoulders and pulls him close. The camera zooms in as Timo looks upward into space while pointing with his hand which holds the bottle of Girly Girl Beer. 'Son, there comes a time in every man's life where he ventures out to become a man. This is accomplished by finding a woman. After finding the woman the man must make her his bride. Most men succeed in this task, however...some fail.' Timo pauses his speech and turns to look at Minion who is staring at the television again. Timo stares evilly and then looks back to Tyler. 'In my case, I had already proven my manhood by scoring four touchdowns in one single football game. But no! I had to venture out to completel the true test. But instead of finding a woman, I found....' Timo looks back to Minion who is still completely oblivious. 'The Creature From The Red Lagoon.' Tyler stares up to Timo taking in every word of wisdom. Timo continues to stare at Minion before he looks back to Tyler, shaking his head. He points down to the inflatable woman. 'You, my first born son, have partially succeeded in that task. Though your woman is still better than mine.....' Timo removes his arm and takes a step back, he then places his free hand on Tyler's left shoulder and smiles at him while nodding. However his tone suddenly changes. 'You little!' Suddenly Timo reaches out and wraps his arms around Tyler's throat, beginning to throttle him. Tyler's eyes bug out and so does his tongue as he struggles under The Samoan Grip of Doom. The shot cuts to show Minion, still watching Oprah and not aware of Timo strangling Tyler behind him. The door opens and Scott Taylor runs into the house. Scott is wearing a pair of denim jeans and a white tee shirt with the female symbol surrounded by a red circle with a line through it on the front. He looks over to Timo. 'Timo! Timo!' Timo lets go of Tyler who has already turned blue, as soon as Timo lets go Tyler falls to the floor. Scott sprints over to Timo and stops to catch his breath before he speaks. Timo looks at his next door neighbor/best friend. 'What is it? Can't you see I'm busy?!' while pointing at the unconscious Tyler. 'This is important, Timo. We need to have a No Ma'am meeting right now! I've already called Bob Rooney and Griff. They'll be over any minute! This is BIG Timo!' The scene fades away to a commercial. What will happen? Will Scott Taylor announce something that will forever change No Ma'am? Stay Tuned!
