Avenging Sir Pimpsalot

2002-03-21

Amy 'Aimz' Campbell

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Note: I'm trying the older style again, just to see which I work best with. This isn't great, but it's a short little idea I got from watching too many rap videos in one morning.) Let's start with a little sing-song... My piiiimp, my pimp... He hit me with a stick. A cane, a whip... He hit me with a brick. He broooke my hip, Now I'm waaalking with a liiimp... When he wants hanky-panky I'll be cutting off his diiiiiiick! With a folded map of Orlando in hand, Aimz was roaming the sidewalk of the heatwave city, Timo not far behind. She obviously wasn't used to the heat of the southern states...wearing a little red tanktop over top of a pair of massive green cargo pants she probably could swim in, while all of the locals around her were wearing spring coats and thick jeans. Silver Sony headphones slung around her neck, she was plodding along, barely even paying attention to her surroundings...looking for somebody she could ask for directions. So many things ran through her mind at once this morning, the main subject being the return of Rent-A-Hero. Jeremy Howard had called her the night before and, after almost an hour, managed to convince her to attend a party being held for RAH's return... She wasn't sure how Rentie would react, nor how she might react to seeing him again, but it was guaranteed to be an interesting time and, just in case, she was making sure to have herself accompanied by Jason Dexter. Still walking, memories of the tag team with the hero were flooding through Amy's mind...but soon enough, everything was interrupted by an accented shout from the alley-way she had just passed... ''Ho! Git yer ass back here, woman!'' What the hell? She quickly spun on heel, turning to find a man around her height dressed in a purple velvet coat, a pair of black leather pants and large, frilled plume hat running after her with a gold-handled cane. So she quickly shouted back at him, stopping. ''Who in God's name are you?'' ''You know who I be! Now where's muh money, biatch?!'' Was the black man's only response, as he grabbed her arm. Amy instinctively pulled away, not knowing who this man was or what in the name of God he was doing. ''What the f*ck're you saying?'' ''Muh money, woman! Gimmie it before 'ah layeth tha smack down if ya know what 'um sayin'!'' She backed away from the odd fellow slowly... ''Look, man...I'm not going to support your drug habits...'' ''Don' make me smack muh bitch up!'' ''Dude...'' Aimz was trying to reason, still with no clue what he was saying. ''...I know addictions can be hard, but I'm not your dealer...'' ''Muh cash money, bling bling BIATCH! I know you's been collectin' tha pay, so gits me muh money!'' ''What money?'' ''Tha moneys you's been collectin' from tha fuckin', ho!'' She was going to run, but turned back immediately at his last comment. ''Whoa, whoa, whoa...did you just call me a whore?'' ''Now we's gettin' somewhere! Git tha cash fo' me!'' ''Man...you're a fucking nutcase!'' The man reached to grab Aimz but, just in the knick of time, Timo Bolamba showed up to step in front of her, speaking to the man... ''I'm the only one pimpin' this biatch,'' He was faking some sort of accent. ''...go gets yer own hoes, boi!'' The guy who was causing Amy grief quickly turned and ran at the words of the large Samoan. ''You alright?'' He inquired, turning to his now incredibly confused tag partner. ''Who the hell was that?'' ''You're Canadian...'' Timo laughed. ''Apparently you don't get many pimps up there, huh?'' ''Ummm...I haven't had much experience with them, contrary to popular beleif...but Canadian pimps are a little more.....uhhh...what's the word?'' She paused a moment, thinking. ''Subtle? Yeah, subtle.'' ''Yeah...instead of ebonics, they're probably walking around screaming ''You don't have my money, eh? What's that all aboot?!'' or something...right?'' He laughed again, nudging her shoulder while they continued on. ''Oh blah,'' Aimz smacked his forearm without power, grinning to herself. ''We're not all like that! I might say 'Eh?' OCCASIONALLY, but I've never heard a Canadian say 'aboot'...honest to God!'' ''Suuuuure,'' 'The Samoan Silencer winked. ''But hey...did Sir Pimpsalot there at least tell you where the TD Waterhouse Arena is?'' ''Nope. That only solidifies my opinion: Pimps are worth shit. I mean, think about it, they don't give directions, and a whore can do just as well standing on a street corner, keeping their hard-earned cash.'' ''You're one screwed up girl, did you know that?'' So their search continued on into the day after that interesting little encounter. The tandem still hadn't found the arena, and it was beginning to wear thin on both their patience...
