The Way Home, part 3

7 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/7/02 Curiosity killed the cat they say, but I've never met a ball of yarn I could resist. Over and over I get tangled up in my own mischief, my easily distracted mind tripping after brightly colored butterflies and missing the hovering vultures. After the trip to Moscow it was hard. Things were falling apart in the fWo. I didn't understand why. It was something about a union, but nothing anyone had talked to me about. No one would have shared anything like that with me anyway. They'd be afraid I would blab it all over the place. Looks like they were wrong. But nonetheless I was ready to work things out, at least I was until I spent a weeks sitting around waiting for the phone to ring, being bored out of my skull. And so curiosity got the better of me. I ordered Ascension. I didn't have anything else to do, and ever since Moscow the thought of Ivan wouldn't leave me alone. I wondered what he was doing, if the fans still liked him, whether or not he and the Red Army and Ed had toppled Jane Reagan and her boy toy, Sebastian Toomes. Curiosity does kill the cat sometimes. ~Rewind, June 23, 2002~ 'Come on Miyu! You're gonna miss the big match!' Tempest called, bouncing up and down on the sofa in her bright pink sweats and a little black half tee. She peered over the edge of the sofa as her friend called back, 'Is that boring opener done yet?' Tempest chuckled and said, 'Oh don't be so mean! Graphic Violence and Rhett Locke put on a fine show. Jane's on, yacking about something, but she's why the mute button was invented.' Tempest rolled her eyes at the owner's image on the screen and dropped her head back in a gesture of resignation. 'Popcorn! I need popcorn to survive this!' Miyuki emerged with a heaping bowl of popcorn and shook her head, tossing her silken black hair over her shoulder. 'You're almost as bad a chow hound as Trouble, you know that?' As if on cue, the little dog appeared, gazing expectantly at the bowl. They both laughed and settled down for the start of the match. Miyuki glanced from Tempest to the screen and back. 'Are you sure about this? I mean about watching it?' she asked. Tempest snorted and said, 'Of course! Miyu, it's only TV, it can hurt me. Besides, don't tell me you haven't been wondering what Ivan's been up to lately.' Miyuki shook her head, 'Not even in the slightest. I've been more interested in what the fWo is up to.' 'Humph, well I'm curious,' Tempest said and stuck out her tongue playfully. They laughed and then turned their attention to the screen where the opening of the match was showing... ~~ Steven: Inside of Mount St. Hells End, Ivan Stanislav and Reno Davis will be encased in the concrete floored, steel walled confines of the mammoth structure where two men enter, and only one man will exit! Nate: The seeds of this rivalry….this hatred, can be traced back to our last Pay Per View, Head on Collision, when Ivan Stanislav battled Switchblade for the OSW World Heavyweight Title… ~~Clips of the Ivan Stanislav/Switchblade match play, ending in Ivan Stanislav looming over a battered and beaten Switchblade.~~ Steven: Ivan Stanislav had Switchblade beaten and was prepared to give the crowd what they wanted, a legitimate champion for the belt….but things were about to take a turn… ~~Still of Ivan Stanislav standing in the ring, watching the infamous 'secret footage' of his match in which he abandoned Tempest. Reno Davis stands on the entryway with the remote control.~~ Nate: Reno Davis played the video, which prompted Stanislav to be confronted by Tempest, who was his then unlikely girlfriend. Steven: She left him, and he got pinned. The ensuing confrontation between the two was anything but pretty… ~~Clip of Ivan trying to console a teary Tempest in which she finally turns and runs away into a car and drives off, leaving Stanislav alone.~~ Robbie: …and the chick moved on to greener pastures!! Nate: After seemingly vanishing to his Arkhangelsk base, Stanislav returned the next week with a vendetta on his hands and soundly destroyed Amy Campbell and set his sights on Reno Davis. Steven: After first denouncing the fans, which Stanislav claimed to fight for, and declaring war on Reno Davis and El Drago, the pain within The Russian Bear pushed him over the edge. Stanislav and Davis clashed on a few occasions, but nothing was resolved… ~~Still of Stanislav throwing a brown bag, which held ZaC in it, against the steel cage.~~ Robbie: …until Ivan decided to fight fire with fire, and take out someone that Reno liked! Needless to say, the little spud couldn’t take a dose of his own medicine! Nate: Ivan and Reno fought one on one for a spot to start at the top of the mountain at Ascension. The physically superior Stanislav made short work of Davis and as he was about to pin him….a face from the past emerged… ~~Clip of the Tempest lookalike, played by Rachel Leigh Cook, plays.~~ Steven: A decoy in the form of a Tempest lookalike caught Ivan’s attention long enough to be pinned, throwing salt in his slowly healed, or perhaps quickly infected wound in his heart. The blows had gone from professional to strictly personal. ~~Clip of Stanislav flipping a car over on a fallen Reno Davis, his maniacal laughter in the background.~~ Nate: Which takes us to a week ago, in which Stanislav laid Davis to waste under over a ton of twisted metal. The camera pans to Mount St. Hells End….. Nate: Now, these two men fight for the ultimate prize: Pride. The wounded heart of Ivan Stanislav against the damaged body of Reno Davis. Now, a man who thinks vengeance will heal his heart goes up against a man who’s personal life has been brutally destroyed and it will all take place within the heart of the monstrosity known as Mount St. Hells End! ~~ Tempest sat silently, her eyes wide and her mouth open slightly. 'I... I had no idea that...' 'Tempest whatever he's doing is his own fault. Don’t you dare start feeling guilty over all that and...' But Tempest wasn't hearing Miyuki's lecture. Her eyes were fixed on the scenes of Ivan's trials. Of particular interest was the run in by Rachel Leigh Cook. 'How could anyone do that Miyu?' Tempest said softly. 'Do what, try to maim a little monkey? I have no idea, but it’s disgu…' 'No, not that,' Tempest said glancing at her sharply. 'I meant how could Reno do that, bring in someone who looks like me to fool Ivan?' Then she was silent a moment and her eyes widened as she looked at Miyuki and said, 'And more to the point, why did it matter? Miyu…' She turned slowly back to the screen as the match began in all its bloody intensity. Her voice quickened and rose in volume, nearly sounding panicked as she said, 'This isn’t how it was supposed to be. Why isn’t he over it? Russia… Russia was what he really loved, and with me gone the trouble would be over.' She winced as Ivan’s head hit the steel of Mount St. Hell’s End. 'He’s not supposed to remember me now. Why is he so angry and sad about it?!' Tears welled up in her eyes as she watched Ivan beat Reno Davis senseless. She seemed to huddle back a little further into the sofa and pulled her knees up to her chest. Gradually horror overtook her expression as the match went on. Miyuki glanced between the screen and her friend and said, 'This is what I was afraid of Tempest. A guy like Ivan is just too dangerous to mess with. This is what happens when he’s really mad. What happens if he gets that mad at you?' 'He is that mad at me,' Tempest said and choked a little. 'He’s just taking it out on Davis. It’s all my fault.' She sobbed softly and then gasped in horror at the end of the match. Ivan lifted a listless and bloody Reno Davis into the air by the scruff of his neck and paraded him around, but his tormented fury was still plainly visible. Tempest fumbled around for the remote and a confused Miyuki found it and handed it to her. She stabbed at the buttons until her shaking fingers found the 'off' button and she tossed the remote away. 'What’s wrong?' Miyuki asked, putting her hand on Tempest’s shoulder. 'I don’t want to see anymore,' Tempest said, shivering. 'I can never go back to him or to the OSW. He’ll kill me.' ~Fast Forward, Today~ 'I’m gonna kill him,' Tempest fumed. Miyuki looked over the edge of her slim framed glasses and said, 'What? You were expecting Ivan to sit idly by while you go up against the scourge of decent womanhood?' Tempest scowled at her and said, 'Yes I was! If he's going to be all mean to me, then he should at least ignore me during my matches, the one time I want him to!' She huffed and folded her arms as Miyuki thought fast and said, 'Well... maybe this is a good sign. Maybe he's warming up a little.' Miyuki grinned hopefully, but Tempest merely rolled her eyes in her direction and said, 'You're fibbing and I can tell. You're just worried about me in the match too.' Miyuki shrugged and looked at her guiltily. 'Of course I am Tempest. You saw... well heard what happened to Aimz.' Tempest nodded and looked at her with a simmering expression as she said, 'And you think I'm gonna be as helpless as Aimz?' Miyuki shook her head and said, 'No, but that's not the point. Once you're handcuffed, you don't have much say in the matter. You keep thinking everything's a fair fight, when it isn't, especially in this case!' Tempest nodded shallowly, fiddling with a pen. The desk they sat at was littered with various contracts and documents, and of course the lava lamp that no office should be without. She sighed and said, 'I suppose so. But then, remember what happened the last time someone tried to handcuff me.' She grinned and Miyuki gulped once. 'I remember.' Tempest nodded and said, 'You should. I'm not some helpless little fluff ball that he's just gonna roll over and have his way with. You and Ivan both forget that! I can be dangerous when I want to, and I don't just mean behind the wheel.' She sighed and said, 'The whole thing stinks though. I don't understand why he's still champ. I would have expected Aimz to pry that belt out of his cold, dead hands by now. But everyone skirts around the whole thing.' Miyuki shook her head very slightly with a look of complete distaste. 'The situation is revolting, that's why. Personally I think they booked the match because they want the belt on someone credible and they know you can get the job done. Here they have a new division they're trying to promote and Corg is its poster child. No one wants to be represented by... that.' 'I suppose you're right,' Tempest said, mulling it over. 'But who can tell who's even doing the booking now. And what else they might have in mind for me? The commish clearly would hope I get hurt in the match. Jane...' She shook her head and said, 'Jane is shrewd, but she just made a big investment in me, so she would want me to win and not get hurt. But this new prez I have no idea about. You able to turn up any news on him?' Miyuki shook her head and said, 'Not a bit. All I've been finding is rumors about Ivan and the fact that he wants to watch out for you. Why do you have such a problem with that if you want to get back together with him?' Miyuki's concerned expression barely hid her motives, but the question was nonetheless real. Tempest sighed and said, 'I don't have a problem with it exactly. But... well this is my first appearance back in the ring! I don't wanna look like someone who needs help. No matter what sort of monster Corg is, he's one I can handle.' There was a long silence and then Miyuki cleared her throat and said, 'It wasn't that long ago you thought Ivan was a monster. You were much more afraid of him then than you are of Corg now.' Tempest looked up at her sadly and said, 'I know. Maybe I'm wrong. I'm starting to get used to the way that feels.' She stared out the window and Miyuki nodded, silently returning to the papers in front of her. What changed between then and now? How can I be so sure I'm doing the right thing? That's the kicker... I'm not. I risked everything to come back here and I'm well aware that it may hurt me in the end. But that can't stop me. I'm tired of running away from the monsters, real or imagined. After sleepless fearful nights and being haunted by nightmares, I have to ask: Are there really any greater monsters outside myself than within? I'm curious. And a match with Corg is nothing if not a golden opportunity to find out. I hope curiosity doesn't kill the cat this time.
