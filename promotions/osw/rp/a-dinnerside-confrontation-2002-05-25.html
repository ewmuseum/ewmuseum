A Dinnerside Confrontation....

25 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-25-02 Alexei Ruslan sat in the ritzy Russian restaurant, surrounded by the big wig politicians, businessmen, and other upper echelon faces known within The Russian Federation. Where they wore tuxedos and high class jewelry, he wore his brown trench coat, hat, and a stare that could cause any of them to fear for their lives. Ruslan brought his arm out and checked his watch. It was 7:01 PM….Ivan was a minute late. “That is not like Ivan Sergeiovich…” muttered Ruslan upon realizing that Ivan was indeed a minute late. He ran his tongue over the front of his teeth, pushing his lip out a bit as he peered up at a larger clock, which said that it was 7:02 PM. There was a commotion within the building as the hulking Ivan Stanislav appeared, wearing his bright red military uniform. Alexei smiled, quite happy to see Ivan as he walked up to the host of the restaurant and waved him by, instantly seeing Alexei sitting at the table, which was built up a bit higher to accommodate his large Comrade. “Comrade Alexei!” boasted Stanislav as he walked into the room, overpowering the string quartet playing in the corner, as well as the talking of the other patrons of the building. Ivan thundered over to the table as Alexei stood and shook his large friends hand, which enveloped his own, “I apologize for my tardiness, I had to handle few things back in town….” Ivan took his hat off and pulled a handkerchief from his pocket and wiped the top off…it was covered with rain. Alexei’s eye quickly caught this clue and looked up at Ivan, “It is, raining outside, Praporschik?” Ivan paused as he pushed the water off of the hat and stared at Alexei, “Why yes..it is….” Ivan swallowed and stared at an unknown object on the ceiling, “It…it is storming quite bad outside….again.” “Did the rain…” Alexei paused and thought for a moment, “…did it, did it prevent you from coming here?” Ivan set his hat on the table and took a seat, his chair creaked under his tremendous weight, “Come Alexei,” responded Ivan, slightly irritated for some unknown reason, “let us not talk of weather!” Stanislav brightened suddenly and brought his arms up above him, “It has been quite a few days since I have talked with you! I have been very busy in Arkhangelsk, and you too, must be quite busy in Moscow, eh?” Alexei nodded and took his seat, “Yes Ivan, things have been very busy to say the least, but then again, when are they not, da?” Ivan laughed and ran his hand through his beard and then stared at his plate, “Yes…when are they not….when are they not?” The reiteration of the last phrase was more of a question, than a statement. Alexei shrugged, “I cannot answer that, Ivan. After all, it is our duty to be busy men! If we were not busy, the world would be perfect, correct?” Alexei grinned at Ivan. Ivan, however, shrugged his shoulders and brought his elbows onto the table and rested his large chin on his hands, “Da…the world would be perfect…..” Alexei breathed in heavily and fixed his tie, “So Ivan, how are things going in Arkhangelsk?” “Things are….well…” replied Ivan, sighing for a moment, “It does get hectic sometimes….” “Yes,” answered his small friend, “I hear that sometimes you are weary up there, in the cold of Arkhangelsk?” “Weary is a strong word, my friend,” answered Ivan, “I would rather say the monotony of it all has begun to get to Ivan.” Ivan paused and looked across the room, “Sometimes, Alexei, I wish to do something different…to do something….uncharacteristic.” Ivan paused and looked back at his friend, “Is there something wrong with that?” Ruslan puffed out his cheeks and leaned back, “Not at all, Praporschik. There is nothing wrong with wanting to do something different!” Alexei and Ivan shared a silent moment as a waiter passed and poured both men glasses of what appeared to be water…or maybe vodka. Alexei nodded to him as he passed and then turned back to Ivan, “Illyana…she says that you are troubled.” Ivan looked at Ruslan for a moment and breathed heavily, his gaze dropping just slightly, “Ah…and the truth of this meeting comes out….” Ivan leaned back in his chair, slightly disappointed. Alexei leaned forward and spoke softer, “Ivan, I am your friend, you know that! I just wish to know if there is anything that I can do to help you.” Ivan stared warily at Alexei for a moment, “I do not know if I should speak. Am I speaking to Agent Alexei Ruslan, or Alexei Ruslan the friend…” Alexei leaned back I his chair, “Ivan, trust me, please. I am your friend, I have always been your friend. Take my word for it…how can I help you?” Ivan crossed his arms and stared at the center of the table, his eyes very still..yet full of some sort of unknown energy, “No Alexei, you cannot help me. No one, save for myself, can help me.” Alexei paused and digested the phrase, “I know about your slightly, unusual behavior, Ivan. Is there anything regarding that that you would like to speak of?” “No Alex Gregorovich,” Stanislav answered flatly, “I do not wish to speak of it. It is not your concern…” “But Ivan, to Russia…” Ivan quickly cut of Ruslan and leaned forward, “No! My concerns are mine alone, and not Russia’s as well!” Ivan shook angrily, knowing what Alexei would have said….that Russia owns him, and thus, his concerns are Russia’s as well. Alexei bit his lower lip, not wanting the conversation to take such a turn. He brought his head back and eyed Ivan, “You know that in my training Ivan, I have learned many areas of not only intelligence, but Psychology as well. Your behavior is not healthy at the moment. Something is causing it…” Ivan sat backwards, “Nothing is causing it save for stress, that is all.” Ivan could not look Alexei in the face and chose to look at his napkin instead. Alexei, however, decided to attack the problem…. “You miss her, Ivan.” Ivan ground his jaw and fidgeted. He scratched the back of his neck and nodded just slightly and murmured, “Yes, I do.” Alexei tilted his head to the side, “Well..what can you do about it, Ivan? She left you….” Ivan interjected, “…because of something outside, Alexei.” He glared at Alexei for a moment, much rage in his eyes. Alexei sat back suddenly, as if to defend himself, “Now Ivan, I had no doing in what ultimately happened.” “I know….but you wanted it, did you not?” Ivan responded coldly, challenging his friend. Alexei fixed his tie, very uncomfortable. “Ivan, I had to take standpoint of what my job called for, and what those above me wished to see. It was not my choice one way or the other…” Alexei answered quickly, squirming like a mouse in a cage for perhaps the first time in his life. “You could not choose between friendship and duty, Alexei?” “Ivan, please….” Alexei seemed to beg, now fully under the pressure of The Russian Bear, “Davis did it, he had the hand in it.” Ivan twisted his head to the side and shook it, “Nyet, I remember, I remember the match and you tried to stop me from getting to her…” “That was because I wanted you to defeat Davis!” Retorted Alexei. “He had already pinned me, Ruslan.” Stanislav shot back. Alexei was silent as he looked for a word. Ivan leaned forward, pain in his eyes. “Alexei….I see her face..her tear streaked face, all the time. The last time that I saw her, she was in pain, and for that I will always remember her as hurt…” Alexei stared at Ivan, his brow furrowed, he felt Ivan’s pain…regardless of his duty, he felt the pain that his dear friend felt. Ivan continued…. “Whenever she talked, I listened to what she had to say, and whenever I talked to her I found myself saying and feeling things that Ivan never knew that he felt.” Ivan paused for a second, questioning if he should be divulging this information, “I laughed, Alexei, I laughed loudly for no apparent reason! And now….I cannot laugh…..I cannot laugh or smile in the way that I could before…” Ivan thought for a moment and swallowed, “She did leave me, Alexei…she did leave me alone. However, it is not solely her fault, and I am aware of this.” Ivan paused and looked around him, struggling with emotions that he had tried to kill, “Why….why can I not love, Alexei?! Why is Ivan Stanislav not allowed to love something? My grandparents must have known love. My parents must have loved one another! Why is it that I am forbidden to love?!” Alexei opened his mouth, but no words came out…. “I know what love is Alexei. At one moment I did not, and then I did. I learned what love was whenever she turned and left me! And they….they forbid it!! They forbid my LOVE!!” Ivan slammed his fist on the table, getting the attention of all those around him as he screamed his final word. Alexei looked around and breathed heavily as Ivan leaned closer to Alexei, talking softly. “She was the only person that…that enjoyed me. She wanted to have fun WITH me, not to appease me or to get information from me, but to make my life better…WITH her! She showed me compassion, trust..and perhaps….at one time, love for me…and no one has ever done that.” Ivan’s jaw shook as he leaned closer to Alexei still, “The circumstances of the past were different, and I should not have left her. The circumstances after the fact were different…and they should not have transpired how they did!” Alexei sighed slowly, “But..they did…” “Yes Alexei, they did,” answered Ivan, “and because of that I must live with it. Reno Davis will pay…he will pay dearly for what happened on that night, but there is more to it than Davis.” Ivan slammed his fist to his chest, over his heart, “There is more conflict than anyone knows. You see, I have learned something, Alexei. I have learned something that they did not wish for Ivan to learn, and now it is too late, for I have!” Alexei looked quizzically at Ivan as he continued, “I have learned the secret, the ultimate secret of Communism…of Capitalism…of it all! Lenin and Trotsky were friends because they together, they made themselves better! Ivan and Tempest…they were individuals…and then….together they were collectivized….as a commune..but…with the power of..of love…they became a collective individual!” Ivan brought his hand up to his brow and thought for a moment and nodded, “Yes, I..I fought for us both..but also..for myself. It was what Lenin always wanted! It was what we have all been fighting for! Do you not understand?!” Alexei breathed in heavily, unsure of this somewhat revolutionary revelation, “I…I do not know Ivan….it is quite…different, no?” Ivan leaned back, “Yes, it is, but it is what I feel.” Alexei paused, “You know, it appears as if you almost have a conspiracy theory, Ivan…” Ivan crossed his arms and leaned back, “Well, you are the one that knows all about that. Is there, Alexei?” Alexei sighed, “Ivan, if you do something like this, many people will be upset. They will not be happy with you. I am not just speaking of the higher ups of Russia, but perhaps the people, wrestlers, many people could frown on you….” Ivan nodded, “Da..but many people would also smile, would they not? And then..Ivan would learn who his true friends are, because they would stand beside Ivan..and not the pencil pushers in Moscow….right?” Ivan glared at Alexei, playing the mind game against the master for once. Alexei fixed his collar and nodded nervously, “But of course, Ivan, but of course.” Alexei did not like the direction of the conversation and decided to turn it slightly, “So anyway, Ivan, what of Reno Davis?” Ivan growled, “I will take care of him. We will stomach working with the whelp on Slam for sole purpose of giving me opportunity to destroy him later on, and that is all. I have also been thinking about what it is that we should do, Alexei, and I have come up with solution.” Ivan paused and rubbed his chin for a moment, “You see, Davis had it right….he attacked me where I could not defend…the heart. And now, I know what I must do. I have destroyed Aimz, I have destroyed his car….but there is still something else far more dear to him….and I know what it is..and I know what to do…” Ivan grinned sadistically as Alexei smiled crookedly as well, “I think I know what you’re talking of, Ivan.” “He will pay for what he did…for if I destroy him I feel that all of my worries will be finished. He is the cause of them, and he will be the end of them. If I can fix him, I will fix myself and perhaps then, things will be much more normal around Arkhangelsk.” Thunder rolled in the background, over all the noise of the room, which prompted Ivan to look up at the ceiling and grab his hat. Alexei quickly interjected, “Perhaps you are right Ivan! Here, let us order our food…” Ivan shook his head, “No Alexei, I must go…” thunder rolled again and Ivan hurriedly got up out of his seat and put his hat on, clearly agitated, “I have something I must do..I did not expect it to occur so soon again…” Alexei stood up, “But Ivan, the food, I had ordered for us already…” Ivan buttoned the top of his jacket and fidgeted with his pocket, “Nyet, I must go, I apologize my friend…I may be able to explain later…” Ivan turned, accidentally knocking his chair over, and rushed out of the building. The thunder boomed again as Alexei stared at the fallen chair. “Ivan Sergeiovich…are you correct?” he muttered to himself, “I do not know…I do not know…” he took a sip of his drink and slumped into his chair.
