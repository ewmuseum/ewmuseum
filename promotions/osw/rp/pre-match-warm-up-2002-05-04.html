Pre-Match Warm-Up

4 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/04/02 Retirement is used a lot around the wrestling world. People retire everyday, but there’s something about wrestling. To Da Westsidaz, wrestling is a drug. They get high on the thrill of being in the ring like they do smoking a pound of grass. But wrestling is a lot like Cocaine too…once you start, you get hooked. You have to take more and more to get the same effects. Wrestle more matches, wrestle in deadlier, bloodier matches. Win more titles, win more matches, hurt more people, get more fans, it all must get bigger and bigger. But think about it again. Like a drug, there’s only so much your body can take. Ed is a prime example. He loved hardcore matches. He loved doing all the extreme stuff. But he can only take too much. After awhile, your body breaks down and you become more fragile and weaker. Da Westsidaz were the same. They wrestled one match too much, too fast and they had to get out. They retired. But like a drug, it’s hard to quit. Sure, if you lock yourself up in a basement, blocked off from even the name of the drug, it doesn’t affect you. Once you see it, smell it, taste it, whatever, just once, the craving is back. Now imagine you ran a nightclub. Imagine that the theme of the nightclub was the drug. If an ex-coke addict had to see Coke every day, every week and every month, he wouldn’t be an ex-coke addict for long, he’d be back to sniffing. The same thing is with the PCW Edge. Da Westsidaz spend the majority of their time in the Edge so they see the drug and want the drug more and more and more. Eventually, the craving is too much. They desire it, they want it and they NEED it. Just like the coke addict, Da Westsidaz eventually succumbed to drug yet again. And already, they’ve spotted the dealer. Da Westsidaz love Primetime Championship Wrestling, but once they realized that Tyler Nelson was in control, they realized that the dealer was bad. The dealer is one of those stingy ones. They give you a little, but they don’t give you what you want to get high. In this case, it was a financial high Da Westsidaz wanted. So in came Jane Reagan. After meetings with her, she turned out to be worse than Nelson. She didn’t give Da Westsidaz enough incentive to buy her drug. Same with Jeremy Howard of RUSH, Boda of NTICW, Alexander Miyagawa of PURE, none of them gave Da Westsidaz what they wanted. None of them had the right mixture down. They cut the drug with the wrong chemicals. Then, Da Westsidaz heard something new… Another man hooked on the drug became a dealer. Ed is one of the drug’s biggest addicts. Then he became in control, so he knows what Da Westsidaz need. He knows exactly what they want. After Jane Reagan was taking out, Ed became in control. Riptah called him to get hooked up the same night. Ed set it up and Da Westsidaz got some more of the drug with a reasonable salary to boot. Then Da Westsidaz struck the ranks of OSW and now have their cross-hairs locked…. ---- “Tonight baby, right here in Montreal! God it’s going to rock! Not only are we back in business thanks to Eddie, we also get to beat up our white rip-offs! Civil D is going to get their asses whupped all over Ca-Na-Da! So how about Rip, how you like our chances?!” The screen switches to a bar in Montreal, Quebec, Canada. Da Westsidaz’ usual pre-match warm-up is taking place. They find a small bar outside of an arena a drink beer and eat chicken wings. Shorty has his face covered in hot sauce, drinking down the spiciness with some Molson Canadian, because as you probably know, it’s hard to find Budweiser in a Canadian Bar. “Chances? Buddy, our chances are off the muthafucking chart! Two little white-boy girls are going to mess us up? Not likely. I mean, this is our first match back! We’ve prepped months for this!” Daisy is sitting beside Riptah, just playing with her hair when she decided to chime in. “Uh, prepped? Riptah, all you’ve done for the last two months is drink and play video games! What kind of preparation is that!” Riptah kisses her and shakes her head. “Daze, you always seem to like blowin’ it for me when I act serious. Why do you gotta do that, eh?!” Daisy snickers at Riptah’s comments and he smiles back at her. He swills down another glass of beer when a small little Quebecois kid comes up to him. “M'excuser le monsieur, est-ce que je peux avoir votre autographe?” says the little kid. Riptah and Short Circuit just stare blankly at him, completely confused by what he just said. Riptah looks to Daisy confused. “What the hell? I thought Canadians spoke English?!” “Rip, this is Quebec. They speak “French” here. I think he just wants an autograph.” “Ah, a’ight. I get it.” Riptah takes out a pen and the child hands him an old PCW Westsidaz poster. Riptah laughs as it shows Da Westsidaz holding up the Tag Titles, standing on a ladder. Riptah gives him his John Hancock. “See ya kid, you better be going to watch us kick some Civil Ass tonight.” “Rip, those two fucking assbags have no chance against us. They said it themselves; they are like our fans or something. They’ll piss their pants when it finally gets through their skulls about who is in the ring with them. Seriously, they can’t expect to stop the Last, Best West! Man, after we win tonight, we’re going to have to throw a welcome back party. The entire Westside, Inc. gang’ll be invited. Then we go after the Tag Champs and take their titles.” Short Circuit wipes his face off and gets up. “Let’s go to the arena already, this amphibian town is starting to get to me.” “SC, don’t say stuff like that. These aren’t REAL French people, their French-Canadian. Good god, just keep your minds on the match. Those two Civil D punks are little cuties, so go easy one them.” “Cute? C’mon woman, doncha talk like that.” “Rip, dun’ worry about. I doubt Daze would see other guys, y’know what they say, once you go black, you don’t ever go back. Well, except Drew Hostyle, he’s living proof just why stereotypes aren’t always fact.” “Hahahaha! Man, that nigga is sad. Anyways, let’s get this party arranged. So after we totally whup some Disobedient Ass, we head back quickly to LA for the week, throw a party, then go back on the road.” A hot, red-headed waitress comes up and SC gives her a credit card. She goes to cash it in and SC looks to Riptah. She comes back and puts the card back on the table and starts to walk back to the counter, although it looks like she pulled up her cocktail kilt a little bit to get someone’s attention. He puts down a $50 tip for the waitress and looks to Riptah. “A’ight, that should make her night. I assume the usual boys’ll show up at the party. You going to invite some nice Project Chicks?” “Dam straight!” Riptah gets up and helps Daisy out of the chair. “Daze, send out the invitations once we get to the arena. This is going to be one helluva party. Of course, it ain’t going to be as fun as punking out Da Whitesidaz. Hell, hopefully Homosexual Cumlust brings there asses to the ring too. We’re going to have a new motto, “<insert name here> Come on Down! You’re the next contestant to get your ass kicked!” Riptah takes off his hooded sweater and hands it Short Circuit. He cracks his knuckles and heads outside. The camera follows the three outside and shows Riptah jump into a huge blue HumVee. He turns it on as Daisy jumps in the passenger seat. Beside the Hummer, Short Circuit hops into the Lamborghini Diablo and revs it, laughing. “You SC?” “Yeah Rip?” “You forgot the punchline.” “Oh yeah!” yells Short Circuit as Riptah rips out of the parking lot. “AWEEEE YEEAHHH! We’re back baby, and back to prove to two Illinoying punks that DA WESTSIDE IS DA BESTSIDE, SUCKA!!!!”
