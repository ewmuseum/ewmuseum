A target has been spotted...

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/22/02 Well, well, well...it's finally come to this. My 'boy', by 'man', my 'homey'...nothing but a punk ass bitch. My life takes a turn for the worst, my world, flipped upside down, my mind...a wreck. And he sees this as an opportunity to talk shit behind my back. But ohh Drew, it wasn't behind my back son, I was standing there listening. Oh the mistakes we make, when we are trying to be cool. Did it make you feel better to mock me? Did it make you feel better to trivialize my life, my past? Did the person on the other end of the line, whoever the hell it was, laugh? Did you get that chuckle you were going for? I sure as hell hope so... Cause it made me feel damn good to hold you up by your neck, to watch the life slowly drain from your face, and to swat away security like flies. Just remember, you're only alive because I allowed you to be, I had total control of your life. It was an amazing feeling, to have a man's life in your hands, and to look on his face, and know that he knew it too. Frankie sits at his kitchen table, in his run down Brooklyn apartment. He sits, eating an apple with a knife, and thinks. He thinks long and hard. The only alliance he's ever made, wrestling or otherwise, has turned on him. The first person he has trusted in a very, very long time, has betrayed him. This caused something to click in Frankie's mind. The indecision, the unstability of the past month has left him. The shrinks say he is still unstable, still crazy. Crazy he may be, but he now is determined, he has direction, he has a goal, or should we say, a target. His driving force in his life...is to destroy Drew Hostyle. So get ready Drew, your life is about to become as bad ass mine... Or should I say, the life I allowed you to have.
