R &R: Reflection and Retribution

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9 - 9 - 2002: A Roleplay Odyssey Ivan turns to see Trashcan Man staring a hole into him, his face a mask of blood. Ivan roars and grabs TCM by the torso, throwing him into the cage, the shockwave shooting jolts of pain through Krayzie! Ivan roars and repeatedly throws TCM into the cage, the entire structure shaking with every throw! Ivan roars and chucks TCM across the outside area, far from the other three competitors. Ruslan rises as Porter slides outside and pins Howard. The two Russians drop and count extremely quick! 1..2….NO! Blitz kicks Porter! Bodies lie everywhere! Dave- This is insane! Ivan and Ruslan are counting fast to end the match, but they’re not counting fast enough!! Jeff- It’s too congested, they’re too close to one another, they can break any attempt at a pin! Steve- I personally like how close they are! Ivan roars and grabs Blitz, throwing him over the top rope into the ring! Stanislav goes to grab Porter, but he slips by and slides into the ring! Ivan roars at TCM, who slides into the ring, and then grins at Krayzie, who lies on the ground in agony. Dave- Ivan regaining order in the only way he knows how! Porter, Blitz, and TCM stand in a triangle and eye each other. All three men extremely tired and put to their physical limit. Ivan and Alexei slide into the ring as Howard gets almost his entire leg out from under the cage, just his ankle and foot remain pinned. Dave- It’s coming down to the wire!! TCM and Blitz charge at Porter, crushing him with a double clothesline! Blitz spins and looks at TCM and throws a wild punch, but TCM blocks it and answers with a punch of his own. He grabs Blitz, and out of nowhere, nails the PYRO DRIVER (Cradle Piledriver) Dave- PYRO DRIVER!! THIS COULD BE IT!! Krayzie finally gets out and gingerly rolls into the ring. Porter stands just in time to see Krayzie, who is still favoring his horribly damaged leg. Porter takes Krayzie down in a double leg takedown, wrenching his injured leg! He flips over Krayzie and slaps on the DISSECTION (Dragon Sleeper with Legs Scissors) Dave- That damaged leg is going to snap!! TCM pins Blitz as Krayzie flails weakly. Ivan and Alexei stare at both men…. Dave- It’s up to the Russians!! Who are they giving the win to?? Ivan looks at TCM and gets ready to count…but stops and looks at Porter, who now has Krayzie virtually unconscious. The two think, realizing that they are about to crown a champion……Ivan contemplates and looks over at Jeff..who waves a Soviet Flag… Dave- Why is he looking at you Jeff? Jeff- Uh…he’s looking at the flag!! Dave- Ivan has to make a decision….let Porter defeat the capitalist….or let the sanitation worker win? Ivan looks at the sickle, which signifies the farmer…and then the hammer, which signifies the worker…and he nods. He looks at Alexei, who nods back. They drop to the mat and count for TCM. 1…..2…….3!!!!!! The crowd erupts into one of the biggest pops in PCW history. Winner and NEW PCW World Heavyweight Champion: Trashcan Man Dave- Trashcan Man wins!!! Ivan picked the worker!!!! Jeff- A communist to the end!! A communist to the end... A communist to the end... A communist to the end... The words which have hung over my brunette head since the nineteenth day of eighth month of the first year of the new millennium. Over a year now it's been. The fate of dealing with the follies of others in the past. The folly of one man...one... thing in particular. Ivan Stanislav. I was what you would have called a gentleman at the time. I still consider myself one, but it appears others don't feel the same. At that time they did however. A real thinker for the public. A positive role model and a person with the charisma to draw the faith of any followers. But then Stanislav's giant ogreish hand fell for the third time, rather than having Jeremy Howard's hand fall thrice, and it all came crashing down. All the doubt came whirling back like a monsoon. All the skeletons came tumbling out of the closet. The whispers echoed in my ears. 'Porter can't win the big one.' ''The Scholar' chokes when it matters.' 'I never saw what the big deal was with him anyway.' 'Didn't he lose to Chuck Manson?' 'Twice?' Heh, yes, it was all there. But now...now the voices have been silenced. Old School Wrestling World Heavyweight Champion. The big one. When it matters. The big deal. The title Chuck Manson doesn't have. Twice. Why? Why is it different now? It all goes back to that fateful eve. It all goes back to me realizing it this honorable by-the-rules nice gentleman bunk wasn't paying off. Then Old School Wrestling opened, and then it happened. The Vax. It was small at first. Like droplets of water. Food poisoning backstage. Falling lights. Locking breaks. But droplets of water are what carved the Grand Canyon. Jane Reagan's kidnapping. The sodomizing of Ed. The Vax. Things had to change. Things had to be different for me, and not to mention my colleagues, to have success. The acts of violence, the kidnapping, the terrorization of OSW...the murder, and everything was different. The Think Tank is in control. I'm OSW World Champion. Albeit comes at a great price, but some prices must be paid. I was pushed past my limit. I was pushed over the edge for the last time. I wasn't going to be a pushover any longer. Had I won the PCW World Title at New Dawning, this never would have happened. I could possibly still be there to this day, or much less have come to OSW right when Jane opened it. But it didn't happen that night, and OSW learned the wrath of the most intellectually advanced men in wrestling the hard way. Yes, Ed would still be alive, Jane Reagan wouldn't still be forced to sleep with one eye open at night due to her constant fear of being kidnapped again. Things would all be different if that one night oh so long ago had ended differently. If one man had made the right decision. Ivan Stanislav. That's right Ivan...you did it all. You killed Ed, you kidnapped and injured Jane, you took out Jesse Ramey, you electrocuted Timo Bolamba and Meanstreak, you blew up Mike Marvelous. It was all because of you. You brought this horrible fate upon OSW with your selfishness, and now you must live with yourself. The blood is on your hands Stanislav. And no amount of pumice could take that blood off. And now there you are. Road to Ruins. Howard Porter versus Ivan Stanislav for the OSW World Heavyweight Title. MY OSW World Heavyweight Title. You will pay for what you did all those months ago. No one will take this belt from me before I have the chance to lock you in the Stroke of Genius and hear that goonish neck of yours snap like a wishbone. A communist to the end... A communist to the end... A communist at the end... A communist at his end... You're finally facing something that's bigger than you Ivan. You are no match for the might of the Tank, that has been proven. The retribution will be mine at Road to Ruins, and no one will get in my way of that happening. I can't let it happen. I won't.
