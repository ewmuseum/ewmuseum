Sneak Preview

19 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Feb. 19, 2002 'Are you sure you're ok?' Miyuki asked, her voice tinged with a note of restrained panic. She sat down next to Tempest, who she sat lounging on a bright red sofa. She lifted her head from the fuzzy white pillow on the back and said, 'Yes I'm fine. Will you stop worrying? It's Ivan that got shot and he seemed not to notice.' She shook her head in disbelief. 'I still can't get over that.' Miyuki looked at her a moment, almost sadly and said, 'And... you didn't freak out?' Tempest seemed to sink a little further into the cushions as she said, 'Well yeah. I just took off running though. So I don't think he noticed. Besides....' she shivered a little, 'why shouldn't I freak out? It's gunfire; normal people get upset by that.' 'Yeah, but since when are you 'normal people'?' Miyuki asked with a chuckle. Tempest smiled and said, 'Agreed. Other than that it was actually pretty fun in a weird way.' Miyuki looked at her strangely and said, 'You're kidding. Tempest, you got shot at, tossed through a wall, chased into a jungle, and then bored to tears whenever your life wasn't in danger. How can you possibly call that fun?!' Tempest chuckled and said, 'Well, you shoulda seen his face after I snuck the hot sauce into his dinner. That alone was worth the trouble!' She rolled sideways and laughed, kicking her heels in the air and said, 'Oh and when he left, he woke up the watch dog downstairs and started screaming in Russian at him. The old guy upstairs, you know the one, the guy who fought in World War II, charged out and chased him around with a toilet plunger.' She laughed uncontrollably and Miyuki just shook her head. 'C'mon, surely you're done with this guy' Miyuki said. 'You've had your fun at his expense, now get out while the getting's good!' Tempest frowned ad sat up as she said, 'Miyuki! That's not nice. I don't have fun at people's expense. Well... she shrugged, 'Not without being nice to them too. But I have to admit, as a whole, that date was far from boring.' Miyuki groaned and said, 'Tempest, that's because he managed to incite a riot!' Tempest looked thoughtful a moment, trying to come up with something and said, 'Well... he makes me laugh.' Miyuki rolled her eyes and leaned toward Tempest as she said, 'Because he's a buffoon! You ran circles around him and tripped him up at every turn, except when he did it himself.' Tempest stood up and started to the kitchen smiling and said, 'Well then it sounds like I need to be nice to him next time, doesn't it?' She stepped out of view as Miyuki rose with a start and squawked, 'Next time?!' She stomped through into the kitchen where Tempest had already fired up a pan of oil and was waiting for it to warm. 'Hey, isn't it neat that that other guy came back to fight with Ivan?! What's his name? Blue Streak?' Tempest asked absently as she juggled a salt and pepper shaker. She stopped just at the stove and sprinkled some of each into the sizzling pan in front of her and Miyuki laughed and pretended to be exasperated as she said, 'No! It's Meanstreak, the Monster they call Meanstreak!' Tempest perked up, and breathed a sigh of relief tat her deft change of subject had worked and said, 'Monster? Wow, so like he and Ivan are sort of a Godzilla meets Mothra kind of thing?' 'Yeah!' said Miyuki. The two looked at each other with an enthusiastic grin and simultaneously squealed, 'COOL!' They laughed as Tempest whipped out a knife and started chopping colorful vegetables at a rapid clip as she said, 'It'll be great fun to watch. And what the heck, I'm sure Ivan could use a little encouragement.' She smiled unconcerned as Miyuki slapped her forehead and said, 'You can't be serious! My god, you have a reputation to uphold. Now is the time to dissociate yourself from that backward, lumbering, conniving, pug ugly throwback!' Tempest looked vaguely annoyed and said, 'But why? I mean is there anything more interesting than an up close and personal view of Godzilla meets Mothra?' 'But you're talking about dating him! That's different!' Miyuki said and leapt to her feet and gesticulated wildly as she squawked, 'Godzilla meets Mothra is meant to be enjoyed from the comfort of your living room, with a big buttery bowl of popcorn, not from in the middle of the valley of the monsters where you can be stomped on!' Tempest winked and said, 'But no monster is fast enough to keep up with a speeding Tempest. So you have nothing to worry about. Besides, I'm beginning to think hanging around here could get very entertaining.'
