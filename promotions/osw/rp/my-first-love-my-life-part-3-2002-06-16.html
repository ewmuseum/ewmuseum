'Love is for the weak' --------------- Her beautiful skin glistened in the moon light, as beads of sweat from every inch of her body. Her eyes remained closed, and her mouth open, allowing the pleasure to course throughout her body. She finally opened her eyes to look deep into the eyes of her lover. He was a big pale man, with long black hair, and a sense of anger on his face. She loved that. She loved the excitement of this man. For he didn't care what anyone said, or did...as long as he was pleased...and in her eyes...that was a turn on. He pumped into her, every second longer, she got closer and closer to climax. She moaned in pleasure, as her lover remained emotionless as he pumped into her harder and harder...just the way she liked it. Rough. He sported nothing but a blank stare, no emotion, no pleasure, no joy, no happiness...just a stone cold face. While she moaned in even more pleasure, feeling his unit hit into her even more. She screamed out in pleasure, as she finally hit climax. She shook in pleasure, as her lover slowly pulled out and laid next to her. He was still silent, and cold. But she was pleased. But noticed that he wasn't very talkative, as usual. It was true he was a strange character, but he usually enjoyed the sex every time. It seemed this time...he just remained silent. She whiped the beads of sweat from her forehead and looked into her lovers eyes. She used one of her free hands to rub the cheek of his face, and the other hand to rest upon his six-pack abs. 'What's wrong baby? You seem bothered by something.' He remained silent, he didn't speak, he didn't move. He remained lying there...no emotion showing in his face. 'C'mon baby, you can tell me. You can tell Krystal anything. You know why?' Still no response. 'Because...I love you' She smiled widely with love in her eyes, as she slowly closed her eyes and leaned in towards her lover. Krystal could feel the warmth of his body coming near, but suddenly...she could feel something else. Krystal's eyes went wide, as her face went into complete shock. She felt her air supply being cut off, but a huge hand that gripped her soft neck. The grip was tight, like a vice, around her neck. She began to slap wildly at the air, while looking into the eyes of her lover. Krystal saw something new in those eyes...something not the same...something that snapped. 'Liar...', he whispered in anger. His vice grip went tighter, as her eyes went wider, coughing wildly for air. Wondering why he was doing this. She tried to bring a sentence from within her, but nothing came out. It was just a mixture of coughing and sputtering. His face was now filled with rage and anger. His eyes were red, and his pale skin was now beginning to flush in pure rage. He stared into her eyes, determined to gain revenge. 'Fucking liar. You don't love me...and you know it. You love me so much, that you slept with Shawn? Is that how much you love me? You love me so much, that the one time that when we hit hard times, you got a good fuck out of someone else...is that it?!?! ANSWER ME YOU BITCH!' She looked at him with a look of fear in her eyes. She tried to break his grip around her neck, but the grip was too strong, and thrived by anger. She tried to answer, but not one word was able to escape her mouth. 'How dare you think that my mind is so weak, that I would believe your constant stories. The stories of friendship with Shawn, the stories of your past with Shawn, the stories of your hatred for Shawn. It was complete and utter mockeries toward my intelligence YOU BITCH. Did you think I was that ignorant not to notice? Maybe I am not smart when it comes to love....but I am smart when it comes to the mind and how it works....and your mind is filled with nothing but lust!', his eyes went wide with rage as he gripped her neck even tighter and finally released the hold...only after he hurled her right into the wall. Krystal's head bounced off the wall, leaving a red stain on the white wallpaper, and leaving a knocked out female. Her neck, red. Her face, blue. Her head, red. Her face, dead. He slowly got up from the bed, and looked down at the whore. The whore who thought she could toy with his mind. The whore who thought he was an ignorant fool, blinded by love. The whore who believed...she was stronger. And no one tested his strength...no one. He slowly kneelt down to Krystal's fallen body, and placed his index and fore finger against the side of her neck. He felt it. The pulse. She was still alive. 'Maybe it's my ignorance of being a new warrior. Or maybe it's my natural pity for women. But for some reason, I feel I must let you live. Maybe to spread the word of my terror....to spread the word of my power...to spread the word of my rage. I, myself, don't know Krystal. But rest-assured...take advantage of this mere kindness....for if you don't...you don't know where you shall find me. Maybe in the future we will meet again....but only if you test me once more ------------- 'The next day, I had found out she had left the wrestling business. I don't know whether she had told people of that night...all I know is that she was afraid. And maybe I wasn't right about her lust-filled cheating. Hell, maybe she didn't cheat on me at all. But that wasn't the point. The point was that love was a weakness. And I was slowly becoming weak because of her. It was HER fault I was once weak, and NO ONE gets away with that. A true warrior destroys anyone who tests his strength...anyone and everyone. That is the law of a true warrior....a law that I live by. Will you see how I prosecute my law? Will you see if another female wishes to test my strength? Only fate knows that answer. I guess, time...will tell' ----------- Maybe it's not that love doesn't find you...maybe it's that you won't allow it to.
