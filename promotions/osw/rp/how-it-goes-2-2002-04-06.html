Date: April 6, 2002 What the hell? Nick left the classroom, walking swiftly down the hallway, and shoving open a door that read 'BOYS' on it. He walked in and took a good look in the mirror. The blonde locks came down over his eyes, but he pushed them to the side to prevent anymore blood from getting on them. Nick turned the right knob, as the cold water started to pour from the deteriorating silver-painted faucet. 'Not good.' Nick whispered to himself. 'Not good at all.' He took at a wad of paper towels out of the broken dispenser, and used them to try and catch the bloods still dripping from his nose about as fast as it was dripping from the faucet. Nick didn't know what the hell happened, it just started bleeding so suddenly. This had started to become a recurring problem, ever since the days of MWW. And you thought you knew him... *** January 21st, 1996 *** One...two....three....four.... Nick laid on his back, with the crowd roaring in the background. He had just been powerbombed off the top rope, and was waiting for the legdrop that was supposed to follow, as madBlack was supposed to be successfully defending his World Heavyweight Championship on this Reality Check. Nick was content for now, as he knew soon enough he would be winning it himself. ...five....six....seven....eight... The lights were shining brightly, as Nick counted them quietly to himself. It was a little known trick in the business, just so you could not give your president an absolute heart attack by having him think you were really knocked out. Because if your lips were counting, it was obvious you were still alive and healthy....well, someone healthy at least. Don't believe it? Look at Shane McMahon's lips after he jumped off the Titantron. However, madBlack wasn't showing up. It was seconds that had turned into minutes into what seemed like hours, as Nick calmly and patiently waited. His breathing started to get more and more back to normal, and he put his hand over his neck as he rolled over to try and make sure he wasn't paralyzed. 'Where in the blue hell is he...' As Angeldust rolled over, he had been looking out of the corner of his eye at the posts where madBlack should have been leaping off of. He searched around the ring, but saw madBlack nowhere. This was getting kind of weird, as he now lost his opponant, which was never really a good thing to have happen when you supposedly knew the outcome of the match already. ANC rolled to all fours, looking around even better towards the announcers table. The crowd was still at the same level they were before, which meant The Dark One wasn't doing anything extraordinary. Not always a bad thing. Nick rose to his knees, and as he got up further onto his feet, he turned around... ...to the taste of a cold steel chair right to his nose.
