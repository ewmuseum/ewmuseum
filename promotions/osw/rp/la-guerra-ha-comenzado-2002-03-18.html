La Guerra Ha Comenzado

18 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/18/02 “Escuche a compañero, usted está en completamente la parte injusta del pueblo. Usted necesita bajar McDonalds pasado, cuelgue una izquierda, entonces usted estará en la arena.” (Listen buddy, you are in completely the wrong part of town. You need to go down past McDonalds, hang a left, then you will be at the arena.) “No man, you don’t understand, we need to get to the arena…you know…Yo Arena…o.” Timo tried to ask. “Usted idiota de f*cking..” (You F*cking Idiot) The scene opened and we find Timo standing in front of his rental car, a 1997 Chevrolet Cavalier, and trying to get directions from a scraggly looking old goat man. Why is he a goat man? Because that’s what he does, he sells goats, and goat related products. The only problem with the situation is that Timo does not speak Spanish, and the Cuban man does not speak English. Inside the car, Timo’s new found friend and tag parter, Amy Campbell is fidgeting. She picks up Timo’s cell phone and dials up a number, giggling as she does. The other end picks up and a young man answers, much to her delight. “Hey Jason” she says. There is an extended pause while she waits for him to finish his sentence, then she starts to giggle again. “One sec man, I need to go ask her what the hell she is doing.” Timo turned and walked to the car. “Oye, ningún problema, yo estoy aquí para su jackass de diversión.” The Cuban retorted. (Hey, no problem, I am here for your amusement jackass.) Timo walked up to the car and tapped on the window, Aimz looked over and smiled, waving at him. She winked once and he rolled his eyes. He knocked on the window again and she turned and let the window down. “Who are you talking to, those cell phone thingies are expensive!” Timo asked. “Oh chill out Timo, I’m just talking to Jason, it isn’t like you aren’t rich or anything.” “Damnit Amy, that’s not the point.” Timo looked over his shoulder as the man started talking to him. “¿Sr. Bolsa de crap, que tal usted regresa aquí y puedo dice usted donde ir tan puedo está activada mi manera?” (Mr. Bag of crap, how about you come back here and I can tell you where to go so I can be on my way?) “Yes, yes, I know this is not the best timing, trust me, I want to know about your day just as bad as you want to tell me about it.” Timo replied. The Cuban points at Aimz and waves his finger while he talks. “¡Oye, dice a la ramera para bajarse del teléfono!” (Hey, tell the bitch to get off the phone!) “Well I don’t really know…I think she is single.” Timo replied t the Cuban. “Do you want me to ask?” The man smiles and nods, thinking he has gotten somewhere with the Samoan finally. “Venga en el hombre, apenas la obtiene lejos el teléfono tan yo puedo ir a casa. Eso todo quiero.) (Come on man, just get her off the phone so I can go home. Thats all I want.) Timo looks at the Cuban man, then back to Aimz, back to the Cuban, then to Aimz. “I’ll ask, but I don’t think she is very keen on the idea sir. You see I think she already has one.” Timo states matter of factly. “Estoy acerca de tres segundos de patear su como compañero. Shit, la prueba para ser agradable a un tipo y la mirada lo que acontece.” (I am about three seconds from kicking your ass buddy. Shit, try to be nice to a guy and look what happens.) Timo stands up and walks over to the man, staring him in the eye as he speaks. The man looks directly on, almost seeming to dare Timo to hit him. “Look sir, I don’t like the tone of that voice. I know Amy might not be the most pure girl in the world, and she does have hundreds of internet bound teenage geeks lusting over her, but that’s no reason to talk your violent voodoo talk like you are.” The Cuban leans forward and growls. “Yo realmente lo odio.” (I really hate you.) Timo scoffs and looks back at Aimz, then to the man. “I don’t think I can handle this anymore. I will warn you, I am a professional…” The man spits at him, making Timo jump back and walk toward the vehicle. Aimz looks up from her conversation and smiles sweetly, not realizing what is going on in the short distance away. “I really don’t think this guy likes you Amy, maybe you should go talk to him.” In the background the man shakes his fist at Timo. “¡Coma shit y muera cerdo!” (Eat shit and die swine!) Aimz looks up and rolls her eyes. “Okay, I have to go Jason, Timo is having about to create an international incident without my supervision. Bye bye sweety.” She hangs the phone up and opens the door, nudging Timo out of the way. The Samoan looks to her and grabs her by the shoulder. “Amy, watch out for his special Camel Spit attack…and don’t listen to him, he has a real silver tongue, in fact I bet he could sell a ketchup popsicle to a lady in a red dress…or was that white?” Timo pondered. “Don’t worry big guy, I can handle any man, no matter what language they speak.” Aimz reassured him. She walked over to the Cuban, much to his delight. The man smiled and slicked back his hair, then pointed at her and whistled. “Oye mamá caliente, quizá usted y yo pueden tomar un paseo en el rollercoaster horizontal.” (Hey hot mama, maybe you and i can take a ride on the horizontal rollercoaster.) “Arrepentido, yo no aprecio el sabor de grasa.” Aimz shot back, surprising the man. (Sorry, I don't like the taste of grease.) The man pointed at Timo, who was leaning against the car, twiddling his thumbs, humming the tune to Gunsmoke. “¿Mis disculpas, él es siempre tal un como?” (My apologies, is he always such an ass?) “¿Yo no realmente seguro, pero él son un tipo realmente dulce, tan si usted piensa aún acerca de harning un cabello en la cabeza, yo hago rasgón sus nueces lejos y lo fuerza a comer ellos, entienda?” she shot back, with fire in her eyes. (I’m not really sure, but he is a really sweet guy, so if you even think about harning a hair on his head, I will rip your nuts off and force you to eat them, understand?) The man backed up and gulped once. He brought his hands up and tried to keep calm as she made the action with her hand of squashing a pair of testicles. “¿Ahora, dónde el infierno no es la arena, y más backtalk, lo obtuvo?” she demanded. (Now, where the hell is the arena, and no more backtalk, got it?) The man looked over to Timo and then back to her. Timo was now standing behind her, towering over her small frame. He balled his fist up and pointed to his guns, all the while Aimz, not noticing he was behind her. The man looked again at the well built Samoan, who was drawing his thumb across his neck and took off in the other direction, running down the street, screaming. “¡Ellos están locos! ¡Y el grande uno huele como pez frito!” he yelled. (They are crazy! And the big one smells like fried fish!) “What did he say! What did he say!” Timo asked. “He said you scared the daylights out of him, and he will never mess with Timo and Aimz again.” She replied, winking. Timo smiled in victory, and held the door for her as she got into the car. He walked to his side of the car and caught a wiff of himself as the wind picked up. “Damn, I kinda smell like fried fish…odd.” Timo got into the car and looked over to Aimz. “Shall we continue?” he asked. “Well, we didn’t get dressed up for nothing Timo.” She repiled. As the scene fades out, the car pulls away, and Timo can be heard as he looks over at her. “Some of us didn’t get dressed up for nothing…you are barely clothed, hell I bet if Tim Walden could see you, the rental car cleaners would be mighty mad right now.” “Just shut up and drive you big lug.”
