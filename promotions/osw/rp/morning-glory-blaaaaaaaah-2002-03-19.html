Morning Glory? Blaaaaaaaah!

19 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 19th, 2002 When I woke up the morning after Slam, every little memory was back... There was Jane Reagan in all her 'glory'...and there I was, standing over a sight three times as glorious; Reagan's limp body, battered in the center of the ring. Timo was there, in the same position over Sebastian Toomes...it was a beautiful thing. We had won a match together...Timo got the clean victory he had wanted all along, and I came out on top for my debut in the biggest federation out there...it was too sweet. I had ACTUALLY managed to get a few good shots in at Sebastian Toomes last night. Yes, the WORLD CHAMPION, Switchy-boy...that was pretty cool. What was cooler, though, was the COMPLETE and utter decimation of Jane Reagan...that whole thing was absolutely PAST sweet. When I opened my eyes, Jason, for a change, wasn't around...which, needless to say, confused me more than a little. Ah, nevermind...I heard someone come in through the door and, soon enough, the shadow of a tall man, about Jason's height, was cast against the morning sun streaming in through the window. ''Morning, Jay.'' Was all I said, stretching my arms up above the covers. No answer. ''Uhhh...Jason?'' ''Nope!'' I heard an instantly-recognizable voice ring out with a slight island accent. ''Even better!'' Timo winked when he stepped around the corner, flirtatious as ever. Then again, I'm not exactly an angel, either...that was proven just in the fact that I never bothered to shoo him out while I rooted around on the floor for a shirt and pants. So what? It's not like he hadn't seen the content of a bra and panties match ever before in his life. Besides, after the Playboy Mansion match back in NTICW, there wasn't anything on me that anybody hadn't seen. ''So...'' I spoke awfully casually, for a girl in such a peculiar situation. But honestly, it didn't seem like Timo even noticed, which was all the better...I don't prance around scant just for attention, most of the time. ''...whatcha' here for?'' ''Just thought I'd see how you were doing this morning...plus I'm taking you to Florida later this afternoon.'' ''Huh?'' ''Next event's in the sunshine state,'' He smiled to me, it became painfully obvious a rib was fast approaching. ''...plus I figured you could use some time to relax, see all the Floridian deadbeats. 'Sides, the winters in Florida kick Canadia's ass any day.'' I chuckled back at him. ''Canada, man.'' ''Canadia, schmanadia...'' ''Can...a...da.'' ''Meh...you say apple, I say tomato...err....uhhhhh....'' Big men were cute when they confused themselves. ''...bah, forget it.'' I finally found my Guess khakis and pulled them up as I snickered. ''Just buy a Japanese slave boy to spell it for you.'' ''Who the hell do you think I am, Bill Gates?'' He scoffed, hopefully it was sarcasm. ''I might be rich, but I can't POSSIBLY afford one o' them fancy JAPANESE boys! Until I get another endorsement deal, I'm only buying Mexicans...and the occasional Canadian.'' ''Ummm...I don't recall Canadians ever being on the white slave market, Timo.'' ''Oh, suuuuuure...at least that's what they tell you...'' It was blatant that the big Samoan was trying to surpress the urge to laugh out loud at his own jokes. ''...but when you least expect it, WHAM!'' He clapped his hands together right in front of my nose as effect while I strapped a studded leather belt on. ''There goes your little brother! And they'll get you too, little girl...all to cover their tracks..........'' I paused a moment, slipping some Metallica t-shirt that was lying on the hotel dresser from the night before over my head. ''Timo...dude, sometimes you're a little too different, creepy even.'' ''Too different? This coming from Miss Tongue Ring...and you're starting to run out of un-punched ear space...I don't think you should be saying ANYTHING about people being different.'' Timo was laughing at me, yet again...not an unusual occurrence lately. I didn't bother to answer, just playfully flipped him the bird, drawing a smile from the big man as I grabbed my copy of 'The News' from inside the local newspaper that roomservice had slipped under the door earlier. A small heading on the front read OSW Confirms Three New Signings and, after skimming it, surprised at the mention of my name, I brought it to Timo's attention. ''Heh...lookie here...'' I beamed, proud as hell. He stepped over, towering behind me to scan the page in my hands. ''What is it?'' ''Listen to this...'' Straightening up, reminded of my mother's constant lectures on posture, I tucked a piece of hair behind my ear to get it out of my face and quoted the snippet I was mentioned in... '' 'OSW officials were quick to release a statement following Slam! that the company had agreed to terms with The Enforcer, Amy 'Aimz' Campbell and Nick Casey.'..........blah blah blah, yadda yadda...............'' ''So...?'' ''Wait, wait...there's more.'' I continued. '' 'Campbell, who made a shocking appearance to team with Timo Bolamba last week on Slam, has previously wrestled under the Aimz in NTICW, is'...'' Had to make sure I put the emphasis on this next piece...''...'one of the most rapidly rising stars in the world of wrestling world.' '' ''Wow! I'm in 'The News' again!!! Oh yeah, and they mentioned your name too...'' He winked, sarcasm was the Samoan's middle name, I'd swear by it. I pouted, even though I sensed the joke and he knew it. ''Not funny...don't be so mean.'' ''By God, you're right...I won't ever get into your pants at this rate!'' ''Cute.'' ''I certainly thought so!'' He winked again, nudging me to sit down with a firm poke in the back. ''...but seriously, here. We've got a match.'' Against each other? I wasn't about to ask, in case he thought it was wishful thinking or something, but I definitely needed to find this out. ''Ummm...what kind?'' ''Toomes...'' Timo snarled at mention of the champion. ''...he requested some kinda handicap match.'' ''Seriously?'' ''Yep...'' The snarl quickly turned into a smile as he explained the stipulation. ''He wants to prove he can beat us without Reagan meddling...but what I don't think he realizes is that, if either of us pin him, I get his shiny little belt.'' ''Whoa, he's seriously that stupid?'' ''Apparently so, he hasn't figured out that the odds are against him yet...his ego's blinding the poor guy.'' ''Damn.'' We sat in silence for a moment, both probably pondering why in the name of God Switchy would request a match that would be this difficult for him. I figured he's probably got a plan...I mean, OSW's World Champ can't POSSIBLY be THIS dumb. I'm pretty sure Timo thought the same, since we almost immediately shared a worried glance, one that I tried to correct. ''Look, you take care of SeaBass or whatever is it you call him, and I'll make sure Reagan keeps her nose outta things. You can handle him, and I can DEFINATELY handle her, so it'll all work out in the end...we already proved that each of us could beat them BOTH last night.'' ''Exactly...you just keep out of harm's way, alright?'' It was obvious that he just felt obliged to say that, trying to show concern...he also seemed to notice that I didn't like being protected, and quickly corrected himself. ''Nah...I know you're a big girl, you can take care of yourself.'' ''Yup yup.'' Until Jason got back, we sat there...idly chatting about basically nothing while Timo complained about his growling stomach in between sentences. We carried on for a while, and eventually ended up leaving Jason to get on Timo's private jet for a three hour trip all the way to Florida... Which reminds me: I'm Canadian...warm weather sucks, and I was about to experience that fact yet again.
