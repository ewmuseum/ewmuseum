Trouble....(Dual Tempest/Alexei rp)

19 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-19-02 It’s not that he didn’t trust her, for she seemed to truly believe in what he was doing. It’s not that he didn’t like her, because she had helped them boost morale and was interesting to be around. It’s not that he was threatened by her, because nobody is able to make him feel uneasy. It’s just the fact that he is always curious…and can’t seem to change it!! The camera fades up outside of Tempest’s apartment room. It appears to be late at night as no one can be seen traversing the area around the apartment, save for one small cleaning lady with a cart of cleaning supplies. We wait for a few minutes and she finishes and rounds a corner. There is another long pause before anything else happens, but sure enough from behind our point of view, a man clad in a brown jacket and hat with dark hair walks and obstructs our view and moves to the door. We cannot see the man’s face, so the camera zooms in on the doorknob from which this individual grabs with a black gloved hand. He tries to turn the knob and it doesn’t yield to him. He sighs, a bit high pitched at that, and reaches into one of his jacket pockets and fishes out a small tool about the size of a pencil. He presses the metallic object to the lock on the door and hits a button…. A faint “shtick” is heard as the man turns his wrist and sure enough, the door silently opens. A chuckle escapes him as he replaces his little item, glad that he had brought it with him. For one moment the individual pauses and looks around. At just the right time the camera pans up to his face as he turns and stares straight in our direction, and sure enough, it’s Alexei Ruslan, comrade of Ivan Stanislav, getting into more mischief than probably is good for him. A crooked grin plays over his face as he walks into the room and silently shuts the door, enveloping himself in the darkness that he is so accustomed to disappearing into. Not even his breath is heard as he reaches into his pocket and pulls out a small red flashlight and turns it on. The flashlight is obviously some special sort as it creates a considerable amount of light for it diminutive size. Under his breath, Alexei mutters, “Well…let us see what we can find…” He directs the beam of light around the room playing over a television surrounded by a circular red couch covered with fuzzy white pillows and a rainbow of small beaded ones. At one end sits a strange glass end table, complement of the coffee table which is made of metal spirals with glass perched atop it. Beyond that was a bookshelf, filled to the top with books and papers stuffed in every crevice. And off to one side in a corner is a whole stand of computers in various states of disrepair, their monitors humming quietly and flashing weird and curious signals. “Hmmm….” Alexei rushes over to the bookshelf, his trench coat flapping behind him and looks at the various books. A wide range of books from old historical books to cooking to storybooks, all jammed in no particular order, clutters the bookshelf. He reads a few titles, 'Blade Runner: or Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep?... I, Tina... The Hitchiker's Guide to the Galaxy...' He shakes his head and mutters, 'Strange girl...' Alexei puts his hands on his hips and thinks for a moment, and then continues on his way. Bringing his hand along the side of the bookshelf, Alexei’s expert fingers feel for any sort of wires, any sort of hidden alcoves in the bookshelf. Anything really. After all, thinks Alexei, we do not really know what her intentions are. I do not want her to hurt Ivan in any way, shape, or form. He pauses and taps his finger on a specific part of the bookshelf and shrugs. “Nothing,” whispers Ruslan, “absolutely nothing.” He shakes his head and directs the flashlight around the room to a closet. Walking over, he opens the closet silently and is suddenly blinded after flashing the light across Tempest's high gloss wardrobe. 'DYAA!' His eyes adjust and he looks around. He wrinkles his nose at the decadent array of shimmering vinyl, iridescent nylon, and neon spandex. “Clothes…clothes….boxes…” Alexei mutters, fumbling through Tempest’s personal boxes. A squeaky stuffed monkey falls on his head. “What a pack rat!” He scoffs. He brings his hand up onto the shelf above her jackets and feels around. He continues to feel around. “Aha!” Alexei exclaims as he pulls out a small shoebox, turns around, and looks in it… A low rolling noise is heard…. THONK! A bowling ball falls from the shelf and nails Ruslan directly in the back of the head. Ruslan lets out a groan and collapses on the floor on top of the shoebox…. As fast as the lights had gone out…they came back on…. Ruslan lies on the floor with a pounding headache. The shoe box lies crushed under him and the vile, vile bowling ball that rendered him unconscious sits directly in front of him. He shakes his head and groans, sitting up slowly and rubbing the back of his head. “Uuhhh…..” Ruslan shakes the cobwebs from his head and looks at his watch… Ten minutes past nine, wonders Ruslan, I got here at nine…must have only been out for few minutes! Good, I can continue…. Alexei hops to his feet agilely only to slump against the wall and grab his head again. He shakes his head and grabs the bowling ball. “Damn, damn, damn! Bowling. What stupid sport.” Whispers Ruslan. He picks up the ball and hefts it back to the shelf and replaces the destroyed box, “She will just have to come to her own conclusions….” Ruslan shuts the closet door and picks up his flashlight. His breathing becomes heavier as his eyes dart around. ‘First rule….never be heard…. Thinks Ruslan as he walks to what appears to be a kitchen door…. Second rule….move with stealth… He pushes open the door…. Third rule and most important…never be detected… Grrrrrr…. Alexei blinks and swallows as the growling of a dog can be heard… Grrrrrrrrrr…… Two beady little eyes can be seen about three feet from Ruslan. He stands completely still, petrified at the fact that some sort of animal is present with him in the room. He swallows again…. “Nice poochy….” Whispers Alexei in the nicest possible tone, “It is just Uncle Alex here…to…to….” The dog takes two steps forward, not yet revealing himself to Alexei. His toenails clack on the floor as he does. Alexei looks around, sweat forming on the sides of his face. GRRRRRRRR…. The growling gets louder. Alexei looses his cool and whimpers. He spins around and tries to run, abandoning his quest. “DYAAA!!!” Just as he turns the dog, who just happens to be Tempests’ little dog: Trouble, that has been well, causing Alexei all this…trouble, emerges into the faint moonlight! Ruslan runs as if a monster was behind him. The yapping dog jumps and bites onto the end of Alexei’s trench coat as it flaps in the air. Normally, a dog the size of Trouble would easily have been tossed through the air from a running Ruslan, but this time Ruslan is caught off guard. He yelps and flies into the air from the sudden weight behind him! His feet shoot into the air, his hat flies from his head, his flashlight shoots into the air, and Ruslan lands HARD on his back, spitting into the air… His feet hit the floor first, and his hat lands on his chest….. WHOOM! The flashlight lands handle down into the crotch of Ruslan, eliciting a weak, defeated, yet all the more painful whimper from the Soviet. Finally, his spit speckles on his face…. “Uuhhhhh……” Ruslan groans and rolls over, clutching his groin and moaning. Suddenly, he feels wet, hot breath on his face….. “Leave me be…beast…..” cries Ruslan as he looks up at Trouble and recoils. The dog recoils as well, surprised at this interesting and funny man that has entered into his home. From the dogs mouth a piece of Alexei’s trench coat dangles. Alexei grabs the flashlight and puts it in his pocket, replaces his hat on his head, and wipes his face. He weakly rises to his feet and puts his hands out in a defensive manner. “Give me back piece of coat, dog…please.” Alexei inches forward, realizing that the dog is not much of a threat. Trouble backs up some more, whimpering. “I said give it back doggie….I need that!!” Yelps Alexei, loosing his cool. Trouble turns and scampers into the kitchen! “NYET!!” Alexei rushes after the dog, limping slightly. Trouble rushes through the kitchen, toenails clicking as he does, and then Alexei’s dress shoes thump behind him…. WHAM! Alexei’s hip nails a chair, knocking him into a cabinet and to the floor. He groans loudly and clambers to his feet… “Little dog..you are killing me….” He groans and chases into the next room which is a small bathroom. After finding no sign of Trouble he rushes into the next room, Tempests’ bedroom. Trouble sits in the corner of the room between a red lacquer dresser and an old fashioned coat tree festooned with feathered things, tail wagging happily and looking at Alexei, cloth still in his mouth. “Give that back..it is mine! Capitalist!!” Alexei pounces, missing Trouble by a hair and crashing into the wall. He spins around, more deliriously than strategically, and swipes at the dog who is a mere three feet from him! Trouble hops back and pants. He drops the cloth on a huge yellow smiley face rug and happily trots away! Alexei pauses and reaches a quivering hand to the piece of his beloved coat. He puts it in his pocket and wearily rises. “It is time to abort this debauchery…” he groans and walks into the kitchen when a door is heard opening…. Alexei stops dead in his tracks as light spills through the dining room of Tempests’ apartment…. Someone is home…. “Troooooouble!!!” Giggles Tempest in her sing song voice…. “Oh my God….” Whispers Alexei. He looks at his watch quickly to see what time it is…. “Ten past nine???! It stopped!! Stupid American watch!” He whispers loudly and slaps the watch. He looks to the left and then to the right and whimpers like a small child, “how long have I been unconscious??” He turns as he hears footsteps coming. “Oh no…no no no no!!” Alexei turns and runs back into Tempests’ bedroom and slides under her bed just as she enters the kitchen and heads towards the bathroom. Alexei curls up in a ball under her large bed, drawing his arms over his face so we can only see his eyes. The door to the bedroom opens as Tempests’ feet clad in the tiniest pair of cowboy boots walk in, covered by the long loose cuffs of her glitter coated jeans. “What did you do today buddy?” questions Tempest as Trouble walks behind her and yaps. The dog’s eyes level directly at Alexei as he sniffs. Alexei shakes his head and squirms back farther till his feet hit the wall behind him. Trouble trots over to the bed and peeks his head under! Thinking quickly, Alexei reaches into his pocket and fishes out a ruble just as Trouble yaps, pushing it into his mouth! “Whatcha doin down there??” Alexei wrestles with the dog to get his ruble back. Tempests’ light blue nail polished hands reach around either side of Trouble and yank him away. Alexei lets go of the ruble, almost knocking Tempest over from the sudden end of tug of war. She reaches down and grabs the ruble, not moving. Trouble trots away again…. “What is this? A ruble??” She pauses for a moment and chuckles, “Guess I'll put it with the Yen, the quarters and the Hong Kong dollars. With Ivan around the change in my couch gets another nationality.' Alexei gasps and covers his mouth…… I am in Tempest's bedroom….if Ivan found out….Alexei’s eyes dart around as this thought penetrates his mind. Tempest walks over to a dresser and pulls her feet out of her boots, wiggling her toes a bit. She opens a drawer and drops a sheer blue camisole on the floor. 'Nah, still too chilly for that.' Next comes a pink thong, a petite deep red bra and finally she discovers what she's looking for. 'Aha!' With PJs in hand she walks into the bathroom, Ruslan’s only form of exit, and shuts the door. He hears her turn on the water and step into the shower. “Rubber ducky…you’re the one….hee hee…” sings Tempest in the shower. Seizing the opportunity, Ruslan crawls out from under the bed and looks around the room. He runs to a window and looks out it…only to find that yes..he indeed is on the tenth floor of his complex. Alexei’s eyes dart around while he thinks… WANG! That’s right! He had told Wang to wait in the car! He should still be there!! Ruslan reaches into his other pocket and fishes out a small phone. He pushes a few buttons and waits. “WANG??” Yelps Ruslan as he listens to Wang answer. “Da! I am still in her room!! How long have I been silent??” “FOUR HOURS???” “DUUUUUUUUUUUUCCCCKKKYYY!!!!” Alexei speaks almost too loudly, but lucky for him Tempest is singing a shrill note, drowning him out. “Wang, I am still here! I am trapped in her bedroom….NO! Not like that! Listen to me, I need a diversion! You have to get her to go outside!! I need to get out of here!” Ruslan pauses, pacing back and forth, “I do not know! Do something!! Anything! You are a soldier!!” Alexei nods, “Make it quick Wang, I don’t have much time!” He snaps the phone shut and looks out the window. Sure enough, a nondescript red van is parked across the street. The door opens and Wang and The Two Round Faced Women exit the vehicle and rush to the building. Wang’ll get me out….he can always be relied on… He ponders this and slaps himself in the forehead. “Alex Gregorovich, you really are in trouble…” He mutters. The water suddenly shuts off! Alexei gasps and dives under the bed again just as the door opens and Tempests’ dripping bare feet sop into the room. Alexei covers his eyes, “Forgive me Ivan, it is not my fault,” whispers Alexei under his breath. Tempest opens another drawer from her dresser and hops on the bed, sitting on it. The springs bounce, slamming into Alexei’s head and knocking the side of his head into the floor! He bites his forearm and whimpers in pain, trying to ignore it as a towel comes into view drying her feet, then a pair of light blue PJ bottoms with cartoon pandas. She slips her feet into them and the bed shift as she wriggles into them. The she hops up, opens the door and exits, walking to the kitchen. Alexei lays there, grabbing the side of his head, and weighs his options. ~DING DONG~ Tempests’ doorbell rings and movement is heard in the kitchen. “Just a minute!!” Yells Tempest as she finishes whatever she’s doing and walks away. Alexei slides out from under the bed and weakly pushes open the bathroom door. He walks gingerly through the room, not wanting to slip on the wet floor. He reaches the other door and listens. “Loyal Communist Party Member Wang!! Two Round Faced Women!! Hey guys!!” Tempest laughs, obviously opening the door. Alexei nods and pushes the door open slowly. He slinks through the kitchen and peers around the corner at the door. Tempest stands with her back to Alexei, facing Wang and The Two Round Faced Women. Wang laughs and claps his hands together, “Hewwo Tempest! We were in awea and thought we would stop by to say….uh..hewwo!” He chuckles as the Two Round Faced Women stare straight ahead. “Great! You guys hungry?!” giggles Tempest. Wang gasps as he notices Alexei and then looks back at Tempest, “Oh no, we ate alweady!” Wang nods quickly. Alexei rushes to the nearest window by the bookshelf and opens it slowly, thankfully with no sound whatsoever. Alexei looks at Wang and points at Tempest and motions to get her out of the room. Wang nods as Tempest looks at him curiously. “So, what awe you doing?” Asks Wang, stupidly. “Uh…I’m cooking. Can't you smell the burning food?” answers Tempest flatly, as if it wasn’t already apparent. Alexei leaps out the window and holds on, crashing stomach first into side of the building. He whimpers and listens to the conversation while pain shoots through his arms. “Weww…we were wandawing if maybe you would wike to….uh….show us awound the buiwding!” “Uh…all right.” Answers Tempest, quite confused and a little suspicious now, “but let me shut this window! There’s a draft.” Alexei looks in our direction and gasps as the window SLAMS on his fingers. “DYYuuuuhhhhh!!” Groans Alex as he lets go and falls in a backwards freefall to his doom…. …or so we think… Alexei crashes onto the end of a rickety fire escape. He lives! “I’m alive….I am alive!!” Alexei laughs stupidly and hops up and down, amazed at his good fortune. He walks the distance of the fire escape, rounding a corner, only to see that it is destroyed and defunct….leaving no means for Alexei to escape except through Tempests’ room…or plummeting to death. Ruslan curls up in a ball and sits, waiting for minutes which seem like hours. Finally, after an eternity, Wang and The Two Round Faced Women exit the building and look up, waving at Alexei. His phone rings….. “Ruslan here!” grumbles Ruslan, “yes Wang, that is me sitting in ball on fire escape.” He pauses and listens to Wang, “No, I cannot get down from here…..” Alexei pauses and listens again, “You have to monitor the door, when she leaves, you must tell me so I can sneak out.” Alexei fidgets for a moment and shivers as the wind blows. “She’s going to sleep!! She had busy day!! She told you this!!?” Squawks Ruslan as he slumps deeper into his coat, “very well, I will remain up here all night if need be…I do not wish to harm Ivan or her….” Alexei shuts the phone off while Wang and The Women climb into the van and wait. “How do you do it Ruslan? How do you do it?” He mutters and rubs the back of his head as the wind blows harsher. The camera pans away, leaving Ruslan to his own misfortune. It’s not that he doesn’t trust Tempest, or that he doesn’t like her, or that he’s threatened by her, it’s just the fact that Alexei Ruslan is an inquisitive person. That….and the fact that wherever he goes, trouble in one form, or another….always follows.
