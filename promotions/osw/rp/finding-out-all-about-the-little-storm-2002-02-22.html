Finding out all about the 'little storm'

22 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-22-02 After the attack against Ivan Stanislav perpetrated by Meanstreak and what has now been called by many of his superiors as the “Congo Incident,” Ivan Stanislav was quick to head back to the icy cold region north of Moscow known as Archangel, or Arkhangelsk. This period of time for Ivan Stanislav has been one of reflection. To not only look at his current situation in the OSW and in the military, but also personally is a task that Ivan Stanislav occasionally undertakes. In times of war, something that Stanislav claims to have constantly been embroiled in, it helps to look back at yourself and see where you stand. Upon his arrival back to Arkhangelsk, Stanislav stayed rather isolated for a while. He did not take any calls from his superiors, claiming that he was busy “creating and executing a sufficient course of action for the future.” The men above him that heard this were pretty sure what was going on, as it translated to, “Ivan is busy, leave me alone.” But while Ivan stayed rather isolated in his office, Alexei Ruslan was much more busy throughout the week. Having a close friend in the upper echelon of the Russian Intelligence was something that Ivan could always use to his advantage, and Alexei was more than happy to carry out any duty that his large comrade needed. What Alexei had to do was something quite simple for a man of his ability, and surely could have been handled by someone less influential than himself. However, for Ivan Stanislav, this was a situation which a close friend had to handle, not just anyone in the bureau. What Stanislav wanted was quite simple: Find out all the information about Tempest that he could, including her whereabouts, her friends, her enemies, her past, her future plans, everything. And naturally, Alexei, being the nosy individual that he is, quickly hopped at the opportunity to flex his muscle. Stanislav himself was not really sure why he was so curious about this girls past. When he found out that he actually had to go out and pay attention to Tempest he dismissed it as a ploy by Jane Reagan to dissuade him and deter him from fighting, but he quickly realized that this Tempest, as scary as it may be, was actually quite genuine in her actions, if perhaps a little confusing, to say the least. Praporschik Stanislav stood in his office in his black shirt, black pants, boots, and red suspenders. He stared out through his window with his broad back to the door to his office, focused on something far, far away. The snow fell lightly from the sky and dusted the ground with a white blanket. Stanislav’s hot breath steamed up the window as he continued to stare with his huge hands on his hips. Yards away, Alexei Ruslan exited an expensive limousine and headed into Ivan’s building, ignored the receptionist, and went straight for his office. Ruslan slinked into the room with relative quiet and watched Ivan, who was oblivious to Alexei’s presence. Alexei leaned up against the wall with a clipboard in his hand and studied Ivan as he continued to stare out the window. Finally, Alexei reached into his pocket and fished out a cigarette and a lighter and with a quiet “fshtick” lit his cigarette, and alerted Ivan to his presence. The Russian Bear didn’t turn, however, for he knew who it was. “What is it Alexei?” Questioned the Soviet Machine, still focused on some unknown object over the tranquil Russian horizon. Ruslan puffed on his cigarette and continued to lean against the wall, suddenly as if in no urgency at all. “Well Praporschik,” Ruslan began, “I have come up with some very interesting information regarding Tempest, just as you have requested.” Stanislav nodded and breathed in deep, causing his huge, broad shoulders to rise slowly and fall. Ruslan continued to speak. “And I think you will find much of it to be very interesting, particularly after your foray into the People’s Republic of the Congo.” Ivan turned and faced Alexei, his face as ironclad and hard as it has ever been before. Alexei nodded and walked to Ivan’s desk and put his clipboard down on the surface of his work station. He puffed on his cigarette and looked at Ivan again, who stood still and waited for the report. Ruslan cleared his throat and picked up the document. “Well, Praporschik, I watched this woman for the majority of the week,” began Alexei, “and I must say that she is an extraordinary woman. My first impression of her is that she seems to have energy that does not subside even for a moment. Psychologically I can gather that she holds herself in very high regard, is confident in her abilities, and is not afraid of a single thing in her life.” Alexei fixed his tie under his jacket and shifted his weight uneasily, catching Ivan’s attention. “Well, almost nothing.” Ivan tilted his head to the side questioningly, prompting Alexei to continue. Ruslan flipped through his documents until he found what he was looking for and began to read. “When Tempest was approximately nine years old, she was walking down the street with her father, a well known doctor that catered to the local poor in Hong Kong, China. As they walked, a number of men approached her father and shot him, killing him. She witnessed the entire thing, actually holding his hand as he fell to the ground.” Alexei looked up from the document and peered at Ivan for a reaction. For Ruslan, this type of thing may not be as horrifying as it sounds for he himself has probably ordered things like this in the past. Ivan furrowed his brow upon the news and ground his jaw. He looked up at Ruslan for a moment and thought, and then nodded to his Comrade. “That is quite a traumatic thing to happen to such a small child, no?” Alexei nodded back, “But of course, Praporschik. That may be contributed to her overall happy demeanor now, for it is a way to defend herself from the hardships of life.” Ivan growled to himself. “And who is it that would do this to a person of the People’s Republic of China?” “It was members of the Chinese Triad, Praporschik. Though their motives were never established.” Ivan thought for a moment. “Go on, Alexei.” Ruslan fumbled through his papers again and picked up where he left off. “Tempest’s father was the only parental figure in her life, and due to the lack of a form of monetary growth, she was forced to live in the streets. While this type of a lifestyle, living in the streets of Hong Kong, was not quite as hospitable as one would like, she survived and actually thrived!” Ivan raised his eyebrows at this news. “Survived? This woman is made of more than I thought! She truly is a fighter...” Ivan put his hands on his hips and listened to Alexei continue his story. “She survived and thrived Praporschik! She became a street fighter in the back area’s of Hong Kong, fighting for food, shelter and money. Throughout this entire time, she fought in the less than proper area’s of the city. While this may not be very good for her, she honed her skills against many, many different types of opponents. However, from what I have gathered, the Triad always had a watchful eye on her.” Ivan shook his head. “What does the Triad have to do with her? Stupid group of fools that think they can overthrow a great communist government. I should march Red Army soldiers into Hong Kong and show them what happens when you go up against a powerful government....” Ivan paused for a second, and then raised another question. “What is it that the Triad wants with this woman?” Alexei shifted his weight and shrugged. “To be honest, Praporschik, I do not know. I am looking into it, but it is even difficult for myself to get information about the deep groups in China. Communist Party Member Wang is keeping an ear out as well in the form of Chinese spies and military intelligence.” Ivan nodded and prompted Alexei to once again continue. “Somehow, throughout the course of all these ordeals, she found professional wrestling as an alternative to her horribly dangerous life. She enjoyed the flamboyant fighters, the bright lights, and the fame, as I am sure you could tell just by being with her.” Ivan chuckled to himself. “You do not even know, Alex....” “Through this, she managed to get small contract in an independent wrestling federation which ultimately made its way over to United States of America...” Ivan shook his head at this news and muttered to himself, “America...BAH! She should have gone to communist group in China and applied for membership in the party!” “Regardless, she made it to United States and was able to rid herself of her rough life and past in the People’s Republic. Through much fighting through smaller, weaker federations she managed to finally get recognized by the fWo federation, in which she eventually became a two time Cruiserweight Title Champion. And then she eventually made it to OSW, and well, met you.” Alexei leafed through his papers some more, looking for another piece of information. As Alexei read the final part to Ivan, a small smile seemed to form on his face, almost of happiness, if that could be considered coming from The Russian Bear. “That is quite amazing, Alexei Gregorovich, I am very impressed by this woman. Not only is she a fighter, but she is also....strong in her beliefs...” Alexei nodded to his large Comrade and finally found what he was looking for. “Now, this Miyuki character that she is frequented with appears to be more like a coach than anything else. She appears to be looking out for her own good, in which she believes that we, are not good for her…” Ivan laughed to himself and pointed to Alexei, “DYAHA! Well then, make her disappear, Alexei!” Alexei stared at Ivan for a moment and shook his head. “Ivan, might I ask you a few questions that may be…slightly personal?” Instantly, Stanislav took a step backwards, as if attacked by something foreign to him. “What do you mean…personal??” Alexei placed the clipboard down on the table and leaned against it, more matter of fact than before. Ivan crossed his arms over his chest in defiance of the question. Alexei stared and studied Ivan for a moment, and then let loose with his question. “Ivan, what do you think of Tempest?” Instantly, The Russian Bear scoffed and turned around, putting his back to Alexei. “What do you mean what do I think of her? HA! She is too silly! Too trivial! She does not understand important things….” Alexei walked around the desk and looked at Ivan questioningly, pulling his talent of interrogation to the forefront. “Too silly for what, Praporschik? Too trivial for what?” Stanislav darted his eyes over to Alexei and breathed heavily. “What are you talking about Alexei Gregorovich? I have told you!” Stanislav growled and turned again, walking over to the Soviet Flag that hung on the wall. Alexei did not move, he merely stood there and looked at Ivan. “You know Praporschik, ever since that little adventure in the Congo, you have been slightly different…..” Stanislav shook his head and continued to stonewall Ruslan. “You’ve had a slight tinge of…energy unlike what I have seen from you before. I think I know what you meant when you said ‘Too silly, too trivial.’” Ruslan walked closer to Ivan. “I think you mean, ‘Too silly…for you. Too trivial…for you.’” Ivan turned and looked at Alexei, quickly answering him. “Listen Alexei, perhaps Ivan had a slight amount of fun on Godforsaken trip, but do not think that I am losing sleep over not having that…walking jabberbox around me!” Ivan took a step toward Alexei, prompting Alexei to take a step backward. “I do not need her silly games and constant headache! I was SHOT because of her!” Alexei took his hat off of his head and rubbed his eyes. “Ivan, I think you had a bit of fun, that is what I am saying! I am not insinuating that perhaps you have major feelings for this woman, but you did have fun! FUN! Even I know the meaning of that word!” At that point, Alexei raised his voice just a bit too high in the presence of Stanislav. With a roar Ivan swung wildly with his fist and pivoted, punching a huge hole through the communist flag behind him and destroying a substantial portion of the wall. As he pulled his hand out, part of the flag was ripped out as well. “That is enough out of you, Alex Ruslan!! You do not know anything about what I consider FUN and what I do not!” Stanislav took three steps toward Alexei. Ruslan didn’t move, however. “I must continue to dominate OSW! I must vanquish those in my way! And I cannot be cluttered with thoughts of some woman!!” Ivan and Alexei stared at one another for a long moment. Finally, Alexei spoke, very silently, almost a whisper. “Ivan, it is okay to….enjoy the company of others….” Ivan roared back. “I do! I am COMMUNIST! I ENJOY THE COLLECTIVE!” Alexei shook his head and slapped Ivan hard in the chest. “Ivan! Listen to me! Even in a collective government, one can be surrounded by one that…that….makes him smile, enjoy time with an individual OF the collective!” Stanislav digested these words for a moment and looked down at Alexei before finally answering, rubbing the side of his head as he did. “Alexei, I thank you for your concern regarding myself, but believe me, the last thing that I need is to deal with a woman like Tempest right now. We have much business to handle that does not include her, let alone bring her into any other facet of our current situation.” Ivan looked at Alexei for a moment, realizing that Alexei was not buying his excuse for a change of the subject. Ivan shrugged and walked over to his closet. He opened the closet and put on a large military jacket. “Come Alexei, it is cold outside, we have other meetings to attend to.” With a shrug, Alexei agreed. “Da, Comrade Praporschik. We have many things to be enlightened of…” Ivan opened the door and let Alexei out. “And try to find more information on this little storm, I would like to find out more about the Triad, and what her affiliation with it is, as well as other things….” Ivan shut the lights off and walked out of the office, as the camera slowly fades to black…..
