When in doubt.......or just piss drunk....(Featuring MINION!)

14 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 14th, 2002 When you stagger into your hotel room, or what you THINK is your hotel room after a long evening of drinking all alone and end up invading what appears to be a purple midget sprawled out on your floor in a bath robe, your immediate reaction is...........? ''What.........the......fuck?'' Light-headed and wobbly, Amy didn't move, even when the imp greeted her with a wide, yellow-toothed grin. She was high on some concoction of alcohol that was mixed to make her fit to be drunk for a week, that much was apparent to little Minion when he hopped up to his feet from the spot where he'd been seated on the cushy, off-color rug. It was then and only then that Aimz regretted having worn a transparent pink mesh shirt, accompanied by a rather skimpy bikini top in the ungodly humidity of that night. Before her, clad as always in his little black robe he sauntered forward towards her, jaws gaping, salivating in wild passion. Minion the Imp had once again infiltrated her room...and her life. ''It's been a while toots.....heyzzies!'' Pipped the wee Imp, wiping his mouth of the drool. She momentarily snapped out of the drunken, confused haze and into reality, leaning the full weight of herself against the door frame. ''Minion...Corg.......now Minion AGAIN?'' Campbell had to pause a moment to mumble ''It only gets worse.'' To herself before turning her attention back to the imp in asking, ''Okay, what this time...anal probing?'' Minion stopped before Amy and took her hand gently. He stroked it with his little stubby thumb and looked lovingly into her eyes...only to be bitchslapped back onto the ground. ''What do you WANT?!'' She shouted, immediately regretting saying it, considering a question prompts a reply. ''Babycakes...don't be like that. Now...you know, Hessian is gone, he's out of here until his arm heals, so I got lots of spare time on my hands to manage, and I thought since you have that li'l title match on Slam!, you could use some help........'' Minion spoke softly, treading carefully on this territory of fire, snatch and intoxication. ''Minion...do imps have testicles?'' ''Excuse me? .......Um....well...it's not quite on topic but...'' Minion stutters....then to Aimz' horror he pulled up his robe and jiggles his little, stark white briefs, Aimz watching as two rather bulbous objects roll around in his groin area. Minion hid them away quickly, watching as Amy gagged and held her stomach in a semi-successful attempt not to regurgitate the alcohol she'd just consumed too early. ''I was GOING to say 'You won't if you keep using those pet names.' but, come to think of it...I'd much rather just keep my hands over here.'' She heaved again, stuffing both shakey hands into the pockets of her knee-length skate shorts. ''Feeling sick? Hey, I'm sure in all honesty you'd prefer that sight to a mental image of me looking down on you and moving horizontally. Like some wise men said....I'll fuckin' fuck you completely! Anyway....yeah, so I had some spare time on my hands, and you need help for this main event!'' Minion giggled as Aimz recoiled slightly, putting her hand over her mouth as she gaggged. Still just barely clinging to her lunch and consciousness, Amy snorted and slid down to the floor, crossing her legs over one another. ''Never even THINK of my name and the word 'Fuck' in the same sentence again or I'll......I'll............'' Shuddering at the chilling recollection of what she just held witness to, she decided to toss the 'Chop your nuts off' finish to that threat out the window. ''.............why the hell do I need YOUR help in this match?'' ''Why?! How can you even ask that? Look at what I did for Hessian by gum! Girl I managed the 2-time NTICW U.S. Champion and former OSW Tag Champ......if I say I can help...I can help! You NEED my help because if I don't help you, Tempest, Lillith and BETHANY YAMATO are going to walk all over you!'' Minion squeaked. ''I've beaten Bethany before...kicked Lillith's ass the other night...Tempest? She's what, shorter than YOU? Heh...I think I've got this handled. Besides,'' Amy sneered at the little imp, the sour expression soon turning into a goofy drunken grin. ''...Reno's all the help I need.'' Minion sneered back confidently. ''Really? Well where is Mr. Reno these days? Last I saw he was watching you getting your ass handed to you and then slashed by Corg...but then I don't see much being only 3'11''.....WELL?!'' ''You're right, you don't see much but height's got nothing to do with it. Let's just say he's been in all the important places...those same important places you 'don't see much' of.'' Okay, so the defense wasn't great...but what would YOU expect from a girl who could barely stand? ''Ahem....who HASN'T been in all your important places?'' Queried the Imp. Amy's face was red...part from the alcohol, part from the fairly controlled rage. ''That all depends...do you want to leave through the window or the door?'' ''Having last time been electrocuted thanks to you using my own electric nipple clamps against me, I'll go through the window, the door, your swingin' lips or through the concrete. WHERE'S THE LOVE?!?! Look I just came here to give you some help, so whaddaya say? All feelings physical and mental aside?'' Minion stands defiant before the redheaded wonder, not scared in the least of her. ''Those 'physical' feelings are about to be heaved up my throat and onto the floor along with a bucket of bile and Bacardi, but fine...just let me sit down.'' ''You're already sitting down.'' The imp raised an eyebrow to her. ''Shut up, that's what I said.'' ''Okay....well.....ladders, what's that all abou-...'' ''Just get to the point and get out.'' Aimz snapped. ''Okay...you, Amy, have the opportunity many do not, a shot at Locke's title. To attain that contendership, you're being forced into a four way predicament with your hopes resting atop a something-or-other foot high ladder. Your mind must be focused....must be sharp as my wit, must be completely 100% dedicated on this match.....and what better way to prepare for the match than....'' Minion pulled something out of his robe and slammed it down before Aimz, much to her surprise. ''......SNAKES AND LADDERS!'' She just sat there, slowly blinking at the game board spread out before her. Minion set out the board and motioned Amy to sit closer. She inched forward slightly, slowly as Minion set the pieces out. ''Now...I'll go first..'' ''Wh-...'' ''DO NOT QUESTION SENSAI!'' He snapped back in a Miyagi-esque tone. He shook the dice, the motion of which is reminiscent to shadow-wanking. Aimz sighed and nearly gagged again as Minion tossed the dice down onto the board. Five, he moved his piece along the board. ''Now, you see that throw? Pure luck. As was your shot at the World Title.'' ''But I worked my wa-...'' ''DO NOT QUESTION SENSAI!'' ''Fine, fine.'' This was all too twisted and hopefully just a hallucination. ''I roll now, Sensai?'' ''Yes...Foo''' Containing herself from throwing the small purple creature through the plate glass window, Amy rolled the die...only to have it stop at one. Drunk's luck, eh? ''Okay, you get one, now, the journey begins to the top. You see? You must takes the chances as they come, the highs with the lows, escalating on your way, working towards that final glory. Ye dig?'' ''But I've been doing that for yea-----'' ''DO NOT QUESTION SENSAI!'' ''HEY! Just a question, but um, wouldn't that have been better help to give as we played?'' Minion furrowed his brow. ''Nope that's it....game over, that's all I wanted to say on that matter.'' Minion suddenly packed up the game and stuffed it back into his robe. He turned back to Amy and reached out at her, holding her by the arms and gently stroking with his little stubby thumbs. ''You see, you're career is like a game of Snakes and Ladders. Chance, luck, Ups, Downs, big throbbing cocks reminiscent of snakes....ladders...this week's mainm event. All you have to do is keep your mind on the game and you'll triumph. Just make sure you're sober on Slam!'' ''That's it?'' ''That's it......now....gizza hug?'' Minion opens his arms and smiles innocently at Aimz, awaiting her reply..... The world went dark to the little guy momentarily as it seemed a lamp shade was shoved over his tiny purple noggin. ''Ah...I get it, don't want me ta' see the goods while you slip into something a little more comfy...'' He squeaked with glee. ''Good plan, sweetcakes...save the best for last! I like the way you think.'' Instead of getting to see ANY goods, or Amy slipping into anything, Minion saw nothing but the parking lot through the top of the shade as he spiraled down from the second story hotel suite towards the hood of a Lincoln Continental. ''E-fucking-NOUGH of this crazy shit...'' Amy'd growled at herself for being pulled into the little imp's game, shortly after smiling at herself for making good on her promise to let him make his exit through the window. ''Need sleep...gotta find Reno.'' The only thing Amy REALLY needed to do, or so it seemed, was find the sober side of herself...one that hadn't shown in weeks.
