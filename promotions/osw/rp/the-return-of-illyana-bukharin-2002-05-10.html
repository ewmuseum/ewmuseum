The Return of Illyana Bukharin

10 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-10-02 Ivan Stanislav had retreated to his Arkhangelsk Base immediately after his short visit to New Jersey in which he found that Tempest was indeed gone. That was the last thread of hope that The Russian Bear had and it was now blatantly clear that she was out there somewhere in the world, and chose for him not to find her. So he did as anyone would do and retreated back to the place where he was safe and secure: Arkhangelsk. The place that served as not only his base and home had given him a chance to relax, believe it or not. He was able to contemplate his future in the military for one, as well as handle other personal matters that he had neglected throughout the past few weeks. At this time, however, Stanislav was staring out his window and admiring the beautiful view of the Arkhangelsk landscape. He caught himself staring out the window a lot in the recent days. He had never noticed how calming it could be to just admire his homeland for a moment. A light snow had dusted the ground earlier that day and gave the ground a glassy look as the wind blew over the snow, shifting it slightly. On the horizon, the sun was fighting a battle of it’s own, however. It struggled to fight through the darkening clouds that surrounded it and covered the rest of the sky, making the visibility of the area considerably darker than it should have been. He turned around and turned on the lamp on his desk, casting a yellow light throughout the room. He looked out the window again as something caught his eyes. He swallowed as a flash lit up far in the distance. It was going to storm. He stared for a bit longer as a knock came softly from the door. He did not bother to turn around but rather responded while staring out the window. “Enter.” Grumbled Stanislav as he saw another flash on the horizon. The sound of the door creaking slithered through the quiet room and shut as Stanislav remained facing away from the entrant. “State your business and what you want…” However, the voice that responded his rather gruff question was a voice that he would not have expected… “I….Ivanushka??” Ivan blinked as the soft, thick feminine voice surrounded him. It was the voice of Illyana Bukharin, a woman of whom Ivan Stanislav had not heard from in quite some time. Those familiar with Stanislav’s’ past may remember Ms. Bukharin as a possible love interest of his that never fully came to fruition. Regardless of what the two would consider each other, it was definitely known that she was a dear friend of The Russian Bear. He swallowed and turned around slowly, “Illyana?” As he turned he saw her, standing there in her beauty. A black leather jumpsuit covered her curvaceous body that she was always known for. Her raven black hair flowed softly behind her with the help of some unknown breeze. Her soft, porcelain skin was unblemished as her dark brown eyes, eyes that were able to swallow men whole if she wanted, gazed questioningly at Stanislav. Ivan swallowed and furrowed his brow, “Illyana..what are you doing here? I..I have not seen you in…” “In a long time, Ivanushka.” She smiled and took a step into the room. In relationship to herself, Stanislav looked brutish, almost barbaric. His voice was gruff and harsh, her voice was thick and rich to the ear. “Yes…yes it has been.” Stanislav answered slowly Illyana smiled slowly and brushed a stray hair out of her face, “And as for what I am doing here, Ivanushka,” she walked over towards a large bookcase and ran a finger along the back of a book and then quickly looked over at him, “I was worried about you.” Ivan blinked, “You were…worried about me?” He straightened and toughened himself, “Why would you be worried about me?! There is nothing about me that one should be concerned!” Illyana chuckled to herself and stared at Ivan as he stood proud. She squinted and whispered more to herself than to anyone else, “Some things never change, eh Ivan?” Ivan blinked and cocked his head to the side, “What was that? I had missed what you said.” To a normal person no one would be able to notice any sort of pain within him, but Illyana knew the tactics of Ivan Stanislav. She nodded and looked over at an empty display case that once held Ivan’s precious “Communist Manifesto.” “Nothing,” she turned and looked at him and smiled, “Ivan.” She walked across the room to Ivan who still stood tall and proud, “What happened to your book? I know your father had given that to you.” Ivan swallowed and looked at the floor, “I lost it somewhere…and am unable to get it back.” He turned and walked away from her. Illyana nodded to herself as Ivan turned. She studied his face. His angular features. “You are sure there is nothing troubling you, Praporschik?” she questioned again. He shrugged. “Why would you think there is something wrong, Illyana?” He looked back at her and blinked. Illyana took another step closer to Ivan, cornering him in the office. She looked up into his eyes, “I see..Ivanushka…I see sadness in your eyes.” Ivan quickly looked at the floor, but she continued her assault, “I see disappointment and pain when you look at things that you hold dear to you.” Ivan turns suddenly, “I think not! I have not changed!” Illyana crossed her arms and took a step backward, “Ivan, I know you better than almost anybody. I..I know what happened between you and Tempest.” “What happened is over and it is dead. I have learned that, Illyana!” He turned and clutched his hands into fists, “I have moved past it and I have forgotten about it!” He stomped past her towards his door. “Ivan Sergeiovich Stanislav!” Illyana barked, prompting Stanislav to stop in his tracks, “If there is one thing that I know about you, it is that you do not forget the past!” Ivan did not turn, “Ivan Stanislav is a man that cherishes the past, a man that learns from the past, and a man that truly lets the past have the effect that it should have to all those that are affected, good or bad!!” Ivan’s head dropped slightly as Illyana shook with emotion. “I know it is bothering you Ivan. I know that it hurts inside. If you want to talk to me Ivan, you can.” Ivan did not move, but rather kept his broad back facing her. “Turn around, Ivan.” Ivan did not move. “Ivanushka, turn around and face me.” Ivan sighed heavily and turned. He looked up at her slowly. She smiled and took a step closer to Ivan. “Ivan, I care about you, you know that. I just want you to be content and not at war with yourself.” She took another step closer to The Russian Bear and paused. “Now, are you okay?” Ivan swallowed loudly and shook his head, “At time I am fine. At other times, I am hurt, Illyana. At times I wish to knock down the walls, and at other times I wish to scream to the sky in rage. Sometimes, I wish to sit alone and contemplate things…..” Illyana smiled at the opening up of Stanislav and rushed forward to hug him. “Oh Ivan….” Suddenly, as she neared, Stanislav recoiled in an almost terrified manner and backed away, literally going to the opposite end of the room and turning, “GET AWAY! DO NOT TOUCH ME!!!” Illyana froze, stunned, as Ivan huddled against the wall, “Ivan..what..what is the matter?” “Do not, do not get close to me….” He growled. “Why?” Ivan turned and shook, “The people that get close to me….get hurt in ways that they should not! It is my duty to myself and those around me to..to protect them.” Illyana frowned and tilted her head, “Oh Ivan, no, do not do that…” She took a step forward, prompting Ivan to shoot his hand out, “NO!” She stopped and swallowed. “Ivan…turn around and look at me.” Ivan turned and looked upon the beautiful Russian woman. “Ivan, you are a man unlike any other. You are honorable, honest, passionate, intelligent, and to those that you care about, harmless.” She paused and licked her lips, “You cannot hurt me, Ivan…” “YES I CAN!!” He bellowed, “I am a soldier! I am a man that was created to kill others! To create war and destruction! That is who I am! I am not a happy man! I am not a compassionate man, Illyana!” Ivan roared and seemed to grow in size with his sudden anger, “I make people cry Illyana, and I get PAID to do it! I take pride in my power! I take pride in my dominance!!” Ivan shook as his forehead quivered. “I tried to change and I failed, and because of it I hurt myself and never again will I do that! I killed what we had and have buried it deep within the pit of my stomach!” Illyana shook her head slowly, “No you are not Ivan. For if you were, you would not have won my heart.” Ivan paused suddenly and furrowed his brow. His rage was deflated, his anger was diffused, “Y…your..heart?” Illyana smiled and nodded, “Yes Ivan, my heart. Since we were young soldiers I have always admired you. You have such control over the people, you instill so much hope within them. And you are compassionate towards them and those that you care about. And you have always been nothing but the complete gentleman towards me.” She grinned and slowly walked across the room towards him, careful not to spook him. Stanislav stood still as he listened to her words, entranced by her beautiful brown eyes. “The thing about you Ivan, is that you are true to the people. You are true to those that you have sworn to protect…” she whispered as she continued to walk slowly towards the hulking Stanislav. “You work so hard for what you think is right, you stand so firmly for what you believe in….I admire that.” Ivan backed against the wall as Illyana continued to slowly walk towards him, closing the distance to a mere foot. She reached out slowly and ran a finger down the side of his cheek, but Ivan did not move, he merely stood stock still. “And that….Ivan…….is why you have won my heart…..” she paused and pulled her finger away from Ivan’s cheek, “and if you so wish, I can have yours too….” Illyana craned her neck up to kiss Stanislav, but Ivan did not move. She closed her eyes in expectancy for Ivan’s move, but it did not come. Ivan breathed heavily as he looked down upon her. Her beautiful face, her soothing words, her hair, and then, he replied. “Illyana….I….I cannot…for my heart….whether I want it or not….belongs to someone else.” Ivan ground his jaw as Illyana opened her eyes and blinked. She swallowed, stunned. “I see….Ivan.” She blinked and rubbed the back of her neck. The two of them stood for moment in complete silence, until Illyana spoke once more, some pain in her voice, “I think..I think I am going to go Ivan.” She turned and walked to the door and stopped. She turned and smiled at Ivan, who still stood against the wall. “It has always been easy for me to find a man, Ivan, I will not mislead you. Many men long to be with me because I am beautiful and strong and politically powerful. I live a life in which the opportunity to be appreciated by someone personally is commonplace.” She paused and looked down at the floor, and then up at Ivan again, “But you Ivan. It is difficult for a woman to learn about who you truly are. You live a life in which you are alone, and yet, surrounded by people at the same time. There once was a time when I thought that Illyana Stanislav might have been a possibility, but perhaps I am wrong.” Ivan took a step forward, “Illyana…” “Shhhh, Ivan, let me finish.” Illyana interjected, “For someone to have hurt you and you not to destroy them is amazing, Ivanushka. For someone to have gotten so close to you and recognized who you really are is admirable and I know that your heart is not something that can easily be healed. I respect your decision Ivan, even if it may be more painful one to decide.” She paused and sighed loudly, “I have to go Ivan. Do take care of yourself and remember, I am always here for you.” “Illyana!” Ivan said as she turned and stopped, “she did hurt me..and she probably will not ever have me again. But…I..I cannot attempt what I did with her with anyone else, it is too difficult, and too painful. I know my place now.” “Do you, Ivanushka?” answered Illyana, some slight malice in her words. “I have given all that my heart could give and it was not enough. No other woman, save for perhaps you, would ever wish to come near me and I will not put you through the torture of living with a soldier such as myself. And while she may not wish to see me ever again….Illyana….Tempest has my heart..for it broke with her. Please understand.” Illyana nodded, “I understand Ivanushka. Goodbye.” Ivan blinked, somewhat confused as Illyana opened the door quickly and exited. Stanislav stood there for a moment and stared at the door and then walked back to his desk and sat down. Ivan Stanislav could see the future, and it was painted with the blood of Reno Davis. It eclipsed his memories of Tempest and the past few weeks and welded itself into his mind. Saturday Slam was a mere day away and on that day, Reno Davis would be his. What Stanislav did not see was that when Illyana exited the room she propped herself against the door on the other side. She leaned against the door for a moment, alone in the hallway. She thought about what Ivan had said, how this woman, this outsider had actually made him happy, and strangely enough she was happy for him too. But too, she thought of the realization that the happiness was not with her, but with Tempest. She looked down at the floor, her beautiful black hair falling beside her face, and tears began to flow down the sides of her face. Illyana Bukharin breathed in deep and quelled the tears, her face still as unblemished and beautiful as ever, and walked down the empty hallway.
