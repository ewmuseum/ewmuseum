First Class Conversation

21 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-21-02 “A Weapon of Choice Elimination match. Un-fucking-believable...” Seth Master complains, looking disgustedly at the piece of paper he holds in his hand. Apparently it lists the card for the upcoming Ascension pay-per-view. He shakes his head in disbelief, smacking the sheet with his opposite hand. “Who the fuck gave their okay to this stupid ass thing?” “I dunno,” Trevor replies with a shrug. “Probably that dicksmack Porter. That would be my guess, at least.” “I repeat,” Seth says, crumpling up the paper and tossing it backward over his head. He cranes his head just in time to see it smack into the head of a middle-aged man a few rows back. As the man stands up to glare around at possible culprits, a half hearted smile comes Seth as he turns to face his partner. “Un-fucking-believable.” The two members of Civil Disobedience, the newly proclaimed most-hated team in OSW, are currently sitting in the first class section of a high-priced jet en route to Los Angeles. It seems an odd setting for the duo, who for the past five months have been forced to cart themselves from show to show in their broken down Chevrolet Celebrity. It seems as if they’ve taken quite the step up. Cross country travel in a car with no air-conditioning and a broken muffler in the middle of summer is a fate that should be wished on no man. How did they make this upgrade, you may ask? Well, let’s just say that allying with Jane Reagan has its perks. She does have indirect control over the OSW travel department, after all. Not to mention the payroll department, which means that Seth and Trevor’s financial problems have more or less...disappeared. Which means the only problems that remain are work-related. At least the most pressing ones. And the most disturbing. “How the hell is it legal for those gothic fucks to bring barbed wire gloves into that match-up?” Seth remarks disgustedly. “I mean, that’s gotta be illegal.” “Sadly, no,” Trevor responds, shrugging his shoulders. “The contracts we signed back in January clear us for any match the booking staff may decide to put us in.” “Come on! There’s got to be something we can do, here. Bats are one thing, but barbed wire is a whole other ball game.” “Personally I wouldn’t want to be hit in the head with a bat, either...or anything else, as far as that goes,” Trevor states, a bit sarcastically. “But that barbed wire has no other purpose but to shred our skin,” Seth exclaims, a fearful expression coming over his face as he runs his hand over it. “It’s easy for them to bring that shit into the match. Hessian and Cain, they’re already ugly beyond description. They run no risk. But me....I’m far too attractive to become a scarred freak like them.” “Well what do you want me to do, Seth?” Trevor responds, sounding frustrated. “I’ve already talked to Jane about it. There’s nothing she can do about it. The contract is rock solid. And if the boss lady can’t fix something, it isn’t going to get fixed.” “Well...” Seth begins, but trails off, unable to finish his sentence. “Fuck.” “Hey, look at the bright side,” Trevor says, raising his eyebrow in a hopeful gesture. “She did book Da Westsidaz versus Heresian Bloodlust the night before the PPV. They’re going to soften each other up, killing each other over those damn belts, and then the next day we’ll go in for the kill on our weakened prey.” “Yeah, at least we got that working for us...” “And if we work through this thing effectively, we might not even suffer any permanent scars,” Trevor offers, though for some reason this fails to inspire relief in Seth. “That’s a big might,” Seth mutters, shaking his head doubtfully. “Barbed wire. Phhh. Why the hell would those sick fucks bring that shit into the match to begin with?” “You don’t know? Well, I think it’s pretty obvious,” Trevor remarks, nodding his head thoughtfully. “I’m sorry I’m not as omniscient as you, Trev,” Seth laughs. “Do tell.” “Let’s think through this, shall we?” “Why the hell not,” Seth shrugs. “It’s not like I have anything better to do.” “Alright, then,” Trevor says, rubbing his hands together excitedly, looking eager to get explaining. “Hessian and Cain have somewhat of an affinity for barbed wire, wouldn’t you say?” “Yeah, I guess.” “And what exactly is barbed wire used for?” “I believe its use originated in the late eighteen hundreds, during the settling of the American west. It’s principle function was to corral grazing livestock, as to not let them stray from their owner’s property,” Seth answers in a very scholarly manner. “Indeed it did,” Trevor nods. “Do you see where we’re going with this?” “No...” Seth shakes his head, looking confused. “It looks as if I’m going to have to spell it out for you,” Trevor says with a sigh. “Apparently so.” “Alright, check it out,” Trevor says, ticking off points on his fingers. “Hessian and Cain like barbed wire...” Seth nods, as Trevor continues. “Which is used to corral livestock...” Seth nods again. “Which means...” Trevor trails off, looking expectantly at his partner. “Hessian and Cain enjoy fucking livestock!” Seth exclaims, an understanding expression finally coming over his face. “Exactly!” Trevor beams, looking thrilled that Seth reasoned it out. “My God! It’s all so simple!” Seth laughs. “I thought so,” Trevor says with a nod. “Sheepfuckers! They’re sheepfuckers!” Seth shouts, drawing odd stares from the rich patrons surrounding him. “I know,” Trevor nods again. “Gross,” Seth remarks, a look of disgust coming over his face. “Well duh,” Trevor states, as a small revolted shudder courses through him. “Maybe if we’re lucky, those Westside dicksmacks will knock the cow-screwing perverts unconscious before we even hit the ring.” “That would be a rather lucky break for us, especially considering Riptah and Short Circuit will be easy pickings at that point...or any point, for that matter,” Seth adds confidently. “They sure do suck balls,” Trevor says, almost as if it were fact. “Indeed,” Seth agrees. The two men stare at each other for a few seconds, an awkward silence taking over. “Well, Seth, I have to say this conversation has been simply grand, but I believe it has run its course,” Trevor says, standing up and slapping his partner on the shoulder. “If anyone asks for me, I’ll be in coach trying to solicit the common women into aiding me in attaining my membership to the Mile High Club.” “Good luck with that,” Seth says, looking up at Trevor as he makes his way toward the back. Trevor pauses at the curtain separating the two sections and looks back at Seth. “And good luck to you, with your...” “Eh, I think I’m just going to sit here and look at porn,” Seth interrupts, holding up a magazine and waving it so Trevor can see. “Ah.” “Look, Trev...Playboy!” Seth shouts back happily. “I can finally afford Playboy. No more bargain bin smut for me!” “We are certainly living the high life, old chap,” Trevor yells back, for talking like English gentleman when you are far from it is always good for a laugh or two. “Hopefully we can secure some gold by the week’s end to make things even higher.” “Indubitably,” Seth answers with a laugh. “See ya later,” Trevor says, waving one last time before disappearing to the back. “Later, man,” Seth shouts back, before turning his attention to the porn. Yes indeed, the more things change, the more they stay the same. Whatever the fuck that means. Heh. FADE OUT
