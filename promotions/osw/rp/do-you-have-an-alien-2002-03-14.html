Do YOU Have an Alien?

14 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-14-02 The sun hangs low in the western sky, heralding the coming of the night, and Timo Bolamba, the resident Samoan Ass Kicking Machine sits alone by a lake in Southern Florida, fishing. He is sitting along side a tree, actually propped against it, with the pole in one hand, a beer in the other, and a straw hat one, covering his face. Timo sleeps in the warm dusk barely noticing as the sun goes down. He yawns a bit and stretches, his muscular frame creaking a bit after his beating on Sunday. The Samoan flipped the straw hat up slowly and smacked his lips a few times after another yawn. (Hmm, it’s getting to be dark, I should probably head in for the night. Actually, it is starting to get a bit cold.) The Samoan reeled his line in and set the hook in a rod eye. He got up and turned to walk away, but tripped over a gnarled old tree root that stuck out of the ground. Though he flailed his arms wildly, it seems as thought nothing could stop the Samoan from hitting, and hitting hard. The world around him became black, and as his eyes closed, the last thing he could remember was seeing a bright flash before him. Timo knew no more as he let out a peaceful sigh. “Hey Jackass, wake up.” “Grog.” “Dammit buddy, this aint no free ride you know!” Timo’s eyes shot open and he was flying over the southern Florida landscape, many hundred feat over the ground. He begins to freak out, trying to get a grip on reality, but nothing seems to be keeping that in check now. “Hey Mack, you wanna quit it? It aint exactly easy to lug your fat ass all over the place!” Timo looks over his shoulder, and sees nothing, so he checks the other one, and still sees nothing. There is a bright light surrounding him however, and to be frank, it fr4eaked the hell out of the Samoan. “Put me down!” He yelled. “Look buddy, we are two hundred feet in the air, that’s the last thing you want right now.” Timo looked down and then around, then tapped his head a few times. (You certainly have lost it this time old friend. And they said you were nuts when you had the fiasco with the computer…and the dream about Star Trek….and the dream about Smokey and The Bandit…not to mention the Emeril Incident, or Willy Wonka. But this time Timo, you really have lost it.) “Oh get off yer horse, you aint crazy…well technically you are, but this is real mack. Look, I’m gonna explain this to you once we get to the ground.” “Who are you?” Timo asked. “Let’s just say I’m your designated driver.” “But I didn’t drink anything tonight!” He shot back. “Whatever rummy, they all say the same thing: Hey, lookee here, I know when I’m drunk, and I don’t have enough blood in my alcohol stream to be drunk, so just fly away and play with the other pixies.” Timo made a strange face and looked up again. He still couldn’t see the figure behind him, but he knew something strange was afoot. The Samoan sat silent, as they flew through the air. He thought of something and looked up again. “Where are we going?” “Detroit man, that’s where you live right?” “Wow can you read my mind?” Timo asked again. “Hell no, you are wearing a KISS shirt that says Detroit Rock City! You think I am some sort of genius buddy?” Timo thinks about his answer for a minute. “Well technically we are flying, that isn’t really something just anybody can do.” Timo had grown impatient by now and he wanted answers. This game had gone on far enough, it was time to find out what this guys game was. “Okay, let’s just land and sort this out okay?” Tim said to the voice. “You really want to do that? Most people can’t really handle the flying, let alone knowing what I am.” “Yeah, lets just land, maybe we can talk this over or something, because right not, I am feeling a little weird about the whole situation. It isn’t every day that some weird guy flies me all over gods creation after I have been fishing.” He states. Suddenly they drop down rapidly, coming to the ground almost at a deathly quick pace, but whatever has been holding Timo in the air stops rapidly and almost as if they were floating, puts him down gently. It was now time to find out exactly who or what this thing was. “Okay now, I want a full explaaaaaa….what the hell?’ As the creature came into full view it was a small green man, about a foot and a half in height, with a cigar hanging out his mouth, a small Unical 76 hat, a wife beater, and a pair of blue jeans. He looked up at Timo and flicked the ashes off his cigar. “Now I be done seeing bout everything…” Timo proclaimed. “Hell no you aint, you ever see an elephant fly?” the wee man asked. Timo shook his head ‘no’ in amazement still. “Well it aint all that impressive. Anyhow, you wanted to ask me some questions, get to it, I got a long fly ahead of me.” Timo sat down on a stump, looking the small strange man over, still not believing this fully. He looked down and pinched himself, and no he wasn’t dreaming. “So, what are you a fairy or something?” Timo asked. “Hell no! Your grabass buddy SeaBass, now that is a fairy! I’m an alien you jackass!” the small man replied. “Aww shit…I knew I shouldn’t have ate that burrito last night…” Timo speculated. The small man looked up and tapped his foot impatiently. “We about done here? You saw what you wanted to see, now you are freaking out accordingly, its pretty mathematical what happens after this bub. You pass out, and I carry you back to wherever you live. I will splash a little Windsor Canadian on you and slap you silly, then when you get home, nobody will believe you. It’s pretty much how this game goes, and I would like to get home tonight mack, so can we go?” Timo looked up and smiled, this was certainly something he had not anticipated. “But what if I don’t pass out? What if I were to…” He lunges forward and takes the wee man by surprise grabbing him by the legs and smiling. “WHOO! I GOT THE WISH!!” Timo yells out. “Damnit you moron! I aint no damned Leprechaun, let me go!!!” Timo stopped dancing momentarily, and looked the wee man in the eyes. “Hey, tell you what, if you tell me your name, I will let you go. My name is Timo.” The small man sighed and extended his hand. “The name is Chief. It’s good to meet you I guess.” Timo smiled and thought about it, a real live alien to talk to. This ruled! Anyone in PTC can have a monkey or pair of robots, or even be a drunken Irishman, but who had a friggin alien to talk to? NOBODY! “Hey buddy, this is a nice bonding moment and all but….PUT ME DOWN JACKASS!” he screamed. Tim chuckled and turned him over onto his feet. Chief walked over and grabbed his hat off the ground and sat on a rock. Timo leaned forward and nodded a bit. “So what is your story?” Timo asked. “Well, let me tell you about where I come from…….” The scene fades out as they talk to one another. It would seem, for the time, that Timo had a new friend. Would this stop him from getting some revenge on SeaBass and Super Bitch though? Time would only tell.
