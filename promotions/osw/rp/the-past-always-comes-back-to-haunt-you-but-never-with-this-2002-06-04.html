The past ALWAYS comes back to haunt you...but never with this thick a plot.

4 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 4th, 2002 ((Note: Sorry...had a 17 page English assignment to write yesterday. I've been sick all day, and super-stressed...but I did this, and it's so-so. I'm gonna start a really corny thing...muahaha.)) All ISN'T turning out to be fair, after all. Amy's learning lesson after lesson...the one she learned in her locker room soon after Slam was to keep doors locked and always be on your toes, NOT to fear Corg and certainly not ''Don't mess with Corg.''. He had wanted to scare her, but it was only temporary fear...when he left her lying there, a river of blood forming from her face and right hand, the only thing she could have possibly felt was anger. It was Corg's own mistake...he'd done it to himself. He may be a dangerous man, but she was equally as dangerous in her own right, especially if she decided to toss any array of weaponry into the mix. Drew Hostyle tried to destroy her reputation once, but Corg and Bethany Yamato seemed intent on destroying her career...and THAT pissed her off. Everybody knew what had happened to Drew Hostyle at the last pay-per-view, he was beaten by a woman half his size...beaten badly, too. When someone's angry enough, they can muster up strength or skill they didn't even know they had, all because of focus. When Amy's angry enough, she can do some big-time damage because the girl's not always a clean fighter. Bethany liked chairs. Corg sure did like knives. Why couldn't Amy favor weapons, too? Looking back upon her indy days, Campbell had a whole lot of fun with baseball bats and sledgehammers. Sure, Yamato probably had fun with bats too...only that fun was well-documented on various videos floating around truck stops in South Mexico with titles like ''All American Game Part 2: They've Got Big Balls''... There was a battered monkey asleep on her stomach and a bandage forcing the hand she used the most to become un-moving. Her hair was messier than usual, it's strands interlaced with clumps of dried blood that also took residence on her face, neck and chest. Lots of aching, plenty of agony...this just wasn't fun. She had a private room to get a night's sleep in, that's all OSW was going to pay for...and that's all Aimz was willing to stay for. Her breaths came in small groans, ribs aching from Slam, her entire body weakened from the loss of blood Corg had instilled upon her... Yep, that guy was one sick mother fucker...that much was for sure. BUT, there was a good side...if anybody was to take on a small guy who wasn't afraid to get twisted like Corg, it was Amy. She, in her own right, had quite the mean streak in her too...Corg wasn't the only one who could fight like a disturbed bastard. And what of Bethany Yamato, anyway? There wasn't a whole lot of concern for Aimz going into this match, even though she had very limited use of her favorite punching hand thanks to the gothic freak who also happened to be taking part in the match. None-the-less, she was PISSED, and had some ass to kick.............okay, so it wasn't entirely likely that, after weeks and weeks of impromptu, un-fair beatings, Amy would have a whole lot of sucess this Saturday against two people who were in perfect condition, no injuries whatsoever... That may be true, but as long as she was standing, Campbell would give it her all. Before her thoughts got any cornier, or came STRAIGHT from the Ali movie, a visitor stepped in to distract her quite a bit... The visitor? Fubu jeans, a Phat Farm t-shirt, red and white New York Yankees visor holding tall, spiked brown hair into place.....the babyface is what made this guy, really beautiful specimin, indeed. The unfortunate part was his identity... Jason Dexter. ''Kathleen Amelia Elizabeth Campbell,'' He had that cocky smile on, as usual. ''...never thought I'd see you again!'' ''It's just Amy to you, NEVER use my fucking full name.'' Aimz was surprised, as anybody would have been...but what Jason said as he stepped over to sit down near her bed was even more surprising. ''Jeez, you're awfully sweet for somebody who called me here!'' ''What the fuck are you talking about?'' She was considerably alarmed, taking into account the fact that she hadn't been anywhere NEAR a phone all evening. ''I've been in this damned hospital since Slam ended!'' At that point, Amy was past angry, actually shaking and contemplating attack on the ex-boyfriend who'd used her because of her slight celebrity...but the story he told intrigued her too much, and this was peaked when he held up a little silver cell phone with a text message reading ''I'm in town for tonight only, room 1025 in St. J's. We need to talk.''... ''That's not me...'' Aimz was disturbed, wide-eyed, but continued... ''...I never sent that......I didn't want to see you again, EVER.'' The sender was signed 'Amy Campbell', so she could see where he may have been tricked...but who sent the message in her name? ''Coulda' fooled me!'' He was visibly aggrovated. Jason had never liked playing the fool, though it was always his unconcious role to. He flipped the cell phone shut and stood up. ''I guess you didn't wanna talk, and I guess you can pay the cabbie who took me up here for ya!'' ''Fuck you, you were the one who cheated on me...you still owe me shit, so don't even START.'' ''Pah...'' He was about to leave, his hand on the doorknob...face red with rage when his phone beeped. ''Oh,'' Dexter mumbled, glaring at the screen. ''...and by the way...according to this second message, I've got to tell you to stick around because I'm 'Only the opening act.'.' That said, Jason Dexter was gone...hopefully never to return.... But the next act was on it's way.
