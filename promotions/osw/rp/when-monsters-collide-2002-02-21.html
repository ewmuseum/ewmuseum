When Monsters Collide

21 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21-02-02 [I]”Welcome to “When Monsters Collide”, I’m John Bunnell. Now unlike our usual “World’s Wildest” genre of programming, this particular program highlights a few incidents throughout history featuring two forces of sorts sought in a battle for supremacy, survival, and even just a natural course. Such as there are hundreds upon thousands of incidents like this worthy of broadcast, the few shown here tonight are both infamous, famous, and just plain normal. The first tonight is well…the first…ever major collision in history…literally…when a universal disaster occurred changing the path of evolution forever…” [/I] A beautiful night sky, the stars are out in full view, twinkling majestically high up in the infinite that is space. All is quiet and peaceful…except for a loud wheezing like a large creature snoring. Suddenly as if out of nowhere the sky is filled with shooting stars. The long, ice blue of the tails following the crimson red of the “stars” themselves is damn near perfect as they sift silently past the earth’s atmosphere and the sky is lit up with these amazing spectacles of nature. After a few minutes of these shooting stars gracing the night sky, the loud wheezing becomes a disturbing gargling, and then finally to an animalistic grunting. As the ¾ full moon shines down on what in approximately 65 million years will be present day Hell Creek, Montana, a large beast awakens from under the shade of a large Monkeypuzzler – a prehistoric tree. Instinctively awakened by the shooting stars, the large beast skilfully uses its powerful hind legs to rise to its full height of 6 metres. Before it sets about any actions, it raises its huge head into the air and takes a strong whiff of the air around, the same old lacking sense of life in the atmosphere. Then the creature sniffs the air to the South, and receives a pleasant surprise. The sweet scent of moving prey graces the beast’s nostrils, and as it leaves the shade of the tree its full mass becomes visible in the moonlight and shooting stars…the Tyrant Lizard itself………Tyrannosaurus Rex. The massive creature lowers itself slightly and ducks back under the tree, as from a quarter mile away the glimmering moonlight shines off of the protective plates on the back of a herd of Ankylosaurs…heading his way. The Tyrannosaur, rather than stalking his prey on the moonlit plain, stays hidden beneath the branches of the Monkeypuzzler, and waits and listens as upwind of his position the herd of around a dozen Ankylosaurus treading towards him. The spectacular array of shooting stars across the night sky continues and lightly reflects off of the plates on their backs. Closer and closer the Ankylosaurs get, nearing the Tyrannosaur in wait…as the sky brightens with a newer, bigger star shooting towards earth… The grunting of the lead Ankylosaurs surrounding their young becomes loud, hoarse and at that moment the Tyrannosaur gets that instinctive feeling, and dashes straight through the herd, snatching a young Ankylosaur from within the “protective” herd and into the moonlit plain. As the 5-month old baby Ankylosaur hollers in pain, trapped within the 1.3 metre long jaw of the Tyrannosaur while at the same time being purposely crushed by the large, steak knife-like teeth of the Tyrannosaur. As the giant predator sprints away from the herd of natural tanks, they instantly realise one of their young is missing, and waddle after the sickening sound of gargling escaping the young Ankylosaur’s throat. The 5-month old baby is dropped to the ground by the T-Rex and a huge foot is stamped down onto its not-so armoured belly. The T-Rex lowers its massive head to the Ankylosaur’s chest and throat and begins tearing off the armour, as the Ankylosaurus screams in an unreal and terrifying manner. Through all of the blood and flesh, screaming and eating, the T-Rex fails to hear the sound of the dozen adult Ankylosaur’s stampeding towards it. With a mouth full of 70kg of baby Ankylosaur flesh and a surprised look in its big, evil eyes, the Tyrannosaur suddenly crumples top the ground and roars in anguish as the lead bull male Ankylosaur rears back and with hefty force drives the big, bony club at the end of his tail into the leg of the T-Rex, instantly breaking the bone. As the other Ankylosaurs check on the dead baby, the bull male takes another swing at the T-Rex, and brings his huge clubbed tail down on the pelvis of the T-Rex, and a loud [I]CRACK![/I] echoes through the plain. At the same time the shooting stars in the sky stop…and the larger star begins to take shape. Its tail isn’t as icy blue as the others or as long, and the “star” itself is bigger, big enough to be an asteroid… Closer and closer the asteroid gets, cascading past the stars and into the earth’s atmosphere… Down on the plain the Ankylosaur rears its tail back one final time, the T-Rex gasping and roaring in pain… The asteroid enters the atmosphere and tiny particles from the 10km in diameter rock begin to beak off… On the plains the Ankylosaur switches its position and raises its tail that little bit higher above the T-Rex’s head… The meteor breaks the atmosphere and shoots to earth with tremendous frightening speed… The Ankylosaur thrusts its clubbed tail down, and roars as the Tyrannosaur’s skull cracks and splinters under the force while at the same time a huge, apocalyptic [I]BOOM![/I] sounds over the horizon. The Ankylosaurs look to the huge burst of light that has suddenly appeared on the horizon… Minutes pass…five…ten…twenty or so minutes pass as all life on earth listens and watches as the light increases, and a huge cloud appears over the horizon. The telltale sign of impending doom hits the instincts of the dinosaurs, but it is already too late, as the 10km asteroid collides with the earth…and sets the toll for the beginning of the end…of the dinosaurs… As the flames and molten rock rain hit the earth, and the creatures of Prehistory slowly meet their fate… ----------------------------- [I]”A cataclysmic event that gave the mammals the chance for supremacy. However unlike the death of the dinosaurs via an asteroid 10km in diameter, the following was brought on by one of the most evil men in history – Adolf Hitler. A brutal encounter which was a massacre on both sides in the fight for survival…The Battle on The Western Front…” [/I] The ground is dug up and the land everywhere for around 5 miles is looking like a wasteland. Dead bodies literally ARE the ground, and what isn’t the corpse of a courageous soldier is an ammunition shell or a hole filled with blood and dirt. Rather than the warm, blue, inviting skies that should grace the French/German border, have been forced by war into a state of drab and seemingly terminal grey. Where Strasbourg once shone on the horizon British and French Artillery are situated, blazing ammunition across the deadly territory of “No Man’s Land” into the Nazi trenches. On the British/French alliance side are the rows of Trenches, filled with soldiers firing into No Man’s Land preventing any Nazis from crossing over, whilst further back and even as close as the 4th line of Trenches, the Artillery fires over into the Nazi territory… The German military of Herr Hitler duck and dive in their own respective trenches, riflemen and snipers near the back and the daisy cutters at the front. Eventually the British generals send word for another advance, and in a spectacular moment, over 100,000 British soldiers emerge from the trenches without fear and run off into No Man’s Land towards the Nazi side. Then it begins, as it has been for the past number of months, the sound of bullets whizzing through the air and catching their targets, embedding into the soft pulpy brain of the soldiers. The 100,000 soon become 74,284 and then below the 10,000’s as the snipers and riflemen cease fire and the Nazis at the daisy cutters take aim. From the wasteland that is No Man’s Land, the 8,422 soldiers stand confused. The sound of soft whirring comes to the ears of the soldiers, and suddenly from the far left and right of No Man’s Land, soldiers begin falling to their knees in a shower of blood as the daisy cutters slice through their knees. Another wave of the 100 bullets per second machine guns savagely hacks off the knees, then the groin of the soldiers. Falling to their chests, the soldiers try to crawl back, but the final wave of bullets catches each of them nicely in the backs of the heads, leaving a huge line of bloodied pieces of meat in the wasteland. The British and French retaliate thusly by setting off the Artillery. The faint whistling of the shells falling befalls the ears of the Nazis, and before they can run off for cover the line of trenches explodes in a shower of earth and Nazi bodies as the shells erupt all around. A low cheer escapes the throats of the Alliance, who resume firing. Another command from one of the hidden generals sends the riflemen to the bottom of their trenches, as the Nazi machine guns blaze up, however the Alliance keep their proverbial chins up as the sound of gears turning and treads being rolled over echo throughout the battle zone. There they are…an schiltron of 24 tanks. The huge, hulking grey bodies trundle across the barren and torn up earth of No Man’s Land. Through the mist and fog and smoke of the artillery fire, the Nazis wait and listen as they hear the sound of the metal tanks heading towards them…however being the early 1920’s the Nazis don’t realise the British and French have invented these weapons of war…until finally they appear through the mist, all 24 of them. The machine guns go off as the tanks trundle along. Inside the driver and gunmen try to communicate through the hoarse engine of the tank roaring away. To the left of the schiltron three tanks suddenly stop in a large crater made from artillery fire. The drivers try to drive through it, but suddenly the treads buckle, and through the slit windows at the front sniper fire enters, instantly killing the drivers. The machine guns continue blazing as the tanks continue through No Man’s Land, firing shells into the trenches and eliminating the Nazis by the Tens. The schiltron breaks as the tanks head over the trench line one-by-one, trundling over machine gun posts and crushing Nazis beneath the mighty treads. All over the place Nazis are screaming and running. [I]” Metallungeheuer!” [/I] is the sound escaping the Nazis as they retreat back to their homeland. However not all Nazis retreat, as the ones still posted at Machine Guns fire into the slit windows of the tanks as best they can. Slowly at a time blood flushes through the slits, and the Nazis move onto the next lot of tanks. Soon the front of the Nazi line is covered with crushed corpses and dead metal monsters. The British, unknowing of their losses, keep firing nonetheless, as the Battle on the Western Front rages on……… ----------------------------- [I]”A truly diabolical man who waged a truly diabolical war on Europe, and indeed the World. However not all battles, even such as War, are about supremacy, but more of survival. The last occurrence is that of a fictional variety…and results in the Survival of the Fittest…” [/I] A tall Tokyo skyscraper falls tumbling to the ground. The inhabitants of this Japanese city run through the streets like ants through an Ant farm. The constant roaring sends chills down their spines as they scatter for cover. Most take the safe route and enter buildings in hope it will pass, but end up crushed under a pile of rubble. Every second sees hundreds of thousands of ¥ being demolished before the eyes of the Japanese. Another bloodcurdling roar echoes through the land, and a strange blue light enhances, and turns to roaring flames, that burn and scold the people below. Yet another heart stopping roar echoes throughout the land, one that belongs to the mythical behemoth itself, the project of Nuclear testing, Gojira as the natives call him, but to the rest of the world – Godzilla. The Japanese killer monster wades through street after street of people until it stops suddenly at the coast…looking out to the West over the Pacific, its instincts begin acting up, Godzilla senses something over the way, and without pause for more thought dives into the Pacific and begins its long journey to the other worldly superpower – The United States of America! Meanwhile in America however, the damn near same thing is happening. In New York City itself, the citizens run wildly through the streets, cabs drive frantically into the nearest garage or alley as through the streets wanders the big dumb mammal that brooded on its own private island for years, the oversized and dangerous creature that Godzilla has instinctively crossed the world to seek. Instead of the intentional violence like Gojira, the huge mammal merely leans against buildings and scratches its back, or looks at its reflection in the skyscrapers, while the police, military and the circus owner whom the 8th wonder of the world escaped from chase him down Broadway. Across the Pacific the huge reptile swims, as tropical sea life and other living monsters of the Pacific turn and speed away from the massive nuclear- powered dinosaur. The great speed at which Godzilla crosses the Pacific with is unfathomable as it closes in on its target… Still in New York, the monster they call King Kong roams around, leaving in its path upturned vehicles and a few fatalities. The cops and the military are reluctant to do anything to the creature to stop it, as a horde of Green Peace protestors appear to have chained themselves to his ankles. [I]”Dammit sir why won’t you give us the order to fire?”[/I] asks a bewildered military officer to his commanding general. [I]”The reason I haven’t given the order to fire, Private, is that there are about 100 damned protestors strapped to that big Monkey’s feet, and I am not going through another “Nuke the Whales” epidemic. God only knows I had enough of those f*cking protestors to last me a lifetime!”[/I] replies a very irate general, as King Kong somehow speeds up and heads for Times Square, and Carson Daly in the MTV studio…………... That night Godzilla reaches the American coast about 150 miles south of Washington. While a nation sleeps, the goliath reptile heads cross-country to the Big Apple itself, where another monster is tormenting the Statue of Liberty and attempting to reciprocate with the French statue, the gift to the U.S. and representation of…liberty. Godzilla continues across America, until he reaches New Jersey and the stench of Monkey faeces and fear rises into the air. Godzilla steps up the pace and rampages through N.J. until even King Kong can hear the intense roaring heading towards him. King Kong replies with an inhumane grunt, and goes back to trying to bend the Statue of Liberty over, but to no avail. By this time however, half of the protestors have drowned underwater, the rest wearing fireproof, waterproof clothing and carrying the latest in protection…a 3ft long joint. Godzilla storms [I]straight[/I] through New York City, knocking over buildings and crushing people. As he roars through N.Y.C. on the way to the Statue, from a small café none other than Ferris Bueller himself – Matthew Broderick looks up and sees the huge monstrosity smash past. [I]”Wow! That thing was huge! Geez…………I wonder how much I’d get paid to make a movie of that thing…ah f*ck it………Go! Go! Gadget Wanted Ads!” [/I] Broderick seems less stunned as King Kong, who again hears a glass shattering roar, and turns around only to be confronted with Godzilla, who leaps into the air and dives down on King Kong, taking him to the ground. The battle begins as Godzilla begins slashing at the tough hide of King Kong, while the giant monkey digs his oversized canines into Godzilla’s leathery neck. The two tumble for a while and head back into the city, clawing and biting each other. They cascade through buildings and crush people without knowledge of them, as Godzilla charges up his atomic flame and blasts King Kong’s chest, sending his fur sizzling and his nipples wrinkling off. King Kong retaliates with a thunderous punch to the jaw of Godzilla, temporarily stunning the giant reptile. Both monsters roar into each other’s faces, and begin brutally biting at each other, Kong taking a chunk out Godzilla’s arm, who in turn snaps four fingers off of Kong’s huge left hand. The two razor in pain, and drag themselves to their feet, bleeding and battered. Suddenly Kong charges at Godzilla and picks him up. Kong thrusts Godzilla high up into the air and moves forward, turning in time to see Godzilla smashing into the cement road. Suddenly from a big flashy restaurant in Times Square, a little fat man in a black Stetson runs out with a clipboard in his hands and begins convulsing profusely. [I]”OH MY GOD!!! OH MY GOD!!! CAN YOU BELIEVE THAT!!! OH MY GOD THE HUMANITY!!! THIS IS NOT NATURAL FOLKS!!! THEY’RE TAKING EACH OTHER TO THE WOODSHED!!! BEATIN’ THE HELL OUTTA EACH OTHER!!!…………………………………………………………Buy my Barbeque sauce!” [/I] The little man disappears back into the restaurant as Godzilla spins himself to his feet and lifts King Kong up by the belly, and thrusts him into the road with a thunderous roar as Kong arches his back in pain, and Godzilla goes sin for the kill… Standing high above King Kong, Godzilla’s spinal plates light up that cold, icy blue again, and King Kong grunts hoarsely in pain as Godzilla unleashes a shower of blue flame onto Kong. For seconds the entire street is covered in flame, and through all the gut wrenching screams of horror, the flame stops and there…in the middle of the street…is a huge corpse…burnt to the bone and releasing a horrid odour into the atmosphere. Godzilla roars once again, mightily beating his chest with his 1.4 arms. As the massive beast turns around however, he is met with an entire street full of military personnel. Godzilla cocks his head in confusion at the range of tanks, artillery, soldiers, guns, ammunition, and the representation of fear in the world. Godzilla rears back his head to roar and blaze flames again, though as the spinal plates light up and his head drops forward, the huge head of Godzilla is blown to pieces and blood splatters everywhere, as every tank and soldier in the street fires a round of ammo into the skull of the nuclear monster. The massive body of the now-dead Godzilla wobbles a little, and slopes forward. The rows of military can see what’s going to happen, and before the order to retreat can be given…the gigantic, scaly body falls forward and with an almighty [I][b]BOOM![/b][/I] the entire street is covered in dust and blood as Godzilla makes his final mark on New York City, and leaves the legacy of destroying a monster with that of forever embedding his image in history……… ----------------------------- [I]”In those three situations alone, thousands upon millions of lives were at risk. Two major forces clashed for one purpose, survival, but in that clash the lives of millions were at risk from the sheer power and divinity of the two forces…this has been “When Monsters Collide”, and I have been John Bunnell…Goodbye………”[/I] As John Bunnell begins walking off the artificial warehouse set for the program, a sickening thud echoes throughout the studio and the cameras turn to see the body of Mr. Bunnell crushed and crumpled in a corner, a huge cinderblock where his skull should be and a large spear in his chest. The cameras swing from side-to-side of the studio, and suddenly every camera is filled with the view of hot, red, flowing blood, and the sound of a soul being sent to hell. As all the camera crew are slowly slaughtered one-by-one and the producers fail to notice from their sky box above the studio, a huge dark figure walks in front of one of the cameras and turns it to the body of John Bunnell. As the camera views change, they still point towards the slaughtered body of the ex-cop and ex-T.V. personality. Again a huge, lumbering dark figure walks past the camera, and an eerie breathing echoes through the studio. [I]”When Monsters Collide…lives are at risk…when monsters collide………the world watches in anticipation to see the outcome…be it War…the Superbowl…a simple cards game…one force dominates another. Two bodies of power face off in a clash for a prize…be it money…power…women…supremacy…it doesn’t matter. The monsters will put their lives on the line for that prize no matter what the outcome…” [/I] One of the cameras turns to black suddenly, as the heavy breathing continues, and again the deep voice continues… [I]”An assault…on a night where a mark was to be made and one of the leading powers was to be halted…an attack that left a Giant scorned and broken…but that was the past…time has healed the mentality and physical state of the Giant…and now he waits…I wait…when the chance for revenge dawns…when I, a [b]Go[/b]lia[b]th[/b], get the chance to eliminate a disease to society…to exact just revenge on a body that believes it is the ultimate power…the power of Meanstreak will be stressed to match that of the T.S.I…………in the past I said Ethan’s time would come…in just a couple of short days I will prove myself to the world as I was supposed to on the night Ivan Stanislav was to be Eternally Damned…and now the chance to face a true monster…to match wits, skills, and power with a man renowned for that very thing. I will not lay down…I will not quit…I will not stop…until…” [/I] The camera in black turns to light again, and focuses in on the face of the man, the huge silver skull of the Mercenary of Satan…Hessian…the eerie glowing Green eyes of Hessian are unmoving as he stares into the screen. [I]”I will not stop until The Monster they call Meanstreak has been driven straight into [b]Eternal Damnation![/b]…………” [/I] The camera turns to static as the giant Hessian ends with a loud, spine-chilling roar, and exits quickly, as the producers look into the studio and gasp in horror as their entire camera crew and John Bunnell hang from the artificial warehouse by rope nooses, and a small explosion destroys all the cameras…and the footage within them. From the shadows and the darkness, a low, inhumane guttural laugh echoes, and from the darkest corner two green glowing embers shine, accompanied by the glinting of the infamous silver skull……… Fin.
