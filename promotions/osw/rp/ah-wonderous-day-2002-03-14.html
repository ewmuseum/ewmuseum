Ah, Wonderous Day!

14 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-14-02 Ah, Tuesday...the most wondrous day of the week. Does that sound so odd? Well, perhaps you don’t view Tuesday as being anything special. It’s only the middle of the road as far as the work week goes. Hell, it’s not even halfway through. At least on Thursday you know that the week is slowly coming to a close. On Tuesday you’re still heading into the brunt of the it. Plus what kind of television viewing does a Tuesday provide? A very dismal selection that’s for sure. When your best choice for nightly programming is to watch Primetime Championship Wrestling’s inferior roster of punk kids not good enough for Old School slugging it out on Turmoil, you know you’re in trouble. So with shitty week placement and an even shittier TV schedule, you’re probably thinking just what sort of crazy redeeming quality Tuesday must have to make it so esteemed in my eyes. The answer to that query is three simple words: Comic Book Day, the day where all the new merchandise arrives at the comic store from the distributors. Ok, so maybe you have to be into comic books to get excited about Comic Book Day, which probably excludes the majority of the audience. It is looked upon by some circles as quite the nerdy hobby. Right above stamp collecting on the cool spectrum. Seth Masters doesn’t care, though. Despite the negative stigma, he looooooves his comics. He won’t let trends dictate behavior to him. He’s the one who dictates the trends. He is one half of the Old School Wrestling Tag Team champions, isn’t he? You don’t get much more trendy than that. He is the epitome of style. He puts the ‘oo’ in cool. He...oh alright, he’s admittedly a dork, but dammit, he’s the KING of the dorks! Seth’s case isn’t helped by the fact that he’s currently in the midst of digging through a box full of back issues of Witchblade comics in the back of the comic book store at the local mall. Flipping through the issues, a smile comes over his face as he pulls out an issue with Sara Pezzini in a black leather jumpsuit on the cover. He turns to the clerk at the counter excitedly. “Dude, this is the Wizard 1/2 issue where Nottingham and Strauss almost blow each other to hell before they’re stopped by Sara! How much you want for this?” The clerk looks up from the Ultimate X-Men issue sitting in front of him to glance over at Seth. The guy is your typical teenaged dork, wearing a Darth Maul T-shirt, glasses with big rims and even thicker lenses, a mussed up hairstyle, and lots and lots of acne. Despite all this he seems to exude an aura of superiority that may match even Seth’s. The clerk eyes the professional wrestler, seeming half-interested and half-uncaring before finally looking back down to his comic. “How much are you willing to pay for it?” he asks nonchalantly. “Um...five dollars. No wait, make that ten!” Seth shouts, reaching into his pocket and pulling a ten dollar bill out from his wallet. He slaps it down on the counter excitedly, waiting for the clerk to ring it up. Instead of doing so however, he just raises his eyes to meet Seth’s and smirks. “That’ll be fifteen dollars,” he says confidently, getting an angry glare from Seth in response. “Damn it, Melvin! You’re such a fucking dickhead. Now take my money and put this comic in a bag, damn it!” Seth shouts angrily. The clerk, now identified as Melvin, seems unimpressed by the display of anger, continuing his confident smirking. “I believe you owe me five more dollars, man,” Melvin states cockily, even going so far as to swagger his head a bit. Seth looks irritated, frowning heavily, but digs into his pocket once again before slapping down another five dollar bill. Melvin laughs, slipping the ten dollars into the cash register and the extra five in his pocket. “Nice doing business with you...dicksmack! Ha ha! Man, I’ve wanted to say that for soooo long!” “Gee Melvin, with a personality like yours it’s a wonder that you don’t have any friends,” Seth says, shaking his head sadly. “Can I get a bag?” “Sure, bag it yourself,” Melvin says, reaching down behind the counter and tossing a plastic bag at Seth. “You’re pretty fucking lazy, Melvin. You know that right?” mutters Seth, as he places his comic inside the bag. Melvin laughs upon hearing this, snorting in typical nerd fashion as he does so. “I’m lazy? Puh-lease! The thing with it isn’t that I’m lazy...I just don’t give a damn,” Melvin says, shaking his head in disbelief. “I’m the lazy one!? Look who’s talking! Sloth Masters! You just sit on your couch all week long, work a show every Saturday, then go back to sitting on your couch for another week. How dare you call me lazy! You’re twenty times more lazy than I am! ” “You would think so...but if I’m so lazy than why would I have this, hmmm?” Seth taps his waist, around which is encircled an Old School Wrestling Tag Team title belt. It glimmers as the light hits upon it, looking quite impressive in the drab mall setting. “You’re still wearing that thing?” Melvin sighs, exasperated. “Man, you haven’t taken that thing off since you won it. Get over it already.” “You know you’re jealous,” Seth states cockily, raising his head in the air with a smug grin. “You might as well just say it, dude.” “Eh, maybe a little,” Melvin admits, before grinning himself. “But the five dollars I just ripped off from you make me feel a bit better.” The grin disappears from Seth’s face and he lowers his head once more, muttering a few profane words under his breath before moving to peruse another rack of comics. Melvin cackles sinisterly, then goes back to reading the latest adventures of the X-Men. The two have been silent for about a minute, doing their separate things, when Melvin breaks the silence. “So where’s your gay ass partner today?” Melvin asks, not even bothering to look up. “I don’t know. Probably sucking some dick, the fucking cock smoker,” Seth says with a laugh. “Yeah, that Trevor guy looks like he done loves the cock,” Melvin chuckles back. “Hell yeah...he fucking guzzles that shit!” Seth answers back. The two smirk a bit, before breaking into a fit of laughter at their homosexual branding of the other half of Civil Disobedience. They laugh for one...two...five minutes nonstop, before finally getting control of themselves. They are breathing heavily and red in the face as they hold their sides, which are sore from the excessive laughing, but it looks as if they’re just about done with their little fit....until the brunt of their joke walks into the store. “Hey guys, what’s so funny?” Trevor asks, walking up to stand in between Seth and Melvin. The two try to hold in their laughter, but instead end up cracking up again. An irate expression appears on Trevor’s face as he glares at both men. “Aw, damn it! You’re making gay jokes about me again, aren’t you? God, fuck you guys!” he shouts angrily, prompting even more laughter. “Ha ha ha ha! Yeah, I bet you’d like to do that. Ha ha ha ha ha!” Melvin yells, laughing even harder than before. Seth is laying on the floor from laughing so hard. Trevor shakes his head angrily at them. After about five more minutes, they finally stop laughing once more. “You two are so fucking immature. Grow the fuck up!” he yells, turning to glare hatefully at Melvin. “Especially you. You know Seth doesn’t need any encouragement, yet here you are prodding him along. You should be ashamed of yourself. Don’t you have a store to run?” “Eh,” Melvin shrugs, before looking back down at his comic. Seth is still trying to hold in his giggles as Trevor turns to face him with a less hateful, but still pretty darn angry glare. “And you...” Trevor lectures. “Shouldn’t you be at the gym training for our match this Saturday. Sure, Frankie Bones and Drew Hostyle may not be as big, strong, and ugly as Heresian Bloodlust were and still are, but that’s no excuse to treat them lightly.” “But...but...I never went to the gym before!” whines Seth. “Why should I start now?” “Because we’re the fucking OSW Tag Team champions,” Trevor says, slapping the belt around Seth’s waist. “And as foolish as you are to wear this thing wherever you go, you’re going to have to work your ass off if you want to keep it. Besides, if keeping this belt isn’t enough incentive, isn’t the opportunity to get revenge on those two cheap-shotting assholes enough motivation for you to get ready for this match?” “I guess...” Seth says, rolling his eyes. “I just don’t think I should a guy that was in a PCW stable called ‘Black Attack’ and a guy that lost to Matt Blaze on the first OSW slam really deserve to be taken seriously.” “They fucking punched us in the face, man! My flawless looks were placed in jeopardy! How dare they try to mar my perfection!” Trevor shouts, getting a chuckle from Melvin on the other side of the counter. “What’s the matter, Trev? Are you mad that those two beach bums might have kept you from being able to pick up guys any more?” Melvin says with a laugh. His laughter dies off though, as he sees the reaction from Civil Disobedience. Seth and Trevor are staring at him with confused looks on their faces. “What? Did I say something wrong?” “Well you got the part about Trevor picking up guys right, but what the hell was that part about beach bums?” Seth questions, ignoring the angry glare he’s getting from his partner. “Drew Hostyle and Frankie Bones. They’re beach bums, right? That’s their persona. Heresian Bloodlust are the Satan worshippers, Twisted Metal are the robots bent on the destruction of the human race, you guys are the two morons who get their asses kicked every week, and Bones and Hostyle are the beach bums. Right?” Melvin asks, not quite sure of his words anymore. “They’re not fucking beach bums, you idiot! What the hell would make you think that?” Trevor inquires back in disbelief. “Look how tan they are!” Melvin replies. “How could they not be beach bums.” “Tan? They’re not tan you idiot!” Trevor shouts. “They’re black!” “What?! Don’t lie to me. It’s obvious that they’re brown, moron. Even I can see that.” Melvin snaps back. “No, I mean they’re African-Americans!” Trevor shouts, amazed that Melvin isn’t catching on. “Af-ri-can...Am-er-i-cans...?” Melvin mouths to himself, unsure of the words. “What the hell is wrong with this guy?!” Trevor asks, turning to Seth with a look of disbelief still on his face. Seth leans in to his partner and whispers in his ear, as to not let Melvin hear. “Dude, Melvin isn’t like you or me. He’s never been outside of our white-bred little section of the suburbs.” Seth says softly. “What does that have to do with anything?” asks Trevor. “It means that he’s never seen a black person before in his life,” Seth whispers. A look of understanding comes over Trevor’s face, as he realizes the situation. Pulling away from Seth he walks over and leans up against the counter, addressing Melvin with a pitying glance. “Hey dude. You really should check out UPN more often. There’s a whole new world out there for you to discover.” Melvin keeps muttering the two words ‘African American’ to himself over and over again in a confused state as Trevor walks back to join his partner. “Let’s go stop by the Smoothie Hut really quick and then we can get back to getting ready for our match. We’ll make those two Brooklyn fucks look so bad that Tazz will be considered the borough’s best wrestler. And you know that’s pretty fucking bad.” “Word!” Seth agrees, placing his Witchblade comic under his arm. The two men proceed to walk out of the store, as the scene fades to black.
