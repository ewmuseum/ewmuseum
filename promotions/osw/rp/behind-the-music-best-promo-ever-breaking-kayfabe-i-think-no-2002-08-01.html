Behind The Music, Best Promo Ever. (Breaking Kayfabe? I Think Not.)

1 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/1/02 As the scene fades in we find ourselves in a rather nicely furnished, leather finished, somewhat decked out vehicle. Sitting on the left side in the driver's seat is none other than Scott Taylor's very own personal bodyguard/manager, Randolph. The balding, over weight, middle aged man looks as he always does. A pair of brown slacks, huge and bulky white tennis shoes, and a plain white tee shirt with 'Taylor Trained Ninja' written across the front with a Sharpie Marker consists of his attire today. He still has the horribly cheap comb-over and this time a pair of big lensed black sunglasses cover his eyes, and help hide the wrinkles around them as well. The view pans around to the right side of the car, as Randolph shifts out the 2002 Chevrolet Blazer, revealing a second person sitting in the passenger's side of the Blazer. The person is none other than Randolph's employer and Kid Wonder's Arch-Nemesis, 'The Man Who Defines The Term 'Tailor Made.' ,' Scott Taylor. Scott's wardrobe consists of a pair of loose fitting black denim jeans and a plain white tanktop, also known as a wife beater, (Author's Note: Maybe that's why Steve Austin has so many of them.) Both of his arms are covered with near week old bruises, they are now a light shade of green as opposed to the deep blue and black they were after the hellacious six person cage match that took place on last Saturday night's Slam! Scott's hair is free of it's usual braided ponytail and is hanging loosely down his shoulders, framing his face. His face, several bruises and some cuts and scrapes, but atleast the pair of thin framed silver glasses help to hide some of the damage. In his hands is a small notepad and a pencil, Scott's left hand can be seen moving, he's writing something. Randolph looks over at Scott and notices his hand rapidly moving, scribbling something down onto the piece of paper. Scott doesn't look up, his attention seems to be on the paper in his lap. There is a short moment of peaceful of silence until Randolph coughs, clearing his throat, and then speaks to his employer. 'Mr Taylor?' Nothing, Randolph waits until around four seconds has passed. He speaks again. 'Mr Taylor?' Scott jumps a little, hearing his name has brought him back into reality so to speak. He looks away from the piece of paper and over at Randolph. Scott blinks and then sets the piece of paper and the pencil on the dashboard. 'What?' 'Where are we going, again?' 'I've already told you. We're going to a place called 'Stan's Music Center.' It should be right up the road from here.' Randolph looks at Scott with a hint of confusion on his face. His two thick eyebrows move up, he doesn't understand the movites behind Scott's plan that the two need to go and purchase a guitar. 'Why do we need a guitar?' 'Because Randolph. You're going to play it.' 'Me?! Play a guitar? I don't know if I can play one, Sir.' Scott looks over to the piece of paper and the pencil and then back to Randolph, sighing a little before he speaks. 'We're going to work on a little side project for this promo, Randolph. You're going to play the guitar while I sing a song that I've written. It's going to be so original that it makes both of us famous. Well, it will make me more famous than I already am. Brilliant, no?' There is a short pause while Randolph comtenplates this idea. Scott leans back in his seat while Randolph thinks, only to lean forward in the seat and scream only seconds later. 'RANDOLPH! LOOK OUT! TURN THE WHEEL!' Randolph looks up at the road infront of him and spots an old lady with a walker crossing the parking lot towards her car. He slams on the brakes and violently spins the wheel to the right, just BARELY missing the old woman. Once the vehicle is stopped, Scott turns to his employee and sneers. He reaches over and slaps the back of his bodyguard's head. His face has turned red at this point and he begins to yell. 'What was that?! You're my bodyguard! You protect me! I don't protect you! Get it right or I'll send your fat ass back on a plane to Elkhart where you'll be unemployed and living in a one bedroom apartment above a bowling alley and beneath another one!' The Taylor Trained Ninja cowers in fear at the words from Scott. He slowly puts the Blazer back into drive and begins to drive again, starting to circle the parking lot trying to locate a space to park the vehicle. After several minutes of looking around Randolph finally pulls into a space. He kills the engine and removes the keys as Scott steps out of the Blazer. Scott steps out onto the pavement and straightens his jeans as Randolph presses a button on the keychain which activates the vehicle's burglar alarm. Scott looks back to Randolph who is making his way around. Scott points. 'There it is. Come Randolph.' The two of them begin to trek across the parking lot at this point. After stopping to let another car pass by, the two of them step up onto the sidewalk. Randolph pulls open the glass door to 'Stan's Music Center' and Scott Taylor steps in. The view switches around to the show the interior of the store. The walls are lined with multiple guitars and bass guitars from various companies. The most common brand though is that of Ibanez. There is a long glass case against the back wall which holds various foot petals, strings, drum sticks, drum repair kits, and other add-ons for your instruments. A large wooden case against another wall has various speakers and amps stacked on it. Your typical music store. Scott and Randolph continue to look over the store until an elderly man approaches the duo. This man looks to be in his late 50's, possibly early 60's. He has a head full of thick white hair, combed to the side, a thick white mustache, and a pair of big framed glasses. He is wearing a pair of tight denim jeans and a long sleeved cowboy style shirt. He looks to Scott and speaks. 'Good day gentlemen, I'm Stan. Can I help you with anything?' Scott looks over the store quickly for a second time and then speaks, while crossing his arms over his chest, wincing a little at the muscle contraction. Damn cage match. 'We're interested in purchasing a guitar.' 'Well, you've most certainly come to the right place.' 'Indeed we have, anyway. We're looking for something along the lines of Accoustic. Whatever you'd reccomend.' 'I think I have just the thing. Follow me.' Stan turns and begins to walk towards one of the walls which sports many a guitar. Scott and Randolph follow behind him, stopping behind him. Stan reaches up and pulls down a somewhat large wooden guitar, he takes a moment to look it over. Seeming satisfied he begins to walk towards the counter. Once again, our duo of heroes follows behind. At the counter Stan lays the guitar on it and begins pressing some buttons on the cash register. After totalling up the price he looks back to Scott. 'Your total has come to one hundred, twelve dollars, and forty-nine cents. I'll need to see some ID as well.' Scott nods to Stan as he reaches towards his back pocket, he removes a black leather wallet and opens it up. Being on the OSW roster does pay well so our hero does pack a hefty sum of money with him most of the time. Scott reaches into the main part where various dollars are, however he stops and pulls out his driver's liscense. He hands them over to Stan. The old man takes them and begins to look them over. 'Alright, says here you are Scott Ta....' Stan stops and looks back up to Scott. 'Wait, I know this name. I've heard it mentioned before when my son is watching tv. Let me think. Oh...you're in that PCW place right?' This comment is followed up by a long moment of silence. 'What?! PCW?! I'm in OSW!' 'Oh, that's right. Yeah, OSW. It's confusing enough, so many places where you wrestlers are. I don't see why you all just don't combine and make one big place. Anyway, here's your liscense back.' Scott takes the piece of laminated plastic back and slips it into his wallet. He pulls out two bills and hands them to Stan. The drawer slides open with a ding and Stan putst he money in, he then hands Scott his change. Scott puts the change in his wallet as Stan places the accoustic guitar in a large bag. Another transaction is made and then Scott and Randolph turn to leave the store. 'This is going to be great. Come Randolph, we must trek out into the world and show them what musical taste is all about.' Fin~
