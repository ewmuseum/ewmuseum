Date: July 29th, 2002 Amy's return to the ring after a few weeks of absence hadn't gone as she'd originally hoped, to say the very least. After getting nipples twisted, shins bruised and heads knocked, the steel cage match had been little more than a great laugh for your usual snide female wrestling 'fan, who wanted little more than a nice parade of bra-burning and 'girl power'. Then, of course, the match appealed in an 'I think I saw a boobie!' way to the market she was basically hired to play to: The 15-25 year-old, hormone-ridden, sexless, lifeless males. Both ways, it left a sour taste in her mouth as she realised exactly WHY she gets little to no respect whatsoever. So there she was, still clad in her ring gear of the evening: baggy Lip Service bondage pants and an uncomfortably clingy shirt depicting the logo of a favorite band of hers, Avenged Sevenfold. She just sat there, idly swinging her legs over the side of the wheeled black crate she was positioned atop of, hands fidgeting around in her pocket as she carried on in conversation with her boss, stablemate and, dare she say it, almost friend, Jane Reagan. ''You didn't seem too pleased with the cage match,'' Jane had her head cocked to the side, hands on her hips as she looked down at Amy with raised eyebrows and a facial expression that mirrored Campbell's, equally as displeased by the events of the bout. ''From where I stood.'' ''Nah, I'm still pissed about that.'' Aimz shifted around a bit in thought, eventually ending up with her knees cuddled to her chest, her chin resting on them. ''How dumb could I have been? I ended up on top of the cage, I could've just jumped down, won the match and faced whatever fate Mammoth had in store for me...it's not like I was any safer inside the cage with that fucker by the door.'' ''Well, you certainly had the opportunity.'' Reagan had never been a woman to sugarcoat things and now was no different. ''You could've hit him with something. I've seen you fly around, I know what you're capable of.'' Amy groaned, her headache was slowly crawling back. ''I guess I just wasn't thinking straight...I had Andre the Giant reincarnate on one end and Spock on the other. If he hadn't wobbled that damned cage around, I would've gotten my shit together and thought to hit a plancha or something.'' ''But you didn't.'' The CEO folded her arms. ''You need to be faster to think on your feet from now on. Maybe Reno's on your mind a little too often?'' The redhead just lowered her head, gazing at her MarcEcko sneakers but Jane persisted. ''Am I right?'' She raised an eyebrow. Amy merely mumbled, ''I can't help THAT, Jane.'' ''Heh...you love him,'' Reagan smiled, pleased with herself. ''Don't get me wrong, I think that's sweet...but you're going to have to juggle all thoughts, since I doubt this is the last time you two will be in the ring at the same time.'' ''I know, I know.'' She nodded calmly. ''I've gotta start concentrating more. Just, you know...this stuff with Corg over the last few weeks, that's a big factor. I never know when he's just gonna jump out at me or something.'' ''Now that, I can understand.'' Jane's expression went sour at the mention of Isiic Raiyn. ''But don't you worry about him, we've got some 'special' security to take care of a match in case he decides to get involved.'' Campbell was only slightly reassured, managing to crack a weak smile. ''Sebastian, Trev and Seth? They're a good plan for when I'm in the arenas...but outside, when Reno's gone somewhere I'm basically helpless because, unfortunately, Raiyn isn't stupid enough to come after me un-armed.'' ''True enough, just keep an eye out but don't worry too much, okay? I'm looking out for your well-being here, a healthy mind means a healthy body.'' ''I'm a big girl,'' Amy grinned. ''I'm sure I can handle it.'' Reagan stammered a bit at Campbell's confidence, but she felt inclined to agree. ''You've already handled more than enough! You've really impressed me, Amy...a lesser person would have quit and joined Witness Protection by now, woman OR man.'' ''Not to say it hasn't fucked me up,'' She laughed. ''Don't assume I haven't thought about hiding, heh. But now I've got another, less threatening however MUCH more irritating force to contend with.'' ''Really now?'' ''Yup! Professor..........something or other...Dorkydude. You know; huge glasses, bad hair cut, decked out in more gizmos than most porn stars have ever seen...'' Campbell smiled, then grew to be much less congenial as she continued growling, ''I didn't enjoy his buddy Andre the Mammoth OR that little nipple stunt this evening and thanks to his steel panties, I've got a bruise on my leg the size of Ivan Stanislav's foot.'' ''Yeah,'' Mrs. 'Phantom Booker' herself tried to stifle a laugh, seeing that Amy didn't find her own pain quite as amusing as anybody else would, naturally. ''That looked like it hurt.'' Amy snarled at the thought of PHD yet again. ''I'm telling you...when I get my hands on him, they'll need six scopes just to FIND his testicles and twice as many operations to retrieve them from whatever orifice I feel like ramming them in.'' ''The scary part is that I can tell you're not kidding.'' Reagan gulped. ''Not in the least, Jane...not in the least. Heh, unless he can invent an automatic boot-retrieval gadget, he's going to be in a pretty compromising position next time I see his scrawny ass. I honestly don't see William Shatner or his doctored nudes of Gillian Anderson saving him this time.'' Watching Jane laugh at the incredibly applicable sci-fi dweeb references, Aimz decided to avert her attention to other affairs, nodding Reagan off with a wave as she hopped off of the crate saying, ''Anyway, I've got a Mammoth-induced headache and a missing boyfriend to attend to, so I'll see ya around, okay?'' ''Alright. Hey, maybe we could go shopping sometime!'' A little startled by the invitation, and the fact that Reagan saw her as the 'shopping' type, Aimz face contorted into an oddly sour expression but she chuckled none-the-less. ''Something tells me we don't shop at the same stores, Jane.'' ''Don't worry, I'm with your style...I LOVE thrift shopping!'' The frightening part was that the CEO hadn't meant it as an insult to Campbell's slightly rougher choice of apparel, she was blatantly serious. ''Umm...okay, yeah...'' Aimz smirked meekly. ''I'd..uhh...like that. Anyway......headache...so yeah, see ya!'' Jane smiled, sending Amy off with her signature thumbs up. ''Bye...hope the headache clears up!'' Soon after waving off from her conversation with the executive, Amy found herself limping blindly through the hallway of the Hershey Park Arena, not really having any goal in mind aside from locating Reno Davis, who'd been escorted out of the ring by medics just before Aimz herself was, which was the last time she saw 'The Unwanted Angel'. As she passed various superstars, she had nothing but aggressive thoughts in her head...the yearning to draw blood from Corg, and now PHD, Mammoth and even in a way, Kid Wonder, who'd won the match earning himself a Cruiserweight title shot and change to battle Corg in what Campbell saw as little more than a fluke. In all technicality, she was in a position earlier where she could have won the shot at the gothic lunatic who'd made her life no less than a true and living hell if she'd have only thought to just drop to the floor when she had the chance. She knew that, of course, and practically beat herself up for it mentally. Unfortunately, she hadn't been given the time to think, which was a fact she needed to live with and find a way around since, as Amy knew, she wouldn't always have a convenient amount of time to work things out in her head before she acted. Following instincts had gotten her into trouble in the past, but from now on she intended to act upon them, considering she got absolutely nowhere doing the 'logical thing' all the time. 'No respect for a working mind', She thought, barely noticing that she'd just walked away from a referee who'd been attempting to greet her. 'People only ever see the body.' In fact, her statement was true in most paths of life, as were many things she'd realised before, thankfully enough. Life had a way of messing up for Aimz, so she'd just generally given up caring for the rest of the world or the majority's thought, which lead to cynical little statements or insights about society in general. One thing no proverb appeared to be helping her with, however, was finding her boyfriend... Campbell began to un-tape her wrists as she stumbled into her locker room, bumping square into the forehead of Davis himself. ''Oww.'' He groaned, eventually cracking a sarcastic smile when Amy said nothing. ''Well hello to you, too.'' ''Huh? Oh...'' Campbell shook her head a bit, noticing that she was getting sleepier by the minute. ''...hi.'' ''ZaC's in the car.'' Reno looked over her face and slung a duffel bag over one shoulder, putting his free arm over Amy's as they began walking back in the same direction she'd come. ''Christ...you're not looking too cheery.'' ''Tired.'' She muttered abruptly in response, rubbing her eyes and sighing. ''Hard night...kinda got stressful.'' Davis just nodded. ''I can see where you'd get that, yeah.'' The two walked in awkward silence for a few minutes, either one nodding to whomever bothered to greet them as they exited the building, both limping a bit, Reno still apparently stiff from his recent leg injury. Neither one of them said a word until they stepped out into the parking lot, Amy had just found security within Davis' grasp, choosing to enjoy that rather than take the energy to make some pointless comment. What good would talking about something like the weather do, anyway? She'd never seen the point in idle conversation...but that was just another little hang-up that had slowly set her to be less and less a 'people person' as she matured. Reno was uncomfortable in the uncharacteristic silence, though...so Campbell readily accepted conversation when he made the offer. ''How's your shin feeling?'' Amy shrugged. ''It hurts like a bitch now, but that'll go away...it's gonna leave one hell of a bruise for a while, though.'' Before he could think up another question, they were in front of the little Honda Civic rental, Reno smiling at the sight of ZaC perched up on the steering wheel as he stepped into the car, tossing his bag into the back seat and scratching the little monkey behind the ears when he hopped down into his lap. Aimz took her time getting in, feeling the same odd chill as if she was being watched as she'd felt quite a few times before... She sat down, buckled her seat belt and sat silently with a glare towards the high bushes in the garden of shrubs at the front of the parking lot, swearing she'd seen movement but just brushing it off as a glint of the street lights shining off of a leaf as Davis slowly pulled out of the lot. Campbell still didn't feel right, staring at the mirror attached to her door when they got out into the street... The same mirror that was shattered by her fist when she saw Corg's image in it, standing just in front of the shrubs to break his sadistic smile by blowing her a kiss and waving with a smirk, mouthing what looked something like 'See you next week.'.
