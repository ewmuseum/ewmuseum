Implied Consent.

13 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 13th, 2002 ((Note: Sorry about this being late in the week and not exactly great...it's been a VERY busy, pretty rough two weeks for me OOC, heh. My birthday's in 4 daaaaaaaaaays!!!!!)) Hospitals aren't exactly amusement parks... Amy Campbell isn't exactly a 70 year-old woman suffering from some life-ending illness. She's not a little girl laying there gasping for breath after an athsma attack... ...Not some freaky junkie spasming and twitching after a cocaine overdose... What she IS, however, happens to be a young woman with serious issues about being idle, sitting in a hospital exactly five days before her twentieth birthday with a very old, but very untalkative friend hired as a body guard for company. It's not a good position to be in...he was a friend from long ago, they hadn't really seen one another in years...they had all the time in the world to sit down and speak now, but for some reason, he'd said all but nothing since he'd arrived. She was reclining back at the head of the bed wearing street clothes, her favorite 'Guess?' cargo khakis and a simple black t-shirt with ''One by one, the penguins steal my sanity.'' written across the chest in white. It was obvious that the redhead was desperately trying to find something interesting about the episode of Oprah on the only channel the private room got, but that was hoping for WAY too much... So she did the expected, leaning against her one free arm, cringing as the slung one shifted a little, she turned her body a bit toward the man who was sitting on a stiff looking chair next to the closed door. He certainly looked like the strong, silent type...sitting there fidgeting slightly with the nylon material of his ultra-baggy Kikwear jeans, occasionally scratching very short but wildly patterned black stubble sideburns or running a hand through hair that was highlighted a brighter red than Amy's natural color... He definitely didn't look like much of a speaker, and he acted the part well when Aimz tried for conversation. ''Look at these girls on TV...they flock to diet pills like Oprah sprints for KFC...'' She grumbled. ''...I say if people are naturally fat, so fucking be it. They always blame all their problems on skinny girls, saying we make fun of 'em...but THEY'RE the ones calling US anorexic all the time.'' ''Meh,'' The man's deep, oddly smooth voice came back. ''...I say anybody who goes on national television to bitch and moan about their weight deserves to be shot. Skinny or fat, doesn't make me give a damn either way.'' ''You really never were a very sympathetic person.'' Amy laughed, finding little more than a stone-faced stare on her friend's part. Even after a few moments of uncomfortable silence he just sat there, entirely motionless until Aimz changed the subject from him. ''So...uhh...yeah! Being out of high school's been...umm...fun so far.....'' She paused a moment to remember some of the more recent events of her wrestling 'career' she left home to pursue after graduation. ''.....I...uh...suppose.'' He was off in his own world, running the evening so far through his head. ''Who was little red?'' ''...little red?'' ''Little guy, hair like yours. Real queer eyes, looked like a fairy or somethin'.'' ''That's Reno...'' She shifted to sit up at the edge of the bed, speaking in an almost challenging tone, ''Why? You got something to say about him?'' ''Nothin' but the obvious fairy queen observation.'' ''Nero! Watch your fucking mouth about him.'' Well, the name had revealed who he was...Nero, an old wrestling ally she'd grown up with as almost a brother, long retired with a back injury. From the looks of things, he'd been called upon to oversee Aimz' security while Reno wasn't around. He could still put up a fight physically, and apparently he was just as fond of verbal quarrels. That much became obvious when he spoke back, at least. In a fairly low tone, just barely under his breath, he muttered, ''...sounds like you're fuckin' 'im.'' ''What was that?'' ''I said...'' Nero stood up, stretching out his fairly muscular 6'2'' frame. ''The way you talk about him makes it sound like you're...'' He tried to word things delicately to avoid bugging his old friend too much, for once. ''...ahem...'sharing his b---'' Before he could finish, in walked Davis with ZaC on his shoulder and Nero didn't look too happy when Amy promptly ignored him to run over and greet who was obviously her current love interest. He sighed irritably when he stepped to the door, 'accidentally' bumping into Reno's shoulder before he sneered ''Well, your REAL protection's here now...I'm sure you'll have fun.'' and walked out, slamming the door behind him. Reno looked at Aimz, then to the door.''Who was that?'' ''Just an old friend with some sort of permanent male version of PMS.'' ''Ah.'' ''Wow...you're talkative tonight.'' She cocked her head to the side, studying Davis' expression closely. ''What's wrong?'' ''Our contracts...'' ''Hmm?'' Reno's somber tone almost alarmed her, she spoke more urgently this time. ''What about our contracts?'' Reno looked at the floor and clenched his fist, ''We can't do anything to Corg...It's called Implied Consent. It means that this fucker can do anything but kill us. It had to be passed after every wrestler started going all 'ECW' on everyone else and companies started to get a lot of legal bullshit in their face.'' ''What? You mean he can get away with this bullshit?'' She gestured towards her slung shoulder and arm. ''He's trying to 'push the limits' and be 'extreme' or whatever...but this is pushing the fucking envelope. Seth and Trevor put it VERY well: This isn't a fucking sport anymore.'' ''I know, I know...'' He soothed, trying to use a calming tone, though he was just as angry and upset as Campbell. ''But look on the bright side, we can REALLY give the psycho bastard what he deserves now.'' Aimz' eyes got a little brighter. ''Hmm?'' ''Well, if he can stab people and get away with it, I'm sure beating somebody within an inch of their life won't get either of us punished. Just let me handle him.'' ''Don't be stupid about this, Reno.'' ''What?'' ''I said not to be stupid...'' Her voice seemed angry, but worried at the same time as she sagged her sore form down into a chair, looking up to Davis. ''A cat gets bored of playing with a mouse after a while, he'll get tired of me and move on soon enough.'' ''Amy, a cat won't leave something alone while it can still run away. They only tire of corpses.'' Aimz bowed her head a little. She didn't like to be proven wrong, but she liked the sad fact Davis had just stated even less; Someone like Corg wouldn't leave her alone until he reached his goal. ''Look,'' She tried to find some hope to give them both. ''...he's locked up for the time being. He can't bother me yet...so let's just burn that bridge when we get t----'' Reno promptly interrupted, mutilating the little glimmer of promise, ''He was bailed.'' Her head darted back up. ''What?!'' ''He was bailed two days ago. I didn't want to tell you, you looked so happy to finally feel safe.'' This time was as good as any to bring the 'Big, tough Amy' act on, so she did, huddling up a little in the chair to hide the nervous shifty movements she'd been making. She felt safe with Reno, but as soon as he was gone, the paranoia would kick back in. Corg was out and now she knew that he could do ANYTHING to her without punishment... If she just found out, Raiyn probably already knew it. After all the gravy sucking pigs of lawyers, she had about as much available money left as a part-time McDonald's kitchen bitch would, but something drastic needed to be done. Reno wouldn't be there all the time...maybe she'd hire a security team or something? Too Eminem. A bodyguard? Too Madonna. Maybe just a buddy with a knife? Heh...
