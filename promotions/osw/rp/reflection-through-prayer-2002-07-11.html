Reflection Through Prayer

11 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07.11.2002 'I'm not a slave ... to a god ... that doesn't exist.' - Marilyn Manson I found myself tired and weeping after a long night on winding road and when I'm tired, I begin to question myself even more than I do when I'm awake. When I'm available to the rest of the world as Kory Storm, I can observe every selfish and cold individual, thus having other's insecurities to think about rather than focusing on my own. However, when you're in a car with nothing surrounding you but a dark sky ... there is a sense of fear. I know. I shouldn't be scared, especially of something which could never hurt me. I'm not scared of the skies, for in that dark hue I see my life and how black it has become. When morning arrives just like a sore thumb, I'm reminded of my once bright future which I ruined by abolishing what I was which made that future for me. Was it really my fault? Of course not, I was a child when it was all taken from me. It couldn't have been the innocent child you now see causing pain in the lives of so many people. No. It was my mother ... the woman who pulled the trigger. For I lost my mind then, along with the royalties and connections which came with it. I had to start from scratch. I arrived at one solution for my problems. Jesus. I was just one year old when my grandmother began to haul me off to church every Sunday. I knew nothing about the rape and extortion ... nor did I ever question the priest's motives. I learned quick in everything I did ... Catholocism was no different. With every candle lit and every wine glass emptied ... scriptures and hidden messages came to me. Some said I had a gift ... a gift sent by Satan himself according to who you asked. Why? The question which was asked last week, this week, and the rest of my life. I couldn't even tell myself the answer. For then I couldn't find myself, just as I can't today, and I thought the church would help me. As I gained knowledge and my mind flooded with these fake ideas, some thought I was learning too fast. That I had some sort of power and was getting a weakness out of something which always seemed to be unstoppable. Even then I raised heads, as each psychopath woman in each pue informed the next that Cory Rutherford, the wealthy one with the neglective parents, would bring the end of the Catholic Church. I knew nothing of that sort ... and it pained me. I told myself that it wasn't true, but I knew that it didn't matter what I thought about the situation. God was the only one who mattered. Confessional. The room in which my church conducted its confessions was very small and hidden from the rest of the congregation. I never bothered to wonder why, at least not at that moment. Now as I think back, I could always hear the screams of young men through the grates in the walls of the pure church. Sound familiar? Yet I packed up my pride and prepared to speak with a priest .... ending up in one of the many small rooms. I began to speak about my problems. In the end, the priest laughed at me. Not even a young boy with an ego the size of the Earth could bring down the church. It had endorsement. I had behavior problems. It had a massive following. I had no friends. Who would win in the end, I remember the mocking voice of Father Parker asking me ... surely not me. I turned away from that cage, thinking nothing of what I grew up on anymore, and ran. Fucking ran like I had in so many other times during my seemingly pathetic childhood. Ever since then, my thoughts on religion and my life in general have been a blur of controversy. Where do I find myself now? Returning to the same place which scorned me. The birds chirp and the sun shines in the small town of Boardman, close to the rusted steel belts of Youngtown and the gritty Italian underworld which no longer exists, and I'm just looking for a place to rest away from the usual noise of the city. All of Ohio is the same anyway ... mindless people following a leader with words like a whip, snapping at their backs whenever he opens his mouth. I'm disgusted. However, it is quiet. I can think now. But while I am here, I feel the need to end the thought of religion which for so long has burned through my head. Does god exist? If he does, the man has done shit for me. Jesus loves the little children? I guess that's why so many priests and ministers are raping them. Everyone is equal in the eyes of God? Then that must be the reasoning behind poverty and racism. God protects everyone? I'm sure that God was aiding me in the long run when he allowed my family to go six feet under, just to end up ending them to their own afterlives of hell in the first place. Spare me the bullshit, I'm out for reality. St. Luke's Church on the corner of Market Street ... a place famous for cheap whores and liquor stores. Maybe I could find the Lord in a pussy or at the bottom of a bottle... Sitting now on my knees, looking up at the gold crosses and visions of Christ written in Latin ... I realize what I ran from. A guiding light driven by anything but a spirit. Money. Power. Lies. Much like what drives me and my partners today. It also leads me to believe that money and power are the primary causes to the oppertunities I don't get in the hallowed halls of Old School Wrestling. Now I'm raised with a question far more important than the church. Should I make my own oppertunity? Maybe so. But as I light a candle for the souls of my deceased family members .. I know that whatever I make will be at the expense of anyone but me. Whoever I am at the moment will not be sacrificed for Ivan Stanislav or Jim Axtell ... just like I wouldn't cave into the ideals of a church ... because they weren't my own. I know now the truth. And as usual ... it hurts. Now I lay me down to sleep... Damn this haunting voice. I pray the lord my soul to keep... I beg of my mind ... stop the pain. If I should die before I wake... Stop it! Stop the lies! I pray the lord my soul to take... Is my sanity God's price for happiness? Maybe one day I'll give a fuck.
