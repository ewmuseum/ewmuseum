Date: Saturday, May 18th, 2002 Electrical cords and wiring snake along the gray cement floor of the Saddledome. Passing by various crates and cases and inbetween booted feet, they twist and turn along corridors and finally begin to branch out in an open space. Upwards, various technicians, security guards, and other staff members scurry about. A couple of OSW wrestlers can be spotted here and there as well. Yet, the focus is on a dark blue - or maybe even black - curtain, which hangs thick, as a barrier between this place, and the arena itself. Griff Truxell paces near this curtain, his heels clicking against the gray cement. His hands folded behind his back, he glances back and forth. Another day, another god damn wrestling company trying to snatch me up. Everyone wants Greatness, but only one can earn it. First it was IWR, they promised me spotlight, but one has to wonder how much quote, unquote 'spotlight' a shithole like that can provide. Then they folded out. Rival promised me gold, the world title within a few months. Then they shut down. Now, OSW is promising me the world. Will they shut down? Not if I can help it. Griff pauses, smiling at a thought that just crossed his mind. He relishes in it for a moment, the grin growing and shrinking subtley as more thoughts run in and out of his mind. A man better be damn sure that when someone offers them a number with that many zeros on the end of it, that he does everything in his power to make sure that money gets to him. Yet, that's a man; Wrestling Itself just has to be there. Besides, I actually think that this CEO has a grip on what he's doing here... ...for once. He peeks through the darkness of the curtain to the arena that lies ahead. His eyes follow the red carpet, leading down the entrance ramp and up and into the ring. There sits Howard Porter, the master of ceremonies, the life-giver. The man that will make Griff the most valuable person in Old School Wrestling history. Even if Griff think he is that already. Life is funny. You come into the wrestling world and even though you are the best damn thing there is, nobody will believe it. They refuse to believe it. So you withdrawl yourself, take Greatness out of the sport and let it sit there to rot in front of your eyes, absolutely sure somebody will pick up on the stench and try to figure out what is wrong. But nobody does. So company after company fold out or go on hiatus. Peons like Doug Troy and Erik Rave and Erik Kelly weasel their way into main event status hoping no one will notice. Ratings plummet and thousands of shit 'indy' federations open up. And after wrestling sinks so low that its at damn near apocalyptic preportions... ...people start bending over backwards to get you back to the ring. Griff smirks, the grin growing with the sudden sound of M.O.P.'s 'Ante Up.' It infiltrates the arena, echoing off walls, signaling the beginning of a new era. Griff jerks the curtains apart, light and sound flooding over him as he sets them to his sides. The light grows brighter with flashes of pyrotechnics, reflecting off of Griff's green eyes. The Truth? Welcome to my world...revel in it.
