Ivan vs. The Winnipeg Police Department

26 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-26-02 Saturday Slam was a tremendous success. Ivan Stanislav got everything that he wanted: He got the win that ensures a match against Reno Davis….. He got the chance to destroy countless OSW superstars… He got the opportunity to destroy that annoying little monkey, ZaC…. And now, Stanislav and Alexei Ruslan were walking through the Winnipeg International Airport, Canada, and were ready to head back to Russia and relax with the thoughts of their deeds firmly rooted in their minds. The tarmac for their plane was clearly in sight as the Russian duo waded through the people around the area, Stanislav standing over a head taller than everyone else. As the two got closer, however, the tarmac was blocked by roughly twenty airport security guards and Winnipeg policemen. One officer raised a megaphone to his mouth, “Ivan Stanislav, you are under arrest! Put your hands over your head and face the ground!” Stanislav and Ruslan paused and looked at one another, and then looked back at the officer. “That’s right Stanislav, you are under arrest!” the office barked at The Russian Bear. Stanislav, however, was none too amused…. “Praporschik,” muttered Ruslan, “what is the meaning of this?” “I do not know Ruslan, but they are not taking me away…” Ivan growled as he quickened his pace, destined for a collision with roughly twenty guards. “Stanislav, you are under arrest for the abuse of an animal and we are instructed to take you down!” Screamed the officer. “I am Praporschik Ivan Stanislav of Russian Federation! You cannot arrest me!” Bellowed Stanislav as nearby people slowly moved away from the confrontation. “The government of The Russian Federation will not allow this!!” “This is Canada, Stanislav, and we have our orders!!” answered the officer as the guards exchanged worried glances and took a more defensive stance. Stanislav stood straight and defiant, “Then this is my warning to you, Canadian officer! If you are not out of my way in the next few moments, I will move THROUGH you and board my plane!” Ruslan looked at Ivan worriedly and then at the officers as more guards emerged from behind the two, surrounding them. “Stanislav, the plane has been ordered not to leave the ground and it will not leave the ground so long as you are on it. Please go quietly.” Ivan ground his jaw and looked around, he took a step forward and then paused and looked around, “This is absurd! It is merely animal that I harmed! I will NOT be insulted in such a way that I am arrested because of stupid ape!” “Praporschik, perhaps you should…” Alexei began to speak when suddenly he was shoved out of the way by a guard as three of them jumped and grabbed Ivan by his shoulders! Ivan roared and spun around, flinging the three guards across the room and sending them sliding across the floor! The officer covered his eyes as the guards must have jumped the gun. He motioned to the guards around him as they moved forward. Stanislav, however, was now flung into a blind rage! He rushed forward towards the advancing guards and flew into them, sending five sprawling backwards/ Three others nearby grabbed his huge arms, but Stanislav merely lifted his arms over his head and brought them up with him and tossed them to the side as he neared the tarmac. Ruslan collected himself and backed away from the fight. He fumbled inside a pocket and produced a cellular phone and dialed a number and then jabbered in Russian to someone. Stanislav roared loudly as the officer pulled out what appeared to be a stun gun of some sort. He aimed it at Ivan and pulled the trigger. A box shot from the end of the pistol, connected to two wires that went into the gun. It attached itself to the barrel chest of The Russian Bear and whirred to life, sending electricity up and into the body of Stanislav!! Ivan roared loudly and backpedaled, bringing the officer with him as his body shook violently!! “DYAAAAA!!!” Roared The Russian Bear as he grabbed at the cord, sending more electricity through his left arm, shaking it in the most unhealthy way. Stanislav bellowed and pulled it from his body, but could not let go of the cord that was in his arm as the electricity prevented it!! Ruslan got to his feet and put his phone away, soundly defeated by whoever it was on the other line, “IVAN!!” he screamed, “They say to go along with it!! Stop!!!” The officer stopped the electricity from within the gun, knocking Ivan clean off his feet from the jolt! He landed with a heavy thud, his shoulders steaming!! Alexei rushed over, “Ivan, they said to let them arrest you..they..they didn’t tell me anything else!” Alexei was shoved away as a multitude of guards grabbed Ivan and hefted him up. Stanislav roared groggily and shoved a few away, but ultimately gave up after hearing what Alexei said. “His arms…they’re too big..his wrists can’t be bound Sir!” said one of the guards who tried to handcuff Stanislav. “Tie it with rope, steel, anything! Just get him out of here!!” answered the officer as they pushed The Russian Bear down the hallway. He growled the entire time, drooling slightly from his mouth. Ivan looked over his shoulder at Ruslan, “Get me out of here Alexei Gregorovich!! Get me out of here!!” Alexei called back, “I will Ivan!! I will!!!” Alexei watched as Ivan was taken around a corner, people staring the entire time. He checked his watch and looked around at the people staring at him, “What are you looking at?!” He turned and rushed towards the plane, already dialing his phone again….
