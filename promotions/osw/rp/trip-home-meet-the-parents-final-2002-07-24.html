Date: July 24th, 2002 ((Note: After a shitty computer melt-down, I LIIIIIIVE!!! This RP isn't exaclty great (<<<understatement of the century!).)) Finally home... Yep, Amy had endured a nine day stay in the hospital, a horrifically boring flight and nineteen years on earth just to go home to celebrate her twentieth. She'd make sure it was worth it. In fact, anything would be Heaven compared to the hell she'd gone through courtesy of Corg for over a month. She'd proven that to herself, she was endlessly happy just sitting in the little rental Jetta where she was, in the passenger's seat next to Reno as he drove down a fairly straight stretch of highway on the 'trip' into New Minas, Nova Scotia. Her legs were covered mainly by a pair of black cotton twill bondage pants with two silvery chains attaching the legs to the pockets as opposed to the regular fabric bondage straps, and they were hung halfway out the window, leisurely dangling there while her head rested on Davis' left shoulder, a rather dangerous and peculiar way to be seated in a moving vehicle, but relaxing never-the-less. Eventually they'd reached the house she'd grown up in, just a simple pale yellow single-story place with a reddish brown trim, easy enough to find in the hub-bub of the fairly small town's main road. Amy'd hopped out of the car before it had even stopped, jogging up to the door only to find it un-locked, with absolutely nobody home. ''Weird...'' She mumbled as Reno stepped in, taking a look around. He didn't say a word, just plunked down on the soft blue couch near the door with a book in hand as Amy left the room for a moment, wandering down a connected hardwood-tiled hallway. Even when she returned, Davis didn't pay a whole lot of attention. ''Well, apparently they're at my grandma's with the dogs for a visit or whatever...they'll 'be here in a little while' to 'welcome' us.'' She'd groaned, falling down onto the couch beside Reno. After a few moments of being nearly ignored, she preoccupied herself with the usual...her voice was little more than a low, tired hum when she finally spoke, twirling a few strands of her own bright red hair in her hand as she studied them. ''I should cut my hair...y'know, for a change.'' Reno laughed and nudged her head from his shoulder, ''You're crazy. What's wrong with your hair, huh? I like it just fine.'' ''Yeah...but I'm bored,'' She whined, sitting back fully upright to pout with ZaC in her lap, gnawing at her belt buckle. ''I need a change.'' ''Uh...alright.'' Campbell poked at Davis, who'd gone back to being immersed in his reading. ''Well you're a lot of fun.'' Reno looked up from his book and smiled, ''But you already know that.'' ''Maybe so,'' She inched a little closer, only to get a book shut right in front of her nose. ''Blah!'' ''Heh,'' Davis laughed. ''Not too quick, are we?'' Amy quickly shot back, getting almost TOO personal. ''Sorry, quick is YOUR department, eh?'' ''I'll show YOU quick, woman!'' Reno laughed again as he tackled Aimz down to the couch, tickling her ribs through the light fabric of her tank-top but careful not to be too rough, since the wound from Corg's knife was still quite tender, from what he'd noticed. The tickling quickly gave into playful kissing on Davis' part, ZaC playing a literal game of 'Monkey see, monkey do' by poking at Amy's sides, screeching with glee when he saw her break away from Reno's mouth momentarily to giggle and swat at the little ape. The monkey pinned one arm while Reno pinned the other, using his free hand to continue the tickling until a tall, young teenaged boy who looked oddly like Campbell herself stepped through the attached front door, his head poking over the divider to peer at Aimz and Davis on the couch before shouting ''Mom, dad...Amy's getting raped again, there's a monkey this time.'' sarcastically behind him. Davis didn't move, just looked up at the boy and laughed as Amy shouted, ''John, screw off!'' irritably from underneath he and ZaC. ''That the annoying little brother you warned me about?'' He nodded toward John, who'd ignored the two to arrange his CD collection underneath the TV. ''Yeah,'' Aimz grunted, whispering while trying to shove Reno off of the couch rather unsuccessfully. ''Ugh...you mind getting off me before my parents come in and bitch me out?'' Too late...the 6'3', fairly large frame of Amy's moustached, balding father had stepped through the door, only to glance into the living room with a smile that dimmed the moment he saw his daughter and Reno on the couch. Of course, her brother could only laugh as Davis sat up again, leaving Aimz to run over and give her dad a deliberately overly-enthusiastic hug. ''Hey, dad!'' She smiled brightly, at the same time thinking 'He ALWAYS picks the wrong time to walk in, doesn't he?'. The large man couldn't help but grin and hug his daughter back, though he kept an eye on Reno, who'd been watching Amy intently until he met her father's gaze and opened his book to read yet again. ''It's good to see you again,'' Forrester smiled, again, but that was soon wiped away when he nodded towards Davis. ''So...who's this? A new friend?'' Aimz looked to Reno pleadingly, hoping he'd sense the awkward moment and break to introduce HIMSELF, but no such luck...he did, in fact, notice the moment just by his girlfriend's expression. However, in a manner that held true to his demeanor, Davis just spread a tight grin and playfully stuck his tongue out at Amy, who promptly flipped him the bird from behind her dad's back as she introduced him. ''Uhh...well...see, this is Reno....aaaand...'' She was fidgeting, rubbing at her elbows. ''...well........you could SAY he's a friend......of sorts....'' ''You probably won't beleive it, but I've already figured out that your mother avoids telling me about your boyfriends to keep World War Three from launching.'' Her dad laughed, nodding to Reno again. ''Don't worry, I won't threaten to kill THIS one...he looks like a good kid. But, if you'll excuse me, I've got a shower to catch...heh. Wait here for your mother, she should be home as soon as she can pry herself from the 'riveting' curtain conversation at your grandmother's.'' Seeing he was in the clear, Davis stood up, readily accepting a hand shake from the much larger man who, as he stepped out of the room, still hadn't bothered to mention the obvious physical 'glitch' in his daughter's latest 'find', which Amy quickly pointed out to Reno once her father had left. ''Jeez, he never even THOUGHT to question you...'' ''Well,'' Reno laid back on the couch, letting Aimz plunk down over his kneecaps. ''My eyes are probably a welcome sight compared to the hair styles and cock rings that those other 'winners' you've brought home to him, huh?'' Now that one only got him smacked on the chest. ''They weren't ALL like that!'' ''Yeah, there was always the gay one.'' ''OKAY! Leave the homosexual out of this...'' She scolded. ''I mean, he never did anything THAT bad. He was just a little...umm...well................different.'' Davis leaned into the arm of the sofa with a smug expression, ''Yeah...sometimes I wonder why you're so attracted to me. I don't wear spandex obsessively, I have zero homoerotic tendancies that I know of, I don't have ten million slivers of steel stuck into various pieces of my anatomy...I mean, I don't even have a tattoo depicting a nude nativity scene!'' He chuckled, ''I'm just a product of a God that's got one hell of a sense of humor...that's not quite 'different' enough to be seen with you, from what I can tell.'' ''Awww...'' Amy patted him on the arm jokingly, noticing ZaC running out of the closet where he'd hidden from her father. ''Come on, you're freaky enough for me!'' ''Ohh, suuuure...no pupils, red hair and a monkey.'' ''Well I might have pupils, but I've got red hair and I happen to be romantically involved with the equivalent of an ape.'' She giggled, ''So really, we're not that different.'' ''Heh...yeah, you're dating an ape like me while I'm with the biggest pussy on earth........LITERALLY.'' He started to crack up but, seeing Amy's disappointed, somewhat irritated expression, raised eyebrow and all, Reno had to apologize as best he knew how. ''Sorry about that...must not be my night for these slut jokes.'' Well...it was SORT OF an apology. ''Fair enough...you fly far away from low blows and I won't have to make any of my own. But seeing as how you felt the need to add your attempt at one, I think I'll deprive you of a very different sort of blo---'' ''NOT funny...just cruel.'' ''Well you're the one who wa---'' She started, only to get a hand over her mouth as Reno sat up. ''...just cruel.'' He followed that up with another tight smile and another of his mildly sarcastic, usually smartass comments. ''Don't make me hire a replacment, woman.'' ''Aha! I KNEW you only took me after your last girlfriend popped!'' Yet another playful argument ensued... Slam would be quite a change of pace.
