Commu-Mania Is Gonna Run Wild All Over Ramey

3 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.03.02 Friendship. Loyalty. 2 traits Krieg models himself on. His Friendship with fellow Communists. His Loyalty to Communism and the Motherland: Germany. But Crowning Glory saw one more of Krieg's traits, one which not many had seen. Venom. Anger. Rage. All amounting to one thing: Krieg beating the living hell out of Jesse Ramey. The little wiener that never stops crying about how his recent loss is an injustice or how a match he's booked in is unfair. “How can he winge so much? Surely, he can see that there is no point crying about something that has already happened,” questioned Krieg as he sat at his desk, filing through various paperwork which had amounted on his working space. “Ahhch! Why must these American’s persist on screwing me out of money? Why, I ask you,” claimed the Berlin native, holding a bill for repairs on the roof of his house, “It was only fixed yesterday and today, they act as if I owe them millions and it’s life or death that they get it straight away. I’m not made of money.....” Shaking his head, Krieg continues to file through the heaps of paper lying about his desk, tossing empty envelopes into the wastepaper bin next to his comfy leather chair. Finally, Krieg manages to sort it all into piles of junk mail, bills and correspondence letters from friends and family back in Germany. Moving the junk mail and bills to the side, Krieg picks up a letter at the top of the correspondence pile and begins to read through it, the letter titled: OSW Signing: Michel Krieg. As he reads through it, Krieg says various bits out loud. “Michel Krieg, you are now.....blah....for one year, you will.....blah.....OSW has the right to.....blah.....ah, here we go.” As Krieg find what he is looking for, he stands up out of his seat and drags his finger over the page, under the words. “While in the OSW, you have the right to wrestle any wrestler on the OSW as long as they accept the conditions of the match-up....Excellent, just what I wanted to hear,” pronounces Krieg, dropping the paper to the desk as he begins to walk over to a drinks cabinet, slowly lowering the swing door as he reaches it. Reaching inside, Krieg’s massive right hand pulls out a small glass and his left hand swings past, a bottle of German whiskey in its grasp. Slowly pouring the smooth liquor into the glass, Krieg begins to speak, his head facing down towards his hands as he continues to pour. “Ramey, we met for the first time on Sunday, at Crowning Glory. We actually ‘met in the ring’ as some announcers say. But our meeting was not formal. Oh, far from it. I burst through the crowd and beat the living hell out of your already weakened body. And made you lose the thing you were striving for: a win. But more over, a title shot. A shot at becoming the champion. The International Champion, to be correct,” Krieg begins to turn back round to the camera, his movement rather slow as he sips from his glass of fine German whiskey. Now facing the camera, Krieg’s head is still facing slightly down. “But it wasn’t to be. Fate wouldn’t allow it. Actually, I wouldn’t allow it. Why should an American scumbag like you possess a title destined for a European? I mean, you’re not even International. You wrestle in your own country, your own state and hometown if you’re lucky. But what about us Europeans? We are far from it. Do I ever get to wrestle in Berlin? Does Tom or Ivan ever get to visit the areas they hail from on a wrestling tour? Well, I can tell the answer is an emphatic No,” Krieg’s head begins to rise as his speech begins to get more intense. “But because you’re American and because you think America is a superpower, you believe that you are entitled to a championship belt which should be in the hands of a proud European. Even better, a proud Communist. It was obvious to see that you are Capitalism. You show the weaknesses of a Capitalist.” Gritting his teeth, Krieg continues, the venom in his speech obvious, “You cry like a baby when you lose, you gloat when you win. You sicken me, and my Red Army comrades. Let me tell you Ramey: there are only 3 world superpowers: Germany, Russia and Krieg!” Krieg slams his glass down on the drawer coming from the drinks cabinet and clenches his fists in anger, raising one to his chest, trying to not explode with anger. “You know what Ramey? I hate crybabies almost as much as I hate Capitalist’s, so this coming Saturday Jesse, you will live true to your first name, and you will be the jesse of the ring, when me and Tom Walczak come down on you and Rhett Locke like a ton of German-made bricks,” claims Krieg, the veins in his right arm pumping as he continues to clench his gigantic fist. “There will be no foreplay, there will be no messing around with our hair or fixing our wrestling attire. Ramey, be warned. This Saturday, myself and my good comrade Tom Walczak are going to unleash some venom on you and Locke like the world has never seen before! I am a man of honour and a man of pride, but I cannot stand Capitalist’s who think they deserve the least bit of respect or dignity,” Growling to himself, Michel walks back over to his desk and sits down in his leather chair as the camera pans back in on him. In a Vince McMahon-style, the light from a lamp hits the underside of Krieg’s face, highlighting the curves of Krieg’s chiselled face. “Jesse, Saturday the 16th of March will be a day to remember, for 16.03.02 will be the day that Communism ran a mock of Jesse Ramey and Rhett Locke,” says Krieg, slowly raising his hand and showing his index, middle and forefinger in front of the camera. “Eins, Twei, Drei….That’s all it’s going to take The German War Machine and The Polish Punisher to defeat the Capitalism represented in Jesse Ramey….” With that, Krieg bows his head and slams his hands down on the table, the scene slowly fading to black as it does.
