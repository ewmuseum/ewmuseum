The Peacemaker

16 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/16/02 It was a cool night for August and Tempest pulled her sparkly light blue sweater tighter around her and stepped into the bar. Inside it was a warm and smoky buzz of voices along with the clatter of pool balls and glasses. Tempest slunk in and nodded to the bartender as she took a seat in one of the booths against the wall. Tonight she wasn't in the mood to party, to dance, to be noticed. She smiled up at the waitress when she approached and said, “What do you have that glows in the dark?” The waitress snickered and said, “Well, I don’t think we have anything quite that strong. Looking to drown your sorrows hun?” Tempest shook her head and said, “Nope, just wanna take them for a swim. Ok, bring me anything with an umbrella in it and I’ll be happy.” The waitress laughed and then walked off with her order as Tempest sat back with a sigh. She wanted so much to be excited about the main event, but it was harder than one might think. After an exhausting time promoting both the OSW and the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament, she was spent. And to top it off, she still hadn't heard from Ivan. I know he's angry, but still... Oh what's the use? He's spent his whole life being angry... and stubborn... and stand-offish. Maybe I'm wasting my time. At least he won’t interfere this week. He’ll probably assume I can handle the girls, which I can. But he’s gonna go out and get revenge on Corg and… shouldn’t I be avenging myself? He’ll put someone in the hospital for me, but he wont’ call me… How did things go this wrong? She sighed as her drink arrived and sipped the fiery orange concoction . Her eyelids fluttered at its strength and she said, “Waaah! Geez, they tried to make it glow in the dark, didn’t you?” The waitress laughed and said, “Those troubles will have to do some major porpoising to stay afloat in that.” Tempest grinned and took another sip, looking around the bar. It was a nice enough place, not too filled with the drunken rowdy. She liked rowdy, but drunk and rowdy tended to end badly, so it wasn’t her idea of fun. But tonight was more a night to drink. She wanted to sleep for a change. As she glanced around, her drink seemed to blur with a similarly colored object and she set it down to see the flame red hair of Amy Campbell, who sat at the bar. Her sorrows had clearly gone out for a swim too, but hers were now waterlogged and sinking fast as Aimz slumped over the bar. Maybe I should go and talk to her. This is hardly a night to be getting sauced when you’ve got an important match to train for. Not to mention that it’s match against me, making it doubly difficult. Wait a minute, I don’t like talking to her when she’s sober, so why would I want to when she’s drunk? Tempest watched Aimz for a time, and was relieved to note that Reno Davis wasn’t around. She hadn’t seen nor spoken to him since her return and was glad of it. But what of Aimz? The incident with Corg had left her scarred and she wasn’t coping at all well. The worst abusers are those who have been abused at one time or another. What will Aimz turn into after something like that, and after no one has done anything to help her? Davis hasn’t made it better, Reagan hasn’t made it better, Corg still has the cruiserweight belt and a job. It’s like the whole world has shrugged it off and said, ‘Gee, that’s too bad.’ It is too bad… for all of us. Suddenly her attention was pulled to the far end of the bar as a clatter arose and she turned to see Lillith with the handle of a beer stein in hand and the rest shattered over the bar. She barked something unintelligible at the bartender and he placed another glass in front of her. Wow, Lillith too? All we need is Bethany Yamato to give everyone a sneak preview of the main event. But I have a bit of an advantage being sober I think. I don’t understand what Lillith is doing here though. She has everything to gain in this match and she’s giving it a wake. I’ve never understood how people could make themselves miserable worrying about the outcome when presented with an opportunity. I may lose. I may also win. That’s just enough to keep me going. But then Lillith is paranoid. Of course, they are actually out to get her too. I should talk to her. I’ve been at this a long time and I’ve learned to cope with the inevitable biases. She’s right that promoters fear her. They fear me too because they’re not comfortable with the idea of a little 133 pound girl beating their big name male stars. But I’m used to it and there are ways around it. She started to stand up and go over to Lillith, but noticed that she was suddenly missing. She looked around and then the mystery solved itself as she watched Lillith careen down the bar, knocking glasses away as she went. A quick glance made it apparent that she had been launched from the hands of Aimz, who stood groggily listing to one side. Her head was bloody and Tempest noted the broken tequila bottle in Lillith’s hand. She started over and looked between the two for a moment, then thinking fast to come up with a distraction. She started to clap. Lillith was the first to notice her there. She had little choice as she rolled off the bar and Tempest caught her. “Whoa Lillith!” Tempest said, trying not to laugh as she propped one of the few wrestlers smaller than her up. “You ok?” “I’m gunna kill tha beeeootch,” Lillith slurred. Tempest managed to scoot a chair up to her butt and glanced over at Aimz who had staggered forward, collapsing onto a barstool as her head thunked solidly against the bar. Tempest waved at the bartender and said, “Another round for my friend here!” She turned to Lillith and summoned up a smile as she said, “Ok, that better?” “The roomz ssscchhpinning…” Lillith slurred wobbling a little in place. Tempest tried to suppress a snicker ands said, “Ooook, well you did a great job on Aimz there Lillith. That’s one less person to worry about come Saturday, huh?” She grinned and punched Lillith gently on the shoulder. Lillith nearly keeled over and Tempest caught her and said, “Whoa! Ok, well… you enjoy that and um, try to stay out of trouble for the rest of the night.” Lillith giggled and said, “I’sss no trouble at all, riiilly.” Tempest smiled and darted over to Aimz, who was starting to stir. She strategically placed herself so that Aimz couldn’t see Lillith nursing her newest drink and said, “Aimz! Hey great job putting Lillith out of our misery. I mean what is she doing in the main event, right?” Aimz sneered in her direction and said, “Yah… sheeez like… nobody. And her roots are showin’ too! She shunna even talk to me… I’m gunna find her and…” “Oh you don’t need to do that!” Tempest said, standing up with another wave to the bartender. “She’s out cold already, why bother? Let’s have a drink to celebrate us getting this grand opportunity!” She nodded as the bartender set another round in front of Aimz and said, “There ya go, on me.” She patted Aimz on the shoulder and she murmured something that sounded like either “thanks” or a gerbil being strangled. With a little wave, Tempest excused herself and flopped back into her booth. “God… peace through intoxicating beverages.” “Does that mean you want another?” asked the waitress coming up to her. Tempest chuckled and said, “Why not…”
