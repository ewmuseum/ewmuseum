Journey of a Trashcan Man (The Beginning)

16 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/16/02 'This is your chance to redeem yourself, Trash,' said Kriegman, hands on his hips, peering sternly down the kneelling Trashcan Man in front of him. Trashy raises his head from the ground. 'My life for you!' cries the Trashcan Man, his face a study in sorrow and guilt. 'I will not fail, master!' Kriegman turns from his 'ward', stroking his immaculately groomed goatee as he strides over to a table, picking up a bottle of mineral water. He takes a small sip before turning back. 'We've got our plan on how to punish that idiot Lexian and his brain-dead companion Extreme for sticking their nose where it doesn't belong. I know THAT won't go off without a hitch. But we still must punish Ivan. He's obviously too strong to take on physically at this point, so we'll have to hit him at his weak point.' The Trashcan Man lurches to his feet, a confused look on his face. 'Why haven't we destroyed the woman, master? Why El Drago?' 'Beating women is...' Kriegman waves his mineral water bottle disparagingly. 'It's so cliche. That's something even that plebian Toomes could come up with. No, you'll destroy one of Stanislav's staunchest allies. Crush the Mexican and we'll drive Ivan into a state of confusion as his supports continue to crumble. Yes, you will do this for me, Trash.' Trashy nods as his face lights up in a zealous fervor. 'My life for you! MY LIFE FOR YOU!! MY LIFE...' Kriegman interrupts. 'Yes, yes, your life for me, that's good. But I don't want just your life, Trash. I want Drago's as well.' The hardcore legend's forehead crinkles in thought. 'Master, I've never fought a luchadore so big. How can I prepare for such a match?' Kriegman frowns as he taps a finger on his lower lip. 'That's a good question, I've never thought of that.' 'Master! I've got it!' Trashy walks over and whispers in Kriegman's ear. Kriegman looks at the hopeful Trashcan Man with a look of disdain. 'You really think that's necessary?' Trashy nods, his eyes wide. 'Yes, master, it's the best way that I can think of! If you allow me to do this, there's no way I can fail!' Kriegman sighs as he reaches into his pocket. 'Fine, Trash, but if you fail me again...' Trash shakes his head quickly. 'I won't fail you, master, all glory to you!' 'Fine, Trash, here, you'll need these then.' Kriegman pulls out a keyring. Light flickers off the metallic objects as they fly through the air to be caught in the orange gloved hand of TCM. 'My life for you, master, and the Mexican's, too...'
