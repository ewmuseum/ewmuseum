Yeah, like you wouldn't!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Jane, honey, are you alright?” Toomes enters the darkened hotel room where Jane sits alone in the darkness. It’s been four hours since the end of Slam!, and after a trip to the emergency room to look at her forehead, they both arrived at the Hilton. Jane had gone up, citing that she felt a little sleepy from the painkillers. Obviously, wrestling and taking bumps wasn’t one of her strong points. Sebastian took it in stride. As much as he would like to snuggle with her in bed and dutifully attend to her, she was a little miffed at tonight’s events – the theft of her briefcase, the invasion of her privacy, and of course, the headbutt Ivan gave her. The last one was just part of the job, but the other two were a little disconcerting. So he went out cruising in his Camaro, listening to some Soundgarten. As ‘Outshined’ slagged through it’s grunge rock dirge, Toomes thought about certain events in the past month. Ever since Jane chose him, he’s honestly been running scared. Not that you’d know it, thanks to his jackassery on camera, but to be truthful, he’s had his ear to the ground. He’s seen a lot of the Phantom Booker on PCW television. Even though he was ostensibly across the country in Las Vegas, or enroute to wherever PCW was holding their next show, Toomes was still worried. After all, he was sleeping with his wife. The Phantom Booker. The man who built the PTC, easily the largest and most slickly run wrestling conglomerate in the world today, and even after his unceremonious ousting from PCW, courtesy of Tyler Nelson, he was still spoken of with reverent tones. Either that or fear. Those two are easy to intermingle. And Toomes was sleeping with his wife. Truth be told, Jane never said much about him. And frankly, whenever she was with him, he didn’t really pay it much mind, his mind…elsewhere. But still, it was at the back of his mind. What could The Booker do to him? He obiviously knows…Toomes doesn’t suffer from the “If I’m not watching the monitors, I don’t know what’s going on” syndrome so prevalent in wrestling, and Toomes isn’t so naïve to think that the Phantom Booker isn’t going to exact some sort of revenge on him. As Toomes drives down the streets of Miami, he thinks “He’s out there…” SCREEEEECH! The brakes of the Camaro squeal as Toomes stomps down hard on them. The Camaro’s rear end swings out a little into the next lane, as Toomes honks the horn hard. In front of him… The blonde biker. The man with the long blonde ponytail turns around to look at Toomes, sneering from over the should of his leather coat. The light ahead turns green, and the man rumbles ahead, turning left. Toomes waits behind the wheel, his breath caught in his throat, as the man guns the engine and roars down the street. “Fucker…” Toomes curses as he lets off the brake and shifts back into first, then slowly enters the intersection. “Well, that was enough fun for one night…” Toomes mutters, rubbing his sore neck. So here he is, slowly entering the hotel room. Jane lies in bed, bundled up in the sheets, the thick comforter kicked off and in a pile in the front of the bed. The TV is on, some tripe about starving children in Afghanistan is on. Toomes steps to the bed and runs his finer along her shoulder. “Hello, Sebastian…” Jane says quietly. Sebastian gets down on a knee and leans on the side of the bed. “Hi, hon…” Jane opens her eyes, the sheet bunched up to her neck, her glossy hair tied back in a bun, but some stray strands falling down her face. With the muted moonlight filtering through the break in the curtains, she looks quite striking, a big goose egg on her forehead notwithstanding. “Hon, how are you feeling?” Toomes says softly, stroking her cheek. “Hurt…” She says, biting her lip. “Awww, I know, hon… You feeling better?” Toomes smiles. Jane nods, wincing. Toomes stands, smiling gently. “Well, I checked in with the desk downstairs and they’re beefing up the security. Are you sure you’re okay?” “I’m fine, Sebastian… Are you okay? Ivan was tossing you around in there. You sure you can handle him at Head On Collision?” Jane says softly. Sebastian bends back, stretching. “Yeah, he’s a big motherfucker… But I’m sure he won’t be moving to fast after we broke a fucking fire extinguisher over his vodka-sodden skull…” Jane smiles softly. “I’m sure you can take care of him…” Toomes walks over to the other side of the queen-sized bed and strips off his Fear Factory Digimortal t-shirt, and flings it onto the carpet, revealing his bruised torso. “God, I hurt… Thank God for painkillers…” Toomes sighs as he stretches. Reaching down, he unclasps his belt buckle and tugs down his blue jeans, revealing blue bike shorts. Sitting down, he undoes he shoes and kicks off his jeans, gritting his teeth. In the moonlight, we can see the bruised flesh, courtesy of Ivan. Toomes crawls over to Jane and spoons up against her, pressing himself against her. She tenses at first, and then relaxes, running a hand back to touch him. He wraps his arms around her gently, wincing as he does so, partly from Ivan, but also because of Jane’s own bruises. “Jane, I was driving around tonight and I got to thinking…” Sebastian begins. “Yes, darling?” “Well… I was thinking about The Phantom Booker…” Jane tenses again, and clutches Sebastian’s hand, interlacing her hand with his. “Yes?” Sebastian gulps, and snuggles closer to her. “Well, I saw him on PCW… I forget what show, but I saw him in Las Vegas. And it just got me to thinking, you know, what is he gonna do about us? And…I don’t know, it just kinda worries me. What exactly is going on…” Jane rolls over to face him, and presses the tip of her finger to his lips. “Shush. Don’t worry about him… He’s gone. Past history. He’s beneath our notice.” “But honey…” “Shush, baby.” Jane says, her green eyes glittering. “He’s nothing to us. He’s over trying to get PCW back, and PCW is a sinking ship. Forget him. He’s nothing.” She kisses him lightly on the lips. “Well, if you say so, honey…” Toomes begins, and then Jane stops him with another kiss, this one much deeper and needier than the first. She lifts up her arms and climbs on top of him. As she sits on top of him, the sheet slides down her back, the sunlight shining off her skin. “No more thinking about him, Sebastian… You only need to think about us.” She lowers herself down, a hand snaking between her legs to tug down the waistband of his bike shorts. “Just…us…” She breathes huskily as their mouths crush together in a kiss, and we fade to black… “The biggest secret Is one you can’t keep Lift up your head If you’re only asleep Deny your body’s screaming But your heart and your soul they’re leaving Just to fall asleep is a godsend Until your demons appear again… I am a witness to your demise I am the one who say through the lies Give to me The one thing you can’t give Take me to The black lodge where you live” Anthrax “Black Lodge” Bleary Miami sunlight filters through the curtains, the kind of greasy light that falls from the skies, too heavy to stay suspended in the air, the pollution dragging it’s captive down to their terrestrial origins…The kind of greasy light only a city can produce. Sebastian lies on the bed, tangled in the sheets, his silver-blue hair spread out on the pillow beside him. His back is turned to the light, and we can assess some of the damage done. Fingernails have furrowed their way down his back, claw marks from the woman who is now in the bathroom, brushing her teeth. His skin is mottled in bruises, and a pattern of light scars criss-cross his back, normally imperceptible on television but all too noticeable in real life. Only a thin white sheet covers the lower half of his torso. Toomes rolls over, and the sunlight hits his face. Scrunching up his face, he rolls back over and looks at the digital alarm clock. Eleven Oh Three… “Christ, I hate the mornings…” he mutters, his tongue stricken with cotton mouth. In the bathroom, the sound of Jane brushing her teeth is the only sound, other than the soft rattle of the heater kicking on. Toomes sits up, the sheet coiled around his waist. He rubs the sleep out of his eyes and stands, taking the sheet along with him. He turns the corner of the bathroom, past the coat closet, and stands at the door of the bathroom, where Jane, dressed in a slinky nightie, brushes her teeth, a towel wrapped around her wet hair. “Good morning.” She says, taking the toothbrush out of her mouth and smiling at him, her mouth filled with toothpaste. Toomes smiles back at her, and he thinks to himself “I guess she’s right. What the hell am I beating myself up for about Booker? He’s a has-been, and he doesn’t even figure into the equation.” He comes up behind her and wraps his arms around her. “Hi, sweetie…” he murmurs to her. “Did you enjoy yourself last night?” Jane smiles wickedly. “Yes, darling… Even with the painkillers, it was wonderful… I hope I didn’t scratch you up too bad, did I?” “Mmmmm…” Toomes begins nibbling at her ears. “So what are we doing for breakfast?” “Stop it…mmm…well…I thought we could catch a brunch somewhere, then we have to get over to the arena so the crew can be briefed about the next show… We have to get some authorization for a steel cage with the people in Gulfport…” “Steel cage?” Sebastian asks, his hand snaking inside her robe. “Mmmm… yes. Timo and Meanstreak.” Toomes winces as she says it. “Nice… And what are we gonna do, lover?” Jane smiles. “You’re going to put your title on the line against Tempest.” Toomes grins. “You really know how to hold a grudge, huh, baby?” Jane pulls away and spits into the sink. She grabs a Dixie cup nearby and squishes some water around, then spits that out as well. “Yep. Hey, at least she can say she got a World Title shot. Now go get cleaned up. I got some calls to make.” “Yes, ma’am.” Toomes says, dropping the sheet that was wrapped around him to the ceramic tiled floor. He slides behind the shower curtain and begins fiddling with the knobs. Jane finishes up and walks out of the bathroom. “Make it quick, though. We’ve got to get going soon…” “Yes, ma’am…” Toomes replies, the hot water jetting out on his tired skin. As the water sluices down his bruised body, he thinks one thing. “If those CCW guys could see me now…”
