Reuniting The Army Part 2

4 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.4.02 “May I take a look inside your case, Mr...Krieg,” asked the customs officer, holding Krieg’s passport a couple of inches away from his squinting eyes, “Ya, but you will not find anything illegal in there. Only some drugs, a couple of guns and some pornography,” replied Krieg with a snigger, Scowling at him after his mockery of the security procedures, the customs officer pulled open the zip on Krieg’s case and went about tossing his clothes and wrestling to the side in search of any illegal items. On not finding any, he shoves Krieg’s belongings back into a pile, closes the case and leaves it unzipped. “Ah, you are clean. But next time, do not joke about drugs, guns or pornography. Not a good idea, my friend,” “Dah! Leave him alone, you goon,” called Tom Walczak, standing directly behind Krieg, Communist Party Member Wang peeking around from behind Walczak’s back at the same time, “Yes. Leave my good fwiend Qwieg alone, Ok? He is true Communist. Not a turncoat Communist like you, you dirty li...” Turning round to Wang, Krieg intervenes, “That is enough for now Wang,” “Whatever you say Qwieg,” replied Wang having got a bit of anger off of his chest, Zipping his bag up again, Krieg flashes the customs officer a fake smile, walking away from the x-ray machines as Walczak and Wang collect their bags after being passed through the same machine. All three men meet a few metres along the gangway. Smelling the air, Walczak turns to Krieg, “Michel, this is Russia. This is place of great opportunity and I think you shall like,” Chirping in as usual, Wang interupts, “Ya, and you will wike the women here Qwieg...Vewy nice, I must say,” grinned the Chinese Communist, Laughing to themselves, Krieg and Walczak make their way down the gangway towards the main opening leading into the main lounge of the airport. Wang jogs behind the two behemoths as they take massive strides, severely outpacing Wang’s short and quick steps. “Wait for me, guys. Don’t walk so fast,” squeaked Wang as he practically ran to catch up with Krieg and Walczak, the two giants chuckling to themselves as they look over their shoulders every now and again to see Wang shuffling after them. As Krieg and Walczak near the opening at the bottom of the gangway, two figures can be spotted looking towards them. As the 2 members of the Red Army, followed closely by their political parrot Wang, near the two figures, it can be seen there is a considerable difference between the two people. One, a massive bulk of a man, his hair short and beard thick, whereas the other, a petite woman, looks dwarfed by her associate. It is none other than Ivan Stanislav and his partner Tempest. Krieg approaches Ivan and outstretches his arm as Stanislav pulls him in towards his body, wrapping a massive arm around his back as the two Communist comrades embrace. After about 20 or 30 seconds, the two men split up and Krieg gives Tempest a slight hug as Walczak and Stanislav greet each other in a similar fashion. Looming at the back of the crowd is Wang, waiting for the right moment to greet his long time buddy, Ivan Stanislav. The moment arrives when Walczak and Stanislav cease greeting and Wang strides over to Stanislav, as best he can for a short Chinaman, and shakes Ivan’s hand, the Russian Bear’s paw suffocating Wang’s tiny hand. “It is good to be with you again, Ivan,” said Wang, extremely pleased to be reunited with his friend, “Ah, the same to you Wang. Krieg, Walczak, welcome to the Russia of Stanislav,” pronounced Ivan as the 5 members of the Red Army stood in the lounge of St. Petersberg Airport, “Ruslan and the 2 Round Faced Ladies will be here soon. They are just attending to some...business,” said Tempest, a little smile cracking the Little Storm’s face, “Ah, it shall be good to greet my good friend Ruslan once more,” said Wang, his emotions getting the better of him once again, The 5 Communist’s continue to chat as the scene, like always, fades out to black with the standard “TO BE CONTINUED” message being displayed.
