Cocksucker

13 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 13, 2002 (OOC Note: This roleplay, sadly, is based on a true story. Download ‘Suicide is Painless’ by Marilyn Manson as the official background music for this RP.) He gave his all And emptiness he obtained Heart-broken with hatred for himself Louis Miles Kidd, a middle aged man, short brown hair, blue eyes; possibly the greatest baseball coach anyone could ever ask for. After every game, Louis would take his baseball team out for pizza, buy them their own personal baseball apparel, and basically coach them better than anyone they’ve ever seen. Pain was a non-issue He felt nothing The hero's stupidity, Led to the ignition of his on-lookers Nate Simmons, 12 years old, the oldest kid on the team. He was idolized by his fellow teammates as a hero and a baseball legend. They all looked up to him and would follow him through almost any situation. Everything that was once perfect, Was now rotting away in the past The end of the season neared, and the parents of the kids on the team wanted to construct some sort of ‘Thank You’ gift for Louis. They bought him his favorite team jersey, and had all of the team sign it. Nate was the first to sign, and being the dick he was, he thought it would be funny to write something mean. So Nate wrote ‘Cocksucker’, with his signature beneath. The hero was given praise, As brutal thoughts filled a betrayed mind The rest of the baseball team followed in Nate’s footsteps, signing their name with such comments as, ‘You’re a bad coach’, ‘Fuck you’, and ‘I hate you.’ Nate laughed when he saw what his ‘friends’ had written. The parents were too careless to check what their kids had written, so they presented Louis with the jersey… The jersey, The hero was honored, Not having any idea of what he had done Heroes are fake Utter cleansing disgust He took one look at the jersey, reading the first comment he saw… ‘Cocksucker. Sincerely, Nathan J. Simmons’ Louis’s eyes filled with tears, as he said ‘thank you’, and went home to cry. He cried for days without consuming the slightest ounce of food. He was too depressed to eat, for his team hated him. The sun rose in the distance A new day was born A day without him A world without Thanks to your heroes Louis no longer had control over his mind, body, or even his emotions. Without the love and support of his team, he was nothing. If his team hated him, then so did he. He hated himself. He hated his team. He hated the world. And he knew how to fix his hatred, he knew all too well. There was a pistol that lay under his bed. A gun. He gave his all And emptiness he obtained Heart-broken with hatred for himself Drenched in crimson The gun lay in his hand Never to be cared for again As the gun neared his skull, he knew that he had to go. He knew that his time was over. Louis Miles Kidd knew that it would all be over in a matter of seconds. Nothing would matter anymore, not his team, not his paycheck, not paying his bills, nothing. All he had to do was flex a simple little muscle in his finger and he wouldn’t feel a thing. As his final tear splattered on the carpet, he heard something calling out to him in his mind. A phrase that he had never heard before, although, somehow he knew what it meant. Ain soph aur… An infinite light is what he saw, it made him feel orgasmic. It made him dead. The hero stayed, Remaining a hero, Symbolizing that no good deed goes un-punished
