Sealing the Toomes

27 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/27/02 “Come on, let’s just go down there one time, what’s the worst that could happen?” Standing before the front grille of a cherry 70’ Hemi Cuda is the man most people know and love named Timo Bolamba. Beside him, as usual is the loyal chick that he had met some six months ago, Gwenneth Cross. “No!” She replies. “You know how I hate going into these places, they give me the freaks.” “Yeah but how else do you face a freak other than to get the freaks about him?” Timo asked. “Now that doesn’t even make sense Timo.” “Sure, it might not make sense to YOU, but you’re not the one that has to kick the crap out of some guy on Saturday are you?” He looks forward and grabs a pair of bolt cutters. “Timo, I don’t want any part of this.” She replies. “But I guess I can’t really stop you once you get on one of your idiotic ramblings.” “Thank you Gwennie, that’s all I ask.” The Samoan walks up to a large gate marked “Midfield Cemetery” and begins to cut the chain links barring the door shut. He clips down on one and it flies apart, the chain falling to the ground at his feet. He pushes the door open and smiles. “See, nothing to it! Now all I gotta do is go in there and find a suitable tomb,” Bolamba said. “Timo, there aren’t any tombs here, just graves, and what you are doing is not only illegal, but also fairly morbid.” She quipped. “Now I could see Clyde, or maybe Vampir doing this, but not you.” “Come on babe, have some class! I’m an eccentric multi millionaire and I live in a mostly imaginary world, what’s the worst that could happen?” “Umm, you could get hauled away to jail and miss your match on Saturday completely? She replied. He scoffs and shakes his head. Like anyone is going to catch Timo, the guy is greased lightning! Well at least in his head he is. The truth is that he isn’t particularly worried because somehow these things always seem to go his way anyhow. Timo heads into the cemetery and takes a look around. He whips out his trusty maglight and flicks the switch, bringing a high-powered beam forth to scan the area. He scans past headstones and graves, being careful not to walk on the grass above the dead, out of respect. He looks down and sees the gravestone of Harry Peters, and stops just a moment to laugh. “Wow, that guy had some bad parents. I know if my old man had named me something stupid like that, I woulda been pissed…but what really makes Bolamba sound dumb?” The Samoan asked himself. “Wow do I have a cool name! I never really thought of it before, but what can you mess up Bolamba with!” Out of nowhere a shrill wind blows through the trees, and it carries two words with it. “Tito Labamba…..” “Eh?” “TITO LABAMBA……” “Now I know that aint my mind…who the hell is out here now?” the Samoan asked as he looked around. Timo spins around on his heel but save for the wind in the trees he hears nothing, and sees nothing. He slowly begins to back up, and trips over a gravestone in the process. “Who did that?” “Tito…..” “Man I am warning you, when I find you, you’re in for a hurting like no man has ever seen!” Timo held the maglight defensively. He heard a noise behind him and swung with the light in reflex, managing to his a tree and knocks the light out, breaking it. Timo sat alone in the dark, holding the steel shaft of the light ready to attack anything that comes his way. “Tito…” “DAMNIT! Who are you?” He yelled defiantly. “Tito, this is your ghost calling you…” “But…there’s no such thing as ghosts!” He swings the maglight in the air, whooshing as it speeds with his arm at control. “You seek to beat Sebastion Toomes, and in doing so seek to find a tomb and deface it in this cemetery…I cannot allow this Tito…” Timo put the light down and put his dukes up. He had taken enough and now he was just flat out pissed. The Samoan put his middle finger in the air and smiled as he looked into the darkness. “I swear to god I will call the Ghostbusters…cause I aint afraid of no ghosts!” A hand reaches out in the night and grabs his shoulder. Timo shrieks like a little girl and jumps nearly three feet in the air. He spins on his heel and cocks back, barely stopping as he swings forward, Gwenneth's face being about three inches from his fist. “WHAT ARE YOU DOING!” he screams. “HAHAHA, you look like you saw a ghost Timo!” she replies. Timo turns bright red, though even in the dark it’s very hard to see. A vein pops out of his forehead and his breathing intensifies. Slowly he tries to calm himself down, after all its just Gwenn, anyone else would have been taking a Samoan beat down by now. Suddenly, off in the distance a spotlight comes on and a voice over a p.a. can be heard. Timo and Gwenn look toward it and then back toward one another, and silently scream. “The jig is up!” yells the Samoan. He picks her up and begins to run the opposite direction of the light, hoping to make it back to their car in time to get away without the cops knowing whom they are. They just about make it back when from behind the hefty stonewall, a voice can be heard. “Hey, what are you doing in the cemetery?” The scene fades out with Timo standing at the gates, and a man with a pitchfork blocking their exit. Timo looks back, then forward, and finally back once more. This situation couldn’t get worse, or could it? Timo still needed to make it back to the Saturday night SLAM! Show!!
