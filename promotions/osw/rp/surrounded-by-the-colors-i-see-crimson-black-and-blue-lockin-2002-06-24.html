Surrounded by the colors; I see crimson, black and blue. Locking open doors again, I'm still afraid of you.

24 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 24th, 2002 Punk ass, are you listening? Can you hear me or are you deaf and dumb to my language? Do the real words seem to hurt you? Well put em' up motherfucker You'll feel it When I stamp it on your forehead So you will never forget That you're a reject And you're a no one And you're nothing Little impotent one A woman by the name of Rebecca Kain, wife of the man who ran the tattoo shop Amy basically grew up in, gave her a piece of advice once that stuck with Aimz for years... ''Never sleep with somebody you don't trust. There's no position in life where you're more vulnerable than when you're lying naked next to a bigger, stronger person.'' Needless to say, Rebecca had never been handcuffed to a mountain of steel and stripped by one of the more twisted predators on earth. Yeah, Campbell could talk tough and she usually backed it all up...but nobody was oblivious to paranoia after having a side so vulnerable exposed like an open stab wound, regardless of how tough they thought they could be in that situation. So she was bunked up in a little motel in some jack-shit town that, judging by the drive that she was asleep for the majority of, wasn't too far from Phoenix. Basically, Amy was a temporary prisoner...but she wouldn't have it any other way. Things were set up well: Corg wouldn't find her, Corg couldn't contact her and that meant that she wasn't in any immediate danger. It wasn't a beating she was afraid of, being violated like she nearly was could easily take a toll a thousand times greater than any near-fatal brawl...she wouldn't allow herself to be in another vulnerable situation like that and, thank God, neither would OSW. So here she was, sitting on a squeaky, cheap mattress, covered well in a pair of worn down jeans that were so baggy it basically looked like she had a huge denim skirt on underneath her Metallica flaming skull hoodie that had gone everywhere with the redhead since she was fifteen and had actually almost become too small, just from how she'd sprouted up throughout the four years of wearing it. She didn't look in a single mirror, not even when tying her hair back...the bite marks on her neck and face were just disturbing to even think of, let alone have to look at. They would scar...well, at least the two deepest bites into her neck and right under her chin would. Corg had sharp, very jagged teeth that mangled her skin the same way a set of brass knucks colliding with her flesh would... Amy Campbell was a mangled, paranoid and incredibly vulnerable hunk of wounded flesh and red hair right now. She knew it, Reagan knew it, Toomes knew it, Reno probably knew it if he was even bothered thinking about her, and she was guessing that the whole world had basically figured it out by that point. Of course, she hated being like this...she couldn't stand feeling defenceless, which is why she made sure she wasn't totally without help, the switchblade in her pocket assured her some semblance of security if needed. Feeling the weight of the silvery stainless steel pressing against her thigh, she remembered the knife's presence and quickly took it out, flicking it around, blade open, in one hand as she watched the one station that cursed motel room got; Some local piece of shit that was airing the news. Fires, blah blah blah, arrests made in a drug bust, yadda yadda yadda...when Aimz was about to reach over to flick it off, the blade of her knife made a sharp turn caused by some unconscious tightening of her fingers while she shifted herself to the end of the bed, slicing a clean little sliver in the padded spot of her palm, right by her thumb while, almost simultaneously, Corg's face appeared on the TV along with the headline ''Outrage of Old School''. Last night, Old School Wrestling put together quite a display in the America West Arena, as they had promised to do. The company was allowed to hold their monthly Pay-Per-View event in Phoenix, stating that they ''Aim to please'' and that, ''With talent like OSW has, we won't disappoint.''. Well, they certainly did disappoint. Spectators and those watching at home were outraged by the actions of Old School Wrestling employee, Isiic Raiyn, also known as 'Corg'. No clips could be legally shown on public, let alone primetime, television, but the only description of the man's action by witnesses was ''Definite sexual harassment and about as close to rape as you can come.''. Amy Campbell, the 19-year-old woman and OSW employee assaulted by Corg that night, was stripped down entirely by the speculated mentally ill teenager, Raiyn, shortly after being handcuffed to a large steel structure that various other wrestlers were battling on at that very same time. Luckily for the young woman, a supposed love interest named Reno Davis was there to stop the travesty by hurdling himself, on a skateboard, from the very top of the 40 foot structure onto the unsuspecting Corg. All those in attendance, and most-likely countless people all around the world who saw the incident, are outraged that OSW would allow Corg, who won the Cruiserweight Title by beating Amy Campbell, to keep his contract, let alone the title, after his absolutely disgusting acti---- Amy turned the TV off before the reporter finished the story. It pissed her off that the media would turn things around to make it look like OSW was to blame for the situation...but what pissed her off even more was that they were all too pansy to call Corg exactly what he was, a twisted little fuck. Then again, that's exactly what he wanted...every single thing he had done throughout his life was quite obviously a desperate cry for attention. The only thing wrong with that was the fact that he was doing some fucking messed up shit to get it. But he must've been happy, he was all over the news, everybody was saying his name...he didn't have to care that they were accompanying terms like 'Sicko', 'Freak' and 'Dumb fuck' with it, because he was getting attention either way...it was better not to bother himself with exactly what they were saying, as long as he was the topic of the conversation. ''Fucking bastard...'' She was mumbling to herself, pressing lips against the cut on her hand to stop the blood, almost wishing it was Corg's instead. After what he did, she wouldn't be at all bothered by the sight of his slaughter...if she was a little less stable, she might even want to murder him herself. Aimz did the wrong thing at that point, she looked to her right side, only to catch a glimpse of herself in the reflection from the night-darkened window. The sight of the stitches and dried blood that had run from the closed wounds in her sleep didn't make the hostile emotions cool any, they only fuelled the fire enough for her to toss the switchblade full-tilt in front of her when she swiftly turned back. A few small glass chips from the TV screen flew at her in the knife's wake...she wasn't strong enough to have shattered it, but she definitely did some damage... This was so fucking stupid, she didn't have the time to be tossing things around in some childish temper tantrum...she should have been out thanking Reno for saving her, or hunting Corg down... The only thing standing between Amy and those actions was the fear of both. She'd been turned away by Davis once, and Corg was just fucking sick...he'd probably be waiting for her in his hotel room with an AK-47 or some creepy Egyptian alter tools. She had a week off to crouch away in fear...that wasn't at all like her usual self, but after being so vulnerable in front of the world, Amy was a far cry from her 'usual self'.
