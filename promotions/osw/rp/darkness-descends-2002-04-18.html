Darkness Descends

18 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-18-02 The sun has long been fallen behind the horizon, and night is in full swing. The locusts have moved in for the warmer months, and sing in the background in the familiar sound of nature. The wind rustles through the treetops in the valley below, and combined with the flow of the river one hundred feet down create a smooth running sound. The night is cool and pleasant, brightly lit by the unobstructed full moon. The stars are barely visible, clouded behind the bright rays of the lunar surface hovering in the sky above. The wooden floorboards of the large balcony creak under Kimbusa's shifting weight. The sumo mammoth stands on the balcony in total silence, the sound of his breath not intruding into the night air. He leans against the steel rail, entrusting his five hundred pounds to its strength. With a slightly nervous look he turns back to the house and looks upward to the third floor. Still dark. Yoshi had not been awoken by Kimbusa's discreet exit. Kimbusa had been avoiding his master all day, in an attempt to find some peace and quiet, away from his master's constant teachings. Tonight, Kimbusa has his own method of preparation for the confrontation to come. Closing his eyes for a moment, Kimbusa raises his head and takes a deep breath of night air through his nose. Savoring the night's sweet scent, Kimbusa finally lets his massive chest fall, and he again turns his attention out to the abyss. His eyes seem to have rested upon something only he can see, something far out into the darker reaches of the valley. Another comfortable gust of breeze flows out through the valley, melding with the rustle of the locusts. The moonlight reflects off of Kimbusa's large black eyes, diminishing their usual ferocity only slightly. The wind flows through his jet black hair like reeds, tossing it about lightly. However, he remains motionless in the night air, still focused on the vision beyond. Suddenly, Kimbusa's head lowers and his chest begins to convulse. With a few heavy breaths the mighty behemoth appears to be fighting back tears, and suddenly jerks his head upward. His hair falls over his face, shading it from the moonlight. His eyes, slightly welled up with tears, shake with a rage unimaginable. His voice stern despite his obvious emotional instability, Kimbusa begins speaking in his native language for a few moments. After a few lines of Japanese, Kimbusa begins speaking in a low tone to the air beyond. 'I will defeat my demons. I will destroy the shadows. I will command my fear. I will regain my honor. I am the mighty Kimbusa, and I will not be silenced!' Kimbusa, catching himself in his growing intensity, grows silent and glum again. After a moment he turns away from the calm valley, and slides open the door. With a few shuffling steps he enters his home again and slides the door closed behind himself, taking great care not to wake his sleeping mentor above....
