Insubordination

22 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-22-02 Ivan Stanislav sat at his desk in Arkhangelsk with a pile of paperwork in front of him. He rubbed his eyes in frustration as sleep seemed to overcome him. The constant flying between Russia and America was taking its toll on The Russian Bear. But Ivan did not care, because outside of Russia he had a home, so to speak, in which he could enjoy a life unlike the one he lived along the wintry coast of Arkhangelsk. Stanislav lifted a large red pen from a pen holder on his desk and scribbled on the papers hurriedly as he approved various measures in his division of the military. Upon his desk a buzzer sounded as his intercom whirred to life. Stanislav put his pen down and hit the button, “Da?” “Praporschik Stanislav,” a female Russian voice sounded through the intercom, “we have a problem involving a soldier that needs your immediate attention.” Stanislav got out of his chair and walked toward his closet, “What sort of a problem, Niki?” “Well, Praporschik,” responded the female on the other line, “a Private by the name of Grishenko thought it would be funny to put shoe polish on Sergeant Shelepin’s binoculars. We thought it would only be fitting for you to handle this…” Stanislav pulled on his heavy brown jacket and zipped it up halfway and walked back to his desk. As he listened, a small grin spread across his face as he heard the practical joke, and then snapped back into reality, “Alright Niki, I will handle it, thank you.” Stanislav pushed a button on his intercom and headed out the door…. The company that housed Private Grishenko stood outside in the cold Arkhangelsk weather. Today was a particularly harsh day in Arkhangelsk, the temperature had dropped far below zero degrees Fahrenheit and the wind chill did not help as it whisked past the white sea across the landscape, and as the company stood there in the cold they knew what was coming… ..Ivan Stanislav was coming. In Arkhangelsk, Ivan Stanislav could be considered the strong arm of the base. When people got out of line, it was the massive Ivan Stanislav that came to straighten things out. As the twenty odd men stood in formation, shivering in the snow, the form of Ivan Stanislav lumbered through the snow towards them, a harbinger of what was to come…. Many times Stanislav had walked towards a company, grabbed the felon by the neck and dragged them through the snow, face down, back to his office. The next day, when they woke up from their dingy tents, they usually found the unfortunate individual digging a trench in the snow, with far more bruises on his face than when he stood the night before. In the remote area of Arkhangelsk, you could get away with anything…. Billows of snow puffed around Stanislav as he kicked through the ankle deep snow, which translated into knee deep snow for some of the men. The snow flew into his beard, giving him more of a tattered look than he already possessed. Stanislav stopped in front of the men an exhaled heavily. A wave of breath exited his nostrils like an angry animal as he looked down at the men. The wind whipped in the background as the lights from the nearby office building became blurred from the snow. Stanislav waited and let his presence set in. The boys in front of him tried their hardest not to look afraid, but it was easily noticed that they were beyond afraid to terrified. Would Stanislav handle them all? Would he take care of Grishenko instead? Grishenko stood in the second line towards the center and tried to blend in as best as possible. “Someone in this company,” began Stanislav as he paced to the left, “thinks that they are funny.” Ivan stopped and looked at the men. His eyes burned into them as he breathed out again, “Well, Ivan Stanislav is not laughing, is he?” “NO PRAPORSCHIK STANISLAV!” Responded the men as one. “You boys may think Ivan does not have sense of humor,” continued Ivan, “but he does, he has very great sense of humor.” Ivan paused and glared at the men, “GRISHENKO!!” Instantly, Private Grishenko emerged from the group and stood shivering in front of the massive Russian Bear. Grishenko could not have been any older than twenty years old, and in the wake of Ivan Stanislav he appeared to be nothing more than a tiny flea in the snow. Ivan glared down at him, “So, you are comedian, eh? I hear that you thought it would be funny to humiliate Sergeant Shelepin?” “Da Praporschik!” “And did he laugh?” “Nyet Praporschik!” Ivan gritted his teeth, “Well, if Shelepin does not laugh, Ivan does not laugh!” “Da Praporschik!” Ivan ground his teeth together and took a step back so as to address the group as a whole, “You children are lucky! If it were a month ago, I would take Private Grishenko here and make sure his number of teeth were cut in half!” Ivan crosses his arms, “But I have decided that this time, you get off with warning, and nothing more….” The group blinked in amazement. Ivan Stanislav was letting them go with just a warning. “If you do anything of the sort again, however, I will personally make sure that your lives will forever be a life of hell, understood?” “Da Praporschik!” Stanislav nodded, “Get out of here, I do not wish to look at you anymore…” The men nodded and marched away as Ivan rubbed his chin for a moment and looked up at the rising moon… …..from the office building in the background, General Leonid Tomifei stared through the snow splattered windows while Stanislav handled the situation… “It is just as I have suspected,” muttered Tomifei in his decorated uniform, “he is not doing a damn thing to those troublemakers!” Tomifei looked over his shoulder to the secretary sitting at a desk behind him, “You!” The secretary hopped to his feet and walked over to Tomifei, “SIR?” Tomifei spoke in a cold, harsh tone, “I want to speak to Praporschik Stanislav in my office IMMEDIATELY! Get a hold of him as he is returning and direct him to my office…..NOW!” The secretary rushed out into the cold as Tomifei buttoned up the top button on his uniform and headed out the door towards his quarters. Stanislav should be there shortly, he had better get ready to handle him… Tomifei sat at his desk and busied himself with what appeared to be paperwork. What it really was was nothing more than a sheet of paper that he wrote on to appear to be busy. It always was a good tactic to ‘appear’ busy as the person you were about to chastise walked in just to show that they were infringing upon your time. Stanislav, meanwhile, made the slow walk down the hallway towards his superiors office. To Ivan, he figured that with the sudden change in his performance in the OSW he was in line for a reward of some sort. After all, the fans had begun to embrace him as well as even more money coming through him towards the Russian Military and the people as well, what more could he ask for? He was in for a rude awakening, however…. Stanislav opened the door, took one step into the room, and sternly saluted to his superior. Tomifei nodded to Ivan, not even making eye contact, “Yes Ivan, sit down…” Ivan blinked once, slightly confused by the reception. He didn’t salute back? He didn’t address him as Praporschik? Things were not going well..and Stanislav had only been in the office for…three seconds? Nevertheless, The Russian Bear took his seat at the chair in front of Tomifei and waited. And waited…and waited while Tomifei busied himself with his paper. Finally, Tomifei looked up from his paper as if he was surprised by Ivan’s presence, “Oh, Stanislav, that’s right, you walked in here, didn’t you?” Ivan crossed his arms over his chest, “That is correct, Sir.” Tomifei leaned back on his chair for a moment and stared at Stanislav, “I would like to talk to you about your current progress not only in the OSW, but also in regards to your overall performance in relationship to The Russian Federation.” Ivan nodded in response, knowing better than to chime in while the General begins to speak. “As for your development within the OSW, we are rather pleased so far. You have garnered a chance to win OSW World Title. You have won over the faith of the fans within the federation as well as bolstered the strength of your group, which you reverently call….” Tomifei pulled out a folder and read it, “The Red Army…I believe?” Ivan answered sternly, but obediently, “Yes General, that is correct.” Tomifei paused and looked through the folder and then glanced back up at Stanislav, “As for your duties within the Russian Federation, you appear to be stretched to your limits, would you not agree?” “With all due respect, General,” responded Stanislav, “there are no limits that can overwhelm Praporschik Stanislav.” Tomifei sighed, “Yes? Well there are some that would say otherwise.” Ivan raised his eyebrow. “Last week we kept track of your travels, Ivan, and here is what we found: Monday through Wednesday: Present in United States. Thursday: Arkhangelsk Friday: Vladivostok Friday Evening: The United States Saturday: Canada And now, Sunday: Arkhangelsk…..” “General, I…” But Ivan was cut off quickly by Tomifei. “Praporschik, I do not understand these constant return visits to The United States. Besides your scheduled wrestling matches, why should you be there?” Tomifei leaned back in his chair and crossed his arms over his chest. Ivan paused and thought for a moment, he knew what Tomifei was up to. He wanted Ivan to talk about Tempest and the time he spent with her, but Stanislav was a step ahead, “Yes General, but you must understand that with being in federation involves also dealing with the public. With Praporschik being larger figure, he must therefore be more noticed within the populace.” Tomifei stood up with his hands behind his back and walked to the window behind him, “Praporschik, tonight I specifically asked that you be called to deal with an insubordinate conscript. Did you do so?” “Da, General, I did.” “If that is the case,” answered Tomifei, “why is he sleeping soundly in his tent?” Ivan rubbed his chin, “Praporschik thought it would be fitting to merely give conscript a warning, rather than deal with him in more…conventional means.” Tomifei turned, as if staring at an animal he was about to kill, “You think that making a joke out of a higher officer is excusable?” “Not at all, Sir,” answers Ivan, “but I find it rather harsh to physically damage a strong, young man for merely having some fun…” At this, Tomifei turned on his heels, “Fun?” Ivan swallowed, “Yes, General, ‘Fun.’” “I do not believe I would ever hear Ivan Stanislav speak about ‘fun’ as being, excusable.” Tomifei raised an eyebrow at Stanislav and stood stone faced. “Perhaps Praporschik is growing soft in his old age?” Ivan growled, a look of rage shot across his face and then subsided, “Permission to speak freely….Sir?” “Denied Praporschik.” Tomifei’s upper lip curled as he continued, “I have noticed that you have been preoccupied, Stanislav, and I am not the only one to take notice of this either. When you entered the OSW you were focused purely at task at hand, but now, it appears that something is clouding your judgment.” Ivan merely sat still while Tomifei continued, “Ever since you met Temp….er….February, you have changed.” At the mention of Tempest, Ivan shot up in his chair and sat taller, “What does Tempest have to do with any of this?” Tomifei stood sternly, “Your request to speak freely was denied, Praporschik.” Ivan stood, his body consumed with an urgent air, “Sir, with all due respect, what does Tempest have to do with any of this??” Tomifei burst into a yell and walked up to Ivan. While Ivan dwarfed the aging General, he still seemed to be quite small in comparison with him. “I THOUGHT I TOLD YOU TO BE QUIET, PRAPORSCHIK!!” Ivan swallowed and remained silent. Tomifei turned his back on Ivan and walked around the table, “Alright, you want to hear what you ask, so be it!” Tomifei stood behind his desk and fumed, “Since you have met this female, Tempest, your performance in the Motherland has dropped! She has broken your will to fight for the state! She has clouded the vision of our program! She has weakened your ability to stand up to your enemies! She has poisoned your judgment! She has sabotaged your plan!” Tomifei walked to the far side of the desk and turned, “You have used money given to you from the Federation to go to her place of living and have your ‘fun’ that you love to refer to….” Ivan tried to interject, “Sir, the people enjoy…” “THE PEOPLE MEAN NOTHING, STANISLAV!!” Tomifei pushed the papers off of his desk, causing Ivan to jump even! “Those people don’t need to know anything except that Russia is the leader of the world, and I intend to continue that WITH or WITHOUT your little love affair! I have NEVER had a problem with you until now, and I do not intend to deal with one now!” Ivan blinked, unable to answer. Tomifei decides to continue, “The consensus of the High Command is that you break off your ties with Tempest! Any objection, Praporschik!?” “Sir,” answered Ivan, who now stood tall and proud, “Tempest is an acquaintance of whom I have met personally, not professionally. Therefore it is not the State’s business as to….” Tomifei shot over to Ivan and pushed his chest with a finger, “YOU are property of the State, Praporschik, therefore you do WHATEVER it says, WHENEVER it says!” Ivan swallowed and looked down at Tomifei. “If you do not follow what we ask you to do, we can find someone else to go fight in the OSW!” Tomifei paused and breathed in deeply, “Never would I have expected the “Great” Ivan Stanislav to fall so far as to insult his commanding officer and the Hierarchy of Russia as a whole! Never would I have expected a mere female to get in the way of you duty to your country.” “Sir, she is not getting in the way, I do not understand why there is such a problem!” Ivan fired back, unable to handle the abuse any longer. “Stanislav, it does not matter what you think! It only matters what you do! And what you must do is make sure she is eliminated from you life. And if you do not, we can make sure it happens through other means!” Tomifei crossed his arms in front of Ivan. Stanislav lurched forward and towered over Tomifei, his voice taking a deeper, more threatening tone, “You will not do anything of the kind….” “Get out of here, Praporschik!” Tomifei pointed at the door, “I will let you know that the President himself will be hearing of your insubordination!” Ivan growled and saluted Tomifei and then headed for the door. Tomifei continued to berate him, “You do what I say, Stanislav! Your life is MY life now, boy!” Stanislav grabbed the door handle and turned, “So I am being reported?” “That is correct, Praporschik.” Ivan paused and swallowed. He thought long and hard, about to do something he never would have fathomed: break an order. “Well then, Sir,” began Stanislav, “I will not break off my engagement with Tempest. It is none of your concern! And if you wish to suspend me for it, go ahead!!” Ivan paused, “But the people will ask questions..and you do not want them to get a reply from their hero!” Ivan turned on his heels and marched out the door. Tomifei stood seething and muttered to himself, “Stubborn jackass….he should know better than to play with the fire of Leonid Tomifei….” Tomifei turned to his desk and grabbed a phone, “Get me Ruslan….Da….”
