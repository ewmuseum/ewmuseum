I Do Not Want What I Already Got

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dear Jennifer Betcha didn't expect to hear from me, did you? Sorry about Jane getting rid of you again - when everything is said and done, she's the boss. And I happen to love my job, even though I'm going through a rough patch right now. I have a title match this Saturday against Rhett Locke. I guess he's pissed that I've been running him down backstage about him and his title reign. I didn't want to end up like Triple H, but I guess that's who I've turned into. I'm not sure if I like that comparison, it doesn't say much for my character. Not that the business ever mattered much to you - I know why you were there, and I know - at least I think I know, how you feel. You wouldn't of shown up at the hospital if you didn't feel strongly about me in some way, shape, or form. I'm not going to presume anything. But right now, I'm bound to Jane. She's given me my career, basically. I owe everything to her. And I think I'm kind of in love with her, although it's like the lead character in A Clockwork Orange, when he's right over a bowl of food, and he says 'I have a feeling something bad is going to happen soon', or something like that. I don't know how, or why, but I just have an uneasy feeling something is going to happen. I can't quantify it, its just there, like that smell before a thunderstorm, the ionizing of the air, the feeling you get on your skin, the intuitive sense of it coming. Then again, that might just be the match on Saturday coming up. I don't know what Porter was thinking when he booked this. He's bringing backstage heat into the ring, on TV, and I'm not at all pleased with that. I don't want to be a Shawn Michaels backstage, being a primadonna, telling people how things should be run. That's how CCW died, and I don't want to see this happen to OSW. At least, I want no part of it. I'll go to the pre-match meeting and see how they're going to book this, but I just don't feel right. Maybe Porter is trying to really wreck me, the same way he did with Armour, only more subtle this time around. All I know is that I don't like this, Jen. It doesn't feel right, and I know its going to turn out bad for everyone in the end. Well, I hope you're doing alright, and I think about you. Even though you hated me...heh heh... You did your job well, and I'd welcome you back if the position was open. But hey, its Jane's business, not much I can do, even with all of my supposed 'backstage clout'. Take care, Jen. Love Seb
