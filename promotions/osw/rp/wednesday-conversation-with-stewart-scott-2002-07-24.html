Wednesday Conversation with Stewart Scott

24 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 24th 2002 Stewart Scott: Hello everyone and welcome to Wednesday Conversation. I know that we usually do this show on Sunday, but we decided to get our freak on. Last week we had Graphic Violence, well this week he's back and he's brought a friend. The most feared duo in sports entertainment is in the house ladies and gentlemen. NC-17. GV and Silence, welcome to the show. Graphic Violence: I thought I told these jackasses at ESPN that I didn't want you to conduct this interview. You f*ckin' annoy the hell out of me. Where's Kenny Mayne? Silence: Kenny Mayne that little pile of sh*t? I would rather have Mickey Mouse do the interview since Disney owns this f*ckin’ station anyway. Stewart Scott: He's taping Sportscenter right now and we like to say that we own Mickey. Silence: What about that Rich Eisen? Stewart Scott: Live on location at the national spelling bee. GV: Well, just make it good Scott. I don't want to have to rearrange that ugly face of yours. I might actually make you look pretty. Stewart Scott: No doubt dog. Now as usual you were stirring up a lot of controversy of the EW 24/7 radio show. The question was asked but I think it needs to be addressed for those who missed it. What's the deal with you constantly interfering in Rhett Locke's matches? GV: It's simple. Every time Rhett steps in that ring he's a move away from losing that title. And I'm not going to let that happen. Rhett will remain champion until he finally accepts my challenge. No one is going to take that title from him but me! Silence: We said it on the radio, what we want is what we get. If we want Rhett to hold onto that damn title until GV gets a match then that’s what will happen. Scott we have said it from the start, NC-17 from the beginning has out-matched fools like Locke and now its just Rhett’s turn to have his head shoved up his ass. Stewart Scott: The fans have dubbed him a paper champion, a puppet if you will. Your actions are not allowing him to prove them otherwise. GV: Exactly. He is a puppet. My puppet. He'll lose when I want him to lose and he'll win when I want him to win. Silence: You need to realize that all of our opponents at one time where our puppets. Just with Locke we have decided to play with him a little longer and sooner or later we will sever his life line and GV will become the World Champion and if not, well Scott you know what an Arabian Face-buster is? Stewart Scott: No, can’t say I do. GV: Lets just say that would make you look f*ckin’ pretty. Stewart Scott: I feel you kid. Now let's talk about NC-17. You brought Silence into OSW and it's well known that when people talk about the greatest tag team in history, a majority of the fans mention NC-17. Silence: Why do they mention us Scott? I will tell you why because when they watch the camera pan to our opponents standing in the ring and our music hits, they begin to realize that pure and utter uncontrollable rage is walking to the ring. You see I bet you have never walked to a ring and watched your opponents shake our sometimes just run away but we have and we will until time ceases our place here. You see we don’t just wrestle our opponents, we give the people in the ER a job to go to at night. GV: That's right. We're the most dominate force in wrestling. And we have entered ourselves into the tag tourney. There is no man that can beat us in single competition. So when you stick the two of us together, it's unstoppable. No team could beat us. Taking the tag titles is going to be like taking candy from a baby. Stewart Scott: BOO-YEAH! GV: Mother fu- Stewart Scott: Now Silence, what's goin' on with SDL gettin' all up in your grill kid? Silence: Kid? You f*ckin’ calling me kid? I haven’t spent 13 years in wrestling to be called kid by some mother f*ck who was last picked for the kickball team and never got a date until the senor prom with his mother. As for SDL he is a has been and always will be. I rearranged his face on Slam and I will gladly do it again. He has a problem with me stealing his spotlight then he better step up to the plate and prepare to f*ckin’ strike out. I didn’t get anywhere in this business by backing down from a young kid who thinks he has f*ckin’ game. Stewart Scott: Yo son, you notice I'm wearin' the new Jordan kicks? These are phat. Silence: Now you want to call me son? Listen here I am about to give a public demonstration on how to kick an interviewer’s ass. And you will be my f*ckin’ dummy. Do you have any idea what I can do to your head with my right foot? Stewart Scott: Fo' shizzle. BOO-YEAH! GV: I'm gonna rip your throat out Scott. Stewart Scott: So you guys will take on Rhett and Tom in a tag match this Saturday. Thoughts on that? GV: It's going to be murder. The people will finally get to see what NC-17 is all about, as a tag team. Graphic Violence and Silence finally back in the ring together after a year. It's going to be a show the people will never forget. Silence: There is a reason that myself and GV team together. We are both on the same page. To many times you get Tag Teams that separate and leave each other because one becomes greater than the other. Well not us, we both are f*ckin’ great and we know what the other is doing before he does it. That and we like to put the hurt on everyone and make their lives more of a living hell then ours. Stewart Scott: Anything else you two want to say before we go off the air? GV: NC-17 is going to grow. NC-17 is going to be the single most dominate force is wrestling. You thought DX was something? You thought the WCW version of the nWo was bad ass? Well let me tell you something Scott, Vinny Mac should be takin' notes from us. He should get his VCR ready and start recording from this Saturday on, because what we're going to do is going to change wrestling forever. And there is not going to be anyone who can stop us. Silence: NC-17 never was a marketing label and never will be. We brought NC-17 to life and no promoter wanted to see us wrestle together again but here we are and what started as a Tag Team will grow. NC-17 is about to become an army and with GV and myself as commanding Generals, who will stop us? Stewart Scott: You guys are straight butter, cause you're on a roll. You two are as cool as the other side of the pillow. You two… Graphic Violence gets up and punches Scott in the face. Scott sits up only to be met with a roundhouse kick to the head from Silence GV: Next time I want Rich Eisen. At least he doesn't use the same f*ckin' lines over and over. Silence: That’s why they call them educated feet. Stewart Scott: BOO-YEAH fellas! That was awesome!
