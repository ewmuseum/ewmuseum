If Ya Stink I'm Sexy....

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23.04.02 ....”If you think I am sexy, come on move your body, you can let the Kriegster know....” A strong male voice roars out from behind a bathroom door, the constant spraying of water from the shower duelling with the hefty German voice, “....Dah, nah, nah, nah, nah, NAH! Dwah-nah-nah-nah-naa! Dwah-nah-nah-nah-naa! Sexy baby....You drive me crazy...” Stomping around in the shower, Krieg’s massive feet can be heard thumping on the shower floor as he ‘gets his groove on’ in the only way he can. Nudging the door open slightly, the cameraman manages to sneak into the bathroom, which is rather steamy. As the steam starts to filter out of the bathroom door, Krieg can be seen inside a misty shower cabinet, swinging his arms around and moving his feet like they’re on hot coals, “Dwah, Krieg! You are such good dancer,” he says out loud, invoking the cameraman to giggle, Krieg hears the cameraman and whips open the shower door to see the weedy American cameraman standing dumbfounded in the middle of his hotel bathroom. Growling at him, Krieg stretches out and grabs a towel, wrapping it around his midsection as he steps onto the bath mat, “What are you doing here, American pervert? Trying to get some of Krieg action,” “Emm...No. I’m just here to see what you’re up to since you’re not booked on Slam,” “I am not booked on Slam, yah? Well then, I think it’s time for Krieg to do relaxing. How about some shopping? I think I should treat myself more,” “Sure. Whatever, Michel,” “It’s Mr. Krieg to you, schmuck,” “Oh...Whatever,” “Dah, let me get dressed and we’ll hit the shops,” Backing away from Krieg, the cameraman walks out of Krieg’s room as Michel closes the door and proceeds to get dressed. Still with the camera focused on the door of Michel’s room, the cameraman, who happens to be called Jeremy, waits for about 5 minutes when the door swings open and Krieg steps under the doorway of his room, narrowly missing it with his head. Dressed in a pair of black jeans, a grey long sleeved t-shirt, black Adidas trainers and a skip cap, Krieg looks set to hit the town, as Jeremy follows him to the lift 50ft or so away from his room, “It is hard to shop for big man like me, you know,” says Krieg, turning round to Jeremy with a grin on his face, “I’m sure it is,” replies the small American cameraman, Both men wait at the lift for a minute or so, Jeremy’s camera pointed at a 45 degree angle upwards just so he can catch the top of Krieg’s head. The lift *pings* as the doors swing open with a crack and both men step inside, Jeremy once again standing behind the massive Michel Krieg. Krieg jabs the “G” button on the lift with his massive paw and the doors slowly close as the lift begins to shoot off down the floors. The floor indicator flies by quickly... 45... 42... 39... 35... 30... “So, Capitalist, where should we go to shop for Krieg? He is picky man, you know,” said the Berlin native, a slight grin spreading across his face, “Emm. I don’t know. You might not want to shop the places I go...Don’t think they’d be to your liking,” replied Jeremy, who also turns out to be one of the half-cast Goth-Moshers, who can make their mind up whether they worship Satan or Kurt Cobain, dressed in a pair of dark baggy jeans, a black mesh sleeveless top with a ankh chain under it, a pair of flat skating shoes with flames on the side, and numerous chains and bangles dangling from different parts of his scrawny body, “Yah, I do not think I want to look like a cross between a piercing nightmare and something pulled out of the gutter,” said Krieg, a look of slight discuss on his face as the lift shot down through the remaining 20+ floors, 23... 20... 17... Looking a little offended, Jeremy looked up and Krieg and thought to himself, I may be a freak, but he’s a damn Nazi oaf! Sniggering slightly, Krieg hears Jeremy’s giggling again and casts him a look of anger, quickly stopping any comical thoughts he might have had. Suddenly, a female voice announces their arrival in the lobby, “Lobby, Ground Floor. Enjoy your stay in the Hilton, and come back soon,” sounded the pre-recorded voice in the lift, sounding just a little bit cheesy, “Dwah! Shut up, stupid woman,” roared Krieg as he stomped out of the lift, Jeremy following closely behind, “I think we shall do some shopping before I meet Walczak in bar later. Follow me, cameraboy,” Krieg strutted towards the doors of the lobby, leading out to the street in front of the hotel. The doorman opens the door slowly and gives some form of greeting, but he doesn’t open them fast enough for Krieg’s liking, as the German monster whips the door open, sending the doorman skidding across the marble floor in front of the doors, bumping down the stone steps with a thud. Taking a second to laugh, Jeremy lags behind Krieg and jogs to catch up with him as the screen slowly goes out of the viewer’s sight, fading into a small pin hole like many 1970’s cartoons do.
