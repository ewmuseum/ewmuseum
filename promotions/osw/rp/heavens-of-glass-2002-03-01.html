Heavens of Glass

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-01-02 Do you remember your first love? Of course you don't. You mindless drones with your pittling little 'love lives'. Hah. You don't know the first thing about love. You think just because this person you're with is on your mind all the time, is constantly by your side, and every evening, satisfies your libido, you must be in love. Primal numbskullery. Each and every one of you goddamned ingrates. Thinking you have nothing while your fellow man is out there on their own, clawing at the earth just to keep alive...those are the people who know what love is. Those are the people who know about devotion, caring, and admiration. The ones we subconsciously long to be with. You don't know as much as you wish you did. You don't know as much as you hope you did. You f*cking deluded, self righteous, glory hugging pricks and your imaginations that can never seem to grasp all you'd like to. None of you know a god damned thing about life. None of you care about anything or anyone but yourselves. Not a one. Sure. Question the validity of that statement. Make your pondering little notions and queries and try to prove me wrong. Make your comments, line up for your ordered questioning. None of it will do you any good. Because, simply, you are all egomaniacal, ignorant swines. Feeding on your wants instead of your needs. Those people, those lowlives as you snot nosed children wish to call them...those are the foundation of your very being. They are the reasons you live...and the reasons you die. They are the reasons you're even able to dream and the reasons you're plagued by your nightmares. Your own silly thoughts playing with your own silly minds. F*cking weaklings. And they're the crazy ones? Hah...get your heads out of the clouds and open your f*cking eyes. You better open them or he will. Lines of saliva snake freely from his jowls like some maddened, rabid dog. Meaty paws lashing out, wishing to strike anything that makes any motion resembling fluid movement. His eyes, usually a calm, psychotic blue, now raging, burning with a lust reserved for reasons other than sexual pleasure. Like a mindless simpleton slamming those fists into the ones before him...4:00 and he's already begun. Supper's mighty early this evening. The way you act, so childish, so unfortunate. All out of jealousy. You want them to procreate with you so your children grow up with that ounce of wisdom and intellegence you were pushed out of line for. But, what will the other cretons beside you think? Their simple minds...so focused on appearance, so entranced by a higher bustline, or a bulging genitalia. This entire world...run by a mass of quivering children who looks upon things so lowly. Thinking you're a god among men. Maybe he's that god. Maybe he's the reason you think you're in love. The lack of violence in your life. The lack of anger you just can't control. Maybe he's the reason your children have grown up morons. He's the reason you're on your knees, choking on three of your own molars. You think you know what you're doing. You like to believe they do, too. It is the actions of those 'lunatics' that keeps you moving. You wait for an action and react, never freely thinking for yourselves. You wait till your other neanderthals get hurt, then rage back like some omnipotent war machines. 'The simplest things are often the strongest'. Makes you think, doesn't it? An entire WORLD, smashing down those lower than them...why? Power. Simple as that. Simplicity is always the reason the masses enjoy their lives. Their entertainment. Find your plot holes, find your ways to degrade it all. You had better wake up and dive out of the way. That lunatic, one of those lowlives, is coming straight at you, armed with his bare fists. Something's driving him, making him lash out in blind rage. Never have you seen such chaos. Never have you heard such screams, such muffling cries of agony. Never have you run as fast as you are now. Men flying left and right behind you, like you're in the middle of some horror movie that you just cannot claw your measily self out of. Women and children diving to the ground for safety. Sticking low is not the thing to do. Spines crack and necks twist in unimaginable pain. The two audible noises that make your assumption the correct answer. The groans emerging from the man continue as the dull packing sounds of fists burying into their targets grows all the louder, as his feet bring him inches from your heels. You turn and all your beliefs change. The banners hanging above you in the shopping mall become fly paper for the people he flings back and forth behind you. They clutch and attempt to cling to the pieces of fabric so as not to suffer the fate of the fourty foot fall to the floor their feeth ad originally been planted to. Glad you got out today? No, I'm sure you're not. You wished you'd stayed in bed with that 'love' and stroked her hair for a few more hours...but...what's this? That's right...he's caught you. You're now dangling from his paw like a fish on a hook. And that look in his eyes...beyond anything present, lost to the things happening around him, thoughts blinking their way out of the back of his head in an instant. A simple growl. Low and abandoning. Your eyes widen as your body and brain try to connect so you can do that useless flailing everyone seems to do in the most dier of moments. You've seen this man somewhere before...and despite his physique, you actually have the gaul and arrogance to think you can free yourself from his grasp. It's in the middle of a shopping mall, now around five minutes after four o'clock and this nutcase is displaying you in the air like a sacreligious idol. You take the moment to peer down to his upper body, for some ungodly reason covered by no clothing, no stitch of fabric...just that jagged lettering over his massive deltoids and that intimidating barbed wire tattoo. You cringe at the thought of just how long it took to mark his body with those things. You wonder what it would be worth, why he would even consider such an odious thing. Growing fear suddenly empties itself through every pore in your body as he swings you from left to right. You don't know what he's doing...and by that consistent, blank stare resting across his face, he doesn't either. He grunts and slams you down to the cold linoleum tile. The back of your head slams into the fuschia square beneath you and all turns black. A flash and another second later, and you swear you saw yourself on your 4th birthday at your grandmother's house. Heh...trauma's exciting, isn't it? The man pulls you behind him now, dragging you like the hunter bringing his catch back to the tribe. But he's in no tribe. No one rushes to his side to help divulge in the carcuss at their feet. No one runs to him and throws their arms around him to congratulate another fair catch. They all just spread, as if he were a human plague and they'd catch the sickness from just looking at his rippled flesh. You try to focus but there just isn't any point to it all. The blood now freely emptying onto the tile beneath you and smearing across the floors seems to be rushing out slower. You don't know if it's because you're running out of the precious liquid or if your skin is trying to repair itself and clot to prevent any further damage. But nothing will prevent the pain you feel as he pulls you up into the air and glares at you. You know your dead. He's killed before. You can see it, no matter how distant his gaze may be. As he lifts you higher into the air and pulls you down just as fast, a single tear shoots out from your eye. Your assumption, this time, quite inaccurate. He blinks, haulting any movement of his massive arms, and stares. Straight at you. Like a recovering heroine addict, finally reuniting with his estranged family. A grin of pleasure, happiness, and pure, infinite euphoria absolutely drenches his face. His eyes widen, mouth opens in a ridiculous fashion...even the way he holds you up seems gentler. You can't help but to smile. Why you do is beyond us and yourself. We don't know why you do it. Neither do you. Nor does he. He lifts you higher again and tosses you. You gasp in surprise and, as your spinning, the world seems to stop. You smile wider feeling like your about to take a trip to some faraway land to meet up with every single friend you ever had. To reunite with every pleasurable moment in your life. Every instant your foolish thoughts spun your mind into a blaze of excitement. You spread your arms as if you were to sprout wings at that very moment and soar through the double plated skylight windows above you. Instead, your 4th vertebrae from the base of your neck, shatters like a dinner plate meeting steel. You fly. But no wings sprout from your undeserving back. Your world speeds back to play from that slow motion button and you see the bench in front of your eyes. With almost pinpoint accuracy, your forehead slams into the dullest portion of the seat and caves in like dynamite blowing a new oraphice into a rock face. You leave us and we go to him. That look of abandon is far from gone on his face. He shoots his head left and right as the people around him stare like he's some sort of freak in a sideshow, labeled for their enjoyment. His hands swat out again, right arm failing to grasp a single soul, left grabbing the entire skull of a five year old in its grip. The girl screeches to such a pitch, our ears fathom how it can be possible. After a moment's observation, we find that pain brings out the impossible. 'Quietcherself...' A command laden with irrepressible rage. The girl does so in an instant as he pulls her up to his face. His attacks are much less chaotic now and much more thorough. After digging his knuckles between the shoulderblades of that last man, he has no idea what he's done. Every recollection of what happened in the seconds he played out the motion, but no clue that the man was actually without breathing. Nothing was on his mind but this now quiet, limp, child in his single hand. Her age shows well upon her rosey, rounded cheeks and chubby knees, but her maturity and self control is amazing. This animal of a man, adorned with the barbed wire, twists and turns her back and forth. A normal person would have yowled in pain from the thrashing. But her...she...all she does is stare straight back at him. His eyes gloss over for a moment and he drops her. His knees hit the ground beneath him and his arms fall like dead beasts to his sides. The girl uncoils and turns back to him from her fetal position, tears now streaming down her face, yet no cries escaping her lips. Slowly, she skulks forward. The man's head locks back and his mouth opens to the ceiling, eyes rolled back into the back of his head. Apparently, he's clocked out for the day. The child seems to think along hte same lines as her slow crawl becomes fairly quicker, speed building up over those knobby knees and little, undeveloped muscles pulling her ever closer. The people seem to be following suit, like a pack of mindless, brain hungry zombies. The little girl reaches out a hand to touch this creature's knee...I can actually hear you screaming for her to pull away, run, and never look back. Regret fills your emty body, resting next to that bench. ...but, she's actualy going to touch him...isn't she? Like Arthur Pendragon pulling the immortal Excalibur from the stone, a single index finger, resting itself with just an inkling of pressure upon the thing's knee...and his head shoots forward. Now is the moment for this girl to shriek...and that she does. VERY, VERY loudly. You want to clasp a hand over her mouth in an attempt to quell any more anger building within the lunatic before her, but you've long since left us. He simply tilts his head and stares at her. A cocky grin slithers across his lips...and he laughs. From the voyeur's homes, the glass upon their television screens rattle, the plastic masking those speakers rumble to such a degree they swear the manmade material will give out at any moment. Now, again, that girl misplaces things she should have done earlier. She turns and stands, the crowd around her screaming in their own lackluster tones as they race away from the beast and the child. Her heels, lined with fear and adrenaline, carry her like a newfound olympian across the tiled floor. He still booms out an uproarious laugh that will forever haunt her fragile little mind. The girl darts forward at alarming speeds...the beast slaps his hands, palm first to the floor. As if he were the child in this scenario, he begins pulling himself forward on all fours. A hideous crawl looking as if it were pulled straight from the screen of a Friedkin picture. She looks back. Pile on another mistake in the five years of her life...and file this one on top. The instant her eyes meet his, fear rushes from her feet and up her legs. It filters through her body like a rapid venom and sends her sprawling to the floor, releasing everything through her chapped lips. The creature of a man leaps forward, astoundingly far, eclipsing her and nearly smearing her in discarded limbs across the tiled floor. Instead, he shoots to his feet, pulling the little girl up by her ankle. We all watch the look in his eyes. That sort of hunger being fulfilled and sustained as he purposely brings the child's head in contact with the floor beneath her. Again, another trophy on display, held up like a bounty hunter claiming his prize. 'SHALL I FEED?!' The crowd stops in their tracks and as if they were a trained army, turn their attention towards the screaming question. They know not what he wants of them, but stare to feed their own greedy curiosities. He hoists her further towards the skylights providing their path to Heaven and roars. An almost inhuman tone that brings those very heavens down in a shower of glass. The crowd dives in panic and fear, screeching to their hearts delights, like lapdogs to a god being overthrown. The underlings gather themselves as his roar dies down, their migrant hearts being placed in a new home, entranced under his power. But, this lowlife, this maniac...one of those beings they despised. Bowing under that display of power...giving in to his violent, unabashed nature...things were finally changing for the better. No longer would they live without knowing love. No longer would they live as dependants...no longer would they fear burdens. However...everything must come to an end. A natural show of arrogance masks one of the men standing at the beast's right. A flow of courage and immeasurable stupidity fueling him to actually attempt a lunge for the girl. Safety and heroism are on his mind. Now the beast's boot blasts them out with the force of a pickup truck. The beastly animal looks down to the man, laying in the fallen one's lap, groaning in agony and repressed hope. 'YA THINK YA CAN STOP ME?!!?' Anger dipping his words in sharp, painful rasps. Arrogance, he obviously has. But he can back it up with his very own instinct. Animal urges driving him to see things before they happen, to live on his own without a care. To apathetically acknowledge their weakness and exploit them for the rest of his compatriots to wallow in. This was no man. This was a monster. Our point is proven further as he brings that child into the air, her world tossing and turning as he flips her into his palm by her throat. Long, muscular fingers clasp and touch eachother behind the back of her neck. He's not squeezing and she is not choking. The blood rushing to her head and rising through the lump in her head makes her highly uneasy. Legs begin flailing and the monster's heart grows restless. Apathy creeps into him as he drops her upon that very bench serving as your tombstone. Her tailbone pushes past her buttocks and cracks as it hits the bloodstained wood. Bouncing down onto your lifeless body, her cry turns his attention to you. Now, he's known what he's done. A foot brought softly to your side in a means to surface any sort of movement brings an eerie, repetitive murmur to your thoughts. You watch as his eyes dart about his now silenced subjects and then work their way slowly back to you. A moment's regret drips into his eyes and those ice cold irises turn a shade darker. The monster thrashes his head back and forth chaotically, as if it were to spring from his head and scurry away from his decapitated body. The man clutches his head and falls to his knees. Your decaying mind races in panic to search for a logical answer to every question rising to awareness. Your first thought is that regret has taken its toll on him. Your second reveals him feeling sorry for such an atrocity. No answers come to you as a fist slams into the chest of what once served as your body. Over and over. Sickening thuds. Every shot of those paws of his echoes throughout the deadened mall. Public eyes stare in disbelief as they witness your body collapsing in movieesque fashion. You wonder just how mentally unbalanced this beast is to be bludgeoning a dead body, ten minutes after its been robbed of life. The girls rolls free to avoid any more injury, nursing her backside and stretching into what we assume is her mother's shaking arms. The two watch as he continues growling and snarling, baring his teeth as he continues his desecration. But, moments after he's begun, he stops. Just as soon, he stands and looks around, widened eyes staring upon him like that freak he is. The man exchanges a few glances and crouches down, tearing the white polo shirt from your body like a grave robber. The cracking sound of every bone in your shoulder rings through the ears of the onlookers as he pulls the last bit of material from you. Your body slithers slowly back into place as he slips that undersized shirt over his upper body and begins to walk away. A look of confusion burdens his face as he takes a few steps forward. You suddenly gasp for breath and his eyes widen in astonishment. One last breath and you're choking again. You turn your head slowly over to him, that one functioning eye registering very little. He blinks and seemingly ignores you. You watch, blackness threatening to pull you in at any moment as he ever so slowly turns back to the girl and her mother. The girl brings her hand up to her eyes to catch her falling tears and he...smirks. You look at his eyes and you know that maddening lust is back. He steps forward as the mother stands there, mouth agape and body still entirely frozen from his earlier event. A lunge forward and she's swooped up in his palm. You try to witness what happens...but that blackness snags you in and pulls you away, leaving you with the rest of your afterlife to ponder what occurred. Shrieks were the last thing plaguing your eardrums. Death has indeed embraced you. You lived a good and prosperous life. You gained a wife that you 'loved' and three children to amend any wrongs you may have done in that life. Now...tell me something...is it really Heaven if you have a child's voice shrieking constantly in your mind till the end of your days?
