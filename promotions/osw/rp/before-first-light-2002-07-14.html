Before first light

14 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2002-07-14 2:13 AM. The heart of the night. Ebb tide. Outside the Gund Arena, the fans have long since gone home. the parking lot is silent except the whipster of newpaper blowing the wind, and the distance sound of a lone city bus struggling up a hill. The arena itself is dark, the wrestlers have already left, secluded in their hotel rooms for a night of restfull slumber, before having to leave for yet another crowd of screaming people, calling for blood and pain. An emergency exit cracks open in the building, revealing nothing but the lightless interior beyond, until a form seems to detach from the inside and move into the dim light of the city, reflected off the clouds. Seemingly made of darkness, it pulls the door shut, and begins to walk into the parking lot. Lillith. The bootheels of her leather books click on the asphalt as she walks away from the building, towards the only remaining car in the lot - a red 1992 Grand Am, with a large dent in the hood. The lights of the car flash briefly as the doors automatically unlock and the car starts. She pulls the door open and slides in. 'Hmmm.... it's Sunday now...' she muses, as she closes the door. 'Church day.' A thin smile plays across her face at that thought, 'I bet Propaganda and Eric are in their hotel room right now, visions of angels and saint and 12 year old choir boys dancing in their heads. I guess there's once good thing to being a preacherman... free boy sex.' Lillith starts the car and starts to drive off, when a side thought gives her the giggles for a good two minutes, 'I wonder if Steven Smith should take up a new line of work.' Lillith drives for a good 15 minutes before arriving at the hotel where the majority of the wrestlers are staying, including aforementioned Propaganda and Eric Sagar. As she passes the front desk, she leave a request for a wake-up call at 8:00 AM. When the night clerk comments that that doesn't leave Lillith much time to sleep, Lillith grins, giving him a sudden case of the shivers. 'True,' she says, 'but I have to get up for... church...' Lillith laughs out loud as she walks to the elevator, and the desk clerk draws back from the front counter, his eyes never leaving Lillith until the doors slide closed.
