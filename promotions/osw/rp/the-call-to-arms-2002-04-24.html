The Call To Arms

24 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/24/02 Date: 04/24/02 From: [detail withheld] To: [detail withheld] CC: [detail withheld] BCC: None Subject: RE: Sunday Plans Dear Andrew and Marcus, I hope you enjoyed the box seats I’ve been sending you. I know you’ve been enjoying the way things work here at Old School Wrestling and I’ve sent some backstage passes to next Sunday’s Head on Collision Pay-Per-View for you. Also sent with the UPS system will be two plane tickets here, so we can ink the deal. It’s set up exactly as you wanted. As soon as you get in town, call up my secretary and she’ll set up the meeting. Everything is perfect for you to come in, considering our Plan A failed/ As you can obviously tell from the programming, were having a fucking hard time keeping these tag teams in check. We got the Champions, Heresian Bloodlust, tearing shit up and running rampant over half the roster here, so we need some enforcement. Civil Disobedience was doing a good job, but they went to do their own thing and even they are out of control now. We need you two to turn this division into the kind you had going in PC-Dub. Just fuck up these teams and put them in their places. Your Plan B, because I don’t think the Red Army is going to be able to stop Heresian Bloodlust’s reign with the shape they’re in. With Ivan screwing around with Tempest, they’re out to lunch and probably aren’t focused, so you gotta keep these champs from taking over the division. I’m only going to be acting CEO for awhile, so you better get over here quick and get this deal done. The contract is perfect for you, you’re making the same amount of money as a Main Eventer for doing half the work. It’s non-guaranteed, so if you get injured or if Jane comes back, there’s a chance you could be gone. But don’t worry, after she checks out the ratings, you’re locked. She’s too greedy to let you guys go after seeing the kind of shit you’ll be doing. Plus, once she sees the way you’re going to pacify the problematic teams, she’ll have no quarrels with keeping you on-board. Even if Hostyle gets a little cranky about, I’m sure his bitchiness can’t beat her money-wanting ass. But don’t even worry about her yet, that Motorbike-ridin’ masked douchebag took her out of the picture for awhile. Just remember, be at the Pay-Per-View. I don’t care what you do, just make sure the Tag Champions and Civil Disobedience know you’re here to mess them up. I know I can trust you, just don’t get sidetracked boys. There’s a lot of “distractions” around here, so don’t get off topic. Remember, you’re here to run the division. It’ll be tough, but if anyone has a chance of taking down those two monsters it’s you. Watch it though, Civil Disobedience won’t take this intrusion laying down. They’re two tough sumbitches too, so don’t get cocky. It’s not going to be easy, but get a hold of those titles by any means necessary, that includes sidelining either team if two’s too much for you boys, so get out there and teach these rookies some lessons that we learn down in Cali. Come down as soon as you can so we can ink this thing and you boys can get in the ring and do what you do best. Da Westside Is Da Bestside, CEO Ed
