Umirat.... (Pt 1. Intervention)

17 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-17-02 He sat in his locker room, his elbows on his knees and his hands clutching his skull. What he had done tonight was borderline criminal. The amount of pain he had caused and the damage he had wreaked was unlike anything even he had ever done. Whenever he thought back to what he had done, it chilled him to the bone, particularly those that had watched him. But Ivan Stanislav wouldn’t have had it any other way. Saturday Slam. Tom Walczak and Vampir Nosferatu are fighting for a shot at the International Title. But backstage Ivan Stanislav can’t wait any longer. He stomps towards the ring while the match continues, knowing that after this match, Corg would finally have to face The Russian Bear in the ring, one on one. A technician gets in the way of Stanislav as he marches towards the entryway…. “Mr. Stanislav, it’s not your…WHOAAA!!!” he screams as Stanislav shoves him angrily out of the way and through the curtain, sending him rolling down the entryway! The crowd cheers as Stanislav emerges and tromps down to the ring where Nosferatu and Walczak continue to wrestle! ~~ Steven: Ivan Stanislav is here!! He looks angry!! Robbie: No shit, Sherlock! ~~ Stanislav climbs into the ring, feeling the sudden heat from the lights above him and the smell of sweat within the squared circle. His mind rages as he wishes for Vampir and Walczak, and old Comrade, to stop their match and delay him any longer. Vampir rushes at Stanislav but The Russian Bear catches him before he ever makes it and levels him with his huge arm. Walczak rushes at Stanislav for a spear but his training takes over before he is even a threat. Stanislav boots Walczak’s iron skull with his size 22 boot and staggers him. The burning inside of Ivan yearns to get Corg into the ring, and therefore he has to eliminate the two men he faced at the moment. “GGGRRRAAAAAHHHH!!!!” Ivan rushes at Walczak, grabs him, and lifts him up over his head with ease! Three hundred and seventy pounds is no weight at all as Ivan hears the crowd cheer for him! He roars angrily again and dumps Walczak out of the ring to the floor, fully aware the Vampir was not completely dispatched yet. He turns just as Vampir locks up with him, but he’s got the size, height, and endurance advantage. With a tilt of his back and a grunt, Stanislav lifts Vampir up into The Red Scare, the move that has eliminated countless many enemies before him, and flings him easily out of the ring. ~~ Nate: RED SCARE OUT OF NOWHERE!! ~~ Vampir lands heavily on Walczak, knocking both men to the floor. “GET OUT OF HERE!!” screams Ivan, his voice unable to be heard from outside the ring as the crowd cheers and referee Betty Lovejoy calls for the bell, which is what Stanislav has wanted the entire time. He notices the microphone as it flies into the ring and catches it in his monstrous hand. The hand of Ivan Stanislav crushes at the metal stick, eclipsing all but the small metal microphone at the tip. He knows that if he clutches harder, he’d shatter the microphone, but Stanislav chooses to save that for Corg’s skull… “COOOOOORRRGGG!!!” Ivan bellows into the microphone, his eyes bear into the entryway. The speakers throughout the arena echo and screech from the assault of The Russian Bears voice, but he does not care. He knows Corg can hear him. “I have waited too long!! Your stay of execution is up!!” Corg doesn’t emerge, which prompts Stanislav to lean on the ropes for one last call. If he doesn’t have the guts to face Stanislav, he’ll find him instead, “COME OUT AND FACE A MAN, CORG!! COME OUT AND FACE A MAN!!” The moment Corg enters the area, after his blasted music hits the arena, Ivan lurches forward and stops himself. Let Corg enter the ring, and then Stanislav will show him what true power is. ~~ Nate: Corg doesn’t wanna fight Ivan!! ~~ Ivan hears Nate over the cheers of the fans. He hears him while the music plays in the background and Speedy Riggs hollers to Ivan over his shoulder. Corg will fight Stanislav or die running away. “GET DOWN HERE NOW CORG!!! YOU WILL PAY FOR WHAT YOU DID!!” he screams into the microphone as Corg shivers in the entryway and shakes his head ‘no’ again. Just as he’s ready to exit the ring and go after Corg, Jane Reagan emerges from the backstage area. The mere sight of the tramp causes Stanislav’s blood to boil even more than it had before. Stanislav barely recognizes what Reagan says as he stares at Corg, all he needs to know is that Corg must fight Stanislav. When she forces Corg to fight him, he must admit that for once Reagan is actually good for something. Ivan discards the microphone and sends it rolling out of the ring as Corg bursts forward towards the ring. He thinks he can get the jump on Stanislav by using his speed, but The Russian Bear is one step ahead of The Silent Assassin. Every time he closed his eyes… he saw the pained, teary faced expression of Tempest. And when he watched last week, Stanislav saw that same look in her face as she fought Corg. Corg would be spared no mercy…
