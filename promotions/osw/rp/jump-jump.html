Jump!Jump!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Begin File.] “Jump! Jump! Jump!” The crowd’s rhythmic chant ebbs and swells in the clear March air. Chico and Cobol hang back in the fringes, their mechanical ‘otherness’ concealed by trendy, yet umm… concealing cloaks. “Unit Gunmetal’s actions are endangering the mission unacceptably.” Cobol’s strangely flat tones cut through the shouting throng’s chatter like a hot chainsaw through water. “That idiot might as well wave a banner, ‘Evil Robots bent on world destruction, heroes apply here.’ The only reason we haven’t been smushed to used hubcaps already is because nobody believes that wrestling stuff is real.” Chico grouses. “Robots are superior to humans in all ways.” “Yeah, whatever. I suppose I’ll have to talk him down. Outta the way, meat bags! Move it! Yeah, I mean you! DON’T you look at me like that, grandma!” Quite rudely, Chico begins pushing through the crowd, pointy elbows in the lead. He presents himself to the police manning the barricade. “Back off, son. You don’t wanna lose and eye or something to shrapnel if that boy jumps.” The grizzled cop spits in punctuation. His partner grins. “Yeah, I seen a bit o’ tooth near go through a windshield before. Happily, it wasn’t the squad car. They take it out of your pay for things like that.” “No, no, they only do that for stuff you can’t prove was in the line of duty.” “Oh, so the donut stains…” “Would be in the line of duty and thus exempt, yes.” Chico waves to get their attention back on track. “I’m his… umm… brother. I think I can talk him down.” The cops instantly click into You’re-a-civilian-and-therefore-not-to-be-trusted mode. “You got proof of that?” “Sure, hang on a sec.” A strange whirring noise emits from Chico’s midsection. Both cop’s eyes narrow suspiciously. “What’s that noise?” “Oh, I had burritos for lunch.” All suspicion fades. Chico hands over a freshly printed photo. “Careful, it’s a bit wet. I’m, uhhh… very nervous that dampness is sweat and NOT ink drying.” “Of course.” “Completely understandable. Ooooo! You were at Vegas?” “Yes. See that’s me over there by the Sphinx with the dancing girl.” “Yes, I can see the resemblance between you two.” “It’s from our father’s side. We had different mothers.” “Sorry to hear that.” “Eh. So, can I go up?” “Yeah, c’mon.” With his newly gained police escort, Chico breezes through security and is escorted up to the top of the building. There, he can see Gunmetal, clinging tenaciously to the hook at the very end of a large construction crane mounted on the roof. Beneath him is nothing but empty air and some very unforgiving concrete. The balding police psychologist squinted at Chico through thick lenses, “Try not to get too close to him when you go out there, he might panic and try to drag you off as well.” “Any other bits of advice?” “Yeah, try to stall him long enough for us to get the hoses set up. The mess is easier to clean up when it’s fresh.” One of Chico’s escorts leaned over. “Don’t mind him, he’s only got a week until retirement, but we don’t have anyone else until some time next Wednesday.” The psych tucked a fresh cigarette between his lips. “You should put on some safety lines… but I really don’t care if you don’t.” Taking this as permission, Chico nimbly scurries up the side of the crane, easily navigating the handholds and footholds until he is at the tip-top of the machine, looking down at the dangling Gunmetal. Vreeeee! With a firm grip on the braided steel wire, Chico slides down towards the giant hook. “I hardly see how this helps.” Gunmetal swivels his cameras up. “Go away.” “You’re upsetting Cobol and jeopardizing the mission.” “And that means what?” Chico ponders a few moments, which is roughly analogous to a few weeks of human time when one considers the speed at which his processors run. “Not much really, but it does attract attention that I’d rather not have.” Gunmetal looks away. “So go!” Chico: “But you have so much to live for! Think of the humans cowering! Think of the world domination! Think of the upgrades you’ll never have! Heresian Bloodlust and 3B and Michael Dame and Jesse Ramey just aren’t worth it!” Gunmetal looks up again. “What in the hell are you talking about?” “What? Aren’t you suicidal about our next match?” “Pfft! No! I am here to get in a little bit of quality time,” Gunmetal pats the giant hook fondly. “Time that you are so rudely interrupting, I might add!” “Really?” “Personally, I am offended that you think so little of me. Suicidal about our match indeed!” Chico ponders again. “Yeah, it WAS a little bit silly, wasn’t it? Comes from listening to humans again, I suppose.” PING! PING! PING! The cord jerks slightly, and the bots rapidly focus in at the base of the cable. There, Cobol is methodically separating and breaking each of the individual strands that make up the wire. PING! Cobol turns to the two. “The humans in this area have learned too much. Units Chico and Gunmetal are to self-destruct upon impact. This unit will eliminate remaining carbons and retrieve memory cores of units Chico and Gunmetal.” Chico holds out a hand in desperation. “WAIT! Couldn’t we just walk down there and slaughter them all instead?” Cobol pauses. “Yes.” PING! PING! PING! The last bits of the wire are efficiently torn apart. [End File.]
