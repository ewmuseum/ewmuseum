An Introduction...

23 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Saturday, March 23, 2002 'This place sucks.' Nick sat on the couch of his suburban home. He didn't really feel like traveling with the rest of the workers. To tell the truth, Rhett Locke kinda scared him. He didn't like the idea of travelling with the infamous super robots, Twisted Metal. And let's just say that Ivan Stanislav scared the fuck out of him. Lousy Ruskies. Flipping through the channels, the overly dramatic Casey always had his neck brace on for the cameras. He wasn't really one to shy away from getting public attention, but he figured it a way to make the crowd hate Vampir more. He thought that if he could pin the Epitome of Evil while going throught he disabilty of a crippling neck injury, well then he'd just be even better off in the eyes of the fans. 'They may not be the brightest,' Nick points out, 'But I love them.' 'Vampir Nosferatu, you don't realize that you made the single biggest mistake of your life. I mean, my debut might not have been much, but it was MINE. It was MY moment to shine, it was MY moment in the sun, and you made such a huge mistake in sticking your dead nose where it doesn't belong. What could possibly make you think it was smart to chokeslam one of the world's greatest wrestlers, EVER?' Nick turned off the TV, and took a long chug of his Pepsi. 'Ah, that's the good stuff.' As Nick winked at the camera, it sickens me. Leave it to Nick to stick a promotion in a promo. 'But now you have to pay the price. Yes, you might be teamed with me. But don't you worry. I will make sure that even if it costs me the match, I will have my revenge. I'm not one to dwell on the past, but I never forget when someone crosses me. The Great Dragon is caught in the crossroads here, but he's not important enough to worry about. The time will come when you can't run anymore, but for now you will just have to watch your back.' Nick further looked through the card that had been posted in the newspaper. 'Great Dragon? Whatever...The Magi? Who gives a shit. It's just the same fWo Survivor kids all trying to make their names off of another federation. Well you know what? Make it here. Like I am going to. But you know what, I can't be bothered with shits like you. ' That's right, Evil. I'm gunnin' for you guys. You see, buddy, pal, friend, they call me The Show for a reason. The fact that you even THINK you can beat me, atrocious. I don't care what you did in Five Star, I don't care if you screwed the VM herself. Truth is Shawn, you don't stand one fuckin' chance against me. It's just another victim to The AngelDriver, and soon, it will be the Angel's Last Fall.' Nick smiled to himself, reading up on the synopsis of the match. 'People ask me why...I'll tell you why. I've been busting my ass in this business for so long to get to where I am. The fact to you're some genetic freak makes me know that it's the only reason you got into wrestling. But calling yourself Evil is no way to make a living. You might be able to powerbomb some idiot in an indy promotion in Texas, but you're not all that bad.' 'I've beaten bigger.... 'I've beaten badder... 'And I've certainly beaten BETTER than you. Seven feet tall and three hundred pounds doesn't scare me anymore. Maybe in my younger days it would have gotten the better of me, but for now, you're just another flash in the pan. It's just like basketall, size can only take you so far, but in the end, it's really only the talent that matters once you hit the big time.' Nick grimmed again. 'On Slam, you got welcomed to the big time.' 'This Slam, you get initiated. By a man so good, he was touched by the hands of God.'
