Elements of Life: Severed

10 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 10, 2002 Corg awakes, to find himself on the same, blood-stained concrete that he was crying on moments ago. It is the same concrete in which his girlfriend has just previously died on. It is late at night therefore darkness fills the streets of Charlotte. Cars are whipping by on the 6 lane [7, counting the turn-lane] road. “Wha… where… where is Shome!” Corg yells at the top of his lungs, barely stable to stand on his two feet. But screaming the name Shome, he suddenly remembers. Something that has been passifying him for the longest time. There isn’t anyone he knew named Shome. Corg is drowsy, and he can’t remember much. He can remember that his love has just died, but he can’t quite remember her name. Sadness, emptiness, thousands of emotions fill Corg’s once happy heart. I can’t believe she’s gone… The psychic was indeed accurate with her predictions, she said that Corg’s girlfriend would die soon, and sure enough she died exactly four minutes later. I never got to say goodbye… “Fuck!” Corg exploits, but he quickly falls down from the energy it took. His vision becomes blurry, and he feels a black out coming on. Goddesses can’t die… Everything a round Corg is looking very odd. There is no definite color to anything, everything has a few shades of a certain branches of colors. He is unable to tell if he is awake or dreaming, or if his eyes are open. What was her name… Just before goes in a long state of rest, he remembers again… His lover didn’t die, she was killed. A taste of Corg’s own medicine. Those fuckers shall die too… A taste of revenge. fin
