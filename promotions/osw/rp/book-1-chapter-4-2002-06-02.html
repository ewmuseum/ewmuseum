Book 1 -- Chapter 4

2 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 2nd 2002 This is a joint chapter. Rhett Locke is featured. Chapter Title: Insanity The camera pans up from outside a building. Once it reaches the top we can see the letters that make up the words “George Pearson Centre Mental Ward”. The camera then quickly flashes to the inside of the ward where a man wearing blue scrubs is forcing another man into a room. “NO! NOOOO!” screams the man. “Don’t make me go back in there. Please! NOOO!” We continue further down the hallway and enter a room on the right hand side. There is a doctor present, and four other hospital employees. Lying on a couch in a straight jacket is none other than OSW Superstar Graphic Violence. “Now tell me one more time Mr. Shadows, what is your name?” “Graphic Violence” “Are you positive that is your name?” asks the doctor. “YES! How many fuckin’ times do I have to tell you. Clean out your fuckin’ ears you stupid prick!” says GV as he begins to get up out of his seat. As he tries to stand the other four men are quick to restrain Graphic Violence. “Now let me the fuck out of here. LET GO OF ME NOW!” “If I let you go, where are you going to go to?” “I’m going to see Rhett Locke.” The doctor rubs his chin. “I see. Do I look like Rhett Locke to you Mr. Shadows?” “No, you don’t you dumb FUCK!” yells Graphic Violence as he tries to kick the doctor. “Ok, that’ll be enough for today. Take him back to his room and heavily medicate him.” The four men begin to drag Graphic Violence back to his room, but not without a fight from GV. “You dumb pricks. You better pray I don’t get out of this straight jacket because when I do I’m going to make sure that you all are placed inside a fuckin’ body-bag.” The men put GV inside his room which is white with padded walls. One of the men sticks a needle in his neck that has some sort of green liquid. The men then run out of the room and lock the door. GV stands, but his eyes begin to get heavy rather fast. He drops to one knee, then another and finally falls to the floor. Sleeping like a baby. **** 'Christ.' I take a long drag from the cancer-stick placed between my lips. It's pretty damn cold for summer in Canada, I can tell because of my hair being blown harder than a guy with a cheap whore. Fuckin' canadians and their cold summers. Mr. Emphysema hits the ground, and I ash it out, before stepping through the doorway. I get a few odd stares when I hear a girl scream and laugh, hell, I guess I'd give me cold stares too. Wow, I forget, I don't give a damn. Smart ass receptionist, 'Sir, are you a visitor?' damned prick. 'Yeah, why not?' She furrows her eye-brows. Hey, watch me give a fuck, lady. 'I see...and who are you visiting this evening, err...' 'Rhett Locke.' 'Okay. Who are you visiting Mr. Locke?' With a slight smile, I run a hand through my hair and reply. 'Steven Shadows.'
