Goodbye.

8 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01-08-02 I looked into the cracked glass of a little old mirror, ravaged by time, barely showing me my own, aging face. I stared back at those red…gleaming eyes, eyes that once struck the weak down as though they were bullets. Eyes that reaped through their souls, almost destroying them before my might could. Those eyes held many vision dearly in the banks of my memory. As I stared into the mirror I could feel myself reliving those moments…I could feel the body of Totally Tubular Tommy crumpling under me as I finished him off for that 1...2...3…for that NTICW United States Championship. I could feel my muscles ache even after I raised my own hand in victory, and showed that title to the world, my first championship…and it was mine… I felt a smile emerge across my face, ripe with tales and stories of the past, detailed into the very scars that graced me. As I looked back I saw the first one, the first nemesis of mine, almost glistening before the holy symbol of Christ…and then I saw myself take him by the throat, lifting The Josh to the heavens…and driving him straight to Hell…breaking through some steel mesh on the way. A smirk. That was probably my greatest achievement. A mark against God. My way of saying “Fuck you” as it were. I had already driven another into the bowels of the Unforgiven, shattering a feeble wooden table as I soared from the top rope, taking that little bastard with me. As much hate filled me then, as much does now, I look back and relish in the memories attained from the time I spent there. I wouldn’t change it for the world. The Josh…Totally Tubular Tommy…The Rush…Xavier Kannon…The Pharaoh…we made NTICW…we made it. My eyes stung. I wiped away the glistening streak of pain running down my cheek. In the background he lay there, tied up like some kind of prisoner…our prisoner. A few expletives graced my ear, his opinion on a big man shedding a few tears echoed in my mind. I looked in the mirror, then turned and gave him my reply…I bet the steel really hurt as it made impact with his face. Gibson’s voice drowned out with a few moans and I resumed my stare in the mirror. NTICW was only the beginning. I had attained gold but twice there…and throughout my stay I had made it clear religion was on my side…as the broken body of a failed Christian showed. But then my mind took me forward. To a cold winter’s day. To a place regarded by many as simply just…Heaven. PCW. Where fighters were men…and men were legends. Again I saw myself, the prime of NTICW were there too. All that made it great were here, ready to revolutionize it…well…the workers anyway. I ignored them though, and simply remembered, revelled as I watched my victims soar over the fine red line and into the burning fires. To this day it’s one of my crowning achievements…but before I could capitalise on it…I received “the calling”, with many others…which made me grin. I cleaned the mirror a little to examine myself. I always looked good behind a mask. I always will. The other ventures…tournaments…duties…I remembered those well. Tough times for me…but I treasured them as I treasured memories of my greatest battles…but to steer back to my main purpose… My heart skipped a beat as I took myself back ever so slightly to the “new dawning”. Another had risen up from the dirt and soared straight to the heavens, nobody could stop it. Not Russian Bear, not Evil Supergenius, it soared up even past all those bright, shining STARs. I had made the transaction almost careless of what I had left behind. It wasn’t worth much. Not as much as the memories I had of the olden days. That would soon change though, as I embarked on a journey fraught with danger. But I liked it. I had a companion on my travels, and together we overcame all odds. Nothing stopped us. We were as an Armageddon. Nothing stopped us. The journey, the danger, Ambrosia to us. Gods feasting on the best they had to offer. How ironic they say ‘Good things come to those who wait’…and then they slap you in the face with ‘Nothing lasts forever’. I looked into this mirror before me, and embedded deep in those ugly, grotesque scars were the very scrolls that read of the demise of that Armageddon. ‘Nothing lasts forever’ is of course reinforced by ‘You can’t change the past’………if I ever met the idiot that devised those sayings I shalt smite him as I smote all the others. My eyes opened suddenly, and I shook my head. The mirror was still dirty, musty and old. A mind is a terrible thing to waste. I sat there amongst my new family, with the only explanation as to why I was there. The Ultimate Evil had plucked me from the grasp of my Final Nightmare, and taken me under a wing, a wing belonging not to my saviour, but to a great hurling beast raining down as though mightier than Hell itself. I was as I am now because Vampir Nosferatu saved me. He…It…might as well have birthed me, for we were of the same blood, thick and oozing, dripping with a sour poison…injecting our venom into everything in my way...in our way. The ages were vandalised with a name shining in the poison…blood…death of others…my mind flashed, I could almost watch it in the mirror…names flashed past…Vampir…Bethany…SDL…Minion…Caleb…Azrael…Riptah…Shorty…Seth…Trevor…Ivan…Tempest…Meanstreak…Porter…Gibson…Axtell…Vendetta…Garvin…Aimz…SeaBass…Frankie…Krieg…Walczak…Drago…Propaganda…Angeldust…Corg…Rhett…OSW…Xavier Kannon…Paddy O’Shea…Clyde…DarkStar…Akiyama…Mexi…Asa…PCW…and the rest. All those names that made my existence an entertaining one to live. I thanked them all. Another tear. This time he wasn’t awake to say fuck all. Everyone that graced my life, all were a special part of it. Be it a match, a meeting, a simple few words, everyone around me I held dear to my black, cold heart. As much as I could hate anyone of them, without them in my life I wouldn’t have been worth the blood and flesh as I am here today. Goodbye. Goodbye, for this is the end of an era…the end of a subtle time…the end of the Peace. Time for a new Beginning? Time for a new Armageddon? The future holds many possibilities…no matter what happens to me………there will always be an Apocalypse watching, waiting for that moment before it all finally ends. The glass in the mirror suddenly shattered…fell to the ground into a million pieces. Into nothing. But………you see there’s something you forgot in all of this……… Hessian will never Die.
