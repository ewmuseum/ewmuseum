Raiders of the lost jobber

10 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 10th 2002 **The scene opens in an open road, in the middle of the desert. Not the Arizona desert, but rather the Sahara desert. The camera shows the blazing sun, scorching the thin sand, as a small breeze lifts some dust from the floor. The camera pans around, showing a small dot in the blazing horizon. The camera zooms on him, and slowly we can see the image of the dot turn into a small figure, then a full-grown beduine, then, as the camera closes on his face, we can see it's Andrew 'Ace' Hawk. The man who OSW has been talking about, is in the middle of nowhere, wearing heavy clothes and looking extremely, EXTREMELY tired. But one casual fan must be asking himself what the hell is the man of the hour doing in the middle of Africa, thirsty to death. The hardcore OSW fan standing next to him will answer, quickly: He's in the quest of his life: The quest for the lost jobber!!** **And how did this start? It all began after Ace found out about the infamous three lines:** DOUG TROY HAS JOBBED TO HANK HANK!! HANK, I SAY!! **The scene changes to a scene of Andrew Hawk's apartment. There, standing, packing his bag, is Andrew Hawk. Alongside him, just watching, are Dick Strang, with a very worried look on his face, Danny, just sitting in a chair, smiling, and Johnny Atlas, standing behind Ace, being a bodyguard. Dick talks, with a worried tone on his words:** 'Ace, I want you not to go...' 'Why, Dick? I'm not going to my death, you know?', answers Ace, laughing. 'But you don't know where you're going... It could be dangerous. You could get injured.', says Dick, visibly worried about Ace's career. 'Look, Dick, there's no way I'll be able to beat Doug Troy if I don't find him...' 'Who?' 'You know... HIM!' 'The lost jobber?', asks Dick, clueless. 'Yeah! HANK~! That's his name!', answer Ace, as he finishes packing. 'I'll find the damn bastard, or my name ain't Ace Hawk'. 'But where are you gonna start?', asks Dick. 'Simple. I'm going at the OSW offices. There's certainly a guy there that would love my visit. Later, guys. See you in a few.', says Ace, before leaving the house. The scene shows Ace waving to them, and Dick, visibly worried, Dan sitting and hanging around, and Johnny walking away. Dick looks at Danny and is shocked to see Dan simply calm, sitting around. He asks: 'How the hell can you stay calm?' 'Because I know he'll come back. Just sit and wait...', Dan says, as he takes a sip from a nearby Bud. Dick looks at the outside, and the scene fades to black. **The scene reopens inside the OSW corporate building. The camera shows an aisle inside the building, very rich. Blue carpet and walls, several OSW stars and events posters hanging around. The scene shows Andrew Hawk walking down the hall and walking towards a lateral door. He's carrying his backpack and doesn't look happy. He opens the door and one single word is heard from the inside.** 'BOCA~!' **The scene changes to the inside, where we see Ace with a hand on his face, covering his eyes, and Steven Smith, the OSW color analyst, putting his clothes on. Ace says:** 'Please, man, put yourself presentable...' 'I'm sorry, dear... I mean, Ace. What are you doing here? Man, you're cute...', says Steven. 'Dude, please. I'm straight...', says Ace, visibly perturbed with the big man. 'Damn... What a waste...' 'So, I need to find the lost jobber, and I need your help.', says Ace, which doesn't make Steven change his face. 'You know... Hank!'. These words make Steven remember immediately. 'AAAAHHH, now I remember. Hank, the only men I'd never sleep with...' 'HAHA, great. Now we're talking. Now, I know for a fact that this man was capable of defeating the great, all-mighty and modest Doug Troy. Is that correct?' 'That's true, it was a shock back then.', remembers Steven. 'Good. I need to find out how did he do that. Where can I find this brilliant man?', asks Ace. 'You can go check on PCW offices. They're across the street. They know there.' 'Thanks. Who should I look for, there?' 'Ask for my big friend John Stevens... He owns me a favor... If you know what I mean...', says Steven. 'Man, I don't wanna even know about it. Please. You do what you want in your free time... I don't wanna know about it. And thanks anyway.', Ace says, before leaving the room. Steven checks him all the way down, and the scene fades to black. **The scene reopens in a PCW ring training area. We can see a few PWA wrestlers training in it with a few PCW newbies, and some trainers looking around. Ace scoops around and talks to a trainer around. This looks like an old wrestler turned trained. Tall, about 6'6' tall, bald, with the few chunks of hair around his ears being blond, and wearing a PCW T-shirt. He asks:** 'So, I'm looking for the lost jobber. Could you help me???' 'Who???', the trainer asks. 'HANK!!! Damn it, nobody knows him...', says Ace, completely pissed off. 'No, no, I know the guy, he was some low carder who beat Troy...' 'YEAH, YEAH, that's the guy. Do you know where is him?', Ace asks. 'Last I heard, he was killed. But yet, he was killed a couple times, so... He could be alive...', the man answers. 'Why??' 'Nothing. Could you tell me where John Stevens is???', asks Ace, kinda impatient. 'Sure. He's right there.', the man says, pointing with his right thumb over his shoulder to a fat man, looking incredibly like Steven Smith, sitting in a chair, eating a hot dog and checking a PWAer's ass in the back. Ace approaches him and asks: 'Hi...' 'Hellooooooo there.', John says, staring at Ace. 'Damn it, I'm straight. Is every PCW/OSW crew guy gay? Geez...', Ace says. 'I wish. What do you want?', asks John, visibly upset. 'I need to find the lost jobber.', Ace says. 'Who?', John answers, clueless. 'HANK, HANK, DAMN IT!', Ace screams, really pissed off, drawing the attention of the entire crowd of trainees in the room. 'Oh, ok. The last time I heard about him, he had gone with his wife to a small house on Montana. I think I still have the address. Just a minute.', John says, before pulling out a small palmtop from his pocket. 'Here it is.' 'Ok, great. Thanks, dude.' 'Come on, not even a little suck???' 'HELL NO, damn it. I'm straight, STRAIGHT!!!', Ace screams, once again drawing the attention of the PWA workers. Ace simply looks around and screams: 'WHAT?', what immediately makes the guys go back to whatever they were doing. Ace walks out of the room and the scene fades to him, the address on a small piece of paper, smiling. He finally knew the quest for the lost jobber might be in the end. With renewed strenght, he walks out of the PCW corporate offices and calls for a cab. He enters in it and the scene fades to black. Tomorrow: Will Ace find the lost jobber? And will he find the way to defeat Troy and become the #1 contender for the OSW World Heavyweight Title? Tune in to see.
