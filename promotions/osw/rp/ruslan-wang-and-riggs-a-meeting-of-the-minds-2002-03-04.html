Ruslan, Wang, and Riggs: A meeting of the minds

4 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-4-02 It has been a roller coaster month for Ivan Stanislav, since he left the PCW and moved to the OSW under the auspices of Jane Reagan, with the intention of not only molding the fledgling federation into a juggernaut against the PCW but also in order to obtain a stranglehold on the rest of the OSW and ultimately the PTC. But that is just on the military aspect, if you will. The aspect of life from which we are all familiar with when regarding Ivan Stanislav and his constant crusade for complete and absolute dominance. The communists that have joined him in his battle: Ruslan, Wang, Riggs, The Women, and Walczak, have all come together in order to succeed in this quest of theirs. But another aspect of one Ivan Sergeiovich Stanislav has been slowly forming, climbing to the surface of The Russian Bear and becoming more and more apparent: Ivan Stanislav is actually a human being. That is something that few people could imagine being brought to light, as the seemingly mechanical Ivan Stanislav has tromped through the wrestling world with plans and graphs and demographics in order to fulfill whatever he sees fit. Now, seemingly coming in the form of a whirling storm, a Tempest if you will, a significant amount of randomness has entered into the life of Ivan Stanislav. Randomness is normally something that Ivan Stanislav will not tolerate, but even he has given slightly in order to deal with this newfound……situation. What is it about this girl that Stanislav seems to be interested in? Obviously there is some form of interest that drives this man, since something that he does not care about gets no attention whatsoever. Of course, we must understand that the chances of a romantic thought in Stanislav’s mind is probably not the case, for particularly for a man like Stanislav it would take more than a few weeks of banter with a woman to gain the trust required for Ivan Stanislav to show that sort of emotion. But there is much on the horizon for Ivan Stanislav. There is much that he must pay attention to. From the upcoming Saturday Slam in which Doug Troy will reveal a mystery opponent to the Pay Per View scheduled to air the night after, Ivan Stanislav has more immediate matters to handle…. As the scene fades up, Ivan Stanislav stands outside of the Kremlin. Stanislav wears his red military uniform, complete with matching hat and gloves. His hands rest on his hips as he stands in front of the main entrance of the massive building, at the foot of the stairs leading to the doors. The wind blows and whistles in the background, pulling at Stanislav’s beard just slightly, but his face remains as pale as it was before, having been long used to such harsh conditions. A nondescript black car pulls up into a nearby parking lot as the back door of the vehicle opens and Alexei Ruslan gets out, his trench coat whipping in the wind as he does. Ruslan holds onto his brown hat and walks towards Ivan, nodding to him to make sure that Ivan is aware of his presence. Alexei jogs across the street and yells to Ivan, “Praporschik! I am glad that you were able to stay!” He chuckles and walks over to the Russian Bear. Ivan nods to Alexei, “I only have couple of minutes, Alexei Gregorovich. I have important meeting with General Tomifei regarding various matters. I apologize.” Says Stanislav. “That is quite alright,” answers Ruslan, “I have many matter to take care of myself. I never got to talk much to you backstage last night but if you do not mind, Ivan Sergeiovich, I was curious as to how things went between yourself and Tempest.” Ivan nods to himself and grinds his jaw. He stares out ahead at the Moscow skyline of building, “We had rudimentary conversation. Nothing that is of much importance. I told you that I wished to congratulate her on her victory in fWo, and I did just that.” Ivan nods to himself, satisfied with his answer. He does not look at Ruslan. Alexei nods and kicks a pebble out from under his polished shoe, “You were there for quite some time, do you not think, Praporschik. And when you returned, you did seem a little, strained, if you do not mind my saying.” Ivan shakes his head and looks down at his smaller comrade, “I was not strained, I was just contemplating a few things, that is all.” Ivan nods and puts his hands in his pockets. “I will give her this, she is very interesting woman, to say the least.” Alexei chuckles to himself, “But of course, with a history like that how could you not be interesting, no?” Ivan nods slowly, “Yes Alexei, history is a very powerful factor, indeed.” Alexei smiles up at Ivan, “Communist Party Member Wang and Speedy Riggs are going to be showing up any moment, we will be discussing matters regarding Ed and how he thinks he can just change the rules around within OSW. We are going to think of contingencies, various points of order of that such. I will be sure to get you a written copy of what we discuss.” Ivan nods and looks down at Alexei, “I would appreciate that, Comrade Ruslan,” says Ivan, “I must go now Alexei, I will talk to you later.” Ivan salutes Alexei sternly and walks away, disappearing from the camera view as Alexei walks up and into the Kremlin. The scene fades out… ..and fades back in within a large meeting room. In the center of the room is a huge oaken table with a large candle holder in the center of the furniture. A large flag of The Russian Federation hangs on a far wall, as well as pictures of Boris Yeltsin and Vladimir Putin to the left and right of it. Standing in the room is Alexei Ruslan, Loyal Communist Party Member Wang, and Speedy Riggs. A briefcase lays open on the table as Wang and Riggs talk quietly to one another about nothing of terrible importance. Wang carries a drink with some sort of clear liquid in it, Riggs carries a can of Coca Cola. Ruslan stands and listens to them for a moment before clearing his throat, getting the attention of both men. “We have arranged for this meeting for the expressed reason of handling the blatant abuse of power exercised by Ed, the Commissioner of the OSW,” begins Ruslan as he pulls out a packet of papers and hands them to Wang and Riggs, “as you can see by this, it contains all the information on Ed that we know of, which is more than sufficient to get a complete profile on him.” Alexei pauses and allows the two men to digest the information. Wang reads hurriedly through the papers, Riggs stares at a fly on the wall. When enough time has passed, Ruslan continues, “Anyway, big things are coming within OSW and we cannot let some man that could not pull his weight in wrestling world bring us down.” Wang and Riggs nod slowly. Alexei shuts his briefcase, “Therefore, I want zero tolerance when Ed thinks he can make decrees and change bookings! If he does, I want complete and utter resistance, as well as physical intervention if need be!” Riggs looks around nervously and brings a finger up, “But Alexei, what if someone like Doug Troy gets in the way? I mean, he tossed me out of the ring using force on Slam!” Alexei shakes his head, “You fight back Speedy, and the fighters of The Red Army will be there to back you up. You will not have to worry about anything befalling you, do not worry.” Speedy blinks, not very enthusiastic about the idea. Wang puts the paper down on the tabletop and fixes a medal on his uniform. “We of People’s Wepubwic of China will be there to pwotect you fwom any twouble.” Alexei nods, “Zero tolerance, remember that. We are not going to stand for any more rule breaking! None!” Riggs and Wang nod. There is a sizeable pause until finally Wang breaks the silence. “Alexei, what is Ivan up to?” Alexei shrugs, “What is that, Wang?” Wang shrugs and takes a sip of his drink, “Ivan has been quite busy with that wittle girl, Tempest. I was just curious.” Alexei nods. “Praporschik Stanislav is merely interested in the communist possibilities that Tempest may bring to the table. She was born in China, Wang, you know that, so there is a possibility.” Riggs grabs a seat and doodles on a napkin. “I understand, Alexei, but Ivan has been quite interested in her,” continues Wang, “and not that that is bad thing, but I know much about this woman, coming fwom the Wepubwic. She could be great ally.” “She does possess a great amount of fight within her, and she is slightly humorous as well. Ivan is merely testing her out to see what she is made of,” answers Alexei, “surely you can understand.” Riggs chuckles, “Heh, I think he likes her.” Alexei shoots Riggs a look, “Don’t be foolish Speedy! Do you really think that Ivan would be interested in a female after only knowing her for a few weeks?!” Alexei stares at Riggs, who blinks, “I thought not.” Wang smiles, “I kinda hope Ivan does like this Tempest, she is good for him.” Riggs nods in agreement, “I think that would be good too. I mean, Ivan needs to loosen up sometimes…” Alexei shakes his head, “Listen, Comrades, I do not want to speak about Ivan without him being present!” The two men nod, promptly shut up. Alexei’s voice lowers as he gains a more sinister tone, “We have a job to do, men, and we cannot let our objectives to be clogged by stupid thoughts!” Alexei grins and grabs his briefcase, “Men, we will be victorious on Slam and at Pay Per View, I know it. Let us get our jobs done, and rise to victory!” The three men clap and stand and begin to filter out of the room as the camera fades out….
