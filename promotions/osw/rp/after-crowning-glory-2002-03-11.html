After Crowning Glory

11 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-11-02 Late in the night, right after Crowning Glory went off the air. A taped and edited version of videotape is shown. Slowly fading in from the black to light. “God damn, fucking Michel Krieg!” Comes the loud roar of “Bad Attitude” Jesse Ramey. “That fucking bastard! He cost me the God damn fucking match. This is complete and utter bull shit!” Ramey grabs the nearest trashcan and flings it across the room causing it to smash against a wall. He then looks at the door. Breathing heavily in rage. He makes his way over to the door, muscles bulging more than they have before. He opens the door and makes his way down the hallway until he come to Commissioner Ed’s locker room. He burst through the door and finds Ed still shaking his head in disbelief at what occurred during the Toomes match. “Ed!” Jesse shouts. “I’ve never caused any problems here in OSW and I’m happy being here, but I’ll tell you one thing. You are a damn fool if you think I am going to let what happened to me go without justification.” Jesse continues. “Michel Krieg stole my victory away from me AND my chance to get a shot at the International Title! This isn’t the first time a fucking member of The Red Army has caused me to lose a match! First, Walczak and my chance at beating Vampir Nosferatu and now! God damn it, Ed! This is fucking bull shit! What am I supposed to do? I’ve tried my damned hardest to impress someone here in OSW and I’ve done the fucking best that I can do. But how in the hell am I supposed to get anything done when I’ve big fucking dumb ass members of The Red Army sticking their noses in my busy every fucking match I have?” Jesse finishes. Ed is still lost in the monitor, and is still shaking his head in disbelief. He finally realizes that Jesse is standing right beside him and shakes off. “Oh, Jesse. I’m sorry, were you saying something?” Jesse’s eyes light up in rage and disbelief, “Did I say something? Where the hell have you been, Ed? Of course I fucking said something! I’m fucking mad and you know why!” Looks as if he is thinking to him self. “Umm, well… Now that I think about it I can really say that I do know why you are mad. Care to clarify for me?” Ed says with a smile. Jesse’s anger seen obviously growing more and more with Ed’s comments. “Okay, Ed. Listen to me and listen good.” Finally capturing Ed’s attention. “I am sick and fucking tired of these big fucking Red Army guys always sticking their fucking noses in my business. You by any chance catch my match this evening?” “Oh, umm… Yeah, good showing. I was, umm… Fairly impressed with your performance. Umm… Who won the match anyway?” Ed obviously had not watching the match. “Ed, you didn’t even fucking watch the match did you?” Jesse asks in an anger voice. Shaking his head sadly, “As sad as it may sound. No. I was busy with some other important things. I’m sorry, Jess.” Ed sadly lowers his head. Now, understanding what he did wrong, Jesse begins to calm down. “Okay, I understand. It’s Commissioner type stuff. So, don’t worry about it. I would just like something done about all of this. If you don’t mind that is.” “Thanks for understanding, Jess.” Ed politely answers, “There is actually something that I could do. From hearing the comments that you just made. I believe that you would be better off not even attending the show this weekend. Maybe the night off would be okay for you. What do you think?” A smile comes over Jesse’s face, “Well, you know… I really like that…” Jesse gets cut off when the door swings open and slams against the wall. Rhett Locke comes storming into the room and looks at both Jesse and Ed. His eyes come to meet those of Ed. “I don’t care what Ramey is in here for, but I’ll tell you one thing, Ed. I want some revenge on that bastard Corg and I’m getting it now.” Rhett belts. Ed begins to think to him self, “Well, I’ve got one idea here. How about I put you two in a match together this weekend?” “Who would be involved?” Jesse asks. “As long as it involves Corg!” Rhett belts once again. “Well, I’m sorry Locke, but it’s not going have Corg in it. It’s the best that I can do as of now, but how would you two like to participate in a tag team match against Tom Walczak and Michel Krieg?” Ed asks. The two begin to think to themselves. “Well, I wouldn’t mind. It would let me have what I wanted.” Jesse says. “It would let me get a feel for what Walczak could have in the ring.” Locke says. “So, you two are cool with it?” Ed says. “You cool with it man?” Jesse asks Locke. “As long as you are.” Locke expresses. “Well, count me in.” Jesse extends his hand. “Same here.” Locke reaches out and the two men shake hands. “I’ll see you in the ring, Saturday.” Jesse says. “Same here.” Locke also states. The two leave the room as Ed leans back in the chair, “Another great job, by the man who has it~!” Slowly fading out into nothing. ~The End~
