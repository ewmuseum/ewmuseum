Date: August 17th, 2002 ''I don't know who in the name of God that was last night...'' Amy was still startled from her first encounter with OSW's latest lady from the night before and she was expressing that quite well to Reno the morning after while they sat at a little table, eating what looked to be a buffet breakfast at the Inn they'd been staying at for the week. ''Well she sure as hell scared you.'' Davis stated that while stuffing down about as much food as he could possibly fit into his mouth at one time. Aimz looked across at him questioningly for a minute, stirring a finger in the glass of water set in front of her nervously. ''Pfft...what makes you think that?'' ''You were edgier than usual all night, really clingy and watchful.'' He added, ''And look at you now...full plate, half an hour and you still haven't eaten a thing.'' ''I'm just not hungry.'' Reno watched as Campbell's eyes darted to every other occupied table in the room and chuckled. ''Not hungry my ass, Amy.'' ''What?'' She was adamant at first, but after a pleading 'look' from Davis, Amy couldn't help but speak out. ''Okay...so she caught me off guard. She handled me too easily...whoever that chick is, she's HUGE and it's fucking terrifying. Like I said, she could handle ME in a fight like I was some paper doll!'' ''Look...'' Reno reached a hand over the table to squeeze her's reassuringly. ''Is she in the match this week? No...so don't worry about her, that's for another day. Right now, you've gotta concentrate on what you're doing this Saturday. If that chick tries to get at you, I'll treat her just the same as I'm treating Corg.'' ''I can fight my own battles.'' Aimz snapped back at him, pulling her hand out from under his with an almost angry glare. Davis just sighed and planted his feet to stand up. ''I know, I know...let's not get into it again. Anyway, you actually gonna eat or should we leave?'' ''Let's go.'' They'd walked out into the lobby hand-in-hand, but something about Aimz' posture indicated a very detached, melancholy mood. Her head was slumped towards the floor that her feet were scuffing against, leaving a streak of black from the heel of her ankle boots with every step. By the time they got back up to room 215 and Reno called ZaC out, her mood hadn't improved in the least, needless to say. He looked at her for a moment with his head tilted to the side, examining the estranged dim to her eyes. Amy could've sworn he'd said something then, but it wasn't until he shook her shoulder a little that she snapped her skull, which was getting heavier and heavier by the moment, back up to acknowledge him. ''Hmm?'' ''I said I've gotta pick up some Colt...'' He looked unusually concerned, but Amy was paying that fact absolutely no mind. ''...want to come?'' She just stood there for a minute, blankly staring until she could process what had been said. ''Uhh...err...no.'' Reno smiled and kissed her forehead in a slight turn around of gestures before brightly saying, ''I was gonna tell you to rest anyway. No drinking or any of that shit while I'm out, either...I'm starting to worry about you with that.'' ''Mhmm.'' Amy was nodding her head like a drone as Davis strode off, ZaC perched contentedly on his shoulder. This left Aimz alone to do something she hadn't been sober to do in weeks; Think. So what happens when Amy Campbell takes a moment to make time stop for thought? Well, she sombrely, silently slinked into the room and the first thing she went for was...? Music, of course. Not the conventional sort of music for her, though...instead of taking hold of a Disturbed or Nine Inch Nails album, she slid Pink's latest release into the small portable stereo Reno had plugged in on one of the night tables and laid down on the cool, comfortably uncomfortable floor in front of it. Her surroundings, the music, everything just seemed to fit her mood all too well and by the time the hook to 'Lonely Girl' came, she was lost in a sea of thought that ran in identical footsteps to the lyrics... Staring at the cracks in the walls 'Cause I'm waiting for it all to come to an end Still I curl up right under the bed 'Cause its taking over my head all over again. This paranoia frightened her, but in reality it was warranted...hadn't anybody noticed that she'd been victimised so much? It didn't even seem that Reno cared how much these last months had scarred her. Then again, she'd been hiding it...she'd excused it all as upset, temporary insanity even. Anything to hide the real feelings... Do you even know who you are? ''I guess I'm trying to find.'' A borrowed dream or a superstar? ''I wanted to be a star.'' Is life good to you or is it bad? ''I can't tell anymore.'' Do you even know what you have? And that was just fucking it: She wanted to be a star. There was so much getting in the way of it, though...sick bastards like Corg, those executives who were too God damned afraid to give girls like Amy a decent booking because they knew she had the skill to beat one of their precious little stereotypical wrestling males...everything was running against her and she'd been trying SO hard to turn it around. Lying awake watching the sunlight How the birds will sing as I count the rings around my eyes Constantly pushing the world I know aside... She knew the next line well, and couldn't help but whisper it to herself as she felt what seemed almost like tears welling from well beyond tightly clamped eyelids. ''I don't even feel the pain, I don't even want to try.'' I'm looking for a way to become The person that I dreamt of when I was sixteen... Nothing is ever enough It ain't enough for what it may seem. Before another note could emanate from it, the CD lid of the little stereo had Amy's fist inside of it. Saturday night would be a small step, maybe...a chance? The lyrics had applied so well...she wanted to be a star, she HAD to be known, noticed...respected even? Nothing would happen without her forcing it to. 'I'm looking for a way to become the person I dreamt of when I was sixteen...' She still was. ((Note: Hey...uhhh....well yeah, I've got a lot going on in my life right now that's really starting to disorient me. Without getting sappy, let's just say I feel oddly detached from everything...it's the weirdest feeling to try and capture, but I'm hoping this managed it fairly well.))
