The Kiss Of Death

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Billy Idol’s “Dancing With Myself” hits. The camera opens to a cabaret-style stage with velvet red curtains behind the hardwood floor, the stage lit with lights. Sebastian Toomes slides out onto the stage, dressed in a tuxedo and a top hat, complete with a cane. He grins, and begins to sing along with the music. “On the floors of Tokyo… or down in London town to go, go…” Toomes twirls the cane in one hand and begins bopping with the music. “With the record selection and the mirror’s reflection, I'm dancing with myself…” He saunters over to one side of the stage, where a butcher block board stands, and with the cane he flips the first flap of paper over, revealing the hand lettered sign ‘THE KISS OF DEATH’ in carefully lettered magic marker. ”When there's no-one else in sight, in the crowded lonely night… Well I wait so long for my love vibration, and I'm dancing with myself… Oh Oh…” Toomes spins around and tilts his head, letting the top hat roll down his arm. He catches it and spins around again, placing it on his head and fixing the camera with a wry grin. ”Oh dancing with myself… Oh dancing with myself… Oh Oh!” Toomes swaggers over the other side of the stage, swinging his hips. “Well there's nothing to lose, and there's nothing to prove, I'll be dancing with myself…” Toomes gets on his knees and rolls over looking at the camera. “Oh Oh Oh-Oh…” Toomes kips up and starts getting into it, swinging his shoulders and arms, holding the cane in front of him as he saunters over to the butcher board. ”If I looked all over the world… and there's every type of girl… But your empty eyes seem to pass me by… Leave me dancing with myself…” Toomes flips over the page with ‘KISS OF DEATH’ on it to reveal a big glossy picture of Doug Troy. “So let's sink another drink… 'Cause it'll give me time to think…” The camera shifts so the butcher board is on Toomes’ left, and Toomes is shifted to the middle of the shot, and to the right, big green neon lettering appears, declaring “HE WAS FIRED”. “If I had the chance… I'd ask the world to dance…” Toomes shakes his head and flips the next page, revealing a glossy picture of Ace Hawk. Still in the same frame, the lettering changes, stating “I HUMILATED HIM”. “And I'll be dancing with myself…Oh Oh Oh-Oh!” Toomes swaggers away, twirling his cane. “Oh dancing with myself… Oh Oh… Oh dancing with myself…” Toomes stops, and leans on the cane, grinning. “Well there's nothing to lose… And there's nothing to prove…” Toomes whips the cane out and points to the butcher block. The camera shifts over to it, where the next page has revealed Timo Bolamba, giving a thumbs-up. The camera shifts to the side, and the lettering states “HE QUIT AND JOINED ANOTHER PROMOTION”. “I'll be dancing with myself… Oh Oh Oh-Oh!” Toomes comes over to the board again and produces a framed 8” by 10” picture of Drew Hostyle. The caption appears and the lettering states “FEUDED WITH ME, THEN JOINED IN A STABLE WITH ME, AND EVENTUALLY DIED OUT” “Oh Oh Oh OH!” Toomes tosses the picture away. “Oh oh oh OOOOOOOO…” Toomes swaggers and slides across the stage. “Oh Oh Oh OOOOOOO…YEOW!” Toomes pumps his fist, ala Billy Idol in the 80’s, grinning from ear to ear. He grabs his cane and spins it before spinning himself. He dances a bit, as the music goes into a solo. “If I looked all over the world… and there's every type of girl… But your empty eyes seem to pass me by, leave me dancing with myself…” Toomes moves over to the butcher block and flips it, revealing a double shot of Jesse Ramey and former CCW Heavyweight Champion Jason McAllister while they were in CCW, the shot taken at the 2001 Christmas Party. Jesse and McAllister look drunk, and they’re clinking beer mugs together. The green lettering appears underneath the photo. “YEP, THEY BOTH FEUDED WITH ME, AND NOW THEY’RE GONE” ”So let's sink another drink… 'Cause it'll give me time to think…” Toomes quickly flips over the page, where 8” by 10” glossy photos of former CCW Heavyweight Champ Neurosis, CCW Independent Champions Danny Bryson and Chris Shaw, former CCW Tag Team Champion Kyle Velocity Lamen, and a rookie Chris Taylor are pasted onto the white paper. The captions read “RETIRED, LEFT THE FED, DISAPPEARED INTO THE BUSH LEAGUES, CRIPPLED, GOT IN A CAR CRASH”. ”If I had the chance, I'd ask the world to dance, and I'll be dancing with myself… Oh Oh Oh-Oh!” Toomes flips over the next page, where Ivan Stanislav awaits, being rolled up by Toomes at Head On Collision. The caption reads “THE ONLY ONE WHO HASN’T QUIT BECAUSE OF ME” ”Oh dancing with myself… Oh Oh…Oh dancing with myself…” Toomes kicks over the butcher board, and the scene changes as the board falls onto the camera, to a blackened movie theater, where a montage of Armour and Howard Porter are playing. ”If I had the chance, I'd ask the world to dance…” Toomes grins as he looks up at the images displayed on the screen, his black sillouette showing on the screen. ”If I had the chance… I'd ask the world to dance… If I had the chance… I'd ask the world to dance… YOW!” Toomes produces a remote control, and clicks it at the camera, and the lettering appears on the movie screen. “WHO MIGHT GET THE KISS OF DEATH NEXT?” it says, as the background singers in the song sing… “Dancing with myself… Dancing with myself… Dancing with myself…Dancing with myself… Dancing with myself… Dancing with myself…” The song and the shot fade out, and we fade to black.
