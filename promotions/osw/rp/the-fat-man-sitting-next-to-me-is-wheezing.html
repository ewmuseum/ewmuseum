The Fat Man Sitting Next To Me Is Wheezing

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'Huuuuuuuu-MMMRRFFFFF...' Toomes sits on a bench, clad in black jeans and a black wife-beater, his once well-maintained silver and blue hair now showing his black roots, tangled and lusterless. Bags under his eyes, his shoulder still swollen from Head On Collision, he doesn't look good right now. 'Huuuuuuuu-MMMRRFFFFF...' Toomes' bloodshot eyes peer over at the huge bald white guy sitting on a nearby bench, his bulk contained by a huge Warhammer 40,000 shirt large enough to harness the wind power to sail a thousand ships, and a pair of tortured jean shorts that hang to his knees, his giagantic bulk stretching the fabric to the consistancy of spandex. The man wheezes, even though he is sitting down, staring listlessly at the Arrivals / Departures board hanging overhead in the airport terminal. 'Huuuuuuuu-MMMRRFFFFF...' Toomes runs a hand over his scalp, his fingers catching in the tangled mass of dull hair, his exhaustion and the stress weighing down on him. So much, so much has happened. First Jane getting mangled in the car wreck. The Vax, and whatever the fuck he's up to. Ace 'I Jobbed To GhettoDogg' Hawk coming in and trying to say he was Toomes' teacher. Maybe teaching the bad example, maybe. Doug Troy coming out of nowhere and talking shit. Ed. Howard Porter. Ivan still lurking around. The Internet Smarks screaming for his title reign to stop, citing that it was time for him to job it. Fucking Scott Keith talking shit about him. The transmission on his car going out. He was running low on steam. It seemed that the last PPV was all that he had left in him. But there he was, still slogging on. Doing in-cards, run-in's and the like. Still touring the fucking circuit, match with Doug Troy for the belt next Slam!... Everything was falling apart. 'Huuuuuuuu-MMMRRFFFFF...' Toomes shakes with exhaustion and disgust. Everything was falling apart. His title reign was running out of steam. He couldn't afford to get by on his laurels. He was the subject of scrutiny, the first OSW Champion, and he was a wreck. A walking deficiency. And you'd think the fat ass sitting next to him would have the decency to learn some deep breathing relaxation techniques. Or at least go on a fucking diet, try and touch his toes once in a great fucking while. Laying off the fucking double meatball sub every 15 fucking minutes. Why do people, in such a great state of disrepair, even bother to go outside? Well, the same could be said about Toomes right now, but that's besides the point. But seriously, this guy was a fucking fat ass. This was obese beyond what is normally considered even remotely healthy. His fucking disgusting lard bulk drips from under his shirt, hanging over his stretched out fucking jean shorts like some sort of cascading waterfall of lipid doom. 'Huuuuuuuu-MMMRRFFFFF...' Fucking Sweet Zombie Jesus, Toomes thinks, he's actually reaching inside his bag. I fucking knew it. Twinkies. 'Huuuuuuuu-MMMRRFFFFF...' comes the wheeze as dexterous fingers quickly discard the cellophane wrapper, and deftly stuffing one in his cavernous maw in one quick practiced motion. How many Twinkies met thier doom to such a well-drilled techinique? Kung-Fu schools would be envious of the practiced speed and thoughtless execution of body and mind...and Twinkie. Toomes looks out of the cage he's made of his hands, his bloodshot eyes peering through the bars of his fingers as the fat guy in the Warhammer 40,000 sail devours the Twinkie, a bit of golden sponge cage perched precariously at the edge of his mouth, prompting a seeking and deadly tongue to lash out, reigning in the errant morsel. It is a thing of perverse beauty, Toomes thinks to himself, to see such a sight. Toomes looks back down to the blue carpet in the terminal. Didn't they have weight restrictions on the flight? Don't they have a law where a fucking fat fuck like this poor bastard were prohibited from being on a vehicle that is supposed to be able to lift itself from gravity's clutches and FUCKIN FLY? PHYSICS, ANYONE? Fuck. He was obessing over this fat fucking Wonder of the Cellulite World when there were more pressing concerns to think about but GODDAMNED, THAT IS ONE BIG FUCKING FAT MOTHERFUCKER - 'Huuuuuuuu-MMMRRFFFFF...' Toomes snaps. 'FUCK!!! FUCK FUCK FUCK FUCK! GODDAMNIT! DID YOU EVER STOP TO THINK THAT IF YOU LAID OFF THE FUCKING TWINKIES, YOU'D BE ABLE TO BREATH NORMALLY WITHOUT SOUNDING LIKE A FUCKING BEACHED WHALE? FUCK! HOW THE FUCK DID YOU GET SO FUCKING FAT? YOU'RE FUCKING ENORMOUS! FUCK! FUCK! FUCK! WADDLE AWAY! STOP FUCKING BREATHING! ANYTHING TO GET YOU FROM SOUNDING LIKE A FUCKING WALRUS IN FUCKING HEAT!!!' The terminal is dead silent. Even in the relative silence of the airport at 2am, you coudl hear a pin drop. Toomes is on his feet, his eye red and crazed, his hair a tangled mess, a light sheen of sweat soaking through his clothes. The fat man looks at him, a little taken aback, and makes a point of slowly eating the Twinkie in front of Toomes. He smiles, his mouth and teeth a mash of partially chewed sponge cake and rich creamy filling. 'AAAAAH!' Toomes turns and grabs his carry-on bag, running down the silent terminals, not so silent anymore as we hear the sound of shoes on tile as Security looks into the problem. -FIN-
