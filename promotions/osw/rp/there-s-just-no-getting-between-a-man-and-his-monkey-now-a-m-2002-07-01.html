There's just no getting between a man and his monkey. Now, a man and his mattress, on the other hand...

1 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 1st, 2002 ((Note: IT'S CANADA DAY!!!! WAAAHOOOO!!!)) A fun little airy progression of chords, Tremonti kicks the Ibanez Tube Screamer in when the drums hit and BAM! The same progression, only heavier. Amy greeted the day listening to Stapp's voice... When dreaming, I'm guided to another world Time and time again At sunrise, I fight to stay asleep 'Cause I don't want to leave the comfort of this place... Little twangy notes played, layered over chords behind Scott's vocals... 'Cause there's a hunger, a longing to escape From the life I live when I'm awake... The song began to pick up as Amy looked into the mirror, fingering through her hair, attempting to clear an opening in it's mass for those dark brown eyes as a chunky palm-muted power chord was easing it into the chorus... So let's go there Let's make our escape Come on, let's go there Let's ask, 'Can we stay?' Can you take me hiiiiii----- Behind her back, the music quickly cut out by a hand slammed on the ''Power'' button. With a frustrated sigh, Amy remembered who she'd shared the room with... ''Hey!!'' She shouted, finding Reno standing over by the bed, stereo beside him. ''What the hell?!'' 'London's Burning' by The Clash started up and Reno struck a dramatic pose as if he was ready to rush into a riot with a baton...he screamed along with the music: ''LONDON'S BURNING!!'' ''Well good morning to you, too!'' Amy strode over, pouting and speaking about as loud as she possibly could to be heard over Davis' music without 'Disturbing the peace'. ''I'll have you know I liked that song!!'' ''Yeah, I like the Clash a lot too.'' Reno said, walking forward and wrapping his arms around Amy as he slid behind her and kissed her neck. ''I was talking about Creed. I HATE British punk...but will you stay this friendly if I pretend I don't?'' She giggled a bit, easing back a little into Davis' arms. Reno unwrapped his arms and pushed Aimz forward onto the bed with his foot, accompanied by a chuckle just before he sung along again: ''London's burnin' with boredom now...'' ''Oh bite me, your taste in mu----'' But Reno immediately sung louder: ''Now I'm in the subway and I'm looking for the flat This one leads to this block, this one leads to that The wind howls through the empty blocks looking for a home I run through the empty stone because I'm all alone.'' ''Okay...now you're just being cruel!'' She covered her awkwardly pierced ears, shielding her poor brain from having to process the noise. Reno walked over to the CD player and switched the song to ''Complete Control'', immediately beating his foot to the floor in tune with the bass drum in the song while Amy walked over, brushing past him as she pulled the plug out of the wall in an effort to save her already battered hearing. ''If I ever hear Brit punk that loud again, I swear I'll blow my head off...'' ''Turn that back on!'' Reno began, and as she turned the corner, he mumbled out the rest, ''...woman...'' Too early, Amy heard... ''Umm...'' She paused in the doorway, turning back to face Davis. ''...excuse me? What was that?'' Reno coughed, startled by her amazing hearing, ''Uh, I said I was a lucky man...'' He smiled. ''Good save, smartass.'' Amy laughed and walked back over to pat Davis on the head, reminiscent of how she frequently handled Reno's considerably furrier companion. ''You're learning already. Not as fast as ZaC, mind you...but there's progress.'' Before either of them could lean in for a kiss, ZaC awoke from his bed across the room at the mention of his name and wandered forward towards Reno. Davis picked up the still sleepy monkey put him in a head lock, scratching the monkey behind the ears. The two play fought and Aimz just stood there, knowing that she'd never replace ZaC...Reno and the monkey just had a special sort of bond that could never be duplicated. She smiled and pondered something to break the silence a bit, but that was done for her when a knock came at the main door... Amy glanced at Reno, who was still rough-housing with ZaC, and jogged over to the hall, opening the door to the warm face of the ever-nosey Breanne LeLievre. ''Bre! Uhh...c'mon in...'' She guided her friend inside the stuffy little Best Western suite. ''Hey Amy...just came to s-----'' When Breanne stepped into the bedroom with Aimz, she was 'greeted' to the sight of Reno Davis standing there with only boxers and a monkey on him. ''Hi.'' He obviously didn't find it nearly as weird as Breanne and Amy did.. ''Well hello there,'' She smiled at Reno, then shot Aimz one of those classic 'And you didn't tell me this because....?' looks. ''...why're you in here, is it laundry day?'' PAUSE! Dictionary moment... Breanne LeLievre- Goddess of dry sarcasm and awkward moments. [ / Dictionary Moment ] ''Actually, I was having sex with Amy over here. Thanks for asking though.'' Reno doesn't lose to awkward moments or sarcasm that easily. As expected, Breanne's jaw dropped, less because of being told what they'd spent the previous night doing and more so just because Reno was that up-front about it. Amy just stood there, biting her lip and cringing before inching over to Davis and whispering in his ear, ''See, there's this little thing we call 'tact'.......'' Reno looked up at Aimz and shrugged, holding his arm out horizontally as ZaC hung off, ''She asked...I answered...'' Campbell managed a weak smile. Reno's ability to not have to think twice before he spoke got him into some sticky situations, unfortunately it was something Amy couldn't help but love. ''Just don't be quite as blunt next time...people don't totally accept that sometimes. You're definitely learning that BEFORE you meet my parents.'' ''Well what'd you want me to say? We were fulfilling our duty to Big Brother?'' Reno turned his attention to Bre while Aimz noted never to read 1984 with Davis, ''Nice to meet you.'' ''Yeah...uhh......you too.'' Breanne nodded to Reno then turned, with wide eyes, to Amy, running a hand through naturally dirty blonde, highlighted purple-ish hair. ''Ummm...can I have a word with you....maybe in the hall?'' ''Sure.'' Amy cast a pleading look to Reno, begging him just to put up with Bre for now. Davis just smirked and went back to horsing around with ZaC while the two girls left the room. When they were in the hall, the door safely shut behind them, Bre spoke in low, excited tones. ''You DIDN'T!'' ''Oh, we definitely did.'' Aimz rolled her eyes especially hard when LeLievre went into what could be likened to a teenaged boy in an 'I got laid' locker-room jig. ''Oh...my...GAWD!'' ''I swear to God...'' The redhead kicked her old friend in the left shin, chuckling a little. ''That was WAY to preppy to be you. Try again.'' ''Alright...'' She sucked in a breath. ''...holy SHIT!'' ''Better...and yeah, that's basically what I said...heh.'' ''So was he any go---'' Breanne began, but got cut off by a deathly stare from Aimz. ''Eep! Oookay then...backing off of the sex questions...'' She paused a moment, then something struck her and with a gasp, she stated her thoughts aloud. ''Jesus...he was THAT BAD!?'' ''Breanne!'' Aimz swatted at her now giggling best friend. ''OF COURSE it was g---'' At that point, she interrupted herself. ''Look, I just like to keep my privacy...okay?'' ''You never liked to before.'' ''Yeah, well...just....UGH!'' Bre then realized, ''You just don't want you parents to find out!'' ''They're the LAST people I want to deal with right now. 'Daddy's Little Whore' is at it again, you know?'' She sighed, ''That's all they'll think.'' ''Heh...think of some of the freaks you've brought home already, now they're getting PHYSICALLY deformed...forget piercings, a guy with big blue eyes and no pupils is REALLY pushing the envelope with your dad.'' ''Turquoise.'' Campbell snapped. ''Huh?'' ''His eyes, they've got more green in them...like one of those Caribbean post cards. The color, it's turquoise.'' ''Whatever...'' She continued, shrugging. ''...you're parents will definitely NOT be impressed.'' ''Which is why you're going to wait and let ME tell them...'' Amy's 'question' was more of an order, ''...right?'' ''Meh. I guess so.'' LeLievre shrugged again, then swiftly changed the subject. ''Did you see the last of Slam yet, or were you a little too...uhh...'occupied'?'' ''Fuck off.'' She shoved Bre, fairly playful at least. ''What happened?'' ''I dunno...but it looks pretty geeky.'' Campbell was, for the first time all morning, concentrated on something other than Reno Davis. ''Whatcha' mean?'' ''All I saw were lab coats, suits, ties and thick glasses, man...they looked like nerds to me! Your team buddies there didn't look too happy about it, either.'' ''Shit! Forgot about them...'' Aimz cringed, rubbing her temples. ''I've gotta call Sebastian...'' ''Won't that be a little...umm...difficult? He and Reno sure don't seem to be the best of friends.'' ''That's why I'm CALLING.'' ''Ahhh...gotcha'.'' Bre cracked her knuckles, continuing... ''Anyway...I'm headed downstairs, this place has a KILLER breakfast menu...wanna come?'' ''Nah, might get lunch with Reno later or something...'' ''Yep...he seems like a pretty hungry guy,'' Breanne laughed aloud, winking at Amy as she walked off down the hall, making her final smartass remark of the morning, ''...I'm sure he'll be eating well today!'' ''Blow me!'' Amy shouted after Miss LeLievre, flicking her off before breaking a smile and walking back into the room, where Reno and ZaC were now half-dozed off on the floor in front of the little TV. The only task ahead of her right now was to figure out what the hell happened in Slam's main event. Corg was HOPEFULLY scared off just enough to keep away for the week, but that certainly couldn't be counted on, considering his unusually congenial (For Corg, at least.) comments on television that morning. Things were definitely getting more interesting...she only hoped that she was a little safer.
