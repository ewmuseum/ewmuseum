Fraud.

Date: Pay-Per-View Location: The Bowels Of Joe Louis Arena Date: April 28, 2002 Time: Just After Angeldust v. Epitome 'Fraud.' The word sounded through Nick's mind a thousand times after the match, blasting away at his brain like a full batallion with cannons. The feud had finally ended...it was never really that heated, but Nick hated the fact that there were still people who thought that they were better than him. Epitome of Evil now layed in the hospital bed, while Nick layed strewn across the bench in the visitors' locker room at Joe Louis Arena. So many greats had passed through this locker room, getting ready to play the famed Detroit Red Wings. Lemuix, Gretzky, Howe, Borque, Gartner, and so many more. And there sat Nick Casey; somewhat famous now-a-days, however there was still something missing. He was still wrestling crappy undercard matches with chumps like Epitome of Evil, however, that seemed to have been put to a halt by Jack Miller. On this path, could he really ever be as well known as those Hall Of Famers? No fucking way. Not the way he was going. As of right now, he was the bottom of the barrell. The scum of the OSW. He was a nobody. And Angeldust HATED it. He hated even saying the word 'nobody.' Nick Casey was a mess of blood and cuts, laying on the bench. He knew he was no longer the big shot, where all of the cuts and scrapes and blood would be healed by the band-aid of glory, however he didn't even have the glory he had back in MWW. 'Nick, you won the match. Get up, get cleaned up, we're going to Canadia.' Nick didn't even smile. Canadia? Maybe Christy Zeller wasn't as smart as she had once advertised. Usually, Angeldust would thrive on this chance to put Christy down. However, he didn't want to even move on this night. 'I didn't win. Jack Miller won.' 'I am so tired of listening to this. You've been sitting here for an hour, just moping.' Actually, two. 'I'm a fraud. I feel like I've been working for nothing.' 'How can you say something like that?' Christy snapped. 'You've worked too hard, too long to even think about that shit! Now get out of your tights, get up off that bench, and GET YOUR ASS IN THE CAR!' Christy's Chicago background started to kick in whenever a male started to get defiant, as Nick continued to mope. 'Fuck off.' Nick replied. 'Not in the mood.' Christy's face turned beet red, 'OH, WELL IF THE GREAT ANGELDUST ISN'T IN THE MO---' she stopped. Tension, anyone? 'You know what, just sit there.' she said. 'Keep fucking up like this, and soon you'll be left with even fewer people who care about you. And it can't get much lower than one.' She stormed out the door, but Nick was even less happy now. 'Jack Miller...will die.' Rationalism, anyone? Not for Angeldust.
