Carrot Top: Menace to Society

27 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-27-02 Our scene opens shortly after the first OSW edition of Seth’s World has gone off the air. We are currently backstage...which means we’ve moved from the living room, through the door on the right into the television room. Trevor is sitting slumped up against the wall, still looking sullen, probably from the combination of getting punched in the gut by Seth and the knowledge that Carrot Top is under the same roof as he is. I can’t really say that I blame him on either point, as one can only watch so many 1-800 Collect commercials before wanting to pull those poofed out orange locks out of the man’s skull. Ooo, just the thought of those damn commercials is driving me into a blood frenzy. Grrr! Just let me get my hands on him! I’ll kill that damn red-headed freak! I’ll...well, you get the point. It’s easy to see why Trevor hates him so much. “Damn I hate Carrot Top!” he shouts, only further illustrating my point. “Obviously,” states Seth, pacing across the room. “If I would have known ahead of time I wouldn’t have even accepted his booking.” “The fact that you accepted his booking is disturbing enough,” Trevor says, looking perturbed. “The fact that he’s actually in the same house I sleep in...that’s going too far.” “Well I don’t know what to tell you, man. What do you want me to do?” Seth asks, crossing his arms. “Kick his fucking ass to the curb!” Trevor yells, wrapping his arms around his legs and burying his head into his knees. “I feel so dirty knowing he’s only yards away from me.” “Argh, I don’t want to have to convince that idiot to leave,” moans Seth, massaging his forehead in dread of what’s to come. “Knowing his stubborn ass, if I start arguing with him, it’ll take hours. Look, maybe I won’t have to kick him out. Maybe he’ll just wander out on his own if we leave him alone.” “I want him out now!” Trevor says through gritted teeth. “Get him out of here before his stench seeps into the walls, thus defiling them forever.” “Do you know the kind of shit that has already defiled these walls?” Seth asks, raising an eyebrow as he slaps his hand on the wall next to Trevor. “Nothing is worse than Carrot Top,” Trevor says, without a trace of sarcasm in his voice. “Get rid of him!” “Fine,” Seth mutters, sighing deeply. “I’ll break the news to him.” “Where is he? He’s not near my bed, is he? Is he?” Trevor questions, a bit worried. “You mean the couch?” “Yes,” Trevor says sadly. “No, the last time I checked, he was in the kitchen,” Seth says, looking off to the left apprehensively. “We have a kitchen?” a confused Trevor questions. “Well, the room with the microwave.” Seth clarifies, pointing in the general direction with his thumb. As he does so, that annoying voice that anyone who has ever listened to Carrot Top wishes they have never heard emanates from the other room. “Hey, this is a nice microwave you got here!” the red-headed nincompoop crones. “I bet you could cook like three bags of popcorn at the same time! I don’t think you could eat that much popcorn, though. You’d probably get sick of it after about a bag and a half.” “Do you hear that!” Trevor whispers to Seth with a shiver. “The man’s an idiot! He makes you look like a Nobel Laureate!” “Oh come on,” Seth argues. “I’m a fucking moron. He can’t be that stupid.” “Man, guys...it smells like updog in here,” yells Carrot Top. “What the fuck is updog?” Seth asks, his face scrunching up in a disturbed sort of confusion. “Nothing much, my homie. I’m just scoping out your kitchen, still.” The sound of Carrot Top chuckling can be heard through the walls. An awkward period of silence comes over the room, as Seth and Trevor exchange looks. “I stand corrected,” Seth says in monotone, all expression drained from his face. It is as if the absolute idiocy of the wanna-be comedian’s joke has stunned him. “Are you okay,” Trevor asks, a bit concerned. “I’m...not sure,” drones Seth emotionlessly. “Well, if you think that was bad, just count yourself lucky you’ve never seen him in Member of the Board,” Trevor shakes his head, a look of nausea coming over his face at just the thought of the terrible movie. “My God, whatever studio executive allowed that to reach theaters needs to be shot.” “No, no...I don’t think anything can be worse than that joke,” Seth says, staring ahead blankly. “It was single most futile attempt at humor I’ve ever heard. I am completely paralyzed by the sheer patheticness exuded from it.” “But no, check it out. In Member of the Board he...” “Please don’t tell me, Trev. I don’t think I can handle any more unfunny jokes.” Seth states. He blinks a few times, but other than that makes no facial expression. “Then you had better get his ass out of here quick. What are you waiting for? Get in there and do your job, damn it!” Trevor says, pressing Seth in the direction of the other room with an outstretched foot. Seth is moved as far as Trevor’s foot can reach, but after that he just stands staring at the kitchen, unmoving. “No...if I go in there, he’ll make a stupid joke,” Seth says, still without emotion. “It may drive me mad.” “Well if you think I’m talking to him, you’re a damned fool,” complains Trevor. “I’m not going into that room for a million dollars. You can act mentally stunned all you like, but I’m not buying it. You’re just trying to get out of kicking him out.” “No,” Seth says impassively, shaking his head. “I’m serious. The stupid fuck is going to kill me with his retarded jokes.” “Well, I don’t care!” Trevor shouts. “I hate Carrot Top! I’ve hated him for years, and I’ll be damned if I have to engage him in conversation!” “But my life is more important than your pride,” Seth argues. “Carrot Top is the biggest dicksmack in the world, and I’ll be damned if I lose my sanity because of his warped sense of humor.” “I’m not talking to the fucking faggot!” yells Trevor. “I’ll be damned if I talk to the retard,” Seth remarks back. The two members of Civil Disobedience go back and forth, back and forth, back and forth, trying to shift the responsibility of getting rid of Carrot Top onto each other. It is right smack-dab in the middle of this exchange that the door to the kitchen swings open and out walks none other than the subject of all the bickering, Carrot Top himself. “Hey guys, guess what...” he says, as Trevor is in the middle of another one of his tirades. “...I can not reiterate enough that Carrot Top is the stupidest motherfucker in the world!!!” shouts Trevor, clenching his fists in anger. As he finishes, both he and Seth turn to see Carrot Top standing in the doorway. A single tear drops from his eye. “I...I think I should be going now.” he says sadly, as he hangs his head. He trods slowly to the front door, as Seth and Trevor exchange glances. Usually this is the part where they run after him apologizing, but, as expected from these two, there is none of that going on. They merely shrug at about the same time and smile. “Alright, see ya later, dude,” Trevor calls after him. “Or even better...not.” Carrot Top is sobbing softly as he gets to the door. As he opens it to exit, he turns back to look sadly at Seth and Trevor, his lower jaw quivering. “All I wanted to do was tell you guys a joke. What type of bee can you milk?” He is answered only by silence. Seth winces, bracing himself for the oncoming punchline. With another sob, Carrot Top delivers it. “A boobie.” The terrible joke sends a shockwave across the room, colliding with Seth and sending him flying across the room. He crashes through a window and lands hard on the ground outside. Carrot Top starts bawling his eyes out and exits the house, while Trevor stares in shock at the shattered window. “Holy shit!” he screams, walking over to the wreckage site. “Seth, I’m sorry I didn’t believe you. I thought you were just BSing me. Who would have thought Carrot Top’s idiocy was so powerful? Are you ok?” We can see a hand reach up to the window sill. To the relief of Seth Masters fans everywhere, it has a thumb pointing up. Trevor is still shaking his head in disbelief as the scene fades out.
