A Quiet Evening

9 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/9/02 Ivan Stanislav and Tempest against Alan Kriegman and Trashcan Man. The Think Tank had plagued the OSW long enough and finally an unlikely group of allies had seemingly rallied together to wipe out the terrorizing group including Ivan Stanislav, Tempest, and The White Mexican. For Ivan Stanislav, this was the most unorthodox and confusing situation he could be in. Even The Russian Bear knew that he had to do something, and that's what brought him to the outside of Tempest's hotel room door. Ivan Stanislav stood outside hotel room 123, which just so happened to be Tempest's door. He looked almost dashing with his hair cut high and tight, his beard neatly trimmed, and every gray hair seemingly plucked from existence. He swallowed and adjusted his black pants and a twisted red suspender strap before checking his hair for a fleeting moment. He ground his jaw for a moment, his face slightly flushed and swallowed. 'Just do it… Ivan….' He whispered and brought his large fist to the door and knocked loudly, three times. There was a shuffling behind the door and a little change of light in the peephole before the door opened to reveal Tempest in all her disheveled radiance. She wore a light blue pair of workout pants and a matching hoodie, from which her spiky black hair poked around the edges. Today it was still dyed partly purple. She smiled, blinking rapidly with her arms folded across her chest and said, 'Hi Ivan. It's good to see you. Um, to what do I owe the pleasure?' Ivan worked up a somewhat twisted grin, but also maintained a somewhat military posture. He nodded to Tempest and quickly made the smile disappear, 'Hello Tempest… I uh…' He paused and swallowed as his stern demeanor almost melted away, his voice shook just slightly, 'Ehem, I uh… knew that you were in the area and…uh… if you were not busy that is, I thought that maybe uh… you'd like to go get something to eat.' He paused and then quickly continued, almost catching himself, 'Because! Because you have been so busy that perhaps you… ehem, haven't had good diet… lately.' His face flushed over as his eyes darted around, somewhat unsure what he was saying. Tempest smiled with a genuine happiness and what almost looked like relief. She took a step toward him and her arms unfolded, almost reaching for him. Then she seemed to think better of it and took a deep breath before saying, 'Well um... I guess I've been a little distracted in the twenty four hours, certainly. Yeah! I'd love to though.' She rocked on her heels slightly looking up at him. Ivan blinked rapidly, seemingly surprised and smiled wide before catching himself and toning down his expression, 'Excellent! Uh… there is little diner down the street if you would like to accompany me. I noticed it on my way here.' Tempest grinned and ducked behind the door momentarily then popped back out, stuffing a key into the front pocket of her hoodie as she said, 'Sounds great!' She let the door close behind her and reached up gingerly, holding her hand around Ivan's massive arm with a hopeful smile. 'Shall we?' As one, three men sitting at the far end of the diner, the waitress, and the cook behind her all turned and stared at The Russian Bear as he followed the diminutive Tempest. Ivan dodged a lighting fixture and looked at the bar, then at the booths, and then at Tempest and nodded, 'It is customary for the lady to choose.' Tempest snickered and nodded toward one of the booths as she said, 'We can talk over there better. You did want to talk, I'm guessing?' Ivan nodded, 'Da Tempest.' He looked at the three men and scowled as they stared until they quickly turned and kept to themselves once again. He turned to the waitress, who yelped and turned, as well as the cook and then grinned at Tempest and let her lead the way. Ivan's heavy footsteps contrasted with the quick padding of Tempest's sandaled feet. He stood as she scooched into the booth and then rubbed his chin, staring at the impossibly small table. He grinned and tried to squeeze into his side of the booth, but his legs were too big. He pushed harder as the table groaned in it's attempt to pull from the floor. 'Uh…heh….' grumbled Ivan as he stood again and stared at the booth. Tempest stifled a chuckle and looked up at him with big blue eyes as he looked around and shrugged. Finally, Stanislav grabbed his side of the booth and pulled it from the floor and placed it farther back from the tiny table. The waitress gasped in the background as Ivan sat down in his revised booth with more leg room and grinned, proud of himself. Tempest chuckled and said, 'Way to go Ivan. Never let it be said that you don't fit in wherever you go.' She handed him a menu and said, 'So what did you have on your mind?' Ivan took the menu and looked at it absently while rubbing the back of his neck, he stared at the menu instead of looking at Tempest as he spoke softly, 'I uh… I know that you are so busy and thought that maybe this would be… nice.' He paused and looked on the flip side of the menu, somewhat perturbed and looked at her suddenly, 'I also know that… you have had a trying two weeks Tempest and… I was concerned.' He quickly looked back down at the menu. Tempest smiled wanly and said, 'Is that an apology for making them harder in some ways or does it just sound like one?' 'Tempest I…' Ivan began, looking at the menu and setting it down, 'You can accept it for whatever you want, perhaps it is apology. Do not think I try to complicate your life.' 'No... I wouldn't,' she said looking up at him. 'Especially when you helped me out so much recently, at the Pay per view and before, and then again last night. I appreciate that. I want you to know I'm not just forgetting about those things.' Ivan shook his head absently, 'Nyet, you need not remember them, I do not expect anything from it, anybody else would do same thing, I am sure.' Tempest reached out and touched his hand as she said, 'No... I don't think just anyone would.' She was interrupted suddenly as the waitress walked up and said, 'Hiya folks, can I take yer orders?' Tempest jumped slightly as the woman approached from behind and then nodded. 'Um yeah I'll uh... I'll have a milkshake, a strawberry milkshake.' She nodded toward Ivan and laid down the menu that she had obviously never read. Ivan jerked and looked at the waitress suddenly, 'Get me… bottle of Vodka will be fine.' 'Scuse me honey?' the waitress replied. 'What??' Ivan answered and looked at Tempest. Tempest snickered and said, 'Ivan, this is a diner. They serve meals, not liquor.' Oh.' He merely nodded and swallowed, 'Get me uh… get me milkshake too then….' 'What flavor hon?' The waitress licked her lips knowing she shouldn't have asked. 'Flavor?' 'Yeah, you hafta get ice cream in it.' 'ANYTHING! SURPRISE ME!' Stanislav barked and slammed his fist on the table, shaking it. She turned and left suddenly as Ivan tried to calm himself. Tempest bit her lower lip trying to restrain her laughter and finally said, 'It's ok Ivan, they're good. Trust me on this one.' She took a deep breath and said, 'I'm trying to relax still from last night. I could just strangle Alan Kriegman and company.' She scowled at their imagined presence and shook her head. 'It is all right Tempest,' Ivan answered as he leaned forward on his elbows which caused the table to creak, 'if you have not heard yet, we have match with Kriegman and his lapdog on next Saturday Slam, you can get your revenge then, and I can get mine…' 'Really?' Tempest said with a delighted squeal. 'Oh boy, we get to team! I can't wait. And against those two... they don't stand a chance. And... your revenge? Who do you feel you have to avenge yourself against?' Now she was curious and tiled her head inquisitively. Ivan winced and rubbed his eyes quickly as she looked at him and shrugged, 'Well, I mean… what they did to you was uncalled for Tempest! I…' He bit his lip and folded his hands in front of him, 'He… he hurt you Tempest in way that goes beyond wrestling ring. I remember I once… told someone I wouldn't let them be hurt…' Ivan shrugged awkwardly and picked up the menu that had been left behind and pretended to read it suddenly. 'I... think I understand,' said Tempest, letting it drop with s soft smile. 'Thank you. For feeling that way I mean. I really was terrified. The damn... I think they had some storm sounds on a CD or something and were playing it. It was so dark I couldn't tell and then the flashes of lightning might have been a strobe or a camera flash...' She trailed off shaking her head. 'It's really embarrassing though. God, I wish I wasn't like that. Or at least people didn't know.' Ivan leaned forward and spoke softer, 'Do not think about that Tempest, it is over and it will never happen again.' He looked around trying to think of a topic, 'How else have you been? I almost expected you to be out dancing or something tonight.' She smiled and opened her mouth to speak just as their milkshakes arrived. 'Her ya go, hon. And you too. I figured vanilla would be safe in your case,' said the waitress as she set them down in front of Tempest and Ivan respectively. Tempest popped a straw into hers and took a sip and nodded approvingly. Ivan looked at the glass and blinked and bent down far to the seemingly minuscule straw. He shrugged and pulled the straw out of the milkshake and brought it to his huge maw, letting half the shake slide down his throat and placed the glass back on the table. He blinked for a minute and winced suddenly, grabbing the side of his head. 'Dyuuuhhhh….' He growled and squeezed his temples harder. Tempest blinked at him, perplexed and said, 'What's the matter Ivan? Ivan growled angrily and kept his eyes closed, 'Head…ache….' He opened his eyes wide and flared his nostrils, trying to stem the pain in his skull. Tempest sighed and said, 'You're not supposed to drink half a milkshake in one gulp. You've got brain freeze.' Ivan breathed loudly through clenched teeth and blinked before his pale face flushed a deep red and the pain subsided, 'Of course! Ivan knew that!!' Tempest opened her mouth with a little smile and then shut it. With a suppressed smirk she said, 'Of course you did. But anyway, I'm a little... I dunno, jumpy. I don't think I could really get into dancing tonight, maybe tomorrow, but tonight's a little too soon. At least I'm not really hurt like last week.' Her eyes flickered to the gash in Ivan's cheek that still looked frightening. 'How are you doing?' Ivan blinked, somewhat surprised, 'How am I doing?' It was a question he was rarely asked. 'Yes, how are you doing?' Tempest said again, her eyes alight with concern. 'I know you haven't had the easiest time of it either. I guess you decided not to hate me over the thing with Mexi, but... how are you? How do you feel about it?' Ivan shrugged, 'I never hated you Tempest but… it is not easy to take… any of it. I think I understand the reasoning but…' Just as he appeared to open up he shrugged, 'Bah, I am nothing more than angry Russian Tempest, it matters not what I really think.' He crossed his arms over his chest and stared at the table. Tempest looked crestfallen a moment and looked at Ivan with her crystal blue eyes and said, 'It matters to me.' Ivan rubbed the back of his neck, 'I know you do Tempest but I really do not know what to think anymore… about anything… about everything.' He blinked, 'That worries me.' Tempest nodded slowly. 'I know. I guess my timing sucks, what with all this trouble with Russia. And I can't help but feel like it's may fault in a way. That and I didn't realize til this morning what all happened last night.' She slumped in her seat, recalling the unhappy realization that she'd cost Ivan the OSW world title once again. 'You mean with Main Event Tempest?' Ivan asked. She nodded sulkily and said, 'not that anyone could keep track of what was going on, but this morning I realized it. If it hadn't been for me, you might very well be the OSW World champion right now. Ivan shook his head and leaned across the table, actually patting Tempests hand absently, 'Nyet Tempest, do not blame yourself. I did not need to help you… I wanted to. And Howard Porter will get his, it only delays inevitable. Do not put blame on yourself, please?' Tempest nodded and smiled as she said, 'I just wanted you to know I was sorry, that I cared. I know in your most fearful moments some part of you must think I'm against you or that I'm nothing but trouble. I'm trying not to be. I'm trying to show you that I'm gonna be around this time.' 'I… I hope so… I hope that Ivan is around too, with the changes in Russia, I do not know where I may be.' Ivan grabbed his milkshake and gulped down the rest, quick to get it out of his mouth and down his huge neck quickly before slamming the glass back down on the table, 'It is getting late Tempest, it is beyond Ivan's bed time…I must get up at five o'clock.' 'Still?' Tempest said quizzically. Ivan grinned, 'I have gotten up at five o'clock for over twenty five years Tempest… though I have occasionally slept until half past five.' Tempest snickered. 'Must be nice. I haven't slept since last night. Maybe I will now though.' She stood up with him and smiled as she stepped closer. Ivan smiled and whispered, 'I hope so Tempest, you should have nothing to worry about.' He reached behind his back to his pocket and pulled out a wad of rubles and threw it on the table and whispered, 'Lets get out of here before she realizes I have no dollars.' He winked quickly. Tempest snickered and huddled close as they snuck out quietly. Ivan stood outside of Hotel room 123 again with Tempest and smiled down at her as he held her hands in his own, 'Tempest… I really enjoyed myself tonight and I just wanted to say…' He hesitated and shook his head, 'N..nevermind.' He tried to let go of her hands, but she held tight and said, 'What?' She looked up at him with those deep, warm eyes and that soft smile that seemed reserved for him. Ivan gulped loudly and tilted his head to the side just slightly, 'Tempest, I know that I have done things that were wrong and I did not mean that to happen and… and I know that you have… reasons… for returning.' He breathed in deep, 'I want you to know that in the wrestling ring I will be there for you… and… and outside of ring I can try but…' Ivan squirmed in her tiny hands, 'What I'm trying to say Tempest is that at this moment I do not know what to do or what to think or… what is going to happen and because of that… I must be cautious for fear that if I am reckless again… I may hurt… you.' 'Or yourself,' said Tempest softly. 'It's ok. I'm not afraid of you anymore. And when you can believe that... then things will be good again. And... I'm gonna stay til they are.' She stepped forward slowly and hugged him around the waist. Ivan clumsily patted her back with his massive paw and whispered, 'We can only try.' He released her and held her hand in on of his own, 'I hope you sleep well tonight, Tempest… and thank you. Goodnight.' Tempest smiled and made a little wave after him. I think for a change I will sleep well tonight. Maybe things aren't as dire as they seemed. I'm not afraid of anything when I'm with you. I just wish the same were true for you. But you're still afraid of what you see n the mirror. Maybe I'm wrong because you do know better than me what sort of monster you're capable of being. But I know better than you that you can be more. I've seen it. And I hope to see it again, like tonight, like a little hint of it. When you're not afraid to look anymore, I'll still be here to show you.
