So Many Things...

31 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-31-02 Immortality is a thing of the greats. The people that have come before in our times of youth. They set the pace for us and they made it possible for us to do things now that we weren’t allowed to do before. They took that road that wasn’t traveled by the others and they beat it down for us. They explored the new horizons and didn’t let a little bit of hard work and determination get to them. Sometimes that path can be trampled, as it was this past week on Slam. Four men, who have decided to remain nameless, took it upon themselves to try and end the career of one, “Bad Attitude” Jesse Ramey. Such dreadful things have happened numerous times in OSW over its short time of being opened. Scott Naket taken out by these men. A curse that has plagued OSW thus far, the ominous injury. So many people taken out by it and so many lives ended. Dreams shattered into nothingness. Taken from what they were to begin with, so vivid and lively. No good could ever come from something this dramatic. Yet, they continue to spread throughout the OSW like a wildfire and no one will stand to challenge the things that cause it, until this past Slam. When Jesse Ramey stood after a severe beating and told the men that had taken him out that he would not stand for it anymore and wasn’t going to let what happened to him happen to anyone else. Ah yes, a little reminder to us all of what happened on Slam. The promo that was cut by Jesse Ramey. Here we have it, in tact and all. The camera follows Ramey, as he wanders around in somewhat of a dazed state, trying to find his way back to his dressing room. After a few moments, he stumbles across a few of OSW’s “crack” security guards. “Hey, you assholes! You remember what I did to those one guys on the last show who weren’t doing their job? Well you’re about to suffer the same fate! You dumb asses were supposed to be watching for those jerk-offs!” Jesse shouts in anger. The security guard jumps in fear at the sound of Jesse’s voice, but shortly after calms down enough to speak. “We’re sorry Mr. Ramey, we weren’t supposed to be watching that area of the building.” Jesse reaches down and flips over one of the drinks tables. “Does that really matter when you are sitting there watching those damn monitors? Don’t you think you and your buddy should be patrolling this whole damn building instead of staying in one spot?” Jesse begins to point at the wound on his head, “You could have prevented this, but I’m going to make you remember that.” “Run!” The security guard shouts to his partner. Shanda Russell runs up behind Jesse and taps him on the shoulder. In one swift movement he turns and looks at Shanda. “What?! What do you want?!” Flinching at Jesse’s sudden movement and the sound of his voice, Shanda finds it inside of herself to speak. Instinctively with a reporters question. “Jesse how do you feel about what just happened to you?” “How do I feel?!” Jesse pauses. “How do I feel?!” Jesse says with empathies. “How in the hell am I supposed to feel?!” Jesse asks angrily. “Tell me that much, Shanda. How would you feel?! How would it make you feel if some of Tyler Nelson’s goon squad was to come and beat you down on every single OSW show there is?! Isn’t it bad enough that I have people calling me queer and homosexual, but these damn ass holes have to come in and try to make me look even more like an idiot than the OSW has already done. Okay, I’ll tell you how I feel. I’m fucking mad!” Jesse pauses. “Get that?” Shanda begins to blink and then continues. “Yes, Mr. Ramey, but how do you plan on taking care of this matter?” Jesse looks at Shanda as though she is the stupidest being on the face of the earth. “Jesus fucking Christ, Shanda. How in the hell am I supposed to deal with this when a person can’t even find out who those assholes are?” “I’m sorry, Mr. Ramey. I don’t know.” Shanda says apologizing to Jesse. “Of course you don’t know. You don’t know anything, but I’ll tell you one thing that I’m going to do right now that no one that these guys have attacked has had the balls to do.” Jesse says with a look of ingeniousness. “What’s that?” Shanda asks with a bit of intrigue. Jesse continues looking over Shanda very heavily with the same look of stupidity on his face. “If you’d keep your mouth shut I’d tell you.” Jesse then turns and looks dead into the camera’s lens. “You listen to me, Nelson’s bitches. I’m challenging you to a fucking match. You want to come in and try to clean out OSW. Well, here is a plan. You fucking bring your asses down to the ring on the next Slam and step into the ring with me! I don’t care what kind of match it is. I want you in the ring so I can show you just who the fuck you are messing with. A one on one confrontation, not some sissy bullshit where you have to sneak attack the other person to get the upper hand.” “So, what you are saying is that you are challenging these men to a match on the next Slam?” Shanda asks. Eyeing Shanda very heavily again, “Just keep your mouth shut and let me do the talking from here on out.” Back into the camera’s lens. “I don’t care who the four of you are and I don’t want any of this sissy bullshit that you’ve been trying to hand out to me and several other of my OSW members, but what I do want is a four on one match. The rules to be named later on this week, but I want you to bring yourself down that ramp and let me kick your asses the way it should be done. Face to Face! Now, do you know why, Shanda?” “Why?” Shanda questions Jesse. Turning away from Shanda and looking into the camera once more. “Because I Don’t Like Their Attitudes!” Jesse turns and kicks the table out of his way. Making his way down the hallway and out of site. Only shortly after this footage was shot did three more OSW members hear the call that Jesse Ramey made and jump to help him out. Clay Vendetta who had also fallen victim to those four men. Doug Troy, PCW living legend and the man over the security teams everywhere, Moose Malone. Today we catch up with Jesse Ramey outside of a hospital in Miami. Jesse sits on a bench on the outside waiting on something. Wearing a pair of blue jeans, combat boots, and a OSW T-shirt. A white bandage surrounds his entire head as he just leans back and drinks from the soda can that he has. People pass by Jesse looking at the wound and some even begin to laugh. One little boy sits down beside Jesse and just stares at him. “You want something kid?” Jesse asks, still looking off into nothingness. Both arms spread across the bench. “I saw you last night.” The little kid says with a sort of lisp, kind of like all little kids have. Now keep in mind that this is a child. Not a teenager. Seemingly around the age of six or seven. Jesse continues looking on into nothingness, “Don’t tell me you were one of my fans. Cause I sure did let them all down last night with that awful performance.” The little child begins to smile and tries to look up at Jesse, but the sun is shinning right in his face, he tries to cover it with his hand, but still squints his eyes, “No, sir, you didn’t let us down. We all knew what was happening with you. We knew the bad men had you and Mr. Vendetta scared and we didn’t mind. I was really worried about you after the show though. I didn’t know if those bad men had hurted you or not.” Jesse begins to breath a little heavily, trying to keep from telling the kid that it was all fake. Until he begins to rub his head and think about the awful scare that was left on his scalp. “Don’t be afraid for me little one.” Jesse says as he takes another drink out of his soda can. “I can take care of myself out there in that ring and those bad men did try to hurt me pretty badly. I didn’t want that to happen to me again, but it did and all I can say is that at least I did know what was going to happen before it did. I was pretty dumb not to notice what was going on, but it happened and I’m the dumb on for going along with it.” “No, Mr. Ramey. You did good. It wasn’t your fault that what happened, happened. Mr. Vendetta should have been thinking himself.” The little boy says smiling. Jesse turns and finally looks at the kid, with a smile on his face. “Thanks kid. I really appreciate those comments. It’s not every day that I of all people get a compliment. Whether it is from a little kid or from my peers.” “But, Mr. Ramey, you’re a wrestling superstar!” The child stands up on the bench. “You’re one of the greatest wrestlers of all time!” He swings his arms in the air. Jesse begins to crack up at the child, “So, you really feel that way about me, do you?” “Yes, Mr. Ramey.” The child flops back down onto the bench and looks back up at Jesse. “May I have your autograph?” He looks away in shame. Jesse then begins to feel bad for the child as a smile comes across his face, “Well, I wouldn’t do this for just anyone, but seeing as how you are such a huge, “ Jesse swings his arms out into the air, “Jesse Ramey fan, I’ll be more than happy to do it.” The child’s reaction turns from shame to unbelievable joy. “You will?” The child asks ecstatically. “Sure will.” Jesse says with a huge smile on his face. “Do you have something I can write with and on?” “Yes, sir!” The child pulls out a tiny pad of paper and pen from his pocket. He hands them both to Jesse. Jesse begins to chuckle as he writes in the little notebook, “Do you come prepared with these every time you come to the hospital?” “Only when I know one of the wrestlers got hurt really bad the night before.” The child begins to smile. Jesse continues the chuckle as Chass pulls up in a car and blows the horn. Jesse is startled at first, but then turns back to the child. “There ya go.” “Thank you, Mr. Ramey.” The child says as Jesse stands up and begins to walk over to the car. Jesse opens the door to the car and steps inside closing it behind him. He looks out the window and waves to the child who is sitting on the bench holding the notebook close to his heart. Chass looks over at Jesse who is now smiling heavily, “What happened to you?” She asks. “Oh, nothing.” Jesse says as he begins to shake his head. “Let’s just go and get John and Michelle. We’ve got a bit of a journey ahead of us.” “They’ve already gone ahead of us.” Chass says. Jesse’s eyes light up. “You’ve got to be kidding me!” Jesse shouts. “How could you let them go ahead of us like that?” “Don’t get yourself all in a mess. I sent them with Vendetta.” Chass says. Jesse begins to laugh. “Come on, you’ve got to have a little more sense then that, don’t you?” “What do you mean?” Chass asks. “Vendetta is only around to try and steal my spotlight. The guy thinks he holds a real grudge against those masked men, but what have they done to him?” Jesse questions. “They took a freaking, whatever the hell it was, and beat me senseless with it. They put me in a dress and made a total embarrassment out of me. What does he really have to be mad about? A couple of bruises and a couple of scratches.” Jesse pauses. “I got a freaking concussion and I’m still going to come down to the ring and kick their asses. Split head and all!” “Well, if I knew you felt so strongly against him then I would have never sent them with him and Niki.” Chass says. “Maybe next time you’ll learn to ask me before you make an type of decision like that.” Jesse says rudely. Chass begins to shout, “Maybe next time you’ll not get thrown into the hospital and scare the heck out of all of us like that! I had to let our tickets go to waste and didn’t want to let theirs go to waste so I did the right thing! Don’t you go getting an attitude with me cause I’ll go and make you eat your words!” Jesse slumps down into his chair, “Sorry.” “So, what is this here then? You becoming such a pansy that you can’t even stand up for yourself against me now?” Chass asks laughing. “I wouldn’t do anything to make you mad. I have more emotions than that for crying out loud. I don’t want to hurt you.” Jesse says looking at Chass. “Well, I guess I can let it slide just this once.” Chass smiles. “So, you couldn’t get a refund on those tickets?” Jesse asks. “No, but I got us another flight tonight at 9:30. I packed all of the things and they are in the back all ready to go.” Chass says. “Good, let’s get going then.” Jesse says. Into the pavement. The black pavement. ~The End~
