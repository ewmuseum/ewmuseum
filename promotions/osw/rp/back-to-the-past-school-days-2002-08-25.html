Back to the Past: School Days..

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-25-02 Another school day. Another guy passes me in the hall. Another insult. 'Hey freak!' But this time I care. This time as he walks away, I turn. He doesn't even hear me. I'm sure if he did, he wouldn't belive it. He knows I never react. I swing with a quickly formed fist, connecting with the back of his neck. He falls. I am astounded. He actually fell. I can hurt him. And something snaps. I take one step closer, draw back a boot and swing. The dull thump fills the hall and he tries to cover his stomach. That only opens his head for the next swing. Something in his face crunches wetly and a spurt of blood colors my boot, black-red on black. The crowd draws back as the third kick shakes blood off his face on to them. Someone called the teachers, but it is too late for that. More blows, more blood. The kidneys, back, spine, ribs, throat, it all blurs together. It barely registers that he has quit trying to protect himself. Quit moving. Quit screaming. Quit breathing. I pick him up by his varsity sweater. It's hard to lift that much dead weight. I turn him so I think that was his face is pointed towards me. It's kind of hard to tell. And then I laugh. And laugh. And laugh. And walk away laughing, with blood drying between my fingers. Only there is no blood. He walks away, unharmed, and I walk away, trying not to cry. Again. Everyday is the same to me now, I wake, I fill up, and I fuck up. No way out for me, just a sick repeating circle for me. Cameron awaits me outside of the school everyday, picking me up in his truck and taking back to the hell hole he calls for us a home. Since that day I found myself in the woods I haven't been able to forget pulling myself back to him. Back to the rape, the drugs, the violence. And now I lay, letting him have his way once more tonight. Then after he is done I clean myself up, crying silently while I dare not look in the mirror. For if I could see what I have become I would surely break down and end it all. His essence is cleared from my body and from the bathroom I lock the door and lay down my plate sized mirror, lacing it with the white powder from my pocket baggy. With a crave that was growing I take it all in to me, sniffing hard and then like the pathetic being that I am I lift the plate like mirror and lick the stuck substance from the surface. But as I do so my eyes open and I catch a glimpse of what I am. The mirror slips from my hand and everything begins to spin in to slow motion. I watch the mirror fall slowly and crack, shattering in to millions of pieces around me. I begin to shake as I step back from the mess, looking around the bathroom for something that could help me. My eyes settle on the door as Cameron busts in, his eyes narrowed. 'What the fuck are you doing in here!?' His eyes look downward upon the broken mirror and I know what I am in for. I can feel my body being snatched from the confines of the small bathroom and in to the bedroom I am drug. The wall stings my flesh as I slam in to it from the brute force Cameron slings me with. As I bounce backwards in to him I feel his rough hands embrace me and spin me around to face him. Again he is inside of me, and again I feel the pain coursing through my body. I try to cry, but my eyes have long since dried. I try to scream but my voice has died, my body aches, my mind raped..And Nothing can save me now.
