'Seven Deadly Sins' series;Part 5/7; 'Lust'

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: ... A bedroom is illuminated by daylight sneaking through the curtains. Then a sort of beeping buzz is heard, it's an alarm clock violently flashing '07:45.' Then quickly 'The Power Himself' Propaganda sits bolt up right throwing the bed covers around viciously. 'It's a miracle.' He gasps. Jumping out of bed, Propaganda hurries into the bathroom as the scene fades to black. The scene fades back in to see 'Reverend' Eric Sagar and Propaganda seated at a table in a small café. 'And this all happened in your dream?' Eric asks continuing the conversation, which started before the camera faded in. 'Yes.' Propaganda answers. 'Ugh… I'm not sure.' Sagar moans. 'I swear to God, Brother Propaganda. In my dream, I was playing Chess with Cliff Richard and then St. Paul knocked on the door and told us to go convert, thou that are unholy, thou that are dressed in revealing robes. And those that abuse their nakedness.' Propaganda says with much purpose and motive. 'So, we're going down to a strip club and convert members?' Eric Sagar asks with disbelief and a slight sense of mocking in his tone. 'My brother, it was a vision.' Propaganda ignores Sagar's comments. 'Well, I've got nothing else to do… all right. I suppose we can.' Sagar groans. Propaganda's skin on his face curl up into a sort of smile, as the scene once again fades to black. Slowly the scene fades back up again. It's very late at night, a street with prostitutes on the corner. Gangsters roaming the streets, a handful of police walking on patrol and strip joints everywhere. It has to be a section of the Pittsburgh's Red Light District. But in the cold, damp street. The camera picks out a very familiar vehicle parked discreetly out of the light of the street lamps, it's the LDV Van that Propaganda and Eric Sagar use. The camera switches to the front of the van, where Propaganda is looking read to pounce, while Eric Sagar is looking very discomforted in the driver's seat. 'Are you SURE this is a good idea?' Eric Sagar quizzes Propaganda. 'As sure as I ever will be, Brother Eric.' Propaganda proudly admitted. Propaganda is about to leave the truck when Sagar grabs his shoulder. 'Wait. Let us pray…' Eric Sagar suggests, obviously trying to buy him some time. 'Ugh, I suppose.' Propaganda grunts, knowing what Sagar is doing; making him pray is his kryptonite; he will stop to pray any time of the day. 'Our Father, You art in Heaven, Hallowed be thy name, Thy kingdom come, Thy will be done, On earth as it is in heaven, Give us this day our daily bread, And forgive us our trespasses, As we forgive those who trespass against us, Deliver us from evil, And not into temptation, For thine is the kingdom, The power and the glory, Forever and ever, A-men.' The duo prays with much effort in unison. Propaganda and Sagar look at each other, Propaganda with an eager grin and Sagar with a worried frown. 'Let's go!' Propaganda encourages Sagar with pride in his tone. 'Yes… Let's.' Sagar stutters uneasily. Propaganda heads out the van while Eric Sagar hesitantly shuffles out. They turn their attention to a strip joint named 'Legs Eleven' which is lit up magically by obscene neon lighting. Propaganda looks on very disgusted and Eric Sagar looks on trying to look disgusted, but in his heart and soul he is scared. He knows the stories told about these sorts of places, drugs, weapon deals and much violence. Eric Sagar knew what was best. He decided to risk all of his respect and admiration he had built up with Propaganda and turn to him. 'Brother Propaganda…' Sagar says with a shaky discomfort in his tone. 'I'm scared.' 'Brother Eric.' Propaganda chuckles. 'If you remember this used to be my scene before I was arrested and had the calling. I know exactly what to do.' 'But what if it's different?' Sagar queries. 'Phillipians 4:13 'I can do all thing through Christ which strengthens me'' Propaganda quotes with much confidence. 'Yes…' Sagar says regaining, his cocky, arrogant snigger. 'Yes. Let's go in and convert so many atheists that we'll be in the hall of fame.' Propaganda nods with a smirk on his face then they head into the strip joint. When they get in, it's a very smutty, smoky atmosphere. There are certain jeers at the get up the two are wearing - Sagar in his suit and Propaganda in his ring attire. They stand in the centre of the room, Propaganda takes on glimpse at the pole dancers and shrieks. 'Cover your eyes.' 'So where do we start?' Eric Sagar asks. 'Let's make an impact, like we did in OSW, when he took out Vampir Nosferatu and Epitome of Evil. Let's take centre stage.' Propaganda suggests. Eric Sagar looks at the door, where two, big, bulky, black, bouncers stand motionless with Uzi's clasped in their palms. He gulps and tries to hold back Propaganda who is striding to the stage, but he is dragged along with him. Propaganda nudges one of the girls out of the way. People start to jeer and boo. 'NOW MY BROTHERS! WHY MUST YOU LUST THESE TROLLOPS?' Sagar barks angrily. 'ALL I CRAVE IS JESUS!' 'Faggot!' shouts someone. Which is followed by chuckles and laughs. '…AND GOD, AND THEY LOVE YOU!' Sagar preaches 'They may love me, but everyone else thinks I'm an asshole.' Another voice says. Which is followed by a raucous laugh. 'PLEASE… LISTEN AND BE MATURE!' Eric Sagar pleads. Then from the back walks the owner, wearing full pimp gear and with two bodyguards, even bigger, even bulkier and even blacker than the ones at the door behind him. 'Is there a little trouble, with these two…' the owner looks up and down at the pair with disgust. 'gentlemen?' 'Yeah.' One of the bouncers at the door says. 'They tryna' preach.' 'Ohhhhh, they're tryna' preach, are they?' The owner laughs mockingly. 'Then why not stop preachin' and say yo' prayers!' On that the two bodyguard and the two doorman all draw their Uzi's. 'OH LORD SAVE ME!' Sagar roars. But meanwhile Propaganda has leaped of the stage and speared, the owner, and the two bodyguards. The bouncers are quick to start to shoot at Propaganda missing him. But one of them is 'unfortunately' whacked rather violently over the head with a wooden chair, by 'The Reverend' Eric Sagar. The other doorman, quickly looks down at Sagar, aims his Uzi then… 'CRASH!' Propaganda football tackles him before he can shoot. 'Let's get out of here!' Sagar commands, his heart racing as, the owner and their bodyguards begin to stagger to their feet. The pair, hurry out of the building and quickly hightail it into the van. 'Hurry up and start the engine!' Propaganda orders Sagar as the five men leave the club. The five men look around the street, then they notice the van owner points to it van and yells. 'THERE THEY ARE!' Sagar keeps trying to start the engine, but it keeps failing. 'What's wrong?' Propaganda asks. 'I think the engine is failing.' Sagar sighs. 'Oh no…' Propaganda groans. 'Oh Lord…' Sagar thinks. The five men start to approach the vehicle. 'If you're up there, what am I saying? I know you're up there.' 'Boys…' the owner begins to order. 'Well anyway… SAVE US!!!' 'Pop them.' He finishes. 'Please.' The bouncers and bodyguards draw their Uzi's at the car. 'HELP US!!!' Propaganda and Eric Sagar scream together. The Gangsters are about to pull the triggers, then, as if by command; a storm cloud forms over the Pennsylvanian night sky and… 'ZAP!' A bolt of lighting hits the space of road in between the gangsters and the van. It startles the gangsters so much they fall over. But then… 'Vrrrrrrrrrrrrooooooom….' The engine has started. The electricity of the lighting had started up the failed battery. 'IT'S A MIRACLE!' Propaganda roars with delight! 'IT MUST BE!' Sagar exclaims. The two drive off as the dumfounded Gangsters look on. 'Damn bro'!' One of the gangsters yelps. 'God is real, man.' The bodyguards and bouncers drop their guns on the middle of the floor. 'Yeah! We quit. We're gonna' do a holier job.' Another says as the four men start to walk off. 'WHAT!?' The owner cries. 'YOU CAN'T DO THIS!' The scene begins to fade to black as the owner of the strip joint has a temper tantrum in the middle of the street. The scene fades in, the next morning. Eric Sagar and Propaganda are having their breakfast. 'The next time I listen to one of your plans again… shoot me.' Sagar moans. 'I said I was sorry.' Propaganda groans taking a spoon full of oats. ''It was a sign' you said.' Sagar imitates Propaganda. 'God got us out of it didn't he?' Propaganda adds. 'Yeah. I suppose.' Sagar mumbles. 'What's on the news?' Propaganda asks reaching for the Television remote control. On the news is the lovely Ms. Sandy Warrington, '…Also in the news, British musician and dedicated Christian, Cliff Richard was arrested last night for causing trouble in a gentlemen's club near his home in San Francisco in the early hours of the morning. Sources say he must have been blind drunk, though breathalyser tests show nothing.' She reports as Propaganda turns to Eric Sagar with a grin on his face. The scene fades to black.
