Another sleepless night

12 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/12/02 Frankie lies in his bed, starring at the clock. It glows 5:00 a.m., sending a red glare on his eyes. His mind is heavy with thought. An overpowering feeling of missed opportunity. He knows the offer to wrestle Ivan was tainted. A desperate white man trying to get a strong black man to do his dirty work, but it was also an opportunity. Why must he be so stubborn? His mind is riddled with second guessing. It is obvious he will get no sleep. Frankie tears back the covers and sits on the side of his bed. His bald head rests in his weathered hands. The pain of the presents just compounds the demons of the past. His mind of full of activity, none under his control. He's struggled with the control of his emotions forever, but it's never been this bad. His mind doesn't belong to him anymore, it is out of his hands. The struggle to regain this control begins now. He is determine to have a clear conscious once again. Frankie grabs the newspaper off his couch on his way to his dinner table, his late night retreat as of late. He reaches in the fridge, grabs a water, and sits at the table to read the paper. His head rests in one hand as the other turns the pages slowly. Frankie's eye focus not on the words, they are a haze. His mind is elsewhere and there's not much he can do to regain focus. Unfortunately for Frankie, this focus comes in a rush of blinding reality. His eyes open wide and his head lowers to intently read the article... Police give up hope on search for local boy By: Staff Police chief Teddy Williamson announced today that they have discontinued an in depth search for a young boy who was reported missing over a month ago. Hardy Sims, a young man living at the Boy's Club in Brooklyn, was last seen playing in front of the Boy's Club. Some reports say that he was seen talking to a man in a black Mercedes, then he was grabbed and the car sped off, although these reports are unconfirmed. The boy was said to have connections to the professional wrestler Frankie Bones, he was unable to be reached for comment. When asked about the subject, Williamson had this to say... 'In a case like this, after this amount of time, it is safe to say there is no hope for this young man. The case is still open and we will take any leads, but we have discontinued our searches and investigation. We ask that anyone with any information please contact us at the police station.' Suddenly a drop of water lands on the article, then another, then another. Frankie sits, with his head in his hands, sobbing. The Legend of Pain, a man who has not shed a tear in over 20 years, is crying. Twenty plus years of oppressed anger, pain, and sadness flow onto the newspaper. The tears are strong and salty, the way one would think a twenty plus year old tear of great proportions would taste. The seconds turn into minutes, the minutes into hours, and the scene never changes. His uncontrollable mind pours onto the paper, yet the pain never lightens...fade...
