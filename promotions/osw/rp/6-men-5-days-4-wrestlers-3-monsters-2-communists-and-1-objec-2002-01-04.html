6 Men, 5 Days, 4 Wrestlers, 3 Monsters, 2 Communists and 1 Objective

4 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1.04.02 The two men stood there, next to the yellow cab, staring up at the front facia of the building. Squinted eyes scanned the wall of the Gulf Centre, Krieg and Wang observing the location of next week’s SLAM! But they were not alone, for there was another man with them who’s face had not been seen on OSW television before. Stepping out of the cab was a giant of a man, one only comparable to men such as Krieg, Stanislav or Walczak. 7’3” of muscle, skin and bone stood tall next to Wang, almost dwarfing the 7’0” monster that is Michel Krieg. As Wang and Krieg drew their eyes away from the building, they turn round to the stranger. “Ah, the building is well constructed. It will need to be with the amount of damage we are going to lay down on Heresian Bloodlust on Saturday, eh Wang,” said Krieg, a grin sneaking out of his tightly closed lips. “Yes, Qwieg. I think we will be victorious this week, under the control of yourself and Tom Walczak,” responded Wang, with his green Chinese military hat slightly to the side on top of his white haired head. “Andreas, shall you be attending the event? I know your are not fond of Red Army but I would appreciate you being there,” questioned Krieg, a little bit of friendliness being shown by the aggressive German, which is quite rare. The tall stranger answers in a broad Turkish accent, “Ah Michel, I do not know. I have many things to do and you know I’m not a big fan of these kind of matches,” “Ah Andreas, Andreas. You shall not need to be involved. The Red Army does not play like that. We don’t need any help fro...” Krieg is cut off by his Turkish comrade Andreas, “Ah, but remember Krieg. I am no Red Army. I am solo wrestler, manager, friend. Whatever you what to call me, I am not a comrade of the Red Army,” shoots the giant Turk. “Ah, Ok. I shall not mention it again then,” said Krieg as he motioned to both men, “Let’s go inside. I want to see the facilities of this place, if it even has any,” All three men walk away from the cab as it screeches off, speeding away from the arena. The doors to the centre are stiff and made of solid steel, clanging on the stoppers as Krieg yanks them open. A reception greets the 3 men, a pretty young girl sitting behind it, shuffling some papers. “Hi there gentlemen. Can I help you,” asks the receptionist, giving the 3 foreigners a nice smile. Not to be taken back by manners, Krieg fires right back at her, “Yes you can. You can tell me where I can find changing facilities in this centre, as well as the main performance area,” “Well, if you just follow this corridor on the right here, you take the first right, walk to the end of the corridor, take a left, pass through a couple of doors, take the right hand door when you come to...” “Listen, I am not Capitalist. I do not go trailing around corridors looking for things. I find direct route. Now, where are the changing rooms,” questions Michel, Wang and Andreas standing behind him in anticipation. “Well, as I was saying, you take the corridor on the...” Krieg suddenly slams his fists down on the reception desk and bawls at the young lady, “Right, let me get this into your thick skull. I want direct route to changing rooms. I’m not following corridors and taking a million lefts and rights. Where are the changing rooms,” roars Krieg. Slightly taken back by Krieg’s aggression, she responds in a weak voice, “They’re just round the corner on the right hand side,” “Thank you,” replies Krieg, the receptionist a little shocked and frightened as she goes back to filing papers. The 3 men walk away from the reception towards the changing areas, “Ah, these damn Americans. They are so complicated...Anyway, I hope the rest of them get here soon,” The scene fades out as a “TO BE CONTINUED” notice flashes onto the screen.
