Myself, Imprisoned: Ape Dos Mil

9 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 9, 2002 This was so fucking stupid. All around me, I could hear the sounds of men going insane, men snoring, or men masturbating. It was all very uncomfortable and displeasing. But fuck it, I didn’t care. All I cared about was getting out of this fucking dump. I was placed in the Detroit Prison after the July 6 edition of Slam. I was sent here because I ‘stabbed’ Aimz in the shoulder. Fuck, I didn’t stab her. Reno tackled me, and she was stabbed as he tackled me on the count of I lost control of my knife. I was surprised that they didn’t punish me any further, because after all I did attempt to rape and kill Aimz. Although, I heard they were saving that for later. The Elite were going to press charges against me or something. This was becoming worse than I thought. I’ve never actually had charges pressed against me… well, at least not for attempted rape and murder. Usually I finish my rapes and killings, and no one ever knows that anything happened. But this time, my victim got lucky. She escaped, twice, and it was all thanks to a fucking skater midget. A skater midget named Reno Davis. The fucking ‘Unwanted Angel’. Woo woo, what a nickname. He sounds really ‘unwanted’, he has thousands of fans across the world worshipping the ground he walks on… yeah, very unwanting. Who does he think he is? ‘The Unwanted Angel’ sounds like some depressed murder or something… heh, that sounds a lot like me. Hell, I could never do with a nickname like that. Then again, I don’t know why I even have nicknames. ‘Corg’ is all it takes. God, I hate this prison… It’s full of perverted old men. I’m just a scrawny little 18 year old punk, whom they all think they can pick on. Yesterday, I showed my former cell-mate Gregg a thing or two. And in the weight training center, I beat the hell out of this middle-aged dude. He was spotting me while I was benching around 150, and then all of a sudden he let the weights drop onto my neck. Luckily I caught it… But still, I gave the guy a beating. I think I might’ve broken his neck, or at least his nose… he was all bloody. It was fucking hilarious. But none of this matters. I just want to get out of this hellhole. I don’t think I can take another day of being here. And to think, I’m complaining after only 3 days, and some of these other shits have been here for years. Fuck, who am I to complain? I haven’t been home in a while. A long while… I’ve been on the road for months, and I haven’t seen Shyme for at least a month. I don’t want to sound like a pussy, but I miss being home… I never thought I’d say that. I miss everything… I’m having second thoughts on all this shit, all of the killings and beatings. Why do I do what I do? ‘Normal’ people don’t go off and kill other humans for fun. ‘Normal’ people don’t own and care for cannibalistic rabbits. ‘Normal’ people don’t look like me, or control a realm, or any of that. Fuck normal people! I’m not normal, goddamn. Why do I even let myself think that far? Although it’s true, I don’t like the way I am… I wish I was dead. No, no, I wish everyone else was dead. I’m just fine with myself, there’s nothing wrong with me. I don’t need to be ‘normal’. Shit. It’s been so long since I’ve been myself… … “Isiic Raiyn?” A man’s eager voice woke me up from my sleep, as I arose from my bed to answer the voice. I was still half asleep. “Isiic, you’re free to go.” Said the guard. “You’ve been bailed out.” What? Shyme didn’t have any no money, and neither did Rhett, nor any of my other friends. “You’ve been bailed out by a person(s) whom would like to remain anonymous.” …what the fuck?
