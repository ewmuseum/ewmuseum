Possibly the Last Episode of Seth's World?!?!?!

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-28-02 Oh shit. We’ve already missed the majority of this promo. I must of overslept. I knew I should have gotten that damn alarm clock fixed. This could very well be the last episode of Seth’s World EVER and we only get to view the last few minutes. Damn it! I really blew it this time. And now I’m wasting even more time rambling on about my mistake, forcing any viewer to miss still more of the episode! Oh, will I ever do anything right?! Will I ever do anything... And now we return to this shitload of crap, already in progress “...And that should just about wrap up our show,” Trevor Hawke says, smiling at the camera. “I’d like to thank our guest, Keanu Reeves, for coming back this week to finish up his interview.” “Though I’m sure he didn’t mind,” Seth Masters interrupts with a grin. “We had a hella cool time this last week, didn’t we Keanu?” Seth gives a playful nudge to one Keanu Reeves, who is at the moment seated on the couch between the two members of Civil Disobedience. The look on his face seems to contradict Masters’ words, as Reeves appears to be walking the tightrope otherwise known as the frayed ends of sanity. His eyes are open wide, unblinking, as he stares in the direction of the rooms exit. He rants to himself, muttering softly, spittle collecting in the corners of his mouth. “Must...escape! Must...get...out!!!” Trevor glances at Keanu for a second and then chuckles to himself, finding some humor in the man’s longing gaze toward the door. “What’s the matter, Keanu? You seem on edge,” “I bet you can’t wait to get home so you could write up one of those fancy Hollywood movies scripts with starring roles for me and Seth. I can picture it now. Bill and Ted 3: Seth and Trevor’s Excellent Adventure!” For the first time thus far, Keanu closes his eyes, but only to shudder in disgust. Putting a hand over his mouth in an effort to keep his lunch down, he returns his stare to the doorway as quickly as possible. “Well anyway, all you Seth Masters fans out there just better be sure to plop down your hard-earned cash to order Head-On-Collision Sunday night to see me make the entertainment industry a better place,” Seth says, managing to take a tone which makes his words sound important. Offscreen we can hear the exciting whoop of a camera, who begins laughing excitedly. “Ha ha! Seth is going to throw the match on Sunday, and forfeit his ability to ever appear on public access television! Awesome!” we can hear the cameraman shout happily. Dancing sounds can be heard from off camera, as he continues to exclaim his joy. “What a great guy, sparing the audience from this crap! I’ll never have to lower myself to working on this shoddy excuse of a program again! Ha ha ha ha!” Watching his cameraman carry on in this excited state of celebration, Seth’s expression turns sour. “I was talking about the fact that we will end the careers of Team Annoying, you dumb fuck,” Seth states angrily. “The show is solid. The show is good. The show is not in jeopardy. Now do your job and stand next to the camera, damn it. And smile!” A disappointed sigh can be heard from offscreen, and then silence once more. Seth, Trevor, and Keanu all sit on the couch, not quite knowing what to say. Seconds pass, until Reeves leaps to his feet. “Goodbye everyone!” Keanu yells, waving creepily at the camera. “Er...that’s not really a good note to leave the show on...” Trevor begins apprehensively. “But too bad, because we are doing that anyway!” Keanu shouts again. “Goodbye!” We shift to an alternate shot, where we can see the recording light on the camera that was filming the show dim and flicker off. Seth and Trevor exchange confused look, before bringing their gazes to the cameraman. “Hey! Hey!” Trevor exclaims at the cameraman. “Just because Keanu is a movie star doesn’t mean he calls the shots here!” “Actually...” the cameraman explains in a sarcastic tone. “I think he does. You see, he offered me a job working on his next film if I would cut the show off early. So I did. And now, I can think of not one reason why I am still here talking to you. Oh well. See you later, guys. I have a real career to look forward too.” And with that, the cameraman nonchalantly walks offscreen, whistling a happy tune. Watching him go, Seth and Trevor once more exchange confused looks. “That little bitch,” Seth mutters, shaking his head. “I can’t believe that. Is there no loyalty these days? Keanu, why in the world would you...” Seth turns to face Reeves, only to find that the star of Hardball is no longer standing there. Sweeping his gaze around the room, Seth sees Reeves making a mad dash toward the doorway. It takes him all of half a second to make the trip to the door, and once there Keanu begins fumbling madly with the doorknob. Twisting the knob, he pushes into the door, which doesn’t give at all. He pushes again and again, but it appears as if the door isn’t going to open for him. Turning around, he looks back at Seth and Trevor, who are now approaching him slowly. Keanu’s eyes open wide in fright, the unadulterated insanity displayed within frightening to all those who had hoped for a Speed 3 being filmed. He begins to shake vehemently in fear, before turning to the window next to the doorway and leaping through it. He shatters the glass as he rolls out onto the lawn. Small shards of glass are jutting out from his arms and back as he gets up and begins to run away from the house as fast as his legs will carry him. A cackling scream can be heard as Keanu Reeves disappears into the distance. We pan back to reveal Trevor and Seth standing behind the now-shattered window, staring out in complete and utter disbelief. Walking over to the door, Seth places his hand on the knob, twists it, and pulls it inward without much effort all. The shocked looks show no signs of fading. “Trev, did Keanu seem a bit...eager to get out of here, or is it just me?” Seth manages to question, as he squints his eyes in an effort to make out the now faint form of Reeves in the distance. “It would indeed seem to look that way,” Trevor remarks, sounding stunned as well. “In fact, I believe I pointed that out near the end of the show, if you were listening.” Seth continues looking after Reeves, not responding to his partner’s comment. Seeing that Seth isn’t paying attention to him at all, Trevor walks over to him and kicks him angrily. “I SAID, if you were listening!” Trevor shouts, as Seth falls down on his side in surprise. “Huh?...Oh, sorry,” Seth mutters, sounding out of it as he shakes his head. “Now as I was about to say, we both know it’s impossible that Keanu would actually WANT to get away from us,” Trevor says, thinking out loud. “How could anyone be that fervent about putting distance between themselves and us? There has to be another reason. I mean, we’re just so damn awesome. To reject us would be to reject everything we stand for.” “And what exactly do we stand for?“ Seth asks, looking up at his partner from the ground. “Comics? Video games? Going to the mall?” “Well...yeah, all of that stuff...” Trevor agrees with a nod. “And just being cool in general! I’m sure a major celebrity like Keanu wouldn’t be so foolish as to just shove us, the epitome of trendiness, to the side. That’s career suicide.” “Maybe we were just imagining things,” Seth offers. “Maybe he had an appointment or something. He couldn’t NOT want to hang out with us...right?” “We can only hope, Seth. We can only hope,” Trevor answers back with a nod. He reaches his hand out in a friendly gesture, pulling his partner to his feet. The two members of Civil Disobedience stand in silent contemplation for a few seconds, running the events as of late over in their heads. “Trev...” Seth says somberly. “What’s going to happen if we get screwed tonight? I’m not sure I can handle not being able to do our show again.” “Don’t worry about it,” Trevor comforts. “Besides, even if we do get stripped of the show, it wasn’t a very good one anyway.” “Yeah, I know...” Seth admits reluctantly. “It would be way more of a blow to actually have lost to Carrot Top and Gilbert Gottfried than to lose the stupid show,” Trevor says, raising a fist in the air. “Let’s just not embarrass ourselves tonight.” Seth looks at him oddly, raising an eyebrow. “Alright, strike that one off the record,” Trevor corrects himself with a grin. “Let’s just whoop some Team Annoying ass.” “K,” Seth agrees, nodding his head. With this particularly unexciting shot, the scene fades to black. Oooo, the tension in the air. Can you feel it? The anticipation of possibly the stupidest match ever-booked in the history of professional wrestling...barring the Pat Patterson vs. Gerald Brisco Evening Gown match...is hanging heavy in the air. Who will win? The biggest sham of a team to ever grace the squared circle? Or will it be Carrot Top and Gottfried? I guess you’ll just have to tune in and find out. May god have mercy on your souls.
