A Punishment Session Turns Into A Brilliant Idea.

24 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/24/02 Since the dawn of ancient civilization people have had bodyguards. People who are specially trained to protect that person no matter what. For the most part, all of these have been successful. Never has the bodyguard let their protectee down. There have been many close calls through out the job, but the protector has been there for the protectee always. Until now. This is where our scene opens up. The immediate setting finds in a very small room. There are four plain white walls The room looks to be in a very low condition, probably not cleaned or serviced in a few years. Pieces of the sheet rock are missing and various cracks down the walls. The carpet is even worse, a very ugly color of brown and the condition gives it the appearence of the infamous disco era shag style, circa 1974. In the middle of this room is a work out bench, the metal is covered in rust and the padding is no better. It's a very dull shade of grey and there are multiple pieces of yellow foam rubber poking out. As the view changes we can see Randolph laying back on the bench, wearing his usual attire. A pair of large and clunky white tennis shoes cover his small feet, a pair of dark brown slacks that bugle out at the wiast due to his large and hair covered belly, and a light blue tee shirt with the same inscription on the front as the white one on Slam, 'Taylor Trained Ninja.' As the view moves upwards Randolph's face is seen, he is grunting and has a look of exhaust and pain etches onto his fat face. A further look into this scene reveals a large weight over his head, moving up and down. He is bench pressing what looks to be about one hundred and thirty pounds or so. Standing over the overweight security guard is The Single Most Technically Sound Athlete In Professional Wrestling Today, The Man Who Defines The Term 'Taylor Made', Scott Taylor. Since this is Wednesday there is no need to be decked out in wrestling attire, Scott is wearing a pair of black denim jeans and a white wife beater, his arms are crossed over his chest and he is looking down at Randolph. Serving as spotter for his own personal lackey, erm security guard who doubles as a manager. Scott doesn't appear to be too happy by the grim expression written over his devlishly handsome face. (Author's Note: Take that all you people who say my poser is ugly!) Scott's right hand is wrapped around the metal bar connecting the weights, helping Randolph press the large weight. But you can not really blame Scott for his sour mood. This past Saturday night was his big change in Old School Wrestling, his first title shot. After a grueling and painstaking match with 'The Qunisential Teen Idol' Kid Wonder and a gracious win, he was all set for this. The title match came against Corg and it everything and then more than our hero expected. Everything seemed to be all wrapped up until that no good Kid Wonder appeared from the crowd and interefered. Laying out The Man Who Defines The Term with a superkick and then infamous 'So Wonderful' Five Star Guillotine Legdrop. (Author's Note: K. better be thanking me for making his move sound very technical.) and costing him a taste of Old School Gold. The members of NOW, including the seven hundred pound Bessie had Scott's back in that match, but his personal security was NOT there! He was not doing his job, which in turn let Scott down! Thus the reason why the two of them are in this run down hotel room owned by a toothless hick named Bubba Hicks. Scott's 'Ninja' made a fluke and this is both training and punishment. 'How could you do that to me Randolph? You were supposed to be out there protecting me and making sure that that run-in didn't happen! But NOOOOOOOOOOOO you had to run to te back and hide from the members of NOW. The Cult easily destroyed all of them and I know you could have handled them. Because YOU are a Taylor Trained Ninja!' . Scott lectures as he watches Randolph exercise. The man is sweating quite profusely and looks as if he'll pass out at any given minute. However Scott ignores this and continues. 'I hired you, and most importantly, I pay you to protect me! You didn't do that did you Randolph?!' There is a long silence, Randolph says nothing. Scott looks down at him and presses down on the weight, pushing it close to Randolph's chest. The man coughs and then speaks for the first time on this pre-recorded promo. (Author's Note: That's a classic. Admit it.) His voice is somewhat high pitched and a slight lisp is present due to the fact that his two front teeth are missing. 'Sorry Mr Taylor, but I wouldn't have been a match for Bessie. She weighs more than Al Bundy's Mother-In-Law. I can't take down a woman of that obesity.' responds Randolph. 'That's not an excuse! You are supposed to be able to handle any situation that is thrown at you! If Eleanor Hall-Kannon can decapitate a man with a slap, I expect you to be able to protect me no matter what the situation. But, since it was your first day on the job I'll forgive you. But you better make a good showing this week.' 'Yes sir Mr Taylor. I think that those various kicks, that you are a master of I may add, will come to a good use. But aren't you in a cage match this week? How am I going to be able to protect you when a wall of steel seperates us?' Scott releases his hold on the weight and it immediately falls onto Randolph's chest. The overweight man lets out a let gasp and attempts to wrestle the weight off of him as Scott turns his back and begins to pace around the room in thought. His left hand is cupped under his chin and a look of real thought is on his face, a rare occurence to be seen on OSW Television. Finally Randolph manages to remove the weight. It rolls down his chest, over the large hump which is his stomach, over his crotch, down his thighs, finally landing on the floor straddling his feet. He sits up on the bench and reaches for the white towel draped over the metal bars which are designed to hold the weight. He whipes his brow as Scott snaps his fingers and turns back to him. 'I've got it!' Signs of Intelligence! Randolph looks inquisitively at his employee. Scott smiles evilly at Randolph and then begins to give away the details of his latest master plan. 'Those steel cages are made of what if basically a very large and reinforced chain link fence. There are holes in chain link fences, Randolph. Which is very convenient for a man with your position. If someone tries to escape the cage before me, all you have to do is ram some object through the holes and cut them off.' Randolph stares blankly at Scott for several seconds. He slowly nods his head and Scott gives him an angered look. 'It is my master plan, Randolph! It will gurantee a win for me! And with that win comes another shot at Corg! And PHD thinks that he is so smart.' Scott begins to laugh as Randolph stands and begins to walk towards the door. As he passes by Scott grabs his arm and pushes him backwards towards the bench. Randolph trips over the weight and lands on his back on the bench. Scott approaches and looks down at him. 'Where are you going? This training course isn't over yet. If you want to be a Ninja you'll have to do alot more. Get to it Gaijin!' Scott picks up the weight and hands it to Randolph who almost drops it on himself. Scott makes his way around the bench and retakes his spotting position as our scene begins to fade out. Blackness appears slowly and then the scene comes to an end with the sounds of Scott audibly counting.
