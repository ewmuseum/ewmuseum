War on Two Fronts

10 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/10/02 Andrew Washington & Marcus Williams Los Angeles, CA, United States of America 134 Cedarbrook Terrace, 90210 To All Who Have A Problem With Da Westsidaz, Odds are always stacked against the good guys. It’s a rule. Civil Disobedience and us had the odds stacked up against them for the last month. Not only did they have to fight each other, they also had two monsters fighting them. They had the Champs own manager as the referee. Everything was against Civil Disobedience and us, but there was a difference between the two. We’re fighters we don’t quit, we don’t pussy out and we don’t take short-cuts. Civil Disobedience, on the other hand, folded like an origami when faced with pressure. Civil Disobedience sold out. They spit in the face of everyone who respected them, especially us. We respected them as athletes and as people, but we had to fight them if they wanted the titles. We though they could handle it, but they gave in to the odds. They went bust, they quit, they cried, they bitched. They went against everything they said they were for and joined a gang. They joined a gang of people desperate for recognition. They joined up with the biggest asshole walking and another sell-out. Sure, now they have strength in numbers. They’re big tough jackals now. But guess what? They’re jackals all right, but us, we’re the mother-fucking lions. “New School”? The name suits them. They’re children, preschoolers, going into the High School and pushing people around. No, it doesn’t work that way. You’re walking into the High School, but people think you’re a joke. But remember, this is High School. We may laugh a little bit, but if start really bothering us, you know what? We’re in High School, so were stupid enough to kick the shit out of you, even if you aren’t in our league. This is a war between Civil Disobedience and us. But now, in typical fashion of all the great downfalls, the man overboard is dragging a few sailors by the ankles kicking and screaming. Hawke and Masters can’t take the fact that they’re out of their league. They can’t take the fact that they are drowning, so they decided to grab “Switchblade” Sebastian Toomes and Amy Campbell by the ankles. Sure, it’ll delay the fall, but eventually Seabass and the Ho-Bag are going to be tired of dangling and drop with you. Welcome aboard friends of Civil D, I hope you enjoy your trip on the S.S. Career Death, your destination is rock bottom. You are now officially guilty. Guilty by association, guilty by you’re your actions last week and guilty in the eyes of Da Westsidaz. So, like all those we’ve tried before, your going to be executed like animals. We don’t care is the odds are three on two, four on two, eight on two, it doesn’t matter. We’re known for beating the odds. We’re known for beating adversity. We’re known for beating people down and soon we’re going to be known for beating the hell out of four pre-Madonna’s thinking they can push us around. Hawke…Masters…we know you are looking for a way to hide. We know you want to be protected and guarded. We know this is just a ploy for you to have a good hiding place away from us. You need the odds to be on your side because you’re afraid. You’re afraid of Da Westsidaz and you’re afraid of what is going to happen to you two. You don’t have any clue what we’re capable of. You don’t have a clue about what is going to happen in a few weeks. The 23rd of June will be inscribed in OSW History. The 23rd of June will be inscribed in your mind. The 23rd of June will be a day that will live in infamy in your eyes. The 23rd of June will be your end. You won’t forget that day, not for a lifetime. It’s uncertain what you two will remember more, though. Will it be the embarrassment that you had when you realized what league we’re in? Will it be the scars covering your bodies, reminding you of the ass-kicking you got? Or will it be the remembrance of the day you saw the era of Da Westsidaz begin? Our money is on the latter. So, this message goes out to New School. It goes out to all four of you attention starved youths. You started a war with us. We hope you are ready to continue you it and we hope you have what it takes to survive it. But, the odds are against us. Not only to we have a union of cocky morons to combat, we also have a union of satanic morons to deal with as well. The OSW Tag Team Champions? A disgrace to the titles. But they still like to act like they own the place. At Ascension, we get to bring our weapons to the big show. Civil Disobedience get to bring theirs. But you know what the great thing is? It’s elimination. I think that Heresian Bloodlust better be worried about that. They’re reign of disgusting antics and pathetic, lack-lustre matches and just plain garbage is coming to an end. They know it. That’s why Cain passed out from complete fear and lack of confidence. They know what’s coming. They don’t want to lose the titles, but it’s their destiny. Maybe somewhere along the line we’ll give them a shot at our titles. But they’ll have to get in line. They need to be punished for destroying the credibility of the tag division. They have to be punished for the injustices they’ve done to Daisy. They have to be punished for their existence. Call us the Punishers. We’ll be the ones to pass judgment on those hacks. If they is anything we hate more, it’s satanic jobbers who live in darkness and plot sadistic things against everything. We hate it and we’ll destroy it. We've been in worse matches. We have a working strategy for every match we've come across. This obstacle can be taking out of the way. This is nothing. Even now we have a strategy. We have Plan A to Plan Z set up and reviewed over and over in our minds. Here's just a tidbit of our plan. Heresian Bloodlust's major disadvantage exceeds their advantages. They can be eliminated right away. That would put a damper on their plans and we all know that. But it’s toughest for us. We have no back up, we have no stable, no guest referees, no Imps, no nothing. All we have is ourselves. All we have is our own skills. All we have is a baseball bat. All we have is a kendo stick. All we have is unbeatable odds. All we have is a war to win.
