The Problem Question- Part 1

27 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-27-02 If the Promo comes out bad, then here is the link to it.. Seems Mac's are horrible for posting things here... http://www.theewu.com/ewu/michaeldame.shtml ===================== Introduction It's been a couple nights since Michael Dame made his debut, facing Frakine Bones in a real show stealer. The match went back and fourth, momentarily until Dame nailed the Damer Kick, his finishing move, and got the 1,2,3. Although, the story of the match was not just inside the ring. Drew Hostyle, and Switchblade, co-members of 3B With Frankie Bones, decided to make a run in, obviously trying to get Frankie the win...although they never made it to the ring. Rhette Locke and Jesse Ramey were waiting in the first row, like they knew a run in would occur, quickly hopped the barrier and started an all out brawl while Dame and Bones finished their match...It was cleaned up, and the show went on... Now, we're 3 nights later. Michael Dame has plans to meet his good friend, and manager Gloria at a local diner for some coffee and talk over their plans for the week, most importantly Saturday! A Fine Meeting- Part 1 The night is cold, and calm. Not much life drifts around the streets on a Tuesday Night, in the middle of February. Camera focuses in on a sign, that hangs above a small street corner store. It reads 'The Fine Diner'...Outside stands a man, the camera focuses in on his face from afar, and can now see it's Michael Dame. Dressed for success, as usual. Around the corner comes a car, going averagly fast, it stops right in front of Dame. Woman From Car- Sorry, I'm late... Let me go park my car. Michael Dame- beh... Dame, a bit unhappy about Gloria showing up so late to their 'appointment'.. Gloria parks the car around the street, and walks up, running her hands through her hair to give it a little spice. She meets Dame with a kiss on her cheeck. Michael Dame- So why are you late, Gloria? Gloria- Oh my god...you're never going to believe this. I met a....man! Michael is a bit shocked, as Gloria is a fine looking women. What does she mean, 'Found a man?' Michael Dame- Care to further 'found a man' for me... They walk inside, as Michael holds the door for her. The diner is very nice inside. Small, but elegant, and clean. They take the liberty to sit where they want, as nobody is in the place, really. Gloria- Ahh... Michael, I met a man named John. He's very nice, and kind, and sweet, and.... Michael Dame- Right... I bet he's perfect. Now, onto business. Gloria- What's your problem tonight, Michael? Michael says nothing in return. He unwrapps his table cloth, and puts it on his lap, as suddenly he thinks of something. Something that he wants Gloria to think would be the last thing Michael would ever do..... Michael Dame- Don't think I have a 'thing' for you Gloria... Gloria- Right, I would never. Gloria fluffs her head back, and flips her hair in a very elegant way. She obviously thinks Michael has a thing for her. Gloria- No, Mike, I would never... (She rolls her eyes as she continues to unwrapp her napkin) Michael Dame- So, I assume you've been with this John guy since Saturday. You didn't even get a chance to say how I wrestled. As soon as my name was announced victorious, you were on your way out. Very strange, I'd say so... Gloria- Ohhh... I meant to tell you about that. I got a nosebleed right before you one, and it was dripping all over the place. I knew you would understand, and especially since I saw you hit the Damer Kick. Wow...it was very impressive. Michael Dame- I'm happy that I still got it. I mean, I knew I would, but it's still a relieving feeling to execute the way I did, especially coming out with the win. Gloria- Yeah, you wrestled amazingly. I was very impressed. I'm going to see about getting you some real shots in the next weeks.. I have a meeting with Jane this week. Michael Dame- Well, lets play it by year. I'm scheduled for a tag match this week, facing Hostyle, and Bones again. Not to be cocky, But I feel this one is in the bag. Just a matter of a week. If I pick up a win over Bones, then it will be a ratings loss to book him against me again, so we can forget about him. Gloria- Good attitude, Michael. But let me ask you, what do you think of this whole Bones kidnapping situation? You know, the kid, and what he had to say when he came out to the ring. Michael gulps very loud, from the water he was drinking. He then slams the glass down quite hard. Gloria- Something wrong, Michael? Michael Dame- (stuttering).. Nothing...nothing at all. Why do you ask? Gloria- (giving a stern face)Well are you going to answer my question? Michael Dame- What's your problem? Gloria- What do you mean, what's my problem? You're the one stuttering, and slamming your glass down when I asked you about the kidnapping. You're lucky that glass didn't break into a thousand pieces. Michael Dame- What?! Get off my back... It's not like I took the kid..! (Michael looks away) Gloria- Who ever said, you took the kid... Jesus.. Michael. Pull yourself together, and call me when you're ready to talk...I'm leaving.. Michael Dame- Fine then...at least I won't have to deal..... Gloria- (Cutting Dame off) Oh give it a rest... See ya Saturday.... Gloria gets up and leaves. When the door hits on her way out, Michael stands up and looks around. Michael Dame- (outraged) I'm leaving this dump of a place, because I can't get any fucking service. Michael obviously quite pissed about what just happens gets up and leaves the diner. Michael Dame- (talking to himself) I'll get through this... It's nothing. Camera Fades to black
