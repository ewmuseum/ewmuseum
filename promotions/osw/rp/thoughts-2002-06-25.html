Thoughts...

25 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/25/02 We see Tom Walczak walking down the street. He is proudly carrying the OSW World Championship Belt on his shoulder. A grin appears on his face from cheek to cheek. He walks into the nearest bar. Walczak proceeds to walk towards the bartender. “What can I get you?” “Do you have any World Champion Discounts? HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!!” “No sorry we don’t.” “It was a joke. Well anyway, I will have a pitcher of beer.” “You got it.” The bartender goes to get the beer ready and Walczak is left there to think some things over… [i] Wow! I cannot believe what is happening to me. I am finally the champion. As I told Doug Troy though, I knew that this would happen. I knew that that chump of a champ couldn’t match my superior skill, and it was only a matter of time before the rest of the world knew as well. Now, I was also delighted with the Red Army’s success… For the most part. You see Michel Krieg lost, and that is unacceptable, considering the consequences it has for Comrade Stanislav. He should have destroyed that Mexican, but instead not only does he lose the match, but his pride as well. You see he fell into the capitalistic mindset, and that corrupted him. Can he make a return? I do not know, but I shall soon see. Then at the end this punk known as Rhett Locke wins the Ascension match. What kind of Garbage is that? He gets destroyed by Graphic Violence, and then gets a chance to face me, your OSW World Champion. Well, no matter because I will destroy him all the same. Though Doug Troy is still hanging around. He wants to regain his fame, and with me standing in his way it is not going to happen… [i] “Here is your beer sir.” “Thank you.” Walczak takes a glass full of beer and chugs it down. He wipes the excess foam off of his mouth. “What will happen? Only time will tell…”
