Diary of a madman

22 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/22/02 Frankie lies, asleep in his bed. The determination of destroying Michael Dame and the rest of OSW has given him a new found comfort. The incoherent thought that has ruled his life for so long has faded away. His head stays clear, clear with thoughts of destruction and violence. Is he a better person? No. Is he more stable? Never. Is he still full of self doubt? Not at all. So with his mind free, he sleeps soundly. But this clear mind has been a misnomer. Introspection and self doubt have faded yes, but they have been replaced by a slowly rising intensity that is unhealthy. He is so intent on the physical destruction of anyone who aggravates him, his mind is becoming clouding with insanity. Although hidden, his insanity grows exponentially. The veil of aggressive wrestling has hidden it from the public and his opponents. But as he sleeps, and more importantly, dreams, this insanity is becoming more apparent. The actual sights and sounds of said dreams may never be known, but his chosen words have been put down on paper, in this manner. Frankie jerks awake from his apparently 'calming' slumber. He lays in a mass of sweaty sheets. His eyes jump from objects in the room quickly, scanning for whatever personal demon was haunting him. As his eyes scan quickly and in a panic, reality creeps back. Breathing heavily, Frankie reaches over and grabs his homemade diary. He opens it up, grabs a pen, and starts putting thoughts down in ink... Bear witness, As I exercise my exorcism, The evil that lurks within, The sin, the terrorism, Possessed by evil spirits, Voices from the dead, I come forth to the mainstream, With a head, full of dread. I've been examined, Ever since I was seamen. They took the sonogram, And seen the image, of a demon. At birth nurses, surrounded me with needles, And drugged me all up, with tha diseases of evil. Look deep into my eyes, You see visions of death. Possessed, by homicide, Is what I am obsessed. The cause of death is unknown to the cops, Cause when I kill em, I'm not one element to adopt. First I'll assassinate 'em, Then I'll cremate 'em, And take all of his fuckin ashes and evaporate 'em. Creep through the graveyard, Lookin' for ya tombstone, Dig up ya skeleton, And stomp all of ya fuckin' bones. Get me in the ring nigga I ain't tryin to hear it, Stomp, stomp, stomp I'll give ya ass a holy spirit. The year 84, November, day 10, Watching the murder of my parents, what a sin. I realize, my ideas, are born, Of 400 years, of blood, sweat, and tears. I saw the torture, Brutal murder of my father, So my brain, became stained, By the horror. I'm havin' reoccurring nightmares, Of being soakin' wet, strapped down to an electric chair, I get tackled, by the demons of my past, They broke me down, 'til I thought I couldn't last. They gave me nothing to eat, For two weeks, Then they sowed by eyelids open, So I couldn't sleep. Ahhhhhhhh, I cry, as the blood drips inside, Of my eyes, refusing to die. Visions of hell, Tormenting my face, So I chew my fuckin' arm off, And make an escape. Frankie puts the pen and pad down. His breathing has slowed down, the sweating has stopped. He sighs heavily as he wipes his brow clean, and lies back down. Pondering whether or not he should go back to this place of dreams, but as his mind contemplates, he drifts to sleep... fade...
