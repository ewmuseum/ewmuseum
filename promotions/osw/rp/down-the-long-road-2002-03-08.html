Down The Long Road

8 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/08/02 The following is the thoughts and sights of Scott Naket. When referred to I, I stands for Scott Naket. Just trying something new. *********************************** Going down the long and curve filled road of life, I have faced many challenges. People always step in front of me, trying to hold me down. My job, and goal, is to always get by them. From using words, to physical action, I try to make a commitment that I won't stop until they are finally out of my sight, and I've gone long past them. That's my case with Clay Vendetta and Mike Marvelous, as I just can't seem to get by them. But this Sunday, oh yes, this Sunday will be... 'Wow, look at the size of that deer!'. Ah yes, Todd, the bum that's traveling in the car with me. I still haven't figured out why I brought him along with me, but I think I could use the company. I mean, looking at this guy makes me appreciate more of what I have myself. He's dressed in gray pants, holes all around them. A gray jacket, and a pretty plain looking hat, with a sports team that probably folded back in the 80's. This guy barely has clothes, and I have my own tee shirt. 'Having fun yet Todd?' I asked him, as he had his head pressed up against the glass of the window beside him. He turned and faced me, trying to keep his eyes on the road beside him. 'This is the most fun I've had since I went shopping cart ridin'' he responded, a twinkle in his eye. Turning back to face the long road, we were driving down one of those long highways that now seemed to go on forever. At least it isn't one of those plain roads like Route 66. I've got stuff to look at, and hell, a tree makes Todd's mouth drop. The poor sucker, never been really entertained in his life. I wonder if he likes strippers? Nah, I'll just stay away from that, who knows what he'll do. 'So, where are you taking me?' Todd asked, as he continued to stare at the afternoon scenery outside. I looked over and pointed to a piece of paper that somehow managed to stay on the dashboard without flying off. 'Pick up that piece of paper and look at it.' I told an excited Todd. For the first time since he stepped into the car, Todd actually took both eyes off the road to the side of the car, and grabbed the piece of paper. The paper actually was a magazine kind of paper, almost as if it was ripped straight from one perfectly. Todd opened it up, and low and behold, he was looking right at the card for OSW's first pay-per-view, Crowning Glory. He looked at the card, complete with pictures, and then looked right back over at me. Taking another look at the card, he then looked right back over at me. I smiled and looked at him. 'Yeah,' I started, 'that's me in the picture.' I said with a grin. Todd just looked back at the piece of paper and smiled. 'You mean you're taking me to the fights?' he asked, the most enthusiasm in his voice since he last got drunk. I won't get into when that was, cause no one really wants to know. He then looked back at me, and laughed. 'So, your a fighter!?' he asked me. 'Yup,' I replied, as I quickly glanced over my shoulder to make sure that light I saw in my mirror wasn't a car. 'But you're fighting two other men,' Todd replied, 'how does that work?' Todd asked, as I sensed a bit of confusion in his voice. 'Well, what do you mean?' I asked him back, not wondering what direction he was coming from. 'Well, aren't you supposed to just go one on one in a fight?' Todd asked, wondering what my response would be. 'What, are you talking about boxing?' I replied with another question of my own. 'Yes.' Todd replied, looking at me waiting for a response. I laughed, but made sure to keep my eyes on the road. 'Well, this isn't exactly boxing,' I replied, as Todd looked even more confused. 'Well,' Todd said right after my sentence, 'if it isn't boxing, then what is it?' he asked. I grinned, 'Well,' I responded, 'the object is still to beat the other men. However, you can do other things then just throw punches. You can kick, you can pick the guy up, you can throw him, perform moves, whatever.' Todd nodded his head, finally getting where I was coming from. He then looked back at the paper, but then got that 'I have a question' sort of look in his face. He turned back to me, 'Does that mean you can bite then?' he asked me, as I responded with a laugh. 'Yes,' I said, 'but biting is for girls, and only someone like Mike Marvelous would do something like that.' Todd nods, and looks down at the piece of paper again. 'Wait, so your facing the biter on Sunday?' Todd asked me, as I couldn't help but smirk at that comment. 'Yeah, I guess I am.' I responded, as Todd just nodded as I tried my best to wipe that huge grin off my face. 'Hold up a second Scotty,' Todd asked again, looking confused, 'if you can bite, does that mean you can punch someone in the goods?' he asked. Again, I couldn't help but laugh at this comment. 'Well, that you can't do,' I responded, 'just because punching a man there is wrong. However, lots of people do it when the referee isn't looking. It's a 'Classic' move that Clay Vendetta uses all the time to gain the advantage.' Todd just turns his head again, and stares down at the paper, 'I see.' he responds. 'So you're fighting the biter and the low blow expert at the same time on Sunday? I'd say the odds are stacked up against you' Todd proclaims, which makes me shake my head. 'Not really,' I said, with another grin forming on my face. 'It's gonna be hard for them, because they don't realize that Sunday, this is do or die for me. I'm gonna give them all I have, and they'll think it'll just be another one of my 'show up because I have to' shmuck tricks.'. Todd just nods, and starts to look at the rest of the card. I focused on the long road ahead, which still just appears to be going on, and on, and on. Gotta get to North Carolina, but for the sake of it, I'm gonna stop by Roanoke first. That's where I am now, the highway to Roanoke. Man I need some food, but I'm scared Todd will scrape my savings clean. I just kept driving down the road, and Todd soon found his way back to his post...looking outside the side window at the scenery. Todd though wasn't quick to find his way back to the card sheet for Crowning Glory. He looked down at the rest of the matches, and thankfully he didn't ask me about the tag team matches, cause we all know how hard that would have been killer for me to explain to a simple mind like his. He did though point out one thing that made me think. 'Hey Scott,' he asked, with that tone of questioning in his voice again, 'why aren't you in the title match?' he asked. I sighed, and gripped the wheel hard, as I always hate being asked about a title situation. It's just something I never really wanna talk about until I think I've deserved to be there, or until I actually have one myself. I turned, and faced him, and gave him the best answer off the top of my head. 'Well,' I began, taking one hand off the wheel to scratch my forehead. 'S*it!' I yelled, as I completely forgot about my scar from those damn punk kids and that buffoon of a 7-11 clerk. Shaking off the sudden pain to the forehead, I pressed my hand back onto the wheel, and continued where I left off. 'I gotta work my way there first. Once I prove to everyone that I'm ready for it, eventually they'll give me the chance to be named that champion. But not now.' I said, which kind of was the truth, but I knew I had to work harder to get there first. 'Is it because you aren't good enough?' Todd asked me, as I held onto the steering wheel tighter to resist the urge to slap the guy in the back of the head. 'No,' I replied, trying to stay calm 'the bookers, or Ed, just don't feel I'm ready to be thrown up there. Hey, Todd, I don't care. I just take the situation one match at a time. And right now, the World Title isn't on my mind. My mind is focused on making sure that Clay Vendetta and Mike Marvelous don't get there first.' Todd gave a nod, and seemed to agree with everything that I had to say. I just continued to drive, as I saw a diner sign approaching me. What the hell I thought, it's not like the guy has a bottomless pit. 'Say Todd,' I asked him, 'how about some food?'. Boy, did I really need to ask him that. His eyes lit up, his body seemed to be full of boundless energy, and his mouth opened right away. 'BOOYA!' he proclaimed, as he was getting ready to take off his seat beat, open that car door and bolt to try and get the best seats in the house. A sad picture I thought, but hey, sometimes when your on the road, you just need a little company to let you feel better about yourself.
