Spinning, spinning, spinning... Whole room is spinning... Back in Santa Monica, back in my long deserted apartment, the landlord musta installed a special device underneath my bed, because the room is spinning. My knee is swollen like a nine-month pregnant chick, and I am soooo doped up. I've got more shit in my bloodstream than most Colombian Drug Lords have in...wherever they store their drugs. They gotta have like, a warehouse full of that shit, right? Right. Christ. My skin is clammy, moist, sweaty, but at least I don't feel anything. I'm wrapped up in a sheet, the ceiling fan and air conditioner are both going full-blast, so much that I feel like a side of beef in a meat cooler. I don't feel the bruises, the deep pain that seems to claw its way to the surface of my skin with every slam to the canvas. I feel... I feel... Well, I know what I DON'T feel like. I tried jerking off earlier today, but with all the shit I'm on, Mister Happy doesn't want to do anything. Paris is away... Not gone... Just...away. The last thing I remember of her is the hotel room...was it in Calgary? Or Manitoba? I don't remember. She could still be there for all I know. Is that bad, when you can't remember where you left a psycho? In one of my rare lucid moments, right before the match on Saturday, I called in on the Malice Show, and Duke was running his mouth off at me. I was so doped up, I could barely talk. I think I was talking to the Phantom Booker...but he didn't sound pissed that I fucked his wife... Did that even happen? Shit, I can barely remember most of Saturday. Most vivid thing that I DO remember is jumping Hawk in the back, and Ivan and Tom and Seth and Trevor driving me to the ER. Porter was yelling at me... Christ... I'm so fucked up right now. Why doesn't Duke like me? Why do people hate me so much? What the hell did I ever do to them? Ooooo... trails. I struggle to stand up, but the bed evidently really likes me, and it comes up to body-block me, and I stick to it. Like I was wrestling with a tar baby. I crawl off again, naked in the cool air of the apartment, my wang hanging out, my hair hanging in my face, and I crawl out of the room, high as a muthafucking kite. I crawl on the cream colored carpet out to the living room, and Paris is there, leaning on the arm-rest of the couch. I up-end myself and roll over on the ground, breathing hard, my legs and arms numb. She glides across the carpet, not making a sound, and gets on her hands and knees, leaning over me, kissing me. I close my eyes, and she's gone. I'm gasping. Heartbeat...really loud... God, it's so hot in here. So...hot... I roll over and vomit what little there was in my stomach. The hard white pills that taste like chalk come up, half-digested, little curls of white intertwined with my saliva. The orangish taint of the Sunny Delight I took to wash them down with is all over my nice cream colored carpet. The smell... I can't smell. I can only gag and heave, convulse and retch. Heave...Ho. Another load of vomit and bile drenches the pile of the carpet, and I know once I get better, I'm gonna have to get this cleaned....Heave....Ho.... More vomit, and its splattering all over, everywhere, and I can't raise myself up by my arms anymore. I fall into a puddle of my own puke. Writhing in it, another heave comes, and its like a fishing hook in my gut, being ripped out by a high strength line from a deep sea fishing boat. Nothing but saliva, mucus, and bile comes out, except for my bowel muscles spasming, and I shit myself matter-of-factly. I'm a mess. Too much mixing the stuff. Stuff I get from the guys in the locker room. Too much intermingling with the stuff I get from this hospital, that hospital, the local pharmacy. I need to call a hospital... Or the cleaning lady. Someone to clean me. I'm a mess... It hurts so much all of the time, I have to keep piling on the pills, just so I can get through another match. So I can drive the hundreds of miles to the next show without cramping up. So I can work out to keep buff for the show. Just so that paycheck keeps coming in. Just so... Puke. Cry. Repeat. Fuck! How the fuck did I ever get this fucking low? Why am I shitting and puking on the floor of my apartment? Why, why the fuck am I doing this to myself... I retch again. Get it all out. Purge yourself. Live through the pain. Get some time off and get your shit together. Purge.
