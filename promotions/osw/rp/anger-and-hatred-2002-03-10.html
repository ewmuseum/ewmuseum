Anger and Hatred

10 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/10/02 The sun has set long ago. There are no stars in the sky tonight. The only thing that lights city are the streetlamps. Despite the late time of day, the city is still full of life. The people in the streets below are scurrying about like the drones of an ant colony. High a top a city building, there is a massive figure standing, watching. He watches all of the people like the keeper of an ant farm. The silence on top of the building is broken by an unseen figure. 'What the hell are you doing up here, Cain? Heh-Heh-Heh.' The massive figure is none other than Azrael Cain. He is wearing his flowing black and tattered robe. His long black hair is down and blowing in the wind. His small companion, Caleb, appears from behind Cain. Cain's eyes do not move from the city's activity. He stares transfixed on the bustling people. There is something haunting Cain's thoughts. The silence has returned. The only thing that can be heard are the honking of horns, the sounds of people screaming, and the occasional gunshot. The small hobo tries to get an answer from his massive friend again. 'Ahem...I said what the hell are you doing up here? Heh-Heh. Big guy must be getting hard of hearing. Heh-Heh-Heh.' Cain's eyes squint as Caleb's mere presence is disturbing his thoughts. After a long pause, Cain finally responds. 'These mortals are so peculiar. I don't understand this thing that they call anger. I know anger and hatred. Those are two things that I am very familiar with, but the definition of anger as they know and use it are lost to me.' Caleb begins to scratch his balding head before he answers. 'What are you talking about? Heh-Heh. I hate it when Pay Per View time comes around. Heh. You always get darker and more peculiar then ever. Heh-Heh. I can never follow you when you get like this. Heh-Heh.' Cain doesn't acknowledge his manager's comment again and continues on. 'Anger as I know it is different then theirs. I look out and I watch the way they behave. I see what it is that makes them angry. What causes them to hate one another. It is their own petty pride that causes them to become enraged. And the one thing that angers them the most is when someone challenges their credibility. And it is that one thing that drives these people to lash out. They don't know the meaning of true anger and hatred.' Caleb rolls his eyes and sighs loudly to display his annoyance with this topic of conversation. Cain continues to ignore the actions of his small friend as he speaks. 'These damn creatures commit violent acts, hurt one another and even start wars all because of their pride and vanity. No one fights for worthy causes anymore. They go to war for glory and pride. They are willing to wipe out entire civilizations in order to prove that they live in a better society. Where is the honor in that? They would rather hurt someone that has more money then they do. And for what? Jealousy!' Cain is noticeably becoming more and more aggravated as he talks about this. 'They get angry when someone mocks their clothing or the amount of money that they make. And why? Because they feel that their person is being attacked. These people are disgusting. They know nothing of true anger and hatred.' Caleb groans from the attitude that his friend is portraying. 'You need to get your damn head on straight. Heh-Heh. You are fighting for the tag team titles this week. Heh-Heh. I swear. I will never understand what goes through head sometimes. Heh-Heh-Heh.' Cain's teeth grind as he snarls to answer his friend's comment. 'That is exactly what I am talking about. These wretched people don't know of true anger. They think that I am fighting those immature pups this week because I want to wear a piece of gold around my waste. Those mindless masses believe that a title of champion is what drives me.' Cain starts to move for the first time since this conversation began. 'That is what makes these people weak. They want nothing more than fame and recognition. They fight for the glory. The cheers of the fans are what drive them to fight. They only know anger when someone makes them look bad in front of the fans.' Caleb sits down on the roof of the building as he realizes that he is going to be there for a while listening to his comrades ranting. Caleb thinks for a moment as Cain is still ranting. He finally decides to have fun with the massive warrior and starts to poke at the beast's short fuse. 'So what is it that makes you so damn different? Heh-Heh-Heh.' Cain spins around with a smug look on his face. He looks at Cain for a moment before he answers. 'Who do you think you're talking to? Do you honestly believe that you can get my temper going just like one of those pathetic mortals. I am the greatest warrior that ever fought in any of the great wars. I was once the Master's greatest Azrael. Civil Disobedience are nothing more than a couple of pups brought in from a spoon fed family. They are nothing in my eyes. They know nothing of what anger really is. They know nothing of real hatred.' Caleb smiles revealing his vacant gums. He starts to chuckle slightly before poking further at Cain's temperament. 'And what is the anger that drives you? Heh-Heh. What is it that makes your anger swell up inside? Heh-Heh-Heh.' Cain is standing high over the sitting hobo now. The beast kneels down so that he is level with the small hobo now. 'I thought you would know what made me tick by now? The root of all my anger comes from the wrong that was committed to me so many centuries ago by the Creator. That is true anger and hatred. I loathe him and all of his creations for what they did to me. Their very existence is what makes my anger swell. I fight because I cannot stand to see them progress any further. My anger grows every time I think about the favoritism that the Creator unjustly gave to my brother. I don't do battle so that I can have a title in some sort of pathetic form of entertainment. I do battle to destroy the hopes and dreams of these infidels. I want to destroy everything that is good.' Cain rises back up to his feet. Every muscle in his body is tense as his temper is beginning to get the best of him. His massive muscles can be seen rippling underneath his thick tattered cloak. Caleb starts to chuckle to himself. Caleb smiles and decides to continue poking at the Fallen Angel. 'Sorry I didn't know all of that. Heh-Heh. I mean it's not like you haven't changed at all since you were released from the Ninth Gate. Heh-Heh. How was I to know that you are even more pissed off then you were before you were captured? Heh-Heh-Heh.' Cain turns to his small manager before he speaks. After a moment of collecting himself, Cain finally starts to address the hobo. 'Listen to me. When I fight Civil Disobedience this week in front of all those people, I am not going to fight for the gold. I am fighting. This time I am going to destroy those two boys and end their constant nuisance. This week, in front of all those mindless people that will be watching across this nation, Heresian Bloodlust will crush Civil Disobedience underneath our boots like the insects that thy are. This is not about the gold. This is all about destroying the dreams of those two fools that are Civil Disobedience.' Cain turns and begins to exit from the top of the building. Caleb laughs to himself. Through Caleb's laughter he let's one of his thoughts go. 'I love getting him worked up before big matches. Heh-Heh. It's so easy! Heh-Heh-Heh.' Caleb then begins to waddle after the beast that is Cain who has already vanished again into the shadows of the street.
