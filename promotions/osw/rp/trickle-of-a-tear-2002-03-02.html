Trickle of a Tear

2 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/2/02 Introduction: A lot has happened since the last time you heard from Michael. He was meeting with his long time manager, Gloria, at a local diner, to discuss his ongoings of OSW. Things led to another, and Gloria stormed out of the diner, furious. Now, we find ourselves 1 day before SLAM! and have to wonder? Will Gloria be at SLAM! to back Michael or a better question, will Michael be there? You have to ask yourself both questions, because not just the conflict of Michael and Gloria is the only thing that has people talking of Michael. He has been indirectly accused of kidnapping Frankie Bones younger 'student' that he mentored. Does Michael even know of the accusations? If he does, how does he feel about it? Both questions only have answers if one conclusion can actually be drawn...that he did it. Michael Dame's Hotel Room- Virginia The hall are empty in the royalty feeling hotel, The Hilton of Virgina. The time ticks past 11pm on the cold Winter Friday. Suddenly you hear a clanking in the far distance, and a shadow approaching closer. Then it's clear the shadow makes out Gloria, Michael Dame's manager. She is wearing a leather jacket, and holding a clipboard with some files on top of it against her chest. She walks rather quickly as she suddenly drops all articles in her hands. They fall all over the place. 'Ugh...' She exclaims as she reaches down to begin to gather everything.. 'You better be grateful I'm coming to patch things up before your big match tomorrow, Michael...Just hope you do..' She explains to herself, trying to avoid her anger. She stands back up, and walks to the end of the hall which reads 67. She looks down at her files and clipboard, but apperantly she didn't pick them back up in the order they were, so the number to Michael Hotel was lost in the pile.. 'God damn' .. She exclaims as she bends down to put everything on the ground. 'Who's out there?' Is heard from inside the end hotel room, number 67. 'Michael, is that you' Gloria answers in reply as she picks up her files once again. 'Yes, it is....Gloria?' Michael asks with a monotone voice. 'Yes Michael, it's me. Open the door. I'm sorry about the other night..' Gloria says sincerly. A pause from inside the room. Then you hear something fall. '...Sure, Gloria. Just wait a minute..' 'Com'on Michael, just open..' Annoyed by the delay...'What do you have a girl in there? It's fine, if you do. I've seen it all before...well, not literally..' She giggles to herself and whispers...' Well, actually one time, when you got done wrestling and took that immediate show...ahaha.. That was funny..' The door swings open. Michael stands fully dressed. Jeans, sweatshirt, and a hat on. 'Are you going somewhere, Michael?' Gloria asks confused, obviously wondering why he is fully dressed at 11pm. 'No, Gloria, why do you ask me...Oh, my cloths? No, I am not going anywhere. Just haven't..(Dame takes a deep gulp)..Gotten undressed yet. So, what's up?' Michael asks as he doesn't budge to let Gloria in. 'Ummm... nothing much, just wanted to make sure everything was cool for tomorrow night...well, are you going to let me in?' Gloria looks straight into his eyes. 'Well, actually I'm about to hit the sack. Sorry, we didn't have longer to talk. See ya tomorrow Gloria..' Michael backs away from the door as he begins to shut it. Gloria is shocked. She holds her arm out to stop the door from being closed. 'Michael, what the hell has gotten into you? The other night at the diner, then that next morning they accuse you of being in Brooklyn, you haven't answered any of my calls, and now this? What the hell is up.' She exasperates. Michael violently throws the door open, and it lands against the wall. 'What the fuck is wrong with me? I should be asking you the same question... YOU'RE the problem here, Gloria, you're becoming more of a neusence then a help. I have a lot on my mind, and I don't need to deal with this... So, like I said, I'll see you tomorrow. Good night' He begins to shut the door. In a low, softtone voice... 'Michael, please. Stop..Talk to me. You can tell me anything. Have you been watching the news? Seen the OSW website. All the fingers are being pointed at you...' She is cut off... 'What..What are you talking about?' Dame avoids eye contact, as he takes his hat off, and runs his hand through his hair. 'The kidnapping, Michael. Everyone is saying YOU took Frankie Bones friend. The kid he was mentoring. Everyone is saying it was YOU!'.. She says in a calm and collected voice. Stressing key parts. Dame stands still, holding his hat in his hand. Within seconds his face turns bright red. He looks up into Gloria's eyes with a develish look and slams his hat onto the ground. It startles Gloria as far as to step back a few. 'Are you kidding me? Gloria? You're coming here, accusing me that I'm a kidnapper, just because I haven't been myself the past week. Just because I have a few extra things on my mind. This, is bullshit! I thought that you would have enough common desecentcy to think I am a man of honor, and respect. To think of my in the position of kidnapping a child, when right now, my kids have been 'kidnapped' from me. I think...(screaming)...YOU WOULD THINK! He violently slams the door into the face of Gloria. She just stands there, as a tear trickles down her face. Crying and talking to herself she says.. 'No, Michael, You just misinterpretted me. I just want to know........know, what you think. (pauses).. Like you said, See ya Saturday.' She turns and walks down the hallway as the picture fades to black.
