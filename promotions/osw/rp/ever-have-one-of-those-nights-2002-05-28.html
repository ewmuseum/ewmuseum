Ever Have One of Those Nights?

28 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/28/02 “GET THE TABLES!” If that quote wasn’t in use by another legendary tag team, well, it would fit Da Westsidaz PERFECTLY. Da Westsidaz are very comfortable using tables now and again in matches, so this isn’t going to be foreign ground for the two. Then again, anything with the word “TAG” in the match isn’t foreign ground for these two men. The scene changes from the clip to a dark hotel room somewhere in Canada. Riptah sits in the center of the room at the end of a single bed. He looks at the floor, scratching the back of his head, mumbling to himself. “The nerve. They challenge us?? No, we should have challenged them. The fucking nerve. Steal my girlfriend?! The nerve.” He mumbles, as if trying to prove something to himself. The big man stands up and makes his way to the washroom. He removes his blood-stained wifebeater and throws it onto the floor, inspecting his injuries. The past weekend was comparable to hell on earth, as the last few experiences have appeared to break skin in multiple places, as Riptah scratches at the not-quite closed wounds. The man has survived not only a thirty-feet fall to the Cell, not a day later he was competing in one of his most hellacious matches ever, a sixty-minute Ironman match that broke every limit Da Westsidaz’ ever have broken. “No, they don’t do shit like that to the best tag team in wrestling. This Saturday, there is going to be hell to pay. Da Westsidaz have never took shit lying down. Heresian Bloodlust and Civil Disobedience are going to learn what it’s like to mess with us. Ascension isn’t going to be a picnic.” He yawns tiredly, trying to just take his shower and rest. He runs the water and splashes it onto his face, dried blood coming off in crusty chunks. Riptah hoarsely coughs and reaches into the shower, turning it on. Mist fills the room, but he is interrupted by his cell-phone going off. “Mutha-fucker. I frickin’ stink, can’t I just take a shower.” He moans angrily, leaving the shower unattended. Riptah kicks the door lightly out of his way and grabs his gym bag at the end of the bed. He pulls the cell phone out from a maze of towels and paraphernalia. Rain starts to hit against the window as the aching Riptah answers the phone. “Yo.” Muffled talking can be heard through the line, but no, one voice can be recognized. An irritated look glazes over Riptah’s face. He shakes his head and hangs up the phone with no warning as the rain starts to come down harder than ever. “Table match? I’ve just been through a war and this week I am booked for another one. I guess the brass is trying to see if I can still do it or something. Fuck. I am not going to be able to get a break this year.” He shakes his head, whipping sweat from raw exhaustion from his forehead. A white scar rips through the sky, followed by a wicked crackle. Riptah isn’t phased by the lightning and tosses his gym bag to the side. He returns to the washroom and kicks his socks off quickly. He unzips his pants, but is interrupted yet again by the phone. He nonchalantly zips back up his pants. “This better be fucking good.” He goes through the routine of finding his cell-phone again as yet another white vein rips through the skyline, visible from the window. The single bulb in the bedroom flickers out, just adding to the man’s frustration. “Riptah here.” “Are you interested in purchasing a new central vacuum service?” squeaks the voice on the other end of the telephone. Riptah’s face slowly turns from being annoyed to anger. “I’m busy. Why don’t you give me your home number so I can call and conveniently irritate you at your home?” He hangs up post haste. He turns to head back into the washroom, mumbling something about Civil Disobedience when a familiar ringer goes off for a third time. The phone however, is never answered. It meets the wall of the hotel room with a swift throw, shattering in a multitude of pieces. Only thunder can be heard, until the sound of the shower beating against Riptah’s body is heard. “What a mother-fucking day.”
