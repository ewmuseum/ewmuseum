The Cure-All

Soft piano music hits as a black 69 Baldwin Motion Chevrolet Camaro rolls down a country road. Inside, we see Sebastian Toomes, dressed in a black turtleneck and black jeans, a somber expression on his face. Noticeably absent is Jane Reagan. Being the owner extends to having the money to fly, whereas the OSW World Champ drives. The reason for his look of despondency? Could it be from the fact that he has a handicap match next Slam? Against Timo Bolamba and the NTICW Superstar, Aimz? No. Christ, if you’ve been reading Switchblade promos you know the difference between a kayfabe one and a genuine one. This is one of the genuine ones. The reason for his pained expression is lying on the seat next to him, on top of his OSW World Title. He could have flown along with Jane, for obvious reasons, but once he got this letter in the mail, he decided it would be better to stew on the implications, give him time to think without Jane’s presence to give the illusion that things are alright. Because we often do that to ourselves. We lie to ourselves and tell ourselves that things are going to be okay. In actuality, we’re all hanging on by a thread, and that might be sour grapes or bitterness talking, but it’s all too often the truth. We create webs of comfort, keeping out the cold harsh reality. And what you see on television isn’t necessarily kosher, either. Could it be the truth? Could it not be? Well, pussyfooting aside, I think we can all safely assess what’s going on. The reality of the situation is that Sebastian got a letter from his ex the other day. Not ex-wife, but with nearly the same impact. For those of you who don’t know, or never followed before, Toomes once had a girl who he loved dearly, but his calling to the world of fantasy that is pro-wrestling, strained and finally snapped that relationship. It all ended in a dorm room on the campus of UCLA, and he disappeared onto the indy wrestling circuit for a couple years before turning up in Cyberspace Championship Wrestling. Since then, Sebastian has had a very low opinion of women. Untrustworthy, lying, cheating, backstabbing bitches. And every successive women merely turned into a convenient scapegoat for his insecurities and his pre-conceived notions of how women fucked him over in the past, so much that every woman, regardless of her own individual merits, fulfilled the prophecy that Toomes carried into every relationship that he got himself into. She had cut him deeply, leaving an indelible mark on him, a scar that was so prominent that it colored his perceptions to such a degree that he always found himself on the outs, feeling cold and alone. Yes, pussy can be warm, but it’ll never maintain the heat. You can fuck all the women you want, it’ll never get that heart that’s been flash-frozen to thaw. Sex didn’t solve anything, it just fulfilled a biological imperative to get your dick wet, to continue the species, even if you sprayed it on her face… And friendship is nice, but then that overbearing ogre of an impulse, the impulse to fuck, gets in the way and ruins everything. And how can you truly say you’re friends with somebody if you want to fuck them? Or even worst, they rejected your romantic advances, and just wanted to stay…’friends’… Yeah, that’s as friendly as driving a used syringe into a nun’s eye socket. Sebastian has been alone for a couple years now, and he hasn’t had sex in months. Even the drive to fuck had left him, the notion that you’ve got to act like a goddamn peacock and go out trying to attract some dippy cunt who’s only going to destroy your self-esteem in one way or another… The concept just seemed futile, and he’s much rather jerk off instead. Much better than risking emotional damage on a psyche that’s taken too many ‘bumps’ already… Eventually, ‘She’ faded out of conscious thought, and only the most fragile of memories remained… Doesn’t mean she wasn’t still there, running amok, causing damage, but she wasn’t making him cry before he went to sleep at night. And to tell the truth, she didn’t do something all that bad… She just dumped him, moved onto greener pastures. Nothing big, right? Big enough for Toomes to be so messed up to this day. He even went as far as to hire an actress to play the part of his ex, so he could do a cathartic interview segment before he left CCW, hitting her in the head with a steel chair, and giving her new husband an Osaka Street Cutter. Just to try and work out the issues… Shit, the boy doesn’t just have issues, he’s got digests… It sucked being alone, but what else can you be when you feel that everyone around you in going to just betray you and leave you? Toomes stops the car on the side of the highway, the engine rumbling in the early morning silence. Sighing to himself, he opens the envelope again for what seems the umpteenth million time, and unfolds the perfectly creased notebook paper. The blue ink reveals tight cursive script, and Toomes clears his throat as he reads. A semi thunders past, shaking the car slightly as he reads, the harsh sunlight filtering out of the cloudy Florida sky. Finally, he folds it back into it’s original configuration, and stuffs it back inside the envelope. His eyes glitter with the onslaught of tears… And then he sees his CD book. Sniffling, he picks up the hefty leatherbound CD book, and unzips it. A few loose CDS slide out, but Sebastian doesn’t care. He pulls out WWF: The music Volume 2… “Yes… It’s the only way.” He mutters as he puts it in the CD player. He goes to the last track. He opens the door and turns up the volume on the stereo. “OOOH, OOOH, SHAWN!” The song begins. “I think I’m cute! I know I’m sexy!” Toomes starts swaggering along the shoulder of the road. “I got the looks! That drive the girls wild! I got the moves! That really move em! I send chills, up and down their spines!” Toomes flexes like Shawn, grinning like an idiot. “I’m just a sexy boy!” Sexy booo oy! “I’m not your boy toy~!” Booooo ooooy tooooo-oy! “I’m just a sexy boy!” Sexy booooo-oy! “I’m not your boy toy!” Booooo-oy toy! Toomes swaggers over to the median, and an alligator looks at him, dumbfounded. “I make em hot! I make em shiver! Their knees get weak whenever I’m around!” Toomes seems the alligator and pumps his hips in the alligator’s direction. “They seem me walk! They hear me talk! I make em feel like they’re on Cloud Nine!” The alligator shakes it’s massive head and scampers back into the drainage culvert from whence it came. “I’m just a sexy boy!” Sexy booo-oy! “I’m not your boy toy~!” Booooo ooooy tooooo-oy! “I’m just a sexy boy!” Sexy booooo-oy! “I’m not your boy toy!” Booooo-oy toy! Toomes is nearly knocked over by the blast of air from a passing semi. He looks back towards the Camaro and grins. How could you feel bad when Shawn Michaels is informing us that he’s a sexy boy, and that no matter what you might think or feel, he’s not your boy toy. HBK was the best… Toomes does have a role-model after all… Toomes trots across the asphalt over to the Camaro, where “Sexy Boy” is finishing up on the CD Player. A smile on his face… For after all, how could you stay sad with HBK playing? You simply can’t. Toomes puts the Camaro in gear, and merges into traffic.
