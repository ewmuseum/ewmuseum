Direction. Purpose.

4 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 4, 2002 He hadn’t spoken since Slam. He barely spoke before it. His mind wouldn’t let him interrupt long enough to speak to anyone. Reno merely wandered and thought. He wandered from Winnipeg to Vancouver, and even to somewhere that he didn’t even remember. The alcohol wouldn’t let him. He thought about his life, his meaning, and his place. Reno Davis wasn’t an atheist. He just didn’t like God. God had given him this naturally bright red hair and these freaky eyes. Although now people pay to look like Reno does, throughout his whole childhood people did not. People hit him, people threw things at him, and people broke his bones. His only friend, ZaC, a little monkey, was Reno’s heart and soul. ZaC had never lied to Reno, ZaC had never stabbed him in the back, and ZaC had never let Reno down. Up until last Saturday night, Reno was able to say the same about himself, but then he let ZaC down. He left ZaC in the locker room where that fucking Alexei Ruslan could grab him and hurt him. He allowed himself to be distracted by this and have to helplessly watch as Ivan Stanislav took ZaC and beat the monkey until he almost died. Until Reno’s heart and soul almost died. Reno, however, felt as if his heart had died anyway, no matter if ZaC lived or not. I’ve always wondered what my place in life was, Reno once wrote, What God put me on this earth for. Why he made me look like I do, why he threw so many boundaries in my way. Why he would never let me find any answers. A man I once called my friend, my mentor, my teacher; He once called me God’s ‘Unwanted Angel’. He was right. I believe that God’s placed me on this earth to show people to look further than the outside, and to reach deep down into someone’s heart in order to find the true person. That no matter what someone looks like, they may be one of the best people you’ve ever met. Unfortunately, God’s decision to put me here on July 24, 1980, was a very ignorant move. God never thought that maybe people didn’t care anymore. People don’t even take the time to judge you by your looks anymore, and certainly not your heart. God’s decision to put me on this earth like this has made me grow to hate the man. He’s realized his mistake though, and tried to kill me many times. Once he got me caught in a train track. Another time a gang of kids jumped me at my school. The next time I was stabbed and shot, and that’s when I realized that God was getting serious. Then I decided that I’d show that piece a shit a thing or two. He wants me dead? I’m going to live. No matter what he throws at me, I’m going to dodge it and recover. That is my purpose in life. At least one of them anyway. I’ve failed my second purpose last Saturday night. I failed to protect my best friend ZaC, but I swear that I’ll never fail again. I’m going to destroy Ivan Stanislav for what he’s done. I’ve beaten bigger, I’m beaten better, and I’ve survived through encounters with people more dangerous that he. Ivan Stanislav is going down. The woman I love. I see her and there are so many feelings. I feel confused, I feel inferior, I feel ecstatic. I felt the same way about Tempest. Then Tempest left my life again. I didn’t think rationally around her, I played footage of something I shouldn’t of, and now a man guided by broken love attacks another guided by the need to protect those he loves. The woman I love I hope will never come close to me, as I’ve realized the consequences of coming close to God’s unwanted angel. Of God’s prime target. I’m on the top of God’s list, and God will continuously destroy everything around me until he eventually gets me. The woman I love is... And that’s all Reno wrote. Reno came out of his trance of thoughts and realized where he was. A red pickup truck bolted by Davis on the highway as Reno oriented him. A green sign with white outline read: Vancouver- 3 km. He was back in Vancouver? He might as well check up on those who have suffered the effects of being Reno’s friend. He would head for the hospital.
