An issue every relationship is plauged with: Boyfriend facing girlfriend in an all-out steel cage brawl? Muaha!

27 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 27th, 2002 ((Note: 3am...must...stay...awake. This RP is supposed to be on July 24th, Reno's birthday...ooooh yeeah. Sorry for the late-ness and slack quality, it's been a rough, rushed week.)) ''Why'd you have to ask to be in on THIS match?'' Aimz was sprawled across what looked to be a rather uncomfortable motel matress, her legs dangling over the edge of the bed while ZaC swung off of one, watching Reno Davis, who was pacing the other side of the room, intently. ''Hey,'' He could only muster one real response, ''It gets me a chance to dismember that fucking disgrace to mankind early.'' ''Yeah, but it might end up with you and I against one another.'' Reno stopped for a moment, cocking his head at her. ''So? We've had to fight before.'' ''I know...'' She shifted awkwardly, obviously uncomfortable with the whole idea of fighting Davis again. ''...but that was BEFORE. You know, before.....this...right?'' ''You'll have to be a little more precise than 'this'.'' ''Well...like...us,'' Amy bit her lip, she'd never been one for the whole 'mushy' thing. ''...this whole you and I dealie...'' ''Before we figured out what we felt?'' Davis laughed, walking over to kneel beside where she laid. ''Now that we're together, just think of it as easier to stomach, okay? I mean, we trust one another, right?'' ''Well yeah.'' He rested his chin on the bed right in front of Campbell's face. ''Then what's there to worry about?'' ''It's in a cage, those aren't very forgiving...'' Her expression was grim, but she continued to speak without getting too upset. ''...I mean, that could put one of us in the hospital.'' Reno was looking over her face, noting what seemed to be sincere fear in her normally bright, deep brown eyes. That's when he chose to go a lighter route with the discussion, figuring it would be a much better evening for both if they cheered up a bit. ''Yeah...'' He'd winked, ''But lots of things we do could put either one of us in the hospital, though you never seem to complain. Heh, just perk up about this...we're not going to have to go after each other with bats or anything. Besides, you know I'd make up for any bumps and bruises, right?'' Davis had suceeded in getting her to giggle, at least. ''Right.'' ''Don't worry so much, it's not like some match is going to suddenly turn me against you.'' He smiled, standing up slowly and turning towards the bathroom as he looked back to Aimz, murmering, ''Quick shower...you stay out here this time.'' Campbell smiled mischieviously when he shut the door behind him. ''I intended to...'' LATER When Reno stepped out of the bathroom, a thick towel covering any vital part, water trickling down from his hair to soak the floor beneath him, everything was dark as could be. Turning the lights on didn't reveal anything, either... Oh, wait...except that giant, glittery wrapped box in the corner. Not to mention that it was curently a moving, human-sized giant glittery wrapped box........... Right. ''Amy...'' He laughed aloud into the dim light. ''Come ON...couldn't you have gotten a little more original?'' Hey, at least the giant present reminded him that it, indeed, was July 24th...his 22nd birthday. Either that, or his girlfriend had taken in a ton of some substance during that shower. Either way, he walked towards the box nonchalantly expecting to find the obvious, Aimz either scantily clad or entirely NOT dressed inside but when he opened it, he got a much bigger surprise by way of ZaC jumping out, clinging to his face. ''GAH!'' The little monkey was soon joined by the feeling of a much larger impact, this time it was something clinging to his back, knocking him forward a bit before he could spin it around instinctively, dropping the form of Amy Campbell unceremoniously into a chair. The only part of the picture of Campbell sitting in front of him that Davis hadn't expected disappointed him only slightly: She was still the same naturally gorgeous girl who tried so hard to look un-pretty, but she was that girl WITH clothing on, still wearing her distressed jeans and a plain, tight black t-shirt. She cast him an innocent look, biting her lip while she held a bottle of Colt 45 in hand. ''Happy birthday?'' ''Colt?'' Reno held a crooked grin towards Amy as she spoke in an unusually cheerfull tone. ''Right before 'you know who' stabbed me that night, you gave me a CD and when I asked how you knew I wanted it, I'm pretty sure you said something like 'I remember these things'.'' He smiled. ''Oh yeah?'' ''Yeah...'' Campbell smiled and stuck the bottle between her knees to pat ZaC, who'd hopped from Reno's shoulder to the arm of her chair. ''I just wanted to show that I remember things too.'' ''So now that you've remembered my favorite drink, where's that stereo I asked for?'' Davis chuckled, folding his arms. ''Hey, whoever said I was one for material gift-giving?'' And at that, the two were left to start and finish the wonderfully alcoholic beverage, basically drinking away any worries of the upcoming Slam. ((Note: Oh God, that was awful, wasn't it? Heh...it's currently 3am.))
