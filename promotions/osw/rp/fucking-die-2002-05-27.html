Fucking die.

27 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Monday, May 27, 2002 she was diagnosed on a friday, the kids were almost home, the kids were on their way back home from school, lying face down in the gutter of unaccomplished dreams and broken memories of things to come. 'He's going to die.' Nick said softly. The match was done, Nick had won. But not before the memory of his mother had been execreted upon. Jack Miller had successfully managed to piss off Nick Casey in a way that he had never been pissed off before. He actually feltl like he could have taken Miller's life right there in the ring. For the first time in his career, Nick had actually hated the person he was wrestling against. For the first time in his career, Nick actually wanted to inflict pain for the sheer eans of revenge and hate, rather than just for entertainment purposes. Whereas with people like Ethan Ryan Alexander it was purely for show, now Jack Miller had crossed the line. He had broken the sacred aspect of kayfabe that the members of any federation are supposed to abide by all the time. And when you break that kayfabe, it is time for you to get punished. Look at what happened to the WWE's main superstar, Hunter Hearst Helmsly, Triple-H. His best friends in the entire world, Kevin Nash and Scott Hall, were leaving the former-WWF that night for the WCW. He decided to go out of character, he became Paul Levesque, and he was punished for it. Like he should have been. Like Jack Miller will be. 'sorry ma'am, i really am. i had to break the news. i had to make the phone call to tell you that you're due, you know where, i'll tell you when, and i suggest you start living these next three weeks, the best way that you can.' How could it be fun to pee on the grave of a cancer patient? Mrs. Casey had a tumor in the back of her brain, given three weeks to live by the time they had discovered it. However, the cause for life became lost on her. This normally vibrant old woman had died at the age of 74 years old, but at first she wasn't physically dead. She was only mentally dead. Yes, the news report said she died of natural causes. And yes, the news report lied. every night for three long weeks, she'd roam the hallways half asleep and as the footsteps fade away in my mind, i could swear, i could swear, i heard her say: don't wait for me, i've got a lot to do i've got a lot to be and in the end maybe i'll see you there. 'He's going to fucking die.', Nick said. 'I've already lost the person closest to me, so what would losing Jack Miller effect me?' He smiled. 'In no way at all.' lost her strength on a saturday. spent the day in bed. yeah, i'm fine, it's just the flu she said with a smile, but when they turned their backs, the tears would flow. 'You want to see what it's like to lose someone close to you, Miller?' she knew she only had a while to live, to breathe, to see, to be to bleed, to stand on her own two weakened feet 'and so I pray everyday: don't take my mother away' 'I'll put a mirror in front of you when I take your life.' and in the end maybe i'll see you there. you know i'll see you there and in the end i'll see you there. -Catch 22 - As The Footsteps Die Out Forever 'You're going to die.'
