I fell from the top Welcomed by Satan's laughter Whilst I lay in bed I feel it in me Hate, woe, all of which feelings Like hell in my soul The crowd gasped aloud As I fell to a near death Blood escaped my wounds Ivan with raised fists Laughed at carnage created Me drowning in blood In the back of truck They told me breathe in, breathe out I felt blood exit The rise and fall of Rhett Not completely beaten yet Close but no cigar It's odd how they say Never give up or give in They never felt pain Agony, defeat All of which are inside me Thanks to damned GV IV inside me Monitor beeping so loud 'Shut it up!' I say Doctor told me no I won't be going to Slam 'Fuck you' were my words Beside me is my foe Next to me--Graphic Violence Wow, what a deuchebag I will be at Slam I swear on my life I will I have not lost yet GV will pay much Destroy him with my bear hands That's what I will do. Thanks to you GV I have found a new purpose: Beat the shit out of him
