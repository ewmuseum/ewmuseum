Getting the information on Tempest....

12 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-12-02 “I don’t care WHAT she looks like, Wang!” The voice of Ivan Stanislav reverberates in the background before the camera even fades up, but when it does, sure enough, the Russian Bear sits at a bar with a bottle of vodka in his hand….. It appears that Ivan Stanislav and Communist Party Member Wang are seated in a car of a train. Behind them is a line of windows that shows the Siberian wasteland sweeping by behind them. Hills and mountains covered with a thick blanket of snow pass by without much notice, and occasionally a large clump of trees can be seen or a few shacks. The ‘clack clack’ ‘clack clack’ of the train as it roars through the area only furthers to drive the scene and creates a monotonous noise in the background. Stanislav sits on a large barstool in the bar car of the train. He wears his black shirt, black pants with red stripes, red suspenders, boots, and beside him on the bar sits his military hat. Major Wang, on the other hand, stands behind the bar in his military uniform. The two appear to be having some sort of a conversation that has carried into the timeslot of the recording time. Other than Wang and Stanislav, the room is devoid of foreign ears. The hanging lights above the two communists jostle back and forth slowly and cast wavering lights around the room, only to have it reflected off of the bottles of liquor behind Wang into the camera. Stanislav brings his hand up to the temples on either side of his head and rubs them, obviously having a headache. “But I am tewwing you Pwapowschik! This woman is velly velly pwetty!” Wang exclaims. He throws his hands up in the air. “I have seen her before! You shouwd have much fun with her! Huh??” Ivan shakes his head slowly and raises the entire bottle of vodka to his lips. He takes a deep gulp, letting the liquid heat of Russia flow down his massive neck into his ironclad stomach. “Wang, I do not need to hear your advice today! I will soon be meeting this woman and we will be having the ‘time of our lives in Congo.’ At least, that is what they say! I have far too many things to do!” In a huff, Ivan gets up out of his chair and tosses the bottle across the room. The bottle slams into the far wall and sends vodka spraying everywhere. Wang covers his mouth with his hand and thinks for a moment. Suddenly, his slanted eyes perk up as he walks out from behind the bar. “I have idea Ivan!” Ivan stares at Wang as he runs out of the car. Ivan shakes his head and puts his huge hands into his pockets. “Why did I let him come along? I would have liked to have kept my mind away from him and his stupid ramblings!” Ivan shakes his head and rolls on his heels. “Meeting this woman in China…argh! I need Wang to show me the way…..” Ivan shrugs and looks out the window. He squints as he stares at the bright white snow of his homeland. “At least we can take Trans-Siberian railroad……” Ivan smiles to himself as the sound of a door rolling open is heard and Wang returns with a HUGE television set in his hands. The television dwarfs him as he struggles to carry it and drops it with a large CLANG on the bar. “Here Ivan, now I can show you how pwetty she is!” Screams Wang. Ivan shakes his head and rubs his temples again as he realizes how much of a headache Wang is going to create on this long, arduous trip… Wang fiddles with a few cords, actually plugging the television in behind the bar. Ivan grabs a barstool and rips it from the ground and positions it in front of the television. Wang produces a small tape from his inside pocket and dusts it off. Above the television screen is a built in VCR. He pops the video into the VCR and turns on the television. The television slowly fades up from black to a blank blue screen. Ivan crosses his arms over his chest. “This had better be good, Wang.” Wang nods to Ivan and grins as he fiddles with a small remote control that he produces from his front jacket pocket. He fumbles and produces it with a sly little grin and then realizes that Ivan is merely staring at him, waiting for the results. Wang grins and finally hits ‘play’ on the remote and the blue setup screen of the VCR suddenly turns into Tempests latest promotional video for the Old School Wrestling Federation. As the video plays, Tempest and her friend, NAME, talk about the bachelor contest, and amazingly, as we notice from Ivan’s expression as he watches, it is one of intrigue and interest. Wang crosses his arms in front of him and smiles as he notices that Ivan’s expression is quite new to him. “Well, maybe he’s just curious and....maybe even a little insecure.” Ivan laughs as he hears the statement from Tempest. “Insecure? Ivan Stanislav insecure?! Insecure about what? That this woman will fall for me? That I will defeat all those that get in my way! DYAAHA!” Wang seemingly fades into the background as Ivan continues to watch. Stanislav actually leans forward to study Tempests face..... Miyuki, Tempests skeptical friend, is next to speak.... “He’s obsessive, out of touch with reality and damn near certifiably!!” Stanislav’s facial expression quickly changes to one of disgust as he listens to this woman debase him, but he continues to listen. This time, it is Tempest to retort. “There’s something weirdly charming about a guy so committed to his ideals...” Ivan looks over at Wang and smiles.... “...that he hasn’t noticed the rest of the world abandoning them.” The Russian Bear whirls around and watches the television again as he hears the last part of her sentence. He shrugs, not sure if it was a compliment or not, and continues to listen and watch. Miyuki fires back, “Charming! You think the Berlin Wall was charming?” Ivan looks at Wang. “But of course! HA HA HA!!” Wang shrugs, not sure if he should agree with Ivan or disagree. The video continues to play until the very last line.... “I will have fun with Ivan Stanislav...even if it kills him...” As the scene fades out and the VCR switches back to it’s normal, dull blue color, Stanislav and Wang are left to ponder that. Ivan runs his finger through the neckline of his shirt, seemingly well, a little hot... “So, what do you think of the wittle girl?” Wang asks, prodding Ivan with his elbow. “Uh....well, she is very interesting......and uh....” “Cute?” “Uh...perhaps.....” Wang smiles at Ivan. Stanislav gets up and shrugs. He speaks with a newfound....happiness. “Perhaps Comrade, this could be a good excursion! After all, she comes from Communist China, perhaps she will be more of an asset to The Red Army than I thought! Of course, mind you that I am only thinking about her in a military and working class sense! I would never let any sort of personal feelings get in the way!” Wang nods, winking once and giving Ivan a thumbs up, in other words, he doesn’t buy a word of it. “What?” Ivan asks, shrugging to himself. Wang smiles and walks over to the television. He pushes the eject button from the top of the machine, but for some reason the tape doesn’t eject. He pushes it again, and again, and again. Finally, as he loses his cool, Wang pushes hard on the eject button, accidentally pushing the television behind the bar! With a snap and a sizzle flames erupt from behind the bar and bottles of alcohol burst into flames!! “YOU IDIOT! GET OUT OF HERE!! DYAAAA!!” Roars Ivan as he rushes out of the room. “Detach the final car!!!” Ivan continues to scream down the next car. Wang yells and follow Ivan as a loud screeching is heard and suddenly, the bar car drifts behind Wang who barely steps into the neighboring car which is the sleeping quarters. Wang hangs on to Ivan like a child. He looks up at Ivan and grins innocently. “Well Pwapowchik..she is a fiewy girl, no?” Ivan does not smile as the camera pans over to see the rapidly growing smaller bar car as it bursts into flames in the middle of the Trans-Siberian railroad and Ivan Stanislav heads to meet his date....
