Understanding the Gi(g)ant(ism)

4 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 4, 2002 The 'Meeting of the Minds' had been going on for hours in the other room. Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman, Alan Kriegman and Howard Porter, otherwise known as the Think Tank, were brewing another plan to rebound off of their previous two failures. Trashcan Man was sitting on his knees by Kriegman, and Armour had told Mammoth a few moments ago he was going to the shitter. John finally had time alone to think. He was having troubles keeping a clean concience ever since his arrival to OSW. He felt like shit for doing the things he did. He hated himself for doing the things he did. But he had reason, everyone has a reason. . . Right? Mammoth wasn't sure anymore. But he remembered the phone call that would change his life. He remembered the phone call that... promised him life. *** The behemoth lay on his bed, peacefully asleep. Doctors assured him it'd be just a few more months before that bed would become his death bed. Several times they begged and pleaded for him to come to the hospital for a health evaluation, to see what the cause of him dieing so early was. Oh sure, he had gigantism. That was no secret, but even then he shouldn't die at his age. Alas he lay there, in white horizontally pinstriped pajamas, under navy blue sheets as a fan worked loudly from a stool by the window. The pajamas were unreasonable. He couldn't find any his size, and tailors were too expensive for cheap Jonathan Warren. So he settled for XXXL pajamas, which were rather short in length on him. In fact, the pants ended at his knees, and the sleeves ended just below his elbows. His peace would abruptly end, with the nagging sound of a buzzing telephone. Mammoth's beautiful emerald eyes slowly drifted open. Quickly he sat up, stretched and yawned, then moved to the living room. The telephone was on it's seventh ring when he lifted it, and slammed it down on the receiver. John yawned once more, 'Too early.' He didn't bother to look at the clock that showed a big hand on a four and a little hand on a one. No sooner than his second step back toward his bedroom, the phone buzzed once more. 'Jesus hell,' He lifted the phone, whispered a four letter word, and said politely 'What do you want?' The voice on the other end was raspy and high pitched, that Mammoth thought was originally a hoarse child. 'I want a Mr. Jonathan Warren, ring name John Mammoth? Class Fighter, Level 12, X.P. . . .' The voice was interupted, 'What kid, you want an autograph?' The voice chuckled, and ended it with a snort, 'I'm hardly a child, Mr. Warren! This is Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman. And I've called you for your ser. . .' 'Dorkiman?' The former college professor was interupted again, 'Ah christ, I'm hanging up.' 'NO! WAIT!' '. . .Why?' 'I'm asking you to join me in an alliance. I save your life, if you help me.' John Mammoth, 'Bullshit. You can't save my life, 'Professor'. That's insulting.' 'Oh,' Dorkiman's voice was of delight, 'But I can. . . I tell you what, we'll discuss details in procisely 0200 hours. Be there.' And the dialtone was all Mammoth heard after. *** 'MAMMOTH! GET IN HERE, NOW!' Dorkiman called from the other room. Mammoth replied with a smile, 'Sorry, I'm busy.' Ah, t'was going to be a long day
