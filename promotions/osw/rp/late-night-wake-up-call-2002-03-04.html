Late Night Wake-up Call

4 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-4-02 Beep, beep, beep, beep! The sound of an alarm clock ringing loudly continues, until slam! A hand is brought from under the covers on to the alarm clock and literally busting it into a million pieces. Out from under the covers pops Jesse Ramey and with a look of shock he begins shaking his head. “Damn it!” Jesse shoots. “I did it again, baby.” Out from under the covers pops Chass’ head as she is trying to keep her eyes open. “What did you do, baby?” She begins to rub her eyes. “I smashed the alarm clock. That makes about the third one so far.” Jesse slides upwards in the bed and raps his blanket over his mid-section, exposing the rest of his body. He rubs the back of his head, lifting his long hair up. “You know, I’m thinking about getting my hair cut.” “What?!” Chass sits up quickly, still holding the cover over her breast. “You can’t cut your hair. It’s too beautiful to be doing anything like that. I like your hair the way it is now.” “Yeah, I know. You like my hair long, but I just can’t make this feeling settle that maybe, just maybe, if I get something changed about myself that I’ll get a little more out of certain things.” Jesse explains. “Like what?” Chass asks reluctantly. “Well, for one. Maybe I’ll be able to receive a little respect from my peers. Maybe the fans will feel that the change was for the best and maybe I’ll be able to get a little more confidence out of it. Cause this damn stuff.” Jesse flips his hair into the air. “It’s just so irritating and it keeps getting in my way.” She begins to pout up, “I don’t understand how getting a hair cut is going to make the fans, your co-workers, or anyone else think any differently about you. Your hair can’t change you or the person inside of you. Trust me.” Chass places her hand on Jesse’s back. Jesse stands up and all we see is his backside as he walks into the bathroom and we hear running water begin to stir. Chass sits up fully and turns on the television. The channel is on MTV and Jesse’s theme song “Party Hard” by Andrew W.K. begins to play and it plays out. The bathroom door swings open and pile of steam comes pouring out. As does Jesse, who has a towel around his mid section. He just looks at Chass. “What do you think you are looking at?” Chass asks aggressively. “Well, I wasn’t meaning anything by it.” Jesse answers. “Can’t I look at you if I want to?” Jesse asks then quickly replies before Chass has a chance. “Of course I can and the only reason I was looking at you was because I was going to tell you to get your ass in there and take a shower!” Jesse blurts and then smiles. Chass begins to laugh as she hops out of bed and runs over to Jesse. She gives him a big hug and kisses him a long, deep, passionate kiss. “You know. I never want you to ever change. I love you so much.” “Yeah, I know.” Jesse smiles. “Now, get that ass in that shower!” Jesse slaps Chass on the ass and sends her on her way into the bathroom. Chass runs off into the bathroom as Jesse makes his way to his suitcase. He pulls out his boxers with Garfield on them and he slips them on under his towel. He then sits down at the bed and begins looking through the phone book. As he runs his finger through the yellow pages he moans and groans. Letting out little sighs, he presses on until he comes to what he is looking for. “Ah, ha!” Jesse emphasizes. “This one looks mighty fine.” Jesse reaches over and picks up the phone. He begins to dial the number and then waits. “Yes, ma’am. I was wondering if you could fit me in for a hair cut today? Yes, ma’am I can wait a moment.” Jesse waits. “You can do it? Great! How about a bleaching and a washing as well? Great! Thank you ma’am. My name? Jesse Ramey. I’ll see you in a little bit. Good bye and thank you.” Jesse hangs up the phone and looks over at the bathroom door. “Chass is going to kill me when she finds out about this.” Jesse begins to snicker as he continues putting on his clothes. ~The End~
