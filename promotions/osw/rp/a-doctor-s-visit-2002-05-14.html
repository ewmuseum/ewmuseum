A doctor's visit...

14 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/14/02 Tom Walczak enters a building. From the outside it seems as if it was an ordinary building, but inside it is a different story. You see this is a doctor’s office, and the reason for Walczak being here is that after seeing his head crash through the soda machine on the previous SLAM! the doctor made a personal phone call to Walczak asking him to come in for a checkup. Walczak makes his way farther into the building until he sees his doctor’s name on a door to the right. Walczak heads into the room, and the secretary sends him to a room in which the doctor is waiting. “Tom, you see I noticed the vicious shot to the head that you took on Saturday, and to tell you the truth it doesn’t help. You know that you have had similar problems before, and as I have told you before all these things shorten your career.” “Well, to tell you the truth doctor I don’t feel the least bit different. Just as the last time my head doesn’t hurt, and I don’t even feel the least bit dizzy. You can run all the tests that you want, but they are all going to give you the same end result. My head has nothing wrong with it. It is a gift that I possess and it helps me in my profession.” “As true as it may be, there is one thing that the tests fail to show, and that is the future. You see although there may be nothing wrong with you at the present moment the fact of the matter is that fifteen years down the road it could cause major brain damage.” “I am sure that what you are saying all makes perfect sense, but I guarantee that it won’t happen. You ran the tests yourself, and you know that my cranium is abnormal. It is something that I have to live with for the rest of my life, and something that I am glad has come up in recent discussion. All these years I was worried that I would suffer brain damage, and that was the reason that I abstained from moves such as the headbutt, but now that I know that my head is an impenetrable force it makes me want to use more than ever.” “Let me ask you just one more question. What would possess you to strike a soda machine with your head in the first place? Why would any man do that? To me it just makes no sense.” “Well doctor, had you watched the weeks edition of SLAM! then you would have seen that Doug Troy blatantly stole my soda, and he would not give it back. A scuffle broke out, and than just out of anger I crashed my head into the machine.” “Tom it was just a soda, it is not that big a deal. I have lost a good twenty dollars to a soda machine throughout my life, but all that I do is shrug my shoulders and move on with life. Sure I may clench a fist, but that is the extent of it.” “Maybe to you it was just a soda, but to me it was more than that it was robbery. Yeah I could have let it go, but what would that do? That would just make him think that he could bitch me around whenever, and wherever he wants to, and to just state blatantly I don’t like being anyone’s bitch. Troy started this, and now it is just a matter of time before he faces the consequences. Now, doc, if you don’t mind I have to get going now.” Walczak leaves the doctor’s office without giving the doctor a chance to respond. The doctor follows him to the door, but realizing that it is a futile effort he makes his way back as the scene fades to black…
