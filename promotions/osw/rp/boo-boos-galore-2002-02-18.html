Boo Boos Galore

18 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-18-02 “Ouch ouch ouch ouch ouch ouch ouch!!!!!” screams Seth Masters as we open with a close up shot of his face. Yes, this opening shot should be familiar to you by now, as this is the third consecutive week you have been met with it. This time, however, it is a bit different. As the camera pulls out, our duo of Civil Disobedience are surprisingly not in a hospital room. That’s not to say that they shouldn’t be in one, though, as they definitely seem to have met the prerequisites for a lengthy hospital stay. It’s about an hour after their match with Azrael Cain and that ratty hobo of his, and injuries o-plenty were dealt out to them in a frustrated post match attack. Both Seth and Trevor are sporting gashes on their heads, and their bodies have enough bruises to make their cuts look pleasant by comparison. Now I’m no scientist, but I’m guessing that that’s what sledgehammers do when they come in contact with human bodies. The sore losers made sure that the winners were sore as well, though in very different way. No egos were bruised on Civil Disobedience’s...just a whole lot of their bodies. Seth and Trevor are still clad in their wrestling gear. When I say that I mean they’re wearing elbow and knee pads, as the rest of their attire wouldn’t go over as wrestling gear no matter what circle you run in. They wrestle in street clothes, for Christ sakes! INSANITY!!! Anyway, they appear to be walking...scratch that...limping down the corridor backstage at the MCI Arena, on their way to the parking garage where their Chevrolet Celebrity piece of shit car is currently being housed. As they slowly make their way back, Trevor shoots a concerned look at his still moaning partner. “Hey man, are you ok?” Trevor asks. “That cut on your head looks pretty bad.” “I’m...cool,” moans Seth, barely able to get the words out. “Are you sure?” questions Trevor, raising an eyebrow in surprise. “Because I’ve really never seen another man bleed the way you did tonight. It’s a miracle that you haven’t slipped into a coma yet.” “Yeah...sure...I’ll...be fine,” Seth replies with a weak thumbs up sign. Seth is still losing blood, and is hardly able to walk at this juncture. Trevor looks back down the hallway behind them and sees a trail of blood covering the ground where they came from. Trevor gives another wry look directed at Seth, who seems to have faded into a state of semi-consciousness, slumped up against the left wall. “Yeah...well I think I’m going to drive to the hospital to get this head wound checked out,” Trevor says, pointing to the cut on his forehead. “You wouldn’t want to come along and have your cut attended to as well, would you?” Seth jerks up upon hearing his partners voice. “What?...No...don’t go to...the hospital. We’ll be...alright...Besides...we can’t...give them...the satisfaction...of sending us...there for three...weeks in a row.” As Seth finishes, he slumps back against the wall once more. Trevor reaches his hand out and grabs Seth by his shoulder, shaking him awake again. “Seth...I know it sucks, but my head really fucking hurts. I’m not going to let some stupid pride shit stop me from getting the proper medical attention.” Seth is face to face with Trevor now, though by looking in his eyes you can see that he’s not all there. None the less he still tries to argue his point. “Come on...it isn’t...so bad. Chicks lose...more blood...every month...than we did...tonight. They don’t...go to...the damn hospital...each time. You don’t...want to...be weaker than...a girl...do you, Trev?” Trevor sighs, wishing that the pathetic nature of Seth’s argument could be attributed to the lack of oxygen getting to his head, but he sadly realizes that Seth would probably have made this same argument no matter what his physical condition was. Shaking his head in pity for his friend he begins to explain to him just how stupid he is. “That argument is really weak, Seth. Not to mention pathetic. Really pathetic. I mean, who do you think I am? You? Just because you try to threaten my masculinity doesn’t mean I’m going to buy into your stupid ideals, you moron. Besides, I really doubt chicks lose a gallon of blood each month like you have tonight, Seth. Listen to yourself. You can’t even string more than three words together at a time. You need medical help, dude.” Finishing up, Trevor stares at Seth to see what reaction he’s going to get from him after that mini-tirade. A dismayed look comes over his face as he sees Seth’s eyes rolling up in the back of his head. Trevor sighs, realizing that he’s going to have to get his friend some medical attention quickly or else he might...you know...die. Hoisting Seth up on his shoulders, Trevor manages to carry him the next hundred yards to the parking garage. Finding their car, he tosses his unconscious partner into the back seat before fishing in his pocket for the keys. Finding them and inserting them into the ignition, he pulls out onto the streets of Washington D.C. on his way to the hospital. He can hear Seth murmuring in the back seat, moaning something along the lines of “Medical attention, schmedical attention.” Trevor feels a strange sense of deja vu, remembering all the times he’d been in a situation similar to this. Like the time Seth picked a fight with Illinois State boxing champ...or the time Seth decided to ride a bike in a demolition derby...or the time Seth punched William Shatner at Star Trek convention. Man, those were some enraged Trekkies. Yeah, Seth sure suffered life threatening injuries at an astounding rate. It’s amazing the guy was still alive. It’s even more amazing that he still hasn’t gotten a clue as to how reality functions as of late. Trevor is chuckling at this thought when he pulls in front of the hospital. An orderly standing outside smoking a cigarette glances at the car half-heartedly, then his eyes bulge out as he recognizes it. He tosses his cigarette to the ground, stomping it quickly, then runs inside with a purpose. Seconds later about five more hospital personnel have joined him, wheeling out a stretcher to the back of the Chevy. They are all screaming loudly two words, over and over again. “Code Seth! Code Seth! Code Seth! Code Seth!” they cry, opening the back door and dragging Seth out. They place him on the stretcher, strapping him in place, as they wheel him back inside, still shouting. “Code Seth! Code Seth! Code Seth!” One of the orderlies, seeing that the situation is under control, stays back to converse with Trevor. He looks rather nondescript; brown eyes, a narrow nose, thin lips, his hair covered by a surgical cap. He nods understandingly at Trevor, striking up some small talk. “So what’s the problem tonight? Bear attack?” he asks, looking after the stretcher as Seth gets transported down the hallway. “Nope,” Trevor says, shaking his head. “Sledgehammer mishap.” “You mean he was beaten with one, right?” “Right,” Trevor says, nodding his head. “Poor guy,” says the orderly as he shakes his head in pity. “He just has no luck does he.” “More like no brain cells,” Trevor responds with a chuckle. “Well the constant beatings he keeps taking don’t seem to be helping matter much,” the orderly says, putting his expert analysis to work on the situation. “That they do not,” says Trevor in a tone of semi-concern. He quickly drops it, picking up a more positive timber. “Oh well. He’ll be better in the morning. All set to come back next week.” “Ah, booming business,” the orderly jokes. “Your friend is a blessing to our country’s health care system.” “Heh. So you guys wouldn’t happen to have one of those Pac-Man machines in your pediatric ward, would you?” Trevor asks hopefully. “Does Mrs. Pac-Man work?” the orderly replies. “Sure does!” Trevor says, walking toward the door eagerly. “Primo...” With that our scene fades to black. Wait...are we missing something here? Oh yeah, a mention of the opponent. *Ahem* TWISTED METAL SUCKS!!!! That’s better. Now we can fade to black.
