The Hunt

17 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 17th Forgive me not starting on the usual note in this particular promo of quoting the latest song that I know you all are bangin’ your heads to, but since so many others have found and used it at their whim, I’ll be my original self, yet again. ;) 'If you can't love the one you want, love the one you're with ' - Anonymous This everyday thought about not taking the ones around you for granted was the foundation of what the handicap match between Ivan Stanislav and 3B, Frankie Bones and Drew Hostyle on Slam! in Indianapolis, Indiana, was all about. Ivan was stuck in dead center of a love circle, in which he sacrificed his everyday life and professional image for the woman he'd grown quite fond of, Tempest. What he didn't understand was that she was, just that. A tempest. When he should've been thinking about competition, she led him astray. He held her back in so many ways it wasn't possible to count them on one hand. But then again, Drew was also sure that Ivan had earned himself some bull sh*t gratification from her too. But to hell with sensitivity. Contrary to what you'd expect though, Drew Hostyle DID want Ivan to love or continue to love, (if they even categorized it as that) Tempest. Ivan would love her when she fell, and he'd love her even more when she triumphed. But what about when she was crushed, I mean literally? Would he love her then? When she couldn't move her mouth, let alone 'give good conversation,' would he love her then? Would he ‘enjoy her company’ when she'd been knocked unconscious or paralyzed from the neck down? All the questions would be answered, and with good explanation, mind you. Ivan was going through the motions with this little chick. Once you see something, you've got to have it. It was instinct for just about everyone who had what it took to become a professional wrestler and in any other industry where the employees were strong physically and/or mentally. But for now Drew would ask ALL Ivan the questions and he'd make them very simple. 3B would ask questions. Jane would ask questions. They were each putting the Russian Bear to the test. Whether he'd pass or fail..was up to him and him alone. Not something he's used to having though, was it? A choice, an alternative. He wasn't a member of a capitalistic society, no wonder he was so puzzled. No wonder the Russian Army was just as confused as he. Choices..could be very dangerous things if extended to the wrong people. But choices were normal for Drew, but new to the entire 'oh so mighty and powerful' Red Army. So he DID have an edge over the highly acclaimed, and Great Russian Bear, heh go figure. The scene opens to show three Afro-American *I know you like the terminology* men in a room that is almost as dark as their skin. However, a few objects are apparent as the camera zooms from man to man. The first of the men has to be Drew Hostyle..because well it's his promo and we all recognize him*bare with me*. The second however isn't apparent, and the third is probably the most recognizable because of his mouth lighting the room with a golden flash when he speaks. This man is Frankie Bones, Old School Wrestler and 'The Legend of Pain' who has in his possession, the only iced out grill in the PTC. He isn't worried about flaunting his money or his teeth in this instance though. Apparently he's not too comfortable or just isn't feeling his current state. Or maybe he'll tell us himself. 'I ain't feelin dis.' Frankie lets out. 'Turn on da damn lights fo the last time, man. Dis shit is whack.' Drew Hostyle, yet another Old School Wrestler and fellow member of 'Brooklyn Born and Bred' with Frankie Bones and Sebastian Toomes, agrees with Frankie. The second man, the one who we didn't identify, then let's out a cough of skepticism and eventually drudges over to the wall with the light switch on it, and flips it towards the ceiling. The man suddenly pries his mouth to speak. 'So much fo trainin, huh? Ya'll give up too easily. The advantage of surprise is too big in this match. Ya'll don't know how strong Ivan Stanislav will be and if he can handle both of ya'll! Open up your eyes and get yo heads in da game.' Jordan X Shabazz warns. As you can tell the voice has now been identified as he turns on the light and we see all of the men in plain vision. 'When I opened my eyes, it looked just like the inside of my eyelids man, I can't deal with dis shit. All for one little match? Hell, we goin to boot camp.' Drew proclaimed. 'My program is not a boot camp, ya'll. It's a preparatory thing for when taking on big obstacles. Plus after the trip up to the backwoods will be the final step.’ 'Nah, don't no bruthas go in the wilderness, man’ Frankie sighed, as he explained for what seemed like maybe the hundredth time over. 'Fo' sho'. I ain't down wit dis campin thang, neither.' Drew finished. 'Nah guys. It's not about campin or the wilderness; it's about huntin. And ya'll are gonna need to get some practice when ya'll go up against Ivan next Saturday.' Jordan explained. 'Oh, so that's what this whole thing is about. Don't worry 'bout ole' Ivan and the Red Army. They'll get taken care of come the right time. I'll show 'em the privileges of capitalism...mainly kickin they asses and not dealing with repercussions.' Drew laughed. Frankie however did not laugh. He just let out a small smirk and then spoke once again. 'I think he's right. We should give this a try.' 'Is you crazy?!' Drew asks, outraged. 'What's da matter?' Jordan begins. 'You scured ain't ya?' 'Yeah, true dat mane. He's prolly scared of dem wolves.' Frankie declares. 'Or maybe dem coyotes.' Jordan adds. 'Nah, nah. Gets this, he's afraid of the dark.' Frankie says then burst out into laughter. Drew raises his left eyebrow and then speaks. 'That ain't it.' 'Well then what you scared of?' Jordan says, still poking fun at him. 'Bears.' Frankie concluded. 'Bears?' Both Copelands: Jordan and Drew ask, confused and shaking their heads. 'You's afraid of bears. The big ole, wrinkled, Russian ones.' 'I AIN'T AFRAID OF IVAN!' Drew shouts, meanwhile getting to his feet in front of Frankie's face without his knowledge . Jordan walks over to the two men and pushes them away from each other. 'Ya'll let's hit the road already. We got everything we'll need.' 'What's that?' Drew asks impatiently, still weary of going for other reasons. 'Well what I need to take a fat ass bear down doesn't come off so easy.' Frankie comments then rubs the brown skin on his right bicep. 'True, true.' Drew comments then grabs one of the bear traps that Jordan has brought. 'This thing might help too.' 'Meh..whatever works.' Frankie says then walks with the Jordan and Drew following him out of the door. Minutes later, around twenty to thirty we fade into see Drew, Frankie, and Jordan exiting an old Ford pickup truck, one of those straight out of the cheesy commercials stating that they were ‘Ford tough.’ The truck is red and its paint chips are barely their on each side of the truck. Jordan is the quickest to grab the supplies they’ve brought with them and among them are bows and arrows, cases of tranquilizers, huge steel bear traps, and lastly Jordan slides a 9mm. out of his pocket, unloads it of its bullets and throws it down with the pile of the rest of the supplies. This pile is near Drew’s foot, and it becomes apparent that Jordan somehow miscalculated and accidentally left one bullet inside of the gun’s barrel, because POP! the gun exploded as it hit the ground and nearly took of Drew’s big toe. Not..a good start, I’d say. ‘YA DUMB ASS MUH-FU...’ Drew stops his rant to fall to the ground and rock back and forth, probably not noticing the condition of his foot and overreacting as he is. ‘You aight?’ Frankie asks wearily. ‘I guess.’ Drew answers slowly, taking the time to examine every degree of his feet. ‘Erm..THAT was the first lesson. Always think..on your feet guys!’ Jordan X Shabazz exclaims. ‘Any more lessons like that and your whole damn education system’ll be shot to hell.’ Drew declares. ‘Please believe it.’ Frankie agrees, shaking his head at Drew’s nearly mutilated toe. ‘Aight guys..whatever. Just help me move these supplies over there, that’s the spot we’ll put the first trap in. Over there’ Jordan points to a spot to the far right of where they stand now. ‘Is where we’ll build fire.’ Both men look at Jordan as if he’s Crazy*get it?* and leave him to set the trap by himself while they go to set up camp further away. Jordan is huffing and puffing by the time he reaches their actual place of camp, and when he gets there he sees Drew and Frankie with their respective tents set up and them lounging in the sunset. Frankie watching his portable television and Drew sitting back listening to random tunes on his silver and black mp3 player that they’ve both brought. Jordan leans on a nearby tree and drops his jaw open, looking at the two men in disbelief. ‘Can ya’ll explain what you thought I meant when I said “wilderness”?’ Jordan asks, disappointedly. ‘No fast food or girls at hand.’ Drew snapped, as if it were obvious, then returns back to his music. ‘Come on guys, this isn’t wilderness. NO TV’ Jordan barks in Frankie‘s direction. ‘NO CD players’ he spits at Drew, while yanking it out of Drew’s hands. ‘NO CIVILIZATION! NOTHING MAN MADE’ ‘Well some guy made me..but imma bout to kick yo ass, boy.’ Drew says, struggling to get to his feet. ‘It’s aight man, I feel what he’s saying now. We gotta focus on da task at hand so we can get outta here though.’ Frankie begins. ‘What we gotta do, Jordan?’ ‘It’s about time someone else understood. Now, you have to trap a bear and prepare it to eat before sunrise tomorrow. This will probably take long, but it’s great motivation and experience for you guys to have. I may be the youngest here, but I know what I’m doing when it comes to this.’ Jordan said. Both other men agree with a simple nod of their heads. ‘Okay..well get started then!’ Jordan shouts. Drew looks at Frankie and they both look back at Jordan. ‘How?’ Frankie asks for the both of them. ‘Figure it out, it has to be your own work, there won’t be a map or diagram on Ivan’s forehand!’ Jordan rallies. ‘Yeah, yeah whatever. Let’s get this show on the road.’ Drew hurries. Drew and Frankie then walk off further into the backwoods of Virginia and Jordan fades out of the scene with a satisfied look on his face. Drew and Frankie hike for about what seems like an hour, but the real length of time isn’t measured. They seem destined to fail at this challenge, neither men can even figure out how to open the stupid steel bear trap. Suddenly a small log cabin is in plain view and so both men figure ‘What the hell’ and walk into it. The cabin is once again dimly lit because of the time of night. Candles had been lit throughout the room though and it was fairly easy to spot the two men opposite Drew and Frankie in the room. One was large and had a sort of shapeless head and bulky body while the guy opposite him has distinct features, mainly pointy nose round head and thin legs, both looked as though they had good intentions however, nothing sinister. The two men look over to Frankie and Drew with their heads tilting downward and then as Drew and Frankie stare back, then quickly recoil. Sensing that the men weren’t..everyday kinds of guys them and with as little of an intimidation factor as he can stand, with deep voice and standing 6’6 he asks: *with bear trap in hand, remember* ‘You know how dis thing works?’ The smaller guy takes on the role of a groupie and goes ecstatic about meeting 3B from first sight. ‘Wow..you guys are....wow!’ He gleams and runs back to the peg that held up his coat on the wall and grabbed a pen and paper out of its interior. Drew then reaches out, knowingly to autograph his sheet of paper and nearly snatches it from the small man, before the man speaks again, with a different look on his face then he had about 30 seconds ago. ‘What are you doing dirty thieves? We don’t have anything you’ll need in here. Scram! Get out of here before I call the authorities!’ Drew eyes open wider in a rage and he walks out closer to the man and holds him up by the collar to show the seriousness he meant to imply. ‘Whatcha trippin bout cracka?’ Drew shouts. ‘I..I..I love your work.’ The small man pants again, and sadly not keeping his mouth shut. The camera then fades out to Frankie who has been watching this and looks as if he’s in a bad horror movie with no plot. ‘Arrrrgh! What da HELL is wrong wit him?’ He says, angrily then turns to the larger guy who been standing dumbfounded in the corner all the meanwhile this was happening. ‘Duh..I think I should be the first to tell you all, guys that my friend is....HEY, LOOK AT THOSE PRETTY COLORS!!!’ The large man screams while running over to the flash that’s coming from Frankie’s gold teeth and stands a mere five inches away from him to steal a glimpse the next time Frankie opens his mouth, which may be pretty soon. ‘Get da FUCK off me, bitch.’ Frankie hollers, a bit perturbed. Only a bit. ‘Damn crazy white people, they all the same really. Look at Ivan and his confusion, it’s all on in da same, by brutha.’ ‘Whatever you say..but da cracka isn’t scary as Ivan..matta’ fact he’s pretty damn dumb.’ Frankie laughs then gives him a little push backwards. Drew drops the small guy from the ceiling and watches him bang his head on the long trip down for a small man his height. He then smirks at it’s results, a tiny little scrap with blood dripping through it. ‘See what did I tell you, look past the size and what..they all dumb as da crackas they portray.’ Frankie chuckles out loud and then pushes the man back..harder this time so that he lands on his butt in a sliding fashion. Not because he had done anything to him in particular, but just to keep the natural order of things. Brooklyn on top, everyone else at the bottom. ‘Don’t ever look into wrestling, crackas.’ Drew scoffs then turns around towards the doorway leading to what appears to be the kitchen as a man walks out in an apron with blonde and dark brown dirty hair spiked up and begins to speak. ‘Dude..that guy like totally has bi polar syndrome and is happy and mad at random times, and him..well let’s just say that his name is Adam Derrick Davis.’ The man says making sure to say the last name very slowly. ‘A..D...D?’ Drew puts it together. ’Uh..well that explains them. But what the hell are you doing in a apron?’ Drew asks, pointing towards the man’s kitchen apparel. ‘Nothin much dude, just how I roll.’ The man only sane man of the three explains. ‘Uh huh..whatever.’ Drew mutters and then prepares to leave the cabin, tapping Frankie on his shoulder to motion for him to follow. ‘Wait a minute. Do you know how to use this thing?’ Frankie asks holding up the bear trap. ‘Why would ya’ll need that, there’s not any...’ the man is cut off by a huge roar and everyone in the cabin falls to the ground, that is if they weren’t on the ground to begin with. ‘Da fuck is dat?’ Drew asks, in between covering his head in lue of some tornado or other natural disaster. ‘I dunno..’ the man in the apron answers and then looks to ADD. ‘What?’ he starts speaking very slowly. ‘Did you bring in the huge basket of food that we’re eating for dinner, INSIDE of the house?’ the man asks, half way knowing the answer himself already. ‘Well..uh, lemme see. I was walking to the door and then..OH! OH! PRETTY COLORS COMING FROM HIM!’ ADD says pointing to Frankie Bones and his mouth of gold teeth. ‘Do people know these things are renewable? Hell, they could even by some themselves’ Frankie says while winking at the camera. ‘Only two thousands ninety nine..’ Drew interrupts Frankie’s advertisement with his conclusion as to what is outside. ‘Damn Frankie B, I think we just caught ourselves a bear!’ Drew shouts. ‘What do we do?’ Frankie asks. ‘To hell if I kn..’ Drew starts then looks to the man in the apron. ‘Don’t ask me, I’m wearing an apron, what the heck would I know about hunting?’ ‘Dumbass cracka..’ Drew rants and then throws his arms up in the air to signal him giving up. Meanwhile, during this time the two retarded or mentally handicapped men on the ground have already figured out how to work the bear trap and are opening and closing it on each other’s fingers, not releasing the true purpose for it. Still ignoring the men on the ground, Frankie remembers the tranquilizers that Jordan forced him to bring along and fishes them out of his pocket. They both gather up the courage to go outside, not facing but a pretty good distance from the bear and shot him with it. RAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH.....PLOP! Was the sound that the bear made at it hit the hard dirt in one mighty leap. ‘....You okay?’ Frankie asks towards Drew’s direction. ‘..Yeah, I’m fine.’ Drew says wiping the dust off of his clothes and getting back to his feet, still trying to look manly in the process, remember and failing miserably. ‘Well..it’s knocked da fuck out.’ Frankie states. ‘You and that guy from dat movie Friday said it best.’ Drew agrees. Both men then simultaneously bend down and start to drag the bear from either side, still keeping a far distance, down the slope they wear on and back to their camping spot to show Jordan their feat and get it over with. ‘Hey Drew’ Frankie calls from one side of the bear. ‘What?’ Drew asks, still moving. ‘You think Ivan will be this heavy?’ Frankie asks yet again, of course cockily assuming that they’d get Ivan even near this position. Drew drops his half of the bear as if he’s surprised at Frankie’s comment and then turns to respond. ‘S’pose he’ll be less hairy.’ Drew says back. ‘That it?’ Frankie asks. ‘Well..’ Drew thinks even harder to himself. ‘Maybe Tempest will be his tranquiliza’ looks like things’ll be easy from here on out fo us. Easy pay this Saturday, easy pay.’ The scene now fades to black..
