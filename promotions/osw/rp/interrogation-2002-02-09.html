Interrogation

9 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Feb. 9th Uh, oh. That's what they always said, whenever you did somethin wrong, in class, at home, wherever. They always gave that fore-warning that did no good at all, it sure as hell didn't stop the trouble from coming, that was for sure. Becuase as you were standing at the front of the class, about to damn near pea in your pants, the teacher, knowing the answer herself always went through the questioning. The scene opened to show the contents of a room with dim lighting. The room had four white walls, and one had a shiny hint of glimmer in it, which meant that someone was on the other side. The rest of the room was dark and vague, until a single light, a flashlight lit the room up. And it shined on the face of a dark skinned man who wasn't easily intimidated by the man holding the light, then to a shorter black man who had been very nervous, even so that you could tell from the dampness of his shirt. The man's face who had been holding the flashlight to the two men, has know become clearer and he smiles at the men geniunely as if switches the flashlight off, then takes a seat next on the opposing side of the table they all were sitting at. He sighs as if he's been there for awhile, and wipes his brow as he takes a breath and begins to speak again. 'WELL DID YA OR DID YA NOT, BOY!' shouts the man. Drew raises an eyebrow at the man, and looks at him as if he's crazy, the man catches on and appears calm and a little collected yet again, as he paces around the table, jumping out at Drew and his partner in crime, Pookie at random intervals of time. 'I'm gon go through this one more time, boys and ya better give me a sufficient answer.' 'Here's a question for you, why the hell am I here?' Drew Hostyle asked, his body language becoming more agressive as he pounds on the table in front of him. 'Listen...sir. You're here for several reasons, one, there's a warrant for some interrogation up in the New York area for a reason homocide case, we don't know down her whether you're to blame or whether you just tried to help the poor lady, but ya been in the news for weeks and anybody's who's got a Television can see that.' 'What the...I mean, I don't know whatcha talkin bout, man.' Drew answers, then slouches back in his chair. 'Listen boy...er..sir, you need to let me finish speakin before you do, I'm even lucky you've decided to open your trap right about now, but I need ya to close it temporarily.' Drew Hostyle and his somehow distant family member, known to OSW TV as 'Pookie', both lean in to the police officer at this point and appear interested in what he has to say. 'Know we're makin some gosh darn progress, here. So don't pretend that you don't know why ya hear, boy. Just tell me..between me and you...did ya kill that woman, or did she really kill herself?' The man asks slyly, hoping Drew and/or his distant..cousin will fall into his trap. Pookie decides to respond to the question and says, 'Nizz-o, whatcha si-zo? Dis fiz-o ain't do na shiz-o. Get ya facts stri-zo, he ain't no criz-o.' Drew smiles then nods his head as if he were agreeing with the statement, which obviously made no sense to the poilce officer. 'You had damn well better translate er respond understandably, and I do mean now, son!' Drew is silent for a few seconds, then holds his head in his hands and sucks his teeth as he's about to speak to the officer. Just then, a white man with a tight pressed blue and white pin striped suit, looking pretty clean cut with dark brown hair gelled to perfection on the top of his head and a squint like he can't comprehend the reason behind something that had ben going on in that very room. Some loud mumbling can be heard behind the 'glass' and man's shadow can be seen trying to leave, but is pulled back in by another figure. The man doesn't acknowledge why he's there to the officer, instead he walks to and looks straight to Drew Hostyle and Drew, says something strictly to him. 'Who the f*ck are you?' 'Oh, only the man hired to save your a$$ today', he answers back, showing his New York style accent and flashing his full mouth of shiny white teeth at him. Drew is still confused and is about to speak again to the New Yorker but is cut off, suddenly. 'Every word you say is costing you $54 an hour, I'd keep my mouth shit if I were you.' The man says. 'Who in God's name are you, and why you are ya in here while I'm tryin to get answers outta these two...men?' 'My name, sir, is Jonathan Black, and your's is Officier Williams, I presume?' 'Well..it depen...' 'Yes, and I'm here because after witnessing your actions towards my client..' 'Your client?' 'My client..I've found that you broke the law that says you cannot arrest ANYONE without the proper warrants for his arrest in hand. And I'm absolutely sure that you, yourself wouldn't want to go to jail for a serious offense like this one, would you?' 'No, but I have it on good authorit...' 'You have absolutely no authority at all sir, and who may I ask is exactly in charge of these facilities?' The police officer grumbles then shuffles in the seat he's taken. 'Excuse me, could you speak a bit more clearly?' 'I say I'm in charge..'says the officer again, this time, less sure of himself then ever before. 'Well then. I'm sure you wouldn't want this hanging over your head, your entire career, so me and my two clients will be leaving now, thank you for your time, sir.' The officer says some more inaudible words under his voice, but Drew and Pookie walk directly past him and make gestures at him as they do. Their lawyer looks back at them with a stern look on his face and they look down at thier toes while making it to the man's car outside of the station. 'What you drivin man? This must be some old school Rolls Royce sh*t you spinnin.' Drew says as he lays his hands on it's hood. 'It gets me where I need to go, like saving poor people like you, every once and awhile.' Jonathan says, then motions his head for the two men to hop inside. 'So what's yo name, son? For yu to be savin innocent brothas like us, you gotta be a decent man.' Pookie says as he adjusts his official black OSW hat with various pictures of superstars on it's brim. 'Jonathan Black, and your Drew Hostyle' he says, looking at Drew on the passenger side of his car, 'And your..Spooky, correct?' 'Mane, my name is Pookie, not no Spooky..' Pookie says then Jonathan leans forward and smacks himself lightly on the head. 'Sorry..I knew it was something like that though' Jonathan answers, then smiles to signal he's joking, Pookie doesn't find it funny, but Drew does and he continues to keep the conversation rolling. 'So, Jonathan Black huh? You sho don't like black to me' Drew winks. 'What do you mean? I'm just a light skinned brotha!' Jonathan says then winsk back at him. 'Oh fo real? Damn..well if that's the case I must be a dark skinned cracka!' Jonathan just smirks but Pookie and Drew laugh very loudly and slap hands with each other. 'Okay, okay. Guys we need to get serious for a minute, Drew as your lawyer, I'm entitled to know what the heck is going on with you. Your lucky that guy didn't have a warrant in hand, heck half the officers in the country are to arrest you on sight.' 'That's a serious white boy, right der Homie you betta tell him the deal, yo.' Pookie says punching Drew in the shoulder. Drew looks back at Pookie with eyes squinted and says to Jonathan, 'There's nothing to explain..' 'Really? Well then how about I help you get started..first off, there's a..rumor I guess you could say about a woman named, well her name ins't important, but she used to keep a journal or diary of things going on in her life at the time. You do agree with that, don't you?' 'Yeah, I guess.' Drew confirms. 'Well this woman..she was a doctor of sorts, and she helped people help themselves. But suddenlyout of the blue, and her diary confirms this, she killed a total of three people..for no particular reasoning, and the only man who could've known why she did this, was a man who'd obviously gronw closer to her than her own boyfriend and any of her patients...this man was..' 'It was me' Drew finally speaks up. 'Precisely..so, Drew it's just me and you..here, and as your lawyer I want you to feel completely comfortable telling me things like this..' 'So you just my lawyer now? How but you be ma manager or somethin man, you get paid betta than this..' Drew says as he looks around at Jonathan's car. Jonathan, sensing that Drew's changing the subject, plays along anyways. 'You want me to work under you?' 'If that's the way you see it..' Drew answers. 'No...now about that explanation..' 'What about promoter?' 'No..' 'Valet?' 'Heck no...' 'What about my publicist, you get paid alot betta than you do now' drew says as he looks around the car. 'Okay, Drew, sure. Now stop avoding the subject and tell me the explaination!' Janathan says as he nearly drives the car off the road. 'Okay, okay. You wanna hear it, fine I'll tell you, but when this goes to trial..' 'IF it does..' Jonathan interjects. 'Then you betta defend me like nobody else ever has, you betta be the best damn..' 'You won't be convict if you weren't to blame.' Jonatahn says. 'And if I am?' 'Well then..we'll see, but if you don't tell me the story, then we'll never know.' 'Well this chick..Wendy, she was a good doctor, btu I could tell that she'd be more to me. She was a right tight chick..i mean her body was bangin, but she was always stressed about somethin. You could tell her mna wasn't treatin her right. But she was one of those complusive type chicks, she knew me so good that she knew somethin was wrong and finally..after all her beggin, I told her.' 'Told her what?' asks Jonathan, already comtemplating the answer. ' I told her about the Benefit.' 'What's the Benefit?' 'The Benefit is a old..gang I was in back in Brooklyn. It's a group of Benefactors who's main purpose is to fight against all who opposin them, to make way for a new future of people who can defend they selves individually and not worry bout terorism or nothin'' Drew explained. 'So this..Benefit, so if I were to join would they let me in?' 'If you tryin ask if they was rasict or not. they ain't. But they prolly wouldn't let in no brotha like you..so seem to weak. how you think they let a guy like me in?' Jonathan looks up at Drew then shrugs as Drew continues. 'I'm the best fither they recruited in a long time, that's why they been ridin me ever since I joined the PTC and ever since I been wrestlin period. When I first joined and wne to the Primetime Wrestling Academy instead of straight to the PCW, they got real mad, so mad that they kicked me out, literally.' 'Well that's great then, now they can't bother you.' 'Nah that ain't it. See, they gave me a whole list of tasks I had to do before I was let back in..and I didn't have nothin else so I tried to do em.' 'What were they?' 'ONe was to get into a federation in opposition to the PCW, since they wouldn't let me in at first..' 'And here you are in the OSW, did you know that Jane Reagan owned it before hand?' 'I have connections, I knew that they wouldn't exactly be friendly with the PCW, and I had a feeling this was the plave to go for revenge.' 'What else was there to do?' Jonathan questioned. 'I had to kill..' 'KILL?' 'Kill..a certain number of people.' 'How many?' 'Let's just say..the number has been reached, and I'm back in.' 'How does this tie into Wendy's homocide case?' 'Once I told Wendy about my tasks, she insisted on helping, she said I showed her the light..what her whole purpose in life was..but it wasn't. I didn't want her to help, but she had to and I..I...' Drew stuttered. 'Are you saying that Wendy Lam..killed her client..her husband and herself, so that you could join that damn gand again?' Drew was silent and then put his head in his hands again. 'I told her not to, but she did..I never meant to hurt her..I never meant to..' 'That's terrible, are you still in the Benefit today?' Jonathan asked. patting Drew on his back trying to sympathize while putting together a case. 'Yeah..they track me all the time..where I go, what I do, they know everything. So, Black, you gotta help me, if I go on trial and say i didn't do it, then I'll get killed by the Benefit, no if's and's or but's about it. But if I don't..then I'll go to jail.' 'You're on a double edged sword here Drew' Jonathan sighs. 'So what you sayin, you don't know how to help?' Drew asked, puzzled. 'No..I'm saying I already found the solution', Jonathan replied as he looked at Drew with a serious stare. The scene then faded to black, but questions are still puzzling in your mind. How much longer can Drew be on good terms with his Benefactors and not be seen as a criminal to the public eye. What kind of solution could Jonathan come up with, to a lose/lose situation like this? Was drew ready for his match at Slam!? Would the things that went on in this promo be a sign of things to come? Is Drew really as good a guy as he seems to be?
