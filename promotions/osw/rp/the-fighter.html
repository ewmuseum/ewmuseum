The Fighter

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Feb. 18, 2001 Perspiration poured from Rhett's face as he pounded the heavy bag. Clinched left and rights slamming into the bag, making it swing back and forth. Rhett breathed through his mouthpiece as he continued his relentless asault on the punching back. ''Ey youz, Rhett, come 'ere.' Dom made a gesture for Rhett to come, and soon he did. Removing his mouthpiece, and exhaling deeply, he soon replies. 'What do you want bro?' Dom chuckled, and took a sip from a plastic cup he held in his hand- more likely beer. 'Wha' do I want? I want youz t' take a breatha.' 'No can d-' 'Oh, youz will take a breatha, youz don' wanna be over-worked befo' ya big match Satuh'day, right?' Rhett couldn't detest those words, so he simply sat down on a bench and watched as two fighters fought in the poorly constructed boxing ring by the bar. A smile slowly spread across his face as he watched the two, so inexperienced. He remembered his days, so long ago in that ring- just like them. Run-away kids, just hoping to win some money for a meal that night. The aroma of a mixture between sweat and blood filled his nostrils... Rhett finally remembered his past...and he now embraced the future. Rhett was The Fighter, and he would win.
