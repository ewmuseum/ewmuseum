The Arrival...

13 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/13/02 The scene brightens and we see Tom Walczak leaving his gate with his puke stained shirt still on his body. People notice the smell on him and they turn away in disgust, but Walczak is not offended. The reason being that he is just happy that his flight is over. Walczak turns around to see if his limo driver is there, but there is no one there that is holding a sign for him. He goes around in circles looking at the various signs, but still no sign of his driver. A man running through the entrance doors causes a racket. Walczak turns around to see what is going on. He sees a man in a suit, and the man begins to dig into his pocket. The man is fairly young, and Walczak dismisses him as being his driver. That is until the man takes out a small piece of scrap paper with the words “Tomek Walczak” scribbled on it. Walczak shakes his head in disbelief and walks over to the man. “You’re late.” “What are you talking about sir? I was here the whole time.” “Are you joking? I just saw you stumble through those doors. I also saw you just pull out that piece of crap sign.” “You must have been seeing things because I was here the whole time. I really was, and to tell you the truth I am a little angry at you for being later than that of our scheduled time.” “You know what I think that I am just going to take a cab, because I don’t feel like putting up with your sh*t throughout the whole ride to my house.” “Come on I really need this job. My boss said that if I lose another customer that I would be fired, and I really can’t afford that right now.” “Well, than maybe you should just admit that you were late. I won’t tell your boss, but you should come on time next time ok?” “What do you mean next time?” “Well, you don’t expect me to have a car as soon as I move into a new place do you?” “No.” “Good, so its done, for the next couple of weeks you will be my driver, and if you are late than that will be the end of your driving career. Do we understand ourselves?” “Yes of course, and there is no need to worry I will be on time whenever I am called upon.” “Ok, now just take me over to the car.” “Yeah no problem. I have a question to ask you though.” “What is it?” “Well, I was wondering, what is it that you do for a living?” “I wrestle for a federation known as OSW.” “Yeah, I think I have heard of that before. There is some Polish guy in that federation right?” “I am that Polish guy you stupid moron.” “Yeah that makes sense.” Walczak takes his bags and follows the guy to the car they are talking all the while and every time that a word comes out of the drivers mouth Walczak stares in awe at the stupidity of the man. They get in the car and ride away as the scene fades to black…
