The Long week Part I: Preparations

7 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/07/2002 'In the world of wrestling, you have to put your emotions deep down a thick layer of dedication and hard work, and concentrate on your goal. In several times throughout history, heart overtaking brains have meant defeat. Hitler, Napoleon, let their greed dictate their actions, instead of reason, and led their empires and their plans to complete destruction. In sports, that's even more real. Steve Prefontaine could have run for the silver medal on the 1972 Olympics, but he ran for the gold, but his heart wasn't enough to supply his body and mind with enough energy, and Steve lost the race. Brazil gave up two matches on the Olympics, in 1996 and 2000, to Nigeria and Cameroon, because they thought they could outlast their opponents with their talent and hearts, forgetting about defending their own goal. Even in the literary world, the best works from writers is usually the one they work on without any disturbances or things to be worried about. Unfortunately, that wasn't Ace's case that long week.' **The scene opens to a big gym, in a tall office building in LA. The place is known for CCW fans, but not for OSW fans. We're granted the view of a big room. The room is very bright, with white painting and floor, and the sun breaking through the large windows to the right making the room very well lighted. Following the windows to the right there are several fitness equipment, like a Bowflex and other fitness equipments. On the back of the room, several posters for CCW and OSW shows and PPVs, and before the wall, several punchbags and other boxing equipment. In the middle of the room, a wrestling ring and two wooden benches running around it. On the ring, we see Andrew Hawk, making push-ups with Danny Jackson sitting on his back, talking to Johnny Atlas, who's sitting next to Ace, with a water bottle on his hand, drinking from it. Johnny is wearing his old black and blue wrestling pants, his old in-ring attire, and black leather boots. Danny is also wearing his wrestling attire, a blue and yellow cut pants and blue boots, and Ace is wearing his usual attire, but with a Brazilian national soccer squad T-shirt. His face is covered with sweat and blood, his eyes burning with rage and focus, his muscles tense and vibrating, Ace releasing a gutural groan every time he pushes up.** 'His entire work that week was not based on Switchblade's talent, because it was Ace's talent. It wasn't based on the match, because Ace was experienced enough to get himself around the ring without having to have a 'game plan' before hand. It was all based on getting ready to inflict the most pain possible on Toomes. It was about shoving his words, his pride, his arrogance, down his throat and out of OSW forever.' **The scene now shows Ace, standing on the punch bag, pounding it mercilessly, and on the other side, Danny Jackson holding it and feeling the punches through the sand bag. After every punch, Ace's eyes get even more opened, his soul transpiring from his pupils, bathing his arms with energy and power, as he focus on a spot and pounds it again and again.** 'Ace knew that could be a major mistake. Ace knew that losing his mind during a match could be fatal. But he didn't care. He knew about the others that had the same mistake, but this was different. This wasn't a mere conquer rampage, or a simple match. This was personal. This was about his legacy being tainted and destroyed by a man that he trusted once. A man that he made, and a man that turned on him. He didn't care how much it'd take. The only thing he could think of was to get bigger and better, and get in that ring, and completely annihilate the monster he created.' **Ace is now shown on the locker-room, his head under the sink, the cold water turned on, the crisp clear liquid running through his hair, his face looking relieved.** 'And there we were, three days before the big day, and we could see how Ace got more and more pumped to the match. Every day that went by, you could see his soul burning, his inner fire getting bigger and bigger, you could see him grow after every second the match got closer. I don't remember seeing Ace that energized, at least since he faced Tim Inman for the CCW Title, almost 2 years ago. Like then, this was about respect, honor and betrayal, and Ace couldn't lose this. No, he wouldn't lose this. Even if he had to die after the match, he'd destroy Toomes before. I can say, honestly, that the thought of losing my friend to insanity crossed my mind, but that thought ended the moment he got his head out of that cold water and came up, looked at the mirror, shook his head and looking at me, cracked a joke about Argentina losing in the World Cup. It was then that I knew he was just being himself: A stubborn son-on-a-b**ch, with the power and talent to take on every single wrestler in the world face to face. That was when I saw he would win that match. But that wasn't the end of the week... Still one little fact would happen, before that day...' To continue
