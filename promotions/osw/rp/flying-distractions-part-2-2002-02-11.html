Flying Distractions Part 2...

11 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/11/02 We see Walczak tossing and turning in his airplane seat. Finally he opens his eyes and sees that darkness surrounds him. All of the windows on the plane are shut and the lights in the aisles are turned out. He looks at the people around him, and sees that all of them have their eyes shut, and are sound asleep. He stands up and gets his bag from the overhead compartment. Walczak looks through the bag, and finds the beers that were given to him by the flight attendant. He takes them out and one by one opens them and chugs them. After he finishes he looks through his bag again, but as he is looking the plane starts to rumble. “Ladies and gentlemen this is your captain speaking, we are going through some turbulence now, and all of you must put on your seatbelts. We should be through this shortly.” Walczak looks around and all of the sleeping faces awaken, and put on their seatbelts. Some of the smaller children in the back begin to cry because of the sudden awakening from their sleep. Walczak takes his seatbelt and puts it on, but still begins to search through the bag. He finds his portable CD player, and looks at what CD is inside. He sees that it is Led Zeppelin IV, and he finds it much to his satisfaction. He pushes the play button and lays his head back. The smooth sounds of “Black Dog” rush into his ears. The guitar riffs put a smile on his face, but once again he is quickly disturbed by a tap on his shoulder. He looks up and sees that it is the stewardess from before. He pauses the music, and takes off his headphones. “I am sorry to disturb you sir, but at this point in the flight I am going to have to ask you to turn off you CD player. I will notify you when you are allowed to put your headphones back on ok?” “Yeah sure, no problem.” Walczak turns his headphones off, and puts them back into his bag. The smile he had while listening to the amazing tunes of Led Zeppelin disappears, and a look of anger and disappointment appears. He closes his eyes and clenches his fist as he tries to hold in the anger, so as not to disturb anyone around him. “The only thing that is keeping me from screaming out in complete and utter anger is that in only a short while I will be in the ring pummeling that fool the Trashcan Man. This flight has gone horrendously especially since I am only going to Poland so that I can set myself up in my new home overlooking the city of Gdansk. However I will also be training for my match with TCM, and believe me, if he thinks that I will go easier on him this time than he is sadly mistaken. I destroyed him last week, leaving him beaten and battered in the middle of the ring, and this week will bring about a similar if not a more embarrassing result for TCM. I know what the doubters will say. They will say that this type of match is TCM’s specialty. They will go on and on about how TCM is quite possibly the greatest Extreme Champion in the history of PCW, and although he has been out a while, and this is now OSW they will come at me with the same line again and again. I know he is a great street fighter, and he doesn’t play by the rules, but what he doesn’t know is that I am the same exact type of fighter. I am also a former Extreme Champion, and I held it when the competition was at its most fierce. I held it while there were actually competitors that could put up a fight. Not in a time where he would fight the same crappy wrestler week after week. This week will prove who the better fighter is, and I am positive that it will prove me correct.” Walczak hears a baby start to cry in the background, but just sighs and rest his head so as to try and get some rest. As his eyes are closed the mother of the baby starts to walk him up and down the aisle to try and get him to stop his weeping. She turns around right in front of Walczak all the while patting the baby on the back. As she is about to leave Walczak’s are the baby’s mouth widens, and he spews all over Walczak and his seat. Walczak jumps out of his seat, and with a look of infuriation rushes to the bathroom as the scene fades out…
