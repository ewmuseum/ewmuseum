Moment of Truth, Moment of Error

16 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 16, 2002 Being a wrestler isn’t all it’s cracked up to be. Seriously, to anyone out there who dreams of becoming a wrestler: Be a fall guy for a major corporation. You get more respect. I think I’ve been in the hospital at least 75 times in the 4 years I’ve done this shit. For all you kids who are less than 6’0, 200 lbs, stay the hell away from wrestling. It makes you a target for guys who are over 7’0, 300 lbs and trust me; the fights don’t end like David and Goliath’s. Goliath usually does something like slam a car door on your neck, break cinder blocks over your knee, or even throw a truck onto you. But here I was again, back in the hospital, courtesy of my favorite Russian Communist: Ivan Stanislav. What a god damned cunt. Amy standing above me, and ZaC is sleeping on my chest. It’s been so long since I’ve seen the little dude. Amy’s great for taking care of ZaC the way she has been, I don’t think I’ve ever met anyone as great as her. “Hey” It was great to see her “You've really gotta stop getting your ass kicked, man!'' She giggled, and my wit hadn’t yet kicked in for a proper comeback. Reno coughs and replies: 'You gotta stop...stupid...Aw, I got nothing.' “Aww...sorry, hehe. How are you feeling?” “Well, I feel great...considering I just had a truck fall on me.” My smile met hers. “Yeah, sorry again...for some reason I don't seem to be with it lately.” Then I remembered that she’d joined up with Jane Reagan’s elite. “I noticed.” “And what's that supposed to mean?” “Well you...nah. Nevermind.” I decided against telling her. She was a big girl, she could handle Reagan. “I really do love you...heh, but you can be THE most confusing person on the face of this planet when you want to.” She had a second thought and continued, “Uhhh.... err.... umm...okay, shutting up.” “I really think you should stay clear of Reagan. She’s a virus.” Nevermind, she had to hear it. Reagan was gonna fuck her up good. “You take what you can get. I want to go somewhere; I don't need the front office against me anymore. Nothing ever works out for me, ala the fucked up idea in my head that you and I might be something, SO I decided to start making things happen FOR me. She can do what she wants afterwards, but she wasn't going to be any less of a 'virus' to me if I had rejected the proposition last night, face it.” She said, going from Amy to bitch in 0.4 seconds. Fuck. “What the hell is wrong with you?” I asked. “What?” “Don’t bite my head off because I offer a bit of advice. I’ve been doing this for twice as long as you have, Amy, and I’ve done this ‘let’s join up with the boss’ bullshit. It goes nowhere. Fast. You want to end up fighting for some indy organization as ‘Amy the narcoleptic chicken’ or something trying to make enough to pay for a meal and a trip to the next town? If so you can just keep it up, because Reagan’s already got a reputation of fucking you over in a heartbeat,” Wait...did she just... “...did you say us?” “Yes, yes I did. What's wrong with that?” Well that took me off guard. I looked into her eyes and felt the emotion that was always there suddenly become so obvious. Shit, How was I so damn blind...so damn stupid for so long? I felt the same way she did, but I’ve been so fucking busy worrying about Mr. I look like I haven’t taken a shit in 7 years Stanislav, that I’ve neglected Amy. That time at the field, I didn’t even think back to what almost happened before ZaC interrupted. I couldn’t keep eye contact and looked to the other side of the room. My heart sank, and I realized what was going to happen. I couldn’t let her be a part of my curse. Watch her suffer like I do. She’s already done so enough just for being my friend... “I...I’m sorry,” I said, looking out my window. “What...did you...just say to me?” I knew she starting to cry. It was in her voice, and when I spoke I knew it was in mine as well. I tried hard to keep my voice steady, stern, sure of myself. “I’m sorry Amy.” I tried too hard though, and my voice came out cold, emotionless, robotic. Why does he do this to me? “That's...rejection? Reno, you can't be serious...” I was, but I didn’t want to be. I nodded my head, barely. I couldn’t bear to look at her, and I couldn’t speak. I nodded and she stuttered. “I...God...nobody's ever...you..ugh....” Then she made it worse. She touched my shoulder and tried to kiss me. Her lips gently pressed against my cheek, and then slowly moved over to my lips. I turned my head away more, pain beginning to spread through my body, not even matching the pain in my heart. Her next move was not expected. BAM! She punched through the wall, causing ZaC to wake up and leap away, landing into a perch on the IV holder thing. It scared me too; I mean shit...she punched through the fucking wall. “Are you that fucking simple? You think you're so fucking alone in the world Reno, but you think that that's anybody's fault but yours? I stand here and pour out my heart for you and you just say your sorry? You are full of shit Reno. You have to grow up or else you'll be bitching about being alone in the world for the rest of your life,” She screamed, walking backwards slowly, she was practically bent over she was screaming so damn loud. Tears streamed down her face, they began to wallow in mine. She left and I remembered everything she’d done for me. Everything she’d ever said to me. I remembered my first meeting with her when I saved her from those crazy CLIT people back in April. I thought she was just some emo kid, but we grew to be friends. She cared for me and did everything in her power to make me happy while I just thanked her and went on with my life. I’ve known how I felt all along I just ignored it. Reno Davis loved Amy Campbell. “AMY WAIT!” I screamed, pulling the IV out of my arm. It may have looked cool, but seriously don’t do it, it really hurts like a motherfucker. My knees buckled immediately once I landed on my feet and began to surge forward into a run. My body seared and burned as I could feel the ripped and strained muscles grinding against each other, twisting and tangling. I rounded the corner and saw her eyes meet mine as the elevator, in which she stood, doors shut. I knew that look she gave me. I saw everyday in my life. I saw it every time I looked into Ivan Stanislav’s eyes. She hated me. I collapsed onto my knees, tears streaming down my face from both the physical and emotional pain. A pair of nurses rushed to my side and raised me to my feet; only I shoved them away and opened the door to the stairwell. I was 7 stories up, but I had to get to her. My descent started well, but by the 6th story I was seeing triple. I fell the rest of the way down. As I exited the stairwell and entered the lobby, I saw Amy leaving the hospital. “Amy wait!” I hollered once more, grabbing my chest in pain. She didn’t even look back...She just left. Darkness began to fill my vision despite my struggle to stay clear. A few onlookers approached me and after that, I don’t remember...
