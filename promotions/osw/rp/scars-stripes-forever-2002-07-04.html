Scars & Stripes Forever

4 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07.04.2002 We fought your wars, With all our hearts, You sent us back, In body parts, You took our wills, With truth you spoke, We offer prayers, For your long lost souls, There is no flag large enough, To hide the shame of men in cuffs, You switched the sides, Then closed the blinds, You changed the channel, Then changed your minds, It remains there is an unjustifiable, Egotistical power struggle, At the expense of American Dreams, - 'American Dream' by System of a Down Here I sit on a bench amidst the wonderful scents and voices which scream out through all senses exactly what America is about. The flags have flown since our buildings crumbled and today, on a birthday far more important than anyone else's, the sparks that were lit on the date of September 11th erupt in a parade of red, white, and blue. All of these excursions are televised of course ... reporters scrambling around every major city in the pathetic country which I call my home hoping to find that one story which will put smiles on faces rather than tears to eyes. For if there can be one article or broadcast about happiness somewhere in this cesspool of immorality which people like myself have created, the broken faces of death and persecution will be gone for one day. One fucking day. The pictures of Elizabeth Smart and the screams of her parents in their sleep, simply wishing for the sick bastard who took their daughter to return her in any shape but limp in a body bag. Gone. The newborn baby of deceased journalist Daniel Pearl and the ideas going through the poor mother's brain ... realizations that the child will never have a father. Gone. When 'America the Beautiful' begins to blare and fireworks begin to stream the skies like a colorful omen, no one remembers the strain which those who never had this disgusting display to look forward to. The dead. The hungry. Those who do not fit the mold of the United States. Fuck America. Why? Once again, that same grueling question which seems to be imprinted in my brain. The answer this time around, however, is simple even for the jaded minds which surround me. What has America done for me? Sure, when the governments reigned my father made his fortune, and thus I grew up privileged. However, how privileged was I? Is it an honor to see your parents' blood draped across your floor like paint on a canvas? Is it a usual occurrence to force a child into running away from terror? Hell no. But America did nothing to shield the youth from what I went through, even in this age of advanced security and technology. They blame it on music, Marilyn Manson and Eminem, yet those who place the blame never conclude with themselves. Shouldn't the parents be monitoring what their children are exposed to if they are too dense to know right from wrong. Yes. But are they? What do you think? If you said no, you're poisoned just like everyone else. Leave your children with raping babysitters while you go and chase the dollar like a dog goes to meat. In the end, you just like that dog are the pet, on the leash of good old G.W. Bush and his bastard group of ass kissers. If the blame cannot be put on the shoulders of the parent, how could you argue that it shouldn't be put on the very shoulders of the people which you idiots vote into office? America Sucks. How could anyone bare to live in a country like this, let alone celebrate it? I always try to shed a tear when I enter through the doors of my estate, even though I hardly ever grace the house I bled for. Why? Damn that question. Once again, it all leads back to a conclusion which even Jim Axtell could figure out. Because of the people who rely on my money and my word to support them, the American people, I'm always at work. I'm in the ring or in the back speaking to brainless followers. With that fact forever running through the head which will forever get beat in for you people ... I find myself unable to cut my slice of apple pie and join the party. It's not like any of you want to be there in the first place. Sure, the break from slaving away in cubicles is just peachy, but won't the work be doubled when you get back? I wouldn't know. I was chosen for a profession which will result in my injury if the work doubles. Will It? In a way it already has with the release of this week's Slam! card. Two men with one common goal ... destroying me. This is bigger than Frankie Bones, for I have always been. They are out for me. Should they win, I'm sure everyone's favorite athlete will raise his flag high and chants will ensue while the boos for the man who really sweats for America lays hurt on the mat. Will you care? Of course not. For I don't fit into your version of the American Dream ... I never have. You shunned me when I was rich, poor, and rich again. Even money couldn't buy me love ... not even kindness. Well fuck you all and burn in hell twice. I don't need any of you helpless sheep, about to be slaughtered by the rigors of Independence Day. I have myself and I have the air in my lungs. What else do I thrive on? Pain. Yes. Come Saturday, once all the festivities have passed, there will be no streamers and sparklers. Just myself face to face with my enemies and my enemies face to face with their fears. Who will be victorious in the end? The dollar or the dream? You decide. Are we having fun yet?
