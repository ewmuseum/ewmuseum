So he drove his black ford probe down the country road and he approached a lady in red He felt an urge, to stop and perhaps make someone elses day a bit better. She was tall, dark hair with eyes to match. Skin that had just tanned beneath the summer sun, slightly moist with sweat. No broken car for miles, she was merely waiting. Waiting for him. He slowed the car to a halt, and rolled his window down. A question and a reply, and the car a c c e l e r a t e d again. She was now next to him, the woman in red. Her destination was vague, but always heading in a single direction. They drove for hours in the silence. They grew more distant from the world as night fell, and as they were engulfed in darkness, the earth was miles behind. The scenery changed then, where there were grass covered hills, now were cliffs of red sandstone. The desert wind howled, and voices cheered. His eyes were focused on the lights ahead. The brightest of lights, home. He knew when he reached there, he would meet his bastard of an ex-boss. The lights from the screaming white bullet were nearer, and now flashed red, yellow and blue. He looked to the seat beside him, and the red dress was laying on the seat next him, with no woman. She had left him. He slumped forward and the car swerved off the road. He smiled, the lady opened his door, closing it behind her. She sat next to him, wind blowing through her hair, and smiled. He lifted his hand to his neck, and when he merely touched it, his fingers were wet with red wine, a deep red He leaned towards the lady, he kept a gun beneath that seat. And he would defend his lady from the evils of the light. More drumbeats as the song continued. The woman was now drenched in the same red wine. He fell onto the red dress, the girl was gone again.
