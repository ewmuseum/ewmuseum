Waiting...What It Feels Like In A Tube

2002-04-23

Rent-A-Hero

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
..::\/::..You know...I still don't know how long I've been inside this weird looking swimming pool, but at times...I happen to enjoy it. I'm so busy fighting felonious fiends, spouting silly catchphrases, defending my title, and loving my red-headed vixen that I often don't get to ONE thign out of all things...THINK. Being in this tube has actually given me that oppourtunity and I must say...the thoughts that have perked up have been quite interesting. Plus it's not like there's much to see outside this darned thing except more darkness then a crowd at a DMX concert. Plus, it's much better to think random thoughts then to even begin to PONDER who was the SICK SON OF A ARKILLIAN who DARED STRIP ME OF MY COSTUME WITHOUT CONSENT!! And the least they coulda did was left me in my Underdog boxers...buuut oooooh noooo, they give me this big ol' Grandma undies instead. Geez, at least I don't have to be in a rush to use the bathroom. But lately...I've been wondering, 'what's it like?'. What's it like when I'm put into 'this situation' or 'that situation.' I've been asking myself more and more random questions just to keep some shred of sanity during this whole 'test tube baby' ordeal crap. Lets see what I've found shall we good ol brain of mine..::\/::.. 'WHAT'S IT LIKE....Knowing your just a genetic failure and just a screw-up in a series?' ..::\/::..Well this certainly kills my ability to say,'There's only ONE Rent-A-Hero!' Geez...it hurts...it really hurts to have to live with the fact that not only were you not procreated by the primitive reproductive rituals of humans or Kryptonians...but ALSO that you were a failure on top of that. HOLY PENCIL ERASERS EVEIL SCIENTISTS, just rub it in why don't ya. I feel as if I turned against myself all this time...because I always MADE myself believe I had a loving mommy and daddy who accepted their little spandex enwrapped child and sent him off to school, where the other Kryptonian kids would play with him and we'd live in harmony with each other. Now I come to find all this time that I've told myself a lie. Is that all life is at times...one, big lie? How can humans lie to themselves...it feels so bad when you have to face up to the truth....and sadly...the truth is stronger then any Marvel supervillain I'd ever hope to slay. I wish I could just raise my hand and BIZNITCH SLAP O'DOOM~!(this tilde legally applied via the Frisco & Kid Wonder tidle service) this evil truth I have to live with...but doing that would not be heroic, for a true hero faces reality, no matter how stupid it is. Hehe, funny how when I face reality, I end up like a goldfish in a kiddie fish bowl. They could at least drop me some pellets or somethin, I mean GREAT KIBBLES AND BITS, they wanna keep me alive right?..::\/::.. 'WHAT'S IT LIKE....Wearing that tight a** spandex?' ..::\/::..Ahhh spandex...a love affair as well documented as Rett Butler and Scarlet O'Hara, Romeo and Juliet...Tom and Jerry! Who DOSEN'T love the tight restrictive feel of that material, cutting off the circulation of the soft skin underneath! Sure, it leaves you with an excessive amount of chaffing and every once in awhile, you lose a GARGANTUAN amount of brain cells...but once you get past those little quirks, spandex will become your very best friend!..::\/::.. 'WHAT WAS IT LIKE.....Making Love To Aimz?' ..::\/::..Oh lordy...what a night indeed. I don't know what it was that snapped in me that night...I don't know what it was that made me do what I did in that car. The strange thing is...it felt right. I was in actuality, scared the entire time. By the time she was talking about, 'Put this here' and 'Lick here' I just wanted to give up and ask for the instruction booklet on the whole sex deal...but in the end it was all worth it. No game I could ever play....no villain I could ever slay...and no Gummi Bear I could taste could EVER...NEVER compare to the feelings we shared in that Rent-A-Car that very night. When she gave herself in so completely to me and vice-versa, it felt like...like...EATING A GREEN GUMMI BEAR WHILE GAINIGN THE HIGHEST SCORE ON TEKKEN TAG AFTER GETTING ASKED BY BATMAN TO TEAM UP WITH HIM IN A BIG CASE!!! That night...I also found out that my love for Aimz would drive me to do thing I thought I'd never do and bend rules I thought would never be bent. At times, I can't help but think she is poison to a hero's libido...but at the same time, I CANNOT let her go and I don't want to. Aimz will always have my heart, and no matter what shall happen to me...it just cannot change..::\/::.. 'WHAT DOES IT FEEL LIKE....To Be Fighting Mike Frampton?' ..::\/::..Mike who? HOLY DEAD CROWD REACTION RENT-A-HERO, it seems NOBODY knows who this guy is. He reminds me a lot of that one guy in the yearbook that nobody really cares about or cares to know their name...wait...that was me in my yearbook? *Sniff sniff* WELL THAT BESIDES THE POINT, the fact is, Mike Frampton is yet to make a true mark in the PCW and one of these days, I'm sure he will....but NOT at my expense. Should I get out of this darned tube, me and him will battle and I shall prove to him just how superior I am...test tube failure or not. I'm a P-O'ed RENT-A-HERO and I'm just HANKERING TO DO SOME SPANKERING, and it looks like Mike Frampton has just lined himself up. If he wants his name known, Turmoil won't be the night...because I will use all my superpowers to decimate him in the middle of my ring and defeat him for the one, two, and three. The only way he POSSIBLY can win our meeting is if I were not to make it due to being stuck in this tube, and that says a lot for his overall ability. Yet, take heed, even at fifty percent, Rent-A-Hero can defeat any mere mortal looking to dethrone his status in the eyes of the many Rent-Heads who love to see their hero clear the rings of PCW's filthiest wrestling trash. Frampton sure better hope I never get out of this tube...because if I do...he will feel a pain much worse then the pain I feel inside at this very moment. He has never seen a Kryptonian when he's angry, but he'll have that pleasure soon enough if I could just find away out. So until then, people will still be asking, 'Frampton who?' and when I'm done they'll still continue. NO ONE will get a win over Rent-A-Hero to build themselves up and I swear on the POWER OF GREYSKULL on that one..::\/::.. 'WHAT DOES IT FEEL LIKE....To have never beaten Juan Borro...your greatest competition?' ..::\/::..Juan....my greatest competition....and lets not forget....I am YET to get a win over the Golden Toe. Ever since I won the PCW Crusierweight Championship...something has been missing and that thing is 'sense of earning the belt.' That belt has no meaning without the one defining match that solidifies you as a champion...Superman solidified his status with the defeat of Lex Luthor...Batman's epic defeat of the Joker made him the hero to a city....and when I BEAT Juan...FINALLY beat Juan when it truly counts, when it finally happens...I will feel whole within my soul. He will supply me yet another epic battle, but this outcome WILL change...it will not repeat like Milli Vanilli's lip sync recording....I WILL pin his shoulders to the mat and be able to stare within my Crusierweight title and feel worthy to be a champion. If you don't 'feel' like a champion, then the belt you keep with you means absolutely nothing. An item is only worth as much as the beholder is willing to value it as...right now, a pack of Dollar Store Gummi Bears holds more place than this belt until I beat Juan..::\/::.. 'WHAT DOES IT FEEL LIKE.....To be tricked into this whole web of deception by C.L.I.T.?' Evil Voice:'Was there ever a doubt. I'm amazed though. Our observations show he displayed drive, motivation, even ended up growing to love and dislike things. I believe when we were originally feeding him the genetic information, there was a screw-up and he ended up like this yapping superhero...but all will soon change, it's not too late to make him what he truly should have been. He even made his soon to be NEW transformation easier...our stats on him shows that he has made love recently, making the original chemical in his body that much easier to activate now. He was never meant to be this yapping...loving...caring...hand-slapping...game playing piece of crap he is now, he was meant to fufill a destiny...to DESTROY all that stood before him with question. I will fix my grave mistake and when I find out what it takes to make him a machine of death...I will soon begin on an army, which will be sold to the HIGHEST bidder.' ..::\/::..AIN'T THAT A BIZNITCH?! They torment my girlfriend....they lure me into this place...then they hit me with this. When I think about what it was he said...I'm kinda happy that I actually came out a 'failure' if it meant that I could feel emotions that this man seems to think are weak. I can't imagine life now without love in it now that I've had the PRIVILEGE to vask in it's beauty. I like to laugh....I like to smile...I LOVE being me. The greatest torture they could do to me is to take my livelyhood, to take my smile from me and convert me to some harbinger of death and destruction. I am a HERO.....I save people from pain, not bring it. I hate C.L.I.T. for what they've done to me and the ones I care about...and they can only hope that I never escape...because if I do...I will do things I regret...like maybe, actually....SLAPPING A GUY!!! They're making me take it to the extreme...heck, I might just scream like a schoolgirl at an N'Sync concert while I'm doing it, that'll strike fear into the hearts of an man or woman for sure!!! I can only float and await my sweet release from this container...hoping that when I'm free I will be the loveable, cuddly, posing, people saving, and entertaining Rent-A-Hero....but for the first time I'm actually scared...scared that maybe...I will become something I've grown to despise since my growing years...an emotionless body filled with the deepest evil and hatred for his fellow man. I fear what I soon may become...I fear not playing video games again...I fear never vasking with the fans again....I fear nevering holding my sweet Amy in my arms again...I fear never enjoying life again. All the things people take for granted....I want them more than ANYTHING right now..::\/::.. 'WHAT DOES IT FEEL LIKE...Waiting for your inevitable destiny, whatever it may be?' ..::\/::..Scary...the scariest thing I've ever had to face. But strangely enough, I show no fear as long as I can remember the one moment in my life that changed the way I saw things and changed my entire focus in this life of mine. I could float here forever and await what could become of me...just please....don't take this memory from me..::\/::.. \-*Our hero is merrily whistling the old-school Batman theme as he is at the wheel of his 'Rent-A-Car' driving through the city streets, his lovely passenger Aimz looking utterly pissed, her eyes glazed in the most firece and angered look. Our hero exclaims in utter joy* RAH: Oh man what a joy that movie was! Oscar worthy I tell you. Don't you think Amy? Aimz: You just don't get it, do you? RAH: Huh? I got the storyline COMPLETELY sweet Amy, and if your talking about me not getting you those Gummi Bears, I'm sorry, I forgot my wallet in my other suit. *Has no clue what she means, Amy releasing her frustration finally in a sea of uncharacteristic emotion at our hero, almost making him lose control of the car* Aimz: You can't fucking figure out when someone is IN LOVE with you! You sit there and watch that dumbass movie while I'm absolutely writhing...I have been this entire time! You don't have the first fucking CLUE as to how a guy should treat a girl, ESPECIALLY one who likes him...you said you liked me, but you've never ONCE bothered showing it. I don't even get a God damned HUG for Christ's sake! *RAH tries to speak, but is once again cut off by the emotional overflowing rollercoaster that is Amy at this moment* Aimz: You watch enough of those damned movies, read millions of comics...you think you'd figure out that there are ways to show someone affection! I can't just sit around for the rest of my fucking life and wait for you to grow a pair! Figure shit out, learn how to treat a girl and then give me a call...I can't just twiddle my thumbs while you go through mental puberty or what-the-fuck ever. *RAH gets strangely, for the first time....quiet. Never once has anyone seen RAH with a closed mouth, no smile, and quiet as a country night. The car is strangely dark and filled with tension, our hero not having a word to say as he concentrates on the road.* Aimz: You know, I...ah fuck it. *His eyes growing just a little bit more intense, our hero switches lanes until he reaches the curb of the road, promptly stopping the car in front of an apartment complex and letting it sit, his silence still kept up as he just blankly stares out the windshield* Aimz: Great, what the fuck is it now? Am I supposed to leave or something? You know damned well that's not what I mean about treating a girl! RAH: Shut up... Aimz: What the...LISTEN... *RAH looks at her, his silent demeanor still overcoming his face as he puts two fingers over the lips of Amy, tilting his head to the side as he looks at her* RAH: I don't want to hear a word out of you unless it's during... *Amy sits startled* Aimz: During what? *RAH promptly shuts the car off, reaching over and hitting a switch that sends her seat all the way back until it's horizantal, our hero dropping his as well before inching towards her, looking her deep into the eyes before moving his fingers off her lips...replacing the place of those two fingers with his lips against hers as Aimz begins to combust in passion, her arms instinctively wrapping around our hero's back as he deepens the kiss, the scene fading with RAH saying to her, his eyes locked upon hers* RAH: I love you... *With that, Amy can't help but give in, kissing him once again as the scene starts to fade, the two in their own little world inside the Rent-A-Car, nothing outside it even mattering*-/ ..::\/::..Once again...I can only sit and wait...and hope and pray that when this is all over...life will be as normal as I could expect and that when you tune in same RENT-A-TIME...same Rent-A-Channel....that the Hero you knew will not be canceled. Until then....what does it feel like to wait? You tell me...
