Ramblings of a Trashcan Man

26 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/26/02 A slight breeze blows past a hunched over, mumbling figure, pushing a gum wrapper past his slowly shambling form. Glare from the streetlights above glint off the metallic paper as it floats along, before it is pushed up against a black sneaker. We hear a cacophony of voices, cut through by a driving bass beat as we see a throng of young adults standing in line to their favorite club. These natives of Hershey pop their gum and chatter along, occasionally glancing over their shoulder at one another, looking for a target in the warfare that is the singles dating scene. A few of them sway in time to the music, and grins light all of their faces as they look forward to another night of reckless fun. These youngsters have no fears, no responsibilities, no inhibitions, for they have youth, and right here, right now, youth is immortal. Sweat glistens on their young faces, the slight breeze doing little to cool them on this hot, muggy evening. We see one young man speaking to a very attractive girl next to him glance over her head and suddenly stop speaking. We follow his gaze to see the hunched, black clad figure slowly making his way towards the line. His paramour looks behind her to see the man as well, and she too is quieted. The group next to them turn to see the shambling figure, and like a wave cresting over a beach, silence reigns over the entire line as they all turn to gawk at the stranger about to be in their midst. His orange gloved hand are constantly flexing at his sides, forming fists briefly before relaxing at his sides. Like Moses parting the Red Sea, the line moves with a deep sigh as these young club goers, responding to some primal instinct not to interrupt this man's travel all step out of his way. The figure moves into the gap before suddenly stopping. His face comes up, and the wind picks up a little more, blowing his shaggy brown hair away from his face, revealing his crazed, unfocused eyes. He stares at something unknown above him, and the only noise we hear is that driving bass beat, which for some reason seems muted in the strange, almost palatable fear that has smothered this night of revelry. He suddenly shakes his head and his eyes come back into focus. He glances to his left, then to his right, a look of confusion on his face. He smiles shyly, reassuringly. Of course, we all recognize him as the once loved but now reviled Trashcan Man. 'It's ok, friends. It's ok. The master said I could take this walk. He has forgiven me,' says the Trashcan Man in a gentle voice. He waves a gloved hand in a happy goodbye before it drops to his side. His eyes become unfocused once again as he once again gazes at the pavement and slowly starts to shamble along. The crowd of kids gaze after him for a few moments. They collectively shudder unconsciously and form back up, once again chattering away like nothing is wrong, but every now and then tossing a furtive glance down the road at the slowly retreating figure. We are suddenly flung once again into the confused mind of the hardcore legend. We notice that everything seen through his eyes appear to be hazy, like an out of focus camera. His vision seems dull, lacking that spark of life that gives everything the sharpness and clarity that we usually enjoy. Once again we hear the disjointed thoughts of the Trashcan Man. All is well. The master is pleased. The master is pleased and all is well. Obstacles have been removed. Destruction has been wrought. We see hazy flashes of the attack on Da Westsidaz from the Trashcan Man's point of view. Disjointed images of Short Circuit's face, almost in focus compared to the rest of Trashy's clouded perceptions, being battered repeatedly by TCM's trashcan. Then a flash of Kriegman's smiling face as he has Short Circuit between his legs, preparing for the Pyro Driver. Strange, though. Kriegman's face stands out in this haze, completely in focus, his every feature captured in exquisite detail, but with a strange, glowing nimbus around him. Then we flash to Trashy hugging Kriegman's leg, still glowing, still in that sharp, super focus, as he watches Armour and Mammoth repeatedly slam steel chairs into Riptah and Short Circuits mangled forms. We're now back in the Trashcan Man's head as he gazes at the pavement slowly passing beneath him as he shambles on his way down the sidewalk. He glances up briefly, then stops for a moment to stare at a rock poster in a window. Through the haze, we see it's a advertisement for Kremmy and The Lords Of The Underworld's new album, 'KREMMY~!' Trashy tilts his head for a moment, and it starts to come into focus, but then he shakes his head and continues down the sidewalk. The master is pleased. But he wasn't. And it hurt. We now flash back to Trashy’s memory of the now infamous scene in Ed’s hospital room. “You never had a chance, my friend…” chuckles Alan Kriegman. Trashy gazes down at Ed’s broken, battered body. It’s slowly focusing in, as if Trashy was trying to truly see it, but something was obscuring his vision. He here’s his master’s voice. “Paralyzed from the neck down, Trash. You know what this means?” Trashcan Man shifts his gaze from Ed to his glowing features of his master. “I..I pleased you? Did I make you happy?” he asks. “No, Trash.” He smiles. A familiar agony began to build His smile suddenly changes to a scowl, and the glow around Kriegman’s face darkens to a primal, frightening black, yet still the Trashcan Man sees Kriegman in utmost clarity. His master smacks him hard across the face. TCM lets out a cry not unlike that of a child being slapped by his father for the first time, and spins away from the man in the black suit. “It MEANS, Trash,” Alan snarls “that you didn’t get the job done!” The pain was fast growing as strong as it had ever been before. Trashcan Man slowly turns back toward his master, his features still sharp through the tears of agony in his eyes. “I..I…” “NO excuses, Trash,” Says Alan while pointing to Ed. “I wanted him DEAD. You failed me, Trash. You FAILED ME.” NEVER had the pain been this bad before. Tears run down his cheeks, the pain in his head so intense that he can’t keep his feet. He drops to his knees and looks down at the feet of his master, clutching his hands to the side of his head. “I..I…I’m sorry master…I didn’t mean to make you angry..” he gasps. “I thought the bad man would die, I heard his neck…I heard it make that sound…” Looking down at his blubbering companion, Alan Kriegman gives a merciful grin. The master kneels down to face Trashy, takes the side of his bearded face in one hand, and raises it up so they may make eye contact. Trashy’s in so much pain that he can barely see the warm, fatherly smile on his master’s face. And like someone threw a switch, the darkness around Alan was once again glowing with that pure, almost holy light. . “It’s alright, Trash. I forgive you. I know you won’t disappoint me again.” He says with that wonderful smile. And the pain was gone! The master took away the agony with his sweet mercy! All glory to him! Relief evident in his voice, Trashy sputters, “Thank you…! I, I promise, I-“ Kriegman raises an index finger to his mouth. “Shhhhh… If you really want to thank me, Trash, you know what you should say.” Of course I knew. What else can I say to the master? What else can I give to the man who delivered me from the depths of my own private hell? My life for you! My life for you!! My life-“ “Shhhh. That’s enough, Trash. Go over and stand by the window for a moment while I have a little chat with Edward.” Kriegman commands, and with that, TCM gets up and walks over to the window across the room, where he stands…just staring out at the moon. We flash back to the Trashcan Man’s hazy perceptions as he continues his journey down the streets of Hershey. He holds the pain at bay with his touch. His mercy. His glory. And he has chosen me. ME! I am his weapon. I will bring destruction to his enemies. None shall stand before his glory. For his is the power, the glory, the mercy. He is. And he has a new target. The giant Russian. Ivan Stanislav. He has hurt me before. But that was when I was already in pain. The master has removed the pain. And he has pointed me at the Russian. Ivan seeks to hurt the master. He will be stopped. He will be destroyed. He will be ended. Trashy looks up and peers in a window of a convenience store. “Ooh, Cheetos!” The Trashcan Man’s face alights with happiness as he steps inside the cool, air conditioned environs of a 7-11 and we fade to black.
