Anger, The Future, and Coca-Cola

16 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 16, 2002 Jesus St Crux grimaces and holds his hand to his ribs as he sits in his chair. Shaking his head in disgust, he mumbles something disparaging about Ian Wright's parentage, then he reaches for the remote beside him. Caridad enters the room as the Mexican wrestler turns on the television. She smiles as she reaches for the door knob. 'I'm running out to get some Chinese. I'll be back in a bit.' Her voice shakes Jesus from his contemplation. 'Si, Dadi. Hasta luego.' Caridad closes the door behind her, and Jesus presses play on the VCR, and the screen begins displaying highlights of the career of four-time PCW Cruiserweight champion, Kid Wonder. Since acting CEO Howard Porter announced the cruiserweight tournament, Jesus had been looking forward to this match up. He enjoyed wrestling against Jason Winters because the two men knew each other well and their matches were always phenomenal. But Jesus St Crux had always made his career by finding new challenges and by wrestling the best in the business, and Kid Wonder was definately in both columns. Jesus leans back in his chair as he stretches, grimacing anew as his ribs ache at the unwanted movement. A slight growl escapes his lips, 'Wright...' Jesus shakes his head as he stands and moves to look out the window. Without really registering the view, he replays the events of the last Slam where he and Jason Winters had once again tried to have their one-on-one match. But first Kanaida interfered, angry at not having been included. And then when Winters and St Crux had started working together and fended off the Northern Wonder... Ian Wright. A dull thud marks the slamming of Jesus' fist into the pane of the window. Jesus shakes his head again and turns back to the television screen. He leans over and ejects the current tape and swaps it for the next set of Kid Wonder highlights. 'Madre de Dios... I've got to stop thinking about Ian. I've got to focus on K-Dub if I want to move into the future.' Jesus takes a deep breath to clear his thoughts as he returns to his seat in front of the television. 'Ian will be around some other time... unfortunately. I'll handle him later. Now to focus on Kid Wonder and the cruiserweight tournament.' The Mexican takes a drink from the cola beside him, and starts watching Kid Wonder's moves intently, looking for the method behind the madness.
