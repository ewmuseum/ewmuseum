Distractions

10 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/10/02 Distractions. Distractions are the things that make the world go round. When someone is intent on doing one particular act, and nothing will stop them, there are always distractions. No matter how hard we as a people try to avoid them, they control us. And Frankie has become very aware of this. His personal life has been turned upside down. In the past couple months someone has tried to kill him and that same person has kidnapped a kid that Frankie was mentoring. And through all of this hardship arises a distraction, the OSW. Turning from a day in, day out introspection, to a all out release of anger and aggression. Jesse Ramey felt this release last night, and he was just the first of many. Frankie's thoughts have gone from inner to outer. Concerned less about his hunt for justice, and more about his hunt for pain. In another turn of events, the demons of Frankie's past have taken a sidestep to let out the demons of his present, those standing in his way in OSW. And with the recent acquisitions of Trashcan Man, Doug Troy, Ivan, and the dumb Polish man, this list has just gotten longer. He sits Frankie Bones, at his dinner table, at the usual time, 4 a.m. The nights of sleeplessness have become too many to count. The reasons may change, but the nights are the same, sitting around, thinking... 'Damn, why do I have to litter my mind with thought non-stop? Why must I thrive to be the best? Why can I not just deal with the cards that are dealt to me? Why am I talking to myself?' Says Frankie. He can be seen looking at something on his table but it can be seen. He has a very sharp knife that he is using to cut slices off an apple. He lays his head down on what he's looking at, slowly banging his head on the table. 'Why can't I get this out of my head? Jesse Ramey and his boy just seem to be in the way. But I can't look past them. There is one man I can't get out of my head. Will Hostyle and Switchblade help me or just hold me back? My experiences with alliances in the past have left a sour taste in my mouth. It's simple, if they don't help me gain my goals, then they are holding me back, and I'll have to drop them. But, there's only one man on top here right now, and I cannot get my sites off of him, even if I tried. So it's decided, he's going down, Brooklyn style...' Frankie picks his head off the table and slams the knife into what he was looking at. He gets up from the table quickly sending the chair flying across the floor. He walks off and the camera zooms in on what he was looking at. It was a press release flyer for last nights slam. On it is Doug Troy, The Trashcan Man, the Polish Punisher, and Ivan with a knife going right through his head. Fade out.
