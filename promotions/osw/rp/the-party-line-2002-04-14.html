The Party Line

14 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/14/02 The scene fades up in a hotel hallway. Everything is as it should be: Quiet, calm, serene....save for a seven foot tall Russian and his diminutive girlfriend, Tempest, who laugh and carry on as they walk down the hallway. Stanislav brings his huge arm around the shoulders of Tempest and laughs loudly, 'DYAAHAAHAA!! So Tempest! What do you think of our great victory at Saturday Slam?! Did you hear the fans as they cheered for us? Did you see the look on Jane Reagan!?' Tempest giggles and says, 'I sure did. It's amazing. Like she didn't actually think anyone was gonna stop her and those creeps.' They reached a door and she dug out her key. 'And most important, it was fun!' Ivan smiles, 'Da, it was fun....not usually do I find wrestling to be...fun...but I believe that I was quite happy and...invigorated to say the least!' He grins wide as she unlocks the door. She steps through and leaves the door open, tossing her purse off to one side as she laughs and says, 'Are you trying to tell me you enjoyed it for its own sake? That it wasn't all about the mission and the cause and all that necessarily?' She looks at him with a mischievous smile. Ivan swallows and looks around quickly. He suddenly gets very grim looking as his face resumes it normal, granite like state. He mouths something and then caves in as a twisted grin covers his face, 'Alright, perhaps I did enjoy it more...and not think of mission...too much.' Ivan walks into the hotel room and looks around. Tempest laughs and flops down, kicking off her shoes and wiggling her toes. 'See? Some thing are just meant to be enjoyed, whether they have a point or not. Like making snow angels or running through sprinklers or playing with monkeys.' Ivan nods as she speaks, but as she mentions 'monkeys' he suddenly cocks his head to the side and puts his hands on his hips, 'Monkeys?' He grinds his jaw, 'What do you mean, playing with monkeys?' Tempest shrugs and says, 'Well I met the little monkey that Wang and them were all upset about and I can't figure out why. I mean he's cute, and really quite harmless.' She leans back and looks at him curiously. Ivan blinks twice, as if it hasn't sunk in yet, 'Wait...you...you mean you....' he pauses and clenches his fists, 'have played with it?' 'Well yeah,' Tempest says. 'He's really quite nice. I mean he's just... playful, sorta like me.' She winks with a smile. Ivan shakes his head, 'Have....' he stops and turns on his heels and takes two thunderous steps away and spins around, pointing a finger at her, 'have you paid attention to ANYTHING that is going on?' Tempest jumps, startled and says, 'Of course I have. What do you mean?' Ivan points at the wall, as if the monkey was just beyond it, 'That monkey has made a mess of MY Army! That monkey has no right to be in federation!! And that.. that....fool that is his keeper....' Ivan shakes his head and grabs his hair with one hand, 'How...how could you be so..so...naive???' Tempest sighs. 'Ivan, ZaC just nabbed my purse and I went and got it. It wasn't a big thing. Just like him bouncing into the locker room wasn't a big thing. You guys are great when you put your minds to something like stopping Jane Reagan and her little plots, but...' She shook her head in annoyance. 'You get so stuck on little stuff.' Ivan growls deep within his throat and punches a hole in the wall behind him. He bellows loudly, shaking the light fixture above him, 'LITTLE....STUFF???' Ivan roars, 'NOTHING that my army does is little, Tempest!! Nothing that I do is little! And that primate deserves to be burned alive and destroyed for what he has done!' Ivan growls and thunders towards the dining room, away from Tempest. Tempest takes a deep breath, hops up and follows him. Rounding the corner, she stares at his back and says, 'Ok, I'm sorry, but it's true. Why get bent out of shape by a little monkey business when you've still got Jane running things into the ground? I mean Reno's not a bad guy, and I told him he was wrong about the Red army too.' Ivan whirls around, 'This is the man that said to me that 'he would teach me lesson!'' He brings his huge hands into the air, 'Are you...are you siding with him against me?' Ivan squared his shoulders with Tempest, looming over her, 'Is that what you are saying? That because HE is not bad guy..I AM?' Tempest looked up at him and blinked her huge blue eyes, looking hurt. 'That's not what I said. Everyone's not the bad guy, just because they aren't in the Red Army. I wasn't in the Red Army, but you figured out I wasn't the enemy.' Ivan shakes his head and turns. He takes two steps around a dinner table, separating the two. He puts his huge hands on the table and leans forward, 'So, you play with this monkey that you KNOW that we are after, and do nothing about it? Consort with the enemy?' Ivan grits his teeth as his massive arms flex and suddenly, the table legs burst to the sides as he flattens the table. 'GRRRAAARRGGGH!!' He whirls around and looks out the window, breathing heavily. 'Consorting with the enemy?!' Tempest frowns and stalks over to him. 'Listen, I didn't know everyone I spoke to was subject to Red Army approval. I'm not a soldier and I don't work that way. It's not like I helped him do something against you. I tried to tell him he was wrong about the Red Army. And maybe if you let me do stuff like that without being al paranoid about it, the Red Army might have fewer enemies.' Ivan brought his hands up to the side of his head, 'So....you're saying that Ivan creates the enemies?' He walks past her, almost knocking into her. He walks back into the living room and turns around, 'I am not paranoid. But I do not talk to Jane Reagan because you do not like her! What would you say if I did that? HUH?' Ivan shakes with rage. A vein forms on the side of his neck that juts out as well as another vein along the side of his forehead. Tempest sighs and folds her arms, looking off to one side. 'You don't talk to Jane Reagan because you know she's the enemy. All I'm saying is you don't have to make one out of Davis and the monkey. Are they really that important?' She looks at him hopefully. 'YES! I do!' Ivan shakes his head, obviously not understanding why she can't get it, 'He invades OUR area....he pokes fun at us...he badmouths my army....' Ivan grabs a nearby vase, 'WHY DOES NO ONE UNDERSTAND!? I CANNOT BELIEVE I AM HEARING THIS FROM YOU!!' He whips the vase across the room into the wall behind Tempest. She jumps an takes a deep breath. 'Ivan, just because I talk to someone doesn't mean I'm abandoning you. I was trying to help the Army the best way I could think of.' She watches him cautiously and hugs herself as she says softly, 'I was hoping you'd understand.' Ivan growls and shakes, and then breathes out loudly through his nose, somewhat deflated, 'I....I just...' he turns and looks at the floor, 'perhaps you just do not understand, Tempest. Perhaps that is problem.' He growls, his anger overtaking him, and then his shoulders relax again. She shakes her head and says, 'I don't. I just... I'm different than you in that... ya have to go a long way to make me really mad. People makes an enemy of me, but I don't really make enemies of them. I just... I don't know how to fight with everyone around me all the time. But...' She dares to take a step closer to Ivan. 'Ivan it's not that I'm trying to undermine you or... hurt you.' Ivan turns, his rage still not completely ended, he takes a step closer to her, almost threateningly, 'So now what? What am I to do? If I tell you to stay away from him, I am great evil! If I tell you to continue to see him, I become laughing stock of that man?' He speaks louder, 'What am I supposed to do? Do what I want and therefore be some barbarian? Or give in to him and allow him to undermine me?' Tempest shook her head and said, 'See him? That's a bit of an overstatement. I just spoke to him once. That doesn't make me on his side Ivan. I'm on your side and I don't want you to doubt that.' She looks up at him once again, with those soft searching eyes. Ivan swallows as she looks up at him and blinks, 'Well.. I...I suppose....but...' his eyes darted around as his chest let out a sigh, suddenly not nearly as threatening as he was, 'yes, maybe you are right...' He blinks and looks at the floor. Tempest smiles very slightly and takes a step closer. 'I'll do my best not to fraternize with what you call the enemy. After all, it's too late with Davis anyway after what happened with him and Michel. Just... remember that I'm not used to that? Please?' Ivan nods and closes his eyes, suddenly very ashamed it would appear. His face flushes slightly, 'Yes, yes, I am sorry...Tempest...I should not have acted like that. I am sorry for my unacceptable behavior....' He turns suddenly and starts to head to the door. Tempest tilts her head and calls after him, 'Ivan, where are you going?' Ivan pauses at the door and turns slowly, 'I..I insulted you...I was out of turn and that was improper and.. unacceptable behavior on my part.' He fidgets and looks at the floor, 'It would only be right for me to leave...I have wrecked your apartment....I have been wrong.' He turns again at the door, 'In Russia I would never have been able to carry on so long.' Tempest shakes her head. 'Ivan, even if that's true, it doesn't mean I don't want you around. I just wanted everything to be ok again.' She smiles encouragingly. Ivan turns with a somewhat amazed look on his face, 'Really?' Tempest nods and steps toward him. 'Yeah, I mean, I can accept if you know it was wrong to do.' Ivan smiles suddenly and takes a step closer to her, 'Yes, I..I acted hastily, I should not have done that.' He takes another step closer to Tempest, 'I will fix the damage. I am sorry Tempest. I am ashamed of my actions.' She smiles and reaches up toward him as she says, 'Apology accepted. But there's a little more to making up. Holding me is very important.' Ivan nods and hugs her tight to him, 'Ivan is sorry. I did not mean to scare you. I do hope, however,' he pauses and looks down in her eyes, 'that you understand at least part of my cause for being upset.' She nods. 'I do. As long as you understand that I'm on your side, no matter what, then it's ok.' 'I know, I do not know what had gotten in to me,' Ivan smiles, 'after all, I suppose we are 'First Couple' of OSW, eh? We should not be fighting.' He smiles and rubs her back with his massive paw, which is about the size of her back. She sighs and snuggles against him happily. 'We are. And Jane and Switchy are never gonna forget it.' Ivan smiled down at her and closed his eyes as the scene faded.
