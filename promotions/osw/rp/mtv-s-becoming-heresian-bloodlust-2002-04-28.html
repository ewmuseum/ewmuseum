MTV's: Becoming Heresian Bloodlust.

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28.04.02 Boom, boom, boom....boom, boom, boom...The thumping base beats of the heavy dance music echoed throughout the bar as Walczak and Krieg sat on stools, looking around like they'd never seen people dancing before. Krieg turned round to his Red Army cohort with his thoughts on the tip of his tongue, “Learn to dance, you fools!,” 'Tom, this bar, it is like a jock-fest. Look at all these College asses. If I had the right mind, I would get up and show them how to dance,' claimed Krieg, slamming the bottom of his beer bottle on the bar, a few splashes of Carlsberg spilling out of the top of the bottle, 'Why wait Comrade, for I would show them to if I was not having so much fun drinking.' 'Dah, you are right, buddy. Why should we show these uncivilised American's our high-quality European dance moves? Let them rot in their disco hell,' Walczak downs another beer, and wipes the excess foam off of his mouth. 'Dancing is dancing, and I can see why people do it even if they are horrendously bad at it, but you know what I don't get? The reason behind Cain, and Hessian's fondness for ‘Satanism’' 'You share my view, Tom. I do not see the big deal in dressing up like a pumpkin and wearing black leather where it shouldn't be. <Sighing> Tom. I just thought of something Maybe we are out of touch with fashions. I know we are high-classed Europeans, but these damn Americans keep re-inventing themselves every two minutes,' 'Are you suggesting that we go and change our appearance just because of these damned Americans? Hell, I would rather spend a day in Hessians life.' A light bulb flashes above Krieg's head as he suddenly gets an outrageous but splendid idea, 'Tom, why don't we become Hessian and Cain for a day? What better way to source out our enemies strengths and weaknesses,' 'That may actually be a good idea.' Walczak thinks back to earlier in the day... 'I actually heard a group of lost American children meeting up at the nearby sump at midnight for some kind of Satanic ritual.' In a Bad Guy from Scoobie Doo-style voice, Krieg replies, 'Wait a minute! We can't just go to some ritual!....I need to get the proper attire first,' roared Krieg, a smile spreading across his face, 'I actually remember seeing a costume shop a couple blocks away. I just hope that they have extra large sizes, HAHAHAHAHAHAHA! Come on, laugh with me Comrade! HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA,” 'DWAHHAHA,' After a couple of minutes of laughing the two leave the bar and head out in search of the costume shop... The scene re-opens a couple of blocks away from the bar, with Krieg and Walczak lumbering out of a rather small taxi, Krieg tossing a pile of notes at the cocky taxi driver, 'Keep the change, you filthy animal,' shouts Michel, grinning from ear to ear. As Walczak looks at him in confusion, Krieg explains, 'It was in a flim, Ok,' 'Ahh, here it is Mr. Monster's Mystery Shop of Mayhem. Quite a clever use of alliteration, but I don't think that the filthy capitalist intended it to be so.' 'Yah, he probably thought it sounded scary,' said Krieg out loud, the two Communist's stepping over the shop's threshold as he does so, A man somewhere in his early sixties steps towards the two men. He has grey hair, and is wearing a flannel shirt with jeans. He has a pair of glasses on, and a pocket protector in his front pocket. 'Is there anything that I could help you with fellas,' 'Yah,' roared Krieg, the small man slightly taken back by his booming voice, 'We need Goth clothes,' 'Hhmm....Well what type of look are you guys going for?' 'We don't know the names, little man, or else we would have asked you for the specific clothes, fool,' said Krieg, the left of his mouth snarling slightly, 'Ok, well we have these hooded cloaks over here. This is a must have for every Goth.' Looking at Walczak, Krieg leans closer to his comrade, 'Tom I'm not going around dressed like the lovechild of the Grim Reaper and that guy from Hellraiser,' Laughing a little, Walczak turns round to Michel and puts him straight, 'Hey man. I know we're going to beat the living daylights out of Cain and Hessian, but what better way to mock them by being one of their own,' 'So, will this be it gentlemen?' 'Yes, and maybe some face paint.' 'Ha! Tom, we are going to look like giant versions of Michael Jackson with that stuff on our faces,' said Krieg, sniggering as he does, 'Well, we may as well put it on it may hide our laughter a bit more.' Roaring with laughter, Krieg and Walczak walk over to the counter with the two coats, some tins of face paint and some other weird and wonderful accessories... Minutes later.... Krieg and Walczak emerge from the shop, both ducking under the rather small door of the shop, stepping out into the daylight, their eyes twinging slightly as they adjust to the light having came from the dark and dingy shop, 'Ahh, damn, why did we put this crap on right away. We need somewhere to hide until midnight. That’s when the whole thing goes off.' In a flash like some sort of cheesy action-hero movie such as Batman, the two men whirl around in a whirlwind-style move and the scene suddenly shifts to their hotel room... Some knocking and banging can be heard as the two men both get changed in the two bathrooms. After a minute or so, the doors can be heard unlocking and the two men come out, standing side by side, looking straight into a tall mirror. The camera looks from the ground up and catch the tails of their coats, their legs and black studded boots.... Slowly the camera pans up as the two men turn around.... 'Krieg kill me please.' 'Tom...I actually think we look rather cool,' said Krieg, his tone rather different from earlier, Walczak looks at Krieg. 'Whose side are you on, if you actually think that we look good than I would recommend leaving right now, although wait these clothes are rather comfortable. Smooth on the skin you know what I mean,' 'You are right, my friend,' replied Krieg, stroking the leather of the fishnet vest he's got on, 'Well we should get going the sump is about ten minutes away on foot.' 'You are once again on the ball, my brother of Polish origin. Let's head to this 'ceremony',' said Krieg, as he walked towards the door in the long black coat, studded knee-high leather boots, black leather pants with white and silver lightning stripes down the sides, a fishnet vest with leather patches, a couple of chains and studded bangles and, to cap off the look, black and white face paint in the style of Kiss' frontman, Gene Simmons... Ten Minutes later the men reappear and are slowly trying to sneak into the ceremony that has already started. They make it to the back and they see that the man in the front is chanting some type of Satanic Hymn. 'Harep, Careb un Zeman a zu.' 'What in the name of Stanlin’s mother and Hitler's sister does that mean,' said a puzzled Krieg, The group looks over at Krieg and the man in front stops and then begins to speak to him. 'My, brother do you not know that tonight is the sacred sacrifice of the cat. That is the chant that we use for it,' The two behemoths look at each other and they begin to break out in laughter only to anger the group even more, 'What is so funny,' 'Why in God's name would you sacrifice a cat,” 'We are not doing it in God's name, but in Satan's,' 'Ah, so Cats are made in God's image,' mocked Krieg, 'Yes, they are, newcomer, brother,' 'So, what does a pig personify,' questioned Krieg, pushing the bounds of reasonable questioning to the max, The men started to stand up and start to dance around chanting something as their 'fearless' leader replies to Krieg; 'A pig, my friend, is God's most holy being! If you ever find a pig, you should torture the animal to show the Lord, as he calls himself, just how much of a fraud he is,' 'If he is a fraud then how could he create a pig?' 'Emm...He is not a fraud creator...He's...emm...A fraud leader, that's it! A Fraud leader I tell you,' screamed the gothic man, his followers shouting back, 'So you would be a real leader,' 'In this religion, my novice friend, there are no leaders...Leaders are the ones who control and preach...I am merely the figurehead our dark lord has chosen to pass on his message,' 'Emm...Yeah,' said Krieg, 'I think you need to have your head checked. And I am not your friend. I'm not a freak like you and your cohorts,' chipped in Walczak, 'So what are you two,' 'We are not freaks like you,' said Krieg as he and Walczak took a few steps towards the group, 'We are Communists! The true religion,' roared Walczak, slowly taking off his leather jacket, 'And we are going to show you Capitalists what Communists do to outcasts like you guys,' screamed Krieg, tearing the leather and he pulled his coat off viciously, 'But...We are not Capita....' The 'fearless' leader of the Gothic Brothers, as the sign above their clubhouse said [which was more like a garden shed], gets cut off by Krieg's paw around his throat and is suddenly hoisted up in the air as Krieg starts to bench press him, a couple of melted holy crosses and chains falling from the man. The other Gothic Brothers start to congregate opposite Walczak and Krieg, 'Don't move or else I swear that I will throw this freak into the fire,' roared Krieg 'Guys, we don't want to hurt you, but we will if you do not realise Jebadia without harming him,' said one of the Brothers in a high-pitched voice resembling the spotty kid who works in the cinema in the cartoon series, The Simpsons, 'Krieg, why is their leader named Jebadia. Is this some kind of Jewish cult,' Turning round slightly, Krieg launches 'Jebedia', if that's his real name, at the crowd of Goths, knocking them all down in a heap, 'I don't know Tom, but lets kick some of these Jewish Goth's asses,' 'I can't stand outcasts,' claimed Krieg as he grabbed a scrawny 20 year old and tosses him against the clubhouse, The two men continue to pummel the cult members tossing them in various directions, and some of them into the fire, 'I can't remember the last time that I had so much fun,' 'I can...It was when I broke up the Gay Rights march in Berlin...Oh, there was pom-poms, berets and pink shreds of clothing flying everywhere,' chuckled Krieg, 'Wish I was there, but I did have my own gay encounter with a bunch of Jesse Ramey fans. Maybe they were lovers, don't remember. Not quite as fun as yours though,' 'I am glad I leveled him during the last Pay-Per-View, comrade. I mean, what kind of schmuck expects to get anywhere in wrestling by dressing like an idiot....Oh wait, Hessian and Cain are in the category too,' chipped in Krieg, tossing a Goth over his head and onto a piles of seats previously used at the start of the ceremony/ritual, A Goth charges at Walczak who subsequently flips him into the nearby sewage, 'You know: I think that I have had enough fun for one night.' 'Yes, ally. Let's hit the road,' replies Krieg, the two Communists walking towards the main gates of the cemetery where the ceremony is set, tossing the remaining Goths who charge at them out of the way. A couple of Goths stand in front of them for a second or two, but suddenly come to their senses and run towards the gate at full speed, sobbing loudly, The scene changes and Walczak and Krieg are now back at their hotel room. They are seen washing the paint off their faces, 'How, Hessian and Cain can subject themselves to such a pathetic life is beyond me,' 'Yah, Tom. I can not see the advantages of worshipping some fake god, cutting up helpless animals and dancing around...To be honest, worshiping a fake god sounds like Propaganda....Cutting up helpless animals sound like our past in-ring experience with Hessian and Cain...and dancing around. Well that's just sounds like the rest of the OSW, dah,' giggles Krieg, sounding like a school girl.... 'What was that?' 'Emm...I don't know Walczak. Let's check it out,' 'Dwahaha....Oh, you were talking about me? I just crack myself up sometimes,' says Krieg, but seeing Walczak's serious look, he changes his tone, 'But I'm still serious about killing those freaks Heresian Bloodlust,' 'Yes, well I think that all we need to worry about now is where we are going to sleep cause there is no way in hell that I am sleeping on the couch again,' Krieg smiles, 'Yah, comrade. As always, you provide the best advice,' patting Walczak on the shoulder as he walks over to the bed, 'Wait! Where are you going! Don't even think about...Oh damn....He did it to me again,' grimaced Walczak, Chuckling, Krieg climbs into the bed as Walczak shakes his head and draws his hand over his forehead, 'If you are not fast, my friend, you are last,' Walczak walks out of the room with his head held low, and he lays down on the couch as the scene fades out...
