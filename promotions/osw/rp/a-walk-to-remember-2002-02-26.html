A walk to remember...

26 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/26/02 Frankie walked through the cold streets of New York City. Brooklyn streets have a tendency of being deserted at night so he was alone with his thoughts. He walks the streets huddled in a black pea coat, pulled up around his neck. His bald head is covered with a black wave cap, and he is wearing jeans. As he walks with his head lowered in thought, mist rises with every breath. His mind wanders...'So, the kidnapper has spoken. Who the hell can it be? I am convinced it is someone in PTC, probably someone in OSW. Odds are it is someone very close to be. I went to the ring Saturday night by myself, so the possibilities are endless. No way it's Drew or Switchy...or is it? Hell I don't know. I've never been good at trust. Those bastards have helped me out a ton though, there's no way it's them...probably...' His thoughts trail away and fade back as he walks. There is no real direction to his thoughts, they are scattered and almost incoherent. He pays no attention to where he's going. Right on this street. Left here. Straight through three intersections. None of it registers, he is getting lost, and he doesn't even notice. He passes many sketchy individuals that would send most people running in the other direction, yet he doesn't even notice. These people know better then to try anything on Frankie, if they didn't know of him before OSW, they certainly do now. Word gets around your home town very quickly, and Brooklyn is no different. The further Frankie gets from his apartment building, the more focused his thoughts get. He thinks...'Well, he said that I have annoyed him for a while. That he is sick of me sticking my nose where it doesn't belong. Who could it be? Ivan? Na, he's got bigger fish to fry. Jesse Ramey? Na, that cross dresser is too busy putting on make up to think of anything like this. Rhett Locke? I wouldn't put it past him. The man is demented like no other. He definitely has the mind to cook up something like this, but it just doesn't fit. Damn, what all did the bastard say? I got two weeks to figure this out or he kills the boy. DAMN!' The rage consumes coherent thought. Frankie walks, head down, down a dark deserted street. An unseen voice rings out... 'Follow your heart, read the clues...' it whispers. Frankie stops cold in his tracks. His eyes frantically searching the darkness for the source of the voice. 'What?' he asks. 'The one you hate, is the one you seek.' Answers the voice. Frankie walks towards the source of the voice. He searches long and hard. He finds no one. He stands, scratching his head... 'Who the hell was that? Follow my heart? Read the clues? The one I hate is the one I seek? What the hell?' Frankie thinks frantically. 'The ONE I hate? Could he be any more vague?' Frankie begins walking back towards his apartment building. His mind fills with the words of the kidnapper...'He hates me...he had a lot of time to think?' Just then he passes a pawn shop with televisions in the window. He sees that one of them is playing a news clip on the release of Michael Dame from prison and it has him at his autograph signing. Frankie snickers as he reads the closed captioning of Dame claiming his innocence and whining more and more. He reads the words as they cross the bottom of the screen, 'Well, it feels good to get out, it was hard time knowing that I was innocent. But, I had a lot of time to think...' Frankie's eyes open wide, his jaw drops open. 'A LOT OF TIME TO THINK?!?!' Frankie turns and glares down the street. 'Dame' he mutters. Frankie sprints towards his apartment building, and fades into the dark street.
