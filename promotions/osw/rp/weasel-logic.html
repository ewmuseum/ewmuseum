Weasel logic

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Begin File.] “Excellent, this unit is satisfied. Units Chico and Gunmetal may leave.” The robotic wrestlers Gunmetal and Chico take their leave of their heartless (literally!) master Cobol. Carefully, Chico closes the boiler room door behind him. “Duh?” “I take it you have questions?” “Only about EVERYTHING! Why isn’t Cobol pounding us into convenient bite size bits right about now?” Gunmetal drags out a large steamer trunk from behind the couch before answering, “Look, it is very simple. Tell me, what was our mission?” Chico tilts his head questioningly, “Are you serious?” “Humor me.” Chico shrugs. “Okay, we were supposed to bump off the Vax and bag a working Pentium X chip, same as always.” Gunmetal nods. “Correct. And we have done exactly that! From a certain point of view…” “Come again? We didn’t even kill one of the fleshy bastards, much less all of them!” “So what? Who says we had to kill them?” Chico scratches his head, producing a horrible nails-on-chalkboard kind of screech. “Cobol did, last time I checked.” “Nope, he did not. He told us to terminate the human designated as the ‘Vax’.” “Same difference.” “Consider this, now that all the various humans designated Vax have revealed their true identities to the public at large, which is currently the Vax?” “All of them.” “My foolish companion,” Gunmetal shakes his head sadly, “not a single one of them goes by the name Vax anymore. And if none of them are identified as Vax…” The LED of comprehension ignites in Chico’s cameras. “Then the Vax no longer exists!” “Exactly.” “That logic’s so twisty it shoulda came from a contortionist-lawyer-pretzel vendor.” Gunmetal spreads his hands expressively. “I try.” “But what about the chip?” “This chip?” Gunmetal holds out the small square of metal with all the flourish of a magician sawing his assistant in half. A bright red ‘X’ decorates the shiny metal surface. “Bah! That’s just that cheap copy we pawned on him before. He’d never fall for it again... wouldn’t he?’ Gunmetal slowly rotates the chip in the in the dim basement lights, causing it to glint and sparkle. “Would he not? Especially considering that this one will work… sort of.” Chico takes the chip and examines the underside closely… “Epoxy? What kind of chip did you glue to this thing?” A mischievous sparkle dances in Gunmetal’s main sensor. “Go ahead… try it.” Chico hesitates. “Umm… I’m not gonna burn out, am I?” “Nope. Guaranteed.” “Umm… okay.” Snick! The little square snaps into place. “Testing chip… got it… running the chip… wakka-wakka-wakka-wakka… the hell?! What is this thing?” Gunmetal opens up the large steamer trunk. Inside is a huge assortment of human clothing, wigs, and miscellaneous bric-a-brac. “It is an old program called Pac-Man.” “This isn’t any good for taking over the world! The boss’ll reject it in three seconds, tops!” “Quite the contrary. He will take it, he will test it and he will thank us for a job well done.” “Him? Thank US?!” “Okay, maybe that is something of an overstatement, but he will register that our primary missions will have been performed successfully. After all, do YOU know what it is that the C.L.O.D. system will do with the chip?” “He’ll use it to control all the robot soldiers we have in place around the world.” Gunmetal holds up one finger. “And how will he do that?” “How the hell am I supposed to know that?” “You are not. And neither does Cobol. All he knows is that he is supposed to get this working X chip to the C.L.O.D. system by any means possible. Whether it works as advertised does not even enter into his thought processes.” Chico’s fist curls victoriously around the faux-chip. “Those damn humans… always lying and making false claims about the quality of things!” “Indeed. In this case, Intel has over hyped the potency of their product about as much as The Westsidaz have overstated THEIR ability!” Chico idly tosses the chip up and down like a coin, flipping it end over end. “Nice segue. Smooth as an earthquake.” Gunmetal shrugs. “It pays the bills. Now try this on.” He holds up a novelty Groucho moustache-rubber nose and glasses combo. Chico takes the cheesy disguise. “What’s this for?” “For an educational film I am doing. Now try it on.” “Umm… okay.” [End File.]
