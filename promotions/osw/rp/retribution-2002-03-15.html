Date: 15 March 2002 Down a dark and twisting hallway, antique oil paintings adorn the solid block walls. Every third painting, lying mounted on the wall and held by skull-like looking objects, lit torches dimly illuminating this musty corridor. From time to time the sporadic conversations of others can be heard, temporarily interrupting the silence. A bit further down on our journey, we find it very hard not to notice the massive amounts of red stains that appear in a particular section of the hallway. Upon closer examination we determine that these stains is in actuality, blood; however the origins and subsequent events leading to this “donation”, remains a mystery. Eventually after many twists and turns one may encounter while traveling down this pseudo maze of hallways, a pair of reinforced steel doors soon will block any further progression. These two reinforced steel doors rise from the floor almost up to the ceiling, being just shy by about six inches. On either side of the door, guarding it, stands two individuals wearing dark brown robes, their faces hidden behind the hoods they wear. As we get closer to the door, the sounds of heavy metal colliding with another can be heard as the door slowly creaks open. We pass through the doors and a giant circular table, made of chromed out steel, sits in the center of this room. Surrounding the table lays twelve chairs, made of black Corinthian leather. Directly behind said chairs, affixed to the wall are twelve oil paintings, of them being paintings of eleven men and one woman, Countess Sasha Bordeux. Finally at each corner of this great room, standing motionless, are red robed individuals and again, the hoods they wear conceal their faces. Behind them stands a silver pedestal with a lit black candle on it. Minutes later the sound of a bell can be heard, echoing through this unknown structure, as a door on the opposite side of the room opens and twelve black robed entities enter the room and proceed to their assigned chair and sit down, all of this is done in total unison. A few seconds will pass when they all, simultaneously, reach up and pull back the hoods on their heads, revealing themselves and their identities, the heads of the twelve families, and Vampir Nosferatu is among one of them. He is the first to speak. “I trust your journey here was as eventful as mine but now is the time to discuss many pressing issues that have been pushed to the side for far too long.” The others sitting around the table look to one another, confused and somewhat perplexed as to what Vampir is leading into. “As you all are aware, I am no longer solely Vampir, nor am I solely Maskimxul, I am however, a combination of the two. The qualities of two, merged into one, yet, I still keep the name, Vampir Nosferatu. Our minds, our strengths, and our abilities, are now as one. With this rebirth, comes new and much needed changes. Changes I am sure none of you will oppose.” Vampir scans the room until his eyes lockup with the eyes Lord Flavio Versailles. “Some of us here in this room, have had our differences throughout the course of time, differences which must be put aside in order to complete what we all set out to accomplish centuries ago.” “Hay mon, whatcha meen put aside our deeferences. There eez matters agenst me family which have gone unsettled and I deemond retribution.” Exclaims Lord Nigel Whitfield, as he peers over to Lord Versailles. “As there are some against my family, Lord Whitfield. However, I am willing to set my differences aside in order to accomplish what our forefathers set out to do”, responds Vampir. “But Lord Nosferatu…” “Business is business, Lord Whitfield, it’s not personal” Vampir returns his eye contact with Lord Versailles, “what is in the past, stays in the past. If we continue to dwell on these issues, much like we all have for centuries now, a great civil war will erupt within us, destroying us all from the inside. Our greatest enemy is ourselves.” “I deesagree…” “Trust, Lord Whitfield, have trust. In time, I assure you, you will achieve your satisfaction.” “You had bettah be right Lord Nosferatu.” Vampir returns a smile and motions for Lord Whitfield to take his seat, for everything is all right. Vampir looks around the room and continues, “Now, onto the business at hand…for centuries we have been trying to annihilate humanity, to rid ourselves of this cancer, but unfortunately, here we are in the two thousandth and second year and they still remain, and quite bountiful at that.” Vampir pauses as he observes twelve white robed individuals enter the room and place a silver chalice in front of each person. Vampir grabs hold of the chalice and sips the bloody contents. Countess Sasha Bordeux breaks the silence, as she doesn’t wait for the white robed individuals to leave in order to speak, “ Ahhh, zis is deleecious, Kennedy 1964, such a good year zat vas, no.?” “Indeed it was, Countess Bordeux, you were always quite the connoisseur.” responds Lord Octavian Augustes, lifting his glass in the air, toasting. Countess Bordeux raises her glass in response as Vampir continues now that they are all alone again. “As I was saying, although humanity remains here today, there is a greater evil lurking in the shadows, trying to destroy us all. These are in fact the blasphemers, the ones who claim to do their deeds in the name of darkness or in some cases, the name of that sadist up there…god. We must unite our forces, sparing humanity for the time being, and turning our attention to these heathens, these individuals who believe they can ride on our coattails.” “Eet will nevah workah. Theez hoomans will nevah allowah satch a eunitee. I ahgree, theez blahspheemahs requiah our immediate disposahl, but, the hoomans will take this opportoonitee to destroy us from behind. They cannaht ahnd will naht be trustid.” Says a whispery and hoarse voice at the end of the table. “Lord Mortis, we cannot continue to live in the past, we must adapt and evolve from our past ideologies, or face the possibility of defeat and subsequently, our extinction.” “NO” a hand slams down on the table, “for centchoories vee haf lived in hiding, refoosing to ahsociate ourselvsah with thah very entitee that we use for food. They vill keel ahs if geeven the opportoonitee.” “That is a scenario that I am very willing to risk taking.” “Eef yourah Fahthere culd here you nowah, he wuld be so deesapointed in you. Ah faliah he wuld believe he is.” “Well, my father is not here, now is he?” Vampir looks over and makes eye contact with Lord Versailles. Versailles turns his head and immediately breaks the eye contact with Vampir. “What hahpenned to yer fahthere ees stillah mysteree, any accusations are in fact…oonfounded.” “Well, that is your opinion now isn’t it?” Vampir returns his burning stare to Lord Mortis, “I however, do know the true story now. You seem to forget that Vampir and Maskimxul are as one, any experiences they may have had is now in essence, shared.” Vampir pauses for a few seconds with nothing but a grin on his face, “Now, let us begin the preparations for this first blasphemer that requires our immediate, and joint attention. His name is fitting as to the nature of the words that expel from his mouth, Propaganda.” A slight whisper and side bar occurs between Countess Bordeux and Lord Whitfield, “Lord Nosferatu…” Nigel speaks up, “ Is this tha same mon who attacked you unprovoked last Sunday at that wrestling event. Da won who proclaims his ahctions are in tha name of that sadist of a peasent, God” “Indeed” Countess Bordeux sits up in her chair, “ Iz zis thee same Propeahgandah that wears black pants wit ze white cwoss on hees leg?” “Indeed” “Ah yes, he iz a coote one, I shall enjoy playing with heese mind.” “Countess, I would like for you, to introduce yourself to Mr. Propaganda in your very special way. Show him the true meaning of faith. Be sure when you deal with him, to make the last temptation of Christ seem like a kindergarten nap break. Attack his mind, begin breaking down and stripping him of all his beliefs, have him wipe his ass with that book of his. Have him cry to the won he worships.” “Zis vill not be a problem, in due time I vill be that irresistible lust zat he vill vorship.” Vampir nods in approval with a smile slowly making his way onto his face when Lord Xian Xi asks him a question,” Lord Nosferatu, and when do you propose shall we begin this war against the blasphemers of evil?” “Well, Lord Xi, we have already begun.” “How do you mean?” “I have already sent my consigliore, Tiamat, to the root where he will give Mr. Propaganda a gift from me.” Vampir’s eyes suddenly begin to glow red as a grin appears. We get closer and closer to his red eyes when a sudden bright flash occurs. We find ourselves in Clarkesville, Tennessee as we see someone walking with a briefcase down a dirt road; however, we are blind as to who this person is. We then see the feet of this person come to a series of brick steps, which he then climbs and subsequently opens a weather worn white door. The individual steps inside and begins to walk on an old and dusty hard wood floor as he passes pew after pew. It is now that the locale becomes evident, the inside of a church, the epitome of the word propaganda. The man walks about halfway down when he decides to step into the pew and have a seat midway down. He places the briefcase on the ground and raises his arms, resting them on the back of the pew. “Clarkesville, Tennessee, the location made famous by the Monkeys. This church, an epitome of propaganda itself, and speaking of propaganda an ideal location to thank the men themselves who were responsible for the atrocious attack on Vampir Nosferatu at Crowning Glory. Hmmmm, interesting how these people conduct themselves.” The man gets up and slides out of the pew and begins to walk back towards and out the door. Once outside he reaches into his coat pocket and pulls out a pair of black Anarchy sunglasses and puts them on. It is at this time the identity of the man is revealed…Tiamat. He walks about a hundred yards away from the church when he stops and smiles, the old white church visible over his right shoulder. He looks down at his watch and while continuing to smile he says, “ Boom.” Suddenly the old white church erupts in a ball of flame, engulfing the entire structure. Tiamat then proceeds to walk as he whistles the aforementioned Monkey’s tune,” Last Train to Clarksville.”
