A Secret Wish

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-25-02 Star light, star bright where ever death is, let it spread its blood to me, let it take away whats left of me, let it whisper to me and tell me im dead, let it bleed and feed my thoughts. I slip in to my bed after my silent prayer is wished in to the sky, next to Cameron do I dare to lay. My face is bruised, my body aching from the rough nights of pleasure for him and pain for me. I wince and cower as I feel him move against me, but I release my held breath when noticing he was merely moving in his sleep. With a shiver I close my eyes, hoping tomorrow will never come. I think I may be pregnant, and that is what worries me while I try to fall in to oblivion. I cannot bring a child in to this type of relationship, I can't and I won't..But silly me. I am working myself up over nothing of course, I mean right? I can't be pregnant, I can't! It is just the past few nights of no sleep getting to me. But even as I slip in to my half asleep, half awake realm I worry on this topic, my hand resting on my stomach as many things run through my mind. The first rays of a light I wish did not shine my way wake me in the morning. A day I dread, a day I wish were taken from me. But to my joy I find Cameron has woken and left this morning, no beating so early and perhaps the time to catch up with my sleep. Sadly my thoughts flash through my head and from them I begin to worry once more. My hand comes up from my stomach and I look it over, I couldn't even tell it belonged to me. The tracks were no longer in my arms alone, but spreading to other parts of my body. A baby couldn't have this type of life. I throw the covers from my sweat soaked body and flee to the bathroom. The door is slammed and locked as I throw myself to my knees and rumage through the counter spaces. A test is pulled from the back, I keep them around for occassions such as this. The package is ripped open and I proceed to do as the instructions read. Minutes pass, seemingly like hours to me as I wait for a result which could break me entirely. As the time runs out for me I take the test in to my hand, licking over my parched lips as I read what it has in store for me. My eyes well up and the test is dropped from my hands.. Positive
