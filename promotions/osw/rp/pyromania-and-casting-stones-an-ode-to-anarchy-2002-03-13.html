Pyromania and Casting Stones, an Ode to Anarchy

13 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Mar. 13, 2002 Odd, isn't it? One minute, I'm on top of the world as OSW champion. The next I'm another in the masses, claiming that Anarchy isn't all it seems. Sure, one might get the idea that with all the chaos that associates itself with some anarchists, that it might just be chaos itself. Of course, that's where many are wrong. With George W. Bush and the rest of the bigwigs 'valiantly' taking pride in this great nation and firing back at Osama Bin Laden and his cult of a demonination of Islam, it's hard to continue to believe the government doesn't protect you. Again, your ass is wrong. I don't care what your stance on cloning embryos are, I don't care. Would you rather find a cure for cancer, and other diseases, or have everyone falling out left and right, flowers falling at funerals hour after hour, and lines of vehicles along with a hurse making a trip to the morg by the second? Ring around the rosies. Pockets full of posies. Ashes. Ashes. We all fall down. How true. And secondly, if Congress, the House, the Cabinet, the whole fricken kitchen, hell I dunno. Regardless, if they cared that much about murder, you'd think abortion clinics would be falling out left and right. What would you rather see: human embryos dieing or human babies dieing? Exactly. And I may be your international champion, I don't give jack shit what you or anyone else thinks about me anymore. I'm going back to my roots. Back to Pyromania and Casting Stones. *** We all heard the sirens approaching, and like cockroaches in the light after opening a droor, we ran. We ran as fast as we f*ckin' could. Ironic enough, the running part of it is more chaotic than the actual crime. I took an opposite route than the rest, thinking the police would be likely to follow them. Twelve or so more had the same idea i did, and it ended up parting into five or six groups, the one I was running with being the smallest. The pigs didn't chase us, we made damn sure we wouldn't be 'defenders of the faith', as they are called. Not tonight we weren't. Nope, we got off the hook, three of the other groups were chased, two of them at more people arrested than my entire group. We ran through the woods as fast as possible, those pigs wouldn't find us out there. Briars and thorns scraped us up as we ran relentlessly through those woods behind the Mayor's building. 'There they are!' 'Get 'em!' We heard the cops yell, and dogs barked. I hopped up on the branch of a near-by tree, pulling a younger pre-teened kid up with me- I wouldn't let a damn kid go to jail for arson. He was scruffy looking, red hair and blue eyes, looked irish even. He spoke as the rest were still scrambling away from the police. 'Than-' I interupted his dumb ass by holding an index finger to my mouth. The crackle of leaves via footsteps went through our ears, and we silently climbed higher into the tree, where the leaves would cover us. A flash-light hit my eyes, i squinted, but didn't say a word. The boy was shivering, I placed a gentle hand on his shoulder to calm him down. The flash-light soon passed. After nearly an hour of staying in the tree, we climbed down. We got away scot-free Scott-free of Arson to a government building. How delightful.
