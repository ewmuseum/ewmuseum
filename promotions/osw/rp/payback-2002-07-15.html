Payback

15 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-15-02 Immediately following his meeting with Aimz, in which he apologized for his injustices caused to her, and perhaps built a small bridge between the two, Ivan Stanislav had planned on checking into a small hotel in Tulsa and to contemplate his next move. However, he never made it out of the hospital. Would Ivan Stanislav please report to the hospital lobby. Ivan Stanislav to the hospital lobby. droned through the hallway of the hospital, via an intercom, instantly warranting Ivan’s attention. Who would be paging Ivan Stanislav in a Tulsa hospital? The answer? Igor Karpov. Karpov had appeared in the past and was well known within Russia. The up and coming lieutenant of the Russian Army, Karpov was the new gold star of Russia. A capitalist, and opportunist, and anything but an idealist. When Stanislav was his age, he could be considered an Igor Karpov, but now, the tables had turned. In the lobby stood Ivan Stanislav of the “Old Guard” and Igor Karpov of the “New Order.” Igor Karpov sat nonchalantly in a comfortable chair. Upon seeing Stanislav, the young lieutenant stood up and scowled at him. The last time they had met, Ivan had almost killed Karpov out of blind rage. Karpov would not let that opportunity present itself again. Ivan blinked, somewhat stunned to see Igor Karpov in a Tulsa Hospital, “Karpov? What are you doing here?” Karpov ran a hand through his jet black hair and fixed his uniform, “We need to talk, Praporschik.” Ivan growled and nodded at Karpov, “There is quite place down hallway, follow me.” Stanislav turned and lumbered down a hallway, almost bowling over a nearby nurse in the process. Karpov followed behind him until Stanislav came to a room and opened the door, “After you, Lieutenant.” He growled, much venom in his words. Karpov entered the small room which only had a table and a chair, as well as a mirror on a far wall. Ivan followed and shut the door. “Now, what are you doing here?” he asked, “Why have you come?” “It has been long time since you’ve been in Russia,” muttered Karpov, “and we were wondering just what you were doing.” “I am wrestling, what do you think Ivan is doing?” Ivan growled back, “We have busy schedule, and it is too difficult to go from Arkhangelsk to venue every week!” “You are not, avoiding Russia, Praporschik?” Karpov asked back, staring straight at Stanislav. His height and size did intimidate him, but he decided to take the chance. “What would make you….” Stanislav began, but Karpov talked over Ivan. “We saw what you said last week, Praporschik. Getting rid of your Red Army and claiming that not only were they some mystical problem, but also that…..Russia was.” Karpov glared at Ivan. Stanislav crossed his arms over his chest. “You said Russia was some sort of poison? Puppeteers? What were you talking about?” Karpov continued, “We are concerned, that is all.” “Lieutenant Karpov, I said what I said, and I meant it! A man is entitled to his opinion, is he not?” Ivan growled. It was funny to hear this from the lips of Ivan Stanislav, who would normally say that the State creates the opinion of the worker. Karpov snapped back, “Nyet! When you work under the power of Russia and with the Russian Military’s money, you have opinions that we set!” He stopped and then continued, “It is not our opinion that The Russian Federation is poison, Praporschik! Have you lost your mind?” For some reason, a great anger welled up within Ivan Stanislav. He took a step forward and threw a chair across the room, “Igor Karpov!! Ivan has NOT lost his mind, but rather regained it!!” Karpov recoiled into a defensive position but hollered back, “Praporschik! I will not forget this outburst!!” The door to the room opened as a nurse poked her head in. Stanislav whirled around, “GET OUT!!” She screamed and shut the door. Karpov straightened his uniform, “If this is the way that you wish to handle things, Praporschik Stanislav, then I am forced to take some sort of action.” Ivan turned and looked at him, “Action?” “That is correct! I have come to review your actions, behavior, and most importantly, your intentions.” He straightened himself, “You have not been to Arkhangelsk in over one week! You have blasted Russian Federation on national American television. You have disobeyed orders and NOW you have threatened a commanding officer.” Ivan stood stunned as he continued, “It is my opinion, Praporschik, that you are not fit to command in Arkhangelsk….” “WHAT?!” roared Stanislav. “…and therefore, I am suspending you from your duties until you can figure out just what it is you are doing.” Karpov grinned and started to walk out of the room, finished dealing his blow. Ivan turned just as Karpov reached the door, still stunned, “What are you talking about? I have been in Arkhangelsk for years! This is how the Russian Military repays me?!” Karpov turned slowly, grinning crookedly, “Look around you. The times have changed, and you cannot catch up with them now. Welcome to reality, Prap…er…Ivan.” He turned back to the door and opened it. Karpov stood with his back to Ivan and whispered, “I told you I would get you back, Stanislav.” He chuckled and walked out of the room leaving a stunned Russian Bear staring at the door.
