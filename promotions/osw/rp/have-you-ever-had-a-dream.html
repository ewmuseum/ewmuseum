Have you ever had a dream?

undated

Amy 'Aimz' Campbell

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Note: I've got a feeling that, had what happened to me on Thursday evening not have been mentioned (Not eWrestling related.), I would've put a lot more effort into this PPV match...sorry Ron and anybody else who it may concern. I'll hopefully be in full-swing, or as close to it as I ever am, on Monday. :) )) Have you ever had a dream? Or is life just a trip? A trip without chances A chance to grow up quick. -Guano Apes, ''Open Your Eyes'' Amy Campbell has two addictions... Number one: Wrestling. Number two: Music. Drug problem? I don't think so! The girl occasionally dabbles in narcotics for social purposes...what's so wrong with that? Everybody's done something drug-related at one point or another, no matter how hard they try to deny it. Amy's no different than any of you 40-something 'parental figureheads'...when you were nineteen, you were doing this shit too...don't bother pretending you didn't and let the girl learn from her own mistakes. Aimz a bright girl...she doesn't need every parenting group, censorship organization and bible-banging association acting like they own her...they DON'T tell her what to do and even though they try, it's not like she's listening. Think what you'd like about her...she wants you to. It's a piece of the cultures she chooses to meld, it helps make her who she is and if she's gotta listen to a bunch of pansies bitching at her for the rest of her life than so be it, but she's not stopping. Who the hell needs those parent groups anyway? Shouldn't they be off policing the use of men shaped like mushrooms in the Super Mario games? Or bitching about how Queen Amidala isn't quite an equal in the household of the new Star Wars movie? What business did they REALLY have to be pestering somebody like Amy? Sure, she was praising 'wrong' things...but even though she was a public figure, talking about 'the evils of the world' wasn't a crime! She was upset about the world, sitting on the bed of her hotel room... Amy gazed out the window distantly, barely taking notice of the trucks speeding by below, or the red lights of an Ambulance probably racing towards some old lady who thought she was having a heart-attack. None of that really got through to her...then again, she had a lot less practical knowledge of the world than she'd ever let on. Sure, if you gave her a problem, scientific, physical or mathematical, she'd create an abstract image in her mind and work through it that way...but if you asked her how to fix a tire, it'd be ''Damned if I know!''. Oh well, she wasn't distant this evening because she was on drugs, she wasn't distant because she was drunk...she wasn't even distant because she was sick. She was distant because she was thinking harder than ever before...Amy couldn't use any system, any little maze in her head to work out her REAL problems...things like her father's illness, the fact that Rent-A-Hero was a maniac or Drew Hostyle's lies... She could solve her Hostyle problem, though...that chance came on Sunday, if he didn't have the decency to let her have her reputation now, she'd just have to beat it out of him somehow...he knew he was lying, SHE knew he was lying...now the world needed to know he was lying. Otherwise, Amy'd never be able to look a kid in the eye without that child wondering how many forms of herpes she had...even the thought of touching Drew Hostyle made her shudder, it was just disgusting...she didn't need every person on earth thinking she'd actually done something so sick. ''Fuck this...'' Was all Amy really had to say, laying back against the bed cross-wise. ''I won tonight.....I'm fine.......I'll do it again tomorrow, damnit!'' She and Reno Davis had won a brawl against Hostyle and Bones...because of DQ thanks to Sebastian Toomes' interference, but they had won none-the-less. Head On Collision was fast approaching and she had toppled Drew once...twice...three...four...five times? She wasn't entirely sure...but it was safe to say that, before Switchy came out, Amy was practically decimating Hostyle; The man just wasn't too bright. The good part was that she now knew FOR SURE that he could be toppled, quite easily in fact. The bad part was that she hadn't done what she needed to do the next night: Make him submit. It couldn't be THAT hard...he was lying about her and she needed to expose that by making him admit to it...so what? The truth would come out sooner or later, so tonight she'd either tear him to pieces, possibly with a bit of assistance, or he'd break down in later life and admit that he never got a chance to bone Campbell. After Sunday, she'd go back home for a few days, rest up and visit her family for a bit...if Rent-A-Hero was available, she'd introduce him to everyone. It's not like she wouldn't be walking...right? At the thought of walking, the ankle Toomes had battered the week prior had throbbed a bit...an innocent reminder that she wasn't exactly one hundred percent. But still, things weren't so bad! She hadn't used any substances at all for the last few days, she was resting up, she was getting lots of fluids...and not just from Rent-A-Hero... This WOULD happen, she had to win...her head was going back and forth between the certainty and the ''Oh...well I could use to pick up a little victory''...but Amy knew what had to be done, right? Right... ...Was she becoming, God forbid, an optimist?!
