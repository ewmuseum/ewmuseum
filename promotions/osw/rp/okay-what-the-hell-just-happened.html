''Okay...what the hell just happened?''

2002-03-17

Amy 'Aimz' Campbell

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Note: Experimenting with a new style, might not be great...work with me, folks.) Before we go any further, how do you get a pile of dead babies into a jar? A blender. How do you get that pile of dead babies OUT of the jar? Nachos. Okay, now that you're sick over the concept of eating oozie dead babies, this might seem like a good piece of work... And thus, we begin. Saturday Night Slam...Timo Bolamba, two terms I don't think I'll ever be allowed to forget. One, the show that marked my debut in THE federation...the highest point in my career so far; March Sixteenth of the year two thousand and two. Slam was THE night, OSW is THE federation and Timo's THE man. Comrade extraordinaire? Could be, he's a good friend none-the-less. What's between the radical Samoan and I? Nothing more than good friendship with a common bond: Hatred of the undeserving individuals who seem to always be at the top. I still wanna know what the hell happened earlier this evening. I can remember Timo, a woman, someone I think was Sebastian Toomes and some big thing nearly falling on me. Pretty nautious experience. I don't think any pain's really set in yet, which is probably why I've got the gall to walk into this Seven-Eleven still decked out in my sweaty ring gear... Whoa, cute guy at the counter... That in mind, Jason instantly invaded my thoughts...where the hell was he now? God, I hope I hadn't left him back at the arena...that'd spell certain death, he really hated feeling left-behind. No, wait...didn't he stay at the hotel? Or was he back in Nova Scotia? GAH! I hate thinking too much. Oh well, here I was...now what'm I looking for? Hmm...okay, better go back to not thinking, things MUCH better that way! Gyah! Someone was speaking to me, sounded like a guy. ''Amy! Congrats!'' Eep! What the fuck?! Okay...creepy guy hugging me...make it stop! I pushed him away slightly, just enough to break the embrace without being overly rude. I don't like being touched by strangers. ''Uhhh...hey.....'' Must think of name, must think of name! ''...fella....'' Did I know this guy? Obviously he knows me... Wait, nevermind...I'm a wrestler, everybody who's anybody (Fat and smelly, that is.) knows who I am. ''I'm SUCH a huge fan!'' A what?! Hot damn, I've got a fan! Oh, wait...I've also got tits, I think that earns me all the perverts by default. Oh well, he knows my name, at least. ''Thanks...buddy.'' When I looked him over, I realized he wasn't the usual wrestling fan...this guy seems to have recently taken a bath! ''How long've you followed my career?'' Heh, look at me...I'm a big girl with a career now! I only then realized how badly that dude needed a bottle of Listerine. ''Since you were just a lil' one!'' What the fuck kinda answer was that? ''I see...'' Grrr, need music. Looking towards the discman flashing 'LOW BATTERY' from the pocket of these supremely baggy Air-Force rip-offs, the calculations of how much cash was in my other pocket began. ''Well...uhhh...yeah. I'm just here to buy batteries, so I'll see ya around, 'kay?'' ''Yeah!'' ''Right on.'' Must...escape...people. There was a rack of batteries towards the front, I remembered from coming in and, by the time I managed to drag my ass over there, I was pulling a pack of two Double-A Energizers off the stand. Time to encounter cute counter boy! When I walked up to him, it became obvious that he wasn't nearly as 'cute' up close as he was from a far but big damned deal. He was too preppy anyway; neatly gelled, bleached out hair, perfectly shaven and the little green uniform sure as hell wasn't turning my crank. Meh, I don't expect to walk into convenience stores to find anything more than ance-ridden virgins anyway. ''Four ninety-three, please.'' Was all he said, barely giving me a second glance. He was a prep, I barely expected him to speak to someone like me. Hell, I barely expected him to have the capacity to form a sentence. Wait just a minute...five bucks for BATTERIES?! Then again, this was America...land of ripping off us cheap Canadians, just look at the damned exchange rate and you'd see that! Meh, I had batteries now...and I walked out of the store content with that fact. When I got back out to the car, my favorite kinda rental, a little red Jetta, I popped the batteries into the discman and off we went, just me and my Queen of the Damned soundtrack. Wait a minute, should I be driving in this condition? I can't even remember what happened more than half an hour ago...but hey, I seemed to be staying on the road for the most part. So back to the Holiday Inn I went, peering through a light drizzle, searching for the blue and gold sign to pop up somewhere amongst these buildings. I despise Charleston...I despise South Carolina. Fuck, I hate the entire United States of America...damn the yankee bastards for having their Hot Topic stores while the punk minority of Canada got pinned down by West 49 and their over-priced snowboarding shirts! Yippie...there was a nice long line up of red brake lights in front of me. That meant cars. Cars meant traffic. Traffic meant not getting to the hotel soon enough! I really do despi----wait, I seem to be an awfully hate-filled person this evening! I really should be happier...but sticking to the depression that my chosen culture forces me to portray is so much easier, it requires a lot less thinking. Therefore, my opinion stands: Hate and social opression breeds punk, so I will hate...I will be socially opressed and I...will...love it! Note to self: Stop preaching to self about benefits of this culture, you know self just likes the baggy pants. Humming along to ''Forsaken'' by David Draiman, my thoughts wandered back to my career, however trivial it had become. I seemed to spend the majority of my time getting screwed over by supposedly 'dominant' female federation heads of office...why? I've got no fucking clue! Honestly, I should be going after REAL wrestlers, but instead I get wrangled into petty fights with pathetic people. Point in case: Mona, the woman who signed my official NTICW release after a long battle that ended in her cracking me in the head with brass knuckles. I see this repeating with Jane Reagan... Conclusion? I have some sort of mixed up superiority complex. At least, that's all I can think of to explain this...I just can't stand not being the best at everything. Isn't that perfectionism? Wait, these women aren't better than me! Damnit, the superiority complex again!!!!!! STOP THINKING, AMY! Ah...much better. I'm really gonna run myself into the ground with this whole thinking thing...it's just never been a pleasant experience for me. Really...''Thinking'' seems to be turning into a dirty word, and concept, for poor little redheaded me. You'd even think I was...............(Brace yourself, another dirty word is fast-approaching.).......................blonde. Now what the hell was gonna be done about this little situation that was constantly occuring between fed-heads and I? I suppose I was just gonna have to show them what a real girl's like...not the prissy little 'High class' ladies like Jane Reagan and company, but a chick with proverbial balls...the social abomination that is Amy Campbell. The concepts, possibilities of how Reagan would react to somebody like me constantly nipping at her heels was, needless to say, more than just a little amusing. From the few instances I can recall, Jane wasn't exactly pleased with my debut and that's too bad for the poor little rich lady, I'm gonna be showing up a lot more than she'd like. But the others...what does Tempest think of Aimz? I shudder to imagine that one...God knows most women hate it when somebody younger, more athletic, better looking and MUCH more tasteful comes along almost as much as I hate that fag who provides the ever-so ladylike vocal ranges for Incubus. She seems like a pretty friendly, innocent woman, though...maybe I can help corrupt her a bit. When I stepped out of the car in the Holiday Inn parking lot, finally out of traffic, the only thing running through my head was that my attitude certainly wouldn't be readily-accepted by the skirts here in OSW, probably because it was far from 'old school'...but was I expected to care? Sure, I supposedly have a genius IQ...but when it came to common sense, let's just say that I'm aware of my weakness. The expectations I had of myself were scarce: Don't think before you do ANYTHING, shoot and ask questions later and, last but sure as hell not least...I shouldn't EVER be afraid of who I am. Speaking of which, who am I? While I walked through the hotel lobby, occasionally glancing up at the cheap plastered ceilings, I pondered that... Now I'm in OSW...I need more confidence. I need to know who I really am, damnit. I've gotta establish myself...can't just walk through life without a thought to the future. Fuck the future. Where was it gonna lead me? I'm not going to be one of those perfect little ladies in a nice prim skirt and coat, two point five children surrounding me while my wonderful husband works his great job at a software company. I guess I should apologize to society for that...but it just isn't me. Who is it? Probably Tempest or Jane Reagan, eventually...but not Aimz. Maybe that's where my age is a factor...Reagan and Tempest are practically dinosaurs compared to my few years here on earth. But what've they learned about the world? I'd like to know when the last time Jane proved how 'tough' she is outside of a bedroom was, or the last time Tempest got her clit pierced. Okay, so I don't have anything 'down below' pierced, but that's not the point! I just wanna know what the hell these other women have done to put them over top of me...I don't see a quality that makes them any different than any other girl walking down the street. Well, maybe a couple hundred thousand dollars stuffed into some plastic surgeon's pocket...but that's the only thing that sets those two 'ladies' apart. Walking down the hall, nothing was really clicking with me, aside from the random outburts to myself about OSW...but finally, I reached a door with the numbers three, eight and seven on it in crooked gold-plated lettering. This was my room. At least I remembered these more important things. Another thing I hadn't forgotten was Jason Dexter. My earlier thoughts of where he might be were finally brought to light...he was standing there near the doorway, awaiting my arrival and looking almost overjoyed when I finally walked in. A hug, a kiss. Definately good, I wasn't about to push him away if he asked for anything more...it was nice to receive that recognizable bear-like grip from him. I was a lot luckier than I probably wanted to admit; Jay was practically perfect in every way...for me, at least. Things were no different now. He put a perfect body on display with his lack of a shirt, and an even nicer feature was made slightly more noticeable with a pair of jeans that just fit him. Damn, he looked incredible tonight, but that was mostly just the delerium talking...Jason hated 'dressing up' for anybody. ''You were fucking amazing out there!'' Well, I didn't get my head knocked around too badly, his deep voice rang out just the same as always. ''Thanks.'' ''Wait...'' He looked at me for a moment with eyes all-too kind. ''...you okay?'' His thoughts were probably correct...I was starting to get sore, too sore. But I wasn't about to admit it, I'm supposed to be a big tough punk girl. ''Yeah, don't feel a thing.'' ''You sure?'' Damn, he had the sweetest smile...it almost reminded me of that oddly innocent expression Rent-A-Hero used to use. ''Wanna lay down, anything?'' Laying down sounded promising, but I was ''rough 'n tumble''...so I just stepped over to hop on the bed, sitting against the headboard, facing the cheap looking television against the wall straight ahead of me. ''Nah, this is fine...does the room have cable?'' ''Dunno, didn't try it yet.'' I had chuckled at him a bit, only then realizing how hoarse I was...damned colds. ''Well whatcha' do all alone in here all day?'' He knew what I was teasing about. ''Actually, as opposed to your popular beleif...I wasn't in the room all day.'' ''Huh?'' ''I made some money.'' What the hell had he done now? I swear to God, if I was about to find out that I'm dating ANOTHER drug-dealer, shit would hit the fan... ''How?'' ''Jeez, I figured you knew...'' Uh oh, that statement usually didn't have a good follow-up... ''I'm not obsessive about the speed of my cars for nothin', you know.'' A street racer? ''Someone saw The Fast And The Furious a few too many times, if you ask me...'' ''No no, I'm serious.'' At that point, he had pulled a wad of bills from his back pocket. ''See?'' Damn, that's more money than I make in a month! ''Jesus Christ!'' Now he was laughing at me, what the hell? ''What?'' ''Nothing...you're just cute when you get all suprised. But now you see why I've got a sweet-ass ride without a job, huh?'' ''If danger wasn't such a turn-on to me...'' I sighed, patting his now shaven head with a lot more love than I was trying to let on. ''I'd kick your ass here and now for being such a fucking retard.'' ''Hey...you're Little Miss 'Jump off of anything more than twice my height and hope I land on somebody.'!'' There was that laugh again... ''Cute, but I don't sit in a fifteen hundred pound metal trap while it torpedoes around.'' ''You do when you're in the passenger's seat.'' He ruffled my hair a bit, acting more like a big brother than a boyfriend in doing that. '' 'sides, you should've figured it out just by the way I drive already.'' ''You've gotta be bullshitting me here, Jay...street racing? I know you're into cars and all, but that just isn't your kinda scene...'' ''I know...'' He smiled at me, still in a loving way but also almost cocky. ''But it keeps you from finding out my TRUE profession...you might get a little creeped out about that.'' My thoughts ran rampant for a moment, but then they eased down to the texture of my skin, which was greasy from the sweat to say the least. ''I've gotta shower...we'll discuss this later, preferably in the morning when I've got my wits back.'' Again a sigh, this time from Jason. ''Alright. Take your time.'' With heavy eyelids, I staggered my way through the room and into the bathroom in the same subconcious haze that's plauged me all evening...what did tomorrow hold? God only knows, let's hope I remember more than just sketchy little details!
