Alone with her thoughts?

20 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 20th, 2002 ((Note: Ugggggh...I hate being sick.)) ''Love you'' Amy had her hero, but things had grown stale...here was her pick-me-up. Actually, here was more...here was that perfect guy, normal to her in every sense...and he almost seemed to actually care. Reno's slip-up comment was torturing her...she mulled over it in her mind again and again, but there were other things that needed to be fit in. Like what? Something that happened when Rent-A-Hero had come back from a little vacation, something that had been on her mind...something that nobody was quite sure of, but it visibly disturbed her. That didn't matter at the moment, she had planned on avoiding RAH for a while because of whatever he had done ...hopefully she'd tell someone soon enough. Ah well...it was all a lot to think about, she had to hunt Bethany down anyway. Amy had been off her guard, she made fun of Beanstalk Yamato, as if there was a person on this planet who wouldn't, and ended up getting tossed into a wall to her efforts. The problem was, Yamato had no idea what it was like to tangle with Aimz when her head was on right, so she'd be in for quite the surprise when Campbell managed to catch up with her. But there was already another little road block...Corg. Here was a fellow who really, REALLY deserved to be shot...he was yet another useless gothic gimmick of a human being, obviously dipping into his stash of Acid WAY too often. Then again, she had to give him one thing...he was the guy who finally got Ritchie Syxx to stop tormenting those in this existance with his whiney voice and 'Please look at me, no girls will!' mohawk, dyed blue to rip off Darkstar in another feat of this evil 'genius'. What matters is this match...Amy's up as a contendor for the Cruiserweight Title, meaning she has a chance to prove to every little ass who ever said ''You won't become a thing without me.'' that she can win titles in the biggest feds, by beating the best people...she knew she could beat Corg, now it was just a matter of figuring out how to do so... Obviously being 'sick' or 'hardcore' wouldn't work with him, when you melt a guy's skin off...it just doesn't get topped. He was a highflier, but he was also larger than her...meaning she wasn't ever about to ground him. So it's a fight for the few who fly high, Eagle against a red-tailed Hawk. ''Man, I REALLY need some rest.'' She had muttered to herself, frusterated by all of this coming at her faster than her signature 450 splash. Campbell just sat on the maple bench that lined the white wash brick wall of Davis' locker room, staring at the door. No matter how hard she tried to get her mind onto other things, like transportation to Washington, where she'd hopefully see Reno at whatever protest it was (She hangs off of his every word, but that doesn't mean she isn't distracted by his face while she's doing so, people.)...no matter what, she HAD to find Davis as soon as humanly possible: after those two words he had used, she had a lot of questions. Unfortunately enough, she was about to have a near heart attack over again, this time caused by nothing short of system shock... The figure burst through the door quickly as she stared off into space, directly at it...when it grabbed her, Amy was too confused to stuggle at all. Soon enough, though, releif came... ''You little flake!'' The farmilliar, female voice called out, shaking her shoulders. ''You SO had a chance with him RIGHT THERE!'' When the redhead's mind cleared, it was obvious who was standing in front of her...Renee Theriau. The long, naturally light blonde hair highlighted a soft pink in the front, a silver eyebrow ring with a neon red captive ball holding it together, the tone that was knowing, very calm but at the same time urgent...it was unmistakable, definitely Renee. ''Heyyyyy...'' The blonde shook her again, ''Back to earth, Amy...'' ''Whaaa...huh...?'' She shook the train of thought, which had been centered around Davis, off. ''Oh...hey Renee...'' Theriau was chunky, but somehow kept from being fat...the only way you could notice that she was chubby was if you got up close, paying a lot of attention to details; She certainly didn't have the build, so the extra weight went entirely un-noticed from a distance. Campbell had always loved the scent that accompanied her second-best friend, too...a perfect mix of chemical-ridden perfumes, manufactered to smell completely natural...and the smell of marijuana...something so sweet, it was almost sickening yet remained so perfect. After taking notice of Renee's presence, Amy paused another moment to think before inquiring, ''...umm...how'd you know I was in here? And how'd you kno---'' Before Aimz got too freaked out, her friend simply pointed to a small camera placed for security purposes in a corner of the ceiling. ''Just asked one of the fatasses.'' ''Ahh...'' Amy laughed, though thinking to herself 'Thank God I DIDN'T do what I wanted to...no law-enforcing security tool deserves that kind of a show.' ''...had me thinking you were psychowhatever for a moment there.'' Renee chuckled, cooly advising... ''I'm not psychic...I might not be able to know what you're doing, but even I can tell that you SO want Reno...and if you don't make a move soon, I'm gonna run in and take him for myself. Heh,'' She continued, making sure to mention her loyalties to her man, hoping Aimz might recall hers. ''...but I've got my Thomas, so I don't NEED Davis...though he's pretty fucking hot-ass if you ask me.'' ''You were always one of those 'Glass half-full.' people.'' The redhead laughed, shaking her head a bit. ''...you think I should go for anybody with a pretty face.'' ''Not ANYBODY...RENO DAVIS! Amy...in case you hadn't noticed, he's into you...and it's not as if you haven't made it so obvious that you want into his pants that I almost wanna stab myself in the eye or anything!!!'' ''Okay, okay...I get the point.'' Campbell tried to negotiate with Renee, but the woman was absolutely adamant on her friend getting what she wanted. ''...I'll keep up being suggestive, but I'm not just gonna jump up sometime and ram my tongue down his throat.'' ''Why not? You've done it befo----'' Aimz didn't look very happy about where Renee's statement was headed, and she made sure to cut it off before complete. ''This is different...I don't want to scare him off like Rentie did to me...'' ''What? How'd he sca---'' ''Nevermind!'' Argh, she almost let the secret out! ''I just don't want to scare Reno off, that's all.'' Miss Theriau looked confused, ''Riiiiiiiiiiiiiiiight...'' So.....she changed the subject! ''...so Bethany Yamato..........eh?'' She nodded, watching Amy give her a peculiar look. ''Yamato is about two pay-cheques away from lap-dancing at a Mexican truck stop.'' ''Huh?'' ''You're high, aren't you?'' ''No...well...yes...well....only a bit.'' Don't worry, feed is gone by the time things have a chance to get any crappier.
