The Shroud of the Dark Side Has Fallen

20 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-20-02 Our scene opens with a shot of the exterior of the Calgary Saddledome, where we are greeted with a shot of a none too happy Seth Masters walking along side his partner in crime, Trevor Hawke. Seth and Trevor have just finished up a heartbreaking night, stealing a title shot, coming oh so close to winning the belts, only to be robbed by the same men they stole the shot from. Much drama going on tonight, yo. For real, just check it out. “Damn it!” Seth Masters shouts, the frustration apparent in his voice as he rams his fist into the exterior of the Saddledome. “We’re always get fucking screwed, ya know?” “What? I know I’m always getting some action, but you haven’t been laid in weeks...” Trevor says casually, before understanding what his partner meant by the comment. “Oh wait, you’re talking about the matches. Yeah, in that case you are completely correct, my friend.” “How can you smile after the shit that went down tonight?” Seth remarks angrily, glaring at Trevor for his nonchalance. “It isn’t funny. We were so on top of things. Those tag team titles were as good as ours and then the fucking Westsidaz come down and ruin everything we worked for.” “Well we did steal the title shot from them to begin with,” Trevor states with a wave of his hand. “Considering all things, we should have seen it coming.” “It doesn’t matter that we stole the match from them,” complains Seth, pausing for a moment to turn his head and spit off to the side. “They never should have had the match to begin with, you know. We’ve been busting our asses here for months already. When Hessian and Cain beat us for the titles, we never got a return bout. How fucking unfair is that?” “Pretty fucking unfair, I’d say,” agrees Trevor, nodding his head. “And then the frickin Westsidaz walk right in the door and get a title shot before we get our rematch, not having shown any in-ring competence, not having proved themselves to the booking committee, not having even won any matches. The only time they stepped in the ring since their return was with us, and those two dicksmacks didn’t even come close to pinning our shoulders to the mat.” Seth shakes his head in angered disbelief, then continues. “There was no reason for them to get a match against Bloodlust before we did, besides the stupid shit they pulled backstage a week ago. I’m beginning to think that actual talent gets you nowhere in this business. It’s all about how big of a sneak-attacking asshole you can be backstage, and how many skulls you can split open with a steel chair!” “Sounds like you’re not such a big fan of Da Westsidaz anymore, Seth,” Trevor grins slyly, not letting his partner’s negative attitude sway him from his cool air. “Heh. A couple weeks ago you were marking out for them like Rosie O’Donnell at the Ho-Ho factory.” “Meh,” Seth spits again, before bringing his serious gaze to rest on his partner. “They’ve just become the latest let-down I’ve had to deal with as of late.” “Along with?” Trevor asks, cocking an eyebrow. “Well let’s see. Ever since we won the tag titles, things have been going straight downhill for us. Frankie Bones and Hostyle ambushed us after the Suburban Deathmatch. Then three weeks later we were robbed of our belts by an interfering Carrot Top. After that Carrot Top caused us to loss to Twisted Metal,” Seth closes his eyes, rubbing his eyelids with his fingers. “Argh! Of all the teams in the world, why did we have to lose to Twisted Metal?” “That was quite embarrassing,” Trevor admits grudgingly. “Yes it was. It was the most embarrassing match of my life,” Seth remarks, a pained expression coming over his face as he reminisces. “You would think things could only go up after that one, but oh no. At Head On Collision we saved my lovely Shanda from gothic sodomization only to be ambushed by those Westside fucks. Then, when we try to get revenge, Heresian Bloodlust interrupts the match with sledgehammer enhanced beatdown. And we both remember what happened last week.” “How can I forget,” answers Trevor, rolling his eyes. “That was quite a bit of trickery on the part of Riptah and Short Circuit. Almost as good as the stuff we pulled this week.” “But we shouldn’t have to pull stuff!” Seth yells, his tone heavy with disgust. “We’re being forced to drop down to the level of these dicksmacks just to stay in contention. And it’s still not working, because tonight we got screwed yet again.” “As I said before, you are completely correct,” Trevor says with a nod. “But just because we start resorting to cheap tactics doesn’t mean that everyone else is going to stop with theirs. There’s no such thing as honor amongst thieves, you know.” Raising his hands to both sides of his head, Seth looks as if he’s about to explode. The pained expression has returned to his face. It appears to be too much for him, driving him to lash out once more. His fist flies against the wall once more, a sickening thud emanating from the concrete. “Argh! Damn it!” Seth screams. Recoiling his fist, one can see that his knuckles are now bleeding from multiple small lacerations across them. A concerned expression comes over Trevor’s face as he surveys his partner. “Dude, you need to chill out,” Trevor says, placing a comforting hand on Seth’s shoulder. One that is quickly slapped off as Masters turns to face him defiantly. “Why? I should just lay down and take this shit week in and week out for no god damn reason? No, fuck that,” he spits, hitting the wall once more.“Fuck that!” “Seth, you have completely lost your cool, and when you consider that cool is the central aspect to our personas, that is definitely not a good thing,” Trevor states in a soothing tone, obviously a bit leery of his partner’s new state of mind. “Settle down, we’ll come out on top eventually.” “I can’t settle down. I’ve never been this pissed off before. I just want to fucking break something apart,” Seth mutters, pacing back and forth. His eyes move back and forth frantically. Eventually they fall on a small hunk of concrete laying on the ground, apparently broken off from the outer layer of the building. “Grrrah!” Picking it up in his right hand, Seth chucks it high into the air, out into the parking lot full of cars. It comes down with a crash in the distance, followed by the frantic wail of a car alarm. “Dude!” Trevor shouts, his eyes open wide in shock at the act he has just witnessed. Seth chuckles in response to his partner’s reaction, a sadistic grin spreading out across his face. Looking at Trevor, he begins to speak softly, his voice full of malice. “Trev, when I folded that steel chair over Riptah’s head tonight it gave me the most rewarding feeling I’ve felt in a long time. Words can’t even explain it. It was almost...orgasmic.” Trevor shakes his head in disbelief. “That is as wrong as anything can ever possibly be.” “Oh, but it’s not. For the first time in a long while I felt...satisfied. And now all I want to do is to feel it again. I’ll make sure that Da Westsidaz, and Heresian Bloodlust, and any other motherfuckers who get in our way will suffer from that same exhilarating crack to the skull, over and over and over again!” A wild fury rages in Seth’s eyes as he speaks, driving Trevor to take a few steps backward. It is at this moment that a revelation suddenly dawns upon him. Trevor’s mouth drops open in shock. “Oh shit.” “What was that?” Seth asks, dropping his self-righteous, semi-demented look for a second. “I finally realized what’s going on here. After seeing Star Wars Episode II three times in as many days, it’s all too clear,” Trevor mutters to himself. “And what exactly do you speak of, my friend?” “You’re being drawn to the dark side,” Trevor remarks solemnly. “I can sense it in you.” “Phhh, don’t give me that shit,” Seth states, waving his partner away. “No, no. I remember the quote from Empire. ‘Anger...fear...aggression. The dark side of the Force are they. Easily they flow, quick to join you in a fight’” Trevor nods as he stares apprehensively at Seth. “I don’t know about fear, but you are definitely flowing with anger and aggression.” After pondering this for a few seconds, Seth nods his head in agreement. “Hmm. You’re right,” he says, his grin widening. “Well if this is what the dark side feels like, then maybe I like it.” “No! Consume you, it will!” Trevor protests in a Yoda-esque accent. “Then let it,” Seth spits. “I will do whatever it takes to get what’s rightfully ours. If I have to destroy every team in the OSW to secure those titles, then so be it!” “That’s madness! And gimmick abandonment! We don’t destroy, we kick ass!” “Mmmhmmhmmhmmhmm,” chuckles Seth, his eyes flashing with…evilness. “I don’t care. Our gimmicks are retarded anyway!” Trevor looks at Seth in utter disbelief at his words, then turns slowly and begins to walk away from him. The walk soon turns into a run and the run soon turns into a sprint. A fearful look paints Trevor’s face as his ears pick up Seth’s demonic laugh in the distance. “Ha ha ha ha ha!” It isn’t long before Trevor has put respectable distance between himself and Seth, entirely out of range of his voice. Now he’s all alone, just himself and his thoughts. Damn, he must be stopped before its too late. He’s already in the beginning stages of a transformation into a gothic jobber. Next thing you’ll know he’ll be worshipping the devil and get a crazy ass manager...like and imp! or a hobo! I can’t let that happen. If I have to fight for common decency, then I’ll fight for it. The shroud of the dark side has fallen. Begun, this shitty promo series has. And strangely, The Imperial March begins to play in the background, bringing this promo to an intense close.
