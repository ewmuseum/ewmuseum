Memories: Part 2

31 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 31, 2002 Looking down on an old, wooden floor. The faint sound of footsteps can be heard. The boards on the floor are heard creaking ever so slightly. The footsteps get louder as the time passes. 10…15 seconds pass by until a pair of bare white feet are seen. They stand still. The footsteps stop. The picture moves up a little to show a bookshelf of sorts. A hand reaches into the shelf and pulls out a large album of sorts. As the footsteps start again, the shot turns around to show Kid Wonder. But we only see him from behind. He’s wearing a white shirt and some dirty jeans. His golden blond hair is a tad messy. Walk and walk…that’s what Wonder does. The room is dark except for the few beams of sunlight shining through a cracked window. Suddenly, K-Dub stops next to an old, broken down, wooden chair. He slowly takes a seat in the chair and hangs his head down. We move in closer to Wonder. His head is looking down at what appears to be a photo album. The album, however, is covered in dust. Wonder blows really hard and blows most of the dust off the front of the photo album. K-Dub then takes his right hand and dusts the rest of the album off. Zoom in on the photo album. On the cover of the album, in a small box, it just reads “Photo Album”. In the background, “Miss You Love” by Silverchair softly plays as the album is opened. The first page is just a few pictures of a baby girl taken back in 1981. The pictures are all protected by a plastic protecting of sorts. The baby in the picture has a little hair, but not much. About what a 3 or 4 month old baby girl. One can only assume it’s a baby picture of Amanda. She’s wearing only a diaper and a smile. Flip the page. More baby pictures. Keep flipping pages. More pictures of the baby girl slowly growing up. 2 years old. 3 years old. 5 years old. Every five to ten seconds, the hand of Kid Wonder slowly turns the page. Every now and then, there comes a sigh or a moan of depression from the man. Time passes. Kid Wonder keeps flipping through the photo album, looking at pictures of his loving girlfriend as a young kid. About 2/3 of the way into the album, we come up to more grown up pictures of Amanda. In one picture, she’s standing to the left of her big brother, Kyle. Also in the picture is another well-known person…Christy Jenkins. The date on the picture is 10/22/97. Flip a couple more pages. There’s a picture of Kid Wonder standing in front of a door at an independent show. He is exchanging phone numbers with Amanda. Off to the side stands Christy, laughing. Again, the date on the picture is 10/22/97. Flip a few more pages to a picture dated 7/4/98. We see a barebacked Amanda wearing nothing but red, white, and blue hot pants. Her head is turned and looking at the camera. She’s got a bit of a surprised look on her face, but still manages a giggle. Flip some more pages. There’s a picture dated 5/21/99. It has Amanda wearing an elegant, sparkling, ice blue colored dress while standing in front of the front door of her house. She’s got a slight smile on her face in the shot. Flip the page. Another picture taken on 5/21/99. Standing in front of a the back of a limo is Amanda. She’s wearing the same dress as the last picture. Amanda stands facing her boyfriend, Kid Wonder. Wonder wears a black tux. The couple have their arms wrapped around one another. Flip a few more pages. Pass by some more pictures. We stop at a picture dated 10/22/00. In the shot, Amanda is seen wearing the Super Duper Masked Jobber King mask most recently worn by Kyle “Velocity” Lamen. Even with the mask on, you can see the grin on her face. She is standing in what was Kid Wonder’s locker room at the first PCW PPV, PCW Dawning. Along with the mask, Amanda is wearing a pink tube top and some leather pants. The hand of Wonder flips a few more pages until he reaches the last page of the photo album. On that page, there is but one photo. The picture is dated 12/31/01. There stands Amanda wearing a rose red colored dress and black heels. Her auburn-blonde hair is tied back. In the background, we can see New York City from out of a window. On her face, as is always the case, is that ever so lovely smile. Kid Wonder’s index and middle fingers appear over the picture. He gently rubs the plastic over the picture. After a few seconds, KW lifts his fingers up. Right after that, a small teardrop drops onto the plastic protecting the picture. Sigh The scene zooms in closer and closer. It zooms in as close as possible until all that is seen is Amanda’s smiling face. The shot stays still for five to ten seconds. And that’s the shot we see as we fade away.
