Woe to the OSW! The Red Army is here!!

3 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-3-02 -It wasn’t what they expected, it wasn’t what anyone had believed. Ivan Stanislav, Russian Bear of the PCW and glorious hero of the Russian Federation’s military forces, jumping ‘ship’ to go to the Old School Wrestling Federation. All of Stanislav’s ‘stock’ was in the PCW. He had a niche there, he had a following there, and he was feared and respected by all!- -However, Ivan Stanislav is also a man with a plan. A plan that cannot be stopped by some fluke attack by men from the past….- Ivan Stanislav stands in his hotel room in Easton Pennsylvania, home of the first Saturday SLAM card. The Russian Bear, who had amazed and astounded the OSW crowd by storming down to the ring, stands in front of a television reliving what happened just a few hours ago. Outside, the sky slowly shows signs of going from night to day as the sun peeks up over a horizon of mountains. The 400 lb monster rests his massive hands on his hips and stares at the television replays the return of Trashcan Man and Doug Troy. The two men managed to damper his invasion but he knows he will get the last laugh. Stanislav always does. His black shirt stretches across his huge barrel chest as if he himself was carved from a strong, sturdy granite. The arms of the shirt almost tear around his massive biceps as his muscles pulsate with strength. His black pants, with dual red vertical stripes running along either end, are wrapped around his huge leather military boots. He shifts his weight while he looks on, causing the leather to ‘crunch’ with its unwillingness to move. Stanislav’s thick red suspenders connect to the front of his pants with two large gold clamps. The red material swings up over his enormous shoulders and cross at the lower portion of his back and clamp again at the back of his pants. The suspenders stretch over his chest, actually leaving his body as they are stretched to the waist of the pants, not even touching his ironclad stomach. His brown hair is cut high and tight on his head in a military fashion, his piercing brown eyes not moving even for a moment as he glares a hole through the television and watches the events of the last night transpire. Behind him, his comrade, Alexei Ruslan, stands with his hands clasped together. Alexei wears his brown trench coat, brown military hat, brown pants, and black shoes. The little man’s upper lip curls as he watches behind Ivan, his blood boiling with every moment that goes by. As the movie slowly ends Alexei puts his hands in his pockets and rocks back on his heels. “At least it could have been worse, Praporschik Stanislav,” says Ruslan, “Steven Smith could have wanted to come inside of the ring.” Ivan growls and looks over his shoulder at Alexei. He grinds his teeth together, causing his temples on the sides of his head to move. “Trashcan Man has always been thorn in my side. Now he is here and in direct opposition of my plans! That will not stand! I will not stomach that! This is the second time that proletariat has gotten in my way….and I will now make sure that this is the last time as well!” Ivan balls his hands into fists, turns away from the television and looks at Alexei. “And Doug Troy! I have sent that man to the hospital twice, and now he will be sent there a third time!” screams Stanislav as he kicks over a nearby coffee table, “he has gotten in my way for the last time EVER! He will wish that he has never gotten in the way of my Communist Revolution! I am not here to battle the ‘old regime’ of the PCW! I am here to educate the younger talent of OSW and lead my army into battle against Tyler Nelson!” Ruslan nods and takes his hands out of his pockets. He points out at Easton, Pennsylvania. “Look at it Ivan! These people have all seen your power in the OSW arena! They are all ripe to be changed to our cause! Come Saturday when you destroy Troy and Trashcan Man they will no longer have anyone to look to but you! You the might Ivan Stanislav and his band of communists!” Ruslan grins, very happy at what he has said, “And do not forget that we have to get Wang, Riggs, and the Two Round Faced Women on board too! They had to return to their respective countries to receive instructions!” Ivan nods and fixes the waist of his pants. “I will get a hold of Tom and try to coordinate various issues as well! Doug Troy and Trashcan Man haven’t wrestled in months and they are not very united on their own front! However, we of Red Army are and will forever be united!” Ivan laughs loudly and continues, “they will not be able to defeat the powerful Red Army, and come Saturday, they will all fall to our might! Woe to the OSW! The Red Army is here!! DYAAHAA!!” Ivan and Alexei begin to laugh loudly as the camera slowly fades to black……
