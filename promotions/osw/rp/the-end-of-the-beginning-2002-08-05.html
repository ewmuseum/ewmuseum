The end of the Beginning

5 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 5, 2002 The knife penetrating my skin slowly dragging it further and deeper. Suddenly it's all gone the tears the hate, the hurt, the sorrow, the emptiness, the aggravation and anticipation all leaking out in the form of my blood. I'd cut once, then make another. The blood glistens in the dull moon light as the numbing takes place. I'm numbed of my depression, numbed of any and all feelings I have. I cut again, focusing on the pain, the one feeling that does remain concentrated on what I'm seeing. I take the knife away from my arm and I see the whiteness of the skin underneath. I watch as the blood slowly leaks through that pale inner skin. As this happens the relief comes. Relieving me from life, the life I wanted to get away from, and did. Even though it only lasts a short period of time it's the quickest way I know that is able to relieve me of everything so greatly, other than death. I don't do it to punish myself, or to get back at others, or for attention or sympathy. I cut because it helps me.. the only problem is, every time I see any of my scars, I see the pain that, that scar had once relieved, the pain is stuck outside of me. And in time it all sinks back in. I laugh and simply drop the knife that is held at my fingertips. No more of this dark delight, instead I have something to retrieve. Something that belongs solely to me and no one else..But to find it means I have to return to the very ring I debuting inside of. The very ring where I received my first hit, and undoubtedly not the last to come. No matter, right now I must settle on returning my property to my side. I stand from my placement on the side of the fireplace where I reside, popping the blade to the side of the bricks and watching as the steel glints from the flames that dance inside. Those flames remind me of something. A dark and sinister something, but I feel the feeling rising like the bile in my stomach and calmly I suppress what I want to release so eagerly. Now with a quick stride I relinquish my stay in my quarters to return to the ring which will become my true home in time. Why I wander alone in this empty place is obvious to me, I have never been the one to carry much company. Then again not much company has wanted to carry me..Vampir, Hessian, and the others have become my placement. They have taken me in and these are the things I think of while I saunter on. But finally I have reached my point of focus and I see the blade shining in the darkness surrounding. I cannot help but allow a morose smile touch my darkened lips while I venture inside of the ring that holds my treasure. As I kneel before the beautiful piece my fingertips reach forward to lightly brush over the twisted surface which is still stained with blood. Once my fingers meet with the steel my minds eye sends a picture and at this picture I have to grin. My mouth covered in blood, the blood of the man which on SLAM should have been my victim. The blood of his own dripping down his face. Ah what a sight, what a feeling..What a rush! Before lifting the twisted dagger from the stained mat I allow my mind to wander in to other events leading up to this arrival in The Cult. Sadly I cannot recall much before my life in the ranks of the ring so no new break through can help me understand my being here. While the dagger takes its place by my side I slip upon a turnbuckle to rest for a moment or two while trying to settle things inside of my head. As I continue to think on I notice a wet sensation running down my arm. My head tilts downward and my silver-blue metallic orbs take in the sight of blood making its way from the opened wound to the mat below me. My head falls back and I laugh while a conclusion forms. 'This is not the first of the blood to flow at my hands.. And it certainly will not be the last..'
