Back To School - Part One

26 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Tuesday, March 26, 2002 'Now children, how many of you watch pro wrestling?' A flurry of hands went up into the air, as Mrs. Sica's third-grade class wondered what this old lady could be talking about. Usually, discussions of professional wrestling had been limited to the schoolyard during recess, however now here she was talking about it right after their lunch period! 'Good, good, almost all of you. Well, that's great, because I have a very special friend who is coming into today. Most of you have probably seen him with the MWW federation and the Old School Wrestling federation, but believe it or not I knew him from his college days, before he was a wrestler.' The eyes of the kids began to glow, as the principal was heard out the hallway, muffled behind the old oak doors of the classroom. 'Here it is Mr. Casey.' One child stood up, but Mrs. Sica was very quick to put an end to that. 'Raymond, sit down, or he's not coming in here.' Raymond's butt hit the ground like a sack of potatoes. 'Now, I'd like to introduce to you, Mr. Nicholas Casey!' The kids began to clap wildly, as Nick ducked under the door, and towered next to the principal and Mrs. Sica. 'Hi kids, you probably know me as Angeldust, but out here, I'm just another guy.' Nick walked around the room slowly, obviously giving a well-rehearsed speech to the children. There was an odd occurance about him, his eyes seemed to glow neon. 'Believe it or not, even a big, strong wrestler guy like me had to start out in a classroom just like this one. Now, I know you've been told before that an education is the most important thing in your life, and I'm not going to tell you any differently.' Mrs. Sica smiled at him, as he walked around. The kids marveled at the size of him, yet the calm, soothing voice he spoke in. Such a far cry from the screaming cannibalistic voice used weekly on Slam! now, and formerly Reality Check. The time passed by, and Nick covered the subjects of drugs, alcohal, peer pressure, smoking, staying in school, and all of the basic things that a speaker should do for kids of that age. 'So the high school diploma is one of the most important things you can get in your life. But don't stop there, keep going on, as far as you can! I got my dream, the challenge is, can you get yours?' The children started to clap for Nick, as Mrs. Sica walked up to the front of the room. 'Okay kids, I want you all to thank Mr. Casey for coming by today...' 'THANK YOU MR. CASEY!' In unison, of course. 'Okay kids, now do you have any questions for Mr. Casey?' A short black girl with braided pigtails stood up, and asked Nick the first question. 'Mr. Casey, why don't Propaganda, EoE, and Vampir WeirdLastName like you very much? I think you're the best!' Nick chuckled at the comment, probably the one he got most from kids. 'Well, you see when they were children, their mommies didn't love them as much as your mommies and daddies love you guys. So they feel the need to take it out on me that they aren't as liked as me, and they aren't anywhere near as good. 'Kids, the moral of this story is don't hate people for being a lot better, stronger, faster, and better-looking than you, like EoE does. Don't blame God for loving somoene more than you, like Propaganda does. And just because half of your face may be rotted away and you have no frieds, like Vampir, doesn't mean you get to chokeslam the new guy. Or you will pay, just like those jerk-offs did.' Mrs. Sica looked shocked, hoping that the kids didn't know what that was. She was in luck. She gave Nick the infamous lesbian death stare (you all know what that is, don't lie), but Nick didn't know what he did wrong. 'Well, anyways, next question.' A taller white child stood up, the buzzcut type who you could tell was going to be the dumb jock when he hit high school. 'Umm...why....ummm...well....umm....you know....when...you...ummm...' This proceded for about another minute or two, before Mrs. Sica again put a stop to it. 'Keep thinking, Brock. I'm sure it will come to you. Anyone else?' 'Mr. Casey, why is your nose bleeding. Did someone hit you on the way here?' Nick looked down, and felt a steady flow of blood coming from his nose. It dripped down his write OSW t-shirt, and landed on the floor somewhat. 'Oh shit.' Nick got the stare from Mrs. Sica again, but was too busy to care. He never got hit in the face, but he ran out of the room, and into the bathroom. It wasn't a past injury, or even a present one. He knew exactly what it was... ...stupid hypocrite.
