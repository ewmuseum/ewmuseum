Who Needs A Stereo When You've Got Krieg (Michel/Reno Series 1/3)

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28.04.02 “...I dragged this lake, looking for corpses, dusted for prints, pried up the floorboards....” Thumping comes from behind a steel plated door as the camera moves slowly from the ground up, scanning the door. As it continues to move, it glances over a name plate and keeps on moving, but flicks back to look at it a second time...Michel Krieg... “...But at the right place, at the right time, I’ll be dead wrong and you’ll be just fine...” Like he did the other day, Jeremy the Cameraman nudges open the door and guess what?...Krieg’s singing and dancing again. Swinging his massive right paw around and holding his left arm in the air, Krieg imitates Alkaline Trio on his Gibson Les Paul ‘Air Guitar’ [Obviously not a real guitar...]. Cracking off some more lyrics, Krieg hops around on his right leg, almost belittling the lead guitarist of AC/DC [You know the one...The guy in the school boy outfit...] . Jeremy once again can not contain his laughter and splutters out as Krieg recognises the childish laugh of his personal cameraman and looks around in distaste, “Why can’t you knock before you come in, pervert,” cracked Krieg, “I didn’t want to interrupt your routine, Michel,” giggled Jeremy, “Ah...Whatever, American...Well are you coming in or not,” asked Michel, motioning for Jeremy to come in the room and shut the door, “So what do you want,” “Ah, I’m just here to see what you’re up to...Just the usual,” “Ok. As you can see, I’m just out of the shower again,” said Michel as he moved his hand towards the towel wrapped around his waist, “I think you time your visits so you get to see me with only half my clothes on,” “Emm..I don’t think so. I’ve already got a boyfr...Girlfriend,” “Well, whatever the case, Krieg needs to get ready. Oh, did you like my impression of Hessian? Dah, me and Walczak are so funny,” chuckled the German giant, “Yeah, whatever you say, Michel,” “I see you share the lack of humour most of your American allies share,” Walking over to a cupboard/closet, Krieg pulled open the doors and pulled out a few garments, walking into the bathroom with them. After a few minutes of Krieg shuffling around and a few bangs off of the wall [closely followed by some German cursing], Krieg emerged from the bathroom, now dressed in black combat jeans, mauve boots, a light blue Vans t-shirt, a dark leather jacket and a pair of sunglasses dangling from the neckline of his t-shirt, ”Now who is smooth,” said Krieg, twirling clumsily in front of the full size mirror in his changing room, “Emm...You, I presume,” replied Jeremy, “Ah, you wouldn’t know style if it came up, pulled down your trousers, slapped your bare ass cheeks and looked you in the face,” replied Krieg, “Anyway, enough talk. Let’s head to the town. I need to finish off some shopping,” Just as Krieg pulls at the handle of the door, OSW’s only female reporter, Shanda Russell, comes toppling in the door and right into Krieg’s massive chest and arms, “Oh, hi there, Krieg,” said Russell, slightly stunned, “I see you were spying on me, Miss Russell,” asked Krieg, slightly suspicious, “Emm...No...I just came to tell you Ed has asked for you and Reno Davis to head into town to do a autograph session before Head On Collision...Just thought I’d tell you...Reno is leaving right now and the bus will leave in a few minutes so you better hurry up,” “Davis, uh...I shall be there...Little does the American know of my plans...” “Oh, another thing. Ed said there’s to be no fighting between you and Davis,” “Damn,” said Krieg, as the scene fades out.
