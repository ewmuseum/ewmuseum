Hiding pain from a loved one.

17 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/17/02 It’s hard to feel pleasure when you are in this much pain. Turning up South Grandview Ave. in Papillion, NE should be a happy occasion…and it is to an extent. It’s home. The weather is perfect on this day, the midday sun shine’s brightly overhead in the cloudless sky. The temperature stands at about 80 degrees right now, with a slight breeze that tries to fight off the humidity. The graphite metallic colored Jeep Grand Cherokee Sport crawls up the hill until it reaches the house number 1107, a tan house on the left side of the street. The vehicle turns into the drive, the dark tinted windows shielding the driver from view as the front door flies open. A black and tan German Shepherd comes dashing out of the house, followed closely by a beautiful blonde woman in a brightly colored summer dress. The Jeep comes to a stop and the driver side door opens. A man steps out of the car and is immediately attacked lovingly by the German Shepherd as the dog stands on it’s hind legs and bathes the man with licks up and down his face. The hat and sunglasses that the man wears can barely shield him from the bombardment of dog kisses. “Down girl!” chuckles the man as he tries to push the dog down. “I missed you, too, Kima.” Soon our attention is turned to the gorgeous woman who steps cautiously around the front of the Jeep. Anticipation fills her face and tears well up in her eyes as she eagerly awaits a look from the man in the Jeep. She places a finger near her mouth, biting ever so slightly on the fingernail. The man turns, and a broad smile explodes across his bruised face. “Hey babe,” says the man. “Oh Jim!” exclaims Kami Axtell. She runs toward him and he steps from behind the door to greet her. She lunges for him, her arms open wide, and falls into his muscular arms. “I missed you sooo much,” says Kami through her tears of joy. Jim winces in pain but still hugs his wife lovingly. He hasn’t told her how badly he hurts. “I missed you, too, babe,” says Jim through the pain. He plants a soft kiss on her neck as he squeezes her as tightly as his injured ribs will allow. Kami takes a step back and the couple engage in a long, passionate kiss. She runs her hands over Jim’s back as he runs one of his own through her hair. Once finished, they separate and Kami gazes upon Jim’s face. Her smile fades somewhat as she notices the bruises and cuts on her husbands face. “Oh my God,” gasps Kami, her voice laced with concern. “Look at you….” Her voice trails off as she gently pulls the sunglasses from Jim’s face. He turns away slightly, trying in vain to hide the damage done to him at the hands of Kory Storm. Kami runs her hand gently over Jim’s wounds, fear and shock adorning her face. “Did that asshole do this to you?” she asks accusingly. “Hon,” Jim starts. “It’s not that big of a deal…..just some bruises and small cuts.” Jim’s effort to calm his wife are to no avail. “Not a big deal?! Look at you!” Kami screams. “This is not what we agreed on when we decided you would try out for Old School Wrestling. I didn’t say you could sign up to get attacked from behind by a sledgehammer wielding son of a bitch. We didn’t agree that you would put your welfare on the line just because some egotistical bastard has a self esteem problem. We didn’t….” Kami breaks down into hysterical tears and Jim pulls her in close. He simply holds her as she sobs on his shoulder. The pain from his ribs is nearly unbearable, almost more painful than the throbbing he feels inside of his head. The sledgehammer has certainly taken it’s toll on Axtell’s body, as it would anyone who had been assaulted with 20 pounds of solid metal. Even Michel Krieg got a shot in on Jim as he, for no other reason than the good fight, tried to stop him from hitting Storm. It seems that Jim has become a target for those who deem themselves better than everyone else. All Jim has ever done is try to be the best he can be and give his all. Kory Storm has chosen to try and bring others down to his level, to make them lower themselves to his depths. Jim Axtell isn’t going to do allow himself to sink down that low. He will win this battle because he has more heart than Kory Storm could ever dream of having. That’s what makes the difference. Heart.
