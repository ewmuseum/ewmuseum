Imp Wars Part I: Heretic Huntin' And Harlot Humpin'

6 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09-06-02 “Minion when I said we need to find Cain and Caleb, this isn’t what I meant…” Hessian groaned, as Minion dragged him along the quiet street. In the deepest recesses of Detroit, Hessian and Minion were on the hunt for something, searching through alleys and abandoned buildings for something, or someone. “Look if Cain and Caleb are anywhere they’ll be somewhere around here. You know Cain, he likes a nice dark, moist hole to hide in…then again so do I!” Minion grins cheesily at Hessian, who groans again, and follows Minion as they hunt for their missing Heretic companions. While Minion seems adamant in his search for Azrael and Caleb, Hessian trundles along the street, kicking at glass bottles and mumbling profanities under his skull mask. “It’s bad enough those four faecal-faced sewer rats eliminated me from that Championship Table Match last Saturday, then I see him for what? An hour last night? I now have a missing tag champion, MY PARTNER and his fucking homeless friend lost, this can’t happen so close to the Pay-Per-View. He’s even A.W.O.L. with the damn tag strap.” “Oh stop talking like it’s a big deal Hess…Azrael is a big boy he was probably just training for Slam! Anyway, it’s not like he’s been missing for ages, but we’ll find him anyway…!” Minion tries to reassure Hessian as he checks inside a trashcan for Cain and Caleb. “Minion you are severely missing the point. When last we met outside of the OSW it was 10 days ago, and then he arrives at Slam! only to win our match. Thanks to that idiot’s slow paced waltz to the ring, those damned suburban wretches eliminated me before I had a chance to get my bearings. If I see Cain there’s going to be hell to pay for his insolence…” Minion sighs, knowing that tension in the group is a bad thing, especially when it’s with 6’10” and 7’2” Satanists. Slowly working through his mind and being projected through his devious eyes, a plan seems to be forming in Minion’s head. Hessian cocks his head at the doting little Imp stumbling as he thinks, then moans as he looks up ahead and sees the familiar lights. “Hmm…if we can’t find Azrael or Caleb how about I tag with you Hessian? After all, that bitch Reagan EXCLUDED ME FROM THE CRUISERWEIGHT DIVISION!!! I mean I’m as qualified as Aimz or Corg dammit! I’m 79lbs, well under the required weight, I’m 3’11”, which means trouble for anyone, and dammit I have a move list too! Ok so maybe it’s not as extensive or as complex as yours Hessian but dammit…this isn’t fair! I am SO going to get S.P.U.M.U.I. onto Reagan or Russell or Ed or Porter or whoever the fuck runs that federation because this is discrimination! MINION WILL FIGHT IN THE OSW!!! I WILL NOT BE OPPRESSED!!!” Minion hops up onto a trashcan and looks to the heavens, posing akin to Superman and grinning a wide, Impish grin, while emitting some kind of purple glow. “I have a dream! A dream that some day Imps and norms can wrestle together in one ring! One ring! In a ring of peace, a ring of harmony…without discrimination or prejudice. A ring where Firesnatch~! is plentiful and Communists are non-existent. I have a dream…and that dream is ‘Minion the Imp: OSW Cruiserweight Champion!’” Hessian stops directly before Minion, and grabbing Minion’s head with one gauntleted hand, Hessian lifts Minion down off of the trashcan and onto the sidewalk again. “Minion, we are out here in the middle of the night searching for Azrael and Caleb in the most foolish places…what, dear Imp, does your dream to be Cruiserweight Champ have to do with trying to find Cain and Caleb?” “I’m just making conversation…HEY! LOOK!” Minion points ahead squealing out, as the large pale shape of a well built man walking along the sidewalk next to a smaller, rougher looking figure. Hessian takes off up the sidewalk, pushing past what few people are walking along enjoying the night. As he closes in on the two figures, he can hear Minion shouting something, but ignores it and slows down behind the two figures. Reaching out, Hessian takes them by the shoulder and spins them around, and grimaces at what he sees before him. “Hey Chico watch de frickin shoulders why don’tcha huh? Deez makeup ain’t comin’ cheap yo! Now wha’ chu wan’ Chico?” “Hey he don’ look like he from around hya! Wha’ chu pay for da getup like dat son?” Hessian steps back in shock as the two she-males make their way on up the street and into some seedy club around the corner. Minion waddles up to Hessian, panting for breath. Tugging at Hessian’s black cape, The Goliath’s attention is caught by the 3’11” Imp. “Tha-…that’s not Cain or Caleb!” Minion says, panting rather heavily for the short distance he’s run. “I KNOW THAT NOW!” Hessian bellowed out, in turn getting him the attention of a pair of fat hookers on the other side of the road, and a dog in the distance somewhere howling. Minion stands rather shocked, and breathes out, then rests a flat hand on his chest, holding the other hand out at Hessian. With a rather camp sigh, Minion begins flailing his wrist and hand at one half of the OSW Tag Team Champions. “Well fuckin’ scuse me for bein’ born! Don’t shout at me you overgrown black, skull-capped condom! Now you see here…if we ca-…” Minion suddenly stops in mid-sentence and looks across the street. Squinting past the fat hookers, Minion’s eyes catch the words “Boogie Room” in neon flickering above an old looking building. The windows are all blocked up, and the only entrance appears to be a pair of big double cellar doors, guarded by two men, wearing customized tuxedos and a serious sort of grimace on their faces. In his little Imp mind Minion’s plan becomes complete, and he looks up to Hessian with big puppy dog eyes. “Hess…I think they might be hiding in there…” Minion says softly, flitting his eyelids at the big Satanist. “Not only are they not in there Minion, but that has none of the atmosphere that suggests a “Hessian friendly” environment…and I do not really appreciate 500lbs hookers trying to woo me with their $5 stretch marks and cheap, clogged arteries.” “Please Hessian, the place looks fun! I haven’t been out for over a week since Amy denied me the flaming beaver.” Minion said, tears welling up in his eyes. Hessian looked across at the club, then looked at Minion. The little Imp’s lip began shaking as he acted a sad little Imp before Hessian. “We aren’t booked for Slam! this week…I don’t see why not Minion. If we must…let’s go…” Hessian gives in and follows the Imp, who zips along the street, and stops dead before the old building. The fat hookers, fore reasons best left to themselves, ignore Minion and watch as the 7’2” powerhouse that is Hessian crosses over and stops alongside Minion. The hookers begin to make there way across to Hessian, but one intense sneer from the giant sends the hookers back to their street corner. One of the doormen steps forward and looks up at Hessian, while the other one checks out Minion. Minion winks at the doorman, while Hessian cocks his head at the 5’5” doorman staring at him. “I know you…I do…I know you…” said the doorman, checking Hessian over. The other doorman glanced from Minion to Hessian, and he gasped, rather surprised. “I know you too! I do…I know you!” added the other Doorman, opening up the cellar doors and standing aside. “We know you…we do…we know you. Go on in! We know you, we do…we don’t know where…but we know you…we do…we know you!” repeated the doormen, standing aside and allowing the behemoth and the midget into the building. As they slowly make their way down the set of stairs the light begins to change from the dark yellow of the streetlamps outside to a beaming red and blue and green. A loud bang emits from upstairs as the cellar doors close, and as Minion and Hessian head further down into the building, the sound of increasingly loud heavy music booms throughout, and faint lights flash and swirl and dart around the bottom of the stairs. Stepping out from the bottom, Hessian and Minion gape at what is before them. To the right, a bar, complete with two bar staff, a menagerie of alcohol and some rather intriguing characters seated at the bar looking across to the other side of the room. Hessian and Minion walk past the bar, along the little path past the leathered up dark alcoholics, then make their way through the seating area, past rather luxurious looking tables and chairs, and not so luxurious drunks and perverts and the like, and take a seat at a table near the front. Hessian nods to Minion then points to the bar, and walks off again, while Minion sits staring for some reason. Various people begin glancing at them, though directing their stare back to the other side of the room. For past the bar and the red carpet pathway through to another large door, are a plethora of tables and chairs, seated at which are many men, guzzling down pints of Ye Olde Gothic Ale and watching, for at the left side of the room is a large platform, extending across the room. Minion stares at them. Majestically and gracefully dancing around the poles is a variety of half a dozen or so pole dancers. Hessian sits back down at the table, planting a large tankard of alcohol in front of Minion, while the Imp fishes around in his cloak pockets, and pulls out $83, comprising of mostly $5 and $1 bills. Minion grins evilly at the pole dancers, and then begins waving the dollar bills in the air. “Minion, don’t harass the little sluts, they’re dirty enough as it is without having to satisfy the needs of a sexually frustrated Imp!” Hessian shouted over the music. Minion sneered at Hessian and instead waited. Surely enough, a blonde girl in tight black PVC shorts and a “bra” made of floss struts over to Minion, and squats before him. Minion stares deep into the big, dozy green eyes of the bimbo, and winks as she smiles cheekily at him. Waving at her with a couple of dollar bills in his grip, the pole dancer blows a kiss towards Minion, then motions for him to come closer. Hessian sighs and takes another drink. Minion hops up onto the table and waddles forward a few steps. Stopping a few inches in front of the dancer, Minion’s evil grin appears again as her heaving breasts become only a few inches from his body, and his eyes widen with glee as the dancer moves closer to him, and puts her lips to his ears. Feeling her breath against his ear, Minion begins to wobble at the knees, as she begins to speak… “I know you…I do…I know you! I don’t know where, I don’t know how, but I do, I know you!” With a disgusted and shocked look on his face, Minion pushes the dancer away and turns back to Hessian. “What the fuck is up with everyone saying that? It’s getting on my damn nerves and we’ve only been here about five minutes!” squeals Minion over the loud music and rowdy perverts. “They must know us!” Hessian replies, finishing off his drink. “Aw fuck ‘em! Stupid crack whores! There must be more to this place than just pole dancers!” Minion shouts again, throwing a few dollar bills at the dancer that annoyed him. “Well then let us venture on through there, explore the place.” Hessian added, pointing towards the large, lavish door near the bar. Minion shrugs then begins downing his drink. Belching loudly, Minion slams the tankard on the table, grabs his remaining $79 then hops off to the floor. Hessian rises up, and the two make their way back through the people and walk along to the door. “We weren’t there very long…” Minion says, pushing open the door open and walking through. “Too bad the dancers are dickheads…let’s check out the rest of this here Boogie Room!” Hessian ducks through the doorway, closing the door behind him. Minion and Hessian stand surprised. Before them is what can only be described as a change in décor. A fine red carpet suits as the floor, while a cream ceiling sports chandeliers along the hall. Minion and Hessian are met by a smartly dressed man, in the same custom tux as the men at the door. The jolly old man extends a hand, which is received by Minion. “I know you…I do…I don’t know where…but I know you!” he says, shaking Minion’s little purple hand warmly. Ignoring what he says, Minion looks up the hallway. “So, uh, what’s up with this place?” he asks, releasing the handshake from the man. “Well as you can see, there are 20 doors here, each one leading to one of our exclusive Boom Boom Rooms, where we have waiting in each, a fine specimen of a female. For a minimal price you and your friend here can rent one of these rooms for a length of time, rates pending, and enjoy the company of one of the 20 fresh young girls we have on offer. Are you interested” Minion looks at the rooms, then at Hessian, who rolls his intense red eyes at the Imp. A massive cheesy grin grows on Minion’s face, and he turns back to the ‘salesman’. “Ok how much for an hour?” Minion asks, fishing around for more money in his pockets. “An hour will cost you $50. All services included.” Cash exchanges hands and from his pocket the man draws a random key number. Minion takes the key, and thanks the man. Nodding at Hessian, the two makes their way along the hallway, while Hessian whispers to Minion. “It certainly is gracious of you to pay such a ridiculous amount for 1 hour of “pleasure” Minion…” “What? You’re gonna join in?” asked Minion. “No, I simply do not want to be in the company of those filthy inbred rats. Albeit I am Hessian but even I have my standards.” Minion shrugs and looks at the key chain. The number 17 inscribed into the brass chain. Minion stops dead, Hessian almost tripping over him, stoops over the Imp, releasing a guttural growl. Minion sticks his little green tongue out at Hessian, who seems to only be getting angrier. Minion finally groans then turns to door number 17, and takes a deep breath. “Here goes Minion…now watch the master at work Hessian!” With that, Minion inserts the key into the door and opens it up. Looking inside, a sudden breeze of lavender escapes the room, and into Minion and Hessian’s nostrils. Hessian grunts and pushes past Minion, and enters the room. From the inside, a curvy figure watches as the giant silhouette enters the room, then turns to a plush cushy chair in the corner. Hessian looks over to the figure on what appears to a bed and nods, his intense red eyes glowing in the dark of the candlelit room. “If you bother me I’ll break your legs then rape you…” The girl makes some kind of a frightened whimper sound, when Minion enters and closes the door behind him. Seeing Hessian collapse on the chair, Minion faces the girl on the bed and trundles over to her. “Don’t mind him, he’s just sexually frustrated.” Minion says soothingly, crawling up onto the little bed beside the whore. She looks over at Hessian, glowing red slits staring back at her. Her attention however is soon swayed from the 7’2” Satanic Behemoth to the 3’11” love machine stroking her leg. “How long did you pay for?” the hooker asks rather nervously. “An hour…now tell me baby doll, what’s a pretty thing like you called?” Minion asks, etching closer to her, the candlelight dancing on her naked flesh. “You can call me Sophia…” she reluctantly replied. “You can call me Big Daddy Minion…” With that, Minion and ‘Sophia’ begin canoodling, with little giggles and Impish groaning emitting from the two intertwined bodies. From the plush cushy chair, Hessian’s eyes get ever heavier, and eventually the tag champion falls into a light sleep. On the love-bed however, Minion mounts the hooker, and begins gyrating. “The Imp has landed!” To Be Continued...
