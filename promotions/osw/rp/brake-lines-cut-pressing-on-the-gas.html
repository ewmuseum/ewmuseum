Brake Lines Cut, Pressing On The Gas

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27 April 2002, the night before Head On Collision Collision My mission When the dawn breaks With a handshake Relaxed and feelin' great Screeching...head on I'm needing a head on All the day's plans All the shaking hands Beepers and suntans Screeching...head on I'm needing a head on Collision My mission 'Collison' Faith No More This was the arena, the exact same arena that saw Sebastian Toomes and current Heavyweight Champion Neurosis nearly kill each other. November. And now in April, 5 months later, he was back for the first time. The first time since the coma, since the death spiral of CCW began, since his career in OSW... This was a crucible, a proving ground. And now, not even a half a year later, things were accelerating too fast, things were out of control, the brake lines have been cut and a cinder block put on the gas pedal. Vax. The biker. The Red Army. Jane in the hospital. Everything was going too fast. There were guys who've been in the buisness for a lot longer than him, still struggling to pay thier dues and get a shot at the big time, but here he was, holding the top belt in the top fed of the PTC. What does that imply? Is he better, more charismatic, faster, stronger than the rest, a shining star like he always said he was? 'Fuck' Toomes stands in the docks of the Joe Louis Arena, where the ambulance was loaded back in November. Empty now, though, except for the ring crew and the set-up equipment for tomorrow night. The sounds of metal clanging around as the set is being erected reverberates through the arena, audible even here in the dock. The OSW World Champ stands there, dressed in a pair of black jeans and a black turtleneck, his silver and blue hair tied into a topknot (ala Jericho debut style, listless as the draft from the street blows under the steel dock doors. The fourescent lights shine harshly down onto the conrete pad, an unnatural glow on the racks of equipment and metal scaffolding, disassembled and awaiting completion at the hands of the techs who were lounging somewhere in the arena. He had left the hospital where Jane was admitted to last night, catching a flight up to Detroit to get ready for the match on Sunday, a match against a guy who was riding on a wave of popularity from the fans, and virtually unbeatable in the ring. Ivan Stanislav. The man who, at Red October, went 60 minutes with Meanstreak in an Anywhere Falls, 60 Minute Iron Man Match. The man who ran rampant through PCW until Jeremy Howard pinned Frisco for the title in a Triple Threat match-up. Ivan never got pinned for the title. And now, the big Russian freak was going after Toomes' title. The OSW World Title. If Ivan won, he would be the second OSW World Champion, Toomes of course being the first. He would bury Toomes if he had the chance. Ivan was known for his hatred of management, and as far as he was concerned, Jane was just another Tyler Nelson, and Toomes was considered to be her lackey. He wasn't even given a second thought. He was considered to be beaneath thier notice. Not worth of being the champ. Not worthy of spending a thought on. Ivan was a legend in his time. Time passes. Sebastian was the first OSW World Champion. He was the man to lead the company. He was the one who brought the fans, he was the one who polarized OSW into a formidable federation, not Ivan. Ivan can have his sweet little love affair with Tempest, and he can have his army of illiterate thugs too,but the one thing he can't have... Toomes pats his mid-section, where the World Title would be, had he not left it in the trunk of his Camaro. No, Ivan didn't do the work, Toomes did. Toomes was the one who performed. He was the one who did the work, while Ivan just coasted on his momentum in PCW. No, Toomes was the one, who coming from a dying federation in CCW, rocketed past his peers and snatched the OSW World Title and the owner of the company to boot. Not too shabby for someone beneath the notice of a Russian who didn't realize that communism was dead for about 10 years now and that his country was a joke. Even with all the other things that were going on - the Vax trying to kill Jane and sabotaging everything, his stable deteriorating and splitting apart, the biker coming in and Jackhammering him on the steel ringsteps - not to mention costing him a match against Aimz and Tempest... Despite all this, and IN spite of this, he would walk out of the Joe Louis Arena on Sunday, not in a stretcher like he did in November, but with the World Title still around his waist. He was the champion. The World Champion. He walks towards the locker rooms, these thoughts swirling in his head, steeling him, feeding him. It would take everything he had to beat Ivan on Sunday, the most high-profile match in his career. Sebastian Toomes versus Ivan Stanislav. No, the jackasses in CCW who were going to be in attendance earlier in the evening, the ones who held him down and said he couldn't do it, wasn't worthy of anything... They would see him, taking on the biggest man that came out of PCW. Meanstreak was a monster, no doubt about it, but Ivan carried alot of respect among the community. It would make Toomes' victory over him on Sunday even more sweet. And he WOULD beat him, because unlike Ivan, Toomes had everything to lose. What did Ivan have, besides his stupid outdated ideology? And a dippy Chinese bitch. Toomes stops at the entrance to the locker room. It was 24 hours before the big thing, but he might as well start now, limbering up. Tomorrow was going to be hell. But unlike Ivan, Sebastian was going to be the one clawing his way out of it. There was no other choice.
