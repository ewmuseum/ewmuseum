GWAR (Multi-Person Roleplay Part 1)

26 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-26-02 Starring: Jesse Ramey from Old School Wrestling as Rob Van Attitude Brad Lamen from Cyberspace Championship Wrestling as The HurraLamen Meanstreak from Old School Wrestling as “The Big Streak” Paul Mean The beginning of “One of a Kind” by Breaking point blasts over the house PA and the fans begin to go totally nuts. One of a kind! I’ve got this feeling deep inside, But what it is I don’t know, My vengeance starts to change, My mind gets filled with rage, I raise my fist up to the sky! Rob Van Attitude walks out onto the ramp way and the fans go nuts. What da ya see? The fear I see when I look in your eyes, Makes you believe I’m one of a kind, The fear I leave in the back of your mind, Makes you believe I’m, One of a kind! “Making his way to the ring, weighing in at two hundred and five pounds, hailing from Battlecreek, Michigan. He is…” RVA stops and as it is said, does the thumbs, “Rob Van Attitude!” The fans go wild as he makes his rounds around the ring. Hey! Hey! Hey! One of a kind! Hey! Hey! Hey! One of a kind! And now I feel like I’m alive, Don’t know just how I got so low, These voices in my mind, Tell me it’s killing time, And that’s a thrill I can’t describe. What da ya see? The fear I see when I look in your eyes, Makes you believe I’m one of a kind, The fear I leave in the back of your mind, Makes you believe I’m, One of a kind! Hey! Hey! Hey! One of a kind! Hey! Hey! Hey! One of a kind! Hey! Hey! Hey! Slowly making his way into the ring he stands up on the ropes and looks at the screaming fans. One of a kind! Hey! Hey! Hey! One of a kind! The fear I see when I look in your eyes, Makes me believe, Makes you believe, The fear I see in the back of your mind, Makes you believe, Makes me believe. The fear I see when I look in your eyes, Makes you believe I’m one of a kind, The fear I leave in the back of your mind, Makes you believe, Makes me believe! He hops down and makes his way over to the other ring post, climbs up on the ropes and looks out at the crowd and does the triple thumbs again; much to the approval of the fans. The fear I see when I look in your eyes, Makes you believe I’m one of a kind, The fear I leave in the back of your mind, Makes you believe I’m one of a kind. Hey! Hey! Hey! One of a kind! Hey! Hey! Hey! One of a kind! Hey! Hey! Hey! One of a kind! Hey! Hey! Hey! One of a kind! RVA walks around the ring, looking out at the crowd and then asks for a microphone. Slowly the ring announcer makes her way up onto the apron and hands him the microphone. “Welcome to GWAR!” The fans go wild in the arena. “Tonight, I was challenged to a match.” RVA chuckles. “What can I say? I accepted, but only because I’m who I am and that’s Rob,” Thumbs, “Van,” Thumbs, “Attitude!” The fans go wild again as RVA begins to fumble around a little bit. “Whoa, I need to lay off the dope.” RVA chuckles. “Now, HurraLamen, get out here now!” “Stand back! There’s a Lamen coming through!” Comes from the loud speakers as The HurraLamen’s theme song hits the house PA. He walks out from the back carrying a microphone. “Evil doer.” Pause. “Do you not know who I am?” The HurraLamen looks at RVA with a weird tight lip. RVA looks at HurraLamen and just shakes his head, “Dude, you wanted this fight. So, if you want to do this, then come on down here.” “Whachamacallit?” The crowd breaks down into a roar of laughter as The HurraLamen makes his way down to the ring. “Dear God fans!” Come the words from the ring announcers’ table. “Watch out RVA, look behind you! This was all one big set-up!” A huge roar of boos covers the arena as “The Big Streak” Paul Mean comes out of nowhere and walks up behind RVA. He taps RVA on the shoulder and he turns around just smiling. Mean grabs RVA by the head and pushes him back flat on his ass. RVA scrambles back trying to escape the massive man, but gets caught by the leg. Mean roars out at the crowd as he lifts RVA over his head. Mean brings RVA with a loud thunderous thud and the crowd is irate. “Damn it. Someone stop this madness!” Come the words from the ring announcers’ both again. “This is sickening. Look at that little arrogant prick of a wrestler, HurraLamen laughing this on.” A shot of HurraLamen is shown laughing and pointing at the fallen RVA. He motions for Mean to leave the ring and he does so. Stepping over the top rope with a single motion. He comes to the ground and stands next to HurraLamen engulfing him in total darkness. “This isn’t all that you can expect Attitude!” Exclaims HurraLamen as he and Mean walk up the ramp and into the backstage area. EMTs make their way down the ramp and into the ring to help RVA back up the ramp and into the backstage area. The fans continue to cheer him on as he walks into the backstage area. Miraculously he rebounds completely and makes his way over to a water cooler where Mean is standing. “Good job, Mean.” Attitude says. “I think it was all worth it. This is going to play out very well in the coming weeks.” “Yeah,” Mean says as he takes a drink of the water he has, “You certainly did your part in it all.” All of a sudden, HurraLamen comes into the picture. “Good evening citizen Mean.” HurraLamen looks at Attitude. “Good evening evil doer, Attitude.” Attitude gets a look of complete and totally irritation on his face, “Listen Lamen! I’m getting sick of you coming into the back and thinking you are still that damn stupid character of yours. You don’t see me coming back here and doing the thumbs do you?” “Calm yourself evil doer.” Lamen says. “I’m going to kill him!” Attitude says as he leaps forward grabbing Lamen by the throat. Changing from the backstage area to a remotely different screen shot of the GWAR logo. “Tune in tomorrow for another exiting installment of GWAR Wrestling.” ~To be Continued~
