Elements of Life: Ultra Violence

25 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 25, 2002 “Dat bitch had no chance!” says a dark man, small, hidden in the shadows of a local Charlotte bakery. “We’d be popin’ her in da back and she ain’t even know it!” “True dat, true dat…” replies a taller man. Neither of them knows what they have sinned. Neither knows that sins aren’t reality. Both know that they have killed in reality. And now their time has come… The two men, ‘gangsters’ from the streets of Charlotte, have preformed two acts of killings in one day: first, killing a rival gang member in the back lot of ‘Jimmy’s Hut’, and the second at a drive-by killing an innocent young woman, Isiic Raiyn’s girlfriend. The smaller man, whom they call Eeze, inhales a puff of smoke from his pack of 50 cigarette pack. “Man, I can’t believe dis crap we’ve done today.” “Damn dawg, I’s be knownin’ how you’s feel.” replies the taller man, also known as Krup. “We done did need some mo’ dough.” “I know’s, but we ain’t got notin’ to steal.” Krup snickers. “Oh yea we do… ya know dat one shop down at da corner of Street 37 and Gritts?” “Ya’ll mean dat one freak shop?” Krup lets out a loose breathe of laughter. “Yea, yea, dat one. Dey call it ‘Gate Une’, dem shittas ain’t know what’s ‘bout ta go down.” Eeze shakes his head. “Nah way, bruddah. Dis is how we’s do it.” Without hesitation, the two gangsters recruit themselves from the shadows, and begin walking down the street. Their destination: Gate Une. After four minutes of walking and talking about their plan, Eeze and Krup reach the entrance of Gate Une, the local ‘goth’ store in Charlotte. Each of tjem pull a gun out of their shinny black pants, and charge into the store with violent intentions in their heads. “Alright ya’ll, nobody move! We’s bustin’ into ya’ll fucka’s place here, and we ain’t leavin’ until you give us some cash!” Krup says sternly. All of the shoppers, who are all mostl;y wearing black with multiple piercings, are paying close attention and following the orders of Eeze and Krup. Krup points his gun rapidly at random directions. “Now, I want everybody ta get dey ass’s on da floor until we’s be outta here! Do it now, ya bitches!” Everyone plops onto the ground, some crying from the intensity level, others just waiting for a chance to escape. But one kid doesn’t follow these directions. The kid just continues to streak his hands through his spiky red hair while looking around the store. “Hey, freak boy, get yo white ass on dis floor befoe’ I blow ya damn head off!” The kid still stands, paying no attention. Instead, he walks over to the checkout counter, and places a shirt onto the glass counter, awaiting assistance. Krip begins to get extremely mad, thus firing a bullet near the disobeying kid’s head. “Get yo bitch-ass down or I swear I will blow ya a whoe’ in yo ass!” he shouts. The kid does not flinch from the gunshot. Instead, he turns around, facing both Eeze and Krup. His white skin shines in the florescent lights, visioning off his minor black face paint. He lifts his head up, revealing his white eyes. “Yes?” the kid asks. Krup turns frantically at Eeze with a questioning look. “Wha’ bia?” The kid smiles a familiar sadistic smile. “Are you ready?” he asks calmly. “Fo’ wha’? Eeze asks forcefully, showing that he too can shake things up. Silence… “To die?” Within a split second, the kid, who is obviously the one called ‘Corg’, jumps up, and kicks both guns out of Krup and Eeze’s hands. Within that same jump, Corg extends his hands into the other men’s jaws, causing them to fall back onto the floor. Corg inhales once, trying to catch his breathe. “I believe we have already met, gentlemen. Earlier today, you two were the lovely men who killed my girlfriend.” A tear rifts from Corg’s eye, as he tries to hold back the sadness which is embedded into his heart. Eeze and Krup look at each other and try to talk their way out of it. Corg just shakes his head, in despise of the two’s ignorance. “Tell me, kind sirs… are you ready for a bit of the old, Ultra Violence?” Eyes can be seen blinking around the globe, as the scene shifts back to Gate Une, to find Corg, Eeze, and Krup to have disappeared. Welcome… The two men float, watching the absorbent colors circle. The feel weightless. This won’t take long…</i. Jesus is seen dieing, and the two watch their savior blow up in flames. This shall be quite painful… And it was. fin
