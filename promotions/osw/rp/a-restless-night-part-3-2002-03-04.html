A Restless Night (Part 3)

4 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/4/02 We see Walczak still lying on the bed looking at the ceiling as the other children come into the room to get ready for bed. All of them look at Walczak lying on the bed his white sheets now soaked in the red of his blood. He does not pay attention to any of them, and just continues to look at the ceiling. Finally a nun, different from the one that had beaten him, comes over to him. “Thomas, you should go and wash up now, so that you can get rid of all of that blood that is on your back.” “I don’t want to. It is a joy of mine. I am sure that the blood will be soothing to me I my sleep.” “Thomas, I am not trying to be mean, I just want you to be clean when you are sleeping. I pleaded for her to allow you to wash up, so please don’t let my strife be in vain, and go wash up.” “Why, can’t that bitch be like you? Why does she have to be so mean? Does she not realize that I am nothing more than a child?” “That is because she is scared Thomas. You are more than just an ordinary child. You are a special child that will amount to great thing, and she is worried that pretty soon your accomplishments will overshadow those of her. Now, please go and wash up.” “I will but just for you, I will never again do anything that that skanky bitch asks me to do.” Walczak takes off his shirt, and heads over to the bathroom to wash up, as the kindly sister starts to change the bloodied sheets on his bed. As he heads into the shower the scene starts to fade out, and in real time Walczak begins to wake up. After yawning Walczak begins to speak. “Wow, what a dream that was. I could barely sleep, and only for that short time at the end when that Sister Carla was consoling me was I actually relaxed. How I hated that orphanage, only a handful of people like Sister Carla actually mattered, and they did not even come close to outnumbering those that I despised just like it is in life today. Jesse Ramey is one of those that I despise. I think of him as that nun, an obstacle to something greater in life, and that what is greater is the International title, and I will do anything to be victorious in this match. I will fight until the very last breath in my body, and even after that. I will be the number one contender coming out of Crowning Glory, but just because I have an important match on Sunday that does not mean that I have forgotten about Saturday. No, I will have pleasure in beating down the despicable carcass of Doug Troy and whatever crony he brings to his side. Now, maybe I can finally get some sleep.” Walczak lies down on his bed once again, and sure enough falls asleep almost instantaneously. As he falls asleep the scene fades out…
