I am reborn...I am demon...

24 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/24/02 Stream of conscious...ain't it great? It takes you, the average ho-hum Joe Nobody, and plants you directly into the mind of a monster. Where you are able to peruse, browse, observe, be truly entertained, and then leave. And go directly back to your boring life. We are all in luck as we stumble on a reborn Frankie Bones. Motivation, determination, all that good shit is back. So his mind must be leveling off right? He's stabilizing correct? Wrong... We find the Frankie of old. Determined, focused, and motivated, but still completely off his rocker. And we find him in a place he has strayed from for far too long. A place he used to call home. A place he used to frequent...his dreams... The air is heavy and thick, as it always tends to be... There is a sense of uprising. Be it smoke, noise, or just that sixth sense of inevitability. Whatever it is, it is clear that something is coming... Then, out of the depths of the darkness, a body begins to arise... As if rising from a swamp, the dark, enormous body of Frankie Bones floats up out of nowhere... His body appears darker then usual, it is dripping with a substance, over saturated. That substance, is blood... He extends both hands as a snarl curls his lips. A low guttural growl fills the air... The beast is being born... The growl grows and fills the air of this unknown space. Many noises join in the uprising. Sounds frighteningly familiar. The laughter of old old ladies, witches cackling, something straight out of MacBeth. The intense sobbing of young children, crying that can only make your smallest hairs stand on end. Are you sure you want to be here? Frankie's eyes appear to glow green, his teeth gleam and sparkle, fangs of gold frame the embossed 'demon'. His head leans back as his hands rise higher. The growl becomes a scream, drowning out the cackling and sobbing. The blood seemingly reverberates off his body... The beast is born... This monster of a man looms large. He turns his gaze downward, from where he arose. The thick air parts, the invisible becomes visible... Frankie is towering above a vast cityscape. Cars, people, everyday life, he is above it all. The snarl turns to a smirk. He is above all, he is on top... A low, demonic voice fills the air as the beast's lips move, 'Somewhere, down in the depths, down in the grime and filth. Somewhere down there wallows the souls of the lost. Jesus St. Crux, Drew Hostyle, Sebastian Toomes, Graphic Violence, Da Westsidaz, Vendetta, Aimz, Stanislav, they have all found their homes in the filth. I have risen above everything. I will return them to their slop, I will expose them in their grime.' He stands, looming above an endless scene of landscapes below. Time ticks by, and he takes it in. The dark red blood dripping from his body, he seems at home in it... After minutes upon minutes of observing, he begins to speak again... 'I am back... I am reborn... I am demon...'
