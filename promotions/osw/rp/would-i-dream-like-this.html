Would I dream like this if I WASN'T a cocaine addict?

6 Apr 2002

Amy 'Aimz' Campbell · Rent-A-Hero — a dream, then home

Would I dream like this if I WASN'T a cocaine addict? Written: April 6th, 2002 (I had the creepiest dream last night...for some reason, I adapted it to RP...so here you go.) The rumor started with the fall of darkness. It spread through the night, whispered on the flower-scented night breeze, sung by the nightingale, mentioned by the owl. The rumor spread with excitement and joy among the young, modern parts of the mind...yet there were those among the more sensible cells, the ones that knew of this X-Chromazoid, that shook their heads, frowned upon this and went to great lengths to caution against it... The rumor? The army of the great red vixen marched this night...with the intent to lay siege to the greatest treasure of them all, a hero's purity. What are we talking about? Sex in a nutshell. Strong among those who objected were the parts of him that had long patrolled and guarded the borders of what he considered to be his super-heroic virginity...they didn't like the advances of this fiery vixen, they didn't like the new emotions she brought with every little glance. These pieces of RAH had watched in awe-struck grief as the woman slowly killed every single defense he had...she had worn him thin, thin enough to give into her desires; That, in itself, would kill off all the good inside of him. They had fought the cruel strippers, hookers Jeremy Howard had wrought upon him long ago, but nothing...NOTHING back then was nearly as difficult to fight as this...this trollop, the very thing that would bring all the good, gloried aspects of him crashing down! In this dream of hers, an army marched across a great border...the magical shield that once sheltered the land, the man in all of it's innocence now fallen to the power of her advancing forces. This dream...that night, the moon lifted high in the heavens, the single moon, the moon that his conscience had never grown accustomed to seeing where stars of innocence, youth and sweet virginity once hung. The guards on the walls, the outposts of the innermost city, beneath the spandex, waited...watching for any sign of their enemy, the army of a well-experienced vixen of sorts and eventually they got what they wanted, they laid eyes upon the march of humans toward their holy city. Her force was small to say the least, twenty knights clad in ceremonial armor to the front and a hundred or so footmen to the rear. The army was a shabby one, even the knights rode afoot...their horses long dead from wear, tire or having been eaten by their starving masters on the long road, during the long battle it had taken to lower the very outermost defenses of this great hero. Only one Knight rode, and that was their leader...a slender, beautiful figure mounted on a steed the color of the darkest blood. A thousand virgin archers, armed with the storied bow of innocence, legendary for it's accuracy, gazed down upon the dark army, and each picked out his or her target. There were so many archers that, had the order been given, each one of these soldiers of sin would have been struck full with as many arrows as there are quills on a porcupine. The woman, the leader, rode unerringly straight toward the silver gates...intent and unflinching as she entered arrow range. She could have easily been struck dead right there but even the good, ESPECIALLY the good, in Rent-A-Hero had fallen to the spell she weaved. Her intentions were good, but she didn't know the sort of forces she was dealing with...thus making her an enemy, a threat to this great barrier. Her knights came behind her, footsoldiers followed...her army, now in sight of these defenses, went to pains to dress their lines, straighten their backs and march proudly. This would very possibly be another long, painstaking battle again the great hero's innocence... And then she woke up. (PS: I hope you've learned something from this...DO NOT take candy from Breanne, no matter what she says it is.) It was the morning before Slam and Amy, as expected, was fairly excited the moment she opened her eyes...but the dream she had just experienced lingered, she couldn't get rid of RAH in the back of her mind. Again, she was thinking about him almost non-stop...it was beginning to make her physically ill and that wasn't even the half of it. Thoughts of Rent-A-Hero were majorly distracting Amy from her main goals regarding OSW at this point...that fact just wasn't acceptable, she couldn't let him invade her mind to that degree. She HAD to see him, or even speak to him...she just wanted that contact, so badly that the realization that she wasn't near him at that very moment made her stomach churn. Was this that love thing everyone's always going on about? She remembered all-but-ancient song, 'Love Hurts' by Nazareth...a golden oldie that set the pace for her head right now. If this was love, it really did hurt...when you couldn't have the person you wanted, it caused near physical pain, a pain that was far worse right now than Meanstreak and 3Bi combined could ever inflict on her. Well, if she could endure this for these long days then she could surely take anything that Drew Hostyle or his Brooklyn Bi and Bred buddies could throw at her. Amy was just barely awake, she must've taken a nap on the chair. She was looking at a small promo photo of she and Rent-A-Hero from their PWA days, when that familiar voice rung out in a pace that Aimz had only recently gotten used to. ''Boo to being smitten with Spandex-Boy!'' She stopped a moment only to pull Amy up to her feet...noting that she was unusually drabby today, wearing a pair of camouflage cargo pants and a little black tank-top. ''You and I...'' Breanne continued. ''...are going shopping!'' Amy looked down on her best friend with an odd expression. ''Bre...you and shopping don't mix, remember? What's gotten into you?'' ''Trying to get your mind off of Rent-A-Fag, that's what...now let's haul ass!'' At least she meant well. Breanne was a friend since ninth grade, Aimz had no reason not to trust her to do this...she wanted only the best and, although she disapproved of Amy being so obsessive about the spandex-clad 'superhero', Bre could always help. She was almost three years older than Amy, twenty two years old at this point, but they were close as could be...inseparable to a degree, and Breanne just wouldn't allow the redhead to fret about 'Hero-Boy', nor to worry about getting hurt on Slam. So they departed, Bre with best intents in mind and Aimz with RAH's feelings, and Drew Hostyle's eminent struggle on hers.
