Date: August 23, 2002 “Just sit still, Isiic, we’re almost there.” I tried lifting myself to peer out the window, but I couldn’t. My spine was tingling, and I couldn’t feel my neck. My whole body was trembling, although I couldn’t feel it. Thanks to Ivan Stanislav, I was a walking piece of shit. A living, breathing piece of shit. Doctors had told me that I was unfit to wrestle, or to do anything for that matter. They said I had a broken right hand, a sprained neck, a dislocated right shoulder blade, fractured jaw, and a collapsed larynx. Now, I had no idea what any of this meant, but I sure as hell was feeling its pain. And as life had fucked with me countless times before, it was fucking with me once again. Even though I wasn’t healthy to wrestle, Jane fucking Reagan put me in a match on Slam. Is she trying to kill me? Doesn’t she know that I was nearly killed last week? Well, she’s pitiful, if you ask me. She doesn’t know who she’s fucking with, because if I had my way, I’d be fucking her. “How are you doing back there, love?” Shyme asked, looking back at me from the passenger’s seat. “Fine, I guess.” I said, leaning against the back of the rattling van. “My jaw hurts like fuck, though.” She smiled a fake smile, trying her best to lighten things up. “Well be happy, we’re ten minutes away.” “Actually, Shyme, if we keep going at this rate, we’ll be there in approximately four minutes and eighteen seconds.” Neil said while he switched to the right lane, preparing to get off three exits ahead. Neil was my good friend, probably my best. He worked in an underground wrestling circuit, and now he was helping me out when I needed him most. I was so depressed that I couldn’t even think, I mean, I was a blob, like I had just gotten liposuction or something. “Shut up, fucker.” Shyme said, glaring at Neil for his smartass answer. “We’ll be there when we get there.” Shyme and Neil never really got a long too well, but they managed. They knew they had to get a long, because if they didn’t, I would most likely be out of a job. We drove across a bridge. It was ridged and bumpy all the way through, it tossed my back around which didn’t feel too great. The pain in my back caused the pain in my neck to get stronger, which caused the worst pain of all which was in my jaw to get even worse than all of my pain put together. But I was able to read a sign as we crossed the bridge, it read … Welcome to New York This was going to be a long weekend. I wish I had some marijuana right about now.
