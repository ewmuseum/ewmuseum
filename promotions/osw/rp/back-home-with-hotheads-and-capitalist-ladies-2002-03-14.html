Back Home, With Hotheads and Capitalist Ladies

14 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14.03.02 He felt like a millionaire. Never before had he had this much money. It felt to him as if he could have anything he wanted. The wind blowing in his hair felt as if a gorgeous blonde was running her hands through his hair, her delicate fingers brushing his hair back with caring hands. Up to 4th gear, the engine roared as he floored the pedal. It sounded as if a canister of fuel had exploded as he car fired off down the deserted country road, passing fields and farms with no other noticeable vehicles in sight. The steel of the pedal under his foot stayed perfectly still as his brand new loafers gripped the pedals like adhesive, 'Roar, my little beauty, roar,' screamed Krieg as his brand new Mustang raced along the countryside road, its blue body glimmering in the sunlight. This is a spectacular vehicle. I need to give the American’s some credit, this automobile can sure tear up the road. The interior isn’t too bad either. I’d have to say: this automobile is one which could rival the likes of BMW and Mercedes, but the only thing that.... As Krieg thinks about the car, his attention diverts from the road and as he screams around a corner, he approaches a set of traffic lights exceptionally quickly, screeching to a halt when he realises what is going on. His heart racing ever so slighty, Krieg looks around the road, and with a Mercedes Compressor sitting next to him, Krieg’s attention isn’t really on the bodywork: more like the bodywork of the 2 fine ladies sitting in the back! Only thing is, there’s two muscular guys sitting in the front, their hair slicked back, their tops tight around their muscular arms and their German pop songs playing on their stereo. The two men catch a glimpse of Krieg looking at, presumably, their girlfriends, the German War Machine flashing a smile at the two ladies as the giggle between themselves. Rather peeved off with Krieg’s charm, the passenger shouts over to him: “Hey you! Hey, you big dumb oaf,” shouted a dark haired man, looking around about 25-ish with a neat goatee beard and shoulder length black hair. “Are you talking to me,” questioned Krieg, turning his stereo down slightly to answer them. “Yeah. Are there any other Neanderthals around here,” responds the passenger, slapping a high-five with his buddy in the driver’s seat. “Well, I could say something about your mother in my back seat, but I won’t go there,” says Krieg, a smirk cracking the side of his mouth. Clenches his fists, the passenger fires right back at Krieg, “We...We....Well, if you’re so tough, how about a race, you imbecile,” “Sure, but you’re mother’s weighing me down in the back. I’ll need to get her out of the car,” “Enough about my mother, you scheinhund! We’ll race you to the Town Hall, and the winner....and the winner gets to keep the losers car! Yeah! That’s a great idea,” proposed the passenger. Thinking about the offer for a second, Krieg replies, “Yeah, sure. But you better make sure that your car is in working order.....My mother needs a new set of wheels,” mocked Krieg. Disregarding the cheap comment, the passenger turns back round to his driver buddy and says something to him, making both men snigger, before turning round to the two ladies and telling them to “sit down and buckle in, ‘cause it’s going to be a fast trip!” Krieg laughs to himself as he flicks open his glove box, and a CD holder slides out, showing a collection of CD’s, most probably Krieg’s favourites for driving too. Flicking through them for a second, Krieg spots the one he wants and slides it into the CD player. The title flicks across the CD screen as the song begins to blare through the car speakers. .....Track 1....“Lit Up” – Buckcherry..... Turning round to his racing opponent, Krieg forces a smile to the two males in the front as they growl back at him, and then turns round to the ladies and flashes a killer smile, sending them into a spell of ‘girly giggles’. The two drivers rev their engines as the lights on the opposite side of the road change back to amber, nearing the time when Krieg’s lights will turn to green. Krieg slams his foot down on the accelerator and the engine roars as the Compressor driver turns round, trying to shield an expression of fear on his face. Suddenly, the lights change from red to amber, and the two men shove their cars into first gear, and in a seconds notice, they shoot off down the road and the lights go green. 2 of the flashiest cars in motoring at the moment speed down the dual carriageway, passing through the suburbs of Berlin at speeds over 90 miles per hour. As the two cars race, the Compressor’s driver looks rather tense and focused on concentrating on the road and not Krieg’s driving, whereas Krieg cruises along, a foot or two in front of the Compressor’s bumper, with a grin plastered across his face as he signs along to the song and looks around at the other car. “I’m on a train, and ride on...staying off the tracks...Yeah...I’m all lit up again!......” Singing the occasional few words, Krieg seems to be enjoying himself as he begins to edge forward, beating the Compressor by at least 6 of 7ft now. Looking ahead of him, Krieg’s face shows a massive smile, and suddenly, he starts to slow down ever so slowly, letting the Compressor catch up with him as it begins to push past his Mustang, the driver and passenger ecstatic. But all of a sudden, a bright light flashes and 2 police cars pull out on the road about 50 metres ahead of the car. Skidding along the road, the Compressor screeches to a half a metre of so away from the two police cars. As Krieg’s Mustang comes to a halt about 20 metres away from the Compressor, police officers from both cars scramble out and drag the two males out of the car, pulling them towards the squad cars. The 2 men try to tell the police their reason for speeding, but the German police officers have none of it and shove them into the back of the car. Ha ha. Nice one. What a pair of suckers....They’ve obviously not been down this road before, it’s renowned for having a well-placed speed camera.... The two blondes make a subtle exit from the back of the car and begin to walk up the road, looking over their shoulders as their boyfriend get shoved into the car. Krieg rolls down the road, pulling up at the pavement near the girls and flashes them yet another one of his killer smiles. The two girls lean over the car, showing Krieg more cleavage than the Adult Channel, and the Red Army member forwards them to come into his car. But something suddenly hits him. “Hey! Wait a minute! Are you Capitalist or Communist,” questions Krieg. “Well, I’m a bit of both, but more Capitalist,” replied one of the girls. “I’m pretty much the same, really,” “Ok,” says Krieg, still motioning for them to get in the car, but as they go to get in, Krieg pushes them back out and slams the door shut, “No Capitalist will step into my car,” bawls Krieg as the two confused girls stand on the pavement. “Get out of here, you dirty sellouts!” “Wha....?” “You heard me! Get out of here, you stinking Capitalists,” roars Krieg, shifting his car into first gear and screaming off down the road, Damn Capitalists. Always trying to get a free ride, living off the backs of good willed Communists. I think it’s time to visit the ultimate Communist.....Father.
