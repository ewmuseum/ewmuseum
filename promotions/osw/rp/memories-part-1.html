Memories: Part 1

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Some takes place on 8/30/02. The rest takes place on 4/4/98. A dark room. A television set. A video cassette recorder on top of the television. A tape sticking out of the VCR. That’s all we see. No Kid Wonder in a pissed off state of mind. No Amanda being tortured by Scott Taylor. We have a TV, a VCR, and a tape. The TV is playing static. The VCR is turned on. And the tape sits there, waiting to be played. That’s the picture we get for a few minutes. Everything stands still. Finally, a white hand reaches into the picture and pushes the tape into the VCR. The static screen turns to black. In the background, the instrumental to “Bleed” by Cold softly plays to set a somber scene for us. We focus entirely on the television screen now. The picture is a bit blurry to start, but the sounds of a girlish giggle can be heard as clear as day. Within a few seconds, the picture starts to become much clearer. The day is April 4, 1998. The scene is Kid Wonder’s 18th birthday. It’s an outdoor event. Three birds are chirping on a tree limb. Under the tree sits a picnic table. On the table sits a giant birthday cake. Various people are roaming about. The picture zooms in on the cake. On the cake, it reads “Happy Birthday James”. “See that?” the voice from behind the camera asks. “That’s me. It’s my birthday.” Again, we hear the girlish giggle. The picture closes in on a close up of a pretty girl’s face. From the looks of it, it looks like Amanda. “Whoa,” James says from behind the camera. “Lemme zoom out a little bit. Hehe.” Again, Amanda giggles. The shot zooms out to show all of Amanda. She’s wearing a black Fiona Apple T-shirt and some denim jeans with a small hole in the right knee. Her light brown hair flows behind her with the gentle breeze. “Lookit. My boy is now a man,” Amanda says. “Now a man?” says James. “Girl, I’ve always been the man.” Amanda giggles again. “Oh, is that so,” Amanda says with a grin. “Well, now that you legally a man, that makes me illegal.” Amanda giggles. The shot does a complete 180. We now see Jimmy’s hair. James, of course, is Kid Wonder before becoming Kid Wonder. We see James’ face. “If I’m in jail or something next week,” Jim says with a grin, “you know why.” Again, Amanda giggles. “Hey son!” A yell from across the yard can be heard. The picture turns over to where the yell came from. Half way across the yard, at a grill, stands a middle-aged man with short, brown hair. He’s wearing a Miami Dolphins shirt and some black pants. James takes the time to zoom in a little closer without walking closer himself. “What’s up, pop!” Jimmy shouts back. We see Don Spinks, Jimmy’s father, flip a hamburger. “How ya like your burger?” “Not burnt!” James shouts to his dad with a little laugh. On the other end, Don flips another burger and lets out a little chuckle. “Oh, you’re a riot, son,” he tells James. “If I didn’t know any better, I’d have actually thought you were funny.” Don lets out a laugh. Meanwhile, the picture does another complete 180. We see James’ lovely face again. “That’s my dad,” Jim tells the camera. “He doesn’t think I’m very funny. I don’t know why. I mean, I’m the funniest person I know.” “Is that so?” we hear Amanda say with a giggle. The picture turns back to where Amanda is standing. “Shush, woman,” James tells his girlfriend. “You’ll ruin my reputation before I even get it.” Amanda just giggles, as she has been doing. “Yo Jimbo!” Another shout for the camera holder is heard. James turns the picture to the fence where a black man hops over into the yard. He’s a young looking black man. About 5’11 and 185 pounds and a shaved head. He is wearing a Warren Sapp Bucs jersey and some baggy jeans. The guy looks to be about 20. In his hand is a box/case. “Yo Andre! What’s up? Thought you’d never get here.” We see the black man, Andre Watson, a childhood friend of James’, walk towards the birthday boy. “Yo boy, have I got a surprise for you,” he says as he stands about 10 feet from James and the camera. Andre opens up the box/case he’s holding. Sitting right dab in the center is a nice stogie. “A cigar?” he asks as Andre snickers. “What am I gonna do with a cigar?” “In honor of your 18th birthday, and the fact you’re legal and all…” Andre snickers a little. “For your 18th birthday, I got you an honorary stogie.” Watson takes the stogie out of the box/case and hands it to James. The birthday boy reaches his hand out to grab it. “You know,” Spinks says to his boy, “I know it’ll end up killing me in the long run, but I’ll light it up.” “What?” Amanda asks. No giggle this time. “Later,” finishes James. “Right now, I’m doing something.” Andre shrugs his shoulder. “A’ight,” he says while putting the stogie back in the box/case. “But if it’s still in there next time I see you, I’ll kill you.” Andre laughs as he places the box/case carrying the stogie down. “Hey Jim!” shouts someone totally new. Jimmy turns towards the gate as a 6’2 white boy who weighs anywhere between 215 and 225 pounds. He has medium length black hair and a small goatee. The guy is wearing a Florida Panthers hockey jersey and some baggy jeans. “Hey dude,” says Wonder. “How was the trip to Germany?” The guy, who is barely 18 himself, approaches with a bottle in his hand. “Dude, it was so awesome!” he exclaims. “I got to get drunk as hell, and my parents didn’t care!” “No shit, Ronnie,” says Andre, who still happens to be standing there. He’s off camera though. Ronald Stillson, another good friend of James’, looks in the general direction of Andre. “Yeah, dude. Did you know, in Germany, you only have to be 18 to drink.” “Really?” asks Jimmy. “Hell yeah, dude! Oh, I got you this,” Ron tells James. Ronnie hands a bottle of Bärenjäger Honey Liqueur to James. Spinks reaches his hand out and grabs the bottle from Stillson. “Alcohol?” he asks Ron. “Yeah, dude. Thought you’d enjoy it,” he says. “Ron, dude, this is America!” he tells his friend. “You can’t drink until you’re 21! Remember?” “Who cares?” says Ronald. “It’s not like anyone but us will know about it anyway.” “You even think about opening that and I’ll kill you!” shouts a female voice from the distance. Jimmy moves the picture over to where the shout came from. Over there stands a middle-aged woman. She’s wearing a white dress with rose decorations on it. You know, the kind of dress a mother wears when totally embarrassing a kid. “Yes mother,” James says in a bit of a dejected voice. “You know, son…” The picture shifts from the Mary Spinks, the mother of James, to Don. “Maybe you should give that to me for safe keeping,” Don tells his son. From behind the camera, we hear a sigh. Suddenly, the picture does a complete 180. “You see this?” he tells the camera. The shot goes to the bottle of alcohol being held in his other hand. Then it shifts back to Jimmy. “I bet I’ll never get a swig of it.” There’s another sigh as the picture fades out. The picture comes back a little later that same day. Again, we are greeted by Amanda’s giggling. In fact, the picture shows her giggling. The camera is now still. Apparently it is sitting somewhere to where it can’t move without someone moving it manually. In the picture, James Spinks is standing up. He is wearing a Trent Dilfer Bucs jersey and some silk pants. In his hand is a piece of birthday cake. The only other person in the picture is Amanda. She’s watching him take a bite of the cake. As Jimmy takes a bite, Amanda giggles. “What?” asks James while chewing. Amanda giggles. “Nothing.” Jimmy swallows the bite of cake. “What?” “You got a little frosting…” Amanda points to her upper lip while talking, apparently trying to tell her boyfriend he’s got something on his lip. Suddenly, Amanda leans towards James and gives him a kiss right smack on the lips. “Don’t worry,” she says with a pretty smile, “I got it off for you.” James doesn’t say anything at first. He just lets out a tiny laugh and has a smile on his face. “Remember son,” we hear Don say off picture, “she’s still 16. Don’t do anything stupid.” A few people around the area laugh, none of which we see. “Daaaad,” Jimmy groans while rolling his eyes. Amanda, on the other hand just smiles. “Don’t listen to him,” she tells her man. Then, again, Amanda gives Jimmy a big kiss right on the lips. Instead of saying something, Amanda just giggles. “What was that for?” asks James. “Just because I wanted to,” says Amanda with a smile. She then grabs her boyfriend’s hand. “Now eat some more cake.” Amanda giggles. Jimmy opens his mouth, but as Amanda is about to shove the piece of cake down his throat, Jimmy backs his head away. The two laugh together as Amanda tries getting Jimmy to eat the cake. The original shot that was focused entirely on the television slowly pulls away from the television. The picture on the TV screen remains, but now we see the entire TV. We also see the VCR that’s playing the tape still sitting on top of the TV. We see the hand reach back into the shot and press the “Stop” button. The same hand then presses the “Eject” button. A second or two later, the tape pops back out of the VCR. The hand grabs the tape and pulls it towards oneself. Shot does a complete 180. In the dark room, sitting on a broken, wooden chair, clutching the videotape is Kid Wonder. He just sits on the chair, holding the tape close to his heart. Sitting next to the chair is dozens of other tapes. Some are piled neatly. Others are scattered along the floor. But the tapes are still there regardless. But sitting on the chair, clutching the one tape, is Kid Wonder. His just sits there. He just clutches the tape close to his heart. He hangs his head down. And through the golden blonde hair that hangs down, we see a small tear come from his right eye. A tear of depression. A tear of helplessness. A tear. That’s all we see as we leave this poor man alone. Alone with his thoughts. Alone with his memories. Alone…without Amanda.
