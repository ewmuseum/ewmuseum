Things Can Only Get Better

14 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14-03-02 In the dark, candlelit dwelling that is Hessian’s chamber, there sit four figures. As the dancing flame warmly caresses the faces of the four individuals, the simultaneous sound of low growling and sighing can be heard. The thoughts of a failed quest ripe in their twisted minds, the four sup from their respective silver goblets, each filled with some kind of exotic alcohol. The four men glance across to each other and finally the man on the second right speaks. [i]”Ahh…very possible, that, very possible.”[/i] claims Minion, swirling the alcohol in its silver container. [i]”Nothing like a good goblet of Sang de Gaul wine, eh Cain?”[/i] adds Caleb, licking the excess from his parched lips. [i]“You're right there Caleb, too true.”[/i] replies Cain, running a cold, pale finger around the rim of the goblet. Azrael opens his mouth to continue, but is stopped in mid-sentence by the booming, semi-drunk voice of his partner. [i]”Who’d a’ thought thirty years ago we'd all be sittin' here drinking Sang de Gaul wine?”[/i] Hessian booms, his wine spilling over the rim of the goblet. [i]“Aye. In them days, we'd a' been glad to have the price of a cup o' Satan’s Home Brewed tea!”[/i] squeals Minion, beginning to giggle slightly. [i]“A cup o' COLD home brewed tea!”[/i] Azrael chimes in. [i]“WI’OUT MILK OR SUGAAAAAAAR…!”[/i] shouts Hessian in a deep, awkward voice. [i]“OR tea! Heh-heh-heh!”[/i] replies Caleb, chuckling gruffly. [i]”OR HERMASWEETA’S!!!……………Or Caniston………”[/i] shouts Hessian, taking a large swig of his “Sang de Gaul”. [i]”We drank our tea out of a filthy, cracked rat…” [/i] says Minion, staring into the candlelight. [i]”We never used to have a rat. We used tae have tae drink our tea………Heh-heh-heh…out of a passing Jew’s slipper.”[/i] Hessian says, laughing deeply and trying to down his booze. [i]”The best WE could manage was to suck on a piece of damp child, fresh from the morgue.”[/i] replies Cain, pointing a satisfied finger at the Titanic Satanic Insaniac. [i]”Aye but you knoooooow, we were happy in’T those days, even though we were poor.”[/i] Caleb sighs, swirling his Sang de Gaul in the goblet. [i]”Aye, and BECAUSE we were poor…my old Dad used to say to me, “Money cannot buy you………a Torture Rack Lengthening Device…” or so he said…”[/i] replies Minion, the sense of wisdom escaping from his withered little beady black eyes. Hessian: [i]”’E was right. I was happier then and I had NOTHIN'. We used to live in this tiiiny little tumbledown old house, wit’… greaaaaat big…holes in’T roof!”[/i] Hessian exclaims drunkenly. Azrael Cain: [i]”A house? You were lucky, to have a HOUSE! We used to live in one room, a hundred and twenty-six of us, no furniture and half the floor was missing; we were all huddled together in one corner for fear of FALLING STRAIGHT INTO HELL ITSELF!”[/i] shouts Azrael, downing his wine in one gulp and grabbing the bottle to pour another. Caleb: [i]”You were lucky to have a ROOM! We used to have to live in’T the corridor, with a family of rats and a monkey to boot!”[/i] Caleb intervenes, his voice showing signs of homeless remembrance. Minion the Imp: [i]“Ohhhh we used to DREAM of livin' in a corridor! Woulda' been a palace to us. We used to live in an old sewage tank on a rubbish tip. We got woken up every morning at 6am by having a load of rotting Jew’s slippers dumped all over us! A house? Hmph!”[/i] Minion the Imp sips his Sang de Gaul and sneers at all three members of the Bloodlust. Hessian speaks up again after a quiet pause in the Chamber, and everyone’s goblets are refilled with the finest wine this side of the Mississippi. [i]“Well when I say wir house………it were more like a big ‘ole in’T ground, covered by a dirty old rotten tea towel stained in the orgiastic remnants of a gothic orgy…and it had a picture of a skeleton on it with a macadamia nut in its mouth, dropped by a passing Flying Monkey. But it was a house to US!”[/i] he shouts out proudly. [i]“We were evicted from our Flying Monkey; we had to go and live in a lake of fire!”[/i] replies Azrael Cain, satisfactorily nodding his head and smirking. [i]“You were lucky to live in a LAKE! There were a hundred and sixty of us living in a small shoebox with a dead cat in it, in the MIDDLE OF THE ROAD!”[/i] screams Caleb, the Sang de Gaul getting to his head. [i]“Was that a cardboard shoebox/coffin?”[/i]queries the 3’11” Imp they call Minion to which Caleb replies: - [i]“Aye they’re all made out of cardboard, aye.”[/i] [i]“Aaaaaaye, you were lucky. We lived for three months in a rolled up corpse in an elephant’s scrotum. We used to have to get up at five o'clock in the morning, unroll the corpse, lick the scrotum clean, eat a crust of stale toenail, go to work down’T mill for fourteen hours a day week in and week out. Then, when we got home, our Dad would thrash us to sleep with a bag full of poisonous testicles!”[/i] Minion retaliates, still trying to get one up on everyone else. [i]“Luxury! We used to have to get out of the lake at three o'clock in the morning, sweep the lake bed clean with our pubic hair if we had any left, eat a handful of hot gravel, go to work in hell every day for tuppence a month, come home, and Dad would beat us around the head and neck with a broken bottle, if we were LUCKY!”[/i] shouts Azrael Cain, the emotions running high in his tipsy head. [i]“Well o’ course we had it TOUGH! We used to have to get up out of the shoebox coffin at twelve o'clock at night, and LICK the road clean with our tongues…from Junctions 1 – 19 including Slipwaays! After a meal of our own shit…We then bathed our genitals in half a handful of freezing cold gravel; worked twenty-four hours a day at the mines………err………”[/i] Caleb stops in mid-sentence and looks at his goblet… [i]“I mean…in hell………oh I’m pissed now………and anyway, we’d work in hell for four pence every six years, and when we got home, our Dad would slice us in two with a bread knife and make a dog shit sandwich with each half.” [/i] Hessian, having sat by and listened to all of this for a while now stands up and throws his goblet of wine against the chamber wall. As the wine dribbles down the wall, accentuated by the candlelight, and looks to all other three men. Hessian: [i][b]“Right! I had to get up in the morning at ten o'clock at night, half an hour before I went to bed!!! Then I had to drink a cup of brimstone and old man’s liquidised stink, work twenty-nine hours a day down’T hell, and pay the hell owner…for’T PRIVILEGE OF LETTING US WORK THERE!!! Then, when we got home, our Dad and our mother would kill us………in cold blood mind you! Then they’d bury us and dance about on our graves singing “Yessir I Can Boogie!”[/i][/b] After an awkward silence the other three give in and giggle quietly, as Hessian sits back down and guzzles away the last of the Sang de Gaul, as Minion concludes the epic war of verbal exchange. [i]“But you try and tell the young people today that... and they’ll be highly sceptical!”[/i] he squeals, to a shower of Sang de Gaul all over his little cloak. Hessian throws the empty bottle of wine against the chamber wall, and growls deeply to himself. [i]“Aye those were the days, and then in this day and age we’re disgraced by a couple of loons who steal one of the greatest prizes in the OSW from us in our own devious plot!”[/i] Azrael gulps down the last of his wine and jumps up, looking Hessian in the face through the low candlelight of the chamber. [i]“Maybe so [b]Go[/b]lia[b]th[/b], but we can quench our thirst on the blood of a Monster and a Communist, you seem to forget. The titles are in the past, let us worry about them AFTER we feed on their egotistical minds and their hulking frames of fear. You beat Giroux once before, we can destroy them both together!”[/i] replies Azrael, with a sly grin on his face and a reassuring hand on Hessian’s shoulder. Hessian nods his head slowly, and begins to laugh in that old, maniacal way. [i]“Meanstreak and Ivan Stanislav are on The Highway To Hell!”[/i] With that the candlelight fades into a smouldering shadow of its former self as the powerful sound of Heresian laughter fills the chamber, and echoes into the night. [u]Fin[/u]
