Date: May 9th, 2002 So much can run through your head in a hospital bed... Like...? Rent-A-Hero, how OSW and PCW executives just told us earlier yesterday to cut off our relationship, for 'Inter-federation Reasons'... Like Ivan Stansilav...how he beat the living hell out of me, how he landed me here...how he caused me this searing pain that draws up with every movement. Every doctor I had spoken to since I came back to conciousness last night said I'd have to be out at least two weeks...so what did I do? Called head office and gave them the go-ahead to book me for this coming Slam........why? That's easy enough to figure out. Why did he do it, though? I'd been hanging around backstage and, since I hadn't been slated to fight that night, I had brought a hack and ended up playing around with it alongside some of the ring crew...it was innocent enough. So along came Stansilav and, almost immediately, I was on-edge...after all, the guy's like a million times my size, I knew the look in his eye meant something bad but you know what? Bears can smell fear...Russian Bears are no different, so I hid what I was really thinking, as fairly rare occurance with me. Okay, so I let a few slips of the tongue to weasel their way in...I think I called communism 'screwed up', though I probably could've used some markedly rougher language...but I slightly value my life, so I held back a pierced tongue. So what the hell was Ivan's problem? Everyone around me had disappeared...and he didn't think I was pissed about it, too? Quite frankly, I was beginning to suspect his Red Army...they could've easily just been looking to rid themselves of competition. I mean, who else would've had access to the information put before Tempest? The Army, in my opinion, which is usually right, seems to have gotten rid of the one thing that was softening their leader... Am I the only one who noticed that the other members of Stansilav's Red Army verbally objected to Tempest's presidence over Ivan's life? I certainly hope not...I mean, look at the facts! They had easiest access to the tools to get Tempest away...they can now brainwash Ivan back into being the heartless, stone-faced beast he once was... If you ask me, Stansilav's own army owns HIM. He's the proverbial puppet king, yet the scenario he's caught up in hasn't even begun to dawn upon the Russian dolt. Oh well...it's not my fucking problem anyway, Stansilav can do whatever the hell he'd like as long as he keeps away from me. If the beating I took on Slam repeats itself EVER again...I'm going apeshit on somebody's ass, whether I need an army to do it or not..........few things REALLY piss me off, but Ivan's cowardice is driving me off the edge now. He lost a chick, get the fuck over it...he's got more important things to think about, like the fact that his 'Army' is playing Gepetto to his Pinochio. Nothing I can say will convince him and, quite frankly, I have no intents of speaking to him in the near future, obviously...but if he's got half a free brain cell left, he'll pull his head out of his ass and realize what's being done to him. Again...what do I care, why am I letting this bullshit drag on in my head? God only knows...I guess I just feel bad for the poor bear. If only I could get out of this damned hospital bed, though...I could start TELLING people what I'm thinking, as opposed to sitting here, pulling the IV needle out of me every time they put it in me! There're few things I really, physically fear...but thin, medicinal needles are the biggies...another reason I despise hospitals with every ounce of my being. Pretty bad field of employment for somebody who hates hospitals, eh? So yeah...I've just laid here, waiting for them to say 'You're cleared to go.'.......but nothing...how shitty is that deal? RAH can't get here until tonight...so if it weren't for the...ummm...interesting guests I had visiting here today, I'd have gone insane by now... What visitors, you ask? I smell a flashback!!!!!! FLAAAAAAAASHBAAAAAAAACK... So everything was quiet in the room earlier that morning... Then the nurse walked in, time for visitor number one! ''Ahem...uhhh...Miss Campbell...'' ''...I'll get you a chair to stand on.'' When Sarah pulled a steel chair up, Campbell didn't even bother wincing at the scratching sound it made against the tiled floor...she was expending her energy trying to lean over the side of the bed to see who, or what, had come to visit. Once the chair was to the side of the bed, the nurse quickly made her exit and the next thing Amy saw was a hood...a purple face.... Then came the squeaky voice................this was definitely none other than...MINION THE IMP?! 'Oh god no! Amy! AMYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYY! CAN YOU HEAR ME??? HELLOOOOOOO!!! Oh poor baby...damn that Ivan...' Minion flopped up onto the chair and tapped Aimz on her shoulder, then looked to the heavens, shaking his fist furiously. 'DAMN THAT RUSSIAN!!!' He looked to Aimz again and leaned forward, then began to whisper. 'Amy.........Amy are you okay?' ''Jesus Christ...'' She shook her head a moment, swearing she was drugged...''That's some strong morphine, I'm seeing shit all purple...............'' 'YOU'RE AWAKE!!! PRAISE THE GREAT LORD BELOW!!!' Minion whooped with joy, and inched closer to Aimz. 'Are you okay? How are things? How many did you have? How are you doing?' He spoke in an almost slurred tone, still shaking nervously from Amy's 'acknowledgement' of him. ''Umm...things are good........I don't know how much they pumped into me and I could be doing better. Aren't you the little purple Yoda that's always with that Hessian guy? You two freak me out...............which brings me to another question: Why, exactly, are you visiting me...?'' 'Pumped into you???' Minion's face turned a shade of red (yeah, and Hell just froze over), and he looked Aimz over, his eyes squinting in frustration. 'How dare they desecrate this visage! Oh poor sweet dear, they've drugged you beyond belief...I AM MINION!!! ME BE HESSIAN'S COMPANION!!! YOU BE AMY CAMPBELL!!!' He paused, then quickly added, ''Oh...I was just in the neighborhood...'' To answer Amy's previous question. ''Well...okay...and you're damned right I'm a woman! Careful what you say about gender...that kinda thing gets a little purple dude into a hefty load of trouble sometimes.'' She was still looking at him, slightly disturbed and slightly confused. ''But...yeah...I'm still wondering why you're here..............everyone left for the flight yesterday, so you must've been a little farther than 'just the neighborhood'. Are you like on some kinda Imp LSD or something?'' Minion thought that over in his head... *LSD? Hey isn't that...Lesbians Sucking Dick? Wiping the sweat from his Impish brow, Minion continued. 'So Amy...where's Rent-A-Hero, shouldn't he be here comforting you?' ''He's busy with PCW and all...'' Aimz was solemn about it. ''...but he's hopefully gonna catch up to me by tomorrow. Plus, we're supposed to keep our thing a little hush...apparently the whole interfederation relationship concept knotted the panties of a few executives.'' Obviously the chance for a quickie has gone, and Minion's resorted to general conversation. Hearing a section of what the Imp had said, Amy looked at him with an odd expression for a moment before continuing. ''Things in OSW? They're pretty good...aside from Stansilav being a blind fool. Reagan's still in a coma and I'm laughin' all the way to the bank!'' 'You know...I do know a pair of giant Heretics...maybe uh, with a little persuasion I could uh, get you some revenge...whaddaya think?' Minion winked at Aimz. ''Nahhhh little man...it's alright,'' The redheaded 'punky' starlet smiled. ''I'm pretty sure I can handle my own...hehe. I don't need the Russian jack-off after me anymore than he already is, anyway......it seems altercations between he and I just don't wanna work to my benefit.'' ''Heh...I'm sure you'll be fine, I don't think even the biggest of bears could have anything against a little purple...ummm...thingy...like you.'' 'HE WON'T HAVE ANYTING AGAINST ME, HE'LL WANT SOMETHING.........you know.........AGAINST ME!!!' Minion squealed frightfully. Silence reigned while Minion looked like his head would explode at any moment. ''Okay Impster...you lost me there...'' 'Never mind...just...if you see me on any Milk cartons you'll know why...so uh, how are you then? You feeling okay? Want me to leave or anything? Fancy a massage?
