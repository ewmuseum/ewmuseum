En route to finality

8 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Jun 8, 2002 Ed. Note: I'm too lazy to figure out all the Spanish since I'm not perfectly fluent in it. Just imagine all the conversation is in Spanish. Jesus St Crux takes a deep breath as he focuses, beads of sweat forming on his forehead as he maintains his stretch - currently, the splits elevated by two steel chairs to either side of him. Eventually he exhales as he raises his arms above him and arches backwards. He leans back far enough that he can pace his palsn on the floor and then he lifts himself into a handstand behind the chairs, ending the splits and raising his legs directly into the air. After holding it for a few seconds, he drops his legs to the floor and rolls to his feet. A quick shakedown of a few stretched muscles signals the end of his cooldown, and Caridad Mariposa lauches a towel at his head, laughing slightly. Jesus catches it before anything embarassing can happen, and he uses it to wipe the sweat from his brow. He then drapes the towel around his neck and over his shoulders as he steps towards his gymbag. Caridad leans against the door watching him as he hangs a referee's jersey on a hanger on the wall. 'Why are you refereeing this? I thought you were trying to move past the UWA?' Jesus pauses as he heads for the shower. One hand clenches into a fist and slowly taps against the wall. 'Si, I am trying to move past. I got caught up in the heat of the moment last week. You know me and impulses...' He sighs and takes a few more deep breaths all the while tapping his fist against the wall. 'But forgetting the past is a mistake. Ian, Jason, Kanaida... whether they realize they are or not, they're... they're making light of my achievements. I've worked hard for everything in my life... you know that.' Jesus turns to look Caridad in the eyes, a deep-seeded determination clearly visible in his own. 'I will not let them take from me that which I've earned.' 'I know all that... but acting as a referee?' Caridad shifted a bit, 'I mean, they're not going to think you're impartial, and any one of them could attack you.' Jesus nods his head, agreeing, 'Si, si... they might. But honestly, I'm expecting that. If they're willing to attack me, then they'd probably attack any referee put in that ring, and at least I'm able to defend myself. As for the impartiality, I really don't care what they think. I've faced them all in the ring before, and I've beaten them all. I just figure this is the best way to get some finality. They all want this belt... they all want the right to say they were the best to come out of the UWA, and to do that, they have to beat me. They'll all keep interfering if I give one a match and not the others, so I figure I'll let them fight to see who gets a match. Then they have nobody to blame but themselves.' There's a strange silence in the air as both reflect for a bit on St Crux's words, until finally he taps the wall again with his fist. 'We'll have this match, and then a Title match, even if OSW doesn't sanction it, y eso es eso.' Jesus reaches inside the shower and turns the water on and then looks back to Caridad. 'It's almost time for the show, so I'm going to get ready.' Caridad nods as she reaches for the door to St Crux's lockerroom. 'Si. You want something to eat?' Jesus shrugs his shoulders noncommitedly. 'Si, just a sandwich, and a drink. I'm still thirsty from my workout.' Caridad smiles. 'Si. Chau.' The door closes behind her, and St Crux steps into the steaming shower.
