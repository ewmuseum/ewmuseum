Spoiled dinner...

5 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/5/02 Frankie stands in the kitchen of his shit apartment in the slums of Brooklyn. He is slowly preparing his dinner as his mind begins to wander, as it normally does. And it wanders to the place that is frequents oh-so-often, to OSW. Namely to the roster, full of individuals that he truly hates. This subject has been exhausted thoroughly. The fact that Frankie hates these men simply because they are on the roster and because they stand in his way. But this time, his mind lands on one man, Drew Hostyle. His mind launches into the stream of conscious that is all-too-common... Well, Mister Hostyle is nowhere to be found. And why? Because he crossed me, because I was set on destroying him? Or simply because he is a scared little bitch. I'm leaning towards the latter. When we were tight, me, him and Switchy, it was always me and Switchy. Drew always hung in the back and cleaned up the scraps. Hell, this 'man' had a feud with a female, a fucking FEMALE! And she dealt with him. So, next thing he knows, he's got about 280 pounds of pissed off black man on his ass. And now, he's gone. Where is he? Nobody knows. But for the past two weeks he has no-showed each Slam! Lucky for him. I was unbooked, simply roaming the back, looking for him. Frankie has left his television on for a while and is hardly listening. The weekly wrestling program he has on begins playing a recent interview with OSW's own Graphic Violence. Hearing this Frankie chuckles and listens somewhat as he finishes cooking his dinner. He listens to GV's random threats and ramblings and chuckles with amusement. The interview is nearing the end and Frankie is still listening. 'Shanda: Well....I suppose that sums up that. What about the other people in the OSW. There are many that could be considered ruthless. Ivan Stanislav for one. Frankie Bones is another. What sets you apart from men like that, if any? Graphic V: Ivan is a fuckin' pussy. He needs an army of men to back him up. I have NEVER had to have anyone back me up. I do my dirty work on my own. He's not worth my time. As for Frankie Bones...I hear that mans been bitching about not getting TV time. Well maybe that's because his black ass isn't good enough to get TV time. They don't let dirty ni(bleep) with gold teeth take up valueable time on television. Shanda: GV..I take great offense to that last remark! Graphic V: Shut up you monkey. Eat a bananna and do your fuckin' job which is to interview me. Nobody gives a fuck about your feelings. Shanda: I think..I think I'm done with this interview, Mr. Violence Graphic V: Eh. Run. Run like everyone else runs from me. *punches glass* YOU DUMB BITCH! YOU'RE NEXT! ' Frankie eyes widen and he glances to the T.V. 'What the fuck?' he mutters as he walks towards the television. Rage... Burning hatred... His mind boils over with anger... Just who the fuck does this guy think he is? He takes a rumor, a fucking untrue rumor about TV time, and calls me out? He has the nerve to call me a ni...ni....a fucking nigger. Oh I fuckin' love it. I've been searching, hunting for somebody like this guy. An egotistical punk with some size who has waltzed through life. Well, GV, I'm no Rhett Locke, you wont look down at me. I'm gonna stand face to face, eye to eye with you, and we'll see what you have then. It may not happen today, it may not happen this Saturday, but our paths will cross. This dirty nigger with gold teeth is gonna take hours and hours of valuable TV time simply devoted to bringing your world crashing down, so you better be ready you punk ass bitch...
