Conspiracy Theory!

4 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/4/02 Bumps and bruises are an everyday part of professional wrestling; heck, if you're not getting hurt, you're not trying hard enough. And when we say bumps and bruises, we're talking not just physical, but mental. To survive in this business, you've gotta be able to handle the stress on your body as well as the hits to your ego. Of course, when your ego is the size of a small house, a minor incident can be enough to cause major structural damage. And so, we're brought into a large hotel, the Hilton, Philadelphia, where Clay Vendetta is laying on a bed, being massaged by his lovely valet, Niki Weathers. Clay, visibly, is not thrilled. 'It's a conspiracy, Niki. It's a goddamned conspiracy. There's no other possible reason that I'd drop a match like that to that... that... buffoon!', Clay rants. 'Ssssshhh... calm down, honey, relax,' Niki purrs as she kneads Clay's back. 'It was just a fluke, it happens to everyone.' 'Argghh... no, Niki. No it doesn't!', Clay retorts, hitting the pillow with his fist (and smushing the chocolate which had been carefully placed there by the housekeeping crew). 'Naket cheated! As I was trying to win, fair and square, and lock on Crowd Control, and then that dirty Canadian gets a handful of my tights and trips me up!' Clay is rather riled at this point, and Niki ceases the massage as Vendetta hops up to a seated position on the bed and continues: '...and *that's* what it takes for those stupid Canadians to win; I mean, come on, no Canadian is successful at anything, ever. Except for Brian Boitano; and come on, it's figure skating! That's like... like... daisy picking! And their ridiculous country can't even grow daisies, it's so damn cold...' 'Sweetie, sweetie,' Niki says softly. 'Don't stereotype; I know for a fact Canada is a nice...' 'It's a blemish on the North American continent!' Clay shouts, thrusting his fist into the air defiantly. 'Canada is millions of miles of land mass that house a couple of hockey rinks and a lot of moose and hosers like Naket and his little buddy Marvelous! I don't think Rick Moranis is particularly funny, I *know* Alex Trebek has lost a step; and don't even get me started on Celine Dion, Niki, don't get me started!' Weathers sighs. 'Great,' she mutters to herself. 'He's like a bleached-blond Bluto.' 'A conspiracy, Niki!' Clay exclaims, jumping to his feet. 'And any country that would get conquered by the French... the FRENCH!... isn't going to conquer Clay Vendetta. This is a year for patriotism, Niki!' 'Uh... I don't think that's quite what they had in...' 'Patriotism!' Vendetta continues. 'Canada is the REAL axis of evil! And the people in it! I mean, look at the two moose-lovers I've got coming up this Saturday... Naket's a proven cheater, and Marvelous? Mike Marvelous, PCW's gutter trash, straight out of the talent wasteland that is FSW? I bet you there was money involved, Niki!' 'Uh-huh... go on, Clay,' Niki says, clearly disinterested (but not clearly enough for the now-convinced Vendetta). 'That's right; I'm thinking this conspiracy extends to the highest levels, Niki... there was money involved; American money, and that's why Mike Mediocre sold out the buffoon... even if it was just a thousand or so... a pittance for a man like myself... God, that's gotta be worth, what, a couple mil across the border?' '*yawn* Sure, Clay. Listen, it's getting late...' 'I've already been informed that that Reagan chick's got it out for me; I mean, I'd be jealous of my Classic talent and chiseled good looks, too; but really, there's gotta be someone at the top who's got their dirty little hands in this. Well, I'm going to get to the bottom of this conspiracy, or the top, or wherever the hell it goes, and I'm gonna start by beating the hell out of those two hosers on Slam!' Clay, clearly much too fired up for 1 AM, starts towards the door. 'You get some sleep, babe,' Clay says to Niki, 'because in the morning we've got some dirt to dig. I'm going to go to the computer cluster and check out the Internet... they know everything on the Internet! Clay marches out of the room, humming 'Blame Canada' to himself, as Niki falls back onto her pillow and sighs... '...you know, my mom thinks I'm crazy for wanting to be married; bleh...' ...and as the conspiracy all unfolds, the camera fades to black...
