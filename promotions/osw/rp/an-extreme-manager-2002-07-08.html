An Extreme Manager?

8 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 8, 2002 meIt was another normal day in the world of two men. Two men that knew what it meant to be successful in the wrestling world, yet lived relatively normal lives outside of the ring. Relatively normal until today, that is. Tom Lexian stared across the busy intersection, catching a glimpse of a very attractive young woman walking her dog. 'Well, I guess this walk wasn't so useless after all, Colin.' Looking at his friend -and tag team partner, Colin Mitchell allowed a grin to slide across his face, 'Yeah... I guess not, although I doubt you'd have a chance.' Tom sighed, shrugging his shoulders a bit. “You’re not exactly Casanova yourself,” He replied to his six foot ten partner, who overshadowed the much smaller Lexian. His massive frame created an eclipse, causing Tom to have to hunch over to from his huge partner. 'True, but at least women feel safe with me,' Colin grinned, as he nodded to the young woman. Tom snorted, taking another lick of his ice cream cone that he had picked up a few blocks before. “Women are like cones of ice cream, Colin. They make you feel…I don’t know, younger and more alive. They give you that boost of energy. You know what I mean?” Colin nodded, knowing exactly what his comrade meant, 'Yeah, I do know what you mean. They're like a drug.' “I’ve been thinking about this whole OSW thing,” Tom continued, changing the subject a bit. “Don’t you think we need a manager?” Colin looked at Tom for a second, then away, 'A manager? Since when have we had an actual manager, Tom?' Tom appeared perplexed as he thought about the question. “Uhm, maybe in DWF, when Thomas Ruble was sort of managing us. But he really wasn’t.” He jokingly hit Colin in the ribs. “He was just along for the ride.” Colin grinned, he remembered the good days in the DWF, 'Yeah... he was sort of, maybe we should give him a call eh?' Colin said in a sarcastic tone, as he lightly -and jokingly- punched Tom in the shoulder. “Oh, I’ve tried,” Tom remarked. “But the poor bastard doesn’t answer his phone anymore. Something about tax evasion and the mob being after him. But anyhow, I was thinking we need someone to be by our side as we take OSW by storm. You know, like a symbol for us and the Extreme Legends.” Confused, Colin inquired, 'A symbol? How do you mean?' “Batman had the bat signal. Superman had his trademark suit,” Tom began to explain. “Hell, even the Thundercats had those two little cats running around behind them. The Extreme Legends needs something like that too. So when people see it, they’ll say, ‘Hold on! Look at that! The Extreme Legends must be here!’” Tom seemed proud of his revelation and eagerly waited for Colin’s response. Colin sighed, and then smacked Tom in the back of the head, 'We're wrestlers... not cartoons, Tom.' Tom looked upset, but it soon subsided to acceptance. “Fine, forget the whole idea. I guess we weren’t meant for GREATNESS.” The duo continued walking down the block, coming upon a local zoo at the end of the block. Tom stopped for a moment, examining a sign posted on the wall. “Penguins for sale?” Tom observed with some interest. Colin looked at the sign, and then at Tom with a worried facial expression, 'Please tell me that you're joking...' “Tom looks at the sign again. “Let’s check it out.” Colin signed, knowing he wouldn't win this, 'Fine... ONLY to check it out.' Tom does a quiet fist pump and hurriedly makes his way into the zoo, followed lethargically by his partner Colin. A few monkeys run across the floor past Colin, causing him to step carefully as he watches a boa constrictor slither its way into another hole around the corner. Colin does not look happy. Somewhat annoyed, Colin stops for a second, but he thought to himself that there was no turning back now. A horny looking koala rubbed against Colin's leg, and he quickly -and reluctantly- continued to follow Tom, who was far ahead of him, 'Hey! Wait up Tom!' But Tom wasn’t listening. He was already at the front desk speaking with a man that bared a remarkable resemblance to… “CROIKEY!” The zoo keeper exclaimed in a heavy Australian accent. “You just stumbled upon the greatest deal in all of San Francisco! These penguins are AMAZING animals and they come at the low, low, LOW price of only ten dollars a penguin!” Colin heard the zoo keeper, and tried to yell across the room to the desk, 'TOM! YOU BETTER NOT!!' Tom shoved ten dollars in the zoo keepers face and extended his hands to accept his grand, new prize. “One penguin please!” Colin saw the exchange of money, 'NO! DAMMIT TOM!' “Relax Colin,” Tom replied, giving his oh-so famous smile to his long time partner. “I’ve got everything in control.” Suddenly, a black, furry little penguin jumped into Tom’s arms, pecking away at Tom’s chest. 'Everything under control eh?' Colin sighed as he approached his friend, and the penguin. “GAH!” Tom exclaimed. He turned to the zoo keeper for help. “Excuse me, Mr. Zoo Keeper, but why does he keep trying to…uhm, peck me?” The zoo keeper just smiled. “Aww, he just likes ya, mate! That there is the friendliest penguin this side of the Bay Area!” The penguin leapt out of Tom’s arms now, tired of pecking, and avoided the outstretched grasp of Colin, who tried to capture him. The penguin ran off as fast as his little penguin feet could take him, scurrying across the floor and …. WHAM! Tom looks at Colin. “Did he just run into that fish tank?” Colin nodded, 'Yeah, I believe he did.' The zoo keeper rushed over and picked up the penguin off of the ground, brushing the dust off of his feathers. “CROIKEY! He’s alright, mate. He just has problems seeing objects sometimes.” “Is there anything we could do to help his vision?” Tom asked looking concerned for his non-flying bird friend. Colin looked at the zoo keeper, then Tom, 'You sure the damn thing even has vision?' “He might need prescription glasses,” The zoo keeper replied with a bit of a shrug. Tom looked exasperated. “Glasses for penguins? Where do we take him? Lens Crafters for Birds?” Colin looked puzzled, 'Tom... where are you going to keep him? And what're you going to feed him?' “Well…” Tom began to say, unsure of the answer himself. “…he can stay in the hotel with us. And we’ll feed him fish. Yeah, that’s it. I mean, penguins like fish, right? They live near the ocean.” '... I thought he had to live in a cold environment?' Colin questioned. “We’ll stick him in the freezer when we aren’t playing with him,” Tom answered simply. Colin sighed, 'You're so stubborn.' Tom reached out and grabbed his penguin back, thanking the zoo keeper for his help. He turned back to Colin with a broad smile. “So what do you think we should name the little bugger?” Before Colin could answer, the penguin leapt out of Tom’s arms again, running out of the store against Tom’s shouts to return. But as he made his way out of the store… BOOM! … ERR! The penguin ran smack dab into a parking meter, knocking itself out cold. A woman getting into her car next to the parking meter saw the entire ordeal and proceeded to faint, falling down next to the unconscious penguin. Colin looked to Tom with a grin, 'He's your penguin... let's go deal with the mess.'
