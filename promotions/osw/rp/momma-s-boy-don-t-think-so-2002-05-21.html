Momma's Boy? Don't think so...

21 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21.05.02 “I will not answer any of your Capitalist based questions, Ameri-kun! Now leave me in peace,” roared Krieg, slamming the phone down as he does, Krieg’s face bared a look of aggression as he stormed away from the table in his hall, walking through the doorway to his living room. Krieg slumped down on a 2-piece leather couch and jabbed his index finger onto the remote control, bringing the lifeless television into action. A music channel made bright flashes around the dimly lit room as some pop video played. Krieg picked the remote control up and started to hop channels, looking for something to catch his interest, “...What’s Love...Got to do, Got to do with…” Click. “...the latest on CNN. The President has…” Click. “…planted these roses for winter, and if they’re kept…” Click. “…On the Westside…” Click. “…of SLAM! With the actions taken by…” Krieg’s attention picks up as he hears the OSW flagship show, SLAM!, being mentioned. Turning the volume up louder, a ESPN news reporter continues to speak about last Saturday’s events, “…Walzak, officials seem to be worried that Doug Troy’s title reign is threatened by Walczak’s obsession with ridding the OSW of Troy,” “That is not true, Capitalist,” shouted Krieg, actually thinking the reporter could hear him, “You lie, Ameri-kun scum…I will put this matter straight,” Storming back out to the hall, Krieg picked up the cordless phone from its charger and marched back into the living room, reading the show’s number from the bottom of the screen as he plugged it into the phone. After a click on the phone, it started to ring out, and a male voice answered, “Good evening. ESPN Sports Desk. How may I help,” asked the voice on the other end of the phone, “I am Krieg. I want to speak on air to Ameri-Kun,” replied Krieg, The man on the other end paused for a second, speaking to someone off of the phone, before speaking back to Krieg, “Yes, Mr. Krieg. We can take you on air. Please hold for a minute,” The presenter on the screen is cut off mid-sentence as one of his producers says something in his earpiece and his eyes light up, “Well folks, I’ve just received word we’ve got none other than the OSW’s Number One Contender for the International Title, Michel Kreig, on the line…Michel, how are you,” Realising he is being addressed, Krieg stares at the screen as he speaks in the phone, “I am fine, Ameri-kun. And how is your Capitalist show,” questioned Krieg, “Our ESPN Round-Up is doing great, thank you Michel. Now, I would like to ask a few questions. They’ve been thrown together at the last minute, so I hope there Ok…Well, here goes. Michel, what are you thoughts on winning a shot at Propaganda this past week on Slam,” asked Jeremy Russ, his name coming up in yellow letters on screen, a small image of Krieg sitting at the top of the screen, “I am pleased that finally, a true International wrestler is getting a chance to return some honour to the title which truly belongs to a European. Not just a regular European, a Communist European. And who better than Krieg to beat that no-good, Jesus loving freak, Propaganda,” “Right, right. That pretty much answers what I had to ask. Next up, what do you think of the rumours spreading that you live at home with your mother,” asked Jeremy, a chuckle being heard over the phone, “Do not laugh at me, Capitalist Ameri-kun! You probably live at home with Mother Russ. She probably make you eggs and toast in morning, and fixes your bed when you go out to the big, bar world o...” Krieg is cut short as a female voice is heard behind him, “Michel, honey, come get your lunch. I’ve made you your favourite: saurkruat and fried toast,” “Mother,” Krieg shouts off the phone, “I’m trying to talk to Ameri-kuns, and you are not helping,” “Is that your mother, Michel,” asked Russ, still chuckling slightly, “No, no, it is not, Smart Ameri-Kun...It is my...uhh...sister,” replied Krieg, covering his tracks, Flicking through his notes, Jeremy fires right back at Krieg, “You don’t have a sister, Michel,” “Ahhh...It is time for Krieg to go. Good day Ameri-Kun,” replied Krieg, pressing the red Call End button on his phone, “Well, that was Michel Krieg and what appeared to be his mother. How about th...” Click. The TV returns to it’s lifeless state as Krieg tosses down the remote control, “Stupid Ameri-Kuns. They think they’ve got me sussed...” “Michel! Lunch,” shouted Krieg’s mother, “Mother, I’m trying to be mad at Ameri-Kuns! Can’t you see,” “....NOW!,” “Coming mother,” replied Krieg, as he trailed off to the kitchen. Fade.
