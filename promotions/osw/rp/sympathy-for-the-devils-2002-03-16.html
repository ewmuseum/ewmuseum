Sympathy for the devils

16 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/16/02 ~The roar of a thousand dump trucks keeps rushing over me and it feels like each one stops and deposits a few tons of bricks on my head. I shoulda been a mansion by now. Water ran over Tempest's closed eyes and over her face, matting her hair to her scalp as she rolled her head from side to side slowly. The skin around her eyes was still dark, a consequence of head injury, and everything seemed to be moving in slow motion. The ringing in her ears had stopped finally, and the patter of water against her head was soothing. She shivered as the water ran down over her shoulders and goose bumps rose on her arms. Her lips, a light shade of blue, trembled as she wrapped her arms around herself. Then a click and a squeak signaled the entrance of someone else into the bathroom. 'Tempest what are you doing?' Miyuki squawked, her dark brown almond shaped eyes widening in shock. She stared at Tempest shivering, fully clothed, in the shower. Tempest opened one eye and said hoarsely, 'Makes my head feel better. Leemee alone...' Miyuki shook her head and said, Tempest you're gonna freeze yourself and catch cold or something. I know it hurts still, but c'mon, let me get you another ice pack or something.' Tempest sighed and said, 'I need a shower anyway. Shoo, I'll turn on the hot water.' She shut the door to the shower and after a few moments the room steamed up. With a soft ~schlup~ Tempest's soaked PJ's hit the floor and Miyuki laughed saying, 'I hope you don't think just because you're hurt I'm going to take care of you soggy laundry.' 'Aww, but why not?' Tempest moaned. 'I thought this would be good for just a little sympathy.' Miyuki laughed and said, 'Oh right, like all the cards and flowers aren't enough sympathy. I swear you're milking this for all it's worth.' 'But I really am hurt!' Tempest protested. 'And besides the cold water really does help. Darn Meanstreak...' She continued to grumble but was interrupted as the phone rang and Miyuki bustled out of the room. 'I'll get it,' she said. For a moment her muffled voice mingled with the pattering from the shower and then she poked her head back in the door looking perturbed. 'It's Ivan. I said you were in the shower, but he doesn't believe me. Keeps going on about a capitalist plot to keep him away from you.' Tempest sighed and stuck her head out a moment, dribbling water onto the floor and said, 'Tell him I'm showering and I may be awhile. After all, I hugged Jane Reagan last week when I went to lunch with her.' Miyuki nodded and disappeared. After a moment she came back and said, 'Ok, that he believed. He recommends lye.' Tempest laughed and said, 'Ya know, I'd almost try that. I can't believe she's done this. I mean what could possibly have changed her from the smart, principled business woman I knew into... well, whatever she is now?' Miyuki shrugged and said, 'I don't know. Maybe the loneliness finally got to her. Maybe she gave up on trying to find her husband and for whatever weird reason thinks Toomes is all she can get. Or maybe she's gotten so viscous trying to get back at Tyler Nelson that she really has changed into another person.' For a moment the running water was the only sound in the room. And then Tempest piped up and said, 'I wish I knew. She doesn't resemble the woman I used to know. How can people change that much?' Miyuki sighed as she leaned against the wall and said, ' Are you sure you knew her to begin with? You have a way of being overly generous with people. Given half the story, you believe the best of the other half before the worst... Which is exactly what bothers me about Ivan. Can you really trust him?' Tempest turned off the water and Miyuki tossed her a towel as the shower door opened and... the camera switched angles so as not to reveal her. Her voice accompanied the shuffling sounds of drying as she said, 'Miyu, we've been over this. What more could you possibly want than what he did for me this week? He took on one of the most dangerous guys in the fed, helped me and even took care of me when I got home. Does that not tell you something about his intentions?' Miyuki shrugged with a resigned look and said, 'I just wish I had been there...' 'Oh why? So you could have taken care of Meanstreak yourself?' she asked and giggled. 'C'mon your brother only graduates once, you needed to be there. Don't feel guilty cuz you didn't get to see me get clobbered. You know you couldn't have prevented it. It's part of my job.' Miyuki smiled and said, 'And have I mentioned that you're also entirely too forgiving?' They both laughed and Tempest stepped into view in a short blue satin robe, on the back of which was an intricately embroidered Chinese dragon. 'No, I'm just forgiving enough. The only thing I'll give you a hard time about is your insistence that Ivan must be a bad thing. I mean, you're the one who wanted to get me with Doug Troy.' She put her hands on her hips and eyed Miyuki with a mildly annoyed smirk. MIyuki shrugged guiltily and said, 'How was I supposed to know he was a jerk? I thought you had a lot more in common with him and you'd work great together. He's comfortable in the club scene, which you love. He's in tune with the fans, he's...' 'Totally self centered and willing to blame his failings on the girl at hand,' Tempest said with a tiny growl. 'How simple minded is that? If there's a problem, it must be the girl's fault. It couldn't possibly be that he's too selfish and immature to think about anyone but himself, thus making him a useless tag partner.' She huffed and added, 'And all that about the clubs is just surface stuff. I don't care whether Ivan listens to the same music as me or hits the clubs or if he thinks Moby is half the title to a novel representing the decay of morals due to capitalist greed.' Her voice had risen very slightly and she winced, placing her fingers on her temples. Miyuki patted her shoulder and said, 'You ok? Listen I don't think you should go to the show tonight. You're not booked and chances are you could get hurt.' Tempest smiled mischievously and said, 'But I have so much to do. I have a match to get and some revenge to wreak.' 'Revenge?' Miyuki said and goggled at her for a moment. 'Against Meanstreak? You can't be serious. Tempest please.... the man is psychotic and already almost killed you. Leave it alone.' Tempest's eyelids fluttered as she offered Miyuki a patient smile and said, 'When have you ever known me to leave it alone? I will have my revenge. And like everything about me... it'll be sweet.' She grinned and winked as the scene faded.
