A horrifying realization

9 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-9-2002 The scene opens up like it has done often in recent times. The scene in question today is the interior of MTV Studios. Inside the Studio, Doug Troy paces around, seething in anger. A large bruise, created when Ivan Stanislav dropped him from the turnbuckle to the guardrail, dominates his face. An even bigger bruise, however, is on DT’s ego. Doug talks to himself as he paces. “First Ivan, then Meanstreak and then whoever set me up with that useless bitch Tempest! What is going on around here? It’s like a bad dream that I just can’t wake up from…” Doug pauses and thinks, then resumes pacing. “It’s not Reagen, cause she has her hands full with Toomes. It’s not Ed… or at least I THINK it’s not Ed. He wouldn’t sabotage me like this…” Doug stops walking and thinks some more. “Would he?” “That mental midget? Hell no,” comes a voice from out of nowhere. Doug turns around to see who it is and it’s Frisco, DT’s old tag team partner and currently retired co-DAMN founder. “I say you should call in the FBI, my friend.” “Tried that. They said they were busy with other cases.” “More important than your wrestling troubles? Bah!” Frisco says with mock disdain. Doug doesn’t notice. “That’s what I told them! Other people have no trouble getting help from the authorities when they need it! Stanislav has the support of Russia, Meanstreak obviously has some cop friends, which would be the ONLY way he stays out of jail with his tendencies. And here I have NO support, even from my parents!” Frisco smirks as Doug rants on and on.” “And furthermore, those two monsters are ruining the OSW and NOBODY IS DOING JACK ABOUT IT! I don’t get it…” “Jesus, you done crying yet? You’re starting to sound like every other sob story out there. Man, and I thought I hear whiners on the presidential trail…” Frisco smirks as he talks, clearly not meaning his words. Doug, however, takes them more seriously. “What?” “I mean, c’mon, you cry and cry and cry about these two destroying the OSW and yet all you ever do is whine like the rest of the jobbers.” Doug’s face is a mask of sheer terror as realization slowly dawns on him. “Oh my God. No, it can’t be…” Frisco raises an eyebrow at Doug. “What?” “This whole place, it’s all WRONG!” “… I don’t follow you, Doug.” “No no no, think about it. When was the last time something weird happened around here?” Frisco searches his brain for a couple seconds. “Actually, I haven’t been around lately, so I wouldn’t know.” “We HAVEN’T. It’s just been a string of mundane things that set up rants and… no no no.” Frisco looks really lost now. “No madcap, no hilarity, no shenanigans, ho hijinx, no tomfoolery… it’s just been me… ranting in front of a camera…” Doug looks at his hands, fear making his eyes wide. “Oh no… I’ve become… DERIVITIVE!” Frisco is taken aback by this declaration. “What? Hey, speak for yourself man. I’m still the millionaire playboy that I’ve always been.” “Sure, except that you’re running for public office! That’s gotta be boring.” Frisco shakes his head ‘no’. “Oh no. I’ve got plenty of amusing anecdotes to share with people about my campaign.” “How many of them involve sex, drugs, transvestites and/or Barry Bonds?” Frisco starts to answer, but he comes up short. “Well, there was… no. And that… no. Oh shit, you’re right! What the hell happened to us?” “I dunno! All I know is that I’ve become no different than all those other scrubs in the business and I… oh God, I feel faint…” Doug completes his thought by crashing face-first into the ground. Frisco runs over to help him out as the screen fades to black.
