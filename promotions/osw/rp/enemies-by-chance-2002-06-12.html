Enemies By Chance

12 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.12.2002 ][Many men have expressed distaste with many things, but only a choice few have chosen to act on it. Those who remain are those who choose to cope, acknowledging the fact that there will always be politics. There will always be greed. There will always be one thing holding a person back. That is just how the circle of life is. But our subject, who long ago realized his distaste with life in general, asks himself and anyone willing to listen why we have to live it the way we are told to. In a way, he lives outside the box ... in his personal life. But when his life plays out like a show, televised for all to see, there is a different side shown through. There is a feeling of predictability and deja-vu. This situation leaves him, and the people who watch this always playing slide show, wondering just how many people there are in the world like him. Growing up, before all of the death which was brought upon his family, he had been told that he was unique. But years later, as he sat in a cold waiting room ... blood dripping from a wound courtesy of Jim Axtell on his face, he realizes that it was all a lie. Now there will be no line crossed. For he will either start a new, or become just like every other self involved bad guy in the country. Decisions ... decisions.][ 'Mr. Storm?' A women who seems completely unintrested with her job sits behind a window, filing her nails. ][Kory Storm, who is completely off in his own planet, comes back to reality and gets up off of his chair, brushing a white bandage over his bleeding forehead. There are so many things on his mind aside from the minor injury. Hell, he only went to the hospital on the urging of the Old School Wrestling backstage doctor, who was obviously unable to stitch a cut. But the blood cannot effect the image, and so the blood cannot be seen. For when a man lives a life in entertainment, the image is the most important thing next to death and taxes. She looks down on her nails, not paying any attention to her patient, as the man who is now unsure of whether he is or isn't 'Perfection Personified' speaks.][ 'Are they ready for me now?' He asks, being unusually polite. 'Yes ... I just need to make sure that you have signed the proper forms.' The women answers back, still focusing on anything but the brooding, bleeding, broken man in front of her. ][Storm, who seems too tired to say anything about her lack of enthusiasm on the job, reaches into the pocket of his Abercrombie and Fitch parachute shorts, pulling out a large stack of folded up sheets of paper.][ 'Here they are ... I hope you find them in the right order.' Kory Storm answers back, confused, shocked at his own abilities to be courteous. Is this newfound attitude really something which has just entered the mind of Kory Storm? Or has it always been there, just covered in shards of animosity and violence? ][The woman begins to browse through the forms, glancing down at the personal information sheet and reading a line back.][ 'Occupation... professional wrestler.' The woman reads with a hint of disgust in her voice, a tone usually reserved for a man like Kory Storm to use. 'Yeah,' replies the fatigued Kory Storm, 'heard of me?' 'No.' The woman says, finally looking at Kory Storm, the tone of disgust becoming more and more evident with every word. 'I find that wrestling is a stupid sport, if you could even call it a sport, which would make me stupid just like those boobs that flock to men like you if I watched.' ][Kory Storm's insides began to bubble. How could this woman, a woman who clearly knows nothing about the industry he loves and bleeds for, say things like that. Better yet, how could Kory Storm agree with her statements? The wrestling fans are stupid, just like teenagers. They like who the office tells them to like and they hate just the same. With this, Kory knows now that he is forced to act the way he does in public. He is a victim of this hatred. And why? Simply because he hasn't been told to be liked. Now the decision has been partly made. Kory Storm hates people. Not just in the ring, where he is paid to do so, but in life generally. He hates those who control him when he thinks he controls himself, he hates the people who are seemingly innocent pawns but who are forced to be more, and he hates the people who commit murder ... the people who forced him to walk down the road leading to a career full of mixed hatred and violence. Now, he knows, that it is his turn to hate. And no one has told him to. It has all been figured out on the accord of the one person who matters. Still, however, he is torn between the decisions which he has yet to make. Should he defend the thing which has created the beast he's become, or should he walk away now and forever. It has been decided...][ 'Listen...' Kory shouts loud enough for the entire waiting room to hear, 'my line of work isn't done for you to like, you ignorant bitch. It's done for people like myself, who need an outlet for their violence, instead of sending people to a place like this with the use of bullets or a blade. As for our fans, you have me there. They are disgusting ... and there is nothing I can do. They, like me, have started falling through the pit which this industry has become. However, there is no turning back, so I'm going to defend them for the first and last time in their pathetic lives. They may be smelly, inconsiderate, and bullheaded ... but you just insulted them. What does that make you?' ][Everyone who sits in that room looks on at the woman who looks wide eyed at the man known as Kory Storm. He has made his decision, and they all know it, he has finally been able to show the world that even those who are paid to be anyone but themselves have minds of their own. In realizing that, these people see themselves and what they have always wanted to do. Break the mold. So with this, in a stunning event, the waiting room breaks out in applause. However, Kory Storm could care less. He isn't out for approval. He bursts through the doors into the main hospital hall, knocking into a doctor along the way.][ 'Ah ... Mr. Storm!' The man shouts excitedly. 'Here to fix that nasty cut before your match Saturday, eh? Yeah, we have another one here with that same problem. Go back to room 718, you'll find what you need there.' ][There was no exchange in thanks, there was just determination. Kory Storm, after the outburst earlier, just wanted to get the fuck out of there. For in the hospital, he was exposed, eyes always on him. All he longed for was to be in his hotel room, silently preparing inside his own shell for his upcoming confrontation with Jim Axtell, something that in all this commotion has exited and abruptly come back into the mind of this sadistic monster. Approaching room 718, he remembered one of the doctor's comments. 'We have another one here with that same problem'. What could this mean for Kory Storm? Is there a kindred spirit out in the open for him, along with others, to see? Hopefully. The door which leads to room 718 is shut, inside a loud voice is speaking.][ 'No! I refuse to let that asshole in here with me!' ][Mumbling][ 'I don't give a fuck! Move me to another room if you have to! I will not allow my safety to be put into jeopardy with the entrance of that turncoat lunatic! We'd end up destroying your whole hospital with one exchange of words.' ][This damn sure wasn't the kindred spirit that Kory Storm was hoping for. Actually, it was someone familiar, but completely different. He knew that voice, and as he looked into the door window, Kory Storm's senses proved to be right on the money. 'Angeldust' Nick Casey. How will this affect whatever is to come in the ever changing and tragic life of the man who even after learning more about himself, isn't sure who he actually is. How will this chance meeting take it's toll on Kory Storm? Only time, and Nick Casey, will tell.....][
