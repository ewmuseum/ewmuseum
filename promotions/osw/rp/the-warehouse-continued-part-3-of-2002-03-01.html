The Warehouse Continued (Part 3 of ?)

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-1-02 Slowly, the blackness turns from that into three words. Blurry to begin with, but coming into the picture at a minimal speed until finally revealed as that of “To Be Continued.” The sound of laughter and smoke fills the room, as the area in which we are placed is that of the same that we last saw Jesse Ramey. Back into the little warehouse were Jesse and his friends had partaken in an aggressive battle that led into a friendly conversation and finalizing into the poker game that we are now watching. “So, tell us, Jesse.” The man takes a puff off of his cigar and then begins to motion it into the air. “How does it feel to have a person of the female gender as a boss?” From the sound of this man’s voice, only one conclusion can be made. That he is Italian, but this man wouldn’t happen to be part of any mafia type syndicate, or would he? “Well, you know, it actually isn’t that bad of a job. So, it really doesn’t matter who you are working for.” Jesse takes a drink of the beer, sitting in front of him. “Work is work and that’s all that matters. You know what I mean?” Laughing, “Oh yeah, I know what you mean.” Looks down at his cards. “So, you need any help in there?” “What does this look like?” Jesse pauses, “CCW? Of course not! I only have you guys come in and take care of my light work when I need it completely beat down.” “You do know that was pretty dirty what you did to those guys and pinning it all on Josh Marshall made it look even better. You see, we thought you were really dead. Then we got that phone call and boy did we feel much better down here. You are a good friend to the family, Jesse, and we love getting work from you.” The man says with a serious look. “I appreciate that, Tony. I really do, but if you’ve been watching the OSW any lately, you can honestly tell that I don’t need any help as of late. You know those boys from Brooklyn?” Jesse asks. Thinks for a moment, “They go by Brooklyn Born and Bred down there in OSW?” “Yeah, that’s them.” Jesse says. “We’ve heard of them. Why you ask?” Tony asks. “Well, have you been watching OSW as of late?” Asks Jesse. “Not really, we’ve been…” Trails off for a moment and then continues in a cool, calm tone, “Busy.” Speaking out, “I’ve been watching it. I know exactly what you are talking about, Jesse.” “You want to tell Tony about Jimmy?” Jesse asks. “Yes, Jimmy, do tell.” Tony explains. “Well, you see. He was able to take them all out single handedly. I mean, Jesse looks bigger and meaner on the television, sir.” Jimmy says. “Good, good. Thank you, Jimmy.” Tony says when the sound of a phone begins to ring. Reaching into his coat, Jesse pulls out a cell phone, “It’s me boys.” Jesse flips open the cell phone, “Hello? Yes, honey, I know it’s beginning to get late. I just had some things that take care of. I’ll start back right now. Yes, I promise that I will. I love you to. I’ll be back in maybe half an hour. Bye.” Jesse hangs up and places the cell phone back into his coat. He then looks at the men. “I’ve got to go guys. That was Chass. She wants me back as soon as possible. It’s been good seeing you all again.” Looking at Jesse, Tony begins to speak as he extends his hand, “I believe I speak on the behalf of everyone here when I say it has also been a pleasure seeing you again and you are welcome to stop in and see us any time that you wish. Thank you for dropping bye, Jesse.” Tony and Jesse shake hands. Jesse then begins to make his way back through the plethora of boxes. The words “To Be Continued” pops back up.
