The Road To OSW part 2

“But I wanna wrestle too.” Mikey Thomas’ big brown eyes were as deep as an ocean with confusion as Reno tried to make him sit outside with ZaC. Reno scratched his head and looked at his surroundings while he thought of an idea. A police officer slowly made his way down the street; his flabby form was a shadow of his young self. Now he was nearing retirement and lost all motivation to stay in shape. He nodded to a man waiting for the bus and then approached two punks sitting against the bus stop. The officer spoke a few words to them and the boys were sent on their way. “We’re waiting for his mom!” One called. “Do it elsewhere my boy, this is for waiting for buses.” The officer replied. The man who was waiting was then approached by another man equally dressed up and the two walked away. Reno looked back at Mikey, clad in his hat with the earflaps and a specially made yellow and black coat with a Boston Bruins logo poorly sewed on. “Mikey...I told you, just go over there to Subway with ZaC inside your shirt and wait for me to finish up with the recruiter for this promotion. Please Mikey.” Mikey gave it some thought and looked up with a question on the tip of his tongue. “What if the men come back?” shit. “Mikey, I’ll be able to see you from this restaurant and if any of the men come I’ll be right out alright?” What could Reno do really? He knew how to fight but lacked in the whole size thing. However he seemed to comfort Mikey enough to make him stand up and wave goodbye as he headed for the crosswalk just like Reno showed him. Mikey tended to just walk through traffic. He had to learn that cars don’t move around him like people do, cars aren’t afraid. The recruiter for the latest federation Reno was attempting to get into sat at a table on the other side of the Grabba Jabba café, right beside two guys snuggling noses and laughing giddily. The recruiter saw Reno and waved him over. Phew...here we go Reno. Don’t blow this one...Shit. What was this guy’s name? Damn. “Reno!” He called. “Hey! How are you?” “Very well, and you?” The man shook Reno’s hand before the two both sat back down. “Quite well, Um...May I have your business card?” Reno faked a smile. “S-...Sure,” He pulled out a small silver case. It opened slowly and smoothly. He handed Reno the paste colored card. ... Ed. Sweet! I rule. “Ah, thanks. I appreciate it. So...” Reno trailed off. “Yes...” The two made a little bit of small talk about the weather and shitty airport service from Moncton to Toronto and back. Then they got into the business issues. “Old School Wrestling is the name huh?” Reno seemed intrigued by the name. “Yes, we’re a relatively new organization, sort of a branch off of Primetime Championship Wrestling. We’re owned by Jane Reagan.” “Ah...A lady. Nice touch.” Ed shot a quick laugh, to humor Reno and move on quickly. “Well, We’d like to have you perform a physical, which is mandatory, and then we can talk about contract negotiations.” “Physical eh? I got this problem with my knee here. A car door was slammed shut on it by a fella named Azrael Ravenell in jOlt Wrestling. Hurt like a mother fucker too.” Ed looked blankly at Reno for a second and shook his head quickly. “Right, well as long as you can use a brace you should be fine. I’ve been informed that you’ve been wrestling in an independent organization here in Monctown?” “Moncton, sir...It’s MoncTON.” Followed by another blank stare. “Kay...But in answer to the question?” “Oh, right...” The two looked at each other for a few moments. “Reno?” “What?” Ed sighed, “Are you currently involved in another organization.” “No.” “Okay.” “Well...Yeah I guess. This little place called Real Action Wrestling. I got to wrestle Kurrgan the other day, remember him?” “Yes, I do remember Kurrgan. OSW asks for our wrestlers to try and free up any other involvements with alien federations.” “...You know, Kurrgan’s looking for work somewhere. I’m sure he’d draw some money in for OSW too!” “Reno.” “What?” “We don’t want Kurrgan. We want you to quit Real Action Wrestling before you are allowed to join OSW. Follow me?” “Yeah.” “Nice meeting you Mr. Davis.” With that, Ed extended his hand towards Reno Davis, a rather difficult person to negotiate with and the scene faded...
