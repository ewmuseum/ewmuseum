How do you smack a bitch and feel good about yourself afterward? Maybe Stephen can help :\

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Thursday night Los Angeles, California. The 'City of Angels' as some people call it. And, who knows, maybe it is. But where we're going, there are no angels. Just a devil and his bad influences. And this devil’s bad influences are leaking over onto our “Quintessential Teen Idol”. Shift to a nightclub on a dreary Thursday night in L.A. Smack dab in the middle of the club we see the teen idol himself, Kid Wonder. He’s signing the cheek of a young lady, an adoring fan, who happened to spot him. However, on the other side of K-Dub is the bad influence. His name is Stephen Williams. Wonder, decked out in a Shaun King football jersey and red, baggy genie sequin pants, gets up and casually walks over to the club DJ. Stephen, in a flannel shirt and tight jeans, drinks a margarita. Next to him sits a white girl. “You look familiar,” says the girl. “Have I seen you from somewhere?” SMACK! Stephen does what he is now infamous for. He smacks the girl. The lady grabs where the bright red handprint is on her face. Looking shocked, she gets up and runs off just as Kid Wonder returns to his seat. “Anything good happen while I was away?” asks Wonder. Stephen, who has his back to the teen idol, quickly spins around. SMACK! Stephen smacks the OSW star. “What the hell!?!” shouts K-Dub. “What are you doing!?!” “Sorry,” Stephen says in his high pitched, somewhat girlish voice. “Thought you were a bitch.” “You’re the bitch ya little faggot,” Wonder angrily says. SMACK! Stephen stares at K-Dub. “I’m not a homosexual,” Stephen claims. “That’s just what jealous people say.” Wonder can’t help but to sigh. “Well,” Wonder says intensely, “stop smacking me and I’ll stop calling you a little homo you little homo.” Stephen sighs. “Fine.” “You’re supposed to be helping me,” Wonder tells him. “Now help me.” “Fine,” Stephen says as he sighs again. “When you smack a little bitch, it’s all in the wrist. A fine, young black woman in a mini-skirt and tight black tube top slowly approaches their direction. “Sit back and watch,” Stephen says to Kiddie. The black woman stands in front of the two men. “Hey, you’re looking awful hot tonight,” she says seductively. “Can I take you…” SMACK! Before she can finish, Stephen smacks her. Wonder’s jaw drops. The lady, not at all pleased, storms off making sure to bump Stephen on her way. Stephen, however, just laughs nearly falling out of his seat. “That was great!” he shouts out with joy. “You saw the look on her face. That’s what you wanna go for, Kid.” “I dunno if I can,” Wonder says. “I mean, I can’t hit a lady.” “You hit that one girl,” Stephen tells Wonder, reminding him of when he hit Bethany on SLAM! “But that was different,” Wonder says. “Bethany Yamato more than had it coming.” Stephen puts his hand on Kid Wonder’s shoulder. “You’ll do fine,” he says with a huge smile on his face. “Whatever,” says Wonder as he removes Stephen’s hand from his shoulder. Meanwhile, in the background, we see a young white girl walking their way. “Here’s your chance,” Stephen says with a huge smile. “Just remember what I showed you.” Kid Wonder sighs as the girl stands next to him. He turns around to face her. “Excuse me,” the girl says in a soft voice, “but are you Kid Wonder.” The teen idol looks a little nervous. “Yeah,” he says nervously. “I’m him.” The girl tries not to act childish and get all giddy. She takes a couple of deep breaths. “Omigod! Can I get your autograph?” she asks. “Sure,” Wonder says with a slight smile. “Lemme get my pen.” Behind Wonder, Stephen has a sick grin on his face as he stares at the girl. “Do it. Do it,” whispers Stephen to Wonder. Every time Stephen whispers, Wonder gets more nervous. He slowly pulls a pen from his pocket. You can see it shaking as the nerves cause Wonder’s hand, his smacking hand, to shake. “What are you waiting for?” asks Stephen. “Do it!” “Do what?” asks the girl. “Do it before it’s too late!” Stephen shouts at K-Dub. “Gah! I can’t do it,” he says, dropping the pen onto the table they’re sitting at. “Do WHAT?” the girl asks again. “This!” SMACK! Stephen hauls off and smacks the girl. A tear wells up in her eye. She grabs her face and runs off, nearly crying. Wonder turns around and looks at Stephen. He looks mad. “What did you do that for!?!” Wonder asks him. “You…have learned…NOTHING!!!” Stephen shouts at the teen idol. “I spend a whole day with you, trying to show you how to hit a bitch properly, and you learn NOTHING!!” Stephen is fuming. “Why do I even bother?” SMACK! Wonder hauls off and smacks Stephen. “You’re supposed to be helping me!” Wonder shouts at Stephen. “Instead, you’re acting like a total spaz.” Stephen sighs. “I’ll give you one more chance,” he tells Wonder. “There’s a major bitch heading this way.” In the background, we see a girl wearing a pink halter top with “STAR” written in silver glitter and some black leather pants walking in their direction. “When she gets here, just turn around and smack her,” Stephen says. “Don’t even think about it. Just do it.” The lady, who we now recognize as Amanda, Kid Wonder’s girlfriend, stands at their table. “Do it!” Stephen yells out. Wonder spins around. He’s about to smack her, but stops his hand just before he makes contact with Amanda’s face. “Amanda, honey, what are you doing here?” Wonder asks he nervously. Amanda stands next to the table. Her arms are crossed and she looks less than amused. “What were you about to do?” she asks although she knows what he was about to do. Wonder looks at his hand near Amanda’s face. He quickly pulls it back. “Nothing,” Wonder says with a smile. Amanda puts on a fake smile. “Whatever,” she says. “Let’s go.” Stephen, letting his homosexuality get the best of him, takes Wonder and grabs him. “No! He’s staying with me, bitch,” Stephen shouts at Amanda while pulling her boyfriend towards him. Wonder looks uncomfortable as Stephen holds him. “Aren’t you gonna help me, babe?” Wonder asks his girlfriend. Amanda sighs. “Let him go before I make you let him go.” Wonder smiles. Stephen, abiding to Amanda’s wishes, lets K-Dub go. Once he does, however… SMACK! Stephen goes and smacks Amanda. “Oh boy,” Wonder says, putting his hand over his mouth and trying hard not to laugh. You can see the anger in Amanda’s eyes as she holds her face where Stephen smacked her. “You little homo!” she shouts out. “I’m not a…AHHHHHHH!!!!!” Amanda, who is wearing high heels, takes one of the heels and stomps down hard on Stephen’s groin. Stephen lets out a high pitched squeal as his testicles are being pinched between the seat of the chair and Amanda’s high heel. After about 20 seconds of damaging Stephen’s man area, Amanda pulls her foot up. Stephen grabs his crotch and falls out of his seat, crying like a little girl. “Wow,” Wonder says with a grin. “That was…ah!” Amanda grabs K-Dub by his long, golden blonde hair. She pulls the hair. Wonder gets out of his seat. “If you wanna beat up girls, I’ll show you how it’s done!” she yells at Wonder. Amanda, pulling her man by his hair, walks out of the club. Wonder screams in pain the whole time as we slowly fade to black.
