Aftermath

30 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30-04-02 “…Ohhhh my head…” Minion holds a bag of frozen peas to his head and groin. “………Fucking hell my head hurts…” Hessian groans wearily, sitting in the shadows near a stained glass window. Cain, Minion and Caleb daren’t look at him, as he holds his steel mask in front of him. Those evil gleaming green eyes look down at the mask, staring back at him, the divine steel covered in scratches and dents. Hessian inserts his massive gauntleted paw inside his mask. Using the steel of his gauntlet, Hessian groans in pain, and forces each dent in the mask back out. Minion and Cain watch on whilst Caleb tends to his tenderised body. Hessian barely smoothes out the tough steel, then goes to work ridding the mask of its scratches and bloodstains. As Hessian cleans himself up in the shadows, Cain lies on a pair of old, dusty pews, pushed together. “…Pain…I never appreciated it until Krieg hit me in the face with my own weapon!” “Heh, heh, heh. At least you still have your titles! Shame about the Red Army though, I’m sure they were looking forward to holding a belt for once! As a wise man once said…DWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!!!!!!!!!!” Cain smirks, and holds a cold, damp cloth to his nose. “I will admit it, you did a fine job of refereeing, Caleb…but that doesn’t mean things between us are ok…” Cain hisses at Caleb, and from the darkness the gleaming green eyes noticeably cock to the side, as Cain stares a burning hole into Caleb. Polishing off his mask, Hessian checks the stitches on his head where the dents in the mask pushed open the wounds. “The Red Army…what a fucking joke. Walczak and Krieg can’t capture the titles from us, and not only did Ivan lose out on the World Title, the big fucker lost his woman! I wouldn’t be surprised if Wang and Ruslan are caught in a ‘Ménages á Trois’ with SeaBass! The Red Army are nothing more than The Pink Brigade…anyway, I’m finished with them…there’s a new breed of prey in town…” Hessian unclips his mask, and fits it back on his large demonic skull. Clipping it firmly into place and locking the small opening mechanism at the back, Hessian slowly gets to his feet, and strolls across the debris-lain ground to the altar, where, still standing somewhat defeated, is the inverted crucifix. Hessian laughs at the mockery to their nemeses, and then turns to Cain, lifting his precious title high above his head. “I feel like celebrating!” Cain stretches, his large, muscular frame tensing up, then releasing all the stress trapped in his gargantuan body. He groans, then sits up and looks at Hessian, standing before the crucifix. “Hessian, although I would enjoy a night on the prowl, unfortunately I, and I’m sure I speak for all of us…must heal. Trust me, as soon as I am fully recovered, we will feast!” Hessian groans. “Cain, you were hit by a hammer, OK, so it’s a hammer, but my damn skull practically caved in! You know what it feels like having your head crushed in on itself? It’s fucking hell. You’re right; we are after all the best team in the OSW…no one, not Civil Disobedience, not The Pink Brigade…not even The Westsiders can stop us!” Minion looks over at Hessian and giggles at the Goliath. “Hessian…it’s pronounced DA WESTSIDAZ, not Westsiders, they may be common folk but they ain’t that common.” “Heh, heh, heh. They’re 5-Time WCW Champions Minion, so don’t be dissin’ them now, sucka!” Minion pauses, looking at Caleb, the intent of bitchslapping crossing his mind. “You fool! Da Westsidaz are 5-Time PCW Tag Team Champions, Hall of Famers and, well…basically the black version of Civil Disobedience…” Cain looks intrigued at the sudden mention of Da Westsidaz… “Hessian…remember late last year, when the PCW hired us…Da Westsidaz were wrestling at the time weren’t they?” “Cain, it really doesn’t matter. If Ivan’s cronies can’t defeat us, and two little suburban misfits can’t beat us…what chance do a pair of old hopefuls have against us? I said it before Cain, no one can beat us…NO ONE! Let them bring Civil Disobedience, let them bring The Red Army, bring on Da Westsidaz, 3B, Twisted Metal, Meanstreak and Ivan, Propaganda and Vampir Nosferatu, Rhett Locke and Corg…NO ONE CAN FUCKING STOP US!!!” In the ‘magic’ of the moment, Cain, Caleb and Minion jump up, Cain roars out and throws the towel aside, Minion wipes the final speck of dried blood from his face and Caleb cracks his back. “The Apocalypse is ever-sweeping through the OSW, and now the time for eternal damnation has bequeathed Civil Disobedience and Da Westsidaz!” Cain says, as he starts strutting across to the inverted crucifix and crushing the head of the statue of Christ. Caleb jumps in the air, clicking his heels in mid-jump…and then falling hard to the ground… “Heh, heh, heh…Ow. Let’s go celebrate, before we either kill ourselves or those 4 suburban bastards!” Minion leaps into the air, performs a 540º back flip and lands on one knee, his left hand held out in front of his face…and a stern, mesmerized look in his eyes. “It’s time, Sucka! Nobody can beat Heresian Bloodlust, not even those damn Westsidaz! Not even those damn Civil Disobedience punks…AND NOT EVEN THE FUCKIN’ RED ARMY!!!!!!!!!” Minion pops an Imp-A-Roonie, then as he finishes and poses, spots Cain’s bloody sledgehammer nestled in between a couple of pews. His eyes light up, he reaches forth, grabs the 30lbs sledgehammer, and holds it up…barely… “IIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIII HAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAVE THE POWEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEER!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!” Suddenly, a bright white light flashes from the sledgehammer, and Caleb doubles over. Hessian and Cain cock their necks in confusion, as Caleb begins glowing, and in another flash of light roars out, as Cain and Hessian glare at what stands before them, clad in extra-strength armour, is none other than Battle Caleb!!! Beside him, Minion struggles holding the sledgehammer, but explodes into a beam of light, and quickly calms back down to his little purple Imp self…or is he? Hessian and Caleb gasp as Minion appears differently to them, covered in bulging muscles and topped with curly blonde locks… “I…AM HE-IMP!!! With Battle Caleb we shall destroy the forces of evil…AND SEND THOSE DAMN COTTON-PICKERS BACK TO THE GHETTO!!!!!!!!!” “Hessian what the hell is Minion smoking?” Cain whispers to Hess, who chuckles and whispers back… “Id turn around now if I were you…” Hessian and Cain turn around and fold their arms, listening as Minion thrusts the sledgehammer high above his head and lets out an almighty roar, overshadowed a tremendous belch by Battle Caleb… “IIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIII HAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAVE THE POWEEEEEEEEEEEE-………………” Minion falls back unconscious to the ground suddenly, as the sledgehammer smacks him in the face, and Battle Caleb whinnies, and returns to his normal state… Hessian and Caleb turn back around, and a few chuckles echo throughout Cain’s cathedral. They each take their respective manager and lay them on a pew. Continually laughing hardily, Cain and Hessian tend to the Imp and the Hobo. “The Power Himself – Minion the Imp…” Hessian manages through guttural bellowing laughs. “………I only hope he wasn’t serious about the cotton picking thing…we may be evil, but we’re not that evil…yet…” he finishes. “Well in any case a new chapter has been opened in the Book of the Apocalypse; Aftermath. Now…Let the Carnage continue…” Fin
