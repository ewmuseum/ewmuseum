Like A Rolling Stone

12 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/12/02 *click click click* 'Hey, honey... so the 25th is alright, right? We'll fly in, check out the concert, then head back on the road.' This is so... wrong. So... dirty. There's something that's just not pure about using the Internet to buy Rolling Stones tickets. Touch-tone phones weren't even around when Mick Jagger first picked up a guitar in his effort to save the world through better audio... and here I am, not at the box office, not even sending a self-addressed-stamped-envelope, for Christ's sake. Ticketmaster-dot-com. Ggggggggh. 'Yeah, sure,' replies Niki from the other room. 'So what, is this like a comeback tour?' *sigh... WHAM* 'You alright, Clay? What was that?' 'Just the sound of my head hitting the keyboard in disgust, that's all.' 'Oh, terrific. Clay, I mean, c'mon. They're like, sixty. Couldn't we see something... you know, newer? Jimmy Eat World? Guster? Hoobastank?' Hoobastank? HOOBASTANK??? Damn, she's a fine girl and all, but sometimes... well, a little sacreligious. With a few more clicks, Vendetta has the receipt printed. As the printer hums along, he gets up out of his seat and walks over to the next room, where Niki Weathers is lounging in workout gear, sports bra and shorts... and watching MTV2, from the looks of it. 'What you suggest is nothing short of an affront on all things rock-and-roll. You're aware of that, right? Hoobastank... please.' 'We'll compromise, then;' Niki says, smiling. 'The Strokes?' Sighing, Vendetta nods. 'Alright. They're no Richards and Jagger, but at least they don't suck... like every other thrash-punk-rapcore piece of garbage out there. I'll check out tickets... later. I'm gonna go hit the stack.' 'You do that, sexy,' Weathers says, winking. 'And I'll be right here, watching music for people and not paleontologists.' Rolling his eyes, Clay heads out of the room and down the stairs to the basement gym, pausing only to flick on a few lights, which hum and whir to life... hanging dimly from the ceiling in the style of old boxing lamps. Taking off his shirt and reaching down, he removes the pin from the overhead press stack. Taking a seat, Vendetta reaches up, pulls down the bar, and gets to work... It was the last comment that cut the most. Make no implications that I cannot move with the times. There is a difference between having impeccable taste... and simply being left behind. 'Classic' Clay Vendetta is left behind by no man. The sound of the stack hitting the top, and then the bottom, of the machine is audible: *whiiiiir... CLINK* But it stops at the bottom, after one repetition... Something is missing... Stepping up from the bench, Vendetta walks over to a sound system in the corner. A Bose receiver can be found there, wired through the gym... but, anachronistically, the Line In is hooked up to an old 45 player. Sifting through a large box, Vendetta produces an old vinyl... and blowing off a little dust, places it on the player, turning it on and letting it spin to life. New equipment; classic music. It's not a paradox, not hard to understand... for most, it seems. Life moves on but sometimes the newest is not simply the best. The needle meets the vinyl, and the words start to filter through the speakers as Vendetta returns to the stack... I saw her today at the reception A glass of wine in her hand I knew she was gonna meet her connection At her feet was her footloose man... *whiiiiiir... CLINK* Griff Truxell... the name on my mind for weeks now; and finally I get the chance to teach, in the ring, where I belong. Do I need another car in my garage? Nah. When I accepted that challenge, it wasn't about wheels... it was about confidence. I could've put up my house, my 'Cuda, my woman... and none of it would have mattered. You've crossed the wrong line, Truxell. CCV is not to be fucked with. *whiiiiiiir... CLINK* This time out, though, it's not even about wheels... it's not about anything but pride. Pride and punishment, Griff. Three times you've crossed me. Now you've crossed the line. As Mick put it so famously... I can't get no satisfaction, Truxell, these days. Saturday night, when you're locked in Crowd Control... *whiiiiiir... CLINK* ...you're gonna be the one shouting Gimme Shelter... and it won't be 'just a shout away', that's for damn sure. I'm gonna teach you a little appreciation. I'm gonna make you pay a few dues. You haven't earned shit around here, Truxell. I've paid in full already. You're still riding on credit, and you've been earning interest for... what, four weeks now? The Vendetta Collection Agency is angry, Truxell. First notice. Final notice. *whiiiiiir... CLINK* As the stack rises...falls...rises... the sound of the music can be heard, plain as day... You can't always get what you want You can't always get what you waaa-ant But if you try sometimes You might find You get what you need... Has-been? Dinosaur? No, Griff. I'm simply CLASSIC. And all the big money contracts, all the backstage perks... they mean nothing, Trux. Nothing. Saturday night... you might find... *whiiiiiiir... CLINK* ...you get what you need.
