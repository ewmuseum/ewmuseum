Figuring Out What Makes Me Tick

24 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/24/2002 But I learned to burn, That bridge and delete, Those who compete, At a level that's obsolete, Instead I burn my hands, Upon the flames of the flag, As I recall our downfall, And the business' that burned us all, See through the news and views, That twist reality, Enough I call your bluff, Manifest destiny, Landlords and power whores, On my people they took turns, Dispute the suits I ignite, And then watch them burn, The thoughts of a militant mind, Hardline, hardline after hardline, Landlords and power whores, On my people they took turns, Dispute the suits I ignite, And then watch them burn. - 'Bombtrack' by Rage Against The Machine ][Sitting in silence, listening to the rain slap against the roofs of so many houses just like society has slapped against the faces of so many broken souls. Manitoba, Canada. A state never considered as a place which would achieve a level of disgust that so many American states and civilizations have done. But who made that consideration? And what exactly what were they considering? For every state and every city, just like every apple, has a bad seed. When planted that seed grows into a whole race of bad seeds. Then when the innocent of the world finally wake up and rise from their lives of happiness and dreams, it's far too late. The poison has been spread. The damage has been done. The product of a society which never gave the bad seeds a chance was now the majority, rather than the minority. And even though our subject isn't from Manitoba or the city of Winnipeg where he stands, he sympathizes. For no matter how many bad seeds there are, he knows that there is something good in every single one. So the anger which builds up inside of him because of the isolation he was a victim of begins to pour out of him like the blood from the wounds he recieved simply by trying to prove himself to the society that never gave him a chance. He was a supposed bad seed. And now, because so many ignorant people ignored him, the seed has grown into something which the OSW is trying to contain, but never will. Something sadistic. Evil. Cocky. Artistic through bloodshed. Something which in the eyes of our subject, is perfect. He was known as your bad seed. Your scapegoat. But now he stands, his heart black, and his body developed. Known as Kory Storm.][ 'As I sat in the ranks of the shithole known as the XWA, they told me I would never make it.' ][The raspy yet deep voice that had just been heard speaking had seemingly come from nowhere. However, the orangish glimmer of fire emerges, contrasting against the dark sky. As the speaking figure raises the light to his face, we see the sadistic smirk which often appeared amongst the talent in the OSW developmental circuit. Kory Storm. Upon lighting the cigarette which was perched in his mouth, Kory throws his lighter to the side and soon started a mass fire which illuminated a disgustingly kept alley. Trash litters the concrete walkway, and the bodies of dead rats and the remains of broken syringes don't make it feel like home either. But Kory Storm, noticing all of the disgust, speaks][ 'I know that I've probably sunken to new levels by coming here,' Kory Storm speaks with a hint of hatred in his voice, 'But this feels like home to me. No this isn't my house in Miami, which is showered by mounds of riches and the calm sounds of flowing freshwater. This is the home that I had before the money and the power. When my father was killed, I lost the fortune I had been handed, and was forced to carry on with my life living as a filthy nomad. With no friends or connections, I was forced to steal, and by doing things which were against the law, I remained friendless. I wanted to give up. But I knew that one day I could make my life better than what I had. I didn't need people like my father to aid me. Hell, I didn't need people at all. I still don't...' ][Taking a long drag from his cigarette, Kory Storm seems to remember his life before the murders and the awakening to his new life as a professional competitor. The life when he wasn't known as Kory Storm or 'Perfection Personified', but instead as little Cory Rutherford. Smiling he thinks ... he feels ... his past rushing back to him. But after the period of reflection, Kory Storm ... the man we know now, is back with us.][ 'Then I learned how to wrestle in Germany...' Kory Storm went on to explain, 'And I was once again alone in a new environment. Sure, I had my trainers and my fellow students. But what did I have to gain by being in the company of old fat ex-wrestlers and their soon to be old fat pupils? Nothing. I was determined to learn but to be my own wrestler in the process. Coming back to the states and meeting a man by the name of John 'Enforcer' Carroll, I thought I had done it. You see, Enforcer was a man just like me. He wanted respect to go with his talent and he had also suffered trauma in his life. I thought I had found my one true friend in life. So I opened up my gates of protection and let the man in, and together we dominated the United States with a combination of talent and sadism. However, I was asked to go work in a promotion with a higher amount of regard then the one I was currently involved with. I told John that I would one day return, or I would find a way to bring him up with me. However, the man wrestling calls Enforcer was always ignorant and bullheaded. Rather than accept my success, he pictured my elevation as mutiny, and on the card of a house show in New York City, he turned his back on me ... leaving me with a bloody face and the injury which would be my downfall.' ][Kory Storm, intentionally reliving painful moments in his past life, begins to pace amongst the rain in this Manitoba alley. However as he sees an old Innovation Of Wrestling poster ripped and torn on the brick wall, he seems to regain his composure.][ 'Being called...' Kory speaks, 'was the best feeling I've ever known in my life. In fact, working in IOW was indeed my first taste of the spotlight. The promoter there had faith in me and upon meeting my manager Lisa Tien, I had been told that I would become IOW World Champion. My destiny. It all came to mold the night I defeated Maverick Manson for the title, showing the world that I wasn't some independant jobber, I was the real deal! I reigned in a superior way, my back injury getting progressively while my paycheck got progressively bigger. I was on top of the world, and to ease my injury, I began the heavy road to addiction by taking my first pain killer. One stint in rehab, a shithole developmental federation, and a year later I find myself here...' ][Seeing an onlooker gaping at him, Kory Storm throws a disgusted look at the fan, signaling to him to go away. Kory Storm returns back to his banter, glancing at the OSW T-Shirt he's wearing.][ 'Back to the old school...' Kory mutters, 'Doesn't matter to me where I'm going back to, where I'm competing, where I'm winning as long as I am winning. Because I strive to make others feel pain ... I strive to make others look bad. And in OSW there is so much dead weight and unworthy talent that rots in the ranks which I shall soon be dominating. So prepare for what is to come, because it is something that will change you all. It will change the fan favorites because they will be purged in a simple effort to show you all what I don't want involved with something that I will soon be the franchise of. Because in my life, I've been denied a childhood, a home, and a family. Now it's about what I want...' ][Smirking sickly into the misty air it seems as if Kory Storm views his future. He sees the bloodshed. He sees the endless pain. He sees the victory. Will the OSW/Kory Storm alliance last long enough for Kory to do what he has been saying since he entered the XWA? Dominate? They say that he who laughs first will strike last ... will Kory Storm prove that statement true? Only time will tell....][
