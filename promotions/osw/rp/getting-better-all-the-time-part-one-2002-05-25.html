Getting Better All The Time - Part One

25 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25 May 2002 'YOU MOTHERFUCKER!' I scream. Ace looks behind him and I tackle him to the ground, and grab a handful of his long blonde sweaty hair. 'What the fuck is your problem?' Hawk screams, but I don't hear him - I just slam his face into the concrete floor of the locker room. Reno Davis, Ivan, Ed, Porter, Tommy Wal, and Kimbusa all rush towards the commotion. I don't care. I slam Hawk's head into the concrete again. If you would have watched the Main Event, you would have seen a few spots where me and Hawk played a little rough. Which was in the script. Which made sense within the context of the story and made it interesting for the viewers at home. Until he hit me with a baseball bat on my bad knee. My well-known bad knee, which had been hurt during the match with Doug Troy. The exact bad knee that I was rehabbing, and the exact bad knee that is filling up with fluid as we speak, and the bad knee which is prompting me to kick Hawk's ass for taking a baseball bat to it when he was specifically told, INSTRUCTED, not to hit below the belt with that bat. Sure, it was designed to break over Ivan's shoulder, it had a tiny jagged cut which ran along the circumfrence of the bat, held together with what else - crazy glue, but that doesn't mean that you have the right to smash it on a guy's bad knee. The bat was designed to break on Ivan's shoulder, a force capable of withstanding the shock and breaking the bond the glue had formed. Why it didn't break on me, I have no clue. So Ivan and Walczak pull me off, me yanking out a handful of blonde hair. I'm kicking and screaming, and Hawk gets up and lays into me, sending a gloved fist into my face, and I tumble down between Ivan and Tom. Hawk lunges for me, but Tom clotheslines Ace back to the ground. Ed gets between us. 'What the fuck is your guy's problem?!' he screams, as more guys from the locker room gather around. 'This motherfucking asshole tried to fucking jump me!' Hawk points his finger at me. 'What the fuck, Toomes?' Doug Troy says from behind me. 'Why don't you leave the work outside in the ring? You assholes are supposed to be working together!' 'Fucker hit me in the knee! Porter and the Doc told you all, no shots to the motherfucking knee! I gotta go in for a fucking MRI on Monday and this asshole hits me in the fucking knee! ASSHOLE!' I scream, the throbbing pain from the knee resonating through the fading cloud of adrenaline. I can barely hold it together, it hurts so bad. Porter takes control. 'Wally, Ivan, get Toomes to the hospital. Reno, Doug, get Hawk out of here. We don't need this bullshit right now, guys. We're running alot of angles and shit, and we need everyone running at 100%. The last thing we need is you assholes stiffing each other in the ring, getting hurt. Toomes, you're no angel, you almost broke Andrew's nose out there. That was uncalled for. Andrew, you fucking knew better about his fucking knee. I thought I had made that abundantly clear. And now you guys are fucking fighting backstage. What the fuck fed am I in? Cause this sure isn't OSW. I was under the impression when I joined that this was a PROFESSIONAL federation, not PCW, where everyone gets into little camps and stabs each other in the fucking back. Now if I hear ANYTHING about you fuckers fighting backstage again, you can start looking for a new place to fucking work. We don't need this shit, and I won't tolerate it. You all got that?' There is a murmur of assention as Tom and Ivan lift me up. A Fourth of July fireworks explosion goes off in my leg, and I scream in agony. 'Take it fucking easy, goddamnit!' 'You got your insurance card?' Ivan rumbles. Standard question, of course he's been doing this longer than I have. I nod and point over to my locker. Trevor Hawke and Seth Masters are nearby and they grab my gym bag for me, and they walk alongside me towards Ivan's rented SUV. The 5 of us get into the Expedition, me in the cargo area with Seth putting on an ice compress. The Expedition rolls out of the parking lot, and Ivan puts on some German techno-noise as we merge onto the main road towards a hospital. Walczak is reading off a complimentary map from the hotel, pointing out directions. Trevor is looking over the backseat, handing Seth supplies out of a first aid kit. Odd, wouldn't you say? This is the way it always goes. Backstage fights, the rides to the hospital... Since I didn't rate an ambulance, what normally happens is a bunch of the guys pile into a car and drive off to the nearest hospital, due to what Jane and Ed's concept of 'shouldering the financial burden'. That is, we ain't gonna pay anymore than we have to. A ride in the ambulance is expensive, and the insurance company is slow to pay off the bills from the hospital, so it's much more efficient and cheaper to simply drive ourselves. My knee is a mess... I feel it swelling, I can just imagine the fluid behind my kneecap building up. I saw Jason McAllister's tape of his knee surgery, and they stuck this long fucking needle in his shit and drained all the fluid out... I didn't look forward to that... And he had to wear knee-braces for about 8 months afterwards... Christ, was I going to become another Steve Austin? Where a kneebrace is a part of the fucking ring outfit? All because Ace wanted to get me back for elbowing him in the nose. A blatantly stiff shot with a baseball bat for a potato in the ring, what the fuck, you know? This is the kinda shit that goes on, beyond the crap 'Reality TV promos' people come up with. People pissing each other off, forcing them to call friends to cut the brake lines of the other person... Yeah, that sounds about right. He could have ended my career. Might as well retailate in kind.
