Disassociative

2002-04-21

Amy 'Aimz' Campbell

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Note: Wow...last night was hell, but Slam ruled so wooooooo!!! This RP might be a little depressing or angry...but yeah...stuff............) Another edition of Slam had come off the proverbial presses fairly well. Tempest and Amy had fought pretty hard...that 'paid off' in the win they picked up over a distracted Sebastian Toomes. But this wasn't what Amy wanted... She had pictured a well-fought victory...not a small package rollup while Toomes was distracted. Then again, there's that wonderful saying from a recent film... By an inch or a mile, winning is winning. It was true, they had won...not by an exactly respectable method, a pinning combination from behind, but they had done what they set out to do; Embarass the champ who was causing everybody so much greif. The only problem with it was that it earned Amy little respect...and that's what she needed. It sounds so commonplace, so 'Spice Girls' to run around bitching and moaning about wanting to be respected not as a woman in the sport, but as a somebody in the sport... But it was true. Tempest was respected...she had been around probably since before Campbell had even dreamt of this. It wasn't a matter of time-testing, though...Amy had to become either more respectable as a person, or a complete maniac in the ring. Yeah, as if she was EVER going to become a respectable human being. So this left her one hope: Giving it her all and just a little more when she stepped up to any mountain of a man between the ropes. This and more was running through her head as she laid in the trainer's make-shift 'sick bay' after Slam. Sitting in an unfolded steel chair in quite an awkward position, one bare foot propped up on a small table as an elderly man, probably in his late fifties, she thought, wrapped stiff bandaging around the foot, the ankle and most of her calve. ''So you ladies beat Toomes tonight...'' The man, who had a name patch showing 'Wayne' on his buttoned medic's shirt, tried to make conversation...attempting to make this a little more comfortable for the redhead seated before him. ''...I saw that, you put up quite the little fight.'' 'Little' fight? Amy struggled to keep her voice even, trying to ignore the slip as she spoke, ''We did...and we won, so it paid off.'' ''Well...you did get that rollup pin...you sure there wasn't any other, maybe fairer, way to beat him?'' ''Look...'' She was rubbing the top of her head, pretending to be scratching at or arranging her hair...remembering the initial impact of the DDT Toomes let her experience after the three-count. ''We won...nobody complains when THEY win with a little or even a lot of controversy. The important thing here is that Tempest and I had our hands raised in the end, so don't try to tell me otherwise.'' ''But...they've almost lost Ja---'' This pompass little old medic had struck a nerve, and Amy snorted as a reply. ''Fuck Reagan...let her burn for all I care.'' He was treading lightly now, ''But she means so much...she signs our pa----'' ''You didn't hear me very well the first time, did you?'' Right now she had a natural emotion to match her natural hair color. ''FUCK Reagan...whether I get paid or not, I'll still fight.'' ''Don't you feel even a little pity? The poor woman was...'' ''Have you been listening here? All Jane does is cause me greif...I'll be happy the day I can pick up a paper and read that obituary. Under any normal circumstances, I'd feel bad about this...but did you step back a minute and remember what she's done? I have! Let's see...Meanstreak's ADD...countless gang-beatdowns at the hands of 3B...constant thorns in my side...yup! I'm glad someone chopped the breaklines up...she got EXACTLY what she deserved.'' ''Bu---'' 'Wayne' was interrupted again while he put the finishing touches on the splint of sorts. ''They say that everything you do in life comes back on you threefold in the afterlife...'' Amy pondered a moment. ''...be it good or bad. She's hurt so many people, stabbed so many in the back...the day she dies, she'll either live hell in heaven because someone took pity, or damnation in hell. It looks like she's getting a bit of that overdraft now. So dead or alive...either way, I'm happy to see Jane Reagan get what's been coming to her for so long.'' He didn't bother replying at this point, closing up the bandage and dropping her foot to the floor, causing Campbell to wince but stand up. She grabbed the black leather boot that was missing from the now semi-repaired foot and slung it into a little duffle bag, carrying it along as she limped out of the room...into the long white hallway that she eventually found her locker room door in. Inside there, nobody was waiting for her... Timo had left to help his homeland, Rent-A-Hero was off with his PCW work...Breanne was probably hanging off of some prep at a bar. Amy had nobody and it phased her more than a little. She tried to think of everything RAH had experienced in the last few days...love, C.L.I.T, the realisation that he was a literal 'test-tube baby'...everything. She had tried to think of Timo's trials, too...but nothing was overtaking her more than a need of companionship, company at that moment. The only friends she really had in OSW were Tempest, who Amy was still slightly reluctant to trust, and Reno Davis...but she had seen Reno leave the arena earlier, and she wasn't about to march into the Red Army's locker room to fetch Tempest... The answer? Music...she'd just occupy herself by listening. The music of choice? The answer to that came when she reached into her duffle bag, pulling our Marilyn Manson's 'Mechanical Animals' album...slipping that into a blood red Sony discman as she clipped headphones around pierced ears, letting ''Disassociative'' take her mind off of things... I can tell you what they say in space That our earth is too grey But when the spirit is so digital The body acts this way That world was killing me The world was killing me Disassociative... She was drifting to sleep...hair let loose, laying across a built-in bench with eyes closed just as a shadow loomed over her...slipping her headphones off, startling her to quite a degree. ''Whaa...huh?!'' She jolted up to sit, blinking at the black and red abdomen in front of her. Eventually Aimz gathered enough sense to look up and, as she did, she was greeted by the sight of a Spiderman replica mask... ''Rentie?'' In the one instant he lifted the bottom of his mask slightly to kiss her, the world lit up...she finally had some company! ''How'd you ever manage to get here?'' She was clinging close, wondering aloud. The hero just smiled, simply saying, ''Early flight.'' He had worked hard to see her that night, but Amy was too tired, so caught up in thought to even think about the idle conversation they quickly engaged in until three words came into play... ''Beat Drew Hostyle...'' ''Huh?'' Beat him at what? Was Rent-A-Hero tearing off into another rant or did he know something she didn't? ''Oh...'' He looked increasingly nervous, the bearer of bad news. ''...weren't you told? He was 'talking smack' about you...someone made a match for Head On Collision...'' Amy leaped up from the bench, wincing a bit after putting pressure on the bad ankle, but trying not to let on how badly it was hurting. ''What?!'' ''An 'I Lied' match...'' RAH was fidgeting now, seeing the look in the redhead's eyes glowing with an almost unbridled rage. ''...one of you has to make the other admit to lying about having a relationship.'' ''What the fuck?'' Yup, that girl was angry...she was clenching fists so tight it was a surprise that blood wasn't drawn. ''I'll be long dead before I ever even CONSIDER letting people THINK I was ever with that rotten 'Ghetto for God' sleaze!'' ''It's okay...it's okay, just calm down. You have a chance to get him down initially on this coming Slam...night before the pay-per-view.'' It surprised Amy how normal RAH could be around her since 'the night', but she continued anyway... ''What? I've got a fight just before my first OSW pay-per-view match?! That's just BEGGING for a broken ankle...'' Wait a minute, she was almost afraid because of a silly little injury! ''...ah fuck it, I'll do whatever it takes.'' ''Well, you've got an unannounced tag team partner to fight Hostyle and Frankie Bones with...'' ''Jesus...this only gets worse.'' She looked to the floor, sitting down to rest her head on RAH's shoulder for some semblance of comfort. ''...can't we just get out of here? I'm so sick of people right now...'' ''Whatever you need.'' And everything else faded off, nothing in the world but the spandex-clad maniac mattering to Amy... Unlike Sebastian Toomes, she had somebody now and, unlike Drew Hostyle, the 'someone' she had wasn't a member of the same sex. Hell, unlike Drew Hostyle...she IS a someone.
