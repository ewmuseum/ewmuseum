Encounters.

15 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-15-02 Callisto Bane had indeed waiting for the gym, and it for her. As you could see it was now late in the evening, around tenish and this one was out roaming the streets. This place was fairly new to her seeing as she had never been here before..She believed it was called Long Island? She wasn't exactly sure, but she was sure of one thing..The night life was amazing. She had been out all night dancing, seducing in clubs and now she was taking a moment for herself in the not so bumping surroundings of a local bar. She hadn't checked the name but so far the drinks were decent and well prepared and the women weren't so bad to look at. But one in particulat was catching her eye. Though this was no ordinary woman, this was Amy Campbell from OSW. The brazen redhead was seated at the bar, but apparently with company as a man was running his down her thigh and then up, to where his hand was running up her shirt now. Callisto tilts her head somewhat before noticing who this man was. She had not spoken with him before, but she was positive it was Reno, another OSW star. Callisto was keeping her distance at a nearby table, simply watching the two for a time while sipping her drink of choice. She was watching them even still as the pair were exchanging a rather sloppy set of kisses. Tongues swirling, fingers caressing everywhere possible to caress. Callisto was intrigued by this act and she was watching out of the corner of her eyes while also studying every move of Amy. Sooner rather than later the Reno fellow was leaving the bar..Without Amy. Callisto was watching him for a long moment until he was completely out of the bar door and what she was sure was a good twenty feet from the establishment. The Olden Beast was then on the prowl for a certain redhead at the bar. With a stron gait across the wooden floor she makes her way up to Amy and sits down on a stool beside the woman. Amy was not paying much attention so Callisto capitalized on this by simply sitting and drinking in silence for what seemed ten minutes. Her eyes then could be seen wandering toward the woman beside her, though she was positive this one hadn't a clue who she was. No matter..Callisto was stricken with a slight grin, just a hint of one, merely visible if you really were looking. Callisto Bane - 'Good Evening..' Amy Campbell - 'Uhh..Hey' And it was true, Amy had no idea who she was. This, in Callisto's mind was a great thing. She lifts her container from the counter top and sips at the contents once and then again before speaking up. Callisto Bane - 'I saw the man leave you all alone..What is his name?' Amy Campbell - 'Oh, my boyfriend? Reno...Reno Davis, why?' Callisto Shrugs her shoulders slightly while stirring her drink with her fingertip before lightly sucking the foul drippings from that digit. Callisto Bane - 'No reason..Why did he leave you here..All alone?' Her head turns slowly to the side to give a sideways glance toward Amy, a rather..Scary look coming from her. Amy Campbell - ''He had to get some...uhhh...things...for........some stuff. Plus he's got a monkey waiting back at the hotel.'' Callisto nods her head once Callisto Bane - 'I see..Will he be back anytime soon Amy?' She knew she said the name..She was waiting for a reaction. Amy Campbell - 'Uhh yeah, I'm meeting him back at the hotel la-----wait......how did you know my name?'' Callisto Bane - 'Ah, a hotel room..How..Cheap.' She ignored the question thrown at her. Amy Campbell - ''Sorry...but usually couples don't sleep in cardboard boxes from town to town. How is that so 'cheap', if you don't mind my asking?'' Callisto again ignored the question and turned to view Amy again. Callisto Bane - 'Let me ask you something Amy..When Corg was about to fuck your brains out on national TV..Did it make you all wet?' Now was the sadist coming out in her. Amy Campbell - 'Excuse me!?' Callisto Bane - 'I think you heard me Amy. But unlike Corg, I won't make the mistake of letting people get in my way..' Amy was getting upset and it was showing, old emtional struggles were popping up inside of her. Too bad she was a little too drunk to be fighting. All the more ease for Callisto.. Amy Campbell - ''...........you've gotta be fucking KIDDING me. How much are you getting paid to say this shit? Here's twenty bucks, go home.'' Callisto Bane - 'Oh Amy, Amy, Amy..I don't need or want your money. I happen to want something else, something you can satisfy me with. And I intend to take all that I want..You may have your contenders shot this week on SLAM! But I will make sure you are walking sorely all the way down the ramp.' Amy Campbell - ''You're in OSW? If you're threatening me or some shit...I swear to God....'' Callisto Bane - 'Trust me..God won't be around to help you.' She reaches for a bottle sitting directly in front of her, and as she does so her arm flexes greatly in plain sight. It would be usely to fight against her even if the woman could in her drunken state. Amy Campbell - ''...who the HELL are you?' Callisto lifts her glass toward Amy. Callisto Bane - 'Callisto Bane.' Amy Campbell - ''That new girl, eh?'' Callisto cuts a grin. Callisto Bane - 'Enough formalities Amy..I didn't come here to chat with you.' Callisto turns around to face Amy, her grin vanishing and being replaced with a sadistic glare. Callisto Bane - 'I know all about your run in with Corg, and I am here to finish what he started..' With a fluid motion Callisto grabs on to Amy's head, twisting and turning it toward her while her tongue runs up the side of the woman's face. An evil Snear passes over Callisto's lips while she shoves Amy away and off of her stool, now standing over her. Amy Campbell - ''DUDE! What the f*ck do you think you're doing? Listen, man...woman...whatever you are......I'm taken and you're DEFINITELY not as good at this whole creepy thing as Corg...'' Callisto Bane leans down and jerks Amy to her feet, slamming the smaller woman's back in to the bar railing. She leans dangerously close to Amy and speaks while her hand wraps around the woman's throat, squeezing tightly to cut off air flow. Callisto Bane - 'That is because I don't play to be creepy..I play to get done what needs to be done. And get what I want out of the entire ordeal.' Amy Campbell - ''What...'' She choked, gasping for air. ''...do you...want out of...this?'' The bartender was coming close and Callisto would not risk a large audience. She releases Amy and backs away with a sinister grin and a mechanical laugh passing through her lips. With that she turns, walking right out of that bar without another word said or another action preformed. The answer would have to wait..
