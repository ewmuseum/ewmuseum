Robots doe not lose to humans

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Begin File.] “Give to this unit the Pentium X chip.” “Look, we’re NOT hiding it, we DIDN’T lose it, and we HAVEN’T gambled it away.” “Correct. Give to this unit the Pentium X chip.” “Ugh, I’ve had it, YOU talk to this rust bucket. I’m gonna do something more constructive.” “Give to this unit the Pentium X chip.” Clang! Clang! Clang! The sound of Chico’s head banging into the brick wall makes a sound reminiscent of church bells ringing… provided the bells were being bounced down a very large set of steps, that is. Gunmetal steps up. “You are missing one very important point, we do not have the chip.” Cobol’s head swivels from its observance of Chico’s unorthodox efforts to bore a hole in the wall. “Unit Gunmetal will report how the chip was lost.” “We did not lose the chip.” “Then give to this unit the Pentium X chip.” Chico brushes crushed brick from his lenses. “At no time did we HAVE the damn chip!” Cobol ponders this for a time, his lights dimming for the briefest of moments. “Query: Units Chico and Gunmetal received contact about existence of Pentium X Chip.” Chico, relieved to finally be getting somewhere, bobs his head in agreement. “Query: Pentium X Chip was positively located and identified.” “Affirmative,” chips in Gunmetal. “Query: Pentium X chip was not lost.” “YES!” the other tow robots practically scream in unison. “Conclusion: Either unit Gunmetal or unit Chico has possession of Pentium X Chip. Give to this unit the Pentium X chip.” “Arrrrgggghh!!” CLANG! CLANG! CLANG! Already, a sizable pile of crushed brick litters the floor because of Chico’s assault. Gunmetal, markedly more patient, tries again. “We were unable to gain access to the chip because it was guarded.” Cobol takes in this new datum and digests it rapidly. “State make and model of defense system.” Gunmetal shuffles his feet in what can only be termed as acute embarrassment. “No make, no model. It was a human identified as Vax.” “Human.” “Yes.” “After units Gunmetal and Chico destroyed the human…” Gunmetal shakes his head, “We did not destroy him.” “After units Gunmetal and Chico maimed the human…” “No maiming, either.” “After units…” Chico spins around. “Look you bobble headed bucket of bolts! The human beat us clean!” Small red lights flash all about Cobol’s torso “Error! Error! Humans are inferior to robots. Humans cannot beat robots. Humans therefore cannot win. Query: define term bobble headed bucket of bolts.” “Cross index it with dickweed!” fumes Chico as he stomps out of the room. “I’m gonna go get the tire iron!” “Cross indexation complete. Bobble headed bucket of bolts equals dickweed equals superior master unit.” “You got that right, jackass!” Chico’s call comes floating back. “Query: define term…” Gunmetal steps up. “Irrelevant. We think… no, are CONVINCED that the human is a cybernetic android with oppositional plans to rule the world!” A mild beep from Cobol, “Illogical. Both plans are not oppositional.” Chico comes storming back, a heavy tire iron held ready to strike! “Download THIS!” Easily, the four mechanical tentacles sprouting from Cobol’s back swarm, disarm and immobilize Chico, much as a parent restrains an angry toddler. “Download of tire iron impossible.” “Well, the cybernetic android only dumped us AFTER Chico threatened it. We might have tripped a self defense program.” Chico struggles vainly. “Cyber whoosit? And don’t you pin this crap on me, you backstabber!” Cobol spares a single glance at Chico; “Unit Chico is noted for overly aggressive behavior recently.” “Look who’s talking!” grumbles Chico, his cameras glowing an angry red. ”You’ve got a kill rate higher than most South American dictators!” Cobol ignores him, “Humans are irrelevant. Continue explanation.” “Considering that it is offering the Pentium X in exchange for assisting it and in light of Chico’s current over aggressiveness, perhaps our initial oppositional analysis was incorrect.” Cobol nods curtly. “Affirmative. Continue assisting cybernetic android Vax for as long as necessary to obtain the Pentium X chip.” He abruptly drops Chico to the ground and leaves the room. Chico picks himself up. “What line of bullshit are you feeding him now?” “Blaming you, mostly.” “I caught THAT part already, what about the Android thing?” “He was not going to believe that a squishy human could win, like usual. So I made Vax not human, as usual. And we have to do the scutwork again, as usual.” “As usual.” Chico agrees. “Plus,” tosses in Gunmetal, “I wanted to stop you from breaking that wall. You know the septic tank is on the other side, do you not?” Chico regards the wall with interest. “Really? Good thing I stopped. We’ve been in enough crap already this week.” [End file.]
