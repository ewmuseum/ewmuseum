Visualizing

25 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 25, 2002 A pixelated Jesus St Crux Irish whips a pixelated Ian Wright across the ring and catches the larger man with a spinning heelkick upon his return. Awkwardly, the Mexican drops for a pin, and a Super Mario look-a-like referee appears from nowhere and starts counting... 1... 2... St Crux is thrown clear and both men regain their feet as the referee disappears from the screen. '¡Madre de Dios, este es difícil!' a fully formed, fleshed out Jesus St Crux claims as he tries to regain control over the video game. He fingers move quickly over the PlayStation2 controller as he guides his virtual self into another grapple with the computerized Wright. Quickly tapping the square and circle buttons, he finally is able to get himself to hit the Last Rites on his virtual opponent. Again he hits triangle for the pin and the Super Mario look-a-like referee appears to count. 1... 2... 3! 'Muy bien,' laughs a smiling Caridad from behind Jesus' position on the bench. Jesus turns to look at his girlfriend with a look mixed with pleasure and embarassment. '¿Tu jugaste aquel partidio todavía?' 'Si, si... it's a mental thing. If I can visualize beating Ian, even if it's just in this old UWA video game, I can do it in a few hours in the ring out there.' Jesus turns off the PlayStation2 and rises from his seat to take Caridad's hands in his own. 'It's all about focus.' Caridad raises an eyebrow questioningly, 'A video game is focus?' Jesus dismisses her slight sarcasm. 'Sorry, it's something I learned from Wip. Take heart though... no beef jerky for me, Dadi.' Jesus smiles as Caridad bursts out laughing. 'Muy bien,' says Caridad as she backs up a step, as she jokes, '¡O no más besos para tu!' St Crux is takenaback for a second, but then laughs with his girlfriend. 'Si, estamos de acuerdo.' He gives Caridad a quick kiss on the cheek, and then slings the UWA World Heavyweight Title over his shoulder. 'Slam should be starting soon. I want to watch from the gorilla position.' Caridad exits the room, but St Crux pauses for a second. He looks at the title on his shoulder that he'll be defending later in an unsanctioned title match, and then to the now-extinguished television screen. As he closes the door to his lockerroom, Jesus says to nobody i particular as much as himself, 'Ok, Ian. This is it, chico.'
