Adventures in Hershey Pt. 1 'Culture Shock'

25 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-25-02 The planet Earth. What an interesting place it is. Floating around in space, hopelessly spinning on it’s axis as well as spinning around the sun, never ending. From this point of view, you can actually see it slowly spinning. But as you move closer, objects upon the Earth begin to become more prominent. Clouds, swirling in a maelstrom of pressures and temperatures can begin to be seen, as well as continents below. Large, powerful bodies of land seemingly floating atop the water below. Beautiful colors of green and blue and brown are noticed as half of the world faces the sun, almost as if it was cooking, and the other side shuns away, enveloped in a sheet of darkness. But we move closer, as our focus appears to be on a large, oddly shaped continent. To many, it could be known as North America, and the specific area? The United States. If boundaries were drawn, we’d realize that indeed, we were focused on the state of Pennsylvania, around Hershey Park to be even more precise. The town of Hershey, known for it’s chocolate, it’s amusement park, and it’s humble beginnings, appears to be the focus of our attention. We notice the tops of a few buildings, a couple of streets, passing cars, and a smallish terminal of some sort. Upon closer inspection we notice that this terminal, which is part of the side of a brick building, has a line of people outside of it leading under a small blue canopy. With the genius of modern technology and the great ability of the writer, our view moves from an aerial view to a side view, so we can see under the canopy in question. The terminal appears to be a cash machine, much like you’d find a MAC machine to be. In front of it, hulking beneath the canopy, is Ivan Stanislav. The Russian Bear appears to be quite perplexed as the line of people behind him look on. BEEP BEEP BEEP The terminal spits Stanislav’s card out at him in defiance of The Russian Bear. “C’mon buddy, we’ve been here for ten minutes!” shouts a man behind Ivan, not too happy that he has had to wait so long. Stanislav turns around and growls, “Wait a minute, fool! The machine is broken….” Ivan growls and looks closer at the tiny computer screen, “Capitalistic contraption! Give me my money!!” Ivan roars and slams his fist on the top of the machine. A few people lose their cool and leave the line, giving up hope on the hulking man, but still a good seven people continue to look on. “Hurry it up!!” “What’s taking so long!!” “Take your time, will ya?” Ivan slowly turns and roars, “If you people would shut up, perhaps I could get this piece of junk to work!” The crowd dies down as Ivan turns once again to the machine. It spits his card out at him. With an angry shove, Stanislav jams the card back into the small slot and reads the screen before him. “Enter PIN number!!” Ivan jams his finger into the console, hitting numbers, and stands up straight, “There, it shall work now!” BEEP BEEP BEEP Groans reverberate through the crowd behind Ivan. A small, old woman pushes up towards Stanislav and pushes a finger into him, “Let us go! You can deal with this later!!” “Shut up woman! Ivan was here first!” Stanislav glares at her, causing her to backpedal, and turns back to the machine. “Why is this not working?? What is it doing wrong?” Ivan scratches his head as the crowd slowly disperses. “Forget this buddy! I’ll go somewhere else!!” Screams one man as he storms off. Various other onlookers exchange their opinions on the matter and walk off as well, leaving The Russian Bear alone with the machine. Ivan puts his hands on his hips and stares at the machine, “Confound you machine! I did enter the correct PIN number!!” Ivan stares at the machine as several footsteps are heard. Over his shoulder Alexei Ruslan, Speedy Riggs, and Loyal Communist Party Member Wang emerge into our view. “Praporschik? What is taking so long?” asks Alexei. “The machine is not working, Alex!” Ivan replies, “Look at it! It is not giving me my money!” Alexei squints and stares at the screen, “Perhaps it is the government. Maybe they are toying with you, Ivan.” Ivan nods slowly and rubs his chin before looking around, “That is possibility…that IS possibility….” Riggs clears his throat, “No, no, no, I don’t think it’s that.” Ivan and Ruslan turn and stare at Riggs. He swallows and looks around, “At least..that’s what I think.” “Nyet, I think it is government, it has to be. They have camera’s..they have spies…they….” Begins Ruslan as he hatches a master plan, involving NATO, the EU, and the powers of the world that secretly hate The Motherland. Riggs tries to talk over Alexei, but Wang chimes in and begins to talk with him, drowning out Riggs. “Ivan…maybe…” “…and then, they sneak into the Kremlin and change your PIN number….” “Ivan..I think that perhaps….” “The Peoples Wepubwic would neva stand for such a thing Comwade….” “Praporschik, I think I know what the…” Alexei and Wang continue their dialogue whilst Stanislav leans his head against the wall and tries the machine again. Riggs begins to interject again, “Ivan, if you’d listen to me…” BEEP BEEP BEEP!!! “STUPID MACHINE!!” roars Ivan as the machine spits the card back out. Ivan yells and punches the screen, breaking it. “SHUT THE HELL UP!!” All the sound stops as the group turns and faces Riggs, who for the first time in perhaps his life, asserts himself as an authority. “Yes…Speedy?” asks Ivan. “Perhaps, Praporschik, your card is expired?” he whispers, slinking into the background again. Ivan squints and turns to the card. He pulls it out and looks at it, “Issued in….1984. It should be all right, da?” Riggs snatches the card out of Ivan’s hand, “Ivan, it expired over a decade ago! It’s not even in English!” Riggs shrugs and tosses the card away, “It’s useless! That’s why it’s not working!” “It worked then, Speedy!!” Ivan retorts. Speedy shook his head slowly, “Things change, Praporschik. What worked in the past might not work now!” Riggs turns and walks away, “I’m going to the car!” Alexei snorts and heads towards Riggs, not happy with his tone and chastises him. Ivan stares at the shattered card a few feet away, “I did not know about that, Wang, did you?” “Well, Pwapowschik, yeah.” Wang answered slowly. For some reason, it hurt realizing that time had passed Ivan and he had not noticed it. It was a giant ironic model for his entire life, in a way. Ivan looked over at Wang and leaned against the sparking machine, “How much have I missed, Wang? Without Russia giving me money, I must do this on my own….” Wang shrugged and smiled up at Ivan. He was more than just a communist to the aged Chinaman, but also a good friend now, “Come Pwapowschik, it is not that bad. We will teach you some of the ways that you have missed. I know of a Panda Palace, all you can eat Chinese food nearby! Lets go eat there, just you and I, and I will teach you how to fill out a check!!” Ivan nods slowly, “Lets get out of this accursed town.” He walks away from the terminal and heads towards his own personal jeep as the scene zooms away slowly and fades to black.
