Arrival Part 2

16 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/16/02 Tom Walczak’s limousine is slowly seen coming up the winding dirt road that leads to Walczak’s house. The limousine gets closer and closer to the house as it rumbles over the unpaved road. Finally it gets to the house that sits at the top. Walczak looks over it and smiles. It is a two-story house with a five-acre lot, and there is an in ground swimming pool in the back. “Hey, Mr. Walczak? Um, I hope that you don’t mind me asking, but this does not look as of you truly live like a communist.” “You sound just like that dumbass moron on the plane, and as I told him, I do live like a communist because communism says that all should be treated equal, and I am working so that all could live like this. It is a new age, and just like all other things communism has evolved.” “Oh, well that sounds good enough to me. Say, I am a bit thirsty do you have anything to drink in there?” “Does it look like I have any supplies at all? I just moved in here, so how in the hell am I supposed to have food or drink? Now that you mentioned it I am a bit thirsty and hungry, so why don’t you get in your car, and go pick up some drinks and food for me?” “Well I suppose that I could, but what is it exactly that you want?” “Get me some cold cuts, fresh bread, some beer, and some other crap, I don’t exactly know what to get around here.” “Sure no problem, but I will be needing some money.” Walczak digs into his pocket and pulls out his wallet. He takes out 200 Złoty, the Polish currency, and he hands it to his driver. The driver gets into the car, and he drives away as Walczak still stares at the house. “Now, I have some peace and quiet and all that I have to do is relax, and as I do so can think about the many ways in which The Red Army will seriously hurt Doug Troy and the Trashcan Man. This Saturday I face the Trashcan Man, and I cannot wait for this opportunity. No, it is not because I am facing a former PCW World Champion, not that would have been an honor for the old Tom Walczak, but now the only thing that I am looking forward to is showing all of Trashcan Man’s fans just how week a son of a bitch he is. I will laugh as I stand over the rotted body of Trashcan Man, and I think of all of his fans around the world screaming in fear, as they have just seen their hero destroyed.” Walczak smiles once again as he starts his walk towards his new home. He gets to the front door and as he walk through, and into his house the scene fades to black…
