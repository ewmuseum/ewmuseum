pfft...Wash Ups!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/12 /02 The sun has been replaced by the black sky of the night. The night sky is filled with clouds not allowing any of the stars to shine through. This is the time of night when all of the riff raff, scum, criminals, and evil people come out to play, especially in this part of the city. The giant abandoned cathedral that Azrael Cain now calls home looms over the city sky line. Inside, amongst the debris and filth Cain, his partner, Hessian, Minion and Caleb can be seen recovering from the latest attack from Da Westsidaz. Cain is raging as a result from the latest assault from the Riptah and Short Circuit. Hessian’s back is turned as he tries to fix his steel mask yet again. Minion is trying to scrub the spray paint out of Hessian’s shirt while Caleb is attempting to do the same to Cain’s back. Needless to say, Cain is not too pleased with what transpired on Slam Yesterday! “Those two pathetic fools are going to pay if it is the last thing I do during my stay here! If they think that they can get away with such disrespect for us, they have another thing coming! We are the Tag Team Champions! Not Them! We are the greatest warriors in this fed! Not Them! We are…” Cain is interrupted by Caleb who is trying to get the last of the spray paint off of the beast’s back. “Will you hold still God Dammit! Heh. This gasoline is spilling all over my hands because you keep moving about! Heh. I almost have it all off! Now just hold still! Heh-Heh-Heh.” Cain rolls his eyes out of utter exasperation from the entire situation as his manager continues to scrub at his back with an old dirty rag soaked in gasoline. Minion can be heard chuckling as he continues to scrub at Hessian’s shirt. Hessian runs his hands over his head in order to feel the new wounds that were just freshly inflicted. He winces in some pain from the dent in his forehead before he chimes in. “I am getting damn sick and tired of my forehead getting dented in week in and week out! Do you have any idea of how bad a headache you get when your skull continues to get dented in? It hurts like hell!” Hessian puts a further exclamation on his point. Caleb continues to scrub on Cain’s back turning the once pale skin to a nice shade of red. After a few more seconds Caleb steps back and nods. “Heh. That should do it, I think. Heh-Heh-Heh.” The small hobo starts to scratch the stubble that is growing on his chin. Minion can still be heard chuckling amongst all the bickering. “Those pricks are just lucky that the Imp wasn’t there to watch your backs! I would have kicked there ghetto asses straight back to rat holes they crawled out of! Then I would have taken that slut Daisy with us and shown her firsthand that Impin’ ain’t easy!” Minion begins to do his best impression of some kind of martial arts. The imp is hopping around a dirty floor with his legs and arms flailing about in a very awkward manner until he is interrupted by Cain lowering his face to eye level with the imp and giving him a glare that could stop a herd of stampeding cattle in their tracks. Minion flinches quickly before Cain can begin. “Listen to me you little worm! I still owe you for that little comment that you had in the ring! So you better watch your mouth before I shut it! Permanently!” “Uh…right…just…uh…kidding…big guy.” Minion mumbles under his breath. Cain crosses quickly up onto the alter area and turns to the rest of the misfits that join him in the cathedral. Cain continues his tirade. “Who do those two washed up pathetic wretches think they are? These are our titles! I don’t care if they one the PCW Tag Titles five different times! They never had to face us! If the fates had brought us together sooner then I guarantee that they would not have one those titles once! This is OSW not the PCW! And those two have never faced the wrath of the heretics!” Cain finishes his tirade by slamming his massive fist down on top of the marble top of the alter. Hessian rises to his feet and places his scratched and dented mask back onto his battered face before he turns around. The massive goliath looks at his tag partner with his glowing green eyes. “You will get your chance at those two thugs this week! We have to defend our titles against them! Then we can get our revenge!” As Hessian crosses across the floor he grabs the tattered shirt from Minion who has just finished cleaning off the paint. The goliath quickly throws the shirt over his massive and scarred upper body. Cain leaps down the couple of stairs that lead up to the alter area of the cathedral and immediately crosses to his tag partner. Cain’s nostrils are flaring like an enraged beast. His famed anger has overcome the fallen angel yet again. “I don’t care what it takes, but this week, those two whelps and their little wench are going to suffer for their transgressions! I want nothing more than to break the spirits of those self righteous wash ups!” Caleb chimes in though in his usual untimely fashion. “Yeah, but are you two forgetting that Ed is playing some kind of role in all this. Heh-Heh. That man can easily screw us all over! Heh-Heh. We need to take care of that situation before it escalates any further. Heh-Heh. Then we can worry about those sons a bitches! Heh-Heh-Heh.” The massive beast that is Azrael Cain becomes even more enraged before he bursts out yet again. “Like I said before, that man is just as pathetic as the wash ups that he brought in to try and stop us! He will fall as hard as they do!” Cain stares down at the small hobo, Caleb. “And all you need to do is make sure that nothing goes wrong in the ring! I want them to suffer, but I know that Hawke and Masters are going to have something to say about that! On that note who knows what Ed has in store for us! You need to make sure that there are no surprises, but that you are there to count…1…2…3 for us!” Caleb shrugs his shoulders before answering. “Yeah no problem! Heh-Heh. But…uh…I don’t think that Masters and Hawke are in any shape to do anything this week. Heh-Heh. There is no way that they are going to win those belts this week! Heh-Heh-Heh.” Hessian sits down on a toppled over pew and braces himself for a moment due to the loss of blood from the attack. His metal glove begins to rub his head as he speaks. “I want them to pay as much as you do, but with all of these new enemies emerging lately we need to be careful. We need to pay a little visit to that crazy bastard Ed this week. Despite the fact that he is denying any knowledge of the claims that Riptah and Short Circuit are making we still need to make him understand that we don’t take to kindly to those sort of threats! Then we will take care of Da Westsidaz!” Cain’s anger begins to subside ever so slightly as a plan finally begins to develop. Cain leans back and runs his hands through his long black hair. He then crosses his massive arms over his chest and begins to brood as do the rest of the heretics in the cathedral. The last thing that can be heard is Caleb chiming in. “pfft wash ups!! Heh-Heh. Only thing I hate more than pretty boys are wash ups! Heh-Heh. I mean they’re pathetic! Heh-Heh-Heh.” Caleb and Minion’s shrill laughter is all that can be heard echoing through the cathedral as the darkness overcomes everything yet again.
