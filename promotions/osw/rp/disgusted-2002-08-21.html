Disgusted

21 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 21st, 2002 The room wasn't very well lit, but my eyes could see through the darkness. I sat there on the floor of the bar, a bottle of alcohol in my hand, and a head that hurt. And here I am, a stranger in my own city. The headlines in the news was the dream match between the Extreme Legends and NC-17 - a match for the ages. Two storied teams that would take each other to the limit, bring a fantasy match up to life, and prove once and for all who the greatest team in history were. We were the best. No, we ARE the best. How could we have lost? And by disqualification. Where is the honor in a defeat like that? Now NC-17 will move on to the next round in a tournament to crown new tag team champions. A championship that belongs around my waist and Mike Extreme's waist. I took another swig of the bottle, allowing the poisonous drink to envelope me. It clouded my mind once more, allowing me to believe that my soul was at ease. But the alcohol just supressed my true feelings. My glasses fell by the wayside, crashing to the dirty bar floor. I usually kept good care of them, but now they weren't good for anything. I had lost my way. The bartender made his way over to me, drying a glass with his towel. He scowled at me, but he had a sympathetic look on his face. 'Time's up, bro,' The bartender said. I arched my neck up at him, looking none too interested. 'Give me a few minutes.' He shook his head. 'Can't. Bar policy is to get this place cleared out. Otherwise you guys never know when to quit.' He looked at my hand with the bottle and glanced around. 'Is that your only bottle?' I nodded tiredly. 'My first and definitely not the last.' I reached up and tried to grab another but he stopped me. 'You can't handle your liquor,' He replied, shaking his head. 'You look like a smart guy. Why are you doing this to yourself?' 'Of course I'm smart,' I replied, struggling to get to my feet. 'I'm wearing a suit and I have glasses. That automatically means I'm intelligent...' My gibberish trailed on as he lead me out. As I reached the cold night air, I took a deep breath. 'Smell that beautiful night...' 'I'll call you a cab...' I waved him off, beginning to walk towards my apartment. At least, towards I thought where my apartment was. 'I'll walk it,' I muttered. 'I'm an athlete. I can take it.' And as I stuttered off on my way, I could hear the bartender behind me saying what a mess I was. I agreed with him, if only I wasn't so drunk. So, so drunk.
