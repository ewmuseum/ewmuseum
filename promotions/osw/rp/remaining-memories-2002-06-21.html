Remaining Memories

21 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.21.2002 When I think back on the highlights of my life, I see a projection of many extreme positives and negatives. However, as I trace them all back to the so-called monster I am today, I find appreciation for each memory ... no matter how sick or sadistic. For I can never let go of them, simply because no matter how long I run from them, these demons inside of me will forever make up who I am. These are the things which allow Cory Rutherford III to travel back to the murder of the only people he ever loved. In that, I can see exactly why I abandoned who I came into this world as and evolved into the one who everyone hates. I can virtually see the creation of Kory Storm. However, in the wave of popped painkillers and title reigns, I can also witness my evident destruction. No, I am not thinking of death, for I believe death is just a reoccurrence of life. This is far more important than the gates of heaven opening up for a seemingly hellbound soul. Because in my quest to finally become pure, I abandoned the one thing I always had the right to be. Myself. In exchange for respect and a high paycheck ... who wouldn't have given up on something which was obviously hopeless? Not me. Saturday, however, the walls which I've built from tools of ego and victory began to crumble. For I lost ... and to my nemesis at that! There is only one way to recover and begin to rebuild the only life I have left. That is to defeat the man who is responsible for all of this, and drive a sledgehammer through the dreams of Jim Axtell.][ 'I have always asked myself how humanity could let itself become so naive. For when I came into the world, which wasn't so long ago in spirit, there were no feelings of love for one another. To be brutally honest, you all were just like me. But now, in these times of war and turmoil, humanity joins hands to form an unbreakable band around the world. What is the problem with peace you ask? Nothing. However, I find a problem with harmony when it is all a lie. To begin with, look at the war which so many family members bleed for. Could one man across the Atlantic be responsible? Of course not. This was all brought upon America by America. We used our power to hurt rather than help and in that we became vulnerable. Then in a form of payback, we sat in horror and watched the events of 9/11, while a country who built up to be just like us gained control of the actual power. Just like in any conquest, revenge became the proverbial bitch. In that same context, think about those same countries of the world. Countries that America's population of fat rednecks and cheating sluts wouldn't have come near a not so distant year ago. No longer do the different colored hands burn when touched by those who once hated them with passion. For why burn skin, when we can all burn in hell?' ][Grim as his words are, Kory Storm seems to feel a sense of truth to these sentiments, speaking about them with pride and conviction. Almost as if he is urging the public to think about it, rather than let some uninformed news broadcaster tell them how to wonder and contemplate. Upon lighting the cigarette which is 'Our Paragon's' only guiding light ... he speaks to the masses inside of his head once again.][ 'Just like the government you all worship uses its flag to blind you from reality, I too have run from truth in my life. In fact, the night when I saw blood pour from the bodies of my family thus causing love to pour from my heart, I ran for hours before settling on the reckless mutiny of my life. Now I refuse to run any longer. I will go to Ascension and not focus on climbing on a meaningless mountain, but climbing over the obstacle of what has been a thorn in my side for one of the most hate filled months of my life. For I must focus on the man who focuses on me in that very same way. Jim Axtell. I must admit that I underestimated Axtell as a competitor, simply because most of the police officers I've ever come in contact with have been completely useless at their jobs or absolutely crooked in doing their jobs. I've been through the beatings like King and I've gone through accusations like Simpson. But I don't claim to be a criminal. I still feel that when I see the cold eyes of Jim Axtell ... that he feels that way. Maybe it is because I've never let myself rest until his life has been made a Hell on Earth. Maybe it is because he sees me as a plague which resembles one from his past. Or maybe, while he sits in his comfort and calls out envy, he is the one with those feelings. Jim Axtell will show a side of himself never seen Ascension ... a testament to the theory that he is sick of playing the hero. I might be every thing Jim Axtell wants to. For he knows that you all are unforgiving and in one slip up, he'll spit on you idiots just as quick as I will. He can't be as stupid as I thought if he can claim a victory over me. Then again, what would his victory be without violence? And how was his violence created? By my sadism. He's earned credibility from the actions I'm despised for. I feel that in front of a sold out audience this Sunday, Blood Sunday ... I'll even the score.' ][Flipping on the TV and pressing a play button on the remote, there is a movie playing of major highlights from last week's edition of Slam! The smiling face of Amy Campbell, shows just how quickly love can turn to hate. Then ... in the last second of visual ... the very view which has replayed inside of his mind graces the screen. One. Two. Three. Victory has passed Kory Storm up and all he can show for it is a match on Pay Per View and a gash on his head.][ 'History will show many things taking place in one solid period of time. Beginnings. Endings. Gray areas. So hate me now, while I allow you all to look into the future which I refuse to gaze at. What will be the culmination of what many consider a lost cause. Bloodshed. Loss. Bruising. Evil. All words which enter my mind all tracing back to nothing in my past or present, but to a future. The future of Jim Axtell.' ][For so long people have tried to get inside of my mind, always trying to figure out something aside from what I allow them to. Always, they find nothing. Am I hidden from the only people who want to know me ... want to hate me? Can a victory over the first person I've hated since myself allow me to become a better shell of what stands in front of you now? Only two men, and a twenty pound sledgehammer can tell.][
