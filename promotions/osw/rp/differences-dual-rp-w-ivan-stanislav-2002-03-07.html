Differences (dual rp w/ Ivan Stanislav)

7 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/07/02 Tempest sauntered along next to Miyuki as the two laughed over some shared joke. Tempest turned to her with a comical imitation of anger and said, 'You placed that darn ad didn't you?' 'Of course!' Miyuki said. 'But only to get you back for putting my number in that one you did that said, 'Nymphomaniac Jello fetishist with a special fondness for Pokemon dolls, seeks limber Eskimo into dining on cactus leaves'.' Tempest laughed and clapped her hands a little as she said, 'But it was cool! And the calls you got were even better!' Miyuki sighed, 'Tempest, that is the last time I rent a room with you. Keeping you entertained is a full time job that not many people are up to.' She shook her head and looked around as they passed a few office buildings and said, 'So what are we doing here anyway?' Tempest chuckled and came to a stop in front of an older building and said, 'Well, you'll never guess whose 'foreign' office is in that very building.' Miyuki rolled her eyes and said, 'Oh no... please tell me you didn't bring me into a hundred mile radius of the propaganda machine. Tempest what are we doing here? You're...' 'Invading someone's territory,' Tempest interrupted with a shrug. 'I didn't have the chance to give Ivan a piece of my mind the other night after all.' 'And you're sure he can deal with that? I mean, this is a piece or your very warped mind after all,' Miyuki said grinning. Tempest scowled and said, 'Oh thanks, reassure me why don't ya? Well, here goes nothing...' She took a deep breath and stepped into the building as Miyuki waved and said, 'Have fun. I'll just wait over on the other side of the river, where the fallout will be less.' She shook her head and watched Tempest disappear into the building. Tempest climbed the stairs at an energetic jog and then walked down the hallway, looking for the right office. She wasn't sure of where she should be until she reached a door covered in the alien characters of the Russian alphabet. She raised her hand and knocked lightly, then decided to try harder since some people probably would ignore anything that wasn't loud. She knocked harder and as she did, the door swung open and she knocked her fist right against Ivan's stomach. 'I WILL NOT BUY ANYTHING FROM YOU CAPITALIST......huh?' Ivan shook his head when Tempest punched his stomach and looked down at her. 'Ow!' she squealed and looked up innocently, while rubbing her hand. 'Oops... sorry.' Ivan blinked and roared aloud, 'LENIN, STALIN, and PREMIER KHRUSHCHEV! What are you doing here??' Ivan took a step back and pointed her away. Tempest pouted and said, 'I just thought I'd stop by for a visit.' Ivan shook his head and looked around at his office, his desk currently full of papers. 'You come where I work to visit?!' He rubs his forehead and turned, heading back to his desk. 'Why not? Besides, I thought you worked in the ring and you sure don't want me around there,' she said with a little huff. 'I am Praporschik, Tempest, I have two jobs. While not in ring I am busy working on the integrity of Russian Federation,' Ivan turned and picked up some papers and dropped them, 'see?' Tempest picked up a piece of paper and said, 'But Ivan, this says 'Joe's Deli Carryout Menu'.' Ivan grabbed the paper and tossed it over his shoulder, 'THAT IS ALEXEI'S!!' He growled and pushed several papers with various pictures on them into a folder and quickly jammed them into his drawer, rather anxious to get rid of them and jams the drawer closed. Stanislav growled and jammed his knee against the drawer, moving the entire desk. Tempest eyed the just closed drawer suspiciously, then glanced up at Ivan. 'MmHmm.... those capitalists do make a good sandwich I'm told. Funny I didn't know you were that involved in military matters,' she said casually while sneaking her fingers into another folder, hoping he didn't notice. SLAM! Ivan's huge fist crashed into the folder, ensuring it stayed shutas Tempest withdrew her hand just in time. He leaned forward, staring eye to eye with Tempest, his skull dwarfing hers, 'Ever hear of international security, little storm?' Tempest blinked and then looked thoughtful a moment and said, 'Yeah it's insurance or something right?' She did her best to hide a snicker, but had little success. Ivan's nostrils flared as his breathing got heavier. He grabbed his papers and put them in his desk. Just as he opened his mouth to talk, his door flew open and Alexei Ruslan barged in, 'Praporschik Stanislav! A contact told me that Tempest was....oh!' Alexei stood stock still for a moment. Tempest turned on her heel and smiled sweetly at Alexei as she said, 'Tempest was... what? I didn't know you still had such an interest in my affairs. Haven't seen you falling from any trees lately. Ivan?' She turned back to him with an irritated expression, huffing a little. Ivan mouthed words and pointed at Alexei. Alexei slapped his forehead and turned, promptly leaving the room. Ivan shook his head, 'Tempest, I have nothing to do with it. Alexei is doing things on his own....' he stopped and shook his head again, 'no, what are you doing here? Why are you at my place of work?' Tempest folded her arms and her fingers seemed to twitch almost involuntarily as she said, 'Well, I was just stopping by to ask why you were being so mean the other night. But now I wanna know what's going on. Alexei is doing his own thing? Maybe I should have a talk with Alexei, maybe me and my Volkswagen should have a talk with Alexei at the next pedestrian crossing!' Her voice has risen to a rare squawk as she fumed. Instantly, Ivan's eyes flared as she hinted towards hurting Alexei. Stanislav grabbed the side of his desk and shoved it across the room, sending it crashing into the wall, 'You will not do anything of the sort! I am Army! He is intelligence! I have no jurisdiction in his actions, and he does not have anything to do with MINE!' Tempest gazed at him levelly and said, 'He's your friend. And I'm not of interest to any nation's security, not even my own. So I don't get why I have to put up with this.' Ivan blinked and shook his head, thoroughly confused, 'Put up with WHAT?' 'THAT!' she snapped waving her hand in the direction of the departed Ruslan. 'Why am I of so much interest? I mean I might not care if you were a little more indulgent of me, but you wouldn't even let me get away with a harmless joke.' She pouted again remembering her broken camera with which she was having so much fun on Slam! With a growl, Ivan pointed at Tempest, 'You were having fun, right?' She nodded silently. Ivan grinned wide, catching her, 'Well, you were having fun at Ivan's expense! Why do you do this to me? So, I had fun smashing stupid camera into nothing, at YOUR expense!!' He laughed loudly, 'It is not as fun when on receiving end, now is it?? IS IT?' Tempest looked at him sadly and said, 'It's not the same. I can laugh at myself. I do it every day. But smashing the camera wasn't funny. It was just mean.' Her head drooped a little as she said it. Ivan looked down at her and muttered softly, 'What did you expect me to do? Laugh? Think it was all something that would be disregarded? Do you think Ivan appreciates when you make him look like fool in front of people?' 'I was hoping... you'd just enjoy it. I mean, it's funny. I love being the person the water balloon hits as much as being the one who throws it,' she said with a sigh. Ivan paused and turned away and looked out his window with his arms crossed over his chest, 'At another time Tempest, that may have been humorous, but not during a time such as that.' Tempest threw up her hands and said, 'It was backstage! It was way before your match! It's not like you were in the middle of some terribly serious thing,' she said, stepping toward him. As she did, she spied a photograph on the floor, spilled from the desk he had sent flying. She leaned over to pick it up and looked at the glossy image of herself feeding her dog trouble, smiling happily. 'Then again, maybe you were,' she said flatly. Ivan growled and slammed his fist through his wall, spinning on his heels and looking at her. He put his hands in his pockets as he caught her looking at the picture. 'It does not matter, Tempest, for no matter what Ivan says, he will not be right, there is no way. You think in one direction, and Ivan thinks in the other, da? Even if Ivan were to say it was for your own good, you would not believe me, no?' He marched over to Tempest and snatched the picture out of her hands and tossed it on the desk, 'Am I right or not? Or do you wish to nose through my things some more and get Ivan court-marshaled? Would you laugh then??' 'Since when is that,' she said, waving her hand in the direction of the photo, 'a matter of military security?' She huffed, her shoulders rising and falling. 'You're right, we do think in different directions. And if you can't stand me playing, having fun, being me, then why the heck should I put up with being watched, followed, bossed around and looked down on?!' After a moment of staring up at Ivan from her lower vantage point, she added, 'I mean figuratively of course.' Ivan's right eye squinted as he studied her face. He ran a hand through his beard, 'You knew this was coming when you came up here. You knew that Ivan would not be happy, you knew this.' Ivan paused and walked around her, standing with his back to the doorway, 'I have to ask you, Tempest, you are good friends with Jane Reagan, and I have heard rumor of your possibility of being in match with Doug Troy. Perhaps if YOU could show Ivan that this is not some trick, some joke that ultimately will leave me bleeding and you laughing, THEN things may change.' Ivan pointed and the Soviet flag hanging on the wall, 'When you laugh at me, you laugh at THAT! I cannot jeopardize my orders! I cannot jeopardize my charge for the Russian People! Can you not understand a word that Ivan says! I have been stabbed far too many times in the back!' 'And you've done what to me?' she asked and shook her head. 'I don't have a cause to fight for, or a nation of people watching what I do. I'm just in all of this for fun, cuz I love doing it. That's reason enough.' She looked up at him and said, 'Jane hasn't asked me to be in any matches yet. She's sorta being paranoid I think, cuz I'm on loan so to speak. So I'm not booked yet.' Ivan disregarded her words, 'Anything related to Jane Reagan is wrong and bound to be damaging to me.' He scoffed and took a step toward her, 'I do not know if you have anything important to say, Tempest, but at the moment I am terribly busy. I will do what I can to stop Alexei, but it may not even be him, it may not be in his hands. You come to whatever conclusion you want and you can take Ivan's word for what it is. So, voice whatever else of importance you have, or I would like it if you would leave me in peace.' Tempest glared up at him and said, 'That's great, dismiss me like I'm some little military page. I have an idea, I'll leave you in peace... for good. And you can then tell Alexei...' She stormed to the door, threw it open and yelled into the hallway, '... And anyone else who cares to listen that Tempest is gone! Finished! History! Not an issue!' She slammed the door and wheeled around as she said, 'I want to stop looking over my shoulder all the time. And I don't know why I even put up with it this long. You don't really like me anyway; you're too paranoid to manage that.' Despite the angry outburst, she looked disappointed as she headed for the door again. Quickly, Ivan grabbed her by the arm and held her, spinning her around, 'Wait a moment!' Tempest sighed and said, 'What?' Ivan thought for a long time and let go of her, 'Fine, go if you wish...perhaps you are right....perhaps we are just two foolish people.' He sighed to himself and fixed a suspender strap. Tempest shook her head and said, 'I never doubted it,' as she walked out the door. 'GGRRRRRRRRAAAAAARRRRRRGGGGHHH!!' reverberated through the building followed by a crashing noise while Tempest walked down the hallway, followed by several loud thumps. For a moment she stopped and then sighed and kept walking down the stairs.
