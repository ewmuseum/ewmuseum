Feelings Like These.

18 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 18, 2002 He had to pack lightly; it would suck to have a heavy knapsack if a riot broke out. Reno Davis stood in his locker-room, only an hour after his loss in the six-man matchup for the International Title number 1 contender ship. The loss hadn’t bothered him, as he normally didn’t fare too well in any match involving more than a tag match. There was a protest in Washington, DC. That Reno was going to attend starting during the week sometime. He wasn’t sure when, but figured he’d check it out anyway. The protest was against the so-called “War On Terrorism”. Davis had strong feelings about many things, but never bothered with protests since they always turned into riots. He figures since this protest was for peace, no fighting would be involved. Maybe there’ll be a bit of progress...ha...not in America... Amy Campbell came into the locker room and snuck up behind her good friend Reno, who had on more than a towel in this meeting. “Boo!” Aimz said, jumping on Reno’s back, surprising him. He laughed and flipped her over his back, with Amy landing on her feet. Reno playfully shoved her shoulder and continued his packing. “Hey,” He replied. “Have fun losing tonight? Hehe...you seem used to it by now. I got my ass kicked by Beanstalk Yamato...so not much fun for poor lil' Amy.” She giggled and sat down beside Reno’s old and patched up red knapsack. “Bah, I never win those things anyway. Put me in a submission or a ladder match and I’ll take on anyone down.” “Oooh...ladder match, sounds fun fun. You seem like one of those ladder match kinda suited guys...” She joked, sarcastically. “Hey! I’ve never lost a single one in my whole career!” Reno countered. “Suuuuure Reno, I beleive you. By the way, Mariah Carey called, said something about leaving her undies at your place...” Reno stopped packing and shook his head, “You’ll get that ‘wit’ thing one of these days Aimz...” He said with a smile. “Awww blow me, at least I found it amusing...So where we headed after this? Or is it one of those 'No Amy allowed' deals?” She ran her leg up his, but he didn’t flinch. “I dunno, I’m thinking of hiking down to Washington to go to some protest. You can come if you want to, but it might get crazy and cops aren’t the nicest to protestors.” The thought of Amy getting beaten down by a dozen nightsticks was sickening to Reno, almost like the feeling he gets watching her get hurt in the ring but ten times worse. “Reno...it's not like I'm used to the police being friendly with me, though I tend to know 'em on a first-name basis...hehe. But yeah, 'some protest' isn't exactly specific...what's it for? If it fits, I'll go.” Aimz beamed, her big eyes fixed on his. He scratched the side of his head and sighed. “It’s about the United States’ ‘War On Terrorism’ that’s going on. They have a lot to wake up to...I don’t plan on accomplishing too much but you never know,” Davis shrugged, “Anything is possible.” “You care about these things? You're awake...I like that. Count me in, I guess...the random shit the USA's doing just pisses me off, they've gotta start thinking...I guess we can shove that a little.” The Unwanted Angel looked at Aimz, at her pretty little face that hadn’t quite seen the aftermath of the wrestling business yet, that hadn’t been aged twice as fast as it should have because of drugs or lack of sleep. That same thought of batons whipping her in the face, clipping her kneecaps, tear gas choking her to hysteria... “No. No, Amy I’d rather you didn’t.” “And why not?” Aimz’s smile faded slightly, replaced with a look of flirty interest. Reno struggled for a cover-up answer, but couldn’t help imagining her in pain. “Just,” Reno zipped up his knapsack and threw it over his shoulder, “Just because. I think I need to take this one alone, Okay babes?” He again gave her a gentle, playful shove. “Babes'? Someone's in a friendly mood...you're not usually so pleasant. But then again, the world usually just blows...so I can't say I blame you. I still don't see why I shouldn't go...I've handled stuff a lot tougher than a bunch of hippies chained to fences.” She replied. Reno went beet red and turned his back to her. He pretended to shrug it off, waving goodbye nonchalantly. “See you later Amy, I’ll try and get in touch before Slam,” Reno opened the door to the hallway and ZaC leaped into his arms, “ZaC! How ya doin’ buddy?” Reno scratched behind the monkey’s ears. “Bye!” Aimz hollered after Reno. “Yup, love you,” Reno said, immediately realizing his words. He and Amy looked awkwardly at each other before Reno acted tired by rubbing his eyes and smiling it off, “Ugh...Sorry about that, I’m just real tired.” “Yeah...I kinda knew that...” Aimz said, eyes not averting from Reno. He waved goodbye and exited the locker room leaving Amy Campbell alone with her thoughts...
