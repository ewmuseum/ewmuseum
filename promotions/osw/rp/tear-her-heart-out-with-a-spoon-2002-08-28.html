Tear her heart out with a spoon

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/28/02 'I'll mangle her! I'll destroy her! I'll cut her up in little bits and bury her! Then I'll dig them up and jump up and down on them!' An angry Alan Kriegman paces back and forth, hands waving in the air, every furious gesture serving to punctuate his ranting. 'That... little girl destroyed my work! How dare she?! How DARE she?! Doesn't she know who I am? Doesn't she know what I can DO?!' He stops in his tracks and spins, facing the Trashcan Man, who is rocking back and forth in a corner, gazing blankly at the wall. 'Oh, she'll pay. She will pay,' declares the evil supergenius. 'And you, my Trashcan Man, will start the collection process.' Trashy looks up at Kriegman and frowns slightly. 'Master, I bear a vague resemblance to Duke 'The Dumpster' Droese, not the Repo Man.' Kriegmsn's jaw drops as his ward gazes up at him solemnly. 'Repo... Man... never mind. What I mean, Trash, is you have a match with Tempest on Saturday. And in that match, I want you to hurt her, and hurt her badly.' Trashy slowly rises to his feet. 'Yes, my master.' Kriegman clenches his hands in front of him. With malevolent glee, he twists his fists slowly. 'I want you to tear that purple haired Barbie doll's head off, do you hear me, Trash?!' The Trashcan Man's eyes fill with a zealous rapture as he takes a step forward. 'MY LIFE FOR YOU! MY LIFE FOR YOU! MY LIFE FOR YOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!' he screams as he turns and runs out of the room. 'Trash? Where are you going? Trash?'
