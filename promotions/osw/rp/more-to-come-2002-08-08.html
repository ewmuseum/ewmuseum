More to come..

8 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-8-02 Searching for our shelter, we enter immortal tombs. The darkness surrounds me almost in contentment. I would rest if my twisted thoughts didn't keep me from slumber. Tears of blood run down my face I rub the silver cross hanging on my chest, not for any beliefs just thinking of better times. Times the sun didn't hurt my eyes, times I still felt remorse. Innocent blood spilled every night. Swathed in the evening's ebony aura. Why does my thoughts cause me so much pain? I am strong. I am a creature of the night! Pitifully weeping for the wretched bastard who took my humanity. I refuse to sustain my bawling, fear and weakness I will not show. I try in vain for the sleep I need. We will rise again tomorrow at dusk. Not because we must, another myth, it is simply that the moon is so much more erotic and the twilight is so freeing. I rise from my chamber of no rest and retreat to the outside comfort of the night. As I sit on the head of a stone gargoyle I remember times past. Should I have not been so stubborn? Should I have given in to the light and allowed myself the forgiveness of a God? Perhaps..But such fun I would be missing. No doubt I have my regrets but we all do now and then. Soon enough like the wounds on the outside they will return inside. A clove hangs from the corner of my motionless mouth, its embers dropping slowly to the ground. Such a warm muggy night, all enhanced by the light of the full moon dangling above. I stretch slowly like a feline but quickly cease and wince at the sharp pain in my arms, chest, and midsection. What an intense work out Fallihein can pound me with. Soon I will heal, as always..So quickly now. After the transformation I have no problem recovering from the worst of things. Biotechnology does have its place in the scheme of things I suppose. But no longer do I rest my thoughts on the mere thought of my pain. Rather now I embrace that pain and relish in the fact that I shall appear in front of millions comes SLAM! This is something that sends a tingle down to the most intimate regions of my body. Such a time I will have while bathing in the blood of the man I face Saturday. While he lays face down, motionless inside of the ring with his own blood becoming a pool for him to swim in I shall reign supreme. I shall kneel and lap from the crimson I will make flow from his body. And when I am through I would have done my Cult proud. As I intend to do from this day onward. We have much to deal with..Much to plan for..And the throwbacks have yet to see what is in store for them. My mouth curls in to a sadistic little smile at the thought of all the things the Cult will be accomplishing. Such fear..True fear people will know after the throwbacks are obliterated before a live audience. Such fools they are for thinking they can take on people such as Vampir Nosferatu and Sepiroth Du Luc. Dave Gibson and Clay Vendetta will scream for mercy when they feel the true meaning of pain from the members of the Cult. No such mercy..will be shown to them. As the night chill picks up I decide it is time for my return back to my lonely chambers. But what is this? Something has crossed my mind. Something..Is brewing inside of my head..And now something will be coming in play to help out my fellow members. Soon throwbacks..Soon Jeff Garvin you all will feel the wrath that is the Olden Beast. The blood will flow, and the first of it is about to begin. And finally when the end is near the blood will form a river and I..I will be the one to swim in it!
