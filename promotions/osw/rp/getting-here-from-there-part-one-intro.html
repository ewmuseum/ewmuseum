Getting Here From There, Part One: Intro.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 090902 Listen my children and you shall hear, Of the midnight rise of the American Ronin. Hmm…that doesn’t quite have the familiar ring, does it? Alright, scratch the poetry, We’ll just do this straight. First and foremost, introductions. For those poor few of you who sat through that excruciatingly long home stretch of the Most Extreme Team competition, you’re probably rather familiar with my client, Matt Register, and his tag team partner, Jeffrey Wayne. As for the rest of you, a few months of travel with those two has led me to believe that they work best when preceded by a buffer. Kind of like the gel coating on medicine tablets, something to tone down what could become a very caustic reaction. For the next few days, you’ll be hearing primarily from yours truly. The name is Duncan Frye. Any reference to me in the past, primarily through Matt, has placed me as his ‘agent’ for strictly business purposes. I suppose that’s true to a superficial extent, but let’s be honest, how many professional wrestlers really need agents? Matt’s a nice guy and all, but I hardly see him hawking cereal or auto insurance in the near future. And barring a turn as “Guy #2” in some Steven Segall bomb, he won’t be making a trip to Hollywood soon. We, aside from RPG4, but that’s another story. All front-stage BS aside, my duties and responsibilities can be more adequately classified under “babysitter.” I’m an advisor, a contact, a liason, and most importantly, a nanny, to make sure that Matt Register does everything that is required of him from a very powerful set of people. I suppose in this case that the best starting point would be there….at the top. The name they’ve come to be known by is Cronus Enterprises. And despite the rather ominous connotation that such a name harbors, it’s about as well known and oft heard as the last track on a Shaquille O’Neil CD. They don’t have an office in New York. They don’t have a stock-trading symbol. Fact of the matter is that the only people you’ll ever hear throwing around the name Cronus are those who are in their employ. Greek mythology geeks will recall Cronus – or one of its many other permutations – as the father of the Titans, the massive beings who proceeded even the gods. Before any of the legends began, there was Cronus…the ancient “granddaddy of ‘em all.” Which of course, for those of us under the CE umbrella, explains quite a lot. Cronus Enterprises is what I explained to Matt as a “shadow conglomerate.” They discreetly have their hands in quite a bit of the world’s commerce. They are a parent company to parent companies. They pull the strings on some of the most well-known puppeteers. Branches extend in several directions, making them a very rich, very powerful group. At least that’s what I hear. Rumors become more prevalent than news for us these days. After all, we only know two things for sure. 1) They are widespread. 2) They are very secretive. #1 is more of an assumption than a proven fact. But it can’t really be disputed. If you want something, and it benefits them to get it for you, you will have it. That’s sort of how the system works. As for #2, I think if that weren’t obvious, I wouldn’t have to be telling you about it right now. Near as I can tell, I’m at the bottom of a very large food chain. I answer to a contact, who himself answers to another contact, and so forth and so on down the line. One-person levels only. Messages are relayed like a giant game of telephone. But no one dares get the communication wrong; they fear what would happen if they do. You’ll have to excuse me, I’m getting caught up in the whole drama mystique. Let’s get to the point. Despite this extensive web of side projects and ulterior industries, Cronus Enterprises is most well known for a business they undertake themselves. It’s that particular business that leads us to our starting point. Cronus operates in the market of taking down-on-their luck persons, pulling them back to their feet via their network of money and clout, and thus leaving said persons indebted to them for what will ultimately become the rest of their lives. These people, once given their new life lease, go about their lives with the opportunity, doing with it as they please. Cronus doesn’t interfere, they merely support. Every subject of this little venture, referred to on the inside as the Titan Project, is given a marker. A ring, a card, a watch, something with a symbol on it to keep them reminded of the Sword of Damocles hanging over their heads. A black circle inside a white triangle. Simple, nondescript. Nothing to let the common public in on it. Those who are assisted and carry the mark, tagged and released into the wild like deer or sheep, they go about the nearest semblance of “normal” lives that they can manage. But then there comes a time in which the other shoe drops. When that magnanimous favor…needs to be repaid. My father was one such debtor. When I was 8 he found himself in trouble with alcohol. Somehow, Cronus contacted him, and they signed out a deal. But my father was too old and weak to serve the purpose they needed. So he gave them his son. Me. For the past 23 years I’ve been under the Cronus Enterprises wing. They dictated my education, my trade schooling, and my choice of eventual career. There are times when I can look back and wonder what I could’ve done with my life, if anything. Unfortunately, reality slaps me in the face every time. This is the best my life could have ever turned out. A career working with people, all the money and subsequent power I’d ever need….but there’s still the bitter taste that such a situation leaves in your mouth. As a result, I’ve wasted what little free time I do have to learn more about the group through underground channels. Over the course of…I dunno, however long they’ve been operating, Cronus has amassed a HUGE amount of such debtors. People all over the continent, and further than that, all over the world, who owe them some sort of “favor” in return for the assistance given to them. They don’t know what it is, or when they’ll be asked for it. They just know that it’s coming. And they know to be ready for it. Three months ago I was contacted by my immediate superior. Whatever it was that Cronus was setting up for, it was going down. I had been asked to monitor a man, a professional wrestler, and had been doing so for about a year or more. Supposedly, he was the final cog in their complicated machine. Now, it was time to collect him. The Titan Project was about to hit its second phase…and Matt Register was our Prometheus.
