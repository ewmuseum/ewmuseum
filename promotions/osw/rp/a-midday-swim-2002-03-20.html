A Midday Swim

20 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-20-02 Happiness and fun, given to one by certain things that fill our lives with joy, but not too many of us can actually find it. Today is a beautiful day in Orlando, Florida, and we are outside. We find ourselves watching over a large pool area, located on the outside of the hotel were Jesse Ramey is supposedly staying. Inside of the pool are Michelle-Marie Ramey and Chass Loraine. On one of many foldout chairs we see John Buchanan, sitting with sunglasses on. All three wearing a sort of swimming outfit. John wearing a pair of trunks that are black with flames coming from the bottom. Michelle and Chass both wearing two pieces, Chass’ with flowers and very colorful, while Michelle’s is a sort of shiny blue metallic. “Look out below!” Is screamed from up above as we swing around to find Jesse coming off the high-dive, right into the water headfirst with a perfect dive. Michelle and Chass turn to look very quickly as they see Jesse hit and John just shakes his head. Jesse emerges from the water and begins to shout at John, “Hey dork, get off that bench and get in this damn water! We didn’t let you come with us to just be lazy and sit on your ass all the time.” John raises his sunglasses and looks at Jesse. “I’ll have you know that I would rather not get in that water with you losers. I’m fine sitting right here.” John says lowering his sunglasses back down. “Come on, you lazy son of a bitch. You don’t need to lie around and get a suntan. What are you trying to be anyway? Black?” Jesse shouts out. John raises up in a look of frustration, “I’ll have you know that I’m not trying to be black. I’m merely…” John gets cut off by a big splash of water covering him. Jesse begins to laugh hysterically, “See what you get you big dumb wigger!” Jesse continues laughing. As Jesse laughs, John stands up and takes a huge run, “Watch out you little bitch!” Jesse turns in astonishment as John comes crashing down into the water causing a monstrous wave to cover both men. Chass begins to shake her head, “Men. Can’t live them, yet we can’t live without them.” Michelle looks at Chass in shock, “We can’t live without them? You’ve got to be kidding me.” “No, I’m not kidding you. What do you mean?” Chass asks Michelle with a raised eyebrow. Michelle looks at Chass a little weird, “Come on you can’t be telling me that you’ve never thought about another women in that way?” “No, I most certain have not!” Chass exclaims. Michelle shrugs her shoulders. “Oh well, your lose not mine. I would make it off just fine if there weren’t any men alive on this earth. Chass looks on in a sort of disgust as Michelle swims off to the other side of the pool, “Jesse come here, I want to speak with you.” Jesse swims over to Chass, “Sure thing sweetie, what were you needing?” “Did you by any chance that your daughter was and is a bisexual?” Chass asks Jesse. Jesse looks at Chass in a funny look, “Oh yeah, didn’t you know?” “No, I had no idea. You really need to tell me about these things.” Chass tells Jesse. “Why? Do you find something wrong with it?” Jesse questions Chass with a weird look. “No, I don’t find anything wrong with it. I just wish someone had informed me about it before now. You do know that she and I have been sharing a bed for a while now. It’s a little strange now.” Chass says. “Don’t worry about anything. She knows that you are my girlfriend and I know she isn’t going to try anything on you. Trust me, you have more things to worry about than Michelle.” Jesse says. Chass begins to relax, “Yeah, I guess you are right. I really don’t have anything to worry about with Michelle. Thanks for the reassurance, baby.” A look of weirdness crosses Jesse’s face, “Only real thing we have to watch out is that Michelle stays away from Steven Smith. I don’t mind her being bi, but he would cause her to turn completely on us.” Both Jesse and Chass have a look of suspense come across their face, “Sick.” They mutually utter simultaneously and then begin to chuckle. Down on the other end of the pool we can see John floating on his back, but up above we see Michelle getting ready to leap out on top of him from the low dive. Michelle begins take a run and go, but “Stop right there!” Come the words from John’s mouth stopping Michelle dead in her tracks. Michelle stops abruptly and sits down on the end of the diving board. “How in the world could you have seen me coming?” “It’s a little trick that a person must become skilled at when you’re in the business.” John says still floating on his back, while still wearing the sunglasses that he had on earlier. Michelle begins to pout, “Does dad know that little trick by any chance?” “Everyone in the business knows that little trick. It’s just something that comes instinctively. Unless you are a communist pig, like Ivan Stanislav, Tom Walczak, and Michel Kreig.” John begins to snicker. “What is it with you guys always making fun of people like Ivan, Tom, and Michel. I actually think Michel is a king of sexy.” Michelle says. John leans up and looks across the pool where Jesse and Chass are playing around in the pool, “Hey, Jesse! You’re daughter thinks that Michel Kreig is sexy!” Jesse begins to make his way across the pool and when he does he looks Michelle in the eyes, “You think that communist bastard is sexy?” In a little bit of a hesitant tone, “Yes, dad… I do think that he is sexy. That isn’t a bad thing is it?” Jesse smiles, as Michelle lowers her head, “Absolutely not. It’s your life and I’m not going to sink so low enough that I would do something like make you not think someone was good looking. That’s your decision.” Michelle smiles and hugs her dad, “Thanks dad.” John begins to look at Jesse a little dumbfounded, “Jesse, man! You’re not going to let her continue to think that way are you?” “Yes, John. I am.” Jesse turns around and looks John in the eyes, “What’s wrong with her thinking that way? I’m only her dad. I can’t tell her what to think and what not to think.” John has a looks at Jesse a little sympathetically, “Dude, don’t you think that kindly gives him an advantage?” Jesse looks at John a little dumbfound now, “Now, John, how in the world would that possible give Kreig an advantage?” “Think about it man.” John pulls Jesse over to the other side of the pool away from Michelle. “Dude, think about it. If Kreig caught wind of this situation, then he could easily take advantage of the situation.” Jesse still a little confused continues, “But how man? I’m not totally understanding any of what you are saying.” “Think about it man. Kreig, Tom, and Ivan neither like you because you’re from America and they would most likely do anything to either get under your skin or make you hurt. Kreig could take advantage of this situation and have Michelle come with him. Turning her on you, dude.” John says. Jesse’s eyes light up, “Wow, I never thought about it that way man. Honestly, that thought never crossed my mind.” Jesse looks up and over at Michelle, “I would never be able to live with myself if something like that was to every happen to Michelle.” “Well, go do something about it man. This is your only chance to keep her safe and out of harms way.” John says. Jesse begins to walk over to Michelle, “Listen here little missy. You can think however you want to about Michel Kreig, but you had better not do anything with him. You hear me?” John smacks his hand into his forehead as Michelle smiles at her dad, “Sure thing dad.” On the other end of the pool we can hear Chass laughing hysterically at what is going on as she begins to talk to herself, “God, Jesse will never really do anything to hurt that girls feelings and John just overreacts way too much.” She climbs out of the pool and makes her way down to Michelle and Jesse. “Hey, John, lighten up. Jesse knows what he is doing with his daughter. He’s never done anything wrong thus far.” John begins to smile a bit, “Yeah, I guess you are right. From what I’ve seen he has done a pretty good job raising her.” “What are you all talking about? I didn’t raise her. Those nuns at that dang orphanage did. I’ve only had Michelle since she was sixteen for crying out loud.” Jesse says. Michelle begins to laugh, “Don’t worry about it dad. Seems like I’ve been with you forever.” They all begin to laugh as John butts in, “Don’t you think it’s time to go workout people? Jesse needs to get ready for this match and it’s never too early to start training.” “John makes a good point, girls. I think I will throw in the towel for today. Now that I’ve been looking over things, I really could use that little bit of an extra boost for this match. Me and John were talking last night and he pointed out some weakness and disadvantages I would have going into it, so we have some work to do.” Jesse says. “We totally understand dad. Maybe Chass and I could go out for a little while today and maybe do some shopping. What do you say Chass?” Michelle asks. Shaking her head confidently. “Sounds good to me.” “Well, we had better be getting out of here.” Jesse says as he kisses Chass, a passionate kiss. “Bye girls.” Jesse reaches over and hugs Michelle. John and Jesse walk into the hotel as Michelle and Chass stand up and look at each other. “It must be a man thing.” Chass says giggling. “Sure must be, now let’s get to using dad’s credit card.” Michelle laughs as the two walk into the hotel and everything goes black. ~The End~
