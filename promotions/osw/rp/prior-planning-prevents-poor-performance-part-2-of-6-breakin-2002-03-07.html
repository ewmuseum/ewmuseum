Prior Planning Prevents Poor Performance(Part 2 of 6'Breaking Benefits')

7 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 7th It's said that revenge is what drives the world 'round. Once you dissect all of the 'adult' matters, everything boils down to a simple game of tag. When that bastard comes up from behind and tags or hits you with something unexpected, defiance disobediance..or whatever it may be. Your initial thought, though many claim to be above it, is revenge. Getting their asses back, chasing THAT person down until you tag them back or do something even worse to them. That's what all the backstage politics, the political scene, and the atheletic fields endorse. That's what we as people thrive on. Revenge. But as for Drew Hostyl'e former gang, the Benefit. They didn't just get mad or want revenge. They depended on it. For if Drew were as dense a man to not expect revenge for leavign the gang, so cocky and arrogantly then the Benefit would not have succeeded in their purpose. And they could already see their results of Drew's nervousness. But what would they have planned themselves to back it up? They didn't just want or get revenge. They needed it. Afterall, the Benefit wasn't about just getting mad..it was about getting even. The scene opens again to the same scene in which we ended last. The camera that we saw OSW interviewer Matt Shivan drop still in the puddle of yellow piss that he..produced while filming the secene as Drew Hostyle'd told him to. The camera now, however has a cracked lens but still visiably shows Drew Hostyle, in his OSW Slam! T-Shirt and jeans on the ground. Still. Helpless and vulnerable. But with no ocular damage though, no damage the face. Why? Because that was what ex lovers and family members went for first. But internal injuries..they came from people who wanted you to suffer. The very same internal injury that Linkin Park talks about in their songs. Thatpain that started mentally and penetrated your whole system until it ate you up physically. So Drew's Big Brotha Benefactor Jamal and the Supreme Benefactor just eyed Drew in his helpless state for the time being. And everytime his body jerked upwards; they laughed. Once the evil laughter reached it's climax Drew had already staggered to his feet and slowly began to walk away from the other two men. Jamal, the darkest of all three black men present, was wearing a blacksuit that looked as if it blended in with his skin. This same man then acted as if he were the man hurt and limped after Drew with that same sadistic smile still on his face. The Supreme Benefactor, however grabbed Jamal by the collar and dragged him back to where he, himself was standing. The smile on Jamal's face then disintergretated to a more humble and embarrassed expression as he put his head down to the ground and waited for the man beside him in all the fancy ensemble to address him. but inside of the consoling he expected, the man keeping dragging him, back futher and further, unitl he was out of the camera's view and they'd both entered the little gray door they'd come out of at beginning of the last scene. 'Don't be stupid..Jamal' The Supreme Benefactor scolded him as they had already entered what seemed shabby from the outside but was really more hearty and of a home then you'd have thought prior to this point. 'I wasn't..but the way he disrespected you..' Jamal began. 'That wasn't about me..that was about you, that was about your own damn self. Nobody else has had a bigger challenge as a little brotha than you have.' 'True..so why'd you assign him to me then?' Jamal asked puzzled. 'Because I damn well can, now don't you go getting smart either. You're the best man for the job and had done a good job to this point.' The Supreme Benefactor replies. 'Well thanks, this is how I'm rewarded then?' Jamal asks. 'I said you've done a good job..to this point. However you've failed on a couple of opportunities.' The Supreme Benefactor answers. 'As in?' 'As in the trial. The trial that Drew was supposed to be apart of.' The Supreme Benefactor replies. '...' 'Speak up Brotha, what do you have to say for yourself?' The Supreme Benefactor asks, rasing his voice a little. 'He was still my lil' Brotha, so I did what I should.' 'And that wasn't keeping the witness alive and sending Drew's ass to jail for good?' 'No..it wasn't.' 'Explain.' 'Why have the authorities full of crackas and weaklings, do what we can't? If anyone puts that bastard away for good, it's gonna be me...er us.' 'Good reason, but that's not how it happened is it?' 'No..see there was only one witness left, the only person who had a chance of being a plantiff, the woman's boyfriend.' 'No shit..it was over all the news' 'True..but I didn't exactly know which cords that Barker guy would need..' 'So you pulled all of them?' 'Indeed.' 'Good work. But the next time you go about getting bright ideas don't leave finger prints on the evidence!' 'Oh you mean these old things?' Jamal then takes out a pair of gloves with bumps along the finger compartments. The finger prints are identitical to the ones found as the last person to touch Ryan Barker after his death we can assume. 'So that's one thing taken care of, the onyl people after him are us.' 'Apparently not..' 'What's all this knowledge you've suddenly grown on Drew's ignorant ass?' 'I've been anticipating this day.' 'As have I.' 'Well then you must known the flaw of your statement..there's another man just as mad at Drew Hostyle as both you and me.' 'Don't question me..finish your own thoughts.' 'A cracka named Rhett Locke.' 'I'm not familiar with the name.' 'Rhett Locke. He belongs to the same professional wrestling federation that Drew does. He's grown into hating Drew even more dare I say, then us?' 'What's infuriated him so much about Drew?' 'There's something at stake, that's all.' 'Something?' 'The OSW's International Title.' 'OSW..International Title..Rhett Locke. Either you research impeccably well or you're a fan of the staged 'wrestling' yourself.' 'A little of both.' Jamal acqueises. 'Meh..I figured.' The Supreme Benefactor said. 'So this..Rhett Locke figure. He'd be willing to..' 'Of course, why would he do otherwise?' Jamal started. 'Have you seen the man lately? Doesn't look like he says the word 'no' much.' Jamal says, in a worried tone of voice. 'Heh, you'd better be right.' The Supreme Benefactor says. 'When haven't I?' Jamal questions. 'My fingers couldn't hold the number' The Supreme Benefactor answers. 'Well..how about we drop that issue and go to find this kid.' Jamal says, changning the subject. 'And where might he be?' The Supreme Benefactor asks. 'Church I imagine.' Jamal says. 'Ugh..when it rains, it pours, doesn't it?' The Supreme Benefactor says, unhappy with visiting a sanctuary outside of his own. 'No exactly. There's nothing wrong with a little faith..' Jamal smirks. 'The only faith you need is into your Supreme Brotha, boy. You should know that by now.' 'What's the matter? Can't take a little joke?' Jamal asks, poking fun at his direct superior. 'My sense of humor is none existent today.' The Supreme Benefactor declares, his tone become less and less friendly. 'So is your intelligence..' Jamal sighs under his breath. 'Just like I imagine Drew.' The Supreme Benefactor says determined, and not hearing Jamal's comment. 'Agreed.' Jamal states, teething as the scene fades to black. OOC: Sorry so short and to the point, but it looks like I'm doing this whole thing solo so I figured I'd make them shorter. His parts weren't to clear to me anyways..I'll settle with what I've written though.
