The search for Troy's weak spot

10 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 10th 2002 **The scene opens today in Ace Hawk's apartment, back in Los Angeles, California. It's a beautiful day, and the scene shows the interior of Ace Hawk's house. The house is comletely empty, as one can see. The camera starts moving around the house, like if it was flying. It goes through the vliving room, with its curtains closed, and through the corridor, passing the TV room and the ball room at the end, going upstairs and finally entering the first room to the right. There, you can see the closet door opened and from it, walks Danny Jackson, Ace's friend. We can see the secret passage behind him, and then suddenly the camera flies down the concrete tunnel, into Ace's secret war room. For those of you who haven't been viewing Ace's promos, this happened during PTC's GTT. Danny and Johnny found a secret door on the closet, which led to a tunnel, and at the bottom of the tunnel, another door led to an incredible war room, where we can see, to the left, a roll of 5 chairs, in front of several big screen TVs. The center chair has a big steel table in front of it, with a modern PC computer over it. Ace sits in the chair and turns the computer on. It begins to initialize, and in the meanwhile, Ace walks to the other side of the room, where several modern gym equipments can be seen. You have a Bowflex, a full-gym apparatus and a wrestling ring, with a video-camera installed in front of it, for computer analyzis. Sitting in front of the steel table with the computer, Ace keeps tapping, looking extremely annoyed. Ace is wearing his ring gear and ray-ban shades, but you can see from his forehead that, whatever he's doing, he's not finding. Johnny, Ace's bodyguard, grabs a chair and sits besides Ace. Johnny finally breaks the silence and asks:** 'Hey, Ace, what are you looking for??', asked Johnny, leaning towards the computer screen and squeezing his eyes. 'I'm looking at Doug's career?', answers Ace, without taking his eyes from the computer. 'Who?' 'Doug. Doug Troy, the man I'll have to face this Saturday for the #1 contendership for the OSW World Heavyweight Title.', answers Ace, dead serious, the mere mention of Doug's name burning his soul. 'And what are you trying to find?' 'It's simple. You see, Doug Troy came up with this little skit on his promo, saying the OSW Title was dominated by mediocrity or something...' 'Ah, I heard it. Totally out of line.', answers Johnny, getting mad.' 'Not at all. He was perfectly on his right to make an ass out of himself.', prompting a majestic laugh coming from the 7'5' tall monster, Johnny. 'So, why are you looking at his career?' 'Because I know, for a fact, that Doug Troy isn't the winner he says.', says Ace, smiling. **Johnny looks puzzled. He asks:** 'What do you mean?' 'Well, he may be a Hall of Famer, but I know for a fact that he lost a few stupid matches on his way to the top.', answers Ace, finally finding something on the screen. 'You mean...' 'Yeah, like me and Ghettodogg. I know for a fact that Doug jobbed to this guy, but I don't know his name... AHA, here it is.' 'Where?', asks Johnny, curious, looking at the computer screen. 'Here. The name of Doug Troy's achilles tendon...', Ace says, pointing to a spot on the screen. The camera zooms in, and we can see three phrases, from a transcript from an old PCW show. The three phrases are simple: DOUG TROY JOBBED TO HANK!! HANK!! HANK, I SAY!! **Johnny and Ace look at it, clueless. Johnny says:** 'This must have been a funny sight.', he says, laughing. 'Yeah, but who's Hank? Where is him? I gotta meet this phenomenal wrestler.', Ace says, before laughing. 'Yeah, but this could be hard. What will you do, Ace?' 'I'll do what no man has ever done. I'll go where no one has ever dared to go. I'll FIND HIM! HANK, THE LOST JOBBER!!!!', Ace says, getting up and pointing to the sky. 'Yeah, LET'S GO', says Jack, also pointing to the sky. 'Hey guys, I brought the pizzas', says an incoming Danny Jackson, with three boxes of pizzas and a 6-pack of Bud on his hands. 'Ok, we'll go after the pizzas. The pepperoni one is mine.', says Ace, flying on Danny, as the scene fades to black. Tomorrow: Raiders of the lost jobber
