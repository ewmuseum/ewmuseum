A dangerous storm....

16 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-16-02 So many questions…so few explanations. The reception that Ivan Stanislav received when he returned to Russia from his escapades in America were slightly different than the previous time he had returned. The civilians that greeted him had wider smiles on their faces and seemed to be even more accepting of Ivan Stanislav. The soldiers and politicians were slightly colder than normal. Granted, a boisterous Russian soldier is a rarity in the first place, but various officials would not answer his greeting when he returned, while others would respond with the most limited of sentences. It’s not that Ivan really cared. The opinions of his peers did mean a lot to him, but he had other things to worry about. The situation within the OSW was mounting as he had come off of a decisive victory against Jane Reagan. His Red Army was more powerful than ever. Tempest and himself were quickly becoming closer. Yes, things for Ivan Sergeiovich Stanislav, on all fronts, were looking on the up and up… …save for perhaps the front that he had always received the most support: The Russian Military. It puzzled Ivan when he heard that the Russian Military was not completely behind him in his actions regarding Tempest. So many people had wished to pry into the personal life of Ivan Stanislav only to find that in reality, he had a very limited one. Now, they see Ivan’s personal life and they berate him for it?! Ivan sat at his desk in Arkhangelsk and pondered these questions in silence. It was dark outside as a slight drizzle pattered against the windows over his shoulders. Save for the dull light from the lamp on his desk, Ivan Stanislav did not utilize any other form of illumination. He brought his hands up to his mouth and rested his massive head upon them as he continued to pick apart the various accusations that he heard through the whispering of grapevines. Something about ‘losing their hold on him’ and ‘not liking the independent way of thinking.’ That did not bode well with The Russian Bear. He was doing his job, was he not? Bringing focus to Russian, bringing money in to the people, as well as systematically destroying the upper echelon of the federation. What more could they want? He rose from his chair and turned to the window. He walked slowly towards it and muttered to himself, “What is it that they want?” Alexei Ruslan’s voice suddenly pierced the room with it’s high pitch and light timbre, “A return to normalcy, Praporschik.” Ivan wheeled around suddenly, caught completely by surprise to Alexei’s presence. But sure enough, the diminutive form of Alexei Ruslan leaned in the doorway, a cigarette hanging from his lips. “They want you to return to the way that you were, Ivan. They do not like this..new outlook.” Ivan squinted as Alexei walked into the room and replied to Stanislav, “And I do not know what side I should take.” “Alexei,” replied Ivan, “when did you get here?” Alexei put his hands in his trench coat pockets and answered, “A few minutes ago, I was trying to see if you would notice me, but you appeared to be….engaged in something far more important….” A bolt of lightning shot through the horizon beyond the window, casting a jagged shadow of Ivan Stanislav across the hardwood floor, “I was thinking about a number of things, Alexei.” Ivan paused and tilted his head to the side, “And what do you mean, ‘return to normalcy?’” Alexei rocked back on his heels and took a puff from his cigarette. For the two being such good friends, there was a tenseness between the two that had never been felt before, “Regarding your recent actions with Tempest. The book….” Ivan growled, “What is it their business what I do with that book?” “I do not know, Ivan,” answered Ruslan, “but Tomifei…” “TOMIFEI IS AN OLD FOOL!” Ivan roared. Alexei gasped and ran over to him, “Praporschik! Silence yourself! He may hear you! He has ears in places that even I do not know about!” Alexei looked around frantically, “Calm yourself.” Ivan grumbled to himself, “I do not understand what all this trouble is, Alexei. So I like the girl!” He turned and walked to the window and continued softly, “So I appreciate what she means to me….” Alexei rubbed the back of his neck, “Yes, well, are you sure she is true, Ivan?” Ivan looked over his shoulder, “At least as true as we have been to her….” Alexei nodded and kicked the ground, “Yes, I suppose you are correct.” Alexei paused for a long period of time and looked up at Ivan, “I…Ivan?” Ivan turned around slowly. The wood paneling beneath his heavy feet creaked harshly in the quiet room, “What…Alexei?” Alexei’s face flashed with a look of almost pain, “I..I think I agree with them.” Ivan blinked, “WHAT?” Alexei took a step backward, “Ivan, I have been wanting to talk to you about it. I am worried about what could come from all of this! We are dealing with something far more….destructive than any attack.” Ivan put his hands on his hips and tapped his foot on the floor, “That may be true, but she is good ally, she is good friend, and she is meaning more and more to me day by day…” “Which,” interjected Ruslan, “means she can cause more damage that way if something were to happen.” Alexei walked over to Ivan and looked up at him, “Ivan, what you must understand is that since you’ve joined PTC federations, you have been unstoppable! The enemies have been unable to gain an advantage over you because physically you cannot be beaten, and mentally they have no leverage,” he paused, “until now.” Ivan turned his back to his comrade, “You doubt my ability, Alexei? You doubt my mental fortitude?” “Ivan, nyet, that is not what I mean, but I am advisor to you! I am giving you advice!” Ruslan barked back at Ivan, “Break it off with Tempest! You are losing face in Motherland!” “Get out of here, Alexei. I will not listen to you any further tonight.” “Praporschik….” “Get out, Ruslan.” Alexei nodded and took a drag on his cigarette again, “Alright, Ivan, I will do what you say. But you think of what I have said, you know what I say is true. If I, your closest friend can make you angry…what could others do if they tried?” Alexei stopped at the door, “There is a storm coming, Ivan…prepare yourself, it is said to be very destructive….” Alexei turned on his heels and walked out the door. Ivan walked to the window and started out at the storm that brewed, “Yes, a storm is coming….but I will survive it….I know I will.” He shook his head and closed his eyes. So many questions….so few explanations.
