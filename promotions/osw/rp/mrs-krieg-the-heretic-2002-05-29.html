Mrs Krieg: The Heretic?

29 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29.05.02 An old fashioned phone chirps away as Berneille Krieg shuffles out of the living room and into the hall, making a bee-line for a long wooden table, where a black 1940's phone, some pieces of paper, a letter or two and a pen sit peacefully, 'Hello,' crackled Mrs. Krieg, screaming down the receiver, 'This is Hessian, of the OSW,' said a dark, mysterious voice on the other end, '...Do you want to speak to Michel, Hershey,' asked Bernielle, not aware she'd called Hessian the wrong name, 'It is Hessian, and it is you I wish to speak to,' replied Hessian, 'Ah, and what do you want from me, Mr. Hersain,' 'I've noticed you, as a Krieg, are not the snappiest of dressers...shall we say...' Hessian growls down the phone, while Mrs. Krieg listens on. In the background she can hear the faint squealing of the one, the only, the Imp. 'Hey...is she hot? Is she purdy?' 'Who is that speaking? Is that your little monkey,' asks Mrs. Krieg, 'That is Reno Davis you are thinking of...No, that is my loyal comrade Minion,' replies Hessian, his voice as harsh and heavy as ever, 'You can beat me like a monkey if you like,' squealed Minion in the background, 'So what do you want with me, Hersian,' 'Like I said, you aren't the snappiest of dressers...and before Michelle condemns you to an old biddy wearing tablecloths, is it possible to show you true fashion, maybe even have you try something out?' Hessian says, trying to hide the sarcasm in his voice, while at the same time being serious. 'Would she fu-...' Minion is cut off as a loud SMACK echoes down the line. 'If you are asking me out of a date Ameri-kun, then I mu-' 'This is not a date, Mrs. Kreig. This is an opportunity to dress like a champion and be recognised as a snappy dresser, unlike your son, who is constantly being monitored by the fashion police,' butted in Hessian, 'That sounds like a good idea,' smiled Bernielle, 'Where shall I meet you and your little friend,' 'The shopping mall near the harbour is the best place in your area for the kind of attire we need,' replied Hessian, 'I shall be there in half an hour. I just need to get Michel to drive me there,' 'Very well...we shall be waiting...' Hessian said, hanging up on a slightly bewildered Berneille. With that, Bernielle hangs up the phone and looks around for a minute, trying to think what to do. After a few minutes of thinking, she stands at the bottom of the stairs and yells up stairs, 'MICHEL! COME DOWN NOW,' A couple of thuds on the roof above follow and Michel Krieg appears at the top of the staircase, before lumbering down the stairs, each wooden step creaking loudly as he slams his massive feet on them, 'What is it mother,' asks a tired looking Krieg, 'I need you to take me to mall at harbour for shopping,' replies Bernielle, 'Why? We've just been out shopping,' 'Do not argue with your mother! I want to go to the mall and you will take me,' screams Bernielle, slapping Krieg across the chest, 'Ok, ok,' The scene fades out to dark grey then to black. After a second or two's delay, the screen fades back in again as the same old fashioned car seen earlier pulls up outside a massive shopping mall, both Krieg's sitting in the front seats. Krieg turns to his mother as she goes to get out of the car, 'Mother, do not let Ameri-kuns harass you! If they do, call Michel and he will beat them senseless,' said Michel, slamming one hand into the other's palm, 'There will be no need for that,' replied Bernielle, 'I can handle Ameri-kuns by myself,' Bernielle steps out of the car, slams the door shut and it pulls away from the curb slowly at first before roaring away, speeding towards a children's crossing, which Michel completely ignores and shoots right past it. As Bernielle walks towards the main doors, two shadows grow larger as two figures step out from behind a pillar into the faint light of the entrance... Hessian and Minion... “We have been expecting you,” said Hessian, stepping out of the shadows, draped in a long dark cloak, closely followed by Minion, who is dressed in similar attire, ”I am here, let us proceed,” replied Bernielle, not wasting any time with introductions, 'Mrs. Krieg....I can only say it is an experience meeting the mother of an imbecile...' Hessian says soothingly, extending a gauntleted hand to Mrs. Krieg. She accepts and nods, then looks towards the purple thing that is Minion. 'Und who is this?' asks Bernielle, looking quite surprised. Minion winks and strolls forward. 'Hey baby, you should be proud because you're one of the few privileged women that get to lay eyes up close on 79lbs of Apocalyptic, Powerful Impbeef. My name is Minion, and it is a privilege to say the least to meet a divine creature such as yourself. One question though; do you swa-...' Minion is again cut off as a swift backhand from Hessian catches him on the cheek, “Wha the…,” squealed Minion, looking up at Hessian with a questionable look, “Bernielle, let’s process,” announced Hessian, motioning his hand towards the automatic sliding doors of the mall entrance. Mrs. Krieg flashed Hessian a quick smile before shuffling towards the doors, Hessian standing at her side and Minion trailing a metre or so behind them, “So,” said Mrs. Krieg, breaking the ominous silence as they walked through the main doors, “What exactly do you do in the OSW? Are you cleaner? Technician, maybe,” Hessian sighs and answers Bernielle, “I am a wrestler, Mrs. Krieg, and I have beaten your son many times,” “Not as many as he’s beaten you I’m sure,” snapped Mrs. Krieg, grinning up at Hessian, Slowly the pair walked past various shops, making their way further into what appeared to be a large mall. Passing a couple of fancy boutiques, a few sports stores, a confectionery stand and a small hairdressers, they finally reach their destination, a dark and gloomy shop called “Hellfire” , “This is the place,” said Hessian, once again motioning for Mrs. Krieg to lead the way into the shop, Mrs. Krieg fires right back at Hessian, “Age before beauty, Ameri-kun,” she said with a smirk, “I’m sure you don’t have latter, so I’ll let you lead the way,” Shaking her head, Mrs. Krieg steps over the threshold of the shop, as beads dangle from just inside the doorway and reasonably loud rock music plays from a stereo in the back of the store. Bernielle looks around with a puzzled look on her face, taking in such sites as voodoo dolls, stuffed goats heads and large crucifixes, dripping with artificial blood. Turning to Hessian, she asks the Goliath a couple of questions about the shop, “What does this place actually sell, Hersian? Clothes or artefacts from Ozzy Osbourne’s rock career,” “It sells clothing to people who don’t conform to the norm of fashion, so to speak,” replied Hessian, looking quite proud of himself as the smile of his face might suggest, “You mean freaks,” snapped Bernielle, “I can be your freak, lady,” squeaked Minion from behind the massive Hessian, “I told you to SHUT UP,” shouted Hessian, laying a massive backhand on the top of Minion’s skull, rattling the bizarre creature’s brains, “Ok. I will give your ‘fashion’ a try and see if it might work for Bernielle Krieg, dah,” “That is reasonable enough, Mrs. Krieg,” answered Hessian, Clicking his fingers at the assistant behind the counter, Hessian tells the young-looking shop assistant that he wants to have Mrs. Krieg fitted out in complete Gothic attire. Nodding, the boy shuffles away, dragging his feet as he goes and walks into some sort of stockroom at the back of the shop, disappearing for about five minutes, before re-emerging with a pile of clothes, handing them to Mrs. Krieg, “The changing rooms are like, at the back of the shop. You can like, go try this stuff on and hopefully it’ll fit,” said the assistant, handing Mrs. Krieg the clothes, Taking the pile of clothing, Mrs. Krieg walks towards the back of the shop, and looks for a second or two before walking through a curtain into a dressing room. Minutes pass as shuffling around can be heard from inside the changing room. After about 5 minutes, the curtain draws back and there stands Mrs. Krieg, clad in leather pants with fire raging up the sides, metal-plated leather boots, the heels of which are a good 3 or 4 inches, a fishnet top, leather spiked wrist bands, black leather gloves, and a dog collar. As she turns to look at herself in the mirror, her expression can only be described as surprised, “It looks...different,” she said as Hessian and Minion came up and stood behind her, “It does look different, doesn’t it,” commented Hessian, “I don’t care! I can still be your bit-“ SMACK! Minion’s body skitters back towards the door after a stiff slap from Hessian, sending him skidding across the floor, “I was only going to sa-“ “Leave it Minion...Mrs. Krieg, what do you think of your new look,” said a semi-grinning Hessian, “Besides the fact that I look like lovechild of Alice Cooper and Gary Glitter, with a sprinkling of Ozzy Osbourne, and could pass as a robotic monkey at a fancy dress party, it’s very nice,” ”Ahh...,” replied Hessian, frowning a little, “If I was looking to achieve the ‘Social Outcast With A Bladder Problem’ look, this would be fine,” 'Would you like to try on something different maybe?' Hessian asked, trying to maintain a straight face. 'Hey, you know Mrs. Krieg, I’d like to wear YOU on my Impbe-...' Minion is cut off as yet again, Hessian backhands him, this time sending the Imp sliding across the floor and into one of the changing rooms. Minion shakes his dazed head and looks up, his eyes lighting up suddenly with delight. Hessian and Mrs. Krieg cover their ears as an earth shattering shriek echoes from the changing room, and a semi naked girl runs out, with Minion chasing after her. 'What else could there be to try?' Mrs. Krieg asked, looking over her attire. 'This is more than just a shop full of black leather...' Hessian said looking over Mrs. Krieg. 'Really? Oh, I could always try on one of the stag's heads...Might make a nice piece of headgear,' said a sarcastic Mrs. Krieg, 'Well, it might make an improvement...' said Hessian under his breath, 'Say something, Mr. Hersian,' asked Berneille, looking suspiciously at Hessian, 'I was just saying it might be...emm....difficult to remove,' replied Hessian, gracefully covering his tracks, Suddenly, from behind a rack of T-shirts, Minion zooms through and stops before Mrs. Krieg, holding a bundle of clothes in his arms. 'Hey, why don't you try THESE on' Minion says, handing the clothes to Mrs. Krieg, who shakes her head and again heads into the changing room. Bernielle slams the door and more shuffling around can be heard, with a few bangs on the sides of the changing room as Mrs. Krieg tries to fit into some kind of outfit, 'What is she doing in there,' said Minion to Hessian, looking up at the giant, 'I don't have a clue...Heh, what exactly did you give her to try on,' 'You'll need to wait and see,' said a chuckling Minion, A few minutes later, the door of the changing room slowly opens as Mrs. Krieg steps out, fully clad in....Bondage Gear? Minion begins groaning delightfully as Mrs. Krieg shuffles around in the rather constricting gear. Straps and buckles and rubber dangle everywhere, as Mrs. Krieg takes a look in the mirror. 'Such beauty...' Minion says, dumbfounded. 'OH don't be cruel, Minion!' Hessian whispers, letting out a small chuckle. Mrs. Krieg turns to Hessian and Minion, looking a little confused, 'What is this meant to be? The previous outfit looked suitable for someone with a bladder problem, but I reckon this one takes the 'high school drop-out' title,' screamed Bernielle, storming back into the changing room, 'I'm taking this off now, and I am not trying on any more outfits suitable for freaks,' 'She's taking it off! She's taking it off,' squealed Minion, trying to jump high enough to peep over the wooden door of the changing room, his height not allowing, After a few minutes, Mrs. Krieg emerges from changing room, dressed in her original attire, holding a large pile of clothes, which she drops on top of Minion, almost drowning the purple monster, 'Hmph...mmfph....mmf....phhh' Minion mumbles under the pile of clothes, to which Hessian replies with a rather wsift kick to what is estimated as Minion's little Imp bottom. 'Ah well Mrs. Krieg, sorry you didn't see anything you liked, but I'll bet it was an experience...' Hessian said, smiling evilly behind his gauntleted hand, Mrs. Krieg heads for the door, calling back to the two men, 'I can assure you, the next time Michel meets either of you, I will make sure to tell him to beat the living daylights out of you for this fashion insult,' Hessian cups his hands and places them around his mouth, calling after Bernielle, 'I don't think there's anything we could have done with your current dress sense anyway! I think the fashion police should be your next port of call,' With that, the screen splits into two, one half showing Minion and Hessian in the shop, chuckling away, and the other showing Bernielle Krieg storms past shoppers on her way to the exit. Bernielle whips out a large mobile phone and holds it to her ear before speaking loudly into the reciever, 'MICHEL! COME PICK ME UP,' screamed Bernielle down the phone, 'I WILL MEET YOU AT FRONT OF MALL,' Fade...
