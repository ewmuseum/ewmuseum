The 'Little Storm' tries to heal a Bears wound. (Joint Ivan/Tempest rp)

25 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-25-02 “RRRRRAAAAARRRRGGGG!!!!!” The voice of Ivan Stanislav reverberates through the hallway outside of a locker room in the Baltimore Arena, shortly after Saturday Slam went off the airwaves. “WHAT DID SHE THINK SHE WAS DOING?????” A loud CLANG rocks through the area again as the camera moves closer to the closed door. The door opens as the cameraman heads into the locker room to view the situation within the room: Ivan Stanislav stands with his back to the camera wearing his black pair of pants with dual vertical stripes, black boots, and his red suspenders. The suspenders are not slung over his shoulders, however, but rather hang at his side beside his legs. Stanislav’s shirt is off for perhaps the second time in PTC history, revealing the physique of the man underneath his cold, dark, and black shirt. The overall build of Stanislav is simply massive and we can tell that the majority of his four hundred pound frame is distributed in his upper body, not that that was much of a question before. While perhaps not ‘ripped’ like some wrestlers such as Meanstreak, Stanislav is a solid mass of muscle and force compacted into one enormous physique. On his back on his right shoulder, a nasty circular scar is visible, about the size of a half dollar. The skin around the scar is irregular and twisted, obviously not healing in the best possible fashion. Ivan’s back is to us, his huge hands on his hips as he stares at a locker. Through his dark black hair, we can see traces of blood running through his hair and dripping onto his back. Along all sides of the room, steel lockers line the room, but to the far right in particular, an entire bench has been torn from the floor and thrown into an entire row of lockers, collapsing them in a heap of twisted metal and wood. By the door, Alexei Ruslan and Communist Party Member Wang stand. Alexei wears his brown trench coat, hat, brown pants, and shoes, while Wang wears his military uniform. The two stand with their arms crossed over their chests and a look of concern on their faces as they watch Ivan rant and rave. Ivan turns and kicks another bench which is anchored securely to the floor via thick, strong bolts. From the front, we can see Ivan’s huge barrel chest, but most notably we can see a large tattoo over the heart of The Russian Bear. The tattoo is in the form of a large hammer and sickle, roughly in the same area as where the insignia would appear on the shirts that Stanislav wrestles in. Ivan’s huge shoulders rise and fall as he bellows loudly. “HOW CAN THAT HAPPEN?! HOW HOW HOW!!!” FWOOM! Ivan spins and drives his enormous fist into the front of a locker, buckling it over like it was made of paper. Ruslan and Wang both jump reflexively at the outburst and look to one another, not really sure what exactly to do. Ivan turns and jabs his finger into his open palm repeatedly. “I had match won! No one was going to be able to eliminate me! No one would have stopped The Red Army from winning! And then SHE interfered and threw my attention off of match!” Alexei shrugs and puts his hands in his pockets. “Yeah Ivan, but why should she have gotten your attention?” Ivan pauses for a moment, taken aback by the comment. He shakes his head for a moment, thinking quickly, and then comes up with a good answer..or excuse? “That is quite simple! She attacked you!! I had to do something to protect you!” Wang nods stupidly and grins wide while Alexei takes his hat off of his head and runs his hand through his black hair. “Ivan, that DDT was not meant to hurt me…I really feel quite well..surely you noticed that.” Ivan shrugs and raises his arms up to the ceiling. “Silence Alexei! It looked as if she was going to attack you and damage you! I cannot allow dearest Comrade to risk being hurt!” Alexei and Wang nod, well aware that it is probably better not to continue to anger Ivan any more than he already is. Ivan nods for a minute and turns around, the trail of blood from his head now slowly slides down the middle of his hulking back. He chuckles, talking with his back to the camera. “You know…it was funny to see that painful look on Meanstreak’s face when his weak little knee was crumbled into nothing. I enjoyed that very much. That is what happens when you go up against a powerful army…that is what happens when you try to stop The Red Army.” Wang nods and clears his throat. “But Pwapowschik, what about Doug Twoy?” Ivan turns and looks at Wang. “Doug Troy is nothing. He thinks the coming of Ivan Stanislav is a mistake to OSW? Well, to a capitalist, Doug Troy is entitled to that opinion. But to those that embrace the collective and love the communist ideal, we are the saviors OF the OSW. And if Doug Troy wants to get in between Ivan Stanislav and Meanstreak, let him, but he will quickly be eliminated and thrown to the side so that we can finish what Meanstreak started….” Wang nods, happy with the answer that was given. Ivan stares at the floor and then slowly clenches his fists again. “But I still cannot believe that….that SHE cost me the match! And I do not even believe she understands the damage that she caused! I was to get that shot at the OSW title and take over federation! But now I have to find other means to do so….” Ivan growls to himself and then darts his eyes over at Alexei and Wang. “Leave me alone for a while...I do not wish to be bothered. I have to think about some things….” Wang nods and walks out of the door. Alexei takes a step forward and frowns, thinking as well. Ivan sits on the bench with his back to Alexei. Alexei shrugs and continues to stand in the room, not really wanting to leave. ....as a knock comes at the door. Alexei turns and looks at the door. He opens it slowly, peering out as the door pushes open and Tempest steps in with a sheepish smile, in her sparkling blue top and dark purple velvet jeans. Alexei gasps and looks over at Ivan, and then looks over at her. 'You had better leave, Tempest....' whispers Alexei as he motions to the seething Stanislav. Tempest looks at Alexei and speaks, loudly enough to be heard throughout the room. 'No I don't think so. I believe I owe someone an apology, and maybe I'm owed a couple as well,' she says, gazing at him and then at Ivan. Alexei mouths something as if to speak, and then stops and looks to Ivan. He shrugs and fixes his hat and leaves the room. There is silence for a moment, and then Ivan bursts into movement, hopping to his feet and pointing across the room at Tempest. 'I do not wish to hear anything! Get out of here!!' Tempest takes a deep breath and folds her arms. 'No,' she says evenly. She takes a step forward cautiously. 'I don't know what you were thinking out there, but I don't accept being ordered around very well.' Ivan roars loudly again and kicks the bench in front of him, pulling a few bolts from the floor. 'REALLY?! Well Ivan does not take very well to being cost match and humiliated in the ring!!' Ivan roars again, blood drips off his pants to the floor behind him now. Tempest's gaze catches the sight of blood and she tilts her head a bit as she says, 'Ok, I'm sorry I distracted you from your match. But... why was it that big a deal?” Ivan blinks, almost like he got blindsided with a punch. 'What was the matter?' He takes a step forward, towering over Tempest. 'WHAT WAS THE MATTER?! THAT WAS FOR WORLD TITLE SHOT!! THAT is what was the matter!' Ivan scratches his head, thoroughly confused as to why she doesn't understand. Tempest nods, glancing once again at he small trail of blood on the floor behind Ivan. 'I know that. And that's why I accepted Jane's invitation to come down and watch with her. I thought you might find it encouraging. I mean, how much attention does it take to just see that I'm there?' She shrugs, entirely lost. Ivan shakes his head again and punches another locker beside him, trying desperately to keep calm. He takes another step closer to Tempest. 'Why are you conversing with that woman?! She is evil! She is only going to exploit you! SHE HATES ME!!' As he turns his back, Tempest gets a good look at the trail of blood and frowns. 'Ivan, you're hurt. Sit down and let me tend to you. Jane and I... we're old friends. I thought you knew that.' Ivan growls. 'I did not know that...and I do not appreciate the realization either....' Ivan grumbles again and sits. 'I do not need your help....let me be....' A smile ticks across Tempest's face briefly as she steps behind him and says, 'Well, you need somebody's. You do know you're bleeding all over the place, right?' Ivan snorts to himself, shaking his head. His huge shoulders rise and fall as he breathes. 'I do not care if I bleed, a little blood never hurt anyone.' He pauses for a moment and continues. 'And don’t think I'm sorry for what I did tonight...trying to get you away from the ring, you should not have been there....' Tempest sighs and shakes her head, then retrieves a first aid kit form a locker that was thankfully untouched by Ivan's tirade. She gently dabs at some of the blood as she says, 'Why not? I work here now too, ya know.' 'You were brought down to the ring BY Reagan to distract me...she has always tried to stop me in anything that I do.' Ivan shrugs and then jumps just once from a stabbing pain. 'It does not hurt....' 'Of course not, and I'm just concerned about the carpet,' she says with more than a hint of sarcasm. 'Jane signed me and I took her up on an invitation. Besides, I shouldn't have been that big a distraction. Not unless you... as they say, can't walk and chew gum at the same time.' She said while swabbing away more blood, still looking for its source. 'Ha ha..very funny little storm...' Ivan says grudgingly. “Anyone that gets close to Jane Reagan becomes an evil capitalist...I..I just suppose I was angry because.....because....' Ivan trails off and shakes his head, a low growl constantly heard from his breathing. 'Because what?' Tempest asks. She shakes her head and steps to a nearby sink for a wet cloth, giving him a moment to think. Ivan sits, not looking at her, pondering what to say. He lets out a low grumble. 'Because she turns those around her into conniving...individuals....and I...grr....I did not want that to happen to you!' Ivan growls, embarrassed at saying that. Tempest smiles ever so slightly and says, 'Well, thank you for your um... concern... I think.' Her brow furrows a bit, and she says, 'Well, I am sorry it I distracted you. But really, you have nothing to worry about. Anyway, I'm surprised you didn’t' know... considering how much you've had Alexei rooting around in my business lately.' Her tone carries just a note of displeasure. Ivan shifts his weight, not looking at her. 'I do not know what you are talking about...' Tempest rolls her eyes and continues dabbing away the blood on his back as she says, 'Of course not, my phone's tapped, my friends are getting weird calls and all these oddly Slavic looking people with a really bad sense of fashion keep showing up wherever I am.' She sighs and then steps back looking slightly dismayed. 'Of course I know my past may not be as.. obvious as yours, but maybe you could just ask me if ya wanted to know?' Ivan stammers an answer. 'Well..I do not know anything about that...but if I ever have that problem..I will be sure to ask you.' Ivan shrugs, perhaps in one way that is his way of saying he is sorry. He glances over his left shoulder. 'And do not think that I am completely in the dark regarding your...past.' Tempest blinks, quelling her own very mixed feelings about that very past, and says, 'I'm sure. Maybe that's why Alexei missed something as perfectly public as my being friends with Jane. Maybe he was a little too fascinated by the... less attractive parts of my past?' She shakes her head and adds, 'Speaking of which, despite this roadmap of scars I can't seem to find a wound back here.' Ivan shrugs. 'It is a head wound..remember? I fell and hit my head off of apron while I was trying to help you! And do not berate Alex, he is only doing his job.....it is not a bad thing if he is watching you...if anything, that ensures that you will not be harmed.' Tempest sighs and says, 'Ah, that explains a lot. No wonder it didn't faze you. And Alex has no business poking around in my life whatever the reasons.' Ivan growls and grabs the bench beside him, the wood is heard snapping as his fingers dig into it. 'He had to be sure that you were not....a spy..or someone from America trying to undermine me....' Ivan shrugs, not realizing he just admitted that he has had her tailed. Tempest silently dabs away the blood from the back of his head, finding the gash at last, and then dabs a piece of gauze in alcohol. 'A spy? Are you sure that was what was on his mind? Are you sure he wasn’t more interested in my ties to the triads? Maybe worried that I might be... an assassin?' On the last word, she presses the soaked pad against the wound. Ivan tenses and breathes through his nose loudly. 'GRRRRRR.....!!' He breathes heavier. 'An assassin? Why would you be assassin? After all..it was your father that was killed..I do not think you would join that sort of a trade. Besides, we of the Russian Federation are not afraid of 'assassins!' I am a soldier, not some rogue....' 'Do NOT speak about my father!' she snaps, then more calmly continues, 'If I'm not a threat then what is Alexei looking for? What does it matter where I sit during a match?' Ivan growls, his patience growing thin. He gets up and whirls around and faces Tempest. 'You were not a problem there until you started to distract me! You were waving and throwing Ivan off!!” Ivan’s brow furrows as he tries desperately to pound his argument into her head. He bends at his waist, looking Tempest in the eyes. “You think this is so funny? You think it is hilarious to play jokes on me! THIS IS MY LIFE TEMPEST! Do you not understand!?' Ivan rises again and looks straight down at Tempest. Tempest's eyes narrow as she says, 'And my life is mine. I'll be happy to stay out of yours if you prefer entirely, but keep your henchmen out of mine. And don't presume to tell me who my friends should be.' Ivan shakes his head and takes a step backwards. 'Alright, I will see if I can get Alexei under control! But I want you to promise me that you will not interfere with me in the ring! But I am also warning you.....Jane Reagan is not one to be trusted....you can take my word whether you believe it or not.' Tempest nods slightly as she says, 'I don't think you give her enough credit. She signed you after all. And may I remind you that it was Meanstreak who really interfered with you, not me.' Suddenly, Ivan growls and slams his fist into the ceiling, collapsing ceiling tile and busting a pipe. He brings his fist down, blood trickling from his fist and blood dripping off of his back again. 'Don't....mention him. You want to understand my reasoning behind this entire dilemma?' Ivan says, with a definite condescending tone to his voice. Tempest's eyelids flutter in irritation as she says, 'Yeah, preferably before you lose consciousness due to blood loss. Will you stop hitting sharp things?!' She shakes her head and grabs another wad of gauze, placing it over his hand. Ivan grumbles as she fixes his hand. 'Jane Reagan hires me to battle the PCW in interfed pay per view, and then she signs men that are my enemies in an attempt to stop me! Furthermore, she signs Meanstreak to come back, who is about as reliable as Adolf Hitler! She double crossed me....she thought she could best me, but she won't. And know that she notices that YOU are.....closer to me....she is trying to manipulate you.' Ivan shrugs. 'You can listen to whatever you want, from whoever you want, but I want you to understand that I am not in wrong here, Tempest, regardless what anyone else will tell you....' Tempest smiles, just a little and says, 'I believe you. Or at least I believe that you sincerely believe that.' She shrugs, 'You could be right. But you're just going to have to trust me not to be that easily manipulated.' Ivan nods slowly. 'It appears that we have come to an agreement....little storm. And Ivan will....try to respect your privacy.....er...Alexei will.' Ivan shakes his head and looks away. Tempest hides a snicker behind her hand and says, 'Of course. And I'll stay away from the ring if you like. Except of course when I happen to be wrestling in it.' She grins in anticipation of her first match in the OSW. Ivan laughs. 'Do not worry, The Red Army will make sure that you do not lose! HA HA!' 'Ugh...' she groansand smacks her forehead. 'We can have that conversation another time. Now... will you sit still long enough, and stop hitting things long enough, for me to clean that up?' She motions to the nasty gash he's managed to open up on his hand. Ivan rolls his eyes and sits again, his head bleeding again anew as well. 'Tempest, Ivan really does not need for you to tend to him..you do not need to do this....' She shrugs and does so anyway. 'And who else will? Especially that one you can't see...' Ivan shakes his head indifferently. 'It would not have been the first time I was wounded without being able to see it, you will notice when you are a man of size and strength as me that no one tries to attack them head on. I will just let it heal up, much like I do my other wounds. It is a sign of character, absorbing the worst that one can throw at you and keep fighting.' Ivan pauses for a moment. 'Besides....no one has ever really wondered about healing a wound of mine....' Tempest shakes her head and says, 'Yeah I noticed. At least there's no shrapnel around here. But hey, I felt bad about tonight. I didn't mean to throw ya off, really.' She pats his shoulder gently and looks to the gash on his head. Ivan pauses for a moment and mutters so soft that Tempest barely hears him. 'Itisquitealright.....' 'What was that?' she says, trying not to chuckle. Ivan hesitates. 'Uh....it uh....it is quite all right....uh....no damage that uh...cannot..be fixed....' Ivan clears his throat and shifts his weight. She smiles and says, 'I'm sure. See? It's not so bad.' Ivan snorts. 'I suppose not...let us just hope that it does not happen again, eh?' 'It won't. Not from me at least. Now if Doug Troy would leave ya alone, that would be a real break,' she says. Then she cringes remembering that it's yet another name he probably doesn't want to hear. Ivan perks up his ears suddenly, a SNAP is heard as Ivan breaks off a piece of the bench. 'WHAT?' Ivan growls under his breath. 'You should not..bring that idiot...up either...' 'Ah ah ah... no breaking anything else,' Tempest says, hoping to at least distract him from any more property damage. 'What's the deal with him anyway?' Ivan shakes his head. 'Doug Troy was supposedly the hero of PCW when I came in to oust Tyler Nelson from power and install my communist regime. Not that he was allies with Nelson, but he held...' Ivan speaks the next sentence like a small child, '....the faaans favor.' He shakes his head. 'It was all for nothing, after tossing him five rows into the crowd in our first meeting, and after potentially breaking his back with my bare hands.....he was forced into retirement....however...' Ivan pauses for a moment and continues. 'Now Reagan came over and decided to bring this battered old fighter back..and now Troy wishes to undermine everything.....' He scoffs. 'He is a fool.' 'He sounds like a worthy opponent,' Tempest says as she presses a gauze pad against the back of his head, finally having some effect. Ivan shrugs, 'They are all fools, Tempest, you will learn...they all learn. They either join me or they are sent out of federation in a stretcher. I try to bargain with them....but in the end that is what happens.' A small smile forms on Ivan's face as he realizes that his head does indeed feel a bit better. Tempest smiles as she says, 'Well, I hope there's more than two options, because I don't think I like the sound of either of those. You'll just have to make an exception in my case.' She giggles a little and removes the gauze. 'There, all better.' Ivan stands and turns, bringing his huge hand to the back of his head and rubs it. He looks up at the lights for a second and then at Tempest, averting his gaze again and mumbling. 'Yes well uh..I would like to say uh....' 'Ehem.....uh...just that uh....' Tempest tilts her head curiously. Ivan rubs the back of his neck and looks at Tempest again and then looks at the floor, speaking very softly again. 'Uh...thank...you for uh...helping……..me....ehem...' Tempest smiles and says, 'You're welcome. After all, I can’t be a pain all the time.' She snickers a little bit. Ivan nods. 'Yes well...perhaps that is true.' Ivan smiles at her, and then catches himself, resuming his granite face. 'Ehem, yes, well, perhaps it is time to call it a uh...night......nyet?' Tempest nods and heads for the door. 'By all means. G'night Ivan, I'll see you next week. Who knows what trouble I'll think of by then.' She grins mischievously and slips out the door. Seconds after Tempest leaves, Ruslan barges into the room and looks at Ivan. Ivan looks at the gauze sitting on the bench beside him whilst biting his lower lip. He shakes his head and reaches into a locker and slides on his black shirt and slings his suspenders back over his shoulders. Alexei clears his throat. “So….what did she want Praporschik?” Ivan picks up the gauze in his hand as a wry smile crosses his face. He places the gauze gingerly in one of the few lockers still standing and chuckles to himself. “I am not sure Alexei…..I am not sure….” Ivan chuckles and turns to his friend. Alexei is now completely confused. “Come Alexei, let us get out of here. Our work is done here.” “Da, Comrade Stanislav.” The camera fades out as Alexei walks out of the room, followed by Ivan. The camera fades out as we get a look at the newly healed wound on Ivan’s head…that a ‘little storm’ managed to mend…..
