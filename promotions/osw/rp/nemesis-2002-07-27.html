Nemesis

27 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/27/02 Through the ghoul-guarded gateways of slumber, Past the wan-mooned abysses of night, I have lived o'er my lives without number, I have sounded all things with my sight; And I struggle and shriek ere the daybreak, being driven to madness with fright. Evil, what is truly evil? Am I an evil man? Am I psychotic? Or am I just a punk? Does evil even exist? Or is it simply a lack of goodness? Damn, why does this shit have to be so fucked up? I need a cause, something to live for, something more rewarding then pain. I have whirled with the earth at the dawning, When the sky was a vaporous flame; I have seen the dark universe yawning Where the black planets roll without aim, Where they roll in their horror unheeded, without knowledge or lustre or name. But I have had a thirst for pain. This thirst has been constant, forever. Undying, unsatisfied, unsated. It has its ebbs and flows, its ups and downs. Yet it is unwavering. I had drifted o'er seas without ending, Under sinister grey-clouded skies That the many-forked lightning is rending, That resound with hysterical cries; With the moans of invisible daemons that out of the green waters rise. I can't remember when I wasn't hunting. When I wasn't searching for those to maim and injure. Is it something a shrink could fix? Is it some existential need to see people suffer as I have? Or is it beyond me, beyond rationality? I have plunged like a deer through the arches Of the hoary primordial grove, Where the oaks feel the presense that marches And stalks on where no spirit dares rove, And I flee from a thing that surrounds me, and leers through dead branches above. I've fought it. For as long as I can remember, I have ran from it. But I can't escape it. The drive to hurt is faster then I am, it always catches me. Am I in control? Or am I trapped? Am I a puppet on a string, controlled by some unseen force? I have stumbled by cave-riddled mountains That rise barren and bleak from the plain, I have drunk of the frog-foetid fountains That ooze down to the marsh and the main; And in hot cursed tarns I have seen things I care not to gaze on again. I have memories that I shouldn't. I know things I shouldn't. I've seen places that I've never been to. I am more then me. My life is not my own. I have scanned the vast ivy-clad palace, I have trod its untenanted hall, Where the moon rising up from the valleys Shows the tapestried things on the walls; Strange figures dischordantly woven, that I cannot endure to recall. Strange memories, blurry memories, drive me to do evil. A drive I cannot control. I drive I often have no desire to control. I have peered from the casements in wonder At the mouldering meadows around, At the many-roofed village laid under The curse of a grave-girdled ground; And from rows of white urn-carven marble I listen intently for sound. At home in evil. Comfort in pain. Solace in agony. I live for what you fear. I sprint in directions you flee. I bathe in pools of your blood and tears. I wallow and sleep in beds of your sorrow. I have haunted the tombs of the ages, I have flown on the pinions of fear Where the smoke-belching Erebus rages; Where the jokulls look snow-clad and drear: And in realms where the sun of the desert consumes what it never can cheer. Praise, Fans, Titles, Wins? These things do not drive me. I have no desire for acceptance, not by the fans, not by my peers, not by anyone. Acceptance is for the weak. I was old when the pharoahs first mounted The jewel-decked throne by the Nile; I was old in those epochs uncounted When I, and I only, was vile; And Man, yet untainted and happy, dwelt in bliss on the far Arctic isle. My evil is forever. My thirst is undying. It's outta my hands, I have to accept that. Oh, great was the sin of my spirit, And great is the reach of its doom; Not the pity of Heaven can cheer it, Nor can respite be found in the tomb: Down the infinite aeons come beating the wings of unmerciful gloom. I have driven my enemies away. My actions have affected many, and my evil is only growing. There are three things that are unfathomable, infinite, the universe, and the scope of my anger. Through the ghoul-guarded gateways of slumber, Past the wan-mooned abysses of night, I have lived o'er my lives without number, I have sounded all things with my sight; And I struggle and shriek ere the daybreak, being driven to madness with fright. So I will continue to be torn. Do I accept my fate, embrace the pain. Or do I fight it? Accepting the pain seems inevitable. I am doomed to be the Nemesis of the universe. So, I continue to search, continue to ask. Is there true badness in the world, is there something that is pure evil? Or have you already answered me.... Here is badness, here is evil.....here....in me. (credits: 'Nemesis' by H.P. Lovecraft and extra special thanks to AIMZ!!!)
