Ripped me apart, to shreds...and all I'm left to do is dream. I've been dreaming a lot in this shitty bed, stupid dreams, nothing important. What's really stupid is what I was dreaming earlier this evening. I was under the sea. We had found Atlantis. Perhaps it was the hospital food, or the Disney Movie that I watched so long ago. Regardless, the city was as beautiful as a single emerald twinkling in the moonlight, as beautiful as the candle light reflects in the darkness. I had met many new people, and then the arch-nemesis of me and mine arived, and awoke me from my dream. “Hello Rhett. Yeah it’s me, you’re favorite person in the whole wide world. Graphic Violence.” Fuck. He was here, in my god damned room. I never awoke from peaceful slumber, but his voice was like one thousand, stinging wasps. Every syllable was a pain in the ass. Every word sent a shiver through my spine...I wanted to wake up and choke him to death...wake up and destroy him. “Have you finally realized that you are no match for me Rhett? Whether it is in the ring or out of the ring. You are playing a game in which you have no sight. You don’t know which way is left and which way is right. You’re playing with me Rhett, and if you ask anyone who’s ever faced me…they’ll tell you that they regret ever getting into a confrontation with me.” His voice was the fiery hell my life became, sheer madness was felt through my veins, blood boiling through my flesh as he continued. Destroying my mindset. He made me travel a road to ruins, my epitome...the epitome of paranoia, defeat...agony. His hand was firmly upon my throat... “I know you can hear me you fuckin’ pussy. And you’ve got to be screaming inside of that head of yours. Screaming in fear. What am I going to do Rhett? That’s what you’re wondering.” GV lets go of his throat. “But I’m not going to end you just yet Rhett. This is just too much fun.” God damn him...god damn him straight to hell...straight to a fiery inferno...I hate you GV! I HATE YOU! “Long days of pain and suffering are ahead for you Rhett. In what world could you have ever of beaten me? Such a place does not exist” . . . We'll see, Graphic Violence, we'll see...
