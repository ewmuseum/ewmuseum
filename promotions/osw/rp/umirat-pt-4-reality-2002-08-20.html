Umirat.... (Pt 4. Reality)

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-20-02 The answer was simple… “Die….Die…Die.” He had committed to killing Corg. Killing him like the dog he was… Rage has completely consumed Ivan Sergeiovich Stanislav. The body of Corg hangs in his massive, pale hands as he repeatedly slams his prone body against the turnbuckle, screaming angrily in his native tongue with every bone jarring slam. ~~ Robbie: Oh my God….. ~~ The least likely person to run to the ring, the least likely individual to be seen in this situation, emerges. Tempest jogs down the entryway, her face covered in worry. Fans reach out to tag her, but she ignores them and remains safe from their reach. Twice she stops as the roars of the angry Russian Bear can be heard, until finally she breathes in deep and climbs into the ring. Riggs lays in the corner and eyes Tempest as she enters, trying to regain his breath. Slam after thunderous slam of Corg’s body against the corner can be heard, the roars of Ivan Stanislav crystal clear within the squared circle. “You will NEVER hurt her again!! Let it be the last words you hear Corg!!” He screams, oblivious to Tempests presence. Tempest takes a deep breath and steps closer. She gasps, realizing how terribly injured Corg truly is. With a slightly shaking hand she reaches out and grabs Ivan’s arm and screams to him, 'Ivan! No! Stoppit!' He heard nothing but his voice and the gurgles of Corg. He wished Riggs hadn’t interfered the first time, but the second time was too much. It was time that Speedy Riggs learn to stay out of his business…. Ivan turns and growls, poised to backhand the interloper. He freezes suddenly, the face of Tempest, the face he saw when he blinked, now right in front of him. Tempest leaps back nimbly, almost as if ready for such a response, and looks up at Stanislav to make eye contact. Her eyes are widen, one step from panic, but she stands her ground. She raises her hands and speaks, so quietly only he can hear. 'Ivan, please... Don't do this. He's finished. And... and I'm ok, see? Let him go.' Ivan freezes, staring into Tempest’s deep blue eyes. ~~ Nate: Beauty….. and the Bear? ~~ Ivan growls heavily as he sees her and squeezes Corgs neck, a soft whimper comes from The Silent Assassin… Stanislav keeps his one hand wrapped around Corg’s neck as Tempest tries to calm him, he continues to stare into her eyes, unable to pull himself away. 'Ivan, c'mon. This isn't helping anything. You've beat him. You've made him pay. It's done.' Tempest takes a quick breath and steps closer to Stanislav. She wraps her tiny arms around his massive bicep and pleads with him, her voice sounds so tiny beneath his angered growls and breathing, “Ivan, let him go. Please, you've made your point.” Ivan stares back and for a moment, his eyes shift and then he growls angrily and grinds his jaw, shaking his head, “Nyet…..” he turns his attention back to Corg. With newfound resolve, Stanislav squeezes Corg’s neck harder. Pain briefly clouds her expression, but Tempest tries again. She scoots around to the other side of the hulking Russian so that he once again can see her, noting his attempt to ignore her. She wraps her tiny arms around his right arm and tugs harder, speaking softly, 'Please Ivan... don't do this and hurt yourself too. You already got him back... in spades. You've done enough.' He growls again and shakes his head absently. Tempests face hardens slightly and speaks louder, with more strength in her voice, “C'mon Ivan let him go. You can't kill him. Don't let him get to you like that. It'll only ruin you, not him!” Her voice was like the calm Russian snow that he loved to watch in Arkhangelsk, it soothed him, even now…. Ivan looks over at Tempest, his eyebrow twitches and just slightly, his hand weakens the grip around Corg’s neck. It almost appears as if he is about to let go, and then he shakes his head slowly, memories of her pain entering his head again, and grips slightly again. His right eye twitches…. Tempest stands up taller on her tip toes and tugs a little harder. It's a useless gesture physically, but the intent is there. “Ivan please... Let him go!” She hears his breathing slow slightly and continues, “I know he deserves it, but this is only hurting you. It's over.” Ivan looks back at Corg, tearing his gaze from Tempest once more. “He….he…” growls Stanislav…more to himself than to anyone else. He slowly releases Corg, until only his hand holds Corg up, pushed against his chest but no longer choking him. Ivan breathes heavily, a sort of humanity plays over his face and suddenly, we realize that whatever grip he had lost in his mind he has regained. With an almost horrified expression he looks to Tempest. Tempest smiles, ever so slightly. 'There ya go. Just leave him. C'mon...' She smiles very slightly at him and starts to back away, still holding his arm. She seems relieved, but wary. “C'mon... let them bring out the pooper scooper for him.” Ivan blinks and slowly releases Corg. The sounds of the crowds sigh of relief hits his ears, his breathing becomes apparent, and the entire situation around him switches from a surreal bloodlust to a somewhat lethargic calm. He notices the pull on his arm from Tempest and takes a heavy step from Corg, the mat shaking under his mighty weight. ~~ Nate: He let go! She got him to let go of Corg!! She tamed the Bear!!! Robbie: I uh..wouldn’t go that far Nate. ~~ She saw it all… all of what he had done. She heard his screams, looked into his eyes, and learned who he truly was… a monster himself. Ivan looks at Tempest and blinks, taking in the entire situation, a blood covered mat, Corg, and Stanislav and amidst it all, her… She was still innocent… and surrounded by carnage that he had created…. Ivan looks at her and shakes his head suddenly, his face switching for confusion to horror. Tempest shakes her head slightly, confusion covering her face as she notices Stanislav staring at the blood on his hands, “What? What's wrong?' Ivan turns very suddenly, turning his broad back to Tempest and exits the ring. The mat shakes with the subtraction of his weight, leaving Tempest in the ring with a bloody Corg and Speedy Riggs. Stanislav covers the side of his face from Tempest and round the ring, slowly exiting up the entryway, the fans cheer him on, “Leave me alone… do not come near me… it is over… it is all… over…” he grumbles to himself and trudges up the entryway and backstage. It is quiet in the ring. The cheers of the fans to not enter the squared circle as the smell of blood hangs in the air. Tempest looks mystified and more than a little upset. She looks down at Corg as the EMTs arrive. 'You better be grateful you're alive. Everyone wanted him to finish you.' she snaps at him and rolls from the ring. He sat in his locker room, devoid of sound or interruption. Alone was Ivan Stanislav as per his request. He had destroyed Corg. He had gotten his revenge. He had inflicted an insurmountable amount of pain upon the Silent Assassin… …but nothing compared to the pain he inflicted upon himself.
