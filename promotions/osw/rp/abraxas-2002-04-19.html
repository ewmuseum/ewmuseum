Abraxas

19 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19th of April 2002 'You lied to me! I trusted you, believed in you and you lied to me! I hate you!' Mara Trinstin, the famous actress, screamed at the shadowed form. 'I never lied to you,' a soft, masculine voice said from the shadows. 'Yes, you did! You inhuman wretch,' Mara yelled. 'No, I never lied,' the soft voice continued. 'I merely didn't present all the facts. It is not the same as lying.' 'You're right, it's not the same - it's worse!' 'Mara, I fulfilled my half of the bargain. You are the most beautiful woman in Hollywood, right? You have many fans and more money than you know what to do with. I did what I promised,' the voice explained. 'And ruined my life,' Mara replied angrily. 'I did no such thing, It was your decision, not mine,' he said matter-of-factly. 'You tricked me into this and I hate you for it,' Mara retorted. 'Hate me if you wish,' the dark man said, his eyes flashing, 'but there's nothing that can put things back the way they were before you met me.' 'Then I'll kill myself here in front of you!' Mara said desperately. 'You wouldn't know how to commit suicide and you're not brave enough to do it,' he replied. 'Then I'll kill you instead!' 'Come on, try it!' he snarled. 'You can't kill me, no one can. Besides, you need me to survive. And I need a companion.' 'Ha! You and your kind are nothing but leeches. You find someone that appeals to you and suck the life from them. You disgust me,' she said hatefully. 'It is my nature, how I survive! I need life from others so I can live. You'll soon understand, my dear,' he replied stepping from the shadows. 'No, I don't want to understand you!' she shouted. 'And you called me a liar. You are lying. You enjoyed it last night. Admit it! You loved it, especially the handsome, young men. You liked building up their trust and when they wouldn't suspect a thing, you took them. 'You wanted them! And most of all, you loved every minute of it! Don't deny your nature or yourself!' the fiendishly handsome man shouted. 'No! No! No-oo-oo!' Mara shrieked. 'Listen to me,' he said grabbing Mara by the shoulders and shaking her violently. 'I'm hungry and so are you, we must hunt. Now get a hold on yourself!' 'Let me go, I'm fine,' Mara said defeatedly. 'There, that's better. Now go fix your hair. It's getting late,' he said soothingly. 'How can I? I can't even see myself in a mirror!' 'Never mind, besides our kind are always beautiful. Let's go,' he said, his cape rustling as he stepped to the window. 'No, I don't want to go! I refuse to go with you!' 'Then starve! Have you ever starved? I doubt it. You have no idea what it's like for our kind to starve. Believe me, you wouldn't like it,' he snapped, his canines catching the light from the moon. 'You wouldn't dare let me starve,' she said astonished. 'Would I? Try me,' he said coldly. 'All right, I'll go with you,' Mara in acquiescence. 'Good,' he said, his eyes fiery. 'Let us depart, I'm hungry.' 'Yes,' Mara said obediently. 'Ah, the night,' he said throwing open the large French windows. 'The thrill of the hunt awaits and I'm ready to kill and drink warm, red blood. This is what being a vampire is all about, love. Come now, Abraxas, Mara is so, so, human… so that I may introduce you to humanity.' And with those final words the vampire bared his fangs and motioned Abraxas to come to him. She obeyed and the vampire and his bride leaped out the open windows together.
