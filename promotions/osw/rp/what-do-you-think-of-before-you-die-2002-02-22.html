What do you think of before you die?

22 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-22-02 You can taste your own sweat. Flooding down into your mouth like a waterfall of toxins, mixing with that blood preceding it. Like chewing on rusted water, thick with mud. Your body gains flight. Becomes weightless for mere seconds before letting gravity be true to its nature. Pain shoots through every muscle; the cold, dank desparation of emptiness surrounding the area about you. Floating to your feet, only to receive more pain, centering about your nasal cavity and cracking away at the portion of your skull resting between your eye sockets. More pressure driving itself against you, feeling familiar, but in greater mass. The sickening thuds begin to perk your senses and...again, that pressure. Once more. Twice. Thrice. The cloud about you corroding your vision suddenly parts like a city evading a descending bomb. You're seeing now. And you know what's happening. The haziness about you becomes crystal clear, sharp as a knife. And your brain. Finally registering what exactly is going on. Skull meeting skull. Your skull. The one striking down, much larger than your own. You try to wince to soften some of the blows, but only feel chips of your cranium fall loose from its base and push against the skin of your forehead. It's difficult to see who it is exactly. The face in front of you comes in flashes, seconds in between each striking shot. You keep trying to focus, but never can you quite reach your goal. Always having it stolen from your clutches by that massive head in front of you. You think for a moment to look down, but that would require movement. You dare not to move. Your deductions assume that your skull would peel off in much larger fragments if you were to do so. But that pain. God, the pain. You can't turn your back on it. You can't let it be just another ignorant understatement. You can't seem to shake it. And it won't stop. It just will not stop. You wonder for a moment whether your attacker is getting winded from his onslaught or if he's feeding off each shattering headbutt. Your vision isn't doing much for you other than leaving your eyes open for injury were something to come into contact with them. Quickly choosing to close them for safety's sake, you do so, but refrain from keeping your eyelids too close together so as not to upset your fractured skull to any greater degree. Then, like an unexpected pregnancy, the stagnant scent of aching reaches its way to your nostrils. Spilling into them like a glass filling with water, the pain almost brings you to the point of unconsciousness. You focus on the pain and yelp, a natural reaction that brings no form of embarassment to your psyche. The pain so great almost outrules that gravity coming forth again as your buttocks meet a solid foundation. The occurance barely registers, but you know it happened, and you don't really seem to care at the moment. Suddenly, remembering that your eyes are still sewn shut, you pull the thread from them and open each, one at a time, like a blind man witnessing sunlight for the very first time. You see a veryprominent scar shrouding the right side of the man's face, but quickly close your eyes as another piece of your skull crumbles from its nestled ledge between your eyes. Naturally, you squint hard and do nothing but allow more bone to stumble free. Another yelp. And laughter. Straight ahead of you. You couldn't tune that out no matter how hard or how long you tried. A low, gutteral, drifting laugh, shaking the solidity of your bones and making your ear drums rumble in accompaniement with that seering headache. You ignore your pain for a moment and throw your cares aside, opening your eyes and looking up at the thing letting out those ungodly tones. A gasp seeps out quietly as you look at the man before you. 'Good lord...' Words escaping your lips to put your attacker into the proper light. The man before you clutches a fist in his own and shakes his head. Blinking, he grins hideously and opens his mouth. Like a bad dream, the words come to you slowly and delayed, everyone of them heart stabbingly filth ridden. 'Motherf*ckin' sack o sh!t...serves yer impatient ass, right, ya c*cksmoke.' He steps forward, narrowing the space between you and setting your mind at a frantic pace. You no longer care of your pain. You no longer care about what he's done. You want out of there. Fast. Immediately. 'Wha-what did I duh.....dooo...' You want to smack yourself, your broken English so drunkedly delivered you fear he might thrash you for it. Instead, his gargantuan arms fall to his sides and his head tilts just slightly to his right. You blink and that pain tries to outweigh the images being projected into your mind. Swiftly they are pushed aside inadvertantly and those pictures sit carelessly in their place. 'What'd ya do? Are ya f*ckin' kiddin' me? Nah...ya can't be...ya wouldn't joke at a time like this now...would ya?' He blinks again, apparently wanting an answer. You wonder again for a moment if you really did do something or if he is just trying to play with your head. 'I'm not mind f*ckin' ya, ya f*ckin' dipsh!t.' Foul words answer your previous thoughts and confirm the first assumption. You try to delve into your own little world and think back to what you did. Again, your eyes close and that pain comes with it, surfacing a shriek, in a highpitched mockery of your own stupidity. Thinking is out of the question. Just an afterthought now. Pain is a clever foe. 'Open yer eyes and stop dreamin'. Talk ta me and gimme answers...ya had no problem doin' so a moment ago.' The beast of a man steps forward as you slowly open your eyes, feeling those fragments of bone claw away at your forehead. Your head turns to the right, a glint of light catching your eyes. At first, you welcome the distraction and allow it to peak your interests. But, with that distraction, it all comes back to haunt you. You know what you did. The twisted hood of your brand new Le Baron reaching up toward the sky...the driver's side door twisted up and over the roog of the car. New images begin flashing into your mind as new sounds birth with them as well. The humming of the engine, key still in the ignition and pressed forward. The sounds of other cars passing, their headlighst bouncing yellow and white colors against the silver frame of your car. You turn your head slowly as you remember the man in front of you. He must have been bringing his skull to your own for some time now. That or the first blow shot your memory to the recesses of your mind, where a a simple digging would bring them to the surface. You look back to the car and notice the grill and bumper both pushed forward, mauled and battered to a hunk of garbage. Like a slow motion video, you remember that man who must be getting angrier with your memory's interruption, walking across the street as you accelerated forward and hit his body with full force. Your mind was in a state of panic the instant his legs met the chrome of the bumper and were pulled from under him. His body sliding with a healthy screech across the hood of your car and slamming into the windowshield, cracking it like an eggshell. Insurance was definitely going to raise for you. You remembered gathering your thoughts and getting out to check and see if he was okay. Your mind raced and wondered how you could have missed such a huge man as you surveyed him on your hood. You placed a hand adorned with your wedding ring on his shoulder for a moment and he sat up. It must have been something like he was doing to you now that he did to you then. Your throat closes under the strength of this man's single hand as he pulls you into the air so his eyes can meet your own. 'What the hell are ya thinkin' ignorin' me like that?' He hadn't a clue you were just now remembering how you wronged him. 'Talk.' His fingers relieve the pressure crimping your larynx shut, but he still holds you up freely in the air as you gasp for your own. '...I...I must...have....have forgotten what...happened...I think my head...messed up when you--' 'I knocked yer memory cockeyed. Heh. Neat.' The man grins and sets you back down to the pavement beneath you. 'Ya hit me with yer car, ya dumbass. I dunno how the f*ck ya managed ta do that, but ya did. Do ya remember what I did?' You look up at the man and shake your head as you bring a hand to your now aching throat. Slowly, you shake your head back and forth as his lips curl upward in a mood shifting smile. 'I stuffed ya right back in your car and---' Your mind races back to what happened. The memories flow out like smoke from a cigar. This man is still talking, but you don't even bother to listen. He'd either exaggerate or just tell it to you in that hideous accent of his. He stuffed you back in his car just as he said. Slid off your hood and stared at you as he shifted his vision to the front of your car. Eyes left the grill and trailed back to your own as you sat in bewilderment. Slowly, he ran his fingers across the narrow space between the hood of the Le Baron and the rest of the body, his lower lips beginning to perk outward in rage. His fingers delved down under the crevice as he stared straight back at you. The latch holding the hood down snapped audibly, but the hood did not lift. You shifted your weight forward, anger leaving you and curiosity replacing it to see exactly what he was doing. Your own car was about to be ripped apart and you were curious. '--ya f*ckin' idiot--' Three words leaking into the monster of a man's story and making complete sense with your preceding thought. You nod in agreement and look down to his hands. You remember them sliding to the sides of the hood and pushing them slowly together. You shake your head as that hood crumples into a wad of paper before your mind's very eye. His hands let the hood free from his grasp and allowed it to shoot upward, poking into the air like some new form of modern art. All you could do was stare at that hood as he came closer to you. He leaned down and placed those hands, those metal bending, latch snapping hands on your open window. The beastly man shook his head at you and pulled outward. One yank and the door opened as if you had pulled the handle yourself. You took a moment to look down and survey the broken pieces that used to hold the door securely, reaching out to you and asking for a swifter death, Your eyes shot back to him as he pulled that door up and yelled. A yell so heavy, so shakily unleashed that the spiderweb cracks in your windshield snaked out into all directions even more chaotically. He tore at that door for a good five minutes. Yanking every little thing he could off its surface, stripping it to the core, and tossing each discarded piece at you with the utmost force. Completely bare, like a beaten child's sore face, the door seemed to scream in agony as he twisted it up, pushed it down and folded all he could to their limit in three quick pushes. '--then I dragged yer gapin' mouthed carcus outta yer bent up car and threw ya against the buildin' behind my shoulders here. It's like ya had wings...' he was smiling a toothy grin '...almost like ya gained the power of flight.' Eerie thoughts cascade over your mind at how close this monster keeps getting to your previous thoughts. Maybe he was crawling around in there and picking your brain while you 'slept'. You shake your head and scatter those thoughts in an instant. Pieces of skull turn again and scrape some more. Another groan frees itself from your body. You try to breathe so you can ignore the pain in your head to concentrate on this thing before you. But those same images keep playing through your head, like you were dreaming and shifting uncontrollably under your cover. You reach up to your bloodied face to hold those stray shavings of your skull steady and shake your head again. You look back up to the man as he shakes his head again at you. Just as he did in the car before making short work of your door. But wait...a piece of the story you played through in your head was missing. 'Sh!t...' You curse aloud to yourself and don't even realize it. 'What the f*ck are ya f*ckin' cussin' bout?' The beastly man looks down at you and sighs, his patience obviously growing thin. Your conscience begins commanding you inside your head. 'Ask the question.' 'What...did you do to me after you pulled the door up?' 'Atta boy.' The man above you breathes in deep, his huge chest rising as he inhales and falling as exhales. 'Ya got me. Just sorta gave me a look like ya messed yerself and I pulled ya out. Then I threw ya.' Maybe the fear had just gotten to you and knew you couldn't take it. Perhaps it was protecting you. You gasp and suddenly have the urge to bring words of apology to this man. The same man that just tore your brand new car apart. The one car you'd saved three years for. The same person who threw you against a wall nearly thirty feet away. The same man you would undoubtedly need surgery to repair your skull. You aren't so sure you want to apologize anymore. But, for some reason, you do it anyway. 'Listen...I'm sorry I hit you...I didn't even see you...' You can feel your blood run cold almost instantly. Something inside you, deep inside you, screams at you for being such an ignoramus. Apologize? What were you thinking? The man steps forward and swoops you up, again with just one hand. He growls and lets out a grunt that vibrates those little fragments of bone in your head as if they were crumbs on a hardwood floor above a noisy neighbor downstairs in your apartment building. You close your eyes as he begins walking, leaving you blind and only with your hearing aiding you. The sickening thud that bellows forth from the contact your back makes with the surface behind you blares out the groan you give at the same moment. Your limbs simultaneously grow He slams you into the wall again. This time, the impact flips one of those chips of bone around and sends it outward, breaking skin and spewing out blood that trickles over your face. The warmth of the liquid almost relieves you of the brisk cold night air. You stay still for a moment, propped up against the wall. After a moment, you gather the courage to open your eyes, apparently the wrong thing to do. The moment you do so, this monster, this thing, this sadistic, sickening, abusive, psychotic creature, trots slowly out into the street. The monster suddenly stops and glares straight at you and opens his mouth. He breathes in a huge breath of fresh Maryland air and pulls you back with his one hand. Your limp body flops like a ragdoll with the quick movement. You try to move, to perhaps futily wiggle your body in hopes of freedom, but you realize you can't. As his arm reaches back as far as it goes, all you can do is close your eyes. The sickening yanking and pulling at your stomach you experience as he whips you forward lasts for only a moment. That cracked windshield gives way immediately when a new body kisses it elegnatly with its backside and pulls you into the front seat of your brand new car. You groan in pain and look up with your eyes, past the redness coating your face. You watch as he leans down for a moment and then stands to full height, arms steadily perched to his sides. The car shifts violently and you know what's happening. You think about your wife, your kids, your brother, mother, and deceased father. You think about your wife and how you married so young. You think about how your father hated her and wanted you to reconsider. You remember how you defied him, married anyway, and took the time to patch things up with him. You shoot back to the wedding and think about her dress, his rage filled eyes, and your mother's pride-filled tears. You think about that first house you earned and that first real job you took to support the two of you. You think about that fetus in your wife's womb developing, three months already, and how you can't wait for it to be brought into this world as your first child. You think about how you wish you had told your wife goodbye this morning and how much you loved her instead of letting her sleep in. You think about how you wished you hadn't stayed late, working on that last car to get more pay so you could afford that crib earlier as a surprise for your wife. Your wife lays in bed at home, three blocks away, thinking of you and waiting for you to return. On the corner of 23rd Street and December Avenue, a brand new 2002 Chrysler Le Baron, turned over and crushing itself under its own weight, shouts into the night.
