Silence

undated

Date: occurs Sunday morning (4/21/02) They couldn't believe it. It wasn't supposed to end this way. The Queen of Extreme tournament was supposed to be Amanda's time to shine. But, instead, all it did was give the critics more to rag on her about. After barely surviving round after round, Amanda was eliminated. How did it happen? How could it happen? I guess some things just aren't meant to be. Now it's the morning after. In a small café in Indianapolis sits the couple. They're just sitting. Sitting at a table. Kid Wonder in his usual 'In Like Flynn' shirt and genie sequin pants. Amanda is in her usual pink tube top and black leather pants. They just sit there. Kid Wonder sits with his head hanging low with a fork in his hand. He stares down at the plate that holds his breakfast. It's one egg, sunny side up, and untouched and a piece of toast. The toast has nothing on it. No butter. No jelly or jam. No nothing despite the fact the said add-ons are right next to his plate. Also, right next to KW's plate is a glass of orange juice. The glass is filled to the top signifying it too has not been touched. Across the table from Kid Wonder is his girlfriend, and recent Queen of Extreme participant, Amanda. On her plate are a couple of hotcakes with maple syrup. It appears a couple of bites have been taken out of one of the hotcakes, but not much. A fork sits on the edge of the plate, out of Amanda's hand. In Amanda's hand is a coffee stirrer. She holds it and stirs her cup of coffee sitting next to the plate. She doesn’t stir it fast or add anything to it. She stirs it in an uninterested sort of fashion. As she does that with her right hand, she puts her left arm onto the table and leans her head against her left hand. All the while, Amanda makes no eye contact with her boyfriend. In the background, other people, mainly older folks who are retired and don’t need to work, eat their breakfast and chitchat to the people around them. Behind the counter, the counter that the people sitting in the stools use and the counter that holds the cash register, a husky man stands. He appears to be in his mid-30s and is wearing a white apron over his clothes. The man slides a menu down to a man sitting in a stool. The man stands there. Quietly. Watching his celebrity guests in their depressing looking state. The worker wants to say something, but doesn’t. Instead, he just watches. He watches and waits to see what, if anything, the couple does. At the table, the two just sit there. They sit there with thoughts going through their minds. Thoughts of pain? Thoughts of failure? Thoughts of loss? Thoughts of revenge? It’s too hard to tell with the expressionless looks on their faces and the uninterested approaches they seem to have this morning. And then, Kid Wonder slowly rises his head and looks at Amanda. He stares at her for a few seconds. She doesn’t stare back. K-Dub opens his mouth, as if he’s about to say something, but doesn’t. Instead, the young man just sighs. Kid Wonder stares back down at the plate. The yolk from his egg starts to drain onto the plate as KW pokes it with his fork. The yolk drains. It drains like Kid Wonder’s body in his never-ending quest for vengeance. It drains, and drains, and drains until it starts running off the plate and onto the table. Amanda looks up. She looks at her boyfriend. She then looks at the yolk from the egg running onto the table. And while looking at these things, she drops her coffee stirrer into the coffee. Amanda looks at her coffee, then looks back down. Simultaneously, they sigh sighs of despair with only the man behind the counter seemingly interested. Fade out
