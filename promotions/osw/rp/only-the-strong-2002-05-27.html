Only the strong.

27 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 27th, 2002 She had lost the match that would have lead to a Cruiserweight title shot. She had lost the match that would have lead to a Cruiserweight title shot because of the ever-meddling Bethany Yamato. She had beaten Yamato up afterwords...but was she pleased? Take a guess. Of course not! Nothing was helping her, either. She'd saved ZaC as soon as the opportunity arose on Slam, but nothing ever seemed to please Davis... This was evident the moment she stepped into the animal hospital. ''Uhh...hey...Reno.'' Reno nodded slightly, his gaze fixated on the floor. Now normally, a response (or lack thereof) like that would have been countered with some smartass remark from Amy. Instead, she was simply confused... ''Ummm......o...kay...'' She looked at him for a moment, his face blank, and then recalled the beating he took on Slam. His attitude made a little more sense then, though not by much. ''How're you feeling?'' He shrugged, not even taking a moment to glance. ''Been better.'' ''Sorry about ZaC and all of this crap...'' Amy ran a hand through loose red locks, it's not like all of this was a picnic to her. ''...what are the vets saying?'' Reno began to speak, but his throat cut out and he merely shook his head. Not a good sign...seeing someone like Davis getting choked up really alarmed Aimz...ZaC obviously wasn't doing too well. Then again...who would be? ''Fuck...is he gonna be alright?!'' ''I dunno,'' Reno said, shaking his head and shrugging his shoulders in slight annoyance. ''Uhh..okay....'' She paused a moment, carefully wording the next sentence. ''...look, I know you're not in the best of moods right now...but ZaC's condition doesn't seem to be the only thing bothering you...'' At that moment, he finally looked up...but with a frightfully distraught glare. ''I just ran into someone from my past who seems to want to kill me, Ivan just crippled and most likely killed my best friend, this guy who wants me dead now knows who you are and will probably try to hurt you as well, all because you just couldn't stay away from that damn protest like I asked. Mikey's arm is really hurt and he has to take time off wrestling, which has him depressed and Frank can't seem to keep him occupied. None of that would of happened had it not been for me. I'd say a little bit is bothering me.'' ''Ouch...I think I've got the picture now.'' Reno had just about snapped at Amy, yet she kept a very calm presence...though slightly annoyed that Davis had to bring up her 'disobeying' him. ''But..uhh...about the protest...you asking me wasn't exactly about to keep me away...I have this tendancy not to listen to people, and you're not exactly my keeper.'' ''Never said I was, just asked you not to go so that what happened to you, wouldn't happen,'' Reno stood up, a fire in his eyes, ''I have to go...take...'' He choked again. ''...take care of ZaC for me. I can't bear to be here...'' He bumped her shoulder slightly when he left, not even looking back. His head hung low, and his pace was even more sullen...but he was gone, Davis didn't like to show a whole lot of outward emotion, to Amy's estimation...and he'd run in time to avoid doing just that. He ran...once again. It seemed to be a pattern with Reno...he ran from very little in a fight, but for some reason the man couldn't stand to actually open up to someone. Unfortunately enough, Aimz didn't have the luxury to sit there and dwell on the subject at the time...she had a monkey to see. Before she could step to the desk, though, a woman in a white coat strode out from behind it. Her name tag reading 'Dana Power', the woman who appeared to be a vet of some sort placed a hand on Amy's shoulder, brushing past flowing red hair to do so. She simply smiled, her face warm as she said, ''Don't you worry about a thing. I saw that entire episode right there...'' Power nodded her head slightly toward the door. ''We'll take good care of ZaC...you need to go take care of yourself.......and somebody else.'' Amy shook her head a little, glancing momentarily at the woman's hand. Even though she knew Dana had good intention, only trying to reassure her, Campbell was naturally uncomfortable being touched by strangers. ''...ummm...what?'' ''Reno there...'' The vet was about fifty, maybe. ''...he's a man, you know...obviously he can't take care of himself.'' She had chuckled, managing to draw a little grin from the taller redhead. ''Beleive me, he doesn't want my help with anything....'' ''No no...you beleive ME, honey...'' She reminded Amy of her old boss, a restaraunt owner who used to treat her like a daughter...and, coincidentally, almost always picked on her playfully regarding the opposite sex. ''...he wants a lot more than you think, he just won't admit it...even to himself. He's a big, strong man...remember? According to big, strong men, they don't need any help.'' Power winked, ''Thank the good Lord we big, strong women are here to smack some brains into 'em...eh?'' ''Uhh...I guess...'' That wasn't reassuring Campbell at all, she still couldn't help but think about how upset Reno was. ''...but you'll take care of him for me, will you?'' ''It's my job...just write a number where you can be reached, we've already got Reno's cell phone number.'' ''Alright...'' She grabbed a little sliver of scrap paper from the desk and, taking a half-chewed pen from her pocket, quickly scratched her cell number down in that classic, uncaring scrawl. ''There...I've got some stuff to pick up, but I'll be back within the next two hours to check on ZaC. I'm sure you'll see Reno sometime soon...I don't honestly beleive those two can be seperated for very long.'' ''Good. If anything changes, you'll be informed.'' ''Okay.'' Amy nodded, turning on her heel to leave. But she paused a moment, and looked over her shoulder, back at the veternarian. ''Look...if Reno comes back...give me a call, 'kay?'' The woman smiled again, this time even wider than before, ''Absolutely.'' ''Thanks.'' When Amy finally left, she was headed to shop .For what exactly, God only knows...but she definitely needed to clear her head, and pick up a few survival essentials (Like food) that she so often neglected.
