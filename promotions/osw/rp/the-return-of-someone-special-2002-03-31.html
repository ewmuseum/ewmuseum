The Return of Someone...'Special'

31 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/31/02 “Lockdown!” The guards of San Quentin Prison call out as they stand near the barred door, sealing them from the underworld of the life most honest people fear. Alone in the dank cell she sat alone, her eyes blazing with hatred for those that wrought this upon her. She vowed revenge, she vowed to get out, she vowed to make them all pay, to be at the top again. This time she would be back, and better than ever. She would find him, and train him harder than ever, there would be no stopping, but damn these bars! The prison life was cold, and for a beautiful woman like her, it was no place to live. She had spent her life teaching the boy, telling him right from wrong, or at least her deluded version of it. She knew in her heart he was out there calling for her, waiting for her to come back to him. The poor guy sat alone, crying on a single cot, made large especially for his size, his eyes welled with tears, his nose running. Every night it was the same, and he missed her. It wasn’t her fault, she was the most wonderful woman on earth. She would take him in when nobody else would bat an eye at the poor monstrous manchild. He looked up as there was a tapping of feet coming down the hall of the foster home. He looked to the window, and saw a faint image of a tall man with a young lady next to him, they talked briefly outside of the room, and that voice, it was so familiar. “Now are you sure about this? He may remember you, and lord knows what he will do without his mother to control him.” “I know the risks, but I don’t see any other way to do this. To train for a monster you need to get a monster, and this was the biggest one of my life.” “I can’t allow you to hurt him, you know that.” “I won’t hurt him, in fact I plan not to. No, I will be the one who is getting hurt.” Inside the room, the voices frightened the manchild, he pulled his covers up around his head and cried, sobbing, confused, angry. This voice was so familiar, and his clouded eyes could not discern the form through the frosted glass. He sat alone, and a tear rolled down his cheek. He was lonely. He was scared. He wanted…her. She sat in her cell, on a cot that was two springs short of comfortable, looking to the sky. Her boy was out there, sitting alone in his room, taken from her. She cursed as she looked up, then pulled out a small knife she had received from one of the inmates not long ago. She scraped it against the wall, and it made a rasping sound that echoed through the prison, but nobody would notice. Late at night she could never sleep, driven by the name of her son, her sole joy in life that was taken from her. She carved it upon the wall, intricately the letters would slowly mark the walls forever. “E”. The Samoan Silencer stood outside of the room of the foster house, his partner in crime, Amy “Aimz” Campbell looking on, not knowing exactly what the enigmatic Samoan was up to. Timo looked up at the door and grabbed the knob, letting out a smooth relaxed breath, hoping this would go the way he wanted. She blew against the wall and a small dust cloud formed, rising up and making her cough a bit. He pretty features were soiled, and wrinkles of worry had befallen her nearly perfect face. She took a deep breath and looked out the window, hoping maybe her boy was safe, not knowing that he was about to be in the capable hands of his worst enemy. Slowly she carved another letter in the wall, smiling as she did. Nothing pleased her so much as the thought of her child. The blade made a semi graceful underhook arch , graceful in her hands, as everything was. The dim light made her smile as she finished carving. “U”. Timo turned the handle and the door squeaked a bit as it opened. He looked inside, his eyes taking a moment to adjust to the dim light of the room, then he saw him sitting there. The manchild was waiting for him, his huge legs swung over the side of the bed, his eyes squinting to see who was in the doorway. “What are you going to do Timo?” It was Aimz speaking. “I don’t know just yet, but I know this might be my only chance.” “I hope for your sake that you are right.” She replied. San Quentin was cold, and the night chilled her to the bone. She refused to play their game, to let them break her and turn her into the model citizen. She was smarter than they were, and knew it. The most dangerous thing though, is a complex mind. Her knife blade dug into the wall again, carving out another letter as she did. “G” “Timo I don’t like the look of this…its just not right!” “I can handle myself, okay Amy? I know this guy, and if I am right, he will be rational…well I hope anyhow.” The monster stood to his full height of well over seven feet and clenches his fists. He knew the man standing before him. They were old pals, one might say. He simply looked down to the much smaller Samoan and growled deep and low, his chest rumbling as he did. “Timo, let’s get out of here!” “NO!” She looked out the window suddenly, something in the air had sparked her mind. She got up and went to the window, looking out. The cool air stung her pretty face, and whipped her hair around silently. It struck her in the face, but she did not care, she could feel it on the wind, something was happening, something was happening to him. She walked back to her cot and sat down. The only thing she could do was pray for the boy she loved, and remember his name in the epitaph she carved in the unforgiving stone wall. “E”. “Do you remember me?” “Timo…” “Quiet Amy, this will work, I promise.” The official from the foster home placed his hand upon the Samoans shoulder, making him spin around. He looks at the man sternly then shrugs the shoulder off. He flashes a million dollar smile and turns around to be met with the chest of the monstrous boy they call… “My poor son, where have you gone? What have they done to you?” She looks at the cold steel bars in the locked down prison and for the first time since her arrest, tears fall down her cheeks. She tries to wipe them away, but they flow, releasing the anger and pain inside, the torment that had welled itself up in her heart. The prison clothing she wore soon became the catch all for the tears, her cold reminder of the better life. “I will find you.” She dug the knife into the wall spitefully, full of anger and rage to those that had did this to her. She drew the blade across the stone, ripping and rending the very essence of thousands of years of nature into dust. She cursed the name of Tyler Nelson as she shredded another letter into the wall. “N”. “YOU!” Timo looked up into the monsters eyes, seeing moments of hate, pain, glory, rage, and fear all at once. These eyes scared Timo, as not much can, because they were so raw, so real. He had never backed off from this man in his life, and he was not about to start. “Wait! Don’t do anything stupid!” “I NOT STUPID! I SPECIAL!!” The brute grabs Timo by the throat and lifts him high in the air so they meet eye to eye. He stares down the choking man, the rage contorting his face into a wicked amalgam of hatred and fear. Timo grabbed for his hands and pried them off just far enough to speak. “I have come….to help you….and your mother…” Immediately the monster drops Timo and sits down wailing like a small child. Aimz looks on at Timo in general disbelief, then at the crying man who is huge. She knew Timo was fully aware of what he was doing, she just didn’t know why. “Come on bud, lets go get her.” Timo reached his hand out, and the monstrous manchild looked up and reached for it. He was so scared and confused, but if his mother would be part of this deal, he would not miss it for the world. He only wondered if she still thought about him. She sat alone, the wind picked up, on it was a small ray of hope, a bit of moonlight. Her tears dried as she looked at the wall and smiled, then carved the last letter. “Oh my lovely Eugene, we will be together again…and you will be Unstoppable. This…I…Promise…”
