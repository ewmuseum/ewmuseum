Date: 8/22/02 And so, our three heroes stood at the end of their quest. “Just a pleasant journey home, indeed!” said Sir Porter with a sarcastic smirk. Sir Kriegman smiled to his friend, and nodded. “Well I suppose there HAVE been a few…minor detours.” he chuckled in reply. The Dark Lord and his vile queen had been defeated; banished to the realm which they sought to unleash upon the land. The Five Stars had been returned to their rightful places once and for all. The Rift was now permanently closed, never again to threaten Tattlia with “Total Violation”. The kingdom Old Schoolidor would go on to usher in a new age of peace and prosperity. The three knights of the Think Tank stood once again on the path that would take them back home to Cramalot. In fact, it was the very same spot on the path where their unexpected adventure had began so long ago. The old signpost still stood off to the side of the road, as did the ancient elm tree that still threatened to come crashing down and make travel much harder. “I suppose, then…” Sir Dorkiman added, grinning to his fellow knights, “that perhaps we should resume our ‘pleasant journey’ home. Hopefully there’s no more trouble to be found on this path.” “I should hope not!” exclaimed Howard. “I beleve I’ve had just enough trouble for one ride home.” Sir Kriegman dismounted his horse and walked over to the old signpost, which he and his friends had initially disregarded. He brushed off the moss and dried mud that had accumulated over the years, and carefully read the faded message on it. He cackled. “What does it say?” asked Howard as he watched Alan laugh. Still chuckling, the knight in black stood aside and allowed the others to read: --TRAVEL CAREFULLY ~ DANGER AHEAD-- “I TOLD you we should have stopped to see what it said!” cried Herbert as Alan climbed back onto his horse. “Yes, yes… Come, my friends. I shall be more mindful of roadside warnings from now on!” The End. It was finally completed. Alan Kriegman clicked the ‘save’ button one last time on his laptop and closed it shut. It’s not a typical feat for an Evil Supergenius to write a fantasy novel, but Alan Kriegman had just done it. Getting MediEVIL By Alan Kriegman His first attempt at fantasy, and perhaps one of the greater intellectual challenges he had ever faced. He pushed back in his chair and kicked up his feet on the desk, taking a deep breath of satisfaction. A smirk crossed his face. “Not bad, Krieggy… You really can do it all.” he said to himself. In the time he and his Think Tank comrades had taken over OSW by force, Alan had begun to dabble in creative writing. Just as a mental exercise. That dabbling led to an interest, and that interest led to a fantasy novel. We all have our hobbies, after all. Alan sat there at his desk, overwhelmingly proud of the finished work before him. His thoughts soon began to drift back to the Think Tank’s siege of Old School Wrestling, and to the epic 12 Man Tag Match this week on Slam! Yet for some reason, he couldn’t concentrate on what would surely be an epic battle that could prove to be a staggering blow to their dominance if lost. Instead, he could only think about the contents of his laptop – something he had worked harder at than anything he could remember. It was his pride and joy…and it would stay with him until publishing rights had been secured. It would come with him to Slam.
