Date: 6/19/02 Eternal the kiss I breathe Sucked in your blood to me Feel my wounds of your God Forever reign immortality Frankie stares hard at the punching bag, his body drenched in sweat. 'Eyes on the prize' so to speak, Frankie has decided it's time to stop the bullshit... I smell of death I reek of hate I will live forever Lost child, pain of death Bleeding screams of silence In my veins your eternity His mind blurry with rage. His eyes fogged over with vengeance. But the path is the clearest it's ever been... I'll kill you and your dreams tonight Begin new life Bleed your death upon me Let your Bloodline feed my youth His knuckles are a mangled bloody mess. His eyes pierce the punching bag. He sees no bag, he sees the face of Sebastian Toomes. His knuckles spread blood over the bag, his fever pitch grows faster still... First breath 'fore I come alive Learn to kill Blood thirst the ways you feed your hunger Dark shy has no rival test your faith in blood Nightime as hunting packs of feeding frenzy The barrage of punches finally stop. Frankie stands, breathing heavily, glaring at the bag... I'll kill you and your dreams tonight Begin new life Bleed your death upon me Let your Bloodline feed my youth The tattered bag sways back and forth. Dripping with blood and sweat, stuffing floats slowly through the air. The rage may finally have subsided... I am the first after last Commune by a single kiss Betray eternally I'll rip inside your soul Contaminating the world Dividing Godless sun He stands, and he returns. The rage boiling like a deep fryer in his soul. He stands, eyes squinting, teeth showing, and low, guttural growl can be heard. He holds it back, letting it build... Black art to face your death There will be a hit for me Lead - King Take the flesh of life itself Prepare to reign a thousand years Finally, the rage takes over, and he explodes. Explodes into the most devastating force he can muster. One punch. One perfect, powerful punch. The chain on the punching bag snaps like a twig and the bag flies across the room... I'll kill you and your dreams tonight Begin new life Bleed your death upon me Let your Bloodline feed my youth Bleed your death upon me Bleed your death upon me Bleed your death upon me Let your Bloodline feed my youth He slowly walks from the room, a sly smirk curls his lips... (credits: 'Bloodline' by Slayer)
