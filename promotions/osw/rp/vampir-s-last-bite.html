Vampir's last bite.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Easter Sunday 'So then they came for him as he prayed in the garden.' We hear from the soothing southern accent, the scene fades in from a pitch black to see Eric Sagar in his trademark grey suit, grey tie and white shirt. Sagar is sitting on a brown wooden chair. Behind him is the power himself; Propaganda, wearing his usual ring attire. The two are talking to a bunch of school children that do not really look that interested. The children are all gathered round sitting cross-legged on the floor, Sagar is leaning forward telling them as his shiny leather shoes let of a high squeak on the floor. There are a few giggles from the children as Eric Sagar lifts his head up from the new hard-backed 'Ladybirds New Testament Tales.' Sagar does not look happy at the children. Propaganda just shakes his head as he is leaned up against the cream wall. But just an inch away from the budding OSW prospect is a window shining on his elbow and through too the light grey hair of Sagar, which in the light looks a shade of gold. 'Listen children this is your lord and saviour we're talking about.' Sagar bargains. 'The Messiah.' Propaganda remarks. 'Indeed' Sagar agrees. 'Anyway, where were we?' Sagar hypothetically asks, 'Ah yes.' Sagar remembers with the aid of his thumb placed in the book. 'So the Romans came for him as he prayed in the garden. The disciples drew their swords to defend their master, Jesus. But Jesus wanting peace throughout told them not to attack.' Then we here a big gasp from a child on the front row. Sagar angrily glares at this little boy wearing grey shorts, white shirt and brown suspenders and shabby brown sandals. 'What now?' Sagar growls. 'Mr. Sagar sir.' The little boy innocently asks. 'Yes?' Sagar patient's runs thin. 'Well if you and Mr. Propagananada--' 'It's Propaganda.' The Power Himself exclaims. 'Propaganda.' 'Anyway you were saying little boy.' Sagar interrupts. 'Yes, well, if you two are Christians and Mr. Jesus says no violence then why are you two in wrestling.' 'Well I'll tell you all later little boy.' Sagar sighs. 'DO YOU KNOW TIMO BOLAMBO?' Another child enthusiastically asks while shooting his arm up in the air. 'Yes.' Sagar grumbles. 'COOL! HE'S MY FAV--' The little boy is interrupted 'I'M CONTINUING WITH THE STORY!' Sagar yells over the boy. 'Well they came for him and took him back to the court of the Romans. Illegally, in the night they threw a trial, an unfair trial may I add. And they found Jesus guilty! So they sentenced him to crucifixion. But Pilot reluctantly humiliated him in public. Then sent him for crucifixion. He was sent up to the hill to carry his cross but one man helped him as Jesus was weak. He got nailed to the cross and jeered by the crowds. But he said upon to his father, God 'Forgive them farther for they know not what they do.' This proves a point my brothers and sisters.' Sagar preaches to the children. 'It proves that despite peoples ignorance stupidity is at the heart. So Saturday Slam! Propaganda over here shall take Vampir Nosferatu--' 'Tell the children about what Vampir Nosferatu does.' Propaganda requests. 'Tell the poor children that he'd rather spread vicious lies about his pretend idealism of Satan! Then worship God and live his life through purity and righteousness. And after I beat him until he almost meets his idol that is Lucifer and destroy him in front of his scum bag followers he calls 'fans.' Propaganda sniggers 'I will show him that like Jesus requested you are forgiven. So after I destroy you I will let you join me, say no to your wrongs and see the errors of your ways. But I have fans,' Propaganda adds. 'I have all my brothers and sisters, God, Jesus and the Holly ghost and of course all of you right?' The Children look at Propaganda blank faced. 'Never mind' Sagar interrupts the awkward situation. 'Does anyone have question about their faith in Jesus?' Sagar looks around the confused kids. 'Work with me here children!' Sagar pleas. Then slowly one kid raises his hand. 'Yes-- You.' Sagar says pointing to the child who has just raised his hand. 'Well… Ya' know Jesus?' The boy asks, Sagar nods awaiting the question 'Well does he, kind of like let you have sweets?' Sagar sighs and drops his head in his hands shaking it. 'Oh Dear' Sagar weeps he slowly draws his head up 'Yes, Yes he does young child.' Sagar says with little confidence.' 'Cool.' The Child expresses his joy. 'Ugh Mr. Sagar sir.' A kid with an oriental origin of him requests attention as he raises his hand. 'How do you feel about different religions, say Buddhism or Muslims?' 'Uhhh…' Sagar starts to sweat. He looks to Propaganda 'I'll let Propaganda answer this.' Sagar attempts escape from the question. 'Umm…' Propaganda 'I'd love to answer this but we're all out of time.' 'No you have twenty one minutes le--' 'No, We're out of time!' Propaganda hastily adds. 'Yes Children. Goodbye.' Sagar bids farewell. Both men walk out of the classroom with pace. Then shoot the door as they begin to speak. 'You know, my brother that was another bad plan to get us more fans.' Propaganda expresses. 'I know, I know. It's just Casey did it and he seemed to get more respect-- But no worries for when you finally destroy Vampir Nosferatu on Slam there shall be a lot more respect heading your way.' 'A-men.' Propaganda adds. 'A-men.' Sagar agrees.
