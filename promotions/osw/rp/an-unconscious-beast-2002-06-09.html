An Unconscious Beast

9 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/9/02 The sun has fallen again and given way to the dark night sky. The sky is filled with stars and the air has cooled down slightly. Not many people are out at this time of night. In fact the majority of lights inside people’s homes have been shut off for the night. Only the streetlights and billboards light the streets. Two figures emerge in front of the falling down gothic cathedral that Azrael Cain and Caleb call home. The cathedral rises high into the air. There are no lights coming from this building either except for a very faint flickering coming from the main hall of the church. The two figures begin to mover closer to the cathedral with unsure footsteps. At a closer look, the two figures are revealed to be none other that Hessian and Minion the imp. The massive goliath that is Hessian towers over his much smaller companion. The two exchange a look. “It just figures that you would insist on looking for Cain in a whore house first instead of this cathedral!” Hessian states with a tone of annoyance in his voice. Minion immediately gets defensive. “What? I thought that the big goon might have wanted to get his rocks off!” Hessian rolls his eyes and slowly places his right gauntleted hand up against the heavy wooden door and pushes the door open slowly. The hinges of the door creek loudly as the door fights to stay closed. Finally, Hessian slides his head in the door to take a look around for his tag partner. Minion’s high-pitched voice can be heard from behind Hessian as he tries to stealthily look for the missing Cain. “What do you see? Huh? Is he there? Let me see! Come on, is he in there or what?” The Goliath quickly spins around to silence the little imp. “Will you hold on a second a let me see? I didn’t even get a chance to see anything before you started to run your incessant mouth!” “Right. Sorry.” Minion replies sheepishly as Hessian peaks back into the cathedral. Hessian’s green eyes grow wide as he sees the cathedral in an even bigger state of ruin then usual. Many of the pews are shattered and splintered in millions of pieces on the floor. Many of the candles sticks have been ruined obviously from being thrown against the walls. The couple of candlesticks that remain intact are lit down by the alter. Sitting up on the alter is the decrepit old man that the missing Azrael Cain call his manager, Caleb. Hessian throws the door open wider and walks in being followed by Minion. Hessian immediately tries to get the hobo’s attention. “Caleb! Where the hell have you been? Where in the hell is Cain and what is going on with him?” The hobo turns quickly. His eyes are wide with shock. “What the hell…oh…Heh-Heh. Good to see you guys. Heh-Heh. We’ve been here and Cain…well…erm…Heh…he’s right here. Heh-Heh-Heh.” Caleb gestures to the floor behind the alter. Hessian’s eyes grow even wider than before as he walk around to the other side of the alter only to see his the other half of the tag team champions laying unconscious on the floor. “What happened to him? Why is he unconscious?” Hessian asks in a total state of shock. Caleb begins to respond. “I’m not exactly sure what happened. Heh-Heh. We just got back here from the card and everything seemed normal. Heh. So I went out and got us some food and when I got back he was in a total rage! Heh. He was destroying everything he could get his hands on and screaming about not allowing himself to be betrayed again. Heh-Heh. And then the next thing I know he collapses right here on the floor. He hasn’t moved since. Heh-Heh.” “Oh and that’s normal.” Minion responds to the story sarcastically. “No something is wrong. Heh. Watch.” Caleb grabs hold of a chicken bone that he was gnawing on and tosses it at Cain. The leg bone smacks the unconscious beast in the forehead, but there is no response from the massive figure on the floor. “Hey that’s great let me try!” Minion states very excitedly as he reaches for a chicken leg out of the Styrofoam container and flings it Cain. The leg smacks Cain in the face, but again, there is no movement from the mighty marked beast. Minion immediately goes for another leg, but is stopped by Hessian’s arm smacking the imp to the side of the head. “How long has he been like this, Caleb?” Hessian’s voice is filled with concern. Caleb’s eyes are still fixed on Cain as he answers. “Not really sure. Heh-Heh. If I had to guess, I would say that he has been out cold for probably thirty minutes. Heh-Heh.” Hessian crouches down by Cain and begins to examine him closely. After a moment or two of not finding anything physically wrong with Cain, the Goliath rises to his feet again. “There is something definitely going on here. Cain has not been himself for the past couple of weeks. He is never around and even when he is, he still seems like he is some place else. And now this. What the hell is going on with him, Caleb? He’s not been himself at all!” Caleb leaps down from the alter excitedly. “You’ve noticed that too. Heh. He’s been very distant with me too. Heh. I just thought he was still bitter about that whole contract thing, but last week I began to think that it was something else. Heh-Heh. It’s almost as if the lights are on, but no one is home. Heh-Heh-Heh.” Hessian begins to scratch at his chin in confusion at the entire situation as he continues to try and put it all together. “And now you say that he was screaming about being betrayed again. Something is a foot, but why the hell would he just collapse like this if he were still enraged about something? This isn’t right at all.” “I always said that there was something not right with him.” Minion chimes in again. Caleb and Hessian turn quickly to glare at the imp who is making light of the situation. Hessian looks on again the unconscious beast who is still not moving except for the bobbing from his breathing. Caleb starts to scratch his greasy gray balding hair that lies on the top of his head. “So…what the hell do we do now? Heh-Heh. I mean, it seems that nothing is going to wake him up. Heh-Heh.” Caleb asks with concern in his voice. Hessian slumps down onto the floor near Cain and continues to stare at the beast with a very concerned look on his face. “I guess we just sit here and wait. Hopefully he’ll come around soon and we can get some damn answers as to what the hell is going on with him.” Minion looks frustrated with this decision that has been made for him. “Oh, that just great. This could take a while. So what the hell do we do until then?” Hessian answers without removing his eyes from Cain. “We can sit here and try and figure out what he meant by ‘I will not let myself be betrayed again.’ We also need to start planning for this week’s Slam, especially if Cain is out of commission.
