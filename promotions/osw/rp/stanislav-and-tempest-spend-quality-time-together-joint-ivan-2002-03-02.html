Stanislav and Tempest spend 'Quality Time' together (Joint Ivan/Tempest rp)

2 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-2-02 There are varying degrees of anger that individuals can go through. Some people are very good at controlling themselves and keeping their aggression in check, others are less than capable of succeeding at such a task…. For one Ivan Sergeiovich Stanislav, anger management, believe it or not, is one of his strengths. When Ivan Stanislav kicked the pregnant Brandi in the stomach, he was just a little peeved. When he broke Ed’s back, he was a little….upset. When Ivan Stanislav pushed a Ming-driven forklift against a wall and collapsed it around him, he was slightly irked. To be honest, it is a feat in itself to push Ivan beyond angry into a rage, it is something that can rarely be done. And just as Stanislav is physically, it is very difficult to find just how to drive Stanislav into such a rage. Pride is a big one, The Russian Federation is another, and perhaps getting personal is a third. However, Stanislav knows how to ward off such attacks. Ivan does not listen to the beliefs of others relating his country, and his pride is so powerful that it would take someone that he holds in very high esteem to really get under his skin, and Stanislav keeps his personal life extremely well guarded….. ….or does he? When Ivan heard of the fallout from fWo Body Count, and the announcement by Monte Burns, a capitalist businessman that he had feelings for Tempest, a woman that has succeeded in getting closer to Ivan than almost any other female in the world, he instantly was flung into a random rage. And woe to the hotel room that Stanislav was staying in, because it took the brunt of his rage…. The camera fades up on Ivan Stanislav’s hotel room. The large couch is ripped in two, a huge fist hole is punched through the big screen TV. The glass end-table is nothing more than shards of shattered glass now. The two large plants that were in the corners of the square room are now impaled in one of the white plaster walls. Two large lines running from the ceiling to the floor on one all can be seen, probably created from Ivan as he punched his arms into the wall and pulled down, destroying a line of the wall. A chandelier lays in a mangled heap atop a pile of splinters that once was a bookshelf. Several large holes have been punched into the large sliding glass door that leads to the balcony of the room, and books can be seen scattered all over the area. A recliner has had it’s back torn off, and reduced to a loveseat….that now has a huge boot hole through the top of it. “RRRRAAAAAARRRRRRGGGHHHHH!!!!” The roar of Ivan Stanislav blasts our ears as an entire hutch flies through the air and crashes into a wall, shattering all over the place and sending dishes flying! Stanislav hulks into the room in his typical dress: Black t-shirt with the hammer and sickle over his heart, red suspenders, black pants, black boots, and a look of anger that we have seen many times before. Stanislav’s clothing has dust all over it, and plaster sticking to the arms. Ivan’s fists drip blood from the bludgeoning blows brought against the furniture. Sweat pours off of Stanislav’s brow as he rushes and kicks the glass door, shattering it….. Elsewhere…. Alexei Ruslan, Ivan Stanislav’s most trusted friend, runs down the hallway to Ivan’s room. Ruslan had gotten an urgent call from a contact in the area telling him that Ivan would have to be calmed down. No one else had the guts to go and attempt the seemingly impossible task, but Alex Ruslan was up to the challenge. Charging down the hallway, Ruslan breathes heavily. His brown trench coat whips behind him while he holds his hat securely on his head. His footsteps echo down the hallway as the roaring of The Russian Bear is heard clearer and clearer. Alexei stops just outside of Ivan’s door and listens, hearing the tell tale signs of an angry Ivan Stanislav. Ruslan slowly opens the door, just as a pot flies and hits the wall right next to his head! Alexei yelps and dives into the room. The camera pans out, showing Ivan Stanislav head butting a wall with his back to Alexei. Alexei slinks into the room and clears his throat in an attempt to get Ivan’s attention…but it’s futile. Alexei thinks for a moment and whispers, “Ivan?” Ivan does not respond, save for bringing his fist down atop the huge big screen TV and buckling it into nothing. “Ivan.” Still not response. “IVAN!” Ivan spins, breathing heavily, and looks at Alexei. Ruslan stands there with his hands in his pockets and looks around the area. “Uh, redecorating, Praporschik?” Alexei chuckles. Ivan shakes his head. “You could say something like that, Alexei Gregorovich. Let us say that I am making the room more suitable for living!” Barks Stanislav as he kicks some glass out of his way. “Ivan, what is the matter? I got an urgent call saying that you had to be taken under control! The police are surely going to be here soon!” Alexei walks closer to Ivan, speaking in a more scolding tone, “What do you think that you are doing? You have to remain under control! What is the matter!?” Ivan stares at Alexei for a moment and grunts. “I received word from Kremlin regarding one “Monte Burns” who appears to be a capitalist businessman in fWo.” Alexei nods, “Yes Ivan Sergeiovich, I know about this Burns fellow….” Ivan growls and grabs the arm of a nearby chair, crushing it to nothing. “I heard about him….courting Tempest, and for some strange reason, it bothers me very much.” Alexei nods and kicks some wood out of his way, “Why do you think it bothers you, Ivan?” Ivan shrugs, “I do not know, I do not know why Alexei. But I do know one thing, I am shocked and appalled at HER actions!” Alexei shakes his head, dumbfounded, “What do you mean Ivan??” “She meets this pig and instantly falls for him! He seduces her and turns her against the communist way of thinking…and…..and….” Ivan pauses for a moment and kicks the chair out of the way, “Other things that I will not discuss.” “But Ivan…Tempest….” Alexei begins to say, but is cut off. “I thought she was a different person than the other weak hearted Americans! I have done research on Burns and he is nothing more than an exploiter of those below him! Bourgeois in every form! I will find this man and I will crush him for this…” Ivan thinks for a moment while Alexei tries to interject. “But Ivan..I was trying to say….” “I do not care who he thinks he is! I will crush him, destroy him for tainting another possible communist!!” Ivan growls, now stomping the shattered wood into the floor. “IVAN!” Screams Alexei in an attempt to finally get Ivan’s attention. He pauses and looks at Alexei. “Ivan, she turned him down, she does not like him!” Ivan shakes his head for a moment and raises an eyebrow, “She turned him down? She does not like him.” “Da, Praporschik.” Ivan puts his hands on his hips and thinks. “Hmph…that changes everything…” says Stanislav aloud to himself, amazingly calm. Alexei leans closer to Ivan and speaks softer, “The police are on their way to look into this situation with the room, Praporschik.” Alexei kicks some plaster out of the way, “You know Praporschik, she is in the area.” Ivan whirls around, “She is?” “Da, she is. She is going to be at Saturday Slam, so we are all in very close proximity of one another.” Ivan thinks for a second, “Perhaps Ivan should go and see her, no?” Alexei blinks and looks at Ivan, catching him. The two look at one another for about five seconds before Ivan blurts out, “…so that Ivan can congratulate her on her win…” Alexei crosses his arms over his chest. Ivan continues to stammer, “Since she is devoted communist, it would be nice to do…that is all.” The two stare again. Alexei nods, “That would be nice…” “Yes it would!!” Ivan shrugs, “But I will go later, I am in no hurry to see her….” Ivan looks around and shrugs. He puts his hands in his pockets while Alexei continues to stare at him. “Yes, that would be nice of me, but I will go later.” Ivan taps his toe and looks at the floor. He quickly looks up at Alexei, “Maybe I should just go now….” Alexei cuts him off, “Yes, maybe you should!” “Just because, I do not want her to not be present in her room then, or anything like that! I really would like to congratulate her!” “Of course you do Ivan! Of course you do!” Ivan pauses and stares at Alexei again, after another sizeable pause he speaks up, “Well then, I think maybe I shall go. If you could, handle the police when they arrive.” “But of course Ivan! But of course! Do not worry about it. Go and congratulate her.” Alexei smiles as Ivan dusts himself off and looks around. Ivan walks close to Alexei and whispers under his breath, “Does Ivan look okay? Is my hair fine?” Alexei chuckles, “You are fine Praporschik, go and see her.” “Da.” Ivan nods to himself, thinking aloud, “Da, I will! I will see you later Alexei, make arrangements for a new hotel room!” Alexei nods as Ivan heads out of the room. Alexei turns and watches Ivan leave, he chuckles to himself and nods towards where Ivan left. “Yes Ivan, go and see her, heh.” Alexei shakes his head as the camera fades out…. The camera fades back up on Ivan Stanislav as he walks down the hallway of another hotel and stops at a door and looks at it. He stares at it for a moment and then turns to leave, catching himself at the last possible moment and turning to the door again. 'Ivan Sergeiovich, pull yourself together.' Ivan mutters. Ivan shakes his head to himself and raises his huge hand up to the door and knocks loudly three times, shaking the entire door. He rolls his eyes and looks to the ceiling as he waits, muttering something in Russian to himself. After a few moments the door opens slowly and Tempest peers out blearily. She blinks several times at Ivan's midsection her eye level and says, 'Wow, you're awfully buff for a pizza delivery guy.' Ivan blinks and looks around, 'Uh...no, I am not....' Ivan clears his throat and rubs the back of his huge neck, 'Tempest, Ivan thought maybe he would uh...stop by to see you, since we are both within close proximity of one another.' Ivan swallows. Tempest's gaze follows the unmistakable voice up and she smiles a little. 'Oh hi Ivan! Yeah c'mon in.' She opens the door and steps back, a little wobbly, and dressed uncharacteristically in a soft loose baby blue shirt and dark blue cargoes. Her feet are covered in a pair of Winnie the Pooh socks. Ivan swallows again and walks into the room, smacking his head off the top of the doorframe and knocking it out, 'AH!' grunts Ivan as he rubs his head. Tempest sighs and says, 'Ah nice to see you again, good thing I don't own the property this time.' Ivan grits his teeth and turns to the doorframe as if to mentally yell at it. He turns and looks around the room and rocks back on his heels, 'Uh..yes, well, I thought perhaps I should congratulate you on your win in Cruiserweight title match...' Ivan grinds his jaw, slightly embarrassed. She smiles a bit as she plops back on the sofa listlessly. 'Thanks. Did ya see? It was a tough one, but I won.' Ivan nods and looks at a nearby wooden chair, he sits in it, eliciting a weak groan from the chair, the legs buckle, 'Yes, I was quite impressed by your wrestling, heh, little storm.' Tempest chuckles a little and winces as soon as she does, curling up a little. 'Yeah, too bad I couldn’t stay out of that abdominal stretch. Monte's a good wrestler, so he didn't give that belt up easy.' Ivan raises an eyebrow as he notices her wince and then narrows his eyes, 'Monte is nothing more than a capitalist that will take advantage of anyone, I am glad that you put him in his place..even after his...advances.' Another louder creak from the chair Ivan sits in. Tempest looks at him curiously. 'Well... I suppose if anyone gives capitalism a bad name it's Monte.' She shakes her head. 'Guy's got his nerve...' Ivan leans forward, speaking more like a teacher to Tempest and shakes his finger, 'Let it be a lesson, NEVER trust a capitalist. He wishes to 'capitalize' against you, no matter what! No one capitalizes against me, because I am not capitalist! You may be more communist than you think!' Ivan nods to Tempest, reverting back to his stern ways, until the chair bursts out from under Ivan and leaves him laying on his back. Ivan quickly gets up, blushing slightly, and brushes himself off. Tempest snickers and then winces again, burrowing into the cushions as she says, 'Don't lecture me. I knew about Monte long before anyone tried to preach communism to me. And the fact that you seem to end up on your butt in one way or another every time you try should tell you something.' She grins conspiratorially. Ivan shakes his head and thinks for a second, 'Tempest, are you..injured?' Tempest nods slightly, raising the side of her shirt to reveal the rounds of tape around her ribs. 'Yeah well, he kicked bejeezus out of me. Funny way to show his affection, wouldn't ya say?' An inward growl is heard from deep within Stanislav's chest as he looks at the damage. He leans forward and looks at the tape on her ribs, he mutters, 'Capitalist pigdog.' Ivan squints one eye and shakes his head, pointing at the wrapping. 'That is a very poor job of wrapping up an injury such as that! Did you do that?' 'Yeah, had to,' she said and shrugged. 'I won't go to a hospital.' The last was said with an unusual determination. Ivan studies her face for a moment and stands, speaking with more volume, 'Well then, little storm, I think it is time to return favor that you did for Ivan last week!' Ivan grins and looks down at her, 'Come on, get on up and show Ivan Sergeiovich where your tape is, I will make sure that your ribs heal correctly! If they are not wrapped properly, they could be injured for a very long time!' Tempest looks at him curiously. 'But how would you know...' Ivan laughs loudly, 'I am Praporschik in Russian Red Army, am I not? I am required to heal men on potential battlefield if need does arise!' Ivan smiles, very amused. He thinks for a moment, and then slowly outstretches his hand to help her up. Tempest chuckles and shakes her head very slightly, with a bemused expression and takes his hand. She stands with some effort and says, 'Just promise me you won't cinch me up in a knot or something. I'm not exactly militia material ya know?' Ivan laughs cryptically, 'DYAAHAA!! I will try not to snap you in half!' Tempest looks pained at the thought, but scoops a roll of tape from the counter top nearby and tosses it at Ivan. 'Ok, just be careful. I don't think they're broken really and... I'd like to keep it that way.' Ivan nods matter of factly and looks around to find a little loveseat in the corner. He walks over and picks up the seat and puts it down by the couch. He stands and looks at Tempest for a moment, and then shakes his head, suddenly moving forward and scooping her up gingerly and setting her down on the loveseat. 'You have to sit up little storm for me to properly bandage you.' He shakes his head, 'How difficult it must have been to wrap yourself up, dyaha!' Ivan fools with the tape. She chuckles and pulls of the baggy sweatshirt to reveal a terrible tape job over a white tank top and says, 'You don't know the half of it. I taped myself to the doorknob in the bathroom.' Ivan shakes his head and grabs a loose piece of the taping on her side, he grumbles to himself as he thinks, 'This may be slightly painful, I have to get rid of the first taping....' Ivan thinks for a second and then pulls at the tape, tearing it from around her waist. She winces a little, but smiles sheepishly. 'Yeah I know it's pretty bad.' Ivan finishes unwrapping the other tape and bites a piece of the roll of fresh tape. He pulls a long strip off and presses it to her stomach, dwarfing her tiny frame as he leans over her. Slowly, Ivan wraps the tape around the base of her ribs, sure to take it easy around the damaged area, Ivan continues to slowly wrap the tape around her. Ivan swallows and speaks softly, but it's still very heavy and angular, due to his accent. 'So uh...why did you not accept rich capitalist? If Ivan may ask....' Tempest snorts a little and says, 'Well, like I said, I may not be into politics, but if anyone really does give capitalism a bad name, it's him. He's arrogant, selfish, and totally untrustworthy. I mean this is a guy who's worked with my worst enemies against me. Even now and...' She sighs, suddenly saddened. 'He says he's not working against me any more, but he didn't lift a finger to help my brother. And he had to know what was gonna happen.' Ivan thinks for a moment, securely wrapping up the waist of Tempest, he makes a cross from the top of her left side to the bottom of her right side, and wraps and does so again, tightening it more and giving it more support. 'Might Ivan inquire as to what your brother's whereabouts are?' Ivan pauses for a moment, 'Ivan understands if you wish not to, your past is something that may not be as beautiful as....' Ivan pauses for a moment and then looks down and focuses on wrapping, shaking a thought from his head, '...eh..other things.' Tempest nods, still with a sad air and says, 'He's in the hospital. Ric Chronos took a sledgehammer to his arm. And they say...' her head droops a little,' that he'll never wrestle again.' Ivan thinks for a moment, trying to rationalize this. He stops wrapping for a minute, 'Your brother is a big boy, Ivan is sure, you should not be concerned for him. If he wishes to wrestle, he will wrestle.' Ivan nods to himself as pure Soviet thinking slips out of his mouth, 'Besides, if you show that you are worried for someone, then that is something that can be exploited.' Instantly, as Ivan says that, he winces. Tempest's lip trembles briefly, but then stops as she takes a deep breath and says, 'Then what exactly are you doing here?' Ivan, who had begun to wrap Tempest again, stops suddenly. He stares at the back of her head, but probably is not paying to anything physical around him, rather thinking something. There is an awkward silence in the room. Ivan's breath comes out slowly with a hint of a growl to it, but doesn't seem to be meant as threatening. He swallows, 'Ivan uh....' He thinks again. 'I uh...' He shakes his head, 'Ivan really doesn't know...uh...wh..why he is....here.' Ivan shrugs and wraps her waist one more time and tears the tape, setting it to her side gently. 'Well, let's just hope it's not a show of concern that anyone could exploit, hmm?' She says fairly quietly. Ivan, for once, is forced to eat his own words. He thinks for a second and rises to his feet and walks in front of her, 'There, your ribs should be much better now,' and looks out a window with his back to her. His huge hands are on his hips. He thinks for a moment and looks back over his shoulder for in her direction. 'I am sorry for what I said, I did not mean to insult you.' Ivan thinks for a moment and turns to her, his eyes darting around her, but not looking AT her, 'Perhaps you are right Tempest, what is Ivan Stanislav doing here....' Tempest smiles a little and looks down at herself, taped up in a way that might actually promote healing and says, 'Well, doing a marvelous job fixing me up. Thank you.' She smiles at him quite genuinely. Ivan slowly smiles himself, amazingly, and nods, 'Yes, yes, I suppose you are correct.' He thinks for a moment, not liking how potentially emotional the situation could become, clears his throat, fixes a suspender and nods down at her matter of factly, 'Now uh, be sure not to do anything that will reinjure those ribs. Ribs are very tender areas and cannot be taken lightly like a normal bruise.' 'So I've noticed' she says with a sheepish smile. 'Guess I should go leaping from any rafters or anything this weekend. It'll be hard to resist.' Ivan raises an eyebrow, not necessarily getting the joke. 'No, you shouldn't, that's why I said to take it easy.' He shakes his head, thinking she was serious. Tempest snickers and says, 'One day Ivan, I'll find a way to donate some of my sense of humor to you.' Stanislav tilts his head to the side, 'You sometimes say the most confusing things, Tempest.' Ivan turns and sits on the couch, facing her. He lifts a leg up and rests the ankle on his knee. 'If I may ask you a question, Tempest, because Ivan has been trying to figure this out. What made you pick me over a crazy superhero and a rich...capitalist, in bachelor contest?' Tempest grins and nestles back into the sofa cushions, 'Well, you sounded the most interesting. I mean there were only two of you that didn't disgust me and only one of you that wasn't...' She stops and tries to think of the right way to put it. 'Well, I didn't have you figured out by the end of thing. The other two were just... too easy.' She shrugged. Ivan nods and digests her answer. 'And you have not yet found any...disparaging traits from Jane Reagan?' Ivan raises an eyebrow and looks over at her. Tempest smiles and shakes her head. 'I haven't really had a chance to talk to Jane much. But... how did you put it about Eddy?' She pauses and adds, 'Oh yeah, that he was big boy. Well, I'm a big girl and I can form my own impressions.' Ivan blinks being mentally slapped, but he doesn't let up, 'I am just telling you what is the truth, that is all!' Tempest sighs and says, 'Ivan, I know you don't trust her. But you have to give her credit for doing two things right at the very least.' Ivan crosses his arms over his huge barrel chest, 'And they are?' 'Well, she hired you,' Tempest says with a shrug. Ivan nods, realizing that perhaps he's being too hard on her. He leans forward towards her, 'Yes, yes, I understand, perhaps she did do two good things.' Ivan nods to her, 'The second would have to be hiring....you, da?' Ivan works up a small smile. Tempest nods smiling. 'Taken together, those two things alone oughtta get her a little credit.' Ivan contemplates this, too stubborn to openly say that she is right regarding Reagan. 'And if I may ask another question. Who is this...Miyuki person?' 'Miyuki? Oh, she's my best friend in the whole world,' Tempest says happily, 'And my manager now that I can afford one. She keeps me in line.' She chuckles at her own admission. 'She tends to be a bit, harsh when passing judgment on others, would you not say?' Tempest looks at him a bit sympathetically and says, 'She can be. But she's only looking out for me. And that's a tough job, as you might guess.' She glances down at her taped ribs and shrugs. Ivan nods to himself. He reaches into his pocket and pulls out a gold pocket watch with a hammer and sickle engraving on it. He opens it and looks, nodding to himself. He replaces the pocket watch and stands, nodding to Tempest, 'Well Tempest, I must apologize but I believe I had better go, I must speak with my commanding officer in thirty minutes via telephone.' He stops for a moment, obviously not done talking. His tone changes to a slightly softer sound, 'Tempest, Ivan just wanted to come over to tell you....' He stops and searches for words. He thinks, absently opening his mouth and closing it a few times, he shrugs, his tone lowering slightly, 'Ivan just wanted to come over and....and.....congratulate you.' Ivan shakes his head slightly, looking at the floor. Tempest stands carefully and steps in front of him with a smile as she says, 'Well thank you. I appreciate it, and also the help with the ribs.' Ivan nods sternly, his face returning to the cold, granite look that he normally possesses, 'And take care of those ribs, no leaping from rafters. You have a good night Tempest, Ivan Stanislav is glad that he could help.' Ivan nods and walks past Tempest, he gets to the door and stops, he looks down at the floor. He stands there and clenches his hands into fists, 'I must say that what I had said before is sometimes falsity, for it is good to care for certain people, and it is good for certain people to care for you, Tempest. I am sure your brother is glad that you care for him. Some of us may not be as alone in world as we think. Das vi danya, Tempest.' Ivan reaches for the door and opens it, disappearing around the door and shutting it gently. For a moment Tempest stands still and then smiles, turning from the door and resettling on the couch, where she drifts to sleep easily.
