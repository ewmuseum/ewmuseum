As I watch the sun f*ck the ocean.

20 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 20th, 2002 (Note: Food poisoning, no sleep and barely having my eyes opened right now have all contributed to the quality of this, or lack thereof. Sorry, I'm always rusty after a week off...hopefully, if I shake this sickness, I'll be back to average Aimz quality for the next card! They'll get longer, promise.) 'TURN...THAT...............OFF!' I barely heard Timo screaming over the music I was blasting from the cheapo rental car stereo. In fact, I had only really noticed him when he flicked the top of my ear... He had hit my Industrial! A barbell through two holes in the cartilege in your ear is NOT something that's overly-comfortable when hit. 'Jesus Christ!' 'Sorry, but it had to be done...that music is SHIT! Who the hell was that?!' 'Boy Hits car, 'Going to India'...they do my theme.' When I turned the song way down, he shook his head a little...grabbing the CD case from me, glancing at the back. 'Is it all that bad?' 'Gah,' I scoffed, shuffling my feet from the sun beaming on the floor of the car. 'I love them...I can't help the fact that your taste bites.' 'I'm not the one listening to that crap...' He smiled. 'Besides, we're at the beach, that's nothin' to be playing!' I practically ignored the 'Samoan Silencer', slipping the BHC album from the stereo on the dash board. 'Meh.' 'And another thing...' Okay, now he looked semi-serious. 'A few things will have to change if we're gonna be seen together in public.' 'Like...?' 'Unbuckle that damned wallet chain, for one.' I made sure to pout, propping a foot up on the dash with folded arms. 'No.' 'Alright, be difficult...but at least take the biker boots off.' 'They're NOT biker boots!' He peered closely, confidently, at my raised hiking-style leather footwear, reading the insigna on the ankle. 'Then why does they say 'Harley Davidson' on the side?' 'Even if they do, I'm keeping them...there's no way I can go without the leather.' 'Fine...AT LEAST pull your pants up. Not that it's a bad sight, I just don't need people thinking that the woman I'm tagging with is COMPLETE trash.' Timo winked, kidding around as usual...though it annoyed me, he seemed to actually, somewhere deep down, want me to change the way I am. Ohhh well, he was a sweetie even though he had bad taste more than half of the time. 'So where the hell are we headed, huh?' 'The arena, as soon as we figure out where in God's name it is.' He was fiddling viciously with a couple of well-folded maps, 'In the meantime, feel free to head down to the water.' 'Can do!' Was my final statement, I didn't feel like sticking around in this cramped piece of junk with with a sweaty, confused Samoan. I wasn't in any rush to find the arena, Timo could handle it himself... So what did I do? Took his suggestion...I bounced out of that so-called car and, tossing my boots back into the passenger's seat, strode down onto the sun-warmed sand of the shore in my bare feet. Why not? Gotta have a little fun...and I needed a little alone time, this was the perfect opportunity. I had to hop down a little hill of dirt and grass before I managed to get all the way to the water, but none-the-less, I got there and sat down against a boulder that had been worn smooth by the constant tide. Perfect spot...I was sheltered from the sun and the rest of the people, not to mention the water was cooling my toes that the sand had managed to sting. So I just sat and, as usual, my mind wandered... This week Timo and I took on Sebastian Toomes...I had promised my Samoan partner that I'd leave the champ to him for the most part, but something that happened all the way back in my first ever PWA match stood out in my mind... We were on the roof of the One Times Square building in a match that's stipulation was anybody who reached the flagpole and lowered the strap won the class title. All was looking good...I was ahead, but I froze...the very second it matter the most, I realized how high above the ground I was and just completely locked up. I couldn't move and, when Switchblade reached the rooftop, he took advantage of that...dangling me off of the roof and eventually dropping me to what seemed a gory death. Toomes could have just walked right past me and grabbed the belt, but he chose to go farther...thank God Boda caught me, or else I wouldn't be here for my revenge this day. Sebastian had cruelly taken advantage of my one biggest fear and he was destined to pay for that in one way or another... I desperately wanted to say that I was going to do my best to win the match for TIMO, but what was truly driving me had to be retribution...I would assist as best I could in making Toomes lose the one thing he loves almost as much as his miniature ram-rod. I doubt that my revenge will end there...it almost seems as if getting back at Switchy was a subconcious motive in my coming to OSW, but I doubt it was any big factor. I'd get what I sought...I already witnessed part of my dream on Saturday, when I saw him laid out in the middle of the ring, practically lifeless. But that wasn't quite enough, I knew that...I could easily take out the other thing he loves, his chosen flagship of sexual gratification...that rusty old machine also known as Jane Reagan. I mean, I had the perfect excuse...she had tried too hard last Slam. She tried too hard to take me out...she wasn't successful, but she did try...therefore making her an enemy; I don't like it when they won't just take the fall, she had to put up a fight. It bothered me that I was forced to waste my time on something as trivial as that walking genital wart, but if I had to, I had to...she was getting in my way, it was bothersome, too irritating. Reagan could be compared to Britney Spears...we all ask the same questions of them both: 'Why is she here?', 'What the hell is she doing?', 'Does she honeslty think that makes her cool?' And another similarity...not one person, aside from mindless perverts that crawl out of their cesspools to see them on TV, actually cares. Why not? Well why should we care about someone who tries to look beautiful, but merely ends up with the appearance of a drag-queen? Oh...wait...getting Reagan mixed up with Christina Aguilara again... Well, same difference...they all try too hard to have some sort of importance, any influence...but the only people that end up caring for them are either the previously mentioned perves, like Toomes, or the past-their-prime women who try so desperately to conform, like Tempest. Gah...fuck thinking, I was confusing myself again, mixing up people all-too similar. I stood, brushed the sand that had so wonderfully collected in my back pockets out and off of me, and headed back to the car, curling my toes in the sand for that funny feeling effect it never ceases to give. When I reached Timo and the car, he spoke simply in a triumphant tone, 'I think I've finally found the arena on these confangled maps!' Thank God! We might be en-route to our final destination after all! Lord only knows what could go wrong from here...but I can almost guarantee myself that something bad's gonna happen, knowing the luck he and I have had so far.
