I reckon Scott Taylor sucks (suck alert sirens)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Tuesday night What happened? What in the bloody hell happened? Saturday night, Kid Wonder went to Dallas in hopes of getting himself an impromptu match. Instead, what the teen idol got was punked out by OSW newbie, Scott Taylor. Not exactly what someone the stature of Kid Wonder wants happening to him. But it did and K-Dub can’t do anything about it. But why? Why did Scott Taylor attack Kid Wonder? And what the hell is he going on about “stroke”? Kid Wonder with stroke in OSW? Heh. Yeah right! If anything, KW has anti-stroke. Let’s all use logic for one moment. If Kid Wonder had any stroke, any stroke at all, he’d be Sebastian Toomes…except Kid Wonder is actually cool. Kid Wonder would be in more matches than he is. He’d definitely be higher up on the card. And if Kid Wonder had some stroke, this Scott Taylor kid would already be disposed of. But he’s not! That occurrence has to wait. Kid Wonder won’t be able to eliminate the enemy until their match on Saturday. A match that K-Dub will certainly be hell bent on winning. Fade in We find ourselves at a cheap motel just outside of Norman, Oklahoma. It’s nighttime. You know this because it’s dark outside and the moon’s bright glow lights up the scene. A sign outside stands roughly 15’ tall and reads “MOTEL” in red neon lighting. The “O” in the sign, however, flickers showing faint signs of energy. Meanwhile, over by Room 6, sitting on a broken down wooden chair is, as you guessed, Kid Wonder. But the OSW star doesn’t look like himself. Instead of the usual genie sequin pants and flamboyant shirt, K-Dub is sporting a flannel shirt and some blue overalls. His long, golden-blond hair is being covered by a straw hat. And in the teen idol’s mouth hangs a toothpick that he is chewing on under the night sky. “What a boo-tiful night, I reckon,” says the young man in a terrible hillbilly accent. “I reckon I could spend every night like this night.” Wonder rolls his eyes. Behind him, the door to Room 6 swings open. From inside, out steps Kid Wonder’s girlfriend, Amanda. “For the love of God, please tell me why we’re here again,” Amanda says while standing behind her boyfriend. Amanda is dressed in her usual getup; a pink halter top with “STAR” written in silver glitter across the chest, as well as some tight, black leather pants. Kid Wonder doesn’t turn around to look at her. Instead, he leans back in the chair and says, “I reckon we’re living the life of a superior NTICW wrestler.” “What are you talking about?” asks Amanda while raising the tone of her voice. “This isn’t NTICW.” “If I’m gonna lower my in-ring standards,” Wonder says while spitting out the toothpick, “I reckon I should lower my not-in-ring standards.” Amanda sighs. She walks and stands next to where the man is sitting. Amanda stands there, looks down at K-Dub, and says, “This is insane.” With the lovely Amanda standing next to him, Kid Wonder can’t help but turn to face her. “I reckon it is,” Wonder tells her in response. “But, ya see darlin’, if I put myself through the ang…ang…pain Scotty went through, I’ll want to use ma stroke to make things all goody again.” Amanda looks at her boyfriend. She rolls her eyes and sighs. Without responding, she turns around and walks back towards the room. “Of all the crazy ideas…” Amanda starts tailing off as she enters Room 6 and closes the door shut. With the faded blue-colored door with the golden “6” hanging upside down to look like a “9” luring in the background behind him, Kid Wonder bends over and reaches under the chair. A few moments later, the teen idol pulls out…a banjo. Wonder begins to strum the three strings remaining on the banjo while humming some sort of hillbilly tune. We don’t stick around for this, however, as we opt to leave this sad scene. Fade out
