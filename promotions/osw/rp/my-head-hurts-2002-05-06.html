My Head Hurts

6 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/06/02 Breaking Point. Everyone has one thing that will piss them off. Everyone has one thing that’ll make them cross the line. The proverbial mouse that woke the sleeping giant. Everyone has something that makes them snap. In PCW, everyone had there breaking point. Meanstreak awoke when Ivan Stanislav ran rough-shod over the Upper Card. The way Media ran things caused the formation of the Outcasts. In OSW, Meanstreak’s actions caused the in-ring return of Ed. Sebastian Toomes’ actions made Ivan Stanislav fight on the side of good. Da Westsidaz and Civil Disobedience becoming threats to the Tag Champions caused Heresian Bloodlust to lash out at them. Everyone has a breaking point. Da Westsidaz’ breaking point is when someone touches Daisy… A betting man would say no one could ever get enough balls to lay a finger on Daisy again. After all, the last time someone caused Da Westsidaz to snap was two years ago, in April. “Hurricane” Hastie and Raine merely grabbed Daisy by the hair about a month before. After waiting patiently for match, Da Westsidaz finally got there chance. They broke Hastie’s neck and spends the rest of his days in Mount Sinai Hospital, hoping to walk again one day. Raine was also beaten beyond belief and hasn’t been seen since. Now, Hessian has had the gull to backhand Daisy. Well, this time Da Westsidaz won’t wait for a match. They’ll be out for blood, and they’ll be out for it soon. They haven’t let anyone push them around before, so now will not be any different. ---- “Jesus, be careful, that stings y’know!” mumbles Short Circuit as he sits in the Hospital Bed of the North Hollywood General Hospital. The doctor is bent over him, parting the wrestler’s hair and applying stitches to his split-open head. “Man, I am so going to kick that Masked Freak’s pussy ass so hard, his little Imp butt-fucking friend’ll feel it.” Shorty flinches as the doctor loops another stitch into his head. Da Westsidaz are currently in the North Hollywood General Hospital, getting looked at after the massive attack by Heresian Bloodlust last Saturday on SLAM! Short Circuit took a vicious chairshot, Riptah took a sledgehammer shot to the back of the head and Daisy got a huge slap courtesy of the Tag Champions. Well, everything is fine now. Riptah rubs his aching head while downing a couple Tylenol. He shakes his head, “Well, those fucking idiots are really going to see what it’s like to be on the receiving end. They think they can actually mess up Da Westsidaz? They think they’re in our LEAGUE? I was about two seconds from tearing Hessian limb from limb and another gay-ass satan freak hits me from behind!! There going to pay. But y’know what? I’m not mad about that. It don’t even bother me. It’s what Hessian did to my girlfriend that pisses me off!” Daisy is sitting in the corner, with her arms crossed. She’s wearing a hooded sweater and jeans, plus black sunglasses. Although the sunglasses block most of it, there is a huge black and blue handprint on her face. She seems really upset and REALLY bitchy. “Rip, you have to show that melee-mouthed bastard that he CAN’T mess with me. Hurt him. Show him that he can’t fuck with Da Westsidaz. He can’t lay his hands on me! Next week, I want them to pay!” Riptah cracks his neck. “They won’t get off the hook, I can promise you that. Whatcha think doc, we done here?” Dr. Ringwald nods his head quickly while flipping through papers. “I’ll need to take those stitches out in a few days for Mr. Williams and I want you to stay outta fights until that concussion passes. Ms. Holden’s eye should clear up in a few days.” “Stay outta fights? Too bad. I’m going after those Tag Jokes the second I walk into the arena. It’s going to be one helluva ass-kickin’ all over T-dot-O! Hess and Cain think they got what it takes to walk into our business? I was going to let them hold their little titles and just focus on Da Whitesidaz, but now, they have to come over here and piss me off. SC, what happens to people that piss off us?!” “They get knocked straight the fuck out.” “Yeah damn right nigga. I even know what were going to do. Kinda killin’ two birds with one stone kinda thing…Those two whiteboys are going down, along with the Satan-loving freaks! Nobody fucks with us. No one.” Riptah cracks his knuckles, and scratches at the bandages wrapped around his head. “We were almost down with those two suburban crackers, and we were just about to pin them, but now Caleb and Minion had to wrap wiring around the ring-support. Hessian and Cain don’t know what they’ve got themselves into.” Daisy stands up and stretches, indicating it’s time to go. “We better get to the airport. We want to get settled in before SLAM! That way, we’ll have everything planned out when before Saturday.” “Those dawgs not only fucked up us and Civil D, that masked brute was raggin’ on Da Westside. He goes down to the Jungle and thinks he owns the muthafucka. Well, we’ll let our actions speak louder than words.” Short Circuit gets of the Hospital Bed and puts on his big Crazy Ass Sunglasses. “We’ll prove to them that underneath the problems, Da Westside still is Da Bestside, whether those bitches think so or not.”
