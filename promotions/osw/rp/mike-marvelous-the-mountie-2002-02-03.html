Mike Marvelous...the Mountie!?

3 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/03/02 ....'Five-Star' Mike Marvelous.... Is it a name that is remembered in the anals of sports entertainment for all time? Is the name respected and spoken of with high regards? Has the name beared any prestigious titles in the past? The answers to the first two of these questions is blatantly 'no'. He is neither legend nor respected. He jumped ship from FSW, a dying federation, and used the FSW to gain recognition in PCW. And his name is most often tagged to such words as 'Reamer' and 'Friggin' Canadian Retard'. But the answer to the last question is....yes and no. Yes...he has held titles. Many of them. But were they prestigious? No. He held the FSW Tag Team Titles with his former partner and best friend(And now retired wrestler 'The Idol' Jacob Jackson. He also was the first ever FSW Primetime Champion, and former FSW Intercontinental and FSW World Champion. But let's face it...what was his competition? Guys like Big Gooey Sac, Onslaught and the Freaks of Nature. And FSW isn't exactly what you'd call an 'elite' force in Sports Entertainment. He DID, however, have a short and very forgettable run in PCW. He even won the PCW T.V. Title by defeating JJ, Badge, Kensuke Suzahara, Johnny Raindance and DarkStar. That raises a few eyebrows...but he only held it for about two weeks until losing it to DarkStar at the New Dawning Pay Per View and then was promptly beaten within an inch of his life and thrown in a coffin by Dark Age and was never seen in the PCW since. Mike Marvelous. Perhaps the best title for him would be 'Underachiever'. Despite his origin, he came to PCW with very high hopes from the back office. They really expected something from him. And all they got at the most was him renaming the FSW World Title the For Short Wrestlers Title and holding a match for it by imploring all PCW Wrestlers to find midgets to enter in the match. He also began to sponsor his own line of products. You may have seen the advertisements. MARVELotion....MARVELade Sports Beverage....MARVELicious Soda, which he tried to make the official Sports Beverage of PCW but was thwarted by DarkStar. He even tried to mess with PCW Hall of Famer Meanstreak and had a match with The Monster Known As Meanstreak and as a result, spent 3 weeks of his early PCW career in the hospital with a separated shoulder. Some say that Marvelous just wasn’t cut out for PCW. Some say he started his downward spiral because of the loss of his partner, Jacob Jackson, who retired. Some say he was put up against too many PCW main eventers and pushed too fast. All of them may be true. Marvelous had a very illustrious career up until PCW. But something went wrong and Marvelous’ reputation as a flawless technical wrestler and a crowned jewel of sports entertainment was tarnished. After he was put out of PCW, he was not seen or heard from for more than 6 months. Not even from his best friend Jacob Jackson! That is...until now. Let’s take a look at the events leading up to Mike’s return to wrestling....in OSW. The camera brings us to what looks to be a large public park somewhere in Nova Scotia, Canada. There is a large frozen duck pond with a little wooden bridge running over it. There are no ducks though, they have flown south for the winter. Many little kids are skating on the ice of the pond while their parents sit on the park benches, occasionally glancing up from their books to check on their kids. There are two large snow-covered fields, one of them has an ice and snow covered playground in it. No kids are playing on this, because it is too dangerous with all the ice. They’ll have to wait until spring. Many trails can be seen leading through beautiful wooded areas where all the trees are capped with snow and the air is filled with the fresh scent of pine. A teenage boy with braces, a backwards yankees cap and a walkman is walking along one of the paths because it is shorter for him to get home if he walks through the park. He is listening to his Discman and pulling something out of his coat pocket. It appears to be a package of Mentos. He pops the last round, peppermint, chewy delight into his mouth and drops the empty package onto the ground. Little does the young man know that somebody is watching him. The camera pans over and we catch a glance of former FSW Grandslam Champion (And Hall of Famer) and PCW T.V. Champion, “Five-Star” Mike Marvelous. But he looks much different than he did when he was still wrestling over 6 months ago. He is dressed in a red blazer with a yellow sash and black pants with two yellow stripes down the side of the legs. He also has on black boots and a big brown cowboy hat. The attire worn by Mounties in Canada. Except they don’t call them “Mounties” anymore, they are the “Royal Canadian Mounted Police”. Anyway, Mike has obviously become a Mountie in his spare time, and has been promoted from “Park Custodian” to “Park Warden”, a very honorable title amongst the Royal Canadian Mounted Police. As Mike sits there on his noble steed and partner, Finigan the Moose, he watches the kid that just dropped the Mentos package. As the package falls, the camera goes into slow motion as it slowly drifts back and forth in the wind like a leaf and then finally touches down on Canadian soil. Litter. The camera does a close up of Mike’s face and his eyes well up with tears. One single tear rolls down his cheek and he dismounts from his trusty moose companion, and walks over to the young boy, who by now is walking away like he didn’t just break the law. “Ahem...young man.” The teenage boy turns around when he hears Mike talk and takes his headphones off. He has a confused look on his face. “Yeah?” “Do you have any idea what you just did....” The boy looks at Mike dumfounded and squints his eyes, trying to figure out what Mike is talking about. Mike just stands there with a stern look on his face and his arms folded over his chest. “Umm...no....what’d I do?” “You just disgraced your country and tarnished the sacred soil of Canada, that’s what you did.” “Umm...I did?” “What, littering is a normal thing for you? So normal that it is just a programmed response for when you are done with your candy wrappers? What is this world coming to....” Mike shakes his head, picks up the package and tosses it in a waste bin. He then walks up to the kids and points a finger at him. “Now, next time you are finished with your packaged goods, you will distribute them into one of the nearby waste bins. Do I make myself clear young man!?” The kid just looks at Mike with a weird look and then a mischievous grin spreads across his lips. “Umm....F%$@ you!” With that, the kid kicks Mike in the groin and runs away laughing as Mike crumbles to the ground in a heap, clutching his throbbing crotch and kicking his feet. “AAAAAAAARRRRRRRGGGGGGHHHHH!!!!!! Cant’t.....breathe........testicles......swelling......” As Mike lays there motionless, two little kids walk up to him and look down at him. One of them talks to him. “Hi mister. Wha-tcha doooooin’?” “Oh.....just.....laying down. Taking....a....break.....” “It looks like you hurt your baaaalls...” “It’s....alright.....little ones.......go play.” “My dad told me that mounties are pansy ass dorks.” “Really?......That’s.....great.” “Umm....mister?” “Yes.......” “You suck ya big poo poo head!” Mike’s eyes go wild as the two little kids begin stomping and kicking his face. “Please....kids.....stoooop!” One of the little kids gives Mike the middle finger while the other one steals his watch. “Oooh! A Rolex!” They both run away giggling as Mike lays there, bleeding from his lip, still holding his groin, and without his expensive watch. After a while, Mike slowly gets up on one knee, then the other, then gets on his feet. He brushes himself off and touches his finger to his lip. He pulls it away and looks at the small blotch of crimson blood. Mike looks upwards, and begins yelling, seemingly to nobody in particular. “No respect....I get NO RESPECT! I am an FSW HALL OF FAMER FOR CHRIST SAKES! I AM YOUR CANADIAN HERO! YOU PEOPLE ARE SCUM!” Mike snaps out of it and then looks straight ahead. Everybody in the park is staring at him. “Umm...sorry folks. I was just...uh....talking to myself. Pay me no attention.” Somebody yells from a distance and Mike hears it: “You suck!” Upon hearing this, Mike’s sking begins to turn red and his eyes grow wide. He begins to twitch and starts to breath really heavily until... “THAT’S IT!” Mike throws his mountie hat onto the ground, stomps on it. “I’M DONE WITH YOU! ALL OF YOU!” Mike rips off his mountie attire and throws it on the ground, jumping up and down on it. He is so angry that he doesn’t even realize he is....naked! Everybody starts to run away screaming. Mothers cover the eyes of their children. One woman screams: “EEEEEEK! HE’S NAKED! PUT YOUR CLOTHES BACK ON YOU PERVERT!” Mike looks up at the woman. He has already snapped and is beyond the point of no return. “NO! I AM THROUGH TAKING ORDER FROM YOU PEOPLE! GOOD DAY MADAM!” Mike runs off, naked as the day he was born, and eventually makes his way to the street. He goes to get into his small junk car when somebody grabs him in a back wristlock and smashes his face up against his side window. He hears a click as handcuffs are slapped on him. He is spun around by the pair of hands and there is a very angry police officer standing in front of him. “Just what the HELL are you doing out in the street naked, you sicko!?!” “Umm...I....you see....I quit my job and....well, they....they didn’t respect me...so.....I....I....” “Are you intoxicated, sir?” “Intoxicated...? Oh! NO! I’m perfectly fine. I mean...I don’t even drink!” “Then it’s drugs, isn’t it?” “No way! McGruff the Crimedog told me to just say no!” “Are you being sarcastic sir?” “Umm...no, I’m not” “Oh...I think you are....” The officer pulls out his pepper spray and sprays it into Mike’s eyes. Mike let’s out an ungodly scream as the cop leads him over to the cop car. “AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!” “Get in the car you moron! You have the right to remain silent....anything you say can and will be held against you in the court of law. You have the right to an attorney, and if you can’t get one, we’ll give you the crappiest mail order lawer we can find!” The cop goes to push Mike into the car put forgets to push his head down, so Mike bumps his head. “OW!” “I said you have the right to remain silent...don’t test me, nudey!” The cop gets in the front and drives off with Mike in the back. Mike looks out the back window like a lost puppy dog as the scene fades to black. To be continued.... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
