The View

25 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/25/02 I simply do not understand. What a waste of my talents to send me after some mere girl. I should be offended. Moreover I should have turned down job. But no, one cannot do that. Orders are orders and they must be followed. Isn't that so? Yet Praporschik Stanislav dares not to. And all for that very same girl. It makes one wonder about a great many things. Just how smart are those people giving orders from confined office in Moscow? It is fortunate thoughts are not subject to questioning, for mine might be treasonous. It is small comfort I allow myself when things become difficult. Look at them... so quiet and peaceful, the very picture of serenity. Who cold begrudge them that? It would appear the high command does. But surely there is no danger in this tiny creature. And especially not to Praporschik Stanislav. He is mighty soldier and hero, nyet? I would place more faith in him to survive whatever befalls him, than for any general to sit through a bread line. Some say Stanislav is getting old. If so, why not leave him alone? Let him have some happiness in his later years. But then reward is not first in the minds of the generals. If anyone is getting old it is them. Take sending me here for example. I cannot even truly carry out my orders because they were thought of by a man who still thinks he is head of the world's mightiest army. He is a bigger fool than Ivan. And Stanislav becomes less one, I think every day. Curiosity overwhelms me in this assignment. I have such a view that I cannot resist the urge to watch and wonder. How did he become so close to this girl who is nothing like him? She is not loyal Russian nor even backward thinking youth who still thinks communism is a great escape from a cruel world. Communism... some of the old hard-liners only think of it because it reminds them of when they were more powerful. But Stanislav always seemed different... as if there was something in that ideal that overcame even his brutishness. It's as if there were only one way to allow anyone near him and he needed a doctrine to do it. Where is your dogma now Praporschik? Lost somewhere in those deep blue eyes? Forgotten in the sound of her voice? But no, I do not blame you. You are still hero to the Russian people. Every day, they face another day of deprivation and wondering whether it is worth it to go on. They look around at a world that they are not ready for and wonder how they will survive in it. And then they see you. Still engrossed in the same thoughts they are, still used to being told every move to make and yet... you come out into that world and succeed. You find yourself with a woman who is anything but the party's choice and she accepts you. No one who knows of Ivan Stanislav and follows him at all, is not pleased... because for a moment they imagine themselves to be you, to be successful and accepted in a world that does not end at a wall in Berlin. Heh... I think too much. It comes from boredom when sitting still for too long. And the world always looks different through the telescopic sight of my rifle. 'Exterminate the threat in as inconspicuous a way as possible.' Hah! As if there is any way to dispose of her inconspicuously... She is never alone. And when she is found shot, Ivan will know who is responsible. There will be nothing inconspicuous. Besides, I see no threat. I will have to answer at some point, but it is not inconceivable that I may never get a clean shot. Yes, that is it. She is too public a figure, surrounded by security, out in public, or with Ivan at all times. There was no way do it and not cause international incident. That is what I will say. But for now, I will just watch these two... and hope that another man, one who thinks less is to come after me. Good luck Ivan. You will need every bit of it. And may your Little Storm remain strong and vibrant, and too fast for the sniper's bullet... for I fear there will be more where I came from.
