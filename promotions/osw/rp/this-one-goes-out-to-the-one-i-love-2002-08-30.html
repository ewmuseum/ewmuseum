This One Goes Out To The One I Love.

30 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/30/02 Our scene opens to show the inside of a vehicle, the usual leather interior is present as with most of the popular vehicles. From what the view allows us to see there are two doors and a single long seat on which Scott Taylor is sitting on. It becomes obvious that he is driving a truck of some kind, though the inside view doesn't give us the best idea of what kind though. Scott leans back in the seat of the truck and props his left arm up on a small arm rest built into the door while his right hand casually rests on the steering wheel, controlling the vehicle and keeping it in the proper lane. He looks straight ahead through the black sunglasses which cover his green eyes, listening to the radio while he drives down a pretty much desolate back road. That's the problem with taking shortcuts, hardly ever any other drivers. Then again, that's probably not a bad thing if you think about it. Somethng is different about Scott, he looks different. It's fairly obvious upon the first glance, his hair is cut. The once three and three quarter inch braid is now gone. His hair is now hanging right at his neckline, where his shoulders connect with his neck. It's still the nautral black, so atleast nothing too extreme like dying it wild and crazy colors hasn't happened. Thank Bishnu. He sighs a little and reaches down to turn on the radio, pressing a few buttons until he finds a suitable station. 'American Woman....Stay away from me.....American Woman...Mama let me beeeeeee!' Scott suddenly bolts straight up right in the seat and practically loses control of the truck. It violently swerves to the right but Scott turns the wheel to the left with the leverage of both hands. As the truck spins in a huge donut on the thankfully open road, Scott slams on the breaks and spins the wheel in the opposite direction trying to regain control of the vehicle. With a loud skidding sound the vehicle finally stops in the middle of the road and Scott leans back in the seat, breathing hard and sweating a little. His sunglasses have fallen off and we can see a little fear in his greens, he looks down at the radio as the song continues to play. He knows the song all too well, mainly because of his appreciation of rock music. But also because someone who used to be very close to him recently began using that song. What is he thinking about? Why, the woman he loves. The young and beautiful, yet horribly tempred Teresa Tomas. '.......Teresa Jane......' Scott mumbles her first and middle names while speaking in a low and deep voice, which only makes his southern accent show through that much more. Slowly he reaches forward and presses one of the buttons on the radio, and just like that the song is cut off in mid-chorus. He leans back in the seat and sighs heavily, his eyes actually starting to tear up. He quickly runs his right forearm across his eyes. His mind begins to wander, to think back to three years ago. The year was 1999, an extremely overweight bussinessman who simply called himself 'Mr Big' invested a little money to open a small independant wrestling company. It was simply called LTL, or Larger Than Life Wrestling. Plenty of unknown wrestlers came in, though only three of them made anything of themselves and promoted the company. Three names.... 'Big Daddy' Rick Waldermarsen....... Scott Taylor....... Jack 'The Ace' Slayter....... Those names have become familiar as of recently. 'Big Daddy' Rick was once the commisioner of NTICW, Scott Taylor found huge success in a singles career, and Jack Slayter has recently returned as the manager of one of the new PCW singees, Teresa Tomas. Still sitting in the middle in the road, Scott starts to think back to those days. LTL was a smally followed place, not too many people heard of it or even knew of it's existence. Teresa Tomas wasn't a wrestler there though. She simply managed the career of Jack Slayter, who at one point was the LTL Champion. But, how does this involve Scott? One night in April of 2000, that's wheen. Jack Slayter threw out an open challenge to anyone in the back who wanted a shot, and Scott Taylor, who at the time was a mere twenty-three years of age glady accepted. That night was history. Scott Taylor, the young cruiserweight, pushed Jack Slayter to his limit and came within mere centimeters of capturing the championship. That was the first time Scott had an encounter with Teresa Tomas, as he lay on the mat with his face bloody after suffering a vicious Tornado DDT, he looked up and saw Teresa's face. She was looking back at him a little concerned since the impact of the DDT wasn't very pretty. Maybe it was.....dare I say it....love at first sight. Several months later, Jack Slayter left LTL on a knee injury he suffered in a ladder match. This left Teresa jobless. Until Scott Taylor came a knocking at her apartment one night. The young man was desperate, he needed someone with business experience to help him. Teresa couldn't help but take pity on the young wrestler and agreed to be his manager. After that, Scott's career slowly managed to come back. Three Reigns at the companies National Champion. Teresa at his side with every win. It was kept under wraps by fear from Mr Big, but the entire locker room knew that love was blossoming between the two. Later on, the company changed hands and with this new man in power, the company just didn't last too much longer. Between the horrible booking and the paying of the wrestlers to take falls....the place fell apart. Even the once engaged Taylor and Tomas fell distant. A tragic end in May of 2001. Until one year later....... Scott Taylor was now a wrestler in a company called NTICW, which was owned and operated by two men. Vice President Xavier Kannon and President Chris S. Scott had managed to defeat an incredibly talented man a year older than he, by the name of Semore Smoke to win his first World Championship. He was living the high life, leading the promotion and having one hell of a time doing so. But besides the upper card, things in NTICW weren't so hot. Enter Chris S.'s idea. He created a new championship, the Cruiserweight Championship and signed various cruiserweights from smaller federations, giving them a chance to compete in the big time. Alot of young and very talented super stars came in. One was, you guessed it, Teresa Tomas. Apparently after LTL fell through Teresa took it upon herself to become a wrestler. To experience the other side, so to speak. A spark was still ablaze and it didn't take long. Scott and Teresa began to see each other again. It was a little weird, seeing the world champion himself spending all of his time with a lower carder who was still making a name. Most dirt sheets wrote it off as Teresa using Scott's status to promote herself. That was bullshit. Utter bullshit. The two still had feelings for each other and were once again enjoying a relationship. Things were going smoothly until June 23, 2002. NTICW was put to rest, put to rest by it's rival known as RUSH Wrestling. The NTICW just couldn't compete and once again Scott and Teresa's federation fell through. Enter PCW CEO Tyler Nelson. Tyler Nelson purchased several contracts. His first draft pick was the World Champion, Scott Taylor. Followed by Scott's oldest rival in NTICW, Semore Smoke. Then three of the hottest tag teams in the company. Semore and the tag teams fell in, but no Scott Taylor. Where oh where was Scott? Cue Taylor's Music. Out walks Scott Taylor, the NTICW Championship slung over his shoulder, and to his right? The owner of Primetime Championship Wrestling's biggest rival...the owner of Old School Wrestling, Jane Reagan?! With a huge smile on her face, Jane announced that she swooped right in under Tyler's nose and purchased Scott's contract for the OSW. With a huge smile on his face, Scott dropped the NTICW World Title Belt and walked away with his new boss. As for Teresa, she went to RUSH Wrestling with the others, but some complications sent her back to the re-opened 'Extreme' NTICW. For two months Teresa was stuck in a truly misogynistic federation while Scott was cruising high in Old School Wrestling, really making a name for himself. Then while reading over a wrestling magazine Scott read some shocking news. Teresa signed a contract to PCW?! Yes, PCW...his federation's biggest rival now under the control of Xavier Kannon, his former boss, and Kannon's wife, Eleanor Hall. How could she?! She did. One Night In A Hotel. Scott and Teresa entered her hotel room. The two had just left their respective shows which happened to be close. Scott leaving a Slam and Teresa leaving her last NTICW Mayhem. Since the two were so close they decided to see each other. Scott had just lost a hellacious tag team match to Armour and the OSW Commisoner Howard Porter. He was aching all over and was having trouble walking. A brilliant idea came from Teresa, a massage. It wouldn't hurt right? So Scott agreed. Things were going well, Scott was laying on the bed in a pair of boxer shorts while Teresa straddled his back rubbing at his tense muscles with some scented oils. The two shared a casual conversation, nothing too big. Until Scott said the wrong thing, and he learned first hand what Teresa's temper was like. A fight. A huge fight where Scott literally had to use his brute strength to get Teresa off of him. BLAM! He slammed her back first against the hotel wall She slowly slumped down and Scott stared, shocked...ashamed....what had he done? WHAT THE HELL HAD HE DONE TO THE WOMAN HE LOVED?! End Flashback. We find ourselves back on the inside of Scott's truck. He's still motionless as all of these events run through his mind again. Three years of Teresa and it all ended because he was a jackass to her, and later on shoved her hard into a wall, nearly knocking her out. He had tried his damndest to push the memories out of his head. But hearing that song on the radio made it all come crashing back again. It was then he realized it...... 'I still love her.....' Scott's hand quickly reaches out for the black cellphone which has fallen to the floor. He picks it up and quickly begins to push the buttons. He places the phone to his ear and listens to the rings. The phone keeps ringing and it seems like no one is going to be answering the phone. Just as Scott is about to hang up, someone picks up the phone. 'Hello?' But the voice on the other end isn't Teresa's. It's a male voice. A deep male voice with a Texas Accent. Scott nearly drops the phone, it's a voice he hasn't heard in a long time. It takes him a moment to muster up the words. 'Jack....Slayter? There is a short pause on the other end of the phone. Finally the voice speaks again, a sort of questioning tone present in his speech. '...Yeah? Who is this?' 'Scott.' 'Scott?' 'You heard me. Come on Ace, it's Scott Taylor.' More silence. Both men are confused, they haven't spoken in two years and neither is really too sure of what to say right now. Ace breaks the silence. 'Well, how have you been? I haven't seen you since I went out with that broken knee.' 'Yeah man, that was rough. I hated to see you go out like that. Is Teresa around?' 'Teresa...oh...she's in the shower.' 'Shower?!' Scott cannot see it but a huge smile forms on Slayter's face. He leans back against two of the pillows on the large King Sized bed and starts to speak again. 'Yeah, Scott. She's on the road again, with PCW this time. I'm her new manager. But I manage alot fo things. I managed her last night, and I'll manage her again tonight. She's one devil in the sack, I should say.' There is no reaction from Scott, he's shocked at what he's hearing. Slayter obviously realizes this and goes on. 'After you left her in that hotel room she moved on. She's back with me again. If only you coudl have heard her scream my name last night, you'd believe me. Sorry, Scotty. But....you lose.' With those cruel and obviously lying words Jack presses a button the phone which ends the call and then presses another which turns the phone off. He sits it down on the nightstand and lays back on the bed, looking up at the ceiling and smiling to himself at his handy work. Scott drops the phone onto the seat next to him and looks straight ahead. He just stares, no emotion. A blatant poker face is all he shows. Then, he slams his head against the steering wheel, causing the horn to go off but he doesn't care. He holds the steering wheel while resting his head against it as our scene fades out.
