Finally...It Has Arrived!!!

26 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-26-02 Our scene opens with a black screen, as the following words scroll up onto the screen. ‘Viewer discretion advised. Aurora public access does not claim responsibility for the actions of the two idiots who produce the next show or any other, possibly more stupid idiots that they may bring onto the program. With that said, may God have mercy on your souls.’ The words disappear as we fade in to reveal the living room of Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke, OSW Tag Team champions. The two members of Civil Disobedience are seated on the ratty sofa that pops up so often in these promos. They are looking like the fashion-ignorant rebels that they are, with their typical garb of heavy metal T-shirts and jeans...as if you expected some fantastic change in wardrobe anyway. Smiling, Seth gives a thumbs up to the camera as a cheap graphic appears on the screen in the foreground, displaying the two words that Old School Wrestling fans everywhere have been dreading for weeks: ‘Seth’s Wurld’. Yes, that farce of a promo idea is back, and back with a vengeance! “What’s up, OSW!” Seth states proudly, “Your eyes are not deceiving you. The wonderful public access show known to the millions of our fans as Seth’s World has finally made it’s way to OSW Television.” “I still hate that name,” Trevor mutters from the other side of the couch. “I don’t know why I ever let that name stick to begin with. You’re not the only one on this fucking couch, you know.” “If you’ll ignore my partner’s bitching, I’d like to get back to introducing the show,” Seth smiles innocently at the camera, while Trevor glares at him. “As I was saying, you may have caught the first episode on GTT TV. Damn, that’s a lot of T’s. Anyway, since the first installment, the folks running the airwaves over there have banned my program from ever running there again. Something about inappropriate amounts of profanity and all around inappropriate material.” “They obviously didn’t know that those things are what our whole careers are based upon,” Trevor breaks in sarcastically. “After that horrible termination of our contract, I wasn’t sure if I had the strength to go on. That type of thing isn’t easy to get over. I mean, I just felt so rejected and unwanted,” Seth states solemnly “The same feeling he’s felt after every single date he’s ever gone on,” Trevor remarks with a grin, prompting an angry stare from Seth. He waves for him to continue with his speech. “Go on...dead air, dead air. Come on, you can’t just sit there.” Seth glares at Trevor a bit more before returning his attention to the camera. “So, as I was saying, I was actually thinking of abandoning the show concept all together. After hours of deep contemplation, however, I thought of all the hard work I’ve put into this show. I mean, we went to fucking Canada and risked our lives to beat the shit out of Mike Meyers, thus earning the rights to keep this show’s name. And then I thought of you, the fans. How could I ever deprive you from seeing the hilarious segments that have yet to be cut on this show? I could never do such a thing to you people. I’m an asshole, but I’m not that cruel. So, rather than giving up on our fledgling broadcasting careers, I’ve decided to keep the show alive by bringing it to OSW TV! Whooooo!” Seth pumps his fist in the air excitedly, only to glance over to the other end of the couch and see Trevor sleeping. He glares at him again, this time complementing the glare with a kick to the stomach. Trevor jerks up, holding his midsection as he shakes his head to break out of the dazed state of sleep aftermath. “What the fuck are you doing sleeping?!” Seth yells. “You’re the worst co-host ever. You should be downgraded to sidekick for something like that.” “Well my name’s not even in the show’s damn title,” Trevor yells back. “I already feel like a damn sidekick. Hell, if you’re going to keep boring people’s asses like that, maybe I don’t want to be on the show at all.” “Maybe I don’t want you on the show!” shouts Seth, pointing a finger into Trevor’s chest. “Maybe I don’t want you on the show!” Trevor shouts back. “It wouldn’t be too hard to kick you off and call the thing Trevor’s World.” “You wouldn’t do that...would you?” Seth asks, not sounding too sure of himself.. “I might, if you press your luck,” Trevor says, grinning slyly. He obviously has no intentions of doing such a thing, but the fact that he could mess with Seth’s mind so easily gives him much pleasure. His grin widening, he decides to play along with it for a while. “I think for now that we should just get on with the show.” “Good idea,” Seth smiles nervously at the camera, as a few beads of sweat collect on his forehead. “Maybe we should bring out our first guest.” “No, not yet,” Trevor says, crossing his arms decisively. “Alright, the guest can wait,” Seth states, his eyes shifting from left to right uncertainly. “No, I changed my mind. The guest can come out now,” Trevor chuckles to himself, as Seth’s head looks as if it’s about to explode. “Ok then. Without further ado...” Seth begins, but is cut off by his partner once more. “Nah, I think we had better save the guest for later,” he states in a thoughtful tone. “Ahhhh! I can’t take this!” Seth screams, holding his head in pain. He falls to the floor, burying his head in between his knees as he curls up into a tight ball. “Geez man, I was just joking,” Trevor says, laughing at what he has done to his partner. “I’m not going to leave the fucking show. I was just messing with your head, you gullible fuck.” Trevor’s grin quickly fades as he sees Seth continuing to writhe on the ground despite his admission. “I said I’m sorry dude. Get up.” he says, starting to sound a bit concerned. “Ahhhh! It’s not anything you did, man! I took a hit of acid before the show started and it’s...just...kicking...in...now!” Seth screams. “Dude, don’t say that!” Trevor says, sounding worried. “This is going on OSW TV. If they fucking find out you’re tripping on acid they’ll fire you...and strip me of my OSW Tag Team title! I’ll end up like K-Kwik after the Road Dogg was released. I’ll be demoted to the XWA...or even worse, the PWA! No, come on, man! You have to say you were joking!” Seth crawls over to the couch where Trevor is sitting, bringing his hand up as if he were reaching out for help...then delivers a hard punch to Trevor’s gut, doubling him over. He collapses and falls to the floor in a heap. Now it’s Seth’s turn to laugh, as he stands up and brushes himself off. “Who’s the gullible fuck now, dipshit?! You really think I’d be stupid enough to talk about how I’m taking drugs on a show that will be viewed on my company’s airwaves?” Trevor can only moan in pain for his response. Satisfied with his work, Seth returns to his seat on the left side of the couch, wiping his hands on the armrest. “With my co-host slash tag team partner being currently incapacitated, I think I’ll use this lull in the show as a chance to discuss our match on this Saturday Night Slam.” “Good plan,” Trevor says weakly, crawling back onto the couch. “Because if there’s anything you’re good at, it’s talking shit. Backing it up...no so much so, but you sure can talk it. I need to rest now.” As Trevor lies in pain, Seth nods in agreement with him. “Damn right I’m good at talking shit, but this upcoming match doesn’t really have a demand for shit to be talked about it, if that makes any sense...which I’m sure it doesn’t. We’re defending our belts against our old buddies in Heresian Bloodlust, Azrael Cain and Hessian. Why we are doing so, I have no idea. The complete and utter lack of the OSW booking committee’s ability to find us a challenge is the only reason I can think of. We’ve beaten these guys in the ring at least a minimum of three times, if not more. It’s just going to be the same match, rehashed once again. I mean, we can try to come up with a new and exciting way to dispose of these losers and thus satisfy the many Civil Disobedience fans, but I can’t make any promises.” “I thought you said you weren’t going to talk shit about the match,” Trevor breaks in, wincing and grabbing his side as he does so. “No, I said the match doesn’t have a demand for shit to be talked about it, meaning that since it is so trivial it isn’t really deserving of it. That doesn’t mean that I wasn’t going to do so anyway.” explains Seth. “Ah,” Trevor nods, then waves his hand. “Continue.” “Gladly. Now, Heresian Bloodlust deserves a little bit of credit. They have beaten our asses many times in the past, and they are two strong son’s of bitches. But they are also two losing son’s of bitches, a statement backed up by the fact that they’ve never beaten us in the past. We’ve simply been too fast, too skilled, and, believe it or not, too smart for them. I don’t see the trend ending anytime soon, but they’re welcome to try. We are fighting champions, after all, and, as I’ve said in the past, ass kicking kicks ass. It’s time to remind Hessian and Cain once again that size doesn’t matter. They could be a couple of seven foot dicksmacks, or a couple four foot ones...in the end, the result is the same. We get the pin, the ref raises our hands, the announcer says our names, and we retain our belts. Did I leave anything out, Trev?” “No, I don’t think so,” Trevor states. “That’s some mighty fine shit-talking, Seth. Very cocky. Very overconfident. You’ve got the art down pat.” “I thought it was rather sub-par myself, but I guess it’ll do,” Seth remarks. “It’s so hard to be a perfectionist. Now how about we get around to bringing out the guest now.” “Alright, sounds cool to me,” Trevor says, still rubbing his ribs. “Who did we get booked today? Some dark gothic person? The guy that played Freddy Krueger, right? Or did you get Wes Craven? Yeah, he would be a good guest.” “Actually...” Seth says sadly, “All we have is Carrot Top.” “What!?” shouts Trevor, standing up angrily. “I don’t want to talk to Carrot Top! The man is a fucking idiot! And he has nothing to do at all with Heresian Bloodlust! If we bring him out this whole show will make no fucking sense!” “Heaven forbid the show make no sense!” Seth shouts sarcastically, getting a glare from Trevor. After a break of silence, Trevor continues his tirade. “Where’s the goddamn flow? It doesn’t link together! This show is supposed to be devoted to promoting our match this weekend and the guest doesn’t even match the theme! Carrot Top is as far as you can get from Heresian Bloodlust! Who the hell booked him! Even if our show wasn’t wrestling related, I wouldn’t want him on! He’s the worst possible guest ever! EVER!” “Whoa dude, settle down,” Seth says, trying to calm Trevor. “If you don’t want to talk to Carrot Top then you don’t have to talk to Carrot Top. We’ll reschedule him or something.” “I don’t even want to continue with this farce. Let’s cut the show off now. After your little antics earlier, I feel like shit,” complains Trevor, as he walks offscreen angrily. “Geez, who would have thought the mention of Carrot Top could provoke such a violent mood swing?” Seth says, shaking his head in disbelief. “Carrot Top is a piece of shit!” Trevor shouts from off-camera. Seth shakes his head again, turning to address the camera once more. “Well I guess the show is over now. I guess it was a pretty shitty first episode for OSW, but, as you can in just about any situation, it’s only too easy to shift the blame to Carrot Top. So, on behalf of OSW fans everywhere, I’d like to give Carrot Top a big fuck you!” Seth smiles and raises his middle finger, pointing it at the screen. It is with this image that we fade out. “Oh, and don’t forget to tune in this Saturday to watch me and Trev kick some goth ass!” Seth says quickly, getting it in right before the feed cuts off entirely. We cut to a board room full of about seven Old School Wrestling accountants. They are all slapping their heads, shooting disbelieving looks at each other, or performing similar acts of irritation as they turn off the television set that was just playing Civil Disobedience’s latest promo. “We fund this garbage?” one of them asks disbelievingly. “Holy shit, that was worst piece of crap I’ve seen since the guy that played Kramer from Seinfeld’s sitcom.” “Or worse, the guy who played George’s sitcom,” another remarks. “Or worse, the chick who played Elaine’s,” a third states. “Yes that was definitely a bigger pile of crap then all of Seinfeld’s supporting cast’s sitcoms combined,” agrees a fourth man. “Eh, it could be worse,” another executive pipes in from across the room. “They could cut promos where they’re walking around pressing their groins up against people, or having their gay manager whack off while watching tapes of their opponents. Those were quite disturbing.” “Agreed,” the other executives say simultaneously. “Say...you guys wouldn’t mind if I borrowed those Switchblade tapes tonight, would you?” a man in a pink suit near the front of the room asks sheepishly, licking his lips in anticipation. And with this the scene fades out again...this time for good. It’s probably better off that way.
