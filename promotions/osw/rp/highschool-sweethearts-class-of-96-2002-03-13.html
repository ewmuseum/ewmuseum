Highschool Sweethearts, Class of '96

13 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 13th, 2002 Glancing over the headlines, screaming out for the readers to cast their beady little eyes upon...thumbing through magazine, resting on magazine, resting on magazine...thinking of how all these little pieces of paper actually inform people with their mindless jargon...all of it...interrupted with the cry of one sultry voice only feet away. 'Ethan?' His head turns, the voicing of his name making those eyes of his widen with confusion and immediate, undaunting rage...literally blinding him. 'Ethan Gabriel Giroux?! Is that you?!?' His mind seems to make that squared head of his bulge in remembrance. A part of him scrapes forth and tears at his insides to step towards that high pitched voice soaked with euphoric tones he hadn't heard before. Confusion floods into him...something is not right, but oh so familiar. 'Ethan? My God...you're so...big...' And, finally, those blind eyes of his rise to that image. Dormant thoughts race to the surface and itch at his skull, telling him he knows who this is before him. He reaches a single hand out in front of him and rests it upon her brow. The touch seems to make the image all so clear. His brows perk up, cheeks rise in a smile, for once not meant to intimidate or shock or terrify...this one's cooperating with all those hibernating, unopened memories...and it's making him happy. 'Clara Deveaux...' He knows her name. Her pink cheeks rise as well, masked in the same eerie fashion as the man before her. Those cheeks cradle the tears slowly rushing from her emerald green eyes, her full lips reaching skyward to convey every emotion within her. She takes his much larger hand in hers and pulls it to her side. 'Ethan...where...what...h...how are you?' Her mind speaks fluent sorrow through her masked smile. 'I'm...I'm fine...' He speaks, not the slightest bit of him knowing exactly why. 'How...are ya?' She turns her face and releases his hand from her grip, shooing away the strands of auburn hair creeping over her eyes. He still has no idea who she is, yet his body and mind seek to tell him differently. As she clutches his hand again, the irrepressible gaze of her green eyes falls upon him once more. 'I've been alright...I have two kids...so I keep myself busy.' 'Good...good...' A simplistic nod and clueless smile from Ethan. A moment passes as she stares up at him, memories of her own sprinting through her mind. Her lips quiver, unsure of whether to place her mind on the good times they'd apparently shared or to frown upon the possible lowly ones. Another tear rolls free from her eye and streaks down her cheek, begging for solice. 'No need ta cry...' A single finger, catching that tear before it made its complete journey down her face, and sending shivers throughout her entire body. Her eyes slam shut and her chest rises skyward. Lips part and release a gasp, hands fall to her sides...her lower body inching forward in remembrance. 'Clara...are ya alright?' Such gentle, eerily frightening tones sweeping forth from Ethan's mouth. So haunting...misleading...uncategorized. A caring demeanor actually adorning the beast like a pair of angel's wings. 'I'm...I'm sorry, Ethan...' pleading through wimpered cries, '...it's just...since you were taken that day...' '...taken?' He has no patience, no time to think. He's done his searching...what little he can tolerate of it...and he's finished with that. He wants the answers now and abandons himself as a resource. 'Yes...you remember that day...you have to.' Ethan's eyes narrow, nearly appauled she lacks the knowledge of what has happened to him. That confusion immediately gets swatted to the side and replaced by that emotion that's fueled him since the very day his own memory was robbed from his free hands. Hands shoot away from her face...and for once, they do not lash right back up for her throat, her face, or her body. They stay stable and at his side, desparately trying to keep his emotions at bay. However, his rage is all too evident in those ice blue eyes. 'I don't, Clara.' 'I'm...sorry, Ethan...what happened to you?' Persistence does not hault violence...a lesson Clara should learn...quickly. A snarl and a growl replace those heartfelt tones present in his voice just moments ago. Ethan stands straight, craning his neck downward and clutching those weapons he calls fists at the curvy redhead pushing herself into his life once more. 'I don't know, Clara...I don't even know why the f*ck I know yer goddamned name.' Her eyes well with tears again, prompting her to turn her head away from the past above her. She tries to focus on anything to make those tears stop coming, anything...but nothing seems to come as a qualified host for her eyes. 'Look at me.' Little does it take to make her do so, her eyes hiding a feeling beyond fondness. Slowly and cautiously, her vision blurred, impaired from her own physical display of anguish, she stares up into Ethan's eyes. She delves for a moment, tries to search for some resemblance of what she once knew inside him. 'You're telling me...you don't remember...me?' Her voice inches out in terrified peeps, trying to creep to a nonexistent means of sanctuary and hide an emotion we begin assuming lies within her. 'No.' So strapped with his own words today...unusual. She never ceases to stare...always looking straight above her own frame, up to this man who used to resemble something of one. Her thoughts bombard her from all sides again as she combs through the reasons he's acting less than civil towards her. All she can do is stare and try to find a door to creep under in hopes of finding answers on the other side. Everything spills out her ears and to the sidewalk beneath her in a pile of unused foolery. Swept off her feet by the lapels of that tight business suit she was wearing, Ethan grasps her freely with one massive hand, knuckles white with pressure. Everything is returning to normality again. This is our Ethan. 'Yer danglin', doll...care ta tell me how I should know ya?' Clara's eyes widen with fear as she realizes she can't even begin to know this man. She might have once...but it must have been before everything. Before all the pain...the agony...the surgery...the institution...all of it. Before all this...change. 'Tell me...' 'Ethan...what happened?' Again, that persistance. The beast pushes her forward and grinds his teeth together, growling into her terrified eyes. Intimidation kills persistance. 'I'm sorry...I'm sorry...' Not the tone of fear he liked...but it would have to do. 'Let me down, Ethan...please...just put me back on my feet...' His eyes blink...and that rage, almost instantly, empties itself from them. He slowly lowers her to the ground, a small smile of thanks falling over her lips. She reaches into the breast pocket of her suit and retrieves a velvet cloth appearing to be wrapped about something. As she pulls the folds away, Ethan's head tilts to the side in curiosity, impatience almost scurrying to the surface. His fists open and close, fingers wiggling in anticipation of what it may be she's taking out. It's almost horriffic seeing Ethan in such an unusual state. 'You gave this to me...three days before your mother died...six before you left Louisiana.' She holds her hand out as Ethan's face goes white. His eyes don't even attempt to register, nor can they do so. Something inside him seems to drop to the ground beneath him and shatter, shattering into shards of shrapnel that dig into every piece of his mangled body. For a moment, he roars as if he were an animal caught in the jaws of a trap he had failed to notice. But, all too quick, he quiets the screech and swallows, trying to keep his eyes focused in front of him as his breath nearly haults and barks out in short, unaltered gasps. 'My God, Ethan...are you okay?!' Clara puts a hand to the beast's chest, which is instantly swatted aside. He growls at her and breathes, snapping back to reality. He groans and clutches his head in his hand, strands of hair floating down over his paw in silent descent. 'What didja do ta me?!' 'I...I didn't do anything..I just said your moth--' Ethan's entire body swiftly turns back to Clara, his arm swinging up in unison. He grips her by the sides of her face and hoists her into the air, stepping forward and pinning her into the magazine rack behind her. 'What...why...what...where...why...what happened...' A disgruntled and confused inquiry blurted out in unbridled nonsense, Ethan's mind shaking his emotions around like a child. Clara's face again is bathed in agony and physical pain as she cries out and begs for him to drop her. Ethan's eyes remain locked on hers as she struggles to free herself, bringing her heeled feet into his torso. He drops her and clutches his head again, falling to one knee and growling as Clara clings to the rack behind her, chest heaving and face trying to quell the waterfall of tears. She stares down at the beast before her and slowly steps to the side. Ethan simply allows himself to breathe, eyes closed and head still turned down to the cement beneath him. He sees her in every manner possible, yet she seems to think she's invisible to his animalistic instinct. 'Doncha go anywhere...don't even f*ckin' think bout it...' Ethan slowly rises, breath resuming to a normal pace as he leans forward over her and presses his forehead against hers. Clara seems to inherit the breath he held a moment ago as he grits his teeth. 'Who...the f*ck are ya...?' An answer she gives with a raised hand, a thick banded ring perched between her thumb and forefinger. Ethan glances up to the object and then looks back down to her fear ridden eyes as she peers at him, hoping for the best. He swings his hand up, snatching the ring from her grasp and making her jump like a scared deer. 'And what's this?' 'That....that's what you gave to me on that day...class ring...class of '96...' Clara barely manages to spurt out her broken English through fits of whimpers and distressed yelps. 'Ya...were my...girlfriend...?' Ethan's face contorts into a worried grimace and slowly fades to regret as he slowly raises his eyes to Clara's face. 'I loved ya...didn't I?' A slow, hesitant nod manages to put Clara's head into motion as she begins standing more freely from her rest against the magazine rack behind her. 'And..I loved you too...very much...and you just...left...without a word...' The beast pushes away from her and pulls the ring up to one of his own fingers, its size dwarfed compared to the thickness of his digits. He turns slowly back to Clara and blinks his good eye, her vision finally flooding to the scar on his face. She gasps as he throws his head back to give her a better view. 'Ya know anythin' bout this?' Sickeningly slow he traces a finger down the shiny, crude scar tissue squinting his right eye nearly closed. Clara slowly shakes her head, still fighting off an onslaught of tears. 'All I know is that you left...the second semester of our sophomore year...no one knew where you went...I mean...rumors went around...that Doug kid you beat up pretty bad said they'd institutionalized you...' A growl packed with such bass that it vibrates Clara's bones sends her clinging to the wall again. '...they couldn't have...could they?' Ethan simply glares back at her, huge shoulders and chest rising with each breath he takes in. She covers her mouth slowly and begins to sob anew, almost as if she was attending a funeral. '...how long?' 'Stop yer proddin'...Ethan's dead, Clara. He went a lil nutty and left me in his place...he left me ta clean up all the sh*t in his life and git things goin' fer the better...' His mind fighting off the emotions that clouded him...they must have been scaring him. Nothing scares this man...this beast...nothing...but his own thoughts and those memories of the sanitarium. They were getting to him and she was aiding in their surfacing. He had new knowledge of his past...a dead mother...he at least attended high school in Louisiana...a girlfriend at the time he was instituionalized. It was all creeping back to him and he knew she wasn't lying...he knew she was telling the truth. Like a psychiatrist's visit, she was waking old, sleeping memories. Ethan was beginning to remember her. He was even picturing them on their first date, hands clasped in youth's foolish classification of love. Inklings of his past start to surface in his mind as he stares at this woman in front of him. He knows she's being honest. He knows everything she said is true. He knows because he still has his memory. 'Ethan...why did they put you into an institution? You weren't crazy...you were so kinda...so caring...you were going somewhere in life...why would they...' 'NUFF!!!!!!!' A command releasing from his lungs as more of a bark than anything. 'You still sound the same, Ethan...' 'Shut the f*ck up!!!' His anger rising once more. Those thoughts leaving him...those new memories beginning to build upon themselves and lay beneath him as a foundation. He doesn't want to surface anything else. Ethan is getting angry. That needs to be dealt with first. 'B*TCH!!!! WATCHER F*CKIN' MOUHT FORE I TEAR YER GOD FERSAKEN LIPS OFF!!!!' '...Ethan.......' Her face showing every ounce of emotion she needed him to see. Fuel to the fire. Ethan's arm, seeming to think on its own, swoops Clara up from under her arm, pulling her dark skinned body into the air and holding her there. A scream of agony and pain fills the air about the people surrounding them...all watching...afraid of this man. They've been observing him from his first move and he's now just noticing. That madness in his head shuts out such factors with the greatest of ease. Always have. Ethan tilts his head left and right, surveying the new people around him as they seem to slowly solidify out of thin air. He looks back up to his crying former love and shakes her viciously. 'Quitcher cryin'!!' Clara tries...but there's no possible way. Serving as her only sense of anything resembling relief from the pain shooting through her body, she sobs and whimpers, stifling each cry that seeps out through her stretching muscles. Ethan pulls her closer and inhales her scent like some sickened dog and pulls his face slowly away from her tiny body. He licks his lips slowly, dragging his tongue along his lips in a sexual manner we'd rather not see on his face. Clara stares down at him, her cries ceasing for a moment. 'Ya member anythin' bout this?' His tongue slithers over her thigh as he lifts her higher. Clara's body screams out in pain as he pushes her even higher to the air. Her mind seems to focus on Ethan's busy tongue as he rakes it across her bare thigh. She tries to shrug off the feeling but fails to cut off a throaty moan that beckons Ethan's face to contort into a sheepish, sickeningly defiant grin. He immediately pull his tongue away and drops her straight to the ground. 'Old memories there, Clara? Ever had my tongue roamin' inside ya like a rusty sextoy?' That bullying brute inside Ethan that is Meanstreak pulls Clara up by that same left arm and holds her there, glaring into her eyes for the longest moment. He breathes heavily, another tactic to intimidate her even further and finally speaks through those thin lips. 'Yer not gonna have dreams bout me anymore after this week, doll...yer gonna wish my mommy never died...yer gonna wish I'da been sick that day they carted me off ta the nuthouse from school...yer gonna wish ya never knew Ethan Gabriel Giroux.' The beast pulls Clara up with one hand and stands to his full height. He releases his grasp from the back of her suit and drops her, abdomen first straight atop his hulking shoulder. Clara's breath escapes her upon impact, leaving her gasping for air and struggling to rush what little she has to her brain to avoid suffocation. Ethan begins to walk away from the cluttered magazine rack, the people surrounding him parting as he passes. The monster's intimidation apparently affects more than just Clara Deveaux. Every single person in his way darts out of his path, regardless of the fact he has a grown woman slung over his shoulder. Cowardice never leaves a man's heart. Or perhaps...it flees when that man becomes a monster.
