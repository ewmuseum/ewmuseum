Pre Match Thoughts...

23 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/23/02 Tom Walczak is sitting in his hotel room hours before the nights SLAM! He has a pair of headphones on and he is listening to Hoobastank’s self-titled album, as he holds his forehead thinking deeply about tonight’s match. He cannot sit still anymore because of the adrenaline that is running through his body. He gets up and walks over to the mini bar on the other side of the room. He pours himself three shot glasses of Vodka, and then fills the bottle up again with water. He drinks the vodka, and goes back to sit down on the bed again. He takes his headphones off, but still cannot stay still. Then he just screams. “ARGGGGGGGGH!!!!!! LOCKE”S LIFE IS GOING TO END!!!!!” He stops and tries to control his anger that comes more from adrenaline than from hate. “I will kick his ass tonight, and after the beating he gets he will just pray that I will let him live. I am torn between two people and I just can’t decide who’s ass I will kick first. First there is the Enforcer, he is a worthless piece of shit, who has the displeasure of fighting my fellow comrade Michael Krieg. If things go my way than I don’t see him lasting more than this will be his last week as part of this federation. Then there is that freak assed son of a bitch Rhett Locke. This kid has to get his act together, but I don’t see him being much of a problem. He is just half my size and no amount of speed or heart that he possesses will help him in this match. I am sure that he will put in a valiant effort, and who knows he may even get a shot or two in on me, but I am determined to wipe the mat with his bloodied body, and by the end of the night he will not be able to stand on the power of his own two feet. Locke you may be the first Intercontinental Champion, but mark my words you will not be the greatest, no that distinction will go to me. You see once I take care of your sorry ass I will prepare to wreak havoc on all those that oppose me whoever they may be. Big, small, smart, or dumb there ain’t going to be anyone or anything that can stop me.” Walczak goes back to listening to his music, and as the CD nears its end Walczak once again goes to take down a couple of shots. He wipes his mouth clean of the remaining liquid and goes to pack his things for the match. He puts his boots and shorts in a bag, and later places his gloves in as well. He goes and fills an empty bottle up with water, and places that in the bag. “Well, now I am off. Tonight will be a great night not only for me, but for The Red Army. Comrade Stanislav will come one step closer to the world title in what shall be a sure victory over Doug Troy. Yes this will be a great night indeed.” Walczak puts on his jacket and gets ready to head outside as the scene fades to black….
