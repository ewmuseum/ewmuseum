So What if Aimz Doesn't Wear Underwear?

12 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04-12-02 “So what you are telling me is that this “OSW” company of yours treats you pretty well?” came a voice. “Pretty damned good when Meanstreak isn’t beating the daylights out of me.” “I dunno Timo, this sounds like another one of your hair brained schemes that almost certainly gets me in trouble.” The scene fades in and we see our favorite Samoan and his good buddy Mexicano Momar standing around the garage of Timo’s rockin Detroit pad. The room is vast and filled with many vehicles of great significance in American history. Timo leans against a large tool chest filled with Craftsman tools, the top opened to reveal several socket driver sets, and an impact hammer. Mexi sits on a stool playing with a small rotary grinder. He puts down and looks up to see Timo smiling. “No Timo, I know that smile, and I don’t want anything to do with it!” “Oh come on Mexi, it isn’t like I’m going to ask you to pole dance or anything.” “No, you have Amy for that right?” Timo gets a sour look on his face and Mexi shrugs. “What?” is the reply to the look. “Man, things aren’t like that between us. Hell, I’m old enough to be her….really old brother!” “Dude.” Mexi adds. “What?” “What’s wrong with you? Every guy in PTC is drooling over her, and here you are saying she is just one of the guys? For Christ’s sake man, you are talking about AIMZ, here.” “So what is that supposed to mean?” Mexi sighs and stands up a moment, clapping his hand on Timo’s shoulder and making a “walk with me” motion. Timo follows as they walks through his shop. Mexi stops and picks up a small black thong and matching bra from inside a Shelby 350 GT and holds them up. “So?” “SO? That’s all you have to say?” “She doesn’t like wearing underwear man, what else is there to it?” “You really expect me to believe that? I know for a fact that you love this car…” Timo holds his hand up and Mexi falls silent. Timo reaches in and pulls a pair of keys out that have Amy’s name on them. “This is her car, I bought it a month or so ago.” “Okay, but explain why her lipstick is on this old shirt over here.” They walk over to a work bench, and Mexi picks up a dirt stained white “Samoan Silencer” tee shirt. It has a dark red shade of lipstick around the collar. Mexi turns to Timo and holds it up” “Eh, what do you have to say about that Mr. Samoan?” “That’s an old shirt man, I have had it since I started dating with Gwenneth. If I am not mistaken, that distinct smell is Gwenneth’s perfume. You know, the one that costs me about ninety bucks a pop?” Mexi smells the shirt, and shakes his fist. “Curse you Samoan…I will expose the truth!” They both share a good laugh. Just then the doorknob creaks and they both turn their attention to the noise. Standing in the doorway is none other than the lady of whom they had just been speaking of, Amy Campbell. “Well speak of the devil and he shall appear.” Said Timo. She walks into the room, and walks over tot he two men that are standing by her car. Timo nods and leans against the bench, Mexi stares at her nearly see through white tee shirt. He shakes his head a bit then turns to Timo. “I guess you were right about her not wearing underwear.” She gives them both a strange look, and Timo shrugs. He begins to whistle and play with a torque wrench while Mexi realizes the error of his words and blushes a bit. “So, what have YOU two been talking about?” Mexi stammers a bit, and Timo laughs and holds his hand out for the high five, which Aimz gives him. She reaches her hand out and puts it on Mexi’s shoulder, smiling. “So then ex-boss, how you been?” Mexi looks back at her and smiles. The red headed vixen of his old fed, and Timo’s partner in crime awaits an answer. He simply nods and extends a hand, which she takes. “Oh you know me, a little of this, a lot of that. I heard about your promotion, good job girl, I knew you had it in you. Lemme tell yah, you are working with the top of the ladder here.” Timo sighs. “What’s the matter good buddy?” “Lately things have been down, I just don’t feel up to par. Aside from Meanstreak beating the tar out of me, I have been in good shape, but I just don’t seem to have the fire in me.” Mexi nods and cracks his knuckles. The Samoan looks up and wonders what is going through the mind of Mexicano, but he sees naught but a brilliantly clever glimmer in them. Timo smiles back and looks to Aimz. As usual, she is grinning ear to ear. “What do you suggest?” Timo asks. “Well man, whenever you are down, what brings you up?” retorts Mexi. “I like cars.” “Well, what if we went car shopping? Maybe it could give me time to think about this OSW you keep talking about. In the meantime, I don’t think we should worry about it, your always going to be working with me in some way Timo.” Timo opens up a “Classic Car” magazine and looks down at an ad for the movie “Vanishing Point”. Suddenly a light bulb flashes over his head and a smile crawls across his face. “Guys, I think I know what we can do for some fun.”
