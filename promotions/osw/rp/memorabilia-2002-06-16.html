Memorabilia...

16 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/16/02 We see Tom Walczak walking through a memorabilia store. It contains various sports merchandise, but what Walczak is interested in is the wrestling items. He begins to look through the various posters, and signed memorabilia, and then a sales clerk comes over to help him. “Is their anything that I can do to help you sir?” “Well, I was just looking at this OSW stuff, and I was wondering if you could tell me whose merchandise sells the most?” “Well, Rhett Locke has become a big hit as of late, and Aimz was doing great, but for some reason sales dropped today. Reno Davis, has been coming on strong the past couple months, and El Drago is a big hit with the international crowd, but I would have to say that the most consistent selling person would have to be Doug Troy.” “HAHAHAHAHA!!!!!! That was a good joke that you played on me, but seriously did you expect me to be that stupid? You realized that I was Tom Walczak, and wanted me to second guess the Red Army’s popularity, and for a while you had me going, but than you brought in Troy and you ruined it.” “Sir, I am not joking. Doug is an enormous hit with the fans. He has support from young toddlers, and the older crowd as well.” “This cannot be so. Well, If Doug Troy is number one than the Red Army surely cannot be far behind, can they?” “The Red Army?? Who are they?? Oh wait, I know who they are, but no one has asked for any of their merchandise in over a month. They were a big sell a little over a month ago, but then they just sort of died.” “Do you have any of their merchandise?” “I think I have a box somewhere over here.” The sales clerk takes Walczak to the back of the store, and opens up a box. As he takes the lid off he begins to cough from the dust that has come out. He removes a couple of cobwebs and takes a picture out. He looks at it and than at Walczak and realizes that they are the same person. “So you are part of the Red Army?” “Of course I am.” “Well, I feel sorry that your merchandise isn’t selling, but there is nothing that I can do to help that.” “I found your problem. You do not display the merchandise, you keep it back here so that it could build up dust.” “Well, I took them down about two weeks ago because they weren’t selling, and if people were to ask about the posters I would put them out again.” “How can they ask if they can’t even SEE THEM ON DISPLAY!?!?!?!?!?!?!?!?” “There is no need to raise your voice sir.” “This is not raising my voice, but I would appreciate if you display them. How can the world champion of OSW not even be on Display?” “Isn’t Mr. Troy the champion?” “For Now…” Walczak turns around with a smile slowly coming to his face, and as he leaves the store the scene fades to black…
