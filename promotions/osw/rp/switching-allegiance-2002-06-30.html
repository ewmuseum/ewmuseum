Switching Allegiance

30 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30.06.02 Who’d ever have thought it? The man who owned so much to Ivan shoving it right back in his face. Showing some appreciation there, huh? The scene opens with Michel Krieg sitting on a couch in what appears to be some sort of green room, directly after Slam!’s airing. Sitting back, the troubles of a few hours ago don’t seem to have affected Michel’s state of mind much at all, a smug grin across his face suggesting this. In front of him, about a metre or so away, a television and VCR combo sits on top of a dresser, Michel holding the joint remote in his right hand, pointing it at the screen. The action on-screen suddenly stops and rewinds, all the motions reversing and the screen displaying jagged static lines across it. The static ceases and the video plays again, and as the camera pans in on the screen, the tape can be seen as one of the Kory Storm, Frankie Bones and Michel Krieg attack on both El Drago and Ivan Stanislav earlier in the evening. Michel stops the tape just after the point where Bones takes down Drago with a vicious chair shot to the skull. The en suite bathroom door opens and out steps one of Krieg’s new best friends, former IOW World Champion, Kory Storm. Spotting what Michel is watching, Storm smiles a sadistic, evil smile as he sits down next to Michel, patting his shoulder as he does, “Man, we couldn’t have done it better if we tried,” claims Kory, still smiling as Michel rewinds the tape to the point where Storm and Bones clock Stanislav from both sides with chairs, “Kory, I don’t think we could,” confirms Michel, “To be quite honest with you friend, I’m quite glad I’ve got that heap of lard off of my back. I was getting sick of Ivan telling me what to do, what to say, how to beat people...” Nodding in approval, Storm agrees, “Yeah man. I would be too. The good thing about the partnership me, you and Frankie have got going is we do what we want, when we want and how we want. That’s the way it should be,” “I couldn’t agree more, Comrade,” Storm and Krieg continue to watch the tape a couple more times, and as they watch it for the third time, former 3B member, Frankie Bones opens the locker room door and comes in smiling as the video reaches the point when Ivan grabs Michel by the testicles and then levels both Storm and Bones. Michel immediately stops the tape with disgust and stands up, greeting Bones with a hand shake and a brief hug, Storm doing the same, “What’s up, fellas,” asks Frankie, standing next to the television, still smiling, “Oh, nothing much Comrade,” answers Michel, “Me and Kory were just saying how well that plan came off...Who cares if they got the best of us at the end?...We cost Ivan the match, and that’s fine for me,” “Absolutely Michel,” confirms Storm, patting Michel on the shoulder once more, “The look on his face after he got pinned was priceless,” The three men chuckle briefly and Frankie continues, “Yeah man. That fat Russian ass never saw this coming, now did he,” Storm and Bones slap a high five and then one of each of Krieg’s massive paws rest on each man’s shoulders, silencing them briefly, “Comrades...I think it is time to plan our next move,” With that, Storm and Bones smile sadistically and all three men sit down, Michel and Storm sitting back down on the couch and Bones pulling up a chair, sitting on it backwards as the scene slowly fades out to black. What was to come next for this next faction of sorts? Was this a permanent arrangement or just a temporary alliance of men with common goals? We will but wait and see...
