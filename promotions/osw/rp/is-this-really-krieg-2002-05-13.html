Is This Really Krieg?

13 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13.05.02 A shadow of a man staggers through the darkness along an alley, knocking occasional bags and trashcans over. Litter goes sprawling over the alleyway, the knocking of cans echoing like bullets. The man stumbles further into the alley and eventually walks over to a door, pulling the large steel bar acting as the doors handle, a flash of light and the blast of loud music escaping from the somewhat hidden venue. Stepping through the door’s threshold, the figure looks large in comparison to the rest of the venue’s occupants, walking towards the bar area as some of the other people near the bar look around at the man, casting a questionable look in his direction before turning back to the bar. Brushing past a few people, shoving them to the side, he continues to stagger towards the bar, eventually reaching it and slamming his arms down on the marble top of the bar, shuddering the rest of the glasses slightly, “Barman,” There was no response from the smartly dressed barman as he continues to serve other customers, “….Barman,” roared the man, stretching his neck out as to get a better view of the bar, Still no response from the barman, who appears to be slightly rushed off his feet. Suddenly, as the barman comes scooting past, the man reaches out and grabs the bar attendant by the collar and pulls him half-way over the counter, “Barman,” shouted the man once more, directly into the barman’s face, “I want a drink and I want one now,” he bawled, “Emm…I’m actually busy sir,” replied the barman, trying to free himself from the man’s grip, wriggling to get out of his grasp, “I do not care if you are busy or not. I want drink…now,” roared the monster of a man, hoisting the barman up another foot or so, the American drinks expert dangling in the air, “I..I…I think I can accommodate your needs, sir,” squealed the barman, being dropped suddenly onto the ground sending him backwards into the bar area, Smiling, the man continues, “That is much better, my friend. I shall have your finest Carlsberg, Comrade,” Looking around in fear, the barman leans towards the stranger, “Sir…We don’t serve Carlsberg here. We do not serve European bears,” The eyes of the man flamed up and widened as he stared straight into the eyes of the fearful barman, “Siii…iir…I’m extremely sorry for not having any beer to yo..” The barman can’t finish his sentence as he is pulled onto the bar once again and goes skidding along on his belly, knocking bottles and glasses off of the bar as he finally careers into the pillar at the end of the bar, “You Capitalist! How dare you disgrace Europe and its fine bears! You are nothing but Intercontinental Scum,” screamed the beast, turning to face the camera ever so slowly, Michel Krieg. Krieg stood up from his bar stool and stared right into the camera, still staring wide-eyed into the camera’s lens, “This is an outrage! I am ashamed to be in such a bar where only Ameri-kun bears are served! I am leaving in protest,” With that said, Krieg staggers towards the door he came in and shoves a few people out of the way, ramming the metal door with his oversized palm and just about knocking the sturdy door off of its hinges, “Dah, this is pathetic! I can not believe the competition I am facing on SLAM,” said Krieg, almost as if he was talking to one of his Communist comrades, even though he was alone as he stormed off down the alley, “Wright: some Irish leprechaun…Kimbusa: a Japanese heavyweight Sushi eater…” Krieg neared the road as he looked around at the end of the alley and made a stiff left turn as he continued to mark off down the road, talking to himself, “El Drago: The Mexican Tequila Freak…Reno Davis: The Kana-dian Warrior…Yeah right,” Krieg suddenly stops dead on the sidewalk and dips his head slightly, “….Ivan….” Suddenly, a jeep pulls up and the window rolls down as Krieg slowly turns round to look in the window, Fade out.
