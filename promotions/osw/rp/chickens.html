CHICKENS~!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/10/2 Scene opens on a small dirt country road that has been worn into the ground by the odd four wheel drive truck that has been looking to take a short cut. The dirt road leads to a big sign that says “Now Entering: Las Vegas, Nevada”. As the camera looks past the sign, we can see that below the rocky mountainside lies the bright flashing lights of Las Vegas. But for now we are still on this tiny insignificant dirt road leading to one of the greatest cities in America. The only movement comes from a tumble weed drifting with the wind and a small white feathery chicken walking across the road, bobbing it’s head and clucking. “Cluck! Cluck! Cluck! Cluck!” The small chicken lowers it’s head and picks a tiny piece of gravel up in it’s beak, and begins to chew it up to help it digest food later on. Once it is finished, it continues it’s slow and noisy trip across the small dirt road. “Cluck! Cluck! Cluck! Cluck!” It doesn’t hear the approaching sound...sounds like a low growl... vrrrrrrrooooooooom..... “Cluck! Cluck! Cluck! Cluck!” The sound begins to get louder, but the chicken still doesn’t hear... vrrrrrOOOOOO..... “Cluck! Cluck! Cluck! Cluck!” The chicken, now in the middle of the road, finally turns around when startled by the noise... VROOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOM!!!! SPLAT! The chicken disappears in an explosion of blood and feathers as a jet black Honda Civic smacks into it at 80 mph. The car screeches to a halt and a man gets out. As he walks towards the site of the collision, he waves feathers away from his face as they drift about, some of them blood stained. When the man comes closer, it becomes apparent that it is OSW’s very own Mike Marvelous. He is being followed closely by Kenny, his over weight personal cameraman, Mini-Mike...his midget clone, and flanking them is Mike’s new found friend, Sparky the Two-Legged Steel-Testicled Talking Wonder Dog. He is pulling himself forward with his front legs as sparks shoot up as his steel testicles scrape against the ground. Mike walks over to a small pile of mangled meat and feathers that used to resemble a chicken. He takes one look at this and does the only rational thing that comes to his mind. He vomits into the ditch. “BLAAAARGH!” Kenny runs over to Mike and pats him on the back, talking to him with a steady voice as he vomits into the ditch with enough force to knock Rosie O’Donnell off her feet. “It’s ok, boss. Just keep it comin’. Don’t try to stop it...there ya go...you’re doin’ good....” “BLOOORPH!” Another splatter of vomit in the ditch and Mike coughs up the remaining bile that is oozing up from his stomach. He wipes his mouth and puts his hand to his forehead in disgust. “Kenny....come here....” “Ye....yes, boss?” Kenny gingerly waddles over to Mike and leans in close.... SLAP! “Kenny! Watch where you are driving! This is the second animal we’ve hit today!” This last comment raises Sparky’s brow and he speaks up. “Yeah, tubbo. Only I was a bit more lucky than ol’ Mrs. Cluck over here...” Kenny runs his fingers through his greasy hair and wrinkles his forehead up. “Ah geez, boss. I’m sorry. I’ll try to be more careful, honestly.” Mike lets out a long sigh and then shakes his head. “I sure hope so for you’re sake. Now...what are we going to do with this chicken. We just can’t leave it here to rot on this tiny redneck road...” “I dunno, boss...maybe we can—“ Mike and Kenny are interrupted as a fat man with denim overalls and no shirt on walks up. He looks down at the former chicken and spits a stream of brown chewing tobacco out on the dirt. He takes his straw hat off and tips it to Mike, Kenny, and company. “Why don’t yo’ boiys jus’ given dat’ der chi’ken over ta me, aw-right? I’ll be takin’ goood care of ‘im...” Mike looks at Kenny and Kenny looks at Mike, then Mike looks at this slack jawed gawker with a slight expression of disgust on his face. “And what, may I ask, will you do with it?” A grin spreads across the face of the hillbilly, showing half his teeth are missing and the ones that are still there are yellow and have brown stains on them from tobacco. “Well...what else but eat im’. Them’s chi’kens is gooood eatin’. It don’t matter none if e’s a bit messed up!” Mike looks at the corpse, then looks at this revolting redneck that stands before him, then nods his head. “Ok...s...sure. Take him...do what you want with him...” Excitement washes over the redneck’s face and he even dances a little jig and waves his straw hat in the air. “Sooooo EEEE!!! Daisy May’s gon’ be so HAPPY! We’s havin’ FRIED CHI’KEEEN to-nayt! Have yer’selves a g’day, strangers. Y’all come back now!” “Yes...yes, we will....Good day sir...” The hillbilly scoops up the remnants of the chicken and slides them into the front pouch pocket off his overalls then scurries off into the woods. Mike stares after him in awe, then he shakes his arms and his head as if he has the heebie jeebies or something. “Good Lord...if I would’ve thought we would have to deal with back woods yuppies like that, I would have told you to take the long route, Kenny.” “Yeah...maybe this short cut wasn’t such a good idea...” “Well...let’s get back on the road...” “Where are we going again, boss?” “It’s a surprise!” “Oh...” With that, the whole gang gets back into the car and speeds off, passing the sign that says “Now Entering: Las Vegas, Nevada”.
