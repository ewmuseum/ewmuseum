OSW Old School Promos — #228

Frustration (Featuring Aimz)

2002-03-24

Jesse Ramey

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Oh, my head.” Come the words from one “Bad Attitude” Jesse Ramey. Not realizing that he is still in the dress that the masked men had placed him in. He begins to rub his head. “Why the hell does my chest feel so damn tight?” Jesse looks down and sees the dress. He goes into a bit of shock and begins to get a look of complete anger on his face. He stands up and rips the dress off of his body and throws it into a near by trashcan. “Those…Those…Those ingrates are going to pay for this!” Jesse then storms down the hall and as he does he passes the vending machine that he was knocked out at. He brings back his fist and puts a massive dent in the machine. He then turns and continues down the corridor, passing two members of the OSW security standing up a table drinking coffee and laughing. “Yeah, you catch that match with Great Dragon in it?” Security guard one says. “Oh, it was a pretty good match, but I’ve heard that Great Dragon may either be the son of Superman, Spiderman, or even Rent-A-Hero.” The second security guard says. Jesse pants his fist into the table and both security men jump. “I don’t give a damn what you two are talking about. It really doesn’t matter. Great Dragon this and Great Dragon that! You two aren’t supposed to be watching the fucking show! Watch a replay of it! Get a tape!” Jesse tries to calm himself down. “What you are supposed to be doing is making sure people don’t get back here and pull shit on us! The superstars!” Both men snicker. “What are you two fucking laughing about?!” Jesse shouts in anger. “You said, us superstars and you’re really not a superstar if you think about it.” The security guard says and they both begin to laugh. “Oh,” Jesse laughs and turns, “Really now?” “That’s what I…” The man is cut off. Jesse turns and punches the security guard right in the face. The security guard falls to the ground and Jesse begins to beat the shit out of him. “So you think you’re going to be funny at my expense do you? I’ll fucking show you and you’re friend!” After Jesse gets done beating he looks up at the other security guard who is looking on in fear. “Come here you fucking bastard.” Jesse jumps up and the security guard takes off running down the hall. “Don’t fucking run, you’re only going to make it worse on yourself!” Jesse screams. “I need back up, someone please get in here. I’ve got a ramped wrestler. We’ve got one security guard down and he is trying to chase me down. HELP!” The security guard says into his little walkie-talkie. Jesse continues to try and run the man down, but out of nowhere a door swings open and almost catches Jesse in the face, but luckily he stops. “God damn it! Can’t you watch what you’re doing? I was trying to run that fucking pig down. I’m going to kick his ass!” “Sweetie, sweetie. You need to calm yourself down.” Comes a lovely voice. Jesse begins to stutter, “Who do you think you are telling me to calm down?” Jesse says in a more calm voice. From around the door steps Amy Campbell, also known as Aimz, and Jesse’s jaw drops almost it. “I’m Amy Campbell, but you can all me Aimz.” “Yeah…Uhh…I mean…Um, I know who you…Ar…Ar..” Jesse can’t get the words out. “You know who I am you mean?” Aimz suggests. Jesse shakes his head. “Well, I honestly think you really need to walk back to your room and clam down. You’re going to get yourself kicked out of the arena if you don’t keep out of site.” Aimz says. “How can I? Those guys were supposed to be watching this place. Didn’t you see what happened to me?” Jesse asks. “No, actually, I didn’t.” Aimz says. “I was just attacked by those masked men that have been causing problems around here. They just beat the crap out of me, put me in a dress, put a fake women’s title on me and…Um,” Jesse opens his hand and reveals the crumpled card. “They left this on me.” Jesse says as he unfolds it. “What’s it say, hun?” Aimz asks. “It says…It says…” Jesse crumples it back up in his fist and then throws it on the floor. He turns and begins to walk away. “What was it honey?” Aimz asks again. Jesse continues walking on, “Tyler Nelson!” Jesse says very angrily and continues walking off into the distance. Aimz walks back into her room as Jesse continues on down the hall with both fists balled up. ~The End~
