What's In a Name? (Inside my Head. Pt. 1)

14 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-14-2002 Old School? The first day I walked into an OSW event, I thought I knew what to expect. Hell I even told other people what I expected, I mean come on, Old School. I thought there would be more normal things, because when I think Old School I think of normal things.. That would have been nice had I been right, because in this time, wrestling is anything but old school or normal for that matter. As soon as I walked through the doors of an OSW event, I realized how wrong I was, which isn't often. I seen things that I didn't plan on seeing. For instance while I was in the back, minding my own business, scoping things out, you know seeing what I had gotten myself into and what OSW had to offer, I kept hearing this strange loud noise that echoed throughout the building. The first time it happened, I didn't pay much attention to it, but then the sound got louder, more frequent and more annoying. I decided to go see what in the hell that sound was so I could tell whoever it was that was making that loud noise to shut the shit up. As I began to walk I seen something real strange coming towards me, it was like a couple of machine, robot type sort of things. They rolled down the hallway just a like a normal human being would walk down the hallway, I found it strange that this place was called Old School and they had robots running around. But hey, robots doing some work for you never hurt anyone is what I thought to myself. That thought sooned changed when I seen the machines or whatever, rolling toward the entrance curtain. Those things wrestle?!!!how? What hell is going on here? They said new Old School Wrestling, not Technical School Wrestling. It doesn't end from there, I was called to attend a meeting with the owner of OSW, you know get all the shit straightened out, make the deal final, sign loads of papers; the boring part. I'm usually always early to important things and of course this was important so I came early, then I found myself waiting on the owner. While sitting in the office waiting I was said to myself, 'I wonder how this owner is, is he cool or is he an asshole, is he down to earth or is he an annoying strict person, will he remind me of owners of the past or will he be his own person and present something new to me;' emphasis on 'he'. All that time I was thinking he he he, but then 'she' walked through the door and introduced herself as the owner. Wait a minute she? She's the owner? She's telling a bunch or men-or atleast trying- what to do? Man I bet she's just power hungry and you can't tell her shit because she runs men and I bet anything that gives her an huge ego boost. Now I understood why this place is called Old School, because a woman named it. You know they like to be as creative and tricky as the can be. The more I scoped things out the more I realized that the idea I had about OSW was completely wrong. AS time went on there I also realized how much I had already had in common with this place just by the name. I made a mistake I should the first person to know not to judge anyone or anything just by the name. I mean look at my name, the Epitome of Evil (EOE). If you're smart you'd know that the word 'Epitome' means to stand for something, to be an example of it's kind; something along the lines of that. Then you have the word evil that everyone around here seems or claims to know, more on that later. But when you put those words to together you get a man that stand for or is the example of evil. 'Evil' that word right there sparks in everyone ones mind a form of a the devil, coming from hell, possessed, talking in tongue; shit like that. That's what people expect to see when they see me. Walking into a wrestling atmosphere with the name evil tagged on you is like covering your hand with honey and sticking it in a bee hive. Why? Because there are so many people who claim to be 'The Evil' the most 'darkest person on earth and it seems you have to prove just how evil you are and nine times of ten, you have to fight everyone who seems to connect themselves with devil or the ones who claim that they were sent here to destory all evil. Look at who or should I say what I'm having now, things/people like Vampir, I don't know if that's actually his face or a mask. I hoping that there is no way in hell that a human can be that ugly, but their if their is, shame on God for creating somthing like that. I found it funny that I was attacked, from behind by the way, by a man that looks like Doctor B. from Tekken and claims to be the person who will fight and get rid of all evil. That already shows me how uneducated he is, because if he knew anything he would know that in order to get rid off all the evil in this world he would have to destroy everyone in this world including himself and his family. Why? Because at times in our lives we all do, say, or think evil things; it's just a part of life in my opinion. So why do I call myself evil? I'll explain in a minute; be patient. I first needed a reason to explain myself to people because I don't like explaining myself for no reason. That's why I decided to introduce myself as the Dark Leige, I knew that would get people's attention and I knew that people would try to come to me and test me to see just exactly how 'dark' I was. And was I wrong? Hell no, my first day in an actual wrestling match I'm surrounded by people who potray themselves as the evil people then I get attacked from behind a so called evil opposer. BAM! There's my reason, there's my reason to let people know exactly what I mean when I say that I am the Epitome of Evil. It's simple, once I step into a ring or an enemy comes my way something comes over me, I get locked in this 'evil zone' so to speak and I do things that make you think that I was possessed by the devil. I do things that would make people think that I was actually sent from hell, I do things that would make people think that I was this sick, twisted, abnormal human being. But the truth is that, I am just a human as anyone else. But when the time comes for me to make a statement, when my opportunity to get one step closer to the top comes around, being normal is what I'm not and I guess one can say the being the devil is what I am. Being good, or considering ones feelings is no longer inside me. I'm a different man when it comes to those situations. So there you have it. Was that simple enough to understand? If it wasn't then come Saturday Night Slam, after I slay this..... 'Dragon', it will be easy to understand just exactly where I'm coming from.
