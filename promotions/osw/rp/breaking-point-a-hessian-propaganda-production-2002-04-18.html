Breaking Point - A Hessian/Propaganda Production

18 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18-04-02 Distant booming voices ring throughout the echoing halls of a monolith somewhere in the urban jungle of Tennessee. As generic pale rooms house speakers debating about different topics, elsewhere another debate is ripe, causing what sounds like hundreds of people to riot, shouting profanities and ungodly gestures to the speakers. Inside this generic room, covered in fliers of a familiar group of people, at the front, behind two large majestic podiums are none other than Eric Sagar…and Minion the Imp… “What?” Sagar splutters, “Are you insane?” He rubs his hand swiftly across his forehead sweeping beads of sweat to the floor. “What’s so insane about stapling Swastikas to some blind monkeys and parading them around the church of bastardom...I mean...Christ???” shouts Minion. The two managers seem deeply fused in a debate about Christ and Satan…a debate for the ages. “Blasphemy is from the sly tongue of Satan himself.” Sagar implies. He stops then sniggers, looking at the vertically challenged creature. “It is obvious you are a puppet for him, as for this somewhat ‘hobby’ of parading a monkey around your place of worship. You disgust me!” Minion grunts at Sagar and growls a little before replying. “I ain’t nobodies puppet, bitch! If anything you’re a puppet to that overgrown Christian ball sack, Propaganda! Anyway, Christians are all believers in a false entity to give their life some meaning! Satan is true, and he exists in all the motherf*ckers and anarchists out there, you have a little cross with a deformed G.I. Joe hanging off of it! Ever seen Monty Python’s “The Meaning of Life”? EVERYONE SPITS ON THIS PISSY LITTLE RELIGION!!!” “Ha!” Sagar mocks. “The tripe that you talk shows the credibility of your religion. If Christianity is a joke how come it is the most popular language of the earth of the lord himself, God? A demented GI Joe you say?” Sagar grunts. “Well a GI Joe to the best of my knowledge is a pro-violence solider. Jesus was not Pro-Violence nor is the Power himself and I! We have to beat you to stop your wicked ways and save your soul. But your ignorance over runs all of your brain cells.” “IGNORANCE???” Minion squeals. “You want to talk about ignorance??? I took a read at that Bible, and you know what I saw? I saw Jesus born...then suddenly it skips to his adulthood...WHERE WAS HIS PUBERTY? Jesus was a little punk bitch! They didn't want to include all the times he spent playing Nintendo A.D. and masturbating over pictures of Elizabeth! The little son of a bitch was a dirty little bastard and if you want to stop our wicked ways in the name of Jesus, you best stop and think to yourself what that infidel spent his life doing first!” “What did that statement just prove you tiny little man?” Sagar asks with a queried look on his face. “Oh wait I know, you did prove ignorance. Ignorance of your knowledge of REAL life my brother.” replies Sagar, grunting. Sagar and Minion stare each other down. Sagar slowly brings his hand to his moustache then flicks the edge he brings it down to his side again. Minion flips Sagar off then looks to the crowd. Before them, sitting praying and sinning are a roomful of Christian brothers, seated on the right side of the room, and satanic followers of Hessian and Minion, seated on the left. Minion's eyes widen and he points to the Christians, and looks to Sagar. “Y'see those people? Look at them! They're not living a REAL life at all! They're spending their days praying and sitting and all that crap, when my brothers on the left here are f*cking prostitutes, doing intravenous drugs and belting out profanities! THAT's life, MY BROTHER! Not some straight A life of a bitch...” he shouts. “Yes?” Sagar questions Minion. “Is that so? Well I want you to try and restate that statement after your death whilst you burn in the reddest pit of hell being stewed with Lucifer’s trident implanted in your skull. While I look down arm in arm with My Brothers of all purity and righteousness.” “What in the hell makes you think Lucifer will do that to me? Do you even know him? Have you supped together? If anything we’ll be given positions as Minions, and help the master to punish all those who failed to do his bidding or displeased him! Azrael Cain, that man has felt the power of Lucifer, and I’ll be damned if Hessian and I are going to fall foul of Lucifer’s wrath! We have thus far done his bidding, and on Saturday our master shalt rejoice...AS-WE-KICK-THE-LIVIN’-SNUFF-OUTTA-THAT-BIG-RETARDED-CHRISTIAN BASTARD-AAAAAAAH!!!!!!!!!” Minion screams, delving into the mind of a Dark Preacher. Sagar curls up the corner of his lip; he looks at Minion then shakes his head. “My Brother…” He sighs, “I thought your religion was misguided, but not this misguided. You, Azrael Cain, Hessian, Caleb, Vampir Nosferatu and your entire fellow Satanist scum will fall victim to the powers of Lucifer. He hast no friends nor does he want one. I shalt tell you what he wants. He wants you mortal soul.” Sagar looks at Minion the Imp up and down in disgust. “Despite how misguided it is.” “No shit! Lucifer is the man, he needs no one else, but the fact he will use us then dump us is highly unlikely, especially with a soul such as Hessian’s. How can YOUR god stand you when it knows that this Saturday you spit on your own religion by standing side-by-side a fellow Dark evil, and a pitiful one at that?” replies Minion, swiping at his long, stringy black hair, falling across his botched little face. “WHAT?” He yells echoing, making many of the audience members uneasy. “DO YOU THINK PROPAGANDA WANTS TO TEAM WITH A SCUMBAG LIKE VAMPIR NOSFERATU?” Sagar leans forward on the podium. “Well if so, you are wrong! Propaganda is teaming with him to take the tag gold to the side of god. Well at least half of it, but believe me once Propaganda has sacrificed you to our lord he will go for Vampir Nosferatu and slowly crush his skull. Which he would have done if you and your ‘boys’ had kept your business where you should have! It disgusts me, your ignorance. You follow a religion that you know will only lead you to the reddest pit off hell brewing for eternity.” “Kept our business wh-...LISTEN-TO-ME-YOU-SCUM-SUCKING-FALSIFYING-NON-BELIEVING-SUCKA-BITCH-CHRISTIAN-MOTHERF*CKER!!! Heresian Bloodlust warned you to get out of the OSW. If there’s one thing we hate it is scum like you! The Josh, Joshua Kosidlo, of the old NTICW, he tried as you did to condemn Hessian, and what did he receive for his efforts? A neck reminiscent of a car wreck! He was a self-proclaimed “Warrior of God” and Hessian reduced him to a blithering pile of rubble! If you so readily believe you can destroy that which is indestructible then bring it on you f*cking ratfink!!! Hessian and Azrael will f*ck you up worse than Sodom itself!!!” Minion wipes his brow, steadily heading for a verbal meltdown with his foe, all the while the opposing religions sit content, harbouring no thoughts of war or violence...yet. Sagar growls, “Well I don't care about The Josh or what ever he has called. He lacks one thing… FAITH! Propaganda will never give up until Heresian Bloodlust and OSW are on their knees praying to God. I promise you… you will learn the errors of your ways.” Minion fixes his lips into a familiar shape, and as the booming “FUUUUU-“ echoes throughout the room, along the hallways, approaching the room from different directions are three heavily religious individuals. Back inside the room, unbeknownst of their arrival, Minion continues... “…YOOOUUUU! You can't get rid of Hessian and Azrael! They're forever! They cannot be killed! They have more faith in Lucifer than you, and trust me, I know how much that is. You can preach all you like, but as a wise woman once said, “Deeds, not words” and motherf*cker, deeds, actions, have already been taken. Didn't you hear about your precious Christian church burnt to the ground on the News? Not even the radio? Hmm? Speak up f*cker!” squeals Minion passionately, for his cause. “Your ignorance excels” Sagar grumbles. “Did you know how Lucifer came to be? He was an unwanted angel.” states the preacher, “He did wrong therefore he was punished an eternity to punish those who have done likewise. He does the bidding for God himself. Therefore you are only worshipping another puppet of God. We are all puppets of God. Just some with higher status.” Sagar exclaims. “Ha…I…you…we…mothe-…F*CK YOU! I don’t have to listen to that! Lucifer may be a puppet of God now, but he is still as powerful, and he’s building an army that will soon destroy your petty existence! NO! I haven’t been watching ‘Spawn’! Oh, and another thing you twisted fu-……………” Minion is halted in mid-speech as two doors opposite the room open, and in poke three heads. At the lower end of the room, a pale, grey head and a silver mask look into the room, whilst on the upper side, a humanly kind, yet focused head looks into the room. At once the disaster looms… “MINION!!!” “SAGAR!!!” Hessian, Azrael Cain and Propaganda gaze from the speakers to each other. For a moment everything is silent, until the three giants enter the room, locking the doors as they enter… “So…” Propaganda blurts with no emotion. “We have the soon to be ex-tag champions of Old School Wrestling. Bah! This disgusts me and it disgusts god.” “So true.” Sagar points out. “So your foolish cult fad finally meets face-to-face with righteousness. Finally meets face-to-face with the Power himself.” Propaganda implies. “I have to agr—“ Sagar is interrupted by a loud yell from Propaganda. “WELL NO MORE!” the audience jump out of their skin as Propaganda roars out. “THIS ESCAPADE HAS GONE ON TOO FAR! With one clean swoop I shall knock down the ignorance.” Propaganda puts his left arm on his opposite shoulder then swoops it out to the other side. “They fail to realise Satanism is a foolish sector of Christianity.” Sagar points out before he is interrupted again. Hessian and Azrael look at each other, cock their heads, and turn to Propaganda. Taking the necessary steps forward to stand before him, eye-to-eye, The Marked Beast and The Goliath stands silently... “Show that heathen bastard why you’re tag champions!!!” shouts Minion, ducking behind his podium as Propaganda glares at him. “So...a room full of brothers and haters...and we’re standing before the biggest heathen morsel of Christianity himself...” Azrael calmly states, squinting his eyes and cocking his head at the 7’0” Power Himself. “If the authorities were not within such a close proximity, there would be a-many dead now...say, Propaganda, how many corpses did they pull out of that little campfire…y'know, the one Minion and I started...” Hessian evilly ribs Propaganda. “GARH!” Propaganda roars. He pulls his right hand back and is about to swing it at Hessian. “Stop!” Sagar orders Propaganda. “Let me take care of this.” Sagar steps out from the podium. “Let me tell you this much my brother Hessian, your ignorance and sins shall not go unnoticed. You burn down a house of God and God will order Lucifer to burn your soul.” “Ha!” Propaganda chuckles satisfactorily. “You are right my Brother Sagar, but what their ignorance covers up making them fail to realise is that their Judgement day is soon. Their Judgement day is SLAM!” Azrael growls deeply, and Hessian’s eyes begin glowing intensely, infuriated. Minion gasps, and waddles from behind his podium to stop the Bloodlust advancing on Proppa-G. “Dammit! Don’t do anything you’ll regret!” screams Minion, holding back Cain and Hessian. “Minion…words have been spoken and actions are soon to be taken, this infidel has raised the bar, and Hessian and I plan to burn it down, much like you burned those inferior Christians into ashes and dust!” Azrael says towards the Imp, while Propaganda takes a deep breath, holding back from any violence. “Yes I must align with a direct threat to my beliefs…and I must fight alongside him against the heathen before me…which I can live with. However! What you did to my brothers, that horrifying act of egotistical hunger for evil or power or whatever it is you seek, was a crime beyond words. Now I could walk to the nearest Police Dept. and divulge all that you have told me, however, as evil as you are, there is an air of indignant-mindedness in that, therefore on Slam! I will personally deal out my own revenge on you, and be wary…I will go lower than anyone you have seen or known…I will do to you what you and your kind have done to my brothers and sister for so long…so beware Heretics…you…are…going…to…die.” Propaganda calmly breathes, as Hessian and Cain seems infuriated, however Cain steps forward, unafraid and seemingly intrigued as to Propaganda’s little speech. “Ok, hear this. You live your life by a book. You believe in some words. You have nothing else to go by but an outdated book of praises and stories of a man long ago that made a name for himself. You know nothing of what I know. While you read a book and collectively believe in something you truly know nothing of, I have seen tings that would petrify you. Turn you into stone. You have spent a few decades believing this, I’ve spent so much longer feeling pain derived from him myself, knowing his wrath, doing his bidding. Until you know what I know, don’t ever dare present yourself as you do before me. I will strike you down with in a manner so ungodly if you ever try your way with me…don’t…dare…” Cain snarls at Propaganda, the tension in the room building steadily every moment. One-by-one the audience get up, and leave, unlocking the door and exiting the room fit to explode. Only true followers remain, their hearts in their throats as these three men inch closer to the brink of…unpleasantness… Hessian pushes past Azrael, and squares up to Propaganda. The 7’2” monster looks down on the Christian man, his only intent evident in the green pulsating eyes of his. “Vampir Nosferatu…that thing made me who I am today. If anyone can kill it, it’ll be me. Upon the Vampir’s destruction, I will personally see to it that the name of ‘God’ is disgraced again. You will feel more pain than Cain himself, at the hands of the Master. On Saturday we’ll unleash hell on you, take that as you may, but someone is in the crosshairs, and I’ll Be Damned if it is I, or Cain. There was no heartfelt or emotion when I incinerated your ‘brothers’ and ‘sisters’, and I will take the greatest of pleasures in showing your fellow Christians just how weak their role model is. ‘The Power Himself’ is nothing, just like Joshua Kosidlo was to me, and in the end all Hell will prevail, and Eternal Damnation will rue your day…” Hessian, Azrael and Propaganda stare deep into each other’s eyes. Hell and Heaven reach Breaking Point as these Behemoths grow ever tense, hatred boiling and rage building. Minion and Sagar back off, nervously, as The Power Himself, The Marked Beast and The Goliath stare deep into their souls, and the sheer magnitude of their hatred flows through their eyes, and into their hearts. Apocalypse Now. Fin
