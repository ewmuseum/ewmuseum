It's All About the Image

1 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-1-02 Our scene opens outside of the Joe Louis Arena in Detroit Michigan, site of OSW’s second pay-per-view Head On Collision. No matter who you ask, they’ll be sure to tell you that it was an eventful night...possibly the most eventful of any night in professional wrestling history. So many surprises, so much drama. Just the thought of the event sparks memories of what took place during the card. The boys in Civil Disobedience, Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke, made good on their words and defeated the painfully irritating Team Annoying, despite the heelish tactics of guest referee David Arquette. Then, later in the night, Seth and Trevor came out again to save Shanda Russell from a devilish sodomization care of Hessian, laying a beat down on Heresian Bloodlust. Things went absolutely crazy after that, when perhaps in the most shocking event of the night Da Westsidaz appeared in OSW to lay Civil D out with steel chairs in hand. And then, of course, a bunch of other people did a whole lot of other stuff, but who really cares about them. Seth and Trevor are the only people who really matter...in Civil Disobedience promos at least. Speaking of the miraculous boons to professional wrestling today, we see Seth and Trevor themselves currently walking out of the back door of the arena to the private VIP parking area. They have duffel bags slung over their shoulders as they walk, and, not surprisingly after the attack perpetrated earlier, they have ice packs held up against the back of their heads. Normally this would be enough to cause rather sour expressions to paint their faces, and indeed Trevor doesn’t look too happy at all. Seth, on the other hand, is simply beaming with excitement, grinning like a damn fool. “Fuck yeah!” Seth shouts in elation, pointing to the lump rising from the back of his skull. “Do you see this welt on the back of my head, Trev?” Trevor stops walking and looks at his partner in confusion. After peering at the knot sticking out from Seth’s head for a few seconds he gives a nod in reply. “Yeah, that’s quite the bulging protrusion you have there. In fact, I have a similar one myself,” Trevor states, pointing at the back of his head, before his face freezes. He shakes his head before correcting his language. “Wait, that just sounded sick. You know what I mean, though.” “Damn right I know what you mean!” Seth proclaims happily, pumping his fist in the air. “Short fuckin’ Circuit nailed me in the head with a steel chair.” Trevor looks at his partner in disgust before turning to continue his trek to the duo’s beatdown Chevy Celebrity, obviously eager to get home. Seth grabs Trevor by the shoulder and spins him around again, repeating his previous proclamation. “Did you hear what I said, man?” Seth repeats loudly, still grinning. “Short Circuit laid me out with a chair!” “Yes, damn it! I heard you the first time,” Trevor says, staring at his partner’s face incredulously. “I’ve just been trying to figure out what the hell you’re so damn happy about.” “Da Westsidaz are fucking living legends, man!” Seth yells in retort, smiling as he holds up all five fingers on his left hand. “Five time PCW tag champs, and shit. And we get a match with them on Saturday. Hell fucking yeah, bro!” “What the fuck are you smoking?” Trevor asks, his tone condescending as he looks at Seth in disdain. “Why do you ask that?” Seth questions back, sounding confused. “I don’t know,” Trevor says nonchalantly, raising his arms in the air in a sign of ignorance before addressing Seth with indignance. “It might be because the last time I checked, getting knocked out cold in front of a pay-per-view audience was most definitely not a good thing, Seth.” “Yeah, but it’s not like we just got knocked out cold. We got knocked out cold by the motherfucking Westsidaaaaaaaazzzzzz!” As he draws out the name of the their assailants, Seth raises both of his hands in the air, with his fingers overlapping to form the letter W on both sides of his head. *SMACK* A quick jab from Trevor connects with Seth’s jaw, sending him sprawling to the ground in a heap. A rather surprised heap, at that. “What the...” “Shut the fuck up,” Trevor says, peering down at Seth in disgust. “But I...” Seth starts, trying to get back to his feet. “I said shut the fuck up,” Trevor repeats with a sigh. “But...” Seth spouts in a pleading tone as he finally stands up once more. *SMACK* Another punch from Trevor knocks Seth to the ground...again. Trevor shakes his head in a pitying fashion, while Seth holds his now swelling jaw with his right hand, making sure all his teeth are still in there. “What part of shut the fuck up don’t you understand?” Trevor asks, his tone expressing disbelief as he paces back and forth along the wall. He holds his head, as if trying to understand just how Seth’s piddly little mind operates. “Is it the shut? Or is it the the? Or perhaps the up?” “What about the fuck?” Seth asks, still holding his jaw as he stands up. “Well I figured you already had the fuck part down, considering you are QUITE THE DUMB FUCK!” Trevor shouts, raising his hand as if he was going to hit his partner again. Seth cowers in fear, but straightens himself when he sees the blow was just a feign. “Hey! There’s no need for insults, now,” Seth says, brushing the dust off of the back of his pants and shirt. “There’s a need for something when you’re praising the virtues of the men who just laid down a beating on us a few hours ago. Insults sound as good as anything.” “But...Da Westsidaz are awesome!” Seth argues, throwing his arms up in the air again. “Don’t you remember before we joined the academy, we used to watch them on TV. They’re like fucking inspirations to us, dude. Don’t pretend you didn’t mark out for them! You had the fucking T-shirt and everything, man.” “You don’t see me wearing their T-shirt now, do you?” Trevor questions, sighing as he shakes his head incredulously. Looking at Trevor, a confused expression comes over Seth’s face. “Uh...you’re not wearing a shirt at all, Trev.” “How observant of you, Seth...” “But if you WERE wearing one, it MIGHT have been a Westsidaz T-shirt,” Seth exclaims, smiling and gesturing with his fingers as he makes his point. “No, it wouldn’t have been a Westsidaz T-shirt,” Trevor says in a mocking tone, before muttering. “You fucking idiot.” “Why are you so mad, Trev?” Seth asks innocently. “So what if Riptah and Short Circuit battered us from behind? It’s not like Hessian and Cain haven’t done the same shit a hundred times in the past. At least this time we actually have people beating us who deserve to beat us.” “See, that’s the problem, Seth. Your perception is all distorted,” explains Trevor. “Nobody DESERVES to beat us. I don’t care what side they come from.” “Not even the west one?” Seth asks, his eyes growing wide, his lower jaw quivering. “Especially not the west one.” Trevor spits. “Seth, I think all the bright lights are finally getting to you. Either that or the excessive brain damage you’ve taken over the years. You’re forgetting what our goal here is in Old School Wrestling.” “Goal? That’s a new one. We have a goal?” Seth asks, his face scrunching up in question. “Of course we have a goal. Do you think we’re here because we have nothing better to do than just hang out and have a good time?” “If that’s not why we’re here, then I feel as if I’ve been living a lie these past two months,” Seth says, staring forward with a blank expression on his face. “Well then you better start dealing with the truth, bro,” Trevor remarks, trying to shake some sense into his partner. No, really. He’s literally grabbing Seth by his shoulders and shaking him. “We’re in this business for one thing and one thing only: to better our image!” Staring at his partner, Trevor looks disappointed when Seth doesn’t answer back. “Come on, man? Don’t you have anything to say about that?” “Yeah...” Seth remarks with a chuckle. “If we’re trying to better our image then why the fuck are we wrestling pay-per-view match ups against Carrot Top and Gilbert Gottfried?” “I...” Trevor starts to answer, but stops before he even gets started. Obviously there is no good answer for that question, so he decides to change the subject. “That doesn’t matter. What matters is the here and now. We’re not going to look cool if we get our asses kicked by Riptah and Short Circuit. No, if that happens, we’re going to look lame as hell, and there’s no fucking way I’m going to let that happen. Do you want to look lame, Seth?” Seth shakes his head to signal the negative. “Damn right you don’t want to look lame. That’s why this Sunday, you’re going to forget all of that idolistic bullshit. Who the fuck cares what Da Westsidaz did? You may have looked up to them before, but that can stop right now,” Trevor states emphatically, pushing his finger into Seth’s chest. “We’re fucking better than they ever were, and we’re going to prove that by beating them this Saturday night. No, we’re not just going to beat them, but we’re going to look good doing it. You got me?” Seth nods his head in understanding. “Good, now let’s get the hell out of here. The further we can distance ourselves from the Team Annoying debacle, the more comfortable I’ll be.” Seth shudders as he cups his hands over his ears. “I can still hear Gottfried’s voice ringing in my head.” “Amen to that, my brotha,” Seth agrees, mirroring his partner’s actions with similar ones of his own. Trevor gives a curt nod to his partner before the two return to their trek to their car. A few seconds later they’re in their trademark beatdown Chevy Celebrity; Trevor sitting shotgun, with Seth in the driver’s seat turning the keys in the ignition. Putting it in reverse, Seth backs it out, then shifts into drive, cruising out of the parking lot. It seems like your normal, uneventful exit is in the works, as they turn around the corner of the arena and drive along the side of the building. And then it happens... Out from behind a row of cars... Runs former WCW Champion/Ex-referee David Arquette... Waving his arms frantically... And shouting apologies to Civil Disobedience... This is your cue to unleash your bloodcurdling screams... AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!! “Hey guys, I’m sorry about the match tonight!” Arquette shouts excitedly as he approaches the car. “I don’t know what came over me! I hope you can forgive me! Maybe we can hang out sometime? I’m cool! Did I mention I’m a former WCW World Champion? And I’m married to Courtney Cox? Because I am!” Inside the car, Seth and Trevor’s eyes both open wide in surprise...and fear...at the sight of this B-level Hollywood actor running toward them. “Oh my god, David Arquette is right in front of us! Hit the gas!” Trevor shouts at Seth. Seth floors the accelerator, slamming into Arquette and sending him flying over the top of the car. Mr. Cox lands hard face-first on the ground, quickly left behind by the speeding Civil D-mobile. Pulling out onto the street, Trevor sticks his head out of the window and looks back at the fallen Arquette, shaking his head in disdain before pulling himself back into the car. “Did I say hit the gas? Because I meant hit the br...no, I was right the first time. You did good, Seth.” Trevor says, congratulating his partner. “Thanks, Trev.” Seth responds with a smile. “Let’s just hope you don’t let me down this Saturday.” “Have I ever let you down in the...oh, wait.” Seth pauses, thinking of his many past follies. “Never mind. I’ll make you proud, Trev. We’ll look real good.” “Like I said...” Trevor says, grinning. “It’s all about the image.” Fin. Wait...this isn't Jaws. I mean, The End.
