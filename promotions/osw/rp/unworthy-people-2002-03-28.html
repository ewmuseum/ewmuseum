Unworthy people

28 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-28-02 Surrounded by idiots.... Ever been grouped with certain people that don't even deserve the privelage of your presence? If not then maybe you're one of the unworthy ones. Doesn't it feel degrading to even have be around them, to even hear their voice, or look at their faces for that matter.? Yea, it does. Well that's how EOE is feeling at the moment. Ever since day one in OSW EOE has been grouped with certain type that are not even on his level, but come Saturday EOE will solve all of that. We open in the weightroom part of EOE's mansion in Dallas, TEXAS! whichi is down in the basement. EOE and Eve are doing their daily workout. EOE is on the incline doing five sets of ten and Eve is is doing leg curls, how many is she doing? However many she feels. As they are working out a conversation starts up. 'Eve, you know what?' ask EOE 'No, but what's on your mind?' Eve says sarcastically. 'Ever, since I've gotten in OSW I've had to look at the same people in the ring. You don't how degrading it feels to be around freak idiots like that. Me having to be to in the same ring as them every Saturday is like, MJ having to team up with the rest of Washington Wizards; poor man I know how he feels now. Can you imagine driving in the lane and you turn and Popeye Jones is staring you in the face. I feel his his pain.' 'Imagine being in that womens dressing room backstage every Saturday. You have no idea.' replies Eve. 'I mean people thought I had problems, but those individuals need to get checked out. Vampir think he has to right to walk around without a bag over his head. Then he think he's evil, isn't looking evil bad enough? Then Doctor B. I mean Propaganda, is trying to eliminate something that people and even greater powers have been trying to get rid ever since humans first entered this world and people will be trying after he dies too. I don't understand. Then Nick, he fits the idiot part more than he does the freak part. He's an idiot simply because Vampir seems to make a fool out of him after every match.' says EOE. 'Well I told, you I told you from the beginning. When we were in Janes office, I told you I didn't like her right from the start. It was something about her, just the way she acted, looked, moved made me feel something was fraud about her. And was I right? Yes, she's scandalous just scandalous. You should expect to be in somethings like this just by looking at her...bitch' says Eve. EOE get up from the incline and walks over to the bench then loads on the weights and Eve switches to the incline adn does the same. 'Yea I guess you were right. 'I know.' ask Eve in an arrogant way. As they are working out and talking a promo for the Queen of Extreme, comes on their big screen. 'Hey Eve, I'm surprised that you didn't enter that, I wouldn't have mind.' says EOE. 'Now you know, the only reason those chicken heads are in that is because that's they only way that they can get noticed. They have to break their necks in order to get on t.v. whereas I only have to appear on stage and there goes the rating through the roof; there's proof in that and you know it. As far as I'm concerned I am the Queen. I kind of feel sorry for them, well not really. It will be fun to watch them make a fool of themselves. For me to wrestle something important has to be on the line and that tourney is not important enough for me....I wonder what prize reward is for winning.' says Eve as she continues to lift. 'Speaking none important, things look at what I have to face Saturday.' says EOE. 'Oh just, chill. When you think about it, other than having to look Vampir in his face, it's not so bad. I mean you have a chance, to kill four birds with one stone' explains Eve. 'What do you mean, four birds? I only have three people to rough up.' says EOE 'Well this is your big chance to take care of three little petty problems of course, then this is your chance to prove that you belong in more important matches, matches worth something. You being in that match Saturday is like me being in that QOE Tourney; it's standard lowering. And do we lower our standards? No. You need to show punks what the deal really is.' says Eve 'Yea, I think it's about time I do that.' EOE agrees. Eve gets up from the incline and walks behind the bench were EOE is lifting, she act as if she spotting him but in reality she just flurting. 'Come on Eve, not right now. What do you want me to do drop this weight on my throat? I do have a match you know.' says EOE Eve continues to flirt. 'As if you have to be 100 percent to beat them.' she says arrogantly 'I know, but it will be nice to breath normally during that match; it would make this much easier.' says EOE. EOE continues to lift as Eve looks down at him. [End!]
