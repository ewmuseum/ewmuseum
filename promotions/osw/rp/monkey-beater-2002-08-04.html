Monkey Beater...

4 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8.4.02 Chink...Chink...Chink, Chink. What the hell is that noise? Chink... A ‘chinking’ noise broke the silence of Krieg’s bedroom as he lay half asleep in his bed, resting after his action-packed night on SLAM! A undertone rumble also started to build up, getting louder every couple of minutes. Oh, what the hell. I’m trying to get some rest and some asshole starts their stinking car outside my window with the radio on. A rumble reached its climax as the constant chinking turned into something else... SMASH! The window of Michel Krieg’s caved in as a boulder came tumbling through the double-glazed window frame of his Kentucky hotel room. What in the...Damn hicks! Why can’t they play “Catch The Boulder” somewhere else? Krieg slowly pulled himself out of bed and lumbered over to the curtains of his window, drawing them back to get a good look at the villains of this dreadful crime. Poking his head through the gaping hole in the window, Krieg yelled to the people below, “Hey, you hicks! Take your boulders an...What the hell,” proclaimed Krieg as he actually saw who smashed his window: a bunch of tree hugging, animal loving hippies carrying signs of protest, “What do we want?...Monkey Fairness...When do we want it?...Now,” yelled the weedy protesters as they marched around in a circle in the car park outside Krieg’s room, waving their boards in the air as they did, Ah! Damn hippies! What do they want now? Catching a glance of Krieg, the protesters get stuck right into him, “Booo! There he is, The Monkey Beater! You should be ashamed of yourself, you dirty Communist! How dare you violate the rights of little ZaC the monkey,” bawled the leader of the group, the greasy haired Green Peace representative, John Del La Miller, Slightly taken back, Krieg roars back as only he can, “Who are you stinking people? I hope you know my personal security team will be here in about five minutes, so if I were you, I’d pick up the little, if any, dignity you’ve got left, just back in your broken down pick-ups and head back to your shanty shacks in Hick-Land before you personally get your asses beaten down by Germany’s finest,” “See this people,” replied Miller, “This is the kind of violence these Europeans, these Commnuists, in the Red Army are polluting our screens with. Do you really want yor children to grow up to be like this man,” preached the hippy, “No,” replied a group of listeners, dressed just as bad as Miller in tie die T-shirts, dirty combat trousers and scanky old boots, “Well, I say we have a sit in and make sure this man can not get to this week’s OSW broadcast,” suggested Miller as he started to sit down across the road into the hotel, his followers gradually doing likewise, Bah! Sit in if you like. The security van will just run over you. Not my problem “Ah, you idiots! Can’t you see? The OSW can’t live without Krieg, the OSW fans can’t get that buzz, that little tingling in their stomachs every week unless they know Krieg is on TV. If you stop Krieg from being on TV, the local economy will drop quicker than an American sky scraper. You can sit there if you like: it is of no concern of mine. Get run over if you wish,” said Krieg, pulling his head back into his room, One of the protesters stands up and makes his opinion known, “John, don’t you think there’s a better way of doing this? You know, like maybe blowing up his car or something? I mean, I can’t sit here all day: I’ve got the kids to pick up from school, I’ve got to do the washing. We don’t all have the whole day to mess around with Animal Rights like you do, John,” “Ah, a weak supporter. Well, if you want to fall out of this moral race, that is fine, but just remem...” Miller is suddenly cut off as another protester stands up and joins in the conversation, “Emm...I need to hit the road too. I’ve got to do some homework for class,” Another two stand up, “Yeah man, we’ve got school in the morning. We can’t be sitting our asses on concrete when we’ve got work to do,” says one of the boys, “Yo John. Why don’t we like do this tomorrow, when we’ve not got any classes, you know? When we’re not busy,” chirps in his friend, Furious at the weakening of his protesting team, Miller starts to rant, “Ah, you are all weak! You are not real activists! Part-time, I tell you, Part-time! You are not true to the cause! You are fakes,” screams Miller, pulling at his organically-made body warmer as more people stand up and start to walk away, Ah, it appears these freaks have got better things to do than protest about some stupid monkey. “Yeah, that is right. You American’s go home. Go make me some fried chicken, you stupid hillbillies,” calls Krieg, a grin cracking across his face as he does, “You just...just...You just shut up, Ok,” replies Miller, his mental condition getting worse by the minute, “Bah! Don’t talk to me, you tree-hugger! You better more your scrawny ass! Here comes my security team,” chuckled Krieg, Turning round, Miller catches a glimpse of a black Ford pick-up roaring towards his position. On the side, an OSW logo is spray painted in exceptionally cool orange and gold paint. Screeching to a stop, the pick-up’s doors swing open and 4 or 5 guys jump out, holding stiff steel batons, swinging them in Miller’s direction as they walk towards him, “Emm...It’s time for me to...go,” said Miller as he began to run for his life, the security guards giving chase as he does, Laughing, Krieg pulls the blinds back across the window to reduce the draft as he sits back down on his bed, his elbows on his knees and his head in his hands. Slowly, Krieg raises his head and looks into the camera, the look on his face serious, “This Saturday, here in Kentucky or wherever the hell this place is, me and Reno Davis will be going at loggerheads. Now, before you jump to any conclusions, I pay my dues to legends just like the next man, and I have done many times to Davis, either on radio shows, in interviews or backstage, but there comes a point when legend status wears a bit thin, and I think it’s time Davis proved why he is a legend, if he still even is one,” From outside, Miller’s screeching voice can be heard as a couple of security guards drag him back to their pick-up, “But I have rights! You can’t handle me like this,” says Miller. Suddenly, there’s a loud crack and then a noise that sounds like a sack of potatoes dropping to the floor...Or maybe it was Miller’s limp body after he got cracked over the head with a baton...Who knows, “IOW. j0lt. Davis has served his time in both, not forgetting fWo Survivor, but this is the OSW. This isn’t John Carroll’s IOW. This isn’t some federation hanging by its teeth to survive like j0lt. And this sure isn’t the fWo. So Davis, I think it’s time we both stop living in the past and bring ourselves up-to-date. Hey, Communism may be small, dead some say, but I still believe in it and so do many others. But you seem to live in the past more than me, my comrade. You aren’t a champion in IOW anymore. You’re not j0lt’s big name anymore. You’re not the man who entered fWo Survivor anymore. You are Reno Davis, a has-been. A man who used to have it, but lost it all. I don’t care much for what you used to be or who you’re going to be. All I care about is what’s going on at the moment, what you are trying to do to the Army, what you are trying to do to Krieg. We are meeting this Saturday, and I suspect it will be a good match. But there is no way you are going to bring my name down by milking your former glory over and over again. So Saturday will be a judgement day of sorts. A judgement day to see if Michel Krieg can beat the has-been legend, Reno Davis. To see if Reno Davis can bring himself back to the top by beating today’s biggest name, Michel Krieg. But there’s one more thing we’re going to see...To see what man can show the world what he is and what he can be. You’ve already shown the world what you used to be, and I’ve already shown the world what I am, but now it’s time to let the OSW fans and the world of wrestling see who will be the next big thing. It’s up to you, Davis, whether you want to push yourself like you apparently used to or if you just want to hand your carcass to me on a plate...I’ll let you decide,” With that, Krieg lowers his head once more, his arms on his knees as he does, and thinks deeply. Fading away from the scene, the camera looks out of Krieg’s room window one more time to see the security guys all standing outside of their pick-up with Miller in the back, looking rather scared, as the guards have a laugh with each other. Fade to black...
