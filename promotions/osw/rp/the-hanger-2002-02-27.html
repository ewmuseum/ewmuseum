The hanger

27 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-27-02 “For several weeks, things haven’t really been going my way. No matter how much I try to gain revenge. They always find that little way to grab the advantage and jerk the rug out from under my feet.” The sound of the voice is heard. Seemingly, an echo is heard in the background. The specific location is not known, or at least not as of yet. “I’ve been bailed out the past couple of weeks. Michael Dame and Rhett Locke, two men that I have not a history with. Why would they do such a thing? Come to the ring and try to, save me!” The voice trails off. This voice is none other than that of “Bad Attitude” Jesse Ramey. A random assortment of planes are seen. All scattered about in a hanger. Sitting in a fold-up chair in the middle of the hanger is Jesse. Feet propped out and leaning back. It is a cold day and the steam coming from his hot breath after it hits the cold is seen. Coming in from the backside, we can tell that Jesse is wearing a heavy coat, but nothing else can be noticed, expect the pair of boots that he is wearing. Black in color and large in size. Not like the kind that Jesse would be seen normally wearing. Slowly making our way around him we now come into contact with the rest of his body. His mouth opens. “A long period of silence. Good for the soul and for the mind. Time to reflect on the things that have come to be. As much as I would like to say, I have not the words that need be spoken.” Jesse comes to an up right position and places his hands on his knees. “For I have not the answers to the questions I do seek. Nor does anyone else that I may seek. I need time to think. I need time to relax and try to sort out these evil presences that hold my environment captive.” Jesse comes to his feet and slowly etches his way to the hanger door. “Maybe I can figure this out on my own.” Jesse places his hand upon his chin, “If I put my mind to these things that are troubling me, then maybe, just maybe, I can do this on my own and not have to burden anyone else.” Jesse turns and makes his way to the nearest little booth looking area. He rummages through all of the papers on the desk and finally comes into contact with a phone. He punches in a number and gets a hold of someone. “Yes, could you please send this call to the room of one, Chass Loraine?” Jesse waits. “Thank you, sir.” Jesse waits. “Chass, honey. I’ve decided that I’m going to be a little while longer than planned. Yes, I know that I told you I would be back before nine, but I’ve not come to my senses on a few things as of yet. Please understand where I am coming from. Thank you, baby. I promise I won’t be any later than eleven. Bye.” Jesse walks over to the hanger doors and turns off the lights. “What once was said, has been said before. I’ve not been let down and I’ve not let myself down. I’ve tried my best and that’s all that can be said. Matt Blaze, you’re only a stepping stone on my way back to the top of the wrestling industry. OSW, I’ve got my mind fix. It’s Time For An Attitude Adjustment.” End
