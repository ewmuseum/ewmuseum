Standing on some dotted line ------------------------- This isn’t some bullshit story about some bullshit person and his bullshit imaginary love interest. You know when they say, invariably, that there’s someone out there for everyone? ------------------------------------ Reacting to the hands of time ------------------------------- What if you’ve already had your chance and you blew it? --------------------------------------- Circumstance that I’ve been fed ---------------------------------- I mean, do we have one big chance, and that’s it? Or maybe some people are luckier than others. Maybe, for some people, one chance is all you get. Maybe, when you’re being judged for your life, you’ll get the reasons and the wherefores and the explanations for why you fucked up once and you never got the chance to love again – but until then, what do you have? ------------------------------------- Leaving questions in my head ------------------------------------ Maybe, just maybe… I’ve blown my chance, over three years ago, looking at the one I love with that souring, bitter taste of defeat and despair, clouded over with the thought “Shit, I can do better’. Overconfidence in myself, thinking that I was better than I really was, taking her and my luck for having her for granted. -------------------------------------- Find the space that was left bare --------------------------------------- Three years ago, and the hurt and the absence is still there. Everytime she called, it was like a scab being ripped open, and I’d feel just as hurt as I was before, the hurt I hoped that would never go away. Dulled by the passage of time, its like a dull knife stabbing into you. Somehow, the dull edge still cuts you and hurts you more now that you’ve had time to reflect on what you’ve done, the words you’ve said, the idiot that you were a constant reminder. -------------------------------------- An empty space I’d gladly share -------------------------------------- I was younger, and I figured I’d ignore it and just fuck the hurt away. I didn’t realize I’d hurt myself more by doing it. Like getting rid of a hangover with a shot of whiskey or a warm beer on your dresser. You think you’ve fixing it, but all you’re doing is exacerbating the problem. And that’s what I did, trying to forget her. But I never did. Sometimes, I think I’m obsessive, thinking about her still after all this time. -------------------------------------- One must live while one must die -------------------------------------- She’s got someone in her life, for a while now, I guess. I don’t want to talk to her anymore. I told her as much in a letter. Everytime she would contact me, out of the blue, it would just hurt more, the ulterior motive always in question? Was it a taunt, or maybe it was acknowledging the link between us. --------------------------------------- Combined, paired up for so many years -------------------------------------- I’d like to think it was the former. I’d like to think that we forged something between us that not even falling out of love could diminish. But then, I think, why the hell isn’t she still with me? I feel like a divorcee who never got a reason why. I feel incomplete, no sense of closure, even though I got more than my fair share of reasons why. Mostly, I feel like I fucked it all up. ---------------------------------------- Not sure if I really wanna shed a tear -------------------------------------- I don’t lie in bed and cry about her anymore. But every now and then, a song will come on, or I’ll go through my old CDs, the ones I used to make mix tapes for her while I was far away, and something will hit me, something sharp and poignant, something that makes her face appear to me. And its not like she was a fashion model, nor that good in bed. But I made her out in my mind to be a goddess, and I guess that goddess is still punishing me with her memory. -------------------------------------- Signing off is always so clearly done -------------------------------------- It’s easy to destroy something. Its easy to kill something like love, which in the past, I’ve likened to a child. Its easy to neglect it, to take it for granted, to carelessly injure it. Maybe that’s a better analogy for how I feel. --------------------------------------- A number two is now reduced to one -------------------------------------- I feel like I’m responsible for the death of a child. Like how women feel profound regret years after an abortion. That’s probably a good way to describe it. Like how a woman might wonder how her child may have turned out, how it would have looked as it played at the playground, how it would have been an integral part of her life. That’s what I’m wondering. How would our love turned out? ---------------------------------------- Find the space that was left bare --------------------------------------- Logic tells me it was doomed from the start – a clinically diagnosed manic-depressive and a borderline bipolar whatever the fuck I am. Logic tells me that even if it didn’t die a slow death in 1998, it would have certainly been dead by now. Logic tells me that the way my life has evolved and turned out, she wouldn’t be with me. But then, that’s logic, and logic never provided any satisfying answers after she left. -------------------------------------- An empty space I’d gladly share -------------------------------------- I would sacrifice anything to get back that feeling, that rush, that sensation of perfection I had with her. Even though it wasn’t that good, it provided me with a center, something to look forward to, the light at the end of the tunnel of the situations I was in while we were together. That’s what she was to me. She was my hope. While I was in a miserable place, she was the hope, literally the beacon across the Pacific, the light guiding me home. Too bad when I got there, a year or two later, it would be all over. But it was perfect, that dream of hope. And now that light is shining on someone else, and I’m left with no hope. -------------------------------------- When your clock is up --------------------------------------- Its hard to explain to someone whose never been in the situation or the mindset. Stationed overseas, fresh out of high school, and slowly coming out of a very weird period in my life, she was the center, she was the person I loved more than anything. It was very melodramatic at times, petty at times, sometimes ridiculous, but it was perfect nonetheless. ---------------------------------------- And you rest ---------------------------------------- I think I made her out to be more than she was, being a year away from her. I built her up to be perfection, a goddess worthy of worship, and when I returned… I found out she wasn’t as perfect as I thought her to be, the foundations of my belief, if you will, were shaken. --------------------------------------- For good --------------------------------------- And I became an asshole towards her. I became demanding. I became something I didn’t foresee myself becoming, and it was something she couldn’t deal with. --------------------------------------- I will cut off my arm piece --------------------------------------- I think I miss the goddess more than I miss the person. --------------------------------------- Just like we agreed we would --------------------------------------- It ended while I was home on leave, and we talked about it. Priorities, dreams, expectations, and affections were all either changed forever or destroyed. I guess hers changed. Mine were over the crest of the rollercoaster, inexorably on a steady decline. ---------------------------------------- One must live while ---------------------------------------- I didn’t need her, I told myself, and I went along my merry way, destroying myself, occasionally coming to a moment of lucidity and crying for what did, but always wading back to the shore of self-denial. ---------------------------------------- One must die --------------------------------------- I tried drowning in pussy, but pussy is shallow. You can’t hide in it for too long. Eventually, you have to come out and face realities, realities you’ve made for yourself and have to deal with sooner or later. --------------------------------------- One must… --------------------------------------- And its not satisfying, after awhile. Numbness creeps in. Sex becomes less of an worthwhile experience and eventually becomes expected, sometimes a formality. Sex isn’t enjoyable unless you have to chase it down, capture it, make it your own. Otherwise, you’re nothing more than a scavenger, picking up some scraps along the way to sate yourself. Sometimes, I think the Christians have it right. Maybe there shouldn’t be pre-marital sex for a reason. Maybe it cheapens it. Maybe it makes it less worthwhile, less special. ---------------------------------------- While the other argues that the world ain’t fair ---------------------------------------- And she made whatever we did special, even if it was no more thrilling than a garden variety finger-bang. Shit, now I can do the kinkiest things imaginable and get no more than a kind of a droll bemusement from what I’m doing. I think that’s another difference. These women I fuck, they could be the ugliest trolls to walk the earth, or they could be beautiful in every way, but they could never invoke the depth that she did. Depth… Deeper than any free pussy that I might come into contact with. ---------------------------------------- But then --------------------------------------- Some people wish their ex’s happiness. Is it wrong to feel the opposite? Or maybe nothing at all about how happy she is with her new man. I guess its better than being driven into a jealous frenzy every time I think of him touching her the way I used to, invoking the reaction I used to get from her, him making her get that look in her eyes that was reserved for me alone - cheapening what I had done and how I had made her feel. I’d be surprised if I’m even a footnote now. ---------------------------------------- Who ever really promised anybody equal share ---------------------------------------- Honestly, I don’t think I wanna feel anything about her. I wish I could just shut out that part of my life, close the door and let the memories fade away. Find someone else that will make me feel the way she made me feel, but, coming full circle, I think I’ve blown that chance. I think that she was my ‘one’. The person I was supposed to have made it work with, but I fucked it all up. And there isn’t anyone on the planet who I’ve got another shot with. --------------------------------------- You might as well forget what you gambled on --------------------------------------- Faults I would have previously ignored, become glaring omissions from the goddess image I have in mind. Its like tasting a fine wine once and having to drink raw sewage for the rest of your life. I didn’t have a chance to savor it while I had it in my glass. I didn’t take the time to fully appreciate it for what it was, too busy looking around myself instead of concentrating on what I had. And now its gone, and I’ll never get another shot. ---------------------------------------- Cause plans --------------------------------------- Nothing can match it, nothing I’ve had so far is anything close, and I seriously doubt I’ll get anything like it again. But someone else is, probably right now thinking of her man and how he’s going to touch her. ---------------------------------------- Never go by the way they’re drawn ---------------------------------------- It seems so trite to think about it, and it really is petty, but I’ve let it consume my life, my perceptions. I’m going to be forever colored by her, and no one can ever live up to the image she represented in my mind. I wish I could be shallow and just ignore it like so many others seem to be able to, to simply shed the experience and the person like a snake sheds skin. But I can’t. Not her. ---------------------------------------- One must live while ---------------------------------------- And even though no one can take her place, there’s nothing I can do about it. And I have to keep on suffering the occasional thought of her, because there is no way I can get the goddess back. She may only be twenty miles away now, but she might as well be on another planet. She’s gone, and I have to forget it as best as I can. ---------------------------------------- One must die ---------------------------------------- It won’t stay dead, so I guess I have to deal with it. I’ll have to deal with the fleeting pangs of regret, because they’re not going to go away. Acceptance is the last step of a loss, they say, but they never mention anything about residual regret. Call it the fifth step. ---------------------------------------- One must live while ---------------------------------------- But, still… Whoever said that it is better to have loved and lost than never loved at all… ---------------------------------------- One must die ---------------------------------------- Was full of shit. ---------------------------------------- Goodbye I cant say goodbye It’s all I can do to get up in every morning And when all else fails I try To face myself and everything else But mostly I wonder why… I cant say goodbye It’s all I can do to get up in every morning And when all else fails I try To face myself and everything else But mostly I wonder why… -----------------------'Bare', Anthrax, 1996
