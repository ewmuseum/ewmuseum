You're not a very pleasant little boy, are you?

17 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 17th, 2002 ((Note: My dad's surgery's over, it went well! WOOOHOO!!! Finals tomorrow, that's not making me a very happy panda. I dunno what this is, I was sitting around fighting with a retarded ex and just said to myself, ''I wonder what Aimz thinks about stuff?''...lol.)) It was a great week, in the beginning. I joined up into a fucking amazing, unstoppable team. We're in the process of commanding all the respect we've earned during our time busting our asses for morons like Ed. We're definitely getting ahead, if you couldn't tell from Slam...I mean, we kicked EVERYBODY's asses, there wasn't a significant stone left un-turned. Before all you Nick Caseys, Michael Kriegs and Kory Storms start bitching, take not of the operative term: SIGNIFICANT. Just in case you didn't catch that: sig·nif·i·cant adj. 1. Having or expressing a meaning; meaningful. 2. Having or expressing a covert meaning; suggestive: a significant glance. See Synonyms at expressive. 3. Having or likely to have a major effect; important: a significant change in the tax laws. 4. Fairly large in amount or quantity: significant casualties; no significant opposition Having or likely to have a major effect. See why we didn't bother with you? Okay then. So...the great start to the week didn't last too long. I got rejected. Yes, folks...Amy Campbell was rejected, but we don't need to talk about that right now, do we? No Reno for me. No vehicle for me now, too. Kinda got a little too pissed...that visit to him ended up in me not only bruising a few knuckles in my favorite punching hand, but I managed to wrap my car around a fucking oak tree, as if things couldn't get worse. Life's bitchier than Corg sometimes, huh? I swear, that kid's a born and bred whackjob...anybody hear 'Fuck'? Sure, he wants us to think he's all about Anarchy, but he probably just ripped those lines off of Darkstar's old shirt. I could say 'Fuck' and add a noun in front of it that many times too! Just watch. Fuck the post office. Fuck the police. Fuck you. Fuck me. Fuck that thing over there. Fuck Minion the Imp. Fuck anybody over seven feet. Fuck morons who can't get a clue when a chick likes them. Fuck all the 'Haters'. Hey, here's an idea... Fuck idiots who make themselves up like drag queens, say how much they hate this, that and the other thing, curse and whine a lot just because they think it makes them Gothic. News flash, Corg: I'm not afraid of a skinny 'Goth' geek, I'm not worried about a skinny 'Goth' geek and you know why? Because, quite obviously, I'm not being beaten by a skinny 'Goth' geek. Haven't you been paying attention these last three weeks? You've used scare tactics, and they got to me at first...I mean, who's not creeped out when a freak hides in their locker room then carves their hand with a knife? Who's not creeped out when that same freak starts stalking them and sending people from their past back to haunt them? I was. But not anymore! See, Corg...I've noticed this amazing pattern over the last few weeks; I'm kicking your ass! That's right, bucko! Cut and dry, I'm beating you without fail, week in, week out...it doesn't look to be stopping anytime soon, either. The sad part is that, dude...you could've saved yourself from it. You could've saved yourself from a whole lot of grief if you hadn't decided to piss me off. Sure, we were gonna have to fight at Ascension anyway...but haven't you figured it out? I'm determined...I'll win whether I'm pissed off or not. Unlike you, I'm NOT angry at the world just because I think it's cool...I'm not headmaster of the Pity Parade...I'm Amy Campbell, and I'm surprisingly NOT pissed off at anything that doesn't piss me off. Yes, that now excludes you. See, you hate me and all...but Corg, I was never really mad at you, man! Sure, I thought you were nothing but a whiney, gothic bitch...but why hate me for speaking the truth when there are so many other people you could be angry at? Yeah yeah yeah, you're scared you'll be beaten by a woman in front of the world...afraid a chick's gonna get ahead of you. Well you said it yourself...I'm already WAY ahead of you. I've got these few built-in attributes that set me apart from your kind at birth... Talent. Beauty. Brains. Unlike you, oh-Satanic one, I don't have to wear a bunch of albino makeup, dye my hair a trillion 'evil' colors and get a bunch of 'In the Navy now!' tattoos because, also unlike you, I don't have to TRY to be different or special... Get a fucking clue, I'm already a thousand wrungs above the rest in the ladder of life. And you are....? Getting your ass handed to you by a chick this Sunday. Practically having your career ended because you fucked with the wrong talent. About to be battered and red-faced in front of the wo---oh, wait...we handled that last week. Essentially...Corg, fuck me? Well fuck you too, sunshine.
