You gotta fight! For your right! To PARRRRRRR-TAY!

19 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-19-02 Bhvvvvv! Bhvvv bhvv, bhvv bhvv bhvvvv! Bhvvv! Bhvvv! Bhvvv bhvv, bhvv bvvv bhvvvv! The sounds of a phat bass kick bring us into this scene, right before the camera fades in to reveal a suburban household, though for a change this time it is NOT Civil Disobedience’s. You can only have about twenty promos from one location before you need to spice things up a bit, break the monotony a little, you know what I mean. This current house we are focused on is obviously the site of a party, judging by the sheer number of cars in the driveway and drunkards passed out in the grass. The fact that the music playing inside can be heard for almost a block away helps with this line of reasoning as well. From a quick glance, the party appears to be quite kicking, as the kids say these days. You know what that means, don’t you? That’s right, Civil Disobedience is bound to show themselves! Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke would never, EVER miss a kicking party, so you know they’re about to pop up on the screen any second now. Despite the fact that they have done so many times in the past, this time the boys from the ‘burbs do not disappoint. In fact, it seems as if they do the opposite of disappointing, in that they measure up to all the expectations made of them and more, by arriving with Hollywood superstar Keanu Reeves in tow, per contract agreement. “You know, Keanu...just by hanging out with us, you give us so much credibility,” Trevor remarks to his defacto indentured servant, slapping Keanu on the back. Reeves sighs in response. “How many more days am I stuck in this agreement?” he asks, a reserved tone coating his voice. “I haven’t really been keeping track. You’ll know when it’s over, though,” Trevor says, before laughing to himself. “Unless we forget to tell you, that is.” “Which wouldn’t be a bad thing,” Seth says, placing his arm around Keanu’s shoulder. “It might be the best thing that could happen to you, getting to hang out with two cool dude’s like us for the rest of your life. Thing of how much we could help your career. For one, we wouldn’t let you accept any more of those shitty roles like the one’s you’ve been taking lately. What the fuck was with Hardball, anyway?” Keanu sighs again, not bothering to validate the question with an answer as he pushes Seth’s arm off his shoulder. “I understand. You don’t want to talk about it. If I were you, I wouldn’t want to be reminded of it either,” Seth states sympathetically, before craning his head to look at the house standing before them. A longingness appears in his eye, and he’s already inching toward the structure when he turns back to address his accomplices. “Hey, you two guys have fun. As for me...well I think I’m long overdue for some good old fashioned getting wasted. So yeah...later!” And with that Seth disappears in an instant, a cloud of smoke in place of the outline of his form, which quickly fades away. Trevor smiles at Keanu and shakes his head in disbelief. “Ah, that crazy kid. He’s going to sleep easy tonight,” Trevor says in a fatherly tone, as he and Keanu begin walking at a casual pace toward the front door of the house. “Yeah, in a pool of his own vomit,” Keanu states with a joyless chuckle. “Eh, at least he’ll be well rested for our match tomorrow night. With all of the partying us three have been partaking in this week, he’s long overdue for it.” “And the hangover he’ll have tomorrow morning won’t pose a problem?“ Keanu asks, raising an eyebrow at Trevor. “It never has before,” Trevor responds with a shrug. “You know, for being professional wrestlers, you guys sure don’t train much,” Keanu remarks condescendingly. “Why bother. We’re only facing Twisted Metal, and Carrot Top is no longer going to referee the match. It’s already in the bag. Why put any unnecessary effort forth, ya know? Especially when there are plenty of better ways to spend your time. Like this party for example.” Trevor explains nonchalantly, as if it were the most obvious thing in the world. By now the two men have reached the entrance of the house. Trevor, not even looking forward, reaches his hand out to pull the door open, but it ends up completing this task for him, flying open from the inside. He and Keanu jump back with a jolt as a form soon lurches out onto the porch. “Whoooooooooooooooo! Par-TAY!” Seth Masters howls as he stumbles out onto the lawn with a bottle of beer in his hand. He makes it all of about ten feet before he stumbles over his own two feet and flies careening headfirst into the ground, where he remains laying in a daze. “Good God, the man is drunk already?” Keanu utters in disbelief. “He was barely in there for a minute!” Trevor walks over to his fallen partner and sweeps over him with his eyes. Leaning down, he takes the beer bottle from Seth’s hand, then makes his way back to Keanu with a wry expression on his face. “He’s not drunk. You should know by now that’s how Seth always acts,” Trevor says, then taps on the top of the bottle with his fingers. “I mean, hell, this thing isn’t even open yet.” “I can’t believe I’m here right now,” Keanu mutters to himself in disbelief, gazing down at Masters, who is just now pulling himself off the ground awkwardly. Reeves is in the midst of rolling his eyes when he is bumped into by a typical punk rocker teenager on his way out of the house. The guy is your average stereotype: dyed green hair, shaved on the sides, piercings jutting out of every visible orifice, dog collar, leather jacket, the whole shabang. Recoiling a bit from the sight of this reject of society, Keanu moves to make room for the kid to leave, but the young degenerate seems to be offended by the physical contact he has just experienced. “What the fuck man? You trying to get all up in my way? Don’t you know that nobody TOUCHES ME?!” he shouts, getting all up in Keanu’s face like some white trash ho off of Jerry Springer. To say the least Keanu is disgusted, most likely by the kid’s stank breath, but the lack of respect probably doesn’t help matters any. “Check your ego, man. I don’t even want to be at this party right now, and the last thing I need is some shit from some little jerk-off like you,” Keanu answers back with a sigh. “Oh, that’s fucking it, man! Check MY ego?” The kid raises his hand in the air and snaps his fingers, before making the shape of a gun with his digits. “You know what that sound is? It’s the sound of your doom, biatch! You’re gonna die!” the kid screams in imitation of an eighties rock singer, ala Axl Rose. Of course he’s just joking. The real sound of Keanu’s doom will be Rosie O’Donnell’s voice the next time she loses control and goes on an eating rampage through down town Los Angeles. That knowledge doesn’t seem register with Reeves at the moment, however, as he is currently looking quite perturbed at the drunkard standing in front of him. “Goddammit!” Reeves shouts, looking up to the sky. “What the hell did I do to deserve this shit?!” Getting up from the ground, Seth sees the confrontation that’s taking place by the door and begins walking toward it. He pauses as he passes Trevor, who is watching the whole thing with a grin on his face. “Why aren’t you doing anything, man?” Seth asks, pointing toward the conflict in front of them. “Because this is some entertaining shit, right here,” Trevor answers with a laugh. “Keanu is going to fuck this dude up. Remember the Matrix? Shit, he’s going to jump into this dude’s fucking stomach and tear him apart from the inside and shit.” “Oh yeah!” Seth exclaims, a smile appearing on his face as well. A second later, however, it fades. “Wait...wasn’t that just a movie?” “Yeah, it was, but they can’t make that kind of shit up. Do they expect me to believe they use some sort of ‘magic computers’ to generate effects like that. No fucking way, man!” “That’s bullshit and you know it, Trev. I expect better of you,” Seth says, raising an eyebrow at his partner. “Hey, I can play dumb sometimes, too, you know,” he answers back. “Ooo look, it’s getting good!” The two return their attention to Keanu, who is currently getting pummeled in the stomach by the young punk. It isn’t long before Reeves is curled up in fetal position on the ground, trying to shield himself from attack. “Alright, I think I’m going to break this up now” Trevor says, leaving Seth staring in wonder as he approaches the punk from behind. “Get your hands off of Mr. Reeves, dicksmack!” he shouts, pulling the kid off of Keanu by the back of his neck. Spinning him around, Trevor delivers a punch to the bridge of the kid’s nose, effectively knocking him down. As he lands sprawled across the ground, Trevor kneels on the boy’s chest and begins to rain punches down on his face. “Don’t!...Fuck!...With!...Our!...Hollywood!...Celebrities!...You bitch!” Trevor manages to lecture the now-unconscious rapscallion between landing blows to his head. “Keanu!...Reeves!...Is the fucking man!” Finishing up his beating, Trevor dusts his hands off and helps Keanu up to his feet. “Come on, dude. That was pathetic,” chides Trevor, as he regards Reeves sadly. “Didn’t you learn any hand to hand combat techniques when you brought Genghis Khan to the future in that time machine?” “A time machine...hmm that’s interesting,” Seth mutters calmly, before losing himself in thought. Keanu, on the other hand, seems to be on the verge of breaking down at this point. “Don’t you realize that was a movie, you fucking dumbass!?” Reeves shouts angrily, wiping a trickle of blood from his nose. “Can’t you frickin’ retards separate reality from fiction!? It’s like you live in your own fucking little worlds!” “For your information, Keanu, my world is frickin’ huge!” Trevor answers back sarcastically, before pointing in the opposite direction. “Now what do you say we just jet and go back to our place. Any party that would allow complete dicksmacks like that little bitch to walk around all cocky and shit is definitely not up to our standards. I don’t care how much bass they’re pumping.” “No matter what we do, I’m sure there is plenty more humiliation coming my way, courtesy of you two bonehead losers,” Keanu says with a sigh. “Did I mention I don’t want to be here yet? Because I don’t.” “Aw...poor Keanu,” Trevor sarcastically remarks. “I’m sure there’s a valuable lesson to learn in all of this.” “Read the fine print of the contract before you sign?” Keanu offers. “I was thinking more along the lines of learn how to fucking fight before you go to hardcore parties, but I guess both ideas would work.” Trevor shrugs, obviously not caring too much about it. “Oh well, whatever. Come on, Seth. Let’s go home.” Seth snaps out of his trance and follows his departing comrades, as they wander back down the street to their oh-so-familiar abode. “You know, I had to break a date with Kirstin Dunst tonight to go to this shit party,” Keanu complains as they disappear into the distance. “Whoa, Kirstin Dunst? She’s hot as hell!” Seth exclaims. “Do you think you could introduce us? I’d love to hit that shit!” “Um...no,” Keanu replies, his agitated tone the last thing heard before the scene fades to black.
