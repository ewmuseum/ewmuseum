Good things come to those who wait.

2 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/02/02 Patience is a virtue. Impatience is a fact of life. Jim Axtell waits impatiently at gate 24 inside of Tulsa International Airport. He checks his watch, then walks over to the monitor hanging overhead. “Doesn’t ‘On Time’ mean on time anymore?” Jim paces over to the large window that spans the whole wall next to the gate, scanning the airfield for arriving planes. His black Dave Gibson “Shut Up and Wrestle” T-shirt fits snugly against his well defined body, and the jean shorts and sandals tell the tale of the heat outside. Finally, off in the distance, a minute speck begins to grow in the sky. A warm smile comes across Jim’s face. “There she is,” whispers Jim to himself giddy as a six year old on their birthday. The plan touches down flawlessly on the airstrip, small plumes of smoke coming from the tires as they meet the pavement. ”Ladies and gentlemen…..United Flight 1220 from Omaha is now arriving and will be taxiing into gate 24 shortly.” The message over the intercom sends a shiver down Jim’s spine. He moves, almost skipping, over to the door leading to the concourse and stands guard. He makes sure he is the first in line and the most obvious to be seen. The airplane crawls into the gate and comes to a stop. The concourse slowly moves over and attaches to the plane, blocking the door and the exiting passengers from view. By now there are several other people, both single and families, standing in wait behind and around Jim. A stewardess opens the exit door to the concourse and moves aside. Soon people start to meander up the concourse, and Jim looks on with eager anticipation. Several people exit the doorway and are met by their families and friends, many warm greetings take place as loved ones are welcomed. Patience is a virtue. Impatience is a fact of life. Jim is almost pacing in place as he can barely stand to wait any longer. His palms are clammy, beads of sweat form on is forehead from the adrenaline rush, and he just looks plain antsy. Then he sees her. Finally. “Kami!” Jim calls out, waving his right arm in the air. Kami Axtell, wearing a white tank top and jean shorts complimented by brown sandals and a white sun visor, looks up and her face blossoms into a huge smile. She quickens her pace up the ramp, struggling with the duffle bag that she carried onto the plane. As she exits the concourse she dumps the bag and lunges into Jim’s waiting arms. “Oh Jim!” exclaims Kami as she wraps her arms around his neck. The couple embraces in a passionate hug and kiss that could win an MTV movie award. Jim lifts her from the ground and spins her around, holding her close to himself. “God I missed you so much,” Jim says in Kami’s ear. “I missed you too, Pooh,” says Kami. “You don’t know how it’s been watching all that has happened to you on the television and not being able to be there to take care of you.” Jim gazes into his wife’s eyes lovingly. “Well you’re here now, babe. This is going to be a great week, I can just feel it.” Axtell walks over and picks up Kami’s carry on bag, then the two walk arm in arm away from the gate. Kami lays her head on Jim’s shoulder and clings tightly to his arm as they walk. “I hate being away from you, but I have to admit that you are doing really well so far. I am proud of you all the while I am missing you,” Kami says with mixed emotions. Her lip quivers slightly as she smiles up at her husband. Jim looks down at her and kisses her softly on the top of her head. “You don’t know how much that means coming from you, babe. Making you proud and happy is the most important thing to me. Pretty soon we will find that balance and everything will be perfect.” Jim pulls his arm from Kami and then wraps it around her shoulder, squeezing her tight. “So,” Kami says as she tries to brighten up and enjoy the time she has with her husband. “Who is this again that we are meeting for dinner?” Jim releases his grip on his wife and the two start to walk hand in hand. “His name is Jesus St. Crux, you know the guy who helped me at Ascension. We are tagging together this week on Slam! against those two assholes Storm and Bones, so we figured we might as well get acquainted with each other in an informal setting. Might give us some chemistry in the ring, you know?” “Mmm-hmm,” agrees Kami. “Don’t worry hon, he’s bringing along his girlfriend Caridad so you won’t be bored with all the ‘guy talk’. She seems pretty nice from the little I know of her,” says Jim. “I’m sure we’ll have a good time, Pooh,” Kami assures Jim. The Axtell’s walk through the sliding doors and out toward the parking garage, leaving the airport and heading toward a week of time spent together. Patience is a virtue. Impatience is a fact of life. Good things come to those who wait. Indeed.
