Search for Competition: Part II

6 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 6th, 2002 The scene opens to the ever so loud locker-room in the Tulsa Convention Center. The locker-room, of course, belongs to the current OSW Tag Team Champions, Riptah and Short Circuit. The two are uncharacteristically early for the show, so they got a rare chance to speak with some of staff around the building. Thus, leading to what we have in the locker-room. Some irrecognizable Old School hip-hop is playing in the background while the locker-room seems rather dull. The OSW Tag Titles are drapped over a chair and two gym bags are slid across the wall, while a lone table sits in the middle of the room. At the table are Riptah and Short Circuit, with a pile of papers in the center of the it. “My God, what takes that woman so damn long?! I told her to be back fifteen minutes ago!” Riptah roars, while rifling through a pile of papers. Short Circuit laughs and leans back on his chair. He’s decked out in the usual baggy jeans, CAUTION tee shirt and big crazy sunglasses. The color of the day is lime-green. “Give her a sec…Who knows, maybe she found us some competition…” laughs Shorty while throwing back a Budweiser. Riptah flips through some more papers, which look like stats of some sort. Riptah is wearing a LA Xtreme Football jersey with baggy shorts on. He scratches his bald head in frustration when the door starts to creak open. Daisy walks in wearing a white, hooded sweater and tight jeans. In tow, she has none other than OSW Referee, Betty Lovejoy! Betty is wearing her ref shirt and tight black shorts as she dumbly smiles. Riptah, however, doesn’t have the same expression. “Daisy, what the fuck is this?” He stands up in disbelief, jaw-dropped in Betty’s direction. Daisy shakes her head in anger. “Uh, you told me to find someone who new about the competition in OSW. Betty’s a referee, so she always sees the competition!” Daisy glares at Riptah, not too pleased with his dissatisfaction. “I said SEES the competition, not SLEEPS with the competition! She’s dumb as a stump! I meant get someone like Nate Dumas or Speedy Riggs!” Riptah sighs in frustration as Betty looks back and forth, utterly confused. Riptah looks at her and shakes his head. “Betty, you see us, Da Westsidaz, are searching for real competition, do you follow me? Well, tonight we have a m-a-t-c-h with Twisted Metal. Do you know anything about Twisted Metal? I mean, will they be true competition or what?” Betty looks at Riptah quizzically and grins, showing off her pretty face. “Of course I know about twisted metal, silly!” She puts her index finger on Riptah’s chest, much to Daisy’s displeasure. “Ya big goof, I may have pretty teeth, but when I had my braces taken out the metal was all twisted and stuff. Me and my brother pretended it was a snake, it was sooooo funny. We were all tired afterward and…” Betty continues jabbering as Riptah ignores her. “Good god, this is gonna be a loooooong search…”
