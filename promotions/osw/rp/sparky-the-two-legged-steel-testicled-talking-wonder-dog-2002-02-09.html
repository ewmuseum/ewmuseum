Sparky the Two-Legged Steel Testicled Talking Wonder Dog

9 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/09/02 The scene opens where we left off. Mike Marvelous is sitting in the passenger seat of his Honda Civic while his personal cameraman Kenny drives, and his manager and midget equivalent, Mini-Mike, sits in the child seat in the back. The Oldies 96’ radio station is on and Mike and Mini-Mike are singing along while Kenny sits there with veins popping out of his head. “Lolly pop lolly pop oooo lolly lolly lolly LOLLY POP!” At the exact moment the pop is heard, Kenny screeches on the breaks and something hit the hood and ramps off the windshield with a loud thunk. Kenny does a 360-degree turn and finally stops. Mike’s hair is standing on end and Kenny is ready to take a heart attack. There is dead silence until Mike finally speaks up. “You don’t think that was a person...did you....” “I don’t know, boss....” “Well...only one way to find out....” Mike opens the door slowly, as does Kenny. Mini-Mike unbuckles himself and also hops out of the car. They all slowly walk to the end of the car. Sure enough, there is some sort of form laying about 20 yards from the car. They slowly walks towards it....closer...closer....closer....until... “Jesus Christ! You guys drive like maniacs!” Mike, Kenny, and Mini-Mike all let out a huge sigh of relief and the guy begins to get up. Except it isn’t a guy...it’s a dog! A Cocker Spaniel to be exact. He shakes the cobwebs out and then at this exact moment, it becomes evident that the dog has no back legs. And also...he seems to have two metallic objects protruding from his groin....steel testicles! The dog begins to walk, pulling himself along the ground with his front legs, and as he does so, his back end drags across the pavement, scraping the steel testicles on the road and causing sparks to shoot up from underneath him. Mike, Kenny, and Mini-Mike just stand there shocked and the dog begins to speak... “Why don’t you bone heads watch where you’re going next time! I only got two legs, but it’s all I have, and I’d like to keep them!” Mike rubs his forehead and lowers his eyes. “We’re....we’re very sorry, dog...and we will try to be more careful in the....WAIT!!! You’re a dog!!!” “No shit, Sherlock. Hey, with brains like yours, why aren’t you off somewhere writing equations for rocket fuel or making machines as small as red blood cells...ass.” Mike just stands there, dumbfounded. His eyes are as wide as saucers. Kenny rubs his eyes to make sure he’s not seeing things, then takes a snickers bar out of his pocket and takes a bite. “Hey, Tubbo...why don’t you put down that snickers bar and pick up a slim fast. You look like you could use it.” “Duh...duuuh...umm...” Mike puts his hand up to silence Kenny then steps forward. “You are a dog...dog’s can’t talk. What is going on, here?” “Don’t ask me, numb nuts, I just use the gifts God gave me. Although, I would really love to have my balls and my legs back. When I had balls, I used to get all the chicks!” “How did you lose them?” “It’s called gambling...” “You gambled your testicles and legs away?” “No...I didn’t pay my lone sharks and they decided to take them as compensation...” “Good God!” “Yeah, I know, that’s what I said. Well, as you can tell...my gambling days are over. I don’t even have a home...” Mike puts his hand to his chin and begins stroking it in thought. He waits like that for a couple minutes and the dog looks at him with a funny look, then looks to Kenny. “Does your friend always space out like this? Oh....he’s into the drugs...isn’t he. You should really send him to some sort of group...” Mike’s eyes suddenly light up. “Dog...why don’t you come live with us? You said you don’t have a home and besides...we owe you for running you over...” “Hmmm...you make a good point....maybe....do you got any booze at your house?” “Absolutely not! I have you know that I do not drink alcoholic beverages of any kind and never keep any in my house!” “Oh....well, I guess I could just sue your asses and call it a good day then...then I could go get some beers with the money I get from you...” Mike’s eyes suddenly widen and he draws in a deep breath. “You can have all the booze you want, but please, don’t sue us!” The dog sits there for a while and then finally looks up at Mike. “Deal.” “Well then, it’s settled. What do you call yourself?” “One sick son of a bitch? I dunno...I’ve never had a name.” “Hmm....” Mike thinks once again and suddenly shoots out a name. “How about...Sparky!” “Do I look like a Dalmatian fire dog on the front of Alpo bags to you?” “No! Sparky...due to the uh...sparks...protruding from your....you know....things...while you walk...' Sparky suddenly pulls a cigarette and a lighter from god knows where, lights it up, and takes a puff. “Sounds good to me, hombre. What do you suck wads call yourselves?” “This is Kenny, my personal cameraman, this is Mini-Mike, my manager and midget equivalent, and I am Mike Marvelous, Canadian Hero, FSW Hall of Famer, Former PCW T.V. Champion, Defender of Justice, Role Model to All, Your—“ “Slow down there, ya pecker wood. I just wanted your names, not your life stories.” “Oh...sorry.” Sparky walks...or crawls...over to the car, sparks shooting up along the way. Mike, Kenny, and Mini-Mike all cover their eyes to shield them from the sparks. Sparky stops near the passenger door. “Ahem.......AHEM.......well, aren’t ya gonna open the door for me, ya ass rammers?” Mike walks over, opens the door, and picks Sparky up and slides him onto the seat. Sparky turns around three times and lies down. Mike then buckles in Mini-Mike and then gets in himself. Kenny starts up the car and drives. Mike eyes the radio and turns it up. A song comes on and he and Mini-Mike begin to sing once again. Mike sings in a particularly high voice along to Whitney Houston’s “I Will Always Love You” while imitating her facial expressions. “AND IIIIIIIIIIIIIII EEEE-IIIIIII.......WILL ALWAAAYSSS.....LOOVE YOOOOOO-OOOOO-OOOOUUU! AND IIIIIII....WILL ALLWAAAAAY-S, LoooOOOVE YOOOOOOOOOOOO—“ Sparky suddenly reaches over with his paw and smacks Mike in the head. “Shut up...SHUT UP! Fer the love of fuck! What is wrong with you, ya queer ass butt pirate!?!” Sparky suddenly looks worried, and eyes his new “friends” suspiciously... “Hey...you guys aren’t gay...are you?” Mike looks confused. “Gay? Well, yeah I’m gay...aren’t you gay? We’re having a gay old time right now if I may say so myself!” Kenny suddenly stops the car and arches his neck so he is close to Mike. “Boss...gay no longer means happy...” “It...it doesn’t? When did this happen!?! What does it mean now..?” “It means...” Kenny leans in close to Mike and whispers something in his ear. The camera suddenly pans away from the car and we hear a loud womanly scream. “EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEK!!!!” The scene fades to black as the car begins to drive again.
