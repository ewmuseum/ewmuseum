For The Love Of The Game

5 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/5/02 4:52 AM, September 4th The night is black, but the moon is shining brightly... I guess self-doubt is natural... Clay Vendetta stands at a balcony window in his Miami estate, backlit by the pale light of the stars, a long shadow cast across the floor... You can only run so long on self-confidence, you know. Eventually something's gotta back it up... Turning and opening the window, Vendetta perches on the ledge, leans his head back, stares at the stars. As the camera pans around to behind him, it can see Vendetta's back, and in the room, Niki Weathers rolling slightly and sighing in her sleep, wrapped up in half the covers. The bed is disheveled. Vendetta is still awake, and bothered. After a big win, it was always a good kind of sore. You know? I could justify it all by saying 'well, yeah, it's the pain of victory'. But now it just hurts. Huge win. Biggest of my career. Destroyed The Cult. Ran my mouth; acted like a smart-ass; but backed it up. Isn't that all that matters? Picked up the paper today. Keep hearing about guys leaving. Quitting. Burnt out. Heart's not in it anymore. Fair enough. Used to never even give it a second thought... couple less asses to kick, right? Right. So why did I pick up the paper today and wonder about my life? 28's too young for a mid-life crisis. 28's too young to be doing anything but living. The shadow flickers lower across the floor as Vendetta slides down onto the balcony; paces back into the room; pauses; stands over Niki's bed. A night doesn't pass without me wondering why she stays. How much do you have to love a man to step out in the line of fire just to boost his spirits every Saturday night? How much do you have to love a man to know that he's broken so many hearts and yet set yours right on the table and say 'I trust you?' I trust you, baby. Still pacing. And then I think about when it's over... if I'm not making the contract, will she still show up? What if I take the sick bump and she's hovering over my bed? She came with me to Ed's funeral. She cried the whole time... not for Ed, but for me, for me... ...how much do you have to love a man? Still pacing. Now, back to the balcony, peering the edge, hands folded on the balcony, head resting askew, on his knees. When it's all said and done, why do I still head out there? All my life it was handed to me. All my life I was living under the charity of somebody else. My parents. My coaches. I could've been a legend at FSU. I wonder if they still remember my name there... Head back, staring at the stars. If I think about it hard enough I can still pick out the first cheer. Might've been a wiseguy in the stands giving me a hard time, for all I know, but it was 'Yeah, Vendetta!' I think I was applying a sleeper hold at the time. Fuck, I *know* I was applying a sleeper hold at the time... 'cause until then, I don't think I gave a shit about this. I still remember Bruno Sammartino. Jimmy Snuka. I remember staring wide-eyed at the legends. I remember being a kid. I remember wasting my life. How many girls did I tell I loved? How many second chances did I waste? When did I stop believing? It was always Clay Vendetta, professional smart-ass. Clay Vendetta, professional jack-off. Ran his mouth a little too often. Backed it up a little less. One day it became Clay Vendetta, professional wrestler, and I was applying a sleeper hold at the time. So the paper says they're dropping like flies. Am I going to be motivated by a little more glory? Or am I motivated now? Back to the bed now, sitting on the edge, hand draped over Niki's leg. Staring, Vendetta pauses, gazes, sighs. One day I woke up and knew I loved her. And in one moment, I woke up and knew this is everything I wanted to do with my life. Some men mop floors for a living. Some men spend forty years behind a desk, dealing with incompetence, dealing with bosses. I'm my own boss. I control my own destiny. I hurt people for a living. What's so wrong about that? Lying back, Vendetta reclaims the covers, rolls slightly to his right. Niki rolls back, wraps in close to Clay, smiling in her sleep. Staring straight into the air, Vendetta closes his eyes... There came a morning under the spotlights of some arena somewhere that I woke up and realized I love this sport. Clayton Battaglia was just some punk kid... but in the midst of a resthold, Clay Vendetta decided he was here to give it anything he had. I am not going to be a note under the 'Hirings and Firings' column of your local dirtsheet. I am not going to be 'Clay Vendetta, the wrestler who could've had it all'. I am not in this for the applause, the glory. There are two people out of 4 billion in this world I will never let down; and they are both in this bed tonight. Fuck everyone else. The darkness is fading... the first drips of sunlight start to head into the room and the sky heads slowly towards a hazy red... One day, in the middle of a sleeper hold, I woke up in love. I love this game.
