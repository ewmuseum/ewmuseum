I have to what!? (Michel/Reno series 2/3)

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 28, 2002 “I have to what!?” Reno Davis exclaimed. He was on the phone with Ed, who didn’t seem to be in the mood for any difficulties. “You and Michel Krieg are going to sign autographs Blockbuster to help promote the Pay Per View tonight.” Ed repeated, annoyed. Reno massaged his temples and fell onto his bed. Reno’s knee throbbed from his match the night before, and he was in no mood to chum around with some German Nazi enthusiast. “Me and Michel Krieg.” Reno repeated, still trying to believe what was happening. “Yes.” “The guy who almost killed ZaC?” Across the motel room, ZaC raised his head and gawked at Reno. Looking at his furry friend with a smile, Reno gestured that it was okay, prompting ZaC to let out a squeal and get on with his business. “Well, there’s going to be some stipulations to make sure nobody gets hurt.” Ed stated. “Such as?” “You like that nice pay check you get?” “Yup.” “If anything violent happens, the perpetrator will be suspended for three months without pay” “Oh...” “Have fun!” Ed said, his tone suddenly more cheerful. “But-” Reno began, only to be interrupted by a loud CLICK as Ed hung up. “Shoot...” Reno said to no one in particular. ZaC rushed onto Reno’s shoulder and tugged at his long red hair. “Well buddy, looks like we’ll be having some fun today!” He sarcastically said.
