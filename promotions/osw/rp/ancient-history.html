Date: June, 1997 Mornings were always the same for him.. a struggle to wake up, followed by the joy of finding his wife beside him. Then to stagger into the kitchen, and help his kids with breakfast? Pure joy. They had a school-aged son and a daughter only two years younger, and they were his pride and joy. Just playing with them made every day special. His wife's family considered them to have it all. A new house (even though it was a small one), a new car (same story), and a government job. In their jealous eyes, their sister had hit the jackpot.. a good family man with money falling out of his ass. This was hardly the case.. it was sometimes hard to get by on a single income, and even though they counted their blessings, they also dreamed of more... That was his downfall. He worked with computers. He had ever since he'd first found out about his first child on the way.. he had given up on a lifetime of theatre as being unprofitable, and instead got into an area that could support his family. And he never regreted it for a moment.. acting could always be a sideline for fun when the kids were older. Paying the bills? That was an absolute nessecity, today. And it was the need for extra money that started all his problems.. and the government's need for a man just like him. He was approached at work by two men in suits, wanting him to come to an internal job interview. It seemed that they had been looking at the files for all government employees, looking for a certain set of skills.. computers, athletics, the ability to work under pressure.. and the ability to be someone else. He had all of those things, and in spades. Until his first concussion in a car accident after graduation, he had been PEI's greatest amateur wrestler, and was on track for the olympics. He excelled in the computer industry, had worked several high pressure projects, and was locally reknowned for his work in several plays. The men introduced themselves as agents of CSIS.. the Canadian Security Intelligence Service. They needed him to to a small job for them.. one weekend, and they would not only pay him well, but arrange to get him a promotion and raise at work. Things seemed to be going well for him.. but the bottom was really falling out. One job turned into another.. and another. Then a week in France. Ten days in Washington. Five days in Russia. And on a supposedly simple three day assignment into China, he was forced to kill a man to defend himself. CSIS used this to their advantage, and made him feel obligated to them.. and started finding excuses to keep him from home. Training, being hired full time, being protected from counter-agents.. they filled him with lies and stories, until he didn't know what to believe. All the while, they trained him.. he went from a slightly out of shape computer analyst into a toned fighting machine. He showed a propensity for weapons, and for stealth. Except for his constant whining on and on about wanting to go home to his wife and children, he was becoming the perfect agent. And then Sullivan came into his life, and everything changed again... John Sullivan and his experiments on mind and body.
