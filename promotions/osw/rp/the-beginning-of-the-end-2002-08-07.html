The beginning of the End

7 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 7, 2002 'Poison-hearted and dying, we walk this earth with nowhere to go, no one to trust and nothing to believe in. Dreams shattering like mirrors, we are falling, with no one to catch us...All because of those who dont understand. MORE... Fear and pain is what we know and trust, for we see and experience it every day of our dying lives, it's almost all we know, sometimes its all we wanna know. People love to hate us and fear us for they dont understand, dont want understand, they are the enemies. We aren't really bad people, we just follow our own paths and love and relish in the fact that we are the dark ones, the midnightmare creatures who scream in the night. Why is it that we are always expected to be 'normal'? To be part of society? Why can't people just face the fact that we are 'us' and not 'them'. Though understanding us would be like trying to understand why people die, and why nothing is what it seems, for we are actually quite not-understandible.' Drip.. Drip.. Drip.. Crimson flows downward on to the ground, filling engraved designs in the floor below and move toward another location. I refrain from allowing my eyes to follow the thick substance, and instead I eye the beauty before me. The chains around her wrists clank while she struggles with the last bit of strength in her form. Such a lovely sight she is..And the way she moves while trying to escape sends me in to estacy. While she struggles I cannot help but watch now as the blood from the lashes which grace her stomach flow in to a container which is sunken in to the ground. Such a slow pace at which the glass fills, making my thirst for more torture grow. Unfortunately I haven't the time to toy with this one any longer. I have more important things to attend to upstairs. 'Shh..Hush now because I have to leave you by yourself now.' I hear her groan beneath her leather gag but I place a finger to her lips to silence even that. 'Not a sound, not even a peep..Nothing.' Nothing was coming past those full lips of hers now, and I could not be more pleased. She is learning quickly and this somewhat surprises me in a way. After finding her on the streets as a run away I figured she would have more vigor and more strive to defeat her captor. I was wrong in thinking this as I am finding out..She is the submissive type after a few hard licks. I head up the cold stone steps now, wanting to find the comfort in the company I knew was awaiting me on the top floor. Once there the chill from the bleakness falls upon me. Such a change from the damp and muggy dungeon I just returned from. As I continue toward my favorite sitting place I suddenly still at the sound of a hum coming from the place of rest I was seeking. 'I knew you would show. Welcome.' He was never one to speak so I figured I had to make the first words enter the air. Though I don't wait for a responce, instead I grab my black bag that is resting near by my booted feet and I sling it over my shoulder. I hear him shuffle from around the chair and finally I am staring in to his shadow laden face. No trace of visibility is able to be made from his looks, nothing but darkness. 'I take it today is going to be a ride I will not soon forget?' Again I do not wait for an answer, I now simply follow him to my destination point while in my head I conjour up the yummy things I have planned for my prisoneer below. Oh how much fun I will have after I complete my first session of true brutality. 'Mmm..'
