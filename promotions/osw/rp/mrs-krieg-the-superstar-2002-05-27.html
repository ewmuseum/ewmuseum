Mrs Krieg: The Superstar

27 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27.05.02 A camera pans in through a glass pane in a door, looking into some sort of living room, as Krieg can be seen sitting at a computer, typing away with his massive fingers. The cameraman nudges the door open slightly and walks towards Krieg and his PC ever so slowly, the German powerhouse slowly replying to some sort of message. As he types, he says the occasional few words out loud, “....Propaganda will be...well as Sagar...new International Champion....lumberjacks will play....that I gain over Propaganda...for your support, Comrade....Michel Krieg...Finished mother,” shouted Krieg, Krieg sat back in his wooden seat and held his arms behind his head, stretching off after answering quite a few fan mails from his loyal comrades. Looking round to the left and turning in his seat, Krieg flashed a school boy smile towards his mother as she waddled out of the kitchen, carrying a small tray, holding a play with 4 or 5 triangle sandwiches, a large glass of milk and a small block of cheese, “You are so good to me mother,” said Krieg, “If not for you, that stinking Capitalist Propaganda may have seized the moment on Slam, and attempted to attack Krieg,” “It is Ok son,” replied Mrs. Krieg, placing the tray down on a small table next to the PC and resting her hands on Michel’s shoulders, “I can not stand to see Capitalist like that religious goon destroy what you have built,” “Ah mother, you are my saviour,” “Since your father,” said Krieg’s mother, he voice rather harsh, “had to leave, I’m the only one who can defend the honour of Krieg when you are hurt, da?,” “Yes mother,” answered Krieg, “Now, if it’s Ok, I need to get on with some work for my fans,” Krieg grinned at his mother as her expression changed to a questionable look, “Are you telling Berneille Krieg to get lost, Michel” “Emm....Not really....I’m just kind of asking....Just so I can work in peace...I didn’t mean peace, I just meant-“ “You watch your mouth, Michel. You are not too big for me to clip you around the ear! And anyway, I will be coming with you to Slam next week,” chirped Berneille, “I am a star now,” Krieg closed his eyes and dropped his head slightly, rubbing his hand across his forehead, “Oh my heavens...” he said quietly, “Did you say something Michel,” asked Mrs. Krieg, staring down at Krieg, “No mother. Not a word,” “Good...Because I wouldn’t want you to be jealous because you’re own mother is getting some big time exposure, now would I,” “Of course not mother,” said Krieg, gritting his teeth as he does, “Now, there’s something I’ve got to ask you Michel,” “Yes mother. What is it,” replied a slightly annoyed Krieg, “I’m really stumped....What will I wear for Saturday night,” asked a puzzled Mrs. Krieg, “....dear god....” Krieg and his mother both turn to the camera, Krieg putting on a confused face and his mother donning a cheeky grin as they freeze still, Narrator: Oh no. What is next for Krieg? Will Berneille be the star she wants to be? Will Krieg overcome the power of evil and down the Capitlist monster, Propaganda? Will Mrs. Krieg look like a superstar or a old German woman next week on Slam? Will Krieg’s passive father return to save the honour of the Krieg’s? We will have to wait a see.... Fade...
