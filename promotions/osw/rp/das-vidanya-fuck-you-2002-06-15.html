Das Vidanya? Fuck You.

15 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 15, 2002 “Das vidanya, in Russian, mean's goodbye, Reno Davis!!” I heard Ivan say, his words echoing in my skull. It about at this point that I realized shit was about to hit the fan. My body ached, my knee seared as if for every millimeter it moved, I tore a vein. The suspension of the car groaned and as I looked above me through my blurred, cursed, eyes I saw I saw floating above the truck. Wait, No I wasn’t...It was just about to fall on me. Lovely. I lifted my shoulder, causing a spiral of pain to flow through my body until I could only remain stiff on the cold ground. Fuck. “Das vidanya, Angel…now you can learn for sure if you are wanted in heaven!” He shouted again. That fucking maniac. I flinched and tried to roll over once again, but I was too weak. My body failed. The truck crashed down upon my corpse, and I knew at this moment that God had gotten fed up. God was bringing me back to Heaven. The fuck he was. The Unwanted Angel doesn’t die for anyone, and if he didn’t know that yet, he needed a slap upside the head. The truck’s roof bent around me, as the weight caused it to form almost a mold around me, saving my life. I heard Ivan’s laughing, his fanatic laughing, muffled only slightly underneath the truck. The pain was absent, as I’d gone numb minutes ago from his previous beating. “You killed a part of me…now..I kill a part of you…DYAAHAAHAA!!!” That fucker screamed. Little did he know that I had a smile on my face! I just cheated death. I just cheated Ivan. I just cheated God. And there wasn’t a damn thing anyone could do about it. Suckers. The waiting game began. The paramedic would be here in seconds, I knew that, but under here it’d feel like hours. I wondered what Amy was doing now, what she was thinking earlier by joining up with Reagan. Fuck, what a bitch Reagan is. She’s something special to look at, but she’d drive me to slitting my wrists within a few minutes of being with her. I was getting bored, as I think I passed out. I couldn’t hear screaming anymore, just silence. Yeah, I must have passed out. I began to sing in my head a bit, to pass the time. The song ‘Half-Assed Jedi’ by John Cougar Concentration Camp was in my head. Fuck I love Star Wars. Wish I had my lightsaber with me. I’d be all ‘voom voom! snick! snick! WHAAAAA JEDI!!!! And cut this truck and half and stuff. Anyway, I don’t actually remember the whole paramedics ever saving me, but I remember waking up and seeing Amy and ZaC looking down upon me in the hospital. I really hate hospitals. But what made my stay even worse was the way Aimz was acting during her visit....
