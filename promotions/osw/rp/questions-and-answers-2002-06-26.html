Questions and Answers

26 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-26-02 From his office deep beneath the Kremlin, Alexei Ruslan was free from the rigors of surface life. He could easily live beneath the earth in his metal encased office for hours upon hours on end if he needed to. From there, he didn’t have to deal with the public, the people that he, as per his job, preyed upon. However, while he had gone back to Russia after Ascension, Ivan Stanislav chose to stay in America. That was strange to him. Ivan Stanislav, willingly choosing to be cared for in America, as opposed to within the safety of the Russian Federation? But even more disturbing was the phone call he had received late the other night, which would have been in the afternoon in America, from The Russian Bear, who was still in the hospital…. “Alexei, it is time for a change.” “A change? What are you talking about Ivan?” “Things have come to light…things are going to be different!” Ivan Stanislav…..talking about change?? This worried Alexei. But Ivan does what is best for the group, he always knew that, and would go along with whatever Ivan wanted. Things were changing all around Alexei Ruslan as he sat in his plush office. Rumblings of an upheaval in the chain of command not only in his sector, but the military sector as well had begun to scare him. But deep within his little ‘hole in the ground’ as he called it, Ruslan felt safe. People could not bother him. People did not DARE to bother him. And whatever Ivan wanted to do, he’d go along with it. RING…..RING……RING….. A large red phone atop Ruslan’s desk rang loudly as he almost fell out of his chair. He fixed his hat, which had fallen down over his eyes, and grabbed at the phone. “WHAT?!” screamed Ruslan, his voice cracking just slightly. He paused and listened to the voice on the other line, “A Cuban and a Major in the Chinese Military?!” He digested this for a moment, biting his lip, and nodded, “Yes, send them down!” He hung up the phone with a slam and leaned back in his chair, “Cuban and Chinamen? What are they doing here?! HOW did they get here?!” Ruslan sat in his chair and waited, trying to figure out how exactly Speedy Riggs and Loyal Communist Party Member Wang found his location. His large steel door opened up, revealing Wang in his military uniform and Speedy dressed in his red and yellow referee shirt and a pair of shorts and sandals. An agent in Alexei’s division nodded to them and walked away as Ruslan stood and blinked as he noticed Riggs, “Comrade Riggs…dressed unseasonably warm for Russia, don’t you think?” “A….CHOO!!” Riggs sneezed, sending white snowy particles from his dark hair and blew them to the floor. Wang nodded and walked into the room, quickly shaking Alexei’s hand. “Comwade Wuswan, I apowagize for our sudden entwance!” Wang took a seat and sat down as did Riggs, he crossed his leg on his knee. Alexei grabbed a bottle of vodka from a nearby cabinet and poured three glasses of it for each guest and sat at his desk. “No apology needed my friends! I am glad that you have come, as matter of fact. Things have been very hectic lately, not just within Russia, but within OSW as well and I am glad that we can sit down as friends and merely discuss matters!” Ruslan downed his drink like a pro and leaned back in his chair. Wang leaned forward a bit, for once he appeared to be very intense, “Comwade, we are concerned with things going on within Wed Awmy…we wanted to speak to Pwapowchik, but he weft! So now we wanted to see if perhaps you can fill us in.” Alexei swallowed as Riggs nodded, “That’s right Alexei. We’re all very curious.” Alexei stood up slowly and put his hands behind his back, “To be honest, I am not sure what is going on, my friends.” He walked over to a picture of Lenin and stared at it, “I want you to keep an eye on Michel Krieg.” Wang and Riggs answered simultaneously, “Krieg?” Alexei turned on his heels, “Da, Krieg. He has been acting strangely. He has defied Ivan blatantly. And I know that you two have seen his capitalistic tendencies.” The two men nod in response to Alexei’s statement, he continued, “Tom Walczak is getting what he deserves. Stanislav has won against Reno Davis…and Krieg has not. We are afraid he might try something strange. He is branching out..it is time to reel him in.” Wang and Riggs nodded and stood. “Good,” said Wang, “I do notice what you mean. Also, El Drago, what of him? Should we come down and make sure he loses?” “Nyet, Comrade,” replied Ruslan, “Ivan wants to handle Drago on his own. I believe he is fighting him for more than just ideological reasons, I think it’s slightly different. Drago is a wrestler, Ivan is a wrestler….it will be a real wrestling match for once.” Riggs nodded, “I will keep an eye on him Alexei.” “How is Pwapowschik?” questioned Wang. Alexei paused for a moment and stared at the floor, “Getting rid of Reno Davis has done a lot to help him….but he is still bothered, I believe. He keeps talking about some sort of ‘change’” “Change?” “Da, change. I do not know what. I am going to see him tomorrow, I will know more then. My friends, something is going to happen..and I do not know what..but I can assure that if you stick by Praporschik Stanislav, all will be fine!” Alexei nodded to both men, who made their way to the door of his office. “Will do Alexei! Thanks!” answered Riggs as he grabbed the door and walked out. Wang nodded to Ruslan and shut the door, leaving Ruslan to his seclusion again. But he had to wonder: What was Ivan talking about?
