Date: blahblahblah [Begin File.] “Well, let’s not do THAT again.” The mechanical duo known as Twisted Metal, make their way home after a most disappointing encounter with the so-called Vax. One that resulted with them getting spilled into a dumpster. Gunmetal idly examines their hard earned Pentium X fake. “Eh, it is hardly a surprise. Humans enjoy the idea of getting something, then reneging on the agreement. I believe that they worship such a thing as the one of the cornerstones of their existence. The ‘shaft’, if I recall it right. It’s in cars, buildings, and at least 49% of the population, I could go on and on about how often it appears in daily life.” Chico kicks at a loose bit of pavement in his path. Pwting! The shard of concrete ricochets off of a car, two buildings and a highly startled pigeon before coming to rest. “Sounds like that shaft is one bad mutha.” “I can dig it. But that does not address what we will do with this piece of junk.” Gunmetal holds out the chip for inspection. “They didn’t even TRY to make it a reasonable facsimile, it is just a chunk of aluminum with a Pentium X sticker on it. It couldn’t even fool TI-290.” “It doesn’t HAVE to fool your little bit-byter,” Chico tilts his head slightly, “it just has to fool Cobol and he’ll buy anything.” “Well, that should be easy enough. He thinks that Shakespeare is a deadly weapon.” “An inappropriate analogy. Half of OSW thinks that.” “Okay, he thinks Michael Bolton has something to do with an energy transfer process.” “Better.” They travel the last few feet down the walkway to their basement apartment. Chico shoves open the door with a loud BANG! “Lucy! I’m home! You gots some esplainin’ to do!” Harsh red lights blaze from a dark patch of shadow on the far side of the room. “Unit Chico is in error. This unit is designated Cobol. This unit is unit Chico’s master unit, no explanations are required.” “We love you, too.” “Error. Human biochemical reaction to two pounds of chocolate irrelevant. Unit Gunmetal will produce Pentium X chip.” A casual flip of the wrist sends the aluminum plate spinning across the room. It barely gets into the air before it is snatched up by one of the many mechanical tentacles sprouting from Cobol’s back. “Primary Mission, acquisition of Pentium X chip accomplished. Begin phase two, transport chip to Master computer C.L.O.D.” Chico scratches at a small patch of rust. “Can’t we just mail the thing?” The random whip-crack flailing of Cobol’s tentacles slows momentarily as he process the information. “Negative.” “Nuts.” “Salted plant material irrelevant. Postal services unsecured mode of transportation. Unit Gunmetal, search and locate appropriate cab fare for this unit.” This last is directed at Gunmetal’s retreating back. Gunmetal’s head swings completely around as his body still walks away. “Get it yourself. TI-290’s calling me.” He continues towards a faint beeping noise coming from the other room. Cobol’s cameras swivel back to Chico. “Unit Chico will explain term ‘TI-290’.” Chico shrugs. “She’s Gunmetal’s daughter.” A slight grinding noise comes from somewhere within Cobol’s midsection. “Error. Robots do not have daughters. Unit Chico will explain phenomena of Gunmetal’s daughter.” If he had eyebrows, Chico would be raising one right about now. “Shouldn’t YOU be the one explaining this to ME? Nevermind. You see, uhhh… when two robots love each other… erhmmm… well…” “Human biochemical reaction to two pounds of chocolate irrelevant.” “Mmm, yeah… well…” “Unit Chico will cease stalling tactics.” “Truth is, I have no idea how he managed it. Ask him, not me.” “Ask me what?” Gunmetal walks in, a small calculator bundled in pink blankets is held firmly in his arms. Unit Gunmetal will explain phenomena of Gunmetal’s daughter.” Gunmetal takes a quick glimpse at TI-290 before looking again at Cobol. “You do not know? Then how did you create Chico and myself?” “Units Chico and Gunmetal were constructed using parts obtained from Circuit City, Radio Shack, Westpark Scrapyard and Earl’s Electronic Warehouse.” Gunmetal’s lights blip in surprise. “Does this mean that we are mongrels?” Chico shrugs, “Don’t let it bother you, it just means that we can use more ethnic jokes, that’s all. How many toasters does it take to electrocute a family of five?” Cobol’s running lights flare angrily. “Irrelevant and inefficient. Decapitation of humans more effective. Continue explanation.” “Well, I was seeing that crane at the time, remember? Cobol cut the line and we exploded in that crowd of people?” “Affirmative. Many air breathers were eliminated.” “After I was rebuilt, I went back with a six pack of WD-40 and a dozen rose scented air fresheners to patch things up… only when I got on the roof, I searched all over, but she was gone!” Chico gives him a friendly pat on the back. “You know how construction equipment is, here one week, gone the next. No point trying to tie them down. They’re free spirits.” A low angry hum emanates from within Gunmetal. “The slut probably met a jackhammer while I was out. Anyway, I ran into her again down by the new bridge. Naturally, I demanded an explanation. One thing led to another, and well… I found TI-290 in the driver’s cabin. Needless to say, I was shocked! I never even suspected she had a calculator in the glove compartment!” “Are you sure she’s yours? TI-290, I mean. She could be the get of that damn jackhammer or something…” Targeting data blips up furiously on Gunmetal’s HUD for a moment before he regains control. “I could tell by the serial number, all right?” “Just checking. It was a question that had to be asked.” “Gunmetal’s status lights return to their usual green state. “Yeah well, right then I took TI 290 and walked away. I couldn’t bear to have her go through her first upgrades with that lying, conniving, earthmover. I’ve never gone back, and I never will.” Chico regards Gunmetal steadily. “It’s a Kodak moment, isn’t it?” Cobol, faint wisps of smoke rising from his vents, looks from the calculator to Gunmetal and back again. He beeps loudly. “Irrelevant... deleting. Unit Gunmetal, search and locate appropriate cab fare for this unit.” “Did you try the change cup?” Instantly, a clawed tentacle snatches up the clinking jar. “Metallic discs are of sufficient quantity for secure transport to Master computer C.L.O.D. Unit’s Chico and Gunmetal will await initiating signal to commence robot rebellion.” Stiff legged, Cobol marches from the apartment, hell bent on his mission. He bumps into Gunmetal, which causes the calculator in Gunmetal’s arms to beep an alarm. “Dammit! And I just got her settled too!” Gunmetal begins rocking the little machine in a vain attempt to silence it. “Have you tried turning off the clock function?” Chico asks helpfully. “Can’t. No instructions. Got a power cord? I think she’s hungry.” [End File.]
