Adventures in Sea World (Ivan/Tempest rp Pt. 1)

26 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-26-02 Saturday Slam was a night of random chaos on many levels. Many, many levels. From the six man tag team match to the four corner tag team table match to the Tempest vs. Meanstreak match and on. But for one Ivan Stanislav, Saturday Slam was a night unlike any other. Gaining the World Title shot against Sebastian Toomes was a great feat that Stanislav surely was trying to accomplish, protecting Tempest was another major area of his plan of the night, and destroying Doug Troy was another major plan for the night. But it was one action that really changed the outlook of Ivan Stanislav. A kiss. Two people touching each other’s lips together as a sign of affection. A sign of trust. A sign of appreciation for one another. And it was with that movement that a huge wall, built high and strong inside the mind of Ivan Stanislav, came crumbling down…. The camera fades up on Ivan Stanislav and Alexei Ruslan, who are standing in Ivan’s hotel room in Orlando, Florida, the night after Saturday Slam. Stanislav wears his typical attire, as does Ruslan. Stanislav’s hands are on his hips, his massive head drawn down slightly, focusing his eyes on the floor. Ruslan’s arms are crossed over his chest. Ivan’s booming voice creeps out of him, not very loud, but very thick and powerful nevertheless. “I do not know Alexei, I do not know what happened.” Mutters The Russian Bear. “It was as random as anything Ivan has seen.” Alexei nods to himself and uncrosses his arms, “Who do you think did it? Who would have known?” Ivan shrugs and looks up at Alexei quizzically, “I do not know, Alex Gregorovich. Whoever it was, they think they can undermine everything that I have worked on!” He growls, turns, and walks into the living room and looks out a large window. “They will not, whoever they are.” “Nyet, I do not think they will succeed, but we have to be careful, Praporschik.” Alexei walks closer to his massive friend, “They obviously are attacking an area of you that you are not used to defending. It is just good that you were able to think of diversion so fast!” Ivan turns slowly and rubs the back of his head. He nods, “Yes, there was not much that I could do, Alexei. And thank goodness that it worked…..” Ivan trails off and stares at the floor…. “Is there something else, Praporschik?” Asks Alexei. He raises his eyebrows and snakes his head out slightly, looking up at the massive Soviet soldier. Ivan shrugs uncomfortably and puts his hands in his pockets, “Well….” Alexei nods and replies very, very softly, “You enjoyed it, Praporschik?” Ivan looks up and walks past Ruslan and stops, avoiding his gaze. “I suppose you could say that, my friend. When Ivan first..kissed her, there was nothing..and then she put her arms around my neck and I felt something..something very strange.” Alexei nods and stands, not wanting to push the subject. Ivan shakes his head and turns, his face very confused, “It was akin to happiness. But not the happiness that I feel when I succeed in mission, or preach communism, or win match. It was much more…inward. Does that make sense?” “I think that means,” whispers Alexei as he takes a step closer to Ivan, “that you yourself was happy, for yourself, Ivan. That is okay, you understand? You can feel that.” Ivan nods and continues, “It was very strange Alexei, very, very strange. I did not want to let go…I did not want to change anything around me.” He looks around and kicks a small end table, “What is going on Alex?” “You are beginning to like her Ivan. Like her very dearly, I believe.” “How is that possible?” Ivan raises his arms, exasperated, “This is woman who, in first meeting, we had war of words because she talked on radio show before me! This is woman that was friends with Jane Reagan! This is woman that played practical joke on me!” Ivan pauses and lowers his arms, “But…” Alexei waits as Ivan doesn’t speak anymore, “But? Praporschik?” “But this is also woman,” mutters Ivan softly, “that appreciates what I am doing. And seems to enjoy ME! And appears to have faith in the cause, and in myself….” He trails off again and tilts his head and looks at Alexei, “This is very strange.” “You are right, Praporschik,” says Alexei, “she is very special.” “I just hope my forwardness did not…startle her. I do not hope she is afraid or uncomfortable..” Ivan shrugs, “perhaps I should visit her? She is in the area, of course.” “You should, Ivan. That would be gentlemanly thing to do.” Alexei grins and walks over to Ivan. “Go Praporschik. Go and see her and have some…fun today, eh? We have much work to do.” Ivan nods and heads to the door, shaking a chandelier overhead with his thunderous footsteps. Ivan turns as he gets to the door, “I will be back later, Alexei.” “Take your time, Praporschik.” Replies Ruslan. Ivan walks out the door leaving Ruslan alone. He smirks and chuckles to himself as the camera fades out…. …and fades in on Ivan Stanislav outside of Tempest’s hotel room in Orlando. The Russian Bear stands outside the door, his head almost hitting the ceiling. He mutters to himself, “Tempest, hello, how are you?” He stops and shakes his head, “Far too casual…” He straightens up in a military fashion, “Hello Tempest.” He shakes his head again, “Too rigid….grrr…” A maid behind Ivan watches him try to figure out what to say. Ivan turns and looks at the maid. She shakes her head and heads away, embarrassing the Russian Bear. He turns to the door and rolls his eyes, “Pull it together, Praporschik!” He knocks on the door heavily and breathes slowly, waiting. 'It is a beautiful thing,' comments Ivan Stanislav as he sits at the front of a row of bleachers at Sea World watching synchronized dolphins perform in an enormous tank, 'to see dolphins working together as collective, do you not agree Tempest?' He looks over at Tempest proudly. No one sits in the vicinity of the two as everyone appears to be more amazed at The Russian Bear than the dolphins..and give him a wide berth at that. Tempest watches with a happy smile and says, 'Only you could find a message like that in there. Do you ever just watch something? Or just enjoy something, and not try to see it as part of that whole communist thing?' She waves her hand as if that communist thing were a fly buzzing about her head. Ivan thinks for a moment and cocks his head to the side and looks at her questioningly, 'You mean just...look at something and not relate it to your goal in life?' 'Yeah exactly... I mean if your life has a goal.' She shrugs and adds, 'Mine doesn't. Except maybe the tireless pursuit of fun and the perfect somersault plancha.' Ivan shrugs, 'Surely you have some sort of goal, Tempest.' He pauses for a moment, 'Somersault plancha...hmm....perhaps a little less than Earth shattering, but I suppose it would do.' He blinks as the hot sun pierces his vision and bakes his pale Russian skin. Tempest smiles and pats his arm. 'Aw, you don't get out much like this do you?' Ivan shrugs, 'Sure Ivan does! He goes on marches, hunts sometimes. I watch orchestra's sometimes...but animals like this...well....nyet, I do not get out like this.' He looks over at her and nods, not necessarily miserable, but uncomfortable. He clears his throat and shifts his weight, 'Uh...Tempest...if Ivan may ask you uh...question...' 'Sure,' she says, with a delighted smile at the flipping flying dolphins. 'Just promise me you won't take me on any of those march thingies; they don't sound like fun.' Ivan chuckles, 'They are not bad, they are quite invigorating! Marching through Siberia with nothing more than military uniform and trench coat on.' He chuckles and looks away from her. 'Uh...I hope that Ivan was not...' he shakes his head, biting his lower lip, 'I hope Ivan did not....' he grinds his jaw and breathes out loudly and looks at her, 'I hope that my forwardness did not...scare you...' He looks away shyly. Tempest finally is distracted and looks at him smiling. 'No, I was... surprised I guess.' She chuckles. 'But just when I was starting to think you weren't human... Well, it was a nice surprise.' Ivan blinks, 'You..you did not think Ivan was...human??' He digests this and looks at her, 'What do you mean?' Tempest sighs and squirms uncomfortably as she says, 'Well, it's just that you don't do things like other people. You weigh everything in terms of your cause. I dunno, it's like you see life as a continuous military engagement. And that means you're always fighting no matter what.' He nods and breathes through his nose loudly again, 'It is not easy being hated by many, many people Tempest.' He grinds his jaw and looks down at the ground, 'It is not that they bother me, I could care less about the people and their opinions, but in order to live for forty years in which almost everyone that sees you has an opinion before they know you, you begin to....handle things in a way that seems to work.' He pauses and shakes his head. Slowly and nervously he puts a huge hand on her shoulder, enveloping her in his massive arm, 'Does that make sense?' She smiles and huddles close as she says, 'Yeah it does. Must be hard having everyone hate you. Especially if you think you're doing the right thing.' She looks up at him questioningly. He nods and looks down at her, 'It is difficult, but it is something that Ivan must do. I do not ask for sympathy, I take great pride in what I do.' He smiles and looks into her eyes, 'And besides, there are those that do not listen to stereotypes and give Ivan fair chance....' He leans down slightly more, getting closer to her. Just at that moment a dolphin jumps and splashes high into the air, raining water all over The Russian Bear and Tempest. Ivan blinks for a moment, his face suddenly very angry, his beard drips. Tempest laughs and wipes the water from her eyes before noticing Ivan's expression. 'Oh come on, don't be mad. They're just playing.' Ivan growls to himself and looks at the dolphins. He runs his hand through his beard and pushes the water out of his beard. He looks around, thinking maybe somewhere else would be more accommodating to talk, 'Come Tempest, shall we take a walk?' Tempest chuckles and says, 'Oh all right.' She stands and leans close to the glass tank as one of the dolphins swims by and waves. 'Sorry boys, see ya later. It was fun. We'll have to do this again some time.' Ivan snorts at the dolphins as they flip and flop around in the water. He offers her his massive arm, letting her wrap her tiny arm around his and leads her out of the bleacher area and down a concrete walkway. 'I do not understand why all these people come to see..animals. Why is it people do this, Tempest?' Asks Stanislav. Tempest laughs, 'Cuz it's fun! They're cute and I dunno, animals are unusual cuz most people don't have them around all the time.' She shrugged. 'I have Trouble cuz he's a great little friend. I mean, he never gets mad at me, is always glad to see me. Animals are different than people like that.' A small child points at Stanislav and runs to his mother, catching Ivan's eye. He mutters more to himself than to anyone else as he notices another individual, a teenager, flicking him off. 'Animals are much different than people,' growls Ivan, 'I suppose.' 'Not entirely,' Tempest says with a warm smile. 'I'm always glad to see you too.' She squeezes is arm a little. Ivan looks over at her with a genuine smile, 'Really?' He chuckles, 'Ivan was beginning to think he was boring old man!' Tempest chuckles and looks up at him as she says, 'Boring? No... not quite. I mean who else could I go out with and be tossed through walls, find myself assailed by angry mobs, and have half my furniture broken?' She smacked her forehead. 'Wait a minute, what the heck is wrong with me?' Ivan grins just slightly and barely laughs, not sure if that was actually a joke or not. He spies a small food stand and looks over at it, 'Are you hungry for anything, Tempest?' He nods to the stand as the vendor realizes that Ivan is looking at him. Tempest swipes her brow and says, 'Ya know, It's pretty hot, I could go for some ice cream, how about you?' Ivan shrugs, 'Ivan supposes so.' He makes his way over with Tempest and looks over at her, 'What kind is it that you want, Tempest?' The vendor cowers behind his ice cream machine. Tempest smiles reassuringly and says, 'Um, how about a strawberry Popsicle?' She chuckled and added, looking toward Ivan, 'It's red after all.' Ivan nods to the vendor, 'Da, two strawberry Popsicle......' Ivan grinds his jaw as the terrified vendor gives Ivan and Tempest a Popsicle each. 'Uh...s...six dollars...s...sir!' Stammers the vendor. 'SIX DOLLARS!' Roars Ivan as he fishes into his pocket, 'That is 189 rubles! I will not pay it!' He scoffs and looks at Tempest, 'Can you believe that! I will not help the American economy!' The vendor blinks while Ivan growls to hismelf, still holding the Popsicle, oblivious to Tempest as his communist ways cloud his mind. Tempest tilts her head at him and says, 'You're kidding right? I mean, you've done great in OSW and PCW before that. You can’t be hurting for money any more than me.' She fishes into one of her pockets, and pulls out some lint, a matchbook, and a gum wrapper. 'Hmm, at least I don't think I'm hurting for money. Should have some here someplace.' Ivan sighs to himself and fishes into his pocket, pulling out a steel clip with a Soviet symbol on it full of dollars, 'This is just something I have, Tempest.' He throws the money to the vendor, 'Take this, thief.' He turns to Tempest, 'You are correct, I do have much money..however, you obviously don't understand. I do not keep my money I get from OSW or PCW, I give it back to my country...all of us of Red Army do that to our respective countries.' He bites half of the Popsicle off, 'However, I do have much money from military.' 'Wow, you send it all back? What for?' She asked curiously as she slurped at her Popsicle. Ivan smiles proudly as he sits down at a bench, still eye level with Tempest, 'My people need the money...Russian ruble is not as valuable as it used to be.' He shrugs, 'It is least Ivan could do for his people. It pains me when I see them starving or freezing. It is communist way in Red Army. We do not keep our money for ourselves, we spread it amongst the people. I am not surprised that you would not exactly understand, few people do.' Ivan looks down at the ground. Tempest smiles and lays her hand on his shoulder as she says, 'That's... really kind. I guess I didn't think about that. But... why can't your people take care of themselves? I thought Communism was supposed to be something where everyone was taken care of and all.' Ivan sighs and shakes his head, 'It is, and during communist time Russia prospered. But when Soviet Union collapsed in 1991....we switched straight to market economy...' He thinks for a moment, staring at something not visible to us, 'Russians are slow moving people, they do not wish to have fast change. So, when Russia instantly changed...no one knew what was going on. And because of that, it declined...and my people suffer because of it.' Ivan pauses and thinks, very engrossed in what he is saying, 'It is the Americans...they did it..it was conspiracy to overthrow my country and hurt my people....' he trails off... Tempest looks thoughtful a moment and says, 'I dunno, America's been really good to me. And I don’t' think anyone of them are really out to hurt people.' She sat down across from him on a high wall, so she still looked him in the eye. 'I mean in a market economy you work and get paid and take care of yourself.' She chuckled and said, 'Ya know, I got Miyuki as a manager because I was so used to being poor that I was acting like I still was... living in tiny cramped apartments, washing my clothes loads deep at the Laundromat. It was weird for me not to wonder where my next meal was coming from.' Ivan looks at her, 'Too many possessions can clutter brain, Tempest, just remember that.' He shrugs, 'Look at what happened to Jane Reagan. She goes from lowly personnel director to president, and see how it has transformed her?' Ivan finishes the rest of his Popsicle, 'Look at these people here, spending exhorbant amounts of money, and it all goes back to rich businessmen who could care less about these people..or these animals.' He shrugs, 'I do not wish to bore you with my beliefs, and besides, it would not change someone's mind anyway, no? But it is something to think about. Besides, I do not think Miyuki likes me...so do not listen to her too much, eh?' He grins crookedly Tempest laughs and rocks back a bit as she says, 'Aww, she's just looking out for me. And you have to agree that that's a tough job.' 'DYAAHAAHAA!!' Ivan suddenly bursts into laughter and slaps his knee, perhaps the most emotion Tempest has ever seen from him, 'DYAAAHAAAHAA!!! That is true!!! That is true!! DYAAHAA---' He suddenly stops as he notices that people are seeing him laugh. He shrugs and suddenly switches back to his granite look. Tempest ignores them entirely and grins. 'Well than don't be to hard on her for it. Besides, she doesn’t know you as well as I do. And I keep reminding her of that fact. Besides, I know that being different isn't such a bad thing.' She winks hops up. 'Oh hey1 There's something really neat her I wanted to show you! I bet you'll find it interesting.' She holds out her hand to him. Ivan blinks, 'Okay..it had better not be some joke....' He rises slowly and takes her hand. 'Me? Joke? Whatever would give you that idea?' she asks and smiles a most devilish smile over her shoulder at him. Ivan mutters in Russian as she pulls him along, the camera fades away as Tempest skips ahead of Ivan, The Russian Bear's hulking figure pushing through droves of people....
