Jane. Jane Reagan, Owner of OSW, has summoned me back to her side. And like the dog I am, I'm there. Paris is waiting in the hotel room we got last night, on the outskirts of Winnepeg. I left her tangled in the sheets, my semen slowly drying on her skin as I showered and got dressed, trying as hard as I could to make myself presentable. Trying to act human, I guess. She's talking, not really noticing me, taking me for granted. Howard Porter and Ed nod in all the right places. Me, I tuned her out. It's all that I can do to keep from wincing from the sunlight filtering through the blinds. I don't belong here anymore. Jane hardly acknowleges my existence, barely giving me a glance, going over the corporate reports - kind of oddly, I might add, as though she was trying to make sense of them, trying to bullshit her way through the meeting. I don't even know why I'm here. What the fuck does she want with me anyway? Don't get me wrong, I don't mind the attention, but shit, come on! The way I see it, Jane was only interested in getting dick and consolidated control over the company with me being the OSW Champ. Now that I've gone down the tubes, what's my use here? And trust me, I looked at myself in the mirror - lately, I'm suprised I'm having sex with a psychotic woman. I guess they don't have real high standards. I look like shit. So now that I'm not OSW Champion, what does that make me to Jane? I'm sure there's something to this, but right now I can barely string together the letters 'H' and 'I'. Does she still want me? Somebody tell me what the fuck is going on. Corporate earnings, buyrates for Head On Collision, the Canadian Tour, blah, blah, blah. All I want to hear is why I'm supposed to be present. Through the haze, Howard Porter and Ed leave and I gravitate over to the leather sofa and sit down heavily, my head throbbing. Jane doesn't even look up at me. Through a parched throat, I say softly, Hey. She looks up from the reports and smiles politely. 'Yes, Sebastian?' How ya been, I ask. 'Oh, I'm feeling alot better now, Sebastian. But are you okie-dokie? You don't look so good. Have you been drinking enough water? You look dehydrated.' Now I know something is up. Normally I would be ordered to be tonguing her pussy until she came at least five times. You know, to relieve stress. Drink water, I mutter. Good idea. 'Glad I could help, hon!' she says cheerfully, and promptly goes back to going over her reports. I get to my feet. I don't feel like talking about this. Things are far too weird right now for me as it is. With Jane acting oddly, this just drops another turd on the All-You-Can-Eat Shit Platter I'm working on right now. And this thing with the Vax. Real or kayfabe? I don't know. All I know is that one minute Jane was in the hospital, next she's gone. Not that I was in the loop or anything. In fact, right now, I'm not sure what the hell I was doing. I guess that's a bad thing. Ugh. Everything is so blurry. I got to go and, uh, train for Saturday, I tell Jane. 'Mmmm, good job, sweetheart. I'm SOOOOO glad you're still on top of things ever since you dropped the strap. You know, most guys would be in a deep pit of despair, lamenting thier lot in life, and quite possibly contemplating suicide!' ... Did she really say that? 'Good, I'll see you around, Sebastian.' is what she says. Am I having hallucinations or something? She looks down at her paperwork and I excuse myself. I stumble down the halls of the arena where the OSW ring crew is setting up, my head down and a baseball cap covering my head. My body aches - seriously. Hips, neck, back, shoulder, knee... Even my scars ache, if that's possible. And pain, no matter what the Goth guys try to tell you, clarifies nothing except for what a jackass you are for getting hurt in the first place. Pain is not cool, pain is not romantic. Pain is a sensory signal to tell you that your body, the fragile thing that it is, is getting fucked up, and supposedly pain is supposed to make you stop from repeating the experience so you don't further damage yourself. So that means that athletes and wrestlers and moody Goth kids who cut themselves are all a bunch of idiots who don't even have the sense to listen to their own bodies. That implies a level of idiocy. And boy, do I feel like an idiot. Somehow, I amble into the parking lot. Why do they make the sun so fucking annoyingly bright? Can't they see that I'm on pain medication? Can't they see that I'm in fucking pain? That every inch of my body is hurting? Fucking sun. Ah, there's the Camaro. I need sleep. I need deep, dreamless sleep. Or better yet, a good dream for once. A dream where I don't end up getting screwed out of 25 bucks at the end of a show because I'm in some indy fed. A dream where I don't have to crawl back to CCW and beg Spider for my job back. Somehow, I make it back to the hotel. Hotels don't even have names anymore. All I do is show the PTC Credit Card and they swipe it and give me the key card, and I scribble something down on a piece of paper. Half the time, I'm so doped up on medication that I can barely stand up, let alone go over the itemized phone calls the hotel so helpfully shows when you're ready to start out on a 12 hour drive to the next fucking show and you're STILL doped up from the pain-killers. I've heard some stories where some hotel clerks charge a shitload of calls to the wrestler's rooms to wherever, fucking Madagascar for all I know, and the wrestler doesn't know shit because they're too fucked up to realize it. And I'm just talking about the guys on painkillers. Don't even get me started on the guys who drink and smoke shit. Those guys, hell, half the time they don't know whether they're coming or going, they're so fucked in the head. And trying to work a match with them? Bad enough you got half the roster looking like the walking dead because they're constantly in fucking pain, then you got the guys who mix the pain-killers with alcohol or other colorful pills. They fuck up moves, puke during house shows, hit ya real stiff because their spacial reasoning is all fucked up, whatever. And the trouble with the cops, shit, back before Head On Collision, the cops busted in on the locker room because that dippy bitch Campbell was talking about drugs during her promos, looking for the shit while we were getting dressed. Fucking Tom Walczak had a drug hound sniffing his fucking dick. What the fuck, you know? I move like a zombie to the room, and slip in the key card. Shambling in, Paris is still sleeping. Is she even real? A nymphomaniac who just happens to be hitch hiking along the very same road I'm driving on. Sounds like a bad Jeremy Howard promo or something. Let me sit down. There. And another thing. Half the promos that PTC guys do is something I like to call Celluloid Masturbation. Now, me, yeah, I do alot of uncalled for shit, but I never make myself out to be more than I really am - which is a jackass. But some of these people, when they get into character, thier promos are like bad teenage Creative Writing assignments. Take themselves way too seriously. Ivan, yeah, he's a little into himself, but he developed his character. And he's a great guy to work with, he carried our feud like the pro that he is. But Ace Hawk? Christ, can you say 'Power Fantasy'? Fuck, his promos are... fuck, I can barely stand to watch them. I didn't watch them in CCW, and I don't watch them now. Much. I mean, come on... This whole teacher thing? The only thing Hawk taught me was how CCW wanted me to take bumps. You know, you've all seen the camps, and just for the record, it's not called the CCW 'Power Plant'. That's a Ted Turner creation. Actually it's a contract awarded to a Toronto-based wrestling school called Mat-Matics, where guys like Urban and Johnny Lamen run a 'Refresher Course', where they take the Indy Workers and run them through the paces... Ugh, headache. Anyway, so I'm there last August, going through the motions, and Ace Hawk comes in, like it's some big fucking deal. He calls me out to the ring to do a bump, and he does this dippy little backdrop, and I take the bump. And he has the nerve, the unmitigated nerve, to tell me... 'You got a lot to learn, kid.' Ok, sure, smile and nod. But wasn't I, just a month later, taking German Suplexes onto trashcans full of baseball bats and other assorted hardcore weaponry? Oh yeah, I was. Chris Shaw, you were a good sport about that, even after I dented a fire extinguisher over your head. You told me you wanted to work stiff. Anyway, now Ed and Jane and everyone signs this guy. He embellishes the story and all of a sudden, I'm the 'Student' and he's the 'Teacher'. Over a backdrop bump? After he waltzes in, trying to look all impressive and shit? Christ, the things people will do. Then again, Jesse Ramey wore a dress. Just goes to show that there is no bottom to the lack of taste in this business. Fuck, I'm going to take another shower and go to bed. This is going to be a long week. -FIN-
