Inside The Mind of a Silent Beast

24 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 24, 2002 Journal Entry #1 OSW. Old School Wrestling. It used to be a name that was filled with respect, honor and dignity. With great sportsmen like Timo, like Lennox Lanthrop, like The Magi...but soon it became a mockery of this very sport. Why? Easy. The names like Ed. The names like Da Westsidaz. The names like Twisted Metal. The names like Sebastian Toomes. Those names came into the OSW, and destroyed all respect, honor and dignity it ever had. These beings made OSW a complete mockery filled with controversy and comedy...things that should NOT be in a wrestling promotion. And than...the saviors... The Think Tank. Myself, John Mammoth, Howard Porter, Alan Kriegman, PHD and Trashcan Man. We cleaned up Old School Wrestling. It first was Sebastian Toomes, a victim of MY own wrath. The first man to feel my power, the first man to feel my strength. What happened to him? He was destroyed! He was taken out of commission...and was thrown aside. I did all I could to harm him, to destroy him...and I did just that. Toomes was the first victim. Than came Ed. A man who named an object in a chair. What a fool, what a waste, what a pile of SHIT in OSW! What did we do? WE PUT THE SHIT IN THE TRASH! We all participated in taking apart Ed...everyone of us. We broke his mind, his spirit...and damn sure broke...his BODY. But it was Kriegman and Trashcan man...that broke...his LIFE. Ed was the second victim. Gold. That's what we saw in the hands of undeserved champions. Da Westsidaz. Short Circuit. Riptah. These men were complete MOCKERIES of OUR Old School Wrestling! Riptah and Short Circuit didn't deserve those Tag Team Championships...they were and ARE pieces of trash...so what did we, The Think Tank, do? We took out the fucking trash! Da Westsidaz were the third and fourth victims. And now, the most recent one...Twisted Metal. Scraps of metal placed together just to completely mock the sport of wrestling. This sport is a MAN'S sport...when the hell did Robots become apart of this? Well, that didn't last long. I stomped on every single scrap of metal that I could...until I saw smoke...and no more movement. I looked at the faces of my partners. Mammoth was silent during the assault...and TCM was just going off on them...I love that. I watched them as they spew out the rest of their oil and were placed in the compactor...at least they were recycled...they can do better there than anywhere else in my opinion. But the bottom line in all of this...anyone that has gotten in the way of The Think Tank...has suffered. Three of the victims are no longer with us, and the other three are in the hospital... No one is ever the same when they encounter us. And I'm just realizing that a new FRESH victim is coming MY way... Dave Gibson. I will destroy him. I will rip him limb from limb. I will break every single bone in his body. I will watch him as he spews out large amounts of blood. I will watch as I hear the snap of his body. And it'll just be another day... In the life... Of The Think Tank. , Armour
