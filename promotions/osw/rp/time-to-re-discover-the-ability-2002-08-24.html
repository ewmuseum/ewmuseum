Time to re-discover the ability?

24 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 24th, 2002 ((Note: This is kinda rushed...big night last night, had a semi-big day today with a car breakdown that landed me three hours away. Anywho, I really wanted to get this up before Slam to get a semi-point across. It's rushed, but here you have it.)) Amy was just stirring, so very early on in the morning... 8am, by the digital clock beside her. That was late enough for the sun to have risen as she'd soon realized, being forced to cover her face with her hands to avoid the vicious rays penetrating her retinas. When the stiff and general soreness of the redhead's lithe frame hit her it was like a ton of bricks, causing Amy to moan tiredly into her palms just before she dragged them down the rest of her face, rubbing her eyes and facing the sunlight as she did so. She extended her arms out and arched her entire body, stretching and rolling onto her back only to have her shoulder blade slump with a light, fleshy smack onto Reno's bare chest. Davis almost instantly rubbed his chest where Amy had landed to sooth the sting of the skin-on-skin impact. ''Well good morning to you too.'' He'd grinned a little, stretching an arm out over her as Campbell tried to make sense of the blur that had made up most of the previous evening. She hadn't realized how chilly it had gotten in the room overnight until Aimz found herself pushing against Reno's side and clutching sheets around her. From the feeling of things, at least she was still wearing the 'bikini'-style bathing suit she'd had on under her clothes to swim the night before...whether that had been on the whole night or put back on after some 'activity' was yet to be determined by Amy, though. When she ran a hand through her OWN naturally bright red hair, it was apparent that the weather only cooled very recently since her hair, which usually proved too thick to dry within even a matter of hours, was still damp with a mixture of the chlorine-treated pool water and sweat induced by the sheer humidity of the prior night's air. ''I need to...'' Amy began, an idea in her head, but she lost her grip on that train of thought. Reno held her tightly to him. For that, Campbell was thankful...a small gesture of comfort in a world that was filling with stress was welcomed with open arms. ''Hmm?'' She finally re-gained the thought and mumbled, ''I need to prepare.'' ''For what, that butch chick?'' ''Yeah...'' Her head felt as if it was still swimming around downstairs as she tried to voice the right words. ''...I've gotta......Jesus....I have to be ready.'' Davis tried to reassure his nervous girlfriend. ''You'll be ready.'' ''I'm gonna go back to my room....just give me some time alone, I've gotta think for a while.'' ''Uhhh...sure.'' Reno mumbled, staring oddly at Amy as she hopped up to her feet and swiftly walked out the door, not even bothering to put shoes on. It was Saturday morning...that's what she hadn't remembered. Amy Campbell was about to fight for her life and she was barely even prepared. Tonight she went into a battle she'd done no research on, it was all to be played by ear and heart...the desire to win was there, the ability to win was there. The problem was, that ability had been canned for almost a year... Was it time to bring out the 'old' Amy Campbell, the actual wrestler?
