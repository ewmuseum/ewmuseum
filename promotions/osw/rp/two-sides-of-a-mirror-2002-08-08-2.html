Two Sides of a Mirror

8 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 8, 2002 Have you ever gotten the sense of someone watching you? I have... all the time. My parents look down at me from heaven, God bless them. But this night... something was different. Jesus St Crux cracks open one eye. Sure he knew that these older hotel rooms would sometimes creek at night, but this time it seemed... different... ominous. The room isn't filled with much light, the moon in the sky is covered by a cloud, but his half-open eyes focus on something... a glint of light approaching. A split second later, Jesus' eyes are wide open as he stares face to face with the golden toothy grin of Frankie Bones. Worried, Jesus looks to Caridad, who thankfully is still sleeping beside him in bed. 'Don't worry, boy. I'm not here to hurt you.' Jesus looks confusedly back and forth between Caridad, asleep beside him, and Frankie, who's going on about... something. In his barely-awake state, Jesus isn't sure what the Brooklyn native is talking about. He pauses and takes a deep breath, then whispers harshly at Bones, 'Que passa? What are you doing here?' Frankie chuckles a bit. 'To check up you. That's all.' His voice sounds as if it could get louder at any minute. 'Are you angry?' 'Rompas mi cuarto! Miras me duermo! Si, estoy enfadado! Estoy muy enfadado!' Jesus restrains himself from yelling, not wanting to wake Caridad. Jesus has no idea if Frankie understood any of that, but the big man seems to smile at the Mexican's discomfort. 'Do you hate me?' 'Estas todo aquel esta equivocado con este deporte.' Jesus narrows his eyes. 'Si... si, chico... Odio tu, pendejo.' Frankie grins, and what little light is in the room gets reflected many times ooff his golden teeth. 'Good.' A pause. 'Bring all that anger... all that hate; you are going to need it.' Again, a pause. 'Bring all these things, and you'll have the best match of your career. Consider this a gift.' 'Pensas necesito algo que ganar? No, chico, tengo ya todo necesito.' Bones rises from his seat, chuckling slightly. 'I'll show myself the door.' He tosses one more evil grin at Jesus and Caridad then nonchalantly exits the room, pulling the door shut loudly. Caridad wakes with a start, and strokes her hand softly across the tense muscles of her boyfriend. 'Que passa, Guapo?' Jesus sits in bed, staring at the closed door for a few seconds. Slowly, he slips out of bed and walks to the door. As if with a great weight on his shoulders, he grips the doorknob and assures that it is locked. 'Jesus?' Caridad asks, her voice quavering a bit due to Jesus' lack of an answer. 'Nada, Dadi.' He sighs. 'Nada. Vas a dormir.' He smiles to comfort her as he slips back under the sheets. Caridad cuddles close to him and quickly falls back asleep, but Jesus stares at the ceiling for a while longer.
