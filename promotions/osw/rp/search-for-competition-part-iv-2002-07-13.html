Search for Competition: Part IV

13 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 13th, 2002 NOTE: This was supposed to be posted like, Sunday, but MS Word problems prevented it. Vampir roars out as Vendetta tightens the hold. The Cultist watches helplessly as Axtell tries to subdue him, and Gibson and Hessian barely stir from ringside. Vampir’s eyes roll into the back of his head as finally Gaston gets into the action. He checks Vampir over. Vendetta tightens the hold to its fullest. Vampir doesn’t show any signs of moving. Gaston lifts his arm. It drops once. The crowd are ecstatic as Vendetta sweats profusely. Gaston lifts Vamp’s arm again. It drops for a second time. The crowd are roaring the roof off of the arena. Gaston lifts Vamp’s arm again. It falls slowly, dropping…dropping…dropping…thunk! it hits the mat. The bell rings as Pierre Gaston raises Vendetta’s hands in victory to the roar of the crowd! Meanwhile, Da Westsidaz nod at each other, then get up and leave, as Clay Vendetta exits the ring and celebrates with Axtell, and the Cultist slides into the ring to check on Vampir. At ringside, Hessian is still out of it as Axtell and Vendetta pull Gibson to his feet and carry him to the back, to an ovation from the fans. Meanwhile Hessian stirs on the outside as Vampir is woken up by the Cultist, but the fans don’t care as they cheer and ovate for the real wrestlers, The Throwbacks. The scene fades into what appears to be backstage of SLAM! that very night. In fact, “Paradise City” by Guns n’ Roses is still playing in the background, leading us to believe this scene was shot right after the Throwbacks defeated the Cult for the No. 1 Contendership to the OSW Tag Team Championship. Many backstage hands are rifling through equipment backstage and rushing to different spots around the Gund Arena. As the music still plays loudly, the curtain backstage flies up, surprisingly revealing not the Throwbacks, but Da Westsidaz, who were present throughout the match at ringside. Riptah and Short Circuit have the Tag Titles wrapped around their waists as the walk through the backstage area, still in deep conversation. “SC, I’m telling you, that was one helluva match. I think we may have found our competition in the form of those two. I mean, look at them, they’re wrestlers! They ain’t going to be push-overs, I don’t care what you say. They’re style is a lot different then we’ve been with in a long time. They’re a good, technical team, something we haven’t mixed with in awhile. I’m pretty sure it’s going to go good next week.” Riptah smiles, finally happy with the thought of a good fight. Short Circuit, on the other hand, still has that bored, dismal look in his eyes. “Rip, listen to me. They are worried about the Cult! John and Clay won’t have their minds on the match and you know it! It’s going to be one of those nights where we just get half the attention from our opponents and easily put them away. It’s just going to be another walk in the park. Plus once Clay finds out what those sick Gothic freaks did to Ms. Weathers, I doubt he’ll even show up.” Short Circuit shakes his head in sadness, as the two walk into their own locker-room. Riptah jumps onto a couch and looks up at the television, barely paying attention to what is going on during SLAM! while Short Circuit digs through a cooler in the corner. Finally he finds what he was looking for and tosses Riptah a Budweiser, who cracks it open quickly and chugs it down. Short Circuit pulls up a chair, still thinking about the match next week against the Throwbacks. “That don’t mean we can take it easy on them though… A distracted Gibson & Vendetta still isn’t as easy as Twisted Metal. We hafta come prepared, otherwise we might get caught majorly off guard.” Short Circuit opens his beer, still pondering what to expect Saturday. “I know we’ll retain easily, but I got a date after the show. I don’t want to get messed up or anything.” Riptah laughs sarcastically, then peers at his partner. “Dawg, first we go around saying that we want competition… then when the competition finally comes around, you hope it’s easy? Man, what’s your problem?” “I’m just foolin’ around. Of course I want a fight. That’s what we’re all about. I’m sick of this, we’re the goddamn champs, dammit! Does management not think we’re ready for competition? Do they not think we’re capable of winning against a real team?” “SC, chill. We proved we’re ready when we beat the hottest team according to everyone, Civil Disobedience, and we destroyed most destructive force in the Old School, Heresian Bloodlust. Now I’m sure once we show the Throwbacks why Da Westside is Da Bestside, someone will step the fuck up.”
