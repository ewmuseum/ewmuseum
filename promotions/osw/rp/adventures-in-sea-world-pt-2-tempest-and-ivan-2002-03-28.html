Adventures in Sea World, pt. 2 (Tempest and Ivan)

28 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/28/02 Tempest giggled and said, 'There! Isn't that better?' She shivered a little, but smiled none the less brightly as they stood in the indoor arctic wing of Seaworld. Ivan looked around with his hands on his hips and nodded, 'Yes, this is much better, much more seasonal, if Ivan may say so himself.' He looked at Tempest, his breath visibly exiting his mouth as he breathes. Tempest smiled and walked along beside him as they neared a seal enclosure. 'I thought you would approve. I've never been to Russia though. What's it like?' she asked. Ivan smiled warmly and looked at Tempest proudly, 'The Motherland is a beautiful place, Tempest. Ranging from mighty mountains to lowlands. It is indeed very cold there at sometimes, when it can get to as cold as forty degrees below zero, but that is during winter months.' He grinned and looked at a seal, unimpressed, 'It takes special type of person to live in the area, but it builds much character, it is something to be quite proud of. You should visit sometime at Arkhangelsk, it is beautiful country.' Ivan grinned to himself, enthralled at his words. Tempest chuckled and grinned at the seal as it rotated it's head and chirped at her. 'Ya know, if you really wanna do your people some good, you should go into tourism,' she said, patting Ivan's arm. 'You make it sounds great even though I'm sure I'd freeze myself.' Ivan smiled and nervously put an arm around Tempest, 'Do not worry, Little Storm, Ivan would make sure you do not freeze to death.' Ivan blinked, 'But...tourism is not something for Russia. It is not some place to visit... to understand and respect it, you must live there...' Tempest chuckled and said, 'Well how would anyone know if they wanted to live there without a visit? And I dunno, I don't stay in one place too long. I always feel like there's something else I should be seeing.' She nodded toward the Narwhal tank, where two of the massive beasts raised their heads, lifting their single horns from the water. 'Sorta like those guys, I guess I'm migratory.' Ivan chuckled, 'They are not migratory, Tempest, they are captives.' He paused and squinted, looking down at her, 'How can you live such random life, Tempest? Without any...stability?' Tempest chuckled and said, 'I meant in the wild. I dunno... I don't feel unstable.' She caught herself and rolled her eyes as she said, 'Ok, maybe I'm unstable, but I don't really feel like my life is. No, unstable was when I didn't know where my next mean was coming from, now the randomness is fun! I mean I can't stand to be bored.' Ivan growled to himself and digested what she said, 'Do you ever...relax? Do something... quiet for recreation?' A huge humpback whale swam up to the glass and stared at Stanislav, one of the few things ever seen to dwarf The Russian Bear. 'Sure! This is relaxing isn't it?' she said with a smile and watched the huge whale swim by with awe. 'But relaxation isn't my strong suit. I only sleep about four hours a night. That's how you can tell if I'm hurt; I sleep more.' Ivan swallowed and furrowed his brow, 'Why only so little?' Tempest laughed and said, 'I've always been that way. I didn't know other people slept more til a few years ago. I just be still that long... Ooo look!' Tempest's eyes lit up and she dashed toward a small open enclosure. Ivan looked where Tempest pointed and narrowed his eyes as a small penguin waddled out from a little igloo atop some ice on the water, Ivan widened his eyes, growled, and roared, pointing at the penguin, 'PENGUIN!!' Tempest jumped, surprised at the outburst and said, 'Gee Ivan I didn't know you were a penguin fan. Aren't they cute?' She stepped over and leaned down toward one as it raised it's beak and honked at her curiously. 'RRRAAARRRGGHHH!!' The loud roar of Stanislav echoed through the area as he stomped over to the glass and slammed his forearm into the glass, sending a small crack up the center. Ivan roared again as the penguin scuttled away on the ice and several other penguins waddled out to see the commotion. 'DAMN YOU!!' Stanislav punched at the glass again, breaking it further. 'Ivan!' Tempest squawked. 'What are you doing?' 'They are evil! Vile creatures!!' Ivan roared again and slammed his massive fist into the glass, breaking through it! He began to tear down the glass wall as the penguins blinked at him. People scattered. Ivan pulled himself up onto the ice and took one massive step on the precarious floor. He slipped and fell forward, sliding past the penguins and hitting the back wall with a tremendous amount of force. The penguins honked and slid around on their bellies all around Ivan as he roared in pain. Tempest stepped carefully through the fractured glass and tiptoed across the ice, careful not to fall. She reached Ivan and said with an air of astonishment, 'Ivan... what in the world is wrong with you? They're just funny little penguins. I mean look at them, they slide around and waddle and honk. What could be funnier?' She laughed as one waddled up to her and poked at her jeans curiously. Ivan stood up slowly, his feet very wide from one another as the penguin poked at Tempest, he yelled again, his voice echoing around the area, 'DO NOT TOUCH HER!!' He brought his arm high over his head and swung down in a hammer like motion, barely missing the penguin and jammed his hand through the ice and into the water, lodging it there. A crack formed on the ice as it began to give away. Ivan roared and pulled, but was unable to free his hand. 'Uh oh...' Tempest said warily. 'Ivan, I don't think you better do that.' She stepped closer carefully trying to avoid the crack in the ice. 'Lemme try...' She reached forward with a quick punch, knocking off a jagged tip of ice wedging his hand. 'There, that should do it.' The penguin watched and honked at her, sounding irritated. Ivan pulled his hand from the ice and growled at the penguin as workers flock outside of the tank. Ivan growled at the penguin, 'BE GONE YOU!!' Ivan bellowed loudly and slipped again, falling hard on his back. Tempest tried to suppress a chuckle and failed as she said, 'Ivan, why are you mad at the poor penguins?' She shook her head, with a mystified smile and petted the one that had waddled closer to her for a better look. It tilted its head toward her and rubbed against her hand. Ivan stood halfway up and slipped, falling on his stomach, several little cracks formed underneath his massive body as he struggled more, 'They have been a bane of Stanislav, constantly interfering in matches and causing Ivan to lose matches!' 'Lose matches?' Tempest asked and looked at the hapless penguin next to her. 'These little guys? You're kidding right? You're playing a joke on me now.' She grinned happily at the thought. Ivan growled between gritted teeth and pointed at the penguin, 'I will show you later, footage of these vile creatures costing me matches! Until then...KILL IT!!' Ivan roared and leapt at the penguin. In a sudden explosion of ice and water, Stanislav collapsed through the ice and disappeared underneath the dark, murky water! People around the area scream as The Russian Bear plummeted below the subzero water. The penguin scuttled to the other side of Tempest and honked. 'Ivan!' Tempest squealed, suddenly terrified. She looked around frantically, trying to figure out where he was. Then she leapt over to the edge of the enclosure, where there was solid concrete next to the ice. She took a deep breath and kicked downward making a hole in the ice. 'C'mon Ivan over here,' she said nervously, hoping he'd seen it. The entire expanse of ice shook from an area nearby as a large pale fist slammed into the ice in an attempt to break it. Slowly, through the ice, a massive form is seen literally walking on the ground towards the hole created by Tempest. Suddenly, in a rush of water and breath, Stanislav burst through the hole, making it many times larger by his frame, and fell to the ground, ice on his beard, his pale skin slightly blue. 'DYAAAAAA!!!' Tempest hopped over and crouched next to him, rubbing his shoulder. 'Ivan? Are you ok?' Ivan shook his head, sending shards of ice flying from his beard and shivered, sitting up slowly. 'Damned P..P...Penguins..' He shivered again as the penguins flitted around the ice happily. 'Awww,' Tempest said, struggling to hide a snicker. She wrapped her arms around him and said, 'Maybe we should head back out into that sunny afternoon after all?' 'I..I..I.......I...I am inclined t...t..to....a...ag....agree....Tempest.' Ivan slowly rose to his feet, his suspenders pulling down extremely far from the extra weight of his pants while water drained from around his boots. Tempest chuckled and stepped through the broken glass, as the crowds stepped back and said, 'I'm sure it's not too big a deal, hmm?' She nodded to what looked like a security guard, who stood frozen, staring at the huge Russian as he climbed back out onto dry land. Tempest cleared her throat to get his attention and the security guard just shook his head in a rapid movement. Ivan shook his head again and trudged through the area with Tempest until they exited the building and walked into the bright sunlight again. 'Do you see... those penguins will be death of me one day!' muttered Ivan between soggy footsteps. Tempest patted his arm and said, 'I don't know how you managed to have such a strange thing about penguins. But see? It's never dull with you around.' She smiled up at him affectionately. Ivan amazingly chuckled, after all that he had gone through, 'I believe perhaps you bring a part out of me that few ever see, Little Storm.' He thought for a moment, realizing that perhaps he said something very profound, and then looked back at her again, 'I believe it may be a combined fact between the two of us.' Tempest laughed and walked backwards in front of him as she said, 'Who me? Aw, I dunno, I just be myself. But who knows, maybe I'm just an example of what you can get away with.' She winked and looked over her shoulder, then turned to a large shallow pool in which several dolphins and a few people were swimming. 'Ooo! I been looking forward to this!' Ivan rubbed the side of his head, 'What in Lenin's name is it?' Tempest grinned and casually peeled off her shirt revealing a purple bikini top as she said, 'They let people swim with the dolphins! You dry out and I'll just hop in and get myself into the same predicament.' Ivan blinked as Tempest took off her top, stunned as if hit with the hardest haymaker ever, 'Uh..well...uh....' He blinked again and shook his head and then suddenly blushed violently. Tempest either failed to notice, or refused to notice as she unzipped her jeans and pulled them off. 'Rats, I forgot to bring a towel. Oh well, we'll both have to air dry I guess,' she said and shrugged with an innocent chuckle. Ivan raised an eyebrow as a suspender suddenly snapped. He yelped and grabbed the strap and fixed it, 'Uh..yes...I uh...I suppose...uh..so....yes....da.' Ivan continued to fool with the suspender, muttering, 'Yes...uh huh...yes!' He looked up again and blinked again and rubbed the back of his neck, 'Ivan will uh..just uh..stay and uh...' Ivan swallowed hard, a huge lump in his throat, '...watch.' Tempest smiled gleefully and leaned over, kissing his cheek lightly. 'I'll be right back.' She turned to the pool and ran forward into a graceful dive and came up near a dolphin that chittered at her as she laughed. Ivan nodded and took a seat on a bench as he watched the lithe body of Tempest dive into the water, 'Da...I will watch out for you Tempest...da.' As he sat various people pointed at him, but he ignored them, children ran, but he paid them no heed, he just continued to watch Tempest in the water with the dolphin, laughing, splashing, and thoroughly enjoying herself. He sat back on the bench and crossed his arms as the laughter of Tempest rang through the area, and smiled.
