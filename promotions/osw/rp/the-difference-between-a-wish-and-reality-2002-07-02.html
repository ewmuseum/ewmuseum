The Difference Between A Wish and Reality

2 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07.02.2002 Why? A word that I have heard since I arrived here in OSW during the ever evolving month of May. Just as the flowers bloomed, my character began to take shape and as I began to appear in front of millions ... the so-called character started to slowly eat away at what I was before. Before it all.... I've often been asked if I was born with a clear picture of myself in my head and if I had always been fond of that image as I am today. I cannot answer either question. For how can I see a perfect view of myself ... when I don't know who I am? Often torn between the poetic side of Cory Rutherford and the sadistic side of Kory Storm, I constantly find myself waking up at night in a cold sweat, stuck somewhere in the middle. I've often found myself praying to a God which I never thought existed, wishing and hoping that I could one day stumble upon my true being. With this, I could be born again, and also express true feelings. No one would approach me and ask me why anymore, for there would always be a motive. I would never act out of anything aside from something that I know is completely real. Yet, my wishes have yet to come true. Thinking back and looking at my past, I see how every thought has shaped me, yet I simply end up asking myself what my past has shaped. Am I an athlete? Strong hearted and willed ... ready for anything? Yes. Am I a poetic soul ... searching for any outlet to express my tortured thoughts? Yes. Am I a person who feeds off the pain of others, just like my own Mother fed off of my pain when she destroyed my family? Yes. Why? I don't fucking know. In my life I have found something which just leads to something else. I have seeked guidance from individuals and I have followed their teachings. Coming to one distinct conclusion is virtually impossible, for life and one's self is like a painting. It can be interpreted only in the eye of the beholder. In my eyes, I see the painting which is drenching in many shades of red. Blood? Yes. The blood of my grandmother, spilt in the wake of my mother's greed, the only person I ever loved. The blood of Jim Axtell, left layered in numerous cities all around the United States by the man I now know not what to do with, which proved to be a lacking effort on my part. What was his blood worth now? He stands for everything I despise ... yet I still couldn't put him away. He now holds my victory over my head, taking it for his own. Is the reign of Jim Axtell a part of me? I should hope not. For I have never enjoyed another person personifying what I always knew, yet maybe I need his help in a twisted way. Axtell can guide me to my weakness, just by exposing his own. In loss, all of these questions have come to me, for loss is something I've dealt with one time too many. I've seen things of importance swivel down the proverbial drain of life, and I've seen it splash to the ground at my knees. Is my life which I have not yet begun to figure out a product of the person I've never really known? I still don't fucking know. So rather than sit and portray a person which was created by heartless bastards and corporate pigs, I'll weep as a shell of Kory Storm. However, I cannot stay like this. I must pull myself up. Forget the death! Forget the loss! Forget the fucking questions! Why? Victory. I have one chance to redeem myself, and it is a chance which also falls on the shoulders of another man ... a madman. Frankie Bones. Yet another piece of filth which I never wanted to interact with, unless it involved my fist and his gold filled mouth. However, in this state, I see his sadism and I see a dark shade of myself within his tough exterior. With him on my side, in an alliance which I can only imagine reasoning for, we take on someone I know well ... the man who might be a piece to my puzzle. Jim Axtell. For the people who hold my fate on a string, however, what I give in my matches with Axtell can never be good enough. They have to stack a deck on me which is already about to collapse. The new card which is in the opposing hand is a man who goes by Jesus St. Crux, a cross between a biblical icon and a petty thug. He entered the shame filled circle of the life which I have no control over, and now alongside Frankie, something sick inside of my stomach tells me he will pay. For I can't lose again! I can't expose myself to be weak, when the one thing I am sure of is that I am strong. Should I lose, it all goes to hell and I'm back at square one ... running away. Running away just as I was after the smoking gun and dead bodies were left at my estate. Running away just as I was when management wouldn't hire me during the addiction. Even during those tough times, there were things that made me. Good or bad ... those characteristics were Kory Storm. Once again, I have no characteristics. Why? Because my wishes to be someone have never been answered. Instead I have been hit over the head with reality. I see my faults rather than exposing the faults of others. I see my future downfall and whose very hand its outcome is in. However, I cannot devote my attention to something I can't change without a key thing. Victory. In Tulsa, Oklahoma ... I may just find a piece of myself. Hopefully it doesn't end in another piece lost. For as the people and my own inner self continue to eat away at who I am, I will no longer have anything left to be 'Perfection'. I'll be common, just like every other parasite who walks these hallowed grounds. I won't be able to win, for winning would no longer be an option. Everything I've ever known will no longer matter. Michel Krieg. Ivan Stanislav. Jim Axtell. They won't matter. For if I end up not doing what I came to OSW to do, win, there cannot be a Kory Storm in OSW. There will just be the burial of what I used to be. In death, I will have nothing left to develop a soul from. My memory will just be a single grain of grass in life's field. So hate me while you can. If I cannot bring justice to myself and draw a win from St. Crux and Jim Axtell ... there may be nothing else about me to hate. God ... Reality Sucks
