The road to OSW part 1

March 23, 2002... Slowly, Reno Davis pushed his fatigued and mangled body up off the plain blue mat and eyed over at his opponent who lay on his back breathing heavily. Mikey ‘Freight’ Thomas was a bitch to try and throw around, standing at 7’4 and weighing in at a massive 540 pounds. Reno got in a lucky knee clip and took him down for a few moments of rest. Up onto his feet, his knee felt as if it were separated from his shins. This injury, caused by Azrael Ravenell in Reno’s final jOlt Wrestling match, was only mildly preserved due to his knee brace hidden under his jeans. Reno’s left leg, however, was fine. Freight looked about ready to get back up after recovering his bearings. better act fast... Reno jumped for the ropes and bounced off in a sitting position, landing knee first on Freight’s chest. A mere groan let out of the mighty man for Reno’s efforts. He was far too big to slap the Straightjacket on; so aerial maneuvers were his best bet. A meaty hand clenched his right ankle. Bad leg...shit Freight pulled Reno right off his feet and then lifted his mighty frame to his feet, still holding the ankle. Up Reno went, until Freight caught his right wrist and slammed Reno onto his back. The small crowd cheered as loudly as they could when Freight lifted Reno up onto his shoulder, back arched dangerously, and then placed his hands on Reno’s left shoulder and under his arse. With that he threw Reno down with an inhuman force. The Power-Driver. Freight placed one size 18 boot over Reno’s small chest and waited for the three count to end before claiming his Hardcore Title and smiling an emense smile for the crowd. *** “M-Mister Davis?” Reno took his head out of his palms and looked at the massive frame peeking from beside a locker right beside the entrance. “Come in Mikey...” Freight was a nickname given to the friendly giant after he assured management that they would call him Mikey. He agreed and went on with his life...Or what you could call a life. The huge black man ducked as he entered the miniscule door and took a seat on a bench beside Reno. It bent in the middle where he sat. “Are you okay? I didn’t mean to hurt your knee.” Mikey pointed at Reno’s knee and gave a childish look to show that he knew he did something wrong. Mikey was huge, and scary looking, yet he was the most thoughtful and friendly person Reno was yet to meet. ZaC popped out from beside Reno, not nearly as energetic as he was one year before. With a bit of effort, the small brown monkey leaped into Freight’s arms and screeched in ecstasy as Freight scratched behind his ears with two pipes like fingers. Freight was immediately in his own world. “It’s alright Mikey. I’ll be fine after some stretching. Just...be more careful on the next show okay?” Reno had been traveling with a small Indy organization known as Real Action Wrestling. It traveled around New Brunswick sometimes, but usually just stuck around Moncton and Miramichi. Reno got to meet some old big time stars such as Kurrgan and a few guys who were lucky to have dark matches in the WWF. Reno had some pretty good matches so far and brought in a little extra revenue for the company. Being in a few big promotions does wonders for that, but not being successful in every one of them just kicks your fan base back down to the ground. Suddenly Mikey realized that he had something else to ask and looked up eagerly at Reno. He caught Mikey’s glare. “You don’t have cab money again do you? Ya gotta stop forgetting it at home okay?” “No. I brought my cab money, see.” He showed Reno the few bills and change in his hand and stuffed it back into a pocket, “But...um. Can you walk outside and wait with me? The men are back.” “Shit..” Reno sighed and spoke at the same time. Mikey had become one of Reno’s companions, one of the few ever. Sometimes Mikey forgot how big he was and got scared easily. Mikey was exactly like a child, never putting much more thought into things than needed to understand. Staying away from politics and news. He was intelligent, a math genius truthfully, yet he was exactly like a child. The people, were like the ones who tormented Reno his whole life due to his looks. Except Mikey got it worse. Reno might have been made fun of for being small and having weird eyes and hair, but Freight is so huge that people fear and avoid him at any cost. Which is why he had to be a freak show in a wrestling promotion. However one thing that Reno never had to cope with, was being black. “Please Mr. Davis? I went outside the back like you told me but they were there and they called me...the word.” “You can’t be scared of them forever Mikey. You know that.” He nodded at Reno’s remark but still persisted. “Alright big guy...” Reno knew that he couldn’t bear to let anything happen to Mikey. Sometimes the racists carry weapons. Reno shuddered at the thought and stood up, now at eye level with Mikey who still sat. “Did you already call for the cab?” Mikey nodded and stood up, crouched over a bit so he didn’t bang his head on the ceiling. Reno picked up his bags and motioned for ZaC to climb onto his shoulder. “Get your stuff dude.”
