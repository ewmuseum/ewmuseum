Rational? No...

2 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Thursday, March 2, 2002 Dear Angeldust, I hope you realize that this letter is brutally honest, for your sake. I've been a fan of yours for many years now, and I've followed your career basically from the beginning, since I grew up in the Midwest. I was there for your first match with the The Now-Deceased 'Screwy' Louie Clay. I watched you beat The Street Thugs in handicapped matches, I watched you in MWW's first cage match, ladder match, and cell match. I know you probably don't think much of that, but I want to express my disgust with you. You used to have heart in the ring. I remember watching you against madBLACK at the PPV's all the time, you two would go on for an hour before even thinking about stopping. You knew you were the show, and you acted like it. The piledriver through the Camery was something that will never be forgotten in wrestling history. And I don't think I've ever seen someone get elbow dropped through a high school stage before, at that house show back in '96. You were everyone's all-time favorite, for more reasons than wrestling. You stayed after that house show for an hour and a half and with Jerry Lupow gave autographs to everyone who wanted them. You even held a conversation with most people, taking pictures when they wanted to, you even had me in a headlock to show to my friends. You were always bright and smiling, even if you had just fought your way through the gauntlet or whatever the ownership had thrown in front of you. You were my role model, Angeldust. But ever since you came of REALM, you changed. Suddenly, you are half assing it in the ring now. You were never even trying to keep up with ERA, you just carried the match technically like you do now. It was a fresh start for you, you could have been the World Champion on many different occaisions, but did you ever win anything worthwhile? No, you got selfish and greedy. You were the highest paid member of REALM's roster and you didn't even have the common decency to get out there and bust your ass night in and night out. I watched your last match with Calamity; two minutes, could you have spared it? Now, we present to you Old School Wrestling Nick Casey. You're no longer that clean cut All-American Track Runner and Wrestler Nick Casey, now you're just like every other punk in the business. You're wasting your life in the low card fighting chumps like Epitome of Evil. Let me tell you lucky you are to have Jack Miller to fight now. You were this great man who overcame adversity to set records and live the dream of many people, and now you're squandering it over nothing. What is it Nick? I've started to lose faith in you. You won at Head-On Collision, yes, but what else have you really done? You didn't even get the clean win at the Pay-Per-View, you had to get help from Miller. This is not the path I wanted to see your career take, when you started out with so much potential. You have a clean slate here, Nick, you have to work to keep yourself alive in the spotlights now. For now, you've lost me as a fan. Sincerely, Michael Norton *** 'Nick, what's wrong?' Angeldust had just started to crumple up the letter, as he sat in front of the burning fire in his cabin. Christy stared from out in the kitchen, as Nick just stared mindlessly into the fire, watching his dreams and fans fade away as the smoke rose from the blaze. It had been a rough week for him, not really getting over the fact that he had not cleanly beaten EoE. 'Just hate mail, Christy.' 'Are you okay, hun?' Nick sighed. 'Don't worry about it. I'll just kill him Saturday.' Unfortunately, Christy was already gone for the last part. Rationalism? Not here.
