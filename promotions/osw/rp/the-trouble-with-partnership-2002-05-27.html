The Trouble With Partnership

27 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05.27.2002 Don't ask me why I'm so angry, Or why I'm so disturbed, Unless you've got a couple, Of good hours, To hear of my troubling words, You talk of truth, Honor and respect, You talk of living, But you haven't lived, Seems to me, You're always on the take, That's because, You've got nothing to give, Seems to me, You've got a couple, Of your wires crossed, You're in the right place, At the wrong time, You don't seem, To be bothering, Anybody else now, But you're sure, Wasting my time, Don't ask me why, I will not pretend, Just walk on by, Because today I'm no man's friend, - 'No Man's Friend' by Mike Ness ][To many people, life is just like a game of chess. You have your pawns, or the manipulated people. You have your knights, those who volunteer to protect others in hopes of being heroes, but in the end they find that they've died for people who really didn't care about them to begin with. Then you have your kings, the most valuable players ... those people which the game revolves around. When the king fails, so do the others. And win the king wins, he shares his glory with only himself. Which means that unless you are in control of your life and you are a king, that you will lose no matter what. However, some players form alliances with those who are stronger in hopes of sharing that power which pulses through the powerful. But when two kings align together for the purpose of only themselves and try to coexist, it all implodes. For our subject, who sits alone, these sentiments need to be carefully gone over. For he now goes into battle with not only himself and his enemy, but Griff Truxell and his enemy. The stakes have been doubled and as Kory Storm sits smoking his signature cigarette, his brain seems to be viewing actions of Truxell over and over again. Is this worth it? Will defeating Jim Axtell bring Kory Storm into heat with Clay Vendetta because it would be a defeat for 'The Classic One' as well? And the most important question yet ... does Kory Storm care? It's doubtful. For he adores the darkside of the game they call life, inflicting pain on anyone who crosses him. Certainly he wants to bloody Jim Axtell more, only because the security guard has crossed him once before and made it out walking. But Clay Vendetta isn't exactly the greatest ally of 'Perfection Personified' either. So our subject has come to one conclusion. He will destroy everyone who opposes him and make sure that he does the one thing he has come to Old School Wrestling to do. Win. Be it Jim Axtell. Be it Clay Vendetta. Be it Griff Truxell. No one will stop Kory Storm.][ 'Hey buddy.' Speaks a smiling man behind a marble counter, washing glasses with a small, white rag as the man in front of him just stares. ][Obviously this is a bar and the man, who reminds Kory Storm faintly of the always polite and jovial Jim Axtell, is the tender. Kory Storm blows a mouthful of smoke directly at the man and then smirks, not caring for a minute about what this man thinks about the man who dubbed himself 'Perfect'.][ 'I'm not your buddy.' Kory answers back with a certain amount of superiority in his voice. 'Far from it, actually. I am your hero and you are my servant, understood? Now do something besides try to be my friend and get me a shot of whisky.' ][Well Kory had done it now. The bartender slams down the glass he was cleaning and walked away to a cabinet of bottles, no doubt thinking about spitting in the drink of Kory Storm. Sliding the drink down the counter to Storm, and not returning to stand and gawk at the OSW superstar, opting to speak to a woman sitting at the bar. As Kory Storm takes a sip of his drink, a barge of blue-clad men bursts through the door of the bar, shouting and hooting, no doubt happy about a victory of some kind.][ 'Fucking Canadians...' mutters Kory Storm, 'no respect for other people. Jumping around and causing loudness, not giving any quiet to the only important person in this cesspool. Me.' ][As the men in blue sit down in a booth, the door to the sports bar slowly opens and yet another guy in a blue uniform strolls in, looking a lot more vindictive and a whole less happier than the others. Sitting down next to Kory Storm but ignoring his utter existance, the kid begins to babble in an angry tone.][ 'It's always Johnny this and Johnny that!' The angry man says to himself. 'He's the savior to our team according to Coach Sonski. But what about me?! What about Dave Anderson?! I made the play that saved our game today, but Johnny still gets all the attention. I'll give him his...' ][Kory Storm has never been the type to feel remorse for anyone ... let alone some ignorant northerner, but he listens anyway, almost as if hearing some hidden meaning in between this guy's, who Kory supposes is Dave Anderson himself, words. Suddenly, a cackle from the table full of Dave's teammates erupts and a large, muscular guy stands up and shouts to him.][ 'Hey Dave!' Shouts the stronger looking player. 'Would you mind getting us a few brews?' 'Anything for you Johnny!' Dave yells back, managing a fake smile. ][Johnny winks and sits down again, allowing Dave to continue the berating of himself.][ 'Fuck him.' Mutters Dave. ][Kory seems to have an awakening and upon Dave saying that, he reaches into his pocket and pulls out a Canadian five dollar bill. He gets one last smell of cheap beer and buffalo wings before walking out of the bar and taking a seat on the wooden deck in front of the door.][ 'Is that a preview of my match Saturday?' Kory Storm asks himself. 'Because there they were, partners together on one team, turning their backs on each other. Will myself and Griff Truxell be subjected to the same fate? No. I won't allow it to happen. Because in his head, the man they call 'Wrestling Itself' knows exactly what I can do. He knows that right when I'm done with Axtell, and when I've taken care of his problem by the name of Clay Vendetta, that if he even shoots a wrong look in my direction that he'll be my victim rather than my partner. Because I'm not afraid of making friends, just not those who are strong. You see, I could go and lead a man like Jim Axtell down the road to hatred and pain, only because he is too naive to reject me. But Truxell is strong. Bullheaded yes. But strong. He knows what he wants and that is the head of Clay Vendetta. He didn't want to help me with Axtell for that isn't in his nature. And I didn't need his help only because I've held Jim Axtell's card in my hands since I met him. So we're similar in the ideals of our minds and souls, but we're different in the way we carry out our business. Griff Truxell has gotten under skin simply because of his high pay and special treatment. I'm under the skin of the OSW faithful because they know that I am real. I'm not telling them that I dominate for them, because I could give two shits about them. They fear me because I am the savior to OSW and because I will soon be the only name left ... whether it's a name they despise or not. I don't lie, as Axtell and Vendetta will find out this weekend. For I will stand victorious and they will lay beneath my feet bleeding like the pigs they are. And why? Because I am the icon ... the superstar ... the paragon! I am 'Perfection Personified' Kory Storm!!' ][So he's told us all his plans. He'll take down Axtell. He'll take down Vendetta. But for the sake of his victory, we hope that he doesn't take down his own partner. And as these ideas pace through the head of Kory Storm, a shrill scream is heard from the inside of Touchdown's Sports Bar. David Anderson has extracted his revenge. A sadistic smile comes across the face of Kory Storm as he throws his still burning cigarette to the ground, walking away just like he walked away from his real home and dead family? Will he win and then hate Griff Truxell for stealing the glory of Kory Storm's victory, destroying the 'Great' Truxell in the process? Only time will tell....][
