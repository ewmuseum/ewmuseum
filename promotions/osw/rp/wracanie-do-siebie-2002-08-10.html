Wracanie do siebie...

10 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/10/02 We see Walczak sipping some coffee in a bagel shop while holding some sort of periodical in his hand. He turns a couple of pages until he gets to the article entitled “In the Know”. The article, written by OSW announcer Nate Dumas, always intrigues Walczak, but a couple sentences through the article Walczak slams the paper as well as his coffee into the table. As the glass from the mug shatters everyone in the shop stops to look at Walczak. “Who does this asshole think he is? Saying that I can get fired. I am sick of this shit. Week in and week out I get this rap of being a bad Champion. What can they say that they have done? Dumas, is some ex-two bit pussy wrestler, and he has the balls to question my greatness. On jest pieronski skurwy sin, co nie ma nic do robienja zmojimi sprawy.” Walczak tries to calm down as a waitress comes over to clean the spilled coffee. He continues to mutter some phrases in Polish as the lady finishes cleaning up. “What must I do to silence the critics? I have already won the World Heavyweight Title, and in doing so have stepped out of the shadow of Ivan Stanislav.” Walczak leaves some money on the table and leaves the bagel shop taking the paper with him. He walks away from the shop with an angered look visible upon his face. “Dumas, you can make all of the comments that you please, but remember one thing. Think about whom you are making them about. Ja bede paczyc na ciebie Dumas, I jak ty otworzysz twoja buzy w zlym humorze to ja niewiem co ja zrobie z toba. Moze zbije jak sobake, moze udusze ciebie do smierci. Kto wie, nie ja nie ty ale wszycy zobacza. Jescze Polska nie zginela!!!” Walczak walks away and the scene fades out…
