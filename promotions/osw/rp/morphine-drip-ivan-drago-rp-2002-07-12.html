Morphine Drip (Ivan/Drago RP)

12 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Friday July 12, 2002 You think things will just get better? That doesn’t happen… Well maybe it does… God’s solution for pain… Morphine. We return to the hospital room that for the moment houses two of OSW’s injured superstars, Ivan Stanislav and El Drago. At this point in time Ivan is still nursing his rebroken rib. Remarkably, The Russian Bear managed to heal quite quickly throughout the past few days, but fear of internal bleeding keeps him confined to his bed. And then there is El Drago, just now has his lung started to return to normal but he is still hooked up to an oxygen mask. Inside is shoulder is still a mess and surgery will likely be required before he can return to the ring, and yet one thing makes everything okay for these two former enemies... Morphine. Breaking the silence of the heart monitors comes the loud, thunderous voice of The Russian Bear, 'DYAAHAAHAA!!! Ivan cannot feel his legs!! DYAAHAAHAA!!' A nurse comes rushing into the room to the enjoyment of El Drago. As she rushes over to the Russian Bear and bends over to check on him he takes full advantage of checking out her 'assets.' 'Are you okay Mr. Stanislav?' She says in a claming voice and trying to sooth the beast with her touch. Stanislav lurches forward, almost headbutting her with his huge skull, 'It is Pra...Pra....Prap.....,' he blinks once, 'Praporschik Stanislav!! NOT MISTER!!' The nurse nods to Ivan, realizing that he's better than she thought, 'Praporschik..I'm sorry.' Ivan nods, 'DYAAHAA!! No need to be sorry little girl...Ivan should be sorry!!' The nurse blinks, confused by Ivan's strange behavior, 'Why should..uh..you be sorry?' Ivan bursts into laughter, 'I DONT KNOW!! DYAAHAAHAA!!!!' Stanislav roars and falls back into his bed, shaking it violently under his weight, 'Let us both be sorry, then..' CRASH!!! The two turn their attention to the Mexican Dragon as he lies on his back looking up the nurse’s skirt after falling out of his bed trying to sneak a peak. 'Oh Mr. Drago are you alright?' She says as she rushes over him and untangles the oxygen mask. As she stands up to put it back near the bed Drago takes an obvious look at her good and then over at Ivan giving him a 'thumbs up' before the two burst into laughter. Stanislav laughs loudly and tries to get out of his bed, 'My dear.....tell me....' The nurse yelps and runs over to Stanislav, 'Please Mis...er..Praporschik! You need to lay down...' But Stanislav continues to barrel over his words, '...does it always just take a small ruckus to have you come running into here?! Because if that's the case....' Stanislav teeters to one side as he stands. The tiny nurse tries to steady his massive form, but it's useless, '...Ivan is going to have to be causing much trouble! DYAAHAAHAA!!' Stanislav rocks backward as he laughs and staggers back into the wall, knocking a sizeable chunk of plaster from the wall and falling to a sitting position. The nurse puts her hand over her eyes as he hits. 'Boom!' El Drago yells out amidst a lot of laughter and the nurse spins uncovers her eyes to see Ivan laughing from the seat of his bare ass against the hospital floor and luckily the gown is still covering. 'You two will be the death of me.' She says pulling at Ivan's arm. 'Let's get you back in bed now Praporschik.' She struggles uselessly to pull Ivan to his feet but he's not moving anywhere. El Drago meanwhile his making elaborate motions for Ivan with his hands and then one we can understand, 'Send here over hear and I'll get her to bend down.' 'Look.' Ivan blurts out almost swatting the nurse as he points at El Drago. She turns quickly and sees him 'choking' and rushes over to him immediately. 'Oh no, Mr. Drago cough it up.' She bends over to check on the Mexican Dragon giving Ivan a show that leaves a big goofy grin on his face. The creak of a door opening can be heard and as the camera pans up we see the two faces of an angry Alexei and pissed off Pablo. Ruslan blinks for a moment as Stanislav looks up at his Comrade, 'Praporschik Stanislav!!' Ivan squints, 'Huh?' Rucha runs over to Drago as Ruslan grabs Ivan, picks him up, and pulls him back over to the bed, 'Lie down Ivan! You have to get your rest!' Pablo isn't so gentle with El Drago as he smacks the back of his head and pulls him up to his feet by his mask-covered ear. 'Knock it off!' El Drago holds his head in shame as he climbs back into bed and Pablo further lectures him, 'No sople su cubierta mi amigo sobre esta mujer. Usted debe recuperar.' Ivan too climbs back into his bed as Alexei turns to the confused nurse, 'Sorry for the trouble, you may go.' The nurse nods and exits the room quickly leaving the two managers to scold their wrestlers. Alexei puts his hands on his hips and shakes his head, 'Ivan Sergeiovich Stanislav! What are you doing? You need to rest! You need to get well! We have skulls that must be cracked, remember?' Ivan nods slowly, 'Whatever you say, Ruslan!' Ivan chuckles to himself while Ruslan fumes. Alexei then turns to Drago who is trying his hardest not to laugh, 'And you, Mexican are bad influence...' 'It is Russian that is bad influence.' Pablo interrupts and Alexei slowly turns to his Mexican counterpart. The two start to argue at a pace to quick to record and after thirty some seconds both stop and yell at the laughing wrestlers. 'Shut up' Both yell, 'And stay in that bed...' further chimes Pablo, 'You are not to leave those beds.' Alexei finishes up and quickly exits the room in a huff and with Pablo right behind him. As the door slowly swings shut the sound of the two still bickering can be heard and Ivan and El Drago turn to each other and break into hysteria as the scene fades out. One word... Morphine.
