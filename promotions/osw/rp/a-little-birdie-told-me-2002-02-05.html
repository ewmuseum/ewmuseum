A little birdie told me...

5 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/05/02 We hear distant weeping on a black screen. However, the weeping comes closer and closer. We hear a heart beat superimposed over the weeping. “How could I allow him to reverse that reverse reversal!!!” Suddenly, photons fill the air, in other words, the light comes on. The weeps belong to Ritchie Syxx, who hides lays on a white couch in his apartment. He wears blue jeans and a bright gold shirt, with brown timberland boots. A wooden wall unit with a stereo - TV system is in the background, many dollars worth of equipment can be seen on its hard wood shelves. The carpets are off white, probably due to juice and soda stains from a clumsy Ritchie. “Why!” Ritchie yells in agony. If you didn’t know, he lost to Corg in his debut OSW match. Syxx did his best, and turned up just a little short. Obviously, he can’t accept defeat. “Damn it! Damn it! Damn it!”...”If only...” “If only Women had testicles, then they’d be men! Or at least some weird genetic freak,” calls a voice from the back, the voice is very high pitched and has an eerie sound to it. “Who...who’s that?” Syxx gets a hint of fear in his tone. “It’s me!” Ritchie looks behind him, to the wall unit, which has a bust of the God Pallas on top of it. Perched on top of the bust is a scruffy, dirty, ugly, black, Raven. “Who...who are you?” “You’re such a dumbass, Ritchie,” calls the bird. “No, I’m not...you are!!” “Don’t you realize that I am trying to recreate the Raven by Poe?” “F--- the Po’lice!” “Iambic Pentameter!!” says the bird, who obviously knows his English. “Oh, you grim ghastly ghouly raven who is perched on the bust of Pallas just above my wall unit floor, what it is you beg of me on this night’s plutonian shore?” “Nevermore” “Heh, we’re doing good,” says Ritchie, who is ruining it....again. “I mean...Tell me - tell me! On the Slam of Saturn’s day, shall 5 plus 1 emerge victorious on the rings highly woven floor?” “NEVERMORE!” “Oh you stupid craven, tell me- tell me! Will I get the count of three on the Slam of Saturn’s day....will my record be one win more?” “NEVERMORE!” “Say something you scruffy beast of the guild of bore of whom is perched just on the bust of Pallas just above my wall unit floor!” “NEVERMORE!” “Talk - please talk! oh saintly raven, for I will be the one whom gets the 45 and makes you lying on the floor!” “EEP!” “Are you going to talk now, or just more nevermore?” “I’ll talk now. Stop being so scared of Corg! You’ll beat him this Saturday, he got lucky last time! He got lucky last time! You‘re gonna show him how they fight in...um...Manchester!” “Yeah!” “You’re gonna enter that ring, give him a few punches, your DDT and you’re gonna pin him!” “YEAH!” “Who’s gonna win!?” “ME!” “Who’s gonna lose!?” “The wildcats...I mean.....CORG!” Syxx gets pumped up, he flexes his muscles, starts jumping around, like he’s getting ready and he finally gets some confidence in himself. “Thank you Raven!” Syxx goes up to the bust of Pallas, just above his wall unit floor, and he goes to hug the raven. But he finds air. “The raven...was never here!”
