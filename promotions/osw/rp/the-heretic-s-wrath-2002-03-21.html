The Heretic's Wrath

21 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21-03-02 In the ring two generic wrestlers are locking up to kick off a house show, when suddenly the lights in the arena dim, and “Carmina Burana” hits the P.A. and the crowd boo hysterically. Suddenly a burst of flame roars up in front of the entranceway, and two giant black figures appear behind it. As the music continues the flames grow higher, and the wrestlers in the ring look up to the stage to see whom it is. Announcer #1: OH MY GOD IT CAN’T BE!!! Announcer #2: IT IS!!! OH MY GOD HELL IS ABOUT TO BREAK LOOSE NOW!!! The two wrestlers walk through the fire, not getting burnt by the very hot flames, and show themselves to the crowd as pyros go off all over the stage. Announcer #1: IT IS!!! AZRAEL CAIN AND HESSIAN!!! IT’S HERESIAN BLOODLUST AND IT LOOKS LIKE THEY MEAN BUSINESS!!! Hessian and Azrael Cain strut down the ramp, followed by Minion the Imp and Caleb. The two wrestlers in the ring are very pissed off that they were interrupted and run out the ring and up to the two Satanists. Hessian grabs one of the wrestlers and hits the Eternal Damnation right there on the steel ramp! Azrael Cain grabs the wrestler that tried to attack him and drives him into the steel ramp with a modified Ninth Gate. Announcer #1: OH MY GOD THOSE WRESTLERS ARE BEING DEMOLISHED BY HERESIAN BLOODLUST!!! WE NEED SECURITY OUT HERE NOW!!! Announcer #2: Yes…but why do we need to talk with the Caps Lock on? Announcer #1: IT HELPS PUT EMPHASIS INTO THIS PIECE OF SHIT PROMO!!! JUST DO IT!!! Announcer #2: AYE, AYE SKIPPER!!!!!!!!! Hessian and Azrael Cain leave the two wrestlers lying, almost dead on the ramp, blood splattered everywhere even on the crowd. They climb over the top rope while Minion the Imp and Caleb crawl under the bottom rope. Azrael Cain grabs the microphone off the ring announcer and puts it to his lips, as the crowd boo loudly. Azrael Cain: SHUT UP!!! The crowd only boo louder, so Azrael ignores them and continues speaking. Azrael Cain: Civil Disobedience stole our tag team titles from us and we want them now! 3B, Twisted Metal and Dame and Ramey can go to HELL because we are the true champions and we are the best and if you do not like that then we will tell Satan to kill you, and he’ll do it and give us the tag team titles!!! Azrael Cain hands the mic to Hessian, who holds it up to his masked head, and the crowd boo as loud as they can. Hessian just roars and they all shut up. Hessian: Heresian Bloodlust have spent their entire lives in torment and pain, but that pain is nothing compared to what you’re going to feel when we get our title match back! We shouldn’t even be fighting against you two, you’re both just little pieces of crap and we are better than you!!! We will send you to the pits of hell, and as an example on Slam!, we’re going to kill all our competitors and drive them straight through the tables until they can’t get back up!!! Hessian goes to hand the mic back to Cain, but is suddenly jumped from behind by the two wrestlers who have recovered somehow. Before Cain or Hessian can react, Caleb and Minion grab the two wrestlers and give them both a Stone Cold Stunner and they knock them out. Azrael Cain and Hessian grab the two wrestlers and give them both a Tombstone Piledriver that sends them crashing THROUGH THE MAT!!!!!!!!! The crowd goes wild, and Heresian Bloodlust make their way back up the ramp gesturing the titles around their waists as “Carmina Burana” hits the P.A. and EMTs rush to the jobber wrestlers in the ring. Announcer #1: WELL IT LOOKS LIKE CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE ARE IN BIG TROUBLE!!! Announcer #2: *Sigh…* HERESIAN BLOODLUST WANT THOSE TITLES BADLY AND IT LOOKS LIKE SATAN IS GOING TO RUN WILD ON SETH AND TREVOR!!!!!!!!! “OH GOD NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!” The shrill scream of Minion the Imp echoes throughout the land, as he awakes from a well-needed slumber. Hessian stops dead in his tracks and drops the awoken Imp from his shoulders, grabbing his steel mask in agony. ”Minion!!! How many times must you be warned? Steel and Screams don’t mix, especially when I am trying to recover from that damned indentation Stanislav forced into my old mask and my skull!” Hessian shouts angrily, as Minion picks himself up off of the old, dirt track. Azrael Cain traipses alongside Hessian, carrying a sleeping Caleb over his shoulder; in the same fashion that Hessian carried Minion. The fields on either side of the dirt track emit a fine odour of freshly cut grass, despite being filled completely with weeds and crabgrass, reaching up to and in excess of 10ft. The distant howling of Coyotes courses through Minion’s veins like electricity, and he scrambles back up Hessian to the safety of his shoulder. “I ain’t afraid of no ghost………” whispers Minion. “No, but the Coyotes seem to have you spooked!” replies Azrael Cain, hoisting Caleb back up onto his shoulder as Caleb sleeps on. Ahead of Heresian Bloodlust the little dirt track continues along to the horizon, an atmospheric little road accented by the glistening moonlight hitting its surface and lighting the way. “♫♪Up and down that lonely road of faaaaiiiith…I have been there…♪♫” Minion sings to himself tiredly, as Azrael and Hessian try to look over the crabgrass for any signs of life. “Minion, stop singing or I will send you back to sleep the hard way!” hisses Azrael, trying to keep anyone from waking Caleb. Hessian stops. He looks over the crabgrass to something catching his attention. Out of earshot, Azrael watches as Hessian hoists Minion up over his head, and laughs maniacally as Minion nods his head and squeals excitedly. Azrael saunters across the dirt track to Hessian, taking care not to drop Caleb. ”Hessian…just what exactly did you plan for us out in the middle of nowhere, and what did you spot over the field?” he asks. “Well first Civil Disobedience get the upper hand over us at Crowning Glory, and then Ivan Stanislav dents my mask into an irreparable state, hence forcing me to mould another one, and quite frankly it’s going to be a while before my skull fully heals itself after that Russian punched me with an Iron Fist………and a Limp Wrist…” replies Hessian, his deep voice and eyes intensifying. “But what exactly does this have to do with us aimlessly wandering around the countryside, Hessian?” Azrael asks immediately. “A man has to take his anger out on something, and the little farmhouse o’er yonder will do nicely!” replies Hessian. Azrael shrugs and Heresian Bloodlust make their way through the field, rich with dead life, weeds, flowers, various other florae, and the occasional small mammals or snakes dead before them for some reason. The cool night breeze sets an eerie tone as Hessian and Azrael Cain push through the thick growth of weeds and natural death surrounding them. Caleb sleeps on, unknowing of the Bloodlust venture, however Minion shakes coldly atop Hessian’s massive shoulder. The frustrating journey finally ends as Cain and Hessian exit the thick brush and step out in front of a small log cabin. Smoke is billowing from the little chimminy, and the distinct sign of candlelight dances in the cheap little windowpanes, but Cain can detect a distinct whiff of something. “Hessian…I recognize that smell………it’s………” ”Marijuana…” Hessian cuts in. “What makes you think that?” “Just look around you Cain, that isn’t crabgrass, it’s grass…” Hessian whispers cautiously………but it’s too late… “What’s that I smell??? Where are we??? Oh wait I was awake anyway………MARIE J!!!!!!!!!” Minion literally jumps into the air, awaking from his little nap and looking around for the source of the smell. ”It’s all around us!!! Sweet merciful Lucifer it is! I’M RICH I TELLS YA!!! RIIIIIIIIIIIICH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! I’M THE LUCKIEST IMP ALI-……………” THUMP! Minion suddenly crumples to the ground, and Azrael’s large pale fist falls back to his side, and again he hoists Caleb back up onto his shoulder to keep him from slipping. Hessian cocks his head in wonder at Cain, and releases a quick chuckle. “What? I clearly stated that Caleb needed his rest, and now Minion is resting………in peace, problem?” Azrael shrugs off to Hessian, who merely laughs and heads towards the house. The candlelight flickers in the window, and Hessian presses his masked skull against it. Through the dusty, dirty window Hessian can pick up the silhouette of a man sitting on a chair, petting what looks to be a little dog. “It seems our prey has a little companion…this should be fun.” whispers Hessian. Slinking as only a 7’2” Satanist can, Hessian slithers around the side of the house to the front door. Azrael props Caleb slowly down, pillowing his head on Minion’s torso. Cain creeps after Hessian, and they wait at the front door. The creaking of the old wood, the slight breeze and the low muffled callings of the dog fill the two Heretic’s ears. Cain slowly takes hold of the old wooden doorknob, and ever so slightly turns it, to avoid creaking hinges. To his surprise the door simply slides open silently, and the two duck under the low doorframe, and stop inside the kitchen, the open passage into the den directly in front of them. Hessian edges forward, and peeks through to see his victim. The old man sits in his old oak rocking chair, petting his old Terrier, a Jack Russell. Cain looks past Hessian and eyes up the old man. “Actually…as I look at that frail little carcass now, he isn’t worth our time Cain…” says Hessian in the most quietest of voices. “Don’t be deterred simply because of his age, Hessian if you wanted to take out your anger on someone, and you dragged us all the way out here, by hell you had better do something!” Azrael Cain hisses into Hessian’s ear, filling his mind with the thoughts of brutality and disease, want and sickness. Hessian’s eyes glow an intense green as he growls in a low, deep tone, and the Jack Russell Terrier turns to them and barks as Hessian and Azrael Cain walk into the den, and into the old man’s nightmares… The little coal fire in he corner of the den finally fizzles into nothingness, as the last of the smoke billows up the stone chimminy and into the early morning sky. Sitting in the rocking chair, snoozing is Azrael Cain, with Caleb resting at his feet, whilst across the room, lying flat on his back on the floor is Hessian. Azrael begins to stir, and eventually wakes up. He stretches, and all the bones that need to begin to crick and creak. Cain sits up in the chair and looks to Caleb, who is still asleep. He shifts his view to Hessian, snoring on the shag carpet by a little wooden table and then to the front of the fire…where a relatively large object lies motionless under a pale blue blanket, lying next to which is the Jack Russell Terrier, resting its head on a red patch of blanket. “Hessian?!” Azrael shouts, finding something to be not right. Hessian himself begins to stir, not unlike Cain, and eventually sits up, and shakes the tiredness from his head. “N-meh…who………oh…Cain. Slept peacefully I hope?” Hessian enquires, and looks to Cain, who is pointing to the fire, and follows Cain’s pointed finger to the heap in front of the brunt out fire. Both heretic’s get up, and walk to the front of the fire, and stare at the blanketed object. Crumpled on the ground, in the foetus position, the blanket is soaked with blood at what looks to be the head region…of the old man. “But………I never killed him…” Hessian says wearily, scratching the back of his neck with his gauntleted hand. “Actually you didn’t…unless you did away with him whilst I was sleeping…” replies Cain. Heresian Bloodlust query over the body at their feet, as an early morning bird coos over the rising sun. “Impossible! Before we entered this room last night the old man had a heart attack and the dog simply whimpered all night!” “Yes you’re right…but then………” Hessian again scratches his neck, perplexed by the events culminating in the body before them. Hessian grabs the blue blanket, and pulls it off the body, the Jack Russell rolling off of it and just lying on the wooden floor, devastated by the death of its owner. Surely enough there before them lays the corpse of an old man; his face twisted and mangled into a look of utter horror, and his neck snapped, the obvious bruises showing which way it was yanked. From outside Heresian Bloodlust here the sound of whistling, and ready they prepare for whatever is outside. The whistling, reminiscent of Lonely Road of Faith gets louder, as the front door opens, and Azrael and Hessian listen as the whistling stops and the sound of little footsteps nears them. As they pull back their fists, ready to attack the intruder, through the kitchen and into the den appears Minion the Imp, drying his hands off with a towel, and wiping blood from his mouth. “Minion…care to explain why you’re so bloody and why this corpse is so dead?” asks Azrael, seemingly disappointed and frustrated with the 3’11” Imp. “Well of course that old man died from a heart attack last night, but I didn’t want to disappoint you both so I broke his neck for good measure…” replies Minion, a sly grin growing across his face. Hessian and Azrael sigh in disbelief, as Minion giggles to himself. “Well we can be thankful for something, the Heretic’s Wrath has struck again and we are one step closer to achieving our tag team championships!” says Hessian. Before Azrael can continue Hessian’s analogy, Minion the Imp bursts in, interrupting the Marked Beast rather rudely. “Oh by the way…you two don’t plan on EVER doing an in-ring promo…do you?” Azrael Cain and Hessian just look in frustration at the Imp, as the Jack Russell Terrier lies faithfully next to its deceased owner, another victim of The Heretic’s Wrath… Fin
