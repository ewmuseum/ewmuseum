'Rave' Reviews (Alexei/Tempest rp)

21 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-21-02 At first I thought that I was mistaken. There is no way this, “testament of virtue” that Ivan has spoken so highly about would frequent a place like this…but much to my concern, she is here….right in the middle of that dance floor… Yes, Russian Intelligence had told me that Tempest would be going to a ‘Rave’ dance of some sort. I thought it only prudent that I should be there personally to watch after her…and what I found was appalling! The music itself shook the very walls! The people packed into the small building made the room incredibly stuff and close….myself, Stukov, and Preobrezenskii, the other operatives that had accompanied me, were amazed that such a place existed! As I stood in the corner and tried to appear inconspicuous, I found that perhaps I stuck out like…what do those Americans say…. ‘A sore thumb?’ Yes, I believe that is it. The scantily clad women wrenched my stomach as they grinded rudely against men dressed just as horribly. This was not a place to dance, this was a brothel! I couldn’t hear my earpiece over the music as it seemed to only get louder and the flashing lights and glitter in the air made me only more the inclined to pull my firearm and end it all right here…. ….the youth of America in all it’s glory. As well as people exchanging what appeared to be a controlled substance, “Ecstasy” as it was known to Americans, to one another and using it blatantly! I wish I had brought my camera, this would show Ivan Stanislav what he is getting himself into…. And so Alexei Ruslan stood in the smoke filled corner of the club with his hat pulled over his brow, his trench coat pulled around his body. He glanced to the other two nondescript men wearing trench coats in the other corner but remained completely still as he watched the events go on before him. Ruslan tried to remain calm. When a girl came up to him to get him to dance he was in for quite a scare! Not only did she have enough piercings to deflate her head, but her breath reeked of alcohol! I remember hearing Stukov ask if he should ‘take her down’ but I screamed ‘no!’ I cannot remember if that was to Stukov..or to the girl. After a quick shove though, she got the point and left. Such animals these people..they call this living…..sometimes I wonder if perhaps nuclear bomb could have fixed the problems of this downward spiraling world. Throughout it all, Alexei kept an eye on Tempest, watching to see if she had done anything that would merit a report back to the Praporschik. However, to his dismay, she did not touch any of the drugs so far as while he was there, and as for the alcohol she only carried a small drink for a short period of time, and then only drank half of it. Standing near the crowd was something of a dangerous position, and after countless close calls Alexei chose to abandon his post on the ground and walked up a set of iron step to a landing looking over the dance floor. He found a table and two chairs and pulled up a chair and sat. A scantily clad waitress walked over to him, but after his sudden recoil from her presence she got the point and wandered off…. I do not get paid enough for this…. Ruslan crossed his leg and surveyed the room from under the brim of his hat. The bass pounded into the skull of the FSB Agent as he tried to remain calm… And that damn waitress came back!!! Sure enough, the waitress that scared him before returned with a bottle of vodka. “Sir,” said the waitress with a smile. “Here is your Vodka.” “Ma’am,” replied Ruslan, “I did not order any Vodka! Please get away from me!” The waitress giggled, “I know Sir, but she did for you.” She pointed to the dance floor directly at Tempest, who looked up at Alexei sheepishly and waved to him. Ruslan’s eyes widened suddenly as he looked at the waitress, who now put it on his table with a glass, “Perhaps she likes you, Sir.” The waitress winked and walked away as Alexei stared at Tempest in pure shock. He quickly grabbed the bottle and opened it, downing a hefty portion of it straight from the bottle. How could she know that I am here?? Amongst all this noise, movement, and commotion she notices ME?! Alexei took another hearty drink from the Vodka bottle. She is a smart woman, I will give her that, but not as smart as Alexei Gregorovich and The Russian Federation! I have a job to do! With the knowledge of this place Ivan will find out and I will inform him of what she really does. It was all fun and games at first when she met with Ivan, but now it has gone too far. She is clouding his perception, she is an ‘Enemy of the State’ in a way, if I may quote General Tomifei. Her erratic lifestyle is not healthy, she is too carefree, she has no purpose except to ‘have fun’ which is entirely unacceptable! Alexei pushed the Vodka bottle away and looked down at the dance floor, realizing that he lost Tempest. He pushed his ear but Stukov and Preobrezenskii had lost her too! He nervously stood up from his chair and looked over the railing… DAMN!! Tempest stood before him with a smile and said, 'Hello Alexei. Fancy meeting you here.' Alexei whirled around, almost bumping into her, 'GAH!! TEMPEST!?!' He blinked a few times, 'What..what are you doing here?' She giggled and swayed to the music a little as she said, 'I should ask you the same thing. After all, this is more my world than yours.' Alexei swallowed and shrugged to himself, 'Well, you do not know very much about me, now do you. Perhaps I frequent this....place...' Alexei reached to his ear as Stukov started yelling and shook his head, trying to get rid of the noise. He turned around quickly and mumbled something, causing Stukov to shut up, and turned back around. Tempest tilted her head and chuckled as she said, 'Something tells me you've never seen a place quite like this.' Alexei shrugged, 'I have seen many things Tempest, some extremely happy, some very grim...but no, I have never seen place like this.' He shrugged and sat at his table, 'Thank you for the Vodka, you did not have to do that....' He swallowed and fixed his jacket, his hand moved to his pocket and then jerked back reflexively. Tempest's eyes followed the motion and then she smiled at him. 'Well, ya don't exactly melt into the scenery here,' she said. “But that's not important. What is important is... why are you following me again?' Alexei swallowed and crossed his arms over his chest, 'Who said that I am following you? It is small world after all, right?' He tilted his head to the side, 'You should not be so judgmental, Tempest.' Alexei ran his tongue over the front of his teeth as he began to unearth his persuasive weaponry that he learned so many years ago in the KGB. Tempest started to say something and then stopped, she took a seat opposite him and smiled as she said, 'You're probably right. After all, I've been telling people how much fun a good rave is. I shouldn't be surprised if people listen.” Tempest rolled her eyes and said, ' And speaking of not being so judgmental...' Alexei sighed and leveled his voice, 'I am just noticing observation.' He looked around him, 'I sure hope Ivan is not aware of this....' Tempest frowned and said, 'Aware of what? That I go clubbing? He Of course he knows. Everyone does. Heck, it's part of my promotion life. Everyone knows that in whatever town Tempest visits, you can find her at the hottest clubs.' Alexei nodded, 'Yes, I am aware of that, but such conditions? Appalling!' Alexei poured himself some Vodka, 'If Ivan would see what people do in here...he may have heart attack!' Tempest shook her head and said, 'You don't give him enough credit. Ivan's not as closed minded as you think. I'm curious though... why is that? You act like he couldn’t handle anything outside his norm, so to speak.' Alexei leaned back in his chair for a moment, choosing his words carefully, 'Ivan has lived the life of soldier for his entire life. That is what he has always done. He has accepted a life of norms and regularities, and one that is firmly against irregularities. Such as loud, noisy parties that break the law.' Alexei took a drink from his Vodka, 'It is not that I do not think he can handle it, I know he can. It is that I know he would not stand for it. If he were in here right now, I assure you he would have killed that disc jockey and arrested all these...miscreants.' Tempest looked around and said, 'The few people doing anything illegal aren't hurting anyone. Besides... you don't really care about them per se...' Alexei leans forward, very angry suddenly, 'I care about order! I care about normalcy! I care about stability!! WHICH YOU...er....these people do not care about!!' Alexei leans back and composes himself. 'Is that all you care about?' Tempest asked. 'What do you mean, 'is that all that I care about?' It is my job, is it not? Is there not work ethic? Without order there is nothing but chaos, right?' Alexei raises his jaw indignantly. 'Not necessarily,' Tempest said with a shrug. 'Sometimes fun and a little freedom come from breaking out of an old order. But that's not my point. You said you care about order, but what else do you care about?' 'I care about a lot of things, Tempest,' replied Ruslan, 'I care about my country, I care about my people, I care about my friends! I care about my work and I care about security. But most of all, I care about getting the job done...by any means necessary.' Alexei leaned forward at the end of his reply, some venom in his words it would seem. 'But... do you care about Ivan?' Tempest asked pointedly. Alexei blinked, as if mentally slapped, 'That is a stupid question! Of course I care about Ivan! He is my best friend! My greatest Comrade!' Tempest smiled and said, 'Good! Then can you please try and help him? Look he's happy with me, so why can't you be? And the Army or whoever else... What is it that they want from me?' Alexei shook his head, 'You do not seem to understand Tempest. Ivan does not need any help, he is big man, remember?' Alexei pulled his chair in slightly and propped his elbows up on the table, 'Ivan has job to do, and he is wavering now, understand? At first, I did not mind your involvement so much, but now that the High Command is involved, things have changed.' He paused as his hand nonchalantly reached to his pocket under the table and froze, 'You....you do not wish to know what they want from you.' Tempest's eyes widened a moment, but quickly resumed their normal look. She frowned and said, 'I don't understand it though. He's been going through OSW like a tornado, even more since I've been around. Don't they see that?' Alexei sighed deeply, 'Yes Tempest, they see that. When he gave you that book it...it opened the eyes of many men,' Alexei swallowed, 'including myself.' Her jaw moved a little, without opening her mouth and she said, 'I'm not good enough for them am I?' Part of Alexei wanted to lie to her and make up an excuse. He fumbled with his drink as his hammer and sickle ring caught his eye and he sighed, 'Tempest, you are not Russian..that is one of the problems...' She nodded absently, looking off to one side. 'I know... and I can't fix that either,' she said. 'But what does it matter? It's up to Ivan, not them.' She folded her arms as her lower lip stuck out in a noticeable pout. Alexei breathed in deeply, 'Tempest, you don't seem to understand. Ivan has a purpose. When he was four years old, Ivan was selected for that purpose, and since then he has done remarkable job!' He paused, 'His judgment is being clouded, Tempest. He is doing things that Ivan Stanislav does not do. He is changing, and we cannot afford that to happen! He is...' Alexei paused and trailed off, preferring not to get wrapped up in his words. Tempest tilted her head and said, 'He was selected right after his father died? that seems... strangely cruel. I mean someone decided what the rest of his life would be back then?' Alexei coughed up the Vodka he was drinking, his eyes wide as saucers, 'HOW DO....ehem..how do you know about his father?!!' Tempest shrugged and said, 'He told me. It's something we have in common even if it is sad actually.' Alexei nodded slowly at her, eyeing her carefully, 'Yes, well, it was a sad coincidence, that is all.' Alexei blinked for a second as something registered, 'He..he told you that? He told you about...that??' Alexei blinked again. The knowledge of Ivan Stanislav telling Tempest one of the most secret things in his life worried him more, 'I suggest you leave Ivan alone, Tempest. Tell him you cannot see him anymore.' 'What?!' Tempest snapped. 'Are you crazy? Ivan's been nicer to me than anyone has in years. And he's your friend and he's happy with me. Why would you try and end that?' Alexei gritted his teeth and clenched his fist tightly, 'Listen, Ivan is losing face in the Motherland, you understand me? HE IS LOSING HIS ONLY SUPPORT!!' Alexei slammed his fist on the table and caught himself, recessing back to his quiet self, 'Ivan is not a nice man, Tempest. Ivan has done things that would horrify you...' Tempest leaned over the table slightly and looked into Alexei's yes as she said levelly, 'And I have done things that would horrify you, things I try not to think about. And for some reason, when I'm around Ivan I can forget about them. If you think I'll give him up without a fight, you're wrong.' She sat back down slowly. Alexei swallowed and shook his head, irritated, he tosses his hat off of his head, 'You will lose if you fight it, Tempest.' Fuming Tempest said, 'Why is it that you and them all seem to think I won't stick with Ivan? That’s what I don't get. I mean, you act like I'm gonna leave or something. Why would I?' Perhaps it was the incessant bass booming in the background, or the loud people around him, or Stukov screaming through his earpiece, but something caused Ruslan to snap. He shot to his feet and slammed both fists onto the table. The Vodka fell over and spilled across the table, dribbling onto the floor as his glass rolled precariously over the side and shattered. Ruslan seemed, for a moment, to grow in height as he seethed and let out a yell that belied his size, his demeanor, and his regular voice, 'YOU DONT UNDERSTAND ANYTHING, TEMPEST!! You have blinders on to entire world!! I cannot tell you why you would leave! I cannot tell you why you would abandon him!' Alexei slammed his fist on the table, 'But I CAN tell you this! You are weakening him! You are making him soft!! YOU ARE MAKING HIM AN INDIVIDUAL!! THAT is unacceptable!!' Alexei blinked quickly and looked around, suddenly reverting to his more calm self. He fixed the front of his jacket and shrunk back into his seat... Tempest watched him calmly and said, 'And do you think that you can stop him from becoming anything he wants to be? Do you really think that if he's happy with me, you'll be able to get him away form me? And more to the point, why would you want to try? If you're the big man with in the KGB, then why can't you handle this?' Alexei leaned back in his chair, his eyes flamed with an anger he had not felt in a very long time, 'You do not understand the power you are challenging, Tempest. He wants to be a communist! And he is! And as for why I would want to, because it is my job to make sure the job gets done!' Alexei got up from his chair, unable to sit anymore, 'Don't you dare challenge me, young woman, don't you dare challenge Alexei Ruslan. Whether you know it or not, I am doing you a very big favor...' He ground his teeth and crossed his arms. Tempest stood and leaned against a railing for a moment watching him. 'You've done a few small ones for me before, and I'm grateful for that.' Alexei scoffed, 'You have a funny way of showing it. You don't understand what is going on Tempest. You should not be too hasty to point fingers and make accusations.....' She nodded and said, 'You might be right. But don't you see that I get scared when you say I should leave Ivan? I don't understand and I don't wanna do it. But I do recall that after all that research you must have done back when I first met him, you didn't choose to tell him a few things. That almost made me think you were on my side.' Alexei rubes his forehead and ran his hands down his face, 'At first Tempest, it was...cute....to see Ivan enjoying your company. Now, it is dangerous.' Alexei rubbed his chin, 'Ivan has saying, 'If you get to know someone too much, they can be exploited.' And that is truth! You can be used, Tempest, and I, as strategic advisor to Ivan, must give him advisory on what his next moves should be.' 'Tempest,' Alexei said after a moment, 'I was never 'on' your side, I have always been on the side of Russia..and I will continue to do that.' 'But you're not on Ivan's side,' She said sadly. 'Alexei, he trusts you and he trusts me. And we may very well be it. I don't want to be anything but good for him, but I can't become Russian and I'm not a piece of a strategy.' Alexei's face twisted, almost as if at war with his own thoughts, 'Tempest, I am in a quandary, I do not know what to do.' He leaned over the table, 'If Ivan fails, we, together, will be burned for the problem, you understand? I do not know what that would mean. You cannot just...fire people like us that know certain things.' He paused and looked down at the table, 'Tempest, if Ivan loses Russia, he will have lost 40 years of his life, do you understand? If he loses Russia, he loses every person in the world that ever loved him..save for maybe...one...' Alexei swallowed, 'Do you understand?' She nodded gravely and said, 'I do. And I don't wanna see that happen any more than you do. But... if you don't know what to do, just say that. Don't just tell me to leave like that solves everything. I wanna help Ivan if I can.' 'If you want to help him, then you will do as I say, Tempest,' he shifted his weight, 'I am anxious, afraid, something very bad is going to happen.' Alexei paused for a moment and reached into his ear and pulled out his earpiece and shut it off. He walked over to Tempest and talked very softly, 'I know you now think I am great enemy and you hate me more than anything, and so be it, but I am telling you Tempest, if you do not get away from Ivan Stanislav, something bad is going to happen. I don't know what...but I fear something will happen.' He paused and looked at the floor, 'I am trying to help in best way I can, it is just not in the way that you would appreciate....' Tempest looked at the ground and said, 'Alexei, can you tell me one thing?' Alexei put the earpiece in his pocket, 'Perhaps, it depends...' He shifted his weight uneasily, not liking the directness. 'If it weren't for all the stuff with the military, would you want me to stay with Ivan?” she asked pointedly. Alexei blinked. He put his hands into his pockets and took a step backwards and thought. He continued to think for a long time and then looked up at Tempest slowly, 'Y..yes...I would...I may be a member of the Intelligence, but I am a Russian first, and I am a Russian friend to Ivan Stanislav above that....' He shifted his weight again and seemed to recoil into his jacket, very uneasy at his openness. Alexei gritted his teeth as Tempest turned and walked into the crowd again. He shook and grabbed his hat and put it on his head. She is stubborn..just like Ivan….so be it…. Ruslan put the earpiece back in his ear and pushed on it, “Stukov, Preo, let’s get out of here.” He paused as Stukov talked, “Nyet, nothing has come out of it….nyet….” She will surely tell Ivan…things are spiraling out of control… …..and I cannot save it….
