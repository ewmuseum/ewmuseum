OSW Old School Promos — #379

Warning: Cheese Ahead...this stuff's real.

2002-04-26

Amy 'Aimz' Campbell

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Note: Not my best...I just needed to get some stuff off of my chest.) Bre had bailed her from temporary lock-down, Rent-A-Hero had returned...it looked like everything had found it's place back in Amy's life again... Jeez, was she ever wrong. One phone call was all it took to drive her over the edge. Had you ever seen Campbell cry? No...but if you had seen her after that one call, you would have. ''Amy, I have cancer.'' That was all it took, all she needed to snap emotionally...it was a phone call from her father, Forrester... He spent the better part of two hours assuring her it was just a little fleck on one of his kidneys, that it was one-hundred percent cure-able with surgery that would take out half of or one whole kidney...but it wasn't enough to calm her down. The word 'cancer' alone is the most terrifying thing a person could imagine hearing...one of her grandmothers had died of cancer on the lungs that ventured to the brain. Now her father had it and, though she was told it was so small symptoms hadn't even started, and that it would be gone, he wouldn't have cancer any longer after surgery...she wouldn't believe it. So many people had cancer...it was such a common thing but nobody, especially Amy, really understands these sort of things until they hit home. And oh, did it ever hit home...this was her FATHER we were talking about, cancer on her dad wasn't something to be taken lightly and, no matter how 'tough' Campbell can act at times, her pride and attitude were swallowed deep down the moment she heard that 'cancer' word from her father's lips. ''Dad...'' She was choking up again, still trying to hold back from showing any 'real emotion'. ''...I'm gonna come home AS SOON as the pay-per-view ends, alright?'' ''You don't need to do that...I'm not sick, everything's fine.'' ''I know...I know. I've just got a lot of crap to apologize for...and I do mean A LOT...is it okay? I know you don't want any special treatment or whatever...but this cancer thing scares me half to death, it's kinda slapped me and made me realize what you're worth, I guess.'' A little more solemn this time, the deep voice on the other end came. ''Okay, okay...if it means that much to you, we could use a visit anyway...everybody misses you.'' ''Alright...I'm gonna book the flight tonight, I'll be home probably Monday morning......'' Amy paused, taking care of what she said...nothing was guaranteed. ''...if I'm not in hospital.'' ''Hey...you won't be! I know that Hostyle fellow has friends, but nothing and I mean NOTHING is going to keep the truth from being told.'' ''I guess you're right.'' There was silence for what seemed like minutes before her father's voice rung out yet again, very quiet this time... ''Uhhh...Amy...'' ''Yeah?'' ''It is the truth, right? You didn't actually ever associate with him...did you?'' ''What?!'' She was shocked, to say the least...and her tone showed that. ''God NO! I can't believe you'd even think that...'' ''Well, sorry...it just seemed from what HE was saying that he might've cared about you.'' Campbell knew exactly what this was about. ''I'm not in a mood to argue right now...I'm really...'' Struggling to keep from using her 'favorite word' at that point, she changed her tune. ''...really.......f.....freaking...upset about you. But look, I know you don't like me being with Rentie...but he IS NOT crazy, he treats me better than anybody else ever has or ever will.'' ''He's just...I don't know...unstable. But I'll have to talk to you later about it...I've got to tell Paul...'' ''Okay...love you and take care, alright?'' ''I will, don't you worry about that...your grandmother's been riding my ass about this...'' He chuckled whole-heartily. ''And I love you too...bye!'' As Amy hung up, everything hit her at once... She had to hold back from the obvious outpour...her father had cancer, BUT it was cure-able. The main thing right now is to get Drew Hostyle...she had to repeat that over and over again, otherwise she'd let family drag her sights from the main goal... Hostyle pretended to what, actually 'love' her? That sleaze would NEVER know love...especially not from Amy Campbell; it just wasn't plausible, nor would it ever be... Before he began talking about her, she had little to no beef with the 'Ghetto for God' 'homeboy'...but now there were real problems. He began bragging backstage...talking about Amy in bed, talking about her having a relationship with him...the man wanted to boost his ego and, she'd admit, she was a good girl to use in that. The problem is that all of what Copeland said were lies...it was all fake and, no matter how tall he built the tower, it would never be real. Unfortunately, he and Amy were the one two who knew that for sure...even more unfortunate was the fact that he liked to build illusions to make himself look good. She couldn't stand it...he was ruining her reputation; only her closest friends knew she would NEVER go for someone like Andrew Copeland...but then again, she IS with a guy who thinks he's a super-hero. Speaking of her hero...she had to tell him about her father, and about her going back home for a few days. Would he be able to come with her? Amy'd love that...it'd give her someone to help her out when she had to confront this medical bullshit...plus she needed to introduce him to her parents sooner or later and, by the looks of things with her father's upcoming operations to rid him of the cancer, it's better to be sooner...wouldn't want him having a stroke while recovering from surgery! Ah well...she'd go to bed and have herself a good cry, for the first time in a very LONG time...it might help her think. Hopefully RAH would be around to see her fight at Head On Collision, but that was doubtful... Screw this shit, the girl needs some time to sort life out...
