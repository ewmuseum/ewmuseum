'Seven Deadly Sins' series;Part 2/7; 'Sloth' - Co-Starring Jesus Crhist

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Over a long peroid of time Out of the car park It's a lazy Wednesday afternoon, a virtually abandoned motorway, just after rush hour. In all the slight wholes or ditches it is full of water glittering under the suns rays as the sky turns a red colour with a rainbow forming. Every so often a car goes crashing through the puddles, most at quite a speed giving the assumption they are probably late back from work. But after a few seconds without cars, we see the famous rusty, old, white LDV Van with a black crucifix and a 'God Loves You all!' bumper sticker. The camera then shoots in from a birds-eye-view to inside the van. Where we see Eric Sagar hunched over the wheel, with beads of sweat running down his forehead which is red with anger. He has an expression on his similar of that to being constipated. Yet next to him - in the passenger seat is God's Ultimatum: Propaganda. The Power Himself is gazing blankly through the windscreen. 'I can't believe it.' Sagar pants. Propaganda slowly turns his head to look at Sagar. 'I mean, who would have thought it?' Sagar queries. Propaganda is about to open his mouth but is stopped when… 'THERE IS NO TIME FOR ENDLESS CHIT-CHAT BROTHER!' He yells 'ONWARDS TO BOSTON!' Propaganda shrugs as Sagar points as if to say 'Off we go!' The scene fades to black. Then up again, we see a block of flats. Peaceful calm, then suddenly screeching to a halt we see the Van of OSW International Champion and his manager, Eric Sagar. Both passenger and driver doors swing open then are slammed shut. From each sides out jumps Eric and Propaganda. They run down the pavement to the entrance of the Flat in a hurry. They go the intercom and Sagar presses a button. A lazy New York accent calls from the other side. 'Who is it?' 'It's Brother Sagar and Propaganda we need to talk urgently!' Sagar screams down the intercom. 'I suppose you can come in.' He mumbles. Sagar and Propaganda rush through the door. The scene then fades out again. Then back in, we see the apartment of a man in his late fifties. He is wearing a string vest that doesn't fit his podgy structure. He has jeans that don't button up well and is wearing an obvious blond wig on his head. Propaganda and his associate, Mr. Sagar, swing the door open. 'So what's up?' The man asks. 'WHAT'S UP!?' Sagar screams. Propaganda simply shakes his head in disbelief. 'Ugh… Yeah.' The man splutters. 'Brother Donaldbain tells me you are having serious doubts about Christianity.' Sagar sighs. 'Oh, Yeah…' The man groans. 'Brother Friar. This is a serious matter.' Sagar informs the man whom we now know as Friar. The scene fades to black then up again, we see Sagar slumped over one side of the table and Friar on the other, Propaganda is striding back and forth in white text we see '1 hour later!' The man is slouched surrounded by a wall of crushed Miller cans. 'It just seems… well pointless?' Friar groans. 'Pointless!?' Sagar splutters. 'YOU'RE CALLING CHRISTIANITY POINTLESS!!!' Sagar raises off his seat, Propaganda quickly steps in and lightly places his hand on the shoulder of the enraged preacher. 'Calm my brother…' Propaganda respires. 'Calm.' Sagar places his behind back in his seat. 'Remember Mozambique '99?' Sagar enquires. 'Ugh Yeah…' Friar mumbles whilst rubbing his brow with his forearm. 'The poor homeless refugee's in need of helping hand, we helped them but who's hand did we use?' 'We didn't, we just preached to them while they were being carried away by flood wat--' Friar is interrupted. 'That's right the hand of the father…' Sagar touches his forehead with his right hand '…the son…' he touches the bottom of his sternum '…and the holy ghost.' He touches both his shoulders. Then kisses his hand. 'But--' Friar tries to speak again but is interrupted. 'Don't you remember the feeling of righteousness?' Sagar asks. 'Well it w…' Friar groans but is interrupted again, this time by the power himself. 'Can I have some toast?' God's ultimatum asks. 'Sure… I suppose.' Friar groans with out paying much attention. 'Well it w-- OH SHIT NO!' Sagar gasps at Friar's foul mouth, but he has more important things to worry about. 'DON'T TOUCH THE TOASTER…' It was too late. 'Zap! Pow! Crackle!' The toaster has electrocuted propaganda. The scene blurs away to white. Suddenly harp music begins to play. 'W… Where am I?' Proppa-G asks. We see nothing but a magical, enchanted, silver gate, sparkling and resting on the clouds. Out of cloud Propaganda steps out as if he has been climbing up some stairs. 'Oh my…' God's ultimatum gasps at the extravagant gate. Then suddenly they are opened to a loud creaking noise. The camera then fixes on the feet of a figure behind the gates. He wears brown sandals over his tanned skin, a long white gown that reaches his ankles, and we see distinct hole marks in his wrists, we then see an almost pointy brown beard and long brown hair. It must be the saviour himself, the one and only, Jesus Christ. 'Jesus! I bow before though in all graciousness.' Propaganda kneels down and kisses the hand of Christ. 'May I have your name my child.' Jesus asks in an angelic tone. 'Atlanta Johnson* my saviour.' Propaganda murmurs in disbelief of whose presence he's in. 'You are the Johnson child?' Jesus asks. 'Yes sir.' Propaganda answers. 'Then you do not belong here.' Jesus commands. 'No… not Heck! NO JESUS! NO! PLEASE!' Propaganda pleads with the almighty being. 'You belong on earth my brother.' Jesus points to the cloud in which Propaganda came from. 'You have true righteousness. You must delivers god's message, as his messenger as… a Prophet!' 'L… L… Like you?' God's ultimatum stutters. 'Yes.' Jesus nods 'Like me.' 'Then I shall go.' Propaganda rises to his feet. 'But when my job is done, make sure you have a spot waiting for me. 'I shall my brother don't you worry.' Jesus encourages Propaganda. Propaganda strides down the cloud and the scene suddenly cuts to black. Suddenly we see a blurred picture then it goes back to black, then slowly the picture is exposed more. (As if it were the eyes of someone lying down) The camera is looking up to Eric Sagar and Friar. The camera then changes to see Propaganda is a hospital bed with Friar and Sagar at his side, Propaganda sits up. 'Don't strain my brother.' Eric tells Propaganda. He ignores this and grabs his glasses from his bedside table. And puts them on. 'You seem fine.' Friar gaps. 'Doctor's said we nearly lost you.' 'Fine?' Propaganda scoffs. 'I'm brilliant.' Propaganda leaps out of the hospital bed to reveal he is wearing a hospital gown. He climbs up on the bed. 'I met him…' Propaganda states. 'Who?' Sagar asks. 'Jesus.' Propaganda replies. 'Jesus!?' Friar gasps. 'Indeed, he told me that I was a prophet, that I have been chosen especially to spread the word of god, just as I am doing. My faith is no longer blotched.' 'See…' Sagar turns to Friar. 'You were just being Lazy, for if Christianity was pointless why would this have happened?' 'You're right.' Friar states. 'Thank you Eric, thank you Propaganda. I am now a full pledged Christian again!' 'Yey!' Sagar cheers. 'And I… I am a messenger of God.' Propaganda proudly roars. 'I WILL PROPHESIES ALL OVER O… S… W!' The scene fades to black/
