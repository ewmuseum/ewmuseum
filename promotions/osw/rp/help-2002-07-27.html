Date: Thursday, July 27, 2002 Dear Angeldust, Dear Mr. Casey, Dear To Whom It May Concern: What is life really about? The quesiton can't be answered simply with a response. You can't really understand what life is about until you've had some time to experience it. Experience doesn't come from a trek across the Antarctic, or descending from a plane with nothing but a parachute on your back. Life is about experiencing the ups and downs, the trials and triumphs. And to tell the truth, the only reason I'm writing to you is that I have nobody else left to write to. My family is all gone. Even if they are not physically gone, mentally they couldn't be more out of it. I only have one grandparent left, but she's too far away to even talk to most of the time. Even if I could, she can barely remember my name half the time. My sister has forgotten I exist, even though we live all of two doors apart. I have no close aunts or uncles, and I am the outcast of everyone else in my family that doesn't fall into the previous catergories. But besides that, we meet the parents. Mom works every night, and doesn't so much care for the fact that I want to be independant of her, that I don't want to live in the shadows of the life she is trying to create for me. She is such a hypocrite, I can't stand it, but if I don't follow her rules then I can't do anything at all. She thinks she understands me, but she really just favors my sister all the time. It's been a while since she told me she loved me.... On to my father. The stupid bastard. He spent the first fifteen years of my life beating the crap out of me in a drunken haze, and all of a sudden he goes ill; suddenly i'm supposed to forget the first fifteen years of life. Granted I'll put the stupid show on for his family, but I'm never going to forgive him. And as he grows mentally stronger, I think he is going to realize this more and more. I needed someone to reach out to. I figured someone who overcame adversity like you could understand the predicament I'm in. You overcame adversity in your life just like I did, you battled the odds and won. Now you're successful, and what words are coming out of the doubter's mouths? There are none; they all shut up. I want to shut people up, Nick. I want to make them green with envy as I take over the world. You'll see, Nick. You'll see. Number One ANC Fan 4Ever, Shawn McCormick. *** 'Oh my God...that is so sad,' Christy said, as the tear started to roll down her cheek. 'That poor little guy has nobody to reach out to when he needs it most.' Christy quickly buried her hands and face on Nick's shoulder, as salty tears flowed like tap water. Nick just sat there; staring at the letter. Suddenly, it hit him. Maybe destroying Propaganda to win the Old School Wrestling International Title wasn't the most important thing on Earth. Maybe he shouldn't be worrying so much about Lillith and Christy and Vampir and The Cult. Maybe he should even reach out and help the poor child. But for some reason, Nick had a sinking feeling in his stomach. It wasn't the Taco Bell he had for lunch, it was more of a feeling of anxiety deep in the pit of his stomach; and deep in the pit of his heart. He was tired of leading the sheep on so innocently to slaughter, even though none had been slaughtered yet. He was just setting them up for it, which probably made him feel even worse. Now, all of his emotions are just bottled up inside Reading the letter sent chills down his spine, gave him goosebumps on his arms. Shawn's story wasn't the saddest he had ever heard, but nobody had ever really reached out to Nick before. After all, he was just a professional wrestler, and we all know they aren't the brightest of the bunch. But the fact that he trusted Nick more than his friends actually meant something to him. Maybe, he was getting through to his fans. 'I'll help you Shawn,' he mumbled.'I'll help you.' Christy slid down to the end of the couch again. 'What are you gonna do, Nick?' Clueless, once again. The blonde set in. 'I don't know, I just feel so terrible.' Christy twirled her hair with her finger, thinking deeply about the things Nick could do to help this poor little boy. 'Win it for him.' 'What?' Christy cracked a smile from behind her blonde locks. 'Win the title for him. He said he's your number one fan, so win the title and dedicate it to him.' 'How does that help him though?' Nick shot back. 'Well....' Christy had nothing. Nick shoved his face into his cupped hands. 'Besides, that shit doesn't matter to me anymore. Who cares about winning when there are people like out there suffering?' 'My goodness! Is Angeldust getting a concience?' Christy laughed. He glared across the room at her. 'Forget you.' 'What?' Another glare. 'I'll help him. Don't you worry.'
