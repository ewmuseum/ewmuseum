Post SLAM! Happenings

4 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 4th 2002 The following has been rated NC-17: Parental Guidance is Strictly Forbidden The scene opens up to the parking garage outside the arena. Slam is a post memory and now fans flock to the parking garage hoping to get a glimpse of their favorite stars. Rhett Locke steps out of the door and immediately he begins to sign autographs before he gets into his limo and drives off. Just then the door swings open again and the crowd begins to yell and scream as two men, both in blue jeans and pull over sweat shirts, step out. Silence and Graphic Violence sneer at the crowd and try and make their way to their car. 'You guys suck! I mean you really suck!' says a fat old man who is instantly cheered on by the rest of the crowd. 'Oh really old man? Like we haven’t heard that bullshit before. Their isn’t a fuckin’ fool out here who is worthy enough to lick my boots let alone tie them.' Silence replies as he pushes a college aged kid out of his way. Graphic Violence glares at the boy. “Listen kid, why don’t you go pop your zits and grow some hair on your balls. Until then you shouldn’t be speakin’ to either of us. Opening your mouth like that is going to get you hurt.” The college kid pushes GV back. 'What? You guys think you can do whatever you want? I bet me and Chuck here can take you to right now!' The crowd begins cheering the two queerish college kids on. 'That’s right Billy, we can take these chumps.' Silence grabs Chuck by the collar and raises him off the ground. 'Billy and Chuck? Like the gay wrestling duo right? I hoped you told your mom what casket you would like because I am about to spend another night in jail.' Graphic Violence quickly head butts Billy. “I told you opening your mouth would get your hurt, but putting your hands on me. That’ll get you sent to the hospital.” Graphic Violence proceeds to place his massive hands around the boys’ neck. He lifts him up and sends him crashing down on the hood of a parked car with a massive powerbomb. Graphic Violence then drags the boy off the hood of the car and grabs his face. Then will extreme force he sends the boys head backwards and into the drivers side window of the car. Billy immediately hits the floor as blood trickles down from the back of his head. “I told you kid, a one way ticket to the emergency room.” Silence shoves Chuck into the wall and places a well done roundhouse kick into Chuck’s midsection. Chuck bends over and swings a wild punch at Silence who just lets the punch hit him across the face. 'I like blood you fuckin’ fool!' And with that Silence whips the blood from his lip and nails Chuck we a spinning fist to the side of the head. 'You see what happens when you pick a fight with a guy who can take you apart with his feet? I’m a fuckin’ martial artist expert you fuckin’ moron and now you have to fuckin’ die!' Silence clasps both of his hands around the kids’ throat and lifts him into the air and then drops him with a Baldo Bomb! He then walks over and picks up a piece of sheet metal on the ground where they are repairing the wall. He then walks back over to Chuck, jumps into the air places the metal underneath him and nails Chuck with an Arabian Facebuster! 'Now get up you fuck!' “They’re not going to get up Silence. These two punks just learned an important lesson. Don’t pick a fight you can’t win, and don’t open up your mouth to NC-17. You two punks are just like what we’re going to do to the rest of the OSW so called superstars.” Graphic Violence gives one last kick for good measure. “They’re all going to bow before us, one way or another.” All of a sudden the sirens begin and sound and Reagan comes out the back door screaming at the two to get back here. GV and Silence don’t pay attention as they get into their silver cadi and drive off. The following was rated NC-17: Parental Guidance is Strictly Forbidden
