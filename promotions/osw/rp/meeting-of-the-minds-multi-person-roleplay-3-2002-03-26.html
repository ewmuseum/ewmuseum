Meeting of the Minds (Multi-Person Roleplay 3)

26 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-26-02 Starring: Jesse Ramey from Old School Wrestling as Rob Van Attitude Jordan Crazy from New True Internet Wrestling as Devon “Crazy” Dudley Bubba McCoy from GWF as Bubba Mac Dudley The door slams shut to the snack room. We see Devon Dudley munching away at his sandwich, probably bought from one of the Quick Stores outside the arena. From around our view walks RVA. He looks at Devon and then slowly takes a seat on the opposite side of him. “Lamen again?” Devon asks. “Yeah, how could you have ever guessed?” Attitude says with a snicker. “Dude, you’ve got to learn to let him just slide off your back. Don’t you know that’s the best thing you could ever do?” Devon asks. “You’ve told me that before man. Doesn’t seem to work all that much.” Attitude says. “Probably because you’ve fried your mind with so much damn weed.” Devon says and then begins to laugh. “Well, that could play a factor in all of this, but very doubtful.” Attitude says laughing. The sound of the door opening and shutting is heard again, but this time it is accompanied by a voice. “Devon!” A short pause, “I got the sandwich!” Everyone in the room begins to crack up laughing as Bubba Mac Dudley walks in and sits down at the table to the right of Attitude and to the left of Devon, who is still diving into his sandwich. “Attitude? To what honor do we owe this visit?” He stops and thinks for a moment. “Lamen?” “You got that right my fine pudgy friend. Indeed you did get that one correct.” Attitude says as he begins to bang his head on the table. Devon looks across at Attitude, “Now, brother, don’t go beating yourself to death. You’ve got a match with this man tomorrow night, and you’re going to beat him from pillar to post! Then you’re going to pick up and lift him high into the sky…” Devon is cut off. “Um, Devon. How am I going to do that?” Attitude asks, but answers his own question. “I can’t! He’s bigger than me.” “Oh, well, we’ll just interfere and help you kill that sum bitch.” Devon shrugs and digs back into his sandwich. Attitude’s eyes light up. “That may just work. How about we do this one totally out of character and show that little bastard what a real butt kicking is.” Bubba gets an interested look on his, “It sound pretty good to me. That little pipsqueak needs to get what he has coming to him.” “Wait,” Devon says as he brings his face out of the more than half eaten sandwich, “Couldn’t we get fined or fired for doing this?” “We’re some of the best talent that this place has to offer. They’ll not screw up over like that.” Attitude says confidently. “You’re right!” Devon says excitedly. “Damn straight, you can count us in.” Bubba says. “Testify, Bubba. Testify!” Devon says. “Then it’s settled. We can converse about this latter on, but for now I’ve got to go and get something straightened out.” Attitude says as he stands up and walks out of the room. Bubba slams his fist down on the table and looks at Devon, “You sunk my battleship!” ~To Be Continued~
