Contact With The 'Cracka' (Part 3 of 6 in Breaking the Benefit)

8 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Mar. 8, 2002 Rhett Locke strolled through the heart of Portland, non-chalantly, something he's done thousands of times in the past on his way to Dom's pub. Clad in leathers of black, meshing with his pale skin, giving him a slight gothic look as he looked above to the clear heavens. Thoughts were soon clearing from his mind as he closed his eyes. Nothing mattered at that moment, not a thing in the world. Though, the feeling would surely be short lived as the faint sound of Bethoven intrupted his brief meditation, and he reached to his waist. A slight sigh escaped his lips as he pulled the cell phone's antenna out, and flipped it open. 'Hello.' A few seconds of silence, that seemed like hours. Rhett never was a patient man. 'Hel-' 'Shut up.' If Rhett expressed physically what his mind thought, his mouth would've dropped to the concrete sidewalk. Though, it soon slipped his mind, and a sneer trailblazed across his face. Malicious and vile words ran through his mind, though he spoke none. Five, six seconds passed as he thought, before simply answering. 'Blow me.' 'Blow yo'self, cracka.' Rhett dropped to silence, before simply giving into the caller. 'What do you want, guy?' 'I believe the question is what tha fuck do you want. Live or die?' 'Wha-' 'Live or die?' '...Live.' The voice on the other end chuckled hoarsely, and Rhett stood in utter confusion as he leaned up against the graphiti painted brick wall of a tenament block. 'Good choice, cracka. Now listen...meet me at tha corner of 35th street, you got that?' '...Fuck you.' 'You wanna say that again?' 'Fuck you!' It was almost as though a fuse blew in the mind of the caller, as he screamed in a rage of fury. In the back-ground you could hear the faint sound of crashing glass, and the man on the other end was inhaling and exhaling deeply. 'Bad move, cracka. Ya meet me at 35th street, or you're gonna find yo'self swallowing the end of a .99, you got that?' Click, dialtone. It took a little while for Rhett Locke to soak in what just happened. Once he did, he couldn't do much. Infact, he could only mutter... 'Fuck.'
