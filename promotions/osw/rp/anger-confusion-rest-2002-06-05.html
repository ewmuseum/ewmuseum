Anger, confusion, rest.

5 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 5, 2002 “Hey fuck head,” Reno Davis snapped as he approached the FUBU shirted man exiting Aimz’s hospital room, “You gotta some sort of problem?” The spiky brown haired man stopped his venture away from Aimz’s room and turned to look at whoever just spoke to him. He saw a very tired looking man with bright red hair and pupil-less eyes that made him shiver. He didn’t recognize the man, whom he also realized had a half drunk Colt 45 in his right hand. “Who the hell are you? And are you allowed to bring liquor into a hospital?” The man returned. Reno picked up from the last part of Amy’s conversation that the man’s name was Jason Dexter, an ex-boyfriend who cheated on her. That’s a crime enough as it is, but the fact that he taunted Aimz just pissed Reno off at all extents. Then again, the drunkenness didn’t help. “I’m Reno fucking Davis, and I don’t like you. Do you know what that means?” Reno asked Jason, who gave a puzzled look in reply. “Hey, I’ll sue for assault!” Jason began, but Reno took a swig of Colt and smashed the bottle over Jason’s spiked head. Golden liquid shot from the bottle and drenched Dexter’s New York Yankee visor. The little bitch went down right away, leaving Reno unsatisfied. A few nurses tended to Jason immediately and Reno left before they got a good look at him, slipping into Amy’s room. She sat there on her bed, not exactly looking great. Reno attempted to speak, but choked out and just glared on. ''Didn't expect to see you here....at all.'' Amy said, looking at Reno with an odd expression. 'Yeah...' Reno nods, looking out into the hallway at something, and then back at Aimz, 'What happened?' ''Just now? During Slam? When Corg sliced my hand up? God, where should I start?'' Amy snapped back, irately. Reno's head sagged and he just stared at the floor. He opened his mouth to speak, a breath of air inhaling, only to exhale and have him slump his shoulders. ''What's wrong? Sorry for tossing a lot at you there...I know you've been going through a lot of shit, even though I can't understand any of it.'' She was seriously concerned. 'Yeah...I've been trying to figure out...stuff. I'm sorry I got you hurt,” Amy missed Reno’s statement. ''You didn't get me hurt, Corg got me hurt...but the way you've been lately is pretty painful to see. I just wanna know, and I have a right to...what's come into you, Reno?'' Reno shook his head a little bit, still looking at the floor, 'He got you hurt...but it was because of me...He's done it before and he'll do it again.' Reno's tone came off as almost a slurred mumble. Aimz noticed the broken Colt 45 bottle in Reno's right hand. ''Reno...you've been drinking? That's not you at all...can you just try to tell me what's happening? Maybe I can help you through this shit somehow...and why did you break your bottle?'' Reno broke a small smile, and then looked out in the hall and back at Aimz, 'I just needed to think. That's all. I'm not going to let anyone hurt you or ZaC ever again. I promise. I won't let him.' Again, Amy didn’t realize whom Reno meant by ‘him’. ''Who...Corg? Or that jackass who was just in here? That was just an idiot ex-boyfriend in the long line of morons who make it a habit to hurt me...he's harmless, really. Wait a minute...you didn't...jeez, Reno...you're really getting violent. Maybe you should talk about this stuff...fewer people would get hurt...'' Reno was lost with the many questions and replied to the first, 'Yeah...Corg and that guy. That's him. He won't hurt you anymore.' Reno decided that Amy wouldn’t understand that someone more powerful than anyone else on earth was out to get him, and lied. ''You're drunk. Lay down. It might not be the brightest idea I've ever had, but I don't trust you to stay up on those flimsy chairs. You really need to sleep this off...did you check out of your hotel already?'' 'Sleeping is a waste of time...' Reno said as he wandered over towards Aimz's bed. He flopped down over her lap and looked up into her eyes, 'Where's ZaC?' ''Probably asleep in the closet over there...he was having a great time rifling through my bags a few minutes before you got here. He's weakened, but doing fine as far as I can tell,” she smiled, “I think I might have to tag along with you for a while more...for the monkey, of course...hehe, he seems to like me.'' Reno's eyes began to shut and he muttered out, 'Yeah, me too...' Then Reno passed out. Amy gave a puzzled look after Reno’s comment and just ran her hand through his hair. Reno was already fast asleep, as he hadn’t slept in days. She heard Jason Dexter’s moaning from outside the room and felt an odd, dark feeling come over her. Reno was becoming dangerous, maybe even too dangerous for Ivan Stanislav to handle...
