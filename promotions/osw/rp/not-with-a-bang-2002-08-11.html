Not with a bang...

11 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08-11-2002 A shadeless lamp lies on it's side on a grey rug, the naked lightbulb within shedding the only illumination in the room. Scattered around it are the broken shards of several beer bottles, what's left of their contents staining the rung with a deep amber, combinging to a color resembling urine. Just outside the semi-cricle of light, Lillith lays on her side. Her left arm is palm-up, flat on the floor, and criss-crossed with thin red lines of blood. Her right holds a large glass shard, its edges digging into her fingertips, drawying yet more blood. Her black hair is eskew is all directions, including over her face, so that just her ice-blue eyes are all that is distinguisable; eyes which shine with tears. 'All for nothing,' she whispers to herself, 'all for nothing...' Her voice trails off as she stares at her left wrist, at the solid blue line that traces its way up her arm. She stares, and knows how easy this can be resolved. Just a quick moovement of her hand, the sharp stining pain of the laceration coursing through her body, and the slow, sweet darkness falling over her as her blood pours out of her body. not with a bang, but a whisper. Isn't that how the story goes? 'What has become of me?' she asks the darkness. 'I'm all alone. Parents dead. No friends. Just my heart beating in the darkness, crying out for help. But help won't come... won't.... help....' Her right hand moves of it's own volition, pressing the shard of glass to her exposed flesh, just enough to draw a little blood. A short intake of breath punctuates the cut, and reasons returns to Lillith's eyes as she realizes just how close she is. ''To die... to sleep... perchance, to dream..?'' But what would that eternal slumber bring? Would it be dreams of sweet splender, or nightmares of unimaginable horrors. She didn't know. She couldn't know. Indecision. Life balanced on the edge of a razor, on the edge of a broken piece of glass. Frozen in time, frozen in mind. A single tear traced down Lillith's cheek. She had to choose. Face her fears, face her life. Face her death? She looked again at the shard, poised at the bring of her existance. '... a whisper....' She chose.
