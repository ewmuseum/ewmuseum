Enemies By Chance / 2B / Kory Who?

9 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sunday, June 9th, 2002 'Hello?' He tried to be nice. He tried to be polite. But that damned woman just sat behind the counter, filing her nails. She obviously didn't give a shit about who was standing in front of her, and by all means, she probably shouldn't. Nick knows if he was behind the counter he would probably have that very same attitude; but he wasn't behind the counter, he was standing in front of it with a six-inch gash in the back of his head. People stared as he tried to get the attention of the girl, but all resorts seemed lost. 'I didn't want it to come to this...' he said, removing the bloody shirt from the back of his head. 'LISTEN!' he said, shoving the blood-ridden shirt within two inches of the face of the girl. She screamed, and damn near fell all the way back in her chair. 'What the hell is wrong with you!' Nick laughed to himself, and put his hands on his hips. 'Hmm...let's think about this for a second...THERE'S A GODDAMN HOLE IN MY HEAD!' 'Well then,' the secretary's smile curled around her lips, 'just fill these papers out, and you are good to go.' She dropped a stack of papers on the desk, and laughed sadistically; Nick could have spent a few hours filling them out, but instead flipped the top one over. On the back he wrote 'I Will Not Sue (signed) Nicholai Casey.' 'There. That's all you need to know. Anything else, I'm on TV every week, it's not like I'm going anywhere. If you need more info, just call Porter.' He was done with them, as he flipped the pen on the desk. He grabbed a nurse and she followed him into room 718. *** 'Wow, this is pretty bad.' Nick quickly turned his head around. He looked the nurse, clad in pink scrubs, square in the eye and said boldy, 'I am not going on the shelf.' 'Oh no, Mr. Casey. We just need a real doctor to put these in. I'll be right back.' Right, Nick thought. He had only been here an hour so far and done nothing but sit around. He was Nick Fuckin' Casey, for chrissakes. 'Well, I can't believe it!' Angeldust already knew he had a wrestling fan coming in. And honest to God, he'd rather have that giant-preist-like fuck walk in the room instead of a wrestling fan doctor right this moment. 'I didn't believe it when I read the chart, but it's really you! This is such an honor to be suturing your head!' 'Yeah, well, it's great to be here Leno...' The doctor hummed along happily as he put his gloves on. 'So how did this happen? Jack Miller finally get the better of you?' Casey just stared him down. 'What the fuck is that supposed to mean?' Nick responded. 'It was a sneak attack, you actually think I'd let Miller, a fucking Five Star REJECT get the better of me? You're out of your fuckin' mind, bro.' Not knowing what to say, the doctor just kind of went about his work, preparing to suture the area. 'Now, first we'll have to shave the area...' Nick jumped up again. 'The hell you will.' 'Nick, I can't suture with hair in the way...' 'Yeah, and I need my hair for the business! When you're this good-looking you just don't tamper with it.' Nick reached down and fumbled through his jeans. He founds his wallet, and pulled out four fifty-dollar bills. 'I will give you two-hundred dollars right now to not shave it. I already signed that I wouldn't sue...' He sighed. 'Fine.' As he began, a nurse came, this one a small Asian girl dressed in blue scrubs. She scruffled her shoes against the ground as she walked against the ground. 'Doctor Fisher, we have another wrestler coming in.' 'Wow!' the doctor shrieked. 'This is like my lucky day.' ANC rolled his eyes. 'Oh yeah...just grand.' Fisher nearly jumped out of his suit. 'So who is it?' 'Umm...I don't know, he said he was fighting Axhole or something like that?' Dr. Fisher looked perplexed. 'Axtell?' 'Hey, why not?' the nurse replied. Nick didn't know who is was, but he didn't want to take the risk of getting attacked again. He got off the suture table, string hanging off of his head. He grabbed a chair from the next room over, and hid next to the door. Suddenly, the door to the suture room swung open, and Nick swung... ...and missed. He ducked. 'WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU DOING!?' A girls voice? Oh shit, it was Christy! 'You crazy asshole!' she shouted, chasing after Nick. However, securtiy was quick to grab her and subdue her. 'I will get you for that one you dick!' 'Yeah....ummm...my bad?' How do you really explain nearly taking off your girl's head with a steel chair? I guess it's kind of impossible. 'Okay, I'm good.' Christy said. 'By the way, some other dude with an OSW shirt is out there.' 'Who?' She shrugged her shoulders. 'Kory Storm or something like that?' 'Who the fuck is Kory Storm?' It's funny when people hear what they want to...
