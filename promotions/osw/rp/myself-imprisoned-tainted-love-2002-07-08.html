Myself, Imprisoned: Tainted Love

8 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 8, 2002 “Hello?” “Hey…” “Holy shit! Where have you been? I’ve been worried sick about you!” “Oh, you know, I’ve been around.” I said. “Around? What the fuck do you mean? I’ve been worried, all this time, thinking that something serious might’ve happened to you, and all you have to say is that you’ve been ‘around’?” Shit, she’s a real firebird. Yep, that’s my girlfriend alright, always getting worried and emotional. But I guess that’s how it goes for most women… is Shyme even old enough to be considered a woman? I don’t know, who cares. Regardless, I love her, and it was good to finally hear her voice. “Yeah, I’ve been around, you know… a few trips to the station, and now I’ve ended up in prison. But, thanks for the support.” I said sarcastically, trying to get her to feel sorry for me. A long pause, I assumed Shyme’s jaw had dropped. “Well it’s good to finally hear your voice, and I’m glad you’re okay and all, but... why are you in prison?” She wasn’t too worried, and neither was I. We both knew I’d get out, as we always do. “Oh, well, you know I did try to rape Aimz, and I attempted to kill her. Then on Saturday, I stabbed the slut. Nothing has ever pleased me more than to torture her… besides our sex, of course.” “I told you that breaking into her locker room was a bad idea!” She said, totally ignoring my part about the sex. “And then you had to go and stab her, what the fuck?!” “Calm down, I didn’t actually stab her. Reno broke in and tackled me, I had the knife pinned up against Aimz’ throat and when Reno hit me, I lost control and the knife was injected straight into Aimz’ shoulder.” “Oooh, how awful. That must’ve hurt Aimz like hell.” “How awful? What the hell, while Aimz is sitting comfortably at home, I’m here in this rotting shithole while some guy named Gregg tries to feel me up!” I never did get in trouble for killing Gregg, I don’t know why… I think the guards thought he committed suicide or something. Those dumbasses didn’t even check for finger prints, I don’t think they liked him too much. “A guy tried feeling you up?” I could tell she was holding back a load full of giggles. I couldn’t blame her, as I too thought it was pretty funny. “Yeah, he stuck his hand up my anus then touched my penis. By then I had enough, so I ‘dissed’ him, and he tried killing me. But of course, I beat the shit out of the stupid fat fuck, and I killed him, “ I said. “But no big deal.” “So now what?” “Oh, they figured he committed suicide or some stupid shit like that.” “Well, fuck him… how are you feeling?” “Tired. A little lonely, I guess. You were my only phone call, you know.” “Ooooh, how sweet” She said, sympathetically. “Well yeah, who else would I call?” “You could’ve called your brother.” “My brother… Rhett? What the fuck good would that do?” “I don’t know, maybe you two could reminisce.” “Yeah… right. Rhett hates me, I don’t care for him, it would be pointless.” “That’s why he helped you win your cruiserweight title.” “First of all, that title means shit to me, and second, he didn’t help me one fucking bit.” Rhett… helping me? Please. “I won that title, without Rhett’s help. Rhett just wanted to be in the spotlight, as he always does. He wanted to be the ‘hero’ which he tries so hard not to be.” “You’ve never given him a chance.” “Shyme, you don’t know him like I do. I grew up with him, he never did like me, and that will never change.” “Whatever…” I thought I had been a little hard on her, after all, she was just trying to help me. “Sorry,” I said. “It’s just that I’m kinda ‘touchy’ when talking about Rhett.” “I know, I understand.” “Well, I’m feeling kinda hyped right now, I think I’m gonna go kick some ass in the cafeteria.” “I love you…” “I know, I love you too. I’ll see you soon, you have my word. Don’t worry about me, just keep feeding our rabbits.” And with that, she hung up. A rather emotionally conversation, but, it was my only phone call, what can I say?
