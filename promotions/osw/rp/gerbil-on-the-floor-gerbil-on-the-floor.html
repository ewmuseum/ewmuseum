GERBIL ON THE FLOOR! GERBIL ON THE FLOOR!

Toomes walks into the white emergency room of the Phoenix Memorial Hospital, dressed in his tan trenchcoat and black fedora, a manilla envelop clutched in his hand. His nose, broken by that fucker Ivan Stanislav, is bandaged. His eyes, red from the pain, are narrowed as he walks into the white tiled ER. He walks up to the reception desk. “Excuse me. I need to see Doctor Barnes, please.” Toomes says quietly to the fat white woman in colorful flower print. “One moment, please.” She says, paging the doctor. Toomes waits, tapping his foot, for Dr. Barnes. Eventually, a tall, muscular doctor with, for some reason, really great hair, walks out of a pair of peach colored double doors, and walks up to Toomes. “Hello there, Mister Toomes…” he says. “Let us dispense with the pleasantries, Doctor. You know why I am here?” Toomes says in his nasa-voice, thanks to the broken nose. “Yes. You wanted information on Howard Porter. Let’s find someplace more quiet to discuss this.” Toomes nods. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- It was 2 o’clock in the morning when Howard Porter staggered into the entrance, his pants around his ankles, a look of profound pain on his face. The nurses put him on his stomach and wheeled him into the ER, where Doctor James Barnes was on duty. Apparently, he had… fallen on something. Barnes, his eyes red and irritated from his allergies and lack of sleep, brushed aside the fabric curtain and stepped into the area where Howard Porter was lying, a nurse trying to hold back laughter as she took his blood pressure. “Sir, just… *stifles laughter* Just remain calm, sir… Here’s Doctor Barnes…” the nurse said, releasing the valve on the blood pressure cuff. In the flourscent light of the ER, the orange colored Habitat Tube, at least a foot long, jutted out from underneath the hospital jonnie, the tell-tale fingerprints and smears of Vaseline smudging the orange translucent plastic tubing. Howard Porter clutched the hospital bed frame nervously. “I was just getting out of my shower…*winces* And… I guess I must of slipped onto something…” Barnes nodded, tired, having heard this story in various permutations many times before. It was amazing how many men left cylinder-shaped objects, greased up with a non-water soluable petroleum-based lubrication, just lying around the bathroom. “Nurse, I think I can take of this… Why don’t you go get us some gauze and some bandages for Mister Porter, here?” The nurse nodded, trying to repress a guffaw, but fails. “I SLIPPED ON IT, OKAY? I SLIPPED ON IT!” Porter screamed. A few gales of laughter erupted elsewhere in the ER. “Calm down, Mister Porter. It’s all right… Now, let’s see what we’re looking at here.” Barnes said soothingly. Porter clutched the metal hospital bed frame, his knuckles white with terror and embarrassment. Outside of the concealing protective curtain, a female voice yelled “THE GUY IN THREE STUCK A HABITRAIL UP HIS ASS! HAH-HAH!” Porter flopped around on the bed, but Barnes slapped him on the ass to quiet him down. “Now, sir, if I am going to extract this foreign object, you need to remain calm and lie still. Do you understand?” Porter sobbed. “Just get it out of me, okay?... And did you just spank me?” “Yes.” “Oh, okay.” Barnes shined a penlight into the crevice, and thoughtfully made sounds of contemplation. “Yes. You definitely have a Habitrail up your ass. Let’s see here… Now I’m going to try to extract it, so I’m gonna need you to be a brave guy for me here, ‘kay?” Porter sobbed. “Yes… It won’t hurt, will it – YAAAAAAAAGH!” Barnes clutched at it, but it wouldn’t budge, something having formed a vacuum seal inside of his rectum. “Hmm.” He muttered. “Let’s see if I can twist it out…” Outside of the curtained-off area, everyone seemed to stop in front of it. Nurses and patients in wheelchairs all stopped in front of bed 3, where, rumor had it, a man had a Habitrail up his ass. “Mommy?” a little six year old boy with a dinner fork lodged in his forehead asked. “Why would anyone put something up their butt?” “Quiet, honey…” she began, but then Porter began screaming again. “I SLIPPED ON IT COMING OUT OF THE SHOWER! OKAY? I SLIPPED COMING OUT OF THE SHOWER!” he brayed as Dr. Barnes tried twisting the Habitrail in his rectum, to possibly rupture the seal that had formed between the plastic and the flesh inside his colon. “Please, sir. This is nothing. Did you know that I once had to extract seven dead gerbils from a man’s rectum once? This is no problem at all. Now breathe deep now…” “YAAAAAAAAAAAAGH!” -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- “Now, you’re saying Howard Porter had stuck a 12 inch long Habitrail tube up his ass… Now why would a man do something like that?” Toomes asked. Barnes flashed a perfect smile. “Well, as a gay man myself, I know the pleasurable sensation that phallic objects inserted into a man’s rectum cause. However, the patient inserted something that in this case isn’t really recommended into his rectum, and since the close proximity of the plastic and the flesh formed a seal, it was very difficult to remove.” Toomes scratches his head. “Okay, but why would it form a seal? I mean, tubes are hollow, not like a coke bottle of something you hear in an urban legend…” “Well, it turns out…” -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- “GAAA! GERBIL ON THE FLOOR! GERBIL ON THE FLOOR! EVERYBODY MOVE!” The entire ER exploded as nurses and patients scrambled for cover as the enraged, Vaseline coated, shaved and clipped gerbil scurried out of Bed 3’s area, and began charging at people’s feet. Behind the curtain, there is the silhouette of Dr. Barnes pulling the 12 inch Habitrail tube free from Porter’s rectum with a loud pop. “Okay, sir, it’s free… Hey, wait!” he cries. Porter, clad only in his hospital jonnie, scrabbles out of the bed and bursts through the curtain. “SCRAPPY! I’M SORRY! I’M SO SORRY! I DIDN’T KNOW YOU WERE IN THERE!” Porter screams, looking around frantically for the lubricated gerbil. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- “And then, he ran off without paying his bill. Cheapskate, really.” Barnes sighs. “I think I’ve heard enough. You’re sure this was Howard Porter?” Toomes asks. “Oh yes. I got a good look at him. It was definitly him. Plus, the driver's license was a dead giveaway.' Barnes comments. Toomes hands over the manilla envelope to Dr. Barnes. 'There you go. You've earned this.' Barnes nods. 'Yeah, if you can make this public, maybe it will shame him into paying his bill.' Toomes chuckles. 'Oh, I wouldn't worry about that.' Toomes walks away from the doctor, chuckling. 'Ivan... Now it's your turn.'
