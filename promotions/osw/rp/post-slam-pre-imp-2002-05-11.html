Post-Slam, Pre-Imp.

11 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 11th, 2002 Look up... Look WAY up... No Big Friendly Giant there! What a gip...whenever Amy looked up, Ivan Stanislav was always standing right there, ready to make her bleed! She beat Reno Davis, she was advancing in the Cruiserweight Title tournament...wooohooo!!! But what was the price of a nice, shiny new victory? A few nice, shiny bruises...about seven stitches and, as usual, aches and pains up the ying-yang! She didn't have a much room for celebration, not even the next day...all she had fit into her schedule was when to take another two Advil, when to change the bandage on her forehead and when to soak in a hot bath with a nice, big container of Epsom Salts. ''OOWWWWWIIIIIIIIE!!!!'' Forget wishing for the BFG instead of the Russian Bear...she needed a little PCP, LSD or maybe E? Not anymore! Amy was gonna stay away from that stuff, all the power to her, too...she'd been working hard to keep off of those killers. It was almost a blessing that Ivan was there to beat the hell out of her, constantly getting her mind off of non-perscription drugs... Well, he at least won her the match... Again came the cost...it just wasn't fun to think about. ''Oh, that hurts?'' Even Advil wasn't helping at this point...she felt like every bone in her body was broken, but she was relentless...Campbell didn't miss the flight to the next Slam, she took it with Reno. After that, she laid alone in her hotel room, counting every tile on the ceiling and then dividing that number by various others...yup, she was certainly nerdin' it up and geekin' it out without 'the drugzzzzzzzz'. ''Yes!!!!!!!!!'' But after that, Bre had come...she was still there, and wasn't helping. ''Well...what if I poke here?'' ''DAMN YOU!!!'' ''Hehe...that spot looks a tad tender!'' Being her best friend and all, Bre shoved a nail at a bruise on Amy's shoulder. Campbell swatted the dirty blonde's hand away, ''Fuck off!'' They had been friends for so long that the redhead could actually hold back from killing Breanne with a spoon...instead, she tackled her to the grey-carpeted floor. To no avail, Bre knew exactly where Amy was ticklish...which sent the battered girl shooting to the bed, where she curled up into what could be considered a decent imitation of the fetal position. ''You're the devil.'' ''And you're not?'' Lilievre laughed, deliberately jostling poor, sober Aimz, ''Oh, I forgot...SOMEONE doesn't do 'evil things' like drugs anymore!!'' ''All the point shot away from that when we got outta high school, Bre.'' Breanne looked indifferent. ''Meh, I still do it...keeps me sane.'' ''Well...to each their own.'' Amy sighed rather loudly, hoping it would reinforce her point. ''It's just not my thing anymore...I kinda grew up.'' ''Fuck growing up...I say we should stay young as long as possible. But speaking of young...don't you think it was a little premature to lay on Reno? Hehe...isn't he a bit old for you?'' Damn, Amy cracked her in the arm quite nicely for that. ''Not funny!'' Campbell got defensive. ''It was a match...it's not like I enjoyed all the contact. And by the way, he's only like twenty one or twenty two...there's not THAT much difference.'' ''Ah...so we're defending the relationship, are we?'' ''Shut up!!!'' ''I don't blame you...'' Bre knew she was pushing Amy's buttons, and it was quite a riot to see. ''...you know, for liking him and all. I mean, he DOES have a Steven Tyler-ish thing going on...'' Here comes an interesting little comment... ''You think? I always thought he looked more like Bret Michaels, or even Joe Perry...'' ''Umm...Amy?'' Aimz sat there a moment, then realized she had lightened up a wee bit too much with her last comment. ''Damnit!!!!'' Breanne was all but ecstatic, ''So you DO like him!!! MUAHAAAAAAAHAAAAAAAHAAAAAAA!!!'' ''Die!! I feel like we're back in high school or something...'' ''Nah, Reno's no Ian Burton...'' ''Gaaaaaaaah!!!!!!!!!!!! Okay, I SO didn't like Ian!'' ''Haha,'' She was in stitches. ''You're talking like a closet prep, this is pure GOLD!!!!'' ''Stop it!!! ARRRGH!!!!!!!!'' Amy was about as annoyed as she could get. ''Bre...fuck off about this, okay?'' ''Awww...'' Lilievre was disappointed, but willing to relent none-the-less. ''...alright, sorry if it pissed you off.'' ''Meh...at least I didn't screw Kyle Capp...'' ''Errr...Amy? You did.'' ''Damnit!!!!!!!!'' Breanne laughed again, ''Now I'm beginning to see why you stopped using drugs...you've forgotten all the hot guys who boinked you...I mean, you can't even remember the first thousand!'' ''Bah! No slut jokes...Skanky McDouchebag.'' Amy playfully shot back, ''Besides...I remember Rentie PERFECTLY.'' ''That's because I said 'HOT guys', Amy.'' ''Hey!'' Well, that was the end of that! Campbell tackled Bre playfully, yet again...they rolled around for a minute, pulling and hitting as if they were young siblings in the family rec. room until... The door flew open... There, standing in the frame, was the little purple Imp known to many as 'Minion'...Hessian's little buddy and seemingly Amy's most recent in a string of stalkers, wearing a white ball cap that somebody, most likely by his little purple hand, had written 'Room Service' on in permanent marker. When they stopped the play-fight, the redhead was practically mounting Breanne and, of course, Minion wasn't thinking of the two wrestling...well, maybe wreslting in a second sense. Conspicuous indeed... Minion just stood in the doorway, shocked. His heart said 'I CAN'T BREATHE!!!', his eyes said 'Ohhh yeah baby', his tongue said 'Grrr, lemme in there', his brain said 'Hey remember what happened in Korea! Just back off dude!', his lungs said, 'In and out, in and out, out with the bad stuff, in with the good stuff...HURRY HE'S PALPITATING!!!'and his little Impy groin said 'WAIT YOUR TURN, TONGUE!', Minion stood in the doorway, ready to explode. The only words he could utter were, 'Ohhh you dirty little redhead...come to Minion...huh huh huh huh huh huh huh.'' ''What the fuck!?'' Both Amy AND Bre hollered at the same time to the little creature. Aimz was the first to stand up, trying to kick at the Imp. ''What in God's name are you doing here, you perverse little SPCA reject?!'' Bre, of course, added her own little remark... 'Okay...no matter how much I want it to be, this was SO not how it looked!!!'' ...which ended in a smack on the arm by Campbell while Minion just stared, still panting. Minion's eyes widened and a million and one thoughts ran through his head at once. His little head shook a little and he shrugged. 'Uhh.........hey I came to fix the fridge...' ''This is a hotel room, asswipe...'' Amy shouted again, ''...it doesn't HAVE a fridge!'' 'Uhh.........anyone order a purple hotdog?' Minion struggled to maintain his excuses. ''I swear to fucking God...if you even think.........I'll become a vegetarian!'' ''Don't worry, little guy, she loves 'meat' too much to do anything THAT drastic.'' Bre giggled. 'Well then why won't she love MY meat???' Minion's eyes welled up, and a devious plot entered his head, so a little crocodile tear appeared on his cheek. ''Awwww...'' Breanne kneeled down, placing a hand on the squared shoulder of the Imp, ''She's just mean...'' ''My God...'' Amy rolled her eyes. ''...she's actually buying into the Imp.' Minion sniffed and snivveled as Bre tried to comfort him. 'It's just...I just never really had anyone of my own, they were all flings or one night stands or tosses or whores or groupies or something...and when I see someone I really like, she goes and hates me!' Minion dropped to the ground dramatically and burst into tears. ''Amy! How could you be so cruel to such a cute lil' fella?! MY GOD, woman!'' Aimz just sighed, waving them off as she flopped down on the bed, rolling over to face away. ''Don't worry, Minion...'' Bre continued. 'I'm not evil, like Amy there...I'll be your friend! C'mon...let's go get some Dairy Queen!'' Minion sniffed again and put on his saddest face ever, looking deep into Bre's eyes with puppy dog's of his own. 'Ma'am, would you do me a favour, pweeeeez.' Minion sniffed again. Minion leaned close to Bre and whispered in her ear... 'I want to eat you out with some liver, fava beans and wash it all down with a nice chianti, phphphphphphphh!' ''WHAT THE MOTHER FUCK?!'' Everything faded out with Amy laughing, Bre kicking the Imp out the door and Minion practically sobbing...
