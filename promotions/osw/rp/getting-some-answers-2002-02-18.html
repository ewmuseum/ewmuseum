Getting Some Answers

18 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/18/02 The fans are cheering wildly as the third edition of OSW Slam has just come to a surprising finish. The crews have already begun to pack up the equipment so they can move it to the next arena. There is total pandemonium in the dressing room area. The name Meanstreak is repeated by all many of the wrestlers in the back, but in the locker room of Azrael Cain there are more important matters to take care of. Cain is still wearing his traditional wrestling attire, tight black pants with red stripes going up the legs, his black leather boots that rise up to right below his knees, his high black gloves. Cain is still in a carnal state as a result of the way things panned out this evening. Cain is still throwing things around in his locker room and screaming at his manager Caleb, who is still icing down is crotch. Cain is screaming at the top of his lungs. “I want some answers and I want them now! If that wasn’t Hessian in the ring then who was that imposter! And where is Hessian! What is going on around here?” Caleb winces in pain as he adjusts himself on the bench that he is sitting on. The hobo’s lower region is still very sensitive and probably will be for days to come. What else would you expect after being kicked in the nuts for with a pair of steel-toed boots? Suddenly the locker room door swings open. The large wooden slams into the cement wall with a loud crack. The wooden door actually cracks in half as it bounces off the wall. Cain swings around prepared for attack. Hessian appears in the doorway holding his head. Cain drops his guard for the moment, but there is obviously a lot of tension still in the room. The dark angel immediately begins to question his partner. “What happened to you? Where were you for our match? I want some answers and I want them now!” “I’m not going to talk about it! Drop it! There are more important things to deal with right now! Like how the hell we are going to get revenge next week on all of those that decided to screw us.” Hessian answers defensively. In a display of anger, Hessian slams the broken door shut. Just as the door is about to close though, Minion can be seen walking while he is reading something. Whatever is on that paper has the creepy figure so enthralled that he doesn’t notice the door flying towards him. SMACK! A loud thud is heard from outside the door. Hessian and Cain share a look for a minute, but decide to ignore the noise and continue their conversation. Azrael lays back into Hessian for missing the match. “Because you disappeared tonight, we lost another match! Caleb was forced to take your place! And those petty little disrespectful brats from the suburbs won again! How are we supposed to be a tag team when I can’t even rely on you to show up to our matches!” In a fit of rage, Hessian flips another table over that was covered with clean and folded towels. The white towels all flung threw the air as the table splinters on the floor. He then turns to Cain and continues with his defense. “Number one, when do I have to answer to you? Last time I checked we were partners, I’m not your bitch! Secondly, what happened tonight was out of my hands! That interfering monster jumped me. There was nothing that I could do, so drop it! We will get our revenge and another person just made the list of people who need a visit from Heresian Bloodlust! We will spill the blood of all those that have gotten in our way!” The tension in the room is still very high as the two warriors stare each other down. Caleb rolls his eyes at the two warriors. He gingerly rises to his feet, while still holding the ice on his testicles. Cain and Hessian are still arguing between each other as the hobo crosses to the damaged door. Caleb pulls on the door in an effort to open it, but due to the damage inflicted upon it he finds that this is not an easy task. Eventually, the door opens revealing a sprawled out Minion on the floor who is barely moving form the hit that he took from the door. Caleb begins to chuckle at the unconscious minion. <p. Something catches the hobo’s eye though that causes him to stop laughing. There is a piece of paper in Minion’s hand. Across the top of the paper in bold letters reads “OSW Slam card for February 23, 2002.” Caleb pulls the piece of paper out the unconscious manager’s hand and he begins to read the scheduled matches for next week. A sinister smile comes across the hobo’s face as he reads the list of matches for the upcoming card. Caleb cleans his throat in an attempt to get the two bickering demonic warriors’ attention. “Well, it seems that we’re going to get our chance for revenge next week. Heh-Heh-Heh!” Cain and Hessian stop arguing between each other and give their attention to the excited hobo. Cain shoots Caleb an insulting glare as he reprimands his manager. “And what have I told you about interrupting us when we are talking about important matters, Caleb!” The hobo shrugs his shoulders as if to say; “I don’t know.” Cain rolls his eyes and turns away in frustration. Hessian then looks at the hobo with a very annoyed look on his face before addressing him. “And what is so important on that piece of paper that is causing you to be even more of a nuisance then usual?” Minion is slowly beginning to come to. He is now moving slightly. Caleb hisses at Hessian and slams the door shut in anger. The door swings towards the already battered Minion. He looks up to see the wooden door coming directly for him. Minion’s eyes grow wide and almost leap out of his head. SMACK! The door slams off his head again. The same thud is heard outside the now closed locker room door. Caleb then waddles as quickly as his bruised crotch allows back into the locker room in order to get Hessian and Cain’s undivided attention. “According to this, it seems that we are going to have the opportunity to get back at all those that have been a pain. Heh-Heh. And at the same time we are going to be able to crush a couple of more wrestlers’ dreams in the process. Heh-Heh. Hessian looks at Caleb suspiciously who now is donning a huge toothless smile on his old and foul face. “What are you talking about, worm? Caleb scowls at Hessian before going into the details of the next card. “Well, it seems that you are going to get your chance to extract some revenge on that no good son of a bitch Meanstreak for what he did to you. Heh-Heh.” Hessian is not amused by this news. “You are telling me something that I am already aware of. I have already arranged it so I have to do battle against him next week. I want to see that freak bleed for what he did to me this week. I insisted that I get this opportunity to dismantle him in the ring.” A frustrated look appears on Caleb’s face, but he continues to deliver the good news. “That might be so, but did you know that your partner was going to be in the rumble this week that is going to decide who one of the number one contenders for the OSW World Title at the Pay per view next month! Heh-Heh. He is going to be one of the twelve men in an over the ropes rumble! Heh-Heh. And Civil Disobedience are going to be stuck facing off against two robots! Heh-Heh-Heh.” Hessian’s and Cain’s eyes grow wide. Cain reaches down and pulls the piece of paper out Caleb’s hands. Both men begin to look at the piece of paper. Cain’s disposition begins to change as he looks at the card. A devilish grin stretches across his lips revealing his two fang like canines. The fallen angel looks at his partner Hessian and they both share a look. Hessian is the first one to break the uncomfortable silence. “So it seems that this week is going to be one of pain and suffering for all of OSW. This week, we are going to open the flood gates and unleash the torrent currents of revenge.” Cain looks at the paper again and snarls like a beast ready for the hunt. “I think that this week is going to be one that all of the mindless fans of OSW are going to remember for a long time. We are going to get revenge on the meddling monster known as Meanstreak and I am going to ruin the dreams of eleven of the OSW’s upcoming stars. This Saturday is going to be a day that will remain in the minds of all those involved as long as they live. This Saturday id going to be Heresian Bloodlust’s day. After that ridiculous match I am going to walk out of this weekend the number one contender for OSW’s precious title and then after the main event I will be the one that holds that belt. And then we will be in control of this organization and all of those underneath us will fall to our reign of carnage!” The two warriors look at one anther and begin to make plans for this week’s card. The men begin to exit the locker room. As the door opens they look down and see Minion who is now beginning to stir. Hessian looks down at his manager in disgust before addressing him. “Will you get up already Minion! This is no time to be laying around! Why can’t you make yourself useful for once!” Cain, Hessian, and Caleb exit the locker room by stepping over the little man that is crumpled up on the floor. As the warriors start to leave the arena, Minion is seen still trying to get to his feet.
