Today in the news...

17 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17 March 2002 'Today in the news…' A TV news reporter's gets drowned out by a busy city. The news came from an electronics store window. Where Small 12 x 24 inch screen Televisions show Channel 8's new coverage on today's events, the young Ms. Sandy Wobblejugs. An African-American news broadcaster with long black hair, which hits her shoulders covered by her brown suite. 'The Hunt for Osama Bin Laden goes on, President Bush said in a press conference this evening….' The scene moves away to see wearing his trademark Grey suit with a white shirt underneath is Eric Sagar. The sun is shining on him and he is leaning on the shop window watching Ms. Sandy's broadcast. The sound of her voice is faded out. 'My Brothers, we see this hunt for an anti-taliban regime, and they're some what hero Osama Bin Laden.' He says in a very calm tone. 'Well this whole Taliban stuff is bull, that is a fact. God sees women as equals.' He says, 'But that is not why I am here… I am here to talk to you about 'The Power Himself' Propaganda and what the combined force of Jane Reagan and Ed have put him.' He looks disgusted; he breathes in and bows his head. 'I mean myself and Propaganda take much offence to be put in a match and forced to team with the likes of The Magi and Epitome of Evil' He shakes his head. 'How dare you associate the power himself with Goths and Satanists' He looks around. 'But me and God's chosen son Propaganda, have come to put a stop to this. Decided to protest, Reagan if you would please stop playing about with Switchblade and keep your mind on your business then OSW would be a happier place.' His tone of speaking gets a certain sense of anger to it. 'And if Ed would stop sitting on his rear all day and drinking bear then maybe he would also get some work down.' He says filled with rage. 'And now the opponents, Nick Casey who we earlier realised was a test tube baby. The Great Dragon who still read Nursery Rhymes and Fairy tails. And of course Vampir Nosferatu, the evilness of his soul angers me. I know God tells us to look after the less fortunate, but it would be much better for them to be locked up in a padded cell with a straight jacket, and getting electric shocks till he is sane, then be let out with us normal people.' He looks round the busy street. 'But looking at today's common man, who is sane? We have other Satanists like Hessain and Azrael Cain. They attacked Propaganda on Slam; well I have something to inform you…' Then he shouts, 'YOU SHALL ALL PERISH! AND YOUR SINS WILL BE RECOGNISED BY THE LORD AND HIS SON JESUS CHRIST! PROPAGANDA WILL STRIKE DOWN UPON YOU WITH ANGER AND FURIOUS VENGANCE!' A man about 6'4' with a very muscular build, shaved black hair and a goatee. He's wearing a white polo neck shirt, jeans and a leather jacket. He walks over moving his shoulders opposite to his legs. 'Look dude, will you shut the @#%$ up in public man.' He says with a pissed off tone 'Your disturbing people.' Eric Sagar looks at him dead in the eye. ''Forgive them father, they know not what they do' - Jesus Christ himself.' 'What?' The man replies is disbelief. 'You don't actually believe in that crap do you?' Sagar pushes the man who is over a foot bigger than him. 'I believe in it, yes, and I believe you are a fool for doubting the word of God!' The Man steps forward and grabs Sagar by the collar and lifts him up so he's face to face. Sagar says in a calm tone 'Foolish Child.' The man gets a tap on his shoulder with a massive hand, 'What is it?' He says with no care, the hand taps him again. 'What?' he says. The man drops Sagar and turns round to only find himself staring straight up at Propaganda. Propaganda is wearing his wearing is usual ring attire. The Man says humbly. 'Oh Urrr... Hi Mister.' Propaganda says without emotion 'Let him go.' The man then replies 'Look dude, I don't want to tread on any toes but this has nothing to do with you.' Propaganda looks at the man… then sighs. He hits a massive right hook and the man flies and his back smacks against the shop window. The man groans, Propaganda gets the head of the man and smashes it through the glass. The Man begins to cry, as tiny bits of broken glass pierce his face and tiny streams of blood drip down slowly. Sagar starts laughing, and then he shouts, 'HIT THE EXODUS!' So Propaganda hits his version of a T-bone suplex which he calls Exodus. The man's body skims across the pavement into the middle of the road. He grabs the man by the back of his head, kicks him in the stomach and throws him through the Electronics shop window falling into the pile of televisions and Ms. Sandy goes off air. As peaces of shattered glass make a dome around The Man's broken body. Sagar laughs 'What a fool, to deny our faith my brother.' Propaganda nods 'Look at him, a broken man, glass in his back. Faithless is his soul. Look upon him my brother half of a man.' Sagar smiles sickly. 'This is true he is nothing without Jesus, nobody is truly human. He has been horribly misguided now… so he has been horribly beaten.' Sagar looks back at Propaganda. 'But as we see him bleed this is as a matter of fact a sign, a sign of things to come.' Propaganda interrupts Sagar, 'Do you mean by the end of slam 5 atheist scum shall lie with there blood staining all around?' Sagar evilly chuckles 'Correct.' Propaganda nods 'I believe this will happen, as sure as Jesus saved mankind. I am sure.' Sagar smiles 'A-Men.' On this the scene fades to black as 'The Power himself' Propaganda and Eric Sagar stand with their heads bowed in front of a medical team tending to the man who was just thrown through a window.
