Turbulence

4 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 4th 'Fear turns to adrenaline as life accelerates, I'm headed to my destiny, how long you guess it takes - to get there? I'm cramped between two cool dudes, one from each coast, Yet we all agree a safe flight is what we need most. Hey stewardess, I can't handle all this turbulence, This ride's gettin bumpy, ain't helpin my nervousness.' Bubba Sparxxx 'Take Off' The Old School Wrestling organization's first ever Pay-Per-View “Crowning Glory”, in which Drew Hostyle would face off against Rhett Locke for the International Title was drawing near. And Drew wasn't exactly nervous, but anxious. Anxious to hold gold and the glory that came with it. Anxious to win. Anxious to feel pain. Anxious. But there were many obstacles he'd have to overcome before he actually made it to the day. Obstacles by the name of Chico and Gunmetal. Obstacles that actually weren't living or dead. There were mechanical organisms that weren't even made of organs. Where Drew's desire was, lied their programmed list of functions. Where Drew's heart was found, could be found in the same place of these creatures, not pulsating or beating, but ticking. Ticking..counting down the seconds, the moments until they found that..piece. Now only God knows where the hell the damn piece is, what it is does, and when they'll find it. But rest assured they won't find it on Drew's time. It was just too damn soon. But destroying their purpose would mean that Drew would have to find his purpose for living in the mean time.. There's always the scientific aspect of things..that says that human's sole purpose in their lives are only to reproduce. And Drew wasn't about to do that anytime soon. But then again there's always that deep, inspirational, spiritual crap that everyone talks about finding..Heh, maybe someone could give him some insite on that. Hmm..that's something to look into. The scene then opens to show Drew Hostyle sitting on a coach on the inside of a huge mansion that sits on a hill somewhere in the dirty..filthy south that is Virginia. The city looks unbelievably infested with trash protruding out of each wide enough crevice in the ground, and the huge house sitting on top of the hill looks spontaneously out of the ordinary. Something that came quite accustomed to the person that lived inside, known to OSW viewers and fans as Sebastian “Switchblade” Toomes, known simply to Drew Hostyle as Switchy. Drew is finally seen in full view by the camera, today wearing a pair of baggy black FUBU designer jeans with red lining and a matching black and red starter jacket. He’s also got a half red and black do rag on his head and his face is in an exasperated expression as he turns to the long stairway of the house and begins to speak, for obviously the house’s owner to hear. “Sebo get yo a** down here, Dog. We gotta get der by at least 2 a clock!” “Heh, and you said I’d take the longest. Talk about him being more feminine..” “Well that don’t take much now does it?” “You’d know you big breasted bastard..” “Well aren’t my two favorite people in the world getting along just peachy?!” “Look, you better be happy I ain't backhanded this chick yet, Yo.” Drew murmurs to Switchblade under his breath and motioning in Jennifer’s direction. “*Sigh* Are we going to go now or do I have to leave by myself?” Asks Jennifer impatiently with her hands on her hips. “Don’t feel inclined to wait on us Miss Moody..” Switchblade snarls at her then smiles, seeing that she’s offended as her facial expressions soon go to tell. “That’s it, I’m leaving. Drew I’ll see you Saturday, I guess.” Jennifer looks to Switchblade and then says. “ And I’ll go as long as possible without seeing YOU.” “Does that mean you’ll miss me?!” Switchblade replies somewhat hopelessly. The only answer he’s given is the door slamming behind his sultry valet, Jennifer Kale, as she leaves, disgusted. “You certainly gotta way with the chickens, man.” “Yeah I know, can you imagine? I mean, Jennifer wants me just as bad as Jane does, you know.” “So what are you gonna like those bunions too?” “They weren’t bunions Drew, they were beautiful, soft, moist...” “Ugh, man quit dat shit. I dun need to hear all that. Let’s just go, fo my appetite get ruined.” With that being said, Drew Hostyle leads Switchblade out of his house and enters the limo he travels to each city he visits with the Old School Wrestling federation in. He and Switchblade both enter the limo and begin conversation en route to an outing that Drew has obviously planned. “So what time does this shin dig start anyways?” Switchblade asks. “It started at noon, but you had to make a fashionably late appearance. But the time we get there we’ll just be late..” “Look, as the future OSW World Champion, I have to be fashionable. God knows that Timon is anything but fashionable. Like I said Sunday, that dark..” “Whoa, Sebo. I know you mean well, but if you say anythin bout Darkies where we bout to go, you goin get yo ass capped. And you do know what that means right?” “I know, I know. You’ve told me a million times. I’m all about giving back to the community Drew. I told you these people are all okay with me.” “It’s not these people Sebo, it’s my people..our people.” “Yes right..our people...our people.” “Hey by the way, how are you for Slam!? Doin anything?” “Nope. I’m off. Might make an appearance though. You know how needy women are these days, Jane might need more of me around, and maybe it won’t just be the toes I’m lickin this time..” “DAMN MAN! Come on I’m bout to spew all over this limo and you goin pay for it, if you don’t stop talkin bout her.” “Okay, okay. For your sake, though. Why’d you bring up Slam! anyways?” “Because I’ve got a match, I was wonderin if ya knew anythin bout the two. They call themselves Twisted Metal or some shit.” “Oh those robots? They wrestle? I thought they were just toting around Jane’s bags or something. You know, someone should be carrying her bags. I think I’ll look into that..” “Well I don’t care what they’re made for. All I care about is that they can take a punch. God’s knows I can hot wire a car in less than a half hour. I could prolly dismantle them things if I thought about it in one match‘s time. But damn, that’ll take too much work. Do they even have shoulders for me to pin? Well..where there’s a button, there’s a person to push it, so I’ll find a way to kill those robots some way or anotha. Or at least keep em down for a while. It’s not like they haven’t been used to being on their little metal knees and...” “Say, you don’t think anyone’s been on their knees with Jane do you?” “I’m assumin that..” “Maybe I should claim my territory; I see the way Timon and Ed look at her sometimes..” “Nobody else wants that, man. Trust.” “Hey! You never know, by the way don’t get to tied up in thinking about those robot Drew. They can’t overcome the power of the soul funk BRUTHA!” “Ha ha ha, Just come on, man. We’re here now anyways.” As they finish their conversation Drew and Switchblade exit the limo and the camera fades to show what looks like the typical Virginia block party. There are lots of people, mostly African American, dressed in the same type of clothes that Drew was seen wearing earlier. Switchblade looks unmistakably like a fish out of water but wears a winning smile on his face as various people look at him like he’s a damn fool. “Hello there!”, “Hi! How’s it swingin?”, “What’s happenin fellas?” Switchblades smiles. One man takes offense to Switchblade’s common courtesy and approaches him. They meet nose to nose in the center of all the people and he crowd starts to hush as the black guy begins to speak to Switchblade. “Listen punk, you may be some wrestler, but we don’t need bitches like you tryin to bring us down, you don’t belong here no ways.” “I’m not some wrestler! I’m the STAR~! And I can go anywhere I..” “He’s wit me man. So why don’t you just step tryin cuse people fo yo personal shit.” Drew says as he steps in and pushes the other man away. “Okay I see how you treat yo own brothas man.” “And I see how you treat yours.” Drew states then walks away from the crowd somewhat quickly with Switchblade behind him. He stops Switchblade near the big stage, on which the MC of this event would conduct what would be going on later. “Man, don’t even worry about people like him, don’t even dignify them wit answers. Matta fact you aint even got to talk period...if you don’t want.” Drew says to Sebastian with a more certain tone of voice. Just as Switchblade is about to answer, the MC himself stands on the stage and begins to day’s ceremonies, or put in more naive terms... “Yo, sup my people?! I’m fin to tell ya’ll how it’s goin down right hur. We bout to have ourselves a free style battle of da Beach beats! Wit special guest my man Drew Hostyle as da judge!” The crowd erupts in applause and begin to shout various roars of approval towards the MC on stage. He nods his head furiously, then holds his hands up to silence everyone once again. “Aight, aight. The Battle of da Beach Beats Free style champion, as ya’ll know, is none other than dat Playboy wit more scrilla than any record dill-a. Rep’n New York and tha whole East Coast..Nappy Head Fred!” The rapper supposably named “Nappy Head Fred” looked true to his word. He pimped on stage with his head in massive black locks that were very sloppily done. He however was wearing very expensive looking clothes and a sideways baseball cap on his head to top it off. He grabbed the mic from the MC, began to spit rhymes off the top of his head, and the people in the audience started nodding their heads to and feeling the lyrics he was saying. “Straight outta New York, But not a fan of da Knicks. Bring home two chicks a night, let em spit on my *censored*. They say my rhymes is sick, and that I need medication. Still run through each city, like Grand central train station. All ya’ll hatas have patience, I’m hitting platinum soon. ‘Til then I’ll stick to hittin young chicks, makin yo daughters’ swoon. One.” The crowd erupts once again, and Drew who was taken his spot on the stage as judge, even stands with a smirk on his face and gives “Nappy Head Fred” a standing ovation. The MC then asks for the mic for Fred and yells into it. “Come on ya’ll, give it up one more time fo’ NAPPPPPPY HEEEEAD FRED!” The crowd continues to cheer and cheer on Nappy Head Fred’s performance as Battle of the Beach Beast champ. “Aight, aight. So what’s poppin e’rybody? Anyone think they can top what my boy Fred came wit?” The crowd hushes not for the first time tonight and a little rumbling is heard then a chorus of laughs and sighs. The rumbling was Switchblade volunteering to challenge the champion and the laughs that followed were..well, they were self explanatory. Drew has a chocked look on his face and then inaudible to Switchblade, yells for him to get off of the stage. Switch just smiles to Drew and then takes the mic that is given to him by the nearly falling down with laughter MC. Nappy Head Fred just looks to Switchblade sternly and holds the golden mic that dignifies his place as Battle of the Beach Beats Champion closer to his chest. Switchblade however isn’t intimidated and rhymes to the dismay of everyone watching. “Yo, yo, yo. Check this out. well my name is Switchblade. But you can call me Switchy. Jane Reagan rocks my boat, but is sometimes a little bitchy.” At this point everyone in the audience is booing their ass off. And start throwing things at the stage. In between dodging a half cracked open forty ounce beer bottle, Switchblade dives on the booing Free style Champion, Nappy Head Fred and rips his hands throughout his head. Drew however, gets over to the scene seconds before the police do and picks up Switchblade who’s holding the Golden mic. The two run off the block and as far away from the block party as possible. And as Drew comes towards his limo and shoves the driver into his seat at the front of the limo, he talks to Switchblade. “What the hell were you thinkin about man?!” Drew asks, out of breath from the run to the limo. “I’m the CHAMP! There was really no thinking required..” Switchblade replies. “Well start thinkin about the shit you do, man. Nappy Head Fred is now bald headed, and probably comin after yo ass.” “How intimidating can he be with those terd locks coming out of his head?” “Just as intimidating you are, being in love wit Jane.” “Jane’s a very feisty woman, I’ll have you know..” “Please man, not this again..” Drew says as he gets into the limo. “No what again?” ask Sebastian,. as he too gets into the limo and the scene fades to black.
