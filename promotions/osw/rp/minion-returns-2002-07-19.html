Minion Returns...

19 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19-07-02 Complete darkness. A single candle flame flickering in the middle of it all. Sitting around the flame, eyes closed and seemingly in meditation, they all think forward to the next night. The events of the past, the promise of the future plague them as they prepare in complete silence… “They found Niki Weathers…?” whispered Hessian, breaking the silence. Vampir and The Cultist lift their heads up, staring at Hessian. The Titanic Satanic Insaniac shrugs and shuffles around. “It matters not how they found that little vixen…” Vampir replies, “For the moment let us prepare for tonight, after all, tonight is a most special night indeed.” Hessian shrugged and closes his eyes again. As The Cultist and Hessian meditate, Vampir begins swaying back and forth, and quietly reciting a chant of some form. With every breath the candle flame grows slightly bigger. Though as the candle flame grows bigger, the room becomes filled with light, and in the dancing flame the stench of rotting flesh is accentuated, while the flickering leads to forms hanging around the side of the room… Vampir’s’ eyes roll into the back of head suddenly, but as he is about to finish the incantation, the door to the room suddenly bursts open, and in steps a fiendish silhouette. The Cult stops and jump to their feet, looking on as the figure moves into the room, and into the light…Minion the Imp. Vampir stares at the little creature before him. Cocking his head in confusion, the Soul Stalker advances a few steps, pulling back a right hand, when Hessian stops before him. “Wait…Vampir, you have been formally introduced to Minion…haven’t you?” “In the months I have spent here, I have only ever caught a glimpse of this little creature…where has he been hiding?” Hessian makes a move to answer, but stops and cocks his head. Turning around, Hessian looks at Minion, and then kneels down, motioning for his comrade to step forth. “Pleased to meet ya Pea-Head, it's good to have you on our side!” Minion squealed, causing Vampir to growl under his breath. “Pray you stay on Hessian's good side...or you'll end up on my bad side...then you will die...Imp, capiché?” “Everything exepct capiché...” Hessian shakes his head and chuckles, then nods at Minion, resting a gauntleted paw on Minion's little Impy shoulder. “Minion…where exactly have you been these past few weeks? I for one haven’t seen much of you…” Minion clears his throat, then puts a hand on Hessian’s big shoulder. “These past few weeks you motherfuckers have been culting it up…while I have successfully recruited some-…” “You did what!?” Vampir replies, his eyes widening in shock as Minion smiles cheesily before them. “You’ll see on Slam!…I look out for my people, and The Throwbacks are gonna get a little surprise...for now, I’ve just gotten done with a column for the OSW! I figure I’m gonna do more, but for now I’m looking to The Cult, because you guys don’t really do much except beat up some people…heh, I'm playin' of course...” Minion removes his hand from Hessian’s shoulder and turns, waving goodbye to The Cult as he leaves the room…heading for the women’s showers…Hessian and Vampir stare at each other, when suddenly the soft, yet frightening voice of the Cultist pipes up. “This is going to be Hell………but I like it………” The Cult grin sadistically, and as the door slams shut to the room, total darkness envelops them, when suddenly the candle flame bursts into an inferno then dies out in a split second, and in that split second…the walls of the room move, and the shapes of robed figures dance around The Cult… Fin
