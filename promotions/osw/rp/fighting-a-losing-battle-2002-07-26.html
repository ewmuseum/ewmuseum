Fighting a Losing Battle

26 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2002-07-26 Lillith has her eyes closed, a look of contentment on her face. Her long hair hangs loose about her shoulders, a set of headphones barely seen through the dark strands. Peace. Serenity. A frown? Lillth's eyes tightened, as she mentally tried to grasp at that place where time and space seem to fall away, leaving a stillness in body and soul... but it would not come. Try as she might, her lessons in meditation would not dispel the dark feelings that have surrounded her the last couple of weeks. With a sign, she tore the headphones off and tossed them to the floor. With the CD player still running, the faint music of Linkin Park flaoted in the air... I tried so hard And got so far But in the end It doesn't even matter I had to fall To lose it all But in the end It doesn't even matter Lillith angrily stood up and kicked her Discman across the room, where it hit the wall and stopped playing. She turned her head to look at the hotel suite she was currenly in, and remebered the feelings that coursed through her as she systematically destoryed it - the mirrors shattered, the matressed ripped, the television imploding with a loud cough. She rembered the anger which boiled her blood... ... but underneath, the cold chill of fear was still there. No matter what she did, how she tried to cope with it, the fear would not go away. It waited in the back of her mind, ready to leap up and grab her at a moment's notice. She couldn't ride in elevators anymore. It was becoming more and more diffucult to get into cars. And the last time she was on an airplane, she nearly screamed when she apprached the bathroom. It was destroying her life. It was destroying her sanity. Just like she was destroying everything around her. Lillith punch a wall in fristration, drawing blood as her knuckles hit the painted cement bricks. Pain raced up her arm in a sudden rush, blocking the fear for an instant, but returning after a few seconds. She was at a loss. not being in control was new for her, and that feeling caused just as much fear as the other. 2 different fears battling each other. And one soul, trying to fight them both off... ... and losing...
