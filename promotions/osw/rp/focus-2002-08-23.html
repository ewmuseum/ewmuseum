23 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2002-08-23 Corg. Corg. Corg. The name thrummed like a heartbeat inside Lillith's mind. Corg. Lillith was kneeling on a small mat, a stick of incense smoking in front og her. Focusing her mind, focusing her energy. Corg. The previous weeks were puched back into a small corner of her mind and locked away. She wouldn't worry about how the wrestling community treated women. She woudn't worry about false hopes given in bookings. She wouldn't worry about how she thought she was being screwed by the system because she didn't have a dick. Corg. All she would worry about now, was making one man pay. A man who had no respect, no decency, no morals. And now had no right, at least in Lillith's mind. Corg. Corg. She planned on picking up right where Iven left off last week, a broken man, covered in his own blood. But Lillith planned on finishing the job. No one from the back would stop her. She would beat on Corg without remorse, until the last gasp of breath was forced from his lungs. Corg. Corg. Corg. She didn't care about the belt. She didn't care about the fans. She didn't care about herself. All she focused on, was destroying one man. Corg.
