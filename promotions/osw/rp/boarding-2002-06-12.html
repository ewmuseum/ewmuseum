Boarding

12 June 2002

Date: 12th June 2002 Ian Wright handed his boarding card to the stewardess, flight attendant, whatever. She looked at his ticket, looked up at him and smiled, her teeth were unnaturally white. Wright supposed that not all air hostesses, that was another term for them, even more outdated then stewardess, looked the same, they just seemed to. Fake tan, only applied to the jaw line that gave the impression that they were wearing a mask, bright red lipstick and those unnaturally white teeth. As a child, no more then six or seven, Wright had once dressed up as a vampire for halloween, cape, waistcoat, bow tie everything. His mother had completed the outfit with a pair of plastic fangs, they too were unnaturally white, they looked like they were bleached, the shade of the stewardesses teeth reminded him of that halloween. ‘Seat 1A, that’s the first window seat to your left.’ She handed Wright back his boarding card, looked to the next passenger and said with a flash of those disconcerting teeth ‘Good morning, welcome aboard, may I see your ticket.’ Wright took his seat, and placed his MP3 player on the seat next to his, signing with OSW had its perks, first class travel was one of them, lots of legroom, it was nice, being the size that he was flying coach was very uncomfortable, being in the first row of the plane gave him even more legroom, he still hated flying though, being stuck next to some idiot for hours on end was pretty much Wright’s idea of hell on earth. He took out his book and opened at the page he had last been reading, “American Scream: the Bill Hicks story” by Cynthia True. It was interesting, Wright had been a big Bill Hicks fan for years, having gotten into him when a friend of his leant him one of his CD’s, when he heard it he was hooked, it wasn’t just the jokes, although the jokes were great, it was what else he said, how he talked about the world that we live in and what he said about the people that we let run out lives, he articulated everything that Wright felt about the world, well most of it anyway, the book was interesting, he was learning a lot about the man that he most admired that he had not known, but that was the whole point of reading the book wasn’t it. ‘Would you like a drink before takeoff?’ asked another flight attendant, same description as before, although she was a different person. ‘No,’ Wright said rudely. He didn’t like people interrupting him when he was thinking, if they left him alone then everything would be fine, if not then he knew exactly how to make people around him uncomfortable. He got back to his book. ‘Excuse me, your whateveritis is in my seat.’ Wright looked up and saw an absolutely stunning woman standing in front of him. Her beautiful face expertly made up, not too much just enough to accentuate her features, framed by long brown hair, Wright had thing for brunettes, and was as the saying goes ‘a sucker for a pretty face’, her face was gorgeous enough for the rest of her not to matter that much, but just for the record the rest of her was just as impressive, large, firm breasts and long, slim, perfectly toned legs that were covered by a very short miniskirt that was part of a pretty expensive looking designer suit. ‘Oh I am sorry, let me move that for you.’ ‘Thanks, by the way I love your accent, you’re Irish right.’ ‘Yeah, thank you. So are you going to Oakland for business or pleasure?’ ‘Business, you?’ ‘Does anyone actually go to Oakland for pleasure?’ The woman laughed, Wright smiled, it hadn’t been that good a joke. He thought to himself that this flight mightn’t be so bad after all and asked the woman if she would like to have a drink with him before they took off.
