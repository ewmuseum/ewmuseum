Everybody's schizo these days...Corg provokes something more original.

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 31st, 2002 ''Narcolepsy?'' Sitting at the bar of whatever God forsaken hotel OSW had set she and Reno up with this week, Amy was flipping through a fat, deteriorated book that was too large to hold, set up on her lap and tipped against the counter. ''Nah...'' She muttered, nervously toying with a zipper at the knee of her plaid bondage jeans. ''...no symptoms of fatigue...'' Campbell flipped through the pages, stopping to press her finger against one. ''Paranoid schizophrenia?'' ''Not THAT psycho...'' She laughed to herself and ended up sighing with a slight smile. ''...yet.'' Sure, she was half in the bag and it was only 9pm, by the swanky little clock just behind the bartender, who looked better and better with each tequila shot, but Amy was practically never exempt from reading. It seemed as if this evening's goal was to escape the world for a small period of time and, as expected, the bar had beckoned her. She'd been haunted by horrifying dreams and odd voices that manifested themselves as subconscious thought for weeks now, but the last few days had really begun to affect not only Aimz herself, but the objects and people around her; Perfect examples of that were the passenger side mirror on last week's rental car and the fact that Reno Davis seemed to have become increasingly depressed just worrying about what the hell was going on in his girlfriend's head. Enough thought, she was too sober to ponder anything interesting. A few more drinks would clear that little predicament right up, though! So the reading continued through several mental illnesses, until she happened upon one, much lesser than the previously mentioned diseases... ''Post...traumatic...stress disorder?'' Amy stammered, reading aloud to herself. Really, it was the only way she could read clearly...pronouncing every word fairly well for a drunkard. ''Victims of PTSD often have delusions or hallucinations remembering the event or events like it. They may experience a degree of paranoia, as if a loved or revered one close to them has turned against them, in the case of many cases that have spawned from some sort of sexual...'' This was all harder to stomach than the liquor, was this a possibility? The symptoms stood out... ''...or....physical trauma.'' For a moment, she just stared down at the page, a little frightened as she swirled a finger around the edge of one in the little series of empty shot glasses placed before her. Who is Most Likely to Develop PTSD? 1. Those who experience greater stress magnitude and intensity, unpredictability, uncontrollability , sexual (as opposed to nonsexual) victimization, real or perceived responsibility, and betrayal. Campbell's expression went blank, ''Uh oh...'' It didn't improve as she read on... ''Because most trauma survivors don't know how trauma usually affects people, they often have trouble understanding what is happening to them. They may think it is their fault that the trauma happened, that they are going crazy, or that there is something wrong with them because other people who were there don't seem to have the same problems. They may turn to drugs or...alcohol........'' She gulped, her forehead wrinkled with a degree of worry. Amy didn't like looking like a fool, even when it was only to herself. She hated feeling weak, consumed by something. Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder? It sounded like an odd concept, but all the symptoms were there. Would she ever consider admitting to having it or feeling it's effects? HELL no...she'd been seen as a weakling who let Corg's actions overcome her, that just wasn't acceptable. So it seemed, she was ill... But she wouldn't admit an illness to herself, she COULDN'T be sick...she was too strong for that, right? Telling herself she was going crazy was so much easier... Corg consumed her thought, day and night...hurting Corg, getting hurt by Corg; she was ALWAYS a victim of his, he could be anywhere and Amy knew that. She was crazy? In her opinion, yes. Crazy FOR Corg? He wished! She wouldn't give him the pleasure of that, nor would she admit to herself that he manipulated her into the psychosis she'd been in since Ascension... Then again, she'd thought...near rape, getting stalked AND stabbed would drive anybody to loose their marbles, or get this post-traumatic whatevermajiggy... ''Now how the fuck do I cure it....'' For that evening, the answer lay in tequila.
