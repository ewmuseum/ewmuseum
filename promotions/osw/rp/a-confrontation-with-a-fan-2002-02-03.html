A Confrontation with a fan...

3 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/3/02 Walczak is seen riding his motorcycle down a block in Copiague, Long Island. He is getting looks from groups of people. The reason is obvious once you look at the two flags that hang from his motorcycle. One is that of his national country Poland, and the other is the Hammer and Sickle of Mother Russia. All of a sudden Walczak swerves off of his path after being hit by an orange. He stays on his bike, but immediately pulls over to the side, and runs looking for the person who did it. Walczak sees a man start running, so he assumes that he is the doer of the deed, but as he runs towards him he is pelted with another orange. He turns around and sees a man run into an alley. Walczak darts towards the alley after the man. The man tries to climb over a fence, but Walczak dives towards him, and grabs his jacket tossing the man to the ground. The man is wearing a long trench coat, and a t-shirt that says Doug Troy, True Old School! The toss to the ground tore the man’s jeans. The man tries to back away, but Walczak steps on the man’s trench coat preventing him from doing so. “Why did you throw that orange at me for? Do you think that that accomplished something? Did you actually think that I wouldn’t catch up with you? Well whatever you think you are going to see that what you did was a mistake.” “Yeah well you will see that attacking Doug Troy and Trashcan Man was a mistake. They will show you what it means to be Old School.” “Oh will they? Those two guys are going to get the beating of their lives. They are going to be taught a lesson, and that is the same thing that I am going to teach you right now, and that is that if you play games with the Red Army you won’t win?” “How can you say that they will get the beatings of their lives? They are two former PCW World Heavyweight Champions, and what have you amounted to Extreme Champion? That’s what I thought.” “Yeah, you are right. I did not amount to much in PCW. I had my spurs of greatness, but I couldn’t get anything steady going, but there is one thing that you are forgetting. This isn’t PCW this is OSW, and this is a time for new beginnings. I came here to start anew, and I plan to take a whole different route. I am not going for small game anymore, no this time around I am going straight for the kill.” “Is that what you think is going to happen? Well, it is not my man Doug Toy is going to make sure of that. You can kick my a*s all that you want, but all that really matters is Saturday, when Troy, and Trashcan Man mess you up.” You know, you are absolutely correct. I don’t need to waist any time on you. Why waist my time on a piece of crap like you? I am just going to wait until Saturday, and be thinking of you sitting at home watching your hero Doug Troy getting his a*s handed too him. And you will see a smile appear on my face, as I stand over the fallen body of your beloved hero. I will envision the look on your face, as you realize the power of the Red Army, and as the thought that we cannot be stopped and you fall to the floor and cry. So get the hell out of here before I change my mind.” The man runs away at a scurrying pace, and Walczak walks back towards his motorcycle. He climbs on and he puts his helmet over his head. “I cannot wait until this persecution stops, and the world realizes that they should join us. They will realize that they can still join, and Mother Russia will once again reunite with Poland, and people will realize that, “Jescze Polska Nie Zginela…” Walczak rides away on his bike and the scene fades to black as many questions still remain unanswered in our minds…
