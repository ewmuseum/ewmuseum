Time to take what is mine...

6 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/6/02 Sitting... Waiting... Preparing... Frankie sits in a damp, dank locker room in the Tulsa Convention Center. Starring at his hands as he methodically wraps them in pre-wrap and tape. His eyes, never leaving his hands. His massive, scarred hands. He chuckles, mumbling, 'these used to be such strong hands.' 'Say what Frankie?' questions Michel Krieg, who's sitting across the room. 'Oh.....nothing,' answers Frankie, not even looking at Michel. Krieg and Kory Storm sit across the room from Frankie, looking at him with wonder. Surely thinking 'What the hell is his deal?' There are two other men in the room with him, but Frankie is truly alone. He's always been alone, he always will be. Frankie glances at the clock, it's 8:55. Slam! starts in five minutes. He goes back to taping his hands, but something is different. Tremoring, shaking, his fucking hands are shaking. This monster, this beast, this demon, has the jitters. This feeling is as foreign as the grounds crew outside of the Convention Center. He cannot grasp it. Why is he shaking? Is he nervous? Anxious? Scared? Gotta be anxious. It all begins tonight...nobodies ready. It starts now, all the hard work, all the pain, all the blood, all for nothing. But tonight that changes. Frankie has decided to take what he deserves, take what is his, and take it with a vengeance. He has help. Two men... Two men who he isn't very fond of, but two men who want exactly what he does. These two men, sitting, talking, babbling...give it up. He pays them no mind, he has no use for them yet, but he will. He glances at the clock...then back at his hands. They are steadying, it's almost time, it's almost on... He's lost in thought, lost in the impending bliss... Something drawing him back, something interrupting him, a voice... It's Kory Storm, drawing him back to the real world, asking 'What do you think about that buddy?' ..... 'What the f...'
