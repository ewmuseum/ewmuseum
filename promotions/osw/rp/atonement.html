Date: Today Her violent eyes twinkled behind her short bob cut. She clutched the ragged doll tightly to her chest. A devious little smile crossed her lips as she nodded. The young one, looked at Bethany with her wicked little stare. “Lady, why would I disapprove of your choice? You have chosen a path. You must stay on it in order to atone for what you have done. I know you can understand that. If you cannot, you will be haunted for life.” Bethany stared back at the little girl’s refection in the mirror as she wiped the dark toned make-up from her face. Many feelings had crossed her mind over the past few nights. Many of which were all too familiar. Atonement.. Cursed.. Destroyed… Hatred… Revenge…. Lilith had to be punished. Bethany had given herself to the Cult in order for retribution. In order to regain her honor she had given up everything that she had known and turned to something she didn’t know. All she knew was that this was her chance to atone for the humiliation she suffered everyday. Looking into the mirror seeing her ugly and wretched scarred face. The girl smiled and cooed to Bethany. “Lady it is not so bad. You must get beyond your physical appearance. For it’s not that that truly matters. Atone for the crimes you have committed are all you should focus on. Your past, your future. They have no bearing. You need to focus in the now; Lilith should be your focus. Disposing of her, torturing her. Making her wish she had never come into this state of being.” Bethany ran her finger against the two jagged scars and closed her eyes. April 13th, 2002 Rupp Arena Lillith reaches down with her left hand, and pulls Bethany Yamato's head up off the ground. She brandishes the straight razor with her right hand. Robbie: ... that's what I thought. Nate: You don't think she's going to cut Bethany... Lillith leans over Bethany Yamato, and her hair hides what she's going with her right hand. When she pulls back up, there are two long gashes across Bethany's face. Nate: SHE CUT BETHANY! Steven: I think I'm going to be sick. The crowd erupts into a chorus of boos. Lillith lets her fall back to the floor as she calmly wipes the blood on Bethany's outfit and folds it back up. She then picks Yamato up over her shoulder, and rolls her, still unconscious, back into the ring. The ref tries to climb into the ring, so Lillith walks over and pulls him inside. As soon as she sees he's ok, she falls on top of Yamato and hooks her leg. Nate: IT CAN'T END LIKE THIS! Robbie: Oh yes it can... 1.... 2.... 3!!! The memory… was just there still fresh in her mind. Saturday was the day, her day of atonement. Lilith was going to know the true meaning of……. Well she would just let everyone see.
