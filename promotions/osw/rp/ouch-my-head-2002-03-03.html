Ouch, My Head

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-3-02 This madness that fills my mind; I can’t take it any longer. The clock on the wall has even turned against me. Tick, tock, tick tock. The sound is getting to me so much that I can hardly stand it. The television is my only comfort as its soft blue glow covers the pitch-black room. My head pounding to this discomfort. Tick, tock, tick, tock. Oh, why does this madness happen to me? What have I done that has made this problem happen to me? “Jesse, it’s time for bed.” Comes a voice from behind. Slowly coming into picture, we see the blue glow of the television and sitting behind it is Jesse Ramey. The sound of the clock in the background is heard. Jesse, sitting on the foot of the bed, still off in his own little world. Dazed and confused. “I’m really not feeling all too well, Chass.” Jesse says, still looking into the television, “I’m considering going for a walk.” Chass sits up, “In your boxers?” She asks a protesting question. “I guess you are right.” Jesse says. “Good, now come back here and go to sleep.” She pats the bed. “I’ll throw on a jacket.” These words escape Jesse’s mouth as he stands up off the bed, grabs his jacket and makes his way out the door. Chass left lying in the bed. She is obviously upset. Jesse continues walking down the little ramp of his second floor motel. Thinking to him self about things that have passed and what are to come. The thoughts from Jesse mind somehow make it out, “My mind! My God, this thing is killing me. What is wrong with me? First it was that damn clock, but now I’m all alone and this chill winter wind is making me calm, but still something isn’t right.” Jesse explains to him self. “So, what am I missing? I know it has nothing to do with Old School Wrestling. I beat Matt Blaze. I’m in a match for the number one contender ship of International Title. Wait!” Jesse stops and begins to think to him self even harder. “It does have to do with OSW! I deserve more! Tom Walczak doesn’t deserve to step into the same ring as me. It should be me in that Main Event match with Switchblade, not Timo. Wait!” Jesse begins to think even harder. “I can’t think this way. What am I saying? I’m not that kind of person. Am I?” Jesse turns and makes his way back to his room. “I don’t think that this time of night is really the best to even be thinking about this. I’ll just talk with Chass about it in the morning. Man, I’ve got problems.” Jesse slowly makes his way back to his room and closes the door behind him. ~The End~
