Proof not to trust a best friend in matters of your sanity... (Part 1 of 3)

10 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 10th, 2002 'Your best friend gets the bright idea that taking you to some sappy movie might perk you up after getting chewed up and spit out all over a monkey corpse by your psychotic and now EX-boyfriend...' 'Well, at least she's trying harder than everybody else...even your own God damned family.' Amy usually over-thought things, but now any extreme of emotion would be perfectly rational. She was staring into a little pocket mirror, making certain she looked somewhat presentable for the movie premiere Breanne had somehow gotten tickets to. Sure, her safety-pinned jeans and two-sizes-too-small Metallica t-shirt didn't exactly measure up to be any ball gown, but was it like Amy Campbell to care? One blue eye flecked with green and another brown eye were peering far into themselves before she glanced above the mirror held in front of her face to once again survey how badly she'd mangled the pricey suite when unleashing some bottled emotions, actually thanks to some bottled vodka, the previous night. ''Look at you,'' She chuckled a bit, facing bold eyes in the mirror again. ''...perfectly free now.'' Just then, the door swung open about as wide as Breanne LeLievre's mouth was when she stepped through it. ''Dude...'' Bre looked at the shattered glass and splintered wood under foot. ''...this place looks like an episode of Behind The Music!'' Aimz shrugged, grabbing a rather distressed leather coat from the bed.''Meh, I'll pay for it.'' ''You totally trashed your room, you rockstar you.'' She winked at the redhead, smacking her on the back before they stepped out into the hallway. ''So? Seems like everybody in OSW can get away with trashing shit...Corg can even get away with killing animals, I think I'm entitled to the occasional temper tantrum.'' Miss LeLievre couldn't help but add her two cents before they made it to the elevator... ''Especially considering the shit you've put up with...'' Translation? 'Watch out for the shit you're ABOUT to put up with...'.
