Going Home (2/2)

21 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 21, 2002 “Ohhh Mikey! You got your juice all over my face!” A man moaned. His perfectly toned abs shone as the light glistened off the thin layer of sweat that accented every chiseled muscle on his slim figure. His face was sharp and accented, with a light brown brush cut not taking anything away from his handsomeness. White liquid streamed down his body, dripping in and out of each abdomen until it eventually reached his tight black spandex shorts. The white creamy liquid collided with his dark, well-tanned skin and when he wiped the fluid from his body, it left a sticky trace. “Ah’m sorry mistuh Johnson. I didn’t mean tah!” Came the voice from the very massive black man who wore a simple black t-shirt and Hawaiian shorts. His simplistic looking face was humorous to see until you noticed his massive 7’5, 545 lbs frame of muscle. That’s when people fan from him, that’s when people feared him. The shapely man, formerly known as Frank Swallows of CLUB fame, wiped the remnants of Mikey Thomas’ favorite juice, Coconut flavored SOBE. It was white like milk, but tasted like coconut and water. The memory of the juice made Frank cringe. Nowadays, Frank went by his real name, Frank Johnson, and due to his only really making it in Indy federations and underground fighting organizations, he made the transition to retirement perfectly; Or, at least if you call taking care of Mikey Thomas retirement. After Frank’s longtime good friend Reno Davis was called upon to work at a company called Old School Wrestling, Mikey had no one to take care of him. It’s not that the big man couldn’t take care of himself in a fight, it just took a lot of motivation to get him over the fear of fighting back that would kill him. The only reason Mikey ‘Freight’ Thomas could wrestle was because Reno told him it was a game. The game, unfortunately, got a little serious sometimes too and with Mikey’s almost 7-year-old mentality (although the man is a math genius!), things got really tough. Moncton had a high quantity of racism, which Reno formerly tried to take care of and now placed the burden into Frank’s hands. On several occasions Frank had to show those fuckers that old Frank Swallows wasn’t all looks. “Being beaten by a fag really ass-rams your reputation” as Frank always said. Once Frank cleaned himself of the putrid drink and Mikey finished apologizing, a few heavy knocks came at the door. Mikey, who was now playing with his Playstation, didn’t even look up. Frank’s shoulders slumped as he hated to be interrupted while cooking supper. “Ah shit! I’m trying to cook dinner Mikey, can you please get the door?” “Okay Fwank.” Mikey said, pressing pause on his game and lifting his heavy bulk to his feet. The heavy steel door slid open briskly and at the sight of Reno, Mikey’s eyes grew to ¾’s his face. “RENO!” He screamed. “Mikey! Buddy! How ya been?” Reno hollered back, arms opened. He was real happy to see the big guy again, even though most of the time he was a pain in the ass. Frank overheard the screaming and threw down his stirring spoon, “What’s wrong Mikey?” He called, rushing towards the door. Upon seeing Reno, tears lit up in his eyes and embraced his good friend. “Reno! Reno! I’m so happy to see ya! It’s been awhile huh?” Frank said, breaking his embrace. All in all, Frank wasn’t any different from you or I, just a bit more emotional. Oh, and he likes men. But aside from that, unless he’s in-character, Frank Johnson was a cool, caring guy. “Frank! Hey!” Reno was getting caught up in his hellos from all the screaming and hollering. “Come in! Come on! Sit down! Mikey, you stay with Reno, I’m going to turn to the stove off.” Frank yelled, running towards the kitchen again. Mikey shut the door behind Reno and followed him into the living room. Reno sat down in his favorite leather chair, where as Mikey got his loveseat that fitted him like a chair. The home was modest, with nothing really special about it. It wasn’t too luxurious, but it wasn’t very homely either. It was a cozy little place, but big enough to allow Mikey access to all the rooms. Reno couldn’t wait to go to his room, since it ruled and he hadn’t seen it since he started in OSW a few weeks ago. Frank, Reno and Mikey were an odd trio, odd since they all lived with each other despite easily being able to live on their own (except Mikey). Frank and Reno’s story of meeting Mikey is an interesting story, but for another time. “So who’s the mean man who hurt you and the lady?” Mikey asked, referring to Ivan Stanislav and Amy Campbell. “He’s nobody Mikey. He’s Just a friend of mine who’s playing a game. It’s all fun, remember that okay.” Mikey nodded and then slammed his feet against the floor, which meant a thought had just come to him. “Mr. Davis!” He often called Reno that for some reason, “I beat the orange man and now,” Mikey puffed his chest out, making his inhumanly large (more so than he already is), “Ah’m the champion!”. Mikey laughed out loud, sending a terrifyingly deafening boom of a laugh throughout the home. Frank entered the room, now wearing some khaki shorts. Swallows rolled his eyes and flopped down onto the large couch to Reno’s left. “Is he still gloating over that little title of his?” Frank asked, obviously he had heard it many times now. “He’s jus’ jealous ‘cause ‘cause ‘cause ‘cause he never won his match against the blue pants man.” Mikey stated, his ego and pride filling the room. Frank laughed and looked over at Reno, who was confused. Reno knew that by the orange guy, Mikey meant Kevin Latlor, the former Intercontinental Champion (since Mikey apparently beat him). Kevin was a swell man, who treated Mikey kindly and was careful not to hurt Mikey in the ring, whereas it was Kevin who was in grave danger. The blue pants man was a mystery to Reno. “He means Sean Lesega. He’s an asshole who decided to attack me when my back was turned. That pussy...couldn’t even fight a little wimp like me fairly! It’s okay though because I’ll get my revenge regardless of this big oaf’s claims,” Frank pointed to Mikey who giggled like a child. It was eerie when Mikey giggled. Reno never knew why. “So it sounds like RAW is turning out to be treating you guys well.” Reno stated. “The men don’t come anymore! Frank beat ‘em all up! With a bat!” Mikey proclaimed. “A bat eh? Geez Frank. What happened to the good old fashioned fag fist bashing you used to throw out?” Reno teased. “Shut up, these guys were big...and there were lots of them. The bat was an equalizer.” “Why are you here?” Mikey asked abruptly, “I just saw you on tv. You hafta be in Winee...Winneeepeeeg,” Mikey sounded the word out and nodded in his satisfaction before continuing, “Wineepeg by this Saturday.” “Yeah, you completely past your destination just to see us. What’s up?” “Actually, I’m heading off to Washington for a protest.” “Protest?” Frank asked, “What for? You don’t ever go to those things.” “It’s actually for a good cause though. They want peace in Afganistan.” “Ahh,” was ass Frank said. He knew Reno had strong feelings about peace in the world, especially since September 11. Reno was down at Ground Zero helping out within the next day, since he was only three states away from New York. “When’s that?” Mikey asked. “I think it’s on Thursday. I’m pretty sure it’s on Thursday.” Reno nodded, accepting his own answer. “According to whom?” Frank inquired. “Some ARA loser.” Reno shot a dirty look at no one in particular at the thought of the ‘Anti Racist Action’ group based in most of the bigger cities around Canada...maybe the US too, but he wasn’t sure. They were no better than the racists in which they fought against, but that’s a rant Reno may bring up some day. “Can we go too?” Mikey asked. “No.” Frank said “Yes.” Reno said, meeting the gaze of Frank after both conflicting each other. “Uh Oh...”Mikey said. “Why would we?” “Why not? Mikey’s never been to Washington!” “Reno, it’s a protest! It’s going to turn into a bit R-I-O-T!” “Frank! It’s a peace protest. Why would they result in violence?” “Point...but still...” Frank considered. Come tomorrow, Reno, Mikey and Frank were going to all be leaving for Washington. Reno knew it, Mikey knew it, Frank refused to know it. “I’m going to bed, see ya all tomorrow.” Reno said, heading from the living room to upstairs. “Goodnight Reno!” Mikey yelled. “Night bud.” He replied. “So, what’s Washington Frank?” Mikey asked Mr. Swallows. -To Be Continued...
