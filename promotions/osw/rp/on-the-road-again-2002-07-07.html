On the Road Again....

7 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07.07.2002 In all my life, I've never known exactly where I'm going. I've been directed by parents and by staff, driven by goals and orders, yet I've always failed to make my own decision and follow my own direction. Never once have I thrown caution to the wind and let my mind be the map, rather than follow the same course. This is another line crossed between myself and the person I portray. I still, as I have so many other times, ask myself why. To this day I know nothing. Nothing about myself. Nothing about where I'm going. Do any of you scum, who think of me in the same fashion, know how scary it is to be alone but still managed by any force but your own? Almost as if an invisible shoulder is holding you back from moving forward, a guiding light which you just want to burn out. I feel this burden everyday ... constantly. It will never heal. I witness change just as often ... unfortunatly it is never within myself. No. When it comes to Kory Storm, or any other personality I may have on a given day, I'll always be the same. Like a dog on a leash, I'll always be led. It used to be by my parents, who could barely save their own lives aside from trying to run mine. I pictured one advantage from their death outside of the personal pain which even at this moment drives me to compete. I pictured freedom. Yet the bars are still placed strongly around me. And I know I'll never be strong enough to remove them, shackles holding me down until I cannot move anymore, then only a casket and dirt restricting me from life. After life, which I feel is only a gateway into a new experience, will I be prisoner of a higher power? I can only wonder to myself ... myself only. I've said before that I can't reveal my true being, for it isn't what people want to see. I've cared for so long about you ignorant followers, and in doing so I became just like you! In the process I've forgotten everything about myself ... at least all the good. The terror which is and was my life is what gives me the blood thirsty urge and the ability. God, no matter how many times I prayed, would never let me abandon the horrible memories which today are 'Perfection Personified'. Now I can determine whether to thank him or to denounce him and say 'Fuck You' like I do to every other. And it all repeats... I've seen this same field and driven on this same tar over and over ... still each time I cross gives me something new to ponder. I try to explain it in words and in thoughts to you ... yet it all comes out the same. You see me as a person who lives two lives, not different from a common psycho or the champion and his dick sucking companion. You all despise me even more for it. After I compromised everything I had for you ... there was spit from millions on it. Now the insides are cold and bitter. Now all you can see is Kory Storm, no matter how sadistic and hate filled I become. Past music once said 'You don't know what you've got 'till it's gone'. You motherfuckers don't know the half when it comes to that statement. You see me do sick things on television, replying to authority with snide remarks, always making sure to get the last word. In truth, I'm just as insecure as every last human is. However, I'm not protected by the average life and the joys of suburbia. Never. I'm never protected. It shows in the gaping hole between my wins and losses. Driving back to the same landmark... I've overcame many obstacles in my life and lived to tell about it. Addiction. Murder. Loss. Injury. Yet I still find something missing, especially in Old School Wrestling. Victory! Jim Axtell has had the best of Kory Storm over and over again, just like the tires of the truck I drive sleepily have trodden these streets over and over again. Yet each time, he takes what I give, and he gives better. Shitting on everything I worked for. I know that he can't be as talented as I am ... I just know it. Yet the statistics say differently. Many men are now dragging themselves into the game which I play for blood and for amusement, yet I cannot focus when I don't have the diamond shining from my proverbial crown. Until I can claim that precious jewel, I'm just as weak as I thought he was. For I've found one mountain that I cannot climb, or at least haven't yet. Jim Axtell has beaten me, and the stakes are risen now that I have two men riding my losing streak. Michel Krieg. Frankie Bones. I always try to keep my friends close but my enemies closer. I can't say that I've stayed on that path. However, I may have found my calling in this forged alliance, for now I have two people pushed back more than me ... backing me up. I've never had that sort of comfort. Not even the warm hug of my grandmother could give me that safety or that acceptance. Now I feel as if I've found it, allowing me to solidify myself in my own mind as the icon I've so long claimed to be. With them showcasing my talent for me, the world will forever know Kory Storm as the Knight ... while the german and the hobo act as pawns. Life is a game ... and it seems as if my game has been unable to setup ... yet it is still up to me when it comes to which position you play. Silence and the open road. Really makes you think...
