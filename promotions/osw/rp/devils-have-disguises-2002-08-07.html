Devils have disguises

7 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-7-02 As it would seem, she had become very accustomed to this new found freedom from her captors. She was now a loose demon on the prowl, this body simply holding her true form to make her more presentable to the public eye. It was night, the moon was full, and her stomach was roaring with the hunger for excitement. She had been informed of a club named Lair, a Vampire club where the blood flowed as freely as the wine. She was on her way right now to investigate the false or the true. The music could be heard throbbing all the way outside, the people lined up around the corner. People of all sorts, dressed for clubbing, raving, sex.. Callisto took no real note of these people, she instead made her way to the bouncer in front. His hand is placed upon her shoulder but one look in to those silver-blue metallic orbs and he steps aside. Callisto grins and makes her way inside of the club. Once inside her orbs scan the atmosphere. The media was amazing, so many different people, so many minds to warp and some of which were already so distraught. Callisto settles on to the dance floor, moving along with the endless moving bodies seemingly creating a human wave. She was beside herself with humor at the couples in the corners, the couples at the bars, and of course the ravers in the back of which she could see little that were taking the vile substances in. She could have all the fun she liked in this place, and there was so much to offer. But what was this.. A dancing, raving vixen. Her approach was a silent one, but her eyes told the story. One strong arm encircles the woman about the waist, causing her to turn and be met by two piercing green eyes. Never allow them to see the true you. The woman gasps so softly while Callisto’s hands roam over her body, taking full advantage of the woman before her. Before long the woman was following Callisto out of the club environment and on to the street. Callisto’s arms again circle around the woman but this time she is wrapped in the velvet confines of the Dark Angel’s cape. Sensations are wonderful, especially when you feel like you are soaring.. But only soaring to a near by side street where the true fun of the evening would begin. Callisto releases the woman from the confines of her cape to watch her stumble backward against the closest wall. The heat was again rising between the pair seeing as Callisto was already pressed against her in a most sexual way. But penetration could not take place because as of this moment Callisto Bane was sent flying backward from a hard straight hit to her chest. She flies in to a pair of trash cans and flips over to lay on her back with a grunt. The woman can be heard screaming before a loud *crack!* is heard..And the screaming ceases. The sound of thudding can be heard closing in to the point where Callisto was laying. She is snatched to her feet and slapped across the face once and then again before thrown over the should of the figure and carried away without a clue about anything going on around her. Time passes quickly when you are out cold..And now she was awaking with the dull pain throbbing inside of her head and the terrible feeling of crushed bones in her chest. What a wake up call eh? Before her stands the figure that had done this to her, and all Callisto can do is Smile. 'You..Couldn't this have waited until after I finished with her? She was a nice catch and I haven't used my newest phallus as of yet.' A chuckles passes her lips but she winces only causing her to smile again. The figure shakes its head and snears while moving to a sitting point to the side of the room. Callisto smirks and rubs her chest which believe it or not was feeling a lot better than moments before. 'No playful side to you..Never was and I suppose some things will not change. I didn't expect to train so early. But I guess Saturday is coming soon and I might as well get it in while I can. What to begin with hm?' The figure simply points lazily toward a wooden platform where a wooden post shoots upward in to the air. Several wooden branches come from the sides of the main post. Callisto stands from her slumped over position against the wall and makes her way toward the platform, now standing before the post. She knew this one all too well, and without a word said she begins to send jab after jab in to the center of the wooden post. The sickening sound of her bare hands slamming in to the thick wood echoes as darkness prevails
