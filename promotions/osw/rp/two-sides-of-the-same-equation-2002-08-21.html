Two sides of the same equation.

21 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-21-02 There is no wetting this frozen ground and no amount of tears will ever suffice to break the iron hand of such ice. Her tomb is so crooked and ill-conceived that one can hardly believe how solid she is. Here inside of her confinement she works, steadily to achieve something already in her grasp. It was late, most would be asleep by now but for the Olden Beast it was simply too early. Truth be told she had not slept since the match had been booked. She was too consumed with the will inside of her. In the distance something can be heard, resembling the sound of dripping water from a leaking pipe on to concrete flooring. But as we get closer the faded image of a form can be seen. The closer we become the more we can make out and soon it is visible that sweat is dripping downward from the face of Callisto Bane on to the area below her. She was grunting ever so softly as she brings the weight resting in her hand upward and then back down only to repeat and repeat. The weight was clearly marked with white writing, one hundred twenty pounds. She was in no mood for games this upcoming Saturday, it was going to be about bringing around the pain. She was mentally set for the night to begin at this very moment, but she was content with waiting it out. She knew inside of that head of hers that the more she had to wait to get her hands on Amy Campbell the more damage that would be inflicted. In and out, her breathing is steady, as steady as the drips of sweat from her forehead to the floor. She was tiring, but not from the training of this weight, from more or less being done with the challenge. She was powerful yes, and able to lift this weight for more than two hours with each of her arms but this..Really was becoming a bore. The next sound of the night is a loud clank as the weight hits the concrete, leaving a small indent from each of the plates as they make their landing. Callisto was not on the bench where she was a moment ago, but now she was standing before a large leather bag filled with sand to the brim. She was loosening up a slight bit before straightening and beginning a series of puches to the middle of the bag. Its chains creak as she stands before it, towering over it almost with animosity brewing inside of her. The slow and calculated punches were now coming faster and much, much harder until the bag was rocking back and forth. Still yet she did not miss a hit nor did she faulter in her attempts to envision Amy instead of that black bag. Bruised face, bloodied body..Beaten to a pulp before the Dark Angel. She was almost salivating with the thoughts that were running through her head. They were even becoming sexual, going as far as taking the beaten Amy back to her locker room and with wild rage going at her. But Elsewhere.. The lithe frame could be seen from afar, the tight work out pants and lose top providing her with plenty of room to move in the ways she needed to. Her hair was up in a messy pony tail, beads of sweat simmering at her brow while she was working out the kinks in her form. It was Amy Campbell, stretching out her bruises and working around her pains while preparing herself for what was to be her greatest challenge. Callisto Bane. The woman seemed uninhibited in her motions, not looking ungraceful in the least. She was beautiful, even while trying so hard to remain tough and durible. Her mat was dripped on by the beads falling from her face while she preforms back bends, front layovers and so on. She was making sure that while not being the strongest of the two she would be the most flexible in case she needed to get out of a tight spot. But just as Callisto had tired of the weight, Amy was tiring of this stretch routine. She was now down on her hands and tippie toes, preforming push up after push up to maintain her tone body for the upcoming bout. Up and down, and up and down until she was heaving for breath that could not fill her burning lungs. She was passing time rather quickly, and she was wearing her body down quite quickly with the rapid push up succession. Soon enough she was falling on to the mat, having worn herself out within a short period of time. But she was not physically worn out, it was more or less mentally that she was so tired. Callisto Bane had plauged her for more than a week, haunting her inside and outside of OSW. Even going so far as to inhabit her dreams while she would sleep. All things she pondered while laying upon that blue mat, all things she could not and did not care to dicipher. She knew that she would have to work hard to get the upper hand come Saturday, but as far as she was concerned, the bitch was going down. Amy pushes herself up, standing now and shaking off the remains of the thoughts inside of her head. She had no time to think of the things Callisto could and more than likely would do to her inside of the ring. She had to be on task, she had to remind herself that she could and would take down Callisto Bane in front of millions. As she readies herself before the pull up bar a thought crosses her mind and for a moment she found herself wondering just how inticing Callisto Bane could really be. The visions of Callisto grabbing Amy and kissing her so forcefully all of those times before flashed over her and brought upon her a shiver so fierce she almost fell to her knees. She felt ill..Sick to her stomach with fear of what she had just had cross through her mind. She felt as if her will was bending to this things will and it made her want to puke. While one would puke another would chuckle.. Before her laid a floor covered in sand and leather bits. She had finally beaten 'Amy' to the pulp she had so wished. But as her eyes study the sand she smiles thinking about the phrase..'To see the world as a grain of sand.' She was pondering that phrase while walking past the bits of broken 'Amy' and now making her way to the bench press near the corner. She had always admired her powerful form, always so strong and beautiful to her. But soon enough her power would be sent straight to Amy Campbell, all the power would be used to completely mutilate the woman inside of the ring. Another smile forms on her lips at the thought of once again ravaging Amy after the match. So much blood to bathe in, covering the bed and their bodies. A sadistic smile crosses her lips while she lays herself back upon the bench and lifts her hands to the bar dangling above her chest. It is weighted down with four hundred eighty six pounds of plate and bar. She was very easily beginning with this weight, not worried about straining herself as long as she proved to herself she could do this without a problem. In her mind she was still smiling about the thoughs running rampant inside. She knew somewhere the woman was also thinking about her. She knew this because all that she had done to Amy the past week had pounded it in to the woman's mind that Callisto Bane was real. And yes, she was a threat. Callisto allows her tongue to lap across her lips while she pushes up, then brings down to repeat. She had licked her lips and when she had done so she had tasted Amy on them. So sweet..She would taste more this Saturday, soon..Very soon. And this one could not wait to step inside of the ring and tear the smaller woman limb from limb and all over again. Faith,..Gotta keep the Faith.. Amy was now drenched, her top covered in sweat and tears. She was crying, yes..She was. But not out of fear, instead out of sheer frustration of the situation she was in. First Corg, and now this freak bitch Callisto Bane. What was she to do? She was already sore enough from the beating in her main event match last week, not to mention still recovering from her encounter with Corg weeks before. Now she was having to go up against a woman three times her own size. She was working on her toning now, sparring with gloves over her hands with a centered plastic post. Martial Arts had helped her in the past, and she was sure that it wouldn't hurt her to brush up on it so she could use it Saturday in the ring. She had been aching in her back for some time now, after those pull ups she had rested for a moment to releave the burning in her biceps. She felt confident enough, but still somewhat worried about the entire ordeal that was in line for her. She wipes her brow, clearing the sweat away for a moment before grabbing a white towel and drying her hands. If she was going to beat the olden beast then she wasn't going to get anywhere sitting down. Amy takes her place on that blue mat and begis a series of round house kicks to that post, coming back around with a straight kick and then a jab. She was going to win..She had to and if not she would die doing her best. The screen cuts to double sided camera action. Callisto Bane is seen on the left, and Amy on the right..Both being encountered with a lust for the victory that could only await one of them. 'Got to win..' 'Going to win..'
