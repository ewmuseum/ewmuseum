Adventures in Hershey Pt. 3 'Cornfield Wars'

26 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-26-02 Ivan Stanislav and Loyal Communist Party Member Wang remained stranded in a sprawling cornfield for the better period of an hour. Behind them a long line could be seen where they had trudged through the thick vegetation, ahead of them nothing more than corn. It was almost funny to watch as Stanislav, who’s head came just to about the tallest of the corn, bobbed up and down and his shoulders rose and fell while Wang ambled beside him in his green jacket and yellow face, much akin to a walking stalk of corn. “How….much…longer…Pwapowschik??” Wang gasped, the heat roasting his yellow skin. “You…said…..to walk…..fool…” Ivan growled back as he tore a large stalk of corn from the Earth and tossed it to the side, “You wanted..to eat…at Panda….” “MMmmm….Panda….” Stanislav and Wang continued to trudge through the horrific amount of corn. The sun beat down on them. They were dirty, tired, and bruised. Ivan’s fight with the bull was one that Wang would tell his grandchildren. How the Russian Bear stood up against Mother Nature and with one swoop knocked her into hamburger. Btu that’s another story. As for the two men right now, they were anything but ready to tell a story. Stanislav had pulled his suspenders to the side of his body, but whenever a stalk of corn got caught and yanked him backwards, he resolved to keep them over his shoulders. Wang had lost his hat as a hawk decided to attack it, probably thinking it was a frog of some sort. The two men came to a small clearing where the corn had grown relatively low and assessed the situation. Stanislav stood with his hands on his hips, “Wang, do you not have compass?” “Well, of course, Pwapowschik, but I thwew it away.” Ivan swallowed, “And why?” “It always pointed North!” Ivan blinked slowly and shook his head, “What am I doing here? You threw away compass! You crashed Jeep!! You blew up telephone!!” “Now, Pwapowschik, that was the cow. I had no hand in that.” Wang answered back quietly. “Come on, let us get going, this sun is killing me!” Ivan glared up at the blazing sun which roasted him. His pale skin had long since gotten red from the sun and his black shirt had soaked through with sweat, as did his black pants. The waist of his pants actually sagged a bit from the weight of his sweat, stretching his suspenders further. As the two waded into the corn once again, Ivan growled as his foot punched into a hole of some sort. DYAA!!! Ivan’s foot sunk into the hole to the shin. He roared and pulled his leg, but it remained tight. Wang continued to stupidly walk ahead. “Wang!! Stop!” Wang stopped and turned, “Oh!!” He ran over to Stanislav and looked, “Looks like a hole.” “No kidding!” Ivan grumbled back. Wang stood stupidly and watched Ivan pull at his leg. After a bit of wrestling, Stanislav roared and yanked his foot free with a large clump of mud enveloping his entire foot, “Confound mud!!” He kicked wildly…. …as we zoom back to a large view of the cornfield. We can see Stanislav in the distance. He kicks at the mud and slips. With a roar his head disappears, his feet fly up over the tops of the corn, and a loud THUD echoes through the area. Wang cringes. Ivan Stanislav laid on the middle of the mud puddle, staring up at the blue sky. Wang peered over at him, “Pwapowschik, are you…WHAAA!!!!” In one movement, Stanislav tripped Wang and forced him into he mud as well as he stood up, now thoroughly soiled, “There, you can have mud bath too! Get up, we must move on.” Wang scuttled to his feet and tried to pry the clumps of mud off of him and followed Ivan. ~~ THIRTY MINUTES LATER The two men continued to walk through the cornfield. Stanislav’s head barely clearing the corn, as always. It was getting sickeningly monotonous, but things were about to get a heck of a lot more interesting. About 100 yards away, Jed Cooper and Buck Cooper, members of the Cooper family on the Cooper farm and brothers with the last name of Cooper, sat in their beat up 1979 Jeep. They stared out from the Jeep into the cornfield, Jed in his overalls and holding a shotgun while Buck sat behind the wheel. “Dat Damn Bear done tore up our fence, Buck!” Jed said before spitting out a thick line of snuff, “We gotta do sumtin’ about it!” “Whas dat over ‘der?” Buck replied, pointing out to the cornfield as he noticed a black piece of fur, or hair, bobbing up and down walking through the field, “I think it’s dat damn Bear!! SHOOT IT!” “Dat varmit’s gonna regret beating up ole Bessy!” Jed answered, loading the shotgun. Editors Note: Bessy is the stereotypical name of a cow….the Editor would like to apologize to any good looking females named Bessy. As for the ugly ones..now you know why you got named. Ivan and Wang paused for a moment. “Wang, do you hear something?” Ivan cocked his head to the side and listened. We focus on a head of corn in the foreground…. CLICK-CLACK….BOOM!! …and it explodes into a shower of corn, buckshot, and leaves! “AHHH!!!” Wang screams as himself and Stanislav duck down quickly. CLICK-CLACK….BOOM!!! Another head of corn explodes above the two soldiers as a “YEE HAWWW!!” is heard in the distance and the revving of an engine echoes through the cornfield. “Ivan, they are shooting at us!!” Screamed Wang. He crawled on the muddy ground as corn was destroyed from the jeep in the distance. “I will hail them! They might be able to help us!!” Ivan roared and stood up. He waved his arms and yelled at them….. As for Jed and Buck, the moonshine had set in and the roaring of Ivan Stanislav seemed more like the Warcry of an enraged Bear. “Der he is!! Shoot ‘im!!” CLICK-CLACK….BOOM!! Ivan hit the ground again as more corn was torn to shreds. “they are shootin at us still Ivan!!” “SHUT UP WANG!! I WILL GET US OUT OF THIS!!” Stanislav and Wang ran through the field, hunched over, as the engine got closer. “YEE HAW!! SHOOT ‘IM AGAIN!!” CLICK-CLACK….BOOM!! CLICK-CLACK….BOOM! Ivan stood up for a minute and looked around..he knew what he had to do. “WANG!! TO THE SIDE!!!” Ivan and Wang dove to the right just as the Jeep flew by them, kicking mud, corn, and dirt everywhere. It drove off in the distance and began to slowly turn around. Ivan whispered in Wang’s ear and nodded in a direction, “GO!!” Wang nodded and ran away from Stanislav, flapping his arms and clucking like a chicken. “BUCK BUCK BUCK BUCK BUCK!!” Editors Note: YOU TRY TO PUT CHICKEN NOISES TO TYPE! The jeep tilted to the side and veered towards Wang, who ran crazily. Stanislav hunkered down and charged towards the object of his desire: A Scarecrow. From an aerial view of the field we can see Wang running to the left of the scarecrow, Stanislav towards the scarecrow, and the Cooper twins speeding around towards Wang. Wang curves around as Ivan makes it to the scarecrow and runs straight for it!! CLICK-CLACK…BOOM!!!! A group of cornstalks explode just beside Wang as he runs and screams past the scarecrow. Stanislav grabbed the scarecrow’s large post as Wang ran by, “OH GOD HELP!! HELP ME IVAN!! HELP!!!” He steeled himself as the Jeep approached, ripped the scarecrow out of the ground, and throttled Jed in the head with the hay filled person! Jed fell off the side of the Jeep and rolled as Buck stopped the jeep. Stanislav roared and charged straight at the side of jeep. He slammed his entire bulk into the passenger side, rocking it up on its side and knocking it over!! Buck stood up groggily, “What he hell happened??” And then fell backwards, unconscious. Wang hobbled over to Ivan, his face a reddish yellow, “Ivan..I…” he swooned and fell into Ivan’s arms. Ivan growled and looked over the two fallen hillbillies, scarecrow, and jeep. ~~ THREE HOURS LATER Ivan Stanislav, with a fallen Loyal Communist Party Member Wang over his shoulder, rounded the corner of the road and saw their destination: The Panda Palace. It was beautiful. It once was a McDonalds, but had since been refurbished to house the Chinese Buffet that Wang had raved about. Ivan Stanislav, for all intents and purposes, was completely red now, not just from rage, but from the sun. Mud caked his body from head to toe, as well as cow feces. He laughed deliriously as he came upon the restaurant, “We..we are here!! WE ARE HERE!!” Stanislav dropped Wang in the gravel parking lot and ran towards the building. “WE ARE HERE!! DYAAHAAHAA!! WE HAVE MADE IT!!!” Stanislav ran to the door and opened it…. …but it did not open. In the middle of the door hung a sign that said one word: CLOSED Stanislav chuckled to himself as he stared at the sign, “Closed..they are closed..heh…heh heh..Dyaha……DYAAHAA!! DYAAHAAHAAHAA!!” The resulting damage to the Panda Palace would be incredible. The pay phone would be thrown through the large bay window. A parking divider would find it’s home through the roof. The siding would be torn to shreds and Loyal Communist Party Member Wang would find a nice, comfortable seat headfirst through a bush. And Ivan Stanislav? The Russian Bear collapsed in the middle of the street. Luckily, a beat up pickup truck would pick him up….the license plate number? “COOPR”
