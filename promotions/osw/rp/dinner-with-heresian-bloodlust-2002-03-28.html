Dinner with Heresian Bloodlust

28 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/28/02 The sun has set in the city a little over an hour ago. The people are trying to rush home to their loved ones. They bustle about in an attempt to get home after a long day of work and other mindless activities that merely fill the time between their birth and eventual death. It just seems to be the natural order of things. That is the way things go for everyone except for two massive figures that have embraced that thing that most people fear most in their hearts. There are two massive silhouettes standing in the darkness watching the way that these animals interact with one another. Their eyes squint in disgust as they look on at the revolting site that is humanity. The figures disappear quickly with any warning only to appear in another place. They are now in an all too familiar place. The two figures are now in the dilapidated factory that serves as the domicile of Azrael Cain. The artificial light from the streetlamps shine in through the broken windows that line the top of the windows. There is a huge campfire burning in the middle of the debris filled floor that provides heat and further lights the factory. The massive figures are none other than the fierce and feared tag team that is Heresian Bloodlust, Azrael Cain and Hessian. Cain wears his black and tattered robe while Hessian is wearing a similar cloak. Cain’s hair is let down and it hangs in his face. Hessian’s metallic mask reflects the lights being cast by the fire. His green eyes are the only way to see any kind of expression from the huge Goth. Cain and Hessian move quickly to the fire and sit down. All that can be heard are the vile sounds of human life outside on the streets outside of the factory. Cain is the first one to break the silence between the two warriors. “So…this week we face those two pups again. But this time we do battle for the coveted gold that so many of these creatures strive their entire lives for. That symbol of excellence that so many never even obtain.” A smirk stretches across the beast’s lips as he thinks about ruining this little title reign of Civil Disobedience’s. “Those two punks think that they are the ultimate tag team in OSW. They believe that despite the fact that they have gotten the “w” in all of our matches that they are the most dangerous team in wrestling. It seems that they have forgotten how many times we sent them to hospital needing medical attention. I think this week we need to remind them of what true pain is all about.” Hessian replies. Suddenly the two massive rusty metal doors open at the other end of the factory. The doors screech as they are pushed open. They ear piercing noise breaks the concentration of the two massive warriors as they rise to their feet quickly waiting to do battle. From behind the doors the two small shadows of Caleb and Minion appear scurrying in from the streets. Both of them have packages in their arms as they try to force the doors shut again. Cain and Hessian look at one another only to roll their eyes in frustration at their tiny little managers that are constantly interrupting things. The two men sit back down on the floor by the fire. Minion’s high-pitched squeaky voice is the first to be heard from the two managers. “Hey sluts, what the hell do you think you’re doing? We go out and dinner for you guys and we come back to see that you two are just sitting here doing jack shit! Shouldn’t you guys at least preparing for your title shot this week? Christ!” Caleb chimes in not being to take a shot at trying to get under Cain’s skin. “Come on you big bitch! Heh-Heh. I mean at least do something with yourself instead of sitting around here all day doing nothing. Heh-Heh. You could at least clean this place up. Look at it, it’s a dump! Who do you think is going to do it me? Heh-Heh-Heh.” Cain raises his eyebrow and glares at the small hobo with a nasty hate filled look. Caleb immediately begins cover himself. “Heh…Heh…I was just kidding big guy. Heh-Heh. Take a joke. Heh-Heh…Hey look. Dinners here. We will have it cooked up here in a minute. Heh-Heh-Heh.” Cain’s eyebrow lowers. The two managers begin to unpack their bundles of packages in order to prepare the evenings meal. There are large amounts of uncooked meat scattered about on the floor as well as bottles of booze be carefully placed on the floor. Among those bottles are two fifths of Jose Cuervo 1800 and two bottles of an aged red wine in dusty bottles. Cain rises to his feet and begins to carefully look at everything that is being spread around the ground. Caleb is spreading a massive hunk of beef on the ground still sopping with blood. Cain leans over and picks up the bottle of tequila with his pale hand. He pops the lid off of it effortlessly and puts the bottle to his lips. He consumes a huge swig and swallows it without even wincing. His eyebrow rises again. “What the hell is all of this?” He asks as he begins to take another swig from the bottle of booze. Minion looks up and answers the warrior’s questions while still getting their dinner ready. “It’s dinner. This is everything that you two asked for. We have the meat, the wine, and the tequila. We didn’t forget a damn thing. Now back off.” Cain snarls at the little imp, causing Minion to flinch. Hessian, who is still sitting by the fire, can be heard chuckling. Cain bends over and picks up a piece of paper that from the dirty floor that has fallen from the floor. He examines the paper for a second before talking again. “It says here that the this cost you about one hundred dollars. Where did you get the currency for that?” Caleb is noticeably uneasy with the question and chimes in trying to change the subject before Minion or Hessian reply. “Well…you see…uh…it isn’t easy to feed two big guys like yourselves so we …uh…robbed a guy for it. Heh-Heh-Heh.” “What the hell are you talking about? We just…” Minion is stopped by Caleb who puts his hand over the imp’s mouth. Caleb chimes in again. “That’s right, we robbed this damn yuppie over on fifth Ave. Heh-Heh. You should have seen his face when Minion bit in the groin. Heh-Heh-Heh.” The hobo tries to change the topic quickly. “You know, I have been thinking about something and we have more than the belts riding on this match this week. Heh-Heh-Heh.” Hessian rises and crosses to the rest of the party. He leans down and grabs a big piece of meat. He takes the huge hunk of meat and places it on a piece of scrap metal. He looks at the hobo with a confused look. “What are you talking about little man? We know what we are fighting for this week and it is not the belts. We are fighting to destroy those two wretches, Masters and Hawke, so that we can make them pay for what happened at the Pay per View. And we have nothing to worry about unless my partner here is going soft.” Cain snarls at Hessian’s comment. “For the last time, I am not going soft. The wench…err…reminded me of someone from the past. That is all.” Hessian chuckles only to walk past his partner to place the meat in the burning flames in order to cook the meat. Cain looks at the hobo who is all smiles because he managed to change the topic so successfully. “And what are you smiling at, Caleb. What is it that has you in such a good mood?” Caleb clears his throat and begins to address both of the men. “Well, you see, I managed to put something into your contract Cain that many people know about. Heh-Heh-Heh.” Caleb begins to rummage about his person as if he was looking for something. Cain looks at his friend to ask him what he is doing. Caleb just smiles and replies; “I’m looking for the contract. I make sure to always keep it in a safe spot. Heh-Heh. Now where the hell did I put it?” Caleb continues to rummage about in every pocket in his clothes before he finally removes it from inside his grungy pants. “Oh…there it was the entire time.” Minion cringes at the thought of what is in those pants. Cain reaches down and takes the contract form the hobo. He immediately begins perusing the OSW contract as if it is all nonsense to him. Something catches his eye though. “What is this numerical figure here on the front page Caleb?” Caleb is putting a piece of meat on another piece of scrap metal in order to cook it. Cain’s question shocks the hobo. He snatches the paper from Cain’s hands and begins looking at the paper as if he has no idea what the beast is talking about. He flips the pages wildly pretending that he has no clue what Cain is talking about as he walks towards the fire. “I don’t know what you are talking about Cain…Heh…what numbers are you talking about? Heh-Heh. I don’t see anything. Heh-Heh-Heh.” As the dirty hobo gets close to the fire he fumbles the paper and the contract goes into the fire and begins to burn up. Caleb gets an “oops” look on his face. “Oh dear…look what I did. Heh-Heh. Silly me…uh…I’ll just have to get another one. Heh-Heh-Heh.” Caleb starts to poke at the burning paper in order to get it closer to the center of the fire. “Yup…nothing I can do about it now. Heh-Heh-Heh.” Cain looks suspiciously at the hobo. He takes another swig of the tequila and crosses to the fire and sits down. The beast’s eyes are drawn to the flames. It is almost as if he is instantly hypnotized by the dancing flames and the smell of cooking meat. Hessian looks at Cain with a confused expression in his eyes. “Cain?” There is no response. “Cain!!!” Cain snaps out of his trance. “What?” He replies as if there was nothing wrong. “You snapped out of it again.” Hessian replies as he looks at the fallen angel. A smirk slowly grows over Cain’s face as he stares into the flames. As he stares into the fire he begins to respond. “I was just thinking about the how glorious it is going to be to beat down on those two fools again. I don’t want to fight for the belts. I don’t want anything form this match accept for the sheer delight of sending those two impudent pups to hospital again. I want to ruin their dreams and hopes and silence their constant jawing for good.” “I’ll drink to that!!!” Minion chimes in as he is trying to remove the cork from one of the bottles of wine. Hessian turns to remove his mask and eat the meat that he has cooked. Minion and Caleb continue to yap between one another as they drink the wine and eat their food. Cain stares at the ceiling and continues to think about the match to come and the opportunity that he ahs to destroy Civil Disobedience one more time.
