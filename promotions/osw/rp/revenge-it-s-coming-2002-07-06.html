revenge; it's coming.

6 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Saturday, July 6, 2002 'Un....real.' Nick Casey was still in disbelief. It was almost a week after the fact, yet he still couldn't get over the fact. He stood in the shower and tried to get it off with soap and water until his skin started peel off. He had brought rubbing alcohol to it, he had tried paint thinner, and scarred his chest with acids. He had tried everything he knew how to do until he realized something; this thing with Propaganda had left the boundaries of the International Title, it was now a battle of good and evil, Atheist and God. Nonetheless; he was branded. He played the scene over in his head a thousand times, and every single time it hurt just as much as the next. Because right now it didn't matter how many times Nick beat Propaganda, Prop would always have the last laugh, since his impression would be everlasting. Still, it crawled through his head... *** Eric Sagar’s voice. The voice should have been enough of an indication that there was something evil brewing in the room. The fact that I even let myself get into the situation makes me think it is my fault anyway. Who am I to be taking on two guys? The shirt was enough, why did I have to try and be such a tough guy... 'In the name of God, I hereby declare that this boy continue the tradition that was started with young Simon Casey, and continue it on to his uncle Nicholai.' Sagar cried out, sitting in the back with a weird instrument in his hand. The unconscious Casey laid down in front of him, on a table, with his hands and feet tied down. First Simon...now me. Hell, I have the feeling I should lock Christy in a closet during the next Slam! I don't want anything to happen to her, nor anyone else in my family. Simon is still in the hospital, and he still is going to need many months of counseling because you agnostic faggots like to touch little boys. Propaganda appeared out of the shadows. 'Do it, Reverend.' And slowly, Sagar went to work, inking the skin of Nick Casey. It wasn't small by any means, but soon enough a black cross covered the chest of Nick Casey; he had been tattooed by Sagar and Propaganda. Quite possibly one of the worst moments of my life. Not only was I tattoed by the two men I hate most in the world, and I was given the insignia of the thing I hate most in the world. 'Being chosen doesn't change, young'un.' said Sagar. 'And you will never be the chosen one.' But it's about to, Sagar; It's only a matter of time. *** Branded for life. It's okay though; They've awoken a sleeping dragon and given it a purpose. 'revenge; the morphine of the emotions.'
