Rage.

10 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-10-02 'Bastard!' Rings out throughout the backstage area as pans, lids, and trashcans go flying across the hall. The sounds of splinters cracking also filters in to the loud crashes as a table cracks into and lands on to the floor. It is then kicked aside as Callisto Bane comes in to view. Her head was bloody, she hadn't allowed the EMT's to care for her after what happened. Her hair was more crimson now than before, and now not just on the ends but in a spray atop her head. She was furious, her eyes depicting the entire story behind her eyes. Erik Kelly..Had ruined her debut with a single shot to the head with a cinder block. She may not have won her match, but she didn't lose either. And the match had been in her favor. But no matter, one thing was on her mind and that was finding the man who had taken her down in the ring. Former FWO champion..Titles did not matter to her. She wanted him and she would damn well have him. This was the beginning and there would soon be an end. The end of Erik Kelly before he could become a problem for her. She was making her way down the area, slinging anything she could get her hands on across the way. Her teeth grit down in to one another while saliva foams at the corners of her mouth. Furious was no longer the term to describe what she was. She was taking her true nature on, the nature of the Olden Beast. Straight down the hallway she goes, and in to the Cult locker room. She had to calm down, she had to..Soothe herself before taking any further measures. Once inside the door is slammed and upon noticing no one else was around she takes a seat upon the couch nearest to her. A sigh passes through her lips as her hand takes its place on the side of her head. With a soft growl she kicks the coffee table before her, leaving a smudge on the wood. Her face is covered for a moment with her hand but once it touches her flesh she furrows her brows, pulling that hand away and looking down upon it. It was stained in blood! Her fingers are brought to her lips and she suckles. 'Mm,..' It wasn't hers..Oh no it was Jeff Garvins. She could taste the blood of the fallen in her mouth and it only served to full her rage which was still mounting. What a night it would be, and she was least of through right now. Up she stands, stretching like a feline while a loud, almost bone chilling laugh coming from her throat. 'Soon..'
