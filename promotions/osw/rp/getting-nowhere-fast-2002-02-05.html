Getting nowhere fast...

5 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/5/02 Frankie knew it was a bad idea going into it, but there wasn't anything he could do. It had been two weeks since Hardy had been kidnapped and nearly a week since he had heard anything from the police. His only option left was to take matters into his own hands. His situation in OSW was looking up but nothing would be resolved until he had some closure in this situation. With his tag team match against Drew Hostyle and Jesse Ramsey getting closer by the day, he knew he must hurry to grab hold of this closure he so desperately seeks. So today he decided it's time to take it to the streets. The only thing that would solve this problem would be grunt work. He decided to go to the Boy's Club, under the mask of work with the kids he would ask around, see if any of them noticed anything in the days before the kidnapping. So, after the long walk, Frankie showed up at the Boy's Club in the Bronx, much to the delight of the kids. Slowly he began to get them aside and ask them questions. The first kid he talked to was a particularly chubby boy who wasn't too bright. 'Did you notice anybody funny hanging around before Hardy was taken?' Questioned Frankie. 'Well, there's Mr. Williamson, the sports director here, he's kinda strange.' Answered the fat kid, with a very odd confused look on his face. 'No you moron, anyone who's usually not around and who you haven't seen since.' Asked the visibly frustrated Frankie. 'Oh uhh, not really, except some big black guy, looks a lot like you. He'd come up and talk to us a bunch. Really nice guy, haven't seen him in about two weeks though.' He replies as he shoots Frankie an inquisitive look before he walks off, shaking his head making his chubby cheeks jiggle. Frankie continues to question various kids and finds one common denominator, a big black guy. This would coincide with the big black arms Frankie saw twice. Once holding the gun that was shooting at him, and the second time reaching from a black Mercedes to grab Hardy. None of the kids seemed to recall the particulars of his face. 'Perfect' thought Frankie as he pondered his next move. It was apparent that the Boy's Club would get him no where. He needed to try elsewhere. As Frankie walked home, his mind searched for different avenues of investigation. As he climbed the stairs of his apartment building he remembered his friend Tyson, who worked at the DMV. He could get Frankie some access to vehicle records and he could check out black Mercedes'. Frankie searched frantically in his apartment, sending paper, books, and CD's flying. Finally, underneath it all, he found Tyson's cellphone number. He quickly dialled and tapped the countertop impatiently as he waited for the answer. A click, followed by a voice, 'Yo.' 'Tyson?' Asks Frankie rather quickly. 'Yeah, this is Tyson...' Answers the deep voice. 'Yo dog, Frankie Bones here man. How are ya?' Asks Frankie, out of manners. 'Good man, what can I do for ya?' Replies Tyson. 'I got a favor to ask ya. I need some info on black Mercedes in the city, probably in the Bronx. Can you help me there?' Asks Bones. 'Yeah man, stop by the DMV tomorrow, and you can just come up to me, ain't gotta wait in line.' Answers Tyson. 'Thanks bro, I appreciate it.' Injects Frankie. 'Later.' Ends Tyson. As Frankie hangs up the phone his mind wanders. It wanders away from Hardy finally, and it goes instinctively to OSW. More specifically his match against Drew Hostyle and Jesse Ramsey. He's not sure what it is about Hostyle, but he's not too fond of him. The next chapter of this hatred is set in stone for Saturday night. Frankie can't wait. Fade to black...
