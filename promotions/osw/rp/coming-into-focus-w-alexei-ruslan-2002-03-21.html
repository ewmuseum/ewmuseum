Coming into Focus (w/ Alexei Ruslan)

21 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/21/02 *brrrrrreeep* *brrrrreeep* 'C'mon Ivan, I know you're not out having lunch at some bourgeois cafe,' Tempest said as she started to get out of her car, a baby blue New Beetle with electric blue racing stripes. Her little purple cell phone tweedled away, awaiting an answer still. 'Ruslan here!' muttered Alexei Ruslan into Tempest's ear. She smiled and said, 'Oh hi Alexei, how ya doin'?' She leaned against her car, with a small bubble gum pink gym bag over her shoulder. There was a pause, 'Tempest? How did you get this number??' Tempest's brow furrowed slightly as she answered, 'Well, Wang gave it to me when I asked for the best way to get in touch with Ivan. Why?' Alexei's voice cracked, 'WHAT?!' He paused, 'Hold on one second.' Alexei's voice was audible talking to someone else, away from the phone, 'YOU IDIOT!' The sound of something flying through the air, hitting something, and a groan seeps through the connection. Alexei then spoke into the phone again, 'Oh, well then, with that in mind, what brings you to telephone, Tempest?' She snickered behind her hand and said, 'Well, I was hoping to talk to Ivan. Is he around?' 'Nyet Tempest, I am afraid Ivan is not here... he is at Moscow at the moment,' answered Alexei, 'Is there a message that I could relay to him perhaps?' Tempest sighed and said, 'Well, I dunno. I was hoping to talk to him... in person I mean.' Alexei paused, 'I'm sorry Tempest, he's very busy at the moment. If you wish....' he paused once again and thought, a computer was heard in the background. 'If you wish, I can go to workout gym in person and meet you there.' 'And... how is it you know I'm at the gym?' Tempest asked, with a note of irritation. Alexei paused and a gasped, 'Uhh....lucky guess, I suppose...' A voice was heard laughing in the background, it sounded like Wang. Tempest sighed, doubtless creating a burst of static on the other end of the line and said, 'Fine, I suppose I could speak to you just as well. It's business, not personal.' 'I will tell Ivan that you called Tempest,' answered Ruslan, 'I am good at talking business... you may not know but I sat at a diplomatic panel for quite some time...' Alexei began to ramble, talking about the Soviet Union, Andropov, and other relics of his past... Tempest took off her slick back sunglasses and plopped her head onto her arm against the roof of the Bug. 'Alexei please...' she said, 'Make it stop. I get it already.' '....and Lenin said...' Alexei stopped for a moment, 'oh, I am sorry, was I rambling?' 'Like a rolling stone,' Tempest said giggling. 'Ok, so have yourself a little master spy fun and meet me here. I'll leave it to you to figure out where 'here' is.' She clicked off the cell phone and trotted into 'Dumbells' gym, smiling to herself. <c>***</c> ~Focus... that's it, just try and think about what's ahead. A head. My head. My achin' head. Ohhhh... dammit, didn't mean to go there again. Where was I? Oh yes...~ Tempest stared at the small punching bag suspended above her... way above her and assumed a stance. 'That's the first trick,' she said and hopped a bit to one side. 'Staying out of his grasp is important.' She leaped and connected with a kick that snapped the bag into a hard ricochet on it's own chain. She heaved a sigh as it squeaked in protest, and mopped her brow with a fluffy white towel. Sweat drenched her heather gray half tee forming a triangle between her shoulders and a line running down her back. She turned, revealing a cartoon drawing on the front of a kitten with boxing gloves. She took a deep breath and once again flew at the bag, this time landing three successive, rapid fire kicks, two sideways, one forward, and combined them with a flip on the way back down that would catapult her from harms way had her opponent had arms to strike back with. In the background a door opened and the form of Alexei Ruslan walked into the building, clad in his trench coat, hat, pants, dress shoes, and a look of absolute disgust. He turned and pointed through the door, 'Do not follow me, beast!' He turned and walked over to Tempest without a hint of happiness on his face, 'There are animals in this place, Tempest!' He eyed the bag beside him... 'Yeah and Trouble isn't even here. Hey Alexei, think fast!' Tempest said as she leapt for the bag. Alexei screamed as Tempest kicked the bag and knocked it straight into him, 'DYUUhhh,' Alexei groaned and fell over, rolled deftly on his back and got up. In an instant his reflexes kicked in and he drew a gun and aimed it at the bag... 'Alexei!' screamed Tempest. 'Put that away!' She sighed and shook her head. 'Look the cold war is over and no counter revolutionary punching bags are after you. So put.. the gun.. down.' Alexei breathed heavily and slid the gun back under his jacket. He fixed his collar and walked over beside the punching bag, 'I am quicker on my feet than you may think, Tempest.' 'Funny, looks more like your trigger finger than your feet, but no matter,' she said with a shrug. She frowned and added, 'But it looked like you were limping there for a second, are you ok? I was just playin'... didn't mean to throw you off that bad.' Alexei took off his hat and rubbed his head which revealed a bandage due to the bowling ball slamming into his skull during his... espionage, into Tempest's apartment. 'It is nothing, hurt it on a mission, that is all.' Alexei shivered for a moment and replaced his hat. 'A mission...' Tempest began and looked at him with a devious smile. 'Of course. Well, I have a another mission for you, if you're brave enough to accept it.' She mopped her brow and leaned back against a weight rack. Alexei crossed his arms, unimpressed, 'Oh yes, and what would that be?' 'Convince Ivan to stay out of my match with Meanstreak,' she said nonchalantly, with a mild smirk as if it were simple feat. Alexei coughed violently and shook his head, 'Uh..what?' She shrugged with an innocent air and said, 'Well, you're his most trusted advisor, so if you really believed it was in his best interest, you'd tell him wouldn't you?' He shook his head, 'But you don't understand, Tempest,' he took a step closer to her and talked softer, 'I want to attack too.' She folded her arms and leveled a slightly less cheery gaze at him as she said, 'Then I guess I'll just have to convince you what a bad idea that would be.' Alexei chuckled and raised an eyebrow, 'Really?? And how will you convince Alexei Gregorovich that it is bad idea?' He shook his head, 'If Ivan and I are in agreement, NOTHING will change our minds.' Tempest looked up thoughtfully and pressed one finger to her lips as she seemed to contemplate this. After a moment she said, 'Well, I could always tie you to that punching bag and convince you by way of a stunning set of flying kicks, or I could simply remind you that this time it isn't actually any of your business.' Alexei's eyes darted to the punching bag and then back at Tempest, he swallowed and took a step back, 'Now Tempest, hold on, force never solved anything...' She grinned and said, 'And that's exactly why you and the rest of the Red Army should stay out of my match with Meanstreak. I asked for it because I have a point to make. I need to stand up for myself against him, whatever the odds are, and do so alone.' She shrugged. 'If for no other reason than to show that I can. So how will having the Red Army attack him accomplish that?' Stubbornly, Alexei paced back and forth and drew his hands behind his back, 'You do not understand our position Tempest.' He paused and turned to her. 'Jane Reagan is using this match to see if she can stop us! We cannot give our ground! We cannot yield to her!!' He turned and walked in the opposite direction, 'If we do, she'll know where she has us! Besides, Ivan....' He stopped suddenly with his back to Tempest. She tilted her head curiously and said, 'Ivan... what?' Alexei turned and stammered, spooked, 'N..nothing..nothing at all...' He jammed his hands in his pockets, seemingly getting ready to leave. Tempest frowned and set her hands on her hips as she said, 'Hold it right there buster. Whatever it is you think this match means to the Red Army is nothing compared to what it means to me. It's my match, not yours. Reagan made it with the belief that Meanstreak can take me out. That's a separate issue though. And none of those issues have to do with Ivan or the Red Army.' Alexei grabbed the side of his head, bent at the waist, and yelled in Tempest's direction, 'IT IS BIGGER THAN THAT!' He turned violently away from her and produced a handkerchief and dabbed his forehead, muttering in Russian. Tempest folded her arms and sighed before asking, 'How is that? What global conflict have you decided to read into my match?' Alexei shook his head and took off his hat, 'Ivan...' he sighed to himself and turned and looked at the floor, muttering very softly, 'Ivan does not want you...to be hurt...' Tempest smiled a bit and said softly, 'I know that. But that's no reason to call up a whole army to protect me. Especially not when I asked for this. This time he's going to have to let me take care of myself.' Alexei blinked dumbly, 'Y..you knew?' He put his hat back on his head, 'How could you have known??' She chuckled and tried to diffuse a bit of color that had come to her cheeks as she said, 'Call it a lucky guess. This is kinda why I wanted to talk to him. But you need to understand why I'm doing this too. It's not about your Army and Jane Reagan, it's about me and Meanstreak and the fact that I have to face him... not to prove I'm not afraid of him, but precisely because I am.' Stubbornly Alexei shook his head and hobbled over to Tempest, 'That is just the point! We are not afraid because we are a unit! No need to be scared if you have friends!' Alexei blinked a few times, 'We understand why you are doing this, but it is folly to do it alone! Jane is using you! Ed is using you! They are using all of us! And this is primary chance to finish them all off!' He shrugged, 'There is no shame in some help from a few communists!' Tempest shook her head. 'If the circumstances were different you might be right,' she said. 'But they're not. I asked for this match. It wasn't forced on me. I have to prove that Tempest is a force of nature after all and can't be derailed by some psycho job with a short circuit between his brain and the few fond memories of his past.' She shivered a little. Alexei nodded quickly, 'Tempest, I..I do not know, I can ask Ivan, but I do not condone the action. We are losing ground here against Jane Reagan by doing this. He will not be happy...and I doubt he will change his mind.' He paused, 'You do understand that. There are several things at stake here.' Tempest scowled at him and answered, 'No I don't understand. The only thing at stake is my having this match my way. It's not your privilege or his to interrupt what I'm doing. I mean...' She sighed and continued earnestly, 'Don't get me wrong, I appreciate the offer. I appreciate what it means. And I'm grateful for him beating down Meanstreak last time, when I didn't really have a chance. But this time I do.' Alexei rubbed the back of his neck, visibly uncomfortable, 'I will voice your concern, that is all that I can do. I will not argue for you, but I will let him know that you truly are...worried about our intervention.' He shook his head disgustedly at himself. Tempest squared her shoulders and said in a firm tone, as unfamiliar to her as it was to Ruslan, 'You do that. Because I will talk to him before Slam somehow or other. And I'll tell him what I'm telling you now... that I won't take it as an act of friendship to have the Red Army storming my match and losing it for me.' Alexei blinked, taken aback. He nodded quickly, 'Da..da, I will tell him that.. I will tell him that.' He paused, 'And I will use that tone too, probably much to his disliking,' he stopped and thought for a second, 'But then, you have to do me favor.' 'What's that?' Tempest said curiously. Alexei's face turned to an almost pleading look, 'Please do not tell Ivan what I told you!! Please!!' He takes his hat off, 'I should not have said... I do not know what got in to me...' Tempest smiled with a slight chuckle and said, 'Of course not. Don't worry about it.' Alexei regained his composure and dabbed his forehead again, 'Also uh...' He looked down at the floor and kicked at a mat, 'Ivan sends his regards, he apologizes for his absence but he had important uh...performance...in Moscow.' Tempest nodded and said, 'I understand. Hey it's gotta be tough work being a national hero and all. Now, if you'll excuse me I have to get back to this.' She tilted her head and gestured toward the much abused punching bag. 'I keep knocking the stuffing out of them.' Alexei nodded and waved her off, 'Yes, fight that bag, it is nothing compared to Meanstreak. Only man more dangerous than him is Ivan Stanislav.' He shook his head, 'I thought that I was stubborn! I will relay the message Tempest. Ivan will return to United States shortly.' He walked halfway across the gym and turned, 'You know, Tempest, when the bag hits you back on Saturday, you may be asking for this gun.' Alexei pulls out his revolver and flashes it. Tempest landed deftly after another leaping kick and said, 'Alexei, no matter desperate I get... I will never be asking for your gun.' She smirked and started into a series of punches and dodges as Ruslan holstered his gun and jammed his hands in his pockets, muttering as he walked out the door. ~Focus... that's it. So it's just a sack of cotton fibers. It's heavy, it's stupid... not entirely unlike Meanstreak. The only difference is that it doesn't hit back... like a freight train. Try and think about what's ahead, not what has already passed. It's time to derail that freight train, to play a spritely Mothra to his Godzilla, and to hope that 'the bigger they are, the harder they fall' is more than just the wishful thinking of the very small.~
