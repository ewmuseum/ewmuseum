OSW Old School Promos — #722

?Questions? and ?Speculations?

2002-07-11

Vampir Nosferatu

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“May the rampant speculations commence Who is the dark robed individual, the one who is very proficient with a baseball bat nonetheless, who smashed a skull last Saturday night on Slam? My oh my who can this quote unquote cultist be? Who is this entity that The Cult has wasted no time in bringing in? Who is the driver of the yellow hummer? Who really is the father of Brandi’s baby? Who IS Propaganda’s hair stylist, let alone his optometrist? Who IS Aimz’s gynecologist? WHAT is the correlation between Inphino Blitz, Aki-yankah, Rent a Hero, and Rent a Villian? Who is Kriegman trying to fool? A species of race so convoluted in the whos, whats, or conspiracy theories within it’s culture, it is almost entertaining to sit back and watch all you mindless beings jump through hoops and play these little games with yourselves. Alright now I shall give this a try, after all, why not give you more to talk or guess about and answer a few questions the Cult has. WHO will be the female that will be taken on Slam and then reappear right in the middle of general population in the local nearby prison, you know as a little present for the local inmates? WHO will be the female that will come into contact with The Cult and never be the same from then on? WHICH member of the OSW roster will have his noggin’ bashed in again by my weapon wielding cultist…. fear not my children for this weapn swinger doth not require muscle enhancing drugs? WHO is the ‘Cultist’? fWo closes......could he be a transfer? IS he a traitor under your very nose? IS he unheard of? COULD he be infamous throughout? WHO is mentally challenged enough to believe that our grasp is contained within OSW and nowhere else?” WILL there be any more additions to The Cult?” Laughter is then heard as Vampir Nosferatu comes into view, his demeanor somewhat jovial, as Vampir seems to be having fun with these little mind games he is conducting. He then proceeds to walk over to a Wheel of Fortune-like wheel suspended in midair. On it are the names of all the members on the OSW roster. Vampir grabs a hold of the wheel and gives it a whirl, the sounds of the wheel hitting the flapper soon is dissipated by the songs of Vampir singing a song as he waits for the wheel to land on someone. “Oh where, Oh where has my little dog gone………..oh where oh where can he be?” Just as Vampir finishes with this first lyric, the wheel stops and a name has been selected. Vampir with an ear-to-ear grin across his face continues. “Well what a coincidence. Here I am singing Oh where oh where has my little dog gone and who should happen to come up on the wheel…. that’s right little Billy wrestling fan; you hit it right on the bifocals…. Propaganda. So where has my little doggie gone? Now I know you had a little mishap with a toaster, dildo, or something electric but come now, how long does it take to heal? Could it be that your just stretching this whole injury excuse just a wee bit? Or maybe, just maybe that hole between your legs is hurting? I thought you would be more than anxious to finish off something you started, hmmmmm, could I be wrong? What about you Eric Ceegar, are you too busy feeling over ERRR breaking in ERRRR overseeing this years batch of altar boys? I thought you would be more than ecstatic at the newly given opportunity to meddle in my affairs again and finally take me out for good. The proverbial line has been drawn in the sand boys, do me and The Cult a favor and cross it, I dare ya.” Vampir lets out a cold hearted chuckle and quickly grabs the wheel and lets it rip, this time the wheel spins much more hastily than before; you can now begin to hear the sounds of metal on metal as the wheel continues its revolutions. A new name is selected. “Ahhh yes The Throwbacks, my new found friends. I would be remiss if I did not speak of the lack of interest and apathy shown by these two gentlemen. On one hand you have Mr. Gibson, who has yet decided to mumble a single syllable and entertain me with his witty quips, his well thought out and insightful insults, or display to me any sense of devotion or motivation.” Vampir pauses for a few seconds and raises his left hand to his chin as he ponders for a moment. “Now I am sure that after reading this latest column by Janet Cads, where she just absolutely puckers up and inflates those egos of yours to unfathomable sizes, that you two will find it within your souls to put up somewhat of a struggle to save your souls. This little silent treatment of yours is somewhat insulting and downright unprofessional. After all, we The Cult want to at least witness you all suffer, you wouldn’t want to let your precious fans down, now would you? Now I do understand that Vendetta has other pressing issues to deal with, like his reconciliation with reporter Damon Trask. I also understand that he is a limelight lusting glory hound and he is more concerned about what others are saying about him, rather than devoting his full attention to saving his career, his life, his soul, and his little female companion. Now speaking of Miss Weathers, would it not be such a tragedy if she where to somehow fall into harms way? Or if she were to be…. actually, why lay all my cards on the table right at this minute, there is no fun in that. Actually, why not we just let our two boys here just wait like every other soul will and receive their penance at their due times. The time has come for all to witness the pain that The Cult will be unleashing in the coming weeks, NO one single soul is immune. Martyrs, all will become…. deceased, all will be. There will be no prejudice as to whom the darkness will force to succumb. The blood of all will spill upon the walls and the soul reaper is set to begin work. The harvest is in my children, whose soul is ripe for the reaping. So it is written so it shall be done.” Vampir concludes this states and suddenly begins to approach us, getting ever so closer until we see nothing but the red glow within his eyes. A bright white flash then occurs and we see visions of a barren and desolate landscape, strewed with the staked up cadavers of warriors past. More flashes occur, with more images between them. The bloody face of a female as she cries for help. The ligaments and tendons of a deceased male being stretched out like a marionette. A baby playing with rattles made of the skull and bone chips of unknown bodies. A of stone being sprayed with blood, the shadow of an individual convulsing with each spray. A female appearing in the middle of general population of a maximum security mental ward as the inmates approach her amidst her screams. A phone ringing…a mother calling to find out if her child is ok, fearing the worst. A body hanging high, crucified on a upside down cross…blood seeping through the penetrations of the nails, his head profusely bleeding as he wears a crown of barbed wire. Darkness. A voice is then heard….”There is one question that I can answer for you my children. Will The Cult spread some more dark cheer? If you do not know the answer to this question, then you are already at a disadvantage. On Saturday Slam, all will be a witness.”
