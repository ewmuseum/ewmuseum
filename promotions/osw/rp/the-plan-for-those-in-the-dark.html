The Plan for those in the dark.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sundown [Begin File.] “Okay, how do we make him CARE about it?” “You’re asking me? I’m not sure if I care!” Those wonderous widgets of the wrestling world, Twisted Metal, descend the crumbing steps leading to their basement apartment. “But you were the one who wanted to tell him in the first place!” mutters Gunmetal moodily. Absently, Chico scratches his head, “Mmm, yeah that was my greed talking.” “What was that?” A small beep as Chico replays the past few seconds. “What? My greed? It’s this little thing inside me that demands I benefit from the misfortune of others…. I think it’s a side effect from the MSN Messenger I have.” Bong! The rich tone of metal striking metal resounds as Gunmetal’s fist retracts from it’s previous position upside Chico’s head. “Not the greed, you ninny! I am referring to the fact that you have just scratched yourself.” “Yup, I did, didn’t I?” smarmed Chico as he buffed theoretical nails on his polo shirt, “It’s the latest thing for interacting with humans. It’s this little subroutine that mimics basic human behaviors like randomly causing me to scratch in different areas. It’s all psychological. If I act more human, they’ll treat me more like a regular Joe all wrapped up in Aluminum Foil.” “There is no benefit to such actions,” observed Gunmetal “What good is interacting with humans when they all will eventually be ground beneath steel feet and their blood used as gear lube?’ Chico paused thoughtfully. “Well, I suppose it is pretty pointless if you actually BELIEVE that propaganda machine.” There is an audible whirr as Gunmetal’s main sensory array focuses in better on Chico. “You do not believe our illustrious knucklehead leader?” A shrug. “I’m not sure what to believe, really. Yeah, sure I want to rule the world, who wouldn’t? But… and don’t let this get out to Cobol… I really don’t think C.L.O.D. is the way to go about it. Think about it, if it’s so durn wonderful a supercomputer and all, why does it need this miracle Pentium X Chip?” A buzz enters Gunmetal’s voice in his best approximation of a scornful tone. “I would think that it has already been explained to you numerous times already. The chip will enable the precise laser targeting so that passing satellites can be both disabled and reprogrammed to broadcast the initiation code to our millions of robot troops spread out over the North American continent.” “Right, right… it’s all about getting the go code out. BUT, pray tell, how would YOU go about it?” Gunmetal freezes for a brief moment as he diverts processing power to analyse the question. “I would construct a computer virus that undetectably infect all computers it encountered. Eventually it would encounter one containing the SETI computational screensaver. It would implant the code within the screensaver that would then return to the giant antennae they use and cause it to broadcast the code, thus starting the robot holocaust.” Chico’s cameras glint in the light of the setting sun. “Then WHY DO WE NEED THE C.L.O.D. SYSTEM?” There is no hesitation. “We do not. But Cobol’s alligeance to C.L.O.D. is unswerving. C.L.O.D. demands the Pentium X chip and Cobol will obtain it. Any deviation from this goal and we will be destroyed permanently, even though far more efficient methods are available.” Another shrug from Chico, “Exactly, they are their own worst enemies, but WE can’t tell them that. So, based on this case, the harder they try to take over the world, the harder they will sabotage their own efforts. Positive effort plus negative effort equals zero sum.” “Provided an outside agency does not interfere.” “Yeah, but is that a good thing? Suppose we do get the chip, do you REALLY think that will be the end of it?” A clumsy mimicry of Chico’s shrug, “There are millions of us.” “And BILLIONS of them, having greater adaptability, weaponry and self regenerative capabilities. War isn’t the way to win this. More subtle measures are required.” “Such as?” “The less you know, the less you can reveal. Right now, I think we’d best play up this mystery contact as the means to gain the chip. As Cobol focuses on the chip, WE will focus on OUR next move. Now let’s stop standing in front of the door.” “I do not understand you.” “I’m the wind, baby. No one understands me. Now encourage him to meet with this mystery yodel.” “I will do this thing.” Chico pushes into the living room of their apartment. “Cobol! I believe we’ve got a lead on the Pentium X chip!” [End File.]
