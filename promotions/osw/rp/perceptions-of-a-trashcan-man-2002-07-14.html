Perceptions of a Trashcan Man

14 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/14/02 Our scene opens up inside the tastefully decorated office of the OSW Commissioner, 'The Scholar' Howard Porter. Porter himself sits behind his oak desk, lounging back in his leather chair, a smile on his face as he gazes at his comrades in arms speaking in an animated fashion. Those comrades of course being Professor Herbert Dorkiman, light from the desklamp glinting off his thick glasses as he sits on a sofa, leaning forward as he tries to make a point to the third of the Vax Triumverate, the evil supergenius himself, Alan Kriegman, dressed as always in his fashionable black suit, leaning back with his legs crossed in the wooden chair on the opposite side of Porter's desk. The colossal Armour stands quietly, massives arms crossed in front of the closed door, silently watching the meeting in front of him. In a corner of the office, sitting with his back to the wall on the floor, arms crossed on his knees is the Trashcan Man. He gazes adoringly up at Kriegman, then shakes his head slightly as a look of confusion spreads across his face. A gutwrenching motion of the camera, and suddenly we are in the perceptions and thoughts of the hardcore legend. The voices in the room are a muted buzz in the background of the Trashcan Man's musings. Flashes of memory overlay the room, hardly staying long enough for the viewer to register them before going back to his view of the office. Why? Why am I here? Why did I do what I've done. What is it that I have done? What brought me to this? One of the memory flashes come across and solidifies. Terror fills the Trashcan Man's heart as the beady eyed, hawk nosed face of his old tormentor and creator cruelly smiles down at him. Trashy looks down his body as best he can, knowing he can't raise his head from the strap securing his forehead to the table. He tests both hands, knowing that it is futile, and is of course correct, as both writs are manacled down. He gazes back up at Dr Lovecraft, who seems amused at his feeble attempts at escape. 'Ah, my finest piece of work, do you think that I would let you escape? I think it's time for you to be punished again...' Trashy tracks the maniacal doctor as he turns towards a machine. NO! We're back to Trashy's current view of the Think Tank, smiling and laughing at their latest evil escapade. Trashy shifts restlessly. Must not go back there again. Too dangerous. Too painful. No stranger to pain. But the headaches... those were worse. Another memory solidifies to the Trashcan Man, staring at himself in a hotel bathroom mirror, one gloved fist rubbing at the side of his head. The other reaches for a bottle of Jack Daniels. He tilts it back and drinks the last dregs before tossing the bottle behind him, smashing it against the tile floor of the bathroom. And suddenly we're back to the office again. The booze didn't kill the pain, but it made it bearable. But it did me no good. The headaches grew worse and worse. Drinking myself into unconsciousness only gave brief spites of relief. And it drove away everyone. This time we flash to an airport. The Trashcan Man is staring down at the wheelchair ridden uncle of Timmy of South Park fame, Kremmy. 'Kremmy!' Trashy raises a brown paper bag to his lips, taking a deep swig of the rotgut inside. 'So you're taking Timmy's place and going on tour with the Lords of the Underworld. What's the big deal?' Kremmy frowns. 'Kremmy!' 'No, I don't want to go on tour with you.' 'Kremmy!' 'And I'm not going to slow down my drinking! I need it, dammit! Are you so fucking selfish that you can't see that?!' 'Kremmy!' 'I don't give a rat's ass if this is your big shot! Just go on, get the fuck out of here!' 'KREMMY!' 'Fuck you too, you little fucking cripple! I'm tired of taking care of you anyway!' We see the Trashcan Man's boot come out and boot Kremmy's wheelchair over. Kremmy struggles to right his wheelchair. Trahsy takes another swig of his bottle and pushes the wheelchair away from Kremmy with his foot. 'Crawl for it, rock star! CRAWL FOR IT, YOU FUCKING CRIPPLE!' Kremmy looks up at the Trashcan Man, a look of stunned disbelief on the mentally handicapped, physically challenged legend's face. 'Kremmy.' Kremmy shakes his head as he crawls back to his wheelchair. Trashy turns and stumbles out of the airport, ignoring all the looks given to him by the onlookers. Back to the office, the Trashcan Man reaches into his pocket and pulls out his trusty old Zippo. He flicks it open with a long practice snap of his gloved hand and strikes the wheel. Sparks fly and the wick ignites. He focuses on the blue and yellow flame. Shouldn't have done that to Kremmy. No, I HAD to do it to Kremmy, didn't I? Didn't I? Didn't he deserve it? He was turning his back on me, like so many others have. Trashy runs his fingers through the flame. Cleansing flame. It purifies. It drives away the doubts, the confusion. I did what was right. Tonight I did what was right. The master told me so. Trashy snaps the zippo shut and gazes raptly at the evil supergenius, whose white teeth flash behind his goatee in a sinister smile. Master. Why is he the master? He is. No more headaches. But tonight... was that wrong? We flashback to a Trashcan Man view of Ed's bloodied face, screaming defiance. So much pain. Ed spits in Kriegman's face. Anger. Rage. He shouldn't have done that. Not to the master. He deserved what he got. Trashy grabs the teetering Ed at the edge of the stage. He sets him up in the Pyro Driver and leaps off, reliving over and over the sound, the sight, the sensation of Ed's neck breaking between his legs as he drives him into the concrete. Ed. Friend. Enemy. Stabbed me in the back more than once. But was that too far? He remembers looking up from Ed's broken form up to Kriegman's approving grin. The master was pleased. The master was pleased. All is well. All will be well if the master is pleased. All will be well, and all will be well, and all manner of things will be well. We flash back to the regular third party view of the room. The Trashcan Man is slowly rocking back and forth, muttering to himself. Armour glances over and frowns. Suddenly, Trashy lurches to a standing position. Conversation stops, and all three members of the Think Tank look suddenly nervous. Trashy takes a step forward, staring down at Alan Kriegman. 'Kriegman...' Kriegman glances at his two stablemates, a look of concern on his face. 'My life for you.' Kriegman sighs in relief and settles back in his chair, a smile teasing across his face. 'I know, Trashy.' 'Master?' 'Yes?' Trashy kneels at Kriegman's feet. 'I have given Ed's life for you as well. Are you pleased?' Kriegman chuckles evilly. 'More pleased than you can imagine, my Trashcan Man.' Trashy smiles up at Kriegman, love in his eyes as the scene fades to black...
