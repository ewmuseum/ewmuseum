Empathy OD

2 February 2002

Date: Feb 2, 2002 It is -14 degrees outside in Detroit, and its metro areas. It’s a new record for Detroit, setting the coldest day in the city’s history. Wind wooshes by, and the snow falls. 17 inches of thick, heavy snow lay on the earth’s surface. Detroit is in winter hell. Hot air blows from the furnace in the apartment’s room #349, generating comfort and semi body warmth. The apartment itself is small, a purple bathroom, and red bedroom, a black kitchen and front room, with little furniture. All the rooms painted different colors. There are different forms of art hung throughout the chamber. Two people are snuggled on the blue and black sofa, with a colorful blanket on top of them. They are shivering, trying to block out the cold. The old apartment doesn’t produce the greatest shelter when mother-nature strikes. “I’m cold.” Corg says, one of the two people on the sofa. Shome, Corg’s newly found love, turns to him. “Really?” she says sarcastically. “I did not know that.” Corg breathes a breathe of cold air. His breathe is even visible in his own home. “This is quite boring, too.” “And it’s cold too.” There is a long pause between their sarcastic form of a conversation. The pause lasts for 15 minutes, before the wind breaks the silence. A cold blast of wind whips against Corg’s back window, causing it to crack. Corg pays no attention to it, he just lays there, with Shome, curled up with mild comfort. Shome’s hair matches Corg’s couch, while Corg’s hair matches the bedroom. It’s an odd branch of colors in the cold room of 349. “You know, you… we still haven’t released anything about us to OSW.” Shome says, slowly lifting her head from Corg’s arm. Corg is slow to reply. “Yeah, true. They probably already know,” he sighs. “They know everything. All wrestling companies are like that.” Silence falls once again, shortening the time span. Neither of the two likes the silence, bravely, Shome breaks it. “Do you have your mind set for tomorrow?” Shome says. “You know, with Ritchie Syxx and all…” Corg, who has been staring at the wall for quite some time, turns and looks at his secret lover. “I’m not worried about Ritchie. He thinks that life is one big party…” Shome interrupts, wanting to get a brutal feeling. “People that take life as a party deserve to die.” She smiles while staring Corg in the face. “And I know that you want to kill him.” “Of course.” “And you are going to, right?” Shome asks. Corg chuckles. “I would like to… but you can’t really kill someone at a wrestling show, I’d get caught too easily.” “You know what I mean.” Shome says, sternly. “I’m not going to technically kill him, but with in the ring’s ranges, I’ll brutally beat him.” Corg says, calmly, with a soft tone. Shome smiles, with a new idea. “What if you weren’t in a wrestling ring?” Corg smiles back, catching her drift. “Then I’d kill him. I’d staple his forehead into a wall as you let the rabbits eat his feet.” Shome giggles, still keeping her mind set at extreme sadist. “Fun… very fun.” “Indeed.” Silence, once again.
