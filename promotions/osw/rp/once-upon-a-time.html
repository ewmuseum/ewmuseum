Once upon a time...

[Begin File.] Once upon a time in a far away land, there was a heroic and handsome knight. This knight performed great deeds on a daily basis and it wasn’t the least bit unusual for him to save the kingdom three times before breakfast. What can I say? The times were THAT perilous. There he was, taking it easy on the bright beach, catching some rays as a just reward for foiling a plot to assassinate the king that involved roughly half of the nobles. This was problematic, because just last week, the OTHER half of the nobles had been collectively put to death because of gross tax evasion… They knew the risks. The punishment was clearly posted on the ‘Wall O’ Laws’ that surrounded the royal courthouse in an ever-widening spiral. Nevertheless, this series of events had left the kingdom in a rather curious position, for the basic concept of feudalism involves the nobles providing the military might in exchange for wealth and position granted by the king. Let me spell it out for you… the standing army was about as effective as France’s was during World War 2, or a certain ex-president’s resolve in the face of temptation. (I.E. The tensile strength of tissue paper soaked in water.) As was usual in such situations, a vast horde of darkness had gathered and was menacing the countryside, an encroaching evil that was building up to sweep away the last traces of civilization into the drink. The knight didn’t mind. After all, job security is a desirable trait in any profession, and he still had payments on two castles, a fast horse, and several shiny swords to consider. Nope, encroaching threat of imminent annihilation, oppression, damnation, etc., etc., etc. aside, things were pretty fat and happy in the knight’s secret elven bank accounts. “Sir! The king requests your presence immediately!” Ah yes, the call to action. That made only seventeen today, but then even unholy fiends liked to have their weekends off. The knight, clanking and clattering in his heavy plate mail armor, stood up and began brushing sand free from the joints. “What do you have for me this time?” “Princess kidnapped by the minions of the Evil Overlord, sir!” “Princess you say? Which one?” “Her most royal princessness, the lady Olivia, sire!” That was the money shot. The king, despite a numerous succession of wives, mistresses, kinky fertility rituals and tinctures of questionable composition, had thus far managed to produce over 28 children, all of whom were female. While he was proud of his near statistically impossible record in ‘Ye Olde Guinness Tome of Realme Recordes’, it did bring up the questionable question as to who would be the one to inherit the goats, as the saying went. The normal manner of handling things (that is, selling off the eldest daughter to the highest noble bidder) was unlikely to happen what with the nobility being summarily disposed of. Likewise, pawning her off on some inbred son of a neighboring land was out of the picture, due to the fact that the gathering horde, as is the wont of such organizations, had neatly swallowed all of the neighboring lands at this point in time. With a member of nobility unavailable at the moment, the only other option was to dig up a hero. A chronic optimist, the king decided that he would make the best of the situation and proclaimed that whoever should rescue his eldest daughter Olivia from certain doom would wed her, thus becoming his son and heir to the throne. Much to his dismay, Olivia was of a different opinion of the entire matter. Olivia was fair of face, of reasonably fair figure, and possessed of a fairly gargantuan dowry, as is traditional in these stories. Unfortunately for the plot, she also had a functional mind. (Though it wasn’t widely advertised in order to keep from scaring away any leftover nobles that might happen to miraculously turn up from wherever mysterious nobles are wont to turn up from miraculously.) Where her younger siblings couldn’t manage to get fully dressed in the morning without getting kidnapped, (Villains had learned early on that it was MUCH easier to toss a half naked princess onto the back of your fiery steed than one that was fully decked out in over sixteen different layers of whalebone and tent-like petticoats… more photogenic, too.) Olivia had nasty habit of increasing the number of serving eunuchs in the castle whenever a would-be abductor dropped by. Sure, it was a bit uncomfortable sleeping with the brace of loaded crossbows under the pillow, and all the knife sheaths did itch on occasion, and there was the CONSTANT weeping of the decoys throughout the night, but at what price freedom? As they say, an ounce of prevention is worth a pound of cure and a poisoned spike pit trap’ll lay the hurtin’ on real quick. But now Olivia, thank the gods, had FINALLY been taken! And it was time for blue-twisted, cold-rolled, double-edged, two-fisted justice to swoop down and deliver the kingdom, or its cash equivalent, into his hands! … oh and to save the princess, that too. Mustn’t forget. “Ride like the wind, Silverlightningthunderbolt! … will you winch me up faster already?!” “Sorry sire.” Stupid lifts, it always took forever to get properly mounted on the damned horse. “Where was I?” “Like the wind, sire.” “Thank you. Hiya!!!” Pausing a moment for the obligatory ‘horse rearing up in front of picturesque scenic background’ (the serfs love that kind of thing) the knight sped off at a full gallop across the equally picturesque countryside. Wait a bit until the director gets it all out of his system… … and cut to an impressive looking castle, all prettied up with banners and whitewash and bits of shrubbery made out to look like cute lil’ animals. Nice to behold, but overall, about as protective as having a belt of steaks on while being attacked by starving wolves. The knight rumbled across the drawbridge, (fixed into place and largely ornamental) over the deep moat, (filled with goldfish and had a diving board) and into the central courtyard (baseball field). Parking his mighty stallion in the ‘Goblin’ lot, level two, he tumbled off the tall horse with a crashing roar of metal, already in motion towards the throne room. “My lord! I am here!” “Ah good… knight number 23, isn’t it?” “37, my liege!” “Oh, sorry… wrong file. Hmm… my, this IS an impressive record. I didn’t know hydrae could even HAVE that many heads.” “There were many witnesses, sire.” “Quite, quite… erhm, you do know why you’re here, right?’ “If all reports are correct, I am to rescue the Princess Olivia, sire.” “Umm… yes.” The king waved in a vague fashion. “Well, hop to it. Ride off and start unsheathing your sword or whatever... y’know, do valorous acts.” “If I may inquire as to who has taken the princess and in what direction they were headed, my lord?” “Hrrm? Oh, yes. It was… now who was it that we sent the invite to this week? Oh, nevermind, here it is… Mak’lavaar the Enchanter. Nice fellow. Sharp dresser, too.” “I see… and in what direction did his trail lead?” “Let me see… ah, the Happy Harlot Inn, I believe.” “The one on Trumpet Street?” “I see you’ve heard of it. Seems the serving eunuchs insisted on buying him a beer or three for some reason...” “The Trumpet Street that is just two blocks away?” “Huh? Not much of a quest, is it? Well, no matter, one takes what one can get, eh? Don’t worry, my boy, the Bards are allowed some creative embellishments when it comes to recounting heroism. So, go hero… things, there’s a good lad.” Seeing as it would take much longer to get his mighty steed as to just hoof it to the bar, the knight did nobly hoof it. Standing outside the door of the Happy Harlot Inn, he could see that quite a party was going on inside. “Mak’lavaar the Enchanter! Come forth and taste my steel!” “I lost my sense of taste after the fifth tankard!” A chorus of snickers and guffaws erupt from inside. “You’ll only be making it harder on yourself if you make me come in there!” “Look, can’t we just pretend that we’ve had our little duel and that I won? I’m in the middle of a darts game right now!” “Come out, varlet!” “But it’s a LEAGUE game!” “Then hand over the princess Olivia!” “Why do you want her? Do you know what she does to guys with her knives?! The stories some of these guys tell would chill your spleen!” Boos and hisses from the inside. “Anyway, I’m only borrowing her. When this match is finished, I’ll just take her back and bag the kingdom. No worries!” “But you CAN’T rescue her from yourself!” “Why not? Not in the rules anywhere! A Vegas wedding, a tragic fall down the steps, and a big happily ever after!” “The hand of the princess will be mine, fiend!” “Oh… if THAT’S all you want, I’m sure we can make a mutually agreeable arrangement.” “AND the rest of her!” “Aren’t we being just a little bit greedy?” “I’m going to count to three… ONE… TWO…” “Can I send out my minion slave first?” “Sure, but no sneaking off!” “I’m not done with my tankard, no worries there.” Crump… crump… the rhythmic tread of heavy footsteps resounded through he streets as a massive figure of cold black steel trudged around the corner of the Inn. “Cripes! What the hell is that? A Golem?” “Yah, you like him? I got him for a song just because some idiot merchant couldn’t figure out the instruction manual. His name is Dos.” “YOU WANT SOMETHING OF ME, INTERROGATIVE?” “Yes. Dos, I want you to pulp this human.” “CONFIRMED, PULP HUMAN.” Like an offering to the gods, Dos’s hand rose higher and higher into the air. “Have at thee, tin man!” Tink! The knight’s sword skittered across the black surface, leaving only the faintest of scratches. “Avaunt!” Tink! “Alack!” Tink! “Hark!” Tink! Dos pauses as his hand reaches full perpendicular to the ground. “QUERY: WHY DO YOU PERSIST IN THIS STUPIDITY, HUMAN?” “Because, Sir Toaster, when I have dealt with you and then disposed of your cowardly master, I shall take the Lady Olivia back to the castle, where we will be wed!” Tink! “WHAT, JUST LIKE THAT?” “Exactly like that, cur!” Tink! “INTERESTING TIMING THAT SHE BE KIDNAPPED ON HER WEDDING DAY.” “T’wasn’t her wedding day until she was kidnapped! When I save her, I shall have her hand!” Tink! “WHAT ABOUT THE REST OF HER? BUT I JEST… SO, WHOEVER BRINGS HER BACK GETS MARRIED TO HER? DOESN’T SEEM THAT SHE SHOULD BE THAT DESPERATE, SHE ISN’T THAT UGLY…” “Her looks have nothing to do with it! The princess will be saved!” Tink! Tink! Tink! “PRINCESS? AH, THAT EXPLAINS THINGS.” “Fight, oversized can of corn!” Tink! Tink! “I JUST WANTED YOU TO FEEL LIKE YOU WERE DOING A GOOD JOB.” Like a mountain falling over, the upraised hand of Dos descended with a mighty clap! A thin mist of settling blood descended around the small crater underneath his fist. “I spilled my beer! Be more careful next time!” “SORRY.” Crump… crump… Dos’s heavy step turned towards the inn. “Good job Dos, you smushed him good.” “YUP.” “You can go back to the horses now, I won’t be long.” “IN A MINUTE. I’M NOT QUITE DONE YET.” “A thin paste isn’t done enough?” “OH, FOR HIM IT IS, BUT YOU TOLD ME TO ‘PULP THIS HUMAN’. AND SINCE THAT’S A VERY SUBJECTIVE TERM, I WANT TO MAKE SURE I GOT IT RIGHT, THAT’S ALL.” Crunchbreaksmashsquishsplattergoresmear! Gunmetal turns to the next page of ‘Fairy Tales for Robots Vol III.’ “When the great and wonderful Golem Dos has finished crushing all of the pathetic human things into grease, he headed back to the horses and led them straight to the castle. The king, overjoyed at the return of his daughter, proclaimed Dos his son and heir in an instant. Dos and Olivia were wed that night.” Gunmetal turns the next page of the picture book. “The next morning, the entire castle lay in ruins with all slain but for the noble Dos. The people were sad that this happened, but happy that they had such a capable and efficient ruler in King Dos. Over the next few days, good King Dos summoned the finest craftsmen in the land and used the kingdom’s entire treasury to construct an army of unstoppable metal warriors like himself. When this was done, he led his army on a bloody purge that swiftly exterminated all of the human vermin from his land in short order… and they lived happily ever after, the end!” Fondly, Gunmetal looks down at the little calculator swathed in a small pink blanket. The display on the small thing blinks out as the battery save feature kicks in. Quietly, Gunmetal creeps from the room and closes the door behind him. He slumps tiredly against the wall opposite. “Whew!” “Tough?” Gunmetal regards Chico levelly. “I thought that she would NEVER get to sleep!” “That’s how it is when you’re a parent, you know.” “Really?” “No, I’m just guessing, really.” “Oh.” [End File.]
