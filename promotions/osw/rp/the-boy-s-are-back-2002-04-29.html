The Boy's Are Back!

29 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/29/02 Old School Wrestling. The way wrestling was meant to be? The new home of Da Westsidaz? Da Westsidaz made a return to the wrestling world not even twenty-four hours ago when they arrived at OSW Head on Collision. Da Westsidaz entered a new world for them. Some old faces, many new, but the targets had something in common. They all were OSW prominent tag teams. What better way to enter the federation than beating their best tag teams? Civil Disobedience and Heresian Bloodlust are two teams that don’t have a pretty past. They both have been at each other’s throats since the inception of OSW. Now, the seem to want to go at it again, only this time, the Westside Wildcard has entered the game. The Boys Are Back and they plan to finish what the started in Primetime Championship Wrestling. Become the greatest Tag Team ever to grace the wrestling ring. They started it in PCW. Hell, they OWNED PCW! They ran roughshod for months and no one could stop them. Many tried, but none succeeded. It was only after Da Westsidaz were left in the emptiness of their victory that they left. It wasn’t fun anymore. Management tried so hard to stop Da Westsidaz, throwing team after team at them. Da Westsidaz just beat them into the ground like flies. They stood no chance. Once Da Westsidaz left the scene, many tag teams popped up around everywhere. Why hadn’t they before? Because they were scared. They were afraid. Da Westsidaz created a Tag Team Graveyard in PCW. Now, OSW is next……… ---- On the road again. There’s nothing more terrible about wrestling than not being able to stay home. Hotels in Detroit are terrible compared to LA. Welcome to 214 Willowtree Cr., Home of Andrew Washington or Riptah of Da Westsidaz. The scene takes you to the basement of his home, Da Westsidaz’ lair. A couch in the center of the room, a Lay-Z-Boy chair to the left, all facing a huge big-screen television. The PS2 is laid on the floor beside numerous cans of Budweiser. Pictures fill the walls of the room, some from matches, some backstage and some are framed articles of PCW Magazine. The Turn Tables sit unused, but now the camera shows the stars of the show. Short Circuit is playing OSW Crowning Glory for the PS2 while he’s sprawled out on the couch. Short Circuit is a black man with curly black hair. He’s wearing a white beater, Nike shorts and seems to be having trouble with the game. He’s built fairly well for a 170 pounder, but strength isn’t his forte. “You fucking suck man, I’ve seen monkeys play better than that,” booms a familiar voice. Da Westsidaz have been together for a long time and Riptah messing around with Short Circuit is common. “C’mon SC, we’ve got to stop playing games and start kicking some more ass. Last night was just the beginning. We’ve got a shit-load of things to do in this federation.” Riptah is laying on the reclining chair with his shirt of and only jeans on. A perfect specimen of pure weight-lifting and body-sculpting. He’s one tough bastard, as he’s proved before. A blonde woman is laying on the same chair as him, cuddled up beside him with her legs wrapped around his abdomen. Daisy has been with Riptah for almost two years now and not much has changed. She’s wearing a tight tank top and sweat pants as she whispers in Riptah’s ear. “No way, we’re here to have fun. Just like PCW! You said you wanted to be just as good, you wanted everything the same, right? Well you got it. We’re here to fuck around and kick some ass. Eddie didn’t give us this contract for financial reasons, he gave us this contract so we didn’t stay bored sitting at home.” SC seems more focused on the game than the conversation. Shorty is playing as Meanstreak, man-handling Drew Hostyle. “Sorry buddy, this ain’t like PCW where we have push-overs running after us. Have you seen Hessian and Cain man? They’re huge. Probably the biggest team we’ve faced. Civil Disobedience look like some tough sumbitches too.” Riptah kisses Daisy on the cheek and continues watching the screen. Da Westsidaz have been up against some tough teams, although they wouldn’t admit it. Heresian Bloodlust is a strength team, while Civil Disobedience is a high-flying team. Da Westsidaz are both. “C’mon Rip, we ain’t going to have a problem with Heresian Bloodlust. They’re big, slow and stupid. ‘Nutha one of those “Satan Worshippin’” fucks. We’ve gone after tougher shits than them.” Short Circuit shuts the game off and grabs the converter. He flips through some channels and puts on the OSW Head on Collision replay. “Don’t worry, we thrashed all four of those boys last night, we can do it anytime, anywhere. These punks may be good here, but their meeting a force they haven’t even been near the same ring as Da Westsidaz. Civil Disobedience thought they could jump the Tag Champs, but they were fucking surprised when we showed up!” “Y’know boys, I don’t really mind those Civil Disobedience kids. You guys should take it easy on those boys. I mean, c’mon hun, there like, small little kids. You’re big and built, so let SC worry about those two and you focus on the hulks. I’m sure you can take ‘em.” “Nah Daze, we gotta have a concentrated effort.” Riptah gets up and walks to the television and shuts it off. He signs for a minute and picks up a black beater. “We hafta work as a team if we want to really beat those boys senseless. We can’t take all four of them on at once, we have to focus our efforts on one at a time. It’ll be tough jumping around to take them both out, but we can pull it off. We’ll start Saturday, a’ight? These punks will learn not to mess with the best. Question is, which one do we knock straight the fuck out first? The first Tag Champs or the current ones? I mean, Hessian and Cain took one helluva beating so their probably still sore and Civil Disobedience seem more our style to go up against…Y’know what, we’ll worry about our match against Civil Disobedience. Screw Heresian Bloodlust for now, we’ll beat those two assclowns into the ground.” Riptah slips on the beater and heads upstairs. Daisy shrugs and stands up slowly. “Ahh…well, at least you guys get to have some fun. Hopefully I can find somebody to hang out with in this dump.” She gets up and leaves the room and SC is left all alone. “Whoa, guys, wait up. We got some plannin’ to do. We’re back and it’s time to prove we ain’t over the hill. Beating these two punks Saturday’ll make our return complete and prove to OSW that Da Westside is Da Bestside.” Short Circuit mumbles as he runs up the stairs to find Riptah and Daisy, while the screen fades to black.
