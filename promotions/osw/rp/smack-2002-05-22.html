SMACK!

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Wednesday, May 22nd SMACK! It was the slap heard around the world. If you watch TV, it's not hard to remember. It was the 7th installment of MTV's popular series The Real World. SMACK! If you watch MTV, it's always brought up. At some point, you'll hear about it again. It's almost natural to talk about it. SMACK! The episode when Stephen smacked Irene. That will be an episode that lives in television infamy. MTV is known for poor taste in shows. How else would TRL be on the air? But even MTV executives, who also happen to be members of the group formerly known as DAMN, have to be ashamed of this. SMACK! 'A marriage between you and I would never work out,' Irene said to Stephen, 'because you're a homosexual, Stephen.' As Irene laughed a little laugh, Stephen seemed less amused. 'You think I'm a homo?' Stephen asked as Iene started to walk away. He then started to jerk off the air. 'Well you're a bitch.' Not very kind words for someone you're supposed to like. But Stephen didn't stop there. With a stuffed bear in his hand, a stuffed bear that happened to belong to Irene, Stephen took it and threw it off the dock and into the water. But it didn't stop there. 'Let it go, Stephen!' shouted one of his fellow housemates. Stephen, however, couldn't let it go. He had to get the last shot in. So as Irene was starting to be driven away, Stephen tapped the back of the car. As the car slowed to a stop, Stphen opened the passenger side door where Irene was sitting. Then... SMACK! Stephen did the unthinkable. SMACK! Stephen bitch slapped Irene. Stephen, a suposed man, hit Irene, a girl. Sure, irene caled Stephen a homosexual, but isn't it true? Stephen, who claims to be a man, hit a ldy. How much of a man do you have to be to hit a lady? SMACK! 'Bwahahahahahahaha!!!!!' In a hotel room, Kid Wonder is watching the tape of this on TV. 'He...he smacked that girl!' Wonder shouts out in the middle of laughing. 'Kinda reminds me of me.' Luckily, Amanda is nowhere around or she'd smack him. SMACK! A replay of the smack is shown again. SMACK! And again. SMACK! And again. And it'll continue to be shown as long as The Real World is on television. Wonder watches and just smiles. 'I need to figure out how he did that so easily,' Wonder says to himself. 'It looks so fun, too.' Wonder reaches to his side and picks up a newpaper. He opens the thing and takes a good, hard look at what is inside. 'Gotta find me a house first. Stupid teenyboppers,' Wonder mumbles. As Wonder glares at the paper, something apparently catches his eye. His eyes widen. A small snicker comes from him. And we can only assume a small smirk is on his face, but the paper is covering it. Whatever Wonder is reading, he doesn't signify what it is as the picture fades out.
