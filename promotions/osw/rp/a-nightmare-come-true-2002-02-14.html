A Nightmare Come True...

14 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14/02/02 “Come on boys time for your first day of school!” In their messy abode of comfort two little 5-year-old faces popped out from behind the soft cotton and silly grinning designs of their quilts. As their mother smiles eagerly through the door at her children they look at one another. On the top bunk the older of the brothers smiles goofily at his little brother below. Atop his bunk the older of the two looks across the colourful network of toys and clothes to two little red plastic chairs, on top of which are a pair of little black trousers, a pair of little white shirts, and two little red ties. “Mommy, what’s school?” asks the younger of the two. Their mother looks blankly at her son and shakes the dazed look out of her eyes. “Why Sebastian, I told you! School is that fun place where you go to learn about things!” “Mommy I told you, Sebastian is a babies name! Call me Seb from now on!” “Ok then if you want me to call you so, Seb. Now hurry up Travis you should be helping your little brother get dressed for school, now get to it!” Seb and Travis’ mother exits the room, leaving her rather slow minded boys to get ready for the first day of the rest of their lives… “Come on boys the bus will be here in 15 minutes and you haven’t eaten your breakfast yet!” The boys’ mother shouts through the hall, and a loving tear wells up in her eye as the boy’s bedroom door opens and out they walk, her two little boys dressed smartly in school uniform. As the boys head for the bathroom their mother wipes her eyes and heads back to making breakfast. A few more minutes pass and the boy’s emerge from the bathroom, clean and presentable. They waddle through to the dining room and hop up onto a chair. “Mommy do we have to do this EVERY morning?” Asks a grumpy Seb. “Yes but the best part is when it’s all over, wouldn’t you say so son?” “I guess, what’s for breakfast?” replies the fooled Seb, not realising he’s in for over a decade of the same routine every morning. At calling his mother about the first meal of the day, Seb and Trevor’s eyes widen to the size of dinner plates at their mother brings through a big plate of 20 or so pancakes, smothered in maple syrup. As she lays it down onto the table the boys look at each other and go wild, grabbing a fork from the plate and digging into the delectable breakfast. Of course nothing lasts forever, and pretty soon the towering pile of pancakes is reduced to crumbs and little specks of syrup. Seb and Travis wipe the crumbs from their shirts and walk back through to the hall where their mother is waiting, with a camera in hand. “Smile for the birdie!” she shouts, and Seb and Trevor grin cheesily as their image is captured to film. Then from outside, as if running by clockwork is the sound of the horn, being rapidly bashed by the sexually frustrated and overall grumpy bus driver. Mother hurries to get the boy’s schoolbags and coats. As with any mother hurrying her child/ren off, the last minute advice comes forth. “Now remember boys, always listen to your teacher, make lots of friends and for goodness sake……………don’t get into any fights…” That sticks out in the boys’ minds. Being brothers, fighting is a part of growing up, and something in their minds tells them that a school full of people wouldn’t be a bad thing…it’d be a good thing. As the bus rolls along the driver becomes increasingly more agitated at the raucous children. “WILL YOU ALL SIT DOWN AND SHUT THE HELL UP! THIS ISN’T A PLAYGROUND IT’S A DAMN BUS NOW DO AS I SAY!!!…………I’m so lonely…” Of course this being a school bus filled with children on their first day of school, not one of them is happy, and all over the bus snivelling children are holding their heads in their hands and Seb and Travis are confused. Travis looks over and notices a chubby little boy sitting by himself looking out of the bus window. Travis edges across the aisle and pulls his hand past his head, then brings it down forcefully against the boy’s head, and watches as it hits the window and jolts back, sending the boy into an almost immediate fit of tears. Before he can turn around Travis has already tactfully sat back down. Through muffled tears the boy feels the bump on his head and shouts out to those in the bus. “Owww…*Sniff*…who was…*Snivelling groan*…that???” Travis and Seb simply look out their window and smirk and grin to themselves as the boy sits, looking around, paranoid that it could happen again. Others in the bus don’t rush to do anything, having noticed the mean looks on Travis and Seb’s faces. The bus pulls into the school grounds and the double doors open. All the children hop and skip out, and like magic begin mingling and making friends. The last two out of the bus are Travis and Seb, who head out into, in their eyes, the big bad world of the playground. Staying close together, Seb and Travis make their way past the swings, the roundabouts, the tetherball pole, and the jungle gym until they reach a quiet recess in the school building itself. Standing there in their little uniforms, self-chosen outcasts, Seb and Travis watch the normal kids as they point at Travis, whispering to each other about the slap on the bus. Then, from around the corner of the recess appears the ample frame of the chubby little boy. “I got told the truth, which one of you two hit me in the back of the head?” Seb and Travis look at each other and begin giggling to themselves. The chubby boy advances, until his mass has blocked any outside view within the recess. “Hey you two, I’m-a not kidding. I still got that bump on my head and I wanna know which one of you hit me or I’ll beat you both up!” Seb and Travis again look at each other and giggle. The chubby kid becomes increasingly frustrated and forms a fist with his thick globular hand. As he is about to draw back and lay one into Travis, another hand thrusts forward from behind the chubby boy and grabs at his hand. The chubby boy’s hand is dragged down, and he is pushed to the side as a slightly taller, and a more weasly looking boy pushes past his chubby counterpart and stares into the eyes of Travis. “Did you hit my brother in the head?” inquires the angry boy. “No I didn’t touch him I swear!” replies Travis, beginning to show signs of fear in his voice. “Those kids over there says they saw you hit my brother, you wanna hit me?” says the weasly boy in a hissing, threatening manner. Travis slowly slumps back against the slab stonewall, watching as the weasly boy continues to stare a hole through him. Then it happens, all at once, as if in slow motion. Weasel boy lunges forward, Seb’s fist thrusts out whilst Chubby boy kicks out, and Travis jumps forward. Travis floors Chubby boy whilst Seb drives his fist into Weasel boy, who in turn trips and lands on Chubby’s foot…with his groin. The four yell as hair is tugged and eyes are punched and nails are dug in. The children around the playground rush over to the fighting boys, and croon and swoon as the pulling and tugging continues. Eventually the large green, intimidating double doors swing to life and from them emerges the dark beast…the true evil of society…Mrs. Waltham the headmistress. The school kids quickly and quietly resume their morning time play whilst the four boys seem to freeze as her presence seemingly floats around the corner to the recess where they’re all laid out. “What do you boys think you are doing?” inquires the old, gnarled wench that is the headmistress. At once weasel boy and chubby answer before Seb and Travis have a chance. “We were just playing Miss, honest Miss!” “Well…what’s your story?” asks Mrs. Waltham as she stares at Seb and Travis. “These two boys hit us for no reason Miss! We were talking and the fat one hit me for no reason, and…and…and my brother tried to stop him but the thin boy came over and hit him and…and…” “Yes I get the picture…right then…” Mrs. Waltham points her old, wrinkled finger at the chubby and weasly boy, “Garry, James, get your rear ends up off the ground and follow me…your first day at school and your being little hooligans!!! Not in MY school!” shouts Mrs. Waltham, grabbing at Chubby and Weasel’s ears and dragging them around the corner and into the schoolhouse. As they listen to her coarse, ageing voice drift off into the morning sky, Seb and Travis pick themselves up and dust each other off. “That was fun! Them boys gots into trouble!” Seb whispers under his breath. “Fun? You’re saying getting nipped and having your hair pulled is fun?” Travis says through welled up eyes and bloody nip marks. Seb and Travis take a couple of minutes to recuperate, a couple of pain-induced tears sliding down their little cheeks, as the other children in the playground look at them, the uncanny look of child’s disgust on their faces. Seb and Travis take no notice as suddenly from above them the school morning bell rings out, and the children all line up at the front door. Mrs. Waltham opens the front doors and looks at the children, each individually and then as a group… “Welcome to school…” ---------------------------- Inside the classroom, the children are all sitting at their little desks, with their sleeves rolled up and a paintbrush in their hand. In the midst of all the handmade pictures, mobiles and models, the children sit painting whilst Mrs. Waltham sits at her desk, looking over maths answers from earlier. Sitting at the very back of the class, painting a picture of a tree is Seb, and next to him, using his hands to paint chickens is Travis. He applies another coat of red to one of his chickens when he hears Seb calling him. “Psssst………Travis! Watch this!” Travis looks over in time to see Seb lobbing a large chunk of paint through the air, and the boys watch as it falls down…down…and SPLAT!…hits Chubby in the back of the head. Seb wipes the paint off on his tree picture and covers himself by finger-painting, whilst Travis looks down and continues with his chickens. In the middle row of desks, Chubby feels the back of his head and looks at his fingers, the little blue globules of paint running down his fingers to his sleeveless arms. His face turns red and Mrs. Waltham jumps out of her seat as Chubby screams at the top of his lungs, tears of frustration and anger dripping down and along the contours of his cheek. “Garry! What is the meaning of this outburst?” Mrs. Waltham cries out, as Chubby’s face goes bright red and he looks up at her with glazed eyes. “SOMEONE HIT ME WITH PAINT!!!!!!!!!” he cries, and almost everyone except Chubby himself looks to the back of the class, only to find Seb and Travis still drawing and conversing between themselves. Mrs. Waltham grimaces at the boy’s as they look up at her. “Sebastian…Travis…did you have anything to do with what just happened?” “Sorry Miss?” replies Travis. “I said…did one of you hit Garry in the back of the head with paint?” “No Miss, we was talkin’ about our pictures Miss…look!” Seb exclaims, holding up the picture of his tree, in the foreground of a very messy looking, yet abstract sky. “Oh yes Sebastian very good…so if it wasn’t any of you two who was it…Come on…’fess up!” “Miss…what if Garry did it to himself to get attention, Miss?” shouts Seb over the crying of Chubby to Mrs. Waltham. “Garry…is this true?” she says, pushing her half moon lenses up her nose and looking at Garry. Garry however doesn’t know what to think and instead goes back into a fit of tears and snivelling groans as Mrs. Waltham approaches him, getting rather frustrated herself. She picks him up and takes him by the hand. “Come on boy, let’s go get you cleaned up then…” As Garry and Mrs. Waltham leave the classroom James stands up and turns around to Seb and Travis. “I know that was you two! You’ll pay,” he shouts, and an atmospheric gasp echoes throughout the classroom as the children take his threat seriously. Then, as Travis looks down to his painting, he notices just within his range of sight, James grabbing a block of paint, and drawing back. As James releases it, and the block of paint flies through the air, Travis grabs Seb and pulls him to the ground. “JAMES! WHAT IN THE SAM HILL ARE YOU DOING?!” The voice rings throughout the classroom as Mrs. Waltham returns to the classroom, without Garry. She looks at Seb and Travis on the floor. As she walks over to them Travis runs his hands quickly over the paint block and smears some on his and Seb’s heads. Mrs. Waltham stops at the last row of desks and looks down at Seb and Travis. “Oh my…are you two boys alright?” “Me…he…who…Ahhhhh” groans Travis, as his eyes well up and crocodile tears spurt forth. “Miss…my head hurts!” groans Seb in return, and he drops his head dramatically, as Mrs. Waltham turns to James, with a look of anger on her face. “GET OUT OF MY CLASSROOM YOU LITTLE TROUBLEMAKER! TWICE IN ONE DAY? I WON’T HAVE THIS!!!…GET OUT!!!” James runs out of the classroom, and Garry returns, wiping the back of his head with a paper tissue. He notices Seb and Travis on the ground, and begins snivelling again, and chases after his brother. “Those two are troublemakers…bad eggs…dunces…weakest links…are you two sure you’re ok?” Mrs. Waltham says to the boys, who look up at her through faking, dazed eyes. “Yes Miss…but it wasn’t our fault…” “I know…you two are innocent, I know.” replies Mrs. Waltham, as she heads off after Garry and James. Lying on the ground with blue paint on their heads and smiles across their faces, the boys high five themselves. ---------------------------- And so, Seb and Travis’ first day goes off without a hitch…in that they weren’t caught. The adrenaline rushing through their little veins as they blatantly lied their way through a 50-line punishment exercise and the ridicule and punishment of their first day foes. Of course it didn’t stop there…and the legacy of lies woven by Seb and Travis continued for a long time after…of course the years passed and Seb and Travis got into many capers, like setting off fire alarms, bullying kids for their lunch money, stealing toys, tripping people…the usual school pranks…and one year… ---------------------------- 8th Grade – Middle School 8 years later… Seb and Travis, 13 years old and coping with a reputation as bullies. They walk through the renovated halls of their old Grade School, refurbished and redecorated after a fire alarm prank that went wrong, again Garry and James got the blame, but the fire in the toilets went out of control…and here they are today as Middle School pupils. Walking along the main hallway, carrying a blue backpack on their shoulders, everyone Seb and Travis pass backs off to the wall as they make their way to class. The bell rings and everyone else ignores the two, as if Intervals and Lunchtimes are their time only. Everyone rushes through the hallways to their respective classes, but as the halls clear, Seb and Travis are still walking along them. The last class door closes and Seb and Travis stop. Ahead of them at the end of the hallway is a row of blue, 5ft lockers. Seb and Travis stop in front of locker 103. Seb searches through his jeans pocket and pulls out a little red object. With one flick the object releases a long silver object… “I can’t believe you bought a Swiss Army Knife without Mom knowing…” “Hey Travis I’m not gonna tell Mom about a dang knife! I didn’t even tell you for 3 months!” Travis’ gasps as Seb checks the screwdriver of the knife and jams it between the locker and the padlock and begins pulling and pushing it vigorously. “Uhhh…Seb…look here…” Travis says, interrupting the useless attempt at vandalism. Seb pulls the screwdriver out of the locker and watches as Travis puts his ear to it, and listens………Click…Click…Click…Click…. The lock finally opens and Travis opens the locker up. “Travis…when did you learn to do that?” “A little thing I like to call Jimmy the Sneak, he’s in 10th Grade.” Travis replies, as he opens up the locker and looks inside. A few textbooks, a black skull pendant and a picture of KISS are all that occupy it. “So Travis…just who is this guy anyway?” “He’s one of them guys that like the devil a lot. Look at those men that look like women in that picture…anyways here gimme your bag.” Seb removes his bag and hands it to Travis. Travis puts the bag into the locker then removes his own bag. Into the locker goes Travis’ bag along with Seb’s. Attached to each zipper in both bags is a piece of string, which Travis grabs hold of. “Now you’re sure nothing that can be traced to us is in or on these bags?” he asks, as Seb begins giggling. “No man totally, go for it!” replies Seb, who giggles out loud, and covers his mouth with his hands as Travis closes the door over far enough and pulls the string. All at once he slams the door shut properly and locks it again, and listens as the sound of scurrying and nibbling echoes through the steel container. Travis and Seb back off laughing, and head off to class, with the world’s biggest grins on their prepubescent faces……… The bell for the end of class rings and everyone exits their classroom and heads for their lockers to change books. Seb and Travis run out of their English room and run down the hallway to locker No.103. “Stop running you two or I’ll book you!” shouts the hall monitor for the week, a skinny rat-like girl with a prominent spot on her nose. Seb and Travis merely slow down to a moderate power walk, and as soon as the hall monitor turns the other way, they resume their rush to the locker. Ahead of them they spot the gothic kid, strutting carelessly down the hallway, giving the nod to friends and girls alike, his head constantly bobbing in rhythm to a Mudvayne CD. As the gothic kid stops in front of his locker Seb and Travis lean against the wall facing him, trying to look uninterested. The same four clicks from earlier, and he opens his locker. His face turns from expressionless calm to sudden shock as out of the locker pours two bags full of little, disgusting, reeking maggots and fly larvae crawl and fall out of the locker over the kid. A look of disgust again graces his face as the stench overpowers him, and everyone bursts into a fit of gagging and laughing. Seb and Travis take the opportunity and let out the most hysterical giggling fit. From the volume and pitch of the laugh, the kid turns and looks Travis and Seth dead in their eyes. However the two keep laughing, as the gothic kid shakes the maggots and larvae off of his body and walks over to Travis and Seb…their laughter is soon silenced as he lifts his hand and points at them. “I know it was you…but I’m not going to retaliate…but you’ll get yours…you’ll get yours…just watch yourselves…you’ll get yours…” The sinister threat hisses through Seb and Travis’ ears as the gothic kid backs up and gets the books for his next class. A few people continue to watch the maggots as a couple of janitors make their way instinctively to the spill, and Seb and Travis look at each other, somewhat in shock… ---------------------------- 11th Grade – High School 3 years later… 2 years into High school, and things have changed little, a new school, still the same people, but a different atmosphere all around. The 12 graders are stalking around, scoping the newly turned 11th graders. In the crowd are a heavily changed Garry and James, the gothic kid, and various other school kids that went through life with Seb and Travis. The brother’s themselves head through the front doors and walk along the halls confidently, now assertive in their 3rd year at high school. “Seb…you bring the stuff?” “Yeah Travis just be cool…” The two teens walk along the halls, but nobody really seems to mind them any more. Along the way they pass the girls that come with a high school. “Oh my she is cellulicious!” “Dude, get a grip she’s like 400lbs…” Seb and Travis sneer at the overweight girl, who turns and literally blows smoke through her nose at the two pranksters. Again they continue along the hallway, checking out everyone as they go. “Seb in all the years be been doin’ this…we’ve never been caught you realise?” says Trevor through the side of his mouth, whilst Seb checks out more girls. “Uhhh…yeah who cares what’s the number of this locker again?” “It’s No.66, but I’m gonna enjoy this.” “Why’s that Trav’?” “You heard the guy on the bus this morning, he sounds like a total homo, man. I hate those guys!” “But why Travis if he likes droppin’ anchor in Poo Bay just leave him be! The guy ain’t done anything to you!” exclaims Seb, still trying to keep his voice hidden. “Shut up and let’s go, it’s our first day in the 11th Grade and I wanna make it a memorable one!” Seb and Travis keep quiet as they make their way along the hallway. All the students and kids just sit talking as the boy’s make their way past them, hands in pockets and shirts billowing in the hallway atmosphere. They reach the halls containing lockers and begin look on either side for number 66. They pass the 1-10’s…10-20…20-30’s…30-40…40-50’s…50’s-60’s… “Hey Seb what if someone spots us doing this?” “They can’t…no one’s hanging around here, hell it is the morning, who in High school wants to get into schoolwork first thing in the damn morning………” Trevor is cut off…as both he and Seb walk unknowingly past No.66, and past a group of “morning school workers”. “No buddy you listen HERE! Kirk was a way better captain than Janeway and Spock was more popular than Seven of Nine ANYDAY!!!” “Ok if you love Kirk so much what does his middle initial stand for?” “Tiberius you dolt! Jeepers you’d think I was a complete nerd or something…*Snort*…ha!” Seb and Travis scrunch their faces up in distasteful fashion as the nerds go about studying Quantum Neutrino Physics and the physics behind the Starship Enterprise. Seb and Travis stop and notice they’re in an awkward area of the hall. “Travis where’s No.66…the only ones I see are 130-140…oh wait…here it is.” Seb walks over to a locker, spray painted black with “66” carved into it, and a similar looking digit beside it. Again Travis performs his padlock-defying skill and backs off as the locker opens to reveal many pictures of gothic and satanic bands like Deicide and Slipknot…many pictures of horny devil-sluts and one textbook…on human biology, of course vandalised. Travis reaches into his pockets and pulls out five green golf ball-sized balls. He places them on the top shelf of the locker and looks to Seb. “Hey gimme that rubber band and the thumbtacks.” Seb reaches into his pockets and removes a long rubber band, a few thumbtacks and a piece of string. Travis takes them and makes the trap. He sits all five green balls on the rubber band, and extends it across the inside of the locker, tacking the rubber band into the metal with difficulty. Travis then continues to tie the piece of string to the middle of the rubber band, and pulls it up behind the top shelf and over to the locker door, where he tacks the end piece to the locker door. Travis closes the door carefully and locks it again, just as the bell rings. “Ok Seb let’s get out of here!” Travis shouts excitedly. “Ok but let’s watch this first! I wanna see if the little homosexual gets stink bombed!” Seb replies with a devious smile on his face. Travis runs across the hallway and leans against the wall with Seb, and they begin generically talking to each other. The horde of kids rampage through the hallway to their lockers, and Travis takes a good look at the locker, and realises something to his horror. “Oh sh*t!!! That’s not locker 66, it’s Locker No.666!” Travis’ jaw drops in horror as he realises his mistake. “But what’s No.666 doing in the 130-140 row?” replies Seb, hints of nervous jitters in his voice. “No, no…look at the locker, it got painted over and someone scored out 13- and replaced it with 66! It’s not No.66 it’s 136 changed to 666!!! Oh sh*t man what have I done???” “Um…Travis…don’t move or laugh or nothin’…I think the guy that owns that locker is heading our way…” Seb points down the hallway where a sore sight for eyes is making his way towards them. The 6’3” 201lbs Goth strutting along the hallway, his long, loose black hair waving across his pale, depressed face. The large Goth stops in front of his locker and again…the usual four clicks…and the locker opens. All at once everything seems to slow down…Travis’ eyes widen and his heart jumps into his throat, Seb begins giggling profusely at Travis and pointing at the big Goth, whilst the 6’3” man himself opens up his locker and gasps as the string snaps, the rubber band flies forward and the green stink balls splash off of his face. Immediately he turns around, looking seriously pissed off and smelling like a rotten kipper left out in the sun. He looks across the hallway and is about to turn back to his locker and attempt to punch a hole through it when a smaller teen almost identical to him taps him on the shoulder…and everything returns to correct motion. “Hey Kyle, those two did it!” The littler Goth, standing at about 6’0” points over at Seb and Travis, and smiles. “Hey guys, I told ya you’d get yours…didn’t I?” he says giggling and laughing, as Kyle makes his way over to them. “Oh sh*t! That little b*st*rd actually did it!” “Travis there’s no time to talk smack, there’s a big Goth about to kill us!!!” replies Seb, and the two brothers turn through the hallways. Kyle and his smaller doppelganger chase after Seb and Travis. The four stumble through a pair of old grey double doors, and without thinking Seb and Travis run down into the dank and dark of the school basement. Past boilers and big, roaring machines Seb and Travis run, twisting through the winding paths of metal, until finally…………… “Gotcha!!!” Seb and Travis stop and snivel as Kyle jumps out in front of them and grabs them both by the throats. He pushes them against a boiler and shakes violently. “You two little rejects have pissed off the wrong guys…and now I’m going to end you…” Kyle tightens his grip on their throats and slowly…slowly they turn red, then blue in the face. Eventually the smaller Goth reaches them and without stopping or speaking lifts a foot up and cracks Travis in the Nads. As both Goths begin to beat the living hell out of Seb and Travis, up in the rafters someone is watching. Two people as a matter of fact. They watch as Kyle and his little friend proceed to bust wide open the little posers, pranksters and general pisser-offers. The two figures breath heavily and look over to each other. “This story sounds familiar…perhaps our two little victims would have thought further before disrupting the paths of their enemies…for it seems apparent that in the end…the weak are destroyed and the cocky follow…” “Ah yes…a violent end to a futile pair…to see such delicious punishment as this one would normally be forced to inflict it on one’s enemies…” “Maybe they will think twice before crossing people bigger…badder…and better…than them…” “May all Hell break loose on their unforgiven souls…” “Eternal Damnation break forth…let the Bloodlust begin……………” Seb and Travis continue to scream in pain as their blood spills on the floor…and from the rafters, Heresian Bloodlust watch on… Fin…
