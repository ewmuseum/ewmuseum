A new meeting.

14 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sunday, April 14, 2002 “I saw you beat the man yesterday!” Mikey’s deep, yet simple voice said. Reno Davis spoke into his motel room phone to his good friend, Mikey ‘Freight’ Thomas, who remained in Moncton, New Brunswick, Canada after Reno departed for OSW. “Yeah, he was a cinch compared to you there buddy.” Reno said with a smile to Mikey on the end of the line. The big man’s childish laugh flooded Reno’s ear so loudly that he had to pull the phone away. “How is ZaC?” “He’s alright, The Red Army still has something up their arse about him having fun. Ah well though, I’ll shut them all up pretty soon.” “You wrestle the other man?” Mikey asked. “Yeah, this Saturday commin’ up. I gotta beat Tom Walczuk.” “Wal...Walcek...” Freight struggled to repeat to Reno. “The bald one.” Reno simplified. “Ahh...” “Yeah, but I gotta go now before the charge for the call goes berserk.” “You shoulda used 1-800-COLLECT!” Mikey’s laughter boomed once more. Reno smiled; he missed the big guy. “Alright, talk to you again soon alright?” “Bye!” Mikey said, before hanging up. Reno hung up as well and looked over at ZaC, who chewed on the hotel standard Holy Bible. “Eww ZaC,” Reno said to the monkey, pulling the Bible away from him, “Figured you’d have better taste than that.” ZaC wasn’t happy with Reno because of this. The little monkey soon got over it when he found he could chew on the curtains. Reno sighed as he watched his little buddy rip his hotel room apart and sat down heavily on the bed. Michel Krieg had given him a pretty bad beating. “Hey! Get away from my car!” A female voice yelled from outside. ZaC was startled and fell off of the curtains with a heavy thump as he hit the floor. “Sinner! Sinner! You’ll pay!” A different female voice screamed, followed by the slamming of a car door. “What the hell?” Reno mumbled as he got up from his seat and looked out his window at the events happening. A group of protestors surrounded a red Jetta screaming and hollering about something. Inside the car, Reno recognized the woman as a fellow OSW wrestler...Amy something or other. They called her Aimz. “You will not corrupt our youths anymore! We demand that you...” One of the protestors started, but was interrupted when... Whump! A Holy Bible bounced off the roof of the Jetta and slid into one of the protestors, knocking her flat on her ass. The entire pack of people’s attention was diverted towards the source, a short man with bright red hair and startling eyes. His t-shirt sported the words: “Jesus died for his own sins, not mine.” Which was a popular saying by the band: ‘Crass’. The women gasped at the sight of the man, who wasn’t impressed. “Shut up! It’s like ten o’clock at night and you think you can be dicking around as loud as you want? People gotta sleep!” Reno roared. More silence ensued until the chief protestor, named Cindy Muffdiver, spoke up. “You! Your t-shirt is atrocious!” She yelled, when another backed her up, saying that the man was Reno Davis. “Reno Davis!?” She exclaimed and looked at the man with disgust, “You are a virus to today’s society with your sacrilegious beliefs! We, The Christian Ladies International Tribunal, collectively known as CLIT, demand that you...” She started, but was again interrupted when she crossed Reno’s line. “Demand? You demand? Who the hell do you think you are? Does this look like Germany in 1933 you dumbass? ZaC! Get out here!” Reno called, and ZaC soon appeared, “Remember that trick I told you to never do again?” He asked and the monkey hung his head in shame. “Well buddy, do it to these ladies.” ZaC looked at the collective of protestors and then preceded to crap in his hand and throw his feces at the CLIT members, who ran off, appalled. The two laughed their asses off and calmed down when Aimz got out of her Jetta. “Fucking churchie grannies!” She yelled with a smile. Reno chuckled, “Yeah, what's that all about anyway?” “...They don't like anybody different. I mean, I'm into that whole faith thing and all...but loud music, piercings, sex and alcohol...I just seem to disgust those skankbags with what I'm into.” She cocked her shoulders and shrugged. “Yeah...right.” Reno looked around, clearly out of comments. Always a smooth talker with the ladies that Reno is. “Meh...they can think what they want, but they're not about to stop me. Free speech and all that jazz, you know how it is...” She broke the silence, “But yeah...we're both Canadians, huh?” “I don’t know...Are you Canadian?” “Born and raised in New Minas, Nova Scotia!” “Wow, Nova Scotia? I grew up right next to you. I’m from Moncton, New Brunswick. That’s tight stuff...Amy right?” “Yep...and I've been wondering, is the underground of Moncton as fucking kick-ass as mine was?” “What? You mean music underground?” Reno was clearly puzzled. “Hell yes...we had indy punk concerts once a month at the Rec. Center.” “Oh...Yeah. It was a pretty kick ass scene. Bad Luck #13 and the whole circle...Good stuff it was. What type of shit you into?” Reno asked. “Anything and everything. I'm mainly punk, metal...that kinda stuff but on a regular day, you'd catch me listening to anything from opera to reggae...I'm just weird with that. But yeah...mainly hard shit...NOFX, Boy Hits Car...but Metallica, to me, rules all music.” “NOFX...punk...gotcha...Great,” Reno’s face was indifferent. But his thoughts were not...‘God she’s friggin hot!’, He thought to himself. “Right on. So...umm...yeah.......what's with the eyes, if you don't mind me asking? Contacts?” She asked innocently, yet struck a strong nerve. Reno realized he didn’t have his shades on and immediately looked down towards the ground, his face reddening. He began to sweat before answering, “Uh...they’ve been like this since birth.” He awaited her disgusted answer. Then realized she hadn’t said anything about them as of yet, and didn’t seem freaked out or repulsed. “Really? That's awesome! Is it like a disease or something...or just like a natural kinda kick-ass gift?” At this, Reno eased up a little. Even in 2002, a lot of people were still close-minded. “The doctors never knew. It didn’t affect my vision so they never really bothered with it. I got kidded a lot at school though.” “When someone roughed you up about 'em...you kicked their asses bloody, right?” “Hell yeah!” He lied. Not that he hadn’t tried to beat up his harassers though. “Good stuff...good stuff.” Aimz said, an awkward silence ensuing, “Well...I'm gonna head back, people waiting at the hotel room and all...so I'll see ya around, probably backstage? We should definitely hang out sometime...my friends and I usually go out to party after Slams when I can walk, I'll snag you if we do!” “Uh, yeah. See ya!” Reno replied, confused as to why she was outside in her car to begin with and why’s she’s heading back to her room now. He shrugged it off and watched her walk away, her pants featured her ass very nicely after all. “She’s an emo chick, that’s for sure. But she seems to be pretty cool...What do you think ZaC?” ZaC stopped chewing on the car tire, and looked at Reno for a moment, then continued to chew on the tire. “Oh well...We’ll just see what happens.”
