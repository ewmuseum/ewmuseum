Date: 1 FEB 02 'And welcome back to Easton Exclusive, YOUR only guide to the wrestling world on Public Access... I'm your host Danny Boiko, and in the studioj we have one of the new OSW stars, Sebastian Toomes!' We open to a public access television set, where Sebastian and his valet, Jennifer (who sits in her chair, studiously ignoring her surroundings), sit. Sebastian is garbed in an OSW t-shirt and black jeans, while Jennifer is decked out in a tight blue top and blue biker shorts. Very nice. The host, one Danny Boiko, shuffles his index cards on his card table of a desk, and absently sips some of the complimentary Capri Sun the producers of the last show left in a styrofoam cooler. You gotta take em where you can get them... He looks at the bored 'couple', and smiles sunnily. 'So, Sebastian, you have been causing some controversey since you've been signed to OSW. In fact, some are calling you a racist. After your last promo, though, it's hard to dispute what they're saying... Any comments?' Toomes sighs. 'Oh yes, everyone wants to point a finger and call me a racist, but actually, all I'm doing is pointing out facts. I did that promo to draw attention to the slew of mindless 'Urban Comedies' there are out in T.V. Land, and how they're resorting to stereotypes. Not that 'White' TV is much better... All I'm saying is that maybe if these people who watched these programs shut off the television and read a goddamned book once in awhile, they wouldn't be rotting in the ghetto. Next question.' Danny looks at the camera, and clears his throat. 'Um, okay! Your anti-drinking and anti-drug stance is quite evident, but noone seems to notice that. Would you like to elaborate for us, your philosophy towards drinking and drugs?' Toomes crosses his legs, and swats the nailfaile out of Jennifer's hands. Ignoring her dirty look, he answers. 'I can relate to some Straight-Edge philosophies, but I'm not really Edge myself. But yes, I feel that people who drink and people who use drugs are irresponsible assholes. Everytime a person drinks, he puts himself and people around him in danger because he is no longer in control of his actions. That's why this party mentality, and the blind-eye our culture turns on alcohol and drugs is so wrong. You even see it in wrestling. Steve Austin drinks on live television - so you're sending the message that it's okay to get drunk and act like a fucking stupid hick redneck. It's mind-boggling... Simply put, if you drink or do drugs, you're a fucking dickhead and you deserve to be curbed. Next question.' 'Okay... What are your thoughts on SLR, who has taken offense to your latest promo? What do you have to say to him?' Danny sips his Capri Sun, and shuffles his index cards while Toomes answers. 'SLR can suck my fucking cock. That fucking piece of trash... I'd call him washed-up, but it looks like he hasn't taken a bath in years. This guy wants to jump on the racist bandwagon, wants to call me a fucking racist... That's real easy to label someone, it keeps you from forming your own opinions by putting a pre-manufactured label on someone. Get a fucking grip, Sleaze-ball. If you wanna blast me for having some fun and making some facts about Black Television public, then quite simply, I'm going to blast you in the ring. I'm going to shove that disgusting cancer stick up into your fucking sinus, then curb you. Next Question.' Jennifer calmly pulls another file out of her purse. Toomes starts to swat it out of her hands, but she stabs him in the forearm. 'GODDAMNIT! WHAT THE FUCK YOU DID YOU DO THAT FOR?' Toomes exclaims, drawing back his wounded arm. An ugly bruise is starting to form, surrounded by a nnimbus of scratched off skin. 'Fuck with my nailfile, you get fucked with, you loser. Next question.' Jennifer snarls, and goes back to filing her nails. 'Fucking bitch... Next question!' Toomes says angrily. 'Well, this is a good of a time as any... What is the relationship between you and Jennifer here? Are you boy-friend and girl-friend like Kid Wonder and Amanda are? Are you... intimately involved, like Jeremy Howard and his valet, Jessica are?' Toomes starts to speak, but Jennifer cuts him off. 'Shut it, half-inch! It's my turn to talk! Lemme give the viewers a little reality check - valets are just that - they're not the wrestler's girlfriend! Got it? It's not real, we don't have sex with the wrestlers. Anyone who tells you different is a liar. Jessica is Howard's girlfriend? Not from the way she acted at the PTC Christmas Party. She was sandwiched between Miyagawa and Hank! Then she had the nerve to go up to Jeremy and kiss him with the same mouth that swallowed a load of Hank-cream! Yes, that's right. And Eleanor! That little prep bitch Xavier Kannon keeps around, she's a little coke-sniffing whore as well! So to quell any fucking rumors, NO, I am not fucking Sebastian here. Got it? Next question!' Danny smirks, and reshuffles his cards. 'Um, that's about all the time we have for right now... Join us in a few as a local band named 'Intention Miscarriage' plays us their local radio hit, 'I Flushed My Baby Down The Toilet'. Be right back!' And we fade out... (OOC- Sorry, pressed for time)
