Suppress The Natural Enemy.

4 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 4th, 2002 ((Note: The end seems seriously, but it's kind of, sort of supposed to be semi-amusing to people like me...so just grin and bear it, pweeeez.)) ''God damnit!'' A barrage of curses was emanating quite audibly from within Amy Campbell's Elite locker room, which gave everybody, including Reno Davis, instant confirmation of her whereabouts immediately following Slam!. While everybody was running rampant about the latest PCW 'names' jumping ship in an attempt to replenish the rotting corpses of their so-called careers, Aimz was off in her own little pain-stricken world... ''Christ!'' More curses, and then the source was revealed. She was seated uncomfortably on a wooden bench outlining the room with her bloodied right pant leg rolled up and a pair of blunt, silvery tweezers prodding into her thigh. Reno had let himself in, getting little more acknowledgement than a glance from his girlfriend before she looked back down to her work. He just cocked his head, inquiring. ''Still can't get the lead out?'' ''No...'' Amy had grunted, biting her bottom lip hard when she poked the tweezers in a little further. ''God, this is gross.'' Davis pulled a folding chair up in front of her and planted himself down upon it, cringing a little himself at the sight of the blood oozing out while Aimz was poking at the flesh of her leg. ''Need help?'' Campbell's eyes brightened when she looked up to Reno, trying to pick the blood that had been encrusted under her nails out as she said, ''Would you? I don't have the stomach for poking around inside of myself.'' ''Yeah, leave it to the master.'' Reno'd started chuckling, but frowned when he saw the unamused 'You could do better' expression on Aimz' face. ''What? I didn't HAVE to try...you made it too easy.'' ''Yeah yeah yeah,'' She grinned. ''Just get the lead outta my leg.'' Back in went the tweezers... ...out came the lead. ''Messy...'' Davis had a sour look on his face, looking down at Amy's blood on his fingers and then smiling when he looked up at her thankful, relieved face. ''But easy enough.'' ''Fucking PAINFUL! That little cock sucker's gonna pay for that. I mean, what the hell IS it with people and stabbing me with anything sharp?'' Reno winked. ''You're just irresistibly pokeable.'' ''I'm serious.'' She pouted. ''I've got no clue...but hey, how about we leave? Those journalist bitches are swarming the place tonight.'' Amy just lowered her head, searching for an excuse not to leave. She'd seen Corg far too much lately, and she wasn't even sure it was really him...paranoia and what seemed like waking nightmares were driving her to the very edge, she didn't feel like chancing walking outside and seeing the real deal. ''I...uhhh...didn't bring a change of clothes. Let's wait for them to go, don't need a bunch of people seeing me all dirty and sweaty...you know...'' ''So? I think I've got a pair of old jeans in my bag...'' He laughed. ''ZaC's sleeping on them.'' ''No...umm...don't wake him.'' ''Why not? He sleeps practically all the time anyway.'' Reno had been confused momentarily, but very little escaped him and, as expected, the truth soon came to light. ''You're STILL afraid of Corg?'' ''What?! You're crazy.'' ''Amy...come on, we're right by the exit.'' Davis pleaded, gently picking up a vented duffel bag that contained not only his gear, but apparently a slumbering monkey as well and hoisting it over his shoulder. ''Bu---'' Too late, Reno had already lifted her up into his arms, getting some odd looks from OSW personalities as he stepped into the hallway, eventually dumping her unceremoniously onto the ground with a rather haughty laugh when they reached the parking lot. ''Owwie.'' Amy whimpered before getting up and walking to the car beside Davis, making sure to stare at her shoes to avoid possibly seeing Corg. As long as he wasn't tying her laces, she was fine. When both were in, Aimz gazed out of her window, only to be 'treated' by the sight of the Think Tank, each and every one of the scummy team dragging what small pieces of their sorry forms that they managed to scrape from the mats at ringside after the show towards various vehicles, one being a rather 'classy' white limousine. For that breif moment, all other thoughts flew from her head, paranoia passed and all she thought was voiced when she muttered a curse upon the only part of society that had been her enemy since day one, since she'd stolen lunch money from what could be easily mistaken (If it hadn't been for the obvious) for children of PHD all the way back in elementary school... ''Death to nerds.''
