The Way Home, part 4

22 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/22/02 It took just about every ounce of courage I had to go down to that ring on Slam. Most people wouldn’t think that after I’d gone and faced so many dangerous men in my career. But this was different. I think most people assumed I was being my usual blissfully ignorant self that night, that somehow I didn’t guess how bad it was. But they’re wrong. I knew exactly how bad it was and that’s the scary part. I swear that roar rattled my bones. I had to make myself keep going. And why? For Corg? He didn’t deserve me taking this risk. No, he wasn’t worth it. Strange as it may sound... I did it for Ivan. Just listen to him. 'You will NEVER hurt her again!!' No, this is much less about what Corg did to me than what Ivan did. Pulling on his arm was quite useless. He didn't budge in the slightest. But I hoped that just the reminder that I was there and wouldn’t go away would convince him. Maybe I was hoping that if he saw me right there, unhurt, undamaged by Corg he’d let go. Maybe I’m still hoping that he’ll accept that I’m not so terribly hurt that I never want to see him again. Tempest takes a deep breath and steps closer. She gasps, realizing how terribly injured Corg truly is. With a slightly shaking hand she reaches out and grabs Ivan’s arm and screams to him, 'Ivan! No! Stoppit!' It's horrible. Not just to see what Ivan's capable of, but to know where it's all really coming from. He says he's a monster and he may be right. But that doesn't change things. The Werewolf turned back into a lovable guy when the moon was gone and Dracula got all the chicks. Besides, Ivan's a monster when he allows himself to be. When he's in pain. And that makes him a monster I had a hand in creating this time around. Ivan turns and growls, poised to backhand the interloper. He freezes suddenly, the face of Tempest, the face he saw when he blinked, now right in front of him. Tempest leaps back nimbly, almost as if ready for such a response, and looks up at Stanislav to make eye contact. Her eyes are widen, one step from panic, but she stands her ground. If you don't think that was terrifying, think again. The swing I figured on. Not that I blamed him. I know what it's like to see red and hit anything that moves. I may not be as big, but I've done my fair share of damage that way. Still, the look in his eyes hurt me to see. I couldn't tell if he was more horrified that he almost hit me, or that I'd seen him this out of control. Stanislav keeps his one hand wrapped around Corg’s neck as Tempest tries to calm him, he continues to stare into her eyes, unable to pull himself away. 'Ivan, c'mon. This isn't helping anything. You've beat him. You've made him pay. It's done.' It's over. Let it go. With Corg, with me, with whoever. Please stop fighting the imaginary enemies that are really just staring back at you from the mirror each day. He growls again and shakes his head absently. Tempests face hardens slightly and she speaks louder, with more strength in her voice, 'C'mon Ivan let him go. You can't kill him. Don't let him get to you like that. It'll only ruin you, not him!' Would you kill him? Would you really destroy someone so completely? And is that not exactly what I feared when I ran away from you? Ivan looks back at Corg, tearing his gaze from Tempest once more. 'He….he…' growls Stanislav…more to himself than to anyone else. He slowly releases Corg, until only his hand holds Corg up, pushed against his chest but no longer choking him. Ivan breathes heavily, a sort of humanity plays over his face and suddenly, we realize that whatever grip he had lost in his mind he has regained. With an almost horrified expression he looks to Tempest. Tempest smiles, ever so slightly. 'There ya go. Just leave him. C'mon...' Ivan blinks and slowly releases Corg. The sounds of the crowds sigh of relief hits his ears, his breathing becomes apparent, and the entire situation around him switches from a surreal bloodlust to a somewhat lethargic calm. He notices the pull on his arm from Tempest and takes a heavy step from Corg, the mat shaking under his mighty weight. Ivan looks at Tempest and blinks, taking in the entire situation, a blood covered mat, Corg, and Stanislav and amidst it all, her… You're safe. I'm safe. Corg's... as safe as it gets for him. Let it go. Don't get that look, that look that I know means you're ashamed of yourself. Ivan looks at her and shakes his head suddenly, his face switching from confusion to horror. Tempest shakes her head slightly, confusion covering her face as she notices Stanislav staring at the blood on his hands. Ivan turns very suddenly, turning his broad back to Tempest and exits the ring. The mat shakes with the subtraction of his weight, leaving Tempest in the ring with a bloody Corg and Speedy Riggs. When will you stop believing that everything hurts me, that I'm so easily damaged? Running was a mistake. Just like you let me see you as a monster, I showed you fear once and now it's all you remember. It is quiet in the ring. The cheers of the fans to not enter the squared circle as the smell of blood hangs in the air. Tempest looks mystified and more than a little upset. She looks down at Corg as the EMTs arrive. 'You better be grateful you're alive. Everyone wanted him to finish you.' she snaps at him and rolls from the ring. And they did. But you're not worth the time it would take to scrape up your remains. In a violent world, how did I manage to imagine real horror, when you were committing it? How did Ivan manage to see himself as a monster when you were there to provide a true model. Maybe it's because you're so damn weak. But I remember the first of these bold moves for me. The first was the hardest, but now they're becoming a regular thing. And you have the most unlikely person to thank for the one that brought me back. *** ~Rewind, July 23, 2002~ Tempest sat quietly as a tape played on her VCR. It was of a familiar scene to OSW fans, a tape of Ivan making his confession to El Drago about her. Her eyes remained wide as it played, the occasional tear rolling down her cheek. ~Ivan looked down and stared at the feather again, 'She meant a lot to me, El Drago. How could one person, one individual, mean so much to a Communist? How could one solitary woman mean so much to Ivan Sergeiovich Stanislav?' He sighed and leaned back against the wall, staring at an unknown portion of the wall ahead of him, 'When I first met her, I thought she was nothing but child. I went on that vacation with her because it would make Russia look good. I did not care for her. I did not care about stupid contest!'~ I almost laughed when I heard that. I wasn't sure I liked him either. And I was a child... in just about every way imaginable. Maybe that's what really went wrong. ~Stanislav paused and shook his head, 'And yet…I felt…..alive.' Ivan looked at Drago again to see if he was paying any attention to him. Speaking to someone for an extended period of time in a foreign language concerned him. But El Drago was listening, understanding was another thing but for some reason the man that just a few weeks ago was a sworn enemy listened to every word. Ivan sighed and breathed heavily through his nose, 'You know, El Drago, she was first person to like me….for me. Even Alexei first got to know Ivan Stanislav because of his reputation, but Tempest….nyet…she ignored all the negatives about me and enjoyed my company.' He stood up straight again, 'And she defended me when no one else would. She stood by my side whenever I needed her…and did I?' Ivan choked back a few words, 'Did I?'~ No, you didn't. But only the once. Why did it have to be such a big once? Because now when I sit back and really think about it, I see how small the one occasion really was. You lied about the thing with Meanstreak, yes. But what about when you came to find me later? That wasn't a lie. Singing me to sleep wasn't a lie. The day in Moscow, and when you gave me that book of your father's in the Alexandrovsky Gardens was not a lie. ~The Russian Bear paused and walked to a window and stared out of it, 'El Drago, I never meant to hurt her. They say that you can see the past perfectly, the present only halfway, and the future is blind. As I look at the past, I see my error…..I see my mistake and if I could change it, I would.' He paused and looked at the feather, his back to Drago, 'I would do anything to change it.'~ I believe you. ~A low growl exited Ivan’s huge frame, 'I am forty years old, El Drago. Forty years of service to Russian Military, and I am suspended. I have no country, I have no Comrades, I have no military. My life, for all intents and purposes, has been wasted. And there is truly one person that could have still not cared and been beside me, and I was stupid…and I hurt her!' Ivan’s volume quickly grew as he knocked the windowsill to the floor with his huge fist. He breathed heavily and rubbed the back of his neck. The huge, muscular back of The Russian Bear hulked up and down. He spoke softer, almost in a whisper, still gazing outside at the sky, 'Whenever she left…El Drago…and I returned to my base….I found that feather. I…I found it in my pocket. And do you know what I did when I got to my base and locked myself in the room?' Ivan paused for a long time as Drago watched on. 'I cried, Drago.' He shook his head slowly, 'I cried in my office as I stared at that feather. ME! The Russian Bear!! When I was shot in Afghanistan, I did not cry! When Soviet Union collapsed, I did not cry!' 'But when she left….I did.'~ I almost didn't believe you. I thought I was the only one drowning in my own tears after Head on Collision. My friends didn't understand it. They kept trying to fix me up, but I wouldn't let them. I told them I wasn't broken. I was lying. ~Ivan turned slowly and gazed at El Drago, pain coursing through his face, 'My mother told me once that she ‘loved’ me. I never understood what that meant. What is love? It cannot be described! It cannot be seen! And for my entire life, for forty years, I ignored love and yet, questioned what it was.' He lowered his eyes to the floor, almost shamefully. 'I know what love is now, El Drago. Love is something that could bring grown man to cry. Love is something that could open the eyes of a man that had forever been blind.' He paused and took a large breath. 'I loved her, El Drago.' He gritted his teeth, 'I loved her for her personality. I loved her for her positive outlook! I loved her for her understanding and her compassion and her joyfulness and her determination and…..' He swallowed and raised his gaze, 'I still love her, El Drago. While she may hate me, I love her with all of my heart and will forever love her.'~ 'I don't hate him,' Tempest whispered and choked back a sob as she hit the pause button. 'Then you know what you must do, right?' said a voice from the shadows. Tempest nodded, wiping her eyes with the back of her hand. 'I know... I... you're right, I have to.' 'I shall see you Saturday then.' Tempest nodded shallowly and said, 'This isn't how I planned to make my next career decision, but... You're right, I have to do this.' A hand covered in a black glove came forward and clasped her shoulder briefly before the small noises of someone letting themselves out. Tempest turned the VCR off, her eyes welling up with tears. This was going to take courage, more than she had ever summoned up before. Related RPs: The Heart of the Russian Bear Belated Birthday
