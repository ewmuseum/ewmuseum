'Submit yourselves then to God. Resist the Devil and he will flee from you.' - James 4:7 and The Dilemma

14 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14th April 2002 We are all stuck with choices in life, what should we do? The dilemma is which road do we go down and how when doing? Well the power himself is in need of guidance in his dilemma. 'The Dilemma' by [name withheld] Tick Tock goes the Satanism clock. Tick Tock, Tick Tock, But time is near, And Time is far, Trying to connect with who we are, When we come down to the end, Through all lives twists and bends, We reach the climax, the final bell, Will it be heaven or will it be hell. Down to the pits or in the tunnel, Through life your choices must be made, We all have a price that must be paid, But through lives twists and turns and many days, In your heart and soul God is with you always, -A-men. 'Submit yourselves then to God. Resist the Devil and he will flee from you.' - James 4:7 We fade into a small section of a dull feelingless bluish grey brick wall. At first there is no sound then slowly it is incorporated to the backdrop, it's a faint muffling of lots of people chit-chatting random words. With a clank of a footstep the camera moves back. We see a bigger spread of this wall, pretty much just more of the wall from before. In the corner though we see what seems to be the start of a glass screen, slowly the camera follows this to see on the other side of this class screen is a wall identical to the one the camera started in. So if you're keeping track, the room has brick walls with a glass screen in the middle. The camera then side steps to see we are in fact in the visiting room of a prison the scene sees a row of normal civilians on one side, then on the other the prisoners wearing orange body suits that the tops are like T-shirts. Every prisoner is talking to a loved one except one man, the criminal nearest the camera. This man is wearing the same attire as the rest, but in a lighter orange and on the left side of the chest he as a number in black text, this man is African American and a built like a brick shit house. He has a baldhead and squinted eyes. He is sitting back in a fold up steel chair starting impatiently at the steel chair opposite him. He leans forward. 'Mr. Sinclair.' He calls behind him, to the prison officer who is behind him. The Police officer before this was as still as a rock, standing at attention like that of what a soldier would do. His fists are clenched and pressed tightly at side of his impeccably ironed navy blue pants that gently skim his shiny leather shoes clenched his fists. The prison's guard chest is stuck out tensely through his light blue shirt. His golden nametag with 'Mr. Sinclair' engraved neatly into is opposite to the prison badge. 'Yes Gerardson.' Mr Sinclair says as he slowly loosens the tenseness in his arms and brings his left arm to pull his navy blue police cap up slightly so that his chiselled lily white face. His face looks as if it has been drained of all joy and emotion his eyebrows are lowered. He looks at Gerardson and grunts. 'What now?' he asks with little patients 'This dude Sagar and his pal, they aint here.' Gerardson complain. 'They'll be here, Gerardson. They're men of god.' Mr. Sinclair carelessly reassures the distressed prisoner. 'What they want with a brotha'?' Gerardson queries. 'I think they're going to try and show you the light so-to-speak.' Mr. Sinclair mocks. 'Oh but what if--' Gerardson's question is interrupted by the long squeak of doors rusty hinges. 'Oh you're here.' Gerardson shockingly murmurs. 'Yes my brother.' We hear the familiar tone of Eric Sagar's southern tone. 'We are here.' Sagar walks on screen and takes a seat on the steel chair. But Gerardson does not notice as his eyes are fixed on something off screen, we hear the deafening scrape of the legs of a steel chair that are being dragged along the shiny navy blue stone tiled floor. We see a massive hand put a fold up navy blue steel chair identical to the others in the room. Then we see the Power Himself Propaganda takes a seat on it wearing his usual attire. By now the whole of the room is so silent you could hear a pin drop. Everyone is looking at the seven-foot beast in amazement and wonder. 'Wh.. Wh… Who is this dawg?' Gerardson bewilderedly asks. 'My brother this is the Power Himself, Propaganda.' Sagar proudly exclaims. 'Propaganda eh?' Gerardson says with a bit of confidence. 'The dude is massive.' 'We are not here to talk about my size, which I got when being graced by god!' Propaganda roars unexpectedly. 'We are here to talk about the wonders of God!' 'Hush My Brother,' Sagar calms Propaganda who has just petrified Gerardson. 'Like us he is a creature of God. Therefor he is our friend.' 'Ugh… Yeah.' Gerardson worriedly spurts out. 'So my Brother what is your name?' Sagar asks with much welcoming in his tone. 'Gerardson, Paul Gerardson.' Gerardson answers regaining his calmness. 'How have you sinned?' Sagar asks. 'Well, it all started a few weeks ago. Me and my hommie's were doing some paper chasin' and we robbed this liquor store well they all got away except me and the pigs locked me up, plain and simple.' Gerardson explains. 'Do you ever feel alone? My brother? Do you ever look up to heavens and feel there is no-on there for you?' 'Why Yes I do.' Gerardson agrees with shock that Sagar knows how he feels. 'WELL YOU ARE WRONG MY BROTHER!' Sagar yells loudly as he jumps to his feet pushing the steel chair behind him away. 'YOU ARE SO VERY WRONG! BECAUSE THERE IS SOMEONE WATCHING OVER YOU, THERE IS SOMEONE WHO CARES ABOUT YOU, YOU JUST DON'T KNOW IT!' Sagar yells with his head swung back and his arms points up to the heavens. 'Yes…' Gerardson stands up, 'Yes there is…' he whimpers with relief. 'IT'S GOD HIMSELF MY BROTHER, GOD HIMSELF!' Sagar's still preaching. 'CAN YOU FEEL THE POWER?' He asks with a very loud tone. 'Yes…' Gerardson says with much confidence then he takes a deep breath and yells 'YES I CAN MY BROTHER!' the whole room applauds and starts to cheer. While Sagar and Gerardson look at each other smiling. A high bitched bell rings, then Mr. Sinclair takes a step forward. 'Visiting time is over say you farewells.' He says with little joy for the conversion that just took place. There is a sudden rush of conversations then both Gerardson and Sagar step closer to the screen. 'Thank you my brother Sagar,' Gerardson heartily thanks Sagar. 'Any day My Brother, any day in the name of god.' Sagar welcomes with a smile on his face. Gerardson walks off with all of the prisoners, the scene then focuses on Propaganda sitting on his steel chair watching Sagar. Sagar turns round quickly and looks at Propaganda noticing his expression is a little glum, it has been like it all day. 'My brother Propaganda, what is up? Are you not pleased we have converted another to the side of god?' Sagar asks with much worry. 'No' Propaganda sighs. 'Well what then my Brother?' Sagas continue to interrogate 'What is troubling you?' 'I'm at a dilemma, My Brother I am torn between two choices.' Propaganda complains. 'Oh? What are they?' Sagar concerns. 'Well my brother. As you know Saturday Slam the foolishness of the management have pitted myself to team up with Vampir Nosferatu. But I'm confused we have to go up against Heresian Bloodlust, the OSW Tag Champions with their titles on the line.' Propaganda explains. 'Well what are you stuck with?' The Preacher asks. 'Vampir Nosferatu is a follower of Satan and so is Heresian Bloodlust, so what is truly right should I co-operate with Vampir and become Tag Team champions or should I go against all three men and possibly leave without gold?' Propaganda queries, this has obviously been playing about in his mind he doesn't know if he should team up with a Satanist in an attempt to gain Tag Gold or try to go against them on his own and he will almost certainly fail. 'Well my brother, in the scripture of the bible James 4:7 it says 'Submit yourselves then to God. Resist the Devil and he will flee from you.' I believe you can show Vampir the light even though he is Satanist scum. But a few weeks back you carried The Magi and Epitome of Evil into a victory against Nick Casey, Great Dragon and Vampir himself so I believe you don't need help from Vampir. But it would be a vital asset if you made him as righteous as me.' Sagar boasts. Miraculously a smile sweeps the face of Propaganda and he calmly mumbles 'You're right my brother. Saturday Slam with help or not I shall take down any Satanist scum that dares to oppose me will meet their demise!' His voice turns into an evil chuckle. 'A-men' Sagar states. 'A-men, My Brother' Propaganda responds. The scene fades to black.
