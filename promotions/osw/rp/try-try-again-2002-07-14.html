Try, Try Again

14 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 14th, 2002 Captain John Lane stood on the deck of the intrepid starship, “Endeavor,” staring out to the view port which showed nothing but stars twinkling in the distant. He could only nod his head as he admired the view and the endless space that lay before him. He stepped away from his captain’s chair, slowly making his way toward the navigation officer who was busily adjusting the ship’s course. Captain Lane’s first officer, a blue skinned alien named Commander Bre’Lik, watched in curiosity. “Is there a problem, Captain?” Commander Bre’Lik asked with a tone of astonishment. Lane smiled. “No, commander. I was just thinking about how beautiful the galaxy is, that’s all.” Bre’Lik merely shook his head in confusion, causing the three bulbs of skin on the top of his head to flop up and down. “I am confused, Captain. I have noticed that many of the humans on board this ship do much of this reminiscing. Staring out into space seems to be the most popular form, however, I fail to see the merits of such an act.” “It just helps put our minds at ease,” He replied. “Having a piece of mind has a lot of merits.” Bre’Lik thought about this for a moment, but was still confused. “Humans are most peculiar.” Lane nodded. “That we are, commander. But we are also a proud people. I’m proud that this crew has been able to hold out against the evil wills of our oppressive government and will fight to the bitter end to defend our freedom!” The entire crew burst into cheers and stood up clapping. Captain Lane pumped a fist into the air, queuing some heroic music. Then, out of nowhere, a red light began to buzz and a junior officer rushed over to attend to it. The fresh faced cadet shouted, “Captain, we have two Earth Gov star cruisers coming out of hyperspace! They are on an attack vector bearing 1.12, Double H, 46.2.” “Right on top of us!” Captain Lane gasped. He took a moment to gather himself and shouted, “Man your battle stations and arm QUANTUM TORPEDOES!” The ship rocked violently as laser fire could be heard outside. Captain Lane braced after every impact, a drop of sweat careened down his face as the pressure of the situation grew. Finally, the ship stopped moving as the lights went out and all you could hear is a siren drowning out in the background. A few moments later, and the emergency lights come on and the crew can see again. But as Captain Lane looks up, he finds himself staring up at the barrel of a Class Three Phaser gun, being held by none other than… …Tom Lexian? “Lexian!” Lane said with utter hatred. “It was you and your Earth Gov cronies that planned this attack!” I stood there, blanking out on my line and then remembered how hungry I was. I should have had that doughnut before coming in this morning. “That’s right, we’re here,” I said, remembering my line a few moments later. “You and your Endeavor crew are illegally operating outside of Earth Gov jurisdiction and I’m here to make sure that you don’t…don’t keep doing…you know, what you’re doing.” Lane looked at me like I was an idiot. What is this bozo doing? He fucks up his lines every two seconds. God, I wish I was working with William Shattner. He picked up his queue, making the best of the situation. “You know we’ll never give up. We’ll fight to the end because we’re right.” He took a deep breath and closed his eyes. “We’re heroes!” I blanked out again for a second. Lane rolled his eyes and repeated his lane. “I said…we’re heroes!” “Oh yeah,” I murmured under my breath. “Uhm, well, then die a hero, you rebel scum!” I pressed the fire button on the phaser and watched as it made weird laser sounds. As I did, Lane pretended to fall over and thrash around on the ground as if he was really hurt. “CUT!” The director yelled. Suddenly the set became very alive with noise as people busily left the set and set workers cleared off the now obviously fake space ship. I dropped my fake weapon of doom to the ground and walked back to my dressing room, fully dressed in my ridiculous twenty-third century outfit. I felt like an idiot. “Hey idiot!” I turned around to the snide remark, only to see the one and only Robert Duke walking past me, otherwise known as the esteemed Captain John Lane. “What do you want?” I remarked. He stopped and took a sip of his water bottle that he so coolly held in his hand. “Next time, could you try a little harder to be a fuck up?” “Look, what do you want from me?” I said, throwing my arms up in frustration. “You could try to learn your damn lines,” He replied. “Is it so hard to remember that I’m the good guy and you’re the bad guy?” “I forget in between all of the phaser fire and the fake laser noises going off every two seconds,” I rebutted sarcastically. “Besides, who cares about all of this anyways? No one watches this show but middle aged nerds who could care less if I messed up my lines. They just want to pretend that it really is the twenty-third century.” Duke stuck a finger in my face and wagged it like my grandmother used to when she was really upset. “Listen Lexian, I know this is just some publicity stunt for you, but for us it’s our life. Get your head out of your ass and do your job.” As he stormed away, I thought about dropkicking him and his precious bottle of water to the ground, but I thought better of it. It would probably just land me in more hot water with the rest of the set and the director, who made it painfully clear to me that I would be saying as few lines as possible. I made my way to my dressing room, avoiding as many eyes as I could. These were all people that made their living doing something that I hated, but I’m still a man of principles. I was here to do a job and I was raised to believe that you do everything one hundred percent. No less, and more if you could. But who the hell listens to what they should be doing anyways? The door to my dressing room swung open freely, and as I stared in, I saw Raymond Burris sitting there on my couch. I sighed as I slammed the door shut. “It’s rude to slam the door,” Burris noted. “It’s rude to come into someone’s dressing room unannounced,” I responded, sitting down at a chair nearby. “What do you want?” “I’m your agent. I come and go as I please.” “You’re not my agent,” I replied. “Let’s just make sure we’re clear on that. O’Brien is my manager.” “He’s your wrestling manager. I am your entertainment manager, and besides, don’t you have a damn penguin as your manager now?” I crossed my arms, not in the mood for games. “Leave him out of this.” He made a note in his notebook and looked up. He smiled, showing those god awful teeth of his. They weren’t yellow or rotted, mind you, but perfect. Perfect teeth are fairly annoying. “It’s fine. I think it’s cute and it certainly gives us bonus points with the female audience. They all think it’s real cute.” “Yippee,” I said sarcastically, making a thumbs up sign. Burris put down his notebook. “Look Lexian, I didn’t want to mention this, but you really need to start putting some effort out there. You go out there without knowing your lanes, no enthusiasm or energy, and every actor out there hates your guts.” I sat there for a moment. “And why are they angry at me again?” “God dammit, Lexian,” He shot back angrily standing up. “You’re making a mockery out of New Wave Entertainment, and worst of all, a mockery out of yourself.” “Sorry to disappoint you.” “You’re sorry?” He responded. “No, you’ll be the one whose sorry if you don’t start performing, Lexian. We’re putting a lot of money into you and we expect some production. Don’t say I never warned you.” I looked up at him, not believing for a second that he could do anything to hurt me. “Is that a threat?” “Did it sound like one?” “Yeah.” “Then take it for what it’s worth,” He replied, grabbing his notebook and heading outside. “You’re booked for another cameo appearance on Buffy the Vampire Slayer. Try not to fuck it up, after all, you are a vampire executioner, right?” “Was.” “Whatever. Just do a better job than you did today.” I shook my head, tired of all of these gigs. “I’m not a space faring captain and I’m not some demon monster waiting for Sarah Michelle to rip me a new ass.” Burris turned around and smiled. “These are fantastical roles that could never happen in real life. After seeing some of your old PCW promos, I’d say this is all right up your alley. Good day, Tom.” I walked over and slammed the door shut. Him and his god awful teeth.
