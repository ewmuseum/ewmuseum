Clay Vendetta: Private Eye in... THE MAL... hey, wait a minute!

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/26/02 ON TODAY's EPISODE OF CLAY VENDETTA: PRIVATE EYE... Clay has finally gotten his hands on the Maltese Cybie... but would it lead him to enlightenment or be just another dead end? We'll see on today's heart-attack-inducing episode of Clay Vendetta... PRIVATE EYE! [in black and white...] Let's see... 6 AA batteries... check. Batteries properly installed... check. Instruction manual read... in duplicate. Check. I think we're ready to finally uncover the mysteries of this thing... Sitting on the floor of his office, Clay Vendetta is about to flip the 'on' switch to The Maltese Cybie. The wrapping lies on the floor next to him, a pile of plastic deritus in bright colors. Setting his fedora on the ground, Vendetta runs his hand through his bleached-blond hair, takes a deep breath... Here we go... *flick* Whirring to life, the dog's eyes light up... 'Yip! Yip! Woof!'... and the Cybie does a back flip. Vendetta looks non-plussed. That's it? No guns? No laserbeams? No Pentium 7 ultra-thinking supercomputer? Does this thing even play chess? Testing his theory, Vendetta gets to his feet and roots through a drawer in his desk as the dog continues to yip. Shuffling... shuffling... and finally he digs out a small pocket chess set. Queen on her color, king by her side... two knights... hmmmmmmm... I think I'm short a pawn. Eh, if this dog's as smart as it seems to be, it'll be able to adjust its game to counter the deficiency... alright, let's dance, Lassie! With the board set up, Vendetta takes a set, placing the Cybie across the board from him. Vendetta opens with a queen's pawn forward two spaces. 'Yip! Yip!' And with that piece of witty banter, the dog does another backflip, knocking over his entire back row. 'Goddammit!' Vendetta shouts. 'I've got a brain-dead dog!' Picking the Cybie up... *CRASH!!!!* ... Vendetta hurls it against the wall, and the dog explodes in a shower of parts, plastic, batteries and LEDs. Kicking his desk, Vendetta slumps to the floor in frustration... Damn it all to hell. No luck, no answers, and one hundred and twenty-nine dollars down the shitter. Things are not looking up. How am I supposed to get to the bottom of this mystery when nothing... 'CUT! CUT! Clay, what the hell are you doing here?' ...huh? Fading slowly into color, the camera pulls back to reveal that the office is... a set? The cameramen surrounding the office look visibly confused at the voice of Niki Weathers, who's marching into the set with a stern look on her face. 'Clay, what's going on?' 'I'm... uh... I'm solving the mystery? What about the dog? All the attacks??' Niki sighs. 'Where the hell have you been, Clay? That's not even your angle! It's the CCW guys who've been behind all the jumpings, all the muggings, and you're up against them this Sunday night!' 'But... I kinda *liked* doing this stuff. You know, Sam Spade, private eye, all that...' 'That's terrific, Clay. But in the meantime, you've got to fight for your OSW contract on Sunday night, not to mention an International Title shot this Saturday on Slam, and you're screwing around in black and white! C'mon, we've got a plane to catch!' The pushy broad had a point. After all, 'Classic' Clay Vendetta's first job was kicking ass, not sleuthing. So, then, if it's asskicking they want... 'And NO more of those ridiculous voiceovers!' Shrugging his shoulders, Clay tosses his fedora on the rack. 'Jesus, calm down! It's CCW, for christ's sake. They might as well have sent the pimply-faced intern legions after me. Those spooges are gonna find out Sunday night that when you step in against Clay Vendetta... it won't just be a match...' ...and the distinctive mournful private eye trumpet wail plays... '...it'll be a CLASSIC!' The music picks up as Vendetta takes Weathers by the arm and the two walk off into the sunset, as the cameramen continue to look befuddled... Thanks for tuning in to CLAY VENDETTA: PRIVATE EYE! This episode has been brought to you by the American Ambulance Corporation. The American Ambulance Corporation: rushing John Jupiter's sorry ass to the hospital when he dies on pay-per-view this Sunday... again. We hope you enjoyed CLAY VENDETTA: PRIVATE EYE! Tune in next week for another exciting story arc... if we can sneak away from Niki, that is.
