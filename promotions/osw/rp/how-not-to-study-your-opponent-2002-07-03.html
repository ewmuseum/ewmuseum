How Not To Study Your Opponent.

3 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/3/02 As the scene opens up we find ourselves in a long line, a VERY long line outside of a large Arena type building. The line consists starts at the entrance fo the areana and then goes backwards, down the sidewalk, around the corner, and keeps on going. The humongous line consists of hordes and hordes of teenage girls. All of them are screaming with their excitedness and all are wearing various shirts which all high light the same thing. The incredibly popular boy band, 'N'Sync.' It has become very obvious that we are in the outside of an N'Sync concert. An EXTREMELY terrible place to be for males and women over the age of seventeen. The line begins to move and the hordes of horny teenaged girls begins to head towards the entrance of the arena. This looks surprisingly very similar to herding sheep for that matter. The line continues to progess and the girls only scream louder. Fade Out. Fade Back In Around A Half Hour Later. The inside of the arena is now completely packed with N'Sync fans, all of them still screaming their heads off and probably giving permanent damage to their lungs in the process. The view takes us through a hallway passing infront of a concession stand where a very disturbed looking young man is standing. He is wearing a pair of dark khaki pants and a button down short sleeved white shirt with red vertical stripes running along it, the name 'Leslie' is printed in small black letters on a beige plastic tag clipped ont the shirt, and topping off the wardrobe is a pointed rectangular paper hat atop his head. He looks on as the hordes of girls head past him towards the concert area, just dying... ABSOLUTELY DYING to see the greatest band in the world! Well, in their eyes anyhow. Leslie is just waiting for it to happen. The girls to suddenly bombard his work post and buy out the entire inventory in a matter of moments in a manner that would make a swarm of mosquitoes sucking the blood out of a cow's ass seem far more tame. Waiting...Waiting.....Still Waiting..... 'HEY! LET'S GET SOME SNACKS TO MUNCH ON GIRLS!!!' There it was, the battle cry that will lead to the actions that will permanently scar young Leslie for the rest of his life. A group of about a dozen extremely preppy girls charge towards the concession stand and begin ordering various things all in unison. This is far too much for the vendor to take, as he runs back into the storage room located behind the glass case. He slams the door shut, locks it with the key and falls back into a fetal position. He'll be safe...atleast until the concert ends. Fade Out. Fade Back In About Fifteen Minutes Later. The girls have now completely jammed pack the arena. There is no where to sit and hardly any room to even stand. Another sold-out show for the 'band.' The real shame is that most of these girls have seen the band live more times than you could count on both hands...or both feet for that matter. Fan-Girls. To the Extreme. (Author's Note: And no, not a Vanilla Ice Debut Album Reference. Word To Yo' Mother.) Suddenly, the lights dim down and some pre-recorded music begins to play, because we all know that those boy-band guys don't even know the proper way to hold a guitar, and the entire arena burts into cheers. It's an even bigger pop than Mick Foley gets for mentioning the name of the city that the event is in. Another tragic shame. 'Ladies and Gentlemen...erm Ladies. It's the moment you've been waiting for. Introducing...the greatest..and cutest...boy band in history. Please welcome-Justin, Lance, Chris, Joey and JC (Author's Note: Thank you very much IMBD) N'Sync!' All five members of N'Sync walk out onto the stage dressed in bright and sparkly clothes and each member wearing a headset, most likely to tell them the lyrics so they can remember and to modify their voices, ofcourse all of this is cleverly hidden behind the stage where a technician is standing infront a large console with more buttons than the control panel of The Enterprise. Studio Magic Baby! The stage is about the normal size of your usual stage, the floor is black and there are varius speakers and tweeters set up ingeniusly disguised as props. The bigger prop however is the large metal 'N'Sync' logo hanging over the stage. The lights slowly come back in but fade to blue and focus on center stage where the five members stand with their right hands cuffed over the left wrist and looking down at their feet. As the music kicks in Justin Timberlake looks up and stares out into the sea of teenage girls, mouthing off loudly two words.... 'Dirty Pop~!' After those two words the music pops and the other four begin to perform syncronized dance steps as the song continues to play. The girls in the crowd are freaking out, screaming louder than ever before and starting to mimmick the dance moves the group is doing poorly. This only continues for about another minute or so before something unusual happens. The large 'N'Sync' logo falls down and crashes into Chris, JC, Joey, and Lance pinning them underneath it. As the fans look in shock one of the ropes that was supporting the logo is seen sliding towards the stage floor, carrying someone with it. The former NTICW World Champion Scott Taylor! Scott lets go of the rope as he lands on his feet. Infront of him Justin is continuing to sing the song, oblivious of what is going on behind him. Scott casually strolls up behind Justin and stands behind him. Apparently having sex with Britney has ruined his senses. He still doesn't notice anything wrong. Scott reaches out and puts a hand on Justin's shoulder. Justin jumps and then turns around, looking up at the professional wrestler. 'Uhm, hello. May I help you?' asks the leader of N'Sync. Scott doesn't answer verbally. He just nods his head and then proceeds to kick Justin in the stomach causing him to double over in pain. As Justin holds his stomach Scott grabs a handful of his curly afro like hair and throws him off of the stage, Irish Whip Style. As Justin crashes into the front row of fans, they instantly orgasm as if Three Time PCW Champion Jason Snow was in the room, himself. Scott looks out at the girls and then reaches into his back pocket, producing a microphone of his own. He taps it a few times for a sound test and then puts it up to his mouth, speaking in his slight Southern Drawl which doesn't go over all that well with the preppy fans. 'Hello uhm...' Scott reaches into his back pocket and pulls out a sheet of folded paper. He opens the paper and looks it. He puts the mic back to his mouth. 'Houston!' No cheap pop. 'Let me ask all of you a question. Do any of you like...Kid Wonder?' Instant Cheap Pop Gratification. 'Right, right. Well, let me introduce myself. My name is Scott Taylor, THE RECORD SETTING LONGEST REIGNING NTICW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPINE! (Author's Note: Yes..CHAMPINE! NOT CHAMPION!), and now OSW Superstar. And I happen to have some news about Kid Wonder.' The preps stare dumbfounded, knowing nothing about wrestling or that various promotions exist anyhow. These girls couldn't tell you the difference between an Irish Whip and Vertical Suplex. He speaks again. 'Well, that news is this....Kid Wonder Sucks!' Cheap Heat. 'It's true. I punked his ass out infront of the world and I'll do it again. Except this time I'll do it in the ring and get a nice little thing called Number One Contendership.' Same Dumbfounded Stare. 'Oh well. Just remember. Kid Wonder Sucks. The Moral of The Day.' Scott looks down into the front row where Justin is now recovering, he hurls the microphone at his head hitting him dead on, right between the eyes. Justin falls back into another girls lap causing another instant-orgasm. Scott watches this, cringes, and then turns to leave. Scott's Moral of The Day is this. Never get involved with teenyboppers to try and study your opponent. Fin~
