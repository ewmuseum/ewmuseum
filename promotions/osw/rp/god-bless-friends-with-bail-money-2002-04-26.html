God bless friends with bail money!

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 26th, 2002 (Note: This would've been up yesterday, and with a much better ending but...well...yeah, I recieved some pretty shocking news along the lines of 'Your father has been diagnosed with cancer.'...I really do hate that 'cancer' word!!!! Anyway, expect at least one more RP...I can't guarantee it'll be great, but I really need to get some emotion out I guess.) Jane Reagan was in a coma...it was the closest thing to death Amy Campbell could've wished for; This was perfect. Without their female figurehead, 3B and Meanstreak wouldn't function properly...but after a nice big crunching chair-shot from Campbell two weeks ago, it wasn't like Streak would exactly be functioning normally anyway. Amy was undoubtedly one of, if not the most controversial public figures in OSW...she was in love with a maniac, frequently tried to justify the use of illicit drugs, got herself into more than enough trouble partying and just generally couldn't seem to behave. It was no different when she was speeding down the freeway leading out of Indiannapolis; cargo pants and Machine Head hoodie flapping in the wind being driven into the little red rentail Hundai Tiburon as her music blared to a point audible to any other car driving by. Tapping on the steering wheel, mouthing every lyric to 'Across The Nation' by Union Underground to herself, she was forced off the road... Why? A police car had pulled up behind her, red and blue lights flashing wildly as they signalled for the redhead to pull off to the side of the freeway... When she did, the reception wasn't exactly warm...three officers, weapons readied, were fast-approaching. ''Turn around!'' One shouted from under a thick moustache. ''Both hands on the hood, NOW!'' Uh-oh...she saw handcuffs... The moment one of the officers grabbed her arms, she struggled, growling back to them as they clamped the steel bindings around her wrists tight enough to bruise if she moved too much. ''What the hell have I done this time?'' Nobody answered, instead they left Amy to sneer as they red her rights, rolling her eyes...she could have recited those rights by now. She saw a slip of paper one of them had been holding...saw something about the word 'drug' scrawled across a neatly printed black line. Only then did Campbell figure out what they were after... ''You're wasting your time,'' She snarled. ''I'm actually CLEAN for once.'' One officer, the youngest, glared at the redhead as he and another shoved her into the backseat of the black and white vehicle. ''We'll see about that.'' They treated her unusually rough; Aimz didn't like that one little bit. But how would she get out of this situation? What EXACTLY did they want? That's all she needed to know...she had only caught a glimpse of the warrant, and the cops were being frightfully silent, giving her grounds for a lawsuit even...they hadn't actually READ to her what she was being arrested for! Here she was, allowing herself to be manhandled by these three officers; One skinny, one chubby and one who has a few too many trips to the bakery under his belt...normally she would've fought back, she could have all three of these guys knocked out within minutes. So why didn't she fight? Those three men had those three badges...that's all that stopped her. Amy was growing very sick of these laws. She hated the things that put the wrong people in power, just generally... She hated Jane Reagan for being the wrong person in power, she hated Drew Hostyle for taking the power he hangs from and using it to project a cheap rumour...she hated Sebastian Toomes for fucking the boss to ensure constant interference. Basically, Amy hated a lot and tended to come out on the shorter end of these power struggles...it wasn't because she lacked at all, it was because the world was unfair; People who otherwise shouldn't have had any were ultimately put into power, she thought this was just fate trying to piss her off. But oh well...what could be done? It was the apparent circle of life and though, through her chosen cultures, or meld of cultures as it may be, she fought it...it wouldn't get her anywhere, but she did fight. With a growing popularity there came a growing need to sing the praises of certain things, to influence certain demographics with a message of freedom that had to be spread...in her mind, you didn't have fun unless you acted the way you wanted or, in this case, the way SHE wanted. Though, she thought, the way she did things was fun for many people...so, in-turn, it could help. Being herself was what Amy knew best...she didn't hold back in anything, if a given task warrented it, she'd pour every single ounce of her being into it and there wasn't anything more important than that. Unfortunately enough, doing whatever she wanted drew a shady side to Aimz; A little too much partying, occasional dabbling into illicit substances and various other activities earned her a lot of flack from law enforcement and parents alike. As one of the officers took the keys to her rental, driving off in it, the police car she was now cuffed in the back of followed...ultimately leading to a large brick building in the heart of the city. Amy didn't bother 'seeing the sights' as the car sped through, she just concentrated on one thing...why had they arrested her like this? Who had tipped them off to ANYTHING? She had two huge matches coming up, there wasn't any way she could allow herself to be locked up like this! When she was processed, papers were signed by a fat man who reeked of sugary candies, they tossed her in a cell...leaving her to wonder aloud. ''Who the fuck tipped you on me?'' ''That's not information I'm entitled to give out, ma'am...'' One of the younger men creeped a little closer. ''But between you and I, A. Copeland.'' ''Huh?'' She thought a moment, not recognizing the name immediately. ''Copeland...Copela-------Drew Hostyle!'' ''Shhh!'' Amy clawed at one of the bars, contemplating what would happen to Hostyle when she got a hold of him this weekend. ''What's bail?'' ''Two thousand.'' ''Can I get a phone call?'' ''Be my guest...'' He grabbed her by the arm, escorting her out of the cell and over to a pay phone, once again hand-cuffed. ''What's the number?'' The taller, younger one who had given her Hostyle's name was being unusually polite. ''Six seven eight...nineteen zero eight.'' ''Alright, here you go.'' He held the phone to her right ear, causing minimal pain against her stretched lobe piercings. The phone rang...and rang......and rang again until finally a distinctively female voice picked up. ''Yeeeeeeelloooo?'' ''Bre!'' Amy smiled, Lilievre could answer the phone...which means she wasn't too high to come downtown and pick her best friend up! ''Amyyyyyyy...what's goin' on?'' ''Well...uhhhh....you see....'' She glanced to the officer, who was tapping his watch. ''...I kinda got arrested...need bail....'' ''Not again!'' ''Yes, again! Now you've gotta do me a favor...that bag I left with you the other night...there's about fifteen hundred in one of the pouches...'' ''What the fuck? You keep that much money around me? Diggity!'' Good ol' Bre and her entirely creative vocabulary. ''Yeah....now take that money out.....go to the bank and get five hundred of yours, I'm gonna have to borrow it.'' ''Okay...but this is the LAST time I provide your slinky ass with bail money.'' She chuckled on the other end. ''...I'll be down in five minutes.'' Thank God!!!!! Bre had the bail, Amy knew who pulled this shit and she'd be out to hurt him soon enough...
