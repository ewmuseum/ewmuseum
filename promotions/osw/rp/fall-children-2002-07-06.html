Date: July 6, 2002 Shit. Everything has been fucked up. I can’t go on like this. I had a plan, a wonderful and beautiful plan. All was going well, until some bitch with a skateboard came flying outta nowhere and fucking hits me from 40 feet up. Aimz… Reno Davis… Aimz… Those stupid fucks aren’t gonna live much longer, they’ve pissed me off enough. I’m gonna kill them. I’m gonna rape ‘em, then kill them. Fuck. I’m gonna fucking kill everybody until I get my fist shoved into Reno’s face. I’m gonna fucking kill all the authorities until I get my dick shoved down into Aimz’s pussy. …God, that fucking bitch. Their blood on my face, fuck that. My knife in their throats… that’ll feel good. Fuck ‘em. They’re dead. This could’ve all been over if it hadn’t been for ‘Mr. Fucking Heroic’, Reno Davis. Thanks to that lousy shithead, everything’s gotta come to an end. Aimz is gonna die. She’s brought this upon herself. She’s gonna fucking die. It’s all her fault… No, no, it’s her fault and his fault. Those motherfuckers. God, what the fuck? This piece of shit title means nothing to me, I don’t give a fuck about it. All I wanted was to fucking rape Aimz, but instead I get myself into a heap of shit… and Aimz gets off easy. Fuck you, Reno. I’m gonna fucking kill your little bitch girlfriend, I swear to god… Fuck off.
