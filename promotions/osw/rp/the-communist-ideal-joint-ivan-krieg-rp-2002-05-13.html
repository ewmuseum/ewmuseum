The Communist Ideal? (Joint Ivan/Krieg rp)

13 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-13-02 OOC: Be sure to read Krieg's previous rp to understand where this rp picks up. Thank you. Ivan Stanislav pulls up alongside the sidewalk as he sees Michel Krieg staggering through the street. He pulls up to the curb and rolls down the passenger side window of his jeep and slows to a stop. 'Comrade?' Questions Stanislav. Looking round in confusion, Krieg stares in the window of the jeep, 'AH! It is Comrade Stanislav,' says a slightly tipsy Michel Krieg, 'I was just thinking of you, my friend.' Ivan nods slowly, 'Da, and I was thinking about you as well. Why don't you allow Praporschik Stanislav to give you a ride, eh? I have things I must talk to you about.' 'That is very kind gesture, Comrade. I think I may take you up on it.' replied Krieg, slapping his hand on the roof of the jeep, As he went to reach for the handle, Krieg started to slip down the side of the jeep, and by a stroke of luck, managed to grapple the door handle in support and whip open the jeep's passenger door, tumbling inside as he does, Ivan stares grimly as he watches Krieg stagger into the jeep, 'Comrade, what is wrong with you? Do you now know that I am against drinking to excess?' 'Ah, whatever you said, Rivan, I mean Ivan. I am just here to have good time, dah.' Ivan swallows and pulls away and begins to drive down the dark street. 'Comrade Krieg, war is not a 'good time' per say, you do understand this, no? This is not a good way to show the OSW what The Red Army is made of. Why have you done such a thing?' 'What have I done to Red Army, Ivan? Have I disgraced Stanislav.' asked Krieg, looking upset by the remark. Ivan shakes his head, 'No, you have not disgraced me. Remember, Comrade Krieg, the individual is not important.' Ivan turns down a few streets before exiting off onto a dirt road. 'You have to understand, however, that we have to be mentally and physically ready for any challenge. Drinking yourself to the point of stupidity is not the correct course of action, would you agree?' 'I agree Ivan,' replies Krieg, patting Ivan on the shoulder, 'But sometimes you just need to relieve some stress, you know? It is hard to be physical specimen like Krieg and not be stressed.' Ivan nods slowly as he pulls up to a large, dark factory. Several large trucks are parked in front of the huge building as smoke pours out of various chimneys and the building glows from the inside. Sounds of hammers pounding metal as well as machinery whirring to life can be heard as Ivan stops the car. 'Comrade Krieg, I have something to show you.' mutters Ivan as he gets out of the jeep, causing the entire vehicle to shake from the loss of Ivan's weight. Fumbling with the handle on the door, Krieg somehow manages to hook his fingers around the plastic handle and push the door open, literally falling out of the car, right into Ivan's arms, 'Ah, Comrade! I appear to have had a little much to drink, eh?' said a giggling Krieg, staggering along beside Ivan. Ivan growls, 'Get a hold of yourself Krieg! You're embarrassing me and no one is even around!!' Ivan lets go of Krieg and lets him walk on his own power as he nears the factory, 'You see this, Comrade? Men work in this factory day in and day out. Twenty four hours a day they work, bettering themselves and the State! Do you understand what I am getting at?' Nodding slowly, Krieg looks at Ivan with a serious expression, one not usually associated with a drunken man, 'Comrade Stanislav, I see what you are getting at. I see you have brought me, Krieg, to this very factory to see me work, and I am proud to be here. But I am puzzled. Maybe it is the alcohol affecting my judgment, but what are you getting at, my friend.' Ivan nods and leads Krieg into the factory, which appears to be some sort of a steel mill. A huge vat of molten steel can be seen in the background, irradiating an ominous glow. Sparks fall from scaffolding above where literally hundreds of workers work on the structure. Ivan walks a few steps ahead of Krieg and waves his arms. 'Behold Comrade, the Proletariat! They work because they wish to better themselves and by doing that succeed. None is better than the other, and if one succeeds, they all do, and if one fails, they all do as well. They share their might, and they share their experiences. This is the way of Communism, my dear friend. ' Ivan clears his throat and grows slightly more grim, 'However, one must also understand that it takes a great mind of a great man to actually coordinate the masses into this mindset, da?' 'I see what you mean, my friend,' responded Krieg, sobering up slightly after getting hit with a heavy blast of cold air when he walked into the factory, 'You are leader of the Army, and a great leader at that. But I am puzzled once more...How are we going to function as a Communist team if we are both in the same match? Surely if we both try to win, one will win and one will lose.' Ivan jumps as the rationale of Krieg, 'AHA! But that is not so! For you see, there is an easy way to overcome this problem, my friend.' Ivan grins and walks closer to Krieg, thinking perhaps the drunkenness will actually play into his hands, 'The only sensible thing to do, is to work together throughout the match and defeat our enemies. And then, of course, when the final two, you and I, are left...you merely allow Praporschik Stanislav the win. Therefore, neither of us truly loses, yet we both win, eh?' Grinning, Krieg looks right into Ivan's eyes, 'Praporschik Stanislav, Comrade. I am all for Communist cause and for us to succeed as a team, but I am not stupid.' Ivan's eyes widen as Krieg continues. 'This is my chance to shine. Stanislav has already been the bright star, and Krieg has not. I suggest we play it as it comes Comrade, and if it comes down to the two greatest Communists to ever face each other in a match, then we shall do what is best for Communism, for the Army....But for ourselves...' Ivan grinds his jaw, 'That's a very independent thing to do. You know, in the past, men before you have been placed with this burden and they have agreed with the Communist way wholeheartedly. You do realize that if I am International Champion, by Communist beliefs, you are International Champion, da?' 'Yes, Praporschik, I agree. But likewise, if Krieg is International Champion, the Red Army is International Champion also...I do not want to dwell on this subject Ivan, because you know how much I like you guys.' replied Krieg, grinning and patting Ivan on the shoulder again. Ivan shrugs off Krieg's hand, visibly angry. 'Alright, you want to do that, you go ahead. You be the individual....I am going to be truthful with you, Michel Krieg, I am disappointed by your individualistic tendency. I just hope that whenever the call to Communism is truly heard, you make the right decision.' Ivan looks around at the workers, 'Perhaps you should take a look at these workers and realize what you are fighting for.' Ivan growls and walks past Krieg, towards the exit of the building. 'Come on, I am taking you to hotel to sleep off your drunkeness...' 'Ah, Ivan, don't be like that,' calls Krieg, stumbling after Ivan as Praporschik Stanislav walks out of the door of the factory, looking quite angry as the door slams off of the outside wall, 'I just want to be friend.' Krieg rushes out the door as the scene fades to black.
