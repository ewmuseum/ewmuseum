Stipulations

11 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-11-02 Saturday night has great importance... It's a nice warm spring day in Dallas, TEXAS!. The weather has finally level out, no more spring like days then winter like days in the same week. Which is what Texans life. EOE and Eve are outside in their huge backyard chillin around the pool. EOE is sitting down relaxing in his chair with his red and white male Siberian Husky laying down next to him, while Eve is putting the steaks on the grill. There's no way in hell that they'll sit inside and miss the beautiful day. While Eve continues to grill she realizes she forgot the potatoes. 'Shit, I forgot the potatoes, hey will you....go nevermind.' She decides to go herself because she notices how comfortable EOE is sitting there drinking his beer. She rolls her eyes and begins to walk towards the back door. 'I'm going to the store watch the meat, if that's not too hard for you to do' she says sarcastically.' EOE hears her but he doesn't reply because she's already slammed the door. As EOE sits there enjoying the sun something hits him. He quickly opens his eyes and sits up and the chair and kills the beer, then with a big burp to follow. The dogs lifts his head then looks at him wagging his tail. As EOE sits their with a puzzled look on his face staring at his reflection in the water various thoughts goes through his head. [Damn, I didnt realize how important this match is. So much is riding on this match in a type of match that I really hate; a tag match. Something always seems to go wrong when Im in tag matches. But I havr to get that all out of my mind, for if I come up short Saturday night who knows what stipulation he would come up with.] EOE gets up from his chair walks over to his cooler and pulls out another beer. As be opens up his bud lights and begins to drink it he walks around the pool to the barbecue pit. He lifts the top up and the smoke comes out and little flames come up. He flips the steak over and of course they begin to sizzle. As flips him over he looks down his Husky who notices him licking his lips. 'In your dreams boy.' he says to the dog. HE puts the top down and then begins to walk around his backyard continuing to think about Saturday.. [I guess Saturday I have to actually communicate with Marilyn Manson's little brother and explain the importance of this match to him. A person like that doesn't even look like he can take anything seriously. My opponents aren't that bright either, one looks like he isn't and the other, well he actually though he was going to get to see my girls goodies, yep he's dumb. But what am I trippin about? If anyone should be worried, about this match it should be Nick Casey. While he nursing nose bleeds I wonder has to taken up the time to sniff that shit up and worry about something else more important. Me with the chance to determine how much hell my opponent will go through in a match with me is better than winning the lottery. In all my years of wrestling I have never had such a chance and I'm not about to f*ck it up now.] As EOE continues to think how much fun it would be to control a match he walks back to his seat around the pool with his beer in his hands and sits down. [Nick Casey has no idea what lies ahead of him if, no not if, when I win the match Saturday. This is just like saying 'Sure Satan, my life is in your hands, go ahead, do what you want with me'. This will be my chance to give another human being to experience just a dose of what my life has been like. Me giving a person just a little dose of my life in some form is enough to f*ck someone up mentally or physically. The question is what kind of stupilation should I make when I win. SHould I be just all out evil and come with something that no one has witnessed or should I just leave it at a simple level? Let me think, Nick hasnt really done anything to piss me off, but that doesn't matter, it's all going to depend on how I feel coming around PPV time.] As EOE continues to think himself, he forgets that he has steak on the grill. Soon he get a smell that's no so pleasant. 'OH SHIT the steaks!' he says out loud. He jumps up and runs over to the grill, he quickly raises up the top of the pit and it met with a big dark cloud of smoke. He steps back because of the smoke overwhelming his face and lungs. Soon after the smoke starts to settle he picks up the steaks and of course they're ruined; and chared and shrivelled. 'Damn three perfectly good steaks gone to waste, shit!' he yells outloud. He looks just at his dog who is right by his side. 'Well consider this your dream, boy' he says to the dog. He throws the three steaks to the dog. [Now what am I going to tell Eve? I'll just tell her he at em.] As EOE looks down at the dog eating the ruined steaks and idea pops up in his head. 'Hmmm chared and shrivelled, I think I may have already come up with my stipulation. Nah that's too much, then again maybe it ain't.' EOE says to him. He continues to to think about his idea that put a smile on his face. 'I don't think they're even ready for this.' [END!]
