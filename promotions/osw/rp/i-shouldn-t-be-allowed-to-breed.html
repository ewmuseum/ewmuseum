(NOTE: To read this RP, readers should have the following things. 1) Have either a copy of The Cult's 'Wildflower' on either CD or MP3 2) Have a strong stomach. Thank You) 'Well, I guess this is the place.' Amy sighed in the warm Arizona night, underneath the neon array of lights. Sebastian Toomes, Trevor Hawke, and Seth Masters stand around her, a look of collective disgust on thier faces. Trevor is the first to voice his dismay. 'And why are we here?' Trevor spits in disgust. Amy sighs. 'Well, family obligations. My dad wanted to know what Uncle Tom was doing in Phoenix, and well, all trails end here.' Sebastian smiles gleefully. 'Well, people, prepare to have an assault on your psyche, the likes of which you've never experianced before...' He steps forward and opens the door. The four enter the darkened club and pay the doorman, thier muscular forms gliding through the velvety darkness, their white clothes gleaming bright in the blacklights. On the stage of the club, a polished marble floor complete with a brass pole, backdropped with mirrors, a man in his late teens in dancing, clad only in a shiny red g-string, flexing and gyrating in front of a throng of frenzied females in their mid-thirties, the house wife types. David Bowie's 'Lets Dance' reverbrates through the club, as he gets in a crab-walk type crouch and begins pumping his crotch up and down in from of a portly woman with too much make-up and overdone hair. She squeals and offers up a fifty dollar bill, which he takes in his hands, licks it, and places it on his hairless inner thigh. He beckons her with his eyes to peel it off his skin and tuck inside his g-string. The woman's eyes widen and she coyly backs away from the stage, but her friends, equally portly and over-wrought, urge her to put her hands down the man's g-string. He peels it off and timidly lifts the waistband away from the young man's skin and reaches in, her mouth an 'O' of delight as she brushes against his manhood in the process of giving him the money. Seth turns to Trevor. 'What the fuck?' The four settle down at a table, three pairs of eyes locked on Aimz as she nervously avoids their gaze. 'Look, I didn't know he'd be here, okay? This is the last place I'd expect Uncle Tom to turn up.' she mutters as she looks onto the stage as the young boy begins some sort of strange humping ritual with the brass pole. 'You know, we could be training up for tonight, but NOOOOO, you have to go and drag us to a goddamned male strip-club...' Trevor grumbles. Sebastian grins delusionally. 'This is outstanding.' Seth turns to him. 'Something you're not telling us?' Sebastian snickers. 'No, I don't bat for that team. I'm just saying, this is one fucked up experiance, and I have a sinking feeling its going to get even more fucked up as the night goes on. Besides, we got some time before Jane wants us at the arena. Why not spend it in style?' Seth shakes his head forlornly. Finally, 'Lets Dance' dies down and the young man begins collecting the money scattered all over the stage, smiling brightly at the women who are cooing in thier various states of drunkeness and moistness. The DJ speaks through the intercom. 'Let's give it up for Byron! He's a saucy little tart and he'll be back later on tonight to strut his man-stuff. But coming up next, we have a little something special for you, hailing from our great neighbors to the north, all the way from Novia Scotia, Canada! Call him wild! Call him uncontrollable! Call him... WOLF-CHILD!' The Cult's 'Wildflower' begins, and from off to the side of the stage, a large mass moves onto the polished white marble stage, clad in a huge wolf-pelt, a Viking helmet on his head. 'Oh. My. Fucking. God.' Aimz gasps. The man begins eyeing the crowd and moving his hips as the scattered hoots and gasps in the crowd encourages him on. 'Hey You...' Uncle Tom says seductively, pointing to a group of young women, whose eyes open in shock and suprise. 'You're a wild, honey child, I'm out of control...' Uncle Tom gyrates in front of them, letting them get a peek on what's lurking inside those wolf pelts. 'Everytime... you are near me...' Seth looks onto the stage in shock. 'What the fuck is that?' he says nervously. 'I don't...know...' Trevor says, his lips quivering in shock and dismay. 'I'm a Wolf-Child, baby...' Uncle Tom beings pumping his hips in time with the music, slowly taking off his Viking helmet and walking slowly towards a young college co-ed who is giving a good impression of the deer in the headlights. 'And I'm howling....for you...' Tom sings along with the music, licking his lips as he squats his ponderous bulk down and puts the viking helmet on her head. 'My heart beats faster...' Tom squats down even lower, supporting his 400+pounds with his arms, and begins pumping up his crotch. Suddenly, he rolls over and puts his gigantic ass in the young girl's face. He screams out with the music... 'Yeah... And I'm overpowered...OWWW!' and he punctuates the OWWW with a slap on his ass, sharp as the report of a rifle crack in the wilderness. Sebastian looks on, his jaw open in disbelief. 'This is Uncle Tom?' Aimz puts her head on the table. 'I... I've never seen that man before in my life.' 'I'm a wolf child, girl, howling for you...' Tom grins maniacally as he pumps up and down. 'WOOOO! TAKE IT OFF, BABY!' a woman with bleached blonde hair, bleached a few too many times, shrieks. 'No, no no no no... Don't do that, please fucking GOD don't do that!' Trevor says in a high, reedy voice. Uncle Tom produces a tamborine and beings shaking it along with his pounds of bulk, the wolf pelts covering a shivering column of flab in front of the 30-Something women who are drunk enough to disregard that the stripper is as heavy as most economy-sized cars. 'Wild Flower!' Tom gets down on all fours and looms his large cranium in front of the older women, who lovingly run thier hands through his hair. 'Star of my dreams the most beautiful thing, yeah yeah...' Tom snakes out his huge head and takes a lick of one older woman, taking off a huge patch of pancache make-up from her face as he does so. He continues to pump his ass in the air in time with the drum beat. 'Yeah you... He backs away, pointing at her slyly, as the older woman puts her purse on top of her lap to hide the growing wet spot. The sensation... of a nation... Trevor begins convulsing, drool bubbling at the sides of his mouth. 'Is he alright?' a passing waitress asks, a pretty young thing with auburn hair tied back in a pony-tail and wearing a tight white t-shirt with the club's logo 'Man Holes' on it in silkscreen. She looks at him curiously as Trevor spasms. 'Nah, he'll be alright... Character building, miss.' Toomes says assuringly. 'You sure about that? Looks to me like he's having a seizure.' she says questioningly. 'No, really, he'll be fine.' Oh, my soul Uncle Tom starts fiddling with the tie on the wolf-pelt. You're a perfect...creation... 'What's he doing?' Seth asks nervously. YOU'RE AN ANGEL, BAY-BAY! 'Looks to me like he's about to shake his money maker...' Toomes says, digging for a pair of blinders in his pockets, only to come up, sadly, with nothing. And I'm howling... One of the ties come loose. 'What...what do you mean, man?' Seth asks, nervously starting to look around for EXIT signs. For you 'I mean he's going to get nearly naked.' Toomes says, averting his eyes. Aimz sobs quietly in grief. My heart beats faster-her... Seth, in slow motion, begins to get out of his seat, a hand thrown in front of him, as Uncle Tom undoes the last tie and the Wolf Pelt begins to fall off his shoulders. Yeah yeah, and I'm overpowered! 'NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!' Seth screams, running forward in slow motion as the film and sound is slowed down as Uncle Tom's wolf pelt falls away, revealing a tidal wave of flab being released, hairy and dotted with sweat and ingrown hairs, pimples and blotchy red areas of unwashed skin. Toomes throws his arms up, shielding his eyes. Seth, his eyes and mouth open in horror, lurches forward and falls into a table, rebounding onto the floor. While still in slow motion, the camera focuses in on Uncle Tom's face, as he slowly chuckles. 'Huh.........huh.....hah!' his deep, distorted voice rumbles. And the film speeds back up to normal. 'I'm a wolf child, girl, howling for you...' Uncle Tom begins to saunter around the stage, a specially constructed g-string not only fitting his massive bulk, but tailored to house another protusion, one that flops around like a silk-covered elephant trunk. 'Wild Flower!' Uncle Tom whirls it around like a six inch fire hose, dazzling the older women who are frantically writing out personal checks and throwing them on-stage. 'Star of my dreams the most beautiful thing, yeah yeah...' Uncle Tom squats down and holds his palm, slapping down his appendage into it with a meaty thwack as one woman feints in excitement in front of him, a throng of her friends holding her up. Seth convulses on the floor, spastically twitching. I'll love you every hour...WILD FLOWER! Toomes gets down onto the floor and begins high crawling to drag Seth away from the stage. Burning down the night, set the world alight... Toomes reaches Seth and throws his body on top of his stable mate... YEAH! Just then, Uncle Tom climbs on the brass pole and impossibly...he begins to swing on it, the top plate connecting it to the ceiling shuddering, flakes of drywall and paint coming off it as the huge man swings around. 'Come on... COME ON!' Toomes screams as Uncle Tom spins around up-side down overhead, the former OSW Champion dragging Seth away by his collar. 'MOVE IT, MAN!' Then, as Seth is moved to safety, Uncle Tom hooks his pudgy fat leg around the pole and locking himself in place, he leans down and spreads his arms, his velvet covered dong flopping down and bouncing off his stomach, which has shifted downwards towards his chest, a big heaving mound of suspended lard, impossibly white in the darkness of the club as the women cheer and hoot. 'GAAAAA!' Toomes screams as Seth is pulled away as the ceiling gives way, and the pole rips loose from the ceiling. Toomes and Seth roll back to the table as Aimz takes a convulsing Trevor towards the door. Uncle Tom, a look of disbelief on his face as he rockets back to the marble stage, slams into the floor with a slap as all the flab hits hard, and he rolls onto the group of drunken middle aged women, crushing thier calcium-deficient bones with his bulk. Amid the screaming and the guitar solo, Toomes screams to Amy. 'NEW SCHOOL, WE ARE LEAVING!' he roars as he throws Seth over his shoulders. Aimz, with tears in her eyes, hooks Trevor's arm around her shoulder. 'Bye, Uncle Tom! Good luck!' she says before she turns and hauls 1/2 of Civil Disobedience out of the club's doors. 'Good luck!'
