A little joke...

17 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/17/02 Tom Walczak is seen in his hometown of Gdansk, Poland. He has a smile on his face as he walks down the street. Walczak looks behind him as he thinks that he hears someone screaming his name. He sees no one though, as he turns back he once again hears his name, but once again sees no one. However, he walks towards the direction from which the voice is coming from. He hears it once again, but this time realizes that it is coming from behind a bush. He slowly creeps up behind the bush, and takes a peak over it. He sees that it is two kids, probably around thirteen. They are giggling, and they do not even realize that Walczak is looking at them. Walczak makes a coughing noise, and then the kids look up. They look into each other’s faces, and scream. They try to run away, but Walczak grabs them by their shirts and lifts them off of the ground with ease. “Do you two kids think that that little joke that you played on me was funny? What if I needed to be somewhere right now, but because of you I missed my meeting? Did you think of that before you started yelling my name out?” “No, we were just playing an innocent joke. We did not think that you were going to even hear us. We just wanted to call at you because we thought that it would be some redemption for Trashcan Man, because to be quite honest we were angered at how you won the match.” “Is that so? Do you think that the way in which I disposed of that piece of shit was a dishonorable way? Was it the fact that I used a forklift, or maybe it was that I took him backstage and proceeded to beat the living hell out of him? Well, whatever you think that it is it doesn’t matter, because it was a No DQ match, and everything that I did was legal, so there is nothing that you can say that will change that.” “Ok, we are sorry Mr. Walczak, we never looked at it in that way. Can you please just let us go, and we will never bother you again. We were just playing a game, please don’t hurt us.” Walczak begins to shake the kids in the air, and they begin to cry, and as the tears fall off their eyes, their pants also begin to become moist. Walczak laughs as he puts the kids down, and they run away crying for dear life. “See, those are the types of fans that Trashcan Man has. They are weak pieces of shit that just complain when their hero loses. They thought that playing that little trick would gain some redemption for their hero, but all it did was make me angrier, and more infuriated at that piece of no good shit. Now this week is an even greater chance for me to step up in OSW. I am in a battle royal to determine the number one contender to the World Title. I know that Ivan is in it as well, and although we are partners I do want to win, but my team comes first, so I will do whatever will be best for the Red Army. I will put forth my best effort, do not be mistaken, but I may not put it forth for a singles victory but instead for a victory of the communist union.” Walczak continues to walk down the street, and as he gets farther and farther away the scene fades to black…
