[/Heresian Bloodlust]

26 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26-06-02 “Awash with death we wallow in amongst the plague of tomorrow…” The deep and raspy voice broke through the breeze, hitting them like a runaway freight train. The intensity was surely felt, and in his presence they held their ground, they daren’t tremble. Overlooking the twinkling lights of the future site of a new threat they watched as civilisation bloomed before them. Keeping a vigil, they listened closely as The Shadow Stalker preached to them. “Long since it has been, that I have felt the cold breath of the dying. So sweet it is, and so sweet it shall be. Dwelling for so long the craving has grown, finally it will be quelled with the destruction of the hated. Like a cancer, we will devour them, break them down and eat them away from the inside ‘til they are no more…then we will move on, uncaring. They are blinded as to what they have unleashed…a toxic disease, we will suffocate them, burn them out and deliver them unto evil.” Waving its hand across the horizon, The Soul Stalker takes a deep breath, retracting slightly in disgust. He turns his head and motions for his fellow heathen brethren to join him. Limping slowly towards him, the massive hulking frame of an injured, baffled and brave man is silhouetted in the moonlight breaking through the night clouds behind him. His companion, a far smaller creature, dotes across the low-cut, dew-dropped blades of grass blanketing the forlorn hill on the outskirts of the city. “One encounter changes everything…one encounter. All it takes is one encounter to get it back. But do you want it back? You saw the destruction that took place, you were a part of it! Much was lost in that encounter, though it can be reclaimed, or it can be forgotten…for now. You see, Hessian…” The silhouetted giant strides forward, with every step he takes delving into the low light of the city below. Halting at the side of his creator, Hessian props himself up against a gnarled old tree. The old, weak wood creaks heavily as the 7’2” behemoth puts his full weight on it. Meanwhile the small, purple lump of flesh they call Minion the Imp stops at Hessian’s side and looks into the petrifying glare of Hessian’s creator, rather intimidated at 3’11”. Turning back to the vast sea of lights, he exhales and lifts his head up, the light shining over the pale, grotesque skull of the Vampir Nosferatu… “…you see Hessian, you have felt the pride, the glory and the power that comes with being a champion. How did it feel? How did it feel holding that gold high above your head to the world? How did it feel overcoming those odds they all speak of? How did it feel being hounded for that which you pride yourself on? HOW DID IT FEEL TO LOSE IT ALL AND MORE????” Hessian shakes his head in silence, knowing he has let down his creator. Vampir however swipes at Hessian and hisses, and points out into the city below. “Forget it now Hessian. With Azrael Cain you were indestructible, however there were faults in the cog of the machine of destruction…and that ended the faction of Heresian Bloodlust. Now, The Cult…you and I, we can take this beast by the throat and make it submit, rip it to pieces and leave it to rot. Two moons ago you and I both lost the opportunity for supreme glory, at the hands and the steel of a new threat…if he could be called that…and upon striking us down…young Dave Gibson signed his death warrant…” Vampir Nosferatu watches as Hessian presses his large, gauntleted paw across his face, of which there are stitches running all over. From his eye to his torn lip, from his slashed nostril to his ripped ear. Hessian groans as the stinging pain thrives longingly in his skin, though as he looks up and meets eye-to-eye with Vampir, he finds his gauntleted hand by his side, and the pain searing through his face. Hessian groans in disappointment and looks to the sky, as it becomes filled with burling clouds of dark, choking fumes. “Worry is not an issue…I have judged, and it was Azrael Cain who was judged. He burned for his mistakes. The Marked Beast preached the word of ‘The Master’…well I am that Master, Hessian…I am the supreme evil Cain preached of…and Cain fell to him. Forget the past. There is a new dawning…The Cult has been birthed into this world. We are the Final Nightmare…the Final Nightmare before death grasps them and delivers them to the confines of their eternal chamber.” “We are the Final Nightmare…” Hessian groans, slumping against the tree, his face itching as the stinging refrains from stopping. Minion back off a little as Hessian begins moaning in frustration, turning to the old wooden tree and rubbing his face against it, the harsh bark relieving the pain somewhat. Remembering suddenly his face is being held together by bits of string, Hessian pulls away from the bark. He breathes a sigh of relief at the lack of pain, but roars in frustration as it comes back and hits him all at once. His face burns as air seeps into the wounds, and he brings down a mighty forearm over the old tree, splintering it and sending it shattering back to the earth in a million tiny pieces. Hessian moans, then looks over to Vampir, his glowing red eyes projecting the look of hatred for what Cain has done to him. Freezing suddenly, Hessian stares at Vampir, cocking his head as a flickering light masks The Soul Stalker’s face, dancing around the bony structure of Vampir Nosferatu’s skull. “Something destructive is afoot…a virus is sweeping through the land, infecting everything it touches…killing off the weak…” Vampir manages a guttural chuckle, glancing at the sea of lights below them, his eyes widening in anticipation as the screams ring out into the night. Hessian and Minion step forward, looking out across the land before them, as the smoke billows up into the night sky, the screams tear through the streets and the raging inferno sweeps across the horizon. “We are the virus…we are the destruction…we are The Cult…” The roaring of the three Heretics rings out through the night, equalling the volume of terrified screams emitting from the inferno below them. The night rages on with the fire, and they watch in delight as Dallas burns to the ground… Fin
