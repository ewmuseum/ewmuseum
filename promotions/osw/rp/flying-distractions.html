Flying distractions...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/8/01 Walczak is seen running into through the airport entrance and looking for where he can check his bags. He asks around and finally gets told where the LOT airline check-ins are. He hurries over, and luckily for him there are no people that are waiting in front of him, so he gets through the baggage check quickly. As soon as he gets his bags checked a voice comes over the loudspeaker. “LAST BOARDING CALL FOR ALL THOSE TRAVELING TO GDANSK, POLAND. THIS IS THE FINAL CALL. THE PLANE WILL DEPART IN FIVE MINUTES.” Walczak quickly grabs his ticket and rushes over to the boarding area. He gets there just in time, and he finally begins to relax as he walks down the hallway that leads to the plain. As he gets in they shut the door behind him, and he then realizes just how close he came to missing the plane. He gives the stewardess the ticket, and she leads him to his place. “Is this your first time flying first class sir?” “Of course not I always fly with style and elegance. How could you ask a man of my stature such a question? Do you not know who I am?” “I think that you are that Polish wrestler, right?” “Of course I am, and my name is Tom Walczak. You can leave now, and you will be called upon when I need something.” The stewardess leaves, and is taken off to the side by one of her co-workers. The co-worker explains what a grave mistake the lady has made, and she herself goes over to Walczak. “I am sorry for the discomfort that the other stewardess caused. I realize that you paid very good money for that seat, and you must feel bad being treated as you were, but is there something that I can get for you that would lighten up your mood a little bit?” “Well, you can get me a couple of beers.” “Certainly, I will come back shortly with your beers.” Walczak lays his head back, and much to his approval there is no one sitting next to him. He is interrupted in a while, and at first he thinks that it is the stewardess, but when he looks up and sees a man he thinks that this is the man that is sitting next to him, and he was just using the bathroom. “May I help you?” “Well, I overheard that Tom Walczak was on the plane, so I had to come over and see for myself.” “Well, do you want something like an autograph of some sort?” “That would be grand, but first I just wanted to ask you a question.” “Go ahead.” “Well, since you are a communist I was wondering why you are sitting up here in First Class when there are other commoners sitting in the back. Is it not the Communist credo that all should be treated equally?” “What kind of question is this? Of course all should be treated equally, but in the best possible conditions. Is it my fault that the capitalist pigs that run the airlines cannot satisfy the needs of all that fly? No, and get the hell out of here before I get really angry and beat the hell out of you.” “What about my autograph?” “People who ask dumb questions like you don’t get autographs. Now get the hell out of my face ok.” The man leaves, and as he crosses back into the coach area the stewardess comes into the First Class area with Walczak’s beer. She goes over to him, and places three beers on his pull out tray. “Is that all sir?” “Thank you very much, but if there is one thing that you can do I would be greatly appreciative.” “What is that?” “Well, I am going to try and get some rest now, so could you please not allow anyone to come up here and visit me?” “Of course sir.” Walczak thanks the stewardess once again, and he rests his head and closes his eyes. He dozes off to sleep and the scene fades out…
