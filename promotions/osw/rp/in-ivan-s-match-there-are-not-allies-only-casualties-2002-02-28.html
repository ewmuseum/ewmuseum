In Ivan's match...there are not allies..only casualties!

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-28-02 Ivan Stanislav sat at his large oaken desk in Arkhangelsk, Russia, patiently awaiting the folders of information that he had sent out for roughly three hours ago. Stanislav wears his black t-shirt, black pants with dual red stripes, red suspenders, and boots as he waits, leaned back in his chair with his arms crossed over his barrel chest. As he waits, Stanislav smirks to himself, thinking about the going-ons of the past few days. The return of Meanstreak is still strong in his heart, but he is not worried. ‘The people think that he will save us all,’ thinks Stanislav, ‘but they are wrong. Ultimately Meanstreak will give way to the power of The Red Army, in one way or the other. I have seen it before, they think that he will deliver them from the clutches of Soviet supremacy, only to find that he is actually more of a problem than a solution.’ Ivan chuckles to himself and pulls open a drawer, slowly pulling a small pouch and rolling papers from it. The Russian Bear leans forward and rolls a cigarette, and then puts the paper and tobacco away. He brings the cigarette to his lips and puffs on it, after promptly lighting it with a large steel lighter with a bright hammer and sickle on the front of it. He puffs for a bit and watches the smoke leave his lips and rise slowly to the ceiling, only to collect and swirl with the slight air current in the room. ‘Yes,’ thinks Stanislav, ‘it will be a glorious day, when the entire Red Army stands in the wrestling ring and Meanstreak lies battered and beaten beneath my feet. I have taken care of him once….’ Ivan then speaks aloud. “Ivan can do it again.” He chuckles and tilts his head to the side and at that moment, his telephone bursts into sound, ringing loudly. Ivan jumps and grabs the phone and brings it up to his ears. “Praporschik Ivan Stanislav!” Ivan listens to the line for a moment and then continues. “Alexei! How are you my friend?” Stanislav pauses again, obviously listening to what Alexei has to say. “Oh yes? You sent fax to my office? Then I should be getting an entire report on said individuals shortly!” Ivan listens again, shaking his head slowly in agreement with whatever Alexei has to say. “Yes, yes Alexei, I agree completely. They will not be a problem! They will surely stand in line and watch The Russian Bear do what he does best, dominate and destroy the competition!” Ivan pauses and shakes his head for a moment in disagreement with whatever Alexei says. He leans forward and speaks softer. “Alexei, I am not worried about her. I had a talk with her, remember? She will not cause any trouble on Saturday…….of course I am not interested in her…….shut up you, I am just curious as to her relationship with Jane Reagan…….be quiet, I trashed the room due to other reasons in which I cannot speak of on the phone……” Ivan shakes his head as he absorbs a large spiel from Alexei on the line, “Alexei Gregorovich, I understand your concern but there is nothing to be worried about! Praporschik Stanislav is in complete control of situation……I know that you never doubted me, but I am just reassuring your beliefs…..that is correct…..da……da……when will you return?” Ivan listens again with a now confused look on his face, “What do you mean you have other assignment that is keeping you in United States? We have expressed permission that all of your talents will be used to further our cause, and not that of the Federation! There is nothing major going on that would require your exquisite talents!” Ivan leans back in his chair and crosses an arm across his stomach, holding the cigarette in his other hand, and propping the phone between his massive shoulder and head. “I am not convinced, I do not hope you are trying to do me some ‘favor’ and not telling me about it! We have much work to do that requires our combined effort……da…..yes, I understand….uh huh……yes.” Ivan pauses and nods again just as his fax machine whirs to life next to him. Ivan nods to himself, “Alexei, I apologize but your report is coming through the fax machine as we speak, so I must study it. Da. I do not know what you are up to my friend, but I have the utmost confidence in anything that you are working on! Das vi danya my friend!” Ivan puts the phone down and shakes his head, visibly confused by the conversation that he had with Ruslan. He grabs the papers as they flow out of the fax machine. He orders them how they are supposed to be and looks at them, speaking the words to himself silently. “Hessian….Azrael Cain….Heresian Bloodlust…..?” Ivan pauses for a moment and continues to study the papers. Finally, he comes to some sort of a conclusion and tosses them to the desk in a careless fashion. Stanislav leans back in his chair and rubs his face, “Another group of fools that I have been paired up with. Someone must do something about that idiot commissioner in that federation, he is going to ruin everything! By the time I take over the OSW, there will be no OSW worth HAVING!” Ivan leans forward and slams his fist onto the top of the table. He reads some more into the papers of Heresian Bloodlust….. “….fighting for the hell of it…….revolutionary war of America…..Minion….Caleb….The Master….” Ivan shakes his head and shoves the papers off of his desk. He stands and growls to himself. “Why must I deal with these fools! Idiotic and confused children that think they are fighting for some great evil!!” Ivan kicks his desk and turns to a map of the world. He jabs his finger into the United States. “THIS is what I want to take over! This is what I need to communize! And now I have to fight with two men that think they were born from the fires of a plane that does not exist!” Ivan turns to the camera, “What is it that I must do before I can finally achieve what is rightfully mine!” roars Stanislav, “why must I deal with these idiotic individuals!!” Ivan stomps his foot on the ground eliciting a bassy thud, “Civil Disobedience is a team comprised of two children that think being ‘cool’ and speaking in words that are ‘catchy’ will garner them victory! Meanstreak has no role in life save for his own pathetic existence, which needs to be finished immediately! And then, Heresian Bloodlust, probably comprised of two teenagers that just wish to have more money due to lack of allowance from their mother, must fight with ME!” Ivan roars loudly and stomps his foot again, “I am a soldier in Russian Red Army, and I have to fight along and against the likes of these men? WHY?!” Ivan walks over to the papers that he had thrown to the ground and picks them up. He promptly stomps over to the fireplace in his office and tosses the papers into the fire, letting them burn into nothing, “These two have been embroiled in war with Civil Disobedience, and we will leave it like that. I am not outnumbered in our team, for I have the rest of Red Army behind me. I do not care of Hessian is angry because Ivan supposedly ‘interfered’ in his wrestling match, because in times of war there is only one rule…the rule in which the fighter himself lives by!” Ivan turns to the camera as more smoke billows into the room, somewhere along the lines of his tirade, Ivan lost his cigarette. He stands defiantly in front of the camera, “Saturday Slam, Meanstreak will be vanquished once again, and he will not rise up against the likes of the OSW and her Red Army anymore! I am here to protect federation from the likes of Meanstreak…AND Heresian Bloodlust. Do not think that Ivan Stanislav has any allies in wrestling ring..you are all my enemies, and you will all become casualties in my great war!!” Ivan laughs loudly at the camera while it slowly fades away to black.
