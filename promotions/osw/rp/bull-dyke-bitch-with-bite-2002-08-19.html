Bull dyke bitch......with BITE!

19 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 19th, 2002 Running back to her locker-room, side-by-side with Reno and his furry companion, her vision glazed over in a haze of what might have been known to any other person as 'Joy', Amy Campbell had a specific lyric running through her head to snap the reality back into what she'd just accomplished... Brad Arnold of 3 Doors Down once sung, ''All my work and endless measures never seem to get me very far. Walk a mile to move an inch now, even though I'm trying so damned hard.'' She'd spent since March walking miles in OSW, only to gain an inch of ground... But hey, an inch by any other name is still a gain, right? At least that came to be some comfort, although she was fairly sure Tempest, Lillith and Bethany Yamato were officially after her scalp for winning that contendership, considering how 'pleased' Lillith and the Little Storm were looking afterwards. ''You're worrying again.'' Reno smiled when they got inside. He couldn't help but laugh at how serious she'd been lately. Hell, she'd grown to be worse than even HE could be sometimes. ''Come on, your name isn't Ivan Stanislav.'' She was smirking, but her eyes were still weary and watchful. ''I'm not worried, it's fine. Nobody can kill my buzz tonight.'' ''Good.'' Davis was still smiling when ZaC climbed up his back, clinging to the thick cotton material of his Black Flag t-shirt. ''So...where you want to celebrate?'' Amy just sagged down onto a bench and began sliding her tattered knee pads out from under the legs of the lightweight nylon pants she'd worn to the ring. She raised an eyebrow and looked coyly up to Reno before she spoke again. ''Depends on who I'm celebrating with.'' ''How about ZaC and I?'' Hearing ZaC put into the picture, Aimz couldn't help but frown in a momentary fit of disappointment, but it soon passed when Davis grinned with a promising wink. ''Uhh...well....'' 'Well there's no way in hell I'll convince him to ditch ZaC......where can we go with a monkey?', Campbell furrowed her brow. ''Ah hell, I just wanna go sleep.'' ''Not feeling social tonight?'' He laughed. Aimz pouted a little and grumbled, ''Not in the least.'' ''Then let's head out.'' When Amy had shoved all her gear away into a duffel bag that soon found itself slung over her shoulder, she slipped up beside Reno as he opened the door and laced her fingers with his while they strode quickly into the hallway. She was in a hurry to get away from all of the meaningless congratulations and pats on the back, so Aimz slid ahead of Davis a little... ...only to be stopped dead in her tracks when her forehead slammed straight into what FELT like a brick wall but, in reality, was the leather-clad collar bone of Callisto Bane and when Reno realized that, he used the grip he had on Amy's had to send her reeling back into his chest. Callisto laughed when she saw that Campbell was angrily trying to jolt forward at her only to be tightly gripped from behind by both of her boyfriend's stronger arms. ''Mmm, Amy...'' She licked her lips while looking the little redhead up and down, making sure to keep an eye on Reno just in case he tried to be the knight in shining armor. ''Do you ever imagine your flesh being cut open with a razor? Slicing into your skin deep and precise. Feeling it lifting up as your nerve ends tingle, realizing it is so deep you cannot feel it anymore, just a warm glow from out the wound. Feeling the warm blood ooze warm and feeling it slide moist across your body spreading it like finger paints. Just curious really. Because that is how it will feel when I disconnect each limb from your body inside of the ring Saturday night...'' Amy took another lunge at her and almost got out of Reno's grip, but he only held her tighter. ''What the fuck are you going on about? You wanna see my scars, bitch? Match on Saturday my ass...you requested something? Like they'd ever book THAT.'' ''Oh Amy...such a child you are. Petty scars mean absolutely nothing to me. If you really want to prove yourself then you will not show up late for our duet.'' Bane chuckled sadistically. ''We DO have a match, eh? And you're already wishing I wouldn't show? Not surprising.'' She was calmer for the time being, but Davis wouldn't relinquish his grip. He knew how feisty Amy was, he also knew how well Callisto had handled her in the bar a few nights ago, and how well she could probably handle her right now, when Aimz was un-prepared, cocky and irrational. ''Heh...did you, I dunno, just happen to take a little peek at a monitor a few minutes ago? You know, when I was WINNING THE NUMBER ONE CONTENDERSHIP?'' ''Ah, but Amy you were up against women of your own stature. You must not begin to underestimate what will happen inside of the ring. As I said before...I will enjoy ripping you apart.'' ''Rip ME apart? Bethany Yamato...six foot four, two hundred pounds. That ring a bell? Because, in case you hadn't been paying attention, I just ripped her AND three other women apart in that fucking ring...'' Amy said this while Reno tried to contain laughter. He loved to see Campbell when she was in 'cat-fight' mode, she was all-too fiery and so angry that the insults just rolled off her tongue like droplets of water from a near-emptied bottle. ''So maybe, just MAYBE, you should take your bull dyke, bitch ass back to whatever little training camp you came from and learn what it is you're playing with before you get in over your head.'' Callisto merely burst into another fit of laughter.''I believe six foot six and two hundred eighty five pounds will be much more to your liking Amy. And as far as Dykes go...you will be getting a much more open view of the things I am capable of if you smart your mouth off once more.'' She glanced to Reno with a comical expression gracing her features. She wasn't afraid of the either of them, she knew her own talents and skills. ''What, the truth hurt? Weren't you the one who was spouting some sick shit to Shanda Russell that you were gonna lick me all over or whatever?'' Campbell dipped her head back towards Davis who, sensing the next statement, curved his lips into a crooked smile. ''Sorry bitch, that's HIS job.'' Callisto smirked timidly. ''The only truth you will know when I am done with you Saturday is the truth..of pain. And as far as Reno goes...'' She was trying to hold back a small burst of laughter. ''I doubt he even knows the meaning of down South.'' With this she advanced, staring Reno directly in the eyes before snatching Amy away and delivering a hard kiss straight to her lips. After that, Bane just tossed Aimz back at Reno, who were both too shocked and jilted to even move after her when she walked out the Employee's Entrance to her car. After a few moments, ZaC, who had hidden the moment he saw Callisto's huge form, emerged from behind one of Reno's legs, only to see Amy spit at the ground, making various 'EUGH!' noises when she processed what had happened. ''What...'' Reno just stared blankly toward the door. ''...the fuck...was that....'' ''Whatever the fuck he, she...IT was.....it touched me...'' She twitched. ''I don't think I'll be eating anytime soon...'' They proceeded out the door after a few moments, ignoring the others who had fallen witness to Bane's forceful 'tactics'. Amy and Davis both stepped into the car in total silence, Aimz still jilted when she mumbled, ''Can we stop at the pharmacy on our way back? I need some SERIOUS Listerine...''
