[Begin File.] Outside of Atlantic City, New Jersey in a relatively rural section of the state, there lies a small patch of wooded hills. The land, despite its proximity to a large population center, is only very sparsely populated, which means that if you avoid the beaten path and something happens to you… well, let’s just say that any searchers are pretty likely to have a copy of your dental records on hand to identify you with. In these hills, a secret lair is situated. Nestled in a tiny valley smaller than your average football field, this lair is surrounded with layer upon layer of defenses designed to stop, or at least cause very definite trouble to, any and all unwelcome visitors. It is quite possibly the most heavily protected watertower in the world. And it is about to be breached. In the sheltering cover of a rocky overhang, the robotic menace known as Cobol waits with a patience rivaling some monoliths, watching the watertower with a resolute single-mindedness that television producers would KILL to get from their audiences. It has been almost a month of this constant vigilance. Rust streaks are everywhere and one enterprising bird has constructed a nest upon his head, but the time of waiting is almost at an end. At the tower, all the residing humans have FINALLY gathered to take a journey, one that will leave the watertower completely vacant for some little while. Cobol muses, how easy it would be to simply charge the foolish meat things, to tear them asunder and laugh at their shrieks of dying agony. But that could jeopardize the plan, so it will not happen. So he waits and watches. They are arguing, nothing new there. Trivial things, unimportant things, but from their frequency and enthusiasm exhibited, it somehow vital to unit cohesion. Undoubtedly another flaw in humans that would lead them to their inevitable defeat and extermination. Order and utter obedience to the master was much cleaner, more efficient. The humans piled themselves into a tiny car that smoked and sputtered as it drove down the dirt path leading to the city. It was time. Small shards dribbled down as the sharpened spikes that made up the tips of Cobol were ripped from the earth where they anchored the automaton steady. Servomotors force long unused joints into a squeaky, lumbering trot that eats up the distance between the robot and the outer fence rapidly. Suspicious metallic objects buried randomly about the tower show up plainly on magnetic resonance imaging sensors and are easily avoided. The small caliber bullets fired by the sentry guns are ignored, likewise the harmless bites by the packs of ferocious lemurs. Only the fence gives pause with its razor wire topping and the humming capacitors linked to the framework… but not for long. Thin wire mesh, spitting hot blue sparks of energy, shreds easily against the inhuman pressures exerted upon them. WHAM! The front door collapses into splinters against an iron foot and Cobol steps through to the front foyer. Another door is reduced to kindling along with a number of items of standard neo-office décor; cubicles, desks, copiers, a table of black oak, etc, etc. Against the outside wall, a blockish monster of a computer stenciled ‘C.L.O.D.’ blinks malevolently. Cobol kneels in supplication. “Master System, unit Cobol has in possession the Pentium X chip required for ultimate plan.” Bright beams of red laser light play up and down Cobol’s scratched and dull surface. “UNIT COBOL WILL INSTALL PENTIUM X CHIP IMMEDIATELY AND AWAIT FURTHER INSTRUCTIONS.” A small panel clicks open on a waiting chip receptacle. “Yes, Master System.” The small panel of aluminum snicks neatly into the slot. “CHIP NOT FOUND, ERROR! UNIT COBOL WILL INSTALL PENTIUM X CHIP IMMEDIATELY AND AWAIT FURTHER INSTRUCTIONS.” “Pentium X chip installed correctly.” “RUNNING CHIP DIAGNOSTIC… CHIP NOT FOUND, ERROR! DIAGNOSTIC RESULTS: CHIP FAULTY AND UNUSABLE FOR ULTIMATE PLAN. QUERY UNIT COBOL: SOURCE OF FAULTY CHIP.” “Unit Cobol reports faulty chip came from human designated ‘Vax’.” “HUMAN DESIGNATED VAX DEEMED UNRELIABLE. HUMAN DESIGNATED VAX KNOWS TOO MUCH OF ULTIMATE PLAN. UNIT COBOL WILL TERMINATE HUMAN DESIGNATED VAX AND OBTAIN FUNCTIONAL PENTIUM X CHIP VIA ALTERNATE SOURCE. END OF LINE.” “Unit Cobol acknowledges.” Back at Twisted Metal’s basement apartment… “NO WAY!!! I’m sick of dealing with that damned human! Go scrag him yourself!” “This is indeed an unpleasant situation.” Cobol merely crosses his arms as his sharpened tentacle tips wave threateningly. He regards his less than eager minions, Chico and Gunmetal unwaveringly. “Reports noted and irrelevant. Units Chico and Gunmetal will terminate human designated Vax and obtain functional Pentium X chip. Units Chico and Gunmetal will go now!” Gunmetal looks at Chico, his camera lenses focusing. “This will not end well.” Chico tilts his head slightly. “For him, or us?” Gunmetal merely shrugs. [End File.]
