An Army of One

4 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/04/02 'Another crazy motherf##ker, livin' it up, not giving a f##k living life in the fast lane' - Fred Durst, 2000 With all men, there is something special. A certain characteristic that makes us all unique. One thing that separates every man and every woman from each other. You know, you can do something better then Johnny that lives down the street. It may be dribbling a basketball. You might be able to get to the second level of Super Mario Bros. faster then anyone in this world. Hell, you may just be better at 'jerkin' the meat' then everyone else. The fact remains, we all have our special qualities. So the question I pose to you is, what is Naket's special ability? We all know it isn't wrestling, cause hell, he ain't perfect at that. He sure as hell isn't the funniest man in the world, but that doesn't mean he doesn't try to be. And we all know he isn't the smartest man in the world. So what is so special about Scott Naket? 'GOD DAMN DOG S##T! RUIN MY NEW SHOES WILL YOU?!' Naket yells at the top of his lungs. We open our scene in a forest somewhere presumably in Easton. The thick marsh of the forest can be seen as far as the eye can see, with trees, bushes, dirt and wildlife everywhere. Nothing outside of the forest can be seen, and you can only see parts of the blue skyline above. With each step taken, the ground cracks from the frost that's clinging onto the ground. All the mud that used to be there from the rain has been transformed into a icy kind of dirt, not exactly ice, but then again, not exactly dirt either. The sounds of Naket yelling in rage filled the forest, as the camera man turns around, looking in the direction of which the yell came from. Nothing. Another look around, and there is still no sign of the Canadian Icon. Then, without any warning at all, all the camera can see is a huge, brown hunk of dog crap flying towards the camera itself. Being too shocked to run would be a understatement here. I mean, when was the last time you saw a huge pile of crap flying towards you without any warning or time to react? Yeah, so that would explain now why the camera is covered with dog crap. To the delight of everyone viewing, all we can see is a mushy brown on the camera. 'What the hell?' the confused cameraman asks. The camera can still see little pieces of light, and it's seen being dropped to the ground. The cameraman pauses, and is seen kneeling down in front of the camera. 'God damn,' the camera man exclaims, 'these are brand new cameras. I'm really gonna get it when I get back.'. The cameraman pulls out a towel from the small bag he is has with him and starts to wipe the crap of the lens of the camera. The sounds of flies around the camera can be heard, as they are trying to now get a piece of their own treasure. Sounds of sniffing can be heard, followed by a statement that everyone could figure out right away 'Man, it smells like s##t!'. Sigh, yes it took that long to figure that out. The camera, still sitting in the ground, sees straight ahead under the cameraman's legs. All of the sudden, a man is seen emerging from the bushes. Wearing a camouflage outfit, complete with a pair of black boots and a green helmet with a bunch of weeds stuck on the top, the man creeps slowly towards the cameraman who is facing the opposite direction. The camera just catches a glimpse of the mans face, which is black and green with paint, as he slowly creeps up, trying not to make any sounds whatsoever. SNAP! The cameraman turns around to see the man standing on a broken stick. The two stand there and stare at each other for a brief second before the man dressed in the army clothes runs at the cameraman and leaps towards him. The slight is blurred with the sudden kick of mud into the camera's lens, but we can still hear the sounds of struggle in the background. All of the sudden, you just hear yelling. 'GET IN THE HOLE! IT'S ME, JUST GET IN THE HOLE!!!' yells the familiar voice of the Canadian Icon. The camera starts moving, and is lifted off the ground. A hand wipes the mud of the lens, allowing the viewers to see once again. The camera turns around and shows the cameraman in a trench like hole, looking mighty confused. The camera drops down into the hole, and is passed back to the cameraman. 'Now,' Naket begins, 'I want you to just sit here, watch me, and do as I ask, you got it?' The camera nods with the cameraman, and Naket, complete with his full flaming pile of s*it outfit, smiles and looks at the camera. 'Alright,' Naket, who is now whispering for some odd reason, 'here is the status of what's going on. OSW is new, but it's now under attack by an army. Now, this isn't no regular army however, these are the Russians. And we all know the Russians are fierce, strong, proud and very brave. However, what they don't know is I have a powerful army too! Yes, we Canadians, like the Russians are also fierce, strong, proud and very bra...' SNAP! 'AHHH, WE'RE GONNA DIE!!' Naket yells as he leans to the side of the trench. He gasps in quick breaths of air as he holds his chest. He takes a big gulp, and slowly stands up to examine the situation. Naket pears over the trench slowly, and looks around. He slowly slides back down and looks at the camera. 'It's ok, it was just a chipmunk.' Naket says with a sigh. Naket takes a deep breath, and looks back at the camera. 'But you know what? I'm gonna just play it cool. Ivan Stanislav and his commie friends can go rip through anything they want. If they destroy Doug Troy and Trashcan Man, so be it, they did what they wanted too. If they tear Great Dragon a new one, oh well, not my problem. But the minute they get involved with one of my problems, let it be myself or some other Canadian, that's when they will have to face the all powerful army of one! And when they...' CRACK! 'THE RUSSIANS ARE COMING, NOOOOOOOOO!' Naket yells as he again ducks for cover. Naket's feet and hands start shaking as he waits momentarily for any other sounds. Naket waits for 1....2....3....4 seconds, and he slowly sits back up. Naket sighs, and looks at the camera. 'False...false alarm I guess.' Naket says as he takes in another deep breath of air. 'So Ivan, and the rest of the Red Army, you have been warned.' Naket grins one of those cocky looking grins, but not one with as much confidence this time. Naket slowly raises to his feet, and looks around. He signals to the camera man to stand up, and he does. Looking around his surroundings a final time, Naket pushes himself up the other side of the trench, and stays flat on his stomach. He then starts to crawl forward through the mud and dirt of the forest. The cameraman follows behind, but on his feet. Naket talks as he slowly progresses forward. 'The reason I'm doing this,' Naket says with a grunt in his voice, 'is so that no one from OSW can find me on my way to Phily.' Naket pauses as he continues to slowly move through the forest. 'You see, I'm a marked man right now in OSW. The Red Army will destroy anything that comes within breathing distance of them, Clay Vendetta wants to do another 'Classic' cowardly attack on me, and Mike Marvelous,' Naket pauses in his tracks and thinks for a while. He smiles, and continues to move through the mud and what not. 'Well, the way Mike has been portrayed the last few months, I'm afraid this guy might rape me or something.' Naket stops and gets on his feet. He starts to run throughout the forest while the cameraman plays catch up. 'The fact remains,' Naket says while trying to keep his breath, 'is that while I'm undefeated, I'll be a marked man in OSW. Everyone will want a piece of me, no matter what.' Naket leaps over a log, and rushes towards the while light, better known as the end of the forest. Naket sprints towards the end, and the cameraman himself turns it up a notch and starts to sprint, amazing himself in the process. Naket leaps over a final log and dives forward past the final trees. Now, a dramatic ending like this is always something Naket wants you to see. Something to leave you in awe, and want to see what he'll do next. Well, little did Naket know that the hill after the forest was pretty steep. So doing a summersault dive out of the forest would be a stupid idea right? 'OH! OW! AH! SON OF A! GOD! CRAP!' Words spoken by a true buffoon, as Naket is seen flying down the hill, unable to stop. The hill is a good hundred feet, and the cameraman plays it safe by staying at the top of the hill. He zooms in at Naket's body, which is laying at the bottom. Naket tries to rise to his feet, and does, but wobbles around. He looks around, dazed and confused. 'Goodnight.' he proclaims, as he falls to the ground in a heap. Yes, everyone has their special something that makes them different from everyone else in this world. Unfortunately for Naket, his special characteristic is that he always comes out looking like the buffoon he claims he's not.
