i. am. what. you. make. me.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'You know Rhett,' Bunker Bill leaned up against the wall, with a cigarette sandwiched between his lips. 'You're the biggest fucking asshole I know. HE HAS MY RING! THE STUPID ASS HAS MY RING!' Rhett lay alone in his hospital bed. Though, I guess we can say he'll never 'truly' be alone. He watched Bunker Bill bury his face into his hands, tears strolling down his cheeks. 'Yeah, no shit fucktard. But I can't get it back! The guy's got me confused in more than thirty ways. He hates me, yet helped me win the title...It's strange.' 'Wrestlers.' Bunker Bill scoffed, he was quite a wise man, yeah. Religious as well. He donned a black cloak, with his face hidden by the brim of the hood. A man of nature, he considered him self wicca. A witch. Something Rhett never had, and quite frankly never has wanted to understand. 'What can you expect? Listen, dolt. Just think about it. WHY on EARTH would Graphic Violence want to have the title on you?' *** 'I watched him through his door window. The Albino, carrying on a conversation with himself had no idea I was spying on him. But it was so intriguing, ladies.' The nurse told her fellow workers over a tomato soup lunch. Her coworkers listened with eyes wide open, and ears peered. 'This is the thing. His eyes... 'His eyes were like ice... 'Cold, cold eyes.' *** 'So you're saying the only reason Graphic Violence helped me was so that he co--' Rhett was crudely interupted. 'There's a girl peaking in.' 'Zuh? Where's she at now then?' Rhett asked Bunker, who went to open the door. 'She ducked.' *** 'I ducked as he saw me. I heard the bed creak and bare feet hit the tile flooring. I was scared...I wanted to run desperately, but I couldn't. I just wanted to meet him...just wanted to see him...' A fat nurse asked, 'WELL!?! What happened!?!' *** Bunker Bill opened the door, and his eyes met hers. *** 'Our eyes met, and...' *** She asked Bunker, 'Wha--wha-- Are you some kind of freak?' He knew the answer, but instead, he answered with the unexpected...something Bunker had a tendency to do. *** 'I couldn't help it! He had a big red star tattooed on his chest...What could I have done!?!' The table was silent, for a brief period of time. Until the one beside her finally whispered, 'What happened?' 'Well, it was the strangest thing...he said... 'He said,' *** 'I am what you want me to be, lady. I am what you want me to be.'
