Post-PPV 'Celebration'? Headed back home...( w/ Rent-A-Hero.) Written: May 1st, 2002 (Note: Yeah...here's an RP. I've been kinda pressed the last couple of days, but things seem to be getting a little better. This has little meaning...I'm just waiting to get back into the 'heat' of an angle...hehe.) ''YOU WON!!!!!!!!!'' A voice came from Amy Campbell's Holiday Inn room doorway. The voice, just the same as ever, was easily recognized by the swift, high tone as Breanne Lilievre's... ''Yup...'' Aimz was sitting by herself, folded up on the grey carpeting in front of the television. She was watching her Head On Collision victory again, smiling all the while. ''...fucking right I did.'' ''That was AWESOME! Oh, Jesus...we've gotta go get fucked up or something!!!'' Bre was as enthusiastic about intoxication as ever, her eyebrows nearly at the back of her head. ''Like, fuckin' Heavenly Blue or some shit...'' Amy noted the excessive language...which was a bit much, even for Bre. ''...You're into something now, aren't you?'' She laughed. ''Jeez...can't leave you alone AN HOUR without you getting smashed...'' ''I'm not drunk yet....only got a teeny bit of some fuckin' rum 'n coke in me.......'' ''That story doesn't sell with me...what about the weed?'' ''Huh? Ohhh... that ...heh heh heh heh.........................'' Good ol' Bre and the innocent yet suggestive chuckle...how many times was that used back in high school? More than Amy even WANTED to try counting. ''I won't even bother...'' She smiled, shaking a head topped with a mass of that bright red frizz. ''...but yeah...I can't go out anyway.'' ''Whaaaaaaaa?'' ''Rentie and I...we've gotta catch a flight back to Canada! Gonna visit dad, let RAH meet the family and get strung out with whoever the rest of the time. You up to coming?'' ''Have you even gotta ask!?'' Breanne was, once again, bright-eyed and bushy-tailed. ''Shiiiiit man...we'll go see Renee and all that!'' ''Definitely...she called me the other day, all pissy that OSW wasn't going to Halifax on the Canadian tour...'' Campbell's face grew rather gruff as she continued. ''...truth be told, I'm a little pissed about it too. They overlooked us totally...but ah well, there's always next time...and I'll be pushing to the fucking extreme to get 'em to book out the Metro Center. I've always wanted to fight back home!'' ''Damn right! I'm hopin' you mean official fights...'cause I remember you fighting QUITE enough down home, heh. But anyway...I've gotta jet I guess, I'll letcha' pack up...I'm gonna call Renee and tell her we'll fuckin' be there. Got a ride back from the airport?'' ''Nah.'' Bre spoke once again with a laugh before staggering out the door. ''I'll get her to pick us up...she never has anything to do but fuckin' smoke up, so count on it.'' ''Alright, see ya!'' Once Miss Lilievre was gone...all Amy had to do for the next little while was pack up and try to get the sweet stench of 'mary jane' that tended to follow her best friend OUT of the room before anybody got suspicious. You don't wanna see that boring shit, right? Good, 'cause you won't! 30 MINUTES LATER. After Breanne had gone, everything faded back in only to find Rent-A-Hero now standing in the room with Amy, helping her pack up a little duffle bag with various pieces of clothing that she'd had strewn over the bed in a fit of ''What to wear?!''. An odd few moments of silence ensued, but RAH quickly picked up the conversational ball and ran with it... ''THANK THE GODS OF GREYSKULL YOU DIDN'T LOSE THAT MATCH!! Having to see my sweet Amy admit she lied would have broken my heart into two...'' Aimz was trying to stuff a case of CDs into the bag, which wasn't exactly working out... ''I don't lie...there's one 'wholesome' thing about me, one and only...I don't have the capacity to backstab like that. It definitely came at a cost...'' Her fingers lightly grazed the bandage square in the center of her usually unmarred forehad. ''...but I made Hostyle's filthy ass submit.'' ''Someone deserves an Aimzy treat...'' Somehow, someway, RAH looked awfully mischievious... ''Jeez, haven't heard that since I had the Class Title in PWA.....'' Amy paused a moment, still trying to decipher what in God's name her deranged boyfriend was saying. ''......umm...what, exactly, IS an 'Aimzy Treat'? Believe it or not, but not all of us understand the words coming from behind that mask...'' ''Weeeeell...it used to mean a piece of candy...but these days my cherry Jolly Rancher haired lover...it's a completely new game...'' Aimz was, once again confused by him untill RAH wrapped arms around her and, with loving affection, he pressed his lips to hers and closed his eyes...an action that used to be a complete mystery to Rent-A-Hero now being second nature when he is with his Amy, who gave into his kiss as her arms wrapped around his neck. She just stood there another moment, gazing at Rent with quirky wonder. ''...I never much liked candy anyway.'' For the first time in a long time, Amy actually giggled...a foreign sound compared to her usual cursing and shouting. ''So you're coming back to Canada with me, eh?'' Not being 'informed' of all the latest patriotic jokes, RAH didn't quite pick up on the 'eh?'... ''Well...you seem to have been mentioning it a lot lately. My Rent-A-Senses tell me it seems like you want me to meet your precreators or something.'' ''Yeah...in the real world, we call them parents...'' She chuckled a bit. ''...but it's all good. My dad's just found out he's got cancer, I kinda wanna go back and just let everyone know I'm still around. You getting to meet them is definitely a plus...but.....uhh....just for my dad.......can you act a little....ummmmm...............normal?'' ''Normal....'' Rent pondered a moment, then burst out in a fit of glee, ecstatic because he had assumed Amy was encouraging him to wear what she normally whines about. ''OH YOU MEAN WEARING MY GREEN LANTERN SUIT WITH THE LIGHT UP SYMBOL!! THAT JUST CRIES ''NORMAL'' MY SWEET!! I'll bring it!'' ''No......I meant something like NOT bringing any specialty suits...and taking this,'' She touched the red Flash replica mask lightly. ''...off...'' ''STOP!! SURELY YOU JEST...this mask IS me Amy...this mask is what I am...'' Campbell was a little taken aback at first...frightened that the hero was so consumed in what she considered to just be a gimmick. ''Do you honestly think that?'' ''Yes...yes I do. If I lost this, what would I be then Amy? The one thing I can't bare to be...human. Amy...you don't tug Superman's cape...you don't show yellow in front of the Green Lantern, and you DON'T unmask a superhero and that's that.'' She HAD to figure out a counter-point! ''Superman...Spider Man...Batman...they were ALL people outside of their alter egos. Rentie, people wear masks to hide who they really are, to play dress up...even Superman had to be human at some point.'' ''I don't even look like ''me''...I look liek the son of the man who made me...Amy, I'd rather live every day with this mask then see what's under it...because what's under it ISN'T me.'' ''I know you're afraid...'' Amy swallowed hard, trying to choose her words carefully. ''...but I didn't fall in love with a mask. I'm in love with what's behind that...your mind, body, soul...everything BUT the sugar coated shell.'' ''I can't promise you anything Amy...'' He sighed. ''I'd do anything for you...move mountains, slay villains, save worlds...but even this is a bit much.'' ''Rentie...nobody'll bother you for being yourself...you don't have to be scared. I mean, for Christ's sake...I don't know jack shit about you! Don't know your name, your parents, where you came from...I don't even have a ball park figure to tell me how old you are!'' ''IF I KNEW THEM I'D TELL YOU!! I'm just a test tube creation Amy! Or do you wanna meet the glass cylinder and chemicals that made me?!'' RAH's outburst caused the now meek and mild redhead to jolt back a bit...never having heard his voice raised in anything but joy. ''I'm sorry, I'm sorry.........but do you even know WHEN you were created? Honest to God, I'd give anything to even know a bit about the person behind that sheild you put up.'' ''All I know is that I was modeled after the son of the doctor who made me...I am his exact image and my original use was to be a killing machine. I was ''the failure'' because I turned out not to be a killer. Amy, I just don't have a past...what I'd kill to have had childhood ask me to be ''normal'' forgive me if I don't know what you mean my love...because I was never meant thoughts, memories of parents, ANYTHING remotely MINE from the past...but I don't get that luxury.'' ''Then start over...at least take the mask off for ME.'' ''.....'' ''Please? I just...'' She staggered. ''...I want to see what's underneath, I need to see who you are.'' ''Amy...I am Rent-A-Hero, and Rent-A-Hero is the face you see before you...not what you see underneath. You and your parents will accept that or maybe we are on two different intergalactic wavelengths.'' Alright, he seemed to be teetering towards a more negative side...so Amy changed her tune slightly, knowing she couldn't win without this ending up a war. ''Fine, so don't take it off now...but what about the future, huh? What if...we had...kids? I'm not going through life dating the latest comic book character replica suit.'' ''....when I take if off, it will be on my OWN terms.'' ...The girl just couldn't keep from arguing! ''Then kindly explain to me what those terms are!'' ''I don't know yet Amy..but when they are known, I shall explain them. I am in no rush to remove the sembelance of the one thing that is ME...not cloned, or genetically created.'' ''Look...what you are under that masks DOESN'T MATTER. One of the biggest things I love about you is the fact that you don't give a shit that everyone thinks you're a maniac...you just don't care what other people think! It's like, the only reason you're like that is BECAUSE of the mask...I'm only just realizing that and it's scaring the hell out of me.'' Aimz took a breathe, trying not to choke. ''I know you don't want to hear it, but you're only doing harm to yourself with that fucking mask on!'' ''Stop...stop..Amy...you've got to understand, when I wear this mask...I am ME, not a clone of Dr.Harkins dead son...not a failure...I am ME. The same ME that fell in love with you...the same ME that earned the respect of fans worldwide...the same ME that learned to love people...the same ME who made love to you...BUT, I'll make you a deal...it's you I trust...so you will see it.'' He lifted the spandex fabric slowly, turning so Amy and Amy alone could see the skin underneath. RAH looked sullen after a moment, noticing that Campbell hadn't said a word. ''Silence...that sure is a good indication of my face...'' ''No!!!! No no...I'm just...shocked. It's amazing to finally SEE who I'm spending the better part of my time with...'' ''Can I put it back on now...the air is irritating my skin...I'm used to that tight, constricting feeling.'' He smiled that same cheesy, million-dollar blinder she had seen so many times before...it was hard not to melt. ''Hehe, anytime you'd like.'' ''As for your parents...that's a Kryptonian star belt we'll atomize when we get there...agreed my love?'' ''Yup. Speaking of getting there...we've got a flight to catch if you're EVER gonna meet 'em!'' So the couple were headed to the airport...Amy was finally going home for a few days, and Rent-A-Hero was finally meeting the two people who created the woman he loves....HOPEFULLY all ends well but, knowing Amy's family, there'll be some interesting road blocks along the way.
