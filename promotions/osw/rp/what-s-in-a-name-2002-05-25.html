What's In A Name?

25 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05.25.2002 How did it start?, Well I don't know, I just feel the craving, I see the flesh and it smells fresh, And it's just there for the taking, These little girls, They make me feel so damn exhilarated, I fill them up, I can't give it up, To me, I'm just erasing, I don't know why I'm so fucking cold, I don't know why its hurts me, All I want to do is get with you, And make the pain go away, Why do I have a conscience?, All it does is fuck with me, Why do I have this torment?, All I want to do is fuck it away, I tell my lies, and I despise, Every second I'm with you, So I run away, And you still stay, So what the fuck is with you? - “Trash” by Korn ][The gentle pitter patter of the water outside can still be heard inside the room as steam emerges from another, clouding visions and distilling images. In a way, the steam is just like another obstacle which won’t allow you to see what is really there. You find those traits in every civilization. Those traits which show you one thing but do another. When the boy who grew up only with a mother receives a call from his estranged father who tells the child that he is on his way up to see him. Naturally the boy gets excited, but the mother knows all along that there will be no playing of the ball, no hugging exchange, no smile on her son’s face. Yet the bitch keeps it all in, praying in the back of her mind that he will show his face and she won’t have to break down inside of herself simply because she knew all along. The father never arrived and the mother later shot herself in the head. Both were in the wrong. You see, the father said he would come to be a father to that boy, but rest assure that the bastard never came. And the mother was worse. When the father left, she made a promise of sacrifice to that young boy, taking him in fatherless and being a mother. Simply because she had a burden on her and knew something which the boy should, but didn’t tell him, she blew her brains out. She has now set this boy up for a life of thievery and disgust. It is their fault when that boy, let down all his life, gets locked up for the murders which he will later commit simply because he wants to take out his pain on others. Why you ask? Because they said one thing, but did another. Our subject, however, never had that problem. In fact, he thought he has the perfect family. The expenses were paid. The dinners were cooked. The clothes were washed. A nine year old boy could do no better. But the night when it all came down, our subject vowed to never say one thing and do another. If he said that he would make an impact on the world … he would. If he said that he would inflict his pain on anyone that crosses him … he would. If he said that he was perfect … he would be. Standing in the bathroom of his hotel room, a towel around his waist becoming clearer by the minute as steam pours out through the open door, Kory Storm realizes exactly what he would do. He speaks to himself.][ “A mirror is used for superficial reasons and provides one with a reflection. But I see something more than just me.” ][Seemingly falling into some sort of memorization, Kory Storm gazes at the glass, seeing himself? Or something deeper?][ “I see every last person who has ever doubted me …laughing at me… …mocking me… and I now know why. You see, coming into OSW I was told to have a lot of baggage. Addiction. Sadistic thoughts. A tortured past. One would think that I would be feared!” ][He laughs to himself.][ “Instead I was the laughingstock. The jobber. And I loved it. Because the more you idiots fuel me with your hatred, the more my hatred comes out for all of you through action.” ][Thinking about his statement, ‘Perfection Personified’ smirks, letting us all know of his amusement for hatred and pain. Not his own. But others.][ “I’m not dumb and you all know that I can string two words together … but I prefer action. Why? Because I can show you all by inflicting my pain just what baggage you all really care about. It couldn’t be my addiction, because the world embraces the addict, they are charity cases. And I know that in the world I must have a kindred spirit, one as sadistic as me, so it couldn’t be that facet of myself that you all despise. My past? Ha! I already know the answer to that one. You peons could give a fuck about my past as long as it doesn’t affect your American dreams of white picket fences and apple pie. Selfishness. So what is it, besides the fact that I hate you, do you hate about me?” ][Pondering quickly, Kory Storm answers himself.][ “My name maybe?” ][Of course! Think of where our subject competes! Old School Wrestling! He claims to be perfection, yet the name Storm in almost anywhere is synonymous with defeat! Especially in this Primetime Championship Wrestling and Old School Wrestling circle, where the average wrestling mark was even been made sick by the infestation of untalented so called athletes, who carried the last name Storm. But think of who we see now. It’s not Mike, AJ, or any other of those disgraces. It’s Kory fucking Storm! He could care less about his name as long as he gets to hurt people, and he could most certainly care less about what the fans and the critics think about it. After all, he was the one who made IOW worth watching! He was the one who eliminated a whole stable with just a lead pipe! He was the only credible XWA Television Champion! He was, and still is, an icon.][ “So you all shudder simply because of a name which I have no control over. I mean, would you people have liked it if I kept my original surname of Rutherford?! ’Perfection Personified’ Kory Rutherford?! No. You would have still mocked! And still jeered. And still drive me over the edge. So I now have a new goal. This is bigger than Enforcer. This is bigger than Doug Troy. This is bigger than OSW! This is me!” ][Shocked at his own anger on the subject, Kory begins to breathe heavily, his cocky smirk once again emerging on his face.][ “You’ll still see the conceit. You’ll still see the flashy. But you’ll now see the rugged. The violent! The evil! The people will now know exactly what type of baggage myself and my name carries. And when it is all said and done, the OSW and it’s ignoramuses for fans won’t be speaking about my jobber status or my latest loss. They will instead be in awe of the bloodletting and pain I cause. Then we’ll see what Storm is synonymous with. And what is the motive for this rampage? Because I need to prove once again just why I am the icon … the superstar … the paragon! Why I am ’Perfection Personified’ Kory Storm!” ][Letting his fist fly into the mirror, the angry reflection of Kory Storm is cracked into pieces. The only souvenir of his madness is the blood which drips from his right fist, shards of glass embedded in his flesh. We hear not one whine. Not one regret. Just the whisper of laughter which seems to show the world how serious Kory Storm is about what he is doing. He refuses to fall. He refuses to fail. He refuses to let the baggage drag him down. And when it is all through and the wreckage has been caused, we’ll be the ones regretting.][
