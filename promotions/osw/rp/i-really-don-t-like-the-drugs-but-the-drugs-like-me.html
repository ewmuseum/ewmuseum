I REALLY don't like the drugs...but the drugs like me?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April (Here's a cheap little thing to elaborate on a possible change...) A pill to make you numb A pill to make you dumb A pill to make you anybody else But all the drugs in this world Won't save her from herself -Marilyn Manson, ''Coma White'' Have you ever had one of those mornings where you wake up and wish you hadn't? It's not just 'One of those days'...it's when you take a look in the mirror and want to spit in your own face, when you're so fed up with everything, with the stupid routines... Amy had finally come home, she'd been greeted warmly by her parents...general chit-chat commenced, RAH went off with her father for a 'Man-to-man' talk that, somehow, Rentie came out of alive...what did that leave? The night, Aimz was going to catch up on some sleep on the Love-seat (Mini couch) of her parents' living room, while RAH took post on the couch, as ordered by Amy's mom and dad, who were against two un-married people sleeping in the same bed under their roof. Campbell had been messed up all day, however...from the moment she woke up on the plane... Friday would have been a great day, had it not been for her mental state. She, RAH and Bre were picked up at the Halifax International Airport by close friend Renee Theriau, who had been pretty heavily stoned...causing Bre to take the wheel for most of the ride, by command of Amy herself. What was wrong? It was stated before...she was sick of everything. Sure, partying, drugs and alcohol had made her time at Horton District High School fun and social, but she was out of school now...into the real world, a place where these things only hindered progress... What was the point? That had run through her head so many times...what was the real point to tripping out on some crazy pill every day, or getting so drunk you spend the next day throwing up three or four times a week? Every action has a reaction...and the partying was starting on it's reaction to Amy, wearing VERY thin. Occasionally dabbling with a drug? Maybe. Drinking two or three nights in a month with some friends? Okay. But doing these things night in, night out wouldn't get her anywhere...it was just routine now, and routines weren't her thing. It gets boring after a while, really...you experience everything once, twice, three times.......but once, twice, three times each week wasn't cool at all, it was just meaningless repetition out of habit. But...........was she through? Only time would tell.
