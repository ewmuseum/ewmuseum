Stepping out of the darkness...

2 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 2, 2002 'Oh hell' he breathes. 'Now what?' Normally, he was a man who was acutely aware of his surroundings. He prided himself on being in control of every situation. So it's not surprising that he was a bit upset to suddenly find himself in the dark, squinting against a harsh white light. As he tries in vain to look around against the glare, he desperately searches his thoughts, trying to remember where he had been, and what had led him here. Finding no answers prompted only more questions. More questions left unanswered only made him more frustrated. Frustration led to anger. Anger led to fear. Fear led to hate. Hate led to the dark side. Snickering at the wanderings of his own mind, Kanaida tensed his muscles. After all, he surmised, with his past if he found himself in a situation where he didn't know where he was and how he had gotten there, it was time to get ready to defend himself. And as regular as clockwork, as soon as he prepared for trouble, it found him. A pair of hands grasped his arms, trying to entangle him. Kanaida's reaction was as instant as it was violent. He pulled his unseen opponent to himself, hammer locked an arm, and spun them around. As he applied pressure to the arm, he leaned in to growl a question where he was guessing an ear to be. It was hard to tell, what with the incredibly annoying light still shining. As he opened his mouth to begin asking his questions, something hit him, mashing his lips against his teeth. As his head rocked back, he saw.. dark? In surprise, he forgot himself, and released his grip on the offending arm. As his vision cleared, he instantly recognized his environment. And why not? Before his erstwhile retirement over six months ago, he'd made many a wrestling ring into his personal stomping grounds. In many cases, literally. For three years, he'd met the best that two federations had to offer, and sent them packing. There had never been a title that he had vied for that had been denied to him. Never an opponent that he could not humble in time. But his meteoric rise to the top was derailed by taking responsibility. He had assumed first control, then ownership of the federation in which he'd made his debut. And, not wanting to book himself to the top, he'd ended his own rise, and instead worried about the fed itself. But the box office and the fans demanded his presence in the ring.. it was too much for one man to take, doing it all by himself. So he'd given up running the federation, and tried to make a go of it as a wrestler again. But the cruel hand of fate intervened, and he was forced to retire. His subsequent comeback at the beck and call of the federation's most evil man completely soured the fans on him, and when he was soon left to his own devices again.. he chose retirement, rather than fighting an uphill battle against the ire of the fans. Lost in thought, The Northern Wonder fell victim to an arm drag, and found himself spinning to the canvas. He bounced up, and glared at his opponent. He tried to see who it was, but they were practically faceless.. his features seemed to change moment to moment. They circle each other in the ring, and lock up. As he forces his opponent back into the corner, Kanaida leans in close, and opens his mouth to ask what's going on here. What happens when his mouth is open, however, comes as a complete surprise to him. 'Every single night, the same arrangement' he sings in a pleasant baritone, 'I go out and fight the fight...' Kanaida whips his opponent out of the corner, and into the ropes. As he does so, he shakes his head.. what is going on here, and why is he singing.. for that matter, why is he STILL singing even as he's questioning singing in the first place?!? But then the faceless opponent comes barreling off the ropes, and the time for introspection is over. 'Still I always feel the same estrangement, nothing here is real' He pauses for a beat as he arm drags his opponent down. 'Nothing here is right.' Truer words were never spoken.. um.. sung, he thinks to himself as he gets up.... still singing his heart out. 'I've been making shows of trading blows just hoping no one knows, that I've been going through the motions, sleep walking in the ring..' He reaches down, and drags the other man up by his long/short (who bloody well knows, it's constantly changing shape and color) hair. 'Nothing I do can seem to change a..' Kanaida pauses long enough to unleash a chop that echoes in the air. ' thiiiiiing... ' Out of nowhere, a large number of voices yell 'Whoooo'. And with each subsequent chop he delivers, an unseen chorus chime in rhythm to his singing with a 'Whooo'. The Northern Wonder ignores this as he drives his opponent into the corner.. the 'whoo' after a chop is about the only normal thing he's experienced since this all started. 'I was always brave, and kinda righteous' he sings anew, and mounts the second turnbuckle. 'Now I find I'm wavering..' He lays in the punches, and hears the unseen fans counting along. 'The fans turn on you' He sings as he looks up and out towards the crowd 'you find this fight just don't mean a thing...' His punches slow, and stop as he sees row upon row of empty seats. out of the corners of his eyes, he can see a packed house, with fans bearing signs that one moment are in his favor, and another are damning him to the pits of hell.. but as soon as he turns his head to look directly, the seats are empty once more. Once again, with The Northern Wonder distracted, his opponent strikes. Kanaida is picked up, trundled into the center of the ring, and spinebustered. He gasps as he impacts the mat. The surreality of the situation is driven home to Kanaida as the faceless opponent stands over him, and sings.. 'He ain't got that swing..' before leaping into the air to hit a legdrop. He misses, as Kanaida kips up to his feet. 'Thanks for noticing' he sings in reply.. then blinks in confusion as the other wrestler is now gone. he turns around, and backs up a step or two.. the situation has gotten both more dangerous and quite a bit stranger. The formerly faceless stranger has now become three opponents, each of which bear a vague resemblance to the greatest opponents of Kanaida's past. There's a slim, muscular female.. a grotesquely fat hillbilly.. and a muscular powerhouse holding a chair. Then he noticed another wrestler tangled in the ropes, helpless. He suppressed a grin as he saw that one, who looked like a member of a boy band, with flowing blonde hair, dressed in what looks to be a cheap imitation of Kanaida's own ring gear. Reminded him of someone, somehow. But before he could give it much thought, the now dancing trio had begun to sing again. 'He does pretty well with jobbers' they sang, swirling through a slow balletic spin 'But hell, lately we can tell..' As they turn their backs again in another slow twirl, Kanaida strikes. He bends the woman over backwards, and drops her to the mat with a vicious Northern Lights Inverted DDT. This only takes a split second, and the other two sing on oblivious to their companion's fate. 'That he's just going through the motions..' they sing as Kanaida gets to his feet, and slips behind the long haired freak with the chair. The black-clad man manages to keep singing even as Kanaida presses him into the air. 'Facing it somehow..' With a crunch, Kanaida DDT's the chairman out of midair. He snatches the chair up, and brains the fat bearded hillbilly as he sings on. 'He's not even half the man he...' The mountain man pauses, and touches a hand to the blood pouring out of the hole the chair had put in his skull. He looks at the blood, and then sings almost questioningly as he pitches over.. 'Ow?' Kanaida moves over to the ropes, still holding the chair, and still singing, much to his chagrin. 'Will I stay this way, forever?' He belts out as he uses his free hand to untangle the blonde girly-man from the ropes. 'Sleepwalk through my life's endeavor?' The pretty-boy extends a hand, and sings to Kanaida.. 'How can I repaaayyyy' he's cut off by the smack of a steel chair against his face, sending him spinning over the top rope and to the floor. 'Whatever..' Kanaida sings, dropping the now ruined chair. 'I don't want to beeee' He holds the note, and turns in a circle with his arms flung wide. Even as he belts his lungs out to the ceiling and spins like Julie Andrews, he can't stop himself. 'Going through the motions,' he sings as he climbs to the second turnbuckle in the nearest corner. 'Working without a plan,' 'I can't even see... if this is really me... and I just want to beeeeeee' He reaches the top turnbuckle, and as he releases the last note of the verse, falls backwards in a nestea plunge towards the center of the ring. 'World Chaaaaamp..... ' He holds this note for an impossibly long time as he floats to the mat in super slow motion. And as he impacts the mat.... he is once again plunged into total darkness. He sits up, and feels something tangled around his arms and chest.. on a hunch, he reaches out to his right, and flips on a light switch. And, of course, finds himself sitting in bed. With a sigh, Kanaida untangles himself from the sheets, and swings his feet over the edge of the bed. He sits there for a moment, his head cradled in his scarred hands. 'I'm getting sick of these damn dreams.' he mutters to himself. 'Every single night, it's something new. Reliving the Buffy musical episode is only the final insult.' A short, harsh laugh explodes from his lungs. He was getting sick of this. Each dream was different, but they always came back to the same thing.. wrestling. It was ironic, really. He would have thought that his life before wrestling would be the stuff of nightmares. But somehow it was the wrestling was staying in his system like a barfly after closing time. With a shake of his head, he gets up, and heads out into the hallway. He passes the kitchen, and goes straight through the living room of the three-story converted Toronto wharehouse that he's made into his personal domain. He walks past the door to the always-vacant guest room, and stops at the last door in the short hallway. He punches in a security code on a number pad, and when the red light flashes green, opens the door. As he enters, the room lights automatically came on, a dim background light. The true lighting was provided by three large spotlights, and a multitude of smaller spots, each shining off a title belt. The walls of the mid-sized room were covered with title belts that fairly well glowed under the blaze of the spotlights, and bathed the room in a golden glow. Kanaida walks around the room, casting an appraising eye on the assorted titles, and the pictures that went with them. His first title defense, the UWA Tag titles. His first actual title victory, the UWA World Title. The pictures of the cage incident that made him famous. His Universal Title win in EDW. Pictures with the rest of Highly Unstable, in all it's various forms. And in a darker corner of the room, shots of steel chairs, black masks, and blacker days. Frowning, Kanaida turns away from the evidence of his downfall, and almost runs into the centerpiece of the room. The glass case was lit from within, above, and all four sides, giving it's contents an almost unholy glow. Inside was the UWA world title that Kanaida had won by defeating Jean Paul LeBlanc. His first world title, and the original UWA belt, before it had been replaced for a prettier one. It was slightly battered.. in the early days, the UWA had not been able to afford replica belts, or even repairs. The leather of the strap was stained with the blood and sweat of the men who had worn it.. fought over it. As title belts went, it looked a bit dingy. It'd been the first thing Kanaida had replaced when he'd taken over the federation. But it held a special place in his heart.. not only as his first world title, but as an object that had helped pull together his broken heart and soul. His early UWA days were dark days indeed for the Northern Wonder... this title had helped him move on, in part, from the horror of his past. Staring at the belt, he came to a decision. Leaving the room behind, he walks back to his bedroom, and starts rooting through the papers on his bedside table. After a brief search, he finds what he is looking for.. a business card serving as a bookmark in the Stephen King book he was reading before bedtime. Stuffing another paper in to hold his place, he stares at the card for a long time. Finally reaching a decision, he reaches out to his phone, dials the number, and puts it on speaker. The phone rings twice, then a voice speaks. 'You have reached the offices of Old School Wrestling..' Kanaida rolls his eyes. He hates recordings. 'C'mon, get the the damn beep already... about damn time. Ed? It's Kanaida. I know that I said no to joining a fed again... but you know what? I need a change. Fax me the contract, and I'll get it back to you. What I'm trying to say is.. I'm in. I know it's not much notice, but what can I say? I'm interested, and you said that the offer was open. Anyway, fax me that contract.' He reaches out, and hangs up the phone. With a shake of his head, he turns out his light, and crosses to his window. Pulling back the drapes, he stares out into the night, and ponders the irony of his life.. when he sleeps, he has nightmares about a period that was relatively happy for him. When he's awake? That's when things are bad. Because the memories of his life, before wrestling, are worse than any nightmare could possibly be.
