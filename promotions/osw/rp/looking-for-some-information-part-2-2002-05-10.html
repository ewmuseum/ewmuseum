Looking for some information (Part 2)

10 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/10/02 Tom Walczak has been vigorously gathering up information about Mexico, and its people for the past couple of days. The reason behind it is that he knows little about El Drago, and he thinks that knowing something about his people will give him and Krieg an added advantage in the match the have against him this Saturday. As the scene now opens up Walczak is seen in his home in Copiague, Long Island. His head is rested on a desk on top of a stack of books about Mexican history as well as the history of Lucha Libre. Just then he hears something outside that suddenly awakens him. He tries to think of what the sound could possibly be. It is something mechanical, and sounds as if it is cutting something. He figures that someone is mowing their lawn, so he goes over to see which one of his moronic neighbors it is. As he comes to the window he sees that it is not one of his neighbors, but a landscaping company. Now, in Long Island most of the landscaping, mostly cutting off grass and shrubs, is done by people of Latin descent. This is just a job that draws to them, much like Arabs, and Hindus working in your Local 7-11. Walczak realizing that first hand knowledge is the best type of knowledge decides to go over to see what type of information he can get from the men. Although he had done this recently he hadn’t gotten quite the response that he had hoped for. Sure, he learned something, but not the info that he needed. He puts his robe on as well as his slippers and makes his way to the men. He approaches the man that appears to be directing every thing. “Excuse me sir? Could I trouble you with a couple of questions?” “What do you want? You want lawn done? $50 for front and $50 for back. You not find more better rates.” “I can see that but I do not want you to do my lawn I just want to find out more about your culture is that ok?” “Yeah sure what you want?” “Well, first off I was wondering if you could tell me any specifics about the lucha libre from Mexico?” “Well, I can not, Carlos can though. CARLOS!!!” The man calls over one of his employees, as Walczak awaits some information. A young boy comes over no more than twenty years old, and it is obvious that he is the youngest one of the working group. “Carlos, this man wants to know some information about lucha libre. Do you think that you can tell him what he wants to know?” “Man, if I can’t tell him than no one can.” “Ok, well it is not the whole sport that I really want to know about, but it is really just one wrestler in particular. He goes by the name of El Drago, and I know a little about him, but…” “Drago, man he is the greatest ever. He dominated everyone, and he could counter to anything. If he fought a lightweight he would use his strength, but if he went up against someone bigger than he would use his speed.” “Yes, but he did face smaller competitors in general right?” “Yeah, but it don’t make a difference he beat everyone’s ass you know what I mean man.” “Well, he is in OSW now, and…” “Yeah I heard about that shit. That must be crazy. Those sissy ass bitches are in for a shock because Drago is going to reek havoc.” “Well, it just so happens that I have a match against him, and I was wondering if you would be able to give me any pointers.” “You? He is going to beat your ass man. He is going to walk all over you.” “Is that so?” “You are damn straight.” “Well, for your information I think that El Fago is going to be in for a rude awakening when he faces me.” “Yeah I don’t think so.” “You are a punk bitch Mexican so it doesn’t matter what you think.” Walczak starts to chase after the man, but stops as one of his slippers falls off. He gets it back on, and then realizes that he should not waste his energy on such a worthless cause. He makes his way over to the house as the scene fades out…
