Book 1 -- Sportscenter Sunday Conversation

10 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 10th 2002 ESPN: Special Edition of Sunday Conversation Stewart Scott: Hello everyone and welcome to this special edition of Sunday Conversation. Today we're going to have on of the biggest names in wrestling, Graphic Violence. Certainly this man is no stranger to controversy as it has followed him his entire life. Welcome to the show. Graphic Violence: Yeah, no problem. SS: Now you've been in the business for only about four years now. You have risen to the top faster than any other superstar in the world. You've been in all the big dogs yard. Why do you think you rose to the top so fast? GV: Why do I think that? Why do YOU think I rose to the top so fast. C'mon mister sports-caster...this should be an easy question to answer. It's simple. I'm the [bleep]ing best in the business. I'm the best wrestler there is, I'm the biggest draw there is and the list goes on and on. Everyone wants Graphic Violence. SS: No doubt dog. I feel ya. Now, your career has been surrounded by controversy. There's the infamous Asylum happenings, then your sudden departure at jOlt. Also the controversy about your fWo Survivor II entrance. Care to explain a few of these things for us? GV: Asylum forced me out. The newbies that came to the fed didn't like me too much. I was the main man, I was the champion. But tA used me to get noticed then spit me out. That promotion was nothing without the name Graphic Violence on the roster. When that fed was still in production and they were trying to hype up their debut...no one was listening. But once they heard I signed, it was a different story. Fact is I made that fed what it is today, but it's clear to see that they're struggling. I mean, when you have to resort to gay ass rapping storylines...I think it's safe to say you're in a hole. But then again, I saw alot of that ass shit going on backstage with Borst and Joe...so it was only a matter of time. SS: BOO-YEAH! GV: Right. Anyway the story with jOlt is all about money. I was the top 'dog' in their promotion but yet I was working for free. Seven months I worked without getting paid. I was supposed to have a contract agreement and a meeting to discuss financial issues...but it kept getting pushed back. So basically I said [bleep] it and walked out. If they wanted to give me the money I was asking then I would of stayed. And I wouldn't have asked for as much if they didn't [bleep] me over for seven months. Well can all see what happened to jOlt after I left. That's right, it crumbled. SS: BOO-YEAH! GV: Uh...and the fWo. Well it's clear that no one wanted me on fWo Survivor II. They thought I was too big a name already and didn't need the Survivor II contest to boost my career. Everyone expected me to voted off in the first round. But I made it to the merger and pissed everyone off. I got a phone call from the original creator of the promotion after the contest. He wanted me to come to the fWo, but there were some things that needed to be ironed out. I waited six months for a yes or no answer, but never got it. When I confronted the person who was running the promotion at that time, we bumped heads. Needless to say he said I could of been there if I was just patient, but I [bleep]ed my chance. SS: BOO- GV: YEAH! God damnit Scott. Get a new [bleep]ing line. Everytime I watch sportscenter is always 'boo-yeah' over and over. You're an idiot. Anyway, let's run down what we've learned just now. Asylum is suffering with good storylines after I left, jOlt closed down after I left, and the fWo closed down. The fWo would be open right now if they just signed me. People would be attending shows and that promotion would be off the hook. SS: No kid, it'd be straight butta...cause it'd be on a roll. GV: Uh...right. SS: Now, with that out of the way you signed with OSW. You made it clear you don't want women in wrestling, you wanted to take on the Red Army, you feuded with Rhett Locke and you're going to wrestle Jim Axtell this week in a bathroom match. Care to comment on these topics. GV: Women don't belong in a [bleep]ing wrestling ring. Perfect example would be Aimz. If that bitch could take care of herself she wouldn't be getting her ass handed to her by Corg. Or her ass probed by his 'bulging member'. The Russian Bore and his Army of Retards were a cancer to the promotion. No one wanted to see them, but yet no one would stand up to them. So I gladly said I'd take on the entire Red Army in a gauntlet match. [Bleep]ing [bleep] backed out. Now Ivan is playing up to the crowd and ditched the Red Army. No with no one to back him up...we'll see how good he does. As far as Rhett Locke is concerned, the man has earned my respect. I mean anyone that allows themselves to get beat up for three months and doesn't run and hide like a sissy girl...earns a little of GV's respect. And I thought that it would be cool of me to lend him a helping hand this past week on Slam!. SS: You helped him with the OSW World Title. GV: Wow Scott. You must watch our program. SS: I'm as cool as the other side of the pillow. GV: Rhett deserves that title. After everything I put him through...it's the least I could do. As far as Jim Axtell is concerned, that guys doesn't belong in my ring...or anywhere near a wrestling arena. The punk is a security guard who's trying to make a name for himself. He couldn't cut it as a security guard so I don't know what the [bleep] he's thinking trying to be a wrestler. So last week on PTC Radio Axtell was on the air sounding like the giant retard that he is, and I called in just to say hello. I told him he couldn't cut it as a security guard or a wrestler. He should be wearing a janitors outfit and cleaning my [bleep] stains off the toilet bowl. This Saturday Jim is going to be a world of hurt. He's biting off more than he can chew by accepting this challenge. Guess he doesn't know that I used to be in the IAV, and a bathroom match is right up my ally. SS: International Association of Violence. Man, there are some extreme matches up in that hizzie. Fo shizzle my nizzle. BOO-YEAH! GV: You're starting to run out of material Scott. Personally you're starting to annoy me and before I rearrange your face for you I'm gonna get the [bleep] out of here. By the way, Kenny Mayne is alot funnier than you. Rich Eisen is even better. You're an annoy [bleep]. SS: You da man dog. Thanks for being on the show. Coming up next on Sportscenter, the plays of the week. BOO-YEAH!
