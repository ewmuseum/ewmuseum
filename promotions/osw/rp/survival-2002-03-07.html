Survival

7 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03-07-02 Let the music load and read on in time to the music --------------------------- “Have you ever watched someone die before your very eyes?” The cold, raspy voice sent a shiver down his spine. In the pale light of the full moon, they wandered through the dark wood, moving in silence. The fog rose from the earth, enveloping them as though to consume them. Surrounded by the beauty of nature, they continued, careless of that which surrounded them, treading through the luscious green grass, the dewdrops moist against their feet as they moved. “We are all victims. No one entity is without a predator. The key to life is survival. Throughout the ages man and beast alike have predated on one another, only the strongest survive Hessian, only the strongest. I have survived as I have because I am a hunter, never the hunted, but that doesn’t change the fact you and I are both victims, as is everything else you see before you.” A field of blood met them as they left the dark embrace of the wood, and stepped out into the full moon, shining down onto the land ripe with death before them. The screams of many filled their ears, though they were alone. The haunting nightmares plagued them, yet they pushed on, as content as though the very horrors that stalked them floated as gently as the nightly breeze gracing them. “Look before you…a canvas of life painted by the victimized and relished by the very evils we have committed to. We often loathe that which we victimize Hessian, this is what makes survival worth living for.” And then the strong aroma filled their lungs. Trembling slightly they advanced through the field, and into a dark clearing. The sweet stench of death burdened them like an itch, though they welcomed it, and came to a halt before a great redwood. Glancing up, their eyes met with the empty gaze of the very subject Vampir had preached to Hessian. A victim. Hessian stared longingly into the glazed eyes as the last breath of life escaped the crucified corpse, running his hand along the many stab wounds gracing the torso, as Vampir pulled something from behinds the corpse and draped it over its lifeless skull. With a simple nod they turned and left, leaving the bloody corpse crucified to the redwood, their sadistic laughter ringing throughout the night, as the bright moonlight shone onto the cloth draped over the corpses’ skull, the words ‘Shut Up And Wrestle’ embroidered on the shirt gleaming as though with pride.
