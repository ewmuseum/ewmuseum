Best Night EVER!

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Ouch ouch ouch ouch ouch ouch ouch!!!!!” screams Seth Masters as we open with a close up shot of his face. doting As we pull out we can see that he’s sitting in a hospital room. It’s what you’d expect of a hospital room. You know, if you’ve seen one, you’ve seen them all. A few chairs off to the side, a bed in the middle, a table to the side of it, a TV screen in the corner of the room, you know the drill. If you’ve been keeping up with Civil Disobedience as of late, this scene will look rather familiar. That’s because it’s exactly the same scene you were met with last week shortly after OSW’s Saturday Slam. Well, almost exactly. This time Seth Masters isn’t exactly screaming about spicy chili. There’s actually a reason for his cries this time, as a bandage is being wound around his head to cover the laceration he received from Minion the Imp’s crutch to the head. Trevor Hawke, who has also been bandaged, is sitting on the other side of the room, watching Seth’s pitifulness with mild amusement. Soon the doctor finishes up wrapping Seth’s wound and exits the room, leaving Seth and Trevor alone. They’re still wearing their wrestling gear, though of course they never really take off their wrestling gear. When you wrestle in jean shorts and T-shirts, you usually don’t have to worry about changing clothes after the match. Unless they’re really drenched in sweat or something, but that’s beside the point. In the room the silence hangs heavy, as the two tag team competitors exchange looks with one another. Finally, Seth decides to break the calm by voicing his thoughts. “Yeah, so...uh...we sure end up in this hospital a lot, don’t we?” “I’ve noticed that too,” Trevor responds, then shrugs. “At least this time the reason we’re here is considerably less serious than last week.” “Speak for yourself, Mr. Knee Injury. Last week I didn’t have shit wrong with me. Now look at my head. My flawless beauty has been ruined! RUINED!” screams Seth, in a mockery of melodrama everywhere. “Quit bitching, Seth. You know it doesn’t hurt.” Trevor snaps. “I know,” Masters agrees, “but I’m hoping one of those hot nurses will take pity on me and nuzzle me up against her ample bosom.” Trevor shakes his head in pity for his weak-minded friend. “Seth...life isn’t like porno movies. Real nurses don’t do that.” The excited look disappears from Seth’s face, getting replaced by an expression of disappointment. “Damn it, Trev. You’re always such a buzz kill,” says Seth in a tone to match his facial expression. His tone now changes to a mocking pitch, as he...mocks Trevor, mockingly. “Oh, nurses don’t press their boobs into your face, urinating in the drinking fountain is against the law, Hessian and Azrael Cain are unbeatable. Well guess what, Trev? I beat them! What’s up now, bitch?” As Seth stands up in defiant pride, Trevor shakes his head once more; this time in a show of reluctant concedance of victory. “How did I know you’d bring that up eventually?” “Because,” states Seth with a grin, “I always like to rub your failures in your face?” “That’s it.” Trevor says, nodding, as he shakes his finger in contemplation. “So do you feel like embellishing on your failure rubbing?” “Oh, you know I do!” shouts Seth excitedly. “Man, didn’t I say all along that size doesn’t matter? Well I did, and I was fucking right. I kicked Cain’s tail all around that building. He was a bloody mess, ya know...he was wearing the crimson mask. I tore him another asshole and then I shoved his own head up the newly opened cavity. Self-sodomization, baby!!! Fuck yeah!” he finishes, pumping his fist in the air. “That’s not quite what happened...” Trevor starts, before Seth cuts him off. “Yeah, I know, but can’t you just let me delude myself into thinking it?” he states in a hopeful tone. Trevor thinks for a second. “Um...no.” The disappointed look returns to Seth’s face, as his head drops in shame. “Aw...” “For the sake of the reality being preserved, I think you need to be set straight,” Trevor says, as he walks over and pats Seth on the back out of pity. “You see, you didn’t beat him at all. We beat him with the Trendsetter. In fact, you were doing a nice job of getting your ass kicked until the Hell’s Angels hit the scene. It was only their arrival provided me the opportunity to interfere on your behalf. There’s no way you would have won without me and the Angels.” Seth looks up sadly. “I...well....you see...damn it, Trevor! You just took all the pride I had from winning that match and flushed it straight down the crapper. I hope you’re happy, asshole.” Seth turns away from Trevor in anger, while his partner just shrugs his shoulders and smiles. “Well see, that’s what you get from your excessive failure-rubbing toward me over the years. How do you like having your good mood squashed like a lowly ant under the foot of some bubbling clod? It’s not much fun, is it?” “You are so right. You’ve just opened my eyes, Trevor,” says Seth, turning back to his friend with a look of enlightenment in his eyes. “I can’t believe I’ve actually acted that way for so many years. I have seen the light, Trev. I have seen the light. “So you’re not going to put other people down any more?” Trevor asks hopefully. “No...no, I never said that.” denies Seth. “Just because I know something, doesn’t mean I’ll act on it. I mean, you think the Americans had to know that giving Rosie O’Donnell her own talk show was a bad thing, but did they do anything about it?” “Ah well, I wasn’t expecting you to change your ways anyway. There’s such a thing as expecting too much,” says Trevor, with a shrug. “At least we proved that Hessian and Cain are beatable, though. Now all we have to do is go ahead and do just that. Beat them next Saturday. Sounds...possible. Yeah.” “Yeah, not only possible, but...um, what’s the opposite of possible. Like you, know...when something is probably going to happen?” “Probable?” offers Trevor. “That’s it!” says Seth, nodding his head. “Probable.” An awkward silence fills the room. Nobody says anything until the door opens and in walks a nurse. This isn’t any old nurse, though. No, this one is wearing a uniform that cuts off on her upper thigh, and is showing off a lot of cleavage. Did I mention she’s wearing some slutty makeup? Cause she is. Anyway, she walks into the room and presses Seth’s face into her titties, rubbing it around. Muffled moans of pleasure come out from the mass of bosom, and when she pulls his head away a smile seems to be permanently plastered on Seth’s face. She then promptly arranges her top and leaves the room again, as Seth looks after her dumbly. He then turns to Trevor and says one simple sentence. “Best night EVER!” “Enjoy it while you can, Seth.” says Trevor. “Enjoy it while you can.”
