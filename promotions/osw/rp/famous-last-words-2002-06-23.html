Famous Last Words...

23 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23-06-02 The famous last words of a Heretic… Blood is Red, Violence is Black and Blue, I’m going to kill you…it’s true, it’s true! The day is finally upon us, Ascension is here, Time to face the Hessian, Your Nightmare, Your Fear, With Cain at my side, The Marked Beast will assist, In taking Civil D and Da Westsidaz, On a trip through the mist, Through the unclear to a place, Where suffering and disaster is, Our dreams, our desire, A place we call…Hell. What did you expect? Well? Tonight you’ll see as the Blood flows, Hessian and Cain, you’ll never stop, As the barbed steel sticks into you, Heresian B will come out on top, So stop fucking acting like you have a hope in the world and prepare to get fucked up worse than you have ever been, for tonight sees your deliverance into the forbidden, where we’ll skin you alive and watch you die slowly, all of you had better pray to whatever fucking god you have because once we step into that ring…all Hell will break loose and the world will see that what you have done is not only signed your death warrant…but pissed off your worst nightmare…sweet dreams fuckers. See you in Hell. Fin
