The Aftermath- Part 2

20 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/20 Introduction- We last saw Dame, in his apartment, simply having some heated discussion with his good friend Gloria, when the phone rang. Dame asked who it was repeatedly, then screamed the foul word 'Bullshit' .. He then slammed the phone, and stormed to the bathroom. Gloria was shocked by what just happened, and was even more shocked when Dame told her it was Hardy. Now, as day has passed, and Gloria is determined on Dame smiling. To let out a giggle. To get the big teddy bear that once sat inside him, awake again! And that all begins today...At an amusement park. The Aftermath- Part 2 Michael Dame and Gloria roll up into a huge parking lot. It seems there are more cars then people in the park, and on that note, not a space open! 'Where the hell do you expect to park..I told you this was a bad idea!' He says frustrated. 'Ohh... come on Michael. They have those capacity laws. There has to be a space for us, or else they're breaking the law!..make a right here!' Gloria finishes saying as she points to the right. Dame turns. 'Right there, I told you...there's the space....ohhh, quick get it before those other people..' She frantically says as it may be hours before they see another space. Dame goes to pull in, but is too slow, the car full of children whips in, and the driver jams on the brakes! 'God damn..' Dame says, but is cut off by Gloria. 'Don't worry there will be another...' Gloria is not cut off. 'No, I mean, how can a parent pull into a space like that with a carload of kids! What a asshole!' Dame says as he drops his head, quickly to pick it up to see the other driver who beat them fore the spot, flailing his hands in the air! You can hear a low whisper from inside the brand new Cadillac SLT Dame is driving. (low whisper)'You fucking asshole..How can you go after a spot like that when you see I have children in the car?!?' He says as he turns to grab his younger child from running into the road. Inside the car, Dame clenches the steering wheel extremly tight. He looks over at Gloria, with a look in his eye, that you only see from Dame inside the ring. 'No, Michael Dont!' Gloria says, but is too slow as Dame has already exitted the vehicle. 'How can I do what?!?' He says as he stops less then 3 feet from the father. 'hey...Dad, that's Michael Dame!...Can I get your autograph Mr. Dame?' The boy asks as he realizes it's Michael Dame from OSW. 'No...Son, this man is a....' The father is cut off by a deep stare from Dame, as well as his voice. 'Sure, kid. You can have my autograph...Here. I'll give you my shirt, if you have a pen, I'll sign it.' He says as he removes his shirt, to reveal his bulging muscles. 'Actually Mr. Dame, I do have this brand new marker, I saved up allll my money just to bring it today. I want to get everyone's autograph inside...you know, all the characters of the park. But, I'll let you use it too, since you want to sign it...Don't you Mr. Dame?' 'Yeah, I'll sign. anything for you kid. What's your name.?' Dame asks with a smile in his face, quickly to be changed with a stern face towards the father who stands and watches. 'My name is Jonny. Jonny Walthrop.' The young boy answers. Dame quickly signs the Tshirt, and hands it over to the kid. 'Thanks Mr. Dame. I really love this. I'm going to where it right now.' The kid exclaims as he puts the Tshirt on.. It's about 5....or 6 sizes too big. 'Hey Dad, do you think I'll get on all the big rides now..now that I have this huge shirt on.. They must think I'm pretty big.. And pretty tough...arrrrrrr' The kid flexes, as both Michael and the father laugh. Gloria laughs as well in the background of things. 'Well, we gotta go now.' the kid states as his dad shakes the hand of Michael. He mumbles thanks, as the family walks off. Michael stops, looks at the park , and turns towards the car. He enters his side, as well as Gloria. 'Well, that was rather...odd. You wern't really going to beat him, were you?......' No reply from Dame. 'Were you? Michael..?' 'No, of coarse not. I was going to explain to him how he's an utter asshole, but since the kid showed up, I quickly changed my plan. And speaking of planns changing..' Dame stops talking, and leaves Gloria with a confused feeling.. 'Huh?.....ahhhhh...' Gloria can't even ask what Dame meant, as Dame is quick to floor the gas towards the exit. 'What do you think you're doing? Acting like that asshole father?' Gloria Exasperates. 'No, I checked all directions for cars before flooring it. Simple enough, yet so hard for such assholes like that guy.' 'Well, where do you think you're going?' Gloria asks, still confused. 'I'm leaving' Michael Replies. 'Huh? What?' Gloria's confusion grows. 'Because inside that park, is thosands of fathers, just like the one we just encountered. And not everyone of them will have a kid who wants my autograph to calm my temper...Therefore, I'm leaving.' Dame says with a sophisticated sound to his tone. 'But I thought that's why we came to the amusement park, to get away from all of that? We can easily inside. Where there is no fighting over parking spaces.' gloria tries to explain. Dame slams on the breaks. 'Listen, Gloria. You really dont know how I'm feeling right now. I feel, you're the only person I can trust, and you're always there for me, and I'm very thankful for that. But outside of that. I've been betrayed over and over, and over. As well as lied to, beaten as a result, hurt, backstabbed. I mean the list can go on and on, and on...' 'But, what about the fans. They forgave you once you told them the real you, and what really happened' Gloria explains in a modest voice. 'Yeah, but the fans aren't in the my thought right now. They're not feeling what I'm feeling, and to tell you the truth, it's an overwhelming feeling a man of regular mind and fortitude would be devistated by...' 'Are you implying something?' Gloria says smirkingly. 'Yeah, that I'm thankful for everything I am.' Dame states, while turning his head into Gloria. Nothing but honesty runs through his eyes as the camera fades to black.
