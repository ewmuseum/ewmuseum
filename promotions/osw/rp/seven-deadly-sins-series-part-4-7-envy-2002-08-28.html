'Seven Deadly Sins' series;Part 4/7; 'Envy'

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28th August 2002 Propaganda and Eric Sagar are on the open road in their white, rusty, old LDV van. Sailing down the highway when after a long spell of silence Propaganda breaks the ice with firmness. 'You're running out of gas.' Propaganda informs Eric with little care. 'So I am my brother. Thanks for informing me.' Eric's voice filled with kindness and sincerity. They pass a blue sign advertising a gas station that is near by. 'Pull over in the gas station, Brother Sagar.' Propaganda demands with a voice filled of boredom. Maybe he's a little excited that he can depart from the smelly, old, stuffy vehicle and be able to knock back a bottle of mineral water and stretch his legs. They pull into the quite gas station and as they park Propaganda quickly exits the car while Sagar puts on the hand brake. 'I'll get something to drink from the store.' Propaganda informs Eric as he proceeds to gill the car with the gas. Sagar acknowledges 'The Power Himself' with a nod. Propaganda walks into the store and heads directly for the refrigerator containing drinks. He searches through with much caution and thought. He finally selects a half litre bottle of 'Diamond Springs' mineral water. He strides over to the counter and is about to slam the purified water beverage on the counter when a boy in his late turns beet him too it. The boy is wearing all black, long black hair, black nail varnish, satanic jewellery and an all-round satanic theme. Propaganda looks at the boy up and down in disgust. 'That'll be $3.59 please.' The middle-aged lady in uniform tells the boy who is buying a small stubby of beer and a candy bar. 'Wait a second she says as the boy is about to hand her the money.' 'What?' the boy grunts insolently 'Are you the legal age to drink?' She asks obviously annoyed by his ignorant tone/ 'Yes bitch.' He address' her. Propaganda is horrified. Reluctantly the lady behind the counter gives the boy his receipt in exchange for the money. 'Excuse me young man…' Propaganda roars with an heir of politeness. 'Why don't you apologise to this young lady! For God is not forgiving on judgement day when he reviews your sins.' 'God… Fuck you churchy.' The boy sniggers but to his bad luck just before he said that 'The Revered' Eric Sagar waked into the store. 'WHAT DID YOU SAY BOY!!?' Sagar roars making the boy to jump out of his skin in shock. 'THAT SORT OF HEATHEN BLASPHEMY SHOULD NOT COME FROM A YOUNG GENTLEMAN'S MOUTH LIKE YOURS! WHO ON EARTH TAUGHT YOU THAT SORT OF LANGUAGE WAS ACCEPTIBLE!?' 'I'm just doing what all my hero's in Slipknot, Spineshank, System of down etc. do!' The boy mumbles humbly due to the discomfort placed upon him by Sagar's stern words. 'WHAT!? These are not the sort of role models for young children! Nor are the likes of 'Angeldust' Nick Casey a blatant PCP addict! THIS PROVES MY POINT MY BROTHER PROAGANDA THE POOR, IGNORANT, GULLABLE YOUTH OF TODAY ARE JUST SUCKERS FOR BAD ROLE MODELS LIKE THESE AWFUL HEAVY METAL AND PRO-DRUG CELEBRITIES!' Sagar roars. 'Yes young man.' Propaganda says sternly. 'I strongly suggest reading your bible when you get home.' 'I Don't have a bible.' The boy says. Eric Sagar looks faint. 'Sweet Merciful Jesus!' he says. 'Come with me young boy, I have a crate full of bibles in the back of my van.' 'Ugh. Okay.' The boy says rather reluctantly. Propaganda turns to the women behind the counter. 'Brother Sagar, he is a miracle worker.' But in the backdrop as soon as Both Sagar and the boy leave the room, the boy runs off discreetly as the scene fades to black.
