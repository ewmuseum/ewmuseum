Life of a Champion: Part II

3 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-3-02 The scene picks up with Walczak in the gym taking part in his daily workout, and the camera crew is setting up their equipment. “Welcome back to Life of a Champion. We are here with Tom Walczak and he is taking part in his daily work out. Mr. Walczak, would you mind giving us a description of what a workout to you consists of.” “Let’s see, there is the bench press of course, and also the leg press. I also do hammer strength machines, and squats, but it all varies. You see one day I can be working legs and then the next chest, or maybe arms, but the only thing that is definite is that I stretch out, and do calisthenics before and after each workout.” “Well, what do they consist of situps and pushups?” “Yeah, but also Diamond pushups, leglifts, and Chinese Pushups.” “Chinese Pushups?” “Yeah it is when you get into a handstand position, and then lower your nose to touch the floor.” “You could do those.” “Of course, I can do about six sets at eight reps per set.” “Ok, well the camera crew just wants to take a couple of shots of you working out if that is not a problem.” “No not at all.” Walczak goes back to training, and the camera crew gets the shots that they wanted. Once Walczak finishes his training they reconvene for a short interview. “Well, Tom has finished working out, and we are going to see how he feels.” “I feel tired, and if you don’t mind then I will head home.” Walczak leaves without saying another word, and the camera fades out…
