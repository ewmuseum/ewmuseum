Yes, we do allow camps in Christianity

24 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 24th 2002 This is promo is dedicated to the memory Joe Smallze, Rest in Peace 'For God so loved the world that he gave his only begotten Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish' John 3:16 The flames of fire burn brightly through the star light night air. 'Kum-by-ar my lord, Kum-by-ar' sings an angelic masculine voice. We find the scene fixed on a blazing campfire. ' Kum-by-ar, Kum-by-ar' again we hear the man sing, now we can identify the voice. This man is kind of tall, yet thin as a rake. He has a scrag of jet-black hair; he is strumming his acoustic guitar wearing a purple hand-knitted jumper that looks extremely thick. He also has on a pair of tatty grey jogging pants that's ends are tucked into white socks with a black and green stripe near the top. Over the socks he has tatty white trainers which are falling apart. The man is sat up as straight as a right angle on an old wooden beer barrel howling his song to the bleak night sky. His backdrop? A forest horizon joining with a stray nights sky. He is the only man to be seen but there are obviously others that are listening. 'Oh Lord, Kum-by-ar.' The man begins to strum beautiful notes as if he was playing a harp. 'Someone's crying my lord, Kum-by-ar' He whales gracefully. 'Someone's crying my lord, Kum-by-ar' He sings to the heavens 'Oh Lord, Kum-by-ar.' The man sings impeccably keeping with the tune. 'Kum-by-ar my lord, Kum-by-ar' The man goes to the chorus. 'Kum-by-ar my lord, Kum-by-ar' With this line we hear others join in, in an out of tune Southern accent. 'Kum-by-ar my lord, Kum-by-ar'. They all sing in unison. 'Oh Lord…' The man sings solo, 'Kum…' He pauses a lot more 'by-ar' he continues to sing the ar for a few minutes then stops and strums one last note. 'Marvellous' we hear from an accent distinct to the Tennessee region as he speaks over his own applause. The camera slowly turns round to see facing the man who was singing is Eric Sagar wearing his trademark suit holding a rusted metal rod with a stabbed marshmallow into the flames. Sitting next to him on a barrel is The Power Himself, Propaganda sitting next to him with the casual look of little emotion. 'Wouldn't you agree?' Sagar asks. Propaganda just glances at the preacher and nods. 'See, My Brother. You truly are talented.' 'Why thank you.' Exclaims the man, 'But in no-way am I as talented as you are Propaganda. You have amazing strength and power. Propaganda looks at the man bleakly and lets off a tiny yet cocky smile. 'He has the same strength given to Samson!' Sagar announces 'By God.' 'And is it not true that you shall have gold too?' The man asks. 'Why yes it is,' Sagar boasts 'Propaganda shall go one-on-one with 'Classic' Clay Vendetta for the honour of being the number one contender to the OSW International Champion.' 'Isn't he being stalked or something?' The man mocks. 'Oh I think so,' Sagar carelessly baffles 'By some masked people… I think there's four or something now. Anyway they will prove no threat as they are obviously against him. But we need no help is…' Slowly the Power Himself rises to his feet this draws the attention of the man and Sagar. 'MY BROTHER SAGAR AND MY BROTHER SCOTTLEY!' Roars God's ultimatum. ''CLASSIC' CLAY VENDETTA IS AN EGOTISTICAL WHINGE BAG! AND HIS ANTICS SHALL DRAW TO A CLOSE WITH ONE BLOW!' Propaganda's yelling startles the man who we know now as only Scottley, yes Sagar seems pretty proud. 'Testify! Testify I say!' Sagar encourages Propaganda. 'ONE POWERFUL BLOW! WITH THE STRENGTH FROM THE TRUMPETS THAT MADE THE WALLS OF JERICHO CRASH DOWN! WITH THE STRENGTH THAT PARTED THE RED SEA AT THE STAMP OF MOSES' STAFF! WITH THAT STRENTH THAT SAMSON PULLED THE TEMPLE DOWN! I SHALL DESTROY 'CLASSIC' CLAY VENDETTA!' 'For he is the evil in wrestling, his sort spoils it along with Vampir Nosferatu, Rhett Locke, Corg and all other Communists and Satanists.' Scottley points out. 'Indeed! And we have tried to reason with them all, yet they take no notice! NO NOTICE! If the Christian propaganda does not swerve thee than we shall, I assure you 'Classic' Clay Vendetta and the other 'superstars' in Old School Wrestling shall either see the error of their ways, flee, or be sent crashing to their knees and destroyed!' Sagar states 'AHAHAHA!' Chuckles The Power Himself. 'I CAN FEEL IT NOW! WITH THE CRUSH OF VENDETTA'S SKULL AGAINST MY FIST, THE CRUSH OF THE ATHIEST, SATANIST AND COMMUNIST SCUM THAT LOITER THE HALLS OF O…S…W!' Propaganda preaches, he then takes his seat. 'You know brother Scottley, with that angelic voice you should really get some kind of record deal.' Sagar suggests. 'Ah… No, I have my audience. I sing to the heavens in such a beautiful night like this,' Scottley informs the duo whilst admiring the graceful sky. 'Indeed, for God has graced us with such a spectacular night. I think we should thank him. Lets join in 'the lords prayer'.' Sagar suggests. As all three men slide off their beer barrels onto their knees, clamp their eyes shut and grasps their hands together in front of them. Sagar clears his throat and starts off. 'Our Father, You art in Heaven, Hallowed be thy name, Thy kingdom come, Thy will be done, On earth as it is in heaven, Give us this day our daily bread, And forgive us our trespasses, As we forgive those who trespass against us, Deliver us from evil, And not into temptation, For thine is the kingdom, The power and the glory, Forever and ever, A-men.' They pray in unison. They all sit back on their beer barrels. 'You know…' Sagar is interrupted as his cellular phone starts to ring in his inside jacket pocket. 'Excuse me.' Sagar requests. He takes the silver cell phone from his pocket flips the lid presses a button. 'Hello' greets the preacher 'Yes this is he. Oh really? Well… Yes, how much? GOD BLESS YOU! I'LL TAKE IT! A-men my brother, Shalom.' Sagar puts the phone down. 'Who was that' Scottley queries. 'It was the kind people of GOD TV again. My brother Propaganda, they have offered you to do a televised hot air balloon trip.' Sagar informs them 'I told them you'll take it.' 'YOU WHAT?' Yells the angry beast. 'Oh don't worry my brother, god will be watching you always.' Sagar replies. 'W… B…' Propaganda is lost for words. 'You're not scared are you?' Sagar asks. 'W… Of course not, I'm the Power Himself.' Propaganda boasts. 'Then you should have no trouble.' Sagar replies quickly. 'U… Okay.' Propaganda sighs. 'A-men, my brothers' Sagar preaches. 'A-men' responds Scottley. 'A-MEN!' Roars Propaganda.
