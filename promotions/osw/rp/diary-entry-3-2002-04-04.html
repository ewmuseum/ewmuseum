Diary entry 3

4 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/4/02 The images and sounds are enough to drive anyone mad. They are nothing new. In the past Frankie used to fight them. Right about now he would jerk awake in a cold sweat, not tonight. He simply sleeps with a smile on his face. He has embraced who he is in recent weeks, and it is paying off. After the systematic dismantling of The Red Army, Timo, and Aimz last week on Slam!, Frankie has been a very happy man indeed. Ever since that day the endless stream of bitching from Aimz and The Red Army was no surprise to Bones. He knew the Red Army would blame Capitalism and America for everything, but Frankie knew it was much more simple then that. Frankie hated each and every one of them, so he hurt them, that's just the way things work. And of course Aimz had her smart ass comments to make, same shit different day. All of these events, leading to tonight, to Frankie's dreams. But these are not the dreams of a happy man, they are the dreams of a mad man, how fitting. Something sparks him to reality. The dream world rushes away. He wonders what has stirred him. He has a running theme of thought in his head yet he can't grasp it, so he reaches for his pad. His homemade diary so to speak, and begins writing. Yo, all I need is one match, One chance, One shot, One nigga frontin My face on the front page. Only if I had one gun, One girl, And one crib. One Dad, To show me the things he never did. Pure, like a cup of virgins blood, Mixed with One fifty one. One sip, Will make a nigga flip. Writtin names on my hollow tips, Plottin shit, Acknowledge it, Three bodies chopped up in garbages, All shit that leads up to what the violence is. This is my hood I'm gonna rep til the death of it, Til everybody come out, Lil niggas is grown, Top dogs, thinking they can throw salt in my wounds, I've seen you before, and I'll see you again soon. I'm eternal like the stars, sun, and the moon. If you really think you ready to die when mine's out, This is one Bones is bout Nigga the time is now. Yo, all I need is one chance, One shot, One match, One punk. Look back at all the hatred against me, Fuck all that, I put you all on your back, Or your face in the mat, All those who doubted me, Are feelin the flack. Demons come and they go, Day in and day out, But if there's one thing I know, There's no use to lash out. You are who you are, You be who you be, But one things for sure, You can never be me. You can't see me, Know me, Feel me, Even hear me, I can see I got you runnin, You know the nightmare's comin. So it's boiling to the top, This madman's gonna pop, But until I get 'one match', This shit ain't never gonna stop. All I need is one match, One shot, One chance, One man... Fade.
