Moments after Slam! and we meet the new guy.

1 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/1/02 The scene takes us into the public locker room found in the backstage of the Reunion Arena located in Dallas, Texas. It has only been a few minutes since his official debut in Old School Wrestling. Kid Wonder was being taken away to see the resident OSW trainers and medical staff the last time that Scott saw him. The ever unseen, atleast to the people it is filming, camera spins around and takes us in to a shot of Scott Taylor sitting on a long wooden bench in the locker room. His legs are crossed and he is tieing the black strings on his left boot. Once the boot is tied Scott uncrosses his legs and reaches over to his left, picking up the piece of white paper lying on the bench next to him. An OSW Contract with his name across the top of it and the signatures of OSW Owner Jane Reagan and acting Commissioner 'The Scholar' Howard Porter across the bottom. 'Who would have thought? Little Louisiana boy, Scott Taylor competing in Old School Wrestling...I guess Satan isn't getting some snow this time of year then.' Scott stands from the bench and folds the contract in half, the long way and slides it into the back pocket of the black denim jeans he is wearing. He begins to walk towards the door of the locker room, easily pushing the swinging door open with his left hand. Once out in the hallway he takes a moment to look around the backstage area. There are still crew members running about doing last minute things, running by Scott on their busy ways. As Scott passes by them his mind begins to drift off, he's always had a tendency to do that. Being the kind of person that is always thinking, whether it be about an upcoming match, a match that has already taken place, or if it's planning for a future action or in the worst case scenario, just daydreaming. For Scott, the last option is what his mind has chosen to do. He continues to walk towards the parking garage as his mind begins to wander. 'Is this really happening? Finally, after seven long years of wrestling it's finally payed off. First NTICW, now OSW. Poor Kid Wonder, I'll show him a thing or two about 'Stroke' at Slam.' Scott can be heard mumbling in incoherent words to himself, confusing a few backstage OSW crew hands as he passes by them. He is referring to the recent events in his wrestling career ofcourse. The big thing on his mind at the moment is his match with the man he brutalized and left laying on the last Slam, Kid Wonder. Now he has to face K-Dub one on one with the winner becoming the number one contender to Corg's newly won Cruiserweight Title. But before the thoughts of just what this could hold in store for him can set in, Scott's mind wanders back to four months ago, the day that Mexicana Momar, also known as The White Mexican, signed him to the new defunct NTICW. A few select moments play through his mind, the biggest being the night that he pinned Semore Smoke to win his first World Championship. But enough of that, that was in the past and Old School Wrestling, it's Cruiserweight Championship Title, are his goals and 'The Quinnisential Teen Idol' Kid Wonder, his main targer. Scott's mind begins to wander back to reality and he realizes that he's already made it to the parking lot. His right hand slips into his front pocket and returns holding a set of keys. A clicking sound is heard as the door unlocks and Scott steps into a jet black 2002 Chevrolet Blazer. The engine roars to life and the headlights follow in suit, shining ablaze. Sounds of rubber screeching on pavement can be heard as the Blazer pulls out, leaving the garage and heading out onto the main road. 'Good ole Dallas. Too bad that I've never been here before. Not like it's my fault though, I don't think that NTICW could have afforded to book a show in a place like this anyhow. Speaking of Dallas...If Tyler Nelson lives here he may try and do something since I signed this contract instead of his.' It's true. Scott Taylor purposely went behind PCW Owner and CEO Tyler Nelson's back and signed the OSW Contract that Jane Reagan offered him. Let's just say that good ole Nelson didn't take that too well. But it's not like even a man of his stature will be able to hunt down one vehicle and one person in a city as big as Dallas. Nothing important now, just another meaningless past event to shove into the back of his mind. But it wasn't just Nelson that was angered. Scott also kept his secret from the rest of the NTICW Locker Room. Even his own stable mates ddn't know. He hasn't spoken to them since Chris S. sold thier contracts, but he's pretty damn sure that there is some bad blood about to boil over there. Oh well, it'll pass. Scott's right foot presses against the brake of the vehicle as he pulls up to a stop light. Anyway, getting back to his train of thought. Those things are all in the past, all that matters now is showing Kid Wonder that you can't hold a talented person down. Maybe after that he'll have impressed the booking commitee enough to get a shot at Corg. Ha! It's nice to think things like that anyway. The stop light above changes colors from red to green and Scott begins to drive again. He reaches down and flicks a switch, a green light shines on and written in black numbers on the LCD screen is the numbers for a Dallas radio station. Ironically enough, the song playing is none other than Godsmack's 'Whatever.' The song continues to play as our scene ends. Fin~
