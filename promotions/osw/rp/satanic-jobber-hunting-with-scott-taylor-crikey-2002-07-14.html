Satanic Jobber Hunting With Scott Taylor. Crikey!

14 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/14/02 Religion. Beliefs. Everyone has them. Whether you be a Christian, A Buddhist, A Hinduist, A New Age Religion, or an Athiest, or Scientologist, you can't forget Hubbard, or be properly bludgeoned by Xavier Kannon. People can deny any sort of beliefs, but you have to believe something. Even the Satanic Jobbers, the dark nocturnal freaks, with their pasty white make-up, fangs, contacts, dog collars, and other freakish forms of clothing. Issic Raiyn, the man who calls himself Corg. The 'Anti-God.' The 'Quiet Killer.' All these various names for a man who says he believes nothing and cares about nothing. He's not even a man, he's still a boy. So what if the law says he's a man? He sure as hell doesn't act like it. Bloody Satanic Jobbers, the only way to understand one is to be one. Man, that must suck. Our scene opens up in a club, the lights are dimmed down alot, to the near point of darkness. There are various strobe lights and other forms of luminesc hang on the ceiling to give some light. There is a large wooden dance floor which is covered by people. All of them look to be between the ages of eighteen and twenty-seven. They all also look like extras from Hellraiser. The music being played is a loud and aggressive death metal. The people on the floor are jumping up and down screaming and slamming into each other. It looks more like a Los Angelos Riot than fun. Away from the dance floor there are several tables spread about, all of them are filled up with the same type of people that are on the battle field, erm, dance floor except for one table situated in the far back corner. The man sitting here looks to be one of the older people here, even though he's only twenty-five, and is easily recognizable as Old School Wrestling superstar, Scott Taylor. Scott is dressed in a pair of loose fitting denim jeans, black Lugz boots, and a black muscle shirt which shows off his physique and the tatoo on each arm. His hair, which is probably the only in the entire club that is nautrally black, is pulled back into a braided ponytail and hanging over his shoulder, away from his eyes as not to impare his vision in the slightest. Scott sits at the table looking about normally, checking out the club and the people in it. Why would someone who is actually normal be in a place like this? One word, Study. To understand a person it is wise to study others like them in their nautral habitat. For sitting on the table infront of Scott is a small handheld camera which is on, recording everything that is going around him. Actually, it's not really for study. Scott is out to learn a little more about goths and get a nice little video to send in to one of those shows in hopes of getting a little money. Scott leans forward a little and begins to speak into the small speaker on the camera, however instead of talking in his normal deep voiced, and slightly southern twanged voice, he speaks like a very familiar celebrity. 'The Crocodile Hunter' Steven Irwin! 'Greetings there, this is 'The Goth Hunter' Scott Taylor, here! Today we are in Dante's Inferno, a small night club located right outside of Cleveland, Ohio where we are studying the elusive Satanic Jobber! Yes, I did indeed just say the Satanic Jobber! These rare creatures are young mammals, possibly right after the pupa stage in their progession, who tend to cling to death!' Scott suddenly ducks down under the table as a tall and rather muscular gothic looking man passes by him. The man is far taller than the six foot three Taylor and built like an oak tree. He is wearing black leather pants, a short sleeved black tee shirt with the saying 'Touched By Jesus, Fingered By God' written across the back. He is very pale, like the usual goth and has short spiky hair which is dyed an even darker shade of black than Scott's hair. He passes by the table, and slowly Scott stands back to his chair and spins the camera around to film the large goth. 'Crikey! We've found a prime example right here! Look at how big that bugger is! He must be on the top of the chain in the society of Satanic Jobbers! Notice the large muscles and the shirt which shows his alleigance to the black metal band, Cradle of Filth! This anti-Christian band is well known for it's front man Dani Filth's high pitched screams which is like a dog whistle to his goths! Wait, Wait! There's something else going on over here!' The camera spins around to show two extremely hot yet gothic girls. One is dressed in a short black dress which cuts off about an inch or so above her knees. She is wearing fish net stockings and black low top tennis shoes. Her face is painted white, her lips black, and enough mascara and eyeshadow to support Two Ton. She is dancing very sensually while looking at the other gothic girl, who is dressed in a pair of overly baggy black jeans, a short sleeved balck shirt that says 'Fuck You Mainstream America!' in jagged red letters. She has very short hair which is fanned out like that one little dinosaur in Jurassic Park. On her face is just as much make-up as the first girl. She too is slowly dancing sensually while looking at the other girl. In between them is a third goth, this one male. The male looks like that transvestite from The Rocky Horror Picture Show. He is watching this act very closely. 'Oh Blimey! How lucky can we be today?! This is an act of mating for the Satanic Jobbers! The two females, who are dressed in their best outfits will slowly and sensually dance with each other and even use slight foreplay to excite the male. Once the male is fully excited he will dive onto both females and a very long session of mating will commence. In which one or possibly both females will be impregnated. After carrying the hellspawn for nine months the child will be borned, and will promptly be named Damien or Lucifer and will be raised in the arts of the Satanic Jobber. Isn't it hard to beleive that only eighteen years ago, that young Issic Raiyn was conceived in thsi fashion? My, they sure do grow up fast.' At this point the camera is suddenly jerked up and away from the act of pre-mating ritual. As the camera refocuses it shows a mugshot of the earlier documented large Satanic Jobber. The camera getsa good view of his face, high cheek bones, short and flat nose, if it weren't for the various piercings and make-up he would actually pass as a fairly handsome man. He smirks and then hurls the camera to the ground, causing the documentary to lose it's feed. 'Fucking Right....' A deep voice rumbles as the man looks at Scott who is still sitting in his chair, with a shocked expression on his face. Scott stands from his chair and only stands to chest-level with the man. Scott slowly looks up at him and then takes a step back. This man is easily close to the seven foot mark and probably can fight like all hell. Scott continues to back away until he bumps into the chair he had been sitting in. He looks back at the chair and then to the man who is approaching. 'Satanic Jobber....I banish thee!' Scott screams out as he picks up the wooden chair and tosses it at the man. The goth easily catches the chair and this is the exacty thing that Scott knew he would do. Acting quickly, Scott jumps up delivering a standing dropkick into the chair. The large goth falls back onto the floor and the OSW Wrestler turns and makes a bee line towards the door, running by Satanic Jobbers of all shapes and sizes. 'THAT BLOODY GOTH LIKE TO HAVE BIT ME HAND OFF!' Fin~
