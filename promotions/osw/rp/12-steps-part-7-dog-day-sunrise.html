Date: MY B-DAY AGAIN! 'And here's Tom with the weather... So, Tom, what good news do you have for us?' Toomes watched the news sullenly, Jennifer Kale, the beautiful brunette valet of his who was kicked to the curb when Jane Reagan came into the picture, sitting near his bed, protectively stroking the back of his hand, mindful of the IVs sticking into his skin. A portly weatherman came into view, showing a picture of the United States, with garish red arrows flowing over Tulsa, Oklahoma. 'Well, John, it seems we'll be in the one-hundreds for the rest of next week, a hot air front moving in from the Gulf. Make sure to drink plenty of water people.' John, the anchorman, pipes in. 'What do you think is the cause of all this swealtering weather?' 'Must be the Oklahoma Gay Pride Week. Coincidence? I think not. The concentrations of Hot Gay Sex is simply heating up the state, hot man on man action from the Panhandle to the Arkansas border. I suggest noone wear leather chaps or a biker's hat, in order to keep those harmful hot man-on-man encounters from happening.' Tom said matter-of-factly. John furrows his brow. 'Damn, I hate faggots.' Susan, his co-anchor, agrees. 'Sure do, John. We sure do.' Toomes shakes his head, trying to get the fog out of his head. 'Did I really hear that?' Jennifer nods and curls her fingers with his. 'Yes, you did. This IS Oklahoma, after all. You feeling any better?' Toomes nods almost imperceptibly. 'Yeah... Feel like shit, even though I'm doped up so much...' A voice from the doorway floats through the air to Sebastian. 'Hey, as long as you don't go hallucinating all over the country again, I'd say you're doing well.' 'Hey, Doug!' Jennifer says brightly. The leggy ex-valet gets out of her chair and embraces Doug Troy, a fellow ex-OSW Champion. 'How was the hotel?' Doug returns the embrace. 'Was alright, I suppose. You hear the weather report?' 'Yeah, Gay Pride Week. Gonna be a hot one around here.' Doug shakes his head. 'DAMN faggots. Anyway, how you feeling, big guy?' Toomes smiles slightly. 'I'm alright, man. But I gotta say, I'm suprised you showed up here. I figured you were taking the whole thing a little too hard, getting plastered and shit...' Doug laughs and begins fiddling with the television. 'Naw, just feeling sorry for myself. You know, you begin taking your in-character persona too seriously, you start taking things personally...' Doug changes the channel to Comedy Central, and Absoultely Fabulous is on. Doug sighs and sits down on the other bed in the room. 'So, you see this Armour guy before?' 'Ain't ever heard of the motherfucker, but he did a number on me...' Toomes groans. 'He was working too stiff, man. I think someone wanted to send you a message... You think he's on the TPB's payroll?' The room is silent, tense, for a moment. 'Yeah... I think so. But that wouldn't explain why Porter and Jane let him in. Jane doesn't like her husband, and Porter has no love lost with Booker. Maybe Booker's planning on making a move on OSW once he gets control of PCW again. And maybe he finally got fed up with me and Jane...' Toomes says, clenching his fists. Jennifer smiles wryly. 'Heh. Maybe that'll teach you to leave me out to dry...' Doug laughs quietly. 'Honey, its not like SeaBass was feeling anything warm from you... I know you didn't want to be the stereotypical piece of ass on camera and off, but c'mon... The guy was looking for some affection and Jane knew how to pitch the angle to him. You gotta cut him some slack...' Jennifer nods and takes Sebastian's hand. 'Well, since I'm not on Miss Reagan's payroll anymore, I guess I can cut the 'Ice-Bitch' act, huh?' Toomes smiles, and interlocks thier fingers. 'Yeah, I guess... Jane's been doing her own thing for the past couple of months... She seems to have gotten tired of me...' 'So what does that mean, SeaBass? I'd reckon she's just coasting on you. You might wanna ask her what's up with you two...' DT says, cracking his knuckles. 'I might... I might... So why are you up here, anyway?' Doug smiles. 'Shit, man, I saw Slam and saw you getting the seven shades of shit kicked out of you, motherfucking getting slaughtered and shit... I just figured since I was near home, I'd come and see how you're doing. You were looking pretty bad out there, and, well, Jennifer here called me up asking about you.' Jennifer squeezes Sebastian's hand. 'Yeah, he was the only one who returned my calls. I lost contact with Jimmy, and I think he's in CCW these days. So we got together to come and check on you. You had us both scared...' 'Huh... Speak for yourself. I knew this louse would pull through just fine. Although I KNOW you wern't blading out there. He really did break your fucking nose.' 'Yeah...' Sebastian says grimly. 'I'm all for fun and games, but I don't appreciate a shoot involving me getting the shit beat out of me for real... Once I'm out of the hospital, me and Armour are gonna have a little talk...' 'Good man, good man. Well, I'm back to the hotel. You gonna stay here long, Jenny?' Doug asks, getting off the hospital bed and heading for the door. 'Yeah, me and SeaBass need to talk some things over.' Jennifer smiles. 'Thanks for showing up, Doug.' 'Yeah, man. I appreciate it, DT.' Sebastian says. 'Heh. Just don't expect me to jump Armour, man.' 'Hey, we'll stay in character and everything. I'm gonna fuck that boy up for real, though. You can count on that...' Sebastian says seriously. 'See ya. Hope ya feel better, bud.' Doug says, heading out the door. Jennifer turns to Sebastian and leans down to kiss him on the cheek. 'Now, about that hair...' -FIN-
