Vampir Nosferatu

11 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11 March 2002 The fist hits a punching bag, and violently flies back while the fist is drawn back, a left hand, which shakes the bag, and then a right with makes it sway. Finally one giant right which makes it fly around on it's string then from the other side a pair of short, stubby fingers attached to equally small and stubby hands which are on grey cuffs stop the bag still. The camera zooms out to see standing in a grey suit with white shirt underneath and a grey tie as he was before is Eric Sagar and Propaganda in his wresting attire. 'Ha! My Brother this so called Vampire, who worships Satan and more than likely drinks blood will not be like this anymore! He is not in with a chance you will pound in his skull to bits, and then hopefully he will get the message. That we're here to help him, and we're here to help the good of the world! If he put him out through a career threatening injury then he will be one less wrestler in the world. And we're one step closer of ridding the world of this awful sport…' Sagar says as he gets angry 'Hear my voice of rage!' Sagar is shaking and red in the face. 'God will not let you off if you continue your wicked ways! So if we have to annihilate you Vampir you will rot in hell! And this is what we'll do if you don't pull out and join us.' Sagar's voice is full with such anger it turns croaky on the last line. He then bows his head, smiles and looks up. 'But it will be all in the name of our lord Jesus.' Sagar rests his hands on the punching bag. Propaganda breaths slowly, then raises his hand in the air, 'He is watching us now Brother Eric! He can see us talking. And he can see into the future.' Sagar gets an interested look on his face 'Yes my brother, he has told me what my destiny is!' Sagar looks up to the sky where Propaganda is pointing. 'And what would it be my brother Propaganda?' Sagar humbly asks. 'He has only told me what I will do on Saturday Slam and he told me I would use the power he gave me…' Propaganda roars 'And crush the oesophagus of Vampir Nosferatu.' Sagar nods normally and laughs evilly. 'Oh, my brother it shall be done.' Propaganda agrees with Sagar by telling him 'It shall… God never lies…' 'He is our heavenly father.' Sagar finishes off Propaganda's sentence. They both smile at each other. 'Do you know what supposedly kills a Vampire my brother Propaganda?' Sagar asks to break the silence. 'I believe I do… Is it not the crucifix our lord Jesus was crucified on?' Propaganda asks. Sagar nods, 'Again you are correct, like you were about predicting your destruction of Vampir Nosferatu.' The silence again reappears until Propaganda asks 'Why did you ask me Eric?' Sagar bows his head. 'Well my brother it's a cross that kills vampires according to old tale. But isn't it Ironic that we will destroy Vampir Nosferatu with the crucifix in our mind and in our hearts?' Eric ponders to Propaganda. Propaganda says in agreement 'Our lord Jesus was sacrificed on a cross for us and if Vampir Nosferatu does not give up his wicked ways he shall be sacrificed for the sake of mankind's improvement.' Propaganda crosses his arms and stands back and for the first time ever to be broadcast on OSW Television he lets off a smile, but no ordinary smile. This smile has a sense of sadistic and yet a hint of satisfaction. 'Well my brother as eager as I am for Vampir Nosferatu to give up his sadistic ways, I would rather you sacrifice him.' 'Oh No?' Propaganda asks curiously Eric's voice picks up volume. 'No! I want you to crush every bone his Body has to offer, despite how much he will beg, despite how much he will plead. Destroy him!' Propaganda is rather confused by this, 'Really?' he asks. 'Really?' Eric replies 'If we beat Vampir Nosferatu to a living pulp, so that his blood stains the OSW Logo covered by his decimated corpse. Then we will get may others to give up!' Propaganda squints at Sagar, 'Why?' Eric chuckles evilly 'Because my Brother Propaganda, when The Power himself has massacred one man for going against his cause. They will know not to get in their way and flee!' Propaganda smiles at this thought, and then he bows his head. 'My brother you are correct! This shall happen. I will make Vampir Nosferatu an example of things to come!' Both Propaganda and Sagar bow their heads and in unison they preach 'A-Men.'
