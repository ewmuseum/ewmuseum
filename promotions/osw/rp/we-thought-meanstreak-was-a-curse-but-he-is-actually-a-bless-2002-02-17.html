We thought Meanstreak was a curse, but he is actually a blessing!

17 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-17-02 “Not exactly what I would have expected. Not exactly who I would have thought to see. But still, what happens must happen for reason…nothing Ivan can do about it now…..” The voice of Ivan Stanislav is heard in the background as the camera slowly fades up on Ivan Stanislav, Alexei Ruslan, Speedy Riggs, Communist Party Member Wang, The Two Round Faced Women, and Tom Walczak all stand in what appears to be a large aircraft hangar. In the background, a large jet of some kind is being fueled, with a banner of the Russian Federation printed on the side of the nose. Russian soldiers tinker around the large jet, wiping it down, fooling with hoses, and busying themselves with various other tasks required with the jet. The communists, however, stand in more of a mock circle, not really in any particular order. A table sits behind them with Ruslan and Wang’s back turned to us as they stare at various papers and documents. The Two Round Faced Women stand on either side of them, while Speedy and Tom stand to the left of the table and Stanislav stands to the right just slightly. Other than Wang and Alexei, the rest of the group seems to be embroiled in a conversation. Ivan points into his open palm and then points at Riggs. “You were supposed to be there to make count Speedy! That stupid Frenchman messed it all up!” Riggs shakes his head and puts his hands in front of him. “I did not think he would have hit me with a bottle, Praporschik!” In the background, Alexei shakes his head and mutters to himself. “How did this happen! How were we supposed to know that Meanstreak was returning! He is ruining everything!” “He is not ruining a thing!” Yells Walczak. He walks over to Ivan and grasps his shoulder. “Listen Ivan, there is nothing to worry about! So the Monster as they call him has come back! So he cheated to get a win on you in your match! He had to! All we have to do is find him and destroy him like you did before!” Ivan looks at Walczak while he continues to talk. “Meanstreak’s been sitting at home doing nothing for months! While you have been fighting constantly to take out all those that have stood in the way of our communist ideal! Don’t worry about him! I don’t think he will be able to defeat Hessian on Slam anyway!” Ivan nods to himself as he ponders this. Wang turns around and puts his hands in his pockets. “Pwapowschik, I would not fwet over Meanstweak! We will take care of him in due time! What you have to do is focus on gweat big Battle Woyal coming up! You should be able to win qwuite easiwy!” Ivan nods and looks at Tom. “That is right, but we are both present in this match, eh Comrade? We will collectively topple those that stand against us, and I say that if it comes down to just Ivan Stanislav and Tom Walczak, that we both get out of ring at the same time, therefore we both must go on in three way match against the other opponent!” In the background, Alexei spins around and looks at Ivan. Ivan cannot see him however. “I have seen the rundown of the opponents, and none of them appear to be of any trouble at all! None of them match our size and ability, save for perhaps another….” Ivan growls in the middle of his sentence and stops, clenching his fists. “….mystery opponent. But I would not be concerned! We are in it together, and they are all but split in their thinking! Their capitalist beliefs will ultimately get the best of them and we will proceed to the World Title match in March!” Alexei nods in the background and walks beside Ivan, between Walczak and The Russian Bear. “Ivan, I would like you to know that I personally will take care of Meanstreak. After all, he was the one that put me in the hospital a while back and he is due for some much needed revenge!” The little Russian rubs his hands together in thought. “Yes, I will take care of him! When I am finished with him he will not have any knees to walk with! Hee hee haa!” Ivan nods slowly and smirks, his morale has been lifted a bit. “You know, right here is what Communism is all about, my friends! Together, we will all make it through this time in which Jane Reagan and Ed think they can stop us! Doug Troy and Trashcan Man are nothing! Meanstreak is a cripple! And together, The Red Army, MY RED ARMY, will bear down upon them with a force in which no man has ever seen!!” Ivan laughs as the Red Army gathers around him. “They can try to stop us with rules! They can try to stop us with enemies! They can try to slow our progress! But ultimately, WE WILL PREVAIL! As this match comes to close on Saturday and Red Army is present in World Title Scene, we will be starting the beginning of our mighty reign over this federation!” Cheers erupt around Ivan. The soldiers in the background cheer as well. “Saturday Slam, the first major step of our great revolution will become reality! On Saturday Slam, the OSW will tremble at the sound of our dominance in wrestling ring! No man alone can send Stanislav or Walczak out of wrestling ring! And no one will thwart our plans!” Ivan laughs loudly. “Come my Comrades, let us celebrate! The coming of Meanstreak was looked upon as a curse, but in reality, it is blessing! We will destroy their morale in one fell swoop, and this ‘Monster’ will be nothing more than broken carcass under the Communist War Machine named The Red Army!!” Cheers erupt again around The Russian Bear as the Communists raise their arms in triumph. Ivan laughs and leads the men towards the door of the hangar. Together, they sing the Soviet National Anthem as one as the camera slowly fades away….
