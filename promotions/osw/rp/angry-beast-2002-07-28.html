Angry Beast

28 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 28th, 2002 Grunting, like a wild man. Anger and Rage circled his entire body. Everything around him wasn't worth his time. The only thing on his mind was to destroy. He was like a heat seeking missile, when he found his targets...he wouldn't let it down...he would continue on forward...until he destroyed these targets. Even if it meant destroying himself in the process. There he stood, punching wildly into the black punching bag in front of him. The shards of glass were still deep within his chest and stomach region, as blood continue to crawl down his bulky front half of his body, and slide down his entire torso. His eyes remained wide and red...as his blood was boiling. With every punch Armour sent into the punching bag, more rage built up and more intent for destruction grew. He was a mad man on a mission. And behind him...sat Howard Porter. His tag team partner in crime, and his closest partner within the Think Tank. Porter was always there to watch as Armour trained...but this time, Porter didn't wear his trademark grin...this time, he wore a face of shock. Porter's jaw had dropped, and his eyes were wide with fear as he watched Armour's bloody body continue to beat the hell out of the punching bag. Armour was covered in his own blood, but it didn't stop him. He sent even more punches into the punching bag, as the bag began to tear open...but Armour didn't care...what he saw in front of him...wasn't a punching bag...it was something else... No one makes me look like a fool...NO ONE! Not one single person makes me look weak...NO ONE! NOT ONE FUCKING PERSON! I won't allow it! His mind was filled with rage...and set on destruction... Ivan Stanislav...you believe you are something BIG. That you can put me down for good?!?! IS THAT WHAT YOU THINK?!?! Notice how you couldn't keep me down as long as your previous opponents! NOTICE THAT YOU FUCK!?!?!? BAM! A huge punch came forth, that almost knocked the punching bag off of its hinges. Porter jumped in fear, as he began to move his chair a bit more farther away from Armour...who was now grunting like a wild beast... Stanislav believes he something that can keep me down, does HE?!?!? I'll show him...you don't FUCK with this tank! I'll show him, that height doesn't matter...but SKILL does!! I will show Ivan...who's the REAL bitch! He'll end up just like his FUCKING FRIEND EDDIE! He'll be put six feet under...and when he does...I'll be there....PISSING ON HIS FUCKING GRAVE!!! BAM! BAM BAM! Porter was in total shock and fear, as he backed away even more from Armour. Porter looked into Armour's eyes from his angle...and saw nothing but pure rage. Armour's eyes were wide with insanity, as he continue to beat the hell out of the punching bag... Tempest!?!? You decide to come back?!?! Are you LOOKING to have your career ended quickly?!?! Looking to retire?!?! If so, you came to the right place you FUCKING BITCH! I've seen you before...with your stupid ass jokes and pranks. WHEN WILL YOU REALIZE THAT CHILDISH PRANKS WILL GET YOU NO WHERE?!?! EXCEPT INTO THE HOSPITAL!!!! YOU FUCKING BITCH! YOU WILL PAY!!! SMACK! One false punch, and Armour had missed the punching bag totally, as he punched the brick wall behind it! Porter shreaked in horror as Armour's punch caused a hole to form in the brick wall...and he noticed that Armour's fist was now bleeding...but it didn't stop him. Porter, although afraid and shocked...got up from his chair and slowly began to make his way to Armour... AND NOW EL DRAGO DECIDES TO COME BACK! THIS FUCKING MEXICAN WANTS A PIECE OF ME?!?! Is that it?!?! This fucking luchadore whats to fight in the BIG TIME?!?!? FINE! That is JUST FUCKING FINE!! I'LL SHOW DRAGO WHY HE SHOULD'VE KEPT HIS ASS IN MEXICO! I'LL MAKE HIM WISH HE NEVER SAW ME!! I'LL MAKE HIM PAY!!!! CRACK! Another punch that missed the punching bag, but connected again with the brick wall... AND TOOMES!! So...you've decided to reawaken...and in fact...you've issued the challenge. You want me in a GLASS TABLE match...you must really want to parish! FINE! You have GOT IT! Toomes, I may have not ended you...but I've changed you! After me...you will NEVER be the same again. NO ONE has destroyed like I have...NO ONE! Not Ivan, Not Time, Not Ace Hawk...NO ONE! I am your biggest obstacle...I am your ONLY obstacle...I am the man that seals your fate! I AM ARMOUR! AND I WILL DESTROY YOU!!!! SMACK! CRASH! With one super charged right hand, the punching bag flew across the room and smacked into the brick wall. The bag broke open, as the sandy insides fell to the concrete floor below. Porter was in complete shock...as he slowly approached Armour...who was breathing heavily and grunting in rage. Porter cleared his throat, which caught Armour's attention, and slowly spoke to his monster.... 'Armour, it is in your best interest if you would seek medical attention for those shards of glass. They are still deep inside of you, and you may want to have it taken care of....' 'PORTER! SHUT UP!' interupted Armour. Porter was in complete shock...and appauled...he went on to begin to yell at Armour. But Armour didn't listen...he heard, but didn't listen. He heard Porter bitching...but it didn't matter. Armour just grunted and yelled out... 'YOU WILL ALL BE DESTROYED!!!!!!' The yell of Armour caused Porter to jump back in shock, as the yell echoed into the night air...the yell of a wild beast. The yell...of an angry...monster.
