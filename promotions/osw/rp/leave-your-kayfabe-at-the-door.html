Leave Your Kayfabe At The Door!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Open to a gym. PCW Superstar Jason Core’s warehouse in Tallahassee to be exact, where the young superstar has made himself a little training ring, is where one would find, on this particular day, Jason Core himself, and OSW World Champion, Sebastian Toomes. Toomes is wearing his ring gear, with a Sepultura 96 World Tour t-shirt on. He leans on the ropes, testing the tensile strength. Core is wearing a pair of black track pants and an old ratty REM Green tour t-shirt. “This is a swank place you got here...” Core smiles and looks around at the warehouse. “Yeah, not really.” “I was trying to be civil.” “It’s a work in progress.” “I can gather that.” Toomes smirks. “So how’s PCW treating ya?” Core sighs. “Pretty well, aside from the ridiculous spots at the last PPV. My arm's still healing up. But I've got the Extreme Strap, and it's what's paying for the school, so everything's alright.” Toomes kneels down and gets into a Push-Up position. “That’s sweet… You come in after Ivan left?” “Sadly, yeah.” Core gets down as well, on his knees, flexing his triceps. “Would’ve liked to have a crack at the guy.” Toomes goes down, his head up, into the first repetition. “Well, I get all the cracks I want at him. I got to defend the strap against him at the next PPV…” “Ugh. You got insurance?” Toomes pops up and goes into the second. “Heh… Yeah, I’m scared, dude. I saw Red October, and what Meanie and Ivan did to each other. Makes my Glass Table match look like a trip down Pleasantview boulevarde…” “Yeah, and *that* was uglier than a Brandi Wilson promotional photo.” Core grins as he matches Toomes’ pace. “I don't know what kinda match he wants to run... I don't feel like being mashed into a bloody smear...” Toomes steps up the pace to ten, eleven…twelve… I mean, in CCW, i never had to deal with any big bruisers, only Bryson, and he's 100 pounds lighter than Ivan… “I hear ya. It's a different world outside of there. I've been up against a 300+ ex-con for over a month. It's like a different sport.” Core grimaces. “Karl?” “Yeah. He's a stiff worker, but he's alright.” Core nods, completing the 20th rep. “You got the strap off him, right?” Toomes asks, going for 30. “Yeah, finally righting that division.” Core matches him. Toomes grins and makes a honking, braying sound. “HANK! HANK! HANK!” Jason glares at him. “Heh heh... Why do they even keep him around?” “They don't. He got his release yesterday.” 35… “No shit? I thought Hank and PCW went together like Ghettodogg and 'Disgraced World Title’ ' Toomes gets to 40. “Like Rhett Locke and fat girls, even.” Core adds. 40 as well… “In the words of Vinny Astone, Word. So what do you do for fun down here? It's almost time for Spring Break, right? “Actually, that was a week ago. Everyone's about back now.” Core grunts. 48. “Fuck, I'm out of it…” Toomes shakes his head. 54. “And 'for fun' I tried to go to a fraternity party to unwind.” “Dude, don't tell me you're a partier.” “No way, it was a favor to some guys I have working at the school…” Core stops, taking a break, bending his knees. “I wound up passed out in a dark room with a sixteen year old, and had to run to keep from her getting her jailbait groove on. No more fraternity functions for me, Seb.” “You didnt get drunk, did you?” Sebastian narrows his eyes. “Well, I didn't EXPECT to... Hell, I was nervous. I'm old, and I don't go out much.” Sebastian looks mildly disgusted. “Dude! How can you get drunk on PCW's schedule?” He’s up to 63. Jason shakes his head, then gets down for another set. “Not really effectively. I had to work the PPV with a hangover. I learned my lesson from it, though…” Sebastian takes a momentary break, then gets down to crank out a few more.“Guys who drink it up on PCW's circuit are bound to get cut. It's a different biz these days. You cant go on a bender and expect to work a good match… Like Blayze... The guy's a pothead, and he's always getting de-pushed. Even in CCW, you need to stay clean.” Core looks over at Sebastian, smirking. “Hey, now wait a sec. Who are you to talk? From what we're seeing on TV, I can't imagine you've had any ENERGY to work in weeks. Besides, I'm off the sauce, unless it's character. Sebastian cranks out ten more, then rests. “Heh heh...” “I messed a spot, and my arm suffered for it. Can't afford to mess up or get cut. I've got bills.” “What did you do?” “At that PPV, I twitched when LeBlanc put a staple gun to me, and it hit an artery.” Core leans on one side, and points to a twisted scar. “Ewww.... I saw that. How the fuck did they convince you to take a staple gun spot?” Toomes does a couple more, and rests again. “Simple. I was out of work, and grateful. I need to get some on air time, it's hard to get on TV with all the talent in PCW these days. The roster's overstuffed.” “True that...” Toomes gets up from the mat, flexing his arms. You know, I'm kinda getting pissed at the Match Bookers myself” “Stanislav, or being booked with women?” Jason remarks, getting up as well, his knees creaking and popping. “Nah, nothing that juicy. I just can't work the match with my opponents. I used to do it in CCW, until Spider got pissed… He said I was just trying to put myself over.” “What about Timo? The two of you looked alright.” Core asks, mopping the sweat off his forehead with his shirt. “I don't know, I like working a little stiffer. Like me and Neuro, we worked out an awesome match. But Timo and me just had to Paint By Number the whole thing. I really didn’t feel right doing it, and it shows.” “Yeah, but you've gotta STILL be hurting from that. Besides, do you really WANT Ivan working that stiff with you?” Core asks, climbing out of the ring. “Well, it's more realistic. I dont know, I just got this thing where I wanna be Shawn Michaels... heh heh…” Toomes nods, as he follows Core, and catches a towel thrown from Jason. “Well, start by kicking Bones through a plate glass window. That might help.” Toomes thinks for a moment. “Hmmm... Your idea has merit. Hell, I don't know. I'm just glad I have a job! Just wish I could exert a little more influence.” Core mops his armpits. “Yeah, well coming from CCW doesn't help anyone.” Toomes looks at him oddly, stretching his arms again. “How do you figure?” Core shrugs. “Kensuke and JJ got reps as good workers, but no fun backstage. And BAS and TMM kinda keep to themselves.” “Yeah, true. Have you worked with TMM?” “Not yet, although I'd like to. I've been doing my undercard thing, and Sunday might be my big break. I drew Frisco.” Core smiles, working out a kink in his shoulder. “You know, I havent seen him around... What's he up to these days?” Toomes asks, taking his water bottle from beside the apron. “He's working with a guy who I'm teaming with, called Legend Inc. Big time indy player. He's playing the DiBiase heel, and playing the foreigner card. Not as over as he used to be, but his workrate's slipped. Still, it's a money match for me if they put me over. He does a lot of good things in the ring, reminds me of you.” Core explains. “Nice. You got a speed bag in this place?” “Yeah, it's in the corner.” Core points in the direction of the bathrooms. Toomes wanders over in the direction Jason pointed… “Over here?” “No, more over by the water fountain.” “Oh.” Toomes meanders over, and taps the battered speedbag with a taped fist. “You think I'm doing a good heel run?” “Absolutely, but I gotta wonder when it's gonna run out. You're not over as evil, you're over as obnoxious.” “I don't think I could work being ‘big evil scarey heel’” Toomes begins punching it slowly, getting warmed up. “That's Hessian's job. Besides, I think as a prerequisite for working scarey heel is smelling of cloves and listening to Black metal” “Ugh. Why isn't Locke heel then?” Core grins, mopping himself off. “No kidding. Maybe if you pulled some more cowardly heel tricks, it could help you out? Drop and run with the title after a 20 minute classic, that sorta thing. Toomes nods as he slowly begins to work up speed, the sound of the rhythmic pounding of his fists on the leather speedbag filling the warehouse. “Yeah, I still gotta hear from Ivan on how he wants to work it. Heh heh... I swear, Goths... Me and my buddies used to beat the shit out of them when I was living in LA” “Well, lord knows Ivan can work. He's pretty generous in the ring, is the word.” “I got this great spot in mind for us, too” Toomes grunts as he batters the speedbag. Jason comes over to observe. “Really? Good to hear that you're thinkin' on it. Too many kids these days rush their matches, no psych.” “Check this. He Red Scares me on the ringsteps. He tries again, releases me, I DDT him on the steps. Oughta give us like 3 or 4 minutes of rest.” Core thinks for a moment. “Seriously, just better watch it and take that move high, otherwise you'll need ten, at least, and two days off.” “Well, better that I work it out before hand... Besides, I can stretch it into five if need be. We can sell the shit out of that spot…” Toomes pants as he hits the bag. “Yeah, not much you can do to get big rest...hey, he's playing a baby now, right?” “Yeah, doing this thing with Tempest... That's weird, though, working with Ivan as a Face.” “Yeah, it's working for him a little...but how could he NOT be cheered with Tempest in his corner. God, she's a looker…” Core grins. Toomes gives Jason a pitying look. “Obviously taste in women isnt required in PCW... She doesnt do anything for me, really..” Core keeps grinning. “Obviously, you're too busy under the boss lady's skirt to be saying anything.” Sebastian gets a solemn look on his face. “I am contractually prohibited form saying anything about me and Jane...” Core shrugs. “Go figure. Too bad you didn't bring that Kale lady with you, she's an awfully good-looking woman. Although, I'm really not much of a catch for younger ladies these days...” Toomes stops, and steps back, sweating heavily. “Oh stop it! Chicks dig the...older, more mature...needing Viagra...type of men. And besides, with the Jane angle, Jennifer isn't required right now.” “Yeah, well...if she needs work, there isn't a single valet in PCW these days with personality. And my prescription for Viagra is about ten years off, smartass.” Jason slugs Toomes in the shoulder. “Heh heh... I gotta wonder, how she feels about this angle...” “Probably thrilled that you're not grabbing her ass or smacking your salami to her pictures, and at the same time pissed because she can't be making any money from it.” Core says, walking over to the water fountain. “Well, do you need a valet right now? I can have her call you, see if she can't get some work with you guys.” “Nah. I still have to play up my, uh, Jenna thing.” There is a moment of tense silence. “Oh.” Toomes mutters. “Yeah...” “Faux pas!” Toomes says cheerfully, and goes back to the speedbag. Jason shrugs. “Heh...s'alright. Not your job to be involved with my personal life. I got some good news though. “What's that?” “I hear that they're moving me up to the TV division after the Pay-Per-View. I'll actually get some on-air character time. Pretty soon, I'll be one of the guys OSW wants to jump ship.” Core says, obviously proud of himself. Toomes grins. “What would you run there?” Jason looks at him questioningly. “Whaddaya mean?” “Like what angle? Why would you be there?” Jason nods. “If I were in OSW? Probably a low-card face. Poor man's Ed-type. I'm in with the current President, and if he loses power, I could be outta the picture.” “We need good faces... We're really lop-sided here.” Toomes says as he steps away from the speedbag once again, tired. “Yeah, no kidding. Civil Disobedience seem to be the most over guys as babies.” “Meh. Okay, enough shop talk. What you doing tonight?” Toomes says as he goes over to the water fountain. “Let me check my schedule...” Core mimes opening up a day-planner. “Okay, I get it. Nothing.” “Heh. Likely wrapping my knees and watchin' match footage. So yeah, nothing.” Toomes grins. “Hmmm. That's funny, I'll be watching tapes too!” Core sighs ahead of time. “PORN tapes! Yuk yuk!” Jason laughs a little. “You know why you're successful?” “Why is that?” Toomes asks. “Your gimmick is just this…” Jason makes a hand motion small hand motion… “far from the real you. S'a shame they don't see the face Sebs too often.” “Heh heh... I get paid to be a horny jag-off. I love my job... Besides, I'm sure a Face turn will be in my future as soon as Phantom Booker destroys me and I lay low for a couple of months…” “Nice extended break, you got any plans for it?” Core asks. “Not really... Why, you got anything planned?” “Nah, just wondering how many of you guys were doing the showbiz thing. That seems to be what all the champs are doing these days.” “The only film I would star in is a porn film.” Toomes shakes his head sadly. “Heh...'Smallville'?” Toomes whips his towel at Core, who moves out of striking range. “Hey, hey. None of that foreplay stuff, kid.” Core grins. “Yeah, laugh it up... But seriously, with my rep, that's about all I could do film wise, right now. You can only seriously get movie roles if you're doing Face-type things.” “Yeah, well you got time, for sure.” Toomes looks out towards the open warehouse door, the sun dropping below the horizon. “Maybe... Anyway, I'm off for a bite. When's your next show?” “Fusion. I get LeBlanc for the fourth time. Fun stuff, but the crowd's getting tired, I think.” “Heh heh... you two deserve each other... Anyway, I'll see ya later, bro...” Toomes turns and grabs his jacket. “Later...oh, and one last thing!” Core calls out. As Sebastian turns around, Core asks “Seriously, if you happened to put in a good word for me with Jennifer, I'd be much obliged…” Toomes grins. “Date-wise or work-only?” Core shrugs non-commitedly. “Both. It's the business, we get to mix business with pleasure, right?” Toomes shakes his head. “I wouldn't know... I'll let her know, okay?” “Heh...thanks, man. Good luck with Ivan.” Core waves farewell. Toomes makes a face. “I'll need it...” Toomes walks out to his Camaro, the dying sunlight reflecting off the highly waxed finish. He kind of came here in a hurry, throwing on a t-shirt after he left official practice for Slam!. He got a call from Jason after Saturday’s show, and since Jason was home for a couple of days before Fusion, Seb drove up from Orlando to see him. Since Core worked with Kyle Lamen and Kensuke Suzahara and was well known backstage at CCW for some spot work, Toomes was eager to spend some training time with the man. They spent most the afternoon working out some kinks in Toomes’ aerial work, and Sebastian’s back ached from the workout. That’s okay though, Toomes grins as he opens the door to his car. Jane’s quite good at the backrubs… Slamming the car door shut, he gives Jason’s warehouse one last look before he goes back to Jane’s hotel room. What a lonely life, he thinks. And that could easily be me once this gravy train derails… Might as well enjoy life while it’s good, because it could go down the shitter at any moment… Toomes turns the ignition key, puts the Camaro in gear, and rumbles off towards I-75 South. Fade Out.
