A Fairy Tale Ending

23 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/23/02 ~Dance tonight for tomorrow you may die... How many cultures have that same mentality, that same belief in the last wild night before a battle, lest they not return? I can only guess. But I've always thought it was a good idea. Come to think of it I've always thought any party at any time for any reason was a good thing. So what does a Tempest like myself do in Orlando? Go to Disney World of course! And after a fun filled afternoon on the roller coasters and rides and pinching Mickey's tail, I find myself here, at Club Mystique. It's fun to be in the only town in the US where you can dance to the latest techno sounds with mouse ears on and no one looks at you funny.~ *Here you are inside of Club Mystique. Yes you. You didn't know you were part of this promo? Well you are now, whether you like it or not, so suck it up fat boy! Anyway...You take a deep breath and convince yourself that your fly isn't open for the third time as you cruise around the dance floor, careful to avoid those bouncing bodies... especially that little black haired one with the mouse ears. She looks great in spite of the funny fashion accessory, in a tight pair of flare leg low riders made of some kind of shiny blue vinyl. It has little speckles of glitter in it that make for some very provocative reflections. Her top is an arrangement of tiny silver chains strung from a loop that winds around her neck. You can't tell what's under the chains exactly, as they sway to and fro, but you're pretty well determined by now to keep watching and figure it out. What you can see though is breathtaking. Yeah it's just her arms and her back. But how sleek and graceful and... just plain inviting, a swath of very light tan skin that invites your touch. Your buddy told you she's a wrestler, but since when do wrestlers look like that? Must be one of those new kind that do all the flipping and flying instead of just rolling around on the mat. Your brain freezes on the concept of any kind of rolling around with somebody like her. urk... Ok, you're past it. A wrestler? No way... your buddy has to be mistaken. Then again he said she's seeing some huge Russian wrestler. Maybe you don't want to find out the hard way that he's right. Still... you take a look around, no huge Russians in sight. Maybe you could talk to her at least, say hi. You want a better look at that smile. And what a smile it is. You try and think about that porn flick you saw the other night, maybe she'll seem more accessible if you can just see her... nope, you can't quite do it. For some reason you keep thinking of the time you saw Sleeping Beauty when you were seven. You didn't really want to go, but once you were there in the dark of the theater, with nothing else to focus on, you were swept up in the story and charmed into a happy disconnection with reality. Sort of like now. You haven't been paying attention and have wandered closer to the dance floor and her. The pain in your toes snaps you back to reality just in time to see her face stare up at you in surprise. Those crystal blue eyes sparkle just as you imagined as her lips form a perfect little O before she speaks and breaks your trance.* 'Oops! Sorry, I didn't see you there,' Tempest yelled above the throb of Pink's 'Get the Party Started.' She laughed and shimmied away from a dazed twenty-something guy with dark hair. He grinned like she had just made his day and as she glanced over her shoulder she shook her head quizzically. She bumped Miyuki with her hip and said, 'What's with him?' Miyuki glanced over her shoulder, between sways and said, 'I dunno, just met his favorite wrestler maybe?' She grinned as Tempest laughed and said, 'Maybe. I'm not sure I'll ever get used to that.' The song ended and faded into something soft and romantic and Tempest nodded saying, 'That's our cue! Time for a drink!' They sauntered over to the bar and Miyuki looked at Tempest pointedly as she said, 'I hope you're not going to order one of those syrupy sweet drinks that contain a ton of alcohol like you usually do. You have a big match tomorrow.' Tempest rolled her eyes as she plopped onto a tall funky barstool with a winged back that made her look like a fairy as she tossed her mouse ears aside and said, 'Who Me? Nah... not tonight. I'm having one of those syrupy sweet drinks with no alcohol whatsoever, thanks.' They laughed as their drinks arrived and turned to watch the dance floor. Tempest sipped hers thoughtfully and said, 'Ya know, for someone facing a match that I'm predicted to die in, I feel remarkably calm.' Miyuki rolled her eyes and said, 'Well, at least one of us does then. I can't decide whether to make your final arrangements or call a press conference to announce that you'll be going into rehabilitation for chronic delusional optimism.' She huffed a little, but Tempest just laughed and said, 'Oh come on. I have a plan, so stop worrying. And I've never been better prepared!' Tempest flexed with a menacing scowl and growled in her most kitten like fashion. Miyuki laughed and said, 'And Disney World was an essential part of that preparation no doubt?' 'Of course! I had to ride all the roller coasters to make sure the dizzy spells were gone from that last hit,' she said folding her arms over her chest. 'Twice?' Miyuki said doubtfully. 'Yes! And... and that whole thing at Space Mountain was intended as an exercise in balance,' she quipped defensively. 'So the fact that you bounced right onto that one guy was just an accident huh?' Miyuki asked with a sly smile. 'Of course it was! Well... he looked like he needed help figuring out how to make use of the low gravity room,' she said and then sighed. 'I didn't mean to smack his head into the ceiling.' Miyuki chuckled and said, 'Mmmhmm... a little over-exuberance I suspect. And what about the incident at Splash Mountain?' Tempest pouted and said, 'How was I supposed to know that balcony was the observation stand. I thought it was where people lined up if they wanted to be splashed.' Her shoulders slumped a little and Miyuki laughed. 'Well as long as no one's dry cleaning bill is too outrageous I don't think you're in trouble. But you have to be more careful. You're getting to be a big star now and someone might start taking your fun the wrong way. They might take advantage of your fame and sue you or something.' Tempest sighed and kicked her heels at the circular bar under her seat. 'I know. That's why I hired you, to keep me out of that sort of trouble,' she said. 'Although I know it's a tough job. I know I need a small army really.' Miyuki smiled gently and put her hand on Tempest's arm as she said, 'I can manage, and I do. But what I really have trouble with is your decisions to face guys like Meanstreak. I mean there are plenty of good, legitimate opponents against whom you could fight and make a name for yourself. Why go after this one?' 'Miyu, you know I can't just let that kind of thing go,' Tempest said. 'It's a point of honor. Not so much to avenge myself or win as to face him. So many people in this business bluster their way along and say they aren't scared of anyone and they're so tough they never hurt. I'm not like that. I can admit to fear, but that doesn't mean I want to live with it. And ya know what else?' She turned to Miyuki, her eyes narrowing slightly and said, 'I can't live with the sight of Clara Deveaux's crushed fingers and useless arm. Someone needs to let her know that the monster they call Meanstreak can't jump out from any closet and get her.' Miyuki nodded slowly, sitting up straighter in her seat as she said, 'I know. I know Tempest, but Clara Deveaux isn't your problem. I feel terrible about what happened to her too, but that doesn't mean you have to get involved and...' 'Don't I?!' Tempest snapped. She smacked her drink onto the bar and the little umbrella bounced out and rolled away. 'Doesn't someone? Yes, I would be safe if I didn't, but that's not the point.' Miyuki looked at her curiously and said, 'Then... what is the point?' Tempest took a deep breath and toyed with the straw in her drink, looking off into the middle distance, her reflection clear in the mirror behind the bar. 'When I was nine, my father was killed by members of one of the triads. I don't know why or if there was even a real reason. But he was gunned down in broad daylight, while we walked down the street.' Miyuki shifted uncomfortably in her seat and said, 'Tempest I know this, it's not new to..' 'Hear me out,' Tempest interrupted. Miyuki nodded silently as Tempest continued, 'He bled to death while I still held his hand. And as soon as his eyes closed, when I knew he was gone, I started screaming. I screamed for what seemed like forever. And while people stopped and stared and I could see the sympathy in their faces, no one stopped. No one reached out to help. They put their heads back down and walked away as quickly as they could. They didn't want to get involved because they knew what kind of trouble it would get them. So they kept walking, and no doubt were safe and untroubled by the triads. They had their reasons, but...' She turned back to Miyuki. 'I'll never be free from that moment and its terror and maybe, just maybe, if someone had tried to tell me it would be ok, I would feel different now. If someone had just taken a moment to try and quell that fear...' She shook her head rapidly and said, 'I can't be one of those people Miyu. I refuse to be. That's the point. Right or wrong, for better or worse, I can't stay back and not get involved. Because I'm one of the few people who knows how much is really riding on it. It is the difference between being able to live with myself and not.' Miyu nodded and said, 'I understand. I just hope that you live long enough to feel that satisfaction.' Tempest smiled and said, 'I will, don't worry. It may not be a fairy tale ending, but it's a start.' She smiled and picked up her mouse ears, and set them back on her head as she sipped her drink.
