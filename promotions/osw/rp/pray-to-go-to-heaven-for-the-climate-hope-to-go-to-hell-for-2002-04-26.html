‘Pray to go to heaven for the climate, hope to go to hell for the company'

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 26th, 2002 'I keep on fallin in.. In love With you. Sometimes I love ya Sometimes you make me blue Sometimes I feel good At times I feel used Lovin you darlin' Makes me so confused. I keep on Fallin In and out of love with you I never loved someone The way that I love you Oh, oh, I never felt this way How do you give me so much pleasure And cause me so much pain? Just when I think I've taken more than would a fool I start fallin' back in love with you I keep on Fallin' In and out of love with you I never loved someone The way that I love you' - Alicia Keys 'Fallin' There was still some doubt as to whether Drew wanted to be serious. Did he really want to come out and tell the world his true feelings for Aimz? Did he want to tell the world that he utterly and completely felt about her? Would he share, about how when she called him a nobody or took a cheap shot at his sexuality..it made him all tingly inside? But her father baggy pants, and her mom's push up bra...they all made this big blob of..woman that found herself so 'cool,' so rebellious, so..unapproachable, so damn...insensitive. These thoughts and many more of their kind insidiously penetrated the head of Andrew “Hostyle” Copeland as known by regular OSW viewers. The sunlight seemed also to slide across the body of the shirtless, dark skinned man in long shorts that mirrored the surrounding’s outlook, very bright and colorful. All this, he’d been doing while lounging on the middle of a chair as he soaked in the bright sun with navy blue mp3 player attached to his ears and his eyes appearing to be glued shut. He was just now starting to come to the reality that after another Slam! and the OSW’s latest PPV, the whole entire world would know the truth about what really had happened between Aimz and he. And because of this they’d all loathe him even more than before and Drew’d of course give the initial response of telling the bunch of them where to stick their damn heat. Or they’d all silently go about their everyday lives knowing that Drew follows a code of honor, that knows no exceptions and is superior over their minuscule amounts of will power. And who to help better lead him on the journey within himself than the family Pastor? Right? Pffft..you’re just as dumb as I thought. The scene fades to show earlier in the same day where Drew was infact at the ‘family’ Pastor in their church in Brooklyn. But to use ‘family’ and ‘Drew’ in the same sentence though would be a huge misconception, so for now let’s call the Pastor, beneficial to Drew’s well being. You see Drew was still apart of a small, but growing in numbers gang of men who called themselves the Benefit. The Benefit wasn’t formed because they wanted to positively infect a society or particular group of people besides themselves. But if they did, who gives a flying f*ck? People, fans, or supporters were mere distractions to the Benefit. The Benefit was about making the future better for themselves, and fighting their own causes in which they’d come together to do. Where they aware that what they did might benefit others? Of course. Did it worry them? Not in the least, as far as they were concerned only the strong survived and they didn’t give a second thought to anyone else. Women weren’t at all they for protective purposes, they’d rather have a man leading the fronts than a woman, under any circumstances. Women were for only domestic purposes. Simply put, these purposes were cooking, cleaning, loving or whatever else wasn’t clearly defined as evil. Everything that historically and not sexist-ish, woman do well. Everything that Drew never had. 'So it is partially about embarrassment, Andrew. But the other portion..maybe it's about your lusting after a woman who's already taken? Maybe it's about bitter vengeance.' The slightly stuttering voice of an old black in a purple and yellow robe reading the words ‘Union Baptist Church’ sounded. 'So now you think I be sinnin'?! I’mma do right, fool. You think I be lustin after some trick wit a man already?! Hell, even if I was like dat dere, doncha think I know that success is da best revenge? I been there and don dat, Rev. I gotta admit she can suck the paint off a 72' Honda, but it ain't worth all the denying she's doin.' Drew spoke out of the side of his mouth while chewing a toothpick on the opposite side. He was been dressed in a sharp two piece black suit and sat face to face with the elderly man. 'Andrew..bruthas under da lawd like us, we have good taste. We only like the best. In jobs, in luxuries...and in women. Don't think I can't see through the denial of one man, if the good lawd hisself can see through many and send his only son in the midst of it. We are not worthy Andrew. He gave his life for us you know. So that we, the ones who do wrong and sin could praise Him and thank Him. We is truly not worthy, my boy.' The Reverend spoke with increasing volume as he prepared to deliver his own service. 'Look, I didn't come hear fo no hour long lecture, Preach.’ Drew retorted, grabbing the toothpick from his mouth and flicking it somewhere near the Preacher’s feet. ‘Yo job is to get me ready fo this match. You and the man up stairs both know I ain't lyin.’ 'The outcome of the match is up for the Lawd to decide.' The Reverend spoke clearly in response to Drew‘s confidence. 'Nah, nah Rev. F*ck dat. I'mma tell you how dat match is gonna go down right now.’ Drew sighed only partially angrily yet, as he began to spit into the Reverend’s face with his intensity that was intended to show that he meant what was being said. Just then he could hear his little brother, Jordan X Shabazz’ response to every counter argument. It was kind of like a bad conscience, you know that good guy above your head on the right and what you really felt on the left, both poisoning his thinking process. I wish she woulda just told her little rock star friends and all her damn groupies the truth. ‘Now, I GOTTA beat her.’ Drew scoffed. ‘And beat her for one reason only. Because she's not so , she’s not even so damn half assed innocent as she wants the whole world to think.’ ‘Heh, heh.’ The Reverend chuckled, now fully amused by Drew’s so called attempts to ‘do right.’ ‘I beat her once. Flat out BEAT her ass. Now dat proved my point of being capable to put her and other women or weak assed men that wanna step up, in her place right dere.’ Drew said. ‘But not for long, because then little Amy then wants to pretend she didn’t like getting covered for the 1, 2, 3 the first time, good enough. I bet those were the longest seconds of her life.’ And that feeling wasn’t neutral? ‘NO, Dat feeling ain’t neutral. I gotta admit, she a whole lot more crafty then I give her credit for sometimes, especially where it counts. But come on, bro. You know when it’s money involved, I do what I must.’ Do you really gotta beat the trick that bad to get her to fess up? ‘Heh, don’t ask me. I’m still stuck on what our rendezvous’ will be like after this.’ Drew laughed, answering his brother Jordan’s questions while the Preacher stared at him dumbfounded. 'It’s just too damn boring fighting her over and over again. What’s my motivation this time, smart ass?’ Drew smirked. How about giving the fans their money’s worth. ‘Heh. I don’t know where you got that damn smart mouth from. The fans can do whatever the hell they want. I gotta do what I gotta do though, sh*t.’ Drew returned. Watch your mouth. ‘Watch your mouth brutha!’ The Preacher shouted towards Drew. ‘F*ck off’ He muttered towards them both. ‘I say, speak up brutha!’ The Reverend asks, who naturally is a little hard of hearing. 'I said, so you think it was good to come to you wit dis before my 'I lied' match?' Drew pretends to repeat with growing anticipation. 'Of course, my child.' The Reverend responds matter of factly. ‘All should be right with the lawd before anyone else whenever ya want a clean slate.’ 'Well den let's go get a drink or somethin for now. Propaganda type nig...er bruthas make me kinda nervous, ya know?' The Reverend looks at Drew for awhile in silence. ‘Have you ever seriously thought about going into priesthood? Drew think for a minute and then answers easily. ‘Nah, it ain’t my thang.’ The Reverend then does a little foot dance and whispers a quick thank you towards the sky. Drew laughs. ‘Heh is that what you been thinkin dis whole time?’ The Reverend doesn’t answer but continues on towards their walk to the door with a look of satisfaction on his face. ‘Damn fool.’ Drew murmurs to himself as that scene fades to black and we can once again see Drew lounging on the chair looking as if all was right with the world while he sat there with ear phones on and tuned out the rest of the world. Drew was thinking about feelings, and emotions and whatever the f*ck else chicks were about these days. But was it useless to even try and understand them? Ah..maybe when it matters? We’re all gonna end up in the same place anyways, right? Maybe I’ll talk to her when hell freezes over. If you’re paying reading this you’re probably on the fast track there anyways.
