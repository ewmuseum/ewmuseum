Inner Thoughts (Ivan/Drago rp)

8 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-8-02 While he laid asleep in his hospital bed, the mind of Ivan Stanislav continued to work out what had happened at Saturday Slam. It was surreal, almost, to think of what he said. That he had gotten rid of, disbanded his Red Army, the group that he had led for over a year. Several times he thought about stopping. Several times he thought that maybe it would be better to weather the storm and try to find another way to feel complete again. But he knew that running was all over. It was time that he face the enemy. The poison, as he called it, that was within himself. Was Ivan Stanislav scared? The answer would be simple: Yes. But somewhere, deep within himself, he knew that it was the only thing he could do. And what were the results? Stanislav felt like he was twenty years younger when he fought El Drago. An old fire had emerged from within his cold, iron heart. Perhaps it was the fans cheering for him. Perhaps it was the great weight that had been lifted from his shoulders. Several factors could attribute to Stanislav fighting how he did, but finally, it appeared that the “Man of the People” was fighting for just that. The People. And now, after a rigorous match, Ivan Stanislav laid in his hospital bed. His ribs taped, a few stitches above his brow..and as he laid there… …he smiled…. ….. and as he smiled so too did the masked man lying in the bed beside was the man that pushed Ivan to the limits... We know why a smile creeps on the face of the Russian Bear, but why is the Mexican Dragon smiling? That answer is quite simple too: He did what no one gave him a chance of surviving let alone doing. He stood in the face of the Red Army, and he is alive while the Army is dead. Was it just time for Ivan to wake up and see the light, or was it because of El Drago that the Russian Bear finally woke and saw the error of his ways? It's debatable but not to him. Not to this man. He lies in his unconscious state knowing that he has earned the respect of one of the most dominant men in professional wrestling. And he earned that by giving his all and not backing down no matter what the odds. He's paid the price but he accomplished his goal, and for these few moments, despite the pain in his shoulder, despite the pain that every breath causes he smiles because he knows he's done good. And still…a nagging question haunts both men… ….what's next?
