Muscle and Mind

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today of Course Bip-Bip...BAM Bip-Bip...BAM Bip bip bam? What on Earth? Bip-Bip...BAM Bip-Bip...BAM A woodpecker? Can't be. Not sinister enough for these purposes. Bip-Bip...BAM A typewriter? Someone at the door? No. Something much, much worse. Armour. The man. Man? Can we get an anatomist's confirmation on that? Tank would be a better term in fact. No, not a Think Tank, one of the everyday, tread-bearing, trample-all-in-my-way variety. Bip-Bip...BAM Armour. Left-left...RIGHT Left-left...RIGHT He pounds away at a boxing speed bag. After all, even tank's need target practice every once in a while. Especially when fifteen other teams of adversaries lie in the way. 'Hmmm...' The proverbial driver of the tank watches on, rubbing his chin, surveying the situation. The driver. 'The Scholar' Howard Porter. Every nuclear plant needs a manager. Without one, limitless power goes untapped. Every hunter needs a gun. Without it, he will starve to death. Only through teamwork is success possible. Only with a combination of the power, and the intellect to successfully harness the untapped potential. Armour and Howard Porter. Power and perception. Muscle and mind. Think and thunk. Er...let's stick with muscle and mind... 'Beautiful,' Porter murmurs a smirk spreads across his lips. Bip-bip...BAM. Like a computer running on a 3.1 Ghz processor, Armour smoothly and cleanly demolishes the speedbag, an ice-cold look on his face. Does he see the visage of Sebastian Toomes plastered upon the leather? Who knows. With Armour, you never know. That's unimportant however. All you need to know is that Armour is brutal, and even worse, he is brutal on command. This could be a blessing if put in the right hands, but unfortunately, the command is in the hands of one individual of the trifecta who have systematically decimated the state of security of Old School Wrestling. Power corrupts? Absolute power corrupts absolutely? Pish-posh. Only because in most scenarios, those in power only received their position from monetary background. They have nothing besides the color green pulsing through their cerebellums. But...but...when power is in the hands of the intellectuals...oh it corrupts absolutely all right, it absolutely corrupts anything and everything set in it's path. 'Splendid. There will be no teams that come even close to matching us, well aside from our fellow Tank teams,' says Porter. Armour stops, turning towards Porter. A single bead of sweat drips down his forehead. His icy stare remains. Porter winks at Armour through his silver spectacles. 'And between you and I, we even hold an advantage over them. But as I stated, that's between you and me.' 'The Scholar' cracks a smile, but Armour's countenance remains stone-cold. 'You know my friend, you can afford to lighten up just a tad. Ed is out of the picture, and Switchblade isn't too far behind. It's obvious no one can stop us,' Howard continues. A seeping breath escapes Armour's lips, as he mutters a single sentence. 'There is always ass to kick.' Porter elicits a smirk at this. 'Well, how can I argue with that,' he concedes. 'No one can argue with results.' Armour nods solemnly. 'You know, I didn't like the way Rhett Locke looked at me the other day,' Porter says, half whining. Armour's knuckles crack as he grinds his fist into his palm, frowning. 'Heh heh, this is better than hearing a live performance of the score from the HMS Pinnefore,' Porter beams. Armour's look remains unchanged. Porter shrugs. 'Perhaps I'll have to pitch that one to Alan.'
