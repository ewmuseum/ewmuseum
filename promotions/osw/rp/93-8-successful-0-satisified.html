Date: Stardate 070102.1 Captain's Log, Stardate 070102.1: My return to squared circle went exactly as my Think Tank compatriots and I expected, except for one little hitch... It happened in Old School Wrestling. The scene opens inside Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman's beige, 1963 Dodge Dart on a dark and gloomy evening. In the passenger seat is the Commissioner of Old School Wrestling, and PHD's long time ally and stablemate, spanning back from their days in the Ultra Wrestling Federation, 'The Scholar' Howard Porter. The rear of the car is occupied by the man everyone thought was dead for oh, so long, the Evil Supergenius himself, Alan Kriegman. PHD squints behind his, black horn-rimmed glasses, as he tries to navigate a foggy, highway 401 North through northern Texas, enroute to Tulsa Oklahoma. Meanwhile, Porter, turns to Kriegman in the back and smiles. 'The couldn't have gone any better, could it,' asks the Porter with a triumphant smile on his face. Kriegman nods, then gives his trademark evil chuckle. 'Indeed,' replies the Evil Supergenius. 'I dare to say it went perfectly.' In the driver's seat, PHD's brow furrows. 'Nothing is EVER perfect,' he says, rolling his eyes. 'This mission was only at 93.8% of optimum performance, which while being more than efficient enough to outsmart our dimwitted OSW foes, and the even more mentally stunted fans in the crowd and at home, it could have been better.' Porter looks at Kriegman, and rolls his eyes. Kriegman pulls out a bottle of scotch and poors a draught into three crystal glasses, which seem woefully out of place inside the threadbare interior of PHD's almost impossibly dilapidated vehicle. Kriegman turns and hands one glass to Porter, then attempts to hand PHD the other glass. PHD rolls his eyes and sighs. 'You KNOW I do not drink anything other than Tang, and Acme brand Creme Soda,' says PHD, shaking his head. Luckily for PHD, the sound of a can being opened rings forth from near Howard Porter. Looking over at Porter, he finds himself looking at a smiling 'Schlolar' who hands him the generic brand soda pop. 'I haven't forgotten, Herbert,' said the Commish, reassuringly. PHD clutches the can in his hand and begins to guzzle, while his other hand stays on the vehicles faded steering wheel. Porter and Kriegman, continue to chuckle about their flawless, well, atleast 93.8% flawless, execution of their plan to completely hold OSW hostage through fear and intrigue. One would think PHD would also be happy. He was once again in the company of old friends causing havok, and had finally returned to the ring. While PHD did appreciate the kinsmanship he had with his fellow geniuses, even if they were still intellectually inferior to himself, something was eating at him... Something was tainted...but what? PHD's train of thought was broken by Porter turning on the vehicles AM radio. PHD mused how it was funny that the thought processes of a supra-genius like himself could be upended by the sudden broadcast of a Domino's Pizza commercial. Shaking off the minor set back, he went back to examining his dilema. It was eating at the former Princeton university professor. What was bothering him? He knew he should be happy...but he wasn't. Then, suddenly, it came to him in the form of a voice on the radio, ironically, at the close of yet another broadcast announcement. 'Make sure you catch all the Fans Wrestling Organization action on Unleashed, tonight at 8 pm Eastern Standard Time, only on the USA television network!!!' It then hit him. PHD stared blankly at the road and blinked at the revelation of why he could enjoy his victory. It was because six months ago, like Luke during his first trip to Dagoba, and for the first time in PHD's life, Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman had failed to achieve something he had set out to do. He failed to win the fWo Survivor competition. Now, the bitter taste of that shortcoming was still so strong, that it destroyed his ability to savor the taste of the incredibly sweet victory he and has comrades scored on Saturday night... ...a taste not even generic brand creme soda could wash away.
