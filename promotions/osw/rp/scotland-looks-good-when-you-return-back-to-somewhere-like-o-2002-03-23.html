Scotland Looks Good When You Return Back To Somewhere Like Orlando...[Part 1]

23 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23.03.02 Bronzed figures. Tanned tourists. Loud music. Sand. Things that tend to be associated with places like Orlando. From a tourist's point of view, maybe. Possibly even a local's point of view as well. But not by a German. Especially not a German like Michel Krieg. When he arrived at Orlando, all Krieg could picture was beach bums with orange skin, overplayed surfer music and dirty beaches. Not exactly the picture travel reps want to give to their customers, is it? The gangway felt rather small to Krieg as he strutted down it, his travel bag in one hand and his German miltiray coat in the other. Nearly scraping the roof, Krieg dwarfed all the other passengers leaving the aeroplane that had taken them from Nuremburg International Airport to this one here in Orlando. Packed with a mix of German's, French, Italian's, Dutch and god know what other nationalities, the plane had landed successfully, delivering the assortment of tourists and business man at their location. Slowly emerging into the airport lounge from the gangway, Krieg stopped and stood tall, closing his eyes and breathing in the air of Orlando. Grimacing and shaking his head in distaste, Krieg began to walk towards the exit when a small Asian man rushed up and went to grab his luggage from him. Looking down at the brightly coloured flannel shirt wearing Asian, Krieg seemed to think the man was trying to steal his luggage and grabs him by the throat, lifting him up in the air. Wriggling like a worm on the end of a fishing line, the small man grabs at Krieg’s hand as he locks around his throat. A small scene of people begin to look on, Krieg’s face filled with anger. “What do you think you are doing, you disgusting American,” roared Krieg. Gasping for breath as he speaks, the man replies, “I..I...I was trying to...to take your bag...for the taxi,” responds Krieg’s ‘attacker’. Still looking puzzled, Krieg questions him some more, “Who sent you? A Capitalist,” asks Michel. “Ja...Jane Reagan,” “As I thought. A Capitalist,” Suddenly dropping him out of his grasp, Krieg lets the small man fall to the floor as he straightens his shirt. Krieg looks at his watch, as it reads 5.14pm, “Well then. Let’s get going. We don’t want to be late for Slam, now do we,” mocks Krieg as the taxi driver slowly pulls himself up off of the floor. “Right. Let’s go...This way, Mr. Krieg,” Krieg and the taxi driver slowly walk away from the scene towards the main doors of the airport, the camera slowly pans out on them, fading to black as they disappear from view.
