Sometimes it Takes Your Managers to Get You Focused

13 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-13-02 There are tow massive planets moving at intense speeds heading towards one another. An unknown force has thrown them from their respective orbits. The two planets are different in nature, but it seems that they are going to share the same fate. Both planets are massive in size. The one is black and cold. The life that once occupied the rock is now dead. It appears cold and callous. It moves at incredible speed and seems that it is fixed for total destruction. The other planet is dark red with flames erupting form the surface. The planet itself is chaotic and in its nature. It would seem that nothing could stop this planet from an apocalyptic end. Finally the planets collide and explode resulting in a force of unfathomable power. The force is so great that the entire solar system is destroyed. Planets are thrown from their orbits and careen into one another. The force extinguishes the twin stars in the system preventing the fusion form occurring. What was once a young a thriving solar system, now lays in waste. From the explosion something else appears. Something more devastating then before. The two planets have now somehow combined into one giant destructive entity. The object has taken on the characteristics of the two planets creating something of unimaginable power the likes of which have never been seen by man. The immense object now propels itself throughout the system under its own power as if it has a mind of its own. As it leaves the system, it leaves waste to anything that may be in its way and it shows no sign of stopping the destruction until their is nothing left to destroy. A cold, but forceful voice booms forth form the darkness of the system. “The path has been set, everything shall be destroyed!” Everything then goes black and fades out. Suddenly the scene changes. The destroyed solar system has been replaced by the old decrepit factory that Azrael Cain calls home. The early morning cold air fills the sanctuary. Debris fills the floors except for a small section in the center of the factory where there is a small fire burning and four bodies laying down sleeping. Azrael Cain is awakened from this dream. He is sweating and in seems to be out of his head for the moment. There is something very feral about the way that he is behaving. His breathing is heavy and sporadic as his eyes move around their sockets wildly. The beast’s massive hand rises and begins to rub his face. After a couple of seconds, Cain finally gets himself under control only to see Hessian awakening across from him in the same manor on the other side of the campfire. As Hessian is trying to gain control of himself, Cain speaks. “Same dream again!” As the masked Hessian is still trying to shake off the dream, Cain tries to explain what is happening. “Relax. You will learn to understand them as you get closer to the Master’s influence. It’s how he communicates to us when we are in this forsaken realm of existence. He can’t finds it amusing. You will get used to it in time.” Hessian rises to his feet and crosses away from Cain. It obvious that the dream has gotten to him slightly. “But what does it all mean? We have been dreaming the same dream over and over again since Sunday night. Why doesn’t he just spill it already. I’m not fond of these games that he keeps playing.” Cain then pulls himself to his feet and stands by the fire. His eyes are fixed at the flickering flames. It seems as if he is hypnotized by the erratic movements of the fire. There is a pause for a moment. Cain finally breaks the silence, but still does not remove his eyes from the flames. “It was the Master that set me free and brought me back to this world as mortal. It was the Master that allowed you to be disfigured and your family to be killed. It was the Master that brought us together. He is not pleased with what we have been doing. That is why he keeps inducing these dreams in us. He wants more.” Hessian is obviously getting annoyed with the cryptic answers that Cain is giving him. “Will you shut up with all the damn Master stuff already! I’m getting really tired of it. I don’t even know why the hell I am putting up with all of this damn stuff in the first place.” Cain snarls at his tag partner’s last comment before he decide to address the situation. “You are putting up with it because only the Master and myself can teach you to harness the rage that you have in your heart. And once you can do that you can get revenge on mankind for what they have done to you and your loved ones in the past. Believe it or not, you are a true warrior deep down inside and only we can expose that side of you.” Hessian turns and looks at Cain for a second. “But don’t forget the fact that you need me! Without me you are stuck with that damn hobo trying to adapt you to this world.” Cain rolls his eyes at the nature of his huge friend. “And don’t you forget what we are capable of doing together. There has never been a force as powerful as the two of us ever in the history of this annoying sport. We can bring our opponents to their knees and destroy everything that gets in our way. Think of the power that we have over the millions of mindless fans that watch this freak show every week. And the more hatred and bloodlust we generate in those zombies the closer you get to obtaining the Master’s end of the deal.” Two smaller figures now begin to stir from opposite sides of the fire. Caleb appears from underneath his trench coat, while on the other side of the floor Minion appears from underneath a ratty old blanket. Both of the managers appeared to be very annoyed with their large comrades. Caleb is the first to address the situation. “Will you tow shut the hell up already? Heh-Heh. Face it, you both are stuck with each other. And like it or not you both have been cut from the same mold. Heh-Heh. Now suck it up and shut the hell up, so we can get some damn sleep. Heh-Heh-Heh.” Minion begins to nod is head in agreement. “Seriously, just shut up already and quit letting your egos get in the way of what we are all really after here. Let’s just get some sleep. From what I can tell, you both need it by the way that you are whining like a couple of adolescent girls. Just leave it alone.” Caleb pulls himself to his feet and waddles in between the two warriors. “I can’t believe that you two are so damn dense that you can’t figure what the hell that dream means. Heh-Heh. He’s telling you two lummoxes that he’s pissed off, that’s what he’s telling you! Heh-Heh. He created the most deadly and destructive duos in the history of time by bringing you two together, but you keep jobbing to those two jackasses, civil Disobedience! Heh-Heh-Heh.” Minion then chimes in. “I can’t blame the big guy for being pissed off at you guys either. I mean seriously, there is no excuse for the two losses that you guys have. You out weigh them by over two hundred pounds, but yet the two of you keep finding a way to lose matches against these little wanna be hoodrats! Sack up and kick their ass already, this is ridiculous. Now quit asking questions about irrelevant things and do something already then maybe we can get some sleep around here. Now quit your damn bitching!” Caleb looks at Minion with a shocked look on his face. “Now I don’t think that I could have said that better. Heh-Heh-Heh.” Minion, feeling triumphant for what he just said, decides to roll over and tries to get some sleep. Caleb looks at the two stunned warriors and addresses them one last time. “Quit asking questions about things that you probably won’t get the answer to. Heh-Heh. Face you both need each other and both of you have a lot to gain by being together. Heh-Heh. Just do something on Saturday, but until then I don’ want to hear anymore of your damn belly aching! Heh-Heh-Heh!” Caleb then returns to his original position on the floor underneath his dirty and stained trench coat in an attempt to get some more sleep. Hessian and Cain remain standing on opposing sides of the fire staring at one another exchanging a dumbfounded look. The men look at each other for a moment then at their managers that have just silenced the two hot headed warriors. Then they look back at each other. More silence. After a while it is Hessian that breaks the silence. “I can’t believe that I just witnessed that. But you want to know something? They’re right. This Saturday, we need to show the entire world and Civil Disobedience that we are a force not to be reckoned with. I think that it is time that we hit these two were it hurts!” A devilish grin grows on Cain’s face as he nods in agreement. “This Saturday, Civil Disobedience is going to not only know what true physical pain is, but we are going to hit them where it is really going to hurt. Together we are going to give them such a beating that their egos will never recover. Trevor Hawke was lucky getting off with just having to get his knee drained. But they are both going to be lucky if they are going to be able to walk ever again!” The two dark figures begin to share a devilish chuckle before they lay down to get some more sleep. Both of them lay down in hopes that the Master’s dreams of carnage and destruction return to them.
