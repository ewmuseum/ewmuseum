Philly Morning Radio Show

7 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-7-02 The air is crisp and chill; a light snow covers the ground of a Philadelphia street. People scampering around trying to make it back inside; vapor rising from the street as the sun hits the snow. This street seems more vacant than anything as the sound of what seems to be a radio announcer is heard in the background. “Good morning, Philadelphia, PA!” The words are screamed. “Wake up you crazy people, today we have a great show on tap for you.” Slowly we begin to see the building opposite to the street. Slowly up the stairs we go and the sounds of “Defy You” by The Offspring just goes off the air. We cut corners and go through doors. “You just heard “Defy You” by The Offspring, that is their new song from the Orange County Soundtrack.” Through more doors. “Today on our show, we have a very special guest.” Down long halls. “You all know him well and he will be showing live this weekend at The Blue Horizon.” Up elevators. “He is a Old School Wrestling wrestler.” Into a room where we see a man sitting behind a microphone. “He is “Bad Attitude” Jesse Ramey and he will be next, right after this next song, “Denial” by Sevendust.” The little light, which reads On Air, flicks off and the man takes a smoke of his cigarette. He looks across the table and begins to motion his hands. “So, tell me, Jesse. How does it feel working for a woman?” He asks. Flipping around to Jesse, “Shouldn’t we be waiting until the show goes back on the air for these questions?” “Well, in a way, I guess we should. Then again this is just for my own humor, so please.” He persists. “Okay, well in all honesty, I’ve never really had to work for a woman before in my life. I have nothing against this, but I really don’t think women should be working behind a desk. They are good for only certain things and some do have good ideas. I’ve never officially met with my boss, but I’m sure I would feel the same about her as I do now.” Jesse says. Rubbing his chin, “Interesting, well, I’m glad I got that question out there. How about Chass? How did you two meet?” Getting a little frustrated, “Dude, don’t you really think we should be saving these?” “No, go ahead.” He exclaims once again. “Okay. We meet back in my hometown of Harts, West Virginia. Her sister was getting married to one of my cousins and we just sort of hooked up from there.” Jesse answers. The little red light flicks back on as “Denial” stops playing. “Welcome back once more, Ladies and gentlemen. We will now take a little bit of time to get some info from the man of the hour, “Bad Attitude” Jesse Ramey. So, tell me Jesse. How does it feel to be working for the OSW?” He asks. Breathing heavily, “Well, in all honesty. It’s almost one of the best places I’ve ever had the privilege to work for. The ring and set-up may not look like much, but the backstage atmosphere and the management are great. This is by far one of the best atmospheres that I’ve ever been in before. The workers are great, as are the wrestlers.” “Now, tell me about this whole backstage video taping thing that OSW has set up. Is there really anything behind this or is it really just the OSW “spying” in on your personal life?” He asks. “Actually, it is just the OSW in a way and a sense. We signed a contract saying that they could do this and well, we really don’t have an idea or clue when this will occur. As you saw not too long ago with my opponent on Slam! He was caught dead in the act and didn’t even know it.” Jesse begins to laugh. “Indeed, that was defiantly a funny promo to watch. However, you were also caught on one of those not too long ago. When Toomes’ girlfriend found his Madonna CD and went on a rampage backstage with it. Now, tell me, was this planned out or did she really gut you in the nads like she did?” He leans in with anxiousness. Laughing, “It wasn’t planned. That was really the way I felt about things. I knew that she was only trying to get back at him, so I tried to make things not work out for her. Then look at what I got. My nads hurt for several hours after that happened.” “Another shocking thing that I would like to bring up is the fact that Matt “Black Ice” Blaze has been having problems. Where he is a cross over wrestler from many other professional sports and certain college sports; he feels that the “boys in the back” don’t wish to have him around the ring at any time. What are your thoughts on the matter at hand?” He asks once more. “Okay, I’m going to tell this to you straight up. I don’t care who you are or what you’ve done in your lifetime. Keep a record of all of your achievements. It doesn’t matter to me. How many titles have you held and what was the record amount of people you had every faced and beat before. None of this means jack to me. The only thing that really matters is if you are able to wrestle. I don’t want to get into the ring with someone who is going to end getting my neck snapped in the end. As for not wanting to work with him? I saw his comments and I’ll tell you one thing Matt. I’ve been wrestling for nearly eleven years now. I just turned twenty-seven and I’ve been doing this since I was sixteen. I’ll work with anyone, but if you put some stupid shit. I’ll not work with your ass again. That’s plain and simple. I don’t think the other boys are willing to give you a chance because they are afraid of you hurting them, but not because your big and bad, but because we’re afraid you don’t know what you are doing and are literally going to hurt us.” Jesse rants. “I’m digging that man.” He comments. “Anything else you would like to talk about before we take some callers?” “Nah, I’m done for now, let’s hit up those callers.” Jesse remarks. “Okay, caller number one, you are on the air!” He says. The sound of static, “Yes, I have a question for Jesse.” “Shoot dude.” Jesse says. “What really went on in the backstage area in CCW?” The caller questions. “Well, in all honesty. I really don’t think I should be handing out that information as of right now, but ever since seeing that Newswire column earlier today in the OSW Magazine I’ve felt like getting it off my chest. I’m sorry though; I’ll have to wait to tell that story a little later on down the road. Maybe you can read about it in an big time column in OSW Magazine someday.” Jesse comments. “Caller number two, the air, is yours.” The talk show host says. “Thanks, Chuck. Hey, Jesse I’ve been a big fan of yours. I’ve actually been watching you wrestle all the way back in the UWL. So, as I know you have turned on people before. I know you are good now, but how long will this last?” The person asks. “In all seriousness, take a look around me.” Jesse pauses. “Who do I really have to turn on if I wanted to? Why would I wish to turn on anyone now? Wasn’t that just a dumb question?” Jesse goes on into a question frenzy. “Thank you caller. Caller number three he is all yours.” Chuck says. “Yes, Jesse, hi my name is Kelly and I was just wondering if maybe we could get together sometime after the Slam show?” She asks. “Sweetie, I don’t know if you took that whole thing with Switchy’s girl to be for real between me and Chass or not. I believe Chass and me may be the only two in OSW that are actually an item. I’m really sorry, but they you never know. Maybe I’ll give you a call later and we could actually go out for cup of coffee or something. Chass usually doesn’t mind if I spend some time with my fans.” Jesse comments in a very nice tone. Looking at the time, “We have time for one more caller and then we will be going to commercial. Caller you are on.” “Hey, Jesse, you dingus! Listen here, this is Seth Masters and I heard about you calling me and my boy Trevor gay. I really found that offense dude and I feel that the only type of person that would sink that low would have be gay himself. You are the epitome of a dicksmack Mr. Ramey. Thank you.” The caller hangs up and nothing, but a dial tone is heard. “I believe that was in fact Seth Masters from the OSW tag team Civil Disobedience and he had some harsh words there for you Jesse.” Chuck comments. With a sound of reassurance, “Yes, indeed, he did have some rude and obscene comments. I believe the only reason he had that to say was the fact that I looked at him and his partner the other night after Slam and told them that they were good wrestlers and then commented on the wedding date. They just looked at me with a sense of anger and I left. I thought they may have figured out that it was a mere joke, but Seth obviously doesn’t catch on to these type of things that quickly.” “Obviously. Now, Jesse, we have to go to a commercial break and when we come back you will be gone. Do you have any final thoughts or closing statements?” Chuck asks. Nodding his head, “Yes, indeed I do. Frankie Bones, Sebastian “Switchblade” Toomes, be forewarned that Slam is only a starting point in my now renewed career. You two are the first men that I will encounter on this long and lonely road to fame and glory, but mark my words. This is only the beginning and that’s not just a promise. It’s the truth. You know why? Because I Don’t Like Your Attitude!” “Down With The Sickness” by Disturbed begins to play as the little On Air sign clicks off and everything slowly turns to blackness. ~END~
