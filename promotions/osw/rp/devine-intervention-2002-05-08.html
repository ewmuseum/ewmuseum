Devine Intervention

8 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-8-2002 “And then Jeezuss, he walked DOWN that mountain! The SAVIOR, our Jesus Christ! Can I get an amen?” The scene fades up on a HUGE auditorium, the seats filled to the brim with people swaying their arms from side to side. The crowd gives off a thundering “AMEN” to the man on stage: a man with impossibly poofy white hair in a fine suit. “Thank you, brothers and sisters of the congregation. The Lord is great indeed, and thanks to the Benny Hinn industries, more and more people are introduced to the Lord’s teachings daily.” Light applause from the audience members greets this statement. The man, Benny Hinn, famous TV evangelist, smiles from his spot on stage. “And remember, none of it would be possible without donations from you people. The shows on the TV, the world tours, ALL OF YOU made the greater teachings of the Lord possible through your contributions!” Louder applause from the audience. “However, there’s a whole lot more people who need the Lord in their lives, so keep the donations up so more people can experience the GLOR-AY! OF JEEEEE-ZUS!” Very loud applause from the audience, with some accompanying ‘Hallelujah’s’. “And now, it is time to let the Lord enter your bodies and CLEAN THEM of the evils of humanity! The cleaning immolation of the Lord’s fire will clean your sins from your souls! ARE YOU READY?” The crowd roars and raises their hands in the air. One set of hands, though, are filled with something metallic. Benny turns around to the choir at the back of the stage. “First the choir! HERE COMES THE LORD! FIRE!” The choir falls backwards in waves. Benny turns back to the audience. “FIRE!” The crowd falls back in waves with the power of Hinn’s verbal fire. However, one person remains standing: the man with the metal in his hands. The man looks at the metal in bafflement, then drops it on the ground. After a couple moments, the crowd has recovered, and the man picks the metal up. Hinn notices this. “Excuse me, sir? Could you come up to the stage please?” The crowd turns to look at the man as he makes his way through the crowd to the stage. As the man gets closer, he see that he is wearing a SOBE~! T-shirt and a pair of khakis. The piece of metal in his hands is the OSW World Title. Hinn smiles before talking to the man. “Hello sir. I couldn’t help but notice that you looked troubled when the Lord’s fires were cleaning your soul. Is there a problem, Mister…” “Troy. Doug Troy. And yeah, there’s a problem. This belt here is still full of sin.” Hinn looks perplexed. “Belt?” Doug lifts the OSW World Title and puts it around his shoulders. “This belt right here. The OSW World Title. It’s still plagued with the sin of medocrity!” Hinn looks even more perplexed. “Um, mister Troy,” Hinn says, the sound of disbelief in his voice. “You want sins cleansed from a wrestling title?” “Well, it’s not the REAL title. If I had the real title, that’d be called stealing and that’d be WRONG. No no, this is a genuine replica of the OSW World Title, sold on the OSWShopzone for only $149.99! Call now supplies are limited blah blah blah.” Benny Hinn blinks, at a loss for words for the first time in about forever. Even the crowd is murmuring confusedly. “Well, what can I do about it, son?” “C’mon, you mean to tell me that the Lord cannot heal the ills of a poor World Title?” “Well, it certainly is unorthodox…” Doug sighs and reaches into his pocket. From it he pulls a huge wad of bills. Hinn’s eyes widen into dinner plates in response. “Okay,” Doug says. “How much is the Lord’s love gonna cost me?” Doug peels a mass of $100 bills out of the wad. “Will $10,000 do?” “Well, um…” Doug pulls some more off the wad. “Okay, $30,000.” “Er, ah…” Doug pulls even more out of the wad. “Fine, $50,000. The Lord sure drives a hard bargain. Actually, here.” Doug pulls two more $100s out of the wad and adds them to Hinn’s wad. “Here’s a little something extra, so God can buy himself something nice on me.” Hinn takes the money, not believing his good fortune today. He counts through the wad, shuddering in happiness. After he counts the money, he pockets it and turns back to Doug. “The Lord thanks you, Mister Troy, and God accommodates EVERYONE who needs help, even inanimate objects like your beloved title belt here. LADIES AND GENTLEMEN! YOU ARE ABOUT TO WITNESS THE LORD’S GREAT POWER AT WORK! TODAY, THERE WILL BE A HEALING!” The crowd roars wildly at the prospect of the Lord healing something, even a metal belt. Hinn continues, building the excitement up to a feverish pitch. “PRAISE THE LORD, THIS BELT WILL BE HEALED TODAY! IT WILL BE CLEANSED OF… of…” Hinn leans in close to Doug and whispers in Doug’s ear. “What exactly is the belt being cleansed of?” “Ace Hawk and Switchblade Toomes.” Hinn goes back to his ‘Praise Jesus’ pose and resumes talking. “THIS BELT WILL BE CLEANSED OF THE EVILS OF HAWKS WITH SWITCHBLADES AND...” Hinn leans in close again. “Anything else?” “The CCW Heathens. Oh, and mediocrity.” “AND THE CCW HEATHENS WHO WOULD WISH HARM UPON THIS POOR BELT! ARE YOU READY TO SEE THE LORD’S POWERS?!” The crowd roars in anticipation. They stand to their feet, holding their arms toward the stage. Doug takes the belt off his shoulders and holds it forward. “I FEEL THE LORD! THE LORD IS IN MY HANDS AS WE SPEAK, AND WITH THIS TOUCH THE HAWKS WITH SWITCHBLADES AND THE CCW HEATHENS WILL BE GONE FROM THIS BELT!” The crowd yells “Hallelujah” in response. Doug smiles as if his greatest wish is about to come true. “FOUL DEMONS OF MEDIOCRITY, WITH THE POWER OF THE LORD, I HEAL THIS BELT OF YOUR EVILS!” Hinn touches the belt and holds it. When he lets go, he quickly yanks it from Doug’s grasp and drops it to the ground. The belt lies there, motionless as Doug looks on expectantly. “Is it working?” Doug asks Hinn. Hinn bends down to examine the belt and touches it. As he does, he gives it a quick shove. He bolts upward. “IT CONVULSES WITH THE LORD’S POWER! LADIES AND GENTLEMEN OF THE CONGREGATION, I HAVE HEALED THIS BELT!” More “Hallelujahs” from the crowd. Hinn turns to the crowd as Doug pulls out a Palm Pilot and starts clicking things with his pen. Meanwhile, Hinn resumes his sermon. “Great indeed is the power of the Lord Jesus. You have witnessed but a minuscule fraction of that power today, so great is it. Through your faith and donations, the Lord may cure you of ills that not even science can cure.” The crowd gives a loud “amen” to Hinn. Hinn smiles as he turns back to Doug, who looks upset. “Brother Troy, is something wrong?” “Switchblade’s still champ. I still have to fight Ace Hawk for the #1 contedership on Saturday…” “Well, the Lord does not condone fighting over material possess IRK!” Hinn’s speech is rudely interrupted by Doug lifting him up and giving him The Take on the stage. Hinn hits the ground and starts convulsing wildly as Doug picks both the title belt and the microphone up. “You lied to me, Hinn! This belt isn’t cleansed! You couldn’t purge the evils of mediocrity from the belt!” At this point, the crowd is just in absolute shock. Doug circles the still convulsing body of Hinn, microphone in his hands. “Looks like the REAL power is going to have to heal the blighted OSW World Title belt! Yes, within my fists, within my boots lies the true power, the power of the Old School! Even now it courses through Hinn’s body, just like it’ll course through Ace Hawk’s and Toome’s bodies! LAWD HAVE MERCY, I WILL CLEANSE THE SUCK FROM THE OSW! CAN I GET A WITNESS?” The crowd remains silent, baffled over the recent course of events that has taken place in front of their eyes. Doug throws the microphone on top of Hinn’s dormant body and starts to walk away. He stops after a couple steps, though, walks back to the body and pulls his money from Hinn’s pocket. “Thanks for the refund, assbag.” Doug walks away as the Benny Hinn show goes to commercial, then fades to black.
