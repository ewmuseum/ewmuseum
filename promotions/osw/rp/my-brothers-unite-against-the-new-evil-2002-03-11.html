My Brothers unite against the new evil!

11 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11 March 2002 The room is an eerie pitch black. There's a dripping noise, as it falls it hits the floor the dullness is sure the floor is stone. But then a cheap light, swinging from the roof on a thick, dirty white cord. It hangs and swings now and again, side… to side, side… to side, side… to side… Then with pace a hand grabs it, the hand is big with thick fingers, and on them are dirty fingernails. There are hairs on his knuckles; the hand span covers the light bulb fully. The hand draws the light over to a short, overweight man. He has blonde combed wig, a moustache that goes all the way down to the chin, bushy eyebrows a grey suite and a white shirt underneath. He has grey tie, he announces in his southern accent. 'My Brothers, we are gathered here today to be told the tale of one man's contact with another.' His what was at first exciting tone drew low. 'My Brothers, I am Eric Sagar, a Preacher from Clarksville, Tennessee.' He proudly projects through the cold, damp, room as a slight echo repeats. 'I… am a good man, I… believe in the lord Jesus.' Eric looks up to the sky. 'He is watching down on your sins,' he emphasis 'your' he continues in a mocking tone. 'My brothers he sees you all watching this so called 'Sports entertainment.' And is disgusted that some many humans waste there lives cheering on Vampir Nosferatu, a satanic Vampire like creature, who is said to defy the laws gave out by the almighty god himself.' He takes a deep breath through his nose. 'So it be said in the good book, the bible. That only one man can, shall and have been re-born on multiple occasions. And that is him, the lord, Jesus.' He bows his head. 'So I've took it upon my powers to send, him to his very near end. But as you may be thinking the bible is against violence. And so are the almighty Propaganda, and myself. So why am I here? Let me tell you the story.' He takes a deep breath and continues. 'One day when I was in Memphis saw this… Wrestling, and I thought why would so many people want to watch excessive violence?' His eyes begin to bulge as he gets angry, and his face goes a shade of pink. 'And Then I saw one man, who drank, smoke, and swore his wrestlers name was Propaganda.' He laughs an evil chuckle, 'You see my brothers one day, Propaganda was caught in a bar brawl. And was sent down to prison for Grievous Bodily Harm onto a mere waiter. He got his head and put it through a window… then got a tray and smack!' Eric makes the movement as if he was smacking someone with a tray. 'He broke a metal tray giving this waiter a comer.' Again he laughs his evil chuckle. He takes another deep breath, and calms down. 'So I went on one of my usual prison visits seeing the scum that I do, because… I am a good man to spend time with the people that are…' he thinks for a second. 'How should I say it? Less… better than me. Yeah, I'm rich, I'm powerful and I have the power of God on my side.' He laughs his evil chuckle, and licks his lips. 'And to my surprise.' He says in an informatively 'I visited the monster, 7 feet tall 350 pounds, that’s right I met Propaganda, and I did all but one thing… And gave him a few reads from the good book.' He takes a deep breath. 'Look at the intelligence around Memento, I should inform you all that The Good Book is in fact the bible not Harry Potter or any of that sort of rubbish.' He breathes out in annoyance, then he says in a quite calm tone. 'He was hooked…' he pauses. 'The greatest power himself was a believer to the greater power, Our lord Jesus and the almighty God!' as he says that line he gets louder. 'So he kept his nose clean, he did all the right stuff and was out within a few months. His federation T…H…W… offered him a place, and he did the good thing he said 'NO' to evil, 'NO' to excessive violence, 'NO' to constant swearing…. But a few months later after anti-wrestling slogans, campaigns and publicity stuns we realised we we're not fighting a lost cause, but fighting in a way which was wrong.' He sighs, 'As the old saying goes, if you can't beat them… join them.' So that’s what we did, we took over THW, and spread the word of god. But what was there left to do? Well myself and Propaganda have come to Old School Wrestling to show the world that we are not a joke, that we are a force… a force to be reckoned with a force that thaw, shall not beat. That thaw shall not hurt. Because ladies and gentlemen…' he evilly chuckles again. 'I bring to you your next Old School Wrestling Champion, Propaganda!' The hand on the light bulb pulls the bulb so the light shines on a face at a higher level then Sagar. This man is roughly shaven, he has glasses, which have a thick black frame, and above his left eye he has a scar that runs from the left side of his eyebrow, just above his eyebrow to the centre of his forehead. He also has gelled and combed back hair. 'Ha, My brothers…' his southern accent is loud and aggressive, 'My Brothers can you feel the power of our lord, of our saviour.' He takes aggressively fast breaths in, and out, in and out. 'In THW I was by far the most talented, the most gifted. But since I found god, I have found myself to be a better wrestler, because no more can I feel pain, no more can I feel the hurt. I am blessed by God, God supports me and I support him along with my brother Eric here. And together in unison standing hand in hand we will have true control of OSW.' He looks at his hand, which is on the light bulb. 'Now it is common fact that after a few minutes of touching on Light Bulbs can be extremely hot, and if you touched them the slightest it could feel like your skin is going to over boil and disintegrate… and I've had hold of this since we started speaking. And I can't feel it. Eric…' Eric gets a folded up blue steel chair he holds it over his head and with a great force he hits Propaganda on the top of the head with it. Eric evilly chuckles 'See he can't feel the pain,' Propaganda stands still normally. Eric then calms down, he says 'Now almighty one, this Vampir or what ever he is a Satanist, he dances with the devil, he is in love with Lucifer, and he is allowed in Old School Wrestling.' Eric is interrupted by Propaganda who wants to get something off his chest. 'Eric my Brother, my contract says I am signed to Old School Wrestling. Notice it says Old School not Satanist Incorporated, I came to OSW, because I wanted Old School Wrestling, like it was in it's prime. I thought a fed with the name OSW would at least have the slightest of standards. And maybe it would retrieve wrestling to its former glory.' Propaganda complains 'Old School Wrestling the motto is even 'The way it's meant to be' it wasn't meant to have drug addicts, Satanists and Thugs.' Eric laughs his evil chuck then implies. 'But Propaganda, these drug addicts, Satanists and Thugs will make destroying them and their souls so much easier.' They both bow their heads and in unison they say… 'A-Men.' On that the scene slowly fades to black, with Eric Sagar having a sick smile and Propaganda with no emotion what so ever.
