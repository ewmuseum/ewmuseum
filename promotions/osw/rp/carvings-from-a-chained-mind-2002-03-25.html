Carvings From a Chained Mind

25 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Mar. 25, 2002 Corg: Why did I do it? Robbie: TRAITOR! Corg: Because if that strap got in the hands of piss-poor communists like Walczak, nobody would ever get a shot at that title. So I thought to myself, “Why not a champion that will put up a god damned fight? And though, I despise you Rhett Locke, I must admit… Corg: You’re one hell of a fighter…but in the end….you will fail at the hands of the Silent Killer…..SOOOOOON!!!! That one scene...that one event on OSW SLAM! has made Rhett's life a living hell the past two days. Whether or not he held the strap that identified him as the OSW International Champion. whether or not the fans liked him, despised him, or didn't give two cents about him... The fact remained, Rhett was playing with a ticking time bomb. tick-tock tick-tock tick-tock Rewind Corg: You’re one hell of a fighter…but in the end….you will fail at the hands of the Silent Killer…..SOOOOOON!!!! Fast Foward The event was slowly dwindling downward into a blur, as most past events do. Though, the more this event played through his mind, the more insignificant it became...the more... Blasé, it became. The future was what he wanted, not the past...it took him long enough to run away from it, never would he take the time to run back. This time bomb, Corg, is quite a sadistic indevidual. How funny is it, that two men so alike are exact opposites? I guess it's Irony's way of a practical joke on humanity. Gullible humanity. *** The OSW International Title shrouded his right shoulder as he leaned back in the recliner, the sound of a television muffled in the background as this phantom of sorts looked down to his feet, and the dusty carpet. From his hotel room, he could hear the sounds of children laughing, birds chirping, water splashing. The pool area was infested with roach like humans, who spread and adapted to their surroundings so easily, that some were perhaps considering moving to this 'beautiful county' Though, as they say... Beauty is in the eyes of the beholder. Rhett's wet hair was brushed down over his face, and his black NOFX t-shirt had red bleach stains in it. Brown eyes glanced up, to the wall, where the following was carved. 'Hector + Maria' Inspiration overwhelmed him, and he stood up. Searching, almost desperately it would seem, for a knife. Soon after tearing off his bed sheets from another room, Rhett raced to the kitchen. He searched every droor and cabinet, until he came across a steak knife. I will make a statement. Rhett slowly stalked to the wall, making sure to step over the strap that represented him as a champion, and moved to the wall. I will free my thoughts. Slowly he carved into that white washed wall, just under the over carving. As carefully as possible he marked an '2' motion, and soon after a 'l'. Other carvings came following. I will show the world... Exclamation mark. What makes me frown. Once his artwork was complete, a wide grin spread across the International Champion's face. Yes, it was evident that Rhett was quite relieved. And as we leave him, we take note what he wrote so precisely on the wall. '2 Lives I've Lived. Cut cut cut cut cut!' As we shut the door behind us, we turn for one last look... Blood oozed from Rhett's wrist. And even more shocking than that? ... The relieved smile that he expressed...
