A Spankathon

3 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/3/02 Introduction- The last Michael Dame was seen was going to the hospital, after a week of not eating, with Gloria. He was as depressed as ever and we're talking about Michael Dame. The man who's been through it all. Although, the rumors circulated that Dame would not be at SLAM! he made a shocking interferance into the 3B tag match. Hardy was about to interfere in the match, and Dame hopped the railing from out of nowhere, pulled Hardy from the ring apron, and SPANKED HIM! A side of Dame you would expect last from his previous events leading into SLAM! Does this mean, he is back to 'normal'? A Spankathon. Part 1 A shot of Michael Dame's estate. His house, and circle driveway is seen. The camera pans down the street and a red Corvette is seen speeding down, and pulls right into the Dame driveway. Out jumps Gloria, once again wearing a long black coat. Her beauty is electrifying tonight. She walks up to the front door without a stutter, and knocks 3 times before ringing the doorbell. ....Ding..... The door swings open and Michael Dame stands there. With simply a pair of sweatpants on, no shirt, and hair in disarray. He looks like he just woke up. Although his face is cold, stern, and once again....depressed. 'Hey...Michael. How have you been?'.. Gloria asks in a confused, concerned voice. '...meh...' Dame replies. He holds the same face looking straight at Gloria's feet. 'Hey...Mike, you alright?' She reaches in to grab him, unquestionably concerned. 'Hey...Back off... I'm fine.' Dame turns towards the innerhouse and walks out of sight. Gloria follows him in. 'C'mon Mike, tell me what's up.' She says as she follows him into the living room. A big screen TV sits, and you can see a reply of OSW SLAM! is playing. It just happens to be on the part where Dame hops over the guardrail resulting in a spanking of Hardy. The crowd erupts, and just as they do, Michael pauses the VCR, and turns towards Gloria. Still standing with a concerned look. 'Do you see this?' A low monotone voice..then suddenly...he bursts out....laughing!.. 'I've watches this countless times today, I mean, look at me. I haven't showered in a day or two, I just keep laughing histerically over this..' Gloria tries to say a word but Dame goes on.. ' But it's not even the part of me spanking him. It's the part in my mind. The part where I remember all the things I tried to do for Hardy.. And then I laugh, and laugh real hard, at the thought of... It doesn't matter. Who gives a crap if Hardy and his Brooylyn Brawler Buddies..notice the 3B..If 3B 'tricked' me.. or whatever... Who gives a shit Gloria!!' He quickly jumps towards Gloria and begins to dance with her... as well as...singing. 'And nothing else matters...' Dame sings the words to the popular rock song, but in a classic music fashion. Laughing and Dancing Gloria finally gets a chance to say something.. 'Oh my goodness, Michael, you had be going there. But, I got one question.' They stop dancing. 'How many times have you actually watched this video?' 'The video? Not once, but this segment...lets just say it's been A Spankathon! They both laugh extremely hard as the camera fades to black.
