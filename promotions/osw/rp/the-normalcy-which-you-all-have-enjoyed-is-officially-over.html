The normalcy which you all have enjoyed is officially OVER.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-5-2002 AD “Well, well, my children, the dark controversial figure that I am, finally returns to this battlefield of a forum that is the OSW. I trust you all missed me? As I sat back and observed the happenings within OSW for the period of time I was away, I could not help but become overwhelmed with the feeling that something is missing. This nagging feeling which continuously gnaws at my heels as I observe fallacy after fallacy continually mock the very core for all of which the dark movement was created; a movement, which has all but ceased, and now, a topic for criticism and ridicule.” The dark, shadowy, and empowering figure speaking, we cannot see, as the darkness within this room overshadows any hint or glimmer of illumination. The air is a bitter and dry cold as chills run up and down your spine, adding to the already pungent smell of what appears to be rotting or decaying flesh permeating through the atmosphere. “Unfortunately, after some of you pathetic entities view this, you will be swept with feelings of sadness and confusion as your worst fear has now become a reality, I have returned. Returned to finish what I started so very long ago, the war against humanity. This time however, I have an ally for whom I have not had the distinct pleasure or opportunity of having for a very long time…time. For you see, the petty and miniscule internal squabbles of the twelve families have since been resolved. Much bloodshed did occur, but the cancers that were the driving force is undermining all that we have fought for are no more. I now have the luxury of devotion, full, undivided, and unrelenting devotion. There are others, which in due time you will be introduced to, that have joined the Dark Army and now stand by me at my side.” A candle suddenly lights up about twenty feet away, which now can be seen as the corner of the room. We can now also observe that the room is constructed of gray stone blocks, worn with the passing of generation after generation. However, along with the mold and mildew, which has accumulated throughout the ages, we can now faintly observe another gruesome substance, forever stained upon these walls, the blood of mortal men. “The key to survival my children, is adaptability. If one’s ways remain unchanged, your enemies will eventually discover and obtain an advantage, which they will use methodically until they begin to secure victory upon victory until such time as the effort is deemed hopeless; hence the term, survival of the fittest. Now, in lieu of this last statement, individuals or rather cancers were given their just and immediate attention and subsequently removed. Those who were sympathetic to the resistance were slit at the jugular, forever embedded into the darkness, where their treason and treacherous ways can do no more harm for the cause. A return to my roots, if this is the correct way to summarize my intentions, is what you all should be expecting over the coming weeks. This materialistic item of superiority for which you all lust and covet is no more an issue in my eyes. This time around my children, the titles that I will lust and covet will be those of how much blood I can spill on one show, how much pain and suffering I can deal to one individual. These are the dark titles that I will become a contender for.” A second candle ignites, on the opposite corner from the first one, illuminating a bit more, the contents of this mysterious room. The dark figure that has been speaking to us slowly becomes a bit more defined as we can see the outline of this seemingly monstrous individual. His skin, skeletal in nature, but heavily massed with the muscles and scars of war. “Now, as the revolution of evolution commences, like Charles Darwin once said many moons ago, ‘only the strong survive…survival of the fittest’, there are certain individuals that I must address. They have had plenty of time to frolic and carry on during my absence. Ample time to achieve the success they so desperately starve for; and ultimately, a generous amount of time to prepare for what their futures hold for them. Soon, and in time, the darkness will begin to penetrate their very existence and cloud their minds, separating their psyche from reality to insanity. They will begin to alienate their allies, as they will find it harder and harder to trust even their subordinate pets, until alas, their cries for mercy and help will fall on deaf ears. Left to fend off their demons all by themselves, in a lonely quagmire of despair and futility. I will eventually tend to my unfinished business, and yes Propaganda I speak of you. Have you enjoyed yourself these last few cycles in my absence? Climbing up the proverbial ladder as you and your little lackey have your way with these inferior souls. Please by all means, dismiss my words as trite as you sit back in your pew while you get satisfaction from your altar boys. Say to yourself, ‘ as the lord god-ah is my witness-ah, I have already defeated this man before-ah, he poses no threat-ah, to me or my career-ah. Can I ah get an amen from the congregation-ah….ah twinny twin twin-ah….Amen-ah. Now send-ah, your donations-ah, to the front-ah, so that-ah, I may get my little boys-ah ERRR, hookers-ah ERRR, parishioners-ah for the night-ah. Brothahs and sistahs can I ah get an amen from the congregation-ah….ah twinny twin twin-ah….Amen-ah’. Propaganda, you are a spiritual and god fearing man are you not? A connoisseur of theology and the bible, yes? Well allow me to put this in a manner that maybe your intellect can comprehend for you much easier. Let us say, shall we, that we put some symbolism into the equation, you know, to allow the hamster to get off his fat arse and spin the wheel within your head. I shall wait a second as your crank-start your synapses so that they may fire properly for I shall only say this once. Picture yourself as the city of Jericho, hooting and hollering as you stand behind your impregnable wall. Saying to yourself, there is no way you can get to us, the laws of war are to our advantage, we are at an elevated position. Now imagine that I am the Israelites, and if you can recall the story, the Israelites for seven days circled around the walls until the seventh day when they played their musical instruments as loud as they could, thus causing that great shield for Jericho to come crumbling down. Ultimately, this event led to the Israelites to slaughter the citizens of Jericho. One battle does not constitute the war my friend.” A third candle ignites in a corner of the room, this time however revealing a corpse nailed to the wall with six inch galvanized steel nails. His face, beaten to the point that his identity remains a mystery forever, as are his arms and legs, scared and bruised. His midsection, however, has had an incision made to it as his internal vital organs are on display for all to see. The man’s large intestine, stretches from the corpse to a table in the center of the room. The man’s heart, pancreas, brain, liver, and other assorted bodily parts lie strewn about this death filled room. The stench is a foul one, mixed in with the damp smell of mold and mildew growing. Drops of water can be heard echoing throughout, until the sound of a person yelling drowns out all others. We then see the reaction of the dark individual as he reacts with an evil grinning smile. “Ahhhh, I see that someone has chosen not to cooperate and are now experiencing the repercussions for such decision, pity. Azrael Cain, after the Ascension pay per view, now knows what the price for failure is. He now sits in a hospital room, nursing and licking his wounds after he was long over due for a retraining session; a minor housecleaning but forward, we move on. Move on, we shall do. What better way to continue my so-called return is to make another appearance, and come Saturday, on Slam, an appearance The Cult shall do. Round and round we go, where will the darkness pierce, no one knows. Who shall be the lucky one come Saturday night as the darkness rolls onto a new venue, and its new victim? Could it be you, Miss Aimz? You and your pretty little red hair, your soul and your fragile mind, all ripe for the reaping. What about you Eric Sagar, Segar, Semen, or whatever else your pathetic entity decides to call itself this week? Do you still sleep at night preaching to yourself that I do not remember the past? All those times you so humbly decided to interject yourself into my affairs, did you once believe that retribution would not come? Now, on to the matter at hand; this Saturday night on Slam, myself and Hessian face off with two individuals who feel that they have an inkling of success against the Cult. However, the time for words has come and gone, actions will definitely speak louder than words. Classic Clay Vendetta, you my child are next in line to welcome the darkness into your pathetic and mediocre existence. You will definitely come to grips with the nightmare that is your reality. You will be perished. So it is written, so it shall be done.” With those final words the final corner in the room lights up as the candle placed there suddenly ignites, now fully revealing the figure that is Vampir Nosferatu. A figure now more sinister looking than prior viewings. A figure, that the battles throughout the course of time has failed to cease. A figure, that will now begin to penetrate every facet of your mind, fears, families, and imagination. The baby sleeps……rouse it from its slumber. The children play in the courtyard….time to play as well. An old man sits in a retirement home, wasting away as he waits for the next woodworking class to begin….answer his prayers and allow me to end him of his misery. A soul in the name of Classic Clay Vendetta has been placed before me….a martyr he shall become. The roster stirs…time to welcome back the innovator of controversy, because after all, it all seems empty without me. A girl speaks on the phone, saying her hizies and byezies….let’s penetrate her mind and devour her from the inside out. The world goes on….time to spread fear, death, and unadulterated mayhem. Ladies and Gentlemen, you have been forewarned, you will not be prepared for what you are all about to experience.
