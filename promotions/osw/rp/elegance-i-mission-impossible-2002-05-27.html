Elegance I: Mission IMPossible

27 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27-05-02 “Why did he drag us out here?” Cain queries as a purple object flurries past both Hessian and Cain and Caleb deep into the shopping mall. “The way of the Imp is a mystery to all. He doesn’t even seem phased by that incident on Slam!” Hessian replied, strolling down the marble floor alongside his fellow Heretic and homeless accomplice. “There better be a pharmacist around here somewhere, I needs me some bandages and lotion and stuff…” Caleb groans, hobbling along with the help of a gnarled old staff. Heresian Bloodlust and Caleb make their way through the busy mall, passing children and senior citizens, who glance at the OSW Tag Team Champions. Fame has changed the public’s view, and instead of the sarcastic comments and snide remarks, people are now, rather than booing and hissing as obligatory in an arena, cheering and yipping at the sight of the Tag Champions in person. “Seems we have a bit of a following outside of the ring…” Cain motions to a group of Goths brooding in a corner, a couple of them sporting ‘Heresian Bloodlust’ merchandise. “When the Hell did we get merchandise?” Hessian cuts in, and instinctively both Hessian and Cain look to Caleb, who looks back and begins shaking in frustration at the implied allegation. “I DIDN’T DO BUGGER ALL!!!” Caleb shouts, catching the attention of a few senior citizens who sneer at the old hobo. “Ok, so I “borrowed” a little money…so sue me! I’m clean, I don’t steal or manipulate or anything! I’m the last person that would steal from you! Heh…heh…heh” “That didn’t stop you stealing from Cain anyway…” Hessian adds, then nods down the mall, noticing the crowds parting suddenly as something rushes through them. People yell out and scream, and Cain, Hessian and Caleb stop as before them the Imp they call Minion skids to a halt. “HURRY!!! HURRY!!!” Minion squeals at Hessian, who cocks his head in confusion at the little 3’11” purple Imp. Hessian looks to Cain, who shrugs, and with a lowly sigh, Heresian Bloodlust follow Minion through the crowds. Minion rampages through everyone, seemingly in some sort of a trance, as Cain and Hessian and Caleb follow, not knowing what to expect. “Can we really afford to be chasing this fool of an Imp around when we should be preparing for Slam! next week?” Cain asks, as the Heretics struggle to maintain the shocking speed of Minion. “It’s only Monday, Cain. We will have plenty of time to prepare for Saturday, and remember whom we are fighting and who we be. We are the OSW Tag Team Champions, and in a Table match again Da Westsidaz and Civil Disobedience we cannot lose…remember we fought Twisted Metal, 3B and Ramey and Dame in a #1 Contender Table Match…the same match minus a team comprising of a transsexual and a generic fool…it won’t be hard. The worst that can happen is we are roughed up a little. Mind, we fell 30ft from that damn scaffolding on Slam! yet we’re still walking.” explains Hessian, when suddenly Minion turns and lunges at Hessian. “THERE!!! THERE!!! I FOUND IT!!! LOOK!!!” screams Minion, and leaps off of Hessian and lands directly in front of his find. Heresian Bloodlust and Caleb stare across at the rather large, lavish looking store. Minion stands hazy-eyed, gazing into a world of lace, silk and crotchless wonders. “Minion, what in tarnation do you want with lingerie?” Caleb asks, looking up at the large, golden arches around the entrance, inscribed into which is the name of the store, “Elegance”. Minion’s eyes water, his tongue rolls, and all those dirty little Imp thoughts run wild in his head, as Hessian and Cain watch on in bewildered amusement. Caleb’s eyes light up, but he remains at Cain’s side, for fear of ridicule. “There is a god…” Minion whispers, which catches the attention of Hessian, but before the Goliath can comment, Minion has dashed inside of the store, and a loud, high YIP echoes out. Hessian looks to Cain, and Cain to Hessian, and they stroll into the store on the hunt for a horny Imp. Inside, Cain, Hessian and Caleb’s eyes widen as the lingerie engulfs them like a deep fog. Panties to the left of them, corsets to the right there they were, stuck in the middle of it all. Around the store Hessian could hear Minion squealing with delight. “Ok, this could be tough. We’ll split up, it won’t be that hard to find him, follow the saliva.” says Hessian. “Wait…why is Minion even in this place?” Azrael asks, seemingly confused about this new and mysterious place. Before Hessian can reply, Caleb jumps up and gasps, remembering Slam!. “I know! Minion said something on Slam! about a Firesnatch~! I didn’t know what he meant exactly, but then I saw Reno Davis just after Slam! and it all clicked into place…Minion is gay!” Hessian is quick to reply, defending his Imp. “Minion is many things, but Minion is not a homosexual! I know all about this Firesnatch~! though I’m not sure anyone other than those that know of it should know…god forbid Minion gets hyper about it…” “WE CANNOT AFFORD TO WASTE TIME CHASING THIS DAMNED IMP WHEN THERE ARE FOUR MEN READY TO DIE AT OUR HANDS!!!” Cain snaps at Hessian, who cocks his head at his partner, his red eyes glowing a little more intensely. “Hey…unlike you I don’t despise my accomplice or manager of whatever you want to call them. Minion is damned important, so you will help me find him…especially after Slam! I’m reluctant to let him out of my sight any more…so help me find him!” Hessian hisses at Cain, and the three take off through the store looking for Minion. Hessian makes his way along an aisle stocked with brassieres of many shapes, sizes and purposes. Cain and Caleb meanwhile head down the main aisle, looking into various aisles on their way down. As Hessian walks down the aisle, he notices the shiny deposit of saliva resting on a 32cc edible bra. “The little sonofabitch…” Hessian whispers, as he leaves the aisle, but notices a rather colourful aisle, sporting rubber items and straps and many other ‘foreign’ objects. As Hessian strolls into the aisle, he looks at the shelves and comes to the conclusion……… “This is bondage…” Hessian’s eyes light up at the menagerie of bondage gear, everything to suit the needs of the most perverse individual/s. Looking around the shelves for evidence of Minion, Hessian feels something tugging at his long, black cape. He turns around expecting to find Minion, but instead finds himself staring into the eyes of an 83-year-old woman with a Zimmer frame. “‘Scuse me sonny, can ya tell me where I might find the ‘lubricant-assisted’ aisle?” Hessian’s eyes widen and he slowly backs off, for even the most ferocious of Heretics have their limitations. Elsewhere in the store, Cain and Caleb are searching for Minion, delving into the aisles to take a closer look at some of the stock available. Cain cocks his head in wonder as he enters an aisle full of Garter belts. “What in the name of the Master are these things?” Cain asks, looking over the lacy garments. “Those are Garter belts…they’re man’s worst enemy!” Caleb replies, snickering behind a dirty hand. Cain simply shrugged and walked through the aisle, and yelled out as a Purple object flashed past the aisle down the store. “It’s Minion! Get him!” Caleb cried out, and the two sinners began chasing the Imp, trying to keep up with the purple wonder as he raced throughout the lingerie store. Strangely enough, clerks and assistants turned away, seemingly not bothering as two massive, satanically attired men, a hobo and a purple Imp run wild through their store. Over the aisles Hessian spots Cain and Caleb chasing something through the store. Grinning, but still frustrated, Hessian dashes past the old horny woman down the aisles. Watching as Cain and Caleb chase Minion along the aisles, Hessian changes directions, running through various aisles to intercept the Imp. Slowly closing in on each other, Cain and Caleb reach within grasping distance of Minion, when suddenly the Imp looks forward and shrieks as out in front of him steps the 7’2” black mass of Hessian. Minion skids to a halt before the T.S.I. and Caleb and Cain behind him. “Minion! What is the meaning of all of this?” Hessian inquires, as all four members of Bloodlust try to catch their breath. Minion pulls out a shopping bag from under his cloak and opens it out in front of Hessian. Hessian gags and pushes it close to Minion’s little body again, closing it over. Sighing, Hessian points to the cash desk, and Minion skulks away, secretly harbouring a grin as wide as Jane Reagan’s legs. Cain looks at Hessian oddly, as does Caleb. “Hess, what the hell does Minion have in that bag?” Caleb asks, trying not to laugh. “All I know is Firesnatch won’t be happy…I guarantee that…” Hessian said with a certain measure of grief in his deep voice. Cain, Caleb and Hessian watched as the now deep purple Imp stood at the cash desk, watching as an elderly woman removed the items from the bag, passing them through the scanner. Minion smiled again, as the clerk removed something eerily electric looking. “That Imp ain’t right…” Caleb groaned, his attention suddenly being drawn to a shelf of “attractants”. “Br right back…” he added, and zipped off to the shelf. Minion paid the clerk and ran back over to Heresian Bloodlust, dragging Caleb back as he ran. Stopping before Hessian and Cain, Minion coughed a little and looked the Heretics in their angry faces. “Minion…why?” Cain asked, in a simple, yet violently enticing voice. “Well………Special gifts for a Special lady?” Minion said shyly. “Oh, and good luck in your Tag Team Championship Match!” Minion added, before running off out of the shop, on the path to ‘total enlightenment’.
