Indecision

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2002-07-31 Lillith was livid. She stood in her living room staring down at a sheet of paper in her hand - this weeks card. Right at the top in bold letters, it read: Lillith vs Bethany Yamato 'What the hell?' she asked allowed, 'Why are they treating me like this?' She crumpled the paper into a ball and threw it across the room. This isn't the way things were supposed to go. Things were suposed to go forward, not backwards. And this wasn't backwards, this was a damned fall from a 10 story building. Lets reveiew for a minute. Two weeks ago: Lillith vs Propaganda, OSW Internationl Championship Match. Last week: Lillith vs Casey vs Vampir vs Propaganda, OSW International Championship Match. This week: Lillith vs Bethany Yamato: curtain jerker. 'CURTAIN JERKER!' she screamed out. 'And against Bethany of all people! I already beat her, goddammit! Pinned her ass 1-2-3! Took the win that I earned! I fought for! And now? Lets dig up the past and have them go at it again. What's the fucking point in that???' Lillith punched a wall, hard enough to crack the drywall. She suddenly, and amazingly, smiled. And punched the wall again, widening the crack. A third punch, and her hand went right through. And the smile twisted, no longer a happy smile.... 'Poor Bethany...' Lillith whispered. 'It's not her fault. But she's the one who'll have to suffer for it....'
