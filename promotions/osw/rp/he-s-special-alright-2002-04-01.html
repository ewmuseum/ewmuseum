He's ''special'', alright.

1 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 1st, 2002 With the first rays of light streaming in through a cheap, fifth story hotel window, our redheaded 'hero' of sorts woke up... She was alone in the bed, as expected...thinking about little else than completely and utterly decimating Drew Hostyle in any way that was humanly possible. Amy could take him out if she played her cards right...maybe even by herself! He was a talentless hack who relied on his 3B comrades, mainly Switchy, to get any sort of heat whatsoever. Who was Drew Hostyle? The guy who stood behind Sebastian Toomes and Frankie Bones at photo shoots...the guy who stood behind many men, in many different places...some of the time posing for photos, the rest of the time posing for amateur video tapes that sell for twelve cents American on eBay. That's who Drew Hostyle was...a Mariah Carey wannabe with shorts and a voice just as high. How'd Amy know anything about how high his voice could go? Sadly, she had walked in when Breanne was watching one of the various ''Brooklyn Bi and Bred: Dark Meat with Backdoor Delivery.'' tapes...the same ones that were selling like hotcakes all over the net...purchased by men the likes of Glen Pierce, Matthew Henstock and Tim Walden. Andrew Copeland...Drew Hostyle or, as Frankie Bones had said, ''Oh baby, harder!''...it didn't matter what the fuck you called him, he was a marked man. Okay, so being the target of a 141 pound girl's aggression wasn't exactly life-threatening...but add that girl's friends to the mix and you might find yourself in a little bit of trouble. Not like Aimz couldn't hold her own anyway...she had proven that she could take on somebody Hostyle's size, usually her opponents had a bit more talent but who was she to be complaining? Little Amy was getting the retribution she wanted... What retribution? Hostyle hadn't done much to her...but Amy had watched 3B pick on a girl who wasn't even as big as she was. Tempest had plenty of spark to her, but obviously she wasn't any match for two men that equalled to around six, seven maybe eight times her weight...especially after they had taken the object of her affections unceremoniously with a fire extinguisher. Sure, Aimz wasn't the friendliest girl alive...but she, and just about any other real woman in pro wrestling, could relate to poor Tempest's situation. Her relationship with Rent-A-Hero only caused the feeling to grow worse, thoughts of Amy possibly being in the same situation, maybe even at the iPPV, were filling her head rapidly...much to her obvious dismay. Timo wasn't to be left out, either...Meanstreak had practically destroyed both the Samoan Silencer and Amy herself last Saturday and the wounds were still healing. Even now, she couldn't do so much as crack her knuckles without wincing from the bruises and she suspected that her proverbial partner in crime was facing the same general pain. The poor guy would be putting up with even more this coming week...he was to face Meanstreak in a steel cage, for Christ's sake. If Reagan wasn't dumb enough as it was, she went and put one of Amy's best friends, her tag team partner in a match like that. Something was going to happen to that woman...be it at the hands of Amy, Tempest or both...Jane wouldn't be making matches like this for much longer and it's safe to say that OSW's two other leading ladies were dead set to make sure of that. But she tried to momentarily put these thoughts at the back of her mind...that was quickly achieved when she heard an odd crashing sound coming from the shower of the unusually posh hotel room... Amy quickly darted up from the bed, finding herself in a small red tanktop and flannel pyjama pants. At least she had decided to sleep in something warm the night before! ''Timo?'' No response. She had to call his name first...Rent-A-Hero was away for PCW, and Bolamba was the only other person who'd ever dare to enter her room this early in the morning. But it wasn't him...well, if it was then he was either drunk, or had taken 'candy' from Breanne, because all Amy could hear were roars and beastly groans that would rarely be heard outside of a Star Wars movie. So she stepped a little closer to the bathroom, where all the noise was coming from...making sure to flick open the small steel jack-knife she always kept close. ''Uhhh...'' Aimz chose her words carefully as she crept nearer. Whatever was making those sounds obviously wasn't in the best condition, so 'What the fuck are you doing in my room, dicksmack!?' might not get a very friendly response. ''...who's there?'' No response. Amy didn't step a single foot into the room before she reached around the door frame and flipped the switch to turn the light on. The moment the bulb lit, OSW's true (and proven) redhead was scooped up in a crushing bear-hug by none other than Timo's favorite man-child, Eugene... ''RED!!!'' He gripped Amy to his chest, her feet now not even near to touching the ground, with one hand while wrapping strands of her hair around and between his meaty fingers with the other. Of course, Aimz had no options here...she couldn't squirm, speak or even THINK of using the knife to free herself in case she somehow set off the monstrous boy...if that happened, she was guaranteed dead before she could even fall to the floor..........and it was a long way down. Her breath came in short-lived bursts of air that passed between clenched teeth...this definitely WAS NOT a comfortable, nor safe situation to say the very least. She could only pray that Eugene tired of her hair's natural color soon. But then a degree of salvation came in the form of Timo's voice...that same slight accent shouting out in the hallways, searching for the lost 'man-child' who he had to care for if he had any hope of controlling Meanstreak in the ring. Amy was dropped to the hard tiled floor as soon as Eugene heard the Samoan's voice...and she was thankful, though now in a lot more pain than she would ever be willing to handle on a more than weekly basis. ''Eugene!'' Timo tried as hard as he could to look like he was happy to see the beast, though he was mostly just thankful to find the boy before he could do any major damage. ''Timo!! I find red!!! I SPECIAL!!!'' The mountain of a 'man' spoke as proudly as ever. Bolamba still hadn't found Amy, who was still laying, gasping on the bathroom floor. ''That's good, buddy...but can you do me a BIG favor and go back to the room we came from for a second? I'll be back with breakfast for you if you do...'' ''OTAY!!!'' With that, Eugene had skipped out of the room...Timo could feel his girth rumbling the floor as he ran down the hallway, the Samoan waited to move away from the door until he knew the boy had reached his room...that came when the shaking earth stopped. Good, now to find his partner...which he quickly did. ''Amy!'' He had rushed to her. ''Are you alright?'' She had to be allowed a moment to gather herself, but Aimz finally spoke, looking up to Timo with eyes that were desperately trying to hide how sore she now was. ''Yeah.........just had to...uhhh...catch my breath.'' ''What happened?'' ''I heard him growling or whatever from the bathroom...so I walked in and he decided he wanted to get a closer look at my hair...'' ''He likes red.'' ''I noticed.'' She tried to give him a half-hearted smile. ''Nothing broken, just try to tell him not to hug me...'' The Samoan Silencer chuckled as he helped her up from the floor. ''Don't worry, I'll mention it.'' Amy managed to return his chuckle with one of her own. ''Yeah...I tag with one giant retard, two is a little much.'' ''And I tag with a bitch,'' He stuck his tongue out, sometimes he was a little less than mature...but it was still cute, almost reminded Amy of her big brothers. ''...so Eugene's a refreshing change of pace.'' They were relatively quiet for a few moments, using idle chit chat about Rent-A-Hero and music among other things as a filler...but Aimz quickly changed the course of the conversation into more serious territory. ''About Slam...'' She was a little nervous about mentioning a fear of Timo's well-being...he could be very confident sometimes, and possibly offended. ''I'm...well.........a little worried.'' He looked nearly ready to burst into a fit of laughter. ''About Hostyle?!'' ''No...about you.'' Amy continued, still treading lightly. ''...even with Eugene, Meanstreak's a big, tough, man...'' ''That's why I was gonna ask you not to come to ringside...'' That statement from Bolamba sort of surprised Aimz, who was used to people like her, who got viciously defensive whenever someone brought up being concerned for their safety. ''Alright, I'll stay in back...'' She was slightly relieved, but knew she would defend her friend...it was an instinct. ''But if you get into trouble, and big foot there can't get you out of it, I'm gonna be down there in a split second...monster or no monster.'' Amy patted the big Samoan, who was still pondering how to keep incidents like this morning's under control, on the back and smiled, trying to reassure him slightly. Hopefully, she wouldn't have another friendly, or non-friendly, confrontation with Eugene...and hopefully Timo wouldn't end up as just another stain on Meanstreak's boot. Lots of things were hopeful...now all that was left to see would be how many of these hopes could pull through?
