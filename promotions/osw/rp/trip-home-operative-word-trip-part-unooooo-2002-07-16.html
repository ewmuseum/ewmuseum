Trip home...operative word: TRIP. (Part unooooo)

16 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 16th, 2002 A rather unexpected visit from OSW's resident Russian Bear left Amy Campbell in quite a state of shock the afternoon following Slam. Both had been in the same hospital, which wasn't a fact Aimz was aware of until Stanislav lumbered into her room a mere hour beforehand. She had yet another week off to safely relax and heal up, which was nothing short of a Godsend on Jane Reagan's part. The plans were to go back home so she could celebrate with friends and let Reno meet her parents, an event that she didn't exactly look forward to but had to claw her way through none-the-less. Courtesy of Corg, Aimz was, and had been, stressed out 'to the max' for the past several weeks and, as was proved when she heard the news of the 'Implied Consent' clauses, things weren't about to get any better. 'Thank God for Ivan Stanislav,' She sat there, awaiting Reno's arrival, thinking as she finished off the first flask of grand ol' Russian vodka the giant had given her, '...thank God for Russians and their alcohol. Hell, thank God for ANYBODY and their alcohol.' The truth was, she really did need the booze. The Russian Orthodox cross was slung on the uncharacteristically dainty silver chain around her neck and it WASN'T there because of the alcohol...Corg was probably hunting her at that very moment, Reno was late coming to pick her up...the plane, naturally, would leave without them. It'd been quite the month...quite the day, even...she just wanted to go home, stay with Davis and her parents for a few days and just leave her problems, including stalkers, at the door. After all, it was the very least God could give her after these last few weeks in what could easily be likened to hell, right? Even though it was doubtful that the next few days could be peaceful, Amy was officially hell-bent on forcing them to be that way. Bad things ALWAYS happened just prior to or on the day of her birthday, well not anymore! She'd suffered concussions, beatings, broken bones and several instances of 'Get out of my life!'s on birthdays of the past, but not yet this year. She had Reno, little ZaC, her friends back home...it would be PERFECT. The only OSW-associated people who knew where she'd be were The Elite and, of course, Davis...it was doubtful any of them would be running to tell Corg where they were going. Her moods were swimming out into some ocean beside her mind, if that hadn't become apparently already. It was partially the vodka but mostly just the fact that, every night for the past few weeks, she'd been 'sleeping' with both eyes opened. She was finally drifting off now, though... What's up? Are you alive, will you listen to me? I'm gonna talk about some freaky shit now... Someone is gonna die when you listen to me Let the living die, let the living die... Wow...Stanislav had in on some strong stuff. Either that, or she'd been listening to way too much Disturbed... Are you breathing now? Do the wicked see you? You still breathing? You're making me known... What's up, I wonder why do you listen to me? I'm gonna make you do some freaky shit now Insane, you're gonna die when you listen to me Let the living die, Let the living die... Uhh...right. Can't you imagine how good going through this will make you feel? I promise, no one will ever know... There will be no chance of you getting caught... He won't stop anyway. So come on and do what you are compelled to... She was shaken abruptly from the trance-like cat nap... ''Amy, you alright?'' It was Reno, thank God. ''Mmm...uhh...yeah?'' She was groggy at first, how long had she been asleep for? ''Oh, yeah...fine.'' Thinking back to the little dream, she'd assured herself it was just David Draiman's voice stuck in her head, she'd been listening to Disturbed earlier in the morning, so it made perfect sense. There was one part that didn't make sense, though... The last few lines being spoken into her ear hadn't been from any song. That certainly left something to be pondered, but Davis snapped her out of her own little world yet again, this time with an increasingly urgent tone. ''What's wrong? You look sick or something...and smell like.....wait, is that booze?'' She could tell he was ready to laugh, it was JUST like her to have snuck alcohol into a hospital. ''Damnit, that stuff won't help that cut heal any faster.'' ''Ivan gave it to me,'' Aimz mumbled back to him as he helped her up out of the chair, keeping her sore arm pinned tightly against her to restrict any movement. She'd obviously puzzled him, ''Ivan who?'' ''Stanislav.'' ''Ivan Stanislav came in to YOUR room and just handed you some booze and a pat on the back?'' She'd been totally serious, looking straight into his eyes...but all Reno did was laugh and put his arm up over her good shoulder, holding her against him. ''You really HAVE been drinking!'' ''No, really.'' She gripped his arm, trying to hold his semi-attentive gaze. ''He did...and he apologised too!'' ''Ivan Stanislav apologises to no one, you know that.'' He kissed her forehead, tossing her explanation off an an alcohol-induced daze. ''C'mon...you can get some sleep on the plane.'' Amy just gave up trying to explain, mostly because even she still doubted that the meeting with Stanislav had ever happened. Things had been weird lately, and today had only made life weirder... Leaning against Reno, she left the hospital, finally able to see the unadulterated light of day for the first time in over a week. Within a few hours, she'd be home and maybe, just MAYBE, things would get back to some semblance of normality.
