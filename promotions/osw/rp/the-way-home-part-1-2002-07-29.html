The Way Home, part 1

29 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/29/02 Thunder rumbled slowly as the clouds rolled in over the city like a steam roller on it's inexorable course. Lightning flashes lit the night sky in competition with the bright lights and the neon glow of civilization. Like ants running before the hands of mere children, the residents of SoHo scurried about in the street, trying to escape the oncoming storm. All but one... Tempest watched the scene below from her balcony and glanced nervously at the threatening clouds. She took a deep breath and pulled her sparkly red sweater tighter around her shoulders. She glanced over her shoulder at the warm, bright safety of her apartment and tried to resist the urge to run back inside. Instead she turned her face to the wind and let it whip her hair back. It stung her eyes, but she faced it nonetheless. 'I wonder if the wind is this cold in Russia right about now,' she said quietly. She paced along the edge of the balcony, watching the clouds grow nearer. She shivered, but stood her ground. I should have known what fury you'd feel when I showed up. It's like the storm, something I can't even hope to control and just as terrifying. It's hard for you to understand fear because you've felt it so rarely. But I've lived with it a lifetime. You did see a part of me that you didn't know existed. For all the people who joke and call me a little girl, you actually saw her. And she did what little girls do: run away and hide. I've never run from a fight. Injury doesn't scare me. It's the things that overpower me entirely that are really frightening, things like the storm and like a man I barely understand, but care about more than anything, and suddenly, I can't trust him anymore. Where does one lie end and the next begin? And how to tell? ~ Ivan: YOU WISH TO NOT GIVE IVAN TIME TO EXPLAIN?! THEN LEAVE! Ivan points over Tempests' shoulder, standing suddenly very defiant. Tempest sniffles backing away. Tempest: I will... you've already explained plenty. Your lie started this... and now it's the end of it. ~ It was terrible feeling my trust in you crumble, like the ground under me was suddenly shifting away from me. Like an unraveling web in which one knot, the truth of this one incident, was undone, and the rest simply couldn’t hold. One lie lead to another doubt and another and so on until I stared at you as if you were a total stranger, and had no idea what to believe anymore. ~ Tempest: No! I've heard enough of your lies! What else are you lying about? Maybe something important, like even caring about me! Ivan grits his teeth. Ivan: I DO care for you Tempest!! Will you listen to yourself!! I am NOT a liar....it is not my fault!! I would not have done that had I not cared for what he have! Tempest: What we have is based on that lie! It's all a lie! ~ It's not the mere fact of being lied to. I had been lied to time and time again. But I wanted so much to believe you. I was willing to trust you to the end of the Earth, and suddenly all the words I had thought myself brave for ignoring, came back to me in a rush, telling me that indeed I had been used, once again. ~ Ivan growls as Tempest manages to get free. She tries to rush past him but he whirls around and grabs her arm, almost jerking her off her feet. The crowd in the arena boos loudly. Ivan: I will NOT let you leave! What we have is based on something...something great Tempest!! Something bigger than both of us!! Tempest looks panicked as she struggles in Ivan's iron grip. ~ I was never truly scared of you until that moment. But was it because I couldn't escape, or because I knew you were right? I didn't want something bigger than both of us. I was just barely getting comfortable with something bigger than myself. ~ Tempest: LEAVE ME ALONE!!! I NEVER WANT TO SEE YOU AGAIN!!! Nate: (solemnly) Oh man… With her eyes puffy and tear streaked, she jumps in the back of the limo which screeches and peels away. Ivan rushes after the car, which is far too fast for him to catch. He then falls to his knees and lets out a mighty roar. NYYYYYYYYEEEEEEEETTTTTTTTTTTTTTT!!!!! ~ I know you'd like to think it was easy to leave, but it wasn't. In fact it was one of the hardest things I've ever done. I thought it would be easy. I thought I would immerse myself in work and touring and appearances and I'd have no time to remember. But I was wrong, as I stared into the darkness wide awake night after night, and cried when left to myself for too long. Eventually I pushed past it and got myself going again. Of course that was until the fWo sent me on a promotional trip... to Moscow. I'll have to tell you about it sometime, if you ever listen to me again. I hope it's soon. I was glad to hear you say my name again, regardless of the doubt that clouded it. You asked about why I returned, but did you listen when I answered? I never imagined that the army and the homeland you fought for and held so dearly would abandon you. I thought I was the tiny cog in the machine, that wouldn't be missed. But now... I know I assumed wrong. And I don't know what I can do now, but I can be here. Maybe this time I can weather the storm and face up to my fears enough to stand by you. I know you wouldn't understand, but maybe being here will someday be enough. Or maybe I'm wrong. For the first time ever, you really looked scared to me. Maybe you can understand more than I guessed, and surprise me once again. I hope so. I hope I won't be washed away in this particular storm. But I'm willing to risk it. Tempest's face was wet as the wind finally died down and the angry skies settled into a calm blanket of blues and purples. The street below was soaked and the balcony was peppered with raindrops. Tempest turned her crystal blue eyes to the sky and a trace of moonlight reflected from the rain on her face. Or was it tears?
