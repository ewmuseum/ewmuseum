Umirat.... (Pt 2. Dialogue)

18 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-18-02 Every time he closed his eyes… he saw the pained, teary faced expression of Tempest. And when he watched last week, Stanislav saw that same look in her face as she fought Corg. Corg would be spared no mercy… Ivan reaches down over the top rope and grabs Corg’s short hair in his iron grip. “GRRRUUUUGGGHHH!!!” he roars as he lifts the extremely light Corg off the ground, over his head, and releases, not caring what happens to him. Rage burns through his body as he turns and sees Corg crumpled on the mat. “You will pay for what you did to her, little man!!” he screams, again the cheers of the crowd drowns out what Ivan says, but he knows Corg can hear him. The mat rumbles as the massive frame of The Russian Bear rushes towards Corg and tosses him into the corner. For one moment, time freezes and Stanislav sees Corg’s terrified eyes staring at him. He grins sickeningly, he rears back, and slams his fist into Corg’s chest. The tiny chest of Corg feels like water against his hammer fists as Ivan rears back and hammers into him six more times. With every blow he feels a bit better, a bit more intoxicated, satiated. ~~ Nate: Those fists of Ivan’s are as big AS Corg’s chest!! He’s a man possessed!! ~~ On the last blow to the chest, blood wells out of Corg’s mouth, “Taste your blood, Corg, learn how it feels to taste your fear in your MOUTH!!!” Ivan palms Corg’s face and lifts him into the air, and then sends him speeding into the mat! He bends down, it feels good to see Corg laying on the mat. “GET UP ANIMAL!! GET UP AND FIGHT A MAN!!” He screams as Corg groans loudly. Ivan rounds Corgs body and sees his tiny hand on the mat… That hand had touched her. That hand had defiled her… With all of his weight, Stanislav stomps down on the hand of Corg, driving the heel of his military issued boot into the top of his hand and crushes it. “Does that hurt little boy?? Does that hurt?!?!” ~~ Steven: I don’t think I’ve ever seen Ivan like this…not even with Reno Davis… ~~ His eyes scan the prostrate form of Corg, he is at his mercy, of which he would get none. The Russian Bear raises his boot behind Corg’s exposed neck and slams down. He hears him cough, he hears him gag, and he feels the throat convulse beneath his foot. “DYAAHAAHAA!” Ivan slams his boot over Corg’s neck again, “Squeal boy!!” He slams it once again, “Cry like you made her cry!!” Again, he stomps angrily, loving every moment of his pain, “Learn the gravity of your misdeeds!!” Ivan grins as he sees the darker blood exit Corg’s mouth, thick blood, blood deep within the body that he himself pumped out with his boot. Ivan grabs Corg by the back of the head and lifts him into the air…. That face of his… that cold, trashy face. That face that tried to lick her…. The crowd screams in joy as Stanislav throttles Corg with a hard fist to his face. He hears something jar inside of Corg’s skull and punches him with his fist thrice more, a fist as large as Corg’s head. “You are not worth being held, swine!” he screams and shoves Corg’s semi-conscious form into the corner. ~~ Crowd: STAN-IS-LAV!!! STAN-IS-LAV!! STAN-IS-LAV!! ~~ Ivan hears the cheers of the crazed fans as they watch him dismantle Corg. It drives him wild with anger and adrenalin as he rushes to the corner. With his huge paw, Stanislav slaps Corg in the face, sending thick, ropey strands of blood shooting from his blood covered mouth into the crowd. “How does it feel?? How does it feel to be afraid and at the mercy of someone bigger than you?!” Ivan roars and slaps him again and points at the ring, “In this ring you have cause this feeling and now the shoe is on other foot! Is it not?? IS IT NOT???” Waves of heat consume Stanislav as he locks Corg in a side headlock. “IS IT NOT? IS IT NOT CORG?? IS IT NOT???” Ivan drags him into the middle of the ring, the headlock smashes his body up against the iron side of Stanislav. He wrenches harder, “SCREAM BOY!! SCREAM IN FEAR! GGRRAAAHHH!!!” ~~ Nate: Folks, you might want your kids to cover your eyes, I think Ivan is just getting started! ~~ He had to be destroyed, fully and totally. Not a bone in his body would remain intact, and it would be his face he would start with…. With his head in such an exposed position, Stanislav slams his fist, now covered in Corg’s blood, six more times into his face. “Ivan! You cannot do that!!” screams Referee Speedy Riggs. “NYET!! SHUT UP RIGGS!!!” Ivan roars back to Riggs. He leans down and bellows into Corg’s ear, drowning out his sobs of pain, “Das vidanya, CORG!!!” With thunderous agility, Stanislav leaps into the air into a massive bulldog. The ring buckles under his weight as he hears Corg’s neck snap. Like a fresh shot of vodka on a cold Russian morning, a warmth rushes through Ivan’s body as he hears the cracking of Corg’s neck. He was no longer Ivan Stanislav. His regimented lifestyle was gone. His morals had fled. His compassion had dissipated. And his conscience was non-existent. He was a man possessed…
