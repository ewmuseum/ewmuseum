The Way Home, part 5

31 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/31/02 ~Rewind, July 24, 2002~ 'Tempest, you’re out of your mind!' Miyuki stared at her wide-eyed, with her hands clutching her hair on either side of her head. 'You cannot be serious Tempest. This is your career we’re talking about.' Tempest breezed by her scooping up clothes, with a makeup bag in hand. She tossed these haphazardly into a huge orange suitcase and tossed her head, flipping her jet black pony tail over her shoulder. 'Miyu, what better way to make the decision? It’s a great deal; you know that. How is it a bad career move?' Miyuki looked aghast and her mouth opened and closed a few times as she watched her friend, as if she were packing for her own funeral. 'Tempest, you can’t go back to the OSW. You said yourself that Ivan will kill you! That tape… that’s some musing on his own with a guy who can’t even speak English! You think he’s just going to welcome you back with open arms, no questions asked?' 'No,' said Tempest with a little shake of her head. 'I don’t expect it to be easy at all. But you gotta see that I can’t just ignore this and let him go.' She looked at Miyuki with sad yet hopeful eyes. 'He loves me. I can’t ignore that. And if that means deciding to go back to OSW to win him back, then that’s what I’ll do. I’ve made worse career decisions, based on much sillier things.' 'But… but…' Miyuki fumbled. 'You can’t just turn your back on these other offers you’ve got! Look at this one from PCW. Now here’s a place you could really get a fresh start if you don’t think the fWo will re-hire you.' She tried to thrust a contract in front of Tempest, but she spun away and darted into the closet, tossing out a few shiny, fringy, colorful things. 'No way Miyu! I’ve made up my mind. And I don’t think fWo’s gonna re-hire anyone. I don’t know what kind of game they’re playing, but if they’re not talking to guys like Erik Kelly and Eli Flair, then they’re certainly not going to bother with me. Besides… ' She poked her head out of the closet. 'Don’t you get it? Going back into OSW is a statement. I’m doing it because I believe in Ivan and me enough to make a commitment to the place, so that I can be with him and help him. Right now…' she sighed, 'That counts for a lot more than which fed might give me the biggest push or pay me the most.' Miyuki sighed and flopped onto the bed only to have a red feathered boa land on her face. She spluttered and tossed it into the suitcase and said, 'It’s my job to lookout for you and even your career when you can’t be convinced to do it for yourself. Tempest, there’s places that will pay you a mint just to show up and wave. There’s places that have promised you championships with in a few weeks of signing, and the bidding is only getting hotter.' Tempest leaned on the shiny chrome corner post of her bed and said, 'Is there anything wrong with the OSW’s offer? I mean I know we ignored it at first because of Ivan, but is there really some shortcoming to it in terms of money or something?' 'Well… not really,' Miyuki said, feeling her argument lose steam. 'Oh, I just worry that it’s not the best place to show people that… well, that you’re the best. You’re still the fWo Cruiserweight champion if you choose to lay claim to it. What have you got in the OSW?' 'Ivan,' said Tempest flatly. 'And that’s enough for now. C’mon, you know that all I have to do is stick around and the rest will come to me in time. I have a good following in the OSW and I know the fans would be happy to have me there.' Miyuki sat up and nodded with resignation. 'Tempest,' she said quietly, 'it’s just a dangerous precedent. I mean, deciding your career moves based on justy one other person…' She shook her head. 'It’s dangerous. No one is worth cantering your life on and making such a big decision as this.' Tempest smirked and said, 'This from the woman who chose to be my manager? How can you say that one person isn’t worth the big decision to follow? You did.' Miyuki chuckled and said, 'I wanted a cool boss for a change.' They both laughed and Miyuki shook her head, but got up to help Tempest pack. 'I hope you know what you’re doing,' she said. Tempest chuckled and said, 'Not a chance.' She paced on to the next room and clicked on the TV, and hit 'play' on her VCR. This time, the tape was not that of Ivan’s little talk with Drago, but a stack of tapes, reviewing the events of the weeks she’d missed. She was on the third, or was it the fourth? Shakira sang in the background as she watched Ivan disband the Red Army. And not long after, watched him be suspended form the Russian Army. ~ You're the one I need The way back home is always long But if you're close to me I'm holding on~ What happened to them Ivan? Di they not know how to function without your iron grip? Did they not know where to turn when they saw you confused? I know it hurt you to lose them, even if you did the disbanding yourself. And Russia... well, abandonment is never fun, no matter what your age. That's when I should have been there. And now, I wish I was. ~ In a world full of strangers You're the one I know~ The song was a gentle counterpoint as she watched the terrifying and intense match between Ivan and El Drago. It was wonderful and horrible at the same time, but she found herself smiling. Is this the real Ivan? Without all the layers of duty and purpose, you're more like me than I had thought. I just wondered if you knew who you were without them. And I wanted to know too. ~ You're the one I need My real life has just begun Cause there's nothing like Your smile made of sun~ The scenes shifted as the tapes played and Tempest watched with horror while Ed battled and then died at the hands of the Think Tank. She cried watching him hit the floor from the stage and realized in that instant that he wouldn't be coming back. Ed... I had so little time to get to know you and now, I won't ever get the chance. I was looking forward to seeing you again in the OSW. Even Ivan respected you. Didn't like you maybe, but respected you. And that says a lot. Maybe there's a fght waiting for me there too. And I'm sure Ivan hasn't let you be forgotten. ~ You're the one I need The way back home is always long But if you're close to me I'm holding on~ The tape played on and Tempest smiled at an unlikely scene as Ivan entered the hospital room of Amy Campbell. His apology was unexpected in all quarters, except maybe one. Can't quite just shrug that one off can you? What happened to her wasn't fair and when you saw it, you then had to think abotu what you did to her too. You r conscience speaks loud and clear Ivan, it's jus that you're finally starting to listen. Just look at him. That's compassion whether anyone's ready to use the word in the same breath as Ivan's name or not. That's caring, that's bravery, that's the real Ivan as much as any other and that's... the man I still love. ~ The way back home is always long But if you're close to me I'm holding on~
