Rock You Like A Jesus-cane

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'You sure this will do the trick?' Jennifer eyed the rat-faced man who had the crinkled plastic baggie in his hand, the object of Jennifer's query. 'Damn Gay... I mean, Straight it will!' the rat-faced man stammered. 'This stuff will have him climbing the fricking walls, man!' 'Good. Here's your money.' She gave the rat-faced man the roll of bills, and quickly snatched the plastic baggie out of the dealer's grasp. The rat-faced man sniffs the air around him, and scampers down the alley. Jennifer holds up the baggie to the moonlight and smiles sinisterly. 'Excellent...' ---------------------------------------- Toomes closes the door behind him as he maneuvers through the hotel room, holding his prize - a Suncoast bag containing his latest purchase... 'If Ya Smell What The Rock Is Cooking', a collection of promos and such on The Rock. 'Such a sad day, for I must get promo-cutting tips from Flex Kavanaugh...' Toomes laments as he slices the cellphane on the video, and rips it away. The cellophane wrapper flutters to the floor, joining the rest of his trash which has accumulated here. He slides the video out of the cardboard sleeve, and pops it in the VCR. The FBI warning comes on the screen as Sebastian sits down on the bed, and suddenly, his quiet sanctum is disturbed... KNOCK, KNOCK, KNOCK! 'WHAT?' Jennifer's muffled voice comes from behind the door. 'Open up, I have a suprise for you!' Sebastian furrows his brow. 'Your slow and painful death?' 'Just open up!' Sebastian mutters as he gets up and goes to the door. 'What do you want?' He fairly snarls at her. Jennifer holds a small birthday cake with a big blue '1' on it, with a lone, single candle, which seems to be not so much a candle as a metal tube with billowing white smoke coming from it. 'For you!' Jennifer chimes, offering him the cake. 'For your one month anniversary in OSW!' Toomes regards the cake suspiciously. 'I'd rather suck the moisture out of Tempest's tampons after heavy flow than trust you...' Jennifer looks hurt. 'How could you imply that I would be untrustworthy? Here I come, with a peace offering' she gestures to the cake. 'And what happens? The moment is sullied by your suspicion and paranoia... Truly, I rue the moment I thought you would appreciate such a labor of love, this tasty vanilla and bananna cake, in honor of your employment here...' She hangs her head and turns to leave. Toomes sniffs the air, and grabs her shoulder. 'Hey, hey, hey... Let's not be hasty here... Vanilla and bananna, you say?' Jennifer nods sadly. 'Yes... the fluffiest, tastiest vanilla cake, with a layer of bananna creme... All to end up in the trash...' She moves down the hallway. 'Gimme the cake.' Toomes intones. 'Are you sure you want it, Sebastian?' Jennifer asks innocently. 'Yes, hand over the cake.' 'You mean...' Jennifer waves her hand over the cake, presenting it like Vanna White presenting someone with a BRAND NEW CAR~! 'This delicious morsel of goodness? You want this?' Sebastian snarls. 'Gimme the goddamned cake, already!' Toomes swipes it, and disappears into his hotel room. The door slams shut, leaving Jennifer alone in the hallway. She smiles wickedly, and rubs her hands together. 'Excellent...' ---------------------------------------- THREE HOURS LATER... 'Guh...' Toomes lies in a puddle of his own vomit, tangled up in puke-sodden sheets on the floor in front of his television. The Rock tape is halfway out of the EJECT port on the VCR, and a religious show is on, with a dramatic re-enactment of Jesus feeding the multitudes...on bread and fish...or something, maybe a Happy Meal. Who knows? 'Guh...' is all that Toomes can utter, as the hallucingenic-laced cake (with the added bonus of a smouldering crack pipe as the candle) has turned the normally sane Toomes into a drooling vegetable. For three hours, he has been watching a mixture of The Rock and Jesus Christ. Under the effects of a powerful mind-altering substance. Anyone else see where this is going? -------------------------------------- Matt Shivan looks intimidated. And as well he should be! For he is interviewing HESSIAN~! Hessian glares down at Shivan, who is doing a fine job of looking spineless. 'Let me tell YOU, something, Matt Shivan! Meanstreak should have stayed dead! He should have stayed out of my path, for I will crush him like Ivan crushed him at Red October!' Hessian roars. 'But...bu..but... Hessian...' 'WHAT?' Hessian roars, evoking an 'eep!' out of Shivan as the interviewer staggers back. 'Huh...huh...Hessian... Have you ever faced Meanstreak before...?' Shivan asks timidly. 'Have I ever faced Meanstreak before? Lemme tell you-' Suddenly, Hessian looks over Shivan's head, and stops. Shivan looks up at Hessian and follows his gaze. And we see it. Sebastian Toomes, with his silver and blue hair tucked underneath a matted and ragged brown wig, wearing a white robe with a purple sash, comes stalking down the backstage area. 'What the fuck?' Hessian murmurs. Toomes walks up to Shivan and pushes Hessian out of the way. Hessian, too stunned to say anything to the contrary, oddly steps back. 'IT DOESN'T MATTER IF HESSIAN'S FACED MEANSTREAK BEFORE!' Toomes yells. Shivan shakes his head in confusion. Hessian looks confused as well. 'Matt Shivan, let me tell you what The Christ is gonna do. He's gonna take Hessian's mask, shine it up real nice, TURN THAT SUM'BITCH SIDEWAYS, AND STICK UP YOUR PAGAN ASS!' Toomes yells. 'Toomes, what the fuck do you think you're doing?' Hessian holds out his hands in disbelief. 'The Son Of God says this: Hessian, Meanstreak, Saturday Night Slam!, The Christ is going to come down the ring and lay the Salvation down on your Pagan asses. There's not a thing Mister 'I Wanna Be A Satan Worshipper' Hessian, and 'I wish I still had credibility with the fans instead of resting on what I was doing a year ago' Meanstreak can do about it. The Son of Christ is here, Matt Shivan, The Son Of God can turn water into wine, The Son of God can cure leprosy, and The Son of God is gonna walk down to that ring on Saturday, throw those heathens out of the ring, and walk out OSW Heavyweight Champion...' Toomes steals the mic, and holds his head back.. 'IF YA BELIEVE...LA-LA-LA-LA-LA-OWWWW...WHAT THE CHRIST...IS...SAYING...' Toomes finishes. Somewhere, in the bowels of the arena, The Rock's music, strangely re-done with organs, begins playing as Toomes walks off, leaving Hessian and Shivan shaking their heads in a mixture of disgust and bewilderment.
