Sweet Victory...

14 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/14/02 We see Tom Walczak sitting in a sports bar. He has a beer in his right hand, and on the television we see a world cup match-taking place. It just so happens to be that the two teams that are playing are the United States, Walczak’s current place of residence, and Poland the country of Walczak’s origin. Walczak sips his beer and anxiously awaits the start of the match. The kickoff takes place, and after a couple of kicks back and forth Polish forward Emanuel Olisadebe takes control of the ball. He heads it toward the goal, but it is blocked. He quickly gets the ball back and strikes it in for a goal. It is one nothing Poland, and it is only the third minute. Everyone in the bar lets out a sigh of disbelief. That is everyone except Walczak. He instead jumps on top of the bar and begins to scream for joy. “W Kojncu cos dobrego zrobiliszcze wy scurwy siny!!!!” “Sit the fuck down you stupid Polish son of a bitch.” Walczak hears the comment and quickly turns around. He looks for the man that has said it, but he cannot see him, and no one will fess up to saying it. “Who said that?” “I did.” A man stands up next to Walczak, and he looks up at him. The man is only around six feet tall, and Walczak laughs. “I am sure that you do not want to anger me. As you can see I can grind your testicles into nothing more than balsam powder.” “Well, what you don’t seem to realize is that we are in the United States, and that the reason we are here is to root on our country.” “That is exactly what I am doing I am rooting for my country.” Before the man can answer another sigh is let out. Pawel Kryszalowicz just scored another goal this time off of a cross from Jacek Krzynowek. “Yet another goal. It seems that your pathetic American team can simply not handle the onslaught of our Polish Squadron.” The man slugs Walczak in the face without saying anything. Walczak retaliates kicking the man in the gut, and then throwing him over the bar. He ducks another mans punch, and then everyone in the bar goes to attack Walczak. He fends off their attacks, but they push him further back, until he is out of the bar. “You only fight because you know America’s loss in eminent. Victory will come to Poland today, and the USA will once again be crushed at the hands of the Polish as I destroy Doug Troy at Ascension.” Walczak dusts off his jacket, and walks away as the scene fades to black….
