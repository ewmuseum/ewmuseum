They Gay World vs. Walczak...

8 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/8/02 Tom Walczak is seen walking down the street, and as he walks down he starts to get strange looks from certain kinds of people. For some reason Walczak starts to get strange looks from men who are walking down the street holding hands, but he cannot really understand why. Some of the pairs that walk by him even spit at him with a look of disgust upon their faces. He can tell that the men are gay, but he can still not digest why they show such signs of contempt towards him. Then one of the pairs stops Walczak and starts a verbal assault on him. “Hey, you fucking moron? Why did you have to say those derogatory comments about us? Don’t you realize that we have feelings to, and are not just low life scum as you would believe?” “What the hell are you talking about? I didn’t say shit about you, in fact the only persons that I made comments about were Jesse Ramey, and Scott Naket, and that was only because they were a pair of fucking flamers.” “What do you think that we are buddy? Can’t you tell by the pink shirts, tight pants, and neatness of our appearance?” “Of course I could tell, but I did not make the comment at the whole gay community only Naket, and Ramey.” “You think that when a comment is addressed at one of our fellow brothers, that it doesn’t apply to us? Do you think that just because we are not in the wrestling business that we were not hurt?” “You know, I don’t care what the hell you think, because your opinion just doesn’t matter to me. You see unlike Jesse Ramey, I am not trying to make a stronger impact on the gay wrestling fans. Why don’t you just leave me alone and maybe I can get that homo Steven Smith to send you a personal photo.” “No, we wont leave you alone, because this is more important than any photo of any….” “Honey, wait, I always wanted to have a picture of Steven hanging right in our bedroom so that we can look together at one of our most outspoken brothers whenever we awake in the morning.” “Well, we can just send away for one, we don’t need this no good bastard to get us one.” “Man, you two guys fight like bitches. I seriously have never seen two more pathetic specimens in my entire life. I cannot believe that I am even standing here, because I can’t be seen with two pansies such as you two.” “When will the comments end? When will we be accepted as equals by the common people? Can’t they see that we want to lead normal lives.” “I actually have some advice for you guys if you want to lead a normal life. Here it is, FIND A WOMAN!!!!!!” The two gays get so infuriated that they start to slap Walczak with their hands and so called man purses, but they eventually leave, and Walczak just walks off smiling as the scene fades to black…
