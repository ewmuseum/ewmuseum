After Thought (Part 6 of 6 of 'Breaking Benefits')

9 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Mar. 9, 2002 The events proceeding in Breaking Benefits took place March 4th to March 7th. Beat me. Hit me. Eradicate me. End me. ...They couldn't. The little sh*ts didn't get the job done. They thought their little games would get them an International title for sure- but I'm not going down without a fight. Call me a dumb ass, or call me courageous, at this point I don't care what any of you think. The Benefit and Drew Hostyle have gone way too far- And in two days, I'll show them exactly what to make out of me. I used to take pleasure in being called an underdog...its time to rise above that. Its time to take the International title away from those goons and Drew Hostyle. It's only fair to me. It's only fair to myself that I do. It's only fair... Take a look at my schedule... Schedule Saturday: I beat the shit out of Drew Hostyle. Sunday: Repeat Saturday, and be the first f*ckin International champion in OSW. Heh, and I have one more question...Live or die, Hostyle?
