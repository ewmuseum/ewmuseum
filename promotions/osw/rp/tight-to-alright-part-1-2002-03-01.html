Tight To Alright Part 1

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/01/02 'Roll Up The Seven, Race Out to Heaven.' -Kid Rock Tight and alright. Sure, the two words rhyme and sound cool together. Actually, they kind of sound kinkish if ya think really hard about it. Cool isn't it? Not for Scott Naket though, who's been anything but cool as of late. Tight We open up inside the local 7-11 convenience store. Not that busy, just a few skateboarder teenagers filling up cups for slurpies, debating wither it's worth risking to steal that porno magazine on the shelf, and counting all the loose change that they have on them. Outside the store, there sits a bum to the side of the door. Complete with his straight from the ghetto hat, which is dirty and has an old school Houston Oilers football logo on it, the bum is dressed in old gray shirts, a pair of brown pants, and two black socks, each with holes in random spots. The bum has long gray hair, and a gray beard. So in other words, your standard television bum, right?! Well, it just happens that this bum is on a mission. To earn enough money to allow him to buy a tasty hot dog and a small drink from inside the store that's almost banned him for loitering. That, or enough money for some booze. And how is our fellow doing in his mission? 'Spare change for the poor?!' the bum cries out, holding his cup of money out as people walk by. The bum shakes the can, as the rattle of change hitting the can is heard. The bum looks around, making sure no one is present, and dumps the contents of the can onto his lap. He starts counting the change, and a smile forms on his face. '$2.23' the bum proudly cries out, with a gliming smile on his face. He licks his lips and looks inside the store. Very carefully, he picks up each coin and drops it into his can. All of the sudden, Somebody’s Gotta Feel This by Kid Rock can be heard from the distance, and a car is heard pulling into the parking lot. The music, obviously coming from within the vehicle, blares louder as the car parks in a stall near the bum. The 1987 Honda Accord, a piece of crap in it's manner, is the car of choice here. And who is in that car? But of course, it's everybody's favorite superstar, Scott Naket. The music comes to a halt as the car is turned off. The door swings open, and out of it walks Naket, showing no emotion on his face. Wearing one of his signature tee shirts from his PCW days, Naket walks slams the door of his car and looks over to the bum. He looks at his keys and decides to lock the door to the car. He walks over, still showing no signs of anger or a pleasant attitude, and the bum looks up at him. 'Spare change for the poor sir?' the bum asks. Naket stops, and with a cock-eyed look, glares over towards the bum. Naket stares down at the bum, who just stares back at Naket. The bum shows no fear, because after all, people like him have absolutely nothing to lose at all. You kill them, oh well, that's one less day of suffering that they'll have to live through. You beat the crap out of them, oh well, that just means he'll have a nice bed to lay in for a few days. Hell, he beats the crap out of you, oh well, that means he gets free meals and bed space for the next few years at the local prison. Picking a fight with a bum isn't the smartest thing to do, because it's a win situation no doubt for the bum, and as for yourself? What do you get for picking on a bum? Wow, what a big man you'd be, and I don't think I need to note the sarcasm there. 'What did you ask me old man?' Naket asks, still with his cock-eyed look, and now with a sinister grin on his face. The bum looks up, and holds out the can. 'Spare change for the poor sir?' the bum asks once again, shaking the can now to encourage Scott to place some money inside of it. Naket turns his head, and laughs. 'Do you even know who I am?' Naket asks, showing no respect to the man that has lived probably one of the roughest lives you could live in North America. The man pulls back his can, and stares at Naket. 'No I don't,' the bum replies, 'But frankly, I just consider you another one of those ignorant rich snobs that doesn't care about nothing but themselves. Good day sir.' The bum follows his rant with a turn of the head, so he can't face Naket. Naket, now with a shocked look on his face, just turns away and starts to slowly walk towards the door. He grabs a hold of the door handle, but stops and reaches into his pocket. He pulls out his wallet, and opens it up. He reaches into one of the side pouches and pulls out a folded letter. Still inside it's envelope, Naket un-folds it and reads the front. It reads, 'The Final Lesson' on it, and Naket sighs. Meanwhile, back inside the popular convenience store, the kids have no made their way to purchasing their items. The Asian clerk starts to argue about something with them, but since we're outside, we just can't hear what it's about. Yes, my guess is they don't have enough money too. Hell, they probably make about as much money as Mariah Carey's producers made from her last album, Glitter. Back outside, Naket has now opened up the letter, and has started to read it. With really neat handwriting with a black pen, the letter reads as follows: Dear Scott, If you've opened this letter, you know you have hit rock bottom in your in-ring career. This is your last hope, and your last hope depends on fan base. You need support from the fans. Support from them will earn you a spot in their hearts, and thus, they'll want to help you in defeating your enemy. But how do you complete this lesson? I can not tell you exactly how to do this, but you must do it on your own. All I can say to help you is this. Do what's right for you, and the company, and do what the fans would think is right. If you follow the right ways, you'll eventually figure out what was missing in your style. You might have not trusted me before, but really you should. Let me remind you of these: Alliances 2000, Television Title, Continental Title. I helped you win those. I made you win those. Without me, you would not have ever won those, and you wouldn't be where you are today. You'd be another washup in CCW. Follow my lead Scott, and success will be eminent. Your Friend and Teacher, Rev Bob December 27th, 2000 Naket looks up from the paper, and looks back down at it again. And yes, that isn't a typo, it is the correct date. Naket folds the paper up and puts it in his pocket. 'So that's it,' Naket says, talking to himself, 'I gotta do what's right, and it'll lead me to success.' Naket says, now with a confused look on his face. 'I never understood that man.' Naket grumbles, as he looks at his wallet again. The bum looks over at Naket and shakes his head. 'And people thought I was crazy.' The bum whispers to himself. Then, without warning, Naket hears these words from inside the store. 'WE HAVE PORNO!' Naket looks up, but it's too late. SMACK! Naket falls back to the ground as the kids rush out of the store, fleeing from it as fast as they can so they don't get caught with their stolen product. Either that, or they are so horny they just can't wait to go whacking off in the bushes of the local forest. Naket, now seeing visions of the OSW Title flying around his head, starts to glaze over, and starts to go into a non-thinking mode. In other words, his knocked out cold, without the out part. Sensing the chance to do it, the old bum looks around to make sure no one is around. He climbs to his feet slowly, and puts his can on the ground. He takes a few small steps forward, and looks around again. Not trusting anyone, though no one is near by other then a glazed Naket and a confused 7-11 clerk inside at his post, the bum still moves back and picks up his most valuable thing in the word. His plastic cup of course. He then walks over towards the motionless body of Naket, and bends over. He taps him once on the stomach, but there is no reaction from him. The bum again taps Naket on the stomach, but harder. Again, no reaction. Sensing this is his lucky moment, the bum picks up the discarded wallet beside Naket's body, and opens it up. Taking a quick look inside, the bum smiles, seeing the amount of money inside. 'It's gonna be a good night tonight.' The bum says to himself, with visions of getting hammered and laid following throughout his head. Thus, we conclude our chapter of the 'Tight' version of Scott Naket. Please, tune in next time to witness the conclusion of our sequence, and see what the 'Alright' version of Scott Naket is really, all about. Fade to black.
