Horns, halos and masks removed.

2 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 2nd, 2002 Slam was one hell of an evening, to say the very least. If you couple the severe beating of Bethany Yamato, resulting in a chance for Aimz to be OSW's first ever Cruiserweight champion at the Ascension pay-per-view, quickly followed by Amy herself getting quite the beating at the hands of her PPV opponent, Corg...then you've already got a pretty radical night. Thought that was enough to toss things off? Well add Reno Davis going practically mental, beating everybody up and then getting beaten down himself, the Red Army getting incredibly confused for the first time in a long time and Propaganda retaining his International title... For lack of a better term, and as Aimz would say...that's just plain fucked up. She was sitting in a little plastic chair that had been placed in front of a little monitor set on a wheeled cart in her marked locker-room, visibly swaying, but not under her own power. Fuck, that was one hard chair shot, right to the ribs. The DDT wasn't a walk in the part either...she'd be feeling this night for an awfully LONG time. Nearly doubled over, Amy was only keeping up by resting the palm of her hand on the seat of the chair, right between her legs...just sitting there watching the clips of Reno going apeshit on all of those people, cringing with every blow he delivered by various means. It's not that she necessarily liked these people, but it was disturbing for her to see Davis so upset. She'd been dabbing at her gashed forehead and right cheek with a Kleenex soaked in rubbing alcohol, which wasn't exactly the brightest idea to rid herself of pain, but at least they were clean. Amy had reached onto the cart by the black and white monitor to grab two well-sealed bandage patches, nearly keeling over the front of the chair as she did so but Campbell managed to steady herself just long enough to apply them to the two main wounds, deciding to take care of the others later on. In case you hadn't noticed, she despised paramedics......well, truth be told, Corg could be lurking anywhere, as could the angry Davis...she was a little afraid of both, though seeing the later in the condition he seemed to be in, not so much being hurt by him, was what really terrified her. Plus...the fact that ZaC was sitting over in the corner, slipping the 'El Bandito' mask on over his little furry head with small, weakened paws probably wouldn't help Reno's temper...she was sure he wouldn't have wanted the monkey there that night, but Amy knew it was the only way to keep the little one safe. ''So, ZaC...'' She said, groaning as she stood up and stepped over to his little form, tugging the mask from his head and tossing off into another corner. ''...think Reno'll like the facial Corg gave me?'' The monkey just sat there for a moment, blinking at Amy with big eyes as she smiled and sat down Indian-style beside him. When she tilted her head down to get closer, ZaC stood up, swaying like Campbell had been previously on his stumpy legs, running little fingers around her forehead and cheekbones to inspect some of the still-bleeding cuts with his little primate senses before sitting back down to tug on strands of her long, deep red hair. Aimz just barely managed a grin and took the monkey's hand in hers. ''Sorry, man...but I don't need to lose any hair, your friend's gonna turn it grey as is.'' ZaC just sat there again, blinking. Soon after, he hopped into her lap and started snapping the draw strings at the knees of her baby blue cargo pants. ''Hmmm...'' Campbell lightly touched the bandage on her cheek, mumbling, ''Some high-flyer Corg is...had to use a chair to get me down.'' The monkey began chewing on the dangling tip of her leather belt. ''Yep...I've got my title shot. I'm supposed to be ecstatic...see how happy I am, ZaC?'' ZaC squeaked for no apparent reason. ''Shh...we don't want anybody knowing you're in here, 'kay?'' She put a finger to the little monkey's mouth, only to get it nibbled on by tiny, yet sharp, teeth. ''...I've got my big match at Ascension, isn't that cool? Heh, I beat Yamato hands down for costing me what should have been my shot last week...and now I've gotta take Corg out the same way for messing me up this week. The good thing's that I've got the shot I had coming to me, so everything seems to be working out to be pretty fair.'' Amy paused. ''Oh my God...'' Her face was grim. ''...I'm having a conversation with a fucking animal.'' Then, she heard it...a rustling at the other end of her room, closely followed by a fit of maniacle laughter. Much to Aimz' horror, when she looked over, Corg had stepped out into the room. It was as if ZaC sensed the danger, he crawled up onto Amy's shoulder, tugging at her hair and pointing to the chalk white painted figure of the man who wounded Amy so badly earlier, before jumping up onto a coat rack where the little primate coudn't be reached. ''What the fuck are you doing here?!'' She struggled to muster up the 'balls' to order him out without backing away from his now advancing form. ''This isn't a ring...you've got no business here. LEAVE!'' ''Pathetic, little girls talking to monkies.'' He drew nearer. ''And people think I'm crazy.'' Closer and closer...close enough, Amy felt the hot breath at that point. ''It's just too bad…'' It was then that he flicked a little switchblade from his sleeve, baring the silvery blade. ''...you're about to encounter another fucking animal.'' Feeling he had said enough to scare her, Corg had jumped at the smaller redhead, lifting her up against the wall by her throat as he leaned in, inches from her face, and spoke quietly, placing the hand that also held the knife against her lips to make sure she wouldn't seek help. He continued choking her, slapping her across the face. ''Even though you act as if you're invincible,'' He punched her, seeing a stream of warm blood flow down from under the bandaged cut on the cheek he had struck. ''I know you can feel this.'' Suddenly, ZaC leapt down from the shelf, picking at Corg's hair, biting him violently...only to be tossed against the other wall, just hard enough to knock the little creature down. ''I could end you right now!'' Amy soon followed suit with ZaC, getting tossed, MUCH harder, against the wall on the opposite side of the room. Corg may have been a high-flyer, but he was a strong man when angered. ''But I won't.'' He belted out a sadistic laugh, walking towards the half-concious, now defeated figure of Campbell, who was laying against a bench, limp. ''I'd rather save your for Ascension.'' He leaned down on one knee, lifting her right hand as if he was about to check her pulse.</i? ''I'd rather beat the shit out of you when it is perfectly legal.'' Obviously he wasn't going to check anything...that much was clear when Corg raised the switchblade and began cutting the anarchy symbol into her palm, covering her entire hand with blood that trickled down Amy's arm as she screamed out. Thank God for her, voices were quickly heard, shouting out...followed by foot steps trampling toward the room. ''Remember, I'm watching you…'' He bolted for the door, looking out one way to see the security before turning back to Aimz, who was slipping nto unconciousness, momentarily. ''I'll be waiting…'' That was that. Corg had taken off down the hall, too fast for even the leanest human, wrestler or otherwise in that building to pursue. Amy was left laying, bloodied and battered on the floor as ZaC came around, screeching over her injured body as the security and medical staff did the same. Reno was berserk, not even there. Amy was alone, no vital protection other than security...and even they wouldn't follow her outside of the arenas. It was obvious that she had gotten herself in a little bit over her head.
