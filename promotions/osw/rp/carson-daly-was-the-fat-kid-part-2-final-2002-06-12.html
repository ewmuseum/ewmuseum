Carson Daly was the fat kid. (Part 2, Final)

12 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 12th ((Note: I'm so medicated right now...Gravol, is it? I took three for nausea...they make me sleepy.)) ''Hey there, I'm Carson Daly.'' Wow, that was enthusiastic. A handshake, a nod. Standing in front of Amy Campbell was the 'legend' of modern journalism...bigger than Wolfe Blitzer. Here was Carson Daly. A whole lot of hassle over nothing was what Aimz thought when she looked at him. He wasn't exceptionally attractive, he was wearing ugly shoes and a 'designer' coat, probably made of cow...whoop-dee-doo. Yep, folks...the Carson Daly you all know and 'love' smells like a cheap leather wallet. Okay, okay...so there wasn't much of a problem with that. What WAS a problem, however, was the fact that this was Carson Daly...member of the gigantic MTV conspiracy to get the youth of the world thinking what they want 'em to...which meant an overload of tiny tops, tight jeans, bleached blondes and belly chains. Belts were put on for mere show, probably because the pants were already cutting off circulation to the girls' lower bodies... Yippie. MTV...how enthralling. Time to end the sarcasm for a moment to put on that nice, fake, Christina Aguilara-like beamer. ''Hi...I'm Amy.'' Even through the act, Aimz had to swallow her pride to make it past the next statement. ''...I've always wanted to meet you, Carson.'' Oh God, that almost burned... ''Gee, thanks Amy!'' Daly smiled. The scary part was, he wasn't putting on an act...the man really is this corny. ''I can't wait to see you again on set! Make sure to see the hair and makeup specialists before you come out.'' The next person to make a comment about how scruffy she looked would get killed, Carson Daly or no fucking Carson Daly. Errr...wait...this is good. If they notice she's opposing all that 'One type of beautiful' thing, maybe the 14 year-olds watching TRL will! Oh well, she was still pissed...mainly for the sake of being pissed. This was like being in Britney Spears' tour bus...MTV headquarters in Times Square was, indeed, behind enemy lines. So here she was, a lone soldier... Before the trumpets began to sound and she put her veteran medals on, a production assistant pulled Amy from her own, triumphant little world by tapping her on the shoulder. ''Excuse me...uhhh...Miss Campbell.'' He stuttered, eyes not quite meeting her face. ''You...uhhh...are wanted on the set...'' Hooray. As he escorted her onto the set, past a packed audience of half-enthused fans who's reaction would probably be dubbed over later with the voices of screaming fans at a Metallica concert, Amy's head was clear: Standing in front of her, taking on the form of a primped up nancy-boy (aka Carson Daly), was the enemy. On all sides, in the form of moronic teeny boppers, was the calvary, in case things got too explicit, they had free will to boo her off of the set. As she put her ear piece in, Carson whispered to her... ''I hope you rehearsed your answers well.'' Campbell just nodded at him, laughing exceptionally hard on the inside. No, really, she CAN laugh. Within a few minutes, the show was on, Carson had, for the 9385279346543rd time, introduced himself to anybody who's head his name hadn't been pounded into and viola...it was time for Amy to answer a few pre-rehearsed questions. ''So, Amy...'' Carson had that big, ten thousand dollar dental bill's worth of a smile on. ''...being a female wrestler, is it tough? You're competing in what many consider a 'man's sport'...worse yet, you're trying to compete as a man would.'' Wow. If the rest of the questions were this lame, she'd find a way to kill herself on set to make things interesting. This is where she was supposed to rant on 'Girl power'. ''Uhh, I dunno. It's...stuff. I get to meet great guys like Reno Davis, so I get my pick of quite a selection...plus I like kicking people's asses, like when I beat Bethany Yamato and Corg.'' Her ear piece buzzed with the sound of a voice, 'Amy, go by the cards!'. Sorry Mr. Producer...lame questions deserve equally lame answers. ''Ah...'' Daly was as befuddled as the rest of the crew. He had to wing the next question. ''So...umm...what's it like...working with guys like Civil Disobedience?'' ''I'm hardly working with them. They helped me win a match, I'm happy about that.'' ''Ohhh...so they just help you kinda slip in the cheat, eh?'' He chuckled. This was national TV, of course the jackass was trying to be entertaining. Too bad he failed miserably. ''Cheat? And WHO the fuck are you?'' She heard shouting in the earpiece at that point, while she glared at America's media darling, not even noticing the shocked crowd around her. ''When was the last time you got into a ring, Mister MTV?'' ''Well...I.....uhhh...'' ''Huh? I'm sure you got your pansy ass kicked enough in high school...but when was the last time YOU had to be in a REAL fight that didn't involve some snarky brat trying to take your lunch money because you were the chubby kid? Fucking reta---'' Security dragged her off stage. The fans laughed, the fans cheered...she got a standing ovation for making Carson Daly look like an ass in front of the nation. She'd have a million parents with pitchforks at her door by morning. Her goal? Party accomplished...she'd embarassed the hell out of MTV.
