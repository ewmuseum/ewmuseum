Returning....

3 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Feb. 3rd, 2002 ~Through the nothingness of life, some things are found. Some things are lost, while other things are found, things that we do treasure, and others that we could care less about. But the one thing that matters most to all of us is the spark to our dear lives. When something this sacred has all but gone and left, it will leave you without hope, without any sense of life. It’s hard to find the courage to make it back, through the long narrow tunnel to find a perfect way out. Some of us can do it, while others may fail to succeed. Those of us who try will almost surely complete it with ease. A story of this has happened, in the federation of OSW. A soul was taken, from a man, whose name we will not reveal, just yet. You see this man was done wrong in a place that was so bad for him. They took his life; they took everything that he had loved so much and they made his go away. OSW and Jane Reagan gave him life; they brought to him a new spark. They showed him the way; they led him into the light. Now his time has come and those who have offended him will not live to see the coming day. Beware OSW the Attitude has arrived.~ Opening upon a small bar, somewhere outside of the Saturday Night Slam location. The bar is very nasty looking from the outside, very putrid and disgusting. It seems as if not even a rat would wish to walk upon its door. When out of nowhere comes a figure, tall, masculine, and cloaked heavily. He makes his way into the bar, shoving the door open aggressively. He makes his way in with the door left open and the cold gust of air flowing in. The men turning to look and see whom it is, but when they notice a smile comes over their faces. The man takes off his hat and tosses it onto the coat rake, surprisingly hitting it right on the dot and that hat just hangs there. He takes off his coat tosses it into a booth and now reveals his under clothes. He is wearing what seem to be baggy khakis, but the black T-shirt is what is most unusual. It has the OSW logo on the back of it, but above that is the words Attitude Adjustment. As this is stated, the man twists around and we see the revealing face of “Bad Attitude” Jesse Ramey. Making his way over to the bar he just turns and walks back to his booth. He leans back and just waits. The television in corner of the bar is playing a taped version of OSW Slam! A smile emerges from Jesse face as the show has now made its way into the part where Jesse and Switchblade have their encounter. A lovely looking waitress makes her way over to Jesse booth and the two just stare at each other. Finally words come forth from her lips. “It’s been a while, Jesse. I thought you were dead.” Says the waitress. “As did I, but all has changed now. Things have been done and I, well, I have made my return to wrestling and back into the world that I so dearly love.” The heart-felt words come from Jesse’s mouth. “Can you explain to my, why this took so long for you to tell me?” She asks. “No, I can’t, not right now. Not at this current time. Things will be revealed, I swear to you they will, Chass. You must come with me though.” Jesse urges. “I’ve been here ever since the time of your death, Jesse. Now you come back from nowhere and expect me to just up and leave with you. I waited for so long, I thought maybe your death was a hoax at first. Since they never found a body, but then it comes to more than three months and still no word from you.” Chass has a hard time speaking. “I expected that you would at least let the people you know and love in life know about what was going on. It wouldn’t have hurt for you to at least given me a phone call letting me know what was going on and why you couldn’t see me.” Tears begin to well up in Chass’ eyes. Words ring clear from Jesse’s mouth, “I wouldn’t have been that easy, Chass. I was forced into this. I was told not to let anyone know, they had me placed in my summer cottage in Canada and I wasn’t allowed to do anything. I was under an ironclad contract and there was nothing left for me to do.” “But, how did you get into OSW. How did they know of these things?” She protests. “It was all around PTC. Things didn’t spread too quickly, as the funeral services were held and they covered things up very well. Even my own best friend was forced into working with it or being fired. How do you think it made people feel? How do you think this made me feel?” Jesse replies. In a lowered voice, “I’m sorry, but this is all so sudden to me. I waited for you and never considered dating again. I went along with your angle with Jess Christine in CCW and that hurt me almost as much as this.” “Why do you bring up Damsel again? I thought we had this worked out prior to any of this?” He asks. “I know and we did. It’s just that I am not a very demanding person, but I can’t live with you always being on the road and the chance of you cheating on me. I don’t want to do what I’m about to do, but it is necessary.” She says with a heavy heart. With a look of enlightenment. “Don’t say another word! I have the solution to this equation and I know you are going to love it.” Looking up with tear filled eyes. “Tell me then, oh God, please tell me.” “Come with me? Come out on the road and travel with me. Be my manager. You can be with me at all times, or at least most of the time. We could make this thing work, just wait and see. Would you do it?” He asks. Smiles coming over her face as tears of joy come forth, pouring down her face. “Yes, I will.” The two embrace in a hug and then Chass retracts and lays a big kiss on Jesse. “Wait here, I’m going into the back and I’m going to change. I’ll be with you this week during your match. I want to come down to the ring with you.” She says. She runs off to the back, but a look of complete fear comes over Jesse’s face. He looks almost as if he has seen a ghost. Speaking to his self, “She wishes to come with me. This is a good thing, but she wishes to come down to the ring with me from here on out. She will be harmed; I must not let her do this. Switchblade or Frankie Bones could do anything to her. I don’t even know Drew Hostyle; he could even do something to her. It’s too much of a risk and I wouldn’t want anything to happen to the person that I love so dearly, but if I don’t then she may not wish to come with me and she may even leave me. This is a chance and a risk that I must take.” Jesse stands and walks over to the bar, where he takes a stool and sits down in front of the bartender. “Long time, no see.” Comes from the bartender. “Yes, I know Jake. It has been a while.” Jesse says. “You want the usual?” He says. “Yes, please.” Looking down at the counter. Jake walks over and pours a cup of coffee. He comes back and slides it in front of Jesse’s face. Jesse grasps the cup with his hands and looks up at Jake. “Tell me, Jake. If you had a hard decision that you couldn’t really decide on, what would you do?” Jesse asks. “I heard your conversation with Chass. I know what you are going through, Jesse. You want Chass and this is the only way you are going to keep her, Jesse. You need to do this and give it a try. It may not sound like the best decision, but it is. Trust me on this.” Jake says with a smile. Taking a drink of his coffee, “You’ve never given me bad advice before in my life, Jake. So, I think I can trust you on this one.” Smiling, “Good, good. So, tell me about this match you have in OSW. I’ve been looking forward to OSW ever since I saw that you were on the roster. We here at the bar, we believed that you were still alive and we had hope. We knew something was wrong and that you must have been forced into that God awful thing.” “Yes, I was and yes, it was. I couldn’t believe the CCW made me do it, but they made me do it because I was becoming way too popular for their liking. I wasn’t supposed to get that famous and they were trying to get Switchblade over. Something that they could never do. The guy was a lost cause from the beginning and he is in OSW as well. His debut match, he lost to SLR. What can be said? This guy doesn’t deserve to even grace the same ring that I am in.” Jesse says with a hint of anger in his voice. Cleaning a glass, “What about this Frankie Bones, guy?” “He got thrown into the mix because of my partner in this match, Drew Hostyle. This has the makings of a great match; I’ll give credit to whoever booked this match. The only problem I had with it was the fact that it wasn’t higher up on the card. Take a look at the caliber of it. Two men of my and Drew’s talent tagging together and we aren’t even mid way into the show! I’ve never had a problem doing these type of matches, oh no. Only because of that fact that they like to start the show off with the best. The fans obviously can’t wait to see my colossal talent that late in the evening.” Jesse smirks. “You are good, Jesse, but you’re not the best.” Jake replies. Jesse looks at Jake with a grime expression, “What do you mean by that?” “Take a look at where you have been.” Jake says. “Yes and your point is?” Says Jesse with a confused tone. Shaking his head, “You’ve been gone for nearly four months. Don’t you in the least bit think that you may have a little bit of ring rust? Something that will put you at a disadvantage. Think about it, Jesse. If what you have been telling me is true, than they have kept you locked away. With no training and nothing to do. It is a wonder you are even in the shape you are now. You can’t just get back in the ring and think that you are the champion once again.” Taking another drink of coffee, “I guess you are right, but I can’t be that bad off. I mean I was at a physical and mental peak in CCW before they did this to me. Yes, my rage and anger has grown over these past few months, but that mentality can only get me further in OSW. That killer instinct, I’ve had to resort back to what I used to be at one time. Back before I even came into wrestling. I’ve been thinking about those times back in boot camp when I was younger. Everything they trained me to know. I’ve gotten back that “not taking shit from anyone” attitude. This can only help me in the end, it can not make me worse.” “Indeed, it could very well help you, but I think you should make a little stop in at your training center on your way to this next show.” He says. “Why?” Jesse asks. “Trust me on this one, just stop in there and train in the ring with some of the younger talent. You will be surprised at what you will find.” Walking away and into the back. Looking into his coffee cup, “What did he mean by that and should I even consider stopping in there? Of course, everything he has ever told me to has always helped me out in the end. So I might as well just stop in there for the hell of it.” From the back walks Chass, with a big smile on her face, her jacket put on and a large suitcase in her right hand. “You ready to go?” Chass says with a huge smile. Smiling, “Yes, dear.” Jesse walks over and grabs the suitcase. Chass walks out the door and it closes behind her. Jesse sets down the suitcase and walks over to his booth. He puts on his overcoat and then grabs his hat. Jesse walks back, picks up the suitcase and walks to the door. Looking back at the bar, one last time, “God, please have mercy on me, Chass, and more than anyone else, my opponents on Slam!” Jesse puts his hat down on his head and makes his way out of the bar. Everything begins to get darker and darker. ~END~
