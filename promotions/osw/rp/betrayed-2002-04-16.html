BETRAYED!

16 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/16/02 The sun has set countless hours ago. The street lights shine through the windows of the dilapidated windows and shine on the debris of the factory floor. There is a small fire burning towards the north end of the huge abandoned warehouse. The fire casts disturbing shadows on the crumbling cement walls. There is a massive figure hunched down by the fire. At closer examination it is revealed to be none other that one half of the current OSW tag team champions, Azrael Cain. The beast sits on the ground wrapped in his black and tattered cloak. His long black hair is down and hangs in his face. His dark eyes are fixed on something that he is holding in his pale hands. His black finger nailed fingers grasp the paper firmly. Whatever it is that he is holding is bother the fallen one greatly. With a loud crash at the other end of the factory, two small figures crash into the domicile through the huge steel doors. The figures are laughing and being obnoxious, ruining the ambiance of the setting. They are laughing in the high pitched squeals as they swear profusely. The beast rises to his feet quickly and looks on to see that he has been rudely interrupted by him and Hessian’s managers, Caleb and Minion. “Hey there big fella! Heh-Heh. How the hell are you doing? Heh-Heh-Heh.” The small hobo, Caleb, squeals as he brings a bottle of tequila up to his lips and takes a large swig. Cain crosses slowly towards the two managers that have just come in from a long night of drinking. Cain’s hair is down in his face still so that they can not see his eyes. The massive warrior’s six foot ten inch frame casts a frightening shadow that slowly grows over the managers as he approaches them silently. Cain breaks the awkward silence with is deep gravely voice. “Caleb, just the little infested creature that I wanted to see. I think that we have a couple of things that we need to talk about.” The hobo starts to get fidgety as Cain draws closer to him and Minion who is slowly walking backwards. Caleb brings the bottle of tequila back up to his lips again. After swallowing a large amount of the Mexican delight, the little man finally responds nervously. “And what would that be…big guy. Heh-Heh.” He tries to change the topic. “You should have come out with us. Heh-Heh. There was enough eye candy to drive a monk crazy. Heh-Heh. You really need to start to cut loose once in…” Caleb stalling is stopped short by Cain’s hand slapping around the old man’s grizzled throat. Cain hoists Caleb up to eye level. Caleb is now staring into the dark eyes of the beast as Cain begins to interrogate his manager. “And how was it that you were capable of paying for this little evening out for you and Minion?” Caleb replies immediately despite that he is finding it hard to breathe from the pressure being placed on his esophagus. His voice sounds like a frog croaking. “Well…you see…we…robbed some pretty boy on the way to the bar. Heh-Heh…” Caleb is stopped again by Cain’s grip tightening. “Is that so?” Cain responds as he further tightens the hold calling Caleb on his lie. Cain holds up what it is that he was perusing earlier that had him so upset. Caleb’s eyes grow wide in fear as he sees that Cain is holding his contract in his pale hands. Cain draws Caleb even closer to his face. “So, you are telling me that you didn’t pay for this little night out on the town with my salary that I get from OSW that I was not aware existed!?” Minion yelps and starts to back up. Caleb groans in pain and fear of being discovered. Cain still keeps his grip on the little man’s throat as his temper takes over. “You forgot to tell me about that little perk of the job didn’t you? That is a hefty some of money that you have been collecting these past couple of weeks. And I am sure that you just forgot to tell me about it right? Or were you going to get around to it eventually?” Cain finally slams Caleb down onto the debris filled floor. The hobo lands violently and rolls across the floor. The hobo begins to back peddle on his back across the floor trying to plead with the angered warrior. “Listen…big fella…you don’t want to do this. Heh-Heh. You need me! Heh-Heh-Heh.” Cain recoils in shock from the hobo’s pleading. “Oh, I need you, now huh! I need someone that is going to lie to me and steal what rightfully belongs to me. I need someone that is going to spend all of my money! You are going to be lucky if I let you escape with all of your limbs still attached!” Cain is slowly approaching the groveling manager as the massive steel doors open for a second time. A massive silhouette is standing in the doorway for a moment before it enters the factory. Once the figure comes into the light is obvious who the massive shadow belongs to. Hessian. His steel plated mask is glistening in the dim light that is being cast by the flames. Hessian approaches Minion slowly taking in what is going on. Hessian lowers his head to the imp’s level. “And what is going on here?” Minion chuckles as he answers. “Cain just found out that he actually gets paid for wrestling.” Hessian’s eyes move in confusion. “You mean he didn’t know?” Minion begins to laugh harder at the situation. “It seems that Caleb was keeping the salary for himself. Cain just liked what he was doing and never bothered share the profits with Cain.” Hessian shouts upright and quickly approaches Cain who appears as if he is getting ready to crush the hobo with his big black boots. Hessian attempts to put some sense into the enraged beast’s head. “Cain stop! We need the little pest!” Cain lowers his boot with indignity as he looks at his partner. “We don’t need him to officiate any of our matches! We can take any tag team on in that entire federation! There is no one that can stop us!” “That might be true, but at the same time, with him officiating, we can assure that we don’t get screwed somehow by anyone.” Hessian replies. Cain looks at the Goliath for a moment and then down at the shaking Caleb. There is a very awkward silence in the factory. All that can be heard is the crackling of the flames from the fire until Cain breaks the silence. “You got lucky this time you little wretch! But so help me, if you betray me ever again! I will crush you like a bug under my boot! You owe me! If you let me down, I cannot promise your safety!” Cain crosses to Hessian leaving Caleb sighing in relief. The hobo’s head falls backwards to the floor hard as he realizes that he has been spared. Hessian looks at Cain with a puzzled look as the two of them cross back towards the fire leaving their two managers at the other end of the factory. Hessian tries to change to subject. “This week we defend our prizes.” Cain sits down by the fire and looks up to his tag partner. “I have feeling that we are not going to have to worry about anything.” Cain turns his head towards his manager, who is still shaking in fear as to what just transpired. Cain turns his head back to Hessian again. “Even if Walczak and Krieg see it fit to come to the ring, there is no way that he will count for anyone else. And if they don’t, the Propaganda is finally going to what he deserves for not heeding our warnings as Vampir will get yet another lesson in getting in my way again.” Hessian seems pleased that Cain is calm now. There is silence as the Goliath sits down on the other side of the fire. Hessian looks at Cain who is staring at Caleb again and tries to get some answers. “What are you going to do now?” Cain turns back to Hessian and through the flames the Goliath can see a disturbing smile stretch across the beast’s face as he answers. “First we are going to crush Vampir Nosferatu and Propaganda. Then I think that I will let Caleb sweat it out for a little while. But first thing that I am going to do is find us a place that is worthy of two honored warriors as ourselves.”
