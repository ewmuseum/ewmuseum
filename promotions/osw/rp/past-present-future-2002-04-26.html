Past....Present....Future???

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-26-02 Ivan Stanislav sat on the weight bench in a black tank top and red pair of shorts, one of the few times he was not wearing his t-shirt, suspenders, pants, and boots. Well, he still wore the boots. Behind him a weight machine stood, a complex system of pulley’s, cords, and heavy weights. Behind the head of Ivan Stanislav hung a bar suspended by one such cord. He sat with his massive, pale elbows resting on his tree trunk-like legs. Sweat ran into his beard and matted it as he blinked a few times. Portions of the hammer and sickle tattoo that had long been drawn into his skin over his heart could be seen from the sides of the tank top. Various other weight machines could be seen in the background of the weight room, but it appeared that he was the only person present at the moment. Stanislav had remained in the United States, probably against what would have been deemed “acceptable behavior” by his superiors. The report that General Tomifei said he was going to submit would still be traveling through the hands of bureaucratic Russians for a few more days and with the recent major events upcoming within the OSW he used it as an excuse to remain in the country. Funny….I choose to remain in the United States when I could be in Russia. Stanislav sighed and cracked his neck as he chuckled. It was true, however. He had the chance to go back to his office, but perhaps he just needed to get away from Russia for a while. Perhaps forty years of Russia was enough for Ivan and he needed a few days to just relax somewhere else. And besides… For the first time in my life, I think there may be somewhere outside of Russia that Ivan could consider his home. Ivan reached his huge arms up to the bar above him and pulled it slowly behind his head. On the machine behind him a long line of weights could be seen being slowly raised as he gritted his teeth and pulled more. The gears and pulley’s squeaked as he brought he bar behind his neck to his enormous shoulders and slowly let it slide back up above his head. Air shot out of his nose as he finished his first rep…. What should Ivan do? Should he go directly to High Command and demand rectification? Ivan grunted and pulled on the bar again and brought it behind his neck and held it in place… I do what they want, and it is still not enough? I get shot at title which I will win, I increase positive relations between Red Army and the people, I bring money to the Russians… Ivan grunted and let the bar up again. …and still it is not enough! Still they are critical of my performance! Ivan began his third rep…. Why do they do this? What is their concern with Tempest? She has raised my morale! She has shown me part of myself I never knew existed and still…she is a problem for some reason. Ivan brought the bar down behind his neck and exhaled roughly. He held it, his arms shook slightly as he did. Sweat beaded around his forehead and dripped between his eyes. This is not what I need at moment! I need to focus on Sebastian Toomes! Not only is Jane Reagan eliminated from equation, but I have the people behind me! Ivan roughly lets the bar rise this time, the weight clangs in the background as he lets it hit a tad too roughly. His face flashes with disgust as he hangs on the bar. And they cause me most grief of them all! Those stupid fools that sit behind their desks and wish to only glorify themselves! When all is right…they must point out something wrong! Ivan reached up and grabbed the bar again and brought it down slowly…. Let them do what they wish. It will not faze me. Ivan will continue to attack situation in his own way…and when it is all over… Ivan tilted his head as his pull faltered and the bar rose a bit, as if the weight had gotten the best of Ivan. He grits his teeth and pulls harder, and pulls the bar down more, but somehow the weight is putting up a fight! …it will be…GREAT…weight off of my shoulders…. Ivan breathed out heavily and tried to bring the weight down as far as he had before, but somehow he couldn’t, as if the weight was heavier… Slowly he eased off, looking startled, but as the weight slapped into place in the stack, a giggle interrupted his thoughts. He turned to find Tempest dangling from the lateral arm of the machine. 'I was hoping for a fun ride!' She said with a sheepish grin. Instantly, Ivan smiled and rose from his seat and chuckled, “Why you little….” He laughed loudly and ran around the machine. Tempest ducked to the other side, keeping the machine between them as Ivan hollered to her, “If you do not let me catch you, I will just move entire machine!” He laughed and winked at her. Tempest looked worried and said, 'Ooo, I hadn't thought of that. But what if I hide in it!' She leapt for the top of the machine and swung upward, dangling by her knees from a high beam. “HA HA!” Ivan laughed as she leapt up and slid into the interior of the machine. “I can rip machine apart Tempest! Ivan will do anything if he wants to get something!” He laughed and tried to push his arm into the machine, but it was too large. He tried again at a different area but was unable to make much headway. Ivan scratches his chin and wrapped a hand around her shin as she dangled, “Is Tempest…ticklish?” He laughed and began to pull off her sneaker. Tempest squealed and fidgeted, gradually rolling backward and flipping off the machine. She laughed and came around to hug Ivan and said, 'Yes! So behave. How are ya?' Ivan laughed and hugged her back tightly. He let her go and held her by the sides of her arms and smiled, “I am well…better than Ivan may have been a few days ago.” She smiled and said, 'And you're still here, which is a special treat!' She tilted her head curiously and added, 'How come you didn't take you usual trip back to Russia?' Ivan swallowed and shrugged, 'I did not feel like it. I think perhaps I should take small break from Russia and allow myself to figure out what exactly is going on. Besides, I'm not alone in United States anymore.' He smiled and winked at her again. 'How did you find me?' 'Oh I just followed the trail of doorways with the tops knocked out of them,' She said with a chuckle. 'Actually I was gonna get in a little workout myself. Most of the time I just un around maniacally and that's enough, but once in awhile it helps.' 'Oh I just followed the trail of doorways with the tops knocked out of them,' She said with a chuckle. 'Actually I was gonna get in a little workout myself. Most of the time I just run around maniacally and that's enough, but once in awhile it helps.' Ivan nodded, 'Well, by all means, help yourself!' He motioned to the bench he had sat on, 'Ivan will adjust the weight for you, eh?' She looked down at the machine and said, '15 pounds... lemme try that!' She giggled and grabbed hold of the bar. She dangled from it, kicking the air. 'Uuuuhhhhhnnn.... I almost got it... really!' she said, straining playfully. Ivan laughed and fixed the weight for her to 15 pounds. He stood behind the machine with his hands on his hips, 'Come Little Storm, it is not very hard!' She chuckled and said, 'Maybe too easy, let's see.' She takes hold of the bar and reps the weight with ease. 'Well then,' Ivan chuckled, 'perhaps I can help you with the weight!' Ivan laughed and grabbed a hold of the weight. The bar abruptly stopped and Tempest tried to pull it down. 'Hey now!' she said with a less than serious scowl. 'DYAAHAAHAA!!' Ivan laughed and pulled on the cord, lifting Tempest up off of the bench by the bar! 'Wheeee!' Tempest squealed. She hung on and laughed while Ivan lowered her and raised her again. 'Hey this is way more fun than a regular workout.' 'Wheeee!' Tempest squealed. She hung on and laughed while Ivan lowered her and raised her again. 'Hey this is way more fun than a regular workout.' Ivan laughed loudly, 'You see?! Two people can play practical joke!!' He laughed and eased her down gently onto the bench and walked over beside her. She snickered and said, 'I never doubted it. So how do you feel? You all ready for Toomes?' She stood up and smiled encouragingly. Ivan nodded, 'But of course! Switchblade'll never know what hit him!' Ivan looked over at a far mirror and nodded to himself, 'Jane Reagan is out of commission, the people are behind me, The Red Army is succeeding.' Ivan paused for a moment, 'How do I feel? Funny...people rarely ask me that.' He smiled and nodded to Tempest, 'I feel good Tempest, I feel very good.' She smiled and said, 'Good. I admit I'm a little disappointed that I didn't get a match, but then again, I have a weird feeling you might need another pair of eyes this time. So it's not all bad.' “I would not want anyone else watching against Jane Reagan and her lackeys.' Answered Ivan, 'I am sure you will get your chance to vanquish the enemy Tempest, and if not, I will let you lay into little Sebastian during my match.' He laughed loudly, very happy now. Tempest wrinkled her nose and said, 'But that would mean I have to touch him. Ewww...' Ivan nodded thoughtfully, 'That is correct, but perhaps you can knock out some teeth and do us all favor! It is calculated risk!' She laughed and said, 'Maybe so.' She looked thoughtful for a moment and said, 'Ivan... do you think the commanders will relent when you win?' Ivan swallowed as an invisible shadow covers his face, 'I hope so, Tempest.' He took a step back and stared at the floor and then looked back up at her, 'They can do one of two things. They can relent their pressure, or they can try to tighten their hold.' Ivan stared at the mirror and whispered more to himself than to Tempest, 'If they try to tighten their hold....I will not let them..I will do something if I have to.' Tempest reached out and squeezed his hand as she said, ' I'm sure it'll be all right. They've just gotta see everything's great when you're world champion. And if they don't... well I can at least.' Ivan squeezed her hand back and smiled, 'I never doubted you will.' He thought for a moment, 'They are stubborn, I doubt they will listen to me. The 'truth' about them is showing more and more....much to my disappointment.' Tempest nodded and said, 'Yeah, what a rude awakening to find that they really aren't who you thought.' She shrugged and said, 'Ya know I read that book, most of it anyway. It doesn’t seem to me like Russia is really that way. And I'm not sure it was when it was the USSR either.' Ivan sighed, “I do not know, for longest time I was on the same side as those that are now critical of me,” Ivan shrugged, “now I am noticing another side. It makes me wonder if I was one of those stubborn officers that glared and yelled at those that perhaps…” Ivan paused, “were innocent.” Tempest smiled softly and said, 'Maybe, but at least you're thinking about it. That’s more than your so-called superiors.' She sighed and shook her head. 'How many of them are actually communist, as opposed to just power hungry? I mean at least you look around and try to be a part of things, not just run them.' Tempest nodded sadly and said, 'That's the problem with economic systems, communism or capitalism... they both assume people won't get greedy.' Ivan snorted, 'And in Capitalist system it is embraced!' Ivan shook his head, 'But why are we talking about this in middle of workout complex, right?' Tempest snickered and said, 'Yeah good point. We could be talking about it over a perfectly decadent cup of coffee or something!' Ivan snickered, 'Or Vodka, eh?' Ivan playfully tagged her with his elbow, almost knocking her over. 'Oof!' she huffed and laughed. 'Or whatever cools you down best.' She hugged him tightly and said, 'I can't wait for Head on Collision. I just know it's going to be a night to remember.' Ivan laughs and steadies her, 'Come Tempest, shall we go grab a...how do you say it...bite...to eat?' 'Yeah!' she said and looped her arm around his. She smiled and they exited the room. As they neared the door exit of the complex Ivan halted and looked over at Tempest, he almost whispered to her, 'Tempest?' Tempest looked up at him curiously and said, 'Hmm?' He swallowed as his brow wrinkled. He licked his lips, 'I...I just wanted...' he thought again and breathed in, 'I just wanted to tell you...' His eyes darted around as his voice dropped even lower, 'I do not know what is going to happen at Pay Per View, good things or bad things could happen, but Tempest, I....' Ivan sighed and looked away suddenly and breathed in deeply, trying to figure out what he was doing. He looked back and straightened his neck, trying to toughen more, ' I wish to thank you for having the patience to appreciate Ivan Stanislav and...' Ivan caught his breath quickly. Tempest peered up at him as he gathered his thoughts. He looked at her, for a long moment, he could tell she didn't know what he was trying to say. He tilted his head and smiled, his jaw seemed to quiver just once and he blinked, his eyes scanning her beautiful face, 'You are special, Tempest. You are special in way that I cannot even begin to use words to describe, but more importantly,' he smiled, 'you are special to me.' Tempest’s face lit up and she wrapped her arms around him. 'Awww... You are to me too. I don't know what I'd do without my Russian teddy bear anymore.' She looked up and winked at him. 'I'm glad you're a part of my life Ivan. And I hope you can stay a part of it.' She gulped slightly and hugged him. Ivan hugged her tightly and lifted her into the air. He brought his head next to hers, 'Forever Tempest. There are some things that will never become history...'
