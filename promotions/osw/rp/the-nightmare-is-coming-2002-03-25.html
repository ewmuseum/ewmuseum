The nightmare is coming...

25 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/25/02 The scene opens as it always seems to. A dark, dank, drab apartment in the slums of Brooklyn. Yet it always seems as if the scene doesn't really matter. No matter where he is, the only thing important to Frankie is between his ears. His mind runs his life. This is rarely a good thing as the years have taken their toll on this mind. Are the thoughts productive? Rarely. Are the thoughts coherent? Rarely. Are the thoughts positive? Never. Are the thoughts clouded by the pain? Always. Are the thoughts nice? Heh. Are the thoughts effective? Yes. Well, there he sits, plain as day. Eyes scanning the skyline of a distant Manhattan. His mind races. With what you may ask? Well, with what is always does. Hatred. Self doubt. Sorrow. Remorse. Revenge. Pain. He lowers his head into his hands and the thoughts race at the speed if light. 'Ahh, what the hell am I doing? Getting this kid, this child, involved in my dirty work. I'm leading him down the path that ruined me. The path of lies. The path of cheating. But hell, the kid is loving it. He relishes in the pain of Michael Dame. Maybe this was inevitable, maybe it was too late for the kid. Maybe no matter what I did, he would turn out bad. I have to think that, I mean, I gotta try and get some sleep at night. I just have to face facts, I am a failure. I failed in my life, and I just failed the kid. I am a bad person, I have to accept that. I have dedicated the last 10 years of my life to going straight, to leading a good life, and to doing what is right for others. That too has failed miserably. And now, I sit here, full of self doubt. The introspection of the past months has exhausted me. I am tired of doubting my decisions. I am tired of trying to do things for others and failing. It is time to say the two words I should have muttered long ago...' Frankie's head slowly rises, his eyes peering into the night. He mutters 'Fuck it.' A smile finally curls his lips. Not the sly smirk of old, but an actual smile. His mind continues, 'Damn right. Fuck it all. Fuck trying to be good. Fuck other people. Fuck self doubt. It is time to accept the way shit is, and to exploit everything. I hate everyone, I live for the pain of others, it is time for these things to be positive aspects, not negative. I will never silence the demons again, I will never push down the bad thoughts, I will never hold anything back, and I will enjoy every minute of it. The self doubt, the sorrow, the remorse, all gone. They are replaced by much more important things, hatred, revenge, and most importantly, Pain. It's time to let go Frankie. It's time to leave it all out there, to hold nothing back. How sick will it get? How much pain can you induce? Where will it end? Will you even survive the full fledged Legend of Pain? Well, it's time for these questions to be answered. Am I gonna be the loud, boastful, asshole that I have tried so hard not to be? Hell no. I may even be more quiet now then ever before. But my actions will speak volumes. And these volumes will compile the diary of this madman. The nightmare is coming.' Frankie stands up quickly, picks up the chair he was sitting in, and throws it through his window. The cold night air of Brooklyn rushes into his apartment. He steps to the window, peaks his head out, and yells, 'FUCK YOU ALL! This shit hole did this to me. Being around all this has made me who I am. Well, they created me, now they have to deal with me. No one, and I mean no one, will stop me. Will I lose matches? Yes. Will I bleed again? I sure hope so. Will I be beat? Yes. Will my opponents ever be the same? Hell no.' He begins pacing his apartment. A very creepy smile stays on his face. He thinks, 'So where do I start? Oh that's easy. Saturday night, The Red Army. Krieg and The Dumb Pollock. I can hear it now, these two, bitching about America. Saying this match is a test between capitalism and communism. Making this to be more then it is. This match is very simple. The two of them, against me and Drew. A ring full of big men who hate each other. Nothing on earth is as simple as this. Time will stand still, the clock will stop, and the calendar will roll backwards, it's gonna get old school.' He stops pacing right in front of a wall. He smiles and proceeds to punch a hole in it. He shrugs, 'Fuck it.' Fade to black.
