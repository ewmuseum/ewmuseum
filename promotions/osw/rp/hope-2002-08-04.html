Hope?

4 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/4/02 Tempest took a deep breath and walked into an older looking office building. It seemed to be something of the Cold War era, with few windows and those frosted. Inside it seemed dark and sterile, the hallways echoing with her footsteps. She seemed entirely out of place in a pair of reflective denim jeans and a little pink top. She stopped at the elevator and looked at the directory. Her finger traced past dentists, lawyers, two palm readers and a taxidermist to the ninth floor… blank. That’s where she’d find him. She stepped into the elevator and waited out the ride then stepped out and walked to the office at the furthest corner of the floor. She sighed and said, “This has gotta be where he is.” And she knocked. She waited for a moment before a high pitched, angular voice was heard on the other side of the door, “Who is it?” Tempest sighed and said, “It’s me Alexei. C’mon I need to talk to you.” There was another long pause as soft footsteps neared the door. A lock was turned, then another, and another, and yet, another, until finally the large door slowly opened just a crack and the head of Alexei Ruslan peered through the crack, “T…Tempest?” Tempest smiled and waved at him. “Hi Alexei! How ya doin’? Hey, you gonna let me in or what? It’s not like I have a troop of marines behind me or anything.” She smirked at him mischievously as his eyes widened. Alexei swallowed and blinked before slowly opening the door, revealing his dank, quiet office. The bland office left nothing to the imagination, with four white walls, shades pulled over the square windows, and the white, flickering fluorescent lighting casting irregular shadows throughout the office. He backpedaled, somewhat surprised to see Tempest at his door, “Uh..nyet..not at all..come uh..come in.” He backed into his desk and steadied himself. Tempest stepped in and closed the door behind her with a nervous smile. “Thanks. I um… I wanted to talk to you, if you don’t mind about… about Ivan.” She fidgeted seeming more out of place, and not merely because of the scenery. Alexei cleared his throat and straightened his brown trench coat, “But…but of course.” He took his hat off and set it on the desk, “Ivan…” he nodded to himself, “Ivan… not at all! What would you like to know?” He worked up a crooked grin. Tempest sighed and said, “Plenty of things really. I mean… I guess… well first off how bad are things for him right now? I mean with Russia. I know that’s tearing him up and I guess I wanted to help. But… now I’m afraid I may be making things worse.” She wrung her hands, looking at him with obvious concern. Ruslan furrowed his brow and sighed, “Things in Russia… are not too good, Tempest. I have concerns about the whole situation.” He thought for a second and looked at the desk, and then back up at Tempest, “I would not worry about causing trouble in that regard… any trouble Ivan is in is mainly between himself and the State, I assure you.” Tempest nodded and said, “Well, I saw some of that stuff when it happened, but I meant that I was afraid of making things worse for him personally. I mean, I know that the State doesn’t approve of me, but… well I hoped that he’d be happy to see me and that would take away some of the pain about it. Now, I feel like maybe I’m making it worse. I wanted to help, but it doesn’t seem like he’s going to let me.” “Oh,” began Alexei, “you mean, personal matters.” Again he paused and thought for a moment and then looked up, “He was happy to see you, believe it or not. He was… overjoyed.” Alexei pursed his lips, “He may not have shown it… obviously, but yes, he was happy.” The smallish Russian shifted his weight and glanced up at the ceiling, “Did it take away some of the pain?” He seemed to ask himself and then leveled his gaze to her and answered flatly, “No.” Tempest nodded slightly, her gaze, falling noticeably. “Yeah, I kinda figured that part. But you think he was happy? I dunno… I guess I expected it in a way. He’s too proud to just forget everything. At least I think that’s it.” Alexei absently shook his head, “You still do not understand it all, Tempest, do you?” He looked straight into her eyes, “I have known Ivan for twenty years and I have never seen him react in such a way to a person like he did when he met you.” He paced to the side and looked at the dirty floor, “Yes, he is a proud man, but there is more to him than just that. A side of Ivan emerged in that short period of time that he knew you that had never been seen in his forty years of life. To let an outsider, such as yourself, close to him was very… trying on him, and yet he was confident in his decision.” Alexei swallowed and rounded the desk, standing beside her, “I have seen Ivan hurt so many people, to levels that one would think impossible and not bat an eye… but when he hurt you… it deeply affected him.” He thought for a minute and clasped his hands together, “I think he gave you his love and did not know that… and when you left…” he thought for a long time once again, struggling with his thoughts, “…he lost a part of himself. Does that make sense?” Tempest nodded a little and said, “Yes… in a way. I can understand that, but what I can’t understand is why he doesn’t want to talk to me or anything. I can understand if he’s scared… wow, never thought I’d say that. But I can.” She rubbed her arms a little and continued, “I’m scared, terribly so. I came back to the OSW for him. Sure I want to have a hand in avenging Ed, cuz he was a friend. But the big, main reason is Ivan. I just couldn’t stay back and act like he wasn’t important anymore. And that’s what it was when I left, an act.” She looked up at Alexei and said, “I don’t know if you believe me, but the truth is I was just too scared and confused to face it. I know that’s not very admirable, but it’s the truth, I just wish I could tell that to Ivan.” Alexei took a step back and narrowed his eyes, “You did not come here because you were out of job?” Tempest shook her head and said, “No, I left the fWo on good terms in spite of all the legal wrangles. I mean, I wasn’t happy sitting around instead of wrestling, but if I had stuck it out I’d still have a job with them when they return to television. But… the time off gave me the chance to really think about things. And the more I did, the more I realized I couldn’t be happy the way things left off with Ivan.” She hung her head slightly and said, “I know he may not take me back, but I feel like I have to at least talk to him, get him to listen long enough to understand what really happened for me.” Alexei breathed slowly, “If it makes you feel any better… I believe your motives, Tempest. But you must understand that your timing is impeccable.” He shook his head, “To say that Ivan is under much stress would be understatement! He is fighting this Think Tank, dealing with people of whom he had once thought were enemies, trying to show the fans that he can be trusted.” He nodded and accented the word. “Trusted, Tempest. Your entry, in way, has moved his problems from professional ones, to personal ones. Never before has he had to deal with such a situation, I do not believe.” He cleared his throat, “Talking with him… may be difficult. That is where his pride may come in. You ignored him… the one person who always was there to listen and understand, ignored him and only naturally, almost by instinct, he is threatened and very defensive about something such as that. I do not think he is listening Tempest… and I do not think it is because he is being malicious… but rather he is overwhelmed.” Alexei shook his head, “Ivan cared for you, and he cares for you now, Tempest. He lied to Russia! His Motherland! The country that he bled for. He lied to Mother Russia for you and defended you whenever he could. He is losing Russia… and has nowhere to turn so he turns inward and tries to make things perfect… but they are not.” Alexei shrugged, “Talking to Ivan will probably not work… you need to somehow show him your intentions. Symbols are important to Ivan. You need to find something, something true, something perfect that he cannot have doubt and then, I believe he will listen. He cannot trust most words nowadays, but symbols…they should do trick.” Tempest blinked rapidly and said, “Well… I tried to help him with the Think Tank. In fact I intend to keep at that. He has to know I’m a good ally if nothing else. As far as symbols… that’s harder. You’re right that I always listened and didn’t judge him. I never minded that. And with everything that happened, I was never afraid of him… until that one incident.” She shook her head and said, “Once he lied to me, I didn’t know what was true and what wasn’t. In a way I was feeling then the way he is now.” She paused and shook her head very slightly. “I don’t know what to do really except be there. And I guess I just wonder if that’s enough now.” “For the time being, it may be enough.” Alexei almost grinned, “You know how Ivan is, he is slow mover sometimes when things such as this occur. He thinks about this a lot, Tempest, more than you could imagine. I think,” he paused and his volume dropped, “I think he is scared, Tempest. And also, he does not wish to hurt you again. He thought he was doing right thing before and it was horribly wrong, and therefore he is doubting himself.” He took a step closer, “Not giving up on him is first step, I believe. If you help him in wrestling ring, that is certainly start. For months while you were gone, he created these walls to shut out any emotional harm, now they need to be knocked down. I cannot knock them down, only you can. And to do that…it may be difficult.” She nodded and said, “I know. And that’s gonna be the tough part. Running away is a survival instinct Alexei. And it’s kept me alive a long time, through some of the worst situations. You have no idea how hard it is to resist the urge to run away when things are hard. But this is different… this time I can’t let myself. I just hope he knows that on some level, and that he knows he’s the first person I ever really tried this hard for.” Alexei patted her arm, somewhat clumsily, “You are strong girl, he knows that. It is difficult to stand in the way of danger, particularly when it is Ivan Stanislav that you are dealing with. Do not give up hope, Tempest. If you do it will never get better. I know this. Ivan does not know how to handle this situation and therefore he cannot act on it. Perhaps the shock of your return has been much and I think he is only acting on instinct. Give it some time Tempest. You cannot expect these things to right themselves immediately, particularly with Ivan Sergeiovich Stanislav.” “Yeah, that much I know,” she said and chuckled. “In a way I don’t mind so much because I know that he at least thinks about things, doesn’t just get caught up and forget why he’s doing something. And thanks Alexei, you’ve given me what I came here for.” She smiled and patted his arm and turned to leave. Alexei furrowed his brow and blinked, “Uh…Tempest? What uh… were you looking for?” She looked back over her shoulder with a sunny smile and said, “Hope.” As she reached the door he almost jumped forward. “Wait!” He looked at the floor, surprised by his outburst and meekly looked up. “How uh.. ehem.. did you find me?” Tempest laughed and stepped out the door, peering back in with a mysterious glance and said, “Oh, I have my sources Alexei, I have my sources.” She winked and stepped out the door, her laughter audible outside in the hall.
