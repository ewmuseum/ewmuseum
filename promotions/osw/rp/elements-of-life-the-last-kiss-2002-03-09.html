Elements of Life: the Last Kiss

9 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 9, 2002 First there were two… Rain drops hard, thunder crackles, Shome and Corg are frantically scurrying out of the Psychic shop, from the awful news they have just heard. “See, see I told you we shouldn’t gone in there!” Corg exclaims at Shome. But yet again, Shome assures him. “Dude, I’m not gonna die.” Sternly from Shome. “Besides, who believes those Wiccans anyway? We’re Satanists, and we hate Wiccans, remember?” Corg is sketchy about the whole situation on the count of he just was told that the closest thing to his black heart is going to be taken away forever. “Yeah, but still… hearing that you’re one true love is going to die soon isn’t really that comforting.” “That was sweet.” Shome says. “But shit man, don’t believe everything you hear. I’m not dead yet, am I?” Shome extends her lips onto Corg’s landing a perfect genuine kiss. The love is felt, but quickly taken away… “Hey, bitch!” a gangster voice yells, coming from around the corner. A loud, echoing chorus of a bullet comes speeding from nowhere, nailing Shome directly on her left spinal plate. The love was broken, but never lost. Their lips spread apart, as Shome falls to the hard concrete, and the once luscious blood spewing from the whole in her pale skinned back. Corg looks shell shocked, everything is repeating itself slowly, like action/adventure movies. “Fuck!” the shell shockness is destroyed, Corg watchers his lover flow her blood onto the grey. “Fuck!” He bends down, reaching for his fallen love. The scene is too horrid, even for his eyes. To watch his insane lover die in front of him, it’s all just too much. Corg quickly does the manly thing, as he grasps onto the whole which was pierced into Shome’s back. He clutches her head, and holds it close to his. For the first time, maybe ever, he feels the one thing that he could never allow… Sadness… A bucket of tears fall gently onto Shome’s pale face, as Corg cries outloud, kissing her for the last time.
