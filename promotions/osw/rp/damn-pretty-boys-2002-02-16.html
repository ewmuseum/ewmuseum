Damn Pretty Boys

16 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/16/02 The sun has long been set giving way to the dark night. There are very few stars that can be seen through the clouds that have set in over the city. There are several shadows cast by the street lamps that line the road. There are more people on the streets than one would expect at this time of night. This can be accounted for the fact that it is the weekend following Valentine’s Day and everyone’s hearts are still filled with passion for their loved ones. There are shadows being cast of couples throughout the streets. They hold each other lovingly as they walk hand in hand. There is a pair of shadows though that stick out form the rest of the love filled silhouettes. The shadows are of none other than Azrael Cain and his loyal friend and manager, Caleb. Cain is wearing his black and tattered robe and his long black hair is hanging down in his face. Caleb is donning his dirty and stained khaki trench coat. The men walk together through the night air. There is a look on frustration on the fallen one’s face. Cain begins to growl through his teeth. “Why are there so many of these vermin on the street at this hour of night? And why do they insist on expressing so much love for each other? This is revolting.” The small hobo lets out a chuckle at his annoyed friend. “Because it is the weekend after Valentine’s Day. Heh-Heh. All these damn fools’ hearts are filled with love. Heh-Heh. Relax. Soon enough they will all be consumed with their jobs and greed again and you won’t have to deal with them being out so late. Heh-Heh. Although, I do have to say that all this shit is making me feel nauseous. Heh-Heh-Heh.” Cain is starting to let his annoyance with all of the love in that air break through. “I can’t take this! Why do they have to be out at this hour of night? These late hours are the only time that I can actually get out and not have to worry about facing these obnoxious creatures and just because some day on a calendar says that the other day was a day to celebrate their love, I have to suffer! LOVE! Bah!” Caleb shares the same feeling of his large friend. “Love, Bah!!! Heh-Heh-Heh” There are a couple of seconds of silence between the dark figures until Cain breaks it. “Love! Bah!” Caleb chuckles for a moment and then despite the risk of sounding like a parrot, “Love! Bah!!! Heh-Heh-Heh.” The massive Cain looks down at his companion with a look of anger. Caleb just smiles at his friend and shrugs his shoulders. Cain then reaches back and slaps the dirty old man in the back of the head like he was reprimanding a child. Caleb squeals in pain. “What the hell was that for? Heh-Heh. I’m sorry that I am not as articulate as you are. Heh-Heh.” The hobo then proceeds to imitate the already annoyed Cain. “My name is Azrael Cain! I hate everything! Mortals suck! Heh-Heh. Blood, pain, carnage, you too will feel all these! Heh-Heh-Heh-Heh.” Cain’s eyes grow wide with anger as he watches Caleb continue to mock him. Finally, Cain snaps and grabs the dirty man up and holds him high in the air. Cain starts to shake the little man profusely. Everyone of Caleb’s limbs are flailing about violently as Cain scolds his manager for his actions. “You ungrateful little whelp! How dare you mock me! Who do you think you are? If it was not for me, you would still be living on the streets!” Caleb begins to laugh uncontrollably at his attacker. “Wait, you’re right! HEH-HEH-HEH! And now I get to live in an abandoned factory with you! HEH-HEH-HEH-HEH.” Cain roars with pure animalistic rage at the hobo that would now remind anyone of a laughing hyena. “You little maggot! I should kill you where you stand! What has gotten into you! And give me one good reason why I shouldn’t send you back to hell this very moment!” Caleb is still laughing uncontrollably as he attempts to give Cain a reason. “Because whether or not you are willing to admit it, I amuse you. Heh-Heh. I give you some entertainment in this wretched existence that we are both stuck. Heh-Heh. And without me, you would be lonely. Heh-Heh-Heh.” Cain releases his grasp dropping Caleb to pavement. Caleb lands with a loud thud. Azrael then lowers his head yells again. “You are just lucky that I have a match tomorrow night and I am saving my energy for it or that would have been the end of your miserable existence!” Caleb fights to get to his feet, but the body of an old man isn’t exactly the most athletic shell a dark angel could be imprisoned in, so Caleb falls to the pavement a second time. Cain begins to walk away from his floundering friend, but the hobo begins to run his toothless mouth again. “Heh-Heh. Yeah, you better save up that energy for Civil Disobedience. Heh-Heh. I don’t think I can tolerate those damn pretty boys anymore. Heh-Heh-Heh.” Cain stops walking. He turns to Caleb. “What did you just say?” Caleb finally gets back to his feet and begins to brush himself off as he repeats himself. “Pretty boys! I hate their guts! Heh-Heh. Well, I don’t hate their guts when you are holding them in your hand and they are still alive writhing about screaming ‘No, No, Help me....” Cain interrupts the ranting hobo. “What are you rambling about now? What is a pretty boy?” Caleb has a dumbfounded look on his face as he responds to the warrior’s question. “You know Heh-Heh a Pretty Boy!” Cain just begins to scratch his head with a baffles expression worn on his face. Caleb sighs and answers. “A pretty boy is like a Heh-Heh...panty waist. Heh-Heh. They’re like those very vain guys that spend hours trying to make themselves look good for their fans. Heh-Heh. There like woman in that respect, but at the same time women adore them. It makes me ill. Heh-Heh-Heh.” Cain looks shocked. “You mean to tell me that women don’t like real men anymore more?” Azrael begins to walk down the sidewalk in bewilderment. “What has happened to this world? I remember the days when women would throw themselves at you if you made it home from battle. The man that was covered in the most enemy’s blood would usually get two or three wenches for that evening.” Caleb finally catches up with the shocked Cain and continues to show Cain the awful truth about women these days. “It’s all true! Heh-Heh. Today, women will throw themselves at a man only if he looks as feminine as they do. Heh-Heh. If you go into war, these days you are usually welcomed home with all of the women protesting you and all the courageous acts that you did. Heh-Heh. Damn feminists. Heh-Heh. You know, thinking about it, Hawke and Masters get a crap load of women. Heh-Heh. Both of them are as feminine as can be and at the same time, they both never do battle in the ring. They just block attacks with their face. Heh-Heh-Heh-Heh-Heh!!!!” Caleb begins to laugh deliriously at his own joke. Cain just sighs and continues to walk through the streets of the dark city. Caleb’s creepy laughter is echoing through the night air. Some of the people begin to stare at the two dark figures. Caleb sees something out of the corner of his eye stops the hobo’s laughter. Caleb’s expression completely changes to one of pure hatred and jealousy. The hobo then bolts down the sidewalk towards a couple that is staring at the small hobo and the massive beast. Caleb let’s loose a blood curdling battle cry as he runs to the couple. “PRETTY BOY!!!!!!!” Caleb lunges at the man a screaming “Pretty Boy” at the top of his lungs and proceeds to pound on the young man’s face. The young lady that was with the man begins to scream and run in panic. Caleb reaches into his pocket. Caleb removes a razor and starts to hack away at the poor man’s face. Blood spews forth from the open wounds that will definitely scar him for life. Caleb finally gets control of himself and stops attacking the poor youth. The blood covered hobo walks back to his massive friend who was watching the assault, but with a strange look on his face. “What the hell is your problem? Heh-Heh.” Caleb asks Cain as the hobo starts to wipe the blood from his razor. “Nothing, you just gave me a good idea. We need to hit those punks where it hurts. Their vanity is their weakness and I think it is about time that we exploit it. I think it is time that we make Civil Disobedience bleed for the way that their presence continues to insult us. Those boys say that they don’t need anything to beat me and Hessian, but tomorrow night, They are going to need an entire army to stop me form getting revenge.” Caleb rolls his eyes at Cain and starts walk past him. “I need to get out of here. Heh-Heh. You were right all of this love stuff is making me sick. Heh-Heh.” Cain and Caleb walk down the poorly lit sidewalk as the sounds of the man’s screams and ambulance sirens pierce through the night air. Cain has a malicious grin on his face as he starts to think about his match against Trevor Hawke and Seth Masters. “Seeing how it is the time of year that calls for mortals to share their feelings for one another, I think that maybe we should let Civil Disobedience know exactly what Heresian Bloodlust thinks about them. Caleb starts laughing at Cain’s last statement. As the two figure disappear into the darkness, The hobo laugh can be heard cackling at his friend’s plans for Civil Disobedience.
