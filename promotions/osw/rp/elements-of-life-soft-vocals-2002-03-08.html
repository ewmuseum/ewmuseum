Elements of Life: Soft Vocals

8 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 8, 2002 “Come on, it’ll be amusing.” Shome pleads with Corg, hand-in-hand. “What do you have to lose?” “You.” Corg replies, sympathetically. Rain looks as if it is ready to fall, hard onto the dry concrete in Charlotte, North Carolina. Corg and Shome are spending the week in Charlotte, all in preparation for Crowning Glory. “When you’re sympathetic, it makes it hard for me to reply. I love you when you’re sympathetic.” Behind Shome is a rather small building. Vitally small in commerce and size, the sign reads Neo-Psychic Joru. Shome is trying her hardest to persuade her boyfriend, Corg into getting a psychic reading. “And I’ll love you even more if you come inside.” Corg sighs. “But they say that Psychics are the works of the devil.” he says unsurely. “But you are the devil.” Corg smiles coming to a realization. “Oh yeah, you’re right.” “So come on!” Shome pulls on Corg’s arm, forcing him into the little psychic building. “Whatever…” Corg moans, in a soft vocal tone. As Corg and Shome enter, a bell begins to ring from the top of the entrance door, signifying that there are customers. As she enters, Shome’s long, dark blue hair wisps in the outside wind. The shop is rather small, and the upper floor, which Corg and Shome are on, has nothing but dirty wooden walls, and a stair case leading to a basement. “Well…” Corg mutters in disgust. “I can see this is gonna be worth while. I’m glad it’s only four bucks.” “Would you hush?” Shome exploits at Corg, in search of some sort of assistance. A voice is heard echoing from the limits of the dark stair case. “Come on down…” Corg tries not to laugh at the stupidity of the situation. “See, it’s happening already.” Shome assures. Corg, no longer laughing, is confused by Shome’s assuring comment. “What is happening?” Shome does not rely. She tightens her grip on her lover’s hand, and leads him down the stairs. Down the stairs… portraits of colors, random colors. Black stairs, with a golden skinny rail barely standable. They reach the bottom, where Corg and shome see a mystical room of art. The room is totally black, with odd paintings filling up the wall space. Paintings of elements… air, water, fire, and earth… a perfectly blended Wicca habitat. “I saw you coming.” Says a soft voice, directing from the left corner. “So come.” A woman, aging at about 40, dark skinned with endless amounts of jewelry and color, sits on a stool in the left corner. “Come…” Rightfully so, Corg and Shome proceed to the woman’s nesting. Without warning, 12 candles appear, lined up in a perfect circle on the floor by the Wiccan woman. 0 “Do not be afraid, just sit down inside of the circle.” The woman instructs. Corg and Shome carefully place their bodies within the circle, Corg more cautious than Shome. The floor is dry, but looks to be very damp with the glare that it gives off. The woman smiles with a comforting sense in her eyes. “Hello Isiic, hello, um… Shome.” She hesitates to say Shome’s name, since Shome’s real name is not known. “You may call me Joru, as you may have guessed from the name of this building. I’m going to have to make this a quick session; on the count of I must visit a friend in need.” Shome and Corg, who are sitting on the floor surrounded by Wiccan candles, nod in agreement. “Okay, first with you Isiic.” The woman mutters, as she closes her eyes, and concentrates on some dense thinking. “Hmm, let’s see… you’re parents, they are dead.” Corg looks down at his lap with an emotionless face filled with sadness. Shome puts her arm around Corg, and comforts him by streaking her hands through his spiky red hair. “But their spirits are all in well, within the heavens.” The woman opens her eyes, breaking the concentration. “I’m not going to tell you what I see,” she informs within reality. “but I am going to tell you what I know.” She closes her eyes, and goes back into her mental, psychic concentration, totally out of reality. “Isiic… I see you, killing another. Killing? Killing the one named Ritchie, although he is not the first.” Her eye-lids tremble as she tries to grasp onto some form of knowledge. “You have ended other lives as well, 57 to be exact.” Corg’s face turns from his normal pale skin to an almost normal tone of peach by the cause of embarrassment, but that quickly fades. The Wiccan continues, while the 12 candles still glimmer around Corg and Shome. “You are going to fall in love, which I can see you already have.” Shome reaches her lips forward at plants a kiss on Corg’s cheek, who is still feeling the emotions of the loss of his parents. “You are going to have an eternal bondage, or eternal love. The most beautiful of its kind.” Corg finally looks up, seeing the happiness that is in front of him. Shome… A gulp passes through the throat of the woman, but she keeps her eye-lids closed tightly. “Shome…” another gulp. “You have already been acknowledged of this eternal love. But the love will not be physical for much longer…” There is a great pause. Corg stares at Shome, who is staring back. The woman hesitates to extract the cruel information. “Your death is near, Shome. Very near…” The death of a goddess. The death of a realm. fin
