Mike Goes to JAIL!

4 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/04/02 When we last saw Mike...he was arrested for indecent exposure and carted away in a police car. Who would’ve thunk it? Well...Mike sure wouldn’t have. But what Mike did in that public park happened for a reason. He realized that he is not cut out to be some sort of municipality worker who gets walked on like a welcome mat by everyone. The job was too small time compared to his past work experiences. He went from Canadian Hero....to Canadian Zero. All in a matter of months. This arrest and the park incident was a severe blow to Mike’s reputation, which he is still trying to gain back since his days in PCW. The camera brings us back the odd scene of a completely naked Mike Marvelous being carted away in a police car. Mike has his head lowered as the cop in the front keeps his eyes on the road. The cop switches between the radio stations and stops at a rock station. He takes out a small package of cigarettes from his right breast pocket and takes one out. As he lights it up, he glances into the rear view mirror and looks back at the naked Mike Marvelous. He can’t help but shake his head and laugh as Mike sits there, shivering and sobbing under his breath. “Hey...you’re that Mike Marvelous guy from PCW, ain’tcha?” Mike just continues to sit there, bowing his head, chattering his teeth and sobbing. The cop takes a couple of puffs from his smoke and then his eyes appear once again in the rear view mirror. “Yeah...I seen you on T.V. To tell the truth...I think ya fuckin’ sucked, but that’s just my opinion.” A call sounds on the radio and the cop decided to shut it off. It’s not very often that he gets to chat with a celebrity, let alone arrest one for indecent exposure. “But hey...every federation needs their jobbers.” Mike shoots the officer a dirty look then bows his head again. “So, you been following PCW lately?” “Not since I got beaten within an inch of my life and thrown into a coffin 6 months ago and then received a pink slip in my hospital mail...” “Well, there’s a new fed now called PCW. It’s run by the Phantom Booker’s wife Jane Reagan. It’s called Old School Wrestling. The debut is this Saturday. It’s pretty cool shit.” Mike suddenly doesn’t look so upset. He looks up into the rear view mirror. “You don’t say...” “And the sweet thing is...Reagan said she signed guys like HANK~! to 2-Million dollar deals, and she said she is signing away all the good talent right from under Nelson’s nose!” Mike’s eyes grow wide. Even he can’t believe this. “Goodness gracious! Things have really gotten messy since I left...” “That’s one way of puttin’ it. Hey...since you were fired from PCW...why don’t you go work for OSW?” A smirk suddenly spreads across Mike’s face. It is a mischievous smirk, but also rather cocky. “I was fired from PCW...not OSW. I hate PCW. Maybe if I join OSW I can get revenge on all the people who have insulted and belittled me. I will show them just who their Canadian Hero is!!!” “Hey, that’s all well and good...but there is the small matter of your little uh....romp around the park in your birthday suit....” Suddenly, the smirk, confidence, and the spark all leave Mike’s face and he snaps out of it. He’ll never get into OSW with a criminal record. “Well, here’s the station, Marvelous.” The cop gets out of the car and opens Mike’s door for him. Mike steps out and the cop puts a winter coat over him. “Here, lemme cover you up for God’s sake...nobody wants to see you walk into the station with your manhood flappin’ in the wind...” And the cop walks Marvelous up the stairs and through the front doors of the police department, a look of discomfort and confusion crosses Mike’s face. “Umm...why didn’t you give me the coat all along instead of letting me sit there naked for the entire drive?” The cop simply looks at Marvelous and winks. “Just try not to be someone’s prison bitch. Enjoy your stay.” The cop hands Marvelous off to another police officer once he steps in and the new officer leads Mike through the office and towards a desk. He takes the cuffs off Mike and pushes Mike down in a seat in front of a small wooden desk. He slides a piece of paper in front of Mike and an ink pad. Mike takes a small black pen that is tied to the table and signs a couple of the papers, and then looks up at the officer. The officer opens the pad up, grabs Mike roughly by the hand, and mashes his fingers down on the ink pad one by one, then pressing them onto little boxes on the paper reserves for each finger print. Mike just watches on as the police officer completes this routine that he does about 40 times a day, and then gasps as the officer pulls Mike up from the chair and begins to push him roughly towards a hall way marked “HOLDING CELLS”. The corridor has large white cinderblock walls and as Mike is pushed down the corridor, he glances from left to right, looking at all the cells, trying not to look the inmates in the eyes. One inmate gets up off his bed from reading a dirty magazine and stands near the bars. As he stands up, many other convicts do too. They all look at Mike. Some of them shooting him dirty looks, some of them dragging their thumbs across their throats, and some of them winking at him. “Hey...looks like we got us a fresh fish! Come’ere fishy darlin’...you can be my bunk buddy!” “Hey new fish! You look awful pretty....I don’t know whether I am gonna kill ya...or fuck ya!” “You betta hope your soul belongs to God, fresh fish.....cause if you’re ass gets thrown in this cell with me....yo ass belongs ta ME!” Sweat begins to trickle down Mike’s brow as he eyes side to side rapidly and then suddenly he shouts out. “I GET A PHONE CALL!” The Officer stops and lets out a long sigh. “Alright....but you got two minutes. That’s it. Any more than that and I’ll use your head to press down on the receiver.” The officer once again grabs Mike and pushes him forward. This time to the end of the corridor, through a barred gate, and into a small room where a guard is sitting eating a powder donut. “Frank, keep an eye on him and put him in his cell once he is done with his two minutes.” The pudgy guard looks up from the Archie Comics book he was reading, brushes powder from the powder donut off his shirt, and stands up. “Will do...” The officer walks away and the pudgy guard turns towards Mike. “Now, you heard him. Two minutes. So be quick.” “OK” Mike picks the phone up and dials the operator. “Hi...I’d like to be directed to Jane Reagan in Easton. Collect call...I’m sure she can afford it.” “Ok..who is the call from?” “Mike Marvelous” “Ok, please hold.” Mike waits and taps his foot and suddenly, he hears ringing and a woman picks up after hearing “You have received a collect call from Mike Marvelous”. “Hello? Marvelous?” “Hi! Jane! It’s me, Mike! I am in a jail at Nova Scotia, Canada! I only have like, a minute and I need your help!” Jane is heard sighing on the other end. “Go on...” “I need you to send somebody to bail me out. And if you do that...I will work a month for OSW...FREE! But I need your help! I’ll do anything!” “Really....and how do I know you won’t get lazy like you did last time?” “Because things are different now! I miss getting in the ring and I miss the roar of the crowd! I need to get out of this place, I am suffocating!” “Well...I suppose. But Marvelous...” “....Yes?” “If you screw up this time....you are never coming back!” “Ok! DEAL! Now kick, get me out of here as fast as you can!” “I can wire the money to the prison right now. Get the guard on the phone.” Mike looks over at the fat guard and hands the phone over to him. “It’s for you...” The guard looks confused, but yet, he still picks up. “Hello?.....yeah.....yeah....ok, hold on, I’ll get him.” The guard leaves the corridor for a minute and comes back with a tall man in a uniform with “cheif” on his badge. The chief walks over to the phone, talks for a couple minutes with Reagan, and then hangs up. “Get out of here, Marvelous. But if we ever catch you naked in public again, no brood with lots of money will save you...” “You got it!” With that, Mike strides confidently down the corridor and looks at all the cons. “Where ya goin’ new fish?!? I thought we had a date!” “Hey...come back here! I haven’t had some good head in a while!” Mike just simply shoots an arrogant grin at them and thumbs his nose to them while sticking out his tongue. This causes an uproar and the prisoners all spring from their beds and reach their arms through the bars, grabbing at Mike to kill him. Mike quickly runs down the rest of the corridor, walks through the front office, waves to everyone, and leaves. Mike stands out on the sidewalk, still wearing nothing but a winter coat, and hails a taxi. The taxi stops and he gets in...OSW bound!
