Wake-Up Call

18 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/18/02 Ugggggggh... An audible groaning is heard, and the camera blinks once, twice, as it becomes apparent that we're viewing a scene through a pair of eyes... blurry eyes, ones that take seconds to come into focus before a echoing voice is heard... Oh no, not here again... godDAMMit! ... and with that last slightly echoing shout it's apparent that we're inside the head of one 'Classic' Clay Vendetta as the scene finally comes somewhat into focus. It's the hospital again, and as we look out of Vendetta's eyes the camera can see the door straight ahead with the sink and a couple of monitors, typical hospital-type stuff. Peering around the room, Vendetta catches sight of a mirror... Oh, shit. I KNEW that those jealous bastards would catch up to me sooner or later... ... says the voice in Vendetta's head as he catches a glimpse of himself in the mirror. The bandages on his head have seeped through and dried, leaving bloody patches where there should have been blond hair. Vendetta has a shiner of the first degree, with his right eye nearly swollen shut. Dammit, I'm gonna need another bleachjob... stupid sons of bitches are gonna pay for that if I've gotta beat it out of their wallets myself... 'Clay? Clay honey; are you awake?' The voice, external, is a pretty one, and as Clay slowly turns his head to the right... ...ow ow ow that is NOT fun... he thinks... but the voice is Niki Weathers, holding onto Clay's hand with a look of urgency and sadness in her face. We see her bend down and plant a kiss right below the camera, on Clay's cheek, and give him a big but gentle hug... 'Hey baby,' Clay says. 'Hey, I'm glad you're awake; I was so worried about you... first the food poisoning and now this,' Niki says, her eyes starting to well up with tears. 'Oh, c'mon,' Clay says, grimacing a smile. 'You knew I was gonna be okay.' Oh yeah, sympathy points. Soon as I get out of this hospital bed the things I'm gonna do to her... and Clay wiggles his toes and bends his knees slightly below the bed covers. 'Excellent, excellent... everything below the waist in perfect condition, which means that the Classic Unit is ready to go... maybe I won't even need to wait that long...' 'Those bastards,' Niki continues. 'Stupid cowards couldn't even wait until you were 100% and it's all that asshole Tyler Nelson's fault! If I could get my hands on him right now... in fact, I wouldn't even NEED to get my hands on him... I'd just punt his puny little nutsack so far up his throat that he'd be coughing up...' 'Whoa, whoa,' Vendetta chuckles softly, still not quite able to speak at full volume. 'I'll take care of that bankrupt CEO wannabe.' You save the energy for the bedroom, tiger. 'Nelson's just dripping with envy that with his financial collapse, I'm on the Forbes list and he's not... and he's too much of a pussy to handle things himself. Then again, if I had a woman do to me what Reagan did to him, I'd stay inside all day too just to make sure nobody could laugh at his vagina.' Niki rolls her eyes. 'Speaking of, you *did* get a call from Jane herself, actually.' Great. Just what I needed to make my day complete. 'Terrific; what does that dumb bitch want anyway?' 'Well, you know she's not particularly thrilled with your ranting a while back...' 'It was JUSTIFIED. It's not 'ranting' if it's perfectly within bounds, and it was... she was screwing me over, and not in the way that our 'esteemed' World Champion is showing that two-dollar whore his 'Five Inches of Fun' either.' '...*but* she did want me to let you know that her defense of OSW wrestlers against outside organizations is one of her top priorities and you can expect that she'll do everything she can to make sure the people who jumped you are caught and dealt with.' Well, I'll be damned... didn't expect *that* card to play out, but that works out great for me, now, doesn't it? I don't trust her as far as I can throw her... which is about to the next streetcorner... but this'll do just fine. 'Terrific; woulda been nice if we'd actually had a little SECURITY though; a bit more than that fat, bald, has-been they've got running the show now. Pathetic.' Alright, it's time to take care of business. Clay pauses for a second. 'You have my Palm Pilot, Niki?' 'I can go and get it.' 'Good. Bring it to me; then get my my laptop, too. I have a few e-mails to send and a few names to pull up. This situation is going to get resolved. MY WAY.' Hmmm... what am I forgetting? 'Oh, and Niki?' Turning around from the door, Niki looks back from Clay... 'Yes, babe?' 'Do me a favor; find that Canadian buffoon's room and put some Ex-Lax in his IV line.' Niki laughs. 'You're horrible, Clay. Grow up.' Winking at him, she leaves the room. Well, they can put me in the hospital week after week; but I've still got my money and I've still got the best piece of ass in OSW. So let me get me priorities straight; first I'm gonna send a message to Tyler Nelson and we'll see who's 'canceling' who... then I deal with that Canuck spooge the way I should've before the shoddy OSW catering department took me out of commission... and finally, I have a few words with the queen bitch around here and let that harpy know that despite her little lover's quarrel with Nelson, I've still know she's got it out for me; and I'll get my revenge, even if I've gotta take it out that paper champion boy-toy of hers. For now? I enjoy my paid injury leave, take it easy in the hospital bed... ... and plan my next move.
