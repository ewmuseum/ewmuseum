Date: June 24, 2002 ‘Where Is My Mind’ by The Pixies begins its “ouuu’ing” intro as Reno Davis and Amy Campbell appear on opposite sides of the screen and begin to inch closer. Ouuuuuuuu Both come together and embrace, and their lips draw closer, nearly touching... Stop. The film cuts, and rolls just as the two begin to kiss, showing Reno turning his head from Amy in the hospital. With your feet in the air and your head on the ground The screen shows Reno ollieing off the top of Mount St Hell at Ascension, flying through the air to save Aimz from Corg. Try this trick and spin it, yeah ...Reno heelflips while flying through the air. Your head will collapse Amy’s head drops to the cage from fatigue as Corg begins to caress her naked ass. But there's nothing in it Her eyes fall shut, as she is visibly unconscious. And you'll ask yourself Reno lands on top of Corg, slamming him down onto the floor, knocking both men out and leaving Aimz out cold handcuffed to the mountain. Where is my mind? The scene cuts to Amy and Reno in the hospital as a voice-over is heard over the music: Aimz: I love you... Where is my mind? ...And Reno turns his head. Where is my mind? BAM! Amy punches through the wall and starts screaming at Reno... Way out in the water Amy and Reno are seen at the beach a few weeks ago, laughing. See it swimmin' Reno watches on as Amy and ZaC swim off in the water, then it cuts back to Reno turning his head on Amy in the hospital. I was swimmin' in the Carribean Amy floating on her back in the ocean... Animals were hiding behind the rock ZaC is on the shore looking at Reno strangely as Davis merely looks out at Amy who is alone. Except the little fish Jelly Fish surround Amy, who swears and stands up, looking at Reno for help. But they told me, he swears ZaC looks at Amy, then at Reno, then at Amy again, and then again at Reno. Tryin' to talk to me to me to me Reno meets ZaC’s gaze and ZaC motions his little arms out towards Amy. Reno laughs and pulls a Vanilla Coke out of the cooler nearby. ZaC slaps his forehead in frustration. Where is my mind? Davis wades out to Aimz and clears a path through the jelly fish with a stick. Reno then turns and walks back to shore. Where is my mind? Amy jumps onto Reno’s back and he flips her over into the water. She gets up and the two stand face to face for a moment, eyes meeting. Where is my mind? Reno shoves her back into the water and takes a sip of his coke, still walking back to shore. Way out in the water Campbell stays behind, looking on at Reno with a confused look in her eyes. See it swimmin' ? Reno sits down underneath their umbrella on the sand and pulls out a Modern Drummer magazine. Amy stands for another moment before the jelly fish start coming back, then she started back for shore as well. With your feet in the air and your head on the ground Reno is fighting Ivan Stanislav, and he hits a Shooting Star DDT. Try this trick and spin it, yeah Reno is lifted up for a powerbomb by Ivan Stanislav during their match at Acsension. Reno’s head bounces off the side of the mountain as he’s lifted. Your head will collapse Ivan slams The Unwanted Angel down upon the concrete floor. If there's nothing in it Blood seeps out from Reno’s crown, and it pans in on his face as he lies there in pain. And you'll ask yourself Amy is backstage watching the match on a television. The screen zooms in and a tear streams down her face. Where is my mind? The screen shows Davis flying through the air on his skateboard again... Where is my mind? It cuts to Reno turning his head on Amy, who just asked him out. Where is my mind? Then it cuts back to Reno crippling himself to save Amy from Corg. Ooooh Amy and Reno approach each other again, much like the start of the video. With your feet in the air and your head on the ground Everything seems to blur out of context around them as they inch closer. Ooooh ZaC leaps in the way just as they are about to kiss. Try this trick and spin it, yeah Reno catches ZaC and turns away from Amy as he pets ZaC behind the ears. Ooooh Campbell looks on, forcing a smile. She then turns her back to Reno as well. The screen pans in to see her face, the smile only a mere sharade to hide the true hurt in her eyes. Ooooh Then the screen shows a shot of Reno’s face, visibly confused and in deep thought after the incident. The screen fades with both Reno and Amy with their backs to each other. Black text fades from the white background as the words appear on the screen: Is it over?
