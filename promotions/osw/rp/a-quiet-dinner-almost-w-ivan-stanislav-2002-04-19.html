A Quiet Dinner... almost (w/ Ivan Stanislav)

19 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/19/02 'Bamboo shoots!' Tempest stood in her small kitchen and shook her head as she surveyed the now empty refrigerator and said, 'I knew I'd forget something. Ah well, I bet Ivan would think it was tree bark anyway.' She chuckled and lit two candles carefully, walking into the dining room as Trouble whimpered at her feet, eyeing the steaming dishes on the table. 'No! None for you,' Tempest said with a playful scowl at the little dog. 'Remember what happened last time you tried to eat the Szechuan beef?' Trouble dropped to the ground and put a paw over his nose as Tempest nodded and said, 'That's right. So you just behave yourself and let me and Ivan handle the spicy food, ok?' Just then a thunderous knock shook the door and Tempest jumped up and ran to the door. Behind her Trouble waited and growled quietly. Ivan knocked again at the door, wearing a bright red old style double breasted Soviet Military uniform with bright gold buttons running up the center, as well as a red military hat with a black band and gold hammer and sickle on the front. Hid beard was trimmed more than usual, and his gray hairs appeared to lessen...as if he had them...colored? Tempest threw open the door and smiled happily. 'Little overdressed are we?' she asked and giggled, standing in her casual black cargoes, with bright pink stripes down the legs, and a little pink half tee. She smiled and leapt up into his arms, hugging him. Ivan caught her and blushed, his hat almost falling off the back of his head as he hugged her tight. 'Uh..well,' he muttered, 'it is customary that the gentlemen look his best for the lady.' He put her down and fixed his collar, 'Particularly in such an...irregular situation.' 'Irregular?' she said, tilting her head curiously. 'What's irregular about it? I mean besides me being a runt and all...' 'Oh no,' replied Ivan, 'there is nothing irregular about your size, if anything, perhaps Ivan is a little too tall.' He ducked into the room and took his hat off and tucked it under his right arm, 'But it not customary for woman to invite man to dinner. She should not have to do any of the work.' Ivan nodded, moreso to himself as he tried to remember what he had learned in school regarding 'chivalry.' Tempest laughed and said, 'Aw, I like to cook sometimes. I live on the road and eat at restaurants all the time, so it's a treat to me if I can find time to cook some. Besides, I like it even better if there's someone I wanna share it with.' She smiled up at him sweetly. Ivan chuckled and grabbed his stomach, 'I am warning you, it takes very much to feed me!' Tempest laughed and said, 'Well, I always make a lot, just usually to live on for awhile and...' At that moment Trouble burst from the kitchen yapping at Ivan madly. Ivan took a step backwards as the little dog ran and bit Ivan on the ankle, 'DYAA! Careful there dog!' Ivan growled threateningly as Trouble yapped and pulled his leg. For a moment a flash of rage shot through Ivan's face and he brought his other leg back and then stopped and reached down to dislodge the dog, 'Calm yourself, Dog!' 'Trouble!' Tempest snapped. The attack broke off immediately and the little border collie slunk to one side, looking up at her pitifully. 'Don't give me that look. You go back in the back and behave yourself.' She pointed down the hallway. Trouble stayed put and whimpered. She looked at him as sternly as she could manage and said, 'Go! Shoo!' At last he slunk off, with a little whimper and glance over his shoulder. Tempest shook her head and said, 'I don't know what gets into him sometimes. I'm sorry, you ok?' Ivan brushed off his pant leg, checking to see if it was torn. He noticed a small hole in his pant leg and looked up slowly. He breathed through his nose sternly, 'It is all right...I wonder what got into that animal...' Ivan ground his jaw, trying to remain calm. Tempest shrugged and turned to the candle lit dining room. 'I dunno,' she said. 'He's hardly ever like this. But I guess he's a bit jealous.' She winked and said, 'After all, he's been my protector all his life.' Ivan laughed and walked into the dining room, 'Yes well, I believe you may have gotten upgrade!' Ivan laughed and then calmed down when he saw the set up at the table, 'I must say Tempest, very nice indeed!!' Ivan eyed the wooden chair warily. She chuckled and said, 'If any of the furniture breaks, we can just sit on the floor, I got good cushions around here someplace. Just have a seat though! I'll be right back.' She turned and started into the kitchen. Ivan grunted and walked behind Tempest, 'Nyet Tempest, I must help! What can Ivan do?' He looked around and smiled. Tempest wheeled and pushed him back as she said with a touch of worry, 'Oh no! I can take care of it, really!' She glanced over her shoulder and thinking fast said, 'It's not polite to look in a single woman's kitchen.' Ivan raised an eyebrow and glanced over Tempest's head and chuckled, 'But...heh...what would there be to hide?!' He laughed playfully and tried to push past her. 'No no no!' She said, pushing back against his chest at about a 45 degree angle as her feet scuffed the carpet seeking traction. 'Really, I don't let people I like into my kitchen. You'll have to trust me on this.' Ivan paused and looked down at Tempest, 'Hmm...strange logic, but I will respect your wishes if that is what you want.' Ivan stopped his advance and smiled. Tempest grinned and motioned him back toward the table with a little shooing gesture and said, 'I'll be right back.' Ivan walked over to a nearby hutch and looked at various trinkets sitting on the shelves, and then saw the case holding The Communist Manifesto he had given her and smiled warmly. After a moment, a few crashes and what sounded like breaking glass emanated from the kitchen. Ivan leapt suddenly as the crash reverberated through the room and turned suddenly and listened to see if Tempest was all right. 'Tempest?? Are you okay?' Ivan began to walk towards the kitchen, a look of concern on his face. 'I'm fine!' she called happily. 'Be right out!' There was a sudden 'foosh'... almost like the sound of a fire extinguisher. Ivan blinked for a moment and turned and walked to a nearby chair where a pink boa sat in a pile. He lifted it and brought it up to his face, trying to figure out exactly what its use was. He shrugged and tossed it back on the chair, except it had wrapped itself around his arm! He fumbled with it and staggered across the room, growling as he tugged at the knotted boa. He hit the wall with a 'thud' causing the hutch to teeter forward! He rushed over and pushed it back against the wall as a vase atop the hutch fell forward! He reached up and caught it, stumbled backwards, hit the wall and placed the vase on the windowsill. He stood for a moment and breathed heavily, and then fooled with the boa again. Tempest emerged from the kitchen with an ornate covered enamel ware dish and chuckled at Ivan. 'Oh no, my attack wardrobe has gotten hold of you!' Ivan fumbled with the boa, trying not to just rip it straight from his arm which would be the much easier alternative. He blushed and finally got himself free, tossing it across the room. He brought his arms behind his back, grinned, and stood beside the table, 'Nothing that Ivan could not handle!' He bent at the waist, 'And what do we have here, dear?' 'We have Szechuan beef and veggies!' Tempest said smiled proudly as she whipped the top off the dish, just as a tongue of flame shot up from the middle. Her eyes widened and she blew it out. 'It's supposed to do that,' she said, as her eyes darted from the dish to Ivan and back, 'really.' Ivan grunted and brought his head up suddenly, the flame licking his beard, 'DYAA!!' He looked over at Tempest and then at the Szechuan. He rubbed his chin and walked over to Tempest and pulled the chair out for her. Tempest smiled sheepishly and sat down, placing the dish in the center of the table. She reached up and patted Ivan's arm as she said, 'Darn it, my dog, my wardrobe and now my cooking attacks you. I'm glad you're sticking around.' Ivan smiled, 'It is not the dog, the cooking, or the furniture that brought me here Tempest. Those things will not make me leave either.' He let her sit down in her chair and smiled warmly and sat in his chair. 'Well it smells quite...different, I must say.' Ivan swallowed, his massive adam's apple bobbed as he did, looking warily at the very exotic food in front of him. He raised his eyebrows and looked at Tempest, 'Perhaps the cook should have the first bite?' Tempest smiled and said, 'Of course! Better make sure it came out all right.' She lifted a pair of chopsticks and deftly plucked out a piece of meat, covered in a flame red sauce and bit into it. After a moment of chewing, she smiled and said, 'Mmm.... perfect. It's hard, but worth the effort to make. Oh...' She nodded toward his plate. 'I gave ya a fork. Didn't want to make you suffer through these things.' She giggled and tapped her chopsticks together. Ivan smiled, 'Good, I am glad....but I can use the chopsticks.' He grabbed the chopsticks and fooled with them clumsily, finally managing to get them between his fingers. He tried to grab the meat and snapped them in half. He blushed embarrassingly and shrugged. 'Uh...' he grabbed the fork and stabbed the meat and brought it to his nose, breathing in. After another moment he opened his mouth and took a healthy chunk out of the meat. Ivan chewed for a moment before his jaw began to shake and sweat formed on his brow, his chewing slowed as he held the meat in his mouth and looked over at Tempest, his face slowly turning red..... Tempest looked at him and said, 'Uh oh... you're not used to this stuff I bet.' Ivan chewed again slowly and swallowed before he burst into a roar, 'DYAAAAAAHAAAAAAAAHOOOOTTT!!!!' Ivan shot his huge arm out and grabbed his glass of water, drinking it in one gulp. He grabbed a pitcher of water and brought it to his mouth, swallowing each with loud, throaty gulps as the water drained from the pitcher. Tempest looked at him dismayed and said, 'Wow, I didn't think it was all that hot. I tried to tone it down a little for you.' Ivan gasped as he put the pitcher down, 'Uhh...you must understand....Russians...do not eat.. spicy food very much.' Ivan swallowed and looked down at the meat and slowly cut off a small piece, 'However, I did not wish to insult cook, it is very good!' Tempest grinned and got up, returning after a moment with another two pitchers of water. She set them down and said, 'I don't have a fire hydrant handy, but maybe that'll help. I'm glad you like it. But I do have something a little milder.' She shuffled a smaller dish toward him, with fried dumplings in it and smiled. 'Aha,' laughed Ivan, 'dumplings!!' Hungrily, the Russian Bear dished some onto his plate, as well as another piece of meat. He easily ate a dumpling in one bite, and while it may have appeared to be rude for someone to eat like this in front of a girl, in relationship to Ivan the enormous bites were quite refined in a strange way. He alternated by taking EXTREMELY small, even by normal standards, pieces of meat and eating them, followed by a hearty drink. Tempest smiled and daintily ate the hotter dish, without seeming to notice. 'Ivan what sort of food do they serve in Russia? I mean after China I came here and I learned to like pizza a lot, and red meat; that was a rarity for me. So what are you used to?' Ivan smiled and stopped eating for a moment, 'It is difficult in Russia to eat certain types of food. It is hard for cattle to live in the area, so most of our diet is comprised of vegetables.' Ivan paused, 'Potatoes and bread are a large source of food as well.' Ivan thought for a moment, 'Personally, I do not eat very exotic food. I suppose it is not in my nature.' Ivan tilted his head and thought, 'Besides, Ivan generally eats the rations that are given to him at the base. However, after eating this, I believe they may be a bit bland.' Tempest chuckled and said, 'I think I'm about to be very proud of myself if that's the case. Rations? Eww....' She wrinkled her nose. I think the most bland thing I eat is... well ice cream and that almost doesn't count.' 'Ice Cream?' Ivan thought for a moment, 'AH! That food you asked what flavor I would be during radio show.' Ivan nodded slowly, 'I do not believe I have ever had ice cream.' Ivan looked at the table and then back at Tempest, 'I must lead very dull life, eh Tempest?' She chuckled and said, 'Oh I dunno, being the hero of Russia doesn't sound dull to me. I know your career in wrestling at least has been pretty exciting.' Ivan chuckled, 'Yes, exciting is understatement, I suppose.' Ivan nodded and then looked to Tempest, 'Yes, but let us not talk about wrestling, eh?' Ivan smiled, 'Here we are, two people brought together at a table with beautifully cooked food, excellent smelling dishes, and no one to bother us, nyet?' Ivan smiled warmly, reached out, and squeezed Tempest's hand. She nodded and said, 'Yup, for a change. Oh, did you see your book?' She nodded toward it up in the hutch. 'I figured that was probably the safest place for it. Trouble can't reach it and it's not in a place where I'd hurt it, just bouncing around all the time.' Ivan nodded in the direction of the book, 'Yes, I had seen it. Have you read any of it yet?' She nodded and said, 'A little. I admit it sorta loses me in places. But the main thing is that I'd have to sit still to read it.' She giggled and fidgeted in her seat. 'No mean feat for me!' 'DYAAHAA,' Ivan laughed warmly, 'yes, I suppose I had never thought of that.' Tempest smiled and then suddenly seemed to remember something. 'Oh! I wanted to show you something. Hang on a second.' She stood and hurried off, leaving a curious Ivan looking after her. Ivan swallowed as she skirted away and looked at the table. He picked up a piece of the meat and smelled it and popped it in his mouth, instantly grabbing for more water and downing it. He swallowed and pushed his plate away and wiped his mouth and waited patiently. Tempest walked back in and sat down, with a small doll in her hands. It was dirty, but the shimmer of it's clown costume and the vibrancy of the colors in its painted face still showed. She looked at it nervously and said, 'Your book made me think about this. That and what happened after Slam...' Ivan slid his chair closer to Tempest and looked down at the worn doll. He swallowed and whispered softly, not liking the sudden mood change in Tempest, 'What is it, Tempest?' She smiled and said, 'Well, it occurred to me that I should tell you about this. See, I'm not a materialistic person. I don't care if a chair breaks under you or a hole gets knocked in wall.' She shrugged and continued, 'That's all just stuff. But other than your book, this is the only material thing I have that is important to me. It's... all I have left of my father.' Ivan swallowed, very moved by what she had just said. He brought his hand up to her back and rubbed it, 'I see.' He thought for a moment and looked at her, 'It is funny how little things can mean so much to people, Tempest. It is good thing you have it to remember him.' He thought, not sure if he should build upon the subject, 'Do you...remember him well?' She nodded, smiling a little. 'He got me this when we went to a circus. They had these great clowns that I just adored. Ya know, they were acrobats too and they flipped and flew and did funny things too.' She chuckled and said, 'I wanted to be one when I grew up.' She looked up at him and added, 'I guess that explains a lot huh?' Ivan breathed out heavily and smiled, 'Yes, it does. And I think that if your father was here, Tempest,' Ivan paused and brought his head closer, 'he would be very proud of you.' Ivan thought for a moment, 'I do not know if there is afterlife, I do not generally ponder it, but if there is, I know that he is very happy for you Tempest for living your dream.' She nodded and said, 'I think so too. I miss him a lot though. But that's not really what I wanted to go into.' She shook her head a little and smiled as she said, 'I just thought it was only fair to tell you that this is the one material object I'll be mad if you break. I don't care about if my couch breaks in the middle or anything like that, but my doll is important.' She smiled and placed the doll up on the shelf, leaning against the case in which the aged book he'd given her rested. Ivan rose and walked over to her, 'Do not worry, Tempest, I would never do anything to hurt you.' He pointed to the doll, 'Particularly things with historical significance, and moreso with personal significance.' Ivan fidgeted, not sure how to handle a more sentimental Tempest. She smiled up at him and hugged him tightly as she said, 'I know. I knew you'd understand. Even when we seem to be opposites in so many ways. I think we have the most important things in common.' Ivan smiled and hugged her back, 'Yes, I believe you are correct.' He opened his mouth wide as he held her and stifled a yawn. However, his huge chest puffed out as he breathed through his nose, therefore pushing Tempest as well while he held her. She looked up and said, 'Tired? Hey, when did you get back from Russia?' Ivan blinked and looked down at her, 'I got here about an hour ago...before that I was in Russia for a day...and then had to go to Vladivostok which is across Siberia...' Ivan thought for a moment, 'I have been through about....five time zones, I believe. That is all.' He smiled and tightened his jaw, yawning again but trying not to show it. He breathes out loudly. 'Aww,' Tempest said with a smile and patted his hand. 'C'mon, let's sit down and relax then. Sounds like you've been running around as fast as me almost!' Ivan smiled, 'Alright, let us sit down.' He followed Tempest to a nearby couch, sat beside her, and wrapped his arm around her shoulder, 'Ah...feels good to relax for once.' Tempest scooted close and nuzzled his cheek as she said, 'I'm just glad you can with me.' She chuckled a little and said, 'Ya know Ivan, you seem to have magically gotten all the gray trimmed out of your beard. How'd ya manage that?' She grinned at him devilishly. Ivan blushed heavily, his face almost turning as red as his jacket, 'Uh..well..uh...I just...er...' He shifted his weight sheepishly, 'I suppose it just...must have all.....fallen out! Something must be making me younger!' He smiled at her, his face still very red. She nodded, but her expression was easily read: she wasn't buying a word of it. 'Mmmm hmmm... I wonder what it could be,' she said and nuzzled his chin. 'It's ok. Just remember that you don't have to be anyone for me but yourself, ok?' Ivan leaned back, actually slouching a bit and rubbed her shoulder and looked at the ceiling and closed his eyes, 'I know Tempest....but remember, Ivan is not getting much younger, he may be old man before long....' He sighed softly. Tempest giggled and said, 'And someday I'll be a little old woman, so who cares, it's all...' She stopped and looked at Ivan's face, suddenly less pensive than she had seen before. 'Ivan?' she said. Ivan's eyebrows moved just slightly, 'Mmm?' He turned his head to the side and sighed heavily while a soft grumble ran through his chest and his breathing became more regular. His arm became slightly more limp around Tempest's shoulder as he continued to snore softly, at peace for once. Tempest giggled quietly, covering her mouth to stifle the sound and simply looked at him. It's weird to see Ivan relaxed. I keep him expecting him to jump up and tell me it was a joke. No wait, he hasn't grasped that concept yet, that must be me. Still, the Russian bear hibernating seems far less frightening. Did I say frightening? Yeah, he still scares me sometimes. Not like I think he's going to hurt me, more like I think he's going to hurt himself. And maybe leave me... I'm not sure who that would hurt worse at this point. It's so alien to him to believe that someone wants him around who doesn't have a use for him, a mission, a goal. And when that mission means that everyone around him hates him, except for his few comrades? I can't fathom it. I can't stand being without friends and I don't know what I'd do without the crowd behind me. But Ivan's never had that. And now... even his own people are stating to question him. And it's my fault. I want to be good for him, but how can I if people around him see me as a threat? And no one understands why I care about him so much. I didn't really intend to. God, he was the worst date of my life easily, but... He was also one of the few people I felt was real. And somehow, when I'm with him, things are more calm. I don't entirely understand it, but it's like he's the only person who I can sit still for, just like this, and be happy. And I stand by what I said way back before I went out with him... There's just something very charming about a guy who hasn't forgotten his dream, even if the rest of the world has. I don't know if the OSW will ever totally accept him as their hero. I don't know if he can remain the hero of Russia with people making all that trouble for him. But I know that he's my hero. And I just hope it's enough. Tempest smiled happily an leaned forward, kissing Ivan gently on the lips. He stirred with a sigh that seemed deeply content as she rested he head on his shoulder and drifted into am unusual, calm slumber.
