He just wanted to hack...

27 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 27, 2002 (pre-Slam) GM Center in Flint, MI “Dammit...Dammit all to hell.” Reno Davis swore as he looked pulled his foot up to his pelvis so he could inspect his shoe. His black chucks had managed to last him a good year and although many have said that they only really lasted a few months, Reno believed otherwise. The sole of the chuck sneaker was a few feet away from where he stood, irritated beyond belief. ZaC, his monkey, skittered off and retrieved the bottom of the shoe. Reno sighed and wiggled his toes a little bit as he pondered what to do. Reno put his foot back to the floor and took a few steps, trying to see if he could do without the sole. He picked up his hackey-sack, in which he had been playing with before his chuck broke and gave it a few uncomfortable hacks. “Dammit, do I have any fishing line in my bag ZaC?” Reno asked his little buddy, who searched Reno’s red bag and came out empty-handed. Reno’s shoulders slumped as he bounced his ankle back and forth, watching the mangled cloth remnants of the chuck wobbled around. “Aww....” was all Reno could manage to say. “Hey there cutie!” A familiar female voice sounded from behind Reno. He looked and saw Tempest, who wasn’t talking to him, but instead to ZaC who was enjoying a good scratch behind the ears. Reno melted as he watched Tempest just stroking his monkey. He started to speak, but choked and made some weird noise like the little Ethiopian children on tv do. Tempest turned and giggled. “Hey you. What’s happening?” She let ZaC hop up onto her shoulder and then walked towards Reno, who smiled. He felt comfortable somehow now. “Not bad, I’m not fighting tonight for some reason. I think they got upset when I lost to Tom Walc...Walooka...Walmcock?” Tempest giggled, “Close enough. Uh, hey what’s with the shoe?” She asked. Reno lifted his foot up into to air and showed Tempest the remains of his chuck. “I had an accident.” “I guess. Here lemme see it and I’ll fix it up for ya.” “Oh. Yeah, alright.” Reno pulled on a lace, causing the whole shoe to fall apart, “...Fuck.” Tempest laughed at Reno until her face was bright red and tears formed in her eyes. She recovered and saw that Reno was visibly upset at the loss of his chuck. “Aw I’m sorry. It’s just that...you pulled your lace...and....and....” Tempest broke back into laughter at Reno’s expense. Again, she recovered and wiped a tear from her face. “You done now?” Reno asked with a smirk. “Yup...Okay...phew. All right, I think I can still help. Wait here!” Tempest ran off and left Reno shoeless in the middle of the backstage area of the GM Center. The Unwanted Angel picked up his hackey-sack and gave it a few hacks before losing control and kicking it in the back of some nerdy looking guy with glasses’ head. Surprised, the nerdy man turned and looked at Reno, who forced a smile. “Uh, sorry man.” “Uh Brother you're not wearing any shoes.” The man said. He was known as Propaganda. Reno didn’t recognize him as anything though, and held both hands to both cheeks and sarcastically said: “Geez, thanks a lot captain obvious!” Reno said, shaking his head. “Don't be so insolent, for your misguided ignorant mind can not understand the complexity of Chritianity.” Propaganda replied “What? What the hell are you talking about?” Reno was seriously confused, and as he scratched his head, Propaganda managed to read the caption on his t-shirt... Jesus Hates You. “WHAT IS THIS? HOW DARE YOU USE THE MESSIAH'S NAME IN FILTH! THIS IS AN OUTRAGE! I SERIOUSLY SUGGEST YOU TAKE OFF THAT SHIRT AND BURN IT BEFORE YOU BURN IN THE REDDEST PIT OF HELL BEIGN STEWED BY LUCIFER HIMSELF!” Propaganda roars in disgust. “Fuck you churchie!” Reno calls back, flipping the nerdy Christian off. “FATHER FORGIVE HIM FOR HE KNOWS NOT WHAT HE DOES! - JESUS CHRIST HIMSELF! It's quote symbolizes you share the ignorance of Jesus' foes.” States the power himself. “Bud, you have some serious problems that need to be consulted. So just go read your little Holy Novel and stop shoving your damn beliefs into everyone’s heads. Or else there’ll be some repercussions.” Propaganda stood up quickly from his chair and stared down the little 5’6, 166 pound Canadian just a few feet away. Reno, noticing his error in choosing enemies, begins to talk fast. “Uh...Yeah that’s right! Repercussions from my really big friends! I mean really big! And tough! They’ll take you out man!” Reno spat out quickly, then looked down at his hackey-sack sitting helplessly beside Propaganda’s boot, “Can I have my hackey-sack back?” “You shall be straightened out by the wrath of the lord.” Propaganda concluded. “Hey, I said stop with the damn God stuff! Nobody cares if God’s going to hurt me when I’m dead!” Propaganda took a step forward, causing Reno to step back and grab ZaC, “I have a monkey and I know how to use it!” Propaganda continued to walk forward, angry and upset as can be. Reno threw ZaC at Propaganda, who caught the monkey in mid-air and began to squeeze the little primate. “Bite him ZaC! Bite him!” Reno yelled to his friend, who obliged and chomped down on Propaganda’s holy little finger. Propaganda yelped and let the monkey go, who ran off with Reno Davis. Propaganda muttered to himself and watched the two little misfits make their getaway... “Reno! What’s the hurry?” Tempest hollered with a bright smile on her face as she watched Reno run right past her. He stopped abruptly and ZaC slammed into Reno’s calf, causing the little monkey to fall onto his back in surprise. “OhmygodTempesttherewasthisfrigginchurchguyandhestolemyhackey-sackandImeanHE WAS A REALLY BIG GUY andhewantedtohurtmeandZaCand...” Reno shot out in what seemed like a mili-second. “Slow down!” Tempest said as she laughed, “Tell me later, I found you a new shoe...sort of.” “What do you mean sort of?” “Well...I just altered one of my boots. See?” She held out a bright blue latex knee-high boot with a little stick thing underneath the heel. Reno shook his head and looked at Tempest. “I don’t think I can wear that.” “Bah! We’ll just break this here off...” Tempest broke off the stick-thing and then gave the boot to Reno. The boot fit, but the colors were very different. “Well...this sucks.” Reno said, still eying the boot on his foot. “Hmm...” Tempest looked through her purse for a moment and then pulled out some black nail polish, “Aha!” “I don’t need nail polish. I need a boot.” “Shush.” Tempest said and she bent down in front of Reno, whose eyes got big, then normal again as what he thought was coming, stopped coming. Tempest began to paint the boots black, to match Reno’s battered chuck, “And we’ll just cover the rest with your pant leg like...so and voila!” Tempest stood up and smiled, proud of her achievement. “Geez, thanks.” “No problem.” An awkward silence ensued. “Well, I uh. Have to go now.” Reno said, “Thanks for helping me with the shoe and all.” He waved. “Yeah! Bye Bye!” Tempest waved as well and turned to walk the other way, each wondering what the other was thinking.
