Lost in Reality

9 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 9, 2002 “I hate my life…” a voice mutters through his chilling throat. “Everything always goes wrong…” says the voice again, in the shadows. There is a faint outline of three people, lying against a cold brick wall. One is a tall male, his hair pent up, the only physical feature noticeable in the darkness. The other is a medium female, with no visible features. They lay in the darkness of an alley, the alley of two commercial competitors, the Holiday Inn and Days Inn hotels. The night is brisk, and the sky is clear. The moon is full, and the stars barely glimmer in the open sky. The female speaks up. “Hating your life will make it suck even more.” The head of the male nods in agreement. “I can’t help it… I hate it…” “That’s why we have each other.” The love is clearly expressed from the female. “That’s what love is for.” The male feels the love in his empty heart, and knows that he must release it. “Shome…” the male says, pulling his face from the shadows, revealing to be the Quiet Killer, Corg. He hesitates to finish his sentence. “I… I love you.” Touched, wonderfully, Shome places her hand on Corg’s thigh. “I know.” She reaches forward, smiling, and places her black lips onto his. It is not a simple kiss, it is a bonding, a bonding of love… of eternal love. Both Corg and Shome are falling into the paths of reality, but with love. With every second of the bonding, they see each other in the other’s eyes. With every second, their hearts are contracted. Love. Shome pulls back from Corg, ending the moment. She leans back against the stone wall, and brushes her long, blue hair back. Corg leans back as well, clutching his hands together to protect from the cold. He still keeps his eyes focused on his tired lover, not daring to swerve them away. “I still love you…” Corg says. Shome turns her head back toward’s her boyfriend, she looks into his white eyes and smiles. “I love you.” The moment’s love could not be painted or portrayed, barely even shown. The love is withstanding everything, and cannot be described through human words. Corg’s reaches his hand to his right, and pulls something out from under the darkness. He tugs, and reveals a hand. The hand of the third person. The hand is pale, and not moving. The darkness fades, and the body is fully visible. It lays there, motionless, with blood running from his neck. A metallic spear of some sort has been injected into the man’s neck, obviously causing him to bleed a lot and die. Sickly, both lovers smile at the dead man’s corpse, as Shome places her hand carefully on Corg’s pale face, and smears it across. Blood was previously soaked onto her hand, which has now been imprinted onto Corg’s face. A big red slash on Corg’s left cheek. To some, a sign of insanity. To Corg, it is a sign of eternal fucking love. > fin
