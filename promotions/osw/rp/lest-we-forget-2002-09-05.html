Lest We Forget

5 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/5/02 Tempest groaned as she rolled over on her bright red couch. A bag of frozen peas that had been resting between her shoulder blades rolled off and hit the floor with a sloppy thump. As Tempest peered back over her shoulder with a small grimace, Trouble toddled in and started sniffing at the bag. She summoned up a chuckle and said, “Silly, you can’t eat those, they’re frozen. But then… knowing you that’s just a small inconvenience.” She sat up slowly, gingerly, and petted the dog’s head as he waggled his whole body happily. Momma was up again and that’s all that matters in a dog’s world. Tempest stretched as much as she could comfortably, the light catching her shimmering blue tank top and the dull blue of the bruises on her face. She picked up the dog and sighed, gazing out at the drizzly morning. “I’m glad I’ve still got you Trouble. Poor Reno…” “Poor Reno?!” said Miyuki as she walked in with a cup of hot tea. “After what that little…” She stopped as Tempest threw her a sharp glance and said, “You saw what happened Miyu. Zac got killed the other night. Of course I feel bad for him. Doesn’t matter what he did.” Miyuki shook her head and said, “You’re not at all angry about the fact that he screwed up things for you and Ivan?” Tempest shook her head and hugged Trouble tightly. “No, I screwed things up for me and Ivan. Reno just provided the ammo. I can’t hate him for that. And with the loss of Zac…” Tempest trailed off and nuzzled her own animal pal, with sad look in her deep blue eyes. “Most people don’t understand. They think your animal is just a pet, like a fashion accessory or something. But I know better. I’d be heartbroken if I lost Trouble.” Miyuki nodded and said, “I was wondering. I saw the card addressed to him and figured that’s what it must be.” She sat down across from Tempest and slid the tea over to her. “I guess you’re right. I was just thinking of how much trouble he’s caused you.” Tempest smiled a little and took a sip of tea, while Trouble loafed on her lap. “You’re looking out for me as always. I can’t fault you for that. I just wish I didn’t need it so much.” Miyuki nodded and said, “Yeah I know. And you do the same thing. You’re so busy looking out for other people that sometimes you forget to look out for yourself. I swear I could strangle both Ivan and Mexi.” Tempest tilted her head, with a small grimace as her smooth neck reached a sharper angle and said, “Why’s that?” “Well, just look at them!” Miyuki said, her eyes suddenly wider with agitation. “They go out and almost kill each other again and then Ivan blames you after Mexi demanded you keep his identity a secret. So here you are, trying to help, worried about whether they’ll still be friends, while Ivan isn’t speaking to you.” Tempest shook her head and said, “But they both came out to help me when it counted this week Miyu. Mexi’s a good friend and I didn’t think it was unreasonable at all that he asked me to keep his identity a secret. I owe him for even coming back after all. And Ivan… well, I’m never sure with Ivan anymore.” Her shoulders slumped slightly and she sighed before adding, “Ivan’s so paranoid. I guess I can’t blame him after all that’s happened, but… I just wish he’d see that I’m really on his side. And I wish… I wish I could still have fun out there. I try, like I did at the pay per view with that entrance…” “That was a classic,” Miyuki said with a grin. “Vintage Tempest mischief. But you’re right; I wish you could do more of that and less of trying to keep people who should be friends from killing each other. Not to mention all the stuff with the Think Tank.” “I know,” Tempest said. “That’s what I wish. Ya know what?” Miyuki looked at her inquisitively. “Right about now is when I miss Ed,” Tempest said sadly. Miyuki nodded silently and for a moment all was still but the pattering of the rain. ~ “All right you!” Tempest squawked as she stormed into Ed’s office, complete with the barbwire edged around the desk. He looked up with a typical shit eating grin and an attempt to look innocent. “What? Who me?” Tempest stomped forward, but obviously wasn’t truly mad. The corners of her mouth kept threatening to turn up into a smile. “You sir, should be ashamed of yourself! I did the best I could to assure that I’d have a straight match with Meanstreak and you have to go and butt in with Bob!” She put her hands on her hips and gazed at him with the best imitation of annoyance she could manage. Ed laughed and said, “And a well timed butt in it was, Miss Eyes-Rolling-Up-In-Her-Head. I admit you look cute when you thrash around like that, but still… I thought it was time to interject.” “Well…” said Tempest losing what little edge there was to her voice. “I got Ivan to promise to stay out of it, but then you step in!” “Ah, that’s because you secured no such promise from me!” Ed said with a chuckle and a wink. Tempest growled, an amusing little noise coming from her. Then she smiled and said, “Yeah it was pretty cool wasn’t it?” “Yeah! And the fans loved it!” said Ed with unabashed enthusiasm. “And those shots with the electricity? Pure genius my little hardcore hottie!” Tempest laughed and said, “I know, it was fun!” She giggled and said, “Ok, you’re off the hook this time. But next time I’ll… I’ll do something dastardly to you!” She shook her tiny fist in a manner that almost entirely failed to intimidate. “Do tell!” Ed said and they both broke up laughing ...as the memory faded. ~ Ed and I understood each other; that’s why I liked him. He wasn’t all about the power, or trying to dominate or being some deranged sicko trying to cause as much carnage as possible. Ed was all about the fans and the fun and the interaction of the two. He knew that the only way we live forever is in the memory of those that loved us. The other night, for the second time, Bob came to my rescue. This time it was without the man who wielded it so well. But it reminded me why I’m doing this, why I can’t let Alan Kriegman rest. He has Bob and he has Trash Can Man and he’s spitting on Ed’s memory. Why do I care, even though I only knew him a short time? Because most people just don’t get it. Most people look past what’s important in the business and if they forget about Ed, or think of him as a loser because of what the Think Tank did to him, then I become a loser too. Come to think of it, we all do.
