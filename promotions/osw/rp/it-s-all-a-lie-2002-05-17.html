It's All a Lie

17 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05-17-2002 Life is a lie. Love is a lie. All the world is just one big lie. Everywhere you look, people are lying to each other. Husbands and wives lie to each other about still being attracted to them. Parents lie to children about everything from Santa to their own childhood exploits, and children lie to parents about not drinking or having sex. Professionals lie. In fact, several professions were created just for liars. Politicians lie, probably can't even tell the truth. Lawyers - well, that goes without saying. They lie for money, can't get money if you lose a trial, and you can't win if you admit your client beats his wife, or sodomizes little children. People applying for a new job lie, about their age, or their experience, or even why they lost their last job. Employers lie, they make up false excuses to fire someone they no longer want, but have no concrete reason to be fired. Lies are everywhere People writing up their taxes lie. People trying to get married lie. People who get into car accidents lie. Little kids who scribble crayon all over the walls lie - and that's really sad, because once a child lies, it's no longer innocent. People who lie, lie about lying. Flip through the channels after dinner, everything you see it a lie. Israel and Palestine - each lying to cover up what their doing. The US Military, lying to hide what they don't show about their actions. Communist China, lying to their own people to keep the power in the select few who wield it. The Catholic church, lying to protect the failing of their priests. But then, the church has always lied, hasn't it. Moses lied to his people about getting the 10 Commandmants - burning bush, indeed! Jesus lied, whether or not you believe he's the Son of God. If you believe, he lied about who he was by not admitting who he was. If you don't believe, he lied by letting an entire religion develop around his existance. Revelations started as a nightmare someone had after eating too much before bed, and it grew into a lie about the end of the world. Even today, the teachings of the church are all lies. The church teaches to forgive, to be peaceful. We know that a lie. The church doesn't forgive. It exploits. It hurts. It holds grudges. It seeks to destroy everything, and establish itself as the ultimate power. It breaks its teachings at the drop of a hat. God is forgiveness. Right. That's just a ploy to get money from the gullible. Give to the church, all ye who hear my words! Give to the church and make yourselves slaves to the Bible! The biggest example of propaganda that has ever existed. Ironic, isn't it. Ironic that a man who calls himself a man of God, names himself Propaganda. Cause that's all he does. Quotes propaganda Brainwashes the people. They don't forgive, even when apologized to. Forgiveness doesn't bring power. Brainwashing brings power. Lying brings power. False hopes and dreams bring power. Destruction brings power. But they have an achilles heel. Power corrupts. And their corruption will be their downfall. Because they aren't the only ones who can bring destrction. They aren't the only one who can give with one hand, and take with the other. They hold power with lies. The only way to fight them, is with the truth. The truth can hurt. It can even kill.
