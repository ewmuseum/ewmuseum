Beauty

5 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-5-02 Ivan Stanislav sat at his desk in Arkhangelsk, swamped with paperwork. From the damage he caused in the “Accident” at Karpov’s office to the explanations regarding his actions against ZaC, Ivan Stanislav was more than a busy man. Not only that, but Ivan Stanislav was also a celebrity. Every morning, bright and early, Secretary Arnold Latvinov was supposed to leave a detailed report on his desk regarding any appearances Stanislav might have. Now, Ivan couldn’t find it. Stanislav rummaged through what literally was a mountain of paperwork in an attempt to find the days assignments. He growled to himself and hit his intercom button. BREEP! “Latvinov! Where is my daily report!!” barked Ivan, none too pleased. It took only a moment for the reply, “I placed it directly on top of the police report, Praporschik.” BREEP! Ivan grumbled in Russian and leafed through the reports and papers, growing increasingly angry by the moment. Finally, Stanislav found the report, wedged between a folder. He pulled out the small stapled packet and stood up, his back to the door, facing out towards the window. “Hmm…” he rumbled as he read, “Da….da…..meeting with Karpov..heh….meeting with Ruslo…..meeting with…Federova??” Stanislav paused as he read the last small report. “Oxana Federova??” Ivan squinted and looked out at the Russian field ahead of him, “Federova..that name is familiar…” “It should be, Ivanushka. She has been all over the television.” A heavy, soft voice pierced his ears as he spun, he knew the voice. “Illyana? What are you doing here?” questioned Ivan. Illyana Bukharin, the beautiful Russian bombshell, stood in her radiance, clad in a skin-tight jumpsuit, ravishing as ever. Ivan then looked down at the paper as Illyana smiled more. “This..this is from your office?!” Illyana nodded and walked deeper into the room, “That is right Ivan. Oxana Federova, if you did not know, won the Ms. Universe contest recently…” Ivan nodded to himself, the light dawning on him. The young Oxana Federova was appearing all over Russian Television as the “Most Beautiful Woman” in the world. He blinked, returning to the present, “What does she have to do with me?!” “Consider it, Ivan,” began Illyana, “a public relations move. After all, you are famous figure in Russia. She is famous figure in Russia. You are of the older generation, she is of the younger. You…are a communist….she is a capitalist. I thought perhaps it would be fitting that you meet her and talk with her. The people love you both, it would be nice gesture to the people, if anything.” She paused, “Besides, Ivan. Perhaps it will be good for you…to talk to…another…woman.” Illyana rubbed the back of her neck. Ivan paused for a long moment and then growled, “I do not have time for this, Illyana! I have work to do!” Illyana chuckled, “Ivanushka, you are right, you do not have time….you meet with her in Moscow in two hours!” Ivan’s jaw dropped, “Two hours?!” With a sudden burst of speed, Ivan Stanislav rounded the desk and lumbered past Illyana, who smiled devilishly. He turned and looked at her, “You will pay for this, you vixen!” “Ohh! Is that a threat, Ivanuska?” she giggled as Ivan turned and left his office. ~~ Ivan Stanislav did make it to his predetermined destination to meet with the beautiful Oxana Federova. He actually made it with some time to spare. Not only that, but a quick trim and plucking of his beard enabled him to look at least somewhat presentable. Stanislav stood on the terrace of the Kremlin, looking down at Red Square and the thousands of people that bustled around the area. The media was forbidden from this area of the Kremlin, as per Stanislav’s request. He hated the media, who constantly poked and prodded into his life and the life of those around him. The sound of high heels on the cobblestones of the Kremlin began to slowly be heard from behind the Russian Bear. He swallowed and turned around too see Oxana Federova slowly walking towards him. She truly was beautiful, long black hair, green eyes, and taller than a typical woman. She definitely warranted the victory that she had won at the Ms. Universe contest. She wore a sleek black dress and had a shawl around her shoulders. It was not extremely cold in Moscow, but chilly enough to require a small bit of protection. Ivan swallowed and smiled as she approached. He brought his arm behind his back and bowed slightly, still much taller than her. She blushed. “Greetings, Oxana Federova. I am Praporschik Ivan Sergeiovich Stanislav. It is a pleasure to meet you.” Oxana smiled up at Stanislav, very flattered if not somewhat intimidated by the massive Russian soldier. Stanislav took her hand and kissed it, as per his upbringing. “Thank you Praporschik Stanislav. I must say I am very honored to be talking with you.” Ivan blinked, fairly confused. “I have heard stories about you all my life, Praporschik,” continued Oxana, “you’ve been a true inspiration to people.” “Come now,” answered Ivan, “I have not done anything any more important that what you have done, or anyone else!” He smiled and let go of her hand. “On the contrary, Praporschik! I am just a girl that smiles for a camera. But you…you work for something, I suppose.” She tilted her head as he led her over to a railing and stood beside her, gazing out at Moscow. “Please my dear, call me Ivan.” He looked over at her and tried to smile. She moved slightly closer to Ivan, who in response, somewhat clumsily, jerked away from her. She noticed this and looked up at Ivan, who’s eyes darted around some unknown object on the railing. The two shared an uncomfortable moment of silence, and then Ivan broke in again. “So….uh…” stammered Ivan, “Ms. Universe. That must be great honor. You deserve all recognition that you are given, my dear.” “Yeah,” she began, “it’s a lot of fun. Hopefully I can help make the world a better place. Perhaps people do it in different ways.” She looked up at Ivan, “So, Prap…er…Ivan…what do you do for fun?” Ivan swallowed, “Oxana…I..I do not have much…fun, so to speak.” “Surely you do!” She giggled and playfully punched Ivan in the arm. Ivan jerked away as she came into contact with him again. Federova furrowed her brow, “I know that you wrestle. I have read how you love to do that because it is fun. I had once read a magazine article about you and…what was her name….Tempest, I believe.” Ivan stared at her when she mentioned Tempests name. It was a wide eyed, almost…terrified stare. “You seemed very happy in the picture with her, Ivan. Where is she? I thought maybe she would be here with you.” She smiled, unwittingly driving an invisible spike into Stanislav’s heart. He turned from her and swallowed, “Oxana…Tempest and Ivan…something happened…we do not see one another anymore..if ever again.” She lowered her gaze, realizing the damage she had caused, “Oh Praporschik, I am sorry. That was out of line of me. I should not have brought up personal matters…” “It…it is all right.” Oxana swallowed. She knew Ivan was hurting, and she felt somewhat responsible. “You know Ivan…to be “Ms. Universe” you can’t be engaged with another man. I understand how loneliness feels.” She bit her lip as Ivan turned just slightly and looked over his shoulder at her. “You know…sometimes..things happen because we have no control. Things happen because….because of outside factors. Some things really are not our fault, know what I mean?” Ivan faced her more and nodded, much more docile now. “Da, I understand. But this is very confusing situation. One that I do not wish to face now, or ever again I do not think. I hurt her, Oxana. I hurt her in way that cannot easily be healed.” Ivan looked down at the floor, but Oxana kept her bright green eyes fixed on him, “Do you miss her?” Stanislav’s throat was paralyzed. He merely nodded in response. “Why do you not find her, Praporschik? You are honorable and proud man…I am sure that she knows that.” Ivan winced, “If only it was that easy. She has disappeared, my dear, I cannot find her for she does not wish to be found.” Oxana Federova paused and moved closer to Ivan. He winced and backed up, but he was pinned between a corner of the railing. She moved closer. “She may just be afraid, Ivan. I am sure she will come back to see you. I am sure she will wish to have the happiness that you gave her return.” She brought her hand up to touch Ivan’s face, he winced. “Why do you shudder, Ivan?” “I am not a nice man, Oxana. I am paid to kill people.” Ivan winced again, enveloped by Oxana. She smiled and ran her hand on his cheek. “Praporschik Stanislav. You took time out of your busy day to see a beauty pageant winner. Obviously, you do not kill people all the time. There must be some time when you make other people happy.” She got up on her toes and craned her neck up high and just barely and softly kissed Ivan on the bottom of his chin. “She’s out there, Praporschik, and she is keeping an eye on you. She will come back. You are a gentlemen and from what I have heard, a beautiful person.” She smiled and nodded at Ivan. “I must go, Praporschik Stanislav. I thank you for the time you gave me to see you. I win awards for being beautiful…” she paused and looked up into his eyes, “..some people should win awards for being beautiful on the inside.” She smiled and walked away, leaving Ivan Stanislav to digest what she said. Just as she reached the door to the interior of the Kremlin, Ivan yelled to her. “Ms. Federova!” She turned, her hair blowing in the wind. “Thank you for your time.” He smiled as did she, and then she turned and went inside the Kremlin. Ivan turned and looked out into the sky. Ivan whispered to himself, “Are you looking at the same sky, my dear?” He paused and whispered again, “I hope so…because it is truly beautiful to behold.” Stanislav sighed as a few stars began to twinkle in the sky as the daylight gave way to the night.
