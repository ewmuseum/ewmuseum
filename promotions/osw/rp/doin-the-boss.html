RECOMMENDED MUSIC – “Between Heaven And Hell” by Zakk Wylde. Just listen to it anyway. We open to a closed hotel room door. No ordinary hotel room door, mind you. This is a CLASSY hotel, with the swank door and everything. Neat little decorative things, like… molding and trim and shit. But you didn’t come here to talk about the door, did you? No, you want the sordid sexual gratification that a Toomes RP is prone to give. Yeah, don’t even lie to me. You love it. You like seeing Toomes in his maladjusted state of mind, be it putting his crotch on people or watching him jerk it to his valet. Well, prepare to be satisfied. We heard the sounds of people having sex, the moaning of a woman, the grunts and gasps of a man just about to reach climax. The springs of the bed creak and the headboard bangs against the wall inside. This goes on for several minutes. Then, through the magic of television, we are transported inside after several more minutes. We are treated to the sight of Jane Reagan lying contentedly on the king-sized bed, her face flush with pleasure. Her skin looks moist with sweat, her hair mussed and out of place, her body covered by the silk sheet, which is damp with perspiration. By her side is the OSW World Champion, Sebastian Toomes. He, like her, is sweaty and quite pleased with this turn of events… Jane turns to face him, rubbing his chest with her cheek. “Sebastian… I’m thirsty. Be a good boy and get me a Diet Coke.” Toomes wipes the sweat off his brow. “I would love to…but isn’t that Room Service’s job?” Jane smiles wickedly. “If you want to get serviced in this room, you’ll get me a Diet Coke. Are we clear?” Toomes gulps. “Yes ma’am!” ++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ Toomes, dressed in a pair of rust-colored leather pants and a tight black t-shirt, his hair tied back in a ponytail, emerges from the room. As he closes the door behind him, we see Jane dozing, looking (if the readers will forgive me) like the cat that just got the cream. Toomes gently closes the door behind him, and takes a step forward. “Whooo!” Toomes yelps as his knee gives out from underneath him. Guess those old CCW injuries are coming back. He falls to one knee (the good one, mind you), and winces. “Oh…that hurt.’ Toomes gets back to his feet and stretches. What a marathon. Even the dreaded Glass Table match was less physically intensive than this. This woman, Jane Reagan, a veritable cyclone of sexual energy, had drained him. Drained him dry – indeed, the OSW World Champ is kinda exhausted right now. Which is exhibited as Toomes sorta slides along the side of the hallway, using it for support. He works his jaws to get some saliva back, for he has a severe case of cotton mouth. “Need… water…” he rasps as he turns the corner to a bank of elevators. What a week. First, he didn’t actually expect to win the World Title. And he certainly didn’t expect Jane to shack up with him. And he never expected to be sequestered in a luxury hotel room for the past three days, having mind-blowing sex. And these leather pants! Who wears these things anymore? Well, if Jane says so, then so be it! He shall wear them! Toomes stumbles to the bank of elevators, and presses the down button. Holding his arm against the wall for support, he continues to build up saliva, trying to coat his tongue in a protective coating of spit. Licking for three hours straight will do that to you. It was, in short, the best night of his life, Sunday night. He finally hit the big time, the jackpot, the Main Event. He won the OSW World Title… The first person to do so. He had made history. And noone else would ever be able to say that they did so. Kid Wonder had said Toomes would be the first to be forgotten, but that didn’t matter to Toomes. From his debut in September of last year, he watched in the wings as Spider, Jason McAllister, Kensuke Suzahara, and Cianan rotated the CCW title around like they were playing Hot Potato. But now… This was his. He did it. Now if only the cotton mouth would go away. The gleaming metal doors part, much like how Jane’s legs parted for him, and he staggers inside. Collecting himself at the rear wall of the elevator, he leans against it while the doors slide back together. Through bleary eyes, he looks up at the latticed ceiling, where tastefully placed lighting provides tasteful illumination inside the car. God, that woman had fucked the shit out of him. It was like she couldn’t get enough. He had always imagined that stories where the woman is some sort of sexual dynamo were in the mythical province of Penthouse Forum, where the Editors coughed up some erotic story from a Mad Lib or something, and simply said “Oh, Jake from Tallahassee sent this in”. But in Jane’s case… Oh, ho ho ho, Jane. After Crowning Glory, after the match, even while the fans were still screaming for his death, Jane had dragged him back to her office and they screwed right on her desk. And in the locker room shower. And on the locker room floor. And , precariously, on the drive to the hotel. Monday was more of the same. Toes, fingers, lips, tongues, nipples and clitoris…es… All were targets. Toomes smiles as he envisions himself saluting in a military uniform. “Mission Accomplished, Soldier! You finally made her tired!” a gruff George C. Scott voice would rumble out of the skies. “Thanks, God! Can I heal now?” “Sure thing, soldier! Put some Bactine on that pecker, son!” The doors open again at the lobby floor, and Toomes staggers out onto the marble floor, his boot heels klicking as he walks. Or staggers, whichever you prefer. Other inhabitants of the hotel look at him strangely as he weaves his way towards the vending machines. (Yes, I know that most high-class hotels have vending machines in a little room on the floors. Just bear with me, okay?) As he rounds the corner, he hears a voice from behind him. “There he is!” Toomes looks behind him and blearily rubs his eyes. “Huh?” Two large Black men grab him, like how mental patients are grabbed by orderlies. Toomes looks at them uncomphrendingly at first, then it clicks. “Diez! Rod! What are you doing here?” he yelps helplessly. Diez looks down at Sebastian and shakes his head. “Nigga, this motherfucka got it bad. He’s done fucked himself half to death!” Rod Thicke nods his assent. “Word, nigga. Dem cracka bitches be the death of us yet!” A voice from behind them says “But I thought white women were your bread and butter!” Rod turns and says to Jimmy The Dog Man, Sebastian’s former manager from his CCW days… “Yeah, but those white bitches will fuck your day up, my hairy cracka friend!” Toomes looks around confused. “Wha…what are you guys doing here? I thought you were out in Cali, leeching off my apartment!” Jimmy The Dog Man nods grimly. “We were, but after your last promo, with the butchering of that Van Halen song, I knew you had gone too far. You have gone astray of what you do best, and that’s… Well, let’s settle for marginal. That’s the best we can hope for here. You went from great promos with Sierra, then your coma promos, then you went to PWA. And even those were tolerable. But now that you’re in OSW, your promos have gone to shit! This will not stand!” Rod chimes in. “Word.” Rod and Diez begin dragging him away, but Toomes is too tired to resist. “Guys, I got Jane Reagan upstairs! She needs a Diet Coke! Come on, let me go!” he whines. Jimmy regards him balefully. “No. This is necessary. You need to go back to your roots before you’re a joke like Ivan Stanislav. Your promos are just a sick joke these days. This is unacceptable.” Toomes frowns petulantly. “But I’m the OSW World Champ! I gotta be doing something right!” Diez yanks his arm roughly. “Damn, he’s really far gone, ain’t he?” Rod nods. “Them white bitches git you everytime.” “Word, nigga.” We fade out as Toomes is being dragged out of the hotel… To Be Continued When I have The Time
