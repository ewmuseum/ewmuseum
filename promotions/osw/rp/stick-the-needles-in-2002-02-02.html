Stick the Needles In

2 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 2, 2002 This love is of unknown origin. To the massive public, which the two try to separate from, is a quest to perceive the privacy. This love is mysterious, and so far has not been revealed of its beginnings. The love is there, and maybe forever mysterious, a phantom. The love is a fragment of the future. “I’m so much in,” says the lover, Corg. “Love.” The word used to hurt Corg dearly, but now, it warms him, the thought of his lover. Shome Yerta, Corg’s one true motivation in life. He has nothing else to live for, and neither does she. “I can’t believe you said it.” Shome says, smiling at her boyfriend who lays on top of her, with his arms supporting his weight. “I’m so in love.” The two are on Corg’s floor, in his front room of his apartment [room #349]. They just gaze into each other’s eyes, seeing something that contracts their hearts. But yet they are still driven with hate, and still purified by love. “Would you pick up my remains?” Shome asks, gracefully, in love. Corg smiles indefinitely at Shome. “I would, and place them back correctly.” They talk in an unspoken language, an odd language of poetry. It is confusing to those outside of their barrage. Corg reaches forward, slowly, and places his hand on her black lips. He strokes her long, blue hair with his left hand, viewing it carefully. Neither can stop smiling, for the love is growing. “I love you.” Possibly the first time that Isiic Raiyn has ever spoken those words, it flows so smoothly in the path of sympathy. With a low touch, spitting out all that is felt. “I love you.” The male and female bondage. In this case, that is false. The Satanists bondage, never thought to be possible. “Rid all that is good, and all that is bad.” “I love…” The love lasts forever… Until it is interrupted by unwanted guests. A knock as heard at Corg’s door, 3 thuds with their knuckles, piercing the oak wood. Corg is still looking at Shome, but turns to the door. “Damn pizza…” he turns back to Shome, love flaming in his eyes. “I still love you… but I’m hungry, too.” Corg lifts himself up from the hard black floor, and walks over to the front door. His red hair is a bit messed up, and he has no shirt, but he does not seem to care. He opens the door, eager to get the pizza, and to get back to what he was previously doing. “Hello sir, here’s your Domino’s pizza and free cina-sticks.” The delivery boy says, his long brown hair pressed flat on his head. He said it just like they do in the commercial. Corg reaches out and takes the pizza from the boy’s hand. He opens up the pizza box slowly, as all the steam comes out, he checks to make sure that his order was done right. “Half pepperoni… half bacon… Okay, it’s all there.” Corg reaches one hand down into his leather pockets, searching endlessly for the money. He comes up with 2 dimes, and 6 pieces of lint. Obviously, Corg cannot pay for the pizza with that. “Um,” Corg stutters. “Can you hold on for a minute? I think I have some money in the kitchen.” Corg sets the 3 pizza boxes down, and walks left towards the kitchen. His kitchen is all black, much like his front room. He has a refrigerator, a dish washer, a little counter top, nothing much. All that he can afford. He looks in the drawers of the counter top, but he cannot sem to find any money. Frantically, he searches some more. Corg’s stumache is growling, it needs food. Only pizza will do. “Where… where?” Corg asks himself. He knows that he has no money, and he can’t remember why he even ordered the pizza in the first place. Corg shrugs, and walks out of the kitchen, knowing that he has sent the young Domino’s boy on a wild goose chace. The first thing that Corg sees when coming out of the kitchen, is the Domino’s boy laying motionless on the ground, with needles exerted into his palms and face. Then, he sees blood smeared across his back walls, and his one lover Shome eating the pizza carried by the young boy. Her hands are covered with red liquid, whether it is pizza sauce or blood… “Isn’t this nice? We didn’t even have to pay!” Shome calmly says, yet excited. Corg looks like he is going to explode, but he just laughs at his insane lover. “Gosh, I love you…” he says. “This is some great wallpaper.” Twisted love fin
