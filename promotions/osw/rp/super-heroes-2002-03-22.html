Super heroes

22 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-22- 02 The Reality of being a Hero... Some people in the world really get it twisted when it comes to being a hero. What makes them want to be a hero? Is it what they see on t.v. that makes them think that they can go out and do the same? Maybe it's what they read; things like a comic book or something along this lines of that. What people need in this world is a reality check when it comes to the matter of being a 'hero'. And who is the person that will do such a thing? Look no further for EOE has the words and brains to perform such a task... We open to the Town East Mall in Dallas, TX. in the magazine/book store. EOE is killing a little time waiting on his girl Eve to get done with her shopping. She has to look at everything so EOE doesn't even bother going into the stores with her because she takes too long to decide what she wants. EOE's interest lies in the music book section, there he reads books such as Vibe, The Source etc. While flipping through the pages he notices a little boy with a shit load comic magazines. The little boy apparently has that he is in a store and not at home because he's sitting on the floor reading each comic book not missing one page. EOE notices the excitement in the little boy's eyes as he reads. EOE stands there shaking his head in disgust... 'Look at that, another young boy's mind is being corrupted, by those damn.....fake ass books. That's why children these days get in trouble. They get so caught up in those comic, they think they can actually perform those out of the ordinary things just like their hero, they're reading about. The problem is that someone, like their parents, forgets tell them that it's all make believe and what they read is impossible to perform in real life. Well today I'm going to save a childs mind, which is what someone should have done a long time ago with the idiots that I'm currently having a problem with. Someone didn't do there job and those boys that, it's impossible to stand up to guys like me.' EOE walks over to little boy who is still reading and then looks down at him. EOE hesitates for a minute then speaks to the boy. 'What's up little man?' 'Nothing' the boy replies quickly as he continues to read not even looking up at EOE. 'Rude little bastard' EOE says to himself. 'Well, I see you love reading comic books, I suppose those everyone on the front of the books are your heroes?' The little boy finally looks up. 'Yes, they all are. I hope to be like them some day...the good ones.' the boy says with pride. 'That what I was afraid of. How old are you?' ask EOE. '9 and a half and my name is Josh.' he says. 'And a half? Then he tells me his name, someone's parents sure as hell ain't their job.' EOE says to himself. 'Well Josh, has anyone told you the real story about those superheroes, that you want to be like so much?' 'These are the real stories' answers Josh in a defensive way. 'No, no, no I mean the real story.' EOE emphasizes on real. 'See, kid let me to you the truth. The truth is, what the super heroes do they can only do no one else.' 'That's not true!' Josh says defensively. 'No need to get mad. Let me tell you a little story..Before I tell you the story let me ask you this. Do you watch wrestling?' 'I find comic books more interesting.' replies Josh. 'Smart kid.' EOE says to himself sarcastically. 'Anyway, There's these guys named, Mr. V, Mr. NC, Mr. P, Mr. GD, and Mr. M. They all don't like each other. But they have one thing in common and that is that they don't like Mr. E.' 'Why does all their names only one and two letters.' ask Josh. 'I don't know. That's not important. No on to the story. Where was I? Oh yea, They all hate Mr. E, infact they hated him from the moment they saw him. Mr. E had no intensions on doing harm to them, hell he wasn't even paying attention to them. One day he even asked Mr. GD and Mr. V to sort of be apart of his little group. They both opposed, in a rude manner which then made Mr. E mad and when Mr. E get's mad things get real bad. So one day Mr. E decided to settle their differences by fighting, and just when Mr. E was about to solve all of the problems between them, MR. P comes out of no where and attacks Mr. E, which was real bad. He didn't let him get his job done with Mr. GD and Mr. V. Then it doesn't stop from there. One day Mr E. had a chance to pick up were he left off he and just when he was about to get Mr. V , Mr. NC came out of no where and prevented Mr.E once again from getting Mr. V. Now Mr. E is boiling mad, infact he's still mad and soon, very soon they will all meet up and I bet you anything that they will all try to take out Mr E together.' 'What about Mr. M?' ask Josh. 'He's not important.' replies EOE. 'The important thing is, that they are all making a big mistake, a mistake that could determine whether they live the nexy day or not.' 'Oh' says Josh. 'The reason that I'm telling you this little story Josh is that all these people that I talked about the hate Mr. E, were once little boys and they were just like you.' 'Just like me?' ask Josh. 'Yea just like you. They read comic books just like you and no one told them what they read in the books about their super heroes and what they see on t.v. about their super heroes, is only for them to do, it can't be done in real life.' explains EOE 'What about David versus Goliath?' ask Josh 'Goliath, was an idiot. Anything that big that get's defeated by someone that small with stones, is no Goliath. I call that a waste of space and oxygen on this earth, it's good that he gone. Now back to my point. These people that I talked about still think they can perform the same task as their super heroes. But soon they're going to find out that when it comes to a guy like Mr. E, the best thing to do is go the other way and don't even try to stand up to him. When you try to be a hero and defeat a guy like him you get destroyed and never heard from again. The reason why I am telling you this is because on down the line you might come in contact with a guy like Mr. E and you might have a problem with him. I don't want you to end up like the guys in the story are going to. The best thing to do is to walk away, I'm telling you while you have a chance, it's to late for the other guys in the story, they have decided their own fate.' 'What's fate?' ask Josh. 'You'll about that as you get older, it's not important to you now.' answers EOE. EOE puts his hand on Josh's shoulder. 'So what have we learned today?' ask EOE 'Um, Don't try to be a super hero, because only the super heroes can defeat the real bad guys like Mr E.?' answers Josh. 'Exactly! you know you're a smart kid you're going to be somebody one day. Luckily I explained this hero thing to you so you want make a mistake like those other idiots.' 'Thanks, for telling me. I'll tell my friends my friends too, the hell with these comic books.' Josh's leaves the comic books in the middle in the floor and begins to walk out of the store. 'Good job, go watch wrestling or something.' says EOE as he looks on with a smile on his face. 'Hey did that kid say hell?' EOE asks himself with a puzzled look on his face. 'That's a damn shame. I tell a story to kill time and Eve is still not finished. This is the last time she's going shopping before we have to catch a flight.' EOE says to himself. He walks out of the store as sends a message to Eve on his two-way, telling her to hurry the hell up. [End!]
