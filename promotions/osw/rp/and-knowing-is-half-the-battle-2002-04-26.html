And knowing is half the battle.

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-26-2002 Blackness. Blackness transitions to grayness. Grayness transitions to the sudden sounds of an old-fashioned movie projector. The grayness takes on a grainy quality as a simple bouncy tune fills the audio. The words “DAMN presents” appear on the screen, followed by the words “A Doug Troy Film”. The words fade out to gray once again, followed by the words “Dead Federations and YOU.” This picture fades out to black. The picture fades back up on the image of a standard suburban backyard. Trees, bushes and various other shrubbery populate the yard. Oh, and grass. There’s lots of grass too. On the ground. No, it’s grass grass, not weed grass Don’t go all Hix on me, people… Anyway, this yard has a new attraction in it now: a crude cross made of sticks stuck in the ground. Poised over the cross is a small crying boy, about 8 years of age. He sobs and cries over the grave as the narrator starts in. “Ah, it’s another beautiful summer day in the Whitebread family residence! The birds are singing, the breeze is blowing and Mr. Squirrel is getting his paws on as many nuts as he can!” An awkward pause follows. “*ahem* It’s a beautiful day today and everyone is in a cheerful mood… except for little Jimmy over here. Hello, Jimmy!” Little Jimmy looks at the camera, tears streaming down his face. “Why are you killing everyone’s buzz on this beautiful day, little Jimmy?” Little Jimmy sniffles a couple times, then speaks. “My dog Fluffy died, sir.” The narrator responds with a heartfelt “Oh no! That’s horrible!” Little Jimmy nods. “Well, it happens to everyone someday, Jimmy. The best thing you can do is accept it when it happens. Soon you’ll get over childish things like crying over dead pets and relatives and move on to more grownup things, like crying when your favorite sports team doesn’t make the playoffs and crying when your wrestling federation dies.” Little Jimmy is taken aback by this. “What, you mean to tell me that you’ve never seen a dead wrestling federation?” Little Jimmy shakes his head. “Heh heh, well it looks like you have a lot to learn about the world little Jimmy. The fact is that dead wrestling federations are becoming a bigger and bigger part of the American life style!” The picture cuts to a graveyard filled with gravestones. Some are very elaborate while others are awfully plain. “Here at the Dead Federation graveyard, new plots are being purchased daily. Recently, the graveyard had to purchase more land just to keep up with the ever-growing demand for burial plots!” The camera zooms in closer to the gravestones so we can read the engravings. The camera pans up to a sign that reads ‘Fedwars Wing’, then pans back down to a plain marker that reads ‘ICWA’. Nestled next to the ICWA gravestone is the CCW gravestone. However, the area around the gravestone has been dug up and the coffin within lies opened. “Yes, sometimes federations come back from the dead to cause more havoc. They are however soon returned back to their resting place.” The camera pans over to a trio of grave markers. The first one is elaborate, but as we go down the line they get more plain. The first one reads ‘JEW’, and the second and third are ‘JEW2’ and ‘JEW3’. Next in the line is a gravestone in bad condition. Chunks have been ripped off the stone, the bottom half is stained yellow and resting atop the gravestone is a brown mass that one can only assume is shit. The writing on the tombstone: ‘GSE’. Written under the fed’s name in graffiti are the words ‘Good riddance’. “All federations die in time, it’s inevitable. However, one thing you can do to prepare yourself is the recognize the signs of a dying federation!” The scene changes to a chalkboard with the words ‘How to know when a federation is dying’ written on it. A scientist-type person stands in front of the board with a pointer stick in his hand. “The first sign of a dying federation is when they start to do the same thing over and over again. Things such as commissioner status changing hands based on a match outcome time and time again are the leading indicator of oncoming death.” The scientist illustrates this point by pointing to a circle drawn on the board. “The second sign is dartboard booking. Dartboard booking is a symptom of a bloated roster, which is a fatal federation disease. Dartboard booking is the act of trying to pack as many people onto a show by making dozens of 4-10 man matches.” The scientist helps out by pointing to a picture of a dartboard. “The third sign is an overabundance of Satanic Jobbers. As we all know, Satanic Jobbers are utterly worthless, aren’t they little Jimmy? That’s right.” The scientist points to a crudely drawn stick figure with horns coming out of its head. “Lastly, the fourth and final sign of a dying wrestling federation is when they try to invade another, more healthy federation. The principle behind this is that they want to leech off the superior status and popularity of the healthy federation and rejuvenate themselves.” The scientist points to 4 crudely drawn stick figures. Words like ‘gay’, ‘pedophile’, ‘assbag’ and ‘furry’ have been written over the figures with arrows pointing down. “Needless to say, this tactic never works.” The scientist exchanges his pointer stick for a sledgehammer, winds back and smashes the drawing. The scene changes back to little Jimmy in his backyard. Little Jimmy talks to the camera. “What’s that little Jimmy? You have a friend named little Tyler who’s wrestling federation is dying? Does he know this yet?” Little Jimmy shakes his head no. “Well then, I think you’d better tell him before it dies on him so it won’t hit him so hard. If he hurries, he can get prime burial ground before the Dead Wrestling Federation graveyard runs out again!” The scene cuts back to the graveyard, where there’s a large hole in the ground. Sitting over the large hole is a crude tombstone that reads ‘PCW’. Under that reads ‘Gravestone provided by Fedkillers Inc. and OSW’. “Ah, see the benefits of careful planning? Now little Tyler’s federation has somewhere to rest peacefully when it dies.” The scene cuts back to little Jimmy. “Good thinking, little Jimmy! You’ve learned a lot from this film! Run along now, little Jimmy! Go help your dad throw Fluffy into the City Dump! And make sure to let little Tyler know about his fed’s upcoming death!” The feed fades back to the grainy gray screen as the words ‘A Doug Troy Film’ appear. After a couple seconds, the picture and the music fade to nothingness.
