Recovery time? Not with CLIT around!

2002-04-13

Amy 'Aimz' Campbell

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Note: Not great, not awful...it's just here, I got bored.) Some folks are just destined to be different... Others are manufactured to be identical. Let's take a look at Brooklyn Born and Bred, the 'lead' faction of Old School Wrestling, for example... There you have three men, all play lap dogs to one woman. When one member has a match, at least two other members of the group are required to show up so that one person can win, even if the opponent is a hundred and forty something pound, nineteen year-old girl... ESPECIALLY if that hundred and forty pound girl has defied them, insulted them and just outright embarrassed the three, or had some part in these acts, before. Why? Well, have you ever seen those nature videos...have you noticed how a pack of wolves singles out one deer and all attack so they score a sure kill? This is present in human behavior, as well. One talentless man twice her size wasn't enough to take home the victory for the group and, because of a streaking Michael Dame, neither were two...so out came their alpha female, with a lead pipe and a can of hairspray for the eyes. That scored the bandits a victory, alright. Unfortunately enough for the poor souls, it's only a good victory in their eyes...in the eyes of the general public, it's a compliment to Amy Campbell that, to take her down and out it took two men who, combined, were almost four times her weight, another woman and two separate weapons. In the end, she had been attacked by Meanstreak...why? Well, she apparently got in his way...plus, what giant doesn't take pleasure in the brutal beating of a small woman? All of this out of mind, Amy was staggering with a cringed expression to the door of her old home. Where? Rent-A-Hero's apartment. Bre had wandered off to explore this new city while Aimz, of course, decided to get some rest...after all, the girl could barely move. It didn't even look like she had bothered to take a look at her clothing when she dressed...a pair of rather awkward, barely baggy jeans along with a plain white t-shirt and untied Lugz boots were all she had on...a little plain for Amy Campbell, wouldn't you think? None-the-less, when she stepped inside it was to be greeted by... Silence. Darkness. Needless to say, this was a little odd. RAH should have been home, he was due back late the night before after his PCW Cruiserweight Title win...but he didn't make his presence known when Amy walked in. But then she was tipped off by a blue glow hitting the wall adjacent to Rent's bedroom...the TV was on, maybe he was there? Well she didn't waste any time! Dropping her bags, the redheaded former sidekick of PTC's favorite hero jogged into the room, finding RAH dead asleep in a chair in front of the television that appeared to be showing some Discovery Channel starfish documentary. Well, at least we know comic book dweebs don't just stick to The Cartoon Network! Amy had to smile to herself, it was so different, oddly reassuring to see Rent-A-Hero's masked face so calm instead of distorted in one of those comedic expressions he seemed to always have on display. She couldn't help but sigh at the sight of him looking so close to normal. Unfortunately, he wasn't in a heavy sleep, and that slight sound woke the hero... ''Huh....hmmm...'' He was mumbling something sleepily before opening his eyes. RAH didn't focus, and only saw the figure of Amy standing over him, a shadow over the bright TV screen. ''WHAA?!'' He jolted upwards, only to get gently coaxed back down by the redhead, who flicked the lights on and the TV off before going back over to sit on the floor in front of RAH's chair. ''Relax, Rentie...it's just me.'' Amy smiled at him yet again, this time with a suspiciously kind expression. While Rent-A-Hero remained quite silent, Aimz decided to just pick up some semblance of conversation, trying to discuss the events of the past working weekend for both. ''So...'' She had to search a moment for an appropriate event to speak of. ''...you're Cruiserweight Champ! That's gotta feel good...'' It's not like it wasn't expected for Amy's mind to wander after ''That's gotta feel good.''...and of course, it did, though she tried to keep the mischievous smirk from surfacing. ''Hmmm?'' Rent answered groggily, just staring at her a moment...appreciating the sight that was seated before him. ''Oh........yes...of course! It feels positively wizzer...I really did give Anton what he deserved, a first-class spankering to the tenth power!'' What could she say, not much had changed about hero-boy since PWA...except the fact that he figured out that he actually liked his former sidekick. ''Well, champ...you feeling alright? No bruises or scrapes?'' Sure, it was a lame question, but Amy was too sore, too tired to think. ''Nothing a little ice hasn't cured!'' RAH flashed her a million-dollar, nearly blinding, smile. ''But you're my concern at the moment, I saw that big bully hurt you!'' ''Meh...'' Amy was suddenly reminded of her injuries, the pain searing down her neck and spine rearing it's ugly head all over again. Still, she couldn't let on the extent of it to Rentie...she made it so he wasn't to be bothered with her troubles. ''...not that bad, just a little stiffness...'' ''No matter how small the Streak-induced injuries, I will make sure he pays...be it now, at the interfed pay-per-view extravaganza or years from now. Don't worry, fair Amy, I will let loose the spankering of a thousand suns all over his big, mean booty!'' ''Oh God...'' Aimz muttered, rubbing her forehead. ''By the way...'' He continued, ''...CLIT isn't very happy with you...'' Did he just say...................hot damn, he sure did! ''What...'' Amy was, needless to say, quite stunned...thinking RAH was speaking on a completely different level than usual. She stood up, leaning against the TV before continuing... ''...did you just say?'' ''The C hristian L adies I nternational T ribunal...'' RAH looked confused, wondering why mention of the organization drew such a reaction. Obviously, the poor man was STILL a full-fledged virgin. ''...they called to talk about the guide you wrote yesterday.'' ''Really now,'' She smirked, raising an eyebrow through visibly disappointed that Rent was still completely innocent. ''...and what did they have to say?'' ''The woman who called said her name was Cindy Muffdiver, she said ''Tell Aimz that if she does not choose to denounce her clever little Cool Guide, the ladies of CLIT will take swift and immediate action.''...'' He cringed, remembering. ''...she yelled a lot at me and went on about how I shouldn't let you influence me.'' ''Swift and immediate action?'' Amy was wondering aloud again, practically waving the message off. ''Pfft...what're they gonna do, throw a Bible at me?'' ''I don't know...'' Rent was rather sincere, incredibly serious...to the point that Aimz was barely holding in a chuckle. ''Amy, Bibles can inflict some pretty nasty paper-cuts...'' Aimz snickered, shifting up to sit on the arm of Rent-A-Hero's chair. ''Nothing worse than I did to Jeanie The Girlscout.'' ''That really was a shame,'' RAH mumbled. ''...I mean, my heart belongs to you and all, but Jeanie just wanted to have a good time.'' ''No, she wanted at something only I'll ever get...'' She stated that more as an order than a comment. ''...and I wouldn't allow for it. That little twinkie absolutely reeked of skankage, she would've gone down on you faster than...well...ME!'' ''Come now, Aimz! I asked you earlier to only use language a hero such as myself would understand!'' ''Nevermind...'' She sighed. ''...I just...well...yeah....nevermind!'' That moment teetered a little too close on the edge of being sentimental for Amy's comfort. The urge to jump RAH always rose and the urge to tell him exactly what her feelings meant was even stronger...it was almost unbearable, driving the redhead to a proverbial breaking point until her thoughts were shattered by a loud ring of the doorbell. She used this opportunity to go collect herself. ''I'll get it!'' Rent didn't object, instead he sat there and watched Amy closely as she left the room...he, too, experiencing the same feelings she had. They were mutually thankful for the interruption, that much was obvious...otherwise it might have become awkward for both. Back to Amy... When she opened the door, it was to the sight of a short, dumpy woman in a flowery, frilly classical 'Sunday Dress'... Looking around a moment, the much taller redhead soon questioned, ''Umm...can I help you?'' ''Yes, yes you can!'' The woman seemed irate. ''Stand still!'' Amy was rather confused, casting the odd lady a weird glance. Just then, she was smacked in the forehead with...a hardcover Bible?! ''What the fuck?!'' Aimz shouted after the woman, ran down the hall in hopes of catching her but, before she could catch up, the lady was out the door. It was worthless to follow her...besides, beating old people up was extreme, even for Amy. She rubbed her now red forehead as she stepped back inside, finding RAH standing just behind the doorway after hearing the loud thud of the Bible connecting with Amy's flesh. ''What happened?!'' ''CLIT began some swift and immediate action...'' She grumbled. ''I think I'll have to act out doubletime now...fucking churchie bitches...'' Rent-A-Hero looked confused, and expressed it well. ''Hmm?'' ''Nothing...nothing. But at Intervention, you're gonna see me get a little wild...'' ''Wild?'' ''Again...nevermind, I'm just gonna show the Bible-bangers what a real fight is, I can start with the Waterpark matchup.'' Amy smiled, almost mischievious. Everything basically went to black at this point...idle conversation bubbling between the hero and the redhead, Aimz still trying to get RAH to act semi-normal for a change.
