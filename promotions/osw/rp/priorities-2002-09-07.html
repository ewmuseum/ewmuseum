Priorities

7 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-7-02 It was cold in Russia. Not that that was much of a surprise, but to Alexei Ruslan it had been a long time since he had felt the stinging cold of his Motherland since he had stayed in the United States with Ivan Stanislav for quite a long period of time. As his shoes padded along the hardwood floor of the Kremlin and he walked towards Igor Karpov’s office, he did something he hadn’t done since he was a child. He shivered. He adjusted his tie beneath his brown trench coat and passed a few office workers. They didn’t dare make eye contact with him, but they knew who he was. Everybody knew who he was. Since he had returned to Russia, Ruslan had not received any word as to why he had been called back. He was asked to return due to extremely sensitive material, but instead he was asked to relax throughout the week. For three days, Alexei Ruslan did nothing more than live a life of relative luxury. Ruslan opened the hardwood door and walked into Igor Karpov’s office. He needn’t knock, Karpov knew he was on his way. Sure enough, Igor Karpov sat at his desk in his military uniform. The major proponent of Ivan Stanislav’s suspension and a member of the new guard in Russia, Igor Karpov was a piece of filth, and he knew it. Karpov stood as Alexei walked in and rounded his desk, meeting Ruslan halfway with a firm handshake. “Ruslan.” “Karpov.” The two men stared at one another for a long second and nodded, but Ruslan wanted to cut straight to the chase. “What am I doing here,” Alexei began, “I am needed back in America with Ivan and I do not wish to be here any longer.” A sly sneer covered Karpov’s face as he walked past Ruslan to his door and opened it, “Come Alexei, lets take a walk, eh?” Ruslan turned and walked out of the room and back down the hallway with Karpov, who locked the door behind him and walked at his side at about an inch higher than Ruslan. His hands were behind his back as he walked, “So, what of America? How is our mighty warrior fairing?” Alexei smiled, he knew this was coming and he knew exactly what to say, “It is going quite well as matter of fact! I am extremely surprised at the progress that Ivan has made…” “Surprised? You surprised at Ivan’s progress?” Karpov interjected. Alexei looked up at him and continued flatly, letting Karpov know his annoyance. “….made on the public relations front.” Karpov nodded and continued to walk while Alexei sustained his side of the conversation. “I would venture to say that after our most recent Pay Per View, Ivan Stanislav is the biggest fighter for the people as a whole in the OSW. Buyers of suspenders, red in particular, has gone up five percent!” Alexei smiled, talking money with Karpov was the way to go. Karpov nodded, “And his wrestling ability?” “Is there any question of his wrestling ability, Igor?” “Well,” Igor began, “Ivan is getting a little… old. Is his body still holding up after the constant battles he has had to endure?” Alexei almost laughed, “Come now Igor, don’t be stupid.” Lieutenant Karpov shot Alexei a sudden look, “Twenty years from now when Ivan is sixty, he will still be stronger than men half his age and you and I know it.” Karpov nodded, he should have expected that. But it was Ruslan’s turn to ask questions. “When is the suspension going to be lifted, Igor? It has been an awful long time since you put it on him.” Alexei looked at Karpov intensely, enough to make Karpov actually falter for a moment. “Uh….” He began, “that is out of my hands Alexei and you know it. You people think I am the one responsible for all of this, but no, it is not I, it is Ivan himself that is responsible for his suspension.” Karpov stood in front of a door along the hallway and opened it, “Follow me, Alexei.” Alexei followed Karpov into the room where a television and VCR was set up along the far wall as well as a conference table. “One of the primary orders for Ivan was to get the OSW World Title. And it has also been one of his primary failures.” “I beg to differ, Lieutenant. Sure Ivan has not won World Title yet, but he will in time.” “Time is something that I don’t have, Alexei. If he does not win the title, I look bad, and I will not have that happen.” Karpov said sternly. Alexei stood defiant and crossed his arms in front of his chest, “It is my belief that the Motherland is being too harsh on Praporschik Stanislav.” Alexei continued, “He has done so much for her for so long and still you ask for more. I am not saying he is not capable of fulfilling the tasks, but do you not think that after so much it is time to at least allow him to have freedom? Is that not what the Russian Federation is all about?” Karpov took a step towards Alexei, not liking the spinning of his own federation against him, “He is a soldier.” “He is suspended.” Karpov glared at Alexei but Ruslan fired back, “He is not some puppet, you cannot pull his strings.” Igor Karpov growled, “Watch me.” Alexei’s eyes widened at the blatant admittance of Karpov. Igor Karpov did not bat an eye and continued, “Alexei, you would do the same thing in my position and you know it. You just will not admit it because for once, you are not the one in control of the situation. I am.” “He is my dearest friend! I would die for Ivan Stanislav!” Alexei retorted. “Aha! Ha! Ha!” laughed Karpov as he took a few steps from Ruslan, “Spare me your stabbing tongue and lies, Alexei Ruslan. You know that you would take advantage of anything and everything if you had the chance.” Ruslan stood stock still, his jaw held tightly. Karpov rounded the conference table and spoke in an almost condescending manner, “All those people you’ve killed, people that you’ve eliminated just because they got in your way. Friendship…” Karpov spat on the carpet, “…the only friend you have is yourself and that revolver hidden beneath your jacket.” Igor stopped and placed his hands on the table, leaning forward towards Ruslan, “You’re no Saint, Alexei Gregorovich, and you know better than anyone how to use people to your own benefit.” “This is different…” “Is it?!” Karpov snapped, staring straight at Ruslan. Alexei decided to change the subject, “Ivan has a shot at World Title on Slam. While I do not know the outcome yet, as it happened earlier tonight in America, I am sure he won. I do not know of a single force on the planet that would stop him from bringing the OSW title to the Motherland.” “Is that so?” Karpov questioned. “I would bet my life on it.” Ruslan fired back. Karpov smiled, “While it normally takes few days to get transcript and tapes of things in America, I had someone wire a copy of the match itself to Russia.” “A spy in the stands?” “Call it what you want,” Karpov said, “I know of the outcome, as well.” Karpov grinned and produced a remote control and quickly hit a button, “This is the force, Alexei Gregorovich.” A scene from the Jane Says Match begins to play…. ~~ Alan Kriegman walks into the picture followed by Trashcan Man. In the ring Mexi and Ivan stare up in shock at the jumbotron and what they are seeing as Trashy twists the kennel so we can see a frightened, beaten and terrified Tempest inside. Nate: OH MY GOD ALAN KRIEGMAN KIDNAPPED TEMPEST!!! Upon seeing this Ivan makes a beeline to the back but Mexi is quick on his tail and gets in front of him. Ivan: What are you doing? Get out of Ivan’s way. Mexi: I’ll go get her Ivan, you stay here and win the title. The Think Tank want you to go, they are expecting you, they aren’t expecting me. Ivan: NYET!!! How does Ivan know you aren’t in on the plan!! I go myself. As Ivan and Mexi disappear backstage the jumbotron crackles again and the image of only Tempest in the kennel can be seen as Alan and Trashy are gone. ~~ Alexei stood in shock as he watched the scene continue. Karpov added his own commentary. “You see, Alexei. You see what he did! He walked away from the ring and gave up on his fight for that girl. That girl! He sacrificed Mother Russia for the girl.” “But she…” “Shut up Alexei!” Karpov snorted, “Don’t talk about her needing help. You once said the job comes before all else, and you know it. This is why I took you away….” “You…” Alexei began, realizing now that it was a trick. “That’s right! To see what he would do on his own, and now I see! Had you been there, it may have been different, but instead I chose to do this experiment.” Karpov sneered, “Watch on Alexei, this is my favorite part…” ~~ In the room Ivan and Tempest are in there own world as two have a death grip on each other after freeing Tempest from her trap. The screen slides to reveal a split screen and the shocked image of Mexi staring into the camera and Ivan still holding and protecting Tempest leaving only Ivan’s soft words as the 2002 OSW copyright logo pops onto screen and the feed cuts out. Ivan: You are safe Tempest. You are safe. ~~ Karpov shut the tape off and growled, “She is safe, that is good. But Mother Russia paid the price instead. I don’t think we should disagree on that, Alexei Ruslan.” Igor stood indignant, “It sickens me to see something like that. Someone so… so weak. After seeing that, his suspension might need to be taken further. You go back to Russia Ruslan, and you talk to that overgrown failure. Many men in Russia are very concerned with his progress, or lack thereof.” Ruslan shook his head, disgusted by Karpov, “This is sickening, what you are doing Karpov.” “You tell Ivan that Igor Karpov is watching his every move. And get a list of priorities from him.” Igor nodded, “Any force in the world huh? How about a stupid troublemaker that does nothing but get in the way? That has precedence over his homeland!” “Do not talk about her like that.” Ruslan growled, suddenly slightly threatening. “Get out of here and back to your post, Ruslan. This conversation is over. And regarding her, you too were supposed to take care of her and you failed, I hope your priorities are straight as well!” Alexei breathed heavily, turned, and walked out of the room. He calmly walked down the hallway, a sentence of Karpov’s echoing in his mind…. 'His suspension might need to be taken further.' He knew what that might mean. Alexei Ruslan shivered again, but this time it was not from the cold.
