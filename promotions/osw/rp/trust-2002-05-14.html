Trust....

14 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-14 Kimbusa…. Who was the man? Was he really a friend? Or was he a spy? Possibly more a killer? Evil he was. She couldn’t judge. Yet… Bethany sat back in a lounge chair. The spring heat reflected off the small beads of sweat that lay on her tanned skin. Kimbusa. Because of this man many things had happened in the past few weeks. Physical as well as mental. She didn’t blame Kimbusa totally for her face. Though she believed he was partially responsible. His interference on her behalf had cost her a great many things. Her beauty… her honor… But foremost… The Queen of Extreme tournament. She had been friendly with him as of late. Giving him a tidbit of advice. But she didn’t trust him. Not as for as her six foot four inch frame could throw his five billion ton ass. The news of her loss in the spot of the Cruiserweight Tournament also made her feelings for him turn into a more violent. Because of his careless actions at the cabin… She had been removed from the tournament and her in ring contract revoked. Though of course she knew that is what he was aiming for. Grandfather didn’t want her to wrestle. The sight of Bethany getting mangled probably gave the old man a heart attack. She could only wish such a tragedy could happen. The day the man died she could be free to do her will. Because of the Lillith incident.. She also lost control of her Syndicate. Her grandfather had made sure she was not welcome back at the club. The place she had started with her own sweat, tears… and other’s blood. Her life’s work taken away in an instant. She knew why she hated Kimbusa so much. He had destroyed the very life she had ever known. Fate was a strange thing though. Was it time for her to move on? Time to start on a different path? Her own way? No one else to tell her what to do? Maybe she was right… Maybe it was time for a new being… One thing was clear. Trust no one. Not even herself.
