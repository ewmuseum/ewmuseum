A Modest Proposal

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Sorry, can't sleep and I have Sociology class in 5 hours. So this is the result of sleep deprivation...) Good Evening, my fellow Americans. As your President, I have seen the enemy. And the enemy is us. We, as Americans, have ruined our much-vaunted homeland, destroyed countless people's lives in search for the Almighty dollar, all sanctified by 'God'. We have over-reached our boundaries, infringed on the lives of other people in far off, distant lands... Waged wars over oil and ideas. Funded and trained killers to topple governments and installed bloodthirsty dictators. All the while, we have destroyed our home, ignored the suffering of our own people, always in the name of God and dollars. Some men have tried to change things, to better us all, but these men have either been drowned out by the sounds of the clinking of silver into palms, or silenced by the harsh report of a gun. And while we glorify these men, we have yet to put their ideas and ideals into action. 'Sure', we say, 'That's a good idea, but I'm not the one to do it. Let our children be the ones to put into effect.' That's what our fathers said. And that's what we're saying. And our children will be saying it to. The buck has to stop somewhere. Somewhere, somebody has to take responsibility for their actions. Someone has to take the future that we're effortlessly squandering and do something. My fellow Americans, the buck stops here. I propose withdrawing from every strategic overseas military installation, taking our material, troops, and firepower with us. Let the hard working indigenous people of where ever get the installation, let them do with it as they see fit. After all, the loss in an installation is chump change when compared to the ridiculous amount of foreign aid we hemmorage each year. Let these men and women come home to thier loved ones, let them do something good for America - because we all know that massing troops in a hostile country where noone wants you there anyway until the bullets start flying isn't really serving America, they arn't really defending our way of life. They're imposing it on someone else, and that, my fellow Americans, is wrong. Let these men and women get an education, become the teachers and workers of our great land, let them do something productive, let them really defend our way of life by being in it, perpetuating it. I propose that, in light of the growing ecological disaster we're all recklessly careening into in our gas-guzzling Sport Utility Vehicles, that we raise the price of gasoline to a measy five dollars US per gallon. After all, the Germans and Japanese have been doing it for years, and they have nowhere near the amount of pollution that we do. Raising gas prices will eventually render driving everywhere not economically viable, and the public, while demanding my head on a platter, will be forced through necessity to look into hybrid gas-electric vehicles. With the subsequent abandonment of old vehicles , which will be drained of all hazardous fluids and used to form a coral reef somewhere, the new fleet of gas-electric vehicles will become the order of the day. They will be expensive... but then there's something called public transportation. Millions of miles of traintracks lie all over this great nation - why arn't we using them? There are buses and planes, to transport you where you need to go. Why arn't you using them?Because your identity is tied up in a material possession, your car? I thought Americans were made of sterner stuff. The extra money raised from the fuel hike would be put into my next point - education. WIth the lack of perponderous military spending and the extra money culled from gasoline, since Americans are loathe to give up their precious SUVs, all community colleges and state universities will be funded by the state - that means the professor's salaries will be paid for by the state, which in turn renders tuition unnecessary. Only private colleges would charge tuition. The end result? More students in the universities, no longer held back by lack of money, no need to be driven into crushing debt to get what we as intelligent beings deserve - an education. The only requirement would be to have the acceptable grade point average and the wherewithal to actually get an education. All public schools in the K012 system would also recieve generous state funding. Our academic standing amoung the nations is pathetic = if you want to support the United States, wave the flag and think you're patriotic, then get take a night class and make sure your kids get to school on time. We've become a nation of drunken morons with guns. Is that what our founding fathers wanted? Stupid fat Americans shooting anything that moves or worst yet, doesn't agree with our outdated moral codes and Victorian-Era repression? We have the priveledge to be free and the shining example to the rest of the globe - and yet we're slaves to the television, letting corporations tell us how to act, dress, think, raise children, impose our behaviors onto others. Disgusting. I propose that there be a true separation of Church and State. No longer will there be a Religious Right making your laws, looking down upon you and telling you how to live. No longer will religious intolerance beallowed to settle into the backs of our minds, telling us how to react to something different than us. No longer will someone be denied the right to practice whatever religion they wish, no longer will young mothers and doctors be subject to nailbombs and death threats. Religious orders will no longer be tax-exempt, no will they be immune to civil prosecution for their misdeeds. No longer will religious leaders be allowed to destroy the futures of their charges. No longer will a church harbor these criminals. Churches and religious organizations will pay taxes and be held legally accountable to the law of the land, not separate from it. If the church resides in the state, then the state will not suffer from the church's lack of contribuition. Churches will be encouraged to contribute to their community, lest they be bulldozed in favor of say, a health clinic or a library. Better that we elevate learning and education and life, rather than repression and isolationism. I propose the legalization and taxation of all mind-altering substances. Alcohol, tobacco, and all forms of narcotics, hallucinogins, and stimulants, the whole spectrum of drugs, will be legal and distributed by authorized sources, heavily taxed and the proceeds will go to the Health Care system. If you want to use, then by all means, but you will be paying for it. And lest we forget, alcohol and cigarettes kill more Americans than crack, heroin, and cocaine combined. A beer is a drug, no different from a joint. With the advertisment of these drugs that are legal in our culture and society, we deem it okay to go out and get drunk. But noone realizes the detrimental effects of these drugs until its too late and someone is crippled or killed. Then the illusion is shattered and the good time beccomes something different. If all drugs are categorized into one group, sold by authorized distributors, then this message becomes clearer. The high prices will inhibit underage drinking and usage of drugs on a wide scale, and the social attitude will change. No longer will drinking alcohol be considered harmless. And with education and understanding, then perhaps we can utilize them safely and carefully. Ignorance of the truth is our greatest handicap. I propose, with the surplus of money that we will get from the legalization of drugs, the withdrawal of military forces and the cut in military spending, and the rise in oil prices that we create a nation-wide health care system that covers every American, not one human being excluded. Why are we allowing our own countrymen to suffer, yet we mindlessly shuffle off money to a Third World country to give ourselves peace of mind? That, my fellow Americans, is obscene, that we would practice such blind-eyed hypocrisy. Don't mistake altruism and humanism with stupidity. Our own sons and daughters are struggling to find health care, in fear of breaking a bone or getting sick because they can't afford a trip to the emergency room. That is obscene, it is reprihensible to thik that we would allow this in the greatest nation on the face of the planet! My fellow Americans, while a starving child in Afghanistan is a heart rending sight, what about the poor starving inner-city child in our own country? Where is our sense of self-worth if we allow such negligence to run unchecked in our own backyard? Finally, I propose a policy of zero tolerance to those who would try to interfere with America's process of healing. Any terrorist attack on our nation will result in swift and bloody vengenace against the country that harbors the terrorist group. To those who wish to make war on us while we allow you to live your lives free of an American presence, you will find that our willingness to make peace is not an indicator of weakness - far from it. We will render your land uninhabitable, we will destroy your cities and your people, and we will destroy everything you have ever created in a maelstrom of nuclear fire. If you start a war, we will end it -permenantly in a flash of atomic light. In closing, we are heedlessly hurtling towards the road of self-destruction. Will we be the ones to change the course, or will we allow the opportunity to make a change pass us by and wait for someone else to do something? We are the ones who can make a difference, our only impediment is the tired, outdated ideologies of old and our unwilliness to endure hardship to produce change. Thank you and good night. (This IS sleep deprivation)
