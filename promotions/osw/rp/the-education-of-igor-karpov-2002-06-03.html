The Education of Igor Karpov

3 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-3-02 Upon his release from the Winnipeg Jail, Ivan Stanislav was unable to return back to his homeland, Russia. Saturday Slam was rapidly approaching and Stanislav would need to work out twice as hard to ensure his preparedness for the match ahead, after all, he had been confined to a 13 ft by 13 ft room for almost an entire week. But consider the fact that The Russian Federation joined NATO and the knowledge that it was Russia itself that denied Ivan’s bail, and then the three days between Ivan leaving the jail and now, finally returning to Russia. That is a lot of time to boil. That is a lot of time to seethe. And that is a lot of time for Ivan Stanislav to decide just exactly what he is going to do…. ~~~~ Lieutenant Igor Karpov sat in his office content as he filled out a report regarding appropriation of money towards ammunition. The young Lieutenant, barely 35 years of age, had a tremendous amount of power in his hands and knew how to use it. One of the proponents of Stanislav’s prolonged incarceration, Karpov knew that the Praporschik would be returning soon and probably be full of the rage that he always had. But to Lieutenant Karpov, this was nothing more than an idle threat. Stanislav wouldn’t directly do anything, he didn’t have the political power. Stanislav, to Karpov, was a bag of wind that just did not want to die. But slowly, he knew that the “Old Communist” would slowly give way to the pressure of the Russian Federation, whether he knew it or not. He had heard Ivan boast about being a “Russian first and Communist Second.” And if that was the case, Stanislav would fall in line just perfectly, and if not, a few manipulative moves here and there would move the unwitting Stanislav back in his place. Karpov paused for a moment and looked around his office and grinned, quite pleased with what he had become. In Russia, Political might, for the most part, was measured not by the length of your tie as in the western countries, but rather the amount of stripes and badges that you have. Who knows, maybe after Putin is gone, President Karpov could be?? Karpov rubbed his chin and pondered this, but his peace was about to be interrupted. The interruption came in the form of a heavy, hollow thud that rumbled through the room. The source? Karpov’s heavy, steel door. For security purposes, a man of such stature in Russia needs to be protected, and a heavy steel door is one way to ensure security. However, as Karpov would learn after the second heavy thud, something was battering the door down! A small indentation, over a foot across and slightly oval shaped, pushed into the steel of the door! Karpov blinked and dropped his pen as a third thud rocked the door and the indentation grew more prominent! Softly, one could hear a loud, angry voice, “kaaaarrrpoooovvv….kaaarrrrpoooovvv….” Something outside was yelling his name, and it was overpowering the soundproofing power of Karpov’s room! Karpov stood slowly, completely dumbfounded. He scrambled into his desk and pulled out a revolver, prepared for anything! “kaaaaAAARRRPOOOOVVVVV!!!” Suddenly, the door gave in and fell to the floor, ripping completely from the wall and landing with a clang as an enraged Ivan Stanislav lumbered into the room, boot first as the door fell, and stood with rage in his eyes. “YOU!” roared Ivan as he pointed an angry finger at Karpov! The young Lieutenant hit his intercom. “Mansur! You there!!” Yelped Karpov, hoping that his secretary was there. “Mansur went to sleep, Karpov! I am here to talk to YOU!!” roared Ivan again as he began to head towards Karpov. Igor Karpov took a step backwards and pointed up at a camera in the corner of a room, “They can see this, Praporschik!” Ivan reached behind his back and pulled out a pistol, swinging it wildly and firing it at the camera, destroying it with precision aim! The bullet hit the wall and bounced off as the room had been bullet proofed. The bullet ricocheted across the room and took out a picture on the far wall. Karpov screamed and dropped his gun as Stanislav put his pistol back in the waist of his pants and grabbed the side of Karpov’s desk. With no more than a shrug, Stanislav sent the desk up on it’s side and sliding across the hardwood floor, where it met the wall and shattered, destroying everything within and on top of Karpov’s desk! Karpov backpedaled into the wall behind him, “P..Praporschik, you will..you will pay for that!” Stanislav took one heavy step towards Karpov, which was more than enough to close the gap between the two, “Well, if Ivan is going to pay, he had better pay a lot then! After all…that is the capitalist way, is it not!!” Karpov brought his hand up to stop Stanislav, but Ivan smacked his hand away, eliciting a cracking noise from Karpov’s hand! Karpov yelped in pain as the hand of Ivan Stanislav wrapped around the thin neck of Karpov and easily lifted him up off the floor, to bring him eye to eye with Ivan Stanislav. “It is much different when you stare into the eyes of the man that you stab in the back, is it not Karpov?” growled Ivan, his skull dwarfing Karpov’s. “I..I…..I didn’t…Tomifei..I…” stammered Karpov, beyond being coherent now. Stanislav gritted his teeth and reached behind his back with his free hand as the voice of Karpov came from Stanislav’s back pocket…. Ivan is old, Alexei. He is not much use to Russia now that we are making these moves towards capitalism. To a communist, Ivan is the greatest thing in world, but to us, he is nothing. He is loud and nothing more. Karpov’s eyes grew to the size of sauces as Ivan clicked off the tape recorder in his back pocket. “It is not smart to speak loudly around an ex-KGB member, Karpov. Do you think Comrade Alexei would not have prepared for your arrogance?” Stanislav squeezed harder, his knuckles turning white as the flesh of Karpov disappeared between his pale hands. Karpov kicked weakly and gagged. “I could kill you right now, Igor Karpov, and I can assure you that I would not get in trouble. I have ways of making people disappear….men that fight for themselves and not for the people are men that I hate…you are one of them.” Karpov grabbed at Ivan’s face weakly, tugging at The Russian Bear’s scraggly beard. “Did you think Ivan would remain in Winnipeg forever? Did you think he would not have the GUTS to confront you?!” Ivan pulled Karpov away from the wall and slammed him back into it as the Lieutenant’s face began to turn a dark shade of purple. A vein formed on Stanislav’s head as he reached back into his pocket again and turned on the recorder. The words of Karpov were again heard. To find out that all you have worked for is wasted. “Is that so, Karpov? Is my life a waste?!” Ivan dropped Karpov and fixed the waist of his pants. “It is not all wasted! I can still fix one thing!” Ivan reached back and pulled out his revolver, he spun the chamber, being sure to have a loaded chamber ready. “IS MY LIFE A WASTE!!!” Ivan screamed, more hysterical now as he leveled the gun to the forehead of Karpov. Stanislav’s eyes widened in a truly psychotic way as Karpov pleaded for his life. “HAVE ALL I DONE BEEN WASTED? HAVE I BEEN NOTHING!! HAVE I WASTED EVERYTHING!!! WHO IS LAUGHING NOW KARPOV!! WHO IS LAUGHING NOW AT IVAN SERGEIOVICH STANISLAV!!!!” Ivan screamed, pushing the barrel of the gun into the forehead of Karpov, “I HAVE NOT ABANDONED RUSSIA!! THEY HAVE ABANDONED ME!!!” Stanislav bellowed again and then blinked, his face suddenly calming, the blood leaving his face. Stanislav dropped the gun as if it burned with an unholy heat and took a step back, looking at his hands. He grabbed the side of his head and roared loudly and then turned and rumbled out of the room as quickly as he had come. Igor Karpov sat in the eerie silence after the sudden assault from Ivan Stanislav. Never again would he speak out of turn about Ivan Stanislav. The stories were true: Ivan Stanislav had pride, he had power, and he had strength. But also, Karpov realized that without Russia, what would Ivan Stanislav have that could seemingly keep his spiraling mind in check? Thunder rolled outside, barely audible through the soundproof window…..
