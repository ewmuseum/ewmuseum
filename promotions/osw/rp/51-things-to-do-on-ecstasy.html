51 Things to do on Ecstacy...

2002-04-23

Amy 'Aimz' Campbell

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Note: I started writing this for a drug-related site a while back...this morning I decided to finish it, and it turned into another part in the 'Cool' chronicles...hehe. All of this stuff is doable IF you use E...they're actually really interesting experiences every once in a while...though I don't recommend frequent use of any drug. **Cough**...neither does Aimz;) ) Ecstacy, above any other drug, creates the greatest experience/feeling you'll ever have. I'm sitting here in this cheap-ass little hotel room, Monday morning after a lovely little trip on Sunday night... Tripping's the most fun you'll ever have (Unless you're sleeping with Rent-A-Hero...which you had BETTER not be!) and I'm here to guide you through the fun times you can have! I might be a wrestler, but I'm also a 19 year-old girl raised in a culture where we've gotta fucking party! So here you go, youth of the nations...this is yet another installment of my writings to help make YOU feel cool. First, get yourself some Ecstacy...it's not that hard to get a hold of, just ask around. Then, follow this list next time you get fucked up... Sweet-ass stuff to do on E! : 1. Get four glowsticks and tape each one on the arms of a ceiling fan....turn it on, lie back and enjoy the show. 2. Water gun fight! 3. Eat a strawberry really slowly and get all the juices out of it...its incredible. Either that, or you could find your nearest super-hero and...............actually, stick to strawberries for the sake of my sanity. 4. While standing, have someone with a metal slinky put the last ring about half way back in your mouth and bite down slightly on it. Look down and close your eyes. Have the person very gently tap the slinky at intervals, and you CAN'T hear the sound but you can mentally feel or hear the sound of the slinky. It sounds like a light-sabre in an echo tunnel all over in your head and body! 5. Sea Breeze...sit down on a couch or something have someone in front of you (on top of you, straddling, whatever) and hold open your eyes and blow in through a tube like a ballpoint pen or something (or just pucker your lips tight) and blow on your face and in your eyes. 6. Fall THROUGH The Floor.......lie face down on the floor with your arms straight out over your head. Have someone stand over you (straddling) and grab you by the wrists. Close your eyes and have the person lift you up by your wrists so your back arches slightly and at the hips. Have them hold you with your wrists at maybe just above their waist level for 30 seconds to a minute, then VERY slowly let you back down. Make sure you have your eyes closed the whole time and your body is relaxed (your head/neck). By the time you're about half way down you will be expecting your face to touch the floor... but it won't! You will feel SO weird when you finally reach the ground. 7. Wash your hair or have someone else wash it for you. 8. Flip your head over and brush your hair upside down. 9. Take your top off and rub your back all over rough carpet, not too hard or the next morning it'll hurt! 10. Have people get fat (not permanent) markers and draw all over your tummy and back...unless you're as ticklish as I am. 11. And finally, bust out dancing like nobody can see you. 12. If you have a black light, go to a toy store and get a neon rainbow colored slinky. (If you don't have a black light, get one, silly!) Then lay on your back, close one eye, hold on end of the slinky over your eye (the open one) and have a friend hold the other end. Have your friend shake the slinky, move it up and down, etc. This is also cool to do to music (Preferably creepy modern opera shit.). Then switch and give your friend a show. If you're alone (Which you're not, nobody could enough to trip on E wouldn't have a friend to roll with!), you can stand and look down the slinky. 13. Run through the snow. 14. Go sleighriding...horses freak me out. 15. Have a snowball fight. 16. Run into the bathroom, stand in the shower and try whispering to your self quietly, 'This is so cool!'...your voice will echo throughout the bathroom so it really IS cool! 17. Lie down on the floor and have a girl or guy with long hair over you and dangle their hair in your face, swooshing it side to side and up and down. This must be done gently but it feels so good. 18. Have someone put their head down and then open your mouth real wide and breathe real slow on the back of their neck. 19. Cloth on the face - take different types of material, velvet, fleece, a terry bath towel, basically anything you can find in the house, a rave shirt. Have someone hold the cloth firmly by the side and then press the cloth into the back of your neck... then slowly bring the cloth forward traveling up the neck and the back of the head, all the way over the forehead and then down across the face. 20. Play-Doh, soften it up and it's wonderful (But don't eat it!) 21. Roll naked in grass or leaves. 22. Lay on ground and have friend pour non-cooked rice then beans on your back with no shirt on... then go take a shower and it's funky shit. 23. Use a feather duster on others back (A new duster, none of us wanna get all dusty.). 24. Try walking/running through wet grass. 25. Skipping - this has the same effect as running or jogging but the hopping feeling of skipping is intensified and you feel like you're hopping twice as high. 26. Ice pops - a fat free dessert so you can eat more than one and the cold sweet icy taste is unbelievable when you're rollin' hard. 27. Have someone drive you to a grocery store or toy store. Walk up and down the aisles. You'll find your own little toys/devices that look cool to play with. Don't bring too much money, you'll end up spending it all... 28. Speaking of toys to play with, silly putty. Its not only cool to feel with your hands, but you can stick it to a face that is printed in a newspaper and it will imprint...then stretch the silly putty and watch the face stretch and misproportion...it's funky as hell. 29. Go with your car through a car-wash lean back and enjoy! (but get a non-rolling friend to drive you.) 30. Popping candy 'Fizz Wizz' or 'Pop Rocks' etc, that pops when it gets wet on your tongue, wet your mouth then drop it onto your tongue. 31. Have a bath. 32. Play with any water (If a bath is not an option.). 33. Food - any sour food will enhance the feeling, like lemon. Also spicy food like horse radish inflame your tongue and give you the best feeling. 34. Ice-cube kisses - kiss your lover or friend with an ice cube in your mouth...if he's a super-hero, it really freaks him out...hehe. 35. Run! Believe it or not, if you're in fairly good shape it feels good to run! You go faster and farther and when you stop, after no matter how long, the rush is SO intense! 36. Foot and hand rubs are divine to give and get, especially if you use scented oil and squeeze or pull on the fingertips and/or toes. 37. Wrap yourself in new fleece, it's the most soft comforting wonderful feeling! 38. Get a glow stick and sit in complete darkness, then move the glow stick VERY FAST in front of your face, it's unbelievable! 39. Watch a fire. 40. Walk barefoot in sand. 41. Try sticking your head out the window while riding in a car (Look out for oncoming objects, though!) 42. Play on the swings at a deserted playground at 2am. 43. Rollerblade or go bikeriding. This is amazing because of the exercise. 44. Ride a roller coaster. 45. Wear something soft like velvet... 46. Have someone sit on the ground with their legs drawn in and their arms around their legs. Then approach them from behind and give them a completely encompassing hug, the feeling, again is indescribable. 47. Vick's cream feels very good while you're on E. Just put some below your nose for a fun experience. 48. Ring pops are kick-ass, because candy tastes and feels so good on your lips. For fun try this: place the ring pop in one side of somebody else's mouth and then kiss the other side of their mouth. This feels great! 49. Back rubs are wonderful also because each time you're touched it feels as if the person's fingertips are radiating warmth into your body. Hand rubs rule too! 50. Hopefully if you're at a rave there will be a giant fan there. Raves are usually very hot, and usually they have a big fan to keep everybody cool. For the best feeling, have somebody put cold water in their hands and smear it on your face and chest and then stand directly in front of the fan. BEST THING EVERY TO HANDLE WHILE ON E: Kittens! If you're gentle enough, they will be too...soft, fuzzy little animals are kick-ass to be around...they're soooo quirky! Happy trips!
