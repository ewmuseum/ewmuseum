Who has the guts to stop The Russian Bear? No one!

11 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-11-02 “Who Has The Guts To Stop The Russian Bear?” That is something that I am thinking to myself at this very moment. Who has the guts to stop The Russian Bear, Ivan Sergeiovich Stanislav of Russian Federation’s great army?! Many men have passed through my mind, for I know that there are many men from the past that would love nothing more than to exact revenge against Ivan Stanislav for showing them the truth to the matter: Communism will triumph over all other forms of creed and eventually become the most powerful force in the OSW and the PTC. They thought that they could get the best of my communist movement, Jane Reagan and that idiot Commissioner, Ed. A man that names a chair is given some form of power within the Old School Wrestling Federation? What are they thinking? Word has flown around PTC like wildfire about who this individual may be! Frisco, Kremmen, Meanstreak, and many others have been considered to be the ones that are to get in my way…. But what does Ivan have to worry about? Ed, trying to recruit someone to help him in his cause to do what? Silence Ivan Stanislav? No man has been able to do such a thing through my entire tenure in the PCW, and it will not happen here in OSW, I can assure you of that! If it is Frisco, bring him to fight Ivan! If he wishes to get in my way again, let him and he will once again feel my pure strength! If it is Kremmen, he again will be forced to leave due to the unstoppable onslaught of The Russian Bear. If it is Meanstreak, I will send him out of federation a battered and bruised failure! I woke up this morning and read the front of “Pravda” and what did I see? “Praporschik Ivan Stanislav hero to Russian people!” That is one of the few things that can bring smiles to Ivan’s face. The people are behind their Russian hero and they know it! I come to OSW under banner of peace! A peace that these people can only see if they realize that Ivan ultimately does this for them, and no one else! Ed thinks he will be some mighty crusader to stop The Red Army’s advance, HA! I hear those funny children like Frankie Bones and Jesse Ramey badmouth me and they think that will get my attention and enable them to fight me, but those men are nothing more than fodder! They are the civilians in this great war for the independence of the OSW! I have some things to handle today, and then I will figure out who this mystery man is, I will know who he is and expose him to world before Saturday SLAM! What can Ivan say about this mystery opponent? Simple, he is a man that is hiding. He is afraid to stand up and be forward to “The Russian Bear.” And that is understandable. Men before him have tried to trick Ivan, tried to deceive Ivan, and ultimately they have been eliminated. Meanstreak, Doug Troy, Ed, the list goes on and on as to those that could not stand up to Ivan Stanislav, and this man, whoever he is, will ultimately pay the price as well. He believes that this cover will protect him? Nyet, it will only serve to enrage The Russian Bear more! “Who Has The Guts To Stop The Russian Bear?” Many men may have the guts, but they do not have the ability. Let them come by the waves, let the entire OSW roster come down to fight my glorious Red Army….it is all for naught.
