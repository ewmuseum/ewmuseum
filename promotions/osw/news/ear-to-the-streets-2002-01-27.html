OSW — Column

Ear to the Streets

27 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
By Marcus Murphy First off, I'd like to start off my premiere column to the OSW audience as saying, I'm Marcus Murphy, OSW's man on, and of the streets. I will interview some of the up-coming but mostly stationary talent throughout the PTC and see what they think about the obvious best new federation in the PTC, as voted by themselves, the OSW. This edition features views and opinions from Duzza, D-Kay Calyx and even Moose Malone, head of OSW security. Read and enjoy. Who cares about OSW? The answer? Everybody. After interviewing wrestlers from that of PCW, the recently formed NTICW, and the infamous Primetime Wrestling Academy, the general consensus of critics and supporters of the OSW, is that with unknown hands at the reigns of it, the Old School Wrestling federation has a lot of added hype to live up to in the coming weeks. But who really should care about the OSW, besides the wrestlers themselves? "Everybody should care bout OSW, from the bottom feedin peeps in PWA, to the top folk in the PCW," said GWF superstar, Duzza. "OSW should be their main competition soon. To not look at what they comin wit, would be bout as stupid as asking for a price check at the Dolla Sto'. It just don't make no sense." Well, I don't know what's so wrong with asking for price checks at the Dollar Store, but I've got good reason to believe that not keeping up with upcoming action in the OSW, wouldn't make much sense at all. As a matter of fact some others put it on their priority list to be aware of what goes on within the OSW on a daily bases. The most likely of which, OSW head of security, Moose Malone commented on what he does, everyday within the OSW to protect the quality and integrity of the OSW wrestlers, though most are completely unaware of his subtle existence. Malone, a former wrestler himself, loves his job and the sport. "Oh yeah, I'm solely responsible for the security around these parts," said Moose Malone. "We kee it nice and tight so that all the gut stomping, fist pounding, internal bleeding action, can be seen by millions of people all over the globe." And trust me folks, gut stomping, fist pounding, AND internal bleeding..will all eventually lead to a better world. While everyone expects to be completely entertained by the action in the Old School Wrestling federation, who do they expect to be entertained the most by? PCW Rising Star Title contender D-Kay Calyx had some thoughts. "I would say either Ritchie Syxx, Frankie Bones or Corg," said Calyx. "All three of them are former NTICW'ers who had the skills there to improve. Now, in OSW, they have the time to work as well, and should be set for a great future. I think Frankie will get to the top, as a matter of fact I hope to God he does, so that I can knock him off his perch at the interfed PPV's" Well, D-Kay, all hope for Ritchie Syxx and Corg may be a lost cause, being as though they're going head to head on the OSW's debut card Slam! February 2nd in a No Disqualification, Falls Count Anywhere Match-up. This is bound to be a deserving main event, that will compliment the many matches we'll see prior to that. Drew Hostyle versus 'Black Ice' Matt Blaze, Civil Disobedience versus two mystery opponents to be announced on the show, SLR versus Sebastian 'Switchblade' Toomes, Great Dragon versus Sho-lin, and 'Classic' Clay Vendetta versus Scott Naket and many more answers and brand new questions are bound to fill the night to capacity with surprises and much more. The most highly publicized of which, of course being the identification of the OSW's owner. All spectulation has tried to be kept far away from the reaching hands of the public, but I, Marcus Murphy however, am giving you the goods on what the most believable prediction is, with help from...D-Kay again. My question to him was, Who do you think is the owner of the OSW? And how will knowing affect the overall product? Negatively or positively? "I think the owner will be decided after the PCW PPV Shang-Haied, with the loser of the Presidency match taking it," predicted Calyx. "It'll be positive if it's Duke, as he will let it flow, but if Tyler takes it, OSW is in for a hard ride" I never thought of that D-Kay...do you know somethings that everyone else doesn't? "Not really, Marcus. But I can't see OSW having an owner who hasn't proved his worth. If it's not one of the above, then we may see a shock entrant. But I would put my bets on Tyler or Duke." Hmm..well having Tyler Nelson as a President wouldn't be too hard on us, D-Kay. That is unless you're naturally a loser, and I think you've managed to find that aspect of your personality quite well, without his help..dumb reject. My next question for D-Kay *since he's the biggest attention hog, I've ever known*, was, 'Do you think most people would look forward to finding out who is the owner of the OSW or who will win the actual matches themselves?' "I guess, I should say the matches," said Calyx. "I don't think anyone would just want to see the unveiling of an owner on a show, heh heh. The matches. Definately. See how PCW's competition fares up." After D-Kay finished laughing at his own dry humor, I asked the next question Well..you're opinion is different from the response I've been getting lately. Fact is, most people care more about surprises and former PCW stars and all that stuff. How would you suggest we overcome this hype, with good old..old school wrestling? Any tips for the wrestlers? or do you think they've already done a good job of it? Former PCW stars are obviosuly going to liven things up, but Old School Wrestling is meant to be that. Wrestling. The Old School style. Taking things back to how it was. Sure, new arrivals will get people talking, but to keep people fully interested, the matches have to be above par, so that when the roster's full, people still tune in. The wreslters themselves get one piece of advice from me, advice that I follow myself. Stick by it. Put on a good show, and you'll be rewarded. Wrestle to impress people. We can all see what, that advice has gotten you, D Kay...no wonder you're such a loser! Here's my advice for OSW wrestlers, stick by it, put on a good show, and wrestle to impress people. I've just about had it now everybody, so thanks for reading and don't forget to check in for my next edition of Ear to the Streets, when I'll be gettin opinion of each individual OSW superstar, who's entered the federation and those who have yet to. Old School Warriors or Old School Wimps? I'll be asking YOU soon.
