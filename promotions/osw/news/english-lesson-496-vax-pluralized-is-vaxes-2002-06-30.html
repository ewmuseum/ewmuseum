English Lesson #496: Vax Pluralized is Vaxes

30 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dallas, Texas- The Vax promised OSW Commissioner, "The Scholar" Howard Porter and former Commissioner Ed that if the pair could defeat the robotic tag team of Twisted Metal, they would receive 'more information' to help get the pair back on the trail of the man who has plagued OSW since its inception. To no one's surprise, the pair managed to defeat the robotic duo, and got their information, which is sure to shock the entire world of wrestling Also on Slam, Corg took his crimes against Amy Campbell to a new level, a new OSW superstar was revealed, who was certainly no stranger to fans of fWo's Survivor II competion, Nick Casey and Propaganda's bad blood came to a boil and Scott Taylor wasted little time in making his presence felt with one of the so-called "guys with stroke" in OSW.
