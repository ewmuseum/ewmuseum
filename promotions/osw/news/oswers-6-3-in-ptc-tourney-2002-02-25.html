OSWers 6-3 in PTC Tourney

25 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OSW started the PTC Global Title Tournament with 10 workers, and that field has been cut down nearly by half as OSW wrestlers went 6-3 with one no-contest in the first round. Winning their matches via walkover were #6 Drew Hostyle, #7 Clay Vendetta, and #10 Hessian. Corg defended his #1 seed with a convincing 5-0 victory over the PWA's Jake Jones, and #3 seed Sebastian "Switchblade" Toomes eked out a 3-2 victory over stiff competition in CCW's Danny Bryson. And in one of the biggest upsets of the young tournament, #15 seed Seth Masters of Civil Disobediance beat PCW star and #2 seed Xavier Kannon to advance. Losing their matches were Ritchie Syxx, dropping his to PCW's Bobby Stormbringer; Tom Walczak, who was edged out by XCWF's Shawn Boyd; and Jesse Ramey, who was taken out by ProWC's Steve Studd. The only OSW no-show of the first round was Mike Marvelous, who lost his #1 seed in a no-contest and will be replaced by Wilde Tanke in a play-in. In the next round, Seth Masters will try to extend his upset streak against BMW's Reality; Drew Hostyle will face D. Black, a play-in from SCCW; Corg will look to continue his top-seeded run against free agent Lance Lethal; Hessian will face a tough battle in BMW's #2 seed, Kid Krazie; and in the match that will probably most interest fans of Old School Wrestling, it'll be the Classic versus the Switchblade as Clay Vendetta and Sebastian Toomes will take each other on. Check Primetime Central next week to see how the results went down! Source: Primetime Central
