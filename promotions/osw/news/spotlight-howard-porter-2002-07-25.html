Spotlight: Howard Porter

25 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanda: Hello OSW fans! I'm Shanda Russell with another Wrestler Spotlight! With me today is Commissioner of the OSW and member of the Think Tank: "The Scholar" Howard Porter. Thank you for your time, Commish. Howard: The pleasure is not only mine Ms. Russell, but yours, and everyone's, as well, since I've decided to enlighten the fans of Old School Wrestling with a few of my choice words. Shanda: Yes, of course Mr. Commissioner. Now, I have to ask you this question. You came into the OSW a few months ago as the saving grace against Jane Reagan, so to speak, but now you're nothing but a tyrant. How can you do such a thing?? Howard: If you're going to appear so simple-minded Ms. Russell, then I'll reiterate that is was a ploy all along. The greatest ploy in the history of professional wrestling I might add. I used everyone's distaste for Jane against them, to bring myself and my fellow Tankers in, where we could ultimately conquer Old School Wrestling. But tyrant is such a harsh word Ms. Russell, I really don't think of myself as anything of the sort. Shanda: What would you call it then? Howard: I think if one word has to be picked to accurately describe my hold on OSW, it would have to be....deity. Shanda: Um..okay...moving right along. So you tricked the entire world and brought in a group of geniuses. We all know what happened to Ed. After all the trust we put into you to right the wrongs in the OSW, you ultimately cost the life of one of the most beloved OSW wrestlers. How could you do such a thing? Howard: It wasn't incredibly difficult. I never saw Ed's life amounting to much anyway. It was probably more of a blessing then anything. Shanda: That is absolutely horrible. Howard: The world is a cold and unforgiving place my dear. To put the theories of Charles Darwin in terms the half-wits reading this interview can understand: Hunt or be hunted. We prefer to hunt. Shanda: Right. Moving right along. Sebastian Toomes seemed to have a problem with you, but you preferred to have Armour do your dirty work. Any reason why? Afraid, maybe? Howard: Afraid? Hardly. I'm the Commissioner of the most popular wrestling promotion in the business, not to mention coordinator of the top stable in professional wrestling. I'm busy making OSW a success, so I haven't time to revel in piddly matters such as the likes of scurmishes with Sebastian Toomes. Armour does a splendid job of keeping me clear of such meaningless tasks and squabbles. Shanda: So, the Think Tank is in the OSW, what do you think the future holds for such a group? Howard: Mental prowess, above anything else, is the key to success. We have the three most intellectually poweful individuals in the business, all on agreeable terms, working as a unit. Scary, no? But I digress, brute strength can also be an important weapon. But that is what Trashcan Man, Armour, and John Mammoth are for. Brains and brawn my dear, we have both covered. No one will be able to stop us, not the Elite, not Ivan Stanislav, no one. We will lord over OSW like fog in London. Shanda: I want you to know that if you didn't control my job, I'd say some things to you right now. Howard: Unfortunately I do, don't I? Until you've had the experiences I have had, you can keep your comments to yourself. Shanda: Yes..well...do you have any final words for the OSW fans? The fans that you betrayed. Howard: The fans? The fans are lemmings. Simply going with what is spoon-fed to them. My Think Tank compatriots and I are here to do things our way. The fans are the lowest common denominator. Of course they aren't going to agree with our actions, they simply don't understand them, because we are on a higher level. But you know what? They'll read this, and they'll still come back for more. They are gluttons for punishment, and they will keep giving me their money for MY show, because they are simply what I stated, lemmings.
