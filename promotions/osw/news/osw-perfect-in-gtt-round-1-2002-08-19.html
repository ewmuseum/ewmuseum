OSW Perfect in GTT Round 1

19 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OSW superstars went a perfect 5 for 5 in the first round of EW24-7's Golden Turnbuckle Tournament this weekend. Advancing out of the first round were: Scott Taylor, Clay Vendetta, Frankie Bones, Tempest, and Corg. Round 2 matchups are as follows: Frankie Bones has the undisputed easiest matchup in the next round... he doesn't have one. He'll be taking the week off after being granted a bye into Round 3. Scott Taylor will be taking on Twister Thompson, a free agent formerly of the WFWA. Tempest will be looking to dispatch Bryan Statis, representing WIW. Corg should face a heated battle when he goes up against another member of the WT community, Chris Matthews from RUSH Wrestling. Clay Vendetta draws The Overweighter, a free agent... but should he advance, Vendetta's most anticipated battle of the tournament awaits him in the next round where he'll be facing either RUSH's Dante Jackson... ...or OSW's own Hessian! Hessian was granted a Round 2 play-in and will be facing Jackson in what promises to be a great match-up. Could the bitter rivalry between The Cult and The Throwbacks extend into the GTT? We'll find out this week!
