WARNING: Scenes May Contain Some Graphic Violence

5 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Montreal, Quebec, Canada- Former CWL and Asylum superstar, Graphic Violence, claimed that his appearance at last Sunday night's Head On Collision Pay Per View would be his only appearance ever on Old School Wrestling Television. Apparently, Graphic Violence, forget to add the words "for next six days," to his statement as the man they call GV showed up and brutally attacked a prominant OSW superstar on Slam without explaination. Also on Slam!, Ed introduced the world to OSW's new Acting CEO who wasted little time in putting his fingerprint on the surface of Old School Wrestling, Andrew "Ace" Hawk called out OSW World Heavyweight Champion, Sebastian Toomes, but found someone else answered his call, Reno Davis explained his actions at Head On Collision, and last, but not least, Ivan Stanislav looked like his "old self," and went on a rampage around the building.
