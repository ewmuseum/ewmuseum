Eclipse to Coincide w/Slam

26 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Reports are starting to trickle in that an unexpected event will be taking place next Saturday. A full blown lunar eclipse will be occuring, which coicidentally it appears that Easton, Pa. will be the location which experiences the longest period of darkness. Police suggest if you are travelling to or from the Easton area, excercise extreme caution as there is no telling what the darkness may bring out. "Everytime there is a lunar Eclipse in these parts it brings out the looneys," said Easton Police Chief, Barry Holmes, who is the brother of former undisputed heavyweight boxing champion, and Easton resident, Larry Holmes. "Its bad enough that a side show like OSW is going to be here ruining the integrity of our arena." When asked about the impact on the integrity of the town's arena his brother's 'Over fifty and feelin' fine' matchup against fellow 50+ year old boxer Gerry Cooney on February 15th, Holmes proceeded cite the reporter for jaywalking and littering. Stay tuned as we will keep you up to date on information as it flows in.
