Spotlight: Amy Campbell

20 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings OSW! Shanda Russell here with another Wrestler Spotlight! Today I have the beautiful and talented Amy Campbell in the spotlight! Shanda: Hello OSW! I'm here with Amy Campbell, better known as Aimz to those outside of the PTC! Aimz is a recent edition to the OSW and has already made quite the shockwave. How are you Aimz? Aimz: Great, actually. Shanda: So Aimz, first off, one of the biggest questions I've been hearing: Why the OSW? Aimz: Unlike PCW, it wasn't filled to the brim with people who'd been around for ages...OSW's newer, so you don't get as much of the backstage seniority kinda thing. Besides, right now it just seems like PCW's a slow-sinking ship...a few of their better men jumped here and the company's beginning to majorly feel those effects. Shanda: Interesting....another question. What exactly is your relationship with Timo Bolamba? Aimz: You have NO idea how many people have been asking me about that! Heh...we're friends, he's a few years older than me...so it's just a flirty kind of brother to sister thing going on, nothing more for the time being. Shanda: I see...and the relationship between you two and Jane Reagan and Switchblade? Thoughts? Aimz: I'm guessing you've figured out that there won't exactly ever be a friendship between two parties there. Reagan and Switchblade...I honestly think they're just together for the benefits, you know what I mean? He's the champ, she's the owner...it's a power to power sort of relationship, I doubt it's very loving. I mean, look at the man, he's COMPLETELY whipped. Some World Champion...he can fight a man in the ring, but he doesn't dare to even argue with a woman half his size outside. Shanda: That's a very good point, Amy. Now, being that you are only other woman in the OSW besides Tempest, do you have any feelings towards her? I noticed slight rumblings of taunts between you two. Aimz: She's competition, that's how I see it. I honestly don't think that two women can peacefully co-exist in the same environment...that's why sisters argue, why wives hate mother-in-laws. We're overly territorial beings by nature, plus...well...I'm just not the friendliest person ever, admittedly. Though I'm pretty sure the competitive environment here has something to do with it...we all wanna be up there. Shanda: That's understandable. Now, another pressing question, you mentioned competition. Some of the competition in the OSW comes in the form of Ivan Stanislav, or Vampir Nosferatu. How do you expect to stand up against an opponent such as that, or do you plan on avoiding them? Aimz: I don't avoid anyone...I'll admit that I'm not stupid enough to go TRYING to cause problems with anybody like Stanislav or Vampir, but I'm not gonna run away if a situation comes my way. By nature, I'm just like that...I don't really see size as a HUGE factor, though I'd probably get beaten into a pulp in the ring with either of them...but I don't fear it. Shanda: That's a very confident statement, Amy. So, what are your future goals in the OSW? Aimz: I seem to be in a decent spot now. If I could stay in the main event, and maybe even win some sort of title, I'd be satisfied for the most part...but my main goal anywhere is to be the best. I know it sounds cheesy, but I work my ass off anywhere, in anything, if it means I can be at the top. I actually own a t-shirt that says "If I can't win, I'm not playing." Shanda: Fascinating. Well Aimz, thank you for your time and attention. Any final thoughts to the roster of the OSW, or anyone in particular? Aimz: Not really...I wouldn't suggest Jane Reagan continuing this little trip she's on, in case we cross paths again...but we all know she's not listening to anything a 'lowly wrestler' like me says, so no...no further comments. Well folks, you heard it here. Amy Campbell has come here for one major reason: Competition. And it is this journalists opinion that she came to the right place. However, what does the future hold for Aimz? Time can only tell. Dabbling in the affairs with the boss is not a good start. Tune in to Saturday Slam to see all the action!!
