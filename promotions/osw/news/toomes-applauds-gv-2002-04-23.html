Toomes Applauds GV

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OSW World Heavyweight Champion, Sebastian Toomes, recently voiced his support for Graphic Violence's stance against women in the wrestling ring. "He was 100% right when he said women do not belong in the ring," said Toomes as he was leaving his Indianapolis hotel on his way to visit still comatose OSW owner Jane Reagan in the hospital. "Women in wrestler are wastes of paychecks." Graphic Violence, who recently said that women should not be in a wrestling ring in response to a challenge made by Queen of Extreme competitor, Lillith, is not formally under contract with OSW. The former CWL and Asylum star will, however, appear at this Sunday's Head on Collision Pay Per View, against a still unknown opponent. Toomes has sparked controversy in the past with his anti-female comments, suggesting that all fellow male wrestlers should "smack a wrestling ho" to help save the indusrty. The National Organization of Women has condemned Graphic Violence's comments, calling him an "insecure neanderthal." We will bring you more on this story as news develops.
