Walczak in Bar Brawl

25 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tom Walczak was recently seen at a bar drinking for the first time in a while. He has been on somewhat of a downward spiral since losing the World Championship Belt to Rhett Locke. However things took a turn for the worse when Walczak failed to regain the belt during the most recent edition of SLAM! When approached by a reporter at the bar Walczak declined to speak with the reporter, and then when the reporter continued his questions things got ugly. We asked the bar owner what happened. "Yeah...ummmm...the big guy stood up right, and then he ummm... cracked the small guy in the head," said Mo Morgan of Mo's Tavern. "The next thing I see is the small guy on the floor. His face was all bloody and shit. Then the big guy spit on him." Reportedly the man had to be given immediate medical attention, and over 30 stitches were placed on the man's forehead. The man could not be reached for comment, but chances are charges will be filed. If the reports are true, this would be the second time in three months Walczak was involved in a barroom brawl. More on the story as it develops.
