KW/Taylor Brawl

12 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The battles between Scott Taylor and Kid Wonder during their OSW tenures have been weel documented. However, on Sunday, the two added a new chapter to their hot rivalry. Sunday afternoon, at a news conference at which they'd sign the contract relinquishing control of their respective manager in the event of a loss, a fight broke out. "The kid kept talking," said Taylor's manager, Randolph. "My friend was only protecting himself when he punched the boy." Amanda, however, gave a different account of what happened. "It was going smoothly," said Wonder's girlfriend. "Everything was going as planned. Then, for no reason, that bastard just sucker punched him. Nothing was said or anything. He just hit him. Like a little bitch, Taylor hit him." "I didn't get the best view of the thing," said OSW spokeswoman, Shanda Russell, who was at the event. "From what I saw, things were going fine. Then, all of a sudden...BAM! The two were fighting on the ground. What set them off is beyond me." The fight in itself lasted all of about 45 seconds before being broken up by security at the scene. Both men suffered some cuts and bruises, but neither of them were seriously injured in the scrap. "I'm sure Ms. Reagan or Mr. Porter will have something to say about the melee," added Russell. Whether any disciplinary action will be taken against the two hasn't been determined yet and both men will go on in their match on SLAM! and at the Pay-Per-View as scheduled. We will keep you updated on the situation as more information becomes available.
