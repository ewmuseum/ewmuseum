Ivan Warns Switchblade

18 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OSW superstar, The Russian Bear, Ivan Stanislav, has not taken to kindly to OSW World Heavyweight Champion Sebastian Toomes' constant trashtalking since winning at Crowning Glory. "Switchblade, is a stupid fool," said Stanislav in a recent interview, after hearing several comments made by Switchblade earlier in the day in regard to no one, including the Russian Bear himself, could stop him. "If he wishes to speak of Ivan Stanislav, he must understand that you are just asking for Stanislav to crush him and his little capitalist whore into the mat." Stanislav was hardly finished there. "Switchbladem, I can assure that when my Red Army bears down on you and that wench, we will see who is smiling," growled Stanislav. "The only true star in OSW is the star of Soviet Flag, and that is truly beautiful thing. Your World Title means nothing to me, for when I get it, I will make it into something that all can be proud of, for not only marking the rise of Communist State in OSW, but also the fall of Switchblade and his piece of American Trash, Jane Reagan." Switchblade could not be reached for comment, with Jane Reagan claminging his 'mouth was too full' to make a statement at that time. We will bring you more on this story as it develops.
