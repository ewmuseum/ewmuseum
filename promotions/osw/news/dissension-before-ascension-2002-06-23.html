Dissension Before Ascension

23 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Los Angeles, California- Less than 24 hours before the biggest event in the history of Old School Wrestling, the rifts within the Red Army continued to widen Saturday night, with on of the group seemingly turning his back on the unit's communist tenants. Experts wonder however if they are witnessing the end of the most dominant stable in OSW history, or is this all just another well placed charade put into motion by former KGB official, Alexei Ruslan, to cover up a more devious plot by Stanislav and Co. Also on Slam, two mean earned spots on the second tier of Mt. St. Hell's End at Ascension via victories in the ring, while another found himself named to the fourth and final second tier spot by OSW Commissioner, "The Scholar" Howrd Porter, Jim Axtell had a friendly chat with his buddy Kory Storm and Graphic Violence and Rhett Locke's feud got all that much more viscious.
