Spotlight: SLR

30 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I recently had the opportunity to sit down with OSW superstar, SLR, during which, I learned why people in OSW management have high hopes for the guy. Below is a transcript of the interview. Shanda Russell: Hello OSW fans! I'm Shanda Russell here with OSW wrestler, SLR. SLR, it's a pleasure to meet you!! SLR: Likewise. Shanda Russell: Thank you! First thing's first! What exactly does SLR stand for? SLR: Shanda, let me say this...I've been asked that question a million times and I never tell. You'll have to wait for the tell all autobiography. Shanda Russell: Understandable. Now, people have called you the "Smoking Man," how old were you when you had your first cigarette? SLR: I've been smoking since I was about 12 or 13. Hey Shanda, is this all the more cutting edge your questions are going to be? Cuz if so, you might want to go back to journalism school. Shanda Russell: Well uh..I'm just trying to get some more background information on you! Ehem! It's been said that at 5'10" tall and weighing only 227 lbs, as well as smoking CONSTANTLY, some believe that while your heart may be in wrestling, you're just not physically built and trained to handle wrestling anymore. What are your thoughts about that? SLR: Well, actually, my heart belongs to you but as for whether or not I can 'handle' wrestling, I've always been a guy who feels that actions speak louder than words and come Saturday, let all the doubters tune in and see if I can 'handle' it. Shanda Russell: Speaking of Saturday, your first match is against Sebastian 'Switchblade' Toomes. What is your strategy to defeat him, and what is your overall attitude towards the first ever card of the OSW? SLR: It wouldn't be much of a strategy if announced it, would it? But it doesn't matter because I use the same strategy every time I wrestle, I go balls to the wall, I put it all on the line and I know that no matter what, I gave you all I got. As for the debut OSW show, I'm pumped, it'll be great to be in front of the crowd again. Shanda Russell: How long has it been since you've last wrestled? SLR: Shit, I dunno, close to a year I suppose. Shanda Russell: Do you think that "ring rust" will be a factor? SLR: I honestly don't think so. I've been working out constantly during my time off, plus, when I'm in the ring, with thousands of fans in the arena, I'm transformed into a wrestling machine and I just don't think 'ring rust' is in my vocab. Shanda Russell: Any thought about the news that Jane Reagan is the President of the OSW? And your thoughts on the incoming "PCW Heavy Hitters" that she has acquired? SLR: Not really. Shanda Russell: Well SLR, thank you very much for your time. Any last comments for the OSW fans? SLR: I won't make lofty goals or predictions, what I will do is make each OSW fan a promise. When you see a SLR match, you're going to see all I have to give, if that's enough to win, great, if not, too bad but one thing is for sure, I'll leave it all in the ring. So there you have it. SLR is bound an determined to rise to the top, and if his past accomplishments are any indication of his ability to do so, he will make it.
