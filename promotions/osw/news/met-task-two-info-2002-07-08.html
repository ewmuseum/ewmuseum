MET Task Two Info

8 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OSW officials announced this week's task and its a test of each team's professionalism and experience, are to completely script a match between themselves, and OSW tag team champions, Da Westsidaz. "Back in the old days, wrestlers had to maticulously script out their own matches, and their ability to do so effectively and tell a compelling story in the ring was what bookers of the day looked for," said OSW spokesperson Shanda Russell. "In OSW, we expect our wrestlers to be able to book and work their own matches with a high level of professionalism." OSW officials expect the teams to each produce a script for their match against Da Westsidaz. OSW officials will pick the best five scripts, with first place taking 10 points, second place seven, third place five, fourth place three and finally, fifth place will receive a single point. Teams failing to submit a script will be eliminated. Specific details of the task can be found on the Events section of the MET site.
