Four 'Queens' Introduced

20 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OSW Officials introduced four more participants for the company's upcoming "Queen of Extreme" competition late Tuesday night, adding Faith Paris, Bethany Yamato, 'Primetime' Laura Wilson and Lillith to the list of competitiors vying for an OSW contract. "We are confident that any one of these young ladies could win the competition," said OSW spokesperson, Shanda Russell. "We expect all the identities of the participants will be made public by Saturday evening. Right now its just a matter of signing waivers and getting medical clearance for the final ladies." Paris, 27, of Virginia Beach, is perhaps best known for being the wife of FATE worker, Mediocre Mark. "I want our children to grow up knowing that atleast one successful professional wrestler is in the family," quipped Paris upon being introduced. "Honestly, I am just looking forward to competing and showing what I can do." Yamato, 25, of Los Angeles, stands an astonishing 6'4, but is still a graceful competitor. Some industry insiders have immediately questioned her addition to the roster, due to her criminal past and alleged ties to the Japanese mafia. "I have survived prison brawls against some of the toughest women on the face of the planet," said Yamato. "This should be a piece of cake." Wilson, 22, of Sydney, Australia, is a veteran of the sport, having worked in SCW and DiWF in the past, holding titles in both promotions. She is best known for her high flying, risk taking style, and has reportedly worked against OSW superstar Amy Campbell in the past. "I didn't come all the way from Australia, so I could bring the ticks from the back of koalas for studies at university," said Wilson. "I came here to win!" Lillith is somewhat of an enigma, having an extensive background in judo. Through years of ridicule and social abuse during her school days for being different, Lillith has built an unfeeling and uncaring attitude towards her foes. She had nothing to say at the press briefing in regard to included in the Queen of Extreme competition. We will bring you more on this Queen of Extreme tourney as it becomes available. Source: OSW's Queen of Extreme Home Info Page
