Kid Wonder Gets "Real"

23 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OSW superstar Kid Wonder was seen in Los Angeles late Wednesday night with a man. That man: Former Real World: Seattle cast member Stephen Williams. When asked about why he's with Stephen, Wonder replied, "I just wated to get the feeling. You know...the feeling of hitting a woman and feeling good aout yourself afterward. After all, I'll be beating Bethany Yamato enseless next Saturday en route to my OSW Cruiserweight title." Stephen, who is infamous for slapping a woman while filming his season of The Real World, refused to comment on whatever alliance he may have with "The Quintessential Teen Idol". Both men made no comment in regards to any training they might do together. Wonder, however, appears more than ready for his match with Bethany Yamato next week on SLAM! We'll keep you updated if anything else breaks involving this story.
