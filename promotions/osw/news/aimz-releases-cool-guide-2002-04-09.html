Aimz Releases "Cool Guide"

9 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OSW superstar, Amy Campbell, recently released a guide on "How to be Cool" to several online wrestling sheets, which have raised quite a few eyebrows as well as concerns among parents. "I am not sure if some hussy in a thong, who like to talk about the size of her interacial lover's penis is in a position to be talking about what's cool," said Mona Dorfenbacher, of the Christian Ladies International Tribunal. "She should be ashamed of her behavior, but even if she is not, we here at CLIT are ashamed for her." Parents have also raised concerns. OSW officials said they are reviewing the guide and will comment later in the week. Below is a guide, as published by Campbell. The All-Aimz Guide To Becoming Cool: Lesson 1: Veganomics Be a vegan. My World Book dictionary (copyright 1972) describes a vegan as: veg*an, n., adj. British. -n. a strict vegetarian: The true vegan excludes all animal protein from his diet, and he may even forego articles of clothing and household equipment of animal origin (New Scientist). -adj. strictly vegetarian: It is difficult for a vegan diet not to result in deficiencies (J.G. Sutherland). [veg(etari)an] I don't have the will-power to be vegan...but you do, because you need to be cool...but don't become a vegan because you care about animal rights and the preservation of wildlife. Be a vegan to be cool. Lesson 2: Being 'Straight-Edge' Become 'straight-edge'. The dictionary doesn't define this one either, but I'll sum it up for all of you kids who need this. Being straight-edge means not using any form of street drugs, being a non-drinker of alcoholic beverages, not smoking cigarettes and/or cigars, and not having sexual intercourse. As you know, I'm the farthest you could possibly get from straight-edge...but I'm already cool, you aren't. Again, do NOT become straight-edge if you don't want to lengthen your life. Become straight-edge to be cool. Of course, if you are straight-edge, you might as well wipe all meaning away from it by drawing a big 'X' on your hand. This shows other punky/emo cool kids you mean business. Now that we're done with the hard stuff, time to move onto the TRULY cool stuff...fashion. Lesson 3: Baggy Pants Men, women, children...this is where you MUST listen, or you are instantly un-cool... Find some baggy pants. Any baggy pants will do, but carpenter pants (I'm sure you've seen carpenter pants before...they've got the loops on the side so that carpenters can hold hammers there and stuff.) work the best. Now to test how baggy your pants are, look down at your feet. Can you see shoes? If you can, they're not baggy enough. For added coolosity, wear your keys, safety pins, sewing accessories, chains, patches etcetera outside or your pants. I wear baggy pants, you should too. Lesson 4: Dad's Closet This is for you wannabe-emo boys! Further reading is also acceptable for semi-punks... Go into your dad's closet, or into any 'Mens' department at your local mall, and find some button down shirts. These shirts must look like any 40-55 year old would wear while doing yard work. Next, find some sweaters...the fuzzier the better. Your dad's closet and/or dresser drawer is bound to be full of these things On your way to the vests. You might want to look in the closet again for these, as they are usually ironed so that dad will look good at the office. Put it on and take it to a sweaty concert. For guys and girls, look for a REALLY LONG BELT. If your dad's fat, you're more than likely to find this accessory. Sweater-vests are a good find as well. If none of these things float your boat, get some really tight t-shirts. Anything that looks somewhat cool, take 'em, and wear these garments to complete your emo/punk/cool look. Lesson 5: Backpacks! Grab your backpack. Take all the unimportant things out...these things include pencils, school books, term papers, calculators, or anything that's taking up valuable space in there. Keep things a cool kid like yourself might need. For example: Vegan food Extra patches in case the ones on your bag fall off CDs Extra hair dye Beaded necklaces Make sure you have all the popular punk and/or emo or metal band's patches sewn onto your pack. You want to make sure everyone knows how much you love these bands so they will think you're cool. Lesson 6: Acting Cool in Everyday Life These next two are very important lessons, because this is when all of the cool kids will be judging you. Act uninterested in everything. Nothing in the world is exciting enough for you. If you walk into someone's living room and there's an elephant watching TV with their three year-old daughter, act like it doesn't excite you. Just yawn, and ask for some rice cakes or beans to munch on. Be really WHINEY about it too. Nothing is cooler than being whiney...whine a lot, and you'll be on your way to acting cool. Lesson 7: Acting Cool/Punk at Punk Concerts Once completing lessons 1 through 5, make your way to the punk show. It doesn't matter how you get there, because any method of transportation is cool exept for mopeds, bicycles, roller-skates (roller blades are perfectly acceptable.), and limos. If you want to be TOTALLY cool, show up in a late-1980's van with all your favorite stickers on the back. Now, the band comes on. You stand up front where you can see the band, and the band can see you. Do one of the following: either cross your arms, or put your hands in your pockets. The only time you're allowed to move your hands is when you clap at the end of a song. Bob your head along with the music, but remember lesson 6...ACT UNINTERESTED. Even if this is your favorite band in the world, you MUST NOT sing along, mosh, bang your head or even cheer after songs. Just applaud. Lesson 8: Music Try to listen to mainly emo/punk/metal music. Acceptable bands are Metallica (early years for full effect.), Boy Hits Car, NOFX, Avail, Bob Marley and The Wailers etcetera. Do all of these things and you'll be well on your way to becoming cool.
