Canadian PM Speaks Out

6 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With OSW Crowning Glory only five short days away, Canadian Prime Minister Jean Chretien has issued a statement on the three-way dance pitting Scott Naket against Mike Marvelous against "Classic" Clay Vendetta. "Though we disagree with the pitting of two Canadian superstars against each other, we are confident that Naket and Marvelous will put aside their differences in order to gain a victory for Canada against the reprehensible behavior of Clay Vendetta. Today the government passed an act condeming Vendetta for his disrespectful comments towards Canada and especially the egregious disrespect shown towards the Canadian flag two weeks ago on Slam. Our border patrols have been alerted that Mr. Vendetta is to be barred access into the country. Best of luck to Mike Marvelous and Scott Naket; we know that they will represent the country proudly." When informed of the statement, Vendetta was quick to reply: "Ha! John Cretin can stick a maple leaf up his ass for all I care. Why the hell would I want to voluntarily enter that shithole of a country anyway?" Canadian sports network TSN also released a statement that they may air Crowning Glory up North in light of recent events. "I dunno," a spokesman said. "It'd have to pre-empt curling, eh, and I'm not sure that wouldn't 'cause an outcry."
