Ramey Prognosis Good

30 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This weekends recent happenings with the Vax and the horiffic tragedy that feel upon OSW superstar, Jesse Ramey, was a very dramatic event. One good thing has come out of the occurance though, OSW reporters were caught talking with Jesse's doctors and this is what one of them had to say. "I've never seen anything like it!" exclaimed Dr. David Sakauisenkoff. "I was expecting with what happened to Jesse for him to be out at least six months if not more, but his determination is higher than anyone I've ever seen before." Dr. Sakuisenkoff went on to give a favorable report as to Ramey's likely return date. "I'd give him about three to four months tops now," said Sakuisenkoff. "They guy is so damn resiliant, he is already out of bed and trying to walk as much as he possible can. The accident he went through could have very easily taken his life. But, either way, I've give him a few months tops before he is cleared to actually wrestle again." In the hospital, Jesse was seen trying to get out of bed and passed word through the president of his fan club, Cooter McCraw. "Jesse told me 'Don't be expecting me not to be seen on OSW TV in my time off,'" claimed McCraw. "He'll be making his return to TV sooner than his in-ring return. Jesse'll not go down with an injury and just be forgotten. That's just not his style." McCraw concluded his statement by shilling his "101 Ways to Cook Possum" cookbook, now available in several major book chains. By all accounts, expect to see Jesse back on OSW TV sometime around late July to early August.
