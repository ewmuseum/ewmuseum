Hawk/Seabass Brawl Update

7 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After the backstage scuffle between Andrew Hawk and Sebastian Toomes, that saw Andrew Hawk being kicked out of the building, Ace was seen storming out of the arena, two weeks ago, and was not seen back for a week. People started wondering if he'd come back for his scheduled match, but he did, and was last seen training hard on his personal gym in downtown LA. "He looks incredibly focused and ready for the match, but I'm worried about him," said one WT Analyst. "I wonder if he can be smart enough to put his emotions in check and not screw up. Toomes is a master of getting DQ wins, and he could use Ace's emotions against him." That was prompty battled by Ace's manager, Dick Strang. "Ace is more than ready, and his emotions are strong," said Strang. "They drive him and they should be the reason he'll win this match. Toomes may be smart, but he doesn't have Ace's heart, and that should be the difference in this match. The last time I saw Ace this focused, he won the CCW Indy Title, and I truly believe he's getting better and better every day. He's reaching a top form, and this match could be a great preparation for Ascention, so I'm waiting this match dearly." With this confidence, one has to wonder whether Dick is truly crazy or a genius. This Saturday, we'll start to find that out. Where is Ace? Is he among OSW's true greats, or is he among the Jesse Ramey'ss of life? This Saturday, we start to find that out.
