Stars Finding Religion

9 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Old School Wrestling has its share of religious superstars, most notably OSW International Champion Propaganda, and the Satan-worshipping OSW Tag Team Champions, Heresian Bloodlust. With the success of Propaganda and Heresian Bloodlust, it seems more OSW superstars are finding religion, in an attempt to get a competitive edge in the ring, as Doug Troy recently appeared on television evangelist Benny Hinn's program, while Queen of Extreme winner, Lillith, was recently spotted have an indepth discussion with a Roman Catholic priest inside a church. "Its not uncommon that professional athletes too look to the power of a god or the supernatural in an attempt to gain a foot up on the competition," said noted psychologist Elliot Foanhoam. "Faith is a powerful thing." Both Doug Troy and Lillith apparently found little satisfaction in their forays into religion however, as Troy reportedly attacked Hinn in front of live audience, while Lillith reportedly spent less than five minutes in the Catholic church. "Benny Hinn is a fraud," said Doug Troy, as he quickly pushed his way through reporters in Toronto. "I gave him $50,000 to exorcise the mediocrity from the OSW Heavyweight belt, and not even he, the supposed hand of God could do it!" Lillith's visit to the church was apparently equally disatifying. "Let's just say I am happy I am not an 11 year old boy," said Lillith, before threatening to kill reporters who were dogging her with questions regarding her visit to the house of God. We of course will bring you an new news on these stories, such as a report from the priest visited by Lillith or the likely impending legal action against Doug Troy, as information becomes available.
