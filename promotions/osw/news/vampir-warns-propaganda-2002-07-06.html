Vampir Warns Propaganda

6 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OSW superstar, Vampir Nosferatu, may be partnering with fellow Cult member Hessian to take on 'Old School' Dave Gibson and Clay Vendetta tomorrow night on Slam, but his mind is still on OSW International Champion, Propaganda. "I will eventually tend to my unfinished business, and yes Propaganda I speak of you," growled Vampir Nosferatu, seemingly forgetting he was not talking to Propaganda, but a reporter "Have you enjoyed yourself these last few cycles in my absence? Climbing up the proverbial ladder as you and your little lackey have your way with these inferior souls. Please by all means, dismiss my words as trite as you sit back in your pew while you get satisfaction from your altar boys. Say to yourself, ‘ as the lord god-ah is my witness-ah, I have already defeated this man before-ah, he poses no threat-ah, to me or my career-ah. Can I ah get an amen from the congregation-ah…" Vampir Nosferatu was not finished mocking and warning his long time enemy. "Propaganda, you are a spiritual and god fearing man are you not? A connoisseur of theology and the bible, yes? Well allow me to put this in a manner that maybe your intellect can comprehend for you much easier. Let us say, shall we, that we put some symbolism into the equation, you know, to allow the hamster to get off his fat arse and spin the wheel within your head. I shall wait a second as your crank-start your synapses so that they may fire properly for I shall only say this once. Picture yourself as the city of Jericho, hooting and hollering as you stand behind your impregnable wall. Saying to yourself, there is no way you can get to us, the laws of war are to our advantage, we are at an elevated position. Now imagine that I am the Israelites, and if you can recall the story, the Israelites for seven days circled around the walls until the seventh day when they played their musical instruments as loud as they could, thus causing that great shield for Jericho to come crumbling down. Ultimately, this event led to the Israelites to slaughter the citizens of Jericho. One battle does not constitute the war my friend.” Propaganda had no comment, and has not been seen in public since suffering an electrocution at the hands of an on-the-fritz toaster. We will bring you more on this story, as it develops.
