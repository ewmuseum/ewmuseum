OSW — Column

Down on the Farm

29 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi OSW fans! It's me, Nate Dumas, the voice of OSW, and here is a scouting report on the talent currently working in OSW's developmental territory, the Xtreme Wrestling Alliance. OSW officials recently signed former IOW World Champion, Kory Storm, who has returned to the ring after a multitude of injuries and personal problems. No one can question Storm's in ring talent, but its been his ability to focus in the ring and stay clear of negative outside influences that have damaged his once promising career. If Kory's head is on right, OSW officials expect him to make an major impact in OSW...once he proves himself in XWA. The massive Livewire is an in ring veteran, who has been in and out of the PCW system for quite some time, but has never gotten a shot at the big show. Now in XWA, he is showing his potential as a main eventer, and it could just be a matter of time before he finally gets his shot to shine on a national stage. 'Mad' Maxx Crimson is another monster of a competitor with an incredible heart. XWA officials say he is a 'student' of the game with a great work ethic and desire to learn. Though he is still a little green, it may not be long before he finds his way onto OSW television. Ex-actor, Sonny Ward, is slick on the microphone and improving rapidly in the ring. OSW is full of top notch bad guys right now, so Ward will likely have to wait a bit longer to get his shot. Warpath is a guy we are very high on. We believe he has just scratched the surface of his massive phyiscal ability and has yet to tap into his overwhelming charisma. Once he is more comfortable on the stick and harnesses his great power and agility, he will not only find his way up to OSW, but straight to main event status. The sky is the limit for Warpath. The Refugees anchor a weak tag team division in XWA, and with a bit of hard work and perfection on their gimmick, these two Afghani grapplers could find themselves working against the likes of Civil Disobedience and Heresian Bloodlust. Jonathan Drachen is one of the hardest working men in XWA, and it doesn't hurt that he works as part of a tag team with Cege Hayabusa. It will just be a matter of time before Drachen's hard work pays off and he finds his way to the big show. Drachen's tag team partner, Cege Hayabusa is also a hard worker, who like Drachen, is not at all hurt by the fact he can work as a singles or tag team competitor. Also like Drachen, Hayabusa is a talented lightweight competitor, which works in his favor. Former FSW worker, 'Five Star' Leon Chambers is a multi-talented lightweight worker, who has been to the big show before. More experienced then the rest of XWA, Chambers could be the first one from XWA 'heading back to Old School' once the need arises. Joe Bananas is a talented worker, who gets over well with the fans. With a name like Bananas, he's almost a can't miss talent. Like the unpasturized milk he delivers, Steve "The Milkman" Carnage is still a raw work in progress. The potential is there, but he still needs time to put together a package for himself, and to gain some experience, before he can grace the squared circle on Slam! Finally, Christopher Shackleton maybe from the land of ice and snow, but he is a red hot prospect that we at OSW have our eyes on. Look for big things from this 230 pounder in the days to come. Nate's Top Five Prospects 1. 'Five Star' Leon Chambers 2. Kory Storm 3. Maxx Crimson 4. Warpath 5. Jonathan Drachen Closing Thoughts XWA is packed full of talented wrestlers, most of which could step up into OSW tomorrow and jump into a program without missing a beat. Look for another edition of Down on the Farm next week, where I will disect the talent of NTICW. Until then, see you on Slam! everybody!
