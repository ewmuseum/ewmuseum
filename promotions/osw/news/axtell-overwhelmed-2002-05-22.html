Axtell Overwhelmed

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After his shocking victory in an impromptu match against Kanaida, OSW security guard Jim Axtell has been getting bombarded with interview and various other requests. "I really don't know what to think of all this," said a dreamy-eyed Axtell. "Ever since last Saturday it's been pretty hectic for me. I'm not used to this kind of attention." Axtell went on to talk a bit about what happened on SLAM! and why he did what he did. "It was just kind of a spur of the moment thing," said Axtell as he stood outside an OSW security briefing room. "I couldn't let that guy talk about Moose like that. He has more important things to do than deal with punks like that. So I went out there and gave it 110 percent, and I was lucky enough to come out with a win. Believe me, I was as shocked as anyone." The wrestling world has been abuzz about this major upset since SLAM! aired last Saturday. What exactly the future holds for Jim Axtell remains to be seen.
