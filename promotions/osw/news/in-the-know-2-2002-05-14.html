In the Know #2

14 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
By Nate Dumas Hello everyone, and welcome to the second edition of In the Know. Without wasting a lot of time, let’s get right into guts of all the dirt from backstage and gossip from around the water cooler in the OSW home office, RIGHT HERE….RIGHT NOW!!! Injuries There is plenty to report on the injury front, as several OSW superstars are still on the mend. Epitome of Evil will be out another 3-6 weeks out after suffering a class three concussion and several broken ribs at Head On Collision. Jesse Ramey is recovering well from multiple injuries he suffered after being buried under a half a ton of bricks by the Vax at Head On Collision, but his return date is still slated for mid-July. No word on how long Meanstreak will be institutionalized or if he will ever return to the ring. It will be a sad day in the world of wrestling if he does not return. Commissioner Ed is still suffering from a back injury, which may require surgery. Do not expect him to return to the ring anytime soon. Vampir Nosferatu is still out rehabbing undisclosed injuries. We expect the Angel of Death to return in 6-8 weeks. Griff Truxell Looks like another former CWL, jolt and Asylum superstar is on his way to Old School Wrestling in the form of Griff Truxell. Internet reports are running wild about Truxell’s deal (potential deal?) with OSW, and the numbers and perks of the deal being thrown around are mind-boggling. I find it hard to believe anyone would be worth $5,000,000 USD per year, let alone an egomaniac with a spotty track record like Truxell. Should the rumors of Truxell’s multi-million dollar deal, limousines, private locker rooms, special travel arrangements and luxury suites be true, Truxell can expect a chilly reception at best from the rest of the OSW locker room. Smarks I have been reading that many of the so-called “smart marks” have been ripping the recent performance of OSW World Heavyweight Champion, Sebastian Toomes. What the smarks don’t seem to know is the multitude of injuries Toomes has worked through, which include a separated shoulder, a hip flexor, neck and back problems and a slightly torn bicept. Like him or hate him, he’s been a trooper, and will be a marquee guy in OSW for a long time to come. The UWA Brigade Though fans have been taking a wait and see attitude to the work former UWAers Jesus St. Crux, Jason Winters, Kanaida and Ian Wright have displayed thus far in Old School Wrestling, OSW officials are confident that all four of these guys will be over with the fans soon enough. One official told me behind the scenes the other day “we have big plans for all four of these guys, and once the fans get to see them on a week in and week out basis, they will understand why.” Rhett Locke For fans not already high on Rhett Locke, you should be. If you don’t like him taking center stage, get used to it, because OSW’s decision to have him feud with Graphic Violence has shown a commitment to this kid, and rightfully so. He is all heart and passion and in the end, that is what makes the difference between a talented in-ring performer, and a superstar. Tempest OSW officials have yet make any announcements regarding Tempest’s contract status, as it appears her break up with Ivan Stanislav has effecting the “Little Storm” almost as much as it has taken a toll on the Russian Bear himself. I have it on good authority, however, that OSW officials are depositing Tempest’s paychecks in an escrow account until the situation can be resolved. Ladies OSW is blessed to have talented women’s performaners, such as Tempest, Amy Campbell and Lillith. Add Bethany Yamato to that mix, assuming she can straighten out her act, and OSW will boast one of the most talented female rosters in the business today. Don’t hold your breath on seeing an OSW Women’s Title, though. Conventional thinking in the OSW front office is that if ladies want to get into the ring, then they need to compete on an equal playing field with the men, and I for one agree. The sky is the limit for the four ladies mentioned above. Ascension Wow! Every member of the OSW roster will battle for 60 minutes on a 40’ tall man-made mountain, forged from steel grating for a shot to become the #1 Contender for the OSW World Heavyweight title?!? Expect blood to be spilled, bones to be broken, dreams to be shattered and legends to be made during this sure to be brutal contest on Sunday. June 23rd, from the America West Arena in Phoenix Arizona. Well folks, that’s all from Calgary for this week. Be sure to tune into this week’s edition of Saturday Night Slam, where Doug Troy will take on Switchblade for the OSW World Heavyweight title!
