Kory Storm to OSW?

11 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Former IOW World Champion Kory Storm, who is known to many fans of XWA as 'Perfection Personified', has recently disappeared from the radars of the wrestling world. When Chris S. vacated ownership of XWA to PCW's Rush Mason, Storm was regarded as the top star there. He was Television Champion and many thought, because of his ability in the first round matches, that Kory Storm would go on to a 'Perfect' reign as XWA World Champion. However, this was all cut short when Mason debuted on Xtreme TV (XWA's weekly television show). He fired Kory Storm in a rage and the man who was the highlight wrestler of the XWA was now back to square one. But why? Many wrestling reporters speculate that Kory's firing was because of his on and off air fued with then XWA Vice President & currently injured OSW star Jesse Ramey. But others expect different reasoning behind the release of 'Perfection Personified' Kory Storm. Many fans of IOW and PCW know all about the darker side of Kory Storm. The side which is even more evil and sadistic then the side he shows in the ring. The addictive side. Kory Storm was released from IOW in a fury by his manager Lisa Tien after the front office discovered a mass quantity of pain killers in his locker room. It was common knowledge that Kory Storm had a harsh back injury, but no one had known that it was this bad. Forced to job to Kent Anthason at IOW's first PPV Sinister Purposes, Kory Storm left IOW a broken man. However, the phrase "the grass is always greener on the other side" proved true as 'Perfection Personified' met with the exiled PCW owner Phantom Booker. Exactly one hour later, Kory cut his first spot in PCW. The lack of talent that the addiction was causing got more and more obvious. Soon the injury as well as the addiction took its toll. Kory began to miss important PCW dates and was again released from a top North American federation. Finally realizing that he had a problem, Kory went to rehab and took care of his problems in four months, returning to the world of wrestling better than ever. He once again began to dominate as he used to and soon the rumors of his signing with OSW began to spread like wildfire over the internet. But where is Kory Storm now? When reached for comment, his agent Angela Burke informed the OSW Newswire that he was in a meeting with some officials at the moment. Immediately the Newswire sensed something and sent cameras out to San Diego where they missed the exit of Kory Storm from the resturant where he held a meeting, but saw the exit of current OSW CEO Howard Porter! What does this all mean for Kory Storm and OSW? We'll be sure to keep you posted!!
