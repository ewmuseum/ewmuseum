Aimz Hospitalized

5 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After Amy Campbell recieved what could easily be dubbed the beating of her life at the hands of a paranoid and dillusional Ivan Stansilav on Slam, the OSW flying diva was hospitalized in the Centre D'Hébergement St. Jude, in Montreal, Canada with what an OSW source termed as 'several minor injuries.' When asked to elaborate, Dr. Eric Trahan had very little to say regarding Aimz' condition. "The concussion doesn't appear serious, but we can't be sure of that yet," said Trahan. "Her nose isn't broken, which was a major concern as far as time she might miss, and she appears to have no broken bones." He was quick to add that "She shouldn't miss too much time, she's insiting to go back within one to weeks but we're not making any promises yet." Concerns of Aimz condition have not stopped her from being booked to face Reno Davis in the first round of the cruiserweight title tourney next Saturday night on Slam! Campbell was not available for comment. We will bring you more on Campbell's condition in the days to come.
