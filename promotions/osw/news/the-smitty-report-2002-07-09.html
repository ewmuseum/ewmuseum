OSW — Column

The Smitty Report

9 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello OSW fans, welcome to the Smitty report. Yes, yes it’s not an original idea and I’ll be the first to admit it’s a rip or “spoof” off the Ross Report, but what else would you expect from me? First let me address something that I would be a fool to ignore and that is the closing of fWo. I’ll be the first to say I never did follow fWo but it was one of the best out there for a long time and it’s a shame to see them go. I won’t go into them too much as this is an OSW column but I wouldn’t be surprised to see them back by the end of the summer. In the mean time I know that several fWo stars have been in talk with OSW personnel but with an active roster of 31 wrestlers and 3 tag teams, I don’t know how much room will be open for them. I’m sure we’ll see a few pop up through out the weeks however, but only one man knows for sure and he’s not writing this column. Speaking of popping up, where did this pay per view quality Slam come from? Ascension was one of the better pay per views I’ve had the privilege of watching from backstage. Sure there were a couple of lackluster, one sided match ups, but what do you expect when only one person is working? Ivan Stanislav gave Reno Davis everything he deserved inside Mt. St. Hell’s End. I’m not going to hold anything back in these columns and I do believe Reno Davis was sitting on his name for this feud and it’s too bad because you had a proven monster in Ivan against Reno Davis who has done a lot in his career already. But Ivan ended up carrying the feud and the final match and I for one am glad to see it come to an end and hope that Mr. Davis won’t sit back on his laurels and do something this time around. Then we had Griff Truxell and Clay Vendetta, how Truxell ever made the news top newsmaker list is beyond me, because he has been very non-existent in his stint here in Old School Wrestling. Vendetta however has turned the snubbing into a legitimate side angle and is building some good heat off of it, as well as putting himself in the spotlight. I for one think he can run with the ball but how long until it’s taken away from him? I won’t bother commentating on the UWA title match, I tried telling them it had no place on our shows but they rarely listen to me and they went through with it and it sucked. I hate to do it, but I told you so. Kory Storm vs Jim Axtell was arguably the worst match on the card, which is harsh considering it, was on a card with the UWA title match. Storm seemed reluctant to put Axtell over and it seems a few of the workers backstage may be thinking the same thing. Then I can’t really blame them, who wants to job to a security guard? Then again it’s a security guard with a fair chunk of talent and once he finds someone to work with him, will accomplish bigger things. I really have to bite my tongue when it comes to the World title match but the fact that neither of the men in it are in a main event scene anymore should say it all. Both vets that got lazy and it showed in their performance. It’s too bad because I have been a fan of both of them in the past but they need some time out of the spotlight and to help get some new talent over before they think about the main event scene in my opinion. Now that the bad is out, what can I say about the rest? The tag match was purely amazing and was perhaps the best match I’ve seen until this past week. Just truly brilliant work and all the men involved in it should be proud for the show they gave us. I’ve been questionable of the push that El Drago has received since arriving as a virtual nobody, but he has shown that although he doesn’t put the time in off camera, when the show starts he has what it takes. His match with Michel Krieg was no different as the two of them put on a solid match taking each other to the extreme, before Drago finally got the victory and thus projected him into a main event feud with Ivan Stanislav, or so we thought, but I’ll get on to that later on. The opening match of the pay per view turns into a potential World Title match down the line. It was a solid work with Rhett Locke showing he can take a licken’ and keep on ticken’. I look for great things from both these men as they get a shot to sign on the highest of levels in OSW. And then there was the beast, the monster the Ascension match itself. It was a very risky move by OSW officials and it cost them a few superstars to say the least. Unfortunately in this reporters eyes they seeded a very distasteful angle between Corg and Aimz. You likely noticed that I skipped their match but believe you me that was no mental error. I have no desire to have any thing to do with that angle and as long as it continues I will refuse to cover it. Word backstage is I’m not the only one pushing for this angle to be cut loose and toned down before some Parents committee starts to raise Hell and it becomes a bigger issue then it needs to be. Back to the match, I was shocked to see Rhett Locke win this match, not because he doesn’t deserve it, but that he was given the opportunity so quickly. Definitely a step in the right direction. All in all it was a great Pay Per View and the workers should be proud of being a part of it. Now as I was saying near the start we went from an excellent pay per view to a regular, maybe slightly sub par Slam, right into a Slam that could have given Ascension a run for its money. Despite the injuries that occurred on Slam there is a very positive feel backstage and so far there is little to report in backstage quarrels at this time. Injuries however, we aren’t so lucky as I have a nice list to run through. And these aren’t just low carders getting hurt, but guys that have been working near the top of the card. New World Champion Rhett Locke had to have water drained from his knee and by the looks of it he is going to need Arthroscopic surgery to repair damage that he has sustained to the knee and could miss up to a month and a half of action. There’s no word how this will affect the World Title scene but if he’s out past the pay per view, look for the title to be vacated for the first time in OSW history. Both Ivan and El Drago were sent to the hospital after their Hellacious battle inside and outside the steel Heel in a Cell on Slam. I think both men need to be cleared with the psychiatric ward before they should be released because despite the respect they showed for each other throughout the match, they nearly killed each other and themselves in the process. Ivan rebroke a left rib and suffered a mild concussion and should only miss this coming Slam before returning to action. El Drago on the other hand suffered a collapsed lung, which could keep him sidelined indefinitely. Throw in that his shoulder has considerable scar tissue and may need surgery to clean it out El Drago could miss up to a couple months of action worse case scenario. Amy Campbell is in the protective ward after her last run in with Corg and in my opinion should stay in there as long as she can hoping this angle will blow over, but deep down I have a feeling she won’t be out long, because Amy doesn’t know what’s best for herself. Sebastian Toomes is looking at around a month on the self with a severe concussion. With four of our top stars going down to injury it could leave OSW in a tight spot. Ivan needs to get back and get back soon. His disbanding of the Red Army has put him over big time with the fans that once loathed his every move and he needs to return after a much-deserved week off, even if it’s only in a cameo appearance but Ivan needs to be back on OSW television. Locke and Toomes will be missed if they disappear for a while and Locke especially needs to get back on the screen if he’s going to bring some dignity back to the belt that ironically Toomes held so well. El Drago on the other hand seems to be in no man’s land as it sits now. A potential feud with Ivan seems pointless now after the match they put on at Slam as anything short of one of them dying would be just a tarnish on the match last Saturday. El Drago needs to heel up and get that shoulder fixed up so that he can be an asset long term to the company. He’s being pushed awfully quickly and there has been some resentment backstage because of that. A healthy Drago is what OSW needs, in the mean time; OSW can get by with out him. As for other rumblings backstage it appears that a couple of former OSW stars will be appearing over the coming weeks as well as at least one former fWo star. A few OSW stars appear to be in heat with OSW management but it’s nothing worth wile to mention here. Where does this leave OSW right now? I can honestly say from what I’ve seen around that OSW has all the tools to compete and bid for a spot as one of the big three with 21w and tA. I’ve gone up and down the roster several times and there is one thing that is missing. Jobbers. Every fed needs jobbers, yet OSW has none. On any given Saturday, no match is a guarantee. I haven’t decided if this is a good thing or a bad thing yet but one thing can be said for sure, from top to bottom OSW is packed with very skillful workers and has proven to have compelling angles. Yes I knew who the Vax was before it happened but it still was compelling watching it unfold and I can only imagine what it must have been like to see it unfold to the blind, as one by one the Think Tank was reformed in OSW. In closing a little off topic I’m happy to say PCW signed DarkStar once again, thus saving me the pain of having to see him in OSW. Then again we are in need of a jobber, wonder if Kriegman could pull HANK~! Back from the dead? I’m Jim Smitty and that was the Smitty Report.
