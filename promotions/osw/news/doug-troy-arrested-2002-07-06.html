Doug Troy Arrested

6 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Former OSW World Heavyweight Champion, Doug Troy, was arrested late Friday evening, and charged with Public Drunkeness and Disorderly conduct, according to police in his hometown of Omaha, Nebraska. "We understand he was arrested and charged, but then released on his on recognisance," said OSW spokesperson, Shanda Russell. "We haven't been able to get a hold of Doug to find out what happened." DT has been rumored to be on a downward spiral, since assaulting a fan a week after losing the title to Tom Walczak at Ascension, reportedly going on drinking and womanizing binges in and around Omaha and New York City. Apparently the rumors were true, and one can only begin to wonder, if or even when, Doug Troy will snap out of his professional and personal tailspin. We will bring you more on this story as news develops.
