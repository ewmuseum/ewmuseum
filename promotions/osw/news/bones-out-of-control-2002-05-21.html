Bones Out of Control

21 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, it was only a matter of time according to OSW officials and it appears the time is now. Frankie Bones has lost it. Completely. "Mr. Bones is completely irrational and extremely dangerous," said an OSW official. "Every time he is approached, he goes on a violent rampage, destroying everything within his grasp." Frankie overheard Drew Hostyle speaking bad about him on the phone two weeks ago on Slam! Since then he has lost all control. His only quotable comments as of late have been mumbled obscenities. OSW officials say that Bones is under the microscope and actions could be taken in the near future, ranging from mandated anger counseling, to possible termination of his OSW contract.
