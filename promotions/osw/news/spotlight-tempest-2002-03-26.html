Spotlight: Tempest

26 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanda: Hello OSW! I'm Shanda Russell here with another 'Wrestler Spotlight!' With me today is the one and only Tempest! Tempest, thank you so much for giving me the opportunity to give you this interview! Tempest: Sure thing Shanda! I'm glad to finally get a chance to sit down and talk to all my new OSW fans. Shanda: So, how are you enjoying the OSW thus far? Tempest: Oh it's great! Well... ok the concussion wasn't fun. But I've had worse. *shrugs* And Jane Reagan's having turned out to be lying, sniveling, manipulative slut didn't help. But it's ok, cuz that just gives me something to focus on. Shanda: So, speaking of Jane Reagan, she has had some choice words regarding you on several occasions. Do you have anything personal to say to her? Or perhaps Sebastian Toomes? Tempest: *sighs* Ya know, slander is her game, not mine. But I just hope she understands that I feel she and Sebby should be locked in a closet with 100 hungry weasels... or maybe just dangled over the roof of the Sears Tower with nothing to land on but a grape. You get the idea. I would tell you how deeply I was hurt or much her friendship meant to me, but that's a waste of time considering she's made it plain how little it meant to her. Shanda: That is understandable. How about Amy Campbell, also known as Aimz. I noticed she has had various words directed at you too, thoughts? Comments? Tempest: Aimz is like... a day without waffles. I like Aimz believe it or not. She's got a lot of talent and promise. But I feel bad for her too, that she isn't a little more confident. She talks about being the best so much she's forgotten how to have fun! What use is this sport if it's not fun? And I'm not the one that felt the need to call her out, before I even got here. I think some's feeling a little threatened. Shanda: Interesting. Now, I have to say, recently things between yourself and Ivan Stanislav have gotten..more and more..personal. Care to talk about that? Tempest: *chuckles* Well... the more personal details are... personal. But yes, we're getting along remarkably well for the most mismatched pair of people on the planet. Ivan's really nice though, to me at least. S'funny how people hate him so much. I don't really understand why. Shanda: And what about his Red Army? Do you cash in on his communist beliefs? Tempest: Nope. At least I don't think so. I'm not really familiar with communism. I grew up in Hong Kong, and al I knew of communist China was that it was a place to be avoided. But now that I've met Communist Party member Wang I can't imagine why. I've never met anyone so non threatening, ineffective, you know, nothing to worry about. Shanda: So, speaking of Ivan exclusively, Tempest. What do you think of Ivan Stanislav the uh..ehem..person? Tempest: I like him. I mean I guess that's obvious by now. I think I'm fortunate though that I got to know him as a person before I really knew much about him as a wrestler. Or maybe he's fortunate that I had nothing to hold against him. Ivan's... very driven. I can't say I totally understand, but he's very attached to his country and it's people, so that's not such a bad thing. I wish I could say I ever had something like that to fight for. Shanda: Well, there are some that would say Ivan is obsessed, so perhaps it is a good thing you don't have a cause such as that to fight for. Now, Meanstreak inflicted a substantial amount of damage on you a few weeks ago, and you managed to get some revenge for that attack. Your thoughts on Meanstreak? Tempest: Ugh... I try not to think about him. He's like that nasty slobbering dog that no one in the neighborhood wants to go near. Although it was kinda fun to muzzle him so to speak. I was pretty lucky in that match, but I had to do it. I don't think you can let people think you're in fear of them. It's like giving away your strength to them. Shanda: So, is there anyone in particular that Tempest has her sights set on? Tempest: Hmm, well we could be here awhile with that one. I'd like to fight almost anyone. I'm not like someone who enjoys beating people up or whatever, it's just fun to me to get in the ring and see who can get the better of the other. Right now though... I'd have to say Jane. Although she gave me what I wanted in the match with Meanstreak, I know she was hoping to take me out. And I don't take kindly to that. She has a lot to learn about the ferocity of this little Tempest. Shanda: Any major future goals in the OSW, or are you just going with the flow? Tempest: Hmm, well I should probably think about a title of some sort eventually. They're fun, and shiny too! But for now, saving the place from it's creator is more important. Jane's sorta like Dr. Frankenstein ya know? Shanda: Yes, I think I understand. Well, once again thank you so much for your time Tempest, and good luck with Ivan and your future endeavors! Tempest: Thanks! I dunno which one will be more difficult. Toodles! Shanda: Well OSW! As you can see Tempest is focused, but also enjoying herself here in the OSW! As we have seen in the past by her performance, she’s a mix of tenacity and bubbly fun! This is Shanda Russell for the OSW.
