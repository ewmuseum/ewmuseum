Spotlight: Callisto Bane

17 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanda: Hello there OSW! I'm Shanda Russell here with one of the newest signees into the OSW, The Olden Beast, Callisto Bane for this edition of Wrestler Spotlight. Callisto, thank you for this time! Callisto: This time is limited, so make the best of it and get along with your petty questions if you please. Callisto: Hessian is recovering nicely..Or so I have heard. As for the status of the Cult and the status of myself in the Cult I'm not sure I can answer. As it seems things have been on the quiet side besides myself engaging in numerous acts for them against my Throwback related opponents. Shanda: Interesting.... now this Saturday Slam you have a match against Throwback Jim Axtell. Jim has shown us time and time again that he's been able to overcome opponents that appear to have a decided advantage over him, and has a never give up attitude. Are you prepared to handle the ex OSW security guard? Callisto: I am more than ready to make him suffer. He may have been an ex security guard but that pales in comparison to the many things I have been before Wrestling. I plan to give him a night he will not soon forget. Shanda: With that in mind, it has been noticed that the OSW's female division, as it would be said is slowly growing, with many capable women wrestlers trying to make a name for themselves. With that in mind, you brutally attacked Amy Campbell recently, what was the reasoning behind the assault? Callisto: The reasoning? My dear Shanda..It is not only going to end with Amy Campbell. You see this week on SLAM! Four women are going in to a match for the number one contendership for the Heavyweight Championship. One of these four women are in the Cult with myself and I plan on making absolutely sure Bethany walks out with the contendership for the title shot. So Amy was only the beginning..Most certainly not the last. Shanda: Well, it is apparent that Callisto Bane is here to say. Now, I must ask that at 6'6" tall and weighing in at 285 lbs. puts you far larger and heavier than most other females in the OSW and could very well put you into the league of the bigger men in the OSW. Most notably men like Ivan Stanislav, John Mammoth, and Riptah of Da Westsidaz. Would you prefer to go up against those individuals or continue to show your dominance in the female scene...and whichever you pick, what makes you think you could excel there? Callisto: I am willing to deal with whatever the high ups in the OSW have planned for me. I have patience,..And I am willing to wait but sooner or later people will realize that they cannot keep me pinned down. One way or another I will make my way up the ladder, rung by rung until no one can deny that the Olden Beast will dominate the OSW scene. I will deal with the women, I will go through each one by one, two by two… However they wish to fall. Then I will make my way on up, surpassing the mentioned men inside of the ring. And what makes me think I can? -Chuckles- Simply look at me… Shanda: Yes well, you surely are a physical...specimen, Callisto. So, moving on, you have come into the OSW and now that you are here, other than making a name for yourself and winning titles, what other major goals do you have in mind? A feud with someone in particular perhaps? Or something else? Callisto: I plan to deal with a certain former Heavyweight Champion… But not of OSW. Instead he comes from fWo… He would be the man who ruined my debut, and his name is Erik Kelly. I do plan to take care of him very soon. Other than the basics I also plan to use the money I make from OSW to find more about my past… Something that has eluded me for years. Shanda: Very interesting. Are you jealous that the four women got a shot at the contendership for the World Title, but you did not? Callisto: Jealous? -No more chuckling..This was a full out laugh.- Not in the least! While I do not doubt I would do well in the league for the Heavyweight title I do not wish to take that on at the moment. I have minor things to deal with, such as taking out Axtell and Erik and then of course..The ladies..-An evil smirk turns up the side of her mouth- Shanda: -swallows- Ehem, yes well I think that just about wraps up this edition of The Wrestler Spotlight. Thank you Callisto Bane again for the time given, and on that note do you have any final words for the OSW Fans, or a member of the OSW roster in particular? Callisto: A member yes..A certain woman. -She peers in to the lens of the camera- Amy Campbell, the next time we meet it won't be your face I will be licking.. Callisto: -She flickers her tongue as she stands and pushes the camera out of her way, leaving.-
