Spotlight: Frankie Bones

1 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello OSW! I’m Shanda Russell with another edition of Wrestler Spotlight! My guest today: Frankie Bones Shanda Russell: Hello OSW fans, once again it is I, Shanda Russell here with Frankie Bones of the OSW. Frankie, how are you and thank you for granting me some of your time! Frankie Bones: I'm good, and don't mention it, no biggy. Shanda Russell: So Frankie, I have heard that you have had to face various "demons" in your past that were created through violence. And now you wrestle in order to combat these demons, right? Frankie Bones: Yeah, it's the only legal way I can take out some aggression. And I enjoy seeing other people in the same amount of pain that I am constantly in. These are the only things that give me solice. Shanda Russell: But, don't you think that you are only promoting the violence that has caused you so much pain. Don't you think you would rather stop the damaging violence? Frankie Bones: In a perfect world, that would be the perfect answer. But I lived without violence for many years, and it just compounded my problems. Wrestling is not senseless violence. My opponents know what they are getting into. They hurt me, I hurt them, that's the way this business works, and it makes me feel better, that's about as philosophical as I get on the subject. Shanda Russell: I suppose I can understand that.I have noticed that you do not have a match for Saturday Slam! Will you be present backstage? Frankie Bones: You can rest assured, that if it is a OSW event, The Legend of Pain will be there. I have some things planned for Slam! I will make my impression felt at every card, whether I have a match or not, you can count on that. Shanda Russell: What are your thoughts regarding the news of Jane Reagan being the President of the OSW? Frankie Bones: Jane who? Oh yeah, I think I heard something about that. The staff sheet doesn't interest me much, it is the roster that has my attention. But I must say I like the woman. And I must say, I like anything that screws Tyler Nelson. As long as there are cards, as long as I am on most of them, and as long as they sign the check, I really don't care who's the President. Shanda Russell: With that in mind, regarding the roster, what are your thoughts regarding the supposed "PCW Heavy Hitters" on their way into the OSW? Frankie Bones: Bring 'em. The more the merrier. I'd love to see some big heads come in there. This isn't PCW, this place is old school, and we will give them an old school welcome. Everything starts over here, everyone is 0 and 0. The past is over, the future is out of our control, I'll deal with the present, on my terms. Shanda Russell: Anybody in the OSW that you've got your sights set on? Frankie Bones: Ahh, figured that was coming. Syxx and I have some old issues that need resolving. I'm sure Hostyle and I will have some home town rivalry, although I have other plans for the Hostyle one. But mainly, it is too early to single anyone out. I'm gonna wait and see how everything pans out. It will become all too apparent very quickly as to who is marked. Shanda Russell: Any long term goals in the OSW other than winning titles? Frankie Bones: I never concern myself with titles. Wrestlers who come into a federation and make empty promises of wins and titles are a dime a dozen. All I want to do is inflict pain, on anyone who gets in my way. I stay determined on the task at hand and the wins and belts will come on there own. My only goal is to leave my mark, and to leave nobody unaffected. Shanda Russell: Well, it sounds like you have a plan. Any final comments for the OSW fans and roster? Frankie Bones: Just to get ready. I didn't get this tattoo on my stomach for nothing. Shanda Russell: Well, thank you for your time Frankie. And good luck in the OSW Frankie Bones: Thanks.
