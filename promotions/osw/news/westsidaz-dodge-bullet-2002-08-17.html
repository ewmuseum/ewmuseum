Westsidaz Dodge Bullet

17 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Recently OSW Reporters caught up with Short Circuit in regard to the recent rumours of Da Westsidaz in hot water with Management. Instead of elaborating on the situation, SC, who was signing autographs at a Los Angeles-based Planet Hollywood, quickly changed the subject to the teams plan of changing there entrance music. When asked about being in hot water with OSW, Short Circuit had this to say... Short Circuit: Yeah, Riptah and I just weren't feeling "Ante Up". Although we're tight with M.O.P., after over a year of listening to it when coming out from the curtain, it gets tiring. So we went to Lasandra and he hooked us up with some phat tunes of N.E.R.D." When asked to comment about the rumors of him being part of the roster cut, Short Circuit signed a poster of himself and replied. Short Circuit: Well, "Rock Star" by N.E.R.D. has some sick lyrics. We thought that it fitted us just perfectly and would be a breath of fresh air. "Ante Up" was the PCW Westsidaz. When we return to action in a few weeks, we're coming back as OSW Westsidaz, and OSW asks for only the best in not only wrestlers, but Pop Culture as well. After realizing Short Circuit wasn't paying attention to any questions our staff had, we reluctantly left.
