Ear to the Streets Crowning Glory edition by Marcus Murphy This week's column is dedicated to all that is the Old School Wrestling's first ever PPV, airing Sunday, March 10th at 8:00 PM from Charlotte North Carolina, Crowning Glory. So heading into this historic occasion, I chose to dedicate this entire column to hyping up the PPV, giving my picks and letting you hear some of the picks of fellow PTC'ers that follow the OSW. Just for you, I'll also tell you some of the nitty gritty on each competitor and their mind sets going into their matches. This week's column will features opinions from that of PWA promising talent Jordan Crazy, PCW TV Title contender D-Kay Calyx, former PCW TV Champ Tim Walden, PCW up and comer Jay Sylo, OSW's own Frankie Bones, Hessian, 'Classic' Clay Vendetta, Timo Bolamba, and Seth Masters along with Trevor Hawke, collectively known as Civil Disobedience. Read and enjoy. Hello again loyal readers, this is of course Marcus Murphy. Old School Wrestling's #1 columnist and my number one column..is, in fact 'Ear to the Streets'. Now, if you forgot in this column I'll be getting opinions from everyone you could possibly imagine and their best friends, and they'll be giving their views on what's going on within the OSW right now. And currently the big buzz is OSW's debut PPV, Crowning Glory. As you know many people have different perspectives on just about everything, thanks to this little thing we like to call a mind, and while nobody bothers to use their minds towards insignificant things like Olympics (God knows that French judge should've kept her mouth shut), it's very important when giving predictions on various things. Now..am I promoting illegal gambling? Most definitely. Do I suggest that you put money on my picks, most definitely. And do I demand a portion of your total earnings? Damn right. Nonetheless, I'll give these picks for free..this time. Without further adieu let's start with the first match of the evening, 'Classic' Clay Vendetta versus Scott Naket versus Mike Marvelous. This match's stipulation is a simple three way dance. Here are 'Classic' Clay's thoughts on how the match will unfold. "Let's see." Clay began. "Two Canadians and a non-Canadian that isn't Boda. How much easier of a pick do you want? Vendetta in a sleepwalk and one hell of a boring match. Next?" Plain, simple, and to the point. However sure I am that Naket is used to three ways with him in favor, he'll have anything but the advantage in this match. Especially due to the antics of the boldest member of the whole feud, Clay Vendetta who's officially been banned from entering Canada. If all it takes is a little disrespect to the flag to get banned from that country, no wonder they're proud of having Scott Naket and Mike Marvelous as citizens.. 'The Legend of Pain' agreed with Vendetta taking the match. But for slightly different reasons.. "Uh, who vs. who vs. who?" Frankie Bones asked. "Anyway, I'll go with Vendetta here I guess, cause he's the only one of these three I don't want to literally kill." One of the few who conflicted with the previous viewpoints was Tim Walden, who said: "Well, I'm going to have to pick Naket on this one, simply because he's got a lot more experience in the ring in major feds, and you've gotta have a hell of a lot of guts to get almost killed and come back to full wrestling action 3 months later..That's the sort of guts that wins matches." Good point Timmy, but must I point out that a great majority of Scott Naket’s experience in the ring, was as apart of the..ah well let's not go into the history of that other place..it's doomed to hell anyways. Good luck there, buddy. Hessian also decided to pick a different pony, this smelly farm animal in the form of Mike Marvelous. "Who? I barely know these cretins, but if it must be so, then Mike Marvelous will defeat his adversaries...I believed to begin with Scott Naket was to be an Old School Champion, now he is going to have his Old School ass handed to him..." Also agreeing on both Canadian’s were Jordan Crazy, Timo Bolamba, and the man alleged to be in cahoots with Naket himself, Jesse Ramey. Jordan Crazy’s thoughts were: "Mike Marvelous 'Cause he's the better Canadian to rep us well." Those thoughts were of course coming from Jordan Crazy, the only the only black Canadian I know of.. "Well to be honest I think Naket will win it, due to helping Marvelous throughout the match then turning on him at the end." Jesse Ramey added. That may be so, but Naket’s turned on the majority of us a long time ago. This rumor between and you and him..it’s no big surprise. "I'll be honest, I don’t really know a lot about Clay Vendetta." OSW Title Contender Timo Bolamba said. "But I do know about Mike Marvelous and Scott Naket. I expect to see Naket win this by virtue of luck." But back to the majority of the decision, D-Kay Calyx's thoughts on Clay Vendetta's initial chances of winning were: Naket? Isn't he permanently injured yet? Oh well. Mike always used to pull something out for Pay Per Views, and Naket, my old PWA teacher, can never be underestimated..but Clay is the underdog, and should come out on top. Even ‘The Sylencer’ Jay Sylo had to agree with the majority of the rest of voters and go with ‘Classic’ Clay. "Vendetta, he's got skill I think he can win it." MY PREDICTION: This match has gotten a slow paced start being as it’s just being added to the Crowning Glory card as of March 4th, 2002. However, all men shouldn’t be counted out to putting on a memorable match. But to give credit where credit is due, Scott Naket’s been wrestling so far back that he probably cut promos with dinosaurs. Now, we‘ve confirmed that he‘s old, but not exactly old..school. ‘Classic’ Clay and Mike Marvelous aren’t new blood, but they have an upper hand for their reputations being in good condition so far. Marvelous with his icon-esque ways and Clay’s arrogant attitude seem to me as if they’ll make the bigger impact on this match up. Though Ramey’s probably going to interject, the only American in the match, ‘Classic’ Clay Vendetta is my pick to walk away with the win by pinning Marvelous. OSW Tag Team Title Match Heresian Bloodlust(Hessian and Azreal Cain) vs. Civil Disobedience(Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke) This match will very..colorful to say the least. All of the opinions I collected, though were very distinct and gave good reason. OSW’s tag team division may be very cut and dry, but the competition is as good as it would be as if it were full. Frankie Bones’ thoughts were: "This'll be a good match. I mean, Civil D is the crowd favorite, and they got skillz, although they both look a little queer. But then again Heresian Bloodlust are huge and powerful. I'm gonna say Bloodlust. After a long fought battle with Civil D pulling some stuff out of their asses, but ultimately falling short." I wouldn’t doubt the pulling things out of their asses deal, Seth Masters sure as hell did pull a victory from down under over Xavier Kannon in the Primetime Central Tournament. Going into this match, you’ve gotta think that these guys are going to keep a level head though, they’re not as obsessed as most would imagine them being. "Civil Disobedience is one pair of backwards pants away from being the Kris Kross of OSW. I liked "Jump", though, and they'll give a match against one of the most ridiculously named teams in wrestling. Should've named themselves "Ludicrously Self-Important". "Overrated" would be more compact, though. Civil D takes heavy abuse but finds a win nonetheless," as 'Classic' Clay Vendetta put it. Well I haven’t got the insurance to say that Heresian Bloodlust is a terribly dumb name..but I will say that the have huge size and weight advantages as Timo Bolamba goes onto explain: "Oh, this is a tough one to call from my point. I love the charisma shown by Civil Disobediance, but in the end, i dont think they have what it takes to make up for the differences in size and determination. My pick goes to Heresian Bloodlust." OSW’s Jesse Ramey and D-Kay Calyx also had similar thoughts. "I don't really like Heresian Bloodlust." Jesse stated. "I never have and never will. I want Civil D. to win, but they don't look big enough to take on a ant farm.." "This is an easy choice." D-Kay declared. "How can anyone pick against GOliaTH ? Hessian is huge...and can give a beating. Trust me, I was on the receiving end of it in NTICW. Heresian Bloodlust to walk out with the belts." One fourth of the men in this match, the Mercenary of Satan himself, commented on his chances of walking away with the straps. "Hmm......two of the most dominating forces in the OSW against two...suburban brats. This should be a hard-fought battle, the winners? Just wait and see what's in store..." Well..there’s one vote for the other side..any more? ; ) As a matter of fact Frankie Bones and Tim Walden mirrored each other’s statements. "Gotta go with Civil Disobedience, since those Bloodlust guys seem to be cursed with losing to them..." "Civil D. Since Hessian and Cain are stuck on this losing streak to them." Jordan Crazy, PWA student, however disagrees. "My pick is Heresian Bloodlust. Hess is currently my PWA trainer and I’ve gotten some major heat from the guys of Civil D. backstage." MY PREDICTION: This match will be the shocker of the evening. My stance is that Hessian and Cain will be all over Civil Disobedience like the last piece of turkey at Thanksgiving Dinner. But Civil Disobedience will snatch the victory from within their grasps. While being a fan favorite won’t be a factor, their craftiness and untapped fountain of wisdom should be useful. Look for Civil Disobedience to pull the upsets. #1 Contenders Match for the OSW International Title 'The Polish Punisher' Tom Walczak vs. 'Bad Attitude' Jesse Ramey When the chance to be named number 1 contender to the Champion to be named later on the same night, you’ve got to figure both men are going to come out firing. Predictions, however were very one sided for the person least likely... "Walczak." Frankie said, confidently. "Ramey is tough and crafty. But the big dumb Pollock is just gonna prove too powerful." "How many Pollocks does it take to beat a closeted transvestite?" Clay Vendetta questioned. Umm... "One, if he's a monster with the strength of Walczak. The only joke in this match is Ramey. Walczak wins in a squash." "Ramey is a good worker, and has a style to himself, but I think Tom Walczak, another early PWA graduate, will pull out the win. The man is underrated and is on his way to the top." Now I be confused as to what you consider.."style" D-Kay, but I’m glad whatever you describe it as, he keeps to himself. "Wow, this is going to be a great match" stated Jesse Ramey, of course commenting on his own match. "But only because I'm in it. A lot of people in OSW and outside of it think that I'll be mauled in this match, but I've got my ace up my sleeve.." Jesse thought, was the huge favorite, as Jordan Crazy, Timo Bolamba, and Tim Walden would attest. "Jesse Ramey is going to win it. Jesse is my dog." Jordan Crazy started. "Canadian's don't like Russians 'cause they talk shit about us in hockey. During the Olympics they did. Said the refs cheated for us. Jesse‘s not Canadian, but given the right chance, anyone could kick Huskies ass." Here were Tim Walden’s thoughts. "Well, having been in Thugs 4 Hire with that worthless wimp Walczak, and knowing it'd annoy you, I'd have to pick Ramey." Not only am I annoyed..but applauded at your stupidity. I can now, knowingly, say that every young woman out their should use birth control, not for her sake, but so I don’t ever come across more children like Tim Walden. ; ) "That wench Ivan Stanislav's cronie will have the upper hand here. A Pole fighting a transvestite won't have much hope...I see the International Title deserving of its name as Walczak will come to prove." It’s a stretch, but I think you might have the most reasonable response of the evening. Jesse Ramey will get this one, he is a great solid talent, and a friend of mine. If he can’t beat Walczak, there’s something wrong with the world. Well there’s plenty of things wrong with the world. But when you have an outlook that’s so, dare I say, "happy", then there’s not much else to it. MY PREDICTION: The Red Army vs. 3B clash seems inevitable. Tom Walczak is my pick to run a rampage on Jesse’s ass in the early going. But due to a Naket run in, which might very well happen, I could see this match going both ways and Jesse and Scott finding an excuse to hug each other at the match’s conclusion whether he wins or loses. Either way, Jesse’s happy. Tom Walczak to win it. Singles Match with the stip to be determined on Slam! Ritchie Syxx vs. Corg We’ve reached a general conclusion in the results I’ve gotten and that is that the stipulation will be a Hair vs. Hair match. However, the real stipulation will be announced on this week’s Saturday Night Slam! Tune in for details. I personally think both don’t have the head shape to go bald though. "I'm hoping desperately that the stipulation on this one is a “Haircut Match’" Classic Clay Vendetta commented. "'cause I'm worried that the FAA is going to start picking Syxx up on radar. When two freaks fight, everybody's a loser, especially the fans. I'll pick Corg to be the one to put a merciful end to this feud, if only because he looks like he's the guy who's more likely to go absolutely postal and bite off Syxx's face or something." Tim Walden was disgusted with the possibility of a Hair vs. Hair match up. As a matter of fact he didn’t like the idea of the match at all. Locke has helped me out a lot as of late." Jesse Ramey said. "But to tell you honestly. A person of his stature and religion will not stand a chance against the man they call Drew Hostyle..." "Rhett Locke, a.k.a. Corg II. He will put up a good fight, however Drew Hostyle may snatch the win here with “a lil’ help from his friends.” I have no idea what you mean by help, Drew’s about as ethical and righteous a man than any other superstar within this federation. Timo Bolamba’s thoughts were: "These guys both have the capability to put their names on the plate big time, and to be honest I really don’t care who wins because it will be a hell of a show just to watch it." But all in all, I think Jordan Crazy says it best when he agreed: "Drew Hostyle...for real." MY PREDICTIONS: This match should at best cut and dry. I suggested taking a bathroom break, then coming back and gettin’ your groove on to Drew’s theme music. I’d definitely look for 3B at ringside to watch. "Conventional wisdom seems to predict that Stanislav and Meanstreak will beat each other to bloody pulps, leaving Doug Troy an easy win, but what conventional wisdom fails to take into account is that I don't think Troy has the brainpower to tie his own shoes, let alone do something smart like wait in the corner for the other two to kill each other. I like Stanislav and Streak because they both scare the living piss out of me; but I think the revenge factor will put Meanstreak on top in this one." ‘Classic’ Clay agreed. "Stanislav and Streak will take each other out giving Troy the easy win." Jesse Ramey, said simply. "Oh my God! What a match to witness!" D-Kay exclaimed. "In my eyes, the Ivan vs. Streak feud has run it's course in PCW, and in OSW it seems just to have been revived. I'll say that Streak and Stanislav take each other out, and Doug Troy takes advantage for the first elimination, and catches an upset for the win." "Meanstreak and Ivan Stanislav are nothing. Meanstreak could not even defeat ME!" OSW’s tag team title contender, Hessian spat. "And Ivan Stanislav is an insecure, "limp noodle" who has to resort to petty intervening to secure his place on his prey...I would have finished Streak off and that Commie Basta..........I mean........Doug Troy to annihilate them both." "This is a match I can relate to, because I have wrestled with them before, well at least in the same fed." Timo slightly boasted. "I would also like to point out that in one way or another I hold two victories over Ivan Stanislav. No doubt the both of these men possess the fabric that make World Champion, though all the controversy surrounds Toomes, he’s my pick to go all the way. The Jane Reagan aspect of it will come into play, I’m not sure how, but it should come to a head at Crowning Glory. FINAL PREDICTIONS: If you plan on making it down to the event, hell, if you plan on watching it 8:00 on PPV, you better bring a hard hat. Because the roof is gonna collapse when all these matches finally take place. I’m predicting a huge night for all members of 3B with each man taking home their respective matches. Also, expect plenty of surprises and things that even I couldn’t have possibly fore warned you of. So until then, I’m Marcus Murphy. And I’ll so YOU at Crowning Glory.
