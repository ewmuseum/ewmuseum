MET News and Notes

24 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OSW owner Jane Reagan announced the 16 lucky teams that will battle it out in the first ever OSW Most Extreme Team competition, for a shot at an OSW contract. The competing teams are as follows. Death Certificate Conrad's Soldaten Adam & Eve Nu Era Uprising Total Koruption Vice Versa The Faculty Redemption Song American Ronin Guardians of the Ring The Black Dragons The Alliance of Violence All About the Benjamins Total Violation The first task of the competition will be announced on the June 29th telecast of Slam. To check out the official Most Extreme Team subsite, click the MET banner above!
