OSW — Column

Overflow #2

22 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ladies and gentlemen, today's installment of Overflow is a little different than the last one. Once a week, a special superstar will be hand selected by yours truly, and an interview will be conducted. This week's superstar was Sebastian "Switchblade" Toomes. After the interview I will go into a sort of recap of how things went, in my opinion, and whether or not that I feel this certain superstar has what it takes to succeed in OSW. Enjoy. Edward Saunders: Welcome to this interview, Mr. Toomes. I am a reporter for OSW, my name is Edward Saunders. How are you this evening? Sebastian "Switchblade" Toomes: I'm talented, unlike the rest of the wrestling world. that answer your question? ES: I guess it may clear some things up for me, but don't you find it a bit, well, how may I put this bluntly. That you may not be the most talented wrestler in OSW. ST: Well, that's a common misconception, and I'd like to take the time to clear I it up. See, OSW is filled with talent, no doubt about it, but if we were all the same, it'd be boring. So, obviously, I was signed to be the standard by which everyone else is judged. See, I am the most talented. I don't rely on make-up or masks or anything, I am technically perfect, good looking, and I have the resume. So to answer your question, don't be an idiot, I am the most talented person on the roster. ES: Ah, yes I can understand where you are coming from, but when you have men such as "Black Ice" and Drew Hostyle in the same federation as you it has to be intimidating. I've been around this business for a long time and trust me, when I say what I say. ST: Don't you find it odd that 'Black Ice' is white, and Drew Hostyle has man-breasts? Fighting a man who is supposed to be black and isn't, and a man with bitch-tits is intimidating. Anything else doesn't scare me at all. ES: A person doesn't get rich by make false acquisitions. I grant you that Mr. Toomes. You don't make it big by feeling intimidated either and you have cleared that one up for us all. What are your thoughts on the roster, though? ST: Well, Corg is a mistake waiting to happen, the guy simply can't accessorize. Sho-Lin and Great Dragon are going to be locked in matches together until the end of time. Civil Disobedience is cool, I dig them. Frankie Bones is black, and he's angry about it. Maverick Manson looks a hell of a lot like Vin Diesel, and apparently half the talent. Scott Naket was cool, but he kind of burnt himself out, turned into a bitch. Jesse Ramey...well, he's supposed to be dead *wink wink*, but he was interesting to say the least. Ritchie Syxx hasn't realized that punk is dead and so is his gimmick... go watch SLC Punk, dude! SLR looks like white trash and needs a beating, and Clay Vendetta...Black Void of Talent, and then there's that Switchblade guy. LOADS of talent, charisma... He's going to be a star someday. In fact, isn't he already the most charismatic star of OSW? Oh yeah, he is, isn't he? ES: Only if you like talking about yourself that way Mr. Toomes, but out of all of these men, which do you find is going to be the biggest challenge and be serious about this one. Which one do you feel that you know the most about and if you weren't a wrestler, which two would you pay to come and watch put on a show? ST: Hmmm....probably Naket and Great Dragon. Both guys got the resume, they got the skills, they got the talent. That and anything with Civil Disobedience, I love those guys. My biggest challenge... I honestly don't know yet? Probably Ramey. I actually never faced him in CCW never got the chance, really. ES: Speaking of Ramey, you do know that him being placed on the roster is rumored to be that of only a hoax and a ploy to throw people off. How do you feel about the situation? ST: Ramey has the habit of showing up in the oddest places. They never did find a body, so... Anyway, why would they bother? I don't see the logic in doing a hoax. OSW seems to have a straight record so far, why pull some crap with hoaxes and such? I'll believe it when I see the body. ES: Yes, I believe in your theory as much as the next person does and if this does indeed occur, you and Mr. Ramey will be two men who have crossed over from Cyberspace Championship Wrestling. Did you leave on good terms with CCW and was there any particular reason for your leaving? ST: The place was going down hill. Ever since the big names left, CCW was in dire need of a good upper card and what did they get? TVO, Brad Lamen, Spider. For months it was like that. Then they brought McAllister and Suzahara out of mothballs and stuck the heavyweight belt on them. Then when they figured out the rest of the guys were being held down, the Commissioner got the belt, but what made me leave was the money, and the security. I never knew when I'd be able to hold onto something I earned. I had my Independent Title stripped off me while I was in a coma, so that kind of incensed me, in addition, they weren't too happy with my decision to part-time at PWA. So basically, we just came to an end. ES: Well, if we are going to bring money into the picture, then why join OSW, I know as well as the next person that the set-up for events makes everything look like a dump, but hell, look at what talent we do have and when you get people from CCW, NITCW, and PCW? Something has to be going on the backstage area wouldn't you agree? ST: Well, for me, I don't know about anyone else, but here... You get to get in on the ground floor. There's no history to weigh you down. We are the founders, we are the...no, I AM THE FUTURE. But you get what I mean, and yes, the money is VERY good... ES: Have you meet the person you work for in person? ST: No, I let my manager handle all of that stuff. ES: So, you wouldn't consider yourself a very political person? ST: Backstage politics don't concern me... Well, not TOO much. If the guys don't like me, then they got a problem, not me. ES: That's a very different outlook on things. It surely is, but if I may say so, with an attitude like yours. You could make it big, or you could have everyone not liking you. If this goes that way, you'll be on the streets before the second OSW show. ST: Unlikely, and I'll tell you why, I wasn't signed for my politicking. I was signed because I am killer on the mic, I am technically sound, and I know how the business works. If OSW is what it purports to be, and then I will have no problem, but if some guys want a piece of me, they can bring it to the ring, don't be a pussy and go behind my back. I don't play stupid sycophant games or suck up to the boss. ES: Very big words from a reasonable big man. If you've noticed half of the roster are, dare I say, midgets... ST: Well, I really don't give the rest of the roster much thought. They're not as visually stunning as I am, and they don't have the charisma I do. So they do have a tendency to fall under the radar, so to speak. Except, of course, for Civil Disobedience. ES: Civil Disobedience is all I hear you talking about, in my humble opinion, they both look kind of funny to me and I don't mean funny in a good way, either. ST: I came from PWA with them, and our last match was together. I really enjoyed working with them and beating down Rent-A-Hero and that loud mouthed little skank Aimz. Plus, their promos are GOLD. Solid Gold! They've got everything you need to succeed. Except.. of course, the fact they're not me. That may hurt them in the long run. ES: Yes, that may hurt everyone in the long run. ST: Indeed! ES: Well, Mr. Toomes it has been a pleasure none the less, but I'm afraid we are out of time. Any closing statements? Or final thoughts? ST: Hmmm... No, I think I'm going to go eat. But I would like to say that if there's any asshole on the roster that comes to work drunk, I'm going to kick their ass. ES: Thank you for your time Mr. Toomes. It's been a real pleasure. Now, as many of you may have felt, Mr. Toomes takes to himself quite nicely. He feels that he is the one and only person in OSW that really matters with an exception to his cohorts. Mr. Toomes, in my humble opinion, seems to be a little funny. Sort of in the same stature that I would have classified Civil Disobedience, but none the less, I do feel that he posses the skills and right attitude that it takes to progress on in OSW. His ego problem may cause him some troubles down the line, but only when he goes up against men of brute strength, such as "Black Ice". His reasoning seems to be in good tack. Final thoughts on Switchblade, do I feel he will be a flop or do I feel he is a big prosperous figure to be handled by the OSW font office with care? I feel that Mr. Toomes will be a big name down the line in OSW, if not sooner. His attitude is heading him in the wrong direction, management wise, but it is getting him somewhere with the roster. Gaining the respect of his peers or just plain out pissing them off. Either way, he could care less. World Champion bound? You and I could both bet our money on it.
