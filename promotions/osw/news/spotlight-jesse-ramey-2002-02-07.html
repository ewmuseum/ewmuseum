Spotlight: Jesse Ramey

7 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello OSW! It is time for another Wrestler Spotlight! My next guest is “Bad Attitude” Jesse Remey! Shanda Russell: Hello OSW, I'm here with 'Bad Attitude' Jesse Ramey! Jesse, how are you today? Jesse Ramey: It feels great not being dead any longer. Shanda Russell: Well, that poses my first question. What exactly was the story behind your supposed...death? Jesse Ramey: Well, it was actually a management problem where I was forced into retirement. Shanda Russell: Interesting. Now, I noticed that you had a slight confrontation with Switchblade at Slam, care to elaborate on the story there? Jesse Ramey: That was actually one of the problems, it seemed that management wanted to have Switchy put over more and who was the one man standing in their way? There was a huge ego trip going on in the backstage area and Switchy was at the top of the list when it came to this. Shanda Russell: Now, several things have occurred on Saturday, mainly the introduction of the major PCW players that have come over. Any thoughts on them or are they on an even playing field with everyone else? Jesse Ramey: Well, some of these men, quite frankly are higher up than many of us. It is a great honor to be working with men like, Doug Troy and Trashcan Man, but in all honesty, I feel that some of the OSW talent may be over-shadowed by their presence. Shanda Russell: That's understandable. Now on next Saturday, you and Drew Hostyle are going up against Switchblade and Frankie Bones. Your thoughts on that match? Jesse Ramey: I'm willing to work with or against anyone. As long as Mr. Hostyle is aware of the fact that he does have a partner, I feel we can make this thing work without a hitch. Shanda Russell: Other than naturally obtaining a title, are there any major goals that you have planned for the OSW? Jesse Ramey: To make it bigger than I ever did in CCW and that isn't saying much. Shanda Russell: Well, thank you for your time Mr. Ramey. Any closing thoughts? Jesse Ramey: OSW, It's Time for an Attitude Adjustment! With Jesse Ramey alive and full of fight, this adds just another wrestler into the crazy mix that is the OSW! Stay tuned for more Wrestler Spotlights!
