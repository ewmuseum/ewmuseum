Civil D. Speaks Out

14 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As of late, Da Westsidaz and Heresian Bloodlust have found it fit to present their views on current events taking place in the OSW. So when an OSW reporter saw Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke standing in a line waiting for the premier of Star Wars Episode II, he took the opportunity to jot down some quotes from them as well. Unfortunately, they weren't quite as eloquent as either of their opponents. "Dicksmacks. All four of them are dicksmacks," Hawke stated. When pressed further, he elaborated with the comments that their opponents were "Shit-faced shit-eaters" and "Cock-smoking cock-smokers." Masters nodded in agreement. It was at this point that the reporter realized how extremely lame his job really was, being placed lower on the pay-scale than the two morons he was interviewing. He then left the scene, hanging his head in shame.
