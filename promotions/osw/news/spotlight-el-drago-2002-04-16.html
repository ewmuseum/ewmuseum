Spotlight: El Drago

16 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanda: Hello OSW! I'm Shanda Russell here with another OSW Wrestler Spotlight! My guest today is El Drago along with his interpreter, Pablo Rucha! Hello gentlemen! Drago: Hola. Rucha: That means Hello to all those that do not understand Spanish. Shanda: Yes..well, if I may ask, El Drago, you obviously can understand English, right? El Drago looks over to Rucha Rucha: Very little, with out me he would be lost in this environment. Shanda: So..he doesn't speak English? Rucha: Not that I have heard. –chuckle- Shanda: Interesting...well..how exactly do you plan on operating inside of an America based federation if he cannot understand what is going on around him? Rucha: How does a child survive in a world of adults? They have some one to guide them, to teach them and look out for them. El Drago has me, Pablo Rucha to not only translate for him what is going on but to guide him and teach him how an American based federation works. Shanda: Alright, but if I may ask....isn't that a lot of say..power. I mean, you basically decide what he does, and what he thinks, and what he knows...right? Rucha: He has a brain, I can not make a single decision for him when he is in that ring. I am merely a guide and a close personal friend. Here in American Wrestling you may think when is Rucha going to stab Drago in the back. Hardly is that the case. In Mexico trust is treasured and would never be what is the word I am looking for here... You'll have to pardon me my English is not as good as it should be. Shanda: That's quite alright, I think I understand. So then, what exactly brings El Drago to the OSW? You would think he would rather remain within Mexico, right? Rucha: Let me ask him. Rucha turns to El Drago. Rucha: Qué trae exactamente El Drago al OSW? Pensaría usted que él hace permanece más bien dentro de México, el derecho?" Drago nods as the words are said and then responds. Drago: Aprecio que México sea un lugar magnífico al wrestle. Shanda: What uh..what did he say? Rucha: What he said is Mexico is great but he has done everything he could do in Mexico. It was the time for change when a talent scout from your organization was in Mexico. The offer was simply to good to refuse. Shanda: Ahh, I see. Well then, with that in mind, is there anyone within the OSW that Drago is paying attention to particularly? Rucha: -Rucha turns to Drago- Hay cualquiera en OSW que usted vigilan? Drago: Yo no tendría incoveniente en que obtiene más entonces apenas mis ojos en Aimz, yo amaría tener intercorse con ella. Rucha and Drago both share a laugh. Shanda looks for a translation but instead El Drago speaks again. Drago: Hay nadie de interés todavía. Todo es igual, todo caerá el mismo. Rucha: What he is saying is that all in OSW are equal, all in OSW will see the fire, all will feel the heat and all will fear him. He isn't a Mexican legend for nothing you know. Shanda: Fascinating. Well, does El Drago have any final words for the fans of the OSW, or perhaps his fellow wrestlers? Drago: Las palabras finales? Rucha translates and El Drago scratches his chin the one piece of skin visible beneath his mask. Drago: OSW tiene cuidado el Dragón viene. Rucha: What he just said there was that the fans will love him, his opponents will fear him, and when he arrives in OSW the year of the Dragon will begin. And on a personal note Shanda, I have to say you are quite a stunning specimen of a female. Shanda: -Giggles- Now, did he say that, or you? Rucha: You think he said all that in five words? If that is everything we must be getting going. El Drago needs to return for his farewell match in Mexico. A good bye to the people. But we will definitely be in touch. Rucha smirks. Shanda: Yes, well thank you, and good luck in the OSW!! Rucha: Your luck is appreciated. Rucha runs his hand on Shanda's shoulder before he and El Drago make there way out, Rucha taking a peak of Shanda's backside as he goes.
