Ed Denies Email

9 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Old School Wrestling Commissioner, Ed, has denied penning an email asking Da Westsidaz to take out OSW Tag Team Champions, Heresian Bloodlust and former champs, Civil Disobedience, that was attributed to him by report on on the popular online wrestling sheet, Wrestlingtattler.com. "I use crayons to do my OSW paperwork," said Ed, via telephone. "What in the heck would make people believe I would write an email, especially one like that!?!" According to the Tattler report, Ed sent an email promising them top dollar and run of the tag team division. Below are some excerpts from the email, as reported by the Tattler. "I’m only going to be acting CEO for awhile, so you better get over here quick and get this deal done. The contract is perfect for you, you’re making the same amount of money as a Main Eventer for doing half the work." Other parts of the email were just as disturbing. "Just remember, be at the Pay-Per-View. I don’t care what you do, just make sure the Tag Champions and Civil Disobedience know you’re here to mess them up. I know I can trust you, just don’t get sidetracked boys." Rumors of Ed abusing his power have been running rampant since OSW owner Jane Reagan's incapacitation and subsequent disappearance. Is this just speculation, or is Ed abusing his power like a rented mule? Stay tuned to find out!
