Wrestler Spotlight: Armour

10 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanda Russell: Hello everyone, I am Shanda Russell and I'm back with another OSW Wrestler Spotlight, this time with one of the newest OSW superstars, Armour. Now Armour, we know very little about you, so why don't you tell us about about your background in the sport? Armour: Why don't I tell you? It's perfectly simple. What does the past have anything to do with the future? You've heard the rumours, you've heard the small reports. Yes, it is true I have been banned from many federations...and yes it is true even No Disqualification Federations....but the reasons? Well, those reasons remain with me. But I wouldn't get too down, I have a feeling history will repeat itself...just look at Toomes. Yes, that's a hint to the reasons I was banned. *motions to continue* Shanda Russell: Interesting... Well, that leads into my next question. Last week you demolished the former OSW World Heavyweight Champion on the behest of commissioner Howard Porter. What exactly is your relationship with Porter? Armour: Strictly business and nothing more. When I was looking for new work, and Jane Reagan kept ignoring my calls, I looked to someone else for a job. That man was Howard Porter. He gave me the opportunity to have a job...if I destroyed Toomes. I have been out of work for quite sometime, with my kind of history. So what did I do? I beat Toomes into oblivion, and MADE sure that he would never return the same again. I take my work very seriously. Shanda Russell: Where do you see "your work" leading you in the near future within OSW? Armour: I'm not a fortune teller, I have no idea exactly. But vaguely I know a few things...their WILL be more destruction to come, and their WILL be more success headed my way. Who will I destroy next? *shrugs* Only the future holds that answer. Shanda Russell: Or Howard Porter. Would that be an accurate statement? Armour: With the right amount of money he supplies...that would be accurate statement. Just mind you it doesn't bother me at all...because I just enjoy destroying...all he does...is supply the victims. Shanda Russell: Tell us three things people should know about Armour. Armour: Only three? So be it. Three things they should know about myself...number one, do not anger myself in any way shape or form. Number two, do not anger Howard Porter...I can't help if he sends me to attack you. Number three, if you see the obvious strength and power difference between myself and you...run and hide...the best you can. Shanda Russell: Immediately upon your appearance on Slam last week, comparisons to now institutionalized OSW superstar, Meanstreak, began with internet wrestling sheets calling you things "Meanstreak-wannabe" and "Poor Man's Meanstreak." How do you respond to these statements? Armour: Notice that I am here...and Meanstreak has left. Notice that he is so weak, he had to leave his fighting grounds...and notice that I am still here. Notice that upon my arrival, I took out one of the TOP names in OSW. Meanstreak-wannabe? In no way am I interested in becoming a weak fighter such as him. Shanda Russell: What do you expect to accomplish here in OSW? Armour: Everything. I attempt to make an impact in OSW that no other man has been able to do. And I think I've come off and a good start in doing so. I want my name to be remembered when I am long dead and buried....and I WILL NOT allow myself to be just a name...but a name feared forever. I expect to help The Think Tank, my comrades so-to-speak, in defeating all of their opponents...all those victims. And further more...I expect to have the name Armour be recognized...and feared...everywhere. Shanda Russell: Any closing comments for the fans? Armour: The fans? Those who watched me brutally beat down The Elite...yes, I have one comment. Expect MUCH more of what you saw last Saturday...expect MUCH more destruction. Shanda Russell: Thank you for taking the time to sit down with me today Armour. That will wrap up this edition of Wrestler Spotlight. I'm Shanda Russell. See you this Saturday night on Slam!
