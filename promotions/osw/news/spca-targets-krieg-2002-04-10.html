SPCA Targets Krieg

10 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
According the the SPCA website, OSW's own German Communist, Michel Krieg, is being hounded by animal lovers everywhere. Following is an extract from their website: "Michel Krieg tossed a poor, defenseless monkey from pillar to post, or turnbuckle to turnbuckle as the case me be, with no respect for its health." Fans are equally outraged. "Not in my life have I see a monkey being beaten worse than that," said Eve Longbottom, a mother of two who had taken her sons to see the event she now calls 'disgusting and foul.' Krieg, who is presently visiting former PWA buddies around the Kentucky area, wasn't available for comment, but his manager, Andreas Odessa, released a statement. "Michel does what he thinks is appropriate and I not stand in his way," said Odessa. We will bring you more on the story as it develops.
