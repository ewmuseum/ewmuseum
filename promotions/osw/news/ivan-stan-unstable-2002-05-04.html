Ivan Stan-unstable?

4 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After the events of Head on Collision in which Ivan Stanislav and his girlfriend, Tempest, unceremoniously broke off their relationship, Stanislav quickly returned back to his appointed office in the Arkhangelsk Military Base to undergo what his ally, Alexei Ruslan claims is “repatriation” or the forceful integration back into ones home country. Since then, reports have been leaking out that after a brief period of what some call ‘mourning’ for his loss in which Stanislav locked himself into his office for the better part of a day and a half, Stanislav exited his office and proceeded to rampage throughout the base. One soldier, who chose to remain nameless, claims that Stanislav, “has sent three men to the hospital, damaged several pieces of equipment, and has generally disrupted the overall constancy of the base itself.” However, when asked about these situations, Alexei Ruslan promptly disregarded the allegations as, “Insubstantial musings by curious watchers outside of the Motherland.” However, the reports such as the one above by the soldier are not singular. Several other individuals within Russia have said that Stanislav, who is generally cool, calculating, and thoroughly precise in his actions, has been very hasty and brash within the last week. Not only that, but reports of Stanislav shifting from fits of animalistic rage to a very mellow and calm side have been noticed, but the mellow spells quickly fade into a rage once again. What does this mean? Is Ivan Stanislav’s breakup transcending the relationship? Is this a violent way for Ivan Stanislav to merely revert to the way he once was? Or is it deeper, more rooted than any of this, and merely a way for him to cope with such a psychological blow? Stanislav reports to be having a major announcement on Slam and to talk about his current status. Perhaps after Slam we will learn how stable Ivan Stanislav is, both politically in the OSW and mentally inside of his calculating mind.
