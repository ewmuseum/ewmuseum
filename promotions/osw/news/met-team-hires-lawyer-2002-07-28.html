MET Team Hires Lawyer

28 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Watkins Group lawyer Tom Core said earlier today that he is considering legal action to guarantee OSW contracts for his Redemption Song tag-team. In a prepared statement which he read to the media via conference call, the team’s manager also sensed fear in former OSW tag-team champions Da Westsidaz. “First of all, the league stated there would be online voting after Slam! to decide a winner for Task 4," said Core. "Instead, the winners were posted a mere hour after the Slam! card had finished. Second, we have learned from sources close to Da Westsidaz that they intentionally reversed the judging order of their card to ensure an American Ronin win the MET competition. This way Da Westsidaz would have a jobber team that they would potentially face in the OSW Tag-Team Championship Tournament rather than a dangerous one. To tell you the truth, its a smart plan, but it reeks of cowardice.” Core went on to say that Redemption Song have still not officially decided to sign contracts with CHZ, instead opting for a 7-city Canadian train adventure to see if Canada is to their liking. “We will make our decisions on whether to pursue legal action after we tour Canada,” Core concluded. More on this breaking story as it develops.
