OSW Fan Poll Results

16 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
According to a recent poll sponsored by the popular online wrestling sheet, Wrestlingtattler.com, Civil Disobedience are OSW favorite personalities, barely beating out Doug Troy and Trashcan Man. Meanwhile, Sebastian "Switchblade" Toomes edged Ivan Stanislav as OSW's most hated individual. Other interesting poll facts are as follows: --Female fans voted Mike Marvelous as their favorite wrestler, just edging out Matt Blaze. --Alternative lifestyle fans favor Jesse Ramey, though their favorite OSW personality is Steven Smith. --Asian fans prefered Hong Kong native Tempest over Great Dragon by a three to one margin. More such polls are planned for the future. We will bring you more meaningless facts as they become available.
