Spotlight: Dave Gibson

11 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanda Russell: Hello everyone and welcome to another edition of OSW Wrestling Spotlight. I am Shanda Russell and I am here with one of the newest superstars to arrive on the scene in OSW, 'Old School' Dave Gibson. Dave, let me start off by saying that you have received quite a response from OSW fans since your debut in OSW. Did you expect this kind of fan support? ‘Old School’ Dave Gibson: Absolutely not. I was one of the most hated men in both IOW and jOlt, mainly because I said things--things I felt were true--that the people just didn't want to hear. But obviously the fans here in OSW have a different set of priorities. The fans aren't cheering me now because of some drastic personality change. Dave Gibson is still the same man he was in IOW and jOlt, still an advocate for the old school, still targeting the gigantic lummoxes that are a blight on this business and any extraneous idiots who piss me off. But to have the people behind me for once... it's great. No doubt about it. Hey, it's Old School Wrestling--why WOULDN'T they love me? Shanda Russell: You mentioned IOW, which is set to relaunch within the next few weeks. Why did you make the decision to come to OSW, rather than return to your IOW roots? ‘Old School’ Dave Gibson: Are you joking? Comparing OSW to IOW is like comparing a filet mignon to a can of spoiled Spam. It's true, I got my start in IOW and I had a lot of success. But behind the scenes... it was chaos. Nobody got along. People quitting or getting fired every week... too many egos. I wouldn't go back there if Winters DOUBLED the salary I have in OSW... which is quite a handsome figure, by the way. Shanda Russell: Since your arrival in OSW, you have been embroiled in a battle with The Cult, and allied yourself with a homegrown OSW fan favorite, Clay Vendetta. Do you think that you and Clay have bitten off a bit more than you can chew by taking on the likes of Hessian and Vampir Nosferatu? ‘Old School’ Dave Gibson: [Rolls eyes] Here we go. Everybody and their brother thinks that I'm suicidal for taking on Grim and Grimmer headon. Yeah, guys that big are gonna kick my ass a few times. But it's who wins the last battle that matters most. Vampir I'm not too concerned with, he's a joke, hell he ACTS like a joke half the time. Hessian's another matter, because he's bought into his own hype. While these two are out mucking through graveyards putting [touches his "Shut Up And Wrestle" t-shirt] top-quality OSW merchandise on skeletons, and thinking up a name for every move from a piledriver to a freakin' side headlock, you know where I've been? Training my ass off. I've got a stronger work ethic than anybody in OSW, except maybe Clay, which is why we're so dangerous as a team. We won't stop. We won't quit. We'll keep coming and keep fighting until we get the job done. And that, Shanda, is what The Cult should be afraid of. Shanda Russell: Fascinating...Recently, Michel Krieg caused a flap basically calling you a gimmick thief because your mother sometimes appears in your taped chronicles. Care to comment on that? ‘Old School’ Dave Gibson: Three points, Shanda. First, my mother's the most important person in my life, and it's only natural she's gonna show up in my chronicles once in a while. Second, at least I don't live with my mother like Krieg. And third, if that Teutonic twit ever had the balls to say something to my face, I'm not a hard guy to find. Shanda Russell: Why is your mother so special to you? ‘Old School’ Dave Gibson: [Long pause, as Gibson stares at Shanda, trying to determine where this is headed] Because she's the toughest person I know. My dad... he left when I was just a baby. Walked out and left us hanging. My mom stepped up and raised me as a single parent, which is damn hard to do. Like I said in my latest chronicle, she's my biggest fan and my worst critic. I wouldn't be where I am today without her. Shanda Russell: That must have been tough. How is it you got into wrestling, without the influence of a male role model? ‘Old School’ Dave Gibson: Actually, I've wanted to be a wrestler for as long as I can remember. I grew up in Charlotte, and I don't have to tell you what a hotbed that is for wrestling. It was on TV every weekend, and when I got old enough and saved enough money, I started going to shows and I was hooked. This stuff gets into your blood, know what I mean? Shanda Russell: Not really. Is there anything OSW fans should know about Dave Gibson? ‘Old School’ Dave Gibson: I'm going to say and do what I want, no matter what the circumstances. I'm not afraid of anybody, and I won't ever back down from a fight. Anyone that thinks I won't last, or that I can't cut it in OSW? Well this isn't just a federation. To me... it's more like a second home. And nobody, and I mean NOBODY, is kicking "Old School" Dave Gibson out of his home. Shanda Russell: Well, that about does it for this edition of OSW Wrestler Spotlight. Thanks to Dave Gibson for joining us. See you this Saturday Night on Slam!
