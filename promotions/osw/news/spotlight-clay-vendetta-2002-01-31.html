Spotlight: Clay Vendetta

31 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello OSW! Next wrestler spotlight is the talented and…mouthy…Clay Vendetta…. Shanda Russell: Hello OSW fans, I'm Shanda Russell with another edition of Wrestler Spotlight. With me today is Classic Clay Vendetta. How are you Clay? Clay Vendetta: The paychecks are in the bank, sweet thing, so of course it's a beautiful day. You had some questions? I can't be here all day, babe. Shanda Russell: Right. First off, how long have you been wrestling? Clay Vendetta: Five years in the pros, though I wrestled as a kid right up through college. All-conference three years in high school, too. I didn't mess around. Shanda Russell: Now, you're up against Scott Naket at the first ever Saturday SLAM! Any thoughts on Naket? Do you think he will give you any trouble in your match? Clay Vendetta: Psssh! Oh, excuse me... every so often these questions get pretty funny. I know Scott Naket; I've kept up on my recent history, you know. Naket is a Canadian buffoon and a PCW reject. I'm stunned that Jane even threw the cash at him; although given what he was making on unemployment, I doubt he's counting hard against the salary cap, if you know what I mean. The only trouble Naket might give me is indigestion; my stomach tends to get a bit queasy when I look at the lack of talent on the roster. Shanda Russell: I read that sometimes, your temper has gotten you into trouble, correct? Clay Vendetta: Hey, my temper has *never* gotten me into trouble. To be honest, I run a very even keel. It's the idiots that I'm surrounded by that are the problem. Every so often, I simply feel the need to make the world a better place by punishing the less mentally fortunate, that's all. Shanda Russell: But, with Jane Reagan at the helm, don't you think that regardless what 'you' think, she may think otherwise. Jane Reagan has been known to not put up with temper uh...tantrums. Clay Vendetta: You see, this is the sort of idiocy I'm talking about. Reagan is dealing with the top talent in the business when it comes to Clay Vendetta. She signs the checks, I make the money, everybody's happy, no problems. See? Next question. Shanda Russell: Would you say that you are...egotistical? Clay Vendetta: I'm confident, Shanda, very confident, as well I should be... I'm simply the best this fed has to offer. I'm disappointed that with the money that's flowing into OSW they can't hire reporters who ask better questions than that. I'm even more disappointed that a supposedly intelligent woman like yourself can't tell the difference between talent and egotism... c'mon. Shanda Russell: Any comments for the rest of the roster within the OSW, or the fans? Clay Vendetta: Just one simple statement: the world has not yet seen what kind of heights Clay Vendetta can reach, and OSW is simply my ladder to the stars. They're calling this fed Old School Wrestling, but by the time all is said and done, they can shorten "old-school" to one word... simply? "Classic". Now if you'll excuse me, I have a Canadian to kick back across the border... this interview is over. As you can see Clay Vendetta leaves, eats and breathes a cocky and confident demeanor and there is no reason to believe he can not ride his skill and confidence straight to the top.
