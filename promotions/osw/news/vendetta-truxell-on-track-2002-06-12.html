Vendetta/Truxell on Track

12 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Road and Track magazine's July 2002 issue, on newsstands now, features an in-depth article on the "pinkslip" match between Clay Vendetta and Griff Truxell, to take place at OSW Acension on June 23rd. The article looks at the origins of the quarter-mile "pinkslip" race, the history of both wrestlers, and a "tale of the tape" between not only both combatants but their cars as well. Interesting facts of note: The original 340 horsepower engine is still in Vendetta's '63 Corvette. The vehicle, after restorations, is worth approximately $75,000. Truxell's custom limo has a base price of about $50,000, but modifications (including custom rims, a pair of DVD players, and a fully-stocked bar) raise the price to well over $100,000. At Ascension, one man will get to choose between driving home in raw power or sheer luxury... and the other will be taking the taxi home. Tune in on June 23rd to see who will get the pinkslip!
