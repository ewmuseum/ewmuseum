Randolph Put to Work

2 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sunday night, Scott Taylor and Kid Wonder fought in what turned out to be an absolutely brutal TLC match. In the end, Kid Wonder, battered, bruised, and bloodied, came out victorious. And because of a pr-match stipulation made by the two men, Kid Wonder gained "ownership" of Taylor's bodyguard, Randolph. One day after their brutal match, Randolph has already been put to use by the "Quintessential Teen Idol". "Yeah, I'm putting my new bodyguard to work," said the battered Wonder. "After the show, Randolph guarded me as I went to the hospital to close this big gash in my head. Then, after that, he guarded Amanda and I while we threw a little celebration party. I know it was early morning, but who cares? Then, this morning, Randolph guarded us as I treated Amanda to a grand slam breakfast at Denny's. So, yeah, Randy's gonna be put to use. He is a bodyguard after all." When asked if their treatment of Randolph was appropriate, Amanda said, "Of course it is! It was he who kept hold of me. Remember? If anything, we're being easy on him by not giving him any food at the party or not giving him breakfast. Hell, the guy could stand to lose a little weight if you ask me." Whether Randolph will be at SLAM! on Saturday night for Kid Wonder's match against Corg was no mentioned. Also, as requested by the teen idol, Randolph had no comment. We'll bring you more on Randolph's stay with PCW's former First Couple when anything becomes available.
