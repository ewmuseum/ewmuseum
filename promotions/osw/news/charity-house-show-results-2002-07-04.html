Charity House Show Results

4 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Old School Wrestling threw their support behind a worthy cause in the form of an abridged card, Wednesday night, supporting Lola Poopingbear, a local Chipoqua woman who recently gave birth to quintuplets, just days after her husband, Luke A. Poopingbear, died from grain alcohol poisoning. The event helped raise over $10,000 for Mrs. Poopingbear and her newborn children. The show started off with local tag team duo, The Scalpers, who despite the name, actually run an gimmick where they are Native American ticket scalpers, coming out trying to sell copies of the birth certificates of the young Poopingbears, much to the displeasure of the crowd. The crowd’s ire, was quickly quelled, as Civil Disobedience hit the ring. The Scalpers, took a quick beating, in a match with relatively few high spots. Civil Disobedience finished off the relatively unpolished Scalpers with Social Decline in 5:12. Post match, Civil Disobedience sent an open call out to the members of the Vax, challenging any two members of the group to meet them in a tag team match next Saturday night on Slam. Suddenly, the theme for the Dr. Who television series hit the PA system, which was little more than a stereo with a low quality amplifier, and much to the surprise and dismay of both Civil D. and the crowd, Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman came out of the back. After telling the crowd how Oklahomans are pro-Puerto Rico for statehood because it’s the only way that Oklahoma could move out of last place of all the US states for CAT scores for children 6-13, PHD accepted Civil Disobedience’s challenge, saying he had a huge surprise planned as his partner. After a bit of an intermission which featured native American dancing, we saw MET competitor, The Masked Mullet, take on Lillith. The Masked Mullet started off quickly, but his head of steam was quickly chopped down to size, as Lillith took control of the match using her judo abilities, and never looked back, finishing off the former CCW superstar with Death Blossom at 4:27. Post match, Lillith reminded everyone she was the Queen of Extreme and warned Corg to watch his back…among other things. After another break which saw a 50-50 raffle, saw OSW newcomer, ‘Old School’ Dave Gibson take on MET competitor, The Violator. There was a bit of mic work between the two men, where massive The Violator informed Gibson that he was the Violator and should prepare for Total Violation, Gibson, with the crowd repeating in unison, told The Violator to “Shut up and Wrestle.” The Violator dominated early with his superior strength, but Gibson used his quickness and ring know-how to off set the big man’s size advantage. The match spilled outside the ring a few times, where the two men battled it out, with Gibson getting the better of the exchanges, twice smacking the Violator across the back with a chair. The end came in the ring, as Gibson locked on the Silencer to pick up the win at 10:27. Between matches, we had a more tribal dancing and another 50-50. After the intermission, Michel Krieg, Kory Storm and Frankie Bones hit the ring and voiced their opinions on everything from The Red Army, to Jim Axtell to Jesus St. Crux. Krieg buried his former Red Army companions, saying not only are his ideas weak and old, but his body was as well. Krieg continued to rip on the Red Army, until the crowd suddenly popped. The three men looked around, and just managed to escape the massive clutches of Russian Bear, who came out of the crowd to a huge pop. With Krieg, Bones and Storm talking smack outside the ring, Ivan said since Ivan was so weak, Krieg would have no problem taking him on in the main event. After discussing things with his new found friends, Krieg agreed. During the next intermission, OSW officials gave away Michael Dame, Lennox Lanthrop and The Magi T-shirts to needy children of the reservation. One child was seen wiping his ass with a Lanthrop t-shirt, before discarding it in the trash. The main event was up next, featuring Ivan Stanislav taking on Michel Krieg…for all of about three seconds. Krieg refused to enter the ring, to start the match and began a shouting match with Ivan. While Ivan was arguing with Krieg, Bones and Storm entered the ring, and attacked the Russian Bear with baseball bats. The Russian Bear did his best to fight off the bat wielding assailants, but Krieg entered a moment later and bashed Ivan with a chair from ringside. Ivan fell to one knee, and the three men continued their assault, until Jim Axtell and Jesus St. Crux ran out of the back, and knocking Storm and Bones out of the ring. The Bear got to his feet, and punctuated the change in tides by nailing Krieg with the Red Scare, knocking the huge German out of the ring. Axtell, St. Crux and Stanislav stood tall in the ring, with their arms held high, much to the delight of the fans. After the show, Ivan Stanislav presented Mrs. Poopingbear with a Russian-made sports utility vehicle, complete with five baby seats, saying that the spirit of pure communism should not be bound by borders and political agendas. Post show, many of the OSW superstars in attendance stuck around to sign autographs for fans. The total attendance for the show, was about 600, which OSW officials were pleased with considering it was thrown together on two days notice. The total raised at the show was $5700, which OSW officials matched with their own donation of $5700, plus the t-shirt and vehicle give away. Dustdevil’s grocery store donated a $2000 food certificate to Mrs. Poopingbear, in return for OSW stars signing autographs outside their shop Friday afternoon. Nick Casey, Moose Malone and Kid Wonder are expected to be on hand to talk with fans. All in all, it was a rewarding evening for both fans, Mrs. Poopingbear and OSW alike. OSW expects to do more such events in the future.
