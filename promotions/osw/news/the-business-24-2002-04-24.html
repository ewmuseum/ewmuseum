OSW — Column

The Business #24

24 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Morning, peons; the King of the Wrestling Media, Robbie Decker here... back once more to enlighten the masses as only I can. This column will be, of course, posted to WrestlingTattler.com, which has been kind enough to grant me a hefty little stipend to go with my already-stuffed OSW paycheck. God, I love capitalism. Of course, WrestlingTattler knows what it's doing; you can't have a site pledging to give the public the inside dirt it wants without going to the best in... The Business. Speaking of, let's get right down to it, 'kay? THE OBLIGATORY PREDICTIONS SHEET: Head-On Collision, OSW's second pay-per-view, is on the way this Sunday night, eminating from the Motor City, Detroit, MI. So, which matches are going to be Chevy Corvettes and which are looking like Chevy Vegas? Queen of Extreme Finale - Competitors TBA: Ever see the Towelie episode of South Park? In the words of the immortal Stan Marsh, "Don'tcaredon'tcaredon'tcare". The Queen of Extreme tournament has bored the living piss out of me since its inception. Wrestling does not need women's divisions. Wrestling needs more women backstage baking pies and delivering them to the announce table, in skimpy clothing. Girls, I prefer apple a la mode, and have a thing for silver bikinis. Got it? WINNER: Whoever delivers me some fresh-cooked apple pie goodness come Sunday night "I Lied" Match - Drew Hostyle vs. Amy Campbell: If Campbell's like every other hot woman I've ever met, she lied. Numerous times. About everything. And then stole the dog, the car, and three-quarters of my assets. Next! WINNER: Drew Hostyle Kimbusa vs. Kid Wonder: Let's run down ten quick facts here; first, I have an immense respect for sumotori and am the reigning PTC/WT aficionado on the ancient martial art. Second, I hate Kid Wonder. Third, I hate Kid Wonder. Fourth through ninth... yeah, you guessed it. And tenth, I'm a big fan of pancakes, which is terrific, 'cause that's all Blunder's gonna be when a quarter-ton of Kimbusa decides to use him as a futon. Don't buy this "lightening-quick-advantage" bullshit... sure, flies are annoying, but how many people kill flies in the US every year? Got an estimate? Good. Now, think about how many flies kill people. Unless Kid Wonder's carrying malaria, Kimbusa's gonna squash him flatter than an Olympic gymnast's chest. I wonder if maple syrup goes well with teenybopper? WINNER: Kimbusa Bloodletting Match - "Angeldust" Nick Casey vs. Epitome of Evil: Wait a minute. These two were feuding? Coulda fooled me. Anyhow, I'm a big fan of any match that features a pair of jobbers undergoing extreme blood loss. Call this one Casey in an effort to put an end to a feud that never really got started. WINNER: Nick Casey Loser Leaves Town Streetfight - Jesse Ramey, Clay Vendetta, Doug Troy & Moose Malone vs John Jupiter, Kensuke Suzahara, Jason McAllister & Spider: I called Team CCW "Team Douchebag" on the Malice Show this week, and I stand by my words, as usual. How dare they come onto my show and try to leech heat? God knows most of these guys have done enough of that already. I saw all of these guys during my time in PCW and I was *not* impressed. Sure, they're going against Vendetta (short), Troy (washed-up), Malone (old), and Ramey (sucks)... but they've pissed off a lot of people, and if, god forbid, it looks like they might win, I guarantee you... I'll throw Dumas in there to break it up. Getting your asses kicked on OSW pay-per-view to get your fed over... yeah, good idea. Punks. WINNER: Team OSW Civil Disobedience vs Team Annoying: Between these two teams, there is the entire spectrum of everything that's wrong with the entertainment world today. I've said that I might bring a revolver to ringside in the event that Team Annoying wins, so I could put a leaden surprise through each of their tiny brainpans... but I've decided against it because I'm afraid that I might just shoot myself when I realize I've gotta sit through this crap. Let's hope CD comes through with the mercy-killing. WINNER: Civil Disobedience OSW Tag Title Match - Tom Walczak & Michel Krieg vs Heresian Bloodlust: Until they change their name, the champs get no love from Decker. WINNER: Walczak/Krieg Graphic Violence vs. ???: I'm normally against big-name free-agent signings, but for a talented guy... not to mention a big fuggin' monster... like Graphic Violence, I can make an exception. If this is a precursor to him joining the OSW roster and not a one-shot deal, I'm all for it. Still, I withhold judgment on this match if I don't know who he's facing. OSW International Title Match - Corg vs. Rhett Locke: So with a pair of loonies like these two, the stipulation is guaranteed to be something ridiculous. Something like a "first goth raver to bust his glowsticks" match or something. Damn hippies. No appreciation for a simple cage match, huh? Anyhow, I like Corg's promo work (in the sort of way I claim to appreciate foreign films without actually understanding what the hell is going on) but Locke's shown a lot and I don't think he's gonna drop the belt here. WINNER: Rhett Locke Ed vs. Meanstreak: In the words of the immortal Black Mage, "sweet zombie Jesus on a pogo stick, we're screwed". The "we" in question here would consist of Ed and anybody who doesn't like the sight of utter human carnage. Yeah, yeah, Ed's hardcore. But in my book, "hardcore" is a synonym for "somebody who doesn't know how the hell to get out of the way of a steel chair". Meanstreak, on the other hand, deserves another term... hmmmm, how about "apocalyptic". Obviously Ed needed some impetus to stay in retirement. After this match, his inability to eat solid foods should be all the convincing he needs. WINNER: Meanstreak OSW World Title Match - Ivan Stanislav vs. Sebastian Toomes: I'm a huge fan of Toomes; this much is known. As for Stanislav? As a fan, I think he's absolutely pussy-whipped. If he had any guy friends, they'd refuse to associate with him. But as an objective observer? His work with Tempest is some of the best stuff I've seen from him yet. Couple that with his size, the presence of the Red Army, and all the distractions that Toomes is up against (namely, a biker, a Vax, and a comatose Jane Reagan) and the cards are simply stacked too high for Switchy. It should be a hell of a treat to watch, though, and an early candidate for Match of the Year. WINNER: Ivan Stanislav AND IN RESPONSE TO A JACKASS...: You know, here's one for the WrestlingTattler people... this weekend I had the pleasure of verbally berating Tyler Nelson. Seems the CEO wanted to give me a hard time on the Malice Show about my shots at PCW... all of which were backed up by reasonable assessments of talent. With Tyler having free reign on the mic, I couldn't get my parting shots in... so what the hell, how about now? Hey Nelson... you kept wanting to know if the Shoot King would come on and "make me his bitch again". You find him yet? Apparently you haven't searched enough dumpsters and homeless shelters. Shoot King disappeared because I put him out of commission, Ty... he couldn't compete and I ran his sorry ass out of town! You think I'm scared of your Ultimate Cannon Fodder coming to OSW? Bring it, kiddies, 'cause none of you could've taken me in my prime, and I'm not altogether sure some of you can take me now. Run your money and your mouth all you want, Nelson, 'cause CEOs come and go but the Third Estate is forever, baby. EAT IT, TWERPS: Wilcox declares for the NBA draft, leaving your only starter next year... Steve Blake. That's right, Steve "I Still Deserve To Be Hit By A Semi" Blake. Have fun! See y'all in Cameron. That's it for this week; be sure to tune in to Head-On Collision this weekend, and of course, be sure to catch our blowout 25th issue in a couple of weeks, where I'll hand out awards for April and have a rollicking good 25th issue time, in which I'll probably spend the print running down every other inferior columnist who could never even contemplate putting out twenty-five issues. Damn, it's good to be on top! Until next time... Hating you all, ---Robbie Decker
