Ring Rat #2: The Vax

2 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello everyone, Well, well, well. It seems intelligence is the key to Saturday's Slam! The Vax. A "man" who has been plaguing OSW since it's dawn is now revealed. It's out in the open. To seek this fly in the soup has been a long tedious task. A task that no OSW official realized in any way, shape, or form. Saying that the Vax was probably smarter than all of OSW put together is probably an understatement. The Vax is truly in higher graces than the likes of Moose Malone, Shanda Russell, Matt Shivan, or even the diabolical Jane Reagan. The Vax has been a part of Old School Wrestling since the first episode of Slam! Sometimes more of a headline than others, sometimes not really showing itself at all. When Scott Naket and Mike Marvelous were actually in OSW... the Vax was there. When the Red Army came to OSW leaving Tyler Nelson's PCW far behind... the Vax was there. When Jane Reagan turned her back on the fans and joined her lover, Sebastian Toomes... the Vax was there. When CCW tried to invade on OSW's rise to the top of the wrestling world... the Vax was there. When a cage was set-up to hold the forces of The Monster and The Samoan Silencer... the Vax was there. When Ed became CEO with Jane Reagan's kidnapping... the Vax was there. What I'm trying to say is... The Vax has always been in OSW! The Vax is as much a part of OSW as a ring or the infamous Mt. St. Hell's End structure. Vax goes with the territory. But what one must wonder is if the Vax is a part of OSW or trying to figure out who he might be and what he'll do next is what makes him a part of OSW. If it's the latter then this Vax ship has sailed for on Saturday Slam!, June 29, 2002... the Vax was... REVEALED! Yes, in an astounding and yet slightly obvious revelation the Vax has turned out to actually be three people, three former Think Tank members, Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman, "The Scholar" Howard Porter, and "The Evil Supergenius" Alan Kriegman. These men performed violent acts that could possibly be construed as terrorism. They set up a cage and electricuted Timo Bolamba and Meanstreak. They poured tons of bricks onto Jesse Ramey. They sabotaged Jane Reagan's car and made it crash. They kidnapped Reagan from the hospital. They framed Ed and got him sent to prison. And most evil and sinister, they wrote VAX on little notecards and sent hundreds of them falling through the air to the OSW ring below twice! Okay, that last thing wasn't that bad. But everything else is pretty mean. Now these three men have a long history. PHD and Porter were both in MWF, UWF, and PCW. Alan Kriegman was also in PCW and there they formed The Think Tank with fellow smarty-pants Nick Taylor. Each man has their own journey they've traveled and have crossed paths with each other many times. They've had their times of glory and their ultimate defeats... most recently seeming defeats. That may be why these three former bossom buddies have rejoined together. To wipe the tarnish off of their beloved egotistical minds. Well, the Vax has been revealed and have seemingly started more chaos and have easily taken names of their enemies. So far the list is Sebastian Toomes, Ed, and Jane Reagan, but we all know as well as we know Martha Stewart is a homewrecker that there are plenty more guys who want at these littluns. The only question is... Who can stop them? Well, until next time sweety-pie, this is The Ring Rat saying... Behave! -"The Ring Rat" Janet Cads
