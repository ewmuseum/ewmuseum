Tempest/Ivan Rift

8 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In recent news, supposed “item” Ivan Stanislav and Tempest have recently hit turbulence, as the two individual’s personalities have clashed and flung them apart. Stanislav, a known left wing extremist and practicing communist from Russia and Tempest, a carefree woman hailing from Hong Kong were both paired up during a bachelor contest that was aired on PTC Malice and have been seeing each other on a relatively normal basis since. However, during last OSW Slam while Tempest was busy taping wrestlers backstage, Stanislav lost his cool and promptly crushed her camera to bits and dismissed her. Subsequently, Tempest confronted Stanislav and asked for an explanation, only putting more fuel on the fire between the two. Stanislav was found and made a brief statement, “Tempest is unable to see beyond her own feelings to those around her. I decided to stop the trite actions that she was taking and if she cannot understand the repercussions of such behavior, then it is her own fault.” Tempest only had a brief comment. "Ivan's no fun anymore," squeaked Tempest. "He's too busy living up to his own propaganda." Stanislav and Tempest have grown slightly closer over the past weeks, with Stanislav even showing a slight side of emotion and caring for Tempest, but after the actions of Saturday Slam, Stanislav has been nothing but the angry Russian Bear that we have known to love. What will happen between these two in the next few days? Tune in to Saturday Slam and Crowning Glory to watch this story unfold!!
