Callisto Bane Speaks

15 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OSW's newest superstar, Callisto Bane, had a lot to say when we caught up with her outside a gothic nightclub on Wednesday evening regarding her short but eventful OSW career to date. "I am still trying to figure out how there can be four women in this week's main event for a shot the #1 Contender's spot for the OSW World Heavyweight Title, yet I was left out," said the She-Beast. "I could crush each and everyone of the other competitors in that match with ease. I would beat Tempest so brutally, that her Japanese girlfriend would feel it. Lillith the Queen of Extreme? Pfft. She can't even fathom the extremes I can go to. Finally, Bethany Yamato? She going to (expletive) the rest of the competitors to death?" Bane wasn't finished there. "Erik Kelly needs to answer for his cowardly attack on me last week on Slam," growled Bane. "If he is reading this, assuming he can read, I am calling you out to meet me in the center of the ring. Oh, and bring your brick again, because I have use for it." Finally, Bane had words for the Throwbacks in response to the challenge their challenge to the Cult for a steet fight at BAMN. "If the Throwbacks want a fight at the upcoming OSW PPV then by all means I, if not the entire Cult, will relish in ripping them limb from limb," said Bane. "Be it a regular match up, a tag match up, a handicapped match up, or a damned streetfight I will be first in line when the time comes. So in response to those Throwbacks and their little offer, consider me accounted for." Callisto Bane had nothing more to state about the subject but from her attitude she is more than ready to meet the challenge! We will bring you more on these developing stories as news becomes available.
