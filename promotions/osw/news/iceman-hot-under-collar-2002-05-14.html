Iceman Hot Under Collar?

14 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The normally icy-cool Northern Wonder is said to be hot under the collar this week. And as is typical with Kanaida, he hasn't limited his anger to one simple topic. He's reportedly furious with Ian Wright and Reno Davis getting chances at the International Title. Backstage rumormongers are whispering that Kanaida is angry with Davis, saying that a man of his stature should not be representing Canada in the match and is angry with Wright for putting off their own match to participate. Meanwhile, yet more whispers about Kanaida abound. Supposedly, he wants to settle things with Moose Malone from Kanaida's debut, and may call out the big OSW Head of Security on Slam. And, even though he's not even participated in a match, he is already complaining about his status on the OSW website, and his lack of publicity in the popular press. And, of course, anger over the UWA title situation. Of course, it is also rumored that Kanaida has spent the past two weeks hiding from Stephen Smith, and this has hindered the big Canadian in meeting with OSW officials to sign matches and get publicity. Truth or fiction? You be the judge.
