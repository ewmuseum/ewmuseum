Ivan Beaming Over PPV

25 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After coming off a huge night on OSW Ascension, the leader of The Red Army, Ivan Stanislav, was more than happy to boast about the wins from his fellow Comrades… “The Red Army applauds Comrade Walczak in his acquisition of World Title against Doug Troy! Once again, the mighty workers have risen up against the capitalists and have brought them to their knees!” said Stanislav backstage after Ascension. The Russian Bear was quick to boast about his own victory as well. “Reno Davis has fallen to the judgment of Ivan Stanislav, just as he should! He lies battered and beaten on the floor of justice while the judge. Praporschik Stanislav himself, put him back in his rightful place!” While these were indeed two huge victories for The Red Army, a defeat was also apparent as Michel Krieg lost to El Drago, thus forcing Stanislav to battle Drago on this coming Slam. Drago demanded a match against Stanislav a few weeks ago, in which Stanislav was quick to throw Krieg in his way, but now must fess up to his words. Stanislav, when asked about the situation, was not happy. “Michel Krieg failed to defeat a mere Mexican, and now the Patriarch of Red Army must once again show the younger, inexperienced “Communists” how to get job done!” While Stanislav boasts ‘togetherness and stability,’ a known rift has formed between The Russian Bear and the German War Machine. Reports of Krieg taking money from wrestlers such as Kory Storm for backstage assaults have been reported, which has only enraged Stanislav more, and his failure to obey a direct order last week on Slam furthered the animosity between the two. “His failure to silence Drago means that once again, Ivan Stanislav has to cover for his shortcomings!” Stanislav was also sure to let us know that his overall anger towards the finish of the Ascension Main Event would not go unheard. “El Drago robbed me of my World Title shot, and Ivan distinctly remembers hearing the buzzer while still atop Mount St. Hells End.” Review taps show that Stanislav was indeed off the top of the mountain when the buzzer sounded, but he disproved them as, “Capitalist forgeries made to hold back the working man.” While he preaches stability, is the Red Army falling apart? Stanislav claims that El Drago won’t stand a chance against the Communist might of Ivan Stanislav, but only time will truly tell. Tune in on Saturday Slam to find out!!
