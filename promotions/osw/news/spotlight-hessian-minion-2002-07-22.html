Spotlight: Hessian & Minion

22 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanda: Hello OSW! It's Shanda Russell here with another "Wrestler Spotlight!" In this edition, I'm here with Hessian and Minion, two members of the upcoming stable known as "The Cult!" Thanks for giving me this time gentlemen...er....gentleimp...er....uh...yes. Minion: Ah yeah...no problem babycakes! Hessian: ...................... Shanda: Yes..well...uh..anyway. Hessian and Minion, you two entered into the OSW on the very first show as a mystery team and have since then been a solid team within the OSW. Up to this point, are you happy with your performance within the federation? Hessian: The performance of HERESIAN BLOODLUST as a team in this federation, has been flawless. As a tag team...myself and my partner conquered the division with an iron fist! It is evident to all that no one team in OSW could stop the path of destruction laid down by the might of us. Up to this point I and my former partner have held tag gold, although complications did arise, as a team we were the epitome of 'tag team'. The competition has been fair, but never so tough as to overcome the power of Heresian Bloodlust. However in this day and age now things can only get better for Vampir Nosferatu and I as we wander the field as The Cult...of course all the while Minion has been supporting like a true Imp... Minion: Do you have any crabpuffs? Shanda: Crabpuffs? I don't think so. Um..yes, your partner, Azrael Cain, had an unfortunate accident in a match it seems a while back. What are your thoughts on him? You appeared to get along quite well..and then had some sort of falling out. Hessian: In his prime, Azrael Cain was a fine warrior...fit to lead an army! As I said, we stormed the OSW Tag Team Division...and we slaughtered everyone and everything...including those cotton picking suburban reject PCW Hall of Famers! Azrael Cain and I weren't just a tag team, we were the team! At Crowning Glory, Civil Disobedience may have had the upper hand, but apart from one title loss, Heresian Bloodlust dominated as the one true tag team of OSW.......and then the insolent fuck made a simple miscalculation that damn near cost him his life! Indifferences? It was all down to that piss-ridden homeless cretin of a manager Cain tagged along with! Caleb lost the trust of Cain, and in the end it rooted its way around the team and caused disruptions between us...culminating in the destruction of Heresian Bloodlust at Ascension. You know Shanda...lovely Shanda......Da Westsidaz claim to have been the ones to destroy us, and sure Riptah may have set it up, but it was Azrael Cain who knocked down all the proverbial pins....strike! He's out........... Minion: Do you have any bagels then? Shanda: Bagels? Um..no Minion, I'm not a bakery. Well, out he was Hessian, I must say you really made sure he was taken care of. But Da Westsidaz have the tag titles. I have to say that whlie you claim to be a great team..you don't have the titles. Minion: Well what's with the yeast infection then??? Shanda: I will disregard that statement. Hessian, if you please.... Minion: You can disregard deez nu-... Hessian: AHEM! Minion: ............. Hessian: We don't have the titles? So? Have you been watching Slam! lately? While The Cult and The Throwbacks collide in the ring, Da Westsidaz dote around the arena "looking" for competition...well do you know what? WE WERE THE FUCKING COMPETITION!!! Civil Disobedience, where are they??? Twisted Metal??? The only goddamn tag team worthy of those titles is gone, and a better pairing has been created, but you know what? Vampir and I cannot take those titles back, because Dave Gibson and his band of little old schooling punks wish to get in our faces, while Da Westsidaz take all credibility from those titles! They have done NOTHING! with them! The Vax were so bored with what was going on Porter had to make a tournament for the titles! Another great act by the Think Tank! Albeit they entered in three teams...but no one...NOBODY and NOTHING can stop the first ever Global Champion in Vampir Nosferatu, and myself...a former Tag Team CHAMPION! I ask you Russell, have you been captivated by Da Westsidaz' title run as you once were when they clashed with Heresian Bloodlust in a Weapons of Choice Match? When we came cascading down from the rafters and onto a Cell? When we destroyed the ring??? Did Da Westsidaz holding the titles provide any entertainment for you??? Shanda: Well uh....I won't answer that question, this interview is for your opinions, not mine, per say. But moving on. What about the entire Vax situation? Where does the Cult stand? Hessian: ......answer the question.... Shanda: Well..it was uh...a little entertai....um..no..not entertaining at all..... Hessian: Exactly....now....in relation to the Vax...There you have what appears to be a supergroup...they have everything...brains, brawn...and no boundaries. Ed found that out when they pulled the plug on him...and now what is left? The most dominating force in OSW next to Heresian Bloodlust, and a roster full of victims. The Cult however? We are four strong and not yet complete. Although that may be the case...unless the Vax disturb The Cult, then we shan't bother with them. We have other worries as opposed to that group... Minion: John Mammoth better watch his back, 'cos if he don't, Ima bizounce all up in his face.....y'hear, biotch? Shanda: Ehem, do you have a license for that animal, Hessian? Well in regard to your members, it appears there are some secret members joining the Cult. Care to give us a hint? Minion: GRRRRRRR BABY...VERY GRRRR-... Hessian: Shut up Minion.... Minion: You don't gotta have that gauntlet shoved so far up your ass all the time you know... Hessian: Hint? Well........the secret members are Tyler Nelson and The Phantom Booker Shanda: Um...riiight. Well, I see that our time is running to an end. Any final thoughts Hessian? Minion's talking priveledges are over. Hessian: Well if the Throwbacks see this and I'm sure they will, and for anyone else who cares, as they stand now...things are going to get far.....far worse for them. As for the Cultist situation, expect the unexpected...The Cult will dominate the tag division...the stable scene...the Throwbacks...the OSW...it's only a matter of time. Shanda: Well, thank you for your time Hessian and good luck in your endeavors within the OSW.
