Spotlight: Rhett Locke

11 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanda: Hello OSW! It's me, Shanda Russell here with another Wrestler Spotlight! I'm here with OSW's International Champion: Rhett Locke! Rhett, thanks so much for the interview!! Locke: Sure, anytime I suppose. Shanda: So, if I may ask, what do you think of the OSW so far? Locke: What's there to f*cking like? We have a whore running the damn place, who gets paid for what she does best and never pays me for what I do best. . .I could rant forever, but I doubt you'd want to go through the horror of such. Shanda: Um...o..okay...if you could just curb the language though.... Locke: Yeah, sure. Shanda: Yes, so, anyway. I noticed in your biography you mentioned something about heroes, and that you are not a hero....what exactly would you consider yourself then? Locke: . . .Anything but a hero. I'm certainly not someone that you'd consider you're child's favorite wrestler, or model merchandise after. I'd consider myself. . .the exact opposite. Shanda: Interesting....speaking of that. Here in the OSW we have Communists and we have Power mongers, we have Monsters and we have Thugs, amongst all of these well defined areas, what is your niche within the OSW? Is there any group within the OSW you dislike or perhaps like more than another? Locke: My niche? The outcast. You don't exactly see me getting along well with anybody, nor do you see me actually around anyone when I have to be. I'm the nomad, I'm the loner. As for your second question, to be perfectly honest, I hate all of them. Shanda: Even me? Locke: Especially you. Shanda: Uh..yes..well..uh..ehem...how does it feel to be the first International Champion? Locke: Like I'm adding ten pounds to my waist-line, defending the strap at some wench's will, and having to fend off some psycho in the process. Yep, a real bundle of fun for the entire family. Shanda: Yes..well..speaking of psychos, you have been having a heated battle with Corg, particularly this Saturday you may be able to set the stipulation for your match at the next PPV, Head on Collision, any thoughts on Corg? Locke: I like his taste, but I hate his guts. His mind seems to tick the same way mine does, but our way of dealing with it seems to differ greatly. Corg is merely the thorn in my side. It's only a matter of time before I remove him from my life, and everyone else's. Shanda: Any idea on a stipulation you're interested in? Locke: As long as I am able to get my hands on him, Shanda, I don't care if the stipulation is the first one to die a horrible death. Shanda: Yes...colorful...well, what are your future goals in the OSW? Locke: Honestly, I don't have any true goals. I'm kind of tied up to the point my future is the last thing I'm thinking about, as ironic as it sounds for any professional wrestler. Shanda: Fascinating. Any last thoughts? Locke: Hehehe, no. Shanda: Well, thank you so much Rhett. And good luck on your future endeavors!! Locke: And you.
