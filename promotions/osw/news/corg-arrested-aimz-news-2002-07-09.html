Corg Arrested & Aimz News

9 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After the events of Slam! involving Corg's stabbing Amy Campbell, who's been a constant victim of his violent ways since prior to the Ascension Pay-Per-View, the OSW Cruiserweight Champion was arrested and put into temporary incarceration by Tulsa authorities. Chief of police for the city of Tulsa made a public statement regarding the arrest late Sunday afternoon in an open press conference regarding the stipulations surrounding the situation. "'Corg' was restrained and later incarcerated that evening, immediately after assaulting Ms. Campbell with the knife that had made several appearances in his prior terrorizing of the young woman," said Police Chief Henry Wilson. "Reno Davis had to be restrained and put into temporary confinement for his and the culprit's safety after lunging at Mr. Raiyn uttering threats to 'Rip his pasty (expletive) face clean off.' but was released very shortly afterwords. Raiyn is very little more than a walking fluke, using loop holes to avoid punishment throughout his lifetime but this time he will not live without consequence from the justice system. These sort of actions, especially in the public's eye, are absolutely not acceptable by any means...Miss Campbell is quite expectedly pressing charges finally, after enduring weeks upon weeks of torment at the hands of 'Corg' and, on a more personal note, I think it's a safe bet that any law enforcement officers present when Mr. Davis finally catches up to Raiyn will 'mysteriously' not see a thing." Along with this statement from police, there was the first glimmer of positive news regarding Aimz in weeks. Doctor (Insert name here) had high hopes and higher promises. "She's doing very well so far. The knife glanced off of the outside of her rib cage, just under her left arm. It's only a miracle that things turned out how they did, right now she's suffering from the obvious flesh wounds, including six stitches on the outside and a small procedure to help speed the process of healing. Ripped and torn muscles aside, the main wounds are to her mental state. She's in a very bad, vunerable state of mind at the moment, but friends and immediate family have taken the extra step to visit around the clock, which only seems to be doing her good...she seems to despise being idle, and likes the needles even less, so it's a basic guarantee she'll do anything possible to speed the healing process. For two or three weeks, her left arm will be, or at least SHOULD be in a sling to keep from straining the torn muscles in the pectoral and under-arm area. As in most cases of injuries sustained by professional atheletes, Amy appears to be healing MUCH faster than the average person and should be able to return to in-ring action safely within three weeks. Again, I can't stress enough how lucky she was not to have been put our for months, or even forever, with a serious injury, as is with most stabbing cases. The wound was mild at best, her life and career were basically saved, by a few inches, mind you, by the men who jumped at Corg at that very last crucial moment." High hopes for Amy Campbell, we'll see what Aimz herself has to say later in the week. A court date has yet to be set, but we'll keep you posted as things in this violent and generally atrocious situation progresses.
