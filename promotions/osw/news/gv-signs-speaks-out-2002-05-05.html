GV Signs, Speaks Out

5 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OSW officials have confirmed that they have signed a working agreement with volatile superstar, Graphic Violence. "We got the deal done last night right after Slam," said OSW Commissioner, Ed. "He's a nasty son-of-a-bitch, but the problems he brings with him should be countered by the ratings swell that accompany him." In related news, Graphic Violence spoke out on a syndicated wrestling program, about his reason behind attacking and ultimately costing Rhett Locke his OSW International Title on Slam. "That (expletive) Lillith interfered in my business at Head On Collision," seethed Graphic Violence. "Now I am going to destroy everyone and everything close to that pale piece of dog (expletive) until I make her realize her strung out ass should have stayed giving blow jobs after marathon Dungeons and Dragons sessions to kids who call themselves Ezekial the Dark back in what even asswad of a town she comes from." Graphic Violence will make his official debut as a member of the OSW roster next Saturday night on Slam, where he will talk on Rhett Locke. We will bring you reaction throughout the week from fans and the roster alike regarding the signing of GV.
