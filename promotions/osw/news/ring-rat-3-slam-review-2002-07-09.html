Ring Rat #3: Slam Review

9 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello sweeties and sweetetes, Well Slam! has come and gone. It was possibly the most filled Slam! to date. Three main events. First was Sebastian Toomes facing off against what was originally thought to be the dastardly Howard Porter, but was replaced by the evil yet hulking mass of muscle known as Armour. Wooo, what a piece of... well, nevermind that. The next was Ivan Stanislav vs. El Drago in a Hell in a Cell. The rematch from last week that left both men in the hospital. Then lastly Rhett Locke is our new World Champion and a much more family morals champion after beating Tom Walczak. Now, these were the main course of this delicate dish of an event however I'm here to talk about the appetizers. The Throwbacks. These two true heros are definitely the ones who make Old School Wrestling old school. These are the Dusty Rhodeses, the Gorgous Georges, the Dory Funks of today. "Classic" Clay Vendetta and "Old School" Dave Gibson are what wrestling is all about. It's about two men getting in the ring to prove that their the best. It's about being crowned champion because you went through hundreds, maybe thousands of matches and have come out the winner. It's about upsets and injuries. It's about careers and strategy. It's about wrestling. It's all about the wrestling. But somewhere every fan forgot that. In came the cages. Out went the technicians. In came the pyro. Out went the ring announcer. In came the weapons. Out went the rules. Somewhere along the line people hated the meaning in wrestling and loved the crap. Everyone wanted the blood without the sweat or the tears. But there are fighters. Men such as Dave Gibson and Clay Vendetta. Men who remember that they're in this to prove that they're great and not to beat up fans. Men who want to win titles and not hurt everyone they see. Men who want to win and not to insult everyone who isn't them. Wrestlers of old were men who gave up everything to wrestle and become the best. They made money that they had no time to use. They had children that they couldn't play with. They had wives that they couldn't be with. They had nothing and loved it. Clay Vendetta and Dave Gibson are who I look up to in OSW. They're who my son Jake looks up to in OSW. And they're who everyone out there should look up to in OSW. You can think that Ed is funny. You can think that the Think Tank is maniacal. You can think Sebastian Toomes is egotistical. But you need to remember that without men like CCV and "Old School" they'd just be a comedian, professors, and a cutie living in a cardboard box. And Vendetta and Gibson would be family men. So next time The Throwbacks wrestle The Cult just cheer a bit louder, love them a bit more, and think a bit harder. Just remember what you're watching. Men like "Old School" Dave Gibson, "Classic" Clay Vendetta, and my ex-husband all deserve your respect. Until next time honey... Behave! -"The Ring Rat" Janet Cads
