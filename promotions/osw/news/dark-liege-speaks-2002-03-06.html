Dark Liege Speaks

6 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Reporters had a rare chance to speak to the Dark Liege and try to shed some light on just exactly who he is. "What makes you think that you're so important that I'm just going to tell you exactly who I am," said the 7'0 tall, robed, mystery fiend. "You'll find out when I want you to find, which may or may not be soon." Dark Liege did not stop there. "But I'm glad to see that there are some people who are smart enough to put the more important matters first. Meaning many people are worried about the wrong things. Things like who kidnapped who, who will be Vampir's partner or even who will be the OSW World Champion. Those things pale in comparison to me. Because when I reveal myself to the world things will never be the same again, and you will agree that it was worth the wait unlike other suprises." The Dark Liege and Lennox Lanthrop will take on Vampir Nosferatu and a Mystery Partner this Sunday at Crowning Glory.
