OSW — Column

Crazy Canuck #2

23 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well boys, the Crazy F’n Canuck is back with a look at the current a-happenings in the federation that is Old School Wrestling. I was happy to attend my first OS Dub show in Toronto a few weeks back and I think it’s aboot time we brought the federation to Canada. I mean, we sell out quicker than most Pay-Per-Views here! Well, my Leafs have made it to the Semi-Finals but our frickin’ coach had a heart attack or something, but let’s not talk about that, let’s get on to the new column in addition to my Pay-Per-View Predictions that I like to call the… Rhetorical Questions Is it time to stick the fork into the Red Army? I mean, c’mon, they’re done! Ivan is doing his own thing at the moment, going around and killing Reno Davis and all while Walzcak is getting a well-deserved Main Event push. Krieg seems to be the only one interested in preserving the Army itself by trying to find additional members and the like. Considering Walzcak and Stanislav are the Upper-Carders of the team, I doubt Krieg is going to be able to hold up the fort while Ivan and Tom are busy in their own feuds. Although Krieg will probably be focusing on El Drago, I wouldn’t be surprised to see him helping out Ivan or Tom. Not likely the other way around. Speaking of stables, 3B=1B? Well, they really weren’t a stable since before the Pay-Per-View anyways. It was just Drew and Frankie as a tag team, occasionally cleaning up for Switchblade. Now it seems Frankie has lost it and wants a piece of his ole’ buddy Drew. I think Switchblade is going to need some new back up and soon. Hmmm…. Civil Disobedience maybe? These guys hinted toward a heel turn in their last RP and personally, this Canuck thinks that the three would be PERFECT for each other. Is it just me, or is Alan Kriegman the perfect fit for the Vax? Think about it. Who else would lose his hair, other than the balding sensation himself? This guy has history with Porter and all the Primetime-marks would love to see Ed and him go a few rounds. He’s an evil Super-Genius so his more than smart enough to reprogram iCybie. This guy is great for the role in my mind and I’d be more than happy to see him on the little rabbit-ears black-and-whiter again. (Editor's Note: Alan Kriegman died in PCW. Get with the program) Is Griff actually worth that much money or is it just a way to make Clay Vendetta look credible? I mark out for Clay and want him to make a run for the International Title as some of you know, but that CCW Invasion didn’t really make him look very good. Actually, it just made CCW look worse. Clay is definite need of someway to make him look credible an squashing Truxell will do it. Shouldn’t Locke and GV be feuding in the UFC? When these guys have a match, it’s going to be deadly. I mean, who the hell thinks that when these two get another match together it will end in a hand shake without blood, broken bones or near-death experiences? These guys have the hottest feud right now and I have a feeling before long it’s going to get booked for Ascension and turn into one bloody mess. Did Kanaida just KILL his career in OSW or what I mean, this guy is in an alright feud, then all of a sudden, boom! Loses to a security guard! I mean, c’mon! What is this aboot? As for Axtell, well he’s probably got a chance to go somewhere now. The guy just started himself a pretty good career, downing a UWA Legend like that. The kid may actually go somewhere, eh? Da Westsidaz are in an angle that has lasted more than a week?! God, someone pinch me! The last angles Da Westsidaz were in were Black Attack and Parental Advisory. Sure they feuded with people, but not REAL feuds. They actually seem to be taking this seriously, thankfully. But they sure got their work cut out for them. I don’t really see who has the one-up in the feud, considering they always even it out each show. Heresian Bloodlust are my favorites in this feud and I see a match with either Da Westsidaz or Civil Disobedience challenging the Tag Champs for the gold. Doug won the title? Is this legit or is just a ploy to hold the title until SeaBass gets up to par? Lately Switchy has been blowing it inside and outside the ring, so I think Doug, although I love the guy, is just filler. I hope the DAMN member holds it for a looooong time, but sadly, I can’t see it happening. So this is the Canuck proudly signing out. I’m off to watch Dana Carvey kick some ass on Celebrity Boxing, so I’ll see all you Yanks, Brits, Scotts and non Canadians in a few weeks for my PPV predictions! GOD BLESS CANADA!
