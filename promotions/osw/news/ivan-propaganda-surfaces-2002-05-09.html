Ivan Propaganda Surfaces

9 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After OSW’s Pay Per View, “Head on Collision,” Ivan Stanislav lost a tremendous amount of fans that he had recently acquired. Afterwards on SLAM, Stanislav only increased the amount of animosity towards him by violently attacking OSW Superstar Aimz and issuing and ultimatum to Reno Davis. The Russian Government, aware that the number of Stanislav fans have drastically dropped, have engaged in a campaign in order to restore the fans that Stanislav once had. As shown on the left, in true old school propagandistic fashion, is a poster portraying The Russian Bear as a man of the people and a hero of the working class. This is the first of many posters that have been not only circulating throughout Russia but also making their way into Europe and The United States. “These posters are only showing the people of the world what type of a man Ivan Stanislav is!” Commented Alexei Ruslan, one of the major proponents of such a move, “Ivan Stanislav has always been a working man and will always fight for the people! After they realize this and apologize to him for turning their back on him he will truly be happy.” Stanislav, however, had a different take on the entire situation. When shown the poster he was stunned, as if never having seen this particular poster before. While some thing he just feigned ignorance, other analysts truly believe this was a surprise to him as he hurriedly took the poster to study and left. Is Stanislav aware of this movement within Russia? Is this a ploy by the Government to increase their hold on the Russian Bear after he almost slipped out of their fingers during the “Tempest Ordeal?” Or is he just tricking us into trusting him once again? More news of these posters, as well as any new ones appearing, as our investigation continues.
