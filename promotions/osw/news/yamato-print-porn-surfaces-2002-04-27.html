Yamato Print Porn Surfaces

27 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Nude photographs of Queen of Extreme competitor, Bethany Yamato, were sent to each and every OSW superstar's hotel room late Friday night, apparently lending credibility to previous rumors that Yamato has a past in the pornography industry. "The pictures are quite tasteful and artsy," said a winded Ed, when reached for comment in his hotel room. "Yes, very, very tasteful." Other OSW workers, who were awake, were quick to condemn Yamato. "The whore probably planted them herself to help her gain support from the men on the roster," said Lillith. "Next time I will cut more than just her face." Propaganda's manager, Eric Sagar, also condemned the photos. "She is the tool of the devil," screeched Sagar, via telephone. "The disease spreading whore tempting the most depraved of sinners!" The photos appear actually some kind of advertisement for what appears to be a Japanese sex club due to the Japanese writing found on the back of them, were delivered over night to each OSW worker's room and slipped beneath the door in plain, manilla envelopes. OSW officials say they have no idea who is behind the distribution of the photos. "Right now, we are questioning all members of the roster, as they are the only ones who could have known exactly what doors to slide the envelopes beneath," said OSW Security Director, Moose Malone. "We will catch whoever is responsible by this time tomorrow. You can count on it!" Hotel officials confirmed no non-OSW affiliated guests received the photos. They also indicated they were checking security photo tapes, but admitted that some of their video tape machines have not worked properly for some time. We will bring you reaction from other members of the OSW roster later on today, and continue to monitor this story for breaking news.
