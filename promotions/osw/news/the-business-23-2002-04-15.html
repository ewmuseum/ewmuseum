The Business #23

15 April 2002

The Business #23 by Robbie Decker Greetings, plebes, and welcome once again to the most enlightening... and longest running... column in PTC, The Business. It's been a wild week in OSW, so no time for chit-chat; let's get down to it. SCARY AT THE TOP: Now that my workload has died down, I've been taking to making appearances on Duke Williams' PTC Malice Saturday nights; not surprisingly, ratings go through the roof every time I'm on. Last week Phantom Booker decided to embarass me by asking me questions on a bunch of worthless federations... but a week back, I was right on my game. The subject? Sebastian Toomes as OSW World Champion. It seems like there are a lot of insiders... Duke included... who simply don't think that Toomes can hold the title for an extended period of time. I say these people are idiots (not like I haven't told Duke that already or anything; after all, his company is putting straps on anyone with a pulse these days). Fact of the matter is, the ruling core at the top of OSW is quite possibly the strongest batch of superstars PTC has ever seen, and has all the ingredients to be the next Media: a stable that could have its way with the federation and dominate the competition. Let's look at the ingredients: 3B: Brooklyn Born and Bred; Hostyle and Frankie Bones give the team a little muscle. Every regime needs its foot soldiers; these two are some of the toughest in OSW and provide street cred for an otherwise elitist group. Jane Reagan: The power and the money, need I say more? Reagan's the top power player in the PTC world and it shows. By aligning herself with the top superstars and pulling all the strings, Reagan's assembled a force to be reckoned with. Absolute power (and a thirst for revenge) seem to have corrupted Jane absolutely... but hey, the ends justify the means, right? Meanstreak: Every group needs a Sherman tank; Meanstreak, on the other hand, isn't just a tank; he's an armed nuke. The trick with Meanstreak is to see if you can get him to explode in the enemy camp and not your own. So far so good, especially since he's hunting the Red Army and Ivan Stanislav. If the feud with Stanislav dies down, though, Meanstreak's gonna need a hobby... and my money says that's when things implode... when Meanstreak wants a shot at the title. To harness Meanstreak, Reagan's going to have to feed him a steady stream of tough competition. Sebastian "Switchblade" Toomes: And finally, the man with the gold. Toomes, coming from CCW, has really stepped up his game to the top level. He's not the biggest guy out there, but he's got plenty of muscle to watch it back, and with his explosiveness and quickness, can get the job done between the ropes. Sure, he's got a massive ego... I called this guy the "creme de la creme" in public a few weeks back and he lets me know it everytime I see him (I swear I want to whack him for it)... but right now, he's a force to be reckoned with. SNIFF... SMELL THAT? HOME-GROWN: I'm very impressed with the booking on OSW when it comes to the titles. I really feel PCW and some other feds have shot themselves in the foot in the past months when it came to put too much importance on major free-agent signings. With our champs, you've got a fresh talented name in Rhett Locke, a guy who's really stepped his game up and made himself a star in Toomes, and tag champions that have come from the PCW/PWA/NTICW system in Horrendous Moniker... whoops, I mean Heresian Bloodlust (have I mentioned how much I hate that name?) The names who have supposed to have been big have flamed out in a hurry. Great Dragon, Magi, Trashcan Man, I'm talking to you... oh, wait, no I'm not, you've been fired already. It's a very simple formula; guys who have worked their way up in the system obviously have the drive and determination to hang around and be major players. Reward loyalty instead of punishing the guys who are working hard. THIS WEEK'S SIGN THAT THE APOCALYPSE IS UPON PCW: Stryfe has been re-signed. That's right... ARRRRRRRGH Stryfe. (That's infamous company he shares only with Slade.) It's criminal that Stryfe has had a PCW World Title reign... and Boda's had two... while Big Alan Steele has yet to hold the strap. If he's not champ by the end of the summer (barring injury) I'd say we've got this little rivalry ours to rights. MARYLAND'S STEVE BLAKE SHOULD BE RUN OVER BY AN 18-WHEELER: That's all I have to say about that, really. Yeah, I'm still bitter. Will be until the games next year. Deal with it. SET TO COLLIDE: It's still a couple of weeks away, but here are three early things to look for at Head-On Collision: 1) If Civil Disobedience can't kill Team Annoying, I'm bringing a Magnum to ringside and offing those two bastards myself. 2) Up against Meanstreak with a bad back? Ed's gonna be deader than... well, his career already was. 3) I like sumo, and I hate Kid Wonder, so I'm gonna love watching the opening match of the night. I plan on bringing plenty of Aunt Jemima, 'cause Kimbusa's gonna make teenybopper pancakes. THE BUSINESS' AWARDS SHEET FOR MARCH, 2002 The Business' Impact Player Award (as voted on by the fans): Sebastian "Switchblade" Toomes. Toomes is the perfect choice for the first OSW Impact Player award. Making his mark by grabbing the first OSW championship, Toomes has really been the shining star he claims to be... so far. Robbie Decker's Employee of the Month Award (my choice): Though I've been a huge admirer of Toomes' work, I don't like to give the EotM Award to guys who have won the Impact Player award, so my Employee of the Month honor is gonna go to Ivan Stanislav. Speaking as a wrestling fan, he's absolutely pussied out. But as an objective wrestling columnist (yeah, that's me) Stanislav has pulled a face turn with aplomb. Since I came into the PTC fold a year ago, I've never seen a guy show as much dramatic improvement and hard work as Stanislav, and he's doing it again. That's it for this week's edition of The Business... until next time... Hating you all, ---Robbie Decker
