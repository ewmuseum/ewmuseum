OSW Facing Lawsuit

25 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Reports out of Scranton-Wilkesbarre, PA, have a potential legal storm brewing in the wake of this past Monday’s charity house show. Rupert Marshetta, Wilkesbarre resident and avid OSW fan, was injured during the course of the Uprising/American Ronin match through what seemed to be accidental contact. The occurrence happened when AR member “The Legendary” Jeffrey Wayne executed a springboard hurricanrana on Uprising’s Poet, sending the man tumbling over the barricade and into the crowd, where he struck Marshetta full-on, sending both to the concrete floor in a heap. It was several minutes before medical personnel arrived to assist him. Now resting at home with what has been diagnosed as a bruised kidney and a strained back, Rupert has announced that he will be filing suit against those responsible for his injury, mainly the team of Wayne and Matt Register, and Old School Wrestling for sponsoring the event. He has also hired high-profile ambulance chaser Johnny Cochran to represent him for the case. “This man is a hard-working, honest, good natured coal miner, who can’t do his job anymore because he went to go see wrestling,” said Cochran of his client. “How can he not seek recompense for the crimes committed against him? When he was struck by a man, the (expletive) hit the fan!” Cochran and his associates are currently investigating what, if any, role OSW may have in the incident. Neither member of American Ronin will comment on the matter. Their attorney, Shyster McDadi from the firm of McDadi & Dadimac, has made the following statement: “While my clients sympathize with Mr. Marshetta for his current situation, they are not at fault for it.” According to hospital officials in Scranton, neither member of Uprising is available for comment at this time. We’ll give you more news on this situation as it becomes available.
