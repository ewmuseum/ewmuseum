QoE Ladies Ready

29 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Each of the eight ladies involved in OSW's Queen of Extreme Tournament are preparing for tomorrow night's first task, which will force each young lady to cut a live segment during the telecast of Saturday Night Slam! Several of the competitors are quite confident about tomorrow night's festivites. "I've been here many times before, and will be there again," said former PCW starlet, Brandi Wilson. "I feel this is an area where I have a huge edge over my competitors." Bethany Yamato, who seemed equally ready, had an entirely different view on tomorrow night. "I have been in prison, in the most dehumanizing, defeminizing conditions on earth," said Yamato. "I think if I made it through that, I can make it through a 120 second segment." Amanda also showed she is brimming with confidence. "The camera loves me, just as everyone out there in TV land loves me," said Amanda. "I was practically born for the camera." Some of the other competitors, however, are not so experience in front of the camera. Lilith, for instance, refused to be interviewed, merely hissing at reporters, while Anna Keyson and Laura Wilson have never worked on a nationally televised program. Who will step up and who will be cast aside? Check out all the action from the Queen of Extreme competition this week on Slam!
