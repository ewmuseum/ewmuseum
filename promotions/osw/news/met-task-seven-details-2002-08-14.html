MET Task Seven Details

14 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MET Task Seven was listed as a time based challenge, but only in terms of breaking a tie. Once again, OSW officials have decided to test the OSW knowledge of the participants. The questions posed are below. 1. Which of the following HAVE NOT been under OSW contract? Lennox Lanthrop, Lance Knight, SLR, Scott Naket. 2. Which OSW superstar was born in Afghanistan? 3. Which female wrestler or wrestlers have received a World Title shot in OSW? 4. Name 15 foreign born wrestlers who have appeared on an OSW telecast. 5. Who were the ORIGINAL members of the Think Tank? 6. Ivan Stanislav is afraid of what animal? 7. Name all the wrestlers of African American descent that have appeared on OSW television. 8. Name all of the female wrestlers who have appeared on OSW television. 9. Tom Walczak is know for having an abnormal feature regarding his anatomy. What is this feature? 10. Name the wrestlers of Mexican descent who have appeared during an OSW telecast. Teams receive two points for each correct answer. The team which answers the most questions correctly gets five bonus points. Answers are due no later than Friday at 11:59 pm EST, and should be emailed to [detail withheld]
