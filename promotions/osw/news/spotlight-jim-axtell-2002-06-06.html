Spotlight: Jim Axtell

6 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanda: Hello OSW! I'm here with OSW's own Jim Axtell! Jim, how are you today? Axtell: I'm good, Shanda. A little banged up. Shanda: Yeah, I was going to say, a bit of a change going from security to wrestler, huh? Axtell: Most definitely. These guys aren't messing around out there, as I have found out first hand in my first two excursions into the wrestling ring. Shanda: It would appear that Kory Storm, another fresh talent in the OSW, has made a personal vendetta against you. Any thoughts on that? Axtell: I don't know what his problem is exactly. I can only assume that he is an egotistical jackass who is jealous of anyone else getting some attention other than himself. He has proven one thing to me for sure, that he is a coward. Shanda: So, how do you plan on taking care of him? He's attacked you already. Is there retaliation in the works? Axtell: Retaliation isn't really my game, Shanda. If Storm wants to meet face to face, man to man, then we can get it on and see who's better. Shanda: So, after being somewhat flung into the OSW, what are your plans now? Axtell: I'm just go out there and do what I've done my whole life, and that's give it 110% and don't quit. If I lose, I lose, but no one can ever say that I didn't give it my all. Shanda: I must say that you appear to have a more respectable way of handling your opponents. But I must ask, how can you defend yourself from more ruthless opponents? Men like Graphic Violence, Frankie Bones, and Ivan Stanislav and his Red Army don't seem to care about the 'good fight.' How do you plan on living up to their attacks, if any should come your way? Axtell: I don't give up, Shanda. I have a will power stronger than any Graphic Violence or Red Army out there. They may be able to beat me, but they will never break me and eventually I will come out on top. I don't back down from anyone, and whether they believe in the "good fight" or not doesn't matter, because I believe in it and will always fight it no matter what. Shanda: That's a very positive and admirable outlook, Mr. Axtell. So, are there any opponents in particular you are looking to defeat? And what do you think about the upcoming event at Ascension? Axtell: Obviously Kory Storm is first and foremost on my mind. He blindsided me with a sledgehammer for cryin out loud. He will have to answer for that cowardly attack sooner or later. There are others out there that have the same attitude as Storm does, such as Griff Truxell and Kanaida. People that think they can just slide by on their name alone. I've worked damn hard for what I have and am proud of it. As for Ascension, it looks like an extremely dangerous and unpredictable match. I'm just looking to make a good showing and work hard like I always do. Shanda: Well Jim Axtell, do you have any final thoughts? Perhaps for Kory Storm? Axtell: I just want him to know that my wife is the most important thing in my life, and I don't appreciate the way he talks about her. When we step in the ring next time you can bet his comments will be a driving force behind me. Ask Kanaida what I was like, Storm, and then multiply that times ten. You've given me extra incentive with your actions and an extra special "Ax to Grind". Shanda: Well ladies and gentlemen! There ya have it! Guard turned wrestler Jim Axtell is in the OSW for sure and it’s gonna take more than a sledgehammer to keep him away!!
