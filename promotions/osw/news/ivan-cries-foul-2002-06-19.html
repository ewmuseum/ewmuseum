Ivan Cries Foul

19 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Upon hearing that if he lost his upcoming match on Saturday Slam he would have to pay for ZaC, the monkey’s doctor bills, Ivan Stanislav instantly exploded in a tirade backstage. “I will not pay for some stupid animals doctor bills! That little monkey had it coming, and I simply did world a favor by shortening it’s life span!” Stanislav’s attack on the chimpanzee ZaC, which occurred roughly three weeks ago, was a direct result of what some call a ‘war of attrition’ between the two men. Both men have systematically attacked key area’s of each others life, from Ivan’s lost love Tempest to Davis’ best friend ZaC which was later followed by Stanislav flipping an entire car on The Unwanted Angel. Stanislav has a double header lined up n the form of first Saturday Slam, in which he must team up with El Drago on the upcoming Slam to battle Davis and Red Army friend Michel Krieg after orders from Howard Porter were issued last Slam, and then battle Reno Davis inside of the mountain at Ascension. Will Ivan Stanislav have to swallow his ideological pride and pay Reno Davis Saturday Night? Will Ivan Stanislav get his much wanted revenge on Davis Sunday night? Or will Reno Davis once again switch through the rampaging Russian’s fingers? Tune in on Saturday and order OSW Ascension and find out!!
