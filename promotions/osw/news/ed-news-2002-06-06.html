Ed News

6 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Former OSW commissioner, Ed, alleged perpatrator of numerous crimes under the guise of the Vax, has been transfered from the Winnipeg Police Department to a Manitoba Federal Prison. "This has no bearing on Ed's guilt or innocence," said Winnipeg Chief of Police Ernie Bostwick. "We just feel due to the heinous nature of the crimes Ed is charged with, the Manitoba Prison is the best place for him to be until his trial gets underway." Ed was unavailable to comment on this situation, but stay tuned for further new updates and tune into SLAM! this Saturday, live from the Key Arena in Seattle, for more on this story.
