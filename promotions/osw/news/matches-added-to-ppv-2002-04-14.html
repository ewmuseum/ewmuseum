Matches Added to PPV

14 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OSW officials announced that they have added two matches of mega proportions to Head On Collision on April 28th, as Meanstreak will take on Commissioner Ed while the massive sumo wrestler Kimbusa will face off with Kid Wonder. Ed, who is battling back from an injured back, has promised victory over Meanstreak, not for himself, but for the fans. Kid Wonder challenged Kimbusa last night on Slam, a challenge the big man was quick to answer. "Hai," grunted Kimbusa. "I will crush that gaijin like the blond haired bug that he is, then scrape his remains from my buttocks with his woman's tongue." This Saturday night, we may get a preview of the pay per view, as Kimbusa and Kid Wonder will step into the same ring this Saturday night on Slam, as part of a four corners, intergender tag team contest tied in with the Queen of Extreme Competition. Kimbusa will pair with Bethany Yamato, will Kid Wonder will team with his girlfriend Amanda. We will bring you more on these boiling feuds as news breaks.
