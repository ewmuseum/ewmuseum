Post PPV Injury Report

29 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OSW officials held a press briefing early Monday morning to highlight several injuries that occured at Head On Collision. Epitome of Evil had to be hospitalized after being attacked by Jack Miller during his brutal matchup with Nick Casey. The extent of EoE's injuries are unknown, as is the length of time he will be on the shelf. Ed has a minor rupture of he fifth disk in his back. While the injury will not keep him off OSW television, it will keep him out of active wrestling for another four to eight weeks. 'Bad Attitude' Jesse Ramey was severely injured when a load of brick fell upon him courtesy of the mysterious Vax during the four on four street fight. Ramey suffered three broken ribs, a broken forearm, and a grade three concussion. He is expected to miss three to six months. Nick Casey, Lillith, Kid Wonder, Kimbusa and now-eliminated Queen of Extreme competitor, Bethany Yamato, all needed multiple stitches to close wounds sustained during their brutal matchups. All but Yamato, who is not currently under contract, were given this week off from in ring activity, and will be evaluated later in the week. OSW officials indicated that 'things were still early' and conditions, status' and the number of wresters injured could change following the Pay Per View. We will bring an update on the injury front as news rolls in.
