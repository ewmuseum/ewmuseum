Tempest Working Overtime

16 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shortly after her return to the OSW, Tempest has found herself with the unenviable task of facing three of the OSW’s most talented competitors for a shot at the OSW World title in the main event on Slam, the very night after what is sure to be a grueling match with CapCW’s Paul Cain. Tempest entered the hotly contested Golden Turnbuckle Tournament and then found herself booked in the second of two high profile matches, last week gaining a shot at the OSW’s Cruiserweight championship. “For once, Tempest’s energy is all being used,” said manager Miyuki Matsudaira. “Tempest’s training schedule is even more rigorous before. She’s going to have to do a show every Friday and every Saturday night until the end of the tournament, assuming she continues in it. And she doesn’t want to disappoint anyone in the OSW or the tournament either.” When asked about the situation with Ivan Stanislav, Matsudaira said, “Ivan hasn’t been in contact with Tempest and so she can’t count on him for any moral support. I just hope she can handle all the pressure.” We’ll bring you more on this story as it develops.
