KW Blames Yamato/Kimbusa

3 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"There's no doubt in my mind about it." That's what OSW superstar Kid Wonder told the popular online wrestling sheet, Wrestlingtattler.com, Friday afternoon in regards to the riots that recently took place in his hometown of Orlando, Florida. "Why else would the fatasses, whores, and Japs be singled out?" asked Wonder. "These people, the majority of which are my fans, were just reacting to the travesty that occured at Head on Collision Sunday night. They reacting my going off on Kimbusa and Bethany's own kind." Kid Wonder went on to make more claims and disparaging remarks towards the two, but asked that they not be published to avoid any slander lawsuits that, quote, "the two bitches might try to pull out of their ass." When asked of what he's gonna do: "I'm gonna rip them apart," he said. "I don't care if they're both bigger than me. They've pissed me off to the point to which they can't stop me. I don't care how long it takes. I'm gonna get back at both of them if it's the last thing I do." We'll bring you more on the situation as it develops.
