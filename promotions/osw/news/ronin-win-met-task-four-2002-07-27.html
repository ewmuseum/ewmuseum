Ronin Win MET Task Four

27 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The American Ronin keep rolling along, picking up another victory and 10 points in Task Four, to take a commanding 16 point lead over All About the Benjamins in OSW's Most Extreme Team Competition. The task, which included cutting live promos on OSW television, saw American Ronin impress guest judge Riptah of Da Westsidaz. "They was phat, with a capital P," said Riptah, from his hospital bed. "Awwwwee yeaaaah!" Total Violation took second place, while Redemption Song took third place, picking up six and three points respectively. Task four also saw the elimination of the Black Dragons and Guardians of the Ring. OSW officials announced that Task Five would be a trivia based challenge with a time based element, leaving teams with less than 24 hours to answer questions relating to OSW. The official questions for the task appear "sometime Sunday" according to OSW officials.
