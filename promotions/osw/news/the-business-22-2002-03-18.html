OSW — Column

The Business #22

18 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
When I chose to walk away from PCW to join upstart Old School Wrestling; giving away a cushy paycheck for an unknown quantity; I was a little worried. Nervous, even. But then I heard three small words that convinced me that I had made the right move: "Boda, World Champion". Like an executive selling his Enron stock at it's peak; like a traveler deciding to stay home and not use his ticket on the Titanic; I've made out like a bandit once again. Let's face it here; Tyler Nelson's going down faster than the Shoot King's mom on a Baltimore streetcorner, and I'm loving every minute of watching him and PCW squirm. Yes, I certainly enjoyed his paychecks; those big, fat, monetary bundles of appreciation; but it didn't take the last check bouncing to convince me that I needed out. After all, I WAS PCW's premier, all-access member of the press. I saw the mounds of crap they had cluttering up the roster and I knew that the company simply wasn't going to be drawing money or names. And when those three words hit, I knew it was the end of PCW's world as we know it; and baby, like Michael Stipe, I feel fine. Meanwhile, not only am I once again in Business, Jane Reagan, that beautiful, beautiful woman, has made me the undisputed king of the wrestling media. When "Don't Resucitate" Doug Troy decided to celebrate his midlife crisis by getting his ass kicked over and over again (what else is new?) Jane tapped exactly the right man to get behind the mic; and though I've got to put up with Nate Dumbass (who, may I add, could NEVER have beaten me when we were both in our primes) and Steven "The Queer Queen" Smith every week on Slam!, the sheer prestige of being the best color commentator in the business... and the GIGANTIC paychecks I'm seeing every week... more than make up for what I have to deal with. And besides; I've been telling that inbred hick Duke Williams for weeks now that his illiterate ass couldn't hold a candle to my journalistic skills, and now I'm proving it. Suck on that, radio-boy. Life, my friends, is good; and you're in good hands. While PCW's press corps is led by the comatose Shoot King and the supremely credible (scoff)... Boobie Boy; here I am, ensuring you the hardest-hitting columns in wrestling. For those of you unfamiliar? I am Robbie Decker... ... and this is The Business. So Crowning Glory has come and gone, establishing OSW as one of the major players in the wrestling world today. But after the initial honeymoon it's down to brass tacks in a federation trying to stake a claim to being the best in the game. Where do the major players go from here? The Tag Team Division: OSW has avoided the recent tag problems plaguing PCW by being sure to get some top-notch talent in the tag ranks. Most specifically, their ability to convince Heresian Bloodlust to stay in the tag ranks has solidified the roster. (Though I'm irked that they weren't also able to convince HB to come up with a name that doesn't scream "closeted gay twelve-year-olds playing too much Dungeons and Dragons".) Civil Disobedience was one of the few talented teams to come out of PWA (believe me, I helped try to train a lot of them... *that* was an exercise in futility) and having the belts makes them great chase targets simply because it drives everyone crazy that a couple of suburban idiots can actually beat them. Teams like Twisted Metal and 3B also provide some solid push in a small division. The International Division: With a lot of the top-end talent being veteran and recruited, this looks like the place for a lot of OSW's fresh faces to shine. Particularly, I like Corg and Drew Hostyle in this division, with Nick Casey, Clay Vendetta, Scott Naket and some of the darker guys like the Magi being dark horses. What OSW could use in the midcard is a guy like Kid Wonder. "What?" you say. "Don't you hate Kid Wonder?" Well, look at it this way; if you want a field to grow, you add fertilizer. And what's a great fertilizer? Manure! And dropping a load of horsecrap like Kid Wonder right into OSW's midcard would be the perfect way to encourage growth by showing all of OSW's new talent exactly what happens when you repeatedly fail to live up to expectations. The OSW Championship Division: Putting the title on Toomes was an excellent idea by our humble leader, Jane Reagan. He's new, he's fresh, he's not afraid of who's toes he steps on, he's a killer with the ladies; I mean damn, people... he's like a slightly-less-talented version of yours truly. Ivan Stanislav, as always, is an absolute monster and will never be far out of the hunt, and Doug Troy... as much as he's a geriatric waste of time... at least has the credentials (if not the credibility) to put guys over. Timo Bolamba I'm not sure about in the long-term, but if he stays hot on the mic and keeps putting on good matches he's a definite possibility. Wild Cards (Guys who could step up and make a name for themselves): Keep your eyes on Rhett Locke. He's a freak, but I could see him pulling away from the endless succession of dark gimmicks in OSW. Speaking of; I also think that Vampir Nosferatu is ready to take the next step; World Title contention. He as well needs to distance himself from ever other Goth out there. Lead Anchors (Guys who are just taking up space): What the hell has Great Dragon done lately, anyway? Send him back on the next boat to China. Tom Walczak continues to be a Polish pile of suck and worthy of every Pollack joke you can come up with. And last; don't believe the hype when it comes to Meanstreak. He's washed up, and I'm not afraid to say it (as I did on last week's Slam!). Give him six months and he'll be locked up again in the psych ward. With Head-On Collision on the way in April, OSW could catapult itself to "undisputed leader" status in the wrestling world... but only time will tell if the roster is up to the challenge. Until next time, kiddies... Hating you all, ---Robbie Decker, King of the Wrestling Media
