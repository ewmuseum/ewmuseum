Six in Hospital After Slam

7 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OSW officials reported that Sebastian Toomes, Amy Campbell, Ivan Stanislav, El Drago, Michel Krieg and newly crowned OSW World Heavyweight Champion, Rhett Locke, were all hospitalized following this week's broadcast of Slam. Toomes was hospitalized with a grade three concussion, which he suffered at the hands of Howard Porter's hired thug, Armour. Toomes is expected to miss two to four weeks of in ring action. Campbell was admitted and is in stable, but guarded condition, after apparently being stabbed by OSW Cruiserweight champion, Corg. No early word on when, or even if, she will return. Both Stanislav and El Drago were hospitalized after suffering numerous injuries during their 25 minute war inside and outside the confines of the steel cell they battled in. Details are sketchy, but neither are expected to be at Slam next week. Michel Krieg is suffering from dehydration and ematiation after suffering from some kind of food poisoning. Locke had to have water drained from his knee, and may require minor arthroscopic surgery. He is expected to miss two to four weeks of action. The above injury reports are preliminary. OSW officials promised a more detailed injury report within 48 hours. We, of course, will bring updated injury news as it becomes available.
