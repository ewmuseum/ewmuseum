Spotlight: Doug Troy

19 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanda: Hello OSW! I'm Shanda Russell here with another OSW Wrestler Spotlight! My next guest is none other than PCW Legend, Doug Troy! Doug, thank you for giving me this time to interview you! Doug Troy: Hey, it's no problem. Been awhile since I've done a straight interview. Shanda: So far, what would you have to say about the OSW now that you've been here for a few months? Doug Troy: Hmmm... well, I'm undecided right now. While it's nice to see some new guys come up and make names for themselves, I really bugs me to see some of the decisions made by higher-ups that undermine the roster as a whole. I know I already touched on this in my State of OSW Address, but I'm still irked by having the World Title just being given to someone as a gift by the owner. Shanda: Understandable. Your State of the OSW Address was very informative to say the least. Now we know that you are decidedly against Jane Reagan, but what about Ed? In the past you voiced some opposition towards both sides. Has that changed now? Doug Troy: In the past, me and Ed had some differences. That whole 'The Mack' thing especially. However, times change and so do people, and I feel that him and I are starting to see eye-to-eye. However, I do have some problems with decisions he's made, especially when he signed Meanstreak. Looks like the concussions he's had over the years are starting to affect his decision-making skills. Other than that, though, it's nice to have someone like Ed in a position to help rather than hurt wrestlers. Shanda: Interesting. Now, at the first OSW Slam you were an announcer and then you quit the position to wrestle, which was spurred by a then destructive Ivan Stanislav and you fought under the name of the OSW. Since then, Ivan Stanislav seems to be fighting with Ed to bring wrestling back to how it is supposed to be, "for the people," against Jane Reagan. What do you think of that turn around? Doug Troy: To tell you the truth, I'm really not sure what to think. It's really unlike Ivan to stick up for the fans. While I support his hatred of Toomes and Reagan, I trust him about as far as I can throw him. Remember that he's part of the political game, and back-stabbing is the name of the game in politics. Then again, I might just be a complete cynic. Shanda: Fascinating. How about the recent signing of PCW wrestler Kid Wonder? Doug Troy: Ooh boy, I knew this question was coming. Me and Wonder, we've got a helluva history. I'll just forego my comments and give a message to Spinks. Spinks, if you read this, there's only one thing I need to tell you: Anytime, anywhere. Shanda: Some strong words from Doug Troy! Now, Mr. Troy, OSW Head On Collision: Any idea who the masked wrestlers are? Doug Troy: Feh. Shanda: Feh? Doug Troy: Yeah, that's my standard reaction to mystery wrestlers. Feh. Shanda: You're a PCW Legend, and I bet you've seen a lot in your career. What do you miss the most? Doug Troy: Tiffany Jacobs. Shanda: Excuse me? Doug Troy: Tiffany Jacobs. Former MWF and PCW reporter. I used to love driving her crazy whenever she interviewed me. Then the whole Judas Steele thing happened and she disappeared. I wonder whatever happened to her... Shanda: Um, okay. So, where do you see the future of the OSW going? Doug Troy: Who knows? I mean, the sky's the limit, but so's the dirt. The OSW can go up, down, left, right, who knows? It doesn't have the burden of being #1 like PCW does. For PCW, there's only one way to go and that's down. With OSW, though, we could go anywhere and do anything. I'd rather not have Jane Reagan be in charge of the OSW ship, though. She's too petty and slutty. I miss the days when PHD and The Phantom Booker ran things, but they're both off doing their own things. Anyway, it's the wrestlers themselves who decide where the federation is going. Hopefully we'll make the right decisions. Shanda: Anybody in particular you have in mind to fight with in the future? Ivan? Meanstreak? Toomes? Or someone else? Doug Troy: I've fought Ivan and Streak before. Toomes is someone I really wanna fight, and not just because he has the World Title. I see in Toomes a little bit of Doug Troy, the Doug Troy who wrestled in the ACW. He's a smartass, he's cocky, he's 'friends' with the boss and he's got something to prove and a big stage on which to do it. I still think he's an asshole of the highest order, but he's an asshole whom I'd love to face in the ring. After Toomes, I dunno who next. Wrestling's a funny business these days. You never know what the future holds.
