New Ivan Propaganda Poster

25 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Continuing with a wave of propaganda to try to bring Ivan Stanislav back into the good graces of the OSW fans, the Russian High Command has released another ‘touching’ photo of Ivan Stanislav. This time, The Russian Bear is seen embracing a smiling baby boy. Contrary to his happy demeanor, however, Ivan Stanislav has seemingly descended into a maddening, vengeful rage that has primarily been directed towards Reno Davis, but also Amy Campbell as well as many other crewmembers of the OSW. However, Stanislav has also been falling into a dark brooding period when left alone as well as he seems to be trying to piece together the realities and the falsities of his life. “Stanislav has been in high spirits ever since Head on Collision, contrary to what the media may speculate,” said Alexei Ruslan, “He has been quite happy to take these pictures of himself, to show the people who he truly is!” Ruslan quickly scurried away, muttering something in Russian as he did. The fact still remains, however, that behind the smile in the picture, there is a very tortured and tormented Ivan Stanislav who appears to be questioning not only himself, but his friends, country, and communism….
