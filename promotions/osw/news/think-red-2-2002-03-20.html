OSW — Column

Think Red #2

20 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings Comrades!! It is I, Alexei Ruslan coming to you from my comfortable office chair deep underneath the Kremlin to bring you another edition of my article: Think Red!! Where should I start? After all, so many things have happened since my last edition, that even I was taken by surprise! I know, it is hard to believe but sometimes even the most intelligent and calculating soul is caught off guard now and again. Individuals will come later, but my opinions on the events of Crowning Glory, amazingly enough, coincide with many opinions backstage and abroad. Ivan Stanislav was robbed. That is correct my friends! I want you all to understand that we have heard your cries of foul after the match against Doug Troy and Meanstreak, and we can assure you that we are doing everything in our power to right what was obviously a wrong! Other issues that can be brought to our attention include the fact that Amanda, Kid Wonder’s little piece of skin, has entered herself into Old School Wrestling sponsored Queen of Extreme tournament. I remember fondly events that involved us personally with Wonder and Amanda, and if I may delve into my extensive archival knowledge of our actions, this is transcript of events, on paper, of course: ~ Ivan roars and lifts Wonder up over his head. The Russian Bear eyes a van parked roughly 25 feet away. He laughs and THROWS Wonder through the air. Wonder soars like a bird almost, and then slams hard, back first, into the van. He collapses to the ground in a heap. Ruslan wrestles with Amanda and hands her to Ivan. He grabs her and lifts her by the waist…. Ivan- Here girl…stay with your little boy…. Stanislav laughs and THROWS Amanda into the van as well, if not harder! She collapses onto Wonder.~ Very interesting, no? She cannot compete. She is no wrestler, she is only in this business to look good and make little boys happy, that is all. I am interested to see just how she holds up against true competitors. I will get to the subject of some other matters later, but first, allow me to run down the upcoming card for Saturday Slam, as well as some unbiased opinions, no? Singles Match Enforcer vs. Mighty Red Army Member, Michel Krieg The Enforcer thought he could be funny and make big deal last Saturday, only to have Comrade Walczak come out and put a permanent sock in his mouth. However, we of The Red Army are not afraid of anyone, and have agreed to have Enforcer battle Michel Krieg in order to see if he is worthy to fight a seasoned veteran like Walczak. The Enforcer is seemingly a well known wrestler, though I have never heard of him. But I have heard of Michel Krieg throughout Europe and I can assure you that his physical power is only rivaled by those within the Red Army itself! Alexei’s pick: Michel Krieg. Comrade Krieg will steamroll Enforcer, who will easily collapse to the might of The Red Army. He will learn why we are what we are, and why our mantra, collectiveness in all battles, enables us to survive! Six Man Tag Team Match Falls Count Anywhere Vampir Nosferatu/Nick Casey/Great Dragon vs. Propaganda/EoE/The Magi An interesting group of misguided misfits, no? The newcomers into the OSW that litter this match is astonishing, and I must say that I am quite interested to see how they size up against one another. However, there is a small problem… Within their own groups they cannot seem to coexist! Capitalism if I have ever seen it! Alexei’s Pick: No Contest. These men have no common ground, save for the fact that they wish to use each other’s head as a football. I see it degenerating into ugly capitalist free for all, in which instead of getting what they want, they wind up with nothing. Complete waste. Market Economists, take note! Singles Match Might Red Army Banned From Ringside Tempest vs. Meanstreak After cowardly and underhanded attack perpetrated by Meanstreak against Tempest weeks ago, in which Ivan Stanislav mightily deferred him and saved Tempest and perhaps her career, Tempest has gotten her wish. Against the great knowledge of Ivan Stanislav, Tempest has requested match against Meanstreak and Ed, collaborator that he is, has agreed to have it done. As well, The Red Army, the only constant form of justice in this chaotic federation, cannot preside to make things right! Alexei’s Pick: The Red Army. That is correct. This “blockade” made by Jane Reagan is to test if The Red Army will answer and adhere to her rulings, which we will not. While Tempest may not like it, and while Meanstreak may fear it, the Red Army WILL attack, make no doubt about it. It would take an act of God to stop us. Just as Lenin and the Bolsheviks stood up against Czar Nicholas, The Red Army will stand up against the bourgeois-capitalist, Jane Reagan! Four Corners Eight Man Tag Team Table Match Contendership for OSW Tag Titles on the Line Heresian Bloodlust vs. 3B vs. Dame/Ramey vs. Twisted Metal A most unorthodox pairing of teams, if I may say so myself! Heresian Bloodlust is a cohesive unit that has shown more and more unity throughout their tenure here in the OSW, and surely their constant battle with Civil Disobedience, current champions, has not altered this perception. 3B and the team of Dame and Ramey are also fighting a more personal battle, which could sway and change their tactics, turning it into a war of attrition rather than a war for the belts. And Twisted Metal? I like the appearance of hard work done by proletariat on these two, but I think they deserve to head to junk pile. The last thing OSW needs is two robots to hold titles! Alexei’s Pick: Heresian Bloodlust. Simply put, 3B and Dame/Ramey will destroy one another. Leaving the metal moron’s against a supercharged Heresian Bloodlust. I already have a bid in for Chico to be made into a big hammer and Gunmetal to be a big sickle, I suspect we will see this after this contest. OSW International Title Match Comrade in Arms and Hero to the People: Tom Walczak vs. Rhett Locke Need there be a question? Since his entrance into the OSW and his affiliation with The Red Army, Tom Walczak has burst through any and all barriers to get what he has coming to him: The International Title. He has done the people of Poland proud, and the Ex Soviet Satellite States and Russian Federation applaud his efforts. Rhett Locke will be swallowed whole by the might of Tom Walczak. Alexei’s Pick: Tom Walczak. Complete physical domination by The Polish Punisher will ensure victory here. Locke may be wiry, but Walczak is unstoppable! #1 Contenders Match for World Title Doug Troy vs. The Hero of the Soviet People: Ivan Stanislav It is hard to call matches in which my personal opinions could tend to sway my beliefs, but I will try to stay as unbiased as I can. Since the beginning, Ivan Stanislav has fought Doug Troy in attempt to dominate him and send him packing. And he has done this. The battered and broken Doug Troy has returned for one last fight, in which this will be it. His spreading of propaganda regarding Ivan supposedly leaving ‘Tempest out to dry’ only fuels this fire between the two more. Alexei’s Pick: Ivan Stanislav. Doug Troy will put up a mighty fight, but I will be first to say this match is paramount to Ivan Stanislav. Not only ensuring a shot at World Title for Ivan Stanislav, but marking the end, and complete destruction of Doug Troy. Handicap Match for OSW World Title Timo Bolamba/Amy Campbell vs. Switchblade I do not understand these people. Switchblade asks for a handicap match against Timo Bolamba and Aimz? I will be first to admit that Timo Bolamba and Campbell do not have any ability at all, but neither does Switchblade, save for his constant blathering. It is too hard to call this. While I do not like Switchblade, he is no idiot. Allow me to retract that last statement. Switchblade IS an idiot, but under the tutelage of Jane Reagan, he has semblance of a brain, even if it is comparative to a small forest animal. Something is not right in this match and I predict Timo Bolamba and Aimz are going to get hurt in the process. Oh well…I do not like them anyway. Alexei’s Pick: Does it really matter? There is no Red Army involved, and the strap is better off in the garbage can than around the waist of either party’s involved. But if I were to make a prediction, I reluctantly agree with my instincts: Switchblade will retain the title through underhanded cheating. On that note, I wish to reiterate what I had said in my past article: We of Red Army do not condone nor recognize any cheating actions taken by any individual within the OSW and wish it to be stopped at once. We understand that the people wish to see fair matches in which a winner is clearly determined, and to that extent this is what we urge: Watch only Red Army matches and ignore the others. The more you focus on us and forget the others, the more you will understand what wrestling truly is. Thank you. Now that I have gotten off of my little soapbox, allow me to comment on some individuals in particular. Jane Reagan I am glad you people can finally see what Jane Reagan is made of: Sexual Drive. We tried to tell you, and it takes all we can not to say, ‘We told you so.’ But we did. Now you people have created a sex driven monster that believes in only her own self betterment at the expense of the masses. I hope that you are all proud of yourselves! However, we are ever mindful that the people need something stable to hold on to, and that comes in the form of the glorious Red Army, who has been battling her for months. Jane Reagan, her beautiful story is over. After Ivan wins the contendership for the World Title and if Switchblade manages to defend against Timo and Aimz, I cannot wait to hear him cry loudly like a baby when Ivan Stanislav directs his full attention to him. Jane Reagan is a monster, and she will be put down. Ed Is it just me, or is Ed about as useful as the Wall Street Journal? Since he has come into OSW he has made poor, pathetic judgment calls and created only further turmoil. I would also like to point out that we of The Red Army have always been against Ed, for such said reasons as listed below: -Doug Troy: Ed thought he could do us favor by bringing in Doug Troy to the OSW, and what has happened since then? An old, crippled wrestler has limped back into wrestling ring only to be used and abused by those around him. It is not that we wish to hurt him as bad as we do, but if he wishes to get into ring with us, what must be done must be done, no? -Trashcan Man: The man that gives “proletariat” a bad name. After going straight for Comrade Walczak, TCM quickly disappeared, against the wishing of his oh so great friend, Ed. -Meanstreak: You fools cheered when he entered, and now you boo when he is around. I hope you are proud of yourselves. Ed unleashed Meanstreak again, and there is no way to restrain him, save for a unified effort of the most powerful men in the federation: The Red Army. Need I say more? These three cardinal mistakes the Ed created has only done more damage to the OSW than good. And mind you, we have been against these individuals, and once again, ‘we told you so!’ As I hear the great clock within the Kremlin toll, I realize how late it is and that I have devoted far too much time to you people. Mind you that these are strictly my opinions, but of course, they are the accurate and correct opinions. I urge you all to register for the Communist Party of the United States of America, or vote for your local communist party candidate in any election forthcoming. OSW, do not fret, The Red Army is here to protect you! For the Motherland!! For the Party!! And for The Red Army: Think Red OSW Director of FSB, Foreign Division Alexei Gregorovich Ruslan
