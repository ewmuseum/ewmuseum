Ears to the Street #2

7 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's said that a warrior is one who engages him or herself, in war and who fights, recklessly, killing all those who come across shooting range without fear of the consequences. A warrior must have talent, desire, passion, and superior physical strength. On the exact contrary, a wimp is a weak, cowardly, or ineffective individual, who cowers in adversity and hides until it's all over. He only then emerges to fight with what little power he has left, which of course, is inevitably useless. Within the OSW, there are plenty of both warriors and wimps, who just by their actions the viewing public, media and affiliates can be classifed already. And once again, I, Marcus Murphy OSW's man on the streets, searched the streets to get the goods from various PTC members throughout the federation. This edition specifically features opinions from that of Civil Disobediance, Switchblade, D-Kay Caylx, Great Dragon, Frankie Bones, Mark Estep and many more. Read and enjoy. Old School Warriors or Old School Wimps? Hello again, OSW fans, I'm Marcus Murphy with the latest question on everyone's mind since officially finding out that Jane Reagan, PCW personel director is actually the owner of the Old School Wrestling federation, contrary to what D-Kay Calyx would've had everyone to believe in my preceding column, only a few weeks ago. And I quote: "I think the owner will be decided after the PCW PPV Shang-Haied, with the loser of the Presidency match taking it," D-Kay stated, matter of factly. And of course he was wrong...but no worries, loyal readers. D-Kay Caylx will no longer make predictions in this column, and purposely manipulate your impressable minds into beliving him, not that I believed him or anything, it's just for your sake. Okay then everybody, as I was researching for my latest column, guess what your man on the streets managed to see crawl up from the sewers? Mark Estep! Former PCW/NTICW star that couldn't cut it and new PWA student. Let's see what he's been up to? Besides not bathing...also joining me for input on this week's column was D-Kay Caylx *without invitation, mind you*, Frankie Bones, and OSW's own Great Dragon. These many different people came together and gave me, Marcus Murphy, their opinions of each and every superstar to date in the OSW, besides those who were not yet mentioned as of Slam!, I will personally take these few men upon myself and a select few to define. Our first specimen, was none other than former NTICW Rising Star Champion, that demented Silent Killer himself, Corg. The question was, is he a Warrior or Wimp? "I'm gonna have to say wimp." said Frankie Bones when asked the infamous question. "Anyone that goes to that extent to draw attention to themselves, and to make themselves look hard, really isn't. Get rid of his spikes, metal, and makeup, and he's just another scared little kid." That's true enough Frankie, just another scared little kid, at the back of the unemployment line. Let's be honest folks, this freak wouldn't been hired in the first place if it wasn't for poor Ms. Jane Reagan probably being scared out of her mind of him.
