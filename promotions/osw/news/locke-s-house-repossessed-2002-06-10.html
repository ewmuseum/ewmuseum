Locke's House Repossessed

10 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shortly after returning from Canada, OSW superstar Rhett Locke, returned to Portland, Oregon to retrieve his things for the drive to Oakland, only to find his house had been reposessed. Apparently, Rhett being the busy man that he is had not checked the mail in a while. For if he had, he'd have known that someone had sent to the U.S. Postal Service an address change for him. Rhett, still in complete and utter shock could barely speak. "I...I can't believe this is happening," screamed Locke. "Who the FUCK did this!?! WHO THE FUCK!?!" He later commented, "It had to be god damned Graphic Violence...I'll get him...I SWEAR TO GOD I'LL GET HIM!" OSW wishes Rhett Locke well through this time of trials and tribulations. However, when offered to have a few weeks off Rhett declined, stating that quote, "For the love of God, I need to wrestle...it's the only thing I fucking have left...that, my jeep, and the clothes on my back." Despite making well over enough to purchase a new house with OSW, Rhett has made a point to 'think about things' before even looking for a new home.
