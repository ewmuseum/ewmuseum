Aimz Denies Report

4 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After vicious rumors about OSW's Amy 'Aimz' Campbell and sworn enemy Drew Hostyle having had a relationship in their days prior to Old School Wrestling, Aimz decided to finally speak out on the topic when approached by cable news representatives backstage. "If that had ever happened, don't you think I would've mentioned it?" the irate redhead snuffed to reporters, "Besides...I was after Rent-A-Hero in PWA, and I might like 'dark meat'...but I don't want to touch ANYTHING that's hung around to rot in a stale ghetto for so long." "I'd bet my bottom dollar," she continued. "That Hostyle himself started that rumor. It's called wishful thinking. Maybe he thinks that if he lies long enough, people might actually believe him." At that point, Campbell had walked away from the ring of journalists, still red in the face from hearing the rumor. We will continue to monitor this story and bring you more info as it becomes available.
