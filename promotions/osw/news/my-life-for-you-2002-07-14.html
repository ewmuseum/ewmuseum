My Life For You

14 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cleveland, Ohio- On a night where surprises, mystery partners and chaos expected, no one could have possibly predicted the betrayal and carnage which took place in the main event... ...well almost no one. Also on Slam, PHD's suprise was not only big, but of mammoth proportions, OSW went NC-17 without as much as a peep from rating censors, Gregor Xaxon saw his OSW career come to a screeching halt, Frankie Bones introduced Jesus St. Crux to a new brand of sports entertainment and Corg introduced Jane Reagan to his "little friend."
