Debut of the Ring Rat

28 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What's shakin' bacon? These columnists are all so busy that I decided to fill up the space with a little something. A little homemade cooking, shall we say? Well, I know that the opening says "The Ring Rat" but believe you me, I am certainly no Ring Rat. If you're not familiar with the term it means "A woman who hangs around the hotels and arenas after shows looking to sleep with one of the wrestlers." Well buddy, I got news for you. I have my morals. I have my dignity. I have my spirits. I also have a great fascination with the sport that is Professional Wrestling. I used to sit on the couch cuddling up next to my ex-husband, reaching into his lap... for the bowl of popcorn that was sitting there, and just enjoying the night of blood, sweat, and tears. Now I sit with my eight-year-old son Jake watching the sports-entertainment. He wants to be a wrestler. I won't have that. No, no, no. If he wants to be a wrestler he can go live with his bum of a father out in those trashy hotels scrounging for quarters, nickels, and dimes. Yes-sirrie, that bastard got married to me, and I kicked him out. He didn't leave, I kicked him out. Me. Laura Luchadore Alice Armdrag Mother Markout Janet Cads... "The Ring Rat" Well, anyway, I gather that I have introduced myself beyond necessity. So onward. As I said I am an avid fan of wrestling and have been watching the fastest shooting star promotion I have seen in years, Old School Wrestling. I have seen these boys work from day one and they've all had a wild ride. From February to June, three Pay-Per-Views, nine title acquisitions, four stables, and twenty-one Saturday Slam!s later they are considered by many to be the greatest promotion around. Just recently coming off of an astounding Pay-Per-View event, Ascension, they have five men strongly leading the way in the title divisions. Those men are the Cruiserweight Champion Corg, the Tag Team Champions Da Westsidaz, the International Champion Propaganda, and the World Champion Tom Walczak. These four hunks all lead the road down to OSW's future. They will be OSW's Pete Borst, Kremmen, High Flyer and Ultra Cool. I'd just like to take my time to give my opinion on these wild ones, a woman's opinion, a mother's opinion. The Cruiserweight Champion, Corg, is a vile creature to say the least. Needless to say that one part of the Pay-Per-View Jake did not watch. That was simply horrible and disgusting. Degrading for women everywhere. I do not see why Amy Campbell does the work she does when it leads to these types of actions. Violent, horrible, abusive actions. I cannot, simply cannot believe a human being would do this type of this to another human being. And the fact that OSW officials did nothing during the offensive to stop it is beyond me. If it were my decision they'd hang Raiyn up be his, "bulging member." That man should less be a champion than a scar on the Earth's surface. A mere firing on Jane Reagan's part would be but a pinch to what this beast deserves. Just the thought of people everywhere having to sit and watch a poor, defenseless, little girl be abused, stripped, and damn near raped brings tears to my eyes and my casserole up to my mouth. Thank God for Reno Davis or all would be lost. Corg is a disgrace to anything he comes in contact with. Da westside is da bestside! Hehe, excuse me. I love those two cutie pies. I used to watch them all through their Primetime Championship Wrestling era and now that they have come to OSW the tag division has life once again. Da Westsidaz have a spirit that everyone around the world has to enjoy. These dears are special and bring prestige to the title belts. Not like Heresian Bloodlust. Yuck! Even that name brings disgust to my tongue! Hessian and Azrael Cain were a burden on Slam! When they walk out, so do I, but at Ascension all that changed and we were pronounced new champs! As for those other muskrats, Civil Disobedience, they need to have some respect. If their morals weren't so out of whack they'd be as cute as two flowers in a starfish vase. But they decided to go the rocky road and become jerks. Needless to say that Short Circuit and Riptah are two boys who truly show the spirit of Old School. The second greatest belt in OSW has to be the International Title. This is now held by Propaganda. I respect this man. He is loyal, fights for the Lord, and has his values straight. However, the actions he takes to get them are rather... old fashioned and just plain mean. He needs to start preaching to those that want to listen and let the listeners choose their own choice. As a champion he is an upstanding citizen in my book. The strap is good around his waist, however he does need to get his means straight. "The Polish Punisher" Tom Walczak is a man that not even this mother can love. Him and his cult following of The Red Army sicken me. They are, without a doubt, the biggest hypocrites to ever walk the Earth. Hey, didn't you hear? Communism went out with The Berlin Wall! Those men and women following the powerful Ivan Stanislav simply need to think about what they are doing. Tom Walczak has that option. He has the option to change his feelings and bring out a lovely future for OSW as it's World Champion. Certainly right now he does not follow the right group, but maybe, just maybe, he will realize. We can only hope that our next World Champion will be a better person than Tom Walczak. Well, I guess that's all from mommy. Jake needs dinner, he's a growing boy you know. Well, until next time, this is The Ring Rat saying... Behave... -"The Ring Rat" Janet Cads
