Stanislav Calls Blockade

17 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Following OSW SLAM this week, the matches for the coming week were announced. Of these matches, OSW’s resident Soviet, Ivan Stanislav, is booked against Doug Troy for the number one contendership for the OSW World Title. Also slated for the next SLAM is a match between Tempest, a seemingly possible love interest for Stanislav, and Meanstreak, arch enemy of The Russian Bear. Stipulations included in the match include that no Red Army presence is allowed near the area. Speedy Riggs, referee of OSW that was scheduled to officiate the match, has already been replaced, holding true to these stipulations. This ruling came shortly after the match was declared by special order of the President of OSW: Jane Reagan. “We of The Red Army do not follow the rules of some stupid, capitalist piece of trash such as Jane Reagan, we will do what we wish! We do not listen or follow any letter of any law that she declares!” said Stanislav in a statement released late last night from the Kremlin, in which Stanislav was attending a secret meeting. “We will do what we want, when we want, regardless of what she believes or decrees!” Chances are that if Stanislav or any Red Army member interferes in this match it will be thrown out and thus awarding a win to Meanstreak and a loss to Tempest. While early speculation says that Stanislav is doing this for the pure satisfaction of destroying Meanstreak, new beliefs are arising that Stanislav is doing this to protect Tempest, who has already been hospitalized once by The Monster They Call Meanstreak. Tempest was well aware of the situation when reporters contacted her, "I'll... talk to them. I want this match and while Jane obviously thinks it'll take me out of the picture, I have a few surprises left for her and Meanstreak too." Stanislav would not comment directly on the statement issued by Tempest, but gave a stern warning to the OSW: “Speculate what you wish, but understand that Red Army does not follow any shady rulings from any form of authority, if that is what Jane Reagan believes she is! Come Saturday SLAM, we will do what we want, regardless of any repercussions that get in our way!” We will bring you more on this story as it develops.
