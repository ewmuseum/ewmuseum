OSW Personnel Report

1 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shortly after the OSW hit Pay Per View, By Any Means Necessary went off the air Jane Reagan, representative of the OSW, made the following statements. “First of all, I would like to congratulate the workers, technicians, security, staff, and the fans at home for making By Any Means Necessary the event that it was hyped up to be: An event unlike any other. We of the OSW are thoroughly pleased with not only the outcome of our matches and our product, but also the fact that as the OSW heads into its seventh month we are stronger than ever and gaining momentum daily.” However, injuries and releases have occurred after the lights went out at By Any Means Necessary, the changes in the roster are as follows: Jim Axtell reinjured his knee in his street fight against the Cult and will be out indefinitely. Sephiroth du Luc has suffered various injuries and due to his lackluster work in the OSW, he has been released. Reno Davis has been given time off to grieve for the loss of his primate pal, ZaC. We of the OSW send our deepest regards to Reno Davis and his family for their loss and he has been given as much time off as is required. Lillith has been arrested by Philadelphia police for her brutal assault against Corg and due to this we have been required to release her. Bethany Yamato suffered various injuries and has been released. Mexi, who was El Drago all along, has been signed with the OSW since his entrance as the Mysterious Mexican and his contract remains as is. Erik Kelly has not signed an official contract, but is being paid per appearance at this time and we of the OSW have enjoyed his work thus far. Reagan also stressed that while various gaps have formed within the roster of the OSW, she has no doubt that some current talent of the OSW will certainly fill the gaps and continue to make the OSW the best wrestling federation there is.
