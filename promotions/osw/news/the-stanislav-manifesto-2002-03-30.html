The Stanislav Manifesto

30 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In light of recent occurrences that have presented themselves in OSW, it is I, Praporschik Stanislav that has decided to spearhead need for change within PTC contrary to what superiors would prefer to have undertaken! As many of you are sure to know, an injustice has been committed against the proletariat and the fans of wrestlers throughout OSW! As you are also sure to know, Jane Reagan, a supposed hero of wrestling world, has proven to us that she is nothing more than common capitalistic tramp! This trend has been apparent through many federations within PTC including PCW in form of Tyler Nelson, OSW in form of Jane Reagan, and NTICW with the lecherous Mona! These individuals, as well as many others, are exploiting US and cashing in on our hard work! For months, men like Tyler Nelson and those who revel in the pain of others, women like Jane Reagan who manipulates the faith of the masses, and Mona who resorts to underhanded tactics of her own, have tried to paint myself and my righteous Red Army as nothing short of common criminals! However, I find it necessary to bring to your attention that we are anything BUT that! Since our entrance into PTC, we of Red Army have fought for equality, rights of workers, and fair treatment towards all those involved! Announcers like Loyal Communist Party Member Wang and officials like Speedy Riggs have seen the advantages of such system, contrary to such manipulary accusations made by those in power! The OSW and ultimately the PTC, threatened by manipulators of all sorts, is in danger of complete and total domination by the individuals mentioned above! Throughout the early stages of this movement and currently as the non-believers have opened their eyes, it is time that a system arise that will free us from these restrictions from which we have unwittingly shackled to ourselves! Since The Red Army’s conception, we have stood for what we believe is correct system of government! Throughout it all we have weathered the berating words, the harsh criticism, and the lies put forth by those that would like to hide the truth from your eyes! Allow me to illustrate various area’s from which you have been blinded, yet our vision has remained the same!! Jane Reagan Jane Reagan has proved without a shadow of a doubt her treachery and her underhanded ways within the OSW. Tricking the people themselves into false sense of security and twisting her words to make them sound goodly, she has revealed that she is nothing more than a capitalist that will take advantage of anyone to get her way! Throughout all of this, we of The Red Army have remained against her, even back to PCW days, regardless of what scrutiny we had to stomach. We knew what she was made of, we knew what her agenda was, and we excuse the people’s inability to share our vision! But now, we remain as we always have been, against Jane Reagan, and as sole force capable of stopping her evil ways! Ed Promising a strong attack against the then “Renegade” Red Army, Ed has proven to us all that he is nothing more than agent of President Reagan, prior to and now during her tyrannical reign. Signing matches such as Tempest vs. Meanstreak, in which he knows it could have resulted in grave injury for Tempest, Ed cannot hide from us the fact that he will do anything to help The President! Throughout his tenure here, we of Red Army have been against him, and fought to stop him from helping his superiors! His answer to this was bringing in Meanstreak, another sworn enemy of our cause! We have not changed our beliefs on Ed, but now the people realize that we have been correct, when they swore that The Red Army was wrong! Doug Troy One of the conscripts brought into the OSW by Ed, Doug Troy has proven to be nothing more than a child fighting in a man’s war! He has done nothing but damage the already thinning stability within the OSW, and for what reason? Why should we follow this man? Because he has interesting catchphrase? Because he ‘says’ that his way is the right way? And if he were to be successful, where would we be, my friends? No better than while we are being controlled by Jane Reagan. Doug Troy entered at same time as Ivan Stanislav, and while hope was instilled within OSW when The Red Army came, uncertainty was created when Doug Troy joined our ranks! Meanstreak The “Saving Grace” of OSW, perpetrated by Ed, has proven to be more of poison to federation than even Ed is! Since his return to wrestling, Meanstreak has run amok throughout ranks of OSW, stopping short of attacking Red Army for he knows the power that we possess. From abducting defenseless children to defaming those around him, Meanstreak has proven to the people that while you put great faith in him, he has ALWAYS been what we have ALWAYS said, a capitalist of highest regard, which is lowest standards to The Red Army! During PCW time, Ivan Stanislav and Red Army stood against Meanstreak while the people remained ignorant of blatant fact in front of them: Meanstreak does not fight for people, he fights for himself! We have excused this and accept it, as the people were blinded by false beliefs before. However, we have remained loyal to our cause and continue to battle the lawlessness that is Meanstreak! It should also be revealed that it was Ed, agent of Reagan that brought this animal into the federation, but it will be Red Army that gets rid of him once and for all! People of PTC! Workers of United Federation!! An opportunity has been borne to us for which we cannot squander! Red Army, mighty classless group within PTC and OSW has risen to challenge of overthrowing these tyrants that have fooled us with their money, their smiles, and their ‘trust!’ I, Ivan Sergeiovich Stanislav, Praporschik of Russian people and fighter of equality, declare that all those willing to overpower the oppressive governments join my communist cause! No matter what federation! Big or small, weak or strong, it does not matter, for boundaries cannot stop those whose passion drives them towards peace! I urge you ALL to get into contact with Ivan Stanislav so that we can coordinate a full counteroffensive and end the string of malicious acts perpetrated by those who we once believed were our friends!! Join me my Comrades as we take back what Jane Reagan has tried so hard to take away! Comrade in Arms Praporschik Ivan Stanislav
