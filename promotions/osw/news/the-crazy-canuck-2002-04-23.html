OSW — Column

The Crazy Canuck

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Alright, I think it’s that time of month where I put down my beer and poutine and watch some OSW television. Yes, it’s the premier column of Cory the Crazy Canuck. I know the name is lame, but can you think of anything else that will start with C? Andrew the Crazy Canuck is pretty bad, I thought. I’m looking forward to Head on Collision this month, as long as it isn’t on the same night as my Leafs are on. Oh yes, by the way, where did you Americans learn to play Hockey! I mean, Jesus! You guys are terrible. Well, it’s aboot time I get to the Predictions then, eh? Well, like most Canadians, I’ll be watching it on my illegal DirectTV receiver. Oh wait, that’s right. It’s illegal to have unsubscribe over in the US unlike Canada, eh? Oh well, let’s get to the first match. Queen of Extreme Finale ???? vs. ???? Well, it’s usually hard to choose a winner in a competition until the final two competitors are announced, right? USUALLY. In this competition, Bethany Yamato has undoubtedly kicked some ass. Although my original pick, Amanda Sampson, didn’t do as well as I hoped, I still get to settle for another overly hot chick! Besides that, Yamato has what it takes to cut it in OSW. She’s got a solid guarantee to not be in serious trouble, due to the 500 pound monster that is Kimbusa. She’s got good looks. She’s athletic and very talented. She needs a little work on the stick, but who doesn’t? I’m really pushing for her to make it because you know she deserves it. Lilith is alright, nothing overly special and Keyson is just plain annoying. I wouldn’t mind seeing Lilith pick up a contract in the future, but not over Yamato. WINNER: Bethany Yamato “I Lied” Match Drew Hostyle vs. Amy “Aimz” Campbell Drew vs. Amy, eh? Well, let’s look at the angle first. Drew says he boned her, Amy says not in this lifetime. Frankly, I agree with the latter. Drew has wetdreams every night thinking that he got to first base with her. But, how can Aimz hope to compete with Drew Hostyle and probably Fankie Bones as well? I mean, seriously. He’s a 250 plus angry black man and she’s 130lbs? Frankly, I hope that pretty little Canuckette just lies and says “I lied” early on to avoid any punishment. WINNER: Drew Hostyle, even though HE lied. Single Match “The Quintessential Teen Idol” Kid Wonder vs. “The Mighty Yakuza” Kimbusa This is another one of the good matches on the card. The Almighty Cruiserweight tries to shake off his reputation as a lightweight by taking on a Sumo Wrestler. Wonder obviously has the experience and agility advantage here, but can he even hope to compete with the raw strength of Kimbusa? A win for Wonder will show that he isn’t fooling around and he is a legitimate contender for the title. A win for Kimbusa will establish him as a future major player in OSW and maybe send him on a run for the International Title. But can Kimbusa really keep Wonder off his feet? This columnist thinks Wonder’s lightning quick offense is too much for the sluggish Yokozuna. WINNER: Kid Wonder Blood Letting Match Epitome of Evil vs. “Angeldust” Nick Casey Hmm…two talented men in a vicious match. Personally, I haven’t paid much attention to this feud and frankly, these men. Although I have seen that both of them can get it done in the ring, I’ve seen Nick Casey look a little more impressive. It’s going to be a good match though, but aren’t bloody matches always good?? WINNER: “Angeldust” Nick Casey Loser Leaves Town Streetfight Doug Troy, Jesse Ramey, “Classic” Clay Vendetta & Moose Malone vs. Team CCW Boy do I wish this could be an elimination match! Dumping Jesse Ramey and picking up Jason McAllister and Kensuke Suzahara would kick some major ass! But sadly, I don’t think it works that way. Something tells me the MOOSE’LL RUN LOOSE over all the CCWer’s asses. It won’t be a sleeper either, five-eighths of the men have talent. I really like Classic Clay and hope he’ll take a run at the International Title as well, actually. OSW can’t afford to lose their security, Douglas Troy and Clay, so I see them squashing Nelson’s goons. Shame he can’t do his own dirty-work, isn’t it? WINNER: Team OSW Hair vs. Broadcast Rights Match Civil Disobedience vs. Team Annoying Good God! How can you actually put Gottfried & The Top on television, let alone their own match? However, it’ll be nice to see these two get thrashed by Civil D. Hopefully Masters and Hawke really unload on these two, possibly breaking legs and the like? The Special Guest Ref will probably end up being the wildcard, but can these two losers even stand a chance against professional wreslters? WINNER: Civil Disobedience OSW Tag Team Championship The Red Army vs. Heresian Bloodlust Hmmm…two very powerful teams. You have Walzcak, Krieg, Cain and Hessian in the ring, so you can expect this to be very physical and a strength-relying match. The Red Army need this more though, because this is their chance to prove that it is an all-around effort, not just big bad Stanislav. If they win this, it’ll be a great accomplishment for the OSW version of the Red Army, which has yet to really prove that they can cut it. Hessian and Cain are no push-overs, but the Red Heart of the two Europeans is going to be too much for the Satanic Freaks. WINNER: The Red Army Singles Match Graphic Violence vs. A Masked Opponent Well, to be man enough to attack Graphic Violence, you’re going to have to be able to back it up. This guy is buffed and is more than ready to take on the Masked Opponent. I always believed that wearing a mask is a symbol that you’re shameful of something. But this GV is aboot ass-kicking, so I can’t see the Masked Man having much of a chance, unless he is just as bad-assed. WINNER: Graphic Violence OSW International Championship Rhett Locke vs. Corg The stip isn’t gunna be announced till Slam! I think, but it ain’t gonna be a bloodless match for pussies. Rhett Locke is really the more talented of the two though. He’s just, good. Corg isn’t a push-over, but he’s going to have to stick to someone more in his league. He’ll end up picking the stipulation, but Rhett is just going to own him. Probably not much of a surprise though, eh? WINNER: Rhett Locke Singles Match Ed vs. Meanstreak My God! If you’re a fan of either of these men, you should be marking out like CRAZY! Luckily, I’m a fan of both. I like Streak’s new attitude and I much prefer him a controlled-monster, not a freelancer. But Ed is Ed, which’ll be a huge obstacle for the Monster. On paper, statistically, Ethan Giroux should maim Ed. He has him beat in every physical way, shape and form. Not to mention the size advantage, strength advantage, mental advantage, hell, the actual wrestling advantage. Meanstreak should statistically murder Eddie. But Ed is Ed like I said. He’s aboot coming back from career-threatening injuries. He’s aboot beating the odds. He’s aboot slaying monsters. This man has a heavy heart that not even the Monster could bench-press. Ed will beat Streak in that match, but it may cost him whatever gas he has left. Needless to say, this match will take the cake. WINNER: Ed OSW World Heavyweight Championship Ivan Stanislav vs. “Switchblade” Sebastian Toomes Another great match for the most coveted title in the game. The Russian Bear takes on the Jackass. Both men have armies backing them. Both men have women backing them. And both men can back themselves up in the ring. Stanislav is a mastermind, as is Toomes. Ivan has the strength advantage, but Toomes is a known cheater. Although, he may have his mind on the Vax and the Mystery Biker. But, I have a feeling Toomes is going to pull an upset on the Bear. WINNER: Sebastian Toomes Well, that’s Head on Collision. Somethings I’m hoping for are fresh angles. I hope Wonder moves up to challenge Seabass for his title, while Kimbusa steps in to face Rhett Locke for his. Hopefully this won’t be the end of Red Army vs. 3B, because I want 3B to challenge Red Army for the Tag Straps and maybe throwing Ivan back in a feud with Meanstreak. I know it’s been done, but eh, it still brings in draws. Troy should immediately turn heel and attack Ed and look for the unveiling of GV’s attacker, the Biker and the Vax. The latter two will probably be in cahoots as well. I think that there’s going to be some liposuction of deadweight in the federation, hopefully shilling off some extra pounds and bring in some fresh faces and maybe someone to throw in a fresh face to liven up Propaganda and Vampir Nosferatu’s seemingly dead feud. Well, that’s all for today and remember, GO LEAFS GO!
