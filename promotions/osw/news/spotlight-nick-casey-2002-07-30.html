Spotlight: Nick Casey

30 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanda: Hello there OSW Fans!! It's me, Shanda Russell here again with "Angeldust" Nick Casey! Nick, thanks for doing this interview! Nick: Hey, it's no problem. Just starting to fend off a few of those bumps from Slam! last night, ya know? Shanda: I know!! That was one heck of a match you put on on Slam! And by the way, Congratulations on the International Title! Nick: Thanks. Most people don't really know this about me, but that International Title means a lot more to me than just another belt. I've been doing this for a hell of a long time, since I was just starting way back in MWW and through REALM. I've constantly gotten cheated and deceived out of titles all along throughout my career, so getting one now is kind of nice. The first taste of gold for me won't be the last though, eventually I do want to take the OSW Heavyweight Title as well. Shanda: Well, you certainly are on your way towards the title, that's for sure. Now, you've been in the OSW for a few months now but until now, you have not really been in the limelight. Why do you think that is so? Or do you disagree? Nick: Well, it's not really an insult when you're in a promotion like OSW, because of the huge amounts of talent around when I got there. I mean I don't mind having legends like Ivan Stanislav or Hessian, or even someone like Tempest. There's not really a problem until I see losers like Reno Davis and Propaganda stepping up in front of me. It bothers me to see scum of the earth like Corg raping people on stage and getting all of the headlines, while I bust my ass to try and win matches. The limelight shouldn't really be a problem now that I have gold, but it bothers me that he's even here. Even someone like Walczak or El Drago moves in front, I know I can hang with these guys. I hanged with the best in MWW and REALM, I can certainly hang with guys like this. Shanda: What about Propaganda? Obviously you two have no love loss for each other. Nick: Ha. Propaganda is scum on the bottom of my boot. We've probably faced each other 4 or 5 times and he's never cleanly beaten me. Hell, I've only lost 2 singles matches since I've joined OSW. The thing is Propaganda can't stand the fact that there is no God behind him. He has to accept that there are some people better; God or not. Shanda: At Any Means Necessary is rapidly approaching and already a few matches have been signed. One in particular is a match featuring you against Propaganda for your International Title. Considering the brutal nature of the Pay Per View, what do you think your chances are against Propaganda? Nick: Propaganda has nothing on me, I've been doing this longer than him, and I've earned my way through the Indy ranks. I've taken my share of bumps and bruises, and I think that that will be the advantage against someone like Propaganda is knowing my way around the ring "hardcore style" will be the advantage. Shanda: Well Nick, lets do a little word association just to see what our International Champion thinks of things, okay? Nick: Okay, Shanda, let's go for it. Shanda: OSW? Nick: Tops. Shanda: Propaganda? Nick: Falling. Shanda: The Think Tank? Nick: Weak. Shanda: The Cult? Nick: Potential. Shanda: The International Title? Nick: Respect. Shanda: Jane Reagan? Nick: If I can elaborate on this one, one of the brightest women I've ever met. Shanda: Interesting....and finally. Ed? Nick: Deceased. Nothing more to really say about that, never really met the guy. Shanda: Yes well, it is a shame you never met Ed. Well, this just about wraps it up Nick, thank you for your time. Do you have any words for the OSW fans? Anything for potential enemies? Nick: Fans, this is only the beginning of something great, so I'd recommend keeping an eye out. Because someday I'll be in all of the Hall Of Fames, and you'll be able to tell your kids after I'm a legendary World Champion that you saw me when I won my first title, and followed me ever since then. As for my enemies, two words; watch out. Thanks Shanda.
