Vendetta Salts Wounds

30 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Our reporters caught up with the always-vocal "Classic" Clay Vendetta today, and he was more than happy to oblige us with a few parting shots at Cyberspace Championship Wrestling. "Who doubted us?" quipped Vendetta. "Yeah, we called that win... just like CLASSIC quarterback Joe "Willie" Namath. The only difference was that the Colts put up a little competition in Super Bowl III, whereas Team Crybabies, Corpses + Windbags simply rolled over and died for us like good little spooges." Reaching into his pocket, Vendetta unfolded a small piece of paper, and continued: "Seems like I managed to miss a few comments by these boys while I was sleeping through their promos, but here's a quote from Spider, captain of the sinking ship, on yours truly: 'I just think he's nothing. What has he done in OSW? Nothing. What has he accomplished? Nada. All that he's done was trash CCW in recent days. Do you really think that he's gonna stand up to CCW? I doubt it.' Hey, Spidey... guess I made that 'trashing' literal when I dropped your ass in that Dumpster, huh? Let me know when you can finally feel your feet again... getting dangled head-first from a fire escape for any length of time will do that to you. Oh, and one last thing; you let your kiddies know that if they ever show their pathetic faces on MY show again, we won't just take out the garbage... we'll do a little incineration and burn that dying fed of yours right to the ground!" Throwing the paper to the floor and spitting on it, Vendetta walked off chuckling. Harsh words from Clay Vendetta, but well-deserved after Team OSW's domination over the CCW invaders at Head-On Collision.
