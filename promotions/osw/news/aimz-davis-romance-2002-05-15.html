Aimz/Davis Romance?

15 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The friendship between Reno Davis and Amy Campbell appears to be escalating with recent events and comments made by many parties. "It's like they don't think, people are gonna catch on after Reno sneaks around backstage and 'accidentally' winds up in her locker-room for the hundredth time," said an anonymous OSW source. "Or when she scurries down the hall of the hotels to 'Borrow a towel' and 'mysteriously' isn't seen until the next morning...it's so lame." Campbell and Davis were recently spotted together at a Calgary branch of Empire Theatres, they purchased tickets to Spiderman and, according to box office staff, "Looked quite friendly while doing so." Davis did not make himself available to comment, while Aimz merely brushed the rumour off as "Some backstage troublemaker's pipe dream." Minion the Imp, when alerted to the issue, had plenty to say... "WHAT THE HELL IS WRONG WITH HER???" screamed the 3'11" powerhouse, Minion. "THAT HUSSY HAD HER CHANCE WITH 79lbs OF RAW IMPBEEF AND SHE PASSES IT UP FOR THAT PUSSY AND HIS GODDAMN MONKEY??? I HOPE THAT BITCH GETS (expletive) NITS OR SOMETHING FROM THAT FLEA BITTEN (expletive)!!! AND HIS MONKEY TOO!!!..............Then again that doesn't mean to say I still don't wanna bone her, but goddamn she really needs some commitment...otherwise she's gonna end up as loose as Jane Reagan...GODDAMNIT I CAN'T CATCH A (expletive) BREAK!!! WOMEN!!! Ugh!" Apparently, from co-workers to Imps, this hot topic's generating quite the flurry backstage.
