SDL to Speak at Slam

26 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Recently signed, enigmatic, OSW superstar, Sepiroth Du Luc, will reportedly address the crowd and television audience on this week's version of Slam, according to OSW officials. "SDL feels he has somethings to say about the past, before he can move on to the future," said OSW spokesperson, Shanda Russell. "He asked for a bit of time on the program, so we gave it to him." Du Luc, who stalked Griff Truxell behind the scenes for weeks, until he finally revealed himself during the main event at Ascension, took revenge on Truxell at the main event for what he described as "Truxell being one of many who have stolen the spotlight from me over the years." As a result of Du Luc's attack, Truxell's career is in jeopardy. We will bring you more on this story as news breaks.
