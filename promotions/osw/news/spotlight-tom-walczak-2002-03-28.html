Spotlight: Tom Walczak

28 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanda: Hello OSW! It's me, Shanda Russell here with another OSW Superstar and with another edition of "Wrestler Spotlight!" With me today is "The Polish Punisher" Tom Walczak. Tom, how are you today? Walczak: Why do you ask such a stupid question when you know that after that garbage loss against Rhett Locke I would not be doing fine Shanda: Um..it's..it's more of a typical introduction that's all... Walczak: I am not a typical person and neither is the Red Army, when will all these damn announcers understand that. Shanda: Uh..yes..well, moving right along then...Tom....how do you like the OSW thus far? Walczak: OSW, it has had its high points and low points. High point a reunion with the Red Army comes to mind, but then there is that one blemish on my record which would be my Title Shot that was blown. Shanda: Speaking of that..if we may, what are your thoughts on Rhett Locke? Personally and professionally? Walczak: I do not feel that such a pathetic pestilential pissant is worth talking about. You see, though I would like to have the International title in my hand, I have already conquered greater goals. I have beaten two former PCW World Champions, in Doug Troy and the Trashcan Man. I have also ended in all likelihood the career of The Enforcer. Shanda: With that being said, would you say The Red Army faction is more powerful now, or before in the PCW? Walczak: Although it was powerful in PCW, its strength is now over twice that which it once was. You see in PCW we had ingrates such as Nikita Morozov, and that pukeface Boda, who wanted the spotlight all to themselves. Now, however we are united, and we have the three biggest and most powerful men in the federation. Now tell me, how can a force such as that be stopped Shanda: Well, I will admit that it is an awfully dangerous force. Many people have speculated that Ivan Stanislav has recently undergone a slight change, perhaps not towards communism, but towards himself and Tempest. Any thoughts regarding Stanislav? Tempest? Walczak: There is no greater man than Comrade Stanislav, and well Tempest is his companion I see no problem with that. You see we Eastern Europeans love three things Power, Alcohol, and Woman there is nothing wrong with that is there? HAHAHAHAHAHA!!!!! Shanda: Um..I suppose not. Another question, and many people have wondered this, what does "Jescze Polska Nie Zginela" mean? Walczak: It is quite simple, It means that Poland is not yet lost. You see I take great pride in my country, and although I felt it was lost when it reared its head from Communism I still feel that there is hope. Shanda: Very interesting. Well, with the recent change of attitude by Jane Reagan and Switchblade, many have speculated that The Red Army are the "Saving Grace" if you will, of the OSW. Some people are actually backing you in your endeavors. Any opinion on that? Walczak: Communists such as The Red Army have always gotten bad press. Mostly for their beliefs, but what people fail to realize that we are indeed good natured people who hate when something that is wrong goes on. You may see more of these heroic rescues in due time, but had you people paid attention previously you would have noticed that this is what we have been doing all along. Shanda: Then how would you explain brutal attacks against individuals such as Doug Troy and Trashcan Man? Or Ed? That is not necessarily the most chivalrous tactic, is it? Walczak: Is it not chivalrous? You see when we protect people we do not do so to those who don't deserve it. No, we do it to those who have followed us or at least kept their distance. Chivalry in ancient times may have been easier to keep because a knight or a king could be kind to one of there enemies and then gain prestige, but Once you are perceived as a bad guy in this business that is how you are going to stay labeled. Shanda: Interesting....so, what does the future hold for Tom Waclzak? Any major goals that you have in mind? Walczak: I am optimistic, but there is nothing that I have specifically placed my eyes on. There is of course gold, but everything will come in due time. Shanda: Well thank you very much for the...interesting interview..Tom, any final comments? Walczak: Not much other that, Jescze Polska Nie Zginela... Shanda: Well folks, there you have, another interesting interview from one of our resident Communists: Tom Walczak! Keep your eyes peeled for more “Wrestler Spotlights!” I’m Shanda Russell for the OSW!
