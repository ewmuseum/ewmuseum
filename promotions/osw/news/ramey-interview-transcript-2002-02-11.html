Ramey Interview Transcript

11 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Recently, Eddie Saunders caught up with Bad Attitude Jesse Ramey, and asked Ramey about all the recent flap surrounding him in OSW. Here is a transcript of the interview. Edward Saunders: Jesse, now, we all know and saw what happened to you yesterday on Slam! Tell me and everyone else out in the world what you think and how feel about this. Jesse: You know that really isn’t a very hard question. (Laughing) I’ve got, (Thinking) numerous bruises, I was busted open in more than four places, had over thirty stitches in my head, had about five or six staples. Um, I forgot what else they told me, but I do know that after it was all done and over that I can actually tell them one thing. Their plan failed. ES: What do you mean? Jesse: Well, I know good and well that their intentions were not to embarrass me. They wanted to put me out of action. Why? Because they knew they couldn’t beat me. Even my own partner was afraid of me. Look at what happened later on in the night; they must be the biggest wimps in sports entertainment. They turned down chances to get in the ring with Ivan “The Big Dumb Russian Koala Bear” Stanislav. You know, if I had the chance, I would get in the ring and beat his ass. Not just because it would be a main event match, but just to prove to everyone in Primetime Central that these four former PCW wrestlers aren’t all that big and bad. I probably couldn’t match strength with Ivan, but I could beat him mentally, at speed, and by all of my high-flying maneuvers. Ivan is going to dominate OSW and do you know why? ES: No, tell me. Why? Jesse: Because everyone is afraid of him and when you have someone that everyone is afraid of management begins to like them and guess what? Instant success! You’ve got a man who is at the top of the charts and no one is going to challenge him for supremacy. Well, guess what? Ivan, I’m calling you out! Get someone in here to make this match, the fans may like you Ivan, but I sure don’t. Don’t get me wrong, the only thing this guy has ever done wrong was thinking that he was the best. He thought wrong, very wrong! ES: Well, let’s talk about the whole thing that went down with the dress. (Pause) I know you don’t like talking about it, but please. Jesse: No, I don’t like talking about it, but it seems I’m going to have to get this out of the way either way I go. The whole thing was a bad gimmick I was told to run in CCW, right before they had me run the death angle. They told me to frigin’ do it. If it had been up to me, I would have never ran that stupid angle. It’s kind of hard when a company basically has your hands tied behind your own back and forces you into doing things you don’t really wanna do. Then you have people like Sebastian who just wanna go and try to rub that in your face. This is one sick pair of people; they deserve to be with each other. I hope they live life nicely with each other and I hope their children aren’t as ugly as they are. ES: What about Rhett Locke? Jesse: I appreciate the help, I really do. I’m a little glad you did come out to help me or else I may have ended up in the same position as SLR. I love doing my job and I’m in complete debt to Rhett and it seems the least I can do is tag with him this coming Slam! Just be forewarned that if you so much as try the same thing on me that Drew Hostyle tried, then you will have hell to pay and even more than that. I will hunt you down and kill you myself. ES: Jesse, it has come to my attention that a lot of people don’t really seem to like you in the backstage or in the ring. Is there some thing that we should all know? Jesse: I’ve been wondering the same thing. I have no clue or idea why people don’t like me. I try to be nice to everyone and everyone stabs me in the back or makes fun of me. I’ve tried being the nice person. It seems that the only way that I’ll ever be liked is if I’m a prick or something. So, I really don’t know or understand it. ES: Well, it seems that our time is running low, so do you have any closing statements? Jesse: Ivan, 3B, and anyone else out there that has a problem with me. Watch your backs, It’s Time For An Attitude Adjustment! Now, it seems that Jesse has been having a rough time here in OSW. Not only because of his past shames, but also because of the fact that no one else seems to like the man for no apparent reasons. In my humble this man seems to have his head on his shoulders, is very physically sound, and knows exactly what he wants out of OSW. This is the end of Overflow for this week. I am Edward Saunders, good-day.
