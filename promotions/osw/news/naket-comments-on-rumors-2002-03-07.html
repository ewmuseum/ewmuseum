Naket Comments on Rumors

7 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With rumors flying around the locker room in OSW days before it’s very first PPV event, Crowning Glory, OSW reporters were quick to catch up to Scott Naket to question him on the rumor and the discussions between him and Jesse Ramey. “So, we talk to each other backstage.” Naket responded, “Big deal, I talk with other people backstage all the time!” Our reporter then mentioned The Polish Punisher, Tom Walczak’s comments about Naket and Ramey being an “item” backstage. Naket quickly turned his head to the reporter and grinned. “Tom said that?” Naket asked, laughing under his breath. “I didn’t even know Tom knew how to speak English that well. Tom, we just talk. From what I know, you and Ivan just talk all the time. Why no one has ever commented on you guys being a couple baffles my mind. I mean, before Tempest came along, all Ivan did was hang around with guys.” Our reporter quickly reminded Naket that this was the Red Army he was referring too, and it wouldn’t be wise to be making comments like that in front of a camera. Naket just responds with a sigh, and looks at the reporter. “Hey, I used to be in their position. They want people to talk like that about them. No one does though, and it leaves their huge egos wanting food. They need a ego feeding. But no one feeds their egos but themselves.” Naket stops, and looks around the hall, and shakes his head. “I’ve had enough of this. First you ask me about Ramey, and let me remind you, we just talk. But talking about Ramey leads to me talking smack about the Red Army, and right now, that’s the last thing I need to deal with. This interview is over.” With that, Naket turned and walked away from us. We still were unable to reach Jesse Ramey for comment.
