GV Responds to Taylor

26 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OSW reporters caught up with Graphic Violence who was at his home recovering from a grueling PPV match he had with Rhett Locke this past Sunday. "I don't know who the mother fucker thinks he is. Saying the big names have kept him down. That he's 'Taylor made for OSW'. Some of us fit this description huh Taylor? The fact of the matter is you were part of a crap federation over there at NTICW. You were a piece of shit champion and if I was there I would of snapped you in half like a twig. You want to be like me. Just like everyone else out there. They all want my success and when they don't get it, it's because I was holding them back. If you were any good at what you did Taylor you would of been main eventing in the big time feds where I was at. Where the big dogs play. TW2k, Ass-ylum, 21w, The CWL...and the list goes on. Hell, the fWo is practically sucking my nuts right now. I could be there as well. You finally made it to a big time fed Taylor. I'm surprised they picked up your sorry contract. But let me be the first to congradulate you. Since it seems that no one else on the OSW pussy roster is going open their mouths and respond to your little quote. You might of been a champion in that fed where I'd whip my ass with you and all the other wrestlers, but here in OSW pal...you're not going to amount to anything. You won't be main eventing here. You'll be opening the show. You'll be cleaning toliets by the years end. And keep your little 220 pounds frame out of my way. Because I'll be sure to rearrange every body part of yours to where a fuckin' dog will look better than you. Stay the fuck out of my way Taylor, and learn to think about what you say before you speak it. I am a name. I am a legend. I am a main eventer. And you're a piece of shit who should be shinning my boots. Understand this, and you might live to see another day here in OSW." Graphic Violence made it clear to OSW staff that he does not intend to go after Taylor, but if paths do cross at a certian point in time that he has no problems with "breaking his neck". Graphic Violence hinted at continuing to make Rhett Lockes life a living hell. Watch Slam! this week to see what he'll do.
