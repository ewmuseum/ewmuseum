Aimz on MTV's TRL

12 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Amy Campbell recently appeared on an episode of MTV's hit show, Total Request Live with Carson Daly, during which, one of MTV's most embarassing moments, outside of any Doug Troy appearance, ensued. While live, nation-wide, Campbell not only broke a formal agreement to all MTV guests stating rules and regulations of speech by using various expletives, but she topped things off by not only commenting on Mr. Daly's childhood weight, but by ending the interview abruptly by referring to him as a "fucking retard" as security escorted her quite forcefully off set. Aimz' comments to the media outside of the building were few and far between, many including the terms "big-ass MTV executives think they can keep me from free speech..." Aimz' comments after the $14,000 fine for breaching contract was laid on her by Music Television (MTV) were considerably harsher. "They're fining me for saying fuck, a word that EVERY single kid hears nightly when their parents are tossing fucking dishes at one another in yet another "I'm divorcing you!" type fight. They hear it every single day in rap music, MTV censors it...big damn deal! So what if I spoke my mind?! It's their own fucking fault for not putting a delay on the show so they could censor. I mean, what the hell would it kill them just to call the show "Total Request Not-So Live" so they could make sure somebody like me wouldn't come on and excercise that little thing that we as Canadian and American citizens have...oh, I dunno, FREE FUCKING SPEECH!? Ugh! I despise corporations, I don't care how rich they are! It's time MTV got a bite in the ass. I've got a decent lawsuit on my hands here! I could get enough publicity and cash from them not only to pay that fine, but bring the bitches down a notch! Let's see how "Awesome" MTV is when every 12-year-old girl in North America knows that they don't want them to say what they think, only what the company lets them think." Aimz, unfortunately, was not done. In short? MTV can eat my ass with a silver spoon." Campbell wasn't exactly brief, nor was she entirely rational but that's never been her way. Apparently a court case has come of this, Aimz claiming that MTV has violated her basic human right of free speech." She's got the media, and various parental groups, locked, cocked and ready to unload on her once again. This can only prove to have an interesting outcome. More on this topic as it progresses. A court date has yet to be announced.
