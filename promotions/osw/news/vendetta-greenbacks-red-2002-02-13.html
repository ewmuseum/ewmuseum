Vendetta Greenbacks Red?

13 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Communism, at its core, is a principle of economics. One has to wonder, then, where the Red Army manages to find its money. According to wrestlingtattler.com, that money may be coming from right inside OSW... namely, from one Clay Vendetta. "Vendetta's got a lot of money from a mystery 'inheritance', a corporation (Classic Enterprises) which nobody seems to know any details about, and a headquarters in Miami, right across the pond from Cuba," said R.J. Persimmon, WrestlingTattler reporter. "Given that his desire for winning by any means in OSW is similar to that of Ivan Stanislav's and the Red Army's, I think it's quite credible that Vendetta could be bankrolling some of their operations." Vendetta had no official comment on the situation.
