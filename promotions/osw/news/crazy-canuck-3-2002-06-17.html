OSW — Column

Crazy Canuck #3

17 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WHOA! Everyone praise the Lord, your favourite Canadian is back with the third installment of your favourite column! Well, I had to find the time to put down the Kleenex now that Hockey’s over and drink my last Molson Ex to come and write this column. So, without further delay and filler, here are my Pay-Per-View Predictions… Anywhere Falls Match: Graphic Violence vs. Rhett Locke Well if it isn’t my favourite match of the night? First question, why is this the opener? It should be the Upper Card, eh? Well, that’s why the fine folks at OS-Dub have me writing opinion pieces and not in Marketing. Oh well, back to the match. For all you old die-hard ECW fans, this is going to have hardcore written all over it, and probably even in the competitors blood! Normally, I’d pick Rhett Locke here. I thought he was a bad-ass, but lately it’s looked like he’s a pussy. Did he say he was afraid of someone? Well, that’s where you can tell he’s not Canadian. With confidence like that, GV is going to shred him. My pick goes out to that mean-ass Stevie Shadows. Winner: Graphic Violence El Drago vs. Michel Krieg Hmmm…I’m just starting to get into this feud, so I hope they don’t kill it. In my opinion, they haven’t really made a big splash until lately. Hopefully they keep this up, but of course they’d have to spice it up. Maybe throw in something to spice it up, like have the Red Army play a more active role. El Drago could maybe bring in some other faces to help him out. As for the match itself, it’s a toss up. I’m going to have a coin-toss a la the CFL Style. The winner is, heads! Winner: Michel Krieg Hollow Mountain: Reno Davis vs. “The Russian Bear” Ivan Stanislav Everybody thinks Reno is going to get pulverized. But when you look at the statistics, who wouldn’t? Ivan should just crush him during this match. But you know what Ivan did? He showed a HUGE weakness after the Tempest Incident. Reno, my favourite Canuck, should just pull something like he did the other week, pull on Ivan’s strings. Reno knows he can take down Ivan with brains and my money is on him pulling off the monster upset win. Winner: Reno Davis Sledgehammer Match: Jim Axtell vs. “Perfection Personified” Kory Storm The match between OSW’s newest rookies is the next match. It looks like it’ll be a good match with some intense action if the sledgehammer gets put into play at anytime throughout the match. My money is on Axtell though and I think Kory will exact his revenge after the bout. Winner: Jim Axtell Pink Slip Match: “Classic” Clay Vendetta vs. Griff Truxell This match could make Clay in my opinion, even though I like both these guys. Griff has really got my eye converting me to a believer with his work over the past few weeks with his work. Of course though, I mark uncontrollably for Clay. This match is interesting though, but it favours Griff because he’s putting some generic limousine on the line, when Clay is putting a frickin’ 1963 Corvette as ante. This match could really put Clay at the level I want him, so I’m hoping he pulls out a win. Winner: Clay Vendetta Defunct UWA World Title Ladder Match: Jesus St. Crux vs. Jason Winters vs. Kanaida vs. Ian Wright Hmm…for the third time now, I’m still not high on this feud. I like Wright, iffy on Jesus and not into either Kanaida or Winters. Kanaida dropped the ball when he lost to that security-punk-turned-wrestler. Oh well, I hope Wright kills everyone and Jesus goes on for the Cruiserweight Title. Winner: Ian Wright OSW Cruiserweight Championship: Amy “Aimz” Campbell vs. Corg Corg has really come into his own as of late, even though I wasn’t fond of him in the past. Amy has really pissed me off as of late, considering her jump to the Elite. Who will win here? Well, it’s really out there. I’m thinking that Amy is going to take it after Civil Disobedience beat Corg into the floor. Winner: Amy Campbell OSW Tag Team Championship, Weapon of Choice Match: Heresian Bloodlust vs. Civil Disobedience vs. Da Westsidaz Heresian Bloodlust has really been destroying things lately. Civil Disobedience may have the Elite and Da Westsidaz’ may have the crowd, but Heresian Bloodlust is seemingly a run-away freight train as of late. I don’t think either team will be able to take the titles off them as of yet…but with a stipulation like that, you can expect this to end up the match of the night. Winner: Heresian Bloodlust OSW World Heavyweight Title: Tom Walczak vs. Doug Troy This match is great. I like the feud, I like the workers, I like the concept. But, most of all, I like Doug. Tom is good and he deserved the push, but he’s not ready to down a fighter like Troy. Personally, I like Doug’s chances in this one, but he has to keep an eye out for the Red Army. Winner: Doug Troy King of the Mountain HOLY SHIT, WHAT A MATCH THIS IS GOING TO BE!!!! With every wrestler in the match, it’s really a toss up, but here’s my prediction. The final two in this match will be Rhett Locke and Sebastian Toomes, but it ends up with the Elite foiling Locke’s chances. Sebastian becomes No. 1 Contender and Doug starts sweating. Winner: Sebastian Toomes Other predictions for Sunday are the opening of new feuds. I think Propaganda will continue his feud with Casey, Sebastian and the Elite will set their eyes on Troy. I think the Red Army will lock El Drago in their sights, but he’ll get some back up. Wonder should challenge the new CW Champ to start a feud there and Epitome of Evil may finally return from undisclosed injuries. Until next time, this is your favourite patriotic Canadian, saying eat your poutine and don’t forget your ehs.
