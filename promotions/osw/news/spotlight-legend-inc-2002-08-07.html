Spotlight: Legend Inc.

7 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanda: Hello there OSW fans! It's Shanda Russell here with another Wrestler Spotlight! With me today is newly signed member of the OSW roster, Tom Lexian, one half of Legend Inc! Thank you for this time, Tom! Legend Inc: No problem, Shanda. Always glad to speak for the fans. Shanda: Well, first things first! You entrance into the OSW was one of the most unexpected and unorthodox entrances in our history to date, particularly with the world thinking that Tuff Tom and Mean Mike of Disorderly Conduct were the newest signees. Any word on Disorderly Conduct? What of the circumstances of your signing, was it in the works for a long time? Legend Inc: I had been in contract talks with Jane Reagan for only a short period of time. Basically, the entire contract was finalized after the main event at PCW's Wrestle Vessel PPV, when I finally realized that it was time for me to finally hit them where I knew it would hurt the most - jumping to OSW. As for Disorderly Conduct, I used to respect them, but now I realize they are just a joke. Hired goons for the Think Tank... Shanda: Speaking of the Think Tank, yourself and Mike Extreme were a bit of a surprise for Alan Kriegman and Trashcan Man. What are your thoughts on the entire Think Tank? Legend Inc: Goons, thugs, scum of the earth...I think that about sums it up for me. I've seen guys like them in power, and it's always bad news for the fans and for guys like me who try to play by the rules. They only look out for themselves and they'll break rules to fit their needs. (chuckles) I guess what goes around comes around, and Kriegman and Trashcan Man both got what was "coming" to them at last week's Slam! Shanda: They sure did! Now naturally, The Extreme Legends are known as being a very dominant tag team and you have entered just as the OSW Tag Title Tournament has begun. Thoughts on the Tag Tournament? Should we expect a late entrant? Legend Inc: Anything is possible, at least that's what the OSW motto should be. Expect the unexpected, and I wouldn't be surprised if we saw some more teams coming out from nowhere and entering or even if the new president has something up his sleeve. All I know is that myself and Mike Extreme have been champions before and we pride ourselves on being one of the best tag champions in the world. Those are words that have been backed up and proven. Shanda: Fascinating. OSW has been blessed with some very strong Tag Teams in the past. Civil Disobedience, Heresian Bloodlust, and Da Westsidaz. What do you think will separate the Extreme Legends from the rest of the competition? Legend Inc: Those are all fine teams, and I don't doubt that they will give EL a lot of hard fought competition, but there's a difference between a team and brothers. You see, those teams are just two guys that got together. Myself and Mike Extreme, we're like brothers, and family never lets family down. We may not be brothers by blood, and sometimes I feel we're closer than brothers, and that's the edge we have over everyone else. Shanda: Interesting. Other than titles, is there anything in particular you yourself have set out to accomplish in the OSW? Anybody in particular you would like to fight in the ring? Legend Inc: Well, I always felt that there was unfinished business between myself and Kid Wonder. He's always had this hatred for me, something I could never understand, so one of these days I would like to step into the ring with the teen idol. There is also Trevor Hawke, one half of Civil Disobedience, who I met in the RPG3 tournament. It was a hard fought battle, and I wouldn't doubt it if he tried to exact some revenge. Shanda: Well, I think that just about wraps out this edition of Wrestler Spotlight. Before we go, any words for the fans of the OSW or perhaps a member of the roster? Legend Inc: I just want to tell the fans that a new age for OSW is about to dawn, one without lawlessness and rampant chaos occurring. And to one Think Tank, your days are numbered. Thank you, Shanda.
