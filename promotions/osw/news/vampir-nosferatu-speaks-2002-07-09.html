Vampir Nosferatu Speaks

9 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Early Monday morning, reporters caught up with Vampir Nosferatu who had a few things to say regarding the issues concerning Damon Trask and 'Classic' Clay Vendetta. "In less than five days, Vendetta and his little lackey Gibson face The Cult in another soon to be bloodspilling affair," growled Prince of Disease. "Yet, Vendetta seems more concerned with what Trask or The News seem to think about him. Vendetta, take your head out of your vain ass and listen very carefully. Continue to argue like a little school girl with Trask for your distractions will have more of a painful and disfiguring consequence if you face the Cult with your mind wondering somwwhere else." Vampir also offered a potential scoop for News reporter, Damon Trask. "If Trask wants a story, then I am sure the unmerciful dismembering of your soul will do nicely for him. And oh, one final note before I dissappear into the darkness, it would be in your best interest if you keep an eye on that little vixen of yours. Afterall, it would be very disfortunate if something serious were to happen to Miss Weathers." With those final words, Vampir stepped back into the shadows and dissappears. We will bring you more on this story as it becomes available.
