New Acting CEO?

30 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rampant Internet speculation is that OSW commissioner and acting CEO, Ed, is overwhelmed with the day-to-day mundane tasks of his acting CEO office, and plans to introduce a new acting CEO this Saturday night on Slam. Several dozen names has been thrown out as possibilities for the new post, with the most prominant individuals being former UFW CEO Jack Orion, where Ed started his career, as well as PCW Hall of Famer, Kremmen, and The Phantom Booker himself. There has been no confirmation or denial of the report from OSW headquarters. We will bring you more on this story should any new information surface.
