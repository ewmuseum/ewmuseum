Spotlight : Graphic Violence

4 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanda: Hello OSW fans! I'm Shanda Russell here with another edition of Wrestler Spotlight! I'm here at George Pearson Centre's Institution for the Mentally Ill, and sitting inches from me, seperated by a protective plastic, is OSW's own Graphic Violence! Thank you for this time, GV. Can I call you GV? Graphic V: Yeah. Call me GV. Anything else you call me I'll rip off your lips and kiss my ass with them. Shanda: Um..yes..well then....GV..uh..how is it going? Do you like the George Pearson Facility? Graphic V: How's it going? How the FUCK do you think i'm doing? I'm talking to you behind a glass wall bitch! Oh i'm just doing fuckin' great. Stupid whore. Go cook something. Shanda: I'd appreciate it if you didn't speak to me in that way. I am a professional woman! Graphic V: Ok, well atleast you're not trying to wrestle. They gave you the correct job for a woman and that's being the bosses bitch. Shanda: Ehem...anyway.....what have you been diagnosed as? Psychotic, maybe? Graphic V: They haven't diagnosed me with anything yet. Nothing but tests, needles, medicine and sitting with that jackass doctor for hours. Shanda: Any word on when you'll be released? Graphic V: No. They don't tell you shit in this place. They're gonna let me out one way or the other. Trust me on that one. Shanda: That sounds like a threat.... Graphic V: Me, do threats? C'mon now. Shanda: Yes well.....if I may ask. You've recently had several...shocking run ins with Rhett Locke..you two seem destined to kill each other. Why is that? Why such hostility? Graphic V: It just started out as some fun to show that bitch Lillith that she shouldn't fuck with me. But then it just got VERY person with Rhett. I hate Rhett Locke with every inch of my body and I will not stop kicking his pathetic ass until he can no longer compete in this game. Editors Note: We at the OSW apologize for the language of Graphic Violence. While we print what he says, we do not condone nor uphold his own racial beliefs. Shanda: Well....I suppose that sums up that. What about the other people in the OSW. There are many that could be considered ruthless. Ivan Stanislav for one. Frankie Bones is another. What sets you apart from men like that, if any? Graphic V: Ivan is a fuckin' pussy. He needs an army of men to back him up. I have NEVER had to have anyone back me up. I do my dirty work on my own. He's not worth my time. As for Frankie Bones...I hear that mans been bitching about not getting TV time. Well maybe that's because his black ass isn't good enough to get TV time. They don't let dirty ni(bleep) with gold teeth take up valueable time on television. Shanda: GV..I take great offense to that last remark! Graphic V: Shut up you monkey. Eat a bananna and do your fuckin' job which is to interview me. Nobody gives a fuck about your feelings. Shanda: I think..I think I'm done with this interview, Mr. Violence Graphic V: Eh. Run. Run like everyone else runs from me. *punches glass* YOU DUMB BITCH! YOU'RE NEXT!
