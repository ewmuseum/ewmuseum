Ivan to Speak on Slam

1 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Immediately following his match against El Drago, Ivan Stanislav scoured the backstage area for the men that cost him his match against Drago: Michel Krieg, Kory Storm, and Frankie Bones. While Stanislav was unable to find the three culprits, he was, as always, sure to let the world know what he was thinking and screamed at nearby reporters. “Comrade Krieg of Red Army is STILL a communist, regardless if he thinks he’s a capitalist or not! No one leaves MY Army unless I allow them! His inability to work with myself and Tom Walczak is nothing more than a weakness on his part that will quickly be rectified!!” After knocking over a vending machine and pulling a fuse box from the wall, Stanislav continued. “My Red Army has been preaching Communism for over one year! Michel Krieg’s violent move towards Capitalism is pathetic, and it WILL be handled on next Slam!!” Stanislav continued, turning his anger towards the country of Germany itself, “Historically, Germany has been nothing more than a leech that has sucked off of the Capitalist and Communist world! Michel Krieg appears to be nothing more than embodiement of that belief! He will either fall in line behind the wall of Communism, or I will make him submit to the will of Lenin and Stalin!” However, Stanislav also has a rematch against El Drago on Slam in a steel cell. “While a Mexican, Drago is man enough to allow rematch after the injustice that was caused tonight!!” Alexei Ruslan was silent the entire time, but muttered something about, “Wiping him off the face of the Earth.” After his tirade, Stanislav calmed down and later returned, urging the fans of the OSW to watch the upcoming Saturday Slam, as he would make an announcement that would, “Change the OSW forever.” The last major announcement Stanislav made landed eleven guards and Amy Campbell in the hospital. What is Ivan Stanislav concocting in his mind? Best bet for the fans of OSW come Saturday Slam is brace yourself, The Russian Bear is just beginning to flex his muscle.
