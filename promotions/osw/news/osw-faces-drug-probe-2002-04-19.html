OSW Faces Drug Probe

19 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Federal law enforcement officials have launched a probe into what they have called "the rampant and irresponsible" use of drugs by members of the OSW roster. "What we have here is, a failure to use good judgement," said Joe Friday III, of the Federal Drug Enforcement Agency. "They have a convicted drug pusher on their roster, a guy named Angeldust, a trail of marijuana residue in local hotels, and a young lady on the roster who openly glorifies the use of drugs. The main concern is their product is marketed towards young adults, to me and many in the DEA, we believe that this kind of glorification of drug use is almost as if they are going into the school zones themselves and selling them, and frankly we will not stand for it." Friday's strong comments towards OSW did not stop there. "All of the so-called superstars in the OSW should know that they are now on notice," warned Friday. "We will be watching them like hawks and if they slip up, snort, smoke or shoot in up in a manner other then directed by a physician, we are gonna be there to bust them." OSW spokesperson Shanda Russell said while OSW's management does not tolerate drug use among its roster, it also does not engage in regular drug screening of its workers. "We do not pry into their personal lives," said Russell. "We believe that what people do in the privacy of their own homes, dark alley ways or in the confines of sleezy motels are their own business." Russell went on to say that OSW has nothing to hide from DEA officials, but cautioned law enforcement agencies that they would not allow the civil rights of their workers to be violated. We will continue to monitor this potentially violatile situation and bring you updates if and when they become available.
