Spotlight: Switchblade

22 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanda: Hello OSW! It's me, Shanda Russell with another edition of Wrestler Spotlight! Featured today is OSW's own World Champion, Sebastian Toomes, also known as Switchblade! Switchblade, how are you today? Switchblade: I'm spectacular, as usual Shanda: So, I suppose this is a question on most people's mind, how do you feel being the first OSW World Champion? Switchblade: I feel that I, Sebastian Toomes, truly deserved this honor, after working so hard and diligently for it. My sacrifices have not been in vain, so it appears... You don't realize, Shanda, how hard it was to attain such fame and fortune, with all the odds arrayed against me Shanda: Um...but I think it could be said that you won with help from Jane Reagan, I mean, Timo Bolamba was on the verge of winning... Switchblade: Those are lies and heresy, supplied by my detractors. Nothing could be further from the truth. I won the OSW World Title fairly and honorably. Shanda: Interesting...and what exactly would you categorize your relationship with our current President, Jane Reagan? Switchblade: I would categorize it with the Famous 3 W's. Shanda: And those would be? Switchblade: Wet, Wild, and Wonderful. Switchblade: Jane is a stellar human being, a true humanitarian, and an all-around good person. Shanda: But, how can you say that when you've seen her abuse her power? Use fowl language on OSW television? Switchblade: I don't know what wrestling shows you've been watching, but I know that Jane and I are above reproach. Shanda: Right. Well, with the World Title around your waist, you have had many individuals challenging you, mainly in the form of Timo Bolamba and Aimz, your thoughts on this? Switchblade: Aimz is... Well, what more can be said of Aimz that hasn't already been said? And Timo? He's a fine human being as well. If you're expecting me to cut them apart in this interview, you've got another thing coming. A true champion doesn't lower himself to useless pot-shots and verbal barrages. He proves himself in the ring Shanda: Recently, if I may say, you had cutting remarks towards Ivan Stanislav, how do you answer that? And your thoughts on Ivan's rebuttal? Switchblade: Once again, you must be confusing me with someone else. Ivan is a paragon of Communistic virtue, and I would be remiss if I were to talk behind his back. Shanda: Perhaps I was...mistaken. Well, Switchblade, what other goals have you set for yourself within the OSW? Obviously gaining the World Title is an excellent start. Switchblade: Well, for starters, helping Jane reduce the amount of God-awful filth that's seeped into this federation. Promos about gays, committing crimes, promoting violence and sex... This won't do at all. If we are to lead OSW to the top where it so rightfully belongs, we need a more clean show. I know I would NEVER do promos with such degenerate filth in them. Shanda: Are you taking medication? Switchblade: Yes, the sweet balm of Jane Reagan's love. She is such a lovely woman, one look at her and I feel good again. Shanda: Yes, well...anyway....thank you for the...informative and true to form interview Mr. Toomes...any last comments? Switchblade: No, Shanda. Anyway, I am off to do some charity work. After all, the OSW World Champion has to set an example to the younger generation.
