Ed Clings to Life

19 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Former OSW Commissioner, Ed, is clinging to life thanks to help from life support machines in a Cleveland area hospital, thanks to the beating he suffered at the hands of the Think Tank last Saturday night on Slam! "Its a day-to-day, hour-to-hour, minute-by-minute fight," said OSW physician, Dr. Grabor Sakuisenkoff, who has been monitoring Ed at the hospital. "The sad thing is, however, is if even if he manages to survive, he will likely never walk again." Sakuisenkoff confirmed earlier speculation that the most of the damage to Ed's spinal column was caused by his long time friend, Trashcan Man. "That cradle DDT off the stage certainly is the cause of this," said Sakuisenkoff. "I am finding it difficult, as a true professional, to continue in my capacity with OSW with this kind of activity going on." We will bring you more on Ed's condition, as news develops.
