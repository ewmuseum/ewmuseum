Media Guide Flap

9 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tempers are flaring in the locker room over "Angeldust" Nick Casey and Michel Krieg's photos being replaced on the front cover of the OSW media guide, in order to make room for recent signees Graphic Violence and Jason Winters. "Das not fair," bellowed Krieg, when asked for comment. "Just shows that capitalist dogs will always try to hold down socialist powers." Casey was visibly upset by the happenings, but refused to comment. Graphic Violence was full of things to say about appearing on the Media Guilde. "Too bad for them," said Graphic Violence, with a smirk. "It's the first thing OSW has done right, putting a proven commidity like me on the cover, and removing those two nobodies. If I were those two, I would be pissed off that two women are still on the cover, while they got the boot. I mean, for those two to be bumped, while those useless, ragged, leathery-assed whores got front page press? Its a sad commentary on their careers, as well as the PR department of OSW." This is the third time that Graphic Violence has made derrogatory remarks about female wrestlers since his arrival in Old School Wrestling, who was already under fire from women's groups thanks to various actions of OSW World Champion, Sebastian Toomes in the past. Aimz and Lillith were unreachable for comment, while OSW declined to make a statement on the subject. We will continue to monitor this story and bring you an update as news develops.
