Keyson Places First

31 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OSW officials announced the 'The Darkness' Anna Keyson placed first in the first leg of the competition of the Queen of Extreme tournament, earning herself five points. "We felt Anna put on a show," said OSW spokesperson, Shanda Russell. "She had style, she had flare and she had elegance, so she picked up the win in very tight voting." Lillith's short, but bloody, segment earned her second place and three points, while Brandi Wilson and Bethany Yamato tied for third, each earning a half a point. "Anyone who is willing shed their own blood to make a statement is someone we will be interested in," said Russell, referring to Lillith. "As far as Bethany and Brandi's promos, all the male judges on the panel were torn between the gratuitous nudity of Bethany's segments, and lord knows how the boys love midgets." The next stage of the Queen of Extreme Competition will pit the seven remaining competitors against one another in an over the top battle royal, next week on Slam! The winner of next week's Battle Royal will earn 10 points, while the second through sixth place competitors will earn seven, five, three, two and one point for their efforts. The first lady eliminated during the rumble is eliminated from the competition. We will bring you more on Queen of Extreme Tourney as it develops.
