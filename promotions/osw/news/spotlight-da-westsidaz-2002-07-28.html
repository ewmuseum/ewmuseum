Spotlight: Da Westsidaz

28 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanda: Hello there OSW Fans, it's me, Shanda Russell again with another Wrestler Spotlight. This edition features Riptah from the OSW tag team Da Westsidaz. Riptah, thanks so much for allowing for this interview! Riptah: No problem Shanda. I'm happy to be here. Shanda: I notice that Short Circuit isn't here. Is he doing okay? This Saturday Slam was a rough one to say the least. Riptah: Luckily SC wasn't seriously injured. He's back in his crib just resting up, y'know, gettin' pumped. I talked to him, he's more mad than hurt. Shanda: And understandable. It appears that while Da Westsidaz have always been a solid tag team, you've also got a tremendous amount of animosity towards The Think Tank. Could you comment on that? Riptah: Well, nobody can just stand there and take the shit they've been doing lately. It really ain't like us to get this emotional in the job, but how can you not with what's been going on lately? The Tank has to be told that this has to stop. They start with all this garbage about the Vax, then the kidnappings, then they even assault us and pull that shit with the titles! Normally we'd just whup their ass, but after that stuff with Ed... Nobody can just stand for someone they know and respect to get done in like that... Shanda: Yes...speaking of Ed, you two are veterans of the PCW, much like he was so obviously you knew him longer than most of the people in the OSW. What can you say about Ed and the entire....situation? Riptah: Well Ed was one helluva guy. I remember he used to buy me n' SC a drink a lot after some tough shows, y'know, to congratulate us and shit. He's was great guy, PCW Hall-of-Famer deservedly so. He was a little more reckless than he shoulda been, down right crazy most of the time. Nobody deserves to be stabbed in the back by their homey like he got it from Trashcan Man. The shit makes me sick and I'll be damned if Ed's memory goes out with a lil' vigil. That's not the way he'd want it. Shanda: It appears that the rest of the OSW is not standing for what The Think Tank did. It seems rather strange to see Sebastian Toomes fighting for what appears to be the good of the federation. Perhaps even stranger is seeing Ivan Stanislav, El Drago, and a newly returned Tempest fighting too. Do you think it's possible to defeat the Think Tank on your own? Or do you think the forces of all those mentioned will need to be brought together to ultimately win? Riptah: Well, I ain't gunna lie to a fine lookin' woman like you. I don't like Stanislav or Toomes. It'll be a cold day in hell before I teamed up with Toomes and I still ain't 100% sure Ivan is on the level. Now, as for Tempest and El Drago, I don't know 'em, but they seem fine by me. As much as I'd like Da Westsidaz' to handle Da Westsidaz' problems, this ain't just my problem. I think that if I wanna have a chance against the Tank, I'ma need more than just SC. As long as Stanislav and his crew fight against the Tank I'm be down fighting alongside them. Don't mean I like the big lug though. Shanda: Understandable, and I would be careful too. Onto other matters though. Da Westsidaz have been an explosive tag team throughout the history of the OSW, and the PCW in the past. What do you think makes it such an unstoppable team? Riptah: Well, definitely I think that it's gotta be our skills as a team. Most teams are either two big monsters or two cruiserweights. Take our last two enemies for example, Civil D and Heresian Bloodlust. Bloodlust were giants and Civil Disobedience we're quick. Since me n' SC are physical opposites, it makes it tough when matched up against teams like them. But we have great in-ring sense. Watch our matches, we always have quick tags, double team moves and most of all, we cut the ring off from our opponents. I don't think there is any other team out there that can work a tag match like Da Westsidaz. Shanda: What are the major differences between the OSW and the PCW, tag team wise, that you can see? Riptah: Well, it's no secret in PCW it was pretty much Da Westsidaz vs. The Blood Brothers. There wasn't any other competition that stuck through it. Occasionally we'd have some problems with an Asylum tag team or Red Army tag team, but other than that we had run of the place. In OSW, there's so much more competition that comes up. We got all of the Tank, Civil D, a bunch of Cult guys, NC-17, the Throwbacks, I mean the list is huge. Especially now with the tournament, I wouldn't be surprised if some kick-ass teams from other feds showed up. It's a lot tougher, but all in all, it just makes us realize we gotta perform better. And we do, simple as that. Shanda: Fascinating. What does the future have in store for Da Westsidaz? Riptah: Think of the rest of our career the same way as the other half. Lotsa wins, lotsa titles, lotsa ass-kicking and most of all, unending proof of why Da Westside is Da Bestside.
