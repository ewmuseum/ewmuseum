Vendetta Fires On Trask

8 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After this week's episode of Slam!, OSW's "Classic" Clay Vendetta had a response for The News editor Damon Trask, who verbally ran down Vendetta a few days prior. "So now Trask's got a 'legal team', huh?" queried the much beloved, OSW superstar. "I guess him and his butt-buddies at the News got tired of playing 'doctor' and now they're playing 'lawyer'. Hey, if Trask wants to make excuses, fine. But I think somebody with an intellect should let him know that if he HAD any credibility, he'd be losing it by letting his grudge against me interfere with his coverage of OSW." Vendetta wasn't finished there, sending out a personal challenge to Trask. "Trask, I know you wish you could be in the ring," growled Vendetta. "I know you'd even give up your vagina to just get in the ring once and do the job to Steven Smith, if you haven't done the job to him OUTSIDE of the ring already. Stop being a little jealous bitch and start at least pretending to be a credible journalist, and then you can start to act like you're anywhere NEAR the league of OSW's True Classic." We, of course, will continue to monitor this story and bring you updates as they become available.
