News About Da Westsidaz

21 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OSW Officials have released a statement moments ago regarding Da Westsidaz' latest condition as of the end of SLAM! "Da Westsidaz have suffered multiple concussions and are very beaten up," said OSW spokesperson, Shanda Russell from Pittsburgh General Memorial Hospital. "Riptah still hasn't come to as Short Circuit has been going in and out of consciousness. We've not been able to tell the tag team of the Think Tank's action about there Tag Titles. We will make sure they know exactly what happened, as they will surely not be happy about it." Reporters then asked Shanda to elaborate on Da Westsidaz' status in OSW. "Well, the two of them obviously got the short end of the stick and it's a given that they well be allowed to continue in OSW, despite their no-show," said Russell. "As for in-ring capabilities, Riptah or Short Circuit may not be physically able to compete in the Tag Tournament, but we know from past experiences that they aren't going to worry about their condition. Off the record, I can assure that Andrew and Marcus will be out for not only the Tag Titles, but will have a personal vendetta with the Think Tank throughout the tournament." Once an official statement has been made by the medical staff at Pittsburgh Hospital, then we will definitely bring it to you.
