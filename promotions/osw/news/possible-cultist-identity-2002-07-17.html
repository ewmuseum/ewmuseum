Possible Cultist Identity?

17 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rumours are flying wild these days as OSW fans are posed with one question, among others, as we are all sure Dave Gibson and Clay Vendetta are asking: Who is the Cultist? “The Cultist of which you speak is not only going to help in the destruction of The Throwbacks, but join The Cult in our quest to annihilate all and reap the souls of many…stay tuned folks,” were the words of OSW’s very own Hessian. However Vampir Nosferatu had other things to say… “He is a beast unlike any other…unluckily for you, it won’t be until you are staring back through bloodied eye and dismembered head that you realise the Cultist is that which you fear…prepare for a new nightmare, prepare for The Cult,” said the always graphic, Vampir Nosferatu. Many assume it could be someone from within the ranks of OSW, be it Rhett Locke or Lillith, though other guesses are flying much further afield, such as Nerezza or even Rave Caprino to be doing the dirty underhanded work of The Cult. Who the Cultist is? Nobody knows, but be sure at some point they will be revealed. Until then one can only wonder what dastardly acts The Cult, along with their Cultist will get up to in the coming weeks. One thing is for sure; it probably isn’t Minion the Imp unless he grew some overnight. Tune in to Slam! this week to see if any *non-violent* clues are dropped as to who this robed individual could be…
