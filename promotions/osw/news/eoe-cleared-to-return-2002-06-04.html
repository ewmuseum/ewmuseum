EoE Cleared to Return

4 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After suffering a cracked right eye socket bone and a severe laceration from a cowardly attack by Jack Miller, OSW superstar Epitome of Evil has been medically cleared to return to the ring by OSW physicians. "We expect a big guy like EoE to be a little rusty after bing out so long," said OSW spokesperson, Shanda Russell. "He has kept himself in great shape, but ring rust isn't something you can shake off in the workout room." EoE himself was reached for comment, where he freely discussed several details of his injury, and his upcoming return. "The injury was nasty as hell, almost as nasty as your job," said EoE, when asked by our own Shanda Russell about his injury. He was equally as vocal when asked about when he will return to the ring. "That's not important, the important thing is, who will be my victim when I get ready to bring the pain," said EoE. "Incase nobody knew, I'm very pissed off." When asked if he had any comments on Jack Miller or Nick Casey, a sly smile crossed EoE's dark features. "As for Jack, he'd better check his insurance policy," said EoE. "Nick, well let's just say he got lucky, but I never forgive or forget." When will EOE return and who will be his victim? Stay tuned to Slam on Saturday nights to find out!
