Kanaida Signs w/OSW

2 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After weeks of little or no response to their contractural overtures to former UWA World Champion, Kanaida, OSW officials were ecstatic to receive word that the "Northern Wonder" was ready to do a deal. "He called out of the blue," said OSW Commissioner/Acting CEO, Ed. "I was getting a little frustrated from the weeks of no communication in regard to our offer, but all is well that ends well." Kanaida, is the third former UWA superstar to sign with OSW in the past three weeks. Fellow former UWA superstars, Jason Winters and Jesus St. Crux, will face off in their OSW debuts this Saturday night on Slam! The net is already buzzing with rumors about the possibility of Highly Unstable, the UWA's premier stable consisting of Jason Winters, Kanaida and Ian Wright, reforming on OSW. OSW officials refused to comment about their interest in Wright, and the Irishman was unavailable for comment. We will bring you more on this story as news develops.
