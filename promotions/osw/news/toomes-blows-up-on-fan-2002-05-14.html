Toomes Blows Up on Fan

14 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Its no secret that OSW World Heavyweight Champion, Sebastian Toomes, has been going through a rough patch as of late. His girlfriend and closest OSW ally, OSW Owner, Jane Reagan, is missing. His muscle, in the form of one Monster they call Meanstreak, was institutionalized. His two confidants, Frankie Bones and Drew Hostyle, are at odds with one another. Now, several witnesses have reported that Toomes verbally assaulted a man at Chicago's O'Hare Airport. "It was awful," said Maria Conchita Gonzalez, of O'Hare Cleaning Services, through an interpreter. "He was yelled at a man just for being fat. It was quite sad." The man in question, one Cecil Flattery, who stands 6'2 and weighs well over 300 pounds, shrugged off the incident. "I was too busy enjoying my twinkie and contemplating the composition of my 2000 point Squat army to worry about what he had to say," said Flattery. "Much like hobgoblins in Warhammer Fantasy, he is obviously affected by stupidy. I suppose during that phase, he must have rolled a "1" prior to acting." Switchblade refused to comment on the story. Toomes is scheduled to defend his OSW World Heavyweight Title this Saturday night against Doug Troy.
