Blaze Speaks Out

6 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Black Ice" Matt Blaze spoke out against the "so-called" athletes in the OSW lockerroom, claiming that senior wrestlers are avoiding hooking up with him in the ring, while at an autograph signing late Wednesday night. "The are definately ducking me," said Blaze. "I don't know if they are intimidated by my sheer physical ability, feel overshadowed by my incredible charisma, or if they are just afraid to be one of my stepping stones on my way to the top, but they are definately scared to face me." Blaze refused to mention any wrestler in particular that was avoiding him, but added that there is resentment towards him because he turned down an NFL contract to pursue wrestling. "The entire 'old-boy network' of so-called professional wrestlers will do anything they can to keep a cross-over talent like myself down," scoffed Blaze. "OSW management needs to step in and tell these guys to do their damn j-o-b-s and stop worry about personal glory." OSW management refused to comment on Blaze's remarks, which are sure to bring heat upon the former All-American football star from other members of the OSW lockerroom. We will bring you more on this story as it develops.
