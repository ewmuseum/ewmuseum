Spotlight: Timo Bolamba

29 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanda: Hey OSW! It’s Shanda Russell again! Welcome to another edition of "Wrestler Spotlight!" I'm sitting here with OSW's own Timo Bolamba! Timo, great to have you here! Timo: Good to be here Shanda, It isn’t often I get a chance to break off of my usual ritual of hanging out and playing Nintendo, but hell, it's worth the drive. Shanda: Nintendo? Shouldn't you be working out? Timo: Ah, but you fail to realize that a wrestlers hand eye coordination can be crucial...plus, who has more heart than Little Mac against Mr. Dream? Shanda: Uh..yes...well...anyway. You recently came to the OSW, how do you like it thus far? Timo: The commodities are a lot nicer than I am used to, although I think after the fiasco involving the light rigging a few weeks ago, the maintenance crew must be monkeys...I investigated this, but to my chagrin, they were mostly guys named Earl and Ted, far from monkey status. Shanda: Yes, Earl and Ted are actually quite good at what they do. Well, next point. You came to the OSW as almost the sole "face" if I may say, against a world of "Heels" including Meanstreak, Switchblade, The Red Army, and many others. Now suddenly, it appears that throughout all of this The Red Army has made a slight turn around, even helping you against Switchblade. Can they be trusted? Do you think it is a ploy? Timo: Well, Top be honest, Ivan even has me stumped. In the world of wrestling though, one never knows who to trust, and recently, I have had a lot of run ins with table scrap pilfering grabasses like Sea-Bass. If Ivan wants to help me, then far be it from me to stop the biggest and baddest dog in the yard from doing so. As for it being a ploy...rarely anything the Army does is considered morally correct, so I will keep one eye open when I sleep, if that helps. Shanda: How about the relationship between yourself and Aimz? Any romance there? Timo: Romance? Naw, I got a woman, and she has this thing about some dude named Jason. Never met the guy, but he sounds as shifty as a six speed transmission. I guess you could call me like a big brother of sorts...one with an amazingly hot sister. Shanda: Jane Reagan, your thoughts on her? Timo: Just like Ronald, she must have a problem with remembering exactly who I am and what I can do. God knows why she sided with SeaBass, but she did, and that’s a choice she is going to have to live with. Sad too, I remember her when I first started in PTC, she was such a nice lady. Such is life Shanda, such is life. Shanda: Yes, that may be true. So, what are your major goals in the OSW? Winning titles? Or something more? Timo: At one point and time, this game was about fame and winning titles, but thanks to a certain jackass from my past, I learned my lesson, and it will never be about fame and titles again. I came to OSW because I want to make a difference in this fed, I want to look SeaBass, Meanstreak, Ivan, Reagan, anyone who gets in my way, and tell them that they will never stop me, the cant stop me, that’s who I am. I forgot that once, I will not forget again. Shanda: Very powerful words, Timo. Well thank you for your time! Any final comments? Timo: Sure, Drink Red Bull, Wave The Flag, and remember to kick ass, because that’s what life is all about! Shanda: Well, you heard it first here folks! Timo Bolamba isn’t fighting for titles or recognition, but for himself! Something other wrestlers in the federation should perhaps look at themselves! Well, this has been another edition of “Wrestler Spotlight!” For the OSW, I’m Shanda Russell!
