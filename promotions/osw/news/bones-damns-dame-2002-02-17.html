Bones Damns Dame

17 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Not everyone was taken in by Michael Dame's heart wrenching recollection of the circumstances surrounding his incarceration, as described to Shanda Russell on Slam! "Did you see that punk-ass, cry baby, sissy-bitch," queried OSW superstar, Frankie Bones. "You know his teary-eyed ass sissied up in the joint." When asked why Bones was so hostile towards a man he never met, Bones was quick to respond. "Did you see how much time they gave that punk," quipped Bones. "I mean, comeon, what does a brotha gotta do to get some love from the camera around here!?! He was crying like a bitch up there about jail time because he was 'set up?' I did jail time, because it is every black man's destiny to set up by cracka 5-0 in our time, and you don't see brotha men cryin', know what I'm saying?" Dame had no comment other then to say "If Frankie Bones wants some, he can get some on this Saturday Night on Slam! We will bring you more on this story as it develops.
