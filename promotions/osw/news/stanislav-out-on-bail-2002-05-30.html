Stanislav Out on Bail

30 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As of 9:00 PM on Thursday, Ivan Stanislav was released from the Winnipeg Security Prison in Winnipeg, Canada, after paying an undisclosed amount of bail money. Stanislav was incarcerated Sunday afternoon after resisting arrest at a Winnipeg Airport. The charge was cruelty to animals, of which Stanislav was charged with for his actions on Saturday Slam against Reno Davis’ pet chimpanzee, ZaC. Stanislav was expected to be released much sooner, but The Russian Federation, in lieu of it’s recent move towards NATO, chose to leave the problem to the Russian Bear as they move away from Communism, to Capitalism. As of Thursday afternoon, Stanislav had no idea of any movement towards NATO, or of his lumberjack duties required of him on Saturday Slam. While Alexei Ruslan was going to pay the bail himself, The Russian Government ordered him not to. Reportedly, the form of money used to pay the bail was in Chinese Yuan, which seems to link Loyal Communist Party Member Wang to the freeing of The Russian Bear, as he was also present for his departure from the prison. Stanislav was outraged at his treatment by Russia, enraged by his treatment by Canada, and livid regarding the recent acceptance of Russia as a Junior Member of NATO. He would not comment while leaving the prison, but made it abundantly clear that he would rectify matters in Russia, and on Slam, vowing to destroy Reno Davis once and for all.
