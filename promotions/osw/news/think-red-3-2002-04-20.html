OSW — Column

Think Red #3

20 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings Comrades! I am Alexei Ruslan here with another edition of Think Red! I thought it would be only customary for myself to write to the millions of fans of Red Army and ensure you that Ivan Stanislav and the communists that fight with him are planning many, many measures to destroy the remnants of Jane Reagan’s ‘machine’ as I type this report. As you have seen last week, the agents of Jane Reagan were soundly defeated by the combined might of Ivan Stanislav, Michael Dame, Timo Bolamba, and yes, Ed. It appears that this team together brings the greatest abilities and strengths of several types of wrestlers and makes a team that is virtually impossible to defeat. From the hardcore style of Ed to the technical ability of Michael Dame, to the aerial prowess of Timo Bolamba and the overwhelming power of Ivan Stanislav, this appears to be a team that will bring order and justice to the OSW and oust the oppressive Jane Reagan and her cohorts. However, this is more than just a single front war. It appears that Tyler Nelson, the former leader of the PCW, is trying to get his claws into the OSW as well by perpetrating many attacks against various OSW wrestlers. And this mysterious I-Cybie dog that seems to cause OSW superstars so much trouble is also an area of concern. However, I would like to assure you that with Ivan Stanislav leading the charge against these interlopers, there will be no other outcome but victory, because that is the only outcome that we of Russia and of Red Army will accept. Therefore, and as usual, I would like to run down upcoming Saturday Slam card and give you my predictions on the matches at hand. As usual, remember that Alexei Ruslan is unbiased, and these are taken from a strictly third person point of view! Tag Team Match Twisted Metal vs. Civil Disobedience A very unorthodox matchup, if I may say so myself. While Civil Disobedience has lost their tag belts to Heresian Bloodlust and have not fought since then, I truly doubt that they will have lost any of their ability from which I am very impressed. As for Twisted Metal, I do not know how effective two artificially intelligent wrestlers truly are. They do not possess the drive to win, they do not possess the ability to think abstractly, and therefore I put them at a decided disadvantage. Alexei’s Pick: Civil Disobedience. It will be very difficult for Civil Disobedience to damage the metal shells of Twisted Metal, but I believe that eventually the speed and the heart of Civil D. will win out in the end. Tag Team Title Match Propaganda/Vampir Nosferatu vs. Heresian Bloodlust It is very difficult not to be biased towards this match. Heresian Bloodlust has tried to repel the attacks of the proud fighters of Red Army: Michel Krieg and Tom Walczak, and have failed in doing so. Our proud communists have fought hard to get a shot at Tag Titles at Pay Per View and personally, I would like to see them win it against Heresian Bloodlust, who is truly formidable opponents. However, Propaganda and Vampir Nosferatu have proven themselves to be competitors unlike any others. For not only having truly unorthodox ways of looking at the world, they are also brutally efficient within the confines of the ring. Alexei’s Pick: Heresian Bloodlust. It will be bloody battle, but Heresian Bloodlust is a team, while Propaganda and Vampir are not truly a team and hate each other for that matter! I can assure you, Tom Walczak and Michel Krieg will be watching from backstage to make sure no foul play ensues. Four Corners, Intergender Tag Team Contest Queen of Extreme Competition Task #4 Corg/Anna Keyson vs Rhett Locke/Lillith vs Kimbusa/Bethany Yamato vs. Kid Wonder/Amanda Needless to say, this should be a match unlike any other on Saturday Slam. The combination of competitors within the ring are quite varied, not only just between being male and female, but skills as well. Corg and Rhett Locke have a very dangerous relationship. Kimbusa and Kid Wonder are at each others throats as well which decidedly divides this match into two distinct camps for the males. As for the females, the teams of Keyson and Lillith are obviously fighting each other as well as Yamato and Amanda. But ultimately this is for the Queen of Extreme crown, which means that all the women are fighting exclusively for that final reward. Alexei’s Pick: Kimbusa/Bethany Yamato. While I am not much of a fan of anyone from Japan, I believe Kimbusa tilts the scales in his teams direction easily. Bethany Yamato is no slouch by any length of the word and while Corg and Locke’s team keep one another busy, Kimbusa will squash Wonder into nothing and pick up win. Singles Match Tom Walczak vs. Reno Davis I do not believe I should have to dwell upon this match very much as I am sure that we are all aware as to who exactly is going to win the following contest. Reno Davis is an impressive wrestler, barely gaining the win against Comrade Krieg last week thanks to interference by his little monkey friend ZaC, but going up against a more seasoned Tom Walczak is something else. We of The Red Army do not condone the actions of Reno Davis and have launched a campaign to ensure that he no longer terrorizes the people of the OSW, and therefore we have sent the last line of defense before Ivan Stanislav to put an end to his escapades: Tom Walczak. Alexei’s Pick: Tom Walczak. The Polish Punisher is all business in this match. He will not be deterred by Reno’s furry little friend and will promptly silence The Unwanted Angel. Singles Match Michel Krieg vs. Doug Troy Again, another no contest regarding the outcome of this contest. Doug Troy went in front of the OSW and spoke of his State of the OSW Address in which he personally blasted The Red Army to the fans, and if I remember correctly the fans stood up against him and would not accept his words. Michel Krieg has risen to the challenge of putting an end to his heresy and silencing him once and for all and we of Red Army stand behind him in his quest. Doug Troy has the advantage of experience that few in the OSW could ever contest, but Michel Krieg has the heart to win and the backing of the fans as well! Alexei’s Pick: Michel Krieg. Doug Troy will put up a fight, that is not a question, but ultimately the might of the people and the power of The Red Army, welded together with the strength of Michel Krieg will overcome Doug Troy and his pathetic attempts to undermine the great movement of the people that we of The Red Army have created! Handicap Match Ivan Stanislav vs. Drew Hostyle/Frankie Bones Again, I find myself in quandary to sound unbiased when regarding this next contest, but I believe that I will be able to succeed in doing so. A handicap match will prove to be difficult for Ivan Stanislav and the hate between 3B and Ivan Stanislav runs very deep ever since their cowardly attack that gave him concussion and broken rib. Will the mighty Russian Bear that has the backing of the people be enough? Or will Bones and Hostyle overcome him? Alexei’s Pick: Ivan Stanislav. Ivan is going to fight an uphill battle, that is for sure and will be hard pressed to defeat the combined strength of Hostyle and Bones. Not to mention fact that there will surely be interference by someone under Jane Reagan since they cannot seem to get job done on their own. In the end, Stanislav will stand triumphant over Hostyle and Bones and deliver them to the feet of the people! Main Event Intergender Handicap Match Sebastian Toomes vs. Aimz/Tempest While some women that enter into wrestling enter only to show off their bodies and get attention from the males of the world, Aimz and Tempest are something much different. They are accomplished wrestlers in their own right and to win the respect of Ivan Stanislav and The Red Army obviously must tell you something about their ability. However, one must also remember that Sebastian Toomes, for all that he is, is still accomplished wrestler as well and will not be easily defeated. Alexei’s Pick: Aimz/Tempest. Aimz and Tempest have the ability to stand up against any competitor in the OSW on their own, let alone to be signed together. Why Jane Reagan agreed to sign this match is beyond me for she should have seen that not only did she sign Toomes up against one powerful wrestler, but TWO. And mind you, two that would like nothing more than to permanently castrate the OSW World Champion. With that rundown of the card, I have taken it upon myself to branch out to the other wrestlers of the OSW and get their feedback on not only communism, but the state of the OSW and the wrestlers involved! I was able to find Propaganda backstage, here is a transcript my comrades! Interview with Propaganda Alexei: Hello there Comrades! I am here with Propaganda, a rather unique individual to say the least. Propaganda...how are you today? Propaganda: Fine my Brother, fine. Alexei: Brother? Sir, we are not brothers. Propaganda: But we are all Gods children so therefore we are. Alexei: But Propaganda, if I may say, I do not occupy my time considering of any higher power. Propaganda: You wouldn't you're just a misguided communists puppet on his unguided trip to Satanism! Alexei: Hold on a minute! I am doing this interview! Satanism? Why would you concern yourself with such an abstract idea? This is wrestling, Propaganda! Propaganda: This is no longer Wrestling, Wrestling died with the names of people such as Jim Duggan and Randy Savage. Since Wrestling has become a smut hole. Now we have people like Vampir Nosferatu, Azrael Cain, Hessian, Drew Hostyle and Communists such as yourself running around a ring! This is no longer Wrestling, but never fear myself and my brother Sagar are here to stop this, and if OSW will not comply we shalt destroy it! Alexei: Do you not think that if we were to consolidate our power, perhaps that would be easier? After all, we of The Red Army wish to create order and a classless society where no one is mistreated. Surely we can work together, eh? Propaganda: I REFUSE TO WORK WITH ATHEIST, COMMUNIST OR SATANIST SCUM! For I have made this heard many times. Yet the incompetence of the management in OSW reams me up with the likes of Vampir Nosferatu. Well it disgusts me, but if it stops the degrading of Wrestling I will team up with Hitler, Lucifer or Stalin to stop it! Alexei: Alright...weirdo....lets change gears! This coming Saturday Slam! you have a match with Vampir Nosferatu on your side. Your thoughts on that? Propaganda: Have I not just expressed I'm appalled at the incompetence of the management in OSW? Well I shall destroy Cain and Hessian without his help. I need nothing of him. Alexei: Yes, for once I can agree that the management is in disarray. But I will admit that I foresee that you will be unable to defeat Heresian Bloodlust without the help of Vampir Nosferatu. Surely you alone cannot handle Azrael Cain and Hessian. What makes you think that you can? Propaganda: I take you to a few weeks back when thanks to the ignorance of the management I was forced to team with two other Satanists, Epitome of Evil and The Magi as we took on the Great Dragon, 'Angeldust' Nick Casey and Vampir Nosferatu. I destroyed all three men with help from neither Magi nor Epitome of Evil. I had a slight 'hand' from my manager, a 60 year old preacher, Eric Sagar. Alexei: Alright, point taken, but if you are truly one of these 'men of the cloth,' aren't there certain commandments or something that you are supposed to adhere to? Like the equivalent of the guidelines Lenin laid out in his Manifesto, aren't there certain things you must abide by, and if so, haven't you violated them? Propaganda: My brother Alexei, do you truly know why I'm in OSW? Obviously not, I'm here because God told me too he gave me the strength he gave to Samson to restore suitable entertainment for the children of today. Let me take a case in example we have seen the 'Federal Drug Enforcement Agency' try to do the same and 'CLIT' has also tried to reduce the actions of Amy Campbell. This is not a suitable programming for the children of today my brother. Alexei: Well, I would prefer to put the children through military school, I believe that would reform them, but that is merely my opinion. Now your name, "Propaganda," is a name that instantly caught my eye, for I am not a stranger to the term. But do you not believe that may be a negative name to give such a...pure individual? Propaganda: Negative how so? I am the Propaganda of the Christian Religion. See the error of your ways or fall to my power, the power of God. Alexei: I never expected your God to deliver such a harsh ultimatum. But regardless, Propaganda, you are beginning to lose my interest. You are not a communist, and you appear to be severely delusional. So in closing, what are your final plans throughout the OSW? Propaganda: For it either to compromise with my demands and take dignity in it's broadcasts or fall drastically to their knees. Alexei: Yes well, Propaganda, thank you for wasting my time...good luck in your endeavors. I will tell you now that it is a shame to see that your pathetic cause will ultimately fall to communism. As you can see Comrades, Propaganda is a misguided individual that much like all the others, will ultimately learn the folly of their ways. However, I can hear the chiming of St. Basil’s Cathedral, marking that it is time to go back to work! Thus, my Comrades, I must end another edition of Think Red! Watch Saturday SLAM and see how we of Red Army destroy the competition and restore order to the OSW! Cheer for Ivan Stanislav! Cheer for The Red Army! And remember…always..ALWAYS….Think Red!! Alexei Ruslan
