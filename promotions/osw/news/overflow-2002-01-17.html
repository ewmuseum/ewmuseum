Overflow, by Edward Sanders The inauguration of OSW, the one day that many wrestling fans have been waiting for, the one day that will go down in the history books as the single most electrifying day in sports entertainment history, or will it? In the views of any wrestling fans, you have to look at the facts. You have talent, as a matter of fact, you have great talent. Numerous members have even gone the lengths to jump ship from Primetime Championship Wrestling to Cyberspace Championship Wrestling. Does this matter? Only if you are a true wrestling fan, only if you watch this great sport and slave over it, day and night. Then you would really understand the true meaning behind it all. Great talents such as Scott Naket, I mean think about it, think about it really hard. This guy was a talent among talents and where did he decide to go? Old School Wrestling. This guy could have probably had the chance to compete in any of the PTC federations that he wished to, but no, he wanted to compete with the OSW roster. He wanted to come and work with the most elite roster to very come into play in the wrestling world. Some may even say that OSW, was a federation that was so well planned, that it will never die. That so much thought and time was put into the planning of this federation that nothing could bring it to disaster. With former NTICW members like Drew Hostyle and Corg, what could go wrong. Obviously one could assume that a lot of money was dished out in the contract signing of these stars. Almost too much, it was rumored that the person behind it all had dished out more than triple what each superstar was making in their last federation and that is an enormous load of cash. More than a billion dollars was given out in contract signing with this company and then you have to look at the cost for sets and the cost for arena rentals. The person behind this all must be loaded big time or have their sources. Signing talent such as Sebastian "Switchblade" Toomes was something very impressive in my views. The only thing I found surprising was the fact that this guy was just beginning to make a name for himself in Cyberspace Championship Wrestling. Did he throw all that he had worked for down the drain to pursue a career in OSW that may never live up to his standards, or will he rise above the clouds and soar more than he has ever done before. Surpassing what he had accomplished in PWA and CCW combined? Only time will tell for this talented superstar, only time will tell. Picking up long time Indy Fed veterans, well, in a sense this may be a good outlook on things, but then again, it may not. You have to analyze the situation, then make your final analysis. People like SLR and "Classic" Clay Vendetta, who seem to be, in my opinion some very rare talent to have just fall into your hands. Two men who seemed to like wrestling in the under promotions a little more than they did working anywhere else, just surfacing in OSW. Seems a little strange to me, I don't know about you. Two talents to watch? Most defiantly. Civil Disobedience? Yes, it is they, as much as they do look gay. This is a team to never take likely, ever. Where they lack in size and strength, they make up in brains and ring knowledge. The only tag team in OSW for now, but they are set to face two mystery opponents on the first OSW show. Who those two will be? I have no idea, but I'm sure that Civil Disobedience will give them a run for their money, no matter who they are. A former World Champion in our midst? Indeed, it is true, the former IOW World Champion, Maverick Manson. A man freaky look could very well intimidate any person.
