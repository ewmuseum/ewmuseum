Da Westsidaz Speak Out

13 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A WT Freelance Reporter caught up with Da Westsidaz member, Short Circuit, sunday afternoon at Pearson Airport in Toronto to discuss recent events concerning them in OSW. First, SC was asked about SLAM! "Well dawg, it was perfectly laid out," said Short Circuit. "We trashed a locker-room, got those satanic boys to kill Civil D and then we got our revenge from last week. We played those boys for fools, dawg. They better up there IQ, cuz if they don't, the same thing'll happen week after week." The freelance reporter then asked him his comments toward Ed. "Well, Eddie denies the email," said SC, rolling his eyes. "I think he's blaming it on the Vax or something, but it's too little to late. Civil D is already pissed off at us so I guess we're going through with this shit. We really didn't mean for Ed to get in any high-water, but it's over with. Ed's still cool in our book, but we're pissed now that we ain't getting the same checks we though we would get." The final question was asking Shorty's thoughts on Civil Disobedience, who they set-up Saturday Night. "We were under the impression that Ed wanted us to take 'em out. We were wrong, but it's too (expletive) late now. We can't just say "Yo, Hawke & Masters, sorry for (expletive) you up and stuff. Friends?" No, you don't do stuff like that. Those guys want a shot at the Tag Titles, just like us. We're in the same boat and it's just going to make things tougher for both of us, so I guess we better watch our backs on SLAM! It's going to be (expletive) hard to keep our minds on our Tag Title shot when Civil D is going to be more than likely out to kick our asses." Da Westsidaz have a shot for the OSW Tag Titles in only their second match back! Will they have a fairy tale comeback or will Heresian Bloodlust show them that old dogs shouldn't learn new tricks, they should just be put to sleep? Tune into Saturday Night SLAM! to find out!
