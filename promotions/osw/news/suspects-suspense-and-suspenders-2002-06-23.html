Suspects, Suspense and Suspenders

23 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Phoenix, Arizona- Ascension hit the air and its safe to say that it not only lived up to its billing, but surpassed all expectations that fans of OSW and even company officials had for the event. Success, always has a price, though, and the aftermath of Ascension will be felt roster wide, as many members of the OSW roster were seriously injured while battling for the right to be named the #1 Contender for the OSW World Heavyweight title on the unforgiving, steel mesh covered slops of the 40' hight "Mt. St. Hell's End" mountain. Also at Ascension, former OSW Commissioner Ed, now cleared of charges he was the Vax, made a dramatic appearance at the show and accused "The Scholar" Howard Porter of being he Vax, only to find out that perhaps his theory was as wrong as Howard Porter's initial findings, two former jOlt superstars made shocking OSW debuts, but in reality, they were not debuts at all, a popular OSW tag team completely self-destructed during the Tag Team title match and of course one lone man stood on the top of Mt. St. Hell's End and was named the #1 Contender for the OSW World Heavyweight title. Don't look for the answers here because this is, afterall, just a tease.
