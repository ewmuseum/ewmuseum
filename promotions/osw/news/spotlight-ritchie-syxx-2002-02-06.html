Spotlight: Ritchie Syxx

6 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings OSW! I’m Shanda Russell with another Wrestler Spotlight. I had the pleasure to sit down with Ritchie Syxx! Shanda Russell: Hello OSW fans! I'm Shanda Russell and sitting with me is OSW's own, Ritchie Syxx! Ritchie, how are you? Ritchie Syxx: I'm doing good...or is it well? Yeah, I'm doing well. Shanda Russell: Uh huh....so Ritchie, let me jump right to the questions! You had a match on Saturday SLAM! against Corg which you unfortunately lost. Any thoughts on that match or your upcoming "gimmick" match against Corg this Saturday? Ritchie Syxx: well, I'm definitely winning this one, Shanda. Corg got lucky, and he's not gonna get lucky this time. I'd like to see a cage match, maybe an inferno match, but fire is a little hot. But knowing how unpredictable OSW is, I don't know what could happen Shanda Russell: There were also several surprises on SLAM including the introduction of Azrael Cain and Hessian earlier, Lennox Lanthrop, Ivan Stanislav and Tom Walczak with his Red Army, Doug Troy and Trashcan Man. Do you have your sights set on any of them? Or are you mainly focusing one match at a time? Ritchie Syxx: I'm only concerned with Corg right now, as long as they don't mess with my match with him on Saturday, it won't seem like I will go after them yet Shanda Russell: Now, in your personal opinion, how tall are you? Ritchie Syxx: I'm 5'11" Shanda Russell: Now, one of your quotes is, "I'm Ritchie Syxx! Who the hell are you!? A Russian Butcher!?" What exactly does that uh...mean? Ritchie Syxx: What doesn't it mean? Shanda Russell: Uh...right....okay! Next question! Jane Reagan, thoughts on her? Ritchie Syxx: she seems like a good gal. I admire that whole "I am woman, hear me roar" thing she has going. Shanda Russell: Any goals in the OSW that you wish to achieve other than winning the World Title? Ritchie Syxx: I'd like to have a deathmatch. Shanda Russell: Deathmatch as in....the loser...dies? Ritchie Syxx: of course Shanda Russell: Anybody in the OSW roster that you would like to have this said match? Ritchie Syxx: CORG Shanda Russell: Interesting..I'm sure he will be reading this. Any opinions on the direction of the OSW? Do you think that this federation has a chance to survive within the PTC? Ritchie Syxx: I hope OSW can get some nicer props, they're not shiny enough. Of course OSW can survive in PTC, given enough time, OSW can beat PCW. Shanda Russell: Well thank you Ritchie Syxx. Any final words for the OSW? Ritchie Syxx: Woodpecker As you can see, while Ritchie may be a little bit out there, he seems very passionate about wrestling and has complete faith in his wrestling ability, as well as a grudge against Corg! What will come of this? See on this weeks Saturday SLAM!
