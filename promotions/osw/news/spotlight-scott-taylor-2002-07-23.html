Spotlight: Scott Taylor

23 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanda: Hello folks, I'm Shanda Russell with another Wrestler Spotlight! Sitting with me today is newcomer Scott Taylor! Scott, thanks for this opportunity to interview you! Scott: Hey, not a problem Shanda. I'm just glad that's it me here instead of another one of those "veterans" using their name to get along. Shanda: Interesting....would you be talking about Kid Wonder? Scott: Kid Wonder is the first person that comes to mind when I say that. Who cares if he's held the PCW Cruiserweight Title more times than anyone and was PCW's 2000 Wrestler of the Year? This isn't Primetime Championship Wrestling. This is Old School Wrestling. You don't hear Ivan Stanislav bragging about his PCW Awards all the time. Shanda: Well..maybe not, but you have to respect him for his accolades to a point. I mean...Kid Wonder helped bring the PCW to what it is. Without the PCW, OSW might not be here. Scott: I know that, I'm just saying that Kid Wonder can't hold on to his past success in a new company. I'm a former World Heavyweight Champion in my own right, but I can't use that to get over in OSW. Shanda: Speaking of history, you came over from the NTICW. Would you say the NTICW helped you prepare for the OSW? What are the key differences backstage between the NTICW and the OSW? Scott: I was "home grown" in NTICW, Shanda. I came there from a now defunct Independent Federation. When I showed up there in February I was a no name guy who was lucky enough to get picked up into a larger company. But it was in the NTICW that I honed my skills, both in ring and backstage that helped me get ready for a huge company like OSW. The main difference that I've noticed is that the wrestlers are really in to what they do and try their best. Reagan and Porter run things a lot better also. Shanda: Well, you've obviously had some problems with Kid Wonder, as mentioned above. But what about other wrestlers? Any in particular you like or dislike? Scott: The only wrestler that I've really spoken to in the four weeks that I've been here is Propaganda. I like the guy, he has a set of moral and religious beliefs that he lives by and doesn't let anyone step on. You have to admire that. As for the others, I was really looking forward to meeting Ed, I've been a fan of his since I was still an indy wrestler. But, he's not with us any longer, I'm not heartless Shanda. His passing really saddened me. Shanda: Yes...yes...it truly is a shame. Ehem..well....what do you think makes Scott Taylor unique in the OSW? Scott: They call this place Old School Wrestling, and our tag line is "The Way Wrestling Was Meant To Be." To me that means to go out to that ring, put on a good high impact match, and have one hell of a good time while you're doing it. That's what I try to do everything I step into the ring. It's a little known fact that in 1999 I did a small tour through Japan as a masked wrestler, there I picked up a few moves and a slightly different style than you see in North America. To me, that's uniqueness right there. A faster style of wrestling mixed with American Ethics, sounds crazy but it's been working for me. Shanda: Very interesting! Well Scott, what does the future hold for you in the OSW? Titles? Feuds? Scott: I still have a few bones to pick with Kid Wonder, and my eye is still on that Cruiserweight Championship. But the tournament announced for the vacant tag team titles has caught my interest. Don't hold me to it, but you may see me pop up in there. Shanda: Well, thank you for your time Scott. Any final words to the fans of the OSW? Scott: You can love me, you can hate me, but don't expect me to go anywhere any time soon. I think I've found a good home in OSW, and I plan on staying.
