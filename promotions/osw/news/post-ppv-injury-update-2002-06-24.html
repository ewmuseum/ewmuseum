Post PPV Injury Update

24 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Buyrates and huge merchandise sales aside, the injury fallout from Ascension may prove much more costly than any amount of money the company could have possibly taken in as a result of the event, according to OSW spokesperson Shanda Russell, who gave us this injury update. Former UWAers, Kanaida, Jason Winters and Ian Wright were all injured by a maniacal Frankie Bones following their ladder match to retire the OSW title. The extent of their injuries are unknown, but Kanaida and Jason Winters' injuries were described as possibly "career threatening." Griff Truxell sustained multiple injuries during the main event, and is expected to be out indefinately. Rhett Locke suffered cracked ribs and required 64 stitches to close various wounds. He is expected to miss next week, but was quoted as saying that there was "no fucking way" he would miss his title shot against OSW World Heavyweight Champion, "The Polish Punisher" Tom Walczak, July 13th on Slam. Chico and Gun Metal both suffered terminal failures, but Commissioner Howard Porter says that the pair will be in the ring next Saturday even if a tow truck is required to hall their steel carcasses, for their match against Porter and Ed. Amy Campbell required 19 stitches to close facial and neck wounds, and is still "understandably" shaken about what traspired with she and Corg during the main event. OSW officials plan on giving Aimz "as much time as she needs" off to recover." Azrael Cain suffered suffered a concussion and broken clavical during the post match beating put upon him by Vampir Nosferatu and former tag team partner, Hessian. He is expected to miss six to eight weeks. A half dozen wrestlers suffered minor concucssions, required a handfull of stitches, and accumulated a number of minor injuries during the main event. More will be known on their status early in the week. We will monitor all of the post Ascension fallout and bring you more information as it arises.
