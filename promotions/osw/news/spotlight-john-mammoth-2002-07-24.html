Spotlight: John Mammoth

24 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanda: Hello OSW fans! I'm Shanda Russell here with a Mammoth edition of Wrestler Spotlight! In case you haven't guess yet, I am here with John Mammoth of the Think Tank! John, thank you for allotting me this time! Mammoth: Eh, it's no problem really. I'm flattered to be doing this. Shanda: Well first things first. So far, how are you enjoying the OSW? Mammoth: It's not exactly what I'd call "great" Shanda... In fact, I'd go so far as to say it's lousy. Shanda: I must say Mr. Mammoth, that we were all surprised by not only your entrance into the OSW, but also the situation of your entrance. Being a member of the Think Tank and also helping PHD? You never seemed to be like that. Didn't you have problems with PHD in the fWo Survivor? Mammoth: PHD... ugh... It's bittersweet Shanda. Bittersweet. I think it's obvious to the viewers right now I haven't been happy with what I'm forced to do... But I have to... I need to. Shanda: Why would you do such a thing John? PHD is waging war against the entire OSW! Mammoth: Life, Shanda. I do it for mine. Shanda: Well....I have to ask.....life has already been lost because of PHD......Ed..he.... -stops to compose herself- Mammoth: Died. ... Yeah, it's... it's hard. It's hard to know that I have played apart in his hospitalization, and ultimately his death. He was a great guy... and... and... I'm a murderer... I don't... I don't deserve what-- ugh. Shanda: How long do you have to work for PHD? How do you know he'll live up to his end of the bargain? Mammoth: I don't know the answer to either of those questions, Shanda. I only know that if I help him, he's going to help me. That's the deal...and unfortunately in this case, I'm a man of my word. Shanda: Well the people of the OSW will NOT stand for that. Already Ivan Stanislav has declared that he will not stand for this type of behavior, and while he may not have been the greatest man in the world, the people are behind him! Is it worth being hated for the rest of your life, to be a murderer for the rest of you life? Men like Stanislav and others like him are sure to find you regardless of your situation! Mammoth: Let him find me, I'm not hiding. Tell me Shanda, can you truly tell me that another man's death isn't worth your own life? ... ... Never mind, I don't think I can... Shanda: Ed was innocent and you helped set him up to be killed. As far as I'm concerned, you're not doing some 'admirable' thing for your own life.... Ehem....I apologize..I'm getting worked up. Mammoth: No, by all means... by all means you're right Shanda. I'm the one that is wrong... Shanda: So then...what does the future hold for John Mammoth? Mammoth: God only knows, Shanda Russell. God only knows. Shanda: Anything that you would like to say to the fans? I think you owe them some sort of an explanation. Mammoth: Shanda, you and I both know that no matter what I say, I'll still be adorned. I'll still be known as someone whom betrayed the people that he loved most. I've chosen this road, Shanda, and I have to deal with the consequences. You have no idea how utterly sorry I am for Ed, his family, his fans. So sorry... but the fact of the matter is I never will be trusted again. Never... never. Shanda: I apologize folks, but I left the interview after that. I could not interview John Mammoth any longer. This is Shanda Russell with another Wrestler Spotlight.
