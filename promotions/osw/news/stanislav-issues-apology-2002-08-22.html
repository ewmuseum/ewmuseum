Stanislav Issues Apology

22 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ivan Stanislav was spotted exiting the OSW headquarters earlier today after a meeting between himself and the OSW President was scheduled. He was more than happy to speak to reporters out on the front steps: “First of all, I would like to apologize to all the fans of OSW that had to witness the horrific events that occurred within wrestling ring between myself and Corg. My actions were impulsive, dangerous, and shocking to many people and for that I am deeply sorry. I would also like to apologize to Referee Speedy Riggs for my lashing out against him, which luckily did not leave him seriously injured. Finally, I would also like to make it abundantly clear that I am NOT sorry to Corg for what I did, for I believe it was my duty as wrestler and as person to effectively teach him long, harsh lesson.” Ivan Stanislav had fought Corg on the latest edition of Saturday Slam and brutally wrestled him to a point where the entire match had to be thrown out and Corg was left with a string of serious injuries. He was also required to pay $1,000 in fines for his actions and for assaulting referee and long time friend Speedy Riggs. “I was not myself and I am deeply ashamed for attacking Comrade Riggs, I have not spoken with him personally, but I surely will and make my feelings known to him.” Stanislav reportedly was more than happy to pay the fine that was issued to him. It was actually speculated that he nonchalantly handed a $1,000 bill to the accountant of the OSW without another thought! “Oh, he was loaded! His wallet was bulging, and that $1,000 bill wasn’t too lonesome!” said one OSW worker, who remained nameless. When asked about Tempest, Stanislav made it abundantly clear, “Any correlation between myself and Tempest is strictly coincidental. I did not react in such harsh way because of what Corg had done to her, but because I could not stand for his lackluster attitude and handling of the OSW roster as a whole.” Stanislav was quick to make himself scarce after Tempest was brought up. Stanislav must team up with El Drago, Tempest, and old enemy Sebastian Toomes and his friends Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke of Civil Disobedience on this upcoming Slam. But while Ivan Stanislav certainly is showing a more compassionate and giving side, we have also seen him fall back into his Russian Bear rampaging state more than once already. While one must wonder if the Think Tank, the team they are fighting is safe… one must also question if his teammates are also safe from his short temper and impulsive nature. More news on Speedy Riggs, Corg, and Ivan Stanislav as it comes to us.
