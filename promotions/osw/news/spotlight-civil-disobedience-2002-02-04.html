Spotlight: Civil Disobedience

4 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is Shanda Russell of the OSW! I recently got to speak with Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke of Civil Disobedience. Needless to say, it was an interesting interview…. Shanda- Hello there OSW fans! I'm Shanda Russell here with Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke, who together comprise Civil Disobedience! How are you gentlemen? Trevor- We're chillin'? Yourself? Seth-Why even ask that question, Trevor? It's obvious she's far from chillin'. The babe is hot as hell! Shanda- Um...well, ehem, I'm fine, thank you. I just want to ask a few questions, okay? Seth- You can ask me whatever you want, babe. Shanda- Well, my first question is for Trevor. I have read that you have a bachelors degree in philosophy. So I have to ask, why wrestling? Seth- Alright...I can wait as long as you need me to. (Seth smiles and winks at Shanda) Trevor- Just ignore my sexually deprived friend. He's harmless, really. As for why I'm wrestling, it's because, as my partner here always says... Seth- Ass kicking kicks ass! Shanda- Hmm....okay. Now, prior to the OSW, have you had any previous wrestling experience? Trevor- Exactly. There's no cooler job in the world than being a professional wrestler. Is any further reason needed? Trevor- We went up through the PWA. Seth- Which was absolute hell. Trevor- But other than that, I guess you can say we're rookies. Seth- Which doesn't make us bad. Trevor- No, it's a testament to our talent that we made it so far, so fast. Shanda- Now, the OSW Tag Division. There currently is not one, so I suppose you expect and easy ride to the Tag Titles, huh? Trevor- Easy? Yeah, that pretty much goes without saying. Not to sound cocky or anything... Seth- Why not, Trev? Sounding cocky is what this sport is all about. Seriously, though. The division could be packed with teams and it still wouldn't matter. We're just too damn cool to not win those belts. Shanda- Don't you think it takes a bit more to winning the belts than being...cooler than the opponents? Seth and Trevor- No. Shanda- Okaay.....your first match in the OSW, Saturday SLAM! is against two mystery opponents. Any clue who they are? Guesses? And comments? Seth- I'm not sure. I've been thinking about it lately... Trevor- Wait a second...You've...been thinking? Seth- Shut up Trevor. Seriously, though. I've been thinking and I've come up with a few guesses. The first one that came to mind was the team of Kris Kross. Remember those guys? The Mack Daddy and the Daddy Mack. Those rappers who wore their clothes backwards because they were soooo awesome? The reason why I think it's going to be them is that I feel the management will be attempting to fill up the tag division with teams that might be able to give us a run for our money as far as style is concerned. It's too bad that no one...even Kris Kross...even comes close to us in that department. Whatever the case, I'm not worried at all. Trevor- (Sighing) You're an idiot, Seth. Shanda- That uh...that could be a uh..possibility... Shanda- Any thoughts on Jane Reagan being the surprise President that won't land you two in jail? Seth- I'm all for women in position of power. Trevor- Seth, just because you're into that dominatrix shit doesn't mean you have to bring it into the public light. Seth- That's not what I meant. I just...nevermind. Shanda- Any chance of singles competition by either of you? Seth- Oh, I'm glad you brought that up. I'd like to use this forum to announce that I have signed up for the Global Title shot tourney, and I'm planning on bringing that belt home to Civil Disobedience. Trevor- You did what? Seth- I'm wrestling for the shot at Vampir Nosferatu for his Global Title. Trevor- Ha ha ha. You are so going to get your ass kicked. Seth- (angrily) Shut up! Shanda- Yes, well, thank you gentlemen for giving me some of your time. Good luck on Saturday and good luck in the tournament. Trevor- Thank you, Shanda. Seth- Yeah, thanks babe, but we won't need it. The first Tag Team of the OSW seems to have a goal, but the means of attaining it are rather unorthodox. However, we can see several things here. The two men know each other very well, and have the potential to be a very solid and difficult team to beat. The OSW Tag Division will surely pick up, and I see Civil Disobedience coming out on top. This is Shanda Russell.
