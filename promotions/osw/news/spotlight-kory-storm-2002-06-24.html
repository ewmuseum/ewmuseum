Spotlight: Kory Storm

24 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanda Russell: “Ladies and gentleman, I am in the intensive care unit of St. Mary’s Hospital in Phoenix, Arizona at the moment … anxiously awaiting the arrival of Kory Storm … who has finally agreed to not only stitch up his wounds, but sit down with Old School Wrestling for our signature spotlight interview. As many of you saw, last night at Ascension, Kory was put through hell not once, but twice. First, he fought a bloody sledgehammer match with Jim Axtell, which even after a grueling fight put up by “Perfection Personified”, he lost. Then, he marched just like so many others onto Mt. St. Hell’s End, and was the first to be thrown off courtesy of the same sledgehammer shot from the same man who caused him loss earlier. I plan on getting his views on the… Oh! Here is now! Ladies and gentleman, Kory Storm!” Kory Storm: “Shanda.” Shanda: “Well Kory, I must say that it is a pleasure to be meeting you, even if it is in these surroundings.” Kory: “Tell me about it! I’m sitting in this hard, metal chair just trying to stop bleeding for once in my life … and what do I smell?! Fucking piss! Piss from these geezers who infest our country with their Medicare and their disease! If I were a fucking devout child raping Catholic, I wouldn’t even come here to see the Pope! Funny hat and all!” Shanda: “Right … I see that the medicine they put you on seems to be kicking in-- maybe I’ll just leave and we can do this at another…” Kory: “You will do no such thing. Sit down before me Shanda, experience the words and knowledge of a god.” Shanda: “Ok. To start off … what are the extents of your injuries and what exactly about last night at Ascension caused them?” Kory: “Are you retarded?” Shanda: “Excuse me?” Kory: “I said, are … you … retarded? I mean, you’re supposed to be some intelligent reporter who sits on her fat hereditary ass and watches what we do in that ring, and you don’t know what caused these multiple gashes on my forehead? What Pay Per View did you watch last night?! I took six shots to the head and the body from a sledgehammer, courtesy of that Wal Mart security reject,Jim Axtell. Thus, I had to come here, though I despise the conditions of most hospitals. Especially those of which are in …ugh… Arizona.” Shanda: “I see. Well, now that the injuries have come and will now soon heal, the books will forever record that Jim Axtell has had the best of you. Will you retaliate in a rematch of some kind?” Kory: “I doubt it. Jim Axtell may have beaten me. Hell, he put a damper on what I expected to be a long career. But he is no way half of the man I am, as his wife has found out on numerous occasions. That peon isn’t worth my time. If we cross paths again, you can bet your horse hair weave that I’ll kick his taxidermist ass. However, I won’t go chasing after a hopelessly lucky jobber who doesn’t belong in my ring in the first place. I have more important things up my sleeve.” Shanda: “Speaking of what you have up your sleeve … would any of your future plans have to do with Frankie Bones? As we saw last night, you directed him almost as if he were a dog, and until “Classic” Clay Vendetta ran to Jim Axtell’s record, yourself and Frankie were certainly avenging your loss.” Kory: “Honestly Shanda, I’m just as shocked as you are that Shrek’s black stunt double decided to march down to the ring. But I must say that the big oaf did a good job in beating the tar out of OSW’s ugliest Cinderella story, much better than that idiot alien Michel Krieg did, and I didn’t even have to pay Frankie!” Shanda: “I’m glad you brought up “The German War Machine”. It seems as if the Red Army is on the verge of collapsing, and much of the heat for those occurrences is placed on you. Could you give us a bit of understanding on your relationship with Michel Krieg, and tell me if there will be anymore interaction between you?” Kory: “To put it simply, so even you could understand. I don’t like Michel Krieg, nor do I have a hidden agenda in aiding the Red Army in breaking up. In my opinion, the big German finally let his Capitalist side get the best of him, and I tried to help him out by giving him a few bills to help me out. He did nothing of the sort, costing me my match with Jim Axtell on Slam!, and then working up enough nerve in his beer swelled body to ask me for the money. Rather than beat his fucking head into the ground so hard that he would think he was back in Germany shoving bratwurst down his throat, I decided to be the nice guy and give him the money I promised for a job not done. I’m a man of my word and whether or not dirt sheets want to make me out to be an instigator and say that I was threatened by Krieg, I stand tall in my conviction that I could give two shits and one of Amy Campbell’s tits about any of them. For in the end, you Ms. Russell … along with your company which has ignored me so long … will not be able to last through the Storm. And do you know why?” Shanda: “Because you--” Kory: “Do I look like a karaoke screen you can read my dialogue from? I didn’t think so. It is because I’m the Icon! I’m the Superstar! I’m your Paragon! Hell, I’m simply “Perfection Personified”. This interview is over.”
