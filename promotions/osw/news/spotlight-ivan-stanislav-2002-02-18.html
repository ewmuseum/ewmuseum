Spotlight: Ivan Stanislav

18 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Due to Shana Russell's trauma on Slam!, she was unable to attend the interview. In her place was Matt Shivan, who vowed to give the best interview of his career.] (The video opens slowly to show a well-lit, cozy dressing room with potted plants, white walls, and cream-colored sofas. Sitting at one end of the room, sitting comfortably in a position of superiority, are Ivan Stanislav and Alexei Ruslan. Across from them, trembling visibly, is Matt Shivan. He turns to the camera) Matt Shivan: H-hello, OSW fans! I'm M-Matt Shivan, filling in for Shanda R-Russel in this edition of Wr-wrestler Spotlight. I'm here with two very f-famous p-people...um... I-Ivan Stanislav a-and Alexei R-Ruslan... Ivan Stanislav: Get on with it you fool! Matt: I'm sorry! I'm sorry! Um...um...oh yeah! Questions! Um...oh, whoops, I dropped my cards. Um, one second... Alexei Ruslan: Stupid man...why must we be here! Ivan: He is American Alex... Matt: There we go! S-sorry, Mr. Stanislav! Um, uh...I d-don't think this was my first question before, b-but...how did it feel to get your ass handed to you by Mean-- HEY! I didn't write tha-- Ivan: WHAT!!!! Alexei: How dare you speak to us like that! Matt: PLEASE GOD DON'T HURT ME! (At this point, Matt falls to his knees and clenches his hands as if in prayer) Matt: *sobbing loudly* I didn't write it! I swear! Please don't kill me! Ivan: You listen to me you little worm. Meanstreak is nothing but another upstart like the rest of the fighters in the OSW! He is washed up, nursing injuries, and will not be a problem! Alexei: Besides, I have ways of taking him out! After all, he did put me in hospital at one time... Matt: B-but, he did, erm, beat you on Slam... Ivan: He Cheated! Matt: N-not really, he didn't break any-- Ivan: Speedy Riggs was there to be referee, and Pierre Gaston interfered! He wrestled under the guise of Hessian and he did not have guts to stand in front of me like a real man! In that match, Ivan threw him all over the wrestling ring! Matt: B-but he was obviously, uh, no-selling-- Alexei: SHUT UP YOU IDIOT! Ivan: Listen to me, the referee counted too slow! He was not ready for pins when I made them! That last second would have given Ivan the win! I am the victim here! I am a victim of the system!! Matt: H-how are you a victim? You viciously attacked Meanstreak f-four months ago! Isn't h-he a victim? Ivan: No! I come into the OSW trying to mobilize the roster to fight the PCW in an interfed match, and instead they try to stop me! I do not understand this! Why are they sabotaging my plans! I told Reagan what I was going to do. Alexei: You cannot trust Americans.. Matt: Well, I g-guess some people would r-rather fight PCW in th-their own w-way... Alexei: Stop stammering you fool! You are getting on our nerves! Ivan: There is only one way to fight the PCW! They outnumber us! We have to have unified attack! I am Praporschik of OSW, therefore I am in command! Matt: B-b-b-but-- I m-mean, s-s-sorry Mr. Alexei -- um, um, uh, m-maybe someone else c-could unifiy O-OSW? Alexei: Who? Ed? That man can barely walk anymore! Meanstreak? He is a loose cannon! Jane Reagan? Yeah, let a woman run the show! HA! Matt: B-but if they a-all worked together... Ivan: That is exactly what I am doing! Unifying the entire OSW under the banner of communism! A collective! No man is more imporant than another! ARE YOU DEAF! YOU STUPID FOOL!!! Matt: DON'T HURT ME! I'M SORRY! I WORSHIP THE VERY GROUND YOU WALK ON! Alexei: You are damned right you do! Ivan: So Matt...let Ivan ask you a question....are you a follower of our cause? Are you with..or against The Red Army? Matt: Uh-uh-uh-uh-uh-- Ivan: ANSWER ME! Matt: I'M SORRY! Uh, well, um, I guess I sort of do, but, uh, Ed's a lot less mean and scary-- n-not that you a-are, Praporshnick-- Ivan: PRAPORSCHIK YOU BUFFOON! (Matt dives to the floor and sobs in terror) Alexei: Ed is a fool! He's a broken down wrestler that names furniture! And Jane Reagan is going to give him a position of power?! Matt: W-well, she sort of already has-- Ivan/Alexei: WE KNOW THAT! (Matt ducks his head again, smacking it against the floor. The camera moves slightly to focus on his ankle, where a puddle of liquid is growing quickly) Alexei: Ivan...is he....uh...urinating? Ivan: I believe he is.... Matt: *sobbing loudly* Please...don't let them hurt me... Ivan: We are not going to hurt you, whelp. There is only one thing you need to know! The OSW has charged us, The Red Army, with the task of bringing them to bear against the PCW! And we will do this in any way that we can! No one will stop us! Not the politics of Reagan. Not the matches of Ed! Not Meanstreak! Nothing! The Red Army will reign! And you will all fall under the banner of communism! Matt: B-but then I w-won't be able to afford my rubber pants! Alexei: You are pathetic, we will supply you with some.. Matt: O-oh, g-good...um...o-oh yeah, the q-questions...um...i-if you d-do manage to c-conquer OSW, h-how will you w-wage war on PCW? Ivan: Simple! Together we will not only win our matches, but take over the management as well! They think these contests will only exist in the ring? No, when the event starts, we will overthrow the men in power and bring it to Red Army hands! We will destroy their belts, destroy their documents, and make sure that it never rises again. Those that do not join us will be fired. Matt: Uh-uh-uh...th-that's not v-very nice... Alexei: THIS IS WRESTLING! WE BEAT EACH OTHER UP IN THE RING! THERE IS NOTHING NICE ABOUT IT! (Matt screams and ducks his head into his shirt) Ivan: This is WAR Shivan! Understand that! Matt: *again sobbing* I-I understand! P-please don't hit me! Ivan: What in Lenin's name! We are not going to hit you! Matt: *still sobbing* Y-yes you are! Y-you're big and mean and e-evil and-- Alexei: Ivan, I know he doesn't want you to hit him..but maybe it would do him some good... Ivan: Yes Alexei, perhaps if we knock him out..he will stop complaining...... Matt: NO! (Ivan rises from his chair and stomps forward, his arm reared back over his head) Matt: PLEASE! I'LL JOIN YOU! I'LL! I'LL...I'll...uhhhnnn... (Matt groans, his eyes rolling back in his head, and goes limp. His head bounces slightly off the floor as it hits. Ivan and Alexei stare at him, then turn to eachother) Alexei: That was easier than I thought..... Ivan: That was most unorthodox interview ever....let us get out of here.... (The two leaders of the Red Army step over the fallen Matt Shivan as they exit the room, and the interview comes to a close)
