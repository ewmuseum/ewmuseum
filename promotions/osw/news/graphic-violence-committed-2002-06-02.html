Graphic Violence Committed

2 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Finally, after many Doctors' notes and opinions, along with many broken bones, Graphic Violence has been instituted in the George Pearson Centre's mental ward, a Vancouver based institution for the mentally ill. On SLAM! Rhett Locke and Graphic Violence were given the week off, however it was apparent to all that when GV's music hit, niether men were at the Hospital. And just when it was thought the two were going to duke it out, Rhett Locke had him institutionalized. What this means for Graphic Violence and his match with Locke at Ascension is unsure. When Shanda Russell asked Rhett about the situation, he had this to say: "What do you expect me to say? The guy's nuts. You should've saw him in the hospital, dillusional little prick. He beat the hell out of the doctor while calling him Rhett, then took off running in a hospital gown!" Rhett paused for a brief moment, and continued. "Well, regardless, I'll be sure to give you a 'progress report' this Saturday Night. For your enjoyment, not just mine." Locke then casually walked away, with a hoarse chuckle. As always, we'll update you on the Graphic Violence, Rhett Locke situation as soon as we get anymore information regarding GV and his stay at George Pearson Centre, as well as the quote, unquote 'progress reports' that Rhett promised us.
