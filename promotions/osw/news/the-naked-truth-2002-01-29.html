OSW — Column

The Naked Truth

29 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The other day, I grabbed a pen, a few sheets of paper, and thought to hell with it, I’m just gonna write stuff down. And why not? Sometimes you feel like you just wanna write about everything or everyone involved with something. Sometimes it could be pointless, and sometimes it could be stuff that’s good to read. But most of the time, it’s just my pen writing down what I think, and why I think it. Old School Wrestling is now open for business, as a debut roster of about 15 people was announced a little over a week ago on PCW Malice. Of course, that isn’t the whole thing, as many speculate that the remains of the roster will be on Saturday Slam! this week. But the question going through everyone’s mind is, how many are there left? There could be 5, 10, another 15 or maybe just 2 more. No one really knows the answer to this question, but I’m sure we’ll have a clear answer in the weeks to come. Speaking with questions going through everyone’s mind, there are two that are always popping up in mine. First and foremost, who are the two former PCW champions that will arrive in OSW? I myself never being a PCW champion can be taken off that list, but if you look down the list of former champions in PCW, the possibilities are just mind boggling! -Going through the list of people, we can start with Kremmen. Kremmen of course was the first ever PCW World Champion, even though his reign lasted only a grand total of 5 days. He is however, a big name around PTC, so could we see him in OSW? -Another person that could show up in OSW is Doug Troy. Doug Troy, though hasn’t had been on a real hot streak since his world title run, would also make a good addition to the OSW roster. -Yet another person that could show here is the Reverend Jon Hull. I think out of all the champions in PCW history, he has the most respect from people there. He too would make a great addition to the OSW roster. Though a lot of the PCW champions have a good chance to be one of the two, there are a few, and only a few, that have pretty much zero chance of showing up here in OSW. -Stewert Hix and Preacher are probably the two with the worst chance of ever showing up not just in OSW, but any PTC fed. Stewert Hix, as we all know, was not one of, but the single handed most hated champion in PCW history. People didn’t like him, never did, and the fact that he left PCW during his title reign (something along those lines) pissed off the entire roster. -As for Preacher, well, is that guy still even alive? Haven’t heard from him in about a year, ever since he bitched about the angle that was going on when he held the title. Couldn’t find him for Alliances, won’t find him for OSW probably. -The Chef probably won’t show either, as he vacated the world title to retire from PCW. -I’d also be surprised if Meanstreak showed up in OSW, considering the three time PCW Champion left the company due to creative differences back in September. Then again, those differences could have boiled over by now. All in all, you really never know who will show up. Trashcan Man, Frisco, Alan Kriegman, Jeremy Howard, Stryfe, who knows?! Even the people who were on my won’t show list could show up, as Saturday is slated to be a big night of surprises. Speaking of surprises, my second thing that won’t seem to get off my mind is, who is the owner of Old School Wrestling. The list of possibilities for this goes on just like the two World Champions. You could have Tyler Nelson himself owning this thing. Maybe The Phantom Booker owns OSW in a plot of revenge to try to regain his ownership of PCW? Hell, maybe the owner of OSW is Frisco, or even the hardcore legend Ed! Maybe I own OSW! The list of people who could own this place just goes on, and unless you have an insiders view, the answer will truly not be known until it is answered by the man himself. I guess Saturday night is the big topic of this week though for OSW, as everyone will get their first look at OSW superstars in action. The card, complete with six matches thus far, will be interesting, but not the most important thing of the show. What, do you really think the wrestling will be the hot topic after Saturday? Who will show up on Saturday is my question. But since we are a wrestling company, lets take a look at the matches that are coming up on the debut Slam! (Note: This card is accurate as of Monday, January 28th and matches may be altered, scrapped or modified as of this writing. Other matches may be added after this writing.) -In the opening match of the night, and the first match in OSW history, “Black Ice” Matt Blaze will take on Drew Hostyle. Though too early to call a winner in this match, I think it’ll be a great one to open up the show and get the fans excited in Easton. -Civil Disobedience so far is the only confirmed tag team in OSW. So far their opponents have not been named, but a few names have been thrown around, including a former WCW Tag Team. I guess we’ll have to wait and see for that one. -Sebastian 'Switchblade' Toomes will take on SLR following this, and right now, I’m gonna say that SLR will come out on top, basically because I haven’t seen anything of Toomes yet. Though, it could turn out to be quite a match. -In a match that I’m really looking forward too, the Great Dragon will square off against Sho-Lin. Both of these guys are from Asia, Dragon from Japan and Sho- Lin from China respectively, and I’m expecting to see a great match put on by these two. -Scott Naket will make his in ring return on Saturday as he takes on “Classic” Clay Vendetta. This should already be a “classic” with both men trying to get a head start with their quest to become world champion. But then again, it may be too early to determine who goes where. -And the first main event in OSW history will see Ritchee Syxx take on Corg. Folks, this one isn’t gonna be pretty, as both men will tear each other apart limb for limb in this match. Expect no mercy from both men. Also on Slam! everyone is assuming that we will get a little bit of information on the first ever OSW pay-per-view and some info on the three titles that OSW has. Expect Saturday to be a big night. Mark it on your calendars, set your VCRs, cause this day will be looked upon as the start of the greatest wrestling promotion that there is to offer! Till next time, don’t find yourself naked beside someone you don’t know!
