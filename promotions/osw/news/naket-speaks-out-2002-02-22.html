Naket Speaks Out

22 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Recently, we caught up with Scott Naket to get his comment the outcome of this past weeks match against Mike Marvelous with Clay Vendetta serving as the special referee. “I’m just really pissed off,” shouted back an angry looking Naket. “First Clay Vendetta costs me my undefeated streak in OSW, then Mike Marvelous rants about being the greatest, and to top it all off, Clay Vendetta uses his special referee powers to deem himself the winner of a match he’s not even in.” While Naket stopped to regain the oxygen that left his body during his little rant, we asked him if he thought he was overreacting about a situation that he could make up for this Saturday on Slam! “Hell no I’m not overreacting,” stated an even angrier Naket. “I have every right in the world to bitch like a little girl about this. But you know what? Things are gonna change now. No more happy go lucky, everybody is a winner in my matches Scott Naket around here. From this day forward, there will be a different person around here then the Scott Naket you’re all used to see. No more Canadian Icon crap, no more buffoons, no more polite talking.” When asked what this meant, and what kind of new Scott Naket we would see, Naket looked at the interviewer for a second, and then looked down, and opened up a can of 7-UP. He took a big gulp from the can, and looked over in our direction. “You’re not gonna see a new Scott Naket, oh no. You see, this Saturday night, on Slam! In front of the entire world. In front of the entire audience in the arena, The Hott One will return, the old Scott Naket. The Scott Naket that knew how to get the job done, and get it done right. And if I was Mike Marvelous or Clay Vendetta. Well, I would make sure to keep looking behind my shoulder every now and again. Because on Saturday, it’s not about me winning a shot at the World Title. It’s about me making sure that those two don’t get a chance to win their shots at it.” Naket proceeded to crush his can with his bare hand and drop it on the ground. He then turned around and walked away, yelling some words we cannot print for you on this site.
