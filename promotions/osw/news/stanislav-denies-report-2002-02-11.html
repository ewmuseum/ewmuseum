Stanislav Denies Report

11 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Recently, a report was made from known online wrestling dirtsheet, www.wrestlingtattler.com about Ivan Stanislav, OSW superstar regarding his sexual experience, or lack thereof. Days after the report, word has finally reached The Kremlin regarding this news and a statement has been released from the Russian Capital itself: “We of The Russian Federation are shocked and appalled by the blatant lying of American reporters regarding Praporschik Stanislav.” Ivan Stanislav himself had a much more colorful answer: “I do not care what you in America think of Ivan Stanislav! It only shows the jealousy that you have towards a man as powerful, influential, and handsome as me! The Russian Women, as well as the women around the world flock to Stanislav and they are never sent away without smiles on all of their faces! I believe that this is a conspiracy by President Reagan herself to slander my great name and try to make light of my endeavors in the OSW! After all, what do you expect from someone with the title “President Reagan!” Stanislav continued to argue his conspiracy theory. “President Reagan can try to do whatever she wants to silence me and turn the fans of America against me, and if she wishes to stoop as low as to make rumors about Stanislav, she is more than encouraged to! This is merely a chance to show the OSW that Ivan Stanislav does not care what you think of him, for he knows that deep down you fear for your lives when he is in the vicinity!” Stanislav recently won a bachelor contest on PTC Malice and has won an all expense pay trip to the Congo courtesy of PTC. His date: Tempest of the fWo. “I do not know who this woman is or what her intentions are, but I can assure you that after the liberation of the Congo, she too will leave Ivan with a smile on her face!” Between fighting Reagan, Commissioner Ed, Doug Troy, and Trashcan Man, Stanislav has surged forward against the entire roster of the OSW in an attempt to “conscript” them to battle the PCW. Also, Stanislav must deal with a mystery opponent on Saturday and go on vacation with a very eligible woman. To say Stanislav is not a busy man is an understatement. However, it is this reporters belief that if there is a man that can handle all these trials, it is Ivan Stanislav. And in the near future, I would not be surprised if he is successful to the final letter of his plans.
