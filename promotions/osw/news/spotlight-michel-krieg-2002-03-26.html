Spotlight: Michel Krieg

26 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello OSW! It's me, Shanda Russell for another wrestler spotlight! Today I'm speaking with a member of The Red Army, Michel Krieg. Michel, how are you today? Krieg: I'm not too bad, since you asked... Shanda: So, you're relatively new to the OSW, what do you think of the company so far? Krieg: Well, from what I've seen of the other superstars in the federation, it seems to be dominated by Capitalists, which I'm obviously not happy about....But that will soon change with the efforts of the Red Army, believe me.... Shanda: Yes, that is true that you recently joined the ranks of The Red Army. What do you think of the group thus far? Krieg: Well, what can I say about the Red Army? They are the most dominant group in all of the Sports Entertainment today, and so far have eliminated Capitalist's left, right and centre...As for the members....Ivan Stanislav: PCW Legend, Tom Walczak: PCW Legend, Tempest: fWo Legend, Wang and Ruslan: Masterminds, The Two Round Faced Women: Absolutely Beautiful Women...What more can a man ask for when he joins a wrestling federation? Shanda: Fascinating. So, why exactly are you so anti-capitalist? Is it personal? Or just ideological? Krieg: Well, what is there to like about Capitalism? They try to dominate politics, rule out fairness amongst their people....Of course, it's also personal. Ever since Germany became one political unit, the Capitalist's have always been trying to make it seem as if us Communist's buckled under their pressure, but what they fail to realize is that one day, Communism will rise again...Now, can we move on, because I really do hate saying the word Capitalism....? Shanda: Okay, so, Ivan Stanislav is busy fighting against Toomes, Reagan, and Doug Troy. Tom Walczak is busy with Rhett Locke. Who or what exactly is Michel Krieg focusing on? Krieg: Well, at the moment, I'm pursuing a mission to take down Jesse Ramey, the man who seems to think he's "all that"...But Ramey's elimination will not be a hard feat, so watch out for Krieg taking out another Capitalist shortly after Ramey's demise.... Ramey's elimination will not be a hard feat.... Shanda: Of course. Also recently we have seen The Red Army almost appear to help Timo Bolamba and Aimz. And perhaps becoming more of a...good stable, if I may say that. Also, Ivan Stanislav has been getting increasingly involved with Tempest. Any thoughts on that, good or bad? Krieg: Well, if Ivan believes that Tempest is good for the Red Army, then I've got no objection, the same way I would expect Ivan, Walczak, etc, to have no objections if I thought a new member would be good for the Army. Personally, I believe Tempest is a very nice person and extremely skilled for a woman....As far as Timo and Aimz go...Well, as I said on PTC Radio earlier today, I'm not too sure what Ivan and the others want to do with Timo and Aimz, but I will follow suit if it is good for Communism. I went out to the ring during the main event on SLAM! to assist Walczak to take down 3B, but that's as far as my personal involvement goes.... Shanda: Interesting. Well, what does the future hold for Michel Krieg? Any major goals? Titles? Opponents you wish to battle? Krieg: The future. The future is skeptical for Michel Krieg. The Red Army is a solid unit at the moment, but things can change, people change, and there may come a time when the Red Army and Krieg are no longer associated, but let's not dwindle on that point. Titles. Well, titles are what Krieg needs, what I need to show the world and the rest of OSW, PCW, the PTC network, that I am a force not to be messed with. My major goals would be to take out as many Capitalist scum as I can, further the Red Army's name and quest for OSW domination and, as I said, win some major gold. As for opponents, I would like to battle quality brawlers such as Hessian, Azrael Cain, as well as skilled technicians such as Rhett Locke and Timo.... Shanda: Well, thank you Mr. Krieg for your time. Good luck in the OSW! Krieg: Thank you for the opportunity to express my feelings, and I hope you realize your destined career is not on television but behind it, doing the housework...But that's just my personal opinion. Shanda: Well folks, that brings another edition of wrestler spotlight to a close! I’m Shanda Russell for the OSW!
