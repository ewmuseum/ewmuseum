Da Westsidaz Update

23 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An official statement was released by OSW Medical Director Dr. Grabor Sakuisenkoff, from Pittsburgh General Hospital today regarding the condition of the former OSW Tag Team Champions, Da Westsidaz. 'For those of you that don't know, Riptah and Short Circuit were brutally assaulted last Saturday Night by the hired guns of the Think Tank: Armour, John Mammoth and Trashcan Man. Firstly, Short Circuit suffered a hyper-extended shoulder, a concussion and bruised ribs. We've kindly thanked OSW Officials for giving Short Circuit this weekend off so he may recover from his wounds. He was released from the hospital this morning. Secondly, Riptah is not in as great condition. Andrew Washington was cut on the forehead following the Pyrodriver into the dumpster and lost a lot of blood. Fortunately, we've been able to stitch the wound and pump Riptah back up with some donated blood. However, we're mostly worried about his back, which he's had trouble with in the past. The last few weeks, starting at the Pay-Per-View have been costly towards his condition and we've decided to keep him here longer until our expert comes back from vacation. We won't know his exact condition until Saturday, so we cannot give him his release to attend SLAM! However, we are confident he will get his release from the Hospital some time early next week.' After this statement was released, our reporters tried to get a word with Short Circuit but he hasn't returned our calls. Sources close to him have sad he's acting unnaturally hostile and lashing out at minor comments or irritants.
