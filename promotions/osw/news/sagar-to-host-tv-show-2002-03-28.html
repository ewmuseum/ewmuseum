Sagar to Host TV Show

28 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Propaganda's manager, Eric Sagar, got a phone call from the producers at GOD TV and on Friday morning, at 8:00 am EST, he will have his very own pilot chat show. "Praise be the Lord for granting me yet another forum to preach his gospel," said Sagar, via telephone. According to GOD TV publicist, Sissy Chastenomore, Sagar's main guest for the pilot be OSW's very own Propaganda. If all goes well the Producers at GOD TV could give Sagar a weekly slot. We will continue to monitor this story and bring you more as it develops.
