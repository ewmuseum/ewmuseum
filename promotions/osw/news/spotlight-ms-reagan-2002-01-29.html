Spotlight: Ms. Reagan

29 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello OSW, this is Shanda Russell with another edition of Spotlight: OSW’s recently named President, Jane Reagan. Below is a transcript of the very interesting interview: Shanda Russell: Hello OSW fans, I'm Shanda Russell here with the recently named owner of the OSW, Jane Reagan. Jane, it's a pleasure to be able to sit and talk with you! Jane Reagan: Likewise, hence why I hired you. Shanda Russell: Now, there are several questions that the fans and talent of the OSW and other PTC fans have, but first, lets go back a bit to last year at PCW Shanghaied, when Tyler Nelson stripped your husband of his power within the PCW. Shanda Russell: Back when that happened, did you already have a plan to seemingly sabotage the PCW, or did that occur more recently, as Nelson became even more power hungry? Jane Reagan: It has been my desire to ruin Nelson since Shanghaied in 2001. What he did to my family was inexcusable. Shanda Russell: You mentioned at Shanghaied that you were constantly harrassed, and almost abused by Nelson in the workplace. What sort of things would this neandrathal do? Jane Reagan: How old are you Shanda? Shanda Russell: Uh...well Ms. Reagan, I'm twenty four years old Shanda Russell: Right..well...do you know anything as to what happened to your husband, The Phantom Booker, after he was seemingly fazed out from the PCW completely? Jane Reagan: I am not sure. He was kidnapped by Media back in March and never seen again. Jane Reagan: The police have found no clues. Shanda Russell: With its smaller roster, and lesser budget, many people believe that the OSW will not be able to compete with various PTC feds, mainly of course, the PCW. Do you really believe that the wrestlers of the OSW will be able to survive against the federations in the PTC? And more importantly, really combat the PCW? Jane Reagan: Tyler Nelson has not even began to fathom the amount of damage I have done to his precious PCW machine. I have strip mined the roster and put overpaid scrubs in their place. Shanda Russell: Care to name some of the....lesser talent that have now become extremely overpaid? Jane Reagan: Hank now makes 2.4 million a year. I recently resigned several other lesser talents to PCW for seven figures. Shanda Russell: That's amazing! But of course, the big question that is on everyone's mind: Who are some of the talent from the PCW that you have signed. Most of the roster has been asked, and all wrestlers deny any knowledge of their buyout. Care to fill us in? Jane Reagan: You will have to wait until next Saturday night and tune into Slam! to see! Shanda Russell: In the current roster of the OSW, are there any individuals that you see coming above and beyond the call of duty and really taking the OSW to where it needs to go? Or do you believe that the roster as a whole are solid enough to do so? Jane Reagan: Great Dragon is, of course, a veteran. I am looking for big things from Clay Vendetta and Switchblade. Matt Blaze has an opportunity to be the franchise for OSW. Shanda Russell: Any idea what the name and date of the first OSW Pay Per View will be? Jane Reagan: That will be announced during an upcoming Slam! telecast. Shanda Russell: As this is the first document in which you are openly speaking to your OSW roster, is there anything that you would like to tell them? Jane Reagan: If you go out and work hard, I will make you stars. If you slouch out there and work soft in the ring, I will bounce you faster then a check from Enron. Shanda Russell: Very powerful statement, President Reagan. Thank you for your time, and I look forward to Saturday Slam! where most of my questions will officially be answered. I can gather various important points from this interview. President Reagan has had a plan all along, and she will not let the PCW run amok anymore. She is very confident, aggressive, and businesslike. Finally, that at Saturday Slam! many questions will be answered. Tune in to Saturday Slam! where all of our questions will officially be answered!
