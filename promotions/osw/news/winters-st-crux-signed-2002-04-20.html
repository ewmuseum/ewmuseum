Winters, St. Crux Signed

20 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Old School Wrestling announced today that it has signed Jason Winters and Jesus St Crux. "Both Jason and Jesus have been to the top of the moutain both here in the US and abroad," said OSW spokesperson, Shanda Russell. "The both bring a wealth of experience and talent to the table in OSW." Both men were once involved in the now defunct UWA, a federation that has since proven to be a goldmine of wrestling talent. Winters and St Crux are two former world champions of a federation that once boasted Wippit Guud, The Northern Wonder Kanaida, current fWo superstar, Mufanga Kagos, Ian Wright, Dan Bayer, Tiara Jade and Justin Sane. But since the UWA's demise, both men have fallen onto hard times. Winters, 23, of Vancouver, has moved from federation to federation, never again finding the success he enjoyed in the UWA. As he moved around, federations would fold, opponents would get hurt, or in one notable case, the head of the federation went into a coma! Jason was still performing well, but never clicked and caught on in his new environments, and kept moving on. Now, this still-young superstar, who seems to have been in the business his entire life, is starting fresh with OSW. According to sources, he's looking forward to joining a fed that offers stability and opportunity. After regaining the UWA World title from Winters on the last show broadcast by the UWA, St. Crux, 26, originally from Juarez, Mexico, fell onto hard times. He had not been as known a substance as some other UWA stars, and was newly on the rise in the public's eyes upon the closing of the federation.. this hurt his chances with other federations. After languishing in indy hell, Jesus returned to his native Mexico, where he enjoyed the respect due to a cruiserweight of his abilities. In a twist of fate, in returning home and giving up on his dream of catching on with a better federation, he caught the attention of an OSW talent scout in Mexico. Since signing these gifted young wrestlers, OSW talent scouts have been actively searching for other former UWA champions. Who can they sign next? Guud? Kanaida? Wright? Sane? Jade? Kagos? Stay tuned to find out.
