Polish Landmark Vandalized

13 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Poland was shocked early Thursday morning by the discovery of graffiti along the northern wall of the Braniski Palace, a historic building that currently serves as the seat of the municipal government departments. Written on the wall in red paint were the words "I ACCEPT." The exact meaning of the words has yet to be determined by Polish officials, but they said that they would get to the bottom of this defacement of one of their greatest landmarks. OSW superstar Tom Walczak, who hails from Poland and is reportedly very fond of his country could not be reached for comment. However, while searching for Walczak, OSW reporter Matt Shivan came across OSW World Heavyweight Champion, Doug Troy. When informed of the vandalism, Doug smirked and said "That's terrible. What a waste of spray paint, putting it all over that worthless Polish wall. I hope whoever did it learns not to waste all that valuable Krylon next time. Unfortunate, too. Sounds like the culprit was a DAMN artist." Doug walked away before any further questions could be asked. If more develops in this story, we'll be sure to bring it to you.
