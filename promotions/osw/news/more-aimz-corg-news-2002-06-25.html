More Aimz/Corg News

25 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After the disturbing actions of Corg in Ascension's main event brawl, Amy Campbell has finally spoken out, though not directly. Shortly after recovering and recieving a total of 19 stitches for various wounds at a local Phoenix hospital, OSW officials moved Campbell to an undisclosed location in what CEO Jane Reagan referred to as 'a measure taken to rule out any further violation at the hands of the maniacal and soon to be unemployed, Corg.' Aimz' chosen lawyer, coincidentally also a close family friend, spoke out on behalf of Campbell to press early Monday afternoon. "Obviously we have solid grounds for quite a sexual harassment lawsuit, one that could, in fact, land Mr. Raiyn in a federal prison for upwards of eighteen months, should we decide to go criminal with this," said the Michael Q. Payton, Esq. "Either way, with this many witnesses and video evidence of the act committed, Isiic Landes Raiyn will be penalized. Bail will most likely be an option to him, but I personally doubt the sum will be anything 'Corg' could possibly afford." Payton continued in a more personal statement. "On a personal note, I've known Amy Campbell all my life and I know some of the experiences she's had, along with some of the environments she's been subjected to but I can honestly say I've never heard Amy this disturbed. We've only spoken on the telephone at this point, but she's audibly shaken by the entire situation...the main worry being that the 'sicko' will pursue her further. She asked me to express a few feelings on her behalf, those 'feelings' involving several choice words that I don't dare repeat in public OR private...so let's just say she's very angry, but still feeling very vunerable and incredibly paranoid after the assault. And as a side note, I'm fairly certain she's still showering from the whole encounter." OSW owner, Jane Reagan, had something to say on the situation, herself. "We are just settling things up with our legal department, but by this time next week, he will be out of OSW for good and hopefully in a prison cell," said Reagan. More to come on this story as it developes further.
