Da Westsidaz Choose Bats

17 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Breaking news just came in from one of our officials in Los Angeles, CA, the sight of SLAM! and home of OSW's own, Da Westsidaz. Spokesperson of the two, Daisy, confirmed reports that Da Westsidaz' would be bringing baseball bats to Ascension this Sunday. "Well, Riptah was screaming about hitting homeruns with Civil Disobedience's heads, but Short Circuit explained that they decided to bring commemorative bats to the Weapon of Choice match," said the manager of the team and love-interest of Riptah. Daisy was also quoted saying, "They're happy with Civil D's choice, considering they had most of their tag success in matches that surrond ladders." This new information means that everything is set for the Weapon of Choice match for the Tag Titles this Sunday, with Civil Disobedience bringing Ladders, Heresian Bloodlust bringing Barbwire Mits and now Da Westsidaz bringing Baseball Bats.
