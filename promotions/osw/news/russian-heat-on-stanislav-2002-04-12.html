Russian Heat on Stanislav

12 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A well known source has told OSW reporters that as of late, the Russian hierarchy has been very critical of the actions of OSW superstar: Ivan Stanislav. Stanislav, a communist in the Russian Military, has been a hero to Russia since his bravery that was shown during the Afghanistan campaign in the mid eighties. However, word has been circulating that as of late his actions have been deemed ‘unacceptable and inexcusable’ by his superiors. The major ‘action’ in question refers to Stanislav’s giving of an original copy of a Stanislav heirloom, “The Communist Manifesto” which was written by Vladimir Lenin himself, to Stanislav’s recent love interest: Tempest. “Stanislav and Tempest, who have been seeing each other for the past few weeks, have grown quite close to one another,” stated one anonymous analyst, “and the actions of Ivan Stanislav could be perceived by Russia as a slap in the face.” Instantly an outbreak of protest by Communist leaders around the world was voices as Stanislav made this decision that could very well have caused him to lose face in the majority of his peers. While it cannot be proved completely true, it does appear that the Russian High Command is not pleased with Stanislav’s action. Alexei Ruslan, Stanislav’s closest friend, refused to comment on the situation while Stanislav himself said, “I do not ask anyone to question my actions. And if they truly have problem with it, come see Stanislav themselves!” What does the future hold in store for Stanislav? Tempest? The Russian Military has made it clear they are not supporting the relationship between the two. Will the stubbornness and…chivalry of Ivan Stanislav win out? Will the influence of the Russian Military intervene and break these two lovebirds up? Only time will tell.
