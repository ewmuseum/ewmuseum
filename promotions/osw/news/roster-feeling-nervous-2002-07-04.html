Roster Feeling Nervous

4 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With recent rumors of the closure, or at the minimum, a suspension of activities of the popular Fans Wrestling Organization, some OSW wrestlers have expressed concerns that they could be squeezed out of jobs by fWo talent. "Sure, it worries me," said the recently signed, Dave Gibson, whose comments were echoed by others on the roster. "During my time in IOW, I was running at the top of the card...then PIW shut down. From then on, most of the original IOWers were relegated to fringe rolls in the organization." OSW officials were quick to respond to the rumors. "It shows little or no faith in OSW management and booking teams of if our roster believes we would go out of our way to bring in outside talent to steal the spotlight from hardworking and loyal OSW superstars," said the angry owner of OSW, Jane Reagan. "It's disappointing that anyone would think we would act so irresponsibly." Reagan said while the company has had discussions with a small handful of former fWo workers, but warned that 'any deals are highly unlikely.' Three members of the fWo's roster at the time of the mega-organization's cease of operations, Tempest, Lance Knight and The Magi, have previously appeared on OSW television. Though Reagan refused to comment on who the company has had discussions with, industry insiders believe it would seem very unlikely that any of the three individuals with previous links to OSW would appear on OSW television any time soon. "Considering Tempest's high profile split with Ivan Stanislav, Lance Knight's high contract demands and the company's public displeasure with the Magi's work during his OSW run, I doubt you will be seeing any of those three on OSW television again any time soon," said Wrestlingtattler.com reporter, Jim Smitty. "In fact, I would surprised if OSW signed any fWo workers at all, considering a high profile signing could upset OSW's already fragile, yet well planned blend, of homegrown talent and established superstars." We will continue to monitor this intriguing story and bring you more news should it become available.
