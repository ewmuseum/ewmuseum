Wrestler Spotlight: Silence

1 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanda: Hello OSW Fans! I'm Shanda Russell here with another Wrestler Spotlight! My next guest today is Silence, one half of the tag team NC-17! Silence, thank you for this time! Silence: Fuck you. Shanda: Um..er...excuse me? Silence: Open your ears, get the shit out of them. I didn't stutter and it doesn't look like you have a hearing problem. Just because my agent said this would be good for my character doesn't mean that I have to be nice during it. Now fire away before I change my mind. Shanda: Uh..yes...okay. Ehem. So Silence! How do you like the OSW so far? Silence: Like it? I don't like the OSW on a whole but more of what it enables me to do. From my first match all the way to the present I have loved to hurt the human body and test its stress points. Wrestling just allows me to do it legally and with people or one person I like. Shanda: Um..wouldn't you say that's a little...cynical? Silence: No sadistic. Shanda: Yes..well, lets continue. When you joined the OSW, you instantly rekindled the team of NC-17 with Graphic Violence. What exactly is your relationship with Graphic Violence. He's quite scary, wouldn't you say? Silence: I'm quite scary, evil, sadistic and violent. Or relationship is based on that. We are two men who are on the same page, its like we where born on the same day of the same year with something in the cosmos that made it to be. Wrestling's dark cloud became a huge storm when GV and myself stepped into the ring together as partners. We never argue and always get along. Men who step in our ring, they are the ones to are scared. Shanda: With the Tag Team Tournament getting into swing, do you think that NC-17 has a good chance of winning? Who do you think will give you the most competition? Da Westsidaz are a solid team and always have been while Civil Disobedience has political pull. Any thoughts? Silence: I could give you the same answer everyone would give you but I will make it simple. No one has ever given us trouble and no one ever will. NC-17 will walk through the tournament and then shove the Tag Titles back up Reagan's ass. Shanda: You know..if I may say....you're not being very nice. Silence: You ever brushed your teeth through your ass? Shanda: Well uh...no. Silence: Would you like to try? Shanda: No thanks. Um..moving right along!! What are your goals for the OSW? Titles? Feuds? Anyone in particular you would like to fight? Silence: I have had 13 years in the wrestling business. At 31 years old I am not looking for titles anymore but now GV and myself have stated that OSW will fall to us and with that I will take all that step in my way. I don't give one shit or your head on who I fight but when you piss me off I will do anything to get you in a fight and if you need proof, ask Marcus Cortiea. Shanda: Marcus Cortiea? Silence: Marauder. Old friend. Shanda: Yes..well on Slam you have a match against Redemption Song, the MET tag team that had some comments to say about yourself and Graphic Violence. Thoughts? Silence: Lets see, I am already in OSW so I have nothing to prove. The guys in the ER just better get ready for a long night when Tanto and The Lone Granger get brought in. GV and myself are not even looking to break a sweet on these fucks and I don't see it going on past 5 minutes. Shanda: Well.....do you have anything you'd like to say to the OSW fans? Or anybody in particular? Silence: OSW Fans? Fuck off, Everyone else...you fuck off too. Shanda: I think we’ll stop…. Silence: (grabs her by the cuff of the shirt) This fucking interview is over, unless you want to wheel yourself around for a while. Shanda: N..no thanks..... Silence: (releases and throws the mic on the ground, leaving.)
