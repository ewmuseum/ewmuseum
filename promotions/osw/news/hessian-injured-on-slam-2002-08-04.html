Hessian Injured on Slam!

4 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Immediately following Slam!, medical staff on hand attended to Hessian, after the brutal beating he and the rest of The Cult took at the hands of Jeff Garvin and Dave Gibson of the Throwbacks. His flesh wounds were treated, however Hessian’s left arm was giving him the worst trouble. On analysing the arm Hessian favoured, three separate fractures were found, one on the Ulna, and one long hairline fracture on the Radius bones of Hessian’s forearm. First reports indicate that the fractures may put Hessian put of action for four to six weeks. Shanda Russell gave a statement regarding “This is really Hessian’s first “injury” in his time in OSW thus far, and probably in his career as a whole," said Russell. "The heat between the Cult and the Throwbacks is only going to grow, and casualties like this can be expected. Gibson pulled a nasty one on Hessian here, and the big man is going to have to take it easy until that arm is healed.” Also on hand to give their opinion were Vampir Nosferatu and Minion the Imp. “Gibson has struck down one of the foundations of The Cult…and while Hessian recuperates, our following will be sure to exact our own revenge until Hessian returns to exact his…the sands of time are hastily running out for Dave Gibson, he best start praying to his ‘god’...he’ll need all the help he can get…” said Vampir. “Yeah, that sommomabitch is gonna pay for what he done gone done to Hessian," added Minion. "Fractured arm or no fractured arm Gibson better watch his back booooy! Next time I see that curly haired, gibbon lookin’ sucka I’m gonna smack his bitch all kindsa up!-…” With Hessian out of action for four to six weeks, The heat between The Cult and The Throwbacks is sure to intensify in the coming weeks. If there is any update of Hessian’s condition in the coming weeks, you’ll be the first to know!
