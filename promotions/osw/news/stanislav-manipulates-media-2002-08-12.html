Stanislav Manipulates Media

12 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Stanislav Manipulates Media Perhaps learning from the past about the power of the “capitalist media” with his sell out article about his life, Ivan Stanislav has once again used the powerful tool of newsprint to further his beliefs within the OSW. “Think Red,” an article generally written by his known friend Alexei Ruslan, has just hit stands in it’s fifth edition and is written by Stanislav himself. While the column has always pledged to be truthful and unbiased, even the average person could tell that the words of Ruslan were nothing more than attempts to win over the fans. But with the fans already strongly on Stanislav’s side, his demeanor has changed. With an upcoming match against Corg on Saturday Slam, Stanislav made it a point to address the fact that not only was he going to “destroy the Silent Assassin” but also to ensure the fans that, “any line drawn between Ivan and Tempest regarding this matter is strictly coincidental in nature.” Stanislav brutally attacked Corg during his match against Tempest on the last Slam after Corg reverted to his old, perverted ways in the matchup. While it appears to be that Stanislav did it for Tempest, the column says otherwise. Stanislav went on to blast John Mammoth of the Think Tank, calling him a “coward that is lapdog to PHD.” Mammoth was not present to comment. While what Stanislav primarily addressed appeared genuine, a few inconsistencies were noticed by various OSW fans with microscopes. Most notably, Stanislav appeared to minimize his situation with Tempest by downplaying the past. “Of all the people I have ever seen in this business, Ivan Stanislav is the last person that would belittle the past and say to forget it!” Said OSW Commentator Nate Dumas. “Furthermore, two paragraphs later Stanislav claimed to hold on to the past and look to the future.” Stanislav went on to criticize Jane Reagan and voice his concerns about the new President of the OSW. “At least we’re seeing where Ivan Stanislav stands on various issues. He’s communicating with the fans in a relatively conventional manner, as opposed to knocking people’s teeth out and flipping over motor vehicles!” Said OSW female star Aimz who had once received the brunt of Stanislav’s anger, “Personally, I hope he kicks Corg’s fuckin’ ass!” Stanislav was livid after breaking up a fight between Corg and his ex-girlfriend Tempest and made it abundantly clear that he would get his revenge on Saturday Slam. The Russian Bear went on and assured the fans that Corg would get what he would deserve not because of what happened to Tempest, but because of what he has done in the federation as a whole. Can the Silent Assassin, a man twenty-two years younger and two hundred and two hundred and eighty pounds lighter than Stanislav have any way of repelling his onslaught? But more importantly… how much of this is professional, and how much of it is personal? More news as it comes to us.
