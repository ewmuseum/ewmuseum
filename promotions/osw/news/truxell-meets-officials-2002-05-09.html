Truxell Meets Officials

9 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
For months now, only a few brief words have been said about former CWL Asylum and jOlt superstar, Griff Truxell. Now, the self-titled "Greatness," has made headlines twice in as many days. Eye witnesses once again spotted Truxell in Montreal, this time late Wednesday night, as he boarded a private Jet en route to Toronto, with several high-ranking OSW officials, including Commissioner Ed and acting CEO Howard Porter. The destination of the flight has fueled speculation that Truxell will once again be backstage at this week's edition of Saturday Night Slam. Meanwhile, the current OSW superstars themselves were chartered from Montreal to Tornonto via a chartered Greyhound bus. When informed that Truxell was seen on a plane with OSW officials, several OSW superstars were outraged. "You kidding me," queried a miffed Clay Vendetta. "I got my knees being ground into the back of a bus chair thats being held together by duct tape, because some seven-foot satanist is decided he wanted to recline, while that jackass is sipping champagne and eating lobster of a private jet with our front office? That's just bull(expletive.)" No word on what exactly took place on the jet. What is known is while Truxell may be an undesirable worker backstage according to previous employers, he is still viewed as a ratings cow by many industry insiders. Many of those same insiders wonder, however, if an ego like Truxell's can do more harm to the company, than good. We will bring you more on this story as it develops.
