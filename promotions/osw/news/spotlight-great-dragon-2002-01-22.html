Spotlight: Great Dragon

22 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
by Shanda Russell I was again asked to sit down with one of our new OSW stars, this time the up-and-coming Great Dragon. I was able to speak to him in his current apartment near OSW headquarters, and he had a lot to say about his past, present, and future. Shanda Russell: Hello again, OSW fans! I'm Shanda Russell, and I'm here with none other than one of our most amazing stars, Great Dragon! Hello, Great Dragon! Great Dragon: Hello there....Shanda. Shanda Russell: Great Dragon, several people have been commenting that you are one of the top men in OSW, and a definite contender for the gold. What's your opinion on this? Great Dragon: I think that I will need to prove myself in the ring in order to justify the statement that I am a contender for the gold. But rest assured, I WILL be one...sooner or later.... Shanda Russell: Probably sooner than later, but we'll see. How did you decide on OSW? Great Dragon: Well, I was interested on some of the talent that was involved in it, and I was familiar with some of the work of the backstage staff. So, I accepted the offer. Shanda Russell: Definitely a good career move. Now, as I understand it, you have a tattoo on your leg of some significance. Would you mind explaining to the fans at home what it is? Great Dragon: It's a tattoo that was left on my leg from my parents. I believe one of them was...well, nevermind. Shanda Russell: I'm payed to mind. Please, go on. Great Dragon: Well, for some reason, 'Rebirth' is written on it. Now, I don't know my parents exactly, yet, but I intend to find out whenever I have the time to do geneological research Shanda Russell: I hope you learn what you need to about them. Now, you've had quite a bit of experience in professional wrestling, haven't you? Great Dragon: Yes. I've been in the sport for nearly a decade and a half, including my year and a half of training. Shanda Russell: Quite a career; most of your peers in OSW don't have nearly as much experience. You've also fought in many 'extreme' and death matches, correct? Great Dragon: Yes, I was in a promotion called 'Shuriken Professional Wrestling', which taught me much about resistance to pain and 'Hardcore' manuevers or matches Shanda Russell: Can you name a few memorable matches? Great Dragon: I was in a Barbed Wire death match against Tikada Mikama which took our bodies to the extreme, as well as one against 'The Beast' Inito Asoya which was also a classic, in my opinion. Shanda Russell: That kind of experience may really come in handy in OSW. Well, now that you're here, what do you hope to accomplish? What -- or who -- do you have your sighs set on? Great Dragon: I intend to give the fans a good show as well as winning a title here and there as a reward Shanda Russell: But there's no one person you plan on taking down? Great Dragon: I do not know the others in the promotion well enough to have made any enemies...yet. But I'm sure I'll find some that are more then willing to give me a challenge Shanda Russell: Finally, where do you expect OSW, as a whole, to be in six months? Great Dragon: I expect it to be a rapidly expanding promotion that will be at the top of Primetime Central, perhaps matching PCW Shanda Russell: Thank you, Great Dragon, and good luck in your match against Sho-Lin. Quite the opposite of Matt Blaze, whom I interviewed a few days ago, Great Dragon seems to be the very soul of humility, drive, and purpose. He exudes confidence, without a doubt, but none of the arrogance that marks so many of his peers. I think we can expect the Dragon to go far in Old School Wrestling.
