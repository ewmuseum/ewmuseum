OSW — Column

Think Red

9 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings my fellow Communists, it is I, Alexei Gregorovich Ruslan for a long overdue edition of my greatest, unbiased column in the PTC: Think Red!! The last time that I endeavored to write to you people, I was a member of the Primetime Championship Wrestling Federation in which Ivan Stanislav was crushing the likes of Jeremy Howard, Jason Snow, and Ed. Now, however, we have come to the Old School Wrestling Federation to come back to the ‘roots’ or wrestling, if you will, as well as continue to wave of terror that we do so love to enjoy spreading! However, I am ever mindful that it is in the best interest of all parties to give each individual their do, and thus I will be speaking from a strictly third person perspective throughout this entire column, without having my personal beliefs interfere with my judgment! OSW Crowning Glory is on the horizon, and we of The Red Army feel very confident in our stake within that venue. Below are my personal beliefs of the following matches, the individuals involved, and whatnot. Three Way Dance Naket vs. CCV vs. Mike Marvelous In my personal opinion, these three individuals are all complete fools! Since the OSW came to be, these three have floundered in their attempts to make an impact within the federation, while those with power and vision, much like myself and Ivan Stanislav, have come to the forefront of the company and are molding it to what we see fit. Naket instantly falls out of my favor, because he is Canadian. And Mike Marvelous, being not only Canadian BUT a capitalist, in the form of his stupid soft drink, only manages to fall even farther out of my favor. As for Clay Vendetta, I do not like this man as far as I could spit, but at least he is not Canadian, nor an active capitalist in the economic sense. Alexei’s pick: Classic Clay Vendetta OSW Tag Title Match Heresian Bloodlust vs. Civil Disobedience Since the OSW’s conception, these two teams have battled one another countless times in an attempt to garner control of the OSW’s slowly growing Tag Team Division. Now, the titles are on the line for the first ever OSW Tag Titles. On the side of Heresian Bloodlust, they have the size and power to easily dwarf their smaller opponents, but I wonder if their mental foundation will be able to hold up. I mean, these are men that think they come from the dark area’s of hell and believe they are here to destroy the world, or something like that? Is that a true goal? I do not think so. Civil Disobedience, while perhaps not the most intelligent people in their own right, will have to use their speed and technical superiority to overcome their larger opponents. And while I do not agree with the way that they act, if conscripted into the right form of army service, I am sure they would make excellent cannon fodder. Alexei’s Pick: Civil Disobedience. CD will manage to get beaten to within an inch of their lives, but they do not have nearly as many enemies as Heresian Bloodlust does. Also, Azrael Cain and Hessian appear to be more attuned to singles competition whilst Civil Disobedience are true Tag wrestlers. #1 Contender Match for International Title Tom Walczak vs. Jesse Ramey Is there truly a question as to who will win this match? I have seen Tom Walczak wrestle for months and he is constantly surprising me with new moves, new tactics, and a newfound energy that I had not seen in him. Under the tutelage of Ivan Stanislav and with the drive of communism running through his veins, The Polish Punisher has been a force to be reckoned with. On the other hand, Jesse Ramey is a cross dressing individual….but I am not sure what sort of edge that is to give him, except for the men in his life! Alexei’s Pick: Tom Walczak. Without a doubt, The Polish Punisher will crush the dress wearing, feminine Jesse Ramey into the mat and be well on his way to his first title reign in the OSW! Stipulation Match Ritchie Syxx vs. Corg Once again, I am inclined to focus on the mental strength of the man known as Corg, as I realize that he believes he can go around killing people for the sheer joy of it, or something related to that subject. This man, a mere 17 year old child, is far too foolhardy to even be in this profession, let alone be exposed to the rest of the world. That goes without saying, however, that Ritchie Syxx is from Great Britain and therefore is also subject to stupid behavior, seems to doom these two to a brutal and very dangerous match. Alexei’s Pick: Corg. Corg will pull the victory out over Syxx, if for any other reason I have heard from various contacts that the stipulation on this match will only increase the danger in the ring, which plays right into Corg’s strengths. Hopefully, however, someone will go to the ring and destroy both men, and rid us of their stupid ways. International Title Match Drew Hostyle vs. Rhett Locke A strange combination of two wrestlers, that is for sure. While we of The Red Army are not very in tuned with the bad blood between these two individuals, I can comment on the pure physical wrestling status between both men. Drew Hostyle appears to have the experience edge, and the help of his 3B friends will be sure to help out as well. And I would like to take this moment to express my anger towards various factions interfering in their respective matches in order to promote the victory of their friends. That is unacceptable and against the rules. We of The Red Army do not condone the cheating ways of 3B, and we apologize on the part of the OSW for any anguish they may have caused their viewers. Basically, I see this match to be a purely technical match, but ultimately the advantage will go to Hostyle. Alexei’s Pick: Drew Hostyle. Expect more lawlessness from these hoodlums from America which will garner the win for Hostyle. Last Man Standing Michael Dame vs. Frankie Bones Last Man Standing matches are always very physical, naturally, and tend to play towards the individual with the size and endurance advantage. I believe that when coming down to pure wrestling ability, Michael Dame has the overall advantage and in a regular wrestling match he may have the edge, but in a pure brute force match such as this, Frankie Bones will probably pull out the victor. Alexei’s Pick: Frankie Bones. Bones has ‘Pain’ written all over him, and not only can he dish it out, but he can also take an insurmountable amount of pain. I may not like him, but I have to go with my instincts, which are ALWAYS right. Tag Team Match Lennox Lanthrop/Dark Liege vs. Vampir Nosferatu/TBA This is to be an interesting match as well. While we of The Red Army are focused on the political stability of the OSW, these men are fighting for ‘darkness’ and ‘evil.’ A very confusing topic to be handled in wrestling ring if I do say so myself. But there is one thing that I do know, and that is how Vampir Nosferatu operates. Since information on his partner is limited, and the abilities of the Dark Liege are scarce as well, I must disregard those factors and put him up against Lennox Lanthrop. And in that context, the answer is quite simple: Alexei’s Pick: Vampir Nosferatu. Vampir is not afraid of anyone, save for maybe Ivan Stanislav, and won’t be swayed by The Dark Liege or Lanthrop, and the addition of his own partner will only tip the scales more in his favor. Three Way Dance, Elimination Match Falls Count Anywhere, No DQ Meanstreak vs. Doug Troy vs. The Mighty Savior of the OSW: Ivan Stanislav I have heard rumblings from other critics regarding this match in which many believe that Praporschik Stanislav and Meanstreak will cancel one another out, and Doug Troy will get the win. Yet, I feel they ignore the “Elimination” aspect of this stipulation. WHEN Ivan eliminates Meanstreak, it will be Stanislav vs. Doug Troy, and then Doug Troy will be destroyed. It is not up for debate, there is no other outcome. Meanstreak will be squashed, Troy will be sent to hospital again, and Ivan Stanislav will once again make the OSW proud! Alexei’s Pick: Ivan Sergeiovich Stanislav, the mighty Communist that will deliver the OSW to the top of the PTC! OSW World Heavyweight Title Match Switchblade vs. Timo Bolamba I am outraged to find that Ivan Stanislav was not in this match, for these two men do not deserve to even come close to OSW World Title, but unfortunately due to Jane Reagan and her cheating ways, they are fighting one another. Switchblade has his head so far stuck up his rear end, that it’ll give Bolamba the chance to…dare I say, capitalize on this and defeat him. Switchblade is easily amused and will probably be busy looking at himself in the mirror, or some sexy looking girl in the crowd, and get rolled up. But ultimately, I see Ivan Stanislav coming down to wrestling ring and defeating both men, and while he is not scheduled for the match, the people of OSW will rise and salute him and recognize him as World Champion!! Alexei’s Pick: Ivan Stanislav via complete and total domination of opposition and embrace of the people! Those are my picks for Crowning Glory, and I would like to reiterate that there is no bias, and these are just the way of looking at the Pay Per View outside of the ‘box.’ Crowning Glory will only increase the strength of the OSW and make it more of a powerhouse federation than it already is! I would also like to mention that as of this date: 8 March 2002, it is National Women’s Day in Russian Federation, and all of those teeny boppers that are in love with Ivan Stanislav, as well as those that may be closer to him than they realize, are wished a happy Women’s Day!! So fellow Communists of the OSW, I urge you, if you wish to join the ranks of Red Army, get in contact with a representative quickly and join our ranks! Read the Doctrine of Lenin! Listen to the Soviet National Anthem! And always….ALWAYS THINK RED!!! Alexei Ruslan Representative of The Red Army
