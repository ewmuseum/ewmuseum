Spotlight: Reno Davis

6 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanda: Hello OSW! I'm Shanda Russell with another Wrestler Spotlight! I'm here today with Reno Davis! Reno, thank you for your time!! Reno: Yeah, Hi. No problem Shanda: So Reno, first thing is first. How do you like it in the OSW so far? Reno: Eh...I've met a few alright people like Aimz and some really great people like Tempest but I think it'd be better if there weren't so many big guys everywhere that wanted to kill me. I got religious fanatics, nazi supremists, communists, and little old chinese men after me and ZaC all the time. It tends to hurt. Shanda: Yes...obviously one of the major questions on everyone's mind is that of, "Why did you seemingly ruin the relationship between Ivan Stanislav and Tempest?" Care to elaborate any more? *Editor Note: Reno did not answer the question* Shanda: Ooookay.....well....then next question! On Saturday Slam, Ivan Stanislav made two things clear: That he was going to destroy those that had 'collaborated' with you, and exact his revenge upon you. Thoughts? Feelings? Reno: Ivan's got the right to be upset at me, but what he did to Aimz was right uncool. Whatever he wants to do to me, should be physically done to just me and not my friends. Ivan is in a rage right now, meaning that he's not thinking his actions through...and he's not the smartest fella to begin with so that's two strikes against him. I'll be here for him and I'll strike intelligently. I've beaten bigger and meaner than Ivan, and I'll continue to do so. Shanda: How exactly do you plan on defeating Ivan Stanislav, if it comes to that? He is almost 3 times your size! Reno: He has his little army, I have mine. Once the odds are equalized, It'll be just Ivan and I in the ring. That's when I shine. As to my tactics, the magician never gives away his secrets...or is it? Wait, yeah that's it. Shanda: Uh huh. Moving right along. If I may ask, Mr. Davis....what's wrong with your eyes? Reno: It was a, uh birth defect. I was born like this...But, I don't really like talking about them. A lot of people wear contacts to give off their freaky appearance, but for me it's real, not pretend. I suppose it's more fun when you can decide whether you want to be normal or not Shanda: You're signed up in the Cruiserweight Tournament this coming Slam against Aimz, who took a tremendous amount of pain that some say is because of you. Any thoughts on the match? On Aimz? Reno: Aimz is doing alright. I turned her straight-edge and she was feeling pretty good about that, but the beating took a lot out of her. She's been feeling a bit better, so she should be tip top for the match, if not a little shaky. I'm going to give her a run for her money, but she's like the sister I never had, so no hard feelings will be held if she mops the floor with me. *Reno smiles* Shanda: Why "The Unwanted Angel?" Reno: it was a name given to me during my indy tenure in POW. My mentor and trainer, Kyle Noiles, coined me that because of the rush of personal problems I've had throughout my entire life. I've endured every single thing that's been thrown my way, but no matter what, something else is there waiting. Kyle said that I was "God's unwanted angel". I guess the name just sort of stuck Shanda: Fascinating. And how about ZaC? Is he enjoying the OSW? What about Loyal Communist Party Member Wang? Reno: Wang's got some horny old man fetish for ZaC, but the old man can barely hold his own wang while peeing, let alone catch and molest ZaC. ZaC's having a great time, excluding the incident with Krieggy. Shanda: Well, barring any severe injury at the hands of Ivan Stanislav, what does the future hold for Reno Davis? Reno: I'm going to keep on doing my thing. Ivan and his footclan can do whatever they want, because as I've said before, I've beaten bigger and meaner than Ivan and the Red Army, and I'll do it again. Reno: goodnight? Shanda: Well, thank you for your time Reno! Good luck in the OSW, and good luck with Ivan Stanislav. I think you'll be needing it. Reno: Yeah, thanks a lot. Oh, and Ivan...I hear you still fear Boda. Shanda: Well folks, some powerful words by Reno Davis. Will he move ahead in the Cruiserweight Tournament on SLAM? Will he survive Slam? Tune in this Saturday to find out!!
