Spotlight: Jason Winters

3 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanda: Hello OSW! I'm Shanda Russell with another edition of Wrestler Spotlight! I'm sitting here today with Jason Winters, one of the newest members of the OSW roster! Thank you your time today Jason! Winters: No problem, Shanda! Thank you for taking the time out of your schedule to do this interview. Shanda: No problem! First off, congratulations on joining the OSW! Winters: Thanks a lot! I'm happy to be here. The attitude in the locker room is amazing, compared with the other places I've been. Shanda: Yes, I've noticed that you have been in a number of other federations. So why the OSW? Winters: Well, I've been almost everywhere in the business, and nothing has suited me well. However, with a lot of former business associates coming to OSW, I felt it was the right move. There was already a good roster in place, so I felt that I would be a good enhancement. Shanda: That is very true that you came with a number of other individuals. So how will Jason Winters stand out from the crowd? Winters: I hope to show that in the ring against Jesus at Slam! Shanda: So what are some of your goals while you're here in the OSW? Titles? Fame? Winters: Really, it's neither of those things. In this business, I've realized that fame and titles are both fleeting things. My goals are to affect at least one person in the audience, to make them go "wow," when they see me in action, and to make myself feel happy and fulfilled when I'm doing that. Shanda: Fascinating. That is a very noble, yet unfortunately rare thing in this business anymore. Now, there are several wrestlers in the OSW that comes from many different walks of life. Is there anyone in particular you are focused on? Anyone you'd like to wrestle in the future? Winters: I'd really enjoy wrestling Reno Davis, Epitome of Evil, and Ivan Stanislav. That would be an amazing four-way match, and I'd just be happy participating in that. Shanda: Well, you seem to have a focus, and it's nice to see someone fighting for the 'right things' in wrestling for a change! Any other comments? Winters: Not really. I just want to tell Jesus that I've put our past behind us, and that I'm sorry for what went on. I really want him to know that I look forward to our match. I hope it's a classic. Shanda: Well, again, congratulations on joining the OSW roster, and good luck with your match this Saturday! Winters: Thank you, Shanda. I'm just glad to be here.
