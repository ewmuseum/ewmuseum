Porter On Vax Trail

8 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After finding a hair, presumably belonging to the Vax, in the manila envelope containing the hospital surveillance tape of Jane Reagan being kidnapped on Slam this past Saturday, new acting OSW CEO "The Scholar" Howard Porter and Commissioner Ed were quick to act. "We immediately sent the follicle in for DNA testing," said Porter. "Hopefully we can find out who the Vax is quickly, and handle the situation accordingly. Don't expect actions against him to be light, as the crimes he has perpetrated are great. Once we know his identity, he will be easy to deal with." OSW Commissioner Ed is also confident that the Vax will soon be apprehended. "Only a sniveling worm would hide behind a little dog and only attack people when they weren't around. "The Vax's reign of terror will soon come to an end." Updates on the genetic diagnosis for The Vax's discovered hair follicle are to follow.
