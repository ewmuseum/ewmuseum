KW Calls Out Reagan

26 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A little over a week after his girlfriend, Amanda, was kidnapped by Scott Taylor, Kid Wonder finally speaks out on the situation. But instead of blasting Taylor and Randolph, Scott's bodyguard, Wonder went after a totally different target: OSW owner Jane Reagan. "I'd have expected something, anything, from Jane by now," said the upset Wonder. "Sure, we're hardly friends or anything, but when Corg did what he did to Aimz, Jane was quick to punish Corg. She stood up for a fellow woman. But when Amanda gets kidnapped by a pair of jackasses, she does nothing. If Jane was willing to stick up for Aimz, why can't she do the same for Amanda? It's a load of crap if you ask me." While Kid Wonder might be upset about Reagan’s involvement, or lack thereof, concerning Amanda's kidnapping, he surely hasn't forgotten the man behind it all. "Oh, don't worry," claimed the teen idol, "that bastard is gonna get his. I'm counting down the days to Philly. And when Sunday does roll around, you can bet your ass I'll be ready. Taylor wanted to make things personal, and on Sunday night, he'll be beaten, bloodied, and broken personally by me." Kid Wonder and Scott Taylor will square of Sunday night in a TLC match with the winner receiving both Amanda and Randolph. We'll keep you updated on any more information concerning Amanda's kidnapping when it becomes available.
