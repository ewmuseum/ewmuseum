Announce Team Named

20 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Old School Wrestling has named its announce team, and it certainly has a PCW flavor to it, with former Primetime Indy Circuit Nate Dumas sitting in the lead announcer's chair, along with now retired, sure to be PCW Hall of Famer Doug Troy and longtime PCW announcer Steven Smith working as color commentators. "We feel both Nate and Doug are just teaming with charisma, especially Nate," said PCW Personnel Director Jane Reagan. "Steven provides us with a bit of an X-factor." Dumas, who has been out of wrestling since winning the PIC Heavyweight title back in 2000 saying 'No federation can afford my ample talent,' has been seen on PCW TV occassionally signing autographs. Known for his all-American boy good looks, winning smile and college education, Dumas would seem to fill the role nicely. "Fans can expect knowledge of the sport, quick wit and intelligent commentary," said Dumas, when asked about his commentary style. "I am as complete a player behind the mic as I am in tthe ring. Troy, who recently retired from active wrestling after his 6th knee operation, was long considered the 'heart and soul' of PCW. With a degree in communications, well spoken manner and quick wit, DT is certainly the man for the job. "It will all be good unless Steven puts his hand on my thigh," quipped DT at the press briefing. "If so, he will find his head driven through the announce table. Nate and I certainly are not going to put up with his nonsense the way Kern and Marx did." The openly gay Smith is joining his third promotion working commentary, previously working for UFW and PCW. "BOCA!" screamed Steven Smith, much to the delight of those in attendence. "It's good to be back."
