Wrestler Responds to Blaze

7 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After hearing about the complaints from "Black Ice" Matt Blaze, Jesse Ramey made his comments apparent to anyone within shouting distance in backstage area. "You know, I've been doing this thing for nearly twelve years now," said Ramey, "I've worked with people all shapes, sizes, and it doesn't matter to me whether or not someone is a cross over star. For all I care, this guy could have been a crossover polo star and I wouldn't have cared. The only real thing that matters is his attitude and his ability." When asked if Blaze's lack of experience had anything to do with his and other workers unwillingness to work with the former college All-American, Ramey was quick to respond. "I don't want someone getting into the ring with me that doesn't know what he is doing and could easily put my career, or perhaps my life, in danger," snapped Ramey. "I'll work with the guy, but I sure as hell will not be doing any jobbing to him. Now that's a damn fact" When asked what size dress he wore during his stint as CCW women's champion, Ramey abruptly ended the interview. We will bring you more on this story as it develops.
