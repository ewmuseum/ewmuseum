Switchblade Says #2

26 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, OSW Fans! This is YOUR NUMBER ONE CONTENDER here, with some factoids from THE STAR himself. First off, as you all know, I, SWITCHBLADE, NUMBER ONE CONTENDER TO THE OSW WORLD TITLE... is...um...THE NUMBER ONE CONTENDER TO THE WORLD TITLE! And Tumor Bologna can say anything he wants, do whatever he wants... But he'll never get MY NUMBER ONE CONTENDERSHIP! I am inviolate, my NUMBER ONE CONTENDERSHIP is firm, firmer than the succulent milky white breasts of Miss Reagan, or as I like to call her... "Goddess of Love"... Speaking of which, have you ever snuck into your boss's office and rummaged around in her luggage? I have, and let me tell you something. My boss, the most powerful woman in the professional wrestling world, smells like a sweet jasmine, a fragrant flower-scent in a sea of unwashed humanity. How do I know this? I sniffed her panties. I figure, hey, why not? I'm the STAR! I CAN DO WHATEVER I FRIGGIN' WANT! I'M THE NUMBER ONE CONTENDER TO THE OSW WORLD TITLE, AND IF I WANT TO SNEAK INTO MY BOSS'S OFFICE AND SMELL HER UNDERGARNMENTS, I CAN DO IT! AND THERE'S NOONE WHO CAN STOP ME! On to other buisness. I came under fire for my two promos for the Global Tourney. Said I broke Kayfabe, and I 'stole' copyrighted CCW material... Well, lemme tell you something, naysayers! I made that federation, and it's well within my right to do whatever I want. You know why? BECAUSE I'M THE NUMBER ONE CONTENDER TO THE OSW WORLD TITLE! THAT MEANS I CAN STEAL MATERIAL, SNIFF PANTIES, AND HIT SAMOANS UPSIDE THE HEAD WITH WRENCHES IF I DAMN WELL FEEL LIKE IT! It seems that Ivan Too-Drunk-On-Vodka-To-Get-It-Up-For-Tempest-ovich or Cumstreak will be my opponent(s) for MY World Title. Now, you people whine and say 'Hey, I know I'm just a lowly fan and I couldn't possibly appreciate what the hell is going on because I'm caught up in my own little world, but you said 'YOUR'World Title. You're just the number one contender! You don't have the title yet...' Then it is my duty to inform you that you CAPITIALIZE that I am THE NUMBER ONE CONTENDER TO THE OSW WORLD TITLE. Secondly, isn't it a foregone conclusion? Am I not THE STAR~? I am, and you all know it, so shut up and accept it. I'M THE STAR, AND YOU ARE ALL NOTHING! See you on Slam!
