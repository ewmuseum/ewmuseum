El Drago/Stanislav Update

8 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After their rigorous match on Saturday SLAM, Ivan Stanislav and El Drago were both sent to the Tulsa Hospital to treat their numerous injuries. Stanislav is reported to have re-broken a rib on his left side after performing an elbow drop from the top of the steel cage during his match. Following that, the steel cage was lowered over his ribs, furthering the injury. Stanislav is also reported to have a mild concussion after absorbing fourteen chairshots to the skull as well as trauma to the back from falling through the cage, to the ring below. El Drago suffered many injuries. The most dangerous being a collapsed right lung. The Doctor made a brief statement about El Drago's injuries, "First and foremost, El Drago will be out of action indefinitely. He suffered a collapsed lung at the hands of Ivan Stanislav. There seems to be several injuries that we do not have on file yet, but after some tests we will discover them all.” "His left shoulder has considerable damage to it including a tremendous amount of scar tissue that may require surgery to clean out. His labia in his right arm appeared to have been broken earlier on and never allowed to heel as it has scarring around the break. Add to the fact that he has only momentarily regained consciousness and it's unlikely that El Drago will be back in action anytime soon." "When he did wake up he made one small request and that was to keep his face hidden. We do everything we can to keep our patients happy so we had a special gown made for him which covers him head to toe." Stanislav and Drago both suffered internal injuries as well, and internal bleeding has been reported. Therefore, the two men who beat each other practically to death, are ironically sharing the same hospital room. News also has hit that with the multitude of injuries that occurred on SLAM, an entire floor has been reserved for the wrestling superstars, who line the hallways in their hospital beds.
