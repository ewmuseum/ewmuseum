Stanislav Mourns Ed

22 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Upon hearing the shocking news that Ed died after being allegedy removed from life support by Alan Kriegman, Ivan Stanislav was quick to issue a statement. “First and foremost, my condolences go out to Edward’s remaining family, friends, and fans. I am happy to say that while he and I did not see eye to eye many many times, my last time working with him we had set our differences aside and fought to rid the OSW of the scum that still exists within it. Ed helped to make the OSW what it is today and his antics, whether appreciated or not, helped to mold it into the federation that it is today. He fought valiantly, albeit unconventionally, for what he believed was right and that is admirable no matter who you are.” Stanislav was also sure to mention the culprits in the situation. “The murderous act of Alan Kriegman, which surely includes his other allies within the Think Tank, will not go unnoticed. The OSW has no one to fight for them. Ed is dead. Doug Troy has given up. Now, Ivan Sergeiovich Stanislav will bring the men guilty of this to justice. On Saturday Slam, Trashcan Man, a man I have soundly defeated many times, and Alan Kriegman, a coward, will both fall to the might of The Russian Bear!” Ivan Stanislav demanded a handicap match against Kriegman and Trashcan Man on Saturday Slam. But what does the Think Tank have in store for The Russian Bear? Is the seemingly limitless intelligence too much for The Russian Bear’s unending strength? Or does The Russian Bear have a trick up his own sleeve? Tune in to Slam to find out!!
