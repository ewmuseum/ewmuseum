Spotlight: Vampir Nosferatu

20 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello OSW! It’s me, Shanda Russell, back with another edition of Wrestler Spotlight! This edition features noneother than Vampir Nosferatu!! Shanda: Hello OSW! I'm Shanda Russell with another edition of "Wrestler Spotlight!" With me today is Vampir Nosferatu!! Vampir, how are you today?? Vampir: ... Shanda: Uh huh...so uh....tell me Vampir, why did you 'jump ship' over to the OSW?? Vampir: Why did I jump ship as you so graciously put, well Ms. Russell I shall tell you. First and foremost, since my brother sacrificed his body so that I may continue, in essence it was a rebirth, a new beginning. A new endeavor that I myself would have been foolish not to take. Also however, it has become somewhat of an entertainment item for myself, for you see, for so long Mr. Nelson feared the enemy from the outside, however he did not realize that the enemy was right under his nose, himself. Ahhh such an enjoyable thing to watch, Nelson's little empire crumble right before his very eyes. Shanda: -laughs nervously- Yeah, but like, we know that this is just wrestling, you can't like...change bodies. So what happened to the old Vampir? He was smaller..are you his brother? -Nervous laughter- Vampir: How closed thy mind is young Shanda. Reality is something you mortals have only skimmed the surface on. Vampir and Maskimxul are as one now, in mind, body, and strength. Shanda: Um...yeah...right. So, now you're in the OSW and things have gotten to a decent start. But I have to ask, what are your thoughts on Propaganda? Vampir: Ah yes, the man who professes exactly as he is named. This peasant from the backwater town of Clarksville, Tennessee has taken it upon himself to interject himself into the darkness. Could it be because he is starving for attention, that he wants to make a name for himself...perhaps. One thing Mr. Propaganda and his little lackey Eric Segar will learn, once you draw the line in the sand there is no turning back. There is no mercy, no remorse...his will be a expiration that will serve as an example and testimony to any other mortal who decides to act in defiance. May their pagan god have mercy on their soul, because soon enough, they will learn that the darkness can be a very bloody and painful experience. Shanda: Interesting....um...let me ask you this then...when you bite people..is that a blood capsule? Vampir: Would you like a demonstration? (grinning) Shanda: Ha..ha..that's funny..um..no, it's okay....I don't want to get any liquid on my dress. Um....so, what are your plans in the OSW? Vampir: To systematically and methodically reap the souls of all who is placed before me. From the tiny peasant, Propaganda to the higher echelon of Ivan and Meanstreak. All, my dear Shanda, will succumb. Shanda: Would that include me?? Vampir: (grins) Shanda: Fascinating....yes, well, I am glad you put aside some time for this interview Vampir but I really must be going...any final comments? Vampir: In just a short time, one soul will survive this Global Tournament and be named my opponent for the PTC Global Title. Be forewarned whoever this brave entity shall be, the prophecies have revealed your imminent futility, your soul will become mine. Warriors of OSW, with a rebirth, comes a new method to my madness, no one is safe, no one is immune...the blood will roll off your lips as you will all become my victims. Prepare yourselves well for the nightmare that will become your reality. So it is written, so it shall be done. So folks, that ends another edition of Wrestler Spotlight. I must say, Vampir Nosferatu seems to have his sights set on something above just regular titles, only time will tell what exactly comes from this mysterious and…unorthodox OSW superstar!!
