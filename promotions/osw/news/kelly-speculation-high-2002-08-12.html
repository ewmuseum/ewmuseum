Kelly Speculation High

12 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OSW officials and fWo World Champion, Erik Kelly, have refused to comment on the shocking arrival of fWo World Champion Erik Kelly last week on Slam, or whether Kelly has a future with the company. "We have absolutely, positively no comment on that situation," said an adamant OSW spokesperson, Shanda Russell. "We won't have a comment today, tomorrow or next week, for that matter." Russell's reaction has left insiders to wonder if indeed the situation with Kelly was a "shoot" and not staged by OSW officials. "I think they really had no clue he was going to crash the show," said Sly Simone, of the popular online wrestling sheet, The News. "I don't fault them for not cutting to commercial when he came out, because lord knows that was money for ratings. On the other hand, if it was a shoot, he did actually attack two people, which means there is something seriously wrong with Erik Kelly." Others feel the situation is a work, and Kelly is already under OSW contract. "Come on," said Wrestlingtattler.com's Jim Smitty. "Would you have left a camera on a champion from another promotion who just smashed one of your workers in the head with a cinder block, and tossed another over the top rope...THEN give him free air time? Seemed a little too diliberate if you ask me." Shoot or work? Only three things are for sure. OSW isn't talking, Kelly isn't talking and we will be all over this story like flies on stink and bring you any new information as it develops.
