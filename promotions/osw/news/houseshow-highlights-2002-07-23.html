Houseshow Highlights

23 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OSW rolled into Scranton-Wilkes-Barre, Pennsylvania for a house show, where 1400 screaming fans packed the Jennifer S. Bullwinkel Jr. High School gymnasium to watch OSW superstars battle in the ring, will all the proceeds going to benefit the Kidney Transplant fund of 11 year old local boy, Michael Dadwasadrunk. The show started off with a surprise visit from Clay Vendetta, who came to the ring and cut a promo about being a fighter. He talked about being undersized his whole life, and being told he “would never make it”, but asked the fans what they thought of him now, which brought a rousing cheer. Vendetta told young Mikey to keep his head up and keep fighting, because his kidney would be coming soon. After Vendetta’s promo, MET competitors Redemption Song and Total Violation hit the ring for the first of four tag team matches on the evening, unfortunately due to technical problems, and a fat lady who was sitting in front of me during the entire event, I missed most of the action, just picking up the outcome, which was Redemption Song picking up the win in a “boot down” match. After a brief intermission, which featured a raffle for several brand new OSW “Original” Jeff Garvin T-shirts, we headed back to the ring, where MET participants All About the Benjamins and Guardians of the ring went at it.
