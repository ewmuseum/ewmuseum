League to Watch in 1997

30 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In 1997, I will posting three cards and two reports for Independent Championship E-wrestling. These cards are: Monday Night Carnage, Thursday Night Mayhem, and Roadkill on Ice. The reports are ICE Cold Magazine shows. ICE is run by Mike Eliott ( [detail withheld] ) and effective January 7th when my league ERWI is merged into it, I will have co-fed responsibilities. Mike and I will be working awfully hard, as well as Neil Marshall and Jen the Cow to keep everything working. Mike writes Mayhem, I write Roadkill on Ice, Neil writes Mayhem, Jen writes one ICE Cold and Mike the other. So in 1997 when you see [ICE] and then a card or report, take the time to read it. Most likly, it will be pretty good. While every card, ppv, or report might not be the best you've ever seen...I really don't think there will be any bad ones. Maybe an average one or a "okay" one...but thuthfully not a bad one. I think that with the quality ICE is capable of, we are a seriosu threat in 1997 come the year end awards next year. IIWF, a league Mike and I are both in, won in 1996. I honestly think we can be a threat to be the winner in the awards. ICE has been around just as long, just undergone a lot of changes. So, take the time to read the cards, reports, ppvs,etc. More often than not, you'll enjoy it. I think we can seriosuly be a league to watch for, both on RSPWF and on the 'Net. ICE will meltdown the rest! Independent Championship E-wrestling Co Fed Hed, Zach Hyatt * http://members.aol.com/[detail withheld]/icehome.html*
