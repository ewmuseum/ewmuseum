ICE to Return!!

7 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ICE set to return! ICE is set to return to the E-wrestling world in the very near future. We are now looking for wrestlers to fill our ranks. Both old and new members are welcome. Just read through the intro below and then fill out and return the creation sheet. Mike ~~~~~~~~~~ ICE Introduction and rules ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Hello and welcome to ICE. As a booked e-fed we will be running cards twice weekly, with the possibility of extra "house shows" during any given week. As a booked fed, your flashes, interviews and angle ideas will help you on your way. Without any of these, your wrestlers aren't going to be doing well here in ICE and will be jobbed before being thrown out of the league. Therefore, when creating your wrestler, make sure that he is going to be someone that you will be able to roleplay well and take places. Our current cards are Monday Night Carnage and Friday Night Mayhem. Both of these cards will be full play by play and include most types of matches (By this I mean, Street Fight, Falls Count Anywhere, Cage etc.) Bigger matches such as Survivor Series Matches and WarGames will be kept for Pay Per Views. Also on our schedule is a twice weekly wrap up show known as ICE Cold. This show includes interviews from wrestlers, recaping of the past card and looking ahead to what is coming up in ICE. Rankings and other items will appear as needed. The shows will come out on Wednesday's and Sunday's after the card. The most important part of this fed is going to be the roleplaying of it's members. Flashes are going to be sent out by me once per day (if there are enough) and NOT mass mailed to the whole federation. On Tuesdays and Saturday's, some may be held over to be made a part of ICE Cold. You may of course write interviews to be made a part of one of the cards. This is fine, as long as they are recieved by me the day before the card goes out. Ie. Sunday and Thursday. As this is a booked fed, many people will think that it is the quantity of flashes that they write that will win them matches. That's not the case. Someone who writes one or two good length flashes, where his character develops or increases the amount of heat in an angle is going to do better than someone who sends in 10 2 line repetitive flashes. Like : I'm going to kick wrestler B's butt and if wrestler C get's in the way I'll get you as well. For matches, I highly encourage you to send in any strategies. If you want your guy to beat on his opponent on the outside for most of the match and it doesn't happen because you didn't tell me, don't blame me. If you want to jump a guy on his way to the ring, let me know. Sneek attacks are allowed, but let's try and keep them to a minimum. There are other way's of building heat. As and when ICE get's it's own web page, I'll let you know. Belts. We have titles here as well! We currently sanction two singles titles and one tag team title. The singels titles are:- ICE World Title and ICE Mayhem Title. And for Tag Teams we have the ICE World Tag Team Titles. All of these MUST be defended at the PPV each month, but may be defended as and when on other cards. Stables and Alliances are welcomed, but again, you've got to tell me otherwise I won't know to write them into a card. We wouldn't want your mystery stable member running into make the save when you jump someone else now would we! ? If there is anything else, just give me a shout. And here's a creation sheet! ICE Character Creation Sheet ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Below is an application form to join ICE. We are a booked fed, so your roleplaying has a huge effect on your characters performance. So, don't design an excellent character, only to find out that you are unable to roleplay him. Think about where you what your guy to go as you fill out this form. Singles Information ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Your Name:- Your EMail Address:- Wrestlers Name:- Wrestlers Weight:- Wrestlers Height:- Hometown:- Entrance Music:- Ring Entrance:- Ring Attire:- Background (what has he done in the past? How did he get where he is today? You get the idea):- Current Scetch (Where is he today?):- Personality:- Gimmick:- Alignment (Face, Neutral, Heel):- Wrestlers Style (brawler, high flyer, technician, etc.):- How does he wrestle (moves onto tables, lot's of high risk moves, slow and methodical etc.):- 5 Favourite Moves:- Finishing Move:- Description of Finisher:- Special Combo (optional) (1-2-3 Kid's kick-kick-Spinning Kick, Muta's Handspring Elbow):- Divide 100 points amongst:- Strength:- Stamina:- Agility:- Cheating:- Fan Support (either "heel" or "face" support ie. Bob Holly is a face but with low fan support. Shawn Michaels is a face with a huge fan support. How much the fans get behind you and encourage):- Speed:- Experience (how much actual ring experience and knowledge he can call on):- Technical Ability:- Aerial Ability:- Managers Name (if any):- Description of Manager:- Will he interfere on behalf of his man:- Introductory Flash/Interview:- Tag Team Information ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ (must also fill out singles sheet for each wrestler) ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Tag Team Name:- Tag Team Partner:- Tag Team Finisher:- Description of Finisher:- Tag Team Description (which man starts off in the ring, style of team, will they fight on the outside, use tables etc.):- 5 Favourite Tag Team Moves (double team moves etc.):- Any Tag Team History:- Introductory Tag Team Flash:- Turnpike evaluation.
