ICE "Deadly" Dylan Vaughn Interview

13 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<"Deadly Dylan Vaughn and his manager Randall Chase are in the lockerroom after the tournament finals against Yahoo..... Vaughn seems a bit tired but is grinning like the cat that ate the canary......> DDV: That's right ICE! You're looking at the new heavyweight champion! Despite all of the stuff Raekwon tried to pull, I still beat his sorry ass... And Yahoo..... I knew once I clinched the STF on you, the title was mine....... Even though the moronic fans were chanting your name, I knew I held your fate in my hands.... There was no holding back "The Deadly One".... You know.... I've been all over the world.... Fought some of the greatest men in wrestling today...... But I never had a single night tournament quite like this...... And winning it only proves how great I am... Whether the fans like me.... Whether the other ICE superstars like me.... I don't care....... It's about winning....... And you're looking at the ICE Heavyweight Champion, baby! RC: That's right..... I knew as soon as I saw this kid that he was going to be great.... That's why I signed him on and brought him here to the ICE..... He's a true talent, and the ICE is lucky to have him as it's first champion! This guy is the leader of the new generation, and he will mold this sport_and_ this federation inot the same greatness that he represents.... Great job, Dylan! <The screen goes black as the two of them continue their celebration>
