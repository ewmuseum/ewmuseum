EAR is unlacing his boots, grimacing with pain after being sent through the table. A wound, caused by splintered wood seeps through the bandage temporarily placed on it. Percival sits in a corner, talking on a cell phone. An arena worker walks in and whispers secretively to him. Percival stands and walks to EAR.) EAP: Alex, an important business decision has arose. I need to go and settle it. Make sure you check into the hospital about that wound. I don't want it infected. (EAR blankly stares, unlacing the last of his huge boots. He slowly stands and begins removing his tights, then puts his jeans. On his right leg is a mammoth purple bruise. He sits, again, grimacing, then puts on his shirt.) EAP: Alex? Here me? EAR: Uh...oh yeah. Infection. EAP: OK...gotta run. (Percival grabs his briefcase and a few papers, then leaves, escorted by the worker. The locker room door closes. EAR snaps into focus and begins staring at the camera. We can see from his eyes, he's not entirely there.) EAR: Well, again Booyah I destroyed you. One of us left the ring walking and the other left it Jake Harris style...on a stretcher. No matter what you say Booyah, you got a serving of Swansea Hardcore. The blood splattered all over your face is testimony to that. But I should have beat you Booyah. I should have. The reason you won was because I beat myself. All that posing. It was a sign of weakness Booyah. I made a mistake that belied my title. I shown you mercy. I let you get to your feet after your neck was crushed. I AM MY OWN WORST ENEMY! Not any more Booyah. I suppose I should thank you. When my shoulders were on the canvas, I was thinking. I've been too soft on the UIWF. They've all had it easy. NO MORE! From now on, people will be tasting Swansea Hardcore on a regular basis! (EAR grabs a bottle of water from off the side and takes a long swig, draining half the contents.) EAR: But Booyah, you're not #1 in my hit-list anymore. No, that distinction now belongs to Big Butch Thornton. I don't know what the hell you're playing at Thornton, but you're in some real pretty shit now. Whilst I commend your bravery, I can only laugh at your stupidity. I really didn't care that you eliminated me from that battle royal in Korea, because I didn't want to be there, competing for some stupid piece of tin. But Thornton, when you smashed me through that table, you arose an anger in me I haven't felt in a long time. What makes this better, is that you attacked Booyah too. And what happens? Casey Evans gets off his slithe little ass and make a match that truly is up my alley. A 3 man dance with us three in it. A lot of scores to be settled here. A lot of scores according to my agenda. (EAR touches the wound on his head, rupturing the already weakened bandages structure. Blood pours freely down his face.) EAR: A lot of blood will be spilled. Bad blood. Blood that shouldn't be. You'll here from me, undoubtedly during the week, but for now, I must congratulate you. On what, I hear you all cry? Thornton, Booyah. Congratulations. You just volunteered to be FLATLINED! ( Fade to black. The face of EAR suddenly occupies the screen. ) EAR: Due to the fact after I was done my initial interview and then later I decided you all needed to hear more from myself I've decided to take the liberty of using something Pedro Kozaro turned me on to ... video editing. ( Fade to black. The words " A DAY LATER " in big, white, letters come across the screen. The words scroll off the screen and agian blackness. The scene opens in the Flatlinerz UWA locker room. LLV and Bucks are sipping on champagne, giggling at the stupid antics of Five-0. EAP is also sipping Champagne, for he has heard that his man is the #1 contender to the UWA Title. ) LLV: Did you see Big Bong? The bastard was sweating like a pig! BB: (Doing an impression) Huff...huff...need more...ganja...URGH! LLV: And what about Daniels? Did you see him? All piss and vinegar. EAP: Gentlemen, I must congratulate you both on a job well done. This is another step towards our total domination over the alliance. BB: What alliance? (The door opens, suddenly and EAR steps through, a twisted look on his face. The wound on his head is now septic, and a yellow glob of pus oozes through the cotton bandage.) LLV: Well...I'm gonna get a shower. BB: And I'm gonna get laid. (LLV stares at him.) BB: I mean...Heh Heh...I'm gonna get laid out, whilst I'm in the bath. LLV: Whatever. (The Vegas Express grab towels off the side and leave the locker room to head to the showers.) EAR: Did you hear the news, Maxwell? Adair really doesn't like you. Out of all the wrestlers in the UWA and even the UIWF, he chose me specifically to wrestle you. To be honest, why he did, or what the thing going on between you and him are about, but let me tell you this. You've had it made for you in the UWA. You've just strutted along into it, bumbling your way to titles, screaming that they are yours. To be honest, I think that the UWA has a hell of a lot of ham n eggers in it. Why else would you win the title Maxwell? Despite what you so called "Big Leaguers" think, in my honest opinion, the UIWF has a lot more going for it. And I'm one of them. (EAR rises out of his seat and straightens his shirt.) If you look back on that Royal Rumble in North Korea, it was all UIWF. The Tyrant, who may I add got his ass kicked courtesy of me, underlined the UIWF's supremacy. How many guys did he eliminate again? Ah, who cares, he just proves my point. Maxwell, you are little more than a jumped up (Bleep!) wit who won that title by a campaign of cheapshots and catfighting. With that belt, no doubt you are the head guy in the UWA. But Maxwell, NOBODY in the UWA can even compare with me. You can't even begin to imagine what you're in for. (EAR walks out of the lockers, and the scene changes to the car park. The last few spectators are getting into their cars. The Floodlights bathe the car park in bright light. EAR walks over to his Caddy and opens the door.) EAR: Maxwell, could I ask you a favor? Could you polish that belt up for me? Not too bright, but bright enough for me to see my face in. Ah, what does it matter? When I get that belt, I'll just be driving it into your face leaving a "permanent" reminder of what you just lost! That's another damn thing that annoys me about you Maxwell. "I'm the permanent this" "I'm the permanent that!" Well, let me tell you this sucker, on Saturday when we clash, I'll permanently put your ass on ice. (EAR gets into the car, and "My World" by Guns N Roses starts playing. The Caddy starts up and EAR speeds off into the evening darkness. The camera focuses in on a bar not too far from the arena. The scene switches to the inside of the bar. It is fairly packed with mostly guys and a few ladies. There are three bartenders working, two which are female. The camera spots Jack Daniels sitting in a corner in the back of the bar so no one can recognize him. Electra is sitting beside him.) JD: What the hell do you have to do around here to get some service? Damn Canadians don’t have a clue what they’re doing. Hey, can I get some drinks here or what? (Daniels is rather annoyed with the service of the bar as he continues to yell at the employees. Electra sits patiently beside him.) Well, you damn cameramen never fail, do you (Talking to the camera)? It seems as if I can’t go anywhere without you damn cameramen all up in my face. So Turken, how did you like the little beating I laid down on you? I’ll admit, you gave me one helluva match, but like I’ve said before, it’s the end result that counts. Now, I don’t want to hear your little whining and complaining about how you had me beat, and how you should have MY belt. Save it. The key word is you “HAD” me beat. The fact is that my hand was raised in that air victoriously, and the belt is still strapped around my waist. Is it my fault that you’re in deep water with Vile Vince Viper? Electra, what do you think? (Daniels looks over at Electra waiting for her answer.) Electra: It’s certainly not your problem Jack. But, Duke has a bigger problem now, doesn’t he honey? Hell, I already showed you one reason why, now didn’t I? But, I’m not done with you, so watch your back cuz you never know when the LN or myself will strike next. Waitress: I’m sorry sir but we don’t have any Jack Daniel’s. (Daniels looks back up at her with another annoyed look on his face. He pulls out some money and hands it to the waitress.) JD: Listen here honey, go down the street and pick me up a bottle then. And when you get back, bring my woman here a Malibu Baby. (The waitress is hesitant to take the money. She looks at Daniels rather awkwardly. She takes the money and heads out of the bar.) JD: Now, if my memory serves me correctly, it seems as if that bastard Nick Wright is my next victim. Boy, this one’s gonna be a tough one (Jack says sarcastically). Listen here Wright, call and reserve a hospital bed now to save you some time. I don’t plan on just pinning you for the 1..2..3. I wanna make sure this little misunderstanding doesn’t happen again. And how am I gonna do that you ask? I’ll just have to put you out for a looooong time. (Suddenly, some guys in the bar begin to look over at Electra. Some of them begin to make some rude comments, and begin to whistle in her direction. Daniels sees this and stands up. He looks over at the guys who are doing this.) Is there some kind of problem here fellas? There better not be. (Four guys look at each other before answering Daniels. They all draw a grin on their faces.) Man: What are you gonna do about it? Why don’t you just let her go cuz she deserves a whole lot better. JD: That’s exactly why she’s over here with me and not with your yella carcasses. (The men move in slowly toward Daniels. They surround him. Daniels picks up Electra and puts her aside in a safe place. Daniels looks at all four of them. The first one charges him only for Daniels to catch him, pick him up and slam him through the wooden table. The second and third guys charge Daniels, who meets them with a double closeline. He picks up a chair and smashes it over the back of one of them. He takes the other guy and simply tosses him aside. The last guy is still standing there. He foolishly approaches Daniels with a right hand. Daniels dodges the punch and gives him a few counter punches. He picks him up and places him on top of another wooden table. Daniels gets on the table as well, picks him up and delivers a Sour Mash that just splits the table into half. Daniels gets up and looks at the damage. He looks around the bar at everyone else, who begin to look away and back away from him. Daniels makes his way over to Electra and helps her up.) JD: Let’s get outta this dump. Wright, this is a little preview of what awaits for you, and a reminder for you Turken. Prepare to…. TASTE THE BITTERNESS OF THE SOUR MASH!!!! (Daniels and Electra both begin to make their way out of the bar. Daniels kicks aside some of the wood from the broken chairs and tables as they head out. And that's where I am right now. I am the best. I am the champion. I'm holding something over my shoulder that very few people get to hold. And it's mine and mine only. But right now I am happy with what I have. You see, when I sign a contract with a fed, I sign in the hopes of becoming one of the best they have to offer. A champ, a hero, a star, a legend. I have already done most of those things. I intend to finish off the list soon as well. But now I am already ready to prepare for my first title shot. The one that will determine how good a champ I am. The first defense of a title is always the most important becuase it sets straight how good a champ the wrestler is. So as soon as pres Evans gets me a defense, I'll be on it. I want to keep this title for a very long time. I want to have a very long reign. I want to be the best dammit. And soon, everyone for sure will look upon me as the best. The best Middleweight champ in UIWF history. Maybe even the best wrestler in UIWF history. While we're on the topic of titles, let's talk about the UWA TV title tourney. Now I have an upcoming match with Yoshi Ego. Now he's going around saying how I should be the most prepared I ever was for this match. He was saying that I shouldn't even bother worrying about the Middleweight title. Well, I did, and look what happened Yoshi. You know, _I_ shouldn't be in the best shape of my life, _you_ should. I already am in my prime, and I am ready to beat you and move one step closer to winning that TV title. Oh yeah, I noticed Diablo was in it too. Maybe I'll get another shot at him. Heh heh. But anyways, Yoshi, you are a big roadblock on my way not only of UIWF supremecy, but of UWA as well. And roadblocks suck. I'm going to beat you like you've never been beaten, and it won't be much fun for you. See you in the ring, punks.
