UIWF Trash Talkin’, 30 June 1998

It is is the early afternoon, about 3:00 PM. People are outside, walking, talking, doing stuff. Cars drive by. A normal Bronx day goes on. The camera then cuts to Yankee stadium, outside of it. A handfull of vendors are out, selling various
Yankee merchandise, such as shirts, balls, etc. The ticket booths are open, selling tickets for the game that night. There are some short ques, waiting to get tickets. The camera man walks through the gates, flashing his pass as he goes.]
[The camera man is now inside the stadium. It is somewhat deserted, only janitors are there. The carts are closed up, the food stands closed for the time being. The camera man walks down to field level.
The field is empty except for the groundscrew. The scoreboard is off. One of the screens has a Yankee logo. A handful of Yankees are probably just arriving outside. The camera man walks down the stairs by the
Yankee's dugout. He sees a man standing at the ends of the seats, leaning against the dugout. He is wearing a white Yankees World Series shirt from two years back, a pair of Adidas mesh shorts, and his usual sneakers. His expression seems somewhat concentrated, yet relaxed.
His hair is brushed straight again. He is Supersonic, who was somehow able to get into Yankee Stadium early.]
Supersonic:Yes, I am a huge Yankees fan. I have been my whole life. I grew up in the New York area. I remember my father taking me to my first Yankee game. When I made it big in wrestling, however, I moved to LA. Yet I still come down here, to see the Bronx Bombers play. I actually came from the New Jersy area, but still. I grew up watching two things the most as a child. Baseball and wrestling. I dreamed to do either. And my dream has come true. I am a pro wrestler. Plus I get pretty damn good seats to Yankees games. And not only that, I am friends with a lot of the Yankees. I grew up worshipping guys like
Don Mattingly, wanting to be just like him. But at the same time I was watching Hulk Hogan and the Ultimate Warrior. Yes, I became a wrestler. And a damn good one at that. But of course I always come back to check my roots. I visit my parents, see my family, all that stuff. I'm a family man. But put me in that ring, and I'm no longer a nice guy. My sheer wrestling talent, along with my intensity, determination, and drive for greatness makes me almost unstoppable.
And like the Yankees, I am on a roll. 3-0 in UIWF, with a title shot in line. Not just any person can have a streak like that.
Only someone like me. And at Summer Shutdown, I intend to make my greatness even greater. I fight Diablo.
Diablo, I am counting the days, the minutes, the seconds until our match. I am prepared. I am psyched. You have something
I want. Something I need. That is a gold belt. I will not rest until I have that title. Diablo, you may be able to bust out of chains, but I really don't think that's gonna help you this coming
July 2. You see, I am on a roll, at my peek. I am sizzling, not fizzling. And soon I will achieve a new status. Not of challenger, but of champion. Diablo, play all the mind games you want. I see right through them. I know that you are attempting to outpsyche me, to scare me. Well, I'm not scared very easily. Diablo thrives on intimidation. He plays the mind games on his opponents. He tries to scare them, so they will not want to beat him. He wants them to believe that they are stepping into Hell. He makes his opponents think they will lose. Well, that last one makes sense though. But still, Diablo, as you know, is playing those games with me.
He's trying to get inside of my head. The chains, the mirrors, the laughing, it's just not working on me. I know better. My ring smarts and skills are too good to fall for some guy breaking some plastic chains. Oh, in case you haven't noticed, Diablo, I still won that match. I just thought I'd let you know. You can go around, acting all Satanic, burning stuff and starting fires, but if you can't pull it off in the ring, you are nothing.
However, I know you are good, because you have the title. But not for much longer. Diablo, you better take out that camera and take pictures of that belt. You better sleep with it at night, hold it, and keep it nice. I want your last days as a champion to be enjoyable to you. But keep that belt in nice shape. I want it to be in good condition. But you know, I'm not worried about that last thing. All I care about Diablo is beating you. I am constantly thinking about it, worrying about it, concentrating on it. I want to beat you. I want to prove a point. I want to prove that I am not bluffing when I call myself the best thing that has ever hit the
UIWF. With that title, I will become one of the best here. According to the rankings, I AM the third best in the league, but that title will definatly move me up. Diablo, I've been working hard for this one. Summer Shutdown has been marked on my calender.
It's been circled with a ton of arrows pointing it out. And this Thursday, Diablo, get ready for one of the longest nights of your life. [Tino Martinez walks out of the dugout, not yet in uniform, but in his street clothes. He stands in front.]
Supersonic:Hey Tino! Tino:Supersonic! What's up? Supersonic:Not much, I'm just getting ready for my upcoming match. Tino:Oh, the Diablo one? Supersonic:Yeah, that one. Want tickets? I have a ton. I have like 100 of them. I've already given them to my family, but I have a lot of extras. Want to bring the team?
Tino:That would be great. Hey, you want field passes? Supersonic:Sounds good to me. Tino:Follow me.
