Summer Shutdown, Part 2

4 July 1998

( Heather and R.A.D. finish shaking the ring posts and walking around the ring. The two then begin to head up the entrance/exit ramp. ) BS: Apparently their satisfied. RR: Congratulations, you've just one RAT's approval! ( The camera switches from R.A.D. and Heather who are slowly leaving to Barry Nelson who is standing in the ring, holding cue cards and a microphone. ) BN: The following match is a stretcher match, the only way to win is by placing your opponent on a stretcher for a ten count. Entering first at a height of 6'5' and a weight of 265 pounds, he hails from Chicago, Illinois...the UWA's very own...CRUSHER STEVENSOOONNNNNN!!!!! RR: Oh well I guess they all can't. CE: All can't wh... ( Evans is cut off by "Man In The Box" by Alice and Chains as it blasts over the PA to a decent face pop. As soon as it starts Crusher Stevenson walks out from the backstage area in typical getup, looking very intense and directly down the aisle where Harris harasses R.A.D. who is simply trying to leave. Over his shoulder is slung a rather old stretcher with several leather straps on the corners and quite a few bloodstains. R.A.D. sidesteps then grabbing her hips he pulls Heather out of the way of a Stretcher shot that Stevenson delivers to Harris's head. Harris in an almost Monty python like manner spins around in a circle on one foot then does a funny walk down the aisle towards the ring, as if trying to walk off the pain from the shot. ) RR: Oh well it can't be that bad watching Harris get the Sh ... CE: Don't even start. ( Farewell to Cheyenne by Ennio Morricone begins to play over the loud speakers. ) BN: and his opponent weighing in at 175 pounds ( Harris now inside the ring holding his head, pushes himself up and grabs the microphone from Nelson shoving him to the outside of the ring. ) Jake Harris: I know all my UIWF fans are thinking that...I sold out. Nothing could be further from the truth...you see that...uh...the UWA still has me under contract...yeah that's it. The UWA sent me down here to learn a few holds...uh...now they want me back. I have to say my time in the UIWF has been fun, and I will miss all of you. Even you Crusher...man did we have thousands of classic confrontations or what? I need to get running...RAD and Heather are here to take me back to the UWA. You have a lot of talent kid and...who knows one day even you might be able to compete in the UWA. Please except this forfeit victory in manner in which it is offered. <Sees RAD leaving> No...wait... ( Before given the chance to forfeit to a referee, Stevenson reaches into the ring and pulls Harris legs out from under him. Harris shrieks in pain as Stevenson yanks him out of the ring. ) JJ: Ouch that looked almost as if Stevenson could have yanked Harris's legs out of joint! RR: It would be a real shame to the wrestling world if Harris lost all ability to walk. ( Jake ignores Stevenson trying to follow R.A.D. only to be nailed in the back of the head by Stevenson. Harris looks up and reaches out to the back of R.A.D. screaming at him to "Take me with you!" Only to be kicked in the back of the head by Crusher... ) BS: Stevenson with Harris on the outside of the ring, Harris looking over to the UWA superstars from the last match who are still waiting around not knowing quite what is going on. Stevenson on the other hand has only one thing on his mind as whips Harris HARD into the steel steps. Harris grabbing his back in pain while walking around in circles on his knees. What a joke! Well the circles stop as Stevenson stomps down his right foot into Harris's head. ( Stevenson continues to nail Harris with thrust kick after thrust kick to the head, Harris almost as if by no choice of his own, continues to move back up after each kick. Eventually Stevenson grows tired so in an effort to buy some time he starts to choke Harris out by driving his boot into Harris's throat. ) RR: Man the referee is actually giving Stevenson a five count what's the matter with him doesn't he realize the only way to win is to put your opponent on the stretcher? I think we should invest into some referee's who know what the hell is going on. CE: I'll look into that. RR: WHAT? The referee is forcing Crusher to break the hold? Is the referee out of his mind, let him rough Harris up a little more! JJ: Your really enjoying this aren't you? RR: Let's just say I wouldn't cry if Crusher messed up Harris's vocal cords and he wasn't able to speak! The only thing that has kept me from challenging the obnoxious jobber is the fact that he used to speak so rarely, recently though he is really getting under my skin. Don't get me wrong, I'd be just as happy to see Crusher on the end of some career ending injuries I just don't think Harris is going to be the one to do them. JJ: Crusher now arguing with the referee, Harris is slowly getting up…and reaching down into his multi-color trunks he has, a, a, what is that? Oh, an international object. Stevenson seems not to notice Harris as he quarrels with the referee. Harris from behind and a…BLATANT LOW BLOW! Common ref, disqualify him. BS: It would seem that Stevenson has convinced the referee about the no dq rule. CE: Well it just goes to show you, it isn't wise to get in the referee's face. ( As Stevenson stands there holding his valuables in pain, Harris falls backwards onto the steal steps and after about a moment springs himself off them, forwards clipping Stevenson's leg. Stevenson who had one hand of the bottom ring rope to stand himself upright spins around in a cartwheel like manner landing on his feet. Harris spins around, however, and nails him twice as hard in the lower area. ) BS: Harris with another low blow this time he brings Crusher down to his knees. So Rex, I suppose you have to be happy that could be considered a career ending injury? RR: I suppose so if your some sort of hot to trot pretty boy, but Stevenson is not pretty and it could be questioned whether or not he was a boy. If it was part of his in ring work I suppose it could, the fact remains that Stevenson uses it as little inside the ring as he does outside. JJ: Harris now jumping up on the steel stair and spring boarding off them to the ring apron. Now, charging along he dives off with a DROP KICK OFF THE APRON!HE JUST NAILED STEVENSON SQUARE IN THE HEAD! BS: Things looking up for Harris he's come a long way since he joined the UIWF a month ago, the old Harris would have tripped missed Stevenson completely and rammed headfirst into the guardrail behind him. ( Harris chuckles upon seeing Crusher bleeding heavily from the nose. In a cocky manner he climbs back onto the ring apron. ) JJ: Harris laughing on the apron, positioning himself above Stevenson and now jumping up onto the second rope, he goes to springboard but wait he just missed the second rope completely now his legs are hooked between the second and third ropes. It seemed as if Harris was going for an asai moonsault, definitely not a move an accident-prone idiot like him should try. RR: You want prone, Stevenson is getting to his feet and he seems a little mad, a tad pissed. Harris is going no where and Stevenson well Stevenson is red in the face. JJ: That's because of the blood gushing out of his nose! RR: Oh. ( Stevenson grabs Harris by the head and throws him downwards, the ropes swing Harris in such a manner that he nails the back of his head on the ring apron. As Harris comes back Stevenson nails him with an incredibly hard right hand, Harris flies back hitting his head again only to swing back towards Stevenson as a result of his legs being hooked. Stevenson continues to take advantage of Harris condition unloading with lefts and rights. ) BS: Harris really taking a beating at the hands of Stevenson he is really getting bruised and cut up. CE: I'm personally not surprised after what he did on terror. ( Stevenson stops to pick up the stretcher, with a windup he swings it nailing Harris hard with such force that it frees Harris who slowly slumps down into the center of the ring. Stevenson tosses the stretcher into the middle of the ring hitting Harris on the foot. The shock of the stretcher hitting his foot slightly wakes up Harris who begins to crawl away from the ring as Crusher enters it. Stevenson grabs Harris by the foot then pulls him up by the hair the crowd starts to chant "Crush her". ) JJ: Crusher lifting Harris up in the air this doesn't look good for Cry Baby. Stevenson holding the much lighter Harris up in the air as if he was nothing, now a huge powerbomb! BS: Yeah and what a powerbomb, right onto that stretcher Harris landed on his head, he has to be unconscious and the referee making a count one, two, three, four wait five, no, Stevenson just broke the count by lifting Harris up. He doesn't want to end it yet, I, I think he's ... he's going for the crusher breaker *** Torture Rack***! RR: Does every guy who does that move way over the hill? JJ: Crusher going for his version of the torture rack wait, Harris with a gouge to the eyes! I thought he was unconscious he must have been playing possum. RR: Actually he still does look unconscious, those hard punches earlier look like they bruised his eyes up so badly that well they look to be closed not that it isn't an improvement. JJ: Harris with a lariat, Stevenson no sells it and Harris is holding his arm in pain. Harris charges into the ropes, comes back ducks a clothesline attempt by Stevenson, rams into the other ropes rebounds and Stevenson spins around to be met by a flying elbow to the nose, yep that started to get Stevenson bleeding again. ( Stevenson staggers, Harris feeling pretty good about himself runs into the ropes. Stevenson sees him going for it and then charges him in a football tackle like manner Harris sidesteps it and Stevenson rams his shoulder right into the ropes. ) JJ: Stevenson right into the ropes, he could have dislodged his shoulder blade that way. CE: Really? JJ: Well no not really, but Harris taking the opportunity to drive a knee into his arm slung across those steel ropes. Harris now with lightning speed to the outside of the ring, he grabs Stevenson by the hair and pulls him out. Harris, now whips him into the guardrail with such force that Stevenson went over. Stevenson now in the crowd where various fans are patting him on the back. RR: What idiots if they really want to help why not just kick the crap out of Harris. BS: Harris now grabbing the case labeled worldly possessions and climbing up onto the guardrail. Stevenson trying to slowly get to his feet, but Harris dives off nailing Stevenson with his suitcase. That idiot, the suitcase just bounced off nailing him in the head now Harris and Stevenson are both on the floor and all the clothing and various junk have fallen out. Harris is the first to get up; he grabs what looks to be a comic book that came out of his case. Yeah, some "Handsome" Henry dark horse comic, he winds it up and he just brought it down on Stevenson's head all he succeeded in doing was destroy his comic! RR: Probably wasn't worth much. BS: Harris now grabbing one of his tacky tourist shirt and is choking out Stevenson! Wait, a fan just kicked Harris in the back of the head Harris letting go and spinning around looking at the audience. ( Harris begins to give a speech about how he didn't sell out so they shouldn't hate him, only to realize that R.A.D. is gone. He begins to look around with tears streaming down his bruised and beaten face not realizing that Stevenson is getting up. ) JJ: Harris spins around on one foot, Stevenson up he looks pissed and now Stevenson charging Harris. Harris jumps and going for a huracanrana but Stevenson counters it with a tiger driver. DAMN…you could hear the thud as Harris's head hit the concrete! The referee telling Stevenson to take Harris back to the stretcher, yeah, it would probably be best if this match ended soon. RR: It should have ended a lo ... JJ: Thank you Rex. Stevenson obliging, lifting Harris over his shoulder and wait he just stopped, oh, he just picked up Harris's suitcase and threw the contents to the audience members around him. Now Stevenson going over the guardrail and tossing Harris into the ring, Stevenson following him in and now what is he doing hasn't he punished Harris enough? Stevenson isn't going for the stretcher but is once again lifting Harris up for the Crusher Breaker and yes, Stevenson has it locked on! Harris is unconscious this...this just isn't right. DING DING DING BN: The referee has found that Jake Harris cannot continue, therefore is awarding this match to CRUSHER STEVENSOONNN!!!!! ( The fans give a huge pop, as Stevenson drops Harris into a backbreaker right onto the stretcher. He then uses the straps to tie down Harris's arms and legs. ) BS: Talk about adding insult to injury Stevenson had the match won, he was right next to the stretcher yet he decided to get the referee to label him unfit to continue that's low. RR: Don't forget he just gave Harris's worldly possessions to the crowd, and cost Harris his chance to rejoin the UWA! HA! There may be some hope for Crusher yet. ( Stevenson lifts the stretcher over his head and tosses it to the concrete floor outside the ring. Paramedics rush down and check on Harris, with a concerned look on there faces they start to move him to the back. Stevenson raises his arms and screams something about removing the trash as "The Man In The Box" by Alice and Chains blasts over the P.A. Harris tries to get off the stretcher as it is wheeled off only to notice that he is strapped down. As the wheel him to the backstage area his bloodied head swings about frantically as he screams "R.A.D. don't leave me in this hell hole! I'm fragile...they don't respect real wrestling...I can't take much more of this abuse!" Finally his tormented screams vanish as his head is pushed behind the curtain of the arena. ) CE: Jake Harris certainly is a strange young fellow who is in need of help. RR: Only the best for the UIWF, right Evans? CE: Err ... yeah Rex. Anyways Fans we gotta take a short break before we return with Evil Alex Roberts and Blistering Bobby Booyah!
