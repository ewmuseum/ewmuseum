Summer Shutdown, Part 3

4 July 1998

RR: And BBB's heat will be finally quenched by Swansea Hardcore. JJ: Unbiased comments as always from Rex...I think this should be a close match...but my heart's with Booyah. After that first victory, he's got something to prove. BS: It's the air attack of BBB against the ground attack of EAR, whomever controls the pace should take the win easily. CE: Well, let's go down to Barry Nelson for the introductions... ( "Superman's Dead" by Our Lady Peace plays to a massive face pop. ) BN: The following contest is set for one fall...Now coming down the aisle hailing from Acapulco, Mexico weighing in at 220 lbs. and standing at 6'1" tall "BLISTERING" BOBBYYY BOOOYAHHHHHH!!! [Face pop as BBB Makes his way down to the ring, BBB slaps the hands that are extended over the guard rail, BBB hops up on the apron and springboards in to the ring.] JJ: BBB looks focused for this match, definitely the crowd favorite... ( "Superman's Dead" fades out, along with the lights... ) ( The lights go down, into pitch black for a few moments. Blue lights come back on, bathing the arena in an eerie blue aura. ) BN: And his opponent, to be accompanied to the ring by Evil Al Percival, representing the Flatlinerz, from Swansea, Wales, weighing in at 320lbs, EVIL ALEX ROBERTS! ( A distortion guitar blares a few out of tune notes then a woman whispers "Flatlinerz.", then the weird symphony of "CLAW!" and "Know your enemy" blares out over the PA, this time backtracked by a Flatlining sound. Before EAR and EAP emerge, fireworks like bombs explode, blinding the fans. EAR walks out, looking straight ahead. Percival is flanking him and giving him a few last words of advice. Roberts steps over the ropes and rips of his "FLATLINERZ" T-shirt. ) BS: And now Evil Al enters the ring, eyeing up BBB, mindful of what happened last time. CE: BBB takes quick initiative, nailing Roberts with a dropkick! And as Roberts gets up, another! Roberts is stunned, and the crowd loves it! [Face pop as EAR gets up, enraged...] RR: C'mon Al, nail him! Crowd: BOO-YAH!! BOO-YAH!! BOO-YAH!! BOO-YAH!! BOO-YAH!! ( Bobby stops for a second to play to the crowd, banging the turnbuckle in rhythm...and EAR sees this and hits BBB with a lariat to the back of the head! ) BS: BBB caught unaware, now EAR's pounding on him with those _massive_ forearms...now he's standing his 320 on BBB's throat! ( EAR points right at Jim Jones while Booyah struggles for breath. ) JJ: What? Me? What did I do? Huh? ( EAR breaks the choke at four and nine-tenths, only to reapply the choke with another stomp across the neck... ) Ref: 1---2---3---4---BREAK!!! ( EAR breaks the choke and drops a knee right to Booyah's skull. ) CE: Booyah's struggling early, he can't outfight Roberts close in...he's gonna have to establish some distance between himself and Roberts if he has any chance at this. RR: EAR now applying a front chancery...and as long as he's working that neck, he's got the word "Eviliser" going through the back of Booyah's head. BS: Booyah's gonna have to escape this hold before he can mount any kind of offense...he's working his way to his feet, but EAR's a lot to work against. JJ: Roberts nullifying BBB's efforts with a forearm smash to the back. An irish whip into the ropes...Booyah with a spinning crescent kick...EAR ducks! BBB smashes his leg into the mat, hard, on that one. ( Slight face pop, quickly silenced as BBB clutches his knee in pain. ) [EAR plays to the crowd again, this time with Percival helping from the outside, drawing loads of boos from the ringside fans.] RR: Roberts smells blood...he lifts up BBB...and...chokeslam!! He hooks the injured leg... Ref: One... Two... CE: Th...nope! ( Heel pop for the near fall. ) CE: Booyah kicks out with the good leg, just barely, while Percival lectures the referee on the number that comes after two and how to bring it from brain to mouth quickly. BS: Roberts back on the attack with a crushing knee drop to the solar plexus, BBB coughing heavily as he tries to regain his wind. ( Crowd gasp, almost in unison. ) RR: Hey Bobby! Don't forget...Eviliser...Eviliser...Eviliser!!! JJ: He can't hear you, Rex... CE: Roberts taking a little more extended break now to taunt the ringside fans, even cracking a mean joke at a chubby kid with a Cold Justice sign. Meanwhile, Booyah's getting up...or at least his coughing fit is over. He's maneuvering behind Roberts...German suplex! He bridges... Ref: One... Two... BS: and a pissed off EAR kicks out at two. RR: Now that did it...EAR whips BBB into the corner, and goes for a back body drop...Booyah with a DDT! Get up, Al! CE: Booyah with a missile dropkick, puts EAR back down, but EAR rolls out of the ring with his momentum! ( Large face pop for the change in momentum! ) Crowd: BOO-YAH!! BOO-YAH!! BOO-YAH!! ( EAR kicks the security railing, frustrated as can be. ) BS: BBB eyeing out Roberts and Percival on the outside...he's up top...PLANCHA!!! CE: Hitting both Roberts _and_ Percival! Roberts inadvertently stumbled right into Percival, and the crowd loves this! ( Even larger face pop as BBB, EAR, and EAP get up in that order. ) JJ: BBB throws Roberts back into the ring...climbs back in, grabs the arm and Irish whip...BBB going for a back bodydrop, but EAR puts on the brakes...standing head scissors, a snap-lift into the... RR: The Evil-Driver ( piledriver )! ( HEEL POP!!! ) CE: And he's gloating again! But I almost think he has time to, as very few men have survived the Evildriver. BS: Percival yelling again at the crowd...he just tore up a sign! ( Loads of boos as the camera shifts to Percival shredding a fan's "Scorch \ him, BBB" sign. Percival holds the torn sign like a trophy.) JJ: BBB with a sudden burst of energy stumbles to his feet, grabs EAR by the waist...reverse rollup!!! Ref: One... Two... CE: EAR is stunned...Three!!!! ( Face pop to the rafters as the hand falls a third time!!! ) CE: BBB has pinned EAR again, and again thanks to EAR's inattention!!! ( As referee Ron Davis lifts Booyah's hand. ) BN: And the winner of the match...BLISTERING BOBBY BOOYAH!!! ( But as "Superman's Dead" begins to play again, Roberts nails Booyah in the back of the head with a running forearm shot! ) RR: The Evil One isn't done quite yet...Percival's egging him on...ANOTHER EVILDRIVER!!! ( Heel pop!! ) JJ: EAR picks BBB up by the hair, front facelock, and an EVILISER!!! ( Standing neckbreaker ) CE: CMON DAMNIT!!! THE MATCH IS ALREADY OVER!!! ( Trash starts to fill the ring as the fans boo in unison ) JJ: Roberts just bashing that time keepers bell over the head of Bobby Booyah repeatedly. Somebody has to get in here and help! RR: Help? Why? This is great!!! JJ: You're about as sick and twisted as Roberts is. RR: Glad to hear. "Evil" Alex is reading my mind. He's going outside the ring and it looks like he has found a table!!! BS: Booyah needs to get the hell outta there because I know Alex and he could very well end his career right now. RR: Oh shut up. Nobody wants to hear the opinions of a has been wrestler. Everybody wants to hear about the demise of Bobby Booyah TONIGHT!! CE: "Evil" Alex Roberts is setting up that table inside the ring and now moving Booyah to the turnbuckle. What in the world is planning on doing? ( The crowd starts to boo very loudly ) JJ: Whatever it is, the blood covered Bobby Booyah is in some serious trouble. If anybody is listening in the back, GET THE HELL OUT HERE!! BS: It looks like Roberts is gonna supersplex or DDT Booyah through that table. But regardless of what move is applied, Mr. Booyah is gonna spending a long trip in the hospital. RR: Alex! Alex!! Put that joke through the- ( Suddenly "Enter The Sandman" by Metallica starts to play over the loud speakers ) CE: That music..... the expression on the kisser of Alex Roberts tells it all....... ( The arena literally explodes as a large figure emerges from behind the curtain at the entrance way, wearing a black Fearsome Foursome t-shirt, blue jeans, and black boots. ) CE: IT'S BIG BUTCH THORNTON!!! RR: WHAT'S HE DOING HERE?!?!?!?! JJ: He wasn't even scheduled to be here but I'm glad he is. Just look at the intensity in his eyes as he walks down the aisle!!! BS: Roberts doesn't looks so evil now. Looks a little shaken up to me! RR: Sh... shaken up? Nah... Roberts will handle him. CE: Thornton slowly approaching the ring but all of a sudden dashing to the ring. He's inside but Roberts is there to meet him with that ringbell!! *RING* *RING* JJ: That bell was just smashed on the head of Big Butch Thornton but he os barely showing the effects of it. Butch looking at Roberts with an incredible look of rage...... Butch blocks another bell shot.... kick to the gut..... BUTCH HAS HIM IN POSITION FOR JUDGEMENT DAY!!! ( Crowd pops ) CE: ROBERTS IS SENT VIA JUDGEMENT DAY THROUGH THAT TABLE!!! BIG BUTCH THORNTON MEANS BUSINESS TONIGHT!!!! RR: He has no business being here! BS: Oh yes he does. Somebody finally ended this mayhem. JJ: Bobby Booyah has managed to get himself down from the turnbuckle but isn't in good shape at all. It looks like he is trying to move towards Thornton! CE: A bloody and staggering "Blistering" Bobby Booyah, holding onto the top rope, is extending his hand to Butch! The former UWA Champion, looking down at Roberts smirking is going to shake Booyah's hand- NO HE ISN'T!!!!! THORNTON SLAM!!! THORNTON SLAM!!!!!! BS: Why did he do that?? JJ: We're about to find out. BBT has the mic. ( Butch looks down at the carnage in the ring, both Roberts and Booyah not moving ) BIG BUTCH THORNTON: I didn't come out here to shake some fruits hand with the nickname "Blistering", I came out to do what I do on a daily basis..... KICK A*BLEEP* AND TAKE NAMES!!! I could care less about Roberts or Booyah. Right now the only man I'm worried about is that Romanian sensation, Vile Vince Viper. Triple V, it's signed, sealed, and delivered. June the 11th, Saturday Slaughterhouse.... just you and me. It's just the start of many... MANY massacres you will receive. Be it UWA or UIWF, it doesn't matter one freikin' bit to me. One way or the other, you will feel the wrath of the Original A*BLEEP*kicker. I GUARANTEE IT!!!!! ( Butch drops the microphone down and exits the ring as "Enter The Sandman" starts back up again. Fade to black ) CE: Stay tuned fans, we gotta leave for a short break! ( Cut to commercial as EAP tends to EAR. EAR looks a bit groggy, as if not knowing what hit him. Booyah, suffering the effects from beatings he took from the two big men, is laying motionless in the middle of the ring. ) ( Commercials air ) Copyright Evil Pig.Inc Productions 1998 http://come.to/UIWF
