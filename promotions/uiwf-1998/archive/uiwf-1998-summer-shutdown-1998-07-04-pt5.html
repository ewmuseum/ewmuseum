(c) Written by: [name withheld] (HJT) | | vs | | Castor Troy Edited by: [name withheld] | |INTERNATIONAL TITLE ===========================| |_______________________________________________| ( Fade back from commercial. All 4 commentators sit at the broadcast table. ) CE: Welcome back fans! The action is really starting to heat up now as we have the second of 4 consecutive title matches for you right now. JJ: The Alliance International Champion, R.A.D., takes on long time Universal Wrestling Alliance wrestler Caster Troy. BN: Well you look at R.A.D. and you notice things about him right away. RR: Like he needs a shower? BN: No Rex ... I've had an opportunity to view many of the International champ's matches and here's what I found out. He's a hard worker, and an excellent technical wrestler. He uses his wrestling ability to frustrate and defeat opponents, and lastly he always keeps in shape and can wrestle all night long. RR: Whatever .. we saw Daniels beat him! BN: True ... and R.A.D. does have weaknesses. He's relatively inexperienced and he needs to control his temper. Daniels exposed his ineperience and that's how he picked up the win against R.A.D. JJ: I had a chance to scout Caster Troy a bit, although I didn't learn much. RR: Big surprise. JJ: Mostly I learned he's a stupid wrestler who doesn't focus enough on the task at hand. He's a leader but that doesn't do him much good in the wrestling ring. CE: Alright, guys, its time for the match to begin, so here comes R.A.D. vs Troy! RR: Booooooooring! DING DING DING BN: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall, and is for the UWA International Championship (cheers from crowd.) Introducing first...the challenger. Representing the Irish Republic.. RR: HAHAHAHAHAHA!! BN: Here is.....Caster....TROY!!!!!! (the crowd boos as Caster Troy heads down to the ring, without music) BS: Troy has always been what you might consider a flashy individual, but it looks like he wants to be all business tonight. JJ: Troy in the ring, and he's not warming up, not listening to the taunts of the crowd..he's just staring at the entranceway, waiting for the arrival of the champion. CE: As you all know, R.A.D. is the man he proved that Christopher Maxwell was NOT to be the permanent International Champion, but only a short time later, Maxwell captured the UWA World Title. You have to think R.A.D. wants to face Maxwell for that belt. RR: Of course he does, but Maxwell is one mean dude. I think with the addition of the world title, he will be much more focused. ( "Bad to the Bone" starts to play over the P.A. system ) BN: And now making his way down the aisle... ( Out from the back walks Ryan Solberg along with three men workers dressed in work clothes. The three workers follow Solberg to the ring. Solberg is wearing a all dark blue three piece suit and is built like Rick Rude. Ryan enters the ring and calls for the mic."Bad to the Bone" stops playing. ) RR: Solberg gonna wresle instead? Is this RAT guy a coward? CE: Patience Rex. Ryan Solberg: Hey for those who don't know me I'm the personal advisor for R.A.D. "The Extreme Machine" The greatest U.W.A. International Champion of all-time. (Crowd boos) I'm I'm here to over look the test to see if this ring is in ANY condition for my client to wrestle in. Boys get to work. And Casey Evans if this ring doesn't pass my inspectors test then my client isn't wrestling tonight. JJ: WHAT?!??!?! RR: What the hell was RAT doing earlier tonight then? BS: Looks like RAD isn't sure he wants to fight tonight. CE: That ring is in excellent condition. There's no reason for this. RR: Is it making you mad? CE: Well... RR: Then it has a good reason. HA! ( Two of the workers jump in the ring and talk with Ryan ear and then show him the check list and the results of his test. ) Ryan Solberg: Well Casey Evans your in luck because lucky this ring passed the tests but let me tell you Casey Evans I'm getting tried of the facilities my client has to wrestle in and though this place is like a dump I suggest that you get some better places lined up for the future or my client isn't going to wrestle in them. ( Ryan hands the mic back to the ring announcer and "Bad to the Bone" starts to play again. ) BS: Apparently Mr. Solberg had to double check for his client. JJ: Where'd tha other worker go? RR: Who cares? BN: Making his way down the aisle hailing from Pittsburgh, PA he weighed in at 264lbs and stands 6'1" he is the Extreme Machine and the reigning U.W.A. International Champion of the World this is R.A.D.! (R.A.D and Heavenly Heather walk slowly out from behind the curtains. They are meet by Ryan Solberg who tells R.A.D the ring is safe and then shows him the check list and results from the test. R.A.D. and Heather quickly look over them and R.A.D. pats Ryan on the shoulder and tells him good job and then R.A.D and Heather walk to the ring. Heather walks out in front wearing the R.A.D. "Extreme Machine" t-shirt, denim blue Daisy duke shorts, tennis shoes and has her hair in a pony tail. R.A.D. is wearing white, gold and black paint splattered wrestling pants with black boots, black Oakley sunglasses, black gloves similar to what Randy Savage wears and has the U.W.A. International Title draped over his right shoulder.) BS: This one's gonna be a barn burner... RR: Bob, I've seen Caster Troy wrestle before. If this is gonna be a barn burner, its an AWFULLY small barn! CE: RAD is exiting the ring. Whats up here? He's walking over to Heather.. BS: Sounds like he's discussing the sturdiness of the ring. Give it a rest, RAD, its safe! RR: RAT seems satisfied and gets back into the ring. He looks over at Troy and SPITS at him! CE: Troy walks right up to RAD and SLAPS him hard across the face! NOT A GOOD IDEA! JJ: RAD dives at him and SPEARS him to the ground. He's hitting Caster with so many rights, he's begging the champ for a left! BS: Caster rolls the International Champion off himself and tries to exit the ring, but RAD has him by the boot. He's pulling Troy back to the center of the ring, but Troy is trying to kick him away. RR: A stomp to the back of Caster's knee changes his mind about escaping...RAD puts him in an Indian Deathlock! CE: The referee is asking Troy if he wants to give it up... RR: DO IT DO IT DO IT DO IT! CE: ...but Troy doesn't want to. RR: Not this early, at least. BS: Troy trying to make it to the ring ropes but...RAD lets his legs go and drops an elbow onto his back. Caster Troy is simply being outclassed in this contest. JJ: R.A.D. gets Troy to his feet and...thumb to the eye by Troy! RAD was caught totally off guard on that one. Caster with a stiff chop to the throat of RAD. RR: Now its Troy with a rake of the eyes...bolo punch by Caster Troy...followed up by a kick to the midsection. He's got RAT on the ropes! Troy is unloading with lefts and rights and a WICKED kneelift to the groin! RAT is doubled over! CE: DDT BY CASTER TROY!!! AND HE'S GOING FOR THE PIN!! 1...2...KICKOUT!! BS: Troy with a stomp to the head of R.A.D., now gets the champ to his feet...standing dropkick by Caster Troy! That one caught RAD right on the chin, and he's almost knocked out by it. JJ: You can see the glazed over look in RAD's eye, kinda like Rex has most weekends. RR: HEY! CE: Troy gets RAD to his feet and puts him in a deep double underhook...lifts him up and..DROPS HIM INTO A DOCTOR BOMB! BS: Troy goes for a new pin.. 1...2....Th...NO!!! He just barely kicked out of that one. Now its Troy going for a rear chinlock to slow this down a little. RR: Slow it down? This kinda hold grinds it to a complete halt! JJ: Guistapo-like commentary by Rex tonight... RR: WHAT?! CE: RAD trying to maneuver his way back to his feet, but a pull of the hair brings him back down! The referee asks Troy if he pulled the hair, but Troy denies it. BS: Like he's gonna say, Oh yes, I pulled the hair. Looking back, perhaps I was in error. JJ: Troy tries to get up again, and an elbow into the sternum of Troy breaks the hold...RAD into the ropes..HIGH kneelift by Troy sends RAD back down. Now a legdrop by the challenger...RAD moves, and Troy is holding his hamstring. RR: RAT grabs Troy by the leg and gives it a jerk! He wants to pull it out of its socket! He goes for a spinning toe hold... INTO A FIGURE FOUR!! CE: But Troy is too close to the ropes and the referee is calling for the break. RAD reluctantly lets the hold go and gives Troy's legs a stomp. He drags the challenger back to the center of the ring and flips him onto his back...BOSTON CRAB!! BS: Troy has the presence of mind to try to power out...and RAD flips onto his back. Troy trying to get some feeling back into his legs. But RAD is right back up and advancing on his opponent. JJ: RAD gets Troy to his feet..and Heather is getting up on the ring apron. Caster is thrown into the ropes..and HEATHER PULLS DOWN THE TOP ROPE! CASTER TUMBLES TO THE FLOOR! But he landed right on his feet. Heather jumps back to the arena floor and rakes Troy's eyes! RR: Troy turns to face her and points at the offending valet. Well, not exactly offensive, but... CE: Inside the ring, RAD has a chair...he puts it a few feet from the ropes and..RAD into the far ropes...SPRINGBOARDS OFF THE CHAIR... SUICIDE DIVE DOWN ONTO TROY!! He took the challenger out with a nasty looking plancha! BS: The referee sends the chair out of the ring, and RAD retrieves it. WHACK!! He smashes it against the head of Caster Troy! JJ: RAD continues to pound on Troy with the chair, and the referee is telling him he'll be disqualified if he continues. Another chair shot, and it looks like RAD is done with the beating. He sends Troy flailing into the guardrail. His back hit that steel HARD! CE: RAD with a handful of hair now...throws Caster back into the ring. RAD re-enters the squared circle and rolls Troy over for the pin... 1...2...two and a half! Troy only barely kicks out. RR: RAT puts his boot on Troy and kicks him over onto his back. The International Champ runs into the ropes...SOMERSAULT LEGDROP ONTO TROY'S NECK! BS: The crowd ooo'ing and ahhh'ing over some of these moves by RAD. He likes to keep 'em guessing. JJ: Troy picked back up and onto RAD's shoulder...RAD going for an atomic drop...blocked by Troy...SHORT ARM CLOTHESLINE by the challenger! Troy with the cover... 1...Yeah right! CE: RAD had no trouble powering out of that pinfall attempt. Both men back to their feet and its Troy with a bolo punch on the champion. He whips RAD into the ropes...but RAD MAKES A B-LINE FOR THE REFEREE! HE JUST TOOK OUT THE REF!!! RR: That was SOOOOOO obvious! The referee was standing in the corner, and RAD purposely went into the buckles to knock the official out! Actually, that's kinda funny when you think about it. BS: Troy just standing there with a dumbfounded look. RR: There's a shock. BS: FROM BEHIND, HEATHER NAILS HIM IN THE HEAD WITH A CHAIR!! Now RAD laying into Troy with a series of kicks. Heather swings the chair again and hits Caster in the small of the back! JJ: Heather sets the chair over by the turnbuckles, and RAD picks up his opponent...RAD sets Troy on the top turnbuckle and stands up on the chair...OH NO!!! HE'S SETTING UP CASTER TROY FOR A PILEDRIVER FROM ATOP THE CHAIR!!! THIS IS WAY TOO HIGH AN ALTITUDE FOR THIS MOVE! HE COULD SERIOUSLY INJURE TROY!! CE: HERE IT COMES!!!! BOOM!!!!! Caster Troy was just piledriven from the height of a steel chair!! Heather throws the chair out of the ring and goes over to revive the referee... RR: She rolls out of the ring, and the referee looks up to see RAT pinning Caster Troy...here's the count.. 1...2...3!!!!!! DING DING DING BN: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of this contest...and STILL UWA International Champion...RRRRRRRRAAAAAADDDDD!!!! JJ: What a despicable act by R.A.D.! BS: R.A.D. rolls out of the ring and thrusts his belt high in the air. A kiss for the title, now one for Heather, as they make their way back to the dressing room. The referee is checking on the condition of Caster Troy, and they call for the medics. They're gonna have to check on Troy's neck. It took QUITE a jolt. CE: Fans, while they're looking in on Troy, we have time for a short break. ( Cut to commercial ) ( Commercial air ) Copyright Evil Pig.Inc Productions 1998 http://come.to/UIWF
