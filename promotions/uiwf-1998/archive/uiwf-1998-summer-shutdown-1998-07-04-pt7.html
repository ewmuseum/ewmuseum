I don't even know where the f*ck they are! ** Few boos** BS: Alright, well, what is your game plan for your match against Jack Daniels? DT: My game plan? Just let my destiny come to me, if it was meant to happen it will. I am laid back and easy about this, don't get me wrong, I am still gonna try my ass off and you will get a show like never before. BS: Jack Daniels is say, the ICON of the UIWF, how do you think you can beat him? DT: How do I think I can beat him? Simple, I beat him once and made him bleed, and we all know, if it bleeds, it can be destroyed! BS: Now about Vince Viper.... DT: No,not now my friend, now is about Jack Daniels....VVV comes later, he is the main course while the drunk is just an entree, now if you'll excuse me, I have people to chat with. BS: Who are they? DT: Shhh, you'll see. ( Fade back to the broadcast booth. ) CE: Well inter sting comments from the challenger. RR: I wonder who he's talking about? Can't be nobody too important. ( On the Universatron the scene switches to the locker room area. The camera approaches a door that reads “LN” on it. The door swings open. Jack Daniels is seen trying to make his way out of the locker room. A cloud of smoke can be seen in the background and some other familiar voices can be heard coming from the room. ) ** Crowd begins to boo save for a few LN fans** JD: Get that damn camera outta my face before I smash it over your head. ( Daniels begins to walk away, but the camera is still following him. ) No what the hell is this? I don't even get an interviewer like Turken? I'm the champion for Christ's sake! What am I saying? I’m talking about the “Underdog”. Yeah, you’ve been here for a while and have experience. But that won’t be enough to overcome my dominance and my intelligence. Listen here Dookie, I have this planned out real nice and simple for you. It’s just a damn shame that you’re too thick headed to realize what it is. Don’t let the suspense kill ya, you’ll find out real soon. Don’t think this is gonna be a walk in the park. You can bet your last dollar that I’m ready to show you and the rest of the world just exactly who rightfully deserves to have that gold around their waist. And let me tell you, there’s no way in hell that your greasy, dirty hands are gonna touch MY belt. Not if I and the LN have anything to do about it. Oh yeah, you can believe that I have reinforcement. It’ll just be a damn shame when your ass is getting beat and there’s no one there to help you. You call Cold Justice backup? Well, you better wake up and smell the coffee Turken, cuz in my book, little fairies that are always worried about their perfect record and all their nonsense is no backup. Nope, there’s no one like the LN, and it’s just a shame that you’re gonna find that out the hard way. Now, don’t let me catch you around my locker room again or I’ll just have to give you a preview of what I’m gonna do to Turken. ( talking to the cameraman ) ( Daniels puts his hand over the camera lens of the camera and shoves it away from him. The camera man fall to the floor and loses focus of Daniels. Daniels slams the door behind him and leaves the camera man on the floor. The scene change to the broadcast booth again. ) CE: Well the champion has an equally strong rebuttal. The war of words has begun and soon will the war in that ring right in front of us! JJ, you've scouted this match extensively, give a preview of what we should expect from each man and what we should expect in this match while I go get ready. ( Evans gets up, removes his head set and leaves. ) RR: Ready for what. JJ: Be quiet Rex. I've got something worthwhile to say unlike ther other remaining members of this broadcast team. RR: Are you bad talking Evans again? JJ: You aren't very bright, are you Rex? RR: ( Rex realizes he's been put down twice. ) HEY! JJ: Like Casey said ... I've scouted both of these men extensively. Duke Turken is a daredevil. But not just in the high flying sense but in the sense he puts his body on the line every night. He'll do anything, including hurting himself, to win a match. That's he's major strength, but also his major weakness. RR: To bad Daniels doesn't have a weakness! JJ: Now you look at the champion, Jack Daniels. He stands 6 foot 6 inches, and weighs in at a mammoth 295 pounds. He'd just as soon beat you in a bar room brawl as he would an amateur wrestling contest. Don't get me wrong, nobody's going to confuse his sheer power with that of Big Bong or The Rocket, and nobody will confuse his technical ability with that of Blistering Bobby Booyah or Supersonic but the man has the ability to wrestle both ways and he combines them well! His major weaknesses are more character flaws then anything having to do with his wrestling skills. He's way over confident, and tends to be unfocused. RR: Nobody cares Jones ... take your scouting and shove it! ** Crowd boos ** RR: Damnit ... we're still on the Universatron. JJ: Well President Evans is in the ring now with his announcement. ( Switch to the ring where Casey Evans stands, microphone in hand. ) CE: Folks in recent weeks many matches that promised to be classics ended in brawl with handfuls of wrestlers involved. That WILL NOT happen tonight. ( Fans cheer. ) CE: I have ordered extra security to make sure NOBODY, and I mean nobody, gets to the ringside area from the back. So it's just Turken and Daniels, 1 ... ON ... 1!!! ( Crowd explodes with approval. ) RR: WHAT?!?! Evans can't do that! He, he can't do that! INVERTED DDT! INVERTED DDT! The Underdog has life! ( Turken wobbles over to the ring apron and begins to collect his thoughts as Electra tens to Daniels. ) JJ: Turken moves in front of Daniels ...
