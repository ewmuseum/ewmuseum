UIWF Card Archive

TNT, Part 3

27 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( Fade back to the broadcast table where Evans and Roads sit. ) CE: JJ and BS have left to attend to the 'matter'. RR: Oh joy, I got Evans for a whole match, all by my self. Which bring me to my next question, what do you have up your sleeve? CE: You'll see. Our next matchup pits former UWA World Champion "Evil" Alex Roberts agaist LN member Big Bong. RR: Yeah! Legalization Nation time! CE: Le ... ( A raspy voice is heard over the PA System ... ) Voice: There is a time for every man to step up. Every man has his calling... Every man needs a change... This is what happened to Bruce Bravo. Ladies and gentlemen... I give you THE "RENEGADE" BRUCE BRAAAVOOOO!!!!!! ( The lights start flickering... finally, the lights completely go out. The voice then continues. ) Voice: Here is the new and improved... Bruce Bravo... If you don't like it, you can JACK OFF! ( You can hear the sound of a rifle loading up... then a gunshot. Immediately following after the gunshot comes green fireworks which fall down from the sky like a shooting star and explodes on the entrance stage. Following that red, green, blue, yellow lazers starts shooting around the arena in the dark. "Temptation" by the Tea Party starts BLARING off the PA System. Two yellow spotlights starts shining on the mouth of the entrance. You can see a long shadow looming around. Then, when mosh pits starts to form... The "Renegade" Bruce Bravo calmly walks through the curtains and stands on the spotlight... He has a drastic make over... He has blonde, spiked up hair where you can see the dark roots and streaks. His cold blue eyes glare around the arena as Bravo strokes his blonde goatee with his left hand. He has his strong arms crossed as he begins to grin ... You can see a long scar across his upper right arm, which is on top of a tattoo of a cross behind a skull. On his left arm, there's a tattoo of the word "Renegade". Under that has a picture of a skull burning in flames. Bravo is wearing a red glittery vest with "RENEGADE" written on the back in white glitter. His vest covers a blue sleeveless t-shirt. A golden necklace of a cross is seen lying over his blue sleeveless t-shirt. Bravo has on a pair of black vinyl pants and black leather boots. Bravo then begins to walk down to the ring, on his way there he kicks the guardrail a couple of times to piss off the fans. Bravo then gracefully walks up the steel steps. He steps on the outside of the ring ropes and keeps his balance on the edge of the ring apron. He looks around the crowd one more time and raises his arms high into the air and recieves jeers from the crowd. Bravo then drops his hands onto the ring ropes and hopes over the top ropes and landing on his feet. He then struts to the center of the ring and opens up his arms in a "what do you think of me now?" fashion. Silver sparks begin to shoot out of the ring post as a few fans takes pictures of Bravo. The lazers suddenly stop shooting around and start to form "BB" about 15 feet off the top of Bruce Bravo's head with green, blue, red, yellow lazers. Bravo then takes off his vest and throws it to the announcer. Bravo then rips off his blue sleeveless t-shirt and throws it into the crowd. That reveals a tattoo on his left pec of a snake wrapped around a dagger. On his right pec, theres a tattoo of a heart with a couple of snakes slithering around it. Bravo then walks over to the corner and asks for a mic... he gets his request. "Temptation" by the Tea Party fades as Bravo gets up on the top turnbuckles and lays on them, lying there calmly and relaxed...] CE: WHAT AN ENTRANCE! The "Renegade" Bruce Bravo: Now... now... now. What the hell is THIS?! LOCO, you kick me out of the l-mob... you think I'm going to cry over this? Hell no. Listen LEGEND, the truth is you never done anything _GREAT_ for me. All you've been doing is bitching about I want this... Bravo do this... Bravo do that. Well, I got two words I've been holding for a long time... LOCO, you can F<BLEEP> OFF!!!!!!!! ( Fans cheer. Lynch Mob fans starts booing ) Bravo: Now, what do you expect from The Renegade? Bruce Bravo DOESN'T follow orders. You can give it to me, and I can shove 'em up your ass. LOCO, you said you can make me into a star... an _ELITE_ wrestler when I joined the Lynch Mob. But what do you do to me? You hang me and hit your little "Hellraiser". Listen, Loc-dawg... I violate the Lynch Mob rules... I disobey you... because Bruce Bravo doesn't do your chores. I'm not your footman. As a matter of fact, I'd like to take you on... September 3rd. It's signed, and your going to get your little masterbating ass kicked! You think you don't need me? You think if every member of the Lynch Mob leaves you... you'll still be threatening? No way, bitch... Your nothing but a mid-carder without Setzer, Pico, or Rey. I'm going to prove that... September 3rd, the main event for TNT. Bruce Bravo verses The "Legend" LOCO. Stipulations... All wrestlers barred from ringside. No one can help you, LOC. It's just you, and me... one on one. No one will back you up, and I'll beat you 1,2,3. I'll show you what I'm about, you think I'm just another mid-carder? That's your opinion. As far as I'm concerned... LOCO is good as dead to me. After September 3rd, The LOC-Dawg will be the _DEAD_ legend. But, I must admit ... LOCO you were quiet the superstar about a year ago. But now, your just a drunk masterbating asshole that slides by the name. This time, Loc... your the one that's going to get lynched... but Bruce Bravo is going to do it with _STYLE_ you fucking hippie. ( Fans cheer ) Bravo: But, what I'd like to talk about is Setzer Van Strife and Rey Jeran. I got one question for the two of you... why the hell do you hang around this asshole? Both of you are better than him. Both of you have the skill and talent LOCO never had. Without you two, LOCO would be nothing... you know it. But, moving on... LOCO. You have a match tonight with Ken Wallace. Win or lose, it doesn't matter to me. Either way, I want your ass in the ring so I can kick it. LOCO, you've been known as the "Independent Superstar". I guess they call you that because you've won more jobber titles than Prince Iasucka. LOCO, I'll see you in the ring... September 3rd, this means _RATINGS_ for Evans. LOCO, I have looked up to you like a mentor... that's why it makes it so hard for me to pound the shit out of you. I'd hate to beat one of the "legends" in the wrestling world. I'd hate to pin one of the biggest stars in wrestling and humiliate him like that... Then again, that'll prove just what you really are, a sack of shit. LOCO you've talked... but what'll happen when you step up against one Renegade you have NEVER... EVER... faced? I won't tell you what's going to happen, but at the end... I knock you out and pin you in the center of the ring. It's just too predictable... ain't it? I also hope you do, in fact... beat Ken Wallace for the World Title. That way, once I beat you September 3rd, I know it's worth my while as I hold the UIWF World Title high up in the air and you get carried out in a FUCKING STRETCHER! Seeing you in the hospital just makes me want to, to ... go dancing. LOCO, it's no secreat that you will obviously get your ass beat down like a sorry dog. But, enough of the trash talk... LOCO, bottom line ... we meet on Thursday Night Terror... all wrestlers barred from ringside. Once your in my grasps... there's nobody to help you this time, _LOC-DAWG_!!! End of discussion, bastard! ["Temptation" by Tea Party starts playing as Bravo walks through between the top and middle ropes. He exits through the crowd... spitting on a few fans and taunting the rest of them. A few people throw beer bottles and foods at Bravo, but he dodges them. He then lifts both his arms high into the air and gives out the middle finger. The fans start booing him... Bravo then drops his hands down into a "middle finger-crotch chop"... a riot is about to start but UIWF Security stops them and holds the fans back as Bravo walks away...] RR: It's the worst of both worlds. DX and Steve Austin! Run for your life! CE: Bruce certainly seems to be a mixed up young man at this point. RR: Who cares, Big Bong is fighting next! CE: This should be a classic battle of the big men, both men having no love lost for the other. Introducing first, already standing in the ring... he stands 7 feet 1 inch and weighs in at 352 pounds... here is... B I G B O N G ! ! ! ( Big Bong raises his arms in the air, with a look of drug use on his face ) RR: HEY! Where's the big entrance? CE: I guess Big Bong didn't want to come down to one. RR: This is a conspiracy! BN: And his opponent, to be led to the ring with his manager, the greatest mind in wrestling today, Al Percival, from Swansea Wales, weighing in at 320lbs... E V I L A L E X R O B E R T S ! ! ! ( As if the fans needed encouragement enough to boo him, EAR walks out in his colorful T-shirt declaring "AL SEZ FUCK YOU!". MAJOR HEEL POP! Percival walks out after Roberts, dressed in jeans and a UIWF shirt. Both men are carrying weapons, Roberts has a chair and Percival favors a wrench and a bat. As they walk to the ring, a foolish kid has made the mistake of having a sign that says "I want Roberts' Ass!" EAR and EAP both laugh, seemingly walking away, when EAR lands a vicious left hook to the side of the kid's head. BIGGER HEEL POP! ) CE: Roberts and Percival carrying an array of weapons tonight. RR: Maybe it's an insurane policy, so to say. Although, with Dark Dragon and Talon Zahn in his corner, it's not as if the LN have a numerical advantage. CE: Well, here's a train of thought. I'm sure Roberts doesn't have a Swansea Hardcore Tattoo simply for decorative purposes. ( Roberts rips off his shirt and lets out a twisted scream. Percival walks over to the booth and bad mouths Rex Roads ) RR: Get away from me! CE: Percival, we discussed this. You stay away from the announcers. ( DING DING DING! ) CE: And we're underway here. Collar to elbow tie-up, a surprising technical display from these 2 men. I wouldn't have expected this. Roberts backed into the corner... referee asking Bong to break... and he does. RR: CHEATER! CHEATER! CE: The referee isn't going to call a DQ for a poke in the eye. ( Bong holds his eye as Roberts clips the big man's leg. Bong plops to the canvas, and Roberts wastes no time grabbing Bong's legs. He hesitates for a moment, before cracking the wishbone. Roberts then bounces off the opposite rope, sending an elbow into the left leg of Bong. ) CE: Roberts is really concentrating on Bong's legs. Looks like he's trying to keep the big man on the canvas. RR: Yeah, he's just afraid to fight Bong man to man! ( Roberts then grabs Bong's left leg, applying a spinning toehold. The referee checks Bong, as he shakes his head. Roberts then stops the hold after a few moments, pausing to raise his arms to the fans... ) **HEEL POP!!!** RR: When will he get the clue that noone likes him? CE: About the same time you get the clue. RR: Hey, I'm loved in the UIWF. I've heard a few people started a "Rex Roads Fan Club". CE: Yeah, your family, and I doubt even that. ( Roberts looks towards Bong, who is trying to get up. Roberts then grabs him up, whipping him to the ropes. Bong ducks a clothesline attempt from Roberts, but Roberts catches him on the way back, the momentum enough to POWERSLAM the big man, driving him into the canvas... cover... ) 1... 2... 2.5!!! CE: And Bong gets out of that one. I thought we had a winner there Rex. RR: Bong is too damn tough for Roberts. He might have been a champion in the UWA, but this is the UIWF... the BIG TIME BABY! ( Roberts goes right back to work on Bong's left leg, once again applying a spinning toehold. The referee checks once again, with Bong shaking it off. Roberts smiles and keeps the hold on. ) CE: Roberts is really trying to damage something. RR: You can't damage Big Bong though, he don't feel it. ( Roberts releases the hold once again, then grabs up Bong once again. He whips Bong to the ropes, followed by a clothesline. Soon after, Roberts bounces off the ropes, nailing Bong with an elbow drop to the leg. Roberts then tries a cover... ) 1... 2... 2.75!!! CE: Bong barely kicked out there. I thought Roberts had him. RR: You can't keep Big Bong down for long! ( Roberts walks to the center of the ring, posing a little bit for the crowd. As he does so, Bong slowly gets up. Percival tries to get Roberts' attention, but instead of catching his attention, he gets the referee's. Big Bong slowly rolls over to Roberts... ) CE: LOW BLOW!!! OOH!!! RR: Yeah! Take that! ( Roberts slumps to the mat slowly, as Bong tries to get to his feet, slowly but surely getting there. Bong bounces off the ropes, then hits a big splash. Bong then goes for the cover... ) 1... 2... 2.99!!! CE: No! The referee waves it off! We almost had an upset here! RR: Dammit! Dammit! ( Bong rolls off of Roberts, still tired from Roberts' beating. Both men try and get up, with Roberts being the one that gets to his feet first. Roberts grabs Bong, already halfway up, and whips him to the ropes. Using the same momentum as before, Roberts hits another powerslam and covers... ) 1... 2... 2.99!!! RR: NO! Whew! CE: Another close call, but Bong just managed to kick out. ( Roberts grabs up Bong once again... EVIL DRIVER!!! ) CE: Wow! What a pile driver! He covers again! 1... 2... 2.99!!! CE: Bong kicks out again!!! ( In a fit of rage, Roberts picks up Bong again... EVILISER!!! ) CE: He has to have it now! 1... 2... 3!!!!! ( DING DING DING!!! ) BN: HERE IS YOUR WINNER... E V I L A L E X R O B E R T S ! ! ! RR: DAMN! ( The crowd begins to buzz. Most stand up and look towards the entrance way. ) RR: HAHA YES! 4:20 squad to the rescue! CE: It's Jack Daniels and Electra! ( As EAR gloats in the ring, giving Bong a swift kick everyonce in a while Daniels and Electra rush to the ring. Electra sneaks up behind Percival and stuns him with the stun gun. Daniels taps Roberts on the shoulder and ... ) RR: SOUR MASH! **Northern Lights BOMB** ( HUGE HEEL POP ) CE: Folks we gotta take a short break, stay tuned. ( Fade to commercials as Daniels and Electra help Bong to the back and EAR and EAP lay in the ring. ) ( Commercials air. ) CE: We're ba ... ( Suddenly the lights inside the arena completely go out! The fans give a fainting pop, a few lighters are seen waving in the crowd of people. ) CE: It never stops ... RR: Damnit why do so many people have so much to say tonight, that's what trash talkin' is for! ( The annoucers go silent, wondering, like most, what is going on. Than out of the speakers, voices are heard...] "The Confessor" Rey Jeran: Let's take you back to last week, Supersonic. You and I... the MAIN EVENT. I had that match WON, until Adair decided to bring out Johnny Coals to ruin the party.... ( The Jeran recording from last week's UWA Saturday Night Slaughterhouse suddenly cuts away, leaving the arena silent once again 'cept for the few fans screaming Lynch Mob slogans. After a few moments, another voice comes on... ) Gordon Adair: LOCO you claim that any attempt that I make to save this organization from the doom that you and your boys bring upon it is futile because I push Johnny Coals as my top star. Well, first of all there's nothing with Johnny Coals. He's a great wrestler...held the Television and Cruiserweight titles... but in no way did I ever push him as top wrestler. Coals has a lot of talent, but I've never chosen him to be the flagbearer of the UWA... ( Gordon's words, also from last week's UWA Saturday Night Slaughterhouse, is also cut off. Than... ) _BOOOOOOMMMM_!!! ( A HUGE sounds blasts out of the speakers, the VideoTron lights up in a flash of blinding white light! As the flash passes, and the fans regain their vision, they see smoke covering the VideoTron. The arena is still quite quiet, fans still not knowing what to make of the situation. But as the gray trails of smoke head north and the VideoTron begins to clear up, there on the Universatron is UWA Superstar, JOHNNY "BAD BOY" COALS! ) ( The crowd lets out a fairly good pop, although not as big as one in a UWA arena. The UIWF fans who recognize the Former UWA Cruiserweight Champion cheer, but there are some who are not very familar with the man behind the mask. Only hearing his name in references made by commentators. ) ( Johnny "Bad Boy" Coals' is shown from the chest up. He is wearing, his now patented, red bandana which covers everything on his face from the nose down. His brown bangs are brushed back to mix with his black hair. He has on a black shirt, although not enough of it is seen to decipher wether anything is on the shirt. A gold chain is alway noticed around his neck, but only half is visible. Coals waits a few moments before speaking, letting the crowd calm down. He than slowly takes off his red bandana, even that gets a rouse from the crowd. ) Johnny "Bad Boy" Coals: What we have now is a misunderstanding. A misunderstanding of trust and allegiance. And the situation must be solved in order for there to be total understanding of what lies ahead... for Johnny Coals and for the U.W.A.. You heard one of MANY comments from Lynch Mob member, "the Confessor" Rey Jeran, from this past Saturday Night. Obviously thinking that I am a mere 'puppet' for the hands of UWA President, Gordon Adair. Well, sorry to disappoint you Mr. Jeran and Mr. Van Strife, but I have no connection with Mr. Adair. I wrestle in his federation and THAT'S as far as it goes. I consider him more of a foe than an ally, at this point in time. Funny though.. because when I was the one looking out for myself and not the UWA, Gordon was there preaching for the UWA. Now, the tables have turned. I guess it's just not in the cards for Gordon and myself to sit on the same side of the battle lines. Yes he sits there and waves his UWA pom-poms, but he doesn't mean it... ( Fans let out a surprising cheer ) Johnny "Bad Boy" Coals: He talks about how he does have respect for the UWA, but than he says he has to think of 'important things' like Television ratings and making money. Gordon, those numbers and dollar signs may be important, but should they be at the top of YOUR list? They may be most important for you, but are they the most important for the UWA? I don't want to start a war with you Gordon Adair, I really don't, but I will if I have to. By the way, are your lips bitter?.. I thought they might be because of all the CRAP that's come out of there this week!! ( Fans let out a MONSTER pop! ) Johnny "Bad Boy" Coals: Now that I have that cleared up, I want to talk about Supersonic... ( Another big pop from the UIWF Crowd ) Johnny "Bad Boy" Coals: Supersonic, you and I will face each other in a mere four days in front of a WORLD-WIDE audience. The UWA Television Title will be at stake... the title that I've held once before and, call be bold, but I WILL be wearing it again! I don't have any beef with you, honestly, I didn't know who the hell you were until a few weeks ago. I've heard the hype, you being near the top of some list, but that really have no bearing as to what I have in store for you because I don't need any list to tell me what I already know... And that is it's time for me to SHINE! It's time for me to take my career to the next level and it may be at YOUR expense. But my goal on Sunday isn't for myself, it's for the UWA and my pride for it. Because Supersonic, you hold the only title that isn't in the hands of the UWA, but I intend to change that this Sunday. I don't have any problems with the UIWF or any of it's wrestlers, but I do know that they DON'T measure up to the UWA... RR: WHo is this goof. Security, Security? ( Boo's from the crowd ) Johnny "Bad Boy" Coals: I'm not claiming that the UIWF is 'bush-league' like Gordon Adair is trying to tell everybody, but I do know that the UWA IS better! It may not please you, but it please me to KNOW that I wrestle in a top company and although there are a lot of wrong's in place. A lot of people who try and disgrace and tarnish the UWA name, those men will be dealt with in due time. I won't do it alone, but there are soldiers in the UWA that feel the same way, who don't want to lose the war that is being fought on the sacred battle grounds that I call home. I've NEVER left the UWA and I won't until the day comes when I don't have the energy to walk down that aisle and fight! It may not be 'cool' to say what I'm sayin', but I'm representing because I am PROUD! So Supersonic, this Sunday, I'm going into the Mile High City to take back what is ours, the UWA Television Title! See you in Denver! ( Johnny "Bad Boy" Coals fades away from the screen, getting a less than favourable response from the UIWF crowd. In Coals absence, the VideoTron is replaced with the words "U.W.A. 4 LIFE". The words appear in silver letters, after about 10 seconds on the screen, enough time for the entire audience to read it and respond with either boo's or chants of "U-I-W-F"! Another deafening... ) _BOOOOOMMMM_!! ( ...is heard as the VideoTron once again flashes a bright white light and as the flash passes, the house lights return and the Universatron shuts off. ) CE: It makes you wonder why, if the UWA is the top company, Johnny Coals would fly all the way to Canada to appear on our show. RR: Why? I know why. And so do you Evans. CE: Of course. The UIWF is the place to be. Why do you think LOCO is here, every week, when he hasn't even signed a contract with us? Why? 'cause the UIWF is the place to be. JJ: Well I'm back ... the matter has been taken care of. CE: Great! BS: Did we make it back in time? CE: Yup, your just in time for the first of two title matches.
