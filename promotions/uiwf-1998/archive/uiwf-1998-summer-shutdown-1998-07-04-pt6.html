Summer Shutdown, Part 6

4 July 1998

LN's own Diablo to take on the quickly rising Supersonic. CE: Should be a great, high-flying encounter, as neither of these guys have fought a man that could match his speed in a long time. RR: Big deal...Sonic's a has-been trying to reclaim an ancient glory...Diablo's all potential, and he's a member of LN. Oh, that lovely Electra...come to papa!!! BS: Lots of action sure to come in this match, and with the UIWF Middleweight strap at stake, each man knows he's got his work cut out for him. High-spots galore for certain, as each man knows that he can't beat the other without risking life and limb. CE: Let's get down to the ring, and Barry Nelson with the intros!! BN: The following contest...is set for one fall, with the UIWF Middleweight Title on the line, to change hands on a pinfall or submission... ( "Give it Away" by the Red Hot Chili Peppers begins to play over the PA system. ) BN: Introducing first, from Los Angeles, California, weighing 234 pounds, the challenger, SUPERSONIC!!! ( Supersonic gets a loud face pop as he walks down the aisle, slapping hands on the way. He approaches the ring and walks up the steps onto the ring apron. He vaults himself over the top rope. He climbs a turnbuckle and holds his arms up to a loud pop. He then hops down. ) JJ: The fans are behind this guy, and they know he's got a devil of an opponent waiting for him. RR: That wasn't funny, Jim. ( The crowd simmers from cheering Supersonic, as Barry Nelson grasps the mic and raises it to his mouth. He takes in a deep breath as suddenly the spotlights burst, one by one, as glass falls from the ceiling. Thousands of lighters ignite to break the pitch black. Suddenly red light emerges from the locker room area that illuminates the patch to the ring. A slow deep drumbeat begins to play as from the locker room area emerges an unmanned horse. The red light shines off its saddle as the figure of Diablo steps out of the back. The drums continue to pound as he and the horse make their way to the ring. ) Diablo: The time has come as an unmanned horse waits to carry your carcass to the gates of hell. There are many soldiers, but the only the true remain to become the memories of all. You are the weak, timid soldier who chooses to fight for glory, than to fight for your life. I am the bearer of unconditional hate. I am the originator of all pain, I will defy all, and especially those who chose to defy me. It has come. A battle of light and dark, but as always dark has remained, pulling at the mind of the light. Playing with their thoughts, as they realize we shall never perish. ( Diablo enters the ring as the horse waits outside. The lights continue to illuminate the arena as they provide the only light for the match. ) CE: Supersonic and Diablo start with a collar and elbow tie up, Sonic with a pronounced weight and height advantage maneuvering Diablo toward a corner. He backs Diablo into the corner and allows a clean break. JJ: Diablo runs out with a shoulder, but neither man budges. Sonic whips Diablo into the opposite corner...Diablo charges out with another shoulder that knocks Sonic over. Sonic back to his feet, runs into the ropes...Diablo ducks, Sonic with a leapfrog...another shoulder...and Diablo goes down. RR: Bo-o-o-o-o-o-o-o-rrrrrring!!! Bo-o-o-o-o-o-o-o-rrrrrring!!! BS: That's enough, Rex, just comment on the stupid match already. RR: See, Bob, even you said it was stupid. Just hand the belt to Diablo, hand me Electra, and let's get to the next match already. I wanna see Electra... ( As the commentators digress, Diablo whips Sonic into the ropes and delivers a back body drop, followed by an elbow, then a second. Minor heel pop. ) CE: Diablo taking an early advantage, hooks Sonic into a front facelock, lifts and...Sonic tangles the legs to avoid that vertical suplex. And Diablo hits Sonic with a low blow...then executes a snap suplex! [Lots of boos for the low blow, Diablo ignores them completely.] BS: Diablo maneuvers into a camel clutch, but Sonic quickly finds the corner and escapes. Diablo charges in, and Sonic greets him with a big elbow...and a massive clothesline! ( Crowd cheers loudly. ) RR: As Diablo gets back up, Sonic takes him down with a double leg takedown, dropping an elbow on the knee. Sonic grapevines the leg and straightens, obviously trying to ground Diablo's aerial attack. CE: Diablo's inching his way toward the ropes, but can't quite reach them...Sonic drops another elbow on the inside of that left knee, and Diablo isn't going anywhere. BS: Well, he's trying, and rakes the eyes to escape the hold. Diablo runs into the ropes...dropkick attempt...swatted away! And Sonic slaps that grapevine back on the leg! ( Loud cheering! ) Crowd: Let's go, Supersonic, let's go! Let's go, Supersonic, let's go! JJ: Diablo reaches up, and maneuvers Sonic into an inside cradle!!! One... Kickout. CE: Both men trying to work their way back to a vertical base...and Diablo with a vicious kick to the knee of Sonic. Follows it up with a snap DDT! Diablo gets back up, off the opposite ropes, and an elbowdrop! ( Heel pop! ) BS: Diablo lifting Sonic by the hair, a whip into the ropes...Sonic ducks a clothesline...goes for a flying cross body...nobody there! Diablo moved, and Sonic flies through the ropes to the outside! RR: Diablo runs off the ropes...leaps...flying tope dive knocks Sonic back first into the railing! ( Loud heel pop as security, led by Pedro Kozaro comes to restrain the fans closest to the action. ) RR: Hi, Pedro! JJ: Don't antagonize him, Rex, he's doing his job. Just like you should be, right now. CE: *ahem* As Sonic stands up, Diablo takes him down with a huracanrana! ( Another heel pop, as the dazed Sonic staggers toward the ring...and the merciless Diablo drop kicks him right into the steel steps! ) BS: I think the referee's count is up to about a seven or an eight right now...Diablo doesn't need to pin Sonic to hold his strap, so he should be perfectly content with a win any way he can get it. But he throws Sonic into the ring anyways, obviously looking to deal out a little more punishment. RR: Sonic's slowly getting back up, Diablo hooks one leg around Sonic's and takes him down with a Russian Leg Sweep! CE: A cover... Ref: One... Two... CE: Kickout at the two. JJ: And Diablo grabs the legs, tries for a scorpion deathlock--but Sonic's twisting, not allowing Diablo to cinch it in... ( Rising crowd emotion as Sonic tries to remove the attempted scorpion deathlock... ) BS: Sonic pulls out Diablo's left leg and turns it into an STF! But Diablo's too close to the ropes, and Sonic breaks the hold immediately. CE: Diablo with a series of kicks, he has Sonic down to one knee, as Diablo picks him back up...and down with a bodyslam! He exits the ring climbs to the top rope, as Sonic gets up...and staggers into the ropes! Diablo falls over... ( Face pop as the fall stuns Diablo! The crowd starts chanting "Sonic!" again! ) RR: Sonic might try to end it all now, as he hooks Diablo into a front facelock in an attempted superplex. But Diablo tangles Sonic and front superplexes him...and Sonic holds onto that as both men come crashing down!! ( Both men are stunned, as each hits the canvas face-and-chest-first. The ref begins his 10-count. ) JJ: 1...2...3...4...5...6...7...Diablo and Sonic both get up at roughly the same time, each trying to shake off the daze. It looks like Sonic might have gotten the better of that exchange, as... ( For all the viewers, the camera shifts to a split screen double feature which shows the awkward angle that Diablo's head hit the ground, akin to a DDT. ) ...Diablo's head landed in almost a DDT position, Sonic's weight coming down on top of Diablo's head... ( Back to the action, as Sonic hammers Diablo with a series of knife-hand chops. ) CE: Sonic whips Diablo into the corner...follows in...hits a leaping avalanche! Diablo is dazed.. JJ: And Sonic just picked up Diablo into a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker!!! ( Mega face pop for Sonic's offensive! ) Crowd: Let's go, Supersonic, let's go! Let's go, Supersonic, let's go! JJ: And a cover... One... Two... Shoulder up at the two and a half! RR: Sonic on a roll as he turns Diablo into a Boston crab in the middle of the ring, but we _know_ that the LN is here to watch Daniels defeat Turken. CE: Uh, Rex...hate to tell you this, but that's the _next_ match. As in, it hasn't happened yet. RR: Might as well have. CE: Then I guess I should just cancel the match and give _Electra_ and Jack Daniels the night off... RR: No-o-o-o-o-o!!! I wanna see Electra, damn it! Pl-e-e-e-e-e-e-e- eeeeeaaaassseeee!!!!!! CE: Ha ha ha, just had to phrase things properly. BS: Sonic has effectively grounded Diablo, as without a good back, those aerial maneuvers are hard to execute... ( Forty-five more seconds elapse. ) JJ: Diablo's been in that hold for a good minute and a half, Sonic almost looking a little frustrated at not being able to get a submission as he cranks back even harder, twisting and crunching Diablo's lower back. RR: And knowing that it was a lost cause, Sonic breaks the hold and stomps away at the back of the superior man, obviously flustered as his own inferiority. CE: Rex, either comment on things professionally or I'll have Pedro come over and put a blindfold on you until Electra's come and gone. RR: *ahem* And Sonic applies a brilliant standing headscissors, hooks his arms around Diablo's waist fantastically and lifts Diablo onto his shoulders spectacularly. Sonic trying for a magnificent powerbomb as Diablo tries to lean back and utilize a masterful huracanrana... CE: I don't like sarcasm, Rex! RR: But Sonic drops into a sitdown powerbomb, holds it for a cover... One... Two... Thr...nope, Diablo kicks out! RR: Ya know, Casey, that is what you sound like... JJ: Sonic picks up Diablo, front facelock and... RR: ...It's called ego... BS: Can't quite lock on a DDT as Diablo sweeps out Sonic's legs in a desperation attempt.. RR: ...Ego masturbation... CE, BS, JJ in unison]: ___SHUT___THE___HELL___UP___!!!!!!! RR: ( meekly ) Sorry, guys. ( While the announcers bicker, Sonic drops an elbow on the downed Diablo. ) JJ: Sonic now climbing to the top as Diablo slowly gets to his feet...and a leaping clothesline levels Diablo again! ( Face pop from the crowd as Sonic knocks down Diablo again! ) One... Two... Thr...foot on the ropes. CE: Sonic, undaunted, picks up Diablo yet again, and perches him on the top rope, climbing to the second. A few shots to the face. Front facelock and...can't get him! Diablo looks to be countering Sonic's setup move! Diablo punches Sonic in the stomach... ...and spins into a tornado DDT!!! ( Mega heel pop at the sudden change in momentum, although Diablo is in no shape to make a cover himself. ) Ref: 1...2...3...4...5... BS: Neither one's moving an inch!! Ref: 6...7...8... JJ: Sonic's up to one knee. Ref: ...9... RR: Diablo's pulling himself up by the ropes... ...and both men beat the ten-count! Barely. BS: Sonic advancing on Diablo...but Diablo moves out of the way, hitting Sonic in the stomach with a closed fist! Diablo whips Sonic into the ropes...hooks on a sleeper!!! ( Heel pop as Diablo's got the hold cinched in well... ) JJ: Given the type of match this has been, Sonic must be exhausted...this could be just the hold to put him away... Crowd: Let's go, Supersonic, let's go! Let's go, Supersonic, let's go! ( Fans try to rally Supersonic, but the sleeper is definitely taking its toll... ) CE: Sonic's down to one knee... ( Fifteen agonizing seconds elapse... ) RR: Diablo's got Sonic down to the mat, and added a body scissors to that sleeper...now, it's even harder to escape... ( Ref starts the hand raises... ) JJ: That's one, just look at Sonic's glazed-over eyes... JJ: Two... JJ: Sonic's hand drops for the third time...only halfway! Sonic's still in it! Crowd: Let's go, Supersonic, let's go! Let's go, Supersonic, let's go! ( Face pop as Supersonic still has some energy reserves left. Crawling, Sonic reaches the ropes, forcing Diablo to break the hold. ) CE: Sonic was just devastated by that sleeper, still out on the ropes, as Diablo tries to finish him off. BS: Diablo leaps up to the top rope facing outward...he's eyeing out Sonic...Sonic releases the ropes and just collapses onto the mat! Diablo leaps...AN ASAI MOONSAULT WITH A HALF-TWIST! ( Heel pop to the rafters as Diablo crashes into Sonic's chest with a crushing impact. ) CE:And a cover... Ref: One... Two... CE: Three! NO!!! Just a 2.9 on that one. RR: C'mon Diablo, don't argue with the ref! Just finish him off! ( Diablo picks up Sonic's unconscious form into an abdominal stretch. ) JJ: That's the start of the Hell's Gate...all he need to do is swing around into a DDT and it's another successful title defense... CE: But Sonic recognizes it and counters with a hiptoss! Sonic seems to be getting his second wind! ( Face pop as Sonic struggles to his feet, Diablo rushing in with a clothesline, but Sonic just drops out of the way. ) RR: About as graceful a fall as a Hefty bag filled with vegetable soup, but still effective in dodging that clothesline as Diablo tumbles out to the floor. BS: Sonic following Diablo out to the floor...Diablo tries to Irish whip Sonic into the railing..but Sonic stops himself and whips Diablo right into a DDT! On the floor! RR: Hey, that's cheating! (Face pop for the DDT on the floor, Diablo looks to be out cold. ) CE: Sonic's desperation move paying off in dividends...but no title belt for a count out. Sonic throws Diablo into the ring. JJ: Now Sonic hoisting Diablo onto the top rope, a forearm shot and Sonic's up to the second, hooks Diablo...superplex!! No, Sonic didn't quite have the energy to get him over, and he drops Diablo headfirst! _Super brainbuster_!!! ( Loads of cheers from the crowd as Diablo bounces once, then lands, motionless... ) BS: Aggravating the effects of that desperation DDT on the floor, Sonic climbs up to the top rope to a rousing ovation! ***MOON FLIP***!!! ( Asai moonsault splash ) CE:And Sonic basically passes out onto Diablo after hitting his move! Ref: One... Two... ( Both men are out, but Sonic's on top! ) Three!!! CE: We have a new UIWF Middleweight Champion!!! ( "Give it Away" starts back up over the loudspeakers. Face pop to the stars as the unpopular Diablo loses his long-held title!!! ) BN: And ladies and gentlemen, your winner, and new UIWF Middleweight Champion...__SUPERSONIC!!!___ ( Sonic gets up as the ref raises his hand and slings the title over his shoulder. ) BS: And the fans here just love it! A new champion, in a helluva match! ( Sonic walks down the aisle, high fiving fans' hands and hoisting his belt aloft in the other, just barely able to walk after a grueling encounter. ) RR: At last, time for Electra in the next match...COME TO PAPA!!! ( Cut to commercial ) ( Commercials air. ) Copyright Evil Pig.Inc Productions 1998 http://come.to/UIWF
