( That familiar sound of bells are heard, as "For Whom The Bell Tolls" begins to blare throughout the arena. The LN fans can be heard cheering loudly and awaiting Jack Daniels and Electra to make their way to the ring. Fans raise their signs once the music begins to play. Such signs as "The Bell Tolls For THEE", "Electra will you marry me?", "LN 4:20", and "Jack DA-Man" can all be seen. A huge cloud of smoke forms at the top of the entranceway. Electra emerges from the smoke to be seen wearing a black miniskirt, a white button down shirt that is tied up above her belly button, and is unbuttoned revealing a black leather bra underneath. She turns to the cloud of smoke and at the same time she points her hands in that direction, a large explosion is heard and seen. Suddenly Jack Daniels can be seen standing in the cloud of smoke.
Some fireworks begin to go off as he emerges from the cloud of smoke. Daniels is wearing a pair of jeans with the belt strapped over his right shoulder. They make their way to the ring, where Daniels helps
Electra up the stairs, and holds the ropes for her to enter the ring.)
CE: Welcome Jack Daniels. We're glad you could be here with us today because in just the very near future, we will be having our SuperCard event, Summer Shutdown. Jack, in Summer Shutdown, your opponent will be....
JD: I know exactly who my opponent is gonna be at Summer Shutdown. You don't have to tell me twice. (Daniels hands Electra the belt for her to hold.) JD: So, it looks as if Turken gets his shot at my belt. But this time
I'll be expecting him, not like last time where his carcass came out of nowhere and he brought those pieces of trash Cold Justice. So, you're the UNDERDOG, huh Turkey. The only thing you're gonna be under is me when I pin your ass for the 1..2..3! Don't start getting your hopes up in the event that you might beat me and take this belt away from me, cuz it just ain't happening.
CE: Well, why do you say that Jack? I mean, Duke has been waiting for this moment for a long time, and I'm sure his intent is taking that belt from you.
JD: Put a sock in it Evans. I've been working my ass off day and night to bring myself to the position that I'm in now. I'm still working day and night to beat these sorry opponents that actually think they have a chance of beating me. Electra has turned up my workouts a notch, and now I'm in top physical condition, like I always was.
CE: I don't think I like your tone. JD: I don't think I care! CE: Well what about Turken? How do you plan on beating him? JD: What about Turken?!?! Yeah, his time has come not only for a title shot, but for an beating. I'll beat him just like I've beaten everyone else in the past. I'll do whatever it takes to retain this belt around my waist and there ain't nothing Turkass can do about it.
You see Dookie, your first mistake was joining those scum bags over there in Cold Justice. Now, if that's how you are gonna classify yourself, as a Cold Justice, then it'll be easier than I thought at
Summer Shutdown. We've beaten them time in and time out and they are still crying about it. And let me tell you this, not only do I, but everyone else in LN looks forward to laying down some serious smack on those Cold Jerkoffs and on you Dookie. You guys think that now you actually have a chance of beating LN. Well, you better wake up out of your dream and step back into reality, if you smell what I'm cooking.
Turken, beware cuz, like I've already told you before..... ( The LN fans can be heard saying this at the same time Daniels says it) THE BELL TOLLS FOR THEE!!!!!!! (The bells strike again, as "For Whom The Bell Tolls" can be heard blaring throughout the arena again. Daniels and Electra begin to make their way out of the ring and back to the locker room area. Daniels makes a few gestures at the crowd, while Electra teases some fans a little. )
JJ: Folks we gotta go to commercial, stay tuned for more action. ( Cut to commercial. ) ( Commercials air. ) ( The screen fades in from a commercial, straight to ringside. "Cult of Personality" by Living Color is blaring over the PA system. The timekeepers bell sounds. )

BS: You gotta favor Supersonic right now, he's on a roll and headed for
Diablo next week! CE: Yes Supersonic has been on a roll and he's undefeated since entering the UIWF/ It'll be interesting to see if he can keep that unblemished record tonight.
RR: I think the Badd Boy is going to take out the trash tonight in the form of that little puke Supersonic.
CE: Well there's only one way to find out and it's coming up. Barry
Nelson is standing in the ring waiting to introduce these two fine wrestlers so let's send it down to him.

CE: IT'S DIABLO! DIABLO IS STARING DOWN SUPERSONIC! JJ: We could have Summer Shutdown right here, right now! ( Diablo lets out a demonic laugh as suddenly he rips apart the chains and stands there, evilly grinning at Supersonic. )

CE: Perhaps we'll get to see Hunter come into his own now as this has been very one sided. BS: A devastating move! Supersonic signaling he's gonna fly! RR: No, no! This isn't right. BS: Put a sock in it, Roads. CE: Supersonic on the top rope...MOONFLIP! An amazing move! Ref: 1... 2... 3! CE: Supersonic continues his path to the top! ( Referee Ron Davis raises Supersonic's hand in victory as Hunter leaves the ring dejected. )
BN: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner, SUPERSONIC! ( Supersonic leaves the ring and begins high filing the fans as

BS: Actually, Evil Al has proven he can get technical too. Both men insanely powerful, though.
JJ: Tyrant just returned after a hiatus, and EAR has been rising up the rankings quickly...
CE: Well it should be interesting ...
