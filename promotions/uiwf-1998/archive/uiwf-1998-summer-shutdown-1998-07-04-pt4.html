Summer Shutdown, Part 4

4 July 1998

The time keepers bell rings 3 times, then Barry Nelson enters the ring with a mic in his hand. ) BN: The following contest, scheduled for one fall with a time limit of one hour is for the UWA World Tag Team Championship. Introducing first, the challengers. They are official ranked UWA #1 contenders...From Thunder Bay, Ontario, weighing in at a total combined weight of 522lbs, "Bullet" Bill and the Rocket...they are the one and only... COLD JUSTICE! ( "Justice for all" by Metallica explodes all over the PA. Fans erupt and the camera pans around a sea of CJ shirts, signs, posters and banners. BBH and Rocket walk out in contrasting styles. Rocket walks to the ring, looking ready for business, whilst BBH high five's the fans, though all smiles disappear when he enters the ring. ) JJ: Remember folks, Forces of Orient are the only team to have put a blemish on the Win/Loss record of Cold Justice and they did that at the last UWA PPV event... ( A Chinese drum begins playing over the PA. The fans again erupt as Chong and Kee walk out, their belts draped on their shoulders. ) BN: And their opponents, from Beijing, Peoples Republic of China, at a total combined weight of 490lbs, Joseph Chong and Jeffrey Kee, the UWA World Tag Team Champions, FORCES OF ORIENT! ( FOO walk slowly, then explode into a run, dropping the belts. ) JJ: The impressive FOO once again putting the gold on the line against CJ...and this match has started! Kee sends Bullet Bill over and now the FOO double-teaming the Rocket...Double team clothesline. Jack Hamp sends Kee to the outside and Chong stomping away on Rocket. Rocket set up for the ride...a knee to the stomach and Rocket drops to one knee. Armbar...Chong really cinching that in...tag to Kee. CE: Kee going up top...shot to the arm and now a Karate legsweep, grounding the Rocket. Thus far, FOO have been in control. BS: You don't get to be a champion for no reason, Evans. Kee attempting a suplex...blocked by the Rocket who counters with one of his own! Kee was slammed into the mat like a little child! Rocket sends Kee into the buckle and a tag is made to Bullet Bill. ( The fans cheer as BBH enters. Kee and he shake hands, then lock up. ) RR: What is it with this sportsmanship stuff? Makes me feel sick! Kee backing Bullet Bill into the corner now...Karate chop to the throat and now a chop to the abdominal area. Kee with a hiptoss out of the corner...blocked and a short fuse clothesline by Hrenchuk and the fans here loving it! JJ: Some great tag teams emerging in the alliance. The LN's 5-0, the 'Linerz Vegas Express and of course these two teams right here going tooth and claw. ( Whilst the commentators praise the UWA tag division, Hrenchuk has slammed Kee to the ground and softened him up with a series of quickly delivered elbows to the sternum. ) CE: Bullet Bill with an abdominal stretch on Kee...Tag to Rocket and a big boot to the ribcage of Jeff Kee rocks the co-holder of the tag team championship. Rocket with a European uppercut and Kee staggers around...Rocket off the ropes and Kee goes down with a shoulder-barge. JJ: Very evenly matched this thus far. Rocket now with a powerslam and Bullet Bill tagged in...snap suplex into a cover. 1...two, no Kee gets the right shoulder up. Kee set up for the ride...Blind tag to Chong...Kee ducks the clothesline and BAM! Right from behind, a vicious drop-kick and Hrenchuk goes down. Chong with a leg hammerlock on Hrenchuk and I think we can see the FOO's strategy here. BS: They're gonna work on Hrenchuk's leg and keep him down. Chong drives the knee into the joints and Hrenchuk felt that one! Chong with another and now a tag to Kee. Kee going up to the second rope...drives the elbow into the knee of Hrenchuk. Hrenchuk stood up by Kee, who bounces off the ropes with a chop block! JJ: Some smart wrestling really beginning to show from FOO. Remember the UWA titles on the line and now Kee trying to get Hrenchuk turned over for a Spinning Toe Hold...no, Hrenchuk showing some upper body power, but Kee slaps on a leg grapevine instead. ( Hrenchuk grimaces as Chong lets out a happy chuckle. ) CE: The UWA champs beginning to take control now. Kee retains his grip on Hrenchuk's right leg and Chong tagged in. Chong adds some leverage to the Leg Grapevine and Carawell telling Kee to break it. ( Kee protests to the ref about something, as Chong sets BBH up in a front facelock. ) JJ: Could Chong be setting Bill up for a Northern Lights Suplex? He's holding onto the leg. RR: No, look. ( Chong puts his hand further down, until his fingers have disappeared into Hrenchuk's trainers. ) JJ: Some kind of suplex being attempted here...Chong executes it...WHAT AN IMPACT! ( The result of that suplex was BBH's ankle being driven right down into the canvas at a weird angle. ) BS: BBH letting out a bellow of pain and Chong with another tag to Kee. JJ: Another smart move by the champions, wearing down CJ with quick tags and moves like that one. Kee drags Hrenchuk over to the corner...Kee now on the outside and, oh man, Kee slapping those legs into the steel. ( Chong makes some sort of gesture to the Rocket, who isn't happy by that at all. Rocket tries to get into the ring, but Carawell restrains him, allowing for FOO to slap on a figure four on the ring post. FOO hold it for about 30 seconds, then break it. ) CE: Hrenchuk just writhing in agony right now. Kee with a tag to Chong. Spike Piledriver! 1...2...Hrenchuk gets up, but he doesn't look at all well. ( Again, FOO goad the Rocket, who again tries to enter. And again, BBH is double-teamed. ) JJ: Chong whips Hrenchuk into the corner and follows with a lariat. Now a chop on to Hrenchuk...but that T-shirt is providing some protection and Chong knows it! ( Chong proceeds to tear BBH's T-shirt off. ) JJ: Chong exposing the bare flesh...Stomach rakes and a double chop. Bill Hrenchuk has been isolated by the FOO and now Hrenchuk needs to make a tag. Scoop Slam by Chong and now Chong signaling he's going up top. BS: This could do it! This could do it right here! CE: Chong off the top...and Hrenchuk moved! ( The crowd is now on their feet, cheering. Some are screaming "CJ! CJ! CJ!" and the others "ORIENT! ORIENT!". ) JJ: Both men need to make tags to their partners...Hrenchuk crawling to his corner, but Chong go to Kee and Kee intercepts the tag attempt. Hrenchuk whipped into a neutral corner and Kee follows up with a big splash. Kee now unloading with a series of martial arts punches and... ( Kee does an Elbow uppercut, which shatters Hrenchuk's nose. A sickening crack is heard all around the arena. ) RR: Haha! Yes, Juice him, Kee! Did ya see that? It was like a raspberry pie exploding! JJ: Hrenchuk's nose just went splat! And Kee now with a belly to belly out of the corner. Kee with a front face lock...snap suplex! Cover! 1...2...Hrenchuk got a shoulder up and now Kee whips him into the ropes...Hrenchuk ducks the clothesline...and again...Flying Body Press! Hrenchuk has the leg hooked 1...2...Kee powers out and nails Hrenchuk on the rebound with a lariat. Hrenchuk set up...Northern Lights Driver! CE: 1...2...thr . NO! Hrenchuk kicking out at the last second! Kee whips Hrenchuk into the ropes, but he's telegraphed and Hrenchuk with a spinning DDT! Both men are down! ( The crowd has started singing "LET'S GO CJ, LET'S GO!" ) JJ: Kee gets to Chong...and Bill gets to Rocket! ( Chong walks back, shaking his head and his hands raised as the fans go mad. ) JJ: Rocket with a big fist nails Chong! Kee runs in and he gets sent over the top! Rocket and Bill setting him up! BS: Chong's gonna ride on the Bullet Train! Chong whipped into the ropes...drop toe hold and a superkick to the face! We have new champions! CE: 1...2...thresus Christ! ( The crowd, ready to scream in victory and let out a disappointed "Aaaaawwww!" ) CE: Pardon my language, folks, but Chong kicked out! Rocket with a powerslam and he's going up top! JJ: The Big man wants to end this now! Rocket Failure *Somersault Leg Drop*! This should be it! Lateral press! 1...2...thr...and again! Chong just took two tremendous moves and he needs to tag in Kee! Rocket with a Gorilla Press and now a tag to his brother, Bill. CE: Hrenchuk needs to be careful now, as the FOO are often dangerous on he break. RR: Huh? CE: Too much of the World Cup...anyway, Bill unloading with a headbutt and now a body slam...Leg Drop right across the throat! 1...2...and Chong again with a kick out! JJ: This is just too much. Both teams sensing the other is weakening and they're wearing each other down! Bullet with a neckbreaker and a tag to the Rocket...double team powerbomb! BS: If you ask me, the tide has changed in this match. Kee and Chong dominated for most of it, but they wore themselves down...Rocket with a perfect suplex. ( Rocket lets out a roar, and the fans cheer. ) JJ: Tag to BBH and Chong whipped into the ropes...blind tag to Kee...Chong gets leveled with the clothesline...Kee flies in, and that's a sadistic headbutt. Hrenchuk's nose pouring out the blood and Kee with a right hand, right on the bridge! RR: Look, Bills crying! BS: No, his eyes are watering and with good reason. ( BBH staggers around, wiping tears and blood off his face as Kee goes to the second rope. ) JJ: Kee with a bulldog! 1...2...Thr and Hrenchuk waited until the last minute. Kee with a Thunder Fire Bomb! Hrenchuk's neck driven into the canvas! 1...2...the OHMIGOD!! Again, CJ survive! BS: Kee looks pretty annoyed. JJ: Reverse sit-down Piledriver and this has to do it! 1...2...3, no, Hrenchuk kicked out before the ref's hand touched the canvas. What is it gonna take. RR: Normally, I'd say let them kill each other, but I wanna see Daniels kick 10 shades out of Duke Turken. CE: That still to come and Kee just hit the Rocket with a left hand and now a DDT on Bill. BS: "10 shades?" JJ: LOOK AT THAT! A Beijing Superplex! A notoriously difficult move to pull off, but Kee made it look easy! 1...2...NO! Again, Hrenchuk with enough to kick out. Kee with a headbutt on Bill's nose now tags in Chong...Full Nelson by Chong and Hrenchuk is dead center in the ring...Suplex by Chong! 1...2...thrilling stuff, this. CE: Chong sets up Bill for a Suplex...blocked and NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX! Hrenchuk hit Chong with the Northern Lights! 2...3 no, no, no! My God, what the hell is it gonna take? Hrenchuk with a shot to the gut and a DDT and he limps over to the Rocket. JJ: Kee in and he clobbers Hrenchuk from behind! The Rocket in and now all 4 men in the ring! Kee tossed over the top and now Bill and Rocket Double team Chong! Hrenchuk tags Rocket and the Rocket is now the legal man! ( Rocket bombards Chong with a handful of punches and kicks. Kee walks in with one of the belts in an attempt to bean Rocket with it, but... ) CE: Kee with the belt...and the Rocket turned around! Kee backing off but Chong with a double axehandle from behind...Rocket didn't feel it! Rocket with a clothesline...Chong takes him out too and now Rocket and Chong are outside! Rocket scoops up Chong! JJ: Guys...incoming. BS: Huh? ( We hear Rex drop his headset and run off, screaming. Chong flies into the commentary booth after being slammed by Rocket. The booth remains structural integrity, but it looks screwed. ) JJ: We still on? Testing! Testing! CE: Rocket throws Chong into the steps! He tears the upper layer off and beans Chong with them! Chong thrown into the steel posts near us. BS: Where'd Roads go? JJ: Who cares? Standing body scissors by the Rocket! ROCKET BOMB THROUGH THE TIMEKEEPERS TABLE! Chong is obliterated! BS: Carawell opting not to make the count thus far! Chong is out cold...but Kee isn't! Kee with a chair shot from behind! Rocket on one knee...and Kee with a shot to the side of the head! JJ: Bill's had enough! Bill running outside and he's taking it to Kee! Kee rammed into the security rail and now both Hrenchuk and Kee are brawling all the way up the ramp! ( Kee and BBH are fighting all the way to the lockers. Kee gains the upper hand and suplexes Hrenchuk onto the steel. Meanwhile, the Rocket has painfully rolled into the ring. Carawell has begun a count. ) BS: Who's he counting? Kee and Hrenchuk? RR: Which Hrenchuk ...? JJ: I don't think so! He's counting Chong, who is still out cold. CE: 7...8...9...10! Carawell's counted somebody. The bell has gone and now Nelson has the decision. RR: WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON HERE? ( Hrenchuk and Kee have brawled all the way down to the ring again. ) BN: The winners of this match as the result of a count-out...COLD JUSTICE! (The fans erupt. Kee looks annoyed, as though he can't believe it. Chong has gotten to his feet and grabbed the belts off the ref.) JJ: CJ have won, but FOO still retain the titles, as it was a count-out. CE: An amazing match. Hrenchuk and Rocket as well as Chong and Kee took each other to the limit. Folks, we gotta take a break, stay where you are, we'll be right back! ( Fades to a commercial ) ( Commercials air ) Copyright Evil Pig.Inc Productions 1998 http://come.to/UIWF
