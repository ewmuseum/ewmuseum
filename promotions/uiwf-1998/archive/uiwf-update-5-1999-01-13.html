This is a booked fed, based on angles and roleplays. Without roleplaying, little heat goes into matches. - Special Interviews: In-rings, in-locker room interviews. Things of that nature. Stuff that goes on the card and makes it that little bit more interesting than just match after match. - Extra Stuff: Send something like an advertisement for merchandise involving your wrestler, or a promo. - Strats: See form towards the end of the update regarding strats. Basically they'll include anything you want to happen in a match that would be a little out of the ordinary, some suggested spots and maybe a game-plan for the match. - Angle Suggestions: I like to see participation from handlers regarding angles. Ideas about what you want your e-wrestler to do, or what story-lines you want followed, possible feuds, alliances and other things of that nature fall within this bracket. ********************************** WHERE TO SEND IT? ********************************** Basically everything goes to these two email addys: [detail withheld] [detail withheld] Whatever you send, please help to keep us organized by labeling it. For instance if it's a roleplay: [UIWF] Pedro Kozaro Roleplay If it's a strat: [UIWF] Pedro Kozaro Strat etc. You get the idea... :) ********************************** SCHEDULE & DUE DATES ********************************** Saturday, January 16th - Card is released in formal update.
