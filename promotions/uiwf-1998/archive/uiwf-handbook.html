-[name withheld] UIWF Vice President * ABOUT THE UIWF The UIWF, first off, stands for the Universal Internet Wrestling
Federation. We're your standard booked e-fed. The UIWF began on April 1st 1998 and held it's first card on April 17th 1998.
We then closed down in September of '98. It's now January of '99 and I, president Casey, am ready to roll again.
Lastly the UIWF is a regional promotion based out of Southern Ontario. We frequently tour the North Eastern United States as well. Although we're slowing building momentum in hopes of challenging that 'other'
Canadian promotion one day. :) * CARDS AND OTHER SHOWS The UIWF, for now, will have two weekly flash packs. Nothing fancy, just a bunch of flashes slapped together. As things progress I will upgrade one of the packs to a live show.
The UIWF will have one weekly card called Tuesday Night Terror which will feature 6 live matches and 2-4 dark matches. The amount of matches and amount of dark matches may change if and when I get more writers.
We also send out a weekly update. It may be converted to a show in the future. Again this is dependent on once we get more writers. ( Ie. I won't be spending the bulk of my time writing matches so I'll be able to do other things. )
* BOOKING Here's how I book. I take all things into consideration. Angles, strats, and roleplays, with roleplays weighing the heaviest followed by angles then strats. There's no real % I give to each, and I don't score people with a point system. Basically me and the VP(s) get together, talk each match out and decide on the outcome.
Questions about all booking should be directed to me. Don't bitch, don't whine. If you want an explanation or are curious about something, fine, but there's nothing I hate more then somebody who can't handle it when things don't go there way.
* TITLES Here's a list of titles in the UIWF, a brief description, and they're past owners/wearers. UIWF HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE As you would expect ... the most prestigious title. It will usually only be defended on the biggest card of the month, and maybe once more during the month. It would be an EXTREME rarity to see this title defended more then twice in a month.
Previous UIWF Heavyweight champions - Jack Daniels Ken Wallace LOCO[yes...THAT LOCO] Rey Jaren Evil Alex Roberts UIWF MAPLE LEAF TITLE A brand spanking new title. A title which will normally be defended 2 or 3 times a month. No Previous UIWF Maple Leaf champions. UIWF Cruiserweight TITLE For the "smaller' men of the UIWF. This title has a 235 lbs weight limit. This title will be defended 3 times per month. A step down from the Maple
Leaf title. Previous UIWF Cruiserweight champions. Diablo UIWF TAG TEAM TITLES The UIWF's only title for the tag teams. Usually defended twice a month. Previous UIWF Heavyweight champions - Cold Justice *NOTE: ALL UIWF TITLES HAVE UNIVERSAL WEIGHT LIMITS* * CONCLUSION I know this isn't the most comprehensive handbook out there, but I hope it helps. Me and the VP will be adding more info to this as often as we can. And yes I know the irony in the fact that the application comes AFTER the conclusion.
* APPLICATION Here it is in all it's glory. The UIWF application. If I haven't personally invited you to the UIWF please include a sample roleplay.
