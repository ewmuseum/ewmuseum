A screaming crowd can be heard. The video kicks in. The camera slowly pans around the Leisureplex in Fort Erie to show 5,000 or so screaming fans. Several signs can be seen, such as ... Electra is God! CJ Rules Title Turken LN 4:20 Prepare to be THE BELL TOLLS __Flatlined__ FOR THEE!! UWA Sucks CJ Rules SS, Supersonic, wins at SS, Put me on TV! Reign Rocks Summer Shutdown The camera continues to pan around the arena showing the fans until it comes to a stop at the broadcaster's table. Casey Evans, Bob Sharpe, Jim Jones, and Rex Roads all sit at the table. All 4 of the men are wearing headsets and " official " UIWF shirts. ) CE: Ladies and Gentlemen, boys and girls, welcome to the Universal Wrestling Federations Presentation of Summer Shutdown LIVE from the Leisureplex in Fort Erie Ontario! ( Crowd goes nuts as Evans' comments can be heard over the Universatron. The camera then proceeds to pan around the rabid crowd once again before returning to the broadcast table. ) RR: Wow Evans ... your a pretty good salesmen. Look at all these idiots that you got to by tickets in this arena tonight! ( Crowd boos loudly as the what the commentators are saying can still be heard over the Universatron. ) JJ: What a night it is! 4 titles are on the line, a stretcher match, and one hell of a grudge match all tonight! BS: Plus you never know what's going to happen when the Flatlinerz, Cold Justice and the LN are all in the same building together. CE: Our very first match will pit the up-and-coming Jake Harris ( Minor heel Pop from the crowd ) against UWA superstar Crusher Stevenson ( Minor face pop from the crowd ). 6 days ago these two met and Harris thought he had the match won. Stevenson some how found a way to win but Harris wasn't finished. RR: No he wasn't! Harris took Stevenson out! That metal strip inside of a hoho combo was classic! Did you see the look on the faces of those paramedics when they came out? HAHAHA! JJ: Well I'm sure Crusher will be looking for revenge tonight and Harris will be looking for a win as he tries to climb to the top. BS: Our next match will feature a battle with a David and Goliath feel to it. Six foot eight, three hundred and twenty pound Evil Alex Roberts ( Heel pop to the moon ) will take on the six foot two, two hundred and twenty pound Blistering Bobby Booyah ( Face pop ). Booyah is giving up six inches and a hundred pounds! RR: And E A R is gonna mop the floor with him. He's Evil ... PURE EVIL! CE: That he is ... last time these two superstars met, two weeks ago, Blistering Bobby Booyah may have got the win but Roberts made him pay after the match. JJ: Let's not forget our only all UWA ( Some boos some cheers ) match of the night. The Extreme Machine, R.A.D. ( Huge Heel Pop ) will be taking on Castor Troy ( No reaction ) as he defends the Alliance International title. BS: We've seen a lot, and too much in my opinion, of the Universal Wrestling Alliance superstar R.A.D. in the recent weeks and we know these fans don't care to much for him. We know R.A.D. has the ability as he almost beat Jack Daniels and then proceeded to mop the floor with the entire LN. On the other hand Castor Troy is a grizzled UWA veteran who can beat anybody on any given night. RR: ( Dripping with sarcasm ) And let's not forget the match everybody has been waiting for. The Forces of the Orient ( Large amount of boos ) take on Cold Justice. ( The arena begins to shake at it's foundations for this face pop. ) CE: We should see one of the best tag matches of the year as the UIWF's best, Cold Justice, take on the best from the UWA, The Forces of the Orient, for the Alliance tag titles! ( The crowd starts a chant of, prolonged pronunciation of the C, "CJ" "CJ" ) JJ: As good as those matches should be the next two should set the wrestling world on fire! First a man once ranked as high as 19th in the world, and a man trying to regain his former recognized status takes on a one of the most mysterious men in wrestling. Of course we are talking about Supersonic ( Face pop ) and the Universal Internet Wrestling Federation Middleweight champion Diablo. ( Heel Pop ) BS: Two guys wrestling the new style. Two guys flying around the ring with no regard for personal safety or the safety of their opponents. Two guys vying for the Middleweight title ... it should be great! RR: Yeah, great to see Diablo tear Supersonic head off, chew it up, and spit it out! ( The crowd begins to boo Roads. ) CE: Finally, the coup de gras, the Main Event, the match everyone's been waiting for ... for the Heavyweight title, " The Underdog " Duke Turken versus Jack Daniels, the Universal Internet Wrestling Federation Champion. JJ: I can't wait! This should be one for the books and it's a match I know I am looking forward to! RR: Settle down there Jimmy boy. Everybody knows Daniels is gonna destroy Turken. I don't see why it's such a highly anticipated match. ( The crowd begins to boo Roads once again. ) BS: I think it'll be a little tougher match for Daniels then you make it out to be Rex. We saw three weeks ago when Daniels dared anyone to come out of the back and take his title, well suffice to say Turken almost did and would have had it not been for the help of the LN. CE: Tonight we will see a clear cut winner. I assure you all of this and you'll find out why later tonight! But now lets take an early look at our next Supercard, Slam-a-thon '98! ( Fade out to a black screen. ) VO: During the chilly Canadian November .. ( Shots of the Canadian winters air ... a kid on a sled, a snow man, kids in a snowball fight, a peaceful shot of snow falling. ) VO: .. is when things will really start to heat up in the UIWF. ( A logo appears on the screen. Behind it is flames. The logo reads like so: Slam-a-thon '98 in big green letter with white trim.) VO: Slam-a-thon 98, it'll be one slam of a time! ( Fade back into the broadcast table. Casey, Bob, and Rex sit at the table. ) CE: Our broadcast colleague Jim Jones is in the ring for a very special announcement. It's all your Jim, take it away. ( Fade to the ring where Jim Jones stands. Jones is wearing a dark blue UIWF golf shirt and a pair of dark pants. He is holding a mic and has a stupid grin on his face. ) JJ: Ladies and Gentlemen it is my pleasure to introduce to you the new Vice President of the Universal Internet Wrestling Federation. Please help me in welcoming Brandon Stateler! ( Brandon walks down to the ring. Some fans cheer. Mostly a " huh? " reaction from the crowd. ) BS: Let me just say it's nice to meet you and i have to say that these are the best fans of the world right here. ( Crowd begins to cheer Brandon. ) JJ: Now nobody really knows who you are so why don't you tell us who you are. BS: Well i come from a small town in McComb, Ohio and that is where i still live today. I have grown up a wrestling fan all my life. I go to all the events that pass through or around McComb. JJ: How many wrestlers have you met? BS: Right now i have met none, in the UIWF that is. Since this is the first live UIWF event I have been to. And it will my job these coming weeks to meet each wrestler here in the UIWF. JJ: What are your plans to do here? BS: My plans are to make this the best fed that there is. We have a lot of talent here and i want to let the world see all of it. I think that everyone deserves a chance to prove them self. If i think that their our matches that need to be signed to settle feuds then i will do everything in my power to make sure that they get signed. If someone should get a title shot then they will get it. JJ: What are you going to do if someone breaks the rules or does something totally wrong? BS: I'm not here to be a softy. If someone breaks the rules and should be fined they will be fined the right amount of money or if they should be suspended they will be. JJ: What is relationship with wrestling? Have you wrestled before? BS: I am a former wrestler. I have been a past champion in singles and tag team wrestling. JJ: Any last words before you go? BS: Yes. I would just like to say that i look forward to the future here and i have to say that these are the greatest fans in the world. ( The crowd cheers again. ) JJ:Ladies and Gentlemen give it up for your new VP! ( The fans go nuts as Brandon returns to the back. Switch back to the broadcast booth. ) RR: ( In a whiny, sarcastic Voice ) Give it up for your new VP ... CE: Your such a treat to be around Rex ... you really brighten everyone's days. RR: Why thank you Casey. CE: Shut up Rex ... I was being sarcastic. RR: Yeah ... well ... I knew that! CE: Folks we'll be right back after this short commercial break!
