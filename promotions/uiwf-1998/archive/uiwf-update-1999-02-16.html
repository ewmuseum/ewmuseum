This is a booked fed, based on angles and roleplays. Without roleplaying, little heat goes into matches. - Special Interviews: In-rings, in-locker room interviews. Things of that nature. Stuff that goes on the card and makes it that little bit more interesting than just match after match. - Extra Stuff: Send something like an advertisement for merchandise involving your wrestler, or a promo. - Strats: See form towards the end of the update regarding strats. Basically they'll include anything you want to happen in a match that would be a little out of the ordinary, some suggested spots and maybe a game-plan for the match. - Angle Suggestions: I like to see participation from handlers regarding angles. Ideas about what you want your e-wrestler to do, or what story-lines you want followed, possible feuds, alliances and other things of that nature fall within this bracket. ********************************** WHERE TO SEND IT? ********************************** Basically everything goes to these FOUR email addys: [detail withheld] [detail withheld] [detail withheld] [detail withheld] Whatever you send, please help to keep us organized by labeling it. McGreggor also seriously injured Hickden and Hickden will be out at least a few months. - Rapier pulled out a hard fought win over Seth Maxo in a one on one encounter which was made that way due to Seth Maxo not showing up. - Andrew "Flash" Tucker won the Battle Royal in somewhat controversial fashion as it had been thought that "TLM", Pico Sauz, had won after Setzer Van STrife eliminated himself to give Pico the win. Tucker subesquently gained one of the no.1 seeds in the Gauntlent match for the Maple Leaf Title. - Cold Justice defeated God's Justice by disqualification as "Bullet" [name withheld] attacked his own brother after not showing up for the match. This signalled the end of Cold Justice as Bill injured his bigger, but younger, brother Rick. - Nick Demola defeated Stigmata in a hotly contested one on one matchup to become the no.1 contender for the UIWF title. Demola will go on to face Rey Jeran March 14 at the "A weekend to remember Supercard" for the UIWF Heavyweight title. The match will be simulcasted on Sunday Night Showdown. - Rey Jeran took home the gold as he was the first man to escape the cage. Jeran is now the new UIWF Heavyweight champion and his first title defense will take place March 14 against the no.1 contender, Nick Demola. However, below is a suggested format which in my opinion is both easy to use and covers anything you might wish to put in a strat. Strats are not essential in a match, but can often help if the roleplays are close. They also help the writers and show creativity and effort on your part. Wrestler Name: Match: Match Date: Entrance: ( Include entrance music, introduction, description of entrance ) Plan for Match: ( Include what strategy you want to use during the match ) Spots: ( Include some suggested spots ) Winning/Losing Situation: ( Include what you're wrestler will do if he wins the match and if he loses it. ) Extra Plan: ( Use this for any interferences, interviews, or any plan going beyond your own match ) All Gameplans go to both the President and VP #1. ************************************************* U I W F S T A F F ************************************************* UIWF President [name withheld] mailto:[detail withheld] AOL IM = [detail withheld] UIWF Vice President #1 [name withheld] mailto:[detail withheld] AOL IM = [detail withheld] UIWF Vice President #2 [name withheld] mailto:[detail withheld] AOL IM = [detail withheld] UIWF Vice President #3 [name withheld] mailto:[detail withheld] AOL IM = [detail withheld] UIWF Webmaster [name withheld] mailto:[detail withheld] AOL IM = [detail withheld] Match Writers ( Regulars ) [name withheld] [name withheld] [name withheld] [name withheld] [name withheld] [name withheld] [name withheld] Joey [The man with no last name] [name withheld] [name withheld]
