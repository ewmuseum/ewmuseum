TNT, Hour 2

22 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The words DURING THE BREAK flash accross the screen. ) ( Fade in to the cinderblock hallways of the arena, where Evil Al Percival has been rushed to the hospital. Jim Jones stands outside the locker room of EAR and Dark Dragon, hoping to get a word with anyone nearby... ) JJ: Folks, for those of you who have tuned in to watch this special report, this is Jim Jones, reporting live from the arena where just moments ago, in a match featuring Evil Al Percival and Evil Al Roberts against Cold Justice,... ( Clip of early in the match, BBH hits a missile dropkick. ) ( Next, Al Percival hitting a northern lights driver on BBH. ) JJ: Al Percival held his own in the match...but soon the match got out of hand. ( Scene of the melee at ringside, EAR and Rocket are in the ring, Talon Zahn is stomping BBH like crazy, and Dark Dragon walks down the aisle with unholy intentions. ) JJ: Before long, Blistering Bobby Booyah, Talon Zahn, and Dark Dragon were added to the mix... ( Rocket raises a big boot to Percival's face as Talon Zahn throws a steel chair into the ring. ) JJ: As was a steel chair. ( Fade to the clip of Rocket piledriving Al Percival on a steel chair. ) JJ: Before long, Evil Al Percival took a piledriver on a steel chair from Rocket. ( Clip switches to Dark Dragon nailing Rocket with Fiery Doom. ) JJ: Although Dark Dragon intervened with his vaunted Fiery Doom spike chokeslam to give Roberts and Percival the duke, Percival remained comatose after taking that piledriver. ( Clip of EAR and Talon Zahn try to revive the fallen EAP. ) JJ: Attempts to revive Percival were futile, and Percival was quickly strapped to a spine board, stretchered, and rushed to the nearest emergency room where he is currently undergoing treatment. ( Something can be heard through the locker room door. ) TZ: (Talon sound anxious and angry) Damn Bill Hrenchuk, jus' like 'im to put a hold on me where 'e's gotta lay on me prone. Tryin' ta violate the great Talon Zahn like that... ( As the scene fades back to the locker room, the door flies open with tremendous force as Talon Zahn's right boot appears out of nowhere. Talon is half angry and half bewildered, but entirely concerned. ) JJ: (stepping back) Mr. Zahn, if I could just get a word with you...have you received any word on Mr. Percival's condition? TZ (winded): What do you want, Jim? ( Jones appears a little stunned by the outburst, even though it IS from Talon Zahn. ) TZ: I SAID WHAT DO YOU WANT! This is madness here...just where da these guys get off with it!!! Did Casey send ya here ta boost the last quarter-hour ratings? I ain't gonna play the pawn this time... CE (from the front): Talon, wait!!! ( Talon starts to run off towards the back, but then Casey Evans runs off behind him, tie flapping in the air. Talon, rather than speeding up, slows down and grabs Casey Evans by the collar, giving him a violent shake. ) TZ: Is this yer idea of ratings? Do you know what else happened? CE (trembling): Uh... TZ: __DO_YOU_!!! Thanks to your little ratings ploy, a man's life is in danger. A loving wife an' children are 'bout ta get hit with a scary, scary message. A volatile man might be losin' 'is mentor, and with it 'is last shred of restraint. And a tortured soul is screamin' fer retribution. Are _you_ gonna tell Al Percival that 'is career's finished, ta go piss up a rope? Are you gonna stare 'is wife and children in the eye, and tell 'em their husband or daddy might not be able ta lift a fork ta 'is mouth again? Do ya have the (stomps) _guts_ ta tell Evil Al Roberts that 'is mentor'll never be the same again? Are ya gonna tell Dark Dragon ta pretty-please-with-a-cherry-on-top forgive yer scheme? I've got matters ta take care of right now...but if I don't see you at the hospital, standing at Percival's bedside, in exactly thirty minutes, I'll smash you. See ya there, Evans. Or see ya in hell. ( Fade, as the flustered Casey Evans straightens his sportsjacket and Talon Zahn sprints out to the back. ) ( Cut back to the broadcast table where all 4 commentators sit. ) CE: LADIES AND GENTS WELCOME BACK TO TNT! Hour one just ended and now it's on to hour two. I know what you may have just saw ... could have been disturbing, and if it was we hear at the UIWF apoligize. JJ: And what a way to start hour number two. BS: Huh? CE: I think Jim knows what I know. We'll be starting off hour 2 with Setzer Van Strife taking on Jack Vars for the UIWF Middleweight Championship. RR: DOUBLE DEATH BLOW PART III! The final saga! BS: This should be a great match. JJ: Indeed. Setzer Van Strife is an aerial master, and the man can take a lot as evidence of his match with his mentor at the recent Night of Glory Supercard held in England. Van Strife and LOCO battled for well over 40 minutes. The match ended with Van Strife giving up the sure win to inflict more pain on his mentor. He attempted and connected with a moonsault from the top of the cage. LOCO later went on to win the match in dramatic fashion. BS: Jack Vars is a man who needs no introduction. He's competed EVERYWHERE in the United States, and no he's up in Canada with us. Numerous titles, numerous federations, numerous memorable match. This man has a resume the length of the New York City Health Code.
