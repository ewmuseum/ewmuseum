( Cut from commercial. The noise of a crowd can be heard. The video kicks in and a raucous crowd of 2500 or so fans are in a frenzy as President Evans stands in the middle of the ring. The camera zooms in on Evans. Evans waits for the crowd to quiet down a little before he begins to speak. ) CE: IT'S GREAT TO BE HOME! Ladies and gentlemen welcome to another LIVE edition of Thursday Night Thunder. We're back home, broadcasting to you, LIVE, from the Welland arena in Welland Ontario! ( Crowd roars up again. ) CE: I don't know if any of you had the pleasure of watching the latest edition of Saturday Slaughterhouse. Since there was no contract signed on this I can change the name of this federation everyday of the week, and twice on Sunday, or I can choose to not change it all. The UIWF is not a division of the UWA. We can kick you to the curb any time we'd like and you can do like wise. So don't act like you put the UIWF on it's path to success or anything. ( The clip jerks into action again. ) Adair: You continue to push your organization as a competitor with the UWA when truthfully, the UWA MADE the UIWF and we continue to be the reason that you are what you are to this day. ( The clip stops, paused again. The scene switches back to Evans in the ring. ) CE: Now Gordon you've really went and made a fool of yourself here. This is one of the single most bogus statements I have heard in a while. Let's take a look at the UWA's ratings since the UIWF joined with the UWA to form an alliance. ( Evans pulls out a piece of paper ) CE: Hmm ... when we first joined up the UWA's two weekly television programs were routinely pulling in a rating in the 2.0 to 2.5. Good thing you have your own TV station Adair or you'd a been booted off the air. Let's look at the rating Saturday Slaughterhouse recieves now. Hmm ... that's interesting Saturday Slaughterhouse has been on the rise each and every week in the ratings and is currently at a 4.3. So you see Adair you have almost doubled your television rating since we came aboard. The truth is, and we all know the truth hurts, without the UIWF you'd be nowhere. How often do I use your wrestlers Adair? What, about once a month I MIGHT borrow a wrestler or a tag team for ONE match. Saturday Sluaghterhouse features at least 4 UIWF wrestlers each and every week. So don't threaten me Gordo, the facts are right in front of the world now and everybody knows the UWA is benefiting most from this alliance. If we left, you'd be hurting a whole lot more then us. ( The clip jerks into action again. ) Adair: Casey Evans, once again I FORBID you to continue what you're doing. And I forbid you or ANY other member of the UIWF to continue referring to the Ontario title as the UIWF World title. If you do ignore these warnings then you WILL hear from my attorneys. ( The clip stops, paused again. The scene switches back to Evans in the ring. ) CE: Adair, next time you see Ken Wallace, or LOCO, whoever emerges victorius tonight, ask them if you can have a look at their belt. You know what the engravings say? UIWF WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION. Those engravings have been on that belt ever since Jack Daniels won the match to become the first champion way back in April. They're gonna stay there. I look over at the UWA and I see you have NO champion. You look at your strap's title history and it's almost a joke. I've never seen a title get passed around like a hot potato before, you say the UWA belt is the most prestigious in the world? Seems to me not a lot of people want it. You like at the UIWF World Heavyweight Championship belt and there's been fierce competition for it ever since it was introduce. Adair, I think I've made myself pretty clear, GO AHEAD and call your attorneys. I know you won't, you know it, I know it, and now that I've laid out the fact, the entire continent knows it, THE UWA NEEDS THE UIWF MORE THEN WE NEED YOU. ( The clip jerks into action again. ) Adair: And I would like to express one last feeling ... that I believe it is injustice to give the Lynch Mob member LOCO a shot at the UIWF Heavyweight title next week against Wrestlingman. LOCO is undeserving of the shot for two reasons. One, he is the man that actually stole the title from Jack Daniels. Two, he has not even fought one match yet. Mr. Evans, is your loyalty to that of the UIWF or to the Lynch Mob? ( The clip stops, paused again. The scene switches back to Evans in the ring. ) CE: Ya see Gordo, once again, your dead wrong. I didn't give LOCO a title shot. I gave him a match with Ken Wallace. It so happened Ken Wallace won the UIWF World Heavyweight Championship. Now you look at what LOCO has done Gordon, he's come in and set the UIWF on fire. Everyone's talking about him and the Lynch Mob. He's a well known Superstar. He's piqued interest in the UIWF. He's getting a title shot because he deserves one. Ken Wallace didn't object to fighting LOCO. He didn't want to be a paper champion and duck LOCO. We know the fans want to see Wallace put the title on the line. What then was wrong in my decision to have Wallace defend his title. NOTHING! As for your far fetched accusation I am pro Lynch Mob your wrong. I'm not for the Lynch Mob. What am for are wrestlers, who see oppurtunities and seize them by the throat. LOCO saw an oppurtunity and he did just that. Gordon my loyalty is to the UIWF and it's fans. Had I not made this match a title I wouldn't have been putting on the best show I could've, now would I? Now I've wasted enough time on you, your ludicrous demands, and your outlandish accusations. We've got an action packed show to present to the best fans in the world tonight and I intend to do just that!!!
