BBH: (accentuating each word with a thunderous shot to the punching bag) That's (wham) JUST (wham) DUCKY! (WHAM! The small bag goes flying off the holder) Doesn't anyone think of the consequences of their actions anymore? God damnit, one of those belts is rightfully MINE! Sharpe: Bill, please... calm down! Sharpe: Well... know that the gauntlet is out of the way, can I get your thoughts on Wrestlingman, Ken Wallace? BBH: He's still a coward, and he's still just a mediocre wrestler. He deserved to drop that belt, and if he hadn't dropped it then, we would have next week. My only regret is that I couldn't have pinned him when he had the belt. Sharpe: Bill, there's been speculation as to the startup of some kind of martial arts division or a toughman tournament in the UIWF. Would YOU enter something like that? BBH: Undoubtedly. I'm an accomplished martial artist, street fighter and wrestler. It would be one hell of chance to show off my skills. I think that'd be a GREAT idea. We could probably use something similar in the regards of a hard-core division too. Rick's been dying for some hard-core matches. Sharpe: Really? Is that what your brother was doing during your hiatus from wrestling? BBH: Yeah, he just went and left Cold Justice to rot in the noon day sun. That's why we aren't speaking right now. You'll never see us fight each other though. We may argue, but the bond between us is stronger than it looks. We're actually quite inseparable (pauses briefly) most of time. I was shocked and dismayed by FOO's actions. It's to bad too... They may have taken advantage of my injury, but quite honestly, they WERE the only team in either league with a chance to beat us at full strength. Sharpe: You mean that you were fighting in the UIWF while hurt? BBH: Bob, have you EVER known me to lose? Doesn't it seem a bit... ODD to you that I can take out guys like Reign and Mark Kiazyk, but not Supersonic, and FOO? Yes, of course I was hurt, I needed back surgery... Sharpe: But you're healthy now, right? BBH: I wouldn't be back if I wasn't. You know what? I've really got to start my karate training again, so I'm going to have to ask you to leave. Sorry Bob. Sharpe: Sure, no problem. C'mon crew, let's give the man his space. BBH: And Sharpe... Sharpe: Yeah? BBH: Give your family my regards, eh? Sharpe: (laughs) Sure Bill.
