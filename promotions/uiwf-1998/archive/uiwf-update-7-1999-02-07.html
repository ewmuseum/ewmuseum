We have picked up a few more writers so we're not as desperate as we once were, but we're ALWAYS looking to pick up writers. => Roster: It's now pretty much locked up ... at least until the first card is over. Expect the roster to stay relatively stable until that time. => Feel free give us feedback. It might just make the fed better. => Talking of feedback: Give me some feedback on this update. Tell me what you like, what you don't like and what you'd like to see in future updates that we don't already have in here. => The applications. I got a few more ... but I still _NEED_ 2 of them. Here are the 'culprits' :p Big Bong Randy Acorn I may have forgotten to add you to this list, although to the best of my knowledge these are the only 2, so if I have get in an app as well. Also I have enclosed ANOTHER copy of the UIWF application for those of you who have lost or for those who are new to the mailing list. => TNT is in good shape. I have a little of it done, including my match, all the ascii art, some of the non-match stuff, and I have received 2 of the other matches. ************************************************* I N F O R M A T I O N ************************************************* ********************************** W H A T T O S E N D ********************************** - Roleplays: They are an essential part of success in the UIWF. This is a booked fed, based on angles and roleplays. Without roleplaying, little heat goes into matches. - Special Interviews: In-rings, in-locker room interviews. Things of that nature. Stuff that goes on the card and makes it that little bit more interesting than just match after match. - Extra Stuff: Send something like an advertisement for merchandise involving your wrestler, or a promo. - Strats: See form towards the end of the update regarding strats. Basically they'll include anything you want to happen in a match that would be a little out of the ordinary, some suggested spots and maybe a game-plan for the match. - Angle Suggestions: I like to see participation from handlers regarding angles. Ideas about what you want your e-wrestler to do, or what story-lines you want followed, possible feuds, alliances and other things of that nature fall within this bracket. ********************************** WHERE TO SEND IT? ********************************** Basically everything goes to these FOUR email addys: [detail withheld] [detail withheld] [detail withheld] [detail withheld] Whatever you send, please help to keep us organized by labeling it. For instance if it's a roleplay: [UIWF] Pedro Kozaro Roleplay If it's a strat: [UIWF] Pedro Kozaro Strat etc. You get the idea... :) ********************************** SCHEDULE & DUE DATES ********************************** Monday, February 8th - Fifth and final rp packet sent out Tuesday, February 9th - Tuesday Night Terror Friday, February 12th - Post TNT flash packet goes out. Sunday, February 14th - Update goes out Monday, February 15th - Pre TNT flash packet goes out. Note the pattern folks. Update Sunday, RP packet monday, Card Tuesday, Rp packet Friday. The process repeats itself week in and week out. ;D *************************************************************** W R E S T L E R R A N K I N G S A N D C H A M P I O N S *************************************************************** ========================================================= U I W F H E A V Y W E I G H T D I V I S I O N ========================================================= For now it's just a roster list. 1) Nick Demola 2) Rey Jaren 3) Stigmata 4) Rip Fury 5) Big Bong 6) Pico Sauz 7) King Snake 8) "The Bad Boy" Randy Acorn 9) Supersonic 10) Evil Alex Roberts 11) "Reckless" Ryan Stitt 12) LOCO [yes...THAT LOCO] 13) Bad Boy Brian Boston 14) Jimmy "Doomsday" Wallace 15) Eric "Cisco" Cole 16) "The Shooter" Scott Matthews 17) ESCAMILLO 18) Andrew "Flash" Tucker 19) Seth Maxo 20) Rapier 21) Mike Hickden 22) Devlin McGreggor 23) "Mr. Charisma" Christian York 24) Setzer Van Strife TAG TEAMS 1) Cold Justice 2) Pimps on Parade 3) Sherlock Shiznit and the Funky Bunch 4) God's Justice 5) Dazed and Confused - Some people have been removed from the roster of there OWN CHOOSING. A shocking arrival ... it's a surprise ) or I just plumb forgot! :) If the latter is the case please let me know ASAP! ************************************************* L A S T W E E K I N T H E U I W F ************************************************* Nothing to report yet :) ************************************************* N E X T W E E K I N T H E U I W F ************************************************* SOMETHING TO REPORT!!! =) UIWF Tuesday Night Terror February 9th, 1999 LIVE from Copps Coliseum in Hamilton, Ontario THREE WAY DANCE 1 ESCAMILLO vs "The Shooter" Scoot Matthews vs Big Bong THREE WAY DANCE 2 Ryan Stitt vs Devlin McGreggor vs Mike Hickden THREE WAY DANCE 3 Eric Cole vs Seth Maxo vs Rapier Battle Royal Participants 1) Mr. Charisma Christian York 2) Andrew "Flash" Tucker 3) Jimmy "Doomsday" Wallace 4) Rip Fury 5) Pico Sauz 6) King Snake 7) Setzer Van Strife 8) Supersonic 9) Bad Boy Brian Boston 10) "The Franchise" Andru Edwards The winner of this will be guaranteed the no.1 spot in the gauntlet match will decide the UIWF Maple Leaf champ which will take place the next week on TNT. A star studded line-up for sure. It's tough for anybody to set a favourite in this match. Tag Team action!!! God's Justice vs Cold Justice. These two rivals from the OWF look to renew there feud here in the UIWF. The first match to kick off the NEW UIWF tag team division. No.1 Contender match Stigmata vs Nick Demola - Winner gets a title shot at "A weekend to remember" Supercard the UIWF is putting a match on. These two men have no love lost for one another and they have come to the UIWF to settle there feud. What's more a title shot is on the line. A heated feud with a title shot at stake? Are you kidding me? This looks to be one of, if not the, best matches of the night. Cage match for UIWF Heavyweight title. Participants LOCO[yes...THAT LOCO] Evil Alex Roberts Rey Jaren "The Bad Boy" Randy Acorn - First man out of the cage is the UIWF Heavyweight champion. All men are former UIWF or TOP Heavyweight champs. The first man out of the cage gets the gold. Will LOCO and his long time friend Rey Jaren team up? Or will the chance at the gold be enough to put their friendship on hold for a night? Will the only champ Texas Outlaw Promotions ever knew, Randy Acorn, take the Gold? Or will home grown to the UIWF, Evil Alex Roberts unleash some Swansea Hardcore? rumours are flying that the longest reigning UIWF champion ever, one Jack Daniels, will be at the card. Will he somehow factor in to this match? ************************************************* U I W F G A M E P L A N F O R M A T ************************************************* If you're thinking of sending in a strat, then by all means send it in any style you wish.
