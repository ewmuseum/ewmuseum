A Detroit-set fantasy federation on the fwrestling.com hub that opened in 2007 after the Chaotic Wrestling Federation was sold, ran a weekly Mayhem and pay-per-views such as All Hallows Annihilation, Purgatory, Dedication and Deliverance, named a memorial tournament for Whitecloud in 2008, and added a second show, Livewire, in January 2010. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Motor City Wrestling opened in 2007 out of the Chaotic Wrestling Federation. That company's last broadcast, on 3 April 2007, said its new owner would rename it and move it to Detroit, and Dazz's roleplays say the owner, Anthony Wallace, bought the Chairman's shares and kept the contracts of the talent who wanted to stay. Its belts were its own: no title or champion of the predecessor carries over. Its weekly show, Mayhem, was set in Detroit, with pay-per-views named All Hallows Annihilation, Purgatory, Dedication, Deliverance, Deadly Conflicts and Nightmare Before Christmas.

Its own site survives in captures of 2008: an owner and two General Managers, Rayne Young and Dazz, a champions page with World, TV, Tag Team and Brutality belts, a Women's title on its last card, and three Mayhem cards announced without results, from the Mayhem after All Hallows Annihilation in November 2007 to 7 April 2008. Rayne Young held the World title from that pay-per-view, Annie Alvarez took it at Purgatory in February 2008, and BROS took the Tag Team titles the same night.

In 2008 the federation named a tournament and trophy for Whitecloud, and the pay-per-view Dedication for the same purpose; Dazz beat Rayne Young in the first final on 21 April 2008. The rest of the surviving record is Dazz's roleplays of 2009 and early 2010 and a five-chapter serial of Vitriol and Ambrosia, which carry a World title from Rayne Young to Dazz, to Angelica Jones and back to Dazz between October and December 2009, take the company to England for Deadly Conflicts, and open a second show, Livewire, on 21 January 2010 with Global and Millenium belts of its own. Nothing later survives.

Chaotic Wrestling Federation (CWF, Chaotic, 2003)

Predecessor to MCW (Motor City, 2007)
Sold and merged into a new company: the predecessor's last broadcast, on 3 April 2007, has its buyer announce the name Motor City Wrestling and a move to Detroit, and Dazz's roleplays say Faith, Priest and he came over when the buyer took on their contracts. The belts do not carry over: MCW's own champions pages show World, TV, Tag Team and Brutality belts of its own, the past World champions a 2008 roleplay lists (Jacob Laymon, Rayne Young, Annie Alvarez, Alcohol Paul and Whitecloud) include none of the predecessor's last champions, and Dazz calls Priest the predecessor's final World Heavyweight champion.
