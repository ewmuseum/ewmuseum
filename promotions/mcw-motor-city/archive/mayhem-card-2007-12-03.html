MCW (Motor City, 2007) — Card

Mayhem card, 3 December 2007

3 December 2007

MAYHEM 3RD DECEMBER 2007. The fallout from All Hallows Annihilation rolls back to Detroit. We have a new champion, but with the MCW Christmas Cracker Interactive show just announced, we have to say, how long will Rayne Young hang onto that belt.
LUKE TANNER Vs. SHITFACE MCKEE. Shitface did well in the Battle Royal...yep, its true. Tanner did exceptionally well...considering. But Jacob Laymon personally made this match, which means he more than likely has something up his sleeve...Executioner anyone?.
PRIEST vs. JONAS GRIFFIN vs. JARED BAKER. No one seems quite sure why this match was booked, but Priest will certainly have one eye over his shoulder. Griffin looked for all intents of purposes like he was going to be World Champion but isn’t, and Baker showed what he is capable off, lasting an eternity in the Battle Royal. Three men, one chance to make an impact.
RAYNE YOUNG & KAPLAN JAMES vs. WHITECLOUD & JOSHUA HAZE. Whitecloud isn’t happy with Rayne Young, that’s a stone cold certainty, and he will be loving the fact he gets the chance to face the World Champion tonight. A stellar performance from Haze at the PPV showed he was no slouch either. Young is one cloud nine having achieved the dream and James...well he just wants to tear someone’s head off. he might just get his chance o this evening.
ALCOHOL PAUL vs. BROTHER JAMES. Paul might have lost the title, but his spirit certainly remains as he tricked Jacob Laymon into a steel cage match down the line. Brother James is eerily reminiscent of Paul in his earlier years...this match could turn out to be nothing short of weird.
DARK MATCH - KIRSTA LEWIS VERSUS FAITH. A poor showing at the PPV from Kirsta compounded a bad night for her, the returning Faith will be one hell of a challenge. In a match that one another day could main event Mayhem, these two wildcats go at it once again.
DARK MATCH - MATT WARD vs. ELRICH vs. JEFF YOUNG. Two men return, another tries to prove he is worth his place in the MCW. With interactiveness just around the corner, which one will make his point tonight.
