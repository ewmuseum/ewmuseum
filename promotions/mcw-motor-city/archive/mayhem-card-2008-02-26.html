Mayhem card, 26 February 2008

26 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mayhem February 26th, 2008. Mayhem returns with a bang as the road to Dedication starts with a blockbuster event on the heels of one of the most explosive nights in MCW history. Titles and careers on in the line in what promises to be an epic night.
Main Event - World Championship - Annie Alvarez (c) Vs. Rayne Young. What happened after Rayne won the title, in an epic match against Jonas Griffin, was short of astonishing. As his partner, cashed in her Title opportunity, no fewer than a minute after his grueling match. Needless to say, Rayne didn't have much left in the tank, losing to Annie and holding the title for a record time of just THREE MINUTES! What will happen, when it'll be a fair fight, this time?
Jonas Griffin Vs. Angelica Jones Vs. Priest. "Ladies and Gentleman, as you know, just a month ago now one of our own passed on, and left his legacy behind for us to continue. Our brother Whitecloud left a real mark on this business, on that shall never be forgotten...especially now
Immediately after Purgatory, a tournament will begin that will involve the entire MCW roster. This tournament will conclude at the next MCW PPV in April which will be entitled quite simply...Dedication. The two individuals who compete in that final will be competing for a trophy quite simply named 'The Whitecloud Memorial Trophy'. This tournament will then take place every year around this time, culminating at the PPV which will be forever known as Dedication, and will be our equivalent of the WWE's Wrestlemania, and will be the MCW premier PPV.
The draw will be made immediately after Purgatory and the first Mayhem will be all the opening rounds for the tournament...that is all and thank you"
