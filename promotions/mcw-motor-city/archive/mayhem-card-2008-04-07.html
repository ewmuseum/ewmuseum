Mayhem card, 7 April 2008

Mayhem 7th April 2008. THE MCW EXPLODES OUT OF DETROIT AS WE MOVE TO WITHIN THE FINAL HURDLE TOWARDS DEDICATION. WILL PEOPLE ESCAPE INTACT FOR THE MASSIVE PPV, AND WILL NEW ALLIANCES BE FORMED, AS WELL AS BROKEN. FIND OUT, LIVE.
Main Event - Special Guest Ref Dazz - Ash Bombay Vs. Rayne Young. With Rayne still smarting from Bombay's inclusion, this match could have massive ramifications for the PPV main event as Ash and Rayne face off , with Dazz calling all the shots. Who will gain the momentum heading into Dedication.
Women's Title - Annie Alvarez (c) Vs. Angelica Jones. Annie will defend her Womens title against someone who is on a roll of late in MCW. It took a whole lot of hard work from Dazz to get past her in the Memorial Tournament...can Annie do the same and go home still holding three straps.
Brutality Title No 1 Contender - Greg Venom Vs. Midas. Two individuals who have set the MCW alight get the chance to face Annie Alvarez at Dedication for the Brutality title in this sure to be intense ladder match. Which of these two hungry superstars will claim the glory.
Contract On A Steel Cage Roof - Enzo Vs. Lance Conrad. With neither of these two recently impressing, Jacob Laymon has challenged them to show precisely how much they want to be in MCW. Only the winner will continue in the MCW...if there is a winner.
Dark Match - Meagan Collins Vs. Dante. Kicking off the show will be either the blooding of another female superstar, or the total and utter destruction of one, as the dark assassin kicks of his MCW career against long time associate of Angelica, Meagan Collins. Meagan has promised quite the show...Dante has promised bloodshed
