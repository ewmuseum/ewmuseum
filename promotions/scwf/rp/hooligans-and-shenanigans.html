Hooligans and Shenanigans

16th March 2004

Eric Cameron — WWA Insider

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“You're wrestling in a triple threat match with a pair of fellow ‘newcomers,’ Eric.” Eric Cameron, Phenomenon-extraordinaire, leaps up from his airline seat. With a beaming smile, he looks towards the Phenomenal Army's Media Consultant, who we'll just call “Tom,” because your narrator forgot his true name, and unless they mention it, how could I, or you, know that I'm wrong? Eric: “Awesome! Does that mean I'll get a good match out of it?” The Phenom beams downwards with a child's anticipation — but “Tom” just sadly shakes his head. With a sigh, Eric Cameron drops back down into his seat. Tom: “Their names are Draco and the Chosen One, and — I'm sorry, Eric. I don't think you're going to get much of anything in this promotion outside of the top of the card. The lower reaches are like a barren wasteland.” Cameron groans. Eric: “So we're zipping back to the good old US of A just so I can waste another night in nothingness?” With a look of inspiration, Cameron leaps back up. Eric: “The WWA hasn't called, have they?! Maybe they realized what happened — maybe they noticed their star is limbo — maybe — !” Tom solemnly shakes his head from side to side once more. Eric's head drops limply to his chest. Eric: “I'm sure they just haven't noticed yet. Maybe they'll watch the show on Friday, and they'll see me there, and — and — and they'll go, ‘Wait a second! There's a Phenomenon wrestling nobodies on that show! How did we let the loser promotion get HIM!?’ Yeah!” Tom shrugs, obviously unconvinced. Tom: “Anything can happen, Eric. From the looks of things, I guess this SCWF is declaring ‘war’ on the rest of the WWA, so I'm sure at least someone in the WWA will be watching the show. Maybe if you put on a show — who knows?” Eric nods enthusiastically. Eric: “Who knows indeed! I'll flip and I'll flop — and this Draco and the Chosen One — they'll be like sacrificial lambs! I'll feed on their mediocre heat like Dracula! And then after it takes me like five seconds to stomp them flat, the WWA will realize I somehow slipped through the cracks to the land of nobodies, and they'll leap on me, and they'll trade me for some loser that obviously got switched up with me. Like Brad Polaris. He belongs here, I belong there, damnit!” Tom's eyes squint down. Tom: “Wait — now it's a singles match. The Chosen One's out — it's just you and Draco.” Cameron lets loose a small groan, but doesn't allow himself to be too deflated. Eric: “Damnit — crushing them both at once would have been a lot more impressive. But I guess I can be more creative with just the —” Tom: “Wait — it's a triple threat again. Apparently the Chosen One's shown up.” Eric: “He has?” Tom: “That's what they're saying.” Eric: “The match changed twice in fifteen seconds?” Tom: “Shhhh.” Cameron shakes his head. Eric: “I don't care. It doesn't matter. I'm not a part of this promotion — they can slap my name on the SCWF roster all they want, but damnit — I'm a member of the WWA that's missing in action, and they better realize that.” Tom: “Singles match again.” Eric: “What!?” Tom: “Wait — triple — singles — triple thr—singles!” Eric: “What are you doing?” Tom: “I believe the Chosen One is hiding in a closet of the booking room, and leaping out and going ‘Boo!’ whenever they take him out of the match. Then he ducks back in when they add him back on.” Eric: “This is what I'm talking about! I'm a legend — a Hall of Famer — I'm far too old to be wrestling amateurs in a fed so totally unprofessional!” Cameron drops back down into his seat, scooping up a drink off the head of Bob the Robot, mixed monstrosity between a Sony television set and a trash compactor, along with long rope-arms, as his robotic compadré passes by. Bob the Robot: “Bob.” Eric stands back up in his seat. The stewardess tries to cry out that the “Fasten Seatbelts” sign is lit, but a drunken chimpanzee kicks her in the shin. Eric: “What's the update?” Tom: “The Chosen One is opening and closing the closet door like a madman now.” Eric: “Fantastic. Is Draco really that bad?” Tom: “Quite bad. He called you a hooligan, though.” Eric: “A hooligan?” Tom: “That's what he said.” Eric: “Tell him I called him a poopy-head.” Tom: “Will do.” Eric: “Good. And the update?” Tom: “The Chosen One has the closet door half-way open, but isn't coming out, so the SCWF is unsure of the status of the match.” Eric: “Bastards. Turn of the camera until we know what's going on.” The scene blinks off. Eric: “Morons.” And there goes the audio.
