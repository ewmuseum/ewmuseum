WWA Insider — article #1139

Movement

16th March 2004

Eric Cameron — WWA Insider

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
When last we joined our Phenomenal Heroes, Eric was threatening to urinate on the European Wrestling Alliance Championship. Nearly two weeks later, however, our world has changed drastically. Accepting his miserable new position in a decidedly third-rate feeder-fed on a backwards and obviously un-Democratic continent, Eric Cameron, one-half of the mighty Phenomenons, tag team legends from a land far more entertaining than your own, has shipped off to set un-Democratic continent. Here he has gone into some shindig the EWA ran, squashing what's his face, and gone into preparations for his impending stampede through the tiny promotion's ranks. And now: On a freight bound for nowhere, in a sea of dreams, they make their way. The large ship slides serenely through the day's fairly calm waters of the Atlantic Ocean. On deck, the giant mass covered by a huge black tarp remains almost entirely covered up. Except for the two giant trunks that, extending from beneath, have begun lobbing a member of the ship's crew back and forth in a game of elephant-sized catch. Thousands of miles above, a plane soars through the air. Within, entire families sit happily as they watch whatever insipid movie the airline decided to play this time. Normally there would not be anything out of the ordinary in that scene — but for the destination of all these families. They're going to Euro-Disney. To Euro-Disney I say. The legions of the elementary school branch of the Phenomenal Army have made their own move — under the guise of a happy Disney filled vacation, they will join their hero's in their quest to dominate the awful European promotion they have been sentenced to compete within. And several feet below those families, in a giant box, a recently freed dog urinates against the side of it. Within, an angry luchador holding a burlap sack swings his giant cane against the side repeatedly. The scene is repeated a hundred times over a hundred different methods of travel. The outcasts of the world have made their preparations, and it all means one thing — “The Phenomenal Army is in motion, Shane.” Eric Cameron grins madly down over the table set up in the four-foot wide shack they commandeered in the earliest days of the European invasion. Across from him, his gigantic partner Shane Stone stares up at him from his seat, a look of stupid devotion in his eyes. And near him, a messenger from that crappy fed they must have accidentally joined. Eric: “We have them coming from a hundred different directions, but within the next month, the entire Phenomenal Army will have relocated here to Europe-land, and when they do —” Cameron shakes his fist madly. Eric: “The European Wrestling Alliance will rue the day they offended the Phenomenons. They will RUE IT~!” Said EWA employee raises his hand awkwardly. “But Mr. Cameron —” The raging Phenom dismisses him with a quick wave of his hand. Eric: “It's only a matter of time now, Shane. The twats gave me a dark match — ME! — and I smacked the monkey around like a — like a —” Shane: “Bag of mucus?” Eric: “Sure. What was that guy's name again? The one I beat?” The EWA messenger glances down to a notebook. “I think —” Cameron ignores him once more. Eric: “Doesn't matter! Because I CRUSHED~! him. Just like I'm going to crush them all. To think they would put me into a match that isn't even televised — I've won more titles than most of these monkeys have pubic hairs — I'm a UWF legend!” Shane: “Yes we are.” Eric: “Exactly! We're Phenomenons — and they put me in a dark match, and Evil Generic Dead Guy #4234534534 gets the main event! BAH!!!!!!” Cameron flings something really heavy across the room. It shatters half a second later — the room's really small. Eric: “And to top it all off, they moved us to Europe! EUROPE!!!!!!” Again the messenger tries to raise his hand, but Eric completely ignores him. Eric: “I don't even know what country we're in now. It's all so frightening — people speaking languages I don't understand — naked old people everywhere —” Shane pulls his knees up to his chest and shivers. Eric: “Teeth — I've never seen teeth so horrible, my friends. Never. It's like an abomination — like they curse the very title of dentist within this atrocious land. And the underarm hair — you can't ride the trains without it rubbing against you everywhere. And trains! Who in the hell still rides the train, anyways? It's like this entire land is still stuck in the 19th century! We invented cars, dumbasses!” He shakes his head solemnly. Eric: “And how long will we be forced to remain here? How long, I ask? How long will it be before the World Wrestling Alliance realizes that a Star~! fell right into their lap from the land of unbridled creativity that is the UWF, and they somehow completely blew it and lost their next superstar to — to — to Europe-Land!” Cameron slams his hands onto the table, looking down solemnly. Eric: “I can't live like this. Already I can feel this place — drawing the life out of me. It's like we've entered the nexus of the universe — Europe, Europe, Europe! Who — who would possibly want to live here!? The uncertainty — the fear — the old people — it's heel on earth. I will not subject the Phenomenal Army to this for the next year. Something has to be done about it!” This time the messenger doesn't bother to raise his hand. “They moved the fed to New York.” And Cameron stares blankly. “You really should actually watch the shows you appear on, you know. That's what I came here to say — the EWA was closed, and you're now a member of the Squared Circle Wrestling Federation, based out of New York.” Cameron blinks. Eric: “Soooooo — they hired me, forced me to move to Europe, had me wrestle one match, and then MOVED THE FUDGING FED BACK TO NEW YORK!?!?!?!” EWA Messenger Man nods. Eric: “Oh. Shane, break him.” Cameron slips out of the shack, just a moment before the messenger creates a rather large hole in the side of it. Eric breathes in deep, and then hacks loudly. Eric: “They're sooooo going to pay for this. I'm going to pee on whoever made me move out here, too.” With his hands on his waist, Eric sticks his tongue out at the countryside. Eric: “God I hate this place.” As do we all, Eric. As do we all.
