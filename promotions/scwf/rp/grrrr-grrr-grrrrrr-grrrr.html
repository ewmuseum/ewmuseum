Grrrr. Grrr. Grrrrrr. GRRRR!

30th March 2004

Eric Cameron — WWA Insider

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens abruptly, flickering to life with the horrible, glaring sight of Eric Cameron's massive head sitting roughly four inches away from the camera. His eyes stare into your very soul, unblinking, hateful. And so the promo begins. Eric Cameron: “I hate the SCWF.” Apparently Eric nods, though you can't tell for sure from the small amount of his head that can be seen in the shot. Eric: “Absolutely, positively, totally hate it.” He thankfully backs up, now letting his face take on a more natural look as he regards one and all. Eric: “Do you know what they told me? They were booking their stupid little card, and they go — hey! Everyone in the SCWF is involved in a massive rumble match to determine who will get a shot at the World Championship. And I smiled, ladies and gentlemen. I smiled. “And then I got this~!” Cameron reaches down, grabbing a random sheet of paper. He gestures rapidly about, pointing at the paper and pretending like it has anything important on it, even though it's obviously blank. Eric: “And they tell me that every member of the SCWF is involved in the battle royal — BUT ME!” He shakes the paper frantically at the camera. Eric: “And it wasn't just that — no, no, no! Then they tell me that I have to — I have to do promo's anyways, as if I actually WAS in the match. Except that I'm NOT IN THE DAMN MATCH!!!” Cameron balls up the paper and tosses it away. Eric: “I disliked the SCWF before this. It depressed me. But this is the final straw. Eric Cameron does not do whatever you tell him to do. Eric Cameron is not your lackey. Eric Cameron will not do promo's when you leave him out of important matches!” Cameron makes a bizarre face at the camera. Eric: “We don't want to bother with giving you a spot in our big match on the upcoming card, but we DO want you to sell our product with appearances, daddy-o!” Cameron shakes his fist at the camera. Eric: “Well I say NAY~! Eric Cameron will not do promo's for the likes of you — unless on his own terms! So here's my damn promo!” He sets the camera down on a nearby counter and begins walking off. Eric: “Ladies and gentlemen, as of this moment, I am on strike. No more promo's until they send me to somewhere that my talent will actually be appreciated. So screw you, SCWF, you can film my kitchen for the next ten hours. Maybe if you keep watching, something will happen!” Cameron crosses his fingers and flashes a winning smile to the camera. Eric: “Here's hoping!” Eric just sort of — walks off. And the scene continues to film. And it continues to film. And it continues. And it continues. And somewhere along the line, they finally shut it off.
