A Plea For Help

16th March 2004

Eric Cameron — WWA Insider

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera abruptly flicks on, showing a shoddy, grainy, black-and-white image. In the lower left-hand corner resides a small logo labeling the entire shot as “Eric-Cam.” And indeed, in the center of the scene is the face of Eric Cameron. A man of international prestige, now tossed aside like yesterday's meat into an upstart training camp. He's not happy about that. Only the begining of his rabidly spiked red hair can be seen, and even then, his hair is just as devoid of color as the rest of the scene. Nothing can hide the look of pure horror on Eric's face, however. His voice comes through the scene as something only slightly above a whisper. Eric: “Folks … I don't know who is seeing this, but I am recording this as a message to anyone in the World Wrestling Alliance. I can only hope it reaches you before I … I …” He gulps audibly. Eric: “My name is Eric Cameron. For years now I have wrestled along side Shane Stone as the Phenomenons. We debuted as a team in March 1999 as part of the Global I. Wrestling Federation. The I. isn't important. Within months we had captured our first championships, picking up the World Tag Team Titles.” His head darts backwards, obviously thinking he heard footsteps. Eric: “Then we picked up the North American Tag Team Titles. In December of that year, we defended our championships in one of the most grueling matches in all of professional wrestling history. We scoured the world for the greatest tag team we could find, and we found them. And so the Venetian Home Rumble was brought to the world by us … a sado-masochistic brawl through the very hell that is the Christmas-time suburbs.” He shakes his head sadly. Eric: “We were cheated out of our titles, though that doesn't really matter. Our match was against ‘The Bastard’ Al Crud and Tom the Christmas Tree, but the cheating Tree swapped himself out for some rampaging psychotic pyromaniac. It was a frightful scene.” He bows his head. Eric: “Nevertheless, we quickly regained our titles after that incident. And we won the titles again and again and again and again and again.” Eric looks up, eyes blazing. Eric: “Seven times we held the GIWF/UWF Tag Team Titles. Inducted into the Hall of Fame at the end of 2000. Tag Team of the Year the same year. Runner's up the year before. Tag Team of the Year the year after. And the year after that. Stable of the Year. Mic-workers of the year. We won everything. Everything.” He gulps once more. Eric: “Our pedigree goes back years. We've won anything and everything we ever came close to. We are tag team legends, and still at the top of our game.” Eric eyes the camera solemnly. Eric: “It is why we came to the World Wrestling Alliance. To ply our trade against new competition. To make sure we … I … still had that fire to win everything. And so we took up Warr's offer, and I applied to the WWA, preparing for the next big challenge. “And that's when everything got screwed up.” He shakes his head. Eric: “They've kidnapped us, WWA. The evil mid-carders kidnapped us. They must have stolen our contract out of one of the WWA offices. I believe it was that Mark Harrington guy … he owns the WWA, doesn't he? I bet he must have realized the EWA sucked, so he had to get a star like me before could play with this SCWF thing he has going. “But I can't escape … they've taken the Phenomenons away from you, WWA. The stars~! that could have revitalized your tag team division … legends among legends that would arrive on your shores like an avenging angel and tickle one and all. “But we're not in the WWA now. I don't know where we are … all I know is that the SCWF has kidnapped us from where we belong, and now I'm stuck in nobody-hell. They're making me … they're making me wrestle jobbers, for the love of Rod!! JOBBERS!!!!” His head drops, and he waits a long moment to regain his composure. Eric: “If anyone in the WWA can hear this … please, the Phenomenal Army needs your help. I can't stay like this … I can't wrestle like this! They don't even have real soda here! I need Coca-Cola … I'm going to lose my mind if I drink one more can of Sam's Choice Cola, Rod damnit!!!!” He shakes violently. Eric: “Please … please! Help me … get this tape to the WWA offices … no intelligent businessman would let a superstar like me rot away in hell like this. They'll rescue me, and all will be right with the world. Please … on Friday, they're going to make me wrestle. I don't know if I can go through with it. But I'm scare of what they'll do if I don't. There's too many members of the Phenomenal Army they could hurt … just imagining Bob the Robot, broken to pieces, strewn across the landscape because I refused to wrestle that Draco guy who actually says ‘hooligan’ …” He looks up, tears in his eyes. Eric: “Please. Please. Please. Please help me. Please. I … I …” “Hey!” Eric: “No! They've found me! Get this tape to the WWA! They must be made aware of my plight! I need rescue! I need rescccuuuuuuuuuuueeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeee!” The camera crashes, and the scene ends.
