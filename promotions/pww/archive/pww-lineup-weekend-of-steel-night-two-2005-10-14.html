Lineup for Weekend of Steel (Night Two)

14 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PWW had one crazy moment after another during night one of the Weekend of Steel! And we'll be back for even more insanity Saturday night, October 30th, from the Kino Sports Complex in Yuma, Arizona! How will the wrestlers respond in the ring only one night after some hellacious battles? This will be warfare at it's toughest! PWW is proud to debut in Yuma with a huge TRIPLE MAIN EVENT! The first is the long awaited steel cage match between hated rivals "PHOENIX" ADAM LAZARUS and "THE WOLF" LARK FENRIZ! These two have fought since PWW's very first show. Lazarus holds a singles victory over Fenriz at Divide and Conquer, but now the battle shifts to within the unforgiving confines of a steel cage. Can Fenriz even this series up at one, or will Lazarus score another victory and send the Wolf even deeper into madness? This one will be bloody, brutal, and not for the weak at heart! The second of three main events will be MICHAEL "LINT" HURLEY's first PWW Arizona State title defense as he takes on FRANK KNIGHT one on one! Knight stole Hurley's title at Divide and Conquer and demanded a title match in exchange for giving the belt back. Hurley agreed, yet Knight still has his belt! Can the champ get it back in Yuma, or will we see Knight use his technical skills and mind games to claim the gold for good? And just announced by Chris Chronic...this match will have a surprise SPECIAL GUEST REFEREE to make sure the action is called right down the middle! Who will it be? Find out at night two of the Weekend of Steel! The third match in the triple main event will be a STEEL CHAIR DEATHMATCH for the PWW Outlaw title, and it will be a rematch of the finals of Violence Party 2K5! JUNYA KONTANI will challenge the undefeated MATTHEW YATES for the gold in a match where more chairs will be used than ever before! Regular steel chairs, chairs taped with light tubes, thumbtack chairs, chairs wrapped in barbed wire...you could see anything in this one! MIKE BISIGNANO proved to "TEXTBOOK" TATE MONROE at night one of the Weekend of Steel that he really could wrestle. Monroe demanded a return match and will get it in Yuma! Can he even the score with the Biz or will Biz make it two in a row? More steel! That's right, this match will see a steel LADDER make it's way into play as four men fight for a contract giving them any match of their choice in the next six months! With Chance McKenzie out of commission, Chris Chronic has decided to put his match contract he won at Violence Party up for grabs, hanging above the ring with only a ladder to reach it! Fighting for it will be FREDDY LOPEZ, "RAZORBLADE" ROB SHARPE, LUCIFER MAGNUSSON and "FIRESTARTER" ADRIAN TRUST! Will Trust be in any shape to wrestle after his brutal cage match with Matthew Yates? Can Sharpe get a second straight win, after defeating the Glam Rock Cop? And what would possibly happen if Magnusson and Lopez, the two biggest men on the PWW roster, squared off? This one will be wild! THE COOL KIDS have challenged SPARTAN and "MENACE" TIM KENNISON to a tag team match, and we'll see it in Yuma! Will Spartan and Kennison be able to work together and beat the men who spoiled their debut match? Chris Chronic introduced three men he called THE NEW TALENT at night one of the Weekend of Steel. WADE PASHION, "DOMINATOR" DON CAMERON, and OLLIE SAWYER will be in action in Yuma! Plus, newcomer SHANE BENTLEY will be in action as well! All this plus much more...who is the #1 contender to the PWW Arizona State title! What is the fallout from night one of the Weekend of Steel? What happens next? And what about that big announcement that's supposed to be coming this weekend? Find out in Yuma!
