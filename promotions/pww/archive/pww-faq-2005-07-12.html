PWW FAQ and Application, Version 1.0

PWW FAQ AND APP, VERSION 1.0 PRO WRESTLING WARS (PWW) I. What is this? Hey, I'm Pete K. and I've run several feds before that you may or may not know of. Five Star Wrestling, Rumble Pro, and I did the Superslam tournaments as well. I've been out of E-W for about a year now, but the itch has come back and so I'm looking to return in a limited basis with this new fed, called Pro Wrestling Wars. Anyways, this is an e-fed. Duh. The main idea behind it is that it's supposed to be fun. I want to have a fed that doesn't just follow the same predictable format every show, that doesn't follow all the "rules" we've gotten used to in e-wrestling or real wrestling. I want a show where you don't know what's going to happen each time you read it. I want to stress creativity and pushing the envelope. This won't just be an OMG WORKRATE~! fed or an OMG XTREME~! fed. I think the key to good e-wrestling is telling a good story. Without that, you won't have anything to draw you in and make you care about the results of matches other then "hey, this is the match my guy's in." I love all types of wrestling; old school, hardcore, high flying, technical, crash TV, anything. So hopefully PWW can encompass most everything. So yeah, there's a not-so-concise description of what I want PWW to be all about. II. Give me some more details, man! Okay, I'll do my best. First off, the cards will be summary style. Why? Because I want this fed to last, and the biggest reason feds close is because of burnout by whoever's running it. And I don't want that to happen. Besides, I think e-wrestling can be enjoyable even without fully written matches. So yes, the matches will be written summary style, and hopefully in a somewhat concise manner. Right now I don't plan on having a "regular" schedule for cards. I find that people miss deadlines anyways, so it's pointless to say "we're going to have a show every week" or whatever. Since PWW is an indy fed, we will have scheduled shows, and this schedule will most likely vary depending on how busy I am. Also, in the event of me having a little extra time on my hands, we will put out sporadic "Unscripted" shows that will be very quickly summarized, and that you will have only a little notice on, and whoever participates gets to be on the show (and more likely to win a match) It will be your job to RP every week there's a show. These RP's will be compiled into an RP show. You can also (and are strongly encouraged to) do oncards to advance storylines, and send in strats for your matches to help your character come across the way you want him to. The intended style of PWW is a "hybrid" fed. That means that all wrestling styles from strong-style to high flying to hardcore to technical are welcomed and everyone is put against everyone. There will be no divisions based on weight class or anything like that. Rules will be lenient, and some weapon use and craziness will be legal. III. Tell me more about the shows. Aside from the aforementioned RP show, there will be the usual Friday or Saturday night indy shows scheduled anywhere from one show a month to several. As mentioned above, there will also be occasional "Unscripted" shows, which are basically meant to be "smaller" indy shows thrown together for an "anytime, anywhere" mentality for PWW. These shows will not have matches booked ahead of time, but instead I will put the show together after everything is in. It will make it tough to strat, but be creative and do your best. Suggest something interesting with your character, you know? If I think it's cool, you know I'm going to use it. We'll see how it works. I want PWW to have an "anything can happen" feel to it when you read the cards. I want to break away from the e-w mold that says "okay, we're going to have a big PPV in six shows, so find a feud and do that until then." I want anything to be able to happen on any show. Titles are ALWAYS at stake, their will be no "we all know who is going to win going in" matches on PWW cards, hopefully. The indy shows will take place in various gyms, armories, etc. Crowd sizes will vary from under a hundred to several hundred for the bigger shows. IV. Give me all the "in character" info I need. Let's start out with the president of PWW, the one and only Chris Chronic, a character I have used in many of my feds. Chronic has worked for over a year to put this promotion together, and with his anonymous money marks he hopes to hit it big with PWW. Other staff for PWW include: -Jim Richards: Typical play by play guy. JR-ish. And by God, those are his initials, too. -Kent Freeman: Slightly heelish color guy, but not ridiculously so. -Ryan Allen: Ring announcer. -Shawn Kelly: Backstage interview guy. Comes from a background in radio and broadcasting, and doesn't know a lot about wrestling. Wants to use PWW as a stepping stone up in the world. Kind of a douche. Todd Grisham-like, I guess. -Lindsay Knoxx: The tremendously hot blonde backstage interview woman. -The three referees are Hank Freeman, Al Grant, and Doug Williams. And we'll add more people when we need them. Next up, let's talk titles. To start, we'll only have two, and the interesting thing is that hopefully they can be on the same level with each other. PWW Arizona State Title: PWW will be split into two branches, so to speak, and this is the first. This is more the "old school" title in that rules apply in regular matches, such as count outs, DQs, etc. Afterall breaking rules is only fun when their are rules to be broken, right? PWW Outlaw Title: This is the second branch, and in these matches anything goes. These matches are much more likely to have stips such as falls count anywhere, fans bring the weapons, or whatever. Tables, ladders, chairs, glass, fire, canes...anything WITHIN REASON (no pulling a gun on your opponent) is good to go here. But of course "Anything Goes" sounds better than "Anything within reason". Your wrestler can compete for either title, and go back and forth between divisions. But when competing for that title, he must abide by the rules (or lack thereof) of the belt. PWW headquarters is located in Tempe, Arizona. In the end, PWW is for people who want to have a good time in e-w, that can have fun whether they're feuding for a title or at the start of the show, don't get too stressed out or pissed about e-w, and want to try out some cool shit in PWW. Be creative, that's what it's all about. Ummm...anything else? I can't think of it. But if you have any questions just write to [detail withheld] and I'll do my best to answer them. ***IMPORTANT*** To sign up for our mailing list, go to http://groups.yahoo.com/group/pw_wars/ and join ASAP! ***IMPORTANT*** V. That being said, if you're still interested... Fill out this application and send it to [detail withheld] ASAP!
