Lineup for The Usual Suspects

7 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
***PWW PRESENTS: THE USUAL SUSPECTS! LIVE, NOVEMBER 26 FROM YUMA, AZ!*** PWW returns with a huge show in late November that's going to heat up the Kino Sports Complex in Yuma, AZ. PWW is back with a vengeance, and here's what's on this loaded card: The Weekend of Steel ended with ADRIAN TRUST dishing out a hideous beating to PWW Arizona State champ MICHAEL HURLEY, and FRANK KNIGHT beating down PWW newcomer and guest referee for one match, JOHN SHOCK! At The Usual Suspects, Trust and Knight will team up to take on Hurley and Shock in a grudge tornado tag team match! This one should be wild and out of control! Can Hurley exact revenge on Trust? How will Shock do in his PWW in-ring debut? Will Trust set somebody ablaze? What will Knight have up his sleeve? We'll find out! As announced last show, there will be a huge six-man contenders match at The Usual Suspects! We'll see a lineup featuring six of PWW's best in what is sure to be a heated match. The match will feature three "rewards" available to the competitors in this match. Number one, a shot at the PWW Arizona state title down the road. Two, a shot at Matthew Yates' PWW Outlaw title that very night. Three, the final slot in the NEO-International tournament. Everytime a fall is made, the man who makes it and the man who was pinned will leave the match. Who will compete in this match? FREDDY LOPEZ, "PHOENIX" ADAM LAZARUS, "THE WOLF" LARK FENRIZ, JUNYA KONTANI, LUCIFER MAGNUSSON, and the wild card of the match, THE SPARTAN! There are more issues in this one than can be covered in this short writeup. It should be wild, out of control, and with so much on the line ANYTHING could happen! MATTHEW YATES will defend his PWW Outlaw title against one of the above six man...he won't know who it will be until the last minute, so PWW officials have deemed that Yates will get to pick the stipulation for this match! Stay tuned, and we'll find out what it is! Want another match with a ton on the line? You've got it! The Usual Suspects will feature a four-corners elimination match with the winner getting the final spot in the PWW Junior Grand Prix tournament! MIKE BISIGNANO, TATE MONROE, SHANE BENTLEY, and TIM KENNISON will vie for the final spot! Can Bisignano keep his hot streak up? Can Monroe finally score a win over his nemesis? Who will earn the last spot for this prestigious tournament? FREDDY LOPEZ may have a big match later in the evening, but he will need to atone for his sins when he takes on the man he powerbombed onto concrete at the Weekend of Steel Night two, "RAZORBLADE" ROB SHARPE! Can Lopez win doing double duty? Will Sharpe get revenge? Two veterans will clash at The Usual Suspects when SCOTT STUNNING debuts against a member of The New Talent, "DOMINATOR" DON CAMERON! Finally, two more members of The New Talent team up when WADE PASHION and OLLIE SAWYER face THE COOL KIDS in tag team action! Pashion has raised the ire of Lucifer Magnusson...will the big man have Pashion in his sights in Yuma?
