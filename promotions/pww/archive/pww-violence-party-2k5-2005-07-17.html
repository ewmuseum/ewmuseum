PWW Presents: Violence Party 2K5

17 July 2005

PRO WRESTLING WARS PRESENTS: VIOLENCE PARTY 2K5! Today, Pro Wrestling Wars president Chris Chronic (yeah, the guy who brought you Five Star Wrestling, Rumble Pro, and Superslam) announced the first major show promoted under the PWW banner - a show that will bring talent from across the world to compete in Arizona in a one of a kind tournament. A show that will be called Violence Party 2K5. "I wanted to start PWW off with a bang, and so this will be our first major show," Chronic said. "It's going to be unbelievable." So what exactly is the idea behind Violence Party? "I wanted to do something that will show people from all over the country and the world what PWW is going to be about," Chronic said. "I don't know who's going to be in the tournament yet, or exactly how big it's going to be, but whatever the final format, it's going to be awesome." Chronic told us that innovative violence would be an important part of this tournament. "I'm looking for the most exciting, innovative, violent wrestlers working today," he said. "I'll be honest with you - if I'm watching a "hardcore" or "extreme" match, I don't want to see guys hitting each other with garbage can lids, and I don't want to see a guy sit there while the other guy busts open his forehead with a scissors. I want to see high flying moves, crazy stuff you've never seen before, head dropping suplexes, crazy limb twisting submissions, the works. I want to see a double stomp off a ladder through a table with a chair over the dude's head," Chronic said, laughing. Chronic will be looking for talent who not only are already committed to working for PWW, but wrestlers from many other companies as well. "It's a great chance for promotions to represent themselves and maybe win some new fans. Everybody wins. It should be a lot of fun." Chronic said he was looking to run a unique tournament. "It's going to be an event for wrestlers of all styles, as long as their cool and innovative and will pop the crowd...and me! Some weapons will be legal too. For instance, in the first round we might let them use tables and chairs. Second round, maybe we'll add ladders and kendo sticks in there. We'll keep upping the ante as the matches continue. I guarantee you that you're going to see so much cool stuff...it's what being a wrestling fan is all about. You just want to sit back, relax, and watch some guys beat the hell out of each other...then stand up here and there to yell "HOLY SHIT!" Violence Party 2K5 is rumored to be a two night event, taking place on August 26-27 (Friday and Saturday night), most likely in Tempe, Arizona. Chronic told us we'd hear much more once he finalizes details. "It's not a deathmatch tournament, it's not a high flying tournament...it's a tournament that embodies everything good about wrestling...the VIOLENCE." Make sure to check out groups.yahoo.com/group/pw_wars for more information on Pro Wrestling Wars! ===================================================================================================== OOC: Okay, so Violence Party 2K5 will take place in late August. Anyone is free to participate, whether you want to be a part of PWW long term, or if you just want to do this one tournament. I won't lie, part of the reason I'm doing this is to help bring some people in to PWW, so I hope people enjoy the tournament and want to stick around, whether its with that character or a brand new one. First off, make sure you sign up for our mailing list if you're going to apply. Go to groups.yahoo.com/group/pw_wars to sign up. As mentioned above, the idea behind this tournament is that it's just that...a Violence Party. I'm looking for handlers (and characters) who can come up with great creative, innovative ways to hurt and maim their opponents. That said, strats will be very important for this tournament. Come up with some creative spots using weapons or whatever. It should be a lot of fun. RP's will also be important. I plan on having some help in judging them, as I've found when doing tournaments it always helps to get a variety of opinions in rating roleplays. This also helps cover anybody who after the fact wants to say "well, I only lost to a PWW guy because that guy wants to put his own guys over" or something like that. My idea right now is for a two night tournament, with you guys doing an RP and a strat before each show. The shows will be summary action, much like Rumble Pro was. Check out the Rumble Pro Invitational (I'm sure you can find it on a RSPWF Google search) to see what that was like. Deadline for apps is August 1st. Just send any app that has all the necessary info on it, and a sample RP if I have no idea who you are. Also let me know what fed you want to represent in the tournament. (You can pick two if you want, but no more than that) Send all apps to [detail withheld] ASAP! In the meantime, if you have any questions or concerns, if you want to know more about PWW, or if you want to send me an app, just email me and I'll get back to you as soon as I can. I am also taking permanent PWW apps, and all PWW roster members will be able to be in this tournament, so let me know if you are applying for the tournament or to be a part of the main roster (and therefore be in the tournament as well). Once again, THE DEADLINE FOR APPS IS AUGUST 1ST!!!
