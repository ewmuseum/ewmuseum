Lineup for Weekend of Steel (Night One)

14 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://groups.yahoo.com/group/pw_wars/ --- ***PWW WEEKEND OF STEEL NIGHT ONE!!!*** PWW returns to action October 28th and 29th for the Weekend of Steel! Night one will take place from the National Guard Armory in Tempe, AZ, and night two will take place at the Kino Sports Complex in Yuma, AZ. The main event will be inside a steel cage with the PWW OUTLAW TITLE on the line as MATTHEW YATES defends against "FIRESTARTER" ADRIAN TRUST! This will no doubt be one of the bloodiest, sickest, craziest, most insane matches in PWW's short history! Put the kids to bed for this one, it's going to be out of control! Can Trust win the Outlaw title, or will Yates return from Mexico better than ever and remain undefeated in PWW? While Michael Hurley won the PWW Arizona State title at Divide and Conquer, there was no time for him to celebrate. Hurley was viciously assaulted by Freddy Lopez for the second time in the evening. It seems that everybody from Adrian Trust to Chance McKenzie to Frank Knight have Hurley in their sights. Meanwhile, the war between Adam Lazarus and Lark Fenriz rages on. Chris Chronic has scheduled a six man tag team match for night one of the Weekend of Steel that will see FREDDY LOPEZ, LARK FENRIZ, and CHANCE MCKENZIE take on MICHAEL HURLEY, ADAM LAZARUS, and their partner, just named by Chris Chronic...JUNYA KONTANI! Also of note is the fact that if Lopez, Fenriz, and McKenzie are able to win, whoever scores the winning fall will earn a shot at Hurley's title! While this may seem like a nice present from Chris Chronic, will it create more trouble between Lopez, Fenriz, and McKenzie? Find out at the Weekend of Steel in this huge grudge tag team match! Also scheduled for the Weekend of Steel is a debut singles match as THE SPARTAN takes on "THE MENACE" TIM KENNISON! Both men will be looking to impress! FRANK KNIGHT has been ordered to give back the PWW Arizona State title he stole from Michael Hurley at the conclusion of Divide and Conquer. Will Knight give the belt back? What will he be up to on night one of the Weekend of Steel? Plus, we'll see plenty more action with guys like GLAM ROCK COP, TATE MONROE, MIKE BISIGNANO, ROB SHARPE, and more of your PWW favorites! Don't miss it!
