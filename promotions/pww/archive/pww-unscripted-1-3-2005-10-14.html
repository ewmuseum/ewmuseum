PWW Unscripted 1.3

14 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://groups.yahoo.com/group/pw_wars/ ---- PWW Unscripted 1.3 (10/14/05, Tempe, AZ) The show started with Freddy Lopez coming out and talking about how he should be PWW Arizona State champion. He then had his match. 1) Freddy Lopez d. Mark Howard at 1:52 with the Iron Bomb. Next, we heard from Mike Bisignano. [We see Lindsay Knoxx standing in the backstage area wearing a black spaghetti strapped dress with microphone in hand. Standing next to her is Mike `The Biz? Bisignano, wearing a black t-shirt w/ ?PWW? in red across the front and a pair of breakaway track pants.] KNOXX: Lindsay Knoxx here and I?m joined by amazing rookie sensation, The Biz. Thanks for taking a moment to speak with me, Biz. BIZ: Sure thing, Lindsay. Anything for a pretty young thing such as yourself. [Lindsay blushes] So, what do you want to talk about? KNOXX: Well, I thought we could talk about what went down at Divide & Conquer ? namely your late entry by Chris Chronic into the T2 match. BIZ: Ah..the T2 match. Clearly Mr. Chronic thought he was doing me a FAVOR when he put me in that match but the way I see it, it?s just another instance of being stereotyped as a ?garbage hardcore style? wrestler. KNOXX: But Biz, if I recall correctly, didn?t you say at Divide & Conquer that you agreed with Chris Chronic about being a `hardcore? wrestler? BIZ: That IS correct but that doesn?t give the administration the right to pigeonhole someone. I mean Junya Kontani is a hardcore wrestler but he was in the Arizona tournament and so was Lark Fenriz and Freddy Lopez. But ya know what ? I?m not going to stand here bitching and moaning because that?s not what I?m all about. I?ve spent my entire life working for everything and my time in PWW will be no different. Just take a look at what I?ve been through thus far ? intense competition at the Violence Party and not to mention my amazing performance during the T2 match at Divide & Conquer. KNOXX: Well said. So what can we expect next from The Biz? BIZ: I?ve got a few things in mind but I think I?ll leave that one to be answered in more detail at Weekend of Steel. [The Biz walks out of frame, leaving Lindsay with a confused look on her face] KNOXX: Umm...I guess that?s it for me...back to ringside. [cut] 2) Mike Bisignano d. Jimmy Jones at 2:54 with the Delmar Dive frog splash. Next, we got to hear from Lark Fenriz. [A great way to relief huge amounts of stress is to randomly stab a solid oak table with a rather dull hunting knife. It's virtually harmless to anything other than the table, plus it doubles as a form of exercise. Not that the monster Lark Fenriz needs anymore exercise. The six foot seven, two hundred and seventy pound behemoth is quite fit for his size. It's that anger that gets him in trouble!] My mother always used to tell me that there was no luck, just certain people were born to succeed and others were doomed to failure. Even as a little kid, I had a hard time believing her. There was too much evidence surrounding me, even then, of people achieving things that they normally could not have done. As an adult, it is even _more_ apparent luck is all around us, helping some, hurting others. How else could you explain my defeat at Divide and Conquer? [Dressed in black jeans and a black wife beater, Lark takes his glance off the camera and focuses it on the dull hunting knife clutched in his right hand.] Anyone with even an ounce of brain matter knows that without _luck_, Adam Lazarus would not have achieved victory in the second round of the Arizona Title tournament. It was _luck_ that caused the referee to finally wake up. It was _luck_ that I happened to be chairing a chair, something that the referee did not luck. And it was certainly _luck_ that my little tug-of-war with the referee allowed to Lazarus to get to his feet and capitalize on my distraction. _BUT_... There is one nice thing about luck that never seems change. [Lark picks up the knife and slams it into the table with a thud. A sickening smile comes over his face.] It has a life expectancy. Eventually, at some point in time, it runs out and whoever has been that 'lucky guy' is up the creek without a razor wire canoe paddle. _That_ time is coming soon for you, Mr, Lazarus. You might have the entire ten people audience of the Pee Dubbya Dubbya on your side. You might even have your little friend, Mr. Chronic, pulling all the strings he can on your behalf. But you cannot escape the _facts_. [Lark nods, looking up from his toy, and now refocusing his glance on the camera.] Your luck will run out at some point, and when it does, Mr. Lazarus, I will be there, armed and ready... With thoughts of _pure unadulterated chaos_ dancing inside my head! [Evil laughter, as the camera zooms in on his face, which now carries a very serious, demonic look.] It shall be a very glorious and wonderful day, Mr, Lazaurus, when Lady Luck finally spites you! I just pray that you will remember something once you regain consciousness! [More evil, deranged laughter as Lark grabs the knife and begins stabbing the table at a mad pace.] 3) Lark Fenriz d. Josh Simmons at 3:10 via submission with the Sammael. Next, out came "Mr. Excellent" Brad Briscoe and "Mr. Popularity" Shane Pratt. These two kids from the PWW Academy now call themselves "The Cool Kids". 4) The Cool Kids d. Mark Ryan and Tim Slater at 3:45 after a fireman's carry/neckbreaker combo on Ryan. Next up, Adrian Trust came out and threatened violence on Matthew Yates at Weekend of Steel. 5) Adrian Trust d. Rodney Furman at 2:42 with the Inferno DDT. After the match... ["I Am Legend" by White Zombie blares through the building while the referee grabs Trust's hand as soon as he scrambles to his feet and thrusts it into the air.] RA: The winner of this match... "THE FIRESTARTER"... ADRIAN TRUST!! [A nice-sized heel pop for the number one contender to the Outlaw Title.] JR: Trust picking up another win here tonight against Rodney Furman and- HEY! [Trust yanks his hand back from the referee and suddenly shoves him as hard as he can, sending him tumbling through the ringropes and crashing to the floor! Boos all around as Trust walks over to his corner and picks up the bright red gasoline canister that he brought to the ring.] KF: Uh oh. JR: This is bad. We need to get some security down here right away. [Trust makes his way back over to the fallen youngster, who's trying to get up, and... CLANG! .... cracks him over the head with the metal canister!] JR: NO! Come on! Get this kid out of here! Get him away from this psycho! [Trust pops open the canister and turns it upside down, dousing Rodney Furman woth the clear liquid. He pours it all over him, even cover his head in it.] KF: Good God, Richards! I can smell the gasoline from here! Somebody get down here NOW! JR: He's reaching into his pocket! [Tossing the canister aside for now, Trust shoves a hand into his pocket, searching for his trusty Zippo. And, lo and behold, a shiny golden object is within his fist when he raises it into the air.] JR: Oh God... Rodney's in trouble here... this just is not right! Somebody's gotta help him! KF: I ain't going near that ring, but you're damn straight... SOMEBODY needs to get in there. JR: Trust now grabbing Rodney by th- [BIG FACE POP!] JR: CHRIS CHRONIC! The PWW President is here! [The former wrestling star charges down the aisle to ringside and grabs Rodney by the ankle, yanking him out of the ring under the bottom rope. Trust reaches through the ropes for him, but Chronic pulls the youngester halfwau up the aisle--and well out of Trust's reach--before helping him get to his feet.] JR: Yes! Chris Chronic just saved Rodney from a very serious injury at the hands of this maniac Adrian Trust. KF: Maniac is the one and only word to describe this guy. JR: Thank God Chronic had the balls to get down here. [Trust is screaming inaudibly at the PWW President. He grabs the top rope and shakes it viciously before running across the ring and demanding a microphone from the ring announcer.] JR: Trust has a few words here... [The Firestarter turns to face his boss, the man who's fined him out of every PWW paycheck thus far due to his outlandish and violent bevhaior.] AT: CHRONIC! I told you to NEVER get in my way again! I told you that once and I'm not going to say it one more time! CC: I'm the boss, Trust. Simple as that. AT: I don't care what your job title is. Now either send that kid back into this ring to get what's coming to him or come take this medicine yourself. CC: You know I'm not a wrestler anymore, idiot. But if you think I'm going to let you go around setting everyone on fire, you're dumber than you look. AT: I don't play by your rules, Chr- CC: Trust, don't you want to make money? I'll fine you your entire paycheck AGAIN. AT: I'm not doing this for the money, Chronic. And what I do to Matthew Yates at the Weekend of Steel won't be for the money, either. Or for any title. Or for any amount of prestige. That'll be just for the fun of it. The pure, unadulterated FUN of watching a man's flesh peel and melt and bubble before my eyes and in front of all these people. CC: These fines are going to turn into suspensions before you can blink, Trust. AT: You think suspending me will keep me out of your shows, Chronic? Think it'll keep me from sending your whole roster up in smoke? CC: I don't know...we'll see. AT: Fine me. Suspend me. Fire me. I'll never go away, Chornic. I do what I want. I set fire to whomever or whatever I want, whenever I want, and wherever I want. And you'd better just watch your back, Chronic. Otherwise, I'll see you, too... in Hell. [Trust grins a devilish, maniacal grin as "I Am Legend" blasts through the arena again. He and Chornic glare at each other as the PWW President backs slowly up the aisle, helping the injured Rodney walk as he does.] JR: What a showdown here on Unscripted. Trust is taking on the PWW President now! He's nuts! KF: I think Chronic is a little nuts himself to be taking on someone as obviously insane as Adrian Trust. JR: Maybe. But Chronic can hold his own, don't doubt that. Next up, we got to see Matthew Yates in action in a non-title match against the Glam Rock Cop! 6) Matthew Yates d. Glam Rock Cop via count out at 6:54. Good match, back and forth though dominated by Yates. The end comes after Yates dumps GRC outside, hits a Tope Con Giro, and then an XCelerator palm strike that knocks GRC out and gives Yates the count out victory. Adam Lazarus is out next to talk about his cage match against Lark Fenriz. But before he can speak, El Cubano Loco attacks him from behind. ECL challenges Lazarus to a mask vs. career match right now! 7) Adam Lazarus d. El Cubano Loco at 6:55 after applying the Lazarus Lock on ECL and getting him to tap out clean in the middle of the ring. ECL is gone from PWW and Lazarus gets a big win heading into Weekend of Steel, showing that he's not afraid of _anybody_. Now a word from Michael Hurley... Somebody fill me in here. I'm just dying for a little insight. [Michael Hurley, fresh off victory in the Arizona State title tournament, impatiently paces back and forth in a back hallway of the Tempe Armory. You'd think he'd be in jovial, festive mood but it's obvious from his agitated tone of voice and swift, hostile movements that he's far from it. There's no uncorked bottles of victory champagne, sweetly-scented Cuban cigars or other such celebratory fodder to be seen. There'll be time for that later. For now, still bedecked in his plain black boots and wrestling tights, sweat dripping from his battle-worn mug, he's on a quest for answers.] What is it about me? [Like a mechanicial soldier, his march is an endless and determined stomp. Wind him up and watch him go. Back and forth and back and forth and back and forth...] What is it about me that so sorely gets your ire? What in the world could I have possibly done to provoke all this feral aggression? [He could be talking to just about anyone here. Chance McKenzie. Adrian Trust. Freddy Lopez. Take your pick. They've all apparently got issues with "Lint".] Did somebody drug me and tattoo the words "I need my ass kicked!" on my back? Do I have a long-lost twin brother who goes around slaughtering kittens and throwing stones at crippled children in my name? I mean, honestly - what the hell is going on here? It'd be too easy to just dismiss your collective violent outbursts as pure and simple jealousy, because let's be honest - there's really nothing for you little savages to be jealous of. I'm little more than a soon-to-be past-his-prime wrestling instructor who's probabaly got more guts than brains. And, yes, I realize the whole self-effacing thing is annoying, but seriously - I've got nothing on those who have set their targets on me. Chance McKenzie? The guy's an olympic-calibre athlete with a healthy mean-streak. Adrian Trust is an absolute freak-of-nature who simply refuses to let pain register. And Freddy Lopez? Dude is - no exaggeration - twice my size. He'd completely dismantle me in a street fight. You slobbering pricks couldn't possibly be driven by jealousy, which leaves just one simple explanation: Clearly you're all just a bunch of licentious bullies who apparently think I'm an easy target. [The way he says the words "easy target" indicate that the phrase doesn't sit too well with him. Clearly he's annoyed with the notion, though his next words say elsewise.] And that's fine. Go ahead and keep that train of thought. Underestimate me. I Invite you to. Who knows, maybe you guys are right. Perhaps I _am_ an easy target. I mean - if you'll excuse the hunting metaphor - it's real simple to target your prey when you've got several hunters tracking it all at once. It's real simple to fire a clean shot off when your prey is all boxed in and confused and it's got no idea of where the next bullet is going to be darting in from. [Oh, is he ever annoyed. It's clear as day as he scoffs and angrily shakes his head.] You goddamn cowards - you never can do anything face-to-face, man-to-man. No - you're always hiding in the shadows with a trick stashed up your shady 'ol sleeves, whether it be a fireball to the face or a steel-chain to wrap around your meaty, gutless fist. You spineless no-account sissies probably still have your mothers test the temperature of your bowl of soup just so it's not _you_ who ends up with the scorched mouth. [The pacing, an endless display of supreme irritation, continues, puncuated by his manic, rapid-fire speech. He hops from one train-of-though to the next with nary a beat to seperate them.] And, you know, what _really_ gets me is that I _should_ be on cloud nine right about now. Despite all the extracirricular nonsense, this _was_ one of the greatest nights of my professional career. Winning this tournament was a vindication of sorts, a reaffirmation of why I do this, why I suffer through the endless stream of unscrupulous BS. Yet I can't even bask in the gratification of strapping that gold belt around my waist thanks to Frank Knight sneaking off with it like a thief in the night. And just what in the world is _your_ deal, "Uncle" Frank? Are you fascinated by the fact that you can see your lunatic reflection in the belt's shiny front plate? Have the voices that guide your unhinged existence suddenly decided to communicate with you through wrestling trophies? [Say hello to angry, sarcastic "Lint".] You know what, spare me the details - I really don't need to hear about how you were drooling all over yourself and howling at the moon when inspiration suddenly struck. The bottom line, Knight, is that that belt is mine and you can be certain that I'll be coming to reclaim it at Weekend of Steel. And as for the rest of you... [Finally he stops with the pacing, staring an angry hole through the camera.] You're all going to find out real quick what happens when you misjudge and underestimate your prey. You think I'm soft, you think I'm an easy target? Well I can guarantee that you'll soon be singing a different tune after your wounded and desperate prey strikes back. Don't say I didn't warn you. [Thusly, he storms off, abruptly ending this angsty little diatribe.] Preview of the Weekend of Steel, and we're out! See you at the shows!
