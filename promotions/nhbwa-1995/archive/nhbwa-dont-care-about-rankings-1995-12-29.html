"NHBWA - We don't care about fed rankings"

29 December 1995
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The No Holds Barred Wrestling Alliance is a Fed run by WLS 5.1. We have great handlers and they're active with trash talk. NHBWA reached 30+ wrestlers (2 per handler) in 2 months without much advertising.

Reading thru RSPWF, half of the posts are flames about who is the best Fed. Well, the NHBWA doesn't care about rankings. We like action! Rankings don't mean much to anyone, because all the feds in those lists are full and don't take in new wrestlers. People get upset about that their fed is ranked too low, or one is ranked too high.

I'm not telling you what fed is good and what fed is not so hot. I don't care. I think that the good ones are the ones:
1) The ones you're in 2) They are fun and you look forward to each card I don't know what the competition between each is all about. No one believes those rankings anyways. Everyone has their own favourites, and you ain't going to change their minds.

My unbiased opinion is that the NHBWA is the best (hehe ;->)!!! That's my story, and I'm stickin' with it.
[name withheld] NHBWA
