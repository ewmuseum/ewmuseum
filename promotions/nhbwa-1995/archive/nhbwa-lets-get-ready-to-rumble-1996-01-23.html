NHBWA — Pay-Per-View

"NHBWA - Let's Get Ready To Rumble PPV"

23 January 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The post opens with a block-letter ASCII banner reading "NHBWA" and "THE NO HOLDS BARRED WRESTLING ALLIANCE," not reproduced here.]

LIVE, FROM SKYDOME IN TORONTO, ONTARIO! — LET'S GET READY TO RUMBLE!!! — NHBWA PAY PER VIEW

Roger Axelen > Welcome everyone to a great NHBWA wrestling event! This is NHBWA Let's Get Ready to Rumble! There is a sellout crowd of 70,000 NHBWA wrestling fans in the Skydome, awaiting a great match! This is a Battle Royal, featuring all the talent in the NHBWA! This match has two rings, side-by-side, containing all 56 wrestlers, each trying to survive and be the last one left. Of course, each wrestler is eliminated when thrown over the top rope and lands on the floor!

Gordon Low > Well Roger, I feel that this is an important match for tag teams. If partners can help each other, the longer they last, the tougher they become. Some key wrestlers to watch: Milano, Bodybag Jones, any Theater of Pain member, Mortician, and the winner of the Internet Title Battle Royal, Scorpio!

Roger > To be honest though, any one could win this match. Even the newest wrestlers — Masked Mayhem 1 and Masked Mayhem 2, "The Deputy" K.K. Lawson, and The Marshall — threw their names in. LET'S GET READY TO RUMBLE!

Announcer > The second last competitor, from the T.O.P., VANQUISH and his partner, forming Total Destruction, and the NHBWA WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION, CONQUER!!!

The match officially begins as Conquer enters. First eliminated was Christian, double-teamed by Monsters Inc. and dumped by G-Zukie. Black Mamba was next, tossed by Missel and Black Panther. Steve "Skullcrusher" Collins missed a clothesline on Dark Angel and was back-body-dropped out. Rocket was sent flying by 2 Hip and Wretched Stock Broker. Face of Fear was snap-mared over by Dionysus. Flyin' Russ climbed the ropes but was slammed to the floor by Diamondback Cobra. Black Panther went out at the hands of Kurtis "Riverman" and Flash. Milano was pounded to the floor by the Masked Warrior and Warlock. The Marshal was eliminated by Tidal Wave. Dark Angel went out via Wierd and Tommy Ace. Jake Hansen was tossed by a choke slam throw from Bodybag Jones.

Jon "Rage" Stevens rolled Soul Taker over the ropes. Vanquish was atomic-dropped out by White Tiger. Riverman was dumped by Dionysus and KK Lawson. Scorpio was suplexed over by "Wrangler" Dutch Daniels and needed a stretcher — his career appeared in jeopardy. Tommy Ace went out via Frankie "Flyboy" Jackson. Warrior was eliminated by Rico Rock and Todd Powers. White Tiger himself was tossed by Embalmer and the Masked Mayhems. Dionysus was chucked out, stunned by 2 Hip and Argentin Rocker. Conquer — the champion himself — was eliminated next, dropkicked out by Argentin Rocker, leaving 36 wrestlers past the 35-minute mark.

G-Zukie was dumped by four or five wrestlers working together. Flash was DDTed and slammed out by Wierd. Masked Warrior went out via a roundhouse kick from 2 Hip. Johnny Banger was hurled out by Pitbull. Bolt held on after a whip from Raekwon but was finished off by "Boy Toy" Mike Baldwin. Shockwave was dropkicked out by Missel. Raekwon was monkey-flipped out by White Angel. Diamondback Cobra and Mortician both rolled out together, pulled down by Wretched Stock Broker — leaving 26 wrestlers past the 70-minute mark.

Punisher was scooped and slammed out by Akula and Seawolf. Rico Rock was dropped out by Warlock and G-Zilla. Embalmer was hiptossed out by Masked Mayhem 2. Seawolf was chucked out by "Boy Toy." Past the 80-minute mark, Argentin Rocker, Seawolf, 2 Hip and Wierd combined to lift G-Zilla out; White Angel went with him soon after, courtesy of Bodybag Jones — leaving 20. Frankie "Flyboy" Jackson was thrown out by Tidal Wave's Storm Warning. Masked Mayhem 2 was double-clotheslined out by Wierd and Mayhem. Ken Lee dumped the unsuspecting Mayhem and applied the Brain Geyser on Wierd, eliminating him moments later via Pitbull and Redwood. Argentin Rocker fell to "Wrangler" Dutch Daniels' Bull Slam.

Bodybag Jones, closing in on the 100-minute mark, was stunned by "Boy Toy" and thrown out by Tidal Wave and company, crashing onto the announcer's table. 2 Hip was busted open by Redwood's Redwood Axe and hiptossed out by Unlimited Fury. Akula was caught mid-air by Todd Powers and tossed. "Boy Toy" was foiled by Wretched Stock Broker's Forclosure and thrown into the crowd. Redwood himself fell when Warlock shook the ropes. This left ten: Warlock, KK Lawson, Wretched Stock Broker, Tidal Wave, Todd Powers, Missel, Ken Lee, "Wrangler," Pitbull, and Jon "Rage" Stevens.

Pitbull fell to KK Lawson's Lawmaker, who was immediately dropkicked out himself by Ken Lee. WSB went out to three Burnt Stake Piledrivers from Warlock. Jon "Rage" Stevens missed a top-rope splash on Missel and was thrown out. Tidal Wave reversed a Todd Powers/Warlock double-team, piledriving Todd and clotheslining him out. This left three T.O.P. members — Warlock, Ken Lee, and "Wrangler" Dutch Daniels — against Missel and Tidal Wave. T.O.P. eliminated Tidal Wave with a double-suplex after working over Missel and Tidal Wave together, but Missel turned the tide, dropkicking Dutch out for two-on-one.

Warlock and Ken Lee double-teamed Missel, but Missel ducked a double clothesline, hit both men with a flying clothesline, then eliminated Ken Lee with a slam. Down to two, Missel went to the top rope, but Warlock recovered and punched him down. Warlock climbed up and executed a Burnt Stake Piledriver (a tombstone piledriver) from the SECOND ROPE, then a second, regular Burnt Stake Piledriver, before tossing the helpless Missel over the ropes.

Announcer > WINNER OF THE FIRST NHBWA LET'S GET READY TO RUMBLE — WARLOCK!!! Roger > Wow. I am left speechless. Incredible. Unpredictable. What can I say but, what a great match as Warlock outlasts 55 wrestlers and wins at 2:27:14!
Gordon > Roger, that was the best match in NHBWA history. I can't believe what I just witnessed.
Roger > I'm sure the other Battle Royal PPV's will be as good as this one. Good night. All titles will be defended this NHBWA Inevitable (Thursday)! I will release the NHBWA magazine — I CAN'T BELIEVE HE SAID THAT — tomorrow, that includes match challenges and trash talk! Get in your trash talk and match requests!
