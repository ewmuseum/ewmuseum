[From the side walks a tall, very well built man. His hair is a short cropped brown, immaculately groomed. His clean shaven face gives him a look that decieves his true age. Attired in a pair of new jeans and a dress shirt with tie, he looks to be all business. Pausing the man looks around, his eyes falling upon Cross. Quickly walking over he extends a hand, surprising the
CTWA boss.]
MAN: "Sir? Mr. Cross?" CROSS: "Yes? Can I help you?" MAN: "It is an honor to meet you sir. I am Robert Kellan. I was directed towards yourself with an inquiry about possible work with the CTWA." [Cross looks at the man.] CROSS: "Nice to meet you Mr. Kellan, but unfortunately I think we're pretty stock filled of talent and I've never heard of you before so I can't go with a shot in the dark. No offense."
KELLAN: "Oh but sir I understand you have never heard of me. I have been working in Canada for the last few months on and off. With the Prairie
Wrestling Association. I under stand it may be a shot in the dark but all I am asking for is a shot sir, one simple chance to prove myself. I have been away so long it would be nice to finally get some work here, close to my home and family."
CROSS: "Well, I respect a family man and all but really, I don't think I can give you a shot."
[Fred begins walking away.] [Looking dejected Kellan begins to walk away himself, only stopped as the door opens and a young boy sprint through.]
BOY: "DADDY! DADDY!!" [Cross turns around, looking at the man with his son.] [Excited as can be the fair haired youngster leaps into the arms of Robert
Kellan. The big man scoops him up, cradling him in his arms.]
BOY: "So Daddy? Did the man give you a job like you said you were going to ask about?" [His face down, Kellan forces a smile, not wanting to disappoint his boy.] KELLAN: "Unfortunately son, things in life can't always work out as we plan. We will be fine though. Before you know it I will be wrestling all over the play and beating all those bad guys for you. It is just a matter of..."
[Cross interrupts.] CROSS: "What your father means is that he's wrestling the opening match of
CTWA: Legacy against 'Supreme' Seth King, congratulations Robert, you have a job."
[His eyes wide open in shock, barely holding back the joyous tears Kellan shoots out a hand, shaking that of Mr. Cross vigorously. The boy himself seems even mre excited, hugging his father tightly before hopping down and running back through the door, calling out for his mother. The boy gone, Kellan turns towards Cross, shaking his hand once more.]
KELLAN: "That was a mighty fine thing you did there Mr. Cross. I guarantee you will not regret it at all. Thank you sir, thank you so very much, you do not understand how much this means to me."
[Cross smiles.] CROSS: "It's okay, I hope you're ready because your the first man up in our press conference, so get ready."
[Cross steps through the curtain, entering the press conference.] KELLAN(Excitedly): "Yes sir! (to himself) Oh I can't believe this, finally...finally." [Kellan walks around, going out into the main lobby area to watch the press conference.] [We cut to a shot of Fred Cross the IIIrd going through the curtain, light bulbs flash as he enters, stepping up to the microphone.]
CROSS: "Thank you all for coming out today. [reporters sit down], let's get this started off right. The first match-up in CTWA history will pit just acquired Robert Kellan and 'Supreme' Seth King--one of our developmental talents. So, for your pleasure, I bring to you, Robert Kellan."
[Kellan looks surprised, not expecting to be called up. Looking around as the audience applauds, Kellan catches the eye of his wife, a beautiful blonde woman, his son standing beside her. Gaining strength in his smile, he makes his way to the podium, evenn if at a loss for words.]
KELLAN(clearing his throat): "Um, hello everybody here, thank you for...um coming. As you can tell I am at a loss...a loss for words."
[See what I mean?] KELLAN: "Um, I would just like to thank Mr. Cross for this opportunity(he looks quickly in his new employers direction). I would like to add that I wish
Mr. King the best of luck in our contest and hope to prove to Mr. Cross that I can be and will be a valuable employee of the CTWA."
[And cue awkward pause. Kellan looks around, sweat forming on his brow.] KELLAN: "Um...thank you." [Kellan stands there for some time before a reporter stands up. The man, a short balding fellow, wears a Texas Rangers baseball cap as he holds up his recorder.]
MAN: "Robert Kellan is it?" KELLAN: "Um...yes...yes that is correct sir." MAN: "I have never heard that name before in any wrestling ring around here. Do you actually have any experience here lad or are you a green rookie trying to make a name for himself just because he looks good?" [Kellan seems almost taken aback by the question but manages to compose himself.] KELLAN: "No sir, there is no fallacy about me. What you see is what you get. You are right though, I have not wrestled in this area, not for a while anyhow. I have been what you could call a...a journeyman. My latest work had me in Canada for several months on and off. I have worked mostly here and on the west coast."
[Another reporter stands up, this one a petite Oriental woman.] WOMAN: "Yes, Lisa Chung here with Lone Star Wrestling. What made you decide to come back here Mr. Kellan? Money? Fame? Opportunity?"
[This time Kellan seems completely confident in his answer, no even pausing in the last.] KELLAN: "Simple...they did." [Raising his finger, the crowd turns to see he is pointing at noen other than his young family. His wife seems almost embarrassed but the little boy, his son, seems to bask in it. Breaking away from his mother's grasp he rushed down the aisle between the chairs and hops up onto the podium and once again into his fathers arms.]
KELLAN: "This is why I am here Ms. Chung. My family comes first no matter what. When I heard Mr. Cross was starting up a promotion so close to home I could do nothing but jump at the chance. Besides I owe this big guy a fishing trip, don't I squirt."
[Giggling as his father ruffles his hair, the boy leans over and speaks into the microphone.]
BOY: "Me and my daddy are going to go fishing and then maybe even go ride our horsies." [Laughter ensues and Kellan simply smiles. No...not simply. This smile is like a portrait telling of the deep emotional bond he carries with his family. Readjusting his son he clears his throat.]
KELLAN: "So again, thank you everyone for letting me speak and especially thank you Mr. Cross for giving me this opportunity. Now if you will forgive me
I got to take Tommy here to this hole that has the best bass you've ever seen."
[The reporters clap as Kellan goes and takes a seat next to the podium, his son on his lap, Fred Cross the IIIrd comes from the side, smiling broadly and up to the microphone.]
CROSS: "Not bad, 'eh folks? It's good people like that which will help the
CTWA grow into the best regional federation this side of the Mississippi! Next up, we have Mr. Kellan's opponent, 'Supreme' Seth King."
[The reporters clap mildly as a young man steps from behind the curtain, he walks confidently, his black hair trimmed and cut nicely, his sideburns going down his face, he walks up, wearing a CTWA shirt, a pair of shorts, and a pair of sandals. He smiles as he goes to the podium.]
KING: [clapping] "Come on everyone, let's all give it up for Mr. Kellan." [King encourages the reporters to clap, which they do as Kellan looks on happily.] KING: "Let's all congratulate him on being the biggest fuckwad I've ever seen in my life."

[An eerie wolf's howl brings the press conference's sound system to life right before "Dig Up Her Bones" by the Misfits begins. The loud, fast rock music seems to clash with the tastes of the reporters as some even cover their ears.
A man with dark complexion and a sharp, angular face steps out from the back area. He smirks just barely, seeming to enjoy the discomfort of the reporters.
He rubs his nose on the back his hand. The bright lights don't agree with this man, he holds his head so as to let his dreadlocks shade his eyes from them.
Bare torso can be seen under his sleeveless leather jacket, nasty silver spikes jutting up from the shoulders of that same jacket. Mostly chrome plated, a flying V guitar rests comfortable across his back, it's black strap digging into his left shoulder. He steps up the podium as the music fades. Standing there a moment, silent, he lets the audience observe him as he observes them.
Like a predator looking for an opening to strike at as he leans over the stand, deepening the shadows over his face. He suddenly flicks the microphone's head causing everyone to jump in there seats. He chuckles before speaking in a smooth as silk voice that ranges somewhere in-between tenor and baritone.]
MURMUR: "Quod me nutrit, me destruit. That which feeds me, destroys me. So does the circle spin. Life begets death and death begets life... Can we turn down these lights?"
[Murmur looks around for a moment for anyone to object then scowls.] MURMUR: "Yeah, yeah, forget I asked. As I was saying,... Well, forget what I was saying. My point is that if think you've seen anything... Just you wait for sunset, mi amigo. Cause that sound you hear when things go bump in the night?
[He pauses, milking the dramatic effect. Letting all gathered hang on his words. Finally he whispers ever so softly into the microphone, with his head bowed, gripping the instrument tightly in his black nailed hand.]
MURMUR: "I made it." [Murmur cackles and backs off from the stand smiling sickly, a bright gleam of sharp teeth seen just under where the shadow from his hair ends. He winks at
Mr. Cross and whispers something in his direction as he passes. Cross looks slightly flustered, but it passes quickly. Murmur waves a sarcastic little flutter of his hand in the direction of the crowd before disappearing from the opposite side of where he entered earlier.]
