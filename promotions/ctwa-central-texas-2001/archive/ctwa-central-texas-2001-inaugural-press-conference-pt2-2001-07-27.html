CTWA Inagural Press Conference, Part 2

July 27, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The man known as Fred Cross the Third sits waiting patiently as the last wrestler exits the premises, having just finished his first press conference of his career in the CTWA. Fred then rises to his feet, just as he’s done all day long, introducing wrestlers and such. Finally he reaches the center podium once again, and speaks into the microphone to the few members of the press, who are pretty much just a few wrestling reporters, that sit awaiting the next wrestler to come out and address them. They’ll relay the news to the awaiting public, who likes to keep up with this sort of thing. Probably people like you, that are viewing this now, who’ll go to the Internet sites to see what happened. Anyway, back from my tangent, Mr. Cross III is standing at the podium, ready to address the people in the small room…)
Fred Cross III- thank you all for being so patient and I hope you’ve enjoyed yourselves thus far. Our next speaker is involved in one of the singles matches set to take place at the very first Legacy. His name is Erik Boyer, so…here he is.
(From the door located on the side of the room, steps a man dressed in a pair of khaki shorts, with a drawstring hanging down about half way. His upper body is attired in a blue Hawaiian shirt with white piranha’s making a systematic pattern, going up and down. His feet are dressed in brown leather sandals, and around his neck he wears a hemp necklace, completing his typical surfer, laid back, type façade. His face is a little tanned, just like his skin, but not too tanned. His hair is shaved almost to a crew cut type deal, with a very trim goatee located only on his chin. He smiles a smile of great white teeth, and takes his seat next to Mr. Cross, and after situating himself, shakes his hand.
Raising his eyebrow for a moment, before beginning, he starts in…) Boyer- hello, to all of you who know me, and to those of you who don’t, I say hello to you too. My name is Erik Boyer, as I’m sure Mr. Cross informed you, and I’m here to wrestle for the CTWA…as you know already. Now that I’ve gotten all of the blatantly obvious statements out of the way, I suppose we should start in with the questions.
(Every reporter located within the press room raises their hand, good question or otherwise. Boyer points to a slightly overweight man, who sits in the front row…)
Reporter- Hello, Fred Sans, Wrestlepro Magazine. I think some people may know you from your time in the AODWF, but for those at home and the readers around the world that don’t know you, could you please tell us a bit about yourself?
(Boyer shrugs and then nods…) Boyer- sure, I’m not sure how much you want to know, just a little background, or like a full history?
(Fred retorts…) Fred- just a little background about you and your career. (Boyer nods and leans forward in his chair, taking a sip out of the water that is ceremoniously placed on every podium from which a speech is made. He then prepares to answer the reporters question…)
Boyer- well, I started out in the I.W.A., which is a Boston based promotion, I hit it off there, tore through their ranks pretty quickly, until somewhere around my third or fourth week I won their coveted World Heavyweight
Championship…Boston Chapter. Heh, anyway, as it is to be expected, I was recruited by several of the bigger feds throughout the world. AODWF was the one that I chose to work for, which is based out of Montreal, Canada, but does regular national and world tours. But I had a falling out with the lead guy there, and their owner, and strings were quickly pulled, and just before I was to take the top spot, my ankle was "accidentally broken." I then went through a time of rehabilitation, and then ended up in Finest Sports Entertainment. I’m sure some of you have heard of it, but if not, it was at one time a premiere federation in the industry, but has since gone downhill. Apparently it was on its downward spiral, as in my first match I won the tag titles, and in my third
I won the World Title. I went back to AODWF for a short period of time, but it didn’t work out…and now I’m here. Is that extensive enough, or do you need more specific?
(Fred stares blankly at Boyer and blinks…) Fred- uh…no…that’ll be fine, Erik. Thanks. (Boyer sarcastically smiles…) Boyer- hey, no problem buddy. Next question, please. (A young woman stands and does her duty…) Woman Reporter- Uh, Rachel Carter of thesuplex.com , I was just wondering, what, if any are your plans for the future in this brand new federation, CTWA?
(Boyer shrugs and looks at Mr. Cross as he laughs…) Boyer- I guess whatever Mr. Cross has for me, is what I’ll be doing. I mean of course I have goals, but to be honest with you, my thoughts right now are to just maintain down here. I mean you have no idea how glad I am to be here…I had a pretty unproductive stint a while back and I’m just glad to be down here having fun, and trying to get back when it was a little simpler, you know?
I’ve had my share of the high life, my share of the fancy cars, and my share of the girls…I just want to have fun, and I just want to wrestle, that’s all. I mean I used to be uptight, high and mighty, and I really don’t want to be like that anymore…I’m NOT like that anymore. I did some pretty screwed up things in my life, and now I’m going to go back to my roots. A small federation, just looking for their piece of the wrestling world.
(The woman nods, but squints her eyes as if she’s not getting what she wants. She asks him…) Rachel- uhm…but you didn’t have any titles or people or anything in mind did you? (Boyer shrugs and looks around before answering…) Boyer- not really, I mean yeah winning a title would be a plus, but that’s not really what I’m all about right now. And there isn’t really anyone that
I can attack, simply because I have no idea who anyone is yet, and no one really knows who I am yet, at least I don’t think so. But I’m sure I’ll find myself a fight somewhere down the road, I always do. But no, I haven’t set any really solid goals yet…I’d love to be the one to represent this federation as the Southwest Champion, but hey, we’ll see where the cards fall, eh?
(Rachel nods and sits down and Boyer asks…) Boyer- are there any more questions? (The room is silent for a moment until an "OH!" is heard, and a teenage boy, probably around the age of eighteen or nineteen stands and begins to roll off his question…)
Kid- hi, uh, my name is Jared Jacobs from the Central Texas University newspaper. I’d just like to know…well…what are your thoughts on your first match up at legacy?
(Boyer lets out a gasp of air that could almost be considered a laugh, as his back and head roll backwards. He grins and answers…)
Boyer- hey, Jared, I really can’t say anything about my match at Legacy. I mean of course I think that I’ll win, but that goes without saying. If you don’t think you’ll win then you should get the hell out of the wrestling business. But then again, I’m not going to sit here and kick my opponent…
(Boyer stops, trying to think of his opponents name, Mr. Cross speaks up…) Mr. Cross- Murmur. (Boyer seems to remember this just as Mr. Cross mentions it, and he throws back his head as if to say "that’s right…")
Boyer- Murmur, that’s right. Yeah, I’m not gonna do the typical badass routine and say that "Oh, this rookie doesn’t have a shot, cause I’m so darn good, blah, blah, blah." Because I think that you all would see me saying that and just laugh. Everyone knows those types of guys are worth absolutely nothing in this business, unless they can truly back it up…and nineteen times out of twenty, they can’t. I’m just hoping that we put on a helluva match for the Waco crowd and steal the show. Of course, I think that I’ll be on top for the 1-2-3, but hey, so does Murmur, I’m sure. So…is that all?
(Mr. Cross nods, as there are no more hands raised in the air. Boyer stands and leans down to the microphone…)
Boyer- I’d just like to thank you for your time, and if I bored you with this conference, then oh well…sorry. [Turning to Mr. Cross…] Do I go out the same door I came in?
(Mr. Cross nods and Boyer nods back, shaking his hand once again. Boyer nods and gives a quick wave to the crowd as Mr. Cross introduces the next person…)
[Fred Cross the IIIrd gets up from his chair, and walks up to the podium, speaking in front of the few reporters in attendance.]
CROSS: "Well, we have a great line-up so far and what would Southern wrestling be without tag team wrestling?"
[Pause for effect.] CROSS: "The Funks, the Von Erichs, and countless others folks, so we plan on starting out with a bang, our first two tag team signees will go against each other."

H: "Well, I wouldn't go that far." [Nexus looks at Horizon questioningly.] H: [after a thoughtful pause] "Well, yeah, maybe I would." N: "Much better. Tequila Team 2000--you've been warned! SOLAR is the team of the future--hell, we're from outer space and [censored]! We can get all futuristic on your ass! Don't mess with us! And you best not be coming to the match drunk, or Horizon here will PERSONALLY raid your liquor cabinet!"
[Horizon nods emphatically, as Nexus sits back down.] [As the press is talking amongst themselves, Fred Cross III stands up and walks to his podium. He raises his hand to hush the audience before speaking.]
Cross: Ladies and gentlemen, I think that this has been by the far the most exciting press conference I've ever attended, and you can expect this kind of excitement and more on CTWA television. Speaking of excitement, CTWA is shaping up to have a stellar tag team division, and now I'd like to announce the first ever CTWA tag team match up! Two very different up and coming tag teams in the Texas area will be going head to head in what should be a breathtaking match-up as SOLAR takes on this team.. Tequila Team 2001!
[Somewhere, "Mexican Hat Dance" begins to play as some movement is seen behind the curtain. CTWA appearance as the trio approach the podium.] BM #1: Hola! Macho Borracho: Listen to me ese. Macho Borracho doesn't like to tell you about himself, because it would hurt too many people if ya know what

[Macho Borracho gets a suspicious look on his face before turning around and taking a look at #2. #2 smiles and nods as Borracho turns back toward the podium to answer the fat man's questions.]

So I did. I was so pleased with what I had taught him that I decided maybe he can be new Borracho and give him my mask. Today you know him as Borracho Mask #1. He really does have a lot of talent, but he is nothing compared to a Mexicano legend like myself. As for Borracho #2... Well... As they say in my homeland "Donde esta muy guapo pantalones". Now sit down, I will speak with you no more!
NEXT! Snobby Whore: Mr. Borracho, you say that tonight is all about the two men standing behind you, yet you won't let them speak. Why is that?
Borracho: Oh.. Oh.. let them speak you say. Okay chica, I let them speak... [Macho Borracho steps away from the podium and encourages his boys to speak for themselves.]
[Borracho Mask #1 steps forward, looking very sloppy and addresses the press, speaking around the California Speed Limit.]
BM #1: Hola y da la bienvenida a nuestra Conferencia de la Prensa. Sr. Borracho toma el cuidado bueno de nosotros, y yo creo que tenemos una oportunidad buena en este igual. Usted sabe, SOLAR realmente no tiene mucho talento en todo, y con nuestro drunkeness y nuestra habilidad, es por todas partes. Usted ve, yo no vine al CTWA a perder.
BM #2: No ese! I already took the vegetables to the zoo! But when I was a farmhand in Louisiana I took a carrot out on a date. Yo camino con carne, por favor?
BM #1: ¿Qué el fuck que usted hablan acerca de ese? Esto es una Conferencia de la Prensa usted moron de fucking. Usted es TAL fucking bebido es unbelivable. ¡El Castillo de las necesidades de Sexiness que nosotros hablar acerca de SOLAR para el godsake, y eso es lo que debemos hacer!
BM #2: Yes, ese, the sun is very bright in Texas. [Borracho Mask #1 just scratches his head and starts yelling at his partner.] BM #1: Escúcheme ese. Luchamos SOLAR, un ramo de ningún idiotaes del talento. ¡Pero esto es un debe ganar el igual! Necesitamos probarnos aquí en este nuevo alimentado. Nosotros no podemos tolerar el fracaso. Usted necesita permanecer enfocó mientras usted es bebido, como tengo razón ahora.
