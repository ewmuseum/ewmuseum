Okay, so your propaganda has gotten to me, how do I join? ============================================================  1. What is the purpose of the fed?  The purpose of the CTWA first and foremost if to have fun. I've been in the e-wrestling game for 3 years now and I've seen the best, and I've seen the worst. I won't claim to be either, really, but I do know that this is a game and games are supposed to be fun. What I'd like to succeed with this federation is for people to grow with their characters, to interact with each other, and to just have fun.
 I'd like to bring the regional, Southern feel into e-wrestling, I know there's other regional federations out there and I won't claim to be the one who will set the standard for the regional federations because really, that won't be my job, the handlers and participators in the fed(hopefully) will be the reason if we ever achieve the pinnacle of e-wrestling.
 I know wrestling, and I know e-wrestling and I hope to mix the styles well whenever the fed does open, now all we need is some handlers and some volunteers and we can get this show on the road.
 =========================================================== 2. Who runs this fed?(Fantasy)  The CTWA president is Fred Cross III. Fred was born in Waco, Texas in 1970 and even at the age of 30(nearing 31) he knows his way around the wrestling scene.
 Born to Patricia and Fred Cross Jr., Fred wasn't the richest kid, actually he was far from it. But, he did have a dream, at the age of 8 he started watching wrestling, seeing the spectacle that came with it he became enthralled with the business and at the age of 17 went to wrestling training school.
 At school he didn't work out too well, although Fred was in shape he just didn't seem to be able to have the athletic ability to become a wrestler, nonetheless while talking to his trainer--Dominic Kaiser--Fred showed his knowledge of what the 'fans' wanted and showed that he really did know what he was talking about when it came to wrestling.
 Dominic said Fred probably wouldn't make it as a wrestler but instead gave him a shot of helping out with the booking and recruiting for Kaiser's federation the 'Freelancers', who traveled around the entire state of Texas, putting on shows in small arenas. Fred helped out with booking and after a few years became the head man in charge backstage, over the years Fred has learned more and more of the business and now--with the backing of many 'silent' partners, he hopes to get his own off the ground, the CTWA.
=========================================================== 3. Who runs this fed(IRL)  That would be me, [name withheld]. I've handled in numerous federations the past few years and still handle in a few, I've worked with many great guys and met many really nice people over the 3 year span in my e-wrestling 'career', I've helped out with many federations, writing matches for many, many feds and have wanted to start my own up for a long time, and I feel now that this is the time.
 Hopefully, I'll succeed. I have the patience and I've been told I am an understanding and nice guy to talk to, so don't be afraid to approach me. :)
=========================================================== 4. What are the titles here?  We have three titles in the CTWA, they are as follows...  4.1. CTWA Southwest Championship The number one title in the Central Texas Wrestling Alliance, you're the 'Cream of the Crop' of the CTWA if you win this, the one everyone fights for.
 4.2. CTWA Central Texas Championship The second-tier title of the CTWA, not meant to be taken lightly, the man who takes this or holds this will be the 'next' big star of the federation most likely, with hard work you can lay your hands on this. =)
 4.3. CTWA Southwest Tag Team Championships The number one tag team in the CTWA will hold these belts, and hold them proudly. YAY! =========================================================== 5. What are the cards?  These are the cards of the CTWA...  5.1. CTWA: Legacy The bi-weekly show for the CTWA, Legacy will be the place to showcase your talent in the ring, on the mic(in front of people, atleast), push feuds, and showcase your angles in any way, shape, or form. We tour in small to medium sized arenas so don't expect huge audiences with a lot of pyros and a huge titantron or anything.
 5.2. CTWA: Recap and Relent R&R is the flash show for CTWA, it will come out weekly, giving you two shots at belittling your opponent in any way you see fit.
 5.3. CTWA Supercards CTWA is a small federation so we will only have 4 supercards a year, no pay per views for this federation.
=========================================================== 6. What do I do to accomplish my part in this fed?  The following three things are the keys to success in the CTWA... 6.1. Roleplays  Roleplays, or flashes as some call them are just a situation with your character, you can use these as a venture of establishing your character, your feuds, your angles, or just to talk trash about your opponent, whichever you'd like. This accounts for the most of the winning percentage in CTWA.
 6.2. Strats Strats, or Strategies as they are called is how your wrestler will perform, his mindset, the moves you'd like to see him/her do, their defense, and possible finishers of any kinds. These are good to do but will only help you out if it's a very close match and I look to strats to determine the winner, it does make it much easier to write for the character and lets you see what YOU want.
 6.3. Angles Angles, feuds, whatever you'd like to call them is a big part of the CTWA. Without angles, how can a fed survive? It's good to be active and work out stuff with your fellow handlers, no matter what, don't be afraid to ask me or anyone else about openings for angles or feuds.
============================================================= 7. Booking Policy  The CTWA is a booked federation, which means that the results are based on strats/roleplays/angles, and in the end, everyone loses if they stay in the federation long enough. If you have a question on why you lost, ask me but please don't jump down my throat and say I have bad judgment, not to say some people don't make mistakes but I will judge accordingly to how I think the results should go.
