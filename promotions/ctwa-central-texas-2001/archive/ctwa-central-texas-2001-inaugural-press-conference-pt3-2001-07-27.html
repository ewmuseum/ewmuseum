CTWA Inagural Press Conference, FINALE

July 27, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CROSS: "Well folks, we have come to the last match of the evening, which of course means that now I shall announce the inaugural Main Event of CTWA:
Legacy. It will pit two tough Texans, JJ 'Bull' Daniels and Jebediah Miller. The first person up of these two great competitors is Jebediah
Miller. Mr. Miller is from Ft. Worth, Texas and has the mean streak of a cat in heat, he plows through people with his astonishing lariats and is definitely an important piece of the CTWA puzzle, folks I give to you,
Jebediah Miller!" [Miller emerges from the curtains, no emotion showing on his face. He towers over the much smaller Fred Cross, and as Cross quickly scurries away in fear, Miller grunts at him, and smiles. Miller smells like a mix of smoke and alcohol and he looks like he has just got up out of bed. His eyes are red, his hair loosely tied back in a ponytail. He wears his black “Fucking
Hostile” T-Shirt and some torn up blue jeans that look like they were just recovered from the Salvation Army. Jebediah sits down on the wood chair, placed in front of the desk, pushes some papers that are scattered on there, off the table and lays his head down to rest, falling in to what appears to be a deep sleep.
Cross looks up and sees his interviewee almost passed out and quickly rushes to his aid, whispering in his ear, his facing getting red, as he is obviously embarrassed and annoyed. Miller looks up in confusion.]
JEBEDIAH MILLER: “Wut? Wut do ya want ya stupit bastard? Let mah sleep in mah peace…and if Ah catch ya again tryin’ ta start somethin’ with mah,
Imma gonna tear ya limb from limb. All Ah wanted was some sleep and Ah git some nosey little fellow with no goddamned respect. Git, ya hear? Git the fuck away, I can handle this here conference mahself, ya stupit little bitch.”
[Cross’ facial expression seems to be the “I am the one keeping you employed” face. Miller just flips him the bird and laughs. He turns to the audience, and just starts rambling to himself as reporters try and ask him questions, he just ignores them and brags about himself.]
MILLER: “Lemme tell ya’ll a bit about mahself, if ya’ll ain’t interested, git like this fucking city slicker ova’ hea.”
[He points at Cross who has now gotten up, and started apologizing to different members in the audience. Miller just laughs. Reporters still and try and ask questions as he interrupts again.]
MILLER: “Have yah come outta tha’ closet yit Mistah. Cross? Or are yah still tryin’ to keep it hidden? Ah think Mistah. Cross and ya’ll are some of tha saddest and idiotic people, this Texan has eva mit. So why don’t ya’ll just shut yah traps an’ listen to mah talk.”
[The people in attendance quiet down as Miller looks over everyone that sits before him.] MILLER: “To ya’ll who have heard of mah, an’ scene what Ah can do, fuck ya and ya Mutha. And to those of ya’ll who haven’t had the pleasure of meetin’ me yit, mah name is the “Straight Flush” Jebediah Miller. Jeb fa’short.
But I don’t give a flyin’ fuck what ya call me, because frankly, none of ya’ll matta to me. Ya’ll are just mindless pieces of garbage that ask professionals like mah tha’ questions. Pathetic I say, pathetic.”
[Whispers from all directions are audible, angry ones at that.] MILLER: “Ah thought I told yah to shut ya’ damn mouths!” [Miller yells to the entire crowd, and they suddenly become deathly quiet, no one saying a word, obviously intimidated by the large man in front of them. Miller just laughs to himself, an evil laugh at that. He has them under his control now, and that’s right where he wants them, and right where
Cross doesn’t.]
MILLER: “Ya’ll want the questions, well, if yah shut ya’ damn faces, then yah might git some of tha’ damn answers that yah want so badly. But first, lemme tell yah’ what I rememba ‘bout mahself. Ah was born an’ raised in
Ft.
Worth, Texas. My pride an’ joy. I grew up in that place, or so Ah think. I gots no family, no one to care ‘bout. I gots no home, and almost nah money.
And the money Ah do git, it’s fo’ mah friend and spritual leada’, Mistah. Jack Daniels.” [Miller chuckles a bit and lets out an enormous belch. He chuckles more.] MILLER: “Now Ah see I got a match for mah to take care of at CTWA Legacy. And about this fed, CTWA. Don’t ask mah, why I am hea. Ah can’t answer tha’ question mahself, because Ah dunno an answer. So if any of ya’ll were wonderin’ that, then ya’ll can shut yer mouths da’ rest of da’ time, yah hear?”
[He strokes his goatee.] MILLER: “Fucking Hostile they call mah. And that’s what Ah am. Fucking Hostile. I take nah prisoners, I make nah friends. I’m ah fuckin’ human wreckin’ machine. An’ at Legacy, that’s exactly what Ah intend to do.
Hey, Cross, who tha’ fuck is mah opponent again, ya fuckin’ horses ass?” [Cross looks up and is apparently enraged, probably by Miller’s mouth. He walks over, whispering something into his ear. He smiles and returns to his seat. He folds his arms over and puts his feet up onto the chair obviously relaxed. Miller does his best Cross impression.]
MILLER: "Well, first off, you need to tone down with the language, Miller, and second off, your opponent is JJ 'Bull' Daniels, a man in the same league as you in your wrestling styles."
[The crowd chuckles a bit at his impression.] MILLER: “First off, did I tell ya’ll you could laugh at mah impression. Fuck nah I didn’t, so shut tha’ fuck up. Second off Cross, if yah don’t want mah to swear, then put some fackin’ ear plugs on yah fuckin’ ass fo’ ah head, and shut yah fuckin’ mouth already. Cross, yah are gonna learn who not to fuck wit yah stupid bastard.
[Cross is stunned at Miller’s response.] CROSS: "Look, just get your thing over with so we don't have to hear you any longer, be glad I even gave you a job, and be even _MORE_ glad that I put your ass in the main event."
[Miller again just chuckles at Cross’s measly attempts at breaking through his skull.] MILLER: “Do Ah look like Ah give a shiet about da’ damn money ya fuckin’ ignorant bastard? If it looks like Ah do, ya’ have yo’ ass shoved furtha’ up ya’ ass than Ah thought. And yah know damn well why Ah’m in da’ Main
Event, and da’ otha’ no talent garbage in this fed ain’t. It’s because Ah am the meanest, da’ baddest sunovabitch in this goddamned promotion. Ah know it.
Ya’ll out there know it, and Cross obviously ya’know it.” CROSS: "Well, quit with your yappin' please, and let me point something out, I _WORKED_ for what I do, let these nice reporters ask you some questions and let's get this show on the road, Mr. Miller."
[Miller doesn’t even let out a breath.] MILLER: “Tha’ name is Jebediah, not Mistah, ya’ worthless excuse fo’ ah human being. Mistah is for tha’ weak and tha’ fortunate, and mah friend, that ain’t me. So Ah’ll tell ya for the last time, tha’ name is Jebediah,
Mistah Cross, and don’t ya’ foget it.”
[Under his breath, but easily audible Miller adds…] MILLER: “Stupit fuckin’ know it all fedheads. They think they is some kinda god o’ somethin’.”
CROSS: "Well, I didn't say 'Mistah', I said 'Mr.' and I'll keep calling you
Mr. all I want, because this is _MY_ federation and unless you wanna go back on the streets, fighting hobos for change, you will respect me, that's for sure. And if you don't prove yourself worthy in this main event, it'll be the last one you're ever in, you'll be curtain jerkin' every moment after that."
[Miller laughs to himself, almost falling asleep at Cross’s “speech”.] MILLER: “Don’t yah know how ta’ shut the fuck up Mistah Cross? I don’t care what tha’ fuck you own an’ what yah don’t because it doesn’t matta much to mah. Yah think that your company and even yo’ have impressed Jebediah
Miller? Fuck na’ yo’ fed is full o’ bastards just like yahself, that can talk tha’ talk but can neva’ walk the fuckin’ walk. An’ go back ta tha’ street fights? Ah would if I could, but tha’ money hea’ is too good to pass up. An’ about tha’ Main Event, who else would you have put into that Main
Event Mistah Cross? Yah got nothin’ but me, nothin’.”
[Miller points to his “Fucking Hostile” T-Shirt and laughs out loud.] CROSS: [sighs] "I'm not wasting my time with you any longer, spew your stuff and quit wasting our time."
[Miller laughs.] MILLER: “Yah got nothin’ Cross, nothin’. So shut yah damn mouth before someone else’s feelins’ gets hurt.”
[He points to his shirt again and laughs.] MILLER: “So he tells mah, that my opponent is some piece of shiet named, JJ
“Bull” Daniels. Mah response to this being my opponent is quite simple.
Ah’m gonna break yah in two and feed Jack Daniels tha’ scraps.” Ah mean what I say. “Bullshit” o’ whateva’ yo’ name is, ya’ ‘bout to be broken in two. Yah about to be stomped into wat’ I like to call oblivion. Mah goal in this fed ain’t tah lose. Mah goal is tah kick yah ass and make yah bleed and Legacy is gonna be mah kickoff show, if yah know what Ah mean.”
[Miller points to audience.] MILLER: Ah gots no time for ya’lls questions today, ya’ll can save them for mah opponent Mistah Bullshit an’ Mistah Cross. Which reminds mah. Mistah
Cross, what makes yah think this operation is gonna last huh? What makes yah think this shithole is gonna make those cheap bastards in tha’ audience proud huh? Yah think yah got the ingredients fo’ ah good fed, Mistah Cross?
Well, Ah may not be tha’ most intelligent man on this god fo’ saken planet, but Ah sure as hell can smell ah shithole when Ah see one. An’ with that…”
[Fred Cross cuts him off.] CROSS: "Hey, if you don't like it, don't stay, don't let the door hit ya' where the good lord split ya'. How do I know it will succeed? Because I'm not a quitter and if you wanna quit on me already, go right ahead, but if you don't, and wanna grow a set and stay with the company and help it flourish--which I do believe you can do, no matter if I like you or not--then do so. Until then, shut your mouth and speak when spoken to."
[Miller glares at Cross.] MILLER: “If tha’ loca bar didn’t close in an’ hour, Ah’d slit ya’ fuckin’ throat for talkin’ to mah that way. Ah guess that “Bullshit” will just hafta stand in yo’ place this Legacy, eh? See ya around ya punk bitch.”
[With that, Miller shoves the rest of the papers on the table off onto the floor as reporters scream for answers, but Miller just laughs and heads to the back, Cross obviously pissed off as hell with his wrestler.]
[Fred Cross the IIIrd walks up to the podium, a little perturbed from Jebediah Miller's Press Conference spiel, Fred speaks.] CROSS: "Well, our final contestant and final member of the press conference today is the other man in the CTWA: Legacy Inaugral main event, JJ
'Bull' Daniels." (Suddenly the curtain flies open and out comes a 6 ft. 2 in. man wearing blue jeans, cowboy boots, a black T-Shirt and a black leather jacket with short buzz cut light brown hair. He walks up to a reporter sitting in a chair and...KICKS HIM OVER AND OUT OF THE CHAIR!!)
*EEEEEEEEEEK!!!* (Reporters begin to scatter as the man grabs a chair and begins swinging it around sending people running everywhere. The man spits at the floor and walks up to the podium and...SHOVES FRED CROSS THE
IIIrd DOWN!!) CROSS: WHAT THE HECK ARE YOU DOING DANIELS?!! (JJ Daniels spits at Cross's shoes) DANIELS: Shut your mouth you Blue Blooded COW MOLESTING HORSE RAPING BASTARD!! (Daniels spits at the floor again and then looks at the scattered reporters) DANIELS: ARE YOU STUPID WOP DEIGOS GONNA ASK ME SOMETHING OR NOT?!! (The reporters all look at each other skeptically and then one of the reporters raises his hand)
REPORTER: I have a question... DANIELS: Well aren't YOU special!! ASK YOUR QUESTION YOU POLISH SAUSAGE SWALLOWING RETARDED CRIPPLED PIG RAPER!! (The Reporter pulls a face) REPORTER: Mr. Daniels I'm not crippled or retarded or whatever a Polish
Sausage Swallower is...
(Daniels leaps over to the reporter and grabs him by his hair) REPORTER: AIEEEEEEEE!!!!!!! *CRASH* (Daniels RAMS the reporter's head into the podium and the reporter falls backwards unconcious. Daniels goes back to the podium.)
DANIELS: Well my answer to that question is YES! (Daniels stands there staring at everyone. The reporters look around. A female reporter raises her hand.)
LADY REPORTER: What...What do you mean yes? (Daniels cuts her an angry look) DANIELS: As I was saying you NO SHAVING YOUR LEGS HAIRY ARMPIT LESBIAN FEMINIST CHILD MOLESTER!!!! Yes...YES JEBEDIAH MILLER MOLESTS CHILDREN!! (The Lady Reporter looks upset) LADY REPORTER: Mr. Daniels you can scare everyone with your violence but I will not STAND for you calling me a Child Molester and..
DANIELS: What's your name you uppity lip smacker? (The lady looks around then puts on a brave pose) LADY REPORTER: My name is Jane ... DANIELS: That's Good Enough...Jane...Jebidiah Miller broke into your house last night and had ILLEGAL CRIMINAL SEX WITH YOUR KID!!
JANE: What?!! DANIELS: AND YOU VIDEO TAPED THE WHOLE THING YOU SORRY NO LEG SHAVING FEMINIST!!! (Daniels...SPITS A GALLON OF SPIT ON JANE'S FACE) JANE: EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEKKKKKKKKK!!! DANIELS: Oh quit your crying!! Did your kid cry while Miller was having his criminal ways with it?
JANE: (BEEP) YOU YOU BASTARD!!!! DANIELS: Go sell it to the ladies you lip smacker... JANE: I NEVER!! (Jane storms away) DANIELS: That was fun...WHO ELSE HAS A QUESTION!!! (A man stands up) MAN: Mr. Daniels I'm Kent Carlson Reporter with the Daily... DANIELS: You're a Cum Guzzling HOMO!! CARLSON: Ah..S-sure...Anyways...I take it from the cut of your jib that you're a rule breaker! A...A bad guy...
DANIELS: WOAH WOAH WOAH! Hold up here.. CARLSON: Yes? DANIELS: What do you mean the cut of my jib? What is that? You checking me out? You think I'm one of..."Your" Kind?
CARLSON: Ah... DANIELS: You DISGUSTING PERVERT!! (Daniels leaps over to Carlson and CLOTHESLINES HIM OUT OF HIS SHOES!!) CARLSON: OOF!! DANIELS: DON'T CHECK OUT MY JIB YOU QUEER!!! (Daniels begins stomping on the back of Carlson's head and Cross gets on the podium.) CROSS: DANIELS THAT IS ENOUGH!! (Daniels looks over at Cross) CROSS: We've had enough craziness for one day just answer the questions and... (Daniels walks over to Cross) DANIELS: Shut your mouth Cow Molestor... (Daniels grabs Cross's pants and gives Cross a wedgie) CROSS: OOF!!! (Daniels then shoves Cross down and shakes his head) DANIELS: The nerve of that cow molestor.... (A male reporter raises his hand) MAN: Mr. Daniels I'm Les Nessman and.. DANIELS: You're.. NESSMAN: What will you do when.. DANIELS: Hey... NESSMAN: Jebediah Miller uses rulebreaking tactics that you might use... DANIELS: Wait... NESSMAN: Against you? What will you do against that? DANIELS: Why You Slight Little BRITISH BASTARD!! NESSMAN: Just answer the question Mr. Daniels. DANIELS: Oh..You think you're all smark and uppity and stuff...You've never stuck your hand into the water and had a Catfish the size of your mother bite it and you yanked it out of the water and beat that catfish to death so you can eat have you?
NESSMAN: You're anctics are all fun but answer the question. (Daniels pulls out some scissors) DANIELS: I'll answer alright... (Daniels leaps ontop of Nessman and tackles him down and then... BEGINS
STABBING NESSMAN'S ARM WITH THE SCISSORS)
NESSMAN: OH GOD! OH GOD HELP ME! OWWWWWWWWWWW!!! DANIELS: Now..To Answer Your question.. (Daniels keeps stabbing away as people run in panic) NESSMAN: PLEASE GOD HELP ME!! DANIELS: If Jebediah Molested Your Kids Last Night Miller wants to pull the rough stuff with me..I'll just pull the rough stuff on him..Seems to me that the guy with the rougher stuff...
NESSMAN: PLEASE STOP STABBING MY ARM!! AIEEEEEEEE!!! DANIELS: Crybaby..Like I was saying the guy with the rougher stuff will come out on top and win and so forth...
(Daniels stabs at Nessman's arm one last time then drops the scissors and walks over to the podium.)
DANIELS: Etcetera Etcetera..Well I'm getting out of here! You horse rapers can stick around but I got things to do you stupid wop deigos...

CROSS: "Well, thank you all for coming out tonight, I hope to see you all on
August 8th, 2001 for the first ever CTWA: Legacy!"
