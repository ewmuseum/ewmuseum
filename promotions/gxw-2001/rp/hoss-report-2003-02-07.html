THE HOSS REPORT for 02/07/03

7–8 February 2003

BuffBellows — 02-07-03, 05:07 PM [updated:LAST EDITED ON Feb-07-03 AT 04:08&nbsp;PM (EST)]THE HOSS REPORT

with Jim Hosselhoff



Greetings, everybody, from under the good ol' Oakland A's cap, and welcome to the Hoss Report. As usual, I'm good ol' Jim Hosselhoff. We've had quite a week here in GXW, so we've got a lot of ground to cover in the Hoss Report this week. So without further ado, let's get to it!





I'd like to shed some light on the status of Chief Executive Dupree, but I'm just as in the dark as the rest of you. Hopefully we'll get some answers in the coming weeks. Until then, we all wish Mr. Dupree a speedy recovery.



Interesting to see Cameron Cruise boycotting the GXW product, but certainly not as devastating a blow as Cam may hope. We can certainly get over the departure of one hoss, but mark my words: In a few months he'll come running back to GXW like a scalded dog.



We have high hopes for Stephen Waltz, Derrick Rodgers, Rave, and Dean Matthews. These are all up-and-coming hosses of the highest caliber, and I'm certain that each and every one of them will amount to something in GXW.



Aitsu Sugawara continues to impress everyone backstage with her in-ring performance. Given time, this young firecracker will definitely become a staple of the women's division.



"The Italian Bruiser" Reuben Fasco has been drawing some attention with his ringwork as of late, especially with his impressive showing against Dan Ryan. He's a talented hoss, but I think his victory over Ricky Gant may have gotten to his head. We'll see how he fares against Gabriel Poe at Revolution.



I will say again that this is going to be a breakout year for John Miller. He's one of the top picks to win King of the Cage, but first he'll have to get past another potential breakout star in Christian Sands. Both men are big hosses and are very close in height and weight. You won't want to miss this match.



Strange to see Dean Matthews booked in a cruiserweight title match. Last I checked, Matthews weighed in at 245 pounds, but I can only assume he's lost weight since then. Rumor has it that he's down 20 pounds. Either way, his match with Zell Hunter should be a joy to watch.



What a shock to find out that Christian Sands' mystery assailant is his own brother, Devon. You talk about bad blood. I've heard from my colleagues that Devon's a top-of-the-line hoss, and should he and Christian ever meet in the ring, he'll give his brother a real run for his money.



Legend has the potential to be a big hoss, but he needs to up his workrate and put his heart into his matches if he wants to get anywhere in GXW.



The match between Judas and Dragon Assassin was an interesting clash of styles, but they somehow made it work. We have high hopes for Judas; he's one of the brightest young hosses on our roster. Dragon Assassin is a talented hoss as well, but his attitude towards the business could stand to improve.



I can't wait to see the Cell Of Destruction match between the Monsta Boyz and the Assassins at Genesis! These are the two best teams in GXW right now by my estimation, and the match between these hosses should be nothing short of a slobberknocker.



Quite a twist of fate for Chris Lehew, as he was defeated by Kin Hiroshi and eliminated from King of the Cage. It seems the war between himself and Zero is in full swing now. Speaking of Kin Hiroshi, GXW's resident Muffin Man is a real hoss who has been underutilized in my opinion. He'll make it at least as far as the semifinals.



What a match between Dan Ryan and Boogie Smallz. Both men are top-notch hosses, and their match lived up to all expectations. It's a shame that both men couldn't win, but that's the way things are in this industry.



We have extremely high hopes for Troy Douglas. This young man is certainly a big hoss, and if he keeps his focus he'll go far in GXW. I wouldn't be surprised to see him in the main event scene someday. He's certainly got the talent.



What has gotten into Kendall Codine lately? He's acting goofier than a pet coon.



Looks like Rob Sampson may be undergoing a change of attitude. He's a real hoss, and seeing him tag with Zero this week on Revolution is going to be absolutely phenomenal. It's gonna be a slobberknocker, I tell ya!



I've got song lyrics for JP. "Na na na na, hey heyyyyy, goodbye, na na na." Good riddance to him. On that same note, I found it highly entertaining to see Hellfighter send Marcus Gottfried running like a scalded dog.





Injury Update:



El Martyr's condition is still in doubt. It seems likely that he may never recover, or if he does it'll take quite some time.



Still no word on Chris Titan's neck injury.



As my colleague Mr. E mentioned in the Insider, Zero took a few bumps to the head recently that may have rattled his brains a little. Luckily, he seems to be suffering no ill effects.



We've had our share of bumps and scraped throughout the King of the Cage tournament, but our expert medical staff has it under control.





Again, I encourage everyone to come see us live. Revolution is emanating from the Odyssey Arena in Belfast this week, and I can tell you right now you won't wanna miss it. It's gonna be a slobberknocker!





That's it for the Hoss Report this week.
