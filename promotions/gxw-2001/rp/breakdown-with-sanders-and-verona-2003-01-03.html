Global Wrestling Evolution — News

GXW: BreakDown with Sanders and Verona

3 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hell_Fighter — 01-03-03, 06:17 PM *(Fade In...



*(GXW Headquarters...



*(Cut to the interior of a television studio set. In the background, there is a backdrop with a GXW logo on it along with the letters, "Breakdown". In front of the backdrop, there is a steel enforced framed desk with a glass top with two chairs next to it. Seating inside the two chairs are Onslaught commentators, Brett Sanders and Gabriel Verona. They are wearing nicely dressed suits. A make-up person applies the finishing touches to their faces. The director gives the que for everyone to clear the set signifying that they are about to go on.)



Director: "Que theme music. We're on in 5...4...3...2...



(Points to Sanders and Verona to let them know they are on the air. Lights come up, and all cameras are on the pair.)



Brett Sanders: "Thank you all for joining us. Welcome to the first of what will hopefully be many editions of the new GXW wrap-up and analysis show, Breakdown. The show where we literally breakdown your favorite GXW superstars and get to the root of what their all about. I am your host, Brett Sanders. Along with me is my Onslaught co-host Gabriel Verona."



Gabriel Verona: "Since this is a new show, let ME tell you what this show is all about. You, the fans, email your questions or comments to us about your favorite superstars. Then we, being the expert journalists that we are, will analyze their good points and things that they might need to work on. Most importantly it should be a show that will be garanteed to be a learning experience for you. Brett, being a former allumni of the old EWI, combined with a former Nthwa allumni...that would be me will bring you the best news possible. Also we will do our best to recap the goings on in the GXW bringing you all to speed."



Sanders: "If you wish to contact us, you may do so at [detail withheld] ([detail withheld]). We will do our best to air your questions as quickly as possible. Now for the first order of business. Since we do not have any emails right now, lets get the ball rolling by talking about a team that probably needs some light shed on them, so all of us can understand them better. Gabe, I believe that you know our first topic better than most people since you are from Nthwa."



Verona: "That's right. I've seen these guys from the moment we first recruited them, their rise and of course recent fall to fame, and I believe that they will rise again. I am talking about...dare I say it...oh yes what the hell...THE MOST GRAPHICALLY COLORFUL TAG TEAM IN ALL OF SPORTS ENTERTAINMENT...True Living Colors."



Sanders: "If I remember correctly, it was you that actually helped seek them out was it not?"



Verona: "Myself and Sterling Mosher. These guys were still fresh on the indy scene in St. Louis Missouri. David could of been a pro hockey player, but his mouth kept him from getting in. Let's just say he wasn't a team player, but he could play for a man his size. Jevon was a was a rising soccer player who was training to be on the World Cup soccer team. It was ludicris what happened to him. Mosher and I, after we did our research on these guys said 'if these guys have a inkling of talent, and this much charisma, imagine what they could do the Nthwa. They were kickin butt on their respective indy level. Nobody displayed the kind of inring chemistry like David and Jevon did. The beautiful part about these two is they were totally opposite. David was a power wrestler while Jevon was a high flyer. They were awesome in the ring. We flew them back to Nthwa headquarters and very easily convinced Mister Dupree to sign them right away."



Sanders: "It was my understanding that you pushed them immediately."



Verona: "We had to. We needed a team that could compete against the best that hadn't been beaten by the best. We needed a no name team. Out best team in the tag team division was the Motor City Maniacs. I don't care what people say, the Maniacs were the best team in wrestling. They beat everybody we threw at them. We needed a fresh face to give them a run. Our answer was True Living Colors, and it paid off on the very first day we debuted them."



Sanders: "So TLC actually got pushed to the very top immediately?"



Verona: "Exactly. They started on the top. They never really had the chance to 'pay their dues' like the old saying goes. As they went along, they proved themselves match after match. It looked like we found our diamond in the rough and the Maniacs found their first true challenge in a very long time. Mayhem and Robertson weren't happy at first, but then again it all paved off in what would be a long heated history between these two teams. TLC actually was underdefeated for the longest time in the Nthwa before of all people, the Maniacs handed them their first loss."



Sanders: "Well that takes care of the history recap, now lets talk about the two members, David Black and Jevon White."



Verona: "Right, well like I said before David is the power and Jevon is the speed. If these guys aren't pissing people off on the mic, and I've seen them tear it up on the mic on a really good day, they back it up in the ring. David is incredibly strong than what people give him credit for. One of their many problems is they get overconfident."



Sanders: "That's an understatement."



Verona: "Brett, you don't know the half of it. True story, Eric Zieba really does hate these guys."



Sanders: "Is it because they beat him up in his office during an episode of Onslaught?"



Verona: "Nah, that came way later. He's always hated TLC. I guess it happens to be because of their egos. I can recall conversations where Mister Zieba flat out told me his feelings toward Jevon and David. Zieba for a while resented me even for recruiting them. At the time Zieba moonlighted between running EWI and also he was in charge of the tag team division for the Nthwa, so he had first hand accounts of TLC's egos. They all three hated each other to say the least. I was certain when the merger happen that Zieba and TLC would not be apart of it."



Sanders: "Why's that?"



Verona: "Because they would of killed each other before then. TLC always had a way of pissing off everybody...even the bosses."



Sanders: "Now we know a little about David, but what about Jevon? Many people think that since he is the smaller one of the two he is the weaker link."



Verona: "Not even close. Just because Jevon is smaller doesn't mean he is weaker. Jevon is fast and very technically conscious in the ring. He can break people down with careful percision. The beauty is he can do it up high or down low. He rather stay high in the air, but he will ground you. Another story, TLC lost a narrow brutal match against the Maniacs at Hardcore Halloween."



Sanders: "In Zieba's patented Genocide match?"



Verona: "You know that match?"



Sanders: "Yeah, I was in EWI. Anything that Zieba and Dupree did in Nthwa, was probably done in EWI first. I know the Genocide match and how graphic it can be."



Verona: "Well anyway, TLC lost this huge match. It's the following Onslaught after the PPV. We're building off the hype of the PPV, and Dupree said to book TLC in a tag match against another up and coming team called Fast Lane. We warned them that TLC was probably still warn out from the match and he said do it anyway. We booked them. The match was a true text book tag team match. Fast Lane isolated Black from White. They literally picked Jevon apart limb from limb. They thought that if they breakdown the smaller guy than they would win the match. They were half right. Their mistake was they underestimated Jevon in everyway. He didn't just get a lucky move, then make the hot tag to David. It literally was Jevon verses Fast Lane. Jevon beat Fast Lane. Yeah David got in the match but not until the very end, but if I recall, I still believe that Jevon got the pin. It was a while ago, but I remember that much about it. Do not underestimate Jevon one bit. Take my word for it these two are a formible team when their on."



Sanders: "Now when the merger happened, somehow this once great tag team took a dive in the ranks, what happened to them?"



Verona: "Well for one thing, remember how I said they started at the top? Well they eventually fell to the bottom of the ladder. It was frustrating for them as well as for me. I really felt their pain, and Dupree is looking at me going, 'what happened?' This team, who once took the Maniacs and brought them to their knees all of a sudden was losing everything. They went on a giant losing streak. I think part of it was the losing streak was causing them to wise up and mature as wrestlers. They started to realize they couldn't go running their mouths at everybody. They needed to humble themselves...whoa I almost sounded like HellFighter for a second."



Sanders: "Ah don't go there."



Verona: "Right whatever. They needed to humble themselves and how they couldn't run their mouths off at everybody. They couldn't rely on trash talking like they used to do in Nthwa. In reality, what happened to them is what I feared. They fell into the vast GXW background, getting lost in the shuffle. Their attitudes were their undoing. Teams like the Monsta Boyz, the Night Cripplers, and of course at the time the Maniacs were not going to listen to them run their mouths at everybody. Their attitudes motivated their oponants to kick their asses. David and Jevon took every single oponant in the GXW for granted thinking it was going to be a free win. Of course they were seriously mistaken. Tag teams were lining up to finally put the most hated tag team in their places."



Sanders: "So you really think that the losing streak brought TLC down to the earth?"



Verona: "Hell yes. I look back on the losing streak now, and believe it was the best thing to happen for David and Jevon. I believe that you will see an evolution, and this will be the year of David Black and Jevon White."



Sanders: "Which reminds me, I heard a nice juicy rumor that David and Jevon have thrown their names in the hat to compete in the King Of The Cage tournament."



Verona: "Brett, you're not supposed to spread unfound rumors without knowing their validity first."



Sanders: "I'm not, I just thought we could talk about this since it deals with the future of this possibly great tag team. What will a move like this do to the friendship of these two men who have known each other since childhood?"



Verona: "Well the way I see it is they are two grown men who know how to separate their friendship for business. They are not the type of men to let something like this get in the way of their team. I will say this that if it is true and I do see the two of them enter the tournament, I wouldn't be surprised if one of them takes it. David can throw people around with his untapped strength. He is a hulk ready to bust out. I will stake my career on that. As for Jevon, he is a fast, agile, technically aware fighter. They would be venturing out into uncharted territory, that always brings out their best."



Sanders: "If this rumor turns out to be true and both are entered in the tournament, what if they have to fight each other?"



Verona: "Simple, for one simple night they will cast friendship aside and fight to go on. They know that sometimes sacrifices have to be made. This would be no different. Lets face it thought, what are the odds of them facing each other at the same time? Since the first round is random. The odds of them facing in the first round is very slim, then if they both get to one another sometime during the tournament, the odds are even more decreased. I personally do not see them facing each other in the tournament. This is what I predict, if they enter the king of the cage, I see them both making strong pressence, but they will not win the tournament. They will put up a good show, but they will fall. If anything the tournament will show just what these two can really do. After the tournament is over with, I see them returning to the tag team division and picking up where they left off making another run at the titles."



Sanders: "Very good point and prediction. Now I am getting the sign that we have run out of time for now. Tune in next week as we breakdown another specimen. Until then, this is Gabriel Verona and Brett Sanders saying goodnight."



(The lights lower, the sound drops, and the credits roll. It ends with the GXW copyright show on the screen.)
