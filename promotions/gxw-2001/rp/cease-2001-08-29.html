The vodka burned like cold fire in his open wound, but the tears that sprung from his eyes weren't from the pain, they were from seeing his hopeless mother, clad in a bathrobe, drinking at seven o'clock in the morning, totally indifferent that her only son was leaving. He later vowed that it would be forever.



Zero looked down at the paper. He still hadn't turned it over to see the address. In that instant, the entire universe felt like it was standing on Zero's chest. All the bad things with Chris, Kristina, the way he snuck out of Cape Kegluneq after he was well without paying, this McCorning character who had been following him, his mother, drunk and dying. And he finally made an ultimatum.



Fuck.



Them.



All.



He crumpled the paper in his fist and threw it into a trash can, standing up. He was going home alright. But things were changing inside of him. He just stopped caring.



But he was going home.



With a wry smile, he could only hope that home was ready for him.





The End
