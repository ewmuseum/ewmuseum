GXW Front Office update, 15 October 2001

EZieba — 15 October 2001 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Oct-15-01 AT 03:05 AM (EST)]Two words ... We're BACK! Okay the LONG LAYOFF is OVER! The GXW is back in session and we're going to do ... no ... we're going to go OUT OF OUR WAY to bring you the excitement you DESERVE!
With the situation in our world today there is no need to sit around and let this fed go to the crapper. THEREFORE we are back and, just to start it off, here is the card for the upcoming X-Perience!
'Triple X' Sean Stevens vs 'Whirlwind' Ken Peters Carnage vs 'Jamming' Jimmy James Sypder vs Jake 'Devil Dog' Dupont REMATCH For the GXW X-treme Title 'The Patron Saint of Violence' Pat Black (C) vs 'The Marauder' Eric Gibson 'The People's Choice' Cole Steele vs The Reaver Non-Title Match 'Mr. Main Event' Rob Sampson vs John Miller (Continental Champion) For the GXW TV Tag Team Titles Insane Pain (C) vs Wicked Sight and Billy Moser MAIN EVENT For the GXW Unified Heavyweight Title 'The Hero' Marcus Johnson (C) vs Eric 'The Dragon' Davis The RP deadline for this card will be Saturday night, 20 October 2001, at 11:59PM EST. This also includes any angles, interviews, attacks, and whatnot. So, if you want it in ... GET IT IN!
Since the long layoff there are a couple of things to address: 1) In or Out. I'm going off the old emails we had prior to the long layoff. So, I ask again, if you are in or out let me know and I will leave/take you off the list. And, when you let me know ... PLEASE let me know your wrestler and email so I can ensure that you are updated/dropped.
2) Active or Inactive. I know there are times when wreslters are overlooked and I'm sorry for that. Therefore let us know if your wrestler has been overlooked and we will do our best to get that man (or woman) a match. Remember we will continue our challenge cards so if you want a match for sure ... that is the place to get it. Remember we are only human people. Things happen and stuff gets overlooked so help us out.
3) Storylines. I know that there are some storylines going on so, if you do have one running, let us know so we can verify it ... and jog our outdated memories :)
4) Help us Help you! The GXW (and the names before it) can't operate unless you, the handler, are involved. Many have added to the excitement of GXW and I want to go out of my way to thank you very much cause you (you know who you are) have been a great help. Remember if you want something to go your way then shoot us some email and we'll do our best to see it through. Granted we'll add our 'ideas' to it, but that's basically the game right? As everyone knows we do our best to work with every angle and we will continue to do this. So, once again, help us help you.
5) Writing. There might come a time that we will need someone to help write matches cause of Real Life stuff (I'm in the Army myself so you would just have to GUESS the situation I'm in). And yes I've got the word of (Is the card ready yet) So, on that note, if you would like to write a match from time to time then let us know cause we might have to call on ya one day.
6) THANK YOU ONCE AGAIN! Again I want to say thank you to all the people that are in GXW. I know we've been in a drought and we're going to do our best to pick it up. Yeah ... we've said it before, but we will work on it to ENSURE that it works out this time around.
Remember (REMEMBER) if there is anything you want to add, suggest, complain, gripe, cry, whine, bitch, beg, ... WHATNOT ... let us know!
