GXW: Cruiserweight Title Tournament

19 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mr Main Event — 19 November 2003 Attention cruiserweight handlers! GXW has decided to hold a tournament to determine a NEW Cruiserweight champion. Rather than holding a standard, run-of-the-mill tournament, we've decided to spice things up a bit.
Here's the deal: the first SIX wrestlers to sign up will be entered into the tournament. In round one, there will be two single-elimination 3-way matches. The losers of those 2 matches are eliminated from the tournament. In round two, the wrestlers who advanced will now TEAM to wrestle against each other. The winners of that match will then face off in the third and final round to determine the NEW Cruiserweight champion!
Sign up now! First come, first serve!
