GXW: State of the Cruiserweight Division

the wing's own Cruiserweight-division Vice President — 24 February 2002 Well after checking over the GXW Roster. I figure I'd give those interested an idea of what we're working with. I have a list of 5 wrestlers that will compete for the Cruiser title. The list can be seen below. Now I still want to push for new Cruisers. The weight requirement is 235lbs or less, so if you want to take a shot at the Cruiser division, send in an app. Here's the list.
1. Armen 2. Kin Hiroshi 3. Inferno 4. Mr. White of the True Living Colors 5. Eric Davis/Alex Young If you're listed above and don't want to be part of the Cruiser Division, please let me know.
Please send all angles, strats, etc that deal with the Cruiser Division to Me along with Chad, Erik and the other's in charge.
[name withheld] Vice President of Cruiser Division.
Armen — 24 February 2002 Hey Les.. I dont have yer email so I'm just responding here.. No offence but I don't really want Armen to be in the cruiserweight division, I meant to send an email about my one cruiserweight match a while back.. but never got around to it..
So yeah not really interested there... sorry :) Btw its because I have a secret prejudice against all cruiserweights... they are just ugh, dont deserve to be alive! :P
-Armen
