GXW — General Diss

GXW End of Year Awards for 2002

21 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
the wing's own booker/consigliere-role staffer — 21 January 2003 Ladies and gentlemen, as an esteemed junior staff member here in Global Xtreme Wrestling it was my pleasure to compile the awards for the year just completed. As I put this together I considered several different formats. A simple list of winners, a "show" complete with interviews and such....but what I decided on was something of a hybrid of the two. I'll be unveiling the awards, and in each category give some insight to why they deserved the award sprinkled with some of my personal thoughts. I felt in the end that a show would be a bit contrived. The WWF (at the time) did a similar show and it usually came off cheesy and pointless. For those who were hoping for a show, I apologize...and yet present....
The GXW End of Year Awards - 2002 One of the things we did differently this year was to add a free form section to the awards. Several of these questions were for internal use only, but several were more conventional. The results were as follows:
The question was asked: If the GXW roster was a clean slate and held a draft, aside from any considerations other than who you would personally want to start your company with and build it around, who would you choose first?
Dan Ryan took this one home, pulling in three times as many votes as anyone else. More on him later...
The question was asked: On a similar note, if you took over GXW and were able to start with a clean slate with all belts who would you put with each title? Unified World? Continental? TV? Cruiser? Tag? Women's?
In each section of this question, I'll reveal the wrestler most voted for in that particular title:
Unified World: Tie: Kendall Codine, Zero Continental: Tie: Rob Sampson, Kendall Codine TV: Tie: A.J. Styles, Boogie Smallz Xtreme: Tie: Chris Lehew, Hellfighter Tag: by a 4-1 ratio - The Monsta Boyz Cruiser: also by a 4-1 ratio - Inferno Women's: Miso The question was asked: Have you been satisfied with the way the league has been run in the last year? This is a simple opinion poll, much like the Presidential confidence polls. A simple YES or NO.
I'm happy to say the answers were unanimous. Everyone polled voted YES. The question was asked: You are in charge of putting together a stable of GXW superstars. You have six slot to use singles or tag wrestlers. Tag teams count for two slots.
There were 18 different teams or wrestlers named among those who responded. The top six, in order:
Dan Ryan, Rob Sampson, Chris Lehew, and a six way tie between Kendall Codine, John Miller, Kevin Powers, Gabriel Poe, Boogie Smallz, and Zero
Now then, with that out of the way it's on the the old tried and true awards. WRESTLER OF THE YEAR: "The Ego Buster" Dan Ryan Ryan won this one by amassing three times as many votes as anyone else named. Ryan spent the great majority of 2002 holding GXW's top prize. He held the Unified World Title two times, first winning in January, losing it several weeks later, winning it back a week later...then holding it until Fallout, the last show of the year. Ryan also went through a tumultuous year personally, losing his family the night of his first title win and overcoming that tragedy to make 2002 the year of the Ego Buster.
'FACE' OF THE YEAR: Kendall Codine Codine broke onto the scene in a big way in 2002. After being revealed as the man behind the Carnage moniker previously, Codine managed to climb the ranks in GXW faster than any man in it's short history. He won the 2002 King of the Cage tournament, which launched him into a memorable feud with Dan Ryan over the Unified World title. Thought he came up short in that pursuit, he went on to capture the Continental title and hold it for a good portion of the year.
'HEEL' OF THE YEAR: Tie - "Mr. Main Event" Rob Sampson and "The Ego Buster" Dan [name withheld] and Ryan share this dubious honor this year. Sampson formed GXW's newest heel stable in 2002, showing himself to be the mastermind behind the attacks on his then-partner Chris Lehew which led to stablemate Jeffrey Roberts winning Lehew's TV title. Sampson went on to win that same title himself, later holding it along with the Continental title simultaneously and fighting for his life in a feud of the year candidate against Kendall Codine. As of this writing, Sampson remains the Continental Champ.
For Dan Ryan, see above ;-) FEUD OF THE YEAR: "The Ego Buster" Dan Ryan vs. Kendall Codine Kendall Codine won the 2002 King of the Cage in dramatic fashion, turning aside rookies and veterans alike, including the then-champion "Executive" Eric Edwards. Ryan regained his title from Edwards shortly thereafter and, seeing Codine as a threat, began a series of mind games with the champion that led to an epic battle at Global Warfare 2002.
MATCH OF THE YEAR: John Miller vs. "Executive" Eric Edwards – Global Warfare, July 16, 2002
I take great pride in the fact that this match won this award, because I came up with the idea for the match and then wrote it myself. It came to pass mostly because at the time I had been re-watching my tape of IWA's King of the Death Match video, and got inspired by the insanity Mick Foley and Terry Funk put themselves through to put on such a show. Big ups to both John Miller and [name withheld] for their work RP this match and really giving me something to work off of.
COMEBACK OF THE YEAR: Gemini Gemini made his big return just in time to qualify for this award and as everyone knows, always makes a hell of a statement. The self proclaimed two faced freak enters 2003 primed for a big run after personal situations kept his handler on the sideline for most of 2002.
ROOKIE OF THE YEAR: Boogie Smallz The closest vote of the awards came down to a one vote difference as Smallz edged out Christian Sands. Smallz came on the scene, not as a rookie to wrestling in general having been a part of a very successful team in the CSWA, but as a newcomer to GXW who opened a lot of eyes with his…ahem..fresh outlook as a character and strong roleplays. Smallz broke through midway through the year with a big win for the GXW TV title, his first major singles title despite being held back by "the man".
BREAKOUT STAR OF THE YEAR: Three way tie – "Career Ender" Chris Lehew, Kendall Codine, and The Monsta Boyz
Chris Lehew finally broke out of his underachieving mold during 2002, winning the TV title and rising to main event level through several huge wins to close out the year.
The Monsta Boyz came a long long way in 2002, from getting attacked upon their entrance to outlasting both The Night Cripplers and The Assassins to win the World Tag Team Championships.
Kendall Codine's year has been well documented, as he rose to become a mainstay on the main event level scene.
STABLE OF THE YEAR: Extreme Tendencies Extreme Tendencies beat out Team Phenom for the award this year. The group claimed titles in bunches, with Rob Sampson carrying the TV and Continental titles at various points, Jeffrey Roberts holding the TV title, The Night Cripplers winning the World Tag Team Titles, and Inferno winning the Cruiserweight title.
ANGLE OF THE YEAR: Co-Owner Chad Dupree vs. Co-Owner Erik Zieba for control of GXW Zieba vs. Dupree was the story of the year as the two battled it out over complete control of the company, with Zieba ultimately coming out on top.
HANDLER OF THE YEAR: [name withheld] Handled Dan Ryan, Jeffrey Roberts and The Night Cripplers who at one time or another held the World Title twice, TV title once, and The World Tag Team Titles once.
All I can say about this is that I'm greatly appreciative to be voted for this, and that I've been very proud to have been able to represent GXW during this past year. From invading CSWA to making our mark in GXW, it shows that this is the place to be. Thanks to everyone.
That about wraps it up! Thanks to everybody who voted! Thanks for a great year and here's to making 2003 even better!
[name withheld] Super Silent Junior Staff Member
