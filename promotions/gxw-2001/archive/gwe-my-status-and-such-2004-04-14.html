the wing's own booker/consigliere-role staffer — 14 April 2004 Hello peeps. I wanted to do this the other day but never got around to it. Yes, it's official. The Z Man is back in the house long term. What does this mean for the staff organization in general and me specifically? What it means is we'll go back to Chad and Erik essentially the top dogs, with myself as a third to help with ties in voting. I will also continue to help with booking, so you can feel free to ask me about those matters. And of course, I will still be handling Dan Ryan and Jeffrey Roberts.
I was more than happy to run the ship with Erik taking care of his own business, but I'm also happy to have the weight of the GWE world not entirely on my shoulders anymore.
Whoo hoo!! But on that note, expect a web site redesign soon as well as a new name for the "X-Perience" show. Many exciting things afoot for the rest of the year.
Thanks again to all of you for making GWE such a great place to be a part of. - Dave
