GXW: "It Was Fun"

14 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
the wing's own Cruiserweight-division Vice President — 14 January 2002 Well fellas I have to say that it was a blast. Effect immediately JT Savage, Cole Steele, and The Motor City Maniacs have retired from the GXW. I'm not sure what Chad has instore for the Maniacs as they are still your current GXW Tag Team Champions. I will be sticking around to book and handle the operations of the Cruiserweight Division. So Cruisers, ( Do we have any?) Please continue to send angles, attacks, and such to my email address.
[name withheld] VP of Cruiserweight Operations.
Hell_Fighter — 14 January 2002 Nooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooo ooooooooooooooooo!! Okay how about that for a flare for the dramatic? I am sad to see your characters go, especially the Maniacs. You alwasy knew how to push me to my limits Les, and Fallout showed it. We can push the envelope. I have this feeling that True Living Colours have to send the Maniacs a going away present. And then, maybe then I can truly be the one to get back my world tag titles once and for all. Hahahahahahaahahahahahahahahahahahhahahahhahahahha hahhahahahhahahah!!
But am I am glad that you are still going to be involved with the GXW, that makes me jump for joy. Who knows maybe they might still make some kind of cameo in the future. So it has been fun working with you Les and look foward to working more with you behind the scenes.
And just remember, since you are head of the cruiserweight division, can I give you a little bit of insight. Since I am slowly trying to push TLC more in singles competition...may I remind you that Mr. White does fall in that catagory. HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!
Well I gotta go for now, God bless Les. See you on the other side man. I just remembered something, even though True Living Colours has never allowed the Maniacs (or anyone once else for that matter) I am giving you permission to have the honors of addressing Black and White by their first names (David and Jevon) You've earned it my friend.
Sincerely, God bless, [name withheld] (HellFighter and True Living Colours)
InfernoDrop — 19 February 2002 Holy Shnykies, pardon the spelling. I'm gone for a month, more or less, and Les' characters go bye bye? There is something not right about that. MCM, Steel, and Savage will be missed. Others will step up to fill the spaces, but no one will walk in their footsteps, so to speak.
the wing's own Cruiserweight-division Vice President — 19 February 2002 Well Bob-o, As you can see on the last card, a certain tag team that will remain nameless didn't let the Maniacs leave quietly. I guess I've just had some writers block with my characters and figured to take some time off, well now I've gotten some incentive. So I'll be sticking it out. Now I just need to get Cole dusted off...:)
