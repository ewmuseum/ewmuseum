GXW — General Diss

GXW: State of the Fed Address

23 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mr Main Event — 23 October 2003 Hey gang. I felt that I needed to address all of you once again after stirring up a bit of controversy with the "Is GXW Dead?" thread. People are wondering if GXW is in trouble, or if GXW is shutting down. The answer to both of those questions is no. We've come too far to stop now, folks.
See, what happened is that I let my personal life affect/effect my entire outlook on things. The end result of that is me looking at the GW threads, seeing no RPs, and wondering if I was wasting my and everyone else's time. I know I'm not now, and I'm grateful for that.
So, long story short, GXW is not going anywhere. Global Warfare will be a roaring success, I'm sure, and will give us an excellent way to start 2004. :)
If any of you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact me. My e-mail is [detail withheld] and my AIM SN is [detail withheld].
Excelsior! ~ Chad
