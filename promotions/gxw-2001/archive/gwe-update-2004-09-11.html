GXW — Front Office

GWE Front Office update, 11 September 2004

11 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EZieba — 11 September 2004 To the current and former members of GXW/GWE, As you know my name is Erik Zieba and normally I would not be writing to anyone that was in the league in the past, but under these circumstances I thought that it would be good.
In case you do not know Chad Dupree passed away on 27 Aug. If anyone knew Chad then they know that he was a nice and kind person to talk to. He always did what he could to help someone else no matter what the problem is. He was a good friend and I wanted to do something for him in his honor.
Now, granted, there isn’t much I can do, but there is one thing. What I want to do is to have one final card in his memory. With this card, I vision, we could have people from GXW’s past come in and have one final match in his honor. There will be no politics, no backstabbing, or anything of the sort. I just want to have one final card where everyone can have fun and enjoy. And, because (I hope) this will be the biggest card ever, it will be the last card GXW/GWE ever holds.
The idea is to have this card around the December timeframe. I pick this month because I’m thinking it might be a big card. If it turns out to be smaller then the date will be pushed up.
So, on that note, if you want to be apart of this card please let me know via email and I will collect all the names. Once I get a decent amount of names we’ll go from there.
Also, if you think anyone else would like to take part in this card please pass the message on to them.
One final card … one final show … for one great man … Chad Dupree. I hope to hear from you soon. Enjoy, Erik Z Owner GWE (This card is open to anyone who would like to be apart of it.)

Hell_Fighter — 12 September 2004 If it's not too late I do apologize for not replying on this topic sooner, but I was having computer problems for the passed three or more weeks.
I was wondering if it was not too late if I could be somehow included on the final GXW/GWE card and in Chad's memory.
If at all possible, I would like to somehow throw "HellFighter" [name withheld]'s name in the hat as I have been looking for a way to retire him and I can not think of a better way to do it than on the final GWE card. I started my efed career in the GXW when it was still MWC, and it was with HellFighter. I started with him. I would like one final way to work with him.
If it is not too late. I would like to know if HellFighter could appear on this final card.
IF so please contact me, and if not I totally understand.
