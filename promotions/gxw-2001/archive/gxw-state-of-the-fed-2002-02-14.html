GXW: State of the Fed

14 February 2002

youknowwho — 14 February 2002 Ok guys, things are getting JUST a bit dead around here so let's start up some discussion on something. How about we post a little bit about what we think about things in GXW right now. You know, what angles you like, what storylines are going on that you can't just wait for the next show to see what happens next, etc. Just something to get the ball rolling.
tylerdurden — 16 February 2002 Well Dave, you asked for my ideas, so here they are! HA! First off, I really like the ##### going on with Dan Ryan right now. It's interesting to see that kind of angle in the backstage. I know that being in Team Phenom is sweet, because it's fun to listen to you brainstorm your ass off.
Ummm.. what else... I really like this stuff with Zero. You know, I have to be honest, when JP brought Zero into the fed at first, I didn't like him at all. That's just the truth. But now I'm so damn interested in what he has to say, and what he's gonna do next. You've done a great job with him JP, keep it up man. The same goes for Zach and Chris Lehew. I never liked Chris Lehew before either, and now he's got me pretty interested in what he's all about. Both those handlers should be very proud of their characters.
I've got an angle in my head about Davis that Chad knows of right now, and trust me guys, I think it's gonna be sweet as all hell. I mean, it might be gay, but who knows. I hope everyone enjoys watching it unfold like I will.
Oh here's a big idea that maybe you guys would want to get into. I dunno how anyone would feel about this, so now's the best time to bring it up. What if there was a MAJOR angle in the fed where Dupree and Zieba decided to split it into two factions again. Dupree has his NthWA wrestlers, and Erik has his EWI wrestlers. And if someone was in both, they are stuck in the middle of where to choose. It would also add great interest because everyone would be wondering who they were siding with. There would be a legitimate battle for the belts, as each faction would want to gain control of one piece of GXW. I was even saying that maybe it could all culminate in a PPV, with the winning side getting the fed back to it's original state. Example being that if the NthWA wrestlers won, the fed would become the NthWA, and if the EWI wrestlers won, it would become the EWI. Or maybe you guys would like this idea better. It's more of a gradual deal. The fed would only switch over to an NthWA or EWI if ALL the titles were in their posession. This could make for some sweet ass #####. Like think of it this way. It would turn an average card like Revolution into almost PPV calibur overnight. Say all the belts except the World are in EWI's posession, and the match is like Dan Ryan defending it against Gemini. If Gemini wins, and all the belts are in EWI's posession, the league changes over. Now that's motivation, and that would be freakin awesome IMO. I dunno about you guys, but just give me some feedback on these ideas. The EWI/NthWA deal, or just whatever.
There Dave, got the ball rollin'.... follow up you terd. =)~ Oh and guys, feel free to pick on Dave as much as you want, it's fun, trust me.
Peace, John
SFZero — 1 April 2002 Sounds like some one (coughwwfcough) liked your idea, John. Case #1235466774655 of WWF ripping off FW. And yes, I'm freakishly bored and reading old posts since there's nothing else to do. -JP
