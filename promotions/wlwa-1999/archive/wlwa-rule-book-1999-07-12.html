WLWA — Rules

WLWA Rule Book

12 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I: OVERVIEW T HE WLWA IS A BOOKED FED THAT HAS MANY EVENTS THAT GO ON THROUGHOUT THE
COURSE OF A CALANDER YEAR. THERE ARE WEEKLY CARDS AND PAY PER VIEWS, AS
THERE ARE IN MOST FEDS, BUT THERE ARE ALSO OTHER EVENTS THAT ARE SCHEDULED
ON THE "FLY" WE WILL START BY TALKING ABOUT WEEKLY CARDS. WEEKLY CARDS THE WLWA WILL START ITS SEASON WITH ONLY ONE TELEVISED CARD A WEEK. THAT
CARD WILL BE HELD ON WEDNESDAY NIGHTS AND WILL BE TITLED WEDNESDAY NIGHT'S
MAIN EVENT. UNTIL THE STAFF IS COMPLETE WITH EXTRA MATCH WRITERS THIS WILL
BE THE ONLY CARD DURING EACH WEEK, AND IT WILL TAKE PLACE EVERY WEEK. THE
FORMAT FOR THIS CARD IS SIMILAR TO THAT OF MONDAY NITRO, OR WWF RAW IS WAR.
THERE WILL BE MANY MATCHES, WITH ALMOST EVERY WRESTLER PARTICIPATING. BE IT
A RUN-IN, AND INTERVIEW, OR A MATCH, MOST WRESTLERS WILL PLAY A ROLE.
PAY PER VIEWS MUCH LIKE THE WWF OR WCW THERE WILL BE PAY PER VIEWS THROUGHOUT THE COURSE
OF THE YEAR. ON AVEREAGE, ABOUT ONE EVERY TWO MONTHS. THE PAY PER VIEWS ARE
HUGE. TONS OF MATCHES, SUPRISES, INTERVIEWS AND MUCH, MUCH MORE.

TOURNAMENTS, RETIREMENT CARDS, AND FEDERATION WARFARE. THE FIRST TWO ARE
SELF EXPLAINATORY, BUT THE FEDERATION WARFARE IS NOT. THIS IS A SPECIAL
CARD BETWEEN TWO FEDERATIONS, ORCASTRATED BY THE FED HEADS, TO GENERATE
GEAT FOR BOTH ORGANIZATIONS. IT IS A DAY WHEN THE COMPETITORS PUT AWAY
THERE DIFFERENCES AND BATTLE IT OUT WITH ANOTHER FEDERATION.
II: CHARACTER CREATION BY FAR THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT PART OF THE WLWA IS THE PARTICIPANTS. WE
INVITE EVERYONE TO FILL OUT AN APPLICATION, BUT STILL RESERVE THE RIGHT TO
PICK AND CHOOSE WHO WE WANT. THIS ACOMPLISHES TWO THINGS. ONE: WE ARE
ENSURED ONLY THE MOST CREATIVE, INTERESTING, AND EXPERIENCED E-WRESTLERS IN
THE WORLD, AND TWO WE CAN WEED OUT THE PEOPLE THAT ARE FAIR-WEATHER
E-WRESTLERS. TO FILL OUT AN APPLICATION FOLLOW THIS LINK. ALL CHARACTERS
MUST BE 100% ORIGINAL RIGHT DOWN TO THE NICKNAMES AND GIMMICKS. I DON'T
WANT TO SEE ANY "HEART BREAK KID" DEAN HALLOWS, OR "STONE COLD" JAY
PHILIPS. THE WAY YOUR CHARACTER ACTS MUST ALSO STAY IN CHARACTER. IF AT ANY
POINT I FEEL THAT YOU ARE IMITATING A REAL WRESTLER I WILL WARN YOU, THEN,
IF IT CONTINUES, SUSPEND YOU. HEAVYWEIGHTS/CRUISERWEIGHTS THERE WILL BE TWO DIVISIONS AT THE START OF THE SEASON. THE CRUISERWEIGHT
AND THE HEAVYWEIGHT DIVISION. CRUISERWEIGHTS CAN COMPETE FOR HEAVYWEIGHT
AND CRUISERWEIGHT TITLES, BUT HEAVYWEIGHTS CAN ONLY COMPETE FOR THERE ARE
OWN. IN ORDER TO ENSURE REALIZM CRUISERWEIGHTS CAN BE NO TALLER THEN 6 FOOT
5 INCHES, AND WEIGH NO MORE THAN 225 POUNDS. HEAVYWEIGHTS ALSO HAVE
RESTRICTIONS, BUT THEY ARE NOT AS STRICT. III: KEYS TO VICTORY THERE ARE MANY THINGS THAT A PERSON CAN DO TO ACHIEVE SUCCESS IN THE WLWA. THE MOST PROMINENT IS BY ROLE PLAYING. ROLE PLAYING CAN BE DONE IN THREE
WAYS, FLASHES, INTERVIEWS AND STRATEGIES. EACH ROLE PLAY IS GIVEN A QUALITY
RATING BASED ON THE TYPE OF ROLE PLAY AND ITS QUALITY. THESE RATINGS ARE
TALLIED BEFORE THE EVENT AND USED AS A PUSH METER. IF THE EVENT IS ON A
MONDAY ALL ROLE PLAYS FROM TEUSDAY TO SUNDAY ARE USED FOR THAT COMING
MONDAY. ROLE PLAYING INTERVIEWS INTERVIEWS CAN OCCURE IN A NUMBER OF PLACES. FOR INSTANCE THEY CAN OCCUR
IN THE RING AT AN EVENT. THERE IS A SEPERATE PAGE TO SUBMIT INTERVIEWS. NO
ONE SEES THESE INTERVIEWS SUBMITTED UNTILL AN EVENT WERE THEY ARE USED AS
FILLERS. THE INTERVIEWS ARE GIVEN A QUALITY RATING BETWEEN 1 AND 20, THE
HIGHER THE BETTER, THIS EFFECTS HOW YOUR WRESTLER WILL PERFORM IN THE RING.
SINCE INTERVIEWS CAN RECIEVE A VERY HIGH RATING THEY MUST BE OF GREAT
QUALITY AND DETAIL. WE ARE VERY PICKY. EVEN IF AN INTERVIEW DOES NOT MAKE
IT TO TELEVISION IT STILL RECIEVES A QUALITY RATING. IF YOU WANT AN
INTERVIEW TO BE AIRED AT AN EVENT THAT MUST BE SPECIFIED IN THE LOCATION. THE INTERVIEWS THAT MAKE IT TO AIR ARE SUBJECT FOR EDITING BY THE WLWA
STAFF. INTERVIEWS MUST BE AT LEAST 200 WORDS (THIS IS NOT HELD STRICTLY
THOUGH).
FLASHES FLASHES ARE LIKE PROMOS FOR A WRESTLER. THESE TO MUST BE DESCRIBE IN GREAT
DETAIL, BUT DON'T ACTUALLY HAVE TO TAKE PLACE SOEMWHERE. IT CAN BE A SERIES
OF HIGHLIGHTS NARRORATED BY THE WRESTLER, WTIH A MESSEGE INTENDED. THE
FLASHES WILL RECIEVE A QUALITY RATING FROM 1 TO 5. THE FLASHES WILL BE DONE
THROUGH A FORM AS WELL. UNLIKE THE INTERVIEWS ALL FALASHES WILL BE SENT OUT
FOR THE OTHER WRESTLERS TO READ. STRATEGIES STRATEGIES ARE YOUR PLAN OF ATTACK FOR AN UPCOMMING MATCH. YOU ARE ALLOWED
ONE STRATEGY FOR AN EVENT. IT CAN BE HOW YOU WILL GO ABOUT YOUR MATCH, IT
CAN BE A RUN-IN DURING A MATCH OR INTERVIEW, OR BOTH. THESE ARE COMPLETLY
FOR WLWA STAFF EYES ONLY AND WILL ALSO BE SUBMITTED VIA A FORM. STRATEGIES
CAN RECIEVE A QUALITY RATING FROM 1 TO 4. OTHER FORMS OF PARTICIPATION RECRUITING THE WLWA IS ALWAYS LOOKING TO EXPAND ITS ROSTER. IF YOU THINK YOU FOUND
SOMEONE THAT IS WLWA MATERIAL, INVITE THEM TO FILL OUT AN APPLICATION. IF
THEY MENTION YOUR NAME IN IT (ie: "MIKE THE CAT SENT ME") AND ARE ACCEPTED
YOU WILL RECIEVE 10 POINTS TOWARDS YOUR PUSH RATING IN YOUR NEXT MATCH.
GUEST MATCH WRITING IF YOU ARE DESPERATE FOR AN EXTRA PUSH WRITE A DARK MATCH FOR THE UPCOMING
EVENT. THE MATCH CAN HAVE ANY WRESTLERS THAT ARE NOT SCHEDULED TO WRESTLE
ON THE CARD, BUT NOT YOURSELF. THERE CAN BE ANY OUTCOME, BUT YOU MUST
FOLLOW THE FORMAT I USE. WE ARE THE MOST STRICT ABOUT THE QUALITY OF THESE
MATCHES. YOU CAN RECIEVE A QUALITY RATING OF 1 TO 30. FOR A 30 THE MATCH
MUST BE DRAMATIC, OR INOVATIVE, OR JUST WELL WRITTEN. THESE MATCHES MAY BE
USED AS DARK MATCHES ON SOME CARDS, BUT ARE SUBJECT TO EDITING.
