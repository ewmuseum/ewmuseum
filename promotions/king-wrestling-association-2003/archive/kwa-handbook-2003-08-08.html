Introduction Welcome to King Wrestling Association, a mid-sized wrestling promotion dedicated to giving its audience the best in wrestling and sports entertainment. Here at the KWA, we encourage simplicity, fun and creativity. Think of this fed as an 'experimental' fed, one where you can try different things that you might be thinking about, whether it be a bizarre gimmick, character development, a weird angle, etc. If you're looking for extra long role-plays, extra long results, and the rest of the things that seem to plague e-wrestling these days, then this isn't the fed for you. But if you're looking for fun, creativity, and action, then you're reached the right place!
b.. KWA History 1.. Fantasy Explaination The King Wrestling Association was founded in early August of 2003, by half brothers Sean King and Aaron King. The sudden, unexpected death of the late-great "King Cobra", Rick King, (a celebrated & well respected wrestler and father of both Sean and Aaron) was a double edged sword; Rick passed away from cancer -- the bad, and he left specific instructions in his will which forced two feuding brothers to work together to run a successful wrestling league -- the good, well kind of.
Sean King was born and raised in New Jersey grew up through all types of adversity to become a successful and powerful businessman. He currently owns several different companies, stores and apartment buildings. Sean is very quiet, refined, and solitary. Aaron King, however is more of a loose cannon who seems to always be in the mix, making things happen. These two never seem to see eye to eye, which can definately make things interesting!

Point System The KWA has developed a point system (similar to other promotion's monetary systems) in which the wrestlers have to earn the right to access of KWA's premeire features. It increases competetion,creativity and cooperativeness. Wrestlers will stack up their points through-out the month, and will have 1 week (the first week) out of every month to "spend" it. The wrestlers can view the King Points page to view all aspects of their KP status -- how much they have to spend and how much their total earned is (in which they will gain status). Role-Plays (RP's) Any roleplays towards a match will be graded on a 5 point scale by both Jayson & James and averaged together. For instance, if Jayson gives a roleplay a 4 and James gives the same rp a 3, the rp's total will have a total of 7, then will be divided by 2, and will receive a final score of 3.5. The roleplay maximum for a SLAM! match is three (3) and five (5) for a PPV event.
4.. Strategies (optional) 1.. Flash (1-5 points) - Flashes are great to put on the shows, because they make things that much more interesting. You can do an interview backstage, an in-ring speech, or a promo away from the arena if you are not fighting at the show that night. They are graded on the same 5 point scale in which roleplays are graded.
2.. Scene/Vignette (3 points) - Special scenes in shows can add to the overall mystery regarding certain angles, can help put over a gimmick, and/or gain heat. For example, the GTV ran in WWF awhile back was a special scene, as is seeing star wrestlers enter the arena backstage before their fight or a wrestler doing push-ups before his fight, etc.
3.. Match Strategy (2 points) - This is a great strat to use if you want to make sure your wrestler does a certain move during his/her match or if you Want him/her to do a certain taunt or something of that nature.
4.. Ring Entrance (1 point) - This strat is for a special ring entrance, such ass comign down with a supermodel, slap hands with the fans, come out to different music, etc. Almost anything goes here, as long as it isn't TOO over the top.
5.. Interference (1 point) - THIS STRAT WILL BE DONE FEW AND FAR BETWEEN. MUST BE CLEARED WITH THE EXECUTIVE OFFICE BEFORE EXECUTED.
f.. Shows 1.. Sunday Night SLAM! Sunday Night SLAM! is KWA's premiere weekly television show. It airs Sunday nights at 9pm, it is taped that pervious Saturday night. (This show has descriptive summary matches, but everything else is full written)
b.. Pay Per Views (PPV's) KWA PPV's are held once a month, unless otherwise noted. (PPV's will be fully written.) g.. Championships 1.. KWA Heavyweight Championship This is the top title of the KWA and can be compared to the World Titles of international wrestling promotions. The Heavyweight title will be defended at every PPV unless otherwise noted.
b.. KWA SLAM! Championship This is KWA's version of the "Television Title. It is the true test of stamina and skill. The SLAM! title will be defended every Sunday SLAM! unless otherwise noted.
h.. Conclusion First, please make sure KWA is the place for you. We understand that we are not for everyone. If you can't cope with the information above, please don't waste either of our time, ours or yours. But if you're trying to have a little fun, then we gladly welcome anyone to apply for KWA.
Secondly, go please check out the roster. Look through it, and check out what types of gimmicks we already have incorporated. Come up with an original character, and fill out an application. But, remember, KWA likes to keep a rather small roster, typically about 16-20 singles wrestlers. This way, people don't get lost in the shuffle and everyone stays active.
