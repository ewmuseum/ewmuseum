ngm wrote: > I can't see any reason why more people would join if your fed became > booked, getting enough people into a fed and then getting them to stay > there is a constant problem ( I have this trouble with my fed). Perhaps > what you should do is use the logfiles as a basis for your reports, mix > in a bit more commentry and make it look like your commentators are > actually calling the holds so everything will flow together better, you > can also cut out a lot of the boring bits in the matches this way. I have to agree with this. I was in the GFWWF, and was their first tag team champions. If Boris had taken the logfiles, and either wrote up a card using them as a basis (or hired someone who could do it), then maybe I wouldn't have dropped this fed when I had to drop some after I started SJPW. It's a good fed, and I am proud that I was able to be the first GFWWF tag team champions, but reading logfiles on a web page is boring (no offense Boris!) I can't see any reason why more people would join if your fed became booked, getting enough people into a fed and then getting them to stay there is a constant problem ( I have this trouble with my fed). Perhaps what you should do is use the logfiles as a basis for your reports, mix in a bit more commentry and make it look like your commentators are actually calling the holds so everything will flow together better, you can also cut out a lot of the boring bits in the matches this way. If you do it this way then you can keep using TNM. What it comes down to in the end IMO is that it does not matter whether the fed is 'booked' or 'simmed' it is the quality of the reports that are sent out that is important. What I do is use the TNM log file to write a report. I run each match two or three times then transfer it all to my word processor where I cut and paste all the good parts together into one good match then add commentary as well as slightly alter a couple of things to add some color that TNM doesn't provide. Everything that I alter really did happen according to TNM, I just change the way it happened. Hope that gives you some ideas.
