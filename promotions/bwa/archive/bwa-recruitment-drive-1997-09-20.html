BWA Recruitment Drive, 20 September 1997

20 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey All The British Wrestling Association is a booked fed, based in funilly enough, Britain! :) We have had the opening two report shows called Raw Footage (Posted here as well), and our opening card comes out on Monday. However, we are still in need of more wrestlers (Isn't everyone? :) ), and if this sort of fed interests you, read on, and into the Players Guide and then, the app form. Thanks. The promotion founded in September of 1997, saw the re-emergence of veteran Wrestling Promoter [name withheld]. The former head of Independent Championship E-Wrestling had found a new haunt in his native homeland. Several British Regional Promotions were merged together to form a stable platform to re-develop British Wrestling on the World Circuit. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= BWA Cards and Shows =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= One show has been penciled in for this new promotion, to air on Monday Nights, both on National Television, and on Satellite feed on BSkyB's channel Sky Sports 1. With contracts being discussed for syndication in the United States, a true sense of a World Wide Promotion can be sensed here, in this National Organization. The show itself, "Monday Night Carnage", will consist of roughly five live matches a week, plus dark matches and the like. Strat's, interviews, and any other important things for these cards needs to be sent to the Fed Head at [detail withheld] , and clearly marked. [BWA] Carnage _____ depending on what it is. BWA also airs a magazine rap up show, airing on Thursday of each week. This show, holding not only reviews, rumours, and latest news, is another place for wrestlers to air their interviews. BWA: Raw Footage, not to be missed. BWA Raw Footage (Thursday) As per information for cards, interviews, rumours etc. need to be sent to [name withheld] at, [detail withheld] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= BWA Role-playing =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The most important part of this fed is going to be the role-playing of it's members. The BWA works on a Closed Flash System. This means that each handler will mail his flashes to the Fed Head, err, me, and I'll mail them out in one lump to everyone. SEND ALL FLASHES TO: [detail withheld] The BWA also runs magazine shows twice weekly. Flashes are welcomed for this, and need to be sent to the respective person responsible for the show. [See above.] Flashes, and Interviews are also welcomed for cards. To make sure that your interview gets in, send it to the Card Writer at least a day prior to the card. As this is a booked fed, many people will think that it is the quantity of flashes that they write that will win them matches. That's not the case. Someone who writes one or two good length flashes, where his character develops or increases the amount of heat in an angle is going to do better than someone who sends in 10 2 line repetitive flashes. Like : I'm going to kick wrestler B's butt and if wrestler C gets in the way I'll get you as well. Swearing is also allowed, but keep this to a minimum. The one big rule on roleplaying is this... keep active! I would like to think that everyone can manage at least one flash per week. There is no set guideline to this, but that's what I hope we will get. Remember, an inactive wrestler will not remain interesting for very long, and I want to keep the BWA very interesting.... =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= BWA Matches and Strategies =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= To gain 'live' matches, you need to stay involved and at the forefront of what is going on in the BWA. These matches are the ones the fans would want to see. Matches for the cards are set up based on what challenges you are making in your interviews/flashes and other matchups which we feel will go down well. Dark matches follow much the same lines, but these are more oftenly booked by the Fed Head. If you specifically want a dark match on a card, just give Mike a shout. For matches, I highly encourage you to send in any strategies. If you want your guy to beat on his opponent on the outside for most of the match and it doesn't happen because you didn't tell me, don't blame me. If you want to jump a guy on his way to the ring, let me know. Sneak attacks are allowed, but let's try and keep them to a minimum. There are other way's of building heat. Again, I need any of this at least a day before the card, preferably two. If it's not in on time, I'll hold it back, and add it to the next show. Strats need to be mailed to the Shows Writer at least a day prior to the card coming out, preferably two-three. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= BWA Titles =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Belts. We have titles here as well! We currently sanction two singles titles and one tag team title. All BWA titles can only be won by pinfall or Submission unless specified. (See Note below for an exception to this.) The singles titles are:- BWA Heavyweight Title (Universal Weight. Must be defended only at PPV's. Other matches can be Title Matches however, at the discretion of either the c champion, or the fed head.) BWA National Title (Universal Weight. Must be defended once a fortnight, and at every PPV. Other matches can also be title matches as well.) And for Tag Teams we have: BWA National Tag Team Titles [Note: If a wrestler or team manage to beat the champion(s) on three separate occasions by dq or countout during one title reign, they win the belts.] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= BWA Stables =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Stables and Alliances are welcomed, but again, you've got to tell me otherwise I won't know to write them into a card. We wouldn't want your mystery stable member running into make the save when you jump someone else now would we! ? =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= BWA Rankings =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The BWA works on a points system for rankings. When a wrestler first enters the BWA he is credited with 100 points. He then gains and loses points depending on how he fares in his fights. The points gains, and losses are as follows:- +50 for a win by Pinfall or Submission +35 for a win by Dq or Count Out +20 for a draw +10 for a win over a jobber -35 for a loss by dq or Count Out -50 for a loss by Pinfall or Submission +75 for winning the Heavyweight Title/National Tag Team Titles +60 for winning the National Title =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= People and Places =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The following are the fictional BWA officials and places in the BWA Universe.
