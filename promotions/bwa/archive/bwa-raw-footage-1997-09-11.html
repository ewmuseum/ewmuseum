Wrestlers Background: Born 5th May 1971 as Patricio Wilheim Carter at his home in Toxsteth, Liverpool, England, of an English Mother and a German father. For the first years of his life, his family had to deal abuse and harrasment from Youths of Toxsteth resulting in him moving out to the rural area of Cumbria. He went to school in Cumbria and met the infamous Travis Williams and his five brothers who got him into wrestling. He would go to their gym every night and work out for an hour, and on Saturday's Travis would teach him and tutor him in wrestling. He left school with 10 GCSE's and 4 A-Levels all at C and above. He got into Liverpool University on a course which had a year in Harvard University, the first year oddly enough. The course of Educational and Media Studies was terminated as almost all the students couldn't cope, but Carter could. He was forced to go back to England, but decided to move toPoland instead where he met Cherry who was on holiday. He started wrestling full-time at the age of 18 in the Polish Wrestling Championship and became highly popular and won every belt at least once, and the tag titles twice with Travis Williams who came with him. During a week in Liverpool with his Mother, father and Sister, his mother and sister were brutally attacked by a gang of youths. They were battered and bruised when they escaped into their car and drove off erratically, the youths followed and they overtook Carter on his Motorbike, who followed the chase to it's climax where the two cars had crashed into a lamp-post knocking it over and leaving all of them dead. He stayed to console his father for a few days and to help organise the funeral and answer some questions, but the inquest concluded it was death by wreckless driving, his father encouraged Carter to forget and move on, which he half did, he has never forgotten and regularly visits his father when he can. He then left Poland for three months to begin teaching in Maryport, Cumbria where he taught P.E. but was fired after a brutal attack by some students on his way home in which he sent all four to hospital after he had to fight his way out of the attack. He was arrested and imprisoned for three nights before the charges were dropped, but he had nowhere to go now, as his house was part of his job. In 1991 he moved to America with Travis, and again met Cherry whilst she was on holiday with her new boyfriend. They had an affair whilst she was here and the boyfriend found out and left the country. She moved in with Travis and Carter whilst they fought hard in the AWA where they were both highly successful, with Carter winning the World Title five time. In his last year of his AWA career in 1995 he met Shawn Harrison in the final of the World's Sexiest Man where they became friends, Carter beat Harrison by 5 votes and became the World's Sexiest Man 1995. In 1996 he join the IFW after his old Liverpudlian friend, Lawrence Buffy, invited him into the IFW, they became Global Tag Title Champions before splitting up and having a small feud. He was fired from the IFW and joined the USIW where he met a friend of Harrison, Matt Hamilton, or Survival. The two began feuding for the World Title which Survival won and they became friends. Then on January 26th 1997 he was brutally double-crossed by some wrestling friends, and he, Survival and another friend, Assaf Levavy, formed the Renaissance Movement. The next day Flex joined and a while later Jeff Matthews before it's pending split up, and the HCW forced him to become mental and he began becoming erratic and took a month off wrestling to rest before coming back. On his come back, he formed the Rainbow Squad, which for a month was merely him and Cherry. The Education Crew soon came out of retirement to join him, and then Assaf Levavy and Jeff matthews joined. A few others, like Tommy Nightmare also hopped on board before Jeff left and beat both :evavy and Carter in seperate matches. Duiring thjis time he left the ACEW in a huff after Dylan Dice attacked him and Cherry, who quickly got out in time as a crowd member entered the fray, the female crowd member was stripped of her clothes, and filed charges against Dylan whilst Carter went straight to the hospital, contacted the President and quit. Suddenly the Rainbow Squad hit the fore front of Wrestling in August 1997, wehn Ryan Flynn was fired for berating a fellow member and turned on the Squad. carter won the NAWF World Title and began getting attacked verbally from every direction.
