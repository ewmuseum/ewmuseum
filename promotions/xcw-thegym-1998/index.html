Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This promotion's surviving record is one card, Summer Blast 98, broadcast 10 July 1998 and hosted at its own thegym.net address, called live by Vince Ross and John Dark — the latter billed as a “former World Champion and all-around jerk” still bitter about losing his own title.

The card opened as a grudge-heavy night: “Loose Cannon” Chris Manson stood as the number-one contender, waiting to find out who would emerge from a ten-man Royal Rumble to challenge him for the vacant World Championship, while a mystery third member of Jack McGriff's own Hell's Knights stable was promised to round out a war against Manson's own French Connection.

Magnus Pike outlasted the Rumble field and then pinned Manson to become champion — only for Crash Jackson to storm the ring, accusing Pike of costing him a rumble win Jackson claimed had been arranged in his own favor, and demanding an immediate title shot. Pike accepted; Jackson's own valet, Miss Precious, turned on him mid-match, clubbing him with the championship belt and letting Pike finish him for the pin.

How the Pike–Jackson rivalry, or the promised third Hell's Knight, played out afterward is not in the surviving record; the last surviving document under this promotion's own name is a commissioner-wanted post two months later, dated 13 September 1998.
