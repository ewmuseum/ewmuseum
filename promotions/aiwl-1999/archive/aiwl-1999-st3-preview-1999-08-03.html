ST3 "Believe the Hype" Intro Masters was wild, this will be wilder. Nuff said. Roleplay Timelines & Rules Not the most exciting part of the pay per view, but they've got to be talked about sometime...new matches will be announced weekly in this preview starting next week. As soon as your match is announced, you may begin roleplaying for it. All RP'ing must stop when the clock strikes 12 AM on Saturday, Augst 21. Stockpiling is, as always, discouraged and will be grounds to cause your wrestler to lose. Celebrities (Note: You can no longer request celebs to show up at the event. We've got enough.) The only catch is that AIWL officials are forcing him to dress up in his old Red Rooster attire from his in-ring days... They will be Michelle Williams from Dawson's Creek, Alyson Hannigan from Amercian Pie, and Mean Suvari, also from American Pie. All I've got to say is badda boom....badda bing....badda BANG. The Commish That's right, the whole F'N show himself will be showing up at Summer Thunder 3. He will, of course, being accompanying his biggest fan, the Rock, down to the ring for his big matchup. That's right, it's Slash McCoy's loving mother, and she'll be accompanying her son down to the ring for his big IC Title match. Ain't life grand? ST3 Matches Stingray vs. "Crazy" Joe Davola (c) World Heavyweight Title Match You heard it announced last night at Bloodrush, and it's going to be one of the biggest matches of the year. These two have more histroy than just about any combination of wrestlers in the league, and this match will be for all the marbles...not only for the World Title, but for ultimate bragging rights. Increasing the importance is the stipulation that the two men will not wrestle each other from a period of one year after the match. This means that the the loser could be effectively out of the World Title picture for nearly a year if the winner has a long reign. Misery vs. Brandon McMichaels US Title Match At Bloodrush we all saw McMichaels get a pinfall victory over Mis in a tag match...despite how tainted that might have been, it still impressed the AIWL executive committee to sign this match. Okay, so they impress easily. The big question is...will Misery even be there? We all saw him vanish on Masters at the hands of the Spanish Antichrist, and nobody has seen or heard from him since then. Slash McCoy vs. Ash (c) Intercontinental Title Match The Reckless Youths had the wool pulled over the eyes of Slash McCoy for quite some time, making him believe that there was tension between Ash and Biff Bradley. Then, last night at Bloodrush, the hoax was revealed as the IC Champ and his friend embarassed McCoy live on network television. Slash is now looking for revenge as well as his first championship belt in the AIWL. Darkamn vs. ManEater Exploding Ring Match I think we all know what's been going on between these two, so I'm not even going to bother recapping the feud. The rules, however, are simple. There's a bomb on the ring, and it's set to go off ten minutes after the match begins. The poor SOB that's left in there when the thing explodes is your loser. And if they're both stuck in there....ummm....I'll decide later. ICON vs. The Rock Grudge Match One of the most bizarre incidents at Bloodrush saw ICON joining the Goodfellas and, in the process, cost the Rock the World Heavyweight Title and, more importantly, his prized vagina. After a few days of not being able to have sex with himself, the AIWL's favorite hermaphordite will no doubt be frustrated to the point of wanting to really kick some ass at ST3. Peg Leg Sam vs. Mercy To determine the #1 Contender to the IC Title There's no real feud between these two, but they're fighting for a shot at some major gold, so there may be one very soon. Peg Leg is attempting to move up to the IC Title division after reigns with the Tag Team and Platinum Titles, whereas Mercy is just trying to move up to actually winning on a pay per view. Battle Royal That's right, we've got a Battle Royal set for our big show. And, no, the winner is not garaunteed any title shots or anything of that nature. It's just for fun. The competitors are... Trench, The Heels, Jeff Knight, "Outlaw" Frank Marshall, HardKore, Wycked, Johnny Rotten, Draycon, Aladinsane, Prophet, Whiplash, Drunk Bastard, Havok, Grunge Mosh Pit, The Deranged One, Organized Caos, Justun Stoppable Cheater vs. The Ultimate Worrier The match of the century If you thought that Cheater vs. Crow at WrestleBowl was spectacular, wait until you see this one. Just when we thought that nobody could match the sheer cowardace and stupidity of the Cheater, the Worrier blasted onto the scene like a bat out of hell. Can this cocky upstart dethrown the AIWL's Prince of Patheticness? Time will tell... Pre-match Interviews Want to ramble aimlessly about your match? As announced on Bloodrush, Matthew Bonner-Davis and Rey Punto will be married on the ST3 card! What would a wedding be without a bachelor party? Since we've got two bachelors, we're going to have two parties! MBD has used his executive influence in the league to FORCE all of the AIWL to attend one party or the other, and highlights from both will be aired on Tuesday, August 17. EMLL Night The AIWL's favorite Mexican wrestling promotion, the EMLL will be putting on a show to entertain the wrestlers on August 18. EMLL is home of favorites like Super Crazy Iguana and Red Acid, as well as the league that spawned Rey Punto. How I spent my Mexican Vacation... If we're spending a whole week south of the border, something interesting is bound to happen, and you'll be bound to see it on the 19th. The AIWL will be airing a special comprised of vignettes about what the wrestlers did with their little vacation in Mexico. AIWL AID On August 20 will be the first ever AIWL Aid concert! The league has booked some of the music industry's finest to perform in this charity gala in Mexico. The charity, of course, is keeping the AIWL open.
