NEW LEAGUE: Join the TWC!

6 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Are you looking for a fantasy wrestling league with the best roleplayers, consistent updates and quality cards that always come out on time? If so, then look no further! The TWC has arrived! The TWC promises to be based purely on roleplaying. We will actively recruit good roleplayers and carefully screen applicants to make sure that the TWC is the best e-league possible. In fact, if you recieved this message, *you* are being recruited! The TWC will be ran by *two* people sharing the workload to insure that cards are on time and updates are frequent. To make this all work though, we need top e-wrestlers to sign up. If you are interested, read on. Here's the lowdown on the league... The home page can be found at: http://www.goodnet.com/~rgerkin/twc/twc.htm There will be four titles: The Heavyweight Title-Open to anyone who thinks he has what it takes to be on top. The Cruiserweight Title-Open to wrestlers under 250 lbs. The Hardcore Title-Can only be won or defended in an unorthodox style match (i.e. Steel Cage, Triangle Match, Ladder Match, etc.). The Tag Team Titles-Open to two-man teams, of course. There will be a weekly card on Sundays and a pay-per-view on one Saturday each month. We are scheduling an eight-man Heavyweight Title tournament and the seedings will be assigned on a first come, first serve basis, so head to the home page and sign up now! TWC Co-Founders, [name withheld] [name withheld]
