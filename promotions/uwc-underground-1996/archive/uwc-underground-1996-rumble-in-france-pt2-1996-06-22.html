Rumble in France part 2: (Scene opens back up with a shot of the TV Title belt a la the old NWA Clash of the Champions intro oh so many years ago. John Cruise begins to speak.)
Cruise: The Television title - perhaps the most difficult championship in all of professional wrestling. It takes a determined wrestler to not only to win the belt, but to put it on the line, week after week. Who will be that man?
Let's find out... Hollins: The televison champ is no chump belt, I'll give it that. You have to have no concern for your body, health, and career. You put the belt on the line week after week, against a new opponent each time. I don't envy who ever wins this.
(Camera cuts to the ring to the world's most dangerous announcer, Winton "The Murph" Murphy) Murph: Ladies and gentlemen, it is now time for the Television Championship tournament. (***Crowd pops***) Earlier this afternoon, there was a random drawing for the entrants into the upcoming Rumble in France for the World Championship. They first eight names, however, were instead placed into brackets to do battle for the TV belt. These eight men will wrestle in a one-fall 10, minute match all the way to the finals.
("The Real Thing" by 2 Unlimited begins to play.) Murph: Introducing first, coming down the ring isle with his valet Miss Casandra....hailing from Woodbridge, New Jersey, and weighing in at 240 lbs....SHAWN "COOL KID" KIDMAN!!!!!
(Mixed reaction, positive from the females in the crowd) (Kidman comes down wearing tight, jean-like wrestling pants and brown leather wrestling boots. He also is wearing a leather Pilot jacket. He enters the ring, removes the bandana he is wearing, and spits on it as he throws it to the crowd. Casandra is a beautiful woman, bearing a resemblance to Cindy Crawford, and is dressed in elegant clothing.)
Hollins: Wow, Kidman got a bad draw. I thought he had a real chance to win the Heavyweight belt tonight.
Murph: And introducing his opponent ("Welcome to the Jungle" by Guns 'N Roses begins to play) From Louisville, Kentucky, weighing in at 305 lbs, here is STEVE "THE ALBINO GORILLA" MERCER!!!!! (***Crowd pops***)
(Mercer is a strapping, very tall white male. He is very pale, and wears white tights with the word "Albino" written down the right leg and a picture of a gorilla on the left leg.)
Cruise: Would you look at the size of this kid, Stevey. My notes say that he's a high-flyer, but he's at least 6'9"!
Hollins: He is a big kid, John. But he's making his debut against an established veteran like Kidman. I don't like his odds.
Cruise: I'd have to say that I agree. I've never seen this kid wrestle, and I know what Kidman can do.
***DING***DING***DING*** Cruise: There's the bell, and immediately, Kidman jumps Mercer from behind. A hard right, and another sends Mercer reeling. Irish whip into the ropes, and Kidman connects with a beautiful clothesline.
Hollins: I can see where this kid gets his nickname. Ugh, he's almost as pale as Huff! Cruise: Hey now, Stevey - that's your colleague your talking about. Kidman hits with a German Suplex and goes for the cover...1...2...kickout by
Mercer. Hollins: That was close. Hey Cruise, did you get a good look at Miss Cassandra. She reminds me of this girl back in '91 who followed me into the bathroom at this nightclub in Dallas...
Cruise: Enough. Mercer back to his feet as Kidman is mocking the crowd. Mercer connects with a hard right hand, and secures a standing headlock on
Kidman. Irish whip into the ropes, and Mercer hits with a flying headbutt.
Follows up with a vertical suplex. He's going to the top rope...misses with the flying leg drop from the top!
Hollins: Kidman immediately goes to work on the left leg of Mercer. What a smart wrestler - very ring wise. Kicks that leg of Mercer..once...twice.
Now he's going for the figure four, but Mercer rolls him up into a pin....1......2...kickout by Kidman.
Cruise: Kidman clips Mercer from behind, and takes out that leg again. That's a good strategy against a high-flyer like Mercer. Drops the elbow right on that knee. Kidman now with a swinging neckbreaker. Goes for the cover...1....2..kickout.
Hollins: Kidman's complaining to the official about a slow-count. Mercer up from behind...hits Kidman with a back breaker. Scoops Kidman and slams him. Hits the ropes and drops the elbow...but nobody's home as
Kidman rolls away, under the bottom rope to the safety of Miss Cassandra. Good move...I envy him. Must be nice and cozy over there. Cruise: He'd better watch out - MERCER WITH A SPRINGBOARD MOONSAULT OF THE
RING ROPE ONTO KIDMAN. Kidman's head hit the back of the steel security railing - he's hurt. Mercer moves in on Kidman and drops a leg. The crowd is getting behind this kid. Picks up Kidman, but Kidman rakes the eyes! Whips Mercer into the ring apron.
Hollins: Good move. Kidman kisses a female admirer at ringside, and rolls back in. The referee's count is up to seven...eight...nine....and Mercer rolls back in. Kidman with a scissor kick stuns Mercer, and he connects with clothesline. He going to the top. Moonsault coming up here?
Cruise: I'd say so, but he's playing to the crowd too much. He's to the top, but Mercer is up. Here comes the Moonsault, but Mercer CATCHES HIM
AND HITS WITH A PILEDRIVER!!! Goes for the cover...1.....2......3!!!!! ("Welcome to the Jungle" begins to play.) Murph: Here's your winner....Steve "The Albino Gorilla" Mercer!!!!!!!! Hollins: I can't believe this. Kidman's got to be kicking himself for making that mistake.
Cruise: What a match to start this tournament! Mercer with the big win, and the fans are loving it!
Hollins: Don't rub it in. Maybe this next match will turn out more to my liking. Take it away Murph!
("No More Tears" by Ozzy Osborne begins to play) Murph: This second match of the TV tournament is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first from Las Vegas, Nevada...weighing 165 pounds...NITROX!!!!! (Nitrox comes to the ring wearing blue and purple tights) (A French version of "Sexy Boy" begins to play) Murph: And his opponent, from Montreal, Quebec, Canada...weighing 234 lbs....LE REBEL!!!!! (The arena goes dark wile white and blue lights start flashing everywhere. A blue light shines on the floor with a white fleur de lys leading his path to the ring. Le Rebel is a well-tanned male caucasian who wears fleur de lys shaped blue shades, and the same sort of pants. He has dirty blonde hair, short in the back with long bangs, slicked back in front.)
(***Crowd boos***) Cruise: Le Rebel struts his way in here, doesn't he Hollins? Hollins: Sure does. He's young, he's talented, he's cocky...but why not? He's got Nitrox here at an extreme size and weight disadvantage. Cruise: There's the bell, and Nitrox immediately opens up with a barrage of kicks. Savate kick to Le Rebel's midsection doubles him over, and a kick combo to the midsection and face sends him to the mat. Nitrox is a fast little fellow, is he not?
Hollins: Fast and shifty...reminds me of you fifteen years ago Cruise. Another thrust kick to the head stuns Le Rebel, and Nitrox connects with a leaping DDT! Cruise: Nitrox going to the top rope to keep the barrage going - He MISSES
WITH THE MOONSAULT. Le Rebel moves in...hits with a nice back suplex.
Whips Nitrox into the ropes....hits with a flying dropkick. Picks Nitrox up, and here's an abdominal stretch.
Hollins: Le Rebel survived that initial barrage by Nitrox, and is taking back at him. This kid looks strong. Maybe he'll get Le Win, or Le belt.
Cruise: Nitrox breaks the hold with a hiptoss and nails Rebel with a reverse spin kick. Hits the ropes...dropkick misses a Le Rebel ducks.
Nitrox up...LE SUPERKICK!!!! Le Rebel connects with his finisher out of nowhere! Goes for the pin...1...2....3!
(French version of "Sexy Boy" begins to play). Hollins: Beautiful manueuver by Le Rebel. I'm going to predict that there's your next TV champ.
Murphy: Introducing the participants of the third, first round match of the
TV tournament...

(He has long, uncombed hair in a pony tail off to one side. He wears a tattered jeans jacket to the ring with a navy blue tights outfit with brown. black, blue and green crooked stripes is a random pattern. Parts of the crowd are heard to chant "Crazy!". His eyes have an unfocused look.)
("Pressure" by Billy Joel begins to play) Murphy: ...and his opponent...ing in at 225 lbs...from Boston, Massachusetts.... ...here is Kenny Marshall!! (crowd pops) (Marshall comes down the aisle smiling and high-fiving fans. He is a young, handsome man with green tights.)

Rumble in France if he were.
Hollins: I've got a feeling... Cruise: These fans have been treated to some the best wrestling since the
Supercards tonight and that's sure to continue....WHOA!!! Marshall's music hasn't even cut off and already Von Gundy has dove from the top rope and onto a very stunned Marshall!!! He was knocked into the crowd and they obviously want no part of this!!! He should be disqualified for that!
Hollins: How can you disqualify a man from a match that hasn't started yet? Kind of like getting diharrea BEFORE going to Taco Bell!
***DING***DING***DING*** Cruise: Von Gundy is really sending a message here...there's the bell...Von Gundy has Marshall's head and is smashing it into the security railing! Marshall is in serious trouble!
Hollins: Von Gundy, being the Rhodes Scholar that he is, finally tosses Marshall into the ring so he can actually win this match. Cruise: He's certainly lived up to his name...now he's biting Marshall's forehead! Unbelieveable! The ref is trying to get him to stop...
Hollins: With obvious success...does Von Gundy even understand English? Cruise: Marshall grabs Von Gundy by the ears...a jawbreaker by Marshall finally makes him let up...a good opportunity for the youngster to catch his breath and get some momentum...Marshall with a single-leg pickup drops the
Texas Lunatic on his back...an elbowdrop to the thigh of Von Gndy...and another...
Hollins: Tough to do a flaming somersalt super-splash or whatever like this monster might be capable of when you can't walk. Smart move, jobber!

Von Gundy to his feet..a wicked elbow to Marshall's head stings him momentarily...irish whip into the ropes...drop down by Von Gundy...back the other way...Von Gundy grabs Marshall by the head and tosses him out of the ring! This could get ugly!
Hollins: If we're lucky. Ha! Von Gundy is screaming "Crazy!" at the fans at ringside! They're pointing at him and shouting it back! I've seen that look in a man's eyes before in this business -- it means "get the hell away now, fat boy!"
Cruise: Oh, dear! Von Gundy with a flying elbow drop from the apron that connects to the sternum of Marshall! Has this man no regard for his own well being?
Hollins: His body is a temple...which he's descecrating at the moment... Cruise: Von Gundy picks up Marshall by the waist...a bearhug?...NO! He delivered a Hotshot onto the edge of the timekeeper's table!!! Marshall's bottom lip is split wide open!!!
Hollins: Let's just pray that they don't make use of OUR table! Cruise: Von Gundy back in the ring...Marshall is not moving...count of four...five...six...
Hollins: The Texas Lunatic is shouting "Tick...tick...tick..." to the crowd! Is this guy's manager the Unabomber or something?
Cruise: We're at eight...nine...TEN! Marshall has been counted out! Murphy: Here is your winner..."The Texas Lunatic" Mercury Von Gundy!!! ("The Movie" begins to play) Cruise: If that wasn't a display of wanton violence, I don't know what is. Anyway let's get to the last of the first round matches of the tourney. Take it away, Murph! York...Viper!!!!!!(Mixed reaction from the crowd.)

Viper is tired of waiting for TKO and he is going after him now. But he runs into a TKO clothesline! I'm impressed, did TKO go to Warrior University?

Cruise: Viper is going outside to think this over. TKO is following him out. This is not TKO area. Viper is getting close to Bartilo and TKO is going after..No! Supekick by Viper! TKO ran into that one.
