Need a new federation? Try UWC...

20 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings one and all: We are announcing a new e-wrestling league to begin in the middle part of May. Our league, the Underground Wrestling Circuit (UWC) will be on the cutting edge of e-wrestling. We know you all have heard this all before, but we actually mean it. If you chose to join UWC, we guarentee you cards when they are scheduled, 12 PPV's a year, the best webpage in e-wrestling and a handler-friendly relationship with us. If you are active and creative with your wrestlers, then the UWC is the place to be.
*** UWC FED HEADS *** President - [name withheld] - [detail withheld] Vice Presidents- [name withheld] - [detail withheld] [name withheld] - [detail withheld] We three are the heads of the federation - one that is a combination of booking and a dice simulation. The majority of the federation will be booked because we believe in promoting creativity, activity, and having fun with your wrestlers. This does not mean that our guys will dominate the league, nor does this mean that you will have to kiss our collective asses to win - like in some leagues. If you are creative and involved then your chances of success go up - it's that simple. Of course, this means that we will not allow any REAL wrestlers or managers in the UWC, or any obvious clone there of. Use your imagination and send in new and original characters, and this league can prosper.
We propose a one month time frame for the organization of this league. We will bring to you our first UWC Pay-per-View, THE RUMBLE IN FRANCE, between the 18th and 25th of May. At this event, we will hold a royal rumble type event to decide our inaugral UWC World Heavyweight Champion as well as a rumble match for tag teams to decide the World tag champs. After this, you will recieve The Underground Pipeline, our weekly newsletter. This will update you on rankings, upcoming events, rumors, and general weekly information. After this, our regular weekly shows will begin.
On Wednesday, the Wednesday Night Workshop. This will be the smaller of the shows, it will include 5 matches and a talkshow. On Sunday we'll have the larger show, Sunday Bloody Sunday. It will have about 7 or 8 matches. We will also have a PPV once a month, 6 major PPV and 6 minor ones.
The minor ones will be like In Your House. Some minor PPV names are: Pier Six at Portland, Hawiian Holocaust, Foothills Fiasco, and Down Under Devastation to name a few. CHAMPIONSHIPS: UWC World Heavyweight Championship - decided by the winner of the Rumble in France singles match UWC Pacific Rim Championship (Intercontinental type belt) - decided by tournament UWC T.V. Champion - decided by tournament - This title will be defended every week on the Wednesday Night Workshop
UWC Tag Team Champions - decided by winners of the Rumble in France tag match UWC Pan Am Tag Titles - decided by tournament *** UWC MATCH STYLE *** All regular matches (non-title) will be summarized. This is to allow for more matches and organazation. All PPVs and title matches will be play by play format. Also any main event on a show will be play by play.
***UWC PERSONALITIES *** These will be the UWC personalities on the shows: President: Alex Ortega a.k.a. The Cleveland Scorpion (Face) Announcers: Stevey "Slice of Heaven" Hollins (Heel) John Cruise (Face) Ring Announcer: Winton Murphy "The Murph" Backstage Announcer: "The Mangler" Erica Huff (Neutral) ***CREATION SHEET*** Here is the wrestler creation sheet: Please use the sheet when you create your guys. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- WRESTLERS NAME: YOUR NAME: YOUR E-MAIL ADDRESS: WRESTLER'S HOMETOWN: HT: WT: HEEL, FACE, NEUTRAL: GENERAL STYLE OF WRESTLER (eg. FINISHING MOVE AND DESCRIPTION: OTHER GENERAL COMMENTS: -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- *** OTHER GENERAL GUIDELINES *** There is a limit of 4 wrestlers per person. If you wish, you may create a stable, but please tell us when you submit your wrestlers. Also, when you submit your wrestlers initially, please note at the bottom of the Creation sheet if you wish for the wrestler to be entered in the Rumble in France.
The first 20 ***Singles*** wrestlers will be the 20 participants for the singles Rumble, and how many ever tag teams we get for the Tag Team version.
Additional information will be sent out to you on the inaugral edition of the Underground Pipeline which will come your way before the Rumble.
If you have any questions, feel free to e-mail any of the three of us, or pose them to us when you send in your character creation sheets. We will either e-mail you personally, or answer general questions in the first
Pipeline. Thanks for your time and hope to see you in the UWC! -Jack, Joey, and Jeff
