Johnson was a bit taken back by Warchief's clubbing attack. Warchief controlled the tempo early with numerous chops and spin kicks. "Big" Buster blocked a slam and turned it into a back suplex. Warchief was in a rage and bounced back up. He had Johnson screaming in pain, when he ran the big man's face across the top rope. At this point, Warchief had Johnson in the corner with some more chops.
Not once did he even attempt a pin. In his mad attack, he made a mistake. He stopped hitting Johnson and demanded where his manager was. Johnson gave him an answer. A clothesline opened the door for him to pound Warchief to his knees with lefts and rights. A Powerslam, a backbreaker, and a BIG elbow drop off the ropes were enough for a two and a half count. Things quickly went down hill for Warchief. After mustering a slam on Johnson, Warchief was powerbombed right after. Johnson went to work on the lower body. Kicks and punches knocked the air out of the realing Warchief. A Neck Cutter (Swinging neckbreaker) was all it took to get the three count. After the win, Ugly Jane came running to the ring and swore revenge on Johnson. Johnson waived her off and strode away.

Corzine dropped Jersey with a side Russian leg sweep,but a two count after a slam wasn't enough. Buddy attempted a piledriver, but was backdropped. Jersey lifted Corzine up for a hotshot, but lost control when The Swagman tugged at his foot. Jersey fell to his knees, as the ref warned The Swagman. Corzine took the win after executing the Hardin County Backdriver (Doctor Bomb).

Hammer into the security railing, but he reversed it, sending Method Man instead into the steel steps. He kicked him in the gut and went for a DDT, but Method Man escaped with a HUGE low blow. He rolled Hammer into the ring and pounded away on Hammer with little resistance, bringing huge boos from the crowd. He then went put Hammer up in an airplane spin and brought him down with a tiger driver to complete the Shaolin Slam. He went for a cover on a motionles Hammer, but his overconfidence got the better of him, and he picked him up on two and a half to the shagrin of The Genius. He taunted the crowd and Johnny Smasher some more while stomping and leg dropping Hammer. He then stomped on the ring twice and to everyone's surprise Mustafa Rauf came out from under the ring, weilding a chair.
Method Man tried to send Hammer into the ropes, but he somehow reversed it and Method Man went in instead, and was summarily clubbed with the chair intended for Hammer. Hammer crawled on top of Method Man and got the three count with his last bit of strength.
