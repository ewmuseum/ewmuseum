Marshall then took Deth and attempted to run him into the post, bu the stronger Deth counterd and rammed Marshall shoulder first instead.
The Dream Team was over quickly to protect Marshall, and both men rolled back into the ring before the count was up. Deth then went on the offensive, hitting with an elbow smash, then following it with a Northern
Lights suplex which got him a two count. The match then went on for about ten minutes, with both men exchanging the advantage and two-counts. With two minutes left in the time limit, Marshall got Deth in a sleeperhold that put Deth to the mat. After a minute in the hold, Deth was rallied by the crowd, and broke the hold by ramming Marshall headfirst into the turnbuckle. Deth then got a two count with a small package. Sensing time was running out, Marshall went for a flying headscissors into a pinning predicament, but Deth caught him in mid-air and nailed him with a powerbomb. Deth then hit Marshall with the "Certain Deth" (dragon suplex from the second rope), and scored the pin with only seconds to spare.
