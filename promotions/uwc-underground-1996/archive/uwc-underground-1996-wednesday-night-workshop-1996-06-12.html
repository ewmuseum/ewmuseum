This was enough to get a two count of his own. Lowe took his time with an armwringer. Dempsey couldn't reach the ropes and was grimacing from the pain. The situation soon changed. Dempsey reversed it and used the hold to sunset flip Lowe into the corner. Lowe caught the turnbuckle on the chin and was staggered.
Dempsey followed with a clothesline and a few punches to the head. He hefted Lowe up to the top turnbuckle and began climbing. "Doc" Holliday was yelling to Lowe, to no avail. He was too weak to resist. Dempsey went for the superplex. The "Rowdy" one held his ground and pushed Dempsey back. Unfortunately, for Dempsey, his legs did the 7-10 split on the ropes. The crowd let out a collective groan at this sight. Dempsey slumped to the mat. Lowe splashed Dempsey from the second rope to get the win.
