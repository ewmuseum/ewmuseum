H2O vs. Elijah Hunter (SWA U.S. Title Match)

10 December 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kent Neuman: Well Sean the crowd has been absolutely electrifying tonight and where about to have a huge match. Sean Diamond: Yeah these two have been fighting over SWA United States Championship Kent Neuman: Here comes our challenger. Orlando Morales: Ladies and gentleman this match is scheduld for one fall and is for the SWA United States Championship. Introducing first weighing in at 245 pounds hailing from Miami, Flordia he is Mr. Sunday Morning, H2O! "Sympathy for the Devil" by The Rolling Stones crashes thru the arena, as H20 extensive entrance takes place the crowd is going absolutely nuts. Sean Diamond: This crowd loves him. Kent Neuman: They can love him all they want his opponent SWA United States Champion "Prime Time" Elijah Hunter is the real deal and is going to have his way with this punk H2O. Sean Diamond: You'll be eating your words ten minutes from now. Kent Neuman: Oh shut your hole Orlando got introduce the champion! H20 entrance is coming to an end he has finally made his way to the ring, when "I'm Comin'" by Silk the Shocker interupts the end of his entrance. Orlando Morales: And Introducing next Your SWA United States Champion Weighing in at 256lbs from Oakland California he is "The Main Attraction" "Prime Time" Elijah Hunter. Hunter makes his way dwon to the ring all the while keepiing his eyes intentily focused on H2O. Sean Diamond: Of course both men ussually have their managers or entourage with them. But, earlier today they where told that they will be banned from ringside, this is a good old fashion beat down dragg'em out wrestlin' match! Kent Neuman: I'll agree with you there my friend this is going to be one hell of a match. By the ring girl is exhibating off the belt to everyone in the stadium, as Hunter and H2O just stare eachother down. The sound of the bell lights up the crowd as the two men charge in and lock up with eachother. Hunter quickly slips out and drives his knee into H2O sternum, Hunter begins to deliver some heavy rights crashing into the skull of H2O, finally H20 Blocks one of the shots and delivers one of his own. Knocking Hunter off balance, H20 quickly reacts and snaps hunter body into the ropes hunter comes flying towards H2O off the ropes he grabs Hunter and snaps back a Belly to belly suplex. Hunter head comes crashing into the mat, he wisely rolls out of the ring to gain his composure. The referee begins to count. Kent Neuman: He could just lose and still retain the title, that's why he's a thinking champion. Sean Diamond: Some might call that cowardly! Kent Neuman: And those people are idiots! H2O argues with Hunter to get back in the ring. Hunter now stands on the edge of the ring, the two men come face to face argueing with eachother Hunter drives his head into H2O and nails a Jawbreaker across the ropes. H2O falls back and hits the canvas Hunter grin grows larger as he slides under the ropes into the ring picking up H20 he drives his head between his legs and Nails... Fallen Angel Power Bomb Hunter rolls up H20 1... 2.. H2O Kicks out. Kent Neuman: That bloody referee needs a talking to he's slowier then Molasses! Sean Diamond: That count was just fine, you just want Hunter to win! Kent Neuman: Can't say your wrong about that! Hunter clearly displeased by the count argues with referee, he pushes the referee into the corner the referee hits the canvas dazed. Hunter continues to yell at the referee, H2O picks himself up slowly Hunter turns around and H2O drives his forearm straight up into the groin of Hunter. H20 jumps to his feet and rakes the eyes of Hunter before he whips into the corner. The crowd goes nuts because the know it's coming H2O looks around and takes it all in. Sean Diamond: TSUNAMI!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Kent Neuman: Oh shut the hell up! H2O nails Hunter with a a big splash in the corner, Hunter stumbles out into the center of the ring and falls down. Hunter tries to climb to his feet H2O stands behind him stalking him waiting for him to turn around and face him. Hunter finally climbs to his feet and turns around unaware of H2O stalking and waiting for him. ALL WASHED UP Sean Diamond: He's out it's over pack up shop we've got a new champion! Kent Neuman: H2O kick is about as powerful as well whatever is not powerful... You know what I mean. Sean Diamond: I know tat I work with a Moron who sometimes doesn't make any sense! H2O rolls up Hunter. 1... 2...... Hunter kicks out. Sean Diamond: I spoke to soon! Kent Neuman: You did and you need to shut the hell up while we watch this epic comeback! H2O jumps to his feet and shakes his head at the ref, before he goes back to stomping a hole in Hunter.Grabbing Hunter he pulls him to his feet and locks int the Double underhooks for the Hydro Spike. Hydro Spike Sean Diamond: Okay now it's gotta be over there is no way! H2O rolls up Hunter once again. 1............. 2............. Hunter Kicks out again Sean Diamond: you've got to be kidding me! Kent Neuman: Oh that's no joke my friend Prime Time has the Heart of a Champion. H2O shakes his head in disbelief he looks down at Hunter barely moving body, H2O quickly decides there is only one way to settle this. Kent Neuman: He's not going too... Sean Diamond: Oh I think he is going to give Hunter a taste of his own medicine H2O prepares and locks in the Inverted STF. Attitude Adjustment Hunter reels in Pain as he screams for Mercy, the referee calls the Match. Sean Diamond: He just beat him with his own finisher how's that for adding insult to injury! Kent Neuman: I'll tell you this I find that rude and very arrogant! Sean Diamond: I find rude and arrogant that doesn't stop you from doing your job. Kent Neuman: Whatever take it away Orlando! Orlando Morales: Ladies and Gentlemen your winner by sbmission and your new SWA United States Champion H2O! H2O is handed the belt the crowd goes nuts as his arm is raised in victory as "Sympathy for the Devil" by The Rolling Stones. Kent Neuman: Can we please hurry this up and get to the next match! Sean Diamond: I told you that you'd eat your words!
