Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

SWA's only surviving document is a single United States Championship match, broadcast 10 December 2008, with both men's own entourages banned from ringside for it.

The challenger, Mr. Sunday Morning H2O, wore down the defending champion, “Prime Time” Elijah Hunter, surviving three kickouts — the last off Hunter's own finisher, turned against him — before finishing him with an Inverted STF for the submission.

H2O left the ring the new SWA United States Champion. No other match, roster, or later notice from the promotion survives.
