Folks, read below about EAPW, Electronic Action Packed Wrestling!!!!!!! ####### ####### ######### # # ### # # # # # # ### # # # # # # ####### ####### ######### # ### # ### # # # ### ### ### # # # ### ### ####### # ### ### # E Electronic A Action P Packed W Wrestling ---=---=----=---=-Macro By: [name withheld]----=----=---=---- I. -=-The EAPW in General-=- The EAPW stands for Electronic Action Packed Wrestling, and is one of the many imaginary fantasy wrestling leagues in cyberspace, in other words, the internet. The EAPW exists to provide wrestling fans with a creative and a enjoyable gaming format. Ever dream of putting on tights and walking down that long aisle into the squared circle? Well, EAPW is a fed where you create your own wrestling character and guide him to the World Title or to nowhere. :) EAPW was created by [name withheld] and [name withheld]. [name withheld] has ran a fed before called the PTWF. The PTWF was great but folded due to the lack of help. [name withheld] has also roleplayed in fed's such as ERWI, ICE, PTWL, EXTREME, HOW, WAeW, IeWF, NOW, USCW, and CHAOS!!!. [name withheld] has also wrote matches for various leagues besides his own. So it's safe to say your first Co-President has a tremendous amount of experience in the front office. [name withheld], has ran the ECWA, which also was great but folded once again due to the lack of help. Mr. [name withheld] has also roleplayed in such feds as ERWI, WAeW, ICE, IeWF, and NOW, and [name withheld] is the current Vice President of NOW. So it's very safe to say [name withheld] has a tremendous amount of experience in the front office too, which means well, your Presidents are very experienced. :) II. -=-Rules-=- EAPW will not allow any "Out of the World" gimmicks. Meaning, the President's, will not tolerate wrestlers with gimmicks that are too far fetched. For example, I don't want any plumbers or clowns in our league. _NO_ comic book gimmicks, just keep it plain and simple. As a general rule of the thumb, applicants whose wrestlers are similar characters in the league that are already established in EAPW, are not admitted, even if they are the top roleplayers. I mean, would you want to have five undertakers running around? _ALL_ wrestlers must be original and not taken off any "real" wrestlers. (i.e. No "Stone Cold's", "Nature Boy's", or "Candian Crippler's".) You can submit as many wrestler's as you want in EAPW, but be prepared to roleplay with them. If you create tons of character's and if that character is inactive for two weeks, that character is no longer in EAPW. We are probably one of the only feds out there who allow unlimited wrestlers, but just keep in mind to roleplay with them or else... [Fires Bullet] He's Fired. :) In order to ensure each player recieves sizable activity and particpation in EAPW, the roster must be kept relatively small. EAPW allows 24 singles wrestlers and 8 tag teams. This allows each wrestler a match once a week and the attention the player deserves in a league. Upon being admitted to EAPW, the President's will ask for a sample roleplay. Here's an idea of what is expected of in your sample roleplay. III. -=-EAPW Particpation-=- Particpation in EAPW is essential and the key to success. Roleplaying (See Roleplaying Regulations Below) takes up much of that particpation. You are required to submit a minimum of one solid quality flash a week. This is your major flash, meaning it's your big time flash, that developes your character. Read Roleplaying Regulations below for full understanding of your roleplaying standards. Other ways of particpation is sending in strats, sneak attacks, and getting involved in your current angles and storylines. We'll get into more detail as the league progresses. IV. -=-EAPW Programs-=- EAPW currently promotes one live weekly card: -EAPW Pain Game (Every Sunday) In addition, there are three features that come out during the week: -EAPW Update (Daily) -EAPW Interviews (Every Two Interviews recieved) -EAPW Rankings (Follow's Update, after Pain Game) Pay Per View's occur every two months. V. -=-EAPW Roleplay Regulations-=- EAPW, emphasis on, good writing=good push. A long roleplay doesn't neccessarliy mean that is a good roleplay. A well written quality 6k roleplay can beat out a long, dull, boring, and dragging 12k roleplay any day of the week. Though, the longer the roleplay, the better. Remember the three most important things when creating a roleplay. Content, Length, and Detail. Your flashes will be sent out every two days, in the feature EAPW Interviews. Failure to produce roleplays of any sort, whether they be interviews, strats, or whatever, in a short period of time, will result in a cautionary warning from the President, and if the inactivity continues, your wrestler may be suspend or fired by EAPW altogether. VI. -=-The Ground Rules-=- Nobody is stopping you from having fun, and that is the main reason you come to EAPW, is to have fun. But like all good things, there must be rules that each player must abide by, in consideration of EAPW Council and your fellow players. Please follow these regulations: DON'T use another player's wrestler, unless there has been an OK by the other party and by the President. Furthermore, don't ask for your wrestler to hosptialize another wrestler unless there has been consent from the other party and the two President's. DON'T attack announcers or any of the EAPW staff, unless there has been express permission by the President. You can attack as many no name jobbers as you want. DON'T use excessive profanity. Often when you go into realization of your character, obscene language is all part of the game when it is hot and heavy that your wrestler is going through. However, dirty and very vulgar words will be edited out in your flashes. Words such as "ass" and "damn" will be allowed, though stronger words than it will not. You have to keep in mind that such language is not acceptable in television programmings. It is very possible to express your anger and extreme emotions without using a string of four letter words. VII. -=-How does the EAPW operate?-=- EAPW is a ''booked'' league. Meaning the match results are predetermined by the EAPW council. Results are not made up of, with that single match in mind. Generally, it accounts for the bigger picture, meaning the angles, storylines, and so forth. And your basis of roleplaying helps a tremendous way in helping the match results. However, for instance, two good roleplayers have a match against each other, both have been flashing all week, and now it comes to conclusion that the President's will take it upon themselves to determine results with a coherent storyline in mind. VIII. Titles Electronic Action Packed Wrestling currently sanctions three singles titles: -EAPW World Heavyweight Championship (Universal Weight. Defended every month, and at every supercard, and PPV, unless specified by the President's.) -EAPW National Heavyweight Championship (Universal Weight. Defended at least two times a month, and at every Supercard, and at every PPV, unless specified by the President's.) -EAPW Flyweight Championship (Weight limit 245 Pounds. Defended every two weeks, and at every Supercard, and PPV.) EAPW also sanctions one Tag Team Title: -EAPW World Tag Team Championship (Universal Weight Limit. Defended every month, at every supercard, and at every PPV.) The addition of a North American Tag Title Championship is currently being considered by the EAPW council. Titles can only change hands by pinfall or submission, unless specified before the match. IX. -=-Rankings-=- EAPW uses a point system for its rankings. Once getting admitted to EAPW, the wrestler starts off with zero points and works his way up by getting victories. A wrestler is not deducted any points if he loses. Point System are as follows: + 20 Points for match if win by pinfall or submission + 10 Points for match if won by DQ or Countout + 5 Points for match if a Draw Rankings will be sent out every week after the Update that follows Pain Game.
