A 1996 e-fed, formerly the United Wrestling Association, running a Saturday Slam and a Monday Unleashed show and crowning its own World and Tag Team Champions through open challenges rather than a fixed card. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

No founding announcement survives for the promotion that became the UCA; its own recruitment sheet describes it as “formally known as the United Wrestling Association,” and its earliest surviving document is already a live show. It ran two weekly broadcasts, Monday Night Unleashed and Saturday Night Slam, building toward supercards rather than settling championships on a fixed schedule.

Its Ultimate Championship was seeded by a ten-man battle royal on the first surviving Unleashed broadcast (25 March 1996), won by Euphoria, who carried that seed through a tournament bracket across the following Saturday Night Slam before beating Executor for the title itself at the promotion's own “FIRST ever King of Wrestling Supercard” on 16 April. The same supercard settled its World Championship by a fifteen-entrant battle royal, drawn from a field that included Mongol, Thonker, PainKiller, Anarchist, Budda Khan, Mistress of Doom, Dan Gravis, Dana Fred, Golfer, Trisha, Roger “Space” Silko, Avenger and Red Buffalo, with Lynx winning the belt outright.

The same card carried the promotion's ongoing rivalry between two tag teams, the Dragons (Hei Dan Na and Lin Chu) and the Kongs (Giant Kong and Monster Kong), and a stipulation match built around a manager rather than a title: Death Dealer defeated Christian Kingsworth in a cage match contested for “time alone” with Kingsworth's manager, Ambition, only for Davey “Sexy Boy” Scott's own companion, Sexy Lady, to intervene and drag Ambition off before Death Dealer could collect. Scott himself returned to the promotion that same night, billing himself as a veteran of several other e-feds, and beat Richard Foro with a finishing move he called the Spindrive.

How it ended isn't in the surviving record. Its surviving documents run only from that first Unleashed broadcast through the King of Wrestling supercard three weeks later.
