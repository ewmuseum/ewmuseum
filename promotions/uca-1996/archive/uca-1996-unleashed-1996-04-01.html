A plot to lure the Death Dealer away from her beloved Christian Kingsworth. What some woman would do.. tonight, he is up against the all mighty Eurphoria.. now this is something we should be waiting to see. First up, we shall see Hei Dan Na in action.. Lynx takes him down with a slingshot facebuster. Lynx catches him in a Northern Lights suplex. Richard Recob counts: One, two, three. The winner is Lynx. Time of match: 0:04:44 JW: A high performance and dominating matchup for Lynx. He shall go onto the round two. Next up, The man with the woman who was responsible for the defeat of Death Dealer.. Christian Kingsworth. After the matchup, CK spoke. ------------------------------- Christian Kingsworth: Death Dealer was just the first dtop on my way to the UCA gold. Did he actually think Ambition wanted him? Ambition: That guy smelled awful! It's a good thing Christian got there when he did. Christian Kingsworth: It doesn't matter who I face next, Christian Kingsworth is the next UCA Champion! ------------------------------ JW: You know.. we all want to know, why she do what she did (reshowing the strip tease portion), I mean, was Christy in trouble? His oppanant is Euphoria.. who spoke.. ------------------------------- Euphoria: A couple days ago, I beat ten other men, and I became the #1 seed in the tournament. Tonight I beat .......... someone! I am the number one seed for a reason. It's because I'm the greatest athlete in this league! I'm the toughest guy in this league! I'm the strongest guy in this league! I'm EUPHORIA! ------------------------------- JW: Yes, and he... whoa .. wait a minute.. I got something coming in.. from.. the Executor.... ------------------------------- Executor: Euphoria.. how dare you talk.. of being strong. I will dominate the UCA by force, and my first victim of circumstance.. shall be you.. rest in peace.. -------------------------------- JW: Ladies and Gentlemen, the Executor has spoken and he has declared a challenge.. man, I remember watching this guy.. very tough and mean. on with the match. Christian Kingsworth whips Euphoria into the ropes, but Euphoria reverses it. Euphoria goes for a kick to the thigh, but Christian Kingsworth blocks it. Christian Kingsworth takes him down with a back heel kick. Christian Kingsworth executes a kneelift. Christian Kingsworth executes an enzuilariato. Christian Kingsworth takes him down with an enzuigiri. Christian Kingsworth goes for a lariat, but Euphoria counters it with a duck-down move. Euphoria nails him with a double underhook backbreaker. Euphoria runs into the ropes. Euphoria hits Christian Kingsworth with a clothesline. Euphoria executes a jack-knife power bomb. Richard Recob counts: One, two, kickout. Euphoria nails him with an enzuilariato. Euphoria executes a butt-bump. Euphoria executes a falling splash. Richard Recob counts: One, two, three.. JW: Euphoria takes out Christian Kingsworth and has won the first contestant of the title match at King of Wrestling supercard. Now Ambition is getting a little redundant.. she tried the same manuever she did last week.. but instead of falling for it.. Euphoria pushed her back and to the ground. Euphoria has no feeling.. none.. and everyone should be afraid, very afraid. Next up is a triangle matchup.. it is Lin Chu vs. Trisha vs. Richard Foro. Trisha tags out to Richard Foro. Richard Foro whips Lin Chu into the ropes. Lin Chu takes him down with a jumping side kick. Lin Chu takes him down with a leg hammerlock. Richard Foro reaches the ropes after being trapped for 5 seconds. Lin Chu executes a kick to the thigh. Lin Chu goes for a tiger suplex, but Richard Foro counters it with a backward kick. Lin Chu nails him with a hiptoss. Lin Chu executes a tiger driver. Richard Recob counts: One, two, three. The winner is Lin Chu. Time of match: 0:09:47. 9 minutes have gone by. ~~~~~ JW: Grueling.. Lin Chu is either the better half or very close to it of the the dragons. That is all the time we have.. so see you all on Slam!
