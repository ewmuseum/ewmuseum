Tragedy Strikes fWo Countdown! Many Dead & Injured!

4 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A fire at the Madison Square Garden has taken the lives of fWo veterans and management, the tragedy striking the heart of the national wrestling promotion as it celebrated one of the biggest shows of it's long and storied history. Sources say that a fire began in the boilerroon of the Garden and even though it was mostly confined to that one room, many fWo staffers were trapped and burned to death in the inferno. What sources cannot tell us is just what started the fire or just why so many key personel were in the dark basement of the Gardens. Those confirmed dead in the fWo tragedy are fWo veterans and personel Neo, Fallen, Spyder, Temario, Samuel Smooth, Twitch, and John Willis. Those injured are Silver Fox of an apparent heart attack, and Flying Frenchie of what is said to be a gunshot wound. Yukio, Geoff Green and Ted Tallison have been treated for smoke inhalation and minor burns. Wrestlers Jack Lundin and the Dynamo are missing as of presstime and are presumed dead. Scores of fWo fans have been treated for smoke inhalation when they were overcome as the arena began to fill with smoke, and still many more are being treated for injuries caused by the stampede to the exits as panic set in. Authorities have been tight-lipped regarding the fire, but our reporters are working around the clock to bring you news of the tragedy as it appears. eWNews would like to extend our heartfelt condolences to the fWo now during it's time of loss. fWo Wrestling
