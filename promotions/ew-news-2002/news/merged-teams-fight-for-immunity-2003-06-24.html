Merged Teams Fight For Immunity

24 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With the Survivor III teams all merged into one group, fighting for survival shifts from a group event into a battle for individual immunity. After a strong showing at Ruahh, Disposable Heroes ensure that they will advance into the next round. fWo Survivor III Contest The remaining nine Survivor III teams showed up on Monday Night Ruahh~!, a packed event preparing for the first ever Friday-night special Unleashed: Battle Bowl where the Survivor III wrestlers will engage in a fierce battle royal where winning isn't as important as showcasing their talent. But in their appearance on Monday Night, one team would walk away with the all-important immunity badge, and the assurance that they would move into the next round, secure from the vote. In the first ever individual immunity challenge, the team of Disposable Heroes walked away with the victory. "Our special guest judge for this round was Martyr... who many remember as being the fWo Hardcore Champion and a member of the Survivor II contest.," said Thumper, fWo Survivor III judge. "We are always lucky to have former Survivor II judges involved in the new competition." Martyr declined comment on his decision, but did mention that he felt Disposable Heroes best showed the affect of the merge on the teams, as well as the effect on their own team, and they elaborated on the interpersonal relationship very well. Martyr also complimented Brotherly Love and The Uprising as being particularly strong as well. "What happens now," said Twitch, fWo Survivor III offical, "is that the teams will enter the voting chamber and eliminate one of their own. The only team that is safe is Disposable Heroes, who are able to vote, but not able to be voted for. They are completely safe from this round. Then the remaining teams all have to struggle to get ready for Friday and the Battle Royale. This is a tough, tough week for the Survivors."
