fWo News: Wrestler pulled from Uprising show

30 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It has been announced that as a result of the actions of Vic Creed in the final moments of the United States Championship match on the July 28th edition of Ruahh~! ‘The Paragon’ Brand Frontier has been removed from the active line-up for the coming edition of Uprising, Thursday night on ESPN . A statement from Brand Frontier was expected, but his intern contacted the web site staff and stated that Mr. Frontier would be making a statement of his own on next weeks edition of Monday Night Ruahh~! That in fact he had many things he needed to address and the time off this week would indeed give him ample opportunity to get his thoughts together. Brand Frontier received 14 stitches to the back of his head and lost a significant amount of blood after the event. Lance Knight and Aimz accompanied the fWo Superstar to the hospital where he was diagnosed with a low-level concussion. He was released the next morning and has not contacted the fWo Administration since the occurrence, speaking only to them through his intern. Tune in Monday Night on ABC to fWo Ruahh~! to witness Brand Frontier's reaction to the events of the past week! http://www.fwowrestling.com
