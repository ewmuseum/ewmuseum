Michaels retains title, Cannon retires at WrestleFest 5!

9 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
At the FWWF's fifth annual "WrestleFest", Kevin Michaels managed to pull off the upset over The RingMaster and retain his FWWF Title, while Christian Cannon met a much different fate. WrestleFest history did not favor Kevin Michaels going into the May 18th spectacular. After all, never before had any champion walked into the main event and walked out with their title. But Michaels, now headed into his fifth month as champion, silenced all of his critics with a victory over the favored RingMaster at the GeorgiaDome in Atlanta. Duke Porter, serving as special referee, did nothing to help The RingMaster's cause of course, doing everything he could to keep the title off the ruffian. And in the other big news of the night, the FWWF bid an early farewell to one of it's greatest champions, and a man no doubt destined for Hall of Fame honors. Christian Cannon lost in his "Loser Leaves Town" bid against Cobra, meaning that he must hang up the boots he once wore so proudly and step aside for the final time in his career. Perhaps more shocking was the sickening display that followed the match where Cannon's wife, Erika, put the boots to him for losing the contest. Former Womens champion, Onya Rampage, would come out from the crowd to put an end to the attack in a move that had the 48,626 in attendance on their feet.
