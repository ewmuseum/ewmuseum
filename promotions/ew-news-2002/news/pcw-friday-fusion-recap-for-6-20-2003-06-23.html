PCW Friday Fusion Recap For 6/20

23 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Among a host of other things, PCW World Champion Skylar Green began his quest to regain custody of two girls who're very special to him, yet are currently legally guarded by his rival, Titu Xansiv, "The Pharaoh." Yeah, yeah, the art's coming...really, it is! The show opens up to Skylar Green being questioned by Tania about his match with Dante Jackson. He explains it’s an exhibition match, and how bookers do this often because wrestling with your rival every show would be redundant Jeff and Dave do their regular beginning show ordeal, getting into a small discussion about Dorn Tracer becoming #1 contender to the PCW World Belt Syn d. Flaymin Aymin after escaping from Flaymin’s finisher, connecting with Confession (a Death Valley Driver) Earlier today is shown….Abner is watching the PCW crew put the ring together, while thinking about the masked German. A security guy walks up and says that Abner should check something at his locker room and they leave Dante makes a visit to Skylar Green asking if he can remove his shock collar with his Celestial powers, and Sky says it’s impossible. Then they get in a little conversation about the children situation with Pharaoh and Sky mentions he has Rush Mason helping him out Stone Burns is being treated by a doctor due to his injuries suffered last week. Lacuna Debris shows up and rubs it in his face as the two have a slight altercation with words Cal Seaver shows up at Skylar Green’s door hoping to get some answers. While waiting for him, Seaver gives a little run down of Skylar’s history ever since winning the world title, and then Sky comes out. Seaver asks about the deal with Isiy Xansiv and Skylar basically says that nothing is going on Abner is talking to Cal Seaver expressing his hate for the Masked German. He says he’s tired of being disrespected and goes on a tirade against the German, and the lights go out. When they come back Abner and Seaver are laid out, having been attacked by the masked man Matt Jackson d. Menolly Xenos, Manuel Labor, and Ganker to become number one contender to the PCW Cruiserweight Title. After a back and forth match between the four, Jackson picked up the victory hitting a shooting star press on Manuel Labor Pharaoh approaches Skylar Green saying he is the real number one contender, but Skylar simply tells him to go talk to Dorn about it Justice Inc. are talking about how they need to get to the bottom of Justice Co. They continue to talk and are interrupted by Justice Co. who continue to badger them James Edwards and Syn partake in a G Force test for the space trip. Edwards handles it well, but Syn loses his lunch Abner is irate at the attack by the German masked man and is looking for revenge. He attacks the man he thinks is the German, only to find out it’s a costume wearing Bruce Willis. The Masked German has left yet another note belittling Abner some more LOVE d. The Order in a Five-Person Handicap Match after Miss Moonlight hit MOONLIGHT on a tied of Sivasian, after a long and rigorous match up. After the match Sivasian and SOCIETY get into a small bout due to losing and end it by agree to do battle on Turmoil. Does this mean it's over for the Order? Stone Burns makes a visit to Lacuna Debris' room and leaves a note for her showing his disgust for her Dante Jackson finally finds someone who can remove his shock collar, but finds himself in another, more hulking predicament courtesy of Gaz Green Pharaoh goes to talk to Dorn about stealing the number one contendership from him, but doesn't get the answers he was hoping for Dorn gives a little speech to the crowd about his beloved number one contendership and goes on a rampage of words against Brianna yet again saying he set up her match, deciding to make himself guest commentator to her match against El Diablo. Brianna Washington d. El Diablo due to a countout as he never came out to the ring. Dorn attacks her verbally after the match saying he gave her an easy win to up her status if she's to be associated with him, which doesn't sit well with Brianna Skylar Green, Tania, Rush Mason, and Isiy Xansiv are having a discussion about how they are going to get the children back finding a lot of dead ends Flaymin comes to the ring and tries to hold a Ten Count Salute for the deceased Angela, and it's constantly interrupted Pharaoh is having a secret meeting planning something unbeknownst to us In the short lived main event, Dante Jackson d. Skylar Green after Gaz Green interferes, attacking Jackson. Shortly after the end of the match more people start piling out from the back leading to utter chaos in the ring, ending with the Steel Masked Man standing tall Primetime Championship Wrestling
