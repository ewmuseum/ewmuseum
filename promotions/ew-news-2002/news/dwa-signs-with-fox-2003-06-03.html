DWA Signs with Fox

3 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dragon Wrestling Association signs a multi-year deal with the Fox Network. DWA Owner, Mason Stone, announced earlier this week that the DWA would not pursue a renewal deal with current broadcasting partner, ESPN, and instead have signed a multi-year deal with Robert Murdoch's Fox Network. The deal places the DWA's primary show, Showdown, on the FX, returning to it's Monday nights at 8PM, and their secondary show, Surge, on Fox Sports, on Friday nights, with a replay on Saturday nights. Stone said that one of the reasons for the lack of interest in returning to ESPN is that DWA's shows were never given a priority, often preempted whenever a special sporting event happened, so DWA's shows were often broadcasted two-three days after they actually happened. Stone said he spoke with ESPN several times concering the issue, and they were unwavering and uninterested in hearing the DWA's side of things, despite the DWA being one of their stronger rated programs. Stone said that negotiations began with Fox nearly a month ago, and things have just now been finalized. The DWA will begin broadcasting on FX and Fox Sports staring on June 9th with Showdown.
