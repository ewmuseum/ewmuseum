hWo: Bringing them In.

6 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Today the Hardcore Wrestling Organziation signed on two more wrestlers to their Roster, Today the Hardcore Wrestling Organziation signed on two more wrestlers to their Roster, and are in talks with another two about joining the ranks of the hWo. hWo has yet to release the names of the two that they had signed earlier today, but hWo said that they will be debuting this sunday on Desolation. Trev Echo had this to say to the press earlier today "I am very excited about the signing of these two new wrestlers, and we have high hopes for both of them, and we hope they injoy working for the hWo." Explained hWo Owner Trev Echo. "As far as the other two wrestlers we are in talks yet, we have yet to come to an agreement, but hopefully we can persway them into signing with the hWo." Watch this sunday for Desolation which Trev Echo said look for a few unexpecting debuts. hWo!
