SbW: Rook Black on Crimson Fist of Glory

24 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rook Black: (laughing) Me aterrorizan de Spyder. No tengo ninguna hermana. ¿Qué él hará? Duermo malo debido a esta posibilidad. Spinebuster Wrestling’s Crimson Fist of Glory featured Champion J. Leslie Voss defending against an unknown challenger, which turned out to be Action!’s Rook Black. Kirk Aceveda: When did you know you’d be main eventing Crimson Fist of Glory? Rook Black: About three days before. Kirk Aceveda: And you dropped everything for that? Rook Black: Well, Voss was honestly in a bind. Bradley Duncan had worked shows for them, and he couldn’t stop signing about how much fun he had. So in effect, I think I might have been set up. (laughs) But in all seriousness, the call came and I was honored they’d asked me to do it. Since I’ve come back from the hand injury I haven’t been anywhere near to main eventing any shows. So there was that as well. Kirk Aceveda: Any idea on why the short notice? Rook Black: It was all part of the Voss’s scheme. Actually, I had heard it was a last minute cancellation, so they needed a spot to fill. Kirk Aceveda: Really? Who were you replacing? Rook Black: Well, this is completely unconfirmed, okay? Kirk Aceveda: Understood. Rook Black: But it’s been suggested that Spyder had agreed to come. Then a few days before demanded some more money as well as other perks of an illicit kind. So suddenly, Voss had little options, and I think my name got picked out at random so he called. Kirk Aceveda: How was the atmosphere? Rook Black: It was a lot of fun. Everyone was really friendly, the entire team was very excited about it all. I got a few ribs for stealing someone else’s opportunity to climb up, but they weren’t serious. Kirk Aceveda: Were you stealing someone else’s spot? Rook Black: Actually, no. The fact is that Voss is the champion right now, a big nasty heel, and the goal was to establish Voss as a credible title holder, so Rook Black was used to help add some strength to that title. A successful defense against a somewhat established name adds more to Voss. At this point, the next step would apparently be to build up the face who’s going to be chasing Voss for that title. This would be the perfect time to use someone like Spyder under those circumstances of course. Kirk Aceveda: ¿Qué usted piensa de las demandas de Spyder de dormir con cada uno a hermanas? Rook Black: (laughing) Me aterrorizan de Spyder. No tengo ninguna hermana. ¿Qué él hará? Duermo malo debido a esta posibilidad. Kirk Aceveda: (laughing) Well, let’s get away from that. Will you be working at SbW again? Rook Black: I definitely want to. I’ve met a lot of great people and we just had a great time. It was a lot of fun to enter into a situation where the atmosphere was so relaxed. We weren’t fighting with television people or anything like that. It was just Voss saying, “What do you want to do?”, and me saying, “I don’t know, what do you want to do?” and Voss saying, “Well what should we do?” and me saying, “Let’s do something good.” And that was all their was to it. Just great fun. And I hope a lot of people were happy with what they got to see. Kirk Aceveda: Except that you were robbed. Rook Black: (laughing) That’s right, I was robbed. I almost had him! Almost. Kirk Acevada: Thanks for your time, Rook. Rook Black: Always a pleasure, Mr. Aceveda. Spinebuster Wrestling
