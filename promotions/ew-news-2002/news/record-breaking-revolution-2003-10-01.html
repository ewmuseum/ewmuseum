Record Breaking ReVolution

1 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PRIME's latest edition of ReVolution managed to break a ratings record that was set over two months ago in the company. The main draw was of course the highly controversial Tony Rolo vs Rock Startling main event. MORE... The viewing figures are in, confirming that Tony Rolo vs Rock Startling was the most watched match in ReVolution history, beating the previous record set by Angello Malenko vs Rik Reno by 0.6 million viewers. Despite being the most watched match in ReVolution history, there was a backlash of opinion and feeling from PRIME's fans. For those who missed the show, Tony Rolo eventually picked up the win after Lance Jackson interfered, but botched his attack, nailing Startling with an aluminium baseball bat. "Complete bullsh*t!" yelled one young fan as he left the TD Waterhouse Centre. "I can't believe they let a decision like that stand. I don't like Rock Startling, but he got screwed tonight for sure!" "Where was a second referee?" Questioned a burly 30 year old Floridian male, wearing a stained Andy Magnum jersey. "That cheap victory didn't do Rolo any favours either apart from giving him a title shot... having said that, I wouldn't mind seeing a rematch between the two, now THAT would be interesting!" Those were just two of the several views we gaged after the show as the fans left the Orlando arena. As it happens, Tony Rolo and Rock Startling will lock horns again this week, but in the form of a tornado tag match where they are paired with Lance Jackson and Mark Blaze respectively. We tried to speak to Taun Pham concerning the match and backstage rumblings for a return bout between the two, but he had little to say on the matter. "As far as I'm concerned, the decision stands." Snapped Pham as he climbed into a company limo. "Why wouldn't it? I've got bigger things to worry about that Rock Startling getting a little hard done by!" This topic looks like it could become a sore point in PRIME, especially with Startlings much publised nine match undefeated streak effectively ending. Of course, the only views on the matter that are going to be important are that of Tony Rolo and Rock Startling themselves. Startling has gradually turned in a brutal machine in recent weeks, leaving Nik VanCore on the sidelines after the PPV. Now that this has happened, who knows how the Startling Onslaught will react? PRIME
