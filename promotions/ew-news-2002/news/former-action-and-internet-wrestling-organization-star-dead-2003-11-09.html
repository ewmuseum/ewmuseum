Former Action and Internet Wrestling Organization Star Dead At The Age Of Thirty

9 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rashard Clark, known by wrestling fans as Syphon Fission, was found dead early this morning. Multiple gunshot wounds to the chest and stomach are to blame. Fans remember him one of the most accomplished wrestlers in the sport. They remember him as a three time Internet Wrestling Organization World Champion and as an Action Champion, a title he held mere months ago. They’ll remember him for a remarkable career that was ended at the hands of Daniel Phillips in one of the most amazing matches of the past year. Now, they will remember him as the latest victim in a rash of Saginaw Michigan related gang violence. Rashard William Clark, known by the wrestling community as Syphon Fission, was found and pronounced dead at 11:50 PM. Police were informed of shots being fired along Davenport Avenue, a street known for drug and gang violence. Police were quick to arrive at the scene. What they saw could only be described as gruesome. Kain Thompson, one of the areas most notorious drug traffickers, was also found dead with multiple shots to the chest. Five others who had been investigated along with Mr. Thompson for federal chargers were also found dead. Police believe that Rashard killed the six men in an act of self defense. “Given the records on these people, I would say they were going into a fight they had no idea would turn awry. I guess now they know.” said an unidentified police officer. Rashard, a twelve-year veteran of the squared circle, recently retired from active professional wrestling and was planning on opening a bar in downtown Saginaw, an area he grew up in as a child. Rashard’s career could only be described as being storybook, given the success and fame he garnered. There is no word as of yet how the IWO or Action will react to this sudden departure from their family. The owners of both companies were not available for comment. Action Wrestling
