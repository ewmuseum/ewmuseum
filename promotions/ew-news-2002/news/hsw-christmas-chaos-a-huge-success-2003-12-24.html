HSW: Christmas Chaos a HUGE Success!

24 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On Tuesday, December 23rd, 2003, Headstrong Wrestling produced its year ending, biggest Pay Per View of the year, Christmas Chaos. It was live from Denver, Colorado and the crowd was packed to see the high profile matches. To start the show off, there was a performance by Trapt which was then joined by the Japanese Rock Star Tai Hashi for the ending of their song and HSW Christmas Chaos theme- Still Frame. The first match was a Fatal Fourway match for the FnX Championship between Skip Rawlin, Edward Twiztid, Blake Rozdale and Anton Chase. Just because this is a show opener does not mean it wasn't worth seeing! Its very hardcore as it should be. The second match pitted Order of Chaos member Bryan Bryn Bryson taking on Brian Rage. A lot of people were placing their bets on Bryson to win this match because of head problems as a result of his coma suffered when he was in Dominant Wrestling Organization(DWO). Did Rage do the unthinkable and pull out his first win in HSW or did Bryson make him just another victim? A Tag Team Match was next as Order of Chaos members "The Future" Aaron Anubis and Devon Jacobs took on the odd team of Tai Hashi and Nate Newsome. Hashi and Newsome struggled to get along at times but most of the match they did quite well. The Tag Team Titles were on the line in a very impressive match between Order of Chaos members The Boogeyman and Bloodhound(The Dark Alliance), X-Rated Ikons, and Vandal Hearts. Not only was this for the titles, but it wasn't a normal match either! There were 19 tables at ringside and the team who broke the most tables at the end of the match by putting their opponents through them were crowned the champions. This is a must see if you are a fan of Hardcore. There was then a Street Fight between the Halliwell sisters Athena and Heather. The loser would have to hang up their boots for good. Who was that person? In a VERY high flying match up, the Evolution title was on the line as Man With No Name defended against El Nutso. You have GOT GOT GOT To see this one!! And finally, another MUST SEE TO BE BELIEVED- the Main Event was a Triple Teir Cage match for the Headstrong Championship. Not only was it a Triple Teir cage match, but inside of the ring and each cage were many weapons which aren't very common to see in any wrestling ring! Assassin, the champion, took on Tyler "Too Sweet" McPherson and Sabre. Would Assassin defend his title? Would the smaller McPherson outsmart them all and pull off a big upset win? Or did Sabre power his way through everything and everyone and capture the Headstrong Title? You have to see this pay per view!
