Sky High #1

9 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A blind man with nothing to do and a Braille-keyed typewriter in front of him... OH, THE HUMANITY! Anyway, this time he brings us up to date on everything that's been happening with him in the past three weeks. Yes, I'm writing columns now. Meh, it's a timekiller. Anyway, Tania, Rafaela, and Ines are all once again under my legal guardianship for anyone who's curious. They seem happy to be together again after The Pharaoh temporarily split them in his attempt to get the PCW World Title from me (something that never panned out, but I give him an 'A' for effort). I rented the PCW office in Hollywood, CA (it's not like they're using it) and refurnished it. The third story functions as the living space, the second story is officially my office space, and the first story is now the Skylar Green School of Wrestling. I'm accepting anyone at affordable rates (as low as $500), complete with complimentary ring gear. Can't get better than that. Enough of the shilling. For some reason, people have been asking me my take on the collapse of WO. It's been beaten to death, but poking the body one more time with a blind man's stick couldn't hurt. Here goes. I woke up one morning and it was done. Just like that, PCW closed. It seemed going well enough. There was a lot of momentum built up from that airship tour. Making global show appearances, doing innovative things in show production... but WO collapsed and just like that the place closed. VP-W and VP-S tried to insulate the federation from the outside issues that caused the split between OSE and the WO (mainly because Ron Starr had a lot to do with the WO ownership, so it was actually a falling out between Ron Starr and Jane Reagan (again... and again and AGAIN) that lead to Old School Entertainment being pulled from WrestleOnline affiliation. In response to this, Ron Starr made an announcement disbanding WO (which in actuality was a statement saying that the relationship between the federations of PWC, OSE, and PCW was nothing more than symbolic, in effect trying to make Jane Reagan look stupid for pulling out of a totally useless affiliation of feds anyway). Rumors circulated like wildfire on the internet about Ron Starr's plan to shoot down PCW's Cloud Nine airship. They didn't pan out though. The guy's not that bad. I wouldn't put it past Alan Kriegman or Frisco, though. Luckily the former was occupied and the latter is under heavy lock and key awaiting a death sentence in his native Argentina. But it wasn't long before the backlash against this collapse of WO led to many people worrying about their jobs, and some others who were leaving PWC in protest. Ron Starr had the money to buyout PCW in a heartbeat, that had those wrestlers concerned. Many of Starr's own workers over in PWC were a little perturbed at Starr's gestures toward OSE and decided to quit. PCW's management wanted as much as possible to keep its workers from the problems outside of the federation, but it was to no avail. With one week and three shows to go until the pay-per-view, the federation shut down. The final show, a Friday Fusion, was never aired. People never got to see that Dorn Tracer defeated me to become the PCW World Champion. I had the title mailed to Dorn Tracer, who supposedly holds onto it to this day. That didn't stop me from having the customary carbon copy made of the belt to keep at his own home, though. I earned that heap of leather and iron pyrite. That's all for this time around. I plan on publishing this column regularly, so you can keep an eye out for it if you wish. Later. eWrestling, Inc.
