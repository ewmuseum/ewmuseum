Reality Main Event in Controversy

28 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The show is only two days away and many of the wrestlers in Real and Graphic Entertainment seem to have mixed feelings over the main event. Many feel the two men involved are not deserving of the spotlight... But what will they do to stop it? There is much controversy surrounding the decision of Tom Mason to book the main event of Reality as Payne versus Shawn Jackson. Despite the fact that it will be a first ever ‘Straight Jacket Match,’ many people feel that neither man deserves to be in the main event for any event right now. The general consensus is that Payne and Shawn Jackson do not have the experience or have earned the respect of everyone to be at the top of the card. However, no one is arguing with Tom Mason for booking the contest. There is a tremendous amount of respect in the locker room for the Owner of the company and generally everyone feels that if Tom Mason booked the match he must see something in the two of them worth putting in the main event. Never has Tom Mason just booked a main event and let the crowd down. For the fans that are unknown to the feud between Payne and Shawn Jackson, this will be the third Pay Per View encounter between the two men. Since Payne debuted in Real and Graphic Entertainment last May, he has gone after the Extreme Champion, Shawn Jackson. They first encountered each other at MayDay on May 25, 2003. Shawn Jackson escaped the winner as Payne literally walked out from the contest. Next, they met at Se7en Deadly Sins in an ‘Ambulance Match’ for the Extreme Championship. In that encounter, Payne was overcome by the finish and Shawn Jackson shut him into the ambulance and drove him to a psychiatric institution. For the past six months leading up to January’s return, Payne has been cooped up in that hospital waiting to be let loose. Now, he is let loose and Tom Mason made it clear that if he does not win at Reality, Payne will be back in that institution for the rest of his life. Will Payne and Shawn Jackson be able to show the fans and the boys in the back of the Real and Graphic Entertainment locker room that they deserve to be main event competitors?
