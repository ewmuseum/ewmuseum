Jamal Wilson and Kory Storm's Violent Saga To End?

17 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jamal Wilson and Kory Storm's violent feud is leading to an interesting culmination match coming up on August 9th, 2003, at the Elks Lodge. The Internet Wrestling Organization Jamal Wilson and Kory Storm have an extensive history. Back before the closure of PIW in December, Kory Storm and Jamal Wilson engaged in a violent feud that included the first exposure of Kory’s sister, Renee, to the professional wrestling world. Renee Rutherford sat in the crow, watching the show that Kory got her comp tickets too when Jamal Wilson came out to a rousing amount of cheers from the crowd. Earlier in the night Kory had challenged Jamal’s sexuality and claimed that the “Hardcore Homo”, was batting for the “homo” side. Jamal steadfastly denied the accusation that he was a homosexual and promised to prove it later in the night. Jamal came down to the ring and promised that he would kiss one lucky female in the audience. By chance, he chose Kory Storm’s little sister. Jamal planted a sensuous lip lock on Renee Rutherford before shocking the audience by throwing up. Jamal ran to the locker room crying about the “cooties” he could have gotten by kissing Renee. Kory Storm promised to get Jamal Wilson the very next week for his disgustingly embarrassing make out session with his sister. But something happened. The next week, Jamal Wilson came out to the ring in front of a raucous bunch of PIW fans and promised to prove that he wasn’t “a girly man” by challenging Kory Storm to a hardcore match that very night. For some reason, Kory no-showed. It was later revealed that the affluent Storm was upset that his contract was being cut almost in half (from $160,000 a year to $82,000 a year) due to the budget cuts that PIW was about to go through. Although Kory did not need the money, he felt it was a slap in the face that PIW decided to cut his salary and not Jamal’s or the other mid-carders. The next week Jamal checked into the Betty Ford clinic, claiming he an alcoholic and having problems with alcohol when he kissed Renee, and promising that he didn’t do it because he wanted to. As Kory sat out the remainder of his PIW contract and Jamal rotted away in rehab, PIW closed for good. This left the Jamal Wilson-Kory Storm feud incomplete. Upon entering the IWO the two were friendly… until Jamal pinned Kory after an intense 15-minute match at Beach Party VII on the New Jersey boardwalk. Kory immediately snapped and attacked Jamal. After this attack Jamal Wilson teamed up with Donnie Daze to gain another pinfall victory over Kory Storm in a tag team match with Hurley Cambria. After the match Kory went into a rage backstage and demanded that he get a chance to face Jamal Wilson at the August 9th Show, Live from the Elks Lodge. But this time with a special stipulation. A No Holds Barred, Falls Count Anywhere, Loser Leaves Town match. “This promotion isn’t big enough for the both of us,” Kory spat at me after the match. “This f*ggot is going to pay for messing with me.” Jamal was quick to accept. “If he wants some of this sexy chocolate, he can have it! I’m sure he has no problems mixing blood with me… it’s not like he’ll catch anything from such a sweet, succulent man like myself.” The match has been signed by IWO Owner, Thomas Ford, and will take place in the “House of Hardcore” in what should surely be an excited sold out crowd in Queens, New York. Get ready for the violent culmination to an action-packed feud. The Home of Jamal Wilson and Kory Storm!
