fWEo Update

2 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Two new matches added to 11/15 show. I'm posting this piece of news because...well, take a look at my roster. It's not like I can leave it up to anyone else to tell all MY fans what I have in store for them the next time the fWEo goes to Beaverton. Plus, the internet loves me. The first BRAND NEW MATCH for "Why Are We Still In Business" is a handicap match, pitting Waru, one of the most hated wrestlers in fWEo history, against the tandem of the Ultimate Warrior & Virgil. Hopefully Warrior and Virgil will be back from Busch Gardens or whereever it is they went in time for the show. I'd love nothing more than to see Waru kill himself in the middle of the ring after having to listen to his opponents ramble AT THE SAME TIME. The second match is...oh, hell, I don't even know WHY I'm letting this happen, but James Cagle will be returning to the fWEo after missing a few shows due to an "injury". Whatever. He'll face...I don't know...probably Hans Krueger or something. The rest of the card is as follows: Black Quicksilver defends the Sphere Heavyweight Title against Mike Heftel. I hope they both get hurt very badly. A seven-team Tag Team Murtoil match, featuring the Codebreakers, Barry Bladberth & Razor Ramon, Mr. T & La Parka, Evil Smokey The Bear & a partner of his choosing, and THREE mystery teams. The winners of this match (which I came up with all by myself) will receive a shot at the Sphere Tag Titles, currently held by New York Matters, later in the show. Senor Funpants will attempt to get Bam Bam Bigelow off my ass. That's actually kind of scary when you think about, what, with Bigelow on my ass and not wearing any underwear. "Hacksaw" Jim Duggan defends the WCW TV Title against the 1-2-3 Kid, and will apparently reveal who paid him off to take out a bottle of Cherry 7-UP. Watch me pretend to care. Claire and Nitro Girl Siren will be doing something that they've NEVER EVER DONE BEFORE. Yes, they'll be doing it together, and mark my words, it's the one and only time you'll see it. Perhaps even more earth-shattering than what Claire and Siren will be doing...yes, *I* will be doing something of great importance. Then again, I ALWAYS do something of great importance, but hey, I'm an important guy. All this and...well, hopefully not much more, since the reason I didn't book some guys in matches is because I don't like them...November 15th, Why Are We Still In Business. WR4I
