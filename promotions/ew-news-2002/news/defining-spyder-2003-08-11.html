Defining Spyder

11 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Some little nothing yapped a question to me. "Who are you Spyder? What are you supposed to be? What have you accomplished?" Esse, you can get a quick answer by asking the thousands of fans on the street, by asking the millions of women and picking up any magazine or any paper and reading it. They know. This is Spyder Go figure hommes, that the PIW rejects and cast aways are upset and crying about some of the truth I lay down about their nothing little promotion. This is a group of sick individuals. Sick and childish, who are too stupid and pathetic to realize that their little game was just that esse, a game. Don't get me wrong hommes, I don't have a problem with all of PIW. No esse, people like Wippit Guud that I work with are smart, intelligent people who have moved on and moved up. No, I'm talking about a small group of whiners. Of children. Of pathetic losers who can't seem to move past the speed bump in the road that was Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling. One of the stupidest promotion names I have ever heard of. These babies think that they own the rights to talk about their promotion. If they don't mention it, nobody else can mention it. In one way esse, they are right. Why should anyone waste their time talking about it? Sylo, Sharc, KroW... get over it. The rags and the fans are laughing at you esse. Your promotion, Action, is embarrassed of you. You talk backstage? You run your mouth in the locker room about how people pick on you? Esse, all I hear is laughter. Your roster, your partners in Action? They think you're a big joke. And hommes, you all are a big joke. And you will continue to be a big joke until you get a life and stop living in the past. Stop blaming others for your mistakes. Most important, stop wrecking promotions with your childish whining. You types drag a promotion like Action down. Sylo asks me who I am and who I am supposed to be. And esse, I laugh because it's so obvious. I could list over four dozen title victories over nine years. I could list the amount of awards I get from the fans, fans I don't really care about anyway. I could list the accolades, the women, the success, the glory. But esse? Mostly I list the respect. The respect I get in this business for rising to the top. For making even the worst wrestlers look good. For going from being a nobody to the top star in weeks. Who am I esse? I'm what you want to be... and I'm what you will never be. I'm a success.
