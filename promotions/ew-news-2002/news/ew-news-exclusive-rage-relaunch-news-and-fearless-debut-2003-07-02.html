EW-News Exclusive: RaGE Relaunch News and Fearless Debut!

2 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Real and Graphic Entertainment realizes that talk is indeed cheap. So, after taking a month off with a lawsuit and court order, business resumes. And business was a lot better last night than ever before! Find out why. One month after disappearing, Real and Graphic Entertainment kept its advertised promise to relaunch itself on July 1, 2003. After fans and other promotions waited all day for the site at Rageproject.com to finally go back up, their calls were answered at midnight as the changing of the days were taking place. The link was replaced on the site and finally those that waited almost a whole month could view the company's major production in a new light. The return of Real and Graphic Entertainment is expected to draw more visitors than the previous original debut in March as now fans have been able to witness the company and pass the good word along to their fellow enthusiasts who are now making the attempt to view RaGE Programming. That programming includes Fearless which debuted after a month of wait due to the injunction placed on June 4 by a judge in the lawsuit that eventually crumbled against Real and Graphic Entertainment. Last night, July 1, Fearless made its debut and for the two hours and thirteen minutes of air time the program had, 3.6 million fans reportedly tuned in for the broadcast. According to the Neilson Media Research Corp, a 3.6 overnight rating has been compiled for Real and Graphic Entertainment's debut of Fearless. If the final number comes out close to that, which is what the company is hoping, it will say that the promoting and relaunch have proven a success. It should also give the company a good feeling heading into Fearless on Saturday night, another special event for the company thanks to Fox and Rupert Murdoch. The 3.6 for Fearless will also be noted as the highest RaGE Television mark to this day. Speaking on the subject of the Fearless debut, may were skeptic about how RaGE managed to change its focus from its June agenda to July agenda. It was pretty easily answered by the company when Tom Mason had already assumed people would question the ease of the change. He noted that the opening segment he made was indeed a taped segment prior to the event that took place, but the producers and himself went back and reshot the segment in order to edit the use of "Broken Home" out and replace it with "Se7en Deadly Sins, of course the title of this month's Pay Per View. Also noted by Tom Mason was that the show did take place later in the day of June 4, after the company already had word of the injunction. So, the use of Se7en Deadly Sins in the program was actually able to be planned in and any references of the other Pay Per View were told to be scrapped, to be safe. Business conducted that day only because the stage was already set up in Phoenix, Arizona, for Fearless, but sadly it was not aired until now because of the court order. Despite the month off, returning just yesterday has been considered a huge success for Real and Graphic Entertainment. Already they have posted this Saturday's card online at the RaGE website and various sections of the website are up and running. The company promises many updates in the coming days and an easily navigable system on the site. For a complete update on Real and Graphic Entertainment, visit the site at Rageproject.com and check back with EW-News.com frequently. Real and Graphic Entertainment
