Realm Preview

24 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In the aftermath of this deadly war, who will come to power, and who will burn to ashes? The time draws near... This week on Realm, things are definitely heating up. With Lights Out Wrestling under new management, what direction will things take? The card seems extraordinary enough, but we have fallen to deception in the form of glorious rewards in the past. One thing is for certain, everyone will be trailing this Mr. Jordan carefully, and he shouldn't expect a moment where no eyes are on his back. There will be an unusual two title shots as well, which should prove to be very interesting in many ways. Last week, Jesse Jamester and Scorpion were revealed as the mystery opponents for Derek Vinyard and Phoenix Rose, which resulted in a victory over the Tag Team Champions. But as the stipulation ruled, this week Jamester and Scorpion will have a rematch, with titles on the line! Also, Iron Lung has worked long and hard to get where he is. He has passed up many opportunities along the way. This week on Realm, he will be facing seemingly unstoppable Judgement, who will be defending his newly won Xtreme Championship. Iron Lung may have sacrificed opportunities in the past, but there is no way he is giving up this shot. In addition to these main events, returning legend CADE will be in action once again, taking on Canyon Thunders in the infamous Lightning Dome matchup. CADE was once called "King of the Lightning Dome," but he seems to have changed since his return. Will Canyon's rise to the top continue? Or will CADE's insanity prevail once again? A full seven matches will take place, and many of these will be extremely well matched. Like Jason Ortiz against Josh Starwind, and Joseph Magnus against Vince Jones. There are so many mysteries to be unraveled, and all signs point to the fact that they will be unraveled this week on Saturday Night Realm! Don't miss it. Lights Out Wrestling
