Everything in ruin; Survivor chaos

8 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The fifth challenge completed, the confusion that ran rampant following the shocking Survivor III merge has had a ripple effect on both Gold and Green teams. Going into a team unification at the end of the week, all bets are off and nobody is secure: Sabotage is the latest to go in bizzare vote. fWo Survivor III If the wrestlers in Survivor thought they understood the game and had plans laid, everything was turned upside down when the teams were shuffled in a represented draft between the Gold and Green teams. Alliances were cracked, strategies were quickly realligned, and a quick 24 hour challenge immediately followed the new team allignment. The combination of all of these put teams completely at a loss, and all sides struggled not only to understand the new nature of the game, but to compete in the sudden challenge. "Unfair? Yeah, it's unfair. The whole bloody contest is unfair mate. What did you think this was? Hopscotch? This is a struggle. It's a struggle that only one team will win. Your odds mate, are awful. You're in this contest because you're proving something. Win or lose, it really is about how you play the game," said Thumper, fWo Survivor representative. "Plenty of teams complained. But if they want an easy contest with no problems, concerns or challenge... hell, there's a game of hopscotch around the block. No waiting." Tensions were high as the challenge completed, a challenge that the Gold Team wound up missing the deadline on. This made a decision easier for special round five judge and fWo Hardcore Champion Aimz, who still grudgingly gave the nod to the Green Team. "Rules are rules I suppose. It isn't like the Hardcore Championship, and that's a shame for some really talented work from the Gold Team. This is a strong team. The results, which have been so weighted to the Green Team, don't reflect how great these people are. I hope to work with several of them in the future." While the story might be that the Green Team picked up yet another win on a long road of victories in this contest (4-1 in favor of the Green Team thus far, a Survivor record) true Survivor III fans and observers understand that this was a completely different Green team, and their victory came more as a formality due to the missed deadline than as a result of superior work. The fact that two of the stronger teams that helped carry the Green Team to victory are now on the Gold Team have to put some doubt in the minds of the team that has been so dominant thus far. Worse still, the fact that a merge is coming soon only mades things more difficult for the new teams... all wrestlers want to put themselves in the best possible position when the game shifts from group challenges to individual contests. One team that won't make it to that point however is Sabotage... the team voted out of the Gold Group as a result of a fifth challenge loss. The decision was tough for the Gold Team, and morale has never been lower amoung the teams. As one team put it, "Things look pretty bleak right now. But this is all just one big learning process. We're bound to get this right at some point in time." Another team was more frustrated, biter about their final choice. "No hard feelings. Seems like the best to go at the time, not that you're not a great worker, just the communication was a little low. Good luck to you in the future." Only one thing is certain... it will only get harder from here. More news as it comes available... Survivor III Web Site
