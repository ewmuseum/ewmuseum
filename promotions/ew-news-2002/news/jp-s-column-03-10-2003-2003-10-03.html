JP's Column 03/10/2003

3 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hot off the press JP looks at all the issues surrounding the MWF and the developments from Dynamite 29/09 Hello everyone, JP's back again to tell you exactly what is happening in the MWF and believe you me there is a lot going on. The MWF is hitting that transition period and we're passing through it seemlessly at the current time thanks to the work of Insane Wayne, Iniquity, Kidd Kidman, NomaD and Troy Knight who could all be considered veterans of the MWF. They're working their socks off to try and keep the MWF on track and we have to give credit where credit is due. The new General Manager Dom Stoate has steadied the ship after the departure of the much loved Fozzy, the MWF is moving on swiftly and Stoate's influence is slowly growing. Walker still stands at the centre of the MWF, something tells me he will never ever leave for as long as he lives he will be a part of the MWF. Dynamite kept the good ship upright and on course to explode at the next Pay-Per-View, Nightmare that will be appearing on the horizon before you know it. Late November will be a crucial time for the MWF as we battle towards the end of the year and the attempt to draw in as many fans as possible. The show kicks off with the first of two fatal four way matches and featured some of the new MWF wrestlers who in the future could shine stars as big and as bright as those currently in the MWF's top bracket. Craig Clayborn, Sean West, Seth Caliko and The Vine are all potential champions of the future and they put on one hell of a match. Sean West however looked tired and he was eliminated quickly by The Vine and Clayborn. The Vine was the next man to see his dream shot down in flames as Seth Caliko took care of him. It came down to Seth Caliko and Craig Clayborn, two wrestlers I tip for the top because of their hardworking styles...Clayborn has the Troy Knight air about him while Caliko recalls the styles of MWF favourites Hart Evans and Rick Jones. Caliko ended up picking the win up, but only just in a very hard fought battle. A backstage segment between the MWF President and the MWF Tag Team Champions followed. Walker announced to Wayne that when he wrestled tonight against Kidd Kidman he would have to make sure Iniquity did not interfere or risk disqualification. The same, Walker explained would apply to the "Untouchables". A wise move from Walker, but would it work? The second of the nights fatal four ways was upon us. This was not elimination but a straight fight between four men who were beginning to establish themselves in the MWF. Iniquity was the clear favourite but faced stiff competition from Tommy Raines, Doomsday, who has already beaten him and The Jeopardizer who wanted his first victory since returning to the MWF. Doomsday and Iniquity ended up being the last two men standing and they slugged it out until Iniquity finished the match off with devestating move. Is there any stopping this man? The MWF President was again in front of the cameras as he walked out to make an announcement about the match between NomaD and Zach Fredricks. Walker explained to the fans that there was a slight change with the match, it would be for the MWF Xtreme Championship and falls count anywhere! Needless to say the fans were extatic. The Jeopardizer spoilt the party. The camera went backstage and we were greeted by the face of The Jeopardizer sitting in the locker room. He explained how he felt and announced he was leaving the MWF. The fans were screaming 'No' in the arena and I felt the exact same way as The Jeopardizer packed his bag and walked off down the corridor for the last time. It was a sad moment for the MWF. NomaD and Zach Fredricks had to take the attention away from the former MWF Xtreme and Tag Team Champion's depature and they did so. The match was the most violent and brutal match I'd seen since their Stairway to Hell match and the two men went back and forth exachanging violence for violence. NomaD always seemed to have that little bit extra and it was that little bit extra that eventually put Zach Fredricks through a window and the crowd were up applauding the two men as they finished their match. Fredricks had to be stretchered off and I'll update you on his condition later. Ian Gonzales was on hand backstage with the MWF's General Manager who was to say the least, not happy with the destruction caused by NomaD and Fredricks. Stoate was not happy that these two men had been allowed to wrestle eight days after such a gruelling encounter at Implosion and he would be having words with the MWF President. The Main Event was one of the most technical matches I've seen wrestled in the MWF for a long time. Both of the wrestlers were on top form as they battled back and forth with each man looking to out-do the others last movements. I for one believed Insane Wayne would win but after Walker's announcement that any man interfering in the match would lead to a disqualification there was too much at risk and Wayne seemed a little subdued. Kidman's speed began to tell and he was in the ascendancy and a quick combination attacks finished off with a shooting star press that had every fan in the arena's mouth open in amazement. Kidman picked up the victory and made a hasty getaway as Iniquity came running down to the ring but was unable to catch the MWF World Champion as he made off into the night, to safety. Dynamite was a great show, it built from start to finish with each match getting slowly better and better until the main event really put the icing on the cake. We don't see enough technical wrestling in the MWF and you don't often see Insane Wayne getting technical but he matched Kidman and I think the MWF World Champion will be slightly worried going into next weeks Dynamite. The MWF is changing rapidly in the personnel department. There have been three major wrestlers leaving their contracts this week. The first and most important of these was The Jeopardizer, I almost cried when I found out that "Jay-Z" did not want to renew his contract. A small bust-up with the management led to The Jeopardizer storming out and he bowed out on Dynamite in a style that I felt was unjust to a man who can be credited with helping the Maximum Wrestling Federation to its current position in the wrestling world. He was a wrestler who when paired with Sam King was always called the second man but in truth he was every bit as good as Sam King. He may never have captured the MWF World Championship but he was up there for months to provide support for the main eventers and his run as MWF Xtreme Champion was memorable. I'd love to see him return one day and The Jeopardizer has not ruled out a return but for the moment he will take time out to aseess his career. Zach Fredricks has been forced out of the MWF through injury. Dom Stoate's words seem to echo around the MWF at the moment and Walker is less than happy with Stoate criticising his decision to put NomaD and Fredricks in a hardcore environment so soon after Implosion. Zach Fredricks couldn't handle the intense pressure of working two by our standards horrific matches and he's been forced to the sidelines for months with a back and neck injury. Fredricks was a wrestler who never realised his potential in the MWF and I felt he would become the MWF Xtreme Champion soon. The third casualty of this past week has been Sean West. He will be forever known as the man without a win as he never managed to put a big W in the column as he marched through match after match suffering defeat. Sean West was a good wrestler and his promotional work featuring the highly talented Ms.Frost will be sorely missed as the chemistry between the two was quite sensational. He will no doubt ply his trade somewhere else and probably come off better than he did in the MWF. The MWF temporary roster has also seen a reshuffle. Seth Caliko made it to the MWF permanent roster and signed a contract thought to keep him for a few months with a view to an extension if performances match the money. A former MCW Television Champion I can see him going straight after Sean Harley and causing the MWF Television Champion a few problems. He showed in the fatal four way that he has the skill to make it through an elimination match and he sure did that. Simon Masters has finally had his contract terminated. I thought Masters could become something great in the MWF but I was wrong. He was overpaid and underworked, Masters was given the chance to be part of one of the biggest MWF angles but let it pass him by without once trying to get something out of it and I know a lot of the roster are glad to see him go. A new tag team has been signed up and I'm pleased to say Sozzey and Mak are two wrestlers who I am looking forward to having in the MWF. They work hard, play hard and will eventually come out with something to show for it. The MWF's Tag Team Championships are currently the centre of attention and it's been quite a while since the roster supported enough tag teams to really make the titles mean something but now Iniquity and Insane Wayne, Kidd Kidman and EyCe, Kidd Kidman and Hell Fire Kidd are really making waves. Hopefully Sozzey and Mak, Kronik as they like to be known, can make some waves. Those are the main developments in the MWF for the time being. It's been a great changing time for the MWF roster and currently some say that there is not enough talent on the roster but you just need to look at the wrestlers coming through to realise there is more than enough. Dynamite this coming monday is packed with four matches that will show the MWF's talent in its best light, under the spotlight of a wrestling beam. Troy Knight will be getting back in the ring and whenever Troy steps into the ring it's a very special occasion. The man has come to be an integral part of the MWF and we cannot deny his contribution to the MWF in past months. Craig Clayborn versus The Vine: Both men will be hoping to put their dsappoint of last weeks Dynamite behind them and look to knock seven bells out of each other in a hardcore match. Clayborn has a style I've already likened to Troy Knight and if he continues with this he should find the MWF easy going, he is a very interesting character. The Vine is a more direct wrestler and he doesn't take shit from anyone, his first run in the MWF taught him some harsh lessons and they could come in handy against Clayborn. Seth Caliko versus Ace Spade: Two very talented wrestlers will go face to face and you can't help feel that Caliko is destined to win. Ace Spade has not been himself since the defeat at the hands of arch rival Troy Knight and Seth Caliko will be looking to play on this misery and send Spade down for the three once again. This could however be the wake up call that Ace has been looking for, it will be entertaining and don't rule out interference by Troy to make sure Spade's nightmare continues. Sean Harley versus Troy Knight versus Flash: The MWF Television Championship is on the line and this could be match of the night. Three wrestlers who know they have what it takes to be the best in the MWF and one of them has already gone as far as to win the MWF TV Title. Sean Harley will be hanging on for dear life as the "Troy Boy" express rolls into town and Flash will be hoping he can pick up some scraps and find a sneaky win. Kelly Day could be a factor but Harley has his brother at ringside and Troy Knight will have the watchful eyes of his entourage including young Bobby. Kidd Kidman and Hell Fire Kidd versus Insane Wayne and Iniquity: A clash of two of the best MWF tag teams for the biggest tag team prize in Illinois. Insane Wayne and Iniquity have restored some pride to the MWF Tag Team Championships but Kidd Kidman wants it all, he's concerned with making his mark on the MWF and if that means winning every belt in the book whilst being MWF World Champion he will gladly do it. Hell Fire Kidd replaces EyCe as Kidman's tag team partner and some are reading into this, is there a split in the "Untouchables"? Wayne and Iniquity seem to have something up there sleeve, Doomsday is swearing revenge and this could be the time to take it...all hell could break loose on Dynamite. Dynamite will be a show to whet your appetite for more to come, there are talks rumoured to be held soon to decide the main event at Nightmare. A fatal four way is currently the top idea, featuring four of the MWF's biggest stars battling for two of the biggest belts in the MWF. It's the old two falls idea, it's very popular at the moment and Walker could be tempted to throw the MWF World and Xtreme Championships into a very big mixer. I'll keep you updated, until next time, take care of yourselves. JP (John Pilkington)
