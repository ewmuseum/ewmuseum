PLAGUE [FIGHTING]: Weeks Passing, Problems Stacking

31 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In recent weeks problems among the PLAGUE [FIGHTING] roster have caused quite a stir, and now an outside problem has caused an even bigger stir. In recent weeks many problems among the PLAGUE [FIGHTING] roster have surfaced. For quite a bit, the primary problem was the insubordinate behavior of the PLAGUE fighters toward owner, Gabe Carter. Now, a new, and more serious problem has surfaced. On the last edition of Friday Night Frenzy, Gabe Carter decided to make his authority known by cancelling the event mid-show. At this point he thought he had won the battle, but his happiness was soon destroyed. A large group of angry fans stormed into the parking lot of the Atlantic City Convention Center and created quite a riot. As moments passed, the riot only seemed to grow larger, and soon enough local police arrived at the scene to restrain the troublemakers. With a fight, the police managed to break up the riot, but unfortunately, this story doesn't have a happy ending. As a result of the riot, several lives were taken. At this point, an exact body count has yet to be confirmed, but sources have confirmed that the future for PLAGUE [FIGHTING] doesn't look too great. It has been rumored that several law suits could be coming Gabe Carter's way, but that has yet to be confirmed. Surely, news will unfold in days to come. PLAGUE [FIGHTING]
