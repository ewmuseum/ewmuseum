King of Violence IV Hello fans, I'm the HWF's webmaster and online publicity manager, Brian Bradshaw. With only days until HWF's fourth King of Violence pay-per-view, many fans are wondering what to expect. The show, to be aired live from Toronto's SkyDome this Sunday, features perhaps the most competitive tournament in HWF history. The King of Violence tournament has historically featured some of the HWF's most dangerous matches. Despite the high risk of injury and increasing insurance premiums that surround such an event, the ratings for the show have climbed higher each year. This year's pay-per-view looks to be no different. The tournament itself should be action-packed. Besides the violent matches that the annual King of Violence tournament is known for, it also will award the winner a World Title shot at September's Indecent Exposure 2003. This year's tournament features a variety of HWF superstars, including Television Champion Scott Anderson and North American Champion Jon Fuller, plus a mystery opponent that is rumored to have connections to CEO Stephen Sharp. Experience versus fresh blood could be a factor, as the tournament features everyone from newcomers "Karma" Kadence Snyder and Paul Duffy to former World Champions Devin "Spike" Staddart and "Cross" William Thunder. Outside of the tournament, the big story is the first main event featuring Hardcore Champion Jayson Chambers and his bid to grab the World Championship from Shawn Collins. However, after Collins and the STDs put Jayson Chambers in the hospital just over a week ago, there is a lot of speculation surrounding this match. Chambers badly needs surgery on his left knee -- but with the World Title at stake, can he really afford it right now?
