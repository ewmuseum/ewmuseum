Undisputed, On it's way

21 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Up and Coming fed, Undisputed, has set it's first Destiny Card after a promising house show this past week. Hello everyone and this is Josh Makela bringing you the latest update on Undisputed Wrestling. The first ever Destiny card is about to be released publically in the next couple hours. There are rumors flying around everywhere about who is going to be facing who, but the big question here is, who's in the main event? The main event got released along with the information that the Destiny card will be available in the next few hours. In that main event we have ToRn and Decadence squaring off. It will definately prove to be a good match since ToRn is a proven wrestler from Evo, Antipathy, and EdW. Sadly though we have no information on Decadence because he hasn't been in any federations that we know about. He must be something special though since he will be main eventing and facing ToRn. We may see an upset, or a dynasty start for ToRn who holds the most experience in the federation. In other news, all three owners will be making an appearance to the first show, which is not a surprise at all. If you didn't show up to your first show, what kind of owner would you be? Not a very good one at all. We'll see Dejuan Rapture, Jorden Snow, and 'Slick' Rick Styles at the show, I hope we can catch up to all of them for a word. Things are looking solid for Undisputed, and a great start is bound to go underway. Undisputed
