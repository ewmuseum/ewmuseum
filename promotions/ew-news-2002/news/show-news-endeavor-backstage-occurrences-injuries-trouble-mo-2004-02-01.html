SHOW News: Endeavor Backstage Occurrences, Injuries, Trouble, More

1 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Was there backstage turmoil at Endeavor? One could easily assume that SHOWrestling is headed in the right direction after the first Pay-Per-View (PPV), Endeavor, was through with at the end of Jan. 28, 2004. However, an insider stated that some backstage felt as if the PPV could have been much better had there not been as much drama backstage. An insider whom asked to remain anonymous stated, "Everyone felt that the show could have been a lot better. But too many egos came into to play." Contrary to what others may think, Ryan Harms, co-owner of the resurrected promotion, felt the event was great as he said, "I was nervous when I woke up that Wednesday morning... but, I really did like the turnout of the event. Buyrates are only projected within the .1 range. However, the outcome of the night should lead SHOW into a much bigger range of entertainment. "The PPV set up most of SHOW's upcoming storylines," Harms said. "Most every storyline is set in place and fans will not be unpleased with what we have in place." On the other hand the same anonymous insider gave this reporter some very strange information. According to him, the ending of the SHOWPrimetime Championship Ladder match was thrown together at the very last second. But the biggest problem with the match itself was that Uncensored and Nik VanCore do have legitimate heat. There was no creative push for the match, so the workers were given complete creative control. Most thought the idea was a good one - at first. But once the match was over, and the two nearly intentionally ended each other's careers, everyone backstage saw the match as a complete disaster. Aside from the disastrous Ladder match, everyone backstage felt that everything else, all other angles, and matches were great. That includes the incredibly dangerous hearse/explosion spot. Most everyone found the stunt a very entertaining way to end the event. And the final PPV note is even worse than the projected buyrate. Morpheus, the current SHOWorld Champion sustained a horrible injury during his match against §adi§†ic. His back nearly broke after he was piledriven through a wooden table. It was because of this that the match was put to a stop so abruptly, fans, and even other wrestlers were confused as to why the match ended as quickly as it did. Morpheus was all ready complaining of minor back pain, but after his match, it has intensified. "Morpheus is planning on hanging up the boots," said Harms. "We are currently planning on him only having one more match before he will call it official quits. On a lighter topic, SHOW's first DVD installment will be released this Saturday, and it will be entitled SHOWrestling: Rebuilding. It was a live event that was taped in Los Angeles.
