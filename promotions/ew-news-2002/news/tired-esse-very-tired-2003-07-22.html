Tired, esse, very tired.

22 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
They hitch themselves to my wagon, and think they are something special. The Spyder Esse, this is a tough world out there. So many people, they are trying to run around and get heat for themselves any way they can. The heel is dead, long dead, in professional wrestling. Today, bunch of gringos out in the ring talking trash... they have lost the art of the way it is. The way it should be. Being a heel isn't a gimmick, and if you hommes are using this gimmick, then you hommes ain't no heel. You're something else... something sad and pathetic. I spent some time arguing with this Studd whatever on eWNews' online web site. At some point you realize that all you've got on your hands is a wannbe something who never really was anything, trying to attach like a greedy parasite to someone more successful and more popular than he ever was. Three championships in four months hommes, and not little championships either... but the best the business offers. I shaped the fWo, I'm talked about every day on the web sites. There are Spyder fan sites, there are praises going out, and people who hate to admit that they like Spyder, but face facts... Spyder is popular. Spyder is something big. Does it surprise me that some gringo is trying to get heat from Spyder, some gringo who is too stupid to even post his column to the right area? I'm not from this country, but come on hommes, can't you read? Keep clinging to the graveyard that is the promotion you called home. It's dead, it's buried, and nobody cares. Or cared. Five fans doesn't make an audience... it makes a circle jerk, and that's all your pathetic PIW was. But I'm tired esse. Tired of the battles, tired of the wannabes trying to suck up. To make a name for themselves. To be something they never will be. These people using tired, worn out promos. Even in my own promotion, watching little Aimz and fluff boy Xias go at each other backstage. And of course you've got Flair watching his little red haired girlfriend's back and scar boy watching Xias' back, and I think to myself... is this wrestling? Is this what it has become hommes? Am I guilty of getting into fights with pathetic losers? Sure I am. The fact that they runoff sperm from PIW have targeted me as their little enemy shows that I put too much work into people who don't deserve it. Hommes, all I'm doing is letting some waste share in my spotlight. No more hommes, the spotlight is all mine. Go out and make something of yourself. Prove yourself. Then we can talk... but until then don't run with the bulls when you're a poodle, hommes. I'm tired esse. Tired of the fighting. It's hard to get these people into the ring so I can whip their ass when they are spending so much time bitching at each other backstage. Are you a wrestler or a talker? It's time to put up or shut up, hommes. It's time to get on the tights and get into the ring. Sure you will get beat by me esse, but that's just what happens when you go up against the best in the business. But at least you go out like a man, hommes. Not like a little shouting girl in the back. This is a business, not a talk show. Spyder.
