RaGE: Change of Address and Main Event Announced!

14 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Recent problems have been dealt with and RaGE now moves to a new server, SE-Live.com. In addition, the main event for New Dawn on January 9 has been announced as well as Heavyweight Championship information! Today, Real and Graphic Entertainment released another press statement from Atlantic City, New Jersey. Tom Mason and his executives were present in RaGE Headquarters with a guest, known as Matthew Lanier, the Owner of Sports Entertainment Live and more for Core Wrestling. This conferference would be the eighth for Real and Graphic Entertainment since the announcement in October that the company would be returning for a 2004 Season. The reason behind Matthew Lanier's presence came in the fact that SE-Live and Real and Graphic Entertainment would be collaborating. It was an agreement made by both Mason and Lanier, and it appeared that RaGE would now become part of the SE-Live address and take over the active server. With Sports Entertainment being defunct for the time being, Real and Graphic Entertainment's involvement with the company and the idea-contribution could affect the future of SE-Live. At the same time, Real and Graphic Entertainment has publicly announced its hiring campaign. Over the next two weeks, the company will be conducting try outs and taking in resumes from interested parties. The season starts on January 9 and the roster for Real and Graphic Entertainment is already at about 14 active wrestlers. More names are expected to be added over the next week. By Christmas, the roster will be finalized and released a few days later. It could be an interesting few weeks leading up to that announcement. As for event news with Real and Graphic Entertainment, New Dawn is scheduled for January 9 from the Staples Center in Los Angeles, California. An appropriate name for the show, New Dawn will feature the last Version Two Heavyweight Champion taking on the once champion. Pat Dwyer and Shawn Kain will go one on one after sparks flew a month ago when Shawn assaulted Pat at a press conference and stole his Version Two Championship belt. Along with that, the process to determine the Real and Graphic Entertainment Heavyweight Champion has been confirmed and New Dawn will feature six qualifying contests for the "Lion's Den Gauntlet" which will take place at Enmity on February 19. More news to come for Real and Graphic Entertainment later in the week. Real and Graphic Entertainment
