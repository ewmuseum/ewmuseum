eVo Facing Legal Action Over Wethers?

30 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
eVolution Wrestling may have to brace itself for a trip to the High Courts as PRIME is rumored to be considering sueing the promotion over their signing of Shane Wethers. Was a deal with PRIME actually sealed before Wethers services were taken to eVolution? Shane Wethers recent return to wrestling certainly has been big news in the wrestling world as of late, with the former PCW star tying himself down to a contract with eVolution Wrestling, despite alleged interest from rivals such as OSE. It was suggested that Wethers was close to inking a deal with PRIME before switching to eVolution, but company insider has claimed that Wethers did actually sign a PRIME contract nearly two weeks before setting foot in eVo. The unamed insider also claims that Wethers actually cut a segment for PRIMEs recent ReVolution show, only for it to be shelved from the final production. His final claim was to accuse Thug roC, who was until recently head of PRIMEs 5-Star Division, of "tapping" Wethers on arrival. We approached PRIME's Taun Pham for comment, but he evaded any direct questions concerning Shane Wethers answering "Since you are giving me free air time, I'd like to advise your viewers to catch our next edition of ReVolution in Sacramento. Thank you." With only a cheap plug as information, is it reasonable to assume that these claims are genuine? Does this Wethers segment exist? Did he sign a contract with PRIME and will they company follow this breach with legal action? It seems fair to assume that whatever the facts are, something smells rotten in regard these two companies relations. Did eVo really "steal" Wethers from PRIME, or is the promotion merely blowing steam? PRIME
