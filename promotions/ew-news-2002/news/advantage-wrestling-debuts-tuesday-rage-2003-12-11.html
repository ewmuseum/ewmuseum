Advantage Wrestling debuts Tuesday Rage

11 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Advantage Wrestling made it's return and with them they brought a second weekly show that is Tuesday Rage. Can Rage measure up to the legacy that is Monday Insomnia? Only one way to find out. Tuesday Rage had high expectations and it did live up to the hype! The beginning of the show was met by a small bus of sorts that is typically used to transfer the handicap children to school that came crashing through the curtains and drove itself all the way down to the ring. It was there that we saw a parody of sorts being performed by Teemu McCorde, Big Poppa, Sapphire and Nova who was wearing the Creature mask. The four of them announced that they were infact still intact as "Supreem Regime". With SR being back in AW it's going to be hard to see if anyone can match their talent. The first match of the night pitted Viper vs. Acrophobia. Viper got the upper hand early but Acro made his comeback and was aboard the top rope looking to cause some damage when low and behold from the crowd came a man who gave furious chair shots that could trigger the richtor scale! This man was then revealed as "The Real Deal" Nathan Slade! Why was this attack performed? What was this man's motive? Well, I shall say it probably wasn't the best motive but it seems as this man was feeling his spotlight being taken away by two former fellow employees joining up with his new found steady slew of friends. For one man it's the money, for the next man it's his recognition amongst co-workers.... With Razor issuing that both Nathan Slade and Acro will be the lumberjacks of Viper's match vs. Teemu McCorde this week I must say... On paper, it looks like all hell will break loose! AW better be all over this one or we're just looking at another lost match. The newcomer Chazz Irvine woo'ed the crowd with his in-ring ability and made short work of Troy Chryst, but does he have what it takes to step it up with AW veteran Brad Daddy? It's a huge push but the guys back at headquarters see alot of talent in the new one that is, Chazz Irvine. Ja'zel Walker and Brian Adkins made their AW in-ring debuts and an impressive one for both up n' comers but in the long run Ja'zel took home the V in what was supposed to be the Main Event up until the point where at the end of Monday Insomnia the Creach and Nova World Championship match was made for the next night on Tuesday Rage. Made it was, Creature and Nova squared off for the most prestigious title in the industry. It looked that Creature may become victorious thanks to his posse and fellow teamate Jack Duncan it was then Supreem Regime (Big Poppa, Teemu McCorde, Sapphire) came out to help their fellow stable mate and friend by evening up the sides. Nova and Creach were still doing battle in the ring and Nova landed a DSD (Dying Star Drop) and the fans went nuts as they thought it was over, hell, I thought it was over watching, but Creach kicked out! The match from there turned into one huge mess and all superstars were fighting like crazy as the show ended. But dont take my word for it, check it out for yourself. This week AW come's live to you from New York with another addition of MI and TR so tune in on the only station for men, Spike TV. Monday Insomnia Announcer & Head Promoter -Sean Levy- AW's Tuesday Rage
