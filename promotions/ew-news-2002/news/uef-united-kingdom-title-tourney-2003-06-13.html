UEF United Kingdom Title Tourney

13 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
UK Title Tourney It was announced late last week that the reigning United Kingdom Champion, Jay Moreno will be taking his lifetime dream trip around the world. The UEF President Derek Elsrod announced that now that his title is vacant a UK Title tourney will begin. We seen a UK Title Tourney last year in UEF which the former rookie of the year one, Criminal Darsis. Now that another one has been announced what names could we begin to see sprout out? Many would say the UEF Krash Course Champion, Johnathon Drachen has a good chance of winning it this year. As well as other names who have joined would be Greg Stevens the new UEF rookie. Whoever wins they have a lot of pride and history to live up to as many have used this title as a stepping stone. Such names as Ryan White, Prometheus, Criminal Sypher, Criminal Darsis and Ace. Other names speculated to be in this tourney are: James Sunderland, Safire, and of course Tama Porter. Stay tuned for more action on WrestleTV featuring all of your favorite UEF Superstars!
