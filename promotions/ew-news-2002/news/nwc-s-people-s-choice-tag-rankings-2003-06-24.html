NWC's People's Choice Tag Rankings

24 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NWC:News staff presents NWC's Bi-Weekly People's Choice Tag-Team Rankings. 1. The Fifth Column- They went from being the regional dynamo nobody knows about in the Pacific to the team that nobody wants to cross. Their upset win over Crash & Burn ended their reign and they proved it wasn't a fluke by beating The Destruction Crew at Across Enemy Lines 5. Then the week after against Dominion, then by crushing Guerilla Zen and now they travel South to face The Sound & The Fury who at last check, were the reigning Southern States Tag Team Champions. Will the body count rise or does it end where Custer made his last stand? 2. Dominion- Their trademark statement is that they're "better than you thought". You'd be hard pressed to find any team in the Atlantic that would disagree. After a rather violent and bloody feud with The Destruction Crew that resulted in Rob Thomas being shelved with a concussion, they've bounced back with a vengeance. They managed to upend longtime reigning and somewhat dormant former Atlantic Tag Team Champions, The Hung Jury. After defeating the Bad Ass Mothas at Greed and just recently fending off the Polar Opposites, there's only one team that they have yet to defeat. Namely the returning Jury that's looking to reclaim their throne and leave no doubt that they're the top dogs in the Atlantic's yard. Time will tell how this turns out. 3. The Sound & The Fury- One NWC star called these guys "Crash & Burn Lite" because of their goofy demeanor. One thing you can't contest is this duo's demeanor inside the ring. This duo started out hot back in late 2002, engaging in a rather heated and highly contested feud with The Hollywood Hitmen. Their first encounter went down at the South's Southern Comfort pay per view which saw the Hitmen prevail. It wasn't until the very first Domino Effect in December that Ian and Gildenstern were able to break through with a victory which upended the brother tandem. Since then, the feud has gotten bloody and figures to get no better with their cage match at Revival. While we don't know the result of that match or how that feud ended as it were, we do know they've got a shot at NWC World Tag Team Champions, The Fifth Column waiting on them at this upcoming Uprising. 4. Trail Of Dead- A while ago, they weren't even on the NWC tag team radar. Psycho Bob was a struggling midcarder in the middle of the Central pack until he brought in his partner and good friend Lindy. Then, in a rather shocking turn of events on an episode of Center Stage, they took on then Heartland Tag Team Champions, Hawaiians For Hire. One shocking pin later, the ToD managed to procure themselves a win and ended the title reign of NWC World Champion Billy Page's proteges. Since then, they've fended off Guerilla Zen and most recently, former Heartland Tag Team Champions The Upstarts. One can only presume that at their current pace, a shot at the NWC Tag Team Titles can't be far away and if they continue at their torrid pace...an NWC Tag Team Title reign might not be as far away as we think. 5. Polar Opposites- What do you get when you take one of a few men that can say they beat Anton Dufresne in the past year and pair him with a brash, brutal newcomer? One of the new phenoms within the NWC tag division. Former Atlantic Heavyweight Champion Ryan "Streak" Stephens and newcomer Chance McMillan have formed one of the better teams in the NWC. Even though the duo failed in their initial attempt to become Atlantic Tag Team Champions, there's no denying this duo's capable of great things within the Atlantic. The only question is, how long will it be before this unstable chemistry between the duo causes them to self-destruct? 6. Crash & Burn- What's to say about a team that set the record for longest reign as NWC Tag Team Champions since two mortal enemies formed The Unmentionables? Since their return to the Atlantic, they've faded from the national spotlight and had a fairly good showing in the Atlantic's Greed Games. In the final of Greed Games, they were dispatched of by BBP and "Illtown" Leonard Aarons respectively. Since then, they were upset by Polar Opposites and haven't done much since. How long before the "Innovators Of Tag Team Offense" return to form and what will it mean for the Atlantic tag team division? 7. The Hollywood Hitmen- They debuted in October in the South and the two brothers from Hollywood...Florida became the Southern States Tag Team Champions in only their second match. Their torrent first couple of months in the South had a few insiders likening them to The Demigods. Until the Hitmen lost their titles to The Sound & The Fury at Domino Effect in December, few could argue that the Hitmen were pretty close to being near unbeatable. Their agent, Terrell J. Hawkins all but demanded for a rematch, but his team had to settle for a rematch before AEL 5. That ended in a DQ when Ravishing Rick Reed brought a chair into the mix. But after much manuevering, the Hitmen finally procured a return match by beating Distill and Disdain. At Revival, the most heated rivalry in the NWC since the days of The All-Stars and The Fifth Column or even Arrogance Aside and Malignance, came to an end inside of a steel cage with the Southern States Tag Team titles on the line. 8. Hawaiians For Hire- These two massive Hawaiians have been brought to the NWC by none other than Bermuda Billy Page. And just like Page, these two Island boys have made short work of all who have opposed them. They won the Heartland Tag Team titles by way of winning what was supposed to have been a triple threat match between The Upstarts, Malign Intent and themselves at AEL 5. But thanks to a pre-match assault by the Hawaiians on Danzing, it turned into a one on one affair that the Hawaiians won. Their reign lasted only a month and a day in length as the upstart team of Trail of Dead upset them for the titles in late March. With the talent these two men have, the question isn't how they'll reclaim their titles, but a question of when. 9. Illegal Operation- The last time the Pacific saw a three man team like these boys, they were "Star-tacular" and were former NWC World Tag Team Champions. These three are associates of "Big Bidness" himself, SLJ. They debuted at AEL 5 and were extremely instrumental in assisting SLJ retain the North American title before he went on to lose it to The Amazing Rando two weeks later in the Atlantic. Their first time out was by no means a flash in the pan performance, as they went out and won a battle royal eliminating the team of Girls Night Out to become Wild West Tag Team Champions. The three man team has represented Bidness Inc. well ever since, fending off all challengers since then. If past history has taught us anything, three man teams such as The All-Stars and The Dynasty have been tremendously successful. Sources from inside the Pacific that have seen these two men believe that before all is said and done, this Bidness-like trio will have their names right alongside those two teams in the annals of the NWC history books. 10[tie]. J.D. Carter and Misery- There's not a lot to say about this team, as they were just recently formed a short while ago. In their first real test at Revival in one of the few matches we were able to see, they dispatched of Distill and Disdain to earn a shot at the Southern States Tag Team Champions at a later date. As impressive as they were in their debut, there's no way of knowing if they can go toe to toe with either The Sound & The Fury or the brother tandem known as The Hollywood Hitmen. 10[tie]. Chinese Fire Drill- A team with two rather clashing gimmicks as luck would have. But despite their first match antics against The All-Stars which got them beat down for their troubles, they've remained highly competitive and entertaining to watch. While they were unsuccessful in their first two on two battle with Illegal Ops, there's no reason to believe this will be a recurring theme. They're an extremely talented team and by most accounts, the leading threats to the Wild West Tag Team titles held by Control, Alt, Delete of Illegal Ops. 10[tie]. The Upstarts- DUUUUUUUDDE! The duo of JD Danzing and Dan E. Dare remain on the radar after a rather short hiatus. After an attack by the Hawaiians For Hire and Bermuda Billy Page, the duo returned a few weeks back on an episode of Center Stage looking to return to form. The talent level of the two is beyond question as is their drive to ultimately become the NWC World Tag Team Champions. Which with the return of the Lucente Cup, there's a good chance that they could represent the Central and ultimately run away with the whole thing. National Wrestling Council
