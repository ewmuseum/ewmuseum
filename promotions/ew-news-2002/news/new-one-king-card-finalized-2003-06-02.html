NEW One King card finalized

2 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ashton vs. Morgana, International & National Titles to be decided, more *insert NEW logo here* After a one week delay (due to the Arena Football League playoff game between Orlando and New York), New Era Wrestling has set the stage for their "One King" pay per view! One of the main stories going in is the feud between former National Champion Jamie Williams and former World Champion Chris Extreme! The blood in Extreme's veins has been boiling, after losing to Williams a few weeks ago in a No Disqualification match, and he challenged Williams to a cage match this week at One King! Williams has more on his plate, however, as he has to face Corey Ashton in another No Disqualification match. These two have been going at it since both proclaimed they are New Era Wrestling's "Millennium Man", and have brutally attacked each other the last few weeks. Ashton also will be pulling double, and possibly triple, duty, as he is in the One King tournament, which will decide the first NEW International Champion and new National Champion, a title vacated when Matt Salensky was released. He will face Joshua Michaels, a newcomer who's won his first two matches, including one over David Strife, the biggest man on the NEW roster! Rumors have been breaking that Ashton and Michaels have been at each others throats backstage, which should translate into a brutal match! The other One King semifinal will see Angel Dawson, an ally of Joshua, take on the mysterious Trigere. Trigere has been in NEW for nearly a month, and still nothing is known about this man, except that he is allied with April Starr, who beat Dawson last week! It will be interesting to see who comes out of each semifinal, and moves on to the final, a ladder match for both titles! As for the NEW World Title, champion Andrew Ashton has overcome tough odds, from Chris Extreme and NEW Owner Reno Frost, to former champion Adam Cameron, and he now stands atop the mountain. However, his dream could come crashing down in a matter of days, as this Sunday he faces the Art of War Battle Royal winner, and Number One Contender, Morgana. Morgana comes in as the near favorite. She's dominated everyone in her path, be it former NEW star Kyle Rayner, who she can take credit for running out of NEW two weeks ago, or Jeff Evans, who was left alone against Morgana and the father of her child, Chris Extreme. Some have even been calling this event "One Queen", in predicting Morgana's win. Extreme himself is an intangible, as his goal since his return on 3/1/2003 has been getting back "his" title. He's resorted to stealing it, however it returned to the grasp of then champion, Adam Cameron. He continues to seek a title match, but for this Sunday, he's banned from ringside. As for Cameron, a series of attacks from Chris Extreme has left him nearly a shell of his former self. He's been on a downward spiral since his title loss to Andrew Ashton. Last week, his opponent, Alistair Millennia, refused to face a Cameron who was destroyed by Extreme earlier in the night. The two agreed to a match this Sunday, and it should be a true "barn-burner", as both can go when the time is right. The opening match can be termed as a "throwaway", but some see it as a shot for NEW's next stars, or in one case, another shot for one of it's former stars. Jeff Evans is looking to get back on track, and hopes he can do so this weekend. April Starr will have Trigere in her corner, but will that be enough? David Strife hopes to use his size to rebound from his loss to Joshua Michaels, and Jimmy Jackson, called a "legend" in some parts, wants to make his name here in NEW. A win over three people will help that greatly. All this will happen Sunday, at NEW One King, in the TD Waterhouse Center! Select tickets are still available, and if you can't be there live, it will be on pay per view! New Era Wrestling (Not the one on EWMania)
