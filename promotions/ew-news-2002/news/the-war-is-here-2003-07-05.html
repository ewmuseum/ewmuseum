The War is here

5 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Core has turned into a bitter war between Core Loyalists and Fitzpatrick supporters as to who will have control over Core. In early May, Eugene Fitzpatrick debuted on Core Wrestling television after forcing a drunken shareholder Ron Rains into signing him an iron clad contract as CEO. Since then Core has been turned into an unfortunate chain of events. Fitzpatrick, with the help of certain few people backstage (Alura and Quinn Masters to name a couple) caused Chairman Matthew Lanier to spend the past couple of months behind bars for the fictional murder of his fiance Laura Brown. After claiming a near 100% control, he would get himself involved with Daniel Adams, costing him various matches during a brief spell managing the Dark Hearts. This would lead to them clashing at Against All Odds where Adams would pin Fitzpatrick to advance, only to succome to Jantz Spalding in the main event thanks to Fitzpatrick. Then there was the return of the British Humour Squad, Former Legacy Champion Fred Fusion and newcomer AWOL. After recruiting them, Fitz went on a rampage, taking Lanier's most prized possesions, and having Preston Lanier thrown out of Paddy's Pub. Now we have the return of Shareholder Robert Schnia. Schnia has booked the Fitz at the PPV against Daniel Adams. Schnia and Fitz have now been forced to work together in order to keep Core stable. Jack Diamond and Aryim also returned to Core in order to find evidence that would free Matthew Lanier from prison. Now they have the evidence, but due to the fact that Diamond and Aryim lost to Fred Fusion and AWOL, it is now in the hand of Fitzpatrick. Schnia now admits though, to have photocopied it before hand. Core has turned into a bitter war between Core Loyalists and Fitzpatrick supporters as to who will have control over Core. With Schnia having this new evidence, it seems that the tide is turning in on Fitz. When will Lanier be returning? Core Wrestling
