[CEW] Airs RitC, Announces National Search, and More ...

20 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In one 24 hour period, the world of Cyber Extreme Wrestling completely changed. For the better? For the worse? Read on ... http://www.cewentertainment.com/images/CEW_eWNews.jpg PITTSBURGH, PA - Three times before, Rage In The Cage has aired for CEW. And every time, Rage has solidified its place as the second-largest event in the CEW calendar. Two years ago, Cyber Extreme Wrestling nearly shut down after the actions of Top Gun and Courtney Westman prevented Rage from airing, tying the matter up in court for months. Last year, CEW met the threat of HCW in the ring, in a series of matches. The winning federation would survive, the loser would not. CEW took it until the last match, but in the end, CEW came out the victor. This year, it is hard to say what history will be created. Three potential superstars: Ryan Anderson, Weezle, and Michael Moseley meet in their pay-per-view debut. Cassandra Avery, Johnny Psychotic, and Lord Skywolf take their long-running field to the ring yet again, fighting over Avery's Television Championship. The newly-turned-hardcore contender Shayne Gowers attempts to take the belt from current FTS champion, Brian Blazer. Two CEW stables collide as Upstarts leader Kris eGo and Mark Knight take on D'z Nutz members Da*Link*Went and Allen Watts. 'Sensational' Jeremy Hunter returns to the CEW ring in a grudge match against "Showtime" Nick Alexander, who attempted to end his former-friend's career months ago. The rules: Fucked Up Ill Shit ... the winner: Whoever survives. And in the main event ... dominant champion Muzzolini will climb inside the cage against five-time World Champion AC. For weeks Muzz has been attempting to humiliate AC, until AC fought back last week. This week, neither man will be able to escape until the other is beaten. The event originally aired on Tuesday, August 19, at 8 pm. If you were unable to order the event, are unable to order the re-broadcast tonight, or do not receive Pay-Per-View in your area, please view the transcript at the [CEW] web site. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ [CEW] announced today that the board of directors have authorised a search around the country for wrestling talent to join the ranks in Cyber Extreme Wrestling. One [CEW] staff member, speaking on the condition of anonymity, was quoted as saying "The board of directors believes that, while CEW is a top-notch company with high quality talent, there are still other wrestlers around the nation that could bring additional talent to the ring and to the fans' eyes." With the recent signings of Michael Moseley and Weezle, and the ascension of developmental talent Ryan Anderson, the young ranks of [CEW] appear to be full to the untrained eye. But with some superstars reportedly nearing an end to their career or having trouble with [CEW] authority, a select few young stars might find themselves in the spotlight rather quickly. Expect further reports on [CEW]'s nationwide talent search in the coming weeks, as it develops. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ On Tuesday night, Cyber Extreme Wrestling, in conjunction with Ultraground Designs, released a new version of the [CEW] web site. This site, while loaded onto our servers in connection with the airing of Rage In The Cage, is still under major construction. As of 12:01 A.M. on Wednesday morning, very little of the site had been completed, though the design was complete, as well as the uploading of most of the scripts. Cyber Extreme Wrestling and Ultraground Designs would appriciate all [CEW] members and fans' cooperation throughout this construction process. ------------------------------------------------------------------ Cyber Extreme Wrestling announced today that on August 12, 2003, the federation signed to receive ratings from national organization Sports Entertainment Live, also known as SE-Live. SE-Live rates a number of wrestling shows on a weekly basis, and is run by individuals experienced in the wrestling world. Thrill 132, the first CEW show to be ranked by SE-Live, recieved a 5.7 rating on a 6.0 scale. Source: Sports Entertainment Live ------------------------------------------------------------------ For more news on Cyber Extreme Wrestling, please visit the [CEW] Web Site.
