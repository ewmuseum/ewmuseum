Former PIW Wrestler Thrown out of the arena...

14 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This week's Pressure Point, just before a commercial break (in the midst of the Trey Vincent segment), a certain fan was kicked out... During the taping of the LIVE! installment of Pressure Point, some disturbance was caused during the Trey Vincent segment which was presented on the Action Tron. It semed that a fan had obtained a megaphone, somehow, and began to verbally bash the federation AND Trey Vincent. The fan was soon chased across the arena during the commercial break. Just before it went live, the security team caught the fan and took the megaphone away...but just before they did, he stated one last sentence... "Don't cry Reed...you at least got ONE HALF of EGO" The fans in attendance literally went wild as they realized the fan was none other than former PIW Wrestler, Sean Studd. Studd has been missing from the wrestling world recently, after declining offers from Action! Wrestling, ACW and Old School Entertainment (all 3 of them offering a good amount of money, but Studd simply wanting more than they were offering). It's stated that Studd was making a few shots at some of the Action! roster....Trey Vincent, Max Danger, KroW, LLB, and a few others were given some insults. But one thing that Studd stated during his little speech was... "This isn't the first fed I'm going to fuck up" So one must believe that Studd will be attempting this act in another federation. The question is...which one? First he had a go with Spyder right on this news site...so one can believe he will be headed to an fWo event. Or possibly an OSE event...or even a ACW event. Only time will tell. Studd's First Victim
