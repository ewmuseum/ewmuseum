The World According To Prodigy.

13 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just your average wrestler wishing to unleash his infrequent angry rant on the wrestling community. From what I hear this is meant to be the most reliable wrestling news site on the net, and what do I read when I log on? Levine (That’s Krow for all you marks out there) doesn’t even know what’s going on his own show. LLB didn’t beat Jeff Garvin because he knew if he had, half the roster would have walked out and his ‘stuck in the nineties’ ass would’ve been to blame. Garvin beat LLB, Max Danger was angry because Roland just happened to make him look like shit when he’s meant to be the number one favourite to land the Action championship, do you know how that reflects on the company? I doubt our beloved Levine does, since he’s too busy sitting on his ass reminiscing about the good ol’ days and wondering when his fat paycheck is going to come in. And then we have Spyder, a cross between Rey Mysterio Jr. and Cyprus Hill, running his mouth on matters he has no idea about. Buddy I must have missed you in the Action backstage area, because for someone who isn’t a part of the company he seems to know a lot about what goes on. This is the guy who lost the title to Erik Kelly, and I’m meant to take him seriously? If it wasn’t for Vic Creed acting like a true champion and getting the title out of the hole you dug and buried it in you’d be the man responsible for the downfall of one of the biggest wrestling companies on the planet. I don’t know what ‘executive genius’ had the idea that you could hang in the main event, but I hope he got his just desserts and works at Burger King now. You’d think that taking up the luchador mantle would mean you were actually a viable competitor, but from what I gather you’re just a spoiled little brat trying to tag along on a legacy you know nothing about. Sorry to vent but when I log on here and see so much bullshit I really have to add my two cents. -Clayton Prodigy P.S. Watch me, on Pressure Point, Monday Nights on the WB.
