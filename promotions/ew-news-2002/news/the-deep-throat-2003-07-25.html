The Deep Throat

25 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Japanese adult film and media diva Yuka "Honey" Hamada gives us her "Big O" on California based independent promotion, The X. The X It’s quite refreshing to take a different approach at media through journalism. With all the on and in front of camera and photography I deal with in my life, it’s like a breath of fresh air to be able to sit back and take my time with this piece. For those of you who may not know, my name is Yuka Hamada. I used to work for PURE in its first generation before it closed, and now I work in The X, alongside my boyfriend Raizo Tenzan and the faction The Saikyo System. But most of you would be more familiar with my more… mature appearances on camera. If not, stop by your local video store and ask if they have “The Succubus”, from Japan. You’ll be quite pleased… ^_^ I write this column not to advertise my film accolades, but to bring much needed attention to The X, the Californian based independent promotion run by former PURE wrestler Jason Marah. Marah and my Rai (Raizo Tenzan to you) have never seen eye to eye, and I share my lover’s opinion, but I will admit that Marah has done surprisingly well with managing and booking this promotion. He has solid members of the roster that draw the fans’ attention, including a “Lizard-man”, a “Cyborg”, a “Demon-Possessed Loon”, and a “Monster”, in the literal terms… though I’m not questioning what Lestat is… that’s another story. I’m one of the skeptical few that think they’re full of it with what they’re portraying, but hey, as a member of the film business, I can see where they’re coming from. Besides the outlandish characters there’s talented individuals like Rich Sherwin, former PCW Tag Team champion with Ryan Moore of Pretty Cool Guys, Teijo Uekado, Ryuji Ishikawa the King of the Deathmatch, former PURE wrestlers S2 Cordozer Stevenson now Memphis Gray, former All-Asia champion Senji Yulav, and the last ever PURE All-Asia and first and last PURE Japan champion… my Raizo Tenzan… oh… and Rent-A-Hero of PCW… But don’t think The X is a man’s world, because you’re wrong. Ladies like Lotus, Reivyn, Nikki Covas, former employee Lumi, and yours truly are considered a few of the top ladies in the wrestling business. Yep, that’s right. Your favorite sexy playmate “Honey” Hamada can take a few of the guys to school on the mat with my amateur wrestling. Currently Ryuji Ishikawa is The X’s World Wrestling Champion, having won off a brutal match against bitter rival Teijo Uekado. Along with his wife Kaori Yamamoto-Ishikawa, the two have joined forces with Raizo Tenzan, Lotus, and myself in the Saikyo System, an anti-authority, no rules “yakuza” style alliance. I quote “Yakuza” because in all honesty… we’re entrepreneurs, not criminals ^_^ Rivalries within The X include: Ryuji Ishikawa v Teijo Uekado Raizo Tenzan v Rent-A-Hero The Outcasts (Gila, Giren, Cyber, Vex) v Jason Marah, Rio Espines, Maelstrom, Lestat) Nikki Covas v Shaun Styles Lotus v Kai Cheung Senji Yulav v Jason S Hughes The X is always looking for new and qualified talent for their promotion, and if you’re interested, come see a show someday and bring a resume. I wont do show recaps, because basically it’s passé. But I will suggest seeing After Dark if it’s available in your location. It comes on late, but it’s definitely worth your time. In The X news, Rob Hayes appears to be recovering well after being attacked by X-Wrestling’s (A different federation than The X) The Dark Messiah, formerly of Global Championship Wrestling. The Dark Messiah appeared near the end of this week’s After Dark program, shooting on the company and it’s roster before beating challenger Rob Hayes viciously. The Dark Messiah was escorted out of the building by security while Hayes was rushed to the hospital. The X owner Jason Marah would only respond saying that he’s very displeased with the actions of The Dark Messiah and will be discussing the matter with XW very soon. Rent-A-Hero is reported to be signed on a verbal agreement contract, as Jason Marah said in a released statement earlier this week. While currently working for thReat under a non-exclusivity clause (from what I hear from my resources), Marah has allowed RAH employment within The X after hearing RAH’s reason for appearing last week on After Dark. “He has personal business with Raizo Tenzan and the Saikyo System, and seeing how I’m not too fond of them either, I allowed him the chance to settle the score.” He closed saying that Rent-A-Hero would be addressing the matter personally with Raizo Tenzan on this coming After Dark. In personal news, production for my new film, “Trine”, is complete, and it will debut in video stores internationally this week. “Trine” is my first produced film, and I hope it becomes the beginning of a genre within the adult film industry. The film tells of three tales involving three women, starring myself, a surprise star, and the debut of new star Gwendolyn Young. On sale on VHS and DVD this week! That’s all for news now, but there’ll be more to come in the next few days. Now as for rumors… -I hear that another PCW star may be on his… or her… way to The X in the next few weeks. With the rise of PCW names joining will The X rise in stock in the wrestling business? -Who was Nikki Covas with backstage last After Dark? The X staff say that it was a member of The X roster… but who among the roster can afford HER prices? - An anonymous source called in to The X offices, and reported that one of the members of the Saikyo System may be in for quite the surprise appearance this coming After Dark. We’ll have our eyes open… trust me. -A star from the premiere months of The X may be making a return to the company soon. But who? That’s all for now… I hope you enjoy this piece, and if I get rave reviews, I’ll “keep em coming” ^_~ Love, Yuka “Honey” Hamada. The X Homepage
