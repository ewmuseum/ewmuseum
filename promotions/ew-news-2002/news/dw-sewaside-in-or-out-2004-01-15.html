Sewaside Vs. Storm? After the card was announced for DW Destruction #87 the whole world was in shock when they viewed the top event and saw that Sewaside was back in DW to settle an old score against Shawn Storm in a Hell in a Cell Match, however some doubts have been raised over whether or not Sewaside will actually be able to turn up at the match at all. With the match being held in DW’s new home of London, England, it seems as if Sewaside has hit some problems getting into the country after his past problems with the law back in U.S.A. Apparently not even Jon Page has been able to pull on the strings of the British government and allow one of his star attractions into the country. If as expected Sewaside is definitely refused access to the country, it is expected that the former International & Tag Team Champion will just hang up his wrestling boots after a long career, simply because there are no better feds that he could join up with. However all may not be lost for fans that bought tickets expecting a Sewaside Vs. Storm Hell in a cell match, DW Owner Jon Page seemed very relaxed when asked about the main event and simple replied, “If it is not going to be Sewaside Vs. Shawn Storm then rest assured it is going to be something just as good, if not better!” DYNASTY WRESTLING
