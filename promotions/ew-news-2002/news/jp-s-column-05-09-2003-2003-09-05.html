JP's Column 05/09/2003

5 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JP gives his weekly view of the MWF, including his view on the return of Insane Wayne and Dynamite 01/09. Hello everyone, I'm back! Yes now the MWF is back and ready to rock n roll once again then I am here to provide my weekly report on all the latest happenings in the MWF. It's been a while since I actually looked at the MWF in any depth and everything is changing so fast! We've got some of the great wrestlers back who left a few months ago and they will no doubt add strength and depth to an already impressive roster. First things first, I want to look at Dynamite and show you exactly how the MWF ended it's short holiday and brought itself back into the spotlight. Credit to all those involved in Dynamite because it was one of the most emotional shows I have ever seen, the departure of the General Manager was a tear jerker to say the least. The show started with the MWF President and his current henchman, Scotty Heart and Sean Harley backstage talking about the upcoming tag team match with NomaD and EyCe. The match was scheduled to be no-disqulification but Walker felt it was wise to change the stipulation to protect his tag team. Scotty Heart was not too pleased at the decision and showed his disgust as Harley n Heart walked off. Opening match was not the greatest, both wrestlers were very sluggish. Duncan McGafferty and Damien Johnson had a great chance to shine but unfortunatly they didn't take the chance. Johnson we were very disappointed with and we were looking for a good performance in front of his home fans, he's a Wisconsin boy. When a worker comes to his/her home town they are expected to give on hundred per cent and nothing less, you don't see the WWE's The Rock give a bad showing in Miami, Florida do you? Needless to say that both men are no longer with the MWF. A very entertaining segment made up for the last match, Ace Spade and Doris were backstage. Ace Spade is exciting everyone backstage as he knows how to build a character and while the fans may hate him I have heard some of the management singing his praises. He and Doris spoke of Troy Knight and of Spade's recent attacks against those close to Troy. A clever way to build up a feud that, attack those close to the source and with Spade playing the evil heel we can hope that the brave Knight manages to fight back and avenge the attacks on his son and 'lover'. A top class triple threat match followed and Troy Knight took centre stage, as he so often does, and controlled the match from beginning to end. Freddy Ryder and Rodney Ferno put in solid performances to help lift the match and entertain the fans, I thought that Ace Spade would appear earlier than he did but when he came a disaster occured and he ended up helping Troy to win the match! Clever work from the writing squad led to "The Player" smashing a chair across the head of Troy Knight to send him tumbling to the mat, Troy ended up atop of Rodney Ferno and counted the pinfall by Grant August. This match as many said was an indicator of who would be challenging for the MWF Television Championship at Implosion in a few weeks time. No surprises but I think Troy Knight will be there. A horrible incident occured after the match, Sean West walked on down and we knew he had some issues with Rodney Ferno but we didn't know it required such a savage beating. We felt Sean West went a little too far but it certainly got him over as a heel with the fans as he beat the living hell out of Rodney Ferno and as you will read later caused a change on the roster. Kelly Day was distraught as Ferno felt the force of Sean West. Dynamite really began after the triple threat match, when "Debaser" hit and the former MWF General Manager made his way out to the ring for the very last time...and little did we know it really would be the least time. I had tears in my eyes as Fozzy thanked the fans for the time he'd shared with them in the MWF, then it happened, Anarkist showed his face and the place went into a frenzy. The MWF World Champion said he had a surprise for Fozzy and Fozzy turned round to find the newly signed Simon Masters breathing down his neck, Masters hit his finisher on Fozzy and I thought the ring was going to collapse. Anarkist walked down and I could feel the hairs on the back of my neck standing up as he stepped into the ring and grabbed the downed Fozzy. The crowd could hardly make a sound because they were so shocked as Anarkist began to threaten to put Fozzy through a set of ringsteps with the Broken Rule. Now for the clever bit... Of all the people you'd expect to come out and try to save Fozzy the MWF President Edward Walker would not be one. Walker came out and stunned the crowd as he pleaded with Anarkist not to destroy the father of his grandchild...Anarkist then flashed the most sadistic smile I have ever seen in professional wrestling, he hit the Broken Rule on Fozzy and I swear his body almost broken in half. Walker collapsed in tears and looked up to find his pregnant daughter standing over him. I could hardly believe it as Laura Walker uttered the words to her father, that Anarkist was actually the father of her baby. The MWF President broke down and I don't blame him, are we to see Walker turn back to face? Well he is going to sympathy route that's for sure. An excellent segment finished with Anarkist, Masters and Laura Walker looking down on the unconscious Fozzy. Main Event time and Sean Harley n Scotty Heart were to make their stand and claim they wanted a piece of the MWF Tag Team Championships at Implosion, the "Untouchables" of NomaD and EyCe were the opponents. A hard fought match followed and I think EyCe missed the opening segment of the show as he slammed a steel chair into the back of Scotty Heart, Galliant called for the bell straight away and the "Untouchables" unfortunatly and unluckily lost the match. Sean Harley did an after match beat down on EyCe and challenged him to a real no-disqualification next week. Dynamite overall was an impressive show, considering the MWF had been away for a week it was even more impressive. A more solid opening match would have added a lot more to the show, and perhaps an extra match to but that was not possible due to financial implications. The MWF has then been splashing out on some new talent recently and I couldn't believe my eyes when I saw what had happened in the past week. We resigned a man who was at the peak of the MWF a few months ago, he could have gone futher than he did but Insane Wayne is a man who may be growing older but still has the tools to challenge Anarkist for the MWF World Championships. Insane Wayne is a former UWF World Champion and is very popular with the fans, he took a real beating at the hands of Anarkist and Ross Allen and was forced from the MWF and wrestling general, he's now back and teamed up with rookie Iniquity. They are a team that I think everyone should stand up and take notice of, they will not have a tag match for some because both men need to prove themselves in the singles arena first. Iniquity had made an impressive start to his MWF career by cutting some sensational promotional material and the joint work with Insane Wayne has shown the two working very well together. They're not the only new tag team in the MWF, the MWF World Champion has joined forces with Simon Masters...another MWF rookie. The two men formed an alliance on Dynamite when they beat down the departing Fozzy and now with Laura Walker at their side they have an ace card over the MWF President. Masters has yet to really show himself on the promotional scene but when he gets in the ring there is no doubting the talent and ferocity of his wrestling. We've had Mickey Bombastic sign up onto the temp roster and he looks to be a great new talent, if he can do the right things to impress the management then he will become a permanent fixture in the MWF...you never know he could be a future star. Kidd Kidman has returned after going AWOL, the management were very worried for the safety and well being of Kidman but he has returned ready for action and we believe it was family problems which led him to leave without telling anyone. I feel it is a great bonus for the MWF to have Kidman restored to the roster and also makes the "Untouchables" a real force in the MWF. Joe Foale has been brought back into the MWF fold, he was previously a part of the MWF but decided to call it a day when offered a chance to tour New Zealand. Now, after six months of Kiwi wrestling he has brought himself back to the MWF and is looking to, like Insane Wayne, reachieve what he did before and that was create an awful lot of hope and expectation. One of the MWF's big hopes has left, Scotty Heart, part of the Harley n Heart tag team has decided to hang up his boots and not tread the squared circle any longer. The man who was such a success in other federations as well as the MWF feels he is too old to compete with the competition...a shame because he was tipped to do great things. Moving on, Dynamite is approaching and I am sure you are all eager to know who I think will walk out a winner or who will end up on their backs. Doomsday versus Iniquity: Should be a real clash of the heavyweights, both of these men are giants of the ring. Doomsday has the experience from wrestling in the UWF and is highly rated by Insane Wayne, the two reguarly battled in the UWF. Iniquity has youth and charisma and is looking to turn his promising promotional work into a promising ring style, the only problem is his size, he will need to be able to move quickly and we will see whether Doomsday can exploit any weaknesses. I think Iniquity will just edge this. Joe Foale versus Mickey Bombastic: Joe Foale is a very good young wrestler and this match has changed so many times in the past week, with the departure of Freddy Ryder and Scotty Heart we've now got Mickey Bombastic. I doubt Foale is too happy about facing a rookie wrestler but he has no choice currently and he'd do well to tow the line and pick up what should be an easy victory. Troy Knight and Flash versus Ace Spade and Sean West: This is could be a really really good match, two rivalries are channeled together to form a tag team match. Ace Spade and Troy Knight have been going at it for weeks but this could see the two men actually battling in the ring officially for the first time. Flash is out for revenge after Sean West brutally attacked Rodney Ferno, Ms.Frost will have her hands full no dobut with Kelly Day at ringside. I think we will see Ace Spade and Sean West pick up the win. Insane Wayne versus Simon Masters: Wayne has made an impressive comeback to the MWF and this could wrap up the impressive comeback. Sean Masters is a wrestler who has the size and the talent to take him far but Insane Wayne has experience and the ability to instantly turn a match. Masters has an ace card, that being MWF World Champion Anarkist at ringside. If Anarkist comes into play then this match is far too hard to predict! EyCe versus Sean Harley: I think, to be honest, Sean Harley is going to be out numbered and defeated. Harley is a good wrestler but he will have to try and win what will effectively be a handicap match with NomaD at ringside and both EyCe and NomaD want revenge for last weeks debacle. Harley will need his younger brother Jamie Harley full alert if he is to stand a chance. That's all from me folks, I've given you my thoughts...Implosion is not far away and I will give my thoughts on that soon. Take care of yourselves. JP (John Pilkington) Maximum Wrestling Federation
