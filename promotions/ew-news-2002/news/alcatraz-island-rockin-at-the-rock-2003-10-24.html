Alcatraz Island: Rockin' At the Rock!?

24 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
America's most NOTORIOUS Federal Penitentiary reopens, and besides for all the glamor and show, there's STILL more than meets the eye! At a well-attended ribbon cutting ceremony in San Francisco, Governor-Elect Arnold Schwarzenegger formally announced the reopening of the most notorious federal penitentiary in the world: Alcatraz Island. Wardens General Brent Addonzia, Gulf War Veteran, and anti-government activist, Che Weiland, were present for the ceremonies, and after giving a brief speech, the audience was treated to a quirky incident involving Che and his motor vehicle. Federal authorities see the reopening of such a place necessary, and hope to send a message to would-be future criminals around the nation. Unknown sources leaked earlier on to the press that, evidently, as elaborate and as polished-up as the events surrounding this historic occasion have been, we haven’t seen half of it yet. Apparently, some rumor has been circulating around that there may be some reality television series made out of act, and if so, it’s quite a bold move by the government. A circus show of freaks and charismatic inmates will occupy this island; for many of them, their home until death. One can only imagine the psyche of such a person, and it makes one wonder how successful this dramatic soap opera will be. When asked for comment, both head wardens rejected, and there was an alleged incident in which a local media reporter caught Che toking up a stash of weed. Strange, to say the least. Legal troubles for the Rock has already begun, and lawyers of the families of the inmates are already filing lawsuits against the state of California. Thus far, they seem to be making very little headway though, and constitutionally, most experts agree they have little ground to stand on. Many family members believe that the staff (including the exalted leadership) of the facility are corrupt and unfit to run such a program. Gen. Addonzia casts aside all doubt though, pointing to his distinguished record as a soldier of the United States Armed Forces, and promises “a strict and purposeful” process of detention and rehabilitation. Che, on the other hand, still has much to answer for. A couple of the names standing out include a number of well-known fugitives, and a couple of oddballs: James Kestel; a Hitler-loving Nazi, allegedly “drove to insanity” by the Jews, Troy Chryst; a world-renowned drug-trafficker, John Edward Garcia; a 7-foot, 350 lb man-beast, John Escober; a man who’s killed outside of prison, and inside as well, and Vincente Mendoza; a weapon-smuggler/drug-trafficker, and all-around sly character. It seems, at the moment, these wardens have there hands full.. - More to come as the story unfolds. Alcatraz Island
