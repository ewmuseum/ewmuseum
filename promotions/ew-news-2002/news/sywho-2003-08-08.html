SyWho?

8 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Keith Scott Zimmerman with an online commentary. Just when we all had hoped he stopped, too. I hope them cigarettes are gonna make you cough I hope you heard this song and it pissed you off I take that back I hope you're doing fine And if I had a dollar I might give you ninety-nine Little miss, little miss, little miss can't be wrong Ain't nobody gonna bow no more when you sound your gong Little miss, little miss, little miss can't be wrong What'cha gonna do to get into another one of these rock 'n' roll songs? --Spin Doctors, "Little Miss Can't Be Wrong" Few people know this, but because I'm connected and in with the in-crowd, I can reveal you the truth. Sylo was in fact raped by Kobe Bryant. That's the best I can figure it out as. Because for someone to start bitching, whining, and moaning, and using the media as a tool from which to paint themselves as some sort of martyr who's been wronged by the giddy middle finger of fate, as near as I can tell these days, Kobe Bryant must've raped them, no? No? Maybe then that's why Sylo's final parting shot at Action! Wrestling as the doorknob hit him in the ass (of course, he didn't sell it) rings hollower than a movie theater showing a Gigli matinee. Let's just look into his statement here so I can tear it to Lewis Blackian furious shreds. Sylo: Why Sylo? I must've been the only one hearing 3 million people shrug and say "You weren't fired already?" Sylo: That’s all I’ve heard lately and I’m here to tell you why. The reason why I left Action Wrestling was because of the politics. There is a game played around there called “kiss the magical ass” in which participants kiss ass to get ahead in the federation. Men like Sharc, Bobby Minio and KroW bleed day in and day out for the fans, they are the heart and soul of federations yet they’re over looked. Hmm...Sharc, Bobby Minio, KroW...what do they alllll have in common? Affinity for SpongeBob SquarePants? Stock in the Packers? Oh! I know I know I know! This is another "What do you MEAN PIW II won't work?" oratory disguising itself as a petition for civil rights! You know, I'm a self-serving jerkoff but at least I've been pretty up front about it. First of all, I just saw Bobby Minio have a good WRESTLING MATCH with Rook Black, the match of the night in my mind at Pressure Point. And it's been going pretty neck and neck with my match due to indy fan sites, so it's obvious if Minio hangs around and builds on it he'll be fine. Sharc's getting over as a face due to him hating Reed because who doesn't? So what this really comes down to once you get the bullshit detector out is KroW & Sylo Bitch & Moan Part Four Billion And Twelve. Commonalities: former PIW World Champions who feel they've never gotten their just deserts in A. All right, since you're both big and dumb I'll say this slow and try to cut back on the three-syllable words: when you changed federations (whoops), your environment (d'oh!) changed. PIW was ECW 2000. It was based around hardcore wrestling, so when you hit each other in the head with trash cans for 20 minutes, it was received well. That's what people expected from PIW: mindless violence. Of course, like ECW, PIW shut down. So you move to Action. Since LLB's a long-time rival, KroW gets a reign. Then he walks out of the federation. Sylo never really gets started and disappears for months at a time. And you come back and obviously the answer is "You're not getting pushed enough?" Jay, Whatever The Fuck KroW's Real Name Is, you ignorant sluts. Guess what? If someone doesn't show up for work for a few months for no reason, they generally get shitcanned. If someone tells their bosses to take a short step off a moving plane and then 7 months later come back on their hands and knees begging for a second chance, the boss will rehire them for the sheer purpose of torturing them right under their heel. Someone needs to tell you two that history is more than just that thing you used to skip so you could run off to gym. KroW hates the company and says so, then is surprised when they won't push him. It's simple: you are an AN ACH RO NI SM. That means, put in your terms, you don't belong here. It's like if you were walking down the street and saw a caveman, or a gay man inside the Playboy Mansion--don't add up. And don't give me this "you hate this company too" shit--I hate the decisions that are made by the company sometimes and say so. Loudly. But not only do I back it up with actual, y'know, good matches, I'm damn grateful they took a shot on me with my pissant experience. Not only that, but you skip off weeks and still get paid! What else do you want them to do, hold your dick for you while you piss? That's reserved for LLB and if nothing else your first tour of A! duty should've taught you that. Simply put, if you want to remake PIW since it was apparently some sort of magical happy fairyland with gumdrop houses on lollipop lanes, shut up, get out, pool your damn resources and just start the fucker over. Otherwise, be grateful you got a job that lets you do if not what you love what pays your bills. Sylo, since you don't know who Spyder is: Spyder es un Campeón anterior de Mundo de fWo que casi es tenido cada cinturón en la compañía en bajo un año. El es un mejor luchador que usted, una mejor promoción que usted, y es más sobre que usted. Usted es un fósil mudo que jode cuyos mejores días están en el pasado; EL es el futuro del deporte y de uno del mejor 10 luchadores que caminan la Tierra. ¿Así que QUIEN en el JODE es USTED, usted malabarismo de haired teñido estúpido de gallo a Tirano Azul sobrealimentado? [Yes, I'll translate: Spyder is a former fWo World Champion who's held nearly every belt the year he's been there. He's a better wrestler, a better promo, and just better than you period. YOU are a fossil who's best days are behind him; HE is the future of the sport and one of the 10 best wrestlers walking the Earth. So WHO in the FUCK are YOU, you cock-juggling overfed Blue Meanie?] For some PIWers like Bobby Minio and Bradley Duncan, they've adapted to Action's wrestling-oriented style and have changed accordingly. But there are some who haven't, and miss the good ol' days when it was about chair shots, cheese graters, and THEM in the spotlight. As easy an out as it would be to quote OK Go's "Get Over It", I'll leave you with this... You're only as useful as long as you're necessary. When you're not necessary, you're no longer useful. A!
