eVolution: Sovereign Returns!

13 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Former Universal Champion Returned At Tainted Love! With Tainted Love now behind us the fans of eVo have been speaking up about the events of the Main Event. Most fans have been anticipating the return of eVo's first Universal Champion for what seems like ages now since he left the Company just short of a year ago thanks to his epic match with James Kimson at Unbreakable in 2003. However rumors are swirling about what is to take place this time around. Fans certainly are expecting Sovereign vs James Kimson II to take place. Insiders at eVo headquarters are saying that it most certainly will take place at Unbreakable and will possibly the most anticipated match in eVo History. A poll was given to 1,000 lucky fans today in New York outside of eVo HQ asking several Questions as to what the fans wanted. Here are the Results.
