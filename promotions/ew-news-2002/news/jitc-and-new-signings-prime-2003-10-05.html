JITC And New Signings (PRIME)

5 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
When the troubled Taun Pham announced the King Of Kings PPV, he was slated by several in and around the company for using a rushed and dated concept. With that being said, the JITC promises to offer up several top draw matches with the tournament winner recieving the added bonus of a Global Title shot. The final brackets of the Jewel In The Crown tournament have been released. The drawing has brought up severel interesting encounters in the first round, of which four matches will take place next week on ReVolution. In order to win the tournament, a participant must go through four gruelling matches, three of which will be at the King Of Kings PPV. All of the matches will be standard one on one contests with a ten minute time limit. If time expires on a match, then no winner is declared and both men will be eliminated, giving their next opponent a bye to the next stage of the tournament. Sixteen of PRIME's superstars have been selected for the tournament and the winner will not only be the JITC Of PRIME 2003, but will receive a Global Title shot. All of this promises and exciting... if arguably unoriginal conclusion come King Of Kings. The matches are listed below, brackets can be viewed at PRIME's official website. Nero Kidd -vs- The First Syn -vs- Steve Starr Dru Dryden -vs- Adam Andy Magnum -vs- Rogue Rock Startling -vs- Carl Ca$h Mark Blaze -vs- Antheus Shawn Johnson -vs- Zyfloi Ian English -vs- Mr Showtime With just under an hour before the official JITC Tournament Brackets were released, PRIME officials have unveiled two surprise new signings, Zyfloi and "The Ikon" Dru Dryden. Zyfloi joins after the unfortunate closure of Dynasty Wrestling. Sebastian used his contacts there to arrange talks with the exiciting cruiserweight prospect and in just days a deal was done. Dru Dryden, a former NEW and N40 superstar, was signed after he took a break from the wrestling business. Now refreshed, Dryden will be looking to recapture his true form in PRIME. PRIME
