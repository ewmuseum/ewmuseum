DWA: Former Television Champion Released Suddenly

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Former TV Champion, Evo Shandor, has been released from the DWA rather suddenly, just hours before he was scheduled to wrestle on Showdown. Evo Shandor came into the DWA, and almost immediately captured the Televison Championship beating current World Champion, Typhoid Mary. However, ever since losing the title, Evo has been in kind of a slump both in and out of the ring, and it came to a head today, as he reported for Showdown tonight, where he was to team with the DWA's resident hero, Donnie Darko, against two members of the FoW, John Reed and Brad Keidis. But instead of coming in ready to wrestle, he went immediately to owner Jordan Brannigan's office saying he didn't want to wrestle any more. Jordan, who has just been the owner of the DWA for two weeks time, gave him his immediate release. Evo has refused comments concerning his release, but when asked by Destiny Mandrake, Jordan Brannigan had this to say. "He wanted out, I let him out. I don't care about his reasons. I don't want him here if his heart isn't going to be in the game," Jordan said to Destiny earlier today. There is no word on how this will affect Zula Shandor, Evo's sister and current Velocity Champion. DWA: Dragon Wrestling Association
