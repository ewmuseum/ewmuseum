UEF Superstars Making Predictions for the Ultimate Fighter Tournament

3 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
UEF Reporter Dex finds out what some superstars think about their current standings in the Ultimate Fighter Brackets With Week1 of the Ultimate Fighter Tournament underway, the newest reporter to the.UEF, Dex, has been on the prowl with news from UEF Superstars about what they think about the tournament. My first encounter with TamaPorter who had this to say: "I'm glad that Ace has won and is advancing to the 2nd round, and I hope he does well." I later asked Ace himself of what he thought of his match and would the Turnbuckle Powerbomb be his winning card if he hopes to win the Ultimate Fighter Tournament, this is what he said: "Tama put up a good fight, but just couldn't hold against the Turnbuckle Powerbomb. as far as my standings go, I'm in the so-called 'Wild-Card' Bracket and I almost did it so last year so I am pretty comfortable at where I am." My last encounter was with ZaZ. He had some harsh words to say about the Ultimate Fighter after I had asked him about the rumors that he couldn't win it this year: "umors are nothing than words of the bigots. If, and WHEN I get to the Semi Finals, I'm going to take either one of those two[Ace and Chris Hardy] for a match they'll never forget." That's all from the UEF's Star Reporter, Dex. the.UEF
