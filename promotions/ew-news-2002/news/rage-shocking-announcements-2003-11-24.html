RaGE: Shocking Announcements!

24 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With another press release, Real and Graphic Entertainment is making a more serious than ever attempt to get set for January 2004. This time, the re-signing of a former Heavyweight Champion leaves a current champion seeking answers. Never has Real and Graphic Entertainment quit, and it looks to never be heading in that direction. Now more than ever, the idea strung together one year ago seems to be a sure thing as Tom Mason presented his fifth press release, the sixth for the company, in the past month and a half. After being absent from last week's announcement, Mason made a big return with impressing announcements this week. First, two new names had been signed for the 2004 Real and Graphic Entertainment Season. Those included John Doe, a rookie signed over the past summer who never was able to debut, and the other was a former Version Two Heavyweight Champion, to be named at the conclusion of the press conference. Without giving everything away that happened, Tom Mason spoke also on the deals of the show volume for the year. As it has been announced, Real and Graphic Entertainment will air just fifteen (15) major events for the year on Pay Per View. Other than that the weekly status of events was a toss up, but now the company is saying to keep drawing crowds and create excitement, there will be live events (non-televised) each week. This will in essence give localized crowds more of a change to see the company up close if they are not on the list for this coming year's 2004 Pay Per View Tour. A good idea it seems and a rather cheap investment as well as all seats range from just $15 to $20. A list of venues and dates will be available shortly on this. Getting back to the issue of the signing, or re-signing, of a previous wrestler, it appeared Real and Graphic Entertainment has become more serious than ever. The man who has been signed as the fifth member of the 2004 roster (officially confirmed roster), made his impact felt in more than just a great speech on Sunday afternoon. This man left an impression on the Version Two Heavyweight Champion, Pat Dwyer. To read about exactly what impression he left, click on the link to Rageproject.com below and go to Press Conference Six. Be sure to check out each and every press conference as well.
