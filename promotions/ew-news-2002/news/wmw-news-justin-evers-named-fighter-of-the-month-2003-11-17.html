WMW News: Justin Evers named Fighter of the Month

17 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After a tough, tough count after a very active voting polls, it has been announced that Justin Evers has been named Fighter of the Month, for the Month of October. Cleveland - After a tough, tough count after a very active voting polls, it has been announced that Justin Evers has been named Fighter of the Month, for the Month of October. WMW Wrestlers had voted unanimously for Evers, after his hard work and dedication in and out of the ring. "It just goes to show that people are noticing," said Brad Johnson, WMW Owner. "Evers is a class act guy. Just a stand up, great guy. His work as Vice President has done nothing but impress me. He knows what he is doing in this business, and is helping mold WMW to be a better, more organized federation." Johnson said he was not surprised about the voting, as he knew in his heart Evers would get the votes. "It wasn't a question of who else was in the running. No one was in the running," Johnson continued. "We all knew at the exact same time. Evers is a great fighter, and a great friend. His work behind the scenes is second to none, and so is his in-ring work. If only I had more people like him, I could finally sit at my desk and just watch." Evers said he felt "honored" by the vote. We hope he continues to push, and continues to achieve success here, in Wrestling Midwest. WMW Wrestling
