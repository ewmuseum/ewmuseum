[ACW] Wanted...Anyone Who Can Land a Suplex!

13 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
All-Star Championship Wrestling is looking for their next superstar, and it could be YOU! Some ACW fans may be quick enough to spot that ACW goes through it's fair share of talent, but according to former ACW World Champion and current staff member SilverHAWK, it's not totally their fault. "Many of the guys that we hire here are guys who have been there and done that, and so far, it's come to our attention that they just aren't up for it anymore compared to a 23-24 year old young up and comer, that's what we've set out on this Star Search." What is Star Search you ask? Well, it's ACW's new plan to find young, local talent on each of it's next 5 show dates, in which it will try out a number of young stars and hopefully, find a few diamonds in the rough while they are at it. "It can obviously go both ways, we could find some nobody who has zero experience but has so much will and drive that we could train him, or her up. If we are lucky enough we could maybe find some indy star who hasn't had the best of luck in previous jobs, and now it would be their chance to do something at a company that is going places." ACW has been known for a while as the federation that gives the nobodies a chance, and SilverHAWK says that this nickname is what is proving to be the deciding factor in fans tuning into ACW. "A year ago who had really heard of your Osyrus', Jason Kain's, Alias' or even your SilverHAWK's for that matter. We have a very good reputation for building up young stars, something that some companies, like the WWE, very much do not have. At ACW, we have no option other than to bring up the talent we have and it seems to have worked so far." SilverHAWK answered the questions of a few internet journalists as they made the announcement about the nationwide search for talent, but HAWK did say that this would be nothing like WWE Tough Enough or fWo Survivor. "It won't be some game show or a competition, we are looking for as many workers as possible and we won't be allowing anyone to split through our fingers if we feel we've got too many. ACW is a fed to give people chances, if you are established or if you are not. Come and give us a try, what do you have to lose?" You can enter the ACW Star Search by attending any ACW shows or you can apply in written form by going to the link below. http://acw.ewmania.com/acw2/main/fans/singlesapplication.shtml
