Dragons' thoughts

15 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Great Dragon expresses some thoughts regarding the online 'feud' between fellow fWo member Spyder and Jay Edward Sylo (with Clayton Prodigy). Ah, the miracles of the internet. A tool which allows anyone to state anything without any real need for justification, or physical confrontation prior to stating some comments, true or not. As much as I have no real reason to back up Mr. Spyder on the matter between himself and Mr. Sylo and Mr. Prodigy, I would like to assert some of my opinions and views into the matter at hand. First, Mr. Sylo, the mask [b]IS[/b] a tradition in the world of Lucha Libre. Greats in Lucha Libre, such as the legendary El Santo, Mil Mascaras, Blue Demon, Blue Panther and Dos Caras were known for not taking their masks off at any time, so long as they were in the public domain. The mask, to them, has several historical reasonings behind its' usage, going anywhere from the mask representing the transition of power into that of a God (Coe, Andrew - "La Máscara! The World of Mexican Wrestling) to merely being a matter of keeping ones' heritage and respect held within a rubber frame. For example, El Hijo de Santo has been known for suing any newsletters who showed any pictures of him or his father de-masked, as have other Luchadors in the past. As for the 'open door policy' of the fans Wrestling organization, I have this to say: Which promotion is alive today? The promotion which was 'selective' in its' nature of hiring employees, or the promotion which has an 'open door policy'? The formula of the fans Wrestling organization promotion has worked for five years, with only one negative incident that caused long term reprecussions in the history of the promotion. How many times has the PIW promotion opened and closed due to managerial issues? The fans Wrestling organization name was named as such as the promotion is built around the most critical part of running a successful promotion: serving the fans. If you fail to keep the fans happy, your product will eventually sink, and the promotion will die. Vincent K. McMahon learned this the hard way, with business decreasing as a whole over the last year (I am neglecting the spike in the asian tour mainly because the promotion never heads there, and therefore it was sold out because it was once a year type events. If McMahon was to run the promotion in asia, I am sure eventually the attendance and ratings would decrease in asia, just like in the United States and Canada.) Mr. Spyder may or may not have the 'street fighting skills' you are so seeking, Mr. Sylo, but unlike the open challenge Randy Savage laid out to Hulk Hogan on his web site (I believe...), I am willing to accept your challenge for a contest. Under what type of rules would you be interested in participating in? RINGS rules? UFC rules? PRIDE rules? Or, if you are unsure about your ability to take on someone with shootfighting training in a shootfighting contest, then what type of contest would you suggest? Also, a request for this contest: If it is to be held, I would like to assert that any winnings I potentially could make in the contest would go towards charity, as I would consider this event as an 'extra curricular' activity rather than as a paying job. Remember, Mr. Sylo: Not all big men succeed in legitimate contests. See Akira Maeda vs. Andre the Giant from 4/29/86. Akira proved that though Andre was a giant of a man, he was still human, and was able to take him down to the mat when it came to skill against strength. fans Wrestling organization
