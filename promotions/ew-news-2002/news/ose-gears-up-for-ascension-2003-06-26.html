OSE Gears up for Ascension!!

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The biggest event in the OSE is rapidly approaching. A new interdivisional title... new management... and new rivalries will all begin this coming Sunday! As the Old School Entertainment Federation shifts gears from Wrestle Online dependent to independent, its major Pay Per View: Ascension, is shaping up to be one for the record books. Coming off of its fluid transition from a strictly wresting based federation to one that houses fighters as well, Old School Entertainment appears poised for record breaking attendance records and a slew of new and excited fans and superstars to watch and participate in the event. “The Main Event,” OSW reporter Shanda Russell explained, “is a battle for the top spot of Mount St. Hell’s End. The Mountain is a steel monstrosity made up of three tiers in which wrestlers will be position on top while their fighting counterparts will battle it out within the structure itself. From within St. Hell’s End, fighters can climb through trap doors leading to the outside or wrestlers, if they so choose, can battle into the interior if they so choose.” The prize for such a battle? The newly announced Old School Championship. “The Old School Championship is the OSE’s Interdivisional Championship. Wrestlers or Fighters alike can win this championship and defend it and it has also grown to become a very coveted belt, and no one has even held it yet!” Unlike divisional titles, the OSE title is far more competitive in the sense that while many believe OSE’s storylines are scripted, no one knows who the Old School Champion will be and therefore it is anybody’s game throughout the entire match. The last person to be on the top tier after the sixty minute time limit will be marked as the winner… but will anyone survive the match itself? “Forty of our bravest and finest superstars will vie for this title.” Russell continued, “However, only one will ultimately win.” While the Main Event certainly is something to be excited about, it is hardly the sole event to look forward to. -Civil Disobedience and the Tag Team of Ronin will battle one another for the OSE Tag Titles as both have claimed to be the most legendary OSE tag team in its history. -Old School Fighting Champion Tempest of fWo fame will defend her title against “The Elegant Savior” Inphino Blitz. -OSW World Champion Boda will defend in a three way match against Timo Bolamba and Dave Gibson. -OSW veteran Ivan Stanislav and OSW President Trashcan Man will battle as well. The winner receives a starting position atop Mt. Saint Hell’s End as well as the presidency of the OSW itself! There is one catch, however, as Tempest, Ivan Stanislav’s quirky girlfriend, serves as the referee! -Fighters Lillith and Jada Hunter will battle in what looks to be a tremendous and dangerous match to prove which of the two vixens is the true “Queen of Extreme.” -Interdivisional tag action between wrestlers Scott Taylor, Teresa Tomas, Sombra Mistica, Torrance Smith, and Cassandra Chance battle against the fighting mobsters Anthony and Fatico DaVatta, Frank Scarpo, Doll and Jinx. -OSW Supergenius Alan Kriegman locks horns with resident anarchist Darkstar. -Fighter Kenyatta Jackson must battle in a best of seven series with the ninja group known as The Clan. -OSW’s United States Title is on the line as the communist duo of Politically Incorrect defend against the largest man in the OSW: Azala Zameer. -OSF up and comer Shawn Stewart dukes it out with PCW and Rush veteran Brock Newbludd. -Six Finger Cisco and Exceptional Dave Hurst battle in the OSW ring. -Fighters Profusion and John Crichton vie for the spotlight. -Old rivalry’s flare up as IOW veterans Enforcer and Shocker wrestle in tag action against The Maximators and The Solution in a three way tag team extravaganza. These above matches are merely the beginning, however. Last years Ascension winner, Rhett Locke, has returned to his roots and has granted a candid interview with Shanda Russell herself and many, many other surprises are in store for the OSE. OSE Ascension is booked to air on Pay Per View Sunday Night! Transcripts of the show can be found at www.oseentertainment.com.
