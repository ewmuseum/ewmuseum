eVolution: Lots of Post-PPV News and Notes

12 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Controversy exploded after the second annual Faith Pay Per View event for eVolution and now the future of the company is left in doubt. Still kicking or dead on arrival? Back in October of 2002 Faith became without a doubt the biggest pay per view of the year for the eVolution. It’s now November 2003 and Faith has come again with yet another explosive show that was filled with twists, turns, and a whole lot of violence as every active title was on the line and some of the Cell fighters remembered their deceased boss, Cameron Griffin, in their own not so special ways. They weren’t the only ones however that had some things to say about Cameron Griffin as Leya Forth also made her feelings known and did what she could to erase any legacy that Cameron might have had. All this and so much more happened at Faith so be sure to check it all out! Now on an unrelated note it has already been announced by Tyler Davidson that Faith will mark the beginning of a two week hiatus for eVolution that will end on November 26th with the return of Legends live from Burswood Dome in Perth, Australia. It is currently believed that the reason behind the hiatus is due to the ongoing investigation of eVolution by the United States. Rumors persist that the investigation is close to be concluding and that Tyler Davidson and eVolution might be cleared of any wrong doing and allowed to return to the United States and run shows. We will find out certainly over the course of the next couple of weeks. In addition to that shortly after Faith went off the air it was announced that there would be a number of cuts made to the eVolution roster and the casualties were officially announced today. Among those that were let go today by the company were Outblaze, Patrick Powers, Kincade, former fWo Survivor III competitor Simon Krask, and former Epic star Jason Winters. Also after all the battling that had been done between PRIME and eVolution, Shane Wethers was also officially released by the company as well for what they described as poor work performance. Former Universal Champion, Jason Jameson, is also expected to officially announce his retirement from pro wrestling as well after sustaining another concussion in the ring during his match at Faith. Brianna Washington requested and was also granted her release from the company as well several days before Faith. In her place eVolution was quick to sign Kramer Wild Wing, the former PCW star and KFC sponsor who made his official debut at Faith. With two weeks still remaining before the next show even happens there are also quite a few rumors going around concerning just what might happen or who might show up as there has been no secret to the company planning on bringing in some new faces now. eVolution fWo Survivor III
