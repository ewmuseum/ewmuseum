NEO-Pro Weekly News Wrap Up

2 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NEO-Pro, formerly NEO, is an indy fed come international powerhouse. Run by a secretive board of directors, it has a new influx of cash bringing it to a new level and new audiences it never reached before. As compiled from: http://groups.yahoo.com/group/neopro_rumblings, with credit to [name withheld] NEWS: --Major renovations are underway at the former NEO Arena. Looks like NEO-Pro plans to keep it, only renovated for their more modern and more expansive set up. Sources state major electrical work is being done inside. --Wallace Storm apparently will have nothing to do with NEO-Pro, having sold it off to pursue interests outside the wrestling industry. --Wrestlers applications and phone calls are apparently trickling in. Some rumors have stated people from Senor Cloak to Ricardo Powell from the defunct E-Pro to returns of Mike Waldrop, Derek Irvin and even Hannibal Carver. --Apparently there has already been some contact with lucha fed MMLL and US indy fed ICON --Look for a 16 man tournament to determine the first Triple Crown Champion to kick off the first tour, the finals happening at the first supercard. --With the NEO-Pro kick off in full gear, more and more wrestlers have been in contact with the company, to be part of the initial start up tour. That tour, an around the world whirlwind, has been announced! The first show will come to us from the revamped NEO-Pro Arena in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. This show will kick off the 16 man Triple Crown Tournament with all 8 first round matches. The bracketing will be decided by a random draw so you truly never know what could happen! The second show comes to us all the way from Mexico and will bring us the second round of the Triple Crown tourney, plus all the fun of the lucha libre world. Like what you ask? How about the roster of NEO-Pro being given free reign to dress up? They can dress up as other wrestlers... as perhaps previous gimmicks they have held... fun fun fun! Add in some possible (invited) MMLL action and this will be an exciting first trip to Mexico. The third show comes to us from Viking Hall in South Philadelphia, US. Why Viking Hall? Where else would be more perfect for The Sky's the Limit 3? Not only will be winner of this one night 8 man tournament get the bragging rights to winning this prestigious tournament but they will become the first ever NEO-Pro cruiserweight champion! The fourth show on this worldwide tour will be a bit different. It's an outside show... outside in the parking lot of Kawasaki Stadium in Japan! The tournament continues with the semi finals, plus expect all facets of puroresu from junior action, to stiff strong style to the insanity of a deathmatch! WAVE, Oita Pro, CHOSEN and BSPW have all been invited to take part... another great show in a line of great shows! And finally, the tournament concludes where it began with the finals being held back at the NEO-Pro Arena for NEO-Pro's first ever supercard! The tour is jam packed, indeed, but what a way to kick off a new company and truly open eyes in the wrestling world! --Japanese company WAVE has confirmed their confirmation in the Kawasaki Stadium show and will be sending a tag match, the only participant announced being Tasogare No Prince. Apparently NEO-Pro has been in talks with Bram Black and Crazy Mohammed about appearing as well. --Applicants and video tapes continue to fly in from around the world. NEO-Pro officials are quite surprised by the number of eligible cruiserweights, nearly all of them being just that. They have said they would like to find some big men to fill in the gaps. --The first supercard will be tentatively called "Stand and Deliver" --The second tour and supercard have been tentatively announced. After the NEO-Pro: Stand and Deliver world wide tour, NEO-Pro will hit the west coast United States, focused in California, culminating in "Beach Party 2003", an outdoor supercard! --Rumors for the Beach Party tour have already started to fly, focusing on cooperation with EMWC as well as new wrestling company SEX (Southern Cal Extreme). --On the EMWC front, rumors are swirling once again about the J*STAR Festival, an interfed show to be based out of Japan. NEO-Pro, of course, will be involved, NEO-Pro owners apparently having a close relationship with Chris Blue --Sources state the first tours after Stand and Deliver have a very tentative schedule. There will be: California, Japan, and Mexico tours followed by possible tours in Australia, Europe and the Southern US. Tours in non traditional markets will apparently be kept shorter as not to burn that market out. --NEO-Pro officials are said to be happy with the amount of interested wrestlers thus far. --On the flip side, officials are surprised that many former NEO wrestlers haven't approached them. Most have found other homes and NEO-Pro is happy for them with no hard feelings. They do state they aren't actively pursuing talent, taking a stance of letting them approach us. ====================== "ABOUT US" ============== NEO-Pro, formerly NEO, is an indy fed come international powerhouse. Run by a secretive board of directors, it has a new influx of cash bringing it to a new level and new audiences it never reached before. NEO-Pro will travel 'tours' encompassing the globe. Japan, Mexico, North America, Australia and more. Through these travels around the globe, they hope to promote a web of friendships throughout the wrestling world, breaking down political barriers and truly giving the fans _the_ VERY best shows possible. Cooperation with international stars and other companies will help expand the NEO-Pro viewership and exposure, bringing in more funds which in turn solidifies the NEO-Pro roster and shows, which in turn brings better shows to the greatest fans in the world. Great heavyweights, the best juniors in the world... hardcore, high flying, technical... NEO-Pro has something for everyone. NEO Pro Wrestling
