RaGE: The Revelation Commeth?

3 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was a company formed on the basis of entertainment and wrestling. What it became was something no one could have asked for. Tom Mason has come clean with an inside look at what happened behind the closed doors of Real and Graphic Entertainment. This week, he addresses the signifigance of January 9, 2004. . . Each week, beginning two weeks ago, Real and Graphic Entertainment Owner Tom Mason has scheduled press conferences to take place. Those conferences have been, of course, in effect to update the public on the condition of the company. Just back in August it was a pretty strange occurance that Real and Graphci Entertainment encountered a problem and production was halted. Now, over two months later, the Owner is revealing what went wrong. Not only that, he is letting out the post-2003 schedule out as well. Last week, Tom Mason addressed the return of Real and Graphic Entertainment in January 2004. The whole new system to the company has been revealed and some questions, as far as its disappearance, have been answered. It was this week, however, that the company made its first big business step. The name of the first wrestler to sign with the company since the August-Lockout is announced and the venue for the return show on January 9, 2004 has been announced as well. Interested parties can visit the official Rageproject.com website for the full press releases by Tom Mason. The November 2 release is his third to date. Real and Graphic Entertainment
