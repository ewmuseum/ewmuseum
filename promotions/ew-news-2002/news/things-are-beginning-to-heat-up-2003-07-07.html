Things Are Beginning To Heat Up!

7 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Desolation 4, hWo's latest event, the Dynamo became the first to capture the hWo World Heavyweight Championship by scoring a win over rookie Foxx Pirelli. An electrifying match saw appearances from hWo Owner Trevor Norman and the hot-headed Jason Chae. With a Champion being crowned so early in the hWo, what more is in store? Things are definately beginning to heat up as the company begins preparations for it's 5th event. Hard-to-the-Fucking-core Sunday, July 6th. hWo’s ‘Desolation 4’ broadcast live across the globe, giving fans perhaps the biggest shock since the companies revival. Many hard fought battles raged on and astounded but one in particular shone brighter than the rest, Two men who had brawled to a double count-out the previous week were again pitted against each other as Trevor Norman threw the hWo World Championship into the fray. It was the anticipated Foxx Pirelli vs the Dynamo number II…. Two men entered the ring. Four men finished standing in it. One man gained the most coveted prize in the hWo. More shocking than Trevor Norman booking a Title match for his fourth show was the fact that rookie, Foxx Pirelli, lost a match he virtually had in the bag. All throughout the close pin-falls and bone breaking near-submissions he had managed to gain composure. The owner himself, Trevor Norman, made an appearance to hand the man the Title which was then reversed as Jason Chae made a stunning entry and saved the night. It seemed inevitable that Foxx Pirelli was to gain the win until he was knocked down by Jason Chae and pinned by the Dynamo which left him with a mild concussion and cracked ribs. He was the real loser of the night as he ended up 0-2 with the Dynamo, 0-1 with Jason Chae and 0-2 with Trevor Norman. Some superstar he had proved himself to be. His intentions however were made clear on the shows dying moments as he confronted the victor; breaking his nose and telling Chae and Norman how he felt. He may be green but he sure as hell knows how to raise a few eyebrows, Foxx Pirelli was unable to be contacted due to rough storms in the city of Chicago but Trevor Norman however was up for a brief conversation. “He wants to call me a cynical asshole?! At least I know how to get a job done!” Other than a lot of swearing not much else was taken from the short phone conversation with the hWo owner. Still leaving the question on everyone’s minds, will there be a Foxx Pirelli vs the Dynamo number III? When asked about this all Trevor Norman could say was, “He’ll never lay eyes on that Title again! Foxx Pirelli vs Squadra Italia has been signed; it’ll be a miracle if he makes it out of the ring on his own two feet!” Rumors are that it’s for the Number One Contendership Position to the hWo World Title, but where does this leave Jason Chae standing and why is the World Champion, the Dynamo, being left high and dry?
