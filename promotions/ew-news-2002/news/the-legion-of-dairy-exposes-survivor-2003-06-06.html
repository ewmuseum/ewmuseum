The Legion of Dairy Exposes Survivor

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The disgruntled survivor duo after yet another loss to the opposing green team, are frustrated and furious. But what could they possibly have to complain about? What you don't know about fWo Survivor could probably fill a book. cHEESE an egg NOG looked exhausted. I couldn’t blame them. So I felt more obligated than usual to loosen their tongues with a little of the hard stuff at this local dive I was familiar with. So egg NOG had a bounty hunter, and ordered a white Russian for cHEESE. I told them how appropriate that seemed, and cHEESE simply shrugged. I started by asking how the contest been going for them. They looked at each other, then they looked at me. egg NOG said, “You go first.” cHEESE shook his head, “F--- no, not tonight.” egg NOG sighed, he gave me a great attempt at an optimistic smile, “Well it seemed like a good idea at the time.” He killed the bounty hunter and I asked him to continue, “Where do I begin? We’re the team that had some initial popularity outside of this contest, and we’re thrust in. We have more experience working together than any other teams. Great right?” cHEESE snorts. He’s drinking a lot more slowly than his partner, while egg NOG speaks, cHEESE stares into space, his eyes occasionally squeezing shut as he sneers, his frustration breaking through the surface. egg NOG continued, “Wrong. We were informed that as the most experienced team, we had a special responsibility. We were responsible for helping the other teams adjust.” cHEESE slams his fist on the table. After this he takes a deep breath and carefully folds his hands on the table, under a watchful eye. He’s white knuckling, so I move just a tad closer to egg NOG. egg NOG said, “After our team’s second loss we were informed that we weren’t holding our side of the teams together well enough. So we tried to get the people motivated a little more, explained some of the nuances of how things should be done, the level of trust that they need to establish with their partner, things like that.” cHEESE quietly whispered, “F---ing s---.” I leaned closer to egg NOG, nodding as he talked, trying to make the action of scooting my chair back appear to be as casual as possible. egg NOG sighed, “After Gold Team’s victory, we were given a little slack. We thought everything was fine.” cHEESE placed his hands, palms down, flat on the table. He shut his eyes. Not good. egg NOG continued, “Then the Gold Team lost again. At this point they laid it out for us pretty clearly, with no room for interpretation.” cHEESE looked up, “We didn’t cut it. The God of Wrestling and his partner didn’t cut it. We were brought into this competition so that Survivor III would have at least one `name’ in it. They told us that our purpose here was get as many of these teams into workable shape as quickly as possible. Our elimination is coming as soon as they feel that one of the `new’ teams has a strong enough popular presence so that we’re unnecessary. What you’re going to see sometime soon is us get our asses handed to us in the ring by the team they think is the bomb. Then we’ll be `voted’ off. End of story.” I stared, my jaw nearly hitting the floor, “And you’re going to tolerate that?” cHEESE reached across the table, grabbing me by the shirt, “Of course we’re going to tolerate it! It’s the eff-dubba-oh, you don’t have a choice but to tolerate it. Because I’m am NOT going back to muther----ing DAIRYTOWN to explain that I’m never going to work again!” egg NOG took his turn to snort, “Dairytown, what a crock of-“ cHEESE let me go and I fell backwards onto the floor. I heard him say, “What the hell did you just say about Dairytown, `partner’?” As I scooted backwards, the table flipped over and I could see cHEESE and egg NOG standing eye to eye, mere inches from each other. egg NOG held his hands out, “Will you just calm down?” cHEESE said, “I will not calm down, jackass! The only thing we have going good for us is this freaking gim-” egg Nog hit him with a forearm. As they started to brawl I began to crawl on my hands and knees, to the door. When I finally got to my car, I got up off the ground and looked at the flailing limbs through the bar windows. I pulled away, completely flabbergasted with this sudden revelation regarding the insidious nature of the fWo Survivor Contest, and what it had done to these two men. Shame. Shame. Shame. Dairytown fWo Survivor III
