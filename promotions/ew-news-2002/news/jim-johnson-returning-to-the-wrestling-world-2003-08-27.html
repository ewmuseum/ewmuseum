Jim Johnson Returning to the Wrestling World?

27 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Is it true? Is the former PIW owner actually looking to be a triumphant return? The rumors have been flying for a good week, and finally the rumors can be put to rest. Jim Johnson, a man best known for owning the Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling, has suddenly appeared from the dark shadows of retirement and looks to be returning to the wrestling world. Does this mean that the PIW will be opening for a fourth time in a row? No. Reports have stated that Johnson is done with carrying an ownership, but would much rather be in the industry as a manager, or a non-wrestler...just to entertain the crowd. But with Johnson's history as being one of the most controversial men in wrestling's history...not many federations will be looking to sign this man... Except one. The World Wrestling Corporation, a federation owned by Paul Levine has been highly rumored to be interested in Jim Johnson. But it's quite a strange scenario. It's a fact that Paul Levine, a former employee of Johnson (who was seemingly dumped on the streets when Johnson closed the fed back in December), joins the club of Anti-Johnson followers. Levine has stated in numerous articles that he holds a major grudge with Johnson, and thinks of him as nothing more than a power triped egomaniac. So how can Johnson and WWC somehow come to terms? Two words....[name withheld]. [name withheld], the original owner of the PIW, is one of the members of the board of directors in the World Wrestling Corporation...and has been seen meeting up with his former partner, Jim Johnson. Many could say it's just two friends meeting back up...but sources say that in these 'meetings' that many papers have been exchanged between the two. Could it be true? Could Jim Johnson actually be returning to the wrestling world? And is WWC truely interested in the prospect? And if so, is WWC ready for the hate mail to follow if Johnson is indeed signed? So many questions, with so little answers. All that is known...is that Johnson has finally been spotted. "You know you love me" - Jim Johnson, September 2002 Johnson's New Home?
