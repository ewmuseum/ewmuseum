El Spiffy! resurfacing in OSE?

17 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The former RUSH and PWC superstar has been booked in several independent shows around Chicago in recent weeks. Sources in Chicago have confirmed that former RUSH Syndicate Champion El Spiffy! has been accepting numerous bookings in the Chicago area throughout the month of August. After not being seen since the closure of the Pro Wrestling Circuit in New Jersey months ago, many had speculated that the charismatic luchador had left the sport. Those rumors have now been replaced with speculation as to his motives for working in the Second City. The answer to those questions seem obvious to many, and come in the form of Chicago-based Old School Entertainment. The consensus is that El Spiffy! is looking to impress officials from OSE, and more specifically, its wrestling branch. "We have not been contacted by El Spiffy!, nor do we intend to contact him," said OSE spokesperson Shanda Russell. "If he wants to work for Old School Wrestling, at this point his best bet is the Chicago Development Community." If the former champion is in fact looking for a contract with OSE, it's a question as to why he wouldn't have gone to the open-doors academy in the first place. It's possible that he could be trying to leapfrog the CDC and go directly into OSW. However, Russell squashed the possibility of that happening. "We're simply not hiring," she said. "Our roster is full enough as is, we've made all the hires that we're going to be making, and you can see them debut in the coming weeks. We don't have time for every former star who wants a job." It's possible this could be a coverup similar to the one the company had been putting on with Xavier Kannon. Kannon appeared on OSW television last week after a string of teases and rumors. However, if it is false, and there are no chances of El Spiffy! signing with OSW, then his presence in Chicago is more of a mystery. While his unique mic work and spot-heavy style are an instant draw to any small promotion, the quality of workers in the city and surrounding areas has been considered sub-par as of late. Many are wondering why he would suddenly demote himself to a regional touring schedule when he would have a chance at several national and semi-national organizations around the country and overseas. More news as it develops.
