Jason 'Shocker' Elliott to Return to US Shores?

15 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The former IOW and OSW star could finally be returning to his home country after an extended stay in Japan. Jason 'Shocker' Elliott has had a lot of success on US shores- he was the first-ever IOW Cruiserweight Champion, and one of the most prominent IOW North American Champions, involved in the epic feud with cousin Evan Maxim. Following a brief, but engaging, stay in OSW by Shocker and cousins Evan Maxim and Aaron Stevenson, the three men traveled to the Japanese promotion Super Japan Pro Wrestling (SJPW) that IOW had enjoyed a long partnership with. Since then, all three became major stars in SJPW's Junior Heavyweight division, as Shocker and Maxim traded wins for the title for months and both generally became among Japan's most popular gaijin. However, it seems that finally, almost a year since his last stint in a US promotion, Shocker is ready to return to US soil. Shocker jobbed the SJPW Junior Heavyweight Championship back to Evan Maxim, kicking off his third reign with the title, in the annual January Tokyo Dome show last weekend, and rumors have been abound since then that Shocker has signed a deal with a US promotion, as SJPW announced the workers for its late January/early February tour and Shocker was nowhere in sight. Those close to the worker have offered no comment so far, but since there seems to be no Japanese promotion stepping forward to having stolen Shocker from SJPW, the only logical conclusion would be that Shocker's getting ready to return to a US promotion. The question now is, which promotion? Shocker's original partner, Xias, is currently busy being a major star in the fWo, while Shocker's older cousin Enforcer is there as well. Shocker made a couple appearances in the fWo in the past year (one against Xias for the IOW World Heavyweight Title last Janaury, the other just a few months back in November, as Shocker appeared as a "crony" to Xias in jobbing to Aimz in an fWo United States Championship match), so that seems to be the biggest possibility. However, it's certainly not the only one. In any event, we'll keep you updated on who's managed to sign this very talented superstar in recent weeks.
