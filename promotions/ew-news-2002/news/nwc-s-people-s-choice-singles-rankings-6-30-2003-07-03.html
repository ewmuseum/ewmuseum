NWC's People's Choice Singles Rankings [6/30]

3 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Once again NWC: Central leads the way in our singles ratings war as NWC Champ Billy Page and North American Champ Teron Hain sit perched atop our fan ratings again this week. For complete coverage and analysis check below. Again, MAJOR props to Len this week for his outstanding work on the rankings. Hell he did ALL the hard stuff this week! Streak ----------------------------------------------------- 1. Bermuda Billy Page (1.)- NWC:Central NWC World Heavyweight Champion. Need we say more? At press time, he's in the Atlantic to face "Mr. Invincible" Jim Beattie and yes, hold your laughter. If there were any questions about the man's ability to get it done in the crunch, he emphatically answered them all by dethroning his longtime nemesis Lance Marshall a few weeks back. Now, with Civil WarGames approaching, can he continue on his torrid pace and retain his newly won crown? 2. "The Future" Teron Hain (T2)- NWC:C NWC North American Champion. Yet another NWC level from the talent heavy Central region. At press time, he's slated to defend his newly won North American crown against Sylis "Spirit" Walker on Paradise Lost. The man has lived up to his moniker and then some by simply crushing all adversaries in his path. Despite the problems he has had with Kyle Cross and just recently his estranged wife, Katrina, the man remains a serious threat to hold on to the title and bring it some much needed stability. 3. Julie Reveli (#- NWC: Pacific West Coast Heavyweight Champion. If there was any question about her being the top woman in the NWC, they've been answered after her win over Bobby Polo on Paradise Lost. Now the only question is...who will her first challenger be for her newly won title? Will it be former West Coast Heavyweight Champion "Shotgun" Daniel Smith or "Insane" Alec Spade? 4. Jake Summers (#5)- NWC: Atlantic Atlantic Heavyweight Champion. After successfully defending his title against Marc Smith on a recent episode of Maelstrom, he was pretty much screwed out of a shot at the NWC World Heavyweight Title by Bermuda Billy Page who chose Jim Beattie instead. Now, to add on to the fact that he's trying to keep his newly won title, he has to contend with trying to find the man responsible for running his son down back at Final Judgment. An incident that coincidentally put him out of action for a few months while his son recovered. The previous attempts to have this shown on the air have been stopped, once by a man with a tazer and then the other was interrupted by an ambush attack by "Illtown" Leonard Aarons. As the plot thickens in this ongoing saga, it'll be interesting to see if Jake can hold it together for the sake of his title reign and his own personal well-being. 5. Edward Lambert (#T2)- NWC:C Central Heavyweight Champion. Good news...he successfully defended his title by fending off Johnny Kapaa even though the contest was thrown out because of how violent it got. Bad news...he didn't have long to recover thanks in part to a bat wielding Devin Fox who put the champ on his face shortly thereafter. There's no question that this man is the LAST person you wanna attack with any type of blunt object. So it'll be very interesting to see how Lambert responds to Fox and what it will add up to on this upcoming edition of Center Stage. T6. Foster Nackedy (6.)- NWC: South Southern States Heavyweight Champion. If at first you don't succeed, try, try, try again. This man is living proof of it. He suffered a heartbreaking loss to "The Foundation" Tony Pride a few months ago that saw his first chance at becoming Southern States Heavyweight Champion slip through his fingers. Then, he took "Sexy" Steve Sire to the limit right before losing to him. Since then, he has spearheaded a pack of rogue faces in their quest to usurp Team BVI and at Revival, he outlasted Vin Alejandro in a classic to finally become Southern States Heavyweight Champion. A quick did you know fact about the Nackedy family and the "Domino Effect". In both versions of the Domino Effect since its format was adopted by Sigurd Von Glatz, a Nackedy in some way, shape or form has won it every time. Ian Nackedy was successful in its first run with Gildenstern as his partner as they upended The Hollywood Hitmen to become then-Southern States Tag Team Champions. This time, Foster walks away with the big gold. Oh and in Vin's defense...he was a finalist in both tourneys. The first time he had Maxwell Thatcher as his partner before seeing his quest ended at the hands of the Hitmen. This time, Foster did him in. T6. Rictor Arkane (10.)- NWC:S Confederate Memorial Champion. Alright, so he joins a rather dubious list of those who wore the North American Championship and didn't successfully defend it. So what? He's also the only man to hold a regional crown since the start of the restructure period and NOT LOSE IT. Not even for a New York minute. After fending off Luke Denton, he'll be teaming up with "Wise Guy" Wyatt Connors to take on the team of Skalpel and "Big, Mean Bastard" Mark Tower. If they're not on the same page, they could be on the wrong end of a beating that's "strictly Bidness". 8. Sylis Walker (UR)- NWC:P This young man has been John Blaze ever since AEL 5 and taking Bermuda Billy Page to the limit before losing in a Hawaiian Street Fight. While he has gone toe to toe with some of the true heavyweights within the Pacific, like "Starman" Lee Clark, you can't question this young man's determination. This week on Paradise Lost, he gets another shot at the big time as he takes on NWC North American Champion "The Future" Teron Hain. With "The Apex" Jeremy Thomas hot on his heels, can he remain focused enough to secure the win and become the region's second representative to win that title? Another factoid...the last time a Centralite came into the Pacific with the North American title...he didn't leave with it as SLJ Pimpslapped his way to the win. Will Walker be able to duplicate SLJ's feat and proclaim victory? 9. "Intense" Bobby Irish (#7)- NWC:A Even though he was recently suspended for a week because of his "antics" with Morgan during a few episodes of Maelstrom, he's still very much a viable threat to Jake's Atlantic Heavyweight Title. One man that he might have to be on the lookout for is an opponent of his this week in a tag team affair which will have Morgan as his tag team partner. He has been rolling throughout most of 2003, with only his stumbles at AEL 5 and the May 18th Maelstrom being his only setbacks...everyone knows he's only a good crowbar swing away from getting his swagger back. Will it start this week at the expense of "Illtown" Leonard Aarons and East Coast Champion Heatwave? 10. Hiro Saotome (#T20)- NWC:S The Little Asian That Could did in a major way on this past Uprising. He scored what has to be a major upset beating Southern States Heavyweight Champion Foster Nackedy in a non-title match. This week, he'll take on a rather disgruntled and extremely furious former Southern States Heavyweight Champ in Jade. Since joining the South from the Central, Hiro has been another card carrying member of the "John Blaze" club and one can only imagine it's a matter of time before he gets a shot at Southern States Heavyweight Champion Foster Nackedy...this time with the title on the line. 11. "Illtown" Leonard Aarons (#11)- NWC:A A year ago, he was simply a loud mouth writing checks his ass couldn't cash as one half of The Destruction Crew. One year later, he has turned into one of the fastest rising solo stars in the NWC. After singlehandedly ending Dravenmania in the Atlantic, he followed that performance up with an emphatic win over the enigmatic Chaos. This week, he gets his hands on a man that hasn't exactly endeared himself to Aarons since January in Bobby Irish. Aarons has been clamoring for this for quite some time and the question remains, can he beat Irish? More importantly, at the rate he's going...how long will it be before the NWC World Champion himself has to take notice? 12. Lance Marshall (#4)- NWC:P Since his stunning loss to Bermuda Billy Page, this Lion has gone quietly. But like any Lion, there's no telling when he'll strike again and one thing can be made cetain. When he does return, he's coming back with a vengeance. 13. Bobby Polo (#9)- NWC:P He suffered a major setback a few weeks ago, losing his Polo West Coast Heavyweight Title to Julie Reveli. But there's nothing to indicate that he can't bounce back from this, as his past track record as one of the most decorated men in NWC history speaks volumes for his ability to do so. The only question is...when? T14. Wyatt Connors (UR)- NWC:S "The Next Step In Intellectual Evolution" and there's very little to dispute that claim. He's a former NWC World Heavyweight Champion and an "Interim" NWC World Heavyweight Champion to boot. He's a man that took Jade to her very limit during her stint as Southern States Heavyweight Champion before the numbers game did him in. He's a vocal leader in the Rogue army and while many have questioned whether or not this leopard will truly change his spots...he has remained fairly loyal to this point in time. This week, he teams with Arkane to face the South's Bidness Inc. combination of Mark Tower and the looney as a pet coon Skalpel. Place your bets... T14. Heatwave (#14)- NWC:A East Coast Champion. A few months ago, this man entered the Atlantic with very little fanfare. All he did was powerbomb Kid Ego through a sink to become New England Champion and then had a very dominant reign with that belt before losing it in a triple threat match at Greed two months ago. Now, a few weeks ago, one week after Morgan defied the odds and became East Coast Champion...guess what he did? He dethroned her and while no dropping folks through any kitchen type appliance was involved, there's no question this man has quietly laid to rest anyone that has crossed his path since December. This week, he teams with his good friend "Illtown" Leonard Aarons to take on Morgan and "Intense' Bobby Irish. Would you want to be on the opposite side of this equation with "The Madman From Miami" and "Monarch Of Maelstrom" locking you in their crosshairs? NWC's People's Choice Rankings
