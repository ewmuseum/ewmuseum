[IWF] IWF Survivor 2003 PPV Preview

22 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Will this PPV event be the IWF's last? This sunday night, the Icon Wrestling Federation will be in the Rose Garden in Portland, Oregon to air the forty-fifth pay per view event in it's history, IWF Survivor 2003. With the recent rumors going around about the IWF's closure, this leads many to wonder whether this will be the last time we will see the Icon Wrestling Federation brand on Pay Per View. Regardless of the circumstances, we are expected to see a hell of a show. After a long and complex feud between John Mchugh and his brother, John "The Iceman" Lugo, the IWF fans have been promised a "final encounter" this sunday in Portland. John Lugo, the Owner of the Icon Wrestling Federation booked a matchup on last week's Lightning in which John Mchugh will face Lugo and his lackey, Ace in a gauntlet. Lugo also added the stipulation that John Mchugh's job would be on the line in this gauntlet. In order to keep his life in order and keep his job, John Mchugh will have to overcome outstanding odds to beat both Ace and John Lugo this sunday night. Some have called the stipulations "unfair"......but John Mchugh has clearly expressed that he is ready to tackle the challenge. All that we can do is hope that Survivor will not be the last time that we see the fan favorite, John Mchugh. The IWF's International Champion, "The Virus" Marco has been on a mission as of late to "take out" the IWF's best. So far, Marco has done a good job of keeping onto his gold but this sunday night, Marco will take on his toughest challenge to date in Jason Drake. Jason Drake being a former International Champion himself would like nothing more than to come out of the Rose Garden with the championship but Marco and his manager, Karl McKarlson will likely do anything in their power to make sure that Marco holds onto his championship. It should be a great one. This year's Survivor Pay Per View will also make history as it will be the host of the third Mick Foley Memorial Match. Unlike the first two Mick Foley Memorial Matches in the IWF's history, we will see the first ever TRIPLE THREAT Mick Foley Memorial Match. The only man to compete in both Mick Foley Memorial Matches, Mr. Rich will be making another appearance in the Mick Foley Memorial Match as he faces Joe Johnson and Miss Phoenix, the IWF Suicidal Champion and Rising Star Champion. After a long rivalry between Mr. Rich and Miss Phoenix, Mr. Rich proposed to face the double champion. However, Some people seemed to have other plans as Joe Johnson proposed a rematch after losing a match against Miss Phoenix at last month's Back 2 School Pay Per View Event in which Miss Phoenix became the IWF Rising Star Champion and the IWF Suicidal Champion. The IWF Front Office decided to solve this problem by giving BOTH Mr. Rich and Joe Johnson a shot at both titles in this Mick Foley Memorial Match. Expect some wild action in that one. The Mick Foley Memorial Match will not be the only special gimmick match to make a comeback at this sunday's Survivor Pay Per View. Also booked on last week's Lightning was a Total F**kin' Madness match in which John Daniels would take on Matty Midget. John Daniels being the originator of the Total F**kin' Madness match is expected to have an advantage in this match....but some believe that Matty Midget has a strong chance of winning this match as well due to the fact that Matty was able to defeat Daniels in their first Total F**kin' Madness match over two years ago. Will Matty be able to win again? Or will John Daniels have something to say about it this time around? This is defintely a match that you don't want to miss. Throughout the last couple of months, Chazz Mendel has established himself as a "franchise" player with his IWF World Heavyweight Championship reign. So far, Chazz has taken on and beaten every contender that has come his way. But this sunday in Portland, some people say that Chazz will be up against his greatest challenge to date...and amazingly enough, it will be against a diva, Blaise Martelle. Don't let Blaise's gender fool you because she has shown throughout her career that she can take on and beat the best of the best in the Icon Wrestling Federation. Chazz Mendel and Blaise Martelle who have built an intense rivalry against one another over the past couple of weeks will put their feud to rest as Chazz Mendel will defend his World Heavyweight Championship belt against Blaise. This meeting will be very significant in that if the rumors are true about the IWF's demise, the winner of this match may very well be the IWF's final World Heavyweight Champion. Who will it be? This weekend's Survivor Pay Per View will be a show that you will not want to miss. With exciting wrestling action and surprises at every corner, the event will make history in the Rose Garden in Portland, Oregon. I'll see you there....... Icon Wrestling Federation
