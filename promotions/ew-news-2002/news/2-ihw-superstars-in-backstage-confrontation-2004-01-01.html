2 IHW superstars in backstage confrontation

1 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
They're at it already? Causing Trouble Since '01 At around 10:00 this morning two newly hired IHW Superstars, "Prime Time" The Vine and Kyle "The Real Deal" Raines met - And oh did they meet! Kyle Raines pulled into a 'Handicap Only' parking slot just as The Vine was getting out of his car. The Vine calmly came to Raines and explained to him that the slot was for handicaped personal only. Raines didnt take to this kindly. Kyle "The Real Deal" Raines blew up at The Vine, big time - What a hot head! The Vine tried to stay calm until Raines pushed it a little to far. Raines got out of his car and the two went toe to toe, face to face. The two giants stood there until Raines pushed it one step further and shoved The Vine back into another car. The Vine continued to stay calm and told Raines never to never do that again. What came next was the 'straw which broke the camels back' so to speak - Raines pimp slapped The Vine in the face and proceeded to laugh at him - That was it for The Vine. The Vine fired back with a right hook that sent Raines to the ground, busting his lip wide open in the process. At this point, the car park was plagued by staff who came out to tear the pair apart. Raines pushed aside the staff which handled him, jumping into his car, before speeding away from the arena. Insane Hardcore Wrestling
