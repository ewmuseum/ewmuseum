RS Presents ....Evolutions

5 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Revolution Studios Presents...... They said we wouldn't last 6 months.... They said we were misfits... losers... Almost a Year later, we're still here. We've been hyped and hacked, invaded, and faded. Now... we Evolve! Revolution Studios Roleplay Network Presents the IPPV spectacular... EVOLUTION. LIVE From the Network Associates Coliseum Arena Oakland, California 9pm EDT. July 4th, 2003. ~ Events to include a special tribute to the men and women serving in the armed forces. ~ An Independence Day Fireworks Extravaganza. ~ The preliminary round of the coveted Revolutionary Championship (RS World Title) [detail withheld] Evolution will be a PPV that will be for the ages. Great matches, touching tributes, and fireworks are all to be found at the PPV. But something seems to be missing. That something is live music. That is why a local boy band from SCWF has offered to play at Evolution. Too Badd, consisting of Vladitor Pfister The Third and Chad Hennessey, want their presence to be felt at Evolution. They believe that since they cannot prove their worth by a tag team match, they can always still put on a good show. Even though Chad will not be participating in any wrestling at Evolution, Vladitor will be in the Holy Grail tourneyment. An advantage that he has is that he has not been a stranger to the wrestling world. He has been in NGW, AW, and CHZ to name a few of them. Even though he has not held a title in any of those federations, he has become well respected. So when Too Badd make their way to Evolution, what will they be doing? Will they be preforming some of their hit songs such as Tag Team of Love? Will they cover famous songs just to get some cheap pops? Or will Chad run in and save Vlad from an embarrassment during the Holy Grail tourneyment? Only time will tell what will happen when Vlad, Chad, and Evoultion will meet. So learn to love it, this isn't a fad, and if you don't like it, well TOO BADD! ***MAIN EVENT*** RS Holy Grail Survivor Series This is the ultimate match, for the ultimate prize... The Survivor Series matchup s for the RS Holy Grail. 6 Teams from the best RS Feds represent their respective rosters in a match to see just which federation is the best in RS. This match contains the VERY BEST talent that Revolution Studios has to offer, and we promise not to disappoint you. Here are the competitors: Team NGW - Ricky Blaze, Jerome Jordan, Van Heist, Matt Douglass Team CHZ - Don Rossi, Drayton Macpherson, Keyser Soze, Harry Manback Team Superior - "Greatness" Garrett Kett, Xero, AJ King, Jack Viper Team EPW - Chris Johnson, Dante Locks, Bad Joke, Robb Hardy Team IHW - Miss Lucky, Rayzor, Steve Hanger, Malakai Other Matches/Titles Defenses Include: RS Freestyle Contest RS Deadly Alliance Title Match RS Blood and Guts Title RS Phoenix Title Match Evolution will kick off live...and you dont want to miss this. RS Evolution IPPV
