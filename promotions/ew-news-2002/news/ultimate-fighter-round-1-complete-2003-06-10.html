Ultimate Fighter Round 1 Complete!

10 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
UEF's Ultimate Fighter Wraps up the first round, with shocking results! This weeks round of Ultimate Fighter came to some very very different results other than last weeks. The first match was between Xavier and Kurt Gooden. Things started off normal between the two stars, but Gooden cheated his way to vicotry with a brass knux shot to Xavier, sealing the win for him. ...or so he thought. After Kurt got the win, the UEF's commish Duke came down to the ring with something to say, but Kurt interuppted him, talking about how he never gets anything done. Moments after a masked figure came down the ring behind Gooden, nailing a weird move on him. Duke commanded Xavier to cover Kurt and forced the ref to count the match over, delcaring Xavier the winner. Things weren't over yet. SSP and Safire went at it next, in a hardcore rules match. The fighters went around the ring before exiting it and going to the backstage and all around the arena, and eventually outside to the parking lot. This is where Safire lived up to her name of hardcore, she nailed of a bulldog off of a 12 foot concrete drop off, knocking out SSP with it, allowing her to come out with the victory! Yasmin Kerr and John "The Truth" Snow went at it next, the match seemed like 'The Truth' was going to take this one over Yasmin, but 'The Truth' had other plans.Yasmin gave up some early mistakes after going after Snow's Manager, Naomi. After a few minutes of back and forth fighting, Snow picks up 'The Jury' on Yasmin and pulls off the win. 'The Truth' ran up the walkway and celebrated early, forgetting about Naomi down still ringside. Before he could even do anything to get back down there, Yasmin had already rolled out of the ring as we all saw Yasmin pull of a devestating display of power by nailing Naomi inthe head with a chairshot. The following match was between Hiddukel and newcomer Greg Stevens, in which Greg was made short work of by Hiddukel. No suprise though, as Hiddukel is considered to be one going into the final 3 for Ultimate Fighter. The biggest upset of the night was between Hardcore Legend, Chris Hardy and the extreme newcomer, Retro. Both men went at it early, with the match going back and forth. Retro hit the remixer early, but not suprisingly. Hardy manged to kick out. Later in the match, Hardy hit the 'Hardy Hit' on Retro, and it seemed to be over, but Ryan.White came down to the ring and broke up the winning three count, and before White could do anymore damage, ShadowDm ran down the ring and nailed the L5 on White. Retro got to his feet and went to attack ShadowDM, who caught him up in the 'Ficking Move' and then pulled Hardy onto Retro. It seemed that retro was going to move, but with last energy, Retro managed to put his hand over Hardy, covering him also. The ref counted both men to 3, and after judgement, declared both of them the winner of the match. Next week we will find out what Duke has to say about this along with all of the other matches that are underway next week! Stay tuned for more next week from Dex! the.UEF
