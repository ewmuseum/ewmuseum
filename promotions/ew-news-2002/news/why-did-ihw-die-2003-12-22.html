Why did IHW die?

22 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
There are numerous rumours circulating as to why IHW died way back in July this year. Enclosed are some of the most realistic and outrageous... There are numerous rumours circulating as to why IHW died way back in July this year. We've yet to catch up with Criminal himself to discuss the matters, so we have yet to confirm any of them to be true. However, a large amount of them seem to hold some truth to them. One of the most interesting theories which I have heard was IHW was shut down finally due to the 2 murders which have took place over the past 2 years - the murders of Tyler Jones and former CEO, Surge. Apparently, Criminal was suspected in plotting both murders and it has even been said that it was Criminal himself who murdered Surge back in the beginning of 2002, as means of capturing those extra shares he needed in order to regain power of IHW once again. In close contention with this rumour, the infamous mob leader from New Jersey, Chris Macavedi - a person who has had a strong beef with Criminal since he (Criminal) decided to destroy Chris's relationship with the beautiful Krissi (A former IHW diva) has also been mentioned with the death of Surge - it has even been rumoured that Criminal hired Chris Macavedi to kill Surge. Like I said previously, I have yet to catch up with Criminal, or any other member of the IHW staff to find out whether this rumour is true or not, but check back throughout the week to see any more work which I have made into this 'case' Other rumours as to why IHW went temporally belly up are as follows: [1] An illegal drug syndicate, funded directly by IHW was found out and in turn, lead to a temporary suspension of IHW. [2] A small underground, betting parlour was stumbled upon at the final show which IHW aired, which again, lead to a temporary suspension of IHW. [3] The IHW TV deal was taken away, due to mis-use of the contract - this rumour may actually hold some truth, due to the fact that IHW have returned to their original 'label' which are a lot more loose on what is acceptable for national TV. [4] Criminal put IHW on hiatus, after hearing the news that his wife, Miss Lucky was pregnant - he apparently wanted to support her throughout the entire process. We've yet to catch up with either Criminal or Miss Lucky, but this rumour seems highly ludicrous. [5] Steve Hanger finally got his way, tapping into the IHW computer systems and bankrupting the entire company. I'll keep you all informed in the matter as the story unfolds; hopefully I'll get the chance to catch up with Criminal himself sometime this week and conduct a civil interview. Until then, keep checking back for regular updates. Insane Hardcore Wrestling
