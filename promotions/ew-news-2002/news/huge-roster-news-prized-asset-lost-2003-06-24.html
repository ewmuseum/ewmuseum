Huge Roster News, Prized Asset lost?

24 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PRIME was today left rocking after the aftershocks of ReVolution III continued to shake the company. The show was explosive, but the fallout is set to de-stabilise the whole federation... The biggest news backstage is that Angello Malenko has been forced to leave PRIME due to a serious injury he sustained in his match with Alex Caine. Initial medical reports suggest that Malenko ruptured his achilles tendon, meaning that he may not work in the ring again for possibly over a year. Taun Pham sadly removed Malenko from the roster and terminated his contract with the company. The injury occured during a spot where Caine delivered a flying clothesline from off of the barricade to Malenko while they were fighting in the crowd. Malenko went down, awkwardly and his right leg buckled underneath him. Malenko was not the only wrestler injured during last nights show. It has also been confirmed that both Steve Slater and Erin Christoph with have to spend a short time out of action. Slater is thought to have possible broken his ankle during his match, while Christoph picked up multiple injuries, including a cracked rib, triggered by being Roloplexed through a standing table in his match against Tony Rolo. Christoph will remain on PRIME TV, but has been told not to compete until he is given the all clear by a Doctor. Malenko was not the only superstar to leave the promotion today however. PRIME also parted company with Simon Krask and Gabriel Ranae. Krask has failed to impress with his poor ringwork so far, while Ranae requested to be released for reasons personal to him. It's not all bad news however as Taun Pham moved quickly to replace Ranae with Scott "Marked Man" Woods. Inside sources suggest that Woods will be pushed into the 5-Star Title Scene to increase the level of competition for that belt, currently held by Mark Blaze. With plans for ReVolution IV and X-Rated now in tatters, Taun Pham has called an emergancy board meeting in an attempt to keep the company on track as it heads towards it's first PPV on July 6th. PRIME
