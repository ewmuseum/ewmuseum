Pressure Point 82 report

We gonna rock down to the 82 review,and then we'll take it higher... Pressure Point Report Date Aired (Taped): 15.11.03 (10.11.03) Number: 82 Location: Arco Arena, Sacramento Posted by Keith Scott Zimmerman Congratulations, mutants. For free--for FREE--you got a top 3 match of the year. A couple commericals, sure, a couple almost blown spots, but an awesome, awesome match that will damn sure earn it's own disc on the KSZ DVD whenever such a thing had come to pass. The first hour was a craptacular, the second hour was one of the best in television history. Enough said. Goodbye, Rashard...Duel's back! Hi, Duel! Hi, Jeff! Bye, Duel!...Reed's got a new lackey, I guess...And in a twist straight out of a UPN sitcom premise, my ex-girlfriend is now essentially in charge. Does this mean I'm turning into Ross Gellar?...Conrad and Julius--get this--still don't like each other. 1. Jason Deline beat Kenny Heckurt by knockout. Moleman 3:16 says you took four minutes from my life and I want them back. (DUD) Segment fever! CATCH IT! Coral got kicked out of the building, and Jeff got to face HardCase, thus making two members of 101 Liz has now screwed over...Halloway tried to become Nash's partner, to no avail...Something happened with Nowell and a chick who looked like SMG. Issue Six: SMG was the anti-Triple H because with the exception of possibly her mother every single woman on the show was hotter than she was. Discuss amongst yourselves...Anyway, speaking of putting people over, Levine made people turn to football. Why oh why are we letting this troglodyte speak when his ass is gone at the end of the month and he treats this place like it's Cleveland?...Halloway apparently read off of the Van Dam cue cards this week...With Liz's promotion, Leigh Landers got promoted to PR. That actually works, as Leigh sleeps 47 minutes a night and only needs her batteries changed every four months to continue functioning...HardCase & Rook traded pleasantries--real actual ones and not a euphenism for brawl. I know, I was shocked too...Nowell left his merry gang of idiots to rassle... 2. Adam Nowell beat Carson Nash. Why the hell are lowercard heels facing off vs. each other instead of faces they can go over and start making names for themselves? Hm? One guy kicked a lot, the other didn't, and Nowell hit the Gangrel Homage for the win. Poor Carson. (*½) More segments! I'm in Sabotage's head. Max, realizing his days of being a champion were going to end so soon after having two titles, cowardly attacked Pierre from behind. Joe got bumped up to lead road agent, which was nice. HardCase AGAIN failed to maim someone in a backstage discussion, this time Donnie Daze. Is this a wrestling show or the fucking Ice Capades?! 3. Bret Halloway squashed the Phoenix. Dropsault, moonsault, next. (DUD) Son of more segments! What made Sabotage talk to Jeremy Bold? SAD. Nowell beat up folks. Liz & her girl ran to the bathroom where they discussed their stamp collections and the best way to lick them. The B title match is now cage, and Max ran away like a bitch to cry about it. 4. ORIGINAL Action Champion Jeff Garvin beat Carnage Champion HardCase in a non-title hardcore match. Anyone who wants to call me on what I said in #2, a) LOWERcard heels and 2) This is happening because the boss is using this as punishment. You got some nerve. Quick but very good, including some stiffness from HC and an Original Slam onto thumbtacks. Jeff, showing surprising adeptness in the hardcore milieu, won with the Lawler Homage onto the flaming C belt. That's right: we own all four titles. DO something. Higher rating had it gone longer. (**½) 5. Brian James beat Rook Black in a no disqualification match. Any time you give me Brian James, Drunken Master, I am there, dudes. Especially when he does the Hwoarang special in the corner. Rook went after his old leg injury and figure four'd him around the post. Joe made the save but fell victim to his son's move taken from him in some sort of Shakespearean commentary on the state of the American family. Horseshoe shot to the face and Ice gourdbuster get IBJ two. Duel got rid of Joe, and Black hit the Tigerplex '85...but Brian kicked out. Stalemate? Ref interferenced out of. IBJ triedended it with the Firestarter but couldn't hold the weight. Looked like Rook was going for the inverted Iconoclasm after Brian kicked out of the Yakuza kick but instead my buddy countered and moonsaulted, barely getting the win. PPV-quality, and a damn shame I had to completely overshadow Brian minutes later. Not thatbig a shame. (****) Bride of the son of more segment: Sabotage vowed to get me. Imagine my fear. 6. I defeated Max Danger in a cage match to win the Bantam Championship and become the first man in Action history to hold both that belt and the Dyad Championship concurrently. Some may ask why the low rating. I just beat Max for the title, and didn't retire him. Per the Phillips-Malone rule of eight enacting from the Juggernaut II recap, got to deduct the point two five. The end of the second-best match in A! this year saw me counter the super brainbuster (because who else would know the counter than I? Am I right, Jeffy?) into a super markOUT for the win and the title. (*******¾) MATCH OF THE NIGHT: Would everyone who doesn't know the answer to this question please present their genitals in a timely fashion to Mr. Chainsaw? BEST SEGMENT: Duel getting DEEEEEEEESTROYED. FUNNIEST SEGMENT: To paraphrase Tracy Morgan every time I hear Levine I crack UP! WRESTLER OF THE NIGHT: See Match of the Night. WRESTLER WITH THE MOST MOMENTUM: See Wrestler of the Night. HOTTEST FUED: Now? 101 vs. Complacency. So this is what it's like to be Oklahoma. UNDERPERFORMER OF THE NIGHT: Whatever that opening "match" was. That's airtime that could've been spent on RUSSA~!, for Portman's sake! FLATTEST SEGMENT OF THE NIGHT: See funniest. LINE OF THE NIGHT: "...and NEW Bantam Champion..." 1a would be Ravencito pretty much ripping off Abby's Sting promo word-for-word except with 175% more boring and no one calling him on it. BOW BEFORE YOUR HERO PUNY MORTAL Pressure Point 82
