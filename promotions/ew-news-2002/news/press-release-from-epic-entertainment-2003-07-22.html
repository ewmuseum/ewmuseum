Press Release From Epic Entertainment

22 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Epic Entertainment Plans To Reopen Epic Wrestling GREENSBORO, North Carolina, United States -- July 13th 2003 -- In a move that shook the wrestling industry today, it was announced by the Chairman of Epic Entertainment William Morgan himself that the world renowned company would be reopening the legendary wrestling promotion, Epic Wrestling. "The staff has already fallen into their places, and we are eagerly awaiting the first Ground Zero of the rebirth as are the members of the roster we currently have. We've assembled an amazing roster. They are an outstanding group, that I must say I can't wait to see in our ring and compete. I'm quite thrilled to see what they'll be capable of against one another. Long time fans of Epic Wrestling can definitely expect to see some interesting match ups as this promotion has been known for delivering in the past." said William Morgan, Chairman of Epic Entertainment and President of Epic Wrestling. Morgan also added that the company has taken great measures to ensure the safety of Epic Wrestling by announcing that Seth Anson will not be allowed to participate in the promotion in any way, shape, or form. Seth Anson, former Epic Wrestling World Heavyweight Champion during a mere five months crippled an Epic Wrestling official, hospitalized his opponent in eight consecutive matches, killed former Epic Wrestling North American Champion in his eighth match in the promotion, and in the same night hacked website of The News Magazine and was completely banned from country of Canada after he hung Jason Winters' dead body from the rafters of his hometown area (Toronto Skydome) The News Magazine Founder, Damon Trask doubted the authenticity of Winters' death. Then with the addition of Shawn Arrows, who had a bitter history with another former Epic member, Carlos Lopez, and the addition of Onslaught, who everyone knew would have one of the most bitter wars ever with Seth Anson, Epic was forced off of television, and shut down shortly afterwards, being unable to find a television deal. When asked about what station was willing to give Epic Wrestling television deal after a stint brutally enough to make some of the most brutal wrestling promotions around look like an episode of Sesame Street, Morgan simply replied that "We (Epic Wrestling) are in talks regarding that very issue and it has been going incredibly well thus far. You may check the Epic Wrestling website at http://www.epic-wrestling.tk for any further developments." "Altough we have quite a few individuals on our roster thus far, Epic Wrestling will be giving everyone a fair opportunity. If you're interested in being a part of our roster, please send in an e-mail to our offices, and we will get in touch with you as soon as we possibly can." added VP of Talent Relations, Gary Jenkins. About Epic Entertainment:. Epic Entertainment is a major world wide conglomerate entertainment company that originated from Greensboro, North Carolina. Epic Entertainment has three major offices, located in the United States, England, and Japan. The company is deeply involved in the fields of television, movies, music, computer software, and professional wrestling. About Epic Wrestling:. Founded by Matthew McGrath in 1981 and purchased by Epic Entertainment in 2001, Epic Wrestling is the professional wrestling division of Epic Entertainment. Since it's Epic's first Ground Zero broadcast on Fox in 1983, Epic has been one of the most well recognized promotions across the globe. In the eyes of many, Epic Wrestling has remained the alpha and omega of the wrestling business today, having provided fans with some of the most thrilling matches of all time. For More Information:.
