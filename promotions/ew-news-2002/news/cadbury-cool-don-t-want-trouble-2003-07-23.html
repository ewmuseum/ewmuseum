Cadbury: Cool Don't Want Trouble

23 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DJ Cool's tag team partner and wrestling ally Jonathan Cadbury has today told reporters that DJ Cool doesn't want to continue the exchanges of verbal attacks in public because he can't stop laughing at those coming from FAW staff members and wrestlers. The 19-year old, who was picked up by Cool and added to his exclusive stable in mid-2002, stated that his compatriot, trainer and friend is sick and tired of Furiously Addictive Wrestling's comments toward the superstar. DJ Cool slammed rumours he is signing for the federation 2 days ago, saying he found them 'laughable', before FAW personnel such as Hardcore Hoefel and new president Lisa Tien struck back - Hoefel challenging him to a match, it seems, in the federation itself. But the youngster said today, on behalf of DJ Cool: "He don't want trpuble. He is sick and tired of these exhanges of words that have been happening over the last few days, because he feels his side my split with laughter should FAW carry on to respond to his claims which are intended to end any argument". He also said: "He won't be fighting Hoefel, SEVENTH Wonder Of Foam or any other FAW superstar in the near future. He has clearly wrote off a move to there and, although he is still looking for a federation good enough for his talents, it seems he has turned down several offers in the last few days from federations including AWA, CDC and DWA". It will be interesting to see where this saga goes now. Will Cool fight Hoefel on an FAW or other federations card? Will Cool still move to fWo? Will he take another break from wrestling and enjoy life in the limelight? Only time will tell, but for now he seems to be trying to end an argument that has only just begun. Primetime Central
