New Cell Champion Crowned

7 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Legends this week saw the crowning of a new Cell Champion and perhaps at the same time a new threat to Cameron Griffins vision for the future. That threat; Damon O'Halloran. When Cameron Griffin woke up on Wednesday mornnig and set off for work, never could he have imagined that by the close of what was meant to be his big night, he would have been on the receiving end of a symbolic slap in the face from his new champion. With Joshua Krowe now firmly kicked back down into the gutter, Cameron was hoping for the start of a new era on Legends; instead he was given a timely reminder that those within the Cell cared about nothing other than winning at whatever cost. During a match which swung back and forth, Damon O'Halloran gained an advantage through immoral means which left the referee of the match on his back and out for the count himself. This was Cameron Griffins big night though, sitting at ringside he had no intention of letting a big money spinner like Norman vs O'Halloran pitter out. Rising to his feet, Evolution fans cheered as they watched the boss himself attempt to get his hands dirty by seeing the match out to a conclusion under his command. Demanding the key to the cell from the timekeeper, Cameron made his way inside the ruthless structure, when in retrospect perhaps he would have been better just remaining on the outside providing the colorful commentary he had scheduled himself in for. O'Halloran was taking no chances. Somehow he had managed to enter the cell concealing a lead pipe and seconds after Camerons entrance into the cell, he made the new outright owner watch as he drove a lead pipe down the throat of Norman causing him to vomit viciously and fall unconcious. It was a sickening sight and one that left many fans covering their eyes in disgust. Even Cameron was shocked by the display, so much so that he stood almost in a trance, O'Halloran soon sorted that though bringing Cameron to his senses with a slap across the face. It was not a vicious slap, but it symbolised something on a huge scale; a total dis-regard for authority. Cameron Griffin is well known to have an ego and an obsession of power. The Griffin stratgey has always been to create an aura of authority; to have one of his mere employees slap him across the face is sure to send shockwaves right through Griffin HQ. Even after the match when Cameron tried to crown the new champion, Cameron was pushed to one side. Forgiveness now seems hard to imagine. Cameron Griffin was livid. Hell hath no fury like the Griffin scorn and if O'Halloran didn't know that before, if rumours are to be believed, he soon will.
