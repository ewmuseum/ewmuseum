THE COUNT DOWN TO RESURRCTION IS ON!

26 January 2004

Some of the matches for next Saturdays ‘Resurrection’ PPV has been announced, and as already known the DW World Championship will be up for grabs in the first ever Marathon Match between Psyko Stevo, Slider, Jason Cash and The Gillesp. Shawn Storm and Chris Boswell are set to square off in a last man standing triple cage encounter, and Casanova has been forced to battle Hardcore Harry if he wants the UK Championship, and Harry will be out for blood after his recent beating by Casanova and the ‘I Quit’ match will be one not to be missed! UK Division manager Alex Sanchez battles his employee Jamie Hell in his first DW match, and Darkhan and Dark Haze will have to slug it out for the International Championship in a singles match. In a show opener, four men will be giving it their all to make a mark on their division managers as Adam Blade, Jesse Micheals, Mike Polowy & Pyro take part in a fatal four way match! With all this and more set to go down on Saturday night, the Resurrection of Dynasty Wrestling is one PPV not to be missed! In other news the recent insights into the death of a DW official last summer has turned up a lead. Police aren't saying how, but a tip led them to a downtown London apartment building. However, they did not find the person who was living there, who has seemingly vanished. They talked to the landlord, who informed police that the man registered the apartment under the name Anthony Boyardee. A name which the police are calling a cover. However, the most interesting fact in the case, is the fact that inside the apartment, strolled across the walls, were two terms, which were written dozens of times in Italian. The first was 'UN-TRENTA UNO-ZERO QUATTRO', which translates into '01-31-04.' The second, 'L'OCCHIO PER UN OCCHIO', translates into 'Eye for an eye.' Police aren't sure what to make of this, and they are still devouring leads, in order to find exactly what is going on, and how this apartment relates in any way to DW... Although, it does seem interesting that 01-31-04 was written across the walls. As it is the date of the Dynasty Pay-Per-View, Resurrection. Coincidence? In any case, our eyes and ears will be open, as we witness what goes down on Saturday night, live, in London, England. Dynasty Wrestling
