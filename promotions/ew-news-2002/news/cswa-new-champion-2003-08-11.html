CSWA: New Champion?

11 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"The Ego Buster" Dan Ryan defeated "The Living Legend" Mark Windham at CSWA SHOWTIME in Seattle in a Match of the Year candidate. Or did he? Only "ON TIME" will tell. Months ago, Mark Windham pulled off a huge win against one of the purest wrestlers in the sport, Evan Aho, to win the CSWA World Championship, his second time holding the CSWA's top title. Last week, Windham was involved in a thirty-minute long slugfest against Dan Ryan that went throughout the building. In the end, Ryan hit the Humility Bomb, the referee started the count... one... two... and the lights went out. When the lights came up, the referee awarded the World Championship to Dan Ryan, and Windham lived up to his other nickname: "The Living Psycho," as he attacked CSWA commentators Sammy Benson and Bill Buckley. CSWA sources have said that owner Chad Merritt will appear on the upcoming edition of CSWA: ON TIME to address the World Title situation. http://cswawrestling.com/archives/2003/08/show0803
