EWNews Review: WrestleWar Tour: Dream Matches

2 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ivan Stanislav w/ Tempest VS Eli Flair w/Poison Ivy, Scott Slugger VS LLB, and Token Weed VS Semore Smoke are just a few of the interpromotional dream matches on the first ever EWNews WrestleWar Tour Show. Read the full results! Charity WrestleWar Tour Show I Toronto, Ontario Benefitting Cancer Research Fan review by Darcy Shaw of Toronto, Ontario. The wait to actually get into the arena for this show was probably the worst part of the night. Me and my friends were completely stoked to see what we thought of as a night of Wrestling Dream Matches! 1. Semore Smoke (PCW) won by DQ against Token Weed (tA) in 12:40 Smoke entered to a huge pop. One part was because of him being a large face in PCW, the other reason, he was fighting one of the most hated men in the Asylum. So when Weed's music hit, the place exploded in boos. Token Weed on a wrestling show, this was interesting. Token came in and started playing Super Punch Out against Smoke. Smoke hit a barrage of wrestling moves against Weed, and Weed fired back with some general fighting techniques. Smoke drilled Weed with a DDT and picked up a near fall. Weed was back on top of his game, went for a Pump Kick, had it caught and reversed into a T-Bone Suplex. Smoke went for the Smoke Alarm, hitting it and getting a 2 count. Weed got frustrated and put Smoke into the corner and beat him bloody with fists out of frustration. When Weed got DQ'ed he went to wrap Smoke in the ring ropes. Was a great showing for Smoke until Weed damn near lost his marbles. Overall: **3/4 Segment: Token Weed of tA going off on the crowd (4:03) Token berated the crowd, said he was probably going to die of cancer, so he was only in the ring working for himself. Told everyone to fuck off, also told everyone said he'd love to be back to make more money for his own cancer research. Then he berated the News for being a bunch of pussies, and not having a fighting show. Behind him, Semore Smoke unwrapped himself from the ropes, stood up and LEVELED Weed with the Smoke Bomb to the cheering of the crowd. 2.Scum (IOW) defended the IOW Heavyweight Championship against Tyke (21W) in 15:34 Scum did not seem all that happy to be at tonight's show, and the crowd felt it. 21w's Tyke was at the top of his game and brought it to the IOW Champion early with a show of traditional wrestling, german suplexes and a ferocious-looking DDT that had Scum looking dazed. Scum fought back and countered with his own swinging DDT and a series of chops. Hot Finish as Tyke went for his patented Superkick and NAILED it. He went for the pin, but Scum was able to get a foot up on the rope. Tyke started to celebrate, thinking he had the match won, but the ref let him know that it was merely a near-fall. Tyke then lifted the Champ up and went for a second kick, but his foot was caught and he was dumped and wrapped into "The Confessional", Scum's finishing Sharpshooter. Tyke tapped out at 15:34. That's the problem with Champions at Charity events, you can always guess the finish. Overall: *** 3.AJ Styles (NWA/TNA) defeated Great Dragon (fWo) by pinfall in 10:31. A highflying masterpiece by the two cruiserweights. The crowd was hot for fWo's Great Dragon, and rained boos down on AJ Styles as he entered the ring. A lot of high risk action which was climaxed by Styles attempting a moonsault but was caught on the top rope and super-german-suplexed for a very close near fall. Styles is lucky to be alive after that one. Great Dragon then started a technical onslaught, with submission hold after submission hold, and AJ scrambling for the ropes every time. Finish of the match was very sharp; Styles reversed a Great Dragon dragonranna attempt into the Styles Clash Facebuster for the pin. A contender for match of the night. Overall: **** 4. Aimz (fWo) defeated Lillith (OSE) by pinfall in 16:17. This was anything but a catfight as two of the industry's top female superstars took to the ring. fWo's Aimz looked a little shellshocked at first as the retired Goth Chick, Lillith, brought a hellacious pounding for her with a flurry of european uppercuts that left the fWo Superstar dazed. The match went back and forth with some surprising chain wrestling, something you don't expect from Lillith, until she got caught with an Aimz enziguri and rolled out of the ring. This is where things got messy. Lillith set up a table outside the ring, on the ramp. Lillith then rolled back into the ring and tried to hiptoss Aimz out of the ring and through tha table. The hiptoss was blacked and then Aimztried her own hiptoss out of the ring, but Lillith controlled her fall and landed on the ring apron outside the ring. Aimz then took in the highspot of the night by doing a corkscrew plancha over the top rope, grabbing Lillith in midair and DDTing her through the table outside, all in one fluid motion! A modified "Dead Aim" DDT takes the win as Aimz pinned her on the cement floor. Fantastic, unique finish. ***1/4 5. Asylum's "Legion of Dairy" defeats WWE's Legion of Doom by pinfall in 12:50 cHEESE armdrags Hawk and a slugfest soon erupts. cHEESE gets a slam and some more armdrags. Tag egg NOG who works over Hawk. Hawk knees him in the midsection and tags in Animal. Animal gets a waistlock, egg NOG reverses but Animal takes him down. Tag cHEESE. cHEESE with an armdrag but Animal takes him down and works on the arm. Animal with an armdrag but cHEESE holds on. Animal sends cHEESE into his corner and Hawk runs in. cHEESE counters a piledriver and tags in egg NOG. Dropkick to Hawk gets one and egg NOG works on the arm. Tag Animal. Animal sends him into the corner and headbutts him but misses an elbow. CLIP! egg NOG with a hammerlock- tag cHEESE who works on the arm. Animal hammers away but cHEESE gets a crossbody press for two. Hawk comes in and beats on cHEESE but misses a blind charge and cHEESE gets an armdrag. Tag egg NOG. Double dropkick to Hawk but he makes the ropes. Gutwrench suplex gets two. egg NOG works on the arm. Tag cHEESE who continues to work on the arm. Hawk elbows him and tags Animal. Animal hammers away but gets sunset flipped by cHEESE for two. Animal rams him into the turnbuckle- tag Hawk. Hawk hooks an abdominal stretch- cHEESE hiplocks out of it, gets an armdrag and tags egg NOG. egg NOG works on the arm. egg NOG with a suplex but Hawk blocks the superplex by sending him out of the ring. Animal beats on him on the outside. CLIP! Animal has egg NOG in a chinlock. egg NOG jawbreaks out of it but Hawk is tagged in and continues to work over egg NOG. egg NOG tries to fight back but gets double teamed. Shoulderblock by Animal and they cheapshot cHEESE to draw his attention. Powerslam gets two. Hawk hooks the STF. Animal comes in and drops an elbow for two. Another cover-two. Abdominal stretch by Animal. cHEESE runs in and breaks the hold. egg NOG and Animal slug it out but Animal rakes the eyes. egg NOG reverses a piledriver by Hawk but Animal is tagged back in and they continue to cut off the tag. Blind charge hits knee and egg NOG gets a lariat. HOT TAG cHEESE! Elbows and punches for everyone. Lariat to Animal, Dropkick to Hawk and he hooks a sleeper on Animal. Animal breaks but cHEESE gets an elbow. Animal stunguns him to stop his rally. Tag Hawk who piledrives him for two. Hawk with a clothesline for two and he works cHEESE over. Animal comes in and beats the snot of cHEESE. Two blind charges hit foot but Animal ducks cHEESE’s lariat. cHEESE ducks Animal’ lariat and hits an atomic drop. Hawk is tagged in and misses some elbows and here comes egg NOG. egg NOG cleans house and hits the superplex on Hawk but Animal saves. They try the Doomday Device, but cHEESE fights off the effort to pick him up. Animal tries to nail off the top rope anyway, but its sidestepped and he's sent over the top rope to the floor. Hawk gets up and walks right in the Sellout, a double Rock Bottom, from the Legion of Dairy. egg NOG rolls over for the pin. GREAT old school tag team match with tons of great spots and egg NOG playing face-in-peril to perfection. 6. EOE (FSW) defeated Razor Blade (FSW/AW) by pinfall in 15:22 The match was originally booked to be Razor Blade V.S. Terry Funk. To the laughter of the crowd, Funk came out, took the mic and said he was too old for this shit.. but knew someone else though that would love to kick Razor's ass. With that being said he announced out EOE who came out to a massive pop since the fans knew he had just recently announced his retirement and this would be his last match. Terry returned to the back as the match got underway. Razor Blade took the upper hand early in the match delivering quick 1-2 combos to EOE. EOE released a counter attack that sent the two wrestlers to the outside brawling with furious lefts and rights. Razor tripped up EOE and started strangling him with the camera cable until the ref had him break the hold in fear of disqualification. Razor then took advantage dropping EOE on his head with a Razor Drop but only got a 2 count as EOE kicked out with authority. Later, Razor threw EOE in the ropes and went for his trademark finisher the Razor Spike but EOE ducked under the grab and delivered a reverse chokeslam, followed by a big boot and a spinning powerslam. Razor Blade got up dizzily and was met by EOE's finisher the 3 Drills To Hell to gain EOE the pinfall victory over Razor Blade. The fans gave off a huge pop for EOE and even a bigger pop when Razor Blade shook EOE's hand and walked off. Most of the wrestlers came out from the back, and the fans expected a brawl, but the guys just gave EOE a standing ovation. A great sendoff into retirement for a great wrestler! Overall ***3/4 7. Action!s LLB defeated fWo's Scott Slugger by pinfall in 17:01 ROCK ON! This was the match that most poeple were paying to see. LLB is awesome. I’ve never seen Slugger outside of the main event at an fWo show last year, but WOW... these guys brought their "A" game tonight. We start off with Scott Slugger holding LLB in a chin lock. Slugger throws LLB into the corner and goes after him with punches and shoulder blocks. Slugger charges at LLB and eats his foot. LLB hits a jumping clotheline on Scott Slugger. LLB hits a swank reverse neck breaker on Slugger, LLB tries at a kick but Scott Slugger catches his foot and gives him a Dragon Screw Leg Whip. Old School offence! Slugger holds on to LLB’s leg and puts on an STF. ACtion's Reed Young comes out of the crowd, to MASSIVE heat, distracts Scott Slugger and he breaks the STF. LLB takes advantage with a DDT. Scott Slugger ducks a lariat and puts on a sort of standing chin lock. LLB escapes using a jaw breaker, and puts on a sleeper. Scott Slugger fights out of it and sends LLB off the ropes, LLB comes back with a sunset flip and Scott Slugger rolls back to escape a pin. Scott Slugger wants the Curveball but LLB kicks him off. LLB tries to pull off a Spear, the Erroneous Conclusion, but Scott dodges it. Scott Slugger whips LLB into the corner and then hits a running knee smash to the face. Scott Slugger throws LLB to the floor and impresses me by hitting him with a sharp running baseball slide dropkick. They get back in the ring and LLB hits Scott Slugger with a variation of a Rock Bottom and hits a piledriver for two. Scott Slugger gets behind LLB and hits a back drop suplex and a second one with a bridge for two. Scott Slugger attempts a vertical suplex and LLB counters with an STO. LLB climbs the ropes, but Scott Slugger hits a hard chop and climbs up to give LLB a superplex, but LLB pushes him off. Scott Slugger climbs back up again and grabs LLB for the superplex. LLB does his best to block it and both men fall off the turnbuckle. Scott Slugger picks up LLB for a vertical suplex but LLB escapes to the back and hits Scott Slugger with a German suplex for two. LLB sets up Scott Slugger for the Spear but he dodges again and counters into position for the Curveball. LLB escapes the Curveball and walks into a big lariat from Scott Slugger for two. LLB tries to give Scott Slugger a vertical suplex and Scott Slugger blocks but he puts himself into position for a powerbomb. LLB hits the move but instead of covering he sets up for the spear. Lots of speed on this one, and he hits it. Slugger sells it HUGE, and LLB hooks him for the pin. Okay the end came out of nowhere. The work was very good, something I expect from LLB, but I was surprised by Scott Slugger’s amazing ringwork. ***1/4 8. Ivan Stanislav w/ Tempest(OSE) defeated Eli Flair w/Poison Ivy (fWo) by pinfall at 19:37 The crowd was absolutely nuts for this match. They cheered everyone involved, but Tempest and Poison Ivy got the biggest pops from the mostly male audience. The match started off with a handshake, and then the war began. Forrmer fWo Hardcore Champion Eli Flair took control early in the match, taking the Russian Bear down with some insanely stiff clothelines. Ivan Stanislav is a legend in his own right, and the fans cheered him into a frenzy, as he powerbombed the hell out of Flair after a grueling set of suplexes. Outside, Poison Ivy and Tempest seemed to get into an argument, which is strange because the two have been longtime friends, even after Tempest left the fWo. It was about to turn into a brawl on the outside until Stanislav rolled out of the ring put himself between the two girls. Not letting the big Russian away that easy, Flair launched himself through the ropes to tackle the big man. Very stiff, very brutal beating on the outside. The fight went back into the ring, where the two men started a chain wrestling sequence that had Ivan Stanislav attempt Eli's "Fallen One" DDT on Flair himself! Flair reversed that into Stanislav's finisher, the Red Scare, a release vertical suplex. The 7 foot Russian Monster powered out of the move before Flair could lift him into the suplex and absolutely beheaded Eli with "The Iron Curtain", his chokeslam/clotheline combo. He collpased onto Flair for the pin and the win at 19:37. One HELL of a match. Overall **** After the bell, Ivy helped Flair up and all four involved in the main event shook hands. Even Tempest and Ivy seemed to get along again. Flair raised Stanislav's hand for the photographers to a huge pop. Damon Trask came out then, got on the mic and thanked everyone for coming out and donating to the charity.
