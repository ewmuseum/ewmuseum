Tyler Davidson and PRIME split rumor confirmed

9 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The rumor concerning Tyler Davidson walking out on PRIME has now been confirmed by Taun Pham. While over the past few weeks there has been a lot of talk about new stars being signed to the rapidly rising PRIME promotion there was also another rumor circulating that has now been verified. Former IOW and $W star Tyler Davidson has walked out on the company. Since joining PRIME, Tyler and former fellow $W star Tony Rolo had immediately got into an altercation that led to what was originally slated to be one of the top matches at PRIME’s inaugural pay per view event, X-Rated. One week before the event however saw Tyler being yanked from the match and replaced by Dominic Fuchelli in a move that apparently didn’t sit well with Tyler at all. For weeks Tyler Davidson along with his close friends, Erin Christoph, and Xero had been getting pushed in the company as the top heel stable. Problems started however when Christoph had been forced off television due to an injury suffered at the hands of Tony Rolo and it immediately hurt the image of the fledging group in the opinion of Tyler Davidson who was not happy already to begin with. Not even a day later the rumors had begun to circulate that after a talk with Taun Pham didn’t go well Tyler walked out of the office and promised that he would not be back with the company again unless they learned what they were doing quickly. After finally catching up with Taun Pham the rumors were confirmed yesterday that the company did indeed part ways with Tyler Davidson. Taun Pham issued a few passing comments on his way into PRIME HQ earlier in the day. "Tyler Davidson just didn't work out." Pham said bluntly. "He was unwilling to compromise in several areas of his character. Sometimes you just can't work with people like that and this was one of those times." With Davidson leaving PRIME, Xero may also find himself cast aside. He is was a very vocal supported of Davidson behind the scenes, but so far his overall impact in the company had been poor. Also to be noted however is that just this past Saturday night Tyler did compete on thReat’s recent pay per view event, Payback, where Tyler faced and defeated Phil Atken to capture the thReat Cruiserweight Championship. Tyler was originally scheduled to face Tony Rolo at X-Rated the night after. If more becomes available on this subject we will be sure to let everyone know about it the first chance we get. PRIME
