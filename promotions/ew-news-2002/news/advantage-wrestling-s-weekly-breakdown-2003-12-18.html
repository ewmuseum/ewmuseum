Advantage Wrestling's Weekly Breakdown!

18 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With recent happenings of Monday Insomnia and Tuesday Rage Advantage Wrestling is in a frenzy as they are closing in on their ppv "Desire". To stay in touch on what's transpiring than read along. Tuesday Rage slammed into the HSBC arena and boy were the fans in for a treat! Rage marked the in-ring return of AW Icon Brad Daddy. Brad Daddy was infamously known throughout his AW tenure as a man who was just unstoppable, holding the longest undefeated streak in AW History. Brad Daddy faced a new challenge in Chazz Irvine and Chazz took it to him, but in the end it was the man with veteranship that prevailed as Brad Daddy scored the win in this contest. Next up came the monster that showed up the night before at Monday Insomnia... a man by the name of Symon Vaughn accompanied by a man in a leather mask who controls him who calls himself The Mind Bender. I must say, I haven't seen a man this big in AW since Johnny Vain. This guy truly is a monster, and I think he proved that by destroying Joy Boxster and tearing up on T2SF.... T2SF! We're talking about our tag team champions and this one man took them to school! I have no idea whats in store with this guy or if he is even signed as an official AW wrestler but when I get the news, you'll be the first to know! Teemu McCorde faced off against Viper and Viper's enemies weren't far behind as they were both Lumberjacks... Acro and Slade took it to both men but in the final outcome we saw all four men battling it out and then the in-ring veteran that is Teemu bailed out... Nathan Slade, Viper and Acro are all set to do battle at the ppv Desire for the number one contendership to the X title... these men are all new to AW and are hungry for their chance at superstardom , it will be more than fun to see which one of these men goes beyond the limits to prevail. As for Teemu, the North American Champion is set to go at the ppv in, just now released information.... a Carribean Strap Match! ... Against the Man Ox .. Jack Duncan for the North American Championship. Jack Duncan took a fork to Teemu's head on Rage and has left him in a wounded state and I doubt he will be fully recovered for next week. I'd say the biggest happening of the night was Brad Daddy slapping the holy hell out of Sapphire. Let me repeat that again so it can process in my mind... Brad Daddy slapping the holy hell out of Sapphire.. . yeeup, that what I said... Yes, it's hard to believe but Brad Daddy sure has alot of fire underneath his belt this go' round and when she got in the way, he took her out! Now, from what I heard, Sapphire didnt take kindly to this attack and has had her talk with Razor Blade and she wants a piece of BD! So... On Desire it shows that she will be heading off against Brad Daddy to extract some much revenge! In the Main Event of the evening we saw our in-ring return of the World Champion Supernova. Supernova faced Creature's partner in crime Jack Duncan. JD and Nova battled a great fight and it almost looked as if JD might have scored a victory until things got a little out of control ... Steve Ewing stood toe to toe with The Force (Creach & JD) against Supreem Regime (Nova, Poppa, Saph, Teems) on Monday Insomnia and we had the conclusion that Ewing and The Force may be allies... however, on Rage we saw Ewing trying to deck Nova but in retrospect decking JD. If The Force and Ewing are in fact in conlusion than they might want to try and work out a few kinks in their reportiore. The hands of time are ticking as "Desire" draws nearer. Monday Insomnia Announcer & Head Promoter -Sean Levy- Advantage Wrestling
