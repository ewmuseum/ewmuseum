AW wrestler injured during indy event!

21 December 2003

Olympia, WA. - Advantage Wrestling superstar, Tomorrow Man, has re-injured his previously torn left rotator cuff. Tomorrow Man was wrestling in an independent match in Olympia, Washington when the slight tear reoccurred. Tomorrow Man, one half of the tag team A Tendency 2 Start Fires and current AW Tag Team Champion, was wrestling Dwayne Jacobs, a second year star in the independent scene. Jacobs was working T-Man a bit stiffly throughout the match-up, causing the small tear in the left rotator cuff to reoccur. Fans of Advantage Wrestling may recall Tomorrow Man being injured during May of this year while attempting a suicide dive. Tomorrow Man worked a few weeks with a slight tear in his rotator cuff and had surgery to repair the damage after Advantage Wrestling when on hiatus. Owner of Advantage Wrestling, Razor Blade, could not be reached for comment but Sean Levy, AW Talent Director, said this of the news: "Due to the injury not occurring at an AW event, Tomorrow Man will not be paid for any time missed because of the injury. This is not only company policy, but it also states it in his contract." Sources close to Tomorrow Man have stated that he will attempt to work some events leading up to the Desire pay-per-view which takes place January 3rd. After the event, Tomorrow Man may under go surgery to repair the tear once again. With the Tag Team Division being lackluster all ready, this comes as a huge blow to for Advantage Wrestling.
