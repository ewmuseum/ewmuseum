False Health Information

23 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hospital gives NGW's Damien Ryan false information on his back injury. A press conference was held today in in Philidelphia, Pennsylvania regarding NGW superstar and his injury from Death Wish. [The screen opens up on a podium with a large black backgrop behind it with the NGW logo on it. The podium is crowded with microphones from different wrestling networks, the large PTC one in the middle. There is an extremely large crowd of photographers and reporters in the room. Then a man in a suit steps up to the microphone.] Doctor: Good evening ladies and gentlemen. My name is Dr. Richard Colby of Toronto General Hospital, I am the primary athletic doctor of NGW's Damien Ryan. Now as you all know, Mr. Ryan sustained a back injury not too long ago in a match with Reggie Rivid. Shortly after the incident, Mr. Ryan was immediatly brough to the Little Rock Hospital in Arkansas, where he was evaluated and released. I have recieved Mr. Ryan's x-rays from Little Rock via mail, and I could see that they missed something extremely important. Later that day I recieved a phone call from Mr. Ryan, who had returned to his home in Toronto for some rest. He was complaining about his back. He came to Toronto General and we, ourselves, did an x-ray. The x-rays came out the same as the one's in Little Rock, and they exposed a hairline fracture on the pedicle of the L-4 vertebrae. This is what is causing the extreme back pain to Mr. Ryan. As Mr. Ryan's athletic physician it is my job to explain to you what effects this will cause, yet we are not sure how this will heal. It depends on the way Mr. Ryan's backbrace fits to his body, and how he rests. It is recommended that Mr. Ryan take ATLEAST 2 months off from wrestling. [the reporters go nuts in questions] Man: Please, please. [quiets down] Now, we have one more speaker. [The doctor leaves and another man walks to the podium, also wearing a suit] Attorney: Hello, and I am Stephen Smith Jr, attorney at law. I am Mr. Ryan's legal attorney and Mr. Ryan has chosen to press charges against Little Rock Hospital in Arkansas for the false information about his health. That note was asked to be made public. Thank you. [screen fades out] So, we now know that Damien Ryan must take off ATLEAST 2 months for rehabilitation to his back. What will this cost the NGW superstar? Nothing seems to been going good for the Canadian superstar as of late. 2 straight losses to Reggie Rivid, and now a back injury that has tossed him out of action, including this weeks pay-per-view Counter Strike.
