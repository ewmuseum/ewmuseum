The Doomsday is here!

20 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Maximum Wrestling Federation signs former UWF star to a major contract... It emerged last night that MWF President Edward Walker shook hands with former UWF wrestler Doomsday to agree a contract that will keep Doomsday in the MWF for the forseeable future. It is thought "The Deadly One" has signed a one year contract at something around the $25,000 per month mark. This makes him a respectable earner amongst the roster. Doomsday is known for his solid in-ring performances, he stands at 6'10" and is a beast in the ring. His promotional work is effective and his recent battle with Insane Wayne and Iniquity has been a major draw. The recent Dynamite match against Insane Wayne drew a large television audience, only second to Anarkist's battle with Iniquity. Elsewhere on the MWF roster there has been talk of Kidd Kidman being unhappy backstage. "The Ruthless Rookie" is yet to hold some gold in the MWF and sources close to the star claim he is desperatly unhappy. Implosion could turn Kidman's fortunes as he takes part in a six man main event with the MWF Tag Team and World Championships on the line. Implosion takes place this Sunday in Milwaukee, Wisconsin infront of a crowd of around 6,000 fans. "The Sensation of Innovation" has also been signed to the MWF. The MWF's General Manager Dominic Stoate was up early this morning to hold a press conference where he introduced Tommy Raines. Raines currently uses the Cop Killer as his finisher and will be looking to innovate the MWF with his technical style. Maximum Wrestling Federation
