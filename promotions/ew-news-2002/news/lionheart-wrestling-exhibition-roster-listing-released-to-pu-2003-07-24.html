Lionheart Wrestling Exhibition Roster Listing Released to Public

24 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Courtesy of Yuri Takayama, recently signed as Senior Media Specialist (a glorified reporter) to LWE, we now have a full listing of the members of Lionheart Wrestling Exhibition. At least, so far... Heavyweights Nathan Kray: an shootfighting Englishman Blind Rage: gargantuan member of the tag team Justice, Inc. Obake: Cousin of Blue Wolf and Blue Dragon, a powerful heavyweight of the Japanese Strong Style Tengu Tenryu: Comical-looking heavyweight with a signature high-flying style Akitoshi Kikuchi: Mixed Martial Arts fighter MUSASHI: A heavyweight in the image of a samurai KOJI Kono: The only submission specialist hired to LWE TUMA: Kono's regular tag team partner, he is a suplex specialist in the American Traditional Style Tomohiko Aida: Father of Hidehiko Aida, an accomplished Pancrase fighter Combo: Rollerblading, fun-loving heavyweight technician who seems to have skated right out of "Jet Set Radio". Unnamed Heavyweight x3: While there is a verbal agreement with these men, official contract signings are still pending. Their names will be released once we receive the paperwork. Junior Heavyweights Makoto Toyota: Native fan-favorite, easily the ace of any company he works for. Called the next Naoya Ogawa. Kotaro Tsuginori: Brother by blood to Toyota, he disgraced his family by changing his surname Sergio Bullent: Diminuitive professional bullfighter from Spain. Kramer Wild Wing: Native American wrestler, recently back in the ring after an almost year-long hiatus. Cimmerian: Deaf/mute luchador hailing from Mexico. His skill does not suffer. Juri Hyobanshi: Self-proclaimed "anti-joshi". Only about 16, but still has dedication to wrestling. Furinto Age: He was looking for work, so we hired him. Warrior: The other member of Justice, Inc., the gaijin superhero team. The Junior Heavyweight of the unit, but larger than most Juniors by Japanese standards. Fukiaki Tamaguchi: Amaresu star who now fashions himself as a mohawk-sporting punk rocker. Inventor of the Tamaguchi Suplex Driver. Kirin Mori: A kickboxing champion who has turned to the ring after a somewhat mediocre stint in K-1. Super Samurai: Though named after a samurai, he dresses more like a ninja. His high-flying attacks are a special sight, indeed. Yohji Yano: A professional weight-lifter and national judo competitor, he was trained by Yoshihiro Asai (better known as the Ultimo Dragon). Syuji Ishiwaka: Another student of Ultimo Dragon, Syuji came from a background in basketball and baseball. Hidehiko Aida: The son of Tomohiko Aida, a promising Pancrase competitor himself. Cube: Another member of Jet Set Future, the joshi is a high-flyer. Sugar: More mat-based in her attack, another joshi member of Jet Set Future. Garam: Leader of the faction Jet Set Future. Can shift seamlessly between any wrestling style. Unnamed Junior Heavyweight x1: His papers of a work visa are pending. The roster, while deep, is always open. Applications for employment, or even temporary tours, are still being reviewed and accepted. Lionheart Wrestling Exhibition
