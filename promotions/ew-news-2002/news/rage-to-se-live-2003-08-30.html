RaGe to SE-Live

30 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SE-Live Takes on RaGE; Tom Mason to Officiate Core Main Event The fine line between "sticking to your own" is crossed on September 5, 2003 as after talks between both Tom Mason and Matthew Lanier were scheduled and a meeting took place on Monday afternoon. The entity of Sports Entertainment Live(SE-Live for short) was interested in undertaking a part of Real and Graphic Entertainment production. Many are aware of Tom Mason's success with RaGE and his recent deals with Fox, which as a prior report says, have been mended. But now Matthew Lanier and SE-Live have turned to make a proposal to Mason, the only question could be "Why?" In effort to expore this topic and the reasons behind it, one must look into the Core Wrestling Main Event of "Winner Takes All" on Friday, September 5, 2003. That event will pit the reigning Legacy Champion, Fred Fusion, against the top contender and Fusion's adversary, Daniel Adams. The long waged war between these two in Core Wrestling has recently led into a tremendous stake teetering in the balance: The control of Core Wrestling. Apparently, the contracts have been signed and read that if Daniel Adams loses the match, Matthew Lanier will relinquish his power to run Core Wrestling to Eugene Fitzpatrick. Fitzpatrick is the current manager of Fred Fusion, but his history in Core Wrestling is well-known as he has been a prominent part in the building of "The British Empire." On the opposite side, if Daniel Adams is to be victorious, Fitzpatrick will forfeit his control in The British Empire and relieve himself of all his managerial positions in Core Wrestling. As one can see, the stakes are high and one thing is for certain that after Winner Takes All, someone will be packing their bags. But the question still yet to be answered is: What part does Tom Mason play in this? Simply put, he will be the acting Special Referee for this contest of such extreme importance at Winner Takes All. The 43-year-old wrestling entrepreneur has no job in Core Wrestling, as he is the Owner of Real and Graphic Entertainment, but it appears that his signing with SE-Live has opened up a potential trust from Lanier in Mason's person. While no one can prove that Lanier has signed Tom Mason to officiate the match to secure his own future, Lanier has released a statement on the addition of Tom Mason as official for the highly anticipated and staked match. "It was a decision made out of instinct as well as a sense of trust that he will do the job needed to be done," says Lanier. "There is no denying that he has a character to do whatever it takes, and at Winner Takes All, I believe it was to the utmost importance to find someone that could be trusted to do the job right. I've spoken to him in private, through the deal with Real and Graphic Entertainment to be involved with SE-Live and the opportunity for a bigger part had presented itself. I feel that if this match is going to mean as much as it does, it should be fair and it should have just the right person to make it fair. Mr. Mason has all the integrity and the attitude in order to insure that this match will be fair." At this point in time, Tom Mason has yet to publicly make a statement as to his involvement in this event. The deal with Core Wrestling and the deal with Real and Graphic Entertainment to be involved with SE-Live were just made public today and Tom Mason himself is, of course, away with RaGE as they get prepared for Unleashed's taping this Sunday in Los Angeles, California. To any extent, this new information presents an interesting turn to the main event at Winner Takes All for Core Wrestling. There could be any number of different reactions for people involved with Core over the appointing of Tom Mason as the official for the match. In just the same way, there could be different reactions from Core fans. Some could feel this is Tom Mason's way of pushing his Real and Graphic Entertainment into the productions of other promotions' shows, whereas some could perceive this as an omen of a bad twist happening at Winner Take All. But, no one can be certain at this point, as Winner Takes All is on September 5, and no one can predict the future. Core Wrestling
