AW: HUGE possible spoiler for next PPV!

18 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You gotta read this to believe what AW is rumored to have up their sleeve for the upcoming pay-per-view. Now this has not been 100% confirmed by any official in AW, however rumor has it that the PPV which was original planned to be entitled Warfare and be held in Chicago may be going with a whole other route. Some information has been received that the new name of the PPV will be Global Warfare and the reasoning behind it.... The pay-per-view will be shot in 3 different locations. This could be a huge marketing campaign for AW which is at an all time high in ticket sales and merchandise sales. When we asked Co-Owner Razor Blade to comment on this, he turned us down however one superstar who will remain anonymous was quoted as saying "I would love for the certain few of us with Japanese ties to be able to put on a good show and earn some respect, while others are respectfully gaining interest in other regions of the world." This leads us to the question of whether or not AW could possibly be trying to find a market outside the United States to be switching over to, however it is unlikely that it would permanently move out of the U.S. Some believe that the new Slap of the Mat Tournament may draw more interest in a different market due to the styles of especially japanese technicians. Other locations rumored to have been contacted for possible hosting have been: Rio de Janero - Brazil London - England Madrid - Spain Vancouver - Canada Dublin - Ireland Interesting that no United States venues are on that list and word has it that if they do decide to go through with this Global Warfare idea, the Chicago booking would be completely scrapped. More updates to come, and as always you can check the site for the latest. Advantage Wrestling
