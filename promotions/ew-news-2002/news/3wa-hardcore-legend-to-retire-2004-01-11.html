3WA: Hardcore Legend to Retire

11 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On behalf of Rancid, his family and Ex Enterprises it is with deep regrets that I read this news release for all of you gathered here today at the news conference. On behalf of Rancid, his family and Ex Enterprises it is with deep regrets that I read this news release for all of you gathered here today at the news conference. " Wrestling has been in my blood for as long as I can remember....It's all I knew how to do until I met Kimber Kenson and her daughter Charity...The two of them taught me how to love as cheesey as that may sound but it is true. I had been on a self drestructive path for a long time and continued to be for awhile even after I met Kimber. Kimber and Charity are my entire world now, they are my family. I have to do what is best for them, what is best for our children that are due in the next few months and what is best for my health. After seeing my daughter Charity sitting beside a friend whom she cares deeply for while he is on life support made me realize something...Many times, countless times I had been the one laying in the hospital bed and it had been Charity and Kimber sitting beside me.... I realized that night that I can't do it anymore, I will not put my family in that position ever again. So it is with this letter that I officially retire as a professional wrestler forever. I do not want my wife and children to be the ones to burry me because of something stupid I've done. I want to thank the fans and the people at Ex Enterprises, namely Ex himself and Marcus for all the support they have given to me and my family....Thank You very much." 3WA: WorldWide Wrestling Association
