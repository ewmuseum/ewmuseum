New DSW champ crowned

25 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The new DSW World Champs thoughts about his career and his future in Darkside Wrestling The man known to DSW fans only as Lotus is living proof that anyone can make an impact in the wrestling industry, and on the night of Darkside Wrestlings PPV Xtreme-War Games Lotus drove this point home by beating out four other men to win the DSW World HeavyWeight Championship, bruised, bloodied and run through a gauntlet of stress, pain, and even self doubt that no man should have to face Lotus savored his victory as Xtreme War Games went off the air, and the fans were behind him One Hundred Percent. "To win a World Championship is an amazing thing...but to have the support of the fans behind you every step of the way is more precious then all the gold in the world, it shows me that my fans want me to be where I am, and to express my gratitude to them I will always strive to keep them entertained, I want them to know that their loyalty means the world to me" Lotus told reporters backstage after his match. Lotus is a true rags to riches story in the DSW and the wrestling industry, coming to the United States a little over two months ago he was jobless and spoke broken english, at the time he was accepted into the DSW he owned nothing but the clothes on his back, while he waited for his contract to be processed he took up residence in a run down section of the city where the discovery of a dead body on the street the morning after a party was common place, Lotus however did not give up hope and his waiting soon paid off, DSW President Ben Lake booked him and four other rookies in a round robin match, the winner would go on to face Sean Campbell for the World Television Title the next week. Lotus shocked everyone by personally eliminating two of the four competitors and winning the match. The next week riding a wave of momentum and encouragement from the fans Lotus captured the Television Title in a hard fought Cage match, his english only slightly improved Lotus found himself thrust into the spotlight that came with being a champion, he adjusted quickly and has fit in extrordinarily well into the DSW roster since then he's been described as a Hard Worker and devoted to his job by his peers. However in winning the World Title Lotus was forced to give up his Television Title earlier in the night, the DSW Man Monster Known as Hawkeye defeated Lotus in the DSW's first ever "No Escape" Match forcing the ref (A personal friend of Lotus) to lock Lotus in the cage while Hawkeye himself climbed the ladder set up on the stage to win the match. "The program I worked with Hawkeye will go down as one of the best in my career" Lotus commented when asked about his opponent "The fans booed him but i don't think there's a person out there who'll disagree when I say he earned it, I hope we get the chance to work together again, but wherever out paths take us I wish him nothing but the best" Lotus also commented on Kensu the previous World Champ who gave up the World Title to make the five man battle royal a possibility "Giving up the title like that was gutsy" he said "Holding this prize in my hands and knowing what it represents...Makes me realize that Kensu made a sacrifice in giving this up, and I respect him for it" At DSW's next show "Intensity" Lotus will square off against Kensu in a world title match, though still several days away the match has high expectations from staff and fans alike, and there's no doubt that it will be a showdown worthy of "Main Event" status. "The fans really wanted to see us go one on one at War Games" Lotus said "But circumstance prevented that, so our showdown next week will be our chance to give the fans something they really want to see, and it will also push me to my limits" Since his victory Lotus has been resting easy but he plans on beginning a heavy training schedual before Intensity and sticking with it for quite sometime "Now that i've hit the big time" he said "I have to be in shape to stay there" Little is known about the past of Lotus and he likes to keep it that way, he was equally enigmatic about his future, when questioned about his plans he responded only with "Tempus Omnia Revelat" Translated from latin this means "Time Reveals All" Whatever time reveals for Lotus the DSW fans will be watching. I hope you will as well. -Alexander Price-
