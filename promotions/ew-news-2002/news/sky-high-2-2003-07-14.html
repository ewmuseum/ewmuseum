Sky High #2

14 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last episode, Skylar introduced himself to the column-reading EW-News subscribers. This time, he weighs in on the issue of gimmicks in wrestling, and his search for a tag team partner. Before I begin, I know that Sean Studd is hoping for a rebuttal. Here it is: I won't debate you on your feelings about me, because that's your opinion, however abrasive it may be and the jackass-ish manner in which you deliver it in a desperate cry for attention; however, you made a comment as to how you'd watch Smackdown! if you wanted to see an illegal immigrant on the microphone. Eddie Guerrero was born in El Paso, Texas (you know, that state that the President is from) and is therefore a legal citizen. He won't be expatriated anytime soon (and before you comment, the man's got to pay his bills, so working for WWE isn't an act of treason, it's a steady paycheck). Now that that's overwith, it's time for the gist of this column. First off, thanks to everyone who answered my call for a tag team partner in my new federation of eW, Inc. But, I feel that you're kind of going about this the wrong way. I can't count the number of people who have approached me asking to be a bodyguard or something that amounts to my slave. People, please, stop it. I don't know what other federations you've been working in that you have to come up with some cockamamie angle to get on-camera, but you don't have to do that in eW, Inc. Just be yourself. Of course, if it is really you to just want to be subordinate to a 6'7" blind guy, I guess I can't stop you. Still, I don't want anything to do with that angle if you present it to me. If you wish to be my partner, just say so. We'll talk, see if we can work something out. Just don't come with a legal pad of introductory angles; the call for a partner is the introductory angle. You don't have to do anything else. You're either trying to hard or not able to give up the WWE method of doing things (if your prior experience in the business is to mimic that joke of a wrestling federation). Onto other matters, going back to what I said earlier, I'm a 6'7" blind guy. Sometimes, people will ask me exactly how it was that I came to choose blindness as a gimmick. I suppose that this is more of the shameful WWE way of thinking and doing things. Blindness MUST be a gimmick, right? If the person is reasonably intelligent, after I remove my synthetic eyes and show them that my sockets are empty of any functional optical nerves or devices, they're fine. The others will wonder if I have on a fake head. That must be it, I'm wearing a freaking body suit! I'm actually an Arkwelian (might've spelled it wrong, but who the hell knows how to spell it correctly in the first place?) like from Men in Black! Oy.... But how pervasive is the notion that things need to be gimmicked? I hear of satanic guys, dark clowns things of that nature all the time. Hell, acting like a dark gimmick was the only way I was able to transfer over to the higher-paying PCW from EW in the winter of 2001/2002. The guys in Primetime Wrestling Academy were told all the time that they needed to get a gimmick. Meanwhile, you've got the Kanes and the Hurricane's and the Rent-a-Heroes; the badasses, fatasses, dumbasses... I could live comfortably for the next 50 years if I had no income; no income save for a nickel for every wrestler named "Frost" or "Spider" or "Virus", some guy from Parts Unknown or Hell, or any of the 948,932,358,761,281,963 guys who all happen to have the initials G.O.D. for a name. Cripes, can this business get any less unimaginative? People, people... if you must work for a company that requires gimmicks in order for you to get paid, then make up whatever you need. But don't expect the rest of us to go along with it. Some people's gimmicks are just... them. Don't come to me offering me to do or be a part of anything that goes against what I am. I'm a nice guy, a family man, and characteristically easy-going and unselfish. Anything that's in violation of my own moral code (not my "gimmick") won't be happening. Don't come to me with ideas for an angle we can run. I don't work like that. If you want to wrestle, I'll wrestle. All that orchestrated backstage soap opera crap is for some other person. Likewise, honestly look at yourselves and tell yourself if you like what you see. Are you happy with the isolation, ridicule, anything that has come to you because of what false gimmick you've taken upon yourselves. I doubt that so many of you grew up wanting to worship the dark lord and sacrifice people by removing their hearts while they're still alive. I really don't think that there was a boy from Dayton, Ohio who told his mom at the age of five that when he grows up, "I'm gonna be a pompous ass who has no friends and bad teeth!" If you want to be an actor, be an actor. If you want to be a wrestler, then be a wrestler. Don't combine the two, though; it only makes the sports industry and those of us who take this seriously look bad. That's it for now, kids wanna go to the park. Later. http://ewrestlinginc.com/index2.php
