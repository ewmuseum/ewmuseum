Stable Wars Result Shocks All

7 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fans of the Universal Wrestling Federation are still in shock after the supposedly joke stable won this years Stable Wars contest at the last pay-per-view. Supporters of well-known and established wrestling federation UWF are still in shock tonight after joke stable WACC Mark 4 Stroke 9 won Stable Wars despite tough opposition from stables such as that including world champion Jamie Shock. The annual contest, which sees 4-member stables compete against each other to win the trophy and a title shot at any title they want within the federation, is known to be won by legends such as Lew Spectre, Stu Dog Escobar and Deke Aurora but this year the team brought together by blackmail and money earned the right to have their names engraved onto the golden trophy. On paper the competition looked to have many solid teams which could challenge for the trophy. The UWF world champion's was one of them with his team, The Prime Alliance, consisting of himself, hall-of-famer Digital Mortality, Joshua Dunn and Ruff Ryder. Another was the Young Bloods with the two time winners able to flaunt talents such as Kenny Mercury and multi-time world champion Danny Jaye. But disaster struck The Prime Alliance when the starting scene opened to Ruff Ryder lying on the ground, clearly beaten to unconciousness, and looking unable to fight. But, as they panicked, NOGOOD demolished the luchador-filled stable Los Trios Mascada by eliminating all members instantly bar White Tiger. His efforts were not enough, though, and NOGOOD had all 4 members intact when he was eliminated in what proved to be the quickest match of the evening. Next up were to be one of the pre-tournament favourites in the Young Bloods. They faced the Corporate Hitmen who had been shocked themselves when UWF owner and mega-heel Sam Gordon announced she was to be their fourth member. However the Young Bloods were quick to dispose of Gordon and her lackeys to net a match against the winner of a match inbetween the Elements Of Destruction and the Shadow Klan. But before that match could take place the show cut backstage to Prime Alliance leader Jamie Shock walking around wondering what to do now that Ruff Ryder was out of action. He is shouting out himself, watched by a lurker who he knows is there. Just before he bangs his head of a wall, though, Shock realises the lurker is actually a formed UWF wrestler named Brad Johnson. He doesn't give him a choice wether he is to fight for them or not and so suddenly he ends up in the stable before he can acknowledge the fact himself. Meanwhile, in the matches themselves, the Elements Of Destruction dominate against the Shadow Klan despite some resistance from weak member Dangerman. Once they are all eliminated and that match is over the next match, inbetween the WACC Mark 4 Stroke 9 and possible tournament darkhorses A Fire Inside. But, as they prepare to wrestle, owner Sam Gordon comes out to the jeers of the crowd. She states whilst, as the rules state, A Fire Inside must eliminate all four Mark 4 Stroke 9 members that only one of A Fire Inside must be eliminated by their opposition. They fight and, although A Fire Inside eliminate a couple of their opponents, they lose to the disgust of watching Sam Gordon. She then reveals that wasn't how it was supposed to happen and they all argue on the way out whilst the winners celebrate. With the first round over the redemption match inbetween all of the losing stables takes place in a large bowl of instant pudding. The Shadow Klan win that and thus get to face The Prime Alliance who received a bye to the semi-finals for no apparant reason off Sam Gordon. A poisoned pyramid deathmatch then takes place for the Hardcore Championship title. Holder Sirian loses that to Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid and thus the title holder changes. The next match in the competition is then scheduled to start but we cut to a video of what happened earlier today - the comical hall-of-famers known as The Phenomenons are trying to get into the arena. They exchange insults with the guards who refuse them entry before their arch-enemies, the Local NVA Company, head past on their rice-cycles. They continue to argue with The Phenomenons before eventually one of the NVA, Duc Chu Song, tells them a back-door enterance they can go through. The hall-of-famers walk off before the NVA start to talk about attacking them and eating the Phenomenons sidekick camels. As they wave their chopsticks at the terrified camels we cut back to the arena where the match inbetween NOGOOD and WACC Mark 4 Stroke 9 is about to take place. It starts and after the WACC Mark 4 Stroke 9 earn the two member to one advantage over the stable which includes the Local NVA Company the Phenomenons suddenly come running in. They slide into the rings with canes and, with the crowd expecting them to beat up Duc Chu Song, they suddenly turn to DJ Cool and Maurice of the Mark 4 Stroke 9 and attack them! The legendary comedy characters suddenly turn heel but the Mark 4 Stroke 9 still somehow win after Phenomenons member Eric Cameron headlocks Duc Chu Song as a joke - allowing Maurice to finish and pin him for three. The Elements Of Destruction then produce another shock by booking their place in the final by beating the Young Bloods before The Prime Alliance just edge out the competitive Shadow Klan in the final semi-final match. After that Misfit steps out of the crowd and into the ring. The A Fire Inside member criticises the competition before confusing everybody and asking them to follow him but, as he exits, preparations for the final begin. The 3-way final eventually gets underway with a fast paced start seeing it fairly even early on. The first to be eliminated is world champion Jamie Shock - eliminated by Bruce Gordy's sleeper hold. Suddenly Mike Montgomery - mentally unstable and arch enemy of Bruce Gordy - steps into the ring. The two stare into each other's eyes before brawling for a while. Soon, though, Montgomery does what he has promised to do all week and performs his finisher on Bruce Gordy - twice! He eliminates him after that but, as he stands to get up, Kevin Starr of the EOD goes for the pin! Maurice of the eventual winners looks eager to interupt but as referee Jerry 'Pops' Kohler stops him Starr delivers 5 shots to Montgomery's head! The chair is discarded when Kohler turns and Montgomery is eliminated. Throughout the match The Plague, Killer Dude, Maurice and Joshua Dunn are eliminated before the tensions begin to rise. With two members of both the WACC Mark 4 Stroke 9 and Prime Alliance left to the one of the EOD it seems The Prime Alliance are going to win. Their hopes are improved by DJ Cool's failed Coolcom leaving him knocked-out and Pete The PG Tips Chimp also out of it. Both remaining members of the WACC Mark 4 Stroke 9 are thus knocked out and so, as Kevin Starr and Digital Mortality brawl in the ring, it seems only a matter of time until it is a two-stable battle inbetween the EOD and Prime Alliance. Starr then climbs further up the steps to the trophy by Super Starr Slamming the powerhouse nicknamed Digimon. Suddenly, however, he turns and is met by a standing dropkick from DJ Cool! DJ Cool then Coolcom's Mortality - his top rope finisher where he diving spears into the opponents lower parts - and despite a perfect execution he only gets the two count. He then performs the finisher again - but he lands on the mat having missed! Mortality manages to get the upper hand before crawling for the tag. As television commentators Rick and Larson start to talk about how the Prime Alliance will win Stable Wars he leaps from the ground - missing the tag and landing tangled in the ropes! Mortality tries again but, as he does, Brad Johnson knocks his head back. The Digimon covers his face as the 'Firestarter' breathes fire out into the face of his own team-mate! DJ Cool picks up the blazing Latino and, soon realising he is on fire himself now, he drops and rolls as he leaves Mortality on the mat. Cool then rolls onto the top of the hall-of-famer, and the 3 count is delivered to the smiles of Brad Johnson who is outside of the ring. After Cool stands up, no longer on fire, the only remaining Prime Alliance member Brad Johnson drags Mortality out of the ring by his legs and begins to beat him up. As he does so Kohler counts - reaching 10 and thus Johnson and the pre-tournament favourites being eliminated. This leaves Kevin Starr, DJ Cool and Pete The PG Tips Chimp in the match. Kevin Starr grins with DJ Cool out on the floor and, as DJ Cool slowly getting up, Pete The PG Tips Chimp does exactly the same and runs for Starr with his finisher - The Chimpy Chompy Crunch! He misses, though, getting Cool instead! Starr manages to down the Chimpmeister again before turning his attentions to Cool - pinning him for the 3 count! The now one-on-one decider looks even for a while until Kevin Starr gets the advantage over Pete. Suddenly, though, the soundtrack of NOGOOD member Seymour Almasy sounds and he comes running down to the ring with his sword in his hand! The RPG-freak, who lost to the WACC Mark 4 Stroke 9 in the semi-finals, steps into the ring. He looks to injure both but Starr attempts to fight him. Almasy swings his sword at Starr, almost making contact with him, but before he can again Starr delivers a perfect dropkick to his face! Starr turns - Almasy in the turnbuckle and knocked out - before his lower extremities are met by Pete's teeth! He continues to dig into his leg as Starr weakens. The commentators scream for Almasy to wake up and for the ring to be blown up but Starr slowly taps three. Pete releases the hold and the crowd cheer wildly as the WACC Mark 4 Stroke 9 win Stable Wars. He turns - and suddenly Almasy delivers his finisher! The screen faded to black with the winners confirmed. But now - days after the event - fans and wrestlers are still shocked. Loser Digital Mortality said it was a "disgrace to all past winners". However Jamie Shock announced how he had planned losing all along. He said: "I planned losing. I created a stable and managed to get Digital Mortality into it. Now he can't have a title shot as a winners award. How else would Bruce Gordy beat me with a sleeper hold?" It is believed that Pete The PG Tips Chimp and Maurice may try for the world title whereas competition star DJ Cool may opt for the Hardcore Championship. But one thing is for sure - a chimpanzee, its tag team partner, a man with mental problems and the King of Coolness have won Stable Wars.
