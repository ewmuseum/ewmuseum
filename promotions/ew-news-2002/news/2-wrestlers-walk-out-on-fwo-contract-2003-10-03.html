2 Wrestlers Walk Out on fWo Contract.

3 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
the fWo Survivor Champions no-show TV taping due to contract squabbles. They don't appear to be coming back anytime soon. Jordan Copeland and Rich Murray, better known as Jordan X Shabazz and RAM of the Uprising, no-showed Thursday’s Uprising in Raleigh, North Carolina due to a contract squabble. Jordan, 26, and his partner and confidant, Richard, 24, were set to join the rest of the fWo roster and appear on the show to do some backstage segments and then travel with the group to Charlotte, North Carolinafor the weekend’s house shows. Jordan X Shabazz reportedly earned a very good $250,000 per year as his partner earned the same due to the fact that they had won the prestigious fWo Survivor competition. The contract was for three years and despite the fact that leaving would breach their contracts, Jordan and Rich had their lawyer, Jerome Jackson, call some head fWo officials to demand more money. “Let’s face facts, the Uprising draw money,” said Jerome Jackson via phone interview from his home in Boston. “They could wrestle anywhere in the wrestling business and make money for the company. Until the fWo decides to pony up and pay them the money they deserve and earned through their hard work and winning a taxing sixteen team tournament, they are content to sit out their contract.” Head fWo officials were not immediately available for contact, but rumor has it that they will allow the Uprising to sit out their contract with full pay until they are ready to return. Apparently some officials believe that “they will get tired of sitting on their asses” and return a few months down the line. “I refuse to sell my clients career into slavery, as these ‘officials’ want me to do. We will not budge.” Jerome finished with a large smile on his face. the fWo. fWo Survivor III
