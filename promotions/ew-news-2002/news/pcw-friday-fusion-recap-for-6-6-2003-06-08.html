PCW Friday Fusion Recap For 6/6

8 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On this edition of Fusion, Violence Jack almost ruined the main event, but a masked savior came and gave hope for the future. PCW Friday Fusion Recap By Cal Seaver -To start the show, SyN gave his offer again to James Edwards regarding the astronaut-hopeful’s future, but this time Edwards accepted -Adam Stryker, on his way to his match with Stone Burns, was tormented by a strange voice that turned out to be Burns himself -Stone Burns d. Adam Stryker via pinfall after botched interference by Kael Law and Stone Burns’ Utter Woe onto Stryker -Dorn Tracer arrived and was furious about his TV Title loss last week, letting both Tessa Windsor-Green and Brianna Washingon know how he feels about them -Abner came out and addressed the crowd on his redneck point of view and what he thought about his upcoming match against Matt Jackson -Haley Hilton, Matt Jackson’s girlfriend, asked him to get rid of the problem of Silvio Rochelli and Jackson finally obliged -Dorn Tracer and Pharaoh chastised Gaz Green for his sins, calling Tessa being uppity HIS problem -James Edwards d. SyN to retain the Rising Star Title via pinfall after the Milky Way DDT -Dante Jackson got Tracy to seduce Gaz Green in an attempt to hit him where he’s weak and get the shock collar off his neck -Totally Tubular Tommy gets warned by Pharaoh and Dorn Tracer that his sins will catch up to him -Silvio Rochelli told Matt Jackson exactly how things were going to work as long as he wasn’t on payroll -A strange person in disguise screwed with Abner’s beer, a dire mistake -Abner d. Matt Jackson via pinfall after the Rednecker, which was allowed by Silvio Rochelli distracting Jackson -Tessa Windsor-Green was congratulated by Lacuna Debris and Miss Moonlight, but the celebration was interrupted when Sivasian Krikorian busted in and told Tessa his doubts about her keeping the title -Pharaoh told Dorn of his intention to take away everything important to Skylar Green, PCW World Champion -Dorn Tracer came out to the ring and called out Brianna for hurting innocent people while posing as the Druid. Washington came out, but seemed to be genuinely bamboozled by Tracer’s silver tongue. -James Edwards learned that his shuttle trip would be on the same date as Cloud Nine, which brought up a serious conflict of interests -Tessa Windsor-Green went to seek out Krikorian and he gave her answers, saying that she is a beautiful girl but won’t keep the title. He then screamed and Tessa and she fled the room -Stone Burns and Lacuna Debris ran into one another and already the blood was bad, as Burns left, fuming -Miss Moonlight d. Sivasian Krikorian via pinfall after MOONLIGHT! -Michael Chang told Missy exactly why he’d gotten a Cruiserweight Title match with her, that she wasn’t ready for SOCIETY, but he would gladly take her belt -Tracy made her attempt to seduce Gaz Green, only to have things go awry, with Gaz eventually sending her from the room and activating Dante Jackson’s shock collar once again -Dorn Tracer d. A.J Trinidad via pinfall after a furious assault with his fists -Abner decided to take a load off with a cold one, but found dreaded non-alcoholic beer, MUCH to his dismay -Michael Chang discussed his plan with SOCIETY about facing Miss Moonlight and SOCIETY asked what could go wrong with it -Silvio Rochelli came out and continued to trash Matt Jackson and fans, but Jackson came out and told him that he is now a PCW roster member, then Jackson chased him from the ring -SyN was informed that he would have to go up in space with James Edwards, if anyone was going to go up, and wasn’t happy about it -In the main event, we saw a no contest when Violence Jack attacked both Dante and Totally Tubular Tommy, then demanding a world title shot. A man in a steel mask came out and cleaned house on Jack, proclaimed he was a warrior of justice, then left. -Finally, Brianna Washington was just leaving when Dorn laid her out viciously on the pavement in the parking lot, then smiled at his carnage as he left Primetime Championship Wrestling
