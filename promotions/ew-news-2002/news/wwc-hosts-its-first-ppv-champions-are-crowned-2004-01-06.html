WWC Hosts its First PPV, Champions are Crowned.

6 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The first Pay-Per-View was a success for the WWC, but the Main Event had MSG in an uproar of anger... Last Sunday, 01/04/04, WWC (World Wrestling Corporation) hosted its very first Pay-Per-View event. On this event many feuds would be ending, and all the champions would be crowned. It was a stellar event, said many of the fans, as we saw such battles as... Extreme Title Bout - August Monday v. Mattock (Mattock via pinfall) Tag Team Title Bout - Tag Team Warfare - Devastation v. Gods of Death v. High Class (Devastation via pinfall) TV Title Bout - Cage of Carnage - KaGe v. Ehren the King (KaGe via TKO) But the world title bout had MANY spectators angry with the WWC. Of course, its common knowledge that Trey Vincent indeed secured the victory of his partner of Team EGO Sean Studd for the WWC World Title...but the way it happened had MSG chanting "RIP OFF" and throwing trash into the ring. It was a large play, in which Sean Studd would lay down for Trey Vincent in order to reform Team EGO (Trey Vincent, Greg Davis, Sean Studd). Backstage news after the event... (Taken from 'The Informer' columnist at WWC Online) So much has happened since the Road to Gold ended... - Bobby Minio, as guessed, no showed for the Pay Per View event. Rumor has it that the WWC is looking to press charges against Bobby Minio for breech of contract. It's also rumored that the WWC is attempting to get Minio back for one last show to finally face Osyrus...if he agrees, charges may be lessened or even dropped. More news to come... - As guessed, Sylo was finally sent to the EWF. It seems that Sylo's stiff work has been bothering alot of WWC wrestlers; many of them have been refusing to wrestle the superbeast. Thus far Sylo has injured; HQ Hendrix, Vien Loc and Luis Hernandez in a matter of seconds. And even at Road to Gold, Sylo had severely bruised the back of Adam Lazarus. WWC officials have sent Sylo to EWF to work out his stiffness and hopefully return to television broadcast as soon as possible. - WWC was VERY impressed with the work of Rune Winters in his debut at Road to Gold. Winters was said to be a pleasure to work with backstage, being very cooperative with WWC officials and wrestlers and actually meeting up with former PIW wrestlers he used to work with. - Rumors have been flying that August Monday may be hanging up his boots soon. It seems the former tA fighter and tFz fighter has been hiding many injuries during his WWC tenure; and after his bout with Mattock; it finally became obvious. It's said that after the match, Monday was said to be coughing blood and sent to a nearby hospital. Mattock, who received a few hard hits, is said to be fine and will be at Menace. But Monday has been speaking to WWC about retiring within the month...or so the rumors say. - Jason Sparks, newly debuted WWC superstar, has been sent to EWF. It's said Sparks wasn't feeling WWC that much and would like to train a bit more before being promoted to full roster. Sparks will be joining Sylo within the WWC ranks. - Chad Villain, WWC's surprise debuter, was said to be very disappointed in his debut. He was happy with what was to happen, but after discovering that he had injured fellow debuter Mason, he was not all to happy with himself. Mason is said to be out 4-6 weeks with a minor neck injury that has yet to be fully determined, but is assumed to be a minor 'stinger'. - Officials were VERY impressed with Jamal Wilson and Steve Muzio's performance at last nights Road to Gold Pay-Per-View event. The two were said to get a standing ovation backstage for their stellar contest. - It seems that WWC has underwent some heavy disputes with Crimsonbadboy Productions (a parent company who manages wrestlers: Ryojin Tachikawa, Steven Chambers, and Jimmy Haze). After many contractual disputes (that have not yet been disclosed) WWC decided to end their relationship and release the three members of CBB Productions. WWC wishes them the best in the future. - Its said that after Road to Gold's triple threat match, Disciplinarian's back was nagging him heavily. It's unknown if it's serious, but with the recent numerous injuries sustained in the WWC; WWC officials are looking to 'soften' the ring to prevent this infestation of injuries. Disciplinarian told officials it was nothing, but he was said to look to be in 'great discomfort' following his match. - It seems that the event was a success, but that many fans were angered by the Main Event. It seems that the trash being thrown was not looked upon as happening; and after cameras faded and cut transmission, fans began to chant 'RIP OFF!'. But as the ticket says...'card subject to change'. In the words of [name withheld], 'Thats what was written to happen, and it did; people bitch way too much sometimes'. - Greg Davis, who was recently signed to a 6 month written contract, is said to have a minor injury in his shoulder from a previous independent circuit show. Davis is to be kept from ring activity for two weeks, but will be making his in ring debut afterwards. - Bryce Ian Ghetti is said to be very angry with the way his match went; but officials disagree and stated it was a fine contest and it went over very well with the crowd. The buzz around the locker room is that WWC roster members are stating Bryce has been known to bitch whenever he has to job...its joked that Trey Vincent has been calling Bryce the new 'Steve Hayden'. (For those who don't know Steve Hayden was a former PIW superstar who complained about jobbing constantly). - Officials were impressed with the way KaGe v. Ehren the King went, as well as the Tag Team Title match. It's rumored that High Class will be pushed within the next semester. Its also stated that Ehren the King may be under going a gimmick change within the next semester to try and have his loss forgotten to continue his career correctly. That's the report thus far...a few injuries, and some small disputes, but the PPV was a success. ---- So in closing... Sent back to WWC's Developmental Community (EWF): Sylo and Jason Sparks. Debuts: Rune Winters, Jason Sparks, Mason, and Chad Villain. Releases: Ryojin Tachikawa, Jimmy Haze, Steven Chambers. Possible Release and Definite to bring to court: Bobby Minio for no showing the event. Injuries: Disciplinarian (bruised back, to return to full in-ring activity by this sunday), Mason (neck injury, to return in 4-6 weeks), Adam Lazarus (brusied shoulder, to return on this week's Menace, missing House Show Events this week).
