Pressure Point 63 Report

15 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A hell of a title match, a legend arrives, a shocking debut, and a shocking confession. This is merely a sampling of PP63 from Action. ACTION! PRESSURE POINT REPORT By: Keith Scott Zimmerman E-mail: [detail withheld] Date: 10.6.3 (taped 9.6.3.) The Big News: KroW will be changing his music to Rage Against the Machine's cover of "How I Could Just Kill A Man". Title Changes: -- Face/Heel Turns: -- Best Match: 6. Black worked Jeff over with an impressive series of kicks; Jeff came back with a chop across the face and a flying Austin press for 2. Dumped on the floor and the Original killed him dead with the MDC into the ramp. And by killed him dead I mean he came back after denting the steel. Here comes the classic comeback: Northern Lights, tornado DDT, moonsault. Wild Bomb almost snatches it for Jeff but Rook hits Psychodrama and Jeff hits the floor. Rook brings him in and gets blasted with the Roaring Elbow. Black fights off the Stalemate and the Jammer but not the Original Slam--but another mighty mighty kickout. He's got nothing left in his back after that and Jeff polishes him off with the Bad Moon Rising. And then things got INTERESTING. I chair him, here comes Darkwing DucK. We all go down except he can't finish off Jeff...because the FLYING FRENCHIE is on board! CHAIR! YOU! FACE! MOTHERFUCKER! Before we can keel them ded the lights go out and they disappear but WE GOT FRENCHIE YES WE DO WE GOT FRENCHIE HOW ABOUT YOU? Worst Match: 3. Basic establishing newcomer match; Firestarter to finish. Absurdity of the Show: No new A Champ in any way, shape, or form. Moment of the Show: FRENCHIE, bitches! Breakdown/Highlights: Our new member was set to show...Coral bothering Max about his late mother pushed him over the edge...Idolo, the new Commissioner, announced Action Man v. Woodchuck Bill in a #1 contender's match next week. I'd be horrified if I thought that'd actually hold up...Brock took a phone call...Erik Estrada made a guest apperance...Donnie Daze & Hurley Cambria got in a scuffle and then went back to the side of a milk carton for six weeks...1) Sneeze and Brock was getting the upperhand before he got distracted due to Ryder overcharging his room service fees. That evil BASTARD. Anyway, right into Blood in the Water, check please...We brought out the fourth man, Joe Black. Rest in peace, Freddie...2) Quicker sneeze that was disjointed, the Nucci kid finished off with a nice looking roundhouse. I heard Schmidt go into histrionics so I like this kid already... The Blacks sort of made up...Another LCW refugee, some luchatastic guy named Deft is coming...I tried to pass my condolences on to Max but Marlee Matlin got in the way so I'll be smacking him around later...3) See above...Shepherd confronted Ryder and then caught the beats...Fiend interrupted Sharc's quasi-reunion and will fight one of his friends...4) Duncan beat the hell out of me before five minutes before I finally got the surprising edge on the floor with a counter and Owenzuigiri. Leaping swinging DDT, Henning tribute, and Benoit tribute for near falls on my behalf. Hit the 101, too. Countered a half crab and pulled off a rana for the win. Quality three star stuff... The Dangers had a quiet moment...5) What, more sneezing? Turned hardcore quick and they did some of the usual. He was thieving Rock and hit the Big 2 only to get the Anderson spinebuster into the mess of chairs..oh, and he got stabbed too...Payne Fosters is due to arrive next week...6) See above...Duel will be wrestling Deft to officially get him on the roster...Heimdal bumped into Phillips...7) Avalon started off hot with a running handless plancha and missile dropkick for 2. Phillips' trick leg acted up and he short-armed him into a single-arm DDT and CUE THE ARMWORK. Japanese armdrags and the cross-armbreaker. Coral Britney Spears him with the bad arm and walks right into a hammerlocked Lights. Malonestrom & Bad Moon Rising (what is this, We Miss Joey night?) but KICK WHAM PERFECT HALO AND TREATMENT TIME. Before Daniel can get the title, Heimdal distracts him by showing up so of course he walks into the Ratings Spike and Golden Elbow. I guess we've got Phillips/Heimie on the docket...On this week's TV Show, KroW's a murderer, and Minio is Zone. Well, let's ignore the whole KroW should be jailed for life and ask who the face is. Grade: B-. It looks like us Bantam-level guys are goin to have to carry the slack with Max not being comfortable enough to return to work and LLB going away due to his nervous breakdown or coke binge or whatever the fuck he is. Last two matches a good sign, and Frenchie'll add to the star power in A. Show Results: 1) Sharc d. Brock Shepherd 2) Giovanni Oliver DeNucci ko. Lukas Wagner 3) Brian James d. Kayden Paulton 4) ME d. Bradley Duncan 5) {hardcore} Fiend d. The Thief 6) Jeff Garvin d. Rook Black 7) [BANTAM] Coral Avalon d. Daniel Phillips Pressure Point 63
