Tyler Nelson Confirms PCW Return

8 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Longtime Primetime Championship Wrestling CEO, Tyler Nelson, has confirmed that PCW will resume operations in December, and that he will once again be at its helm. "The rumors are true," said Nelson, from his home in Dallas. "The wrestling world will once again go Primetime on December 9th." When asked about his involvement in the promotion, Nelson was quick to respond. "This is a can't lose investment. Primetime Championship Wrestling is still a valuable commidity," said Nelson. "Video tape sales of old PCW events have increased 700% over the past six months, while live attendence at other promotions continues to decline. That is a clear indicator that the wrestling fan is hungry for the type of action and entertainment that PCW can provide." Nelson confirmed that several "longtime PCW superstars" have already been re-inked their deals with the company, as well as several new, up and coming superstars. "We can not merely rest on the reputations of our old superstars alone," said Nelson. "It's a new era in PCW, and we need to bring in fresh, young, enthusiastic talent in order to ensure the product does not stagnate. With that being said, several links to the past are only natural, to help keep the overall feel of the promotion intact. I am sure we will find the right mix of the old and the new to ensure a top quality product for our fans." Nelson declined to name any of the wrestlers inked to new deals with the promotion. He went onto to indicate that independent workers were welcomed to send videotapes of their work to the PCW Head Offices in Atlantic City, NJ for review.
