Totally Turmoil!

4 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Turmoil is returning LIVE! to the Allstate Arena, in Rosemont, IL, to kickoff 2004! Will you be there? Maybe. Can you afford to miss it? Absolutely NOT. THIS...IS...BIG... This week's Turmoil will are LIVE! from the Allstate Arena in Rosemont, IL, January 6th, 2004, at 10PM EST. Starting off the night will be a singles match featuring Irish Man Terlayne Sterling Vs. another one of his PWA opponents, Avery Vance. The winner of the match will recieve a coveted PCW contract, or, if both impress, maybe more... Critics of PCW on EW-Planet were very vocal after the last Turmoil aired, some even calling for the death of PCW. Has Duke Williams lost control of the federation, just one show into his tenure? Well, in order to prevent the bottom from falling out, these two will give it their best Which, hopefully, is enough. Recently, new roster members have quit, citing Duke's lack of experience in running the company. Will Vance be beaten down by the tough Irish Man, Terlayne Sterling, and will PWA produce the next Xavier Kannon? The second match will be Shaun Styles Vs. Cyrus Raynes . Shaun Styles is looking to make a name for himself in PCW, and indeed, the wrestling world as a whole, while Cyrus Raynes just wants his name known to begin with. An early loss this early in either of their PCW careers could be detrimental. But a win could catapult them over the top... Thirdly, we have a Handicap Match! It'll be the sensationable Jay Phoenix Vs. the mighty Afwa and the new-face, oddball, homosexual weirdo, Bungle! Ron Starr is gone, but Phoenix’s problems are far from over. Seems as if Ron Starr is still barking out orders to his stooges somehow, in getting this handicap match booked. Afwa is one angry monster, hell bent on carrying out this hit. Bungle’s favorite member of the Village People was always the Indian, and he’s got hot just thinking about Jay’s thunder stick. Can Phoenix handle the challenge, or will Starr be smiling from his cell? And will the Apache war cry send the British Teddy Bear crying home, or will Bungle, with the help of his little friend, make play cowboys and indians, and make Jay cry "who's you daddy!?" (Quite frankly, its' disgusting to think about) Tag Match: Abner and Jacques Vs. Rent-A-Hero and Skitzo A redneck, a black, a super hero, and a Schizophrenic…. No, this is not a corny joke, it’s our next match!! How can these different personalities get a long? Well, probably not very nicely. Off a fresh loss to a cheater, the raging redneck in Abner is furious, and food-fight Jacques is too. Skitzo, just off a night of pranks will team up with RAH, maybe PCW's biggest star, to see if the can't divide and conquer PCW's most unlikely team. And.... The Main Event will be none other than PCW World Champion, Skylar Green, Vs. Sebastian Toomes Vs. e2 in a three-way collision for the World Title. Skyler Green thinks he’s still the champion? Mr. Starr’s assossaties are not to happy with this decision, and will do all they can to strip the title off Green. The FIRST EVER OSW World Champion, Sebastian Toomes, is just the man, methinks says Ron Starr, to get the job done. And E2 are the Tag Team Champions, ready to prove themselves in defiance of the established order. This will be the end of Green’s reign….or will it? Also on Turmoil, we will find out who destroyed the Hall Of Fame, as a returning legend gets to the bottom of it. Will Duke keep his job ,or will Starr return? With PCW coming off of one of it’s most disorganized show, can it rebound? P-C-F'n-W!
