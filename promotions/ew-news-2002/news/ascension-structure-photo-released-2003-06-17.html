Ascension Structure Photo Released

17 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With the days ticking down and the participants announced all that was left was to reveal what the Structure known as Mt. St. Hell's End would look like this time around. OSE Presents Ascension And Change it has, although still the original shape and size, the inside of the steel structure has been filled with cages, trap doors and ladders. The image below shows a side profile of the outside and inside of the structure, but for those that want more then just a visual representation, let me explain to you a little bit more of what this monstroisty is comprised of. We'll start from the inside out and from the bottom up. Located on the bottom level is a 10 foot high, 45x45 foot square cage. Dividing that cage are several dozen cage doors and several trap doors leading to the outside, and one can not forget the array of ladders inside the cages to allow one to climb out. On top of the bottome cage is a smaller cage in width and length at 30x30 feet, but taller in height at 20 feet high. This cage is divided into four different sections all with trap doors leading up, leading down and leading out. Ladders once again are placed in each section of the cage. The final caged area on the inside of the structure is a 15x15 foot cage, that is 10 feet tall. Located in the center of this room is a trap door that leads to the top tier and several trap doors lead dwon to the middle tier, only one ladder is left in this cage with the only means being to climb it to reach the top. Now on to the outside of the structure. The first platform actually is shaped on top of the bottom cage 45x45 feet and comprised of unforgiving steel. The only way off this level is a ten foot drop off the sides or a ten foot drop through one of the trap doors. Climbing upwards is a 45 degree slanting steel climb, this portion of the structure is wrapped in a mesh cage to give something for the combatants to grip onto as they scale up to the second level. This one, much like the first rests on top of the second caged area, with trap doors on the floor leading from the inside caged area. Once again we go up the slanted side up to the very top of the summit, which is comprised of a 15x15 wrestling mat, with only a center hole cut out leading down to the top tier cage. It is this platform that after the 60 minute marathon, the lone wrestler or fighter that stands here will be crowned the first ever Old School Champion. Two Wrestlers shall start on the top teir outside, Two fighters will start on the top teir inside. Four wrestlers shall start on the middle tier outside and four fighters shall start on the middle tier inside. Finally the remining 14 wrestlers shall start on the bottom tier outside and the remaining 14 fighters shall start on the bottom tier inside the caged area. Stay Tuned next week as we get closer and closer to the event itself. See the Structure
