DWA: Gideon Nazareth Resigns as Commissioner

14 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It has been close to four months since Jordan Brannigan hired Gideon Nazareth as his Commissioner, for compensation for helping him screw over former DWA owner, Mason Stone, now his time has ended. Gideon resigned as Commissioner of the DWA in order to receive a World Title shot at Downward Spiral, which he has been attempting to get for awhile now. Jordan came to him with the offer last week, as a way to rid him of the Commissionership title. Gideon, although reluctant to give up the power of the Commissioner, resigned, because holding the World Title is something that is more important than anything else. With the title vacated upon LeStatt Knight sudden leave of absence, Gideon Nazareth will face off against the winner of a fatal fourway elimination match. The match will take place over three nights, starting on this Saturday's Surge, continuing on next week's Showdown, and finally ending at Downward Spiral itself. The fourway match itself has, what Jordan Brannigan considers the cream of the crop, and will feature former World Heavyweight Champions, Alex Grey and Typhoid Mary, and also Donnie Darko and current Television Champion, Brad Keidis. The winner will face off against Gideon Nazareth in the main event at Downward Spiral to determine who is the undisputed World Heavyweight Champion. Who will replace Gideon Nazareth is unknown, and it is being reported that Jordan doesn't even plan on hiring a new Commissioner, for fear they may try to take some of his own power away from him. However, per DWA by laws, it will be necessary for him to find a new Commissioner by the end of the year. DWA: Dragon Wrestling Association
