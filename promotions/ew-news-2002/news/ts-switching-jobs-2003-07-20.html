.: TS :. Switching Jobs

20 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After long business talks, including conversations of future business expansions, the Chairman of Twilight Slams steps down from his spot, offering his tasks to someone else of greater value. TS Breaking News The rumors have been proved true, Rob Koch has stepped down from his spot as Chairman of Twilight Slams. His compensation for this act? He gets a spot on the creative staff, helping with maintenance inside of the organization’s mainframe, but he won’t be alone. In a huge deal yesterday, Koch gave up his position to the man he had been dueling with ever since he stepped foot into his industry, Matt Robinson. Along with Robinson, another man known as Juan Martinez took the position as co-owner. Now there are two owners of the entrepreneurial organization, and Koch is watching over them with his Creative Staff co-worker, "Chris" of Shootclub. So was this a good idea by one Rob Koch? We’ll just have to wait and see, but for now Twilight Slams is dwelling on its return to the mainstream and the first LiveWire since its return will be run sooner than anyone could think, as soon as August 2! Twilight Slams
