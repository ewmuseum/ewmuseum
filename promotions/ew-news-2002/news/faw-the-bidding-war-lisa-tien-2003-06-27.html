FAW: The Bidding War - Lisa Tien

27 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lisa Tien is one part of the jigsaw that is the future of Furiously Addictive Wrestling - but why should we believe she's the right person to revive Furiously Addictive Wrestling? Read On. Hello, Wrestling fans. I’m Nick Tampakas, and I’m a close friend of Lisa Tien, I’m looking forward to giving you some brief information on the importance of Lisa Tien attaining the rights to FAW. For weeks now, FAW has been in administration due to its dire financial situation. The purse strings have been pulled tightly like never before. From wage caps of $30,000, to talents being released. You couldn’t possibly say that the administrators haven’t been doing their job properly because the evidence is there to see. You may ask, what am I talking about? Furiously Addictive Wrestling officials, althought not openly admitting that FAW has been in decline for sometime, know that it’ll take a lot more than money to revive the Furiously Addictive Wrestling we once knew and loved. I hate to sound pessimistic – but it’s the truth! Out of the two current bidders, Lisa Tien has far more experience in the wrestling industry than that of Shadow Entertainment. If anyone can turn FAW around it’s Lisa Tien. Here’s why: Lisa Tien is a gallant figure. Lisa Tien’s innovative ideas have been the cornerstone of her success at running promotions. Her laid-back but demanding approaches means she’s always open to ideas and gets instant results. Lisa Tien has a keen eye for the wrestling industry and is looking to learn from past experiences. Personally, I think Furiously Addictive Wrestling holds a wonderful challenge for Lisa. Nobody has ever questioned her potential ability to manage and organize but we’ve not seen the best of her YET. I think it’s fantastic that a talent like Tony Vigilante, who has worked under Lisa Tien in the past, has made it clear that he supports Lisa Tien’s bid to acquire the rights to the FAW brand. This tells us a lot about Lisa Tien as a man-motivator. Lisa obviously has the raw talent to attract stars and to motivate current stars and get them working to their peak levels. It’s a big task to ask of her, but we all know she can do it given time and full backing. Lisa Tien would be a fantastic asset to Furiously Addictive Wrestling and I think a lot of people should start supporting her bid over Shadow Entertainment’s. Shadow Entertainment may have knocked on the door with a sack full of money but what can they do once in charge? We really don’t know – but what we do know is that Lisa Tien can work miracles. I really do hope that Lisa Tien bags the Furiously Addictive Wrestling band very soon. If Shadow Entertainment wins the bidding war, I wish them the best of the luck and if Lisa Tien, my good friend, misses out on a good opportunity, then I’m sure she’ll bounce back. Still, I remain confident that Lisa Tien will become the New Owner of Furiously Addictive Wrestling. Just watch and wait. Furiously Addictive Wrestling
