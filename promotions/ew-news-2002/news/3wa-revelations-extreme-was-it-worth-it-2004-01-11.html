3WA: Revelations Extreme: Was it worth it?

11 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fans and critics alike have condemned 3WA after their Extreme version of the show on Monday night, after several stars were injured severely during the event. Fans and critics alike have condemned 3WA after their Extreme version of the show on Monday night, after several stars were injured severely during the event. The most serious of which was Jarred Mathews, who received a broken jaw, fractured skull, internal bleeding and possible further brain damage after a shocking blow to the head by X with a steel pipe. While officials have stated that it was not meant to hit Jarred, this has not calmed the fans who are furious at 3WA for staging such a brutal event, and Jarred is not expected to return to the ring anytime soon. Others have cheered, saying it was amazing action and will never be equalled again, but after the injuries list has been tallied, fans may be glad that is the case. Others who were hurt include Cage Kobain with a spiral fracture to his wrist and severe bruising. Charity Kenson also was treated after being hurled from the steel dome match by Risk and slamming her head, as well as injuries sustained to her ribs during the matchup. Jade Evans was treated for a cut under her chin, male fans sure to be glad that it will leave no scar and will heel quickly. Earlier in the night, Xavier Frost was treated for neck and back injuries after being hurled from the stage, and in a major blow to competition, Johnny Lexicon is reported to have seen a specialist over injuries to his lower back. Reports say these injuries are all in the early stages, and there is no word on how long people could be kept out of action. It could be months, they could be back next week, but one thing is for certain, it is a major blow to the already troubled 3WA. Van Brenner has been treated for a knee injury but officials have stated that it was not a serious injury, as was X for a sprained wrist and a contusion under his eye. Victory Dragoon, the person behind the evnt has declined to comment, but has issued a statement to the press saying that “It was the highest ever rating 3WA free to air event, and we have superseded all expectations. All 3WA stars will be given the best of care, and will receive a hefty bonus for their efforts, as while this was a night of pain and hardship, it was also a night of great prosperity, and for the greater good of 3WA as a whole.” 3WA: WorldWide Wrestling Association
