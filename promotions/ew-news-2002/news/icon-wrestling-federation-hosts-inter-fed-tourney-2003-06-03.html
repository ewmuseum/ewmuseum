Icon Wrestling Federation hosts Inter-Fed Tourney!

3 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
IWF decides to host "End of Days" Inter-Fed Tournament! Details inside.. Owner and Chairman of the Icon Wrestling Federation, John Lugo has recently made a huge announcement that the IWF will be the host of an Inter-Fed Tournament being named the "End of Days" Tournament. The tournament is scheduled to begin sometime in late June and is expected to run through the end of June and all the way through July. The invitation for this tournament is extended to all workers from promotions across the world. This Tournament will ultimately be run for fun and upon winning the title of the "End of Days" Tournament Champion, the winner will gain massive bragging rights for themselves as well as their feds. On top of all of that, the winner is also expected to recieve publicity on the IWF Web-Page as well as his or her own fan board on the IWF's Internet Message Boards. For further details, check out the IWF "End of Days" Inter-Fed Tournament Site! Wrestler Applications will open on Saturday June 7th and every wrestler in the game will be eligible to apply. This is expected to be a tournament of mass proportions so make sure not to miss out on the excitement! IWF
