Behind The Ropes Vol.2 # 1

3 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Behind the ropes returns! We need an art button ;) Hey folks, it's Sam "Da Man" Goodman, back in the UTA from my promotion tour in Japan. I'm here to kick off the new Behind The Ropes information series of all the stuff you wont find anywhere's else. If you don't like hearing about "real happenings" or spoilers of upcoming events, turn away now! Well, as it seams The Hell Razors have been dominating the UTA for the last few weeks, however now with Kevin hawk expecting a child from an unknown woman, look for him to become inactive soon. But does this mean he'll lose his title to Dark this Monday Night on Explosion? I think that Dark's chances of walking out a three time champion have risen greatly (check out EW-Betting and place your bets NOW!). Wouldn't that just send Scheme Gene over the edge? Speaking of this entire situation, the sudden impact of Kevin Hawk's situation will destroy angles and storylines heading up to Heaven or Hell. Right now there are talks of this pay per view being the first in UTA history to have NO World Title Match, but have Dark take on Scheme Gene in a loser leaves town match. That is IF Dark holds the belt at that moment in time. By then anyone, including The Spectre, Ron Hall, JT Titan, or even Klash could be champion and their storylines fold into being also World Title Matches. Wouldn't that be great? JT Titan vs. The Spectre: Barbed Wire Glass Table Last Man Standing World Title Match? WOW! Lost my breath just saying it. Word is that Michael Owens has pulled out of his one time deal contract to meet Ron Hall at Heaven or Hell. The suits are looking to place someone like CRIMSON LORD or DEVON LYNCH in his place. However, that would damper Devon's ultimate plot... Speaking of that, what's up with Devon lynch and his sick obsession with Killer Kelly? He may want to watch his back, with G-man being a full throttle UTA member and apparently the leader of The Hell Razors, he may walk straight into a bad situation himself. Tired of one man shows featuring that boring no good Jaime Sawyers? Well, in the coming months Jason Blackfront plans to fill the empty seat beside him with a new face, a little bit of evilness to counter Jaime's pureness. Well, the divorce war of Edward Peterson Sr. and Patricia Peterson is almost over. Once it's through, will Eddie make his TV return? Of course the fact that Patricia most likely will walk out of court owning 75% of the UTA puts a bit of a damper on that... Well, Matt's done it! He recently disappeared from the drug rehab clinic which he checked himself into a couple of months ago. As his court date approaches, Matt has vanished into thin air. A warrant has been issued for his arrest. Speaking of the Fury family, Look for Bryan Fury to return to UTA television. After several neck surgeries he wasn't expected to ever be able to wrestle again, but two years later and a lot of physical therapy, the signs for a mid 2004 return are hopeful. It's true, Scott Vicious, aka Crimson Lord, is now almost the highest paid wrestler in the UTA. Only being topped by the likes of The Spectre (Mid Seven Figures) and Ron Hall (Low Seven Figures), Crimson is making a very high six figure number to not only appear and wrestle in the UTA, but via his new contract he has one hundred percent creative control over himself and his manager Gaze, plus has become a creative partner in the development of angles and storylines. Could this mean a gothic future for the UTA? We've already seen caskets and druids since his entrance into the creative team, is this only the beginning of "Darker Days" for the UTA? The suits are reportedly very happy with Aaron Marty and Stone Cold Thug's return to the UTA. You may be shocked at this, but look at their track record. These two guys have been in the ring with some of the biggest names to ever step into the UTA ring. Yeah, they may lose 99.9% of the time, but they get paid very good to. Randy Sanchez has been working the live events and training grounds as a main eventer as of late. yeah, less Explosion time seams to be bad, but the fan base he has acquired is quite outstanding. Predictions at his popularity when he does return full time to UTA television is that he'll shoot from mid carder to a main eventer in 2-3 months. Jason Blackfront is in the works of bringing a new UTA title to the picture, an original unnamed as of now title. look for it to arrive mid 2004. Speaking of Blackfront, he has recently brought in almost a new refereeing team. It seams too many disputes over pay has forced him to have a changing of the zebra, which is fine by this man here. The UTA official website may have been only the first block to a whole UTA image makeover in 2004. Look for the NEW UTA to start showing it's colors in the coming weeks as new logos, stage sets, rings, and ever referee colors may go into effect. Well, that's it for this issue of Behind The Ropes, catch me next time as I hope to get an interview with an UTA superstar! Good bye! United Toughness Alliance
