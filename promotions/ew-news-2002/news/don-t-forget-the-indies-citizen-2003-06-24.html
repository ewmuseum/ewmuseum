Don't Forget The Indies, Citizen

24 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo, Action!, OSE, and The Asylum get more then their fair share of hype....but what about the little guys? “I’ve never seen an arm injury quite like this….you’d have to work like a madman to make a comeback from this.”- Dr. Dean Harper, Medical Specialist at Miami Medical College 7 and 1/2 hours of surgery, a steel rod, and some much needed fan support later…and here I am citizens…back and fighting the sanctimonious scoundrels in the world of wrestling. But it wasn’t an easy road back. However, I’m glad I didn’t take the easy road back. What’s this “easy road” earthlings? You know, that one where your name recognition alone will land you in ANY fed you want…even if your attitude or skill isn’t enough to back it up. Let me use the speed of the Flash to try to get to what they call “the point” if you will. I never thought I’d have to go job hunting again. I always thought I’d live PCW and die PCW…until I got that FedEx saying that with budgets tight, my services were no longer required. That was a hit Rent-Heads….the kind of hit that would make the Hulk cringe in his non-existent boots. It took quite a while for me to come to grips with the fact that one arm submission at a pay-per-view cost me my career, but I had high hopes that my options were open and I had what it took to make it elsewhere….you know, you’re Action! Wrestling, fWo, Asylum, etc. All those feds you can’t help but see when you pick up your monthly wrestling mag….hopefully while making your comic book rounds my fellow Rent-Heads. –wink- How wrong I was. I could barely even get the numbers to schedule DARK MATCHES with the “big name” companies. If you weren’t established, you could bet your spandex you weren’t getting a shot soon. HOLY UNKNOWN NINJA’S, some of these companies didn’t even have a clue who I was?! ( I guess EVERYONE’S running around in full body spandex costumes and fighting crime while still collecting a decent paycheck.) With recovery time on the way and my options fleeting…I turned to what I completely looked over and what many fans these days seem to be looking over as well….a place where I reclaimed my love for this business…. The Indies. By the hammer of Thor, it seems like reporters and fans alike are so busy discussing the latest politics backstage in the fWo, or the never-ending flood of violence in Asylum, and even the next big OSE pay-per-view….but they forget about where the future will come from. The future comes from the lesser known. Don’t pretend you’ve been brain-washed by Zurgulon mindsweepers! I’m talking about the federations stuck on the graveyard TV slot of your triple-digit channels… The independents who can only promise $10 to their main eventers…on a good night… The journeymen, and lately journeywomen, wrestlers taking any booking they can just for the love of the game…and getting paid in experience…. The promoter’s with a dollar and a dream, knowing that success is slim in this game with federations closing down all the time, but having the belief THEY will be that next fWo…. The independent shows where stars from all the big name feds meet, facing off in interpromotional battles that their respective brass would never dare try to bring to the fans… The federations that could care less how much you say “they rock” just as long as you keep watching…. And the federations where “advertising” is word of mouth, tape trading, and shoddy 30 second commercials before late night infomercials. I didn’t care that I wasn’t tied down to a fat 6 figure contract. I could care less about the long drives from booking to booking. It didn’t matter if I was facing a fresh-faced, barely legal youth or an over-the-hill old schooler with that fire you see in 16 year olds. Every single match and angle in their presence reminded me of why I fell in love with this buisness. It was so pure. Like it or not…it will be these fledgling federations and these unknowns who will one day grace your TV screen. The men you’ll cheer and boo for while complaining about their card status or politicking. For every Aimz, there’s a PMS, a Karina Wolfenden, Gunner Addison, or Brianna Washington. For every Villiam Ender, there’s a J. Leslie Voss, Ryuji Ishikawa, or Enigma For every LLB, there’s a Matt Jackson, Paul Duffy, or Tristen Belmont FOR EVERY FWO, ASYLUM, OSE, OR ACTION!....there is a WWC, LOW, The X, American Wrestling X, FAW, IOW, SbW, Steel City Wrestling, Dynasty Pro, and about a bajillion other feds with X somewhere in them… My point is, so much hype and time is spent on what we already know is big. Don’t be afraid to put that money down for that OSE ticket and see an indy show sometime. No glass ceilings, full of surprises, and guys who’d rather work for the sake of working….and not for the sake of justifying their salary. Even now with my employment with thReat, I refuse to quit supporting and following the many independents scattered throughout our great country of ours and internationally. Stay up and catch a fledging fed late at night. Go see a local indy Saturday. Check out the fed vault on this very site and click on the feds you don’t know. Learn the names of the faces you see and follow them…. When the future comes, you’ll know what his, her, or the promotion’s name is. And if you forgot why you love wrestling lately….you may find it for just $10 at a high school gym. See you SAME RENT-A-TIME…SAME RENT-A-CHANNEL!! Rent-A-Hero can be seen as a regular on thReat Wrestling’s Sunday Flaw. He is a veteran of PCW, PURE, and various other indies in the wrestling world. thReat
