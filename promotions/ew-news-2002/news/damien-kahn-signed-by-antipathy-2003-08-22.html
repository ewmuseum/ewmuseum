Damien Kahn Signed By Antipathy

22 August 2003

The string of good luck nonetheless continued late Wednesday night, when after his third tryout with Antipathy, Damien Kahn finally signed a contract with Antipathy. It was early last month, that Damien Kahn first attended a tryout with Antipathy but fell short when he was forced to leave the tryouts and suffering a severely sprained ankle. he wouldn't get another chance of the end of last month when officials decided to give Kahn another shot with Antipathy. After putting a great show in front of the trainers of Antipathy, the string of bad luck continued to haunt Damien Kahn. After showing his power and athleticism and performing a spectacular move, Kahn reinjured that ankle and was forced once again to leave tryouts early. But the good luck would set right back in. With the slump of the roster for Antipathy in the shape it is is now, Antipathy decided to give Kahn one final shot to make the talent roster. This time, the third time was the charm as Kahn attended this tryout and gave the trainers and officials something to talk about. "Damien Kahn's in-ring performance was remarkable. It has been a long time since I have seen somebody with this much talent. We wasted no time in getting this man signed to a contract after he completed his first tryout." One of the officials stated in a press conference after seeing Kahn perform in one of the tryouts. Antipathy wishes Damien Kahn with nothing but good luck as he sets of his journey of being a pro wrestler. Antipathy Wrestling
