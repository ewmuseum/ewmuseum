Jamal Wilson gets into backstage turmoil

24 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It seems the comments made by Jamal Wilson to the public radio show hosted by the Wrestling News Network sparked some concerns backstage in the WWC. Jamal Wilson has become the KroW of WWC fairly quickly. It was only a few short moments ago that this piece of information was passed on to the Wrestling News Network, and it was quite a big piece. Wrestlers of the WWC have been asking for Jamal Wilson to be released. Surprising? Yes and no. It's commonly known that many people within the ranks of the WWC did not enjoy Jamal Wilson's past in federations, while some had a good friendship with the man. But it was after Wilson's comments, in which Paul Levine had heard...that angered the WWC management. Paul Levine urged Executives to fire Wilson, but the board of directors (consisting of [name withheld], Aaron Tact and numerous others) voted against it for the sake of the federation (the company runs on a democracy basis). So what did this bring about during the taping of the WWC Menace program? Nothing but complete chaos. The backstage area was a madhouse of backstage turmoil; with it all starting with Paul Levine and Jamal Wilson. Levine, being very upfront, seemingly confronted Wilson face to face and it seemed both men were provoking one another. It looked as if the verbal war was going to be escalated, but wrestlers: Chino Hernandez, Steve Muzio, KaGe and Ehren the King were forced to break up the brawl that was nearly definite to happen. When asked of the brawl, Levine's associates gave a "no comment"...but surprisingly, Paul Levine had called the wrestling networks back with the following statements... "You're damn right I confronted the little prick. When I started this company, I said I wouldn't deal with the bull---- of the politics, and that I refused to deal with wrestlers egos. You're right, I'm no Reed Young and I'm no [name withheld]...I don't take the bull---- of Jamal Wilson," Levine angrily said into the phone, "It's funny how he turns stories around to make me look like the bad guy. If it wasn't for the democratic policy of the WWC, you bet his ass would be gone. Jamal plays his character in and out of the ring, he's just a little f-----t who acts as a prima donna. He feels he's this major star...when wrestling is supposed to be about the sport itself, not making yourself a star. I got guys on my roster that bust their asses day in and day out to please the people and do what they like to do. Jamal only does it for the money" When asking about his 'disloyal' past...Levine replied: "You're damn right I said he was disloyal to previous federations. When the PIW was under siege against all other federations, Wilson was the first to ask for his pink slip...being nothing but a coward and a wuss to deal with criticism. And when it came to the uW and Action Wrestling...how many shows did he show up to? He left BOTH feds high and dry, as he did with PCW as well. All I said was what needed to be said for YEARS. Jamal Wilson is a front runner. Nothing but a no good common day Hulk Hogan...all about the money and control. It's a damn good thing I have dubbed his pay big time....I'm sure he'll be happy about it." When asked if Wilson was indeed going into a legal battle with WWC: "He threatened, and if he does, I've got lawyers that make O.J. Simpson's look like district attorney assigned to a hobo. I've a dream team of lawyers behind me, if he wants to get legal...than let's get legal" Many other questions were asked...but many of the answers were filled with too many cusses and slurs to be published. But in jist...Levine has challenge Wilson's ability to wrestle, as well as his ability to entertain. As it was seen...Wilson was NOT getting over with the crowd as well as he wished to. The story continues within the WWC... WWC Online! -- A Place Wilson Isn't Wanted (so it seems)
