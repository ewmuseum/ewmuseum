EWC [WWA: England] Presents a Late Night Christmas Special

21 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
English Wrestling Championship Presents Christmas Late Night Special! Live from Wembley Arena, London on 25 December 2003 EWC will be live on Christmas day and it will be a Christmas that either Dark Wolf nor Ryan Matthews will ever forget in one of the most brutal contests ever invented. With the Heavyweight Title on the line, plus supporting contests, including a tag team tounrament, for the vacant English Tag Team Championships. English Heavyweight Championship Match Dark Wolf Vs. 'The Real F'N Deal' Ryan Matthews Last Man Standing Circus Deathmatch Each man was given the chance to name some of the stipulations for this contest. The ropes off the ring will be taken down and replaced with barbed wire. There shall be four C4 exploding barbed wire boards inside the ring. Each corner shall also have a pole, hanging from that will be an item bought from a hardware store, these are crowbars, halogen light tubes, chains and even a pair of hacksaws. There will also be flaming tables, an electic heater and a tank filled with ice all at ringisde. And just to make things that little bit more violent, if the two do not fiish by the 30 minute mark, the ring shall explode. The only way to win, is to beat your opponent up so much that he cannot answer the referee's ten count. This is the final of the English Heavyweight Title and has been the focus of EWC television since it's return. Wolf and Matthews got involved in a heated confrontation at the end of the last episode of Monday Night Slaughter when Dark Wolf attacked Matthews with a barbe wire baseball bat and it ended up with Matthews bleeding like a pig from his bag and Dark Wolf lying on the canvas, in a near paraletic state. This match will be violent that after this late night showing, the only other way to view it will be on home video and DVD. English Tag Team Championship Tournament Final ?????? Vs. ?????? Number 1 English Contender for the World Cruiserweight Championship Sam 'The Man' Horrey Vs. 'The TripleCrown Kid' Aaron Rain Since EWC has opened back up, both these young men have more than impressed. Rain has had a close match with Ryan Matthews and Horrey who is keen to live up to his friends reputation will now take on the TripleCrown Kid in this match English Tag Team Championship Tournament Semi Final The New York Wrecking Crew Vs. The Doomers The White Giant & Lassereyes will make their debut as a tag team and will have teh whole of Team CWF backing them up. The winners of this match will advance to the final, scheduled to take place later on in the show. The Black Widow Vs. Isaac Galweigh Vs. James Keiler Three names from the past have been booked for this Christmas Day feast of wrestling action. The Black Widow who was a former English Heavyweight and World Cruisherweight Champion has not been seen for 18 months. The other two wrestlers are better known in EWC as Isaac Spy and James Double-Ooh who wrestle as the Shaken Not Stirred tag team, they are former English and World Tag Team Champions. This match came around after Isaac and James met The Black Widow in a bar. Josh Styles Vs. Teddy Ted Josh Styles is a new signing and once Ryan Matthews and Sam Horrey heard a new star was landing they rushed to the EWC matchmakers and after making a few phone calls have brought in a surprise of their own. While 'Big Dogg' Teddy Ted is well known for his past in EWC and will unite with Horrey and Matthews in an attempt to rid EWC of Team CWF. English Tag Team Championship Tournament Semi Final Sin & Mercury Vs. Northern Stars Mercury will team up with Sin in his first match back. Sin, who is a former English Heavyweight Champion and Mercury who held the short lived British Heavyweight Championship have been teamed up by EWC Matchmakers thiking that they will do well as a team. The Northern Stars are a thre man team consisting of Rune Archer, Tommy Drake and Jonathan Cross, although only two will compete in said match. English Wrestling Championship
