Why everything sucks but Spyder

7 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Many people ask, hommes, why are you in this wrestling business? Do you enjoy the competition? Do you enjoy the millares de mujeres a que usted duerme con cada día? What does a legend like yourself have to say about the wrestling world? Well, I say esse that the wrestling business is as crappy as ever. fWo, the place that is lucky to have the Spyder As the fWo Internet Champion it is my responsibility to listen to the fans and give my opinions on the wrestling business. Well esse, it is well known that I have been carrying the business for years, in Mexico, in Japan and in the United States. Several months ago I joined the fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization. For the first time esse, I'm going to explain what that contract signing was like. You see, this organization begged me to join them because they were in this huge slump and needed a charismatic performer to help boost their ratings. It wasn't long before I had taken three championship belts in nearly as many months. I've been the World Champion, the Cruiserweight Champion, and now the Internet Champion. I'm not one of those greedy bastards who keeps the belt; when I'm tired of it I'll lose it. But I've promised esse, promised to the fans that I'll win all of the fWo belts this year. A feat never before accomplished. But as I look around hommes, I just think to myself, Spyder, por qué hay tanto mierda que camina alrededor como si la mierda tuviera las piernas y los pies? Por todas partes la miro soy crap, sentada del crap, crap en tights y crap luchando. It's sad esse. You've got people on the internet making their little lists about the biggest wrestlers and the biggest heels and who do I see? I see people like steroid man KVC who has no mobility hommes, who can't do anything esse but stand there and wiggle his little breasts around. This is the big heel? This the big man people put on their pathetic internet lists? eWNews, this is a joke, hommes. I'm the Internet Champion and as such I deserve the respect of a champion. It should be my face on your web site, not that baby faced ugly son of a bitch Kodiak Vic Creed. Who cares about him? The wrestling business esse... this business is about making stars and being something. Hommes, this guy is nothing to me and should be nothing to you. I don't care if your world champion is some raping unic or a dumb Russian bear. This is what the wrestling business has come to? Rapists and Russians? Come on hommes, éste no es ningún talento y ningún negocio legítimo. No de donde estoy estando parado. I recently recorded an interview for eWNews magazine and their little web site. In my interview I talked about what I think esse, what I think about the fWo, the Asylum, OSW and lots of other jobber promotions that can't hold a candle to a real champion. But as your internet readers should understand esse, Spyder is a legend and as such should be better represented. You get your ass onto the internet hommes, and you list Spyder as your number one heel. Or you list Spyder as your favorite wrestler. Your mother already believes in Spyder. Hommes, she was believing all night long. Come visit my home page soon, esse.
