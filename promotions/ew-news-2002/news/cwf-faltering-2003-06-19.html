cWf Faltering?

19 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Championship Wrestling Federation, which opened back up since its hiatus from the 1970's has lost already one of its members, Masahiro Zangetsu. A potential main eventer in the rebirth of cWf formerly the CWA, Masahiro Zangetsu was one of the first people signed to the rebirth, but after a pleasant few days, a power battle ensued as co-owner, Levi Brooks and the afformentioned, Masahiro Zangetsu began an intense argument about his position in the cWf. It is rumored that Masahiro Zangetsu wanted to be in the main event slot feuding for the cWf World Championship, but Levi Brooks countered and offered the rumored, "Intercontinental" Championship. A huge fallout soon ensued with Masahiro Zangetsu at the end requesting to be released from his obligations. AJ Brooks, co-owner of the cWf had this to say, "What?! HE LEFT?! Son of a... THAT GUY WAS GONNA MAKE ME MONEY! HE HAD ONE EYE FOR GOSH SAKES!" More to come... cWf - Championship Wrestling Federation
