DW's 'The Loop #1' Good evening ladies and gentlemen, you fans of DW. This is "The Loop", and I am Lorenzo Fiorentine. Well, after being closed for a few months, Dynasty is back on its feet, ready to take on the world of wrestling again. But is it as strong as it once was? Sure, it still has some big names on the roster, like Psyko Stevo and Suicidal, but the big guns of yesteryear, such as Manning, Rick Lacey, Chris Boswell, Hellfire X, and even Gillesp, are nowhere to be seen. The guys making splashes here right now are men from this group, Disturbed Inc., who I have never heard of, and a slew of rookies, at least here in DW. What remaining superstars we have, other than the two mentioned, are inactive at the moment. And what of these Division Managers? Of course, Jon Page, the Owner of Dynasty, will be watched closely in the World Division, seeing how even he just returned after a mysterious absence in which Mike Vitucci took over, and, according to some, drove DW into a hole it couldn't climb out of. I just viewed the last Destruction on the archives. As I mentioned before, Psyko Stevo is still in DW, and in contention for the World Championship. On the last Destruction aired, there was a three-way-dance being anticipated at the coming PPV, in which, Stevo, Christian Idol, and Slim Stevie Bone were going to compete for a shot at the World Champion Gillesp. The Straightest Man Alive is nowhere to be seen in DW. Slim Stevie Bone is in the Hardcore Division, and inactive at that. Christian Idol has disappeared from DW. He was seen at the meetings in Europe, but hasn't been spotted since, and walked away without a contract. What does that say of the World Division? Suicidal is there, but who else? Apparently a few of these are returning stars. Apparently they didn't shine bright enough for me to remember them. Then there's the IC Division, by far the most promising. Head up by Ken, the IC boasts of former stars Darkhan, Jet, and Adam Blade. The other three look promising. The men (and women) in this Division will not be stuck there for long. Then the UK Division, with Jesse as their Manager. This is the biggest challenge for DW. None of these guys, save Hardcore Harry, have been in a DW ring, at least as far as I know. Although a few may be promising, it's hard to expect much. Then there is the Hardcore Division, the only Division with a Manager who has a last name. Dave Brunswick. I worked with Brunswick back in the Revenge Wrestling Federation. He is a capable owner, but his wrestlers did not like him. Seeing as that there is only one person, himself being a pain in the ass (SSB), this division could wind up as the joke, or the biggest draw, of the federation. This will be a division in which people will HAVE to win to move up. Nobody wants to be in the Hardcore Division, unless you consider it your "home" like Christian Idol, or you can't seem to win any other way, like SSB. Knowing Brunswick's track record, he could pull numerous aces out of his sleeves, or he could live up to his reputation as a royal ass, and his division could fall. So why this bleak outlook, you loyal DW fans? I wouldn't call it bleak. I would call it realistic. Who wants to expect miracles, and then taste the water and it still is water, not wine? Some are even wondering why I have the balls to talk like this, especially when I'm employed by DW? Hey, when you grow up as a skinny white kid in Chicago with the name Lorenzo Fiorentine, you end up with some pretty solid balls. DW will be off to a roaring start on Monday night, January 3rd, 2004. I'll be watching, and I'll be rooting for it to go off without a hitch. But my hopes will not be set on overdrive...let's just see what Jon has up his very able sleeves. Until Next Time, Lorenzo Fiorentine, The Loop Later this Week: Predictions for Monday Night! Dynasty Wrestling
