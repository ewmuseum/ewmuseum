Advantage Wrestling's Desire? What Happened!?

5 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last night Desire was supposed to have aired, well, it did air, and it was live, then how come all the ppv viewers didnt see nothing!? Well folks, there was some kind of techical difficulty that made it just appear as a blackout, we're guessing this could be from alot of faulty things with the AW Audio/Visual team. The cameras were still rolling, so most everything has been recorded, well, like I said, most everything.... It seems as if a few cameras inside the arena had wire shortages and oddly enough it just appeared as if they were rolling. We do however have some angle shots but we are missing that of which from the North East side of the arena. We're pretty damn sure that Spike TV isn't going to be too happy of this happening however this was on PPV and not on their network. Never the less, we apoligize to all the fans and PPV viewers and the PPV will air ASAP and your money will be refunded. Now, the bigger isssue at hand, it's very important to put out a quality show that we have all camera angles... Even though it is illegal we've known that some people out there take that chance and bring camcorders anyways. If there is anyone out there sitting in the Northeast section of Qualcomm Stadium who recorded the event then Advantage Wrestling would offer you 10,000 dollars for this video tape and front row tickets to our next ppv venue Crossfire along with airfare and hotel expenses. If someone indeed has a videotape we urgely hope that you contact us ASAP and you can get ahold of the AW HQ lead secretary's phone # at [detail withheld]. AW
