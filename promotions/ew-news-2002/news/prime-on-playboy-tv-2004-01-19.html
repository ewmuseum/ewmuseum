PRIME On Playboy TV?

19 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Proffessional Wrestling and Adult Television? It's hardly a logical combination... or is it? Well yes, apparently it is according to reports coming out of PRIME's head office today. In a bold and in many ways a baffling move, the Premium Adult TV station will be screening PRIME's ReVolution shows starting from next Monday night... MORE The move appears to have been orchastrated by Sebastian Starr in a last ditch act to pull down Toshiaki Motoki's vision of PRIME for 2004. Despite signing over all of his stake in the company to the new co-owner, Sebastian drew up plans with Playboy TV representatives late last year, using his links with Hugh Hefner to seal the deal. Since Toshiaki Motoki was not involved in the original TV contract PRIME held with UPN, neither his signiture or his approval were needed in the process and now he finds "his" promotions flagship show allocated for Playboy TV. So how will this effect the fans ability to view ReVolution? Well unlike the rest of Playboy TV's content, viewers will not need to subscribe to the channel inorder to view PRIME programming. Instead, ReVolution will still occupy the old timeslot of 9PM EST, lasting for the traditional two hours and then will be followed by the 11PM Playboy TV Free View before the station begins with it's private programming. This also means that viewers in UK will be able to tune into ReVolution when is is repeated across the pond on tuesday nights. "When Sebastian first approached me with the idea, I must admit it was a blend of entertainment I hadn't considered before," Confirmed Hugh Hefner on location in Costa Rica today. "But it makes perfect sense. We offer PRIME a platform where they can air their programming censor-free, then afterwards it gives Playboy TV exposure to a whole new audience demographic that we hope to keep hooked for the follow night of sizzling entertainment." The network heads hope that a majority of PRIME's 18-24 viewership will continue watching the station after ReVolution and be tempted to purchase Playboy TV's following content via the persuasion of a 10 minute freeview. Of course not all of PRIME's viewers and fans are male and over the age of 18, hence this move causing outrage amongst some womens rights and childrens groups. "This is a sickeningly covert attempt to break young male viewers into the world of adult entertainment!" Complained Amy Neverlaide, co-founder of a significant womens rights group in Philladelphia. "I am demanding that all responsible mothers and females alike call for a boycott on PRIME Wrestlings TV shows!" If Miss Neverlade thinks that she is annoyed by this televisual move, she had best consider how Toshiaki Motoki is feeling today. Powerless to prevent this deal, Motoki has been humbled in the public eye once again and knows that this time next week, he and the world will finally find out the identity of PRIME's other co-owner. Still though, Motoki promises he has an ace up his sleeve and will reveal what exactly that is on ReVolution next Monday night... LIVE on Playboy TV! PRIME
