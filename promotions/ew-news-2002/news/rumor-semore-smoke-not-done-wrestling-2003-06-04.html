Rumor: Semore Smoke not done wrestling!?

4 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
That's right folks, even though it's a rumor right now, there is a huge chance that Semore Smoke, former NTICW World Champion and News Maker may not be totally done with wrestling just yet! Today I recieved an anonymous tip off that stated that Semore Smoke has been indeed seen talking with wrestlers and executives at both fWo and tA. As of right now there is no confirmation if any have signed Semore Smoke or not, but this leaves the question open, is Smoke just visiting old friends, or is he trying his luck to get signed by another wrestling federation that is out there. fWo has so far not confirmed anything, but they haven't totally denied it either, we were unable to reach fWo executives for an answer, we did get with fWo wrestler, Aimz, who was seen talking with Smoke, she had this to say... "Semore is just an old pal that I worked with in NTICW back about a year and a half ago, we were just talking about the old days. He seems content at home with this bong." This leads us to suspect that he isn't joining fWo, so what about tA? Well we have numorous hints from tA officials that he may be debuting on the next tA show! But this is unconfirmed and only suspect, so we don't want the fans to get too excited, not that they would anyways, I mean it's just Semore Smoke. But could Semore Smoke be turning his career around for the better? No one knows, we'll definitely keep you posted on this as more becomes available!
