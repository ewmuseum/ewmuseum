JP's Column 10/10/2003

10 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Read my thoughts on the current backstage situation in the MWF...and also on the Dynamite that showed how the rebuilding the process is slowly beginning to happen. Hello everyone, Back again for another dose of MWF news and comments. I've been troubled this week by the morale level in the MWF backstage, it seems to me that the departure of top stars such as Anarkist and The Jeopardizer has really begun to take effect on the wrestlers. We've seen some angry scenes backstage and I for one don't like the look of them. We are all in this together and getting angry is not the way to deal with it. It is a very stressful time for the MWF as Walker is attempting to rebuild a roster badly hit by injuries and retirements, unfortunatly it seems as if patience has escaped many wrestlers at this time. Dynamite was a show that was lacking in its usual spark and it's simply because the level of commitment is not the same as it was a few months ago. The under card is still developing, the mid card has been thrust into the main event and it will take time for the fans and the workers to adjust, I myself am still adjusting. The show opened well with Ian Gonzales interviewing Insane Wayne and Iniquity, these two men have to be recognised as a very important part of the MWF. Wayne's one of the most, if not, the most experienced wrestler on the MWF roster and Iniquity is being slowly nurtured into a wrestler who will given time be an all round champion, and a good champion. The two men spoke about the MWF Tag Team Championship match coming up later the same night, they seemed relaxed and assured and declared they had a trick up their sleeve. The opening match was a good hardcore bout. I am not sure why these two men were given the hardcore rules to fight under as they simply do not wrestle in that style. The Vine and Craig Clayborn do enjoy hitting people with chairs but I am very sure they would be far better in a technical bout. A short match finished with The Vine picking up the win with the Vine Bomb and I hope "Prime Time" can keep up the good attitude he's shown since re-joining the MWF and this time when earning a full contract is able to keep performing. We were straight into the second match as Seth Caliko took on Ace Spade. This was a big match for Caliko as just after winning his first match and a full MWF contract he would be looking to try and show exactly why he was given this. Spade was a shadow of his former self as he looked seriously affected by the loss of Doris Spade. His wife is no longer at ringside and Seth Caliko was able to control the match and finish Ace Spade off for a relatively easy victory. The question I will ask later is, what next for Ace Spade? The MWF Television Championship was on the line straight after in a triple threat match. You'll never believe this but Sean Harley has actually dropped the belt, Troy Knight is finally the MWF Television Champion and it has to be said it's about time he had some gold around his waist. Flash was simply an innoncent by-stander in the match as Harley and Knight battled it out for the belt. Troy showed the greater desire and with the crowd on his side he beat down Sean Harley to a standing ovation from the crowd. It wasn't over for Troy Knight as the MWF's newest tag team Sozzey and Mak came out and made an impression on Troy Knight's face. The MWF Television Champion was beaten down by the rookie tag team and they showed a mean strike that we haven't witnessed since the days of the "Canadian Connection". Kidd Kidman and NomaD were shown next, backstage talking about Kidman's shot at the MWF Tag Team Championships that was fast approaching. NomaD assured Kidman that no matter what happens he would have the MWF World Champion's back and he hoped that Kidman and Hell Fire Kidd could wrench the belts from Wayne and Iniquity. The match began and it was an incredible epic. The show itself would have been rated as poor if this match had not shone through. Both teams were up for it and Kidman and Hell Fire Kidd started well and looked set to win the match at one point. Insane Wayne and Iniquity are a team that is growing in status and experience, they're fighters and that fighting spirit showed as everytime they seemed beaten they found that something extra to kick out, execute a better move, or whatever else they wanted to do. Chaos then broke loose as from the crowd came Seth Caliko and Doomsday, the look on Insane Wayne's face said it all as his new recruits beat down Hell Fire Kidd on the outside, finishing him off with a brutal chair shot. There was even more chaos as NomaD and EyCe came running to the rescue, the MWF Xtreme Champion quickl made his presence felt. The fun didn't stop there as the MWF President came out after the match had been ruled a no contest by referee Charles Galliant. Walker said it was a disgrace that this has been allowed to happen, he didn't want to see this sort of chaos again and ordered that NomaD and EyCe would take on Doomsday and Seth Caliko in a tornado tag team cage match. As Walker left all hell broke loose again and it ended with Iniquity standing over the limp body of the MWF World Champion. A sign of things to come? Dynamite won't have pulled in any new fans despite it's strong ending. What it does show is that next weeks Dynamite could really be a good show to watch, new storylines are developing and the pace at which the MWF's writing team likes to execute them. Perhaps the time has come to take a more laid back approach. The MWF's roster may be disappearing faster than George Bush's majority but it does contain some wrestlers who are being terribly under used. The Ace Spade and Troy Knight feud was incredible, nothing more and nothing less. I was impressed by both men, their work rate, their talent and they enthusiasm brought new life to feuding in the MWF. The feud supposidely finished when Knight and Spade when one on one at Implosion in a match that will stand the test of time, Troy won the two out of three falls but lost his long golden locks in the process. Ace Spade since that match has had to stay quiet. He was beaten, his wife's gone on the rung (although we have a pretty good idea where she is and what she is doing-. Ace needs to be used in a way that brings his heelish ways to the forefront, an idea that I know would prove popular is for Ace Spade to move into managing a team. He has experience outside of the MWF but most importantly he has charisma that a lot of the new wrestlers coming into the MWF are sadly lacking. Spade on the other hand could continue to feud with Troy Knight and now that Knight holds the MWF Television Championship it's about time Spade got a shot, booking him against mid card fodder will not help him develop. Walker signed up new tag team Sozzey and Mak quite obviously with a view that these two can attempt to replace the "Canadian Connection" as the MWF's premier heel tag team. Ever since Jones and Evans departed from the MWF there has been a sore lack of good heel tag teams, Insane Wayne and Iniquity have now turned heel but the fans still appreciate them. The MWF lacks a tag team of pure evil and after Sozzey and Mak's introduction on Dynamite I get the feeling they could be just what the MWF needs. The most interesting part of their entrance was not the attack itself but the MWF General Manager's reaction to it. The laughter that echoed from Dom Stoate is to me a clear sign of what is to come, you cannot have Walker and Stoate has popular faces in the MWF...one of them needs to be a heel, whether a comedy or serious heel it does not matter. The fans simply will not be interested unless the management are contrasting. Stoate has been hinting at turning heel and Sozzey and Mak being appointed as his henchmen could be a major step towards this. Dynamite this coming monday will be a packed card as usual, I have reason to believe the Springfield crowd will not be filling the arena. Four and a half thousand tickets are expected to be sold, but currently sales estimates put the sold figure at around three and a half thousand. This is the sign that the MWF's drawing power has been significantly reduced in the past few weeks. Ace Spade versus Hell Fire Kidd: Kidman's going to be at ringside, it will be strange for him to be out on a show so early after his elevation to MWF World Champion. Hell Fire Kidd will want to capitalise against Ace Spade who is not used to wrestling week in week out. Spade is best used in matches when really necessary, he cannot wrestle week in week out or the fans will simply turn off. Hell Fire Kidd will probably get the nod. Sozzey and Mak versus Troy Knight and Craig Clayborn: I've heard good things about Sozzey and Mak once they get in the ring, their promotional work is currently a little rigid and unimaginative but they do the simple things well. Troy Knight as well all know is the complete opposite, flare and flamboyance are the name of the game. It was interesting to see in his match against Sean Harley how he suddenly switched and hit a lot of basic moves to get himself back into the match...Troy may come across as if he doesn't know much about wrestling but many forget just how long he's been wrestling. Clayborn is an interesting introduction to the match, it appears as if he is there only to fill the space but Clayborn is secretly groomed as the new Troy Knight. Insane Wayne versus The Ginga: This will be one for the fans. These two men are veterans of the UWF and if you go around the crowd you'll find many a former UWF fan, before the MWF the UWF worked the Illinois territory and both Insane Wayne and The Ginga were huge stars. Insane Wayne especially, The Ginga was never really in the main spotlight but had a loyal following. These two met before in the UWF and old grudges will be brought back to life as The Ginga looks to pick up a debut win while Insane Wayne, with Iniquity at ringside will want to make sure The Ginga is not allowed to promote himself by beating one half of the MWF Tag Team Champions. Doomsday and Seth Caliko versus NomaD and EyCe: Inside a cage is the home of NomaD and that is simply why you have to tip him for this match. A wrestler of incredible hardcore experience and the MWF Xtreme Championship reign by the "Crown Prince of Hardcore" could well show why he is a future MWF World Champion. Doomsday and Seth Caliko are not to be written off, not by any means. Doomsday is an experienced wrestler who surprisingly is yet to taste the bright lights but I have a feeling he will in the MWF...Caliko was a joy to watch in the MCW and I expect it'll be the same here. EyCe could be the major factor in the match, he's young and energetic and could provide the perfect foil for NomaD...however the inexperience could prove a problem. This match will swing the momentum either for the "Untouchables" or in the opposite direction for Wayne's motley crew. Dynamite will be a show that you don't want to miss. The biggest names are no longer with the MWF but the likes of NomaD, Insane Wayne, Kidd Kidman and Iniquity are intent on making their names part of the MWF's illustrious history and Illinois will soon start waking up to their talent. Until next week, stay well and I'll see you soon. JP (John Pilkington) JP's Column
