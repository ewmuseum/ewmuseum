Pressure Point 84 Report

30 November 2003

Comebacks, debuts, PPV shilling--this show's got something for everybody. (I'm going to pretend the first hour never happened.) Pressure Point Report Date Aired (Live To Tape): 26.11.03 (24.11.03) Number: 84 Location: Tacoma Dome, Washington (state) Posted by Keith Scott Zimmerman And immediately we delve into Soap Opera B-Theatre, as the guy who jumped LLB and is using his real name and old music is Sean Cylear's brother. Revenge takes six months, kid? I wonder how much cash it'd take to have Marilyn Manson have the scoreboard fall on them both...Gave Liz and Barbie my late birthday gifts...Ew, Bradley's making the goo-goo eyes at Leigh. No good can come from this... 1. Sharc beat the Ringer to advance to the Elite 8 of the Player's Tournament. You know who rules? Sharc does. He bumped like a fiend and still got the win. He debuted the Styles Clash and the RKO with more Sharcentric names before locking in the submission of DETH. It'd be really cool if Sharc won. We should have Sharc's birthday off. I [heart] Sharc. Match was Perfectly Acceptable Wrestling. (**) The 101 Elite cut an old school bubble bubble toil and trouble promo; Garvin/Avalon is now Last Man Standing. That is going to redefine awesome...Woodchuck Bill's still alive? BAH!...the Ring of Action plotted evilly, because good plots don't exist. 2. Deft beat Clayton Prodigy by forfeit to advance to the Elite 8 of the Players Tournament. He cut a heelish promo after. This just in: Sharc RULES. 3. Hiro Kitagami squashed Woodchuck Bill to advance to the Elite 8 of the Player's Tournament. Wow, Sunday Hiro's probably actually gonna have to wrestle somebody worth a shit. THAT oughta be interesting. (DUD) Oh, Fox hit Bill after. I'm sure someone cares, but that someone is not me. Max & Coral auditioned for spots on "The View"...Sabotage, full of idiocy, signifying nothing... 4. Tanya Isama beat Kurt Angel to advance to the Elite 8 of the Player's Tournament. Actually, this was pretty close to a sqDID THAT BITCH JUST JACK MY BRAINBUSTER FOR THE WIN! I KEEL YOU AGAIN! TIME TO PAY THE FIDDLER, WHORE! (-½*) This is where I begin to ignore Kayden Paulton completely... 5. Max Danger squashed Anaconda to advance to the Elite 8 of the Player's Tournament. Oof...wow, this show is damn near as bad as last week's. So, Max is going to regain his killer edge over old never-wases and janitors. Yeeeah. Hmm, now Max, first Airborn, then Aroma, then Prodigy...I could call myself the Career Kill--nah, that'd never work. (DUD) Max ran into Jeff, but since Jeff isn't eating meals through a straw yet he ran away to the kitchen so he could make his master a samich...Sabotage kept looking for a victim/teammate... 6. Coral Avalon beat Xiao Suarez. Match took a while to get started 'cause Coral pissed off Barbie somehow. Maybe she thought he actually had a vagina due to these reports and allegedly found out different. Coral worked over the back for nearfalls until he caught a blind rana. Did Xiao just intentionally hit the Buff Blockbuster in Tacoma? GAH. Corkscrew Shining Wizard for a 2. 450° catches knees and you know the Avalon comeback is on when he hits the Play of the Day. Curveball but Suarez gets a foot on the ropes. She came back with a springboard tornado DDT and Avalon busted out an implant Ratings Spike for the win. Hey, my old buddy Nicholas Malloy had that for a finisher! (***) The Ring of Action layed out Paulton...Undertaker v. Fake Undertaker this Sunday SUNDAY SUNDAY... 7. Conrad Ramsey & Donell Arcade fought to a no contest. Someone took a whiz in Granpa's Metamucil because he actually jumped Cracky before the bell. AND thumbed him in the eye! He has nothing but axioms for this society! Mr. Conrad Ramsey is UNLEASHED! Well, at least until he blew the Flair Flip. Donell worked over the ribs but Ramsey procured the MetamuciLock! Oh, mylanta, did he actually diss someone's mother? Now this is a Conrad Ramsey I can get behind! Donell kicked free and it broke down into a Pier 4 that the Arcades won with a Conchairto. No Holds Barred at the PPV. You know, holds aren't barred in most matches, you couldn't say tornado rules or hardcore? (**½) It appeared Sabotage had enlisted Rook but... 8. Frank Heimdal & Sabotage beat Wrestling 101 via chicanery. Bastards. (***) 9. Allison Lindum beat Julie Garvin via disqualification. Match was a squash and red herring to set up Jeff punking out Allison with the MDC & Jammer because Coral couldn't do anything about it due to the no physicality stip. MATCH OF THE NIGHT: 6 or 8. That first hour...*shuddering violently*. BEST SEGMENT: Cutting an old school we's-gonna-hurt-you promo backstage. Respect the old, search for the new. FUNNIEST SEGMENT: I laughed when Paulton got punked out, so let's say that. WRESTLER OF THE NIGHT: I'm giving it to Sharc. He made the Ringer look like not so much of a midcard comedy act, which we suddenly seem to be clogged with, and got Raven Lite off my screen if only for the week leading into the PPV. WRESTLER WITH THE MOST MOMENTUM: *shrug* HOTTEST FUED: Avalon/Garvin. UNDERPERFORMER OF THE NIGHT: Hey, remember when I was all hyped for a C Title match? Yeah, me neither. FLATTEST SEGMENT OF THE NIGHT: LLB immediately getting back his heat on evil LLB. LINE OF THE NIGHT: "You best keep your tight little white ass in check!" Sunday SUNDAY SUNDAY! Pressure Point 84
