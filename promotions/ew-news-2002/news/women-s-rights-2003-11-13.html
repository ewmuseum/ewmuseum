Women's Rights

13 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After a confrontation with Ashley Strife on Desolation 10, J. Leslie Voss takes on the ideas of Women's Rights and where he thinks they stand in today's world. I'm a modern and up to the times man. I'm all for women's liberation and stuff. I'm all for a chick not shaving her bikini line and getting a tattoo of barbed wire around her upper arm and shaving her head and calling herself Bruce. BWAHAHAHAHAHA~! I crack myself up. What cracks me up even more is the fact that more and more women are becoming wrestlers. That they think that they can genuinely cut it with males inside the ring. I mean, the demographic for most federations allow this, but I like to think I appeal to a much higher demographic than the one that thinks women can at all be slightly superior to men inside the wrestling ring. HA! I've been booked to wrestle against a woman this upcoming Desolation. Me wrestling a woman. In my day, we called it "foreplay". And even so, who could be bothered with that. Let me go through the top 5 things women should be doing... Number 5: The dishes. Every good woman should know her place in society and do the dishes. I don't care if you're visiting a friend's house or you're at home. Dishpan hands are not something that should be an affliction to the variety of human that sports a penis. There's a reason why women have smaller hands, it's so they can get the dish cloth inside a glass easier. Number 4: Cooking. Since the dawn of time men were the hunters and the women cooked. It's because women back then knew where they stood. They couldn't catch and kill the meal, so they made up for their lack in standing tall in society by cooking the meals. It was only fair. And it's the same today. We all like to think women can earn as much money as men but it's not true. So, to return the favour of your husband bringing home a fat pay cheque so you can wear pretty dresses, cook for him. And make sure it's a decent meal. Who wants to come home from work to slop on a plate that you women call "an easy meal". Number 3: Cleaning. This is very similar to number 4. The male works hard all day to put that roof over your head. So why don't you reward him by keeping it neat and tidy. Show some respect for what your man has done to you and take pride in his work by cleaning the byproduct of his earnings. The home. Hell, you shouldn't leave it anyways so what better things could you have to do than clean? Number 2: Entertainment. Now this might not be clear for you. So I'll make a small list of the things that come under entertainment... - fetching a beer - being quiet - blowjobs - finding the TV remote - fetching slippers - silence - blowjobs - back rubs - fetching snacks - did I say blowjobs? And now... The number 1 thing that women should be doing... BREEDING! That's right. That's what you were put on this earth for. Making babies. Carrying the fetus of what one could only hope to be a son. You were made for the man to satisfy his beastly desire of sex and to bear his children. We gave up a rib for you. The least you could do is give up your poontang and take one for the team. It relaxes your alpha male and he probably won't beat you too much if you annoy him if he's dumped a load in you the night before or that morning. That reminds me... What do you tell a woman with two black eyes? Nothing. You've told her twice. But seriously folks, you'll find in my top 5 that wrestling wasn't one of the things I think women should be doing. Because they're not entertaining. And the only way a woman can be entertaining is if it involves baby oil, a fireman's pole, a 12" dildo and a wad of dollar bills. That's right. Stripping. Nobody wants to see two women pulling each others hair out and scratching their faces. Let alone getting beaten up by a man inside a wrestling ring. Do what you do best and bare your breasts. That goes for you, too, Ashley Strife. Leave the wrestling for the men. Yours in entertainment, J. Leslie Voss. Hardcore Wrestling Organisation
