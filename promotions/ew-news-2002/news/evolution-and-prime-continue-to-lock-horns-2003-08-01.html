eVolution And PRIME Continue To Lock Horns...

1 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just yesterday Tyler Davidson made his views on the whole Shane Wether affair very public, launching a very personal attack on PRIME and it's owner Taun Pham. Without a doubt, this situation can only get uglier and today, Taun Pham released his own statement to address some of the points and comments brought forward by Davidson. Some members of the wrestling community were surprised by the nature of Tyler Davidsons scathing attack on PRIME. It was clear to many that Davidson and Pham are still fuming over Davidsons bitter walkout on the company. While the parting seems to have had no adverse effects on either party, the whole affair is like an open wound and both men continue applying salt by the bucketful. Before this week, these differences had been kept fairly low profile, however the raging debate over the nature of Shane Wethers signing to eVo has forced the sour relationship into the public eye. Our reporter caught up with Pham in Sacramento as he prepares for PRIME's upcoming edition of ReVolution. Upon being pressed on the issue of eVolution Wrestling, Tyler Davidson and Shane Wethers, Taun gave a wry smile before giving us his views. "Well, it seems Tyler Davidson's has as much trouble nailing his facts, as he did most of his moves. Firstly, I have neither lied, nor threatened Tyler Davidson and his promotion. If Davidson would stop taking rumors and backstage talk for fact, then he might be able to advance with his career rather than being submerged in bitterness. I was going to ignore this whole episode, but since Tyler Davidson is so insistant on trying to push the issue and present me as a corrupt businessman by attacking my credability, I had no choice but to respond. Sebastian heard Shane Wethers was looking to get back into wrestling after spending months on the shelf in the now defunct PCW and sent him down to me. We trialed him in a couple of dark matches to judge his fitness and see if he could still get the job done in the ring. He impressed, so we offered him a deal that he signed on the spot. Shane wanted to get off the mark right away, but since this took place on a friday, we only had two days until the next ReVolution with the show fully loaded with matches. So, long story short, Shane Wethers decided that a small inshow segment would be best and was shot at the weekend. Due to our recent production troubles the clip never went to air, then in the following fortnight Shane Wethers is announced as part of eVolution Wrestling. Now I'm not sure exactly how it happened, but I've been informed by various officials and PRIME superstars that the recently redundant Thug roC played a major role in unsettling Shane." Pham went on to talk about his more personal feelings about Tyler Davidson and Thug roC, then onto the future of PRIME as a company and their recent upturn in fortune after the troubles inflicted on them by rebel superstar Jacob Cross. After several minutes, the issue spun back around to Whethers and this alledged law suit against eVolution Wrestling. "Yes, there was talk in the board room of a lawsuit, but I dismissed it, prefering to focus on issues that are far more important to the company. Somehow this has been leaked to the press and now Tyler Davidson is responding with all guns blazing... or shooting blanks as the case may be. I'm surprised he is so confident of defeating me if this ever went to court. He can waste his money on the best lawyers in the land, I'd love to watch them attempt to explain away a signed contract that has both my own and Shanes name on it, as well as a segment he recorded specifically for PRIME. You know, I might just air the segment in the coming weeks to prove my point..." With that the conversation ended with Taun saying that he had more pressing matters to attend to. How will eVolution Wrestling react and will Pham make true of his threat to air the alledged Segment? PRIME
