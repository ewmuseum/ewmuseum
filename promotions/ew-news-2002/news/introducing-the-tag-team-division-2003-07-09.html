Introducing The Tag Team Division.

9 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After over a month of operation, PRIME is bringing in a Tag Team Division. The new title scene promises so much already, with Taun Pham agreeing to give the new straps large status within the company. MORE... PRIME now nearing it's fifth live edition of ReVolution has seen many releases. Releases of such that could be summed up with three words....The New Breed. Tyler Davidson, Erin Christoph, and even Xero have all decided their place wasn't within the confined grounds of PRIME. Therefore, they're no longer on the payroll. Eversince the beginning of June 2003, one man has been in contact with the one and only Taun Pham, the man of all headquarter decisions. This man has paved the way for a newcomer, a very newcomer in which will rise the ratings of PRIME's very well beefed up topic......THE TAG DIVISION! Representing the well thought out idea is none other than multi-personality, Charlie Richar (Ru-shar). Mr. Richar has enlightened PRIME with many ideas on how to run not only a successful division, yet also a successful employment list. Thus far he has kept his word. Mr Richar was caught walking the sidewalk just outside of the awaiting location of ReVolutions fifth anticipated event and a news reporter was there to stop him..... Mr. Richar, is it true that you've finally finished arranging all business with Taun Pham regarding the Tag Action Division, and at this current time could you even possibly specify when the pinnacle of this tournament that's been talked about will display? Perhaps at the next scheduled pay per view, Fatal Flaw? He commented on such ideas of his and his followers that will have an honorable mention later on. "This tag team division will operate as the other divisions that PRIME has which is not an centimeter below perfect!" He continued to comment on the big issue: "PRIME has been interested for weeks now of obtaining their very own division, I am the man that will represent it and hold it together. I will assign the matches, I will decide just who is fit for this division. That is my take in this cake. I am not here on any specific or unknown deeds, my presence is simply to bring the best tag matches any and everyone has ever watched LIVE!" Charlie also went on to discuss how the new Tag Division would begin...... "Basically for anyone who hasn't ever been apart of this business and even for those who have seeing as how you forget at times, this division will begin in such a fashion of holding a tournament." Charlie paused... "However I like to fancy my captions, this one happens to be the Lethal Lottery Elimination. Randomly from glass boxes aside of each other two teams will be picked to compete the night of the current event. For the next two or three events this will happen. Each team that ends up winning will advance damn sure into Fatal Flaw for a possible Triple Threat or Four Way action packed tag title match. That simple folks. Those that don't win, won't advance. However in MY DIVISION, their not advancing won't end their chances... It's what our superstars make out of this division, no other way may it happen. Thank you for your being nosey, but I must depart now...." PRIME with a new face around the front office, could this be good or bad? Is the entire company prepared for this mans ideas, there's only one way to find out.... PRIME
