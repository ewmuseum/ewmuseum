Media Discourages Upcoming Uncensored Debut

13 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It is expected to be not only a wrestling television show, but a show that will be unpredictable, unscripted and totally uncensored, like the name suggests. See what the media is saying about the debut of Uncensored on Sunday night! With all the talk in the company of Real and Graphic Entertainment as far as the way Sunday night's debut of Uncensored will go, the Media is beginning to put its focus on the upcoming debut as well. Many television networks are putting through segments on their news shows in the evening, putting the upcoming start to shame by saying it is going to "demoralize television" and be a negative influence for children. There have been news programs on popular stations ABC and NBC that have been actually going as far as prompting parents to prevent their children from watching Uncensored this Sunday night and every following saying that the show "will contain graphic and unsuitable nature like nudity, violence, gore, and psycho-behavior." All of this of which cannot be said to be true because the show airs live and of course is promoted as being unscripted. This appears to be the media's chances to spoil the good fortune in the wrestling and entertainment market that Real and Graphic Entertainment was aiming to have. It can be noted that Fox is one of the few networks not to run any story against the show's nature, which may be due in part to the show's involvement with the Fox Network. It can also be pointed out that Uncensored has a binding contract to run until August 31, where Fox can do what it would like with the show. Real and Graphic Entertainment
