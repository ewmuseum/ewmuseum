[PWR] Evolution 2004 PPV goes off the air with a bang!

29 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Great action, surprises and twists at every corner......what more can you ask for? Last sunday, January 25th 2004.........Platinum Wrestling Revolution rocked the Savvis Center in St. Louis, Missouri with it's second pay per view event, Evolution. In the night's main event, a war would come to an end. PWR World Heavyweight Champion, Nick Dangerous and number one contender, Scott Monroe have been at each other's throats for years. In what was promised to be the final encounter, these two warriors engaged in a final battle for the biggest prize of them all, the PWR World Heavyweight Championship. The PWR Territorial Championship was also put up for grabs as the champion, Erik Murdock defended his gold against the number one contender, Drone. This would be a rematch from last month's Christmas in Hell Pay Per View in which Erik Murdock defeated Drone to win the then vacant PWR Territorial Championship. This time around, Drone has vowed that he will get his revenge. Was the masked daredevil, Drone able to overcome the 6'8 monster, Erik Murdock this time around? PWR newcomer, Creed Londos was given a huge opportunity last sunday at Evolution as he battled the seven foot monster, Gargantuflem for the PWR Primetime Championship. Creed Londos won a shot at the title the week before on Glory when he defeated Jamie Adams in a number one contenders match. Creed Londos was now faced with the BIGGEST challenge of his career thusfar.....Gargantuflem. Was Creed Londos able to take advantage of the opportunity? The PWR Suicidal Championship was on the line as well as the champion, MacBeth defended against the number one contender, John Daniels. These two have quite a history.......and in a point in their careers, they even called each other their partner! Both superstars have a similar style that they brought to the table in this extreme atmosphere. Was MacBeth able to rise up to the occasion and keep the gold? Or was John Daniels able to add a PWR Suicidal Title win to his long list of accomplishments? While Nick Dangerous was set on keeping his World Championship in the Main Event, his "son", Zachalous Dangerous had plans of his own as he went up against Pure Talent Incorporated's own, Ryan Extreme in a tables match! The last time we saw a tables match in PWR, we saw Zachalous Dangerous put Kobra through a table.......will Zachalous be able to keep his undefeated streak in these tables matches? Or will Ryan Extreme seek help from his PTI buddies to defeat Zachalous? Ryan Extreme was not the only PTI member in action, Michael "Giant" Reed also was set on taking on PWR newcomer, Ryne Deth. After nearly having his manhood chopped off last week at the hands of Scott Monroe, Ryne Deth surely was coming into this match fired up. Was he able to defeat the giant? A few weeks ago, "The Virus" Marco was suspended by PWR Co-Owner, John "The Iceman" Lugo for his violent behavior in and out of the ring. He was expected to serve a one month suspension........however PWR Co-Owners, John Lugo and Cazzo gave Marco an opportunity to fight for his job. If Marco wins, his suspension is lilfted..........but if he loses, his suspension would be doubled. His opponent was Stephen Jackson. Stephen Jackson walked into this match as the under-dog but he has been known to pick up a few upset victories here and there. Will Marco get his job back? PWR Evolution was a superb show from beginning to end.......and it all started with a five way elimination match pitting five of the newest up and coming talent against each other. The match involved the winner of the new PWR reality show, "Creme of the Crop".......Riley Myers, Ryan Burns, Mr. Rich and PTI members, Deathman and Devon Stevens. Which one was able to step up and pick up the victory? Did the two PTI members join forces to eliminate the other talent? PWR Evolution was a night of so many surprises....it also appears that PWR Co-Owner, Cazzo found out who has been playing mind games with him the past couple weeks. Who are the culprits? Find the answers to these questions and so much more as you see PWR Evolution 2004! PWR Evolution 2004
