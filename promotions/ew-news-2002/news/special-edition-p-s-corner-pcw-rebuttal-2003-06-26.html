Special Edition: P.'s Corner PCW Rebuttal!

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, since Cal Seaver wants to go ahead and send in his opinion on my column, I wanted to write my response as soon as possible. Prepare for a side of Mick P. like you've never seen before... PCW Rebuttal!!! Well, this week I plan on doing two columns, this first one just a rebuttal column to the Cal Seaver column that was posted just earlier tonight, and another one that will be my normal P.’s Corner column of the week. In this special edition of P.’s Corner, I plan on just defending myself against the strong comments of an asshole known as Cal Seaver… First off Cal, I can agree with you on some points, I won’t lie with you, you did have a few good points. Yes, there is totally new management now then there was when those listed before in my column and yours, but you can’t deny that maybe, just maybe there were a few bad booking decisions by the other management… First off, I’ll go with the wrestler I know well, Semore Smoke. Semore Smoke is a talented wrestler, something that you failed to mention about him, and you know he’s talented, yes, maybe entering a fed against Jon Kano and CCW isn’t a good idea, but! What makes you think that it was his idea? You don’t know that? Maybe he was just set up with a feud with the management at the time. Tyler Nelson, was the CEO of PCW at the time, I have it from Semore Smoke himself, that when he was signed, he was offered the spot, and I know this will break kayfabe somewhat, but he was offered the feud spot, because he wanted to enter a feud upon entrance, and it was the only one that he was able to touch, therefore, he was willing to take it, but it wasn’t totally his idea. As for a few things you said about him… well… you might want to check out your own PCW knowledge. Semore Smoke won that Rising Star title tournament, something that you said he “lost”, he won it actually, every title shot that Semore Smoke was ever presented, he won, something that many people don’t realize, he’s capable of it. As for Semore Smoke losing to The End, well… That’s Skylar Green, the current World Champion… and if I’m not mistaken, Semore Smoke gave Skylar Green the fight of his life in that match and both wrestlers came out looking better then ever, just that Smoke took a little too much punishment. I think you don’t give Smoke as much credit as he deserves and you make it seem like he’s a push over wrestler, in which case, he’s totally the opposite. As for Tim Walden, and both members of The Uprising, they had their moments in PCW, but I don’t recall Jordan X. Shabazz ever getting a “World title shot” as I believe you put it in your column, he never got that, because Andy M. was never champ. Therefore that couldn’t have happened, Shabazz did have a long TV title feud with Chricton as well, no denying that, but Shabazz never won the title either… something that booking didn’t allow him to do, no matter how many chances he had, maybe he could have won it himself, maybe he just didn’t try hard enough, but right now the past doesn’t matter anymore, and he’s now kicking ass in fWo Survivor III and that’s all that needs to be noted on the subject. As for Tim Walden, well, everyone knows that Walden has untapped talent that hasn’t been reached, by any fed yet, Walden is World Championship material when he’s at his best in the ring, which he tends to be only a few times during his career, I’ll agree with you on that. But right now he’s making headlines through fWo Survivor III, and he’s getting respect, which I enjoy seeing wrestlers get who have a hard career elsewhere. PCW did in fact mishandle talent, and you know it, it hasn’t tapped correctly into its talent pool, and I feel that some people get pushed way too easily in PCW and others get held back, Tim Walden, Semore Smoke, Jordan X. Shabazz, Andy M., etc. were all held back, while people who didn’t have as much talent, or just disappeared almost completely after the push include the likes of Venemon Pendragon. Yeah, he was talented, but what happened after he got his push? He just fell apart, sometimes you need to allow talent to just grow a bit in the lower card, and Tim Walden had that opportunity as well as other talent, and were never pushed. Maybe they didn’t work well because they thought they didn’t need to, that there wasn’t much of a reason to, sometimes a guy needs an incentive to work well, and maybe a push or two could have been that incentive. Just think about that Cal, think about it. As for bashing me as some two-bit guy who lives “with his mother”, well… for one thing, that wasn’t needed, just because I have the guts to talk about a fed like I want, doesn’t give you the right to slander me with certain phrases. I for one am a married man who does this work part-time for a few extra bucks, money that you could only imagine making from that little fed you call, PCW. PCW isn’t worth that much time bashing, I just say things about it and how I feel about it because I know people who have worked there and who have complained to me about things such as that. PCW isn’t the best federation out there and it does misuse most of it’s talent, and if you don’t agree with me, and if you want to argue me, then go ahead, but the simple fact will always remain the same for as long as PCW is around. It was true when The Phantom Booker owned PCW, and it’s still true to this day. Maybe the only talent that was never misused could be Ivan Stanislav and Boda, but both of them think they have no chance of making the PCW Hall of Fame! That’s because they didn’t make as big an impact in PCW as they could have and as they have in other places, such as OSW(E)! So keep that in mind Cal, and if you want to do another rebuttal column, then go ahead, be my guest, but I’ll just be back to retaliate. That’s all for now I suppose, and I hope you enjoyed reading this… and Cal, I will return your wishes… Have a nice day everyone… including Cal Seaver… -Mick P. Thank you. fWo Survivor III
