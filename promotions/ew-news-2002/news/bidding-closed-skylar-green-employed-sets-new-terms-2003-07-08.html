Bidding Closed; Skylar Green Employed, Sets New Terms

8 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After only two days of bidding, Skylar Green has come to an agreement to work for an up-and-coming promotion. Skylar Green is officially employed by eWrestling, Incorporated. He made the announcement earlier today. The package of $4 million dollars total ($1 million for each charity) has been sent to the charities listed by Skylar Green, and he has faxed them a signed contract. Skylar Green had this to say: "FAW put in a bid, but they shot themselves in the foot when they mentioned that the multi-millionaire bid five thousand dollars for each charity. Cheap bastard." An emoticon of ":-P" followed the "Cheap bastard" comment. Skylar further added: "eW, Inc was willing to give $1 million dollars to four charities to procure my services. No one beat that bid, and so that's where I'm working." So the last PCW World Champion has now found a new federation. That would be the end of this news story, if Skylar Green hadn't kept talking. He went on, saying: "I invite anyone to come apply to work for the organization. They're generous enough, they can't be all that bad." Skylar babbled on for another half-page of text before getting to the heart of another matter. "Now that I've joined eW, Inc. there comes the matter of what I will do now. I mean, I've been a cruiserweight, wrestled in hardcore matches, and gotten to the World Title in PCW. As far as singles goals are concerned, I've done everything that I've wanted to do. One thing I never got the chance to accomplish, though is to achieve tag team success. "I'm a fan of the concept of tag team wrestling, and any division that is healthy is worth keeping track of. As of now, there are three tag teams in eW, Inc. I offer to any of my friends from PCW (or anywhere else where I've worked with you) to join me in this federation as my tag team partner." Skylar Green is not only employed, but he now has set out an invitation for any of his friends from past federations to join him as a tag team partner. Will anyone accept his offer? The eW, Inc. Personnel Director commented on Skylar's press statement by saying this. "It's great to see someone come into the federation and promote it like this all in one day. Skylar's reputation as a trustworthy individual should help out very much in garnering attention for the federation even more than we were already getting. "Skylar's free agency terms were unique, and quite selfless. We would've paid that much directly to him to procure his services, but to do it for charity was all the better. It really helps our image, and gets our name out there. He wasn't doing that to get attention for himself, it was a way for the federations to put themselves in the spotlight by doing a good thing. It's a shame that more wrestling federations didn't catch onto this. "Skylar's offer to anyone to be his tag partner is another great thing. It means that even more wrestlers will be taking a gander at our federation, and hopefully, they'll like what they see. Even if they don't want to be his partner, just having more quality wrestlers around will be a boon to the federation. I'm very excited about this." There is no word as to when Skylar Green will begin work in eW, Inc. but it is assumed that he will be placed to work in the singles roster until he finds a tag team parter, as that seems to be his preferred working condition. We'll release more news on this to the public as we receive it. eWrestling, Inc.
