DW: Boswell set to adress fans at GT II.

23 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Since winning Dynasty Wrestling's biggest match to date, two time Dynasty World Champion Chris Boswell has been on a decline. DW recently went through a "clensing" period to rid the booming federation of older superstars. Boswell being the latest victim is expected to announce his future with Dynasty and Wrestling in general. Two world titles, one International title, headling the biggest ppv of the year, winning Dynasty's biggest match.Chris Boswell, nearly 40 has done all of the above. After a carrer spanning over 20 years Boswell has finally fallen victim to the new breed of wrestling entertainment. Dynasty Wrestling recently went through a "clensing" period in an attempt to rid the booming federation of its older stars, but this isnt the fist time. The now missing DW owner Jon Page had tried every trick in the book to rid Dynasty of Chris Boswell, failing in ever attempt. Since Jon Page's dissaperance 4 months ago Dynasty Wrestling has been on a roller coaster ride of champions and superstars, which didnt sit well with temp. CEO Mike Vitucci. After Boswell won the world title at DW Titan's it was apparent that Dynasty Was back on track untill Boswell failed to show for matches two weeks in a row. In a attempt to bring glory back to Dynasty Wrestling, Mike Vitucci ordered a match in which Boswell could not win. A triple threat title match featuring himself as special guest ref. It's been nearly two weeks since Boswell has lost the title and today he announced that he address Dynasty Wrestling and its fan's on his current position with the company and his wrestling carrer in general. It is expected this announce ment will be made at DW's largest pay per view of the year, Global Takeover.
