[CEW] Pulls Out Of SE-Live

21 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What could have possibly dissolved this association so quickly? Also, the upcoming [CEW] Thrll card is leaked. Read on ... http://www.cewentertainment.com/CEW_eWNews.jpg Just two days after announcing [CEW]'s joining with Sports Entertainment Live, [CEW] has announced from their corporate headquarters in Pittsburgh, PA that they will be dissolving their association with the company. "Our decision to remove ourselves from SE-Live is in no way an indication of a problem with the community within SE-Live or the reception we received. Some of our most senior officials were not told about our association until the news went public. They have expressed a desire to remain independent, and, as these individuals are irreplaceably important to our operation, Cyber Extreme Wrestling has decided that pulling out of SE-Live would be best for all parties," [CEW] officials said in a statement. "We would like to apologize to all [CEW] and SE-Live fans who were looking forward to our association, and the possibilities of future corroboration. Perhaps the door to SE-Live will continue to be open in the future, but at this time, we cannot step through that door without the support of all of our senior officials. Those responsible for creating the connection with SE-Live chose not to tell other senior officials, a mistake that they greatly regret." [CEW] held its most recent Pay-Per-View, Rage In The Cage, on this past Tuesday, with their weekly Thrill card set to return next Tuesday. --------------------------------------------------------- Also today, [CEW] sources leaked bits and pieces of the upcoming Thrill card to the [CEW] Hotline. AC, who was rendered unconscious in his bid to win the [CEW] World TItle at Rage In The Cage, will face "Showtime" Nick Alexander in the main event for #1 contendership for the World Title. Muzzolini would defend his World Title against an unknown opponent. North American champion Kris eGo, who did not defend his title at RitC, will give a title shot to red hot up and comer Weezle. Johnny Psychotic looks to throw an open challenge to the [CEW] roster for men to join him in a rumble training match. Are there four men willing to step in the ring with the "Human Heart Attack"? Also expected to appear: Jeremy Hunter Allen Watts Lord Skywolf Buzzsaw And James Everett III [ cyber extreme wrestling ]
