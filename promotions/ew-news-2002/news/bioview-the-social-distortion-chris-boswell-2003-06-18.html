BioView - "The Social Distortion" Chris Boswell

18 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Having won the Dynasty World Title at Titans 2003 for the second time this week eWNews takes an inside look at the "The Living End" Chris Boswell. 2x World Title Champion - Chris Boswell Wrestler Information Name: "The Living End" Chris Boswell Theme Song: "Prog" by Pinback Age: 32 From: Long Beach, Cal. Height: 6'3" Weight: 232lbs Move Information Wrestling Style: Brawler Finisher Move: Crucifix Powerbomb Finisher Name: Offline PK Signature Moves: Double Arm DDT, Pump handle slam, Belly to Belly Suplex, Gargoyle Suplex, Snap Suplex, Belly to Back Suplex, DDT, Figure 4 Leg Lock, Back Body drop, Spinebuster Wrestler History Former Feds and Titles: XWC (World Champ x2, Hardcore x1), EWF (World Champ x1, Hardcore x5), UCW(Grand Slam Champion), WOW, WIW (Primetime Champ x1), OWA (Internet Champ x2), WWA Current and Past Teams & Groups: Unholy Alliance (w/Shawn Storm and Hellfire X) Straight Edged (Jet, Jon Page, Clyro) Title History: World Title 7th July 2002, DW Global Takeover International Title 6th May 2002, DW Destruction Awards History: Wrestler of the year nominee December 2002 Match of the year nominee December 2002 Bio: After nearly two decades of control in the wrestling industry one of the most well rounded and respected wrestlers ever Chris Boswell has become. No other man has ever been able to explain the story or the character of Chris Boswell, never. Ask any man he has faced in his career spanning over 18 years and they will tell you he is like lightning, he never strikes the same way twice. Matching the mental bulge that Chris Boswell presents in his promotions is nearly impossible, the man holds more knowledge on the business and the world around him then the most seasoned vet. The Legend of Chris Boswell is still being written at this moment and will be until the man is unable to lace up his boots like the rest of us. To Chris Boswell settling a vendetta is more important than any petty record. When you look at the mental aspect of a match with Chris Boswell you never really win. Find one man who can claim they have left Chris Boswell to nothing and I will find you the entire community that he has done the same to. No longer is fighting for gold or fame an option, but to teach the way of the ropes and show why we each interact or attended a show. As one of the few remaining men who can be claimed as "Old School," Chris Boswell when he first started was already labelled as ahead of his time and yet in some aspects still is today. Watching one of his matches is like a bottle of wine, it gets better with age. Every time the quote "Past your prime" arises Boswell looks in the mirror and looks for the thousands of stitches in his body and laughs at the nay-Sayers. The book of Chris Boswell's career is an endless series that passes that of any author. All that is gold does not glitter, not all who wander are lost, the old that are strong do not wither, deep roots are not reached by the frost. From the ashes a fire shall be woken, a light from the shadows shall spring, renewed shall be blade that is broken, the crownless again shall be king. DW - Dynasty Wrestling
