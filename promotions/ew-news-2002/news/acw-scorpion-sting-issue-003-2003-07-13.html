[ACW] Scorpion Sting - Issue 003

13 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, this is interesting. I've been so busy these last few weeks, I bloody forgotten that I have a computer. Guess what, children? With yet another horrid PPV down the drain, your saviour -- ME -- is here to tell you just how badly the Glory PPV sucked. Well, this is interesting. I've been so busy these last few weeks, I bloody forgotten that I have a computer. Guess what, children? With yet another horrid PPV down the drain, your saviour -- ME -- is here to tell you just how badly the Glory PPV sucked. It sucked bollocks, ladies and gentlemen. It was a sodding disgrace to be a part of it, but in due time, my influence will be greatly felt in the company. And everything will be hunky-dory in the ACW. Alright, before I move forward with the review, I've got a little bone with pick with... yes, you twats guessed correctly; SilverHAWK. A superstar such as myself shouldn't have to be forced to take part on a show which isn't going to be broadcasted live. Tonight's edition of Courage, apparently, is to be taped and screened on Wednesday night. That is absolute bollocks. Don't bother welcoming me to whatever dilapidated arena you've booked for Courage tonight, SilverHAWK. Now, let's move on. --- Opening Match: Shodan vs Elijah Arson I have to admit, I actually enjoyed this bleeding contest. Shodan looked quite impressive, and the way he grabbed the victory at the end was pure genius. Mr Arson should realise that he's not the greatest thing since ME in the ACW; demanding the World Title? From SilverHAWK? Madness, I tell you. If anybody deserves the World Title, it's me. But we all know the universal fact that SilverHAWK is a sodding cunt so let's simply move on, eh? Grudge Match: Hillary Small vs Jade Was a boring 'un, the Mexican cunt that I defeated weeks ago showed up in this match. Until today, I don't quite know why. Jade won after squashing Hillary, but really, this match was an utter waste of time. The spot could have been given to me, receiving the World Title. But we all know the universal fact that SilverHAWK is a sodding cunt, so... I digress. Homicidal Tendencies Match Just a note to the transcriber of the match, it's HOMICIDAL. Not HOMOCIDAL. With that out of the way, I have nothing to say about that match. No, not because my men did not win. I'm not a bitter bastard like some people with the name SilverHAWK. I'm actually quite glad that Liam & Drake didn't win the titles. Because if they did, they would have cemented themselves as the best out of the four teams present in the company. Two of those teams were in the match, and the last team are a pair of bleeding twats. I mean, Flying Scotsmen. What the bloody hell kind of name is that? Oh, right, something that would solidify SilverHAWK's universal status as a sodding cunt. Fact of the matter -- this match, while bloody exciting, was a devious ploy by SilverHAWK to rob my boys off their deserved status. No matter. Last Man Standing Match: Chad Stalin vs Jason Reaves Chad Stalin is someone I actually admire, because he's a fighter and not a big a cunt as SilverHAWK. That being said, it's such a pity he had to waste his time demolishing Reaves, who's a twat for even thinking he could ruffle Stalin's feathers. I have a feeling Stalin & myself could be great partners, but there's that little matter of the boy's out-of-control anger. Good to see Chad actually win and injure that fecker Reaves in the process. Three cheers for that. Scorpion Title FIGHT: ME vs Gabriel So'ialu What can I say? I had told everyone that So'ialu was NOT in my bloody league. I told him that he wasn't worthy, and that he sucked donkey bollocks. And in the end, I've gone ahead to prove myself absolutely correct. Fine, I hear you lot bragging about how the cunt dominated the fight. Well, you numbskulls, who walked out the winner at the end? Who walked out STILL the Scorpion Champion? Who didn't limp out the arena, ego deflated and in a state of mental anguish? That's bloody right, ME. I was victorious. I WILL admit that Gabriel is a decent fighter, but compared to me? He's shite. Enough said. Television Title Battle Royal Yet again, it has to be said. SilverHAWK's universal status is that he is a sodding cunt. I mean, what the bloody feck? He could have given the Television Title to ME. But instead, he throws in several buggers into a pathetic Battle Royal, and the winner? That git, Quinton May. Quinton's been in the federation longer than me, and yet, it has taken him close to seven months to finally earn a championship? Bah, it boggles the mind! Of all people, the twat wins. He hasn't even been active the last couple of weeks. Horrid match here, with a horrid ending. Horrid. Truly horrid. Steel Cage United States Title Match: Dante Inferno vs A*Dubbs NIGGA WHAAAAAT? Finally, the speech-impaired pillock has lost his precious championship. But, to all people, Dante Inferno. Dante used to be a role model for children everywhere, until he decided to be best mates with SilverHAWK or some other. For that reason alone, this contest shall be declared a sham. Grudge Match: Jason Kain vs Osyrus BO - RING SHITE. Finally, however, I think these two cunts have ended their little war. About bloody time. Some Lame Gimmick Match: Vince Jacobs vs Alias BO - RING SHITE. Two over-the-hill faggots in the lamest match in history. Bleeding hell, this match alone destroyed the entire PPV. Bloody horrific. --- So, that concludes the rundown of another abysmal PPV in the ACW calendar. What a surprise, eh? I thought the previous one was bad, but bleeding hell, Glory turned out to be a ghastly and hideous event. It even compares to the atrocity that was Titanic, and Lords Of The Rings. Truly dreadful. Now, a little treat for you fans of mine, however heavily marked for death you are -- starting from tomorrow onwards, I shall be expanding my horizons. Gabriel So'ialu was touted as the man who would derail me and eradicate the rodent hailing from Manchester. He has failed, and looking at the roster sheet, I see nobody at all who can defeat me, much less stand up to my greatness. So, I am in search of people who are worthy. If you think you've got what it takes... please, kill yourself. Only I know what kind of competition is suitable for me. With each passing day, ACW looks as if it's crumbling with SilverHAWK running the shots. If only he'd bring in fighters instead of twats like Elijah Arson & Tommy Taylor, I wouldn't be forced into this nationwide tour. As it is, I'm looking forward to it. And Quinton May... I do believe it's time for us to lock horns. Be prepared for the scalding of your life. -VP All-Star Championship Wrestling
