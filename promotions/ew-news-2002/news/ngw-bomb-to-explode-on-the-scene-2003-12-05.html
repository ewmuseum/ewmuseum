[NGW] Bomb To Explode On The Scene?

5 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sources close to the NGW headquarters are suggesting that KrazE is trying to lure Time Bomb back into NGW as a crucial staffer. Sources close to the NGW headquarters are suggesting that KrazE is trying to lure Time Bomb back into NGW as a crucial staffer. They have a very good working relationship with each others and respect each other greatly, it has been no surprise that Time Bomb has already been spotted in meetings with KrazE in various places within the past couple of months. These rumours have been spread before but nothing came of them, could this be the same again or will KrazE's recruiting skills bring the former NGW hall of famer back into the limelight? We were able to catch up with KrazE to hear these words... "Well who can blame me for wanting to bring in someone with knowledge, history and background of NGW like Time Bomb has?! He was an exceptional wrestler - look at his record in all federations and that proves it, but most of all he is a great staffer - he has what you need to succeed. He doesn't take shit, he's got the contacts and he has an eye for talent. Most of all he knows what is right and what is needed for NGW to step up another notch and take NGW to the heights it should be reaching. Let's not forget what he has done to transform NGW from what it was - he's a historical figure and many loyal NGW fans will know what he has done and can still do, I believe he is a crucial figure in taking NGW to greater things." Obviously KrazE's respect for Time Bomb is truely of the greatest, but it all depends on what Time Bomb will. We were lucky enough to get a phone interview with Time Bomb and concerning the reports of him joining NGW - well, this is what he had to say... "Well of course there is no smoke without fire and I am always being linked with NGW due to me taking such an active interest with them, NGW was where I was raised - I made my name there and achieved many monumental things that made me what I am today. I don't want to blow my trumpet but come on... I made NGW what it is today, well technically Scottywood has taken it down a few notches since I left but never the less - when NGW was at it's greatest I was running the show, pulling the strings. The only reason why I didn't re-join was because I didn't have the time to, but good things come to those who wait and the time has passed by and now I seem to have more spare time on my hands and I don't like doing nothing so I may invest myself into a hobby - perhaps NGW, who knows!" As if that's confusing enough for us to mull over, then why do we keep seeing Time Bomb hanging around with KrazE backstage? Of course this is never shown on NGW's weekly show Tuesday Night Storm but deep into the backstage where the cameras cannot go we see Time Bomb and KrazE having "private" meetings that even NGW owner Scottywood is unaware of. Will he re-join? Will he just cause more confusion? Who knows what Time Bomb do, we don't but we hope he'll explode onto the screens of NGW as we all know what he is capable of - only good things! From me, Chris Smith, over and out until next time. Stay tuned for more NGW news. Next Generation Wrestling
