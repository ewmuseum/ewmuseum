NWC:A Maelstrom Review

21 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lots of happenings on this weeks Maelstrom, check 'em out! -Opening Credits -Dark Matches for the night had Newcomers Icon and Eddie Heartbreak winning over Ian Paul Freely and Jim Beattie respectively, while Max JaXson lost against Adam Kaplan in Max’s second tryout for Atlantic. -Atlantic Heavyweight Champion Jake Summers arrives to the arena and Ross Boswell, Nathan’s father, catches up with Jake with information on who ran Summers kid down. -Apostle and Victoria have a little argument in their locker room -Derrick Daliente arrives at the arena talking on his cell phone to “Ben.” He then catches up with a guy named Hugo who gives him the run down on Anton DuFresne and Leonard Aarons. -Bad Ass Mothas v. Apostle and Amazing Rando -Clip of Icon leaving the arena, but not before getting in a word exchange with Leonard Aarons and catching up with Heatwave -Jake Summers in his locker room getting a surprise visit from his father, which didn’t go as well as Jake’s father had planned -Vicki Vixen catches up with Damien’s girlfriend Claudia, but asks to many wrong questions and gets thrown out of the room. -Vinny Azzaro and Halloween v. Mark Masterson and “Living” Legend Magnum MT -Leonard Aarons cuts a promo on Jake Summers and why Aarons is better that Summers -Eddie Heartbreak requests that Joey Long give him a match with someone on the roster next week, in which Long agrees and sets up Eddie v. Halloween for next weeks edition. -Morgan and Bobby Irish arrive to the arena and Vicki Vixen catches up with both of them. New England title Match - Damien Kahn v. Nathan Boswell© -Hugo catches up with Leonard Aarons, who tells Hugo to tell Derrick that the two have nothing to talk about. -Jake Summers and Ross Boswell have the accused man in custody, but Jake unties him as Ross and the man yell at each other. Nathan walks into the room and wonders what is going on. Leonard Aarons v. Chaos -Heatwave and Marc Smith have a verbal confrontation backstage, while Damien Kahn and Mark Masterson have a physical confrontation backstage. -East Coast Title Match- Heatwave v. Morgan© -Vicki Vixen catches up with Leonard Aarons and gets his scope on the Lockdown last week among other things. -Jake Summers heads to the ring for a long rant on Leonard Aarons. -Aarons sends Vanessa home as things are about to get ugly. -Atlantic Tag Team Title Match- Polar Opposites v. Dominion©. Plus a shocking return in the post match. -NWC World Champion Billy Page meets with Joey Long and Page reveals who in Atlantic is getting the World Title shot next week. -Main Event- Atlantic Heavyweight Title Match- Marc Smith v. Jake Summers© Post Match- Summer’s father comes out and plays a video of the car that ran over Jake’s son, but not revealing who actually drove the car. Jake lays out his fathers bodyguard, and attempts to take out his own father, until a masked person comes to the ring and lays Jake out, who later is revealed to be Aarons. SLJ then comes running to the ring, with Aarons hightailing it out of there. -In the final segment of the evening, Derrick and Aarons catch up and get into a brawl backstage, which ends up with an injury to someone other than those two. But to find out, along with the match results, you gotta check out the results for yourself. NWC:A Maelstrom
