SOCIETY announces signing with Lionheart Wrestling Exhibition

25 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In a press conference today in Osaka, Japan, former PCW star SOCIETY discussed many issues, the foremost of which being his signing with the new Lionheart Wrestling Exhibition. SOCIETY, best known for his feud with Miss Moonlight and her Lovely Overdrive Vixens Extreme faction in Primetime Championship Wrestling, has signed with Lionheart Wrestling Exhibition. "He was one of the athletes who had agreed to a contract verbally that I had reported on earlier," said LWE reporter Yuri Takayama. "LWE officials are glad to have his name on the dotted line. As per puroresu tradition, SOCIETY officially made his announcement during a press conference, after taking a red eye flight from San Francisco to Osaka. "I am excited to have signed with Lionheart Wrestling Exhibition," SOCIETY said, through an interpreter. "The opportunity to test my skills in such an environment was too much to turn down." SOCIETY, who stands 6'10" and weighs 358 pounds, is the largest signee to LWE thus far. Keen watchers of the puroresu scene expect him to take on the role of large powerhouse gaijin, as it is something LWE lacked before SOCIETY's signing. A full transcript of the press conference will soon be available at the temporary Lionheart Wrestling Exhibition website. The Temporary Home of Lionheart Wrestling Exhibition!
