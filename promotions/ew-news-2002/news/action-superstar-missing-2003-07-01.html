Action! Superstar Missing

1 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Action! Wrestling is disturbed by the Silent One's silence. At Action!'s recent PPV, 'Til Death Do Us Part, superstar Bradley Duncan aka The Silent One, got into a non-verbal arguement with production manager, Joe Schmidt. Through a sign language translator, Joe was able to determine that Bradley was highly upset at not being on the PPVs card. When he was refused a match ("There, honest to god, was nothing to be done about it. We were booked solid," remarked Joe afterwards.), he nearly became violent. He threw a chair and stormed away, looking mighty upset. Following the Max Danger and Conrad Ramsey tag team victory over Topex and Le'Java(collectively known as Accidie), Bradley Duncan stormed into the ring and kicked the former Bantam Champion, Ramsey, hard in the back of the head. He then grabbed his former tag team partner, Max Danger, which he held the PIW World Tag Team Titles with at the end of 2002, and started to choke him. It wasn't until Jessica, Danger's wife, got involved that Bradley released his supposed bestfriend. After that incident, which was part of the show's script, albeit a little more real that it was intended, Bradley Duncan fled the arena. He never showed up at the hotel, where he was sharing a room with Mr. Ramsey, and vanished out of sight. Right now Action! is uncertain as to where Duncan is. "He pushed me against the wall, hard, as he was leaving the building," commented custodian Homeless Gus. "He looked.. deranged." They are hoping that he will show up for next week's Pressure Point in Montreal, Quebec, Canada, or hope that someone is able to find his whereabouts. "He's a sweet guy, who's going through some difficult times," Jessica told me. "I mean, his bestfriend's mother died, he's been in a horrible slump professionally. It just all came to a head Sunday night, it seems." Many more people expressed their concerns for the young man's mental state. "I just hope he's alright and isn't doing anything to get himself in trouble," Jessica closed by saying. Action! Wrestling
