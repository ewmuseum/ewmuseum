hWo Status Report

23 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ron Sizeman here. I'm gonna be giving you all the inside goss on the hWo. Squashing rumours and maybe putting some truth behind others. All this and more in my status report! hWo! Ron Sizeman here with a road agent's update on the current status of hWo. In this news article I'll be dealing with rumours and all the latest news on how superstars are doing inside the Hardcore Wrestling Organisation. The first Desolation in Mexico was a complete success. Fans greeted the hWo and it's star with open arms and were very receptive to the athletes as they showed their talents on Desolation 19. They were excited to see J. Leslie Voss make his official return to the ring, and were exceptionally happy to see the numerous title defenses by Dane Manetta, Randy Lancett and Team l33t. The Mexico City community are looking forward to the hWo holding more events from their No Holds Barred Arena and from all reports, many of the fans are excited to go back for more. The rumour mill has been working hard recently talking about former ACW star Jade being seen around hWo head quarters. Well, it's not my job to squash a rumour. The rumour mill has proven to be true this time. Jade has in fact signed with hWo on a six-month verbal agreement. hWo are hoping to get the former ACW star onto full written contract before her six-month period is up. Her fiancee, Jason Kain, is rumoured to be signing with hWo. Kain, another big name from ACW, the biggest some might argue, has been seen around the hWo headquarters with Jade. Talks of the star signing have been soaring through the rumour mills but I have yet to find a source to squash this rumour or clarify it to being true. And speaking of stars, the top rumour at the moment is that former hWo Legacy champion and Carnage Wrestling superstar, Ben Haynes, will be signing a hWo contract. My sources tell me that we have been in talks with the big man, but there has been no talk of contracts or pens to sign them with. There is no further news of J. Leslie Voss signing a written contract, but the topic of his contract has been high on the agenda recently. WWC (http://wwc.wrestleworks.com) have been in talks with their lawyers about filing a law suit against the hWo for injuring J. Leslie Voss at their One Crazy Night Pay-Per-View. WWC had also attained Voss on a verbal agreement, hoping to push the superstar to their upper-midcard but with his injury sustained, Voss has put WWC on the back burner. WWC and hWo are in discussions as to what to do in this situation, where Voss is still keen to work for both promotions, only not WWC right now. J. Leslie Voss' injury is a torn ACL in his left shoulder. It was torn in the main event at One Crazy Night and Voss is in a lot of pain due to the injury. He has had some minor surgery on the shoulder which is hindering his movement in his left shoulder. hWo are proud to have such a brave member on their roster as Voss still manages to entertain without getting inside the ring. We wish the young superstar a speedy recovery so he can once again perform inside the hWo ring. More recently on Desolation programming, we've been seeing Paul Armstrong and J. Leslie Voss going head-to-head. On the last Desolation we saw C.J Trenton find a syringe at the end of the show, it having fallen out of Armstrong's bag. Internet sources are calling this part of the Voss-Armstrong program a shoot, that Paul Armstrong is actually using pain killers on his knee. But we hear at the hWo are hoping to nip that rumour in the bud and we can safely say that it is just a part of their storyline. hWo has been impressed with Toomes recently as the star is making himself more known throughout the recent programming of Desolation. Not only is Toomes impressing in the ring and backstage, he has been somewhat of a leader to the younger stars backstage. hWo has high hopes for Toomes and plan to push him a little later on in the year. That's all for now from the hWo and until next time... Is it's not hardcore... it's a plain bore. - Ron Sizeman signing off. Hardcore Wrestling Organisation
