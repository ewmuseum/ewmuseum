HSW: Shocker Results from Wichita

6 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Headstrong Wrestling presented Shocker live from Wichita, Kansas on September 6, 2003. Besides 3 entertaining singles matches, the final match was a huge Battle Royal that crowned the fed's first Headstrong and Evolution Champions! HSW Shocker was off the charts. Here is a layout of what actually happened! -Owner Joey Matthew came out to the ring to discuss many HSW matters such as his goals to make the company huge and he talked about tonights matches. -Commercial Break -Tyler "Too Sweet" McPherson voiced his opinion on his opponent for tonight as well as the Battle Royal participants in a pre show promo. -Match #1-Freddy Charles pinned Hazard via The Nightmare -Commercial Break -Damian "D-B" Bartlett cut a segment talking about Assassin. He completely trashed Assassin's car outside in the parking lot and rapped a little for the audience! -Match #2-Tyler "Too Sweet" McPherson pinned The Veteran via chairshot when the referee had his back turned. -Commercial Break -Match #3-"Grade A" PKA's one time appearance match saw him defeating friend and HSW Color Commentator "Red Hot" Kris Red via the Deadly Star Press. This is a match that you have to see to believe! -El Nutso and Assassin introduced themselves in a backstage segment. -Commercial Break -Match #4 Main Event- Battle Royal The Rules are simple, 15 men enter the ring at once. They can win only by tossing their opponent's over the top rope and their feet must hit the ground. Once there are only 4 men left, the match becomes a Fatal Fourway Elimination in which you can win only via pinfall or submission. When only 2 men remain, the match becomes a No Disqualification Match. The winner becomes the Headstrong Champion, the Runner-Up becomes the Evolution Champion. The elimination order went as follows: 1.Hazard by Assassin 2.Civvik by El Nutso 3.Nitro by Tyler/D-B 4.Jacknife by Travis Smith 5.Tai Hashi by The Veteran 6.Tank Andrews by Assassin 7.Jason Glasgow by The Veteran 8.The Veteran by Tyler McPherson 9.Freddy Charles by Rachel 10.Travis Smith but D-B/Rachel 11.Rachel by D-B 12.D-B by Assassin 13.El Nutso by Tyler McPherson 14.Tyler McPherson by Assassin Headstrong Champion-Assassin Evolution Champion-Tyler McPherson To read the results in its entirety, click on the link below and go to "Shocker" under the Results image.
