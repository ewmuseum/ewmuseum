BioView: "The Street Hustler" Jay Quickness

A quick look into the life and strengths of one of the members of Playaz Inc. Written by Jon Stylez Jay Quickness Real Name: Jay Quiten Santagio Also Known As: Jay Q. Date of Birth: September 7, 1980 Hometown: Bronx, New York Height: 6'2" Weight: 211 lbs. Status: Heel Entrance Music: 'Many Men' by 50 Cent Wrestling Style: Innovater/High flyin Favorite Moves: Super kick, Top Rope Leg Drop,Hurrincrana, Split leg moonsault, Dragonrana, Sharpshooter, Tazzmittion, Spinning heel kick, Wheel kick, Flipping drop kick, Snap Suplex, Underhook suplex, Hurrincrana Pin, School Boy Pin, Suicide Dive, Sucide Flip, Springboard leg drop, spring board wheel kick, springboard ddt, GangStarr Stretch (Elevated clover leaf) Signature Move: Project Slam ( Jay Quickness grabs his opponent on his back in a reverse DVD position, and spins his opponent body around and jumps to the floor.) Finisher: 1-8-7 (Jay Quickness grabs his opponent in a pedegree position, picks up as for a pildriver then lands in a x-factor position.) Past Federations: Vintage Wrestling Past Federation Title History: Vintage Wrestling tag team champion w/ Rey Campbell Past teams: M2S (Rey Campbell, Mark Fort'e, Jay Q.) Playaz Inc. (Statuz Quo, Duzza, Jay Q.) Allies: Rey Campbell, Status Quo, Duzza, Shawn Fox, Mark Blaze, Tommy Lorenzo Enemies: Ender, Oscar, Total Hatred, Biography `File Open Not much is known about Jay Quickness. Jay Quickness is in his early 20's, when he was a little kid he was in a gang named Piru aka bloods. He hanged out with the wrong crowd. Now he is a little older and is trying to calm down. While he was under contract with Vintage Wrestling he was dealing drugs and got shot by a rivial gang member from the crips. Jay Quickness got even when when of his customers told his who it was that shot him. The customer set up a deal between Jay Quickness and the man who shot him, Jay Quickness ended up beating the crip member to a bloody pulse with a metal bat, leaving him in a pool of his own blood, Jay Quickness is trying to change his life around by wrestling, That is all that is known about Jay Quickness. `File Closed ACE
