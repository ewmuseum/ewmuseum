hWo Desolation results (6/15/03)

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
hWo made its huge return to the wrestling world on June 16th 2003 with Desolation! With former stars from ACW, CWL, IOW and many other places Desolation was assured greatness before it even went on the air. In the first match of the night Ben Haynes took on Dante Daze. The match was a decent opener with few surprises on the wrestling end of the match. Ben Haynes was able to pick up the win in this one. The next match saw Joseph Spectre and Phate of aWc fame hook it up in a hardcore rules match. Phate is able to get the three count on Spectre after many high spots. The next contest was a tag team match between Squadra Italia who were runners up in the 2003 fWo Tag Team Survivor competition against Jay Jaxxen and Dean McDaniel. Some would say that Jay Jaxxen and Dean McDaniel pulled an upset as they picked up a win against Squadra Italia. The next match between Frost and Omega could have headlined a PPV. Many things such as the debut of former CWL and riVal superstar Big Little Italy and his attack on Frost allowed for Omega to pick up a huge win against Frost. But the big question emerging from this match is, “Why Did Big Little Italy attack Frost?” Many critics and fans have said The Dynamo vs. Rhett Locke match stole the show. The feud that has spanned many federations ended in the hWo and both men really gave their all. This match was really history in the making. Even though the match ended in a no contest everyone in attendance walked home happy after watching this one. This match then lead us to the main event of the night which featured two former ACW superstars, Branson Blair and Dane Rivers. Although in the past there has been some speculation over Dane Rivers work ethic, Dane obviously put that issue to rest as many say he put on the performance of a lifetime against Branson Blair. Branson ended up winning this contest to the surprise of many. After the match Omega made a surprise interjection into the main event by attacking Branson and insulting his religious beliefs. With such a strong card, it will be hard to try and top it next week. However next weeks Desolation will come to you live next Sunday June 22nd! Desolation One Glory 2003 fWo Survivor III
