MWF Wisconsin run to end...

18 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The MWF will be live in Wisconsin one more time before heading onto new lands... The MWF will finish its run in Wisconsin with Dynamite live from Madison. Doomsday and Iniquity will open a night which is excpected to host a hell of a lot of the fallout from the Implosion expected the night before. These two have already met in the ring and Iniquity will be fresh off a huge match at Implosion in which he has the chance to net more than one MWF Championship. Doomsday will be looking to exact revenge and Iniquity will still be bitter from the chairshot he received at the hands of Doomsday last week on Dynamite. The Main Event will see three wrestlers who are the future of the MWF competing, Sean Harley and Mickey Bombastic have a score to settle after their meeting in the MWF corridors on last weeks Dynamite. Sheldahl will have a chance to introduce himself and be part of what should be an interesting show. Who will come out best from Implosion? Maximum Wrestling Federation
