MWF signs home talent...but loses young star!

11 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Maximum Wrestling Federation has given a three week trial contract to Illinois wrestler Shaldahl. The Illinois based promotion signed the former EPW wrestler after he completed a medical. Shaldahl is a high quality wrestler and the MWF has signed him up as part of their Temp Roster scheme. In a statement to the press the MWF's new General Manager outlined the MWF's intentions. "We have signed Shaldahl as we see this as a step forward in bringing local Illinois based talent to the MWF" he continued..."Shaldahl has worked for a number of promotions and we hope his experience and talent will be able to shine in the MWF." Shaldahl is expected to make his in-ring debut in the near future, many predicting he will play a role in this weeks Monday Night Dynamite. Elsewhere in the MWF the roster has been weakened after the departure of bright young star Joe Foale. Foale has joined small promotion XFWF and will no doubt make a star of himself there. The MWF front office claimed there was no bad blood between Foale and the company but many insiders predict a bust up between Foale and MWF President Edward Walker may have played a part.
