Jamal Wilson fires the last blow?

25 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jamal Wilson fires back at Paul Levine's latest news story. Jamal Wilson worked as a wrestler and a road agent while on aWc's payroll despite wrestling in front of dismal crowds and being paid very little by Jonathan Enders. Jamal Wilson left a message on Scott Powell's answering machine again to make his final statement regarding the backstage heat he has amassed with the WWC and it’s owner, Paul Levine. “Look, the way I see it is that I have no problems with anyone on the WWC roster with the exception of those few who like to bash Action! Wrestling. I wrestled for Action briefly with Chino Hernandez, a good friend of mine. We were to enter the tag team ranks and make some money there. But, at the request of Jim Johnson, then PIW owner, I left. I talked to Reed and a few of the road agents and it was made clear that if I ever needed a job, be it in the ring or in an office building, I would have one. We left on good terms. The whole situation with Christian Keaton and uW happened ages ago and I don’t even remember how everything went down, what I DO remember is that I patched things up with uW and there is no heat whatsoever. Now regarding PCW… I’m just curious, Paul, have you been smoking or snorting whatever KroW has been? If you can tell me ONE time that Jamal Wilson was on a PCW show, I’ll give you all of my salary and all the royalty checks I get whenever some jackass says “Byezzies!” on television. I. Have. Never. Wrestled. For. P. C. W. I didn’t even want to get into all this, but I had to reply after reading the second news article. If anyone in WWC has a problem with me, I have a cell phone, I have an email address, I have instant messenger, which I occasionally reply to when I’m not checking out David Beckham modeling underwear… mmm.. David Beckham. Err.. Back on topic, I’m calling a truce to Paul Levine right now. We can put this whole thing behind us. But if he still has heat with me, I BEG you, Paul, I BEG you, to somehow convince the executives to get rid of me. I’ll have no problem finding work. And anyone who knows me should know that I’m not a prima donna, I don’t flatter myself with unnecessary praise or put myself over at the expense of others. Reed Young, [name withheld], Jonathan Enders… they know me. I worked for Jonathan Enders despite financial problems and working in front of less than 5,000 people a night. I even worked behind the scenes to help aWc get off the ground. Things didn’t work out, but I proved my loyalty and I proved that I’m not in this for myself. I treat my friends like they treat me, with respect. And if Paul doesn’t want to treat me with respect… he can kiss my rosy black ass.” Wilson then hung up and said he wouldn’t leave another message on Scott Powell’s answering machine unless he felt provoked to.
