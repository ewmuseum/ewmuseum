Another team eliminated as contest changes

23 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
If there is one thing certain in professional wrestling, it is that nothing is certain. In what has now been declaired as the final elimination of the Survivor III contest, Disposable Heroes walk away. Survivor III In what has been seen as the final stake in the heart of the much discussed on the internet "Gold Alliance", the team of Disposable Heroes were voted out in the latest round of the Survivor III contest. This largely marks the end of the Gold Team, who had troubles in the contest due to bad luck and a string of losses in early challenges. But the vote was not a decisive one, with four votes cast for Disposable Heroes, one vote for JLA, one vote for Tim Walden and Jane Matheson and one vote for Brotherly Love. Teams commented on the elimination with some regret, "When I came into this game, there was the Greens, and there was the Golds, and they were getting rid of each other. As much as I hate to say it, it looks like the merge didn't change things. The last two teams gone have been Gold from the beginning, and if following the trend is what's going to keep me in the game, I'm all for it." Another team had similar things to say, "You are an extremely talented team along with the other five teams in the contest, but weighed my decision on your attitude which I felt was worse than the other five teams." Still another team, on his decision to select Disposable Heroes was less magnanomous, "You live by the sword, you die before the sword. There has never been a time I enjoyed eliminating someone from the game... until now. Your underhanded, dirty, and sneaky tactics have landed you where you deserve to be: on the outside. Good riddance. The last of the Gold alliance is done." Strangely, Disposable Heroes seemed to anticipate the move, understanding that the vote would undoubtably be cast against him. "I'm gone anyway," said the team. Many onlookers expressed deep regret, as the Disposable Heroes tag team was a fan favorite amoung several Survivor III followers. But in another strange move the Survivor III officals have notifed fans that this will be the final elimination in the contest, which has suffered in recent days through long delays. "Our core staff had a serious accident, it's better now, but it has marred the contest," said Twitch. More news as it comes available... fWo Survivor III Online Site
