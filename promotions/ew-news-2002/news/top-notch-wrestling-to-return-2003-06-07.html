Top-notch Wrestling to Return!

7 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Adam Morgan began his promotion with high hopes April 1st 2003. Unfortunetly the profanity of one of his workers nearly crushed his dreams. However, we have new reports that Adam's dream will live on. Adam Morgan, former independent worker who was confined to a wheel chair for the rest of his life when a dangerous stunt went wrong, decided to run his own promotion. Things seemed to be very promising for the up-start promotion. That was until Adam Morgan ran the company's first televised event. E!, the channel that has brought television viewers controversial shows as "The Howard Stern Show" and "The Anna Nichole Show." Wasn't too happy when on of Adam's workers, William Banks (known to the wrestling world as "Uncensored"), used many highly profane words over and over again despite the prewritten script. E!'s punishment to the up-start company was canceling T-nW's "Rising Action" and not making their other show, "Top-notch Television" live. In response, T-nW have William Banks a fine of $5,000.00, and threatened to do so over and over until William would finally decide to obey. To no surprise of anyone, William Banks continued is profane ways at the next show taping. Once this happened E! decided to no longer support the company, and canceled "Top-notch Television." Many thought this would have been the final blow to the company. However, Adam Morgan was determined to get the company off of the ground. So he continued to take tours throughout Europe and the United States. The fans kept on tuning in even with the high ticket prices and the fact that they no longer had the chance to be shown on TV. The word of mouth, which is the best form of advertisement, started to spread in the continental United States and Europe. It would only be a matter of time before a television channel would be in Adam's ear. Sure enough, the offers were through the roof! Stations such as USA, HBO, FOX, and others were offering live primetime deals for Adam's Top-notch Television. Adam has said to have made up his mind about which channel he will decide to go with. However, he has not released this information. As for William Banks' uncensored-ness, the channel that Adam decided to go with reportedly will have no problem with William's profane words. Adam decided that the Top-notch Action will re-begin June 30th, and the "first" Top-notch Television will be on July 8th. Top-notch Wrestling
