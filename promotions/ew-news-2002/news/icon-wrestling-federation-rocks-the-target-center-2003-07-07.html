Icon Wrestling Federation rocks the Target Center!

7 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
IWF was the host of Sunday Night Lightning in Minneapolis, Minnesota! This sunday night, the Icon Wrestling Federation held it's flagship show, Sunday Night Lightning live in the Target Center in Minneapolis, Minnesota. Early on, we saw IWF Rookie, Mac McCullough make a hell of an impact when he wrestled and won his debut match against the 330 pound, Fat Man. Many were impressed with the young McCullough and is said to possibly be a great asset to the company down the road. The IWF's own Hardcore God, Pure Hell did not arrive to the Target Center for his tag team match on sunday night. As a result, Pure Hell's partner, Marco was forced to wrestle in a handicap match against The Icons, Ace and John Mchugh. Marco put up a good fight against The Icons until the Icons were forced to result to their cheating ways to pull out a victory. In a "champion versus champion" match, the IWF Internet Champion, The Red Freak went one on one with IWF Suicidal Champion, Matt Stryker. Both have been red hot as of late but after an exciting match, it seemed as if Internet Champ, The Red Freak had the edge in this non-title contest. After defeating Matt Stryker, it seems as if The Red Freak may be ready to take the next step towards superstardom. Speaking of superstardom, the young Rising Star Champion, Scott Clayton will be headlining the IWF Armageddon Pay Per View set to take place on July 20th when he faces RPG for the vacant "Icon" Championship belt. This sunday night however, Scott Clayton put his Rising Star Championship on the line against IWF Legend, Mr. Rich. After an up and down battle, it was Scott Clayton that pulled out a victory over Mr. Rich. Scott Clayton seems to be showing the world that he is ready to take on RPG. In a match booked by IWF Commissioner, Blaise Martelle......Chazz Mendel took on Clu in a Tuxedo Match. Chazz Mendel took the smart way out of this one by wearing a snow suit over his tuxedo. Clu held his own with Chazz Mendel but Chazz simply had too much clothing. In the end, it was Chazz Mendel who ended up winning the match stripping his opponent down to his boxer shorts. After the match, Blaise Martelle sent her goons, Marco and Powerhouse down to ringside to take out Clu and Chazz Mendel. Blaise was spreading the message that she didn't like to be "f**ked around" with. Blaise Martelle also booked a match for next week's Lightning in which Marco will take on Clu in a No Disqualifications match with Blaise as the special guest referee......and she also booked a match for next week in which Chazz Mendel will battle Mr. Rich with Chazz's enemy, SyN as the special guest referee! It will be interesting to see what transpires on next week's show! But back to the show, RPG took on Drake Laps next in what was Drake's Return match in the Icon Wrestling Federation. After attacking RPG on Lightning last week, Drake knew that the odds were stacked against him in this one. But in the end, the Highflying Miracle, Drake Laps was able to overcome those odds and get a pinfall victory over the man who could very well be our next "Icon" Champion, RPG! This time around, if Drake is pulling off victories like these, he's going to end up going places. In the night's Main Event, Powerhouse took on SyN in a Last Man standing match. Both men known for their extreme fighting style made this match an exciting one to watch. But the tide shifted when IWF World Champion, Chazz Mendel made his way down to ringside. Chazz Mendel hit SyN in the head with a rubber chicken with a brick inside of it! SyN was then counted out and Powerhouse was declared the winner. Shortly after the match, Marco came down to the ring to make his mark as he attacked both Chazz Mendel and SyN....and left them in pools of their own blood. But the carnage didn't stop there, after IWF Officials drove Marco away from ringside, The Red Freak ran down to the ring and viciously attacked Powerhouse! The Red Freak made an all-out attack on Powerhouse's ankle as he nearly shattered it after trapping P-House's ankle in a steel chair and then breaking a cinder block on the chair. What the world wants to know now, what were the intentions of Marco and The Red Freak? Will Chazz Mendel, SyN and Powerhouse be able to compete next week on Lightning? All in all, this week's Lightning was an exciting program to watch on the TNT Network. But then again, it always has been. Icon Wrestling Federation
