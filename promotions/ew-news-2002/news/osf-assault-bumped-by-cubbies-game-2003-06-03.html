OSF Assault bumped by Cubbies Game

3 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Assault Postponed Only the third week into the program, OSF's Assault has hit a snag. Carlos Vierra released this offical statement earlier this afternoon. Old School Fighting: Assault "Der will be no Assault dis night. We have had to push da show back a night as Da Cubbies have got da time slot." OSE's Broadcast rights were sold earlier this month when the decision to host the majority of their events from the recently purchased OSE Arena in Chicago. WGN Channel 9 in Chicago purchased the rights airing both Slam and Assault on the local channel as well as the nationally broadcast WGN Superstation. WGN of course has a lot of local sports coverage and due to the short nature of the signing this weeks Assault has been bumped back. When asked about the short notification, WGN personal replied that it was a case of human error and it won't happen again. Assault is slated to air late Wednesday night after the movie "Outbreak" replacing the nightly news. It will also air encore editions at 10:00 am and once again at 7:00 pm on Thursday Night.
