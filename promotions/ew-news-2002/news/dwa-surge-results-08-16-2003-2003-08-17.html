DWA: Surge Results 08/16/2003

17 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With just a week left until the pay per view, Downward Spiral, the Fatal Fourway Match for the Title began on Surge. Alexandria Simmons, the DWA ratings consultant and girlfriend of Owner Jordan Brannigan, was in placed in charged tonight by Brannigan himself, as we learned that Brannigan took an unexpected trip to Japan. With Alexandria in charge until the pay per view, anything can happen, and she seems to be planning just that. Lupus Feral vs. EZ Target --After a hard fought match between the two, EZ Target reversed Lupus's own Feral Throwback, into his own EZ Does It, to pick up the one, two, three for the victory. Ninja X, who was told last week by Alexandria, that if he did not impress her by the pay per view, he would be fired, stormed her office, demanding an audience with Brannigan. Unfortunately, with Brannigan being gone, he had no one to talk with. However, Alexandria gave him the chance to impress her by putting him in a match with Gideon Nazareth, the man guaranteed a World Title shot at Downward Spiral, on Showdown. He accepted, but vowed to talk this over with Brannigan. The main event was the first round of the Fatal Fourway for the Title. The rules are simple. The four wrestlers involved in this match will face each other until one is eliminated. Then they move on to Showdown, as a triple threat match, where they will face each other until another is eliminated. The final two will face at the pay per view, where the winner of that match will take on Gideon Nazareth, and the winner of that final match will be crowned the Undisputed World Heavyweight Championship. Alex Grey vs. Donnie Darko vs. Brad Keidis vs. Typhoid Mary --This was a huge match that pitted the very best against each other. In the end though, Alex Grey nails Brad Keidis with A Lesson in Payne, and pins him for the 1, 2, 3, eliminating the TV Champion. That leaves Grey, Darko, and Mary for the triple threat match on Showdown. After the match, Grey cleared the ring of both Darko and Mary, throwing them viciously to the outside of the ring. And that was where the show ended, with Grey the only man left in the ring. DWA: Dragon Wrestling Association
