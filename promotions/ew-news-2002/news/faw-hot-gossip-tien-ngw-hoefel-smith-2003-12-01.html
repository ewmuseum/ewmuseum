FAW Hot Gossip: Tien, NGW, Hoefel, Smith

1 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This week's Hot Gossip features Lisa Tien, Next Generation Wrestling, Hardcore Hoefel and the "Lee-gend" Lee Smith! Well, what a week-! A week that started with the controversy of the transcript release of the November 24th epidose of Panic ended with Furiously Addictive Wrestling superstars raging a media war with Next Generation Wrestling. Hardcore Hoefel and Emilio Dervante of NGW are set to end their war of words with a war of attrition on NGW Tuesday Storm. But Hardcore Hoefel isn't the only superstar from FAW trading blows with an NGW star. The "Lee-gend" Lee Smith's confrontations with Damien Ryan could lead to a match on Panic sometime in the near future. Damien Ryan was once a FAW superstar but his relationship with FAW has been well documented in recent weeks. And last but not least, a week that saw Lisa Tien accusing Orlando Rivera and our World Heavyweight Champion Violator of giving the investigation into the shooting of Tony Vigilante nothing. And she wasn't finished there when she accused them of holding back the truth. Do Rivera and Violator really hold the missing evidence to leading to the villain? FAW: Furiously Addictive Wrestling
