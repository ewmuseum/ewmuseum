KSZ reviews Pressure Point 80 with RECKLESS ABANDON~!

6 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
KSZ Pressure Point analysis 10/27/03. Date Aired (Taped): 29.10.03 (27.10.03) Number: 80 Location: Cow Palace, San Francisco Posted by Keith Scott Zimmerman Sorry I'm not stirring the pot on the whole "should the Sharc/Levine" ended the show thing. I'm on the biggest roll of my life, let alone my career. Duel hasn't done anything since the word recall was applied to faulty strollers, and Levine is out of here at the end of the month. So what do I care? Show started proper with a Sharc/White Villam promo. You know who doesn't like each other is 101 and the Dudes With Attitudes. Good stuff, but sort of odd that we were all in costume and threatening bloody reign on each other. Some THING happened with one of the Legacies. The girls are still in love. 1. Bret Halloway beat Carson Nash. Nash began to rail when he got interrupted. The new ref screwed up by giving a 10 instead of a 5 count off a choke. Kudos and other snack bars to Nash for following up with a sleeper, one of the rare spots in which I've seen that old relic hold make sense. Crowd popped for the new kid's moonsault dropkick, and they traded Eddy spots with Bret missing the frog splash and a nearfall off of Splash Mountain. When in doubt, Eddy it up. Nash took out the ref and then hit the Vertebreaker. Smart. Halloway recovered, chaired him, and Lionsaulted his way to victory. I don't know why people debuting with feds are starting to run "losing streak to adapt" angles. At least for Carson's sake I hope this is an angle. Dictionary definition of decent opener. (**) Segments! Daze & Case kept scrapping, this time through the catering table. Adam Nowell made his debut by bouncing off the Dangers' attire, and may or may not be evil. 2. Sabotage beat Arcade & Son. The Survivors busted out the Device. Really well-worked opening, slowly building to a first two count off said Device. Duncan showed up at this point, drawing the proper amount of non-match-deciding attention from the participants. Back and forth between the older members and Julius busted out the dragon sleeper for the rest spot. Arcbreaker leads to hot tags but Logan hits semi-rolling dropkicks to completely nullify the momentum Sanford built up. Tim hit the Norristown Hangover and Donell the Drop for nearfalls. Donell foolishly looked at Bradley and he's not a crook, son; he's just a shook one. Namely a Shook Down one, and the ex-champs go down. Great match, which even better in my eyes destroys any Arcade complaints about where their rematch is. (***¼) More segments! The Dangerettes promised to turn it around; but they said on camera so they're doomed. The Arcades stewed. I electrified the crowd with my "freestyling"; this rap I did here was the one I prewrote the week leading up to, and Max refused to give me my shot so FU. *shoulder shrug* It's O....K! 3. The Legacy beat Honor & Glory. The girls tried alternating between quick and slow to get Ramsey off-balance, but it took a while to work. Conrad kept the crowd in it, thankfully. They hit some crazy-ass spike piledriver with a flip or a springboard or something. Crazy bitches, and I mean that in the most glowing of terms. Anyway the momentum shifted so greatly Conrad had to tag out after a double eye poke. Two consecutive M's in Zimmerman for the check, old man; don't make me make Reed garnish your Social Security. Anyway, hot tag, Jizz Cutter for a nearfall. He immediately follows it up with Mountain Dew 2.0 but X breaks it up with the hyperpsycho Perro Aguayo. The girls tried to clear house again but Bradley STAN HANSENED THAT SHIT DOWN IN STEREO. Pier 4 leads to assissted Sliced Bread #2 for 2. YIKES. Brad goes back to the Code Red but the girls actually win finally with X hitting a 450 senton bomb. So it's offical: the Dangerettes are no more. They are just the Armstrong Brothers after reconstructive surgery. Match ruled, but you knew that. (****) Son of more segments! Daze's revenge, via car door on HardCase's head. TV & Homer Reedson did stuff. 4. Jeff Garvin made Bobby Minio submit to retain the ORIGINAL Action Title. To help Bobby get back his heat, Jeff took an immediate and violent Low-Ki asswhomping to start. Bobby took a couple of dragon screws but stayed ahead with a brainbuster. Bobby hits a lionsault but misses my triple jump quebrada you fuckface and Jeff rightfully smites him with a swinging STO. STOMP. Side Russian into the corner, superplex, Wild Bomb, running Wild Bomb, but Minio shakes it all off. Really thought that was the finish. Bobby came back with the Raven Effect and Vegas Hangover (running gag tonight) but Jeff snapped on the Jammer for the tap out. Cool beans. Can we get Bobby something to do assuming he shows up for consecutive weeks? (***) Bride of the son of more segments! The challengers prepared to die. 5. Trey Vincent and Kirk Angel did things. I didn't care. (**1/2) Daughter of the bride of the s...ah, fuck it. Ran into Sabotage, Daze/Case for the C in an undetermined stipulation match at the PPV. 6. The International Workrate Connection beat Coral Avalon and Max Danger to retain the Dyad Championships. Faces started off with stereo running handless planchas. Vegomatic by us, but Max quickly gets ahead of me. They fended off our Device, but got some of the old school RnR goodness. Stolen Danger hit a wheelbarrow bomb facebuster for 2, and then Lindum accidentally got KOed by Pierre. Delacroix Device suicida but Max posts me a few times, only drawing two for Coral. Kleptosault for 2, and I shoryuken me way to a tag. Max gets in and puts Code Red on but I break it up. K-bradora and me & Pierre hit rolling germans into the Derailer (no, really) for a nearfall. Full-on Falcon Arrow for 2, and then we do the old Mounties finish for 2 since that's how Pierre dressed up. Danger takes me out with a German on the floor and they double-team Pierre with the Curveball and sandwich springboard dropkicks, but we didn't win these belts and Pierre didn't become a demigod by reaching into a Cracker Jack box. Hyper 3D from us to Coral, tornado bulldog facebuster on Max. Time Bomb & Benoit on Coral, but Max Eddys the save. Bah. I take out Max with the OLD SCHOOL F1, Coral takes me out with the leaping Regal, then Ratings Spike on Pierre. Crowd totally bit on that being the finish. Dangerbuster & RVD moonsault on Pierre, Wild Dazed and Confused Bomb on me but Pierre saves the silver. Sellout and sandwich Zimmerman CIs on me, but this is my hometown and you'll have to shoot me. Me & Max Roaring Elbow each other out of the picture. Intermission. :) Superkick enhanced OLD SCHOOL II ELECTRIC BOOGALOO with the Bottom Line (Spanky who? London what?) Coral cuts off my triple jump quebrada and I get hit with a stacked Sliced Bread #2. OW. Frenchie gets taken out with the MDK bomb to the floor, allowing Max to trip into my steel chain. Whoops. Hey, a 3 count! (****½) Then I beat Max about the face with Black Steel In the Hour of Chaos, and there was much rejoicing. Not quite Public Enemy #1, at least not here. And finally... 7. Raven Junior beat Sharc to be a complete and total anti-climax after the awesomeness I brought to the table, and in his complete idiocy failed to even get anything out of Sharc. To quote a great philosopher of the 20th century, "What a maroon." (***) MATCH OF THE NIGHT: 6. 2 thru 4 were also superior to the average and PPV-worthy. BEST SEGMENT: The part where everyone was laughing at Max. FUNNIEST SEGMENT: Nothing struck me as hilarious, though I sort of like the Grosse Pointe Blank vibe from the C Title segs. WRESTLER OF THE NIGHT: I'll give it up to Sabotage for making me care about and actually like an Arcades match. WRESTLER WITH THE MOST MOMENTUM: *points* *to* *self* This is Memorex AND it's live. HOTTEST FUED: Wrestling 101 vs. the Adult Swim Power Rangers. UNDERPERFORMER OF THE NIGHT: Rook. Late on the Crouching Tiger Hidden Dragon deal and he didn't even wrestle. BOO. FLATTEST SEGMENT OF THE NIGHT: None. Consider yourselves lucky. No, wait: the Legacy segments seemed slightly extraneous and unnecessary, but that's a minor nitpick. LINE OF THE NIGHT: "Shut the fuck up, you lumpy-faced asshole." Action! Pressure Point 80
