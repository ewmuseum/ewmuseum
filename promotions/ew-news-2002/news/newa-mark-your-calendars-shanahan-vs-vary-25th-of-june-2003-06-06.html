[NeWA] Mark your Calendars! Shanahan vs. Vary - 25th of June

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ricochet Wrestling's [RW] management called in a Press-Conference today to reveal the fight-night between "Simply Superior" Trent Shanahan and Vic Vary. Also... VV2 Profile: Lance Panzer! Trent Shanahan & Vic Vary - Fightnight of the 25th! Stay tuned! The wait is finally over. Yesterday [June 5th] the RW management gathered in their offices in Sheffield, England. After a short formal meeting to approve Shanahan's title-shot - RW Head-Honcho's decided to call a Press-Conferece. Television Teams from Ohio and Louisiana also showed up for the formal announcement of the match between "Double-S" Trent Shanahan and NeWA World Heavyweight Champion. The conference was very festive. The President of RW finally announced the long-waited match. "Mark your calendars, gentleman! The 25th of June is our triumph night. We invite one, we invite all to join us in a special Wednesday Night Crossfire. Join us in what maybe the crowning of a new NeWA World Heavyweight Championship." Said RW President in a rare and welcoming statement. Richochet Wrestling still preparing their *special* Vic Vary Invitational. The NeWA is still gushing over the announcement that *both* Shanahan and Vary would be a part of Ring Number 4. The RW expects those two Crossfires to be the main Fan-Targets in the next couple of weeks. RW are hoping that the night of the 25th would be their most financial productive night in their Region's history. Also... According the rumors ESPN are still in negotiation with the NeWA management regarding inking a Television Deal. Rumors say that the NeWA have been offered air-time in ESPN2. NeWA have yet to reply the offer. In anycase, ESPN is expected to bring you the fightnight of the 25th - Trent Shanahan vs. Vic Vary. Until next time... National Wrestling Alliance
