OSW SLAM! Recap.

9 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Like the official preview reads: "Much like the Slam two weeks ago where a new champion was crowned, this event will be remembered for what WASN'T planned." And that it will. Each week, Slam! seemingly grows by leaps and bounds, and this was no exception. In the first match of the night, Teresa Tomas went up against Rhett Locke. Of course, with Teresa Tomas around, you know Tessa Windsor can't be far behind. Was she able to keep herself out of Teresa's business? I wouldn't go staking the farm on that bet. A couple of gate crashers from OSF joined the wrestlers on Slam! and you can bet that it wasn't taken very kindly to. By one man, in particular. An impromptu match took place because of it, and you wouldn't believe who was being cheered by the fans for sticking up for OSW. Kraig Carter and M2 faced off with the team of Jack Murphy and Silvio Rochelli, Tempest's "Soul Subjects". Well, actually Kraig Carter took on Murphy and Rochelli, as M2 was taken out of the equation earlier on in the night. For the American Heavyweight Title, Jeffrey Wayne defended against Darkstar. Recently, Darkstar hasn't been fairing too well, and Wayne has had his problems with Hoyt Williams. Would Williams make his presence known, or was this a fair one-on-one match? Power Play - specifically Cassius Deamont, Chris Matthews, and Jude Kensington - took on EDH and Asa Fountain. EDH has had his problems with each of the men in Power Play since they ejected both himself and Dave Gibson from the group. Now Hurst has found an ally in Asa Fountain to combat the three men, but could even the two of them overcome the odds? And in the main event, The Red Army squared off with The Asylum. The Red Army consisted of Ivan Stanislav, Boda, Alexei Ruslan, and Politically Incorrect. On the other side of the coin, The Asylum was made up of Miyagawa, P.W.A., Xavier Kannon, and the World Champion, Six Finger Cisco. For one night only, The Asylum was in full force, and the match was one that will be remembered for years to come. Not only that, but the ending of Slam! may be remembered even longer. All that, as well as Freakachus, Angelus Jones, Tempest and her followers, Hoyt Williams, a returning OSW star, and much, much more! SLAM!
