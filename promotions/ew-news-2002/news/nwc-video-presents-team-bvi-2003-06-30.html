NWC:Video Presents Team BVI

30 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NWC:Video presents video footage of the struggles against Team BVI. Is there any hope? [The images are in black and white the have a grainy texture to them. They are from the National Wrestling Council’s Biggest Pay Per View of the year, the Granddaddy of them all, Across Enemy Lines.] [Samuel Barbers ‘Adagio for Strings’ plays.] “I Remember the Beginning…” [A voice can be heard.] “I saw an angel rise…” [Lance Marshall with a running Power bomb on Anton Dufresne into the corner turnbuckles, trying to drag him towards the centre of the ring Marshall collapses upon Dufresne and becomes the new World Champion.] “I felt a great evil at work…” [Damon Hayes. Returned as a monster, a physique of mountains of muscle upon muscle. The old NWC World Title around his waist.] “I saw a great star fall from heavens…” [Hayes with Marshall high above his head, then dropping him across his knee, the sound of thunder rumbling in the background. Adam Calloway striking Marshall in the ribs with a golf club. Ben Van Iten shaking Damon’s hand and then pulling him in for an enthusiastic hug.] “But still I have hope…” [“The Foundation” Tony Pride giving Hayes a Superstar kick sending him out of the ring. Dread Saxon nailing Sean Laughreah with a baseball bat.] [Together Arch Enemies Eugene Robinson and Tony Pride disposing of “the Sexiest Man Alive” Sexy Steve Sire.] [Lance Marshall, bruised and battered, ripping Anton Dufresne off of his wife Alanna and then showing super human strength to press Dufresne high above head and throw him out of the ring.] “But is hope enough…” [Lance Marshall stands almost for a second emitting a visible aura of invincibility, he closes his eyes, stumbling slowly, falling and crashing to the mat.] [The music calms. As a throng gathers around him.] [Slowly darkness slices through picture and we have silence.] “Where are our Heroes?” [Images of Heroes from the past, some bloodied, some during moments of victory, some in moments of pure rage, Peerless Hunter Sabuani, GD West, White Tiger, Alexander, Devo Tremors…] [A new image arises.] [The White pants and sandals, the floral Hawaiian shirt, That coy smirk, those evil eyes, that sharp tongue and all the power in the world at his finger tips.] [Ben Van Iten.] “Who will stop this?” [Quick interchanging flashes violence and mayhem from the hands of his troops in his personal war to destroy the National Wrestling Council.] [Sean Laughreah, Jade and Karnage.] [Jeremy Thomas, “Nine Iron Justice” Adam Calloway, “The Dead Perfect Ripper” Derrick Daliente, “Ill Town” Leonard Aarons.] [“Sexy” Steve Sire, “The Anthesis” Anton Dufresne, “The Sheriff” Damon Hayes, “The Flawless One” Raymond Pierce and finally “Bermuda” Billy Page holding the title aloft.] [But back to the tasteless floral Hawaiian shirt, that smirk and those eyes he finishes this all himself.] "Even God himself can't deliver you from this…" National Wrestling Council
