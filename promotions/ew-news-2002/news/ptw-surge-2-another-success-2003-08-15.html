PTW Surge #2....another success?

15 August 2003

Well the second card since the PTW reopened and all has seemed very well. The ratings... Well the second card since the PTW reopened and all has seemed very well. The ratings have came out and it seems the show scored out of 5 for the first hour a 3.4 As for the second hour, Surge get's a big 4.0 on the rictor scale! The event was a success with the two females Cass Blackfire and Vanity debuting on the card and both pulling off amazing appearances in both matches. Also Dark got some good news, he still get's his World Title shot and after beating Lance Romance he get's to pick his opponent? Was Slugger on crack or something? Speaking of Slugger as well, he managed to get himself into a big mess on Surge....we won't tell you how but take a look and see how is daughter really treated him on Surge! Below is the lineup as well in case you are interested: Singles Matchup Dark Angel vs Revolt Singles Matchup Scarface vs Nate Bromont Singles Matchup Austin Douglas vs Cass Blackfire Singles Matchup Vanity vs Coyote Singles Matchup Lance Romance vs Dark Main Event: Singles Matchup "Mediocre" Mark Johnson vs The Great Tsunami Tune into the website for more and also EW-News.Com for leads and updates on this story!
