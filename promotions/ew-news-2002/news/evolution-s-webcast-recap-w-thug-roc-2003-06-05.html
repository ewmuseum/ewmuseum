eVolution's Webcast Recap w/Thug roC

5 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This was sent in from fan, Jimmy Figgins: Hey guys, I just got done hearing the Thug roC webcast and this is what he had to say. -He started by thanking the fans on staying tuned to eVolution this past year and how he is happy to be with such an organization. It pleases him to finally find a fed where he is comfortable and feels that he is a piece to the puzzle. -He touched on the front office of eVo, saying that they truly are a well knit group. Started talking about the fundamentals of any organization, said that eVo had all of them. He commented on $W a bit and said that even though it was a great fed to watch as a fan, it could sometimes be hell dealing with the people that were there. -A caller called in and asked about his nose that was recently broken at From the Ashes. The fan asked if it was legit or just a spot. Thug roC said that it was indeed a real injury and that his nose is indeed broken. We then saw a clip of the Perfection move that roC felt at From the Ashes and he said that like most moves in a match, it doesn’t hurt that much because of the adrenaline rush, but it hurt like hell afterwards. -He commented on politics a bit, and said that even though most of eVo wrestlers look out for others just as much as they look out for themselves; there are wrestlers that only want the win. Said wrestlers that only want the big win and get their name in lights make the federation look bad, and make others refuse to work with them, therefore it dampens the spirit of the backstage area. -Said he enjoyed his time off of wrestling between December and March. It’s been a while since he had time off of wrestling jumping from fed to fed until he found a home. -When asked about his physical health, he said that he isn’t the guy he use to be. He asked the listeners rhetorically, why people would criticize him for not wanting to work too many matches? He said that as a wrestler, there’s a lot of pain and permanent injury you must go through, and different guys age different ways, and that’s just how it is. -Also stated that eVo will most likely be his final in ring performance. Although opportunities lie within PRIME wrestling where he knows a lot of guys, he doesn’t think he has what it takes to do it all over one more time. -He closed off by promising the fans that if they stay tuned to eVo, that they will get the best in the business, and that’s himself. Overall I enjoyed the webcast, although it could’ve been better. I do look forward to seeing other wrestlers on the eVo webcast in the future too. eVolution
