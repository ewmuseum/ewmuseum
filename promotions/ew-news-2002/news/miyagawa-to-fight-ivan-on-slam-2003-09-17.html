Miyagawa To Fight Ivan on Slam!

17 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Two former PCW World Champions go face to face for the first time in 14 months this Sunday night! Following his loss of the OSW American Championship last week to Jeffrey Wayne, Ivan Stanislav immediatly petitioned for a match against Alexander Miyagawa. His reason? Well he hasn't exactly given one. Could it be that he's finally wanting to get his own back on Miyagawa, a man who he hasn't fought for fourteen months or so, and was his final PPV opponent in PCW? Maybe it's because he wants to kick the living hell out of a puny Cruiserweight and Miya's name came out of the hat? Or perhaps it could be that Ivan is going to be annoucing that Miyagawa was the one who kidnapped Jane Reagan this Sunday Night and wants to get his own brand of revenge on the man who placed the blame squarely at the big Russian's feet? Perhaps... well, Miyagawa's wife is Russian. She's also getting back to him, and it's well known that Ivan had some problems with that marriage when he was in PCW, so maybe... just maybe... he really wants to split those two back up yet again. Of course, not much has been said for Miyagawa's part in this match up. It's his first Main Event matchup since arriving back in OSW several weeks ago. In fact, it's his first Main Event matchup since leaving PCW. Miyagawa however did suffer an immense beating at the hands of Ivan's stable partner and World Champion, Chris 'Boda' S., last week on Slam! and isn't in a really properly fit state to compete, however he was reached for comment and he had the following to say: "Fit to compete? Let's face it, I'm going into a ring with a guy who's over a foot tallker and a hundred and ninety pounds heavier. If I'm already coughing up blood, it just saves him some time. Ivan's got his reasons for demanding this match against me, and with this whole Jane Reagan thing going on, if I refuse the match, it'll be like an admission of guilt." When quizzed on the whole Jane Reagan affair he simply stated the following: "Why would I bother? I wasn't even in OSW when it happened!" Old School Entertainment
