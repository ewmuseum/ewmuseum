HSW: Interview with Headstrong Champion Assassin

10 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On Tuesday Evening, HSW Reporter Sean Armstrong had a chance to sit down and speak to the new Headstrong Champion, Assassin. Here is what went down! Sean Armstrong: Assassin, thanks for taking time out of your busy schedule to sit down with me. Assassin: No problem, Sean, the pleasure is all mine. Sean Armstrong: First off, congratulations on your big win this past Saturday in Wichita. Assassin: FIRST OFF, where are the cookies you promised me? Sean reaches back over to another table, and grabs a brown box, he hands it to Assassin. Assassin smiles as he opens the box, and stuffs his mouth full of cookies. Sean Armstrong: I baked them myself. Assassin looks up, wide eyed at Sean. As Sean looks down at his notebook to read the first question, Assassin takes the ball of cookie out of his mouth, and throws it over his shoulder. He picks up the box, and throws that too. Sean looks up at Assassin. Sean Armstrong: Wow, you mustve really liked those cookies, you're done them already! Assassin: Uh.. yeah! Sure, whatever. Sean Armstrong: Well, let's get down to business. Did you ever imagine that you would be at the top of Headstrong Wrestling so quickly into your career here? Assassin: Well, Sean. I've always had dreams of holding a main event title in a large promotion. I never thought it would've happened in the biggest of the big. Headstrong wrestling has to be full of the youngest, and best talent of anywhere I've seen and been. Headstrong Wrestling is the "main event" of all federations, amd for me to win it's biggest title, is just unbelievable. Sean Armstrong: Obviously you did very well for yourself last Saturday. What was your strategy going into the match, and who were you targetting the most? Assassin: To tell the truth, I didn't really have a strategy. Going into the match, All I wanted to do was win a title. It would've been a huge shock to me, just winning the Evolution title! But now that I'm the Headstrong champion, I'm on top of the world. You can't believe the feeling. Sean Armstrong: And who exactly were you targetting? Assassin: The feeling is amazing! You have fans everywhere who want your autograph! You have these beautiful women, getting naked and... Sean Armstrong: HELLO?! My Question! Assassin: What question? Sean Armstrong sighs. He looks down at his notepad. Assassin: Just kidding with you, man. When I stepped into that ring on Saturday, I had two men in the crosshair. First off, Damian Bartlett. The punk thought he could pump up the crowd with a cheesy rap? What was he thinking? Everyone knows that Dysfunctional is the greatest tag team in the history of wrestling, and come on, who wouldn't love to see us throwing one guy over the rope after the other. Whooping EVERYONES ass! Now that's what I call entertainment. Assassin grabs a bottle of water from a nearby spectator. He takes a drink, and spits it out. Assassin - It's warm! What are you thinking giving me warm water? Man- But, you said you liked it warm.. Assassin - Get out of here! Where was I? Oh yeah. Tyler McPherson. After I was done throwing D-B over the top, and maybe findling Rachel a little, he was next on my list. He thought he could push me around on camera? He thought he could take Assassin one on one? Well, he proved himself right. He put up one hell of a fight, but I guess experience and proper training overtake all else.When you're trained by the best around, Stu Hart, you know you've got what it takes to win matches. Sean Armstrong: Well, let's dig a little deeper into what you think of Damian Bartlett. D-B tore up your car all because of what you did earlier in the week..I actually think he took it a little far, but what are your thoughts on what he did? Assassin: What that idiot did on Shocker, went way too far! And you get bet your shiny penny, I'll get revenge on him for that. Sean Armstrong: Well, this Saturday you may get some revenge on D-B, as you take him on in a singles match. Do you think you can take him? Headstrong Wrestling
