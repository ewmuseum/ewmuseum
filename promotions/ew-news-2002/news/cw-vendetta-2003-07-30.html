cW: Vendetta

30 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On August 3, 2003, Core Wrestling will be coming to you live from Cleveland, OH, with its third Pay Per View. On August 3, 2003, Core Wrestling will be coming to you live from Cleveland, OH, with its third Pay Per View extravaganza, Eugene FitzPatrick Presents: Vendetta. Opening Bout Nicholas Creed vs. Shawn Cable vs. Ke’Anen Luciman Three relatively newly signed talents in Core Wrestling open Vendetta up in an explosive Triple Threat Match. All three of these superstars have the talent to be future champions. DNA Test Match Alura versus Artemis If Artemis wins the match, Alura is forced to take a blood test to prove she is in fact Matthew Lanier’s ex-fiancé Laura Brown. Brown was manipulated by Eugene FitzPatrick into not marrying him with the help of former cW Star, Quinn Masters. Lanier was then framed with the murder of Laura Brown and spent the majority of two months in prison while FitzPatrick ran his company to the ground. Classic Championship – Tables, Ladders, and Chairs Sidarian [c] versus AWOL AWOL of the British Humour Squad, along with Fred Fusion has repeatedly played childish pranks to Sidarian and his partner Jantz Spalding. Sidarian has obviously had enough and the two will face off in a TLC Match for the Classic Championship. Tag Team Championship Dark Hearts [c] versus The Diamond Duo This match came about due to the result of a House Show. Aryim and Nos fought in a singles match that turned into a big brawl between the two teams. So Aryim and Jack Diamond of the Diamond Duo take on Nos and Fenix of Dark Hearts. Icon Championship – Cage Match J-Sin Knight [c] versus Genocide Genocide steps up to the plate for Knight to defend his Icon Championship. Knight has had the longest title reign of any title since winning it and there just doesn’t seem to be anyone who will be able to take it from him. Genocide takes his chances in a Cage Match for the Icon Championship First Main Event – Knock Out Match Eugene FitzPatrick versus “Death’s Angyl” Daniel Adams FitzPatrick has continues to try and make Adam’s life a living hell week after week. The 17 time world champion laces his boots for the third week in a row to take on Daniel Adams in a Knock Out Match. Can the 50+ year old man knock out Adams in his prime? Second Main Event – Legacy Championship Jantz Spalding [c] versus Fred Fusion For the second of the two main events for the night, we have the epic battle, Fusion versus Spalding 2. The two met at the first PPV in a ladder match for the Legacy Championship and Fred Fusion was able to win the title from Spalding. Can Spalding hold on to it again or will Fusion take it again like last time? Core Wrestling
