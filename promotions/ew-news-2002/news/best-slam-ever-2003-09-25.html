Best SLAM! Ever?

25 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What many people are calling the best SLAM! to be seen in quite a while hit the airs Sunday, and now we bring you the quick recap of just what went down that very night. September 21st was a big night for many people. Anyone involved in that night went down in history, as what many call the best SLAM! to come around in a while went on the air and proved itself to be a memorable show. Just how memorable? Well, you be the judge. Teresa Tomas went head-to-head with newly-arrived James Edwards. Of course, the match didn't go quite how we expected. Why is that? If I told you, it wouldn't be any fun, would it? The man who brutally attacked Kraig Carter last week went up against Xavier Kannon. Kannon can't possibly be 100%, and with Speedy Riggs reffing the match, did XK have enough in the tank to overcome the odds and score a victory over Jack Murphy, or did The Bull run right through him? The Solution had the chance of a lifetime as they faced off with William S. Powell and Asa Fountain. Powell recently turned on Fountain, now neither man can trust the other. But they have no choice in the ring, as Deamont and Matthews are truly a force to be reckoned with. Did Power Play members walk out with the titles on Sunday? EDH and Matt Register kicked it up a notch in one hell of a match. Register has the Power Play on his side, but Hurst has revenge on his mind. Could Register defeat The Exceptional One, or did EDH walk out with a win? Last week, Ivan Stanislav lost his American Title to Jeffrey Wayne. This week, he looked to put the nail in the coffin of Miyagawa. Did the angry Russian Bear finish him off...or was there more left in Miyagawa than anyone expected? And finally, in what people are calling a MOTYC, Six Finger Cisco managed to obtain a contract to face Boda for his World Title. Boda wasn't too pleased, but with no other choice, he battled Cisco in the main event. After possibly the greatest match these two have fought in together, did the smoke clear to show we had a new World Champion, or was Boda the one standing tall? Also, Stanislav fingers a suspect, Hoyt Williams makes an impact, Grant Hill appears on OSW tv, and much, much more! http://www.oseentertainment.com/slam/slamresults.shtml
