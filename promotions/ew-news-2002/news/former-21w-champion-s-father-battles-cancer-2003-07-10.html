Former 21w Champion's Father Battles Cancer

10 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Bob Carter, the father and trainer of former 21w World Champion, Ace Carter, has confessed to a long standing battle with cancer. On June 8th, 2001, at 21st Century Wrestling's first pay-per-view, 20 year old rookie Ace Carter defeated the legendary 'Golden' Glen Miller to become one of the youngest and quickest World Heavyweight Champions of all time. How rapidly things have turned around for the Carter family. Carter has since been forced into retirement due to a long term leg injury and to add to the disappointment his father and trainer, Bob, yesterday came out and publicly admitted to having struggled with kidney cancer for four months. I recently caught up with long standing 21w superstar, Coby Wright, for his thoughts on this upsetting news. These were his words: "In the wake of this unfortunate news and on behalf of everyone at 21st Century Wrestling, I would like to send out my deepest condolences to the Carter family." ...When asked to comment on the relationship between Ace and his father, Mr Wright had this to say: "Bob's stern approach towards getting his son to the top has been well documented. During the opening months of 21w he would be at Ace's side almost every day, constantly talking things over. The two also spent more hours in the gym than anyone else on the roster. The week after 21w Mortal Mayhem, when Ace became World Champion, he told me of how he resented some of the things his father had tried to impose upon him as a child and that he was continuing to do so. My response was that hopefully one day he'll be able to look back on what he's achieved and realise just how much his father did for him...and that he'll be thankful for that." On the subject of 21w's return to television, I am unfortunately not permitted to disclose that information as of now. All I can say is, keep your ears to the grindstone. -Hank 21st Century Wrestling
