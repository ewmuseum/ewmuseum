TV Network issue warning to The X

2 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Broadcaster of The X's weekly show, After Dark, asks for a "tone down" The UK television channel, Bravo, are asking the producers of its 3am – 5am slot filler on Sunday mornings, The X, to tone down the violence. Bravo, who are known for their raunchy and violent programming, think that the two hour wrestling show “After Dark” is just “too gory”. A representative from Bravo had this to say: “The X have guys slicing each other to pieces, choking the daylights out of others and just about killing everyone in their bloody way. Guys like Vex, Gila, Ryuji Ishikawa and Cordozer Stevenson need to just learn to be not so hell-bent on making their opponents bleed. It’s just too gory for television and those watching this show probably have a gut full of lager and curry – the last thing they need is some guy getting his legs ripped off or whatever.” Head producer of “After Dark” Ryan Miller said, “These guys are fighters and athletes, we let them fight. I don’t think we have the right to stop two men from beating the absolute shit out of each other. If God didn’t want people to fight, he wouldn’t have given us fists, ya know?” The X could soon be without a representative show in the UK and Ireland, but with Rupert Murdoch’s “Sky” rumoured to be bringing out an all wrestling channel, the absence may be a short one.
