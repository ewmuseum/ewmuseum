Critics Claim eW, Inc. shows not all as good as it is Hype to be?

11 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
eW, Inc. polls say that eW, inc. Shows aren't as good as they are said to be? Earlier this week, a poll was taking on how well was the Monday Night Vintage show and how well was the Thursday Night Genesis show was for a debut show? Former FAW owner, Buddy Digby answered the poll with great detail. "eW, Inc. came into the world of wrestling with alot of hype in their favor. Many great talented stars that we all know, and I personally even had the chance to work with before are on the roster and I know what kind of shows they can perform. But as a owner of a federation, I must say eW, Inc. first two shows were not all what it seemed to be." Many of the eW, Inc. fans answered the poll and it all revolved around the same statement. 68% of the fans agree that eW, Inc. shows can be a little better for a talented roster. Vintage and Genesis GM's Paul Harris and Simon Escabar were both furious about the poll as being in competition with one another, but are doing poorly. With the fact being known, both Harris and Escabar has made it known that from now own, shows will be more exciting than ever. "Being a man behind underground shows, I have a slight idea of what the fans like to see. So as long as I am around as Genesis GM, I am going to give the fans what they want." Quoted by GM Simon Escabar in a press conference. "I too beileve that the shows need to be better. I am going to do all that I can to give the fans what I want, and I still plan to make eW, Inc. Monday night Vintage, the best show on Monday Nights!" Quoted by Paul Harris in a Press Conference. Both GM's has begun hiring more staff to help out with directing the shows and roster. The Change in the better shows will take place starting this Monday on Vintage. Paul Harris has alot in stored for the fans this week on Vintage. Damon O'Halloran and Norman Richards is set to take on the disturbing Corruption, Inc. Tyler Price is set to take on his second opponent in his quest for the Asylum title. Also, The Big Rematch, Hardcore Hoefel vs. Tony Vigilante. More matches are to be announced later this weekend. Everyone Tune in for Monday Night Vintage. eW, Inc.
