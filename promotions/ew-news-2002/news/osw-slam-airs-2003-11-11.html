OSW SLAM! Airs!

11 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last week, Ivan Stanislav left OSW. This week, the entire roster stepped up to fill the void left by The Russian Bear. In the first match of the night, Dave Gibson and Asa Fountain met in their rematch from one week ago. Gibson won the last match, but it was a shady ending, to say the least. Now there's no room for error, as these two went one-on-one again this week. James Edwards and Xavier Kannon met in the squared circle to determine who would face Matt Register for the OSE Old School Title. The most prestigous title OSE has to offer, and the chance of a lifetime is there for one of them. But which one? Jude Kensington came into his match against Matt Register, with the OSE Old School Championship and the chance for revenge for what Register did to him. Register came into the match looking to take back what's rightfully his, and finish what he started. Putting his Hardcore Title on the line, Azala Zameer defended against the strong up-and-comer, Angelus Jones. With the chance to gain some gold, and with added power in his corner, could Jones take what Zameer fought so hard to earn? And in the main event, The Forsaken faced off with Politically Incorrect...and the OSE Tag Titles were on the line! Two men looking to retain + two men looking for their first OSW title reign = one hell of a match up. Plus! EDH and Boda made appearances...a few times. As well as The Forsaken and Politically Incorrect sharing some pre-match words, while doing their own thing during the night. Clyde continued on his journey for more power, P.W.A. kept trying to get a certain something signed, Jude's show debuted with a special guest, and Hoyt began a new conquest in OSW. All that and more, on OSW Slam! Recap
