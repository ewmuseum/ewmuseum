NTICW Immortal 7 airs!

23 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With the match between newcomers, Coleman "The Prophet" Dowell and Aaron Ward not televised due to it being a dark match, you really be kicking yourself for missing NTICW Immortal 7!!! The night started off with a match singles match between a returning superstar and one of 4 debutants to the company. Guess who won by the end of the opening match, the new guy or the weasle? NTICW owner, Miss Vicky Dixon, approached World Champion, Mike Wade, in his dressing room and warned of his actions last week when during his first title defense, he picked an unexpecting newcomer on the roster as his opponent and hit him with several chair shots to the back. Wade was at his usual egotistical self when Vicky Dixon made an announcement about his World Title which made him sweat and could also possibly affect the main event between Wade & Specter against Nathan Kaye & The Paralyser. What was it? The Hardcore Title was on the line again but this time against former champion and first ever champion under Miss Dixon's regime, seVen. Finally getting his deserved rematch, how pumped up is seVen? Last but not least, the tag team main event itself. Will Nathan Kaye & The Paralyser be able to work together after last week's incident where Paralyser powerbombed everybody including Kaye in a fit of rage at the end of the show? What about the announcement that was made by Miss Dixon earlier? How will that affect this match? To find out about all this, head on over to NTICW's official web site for the online transcript!!
