WfWA New Members and Shockwave Line Up

18 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The World Wrestling Alliance not only doubled its size recently by adding four new members it has just released the line up for its Shockwave Pay Per View event. The World Wrestling Alliance recently doubled in size with the addition of four new member regions to its already wealthy list of member promotions. Outlaw Championship Wrestling run in Texas and boasting "Little" Joey Average, Jimmy "Tic Tac" Boone, Adam Rock & Brett Rivers and The Jury. Sadistic Wrestling Federation from Michigan has a roster full of talent that is being welcomed into WWA including Tony Floyd, Logan Cain, "Vicious" Vic Venom and Jonathan Cross. Burning Phoenix Puro is based in Japan and will act as WWA Japan. The promotion is looking to book and complete its first tour very soon and crown the first All-Asia Heavyweight Champion. English Wrestling Championship has had previous success in WWA although pulled out 14 months ago. Back in and has former WWA Double Crown Champion Dark Wolf and the always respected Ryan Matthews on board. WWA has also announced the line up for the Shockwave Pay Per View that is due to take place on 11 January 2003 as well as the Pre Show Event that will take place on 9 January 2003.
