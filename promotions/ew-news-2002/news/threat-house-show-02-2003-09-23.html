thReat House Show 02

23 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The race was on for Troy Mason. A man who had a lot of work to do with thReat, but within a very short window of time. And with all the police & government investigations hanging over the company, Mason realised that even if he could do something magical with the three chances he had, thReat might yet disappear from the face of the planet due to the fact that he was basically harbouring fugitives. The race was on for Troy Mason. A man who had a lot of work to do with thReat, but within a very short window of time. And with all the police & government investigations hanging over the company, Mason realised that even if he could do something magical with the three chances he had, thReat might yet disappear from the face of the planet due to the fact that he was basically harbouring fugitives. It would be easy to fire the likes of psycho-Rio and such, but the compensation that would have to be paid would cripple the already horrendous financial state of the company. Marred by one Colin Gear, the Commissioner. At least the signs of a revival were encouraging, with the overall product of House Show 01, with the only blemish being Vincent Pembridge assaulting a female member of the roster. Time wasn't on Troy's side, however, and he now had to use all he had to keep the company afloat. By hook, or by crook -- Date: 12 September 2003 Location: Morgensen Gymnasium Complex, Salt Lake City, UT - Troy Mason opened up the show and reiterated the Main Event for the night; psycho--Rio vs Vincent Pembridge vs General Sanders for the thReat World Title. - Tyler Davidson defended his Cruiserweight Title against the promising Sergey Kovhachek. - Colin Gear was stripped down to his boxers. - Vincent Pembridge's lackeys took steps to hand psycho-Rio over to the police, and the Sociopath wasn't too happy about it. - haTE & Christian Elijah took on R.A.T & Focker in a Tag Team Match, that was supposed to have happened at Double Or Nothing. Plenty of bad blood here, and the ending was shocking. - Colin Gear had another showdown with Troy Mason, before some 'special' guests interrupted and had a little performance. - Chris McCluskey defended his International Title against Vandal. - A bunch of Mormons protested outside the arena, and when Kaylee Young tried to do an in-ring promo, all hell broke loose. - Louis Bourbon & PhD returned to action, resuming their rivalry. - The injury list grew, much to Troy Mason's displeasure. - General David Sanders found out that many backstage officials no longer respected him. - blind & Phil Atken locked horns with the #1 Contendership to the World Title at stake. - Troy Mason explained why he gave Vincent Pembridge & psycho-Rio shots at the World Title, before the Pembridge/Sanders showdown transpired. thReat House Show 02
