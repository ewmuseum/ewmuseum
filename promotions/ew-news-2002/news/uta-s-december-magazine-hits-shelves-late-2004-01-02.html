UTA's December Magazine Hits Shelves Late

2 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A little late on the shelves, but catch the December issue of UTA: The Magazine. We need an art logo ;) Welcome one and all to the UTA Magazine. With all the news and rumors for the promotion that's fit to print. UTA TOP 10: (Rankings for period ending 12/22/03) KEVIN HAWK: 6’3, 289 Oakland California UTA WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION 1. Dark : 5'11 265 Los Angeles California # 1 Contender UTA World title 2. Spectre: 6'8 299 Parts Unknown # 2 Contender UTA World title 3. Southern Rebel Ron Hall 5'10, 225 Roanoke Virginia # 3 Contender UTA World Title 4. Klash: 6’3 239 Madison, Wisconsin # 4 Contender UTA World title 5. JT Titan: 5'10 220 Washington D.C. # 5 Contender UTA World Title 6. Randy Sanchez: 6'2 232 Las Vegas Nevada # 6 Contender UTA World Title 7. Kaution: 6’ 1 221 Charlotte N.C. # 7 Contender UTA World Title 8. Crimson Lord 7’3 328 Parts Unknown # 8 Contender UTA World Title 9. DMT 6'5 295, Atlanta Georgia # 9 Contender UTA World Title. 10. Mr. Fantastic 6'3 250 Los Angeles California #10 Contender UTA World Title The tentative show for Heaven or Hell on January 25th, looks like this: Dark vs. Kevin Hawk UTA World title match Spectre vs. JT Titan Barbed Wire, Glass Table, Last Man standing match II Ron Hall vs. Jason Blackfront Street fight Klash vs. Mr. Fantastic Arron Marty/Stone Cold Thug/ DMT Triple Threat back alley Brawl Devon Lynch & Randy Sanchez vs. G Man/ Killer Kelly News and rumors: Goliath’s rehab continues to go well and with luck the UTA could have him back in the ring come June. The Surgery preformed on his back and neck by Dr. James Andrews was successful. Nathanel Draven has been deactivated for lack of RPing, and will probably be released when his contract expires in March. Mr. Fantastic’s return has created quite a buzz in the locker room. This time around the UTA has high hopes that Fantastic will claim the elusive UTA World title. Fantastic’s return has created hope that others such as Tsunami Tiger Tanaka, Michael Owens, Chris Phoenix, and others will return to the promotion. With Fantastic’s return, could the full reunion of Spawn be too far off? Many feel that Spawn just isn’t without Fantastic. Speaking of Fantastic, where is J.P. Richmond? Rumor from the back has it that G Man and Killer Kelly aren’t the happy couple they want everyone to think they are. Dark vs. Spectre, according to sources, the UTA would love to have this for the World title. Some in the back wish these two would just settle it in the ring already. Their petty arguments, and antics are wearing thin on some of the others in the locker room. Some feel that Dark shouldn’t have been World Champion, others feel he wasn’t given enough of a chance. You be the judge. Now that Kevin Hawk is World Champion, it could be only a matter of time before the long awaited Klash/Hawk feud takes place. Hell Razors are benefiting from the group push their getting, could they be the Super group the UTA has been looking for? Crimson Lord is due back within the next few weeks. Look for a feud with The Southern Rebel Ron Hall to take place over the Spring. Arron Marty and Stone Cold Thug are rumored to be on their way out, no one will miss them, no one will care. Randy Sanchez continues to confuse a lot of people, one week he’s great, one week he’s not. He seems to play to the level of his competition. The UTA would like some more consistency out of him. JT Titan continues to impress. Where Goliath was suppose to be the next phenom, JT is quietly building his way up the ladder. J.T. is being mentioned in the same phrase as “Future World Champion” J.T. is rumored to be up for some title shots after Heaven or Hell, a strong showing will help. The Southern Rebel Ron Hall continues to play the “What’s in the envelope?” game with Spectre. Whatever it is, this feud figures to take up a long while, probably possibly till Summer. Hall right now remains out of the World title hut, and he may not rejoin it till what he feels is the “right time” to rejoin it. This has been the UTA Magazine for the month of December. Look for the next issue after Heaven or Hell on January 25th. United Toughness Alliance
