OSE's Register endorses supplement

1 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fresh off winning the Old School Title at OSE's Ascension Pay-Per-View, Matt Register revealed Monday that he had been taking an ephedra-containing dietary supplement for over a year, then inked a deal with the company. First-ever Old School Champion Matt Register made his first public appearance since winning the title on Monday afternoon, at the General Nutrition Center in New York City. There he announced an endorsement deal with MetaTrim, a newly released dietary supplement containing the controversial herb ephedra. “MetaTrim is proud to announce what will hopefully be a mutually beneficial partnership with Old School Entertainment Champion Matt Register,” said company spokeswoman Janice Bligh to a crowd of reporters outside the store. “His testimonial to the success of our product is proof of its overwhelming success among adults who are wanting the most out of their bodies.” During the conference, Register also revealed that he had been taking the supplement for over a year, and attributed to the drug his success not only in OSW and its Most Extreme Team competition, but also previous endeavors, including the prestigious Wrestling God 3 tournament in Los Angeles, in which he was a semifinalist. “Following the closure of UCW, I was approached by the MetaTrim people as a possible test subject for their supplement before it was released publically,” Register said, reading from a statement. “Since then, my career has gone nowhere but up, and I have no one to thank but MetaTrim. This is my way of saying ‘Thanks.’” Ephedra, the primary ingredient in MetaTrim, has been in the news recently for linkage to a series of deaths in athletes due to its affects on the body, most recently Baltimore Orioles pitcher Steve Bechler. Most professional sporting leagues have banned the substance to prevent abuse, but without a banned substance policy in wrestling, its members are free to take whatever they please. The announcement was a surprise to everyone, including OSE officials. “While we did not expect something like this from a competitor like Matt, as our champion we have an obligation to stand behind him,” said spokesperson Shanda Russell. Rumblings from the locker room already have some up in arms as to the ramifications of the announcement. Betrayal, anger and disappointment are just some of the words flying around. However, Register assured everyone in his statement that nothing should change. “Although I will continue to take the supplement, I have no intentions to bring it into a public forum such as OSE. My private choices are not meant to be discussed publicly, and I will continue to do my best to represent Old School Entertainment to the best of my ability.” More on this story as it develops. OSE
