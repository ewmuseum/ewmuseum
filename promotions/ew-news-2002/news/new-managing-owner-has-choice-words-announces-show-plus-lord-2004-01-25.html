New Managing Owner has Choice Words, Announces Show; Plus, Lord Skywolf News ...

25 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This past Wednesday night's Icestorm Pay-Per-View had far-reaching consequences Cyber Extreme Wrestling. As the company celebrates its tenth year as a federation and fifth year as a brand, a new managing owner is in place, and a dark fight is brewing on the horizon. PITTSBURGH, Pennsylvania -- On Wednesday, January 21, Cyber Extreme Wrestling kicked off its fifth year of Pay-Per-View events with the one that started it all (in name at least): ICESTORM! The 2004 edition of the show was highlighted by six-time CEW World Champion AC defending his gold against the high-flying shooting star Cassandra Avery. AC was able to defend his title, though he was put through an incredible battle by Avery. However, AC's stablemate, and managing owner of CEW, James Everett III, did not fair so well in his match against AC's heated rival, 'Showtime' Nick Alexander. "It's safe to say that SNA thoroughly defeated Everett in Wednesday's match," said CEW Head of Talent, Tim Weich. "In fact, you could choose hundreds of set of words ... absolutely beat, kicked the crap out of, demolished ... and you'd probably still be right." Alexander's victory gave the wrestler full power as Managing Owner of CEW until the next CEW Pay-Per-View, Unleashed. This means that not only does Alexander have power over the next few weeks of Thrill, including the very special Thrill 150, but also Unleashed as well. Since taking office late Wednesday night, Alexander had remained silent about his position, until an address of the CEW employees and fans late Saturday night. Among his announcements, Alexander announced the creation of a new weekly show to air on HBO. As per 2004's celebration of the history of Cyber Extreme Wrestling (10 years as a federation, 5 years as a brand), Alexander decided to bring back CEW's original flagship show, Sunday Storm. Storm will debut on February 1, and is expected to showcase some of the smaller names of the CEW roster, including returning stars and up and coming rookies. Alexander also had choice words for AC, who, after successfully defending his title against Cassandra Avery, proceeded to attack her post-match with his title belt and delivered a second AC bomb. The World Champion was prepared to deliver a third AC Bomb before Avery was saved by her Tag Team Champion partner, Da*Link*Went. Alexander promised AC that there would be retribution for the attack, and judging by the constant hatred between the two, the retribution may be total. Alexander also spoke about Avery's performance in her match against AC, as well as Da*Link*Went's actions to save Avery from a third AC Bomb. For a full transcript of Alexander's address, please click the link below, or head over to www.cewentertainment.com. In other news from Wednesday night's Icestorm ... "It appears as if Lord Skywolf played the entire world for chumps, as he turned on his mentor, Mark Knight, immediately after winning the CEW TV Title. Afterwards, he spoke of the coming destruction of the 3-pAC, as well as his plans for federation domination. Offering anyone a spot beside him who wishes to join his cause, he left the fans and wrestlers to decide whether they were with him or against him. Claiming that he is not the man to be hated, he continued on during the week by stating that his plan is a complete takeover of the CEW, starting with the elimination of the 3-pAC and anyone else who would stand in his way." The Television Champion has a shot at another belt this coming Thursday at Thrill, when he is set to face North American Champion Weezle in one of the evening's headline bouts.
