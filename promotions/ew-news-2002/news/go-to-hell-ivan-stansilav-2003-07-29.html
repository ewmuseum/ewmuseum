Go To Hell Ivan Stansilav

29 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I fucking beat that fat piece of communist shit! Where's the hammer and sickle? So Ivan? Did your little invasion work? I believe it was shut down the moment I put you on the outside of that cage. Rather fitting if I say so myself. I mean really, now you'll always be on the outside looking in. How does it feel Ivan? You thought we would just roll over and die, that I'd take a pay cut down to the pay that the poor saps get. You thought there would be no resistance from the men who make their living fighting in cage? Man, you really were wrong. You thought we'd let the Russian National Anthem play as we walked out and stepped into the Asylum? You actually believed that you'd win a war against the best fighters in the world? Ivan, my comrade I thought you had brains. I thought you would put the motors in motion and at least have a back up plan. Have some way of clawing your way back into the Asylum like so many outsiders have before. But I will give you this, you fought me straight up, and when it was all over, when you lost and I emerged victorious, you left. But really Ivan, you think this is over and done with? You think I'll stop at just exiling you from my home Ivan? No Ivan, see thats where your wrong. This is where I bide my time. Where I think, and make a strategy that crushes you, and your Red Army. Ivan, what if I were to work my way into your apartment into the middle of the night? What if I crawled in through the window and found the Russian Bear at his most vulnerable? When he's sleeping? Then say I take a knife, and quickly thrust it into your neck. What would your comrades say when they walked into your apartment one day wondering what happened to you. Only to find a gouge in your throat. Blood covering your sheets and your clothing. See Ivan, there's a million things I can do to get to you. I could slowly work my way up the ladder, having each one of your men picked off, until your all alone. Backed into a corner, no where to run. Yes Ivan, I believe a cornered bear is dangerous. But, when it's out numbered, clearly the bear would lose. Ivan, lock your windows. Ivan, hide your friends. Ivan, become fearful. Fuck you, Sean "Token Weed" Williams the Asylum
