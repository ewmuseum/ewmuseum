Superior Wrestling "Anarchy" PPV Preview

23 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Superior Wrestling Presents Anarchy Date: Sunday, June 29th, 2003 Place: From the Tokyo Dome in Tokyo, Japan! Anarchy 6 Way Free For All - First Pinfall Wins Jonny Johnson vs Johnny Page vs Shang Maxwell vs Final Judgement vs La Mascaras vs Rome Turner It's an all out war as six of Superior Wrestlings elite young crop fight it out to become the number one contendor for the Insanity Championhip! All six of these men have the potential to win this match. Even better is the fact that these six men will be using any and every technique and any and everything they can find to achieve the excellence of Superiority! This match could be the show stealer! Pinfalls Count Anywhere - No DQ Match Vlad vs Joshua Icewind This past week on Shocker Icewind took out his frustrations on Vlad's Too Badd partner Chad. So this week Vlad is out for redemption as he challenges Icewind to an anything goes match. This match should be awesome as both competitors will be going all out. #1 Contendor Network Title Match Kid Dragon vs Heretic This two tore the house down last week on Shocker. They put on one hell of a show. Devon James was so impressed that he signed a rematch between the two and put the Network Title shot on the line. With a chance at gold so soon in their SCWF career's both men will be going all out! Regular Match Zsasz vs Syphon X Zsasz and Stevens agreed to have Zsasz face Syphon X with Stevens life on the line. If Zsasz wins he finally gets what he wants. One last match with Stevens. I Quit Match Snoops vs Derek Mota Derek Mota has made his presence known in Superior Wrestling! It all started weeks ago when CEO Bryce Stevens signed Derek Mota and assumed he would be a part of the New Era. Ever since then, Derek Mota has rebeled against The New Era and has had several encounters with them! On June 15th's edition of Shocker, Derek Mota attacked the last member of the New Era who he has not had a match with, The Snoops! This one is going to be huge! Who will say the two magic words, "I QUIT!" Insnaity Title Match Twiztid vs BSGWDT vs Suzanne We all witnessed the horrific falls that BSGWDT and Twiztid took on Shocker this past week. The question is not, how good of a match this will be, the question is, will these two men even make it to Anarchy? My money is on yes. These are two of the toughest son of a bitches alive, and they both are wanting that Insanity Title! Glass Title Match Jack Viper vs Brian Hulin This one has been building for a long time now. Hardcore Hulin has never lost a match against team WIW! He finally gets his shot against Jack Viper as these two men put it all on the line! The Glass Championship which has long been laughed at for its lack of integrity has now been pushed to the limit. It all comes down to this night at Anarchy! Will Hulin accomplish the last feat or will Jack Viper finally turn the tides with a huge win for the WIW? Superior Championship Match AJ King vs Todd Dream This match came to the for front this past week on Shocker when Devon James announced that the vacated Superior Title will be up for grabs when AJ King and Todd Dream meet inside the squared circle. This one is sure to tear the roof of the Tokyo Dome as Todd Dream looks for retaliation against AJ King for costing him the Network Championship this past week on Shocker. Card Subject to change.
