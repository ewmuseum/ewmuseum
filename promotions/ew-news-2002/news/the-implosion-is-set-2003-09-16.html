The Implosion is set!

16 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sunday September 21st will see the MWF Implode all over the arena as the top names battle it out for all the major titles... The MWF will entertain the Wisconsin crowds yet again when it arrives in Milwaukee to host Implosion. It promises to be a spectacular show and the MWF Xtreme Title is the first on the line as NomaD defends against Zach Fredricks in a Stairway To Hell match...a match not seen since the days of Extreme Championship Wrestling. NomaD has accepted the challenge of Fredricks and Zach will be looking for a chance to prove himself in the MWF. Ace Spade and Troy Knight will finally get a chance to go one on one and those lucky fans will get to see them possibly go at it three times as they compete in a two out of three falls match with some rather...interesting stipulations! The MWF Television Championship will finally find a new home when four men step into the ring with their eyes on the prize, Sean Harley heads to pack with Flash, Sean West and a Mystery Wrestler also announced to be in the match. Can Sean Harley keep his good form or will this Mystery Wrestler prove a step too far for him? The Main Event will go down in MWF history, both the MWF World and Tag Team Championships will be on the line as three teams step into the ring to prove they are the best. Anarkist will not only have to defend his MWF World Championship but also attempt to win the MWF Tag Team Championships. The first fall claims the MWF Tag Team Championship and then no dobut it will be every man for himself as the second fall is for the ultimate MWF prize...the friendships will be tested. Insane Wayne and Iniquity emerge as the main contenders for the crown, but EyCe and Kidd Kidman will fancy their chances. One thing is for sure, the MWF will implode with action on Sunday night! Maximum Wrestling Federation
