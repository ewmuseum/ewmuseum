DWA: LeStatt Knight Interview

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After the ppv, the DWA interviewers were busy. Destiny Mandrake was no difference as she caught up with one of the DWA's biggest stars, LeStatt Knight. ( It’s just a few hours after the cameras stopped rolling at Hostile Takeover. Destiny Mandrake has just entered the locker room for an interview. The man she was sent to see is none other than LeStatt Knight. He is sitting on a chair, wearing black break-a-way pants and no shirt. A towel rests over his shoulders. He looks up at Destiny walks in, he gives a slight smile, but his eyes tell a different story. Destiny shakes Knight’s hand as she sits on the couch that’s directly in front of him. ) Destiny: Hello LeStatt, May I have a few words with you? LeStatt: Sure Ms. Mandrake, Anything for you. Destiny: Well it’s been barely an hour or so since you and RaZiel lost to the Revolution … but before that I must ask, how long ahead of time did you and RaZiel have this planned? LeStatt: Does it really matter now? Our plan was foiled … we lost the match even with the element of surprise on our side. Granted Payne and Wylde used some very devious tactics … they still came away with the victory. Now once again I may have helped cripple my best friend … as he is in the hospital as we speak checking on his leg. His previous leg injuries were quite severe, so they had to hurry to make sure this didn’t create any more permanent problems. I shouldn’t have brought him back into this, it’s my fault. Destiny: Well people make choices on their own … I am sure you didn’t put a gun to his head. LeStatt: Ah perhaps not, but He would do ANYTHING for me if I needed it. He saw that I was in need of some assistance in my career and especially in getting my revenge on The Revolution. So we got together and came up with this … Failure. It seems perhaps Payne is right about me. Maybe I am nothing … maybe LeStatt Knight is only REAL in my own mind. Destiny: LeStatt Knight has tremendous talent … and everyone in the DWA knows it, whether they are like Payne and admit it or not. LeStatt: Really now? Do you know what my Pay Per View record is Ms. Mandrake? All of the matches that have mattered …. I’ve lost. Sure I won two tag matches in the battle ground tournament … but the one that truly meant something … it was ME who lost the match. Now championships? The American Title … I beat our current Champion Typhoid Mary … but yet, I lost it less than a month later to Loki. Tag titles? A secondary prize for losing to Mary and Grovel in the tournament. Add that into the numerous times I’ve been on the receiving end of a beating by Payne and Jeff Wylde. You see Skill and talent have nothing to do with what I mean by "LeStatt is NOTHING". I have skill I know this … but it just hasn’t translated into anything here in the DWA yet. Destiny: Okay while all of this is true, you can’t just be giving up or something. LeStatt: And why not? Should I try and battle the odds? Become a HERO!? Fight through all of the tragedy in my life and all of the hardships and come out on top? Pretty good fairy Tale right? Well … You’re right Destiny. I am not quitting nor will I ever. You see RaZiel and I did fall to The Revolution … they Pinned RaZiel 1 ..2 .. 3. But the keyword is RAZIEL. You see I direct this at PAYNE … This IS NOT Global Championship Wresting. You and Jeffry need to realize this. You can say I just keep coming back … but you putting me through a table and smashing chairs on my head are nothing. I’ve been in this business long enough to know that all of that does nothing to break my confidence. And beating me in a tag match? It’s nothing … I have mentioned what I feel about the tag division many times. Payne since we’ve both been in the DWA has you EVER pinned my shoulders to the mat … for the 1...2..3? NO!! Have we ever gone ONE on ONE? NO !! So you can continue to spew whatever you feel needs to come from that mouth of yours, but the truth is … UNTIL you can face me one on one … No RaZiel, No Jeff Wylde, no Good Cop/Bad Cop, no Killian, no Martin Smalls, no weapons, no tricks … ONE on ONE. The way wrestling was meant to be. Until you can do that … YOU are full of SHIT !! Destiny: Well LeStatt You may be glad to hear that earlier tonight … Payne has issued a challenge … To you for next Showdown Payne vs. LeStatt Knight one on one. LeStatt: ( a genuine smile spreads across the face of LeStatt ) Showdown … ( he laughs now ) Payne … all I can say is bring you’re A game. I am ready for you … I am ready to end this … to prove that you are beneath me … Let’s just see if you have the balls to keep your gang away from the ring and face me man to man … and that’s a BIG IF … See you next week ALEX. Destiny: before I go LeStatt, I have one more issue to discuss… What are your thoughts on what happened to Mason Stone, and the DWA with Jordan as the owner ? LeStatt: Frankly, I don’t give a damn who owns the DWA. Jordan says he only wants winners? He says that The product will become edgier? Well I like the sound of that. Mason obviously was a fool to put his trust into a man in which he has hated since the beginning. Jordan will eventually fall victim to Gideon as well … but he is blinded by all of the power that is being placed in front of him. Jordan will now pay my checks … and in return I will go out and entertain like only I can … and in the process hopefully DESTROY the Revolution … That’s all that I care about. So Destiny … Thank you … but I must get going now. ( Destiny and LeStatt shake hands again, and she leaves the room ) DWA: Dragon Wrestling Association
