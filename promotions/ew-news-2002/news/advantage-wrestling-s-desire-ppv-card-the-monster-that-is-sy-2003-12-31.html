Advantage Wrestling's Desire PPV Card & The Monster That Is, Symon Vaughn

31 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, Well, Well... Sean Levy here... I must say, Monday Insomnia and Tuesday Rage were fantastic and things look to be great and falling perfectly in place for one hell of a ppv that will be "Desire" live from Qualcomm Stadium Qualcomm Stadium!? Yeeup! You heard me correct... AW will be hosting its ppv from the baseball stadium... an open dome at that... things should get intresting especially since its the beginning of January and it's going to be freezing! Freezing or not the event is Sold Out and has been for over a month now...the matches are stacked and the night looks to be action packed ... Battle Royal! (Ron Von Jobber, Swaggie The Kidd, JJ Skittles, Eric Tione, Angel Dawson, Blake Cassidy, Gwenivere, Mark Adkins) #1 Contender To North American Title Chazz Irvine vs. Symon Vaughn w/The Mind Bender #1 Contender To X Title (Triple Threat/NO DQ) Viper vs. Acrophobia vs. Skylar Clay X Title Match (Grudge Match/Anything Goes) Big Poppa (c) vs. Steve Ewing #1 Contender To World Title (Supreem Cream Ladder Match) *First To Grab The Supreem Cream Pie hanging in the bucket and then Pie their opponent wins.* Sapphire vs. Brad Daddy Tag Team Championship Match Skarrphace vs. T2SF North American Title Match (Carribean Strap Match) Teemu McCorde (c) vs. Jack Duncan Main Event World Title Match (Cage match) Supernova (c) vs. Creature With all this and much more this is definitely and AW venue your not going to want to miss! Now, there is one thing on that card that tickles my fancy more than any other... And that's the in-ring debut of the monster that is Symon Vaughn! This guy is HUGE! He's been terrozing AW superstars week in and week out for quite some time now and no one has been able to stop him.... not even AW's franchise Big Poppa himself! This week he's set to do battle with Chazz Irvine... a sick and twisted bi-polar guy who believes the devil lives inside him.... it seems that if anyone is crazy enough to take down this monster than Chazz Irvine is just the man, or devil as he would say. It looks like at this event we will truly find out which superstars have the Desire to be victorious! AW
