[PWR] Glory Review

22 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PWR Glory goes off with a bang just one week before the Christmas in Hell PPV! You were a fool if you didn't tune into Platinum Wrestling Revolution's flagship show, "Glory" on sunday night. After opening it's doors only three weeks ago, this show was a great one leading up to PWR's first Pay Per View event next week, Christmas in Hell which will air live from The Palace in Auburn Hills in Detroit, Michigan. This week marked the first show that the new PWR Announce team of Neil Down, Roger Harris and new female commentator, Samantha Shane would call together as a unit. And they really did a great job with that with surely more great performances to come. The show began abruptedly with "The Virus" Marco calling his foe, Joe Johnson out to face him a No Holds Barred Match later on in the night. Both of them were already scheduled to compete in the Main Event later on in the Four Way Match in the PWR Title Tournament but apparently, Marco didn't want to wait that long! PWR's Co-Owners, Cazzo and Iceman made an appearance next. Cazzo was on crutches after having his leg broken by MacBeth on last week's show. Ironically, since Cazzo would be unable to compete in his PWR Title Tournament match, MacBeth was the man chosen to take his place. But in this specific piece with Cazzo and Iceman backstage, Cazzo decided to ban the move that put him out of commission last week, the Outcast and if he uses it, he will be disqualified! The twist here was that Cazzo and Iceman both decided that they would not even tell MacBeth about the ban! The next segment was a short one backstage involving The Frat Pack, Wayne Manelli and Todd Mitchell. They run into the seven foot monster, Gargantuflem and their obnoxious nature causes G-Flem to scare the two off! Talk about a close call for the Frat Pack! The first match was a four way match involving MacBeth, Forsaken, Mr. Rich and PWR Co-Owner, The Iceman. The match would be two falls in which the first fall would be a tables match for a spot in the PWR Suicidal Championship Match next week at Christmas in Hell. After that, the man who was eliminated from the match and the man who won the first fall would both be sent to the back......and the second fall would be a standard singles match in which the winner would get a spot in the PWR Primetime Championship Match next week at Christmas in Hell! In the first fall, Cazzo made his presence felt as he came out to make sure that MacBeth did not use the move that he banned, The Outcast in the match. As MacBeth appeared to set up Forsaken to give him the Outcast through a table to win the first fall, he grinned at Cazzo and stopped himself from using the move! It remains a mystery about who told MacBeth about the ban.......but soon afterwards, MacBeth would hit Forsaken with a Deicide through a table to win the first fall and win a spot in the PWR Suicidal Championship Match at Christmas in Hell next week. The second fall was a singles match between Mr. Rich and the Iceman. When it appeared as if Iceman had the match won, the lights began to flicker.....and Iceman's brother, John Mchugh made his presence felt by attacking Iceman to cost him the match! Mr. Rich would advance to win a spot in the Primetime Championshp match at Christmas in Hell! The next segment sees Joe Johnson come out waiting for Marco. Joe Johnson is attacked by one of the camera men who is revealed to be Marco! Soon after, the carnage is stopped by the arrival of PWR Commissioner, Aaron Gold! When Marco refuses to come to the back with him to talk business, Aaron Gold brings out police officers and he accuses Marco of murdering his former manager, Karl McKarlson! Video footage is shown of the incident and in it, it is apparent that Marco had something to do with it! Therefore, Marco is taken out of the ring by the police officers and brought backstage. The next segment would be another in which we would see Wayne Manelli and Todd Mitchell but this time, the duo would accidentally hit Kobra with a door injuring him and taking him out of his match later on in the night! Wayne and Todd celebrated saying that Kobra was their first victim.... The next match which was scheduled to be a four way would now be a triple threat match since Kobra was unable to compete! It would be Gargantuflem, Zachalous Dangerous and Travis Cage! Oddly enough, Kobra runs down halfway through the match to involve himself in the match! This fourway match would have the same rules as the first match did. Soon after Kobra's arrival, he was put through a table by Zachalous Dangerous! Kobra was eliminated and Zachalous won the opportunity to fight MacBeth next week at Christmas in Hell for the PWR Suicidal Championship belt! The second fall was a singles match between Gargantuflem and Travis Cage in which Gargantuflem defeated Travis Cage and won the opportunity to face Mr. Rich in the Primetime Championship Match next week at Christmas in Hell! The next segment would bring in Marco for questioning from a police officer about the murder case. Aaron Gold steps in and he gets Marco going. Marco tries to escape the clutches of the police officers but he is not able to. It's becoming apparent that Aaron Gold is playing mind games with Marco! The third match would be a four way elimination match with Jose de Vaca, Logan Darkholme, Stephen Jackson and Erik Murdock! The match would stop when only two men are left in the match and the two winners will win the opportunity to wrestle in the Six Man Hell in a Cell Main Event next week at Christmas in Hell for the PWR World Title! Jose de Vaca would be the first one eliminated but in a shocking turn of events, Stephen Jackson would roll up Erik Murdock for the upset victory clinching himself a spot in the PWR World Title match next week at Christmas in Hell! Stephen Jackson and Logan Darkholme will be two of the six participants in the Hell in a Cell World Championship Main Event next week at Christmas in Hell while the two losers, Erik Murdock and Jose de Vaca will compete for the PWR Territorial Title next week at Christmas in Hell! The next segment was an entertaining one in which Nick Dangerous debuted his show, Dangerzone to the PWR crowd! His first guest was his rival and enemy, Scott Monroe! As the two traded insults, Nick Dangerous dropped a bombshell on Monroe by bringing out his parents, Jacob and Andrea! Monroe didn't appear to be happy with them being there......as he expressed his displeasure and then attacking them soon after! I look forward to seeing more from these two. The next match would be a four way elimination match with the same rules as the last one between Nick Dangerous, Chazz Mendel, Drone and Blood Thirst! After a long period of exciting action, Blood Thirst was eliminated from the match leaving Drone, Chazz Mendel and Nick Dangerous in the match. At this point, it appeared as if Nick Dangerous was about to eliminate Chazz Mendel from the match securing his and Drone's spot in the Main Event at Christmas in Hell.....but Scott Monroe's face appeared on the screen. Scott Monroe then threatened that if he did not come to the back and stop him that he'd harm Nick's "son", Zachalous Dangerous. When it looked like Nick was going to walk out on the match, he returned to ringside to finish off the match. With Chazz Mendel's stablemate, Scott Monroe backstage.........another member of the Anarchy group, Joe Johnson came down to help Chazz Mendel pin Drone! Chazz Mendel and Nick Dangerous would advance to face Stephen Jackson, Logan Darkholme and two other men in the Christmas in Hell Main Event next week! Drone and Blood Thirst will be in a mini tournament next week for the PWR Territorial Title between themselves, Erik Murdock, Jose de Vaca and two other participants! Scott Monroe dissapointed by Nick's decision, he decimated Zachalous backstage upsetting Nick Dangerous further for making the decision that he made. The next segment would be an interesting confrontation between PWR Commissioner, Aaron Gold and Logan Darkholme. Aaron Gold walked off with Logan to talk "business". The next match would be the Main Event. It is a four way elimination match like the previous two matches were and this one would be between Marco, John Daniels and two members of the Anarchy stable, Scott Monroe and Joe Johnson! Early on into the match, Nick Dangerous comes out to play mindgames with his enemies in Anarchy by bringing out a bloody Chazz Mendel! This distraction would lead to Joe Johnson being eliminated from the match! After that, Nick says that his job is done and he throws Chazz Mendel off the stage onto a table below! Soon after, Scott Monroe would go on an angry rage and pin John Daniels to end the match! The show would end here with us seeing Scott Monroe and Marco winning to move on to face Logan Darkholme, Stephen Jackson, Nick Dangerous and Chazz Mendel next week in the six man hell in a cell match for the PWR World Title at Christmas in Hell! Meanwhile, the two losers of the match, Joe Johnson and John Daniels will be involved in the mini tourney for the PWR Territorial Title next week at the Christmas in Hell Pay Per View! All in all, it was an amazing show to set up an even more amazing card for next week's Christmas in Hell Pay Per View in the Palace of Auburn Hills in Detroit, Michigan! If you've missed PWR programming for the last three weeks, this week's Pay Per View event will be one that you won't want to miss! PWR Website
