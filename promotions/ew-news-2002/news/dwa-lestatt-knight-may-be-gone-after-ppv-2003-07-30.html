One of the most recognizeable and recently one of the most loved personalities in all of the DWA has reportedly walked into Jordan Brannigan's office, on Tuesday July 29th 2003 and has apparently said that Testament may indeed be his last DWA show ever. Sunday Night Juan De La Vega from Spain, a Rich and very powerful business man died in his sleep, after fighting a battle with cancer. He was a very good man witha great heart, and the world mourned his loss. WHat was not known until This morning was that He once raised a young boy , his nephew Roberto Juan Abitua ... who is now the DWA superstar LeStatt Knight. LeStatt and Juan have been out of touch since late 1997 when LeStatt was the cause of a Drunk Driving accident that took two lives. LeStatt was sent to the states ordered never to return again, because he tainted the family name and household. LeStatt missed yesterday's funeral but has reportedly been called by Juan's Wife or LeStatt's Aunt whom was more of a mother to him. LeStatt has been asked to come home and to help run His Uncle's company until they can find a suitable replacement ... and because His Aunt Sandra De La Vega wants to patch up their relationsip and right the wrongs. LeStatt has told Jordan that he is not sure yet if he will definetly go to Spain and take the company over. But he has put him on alert. For Jordan and the DWA this couldn't come at a worse time as LeStatt is a huge money maker, and he is in the midst of finishing perhaps one of the most brutal feuds in all of Wrestling as he battles Alex Grey / Payne for the World Title at Testament. When questioned about it Jordan Had this to say ... " It'll be a huge loss this I know, But We've worked around losing big stars before and we'll survive this one if it indeed comes down to it ... " LeStatt Knight wouldn't take any calls and hasn't been sighted since ... but his best Friend RaZiel was found and did comment. " His Family has always been important to him, regardless if he likes to admit it or not. He misses them ... This is a huge decision for him to make, and whatever he choses to do ... will be the right choice ... I know I'll miss him though " We will keep you posted on any and all developments on this blockbuster story as we get more information ... DWA: Dragon Wrestling Association
