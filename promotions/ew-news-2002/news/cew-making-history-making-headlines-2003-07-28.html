Making History, Making Headlines After a few weeks off to deal with personal issues and other assignments, Jeff Cross returns with a look at what news, events, and people are making the headlines (whether tabloid or otherwise) in the world of Cyber Extreme Wrestling. Well, it has been a CRAZY couple of weeks in the CEW. Hopefully some of you have been able to stay up to date on your own, but if you haven't, well, that's what I'm here for, to tell you what's been going on. (Note: A large amount of this article is a review of what has happened over the past few weeks in Cyber Extreme Wrestling. This article WILL contain spoilers for those that have not kept up to date on CEW news. I suggest that anyone not wishing to read spoilers go to http://www.cewentertainment.com/archives.shtml, and view the transcripts of Thrill 127, Thrill 128, and High Impact 2003.) When I last left my loyal eWNews readers, CEW was gained steam and heading into our most recent Pay-Per-View event, High Impact. The stage was set for an epic four-way match up between World Champion Muzzolini, Da*Link*Went, "Showtime" Nick Alexander, and Descendent. ** SPOILERS ** At Thrill 127, a main event match-up between Muzzolini and Descendent set the stage for all four men to hit the ring. By the end of the night, all of them had been laid out by Muzzolini and his dreaded "Da Gat" finisher. Two Gats were inflicted upon Da*Link*Went. ** SPOILERS ** A week before High Impact, at Thrill 128, the stakes were raised as all four men descended on the ring again, this time during a SNA vs Link main event match. Within moments SNA and Descendent found themselves handcuffed to the ring ropes, held on the sidelines while Muzzolini nearly destroyed the young Da*Link*Went. That night, Link again received 2 Gats on his battered and bruised body, a third only prevented by the last-second save at the hands of fan-favorites Mark Knight, Buzzsaw, and AC. ---------------------------------------------------------------- High Impact was an event that it would be difficult to describe with my own words. So much happened, it would be very hard to describe it all here and still do it justice. ** SPOILERS ** Lord Skywolf and Johnny Psychotic ended their violent feud. Psychotic would become the victor, but with a very interesting plot twist. James Everett III and Mark Knight stepped into the ring to see if the savvy veteran (Knight) could overcome the company's managing owner (Everett). As Knight has with the previous challenges that Everett has placed before him, Knight came out of the fight as the victor. Dru Jericho faced Brian Blazer with Jericho's Upstart membership hinging on a victory. Jericho did gain the win, though it was not without some controversy. Phoenix Murdoch defended his FTS title against the CEW Hardcore Monster Buzzsaw. Murdoch was able to win, though, like most Upstart matches, not without some help. Cassandra Avery faced Kris eGo for the North American title, and an eGo victory would require Avery's inclusion into The Upstarts. Avery would lose her title ... but how it happened was perhaps the biggest shock of the night. But the biggest buzz of the night surrounded the main event, specifically, Da*Link*Went. After the second assault at Thrill, Link was instantly been rushed to the hospital. "He could barely talk to me in the ambulance," his girlfriend, Molly told me the night of Thrill. "He just kept murmuring thinking he was talking to me. He's not in very good condition, and I don't approve of Link fighting in [next week's] match." After viewing X-rays doctors determined that Link had avoided any major injuries, though warned that a third impact, such as Muzzolini was prepared to deliver, could have been "very detrimental to his health, even put risk to his career." Doctors were unsure of Link's health status or how he would recover before the High Impact main event. As a precautionary measure, they refused to grant Link medical permission to fight in match. But everyone within CEW knows the kind of man that Link is. He has told other reports that those two gats "Weren't shit," and that he would be at High Impact. To say that Da*Link*Went showed at High Impact would be severly understating the young fighter's accomplishments. Not only did he show at the event, and appear to be largely healthy, Da*Link*Went SHONE brightly at the event. Mere words do not properly describe the magnitude of Da*Link*Went's performance. Though the high-flyer did not leave the arena with the World Title around the waist, it could be said that Da*Link*Went's performance not only built a main event star, but also solidified his position as one, all in ONE NIGHT! ---------------------------------------------------------------- The Sunday following High Impact, CEW held a press conference, announcing some key facts about the upcoming PPV event, the second-largest of the CEW calendar, Rage in the Cage. Among the announcements were the location of the event, as well as a look at the challengers that World Champion Muzzolini could hand pick from to face at Rage in the Cage. A full transcript of the press conference can be found at http://www.cewentertainment.com/ragepress.shtml. ---------------------------------------------------------------- This past Tuesday, Muzzolini DID hand-pick his challenger for the Rage in the Cage main event. But, like always, that wasn’t the only story of the week in the CEW. This week's Thrill also saw the debut of Michael Moesely and Weezle, two of the newest potential superstars to enter the federation. This coming Tuesday will see the debut of a man who was signed to a development deal just a few short months ago, Ryan Anderson. All three men look to have amazing futures within the company. The bar ... has just been raised. Also this week: Phoenix Murdoch put his FTS title on the line against Brian Blazer. Johnny Psychotic to face Buzzsaw. And Descendent with a special announcement. A full transcript of Episode 129 of Thrill can be found at http://thrill.cewentertainment.com/archive/129.htm. ----------------------------------------------------------------- With just a few hours until the next episode of Thrill, and less than a month until Rage in the Cage, be sure to keep your eyes on CEW ... always history in the making.
