Fatal Attraction leaves the game

15 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With an eye to the upcoming merger and an unusual event for the seemingly unstoppable Green Team, a tough vote sends recent Green Team member Fatal Attraction home. fWo Survivor III For several challenges the fWo Survivor III contest has been dominated by the Green Team; after six challenges the Green Team still leads the Gold Team 4-2. But after a recent appearance on the fWo Uprising program that saw four 1-1 tag team matches between groups, the Green Group lost the challenge, albet barely. This sent the Green Team back into the voting booth for only the second time in the contest's history. After several hours of tough choices and multiple switched votes during the time period, Fatal Attraction was selected as the eliminated team in round six. "It's worse going into here the second time than the first," said one team. "You are a great guy and you contributed greatly to our team since you joined, but I didn't think you meshed with the group after the mix-up, so I decided to base my decision on that." Another member put it this way, "It's nothing personal, but right now we as the Greens need to be as strong as we can, and though there are no weak teams now, somebody had to be eliminated. They're a good pair, and I wish them the best of luck." One member of the Green Group admitted to the complete random nature of his selection, "It wasn't easy. In the end it really came down to just picking a name out of a hat and hoping no one took it too badly." One of the newer members of the Green Team after the recent redrafting of groups laughed about the frustration of the voting chamber in general: "This stupid voting booth follows me wherever I go." Votes were cast for Fatal Attraction, Brotherly Love and the J.L.A. More news as it comes available... fWo Survivor III Online Site
