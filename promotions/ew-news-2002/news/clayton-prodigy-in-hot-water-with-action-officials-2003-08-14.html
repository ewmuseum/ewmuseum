Clayton Prodigy in hot water with Action officials?

14 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After Clayton Prodigy through his hat into the ever-enlarging ring that is the online feud between Spyder and Sylo, reports from various sources have been flooding in that he only did it for the exposure he would hopefully gain from it., as well as Action higher-ups being less than ecstatic. Yesterday, Action star Clayton Prodigy made his thoughts known on a host of ‘sore subjects’ ranging from Action’s backstage problems, to Spyder and Sylo’s Internet feud. The 22-year-old former Indy star pulled no punches, and as usual EW-News was first with the scoop. However, after talking to various members of the wrestling community, EW-News can reveal that Action’s backstage officials were far from happy. Rumours have continued to circulate regarding a suspension or termination of Prodigy’s contract, which has alerted various other companies to the fact that he may soon be available. Clayton had previously been regarded as the model worker backstage, and has been known to be one to always attempt to calm down an argument, but after his recent outburst officials have been scrambling to get in touch with him and give him a stern ticking off. Rumour has it that Action officials are far from pleased with his comments, and are considering taking the most severe possible disciplinary procedure, as means of making an example of Prodigy, and making sure that nothing like this happens anytime in the future. More on this story as it develops.
