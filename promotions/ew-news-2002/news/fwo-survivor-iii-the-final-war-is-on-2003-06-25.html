fWo Survivor III: The Final War Is On

25 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In their first vote as a unifed Survivor team, the merge shows that there are deep lines of mistrust, and that alliances are alive and well. The Enlightened is the first team to go in the new merged order. fWo Survivor III Contest It was meant to be a sign of unity... a sign that the teams, which had competed against each other as seperate groups were now coming together. But in the first vote of the Survivor III contest following the merge, it was clear that old group lines were still intact, and a deep division existed amoung the teams. While the contest has shifted from group to individual immunity, all eyes on the contest have to be concerned with the very divisive outcome. Worse still, one team neglected to vote... and it was one of the teams that was in high jeopardy of being ejected from the contest. "I don't know what the hell happened. These teams that don't vote... it's really troubling." These words from fWo Survivor Twitch, speaking of the Jesus League of America, a team that has been strong in the contest thus far, but did not register a vote during the 24 voting period. "I don't know what it means for that team... but I would hope there is a damn good reason for it. The f'ing exodus or something would be sufficent." The vote was a close one... five to three with the JLA not registering a vote. Ultimately it was the team of the Enlightened that tasted the biter taste of elimination from the Survivor III contest. "In this game you've got to be level headed and work with one another, not hold grudges and get confrontional the minute somebody says something to your liking," said one of the teams who cast a vote for The Enlightened, speaking about rumored backstage comments that were reported on several wrestling news sites over the week following the Ruahh~! appearance of the Survivors. Another team had this to say, "What can I say? When you get to this point, you've gotta do things quickly and effectively. They weren't a bad team; they've just been rubbin' me the wrong way in comparison with the other teams lately." On the other side, teams that went against the JLA had equally strong comments. "Sorry dude. I hate voting off people, unless I hate them. But you're really cool. I loved your work towards the beginning of this competition, but I just got a little too sick after the multiple doggy incidents." Another team only cautioned, "Good luck." to the team, while a third claimed, "I wish you the best. Good luck in the future and may we meet again some day." With the elimination of the team, the Enlightened now moves to the final jury that will decide the ultimate Survivor team along with former and ejected teams to come.
