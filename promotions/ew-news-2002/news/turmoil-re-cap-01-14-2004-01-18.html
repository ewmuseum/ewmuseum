The third Turmoil aired with an aura of discontent, with PCW moving on to it's second month of life with a less than stable outlook for the future. With the permanent owner of PCW still unknown, it wasn't a shock the night was to be a litte edgy for fans and wrestlers alike. But with PCW, really, what would you expect? To start the night off, Jay Phoneix got quiestioned by an angered fan's mother...and hey, with a big, gay hug from a big, gay bear...would you not expect his tastes to be judged? When we turn to our monologues, Dick and Zoober interrupt things for a very special announcement: after notifying the wrestling world that the new head of PCW security is none other than Titan, they also announce, to the dismay of the fans that Prodaganda will be Skylar Green's opponent for the title! But no, wait, hold the phones! It's Honky-Tonk President Williams, with a bottle of JD in his hands! After Dick and Zoober verbally assalt him and promise to enact legal measures against him for control of the company, they also announce that tonight, live on Turmoil, he fights Propaganda for the number-one contendership to the PCW World Title! Duke gladly accepts. Next we fins E2 backstage, up to no good....but what? Maybe we'll find out later. Next, Skitzo finds himself "searching for his mistakes," compliments of Robert Agg, his new watch-dog, compliments of the Boss Hoss. The "Buzz" Cal Roberts makes his unfailing lust for PCW's top-prize known, with yet another challenge to our blind chapion. But did it pay off? In the first match Avery Vance gets the big "W" over Arsob, but with a little help from a friend. ...But it's not over yet! Newcomer Donavan Emerson takes the stand, coming in the ring to decimate a surprised Avery Vance for the second week in a row! And after the match, the President walks in on his foes to find them...plaing video games? Next, RAH gives the Buzz a lesson is good citizenery. Safiya, the Egytpian Queen makes her rookie presence felt again, and she's invited a guest... President Williams then returns to his room only to be confronted by a man who ~ROCKS~! Is Kano, the PCW HOFer on Duke's side, or is the old friendship severed for good? The Mega Soaring Arrows are alive and well, and complaining about the bad press. But with a little help from an Old School legend, can they find their place in PCW? Awfa is here! And he's mad! In the second match, the "Buzz" makes Irish-born Terlayne Sterling feel a buzz, and a little bit more. Safiya introduces her guest, a mystery man....or the return of a former PCW World Champion? Things get even more heated up between Arson and Styles. Kano meets up with Zoober and Dick, and decides to make a few calls... PFIK committing adultery? Abner breaking into the president's office? And the gays are back? Phoneix finally realizes the fault of the loveable, fluffy little teddy bear. In a much anticipated match between a Native American and a Gay Bear, a fat Somaon takes the day...funny, huh? Well, not if you saw thearnage! And to add insult to injury, they just had to air a segment with Abner being dressed up like a girl in a primerime audience, right when the most people are watching due to high ratings from Awfa. And then they go right back down, due to the abnoxious shananogins of PCW's two resident assholes. Is there nude photos of PCW's resident redneck on the market? I don't know. Log onto E-bay to find out! Next up, Safiya goes a little too far and turns a locler room into King Tut's palace. Would you expect Skitzo not to pull a prank? No. And after catching his manager off guard, he decided to have a little fun of his own...Skitzo style! In one of the most competitive matches of the night, the Malices Man falls to a giant, but before reaffirming an old friendship. You can't have the night without the asshole who jeers at the fans, unable to explain his or her bad behavior. And Donavan Emerson, in his PCW debut, fit this role. Skitzo ends the night with a bang, and Abner gets reappointed PCW's DRA (Director of Redneck Affairs). And before the main event, the PopStar's mouth is shut by none other than...Dark Star? Yes! The PCW-turned-OSW-legend makes his triumphant return...and in style! In a match to decide the frist sanctioned title of the new PCW, the resident redneck, Abner, took Styles to task coming up with the win (and the title), but not before the mayhem that inevitibly follows PCW... PCW Wrestling!
