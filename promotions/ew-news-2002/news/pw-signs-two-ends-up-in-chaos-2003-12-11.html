PW signs two, ends up in chaos!

11 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Two new wrestlers were signed by the PW recently and amazingly the two went against one another. Phenomena of Wrestling The contract signing with two new wrestlers added to the PW, ICE and Angel was complete. The two are highly aggressive wrestlers. At the signing both men arrived at the same time and an unexpected argument broke out between the two. They had to be shifted into separate rooms as they wouldn't stop dissing each other. Angel is a submission specialist, one of the first in the PW. He has been through a lot in his life, he wrote it all down for us in his history section. Many feel sorry for him, but instead he feels sorry for them after he takes them down. Angel has had a hard time in this world and now he has ended up in the PW. Lets see what his future shows here. Ice is a Ohio Valley Wrestling School graduate, he wrestled in a local wrestling academy in his town for 5 years before going to OVW. Him and Angel already have a rivalry here in the PW after ICE slammed Angel's head on the desk at the press conference and knocked him out... ICE says all he wants before he takes the title is to kick Angel's ass which he thinks he can... Stating that he would fight Angel with a blindfold over his face and one arm tied behind his back. ICE is known for his agility and power style fighting. He don't put up with people's bullshit and that's why he'll be so successful here in the PW. Phenomena of Wrestling
