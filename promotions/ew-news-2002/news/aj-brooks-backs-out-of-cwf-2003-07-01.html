AJ Brooks backs out of cWf!

1 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A.J. (Andrew Jonah) Brooks, the main financial backer for the cWf has decided to leave the cWf in search of opening his own business out in the Los Angeles area in California. With the several million dollars he garnered from his shrewd stock deals back in 2000, A.J. was the main person responsible for the money handling and giving in the cWf, as well as the keeper of most of the income money, but now that A.J. Brooks has decided, for the mean time, to abandon the hot cWf project, Levi has been in a rush to find a new financial backer. He is currently in talks with Nascar racing, a financial backer for Tennessee promotion, NWA:TNA and even the majority owner of the St. Louis Cardinals, Fred Hanser has been in talks with Levi Brooks in terms of owning the cWf. Levi Brooks failed to answer any questions but his brother A.J. was more than willing minutes before his departure to LAX (Los Angeles Airport). "I hope that my brother does some good stuff with cWf... but ever since our losses in terms of roster people are concerned, I became discouraged and pulled out. If Levi needs some money, sure, I'll lend him more than enough to start cWf up... but I'm not going to risk my money on something that's not a sure thing. So as of right now, I'm out of the cWf." Levi Brooks has decided, because of the financial situation, to delay the cWf's first show Avarice and failed to comment more than "Not the right time." Stone Temple Pilots and Velvet Revolver singer, Scott Weiland has backed out of his appearance but said that his best friends, the DeLeo brothers (the guitarist and bassist for Stone Temple Pilots) would be at the premier Avarice with none other than Linkin Park front man, Chester Bennington! It was also announced that the cWf has apparently contracted "The Hollywood Monster" Impact, or Michael Scopes to a long term deal with incentives on performance and drawing power. The cWf is still in talks with "Superstar" Vince Jacobs about signing a long term deal, but for the mean time, the contract for his appearances have been signed. cWf - Championship Wrestling Federation
