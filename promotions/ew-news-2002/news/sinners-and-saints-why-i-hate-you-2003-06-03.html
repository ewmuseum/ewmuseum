Sinners and Saints- Why I hate you

3 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welp, time to piss you off, but first I'll explain to you every question you have asked. We Fucking Hate You All in the sake of entertainment right? Add the pressure Finch. There's been men like you before, and there will be men like you again. Does the name Thom Downey mean anything to you? Hell we've even had your catholic fellows come in and run their mouth's. Yea, we broke his fucking neck too. How are these acts torture I ask? We take the money to fight, from there your on your own. You have to assume the type of men who do this job aren't totally right in the head, and you play the head games to the fullest extent. How do you think I've managed to stay alive this long? Hell, I've fought your so-called hero in the cage, and I'll tell you. KODIAK VIC CREED IS A FUCKING PUSSY. And about the twat named Ender? He just ups and leaves when he wants, you'll find him in a gutter in less than a week. And Mr. Finch, we'd help an old lady across the street, but on the other side we'd beat her and take her money. But at least the old lady made it across the street right? Boda's a fucking communist for christ sakes, and your saying he's a better person than myself? All I do is make money off of all of your pathetic desires. Wait Mr. Finch, I think I sold your little boy a rock of crack. Said something about it being for his Pa. Wait, Finch, why was he walking all funny? No, your a sick fuck, he's an alter boy aint he? So when you want to diss our profession, please get a fucking handle on yours. Fuck you, Sean "Token Weed" Williams Asylum
