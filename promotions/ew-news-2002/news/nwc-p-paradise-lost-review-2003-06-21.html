NWC:P Paradise Lost Review

21 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lots of happenings on Pacific's recent Paradise Lost. Check 'em out! After the opening credits, the announcers babble about Lance Marshall losing the World Title to Billy Page, and they give a rundown of the matches on the card. Alec Spade arrives, and Brian McCoy tries to interview him. Spade seems nice, even though he gets no title shot this evening, and McCoy points him to the locker room. The first match of the night is a couple of newbies fighting each other. Masafumi Satake and Pete Moss. Satake wins it with the...um...the Mat...um...with a reverse neckbreaker that he calls something else. Julie Reveli shocks the entire Council by admitting her faction of Traditionalists is a failure, since Lance Marshall lost. Alec Spade gets into Donahue's office by being all nice. Donahue is confused, as usual, and Spade says he's going to mingle with the patrons. The second match of the night involves two television debuts. Ticonderoga Wu faces Jason Stylez. I would at this point like to say that any idiot who falls for the handshake routine should be fired immediately. Anyway, Ticonderoga Wu hit the 37th Chamber and takes the victory. After the match, things got a little...chairy...what the...I didn't write this! ::sigh:: "Insane" Alec Spade ran out and nailed the winner with a steel chair. He then did the same thing when loser Stylez got back up. I think Spade wants a title shot. Julie says that the Traditionalist cause is not really dead because there are people that still think they have a chance. I don't think BVI has a thousand people, but whatever, Julie, do your thing. Oh, and she says she hopes to beat Bobby Polo later in the night. Daniel Smith enters the building. McCoy gets a few words. Smith says he doesn't trust SLJ and that a "self-proclaimed great man" will fall. Chinese Fire Drill then beat The Executioners in the only tag team contest tonight. This division needs help, seriously. Brian McCoy enters Donahue's office, and Donahue immediately calls security and tells them to detain Alec Spade. Chris Saint, Frontier Champion, says he is a true champion and he will do all the talking in the ring. OK. Next, super-manager Project Pat took on Richard Hawley. After a lot of attempted pinfalls, Project Pat decided to try submission instead, and won the match with a Figure-Four. Hawley was an inch away from the rope too. Quitter. Oh, after the match, Hawley punched Project Pat. Didn't do a damn thing. The last Paradise Lost had Ryan Adams getting his ass handed to him by Derrick Daliente. He's in the hospital now, and he gets a visit from friends Dennis and Alex (Alex is a girl). Dennis gets crap for bringing Alex, then Ryan Adams says that all of the faction people in the "war" are a bunch of idiots. Sylis Walker comes out, calls Lee Clark out, and attempts to recruit him into the Rogue faction. Julie Reveli then comes out and pleads with Clark. Then Project Pat walks out and bitches at Walker and seems to be on Reveli's side. Oh, and he quit as Walker's manager. "J-Money" Jerome Morgan, some newbie, says he's better than all of the current champions in his little promo. Except he may not even join this region. So what the hell was the point? Alright, the first title match of the night. Chris Saint defends his Frontier Title against White. One of the announcers can't get the title Saint holds right. Saint hit the Lost Hope on Whitey and retains his title. But not so fast! Alec Spade makes his second after-match attack of the night, hitting Saint and then White with a chair. For the love of humanity, give the man a shot at a title! He'll just lose by DQ when he tries to use the chair again. He runs away from security. Donahue fires all of his security guards, and then calls the police. The police were too busy fighting over the last donut. OK, so they weren't, who cares? Next match faced "Shotgun" Daniel Smith up against "Powerhouse" John Porter. Smith for some reason does not get DQed after blatantly hitting the ref with his shotgun. Smith pulls off the win after hitting the 12-Gauge Slam. Smith tells off Team BVI, and then badmouths Bobby Polo. But then, "unoriginal" Alec Spade comes out...oh for Christs sake, you know what he did. But he gets arrested this time. Donahue is happy. Wyatt Connors and Julie Reveli run into each other backstage. Connors thinks the Rogues and Traditionalists should team up. Julie says no, because she's too weak to pick up a foreign object. Because, you know, women are the weaker sex. Right? No, I'm not gay, I was joking. "B-b-b-b-b-bad, bad to the bone." "The Strangler" Seth Bishop makes an appearance. He's making his return to the NWC and hes joining the Pacific region. Yup, he sure is. Daniel Smith's walking backstage, when he runs into Julie Reveli, just like everyone else. Smith wants to interfere in her match, but she doesn't want him to. Yeah, that's about it. Up next is the Rocky Mountain Title contest. Lord Winslow Darkthorne, the Lord of the Rings, defends against Lazarus. For the first time ever that I have seen, Frodo Baggins passes out in a Texas Cloverleaf. Lazarus becomes the new Rocky Mountain Champion. Ryan Adams left the hospital. He's recording the follow-up to "New York," his hit single. It will also be really bad. Royce Donahue wants someone to bring him The Man. He will give whoever does so whatever he wants. I bet that person wants an NWC World Title shot, because its painfully obvious. And besides, I AM THE MAN!!! And finishing up the night is the match everyone's been waiting for, "Every Segment Involves Me" Julie Reveli battles the West Coast Heavyweight Champion, Bobby Polo. Daniel Smith shows up at ringside halfway through. He winds up just standing there, looking like an idiot. Reveli wins with a cross-body splash. Polo apparently forgot how to kick out. Julie Reveli is the new Pacific West Coast heavyweight champion. Eh, it was an average show. For some reason the BVI Sucks Invitational presented by Bidness, Inc. has not been aired yet. That should be entertaining, and I will review that when it is released. National Wrestling Council
