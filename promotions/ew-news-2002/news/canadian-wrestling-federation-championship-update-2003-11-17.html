Canadian Wrestling Federation: Championship update

17 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Canadian Wrestling Federation: Championship update 11/14/2003 By [name withheld] Edmonton Sports Scene The action is really heating up in the CWF as 24 wrestlers are vying for the newly created Canadian Championship Title. Already Robert Greene – who has announced we will transfer his contract to the CWF in order to stay in the tournament –, Midnight Flyer and Mr. Meltdown have already advanced in the tournament. Three more will move on this week, as on CWF Canadian Content the tournament will move forward. So far, the matches announce are Zane the Insane vs Todd Zadik and Donovan Kayl vs Theodore Logan. What a powderkeg it will be as Zadik and Kayl have been at each other’s throats from the moment Zadik came to the CWF. Not only is that explosive duo going to be near each other, but Mickael Goodmarrow will have to face not one, but two CWF Superstars and he doesn’t even know who they are yet. And finally, if that wasn’t big enough, the fighting brothers Jason Angle and Steve Steele face off against each other one more time. And the winner continues on in the Canadian Championship Tournament. Plus, how with CWF react to Lewis Manchester’s announcement and much more. This week, on Canadian Content.
