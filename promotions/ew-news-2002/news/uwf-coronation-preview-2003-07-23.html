UWF Coronation Preview

23 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
One month before the UWF's biggest PPV event, Live and Loud 3, the UWF's best will go at it. Five titles will be decided, and the #1 Contender will be determined in the Regal Rumble. This Sunday, July 27th, the Universal Wrestling Federation presents Coronation, live on pay-per-view. The name is appropriate, as five titles and the #1 contendership to the UWF World Title will be on the line. Here is a preview of what you can expect at what should be an amazing show. UWF Television Title Match The Fool (c) vs. Dante, Bruce Gordy, or the Trailer Park Luchador On the edition of Universal preceding the PPV, Dante, Bruce Gordy, and TPL will go head to head to head to determine the challenger to The Fool's title. The TV Title, notorious for being defended every show, has bounced around quite a bit lately. Can the newcomer Fool hold onto the title, or will one of the three UWF veterans unseat him? (T)he (H)ardcore (C)hampionship Match Miniature Golf Course Deathmatch Killer Dude (c) vs. The HIT In this most unique match, longtime UWF star Killer Dude takes on the HIT, known as one of the most hardcore men in the business for his exploits in the IWO and PCW. In this dangerous match, the winner is the first man who can get his golf ball into the eighteenth hole. But, let's be honest, the real attraction of this match is seeing two grown men destroy a mini golf course. Will professional wrestling hear the words "And he gets thrown into the windmill!" for the first time? Most likely. UWF Intercontinental Title Match Ladder In A Cage Match Kevin Starr (c) vs. Mike Montgomery vs. Joshua Dunn Champion Kevin Starr has a tough challenge ahead of him. Not only must he fight Mike Montgomery, who has secured victories over some of the UWF's best as of late, but he must contend with Joshua Dunn, who got into this match by kidnapping five year old David Starr. The stipulation assures that there will be lots of bashing of body parts into cold hard metal. All three athletes are capable of bringing home the gold, making for a most interesting contest. UWF World Tag Team Titles Match Local NVA Company (c) vs. Black Star Revolution vs. Misfit and Lew Spectre The Local NVA Company, two North Vietnamese Communists, have been multi-time UWF Tag Team Champion, proving that successful communism isn't just exclusive to Old School Wrestling. Facing them are two of the UWF's soundest wrestlers, Kenny Mercury and Ben Jericho. Coming off a strong semi-final finish in the Primetime Invitational Tournament, Mercury is ready for this high profile match, and Ben Jericho is cocky as always. Together, they form the Black Star Revolution, a serious threat to the NVA Company's titles. The true x-factor in this match is the Misfit/Spectre tandem. Misfit's anger at Spectre is well documented. Yet, the two men are teamed together. Both excellent athletes, can they overcome their differences and become tag team champions? The Regal Rumble Winner gets a World Title Shot at Live and Loud 3 Competitors: Sirian, Shadow Blade, Thai Trang, Duc Chu Song, Ben Jericho, Kenny Mercury, Misfit, Kevin Starr, Mike Montgomery, Joshua Dunn, Killer Dude, The HIT, Danny Jaye, The Plague, The Fool, El Turtlé, Dante, Chris Thunder, Max Lightning, Digital Mortality, Max Damage, The Phenomenons, Brad Johnson, Justin Donovan, Legend in Training, SKuLL, Nyne, Maurice, A.D.D., Bruce Gordy, Trevor Rayburn, Lew Spectre, and, as usual, SOME MYSTERY COMPETITORS! This is it. The Regal Rumble. One of the UWF's traditions, the winner of this massive battle royale is the #1 contender to the World Title, recieving his shot on the biggest PPV of the year, Live and Loud 3. In addition to many of the athletes mentioned earlier, UWF mainstays such as Danny Jaye, The Plague, Sirian, and Shadow Blade are entered in this match, and all can be considered favorites to win. Who will the mystery men be? Who will be the winner of the Regal Rumble? You'll have to tune in to find out! Main Event-- UWF World Heavyweight Championship Match No Disqualification, No Countout, Anything Goes Petey the PG Tips Chimp (c) vs. Seymour Almasy This is it. After a year of competition in the UWF, Seymour Almasy recieves his title shot. Against a chimpanzee. Yes, Petey the PG Tips Chimp, a former tea spokeschimp, won the World Title on a fluke against Jamie Shock, with his patented Chimpy Chompy Crunch. The Final Fantasy looks poised to ascend to the position of World Champion, but stranger things have happened. Can Petey turn back the challenge of Seymour, or will Almasy roast the monkey with his trademark Firaga? All in all, the card looks to be another great one. Stay tuned to the UWF, for Coronation, and all of our other great events! UWF Online
