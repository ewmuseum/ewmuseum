PWC Heading For A Holy War?

2 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
6’1" Limerick defied the odds by not only proving he could walk with Giants, but could beat them too as he was forced to run the Revival gauntlet this week. After making a rather impromptu debut in PWC, by jumping ships literally straight from OSW to PWC and breaching his contract, the seven foot monster Propaganda and his dastardly manager "The Fat Preacher" Eric Sagar have landed with both feet and causing quite a shake. Teaming up with fellow dedicated Christian and PWC TV Champion Hoyt Williams, the son of PCW Legend Duke Williams. Together the three form a stable known as "The Revival" who are intent on reviving not only the wrestling world but the whole human race. The Revival have started by attempting to destroy PWC’s Irish-Catholic Tag Team, The Holy Underground. Who objected to Eric Sagar using the Gold plated bible to aid Propaganda and Hoyt Williams during their matches. Also the Revival are Protestant making this feud even bitterer by adding centuries of holy wars, especially in Ireland. Last week "Gods Ultimatum" Propaganda, "Your Personal Jesus" Hoyt Williams and Eric Sagar humiliated McGlockan giving him a concussion meaning he couldn’t compete in the next week. McGlockan is a former RUSH superstar. No McGlockan meant that the mute and partially deaf, six foot one Limerick had to fight alone on this weeks show. And that he did. Hoyt Williams took advantage of his disabilities and forced him into having to run the gauntlet against The Revival. Not before insulting the Irish people and the Catholic faith, however. In the first round, he took on the seven foot former OSW International and Tag Team Champion, Propaganda who gave the Irishman a tremendously big beating almost breaking his body and back. He beat him from pillar to post. Then, in a sudden burst of light Limerick turned it around and got the one, two, three. Then, straight after that, Limerick had to fight Hoyt. Hoyt is the current reigning PWC Television Champion, coming from a Wrestling family and has Eric Sagar in his corner. Like Propaganda, Hoyt beat on Limerick until he was almost dead. Then Limerick span things around in the blink of an eye. Claiming the pin over Hoyt. Leaving the professional athlete to go one-on-one with the just over five foot, fat, waddling, 60 year old preacher. Who has only ever wrestled once, in which he lost to Minion The Imp. Limerick beat him, beat him like a dog. To the sheer enjoyment of the crowd. The devious preacher got his just desserts. So what’s going to happen next week in this immense and intense feud? McGlockan should be all cleared, will he be smug about how the whole Revival couldn’t take down the six foot one Clairvoyant? Will Hoyt Williams be bitter and have an extra edge to his harsh dagger that he can pull out? Will Limerick still be thriving from such a marvellous victory? Eric Sagar is apparently nursing a black eye from Limerick’s beating. Will he set his monster; Propaganda onto the Holy Underground? We cannot be sure about either of them. But we can be sure that this feud is only going to reach higher temperatures. Tune into PWC TV next week! New Jersey's Own PWC
