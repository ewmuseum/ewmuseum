XWrestling News: Hartman`s Headlines for 5-28-03

2 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The ever-outspoken Jeff Hartman is back to active duty within Xtreme Wrestling, and brings a new edition of Hartman's Headlines. Originally Posted in Xtreme Wrestling Magazine by Jeff Hartman on 5/28/2003 Hartman`s Headlines 5-28-03 by Jeff Hartman That's right! Your eyes did not decieve you...I'm baaaaaaaaack!!! With the impending return of Xtreme Wrestling, the ol' rumor mill is running full steam. I could bore you with graphic stories of my sordid sexual exploits now, but there's just too much news to waste any more of your time, so here we go: -Nuclear's XW contract has run out, and there is no word yet if he will be resigned. -XW has signed a former XW wrerstler who will make his re-debut on the first eXtreme back. Rumors as to his...or HER...identity are swirling. -Crucifix asked and was granted his XW release. Good luck to him, and I'm sure you'll all agree that the world would be a better place if his aim was better. Right Derek? -This is an interesting tidbit for you: Rumor has it that a new commisioner will be named on the first eXtreme back from the current hiatus! -Here's another one: Word coming from the President's office says that with the limited roster, look for less matches on the eXtreme TV show until the roster picks back up. Good! Makes for less worthless crap for me to announce! -One final bit of information for me to pass on this week is that there is a possibility that a faction of wrestlers that everyone will be familiar with may be reforming as we speak. Who could it be? Until next time, I'm Jeff Hartman, and I have a small penis...hey, wait, who's writing this crap for me?... Xtreme Wrestling
