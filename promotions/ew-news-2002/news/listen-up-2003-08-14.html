Listen Up

14 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Adding my ingredients to the pot, so you bleedin' yobbos get it straight. Shut your gob and listen up. I’ve finally sussed out what I want to do with this measily job I have here when I came across a war of words between two berks caught my attention. Is that how, you Spyder and you Sylo, waste your time… dumb sausage jockeys. I’d hate to barge in on your two’s party but I must. I very well am going to put it all on the line and not hold a thing back, so understand that I hate you all and you are inferior. PIW was filled with, produced, and left us with a bunch of twisted twonks. PIW never was nor never would have reached any level of respect in the wrestling industry, no matter how hard they tried to make people believe they had. It was all bollocks, from LLB’s multiple title reigns to Chris Fox’s dull comedy to Sylo’s piss poor championship victory. Now that I’ve gotten that point across I want to ask our steroid prone friend Sylo exactly what he was thinking when he tried to challenge Spyder. How did you expect to out-do that wog when you can’t even tally 1 and 1 to get 2? Did your lot of twonks sit at the desk and help you type out your pitiful article? I suggest in the future you try and study up on what you’re going to discuss rather than dipping in the water without knowing its temperature. Better yet… throw your bleedin’ computer out the window and cancel your internet access, mate. Sylo, you tried but you’ve came a cropper. Now it’s time to move onto our lairy yobbo, Spyder. Shouldn’t you be more concerned with sending your monthly pay back to the family in Mexico instead of smacking around knackers? I’m very well sure you should be worried about customs picking you up rather than what some poofter has to say. Here are a few words of advice… green card. The pigs will be onto you eventually so rather than spending so much time paying attention to what prats have to say, focus on what’s important. Now that I’ve said what I needed to I’m going to get ratted arsed and shag some muff until the early morning dew rises. Naff off.
