fWo US Champion Voran Xias Places Open Challenge to Any Secondary Champions

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Following his controversial "shoot" segment on Monday Night Ruahh~!!, Voran's looking to back up his words. The always-controversial fWo United States Champion, 'Undeniable' Voran Xias, insulted secondary champions of several federations, including Action!, OSE, and the WWE. All three federations declined to comment, but rumors were that they were none too happy with Voran's comments, and lawsuits could be pending. Meanwhile, Voran is looking to back up his comments by stepping into the wrestling ring with another secondary champion from another federation. "My requirements are simple." said Voran. "If you have a title which is recognized as the #2 title belt in your home federation, step up and answer my challenge. Whoever does will face me on this Friday night's fWo Unleashed show in two seperate falls. Fall 1 for that federation's title, fall 2 for my United States Title. I guarentee that when it's all said and done, I'll be a double Champion." So, the question is now- who, if anyone, will step up and accept Voran's open challenge? We'll be sure to keep you updated on the situation.
