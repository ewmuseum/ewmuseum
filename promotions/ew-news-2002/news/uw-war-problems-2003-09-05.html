uW:wAr problems..

5 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Since the great signing's such as Dante Saffron, Jamie Starr, Dawson and Nix WAR has been having a few problems on the recruiting side of things.... WAR has just recently put up its first card which is to be debuted on Wednesday 10th. However, since the last signing of Jamie Starr which was about four days ago, WAR have not recruited anybody, leaving their roster size at a small 11. WAR owner Jamie Charman has been said to be very disappointed in the lack of recruiting as of late and is looking to change things in this department. It has also been said that WAR's first champion maybe decided soon as the sabotage voting begin's on the first show. The Sabotage game is a game in which the roster vote for whom they don't want to win the title, and the last superstar wins the title. Many have said this is not a good idea to crown the first champion, where as many have also said it is a great idea. We will just have to see how well the show goes. Jamie Starr the newest recruit to the WAR roster has stated that he wants to be the first to go for the title, but other top WAR superstars such as 'Dante Saffron', 'Nix', 'Devin Dawson' and 'Brad Polaris' have all stated they want a shot for the title.Jamie Charman has told each one of them that they must prove themselves first before even being thought of getting the title. uW:wAr
