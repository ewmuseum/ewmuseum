Pressure Point 65 Report

1 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The pre-PPV Pretty Hype Machine fires on all cylinders. Great wrestling, no Glass Ceiling, and fresh faces get set to main for the first time. ACTION! PRESSURE POINT REPORT By: Keith Scott Zimmerman E-mail: [detail withheld] Date: 24.6.3 (taped 23.6.3.) The Big News: Guess who's main eventing the PPV? SIX MONTHS. You may look in my general direction with jealous awe now. Title Changes: -- Face/Heel Turns: Max Danger...he...I...there is no emoticon for what I'm feeling right now. Best Match: 6. Gotta love anyone down with "Electioneering". The old man and Wombat worked some tech before Donell tagged in, and DA did a nice spot with a Stun Gun out of a quebrada attempt. Santos came in and luchaed it up with some shots to the back of the head but got too giddy and Donell took him back down. Double sit-out dropkick by them, but Donell bombs out a rana and tags out. Wombat began to play Morton as the champs worked over his leg. Cristo took a beating but came out the other side with the MURDERDEATHKILL Eddybomb. Tags made, and Julius KEELS Wombat DED with a super kneebreaker into inverted Indian Deathlock (thank you, hHh). Luchadores turn it around again as Julius takes a climb-up Owenzuigiri and bumped to the apron while Donell got a super bulldog. Santos used the Garvinator and they finished with a pendulum/guillotine legdrop hybrid. Great stuff. They'll lose at the PPV but at least it'll be in a good match. Worst Match: 2. Total GOD squash, more to set up Fosters/DeNucci shoot fight at the PPV than anything else. Absurdity of the Show: See Face/Heel Turns. Moment of the Show: The KSZ Driller through the announce table. Breakdown/Highlights: Conrad & Max...made up. This must be what it was like the Day the Music Died. I feel sick, literally sick over seeing the force of pansy-ass goodness claim Max as a victim and will now be switching my demigod worship over to the Archibishop of the Cruiserweights. Max/Topex for the C tonight, French Connection v. The Doxers. I shouldn't be rooting for the French against an American. Where have you gone, Joe DiMaggio, a nation turns its lonely eyes to you...1) I like the fact we're doing more heel/heel matches, though Sharc was a quasi-face since it was in his backyard. Sharc did some quality work and hit a chair shot followed by his big 2 for the W...IBJ was doing some family business and wouldn't be at the show...Ryder/Shepherd at the PPV with the former working the bad hand and running him into a wall with a forklift...DeNucci vs. Homeless Gus tonight--set the TiVo...Pleh gave me back NATALIE~! and booked me a slot...2) See above... Case beat the crap out of Prodigy in retribution for last week, they're on for the PPV too...Obviously with IBJ away, Russa won by forfeit...Rook screwed with HardCase...Paulton promo with Duncan...I challenged Avalon to a B Title match at the PPV...KroW cut a broody Raven promo from Minio's dad's grave...Fiend got beat up by another one of Sharc's friends...3) Good face/face stuff here. Duncan kicked out of the Pearl River Plunge but was pinned while doing the Dangerous V...Sylo slowly drifted towards bad-ass Jesus Push heeldom...4) Great back and forth with the Anderson spinebuster doing in another of Sharc's boys--Sharc/Fiend in a knife fight at the PPV? Mkay... Joe Black did TV and pumped up 101...Brock/Caden is FCA...5)The crowd got Rook with water ballons. Why would they accept water ballons from Hardcase? Pretty good back-and-forth, HardCase won with the Flair pin...6) See above...Minio & KroW exchanged words...Cambria & Wagner exchanged pleasantries...7) Max won with Double Jeopardy, the Frenchies beat down him & Conrad after. Huzzah...KroW/Minio, Buried Alive at the PPV. I'm looking forward to the match but that stip's so 1982 it's pathetic...8) Good to great match, again with parity. Duel may've won on points but he went down to two Britney Spears. Me & Coral brawled post match and I gave him the Driller through the table. Grade: A-. LLwho? Everything after the new kid's Gus squash was at worst good and in some spots was great. A really strong WRESTLING show to lead into the PPV, with some new people getting their share of the spotlight now that the Glass Ceiling seems to have finally been shattered. Rumor also has it Reed will address the Action Title situation at the PPV or the PP after, and I wouldn't be surprised to see the pink slips flying. Keep watching the skis... Show Results: 1) Sharc ko. Clayton Prodigy 2) Giovanni Oliver DeNucci s. Homeless Gus 3) Kayden Paulton d. Bradley Duncan 4) {hardcore} Fiend d. Diamondback 5) HardCase d. Rook Black 6) {non title} Santos & Guerrera d. the Arcades 7) [CARNAGE] Max Danger d. Topex 8) [BANTAM] Coral Avalon d. Duel Pressure Point 65
