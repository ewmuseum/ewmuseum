a World with a View

14 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Now in Compact Anti-Esse Carrying Size! I'm not going to lie, I am a big time reader of the News. I know people in the business aren't supposed to follow the dirt mags, and then they aren't supposed to endorse them, but I'll be honest with you. I have watched Spyder gloat through columns every few days. I decided to stay out of it. But Spyder decided to throw my name into the mix, so I have a message. Spyder, keep my name out of your mouth unless you are challenging me to a match. Your opinions of me, are garbage. You're perpetrating a B.S. rumor that is blatantly untrue. Sylo, Sharc, KroW... If all three were crawling through the desert, I wouldn't waste a drop of water on those chumps. People tend to forget, I didn't get along with ANY of them in PIW, I don't get along with them now. KroW credits himself on my father's cancer related suicide, do you really think I sit around and shoot the sh*t with him over a place where I was basically unwelcome? I don't endorse PIW. If you remember, Jim Johnson and I, and [name withheld] and I, always butted heads. The only reason they kept me around was because I put people in the seats. THATS what I know about the business, esse. Sylo, shut your trap. The blue dye has sunken through your skull and you're back here talking about issues you don't understand. Spyder, you give that lucha mask a bad name. I would wrestle you under the mat. The only BITE in your arsenal, is your STYLE. I won't lie, you have an impressive record, in the past year alone you've taken six fWo titles, I will give credit where credit is due, but when I look at your steady decline down the card, its depressing. From fWo World Heavyweight champion to a Hardcore war with Aimz? I've done my time with Woman in the ring and trust me, its not anything that brings you up to bragging rights. If it wasn't Aimz I would rip you a new one on it, but, I'll have Brand Frontier over in my lockerroom quicker then you would botch my trademark move making me his personal meat puppet, so I will keep my clowning on you, esse. Six words, esse. 'Konnan did it FIVE years ago.' Spyder, I'll be simple. You don't talk about me, I don't talk about you, unless we're talking about sharing a ring together for me to kick your South Los Angeles Stereotype Ass. Peace Action! Wrestling
