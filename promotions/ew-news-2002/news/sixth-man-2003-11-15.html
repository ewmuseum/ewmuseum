Sixth Man?

15 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Since the State Penn Match was announced only five of the six participants have been officially confirmed. We all know that Tony Rolo, Lance Jackson, Rock Startling, Ian English and Mark Blaze step into the chamber on November 30th, but who will be the last person to join them? Rumors have been flying around PRIME ever since, with several names cropping up, but finally Hin See has announced that all the speculation will end on ReVolution XVIII when the sixth participant will be officially revealed. That huge announcement will add more interest to an already highly anticipated show where for the first time ever, the Global Title will be defended at ReVolution on UPN. The last time the Global Title was on the line, it was way back on ReVolution II when Angello Malenko met Rik Reno in a Trail 2 Glory Lumberjack Match. The match eventually ended up as a draw when Tony Rolo, though to be too injured to compete at the show, decided to interupt and start dispatching chair shots as he pleased. Then there was no Champion though, at ReVolution XVIII Tony Rolo will have to defend his belt against Ian English who has shot through the ranks of PRIME in the last month. It's also worth noting that so far, the Global Title has NEVER been successfully defended and even if Rolo does defeat English, he then has to put the gold up for grabs again just six days later in Japan. In post ReVolution notes, Shane Wethers is still scheduled to face Weed Smoker this week, despite Doctors originally not clearing him to wrestle after the damage he sustained at the hands of Kincade this past Monday. Plus there are strong indications from the front office that Adam and The English Luchadore will be forced to defend their Tag Team Titles at Onslaught. Finally we have still no concrete details of who Toshiaki Motoki was meeting during ReVolution concerning training facilites and other promotion related services. PRIME
