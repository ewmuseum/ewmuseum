Fourth annual Chris Williams Memorial Cup Begins

5 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The card for the PWW Chris Williams Memorial Cup has been announced, showcasing some of the biggest names in the wrestling industry as they remember one of their own. Participants hail from PWW, XUW, OSW, UWF, FA, WWA, 4WW, FAW, GW, CW, EPW and WMW PWW presents: Chris Williams Memorial Cup 23rd November 2003 Live from Calgary-Alberta Canadian Airlines Saddle-dome CWMC Tournament Match 1 ~ Battle Royal ~ Joshua Dunn - Digital Mortality -Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid -Kenny Mercury -Fey McClancy -Sean Summers - Mike Marvelous – BlackJack – Saij - Samurai Bob - Jack "The Ripper" Nobbledy - The Shadow – Apocalypse – Horazon – Vortex - Chris Johnson - Dante Locks - Adam Law – Mana - Tony Stecchino - Akio Hakura - Rokku Akodo - Ely Braxton - Mal Somers - Wes Sanders - The Chuckster - Jason Hughes - Ben Jericho – Fnord – Keegan - Bubba Bailey – Jade – Chryste - David “Snake Eyes” Masters - Ritoru Joufu - Jean-Francois Lacavalier - Patrick Powers - 'Ruthless' Tommy Tremonti – Enigma - Akio Hakura - Exceptional Dave Hurst - Chishio no Tenshi - Vinnie Vice – Chushy - "The Extremist" Johnny Romeo – Angel - Sub-beast - Justin Evers - Noah Hanson - AWS Man - Jason Marah - Innuendo - Bone Crusher Inter-fed match ~ Tag team Grudge match ~ OSW - Politically Incorrect vs. UWF - The Phenomenons Four Winds Wrestling ~West Wind Title Match ~ Entrants to be announced PTC ~eXtreme Title Match - Hide and Seek Rules ~ Tristan Belmont© [FA] vs Fathom [WWA] vs. Sean McClancy [4WW] vs. Lee Smith [FAW] CWMC Tournament Match 2 ~ Elimination Match ~ Last eight (8) men from the Battle Royal face each other in an elimination match (pinfall/submission/countout) until there are only two (2) left.
