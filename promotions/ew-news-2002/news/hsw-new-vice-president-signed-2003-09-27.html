HSW: New Vice President Signed!

27 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was just announced by Joey Matthew that he has signed a brand new Vice President to Headstrong Wrestling! The new VP's name is Shane Summers. This is what Joey Matthew had to say: "Well, I hired him for many reasons. He is helping a lot in Headstrong Wrestling as of late, and he wasn't even under a major contract, he just wanted to help make HSW one of the biggest feds out there. That is what made me ultimately decide to promote him to Vice President. I believe that he will do his job and serve HSW just as well." HSW Reporter/Commentator Kris Red was on the scene to interview the two men, and after he spoke with Matthew, he went to interview Shane Summers, his comments were as follows: "Well Kris, I am very happy that Joey has promoted me to Vice-President. I will do my damndest to help keep this place the best! Now I did try to start a place[Fed] by the name of Xtreme Mayhem Federation, but once I got an offer to just work in HSW, I jumped all over the offer! I had no clue that I would become the Vice-President of such a great place, and its just a shock to me. I have been helping backstage, and I guess that is why I was promoted. Now if you will excuse me, I have work to do!" With this happening, there are rumors going around that he my be put in an on-camera role as VP as well, but there are speculations as to whether this will go down or not. Most believe he will not be brought into storylines so soon if this is to happen. Headstrong Wrestling welcomes the new Vice President Shane Summers to the fed and wishes him the best! Headstrong Wrestling
