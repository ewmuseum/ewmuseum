Kevin Hawk Wins The UTA World Title! And more from EXPLOSION!

23 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After 6 years with the company, and a lot of hell, the man formerly known as Kor, has done it. From mediocre, to horribly bad, to superstar, to WORLD CHAMPION, Kevin Hawk has paid his dues and stepped up to the plate. We need an art button damn it! :) Monday Night was insane in the UTA on Explosion. We learned of John Doe, the mysterious man being terminated. Then, when we where about to learn the big secret that the Spectre has been hiding, The Spawn stopped Ron Hall in his tracks by distracting him with having his car towed away. That's not all, Mr. Fantastic made his HUGE return to the UTA! After a year of absence, he returned. But that's not all. G-man of The Hell Razors got a good match from Klash, Kevin Hawk's former partner. Klash was on the way to victory until G-man's stable amtes stepped in, allowing him to hit a pedigree and fuel his anger for later in the night. Now, Dark has been a hated champion. Not because he's a bad guy, just misunderstood. He was hated for the way he won his title, but stepped in and defended it with honor agaisnt Kevin hawk. Fouling many attempts of The Hell Razors cheating. But fianlly witht eh help of brass Knuckles, Dark went down and got pinned. However, Klash ran to his aid, a bit late. This, though, set up the match next week with all three Hell Razors taking on Klash and Dark in a three on one handicap match. If Klash and Dark win, Dark gets a title shot with no interferances. The show was great and worth checking out, so head on over to the UTA now! United Toughness Alliance
