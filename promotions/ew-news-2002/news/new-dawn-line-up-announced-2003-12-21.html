New Dawn Line-Up Announced!

21 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Real and Graphic Entertainment is now set to make its return in 2004 as Tom Mason and company announce the full line-up for the return Pay Per View Event on January 9 in the ninth press release. The company filed into the press room on Sunday, December 21, 2004, set to discuss much news including the show scheduled in January. New Dawn would be live on January 9 and the promised announcement was the first six matches in the Heavyweight Championship Qualifications. Staying true to his word, Tom Mason made the following announcement for the six matches. "As we move onto the next issue, the introduction of the Heavyweight Championship Qualification matches, only twelve men on the nearly thirty man roster could be selected. As it is already known, Pat Dwyer and Shawn Kain will meet in a one on one contest to settle their own heated rivalry," Tom Mason explained. "These other six contests will determine the first few men to enter the Heavyweight Championship Gauntlet Battle at Enmity on February 20." Those matches included: Adrian Williams IV vs. Buster Gloves The Captain vs. Rey Campbell J.C. Mayhem vs. Devon Domain John Doe vs. Mr. Crazy Jesse Ramey vs. Jeremy Momma Payne vs. Nix Tom Mason added, "Of course, all of these contests will be wrestled under standard Real and Graphic Entertainment rules. There must be a winner in every contest, no matter if it is by disqualification, submission, or pin-fall. There is a twenty-minute time limit on every contest and if the time limit happens to be reached, I will make the final decision on what happens then and only then." The other big announcement involved the main event at New Dawn pitting Pat Dwyer versus Shawn Kain. The match has now been deemed to take place in "The Crucible." The only explanation for this match is as Tom Mason said in the press release this morning. "I mentioned that Shawn Kain and Pat Dwyer will be here next week as it pertains to their meeting at New Dawn. The reason they will be here is to sign the contract for their match in a nonphysical confrontation. It has also come to my attention that the match should not be so bland and boring... So, at New Dawn it will be 'The Crucible.' This match being one taking place in a modified steel cell, with a raised platform rubbing against the ring. Beneath that platform, on each respective side of the ring, will be a cage holding one man who will come into the match after five minutes. This is a team event because Pat Dwyer and Shawn Kain will each select two men to be on their side in this contest. And the winner will be determined by the first pin-fall on either side." More information on RaGE, New Dawn, and the main event for the show will be discussed next week in the final official press conference before RaGE returns!
