Exclusive: Indepth Look at Se7en Deadly Sins

20 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
For the first time, an event themed around a number. But that number is only the beginning. Real and Graphic Entertainment and EW-News bring you the full length, indepth preview of Se7en Deadly Sins for July 27! The show is one week away, and it has been eagerly anticipated for weeks now. It has finally come and on July 27, 2003, Real and Graphic Entertainment is pleased to present Se7en Deadly Sins live on Pay Per View from Baltimore, Maryland. In coordinance with this specially themed event, Real and Graphic Entertainment has signed a seven match card to start exactly at 7 p.m. This rare and obviously interesting themed Pay Per View will be beginning when most are setting up, and ending when most are actually getting to their good content. But one thing is certainly agreeable, there is not a bad match on this card for Real and Graphic Entertainment. From the bottom to the top, back down to the bottom, the company has managed to make an all-around solid show and it will be without a doubt something all fans will enjoy. There are intense rivalries, extraordinary match ups, and stipulations that will put any other show to shame on the night of July 27. Living up to it's company emblem, Real and Graphic Entertainment is destined to provide and showcase the realism and the graphic nature of it's product. And if the show's card and hype is not enough to do it, the company has dealt with the Pay Per View sponsors to offer the show for a special $20 price. Normal Pay Per Views reaching three hours will cost $30, but to gain more interest with a product that is already seemingly popular now, Real and Graphic Entertainment is out to do anything it can to surpass the competition. Now, without a further delay, here is what any fan purchasing the show can expect to see: No Boundaries Heavyweight Championship Hell in a Cell Chris Collins w/Victoria Vengeance versus The Duff © It has a rivalry that has built since November of 2002, when Tom Mason denied Duff numerous opportunities to face Chris Collins for the championship. Duff won contendership, but Tom Mason refused to honor the title Duff had won. But through all the struggling, Duff was finally able to earn himself a match with Chris Collins for Enmity on June 1. That night, Duff was able to Educate Collins and force him to tap out, winning his first Heavyweight Championship in his seven year career. Now, it is nearly two months later and Duff is being put to the test against Chris Collins again. Will Duff be able to sustain his reign as the Heavyweight Champion or will Chris Collins be able to reclaim the championship that he held for nearly seven months? Version Two Heavyweight Championship Pat Dwyer versus Shawn Kain © Dreams do come true, and for Pat Dwyer he is well on his way to making that life long wish of earning a Heavyweight Championship come true. Three years ago Pat Dwyer entered XCWF and spent his time trying to capture the gold of that promotion. The farthest Pat has gotten was the Extreme Championship. The man opposite of him, Shawn Kain, is only a one year wonder thus far, already capturing more titles and main eventing more events than Pat has in his whole career. There is a great deal of respect between both men, without a doubt. But Pat Dwyer is making it clear to Shawn Kain that he wants that championship and there is nothing he won't do to get it. He expects Shawn Kain to hold nothing back, just as he will come full force himself. Pat Dwyer makes his historic first main event spot in a Pay Per View at Se7en Deadly Sins... But will it be a successful one? RaGE Omen Championship Devon Domain w/Jamie versus Jesse Hallmark © It is another rivalry that has stemmed out of a previous event, only this one his more on a personal level than any other match you will see. Jesse Hallmark make the mistake of admitting to a relationship he had with Jamie, the fiancee of Devon Domain at the time of the beginning to this controversy. In May, Jamie had a child, whom was believed to be the son of Devon Domain. The couple had plans to get married, until Jesse Hallmark inserted himself into the picture. Hallmark claimed to be the father of the child and following some blood testing, a horrified Devon Domain watched as the results read that Hallmark was indeed not lying, he was the father of the child. Jamie was devastated and could not speak for quite some time. Devon demanded a match at Enmity on June 1, and he put the Omen Championship on the line. It proved only a mistake as the mental destroyed Devon Domain lost the title. But the truth soon came out as Jesse Hallmark decided not to hold back any more. In fact, Jesse admitted to lying about the child/father situation, revealing that he had switched the blood samples in the hospital. Devon Domain was shocked, both because he was the real father and because Jesse Hallmark had been lying to him. Jamie appeared to have known and just been forced into going along with this, but she unleashed her own strength on Jesse by hitting him with a chair. Now, Devon Domain has a score to settle with Hallmark at Se7en Deadly Sins. RaGE Tag Team Championships The Caperilli Brothers versus Total HaVok Two respectively different tag teams, and champions of their respective promotional backgrounds. Predator and Kamikazi, collectively Total HaVok, have an eight year history of winning championships and defeating some of the top talent in the tag team world. The competition, Vincent and Joey Caperilli, are fresh faces to the wrestling world, knowing only what their once wrestling star father, Vito Caperilli, tells them. Living their lives in the hopes of pleasing their father, Vincent and Joey are going up against their biggest challenge every and all for the chance to earn some respect. But if respect is earned by ambushing and assaulting one member of the opposing team of different occasions, so be it. At Se7en Deadly Sins, they will have to resort to facing Predator and Kamikazi in an evenly matches contest and only one team will leave with the RaGE Tag Team Championships. RaGE Extreme Championship Stretcher Match Payne versus Shawn Jackson (c) In April, Payne made his debut in Real and Graphic Entertainment. He set his eyes on one man, and that man happened to be the Extreme Champion, Shawn Jackson. Marked with a red and white bull's eye, Shawn Jackson has endured blow after blow at the hands of the monstrous Payne. With an undefeated singles record, Payne has yet to be totally dominated on one occasion. At MayDay on May 25, Payne faced Shawn Jackson for the first time, and the contest ended in a walk out as Payne threw Shawn off a 15 foot scaffold through the mess of production tables. That injury left Shawn Jackson on the disabled injury reserve until just recently, when he finally decided to make his return on television. Shawn never forgot what happened and made it a point his first night back to get revenge by surprising Payne in his locker room. That attack did nothing for Shawn as Payne walked away unscathed somehow and still managed to win an amazing match that very night. But on the debut of Uncensored Payne struck back and he made a point as he beat the living hell out of Shawn Jackson once again, forcing him to be loaded into a stretcher and removed from the ring. What no one knew until Payne said it, was that stretcher played an important part. That stretcher would be the meaning behind their rematch for Se7en Deadly Sins. It would be a stretcher match and the winner would be forced to load his opponent onto a stretcher and strap him down, carting him into an ambulance at ringside to win the match. Payne was definitely out to outdo Shawn Jackson. But will he? RaGE N.E.W. Championship Menace versus Evan Darkness (c) One year ago, Evan and Menace were partners in crime in the very infamous stable of the Board of Directors. Along with Mimic in XCWF, the three of them had more power and pull in the company than even Tom Mason for a while. But somewhere between then and now, that bond was severed. Menace had broken away from the B.o.D. in July of last year, and Evan Darkness only on few occasions went head to head with him. But when the two were to team up against The Duff a few weeks ago, Menace seemed to be focused on the spotlight and not worrying about his partner. Because of this jealousy and rage building up, Evan Darkness ended up turning around and delivering Achulophobia to his partner that night, and still winning the match for himself. Since then, there has been bitter tension between the two. The following week, both me were against Shawn Kain for the N.E.W. Championship and Evan Darkness was able to again take down Menace with Achulophobia and he pinned him to win the championship. All this has fueled a fire that is ready to burn down the First Mariner Arena at Se7en Deadly Sins on July 27. REBEL Tournament Finals Winner Faces NEW Champion @ Unleashed TBD versus TBD Four men are left to fight for the chance to meet the N.E.W. Champion at Unleashed. Eight men started in this small scale tournament that would name a man who would eventually scale up to the highest peak offered in the company. Look at what it did for Shawn Kain, one year ago he won the N.E.W. Championship at Unleashed and now he is the Version Two Heavyweight Championship. Last year, Menace won the REBEL Tournament against Jackson Rust, formerly of DWO. Menace ended up moving on to the Heavyweight Championship, winning it at Unleashed the following month. As for Rust, he created a stint with Primetime Championship Wrestling until it's departure from the business recently. The success comes to those who enter themselves and make it far in this tournament. This year, Boomer Cloud, Johnny Reno, DaShea Strong and Adrian Williams IV are left. Two of them will make it to the final match at Se7en Deadly Sins. The other two will have to wait until another night to be given a second chance at achieving a successful note in only one match. Which two will make it and which one man will win? Low and behold it is a show of huge proportions for both Real and Graphic Entertainment and the wrestling industry as a whole. And everyone can remember that if they are not able to catch the show live on Pay Per View, Real and Graphic Entertainment will offer a replay on Tuesday evening. For those fans without Pay Per View accessibility and those who just do not believe in spending the money, the company will have the full transcript of the show up on Rageproject.com Monday Night, July 28. http://www.rageproject.com
