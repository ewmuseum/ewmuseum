NWC's November Rankings

7 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
November has come and gone and Fox Sports Net along with Artis Inc. collaberated to put together NWC's November rankings... World Champion. BloodAngel (Champ) - BloodAngel continues his reign and is doing so in grand fashion. With a recent victory over the top two contenders to his title in the form of Sean Laughreah and Foster Nackedy the champion is looking towards Pacific and the ever-manipulative Alec Spade. 1. Foster Nackedy (2) - Just when you think the storm clouds have appeared and the rain will never stop, they part and a ray of light shines through. Foster Nackedy’s tragic existence got a small glimmer of light as he came up short in his quest for the NWC title but managed to recover and defeat Sean Laughreagh for the NWC: South championship. 2. Julie Reveli (4) - Julie Reveli has defied the odds her entire NWC existence and did so just recently in Pacific. With her championship on the line against the NWC’s most decorated competitor in the form of Tony Pride, Julie survived with a disqualification victory and retained her championship. 3. Sean Laughreah (1) - The NWC’s most powerful competitor Sean Laughreagh had a disappointing month in November. Competing for the NWC championship he came up short against BloodAngel, and then minutes later lost his Southern championship to Foster Nackedy. Jade’s recent decision to chase the Southern title complicates matters as Laughreagh has his heart set on regaining his crown. 4. Wyatt Connors (12) - Wyatt Connors has made an NWC career out of surprising others. Admittedly not the NWC’s most talented wrestler and certainly not its biggest, Connors has built a career on being one step ahead of those he faces. After defeating Lance Marshall for the second time in his career, a feat not many can claim, Connors has ironically been thrust into the spot of being one of the men to rescue the council from extinction. 5. Leonard Aarons (9) - NWC: Atlantic champion Leonard Aarons has set out to make a name for himself at the expense of some of the NWC’s veteran wrestlers. Despite a loss to BloodAngel for the NWC World Heavyweight championship he rebounded nicely by defeating Bobby Irish in his first major defense of his Atlantic crown. 6. Jade (3) - Despite losing the NWC title, Jade is still a woman to watch. Inactive for November she found herself dropping a few notches, but during her very own Jade Appreciation night she made it clear that she has one goal now. To regain the NWC: South’s championship, even if her lover, Sean Laughreah, stands in her way. 7. Devin Fox (8) - [name withheld]’s private doomsday weapon continues his march towards the Return to Purgatory. However Fox’s image as an unstoppable weapon has begun to come into doubt as Sean Slay surprisingly rolled him up and pinned him for the first time in nearly 2 years. The agenda still seems to be the destruction of the National Wrestling Council, but perhaps Sean Slay has shown that Devin Fox is not unstopable. 8. Bobby Irish (6) - Would the real Bobby Irish please stand-up. Bobby Irish continued towards Purgatory be defeating the massive Central champion, Nathan Boswell. Next up was a battle with NWC: Atlantic champion Leonard Aarons, where he was defeated by the young champion. Next up for this un-predictable competitor is perhaps the NWC’s most unpredictable man ever in Tony Pride as the two battle for the fate of the NWC. 9. Nathan Boswell (7) - The NWC’s biggest competitor has had a month of set-backs. He then lost in the Purgatory tournament to Bobby Irish, and then was pinned in a 6-man match by Sean Slay after Slay had already pinned Boswell’s mentor, Devin Fox. The Central champion now has to prove that he deserves his place atop that region and that he is a force to be reckoned with, even if it is at the expense of fan favorites Sean Slay and Lance Marshall. 10. Lance Marshall (5) - Few NWC competitors strike the same awe-inspiring image when staring across the ring as the NWC’s greatest hero, Lance Marshall. Yet recently dark times have begun to bear down on the NWC, and the NWC’s most heroic wrestler found himself losing to its least likely hero in Wyatt Connors. With times changing and a feud on the horizon with the NWC’s largest competitor, Nathan Boswell, one can only wonder how soon until Lance Marshall gets his shot at Julie Reveli. 11. Lazarus (10) 12.
