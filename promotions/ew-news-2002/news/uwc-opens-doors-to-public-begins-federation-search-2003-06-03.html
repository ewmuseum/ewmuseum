UWC Opens Doors to Public, Begins Federation Search

3 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The newly-formed Universal Wrestling Council announces its plans for the future. Details inside. Today, the Universal Wrestling Council officially opened its doors with plans of complete domination of the wrestling industry. Lead by the Universal Wrestling Council Board of Directors, most noteably Chairman of the Board Evan Matthews, the UWC plans to bring old-school tradition and values back to wrestling- while mixing it in with some of the best action the world has ever seen! "It's our opinion that today's wrestling world needs a group like the UWC again." said Evan Matthews at a press conference. "A governing body to determain the best wrestlers in the world- out of all the best promotions in the world! Only the very best can become the UWC Universal or International Heavyweight Champion!" Evan said that the UWC's talent scouts will be going across the world looking for federations to join the UWC, but any interested federations are welcome to join up by going to their online website and filling out a form. According to Evan, they'll fill up all eight spots in the Universal division first, launching that one first, and then fill up the eight spots in the. Once the Universal division has "a workable number", the first Breakdown card will go up. The first UWC Interfed-PPV is rumored to take place in July- we do know that I-PPV will feature the crowning of the first UWC Universal Champions.
