TWC 2 Night From Pittsburch

10 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A night filled with matches, struggle, and deception...so what's new? Tonight TWC 2 Night aired live from the Mellon Arena. In what was an action packed night, we were brought four strong matches. Opening up with Jeff Johnson vs Jiraya. Two men who want nothing more than to climb up the ladder in TWC, two men with nothing to lose and everything to gain. Both these men put it all on the line, with only man walking out the victor, that man being Jeff Johnson. Next TWC pleased the fans with a brutal Tag Team Table Match. Angus McCurt and Durgo Syn squared off against the luscious Ravyn and the Extreme Champion, Curt O'Hare. A brutal match, lots of wood, and a victorious Ravyn and O'Hare seemed to please fans from all over. In perhaps the show stealer of the night, "Prodcut of Hate" Josh Cantrall squared off against the highly touted Maelstrom. The young Cantrell may have sketched his name in stone as a top player in this one, as he finishes the match off with a variation of a powerbomb from the top rope, sending Maelstrom smashing face first into the mat. Just prior to the main event, the one man who can't get enough of himself, Dylan Brecht, made quick work of Romeo. Then it came time for the main event, the six biggest names in TWC all gathered in one ring. In one corner was the Millionaire's Club, consisting of TWC Champion NightTrain, Transcontinental Champion, Terence Madison, and Connor Banks vs former Trans. Champion Mark Gunn, Commissioner Drew Thomas, and possably the franchise wrestler of TWC, Trinity...what a match to cap off the show.
