IBJ pushed out of X Wrestling

17 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Impluse" Brian James has been X Wrestling since November of 2001, and his time seems to becoming to an end. It looks as if “Impulse” Brian James is finishing up his last days in Xtreme Wrestling Federation(or known as on TV as X Wrestling), as his contract expires on August 11th, 2003. The day after there Xtreme Violence Pay Per View. But that isn't the story. The real story is that it is rumored that IBJ doesn't want to leave XW. At first is was presented as part of an angle to the media and fans when on the June 10th, 2003 eXtreme Blazer said the following words. “I'm not going to fire IBJ.... no that is heartless, but I did notice that your contract runs out on August 11st, and after that date we will no longer need your services. So we will not be resigning you.” But it was later discovered that in fact was the truth that he was not going to be resigned. Rumors are floating around that someone in management is responsible for pushing IBJ out of a company that he has said on numerous occasions he loves. I have tried to get in contact with both Brian James and X Wrestling officials, neither have yet to return my calls. More information on this as I receive it. X Wrestling
