Jamal Wilson Speaks out on his debut with WWC.

24 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jamal Wilson made his feelings known today on wrestling for WWC. The promotion that Jamal claims is holding him 'hostage.' Jamal called “The News” today after reading the WWC’s press release on his signing and debut with the promotion to clarify a few errors in the article. “If Levine doesn’t want me on his roster, I implore him to take me off. I signed the contract in the summer because I felt as if the promotion had an interesting future and a decent roster,” Jamal began. “The article claims that I’m ‘unloyal’ to federations. Is unloyal even a word? What kind of promotion sends out press releases using words like ‘unloyal?‘ It sounds like something you’d hear on Rugrats. Well, anyway maybe when I was starting out in the business I made a few mistakes in spreading myself too thin, but I’ve learned my lesson. Evidence of this is my recent stint in the IWO. I worked hard for [name withheld] because I LIKE [name withheld]. He’s not the type of guy who will talk down on roster members in a promotion he works for or use every ounce of television time to put himself over. I worked for him up until losing a “Loser Leaves IWO” match to Kory Storm, which reveals another mistake in the article which stated that I disappeared after leaving the Asylum. In fact, knowing Paul Levine’s relationship with KroW and the possibility that he would become a roster member of WWC one day, I tried to get out of my contract. I asked Paul if I could be released and not put on any shows but he didn’t want that to happen for whatever reason. They’re holding me hostage. Look, if it was up to me, I’d go home to San Francisco or buy a house in Boca Raton, Florida to enjoy my time away from the business, but it’s not. I’m contractually bound to appear on WWC shows unless Paul Levine releases me, and since I bring publicity to the fed, I doubt he will. We all know how much of a publicity whore the guy is.” Before any questions could be asked if Jamal, Jamal hung up. He is scheduled to appear on WWC’s Menace #3 which is airing from the Mid-Hudson Civic Center in Poughkeepsie, New York on Saturday evening. The Home of a Promotion Jamal Wilson Approves Of
