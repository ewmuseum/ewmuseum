J. Leslie Voss says "No!"

3 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The hWo and J. Leslie Voss have been in conferences for the past few weeks trying to sign the 22 year old to a written contract, but Voss isn't interested. Since the start of November, the front office of the hWo has been trying to get the John Hancock (signature) of one particular roster member onto a written contract, but seemingly, it may be impossible for them to get it. They've been battling with top heel performer, J. Leslie Voss, to sign to a written contract but the Australian continues to reject their offers. "Originally, both parties decided that a verbal agreement would be the best idea for the time being as the stability of the promotion was at stake and we couldn't afford to put him (J. Leslie Voss) onto a written contract," Trevor Echo told press as the leak of the contract war seeped from hWo HQ. "But the performances given by J. Leslie Voss have been outstanding and he has become one of the faces of hWo besides our current World champion, Ike Rogers. Having J. Leslie Voss on a written contract would increase the stability of our promotion," Echo admitted. J. Leslie Voss began his professional wrestling career in thReat. But he only agreed to a verbal contract there as well, and after the period of one month was up, he told officials that he no longer wished to be a part of the promotion. During that time, Voss opened up his own promotion with friend Jefferson Phillips, the promotion lasted a little over four months, and in that time Voss held the Spinebuster Wrestling World title for three of the four. According to insiders, Voss did not want to job the title to Action! Wrestling star Conrad Ramsey and told Phillips, "if I wanted to job to old men I'd try-out for the WWE." And with that, Voss folded the promotion. He's made two guest appearances, the first being on Action! Wrestling Nationally broadcasted show, Pressure Point. Having made some friendships during his stay at SbW with Marcus Dox (Max Danger), Bradley Duncan and Randall Black (Rook Black), Voss was invited by Danger to appear on Pressure Point. The idea was to help with Danger's face turn and attempt at putting him over. Voss got the response from the crowd they were looking for, but Action! Wrestling's officials decided that they already had enough heels on their roster and weren't looking to exend on it. They felt that Voss' character would clash too much with other heels, like Keith Scott Zimmerman and Jeff Garvin for example, and therefore thanked him and paid him for his services without an offer of stay. Rumours have it that Voss as interested with staying around and working with the friends he'd met in Spinebuster. Voss WAS interested in signing a written contract with Action! Wrestling. He also appeared on ACW's Courage, where he worked the final main event of the promotion with World Champion Alias. Reports were Voss was close to signing a written contract, the first of his career, with the promotion. Unfortunately for both parties, the promotion folded. But for the last month, Voss has been showing his talents to the world on the stage of hWo's Desolation and plans on to continue to do so, but on his terms, not Echo's. "Yes, I'm enjoying my time here in hWo and plan to keep enjoying my time. But I feel if I sign a written contract I might be doing my career more harm than good," Voss told press yesterday. "I haven't even been in this business professionally for a year and I'm getting a lot of offers. I want to be in hWo for a long haul but I don't want to tie myself down with a written contract, I have plans to expand on my career. There was a time where I planning on signing but that was a bigger stage. But I don't think now is the time," Voss admitted. Trevor Echo says the hWo plans to continue to try and get J. Leslie Voss onto a written contract as they wish to keep him there for a very long time. Voss tells us doesn't plan on leaving hWo, he just thinks he'd like to have the opportunity of testing out other waters.
