Payne of Action?

25 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Payne Fosters' reply to the blantant attack from this Mister.E Earlier this morning I came searching this site for another Sylo bash fest, instead I found leaked information about the backstage problems of Action. Namely a fight between Bobby Minio and I last night at a house show about management. I complained sure, I don't like the way A!W is heading, it's gonna end up like wcw. None of that's the point though, this is about me and my side of the story, so that's what you are going to hear. Back when I joined A!W I had no idea what was going on, I had no idea about the fights between new management and roster but I found out about it fast. The new management was pushing for the A!W brand to be based soley on wrestling, leaving guys like Sylo, Krow and the rest of the 'PIW outlaws' fucked. Our ideas were shotdown time after time, we were shit on and branded as crap. Was I in PIW, no but I got treated like it. I couldnt get fueds cause management didnt find it necessary, they thought that all the wrestler should find their own and report to writers and that was bull, it left the top guys staying top and the new guys going no where, I was unhappy about this and god damn it I protested. My answer though was a foot in the mouth from just about every A!W superstar on the face of the fed, I got no where and I was binded by contract so I couldn't do anything but bitch. That's not to say that when the loose ends were tied I wasn't piss, mostly everyone who was happy was still happy and those who weren't just weren't saying anything anymore, or were backing out of their contract. I on the other hand couldn't back out of my contract, I couldn't get by without the money, so I went with it. This all lead to last night, the management were talking and I spoke my mind, got shutdown and ignored again, so I got pissed and left. When the meeting was over everyone came outside and I was still bitching, that was when the verbal fight between Bobby and I started. He didn't see things the way I did, he thought everything was a O.K, he called me a rookie, he called me immature, he called me this and he called me that. In the end he left and I stayed, I got all my shit out of my locker and left. When I got home I did some thinking, I had enough money to last till I found another job, why don't I just quit? I said nah, I'll just change to what they want but then this morning I read this little piece of trash off the internet, some little locker room jockey released it all and since they didnt know the whole story it came out as me the ass. I'm fine with that, whatever but this afternoon I called Reed and said I quit, cause I'm not gonna deal with it anymore. They wanna contact me and treat me with some respect that's fine, but until then F*CK THEM. The bottom line is, I've been in talks with the soon to open LoC and WWC, due to contractual agreement I cannot comment about either of them, but to my small cult of fans, you might wanna watch those. With A!W, I wanted to be part of it, I really did but it's too much like a wCw rerun, only thing is Reed is too stupid to give it up and start a new, or better yet go back to the old ways. P.S Before you all go to bash me about my grammar or mispelled words, remember, I'm not a writer, I'm a f*cking wrestler.
