Business

14 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Let's figure this one out together, esse. Let's figure out what makes the big man tick, what makes the little monkeys in your head rub their sticks together to try and make fire. It's a joke really, you trying to cut promos against me. You're so pathetic at it hommes, you don't know what you're doing, you're out of your league, out of your little safe zone. Pick up, lick up, suck up... go to school esse, get yourself some material. You're bombing here. Spyder Let me explain something about this business hommes, something many of you fans just might not be aware of. See, I don't want to expose the little business, but on the other hand, I do what I want, when I want, how I want. Managers and road agents they talk, and if it makes sense to my wallet, I listen. So I've been warned in doing this little internet column, warned by Buckly, warned by Fox, warned by the old timers, the self-proclaimed locker room leaders... warned not to give the business away. Well esse... I do what I want. In this business there are enemies. Lots of them. You take little muchacha Aimz. She's untalented, a locker room cancer, she talks too much, talks to the wrong people, gossips, and throws her weight around like she runs the fWo. I don't like her. I don't ride with her. If I'm in the ring with her, I slap the taste right out of her little lipstick mouth. That's my personal opinion. But esse, there are fans that come to the shows, who knows what the gringos are thinking, because of little girl Aimz. When fans come to the shows, I make money. When I make money, I take your mother and sister out for a steak dinner before I go back to the hotel room and ruin them like a dirty suit in a mud wrestling match. I hate Aimz and I hate her little clique. But Aimz on the show makes me money. So esse, that's what business is all about. I work with people who are beneath me, who can't compete with me, and who will never be at my level. I do it because it's money hommes, and don't forget it. On the other hand, you've got some yapping hardcore wannabe on an internet site who calls me little names and thinks he's in a fued with me. Esse, you're not in a fued with me. You don't make me money. You don't mean anything to me. At your level, you never will. I know it's cute hommes, to toss shit around. To use little insults that you learned in grade school. But seriously esse, 'a never was'? Really? 'A never was'? Come on hommes... come on. You're never going to last in this business if that is the best you've got. You question my background? What... can't you read esse? Somewhere between the steriods and the hair dye did you lose your ability to think? To read? Be hardcore hommes. Be hardcore. It will do you well when you're working in the supermarket. At the car dealership. Or hey esse, I'm real. Some little promotion will pick you up. By all means esse, start ragging on how you're not getting the same respect that you got at PIW. Complain and bitch about how you were a legend in PIW. Keep bringing the PIW ghost up, because you bury that preschool a little more each and every day you whine about it. You and your little wannabe friends. Do you talk about it hommes? Do you have little conversations where you whine about how fewer and fewer people buy your shit that you used to be a star? Do you? Yeah hommes, I can picture it. You, little Minio, little Sharc... all sitting around and drinking coffee and getting madder and madder than nobody cares anymore? That fewer and fewer fans remember? Esse, you've got to do something to be remembered. Whining like an old woman... you can see that on any street corner. You're not special... you're one of a million just like you. Me, I'm one in a million. Ask anyone. Ask your mother... assuming you know who she is, hommes. So you tell me esse, how are we different? I can talk about the title reigns, the championships, the awards, the promotions... the years of being at the top of this industry. Been there gringo, done that. I can talk about how, unlike yourself, my promotion knows no matter how much trouble I cause... getting the promotion banned from the country esse... I'm worth too much to let go. Yours... well esse, I cut promos management isn't happy with every week. You... you're easily replaced. Does it hurt, or have you fooled yourself into thinking you're better than them? Did you drink too much of Cusi's little Kool-Aid and have forgotten what it takes to make it in your business? I'm sorry hommes... I don't mean to laugh... but did you really think you were worth something? With an interview ability on the level of a grade schooler? With matches that, let's face it, remind us all the most of the Big Bossman as the Hardcore Champion of Vince's little playground? Poor boy, esse, poor boy. Did nobody else have the guts to tell you? Did nobody else respect you enough to explain what you were worth? Sorry hommes, I don't spare feelings. I just tell the truth. Take it from someone who is more successful in this business than you ever will be... or just listen to the countless people on the internet who may hate me, but have to respect what I am to professional wrestling. Wash out your little Britney Spears hair die, learn how to wrestle, my god hommes, learn out to actually cut an interview, and maybe you can be something. Somewhere. Maybe one day you can help put money in my pocket. Right now, this is pro bono... you're not making me anything arguing with you on the internet. This is charity work, esse, and if I ever met a wrestler than belonged on the little school bus, it's you. You may notice hommes, that I'm not mentioning you by name. No oversight esse... see, remember the earlier point. You're not making me money, hommes, I'll mention you by name again when you're worth it. For now? Revenge hommes... watch the shows. All the shows. fWo Wrestling
