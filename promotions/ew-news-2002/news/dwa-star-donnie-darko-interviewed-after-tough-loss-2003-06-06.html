DWA Star, Donnie Darko, Interviewed After Tough Loss

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just an hour after losing the tag team championship to the FoW and mere moments after Jordan Brannigan hostilly tookover the DWA from Mason Stone, Pete Henderson sat down to get Donnie Darko's reactions. (The hotel lobby is empty for the most part, considering that at least one section of it has been blocked off for DWA purposes. Those exact purposes would be the exclusive interview that Pete Henderson is about to conduct with Donnie Darko. Both men are sitting across from each other in the comfy chairs provided by the hotel staff. No time is wasted before Pete starts things off...) Pete Henderson: Okay, Donnie, what say we skip all the formalities and just get right down to it? Donnie Darko: (chuckles) Works for me. I'm always willing to lay it on the line. Pete Henderson: Glad to hear it. Now then, we're going to have to start with what happened at Hostile Takeover. You, and Derek Cantina, lost the tag team titles to Future of Wrestling. How do you feel about that? Donnie Darko: (smirks) Okay, let's back the train up for a moment, Pete. We didn't exactly -lose- to FoW. There were a couple of factors that played a major part in our defeat. Pete Henderson: By factors you mean... Donnie Darko: Brad Keidis and the Dark Angel Evo Shandor. Neither one of these guys had any reason to be down there and interfering in Intoxicated Justice business. But, around here, that's pretty much the norm. Anyway, as for my feelings on what happened, I'm a little pissed off. I mean, FoW didn't even -beat- us. It was a fluke. Pete Henderson: A fluke that has made them the tag team champions, though. Donnie Darko: Yeah, well, every dog has its day, right? Pete Henderson: True. Moving on, I have to get your thoughts as to the rest of the night. Especially, concerning the fact that Jordan Brannigan now has complete and total control of the DWA. Donnie Darko: (with narrowed eyes) Jordan..... (Pete blinks and tilts his head. Okay, he's never heard Donnie sound like that before. The tone is obviously anger with a hint of hatred behind it.) Pete Henderson: Donnie, are you alr..? Donnie Darko: (interrupting Pete) There are some men, Pete, that in the grand scheme of things... really push my buttons. Two of those men did exactly that at Hostile Takeover. Those men are Jordan Brannigan and Gideon Nazareth. (pause) And y'know what, I could be puttin' my job on the line here, but I honestly don't give a damn. What the Dynamic Dumbasses did to Mason? It was cute. Real cute. But I'll tell you one thing right now... whatever Jordan has up his sleeves for the DWA, he better just leave it up there. Cuz Darko ain't goin' nowhere baby. Nowhere. Pete Henderson: So, I guess it's pretty safe to assume that you don't trust him. Donnie Darko: Hell-no, I don't trust him. Never have. The man's got Lex Luthor Syndrome or something. That being the case, for every little step he takes... I'm gonna' take two. Just to make sure the DWA stays afloat during his evil, tyrannical reign.... Pete Henderson: So, you believe that Jordan has nothing but bad intentions in mind for the DWA? Donnie Darko: And then some. I'm Donnie Darko, Pete. I can smell the stench of evil a mile away. And both Jordan and Gideon wreak of that stench. Pete Henderson: I see. Well, before we wrap things up, let's take a gander into the future shall we? What's next for Intoxicated Justice? Donnie Darko: First of all, let me reassure everyone that Intoxicated Justice ain't goin' nowhere. We may have lost the battle, but the war is far from over. We owe the FoW a little somethin' somethin', and you can bet that when all is said and done, they'll get theirs... and once again, we'll be the tag team champions. Trust me, on that one. Pete Henderson: And for you, personally, Donnie? Donnie Darko: For me? I dunno. I don't know what's next for me. I've got a mission and a duty to the fans and to the Dragon Wrestling Association. And that duty is to serve and protect all those that can't protect themselves. To fight for what's right and what I believe in... no matter what the odds are. Right now, though, the only thing I can do is wait. Wait for moves to be made. And then, when the time is right.... I'll make my move. Pete Henderson: Any last words? Donnie Darko: Yeah. I've got a few words for some certain people in the DWA right now... (The camera gets in close on Donnie, so that the seriousness in his eyes can be felt at full effect.) Donnie Darko: Don't make me angry. You wouldn't like me, when I'm angry... (Fade) DWA: Dragon Wrestling Association
