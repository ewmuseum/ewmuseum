McW takes Chatanooga by storm

16 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The official card for the Dec.21st Saturday Night Implosion has been released. MaXimum Championship Wrestling invades the UTC Arena in Chatanooga, Tennessee The UTC Arena in Chatanooga, Tennessee is making preperations when it hosts the Dec. 21st Saturday Night Implosion! The flagship program for the highly rated MaXimum Championship Wrestling or McW, is always LIVE and in your face. Recently signed Doug Silver, or the 'Sparkler' as he is called, goes one on one with Steel. In more singles action, 'Judge Platnium' Ken Justice looks to stablize his place on the McW roster when he takes on 'The Great White Dope' Corey Letson. Letson looks to add his second victory to his record. All Hell breaks loose, and I mean that literally, when The Devil steps into the squared circle to take on McW hopeful, B.L. Dangerous Brian Lee. The ring heats up as six man tag action is up next. 'The Ego' Landon Rice teams up with La Famiglia, the team of 'The Italian Stallion' Sonny Perelli and 'The Italian Pitbull' Gino Battisti. Thier opponents, the reigning X Division Champion, 'The Storm' Tadashii Kaiou, teams up with 'Master of the moment' Jay Gold and The Ginga. Another tag match pits the legendary tag team duo, Taxx and Nailz, the Grave Diggas, against 1/2 of the Lynch MOB. Reigning Co-holders of the McW King of Kings Championship, the team of 'The Company Psycho' Al Cohol and 'The War Machine' Grinder. Better known as The Tormentors. It's a night for great Tag Team action. 'The Billion Dollar Man' Freddy Ryder and his partner, 'The Undesirable' The Desired Toe do battle with the team of 'The Epitome' Scotty Idol and his partner, the reigning McW United States Champion, WidowMaker. Special Stipulation: Freddy's brother, Flash Ryder will serve as Special Guest Referee. And ending a Tag Team night, the Main Event pits the team of 'The Man without Fear' Andrew Bishop and 'The Flash and Flare of Pro Wrestling' Seth Caliko up against the other 1/2 of the Lynch MOB, 'The Headliner' Hart Evans and the reigning McW Undisputed World Champion, Rick 'Acid' Jones. Be sure to tune into the Fox Network Saturday night at 8pm EST 9 central. McW
