Lisa Tien Slams DJ Cool Claims

22 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Following the recent claim made by DJ Cool, who was linked with the fWo, Lisa Tien, has publically made it clear that DJ Cool didn’t receive any offer to work for Furiously Addictive Wrestling. Following the recent claim made by DJ Cool, who was linked with the fWo, Lisa Tien, has publically made it clear that DJ Cool didn’t receive any offer to work for Furiously Addictive Wrestling. Lisa Tien called for an immediate press conference in Minneapolis, Minnesota, the location for this Sunday’s Panic, FAW’s weekly show, to play down the claims made by DJ Cool. Lisa Tien, in an argumentative mood, had this to say: “First Skylar Green, now DJ Cool. It appears as though people enjoy linking themselves with FAW. We don’t mind. But what we do expect is the truth, at least. Not fabricated stories, which tell nothing but lies.” Lisa Tien didn’t look too happy with the comments made by DJ Cool, but slowly turned buoyant, and continued: “DJ Cool was linked with the fWo? Hah, please. Having appeared on fWo Television myself, most notably to bring back the IOW World Title from current fWo United States Champion, Voran Xias. I can safely say the likelihood of DJ Cool entering the fWo can be put to bed. He isn’t fWo material or FAW material for that matter. We never discussed a contract with him and don’t intend doing so in the near future.” Lisa Tien wasn’t finished there. “When DJ Cool says ‘a place like that’ when referring to Furiously Addictive Wrestling, I think it’s evident that his attitude simply stinks. I’d recommend DJ Cool take ego therapy for his tragic illness.” Lisa Tien put to rest the DJ Cool claims and in familiar fashion, as she left the media speechless with her comments. We’ll give you more news on this story, as and when we receive it. FAW: Furiously Addictive Wrestling
