DWA Backstage Party: The Night Before Testament

30 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last night, the DWA took a break from all the usual feuds and business, and had one of the biggest parties in wrestling history. The DWA Backstage Party was held in the parking lot of the Astrodome, the site of DWA's biggest pay per view, Testament. It was a free all-day celebrate where several DWA superstars made appearances, and there were a ton of local musical guests during the day. However, at night, things got that much bigger, as did the concert talent. DWA superstar, EZ Target, took the mic and rapped for the crowd, much to their delight, then fellow DWA superstar, Derek Cantina, brought out his band, The Whiskey Junkies, and played a great rendition of "Drift Away" the current Unkle Kracker hit. The night didn't end with them either, as the real stars of the night came out...Linkin' Park played two of their hits songs, both of which have been used in the DWA's past. Through-out the night things happened though. Grave Matters made a huge DWA appearance. The former World Champion, who had his career cut short by Stax Jackson a year ago, made a dramatic return, but it was just a one time appearance. Despite this one time appearance, his number one nemesis, Gideon Nazareth made an appearance and the two went nose to nose, but it didn't come to blows. Also, Typhoid Mary finally found out who her other "admirer" was, after finding out that one of them was Faust, her former lover. And while Mary and Faust meet at Testament in a brutal hardcore match, she was not expecting who she saw at the Party. Lazlo Ferngelder, former DWA superstar and Mary's ex-husband, made a shocking return to Mary's life. He just came right up to her, and told her he was back. He said he knows she is with LeStatt Knight, and that he is a good man. However, one thing was very different about Lazlo. Apparantely, in his year long absence, something severe happened to his arm, as he now wears a prosthetic. However, it was not the reunion Lazlo was hoping for, as Mary knocked him down with a hard right hand. The party came to an end, with challenger for the World Title at Testament, LeStatt Knight, made a brief appearance, and said simply..."Twenty-four Hours." Tomorrow night, the DWA presents, Testament only on ppv. DWA: Dragon Wrestling Association
