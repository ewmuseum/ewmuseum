President's Review: Assault 10/9/03

14 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Another review by the President of his own show and the fighters on it. Another good show, and NO commercials! Well, it seems that we're together once again, as it has come to me to review Assault for the ninth of October. I usually need an icebreaker for this sort of thing, so let's focus on our World Champion, Inphino Blitz. He's seeming to have some head problems (more than usual, anyway). It seems like he's completely disbanded the way of Miyabi. But, why did he do that in the first place? Here's this week's Top Five Reasons Why Inphino Blitz Went Miyabi in the First Place. 5) He was beaten with a jackass stick shortly before making the transition from the Black Dragon Master. 4) He may have known 40+ fighting styles, but the Black Dragon Master couldn't pay the bills. 3) It was the top suggestion on a career aptitude test. 2) He didn't have the heart for a gay gimmick, but desperately needed to try something new. 1) Some black guy sitting at a computer dared him to do it. But with that, let us move into the Assault Review. No commercials this time around. I suspended the commercials because we weren't actually making any money from it. PCW, PWC, LOW... all closed. No one was paying us, but no one bothered to check on it. 1) Jake Hancock makes his return, and Tony Scarpo (who has no actual authority whatsoever in Old School Fighting) grants him a match with Marcellus Cosell. I quickly went to work to actually make that happen. Hancock, done with the family emergency that took him away, was eager to get back into the octagon. 2) Boon Crocket asks a rather well-dressed Olf Svensson how he could afford the suit that he was wearing. Olf said that Carranza just scored a lucky hit to beat him. 3) Inphino Blitz comes to see me in my office, and just talks about nonsense. Gloats about his attack on Kenyatta Ahmed Jackson, then says that someone else will have the same happen to them. He has a serious ego problem, but at some point I know that he'll blow up and just go Ken Shamrock on the entire front row. 4) Marcellus Cosell left a note on Agent X's door. Basically, he's refusing any chance of an apology and coming after X. 5) Haley Hilton, who hasn't been seen since she teamed with Matt Jackson -- who seemed to have a better success rate without having to carry her in the ring -- in PCW is now Jake Hancock's manager. She tries to give him words of encouragement. Just a moment ago, Jake was really eager and sure of himself about getting a fight tonight. Now that he has one, he's worried that he won't do well. I hope he's got a bi-polar problem, otherwise it's REALLY not healthy to flip right from one feeling to another in less than five minutes like that. 6) Jake Hancock v. Marcellus Cosell. Starts with Duke and Paulo doing the typical antagonistic commentators deal. They start slap-boxing each other; Paulo's a month or so shy of 18, Duke's been making people cry for decades. Paulo better watch himself. As for the actual match, there is some evident rust on Hancock, as he can't keep up an attack for very long. Cosell outfights him and wins the match by submission. 7) PMS is summoned to my office, a brief chitchat about the alleged pay-off to allow Carranza to win. I just let her know that if it was true, then don't do it again. If it was false, then she simply needed to be more careful abou her actions that they aren't misconstrued as a pay-off. She, of course, couldn't care less. Ah, to be a minor and completely infallible again.... 8) Olf Svensson talks on the phone with someone. It's not a very good conversation, apparently. He breaks a bathroom stall door, which he of course pays for. 9) Agent X and Chris Locke bump into each other. Following the usual male egotism and bravado, they talk some more. Agent X criticizes Chris Locke on how he conducts his business, but then Locke challenges X to a fight. X is worried that Cosell will sneak up and attack him. He's very startled by everything and visibly nervous. You know, for a guy who was supposed to be like this supersoldier/superspy capable of handling entire surgical strikes on his own, he seems a bit too afraid by just another guy who wants to punch him in the face. Might just be me. 10) Inphino Blitz gets rid of Miyabi, Miyabe, Grey Ichiro, Cianan, and Lillith. Keeps Mina, though. 11) Jake Hancock attacks Olf because the DaVattas apparently bet on his winning against Miguel Carranza III. This does one of a few things. The first, it presented televised evidence against the DaVattas that they are betting significant sums of money on sporting events and recooping their losses by extorting money from the athletes. Nice job, Jake, I'm sure that the DA will be thrilled at your zeal to expose your employers. Second, it allows me to fine Hancock.... oh, let's say one hundred fifty thousand dollars. And just to make sure that you shut the hell up about it, for any mention on any media anywhere in the world that $150 thousand is a trivial amount, I can easily increase the amount of the fine until which point it actually becomes a punishment and not just a trifle. And the third, Olf Svensson needs only to name the place and he's got you in a cage with my blessing. Have a nice day, ya'll. 12) Mina De Margo is happy to have things back to the way they were, though I see to recall Blitz being less abrasive back in the day. Olf bumps past, Inphino acts as though he's going to kick ass, but instead just walks away. 13) Lillith, distraught at being cut loose by Inphino, is crying as she goes through the hallway. Not really having a place to just go and be alone, I let her use my office. I mopped up after some spilled soda, and Manuel Labor made a really quick appearance. 14) X is still scared of That Black Guy. He has to get coffee delivered to his room. Come on, X, don't be such a punk about it. Cosell has another note sent to X. 15) Agent X against Chris Locke, back and forth until Sonny Silver's music distracts Chris Locke, allowing X the knockout win. Well, well, it seems as though these two just can't sleep at night without causing some sort of distraction in each other's matches. It isn't bad enough that you guys are going to be cleaning the highway roadside of garbage? Very well. Thanks to Sonny Silver and Chris Locke's attempted-transgression last week, you'll both be helping sponge-bathe elderly women at Tranquil Waters Home for Senior Citizens. A little word of warning -- sometimes, they get so old and everything droops so much that the uterus can actually fall out. In those situations, you just have to grab it and stuff it back in. Now, that's the new community service punishment for both of you, spending 20 hours a week helping out in the home. As you two continue to find ways to distract each other and ruin fights, I'll keep finding ways to add to your community service punishments until the point that I feel suspension is the only friggin' thing that you'll listen to. 16) Gideon Poe is backstage. We just chat for a second. I did indeed offer him a contract for OSF, and he seemed more than happy to oblige. 17) Shawn Stewart apologizes for his actions and promises to beat the living hell out of Matt Register, somehow. 18) Miguel Carranza and PMS talk about the degrees of victory and their respective roles. If you want more detail, watch the segment on the website, it's there. 19) Sonny Silver and his family are having a meeting about something. I don't even know what. 20) Shawn Stewart and I talk about my sending him home last week. I say that I'll try to get him another shot at Register, which I'll do. 21) Marcellus Cosell comes out and talks about how he was wronged, which he was. Of course, unlike last week when he sounded like Booker T, he's adopted more of an Eddie Guerrero slang for this interview. Ends with Cosell telling X that he's on the hitlist. Is that like Richard Nixon's list? 22) Dj Gallagher -- don't ask, I don't know who he is either -- shows up, talks about how everyone ignored MET II, and promises to make people pay attention to him. 23) Cosell runs into X in the back and attacks him. Tsk, tsk. I wish this place could go a week without somebody getting themselves punished. 24) Shawn Stewart now declares Sonny Silver to be his next victim. 25) Tony talks about Olf, and Hancock says that he'll take care of it. 26) Sonny Silver shows up in a Seahawks uniform... that's like showing up to a KKK rally dressed like a Black Panther. Though it does go back and forth, Stewart wins in the end by knockout. Old School Fighting
