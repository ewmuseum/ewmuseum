OSW SLAM! On The Air!

18 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A few days after taking place, OSW SLAM! is being replayed tonight and tomorrow at 9pm est. times. If you haven't already seen it, be sure to check it out, and see the preview below for details! Kicking off its touring, OSW pulls out all the stops to show why it is the top promotion in the world today! The show will feature pay-per-view quality matches, along with a daring feat, and bloodshed with a side of betrayal. And to start off the night, we will see Dave Gibson take on Silvio Rochelli in a match for the rights to use the name Cha-Ching! Both men use this name as trademark moves, and after Sunday, one man will be the sole owner of the name. There's only one way to find out who will be the one Cha-Ching'ing next week. Hoty Williams returns! And he faces off with M2 in his first match back in OSW after his injury. Sunday night, it will be rapper vs. preacher, ghetto vs. heaven. Which one will come out on top as Your Personal Jesus squares off against The Lyrical Assassin? After her betrayal of Scott Taylor, Teresa Tomas makes her in-ring return as she goes up against another who is making an OSW return; Kraig Carter. Can Carter make the transition back to wrestling smoothly, or will it be Teresa Tomas walking away with a "w" next to her name? An Anywhere Falls Match. A perfect place for two men with so much history and hate to come and unleash that in front of a live crowd. That's exactly what will happen when Miyagawa goes toe-to-toe with Boda. The first time in over a year, the brothers-in-law will see who will be victorious on this deadliest of playing fields. The Forsaken get their shot at gold, as they take on William S. Powell and Asa Fountain. Freddy has officially been banned from ringside, so it will be fair game between the two teams. The Forsaken have had a recent run of luck, but will it end at the hands of the unlikeliest tag champions we have seen in a long time? And in the main event, Ivan Stanislav defends his OSW American Heavyweight Title against the challenge of Jeffrey Wayne. Many people have called Wayne the "Marty Jennetty" of Ronin, but with a win over Stanislav, coupled with his recent luck, and he could easily silence the critics. The question is...can he do it? Nerezza will be looking for revenge on The Solution. Will he find it? Also, what will come of DarkStar's daring shark jump, or Ivan's hunt for the kidnapper of Jane Reagan? If you seek, you shall find...at OSW SLAM! OSW SLAM!
