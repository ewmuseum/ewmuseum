The X's first family deported

13 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Family of Gypsies, Thieves and Whores get sent back to Bulgaria The Bulgarian family, the Romanov's, were deported today. The family of gypsies, thieves and whores had illegally entered the United States in late April. Mila, Pavel, Viktor, Filip and Nikolai appeared regularly on The X's "After Dark" show since it's opening and Pavel wrestled his last match in the main event slot against Lumi. Ryan Miller, the man who suggested to Jason Marah that "The Romas" would be a good signing denied knowing the family were illegal immigrants. He said: "Of course I didn't know! Who are you working for? What sorta of man do you think I am?...Don't answer that." The rest of The X's foreign legion may become suspect to an investigation also. The X
