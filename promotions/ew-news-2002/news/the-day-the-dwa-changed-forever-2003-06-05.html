The Day the DWA Changed Forever

5 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In their latest pay per view, the entire DWA was changed forever. Short Results Televison Championship Swinger (c) vs. Brad Keidis --Brad Keidis becomes the first member of the Future of Wrestling (FoW) to capture gold in the DWA by defeating Swinger. Velocity Championship Grovel (c) vs. Zula Shandor --After months of competition between the two of them, and neither earning a clear cut victory against the other, this match would end all the discussion as there must be a winner. Loads of near pinfalls, but in the end, Zula captures her first title here in the DWA by pinning Grovel and winning the Velocity Title. --After Zula's match, she is viciously attacked in the hallways on the way back to her lockerroom. Her attacker? Brad Keidis, who won the TV Title earlier in the evening. After a vicious attack, where Zula's nose was apparantely broken, Keidis swore it was just business, although who's business, nobody knows. World Tag Team Champions Intoxicated Justice (c) vs. FoW --After some great tag team action, the referee went down, and Brad Keidis, who was at ringside with the FoW, decided to interfere. He attacked Donnie Darko with a huge thrust kick, but then The Dark Angel Evo Shandor showed up on the seen, seeking revenge for Keidis attacking his sister. Evo accidently nails Derek Cantina, and then goes after Keidis, chasing him into the back. In all the confusion though, Drastic rolls Donnie Darko up, pulling the tights. Despite the tainted victory, it is a victory none the less, and the FoW capture the World Tag Titles. --later in the night, Mason Stone, the Owner of the DWA, was scheduled to take on Jordan Brannigan, the President of the DWA, for full ownership, in kind of a loser leaves town match-up. However, earlier in the week, Mason was injured. He stated that since he was the challenger, he could name his own replacement, so he names former World Champion, Gideon Nazareth to represent him in the Main Event. Jordan leaves in a state of shock. The Revolution vs. LeStatt Knight and ??? --Knight has been at odds with Payne and Wylde ever since they joined the DWA. When Knight made his return in early May, The Revolution added one member, in the form of the giant and one time friend of Knight, Raziel. The three dominated Knight all month for the most part, but Knight kept coming. The Revolution gave him a match to take on the three of them with a partner of his choosing. That partner turned out to be...Raziel, as Knight pulled one over the eyes of The Revolution. The two friends took it to The Revolution, but in the end The Revolution, using some very dirty tactics and a steel chair, had Payne pinning the big man, Raziel, allowing for The Revolution to capture the victory. World Heavyweight Championship Typhoid Mary (c) vs. Loki --Mary captured the title from Loki last month, and now Loki wants it back. This match was great, and after a lot of back and forth action, Loki's partner, Dacar, decided he wanted in on the action, but Mary put an end to that, sending him through the announce table. Loki would then almost deliver his devestating finishing move, but Mary would reverse it, nailing a huge inverted spike DDT from the top rope to retain the World Title. However, all was not good for Mary, as The Revolution made their way to the ring, and while Jeff Wylde stood guard, Payne proceeded to destroy Mary, and laying down all but a challenge against her. Fate of the DWA Gideon Nazareth vs. Jordan Brannigan --Jordan is not a wrestler, and it showed in this match, as Gideon bullied him around the ring. However, Jordan got some good shots in, eventually slamming Gideon into the steel post, taking him down. However, this is where things get interesting. Mason Stone, who was sitting ringside begins to yell at Jordan. Then Norville Matthews, one of the commentators, climbs into the ring, hits the referee with a taser, and takes over duties. Jordan and Mason begin to fight, with Jordan getting the advantage, and injuring Mason's arm further, but then Gideon nails him and throws him back into the ring. However, then the ultimate "huh" happened, as Jordan nails Gideon with his finger, and Gideon goes down. Jordan pins Gideon easily. As the crowd is in shock, Jordan Brannigan is the new owner of the DWA. And this was a point capitalized on, as Gideon Nazareth completely destroyed Mason Stone on the outside of the ring. With Jordan Brannigan in control, what does the future hold for the DWA? DWA: Dragon Wrestling Association
