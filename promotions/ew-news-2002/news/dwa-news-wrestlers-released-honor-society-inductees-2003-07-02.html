DWA News: Wrestlers Released, Honor Society Inductees

2 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After Dead Men Walking, several wrestlers received good news, while others received bad news, as Jordan, in the same breathh, welcomed five new members into the DWA Honor Society, and then turned right around and released several wrestlers from their contracts. The Good News With Dead Men Walking over and done with, it is time for another DWA tradition to be announced. Every year, after Dead Men Walking, five new inductees are announced into the DWA Honor Society. This year was no exception. This years inductees were as followed: Former World Heavyweight Champion, Loki Dead Men Walking Royal Rumble Winner, LeStatt Knight Current World Heavyweight Champion, Payne DWA Resident Superhero, Donnie Darko and finally, Former American Champion, Pepper Ortega Each one of them have made a profound impact in the DWA since their arrival, specifically in the last six months. They now join the ten other Honor Society members. The Bad News After Dead Men Walking was over, Jordan Brannigan called several wrestlers into his office and told them they were immediately released from their contracts. This came as a complete shock to many of the wrestlers given their release, but some saw it coming on the heels of former World Champion, Blue Lightning's release. The wrestlers that were released are Robert Cummings, Brad Clinton, Impact (who made his return to the DWA at Dead Men Walking), and NeX. The two other names were originally listed as being released, but later turned out they were put on probation. Those names were recent Honor Society inductee, Pepper Ortega, and Jimmy Changa. They have been given this month to start producing in the ring once again, or their contracts will be terminated. "I'm not going to have wrestlers under contract who come out to the ring and simply lose all the time," Jordan Brannigan said when asked about the sudden releases. "Likewise, I'm not going to have wrestlers sit at home who aren't doing anything except collecting a pay check." Jordan feels that wrestlers should either produce or get out, and if they aren't willing to leave on their own, then he'll help them out, which is exactly what he did.
