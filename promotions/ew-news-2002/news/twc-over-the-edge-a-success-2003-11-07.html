TWC Over The Edge a Success

7 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A stellar pay per view, big returns, big debuts has TWC riding high... TWC this past Sunday followed up their biggest Pay Per View of the year, Mercury Rising, with another great PPV, Over The Edge. The night was huge for TWC. With several titles on the line, Drew Thomas was able to walk out of a three way match the new TWC Transcontinental Champion, defeating Chase Martell and Sammeal. Inferno was able to win a very brutal match to defeat Durgo Syn to become the fifth Extreme Champion in TWC history. We also the debut of former uW mainstays, fWo (funky World order), much to the pleasure of fans world wide who eat up their beer chugging, disco dancing, bell ringing, womanizing ways. Two new faces were also introduced in the sense of Sylence and his barrage of circus freaks, leaving TWC fans worldwide wondering just what this man represents and the debut of God's Machine, a mystery character who is primed to make an everlasting impression on the world of wrestling. Old faces returned, too, in perhaps the two biggest stories of the night. After being suspended for approximately three months The Fire and Fury, Dante Saffron made a shocking comeback, decimating his arch nemesis, former TWC Champion NightTrain as the The Project was revealed, a group of wrestlers lead by CEO Seamus McGibbons, who declared Dante Saffron the next TWC World Champion. Still not done, Saffron would interrupt the Main Event, assuring there was no finish after taking out the champion, Terence Madison. And what a cliffhanger. On the same night the hated Seamus McGibbons made a huge power play on his powertrip, Thomas McGibbons, TWC Owner, awakened from his coma leaving McGibbons and The Project in shock.
