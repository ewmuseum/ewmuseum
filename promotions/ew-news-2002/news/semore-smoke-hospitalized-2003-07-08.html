Semore Smoke Hospitalized!

8 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As seen on hWo's Desolation 4, Semore Smoke was brutally attacked at the end of the show by Lucas Field... Semore Smoke Hospitalized!!! Close friend of mine, Semore Smoke, has informed me that he has been hospitalized for the past couple of days, and will remain in the hospital through the week due to his injuries substained at the end of the last hWo Desolation from his former tag team partner, Lucas Field. Lucas Field was unavailable for comment on his actions, and hasn't been seen since his attack on Semore Smoke. But it has been noted by others that he did seem extremely pleased when he left the arena Sunday night after the show. Semore Smoke however left the arena in an ambulance and has been administered by doctors on a daily basis due to head injuries substained from several blunt blows to the head with a lead pipe. Semore Smoke needed 12 stitches to the forehead and is undergoing physical theropy to try and help him walk straight again, Smoke has made great progress since Sunday and it looks like he'll be out of the hospital and ready to go within two weeks! You can bet Semore Smoke will be looking for revenge on Desolation 6, when he makes a return to hWo television. I'll be sure to keep you all updated on Semore Smoke's condition if it worsens or gets better. hWo Wrestling!
