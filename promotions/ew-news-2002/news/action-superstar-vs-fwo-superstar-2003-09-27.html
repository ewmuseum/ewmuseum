Action! Superstar vs. fWo Superstar?

27 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Danger looking to defend a championship against outside competition? Looks that way. Current Action! Wrestling Superstar, Max Danger, has been gaining popular by the day. The former Dyad Champion, and reigning Bantam and Carnage Champion* has been on a major tear since becoming the second ever Double Champion in Action! Wrestling year-long plus history. As a Double Champion, it is Max's duty to fight any and all comers for his belts. And that is exactly what he is doing. With the return of the Whatever show on the WB every Friday night, to help with ratings(and everyone knows, Reed Young is all about the ratings) Max will be defending the Bantam Championship each week for the next six weeks. There is, of course, the chance that he could lose the belt at one of these events. If that happens, then the new champion will just take up the matches. And because of this, four of the six matches have already been booked, but those won't be announced just yet. For the other two, Action! is looking outside of it's own roster for opponents. A little cross-promotion if you will. The Danger Man took it upon himself to call up a friend of his. A man he has known for nearly six years. Tim Walden. Yes, one-half of the fWo Tag Team, Tim Walden & Jane Matheson, who were runner-ups in the Survivor III competition held by the same promotion. Currently they are embroiled in a feud against fellow Survivor III competitors, Brotherly Love. You can see the next chapter in said feud this coming Monday on RUAHH~! when Walden takes on Elliot Harper in the Wrestler of the Year Tournament. Not that we're condoning you watch Ruah over Pressure Point. But there isn't a Pressure Point this week(post-PPV and all that), so have at it. But only just this once. (The last time Action! and fWo did a little cross-promotion such as this, then-US Champion Voran Xias took on then-Bantam Champion Coral Avalon in a two falls title vs. title match. Coral retained the Bantam in the first fall, and Voran kept the US around his waist in the second.) Tim and Max go back to October of 1997, when they first met on Walden's first ever tour of Japan. Danger had been working the scene off and on since 1994. The two worked against each other - and a couple of occassions as a team - from then until March of '98, when Tim went back to tour the American Indies, and Danger left for a run through Mexico. In any event, the two men had kept in contact all these years. Developing quite a good friendship. They, however, had never crossed paths since that last month in 1998. So, when Max was given this opportunity to find two outside opponents for upcoming title defenses, the first man he called was Tim Walden. A man who is making great strides in the business. "When Joe Schmidt came to me about this, I was thrilled. I mean, Action! has some of the best wrestlers in the business on its roster, but you can't keep having matches with the same people over and over again, you know?" The Danger Man was saying about the situation. "So, I knew Tim was starting to make a big name for himself in fWo, and I hadn't worked with him in a long, long time, so I phoned him up, and asked if he was interesting. Bada-bing-bada-boom, next thing you know, he's being faxed a contract to work one night." Tim had a quite a bit to say; When asked about his reaction to getting the call and the big opportunity, he said, "I was to say the least surprised, as it's been a while since my last one-on-one bout, what with the Survivor tag thing and such. However, it makes a lot of sense when you consider the range of guys he's been taking on. He's the sort who likes to go above and beyond the obvious when he's looking for a challenge." On getting to face the King of Submission - who wasn't dubbed that back in his more high-flying days - again: "I'm very excited by the opportunity to fight Max again; personally, he's been one of my favorite wrestlers to watch in the ring whenver I could get the chance, and the challenge of wrestling him is certainly just as great as the charge I get from watching him wrestle." On his decision to take the offer: "I had decided almost from the moment I received the invitation to take up Max's offer; after five years apart and the way his skill has broadened even further in this time, I'd be a fool not to try it. His ring presence and technical skill is almost impossible to match, and while I've had a few opponents who gave me a run for my money, I can safely say that Max is the first man who makes me feel a little outclassed at times." So, tune into the WB on Friday to catch Max Danger defend the Bantam Championship against Tim Walden of the fWo on Whatever! *On Sunday, September 28th, at Action!'s "Out of Control" PPV event, Danger is defending the Carnage Championship against HardCase. Since the event has not yet taken place, technically, he is still the champion. But, if you are reading this after the event has taken place, he may not be the holder of the silver. http://action.slike.com fWo Survivor III
