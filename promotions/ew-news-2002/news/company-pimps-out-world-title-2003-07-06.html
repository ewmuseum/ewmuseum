Company "Pimps Out" World Title?

6 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Some are calling this a huge blow to Empire Wrestling, but owner D.jack rebukes those claims! In what can only be explained as a huge blow to Empire Wrestling, Jason Relms has backed out of his wrestling contract with Empire. President/Owner of Empire, [name withheld], has a different word for it. Opportunity. “This is not a huge blow as some of you buzzards in the press are calling it!!” D.jack said. “This is a huge, let me re-iterate that!! This is a HUGE opportunity. We had a wrestler contractually obligated to be apart of Empire Wrestling. Now we don’t want anyone in Empire that doesn’t want to be there!! So yes, we granted him permission to leave EW, since we haven’t put much money into his role in EW thus far. But that’s the small news. The big news is that with Jason's departure of EW, that fifth and final spot to compete for our world championship is now open once again!!” Some may remember a press conference a couple weeks ago, in which D.jack stated that EW has since combined their financial efforts with the likes of Burger Kind, Showtime, Universal Pictures and Sony, along with other companies. Along with that announce it was made known that D.jack is, to some, bribing others into join EW with a world championship title shot. Others feel that D.jack is, “pimping out”, as it were, the world title. D.jack rebukes those claims. “Ladies and gentlemen. This is sports entertainment. This business puts on a show for the fans. I am the ultimate showman!! Empire will proves everyone who thinks this is a bad idea, wrong! We will show everyone that this so called “pimping out” of the title is nothing of the sort. In actuality it is just an ingenious idea, that other promoters are angry they didn’t think of first.” For all those interested in Empire Wrestling, they are airing a pay per view special exhibition show for their financial affiliates on July 15th.
