The X confirm All-Asia Champ on After Dark

9 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A former PURE All-Asia Champion was confirmed as a signing on The X's Saturday night show, After Dark 5 The fifth installement of The X's Saturday night show, "After Dark" closed with confirmation of a former PURE All-Asia Champion. Owner Jason Marah was filmed talking on a cell phone with an unknown man, but the information spilled by Marah narrowed down the possibilities of man's identity to either Senji Yulav, Kohana or Raizo Tenzan. Tenzan seems the most likely to be the new signee. He was linked with The X last week and staff at the promotion had confirmed their interest in the star. The first All-Asia Champion, Senji Yulav, is second favourite. He's got no written contract with any promotion at this time but has been heavily linked with PWC in the past while. PWC has a bigger fan base and better reputation than The X, so if Yulav had to choose between the two promotions, he'd probably choose PWC, where he would meet up with his old rival, Kohana. Kohana has been almost thrown out of the running to be this mystery man as he is signed with PWC. Tenzan seems to be the more likely candidate for the job and we expect to see him being revealed as the new signing on After Dark 6. In other personnel news, The X has signed a dark horse in Gabriel Keiserburg and a man we know little about, Slater Hughes. Both will debut on AD 6. The debut of Hong Kong wrestler, Kai Cheung, has not been confirmed. The X
