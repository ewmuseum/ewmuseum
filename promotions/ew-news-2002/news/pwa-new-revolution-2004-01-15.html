PWA - New Revolution

15 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Things Are Changing With The Help Of ICW Owner Al Ritter And Lee Ashworth! Professional Wrestling Association, who have been working towards a high status in the Wrestling World, have started that journey again, by making some huge announcements! Lee Ashworth and Former ICW Owner, and huge backstage help to DCW, Al Ritter have been hired by Maxwell Qwest! Their jobs are un-determined at the moment, but with the success that both ICW and DCW had, is an obvious statement that PWA wants to run with the pack! Wednesday Night Warfare also saw Adam Vanstt take his position back as General Manager. This did not please Maxwell Qwest, who attempted to have Police escort Mr. Vanstt from the building! However, intervention from The Thrill Kill Kult put this to a halt. Vanst then stated he was here, and here to stay! Maxwell Qwest wanted to punish TKK and booked a huge Main Event for next Warfare. It will be PRIDE members and Tag Champions, Brian Blaze and Stryke, along with Sean Malone will take on the TKK, Josh Briskout, Gary "Hardcore" Gore and Guy Hieler, in what is going to be a huge battle! Warfare also saw Damien Steele pick up the win against "The Dark Angel" Kenneth Dunn, when he was attacked by Josh McKlayn! Also Brian Blaze and Stryke took Joey Scars and Braindead for all they had and more by winning the Tag Titles! Things in PWA are heating up, as they continue to try and hang with the big boys of Wrestling! Lee Ashworth - PWA CEO Professional Wrestling Association
