Sky High #6

2 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
So I was sitting around the other day, not much to do since the federation I'm working for has only one show a week, and I'm not even booked every week, and I decided to catch up with a few old buddies of mine. And having little else to write about at the moment, I decided to put the conversations down for my Sky High column. Sky: Today, I'm talking with former RUSH and PWC wrestler Gideon Poe. We're sitting here at some weird sushi bar where we have to be arsed to make our own sushi. Where the hell are we, Gideon? Gideon: This Uncle Saskae's Sushi Bar, just outside of New Jersey. Don't get your hopes on New Jersey finally getting a glamour make over though, the owners name is really Sam and he's as much from the East as I am from Hollywood. Plus I think I seen a rat scurry into the kitchen about 10 minutes ago. Either way it's cheap and serves its purpose...... and Sushi. Sky: Thanks for letting me know about the rat AFTER I started eating. Anyway, why this place? Gideon: It's low key. Only those dumb enough eat here on a regular basis continue to attend, and as you can see that's a pretty small number. I'm no celebrity or franchise player by any means, but still, you know what the wrestling media is like today. They'll try and interfere in anything, their like sharks sensing blood from 200 yards away. Plus I like it low key like this, it makes me feel more comfortable. Plus the scam here is if you get food poisoning, they blame you seeing as you're the one who makes your own sushi. Clever huh? Sky: Remind me to pretend to be blind and kick Sam afterward. So, what have you been put to since the closing of PWC, guvna? Gideon: Not a lot to be truthful. However it seems a lot of people working for underground record companies and promoters seemed to be PWC fans. It didn't take them long to figure out that my bandmates and I in Cadaver Theory still lived in close proximity of each other. Since then we've been playing a few shows around the East coast. A lot of our die hard fan base came out of the wood work too which was nice. It pays the bills to which is a bonus. It's nice to be your own boss for a change instead of answering to Ron Starr. Sky: On the downside, though, it requires a lot more thinking, I've been told. Gideon: No more than it takes to think how you're gonna walk home if you make it out of an Indian Deathlock. Sky: Heh. Speaking of which, I'm certain that you still have the wrestling bug in you. Doing indy appearances anywhere? Gideon: I've had a few offers to make a few appearances in some local indy and house shows. I've attended some, it's always nice to keep in some shape, plus I hate the feeling of having ring rust stuck on me. I was surprised at the overall response I got from a lot of the fans. You don't think people will remember you, even when you've been for a short amount of time as I have. For a business I was forced into I've slowly begun to appreciate it more and more each time I go out there. Sky: It must've been nice to work in places that don't distort your dimensions. I'll scream if I see another listing of myself as being 6'8" or any more than 250 pounds. Gideon: Heh, yeah it was. It was nice to just step into the ring and go at it with an opponent without having to worry about politics backstage or, as you so aptly put it, any of my own dimensions being distorted. Sky: I hear you're working on some new moves. Don't make the regional workers look too bad. Gideon: I've just been trying to keep myself busy with whatever it takes. Plus I get bored easily in a match when I know I'm mainly limited to a few select moves in my arsenal. I like to try and be unpredictable, unorthodox, in the long run harder to prepare for. If I'm busting out the same three moves week in week out, I'm going to get bored, and my opponents going to get an easy victory. Sky: You sounded like you were going into a promo there. You're definitely coming back into the game full time at a later date. Gideon: Hopefully sooner than later, yeah. I've had offers, but the fact is I can only accept one. Sky: Any secrecy order they're keeping you to? Gideon: None that they've told me about. That doesn't mean I'm going to let any near future plans out in the open yet though. Sky: Very well. What are you making? Sky: Looks like it was a pigeon at some point... Gideon: Heh, I just have a habit of playing with my food. Tonight, and more often than not, it's safer to just play with your food here instead of digesting it. Sky: You're giving them a lot of credit by saying that it can even be digested. Man, that reminds me of that one house show we had to cancel during the PCW/RUSH combined shows because everyone got sick from the bad seafood the caterers brought in... I think the only one who wasn't affected was Dante Jackson. Damn vegetarian. Gideon: Heh, yeah. I would have been safe too if Marvin Harris hadn't talked me into going to dine with him that day. That was near the start of the fusion of both promotions too if I remember, so it was an interesting way for PCW and RUSH guys to bond to say the least. Waiting for a cubicle is one way to get to know the guy in front of you well, maybe a little too well. Sky: But then again, I think the best "quality time" was that once instance in which there was a certain female walk in on you in the shower. It's about time you came clean with that story and told everyone what happened. And I'll let people know when you're lying about something, so tell the story right. Gideon: Well, as you can imagine, morale in the Rush Resistance back then was never really at high peaks, so we were constantly trying to find ways to keep each other spirits up. One of the things we participated in was the time honored tradition of......ribbing. It started of small and mediocre. Sprinkling pepper on Marvin's sandwiches when he was highly allergic to the stuff. Wet grass and mud in someone’s gym bag and hotel bed, that kind of stuff. Sky: Whose idea was it to put itching powder in El Spiffy's tights? Gideon: Clem’s, The Masked Mullet to most of the viewing nation. Me and him were also responsible for dying Elias's wrestling pants bright pink and turning his Jumbotron images into scenes from the YMCA video. That was when things got REALLY big. You have to remember that Clem was untouchable throughout this game. No one could rib him, nobody. Elia knew this when he plotted his revenge, and therefore just plotted right against me. Sky: Keep in mind, readers, that Clem's alter ego, Ashton Kutcher, may be seen hosting "Punk'd" on MTV. Gideon: Clem is more dashing of both ego's in my opinion though. Later that night, I remeber walking back to my locker room to get my stuff to go and get a shower. I'd been through a long, intense match with Ariz Ona. The guys knew I always liked to shower after most of my matches. So anyway, Elia seized the opportunity. He knew that I was friends with one of the sound technicians, a young girl called Gina. We both had similar music tastes, I'd get her copies of my bands old EP's, we'd swap albums, and so forth. Anyway, she was the perfect victim for Elia to manipulate. Elia knew fully well that over the duration of that week I'd been going to a lot of trouble to get a hold of a CD that Gina had been after for a while. Her birthday was coming up, so I figured it would be a good gift. I think it was a DOWN album. Anyway, Matt fed Gina some lie that I'd gotten a hold of the album for her, made up some bullshit line that I'd be out of town for the next few weeks working some house shows, and that I wanted to give her the present early. Gina ate every word of it up. So Matt led her to the locker room and said for her to just go right on it. So Gina did. Let's just say she got something for birthday she didn't expect to get and something I hadn't planned on her having. Sky: And there you have it. The Gideon Poe shower porn story. Gideon: Available on 8mm and every good search engine. Sky: So I take it that's where you got the idea to have Bob from Accounting hop into the shower with Matt? Gideon: ........ Bob who? Gideon: Looks like me and Mr. Elia will have something to talk about next time. Sky: Apparently so. Did you hear that RUSH was purchased by some guy in PTC? Maybe they'll do with it what they did for NTICW and close and reopen another ten times. Gideon: Yeah I'd heard. I wasn't over the moon about it, but I just hope a name change will occur or something along those lines. RUSH doesn't deserve to be slaughtered and resurrected constantly like NTICW. RUSH has had it's legacy, it needs to be left alone now. Sky: I like Christian's (Xavier Kannon for the uninformed) idea for all the former WO feds. Buy them to keep them closed. Gideon: It was a wise move, I'll agree. Whether or not someone will dig deep and supply the cash to do that though is another thing. However I think it needs to be done. I can't understand the logic behind a lot of it anyway. Wouldn't people rather achieve success in their own creation instead of achieving it with someone else’s? Sky: This is America, land of dotcoms, millionaire game shows, and a myriad other ways to be lazy and get rich doing it. Gideon: Yeah, don't remind me. Sky: Well, while we're here, are there any rumors about you that you'd like to dispel? Gideon: None really, besides one, and that is I asked for my release for PWC, there was no firing involved with my dismissal. Sky: I'd like to clear one thing up -- I did not, repeat, DID NOT let the dogs out. I suck. So, is there any chance of a Green/Gideon tag team in the next year or so? Everyone wants to see it. Gideon: Well… It'd be a crying shame not to give the people what they want. Sky: You heard it from the horse's mouth; Gideon Poe, Skylar Green, coming to a set of tag team titles near you! Now if I could just convince Tessa Windsor to be our official rally monkey, we'd be set. Gideon: Heh, indeed we would. Maybe Torrance Smith is free though. Sky: Unless OSW management wants to carry out that bisexual fetishists angle with she and Sombra Mystica, forming the team "2 Fast, 2 Curious." Not one of their better ideas. Gideon: let's just call it a work in progress for now then. Sky: Wait, there goes the rat. Hell, that thing is huge. You're sure we're not on the Jamie Kennedy Experiment? Gideon: Doubtful, although I heard Clem is filming a new series of Punk'd. Sky: And after they punked Kelly Osbourne, there's really no need to ask if we're big enough celebrities to warrant a pranking. They'll take anyone. Gideon: They took Jessica Biel, so you're right. Sky: Did you happen to catch the MTV Awards? Gideon: I did indeed, I've been making the most of my time off by catching up on TV. Sky: Then perhaps you can explain why Duran Duran got a lifetime achievement award. Nothing against them, but they skip over Johnny Cash to give it to Duran Duran? Gideon: Well, I'm a big Johnny Cash fan, and it left a bitter taste in my mouth after seeing it, even more so with him just getting a sympathy award from Justin, too. Either way, I'm sure Magnum Wolf wasn't complaining. Sky: He was at the show. Made a big fuss about Avril Lavigne going out to present the award instead of him. She was a last minute choice, but to her credit, she wouldn't have acted like a jackass onstage. Gideon: For once anyway, her and Kelly Osbourne seemed to be joined at the hip that night too. They on the same label or something? Sky: No, they were paired because of the fact that Kelly Osbourne standing next to any other teenager female recording artist immediately makes the other one five hundred percent more marketable. Gideon: Only 500%? Maybe they should have had Jack stand next to her. Sky: That would grant "God" status to whomever stood beside him. Gideon: Knowing him he'd be standing next to Natalie Portman for the duration of the awards. Sky: Is she still planning to move back to Israel? Gideon: Not if Jack can help it I'd imagine. Sky: Jack couldn't stop a car with his foot on the brakes. Gideon: Good point. Sky: Okay, to wrap it up, I'll ask a few quick questions, try not to think about them too hard. Gideon: Okay Sky: What's in your CD player right now? Gideon: Altering between "Four Ways To Scream Your name" by Funeral For A Friend and "Nola" by Down. Sky: Contra or Metal Gear? Gideon: Metal Gear Sky: What are the best three movies you've seen this summer? Gideon: Haven't gone to the movies. But "Almost Famous", "The Boondock Saints" and "Training Day". Sky: Hilary Duff -- the next Tara Reid? Gideon: No Sky: Are you on the ballot to run for governor of California? Gideon: Don't give me any ideas Sky: Pokemon -- Yu Gi Oh's bitch? Gideon: Nope. Sky: Cadaver Theory... what's the biggest group to ask you to tour with them? Gideon: Poison The Well and Cold. Sky: Any plans for a video? Gideon: None yet, but I'd love to shoot one with the right director. Sky: Well, there's a two-foot-long rat is gnawing at my leg, so I have to go kick the crap out of Sam. We'll keep in touch. Gideon: Definitely.
