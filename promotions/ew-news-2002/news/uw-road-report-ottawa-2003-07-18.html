uW Road Report: Ottawa

18 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Not very many surprises except some great action usually not seen on houseshow tours. The uW Road Dogs deliver again, this time from the semi-Great White North. Hi there! This is Good ol' JJ Adams up here in the great white north we Americans call Canada. I'm following the UW tour through the North and we had a House show in Ottawa. Ottawa is a great city. Beautiful buildings, friendly people, lots of geese. All in all, it's been an awsome trip, and it has me wondering why UW doesn't come up here more often. Now on to wrestling news. We couldn't get our digital cameras to work this week, but I thought it is always a must for our fans to know what goes on in the lesser known world of UW, which is the House Shows. This weeks show was nearly sold out which surprised me, because that's weeks was only filled about three fifths of the way. Word must be spreading about what a great time the UW House Show is. And let me tell you... It was one hell of a show this week folks! It started off with a slow match pitting Bane against Xperiment. It was a slow match for both these men are two of the mammoth beasts that now occupy the UW Roster, but it was interesting to see what Xperiment guy was really about. It was a great debut for him, because we got to see him wrestle and he surprisingly picked up a win against Bane. But you can't give him full credit for Turk came down and decided to help Bane out by throwing him a chair. That would have been all fine and dandy had Xperiment not have reached it first. So there it was... A giant blow to the head and Bane was done for. And you should have seen the glare that he gave his supposed partner afterwards. All in all I give the match a 6 out of 10. There was then some ruckus backstage with Shawn St. James harrassing a crew member. The next match was a much better paced match. It had Jackrabbit in his first singles match against Hullu Lentää. Hullu is slowly becoming a personal favorote of mine for he can move in that ring and is fierce with his high flying abilities. He beat Jackrabbit in this contest but it was a good, battle that was fought out for a long time. Hullu won it in 27 minutes and 14 seconds. I give this match a 9 out of ten for it was exciting, and it went off without a hitch. Jackrabbit's buddy Kryptonite stayed close by making sure NOTHING would interupt Jr's first singles competition! The next match never officially began as it was supposed to be Hector vs Wes, but Leroy and Kirbinati wouldn't leave their partners sides and it become a brawl to the finish. It took about 6 referees and three security officers to break it up. They got to rumble for about 3 minutes. I give this match a ten for it hit my Heat meter to the max. This feud is going somewhere, and I can't wait to see where! I'm sad to say it that the next two matches were boring, but for different reasons. First we had Ashton Crowley take on Brookes Boren. I thought, "Great! This is going to be one hell of a contest if you ask me!" Well it wasn't! Crowley decimated Brookes definately sending a sign to Reckless wrestling. I give it a three out of ten because it was boring yes, but stil entertaining! There's nothing better then seeing UW's Champ beat a man to his last breath! The other match that dissapointed me was Supply N Demand vs Parental Advisory. These two teams are equipped to both be great, but their hearts seemed to be out of it. Parental advisory but have had too much of this great Canadian Beer! I give it a 5 out of ten. Supply N Demand picked this one up, but it was close, and I like the pairing of the new tag team. If they start taking their heads out of the clouds they could go far! There was then some more jibber jabber backstage with St. James. He was trying to I guess find out Tony Fields' phone number so he could call him. Freaky! I wouldn't want that psycho to call me! But you have to think Was it to Tony? And if it was, what was his purpose? I give this a question mark on my heat meter because I am freaked out by the thought of this whole situation! The main event then came and it was one of the best pieces of wrestling I have seen in a long long time. The House show audience definately got a treat with this next match! We had Tyreal facing Stephen Rawlings for the Hardcore title. Rawlings was quite happy that Tyrael gave him a rematch after what happened with the cops. Tyrael understood so much that he even offered a title shot. These two guys are very classy. The match was competitive and upbeat. There wasn't a dull moment the entire match! They men had it all. Tables, chairs... Rawlings even did a swandive off the balcony putting Tyrael through a title. When it came down to the final pin though Stephen Rawlings couldn't get it done! Nuissance showed up with a chain and turned Stephen into a whipping boy. This received the loudest boo of the night. Tyrael then helped Rawlings fight off Nuissance and that recieved the loudest pop of the night. Tyrael then pinned Rawlings at 35:43 and retained his Hardcore title! I gave the match a 10 out of 10 and a very high heat rating as well... This is what UW was all about. All in all a great show! An A- show if I were to grade it as a project! I enjoyed it, the fans enjoyed it and hopefully all of you at home will enjoy it. Until next week this is JJ Adams. universal Wrestling
