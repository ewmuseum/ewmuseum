Pressure Point 64 Report

23 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LLB, Avalon, and the original Snowball. Name three things that are dead. ACTION! PRESSURE POINT REPORT By: Keith Scott Zimmerman E-mail: [detail withheld] Date: 17.6.3 (taped 16.6.3.) The Big News: LLB's dead. *shrug* Title Changes: -- Face/Heel Turns: -- Best Match: 8. Sharc counters Max's submission holds and gets him to the floor where he puts him in close proximity to the ramp. Into the steps, into the rail, guardrail powerbomb! GUHYEE. Sharc chases Jess around and Max sends her to the back once he's got the upper hand back. Sharc works the back but Max ranas him out . Sharc gives him a quebradora onto the guardrail! Damn marks chanting Max. Bulldog slam on the floor but when he tries 2 Max pushes off and posts him, natch. Series of crazy-ass reversals leads to Walls of Sharc but Max swings backwards and chairs out of it. Sharc continued the beatdown and Boston crabbed him after a German. I like how he's not pulling out a submission but is sticking to his style with the intermittent basic submissions they teach you in the first month to season. Jessica comes back, leading to Max doing the same: Double Jeopardy, Wild Bomb, Walls of Danger. Hammer Jammer transitioned into Sharc's wn spinebreaker and Sharc passes out. Worst Match: 3 & 4 weren't bad but they were short. Absurdity of the Show: Apparently we've got stabbings and murder going on but it's all right because we're televised. Don't think about that staement for more than 2 minutes or blood'll shoot out your nose. Moment of the Show: the markOUT on the alleged B Champ. Breakdown/Highlights: 1) We're starting with A MATCH! They know how much I love that! What sort of self-respected luchadore uses freaking t.A.T.u for theme music? Get some Los Fabulosos Cadillacs or something, "El Matador", or at least use the Movie Awards Performance. He got in a couple spots before Duel took him out with a submission. Duel's one of the five most solid guys on the roster at this point, I must say...the Arcades came out for gloating time and met the new luchador tag team we've hired...SSI & Homeless Gus did, uh, something...DeNucci'll be on the card--THAT'LL be interesting...Neko Case has returned after a lengthy spell having been wished out to the cornfield...HardCase & Rook just sort of chilled out backstage...Donnie Daze tried to cheer up Sylo about not being on the card. HEEL TURN DAMMIT...Neko & Clayton Prodigy (who?) got a quick summation of the match by Reed...2) The kid no-sold and didn't do the Irish whip, leading to Payne getting--you guessed it--knocked the HELL out. Respect the kicking ability, but c'mon: if I have to sell the Irish whip your newbie ass does too... Reed loves Gio. Joe hates Gio. Friction, baby...Luchadores v. Arcades non-title next week...The alleged #1 contender's match never came off and Cylear dished out some punishment...3) Wagner came out and introduced himself. He came off pretty neutral and well, saying things that got booed and cheered. Challenge to Daze accepted. Sneeze, Cambria interference, German suplex (appropriately enough) ended it...Heimdal/Phillips at the PPV...4) Another sneeze, series of rollups lead to a Rook victory. Probably still hurt from the ladder match... 5) Back-to-back matches? Is this some sort of parallel universe? "Times Like These" for theme music for a newbie, too? Your last name's PRODIGY for Jeffsakes! Brain! USE IT! Apres moi, le deluge indeed. Clayton decided against using weapons in the match, thus guaranteeing a loss. Case hit his Gay Ass Rolling Thunder DDT, the worst move this side of Jackie Gayda's "bulldog". After an ugly stalltastic period they got the house in order and Case closed with the EVERYST FREAKING CATACLYSM only for Prodigy to give him the beats after...Case called Black lucky...This week's TV Show had Duel and a whole lotta rambling and insider talk that was flat...6) Rolling swinging neckbreakers? What hath Benoit wrought? Brawl into the crowd where Fiend hit a fluid DVD into some chairs and then the Arabian facebuster. Darkness Falls was kicked out of and Fiend had to rally to finish with the Anderson spinebuster... Ninja K got ready for an attack that never happened...7) How about busting out the STO? Pretty clever (note to self). Kbradora and there's a Matrix spot with the Roaring Elbow before I hit the Christian DDT. Bad Moon Rising, Boral? And now for the BEET the FOCK out of you portion of our evening; Klept-O-Matic no, KSZ Driller no, Britney Spear no, and markOUT OH HELL YES. A winner is me. Next time, I get the belt, pretty boy...Max mocked Coral...Minio was about to exercise his right to unneccessary violence on SylO, I mean KroW...Sharc pissed off Max...IBJ will have a special manager next week again...KroW threatened the reverse to Minio...Whoever got Sharc's friend last week got the new one this week...8) See above...KroW delivered the chair beats on Minio to close. Grade: B. Without the overhang of the World Title, despite the good matches--well, as David Byrne so aptly put it: We're on the rooooad to nowhere, come on inside. Taking that riide to nowhere, we'll take that ride... Show Results: 1) Duel s. Deft 2) Giovanni Oliver DeNucci ko. Payne Fosters 3) Lukas Wagner d. Donnie Daze 4) Rook Black d. Hard Case 5) {hardcore} Neko Case d. Clayton Prodigy 6) Fiend d. Lowlife 7) {non-title} ME d. Coral Avalon 8) [CARNAGE] Max Danger d. Sharc Pressure Point 64
