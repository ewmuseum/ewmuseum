DarkStar to reveal plot at PPV?

17 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Weeks ago DarkStar mentioned his desire to destroy PCW? But how? DarkStar, PCW's resident masochistic dark gimmick, stated weeks ago when he returned to Primetime that he 'was not the savior that the fans were hoping for' and that he 'would destroy PCW and everyone involved with resurrecting its corpse'. He then became involved in a feud with Wannabe Pop-Icon Alexander Pryce. The reasoning for this was not known right away until last month, when DS revealed he was 'starting from the bottom up to break PCW down into nothing.' Pryce and his manager\mother have been a bigger thorn in his side then he had expected, however, and apparently he has become fed up with waiting to spring his plot. At Shanghaid he may do something drastic and let the rest of the world in on his plans. The 23 year old DarkStar was a mainstay in PCW for three years before jumping to OSW in the middle of last year. Shortly after his abandonment, PCW closed. While in OSW DarkStar had feuds with Alan Kriegman and Rhett Locke, but it was during the Locke feud that he became disinfranchised with the state of pro-wrestling and vanished, only to appear during the re-opening of PCW. He was bitter about the way fans forgot him after being a relatively major name for the better part of his career and vowed revenge. Duke Williams, PCW's acting president, dislikes both DarkStar and Pryce. The rumor is that DarkStar will simply be banned from PCW after the PPV due to not being under contract. The only reason he has been kept around this far is so Duke can kill two birds with one stone if the two end eachothers careers. Williams could not be reached for comment. What will happen at the PPV, if anything? Will it be as destructive as DarkStar has claimed? You, like me, are going to have to tune in to find out. Primetime Championship Wrestling
