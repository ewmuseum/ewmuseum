Sinners and Saints #1

30 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Help me Close the Asylum. Doesn't this image just say it all? What does the American Goverment do when the local jails and mental hospitals are full? Why, they take the convicts, the sadists, the murderers and the rapists and enroll them into the Asylum, this fighting fed that has swept America up in their weekly televised acts of torture and brutality. Some may say this a good thing, that it seems that Asylum "Stars" seem to get themselves killed within a month, either by angering a fellow lunatic, or by doing us all a favor and offing themselves. Yes, this is a good thing... it's Darwin's law in full effect, I say. But must it be televised across the nation for our children to see? This is not professional wrestling, where we are comforted by the fact that the whole show is scripted, that it is more of a soap opera or gymnastics competition than real combat. The Asylum, on the other hand, does not even attempt to hide the fact that they are paying men to hurt each other for public benefit. Have we learned nothing from the Roman Gladiators? Must these sadistic killing rituals go on during America's primetime? This is what the Asylum is teaching our children... The Champion, who is traditionally the man that our children look up to as a role model, is a self-hating, murdering, man-raping, clearly deranged eunic. This is not the fWo's Vic Creed, a Champion we can be proud to call our children's idol... this is not OSE's Boda, who is known to help old ladies cross the street. No, this is something much worse. This is Villam Ender. Yes, the same Villam Ender that made headlines last year by raping another man on national television. And what does Joe Campbell, the owner of the circus of oddities we call the Aslyum, do? Does he reprimand Mr. Ender for this act of wanton brutality? Does he fine him? Fire him? Report him to the authorities? No, he makes the bastard his champion. This is what the Asylum is teaching our children. To get ahead, you must kill your enemies. You must rape them. You must commit acts to them so deranged, so foul, that I cannot put pen to paper to describe them. How did come about you ask? Well, let me tell you. My son punched his brother in the face this morning, all because his brother snatched his bowl of cheerios away. My son, a good christian boy, punched his brother for swiping some frosted cereal, and do you want to know what he said when I asked him why he would do such a thing? "Because he's a fuckhead", he said. And that, in that one breath, is the exact reason that we must close the Asylum. Sign the petition when you see it, my brothers and sisters. Stop the evil that is Joe Campbell. Stop the madness that is named Villam. Hallelujah. the Asylum
