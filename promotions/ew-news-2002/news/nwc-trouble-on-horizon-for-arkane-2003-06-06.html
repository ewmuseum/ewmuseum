[NWC] - Trouble on Horizon for Arkane!

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It seems "The Lethal Cocktail" is back in the NWC and he wants a piece of his nemesis former North American champ, Rictor Arkane. It's bad news for Rictor Arkane post-Revival! His former arch rival and lover of Maria Lopez, Paul Cain, is rumoured to be negotiating a deal with NWC South. Cain had previously signed a contract with the South, but was forced to pull out of his debut match due to injury, causing him to leave after Sigurd's fears the injury would limit his South contribution. Now though, Cain is back and ready to try to promote his "Cocktail Bar" interview show, whilst also keeping an eye on his nemesis Rictor Arkane. NWC South fans get set for explosions aplenty if these two collide! Many of you may remember Paul Cain as the man who lost 4-3 to Tempest in the first round of last summer's GTT! [NWC] - National Wrestling Council
