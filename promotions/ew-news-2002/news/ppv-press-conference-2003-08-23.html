PPV Press Conference

23 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Press Conference held concerning the main event at the September 5, Pay Per View. RALIEGH, NC – August 23, 2003 – Last night, Fred Fusion and Daniel Adams were flown in to North Carolina to the cW HQ for a special press conference regarding the upcoming Pay Per View on September 5, 2003. The name of the PPV has been officially entitled “Winner Takes All.” “There is a reason we named it this,” Lanier commented, “So much has been contracted to be on the line at the main event.” The two Core Wrestling Superstars sat down and signed two contracts. One contract was agreeing to the match at Winner Takes All to be a Legacy Championship Match that will be No Holds Barred. The other contract states the special stipulations of the match. Below is a quote from contract explaining the stipulations: “Both parties will agree to and follow the terms written below upon signing their name at the bottom of this contract. The first stipulation on the match is that it will be for the fate of Alinie Fanco. This clause was drafted by the lawyers of the British Empire. It states that if Daniel Adams wins the match, Alinie Fanco will be returned to his side unharmed. In contrast, if Daniel Adams was to lose, Alinie Fanco becomes a permanent member of the British Empire, even against her wishes. The second part of this clause was written by the lawyers of Matthew Lanier and Core Wrestling Entertainment. It states that if Daniel Adams wins the match, Eugene FitzPatrick will be forced to leave The British Empire, disregarding all his managerial responsibilities and leave Core Wrestling. In contrast, if Daniel Adams was to lose, Matthew Lanier will sign over all rights of operations of his company over to Eugene FitzPatrick and he will leave Core Wrestling. This does not mean Matthew Lanier will still not own Core Wrestling Entertainment, it just means he will be forced out of Core Wrestling but it able to operate the others parts of the companies under the cWe banner.” Both contracts were agreed upon and signed by Daniel Adams, Fred Fusion, Eugene FitzPatrick and cW Owner, Matthew Lanier. The British Empire then left the press conference after an exchange of words between them and Daniel Adams when Adams requested a little information on the whereabouts and condition of Ms. Fanco. This week on Smash, Daniel Adams and Fred Fusion square off in a preview of what will happen at Winner Takes All. Core Wrestling
