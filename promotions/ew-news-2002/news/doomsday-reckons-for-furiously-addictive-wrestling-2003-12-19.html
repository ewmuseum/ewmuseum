Doomsday reckons for Furiously Addictive Wrestling

19 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Doomsday reckons for FAW, as Apocalypse refuses to sign a new contract with Furiously Addictive Wrestling hightening speculation that he is set to depart and transfer to a rival federation. Furiously Addictive Wrestling have reportedly offered superstar Apocalypse a new contract after his impressive work led him to an International title chance on the Christmas Special episode of Panic set to take place on December 23rd. Apocalypse, 31, has worked extremely hard in the past two months and Furiously Addictive Wrestling want to reward him, but the International title Number One contender has reportedly refused a lucrative contract offer. Apocalypse returned to Furiously Addictive Wrestling in June and has been a hit thus far but the 31-year-old's future is in serious doubt and an imminent transfer to a rival federation is expected to take place in January when his current contract ends. Bonita Lake, Head of Talent at Furiously Addictive Wrestling, said at the weekend that FAW was concerned about Apocalypse's commitment to the federation. She told the local press: "The problem is that if Apocalypse goes, we lose a great talent and possibly the International title should he win it. "We cannot afford to let Apocalypse slip out of our grasp, especially to a rival federation. We will talk to Apocalypse and his agent again and will attempt to come to an agreement that is beneficial to all parties." The contract saga will continue well into the week, as FAW prepares for it's final show of the year on December 23rd with the Christmas special episode of Panic. We'll keep you updated. FAW: Furiously Addictive Wrestling
