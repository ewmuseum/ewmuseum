AW Wrestler with mental history may be having problems again...

25 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
New York City, NY- Dr. Resnik, a psychologist in New York City who reportedly analyzed current AW wrestler Creature recently spoke out about a controversial video released by the wrestler himself. "It was a couple of years ago. He assaulted a police officer, and I was commissioned to determine if he was mentally stable. I found him to possess the ability to appear quite sane on the surface, but once you dig to a certain level of his psyche, it is obvious he is very much insane," said Resnik. "I saw the video myself. One of AW's employees, a Tyler Robinson, who knew of my work with Creature, sent it to me. He is always supposed to be taking medication I prescribed him, but Mr. Robinson knew nothing of that. To tell you the truth, he seems to have gotten worse." In recent months, Creature's wife of one year Kim, whose family requested her last name be withheld, was murdered. An investigation was launched, but nothing ever came of it. Creature himself has been in and out of the courtroom on charges ranging from assault to murder. He has never been convicted. Creature, who is expected to headline the AW's Pay-Per-View "Desire" on January 3rd, was unavailable for comment. His current place of residence is unknown. AW Owner Razor Blade's only comment on the matter was, "Not only will we air the tape...but he'll be at Desire. Guaranteed." To view the tape and more, visit AdvantageWrestling.com.
