Think Red #4: Age of Greatness

8 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
At the venerable age of 42, Ivan Stanislav has seen many, many men come and go in this sport. Now, Stanislav takes a candid look at those that shine brightest in the OSW while trying to keep communist propaganda at a minimum! Most Unbiased Print in US of A Comrades! Once again I, Ivan Stanislav, come to you with another edition of the most unbiased piece of print in the Wrestling Community: Think Red! Many people have told Praporschik Stanislav that while his words are all indeed true, that perhaps he should focus on other aspects of wrestling as well. With this being said, Ivan will turn his attention, for once, FROM Communism and talk about something that all of us in this grand sport of ours has dealt with: age. As one of the oldest members in the Old School Wrestling Federation, it is no doubt something that Ivan’s detractors will latch onto and use against my will. At the age of forty-two, there are many things that I have seen that many members of this sport would never believe. From wars and pieces, to the collapse of entire empires and modes of thinking. I have seen death and gore as well as compassion and love. It is these things that have molded your truly into a man of great breadth in terms of emotion and mental poise. An interesting thing is happening in the OSW the longer that Ivan is there, however, and it is very interesting indeed. Since its inception, the OSW and now the OSE has always been place where any athlete can make name for himself or herself. Names that instantly spring to Stanislav’s mind include Rhett Locke, Matthew Register, and even that little devil Six Finger Cisco. So as another dawn rises upon the OSW, one must look to the future. It is true, unfortunately, that Ivan Stanislav is not getting any younger. While more years of wrestling are certainly in order for myself, we all must look to the younger members of our roster, and that, my Comrades, is what we will look at today. Matthew Register: Roughly 27 years old, Matthew Register had entered into the OSW as merely ½ of a tag team known as Ronin. Now, Register stands at the apex of the OSE as our OSE Champion. Quick wit as well as a cutthroat attitude has aided this rising star into something that certainly will propel the OSW into a direction that we cannot even comprehend. While Stanislav may have pushed the boulder, Register now find out if he can push it along as well. Though Stanislav would say that there is some small portion of integrity somewhere within the heart and soul of Matthew Register, his smart-ass manner and personal demons appear to be one of the few things that hold this man back. He would make a wonderful communist, of course he would have to change his last name because “Register” is too capitalist sounding, but that could certainly be arranged. Six Finger Cisco: Twenty-two years of age as of now, soon to be twenty-three, Six Finger Cisco is a mixed bag. I have watched this man start as something no one ever mentioned and slowly become a superstar. This man, love him or hate him, indeed has much heart and utilizes everything his physical frame can offer to bring about some very surprising wins. A man that came into the OSW amongst turmoil, Six Finger Cisco has indeed been there to help those that were in need. Mainly, the stragglers that were too blind to understand Communism, otherwise Stanislav would have been there to help them as well, but this is another matter as well. Cisco’s feud with Matt Register for the OSE title was something of masterpiece that has propelled both men to the threshold of greatness. Of course, with greatness comes confrontation with The Russian Bear… Xavier Kannon: Xavier Kannon is interesting individual indeed. For his entire career, any time that Xavier was in federation with Stanislav, he was nothing more than dirt on his boot heel. This has not changed, mind you. Xavier Kannon epitomizes what stupidity and naiveté can do to a man. Twenty-seven years old is the age of Xavier Kannon and already he has more injuries and physical problems than virtually anybody in the OSW. It would appear now, that Kannon must get into physical contest with The Russian Bear, which will only doom his longevity in this federation even more. Those that want to make names for themselves need only look to Kannon to understand what haughtiness and disregard for ones body will do to a man. Kannon is a mediocre performer, but his better days are behind him. He lacks the longevity and intellect of one such as Stanislav, and he lacks the fortitude of many men he would call colleague. Jeffrey Wayne: The other half of what used to be Ronin, Jeffrey Wayne, at the age of 27, is also rising up through the ranks like Katyusha Missile fired from artillery. It appears that the team of Ronin has served Mr. Wayne and Register well as the two men share a parallel course, but with different twists and turns. Jeffrey Wayne, like Register, is true competitor. He is cunning and well versed in his knowledge of wrestling, this is no secret, but it will probably be only matter of time before Wayne and Register find themselves at one another’s throats. The problem with both men, as well as a sign of their age, is that they will grow too egocentric and throw away their collective bond for a more capitalist and singular victory. It will certainly be great match and regardless of any outcome, Jeffrey Wayne will continue to wow his fans. EDH: “Exceptional” Dave Hurst may be the most moronic individual that Ivan Stanislav has ever had to listen to speak. Dave Hurst may be also the most delusional and misguided person on the face of this planet. Also, EDH may be nothing more than a puppet of others around him. However, the age of 24 does not stop Exceptional Dave Hurst from fighting competitively and finding ways to usurp control of a situation. Somewhere, beyond all the fluff that floats around in this young man’s mind, is a very calculating and crafty individual. Too bad it has been suppressed and shoved into a box that is beneath some sort of ocean… Ehem… enough with metaphors. Dave Hurst may have his flaws, but he has a bright future ahead of him. Along with the Cisco’s that were more of a supportive war in the past stable wars, Hurst’s usefulness and heart has kept him in the running for awful long time. He will be making a name for himself… in the “Exceptional” way that he only knows how. There are others, naturally, that will lead the OSW onwards towards the greatness that is has always known. Men like Silvio Rochelli, a man who is a capitalist to the core and forty years of age, has much to offer for the OSW. Granted, once Ivan Stanislav, which will happen eventually, reduces him to groveling mass we will have to see just how great he is. At Stanislav’s venerable age, many people come and go. Since Ivan’s entrance into US of A the men and women who have competed in wrestling have melded into just a few faces. Names like Jeremy Howard, Doug Troy, and even the loathsome Miyagawa and Clyde spring to mind. The above-mentioned men, however, rapidly will gain their independence from the rest of the pack and lead the OSW down wonderful path. That is, until they meet one Russian Bear… and then they will meet their true match. I see that my time is just about up, Comrades. The above names are men that will lead the OSW in the coming months. Of course, they do not realize that with greatness comes much sacrifice and more danger, for there is always a Bear lurking around the corner. Ultimately, while those men are on their way up, The Red Army and my Comrades have always been on the top and will remain there. Only with the true spirit of communism, Leninism, and Stalinism can someone truly achieve the oft sought abilities that we in The Red Army possess. For this reason, I urge all the above mentioned to become Communists and learn the mystical ways of Communism! Until next time, my Comrades, think about the greatness that we all can achieve. Think of the might of Ivan Stanislav and his wisdom. But always, ALWAYS Think Red!! Praporschik Ivan Stanislav Red Army Commander
