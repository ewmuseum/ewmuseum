fWo spoilers and notes about Unleashed event

27 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Despite tight security surrounding the upcoming Unleashed event, rumors and confirmed sightings have given some clues into guest appearances and possibly storyline outcomes for the weeks ahead. fWo Wrestling It has been confirmed that the fWo has contacted William Bailey, AKA Speed Demon, a wrestler who worked the fWo and the indies regularly through 1998-2000 before leaving the wrestling scene after the freak accident that killed Ryan Moore, the wrestler known as Joy. Bailey has not been in or around a wrestling ring for several years, but has been confirmed as backstage and in wrestling gear for tonight's Unleashed program. Bailey worked storylines with Fallen and Joy in the 1999 timeframe, and rumor is that Bailey will be one or all of the surprises planned for the final in the Worst Of Five series slated to end at Unleashed tonight. Coral Avalon is backstage as well, aparently scheduled to wrestle Voran Xias after a recent "shoot" interview from the current US Champion. Xias is reported to be on bad terms with management and former title holders Brand Frontier and Lance Knight, both of whom were slated to work matches with Xias in the next several weeks. Upcoming shows are advertising XXXStasy vs. Spyder for the Internet Championship, so it is likely that tonight's scheduled match will not be the final in their program. Finally, it appears as though last minute plans may wind up keeping the tag-team titles around the waists of the Black Quicksilver and Jon Crisp, as a strong singles push for Jon Crisp is slated not to begin for longer than expected. fWo Wrestling Unleashed Battle Bowl
