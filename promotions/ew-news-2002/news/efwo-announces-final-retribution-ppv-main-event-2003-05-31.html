EFWO Announces Final Retribution PPV Main Event

31 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On June 29th, 20 Men will compete in a unique gimmick match to crown a brand-new EFWO World Champion! The E-federation World order recently announced that the main event at its next PPV will be for the vacant EFWO World Title. At least 20 men will compete for that title in a match by the same name as the pay-per-view itself… Final Retribution. The EFWO title became vacant after EFWO President Brian K Oxenreider shocked everyone and fired world champion Damian Spires at the end of the Net Mania Pay-Per-View. Spires had just successfully defended the title against The One True Warrior. The match set up is complex. Although some have called it a version of the popular kid’s game King of the Mountain. The ring area will be constructed into a huge caged pyramid like structure. Ring construction: Main pyramid sides - 2 inch thick steel bars in a crisscross pattern (ala fencing) w/ holes large enough for foot holds. Platform levels – 3 inch thick steel grating 5 feet wide supported every few feet underneath, with more 2 inch steel bars attached to the sides of the pyramid Final Level – 5 foot by 5 foot square “Moat” – 15 feet deep, 10 feet wide, empty, mats cover concrete floor to provide some (albeit little) protection from a fall. One ladder on each moat wall for exit access after elimination has occurred. Officials: 4 referee’s for the base level and levels 1 – 3, 1 ref for the top level. All refs located on opposite side of the moat and at the same level as the platforms on the pyramid. The competitors will enter randomly ever 30 seconds in a style similar to a royal rumble. After all the competitors have entered, a 45 minute timer starts. Who ever is at the highest point of the cage when the 45 minutes runs out, wins the EFWO World Title. Gaining entry into the match might be tougher than the match itself. A competitor must defeat 3 opponents in a 3 on 1 handicapped match in order to gain entry. If they fail they can try again and again until they achieve entry or the there are no more shows before the event. No other matches have yet to be announced for this card, but should be shortly.
