The Whole FAWcking Truth: Edition Two

1 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Whole FAWcking Truth written by [name withheld]. The views do not necessarily reflect that of Furiously Addictive Wrestling. Everything written in this article is solely the opinion of [name withheld]. Apologies for the gap last week – but never fear as the second edition of “The Whole FAWking Truth” is upon us! This week, I’m dedicating this piece to this year’s Furious Awards. Six awards will be up for grabs come December 20th. Voting closes on Monday (1st December), so make sure those last minute votes are in! 2003 has seen FAW once again break into new heights and cement it’s status as one of the best roleplaying federations in e-wrestling today. Unlike previous years, there has been less title changes this year, but a very intense degree of competition nonetheless. Battles such as those between the 9th Wonder Of Foam and Rzarector, and the final curtain in The British Bomber vs Chris Thunder rivalry have kept fans on edge for most of the year. Breakthrough superstars such as The Meditator have made serious impacts among the FAW locker room, and the re-emergence of Lee Smith as a fully-fledged superstar have breathed new life into the roster. 2003 has undoubtedly been a year of re-building for FAW as far as the main event landscape goes. The days of Pyro, American Psycho and even JLC are long gone. New stars such as current FAW World Champion Violator, Orlando Rivera and Sniper are the future of Furiously Addictive Wrestling. Over in the tag division, things are hotting up between the current Tag Team Champions, The Vice Squad, who are set to sweep the Tag Team award on December 20th, and Mafia newcomers Sharky De Niro and Kash Pachino. The Italian newcomers have been a popular combo thus far, and are gearing up for a rumoured Tag Team Title rematch at When World’s Collide in January. As for some of the mainstays in FAW, 2003 has been a rocky year for the old school. With departures from the 9th Wonder Of Foam (who recently made a return to the announcers booth), JLC, The British Bomber and Spawn, many stars have jumped at the chance to take their places and create their own legacy. However, one star who has recently made a return to the squared circle is Don Corneoli, who seemingly has some unfinished business with Chris Thunder from their Extreme Beating Three encounter. The black chair controversy which eventually retired JLC is considered one of the best storylines FAW have produced in a long time. We could expect the return of the black chair in Thunder’s arsenal should Corneoli dare to mess which a much older and wiser superstar than back in the day. But on with the awards – with six categories, and the ceremony set to go down on December 20th, this will be the essential event for recapping a successful third year in business for FAW: Take a look at the categories: - Newcomer Of The Year Nominees: Sniper, The Meditator, Violator, Orlando Riviera Views: All four men have appeared on the latter side of June but have been the most outstanding newcomers in a long time. Sniper’s holds wins over The British Bomber, Chris Thunder and Spawn and has been FAW International Champion for almost five months. Violator is the new FAW World Champion after emerging from Deceit with the gold in a match which also included Orlando Rivera and Tony Vigilante. Rivera has now been inserted as the main contender in Vigilante’s absence. Also, The Meditator went through a gruelling program with Lee Smith spanning two months of pure hatred and is by no means an extra in this contest. - Tag Team Of The Year Nominees: The Vice Squad, Marcus Stiles and Tristan St. Clair, Legions Of Dead, House Of Pain Views: Despite this being the flattest year of tag team action in FAW’s 3 year history, it has also produced the most exciting team in it’s history. The Vice Squad, Lance Carter and Hank Sharpe, have set the world alight with their 80s-like antics and very talented ring abilities. Sure to collect the award for Tag Team Of The Year come December 20th, without a doubt… - Champion Of The Year Nominees: 9th Wonder Of Foam (World Champion), Sniper (International Champion), Rzarector (World Champion), The Vice Squad (Tag Team Champions), Dave Tronick (International Champion) Views: A very close award this one. All five nominees held their respective titles for a lengthy period and any one of these men could be taking home this award to place on their mantle. Foamy and Sniper are rumoured to be leading the way, which should in the end, be a closely fought contest. - Match Of The Year Nominees: Rzarector vs 9th Wonder Of Foam, The British Bomber vs Chris Thunder, JLC vs The Great One, Tony Vigilante vs Spawn Views: Five main event crackers of 2003 – this award is also set to be closely contested. Rzarector’s 2003 campaign began in extraordinary fashion, becoming the winner of the WWC Rumble and defeating Foamy in the main event of Extreme Beating Three. Also, Don “The Great One” Corneoli and JLC went head-to-head in a retirement match in February which Corneoli won, only to be retired by Chris Thunder the following month. Thunder again ended a career in July when he retired The British Bomber after a very entertaining street fight. And, in a more recent encounter, Tony Vigilante and Spawn went head-to-head in what is considered to be in pole position for Match Of The Year for 2003. - Most Dedicated Superstar Of The Year Nominees: Lee Smith, Chris Thunder, Tony Vigilante, The Vice Squad, Rzarector Views: All of these men contributed wholly to FAW for the whole of the year and have truly made names for themselves in the past twelve months. Vigilante at the present time is tipped to scoop this award and deservedly so, with a decent run as FAW World Champion, his second reign and continuous service to Furiously Addictive Wrestling. - Performer Of The Year Nominees: 9th Wonder Of Foam, Spawn, Tony Vigilante, Sniper, Lee Smith Views: And finally, the award we’ve been waiting for: Performer Of The Year! These five men have had a consistent and successful year and have entertained us throughout. We now salute the most deserved superstar. Currently, the votes are going up and down and we are set for a nail biting end to the awards ceremony on December 20th. Good luck to all the nominees!
