[CEW] Airs Another Thrill

1 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cyber Extreme Wrestling gains more steam toward this month's High Impact with another amazing episode of THRILL The history of Cyber Extreme Wrestling has been well-documented through EW-News and other wrestling news sources. This reporter has been with CEW for most of its more recent, five-year history ... And yet, through those 4 years and 200+ shows, I have never seen a show as big as tonight's show in Seattle, Washington. With two weeks left until the High Impact Pay-per-View, CEW is defiantly heating up. The main event, which will showcase Muzzolini, Da*Link*Went, Nick Alexander, and Descendent for the CEW Title, heated up even more this week as all four men interacted at one point or another. Did you miss the show? Did you miss Brian Blazer's Upstart Initiation Assignment? Or what about the tag match: Presidents of Power vs AC and Link? Or Cassandra Avery and Lord Skywolf defending their titles against Kris eGo and Phoenix Murdoch? So much happened this week, there's no way I can tell about it all here. Just head on over to www.cewentertainment.com and check out the show for yourself. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- 9 years of history ... 5 years of the CEW brand ... 38 Pay-Per-Views, and counting ... 127 episodes of CEW Thrill, and counting ... 29 World Title changes, shared between 20 men ... and counting? FIND OUT IN 2 WEEKS! Muzzolini ... Leader of Murder Inc. One-time owner of the CEW. Master of the mind game. CEW World Champion. Descendent ... Co-founder of D'z Nutz. One-time CEW King of Deathmatch. Hardcore monster. Wants revenge on SNA for trying to take him out of the game. Da*Link*Went ... Co-founder of D'z Nutz. Watched helplessly as Muzzolini took a chair to his pregnant girlfriend at the last Pay-per-view. Lost an unborn child as a result. Young ... and dangerous to everyone. "Showtime" Nick Alexander ... Co-founder of the original Upstart stable. One of the fastest rising stars in CEW history. Three-time CEW World Champion. Has ruined many careers: Damian Watts, Shank, Scott Slade. Sidelined five-time champion Aaron Crabill, and put Descendent on the shelf many months ago.
