Is It Just Me? #1

3 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Is It Just Me?” is an opinionative column written by Charles Smith, a former columnist for the defunct Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling promotion’s magazine with a bad attitude and a mind stuck in the golden age of professional wrestling. Did we mention that he is fifty-one years old? And you thought old people were sweet and harmless… …In the first edition of “Is It Just Me?”, Smith speaks his mind on a number of issues including the content of wrestling’s television shows and women involved with the profession. Read at your own risk! The Fans Wrestling Organization is one of the world's top promotions. So why sign feeble women to contracts and waste roster space? Is It Just Me? #1 Hello marks and welcome to the first ever edition of an opinionative column I like to call “Is It Just Me?”. First, allow me to introduce myself. My name is Charles Smith. You may remember hanging on my every word while reading a short-lived column I wrote for PIW Magazine entitled “Climbing the Ladder”. Since PIW is long gone and my rent is due, I’ve signed on to The News’ staff and will bringing you this column whenever I feel like gracing you with its presence. I know, you’re ecstatic right…? …I thought so. Please, don’t drool just yet though. Wait until the column is through. After all, you’ll have more reason to do so then. Let’s move on shall we? Is It Just Me or has wrestling become too dependent on shock value to garner itself fans? I mean, I can’t turn on my television these days without seeing some penis-less freak parading around a cage or someone toting around a mutilated belt and claiming that she’s (more on you later you drug-infested whore!) extreme. What ever happened to good old fashioned competition? Speaking of which… …Is It Just Me or has the classic heel and face combination been replaced by a bevy of neutral characters? What the hell is the point of being neutral in a wrestling federation? We’re not at war here idiots! We’re trying to entertain people! Christ! If guys like Lou Thesz and Gorilla Monsoon were alive today, they’d spit on what you no-talent hacks are trying to pass off as characters. Haven’t you realized yet that there is no gray area? There is only white (faces) and black (heels). Get used to that or get out. Is It Just Me or have women gotten a bit too big for their britches? Back in my day, women were good for three things. Cooking, cleaning, and having children. Women in wrestling weren’t much different though. Just a bit better. Why? Simple. Women involved with professional wrestling and professional wrestlers were good at four things! Cooking, cleaning, having children, and escorting their men to the ring. Now you’ve got a bunch of females prancing around rings in skimpy outfits, but trying to wrestle at the same time! Are you eye candy or are you competitors?Make up your minds already! Oh wait, you’re women so you probably can’t! I seriously cannot fathom why seemingly intelligent bookers like Silver Fox and Reed Young hire women like Amy Campbell and Sweet Aroma. What are they good for? Honestly? They sure as hell aren’t making you sandwiches, so what? Do you actually think they can make impacts on this business? Or are you just keeping them around long enough to boost your ratings? News for you jackasses, booking a federation isn’t about ratings! It’s about protecting the profession that our forefathers handed down to us. Advice to you bookers out there, especially those already making mistakes pertaining to having women on your rosters: Don’t go down the same road Vince McMahon did! He pushed Chyna to an upper-card position and now look what’s happened! She’s posing buck ass naked for a bunch of horny old men in Playboy and his promotion is down the toilet. As for you women wrestlers out there: Stick to what you’re good at, which obviously isn’t wrestling in the squared circle with real athletes. If you want to get involved you can bake the guys cookies or knit some sweaters for them when they travel in cold weather. Either that or you can be like Poison Ivy and write a column. By the way Ivy, I really enjoy your insight. It’s good to hear from a bitch that isn’t all about make-up and gossip… …Something I said? I didn’t think so. Anyway, I can’t gripe about every aspect of today’s version of the wrestling business, unfortunately. There are some people who are keeping the dream alive. Spyder – He’s what I would call your stereotypical heel. He wins titles, can hold more then his own in the ring, can work the microphone in not one but two languages, and cheats to win. What more could you ask for? By the way, I advise you all to head down to the Taco Bell establishment located in El Paso, Texas. Spyder’s cousin makes an excellent bean burrito. Jeff Garvin – They call him ‘the Original’ for a reason. He doesn’t buy into all of this hype that surrounds today’s promotions, even while working in that dingy pit known as the Asylum. Let’s just hope that he can do what Exxa Decimal couldn’t and destroys that place from the inside rather the succumbing to the temptation of being an …ugh… fighter and blowing out his brains like Kenny Rock. Survivor III – This contest keeps the spirit of competition in wrestling alive and doesn’t rely solely on controversy to keep it going. Plus, it revives a dwindling art in tag team wrestling. If there’s one thing that the Fans Wrestling Organization should be applauded for, it is this. Voran Xias – People like to call this guy cliché, but what they don’t understand is that wrestling needs someone to be that clichéd heel. I personally like what he’s doing and love the fact that he can get a rise out of you geeks. Plus, he has his own chant! You cannot resist a guy with his own chant! This is why I propose an idea to all of you. Why not fall to your knees and begin chanting “Charles Rules” whenever you see me in public? I can just hear it now… Until the next time this has been Charles Smith and the very first edition of “Is It Just Me?” P.S: Feel free to send feedback and/or hate mail to my eMail address, which is [detail withheld]. If your message is somewhat intelligent (which I doubt) and is actually worth my team, I may glorify you with a response and make your day by replying. That is all. -Charles fWo Survivor III
