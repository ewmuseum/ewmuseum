Xw's U.S Champion KaGe speak on challenge.

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Earlier today Voran Xias, the fWO U.S Champion, made a challenge to any champion in any federation of the same 2nd ranked status. The match is now set for Xias to face Coral Avalon of A!, but this match doesn't sit well with everyone, namely the U.S Champion of X-Wrestling. Once the match was booked KaGe had these comments. No Longer a Silent Force "I heard the story to late to respond, but I still want to accept the challenge. Xias has a lot of gull to give such a broad challenge and he should be happy it has been accepted already or else I would have to destroy him." He went on to state "Hopefully this won't be a one time thing and I can get my chance to throw my name in this hat of mayhem." He finished by saying "The FAW International champion, The British Bomber, has also accepted the challenge but I guess he is in the same boat with me, but my words aren't just for Xias. Xias vs Avalon is already booked, so that leaves KaGe, Bomber, and everyone else.... this could really get interesting." So KaGe, who holds the Xw United States Title, has thrown his name into what he has dubbed a "Hat of Mayhem". Will his voice be heard and if his challenge is accepted whom will it be from?
