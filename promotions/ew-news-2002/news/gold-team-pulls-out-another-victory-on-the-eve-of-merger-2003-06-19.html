Gold Team pulls out another victory on the eve of merger

19 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With the merger now confirmed to take place after Round Seven voting, the Gold Team picks up the final victory sending the Green Team to the voting booth once more. But this is the final time that the groups will compete each other... from this point on it will be every man... every tag team... for themselves. Survivor III Contest Special judge Fallen had the unfortunate task of making the final call in the Survivor III contest. In the end, Fallen went with the Gold Team, who now has narrowed their loss margin against the Green Team to 3-4. Fallen explained his decision this way to reporters: "I'll try and explain how I judged the way I judged. I know these contests. I know how tough it is for those who come up with the short end of the stick. A couple things led me to pick the Gold Team. If I was guessing, I'd call that team backed into a corner and struggling to stay in the game. They also had another advantage in that they only had four teams to put material out for rather than six. That really plays to the Gold Team's advantage. As I understood it this challenge was to get the teams ready for being merged into one. So by this I think that you've got a push to go individual but still want to acknowledge the fact that a group still exists. Believe me, after six years in this promotion I know what being alone is like. The rising panic and fear was really expressed well by the Gold Team, who framed their material with a common theme, but it wasn't that common theme that won it for them. It was how you took one situation, one place, and they really showed the differences in the team. The Gold Team really helped show that here is a group that is starting to have cracks... both because of the contest thus far and knowing that any alliances are going to be tough to maintain in the weeks to come. I really liked the Emo Kids. I think this is my favorite team in the contest and their work was done very well. Tim Walden & Jane Matheson put out great material. I'd really like to work with them. On the Gold Team, the work of Uprising and Enlightened really spoke to me in this round. I really liked the interplay between RAM and Jordan. The jabs at RAM's weight really speak to the dynamic of this team. So in the end I go with the Gold Team, which I suppose plays into the hands of the faceless corporate owners of this project who want drama... the contest goes forward regardless. The conspiracy is out there, and I do what I do. Alone. Always." The Green Team now heads to the voting booth, while the Gold Team disbands and readies themselves for an all out bloodbath.
