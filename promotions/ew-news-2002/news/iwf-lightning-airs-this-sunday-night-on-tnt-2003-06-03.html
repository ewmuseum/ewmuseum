IWF Lightning airs this Sunday Night on TNT!

3 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Check out some great IWF action this sunday night as we prep up for next week's pay per view! This sunday night, June 8th will mark the 122nd episode of it's flagship television show, IWF Lightning from the Gaylord Entertainment Center in Nashville, Tennesee to air LIVE on the TNT Network. We are only one week away now from the IWF Ringmaster 2003 Pay Per View which will highlight the amazing Ringmaster Tournament as well as other great matches expected to be on the card. With the Pay Per View only being seven days away, the card is really expected to shape up. This week on Lightning, a double main event has been promised. The first part of the double main event will highlight the IWF's primetime performer and IWF "Icon" Champion, Jason Drake in non-title singles action against John Mchugh, a member of the heel stable, "The Icons". The Icons led by IWF Owner, John "The Iceman" Lugo have still shown bitterness to Jason Drake after he turned down their offer to join their stable last week. John "The Iceman" Lugo has also used his power to appoint himself as the special guest referee for this match. With the odds stacked against him, will The Icons get the last laugh before next week's Ringmaster Pay Per View? Or will the IWF's "top dog", Jason Drake somehow overcome the odds and get the last laugh? In the second match of the show's exciting double main event, the IWF World Heavyweight Championship will be on the line as the Champion, Chazz Mendel looks to successfully defend his title in his first title defense as Champion against Johnny Toxic. After defecting from rival promotion, the EWCL last month, Chazz Mendel returned to the scene to make a huge impact in the IWF as the World Heavyweight Champion. Johnny Toxic who returned to the IWF as well three weeks ago will be looking to carry the gold for the first time. Both superstars are known to have an exciting, high-flying style inside the ring so this will be a match that many of the IWF's fans will be looking towards. After a reign of a female tag team as the IWF World Tag Team Champions, Blaise Martelle and Chase Martelle decided to step down as champions last week as Blaise Martelle isn't medically cleared to wrestle and her sister, Chase Martelle has taken a leave from the promotion for Hollywood. Last week on Lightning, Blaise Martelle was able to use her power as a staff member in the Icon Wrestling Federation to book a tag team matchup for this week's show to determine the next IWF Tag Team Champions! In this match, Blaise decided to stick two of the IWF's veterans, Marco and Mr. Rich on a team to face the team of two feuding rivals, Matt Stryker and Scott Clayton. This booking job by Blaise Martelle leads many to believe that she is attempting to stir the pot between Matt Stryker and Scott Clayton to heat up their feud. The people are left to wonder whether Stryker and Clayton will be able to co-exist as a tag team to win the gold against the established superstars, Mr. Rich and Marco. Last week on Lightning, the first two qualifying matches were fought for the IWF Ringmaster Tournament in which we saw Scott Clayton advance when he defeated Johnny Toxic and Matt Stryker advance when he defeated Clu. This Sunday Night, the final two qualifying matches will take place for the Ringmaster Tournament to solidify the final two spots in the Semi-Finals. The first qualifying match will also have IWF Suicidal Champion, Freak Daddy putting his championship on the line against the IWF's "Hardcore God" and member of the Icons, Ace. Ace and Freak Daddy are known to be two of the toughest big-men that the wrestling industry has ever seen......and if our expectations for this match are correct, it will be a "show stealer". Both men are also former Ringmaster Tournament Champions that will be looking to regain the crown once again! The final Ringmaster Qualifying match will showcase IWF Superstar, The Red Freak competeing after a two week hiatus against Johnny Onyx who will be making his IWF debut. Onyx who took the independent scene by storm will now be looking to showcase his talents in this Ringmaster Tournament against another high-flyer and seasoned veteran, The Red Freak. Many want to believe that The Red Freak will walk out of this match the winner but nobody knows what to expect yet from the rookie, Johnny Onyx. It is expected to be a hell of a match. And last but certainly not least, Clu will be looking for revenge on his former best friend, Tommy "The Archenemy" Ortega after Tommy turned his back on Clu a few weeks ago. After Tommy stated that he was simply "getting rid of dead weight", Clu has asked for an opportunity for revenge on The Archenemy this sunday night on Lightning. Clu will be getting his wish and will be looking to prove Tommy wrong and gain his vengeance for what Tommy had done to him. On top of all of this action, so much more is expected to take place on this sunday night's Lightning! Also, tune into Lightning an hour early this week for the IWF Hall of Fame Show! The show is expected to induct two new superstars into the IWF Hall of Fame and honor the careers of the IWF's Hall of Famers. So be sure to check for the IWF Hall of Fame Show at 7:00 PM Eastern Time and Lightning from 8:00 to 10:00 this Sunday Night on the TNT Network! Icon Wrestling Federation
