NWC:A Action Re-Play

22 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Review of Atlantic's latest addition of Maelstrom. Just when you thought it was safe..... Before I start off with my usual musings and ramblings, I was amazed to see a guy called Cade Clouds taking the journalistic reigns and looking in some detail at the failings of Lee Clark, along with the predicted great future for Polar Opposites. Now believe me, P.O are good, but with the tag team division the way it is in Atlantic right now, I reckon Cade has his head up in the clouds! Unlike my good self, Cade writes without having looked at the big picture and if he saw this edition of Maelstrom, well...he might just realise why his statement was kind of premature. As for Lee Clark, maybe he had a point there, but Polar Opposites? Sure they might be champions....ONE day...but they got some wait yet. Two words. Hung Jury. It was one of those nights on Maelstrom where you wouldn’t swap places with certain people if your life totally and completely depended on it. Nathan Boswell, Leonard Aarons and Jake Summers sort of spring to mind here. And you also probably didn’t really want to be a champion either on this night of big title defences. However, before I shoot the breeze over what took my eyes, let’s go and do this regular spot...well...it’s only happened once, but hey.... Video Of The Week: Heatwave – Lovin’ You Is Easy Cuz You’re Beautiful I spoke out very decisively last week about the fact that Crash N Burn made me stop me in my tracks and watch attentively. This week things were made just that little bit harder by the works of both Nathan Boswell and Heatwave, two men familiar to us and familiar to each other. We all look to the main eventers to do the big things and impress us with the big words but this week was about the up and coming stars of the Atlantic. Whilst Boswell showed us just how tough his world can be, in his “Risky Business” film, Heatwave demonstrated something a little bit different. Whilst Crash N Burn reminded us about Memorial Day, Heatwave demonstrated the hardships of the road trips and family life, showing that neither totally marry up successfully, although there is always hope. Again, he took the time to show the wrestling fans, that he hadn’t forgotten about us, by giving Morgan a dressing down. Solid in his game plan, I couldn’t help but be touched by the rapport between the family members, pre-Morgan attack. I’m sure the whole thing gave Bobby’s ‘babe’ plenty to think about, it certainly did for me. Morale of Heatwave’s story? Don’t work long distance, or double bag before you lay down with the chick of your current choice! Seriously though, very impressive work from both Boswell and Heatwave, but Heat wins the gold star for the morality and the Morgan attack! What would it take for me to swap lives with Leonard Aarons at the end of Maelstrom? You don’t have enough money in the world, sorry! You certainly wouldn’t have me trade places with Vanessa either. Now I gotta admit, Derrick Daliente wasn’t doing cartwheels about the inadvertant attack on the lady, but then I didn’t see him showing her infinite concern either. I’m not about to jump on Aarons’ Ratings Express and declare myself his number one fan, but Daliente’s “actions” were kind of extreme. If you’re Leonard, you gotta be pretty stupid to suggest that your future attacker goes back and performs in oral interactions with his leader, Ben Van Iten, so you can’t have all the sympathy in the world for Mr Aarons. So whilst Len and Vanessa were left laying in a battered heap at the hands of Derrick Daliente, you have to say, sooner you guys than me! Another guy who’ll be cursing his luck, will be the soon-to-be-forgotten MaX JaXon. He will be considering his employment options after a quite hopeless display against Adam Kaplan. Still, I’m sure his old job at Burger King won’t have been filled yet.... Jake Summers wasn’t having the best of nights either. It’s strange, I wonder whether Aarons is cursed and his bad luck is rubbing onto others? I think everyone should warn Jake about staying right out of other people’s personal business, his temporary association with Ross Boswell did him no good whatsoever. It left Nathan Boswell wondering what the hell his father and Jake were doing together, and it left Jake ending up laid out by Aaron’s, who gets chased out of the building by SLJ! All this just to find out who ran Jake’s son over. People, people, people...The morale of the story is, don’t bring your personal life into the office! Family + Wrestling = Incompatible. Now I got big sympathy with Nathan Boswell. He went up against a monster of a man in Damien Kahn and lost his New England Title in a brutal match. You can’t blame him though, there’s no doubt that the psycho games of his father Ross, must have screwed with him pre-match. Trouble is you can’t go into a match with Kahn and not have your game head on. Any sign of weakness and it seems like that a-hole will have you regretting it. What was with Kahn spitting on Boswell after the match? That was just pathetic. Aarons may be a jerk, but at least he’s a jerk with some class. Kahn’s just an ass. I won’t tell him that though. Not without some of America’s finest around me first! As a fan, I’m looking at Boswell going far, just like I was with Heatwave, who did a number on Morgan to take away her East Coast title. You got to see this as a huge feather in the cap of the Heat. Bobby’s girl is well trained and a damn tough cookie, so to take a belt away from her, is quite the achievement. Now if Boswell can get his family troubles and his background strife out of his mind, then the Heat may have a contender not too far away. Look for that to ignite again real soon. Quite where Morgan goes from here is anyone’s guess, probably to Irish’s bedroom for some TLC and regrouping....NO! Thoughts like that don’t make me hot! It’s just that....well...curiousity...? NO! NO! Not about him, I mean her, I mean it! Damnit. Move on. I love it when the NWC Champ comes to town. I can’t help but be patriotic when it comes to the federation I love watching. So who was I expecting to get the big shot? Bobby Irish? Jake Summers? Hell, a long shot like Aarons? Perhaps Billy Page would feel really generous and give it to someone like Boswell or Morgan? Erm, nope. Didn’t happen. What did we get instead? The big event, the big shot, the big chance for Atlantic?? “Bermuda” Billy Page Versus *drum roll* Jim Beattie. Yes. Jim Beattie. Now nothing personal here, but WHAT IS THE WORLD COMING TO?! I couldn’t bear to think of the NWC title being worn by someone whose name reminds me too much of Warren Beattie! Yeah yeah, he’s Atlantic so I should be backing him, but come on! This kid has won like one match out of probably around one hundred! He’s a fluke! A miracle! A one hit wonder! We have been conned by Page and frankly I think you all should write a letter of protest to Joey Long to try and get us a second go. If the kid does the business, I tell you what I’ll do....I will strip down to my underpants and stand in Time Square, singing “I’m a little teapot”! Sheesh, what is the world coming to? Oh well, I suppose I should cheer Beattie on. If I must. Nice work Page. I guess we would all do the same though. It’s basically a night off after all. Go Jimmy. [sigh] It makes me wonder what I’ll be telling you all about next week?! “Bermuda” Billy Page agrees to fight a man in a large pink rabbit costume? The mind boggles, but you know what folks? Keep it locked on Maelstrom, keep it loaded on Atlantic, because where Atlantic and us, the fans, go, hell will surely follow! I’ve been your host P.J Smith for this enlightening tale and I await the millions of letters from parents whose children have had nightmares as a result of this column. Compensation will be in the mail. God bless, Be Lucky P.J Smith National Wrestling Council
