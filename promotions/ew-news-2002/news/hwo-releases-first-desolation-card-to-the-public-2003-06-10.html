hWo Releases first Desolation card to the public

10 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With June 15, 2003 approaching very quickly Trevor Norman has finally released the first official card for Desolation - 'F-ck Old School'. Hello all I am [name withheld] and I have just been hired as the hWo’s newest Internet reporter. I am here to give all you fans the news and dirt that is going on around the hWo. I am really happy to be aboard a young federation that will go far in the wrestling world. Hardcore Wrestling Organization will go above and beyond the ordinary to bring you fans the best in ‘HARDCORE’ action. My first official act as the hWo’s Internet’s reporter will be to bring you fans the first official hWo Desolation card that will air live on June 15, 2003. Desolation – ‘Fuck Old School’ June 15, 2003 Live from Miami, FL Only on FX Hardcore Rules Match Dante Daze vs. Ben Hayes Hardcore Rules Match Maroon vs. Phate Tag Team Tornado Match Squadra Italia vs. Jay Jaxxen and Dean McDaniels Triple Threat Match Majik Seven vs. Anima Vs Kelvin Kash Table Match Frost vs. Omega The Ending of a Great Feud Rhett Locke vs. The Dynamo Main Event – Hardcore Rules Branson Blair vs. Dane Rivers hWo
