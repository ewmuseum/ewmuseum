Secret Meetings in uW

23 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Needed change in Universal Wrestling? With a new T.V. contract and a band of new faces, the federation is still faced with slumping ratings. Meetings over the weekend could spell the end of current promoter Tyler Bayne. The uW news division offices fired up into a fever pitch late Monday afternoon over reports of a secret meeting held over the weekend. Unnamed uW execs have hinted at talks with a powerful old promoter. Current contracts forbid anyone from Universal Entertainment to discuss executive hiring without permission from Tyler Bayne's attorneys or Bayne himself. This would make the meetings this weekend grounds for termination of any uWE employee involved. One source commented; "There's changes that need to be made in the organization. Bayne has taken uW as far as some of the execs feel he can take it." This comment begs the question; "Just who do the execs feel is competent enough to take the front office and television persona of uW back to where we once saw it?" Ratings began to slump when uW's old T.V. contract with USA Networks was pulled for too many violations of FCC regulations. HBO quickly signed the promotion for a deal rumored to be worth only just over two million for two seasons. These figures are reportedly little more than what it takes the company to break even, costing them longtime fan favorites like Drachenfyre, Shawn St James, and even Frost. Frost, an upstart new face, was quickly a crowd favorite, however his injury of a few weeks ago is said by some to be "exagerrated". Frost was in the middle of contract negotiations with uWE when his "injury" took place. The promotion hasn't had a house show since late april due to money factors. Pay-per-view buy-rates are the worst the company has ever seen in it's almost four years of operation. Many wrestling reports say the federation is in it's twilight. "They've been around for a long time. They've faught through mediocrity and put on great shows with talent that have went on to become superstars." Rich Remick writer for WrestlingWorld continues; "Guys like Big Little Italy from CWL, to gay Jamal from PIW fame have all appeared there. uW has been a place guys love to wrestle, but the leadership has never been solid. They've had constant front office drama. This has caused a great fed to be a 'one-show-promotion'. One huge show a year that everyone appreciates, but no one watched week-in and week-out." "Tyler Bayne cannot be held fully responsible," comments one unnamed talent; "but he sure as hell isn't the answer." Universal Wrestling
