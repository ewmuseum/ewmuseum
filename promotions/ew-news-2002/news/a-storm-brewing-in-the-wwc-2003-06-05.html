A "Storm" Brewing in the WWC?

5 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WWC's Talent Scouts have finally released to the press, their first prospect on their growing roster. World Wrestling Corporation talent scouts have been scouring the country’s independent federations in search of professional wrestling’s next big thing, all desperately wanting to please the WWC’s owner Paul Levine, who wants nothing but the best talent on his new promotion’s roster. However, one of these scouts, more specifically former professional competitor Lauren Host, has done the exact opposite and has officially announced the signing of a man that may have been considered one of wrestling’s next big things about two years ago. Host reportedly met with the man known as Kory Storm (Real Name: Cory Rutherford III) and his agent Christopher Kingsley in Storm’s hometown of San Diego, California and signed both men to a six figure contract with Levine’s upstart federation. We reached Ms. Host via telephone and asked for her thoughts regarding Kory Storm and her reasoning behind signing him to an iron clad WWC contract. “Storm has competed in many federations in many countries and has won many titles while doing so.” Host said confidently. “He’ll be a very valuable asset to this promotion. I can feel it.” When confronted with stories of Kory Storm’s past personal demons, demons that have damaged his position in federations like Old School Wrestling and Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling, however, Host had this to say: “Kory and his people have assured me that these problems, most of them involving an addiction to painkillers, have been taken care of and will not be present during Storm’s tenure in the World Wrestling Corporation.” Lauren told us through a muffled telephone speaker. “If they do turn up, however, we will show no remorse when it comes to releasing him just as the federations before us have done.” Storm, who is a former Innovation of Wrestling World Heavyweight Champion and also the former owner of that same promotion, was unavailable for comment due to his rigorous training schedule. We here at the World Wrestling Corporation’s headquarters were able to get in touch with “Perfection Personified’s” agent, manager, and best friend Christopher Kingsley. Kingsley chuckled when presented with the negative statistics regarding his client that we spoke with Lauren Host about earlier. “You fools are only looking at one side of the superstar called Kory Storm. You talk about his injuries, his failures, and his addictions. Yet, you fail to recognize the positive things he has done for this business. You must have forgotten about the countless victories and title reigns that he acquired in the squared circle! You must have just conveniently ignored the fact that when Kory Storm appears on television, the ratings shoot up like an old man’s cock on Viagra!” As you can see, there are many views regarding Kory Storm and what kind of impact he’ll make on the promotion once he arrives here. Lauren Host, however, remains on the optimistic side of the proverbial fence. “There is no way I could have not signed him. I mean, he has talent as a promoter which we saw during his last run in IOW, can still go blow for blow with anyone in the ring, and can work a crowd anywhere in the world simply by opening his mouth! He’s an icon in this business and I’m looking for him to prove that statement true once the World Wrestling Corporation starts up!” Host exclaimed. Kingsley chose to sum things up in a much more conceited manner stating: “No matter how you critics slice it, Kory Storm is an irreplaceable figure in this profession and a true superstar. Why do you think we call him “Perfection Personified”?” Only time can tell exactly what Kory Storm will do once the WWC goes live, but one thing is for sure. The world will be watching.
