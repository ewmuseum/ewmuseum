Stanislav leaving the OSW?

5 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With the fallout from Ascension, the problems with Tempest, and his new focus on revolution, former OSW World Champion Ivan Stanislav is looking in the most unlikely place for 'asylum.' The Asylum may be adding another member to their ranks in the form of one Ivan Stanislav, Russian soldier turned wrestler that has been long time roster member of the OSE and the late PCW. What appeared to have started with a scathing article on eW News itself from The Russian Bear, in which he publicly called for a revolution within the walls of The Asylum, appears to be moving forward quicker than expected. Stanislav, who has been in the OSE since it’s inception over a year and a half ago, has grown increasingly unhappy with the situation in the Old School Wrestling division of the OSE. These doubts were solidified with his defeat at Ascension in which he failed to attain the Presidency of the OSW from Trashcan Man. With multiple awards and annals beneath his suspenders, Stanislav has proven to be one of the heaviest hitters and biggest draws in the OSE, but it would appear that he’s prepared to part ways. “Being first members in this wretched federation,” Stanislav’s longtime friend and Intelligence associate Alexei Ruslan commented, “we do not need to go through red tape of contracts to leave. Quite frankly, we would not honor anything anyway. What are they going to do? Fine Mother Russia? Hee hee haa!!” Ruslan went on to say that if Stanislav leaves the OSW he’d take all of his Comrades with him, if they so choose to go. While it may appear to be a relief to know that Ivan and his Red Army may be leaving the OSW, it would leave a large void in the federation itself, regardless. “Asylum is in need of order and revolution! We are the men to do it!” Said Ruslan. The former OSW World Champion has further severed his ties with the OSE after fallout with his long time girlfriend, Tempest, and it would not be surprising for Ivan to want to distance himself more and leave. While members of the Asylum were not available for comment, certainly the front office is aware of Stanislav’s repeated calls. Can the Asylum handle someone as regimented and controlling as Ivan Stanislav? It appears the Bear may be migrating. “It will be Red wherever he goes! Red with the blood of his enemies and Red with the beauty of communism!!” –Alexei Ruslan Old School Entertainment
