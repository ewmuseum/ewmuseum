DWA: Showdown 06/23/2003 Recap

25 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The last Showdown before Dead Men Walking is in the bag. A five man battle royal ensues to see who will be joining the Royal Rumble next to last. Shockwave will be taking on member of the FoW, Drastic, and in the main event, the World Champion, Typhoid Mary, will team-up with LeStatt Knight against The Revolution, Payne and Jeff Wylde. Did I mention Jordan Brannigan announces the order of the Royal Rumble as well? Showdown began in Jordan Brannigan's office, where he received a surprise visit from currently injured wrestler, Velocity Champion, Zula Shandor. She attempted to "help" Jordan by giving him resumes of several referees, as the DWA only has two referees at the moment. Jordan thanks her, and sent her on her way, before throwing the resumes away. Then the DWA was treated to a preview of what will happen at Dead Men Walking, as five superstars (Ninja X, Ashura, Cemetary Gates, NeX, and Jimmy Changa) took part in a battle royal, where the winner will enter the Royal Rumble at the next to last spot. Why the next to last spot, because Commissioner Gideon Nazareth has already granted himself the last spot in the Royal Rumble. It was a great match, as NeX and Jimmy Changa were eliminated within moments of each other, and then Ninja X was eliminated, and then newcomer Cemetary Gates, in only his second match, eliminated Ashura, entering the Royal Rumble at number 29. Then Jordan Brannigan made another appearance, announcing the order of the 30 Superstar Royal Rumble... 1. By request of Commissioner Nazareth…Grovel. 2. LeStatt Knight 3. EZ Target 4. Unnamed Wrestler 5. NeX 6. Unnamed Wrestler 7. Donnie Darko 8. John Reed 9. Brad Keidis 10. Zula Shandor 11. Jimmy Changa 12. Newcomer, Starr List 13. Shockwave 14. Jeff Wylde 15. One of my little surprises 16. Ninja X 17. One of my little surprises 18. Pepper Ortega 19. Drastic 20. Ashura 21. Brad Clinton 22. Newcomer, Lupus Feral 23. One of my little surprises 24. Derek Cantina 25. Ronnie Johnson 26. Robert Cummings 27. Unnamed Wrestler 28. One of my little surprises 29. Cemetary Gates 30. Gideon Nazareth After finding out that his name was included in the rumble, commentator, Ronnie Johnson, got up and stormed to the back, demanding to see Jordan. Then Shockwave took on Drastic. As a member of the FoW, the 1/2 Tag Team Champion, was accompanied by John Reed and Brad Keidis, but that's ok, because Shockwave was accompanied by Intoxicated Justice. FoW try to get involved, but are stopped by Intoxicated Justice, and Shockwave nails the Whiplash to pick up the 1, 2, 3. However, the action did not stop there, as all six men started a war that was only stopped by security. Back in Jordan Brannigan's office, he announces that the LeStatt Knight vs. Jeff Wylde match has been changed to a Last Man Standing match...right before both are scheduled to compete in the Royal Rumble. Then Ronnie Johnson storms in, and demands to know what Jordan is up to. Jordan says it was Commissioner Nazareth that put him in the rumble, but he'd strike a deal with him...if he wins...instead of the title shot...he'd give him the job as Commissioner again and fire Nazareth. Ronnie agrees. Then it was time for the Main Event as Typhoid Mary and LeStatt Knight took on The Revolution. It was a brutal match from the opening bell, as the rules were thrown out almost immediately. Everyone was busted open by the end of the match, but it ended when Mary nailed an implant double arm DDT on the person she defends her World Title against on Sunday...Payne...picking up the 1, 2, 3!
