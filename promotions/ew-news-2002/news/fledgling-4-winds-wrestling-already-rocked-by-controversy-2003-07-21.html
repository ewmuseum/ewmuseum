Fledgling 4 Winds Wrestling already rocked by controversy.

21 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With only a few shows under it’s belt, the 4WW is starting off with a bang. From a wrestling veteran coming in and claiming it’s North Wind Title only to spite the owner, to an open male chauvinist who can’t seem to win a match, stealing the South Wind Title. First, former multiple time Wrestling Champion, Jay Phoenix paraded his way on in to win the North Wind Gauntlet, only to begin his revenge against the married couple that owns 4 Winds Wrestling, Fey and Ian Childress. A move that has sparked it’s own retaliation in Ian Childress booking Jay Phoenix in a coal miner’s glove this last week against Psymon. A match that ended with Psymon and Impulse, one of Ian Childress’ wrestling alter-ego’s, taking the North Wind Champion out. And now, in it’s light of crowning it’s second champion, the South Wind Champion, has spawned a controversy that will have to be settled, and settle soon. After a controversial win this last week, and being on the receiving end of a huge chair shot, Patti Anderson, ‘The Darling of the 4WW’, will again meet Pajaro de Presa in a rematch to determine who will walk out with the silver strap. But after this last weekend’s edition of StormFront, where Sean Summers, the very man who used that chair on Patti Anderson, interfered in the match, and then took the South Wind Title for himself, it’s going to really heat up in Phoenix. “Simply Stunning” Sean Summers has been given his chance to not only finally claim a victory in the 4WW arena, but if he wins the opening match, he’ll move on to make the main event a triple threat match. Fey McClancy-Childress has sworn that someone will leave as the champion this week. Although many know that Fey would not like to see Patti Anderson leaving under her own power, let alone as the South Wind Champion. While Sean Summers has the pressure of proving himself in the ring, Patti Anderson has so much more on her mind. From her guaranteed opponent this week, Pajaro de Presa offering to take her back to his hotel room for some kinky cheez wiz games, to finding out that her boyfriend and valet, former wrestling champion, Sean McClancy is already promised to another…will Patti Anderson be ready for a match of this magnitude this week. Mentally , and physically. Current odds makers are putting the spots on Pajaro de Presa to leave with the title, as the only thing he’s got to worry about is getting out of the dumpster he lives in before the trash men come to pick it up. But another 4WW star has taken notice of the luchadore wrestler and could be making his move on the Rudo with an attitude-o in the near future. Tune in Sunday for StormFront to find out more! 4 Winds Wrestling!
