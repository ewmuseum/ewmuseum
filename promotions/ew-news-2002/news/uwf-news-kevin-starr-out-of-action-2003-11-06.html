UWF News : Kevin Starr Out of Action

6 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After a recent SAS where Kevin Starr sliced off his own pinky finger using a sword, he was rushed to a hospital... or just back to his emergency room as he was already in a hospital in the first place. Kevin Starr had surgery to try and connect his pinky finger back to his hand right away. Unfortunately, the doctors there were unable to do so, leaving Kevin Starr with only 9 fingers. When I asked a doctor when we can expect Kevin Starr back in action, he answered... "He will most likely be able to return in less then two weeks. It's not that type of injury that can lasts for months upon months. I'd expect him back for next SAS." Then I turned toward Kevin Starr himself and asked him when he would be ready to return to the ring, he replied... "Well... all that I can say is that you won't be seeing Kevin Starr in a ring for awhile. This pinky injury is not only threatening me being a World Champion, but my wrestling career as well. I'm going to have to go back and adjust to all of my moves without having to use my pinky, which may take away months out of my wrestling career!" I then told Kevin Starr that the Doctor in charge of the surgery had cleared him to go back to wrestling by next week, he shot back quickly at me by saying... "Well what does he know! He's not a wrestler! I am! I'm the wrestler! He doesn't know what it's like to lift a 250 pound man over your hear with only FOUR fingers! This injury is much more serious to a wrestler! I won't be able to return for at least a month!" I tried asking Kevin Starr another question, but he quickly left the room. So exactly how long will Kevin Starr be out of action? I don't know. 2 weeks? A month? A year?! The answer is still a mystery, but for now, Kevin Starr will remain on the unactive list UWF Wrestling
