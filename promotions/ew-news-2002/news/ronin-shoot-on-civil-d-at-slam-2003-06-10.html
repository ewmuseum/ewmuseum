Ronin shoot on Civil D at Slam!

10 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Viewers of OSW's flagship program, Slam!, on Sunday night got more than they bargained for when the Old School Entertainment Tag Team Champions, Ronin, broke script and shot on former OSW greats Civil Disobedience, who were in attendance. Several weeks ago, OSW owner Jane Reagan had an idea. Pitting her current tag team champions, Matt Register and Jeffrey Wayne, against the returning legends of Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke. The meeting of Ronin and Civil Disobedience, respectively, two teams considered to be the greatest in OSW history, looked to be a marquee match for the upcoming June Pay-Per-View, Ascension. However, the perfect plan has shown itself to be everything but. Trouble began when Masters was unwilling to return to OSW for the contract. After being blackmailed into attending Sunday's show, it seemed he had little choice but to work with his old partner again. And any doubt was erased once the reason for his being there became obvious. As Ronin appeared in a live interview with OSW backstage man Matt Shivan, reportedly scheduled as punishment for their no-show a week before, they ignored convention and shot on the former OSW champions, accusing them of politics and holding other teams down during their time as tag champs. "I mean, we wanted a match under real circumstances to show everyone who was the best, but they’ve got their tails tucked and they don’t want their “legacy” tainted," Register said on-air. "All that really means is that they’ve lost their weight with the men upstairs." The comments noticeably upset the former champions, who were restrained by security following the interview. Any chance of a Civil D/Ronin match to determine the best team in OSW history is currently unknown, although Seth Masters, the lone holdout, was noticeably screaming "Sign it!" as he was removed from the arena. There is no word on if officials will punish the tag champs for their actions. Ronin has shown a history of defiance and troublemaking for administrators since their arrival, and action against them seems to be imminent. Sunday's Slam!
