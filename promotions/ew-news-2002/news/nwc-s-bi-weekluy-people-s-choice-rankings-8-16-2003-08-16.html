NWC's Bi-Weekluy People's Choice Rankings [8/16]

16 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Billy Page remains atop the NWC fan rankings, but nipping at his heels include NA title holder Teron Hain and two of the top contenders for his belt, Pac's Julie Reveli and Central's Edward Lambert. 1. NWC World Heavyweight Champion Bermuda Billy Page- [1] Well, like fine wine this guy gets better with age. Since beating Lance Marshall on Center Stage, Page has been Billy Blaze ever since then. He gave Jim Beattie a severe case of Heat Stroke...five times over. Then, at Patriot Games in Washington D.C., he held off tenacious young challenger Teron Hain. This coming week at Uprising, he'll defend his crown against tenacious hellcat and Bitch-Goddess Supreme...Jade. 2. NWC North American Champion "The Future" Teron Hain- [2] There was a point in time that if you held this North American title, you were almost assured to lose it rather than defend it successfully. That has changed thanks to "The Future" and his firm hold on the belt since he upended Rictor Arkane on Center Stage. He has successfully fought off the likes of Sylis Walker and East Coast Champion Heatwave, but came up short in arguably one of the best World title matches since the restructure at Patriot Games as Page retained. Yet, in a match that would've ended the NA title as we knew it, Page came out of the match with a great deal of respect for Hain and the North American title. So it lives on and now, the hunt is on to see who can make "The Future" a past thought the next time he defends his North American title. 3. Pacific West Coast Heavyweight Champion Julie Reveli- [3] Whoever said a woman's place is in the kitchen, never met this woman. More than likely, if he said it in the ring in front of said woman, he wasn't standing at the end of the night. Ever since defeating Bobby Polo for this title, she has had to fight off Alec Spade and then the machinations of one Polo who got the belt off of her through a power play. At Patriot Games, the power play was killed by The Man who repealed Bobby's move and put the belt back around Julie's waist. In the top heavy main event roster that the Pacific has, Reveli is a marked woman and figures to be one of the odds on favorites to represent the region as its number one contender for the NWC World Heavyweight Title. The only question is, will she be given the opportunity of a lifetime and if so, will she be able to capitalize on it? 4. BloodAngel- [5] His reign as Central Heavyweight Champion was cut short by the hands of Inquisitor on an episode of Center Stage prior to Patriot Games. Then, as if that wasn't bad enough, there was the returning Devin Fox after him with [name withheld] wanting Lambert for his little cause. Fast forward to Patriot Games itself. Shortly before the War Games main event, The Six led by [name withheld] came out to address the crowd. Lambert and Saotome came out to address Chris when out of the clear blue, Saotome blindsided Lambert with several shots with a baseball bat. While he was unsuccessful at War Games in helping Team Central get the win, he's got a whole new war to worry about now. It's him versus Six. But it's the one named Saotome he's after. 5. Foster Nackedy- [T6] He was riding an incredible high after finally securing the Southern States Heavyweight Title. Then, things went downhill slowly but surely. First, there was Bobby Irish's love interest calling him, which lead to an angry encounter with Bobby's favorite weapon of choice known as the crowbar. Then, there was a chance encounter one on one with Hiro Saotome which lead to him losing to Hiro. Then came his first title defense against Jade's love interest, Sean Laughreah. Surely the man can catch a break here, right? Nope. Sean surprised Gup and walked away with the title, but bad news comes in twos. During the post-match theatrics, Anton Dufresne managed to find his locker room and proceeed to nearly kill Foster's son, Connor. This needless to say, didn't sit well at all with Foster. So much so that Foster waited until after the Dayde/Dufresne match and with the aid of a steel pipe and a few well timed swings, he managed to paralyze The Technical Terror. 6. Southern States Heavyweight Champion Sean Laughreah- [NR] Stop laughing, we're serious here. For a while, this former TSW Heavyweight Champion was nothing more than the other half of the dWo or BVI's Traveling Humiliation Circus. The laughs, catcalls were all there until...he dethroned the man that beat his lady in order to become Southern States Heavyweight Champion. Sadly enough, his was omitted from Team South in a move many feel could've put the South over the top. That of course doesn't mean he wasn't "active" at Patriot Games. In a bizarre twist of events, Tabatha Albright was brutally beaten by Jade or so we think. Shortly after carrying her to an ambulance in the back of the MCI Center, he ran into one crazed Bane that wasn't in the greatest of moods. One of the single bloodiest backlot brawls ensued and from it, we've got the South's two top dogs set to lock up for the Southern States Heavyweight Title this coming week on Uprising. 7. Atlantic Heavyweight Champion "Golden Boy" Jake Summers- [4] Since beating Bobby Irish for the title back in mid May, he hasn't been stopped. He has since thwarted the likes of Marc Smith and the recently departed Nathan Boswell in consecutive title defenses. With the recent changing of the guard in the Atlantic, it remains unsure who will oversee the day to day operations of this region. Much the same can be said about who the number one contender is for Jake's Atlantic Heavyweight Title. Grumblings have been running rampant that "Illtown" Leonard Aarons is clamoring for a shot. Only question is, with the personal history between these two, how soon will it be before these two finally collide? 8. Hiro Saotome- [10] No more Mister Nice Guy...and then some. The Little Asian That Could took out every preconceived notion about what a nice guy should be with several swings of a baseball bat. Now officially aligned with [name withheld]'s "Six", Hiro has now incurred the wrath of one large and extremely angry Hardcore Legend in his former best friend and tag team partner, BloodAngel. 9. Pacific Rocky Mountain Champion Lazarus- [16] Half mask, half amazing. No doubt. This young man has risen from the depths to not only become Rocky Mountain Champion, but his awe inspiring performance at Civil WarGames is the reason that the talent heavy Pacific owns all of the title shots going into the next NWC pay per view. Unfortunately for all that's going right for this young enigma that just recently aligned with [name withheld]'s troop, he's got a tough task ahead of him in the form of highly decorated and dangerous Bobby Polo this week on Paradise Lost. 10. "Starman" Lee Clark- [22] There doesn't seem to be much that can slow this man down. In his ongoing pursuit of the NWC World Heavyweight Championship, he was a member of Team Pacific which came out victorious at War Games and now, he only hopes will bring him a step closer to becoming NWC World Heavyweight Champion. 11. "The Foundation" Tony Pride- [NR] Back at Revival, he lost a Loser Leave South match to "High Society" Eugene Robinson. For a while, it was assumed that we had seen the last of Pride in the NWC. Then...just when ya thought it was safe to turn on the tube, he returns like a bogeyman with murderous intent. Not only did he return on Paradise Lost, he singlehandedly managed to ambush Julie Reveli following her match with Alec Spade, sing a song that got cut short and oh yeah...did we forget to mention he got face to face with Alexander? This little face to face led to their epic battle at Civil WarGames which ended in a no contest after the interference of...well, everyone and The Devil Himself. With Pride back in the Pacific and there being unfinished business between himself and Alexander, only time will tell what happens to "The Foundation"...humbly speaking of course. 12. Jade- [NR] The Bitch-Goddess Supreme saw her reign as Southern States Heavyweight Champion end at Revival in Domino Effect 2. Now, heading into this upcoming Uprising she's got the chance of a lifetime. It'll be her going one on one with UME alum and reigning NWC World Heavyweight Champion Bermuda Billy Page. 13. Confederate Memorial Champion "The Bane" Rictor Arkane- [T6] Call him what ya want. Call him crazy as a pet coon, you'd be right. Say he's got the fuse of a rabid pitbull and you'd be dead on. Call him a former champion and you'd be dead wrong. He is the longest reigning champion in the NWC and even though it's a secondary title, there's no denying this man his just due. He has been a "Bane To Your Existence" and that of anyone that stands across from him. Despite being omitted from Team South, he still found time to brawl with Southern States Heavyweight Champion Sean Laughreah which now leads to an epic showdown on this week's Uprising. Can he finally seize the brass ring which has alluded him for so long? 14. "Wise Guy" Wyatt Connors- [T14] 15. Central Heavyweight Champion Inquisitor- [NR] NWC's Bi-Weekly People's Choice Rankings
