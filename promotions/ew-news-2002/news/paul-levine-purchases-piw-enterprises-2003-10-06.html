Paul Levine purchases PIW enterprises.

6 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Paul Levine, owner of the WWC (a federation currently building itself as a full force), currently had a meeting with former PIW employees and lawyers to meet an agreement to have all material PIW copyrighted to be allowed to use on WWC television. Paul Levine, the currenty owner of the World Wrestling Corporation, was most well known from his tenure as head booker/writer/road agent in the now defunct Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling. After Levine had secured enough wrestlers to open a full fledged roster, and enough time to gain a television contract with Home Box Office...Levine had finally took a step further with his money, and went into meetings with former employees and lawyers of the PIW, in interest of purchasing all copyrighted PIW material. "To make money, you need to spend money," said Levine, "This move towards our opening will get us publicity. It's been rumored that we, at the WWC, have picked up alot of PIW roster members...and even some developmental characters from the Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling. In order for us to make promotional material on these wrestlers, we will need clips of their previous federation. With PIW being closed, I figured it'd be cheaper and easier to buy the material now...since it is no longer needed by these executives" One big problem was that the lawyers of the defunct PIW, stated that the material could be used to mass produce PIW video tapes in order to gain a profit. Levine reached an agreement that the two sides could share royalties to the PIW profits by splitting it 70/30...WWC receiving 70%, PIW executives receiving 30%. WWC also has plans on using the material in promotional material for WWC commercials/promotional videos/such to that extent. No further information was given, but it seems the contracts have been signed, and Paul Levine and the World Wrestling Corporation, inc. currently holds 70% of the rights to PIW related material.
