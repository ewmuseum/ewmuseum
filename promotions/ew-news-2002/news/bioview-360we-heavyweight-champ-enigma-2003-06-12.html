BioView: 360°WE Heavyweight Champ, Enigma

12 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A quick look into the life and strengths of 360°WE World Heavyweight Champion, Enigma. Wrestler's Name: Enigma Wrestler's Age: 25 Wrestler's Sex: Male Wrestler's Height: 7'2 Wrestler's Weight: 346 Wrestler's Appearance: He has long straight black hair, a thick goatee without the mustache and beaming green eyes. He is well built. He likes to wear black leather pants with either a black or white tanktop and a long black trench coat during his entrance that he takes off before the match starts. Wrestler's Gimmick: He pretty much acts like a badass that is neutral and a powerhouse. Favorite Moves: chokeslam, vertical and german suplexes, clothesline, big boot, gut wrench powerbomb, a couple technical moves like an armbar, grapevine, and abdominal stretch. other moves include an implant ddt, spinebuster, helicopter backbreaker, underhook sitdown powerbomb w/out pin, flapjack, somoan drop, t-bone suplex, military press into faceplant, and an inverted atomic drop to name a few. Finishing Move: Blackout; Punishment Finishing Description: Blackout- Its a top rope powerbomb except he follows his opponent off the top rope allowing him to make more of an impact, and it is set up with Punishment which is a double handed chokeslam. and sometimes he'll just use the Punishment to end the match. Wrestler's Entrance: The lights in the arena slowly dim and the arena stays dark for a few moments. Then slowly, "Gently" by Slipknot begins to play. The bells and drums of the song slowly beat as lights under the stage light up and fade with the drums and bells. Fog slowly begins to roll out of the entry-way as the music cuts off sharply and a voice comes over the speaker system. The voice is deep, scratchy and has an English accent, not the light kind, but the heavy type that was more common in the medieval times. Voice- Once again the darkness has taken a hold of his soul and turned him in what he was destined to be. But this time something is different, something has gotten hold of him that will never let go. The final descent into the eternal abyss of darkness has begun, and with time this force will be everywhere and everything. The darkness... will consume... you all. When the voice fades a huge explosion goes off on the stage and the arena is lit up for a split second. When the fire fades the arena is then dimly lit by little candle like flames all around the stage and all the way down the ramp. "Gently" starts up again and as it does Enigma walks out of the entry-way shrouded under a heavy cloak, which is completely black. And behind him out walks Niki, dressed in tight black leather pants, a black tight shirt with a random tribal sign across the chest. Her black hair danles across her back as she stands next to Enigma. Enigma pauses shortly at the top of the ramp and then proceeds down the ramp. As he walks down the ramp, the little candle-like flames extinguish. When Enigma and Niki get ringside, the fog swirls around the ring. Enigma climbs up the steel steps followed by Niki. The two climb into the ring and stand in the middle as the fog dicipates and the lights return to normal. Niki takes Enigma's cloak and steps out of the ring with it. Manager's Name: Niki Manager's Sex: Female Manager's Age: 24 Manager's Appearance: Tall, slender legs. Straight black hair that extends down to the middle of her back. She has glowing green eyes. Her face is very dark, as in her eyes have dark shades of eyeliner and masscara around them. She wears black lipstick, and pretty much everything about her is dark, but in an exremely sexy way. 360° Wrestling Entertainment
