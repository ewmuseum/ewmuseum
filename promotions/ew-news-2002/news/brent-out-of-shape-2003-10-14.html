Brent Out of Shape

14 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The only insight to the twisted and Explosive world of..XwA Yo, XWA! Brent is back with thoughts and smack! Okay, so maybe I'm not destined to be a rap star, but let me talk about something I do know - Wrestling! This week's heart-pounding episode of Implosion began with our usual match-up rundown by KC and The Jester. I cannot stress how important it is to keep the fans informed on what's going on - if we miss that opening draw, we can lose our viewers no matter how exciting our shows are. We headed backstage for another Bradley Kinkaid vignette. Finally, we learn that the voice on the other end of Kinkaid's phone happens to be his boss... and his boss is coming next week! It should be interesting to see exactly WHO calls the shots. Next up, we saw an intense match up between Nailz and Devil's Advocate that saw the match end only by a referee decision. DA put on a great performance, and Nailz showed he's primed and ready for his No. 1 Contender's match with Hart Evans at Deadly Games. We were treated to back-to-back matches as Taxx took on Omen. Omen was working with an injury, and it showed. Taxx got a quick win, and I wish Omen a speedy recovery as I know he's gonna be big when he's at 100%. Then, DA cut one hell of a promo, albeit short. It had a slightly heelish feel to it, but either way, I feel DA it set to make a big impact sooner rather than later. Finally, we were taken to our main event! What can I say folks? The main event is truly where it's at in the XWA. The interaction between Genesis, Hacklust, Diablo, the Connection and now Brad Kinkaid himself has been the top draw in the XWA. Diablo's surpise turn and the match set up by Kinkaid added to the excitement as Impulse went off the air. All in all, fans got their money's worth at this Implosion - it did a great job of it's chief goal - building towards the next Implosion! Wait, what can we expect there? You'll just have to check out "Brent Out of Shape" on Wednesday! Rhetorical and Unanswered Questions!!! Imagine if the XWA didn't have cell phones? When was the last time you saw a ref end the match of his own accord? Wasn't that some performance by DA? Wasn't that some performance by NAILZ?! How incredible was Taxx in that matchup? I wonder if that's an OMEN of things to come? Didn't Advocate give Courtney Foxx a GREAT example of what she can expect from the XWA superstars? Didn't the missed tags during the main event just scream out "HEEL TURN"? Aren't you interested to see how Hart Evans performs one-on-one? No? You'd better be, or the Connection's gonna cream your a--er... sorry, I'm a huge Canada fan. Does anyone else here get the distinct impression that Kinkaid does not like Diablo? Check me out again on Wednesday for my Implosion preview!!! Later days, Brent Fawler Friday Night Implosion
