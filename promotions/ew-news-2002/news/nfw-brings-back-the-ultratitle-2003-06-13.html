NFW Brings Back the Ultratitle!

13 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
First, the fWo takes on the WWE and now someone wants to take on Major League Baseball? Well, that might not be the intent of NFW, but this summer could commence a wrestling revolution as we know it. 24 men wrestle over a period 20 weeks, ONE trophy to be held by a victorious champion. While retaining a core of twelve wrestlers from their previous incarnation, NFW has also leaked word to the press that they’ve signed more than a handful of national superstars interested in competing for the ULTRATITLE – a trophy not seen since the mid ‘90’s in the CSWA. President Quentin Sullivan explains, “Originally, the Ultratitle was seen as a way to determine who REALLY the best wrestler in the world was…With so many leagues out there, none of these wrestlers really know if they’re the best, our hope is that the Ultratitle will answer that question for them.” Wrestlers will participate in a 16 week season that’s format NFW has kept secret, but Sullivan has promised “plenty twists, turns and innovative matches” to go along with this innovative spin on a wrestling league. What we do know is that the 24 wrestlers signed up will be split into two conferences this weekend at the Royal Senestra Hotel in Boston, Massachussetts. Drawn in a lottery, 12 wrestlers will go to the North Conference and 12 wrestlers to the South Conference. NFW plans to air their CRASH TV soon afterwards showing all the introductory festivities. Quentin Sullivan commented, “We’ve chosen conference chairpersons that put their own unique brand on the conferences. From what I’ve heard, the North could be worse than riding down the River Styx.” Not only will they have their own brands, but apparently NFW has renovated the North Charleston Colesium and now own two arenas separated by the Mason-Dixon line. Sullivan hinted at many surprises being involved with that, including a weekly interleague match to determine where next week’s CRASH TV will be taped. What else does NFW have in store? DirecTV wanted in, so not only does NFW broadcast a Sportscenter style highlight show weekly. But get this – All matches will be taped in entirety and available at any hour on any day if you purchase the NFW Season Pass – a channel that features every match from every forthcoming season, as well as their classic archives. How about their world renown tag team division entering their own unique fray? How about a season-ending Championship PPV? Sullivan finishes, “Our intent is to build something where wrestlers around the world recognize NFW as the only place to prove they are the BEST in the WORLD. We want this to be wrestling’s Lombardi Trophy, our playoffs to be wrestling’ March Madness. We’re the best at we do and TRUST ME, we’re only getting better.” Stay tuned folks, this could get HOT. http://fwrestling.com/host/nfw/Index.htm
