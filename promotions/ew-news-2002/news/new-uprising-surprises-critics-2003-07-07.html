NEW Uprising Surprises Critics

7 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Coming into last night's NEW Uprising event, many were predicting it to be pure anarchy... When news broke that NEW Head Booker Rufus Frost wouldn't be attending last night's NEW Uprising PPV, many outsiders quickly predicted the show's failure, despite strong buildup to the Morgana vs. Corey Ashton main event. The promotion delivered an excellent show, however, and silenced anyone who doubted their creative team. The show opened backstage, as we saw recent acquisitions, Kinkade (associate to former GWA star Jakob Grimes) and Alexander Theudis (former GWA star himself), deep in conversation. That meeting was broken up by another newcomer, Anton Rayge. Rayge was slated to face Kinkade, but would find himself without an opponent after his steel chair found Kinkade's face! Theudis scrambled, and we were then introduced to the PPV by commentators Dick Morosi and Seth Ericson. The opening match was Alexander Theudis taking on AVIREZ, a competitor who was recently seen in the IWF. Early on the match degenerated into a brawl, with AVIREZ taking it back to a wrestling contest with an Evenflow DDT that got two! Theudis turned things around and hit a hard sidewalk slam to get the pin over AVIREZ. Backstage after the match, Bobby Parks was with Angel Dawson, who would be going for the NEW International Title later that night. Jamie Williams, the champion, stuck his nose in, proclaiming himself to be superior. The two were nearly into a pre-match fist fight, but cooler heads prevailed for the moment. Next was scheduled to be Anton Rayge vs. Kinkade in a No Disqualification match, but with Kinkade out of commission, it wound up being Dark Predator, a comedy character, taking his place, before quickly falling to the Reality Check of Anton Rayge. With the closing of Ultimate Pro Wrestling, NEW's Development Territory, David Strife was quickly welcomed back to NEW itself, and found himself facing Jeff Evans, a former star who has fallen on tough times recently. A competitive match that saw Strife kick out of the impressive Astrolux finisher of Jeff Evans, finished in the favor of the returning David Strife, who hit a chokeslam and his trademark, the Big Tornado for the win! The fans were treated to a big surprise next, as NEW Legend, and former NEW World Champion Adam Cameron made an appearance, being interviewed by Katie Winter about what he's been doing since taking a hiatus, the mysterious fire that occurred at his home, and his upcoming movie, "Diablo Nights", released on August 1. However, the interview was short lived, as David Lunar and Andrew Ashton attacked the former champion, beating him down before leaving. Angel Dawson and Jamie Williams were up next in the International Title Match, and went at it in a heated battle! Angel Dawson ended up winning, using her patented Angel Breath submission maneuver for the win and the title! Up next was scheduled to be "Semi-Late Night with Corey Ashton", but before Ashton could say anything about tonight's main event, Morgana came out to chase him away, pleasing the fans. Adora faced Andrew Ashton next, but it was a short affair, with Ashton winning after his Lights Out superkick. After the match, he and David Lunar were chastized backstage Rufus Frost in an obviously pre-taped segment, citing the previous match and the news that Frost wouldn't be at the event as the reasoning. In possibly the match of the night, David Lunar took on Kyle Rayner in a two out of three falls match! The first fall was littered with mat wrestling between these two hated stars, but Rayner seemed to gain a small amount of fans after his Starlight Extinction won the first fall of the match. Seconds later, David Lunar his his Lunar Eclipse superkick, but only got two! However, the kick was enough to stun Rayner into a position for Lunar to hook in his Lunar Death Lock, and procure the second fall, tying the score at 1-1! Rayner hit the Starlight Extinction, but refused to go for the cover, but instead hooked on the Sharpshooter, in the center of the ring! The referee checked on Lunar, and tested to see if he was unconcious. Lunar was far from it, however, and made it to the ropes. Quickly after, he hit the Lunar Eclipse to get the three and the win. Backstage, NEW National Champion Jimmy Jackson interjected himself in the proceedings, to the joy of the fans. He belittled his former promotion, the Gladiator Wrestling Association, but did announce that he would be representing them in the upcoming Reign of Power tournament, which NEW is also going to be a part of. Earlier today, when brackets were released, in groups of six, it was found that Jackson would be in the same group with former NEW Champion and NEW Legend Andrew Ashton! However, he also talked about his "controversial" win over Corey Ashton two weeks ago for the NEW National Title. All he would say when questioned about the World Title Match was that he didn't care who was World Champion, because he was better than both of them! That brought this PPV to our main event, Corey Ashton and Morgana for the NEW World Title. Ashton basically got destroyed in the early part of the match, but used mind games to get the upper hand on the champion. He used every trick he could think of to psyche Morgana out. After the referee went down, Corey used a steel chair to gain the advantage, and bust Morgana open! However, when he went for the finishing hit with the chair, Morgana dropkicked it back into his face, and went up, hitting the Morgasm, her trademark 450 Splash, for the pin and the title! After the match, Lunar and Andrew Ashton charged the ring again, and attacked her, until Adam Cameron came charging out, hitting the Depth Charge on Ashton, and his high impact finisher, the Decline on Lunar, saving the champion! The PPV ended with David Lunar and Andrew Ashton at the entryway, and Cameron and Morgana in the ring. New Era Wrestling
