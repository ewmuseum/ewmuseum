Wrestling - The truth hurts.

14 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You know so many have labelled me as a rude mouthed son of a bitch. Why? Because all of you can’t take the goddamn truth like a man. How pathetic. You know so many have labelled me as a rude mouthed son of a bitch. Why? Because all of you can’t take the goddamn truth like a man. How pathetic. Everywhere I go people just cannot stand the truth. What do they want to hear? The truth or a lie that just hides the sad fact that they cannot stand the truth. Everyone goes around asking, “please tell me the truth”, but if you do it they piss off crying to someone. Just because I have been branded a rude mouthed s.o.b they can only sit back and curse me off the streets. If I told women her hat sucked she would cry on the spot, and you would get the eye from the old people who are always looking to say something, because they cannot keep their mouths shut. It’s bloody annoying! You see the truth decides how strong you really are. I couldn’t give a shit if you were as built as Kodiak Vic Creed, or as skinny as those 15 year old girls who think they are fat; these people still as weak as everyone else unless they can stand up, and take the truth like a *real* man while not having the idea of giving you the middle finger. Ever say to the girl: “Your too damn skinny bitch, eat some food and get fat like yer mom”, and she’ll fire a display of whining and waterworks at you. Why? Because she cannot stand the truth. At the end of it you’re always asking yourself “What did she want to hear from me?” Lets face it, everyone on this planet live in a basket of lies, always hearing what they want to hear. It’s the same for this pathetic sport. Wrestling is a mouse’s sport, not man’s sport. If you can’t stand the truth then you aren’t fit for this sport, and isn’t that the truth! And isn’t it ironic that nearly every superstar that sits in a federation cannot stand the truth, because if they hear the facts, they only reply with a chorus of lies back at you. PRIME, is just one candidate of many federations that are not fit to be in this sport. Everyone in the backstage staff cannot stand the truth. Ever wonder why Sebastian Starr decided to shove me in a gauntlet of matches week after week? Because he couldn’t take the truth like the rest of his bitches. So what if I exposed it in public, if he was a man he would have confronted me personally about it, instead of cowering on holiday and putting me in a match, not even bothering to explain his actions. Starr like the rest couldn’t admit that he sucked in bed, and sucks at owning a federation. Talking about PRIME, I look around at every one of the backstage and wonder why the hell I am still sitting in the dump, listening to this crap. Starr’s bitches of the week Michael Cade and Jacob Cross wonder why they are not being pushed for the world title. I’ll explain why: because they fucking suck. They like Starr just do not understand the truth. They suck, but yet they cannot sit up and take it like men. Now I could explain that these two are just a growing trend in homosexuals, who are ashamed of their lifestyle, but the fact is they are not. When two lovers try to do things together it never works, it fails because they’re always trying to jam each other’s fingers up each other’s ass. Disgusting it may be, but while these two run around with Rock Startling trying to trample on the entire PRIME roster, they keep forgetting that you need some intelligence and talent to do that. I have a gut feeling that Rock Startling wonders why he holds them on his shoulders week after week, because Cade and Cross are just pathetic. But yet if you were to tell them the truth, they would just suck each other’s thumbs and react like the rest of you morons out there; give you the middle finger and try and kick your ass. Grow up people, we are here and we are different. But if you can’t stand the truth, then don’t try to cover it up, live with it, and let it be. Just don’t be like Cross and Cade. This has been a [name withheld] rant. Till next time. PRIME - Where the truth hurts,
