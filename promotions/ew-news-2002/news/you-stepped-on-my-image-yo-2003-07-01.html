You stepped on my image, yo

1 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Don't worry, I couldn't possibly offer this kind of insight on a weekly basis. Alright, so we've seen what Mick P. had to say...we've seen Spyder speak, we've even heard from people who don't deserve to breathe - no less write - in Rent-A-Hero... We've proven time and time again that wrestlers really shouldn't be allowed to publicly speak, because nothing halfway intelligent ever comes of it. We've proven that the only person who actually deserves a spot on the little eWN columns page is Ivy McGinnis...so why in the name of God am I here? Trying - and most likely failing - to save face and prove that there's at least one person in this ridiculous business with a brain. And, quite obviously, I'm not talking about Vince McMahon. Quite obviously, I'm not even going NEAR the unlit ballpark of a certain Dice God. Even with those glaring examples of idiocy, coupled with the indy-pushing dumbells who post here about why they're the best and yadda yadda yadda, there actually is a pocket of brain cells in wrestling today. Sure, you have to dig pretty deep...but it exists, I swear! So who's Aimz to talk intelligence, right? She's the fWo Hardcore Champion...she beats people (And herself) up on a bi-weekly basis using weapons and physical pretzels of any sort. She has a terribly affliction in her love of drinking away any issue that passes. She's publicly made a fool of herself thanks to substance abuse on various occasions. She's got a temper fit to triple any redhead, and all you have to do is look at her the wrong way to see an example of that, or one of many pent up rants. Hell, she's pissing Kellen Kinkade and Vic Creed off almost constantly nowadays - She really isn't any great example of intelligence in wrestling. But she also doesn't like wrestling - not even the slightest bit. It's not the stupidity behind kicking the shit out of yourself almost every day that gets me...it's the gimmicks. The exact same reason there's a lot of mainstream pop and rap music I don't like - Too many gimmicks. There's where the brains get wasted in this business. Here's an example: You've got Spyder, so caught up in his "I don't care" attitude that he just banters off ridiculous bullshit constantly, thinking it'll make him look like more of a vigilante. Yeah, he's distanced himself from the fWo roster in doing so - there's one pointless goal accomplished. Bravo. What else does it do for him, though? It doesn't make him happy, that much is obvious...the little guy's always angry and going off about this, that and the other thing. What purpose does that serve him? It puts him in a constant verbal state of roundabout. Strike all of that, now reverse it...Ivy McGinnis. There's no gimmick to Ivy - She is what she feels. If she's pissed off, eWNews is probably the first to hear about it. If she's happy, same deal. Through her running on whatever she feels, we've been able to read some of the most insightful looks into this ridiculous race - Things good enough to make twats like Dave Meltzer and Rent-A-Hero want to slit their wrists.......well, hopefully. Gimmicks impair so much in wrestling. I'm not talking about the fluffy show you see on TV, where someone could call themselves the greatest wrestler ever, win two matches in their entire career and still be believable. I'm talking about the general stupidity when you walk away from that show...the people who let their gimmicks carry on both when there's a camera in front of them and when there's nothing but reality in their faces. The kind of people who ruin the backstage environments for everybody else. They do nothing as far as contribution, though they might think going out on TV makes them worth something...it doesn't. I'm not just talking about the guys who think they need to act 'macho' and shoulder girls out of everything they possibly can based on gener - No, because this isn't a rant about 'Woman power' or whatever bullshit Jewel's trying to feed you with her latest gimmick reinvention. Gimmicky people do everything one way and one way only, and they have no idea what they're even talking about half the time...it's all just to progress the image, and that's the only thing driving them to care - What they look like. Vanity and ego's always been the downfall; It's an example throughout the ages in nations, businessmen, rulers, politicians, policemen...every single one of us has been a victim of our own self-obsession sometimes, whether it's caused by insecurity or actually honestly thinking we have to be a certain way to impress people. Everybody who's ever complained of being 'held back' in the locker room needs to take a serious inventory of their attitude...nine times out of ten, you've got a gimmick that's keeping you from acting certain ways. Take a look at guys who think they need to play up their tough image - Do they get wives and actual loving relationships in life? No. Are they ever happy? No! Their image doesn't allow them to be. That's not the only type of gimmick there is, though. Take a look at the 'soft, artsy type', for instance...they end up spending their lives complaining and getting their asses handed to them. Why? Because they won't stand up and defend themselves - That'd be a breach of character. Gimmicks are rooted in insecurity. You need to fit in with a certain crowd or mentality and never escape it. You either act like a prick to everybody and try to start fights or act unnaturally nice to an absolutely sickening, almost immature point (Yes, I have a wonderful boyfriend in Brand Frontier who frequently falls victim to this - But YOU try scolding the big oaf.) without ever sticking up for yourself if the situation calls for it. Either way, there's always a side you're going to be missing and a whole pile of friends you aren't making. There'll be countless things you can't do or say because it would be out of the ordinary, you wouldn't impress the crowd your gimmick is tailored for... It'll bite you in the ass one way or another - So why not take away this ridiculous simplicity and be yourself, instead of hiding behind a façade that holds you back FAR more than it'll ever help you? What's the point to an image if it's not what you feel? You'll never be happy that way, and there'll always be a near-majority you're not pleasing. Yeah, you're reading this and saying "Pfft. Fuck 'em, fuck this bitch...she's full of shit, I don't need anyboy."...but the truth is right there - you do. You act like you don't care, you might've even convinced yourself that you don't care but the fact behind a one-way attitude, a gimmick, is that you really are just trying to fit in with a certain crowd no matter how big or small that may be and there's absolutely no good God damned point to it. So I'm a punk rocker, right? That'd probably be my 'gimmick', if anything. But there it goes, straight out the window: I'm about to quote a disturbingly mainstream nu-metal band... "Bold, motherfucker - Don't you limit your mind." Be yourself, you don't have to please everybody...but you want to be happy - it's human nature. Run with it, you'll be capable of doing a whole hell of a lot more that way, instead of being the 'big heel' or the 'whiney face'. It'll make our lives backstage a whole lot happier... But hey, do what you want - you're only holding yourself back, and that's not my problem as long as you don't tell me about me. Uhh...this is Amy? fWo - Fans Wrestling Organization
