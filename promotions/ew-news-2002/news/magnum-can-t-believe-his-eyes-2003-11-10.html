Magnum can't believe his eyes!

10 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sources close to former PWC and FSW superstar Magnum Wolf were happy to find out that he was not, in fact, dead. [name withheld], better known as former wrestling superstar Magnum Wolf, made himself suddenly VERY available for comment on a situation that has come to his attention. Calling from a number that, after we traced it, we found out was from [detail withheld], [name withheld] was livid at the discovery of Karina Wolfenden on the television show of RUSH Pro Wrestling. Making it's debut last night, RPW crowned Wolfenden their Eclipse Champion last night. [name withheld] has a very storied history with Karina in and out of the ring. According to [name withheld], "We know each other well, if ya know what I mean... wink wink nudge nudge..." The two created possibly the best television for Ron Starr's former project Pro Wrestling Circuit. Whether that television was a product of a true animosity between the two, or just good angle writing by the team at PWC is unclear. According to [name withheld], however, the hatred is all true and honest. "That useless bitch can win a title in RPW?! Did these people even WATCH PWC? I mean, if she can be making millions and winning titles in a promotion like that, why am I sitting here in this piece of shit ho--- errrrrr... I mean, why am I on hiatus?!" After answering a few questions we threw at him with stupid answers that don't really seem to make any sense, he told us that he was sure he would be at RPW's next show. One way or another. What this means for Rush Mason's new project, we don't know, but you can bet it can't mean very many good things. We'll keep you updated as this story develops. http://rpw.sheepsden.com
