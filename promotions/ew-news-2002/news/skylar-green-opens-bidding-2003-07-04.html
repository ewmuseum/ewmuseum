Skylar Green Opens Bidding

4 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Every other day, a free agent comes along and demands large sums of money for their services. This is yet anotherone of those times. Skylar Green issued a press statement from his new home in Hollywood, California, where he purchased a three-story office building from Bank of America. The building was formerly under lease from the now-defunct Primetime Championship Wrestling federation. Skylar, who lives there with his three adopted daughters, has been out of work for only a little over a week now, but he's already seeking out new employment. He sent this statement to the Associated Press earlier today. "As most of you know, PCW is now closed, as well as PWC. The WO organization is collapsed. It's basically the end of an era in my own career, as I'd been in that region for two years, and it had come to be my home. But, things happen, things change, and it's time to move on. "I'm qualified for other occupations, but wrestling is what I'm best at, and what I would like to do until I decide for myself to leave the sports industry. But since I don't really know what kind of federations are out there, I open the floor to free agency bidding. If there are any federations that wish to procure my services, you must bid the most money. "However, I don't have much of a need for it myself, I've made enough money over the past few years to live comfortably on a non-extravagant lifestyle. Therefore, the bidding will not be a gauge of my salary; the bidding will be be a declaration of that federation that they will donate so much money to charity to have me work for them. "The donations need to be made out in the name of the company, must be paid in full before I agree to work for that company, and the donations may only be made to a charity that I approve of. Your best bets are: The Make-A-Wish Foundation, The American Cancer Society, American Diabetes Association, or the American Indian College Fund. "After any bid is entered, another federation has 24 hours to enter a counter bid. The bidding starts at five-thousand dollars and counter-bids can only be made in multiples of one-thousand dollars. "I hope that a lot of wrestling companies will take advantage of this chance to do a good deed." Skylar is known to be a rather passive worker, one who lets the opportunities come to him, so to declare his free agency like this is somewhat uncharacteristic. Then again, it's not a surprise that he would waive a large salary. An advertising contract from Fanta guarantees that he clears at least $500k a year, and anyone who knows Skylar knows that he doesn't live a high-income lifestyle. The table has been set, and the bidding is open. Any company that wants Skylar's services is free to enter a bid. The terms of the bidding actually eliminates big companies that pride themselves on a violent, uncaring image. The playing field is a little more level in some ways. We'll see how this turns out.
