Action!: Joey Malone retires at Juggernaut II.

2 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In the aftermath of a stellar pay-per-view, IWO and Action! Hall of Famer and two-time World Champion finds himself without a job. Action! Wrestling Juggernaut II has come and passed, and admist stellar contests between KSZ and Sweet Aroma, Max Danger and Conrad Ramsey, Coral Avalon and Jeff Garvin, and LLB and Christopher Fox, there was another match that ended with one consequence, and that was the Joey Malone and Daniel Phillips co-main event. The contest, a career match between the two former tag team partners, ended with Phillips pinning Malone, thus ending his career. Some may ask if Joey Malone will return at some moment, but the former Action! Champion had this to say about those questions, "In these last few months, I had been going through some difficult times, and wrestling was at the core of every problem I've had. I've put my family in difficult times, especially when I elected to work some dates for 21st Century Wrestling and even the fans Wrestling organization last year. Having accomplished everything I ever wanted to accomplish in this industry, and even some things I didn't intend to accomplish, I can safely say that my retirement is permanent. If I returned, I'd be making what I had worked so hard to get done in my career into a mockery. Plus, my knee's been a mess for a while, which is the reason why I hadn't wrestled that much leading up to the pay-per-view." When asked about Daniel Phillips, Joey Malone had this to say, "Daniel was legitimately upset about what had happened to him and where I went after our team broke up back in 2000. He managed to convey his venom towards me in IWO and Asylum, but when he got to Action, we had managed to talk through his problems with me, which is when I shot him the idea to end my career at Juggernaut." On the subject of his rivalry with Keith Scott Zimmerman, "Keith was the one who started saying stuff about me. I gave him the first match with me to show him that I'm a sport, and carried him to a decent showing. The thing is that Keith works by a strict amount of plans, while I like to improvise, and I bled hardway in our first match as a result of Keith's stiffing the living hell out of me. And, uh, well, more of the same the second time around, except that I put him over just to shut him up." On the subject of his family, "My wife's been pregnant for the last five or so months, now, and she was the main reason for my retirement. I didn't want to be halfway across the world while my child was getting born, and I felt like I owed Keri a lot for standing by me through everything, even despite the Anthason storyline. My dad died last month, and I got left with my fifteen-year-old little sister to take care of, and between her and my future child, I know I'd have my hands full." On the subject of his future, Joey had this to say, "Reed still has to pay me for the other two years on my contract. While Reed and I agreed that I shouldn't wrestle any more, I can say with certainty that Juggernaut will not be the last time you'll hear from me. I just want to get my family stuff in order before I start making guest appearances. As for what I want to do now that I'm no longer a wrestler, it's all up in the air." Action!
