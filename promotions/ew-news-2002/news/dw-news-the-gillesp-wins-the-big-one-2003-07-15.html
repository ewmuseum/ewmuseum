DW News: The Gillesp Wins the Big One!!

15 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
New World Champion Crowned on Monday Night Destruction #79 The 79th Dynasty Wrestling's Monday Night Destruction went out with a bang as a New World Champion was crowned in a controversal triple threat clash with new owner Mike Vitucci as special guest referee. Not only is a new face at the top of the DW Mountain, but 2 former World Champions returned and certainly made their presence felt. With DW still on the rise after 3 successful years, everyone is excited as the anticipation builds up towards the biggest Dynasty event of the year Global Takeover 2. 2 New matches were signed for GT2, and even more emphasis put on the much hyped triple tier cage match between the two most successful DW Champions of all time - Havoc and Shawn Storm. Global Takeover 2 Set to mark the end of Shawn Storms illustrious career with a place in the DW Hall of Fame within his reach. Will he reach DW's highest honour or will he fall short at the last hurdle? Monday Night was a night to be remembered and there definately will be Hell to Pay. Is this man the right champion to take Dynasty Wrestling forward into this new era under the reign of Mike Vitucci? Is it time for the World Championship Curse to be broken? Only time will tell, but one things for sure, no matter what happens it's going to be one hell of a ride. Dynasty Wrestling
