Tom Mason Agrees to Exclusive Interview

8 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He is known not for his successes over the past few years, but more for his ego. Tom Mason may own Real and Graphic Entertainment but in his mind he owns a lot more. For the first time since last year's company troubles, Tom Mason will sit down in an exclusive interview and answer any and all questions that are posed. The leading man behind Real and Graphic Entertainment just agreed the other day to make an exclusive interview with SE-Live.com following New Dawn. This may not look like it is big news, but the fact that the Owner has decided to talk about anything and everything asked in the interview, it looks like it could be a promising encounter. A lot has gone on in the past and things are still developing now as the future is being shaped, and one can only imagine the sorts of questions that one, Michael Dennis, will present to Tom Mason in this exclusive interview. This Monday night, following New Dawn, the gloves come off and Tom Mason will freely speak on many topics that are in question these days. One of the most important: the current company status of Real and Graphic Entertainment. No one can be sure what will come out of New Dawn on Friday, but the company seems extremely hopeful. So, when Tom Mason sits down for this interview, a lot of the focus will be on the outcome of the show. Everyone will be wondering not only what happened if they did not see it, but they will be wondering exactly how the company faired financially and ratings wise. There is nothing Tom Mason has ever done outside of a ring that will create this much stress. As he takes the proverbial gloves off and sits down for this interview, it cannot be emphasized enough just how interesting things could get. Michael Dennis does not risk being fired for this interview and it definitely takes a burden off of him for that, knowing well of the short temper of the boss. When these two sit literally feet away, Dennis could hold nothing back and drop some of the most difficult or even personal questions on Tom Mason and he has contractually agreed to answer them. That will be this Monday, January 12, 2004 and it will be exclusively available at the official Real and Graphic Entertainment host's website: SE-Live.com! Real and Graphic Entertainment
