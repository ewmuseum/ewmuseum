[IWF] Sunday Night Lightning takes over Hawaii!

14 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
For the first time in IWF History, Lightning airs live this sunday night from Honolulu, Hawaii! After the Icon Wrestling Federation blew the roof off of the HSBC Arena in Buffalo, New York last week with IWF Sunday Night Lightning, the IWF's flagship show will now be taken to Aloha Stadium in Honolulu, Hawaii. IWF Veteran, John Mchugh was available to comment. "This will defintely be an exciting experience for me and the rest of the guys. We've never brodcasted a live show from Hawaii before so hopefully this will be the first of many more visits." The show will only be seven days before the IWF's next Pay Per View Event, "Survivor" so it's a guarantee that there will great action and a load of surprises in store for the fans. Since Hardcore Criminal's return to the IWF after nearly a two year absence, he has not been able to come out of the ring with a victory yet. Hardcore Criminal will look to get that win this week in Honolulu, Hawaii when he faces off against "The Messiah" Chris Christ. "The Messiah" will be looking to rebound this week after losing to Matty Midget two weeks ago so he will be looking for a victory in this match as well. Both Chris Christ and Hardcore Criminal have alot to prove this week but only one of them will walk out successful while the other will have their losing streak extended for another week. Shortly after last week's Lightning went off the air in Buffalo, the boss, John "The Iceman" Lugo booked himself in a match in which he will be teaming up with his long-time ally, Ace to face off against his brother, John Mchugh and a partner of Mchugh's choosing. Mchugh has been a marked man as of late with Ace and Lugo on his tail but this week, Mchugh will be able to choose a partner to even up the odds. There's been alot of rumors floating around about who Mchugh's partner is going to be but we won't know for sure until this sunday night! Also, in singles action, IWF Legend, John Daniels will make his in-ring return to face Miss Phoenix. Miss Phoenix has made a name for herself wrestling and defeating the men of the Icon Wrestling Federation but her critics are questioning whether she will be able to contend with Daniels. There have also been questions about John's physical condition after his absence from the ring. Some fear that John Daniels may be entering the ring too soon. That will not be the only match this week that will pit a man against a woman but unlike the other match, the next match will pit a woman against a MIDGET! The "Diva of Darkness" Blaise Martelle will take on the three foot, seven inch anarchist......Matty Midget in singles action. Blaise Martelle and Matty Midget have both fought tremendous odds throughout their careers with the both of them being former World Champions........so it should be rather interesting to see how each of them will fare against one another. You also have to wonder whether John Daniels will have something to do with this match, Matty Midget having had classic wars with Daniels....and Blaise Martelle being Daniels' ex-girlfriend, they will both have a bullseye on their chest this week in Hawaii. And this week's Main Event will bring together possibly two of the greatest wrestlers of all-time, Chazz Mendel and Jason Drake as they take on one of the greatest tag teams of all-time, Mr. Rich and Marco. This match came about last week when the boss, John Lugo came out to prove his superiority against the man who was appointed as Presidential Advisor, Jason Drake. Lugo's intentions were to book Drake in a handicap match against the team of Mr. Rich and Marco. However, Jason Drake was able to work out a deal with Lugo in which Drake would be allowed to choose a tag team partner for the match. The announcement prompted the World Heavyweight Champion, Chazz Mendel to come out and accept the invitation laid out by Jason Drake. Having defied him last week, will John Lugo try to have the last laugh this week and screw the team of Chazz Mendel and Jason Drake out of a win? Or will Jason Drake and Chazz Mendel be able to rise to the challenge and defeat the team of Mr. Rich and Marco? Expect all of this and so much more, this sunday night on IWF Sunday Night Lightning which will once again be live from Aloha Stadium in Honolulu, Hawaii. Be sure to watch the show at 8:00 PM Eastern Time on the TNT NETWORK! Icon Wrestling Federation
