PRIME In Turmoil?

14 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, what do you expect when one half of the company is owned by a cat!? MORE... The ramifications of the Point Of Impact event are still being felt through the company. After the show last Sunday Toshiaki Motoki staged a crisis meeting to try and establish some sort of order with his new partner in power, Mr Bojangles. Taun Pham of course was present as Bojangles personal assistant whilst Hin See, Greg Manix and other senior members of the company also attended as the group tried to thrash out some terms to create a common ground in the promotion. The meeting however broke down after nearly two hours and since then there have been no confirmed attempts to renegotiate. Refusing to comment to reporters, Toshiaki Motoki has been in training all week in order to prefer for his much unwanted encounter with The Eternal Punk Shane Wethers. The pair lock up in a Hell In A Cell Match which was requested by Wethers LIVE at the PPV as he made his return. Wethers is fighting for answers as he looks to finally unravel the mystery behind the ordeal endured by the Eternal Punk over the last eight weeks. Usually when a promoter tops the bill of a wrestling program, then the fans switch off, but on the contrary this weeks ReVolution is expected to be one of the most watched ever. The Target Center has already sold out with fans queuing through the early hours of the morning in order to get tickets. The general concensus is that people simply can't wait to see Motoki get whats coming to him as his dramatic fall from grace continues. Meanwhile having fought himself at the PPV, Hin See is expected to miss the shows while he recovers from the effects of the Roloplex he was given last weekend in his DQ loss to Tony Rolo. In other news, all eyes will be on Ian English this week to see how he responds to losing his 5-Star Title to Joey Troy. Englishs 5-Star Title reign was the longest title reign in PRIME history and many expect the limey to aim straight for Troy once more in order to reclaim his throne as the 5-Star Tsar. Staying on the title front, Lance Jackson is due to appear at ReVolution despite not having a match. It's thought his omission from the card was due to a minor concussion he suffered from a spot in last weekends Global Title match where Jacksons head was slammed into part of the entrance set. Finally, the rumor has been confirmed. Mike Wade is leaving PRIME. The charasmatic 23 year old Irish man has been rumoured to be leaving since a backstage rift was made before Point Of Impact. Wade was unhappy with the direction of his character and felt he was being made a fool of. Wade has signed with rival company Primetime Championship Wrestling and was even featured on one of their shows last week. This Monday on ReVolution will see his last match against Driver. Surely this is not a Rev to be missed as many of the Wade Parade will show up in support of their hero looking for one last glimpse before he goes in search of greener pastures in PCW. PRIME
