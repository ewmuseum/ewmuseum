Why the internet isn't paying enough respect to Spyder

11 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Esse, we have a problem. I am your fWo Internet Champion, the most important wrestler in the entire world but when I look around at other promotions and the way idiots hype up nothing and talk about stupid gringos I wonder if the whole world has gone loco, or if Spyder just needs to slap the hell out of them all. Your people's champion Hello my loyal internet readers, Several weeks have now passed since I became your fWo Internet Champion. Already we are seeing the results, hommes. Internet traffic is up according to Network Solutions and even companies like AOL, Earthlink and MSN have talked about an exceptional increase in subscribers. What can we attribute to this growth of the internet? I think all know. It is the Spyder, and the fWo Internet Champion that I hold. I represent the internet, so of course it makes sense that all these grigos would be flocking online to try and get the rub from the most charismatic and exciting luchador the wrestling world has ever known. This is not a surprise to me, nor should it be a surprise to you. Every week I get e-mails, letters, faxes and plenty of panties from drooling women who just want to play slippery slide with the masked dragon and his long, long tail. I do not brag esse, this is just facts. Yet still there are problems. What you say? ¿Problemas para el luchador más asombroso de la historia de toda la lucha? ¿Problemas para un dios, hombre tal como esto? ¡No puedo creerla! But no esse, it is true. Problems do exist. When I turn on the television and watch some of the competitors, and I use that term loosely, I do not see the announcers talking about Spyder. Why is this? In the Asylum for example I see lots of the usual punching, raping and cursing but are there any discussions or running text scrolls about Spyder's Internet Title reign hommes? ¿No, y porqué es éste? That Campbell is a piece of mierda, esse, who doesn't understand the business. He has all this dirt on people? You have no dirt on Spyder esse, you're nothing. So I want to see my name, Spyder, on your next show or I'm going to show up and kick some ass on my own, you hear me hommes? Same goes for you Action! people. Your champion is pathetic esse, at least spare the viewers some pain and show them a real champion, even if he isn't on your crummy little show. But this is not the only problem esse. There is also the problem of the internet itself. Stand back! ¿El Internet? ¡Pero Spyder que usted dice, Spyder, usted es el campeón del Internet del fWo! ¿Cómo puede haber problema con el Internet? It's true. This Monday on that Roaring show that they should have called Monday Night Spyder and Friends, I canceled the main event. Yes esse, that's the power I have. I was scheduled to wrestle the Scotty Collins but after reading some of the comments on the internet I had no choice but to punish the fans for their ignorance and stupidity. I go to one message board and all they talk about is kissing this one idiot's ass and spreading this gay glitter all over everything. That's not even the internet esse, that's a joke. Then I go to another message board and this time at least there is conversation, but it isn't intellegent because they talk about wrestling's best heels and where is Spyder? At least one Spyderite understood and paid homage to the greatest luchador in the business, but by and large these people are talking nonsense, esse. This can't be the internet I represent. Not with such stupid people. So I did some thinking. ¿Qué debo hacer? ¿Después de que consiga hecho teniendo sexo con todas las madres de esta gente para el curso, y después de que consigo hecho teniendo sexo con todas las hermanas de esta gente también, entonces qué? ¿Qué debo hacer? El Internet, el lugar que represento se llena de los idiotas que no están pagando respecto apropiado al hombre que es su dios. It is a problem esse, and one that I need your help with. So I want to see those message boards filled with suggestions on how to teach these idiots a lesson and make sure that the proper respect is being paid to the God of lucha libre and the man who represents all of the wrestling internet. Help Spyder, and maybe I'll only sleep with your sister one time rather than make her a complete whore who can't live without the Spyder. A lot of good topics today esse, but this is no surprise to all of you. You're already down with what I'm saying. So until next time, you tell that woman Poison Ivy to get back in the kitchen and make Spyder some pie rather than running her mouth, and I'll keep doing what I do best... which is everything esse. Homenaje de la paga al rey... Spyder. Future home of Spyder on the web
