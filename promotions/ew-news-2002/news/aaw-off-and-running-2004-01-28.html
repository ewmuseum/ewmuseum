AAW Off and Running In a teleconference, held earlier today, AAW President of Media Relations, George Carver, announced that the AAW is officially online. ‘The graphics team at Pedi Enterprise has been working round the clock to get this thing going.” He went on to say, “The major parts are in place. Our lifeline, the forums are up and running. The application is working nicely…we have some good things working here and I for one am excited. These energized words come off a rough week where nothing seemed to be going right. Minority Owners Dutch Felner and Roger Herman where set to meet in New York City earlier this week, but due to inclement weather Felner’s flight to the Big Apple was delayed. The two were set to unveil numerous details about the AAW’s flagship show Friday Night Slam Masters. The two are sitting on a wealth of ideas that the general public is salivating to get at. With things falling into place it may be wise for the duo to stay tight lipped and build off this early buzz. - Hoss Gotfried http://aaw.fedhead.com
