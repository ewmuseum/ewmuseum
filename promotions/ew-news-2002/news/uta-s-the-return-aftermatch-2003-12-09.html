UTA's "The Return" Aftermatch

9 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The opionon of UTA reporter Scheme Gene following up after the highly anticipated and viewed UTA PPV "The Return." Well, Hello, everyone. Scheme Gene here. Well you can probably guess that this week has been nothing less than abyssmal for yours truly. While the majority of the PPV was satisfying, by the end I was left with a hole the size of D**k's stupidity in my stomach. That same one word question keeps popping into my head again and again- WHY? That's the same question that some backstage have asked themselves as well- WHY? The problem is that with most everyone wanting to know the answer, the problem is that right now there IS NO answer to that question. For those still in the d**k, that question revolves around D**k winning the World Title. EXACTLY! See? You're asking yourself that question, too. Scheme Gene finds it rather sh*tty to throw the title around the waist of some screwball who purposely makes an ass out of himself every time he opens his mouth. You think Hannibal Lecter could let the UTA borrow his headgear to shut D**k up? I was left appalled and disgusted that GOLIATH got the shaft, and Dark got the title, and a bigger payoff- NEITHER of which he deserved. GOLIATH busted his ass last Sunday, was involved heavily in the Spectre/JT Titan match, proved his dominance throughout the night, PROVED he is Main Event material, PROVED he is like a Timex watch where he takes a licking and keeps on ticking, PROVED he should be the World Chapion right now! What did D**k prove??? .... Exactly! His performance, or LACK thereof, on Sunday didn't change my opinion of him. He is still overrated, still an idiot, and yes, STILL being pushed. D**k deserves the title? What did he do? Oh! He did a couple of moves on Kaution, got a BYE to the final round, took advantage of a guy who, by all acounts shouldn't have even been wrestling in the final round due to the severity of his injuries, and the UTA World Title was GIFT-WRAPPED to him. Hell! Jason Blackfront TOLD D**k to leave the cage, so that shows who's behind D**k's push. I'm sure some will point out D**k did as the boss said, and even showed concern after the match was over due to Goliath's condition. TOKEN! Token work. It was all an act by BOTH Blackfront AND D**k. But how do you know it was act, you may ask? Since WHEN did Blackfront either not be kissing up to Peterson's ass, or not concerned about his sub-par looks? And D**k has been piledrived on his head too many times to have any form of compassion for another human being. Goliath is still recovering from injuries, and is pretty down that he didn't win the title. But rest assured he will have his sights set on D**k as soon as he can. In the meantime, fans will have the honor and pleasure of dealing with Jason Steele Version 2.0 as World Champion! What a way to kick off THE RETURN? Ron Hall tries to get into the arena, and his car is totally wrecked by The Spectre who may or may not have struck Ron Hall on purpose. Most have their doubts that it was an accident, given the history between the Southern Rebel and The Spectre, and honestly so do I. BUt if this is the tip of the iceberg to a future feud, then ladies and gentlemen, hold on to your hats! Sebastian Bush no-showed for the PPV which got me to thinking...didnt he do this before in a previous UTA stint? Most mackstage were not too thrilled about Crimson Lord being eliminated in the first round of the tournament as they felt the loss hurt Crimson's image. However, Crimson Lord was gracious about his loss, and commented later that it didn't bother him about the loss, and that he was just out there to put on a good show, and put Ironside over on his last night with the company, while still showing everyone that he has the potential to be a ruthless individual in the UTA. How about the return of the Hell Razors led by G-Man? We kinda knew SOMETHING was up for the past couple of weeks with these "mysterious" conversations with faceless people, and secret meetings as well, but didn't figure on this. G-Man suprised a lot of people with that sudden move. It looks like G-Man is positioning himself to be a major factor in UTA before any OFFICIAL documents to join the UTA are signed. One very intersting move he made was to bring Kevin Hawk into the fold. Perhaps G-Man saw something in Kevin that made him give the green light to bringing Kevin in as a member of the HELL RAZORS. They really did a number on Klash AKA Seth Payne FKA Jace Hardy, and that attack in itself will surely lead to an eventual fued between Klash and Kevin Hawk, who used to be tag team partners. This feud could really be something over the next couple of months with the culmination being at the next PPV. This could be the make or break point for either of these guys. HELL RAZORS was the first established stable of the night, but it would not be the last. THE SPAWN made it's return during what has to be one of the best matches in UTA history. JT Titan and Spectre are the ones who deserve that bonus for putting out a performance like the one we saw during that hellacious Barbed Wire Glass Table Last Man Standing match. Blood almost came out in buckets. As expected, it was brutal on both men, even the 3rd party GOLIATH in JT's corner. JT was shocked and taken off guard when Spectre's demeanor turned into a demented freak once that bell rang, and fought tooth and nail (and minus part of his ear) to the end. There were a couple of surprises though during this match: One, when Brian Ironside came down, initially we thought it was to get revenge on his loss to Goliath in the 2nd round tournament, but it was later revealed that Brian Ironside was not only the mysterious "Dear Robin Isni" that Spectre was referring to, but that Brian Ironside was to be the newest Spawn member. Two, the strange "lady" in the audience was not only a swerve to make everyone think it was "Dear Robin Isni", it turned out to be not a lady at all, but Spectre's long time friend, Devon Lynch. Once Devon Lynch got physically involved in the match, the lights went out for JT Titan as Spectre used that "free reign" rule to knock JT out with chloroform. Being familiar with both JT's and Devon's style and career, I can see this as another PPV matchup at the next PPV. Finally, one of the most touching and emotional moments came when Brian Ironside was given a sendoff from of all people- Spectre. Anybody that knows Spectre knows that this guy usually wears his emotions on his sleeve, and isn't one to break "character" in front of the fans. But break it he did as he gave Brian Ironside his just due, and paid respect to the man that many say was underutilized and possibly one of the greatest wrestlers to never have a real chance to reign as World Champion. Spectre and Devon Lynch gave Ironside an "Honorary Spawn Member" T-Shirt, but the real honor Ironside received was when Spectre pulled out the 2002 version of the UTA World Title and placed around Ironside's waist. Truly an emotional moment for just about everyone, including yours truly. I've known Brian a long time, and I will truly miss his presence. United Toughness Alliance
