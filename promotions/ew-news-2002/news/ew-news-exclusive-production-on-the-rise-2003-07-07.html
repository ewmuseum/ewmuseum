EW-News Exclusive: Production on the Rise

7 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Many companies are faltering; others are heading into a spiraling downfall. It appears that Real and Graphic Entertainment is on its way to just the opposite, a glorious future... It has been exactly seven days since Real and Graphic Entertainment re-entered itself on the market after a one-month break. Already, the company has produced two shows on the Fox Network and a third is coming this Wednesday night. Ratings have been high with the July 1 show scoring a 3.6; and the July 5 show scoring a 3.7, two extremely high and pleasing ratings for the company. As the production line will even out into its standard schedule this week, with shows on Wednesday and Sunday nights, a total of three hours of television time on the Fox Network in all, Real and Graphic Entertainment is attempting to reach a bigger and more ranging audience. The company’s attempt to combine the real nature and the entertainment aspects of what is quickly becoming part of the every day wrestling world is only showing success. Each and every day, the market is opening up more viewers for Real and Graphic Entertainment. This is do mostly in wake of the fall of an extremely popular and one of the companies that dictated the pace of the wrestling industry of today, Wrestle-Online. The popular organization comprised of numerous promotions that had an extravagantly high number of followers and quite the history as the independent community known as Primetime Championship Wrestling, started back in 2000. Three years later, the wrestling world is struggling to find a new company or promotion that can stand out and in due time become the dominant power that PCW/WO became until recently. Sources and officials at Real and Graphic Entertainment cannot confirm or deny that they would like to be the next big gem in the eye of the wrestling business. But through promoting and continuous hard work, Real and Graphic Entertainment is well on its way to becoming a contender, even in its early state. Officials over in Real and Graphic Entertainment have said that there has been a peaked interested in Real and Graphic Entertainment since its return on July 1. It is said that between July 1 and 2, traffic to the site more than doubled what it was in the past and still is on a daily basis. Exactly what the company expects to prove by this is unknown, but it is proof that an increase in site traffic can be a good sign, meaning more people are becoming aware of and adjusting themselves to the company. Real and Graphic Entertainment has already reported its confirmed signing of four superstars since its reopening on July 1. The company has plans to negotiate with more free agents on the market, and has even confirmed its interest in a few former Primetime Championship Wrestling stars, which are currently unemployed. Any word on the company actually talking to or offering contracts to that of the former PCW or PWC wrestlers is yet to be discovered. More information on Real and Graphic Entertainment and its growing popularity in the wrestling industry will be provided through both EW-News.com and Rageproject.com. Check back with both sites for updates on current and upcoming Real and Graphic Entertainment events and everyday agendas. Real and Graphic Entertainment
