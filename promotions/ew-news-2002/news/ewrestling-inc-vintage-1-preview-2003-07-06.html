eWrestling Inc. Vintage 1 Preview

6 July 2003

After weeks of anticipation, eWrestling, Inc. has finally opened it's website to the public! With the last phases in progress and nearing their end, the staff is preparing for the debut show. Rumours are that the debut may be pushed back to Monday Night Vintage on July 7, but nothing is confirmed. After weeks of anticipation, eWrestling, Inc. has finally opened it's website to the public! With the last phases in progress and nearing their end, the staff is preparing for the debut show. Rumours are that the debut may be pushed back to Monday Night Vintage on July 7, but nothing is confirmed. Our reporters managed to catch up to Cade Johnson today. Here's what he had to say about the debut. "Well...it's up a little later than we expected, but we didn't take into account those bumps and snags you always seem to run across. I'm confident that this will not hinder our goals, and we will still rise to what we set out to be. The most dominant federation in the world. eW, Inc. is here...make yourself at home!" After several days of rumours festering in the world of media, Cade Johnson has officially answered a lingering question in the back of everyone's mind. Due to unavoidable difficulties, the originally scheduled debut of eW, Inc. (Genesis, July 3) will now be moved to Monday (Vintage, July 7). Scheduled to appear will be many returning stars from the now-deceased Vintage Wrestling, also a PCW Hall of Famer, and current eVolution star Damon O'Halloran is set to make a special appearance, as well as some new blood. Cade Johnson is also rumoured to have big news concerning a few of eWrestling, Inc.'s title, and staff positions. And, let's not forget, the triple threat match pitting Vortex, Bryan Bellicose, and Pat Berry against each other for the eW, Inc. Asylum Championship. All in all, it's shaping up to be one hell of a debut. Be sure to tune in Monday night @ 9:00p EST on the FOX Network for all of the action! Until then, check in daily for the latest updates.
