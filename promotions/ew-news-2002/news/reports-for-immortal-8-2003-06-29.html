Reports for Immortal 8!!!

29 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The night began with all the hype surrounding the main event between The Parlyser and Nathan Kaye. With the stipulation being the winner will face current World Champion, Mike Wade, for his title at End of Days pay-per-view on July 13, it's a match that every NTICW fan would not want to miss. The action rolls along with Johnny Newmonic vs The Prophet and followed by The First vs Aaron Ward. These new superstars have a lot to prove to the NTICW faithfuls and they didn't want to be at the losing end but alas, someone had to cheat to win. In a non-title match between Television Champion, Sin Specter, and NTICW's goodwill resident, Johnny Goode, Johnny decided to prove to the world that evil doesn't pay and being 'goode' rules all. Did he succeed? Despite getting the night off, Mike Wade made an appearance and brought along someone new himself as an ally. Who is this guy and what will a new ally mean for the future? In match supposed to be for the Hardcore Title but made non-title on the night, champion The Dark Angel put up against Weezle. If Weezle picks up a victory here, he's up for the Hardcore Title at the PPV. Finally, the main event. We shall not let out too many details about it but we shall leave you an excerpt of the last few moments that took place before the show went off air. "Mike: I hate to rain on your little parade honey but I have this little thing called a contract. Yes that contract states that I am only required to defend MY belt on PPV’s and on Immortals only if I want to. And quite frankly I don’t feel like giving that piece of shit a shot. (The crowd boo) Mike: Wait!! I’ve just had a thought!! Vicky: Did it hurt?? (The crowd laugh) Mike: NO!! I tell you what smartass. I will defend my title next week against Nathan Jones if………" Interested? Head on to our site for the whole show! NTICW
