fWo World Champ Misses Show Due To Injury

9 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Possible medical condition has many concerned. August 9, 2003 – The fWo had previously reported that the fWo World Champion, Kodiak Vic Creed, was a no-show at this past Thursday’s broadcast of fWo Uprising because he had missed his connecting flight to Hawaii. It has just come to light that the reason for the no-show was due to a medical condition, which has some fWo Executives concerned. Sources close to Creed have said that he’s been suffering from bouts of numbness in his limbs since his match against Eli Flair at the fWo Meltdown Pay Per View. It’s been hinted that Creed injured his neck during the match but refuses to seek medical attention. Creed himself insists that he is fine and has ignored requests to have his condition examined by professionals. “On my worst day, I’m in better condition than anyone else on the roster,” claimed the Champ. “I don’t know where you people are getting your information from, but the Juggernaut is ready to kick ass anytime, anyplace.” Others in the locker room aren’t convinced. “He looked rough when he came back from his match against (Eli) Flair,” said one source who asked to remain anonymous. “He almost collapsed on his way to his locker room. I think there’s something seriously wrong with him. If he won’t get himself checked out, maybe someone higher up should make the decision for him.” “Working through injuries is part of the business,” said Slade Wilson, father of fWo superstar Mitch Wilson and the man who trained Kodiak Vic Creed. “If Vic is hurt, he won’t show it. People take shots at him, saying he’s on this or that, but you can’t deny the man’s toughness. Being fWo World Champion is everything to him and he’s not going to let anything stop him from carrying himself like a Champion.” Vic Creed is scheduled to appear on the upcoming RUAHH~! in Maui, Hawaii. Whether he’ll be competing or not is still unclear. fWo Wrestling
