eWNews — Column

Note the Sarcasm

14 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You May remember me, you may not...but I'm sure this article will re-introduce what I'm all about. You think you know, you have no idea...this is the rant of Sean Studd. Smell the ratings. For months, I’ve stayed silent, but that stops now. Ladies and Gentlemen, one half of the most controversial teams in Wrestling History (a team ever-so-popular on the Forums for those with no social life outside of a Personal Computer), finally rises from the grave to speak out on a few things. Now I, Sean Studd, have stayed away from the wrestling world…business wise…but I can’t help but watch what’s going on in today’s wrestling business, and I must say that I am very disappointed in each and every federation thus far. Ever since the stunt me and Trey Vincent pulled in the Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling (you know, the federation with the number of lives that equals a cat), I have yet to see an ounce of entertainment spew from any of these entertainers. I look over at places like ACW, and what do I see? I see the rookie from the PIW, Osyrus, doing absolutely nothing but attempting to become a combination of my good looks and my excellent personality. I think we all need to tell Osyrus, and ACW alike, that attempting to pull off a ‘breaking kayfabe’ character can only be done properly with a man such as myself. ACW is one of the many that have disappointed me. I mean, a federation with Janitors as tag team champions should not even be deemed a Cult federation, it should be deemed a backyard federation and forced to be placed on the Local Area Channel…so that only local viewers have to endure such horrid excuses for entertainment. Than we look over to the Asylum…hey folks, get this…they have upgraded from ass rapings to ‘we’ll have a show when we feel like’…oh yes, smell the ratings. Asylum is one of those places that proves to me, and the world, on how utterly mentally challenged today’s fans are. What moron would pay good money to watch a show that runs 2 minute matches with 40 minute segments. Believe me when I say, I am not the only one who can give two shits about Token Weed and Joe Campbell, who is seemingly in EVERY FUCKING MAIN EVENT ANGLE. Joe, you need to learn something from good old Vince McMahon, the whole ‘owner is a bad bad man in the main event angle’ got old a LONG time ago. Get over it. The last time I actually enjoyed an Asylum event was when they had that little candid camera action of the babes in the shower…Nerva could be a sadistic bitch all she want…doesn’t change the fact that she’s hot (don’t get me wrong, I wouldn’t let her near…the pride. A crazy bitch like that would get off on cutting it up…that ain’t gonna happen). But lets get away from the fighting world, and go to everyone’s favorite fetish. A federation filled with underage teenagers wearing clothing that would make Christina Aguilera look like a nun (*cough*Amy Campbell*cough*), Mario Bros. Part 14 (*cough* Fuse Brothers…like you couldn’t figure that one out…jackasses), and everyone’s favorite slacker (*cough*ETR*cough*). This federation is one that has topped my charts… As the most overrated federation of all time. When you have a superstar like Spyder holding more than one title in his short, some odd month, career…you know you’re running a poor federation. Please, esse, if I need to watch a greasy illegal immigrant talk on the microphone…I’ll tune into Smackdown! And listen to Eddie Guerrero talk for twenty minutes about how his ‘ride’ was hurt. I wouldn’t be surprised to see this bilingual jackass coming in with a 1969 Chevy Camaro, while bobbing his head to ‘Lowrider’. Hell, it didn’t surprise me when they ripped off an entire WWE/Austin article with Eron, why would this not surprise me? It seems to me that the fWo was a fuck up from the start back in November folks…besides the over populated roster of complete unentertaining mounds of shit, you also have almost every one of these wrestlers thinking they can write an entertaining column…when in fact they are all writing the same bullshit, but in different ways. Granted, I’ve read Ivy’s little reports, and she was the originator…I won’t take that away from the School Marm looking tramp; but than you have guys like Spyder and women like Amy Campbell thinking they can produce an entertaining piece of work. Please, the only thing entertaining about Spyder is the fact that he can even write in English. And the only entertainment spewing from Amy Campbell’s body is what she does behind the backstage area…but of course you’ll never hear Ivy talk about it. What makes me laugh is one of the primary lines of Campbell’s Column…”We've proven time and time again that wrestlers really shouldn't be allowed to publicly speak, because nothing halfway intelligent ever comes of it.” Than why in the name of god did say anything? Those herpe infested fingers of yours could be doing so many other things besides typing on the keyboard. But thanks to good old Ivy, wrestlers around the world seem to think they by writing a column that it will effect their overness in the long run. Me? I only write this because the tension has been building up for so long, that Sean Studd just HAD to vent. For instance, when I see an article written by Skylar Green, I ask myself…Skylar Green…WHO? Who the HELL is Skylar Green? From what I gathered up, this guy is a PCW World Champion…congradulations Green, you won a title from a federation that amounts to absolutely no one. Boo-fucking-hoo WO has closed down and died, cry me a river…than do me a favor, after the river is filled…build a bridge and jump off of it…it’s more entertaining than your article. For weeks, I’ve heard nothing but ‘cry for WO’. Sure, I’ll cry for it…cry of laughter. Now that WO has died…millions of jobbers are left on the unemployment line…you can’t tell me that it doesn’t make you laugh. But after all the ripping…I have one more victim. Yet, another, immigrant…but this one is from Russia. No folks, it isn’t that old guy from WWE, it’s the old guy from OSE. Ivan…spell-his-name-right-and-I’ll-give-you-a-cookie-say-it-right-and-I’ll-shake-your-hand. Now, Ivan I have to admit something to you…from entertainer to entertainer…listening to a thick-accented Russian type in the third person has to be the funniest thing I’ve read in my life. But hearing you dictate every single thing known to be American, is even funnier. And Ivan…tell me if this is good or bad thing…you giving up the entire little Russian gimmick and going to something new. You’re a big guy, maybe something like ‘Dead Man Walking’ could be better for you…because honestly, I can’t take another one of your “DYAAAHAAA!” or whatever the fuck it is you do. And, if I could get intelligent for a moment, wouldn’t you be ashamed of your Russian heritage? Maybe I missed something out of your whole gimmick, because honestly after reading your article I have no interest in reading up on your career…call it ADD if you like, I call it having good taste in seeing which angles are good and which angles suck…but didn’t the Russian Empire fall? What, is your whole gimmick about rebuilding a country in a wrestling federation? Wow…the writers of wrestling have hit an all time low. Well folks, that is my rant thus far. I am expecting somewhat of heat, or just a complete ignorant ‘ignore’ icon on my article. But that is fine and dandy, after all, I’m just some guy in the PIW that was yelled at for ripping on every single man known to the Forum Business…what do I know? And who am I to get a reply? After all, my home federation doesn’t include such greats as Erik Kelly as World Champion…note the sarcasm.
