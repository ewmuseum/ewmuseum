The Return of IWO.

4 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
IWO recently began the slow trek back to it's former mainstream status with help from the online transcripts of Hostile Takeover, Beginning of the End of the Beginning. Blessed be the IWO. If you were one of the lucky few, privileged enough to join the returning IWO for it's comeback show, by no means were you let down. For those of you that were unable to attend the show, the IWO has released the transcript of the show for your reading pleasure on it's website. Here is a quick rundown of the matches. 1) Action!'s Coral Avalon defeated fWo's High Flyer via pinfall in the opening bout. 2) H.I.T. came away with an impressive win over Samuel Potright in a technical exhibition match. 3) In a battle of people with Hard in their name, Hardcore God pulled out a "W" against Action!'s HardCase. 4) Following a brief intermission, a Tag Team Scramble match occurred, featuring some of the top tag teams today: the Legion of Dairy; the Deadlier Sins: the Super Martin-O Bros; and Those Damned Mexicans. In the end, it would be the Super Martin-O Brothers getting the win. 5) Next, two former IWO World Champion squared off as Donnie Daze and The Mysterious One went toe-to-toe with amazing results! Score one for the Dazed one. 6) And finally, the main event featured BOB Wrestling mainstay Trey Vincent as he took on Greg Allocca. Vincent would ultimately pick up a win, here. But not before something completely unexpected happened! But you'll have to see for yourself! That's all I have for now! Be sure to check out Beach Party VII! IWO. Because your life depends on it.
