PCW: Tuesday Turmoil - The Return

7 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The wrestling world has seemed to have stopped in anticipation for the return of the legendary Primetime Championship Wrestling. And the good news is we’re only 24 hours away, this event promises to be a spectacle of entertainment. Tuesday Turmoil, despite being one of the most anticipated wrestling shows in history, has been great for New Jersey tourism industry. With people flocking into the state for the show, airports are packed and roads are busier than ever. And with only a day until the event it is virtually impossible to book a room in a hotel in New Jersey. Ever since PCW "died" earlier this year the demand for the return of this historically brilliant fed has been outstanding. Involving rallies, protests and random donations to any organisation or entrepreneur who could possibly reopen it. Tuesday Turmoil for December 9th, 2003 Live from Boardwalk Hall in Atlantic City, NJ Singles Match Terlayne Sterling vs Judgement Who versus who? The best PCW could do in their return was to trot out two greenhorns in the opening match on Turmoil? Seems to be so. Or, just perhaps, the genius of Tyler Nelson saw something in these two kids. Perhaps he recognized the sheer power and speed of Judgement. Perhaps the hardnosed, blue collar work ethic of Sterling caught his eye... ...or perhaps a beast from PCW's past will come out to make a statement on a couple of curtain jerkers to lead off the show. This is PCW. Anything is possible. Tag Team Match Products of the System vs Mega Soaring Arrows All of Seattle is humming with excitement about the exciting young Japanese transplants who call themselves the Mega Soaring Arrows. Warden Widger, on the other hand, hopes to be humming taps for the MSA, due to the beating he envisions his new, massive version of Products of the System back to PCW. Singles Match Abner vs Fantoche PCW's resident hick is back, and looks to get off to a fast start against the mysterious Mexican luchador, Fantoche. Singles Match Jonah vs Javier Padilla Don't leave your seat for a moment when these two high flying, risk takers enter the ring. Rumor has it that these two are among the most exciting wrestlers to grace a PCW ring since the likes of Kid Wonder and Miyagawa called PCW home. Truth is greater than hype... And the truth comes this Tuesday. Singles Match Michael Jacques vs Titan Former OSF worker Michael Jacques will try fire off a successful launch to his PCW career against the massive Titan. Titan, who is full of promise and ability, has always been just on the cusp of greatness, but something always seems to hold him back. Is Titan ready for Primetime? Tune in on Tuesday to find out! Singles Match Brian Arson vs Shaun Styles This is a match between two guys who are hungry for success, and desperate to make their mark in the industry. Styles labored almost endlessly in the PWA and never got his shot... Until now. You can be damn sure nothing is going to stand in his way from rising to the top. Arson is a cocky, former DWO worker, who knows what its like to be the big fish in a promotion. Well, Big Fish, welcome to the vast blue ocean. Welcome to Primetime Championship Wrestling. Singles Match Afwa vs Jay Phoenix Jay Phoenix has been successful on every level and in every promotion in which he has competed. Except PCW. Its like an itch he needs to scratch. Unfortunately, for Phoenix, in Afwa's eyes, he is more like meat that needs to be tenderized. Main Event Three Way Dance PCW World Tag Team Championship E2 vs TD & C vs Brothers in Blood Entertainment Extreme is back, and they have brought the tag team title straps with them. But who are these other guys? E2 and the rest of the world is going to find out this Tuesday night. Also on Turmoil, fans will be treated to a walkthrough of the newly opened PCW Hall of Fame, we will hear from the FIRST EVER OSW World Champion Sebastian Toomes, and CEO Tyler Nelson makes his triumphant return to the company he helped build into an industry juggernaut. What are his plans for the direction of the promotion. Betcha he spills the beans this Tuesday. So tune in this Tuesday night as PCW and Turmoil return to the air!!! http://primetimewrestling.com Turmoil 132
