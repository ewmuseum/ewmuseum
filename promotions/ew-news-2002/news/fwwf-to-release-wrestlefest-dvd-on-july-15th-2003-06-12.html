FWWF to release WrestleFest DVD on July 15th!

12 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Parterning with Sony Music Studios yet again, the FWWF prepares to release it's recent "WrestleFest 5" extravaganza on DVD .. and with a few extra surprises included! To view the cover artwork for the video, visit the below URL: http://www.icouldcareless.com/fwwf/wf5cover.jpg The DVD release will deliver all the exciting matches from this year's unbelievable spring classic! This video also features some exciting extras and bonus matches. Below you will find all the information you need to know about this compilation, which is slated for release next month. Comes with a seven year anniversary edition seal!
