Is Action going to be Plagued in 2004?

20 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rumor mill churns with speculation former fWo World Champion to sign After the signing of the last ACW World Champion Alias, rumor has it new backstage leader Elizabeth Pleh is looking to continue pushing Action towards supremacy in the year to come. And it appears high on the list of targets is former fWo World Champion and inaugural Survivor "the Black Plague" Ruben Ross. Ross did a one-off stint back in March against the missing in action Daniel Phillips to a great response to fans, who regarded him purely as a babyface and not the heel he left the fWo as earlier this year. Outside of that match and a match ironically enough against Alias, Ross has not wrestled since his 24th birthday on February 9th. But it is rumored that leading into 2004, Action wants to take steps to finally plant itself as the premier wrestling federation in North America. Ross is still in in-ring shape, due to his school called the Quarantine out of San Diego. It is rumored that should he sign, he would be pushed as one of the, if not the number one, pre-eminent babyfaces on the roster and would likely spearhead a charge against Wrestling 101. Likely opponents would then be one of the current titleholders, Keith Scott Zimmerman for the Bantam Championship or Jeff Garvin for the Action Championship. While Ross has a history with Garvin from their run in the Asylum as part of Stranglehold last year, the loudest buzz is Ross coming out as KSZ's mystery opponent at the pay-per-view that would kick off a long program between the two. Ross was unavailable for comment as of this time, and while Action has been known to talk to Ross (the March run could've become something more) they were quitely noncommital on whether they'd talked to Ross since then. More to come as events warrant. fWo Survivor I
