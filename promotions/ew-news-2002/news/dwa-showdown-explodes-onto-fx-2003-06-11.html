DWA: Showdown Explodes onto FX

11 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In their debut show on their new network, FX, Showdown saw some serious changes, as Jordan Brannigan began his reign as the new DWA owner. Showdown began as Jordan Brannigan, much to the hatred of the crowd, made his debut as owner of the DWA, coming down to the ring to make a few announcements. He confirmed that he fired Senior Referee Lee Tanaka and promoted Simon Johnson to the position. He then called out Commissioner Ronnie Johnson, who told Jordan to go to hell, but then the man who threw his match to allow Jordan to win, Gideon Nazareth came down, and contained Ronnie. Jordan announced that he was demoting Ronnie all the way down to commentator alongside Jeremy Taylor. He then sent Ronnie away, and announced that Gideon was now the new commissioner of the DWA. Then something unexpected happened, as Alexandria Simmons came down to the ring. Alexandria was the woman that Jordan was arrested for sexually assaulting, and although the charges were dropped, answers were never clear. Well, they became clear tonight, as Alexandria climbed into the ring, and kissed Jordan. It was revealed that they have been 'partners' for over a year, since right after Jordan entered the DWA. Just more of the conspiracy revealed. Then Norville Matthews, the man who became the referee to complete the screwjob at Hostile Takeover, was called out. However, instead of rewarding him, Jordan admitted that he never liked him, and demoted him all the way down to ring announcer. Norville was shocked, and then was attacked by Alexandria when he didn't leave the ring. The DWA would never be the same again after Jordan's opening moments. Evo Shandor took on FoW Member and 1/2 of the World Tag Team Champions, Drastic. With the help of Brad Keidis, Drastic was able to pick-up the win, and then afterwards, the FoW attacked Evo, but that only drew out Intoxicated Justice who helped fend off the FoW. Donnie Darko took on Grovel in what was an awesome match, however, once again the FoW interferred during the match, and without Grovel's knowledge nailed Darko with a powerful move, and draped his arm over him, allowing for Grovel to capture the victory. The main event saw long time foes face each other, as LeStatt Knight took on Payne. The match did not last long, as the two immediately went to the outside of the ring, and after several moments, Payne threw referee Simon Johnson off the top of the ramp, and the two continued into the back, where Payne's partner, Jeff Wylde, attacked Knight from behind. The two began to viciously attack Knight, but then Knight was saved by World Champion, Typhoid Mary, with her trusty barbed wire baseball bat. After nailing Payne with the bat, The Revolution were driven off, and Mary helped Knight, affirming the fact that due to mutual enemies, they had an alliance. At the end of the show, Jordan Brannigan asked what Gideon planned to do as his first act as Commissionership. Gideon said that he was going to book himself into a World Title shot against Typhoid Mary, however, then Jordan dropped the bombshell on him, saying that he could not book any title matches for himself. Gideon was very pissed as Jordan rode off in his limo. DWA: Dragon Wrestling Association
