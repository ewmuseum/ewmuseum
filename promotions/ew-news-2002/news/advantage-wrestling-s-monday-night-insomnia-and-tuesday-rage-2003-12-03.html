Advantage Wrestling's Monday Night Insomnia and Tuesday Rage Previews.

3 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Advantage Wrestling is live this week in Pensylvania. Monday Insomnia airs in Philly while Tuesday Rage airs in Pittsburgh. Advantage Wrestling is looking to continue the greatness that was and do so with the set of their first shows back on air. With the new addition of Tuesday Rage rumors are spreading rampid as to how this will effect AW in general. It's going to be a site to see that's for sure! Greetings and salutations, Sean Levy here. Firstly, this just has to be said... the card's this week look WEAK ... But I know my employer well and I'm sure that Razor Blade has alot more that meets the eye scheduled for these two shows. Least I hope so being as its the return of AW! Myself and broadcast colleague John Fulmer are pretty pumped about this return and cant wait to see what all the surprises are that are expected to go down on December 8th and 9th. The matches scheduled are... MONDAY INSOMNIA Event: Insomnia 18 Place: Philadelphia, PA Arena: First Union Center Capacity: 21,600 Date: 12/08/03 Time: 10:01 PM Central Main Event Sapphire vs. Angel Dawson Cody w/ T-Man vs. Skylar Clay Sean Casey vs. Blake Cassidy Gwen vs. Joy Boxster TUESDAY RAGE Event: Rage 1 Place: Pittsburgh, PA Arena: Mellon Arena Capacity: 17,537 Date: 12/09/03 Time: 8:00 PM Central Main Event Brian Adkins vs. Ja'zel Walker Troy Chryst v.s. Chazz Irvine Viper vs. Acrophobia The tickets are already sold out for the event but if your in the area you may be able to catch a few from a ticket trading agency or ebay. Good luck to those still aspiring tickets, and remember that's on Spike TV so tune in! Monday Insomnia Announcer & Head Promotor -Sean Levy- Advantage Wrestling Events
