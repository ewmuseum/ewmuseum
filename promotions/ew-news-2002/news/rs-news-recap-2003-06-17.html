RS News Recap

17 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
All the latest news from the wrestling federations on the RS circuit. EWP – Excite Pro Wrestling Mr. Morales, who recently had a run in with PTC Owner ‘Trigger’, was announced as the manager of EWP American Champion Drew Haunted, and immediately set his sights on helping his new client capture the World Championship crowd. Mr. Morales had this to say on his impressive client, who captured the American Championship after only two matches in the federation. He is yet to be beaten and with him under my wing, I can see him going places. I might not be a trainer, but I am a businessman and I will do my best to make sure that Drew gets what he deserves - media deals, photo shoots, sponsorships and of course, the EPW World Championship! Drew names it and he'll get it, within reason of course - you can't for too much too soon but I'm he knows all that." DW - Dynasty Wrestling Acting C.E.O Mike Vitucci kept his promise true with an awesome main event at DW Titans 2003, in which Chris Boswell triumphed over Havoc, Shawn Storm, Gillesp, Sean Manning and Hellfire X to win the World Championship. With DW Titans the last PPV before the Dynasty Wrestling event of the year – DW Global Takeover, many are wondering what the next month will through up in terms of action heading into GT2. Sean Manning suffered an injury in the Six Pack Challenge Match at DW Titans, and is expected to be out for quite some time with a broken bone in his neck after being hit with a ‘X-Virus’ onto a chair by Hellfire X. Jet and Zyfloi will be forced to make sure that reach other don’t lose there matches after being crowed joint champions of the new Cruiserweight Title. If one was to lose the match then both will lose the title. CHZ – Canuck Heat Zone It was all action in CHZ with North American Champion Keyser Soze announcing that he has challenged rival Lucifer to a match for the strap at the next CHZ PPV. However has the Champion got the odds stacked too high against him due to Lucifer’s alliance with Diablo, Hawke and Skeleton Jackoff? Only time will tell. Masters and Derceto will both be renewing old rivalries from their IUWF days when they take on Keyser Soze and Wild Dog of the nBk this week. They both view this weeks matches as a chance to show the entire CHZ community exactly what they are capable of. SCWF – Superior Championship Wrestling Federation The coming Sundays SCW show live from Yokohama Arena - Yokohama, Japan looks set to be an exciting occasion with such matches as Johnny Page vs. Loki Nevarro and Rome Turner vs. Shang Maxwell vs. Syphon X confirmed to be on the card. Brian Hulin & The Snoops vs. Suzanne Gray Bear & Penny Preist will end Weeks and weeks of anticipation. The Tag Team titles will finally be dusted off the shelves and placed around the waist of one of these two teams. This should be a classic tag team bout with the better team coming out on top. So tune in this week as SCWF kicks it into high gear for Anarchy! AW – Animosity Wrestling At a yet to be determined pay-per-view, on a yet to be determined date, two men will collide to determine the new Animosity Wrestling World Heavyweight Champion. You could be forgiven with not being excited with the sketchy details, however Rumours swirling around are that Corey Gibson will hold two battle royals to find two participants which should certainly heat up action in AW! Over the weekend, Erikk Faust laid down a challenge to the man he could never beat, Sky Nash. Sky Nash has phoned in his acceptance and in two weeks, Sky Nash hand Erikk Faust will collide once again to see who will come out on top! IHW – Insane Hardcore Wrestling Due to the recent departures of newly signed IHW superstars, Emag and Johnny Rose - soon to have places in the Hall of Shame, the King of Insane II card has had to be updated. The Morbid Angel and Decimation have been added to the 8-man battle royal, fighting for a chance to compete for the European Championship. Big Daddy has been added to the King of Insane tournament in place of Johnny Rose. With such stars as Hardcore Jono on the roster, IHW looks to have a great future in the wrestling business. NGW – Next Generation Wrestling In the last couple weeks NGW has been on a signing spree picking up some big name talent to improve the federation. An example of the calibre of talent that the federation has recently signed is a very talent superstar in Rob Zone. Zone is a very established wrestler who has fought in RXW, UwA, RWWF and RXWF. As of now Rob is not expected to make an appearance at Night of Kings but is expected to debut on the following Storm. NTICW – New True Internet Championship Wrestling James Hitachimoto had decided to quit NTICW. His contract will be terminated with immediate effect. NTICW wishes James all the best in his future endeavours. To make up for the departure, NTICW has announced several new signings, 7 of them to be exact, into the company. Watch out for some of their debut in Sunday's Immortal 6. If 7 new signings was’nt enough, Ronan Farrell is also set to make his return to NTICW. Farrell is rumoured to compete in his first match back this Sunday against Defiance. IIW It was reported out of the IIW headquarters that Shawn Hunt and The Original Balla had a little bit of a confrontation ending in a fight. Both wrestlers haven't been punished but one of the witnesses reported that Shawn Hunt walked away saying "Your ass is mine and that nobody disrespects The SHOWSTOPPA". In other news rumours have been running like crazy about Mortis and Sean Andretti. What exactly is going on here?, is there an affiliation? or do these two just want to tear each other apart. Mortis has been reported saying he wants to take over and destroy IIW while Sean Andretti hasn't been phased. One wonders when these two will actually collide if they in fact do the question is when and where?...and if they do will Sean Andretti wrestle under his real name or the name he competes in other companies in as "The Canadian Sensation" Rage. All of these questions should be answered in the matter of weeks all you can do is sit back, relax, watch Insanity and wait! UNW The UNW has been under pressure in recent weeks, because of six men who want everything their way...and it's not going to get any better until these fights are settled in two weeks in Hamburg, New York. Fusion and Adrenalin have met twice before, their bodies colliding in an unfair match at SBD:::03. But, at Asphyxiation, both men will collide in the ring in a fair match to prove who is the better wrestler...the better fighter...the better MAN. Tony Forreli and Pug Cataldi have been trashtalking to each other for what seems to be a long while. When will these two men settle their differences? Who will be the top mobster? Asphyxiation 2003 has these two men squaring off in a humongous match that might turn numerous heads. Anybody Killah and !nterogative have been friends, but are now enemies. In probably the most shocking main event in a while, Intero promises that ABK will fall down to the mat in pain. Will it happen? Will it not? Three huge feuds. Six people who are willing to tear eachother apart...all at Asphyxiation 2003, on the 29th of June. All this and more, including the finale of the UNW North American Championship match and the first ever UNW Osmosis Title match looms at UNW Asphyxiation 2003. Revolution Studios
