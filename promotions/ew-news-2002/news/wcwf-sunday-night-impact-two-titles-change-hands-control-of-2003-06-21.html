WCWF Sunday Night Impact: Two titles change hands; control of company placed in balance

21 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Twisting, turning, wheeling and dealing were the order of the day. And at the end of the show, the future of the World Class Wrestling Federation was a lot less clear than it was at the beginning of the night. The June 15 edition of Sunday Night Impact started with a surprise challenge from Dane Rennier. Rennier, already scheduled to face Matt Walker for the United States Title, hit the ring and demanded that the match be made a ladder match! Walker came out, accepted, and it was on. The last title the Fates needed to control them all would, literally, be hanging on the line. But an even more shocking development was taking place backstage. Nathanial Duke and Raymond Chandler, along with WCWF owner Bill Jackson and commissioner Jackson Therapy, reached an agreement: if, by the end of the show, Duke and the Fates didn’t hold all of the titles in the federation, then they would call off their attacks on the rest of the wrestlers until after the pay-per-view. If and when the Fates did achieve total control, all booking power would go to Duke and Bill Jackson… and Chandler would have to leave the WCWF forever. Finally, the matches started. Dan Bonez took on “The King” James Michaels in the opening bout. Unfortunately for Michaels, it didn’t last long as Rennier, obviously still upset that Michaels walked out on him at Opposites Attract, attacked him on his way to the ring before rolling him in to Bonez, who made an easy pinfall afterwards. Backstage, Walker confronted Chandler about the deal he’d made. Chandler assured Walker that it was to ensure that Judge got a fair shot at the World Title and said he’d do what he could tonight to take some of the pressure off of Walker’s back. After the commercial break, we’d learn that Walker had other ideas. Stan Stone was being tended to in his locker room by EMTs, and security footage showed Walker bursting in and attacking Stone, driving a chair into his knee as payback for what Stone did to Thump at OA. With Rennier’s only true ally seemingly out of commission, had Walker just saved the WCWF? Back to the ring. Two members of the Fates, WCWF World Champion Jester and WCWF Hardcore Champion Brad Jackson (no relation to Bill) scored a somewhat surprising(ly easy) win over multiple-time tag team champions The Atomic Brothers. This sets up a very interesting match for next week, as Jester and Jackson will face Bad Blood — also known as their stablemates Rennier and Stone — for the tag team titles. And in a possible precursor to the bad blood that already exists within the Fates, Rennier attacked Jester after the match. This led to the ladder match, which did not disappoint. Walker survived a Dane-O-Might (Shooting Star DDT) through the announce table, then came back to deliver Beat The System (springboard tornado DDT) off the ladder. Walker climbed the ladder and was a step away from retaining his title when Rennier flattened him with the Final Eclipse off the ladder. He then climbed and claimed the U.S. Title for the Fates. Post-match, Stone made his way to the ring and smashed a TV camera in Walker’s face, then tried to electrocute him with the open wires until Chandler hit him with a steel chair, then took the chair to the knee that Walker had injured during his earlier attack. With that, the main event — Stone’s International Television Title defense against Chandler — began. Chandler dominated the freshly injured Stone early in the match, but Stone used his power to stay in the match, and he appeared to have things well in hand after hitting Chandler with the Point Of Impact (high angle DDT). But he climbed to the top turnbuckle, looking to injure Chandler’s knee the same way he injured Thump’s at Opposites Attract. Chandler rolled out of the way at the last second to avoid Stone’s attack, then planted him with Police Department Brutality (full Nelson faceslam). Stone passed out in The Line (full Nelson camel clutch), and Chandler won the INTV Title. After Chandler’s victory, Duke reappeared, congratulating him on winning and keeping his troops safe until the PPV. But… between the end of the ladder match and the end of the main event, Duke had control of every title in the WCWF, giving him time to set up return matches for both Stone and Walker for the PPV. The catch? The match is a best of three falls tag match — Walker & Chandler against Rennier & Stone. The winning team gets both the INTV and U.S. titles… and there’s another stipulation that Duke won’t reveal until the PPV itself. Sunday Night Impact Results: Dan Bonez d. “The King” James Michaels by pinfall Jester & Brad Jackson d. The Atomic Brothers by pinfall U.S. Title Ladder Match Dane Rennier d. Matt Walker (c) — Rennier wins second U.S. Championship International Television Title Match Raymond Chandler d. Stan Stone (c) by knockout — Chandler wins second INTV/TV Championship World Class Wrestling Federation
