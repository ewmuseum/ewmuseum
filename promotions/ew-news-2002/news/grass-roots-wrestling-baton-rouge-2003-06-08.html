Grass roots wrestling = Baton Rouge

8 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Spinebuster Wrestling is preparing to hold their third event, June 21, in their short history, and it has been said the young promotion has everything it takes to make it big in this business. If someone told you a small promotion based in the heart of Baton Rouge, Louisiana was turning some heads, you'd probably tell they been sipping too much of Grampa's magic medicine. But Spinebuster Wrestling is preparing to hold their third event, June 21, in their short history, and it has been said the young promotion has everything it takes to make it big in this business. They currently prepare themselves for Crimson Fist of Glory, hot off the tails of Refuse, Resist and their debut show, Medic Alert. Owner J. Leslie Voss had this to say about his promotion... "People talk about fWo and Action! and the Asylum but what they distinctly forget to do is take a look at grass roots wrestling. Well, that's what I'm providing for the wrestling fans of Baton Rouge, the chance to take a look at wrestling in it's purest form." Spinebuster plan to hold a massive 48 man battle royale in what Voss calls the "Crimson Fist of Glory" match. "It basically comes down to this, four men begin inside the ring. And every three minutes another four men are sent down. This will happen until all 48 participants enter the ring and all of them are battling for a chance to wear the SbW World title around their waists. But there's only one way to eliminate your opponent. "Make them bleed." The contraversial ex-thReat wrestler has the belt around his waist. Critics were saying that he only started his promotion to get his name out there, and what better way than putting the title around his own waist. "Those Internet surfing, puss ridden, band geek virgins can flap their jaws all they want. The fact of the matter is no matter where I go I WILL be the Main Ratings Draw. I put bums in seats and that's all that matters. I want Spinebuster to get to the top, and I'm the one that's gonna do it. "I didn't just hand myself the title. I've already defended it twice against two formidable opponents in Alex Wood and Bradley Duncan, there's no way you can deny that I am a fighting champion." Other matches featuring during the evening is a three-way title bout for the SbW Hardcore title between Midas Brick, the champion pain GRILLÉ and cHEESE. But the stipulation of the match is what young up and coming star, Midas Brick, calls a Ball & Chain match. Midas had this to say about his up coming title shot... "Look, I'm coming into this business and I'm not taking my time. I'm going straight to the top. People are gonna wanna stop me. People are gonna want to see me fall on my ass and fail miserably. But there's one thing that stops me from failing, sheer determination. My heart is in the business and I want that title around my waist. "Bring on your big names. cHEESE and Duncan, whoever. Even these new stars like pain GRILLÉ. What I promise to do is entertain the fans night in and night out, and hopefully win a few titles on the way." Voss also promises to defend his title against someone, but he has not released his opponent for the show. All he has said is that Crimson Fist of Glory will be an absolute blockbuster, and that everyone fan of wrestling in the Louisiana area should try to get down to the F.G. Clark Activity Center in Baton Rouge. No doubts about it, Spinebsuter is clawing it's way into the spotlight, but in my opinion, it'll be a while before it affects the wrestling industry at all. Spinebuster Wrestling
