Think Red #5: Home

23 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With Russia breathing down his neck, Praporschik Stanislav finds himself in an interesting quandry. DYAAHAAHAA!! Once again, Ivan Sergeiovich is here with one more installment of the most unbiased piece of media in America, “Think Red!” As many of you are sure to know, Mother Russia has been placing considerable pressure upon the shoulders of Ivan Stanislav. Pressure that is beginning to demand more and more results to be shown to superiors. These pressures have come to such point, that the possibility of Ivan Stanislav being taken from America and from OSW could be possibility and with that, the end of Ivan’s career in OSW. Unfortunately, many of you will probably be rejoicing the possibility… few of you will care not, and very, very few will wish for one such as I to stay. Ivan accepts this, knowing full well that many people despise him and many people curse the ground that he treads upon. It is interesting thing, however, whenever Ivan heard that he might be leaving the OSW against his will. A strange feeling overcame him. A feeling of… sadness. This emotion… sadness… is something that rarely has its chance to effect me however, whenever it does, it can become quite a foe indeed. What is it that created this feeling of sadness because I might need to leave OSW in future? After all, Ivan loathes structure of OSW and those that run it. Ivan longs to right what they do wrong and revamp the hierarchy. Yet, even with all these wants and wishes… he still longs to be within structure, corrupt as it may be! To go back in time, back years in past to whenever Ivan was in Primetime Championship Wrestling, he never feared leaving. He never feared disassociating himself with PCW. There was no sadness when Ivan moved to OSW. There was no regret, no fear, and no remorse. It would seem that Ivan’s relationship is completely different when dealing with PCW, or when dealing with OSW. Perhaps I feel these feelings because Ivan Stanislav was one of first men to sign to OSW and start it, as fledgling federation, and see it grow into beautiful and powerful organization. Perhaps it is because I see myself, in some twisted sort of way, as father to some sort of child. Perhaps I am devoted, much like Ivan is to protecting Motherland, to protecting this federation that I have been with for so long. This is dangerous thing, growing attached to something. Particularly something that you feel so passionate and protective of. It takes very much to conjure up these feelings with Ivan, and yet they have grown. Ivan has been in this sport for very long time and he has seen many, many things. He has seen many different types of people with different agenda’s that have come and gone, succeeded and failed. It appears, however, that in this day in age there is no longer feeling of ‘home’ within many federations. This may be merely Ivan’s assumption, but it appears to be true. Do not believe that this is merely geared towards the younger men and women in this sport. They are not only ones that are guilty of these feelings… or lack thereof. As Ivan looks at OSW, he sees it as secondary home. He will protect her… he will defend her… and he will fight for her no matter what cost. But as Ivan sees others in his profession, there is no camaraderie. There is no collective pride. Men and women come and go at their whim because they merely wish to better themselves and do not think beyond the self, when their actions effect entire federations. Stanislav looks around, and he does not see many men or women that do not just fight for themselves, but for betterment of their federation as well. Even as OSW does things that Ivan does not agree with… he still is loyal as he can. These feelings of individuality can be very dangerous, for they produce ego’s and lack of loyalty and respect. However, how does one conjure up these feelings? How does one enable individual involved in federation to be loyal to federation? This question, Ivan cannot answer, for he does not know. For himself, his loyalty came through watching the successes and failures of OSW. Seeing the truly proud moments… and truly disastrous times. For seeing that beacon of light that OSW was for greatness in sportsmanship… and seeing it as vilest, most evil thing imaginable. Many storms have come and gone and Stanislav stands tall. Men and women need to understand that federation is their home. Believe it or not, they have many brothers and sisters counting on them. Even those family members that they do not like, or would prefer to Red Scare into walls… they must understand that the individual will fail in long run. You must respect your leaders, listen to peers, teach your youngsters, and learn from yourself and experiences. The fruits of devotion are delicious indeed. Ivan does not know if Russia will take him away… though he hopes she will not. While trumpets may sound and cheers will be raised if Ivan must leave… he will be sad within his heart. Believe me, Ivan has one. Ivan was born from Mother Russia… but his current home is OSW. He intends to stay there. Ivan Stanislav Red Army Commander
