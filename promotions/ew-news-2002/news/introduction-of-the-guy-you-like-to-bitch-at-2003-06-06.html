Introduction of the Guy you like to Bitch At

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey there, I’m Claude Mitchell, your friendly neighborhood “Chairman of Talk”. If you don’t remember me, I don’t really blame you, I mean how could I, in the last two years we’ve had as many episodes as Macy Gray had hit tracks. If you like Macy, I apologize, she doesn’t appeal to any of my senses. In all seriousness though, I’ve went from a minor on screen character to somewhat of a minor celebrity, because of the debut of my show Bitch With Mitch back in the summer of 1999. At the time, it was a simple radio show, I interviewed people who I could get to through my nephews who at the time were wrestling (Corteia family guys, don’t feel like getting bogged down describing them), but apparently some higher up smoked something wrong, and I got a national deal with Fox Sports Net. Think about that, from local radio show hosted out of Chester County, Pennsylvania.. To a show that’s played on a huge network that is carried by cable providers across the US. I mean, I had no room for complaints, but as we got more recognition, I became high maintenance, but heck the first season is still my most successful. When the second went along, I got in contract disputes with Fox, as for some reason I thought my success earned me the right to be broadcasted on Fox. I don’t doubt my mind state there, because look Fox plays crap, always have, and they ride their few success stories to the ground. And I felt like I deserved to have BWM rode into the ground, but eh.. They disagree and the contract was terminated, they showed reruns for a few months but that was it of BWM. Until I got the deal with HBO, but I found out first hand that I was just in-between season fodder.. What else could I expect right? Here I am, I have the first interview with highly controversial bad guy Villam Ender, and from what I hear it was a success, but higher ups must’ve felt something wrong with it. They tried to run my show, and then trying to appease everyone, I got Mega Job on next. And from the response I got from GLAAD (I love you guys too, not that way though) they didn’t like the fact that Janito was being mocked for being a fag. It’s not my fault, his lifestyle is up to him, but I mean it’s my show, I’m free to mock anything.. Right? Meh, it didn’t matter anyway, because I found out my interview with KroW, that my timeslot was taken by Bob Costas…. …… …… Bob Costas?! I asked about being replaced somewhere else, but nope, I was done. I mean, Bob Costas?! Only thing that would’ve disgusted me anymore was if some useless bimbo manager from fWo or some place got my time slot. Wait, that wouldn’t be worse, since I’d dump me for a bimbo too. But Bob Costas? Things like this make me happy that HBO is falling back into their hole they created, Oz sucked it’s last season, Sopranos is a boring soap opera, and Sex in the City features a quartet of women past menopause dating guys. Oh, and they have some show with funeral people that’s more boring than well.. Bob Costas! Well, yeah, I don’t like HBO, if I didn’t get satellite for free, I would’ve cancelled my subscription. So why am I telling you all this? No, I’m not having a Jerry Springer moment, where I try and make a moral out of a group of trailer trash’s actions, instead I’m here to explain where I am now. After several assaults suffered on my show, I have help who will work as a co-host of sorts to me. Who is it? To be perfectly honest, I’ve never met the guy, I hear his name is Bennie or Bernie something, I hear he’s truly a card though. And our interviews for Season 3.2 of Bitch With Mitch, and all will all be archived right here on ewNews, don’t ask me what the “ew” stands for either.. I don’t have a clue. Oh yeah, and what type of introduction column would this be if I didn’t whore the show out a little? Our first interview back will be with Action! Wrestling’s very own “Impulse” Brian James, it’ll be his second interview on the show. Look for the interview on Monday, June 9th, on top of that if you have any questions for everyone’s favorite firestarter.. Feel free to e-mail them to me at [detail withheld] Well that’s all from me this time, let me go and thank the guys at ewNews for giving me this opportunity, and hope unlike the past ventures of BWM, the rug isn’t yanked from underneath me. Until next time, -Mitch Bitch With Mitch
