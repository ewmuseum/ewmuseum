Jane Reagan Pressured Over Kannon Rumours

5 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Is OSW's owner once again trying to trick the fans, and return to her lying ways? Or is she really just an innocent amid recent rumours? With new evidence uncovered, she sends a plea to Xavier Kannon himself to deny the story that’s dogging her. Following the rather confusing reports regarding Xavier Kannon and Old School Wrestling that surfaced last week, it was expected that Jane Reagan's categorical denial of any approach would have nailed the coffin shut on that story... But it seems someone has other plans. A number of wrestling journalists were faxed travel information for 2 OSE representatives... And their destination? Barbados. With Xavier Kannon rumoured to have left the United States for the Caribbean paradise following his retirement, this news seems to directly conflict with Jane Reagan's original denial of any contact, negotiations or knowledge. "It seems a little suspicious now," remarked Jim Smitty, a now freelance reporter following the collapse of wrestlingtattler.com, "Jane saying she didn't even know where he was, then sending 2 of her people to his new home... Looks like she was hoping to sign him on the sly." Still no comments have been heard from the Kannon side of the story, the ex-PCW Champion seeming content to stay veiled in his privacy... But again Jane Reagan was contacted, and in the face of the 'evidence', still flat-out denied any knowledge. "If any officials from OSW are on a flight to Barbados, then they're now fired for taking a holiday without booking time off," the owner snapped, "I have had no contact with Mr. Kannon, OSW has had no contact with Mr. Kannon, and Mr. Kannon has certainly not contacted us. I urge Xavier to come out and deny these RUMOURS also, so that we can both get along with our lives in peace." Given some of Jane's actions in the past while heading OSW, many can be excused for not entirely trusting the company's owner... And many don't. Is she trying to fully utilise the element of surprise for bringing in the newly-inducted Hall of Famer? Or is she really as in the dark as the rest of us? With her plea to Xavier to denounce recent rumours, we may find out very soon whether Jane has been entirely honest. Old School Entertainment
