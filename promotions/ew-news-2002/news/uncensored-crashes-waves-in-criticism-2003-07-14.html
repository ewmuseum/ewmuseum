Uncensored Crashes Waves in Criticism

14 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Primetime Television was not ready for the swearing, the blood, and the violence that Real and Graphic Entertainment brought on Sunday night... Thank God it was only one hour, not two. Real and Graphic Entertainment was prepared for what would come following the debut of Uncensored on Fox last night. The company had scheduled only a few things that took place on the show, one of them being the Robert Maxwell segment where the work 'cocky' was used to fool the censors. Of course, that drew quite an uprising and backlash on the company from the networks on television. Surprisingly, even Fox had something to say after the show aired, but other than ask the company to tune down what it is doing on television, Fox has agreed to a contract lasting until September 1. Real and Graphic Entertainment is free to say or do whatever it feels like on national television until that date. However, Uncensored was able to draw an impressive rating for it's first show, scoring a 1.9 according to Nielson Media Research. It is a number substantially lower than Fearless has been drawing, but the company was said to be expecting nothing more than a 1.5 from Uncensored. The basis and the wrestlers on the show were not of the caliber that Fearless has, and the company was fine with that. "We are doing Uncensored in order to give our fresh talent a chance to get some spotlight shone on them. It worked and we will continue to do it each week as long as it continues to work like it did this week," says Tom Mason, Owner of Real and Graphic Entertainment. No one has revealed what the company has planned for Uncensored next week, but many are assuming the product will be just as racy as last night's show was. Rageproject.com will have the card on this site when it comes out, guaranteed. Real and Graphic Entertainment
