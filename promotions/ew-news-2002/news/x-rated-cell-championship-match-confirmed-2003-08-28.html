X-Rated Cell Championship Match Confirmed

28 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Alot of rumours had been circulating about what Cameron Griffin had in mind when he promised a proposition for Sean Sterling tonight on Legends, but Evolution could never have imagined the explosive match he would sign for this coming Sundays PPV heralding from Twickenham, London. Evolutions Cell Division continues to grab the headlines, this time for the triple threat match which has been signed for X-Rated pitting three of its finest talents in one cell, at one time, for the right to win one title. Ever since becoming Cell Champion, Damon O'Halloran has stopped at nothing to get his own way and that has included angering the self professed 'Peoples Director' Cameron Griffin. Cameron had tried to communicate with words to no avail, Damon was his own man and slowly but surely over the course of time it has become apparent a leopard never changes its spots. So what next? Cue Sean Sterling. Since arriving in Evolution, Sterlings rise to prominance has been nothing short of remarkable. Only a month after his debut and he found himself competing against Damon O'Halloran for the Cell Title, on that night he left with the bitter taste of defeat, but had it not been for Terylanes interfearance things could have been very different. To some extent Terylane has grabbed the headlines for all the wrong reasons. Brash statements off camera though, have been coupled with determined and innovative performances inside the sickening structure of the Cell. Terylane may be a sandwich short of a picnic, but in the Cell he is one of the most dangerous men around. Having made his presense known during the recent Sterling vs O'Halloran title match, he now finds himself thrown into this title match having heard Sean Sterling ask for a shot at revenge. Its a gamble by Sean Sterling, but it seems the newcomer is confident in his own abilities so much so that he believes he can seek revenge and still have time to complete the job in hand; leaving X-Rated the Cell Champion. So whose the favourite? On form it has to be either O'Halloran or Sean Sterling. Cameron Griffin has not hidden the fact he wants to see the gold on the shoulder of a champion worthy of his title and a respectible corperate face. So write Terylane off? Only at your peril. In short this is a match built on the past, creating alot of excitement at present and promising to showcase the very future of Evolution and its Cell Division. My prediction? Don't expect Cameron Griffin to be too far away from the action.
