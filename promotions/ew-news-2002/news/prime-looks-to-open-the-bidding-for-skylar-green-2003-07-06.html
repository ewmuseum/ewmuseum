PRIME Looks To Open The Bidding For Skylar Green

6 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Skylar Green recently declared his free agency and laid down his own unique terms as regards to acquiring his services. PRIME Owner Taun Pham looks set to be quick off the mark, opening the bidding proceedings. With Skylar Green declaring his own terms just yesterday, it appears the PRIME owner Taun Pham is the first man to enter the bidding. Green stated that bids for his service rested on one key principal, charity. So far Pham has had little trouble landing the superstars he has pursued tying names such as Rik Reno and Michael Cade to multi-million dollar contracts. In fact, just earlier today Pham completed a deal to bring Jake Keetons younger brother Josh Cantrell to the company. The nature of Greens free agency has the PRIME owner intrigued. So far Pham has proved to be a generous owner, good hearted with concerns for his employees, despite this at times being exploited as a weakness. "I've had my eye on Skylar Green since PCW announced closure, so I intend to be first to enter the bidding." Pham said speaking via a satelite link up. "Mr Green named four potential worthy recipients when it came to money, so I wish to start by offering $5000 to each of them." The Make-A-Wish Foundation, The American Cancer Society, American Diabetes Association and the American Indian College Fund were listed by Green and stand to recieve his opening bid valuation each with Phams offer. Pham is still revelling in the vast fortune he made by selling his majority shares in Kobe based firm J-FX. Pham left his position in order to pursue his dreams and open a successful wrestling promotion in the West. "Also..." Pham added. "I am willing to install a clause in Skylar Greens contract that would see an additional $1000 donated to charity for each match he wins in PRIME." The PRIME owner has declared is interest and placed his official bid. Another drawing factor for Green could be that one former PCW star has already been signed, although his identity is still being closely guarded. Pham is reportedly close to sealing a second deal with another PCW star and is now sighting Green as a possible third addition. PRIME
