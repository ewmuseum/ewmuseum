[CEW] Thrill One-Hundred Fifty

12 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The one-hundred fiftieth episode of Cyber Extreme Wrestling's Thrill takes to the air Thursday night ... PITTSBURGH, Pennsylvania – The one-hundred fiftieth episode of Cyber Extreme Wrestling's flagship show, Thrill, will finally become a reality tonight, February 12, 2004. 5 years of [CEW] history, and 10 years of the company's existence, will be celebrated on this night as well, with a series of reflections and replays of events of years past. In brief, Cyber Extreme Wrestling began as the dream of Jeff Atkinson and Blake Coleman in mid 1999. The federation had enjoyed a brief run in 1998 as a rival federation to Atkinson's NWF. In 1998 the [CEW] name was used in angles to create a feud between NWF fighters. In 1999, it would be most of those same NWF fighters who would jump from Chris Powers' HCW to the [CEW]. Except for a few months in 2001, [CEW] has remained in constant operation since the opening in August of 1999. And the federation's weekly Thrill shows have been the timepiece that the federation measured by. Every week, always on schedule, week in and week out, for one-hundred and fifty episodes. Very few federations in the entire world can match that feat, even less can top it. While tonight represents an important benchmark in [CEW] history, it is the future as much as the past that will be showcased this evening. Five-time (and current) World Champion AC takes on Da*Link*Went, one of his most persistent competitors in the past few months. On the line is gold ... and pride for both men. North American Champion Weezle puts his title up for grabs in a three-way bout, with rookie phenom Ryan Anderson and longtime HCW veteran Deimos stepping into the ring. 'Showtime' Nick Alexander, one of the most recognized Cyber Extreme Wrestling stars, and current managing authority of the federation, steps into the ring with Shayne Gowers. Gowers holds two victories 'over' SNA, in tag matches where Alexander was not the man pinned either time. Now both step into singles competition against each other for the very first time. CEW Legend Mark Knight, once known as Top Gun, and one of the only men to hold every singles title in CEW history, will take on a man known only as Lord Skywolf. After Skywolf turned his back on his mentor at the last pay-per-view, Knight has a lot of payback to give. Cassandra Avery took AC to the very limit last month at Icestorm. But now she has taken on what some feel is an even bigger challenge, 6'9" and 285 lbs of Jakob Mayhem, the most dangerous newcomer in [CEW] history. Mayhem and his brothers have left a path of destruction in their wake since joining [CEW] ... and now a woman tries to stand in their way. A power-filled match of nightmarish proportions as [CEW] Hardcore Icon and 7-time Hardcore Champion Buzzsaw steps into the ring with another member of Total Mayhem, Ezra, and Jeremy Hunter, one of the most dangerous men inside the ring. Two rookies team up against the longest-running tag team, in name at least, in [CEW] history. Alex Coleman and Vincent Francis take on two of the three Presidents of Power, Brandon Plammondon and Sai Daichi. And finally ... not sanctioned by [CEW] (which is saying something!) ... an Fuck This Shit title match that will be rivaled only by their King of Death Matches championship match last month ... Allen Watts takes on Johnny Psychotic! All of this ... plus a look back at [CEW]'s history ... TONIGHT on HBO! Cyber Extreme Wrestling ...
