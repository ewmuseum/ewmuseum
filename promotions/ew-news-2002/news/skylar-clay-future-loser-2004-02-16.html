Skylar Clay. . . future loser?

16 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Though Skylar Clay believes to be the next breakout superstar of Razor Blade's Advantage Wrestling, the evidence is beginning to pile up that he is the next big loser of Advantage Wrestling. Since his debut in Advantage Wrestling, the self proclaimed "Limitless" Skylar Clay has boasted big game. Through his physical and mental threats to other superstars in Advantage, one would thing that Skylar Clay could live up to the high standards he has set for himself, but to Clay's demise, he is just not what he says he is. Let's recap. His debut match in AW was against Cody of A Tendency 2 Start Fires. Clay boasted big game about destroying Cody in the ring, Clay even went onto the famous television show Late Night with Jay Leno and threatened Leno with a 9mm pistol. Did Clay win? No. As most of you are aware, Advantage Wrestling kicked off its Desire pay per view a few weeks ago. Skylar Clay was pitted against the now popular Viper in a match for a title shot against Big Poppa at AW's recent Crossfire program. Rather then stepping up to the plate, Clay blew the match off and trained at the last minute. He win? No, plus Viper went ahead to nearly destroy Big Poppa for the X Championship. Now, we are brought to the recently produced Crossfire pay per view which featured the awaited Rumble match. Not only did Clay not live up to his promise of remaining in the top three men, he was eliminated shortly after entering. What's the point one might ask. Why bother talking about such a weak wrestler? The point that can be gathered from this evidence is that Skylar Clay is not only a has been, he is a never was. We at the Coalition Against Skylar Clay request any and all readers to sign our potetion in order to rid not only Advantage Wrestling, but all of the wrestling world of Skylar Clay.
