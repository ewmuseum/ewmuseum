Pressure Point 76 Report

17 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'd like to welcome all y'all to the Jeff Garvin Show... ACTION! PRESSURE POINT REPORT By: Keith Scott Zimmerman E-mail: [detail withheld] Date: 17.9.3 (taped 15.9.3.) The Big News: I'll sing it until it falls from #1 on the Action Event Modern Rock Chart: Jeff is your Jeff is your Jeff is your only god. Title Changes: None. Face/Heel Turns: None. Best Match: 5. Jeff kept Marlee Matlin from cheating while Pierre & I chopped Ramsey for a five count, breaking at the designated time. Ramsey tried to be the oldest whitest Guerrero but Jeff put a stop to that, allowing us to legally chop Conrad for all five seconds before relenting as per the rules. Time Bomb connects. Ramsey--and the audicity of this is really quite deplorable--pulls that trick where instead of tagging you clap your hands together. Good thing Jeff knew to look out for that from Lawler's early days. Duncan cheated his way in but fell head-first into a chair twice and in order to keep him healthy I put him down easy with Zimmerman CI. I know what you're thinking, and your thank yous are gratitude enough. That and your money. BUY THE SHIRT! I can do better than #6, ya bastards! Worst Match: 4. Mass squash, Vincent hit a second-rope pussy-assed version of the Slash & Burn before delivering The Most Overblown Fistdrop In Sports Entertainment Today for the victory. Absurdity of the Show: The level of kayfabe smashing in the Robinson promo only to be followed up by a participant jobbing. Moment of the Show: Us beating the shit out of the Dangerettes. Let that be a lesson to the rest of yas. Breakdown: Ew, someone pulled an L7 on Brian. Anyway, he ran down Avalon which in this business means Max came out. So...1) Max won with the Jeffshooter. I'll have his belt for that someday--B obviously...Your champion has arrived...Sharc cut a Mankind promo and Fox promised he'd get jumped...Kayden was looking for A!M...Deft went whining to Captain Zoloft about the Ninja teaming...Minio & Halloway are in line for pushes towards my B title. Kayfabe? What's kayfabe?...We may have Jeff kicking Grandpa's ass at the PPV too; thy havet been warned...A!M showed up... 2) You know, something's missing from the new kid's entrance, and that thing is MORE COWBELL. Deft's switched off from "Ashes", too. Good, quick but not fast face/face; Deft wins with Double Jeopardy. So what's happening to that push?...Deft and Ninja K? Still gaining acceptance...Coral & Jeff talked smack...Sabotage (well, the kid, at any rate) wanted another crack at the LoD...Ellen & Anne: Cover Girls for the first Action Magazine...Scott looked for Daze--try the lost & found, John Boy...The Two Man Ratings Trip came out and babbled about something, then there was a big fustercluck with Levine, Sharc, LLB, Bill, Mezz, Fox, and the 37th Airborne vaguely disguised as a tag match. LLB ran down Mezz but got nowhere...Coral & Allison have BENNIFERED THAT SHIT DOWN; previous comment thus fulfills my Fill-In-The-Blank THAT SHIT DOWN quota for this week's report...Some guy kicked some other guy's asses...Ringer & A!M are in cahoots. Cahoots is kinda a slut then...The guy from two segments ago is coming our general direction...Pleh tried cheering up the Hamburglar...Daze stalked Scott...Paulton segment. Rather than let my teeth escape my mouth to try and reach around to eat my brains, I'll just move on...Someone kidnapped Jessica. I myself was busy listening to Cash's American IV and there's a lot of collaborative evidence over here by the throttle to prove so...3) Avalon's using a lot of Regal uppercuts as transitional, and Jannety sells a short lariat. Blue Thunder for 2 and goes to the camel clutch before Coral steals my SHORYUKEN with a Regalcut substituted. Cash, or Visa, Hamburglar, because Keith Scott Zimmerman doesn't take American Express. Avalon reeled off the Play of the Day and Lesson One but he got sloppy and Prodigy laid him to waste with the Wish I Was KSZ Driller. IBJ hit the Prodigy (coincidentally enough) on him and you can book that for the PPV. Good, excellent given more time... The Daze & A!M storylines wrapped up for the evening with the heels laying out the faces...Jeff dropped off a tape...4) See above...HardCase is behind the kidnapping, which is quite hard to take seriously given the fact they have those magic phones where you can hear both ends of the convo...Sabotage didn't get their rematch, as Joe explicitly explained to them...Raven's little renamed brother cut his I Am PIW, Therefore I Am King Shit promo of the week. Sing it with me, kids: BATHROOM BREAK!...LLB was both himself AND Reed, so you can do both #1 AND #2 and just clean yourself out completely before the long ride home or walk to the fridge...Max got orders to be somewhere...Grampa Simpson started telling this story about how nickels used to have bees on them and Shelbyville had a different name and the ferry only cost a bee (give me five bees for a quarter you'd say) when Jeff shut him up and showed Ramsey crying at the wife's grave. So, in lieu of Jeff beating him up AGAIN, we decided to do it for him....5) See above...Hardcase/Max for the B next week. Grade: B-. Pretty segheavy this week, though the undercard for the PPV seems to be pretty terra firma. Not a bad show per se but if you missed it I wouldn't say you missed anything earth-shaking. Show Results: 1) [BANTAM] Max Danger s. Brian James 2) Deft s. Bret Holloway 3) Clayton Prodigy d. Coral Avalon 4) Trey Vincent d. Woodchuck Bill 5) International Workrate Connection d. the Dangerettes (I pinned Duncan off of Zimmerman CI) Action Pressure Point 76
