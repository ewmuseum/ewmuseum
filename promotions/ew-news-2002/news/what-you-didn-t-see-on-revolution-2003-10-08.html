What You Didn't See On ReVolution!

8 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
When ReVolution left the air on UPN Monday night, the cameras didn't stop rolling. What the fans at home didn't see, was the fall out from Hin See identifying his own son Taun Pham as the man who instigated his kidnapping. The results were explosive and word is that PRIME officials will screen the extra footage on the opening of ReVolution this week. PRIME itself is in a state of shock. Why would Taun Pham inflict this fate on his father? What happens next? As of this point in time, Taun Pham has not been officially charged, but he is being interrogated by the police concerning his role in the abduction. Also interestingly, he has been included in the final edition of the preview billing for ReVolution XIII in Edmonton and is still expected to be present on the show. As for the other PRIME co-owner Sebastian Starr, he has been hounded by reporters trying to get the inside scoop on just what happened when ReVolution finished last night. We all know that Sebastian, along with Mr Motoki, Detectives Stone and Cassidy, plus a handful of EMT's were in the same room as Pham and his Pops when Hin See regained consciousness. Unfortunately no one is revealing what happened behind the scenes. In other post ReVolution news, Lance Jackson is said to be seething after Tony Rolo drilled him in the head with a chairshot in the aftermath of their Tag Team loss to Mark Blaze and Rock Startling. Startling also set a new benchmark in PRIME, by becoming the first superstar to rack up 10 wins in the company. Could it be the start of another winning streak? Then fans seem to think so, with 84% voting that Startling would not lose again in 2003 in an online poll. In a seperate poll, 57% of fans think that the English Luchadore and Ian English are one of the same, despite the screening of a segment that shows both men together in a hotel room. Ian English is headlining the card with Syn as they battle for the 5-Star Title inside of a Steel Cage. Will EL appear or will Syn get sweet revenge for her loss at Fatal Flaw? On the roster front, it appears the PRIME officials are once more ringing in the changes with new recruits, the latest of whom is none other than the much sought after free agent TAJ Escobar. "The Misguided Angel" and former IWA and ACWF talent signed a lucrative deal with PRIME two days ago, rejecting offers from several rival promotions and is set to meet up with the rest of the roster in Alberta tomorrow. Rumor has it that PRIME is also in negotiations with two other superstars, with a view to bringing them into the company in the near future. PRIME
