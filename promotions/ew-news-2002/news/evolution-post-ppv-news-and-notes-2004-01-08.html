eVolution Post-PPV News and Notes

8 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
eVolution has released today quite a bit of news. First and foremost as rumored for over a month now an agreement was finally signed with the United States government that allows eVolution to return its product to the United States of America with all criminal charges against the corporation dismissed. This will of course take effect immediately as Legends LXII’s preview has been released originating live from the American Airlines Arena in Miami, Florida. The show will feature in a good deal of the new talent that has recently arrived in eVolution as well as a Tag Team Title match. Tyler Davidson has already been confirmed to be at the show this coming week with an announcement he said concerning one of the Committee members currently in charge of eVolution. Tyler has already stated that the Committee will remain in charge of eVolution events as he has no intention of returning in that capacity at this time. The votes have also finally been tallied as the Year End Awards have been unveiled. To see the results there is now a link in the Front Office where you can view all the winners and in some cases losers of the year. And finally a series of releases were also made over the past couple of days. Chris Jeopardy who had been with the company only a couple of weeks has already been released from his contract and former eVolved Champion, Scott Evans requested and was granted his release as well.
