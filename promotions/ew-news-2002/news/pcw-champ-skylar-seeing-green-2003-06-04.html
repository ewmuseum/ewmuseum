PCW Champ Skylar Seeing Green

4 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Following this week's edition of Tuesday Turmoil, it's been announced that PCW's new world champion, Skylar Green, shall finally begin to collect... Sources have confirmed that PCW's current world championship titleholder Skylar Green has finally started accepting paychecks from Primetime Championship Wrestling. While this may seem to be an impertinent detail, longtime PCW fans will recall that Green publically waived his pay in protest to the former Front Office's taxation policy. The Front Office has since disbanded but Green never requested to receive his paychecks, insisting the money be spent to hire new talent. However, Green is now insisting that he may once again collect his wages as he hopes to support a family that he is helping to 'build.' Skylar Green has officially claimed legal guardianship of three young women, whose father figures have since been brutally murdered. The two father figures, by the names of Julio Garcia and Marcus Jackson, the original Rhythm and Rhyme of UWWC fame, treated Green as the family, the family he never had. In hopes of returning what has been given to him, the PCW champion hopes to honor them by caring for their three children. It may be noted that Skylar Green is not requesting to be compensated for past missed checks but is only requesting to be paid from this point onward. Primetime Championship Wrestling
