Chris Williams Memorial Cup

8 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
the fourth annual CWMC hit the airwaves earlier this week, and on the card we saw wrestlers from many different federations ... independants, PTC affiliates, and OSE... take part together for one night only to honour the memory of Chris Williams, better known as "Kain Threat" of the PWW, who was killed in a car accident in 1999. Chris Williams Memorial Cup Four years ago, [name withheld], former president of the PWW, organised a special tournament to remember former PWW TV Title Champion Chris Williams with the first Chris Williams Memorial Cup. It brought together people from many different federations and regions at the time, for a special week long fan extravaganza, culminating in a thirty two man elimination tournament. For three years after this, PWW has continued to hold the Chris Williams Memorial Cup, bringing many more federations (GIWF/UWF, WWA, TWC/TWF, and more) and wrestlers into the 'family'. This year, wrestlers from PWW, XUW, OSW, UWF, FA, WWA, 4WW, FAW, GW, CW, EPW and WMW converge on Calgary, Alberta to take part in this historic annual event!
