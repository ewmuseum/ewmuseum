[CEW] - Looking For More For Rumble ...

27 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The annual 30-man Slamfest rumble is looming on the horizon for Cyber Extreme Wrestling. The current roster is preparing, but federation owners want to add a little spice to the mix ... A few weeks ago, Cyber Extreme Wrestling announced that the company was engaging in a nationwide talent search in an effort to bolster their roster. Thus far, for reasons that have not been announced to the public, the search has been lackluster, at best. [CEW] has decided to engage in a new course of action in order to bolster their roster for their upcoming pay-per-view, Slamfest. At Slamfest the federation will hold their second annual Slamfest rumble, in which 30 men enter the ring. The last man remaining in the event is guaranteed a title shot later this year, likely at the next announced [CEW] Pay-Per-View. This past Tuesday saw [CEW] superstars already beginning to prepare for the Slamfest rumble, as Johnny Psychotic challenged four other men to a training match. The match was interrupted by both Buzzsaw and Allen Watts, which led to a four-way match being signed for this coming Tuesday, with freshly signed Ryan Anderson completing the foursome. But all the preparation in the world could not prepare [CEW] superstars if some new, fresh talent showed up in Atlanta, Georgia on the 16th of September. [CEW] officials have indicated that the federation is searching for new faces to join the ranks of the rumble, to mix things up and give their fans someone new to see. The same sources have indicated that, with a good performance, these participants could be offered full [CEW] contracts. Do you think you have what it takes to step in the ring with [CEW] stars and with a World Title Shot on the line? [CEW] - Cyber Extreme Wrestling
