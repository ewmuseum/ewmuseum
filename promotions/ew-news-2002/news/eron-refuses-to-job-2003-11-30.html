Eron Refuses to Job

30 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The real truth behind Eron's sudden retirement What's next for Xias now that Eron is gone forever? fWo fans around the globe were shocked when last night at Unleashed: Mob Rules, Eron was suddenly defeated by fWo talent Spyder in a Retirement Match. This seemed to come out of nowhere- Eron wasn't feuding with Spyder, and in fact had two long-running feuds going on (with Martyr and Xias)- and Spyder was a fellow heel. But now, the shocking truth behind the retirement has been revealed. As it turns out, Eron was placed into the Martyr and Xias feuds with the intentions of creating two new face stars. Eron was growing rapidly displeased with his position in the company, however, saying that he was being used to do two big jobs in two staight months (to Martyr at Countdown in December and Xias at Body Count in Janaury), and the fWo had no real future for him after that except as a continuing job guy. Rumor has it that a face turn against fellow Rogues member High Flyer, in which again he would be putting someone over huge to try and make a new star (at Cyberslam, according to my sources), was the proverbial last straw for Eron. Through shady circumstances, he managed to get out of his contract following Unleashed, where he would put over the one wrestler who he wanted to put over- fellow heel sensation Spyder- in a Retirement Match. Now without an fWo contract but still plenty of money to live on for the future, Eron is said to have washed his hands from the wrestling business once and for all. I have been told by my sources not to expect Eron to show up Monday for Action!'s Pressure Point, simply becauase he just doesn't have to. It really seems like this is the final end of Eron. Meanwhile, this leaves the question of just what is next for the careers of Martyr and Xias, two men who's characters were depending on the Eron feud for many months, now left without anything at all to do. Can they recover from such a huge hit, and how? While the fate of Martyr is unknown, Xias had seemingly heard rumblings of Eron's impending retirement last weekend, which is why he skipped on Ruahh~!! and his mini-feud with Aimz to kill time was dropped suddenly. Now, Xias is expected to return on tommorow night's Ruahh~!!. The question is, what will he do now that Eron's gone for good? fWo
