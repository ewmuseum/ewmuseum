Turmoil Starts, Ends in Turmoil: PCW's Back

21 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

And now, with their creation gone rampant, the doctors are gone. [All, aka Duke Williams, new head of PCW, directed me to submit this here. So here ya go... Thanks.] After six months of obscurity, two legendary wrestling geniuses devised a way to bring this once glorious fed back to life... But what they got was a monster. And now, with their creation gone rampant, the doctors are gone. In a stunning turn of events, it was learned, just hours before FOX threatened to cut PCW if Turmoil was delayed, that PCW owner, and long time wrestling captain of industry, Ron Starr, had indeed been arrested on counts of tax fraud and other financial illegalities. And the same went for PCW president, and long time Hall of Famer, Tyler Nelson, as well. Upon hearing of this, the Front Office (what was left of it) had no choice but to act, airing PCW Turmoil, live, with hopes of someone to fill in their boots, if only temporarily. And someone did fill in these boots. With cowboy boots. Yes, no one saw it coming, but alas, the fans were treated to find that the acting president of PCW was to be none other than...Duke Williams! (Former PCW President, former PCW World Champion, and Hall of Fame inductee.) And the show started off with a bang, and things never really cooled down... Judgement picked up the win over Terlayne Sterling, with both rookies looking strong in the first match of the new era of PCW. We were then treated to an ever-so-odd promo by RAH, PCW's superhero, who was to be an ever present figure throughout the night, and perhaps the most popular PCW star to grace the roster. Skitzophrenia ran wild on PCW once more, with Skitzo devising a dubious plan to spoil the night, which somehow involved tempting Bungle, the big, homosexual, child-molesting teddy bear, with gay porno. Next up, Shaun Styles and Brian Arson met with words, and would later meet in the ring. The McCain brothers put in their two cents, one more than the other. Products of the System were unchained (literally) and talked the talk, but were put to the test by the Mega Soaring Arrows, who took them to task in a tag match. Through disqualification, POS, PCW's prison couple, one, albeit unfairly. Another Rent-A-Hero promo, followed by a few words between the Mega Soaring Arrows, and their fellow Japanese countrymen, PCW official, Referree Koji. Skitzo plans were foiled (though not before pruchasing over $500 worth of pornography), and up next, PCW's resident redneck, and former Director of Redneck Affairs, Abner, came back, and immediately got to the task of drinking... The return of Skylar Green! Starting with one of the many acts of harrassment to be committed to the World Champ by various newer members of the roster, he was confronted by one, "The Buzz," Cal Roberts, who expressed his dismay toward the blind man. In a spectacular match, newcomer and recent Mexican immigrant, Fantoche, managed to pull a win over Abner, though not without the help of a foreign object (of sorts). Maddux tried to get in a word with an odd character in Michael Jacques (a black Brit who considers himself a white gentlemen), and E2 threw in their two cents about the main event. In what was probably the most competitive match of the night, newcomers Shaun Styles and Brian Arson squared off, with neither able to come off with the victory. Jason Rhodes made his introduction, coming out of years of retirement. Then, backstage, Arson made a pass of his own at the world champ. Bugnles showed his...uh...gay side, by taking naked photos of PCW newcomer, Judgement...but FOX refuses to re-air the piece...very disturbing... Michael Jacques, still refusing to have a sanitary interview, drinks milk... Jay Phoenix defates the mammoth Somoan, Awfa, putting himself toward the top of PCW's list of best competitors. Next up, Abner releases some steam, fresh off his loss from Fantoche, and no, this redneck is NOT happy... Safiya, PCW's newest female talent, had her own time, leaving us with more of that good old jargon we were used to from the likes of a former Egyptian PCWer... After this, R-A-H plays video games, and in the process, saves the sex life of a happily married couple. Then, Skin and Scar, of POS, get in the champs face...and Sky dishes it right back out, obviously fed up by that point. And after this, R-A-H comes to comfort him. Uh...kinda... Abner and Jacques kick off PCW's first new-aged foodfight, and the Brit gets the short end of teh stick. Toomes and PCW newcomer, Jonah, get into it, and this time, the former OSW champion does not get the last laugh... After some freak seemingly destroys PCW's newly built Hall of Fame building, Jon Kanon (whom, by the way, ~Rocks!~) had a bit to say, much to the fan's delight, and sent out a warning to those who would destroy his beloved company. Then, the McCain's are back at it... Finally, in the main event, Brothers in Blood and TD&C get taken to task by the reigning Tag Team Champs, E2, only for the lights to dim down, and... The return of Ivan Stanislav. Probably not. But we'll see... The Return of Tuesday Turmoil, HERE!
