Xavier Kannon/OSE Mystery

3 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With no source or foundation... Why is the rumour still hanging around? With the recent fall-out from PCW's abrupt closure, and the Indy fed PWC going out of business, many rumours are flying around as to where the federations' substantial talent may wind up. Already the OSE has been linked with names such as Clark Carlisle, Karina Wolfenden and Paula Michelle Smith... As well as the possible returns of Frankie Bones and Propaganda, not to mention the signings of Magnum Wolf and Angelo Deville... But amidst the speculation of to where free agents will be heading, news of a retired wrestler's possible signing has found it's way from the lips of an anonymous source and into the ears of several wrestling journalists. "The news sounded a little sketchy," reported ex-'wrestlingtattler.com' reporter Jim Smitty, "But others have heard the same thing, and there's no smoke without fire." The superstar being touted as the next addition to the OSE roster that's already brimming with talent...? ...None other than former PCW Champion - Xavier Kannon. Nobody in the wrestling world has seen sight of Kannon since PCW's Spring Fling PPV, and all reports suggest that he's left the country, after selling up and donating most of his accumulated wealth to various good causes. "Hell, the guy could be living in a cardboard box somewhere in Mexico City now for all I know!" added Smitty, after admitting he had no idea where this rumour had emerged from. OSE's Jane Reagan was quick to deny the rumour, announcing there was no fabrication whatsoever. "OSW, and as far as I know, OSF, have had no contact with Mr. Kannon or ANY representatives of his. I, along with most people, have no idea where he is... And as far as the business goes, he's announced his retirement months ago. That should say it all." With the rumour seemingly being without truth, or a named source, the question now begs... How did this mistruth snowball and become widely reported? Well, as nobody is willing to step forward, and there is no way of contacting Xavier himself, it will have to go down as a mystery. Old School Entertainment
