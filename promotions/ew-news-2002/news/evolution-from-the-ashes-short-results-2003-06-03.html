eVolution From The Ashes Short Results

3 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With eVolution rapidly approaching its one year anniversary they recently aired in full its latest pay per view endeavor, From The Ashes. This show would have a huge impact on what the future of eVolution would have in store for the superstars and fans alike. Here are the short results for the show. Standard Match Siege defeated Seara Cell Match Trevor Norman defeated Xero After the return of the Cell match Trevor Norman looked to continue his assault on Xero when he was interrupted by the sudden appearance of Geneva and Marcus Escobar, better known as The Faithless in thReat. Xander was later seen talking to his former tag team partner Joshua Krowe, the man who was now the manager of Xander’s opponent in the main event. eVolved Title Ladder Match Hedges defeated Dane, Profusion, and Malcolm Luther Xavier© Six Team Tag Match Tribute and The BodySnatchers defeated St. Violence, America’s Guardians, Team Network, and Savage Society Continental Title Match Sean Mason defeated Asis© Tag Team Title Hell In A Cell Match Twisted Reality© defeated The Syndicate Flaming Table Match Shocka defeated Ronan Pierce Grudge Match Thug roC defeated Jason Jameson Grudge Match James Kimson defeated Erin Christoph No DQ Match For Control of eVolution Tyler Davidson defeated Daniel S. Universal Title Hardcore Match Xander defeated Scott Frost© All in all it was another top performance for many of the stars of eVolution on a night that saw three titles change hands and the control of eVolution finally go back to the rightful person.
