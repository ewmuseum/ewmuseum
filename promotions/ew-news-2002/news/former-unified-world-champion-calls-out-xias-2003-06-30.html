Former Unified World Champion Calls Out Xias

30 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Former Unified World Champion and four-time CSWA World Champion Hornet was approached for comment on fWo US Champion Voran Xias's recent interfed challenges. Hounded by reporters following his no-show as part of the CSWA's BATTLE of the BELTS pay-per-view Main Event, former CSWA World Champion Hornet remained mum regarding his status in the CSWA, giving only a "No comment." Speculation continues on whether Hornet will appear at CSWA PRIMETIME in Anaheim, and whether he has lost what it reportedly the most lucrative deal in the sport. However, Hornet was willing to discuss other issues in wrestling, not related to the CSWA. He briefly commented on CSWA superstars involved in the weekend's fWo Unleashed show, including former "ClaimStakers" stablemate, Eli Flair, who was involved in the Main Event. "It wasn't the night for Deac, Trip or Eli, was it? That's how it goes sometimes, especially when someone tips the odds in their favor with with a chair or a title belt to the head." Asked about Flair's upcoming title match with "Kodiak" Vic Creed, Hornet said, "I'm not one to bet against Eli in a title match. But he's got his hands full not just with Creed, but with everything going on around the two of them." A question about Poison Ivy elicited another "No comment." Hornet was linked to Ivy romantically on-and-off for several years. A final question about Voran Xias's recent interleague challenges got the most out of the CSWA franchise superstar. "I'm probably not the best one to ask, but since you did," Hornet said. "When I challenged other 'World' Champions in 1993, it was to create something larger. Did it raise my visibility and the visibility of the CSWA? Absolutely. But I also had the backing of the league I worked for and the other leagues involved. And I didn't put any stipulations on the matches either." "Fact is, Voran's throwing a load of crap against a wall and trying to see what sticks. He can't get over the way he wants, so he's decided that he's going to steamroll the fans, and expose as much of the business as he can, as long as it gets him what he wants." "Voran's not unlike a hundred other 'up-and-comers' who hit the national scene for the first time and decide that their mere presence means that they're 'owed' a World Title, and a truckload of cash, and respect. Of course, then it takes a steel chair for Mr. Cheap Heat to win the title he's currently got around his waist. If he's that good, then why couldn't he beat Brand Frontier straight up?" "Because he's scared. Scared that he's gonna fail. And that leads him to being even more cocky, even more provoking, then before. How better to put himself over than to have the fWo ship in other champions to feed to him?" "But when you dance with the devil, he always eventually comes for his due. What happens when somebody throws away your script, Voran? What happens when the thrill of appearing in front of an fWo audience wears off and somebody decides to change things up. You're the one 'shooting', right?" "If the vaunted Voran were truly concerned about taking on the best, then why isn't he wrestling fWo superstars? Why didn't he show up on list for the NFW season for the ULTRATITLE about to start? Eli Flair did. Triple X did. No, Voran's not interested in competing... he's only worried about being seen." Voran Xias is scheduled to compete against an unnamed challenger on tonight's fWo Monday Night Ruahh~!! Hornet is scheduled to face former Presidential Champion Cameron Cruise on CSWA PRIMETIME, airing tomorrow night.
