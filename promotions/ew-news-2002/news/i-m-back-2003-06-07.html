I'm back!!!!

7 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Did you miss me? Of course you did! Who could ever forget the lovable former president of NTICW! Yeah, NTICW is long gone from my mind, and so is working with the somewhat legendary Evan Levine, but what have I got up my sleeves now!? Mick P. returns! That's right folks, I've made my return to the wrestling spotlight, Mick P. is back! But what am I up to nowadays? Well, I've solidified my spot at the top of the wrestling world by becoming a fellow member of the eW-news magazine staff! Expect an opinion piece from me once a week as I run down the news in today's world of wrestling. First off, Survivor III, is it just me or has this thing gotten a bit old? The first Survivor in fWo was great... the second was ok... but now just because they decide to go the tag team way with it, it doesn't make it much better then the first two. But then again, it is getting huge ratings, but so does everything else that has to do with fWo, so maybe it'll have a chance for a fourth run, but after that, I doubt fWo will try anymore. Predictions: I'm gonna do a few predictions on the matter however, I predict that Gold team pulls an upset win this week, causing the Green team to vote again, the next team gone will undoubtedly be Tim Walden and Jane Matheson, in a competition where only the strong survive, this has to be the almost sure thing. Other happenings, tA finally aired it's long awaited show just over a week late. It was pretty good actually, surprisingly good to say the least, considering it took a stretch from the normal fighting and added some wrestling into the mix, the show was good overall, but was missing a few elements, including more Token Weed segments on the show, by far one of my favorite tA fighters of all time. The Show, as it's called, was good and it'll be nice to see what tA has in store for the next "The Show", which airs on TNN. Ok, so what, I'm just basically working my way through a few of my favorite federations or organizations in the wrestling/fighting world and speaking on them, I know that some will criticize me since it seems that I'm following no real pattern what so ever, that just makes me unpredictable you know. As for other things that I'm poking my interest around, Semore Smoke gone from PCW lately, one of the guys I worked with in the past, that and PCW obviously sinking in the ratings dramatically... When head to head with OSE's new combination of fighting and wrestling, I doubt it'll stay afloat for long, changes need to be made in PCW, and I believe the Wrestling company is just pushing too many young stars fast, keep your established stars at the top, and work the younger stars at the bottom of the card more often, examples of this include Dorn Tracer, who hasn't been in the federation for too long, but is already an established upper-carder, a spot that should be saved for people like The Pharaoh, Skylar Green, Violence Jack, etc. Speaking of Skylar Green, new world champion? I feel that The Pharaoh deserves such a title, in fact, he deserves a shot at it right now! The Pharaoh will have the title next, and remember, that's a Mick P. guarantee right there for you. OSE is running great... Davetta Inc. however in OSF just seems like nothing right now... it's making huge headways in the Fighting organization but hasn't won a single match yet... what's up with that!? As for Tempest becoming the first OSF Champ, I believe she worked hard for it and by far will join Nova at the ranks of women who have won Fighting Championships, I mean, who can forget when she won the tA title? Tempest will go a long way in her career, and has done so since her days in fWo, I can only expect more from her. As for OSW, I haven't followed that place for two or so months, but I hear that Boda is the World Champion, a guy I know real well. Former PCW World Champion, as well as PCW2 World Champion, Boda is by far an established star at the top of the wrestling world and he'll only get better as he goes along, as for Ivan Stanislav, by far a great wrestler and will only become better as well. Hmm... what else can I talk about or spout on about? I guess that's basically just it... I hope you readers enjoyed reading my column, and if not? Then you can just suck my white ass then... I'll see you all back next week! Mick P. Direct all comments on this column towards [e-mail withheld]. fWo Survivor III
