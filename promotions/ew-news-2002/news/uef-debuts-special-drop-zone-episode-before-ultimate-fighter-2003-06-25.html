UEF Debuts Special Drop Zone Episode before Ultimate Fighter PPV

25 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Three days before Ultimate Fighter, UEF is forced to air an emergency live event known as 'Drop Zone' to fill the brackets. Sunday on Protocol, Superstar's Hiddukel and Ice Cube were supposed to have a match to advance either one farther into the Ultimate Fighter brackets, and send one to the PPV this month. But the match didn't happen, so what was the UEF to do? Ice Cube, who was supposed to be at Protocol, was waiting at a private New York airport, and could not leave, due to violent weather and harsh conditions that left all airplanes in New York grounded until the storm passed. Duke, when reached for comment, had this to say: "Sunday was a let doen in the UEF. Hiddukel and Ice Cube was set to go down, but due to storm, Ice Cube couldn't get here. It's a let down to the fans, and to the staff here." The UEF will issue a Thursday shown known as 'Drop Zone' to fill up the Hiddukel / Ice Cube match. It will also bring other people to the card, all of them un-named. the.UEF
