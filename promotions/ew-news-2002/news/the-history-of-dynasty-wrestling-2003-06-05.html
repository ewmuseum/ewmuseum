The History of Dynasty Wrestling

5 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Look into the past of Dynnasty Wrestling, from their beginnings as a regional Indy fed, no the national status they now enjoy. Dynasty Wrestling began as the EWF (Extreme Wrestling Federation) in 1992. The EWF was a small Indy promotion that ran out of and around Florida. EWF was a small fed that had a small but loyal following. The EWF was never a competitor in the wrestling world with the big feds such as WWF and WCW. EWF had a mix of extreme and technical wrestlers that always had memorable matches. In 1993 EWF crowned Mike Vitucci as the World Champion. Vitucci went on to become an icon amongst the EWF fans holding the title for over a year. When in 1993 Vitucci finally lost the World title to [name withheld] who was at that time was the top heel in the company, some say that Vitucci had lost his edge. Vitucci who was a great technical wrestler was dismayed as no top feds had come in for him, and his matches started to lose quality. The top EWF star was losing his edge and the EWF officials were faced with a dilemma. They immediately began to push some of there younger wrestlers so that they would have more main event wrestlers. The EWF was never the same as the younger wrestlers became even more extreme to try and get over with the fans quicker and the technical wrestlers were soon buried under the extreme youngsters. While the fans enjoyed the extreme action at first, they soon got bored of seeing people get hit by chairs and going through tables each night and soon stopped coming to watch. At the end of 1995 the EWF was losing money and the owner Steve Morris decided to sell up. After waiting along time for a buyer, Morris was forced to keep lowering his asking price. When a buyer finally came in for the company the asking price was next to nothing. Rick Colley was the man that bought EWF in the summer of 96'. He began to tone down the violence in the product and began to introduce more wrestling to the product. Colley also managed to secure a deal with a local wrestling school that allowed him to bring in the local talent. The fans started to come back and watch and the EWF built up a relatively large support. As the money finally began to come in for the EWF there were still big problems. WWF and WCW were now two huge companies and were competing in a ratings war with no other wrestling companies even came near them. Colley set about expanding the EWF and began to book shows in other areas then the EWF was used to being at. This brought in more fans but did not always generate the same amount of money as had been made in the local area. In 1998 with the EWF on the rise disaster struck. Colley was killed when a car hit him. As Colley's only son had no interest in owning a wrestling company he sold EWF to the current owner Jon who kept his identity as owner secret to the fans and competed as a wrestler. With the WWF and WCW still at the top of the ladder there were now some challenging feds such as the fWo. EWF remained a small fed with minor success and travelled around the south of the country promoting their product. EWF grew in 1998 to 2000 more than it had in its history. In 2000 how ever the competition was stronger than ever with The Asylum and jOlt also joining the fray as the top feds. 26th August 2001 is the day that many believe the EWF was really started. That day and the following weeks many of the top EWF stars of today were signed. Havoc, Chris Cage, Chooch, Danny Y2D as well as former EWF stars such as former World Champion Matt Anarchy, former EWF International Champion Arron Bomb, former Tag Team Champions Hardcore Harry and SSB and also former Tag Team Champions 'Pretty Boy' Peters and 'Bad Boy' Billy Thomas. The EWF went higher than it had been before getting its own time slot on TV and even releasing its first PPV Doomsday. Also during this period was the invasion of the EWF by the rival ECF organisation. ECF (Extreme Championship Federation), which was led by Hardcore Harry, started up at an EWF show and boasted such talents as SSB and later EWF defector the ECF World Champion T.J Dreamer, who came within a whisker of winning the EWF World Title. Havoc then captured the vacant EWF World Title at EWF Carnage the second ever PPV just as more new talent was arriving in the EWF including such names as Chris Cage, The Spiker and BeYoNdEr. At the end of 2000 Jon made his identity as owner known to the fans in a dramatic heel turn helping out Havoc. The EWF then entered the year 2002 but Jon knew that is the EWF was to ever become a big well known fed that it needed to change. There fore he took the first steps and changed the name of the fed to Dynasty Wrestling. With Dynasty Wrestling Jon offered a new brand of exciting entertainment to the Wrestling world. In the first ever PPV under the new Dynasty Wrestling name BreakDown finished controversially. Chris Cage managed to win the World Title from Havoc in a scaffold match however there was tension backstage. Chris Cage was stripped of the belt two weeks later after failing to show up for four shows and going AWOL. Jon Storm decided that at the next Dynasty Wrestling PPV extravaganza Destiny, that the World Title would be put on the line in the annual Destiny Rumble. Adam Striker entered the Destiny Rumble number one and managed to survive to the last two wrestlers until being eliminated by Genocide. Suprise entrys in the rumble included the returning World Champions Havoc and Chris Cage as well as SSB, Hardcore Harry, Slider and Python. Dynasty Wrestling
