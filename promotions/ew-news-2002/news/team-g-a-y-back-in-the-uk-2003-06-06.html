Team G.A.Y back in the UK

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Team G.A.Y are back on British soil, but vow to return to America to prove themselves. At 7:50am this morning Team G.A.Y of fWo’s ‘Survivor’ competition landed at Heathrow airport, London after early eliminated from the tournament. G.A.Y received six votes from their fellow team mates, resulting in G.A.Y being the first team to leave Survivor III. Steven Fray of SKY News caught up with Gary Royale, who had the following to say “me and Colin are very tired. We wanted to get away sooner, but fWo staff said it would be wise to hang around and talk to a few wrestling magazines and the local press, but as it turned out nobody is interested in us” Colin Khââmp began to cry and had to be consoled by Gary. “Oh, we’ll be back in America, be it on any network or any promotion, were not done with these yanks yet!” Colin added Colin and Gary have never won a wrestling match on American soil, and this time around didn’t even make it to the ring. “Maybe it’s a good job we didn’t get to wrestle, as we would have destroyed any scally who got in our path!” Gary aggressively added. eW News staff were able to reach fWo fans for reactions. “The two gay dudes?.....man they sucked” said one angry fan, “I’m gonna laser-pen their eyes when they come back” Team G.A.Y are scheduled to return to America and indeed the fWo to be a part in the final vote to determine the winner. In the meantime they are going to work on their in-ring work and watch the rest of the Survivor contest eyeing up each contestant (No not admire their asses) to see who is a strong candidate for a winner. fWo fWo Survivor III
