Thug roC speaks on Whethers issue

5 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thug roC steps up to states his claims during Whethers issue Thug roC speaks As it has been very well documented that Shane Whethers was close to ink a deal with PRIME before making the switch to eVolution wrestling, it seems that Thug roC may have had a lot to do with the switch. It was known at the time that Thug roC was indeed in the front office at PRIME at the time while competing in eVo. During the negotiations with Whethers, Thug roC was very unhappy in the direction that PRIME was going, and a lot of people were unhappy in Thug roC's actions. Speculation states that Thug roC might've been the one to persuade Whethers away from PRIME, only days before Thug roC would officially leave eVo. RoC had this to say, when asked if he had something to do with Whethers' eVo signing. "Shane Whethers simply saw the light. He saw that eVolution Wrestling was the place he needed to be at. If he wants to shine in eVo, then fine by me. I had nothing to do with him turning down PRIME." Thug roC hurried to get by the reporters, but was stopped for a few more questions. He was then asked about the promo that was shot for Whethers, that never aired. "Yea, it aired...it aired on eVolution." Thug roC simply stated in a smartass fashion. Thug roC, known for cutting corners and taking shortcuts might've done so here, but there's no evidence to support it. Whether Thug roC was being a smart ass, or just telling the truth is yet to be known. Finally, roC was questioned on his release from PRIME, and it's effect on his relationship with the PRIME officials. "I wasn't released to state the truth, I left. They didn't want me there, and I didn't want to be there. As far as my relationship with PRIME officials go...lets say that I don't expect and Christmas Cards from them this year." Thug roC then took off before being asked any furthur questions. Funny to say the least, Thug roC signed an contract extension with eVo just days before he left PRIME. Whether or not that was a deciding factor, we may never know. Thug roC still states that he had nothing to do with Shane Whethers signing with eVo, but he also made that statement in a smart manner. Only time will tell before we may find out what really happened.
