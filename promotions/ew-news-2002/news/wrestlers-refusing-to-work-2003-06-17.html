Wrestlers Refusing to Work

17 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"The guy is just too dangerous. Has no care for the people he's in the ring with." There are many chaotic things going on behind the scenes of Action!, some of which have already been reported, while others are being covered up as best as possible. Reed Young definitely doesn't want a lot of things leaking to the press. One of those things has landed on our desk here at TheNews. It appears as if there is a minor-strike going on amongst the wrestlers. Not all of them, but a good majority of them. Mainly the lowercard workers. The reason? Giovanni Ollivander DeNucci. The nineteen year old son of a billionaire was signed to a pretty lucrative contract by Reed Young three weeks ago despite Giovanni having no professional wrestling experience. The young man had walked out of his contract with Advantage Wrestling two weeks prior to arriving in Action!. His reputation wasn't too high to begin with, as you might imagine. Action!'s Production Manager, Joe Schmidt, has been very adament about keeping Giovanni out of the ring until he learns to work properly. "The boy is from the world of MMA and Karate Tournaments. He just doesn't "get" wrestling. Either that or he just finds it funny to rough people up," Schmidt said recently. Three men have already felt the unprofessionalism of Giovanni(Chen Long Li, Lukas Wagner, and Payne Fosters), and after witnessing the actions of Mr. DeNucci, other wrestlers have taken a stand and are refusing to face him. "The guy is just too dangerous. Has no care for the people he's in the ring with," one worker was saying. "I'm definitely not going to get into the ring with him. Personally, I don't like getting concussions. Maybe that's just me." Word is that Reed Young is really high on the kid, for whatever reason. It is unknown whether action will be taken with Giovanni or if they'll get someone to teach him to properly work in the realm of professional wrestling. "I hope someone teaches him a lesson. It's not my style, and I normally don't wish harm on anyone, but it's just uncalled for what he's doing," remarked a wrestler who wished to go unnamed. "Those three guys[Fosters, Wagner, Li] have all be lucky. But eventually the kid is going to mess up and ends someone's career. So something had better be done now, or you could see a walk-out of the wrestlers. And that's going to be a damaging blow to a company that relies on it's ringwork than any other." Time will tell what will become of this situation. We'll keep you updated on this situation and other Action! related news as they leak to us. Action! Wrestling
