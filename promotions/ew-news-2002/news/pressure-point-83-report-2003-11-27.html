Pressure Point 83 report

27 November 2003

Is the old adage true? Do shitty shows make for awesome reports? Pressure Point Report Date Aired (Taped): [i]2[b]3[/b][/i].11.03 (17.11.03) Number: 83 Location: Rose Garden, Portland [i]Posted by Keith Scott Zimmerman[/i] WARNING: Show allegedly contains wrestling-type properties and is massive letdown after my awesomeness last week. Here we go: I cut a victory heel promo, and like every good opening heel promo, it got interrupted. To distill it down to its essence, it's Sabotage & Victim to be Named Later vs. the 101 Elite, with their title contention vs. a title shot at the PPV. Some of you may be asking why this is up in a SIX man and I say to you, I'm still fucking hurt...Sharc kidnapped Levine, which was good but what would've really gotten him over was running him into a cactus...Liz got the Michelle Branch CD and an anklet from Humburglar and My Bitch. Good for her--Branch is about a ***¼ album... [b]1. Tanya Isama beat Adam Nowell.[/b] Q: If Nowell is such a bad-ass, why is he coming out to the Blue Man Group? Nowell beat her up with kicks, the Gangrel Homage, and the MDII, but she kept kicking out. She's PLUCKY, you see, and not in the not-your-ellelator-MY-ellelator way. Anyway, he set up something else, she rolled him up. M E H. (*) Segments--they're faaaaaaaaantastic! So the Westside Connection/Dangerettes fued is back on. Did I mention I've beaten them all already and they bore me? Chain match between Marlee Matlin and Cracky...IBJ/Paulton in the tournament; the former gave Liz some beer...More Nowell weirdness... [b]2. Brian James viciously pummelled Kayden Paulton to advance to the Elite 8 of the Players Tournament.[/b] I've had longer orgasms, and in some fortunate cases there's been someone else there giving them to me. Exodus movement of jah people next! (DUD) ¡Mas segmentos! No physical contact for Coral or he loses his shot...Hardcase pummeled Nowell's Rocker-looking friend...Chris Chambers tried giving a gift...Speaking of retards, LLB dared show his face and is continuing further down the entrance theme scale. Out came the Ringer... [b] 3. LLB squashed the Ringer.[/b] And he challenged Action Man, too. Dolores O'Riordan 3:16 sez "does anyone caaaaaaaaaareeeeee?" (½*) [b]4. Donell Arcade choked out Bradley Duncan in a chain match.[/b] Mindless violence, my old friend. Donell reversed Code Red into a chain chokeout. It led to another Pier 4 backstage after the fact. (*½) Rocker was again pummelled. [b]5. Xiao Suarez squashed Jason Deline FLAT. [/b] See, this is how I know this world is a hell designed specifically for me: you ask for more matches, and you get them, but they all suck and/or are squashes. (DUD) [b]6. In a sad, pathetic attempt to still prove his alleged masculinity Max Danger hunted down the janitor and squashed him, then gave a self-serving "no, I'm still good, really I am speech" after.[/b] I really fucking hate this show. (negative 47) Chris Chambers found someone he can beat, and is that blood I see running in the streets?...Liz got a swank jacket from Joe. Of course she did, Joe's good people. And a puppy to boot... [b] 7. Chris Chambers beat the Phoenix.[/b] No wonder I feel like I have to wrestle every show. (DUD DUD DUD) Action Man & Ringer had a plan...Jeff gave Liz a great gift that she didn't like for some reason. I got them something but it got lost in the mail. No, really... [b] 8. LLB squashed Action Man but FUCKING LOST AAAAAAHAHAHAHAAHAHAHAHAHAHHAHAHA.[/b] Oh, this was a (DUD) but some new guy beat up LLB and threw him off the stage. So that was five stars. [b] 9. Coral Avalon beat Deft.[/b] This show's depressed me too much. Coral is like a new age Ricky Morton, and I mean that in both the glowing and non senses of the term. Yes, he came back with the leg lever takedown and finished with the Spike. Can I go home now? (**½) Liz & Trish celebrated with a stripper. Good for them...Sharc burned Levine's contract, the PIW Title, and proceeded to light his surrounding area on fire. Note to self: worship Sharc. MATCH OF THE NIGHT: #9, but it was like winning the Special Olympics. BEST SEGMENT: Watching Sharc ruin everything good in Levine's life was an excellent dessert after a seven-course dinner of shit with shit on the side. FUNNIEST SEGMENT: I was laughing as the PIW title burned and as the new guy threw LLB off the stage, does that count? WRESTLER OF THE NIGHT: Sad to say, Coral for having a good match and building towards getting wiped off of the face on the earth at the PPV. WRESTLER WITH THE MOST MOMENTUM: Ugh,this is Avalon too. HOTTEST FUED: Avalon/Garvin. The stip's old school but I like it. UNDERPERFORMER OF THE NIGHT: Everybody between my opening speech and the last match. Worst. Show. EVER. FLATTEST SEGMENT OF THE NIGHT: See above. LINE OF THE NIGHT: "...and when she's REAL excited she makes dolphin noises." Groundhog Day rules it.
