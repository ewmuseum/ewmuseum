The Sport Of Wrestling Afraid To Go "Beyond The Ring"?

23 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FX is set to air controversial new show shining a light on the dark stories inside and outside the ring. Will the wrestling world be able to accept the show's no-BS approach to the REAL world of wrestling? Daring...gritty....real...unapologetic...raw... All words used liberally to describe the virgin series picked up by FX entitled "Beyond The Ring". Developed by J Mysterio Entertainment, the show covers the trials and tribulations of a fictional wrestling federation in Japan with all sorts of action going on inside as well as OUTSIDE the ring. Early reports of the series point to issues such as backstage politics, drug usage, and other personal demons running rampant throughout the show; the irony being none are exaggerated, for they are real problems within the harsh business of wrestling. "If your protective over the REAL issues within wrestling, this is not the show for you, I suggest you turn to something more "sugar-coated". I made it very clear that I wanted no prisoners taken in this series and the issues that happen in this federation may seem disturbing...but they are issues promotors, bookers, wrestlers, and even families face at all times when you have to deal with this business. I take pride in saying that my show is no fairy tale story with a happy ending every hour....the audience will watch and experience tribulations that "smarks" are only able to read about in the latest dirt sheets that cover fWo, A!, and other big federation news," proudly commented series creator and director, Jamar Wilson. Early reviews also pointed out to the "deeply realistic" characters that bring out the harsh stories, such as ego-driven roster hogs and frustrated enhancement talent within the controversial setting that is Burning Knuckle Wrestling, the fictional federation where the drama is centered. Wilson was also smug in pointing out to wrestlers, promotors, and other buinsess insiders that yes, some character are based on real life counterparts that may plague certain big or smaller level federations....and he has no remorse in bringing that to light in the new series. How will the wrestling world react to the daring drama that completely shatters kayfabe and dissects to it's raw core? Your best bet is to tune into the premiere episode of "Beyond The Ring", coming very soon to FX. The show will be heavily hyped with commercials until it's pilot episode is aired, which should be very soon. Check your local listings for airing dates. Beyond The Ring TV Promo
