Marc Knight Press Conference

27 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Marc Knight had alot to say about OSE, Xw and most importantly. WWC "These three will be the future of WWC...." Xw superstar and 'Agent of the Stars' Marc Knight held a press conference earlier this morning and that statement was by far the most profound. He started the conference speaking about one of his current tag teams, Gunns and Roses, who are not doing to well in OSE's METII tourney. He went on to speak about his current ring debut and his reentrance into the wrestling ring. He stated that he was looking to Main Event every card for the next five years, some thought this was a joke but Knight was dead serious. But the biggest announcement of this press conference was when Knight began to speak on WWC. It seemed strange him speaking on this fed, since the rumor of KaGe making his debut had been killed... But Knight brought new light to the issue. His exact words were: "WWC will not know what hit them. Not only will 'Tha Red-Eyed Warrior' KaGe be making his presence felt in WWC, but the best tag team in the world, Devastation have also signed contracts with WWC." It was not said how much and how long these contract were for, but it was assured that when the WWC doors open, KaGe and Devastation will be three of the many to walk thru. -Warren Wilkerson
