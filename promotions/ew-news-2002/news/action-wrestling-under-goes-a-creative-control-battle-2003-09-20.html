Action Wrestling under goes a Creative Control battle

20 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LLB (Real Name: Roland Miles Ermann) and Levine (also known as KroW) have never been the best of friends...but recently...both have went into a big war backstage... Recently, Action officials came up with a very good idea for a possible contest to take place on this Monday's Pressure Point. Rumors have been circling the entire wrestling industry that Action Wrestling has been looking to actually sanction a contest between two men who have the worst case of 'bad blood' for one another...in the ring, as well as backstage. LLB, Roland Miles Ermann and Levine (KroW) have always been bitter enemies ever since their altercation back in September, when both men actually began to brawl in the middle of the street after Levine had decided to leave Action Wrestling during a very important angle. But recently, with the news of Action looking to place both men into an actual bout sent Road Agents, as well as officials, into a frenzy. Arguments such as, "Both men do not get along, it would be very dangerous to place them in a professional environment" (said by Road Agent: Joe Black) have been pouring from the mouth of Action Wrestling's backstage area. But, surprisingly, Levine seemed all for the idea. No comment was given from Levine, but it was obvious that he just wanted to face off against LLB. "Levine isn't a really great influence backstage," says senior official Ryan Watts, "Many believe that the only reason he would agree to this, is because he would just like to sock Roland one good shot...breaking kayfabe, and possibly causing a riot within the ring during a LIVE event...a great risk, indeed" But it seemed that LLB had different plans, and completely refused to go through with the contest. LLB, Roland Miles Ermann, believed that 'being professional' is one of the best aspects in wrestling...and to break that truce, makes the sport not fun. And thus, requested that his match be changed to face someone other than Levine. Thus...the war began. It seemed after a recent house show on Friday Night, Levine and LLB (once again) got into a verbal warfare backstage about the possibility of a contest on Pressure Point. According to Action personnel, it is said that Levine was the one to begin the argument/fight to begin with. Beginning to call Roland a 'p***y' and believing he was a coward for not agreeing to the contest. Roland, not being one to take a verbal bashing, returned the verbal attacks. The scene was broken up before a single punch was thrown...aka, just in time. It's said, that ever since the backstage warfare between two of wrestling's veterans, that Levine has been fighting Roland's decision by using his creative control card. A creative control clause in Levine's contract states that 'any, or all, of Mr. Levine's decisions concerning matches and/or promotional material must be agreed to by Mr. Levine himself. Also, if Mr. Levine sees that a match presented to him is agreeable by terms of the contract, than the said match/promotional material cannot be declined after an agreement has been made". Unfortunately for Levine, Roland Miles Ermann also has a creative control clause in his contract that gives him full rights to any/all decisions concerning his character. "It's been a complete creative control war," stated Vice President Jeremy Bold, "A war that cancels out both men's requests" And just when the problem seemed unsolved...Reed Young had stepped in, stating that the match would not take place...believing that the match itself was a bad idea, and that placing Levine and Roland into any sort of plan together would be disastrous. "It's complete and utter bull****," stated Levine, "The only reason that the match is not going down is because Roland has something more than just a creative control clause with the company...and that's an unsackable contract with his cousin, Reed Young. Action and Roland spew all the human feces they want from their mouths...but we all know why the match won't go down. And you bet your ass I'll be sure to voice my opinion on the matter" LLB v. Levine - Part II?
