Iceman's Review of EFWO's Helloween2k3

6 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last night at The Pepsi Center in Denver Colorado the EFWO spectacular HELLoween2K3 was held and was an undeniable success. Last night at The Pepsi Center in Denver Colorado the EFWO spectacular HELLoween2K3 was held and was an undeniable success. Just over 19,309 crammed into the home of the Avalanche. The Chosen one was there and my thoughts on the night are conflicted. The actual event was kicked of by my stable mates marriage. Sheila Peters and Damien Spires, the current EFWO world champion tied the knot in a wonderful ceremony with none other than the Idollic icon as usher. However the ceremony was disrupted by some over zealous fans, who needed to be put back in their place, which dually happened. And with that, Michael Jackson’s Thriller kicked off what promised to be the biggest boldest pay per view ever broadcast. It didn’t disappoint. The first match on a very impressive card was the ‘loser leaves town match’ between Avery Dorn and Donovan Troi. Now I have absolutely no interest in any of these losers. And to be honest I was praying for a double count out but the guys went out and gave it their all. Close fall after close fall came but eventually Donovan Troi’s in-ring experience helped secure both the win and his place in a company that employs the chosen one. A true honour. Next up came the ‘Diva rumble’. Now I am a huge fan of ladies. That’s ladies in general. Not lady wrestlers. I think they lack talent and try to make up for this with their willingness to bear cleavage. Something they no doubt took from the WWF’s days with superstars such as ‘Earthquake’ and ‘Yokozuna’ headlining. No matter the talent in the female division is lacking with only 2 clear stars. And neither where in this match. Having said that though, I am a red blooded male. So I watched this one with great interest. The stipulation was, the losers would be slaves for a day to the eventual winner, with elimination rules taking place. 5 diva’s stepped into the ring, Trinity, Sapphire, Emerald, Lilandra and Michelle Arboleda. Emerald was the first to go, and then Arboleda followed shortly by Lilandra and Sapphire respectively. Thus Trinity had won, and she would have each of the other females as slaves for a day each. Now lets all hope they film that! Then to follow was a match I had greatly anticipated, and so had the fans in Denver. It was the TV Title match between Creeping Death and Nathan Summers. An Absolute TLC match, with the object being to place your opponent in the casket then climbing the ladder to the gold. Now as everyone in the wrestling world knows, I gave the TV title to Nathan Summers. Then we had a lot of pent up rage between us which ended in The Iceman ending his undefeated streak and beating him week after week, just because I could. So when I sat in my dressing room to watch this I wanted Creeping Death, a man who I had carried through a tag match only weeks prior, to not only win. But win in style. And the match did not disappoint. Both men fought for the gold like it meant everything and it became obvious, to me at least, that in the afore mentioned tag match, Creeping Death had learned a lot merely by the presence of the chosen one. He had gained so much, so for that he’s welcome. But take nothing away from him, he did himself proud. And as much as it pains me to say it, Nathan Summers did everyone of his fans proud too. All 6 of them were ecstatic. The match ended up in triumph for Death who captured the title, but many may say he didn’t have the look of a winner as Summers sucker punched him afterwards. And that’s not mentioning that Jason Glenn got involved. But we’ll get to that worm later. Then afterwards Mystery also attacked Death, and unmasked himself and much to the amazement of the crowd was NOT Red Star, who did make a surprise comeback. To follow that would be suicide. But the only man who could do that. Did. Yep that’s right it was time to grace Denver with the Idollic icon. But first I had my own little treat for the Helloweeners. Live in the Pepsi Center, Limp Bizkit performed their single ‘Gimme The Mic’, from their new album in stores now, to lead out The Chosen one. So the crowd erupted the ground shook and it had begun. Me versus David Gibson. A leather strap match with a difference. To win I had to make my opponent utter the words, I quit. So we went out and got strapped up and what followed would and should be remembered as one of the finest matches ever to take place in this business, this company and this era. I went out with no regard for my safety and put the Orders so called enforcer through a table. I bust him open. I threw him off a 20ft stage. I hung him form a tier. I hit him with a sledgehammer. I hit him with my signature manoeuvre the deep freeze. I flung him down sets of stairs in a bin. Not even super slug Jason Glenn’s sneak attack could derail the first Renegade match of the night, I was carrying the torch to set the standard and refused to accept defeat. Then when I was bored, I locked in a modified Ice-rack, another of my signit moves, and then just as predictable as the bible was Gibson’s murmurs. I quit echoed around not only Colorado but also the world that night. And what followed? Chad Hartl’s glorious return to where he belongs. The EFWO. The Renegades. The Main Event. He returned just as expected to wipe the smile of Davy Gibson’s face. So here I was, bleeding and the adrenaline flowing. So I prepared just as the rest of the PPV world did, to watch the match between Frost and Triple C. However that plan was changed when it was seen to be Showtime not Frost facing off against Triple C. The arena was stunned but no-one more so than Triple C. This was less of a match and more of a street scrap, which ended in both Triple C and Tiffany becoming trapped in a burning building. My heart bleeds. This match was followed by the 22-man masquerade battle royal. Many guys in costumes took part in something that was obviously as demeaning as a tag match with Shane Adams. They dressed up and with each minute more professional respect etched away from them. That’s not to say it wasn’t hilarious and hellacious both at the same time. Geryon, Williams and Caesar were the first to go. Red Hot, Cole Cash and Trent Rudiger quickly followed them. As this match went on it became more and more obvious at the lengths these guys would go for that title, and many seemed to be willing to go further than the usual call. Vane Lazarus, The Executioner and Nightmare then were ejected fro the match. Things started to get more and more brutal as the level of what was needed kept on climbing. Weapons came and went. Blood flowed like wine. The fans were in heaven. Gohnja Rahma then went, then Shadow Stalker, then Cage. One by one as the heat intensified, and not just on Triple C, they separated and dropped like flies. Grim was poisoned then Ace McCoy and Johnny Thompson followed suit in that they were eliminated too. The styles of the four remaining were becoming apparent and by simple math, their identities were too. Although after the time that had passed, styles meant nothing and it was becoming more and more about who would go the extra step. Regulator and Rage then fell to leave a stand off between Wes Outlett and Mystery. Everyone was on their feet as Batman swung a tire iron and connected with the side of the face of the generic mask, then on the chin, then on the top of the skull. Mystery had claimed gold. Now to follow such a brutal lengthy match would be hard. But Gregory Glenn, Jason Glenn and James Rhydin tried to do just that. Well they were going to until Gregory aka Lord Lucan disappeared. However I did have a minor vested interest in that I don’t like James Rhydin and I had humiliated Glenn the show before. So I sat back and enjoyed. Well, I tried. The match quite frankly bored me to death. Much like it did to the 19k+ inside the Pepsi Center. Make no mistake, both gave it their all. Its just that their all added up to as much as half of Kevin Frosts beer gut. So I sat and winced as move after move brought my eyelid nearer and nearer the bottom of my eye. Then in probably the most anticlimactic ending in the history of sports, both men counted each other out for a draw. Next up was the much-awaited Heaven and Hell match, with Ferrell going up against Adams for the commonwealth title. This match was one that no Gade’ was involved in that I was looking forward to. I thought Ferrell was fairly talented in a sorta generic sorta way and hoped he would do something that has needed to be done for weeks. Shut up Shane adams. Unfortunately that was a feat to far for Derrick. However that didn’t mean the match disappointed in the slightest for it did not. Both men did surprise me in both their intent and their fearlessness. In the end, Adams had to sacrifice his hair to be ‘crowned’ the winner. So then came the intercontinental title match between Sean Williams and Top Dog and good friend Jack McGraw, Now I couldn’t watch this 100% as I was ready in case the slug Glenn tried to interfere etc but I also seen it as a foregone conclusion. I knew McGraw would kick Williams from corner to corner before being crowned new IC title champion. And the big dog didn’t disappoint. He did exactly like he said. The it was Beauty vs the beast. And??? Well just like the previous Renegade matches before Sheila did EXACTLY what she said and buried Jill’s cottage cheese ass in the grave thus winning and still being the Woman’s world champion and an icon for the who of womens wrestling. So there you have it the Iceman’s prelude to the big one. The one everyone had waited for. The best in the business today, FACT, going against Steve Tustin for the World title. And the outcome? Was there ever any doubt? I sat and watched, ass firmly on my comfy deluxe chair in my dresser, supremely confidant that Damien wouldn’t need any help whatsoever. He then did just as I had done, just as McGraw had done, just as his new wife Sheila had done. He continued the Renegades night of unrelenting success just as HE said he would do. He retained his world title after a absolute cracker of a match. He kept the one thing Tustin promised to take. And to make things even sweeter James Rhydin unveiled the new president of the EFWO… Damien Spires. So, to cap it all off, I watched people be burned, be busted open, flung from huge heights, get pinned, get put in caskets, get buried alive, climb ladders, say I quit, and I saw all the Renegades winning as they foreseen. Then I saw them take complete control of the EFWO and yknow what, I suddenly realised. There is a whole of a lot more to come. Iceman. EFWO Wrestling
