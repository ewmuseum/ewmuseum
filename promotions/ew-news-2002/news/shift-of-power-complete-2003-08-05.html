Shift of Power Complete

5 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With Prime and Tyler Davidson involved in a petty feud, Cameron Griffin stepped out before the cameras and attempted to make the headlines with some eye catching comments on that very rivalry and his own future. Revelations, was just that, a success without doubt. It was a show which captured the imaginations of its audience and telelvised a number of suprises which kept its fans on the edge of the seat. With the arena now empty though and the show little more than a memory, attentions are now turning toward its aftermarth. Most notably for Cameron Griffin, the shift in power we saw in the eye catching Cell Division. eW-News caught up with the man himself. In a high stake match which saw both the ownership of a division and a championship belt on the line, we saw the crowing of a new champion and a new outright owner. Cameron Griffins reaction? Sheer elation. "Its been a long time coming, but finally we are at the end of a road. I never asked for Joshua Krowe to be installed as my partner and as the weeks ahead are going to prove I certainly didn't need him." For those unfamiliar with the arrangement. Two months ago, the Cell Division of Evolution re-opened its doors and announced its dramatic return which was aiming to take professional sports entertainment by storm. In a division based wholey within a steel cell the rules were simple, beat your opponent senseless or into submission. Simple but effective. In the last few weeks we have seen the Cell Division achieve strong ratings and steal alot of focus as fans around the world have been bitten for the bug. All good news you would think, but no, not according to Cameron Griffin. "Its been hard work these past few weeks, I wanted to run a promotion, not become the next Clueso. Unfortunately though trusting Joshua Krowe was not an option and as I found out to my cost his devious ways made me look like angel gabriel. No man of his previous position should have been embroiled in a 'dollar for domination' deal as I will call it and I certainly hope that we can draw a line under the whole issue. Jinx has left Evolution. Joshua Krowe has left Evolution. Now its time to look forward and in many ways despite our obvious reliance on the promotion, attempt to carve our own distinction and continue to drive forward hopefully without the lead weights this time; Joshua Krowe I mean. After all there is only room in The Cell for one cunning and manipulative business man" Cameron smiled. So what now? "Well hopefully the future is as exciting as the past few weeks have been. Damon O'Halloran continues to impress and the youngsters such as Terylane, Sean Sterling, QBX and Machine look good bets for the future. I'm excited as I would imagine every Cell fan is worldwide. I didn't want to fully sink my reputation and money into something I felt was doomed for failure, but with Krowe now little more than an after thought and myself at the helm the future is more than rosey. You have to remember that my brainchild, OSW, made the likes of Dan S., Sovereign and Bob" Cameron said trying to control his grin. "Thats not a bad CV and in truth it should serve as a timely reminder to both Evolution as a whole and Prime, that while they are enjoying a bitchfest they would do better to spend their time being weary of what the Cell Division might yet become. Evolution is a setting, it is not a governing power. Thanks to a few timely strokes of a pen they are very much distinct entities, sharing common ground". So Cameron is not impressed with the whole Prime and Evolution issue? What about Whethers? "What about Whethers. In the words of Justin Timberlake, go cry me a river. Theres to many top talents in the Cell for me to be worrying about some guy who might have just as little impact as Carl Cash has had despite his hyped entrance. Cash's name means jack and so will Whethers until he proves he is worthy of airtime which could be better used to promote The Cell. Davidson has alot to prove. Prime has proved nothing. Like I say its petty between the two of them and they would spend their time better if they focused on what truely mattered, condeming this petty rival to become little more than glorified chip paper" Strong words from the Englishman and bold predictions for the future. One things for sure, as time passes he certainly isn't going into his shell.
