Breakdown Wrestling Re-Opens!

17 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Today, in Shreveport, Louisiana, huge news was made concerning the return of Breakdown Wrestling. [name withheld] held a press conference makring it's return. Today in Shreveport, Louisiana became a special day for many people in the wrestling world. [name withheld], former owner of Breakdown Wrestling came out of seclusion in a suit making an announcement many have wished for for many months. As the media circled around Wood to listen to what he said, he said the following words, "Today is a monumental day not just for myself, but for people around the world. As of today, after a surprise meeting from an old friend, Breakdown Wrestling will be making it's return in 2004." Wood made the announcement months after he closed Breakdown Wrestling and went into seclusion. Breakdown Wrestling was a former member of Primetime Central and was considered one of the best rising f ederations within the network and other acclaimed networks such as EWPlanet and EWNews. However, due to reasons still unknown, Jeffery called it quits shocking many. He continued his press conference with the following, "After talking with my friend, we have decided to re-open Breakdown Wrestling in February. The first show will be Sunday Sanitarium which will be on February 1st, 2004 live here, in Shreveport, Louisiana. We are currently in talks with several of the larger networks who are interested in picking up an exciting brand of wrestling. Our brand of wrestling has been a favorite among many and we hope to continue this trend in the new year." Since the announcement that Breakdown Wrestling would make it's return and that they were in talks with several networks there have been rumors on which network will pick up the re-opening company. Some people have said that NBC and Fox are in the forerunning with WB also being mentioned. However, one source has told us that ABC is close to inking a deal with Wood and the other co-owner. While it is a rumor, many have wondered how that would work with fWo being on the same network. Other rumors have also started to circle around about who Wood's secret friend is. When asked, Wood would not comment and moved on to a new subject. However, after several meetings with this person, many are saying that it is a veteran in the business and someone who could help take BDW to a new height. He is said to have been gone for a while and looking for a way back. Wood would then close the press conference. As soon as we hear more information, we will report it soon.
