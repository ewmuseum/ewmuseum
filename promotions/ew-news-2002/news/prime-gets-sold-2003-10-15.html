PRIME gets SOLD!

15 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Taun Phams fall from grace was complete this week on ReVolution when he signed over his 50% stake in PRIME and ordered the familys legal advisor to sell it off. Just one day later and we have ourselves a successful buyer... Taun Pham signed over his share in PRIME on Monday night and no sooner than 2 days later, it has been sold. The deal was reportedly completed in the early hours of this morning in a meeting attended by all of PRIMEs remaining top officials, including Sebastian. The accepted bid has yet to be released, but is thought to be well in the region of $3 million. The buzz around PRIME HQ today was one of excitement, although a lot of mystery currently surrounds the identity of the new co-owner(s). They have however released a brief statement concerning their intentions for PRIME, which were presented to the media by Toshiaki Motoki in a press conference this morning. Motoki explained that the new body in power has no desire to close PRIME or change the core elements of it in anyway. With that being said however, a full revamp of the companies flagship program ReVolution is apparently a top priority... starting this week! The card for the event has already been set, but we're being told that this weeks show should be heralded as the next step in PRIME's evolution. There will be a few job losses corresponding with this new ownership, but it is initially thought to involve only behind the scenes staff linked to corporate affairs and relations rather than in ring talent. Hin See's position in this is not clear, but rumor has it that he has booked a flight to his home country of Japan for Friday morning. With Taun Pham now severing a majority of his ties with the company, funding for PRIME was a key issue in finding a new ownership. The new man/woman in charge has promised a near "bottomless trough" of money for the company to use and promises to herald this new era with a couple of high calibre recruits. PRIME is changing by the hour. Only time will tell whether this is a turning point for the company, a new golden era or the first step on the road to oblivion. PRIME
