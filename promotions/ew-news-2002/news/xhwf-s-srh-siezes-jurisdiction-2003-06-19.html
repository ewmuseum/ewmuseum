XHWF'S SRH siezes jurisdiction

19 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Monday Night’s Whiplash strayed convention as the team of Cocked Milk, consisting of Mark Adkins and Slipknot, fought heroically to become the world champion and number one contender respectively. Over the past few weeks the domination of the new un-official stable Stoners Reeking Havoc have started match by match dictating their opponents and earning all the publicity there is to offer. However Mark Adkins on the back of four weeks strain defeated the Vine in an epic half an hour crusade to become the XHWF’s new World Champion, closing the reign of the longest running XHWF World Champion. Mark Adkins beat four men in the tournament leading up to Whiplash and defied all odds and ended up being favourite at Whiplash. Where he only picked up the victory thanks to an interference, by Chevrulec and then laid the Ego Trip on Vine as he rejoiced being crowned. However Slipknot battered his way through a four man match to step our victorious as the number one contender, despite the odds packed against him. Mark Adkins and Slipknot will step toe to toe at Stardom with the XHWF World Title in sight for both the pair, both vow to walk out XHWF World Champion, however with the Vine joining Cocked Milk, there could be possible tension in the weeks to come and with Matt Madness dominating news bulletins with his departure, could this cause a doubt over SRH’s dominance? Or will the new threesome run things in the XHWF? XHWF Wrestling
