Paul Levine reaches agreement with Network

11 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The up and coming federation of the WWC has been looking for a network to support their product. All seemed lost until a certain network had finally contacted the up and coming federation. It has been nearly one month this the first ever event of the World Wrestling Corporation. The rising sun of the wrestling world seemed to have impressed fans, as well as critics, alike. Numerous wrestling fan sites, or even a few news sites, began covering the house show for the new up and coming federation and many were very pleased with the product. Unlike many of the new age federations, WWC seems to be bringing up more of the newer superstars into the federations. Names from XWF and EWF weren't very known up to August, when Nassau Coliseum was blown away by the WWC's product. But the fans weren't the only ones blown away. As stated previously, executives from numerous networks sat down and watched the product themselves, determining whether or not they wish to have such a program on their network. The following networks attended the event: Home Box Office (HBO), the FOX Network, American Broadcast Corporation (ABC), Warner Brothers Network (WB) and Ted Turner's TNT. Recently, on the official websites of ABC and WB, the following statements were made... American Broadcast Corporation: "After heavily discussing possible endeavors with the World Wrestling Corporation we, at ABC, have decided to decline the offer. With our current production mainly on sitcoms and more educational shows, the wrestling product would not fit with our current shows" Warner Brothers Network: "It has been one month since we have viewed the World Wrestling Corporation's product. At first, it seemed like a set plan after many of our executives were quite pleased with the product. Unfortunately, with our past in wrestling federations, WWC would only be compared to the ever so popular, CWL. If you remember, our last wrestling product only lasted alittle under six months, until the owner of the company had sadly passed away. If WWC was to be brought upon WB, it would immediately be compared to CWL...something we are not looking for at this point" It seemed all but lost by the World Wrestling Corporation, until a ray of light shined upon the owner of the company, Paul Levine. Just last night, Mr. Levine received a phone call from one of HBO's top executives, which stated that they wished to talk over possible deals for a Monday Night timeslot. Now, many federations would decline a Monday Night Timeslot...as they would be competing with two top notch federations in the forms of: World Wrestling Enternation (WWE inc.) and fans Wrestling organization (fWo). But Paul Levine seemed MORE than happy to talk over deals, and gave us this little bit of information on why he had accepted. "A great man once said that the only way to get around an opposition, is through it. WWE and fWo have been federations that have long surpassed the common day 'two month feds' of today. Throughout the 1990s, these two federations have been the leading force in sports entertainment...what better way to debut than by competing with the best?" Levine said with a smirk across his face, "I am not threatened by either federation, in fact I bring the old saying of 'bring it on'. Because I think actions shall speak louder than words...and when our product is released, we will do just fine against the competition." Meetings between Home Box Office and World Wrestling Corporation are set to take place on September 27th, 2003. Ordered to be in attendance for the WWC are: Aaron Tact (Vice President), Paul Levine (President/Owner of the WWC), Brandon Ramos (Head Road Agent), and [name withheld] (Head of the Board of Directors). This will be an extensive meeting, but according to HBO... "We WILL have a deal set with the WWC directly following this meeting. The moment we hit the agreement, expect the announcement to be made" WWC Online
