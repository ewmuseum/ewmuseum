Financial Backing and Cornerstones

27 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Owner of Empire Wrestling might be commercializing sport. With the start up of the wrestling promotion, Empire Wrestling, Owner [name withheld] a.k.a. Djack, has been in meetings, on the cell phone and interviewing business men and women from all different of company’s. At a press conference D.jack announced the advertising deals that will seem to really commercialize this promotion. “Empire is going to be huge, but to try and attain that we were in need of financial backing. One man’s budget can’t attain everything on it’s own. So with that being said I would like to announce that Empire Wrestling has committed to a five year contract with the following companies. Budweiser, Mountain Dew, Snickers, Burger King, Sony Entertainment and Universal Pictures.” D.jack announced. While the terms of the contract were not available for the public, nor was anyone willing to announce the amount the companies and EW negotiated on, D.jack was willing to announce in what ways the companies will affect EW. “Instead of cotton candy and peanuts being sold in the crowd, Burger King is going to sponsor all of our shows which will include the shows we will be planning out of the country. You can eat a whooper and watch the match. Budweiser, Snickers and Mountain Dew have exclusive contracts to that of presenting our first and future four pay per views. Yes three companies for a total of five pay per view shows. Snickers will present one while Mountain Dew and Budweiser will present two each. All of our technical equipment from microphones to monitors to even our Mega Tron on the stage will be by Sony Entertainment. And Universal Pictures, I am proud to announce, will be presenting our inaugural 1st annual pay per view, which has yet to be named. Along with that, Universal will also be backing all of your shows that will be available at later dates in DVD format, plus the many DVD’s we plan on releasing in promotion of our superstars. In these ways and many others these high profile partners of Empire Wrestling will be apart of a great new wrestling promotion.” It didn't’ stop there though. D.jack made an unprecedented announcement, that some wrestling fans are looking down on. “I’d like to announce that we’ve hired two developmental contracts to EW. Those being Anthony Acosta and Kris Extreme. While these two talents are in developmental contracts, I am offering the first five wrestlers we accept, to non developmental contracts, a great opportunity. That being the first five wrestlers we accept will be automatically entered into a five way cage match at our first pay per view for the world title!” This announcement is in the hope that EW will receive some talented resumes. But wrestling fans don’t like it! “That’s stupid!! Your suppose to earn a title, even a title shot!! Not be given it!” One fan said. “I don’t like it. You automatically given a shot at the biggest prize in the company just for joining. It’s not sportsmanship like.” Another said. Whatever the view points, D.jack certainly seems to be trying to shake up things in the wrestling industry! Empire Wrestling
