Immortal 10!!!

10 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NTICW Head Interviewer and News Reporter is here with a recap of a special Wednesday edition Immortal 10. End of Days pay-per-view will be this Sunday. Look out for it!! New True Internet Championship Wrestling House Show Date: 9th July 2003 Arena: Thomas & Mack Center, Las Vegas, Nevada. Reporter: Stacy Curtis Hey NTICW fans! Just got back from the Wednesday edition of Immortal 10 and it was one great show! I cannot keep it all in so here goes! Just as I was about to get comfortable with my ringside seat, I received word from the back that a black limo had arrived in the building and I’ve been instructed to get there as quickly as possible for an interview. Here was how it went: The camera focuses on the Tron where it is showing the parking lot, the camera switches to one in the parking lot. A limo pulls up and Stacy Curtis is standing out side it. The door of the limo opens and Bill the Geek emerges from it then Chris Chaos gets out to a pop from the crowd. Vinny: It’s Chris Chaos! Sarah: Well Duh! Stacy: Chris? Chris can I get a few words? Chris Chaos: Sure. Stacy: Chris you aren’t booked on tonight’s card and neither are you on the Pay Per View card have you any clues on what you’ll be doing on Sunday? Chris Chaos: Well I am not booked for tonight’s show, but I am here to get myself on the card for Sunday. Stacy: What are you going to do? Chris Chaos: You’ll find out later. Stacy: Chris you’ve been a little displeased with Mike Flash over his nick name. Bill: Well you see Chris is the Real Deal, as you can see, whirs has been the Real Deal since back in the day since before Mike Flash was dreaming of his first match in any federation. Chris Chaos: It’s clear to see that he’s no Real Deal and I will catch up with Mr Flash about this little grievance I have with him. Chaos goes to leave. Stacy: Chris, in the two matches we’ve seen you in there has been a lot of great sports man ship, with Weezle and with Laura Pfenig, it’s great to see it. Chris Chaos: Well you see Weezle and Laura were great to wrestle and I like to show my appreciation for great performances. It’s all about how you conduct yourself in the ring, shaking someone’s hand before you take them on is a great show of respect. There’s nothing I hate more than people being un-sports man like and with no character. Stacy: Thanks very much Chris. That was basically it. Just like everyone else, I was excited about how he planned to get on the pay-per-view card. 1. Goode vs Laura Pretty good match for the opening. Both competitors put in a great amount of offenses into the match. Goode was hesitant at first with how he should approach the match since he was facing a girl in the ring but he soon let go of the thought and let loose when Laura send him reeling onto the mat with a powerful flying clothesline off the top rope! Impressive show of wrestling but in the end, it was Johnny Goode who won via pinfall. These two had a good handshake after the match. It seems like sportsmanship is starting to show more of itself in the last few shows amidst all that nonsense that is going on with Mike Wade. Had to rush backstage as I was scheduled to conduct a short interview with Weezle. Here was the interview: Stacy: "I'm here with Weezle who will be challenging The Dark Angel at End of Days for the Hardcore Championship. Weezle, what are your thoughts on the current champion? (A nervous looking Weezle walks into shot, rubbing his hands together.) Weezle: It doesn't matter what I think about the current champion, because according to the plan, I will beat him! BWAH! Stacy: And what exactly is this 'plan' you have been talking about? Weezle: BWAH! I said already... The plan cannot be revealed! Everything will make sense when it all unfolds! Plan... plan... PLAN! BWAH! Stacy:....And...what about... tonight? Weezle: I'm not on the card. That's part of the plan! I think... YES! It's part of the PLAN! The plan states I need to be rested... yes... So tonight is part of the plan! BWAH! Stacy: Is there anything about the plan that you can reveal? Weezle: Actually... There is. Stacy: And what is it? Weezle: According to the plan, I will be alone and staying in room 518 at the Holliday Inn on--BWAH! (Weezle slaps himself in the head.) Weezle: I HAVE SAID TOO MUCH! BWAH! (Weezle storms off as REPORTER looks on quite dumbfounded.) That was one weird of an interview but nevertheless, I went straight back to ringside for the next match. 2. Aaron Ward vs Newmonic A quick match which lasted all of 8 minutes. It was a total one-sided affair with the bigger Aaron Ward dominating the whole match with his size advantage and power moves. Johnny Newmonic did get a few attacks here and there and even tried to slow Ward down with a Figure-4 submission but it was not enough to earn him the victory for the night. Aaron Ward ended the match with a huge powerslam and the pin. Just then on the Tron, the camera switches to the outside of Vicky Dixon’s office. Vinny: What’s going on now? Sarah: Looks like Vicky Dixon’s office. The door opens and we can see Chris Chaos and Vicky Dixon is opening the door. Chris is leaving. Vinny: What’s Chaos doing there. Chris Chaos: So it’s settled. All I need is his agreement. Vicky Dixon: Yeah, all you’ve got to do his get him to agree and you have your match at End Of Days. Chris Chaos: Thanks, you won’t regret this. Vicky Dixon: I’ve been waiting for someone to stand up to these guys. She closes the door and Chris leaves. Vinny: What was that about? Do you think he requested a match with Mike Flash? Sarah: I’m sure we’ll find out later. Oh, all that waiting was making me nervous! 3. Coleman “The Prophet” Dowell vs Kaid Vagabon Kaid Vagabon who have been missing in action for the last few matches due to personal problems, had his first match ever since being booked for Immortal 6. However, his absence may have caused some ring rust and as a result, The Prophet, hungry as ever for a win to be added to his record, seized the moment and eventually put the Canadian down for the 1, 2, 3. 4. Tv Title: Sin Specter vs seVen Last Sunday, Sin had a cheap shot at an unknowing seVen when he was talking a walk in the back. Sin completely reduced the former Hardcore and first ever champion in Miss Dixon’s regime, to a motionless state. Since Sin openly challenged the unconscious seVen at that time after his attack to a match for his TV Title, owner Vicky Dixon booked the match and it was on! Fans were psyched up for the match myself included but we were disappointed by the antics of the annoying Mike Wade, Mike Flash and the rest of the Parade. Those suckers! They blatantly interfered in the match and the referee had no choice but to throw out the match, causing Sin Specter to retain his title. The arena threw a huge chorus of boos when the referee made the decision but they continued their relentless assault on seVen. Sin brought a chair in and while “The Real Deal” Mike Flash hold seVen up, Sin smashes the chair onto seVen’s face not once, twice, thrice but FIVE TIMES and proceeded with executing the Free Spirit! That was sickening and the fans hated it so much that they start to throw empty cans and bottles into the ring. Some of them even tried to get over the barricade but were stopped by the guys working as crowd control. SeVen was lying in the ring motionless and medics rushed to the ring, pulling seVen out in a stretcher. While the medics attended to him as they sent him backstage, the Wade Parade celebrated in the ring. However, fans were soon came onto their feet when Chris Chaos came out from the back and had a verbal showdown with Sin. This was how it went down: Audioslave Kicks in over the PA system and Chris Chaos with a mic in his hand, appears to a Huge pop from the crowd. He strums his air guitar with the beat of the song. Vinny: I guess Chris is going to tell us what his meeting with Miss Dixon was all about. Sarah: I hope he’s in some sort of match where it involves him take off his clothes. Vinny: NTICW fans wouldn’t like to see that. Sarah: Maybe so, but I would. Vinny: And on that note I think we’d better pay attention to Chris Chaos now. Chris is puts the mic to his mouth. Chris Chaos: Hello LAS VEGAS!!! The crowd pops and Chaos smiles at the cheap pop. Chris Chaos: Anyway you all must be wondering what I was doing in Vicky Dixon’s Office. Well to fully understand the story I have to take you back to last week, run the footage. Footage of Sin Specter beating on seVen is shown on the Tron. Chris Chaos: Well I must say it takes real balls to beat someone up with a steel chair when they are not looking. This is what is wrong with NTICW, people in this company with no character, fortitude and respect. In the ring, Sin is clearly not happy by the remark that Chaos just put across. Chris Chaos: Well Specter has shown that he can beat up someone from behind with a chair. Well I don’t think that he should be getting away with that. In fact the whole Wade Parade has been getting away with a lot over the past weeks! I’ve requested a match with Sin Specter this Sunday at the End Of Days so I can show him the meaning of respect and Miss Dixon has agreed to giving me the match and it will be for the NTICW Television Title!! The Crowd cheer in excitement. Vinny: Whoa! Chaos v Specter this Sunday and it’s for the title!! Sarah: This is rather interesting. Sin gets a mic handed to him and begins to talk. Sin Specter: Sure thing, Chris……… But! Vinny: There’s a but? Sarah: I like Chaos’ butt. Vinny looks at Sarah with disgust. Sin: But under only one stipulation. That stipulation would be………the match will be fought in a LADDER MATCH!! The Crowd is on their feet in anticipation of Chaos’ reply. Chaos: No problem Sin! However, there’s a slight statement that I want to make……… Take a good look at that title of yours. Be it ladder match or any other match, your a** is FRIED and that title is MINE!! Vinny: Well this is an interesting development for this Sunday. Now that it’s been decided, Sin Specter will face Chris Chaos for the Television Title at End of Days this Sunday with a special stipulation, A LADDER MATCH! Surely a match to wait for, so tune in to End of Days this Sunday!! 5. “The Real Deal” Mike Flash vs The Paralyser Finally, the main event. Mike Flash tried to play Teep out with his in-ring mind games such as frequently getting out of the ring pretending to get a breather when in actual fact he was delaying the match and as if he was waiting for something to happen. After a fair exchange of grapples, slams, boots and punches, true enough, the Wade Parade got involved again and tried to blindside Paralyser with a shot to head from the World Title belt by Mike Wade as the rest of the Parade had the referee distracted. However, Teep had expected it all and was more than well prepared for it. He dodged the attack and sent the World Champion flying out of the ring through the ropes with a huge superkick which hit perfectly on the jaws of Wade! Flash attempted a quick roll-up but the distracted referee game plan backfired on him and he did not get the three count he expected. As he got up from the pin and with Teep out of the way going after Wade, Chris Chaos came into the ring from the crowd and let lose a barrage of attacks on Mike Flash much to the delight of the fans in the packed arena! The crowd was cheering on as Teep and Chris Chaos cleared the area of the Wade Parade and sent them running up the ramp. As they were heading backwards, NTICW very own, Vicky Dixon, stood on the stage with her arms folded and a grin on her face. She was motioning her hands to Mike Wade that his time is up. The crowd cheered! Well, that was all for Immortal 10. Very interesting build up for End of Days! I’m sure many of you out there are looking forward to it, just like I am! Till next time, this is Stacy Curtis signing out! NTICW
