OSW Superstar Dies At End Game

15 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Old School Wrestling's final show, End Game, was marked with tragedy as roster mainstay Matt Register died unexpectedly during the show's main event. The Northampton County Coroner’s office announced the death of Old School Wrestling superstar Matt Register shortly after the end of OSW’s final program, Slam: End Game. Register, wrestling in the main event of the show, collapsed following a heart punch by his opponent, former partner, Jeffrey Wayne. Results later showed that the punch was not the cause of death, as was earlier expected. The cause of death, according to the coroner, was an overheated and overexerted circulatory system, which caused hyperthermia and an acute heart attack during his match with Wayne. Full results as to what caused the complications will not be released for several more days; however, traces of the herb ma huang, otherwise known as ephedrine or ephedra, was found in Register’s system. Up until a week ago, Register had served as a spokesman for MetaTrim, a supplement company specializing in ephedra. That contract was terminated without explanation right before OSW’s final Pay-Per-View show, Resolution. When answering questions, the coroner confirmed that if Register had stopped taking the drug at the termination of the contract, it would have been out of his system already. “He would have to have kept taking it in order to have this much of it still in his body,” Dr. Lee Boone told reporters. “Not only was the dosage that we found on him in excess of a recommended amount for someone his size, but it was not yet fully digested, meaning he could have taken it as early as yesterday.” No one within OSW could answer questions as to whether or not Register was continuing to take the drug after his sponsorship deal ended, much less why. Those questions were answered slightly today, when MetaTrim spokeswoman Janice Bligh came forward to announce details of the cancelled deal her company had with the deceased. “Our decision to terminate the deal with Matt came on the heels of our discovery that he had been abusing the drug,” Bligh said. She went on to detail the company’s alleged efforts to force Register into drug rehab. When he refused, she said, they cancelled the deal. “We did not want to be associated with someone who was willing to compromise his own safety to increase the benefits that he was already receiving from the product.” Once again, Jane Reagan, the only remaining OSW official, declined to comment on the subject, and cited that neither she nor anyone else knew about Register’s habits, due to his extreme isolation. With no other explanations available, Register’s death seems to be nothing more than another case of an athlete wanting too much too fast, one willing to compromise his health for fame, only to have his practices catch up to him. Several lawsuits were pending regarding psychological damage to children who saw the death live, but all were resolved with the liquidation of OSW’s merchandise stock. There is no expected action by WGN against OSW, as the company is now little more than a name, so there is very little else to come of this. Register’s family could not be reached for comment.
