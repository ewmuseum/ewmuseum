A piece of excellency - 1st edition

23 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A few months ago the biggest underground organisation in the world turned corporate and there was a lot of hype around it. But suddenly it seemed to disappear from the face of the world. What is the real story behind all this? Hi and welcome to the first edition of a piece of excellency. I am your columnist X[Cell]nT. Before going through the details of the column, I would like to start with a little introduction about myself. I have been following wrestling for the last couple of years. For the last two years I have been following mostly fighting instead of wrestling. And fighting is what my columns will be about. In this first edition I want to talk about a promotion that seemed to dissappear from the face of the earth in the last couple of months. I am talking about The Fighting Zone. The Fighting Zone legacy goes back to the mid-50's and it was always an underground promotion, where nobody knew exactly what went on there. But it all changed during the last part of the year 2000 when Gino Di Maggio took total control of TFZinc., which was the mother company of The Fighting Zone. In the couple of months everyuthing changed. All that was sacred in the underground, became available live on television. Gino Di Maggio has changed a family legacy. But one thing that Gino Di Maggio never realised was that he had awoken a sleeping giant. His father, Salvatore, was like him when he was young. He was an unscroupolous man. The actions by his son brought that rage in him again. He promised himself that before he will die he will get TFZinc. out of his son's hands. Using the best lawyers in the world, Salvatore succeeded in finding a loophole in the contract that brought TFZinc. to Gino Di Maggio. Thanks to that loophole Salvatore got his company back. But this time he want it to close it for good. He knew that if he didn't his son will do the same thing again, this time better. Thus after manipulating the company's funds, Salvatore Di Maggio filed for bankruptcy of TFZinc. During the first week of May a High Court declared TFZinc. as bankrupt. This meant the closure of a more than 50 years legacy. Salvatore had succeeded in getting over his son. But one must ask. Is the family war really over in the Di Maggio family? Those close to Gino know that he doesn't like to lose. Rumours in the fighting industry has started to grow recently on what Gino is planning. Will Gino react? And what is going to happen of the future of the former members of The Fighting Zone. With TFZinc. being declared bankrupt, their contracts were imnmediately terminated. So what does their future holds? This has been all from me. I hope you enjoyed the first edition of a Piece of Excellency. You will hear from me soon on more word on the fighting industry. I am your host X[Cell]nT KUMITE
