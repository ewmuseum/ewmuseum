Splink Comment on Title Win

5 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
New Asylum Team Champions, Splink, held a press conference earlier today. Well, actually, by early, it was at 3a.m in the morning, the reason for this was that apparently they wanted to do it at a suitable hour GMT for their 'proper' fans. Splink came into the room clutching a bottle of Champagne each. Trailing TMM and Slapnutz was Wincy Willis, the infamous whore that spouts filth whenever she gets the chance. The two men announced they would be fielding questions from the reporters present, however, due to it being at 3 in the morning, the turnout was pretty low. In fact, it turned out to be a sit-down interview between me and Splink. "So, Splink, how does it feel to be the new team champions of the Asylum?" I asked the two men. Naturally the question sucked, but I was running on very little sleep which wasn't good. "Right-o," TMM began. "Let's get one thing clear, I did not embezzle ANY funds from the record sales. In fact, I'm operating at a loss bigger than that of a small third-world country." "I believe they prefer to be called developing world countries," Slapnutz interrupted. "Whatever. Anyway, now that Camp Joe has allowed the Show to be broadcast, we can celebrate in public without having to get drunk with hobos all the time." Slapnutz cleared his throat and prepared himself for a monumental announcement: "Yeah, it rules to have belts now. They look special around my waist." I looked on, dumbstruck, at the two buffoons known as Splink. Then, after realising they were sitting for five minutes without me asking them a question, I snapped out of the trance. "Well, what are your plans for the future?" TMM stopped Slapnutz from answering. Apprently 'Nutz was going to rant on about toblerones and Nestle chocolate. "Since there is no real challengers for our titles, we are issuing an open challenge for our belts. However, to be accepted for this open challenge, you must be under four foot tall, weigh over four-hundred pounds and not smell of cheese." Slapnutz stiffled a small laugh, which was peculiar for a guy his size. Usually it's small girls and old women that giggle like that. Unfortunately, without prior notice, Wincy Willis interrupted the conversation. "Look, pubic hair, Splink are the most dominant, shit-stabbing, team in the Asylum. Scrotum monkey. And they have defeated all, piss-flap, comers. Now there is no one left, jizz spray, for them to beat." By now, my patience was wearing thin. I got out of my chair and was ready to leave, when a young reports named Astley came running into the room. "Hi, I'm Astley from the Stoke Reporter and I would like to know what Slapnutz plans to do for his next single now that he's a champion." "Finally a question I can answer," Slapnutz beamed. He was happy to be able to answer something since TMM had become the Splink spokesperson. But, before he could manage an answer, TMM stepped in. "There are lots of things in the pipeline. The most important thing is to get some financial backing so I can make some purchases and secure rights for backing dancers. Now, this press conference is over. Please, everyone, go home." With those words, I finally got the chance to get away from those idiots. But, ít does seem the the Asylum is lacking serious challengers for the Team Titles that Splink are holding on to.
