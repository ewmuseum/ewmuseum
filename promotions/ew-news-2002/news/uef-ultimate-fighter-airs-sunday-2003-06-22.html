UEF ULTIMATE FIGHTER AIRS SUNDAY!

22 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
UEF ULTIMATE FIGHTER PROMISES TO BE AN AWESOME PPV! With the tourney coming to an end at Ultimate Fighter as well as DM/STRUMMER II it promises to be nothing but awesome. As we will see the first Ultimate Fighter be named since Shadowstorms two year winning streak. Only seven men remain only one will walk out as THE ULTIMATE FIGHTER! We Have.... John "The Truth" Snow- New to the UEF Spotlight and still a rookie, but would he be here if he didn't fight for it? Ice Cube- Member of the UEF Grand Slam Champions club, making his return for possibly his final run, could he take this run to an ULTIMATE level? Hiddukel- A big man, and a UEF veteran, he is looking for nothing less then a win, will he get it? Drachen- Possibly the soon to be named e-fed rookie of the year. He has fought his way here and sure is not going down without a battle! Prometheus- A man who has been here in the UEF the longest without a title shot will he prove that he deserves one Sunday? Ace- The man who has been the farthest in the tourney in last year making it all the way to the finals. He is almost here again and looking for revenge with a win! Zaz- A man who was down in the dumps before the tourney started but making two huge career wins against Scorpion and Chris Hardy, its no more then a win/win situation for him! Don't miss the tourney as well as DM/STRUMMER II along with much more action packed evening! the.UEF
