Core News: Smash Return And More!

10 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Smash last night sent shocks through the spine, as Fred Fusion made a return to Core Wrestling. Speculation has been running rampant around the internet as to what will happen next Smash last night sent shocks through the spine, as Fred Fusion made a return to Core Wrestling. Speculation has been running rampant around the internet as to what will happen next. We have not been able to contact Fred about this, but he must be happy after his attack on the FitzPatrick Administration last night. Eugene FitzPatrick had this to say about Fred's return. "It won't happen again!" he said. "I'm going ot hire some fockin' better security, Fred is fired and he is staying fired, and nothing like this will happen again!" Mr FitzPatrick seemed to be adament about this and started to phone security firms as we left, so don't expect Fusion to be around anytime in the near future. Also, Fitzpatrick and Lanier will be settling there differences in the ring at RingMaster, with if Matt wins he gets 50% of Core, and if Fitz wins, Matt will have to leave Core forever. Things already have started heating up as this Smash, Fitz has booked the FitzPatrick Administration against a five man team that Lanier has picked for Smash. The teams are as follows, Eugene FitzPatrick, Patrick Powers, Morpheus Omega, Artemis and the newest member of the Fitzpatrick Administration taking Alex Caine's place is yet to be named, as Fitzpatrick says that he wants it to remain a mystery. Matt Lanier's team consists of Daniel Adams, Jantz Spalding, Quinn Masters, Lanier himself and Alinie Fanco, who has been shockingly allowed to go free by Eugene FitzPatrick after he said she has just "got in the way". Ratings for Core are improving dramatically on the network. Many are saying that the RingMaster Tournament is one of the things helping it rise back up. Smash, unsuprisingly was at its highest during the last quarter, when Fusion made his return, alas short lived. Core Wrestling
