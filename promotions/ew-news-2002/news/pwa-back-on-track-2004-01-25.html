PWA Back On Track?

25 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Adam Vanstt today took full control of Professional Wrestling Association, as he leads them into Wednesday's No Remorse PPV! New CEO of PWA Adam Vanstt looks set to lead PWA into 2004 with a big bang! A huge mess has been caused by former owner Maxwell Qwest, and Adam Vanstt has shown his professionalism, by handling the pressure with a great care. "Maxwell Qwest is just showing why he didnt have what it took to run PWA into the future." Vanstt said at a Press Conference. He also dismissed the other parties involved in the comotion. Former wrestler Damien Steele was an instigator in this, with himself and Maxwell Qwest having history with each other. Adam Vanstt commented "Damien is an awesome force inside that ring, and has been a mainstay in this company for a while. However on and off performances as of late, and him being one of the reasons to this argument, Damien will never appear in PWA again." A risky decision in view, but the fans seem to have taken the news well, and know Adam has PWA and its fans at interest. Now PWA goes through the difficult task of re-structuring, over several firings and leaves because of the managerial team shake up. However Vanstt has come through and hired an opponent for Jr. Heavyweight Champion Ice. Ice will go head to head with British wrestler "Urban" LA Pheonix, who looks good during a House Show Event. "This man has been impressing a lot of people in England, and is certainly the next step for PWA.....His first appearance was not as good as he can give, but nerves always play a part." Also on the No Remorse card we will see Gary "Hardcore" Gore defend his Xtreme Title against Dave Damented, who has been having a lot of trouble with the PDA Posse as of late. However Gore, a member of the TKK, has an intense rivalry with the PDA Posse as well, but will the two put their own differences aside if Al Ritter and his henchmen DJ Boy With A Bomb Strapped To His Face and Fat Man appear? In a triple threat match we will see Kato Suarez, Hellseeker and The Masked Burrito all pit out. Braindead will get his chance at the Impact Championship as he goes head to head with "The Silent Assasin" Brien Cage, who has held the title for a relativly long time now, the only man coming close to winning the title was Damien Steele who beat Cage earlier in 2004, but was fired before he got his shot. Japanease sensation Stryke will go into a Fatal Four Way with former GWA World Heavyweight Champion, The Dean, a member of the TKK Gus Heiler and a Mystery Man. But the main focus of the show, is Universal Champion, Sean Chandler defending his title against one half of the Tag Team Champions, Brian Blaze, who has turned the heat up since returning from knee surgery. No Remorse looks to be picking up pace, and lets hope this is a step in the right direction for both Adam Vanstt and PWA! I'm Joe Shoe, thanks for tuning in! Professional Wrestling Association
