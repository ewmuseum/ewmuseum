eVolution's A New Dawn

29 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
eVolution presented it’s A New Dawn pay per view tonight with the Caged Rumble and Chambers of Survival matches featured. Below is a quick rundown of what happened throughout the show. Damon O’Halloran and Ali Khadafi got themselves prepared for the Chambers of Survival match that they would both be competing in for the Cell Title. The suspended Morpheus Omega showed up at the arena and ended up causing trouble throughout the entire night in his quest to force himself into the Caged Rumble. Tyler Davidson tried to convince James Kimson to withdraw from the rumble due to his injured elbow. America’s Guardians and The BodySnatchers battled in a TLC Flag match. Eric Knight defended the eVolved Title against Scott Evans. Tyler Davidson introduce Sherri Valmonté as the new Advertising Consultant for eVolution. Deacon was found destroyed in the backstage area presumably by Morpheus Omega. The Tag Team Invitational went down and saw quite a bit of impact from The Syndicate with teams deciding it was in their best interest NOT to compete. Damon O’Halloran and Ali Khadafi continued their search for the person that attacked Ali with a lead pipe on Legends. A former eVolution whose career was ended as result of an in-ring accident made a surprising return. The Chambers of Survival match for the Cell Championship featuring Trevor Norman, Marcus Escobar, Machine, Terlayne Sterling, Malcolm King, Question Beyond X, Damon O’Halloran, and Ali Khadafi. Cameron Griffin and Tyler Davidson discussed what they could do to replace Deacon in the Caged Rumble knowing that they had no one left on the active roster. Shocka and Greatness finally had their one on one battle go down. The Twenty Man Hour Long Caged Rumble match that saw the return of some stars and the debut of another. Sherri Valmonté approached Siege with a proposition. Xander defended the Universal Title against Tyler Davidson. A New Dawn
