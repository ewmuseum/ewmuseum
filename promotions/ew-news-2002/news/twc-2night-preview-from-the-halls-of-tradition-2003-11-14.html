TWC 2Night Preview from The Halls of Tradition

14 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just over two weeks away and the world will witness Revolution. As the path to Pinnacle continues, TWC continues to entertain. TWC Presents: 2Night, November 16th from The Halls of Tradition, Pittsburgh, PA This jam packed card will include: MAIN EVENT: 8 MAN TAG The Project: Saffron/Professional/Jiraiya/Brisbane vs. NightTrain/Connor Banks/Curt O'Hare/??? ---Seamus and Thomas McGibbons are at the epicenter of a power struggle the wrestling world has not seen before. Both men are in partial control of the company that Thomas created, and even Thomas will admit that there is nothing he can legally do about it. What they will do is take it out on each other through the matches. The Project is Seamus' hand-picked destruction squad and to counter that, Thomas has signed on some of the people that he has guided and trusted in this industry. One name remains a mystery, and sources say that no one will guess who it could be. Guess, you just have to tune in to find out. TWC HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE MATCH "The Heir Apparent" Terence Madison(c) vs. Nuada ---Seamus McGibbons continues to try and "soften" up Terence Madison by forcing him to defend his title each and every week. This week he has signed Madison to face off against the dark monster himself, Nuada. Nuada is not a man to be taken lightly, and in order for Seamus to consider Nuada a success, he does not have to win, just beat Madison severly enough to make it easier for his hand-picked successor, Dante Saffron, to get the win in the future. TWC TRANSCONTINENTAL TITLE MATCH "Big F'n C" Drew Thomas(c) vs. Sylence ---Drew Thomas won a hard fought victory at Over the Edge and will put his belt on the line for the second consecutive week against another impressive newcomer. Last week Sylence unveiled his 'Carnival Rules' match, and Ravyn was on the receiving end of quite a beating by some of Sylence's minions. Will Drew Thomas have to fight 4 on 1 like his girlfriend? Or will the Big F'N Cat take out the entire circus on his own? SINGLES MATCH Ravyn vs. "Betrayer of Hope" Sammael ---Sammael is on the trail of Drew Thomas and his Transcontinental Title and he figures if he can take out Ravyn then Thomas will have to put up the belt and fight him to restore her honor. What he doesn't realize is that Ravyn will not just lay down, and Sammael could be in for quite a long night if he doesn't focus. 'The First Lady of Professional Wrestling' is ready to move back up the ladder and reclaim the throne, an Sammael may be the first rung on that ladder. TWC 2NIGHT TITLE MATCH "Product Of Hate" Josh Cantrell(c) vs. Angus "Heinous Hurt" McCurt ---Cantrell and McCurt have been after each other for quite some time now. It was not too long ago that these two men squared off for the 2Night title and turned a 'Match of the Year' contender. Last week McCurt and Cantrell had a very heated exchange which resulted in this match being signed. Will these two be able to replicate their performance from a couple weeks ago? Or did it take too much out of them? TAG MATCH fWo w/Discoboy vs. "The Bitch" Chase Martelle/Durgo Syn ---The funky World order is officially signed to TWC contracts and are ready to start getting the funk IN the TWC. Their first order of business will be to funk out Durgo Syn and Chase Martelle, not a very easy task. Neither Syn nor Martelle seems to be the type to want to get funked up. Will Hector and Leroy be able to show what made them world-wide stars in universal Wrestling? SINGLES MATCH Trinity vs. Braeden Tackert ---Both of these TWC superstars have drawn some unwanted attention recently. Trinity seems to be getting stalked by Sylence and his Dark Carnival, an occurrence that has everyone scratching their heads. Tackert and Callous have been developing quite a hatred for each other over the past few shows, and there seems to be quite a backstory between these two. Will this match stay a one-on-one encounter? The Vegas line is betting heavily that there will interference, and better then average odds that it will be more then one happening as well!!! ALSO THIS WEEK: ---Things between the Brothers McGibbons have been strained this week, with each trying to out-book and out-think the other. What will this mean for Revolution? Will we see more moves that are made to piss each other off? Will this personal rivalry take the federation down from it's now lofty perch? ---Thomas McGibbons has been quite tight-lipped about the identity of the three men that he has asked to wrestle on his behalf at Revolution. No one in the front office is privy to this information, as Thomas has taken it upon himself to handle all aspects of their contracts and employment. Will any clues be given as to their identity? Can they really help Thomas in his fight with Seamus? ---Seamus McGibbons has named Dante Saffron the 'next champion' of TWC. The only problem is that Saffron has not gotten a title match from Madison. Will that change this week? TUNE IN AND FIND OUT! Tradition Wrestling Council
