Asylum "CARNAGE 2001" DVD Preview

12 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A short preview of what you can see on the new "CARNAGE 2001" DVD released by Asylum Enterprises. CARNAGE 2001, from the Air Canada Centre, Toronto January 27th 2001 'The Legend' [name withheld] Vs Boomer McAugust EXERPT: Boomer staggers back into [name withheld] who spins him around and executes the “Legend “Bomb on the barbed wire Stool. Smashing the stool to pieces. Spratt wins by 10 count. Hypnosis Vs Impetigo EXERPT: Impetigo pulls up a bloodied Hypnosis and sets him on the glass Impetigo climbs up on the now setup ladder and is about to leap when Hypnosis rolls off the glass and jumps up on the ladder hammering away at Impetigo. Suddenly from the crowd Deicide appears and as the two men brawl he pushes over the ladder and sends both men falling , Impetigo lands on the hard wood, But Hypnosis falls dead center and through the glass into the pit of tacks. Both men are cut open badly as the blood begins to really flow. Hypnosis manages to sit up and his back is coated in tacks and they are embedded in the back of his skull as Impetigo slowly rolls off the wood and crawls to the back. Deicide laughs before making an exit through the crowd. After a disturbing and controversial ending, Impetigo wins via an assist from Deicide. Reno Davis Vs Koji Tamura EXERPT: The ref got close to Koji and asked him repeatedly if he wanted to give, but to no avail. Koji refused as he clenched his fists and tried to break the uncanny hold. Reno then rolled back and bent Koji's neck forward as it bashed against the mat. This move would be considered a pin if it was a normal match in a normal ring, but not in Asylum. Reno yelled at Koji to give but Koji shook it off and continued to struggle until finally, he passed out. The ref called for the match to end immediately at 20:12. Reno released the move a few seconds later and got up, somewhat disappointed in how he got the win. Bone Breaker Match the Irish Brawler Vs the Inmate EXERPT: He began to pull back, about to break Inmates neck until out of nowhere, Inmate reached back and pried at one of the Brawler's fingers and broke it. The bone popped out and the ref noticed it as Brawler broke the hold and backed away. The ref signaled for the match to end with Inmate winning at a premature 15:24. T.V. Championship Griff Truxell(c) Vs Kenny Rock EXERPT: Kenny is now busted wide open and is barely moving. Griff is quick to his feet and tells the crowd this one is over. Griff lifts Kenny's limp body up and hits a piston DDT. The ref is checking on Kenny, he's not responding. The ref calls for the bell and gives the TV Title back to Griff as he has won this battle by way of knock out. Griff was pushed to the limit by the cocky rookie, but tonight was not Kenny Rocks night as Griff Truxell is still the Asylum TV Champion. Extended Asylum Graphic Violence Vs Tommy Gunn No Exerpt due to a dodgy tape, but it ends as a No contest after interference from Joey and Joseph Gunn. Asylum Championship Archangel(c), Nicodemus, Silence, Chris Credible Vs Mikey Riggs, Sanjuro, Noel, Rico EXERPT: Borst winds up and swings hard on ArchAngel's back! He screams loudly as the razor wire gets caught in his t-shirt. The mace is hanging on ArchAngel's back, caught in the material on his t-shirt, and probably his skin. The referee begins to try to help ArchAngel out of cage, but Borst grabs a 2x4 and pushes the ref out of the way. Borst leans over the Asylum cage and smacks the 2x4 over ArchAngel's head breaking it in two!!! The referee calls for the bell stopping the match!!! Borst has done it! He has regained the Asylum Championship and got some revenge on ArchAngel! Borst is handed the Asylum Championship and raises it high as D'yer Know What I Mean? plays. Borst looks over to ArchAngel who has just been pulled out of the cage and grins. "This is just the start Archie, just the start…" Borst raises the title high once more as the fans cheer and Carnage comes to an end. Asylum
