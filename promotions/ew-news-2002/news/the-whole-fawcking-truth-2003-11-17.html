The Whole FAWcking Truth by [name withheld]

[name withheld] is current Head of Talent Relations for Furiously Addictive Wrestling. The Whole FAWcking Truth written by [name withheld] So, we’re heading for the Christmas period. A time when FAW business is usually down and an impromptu hiatus takes place over a week or so… But this year is so much different. Last Sunday at Deceit, one of the most important angles in a long time began, and everyone’s eyes will be on Furiously Addictive Wrestling broadcasts on a regular basis once again. Tony Vigilante was gunned down live on pay-per-view, which brought his two-month reign with his second FAW World Championship to an end. Carted out of the arena a bleeding mess, Vigilante has vowed revenge. But what does this mean for the FAW World Title picture? With Tony V now out of action indefinitely, Violator as FAW World Champion and many upper-carders currently in shape and looking for a taste of success, a power struggle is most certainly emerging. Here are just a few of the contenders who will be vying for the gold as we turn into 2004: Sniper: Currently bathing in his sweet dream as FAW’s wonder kid of 2003, Sniper has set the world alight with his rise to the upper echelon of Furiously Addictive Wrestling in a few short months. The current International Champion has a legendary win over Spawn behind him after last Sunday’s Iron Man Match, quite possibly the match of the night, and a very promising future ahead of him. Orlando Rivera: The third piece of the jigsaw in the triple threat main event at Deceit, Orlando Rivera was tipped to take the gold from Vigilante. He’s now in a position where he is the most likely candidate to be a dangerous threat to Violator’s reign. 2004 will be a vital year for Rivera, and at the moment, he must capitalize on his chances. The first one he blew, the second – he may just succeed in grasping. Lee Smith: Fresh off a very successful campaign against The Meditator in a five-match series and after going through hell in a Steel Cage on pay-per-view, the former president has progressed very well over the last few months to be considered a serious contender for top gold. The “Lee-gend” may just have to settle some old scores before he is allocated a World Title shot, but the International Title is most certainly on the horizon – this Monday night on Panic to be precise. Apocalypse: 2002’s success story has had an underwhelming 2003, but recent successes may just help him get back into the race for the World Championship. The September-December period last year was the most successful for Apocalypse since his FAW career began. So far, he’s done everything right. He just needs the commitment and he could be one of FAW’s top stars. He’s most definitely tipped for stardom in 2004. Rzarector: I’ve always had mixed views on Rzarector, but I have faith in his ability. Two bumper feuds with the 9th Wonder Of Foam and Tony Vigilante have kept him busy for the most part this year. He has two paths he may choose – a high profile feud with the returning Hardcore Hoefel or mounting a World Title challenge. Whatever path he chooses, they will be beneficial to his 2004 campaign. But what about the new champion? Violator is lucky to be in the position he is right now, with no Tony V rematch to worry about, he can show up to Panic this Monday night and have a carefree night. But we all know that’s not going to happen… The new champion will most certainly be looking to impress Lisa Tien and would be foolish to sit back and relax while the possible challengers gear up to confront him. Meanwhile, The Vice Squad will be less than happy defending their Tag Team Championships for the second time in eight days against FAW’s new Italian blood, Sharky De Niro and Kash Pachino. In a disappointing match at Deceit, The Vice Squad retained thanks to a double countout. Though I have a feeling Monday night’s rematch will be a completely different story. It’s time for Hank Sharpe and Lance Carter to make some viable defenses against decent opposition. Monday night’s main event won’t be one to miss as Sniper makes a rare TV defense of his International Title against Lee Smith. Orlando Rivera and Violator are both missing from the card but are expected to be in the house.
