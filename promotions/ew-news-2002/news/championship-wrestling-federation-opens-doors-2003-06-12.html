Championship Wrestling Federation opens doors.

12 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With the anticipation of companies like WWC, SCW, hWo, one organization has opened and has promised to shake the very foundation that the wrestling industry stands on. On July 10th, 2003, Levi Brooks, son of former fed promoter Don Brooks, announced during a press conference that the Championship Wrestling Federation, a federation that was once known as the Championship Wrestling Alliance, had been handed over to Levi Brooks in his father's will. The CWA had ran throughout the 1970's where Don Brooks had to compete against the likes of the WWWF and the NWA. The CWA struggle for airtime, and soon filed for bankruptcy. Don Brooks had kept the copyright to the CWA, and in 2000 confronted by his son Levi about a possible rebirth for the CWA. Neither Don or Levi had the financial aid to do so. Levi had dreamt of one day having the money and the rights to his own wrestling promotion. In 2001, Don Brooks passed away at the age of 68. Levi Brooks was left with the rights to the Championship Wrestling Alliance. A.J. Brooks was a successful business man, a man who was an executive for his own communications company, a man who had always found a way to succeed in life. When A.J. was informed of Levi's company, he immediately set forth plans to help Levi out financially. On one condition... That Levi handed half of the company's ownership to A.J. They were now business partners, and with a new name, new attitude, the two brothers set forth two different goals on one project. To become even more wealthy. To entertain the fans, and show high quality wrestling. The cWf has begun discussing employment opportunities. At the moment, Gabriel So'ialu and Elijah Arson of ACW have been signed to contracts as well as former IOW/thReat Superstar, Christian Embers. More information to come....
