DWA: Tag Team Championships Retired?

10 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just a few weeks after a World Tag Team Title defense was signed between champions, Future of Wrestling and challengers, Intoxicated Justice, rumor ensues about the possibility of retiring the titles after Testament. It has been no secret to anyone that when former DWA owner, Mason Stone first introduced the Tag Team Championships several months ago, he did so reluctantly. "So many feds have tag team title belts that do nothing but sit and collect dust," Mason once said in a previous interview. Despite his apprehension, he went ahead and created the tag team titles in response to the unification of the World and American Championships. And with the formation of so many new tag teams in response, Mason forgot about the apprehension he felt. Fast forward to present day. In just a few weeks, the DWA will broadcast their biggets pay per view to date, Testament, and it could very well be the last time the Tag Team Championships are defended. Jordan Brannigan has not hid the fact that he feels as if the current tag team division is a failure. "Look, you've got the FoW, you've got Intoxicated Justice...and that's it," Jordan says in response to the rumors. "Sure there's The Revolution, but when one member of the team is the World Champion, there are other things to concern yourself with." While Jordan refused to comment one way or another concerning the fate of the Tag Team Titles, things don't look too good, and whoever walks out as the tag team champions at Testament may be the last one's in the DWA history. DWA: Dragon Wrestling Association
