[NGW] Scottywood banned from Storm

30 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NGW Owner and the creator of NGW Tuesday Night Storm, Scottywood, was banned from the show Sunday by John Hitchin's "final" executive order at Xtrememania IV. If you though all the shock of Xtrememania was over when Scott King won the World Title then you were certainly wrong. The second main event of the night feature NGW Owner Scottywood going against the Self-Proclaimed Owner John Hitchin in The Perfect Match. The match itself will go down in the books as one of the greatest in NGW history. Scottywood himself took an at least 20-25 bump as he hung from the suspended cage and tried to drop a elbow on Hitchin who was on top of a table which was on top of the 2 floor cages. But Hitchin moves and Scottywood went through the cage and landed on the mat hard. He was taken out on a stretcher and Hitchin was just about to be proclaimed the winner when Scottywood limped back out to the ring and set it on fire! The two then ended up inside the 3rd and final cage, which was suspended in the air. Hitchin shot Scottywood with a paintball gun once and deliver his finisher the MayhemBomb, which Scottywood kicked out of. Scottywood then made a come back and delivered the Scottydriver and Hitchin kicked out. Scottywood then cut a hole in then cage wall and tried to throw Hitchin out of it. But Hitchin reversed it and was able to MayhemBomb Scottywood through a barbwire wrapped table and picked up the pinfall. In the stipulations it was said the John Hitchin would be given one last executive order if he won. No one had a clue what he would do before he left NGW, and no one would have ever guessed. John Hitchin took over full control of the NGW's flagship show Tuesday Night Storm and ordered Scottywood be banned from the show, never to step foot at it again! So this means Hitchin will be staying with NGW and will not leave. But what about Scottywood? He has been banned from the show he created and owned for 3 and a half years. What will happen on the very first 100% Hitchin controlled Storm? Right now we have no idea but it is certain that Storm this week will be one hell of a show. But don't say your goodbyes to Scottywood yet, the Owner of NGW will still be given TV time on the now returned Friday Night Extreme which he still has control of and will air on August 1st, which he will certainly have something to say to John Hitchin. Right now the whole balance and control of NGW is in question and hopefully after Extreme and Storm we will have more answers to the millions of questions NGW fans have. Next Generation Wrestling
