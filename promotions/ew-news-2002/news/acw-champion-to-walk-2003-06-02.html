ACW Champion To Walk?

2 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As ACW's problems mount, it seems that it's World Champion, ICU, seems to be having major contractual issues. After last night’s weekly Courage, one of the major stories coming out of the show was the news that ACW, is starting to cut costs as it’s fight to stay with the leading competitors in the market is costing them more money than they can afford. The biggest story of this cost cutting scheme is the re-negotiation of wrestler contracts, with the current ACW World Champion, ICU, being one of the first to be contacted about a revised contract. However, upon meeting ICU, his reluctance to speak to ACW staff about a lower paid contract has caused chaos in the ACW camp. A spokesperson at ACW HQ said. “We have held initial talks with ICU, and a couple of other superstars about a revised contract, some of them have went well and some of them have collapsed, such as ICU’s, however, we are working on another meeting with ICU where this should all be sorted out.” Many internet journalists are sceptical about ACW’s chances of making their World Champion accept a reduced contract, but one thing is for certain, if the talks do break down, ICU will be one of the biggest free-agents around today, and with the ACW World Title around his waist, ACW could stand to lose not just their top draw…but their reputation as a formidable force.
