HSW: Shocker Preview for 1/11

1 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Headstrong Wrestling holds its first show of 2004 from Miami, Florida as Shocker is live! Listed below are the matches scheduled for this event from the Main Event down to the opener. Main Event Champions vs Champions Non Title Tag Team Match Assassin & The Man With No Name [VS] The Dark Alliance [In a match booked by Joey Matthew, the Headstrong and Evolution Champions will team up to colide with the Tag Team Champions on the first Shocker of 2004 in the Main Event! Commissioner Rey Valiente has to be hot about this high profile match Joey has booked, but will MWNN and Assassin really get along?] FnX Championship Match FnX Division Rules Devon Jacobs [VS] Blake Rozdale [Commissioner Valiente's pick for the first FnX Title challenger was Devon Jacobs. Will Jacobs score and upset win over the newly crowned FnX Champion, or will Blake Rozdale prove that he is FnX title material by retaining?] Evolution Title Contendership 6 Man Elimination Match (Pinfall or Submission) Participants: Bryan Bryn Bryson, Aaron Anubis, Jason Kage, El Nutso, Tai Hashi, Heather Halliwell [With the news breaking of Sabre walking out on Headstrong Wrestling and Man With No Name no longer having a good opponent for his eVo title, Commissioner Valiente has decided that a Battle Royal will decide the next challenger for the Evolution championship.] Headstrong Title Contendership Match Heather Halliwell [VS] Tyler "Too Sweet" McPherson [HSW Chairman Joey Matthew originally had Tyler McPherson and Sabre in mind for a match to determine the new Headstrong Title Contendender, but with Sabre leaving HSW, Joey had no opponent for McPherson. Commissioner Valiente stepped up and said that Heather Halliwell should get a shot. What is going on in Valiente's mind? It seems he believes McPherson will have a walk in the park in this match, will he be right?] Singles Match FnX Division Rules "Outrageous" Brian Rage [VS] Skip "MFin" Rawlin [Brian Rage has informed HSW Chairman Joey Matthew that he can definately be hardcore when the time calls for it. He said that he is tired of people doubting him, and on Shocker he gets the chance to prove just why he is called Outrageous against Skip Rawlin!] Tag Team Match X-Rated Ikons [VS] Reality [The X-Rated Ikons barely didn't win the Tag Team gold at Christmas Chaos, but they aren't going to stop there. They will 'welcome in' HSW's newest tag team, Reality, on Shocker!] Singles "Debut" Match FnX Division Rules Kostoff [VS] Paul Methric [Kostoff and Paul Methric both make their extreme debuts to Headstrong Wrestling on the first edition of HSW Shocker of 2004. Kostoff is well known for being extreme, and the newcomer, Paul Methric, has also been said to be very extreme himself. There is sure to be a lot of pain and suffering in this match!] Headstrong Wrestling Rise of the Legends 2004
