P.'s Corner # 3!

23 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, he's back this week with yet another controversial column. But this week, what will he bitch about? Where's my graphic~?^^^^ All right folks, I'm back this week with another boring day. Today hasn't been the best day for the wrestling world as we all just found out how boring it really was. First off, fWo Survivor III has met their merge and Tim Walden and Jane Matheson are still in the competition... Ok... good luck to the two of them, but I'm really surprised that Walden has made it this far in anything, a guy who was a virtual jobber in PCW is now looking good to win the entire competition, even though the odds on in favorites for the win are still L.O.D. With the Green Team members still seemingly outnumbering the Gold Team members at the merge, one can only imagine that the Green Team will take the Gold Team out and then work on each other. And if this happens, then I will hate things for L.O.D. in the end, as of course, they're a former Gold Team member. As far as other wrestling tid bits. hWo has kicked their re-start off with two bang shows. Of course, I'm only following the federation because old pal, Semore Smoke, is currently on the roster working a great comeback to his wrestling career, which was killed in PCW... hmm... maybe Tim Walden is worth something, since his career was pretty much killed in PCW as well, do you see a trend? I do... First you had Token Weed, he was in PCW for a short time, he leaves, joins tA and becomes of the biggest stars ever. Padrona, now of fWo fame, is formerly Primetime Championship Wrestling, as well as Semore Smoke, Tim Walden, Ivan Stanislav, and many more. Seems PCW can't handle talent the way it should now does it? But I'm not gonna take shots at PCW, it isn't worth taking shots at, feds like tA, aWc, ACW, etc. are. But back to hWo, since I know I'm jumping around horribly. hWo has signed a great roster, and with last nights show, it seems to be almost impossible to stop now. With current fWo sensation, Dynamo, now on the hWo roster, the federation can only grow better. With an all-star roster of a bunch of former ACW (no pun intended with the "all-star" statement), aWc, 21w, PCW, etc. stars, the federation can only gain success. But that's my one fed plug of the week, other feds that deserve a mention I should say could be fWo, which is running strong as usual... that's about it... where has all the hype gone? I don't see much worth reporting or mentioning this week, it's almost pointless to even write a column. Oh well, to my personal rant of the week I suppose. Why the hell does just about everyone that does a column for eWNews now have an image for themselves, except for me! I swear! I will have graphics on this site before I die! I've already looked into getting some people to help make sure of that anyways. One being a guy who goes by the name of Brandon E., a well-known wrestling webmaster, who's done several jobs in the past, and has even helped run a few feds in the past, even the old FSW, which was owned by Painkiller back in 2001. But I don't see why Poison Ivy, Mitch, and now Stanislav can have graphics! And I don't! It's degrading to me! A respected name in this business! But screw it, I'll just bitch about it until I get what I rightfully deserve and want, and I'll have it soon, and that's for sure. What else to talk about... Geez, this week was just pathetic, there's nothing left to talk about. Oh wait! OSE leaving WO, there's something worth reporting! OSE recently left WO after an argument between OSE owner, Jeff S. and WO owner Ron M. AKA The Phantom Booker. It appears that the two didn't see eye to eye on something and Jeff took the injuctions of removing OSE, a federation that The Phantom Booker helped build, away from WO for good, which some say is the best move anyways and that WO was only holding OSE down from it's full potential as a wrestling federation. With OSE becoming wildly popular and almost catching fWo and WWE in ratings on TV right now, one can only believe that now OSE will look to bring in new fans and that it'll then surpass the two in ratings soon, so keep an eye on OSE as well... well, that's two feds this week that get a spot, good for the two of them, hWo and OSE. Damn... that's it for now? Crap, I swear, this isn't right, a week that is so boring doesn't even deserve a column, but I love you fans all so much that I'm willing to give you one, even though this week in review is just crappy. I hope you did enjoy, even though I doubt you did this great column. Since you're all idiots who suck monkey balls. Later -Mick P.
