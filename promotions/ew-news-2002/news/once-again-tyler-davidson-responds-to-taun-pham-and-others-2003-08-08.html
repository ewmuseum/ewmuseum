Once Again Tyler Davidson Responds To Taun Pham and Others

8 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The war of words between PRIME and eVolution continue. The war of words between eVolution and PRIME has continued to heat up over the past week with many more members of both federations taking part in the crossfire. Cameron Griffin, Terlayne Sterling, Rock Startling of PRIME, Shane Wethers himself, and Taun Pham have all come out this past week and had some comments to make concerning the situation. Tyler Davidson had kept quiet for the most part since his initial comments concerning the potential lawsuit that Taun Pham of PRIME had threatened him and eVolution with as a result of signing Shane Wethers to a contract. Now though with the hostilities not dying down at all Tyler has decided to comment again after being caught up with backstage Saturday night at the taping of thReat’s Flaw show. “Taun backtracks more than a major league outfielder on a Barry Bonds fly ball. Whether his comments concerning a lawsuit or other nonsense he wants was rumors or fact is irrelevant at this point and nothing that he does from this point on will be of any concern to eVolution or myself. Taun and I both know that it is indeed a load of crap that Wethers was signed to PRIME and his primary motive was to try and get a little bit of revenge against me for walking out on him just days before PRIME’s first big pay per view event. Shane and I have already had a long discussion concerning the situation and he’s assured me there was nothing signed between the two of them and it’s not our fault that Taun doesn’t realize just because someone signs a contract with the name Shane doesn’t mean that it’s Shane Wethers signing the damn thing. Taun just needs to head his happy go lucky ass back to Japan I think and retake those business ethics courses he obviously failed in the past. As far as I’m concerned the situation is over as Shane has already made his eVolution debut and is looking forward to a long and prosperous career in eVolution now that he is once again healthy. For those who haven’t heard yet Shane also had some things to say concerning Taun Pham on Legends this past week and a full transcript is up on our website so feel free to head on over there and check out Shane’s take on the matter.” What about his own workers, or former workers in the case of Terlayne Sterling, that have spoken out so far concerning the situation between the two companies? “As for Terlayne and Cameron Griffin’s weighing in on the situation there really isn’t much I could say on the matter. Both men have always been very outspoken in their views and I come to expect it whether they criticize me or not. Terlayne isn’t even an active member of the company anymore as it is following his retirement so even if I wanted to punish him in some form I wouldn’t be able to. Of course why would I want to anyway? He’s the Jeremy Shockey of wrestling as far as I’m concerned. As for Cameron I trust him to keep his attention on the Cell Division where it belongs and not worry about how I’m handling things. All he needs to worry about is making sure that my signature is at the bottom of his paycheck when pay time comes around. He of course might also want to look out for Damon O’Halloran now as well as he seems to be on a bit of a warpath with that Cell Championship now in his possession.” The only member of PRIME thus far other than Taun Pham to speak out was Rock Startling who also reportedly made some comments towards eVolution stars being something along the lines of “common folk” compared to the stars of PRIME. “Rock who? Are we talking about the movie actor or someone else here? Either way it really doesn’t matter now does it? Rock is more than likely just a bit upset that it was Wethers that was offered a contract and not him. End of story, end of issue as far as I’m concerned. PRIME just needs to move on and get over the fact that Wethers chose the spotlight over the flashlight.” With that Tyler ended any further discussion with reporters and instead went to begin his own preparations for his Cruiserweight Title defense against Sergey Kovhachek later in the night. eVolution
