DWA: LeStatt Knight Comes to a Decision?

4 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As reported earlier in the week DWA SuperStar and now Current Velocity and World Champion LeStatt Knight may have seen his final match in the DWA and quite possibly his entire career. As reported earlier in the week DWA SuperStar and now Current Velocity and World Champion LeStatt Knight may have seen his final match in the DWA and quite possibly his entire career. It seems after an emotional night following Testament, and doing alot of soul searching the DWA Champion has come to some sort of decision regarding If he shall stay here in the DWA or go and take care of his family business in Spain. No one besides his Best friend RaZiel knows and he wouldn't spill it when questioned. When I myself went up to RaZiel aka David Sandy this is what he had to say ... " Roberto has had a wonderful and very emotional night ... beating Alex Grey and becoming the World Champion to the delight of the crowd is something he has dreamed of all his career. He has indeed made a decision regardling his family issues ... I can't say what though. He will however Make his announcement Live Tuesday night at Showdown ... so stay tuned ... " What LeStatt has decided is unknown ... but He has had an incredible career not just in DWA but in his two former federations of XCW and the very well known formally Steven Caldera Owned Global Championship Wrestling. No matter what the outcome is From myself, Mark Post and the rest of the DWA family ... we wish LeStatt Knight the best of Luck ... God's Speed. DWA: Dragon Wrestling Association
