Antipathy Wrestling: Wrestler Diagnosed With Alcohol Posining

11 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Reports coming off of the sports wire, are that Antipathy Wrestling star, Stewart Hix was checked into a hospital for possible alcohol poison, according to a unnamed source. Stewart recorded his latest tendency and after wrapping it up he continued to drink until passing out. Jessica Hix, Stewarts wife came home to find Stewart passed out on the floor and not breathing. She then called 911 and paramedics arrived on the scene, taking Stewart to the local hospital in Tallahassee. He was reported doing fine once he came to, after which he walked out of the hospital. We called the hospital and talked to the doctor that was helping Stewart out to try and get a inside on the story. Here is what the doctor had to say. "Stewart Hix at 9:00 am this morning checked himself out without my consent. Stewart is in no shape to be out of hospital care at this time, but what could my staff and me do? We tried to tell Stewart he is in no shape to leave just yet, but Stewart replied back that them nor anyone else tells Stewart Hix what to do." aW officials had no comment on the matter at hand, and weren't if Stewart would be attending Disturbance this week or not. However, we did happen to speak with an aW official who asked for his name not to be released. "In my opinion Stewart Hix shouldn't be at this weeks Disturbance until he is well, 100%. He seems to be having some major depression problems at the moment, and with the use of alcohol, isn't going to make things any better. But as we all know, Stewart is going to be Stewart and he's going to do what he wants, so at this time who really knows if Stewart will show or not" said the unnamed source. Makes for an interesting topic don't you say, more news at this story develops... Antipathy Wrestling
