TS: Major Changes

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After many hours of deep thought and concept discussion, President Koch of Twilight Slams decides to rid of Saturday Night’s namesake. When you think of the name Saturday Night, perhaps one corporate affiliation comes into mind, the creation staff of World Championship Wrestling and their flagship Saturday show of the same name. With the threat of a lawsuit on their backs, President Koch made up his mind to expand his franchise even further, changing the aptly named Saturday Night into Livewire. Within this new name is a new contract with the Prime Time Central Channel syndication. Many changes have been brought to Twilight Slams over the course of their grand opening on January first of the year two thousand and two. We have seen many wrestlers fired from their positions for breaching their contractual duties, we have also seen many step into the dark and part from Twilight Slams. However, in all this time of despair, President Robert Koch kept pushing himself beyond his own limits. Koch has decided to change the name of the Saturday Night program into Livewire, and the name says it all. Koch has stated that the show will be more intense and deliver the charge that the TS franchise needs to come up on the top once more. There have been many doubts about this new move since World Wrestling Entertainment copyrighted Livewire for their weekly Saturday morning television recap show. Livewire is also the name of the summer 2003 drink put out by Mountain Dew. Koch has stated that Twilight Slams is in now way affiliated with either of the companies. So, with that being said, be sure to check out Livewire in a new time slot next Saturday on the Primetime Central Channel. This is of course if they are signed to a new contract with the channel. If so, Livewire will be live at ten o’ clock Eastern Standard Time, still on its regular Saturday evening. Twilight Slams
