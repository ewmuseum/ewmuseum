tA The Show finally airs!

4 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After a long wait of about a week, Asylum Fighting has finally aired it's long awaited show, but what surprises happens in The Show that may change how every tA fan looks at Asylum again? Well you'll need to check it out to find out! tA The Show! Delayed due to some technical difficulties, or an unknown cause, The Show didn't air on it's original set date in late May, but has finally aired and you can find the full written results if you missed it on TNN! The results link can be found below, but here was the card for the show. Eddie Scott Poser Vs Fatts McGarron Legion of Dairy© Vs Splink Sebastian Thompson Vs Null-State Ty Hughes Vs Lucas Main Event: The Freak© Vs Tommy Gunn These matches plus tones of other surprises from the Asylum Fighters! tA The Show for May 26, 2003
