Lisa Tien Rocks FAW

15 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lisa Tien made her debut as FAW Owner/General Manager at the Diabolical Day Pay-Per-View this past Sunday, and she left her mark in familiar fashion. Diabolical Day aired and it’s safe to say that it not only lived up to its billing, but surpassed all expectations that fans of FAW and even company officials had for the event. Success was on the line in the Diabolical Day Tournament, and came at a heavy price, as destiny and emotion drove Tony Vigilante to his glory. Tony Vigilante walked away with everything and a guaranteed shot at the FAW World Heavyweight Title while three were left wondering what could have been. Also at Diabolical Day, Lisa Tien made her first calls as FAW General Manager and immediately made an impact, rocking the foundations of FAW, as we know it. Her actions will be felt fan-wide on Panic as the proposal to bring back FAW is WAR to FAW programming will be decided by the fans. Lisa Tien wants the fans in attendace to make a decision at the beginning of Panic that simply involves them holding one of two signs into the air. The first sign reads ‘WE WANT FAW IS WAR’ and the other reads ‘WE DON’T WANT FAW IS WAR’. Diabolical Day Results: - Enigma defeated Francesco Garcia by Pinfall. - Tony Vigilante defeated Chris Thunder by Submission to advance to the Diabolical Day Tournament Final. - Spawn defeated Sniper by Pinfall to advance to the Diabolical Day Tournament Final. - Khristian Call defeated The Meditator, Damien Ryan and Horazon to become the New Extreme Champion. - The Genome Project defeated Hardcore Hoefel by pinfall. - The Vice Squad defeated the House of Pain to win the vacant Tag Team Titles. - Chris Thunder defeated The British Bomber in a Last Man Standing Retirement match. - Tony Vigilante defeated Spawn to become Diabolical Day King 2003. - Rzarector retained his World Title as he defeated the 9th Wonder of Foam.
