ACW NOW GETS RATINGS~!

1 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's been a while since anybody heard from him, but on Courage it seemed the Big, Bad VossMan has made his return. And here are his thoughts... Well, it's been a long while since my pinkies graced the keys of my board, so I thought I'd give a shout out to all those keen wrestling fans that can read. Hey there Peter and Miguel. Now that that's taken care of, I've got an important announcement to make... As you guys know, I appeared on Courage last week. It was quite a surprise to a lot of people. Me stepping down a level and competing in such a bottom rung fed and all. But I assure you, they have a fantastic television time slot and it's a lot of exposure. It'll bring at least two zeros to the end of my paycheck. I can assure you of that. The important announcement you ask? Well, there's a couple. So hang in tight, like your mum's thighs around your ears. The first announcement is I, J. Leslie Voss, put Alias over. It was really quite impossible feat. There were a lot of people behind the scenes telling me that it couldn't be done. That there was no way in Hell that I could actually make people remember this band geek's name. Well... Shock horror horror. I did it. I mean, him speaking on the mike didn't do him any favours, but luckily I could keep him to somewhat of a minimum. Just imagine what it would have done to his name if I hadn't come out there at all and stop him trying to whip the fans in a frenzy with his catch phrase (don't get me started on that again) any longer than he did. FOR CHRIST'S SAKE! I GOT A BETTER REACTION WITH HIS CATCH PHRASE~! But if I could be serious for a minute (waits for a letter from WWE for breach of copyright). I don't think there is a more pathetic excuse for a World champion in my life. I know. I've been a World champion. I held the Spinebuster World title for three months! THREE MONTHS! I know the prestige and responsibility that is put on a World champions shoulders. You're carrying the company. And if they think that a Net surfing virgin like that can carry their company, they've got another thing coming. He's about as honourable as registered voter in California. He's got the charisma of a potato. I know some of you from Idaho are arguing the point you know a potato with charisma, Hell, I know your mother too, but Alias is plainly and simply like shooting heroin. One word from his mouth and he'll leave you dazed and confused. The man forms sentences almost as well as Sylvester Stallone. EXCEPT SLY HAS CHARISMA! I can hear you smarks saying that Alias pinned me with breakDOWN. SilverHAWK's finisher. But I'll have you know that it was all a ploy. I'll have you know he didn't have me for a second. It was just a cog rolling in the big machine that is taking that band geek down. Did you not see Khristian Keller come running down and destroying Alias with the chair? Did you not see how quickly I got to my feet to destroy the "Tin Angel"? Tin Angel? What kind of nickname is that? For crying out loud! That's like getting the silver medal at the Paralympics. Not only are you the first loser, you're deformed, too! But enough about Alias. Quite frankly, the thought of him make my skin crawl in the way your mum does when she does toe touches. And I'm talking about her camel, if you know what I mean. I want to get to my big news. The news that shocked the wrestling world to its very core. COURAGE GOT RATINGS! Yes. For the first time in All-Star Championship Wrestling's history, it actually got some good ratings. Granted, next week's ratings should be much more impressive, considering the fact that I'll be there and more people will know about it. Last week was a surprise, but phone calls were going all across America letting people know I was on telly. ACW got ratings. I know. You're shocked. And there's only one man to thank for it. Only one man who made this grand event happen. One man who made it all possible. The Main Ratings Draw. The Seat Filler. The 5.9 Nielsen Pointer! The Big... Bad... VossMan! I brought the ratings into Courage. Not Alias. Not Jason Kain. Not Keegan or Quinton fucking May. It was me. And me alone. Sure, Khristian Keller, SilverHAWK and SVJ entertained a little, too. But it was because I opened and finished up the show that the people stayed watching and more tuned in. I don't mean to brag, but I'm fucking good. I'm the best there is. I'm wrestling's alternative to sex. Everybody likes it big, entertaining and smooth. And that's me. I make the ladies make puddles. I make the men jealous. I make the children cry. I am J. Leslie Voss and you definitely aren't. I'm the best thing that ever happened to ACW. I beg to you to challenge me on that. You just keep watching and I'll keep entertaining. Yours in entertainment, J. Leslie Voss. All-Star Championship Wrestling
