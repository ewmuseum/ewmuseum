Alinie Fanco Kidnapped on Smash 22

17 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fred Fusion and Eugene FitzPatrick kidnap Alinie Fanco on coreWrestling's flagship show, Smash. This past Friday night on Episode 22 of coreWrestling's weekly television show Smash, Alinie Fanco, girlfriend to coreWrestling superstar "Death's Angyl" Daniel Adams, was kidnapped in retaliation to Adams' attack on current Legacy Champion, Frederick Fusion. On coreWrestling's first Smash episode after their most recent Pay-Per-View, Vendetta, Daniel Adams was brutally attacked by Frederick Fusion during his main event match with Jack Diamond for no apparent cause other then for Fusion's amusement. This attack came in the aftermath of a segment revealing Adams to be Alinie Fanco's much coveted mystery man. On Smash 22, Fusion came to the ring, calling out Adams to explain his reasons behind attacking Adams on the previous Smash. Fusion said that his attack on Adams was out of pure jealousy for Adams holding the main event position while Fusion, the Legacy Champion, had not even had a match on the previous week's Smash. Furthermore, Fusion added that the attack was also precipitated by his learning of the half-british Adams' decision to participate on Team USA rather then Team UK in the 8-Man Elimination Tag-Match scheduled for next week's Episode of Smash. Eugene FitzPatrick then came out, wearing a mask of the English flag, and attacked Adams from behind leaving Adams sprawled out in the ring and revealing FitzPatrick to be Fusion's new manager. Adams' match at Vendetta aganist FitzPatrick, where he subsequently lost, had been thought to be the end of the feud between the two. In retaliation to the sneak attack by Fusion on the previous week's Smash and then again by FitzPatrick on this week's Smash, Adams attacked Fusion in his locker room backstage on Smash. It was an attack Adams had alluded to earlier in the show during a segment with girlfriend, Alinie Fanco. Fanco was then taken from Adams' locker room during a televised segment at the conclusion of the show by FitzPatrick who took her to a van where Fusion was also waiting. The kidnapping was a retaliatory action to Adams' own attack aganist Fusion earlier in the evening. The segment concluded with Adams reading a note that said if he were to call the police on FitzPatrick and Fusion that Fanco would be killed. Adams nor FitzPatrick or Fusion have been able to be reached for comment as of press time, though Fanco has released an Insight from the cell where she is being held captive on coreWrestling television. Fanco has been booked in a tag-team match with long-time rival, Artemis aganist two newcomers to the scene in coreWrestling on this week's Smash (Episode 23). It has yet to be forseen has to how her kidnapping will affect the match in terms of her participation. Adams also will be participating in an 8-Man Tag-Team elimination match in which Fanco's captors, Fusion and FitzPatrick will be participating. More news will be disclosed on the situation as it unfolds.
