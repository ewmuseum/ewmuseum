Vitucci Lays Down Challange To Dynasty Stars......

31 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DW C.E.O Mike Vitucci challanges the stars of DW to make Global Takeover 2 the biggest and best show ever.... DW GT2 - This Sunday Speaking earlier on local Californian radio to hype up next Sunday's DW 'Global Takeover 2' PPV, Mike Vitucci made it clear that the ball was now in the superstars court if they wanted to make their legacy and make GT2 what it has the potential to easily be, the biggest wrestling show ever. "When I came in I announced to the world that GT2 would be the biggest and best show ever, I've done my part now and I've booked the dream match ups like Gillesp/Hellfire X for the World and International Championships, Cage/Lacey and Havoc/Storm in a triple cage. It's now up to the guys in the locker room to step out and give it 110% percent for this company." When asked if there would be any special surprises at 'Global Takeover 2', Vitucci would not go into any detail about what was planned, "Lets just say that Global Takeover 2 is all about the big time so there will be some big things going down. People will be out there trying to make their name and I am sure that just like last year when the legacy's of Shawn Storm and Chris Boswell were produced after their Iron man match, there will be people that will take their chance to go down in history on Sunday."
