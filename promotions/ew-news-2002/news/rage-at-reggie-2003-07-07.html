Rage At Reggie!

7 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In just a week at the Asylum Reggie Harrison-Willis could be in a spot of bother with boss, Joe Campbell. Joe hasn’t exactly had a smooth time over the past few weeks but recently Reggie Harrison-Willis has proved a pest to him and the Asylum viewers. RHW debuted on Fight Hell in a spectacular display of silver confetti and flashing lights. Reggie then in all of sudden broke out into a temper tantrum due to annoying the crowd. Then Reggie being literally booed off stage from the crowd. Then on The Show this week Reggie was pestering owner Joe Campbell about ideas for a new set. Which really annoyed Joe. Reggie ended up accidentally foiling Joe's plan to cheat Token Weed out of a victory against August Monday. Resulting in Token getting a victory. Due to childhood circumstances Reggie has a craving to be the centre of attention. And next week he could get Joe's attention. Joe is reported not to be happy at all. What will Joe do to punish Reggie? Is Reggie one of the most annoying people we'll see in Wrestling? Tune in to The Show to find out. The Asylum
