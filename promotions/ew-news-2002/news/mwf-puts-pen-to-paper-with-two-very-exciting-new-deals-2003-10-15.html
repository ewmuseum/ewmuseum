MWF puts pen to paper with two very exciting new deals...

15 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Vine and Craig Clayborn are two of the MWF's next generation stars and in a press statement it was clear they had bright future's. The Maximum Wrestling Federation has released a statement on the contract signings of The Vine and Craig Clayborn. The statement read: "The MWF is pleased to announce that The Vine and Craig Clayborn have been added to the permanent MWF touring roster after impressive spells with the company during their trials. The Vine showed commitment to the cause and is set to become a main stay of MWF television for many months. Clayborn brings to the MWF a unique style that will help him develop into a future champion amongst the MWF roster. We are very happy to be able to secure the services of both these wrestlers." The Vine has started his MWF career with one win and one defeat and will be looking to try and secure another victory this coming Dynamite when he takes on former MWF Television Champion Sean Harley in a singles match. Craig Clayborn has yet to claim a victory in the MWF but insiders are confident of Clayborn receiving a push in the coming weeks to boost his popularity and win percentage. Being the tag team partner of MWF Television Champion Troy Knight on Dynamite this past week has pushed Clayborn into the spotlight...unfortunatly he was unable to help Troy to the win otherwise his popularity would have gone through the roof! The MWF has also signed up LaVey to the temporary roster. A mysterious wrestler using a satanist gimmick he could have the potential to create a lot of good storylines and being put into a first match on Dynamite with The Ginga can only help him. The Ginga has made good progress sicne joining the MWF and sources close to the management say if he keeps up the good work a title shot could await. Elsewhere, the news that FOX networks are looking for a monday night show has got everyone in the MWF talking backstage. Monday Night Dynamite is a product looking for a good television deal and President Walker is thought to be formulating a bid to try and tempt FOX to take on Dynamite as a ratings winner.
