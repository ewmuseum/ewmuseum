NWC:Pacific Back To Paradise

21 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello Pacific fans! My name is “Prodigy” Eric Patterson and Welcome to the first edition of Back to Paradise, A mix breed of a recap and commentary show for that oh so ever popular NWC: Pacific weekly show, Paradise Lost. Hope you’ll like it. The show this week comes to us LIVE from the sunny city of Albuquerque, New Mexico...the hottest damn place in the universe. No offense to you Albuquerque fans out there but I for one hope it is a very long time until we go back to that frying pan. Anyway, the show starts us off with Joseph London talking about Lance Marshall losing The NWC World title belt to “Bermuda” Billy Page, a vicious blow to both the members of the Traditionalist and the Rogues being that Billy Page is rather BVI friendly. Joseph London, Matt and Luke Robinson then goes on hyping up the card of the night for a little bit, which includes 3 NWC: Pacific titles that would all be put on the line later on tonight. From here we kick off our first segment of tonight, one that involves “Insane” Alec Spade. Now usually when Alec Spade is in front of you on the tube you pretty much know what is going to happen. Some random person, perhaps a reporter or something, will go up to Spade and ask the wrong question or say the wrong thing and from there on in Alec Spade would resort to kicking that random person’s ass to prove whatever point he’s trying to make at the moment. Well that wasn’t the case tonight (well at least not early on tonight, but I’ll get into that later on) in this segment Alec is seen coming out of his black Ford Explorer (which he decides to park in the middle of the road) and then heading towards the building when he is suddenly stopped by Brian McCoy who then begins to ask questions concerning why Spade was there tonight, being that he isn’t scheduled for a match. McCoy ends up making another statement to Spade but he must have said the wrong thing because what follows next was Spade grabbing the reporter by the collar of his shirt. Now here is where Spade would usually resort to violent, but to the surprise of everyone (especially McCoy) Alec doesn’t hurt the poor kid. Instead he says something about having the utmost respect for the leadership of the company and being confident that the person in charge will make the right decision. Eventually Spade releases McCoy’s collar and asks for directions to the arena’s locker room. McCoy’s point to the locker room’s direction and just like that, Spade is off...no beat down or anything. Yep, something is definitely not quite right about this. Newcomers Match: Masafuma Satake versus Pete “The Soiled Mind” Moss Masafuma Satake soils the mind of Pete Moss here with the win by reversing a fireman’s carry attempt made by Moss into a Reverse Neckbreaker that Satake calls the “Matsuzaka Cutter” This is the move Satake uses to finish up the Soiled Minded one for the 1-2-3. Short but fast paced match for the newcomers with Satake having the edge this time around. Better luck next time Moss. Quickly following after that match is a segment footage done by the lovely Julie Reveli. Reveli goes on a rant here about failing the fans and the council for losing the war. Apparently when Lance Marshall lost the NWC World title to Bermuda Billy Page the war has suddenly come to a complete end…I guess. Near the end of this one, Reveli comes to the conclusion that we (I’m guessing she’s referring to those Traditionalist folks when she said we) have lost and that the fans should turn off their television sets and just completely forget about the Council since it is dying. Personally I was disappointed with Reveli’s word. Thankfully though, she would make up for those statements she said later on tonight...but again I’m getting ahead of myself. Next we cut back to Royce Donahue’s office. Here, Pacific’s favorite residential whack job, Alec Spade, decides to pay a surprise visit to the Boss man. This of course did not bode too well with Donahue but in actuality he didn’t have anything to worry about because Spade was uncharacteristically polite and kind during the meeting. Now, anyone that has even an ounce of an idea on who Alec Spade is knows that the words “polite” and “kind” just does not fit the mode of the “Insane one” at all. Spade is definitely up to something but what could that something be? I don’t know (well I didn’t at the time). Getting back to this segment of the show, Spade and Donahue are having a discussion and Alec Spade tells Royce that he supports Donahue’s last week decision (For those of you not in the know, the decision Spade is referring to is Donahue’s refusal to give Spade a title shot). After telling Royce this, Alec Spade walks peacefully out of the office leaving Donahue dumbfounded, and for good reasons. Yet Another Newcomer match: Jason Stylez versus Ticonderoga Wu Ticonderoga WU (By the way, no relation to the Wu Tang Clan...though he does come out to music by GZA and Method man who are WU Tang Clan members. Hmm...) Takes this match ending it with the 37th Chamber finisher. Wu doesn’t have much time to celebrate though when suddenly out of nowhere Alec Spade comes into the ring and knocks the living hell out of Wu with a steel chair! He also makes sure to do the same thing to Jason Stylez, just for the hell of it. Now THIS is the Alec Spade we have come to learn to hate. Spade basically demands that he be given a title shot right this very instant before heading towards the back of the arena. After that fiasco, a commercial for the council-wide Civil War pay-per-view was shown. Should be an excited card, can’t wait to see who will be in it. Coming back to the show, the lovely Julie Reveli is shown to us once again. She’s asking us why we’re still watching the show after asking us to leave (Seriously Julie, do you really think we would listen to you? I mean you’re cute and all but come on.) Anyways, she continues on with that rant of saying all is lost on the war against BVI, well at least that is what it seems like at first. Later on in the seg though, she basically turns it all around and says that she, nor her allies, have given up on the fight and that they will continue to fight against Ben Van Iten despite the odds against them. That’s good to hear I suppose. In the end she closes up her little speech with those “King of Pop” platinum gloves logo that she call hands along with the famous “All the power we need is in Two Hands” quote. From here we go backstage and See Daniel Smith walking around. Smith sees a sign pointing to the BVI sucks Invitational but walks on by. Enters Brian McCoy, who uses this opportunity to pimp himself another interview. Daniel Smith tells McCoy that he doesn’t want anything to do with SLJ or people affiliated with him. McCoy notices though that Smith was unusually happy and asks him why he was. Smith then tells McCoy that tonight a self-proclaimed great man will fall. When McCoy asks for the person in whom Smith was referring too, Mr. Shotgun just brushes Brian off, telling him to find someone else to interview. This must be a good night for McCoy because he manages not to get beat up viciously again...though he did reveal himself losing his sandwich in this segment, and that aint never a good thing. Tag Team Action: Chinese Fire Drill versus The Executioners CFD reigns supreme over the Executioners finishing off Defiant with their Arson finisher. Eh, The match was so-so I guess. Could have been better. After the match the shows cuts back to Donahue’s office where Brian McCoy tries desperately to get Royce’s response concerning Alec Spade’s earlier actions. Donahue’s was obviously upset about the situation and really didn’t want to speak to McCoy at all. McCoy managed to ask Royce Donahue what he is going to do about the situation. Royce quickly answers that question when he begins speaking into his Walkie-Talkie and demanding the security force to detained Alec Spade by any means necessary. The segment ends with Donahue kicking McCoy out of his office. Cut to a short promo from Chris Saint. Here he talks about being the Frontier Champion again, a real champion unlike Bobby Polo (Good way to try get heat I suppose.) He then goes on stating something about concentrating on the match at hand and, um well that was basically it from him. Project PaT versus Richard Hawley Project PaT takes the winusing the Figure four leglock on the big guy. Hawley definitely gave Pat a run for his money though in this match and to tell you the truth from the way Hawley was dominating earlier on I thought he was going to win this thing. Hawley was a fighter to the very end though; he didn’t even tap out to the Figure four (rather passed out) so that has to count for something. All and all this was one of the better matches on the card. Again the show cut to a commercial, this time blatantly self-promoting Paradise Lost. The show comes back to us from commercial showing a badly beat up Ryan Adams in a hospital bed, all thanks to “The Ripper” Derrick Daliente. To his surprise his fiancée, Alexandra Rice, is there to visit him. He isn’t really happy to see her though, not wanting his wife-to-be to see him in the condition that he is in. He requested for Alexandra to go and wait in the lobby for a sec so that he could discuss some things with Denis. Ryan Adams is obviously upset that Denis allowed Alexandra to come and see him like that, but Denis told Ryan that he couldn’t prevent her from seeing him, nor should he had tried to. The conversation eventually leads to discussion of the Traditionalist, Rogues, and Team BVI. Adams doesn’t want anything to do with any of those factions. It was Team BVI’s fault that he was in this situation to begin with and neither the Traditionalist nor the Rogues came to assist him when he took that beating. As far as Ryan Adams is concern he doesn’t need any of them. This footage ends with Adams demanding Denis to take Alexandra back home followed by Adams making a phone call. From here the show heads back to the ring, but not for a match just yet, rather Sylis Walker decides to embrace the fans with his presence. He uses his airtime talking about that oh so famous battle going on between the Traditionalist, Rogues, and Team BVI. Walker talks about how every other faction other than the Rogues are currently on a recruiting spree, of course being a rogue himself he decides to change that and calls out the NWC legend, “Starman” Lee Clark. Walker invites Lee to become part of the Rogue faction in the Pacific, but before Lee could give him a response, Julie Reveli decided to show up and tries to convince the Starman not to fall for the Rogue’s propaganda. As if three people in the ring weren’t enough, Project PaT decides to use this situation to get him some airtime as well. PaT, for the most part, shares the same view as Reveli. Julie, Sylis, and Pat continue on arguing their point while Lee Clark listen on with that confused British expression of his. Eventually, PaT reveals to Sylis that he no longer wants to be Walker’s manager. The scene then quickly cuts to another guy doing a promo, this time it is by someone named “J-Money” Jerome Morgan, and yes he really does call himself that. Apparently Mr. J-Money here was once part of the Atlantic division of the Council but now he’s looking for a new home either here in the Pacific or in Central. He spent his airtime insulting all of the Pacific current champions then he ends up telling us how much he hates Joey Long and BVI. In the end of his little speech, he promises that J-Money will be coming to a region near you. Which region will that be? We don’t know yet. NWCP Frontier Title Championship Match: “The Dreamer” Chris Saint (champion) versus White Chris Saint retains his title beating White with his “Lost Hope” Finisher in what was yet another great match that could have went either way. But the story doesn’t end there because Mr. Alec Spade decides to once again go on another roid rage and lay everyone out in the ring with a steel chair. After doing this, the “Insane One” once again demands that a title match be signed for him, heck he even got the fans chanting, “Sign the match!” in his favor. The security starts to come out and attempt to capture Spade, but he escapes through the fans...wow, didn’t see that coming. Yes that was my lame attempt at sarcasm. After that assault by Spade the show takes us directly back to Royce Donahue who of course is extremely upset with the Spade situation. Royce Donahue had fired the whole security department and instead replaced them with the Albuquerque police force. Wow, smart move Donahue replacing your feeble-minded, but cheap labor with an armed group that is known to put people on hold during a 911 emergency call. The show goes on to commercial break for the third time tonight, this time the NWC: Pacific teases us with its next Pay-Per-View called “Who’s the man.” Could this be a hint that the Man Agent is finally about to be revealed? Oh and for those who are wondering, no, I doubt Dr. Dre or Ed Lover has anything to do with this Pay-Per-View, despite the name of it. Daniel Smith versus John Porter Yep right from the commercial we go into this match. Smith becomes the victor here after using his 12-gauge powerslam on John Porter for the 1-2-3. Another seesaw match right here with Smith coming out on the top this time. But I will say that I am damn surprise Daniel Smith even got the win at all, hell he pretty much knocked the referee out-cold with the butt of that shotgun that he carries around but apparently the ref must have forgot about that little incident and did not call for a disqualification...or something. Well the referee’s decision is final in this case and “Shotgun” Daniel Smith is the victor. After the match, Smith ask for the mic and makes a few comments out to Van Iten concerning destroying the hired gun (That would be John Porter for those who aren’t in the know) and promises to do the same to whoever BVI sends his way. Smith also makes the promise of ending Bobby Polo’s title reign later on tonight. Daniel Smith ends it with that, but unfortunately to our shotgun toting pal a person named Alec Spade is going to be stealing his thunder. Yeppers, that’s right Spade comes right back out and start swinging away with that steel chair of his again, this time taking out Daniel Smith (and leaving him bleeding all over the ring). The police and security must have learned from their pass mistakes because THIS time they surround Spade around the ring area. (Hey, I thought Royce fired the rest of the security, must have changed his mind and came to his senses). Well anyways, Porter finally gets up from off the ground, having just waken up from Smith’s finisher, but that doesn’t stop Spade from using the steel chair on that poor guy as well. The cop and security team used the opportunity to charge into the ring, and FINALLY they managed to apprehend Spade. They lead Mr. Insane back towards the entrance where Royce Donahue is standing there waiting. He tells Alec Spade that if Spade can survive jail time, he’ll see about giving Alec that title match that he wants. The cops and security team continue on to escort Spade out of the arena after that. Up next we have a segment where the lovely Miss Julie Reveli (My, she has been quite busy tonight huh?) managed to run into the most Shadiest (in my opinion) NWC wrestler of all time, “Wise Guy” Wyatt Connors. Aaah, classic Traditionalist/Rogue conflict! This was actually an interesting segment, yeah you had your basic bickering between the two representative of each respective faction going on, but with a twist, Connors wants to combine the force of the Traditionalist and the Rogues together to fight against the evil forces that is Team BVI...but Julie wasn’t buying that. She didn’t trust Connors (and who can blame her, he’s Connors) or the Rogues and felt that the Traditionalist would be better off trying to take out Team BVI by their own means and standard rather than come to a temporary compromise with the Rogues. The situation pretty much ended with both side not coming to an agreement, but one good thing did come from all of this and that would be: The Prodigy’s SCENE OF THE WEEK Indeed. Every week, I will try to find a scene that pretty much stands out from everything else that was on the show and talk a little about it. This week, the honor goes to Julie Reveli and Wyatt Connors. The scene in question takes place near the end of the segment; here is what they had to say... [Cut to clip] Julie: The situation may be desperate, Wyatt. But I would rather put my faith in lightning coming down from the sky and striking Ben Van Iten right in his head, before I would put faith in your word. Connors: [shakes head] You're making a big mistake, Ms. Reveli. You and I could watch each others' backs. Julie: The last place I want you, Connors, is watching my back. [Cut back to the show.] Interesting scene for one main reason, therein, in my opinion, lie the main problem that I, “Prodigy” Eric Patterson, have with the Traditionalist and Rogues. Julie herself has already said earlier that the odds for them winning this war against BVI were against them, so why not compromise a bit with the Rogues? That’s not going to happen because the two sides just don’t share the same philosophy of what needs to be done in this war. And though I can respect that, I truly do believe that their inability to work with each other is going to be their own downfall. Ben Van Iten doesn’t have to and fight this war for victory because the Traditionalist and Rogue are doing a damn fine good job winning this war for him all on their own! That is my opinion on the matter. I could be wrong, but usually I’m not. Let’s move on with the remaining of the show. Following right after the Reveli/Connors drama is an in ring interview from a returning NWC superstar, one that I am extremely excited about! Believe or not kiddies, the NWC that you know now was completely different from the NWC of three years ago. Back then regions were usual located in one state, rather than traveling in different region of an area like we do now. Some of the best NWC feds of all time stayed in one state traveling to different city of said state. Names like MWA, NEWS, HIW and GCW all come into mind...speaking of GCW, the returning superstar that I am talking about is from this fed. He is known as “The Strangler” Seth Bishop, one of the toughest son of a gun you will ever feast your eyes upon in this sport. For a fan that has been following the NWC for quite some time, I am happy to see Seth return back to the new NWC. It will be great to see how he handles the new generation of wrestlers here (and I personally don’t think he’ll have any troubles with doing so). His in ring was pretty much standard, promising some ass-kicking in the future and the likes. He did get a lot of people’s attention though when he announced that he was the only person in the NWC who had a perfect winning record over Lance Marshall. That is definitely an accomplishment to be proud of and it speaks volume for the kind of competitor Seth Bishop is in the ring. I truly think the NWC: Pacific hit a goldmine with this guy and I’m looking forward to watching him get back into action. Cutting backstage, once again Julie Reveli shows up on Pacific Television (Not that I’m complaining), this time she’s in a confrontation with the guy that carries a Shotgun around all the time, Daniel Smith. The two argues about Bobby Polo in this seg. For those who may have forgot, Daniel Smith blames Polo for cheating him out of the Pacific West Coast title a few weeks back, so obviously he’s going to want revenge. The problem is, Julie doesn’t want Smith to interfere in her match with Polo tonight, and she wants to win this thing on her own. The two argue back and forth about Polo for a while until the segment ends with Julie warning Smith to stay out of this match or she won’t be held responsible for what will happen. NWCP Rocky Mountain Title Match: Lord Winslow Darkthorne (Champion) versus Lazarus I think this one might have been my favorite match of the night. It had a bit of everything in it, some high-flying, some technical, and some brawling. Lazarus becomes the new Rocky Mountain champion beating Lord Winslow Darkthorne with a Texas Cloverleaf. The former champ LWD gave him a good challenge though, just not enough to retain his title. From here the show cut right back to the hospital that Ryan Adams is currently residing at. Thing is...he’s not in his room. Where could he be, on the toilet perhaps? Or maybe he’s completely out of the building? We may never find out...Okay, more than likely we will but it sounds more mysterious when I say that we may not, and mystery is obviously what they’re going for here in this segment. The show then cuts to its final commercial of the night. Again it is a commercial concerning NWC: Pacific upcoming Pay-Per-View called “Who’s the man,” only this time around the commercial is a bit extended. Royce Donahue is in his office and he’s mad as hell (what’s new?). This time he is pissed off with the Man Agent, the guy who secretly runs the NWC: Pacific in a Dr. Claw sort of way for those who are just getting around to watching the show. It seems that the Man isn’t happy with the way Royce Donahue have been running things as of late. Donahue feels that his power is being threaten and have decided to try to find out the identity of “The man” and deal with him so that he can continue to keep said power. He’s calling on all of the active wrestlers of Pacific to help him with that, promising anything they want if they can successfully figure out “Who the man” is. Again, I am looking very forward to this Pay-Per-View when it comes out, I’m sure that we’re going to be in for tons of surprises. Main Event, NWCP West Coast Heavyweight championship title match: Bobby Polo (Challenger) versus Julie Reveli And finally we get to the main event of the show! What a promising one it was too putting two of the Council biggest stars up against each other. As a rule of thumb, since this is the main event I’m going to go a bit more into detail with what has happened in the match. Julie makes her appearance first, being the challenger and all. Her famous hand logo displaying the words “all the power we need is in two hands” flashes on the television screen. After Julie was introduced, Bobby Polo was then announced into the ring. Polo comes out in his Polo mobile, which he nearly crashes. Bobby had a logo himself to display on the television screen. In design the logo was similar with Julie’s with the exception of having blue jean pants instead of two hands and containing the words “All the power we need is in my pants.” Funny little parody, I thought. After Reveli’s and Polo’s grand entrance, the match gets started. Reveli quickly takes control of the match early on, starting her onslaught with just a simple shoulder block and constantly surprising Polo with multiple dropkicks and then keeping him grounded with a rolling armdrag takedown and a reverse armbar. Eventually Polo gets out of the armbar and the two wrestlers are back on their feet again. Reveli then attempts a roundhouse kick, but Polo was able to grab her feet, that didn’t matter though because Reveli was able to counter his counter connecting an enziguri on the champ! Once again Reveli seems to be in control, Irish-whipping Polo to the ropes and dropping him down with a Samoan Drop. After that, Reveli then makes her way up to the top rope and execute a corkscrew moonsault on the West Coast Heavyweight champ. Polo is able to get up from that move but Julie made sure to bring him right back down after kicking him in the midsection and executing a spinning neckbreaker! Reveli pins but only gets a near three count on the champ. After the kickout, Polo attempts probably the worst move you could EVER attempt on a woman...a low blow. People, what the hell was he thinking? I guess the man’s is so use to using that underhanded tactic that he didn’t take in consideration on what kind of affect that would have on a “woman” I guess. Not too surprising, that didn’t have ANY affect on Julie Reveli other than making her more pissed off. Julie punished Polo’s attempt at cheating with a powerful kick in the jaws, looked like it hurt too. Naturally, Bobby Polo wasn’t very happy with that move and charged right at Reveli. Big mistake. Reveli counter the charge with a fireman carry followed by another Samoan drop on the champ. She follows up with a ring of Saturn submission move looking to get a submission win...unfortunately “The Polo” begins distracting the referee on Bobby’s behalf. Julie let goes of the hold, knowing that she cannot win without having the ref’s full attention. Bobby use this to his advantage attacking Julie from behind, followed by an Irish whip and a devastating superkick that sends Julie falling down HARD on the ring’s canvas. Now it seems that Polo is in control of the match now. As soon as Julie is back on her feet, Polo is quickly on her connecting a nice bulldog on the challenger. Bobby then quickly locks on a figure-four leg lock, but Reveli responds by quickly attempting to get to the ropes. Julie does eventually get to them but Polo just flat out refuses to let go, thus the referee tries to force Polo out of the lock and eventually does so. Polo has a few choice words with the ref (obviously not caring if he gets disqualified or not) while Julie struggles to get back up to her feet. As soon as she is up, Polo levels her with a POWERFUL right cross. Despite that, Julie is quick to get back on her feet again, but Polo follows the same suit as Julie earlier and brings her down with a nice dropkick for good. After this, Bobby Polo forces Julie up on her feet, kicks her in the midsection and brings down a double axe-handle to the smalls of Reveli’s back. While Julie is leaning down, Polo forces Reveli’s head in between his legs (shut up) and executes his Polo-driver on the poor girl. The way her body bounced from off that canvas after Polo executed that maneuver is still etched in my memory, and I tell you what folks it aint pretty. Bobby Polo makes his first pin and manages to get a two count out of Julie Reveli before she miraculously kicks out. That count didn’t slow Polo up one bit though. He continues on with the onslaught punching Reveli with a closed fist then whipping her hard into a ring post. Julie bounced back off from the ring post and from here Polo executes a scoop slam on the woman, followed quickly by a moonsault splash. Polo hooks the leg and once again pins Reveli, getting another two count on the tough challenger. Seeing that wrestling wasn’t working to his advantage, Polo begins now to resort to cheating, putting a chokehold on Reveli. The Ref eventually pries him off of her but he accomplished what he wanted to do. Reveli then tried to whip Polo into the ropes, but he reverses and counters with a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker. Keeping the assault going for his favor, Polo quickly follows up with a waistlock and then a guttwrench powerbomb! It looks like it may be curtains for Reveli at this point with Bobby Polo applying a lateral press and with “The Polo” helping the West Coast out on the outside of the ring by putting Polo’s foot on the rope for leverage. The referee once again makes the count. The count reaches three, but Reveli is able to kick out before the ref put his hand down for the third time. This was the closest pin of the match thus far. Bobby Polo is shocked that Julie Reveli was able to kick out of that last pin and Julie attempts to use this distraction to her advantage, connecting fierce left and right combos on the champ. Polo quickly takes back control though after hitting Julie with a stiff knee lift followed by a shoulder block that sends Reveli back into the ring post. From this point, Polo uses multiple shoulder thrusts on Julie and then sets her up on the top turnbuckle and attempts to execute a superplex, but Reveli escapes halfway through and counters with a dropkick. Polo manages to stay up on his feet though so Reveli brings him down with a somersault clothesline! By the time Polo does manage to get back on his feet, Reveli comes off the far ropes with a roll chop block and follows that move up with a moonsault splash giving Julie the control of the flow of this match once again and putting Polo’s title in jeopardy. Reveli waits for Polo to get back on his feet to scoop him up for a slam followed by an elbow drop. Julie hooks the leg and pin for yet another near three count, but The Polo from outside of the ring managed to put Bobby’s leg on the ropes. After seeing the Polo from the outside assisting Bobby Polo, Julie suddenly starts to resort to P.M.S. mode (as I like to call it) and becomes a bit more vicious with the West Coast Champion. She hammers away on Polo with fast and vicious punches with one of her right hands knocking Polo completely down on his ass! Bobby is quick to get back on his feet, though a bit surprise by her aggressiveness. Julie again takes Polo down with a clothesline and it is at this point of the show that “Shotgun” Daniel Smith shows up. His appearance distracts Julie momentarily and Polo uses this to his advantage, rolling the challenger up for a pin, pulling down on her tights. The ref makes the count, and just BARELY is Julie able to kick out. Julie again uses that P.M.S. rage and starts to wallop on the champ with her fists of fury. Reveli Irish whips Polo and when he comes back from off the ropes she connects a spinning heel kick on the champ. He gets back up again, and again Julie hits him hard with yet another spinning heel kick. It is Reveli’s next move that will once and for all determine the fate of this match. Reveli climbs to the top of the ropes and jumps off. From this point on, Julie Reveli executes the most POWERFUL...DEVESTATING...EXCRUCIATING...cross-body splash of all time! ...Well it had to be because that is what she used to get the three count! Yes kiddies, Julie Reveli is your NEW NWC: Pacific West Coast Heavyweight (Heavyweight? Julie?) Champion! Beating Polo with her...cross-body splash.Okay, truth be told I was digging the whole match until that point right there. The finish should have definitely been better, but it is a finish and she did win with it so what are you going to do? Oh well, for what it is worth, I am glad to see Julie finally get Heavyweight gold and I’m sure she will be a good West Coast champion. I wouldn’t quite count out Bobby Polo though either, I have a feeling he will be back in the title-hunt real soon. Final Thoughts All in all, I thought this was a pretty good show. It had a good balance of segments and matches. The commentary was pretty good too, especially Luke Robinson. The war between the Traditionalist, Rogues, and Team BVI seems to be heating up more and more each passing week on Paradise Lost and I cannot wait to see what each faction have in store for us next. I am also happy to see some old faces returning back to the council and coming to the NWC: Pacific region to once again wrestle before their fans. Yes this was a pretty good show and I even managed to learn three very important things that I can take with me for the rest of my life: 1) If you ever want to get a title shot, all you have to do is whack a few wrestlers in the head with a steel chair and do a little bit of jail time. 2) If you know you’re going to cheat in a wrestling match just remember one thing, low blows DO NOT work on the female anatomy that well. And last, but certainly not least 3) Julie Reveli has the most devastating cross-body splash in the biz, bar none. Yeah...Well that will do it for the first edition of “Back To Paradise.” My name is “Prodigy” Eric Patterson and I will see you next week...I suppose. NWC:P Paradise Lost
