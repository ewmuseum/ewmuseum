NWC's Bi-Weekly People's Choice Rankings

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NWC:News presents NWC's Bi-Weekly People's Choice rankings. Check out South's falter & Damien Kahn's massive leap into the top 15 below. Ladies and Gentleman these are the NWC's Bi-Weekly People's Choice Rankings as voted on by the fans of NWC. Anyone can vote. These rankings are not actual rankings, but merely the fans opinions so keep that in mind when scoping these out. I'm sure these rankings will tend to fluctuate a lot, but again, that's why they're the fans opinions and not official rankings. Note: Previous weeks ranking in parenthesis. 1. Bermuda Billy Page (#4) - NWC:C Greed Games winner, followed his performance up by dethroning Lance Marshall as NWC's Heavyweight Champ! T2. The Future Teron Hain (#12) - NWC:C Defeated Arkane for the NWC North American Title. Maybe someone will finally bring stability to the belt. T2. BloodAngel Ed Lambert (#5) - NWC:C After defeating Perry Evans to become Central HW Champ, Lambert defends this week against ½ of Hawaiian's for hire, Johnny Kapaa. 4. The Lion Lance Marshall (#1) - NWC:P The Lion's impressive NWC HW title reign ended last week, but he's still one of the most feared men in the council. 5. Golden Boy Jake Summers (#6) - NWC:A Summers defends his Atlantic HW title against Marc Smith this week in a rubber match after Smith ended Summers reign as Atlantic's East Coast Champion. 6. Foster Nackedy (#9) - NWC:S The Rogue faction leader and man to beat in the NWC:S, Foster takes on Hiro Saotome this week. 7. Bobby Irish (#4) - NWC:A His drop in the rankings is most likely a result of lack of fights as of late. Irish is still one of the men to beat in Atlantic. 8. Julie Revei (#7) - NWC:P The results of the Polo/Reveli HW match haven't been televised yet; however, the fans must think the NWC's top female is the victor as she's ranked one spot higher than Polo. 9. Bobby Polo (#8) - NWC:P Still ranked one spot below the women aiming for his belt? What does Pacific's funny-man have to do to get some love? 10. The Bane Rictor Arkane (#2) - NWC:S After losing his NWC NA Title belt to Hain, Arkane jumps right back into action putting the NWC:S Confederate Memorial title on the line against Luke Denton. 11. Illtown Leonard Aarons (#13) - NWC:A He recently rid NWC:A of Jay Draven in a loser leaves match. Now he's pitted against the crazy man with the pet squirrel, Chaos. 12. Damien Kahn (#36) - NWC:A What a leap in the rankings for Atlantic newcomer Damien Kahn! He takes on Nathan Boswell for the NWC:A New England Championship this week. 13. Whitney Sarven (#T24) - NWC:C Another big leap in the rankings. NWC:C North Star champ teams up with Nathan Wylde against Ryan O'Brien and his partner this week. 14. Heatwave (#23) - NWC:A Heatwave was a tour de force with the New England championship. His chance to move up the card comes in form of a East Coast title shot against Morgan who he's ranked higher than!? 15. Nathan Boswell (#T24) - NWC:A The NWC:A New England champ becomes the sixth Atlantic wrestler ranked in the top 15 this week. Other Notables: 18. Vin Alejandro T20. Lee Clark T20. Hiro Saotome NWC:South After leading the charge with 5 guys in the top 15 in the last rankings, South only has two this week. This could be because of their ongoing problems with Fox Sports Net and the fact that their last televised show was quite some time ago. It will be interesting to see if they return to their original form as they attempt to get back on track with their 6/26 Uprising. [NWC] - National Wrestling Council
