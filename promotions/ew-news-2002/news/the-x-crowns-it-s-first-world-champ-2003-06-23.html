The X crowns it's first World Champ

23 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Californian promotion, The X's first ever World Champion was decided on the latest After Dark On The X’s weekly show, After Dark 7, the promotions first ever World Heavyweight Champion was crowned. The Main Event spot was left “TBA” all week and it was announced by After Dark Producer, Ryan Miller, that foes Teijo Uekado and Ryuji Ishikawa would battle as they did last week but this time for the winner to call himself the best The X has got to offer. The gruelling match ended with the most hated man in The X, Ryuji Ishikawa, finishing Teijo off with a massive Thunderfire Powerbomb off the top rope. Ryuji was given the title by The X Owner, Jason Marah as Ryuji stood proud beside his wife, Kaori. What was to follow was a despicable act, criticised by almost everyone who saw. Teijo, who had struggled to get to his feet, offered congratulations to Ryuji. In return, he got a palm to the face and became the victim of his own finisher, the Gomen-nasai from Ryuji himself. When asked, Jason Marah had this to say, “The guy fought a great match out there, but what he did after the match was fairly bloody unsportsman-like to say the least. I’ll stand behind my promotions champ for his ability in the ring, but by god he’s on major f***in’ a***hole.” Whether you like him or not, you can’t deny that Ryuji Ishikawa is a major force in the ring and is now The X’s World Heavyweight Champion. It will take someone very special to take that belt off his waist – you can be sure Teijo Uekado would love to act out revenge by doing so in the near future. The X
