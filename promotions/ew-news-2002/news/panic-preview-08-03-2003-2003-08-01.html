Panic Preview - 08/03/2003

1 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Furiously Addictive Wrestling presents PANIC! Live from the Joe Louis Arena in Detroit - the hometown of Tony Vigilante - in Michigan! Catch it on the WB Network this Sunday. Date: Sunday, 3rd August 2003 Venue: Joe Louis Arena City/State: Detroit, Michigan MAIN EVENT Rzarector vs. Hardcore Hoefel FAW World Heavyweight Champion Rzarector will have his hands full at Panic when he takes on Hardcore Hoefel in a non-title contest. Will Rzarector defeat Tony Vigilante’s new buddy Hardcore Hoefel, or will Hardcore Hoefel score a victory over the World Heavyweight Champion? FAW Tag Team Championship Re-match from Diabolical Day The Vice Squad © vs. House of Pain The Vice Squad picked up the vacant Tag Titles at Diabolical Day with a win over the House of Pain, and since then Vince Valentine and Lance Carter have gone head-to-head and Ricky Rogue and Hank Sharpe have also gone head-to-head. Will the House of Pain make up for their loss at Diabolical Day, and be crowned the New Tag Team Champions or will The Vice Squad continue to enforce the law? Violator vs. X-Rodd The Violator had been impressive since his debut in FAW, but lost the chance at a shot at Khristian Call’s Extreme Title. X-Rodd will be trying to make a name for himself at the expense of Violator, but will the Violator pick up the pace once again and brush X-Rodd aside? Tag Team Contest The Legions of the Dead vs. Spawn and The Genome Project The Legions of Dead make their debut at Uncut, but will they be able to stop the monster in The Genome Project and the animal in Spawn, who has been more than displeased since losing the Joe Smallze Memorial Cup to Tony Vigilante in the Final. Will Spawn and The Genome Project be able to co-exist, and will the Legions of the Dead be able to overcome a Five-Time World Champion and a ever impressive Genome Project? Joe Taylor vs. Marcus Stiles Joey Taylor takes on the returning Marcus Stiles. Marcus Stiles was injured during the Battle Royal on Panic a few weeks ago, which Khristian Call won, but now finds himself taking on Joey Taylor! Will Joey Taylor succeed or will Marcus Stiles pick up a win on his return? Debut Match Apocalypse vs. Drake Douglass Apocalypse returned this past week on Panic, and his in-ring return sees him pitted against another debuting star in Drake Douglass. Will Apocalypse start off his return to FAW with a victory, or will Drake Douglass forge an immediate impression by defeating Apocalypse?
