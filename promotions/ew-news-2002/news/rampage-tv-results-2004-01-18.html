Rampage TV Results

18 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An explosive night for the GWO. With a jam packed house Rampage TV hit the airwaves with a spectacular card. Along with a hit and run and Layne Walkers Surprising retirement the card went on and was indeed the best show in the early 2004 year. This week's edition of RTV has been one of the most action-packed in recent times. An un-noteable amount happens in this installment. Two of the biggest stories to come from it though, are the attempted execution of Trainer Jim, and the last ever match of GwO Legend, Layne Walker. To find out more on each of these subjects, and a whole load more exciting stories and matches be sure to check out the home of GWO. RTV Results for 1.16.04
