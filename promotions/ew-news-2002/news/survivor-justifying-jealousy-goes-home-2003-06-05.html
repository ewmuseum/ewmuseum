Survivor: Justifying Jealousy goes home

5 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In the closest vote thus far in the Survivor III contest, Justifying Jealousy gets the nod despite strong team performance and a crowd-pleasing gimmick. But this vote serves as a landmark for the contest... for everything is about to change. fWo Survivor III After going 3-1 against the Green Team, the Gold Team is forced into the elimination chamber once more, and this time has a tough task ahead of them. In the closest vote thus far in the contest, Justifying Jealousy is removed from the contest by a margin of only one vote. "Some curious things occured in this round," said Thumper, fWo Survivor III representative. "In the second round vote when Mystical Mix was eliminated we suspected that an alliance might have formed amoung some of the Survivor III members. But this vote seemed to reflect a different story... and perhaps a different alliance." The vote, which was close, also had one other original element to the contest. Namely; one team didn't vote. "We're not sure if the confusion over the judging had something to do with it, or if the team just didn't want to stake out a claim in this round given the personal nature of it... but it is curious and hopefully not part of a growing trend," said Twitch. Other members of the Gold Team had positive things to say about the departing team. "Hardest. Vote. Ever. And it doesn't get any easier from here." said one team, and other teams echoed these sentiments. But it was also a strangely quiet round of voting for the teams, as many simply made their selection and moved on. Justifying Jealousy, Team Fury and Fatal Attraction all picked up votes, leaving Disposable Heroes and, strangely, the team many suspected would have a large target painted on their backs, The Legion Of Dairy as the only teams who have not recieved votes thus far. But for teams that think they understand the contest and have it in the bag, Thumper had an ominous warning. "Don't get comfortable. It's our job to shake things up and in less than 24 hours all of your alliances and plans may be thrown out the window." More news as it comes available... fWo Survivor III Online Site
