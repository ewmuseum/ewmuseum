Post ReVolution News

20 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After a company shuddering edition of ReVolution this week, the details of the fall out become apparent as PRIME heads into its last show of the 2003 in Las Vegas. The Key Arena should have been reduced to a mere husk of a structure on the horizon after the explosive happening on last nights show. As Sebastian stated before the show closed, he has sold his share in PRIME to a mystery buyer who will step fully into his co-ownership boots in 2004, but for now PRIME is stuttering through it's most unstable period yet as last night Toshiaki Motoki and Hin See continued to abuse their power in the company throughout the two hour show. While Stormm (Justin Michaels) might have got the upperhand on the self styled dictator, many other superstars found themselves on the receiving end. For Tony Rolo, it was not a night to remember. Ruled out of action by concusion, Rolo could do nothing but watch as Joey Troy pulled out all the stops, yet couldn't overcome Rock Startling in a result that consquently means Rolo with never be eligable to compete directly for the Global Title again in PRIME. Startling on the other hand displayed all the symptoms or an over-inflated ego as he belittled nearly every member of the company including the very man who gave him his Global Title shot, Hin See. Not only that, but he suspended Steve Starr for no real reason other than to toy with the now former Intense Champion. Now "Mr T.R.U.E." looks to dismantle the so-called Global Title Curse, which has seen every defending Champion fall at the first hurdle, by requesting Hin See handpick a "suitable" opponent for him to defeat. As for "Wethers Investigates", the Enternal Punk drew nothing but blanks on ReVolution without seemingly getting any closer to finding the answers and culprit he's looking for. On a low note, Lance Jackson has been forced to sit out of ReVolution following a shoulder injury he sustained when Tony Zatarok gave him the Zero Tolerence on the entrance ramp, leaving Actionators around the world disappointed. While Deacon Holmes suffered significant internal injuries following the aftermatch beating he sustained at the hands of Kincade, meaning that he may be missing from action for several weeks to come. Apart from that the remaining roster has a clean bill of health for the last event of 2003 and by the sounds of what Hin See has in store for them, they'll need it. PRIME
