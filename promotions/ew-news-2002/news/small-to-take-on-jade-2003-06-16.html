Small To Take on Jade

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
For weeks Hillary Small, the 6'8 "she-male" and Jade - her beautiful fellow female, have been at each others throats. Punches have been thrown, blood has been shed. On last nights ACW Courage an announcement was made that will see them meet.... Hillary Small is an amazing wrestler. She is one of the best brawlers in the business, and probably the most talented female brawler to ever step in the ring. Jade, on the other hand - is more of an all rounder. She lacks the immense height that Hillary has, but makes up for it with pace and innovative moves. At last nights ACW Courage SilverHAWK announced that the two women will go one-on-one next week - a dream match for fans of the womens wrestling industry. It all came about when Jade made her way to the ring and began taunting Hillary, saying that “She’s jealous of me, and decided she wanted to attack me after just getting out of the hospital. Yeah, way to go you fuckin’ psycho! So, I made her eat my crutch!” . Hillary then made her way to the ring and attempted yet another assault of Jade, which was about to turn into a reality when SilverHAWK appeared and claimed that Hillary was unable to lay her hands on Jade due to a clause in her contract, and that in fact, she was in breach of contract at that moment as she is also not allowed to be within 100 metres of her rival. And so, as any sensible boss would do, he booked the two women against each other next Sunday live on Courage. It will be a night to remember, that's for sure. ACW
