TWC 2 Night Preview

14 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Once again in Pittsburgh, TWC is ready for yet another stellar show. Tradition Wrestling Council comes live from Pittsburgh, PA on Aug. 17th, and what a show they have booked. MAIN EVENT: TITLE vs. TITLE TWC Heavyweight Championship TWC Transcontinental Championship Special Guest Referee: Mark Gunn NightTrain (c) vs. Terence Madison (c) --- Last week NightTrain shocked the world again by turning on his recently formed coalition. Terence Madison is not too happy about this, and is willing to put up his prized Transcontinental Title on the line to prove it. Mark Gunn has had enough problem with both men to be the perfect selection for referee, since there is no way he is going to play a favorite here. One man could walk out with two-thirds of the gold in TWC, who will that be? TAG MATCH Durgo Syn/The Professional vs. Drew Thomas/Ravyn --- There is a strain in The Revolution over the past few weeks. The dismantling of Callous has left a bitter aftertaste in Ravyn's mouth. This week she needs to put that behind her and work side-by-side with Drew Thomas to take out two professional killers. The Professional and Durgo Syn are relentless, methodical, and effective, both are self-proclaimed loners so will they be able to work together to take another chunk out of The Revolution? SINGLES MATCH Dylan "Ice" Brecht vs. Trinity --- The Iceman has not been making many friends in the TWC locker room. First he attacks the 1st Lady of Wrestling, Ravyn, and insults her abilities. Then he turns around and nearly spits in the face of the TWC Champion, NightTrain. This young man has some ego issues he should get in check. Trinity is out to prove to Brecht that women have a place in the ring, and it isn't holding up the Round Cards!!! TAG MATCH Angus McCurt/Connor Banks vs. Curt O'Hare/Jeff Johnson --- Johnson and O'Hare were part of a big night for The Revolution last week, and the two will look to continue their hot streak against Millionaire Club member Connor Banks and Extreme Title Number Once Contender, Angus McCurt. O'Hare and McCurt face off in just under 3 weeks at Ground Zero, who is going to fire the first shot in this feud? TRIPLE THREAT MATCH "Product of Hate" Josh Cantrell vs. Romeo Earl "The Duke" Wiggington --- Josh Cantrell has burst on the scene as the hottest young prospect to come around in quite some time. He has staked his claim by destroying TWC Veteran Maelstrom, and caught the eye of our champion. The young man seems to have some skeletons in his closet, but don't we all? Will Cantrell continue his impressive streak? Or is he a flash in the pan? This match will also mark the in-ring debut of Earl Wiggington. Will this promising rookie show us something special? Or is he just another blue-chip bust? SINGLES MATCH "The System" Keith Brisbane vs. "The Rising Son" Jiraiya --- TWC has been active in the talent acquisition department. We have seen guys like Dylan Brecht and Josh Cantrell make their mark on TWC in a short time. The next wrestler tabbed to debut is known as 'The System'. Real name Keith Brisbane, he hopes to show the folks in Pittsburgh just what he has to offer. Can Jiraiya break out of his mini-funk and derail the debut of another young stud? ALSO THIS WEEK: ---The Professional has not left Drew Thomas alone since he arrived on the scene. Should Sunday be any different? ---Only one match has been set up for Ground Zero and we don't think that is enough to get people to buy a pay-per-view. Will the cloudy title picture be cleared up at all this Sunday? ---Rumors are flying around that as many as six people have been hired over the past two weeks. Last week we saw a promo advertising someone known as "Nuada," what the hell does that mean?? ---The search for a new commissioner has been kicked into high gear. Will Ashlea Eastman have anyone ready to be interviewed yet? TUNE IN AND FIND OUT! Tradition Wrestling Council
