MWF Monday Night Dynamite (June 9th) Preview

5 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Maximum Wrestling Federation will open its account with the UWC when it takes to Minesota on Monday June 9th. A top quality card has been booked and no doubt scouts will be flocking to see what the MWF has to offer as it continues to build on a strong Pay-Per-View, MAYhem. Dynamite begins its run into Minestoa and what a show to kick it off...MWF rookies Shawn Richards and GP Richter get their chance to shine as the two men go head to head in what should be a great battle...will the "Stuntman" pull off some tricks? A fatal four way will show the fans just exactly who the MWF's next big thing will be, Chris Drebbin, Matt Moore, Mike Savage and Kidd Kidman go all against all in what should be a fascinating contest. Wildfire and Rick Jones, for weeks the two men have traded verbal blows and Wildfire has defended his title twice against the "Acid" Canadian...but Monday could see it all come to and end as a brutal match should ensure the Canadian gets a third time luck. The two men will be put inside a cage, the MWF TV and one half of the MWF Tag Team Titles will be hung from the cage, the winner is the man to grab both titles...if Wildfire wins he has to join the "Connection" and defend the titles with Hart Evans! The Main Event builds on what started at MAYhem, Anarkist and The Jeopardizer team up as a duo for the first time and the fans will come alive for the two men, the MWF World and Xtreme Champions. Sam King and Joe Foale, two men who look down and out have a chance to gain some pride and put one over their rivals...will the mayhem come again? MWF -Monday Night Dynamite
