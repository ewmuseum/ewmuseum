Action!'s Backstage War

21 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The war between writers and wrestlers have traveled from the WWE to Action! Wrestling. But the writers versus wrestlers isn't the only war going down...a war BETWEEN wrestlers has also risen. It all started about three months ago...when KroW, known to many as Levine, returned to Action! Wrestling. Many Road Agents and Bookers of Action! were not happy with Reed Young's decision in letting the Satanic God back on the roster; but amongst their negative reactions, KroW was indeed back on the roster. Ever since KroW's walk out on Action!, back in November of last year, the relationship between KroW and Action! has never been good. But it seems Action! are more angry with KroW, than ever before. About three days ago, at an Action! Wrestling House Show in Conneticut, one match on the card was scheduled to be: Triple Threat - Sylo v. Bobby Minio v. KroW. Halfway through the match, fans began to anger the wrestlers...wanting to see more of an ROH-style of wrestling; rather than the hardcore brawling of the three wrestlers. In the contest, Sylo had simply walked out...and within two minutes, Minio was quick to follow. And who did Action! blame for this? KroW. Action! officials have stated that Sylo, Bobby Minio, Fiend and even Sharc; have been VERY hard to work with ever since KroW rose back from the grave. Backstage in PIW, KroW was known as a locker room leader...someone who said what he wanted, and helped newer guys get the hang of things...but in Action!, KroW is known as a rebel. Sources say his attitude hasn't changed much...he still is a loner backstage..but in Action!, he is much more harsh and angry. Action! officials believe KroW has been spreading his hatred of Action! to other former-PIW members such as Sylo, Sharc and Minio. All the wrestlers who seem to prefer the hardcore style of wrestling...seem to be rebelling against Action! Wrestling. Sharc, who recently conducted an interview with a Channel Five News (in New York), stated his hatred for his work place...and returned to Action! as a 'hated rebel' (alongside KroW). Sharc was repremanded for his verbal assault, but was not suspended in speaking his mind. Sylo and Bobby Minio, however, have been suspended for two weeks without pay. Terry Jackson, head booker/road agent of Action! stated, "They did the exact same thing Levine did last year...walked out on the company. They returned, and were taken out of the building by security. I won't deal with this bullshit, and I refuse to deal with wrestlers with chips on their shoulders; to just walk out whenever they feel like it" KroW was also given some time off...but since Action! really has no evidence that KroW started the entire backstage war...he is suspended WITH pay...basically, being paid to stay home and away from Action! Television. The plans for Sylo/Minio/KroW feud have been taken off this week's Pressure Point, and will be used on the next week's version. KroW, who has not been a big fan of A!W since his return, stated, "You try coming from a locker room like PIW, where you were greeted and got along with nearly every one backstage...and than go into a locker room where people are giving you cold stares everyday. I won't hide my hatred for every single person backstage at Action!...because they know I hate them all. Whether it be Garvin, Duel, Heimdal...they all know I can see right through their fake masks. That's what makes them hate me" "I swear, sometimes I wonder if Action! has a new paragraph in their contracts. 'By signing this deal, you agree that you will perform at top quality...and hate KroW'...it gets me wondering" No comments from Bobby Minio, Sylo, Sharc, or Fiend were said...but it's known they share the same feelings as Levine. Action! Wrestling hasn't taken EXTREME measures just yet. Minio and Sylo were given warnings...Sharc was given a verbal bashing from those backstage, and KroW is/always has been on the 'bad list' of Action!. Will the backstage war effect ratings? It's known a lot of Action! fans are/were hardcore PIW fans...and once the PIW closed, they went to Action! (where many PIW stars began to spring up). Until more information is given, this is James Attwood signing off. The Backstage War in Action!
