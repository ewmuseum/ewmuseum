[NeWA] Dying Degree & Rebirth - Hot off the presses.

3 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
National eWrestling Alliance presents it's double-night Pay-Per-View - Dying Degree and Rebirth. Last night the National eWrestling Alliance completed the release of it's Double-night Pay-Per-View: "Dying Degree" and "Rebirth". The first night featured the young and rising stars of the Alliance. The second night featured NeWA title-defenses and the "Hannibal Cup". The Main-Event of the nights featured the NeWA World Championship title defense. In that spectecular match - Vic Vary, NeWA's World Heavyweight Championship, defended his title against Curator. The first night crowned Trent Shanahan as the Number One Contender for the NeWA World Heavyweight Championship. Shanahan will get his rightly won title shot in RW's next Supercard. Short card summary - "Rebirth" : - Trent Shanahan *special* Interview. - Twenty Man Battle Royal, following Kid Gorgeous. -Ironman Tournament - Trent Shanahan [RW] vs. Jeremy Knyte [BWA] -Ironman Tournament - CJ Hunter [OWC] vs. Titan 3 [ASW] -C.J Hunter Promo. - Ironman Tournament - Jeremy Knyte [BWA] vs. Titan 3 [ASW] - Ironman Tournament - CJ Hunter [OWC] vs. Trent Shanahan [RW] -FINAL IRONMAN MATCH - Trent Shanahan [RW] vs. Titan 3 [ASW] "Dying Degree" - Hannibal Cup. - Cruiserweight Stampede - Six-Pack Match Brayton Pulse vs. Mark Adkins vs. Kestrel Falcos vs. Nova vs. Justin Double vs. Bryan Smith -Angello Vialleti Promo. - NeWA World Tag-Team Championship Match: Static Syndrome [c] vs. Steven & Finale -NeWA North American Championship Match: Malik Johnson [c] vs. Angelo Vialetti -NeWA World Heavyweight Championship Match: The Curator vs. Vic Vary [c] Until next time...
