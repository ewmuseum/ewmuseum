I Could Swear I Was in High School!

8 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
For whatever reason, Old School Entertainment just can not let the issue with Magnum Wolf go. For whatever reason, they are now covering up the issues between Wolf and Stanislav. Maybe [name withheld] should just get a job at EW-News! After the statement from OSE, I had to check back with Mr. [name withheld] to find out if the story he gave me was false. Upon hearing the accusations of his former employer, Wolf was dumbfounded. "This is wrestling. For Christ's sake, do we really believe that OSF and OSW are that seperate?! They're held in all the same arenas! Taped on the same night! What kind of childish game are those people playing with me? They want everyone to believe that the characters we play on TV, and the interactions we have on TV are the only relationships any of us have together? PLEASE! Ivan has his hand elbow deep in the book right now. He has just as much power as any one of the DaVattas in OSE! OF COURSE I've met the guy... OF COURSE we've had to deal with one another. This little game of he said she said needs to end. Now." The character Magnum Wolf has recieved offers ranging from hWo to the new Rush Pro Wrestling even as far reaching as uW. [name withheld], as a human being and not a wrestling character, has chosen to take a little hiatus from the wrestling world before making a decision on where to go. "I just wish PWC had never closed. That way I'd never have had to go try and help out a friend launch a new division. A friend that would soon spin me around and jam a knife in my back." Here's hoping that this situation is over, as Mr. [name withheld] was VERY upset at being summoned to an interview by me once again. We'll keep you updated on where [name withheld] ends up wrestling again.
