Rhett Locke Signs with OSE

20 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After asking for a release from the Pro Wrestling Circuit and promptly receiving it, OSE officials wasted no time in making the signing official. Former OSW International and World Champion Rhett Locke will be returning to OSW where he rose to fame. Old School Wrestling Shanda Russell announced in a press conference Friday that former OSW World Heavyweight Champion Rhett Locke has been signed to a new, long-term contract. 'Rhett is a changed man with a changed attitude,' Russell said. 'He's shown nothing but cooperation and a positive outlook since negotiations between him and OSE began.' Russell also rebuffed the claims made in PWC's 'news story.' 'First off, Rhett quit PWC; he was not 'released', as Ron Starr claims,' Russell said. 'He approached us with an interest in returning to OSW. After our people expressed interest, Locke handed PWC his notice.' Locke is a former OSW World and International Champion, the only competitor to win both titles in the federation's history. The winner of the inaugural Ascension match, Locke may be best known for swerving OSW's fans at last year's By Any Means Necessary and allying himself with Graphic Violence. Both individuals subsequently left the federation mere weeks later. Russell said that Locke intends to 'win back the approval of the OSW fans'. The engimatic and well-traveled competitor will have a chance to tell his own story--at Ascension. 'I'll be filming a special interview with Rhett at his home, to be aired at our Ascension pay-per-view,' she said. Rumor has it Locke has a major announcement in store. Be sure to watch Ascension and see what this news could be! OSE
