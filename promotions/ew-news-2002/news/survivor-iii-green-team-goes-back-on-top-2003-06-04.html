Survivor III: Green Team Goes Back On Top

4 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With the task before them of promoting outside teams through the Survivor Contest, all of the Survivor members made the wrestling community proud through their hard efforts. But Jon Crisp makes the ruling, and the Green Team picks up another win. fWo Survivor III: Running from May to July It was a rare display of honor in the sport of professional wrestling as the Green Team and Gold Team worked to promote other teams who applied for the Survivor III contest but were not able to get in due to lack of space. Thus, for the fourth challenge the Survivor contest worked to promote the industry and the valuable resource of tag team wrestling. "It was an amazing thing to see," said Twitch, one of the Survivor III coordinators. "Egos were set aside and the spotlight shone on some other people for a change. You've got to really respect these efforts." Jon Crisp, one half of the current fWo World Tag Team Champions along with his partner Black Quicksilver made the call and had this to say: "You mean I actually had to watch Venereal Ass and Jailbait for this? "I'm Jon Crisp, one of the two people YOU want to become...and I'm pissed. "Of all the times for me to be asked to judge this...I actually had to be picked on the night that the two most uneducated and most uninteresting duos in the fWo teamed up to give us something that makes watching Triple H on that other show something more pleasurable. So, since you peons...none of you deserving of THIS title...made me sit here and watch you trample through ridiculous ass-kissing of people who didn't make it this far...and I have to JUDGE this? "I'll make this short and sweet. Green team wins, thanks to The Uprising. If there's one thing I hate, it's unjustified asskissing...no matter what the idiots who made this stupid thing make these teams do. At least one team did it and made it seem believable. "So, green team...congrats. You made the Gold Team's job tougher. And you made it tougher on yourselves...because one of the unfortunate teams that make it here will deal with BQ and The Cult Icon. "And we'll make you wish that you lost and went back to being second-rate indy stars." Jon Crisp's comments aside, this puts the Green Team up a dominate 3-1 in the challenge thus far, and the Gold Team must return to the elimination chamber. More news as it comes available. fWo Survivor III
