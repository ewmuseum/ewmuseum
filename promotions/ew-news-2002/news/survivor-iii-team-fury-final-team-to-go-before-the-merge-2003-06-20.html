Survivor III: Team Fury final team to go before the merge

20 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As the Green Team headed in for elimination for the final time as a group in the fWo Survivor III contest, Team Fury is the last to go before it becomes every man... and woman... for themselves. But fWo officials promise that some nasty twists in the final weeks will make the survivors wish they had been eliminated. fWo Survivor III Perhaps sticking to the original Green Team structure, Team Fury, who was added to the Green Team when the roster switch occurred two weeks ago, was eliminated in the final group vote for Survivor III. "You never can quite tell... but it appears from the result that the original members of the Green Team decided to stick together in the final moments of the contest.," said Twitch, fWo Survivor III official. "Team Fury had nothing but rave reviews from the fWo staff and Survivor III viewing audience. I suspect that Team Fury will have several contract offers in the next couple weeks... any promotion would be glad to have them. But if you look at the voting, you see that the Green Team didn't seem to hold by alliances... this looks a bit like a panic move heading into the merge. The Gold Team is known to have alliances... this is likely to strengthen their standing heading into the finals. The Gold Team did the same thing two rounds ago when they voted out Sabotage." Despite the vote, members of the Green Team expressed frustration with their third trip to the elimination chamber. "Each time this gets worse, GAH," said one team, "Well I decided to go with my gut this time, and this is what it told me." Another team put it more directly, "There's nothing much to say here. This a crossroads in the game, and someone has to go, so I'm going with the one I think stands out most from the others." All members of Survivor are jittery going into the merge, which changes the challenges from group to individual immunity. Alliances are likely to form and dissolve rapidly, and fWo officials claim to have a host of nasty surprises to keep the teams on edge. "Yes, there are some new surprises this time," said Thumper, fWo Survivor III representative. "We will have immunity challenges and reward challenges... but every challenge will be a double edged sword." fWo officials denied that they would allow members who win immunity to bestow it upon others. "No, it's not a good idea given the timing of the contest. But members who win challenges will have opportunities to impact others in the game directly."
