CSWA: ON TIME Airs In New Format

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The youngest CSWA show got a new format this week, featuring a recap of the week's action on the house show circuit as well as follow-up from the BATTLE of the BELTS pay-per-view. CSWA: ON TIME aired for the first time in its new format Sunday. CSWA commentator John Simons hosted the show, featuring behind the scenes reactions to the BATTLE of the BELTS pay-per-view, as well as some of the best of the action from around the CSWA's house show circuit in the West. The CSWA continues to try and pull out of their ratings slump, caused by internal issues and preemptions on their partner network, NCN. The new ON TIME format is hoped to bring in new and former viewers, while putting additional focus on the house shows that run throughout the week. This edition of ON TIME featured "Good God" Kevin Powers facing off against everyone's favorite Michael Jackson impersonator, Shamon. "The Strongest Arms In the World" GUNS faced off against Exchange member Nathan Storm. CSWA newcomer Ryp Fandango took on former Presidential Champ Cameron Cruise, who was recovering from being attacked at the PPV. Former World Champion Deacon faced Jean Rabesque, and for the Greensboro Championship, Kin Hiroshi defended the title against Nathan Cross. CSWA: ON TIME airs Sundays on NCN. CSWA's Main Event
