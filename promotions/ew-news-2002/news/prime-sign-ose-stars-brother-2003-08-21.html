PRIME Sign OSE stars brother!

21 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PRIME wrestling officially announced today, Thursday 21st August 2003 that they have signed OSE Superstar Profusions brother, Deavolo. It was rumoured that Old School Entertainment where on the verge of signing Deavolo due to his brother working with the company but PRIME came in at the last moment and landed the rising star. PRIME had this to say. "We are thrilled to sign such a name to the PRIME roster. Deavolo may not be a well known name unlike his brother, though we will guarantee that Deavolo will be on the tips of everyones tongues when he makes it in PRIME." As of now it is not known when Deavolo will debut in PRIME wrestling but we should find out more over the coming days. Profusion, Deavolo's brother insisted that he did not want to comment on the current decision by his brother to wrestle in PRIME but from the facial expressions, Profusion did not look pleased at all. Deavolo is a highly rated wrestler from many experts and has been pin pointed to be a huge success in PRIME as it was stated it would only be a matter of time before Deavolo was snapped up by a big wrestling company and PRIME seem to be the lucky company. PRIME
