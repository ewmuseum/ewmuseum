DWA: Payne Responds to LeStatt Knight and Others

11 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just hours before Showdown, Destiny Mandrake sat down with controversial wrestler Payne to ask him about his match against LeStatt Knight on Showdown, his attack on World Champion, Typhoid Mary, and his thoughts on Jordan Brannigan becoming the Owner of the DWA. It was good to be Payne Destiny could see the self satisfied smirk on his face as she sat across from him, secretly thankful there was a desk in between them Destiny had no desire to be even this close to the man, especially after what he'd done to Typhoid Mary at Hostile Takeover, with the exception of Killian Blayze Payne seemed to have no respect for the female gender. But Destiny had a job to do, and a follow up interview with Payne after Lestatt Knight was as good as gold, as much as the two hated each other they were a pair that was going to make the DWA very rich, newly crowned official President Jordan Brannigan knew this, the two were a marketable pair and Destiny would see to it she had both their words immortalized before Showdown...even if she had to subject herself to Payne's grin and his roaming eyes, how a man like this had ever come into the power of a multi million dollar company would had to have been an act of god...or perhaps the devil. "Payne...thank you for sitting down with me" Destiny began "Never a problem Destiny" Payne said in a voice that really didn't mirror his words, Payne was playing nice for the moment...but as Destiny and others unfortunate enough to interveiw Payne in the past had found out that could change at any minute "Well first and foremost how are you feeling after Hostile Takeover?" "Destiny Hostile Takeover was without a doubt on of the most controversial Pay Per Views the DWA has ever had and i for one was happy to help make it that way" "There were several injuries to be counted for that night...and you were to blame for several of them, do you have anything to say about that?" "Destiny you make it sound like a horrible thing" Payne's lips broadened into a grin that had nothing to do with friendliness "Frankly i wish i could have done more...I wish i could have shattered Raziels Kneecap with that chair...I wish i could have split Knight's head open wider...and I wish i could have Snapped Typhoid Mary in half over my knee...but instead i had to settle for giving only fifty percent" "So you're not the least bit sorry for your actions?" Destiny asked "The only thing i'm sorry about is not being able to have ended Lestatt Knights Career" Payne said gazing into Destiny's eyes, he was trying to get under her skin she knew... "Ok...Let's change gears here...how was it you came to own a multi million dollar company?" Destiny asked changing the subject...Payne however wasn't going for it "I found it" he said with a smile "Excuse me?" "I said i found it...i was walking along one day and it just jumped into my lap...Kind of like Jordan Brannigan coming by the DWA, but we wont go there...yet" "Now you're not even being serious" Destiny said narrowing her eyes "And YOU'RE asking BULLSHIT QUESTIONS!" Payne exploded "Who the hell do you think you're fooling here Destiny? Do you think the general public gives a shit about how i came by this company! NO! They want to know what Payne thinks about Lestatt Knight's latest "Speech" on the FOX Network" "Alright..." Destiny said "Lesttat Knight then...Come showdown you two will step into the ring for what some say could be for the very last time..." "What WILL be the last time" Payne cut Destiny off "Lestatt you want to go one on one? Man to man? You've got it! You sniveling little shit...talking about devious acts with chairs? Why didn't you tell destiny about the "Devious" acts you pulled back in GCW with a chair...i didn't do anything you haven't done Roberto i just took a page out of your book! And you have the balls to challenge me one on one? The fact of the matter is you have no one left to count on! There's ONLY YOU! While i'm backed by a veritable army...this is a war Roberto, and come Showdown we wont just be fighting another battle in the course of this war...no i've won battle after battle and i'm getting sick of you! This time I'm going to win the war!" "How do you feel about Jordan..." "Shut up Destiny!" Payne growled "Lestatt They say victory favors neither the good or the bad...it favors the prepared! And i'm more then ready for you Lestatt...I've been ready for this for years, i've been ready for you...and i want this so badly i'll step into the ring one on one...hell You can bring the entire roster with you Knight...I want this so bad i'll go through all of them just to get my hands around your throat and slowly squeeze the life out of you!" Destiny was beginning to think the interview had been a mistake, Paynes eyes were beginning to flare with a light that suggested she should begin to bring the interview to a close "Alright enough about Lestatt..." she said wanting to change the tone of the conversation "How did you feel about the shocking climax at the end of Hostile Takeover? Now that Jordan Brannigan is the official President of DWA do you feel he'll be taking it in any different directions?" Payne snickered "Jordan Brannigan...i do give him credit for pulling off the biggest Mindfuck in the history of the DWA...it takes a twisted mind to think up something like that...but you see Jordan Brannigan has made it a point in his career to pointedly ignore me...he gives me more or less free reign to do what i please...but have you ever seen Brannigan respond to a thing i say? No you haven't...see Jordan knows he has Gideon in his pocket...Gideon is desperate to keep his career from being snuffed out like a candle in the wind...so desperate that he'd lay down for Brannigan to help screw Mason Stone over...the difference between me and Gideon Destiny? I will not compromise my dignity for Jordan Brannigan...everything i've done in this company i've done because I'VE done it...and what has Jordan done for me? He threw me one half of the Tag Titles like tossing a dog a bone! That son ofa bitch paired me up with the man i hated most in the world! Lestatt God damn Knight! and you know the said thing Destiny? We held those titles away from everyone...that is until Knight cost us those titles...but to be Lestatt Knight is to fail so can you really blame him?" Payne rose from his chair and Destiny flinched "So allow me to sum up what i think of Jordan Brannigan...Jordan you're trying to impress the Fox Network by dropping your "Family Show" Image? What do you think i've been doing all along? Face it Jordan you're like a shark that smells blood and your following the smell of the almight buck...lemme tell you Jordan i've got all the money in the world and it's overrated...it's just green paper...so to your new business choices, to your plans coming together on Hostile Takeover, to your personality...i say this..." "Fuck you" Payne's smile could have frozen water "Sounds familiar doesn't it Jordan? But it doesn't sound as big and tough to you when it's not coming out of your mouth does it? I'm not in your pocket like Gideon Jordan...Payne serves himself and only himself, and as far as Lestatt Knight is concerned it's good you're having doubts Knight, it's good your feeling useless, it's good your feeling hopeless...you've got just enough in you to step into the ring for one last beating and then you'll go the way of Gideon" "Lestatt Knight you're just another step in the Evolution of Payne...and come Showdown I'm going to deal with you once and for all...now if you'll excuse me Destiny...I have a company to attend to" Destny sighed heavily as Payne left the room with what could only be described as Relief.
