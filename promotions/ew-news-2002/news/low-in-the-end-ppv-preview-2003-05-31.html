LOW In The End PPV Preview

31 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lights Out Wrestling prepares for its first-anniversary show. It has been announced that on Wednesday, June 11th, Lights Out Wrestling will embark on its largest Pay Per View extraveganza yet. Entitled "In The End," the show will not only feature ten well-hyped matches, but will also mark the one year anniversary since LOW opened its doors. "It's really incredible that we made it this far," said Casey Sanders in a press release earlier this week. "You always aim for a milestone like this, but figure something will go wrong." Many share the same sentiments. Since its opening day of June 11th, 2002, Lights Out Wrestling has been plagued by wave after wave of forces determined to destroy it... All controlled by Mike Meyhu, a man bent on revenge for past encounters with the founder of LOW, Trigger. Though it is unconfirmed as of yet, sources say that it is likely the two will step into the ring for "some kind of confrontation" at In The End. One match that is definitely confirmed will be Tristan Belmont defending the World Championship against Jesse Jamester. After playing by the rules for twelve months, Jamester is done getting "cheated," "held down," and "ignored." After disrespecting the late Lotus at his induction into the Hall of Fame, and disowning the Warrior ways which Belmont thrives on, the World Champion chose to fight back. Jamester wants his crowning glory to take place a year from the date that he signed a contract, while Belmont wants to make that a crowning glory of his own. Perhaps the most vicious match will be Jason Kage and Eric Russo, going head to head for the first time. For time dating back before LOW's creation, the two Shadow Riders were closer than teammates-- they were brothers. But at Shell Shock in April, Kage revealed that he had been unhappy for the past several months, and wanted to step out from the shadow of "The Superstar." Every Realm we lay witness to more and more destruction caused by the infinite hatred between these enemies. Every Realm it gets more intense, and at In The End, it will certainly blow over the top. Another exciting matchup will be the finals of the Tag Team Tournament, to declare champions for the belts that have been vacant for nearly six months. After the semi-final rounds that took place on a special edition of Aftermath this past Friday, it is confirmed that Josh Starwind and The Blue Coral will take on the team of Phoenix Rose and Derek Vinyard for the prestigious gold. Any follower of LOW knows that Pay Per Views are events not to miss, and the magnitude of In The End will be ampliphied by a hundred. It won't be a show to miss, and we'll be covering the updates as they unfold. Lights Out Wrestling
