The WWC's Dispute over two EGOs.

19 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
One half of the most controversial team in the history of the wrestling world (Team EGO), Sean Studd has been missing from the wrestling world since his departure from the XWF in March of 2003. Greg Davis, another member of the old school Team EGO, seemingly vanished from the wrestling scene after his short stint in IOW...could they both be returning? Sean Studd and Greg Davis, collectively known as the Ring Rage Wrestling (RRW) version of Team EGO (the first ever edition of the team), have been missing from the wrestling world for some time now. Both men became fairly famous in their stints in the wrestling world; Greg Davis being among the elite within the fWo rankings, as well as the IOW rankings; while Sean Studd joined the ever rebellious ranks of the PIW, along with a short XWF stint. Paul Levine, and WWC executives, have been qutie interested in the two prospects, but it seems the company has been having some troubles in coming to an agreement with the two men. The main purpose of WWC being fairly interested in the two egomaniacs is that they feel their charisma can bring alot of fans over to the WWC. "There is no doubt both men hold a great deal of talent in the ring," says WWC's Vice President, Aaron Tact, "But it's also well known that both men are notorious for their attitudes backstage...we hope to have that settled within the contract" The amount of money desired by both parties was held a secret, but it seems that has also become an issue. "What these guys need to realize is that we are a new promotion," says Road Agent, Brandon Ramos, "And we can't offer that much money to them without be assured they will produce. Look at the WWE and Goldberg...I bet they are kicking themselves in the ass for that one" It seems alot of complications need to be settled...and there is no telling what storylines are available if these two men sign. One storyline that has been heavily rumored is a possible Team EGO reunion. Seeing as how Trey Vincent has already by contractually signed to the WWC, possibilities between him and his fellow Team EGO cohorts is endless. WWC Online Legends 2003
