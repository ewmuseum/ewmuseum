P.'s Corner Return!!! (#5)

29 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After almost a year of being on hiatus from this great establishment, I've returned to give you my scoop and two-cents on the world of Wreslting today! Mick P.'s Back!!! Hello everyone! I know, the last time I said I was back, I was only back for about a month, well this time is different, I'm back for good! But onto the real reason for this return column, Semore Smoke. Yep, my long time friend and companion, Semore Smoke has made his long awaited return to the wrestling ring in the United States, after his recent trip to Japan. The following is a transcript of my conversation with Semore Smoke and how it pertains to his recent interest, here in the States. Mick P.: Semore, it's nice to see you again my old pal. Semore Smoke: Hi Mick, call me Simon. I've dropped that retched middle name for good this time. Semore Smoke was the name of my former self, I'm now my real, true, Christian name, Simon Smoke. Mick P.: Ok, Simon. So, from what I've been hearing, you've signed with a major organization already upon your return, do you think you can tell us who that is? Simon Smoke: Of course my good friend. I've signed with the new EYE Wrestling organization. It's on the rebound now and I just happened to be there to join aboard. Mick P.: Ok. Cool. Well, here's my next question. You seem to have changed since your trip to Japan, could you enlighten us on what your new mission is in life and why you've changed all of a sudden? Simon Smoke: Well, my new mission is simple, I'm here to splead the blessing of the Lord through out my kind. While over in Japan, I met up with a group of young people there on a mission trip. They showed me the way to Christ and I've been changed since then. Since my return, I've been off drugs, I no longer curse, and I have a new light in my heart that will never burn out. Mick P.: Wow, you've... changed quite a bit. More so then I had originally thought. So... You intend to use your fame as a wrestler to promote your new views of life while in EYE? Simon Smoke: Most definitely Mick. I will help spread the word of the Lord to all my fellow colleagues as well as to all the fans that I meet. They will understand what it is like to actually meet the Lord and to actually know how it is to have God and Christ in their heart, just like I do today. Mick P.: Well, I would be lying if I didn't tell you that I did notice you had some sort of new shine to you today, but I thought it was because you were smoking some new herb or something. Well... this is definitely a shock to me ladies and gentlemen. Mr. Smoke has now become a full-fledged Christian activist. Would you consider yourself on a crusade of some sort Simon? Simon Smoke: Most definitely. I'm out to fix the problems in our world today! Everyone will have the Lord present to them in my form! Mick P.: Ok... well, would you mind if I asked you another question, one that I didn't plan on asking you? Simon Smoke: Sure, go ahead my friend. Mick P.: Which denomination are you part of now? Simon Smoke: Easy, the one that is truly blessed, I'm part of the Free Will Baptist family now. Mick P.: Ok... well... whatever those kids told you in Japan, it must have been some strong words. Anyways, that pretty much wraps this interview up for today, do you have any final words Mr. Smoke? Simon Smoke: Sure thing. God is on his way, he is vengeful to your new lifestyle and he will change your retched ways. I am just the voice, but the body will be here soon enough, you'll see! Mick P.: Ok, that's some freaky stuff man. Anyways, that's all for today's interview, I'll be back next week with an update on this situation as well as more news and information from the world of Wrestling. Later all! Ok, as you can probably tell, I was shocked when I found out that Semore, I mean Simon Smoke was now a full-fledged religious buff. Here's a guy I've known for years and he's changed after just a month long trip in Japan? One can only wonder what they did to him in Japan. Oh well, in any case, I'm gonna end this column off with a special shout out to a new federation I've become aquainted with out there in our world of Wrestling. It's small-time in size, but it has reached a recent TV deal with NBC where it will show it's weekly show on Friday nights at 8:00 Central. This new federation goes by the name of IWZ and you can find their official website in the source link below. I urge you all check this new fed out and follow their every move, they'll be huge some day! Anyways, for now, that's all folks, I'll return next week with my views on the past weeks events. -Mick P.
