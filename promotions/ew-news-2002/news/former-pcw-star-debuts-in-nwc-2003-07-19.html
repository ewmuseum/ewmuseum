Former PCW Star Debuts in NWC!

19 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Former PCW MegaStar 'CEO' Tyler Nelson recently debuted on NWC:Central's regional show Center Stage. The CEO Tyler Nelson debuted on NWC:C's Center Stage stating, "I’ve never seen such a pathetic group of so-called talent in my life! This organization is filled with trash from top to bottom, and back to the top again where that incompetent piece of rednecked, premeditated retardation Adam Calloway sits. How in the hell can the President of a wrestling promotion, in his right mind, collect this much utter worthlessness? I fail to comprehend the immense mental inferiority it must take to be on the level of one Adam Calloway." The harsh words offended both NWC:C wrestlers and fans and President Adam Calloway came out to defend his region, but ended up getting Nelson's 'Greed is Good' move executed on him. A huge banner fell from the rafters that read "PCW>NWC" NWC:News reports that big things are expected from Tyler Nelson who will debut against Nathan "F'N" Wylde on the upcoming Center Stage. Many of the wrestlers felt that Nelson's entrance was 'too big' for someone who had never proven themselves in the NWC; therefore, it will be interesting to see how the CEO goes about proving he deserved the dramatic entrance in the upcoming weeks.
