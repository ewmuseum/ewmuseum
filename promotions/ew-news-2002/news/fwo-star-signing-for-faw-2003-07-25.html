fWo Star Signing For FAW?

25 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Various stories around the internet, and also on TV have said that a fWo superstar is interested in signing with FAW and is in discussions regarding a contract......... http://www.faw-wrestling.com/faw_ewnews.jpg We have just heard that a fWo superstar is interested in signing a 2 year deal with Furiously Addictive Wrestling. The TV channel said that the deal could be concluded as early as tomorrow, but the superstar has yet to be named. FAW officials released this statement moments ago. "We refuse to confirm or dismiss any rumours linking FAW with a fWo superstar, they do have some good talent there, like we do, but we refuse to comment on whether or not we shall be signing anyone over the next few weeks. If people wish to speculate then so be it, but we can't confirm we have spoken to any fWo superstar, and we shall not confirm if we have not spoken to a fWo superstar." FAW officials didn't deny the story yet they did not confirm it either, FAW has recently been falsely linked to Skylar Green and DJ Cool, could this be the real thing or could this be a hoax? FAW also confirmed they would like to signed former OSE star Tempest although they denied any approach being made. But if FAW do indeed sign a fWo superstar, there would be huge ramifications at Panic! this Sunday. Furiously Addictive Wrestling
