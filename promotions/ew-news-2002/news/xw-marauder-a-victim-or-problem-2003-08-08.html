XW: Marauder a victim or problem?

8 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A report last week broke on a backstage brawl that took place on the 29th of July, where Marcus Corteia, known to the wrestling world as Marauder, got into a scuffle with indy worker Tank. Word out of the XW camp, reports that the fight was started by something Marauder said over the phone, that Tank overheard and took offense to. Recently, I got in contact with Marauder, after his XW house show appearance in New York City. He had this to say: “I’m a victim, what can I say? My entire career has been surrounded by people trying to spin my words around, and make me into the bad guy. Trust me when I say, I know what Mike Tyson feels like. People don’t like seeing a black man be a success, and now it’s obvious that I’m getting back into rhythm. I’m not a bad guy, I’m just a man trying to recover from injuries, and trying to get his respect back, is that a bad thing?” Marauder then went on to make comments of Tank being a “hack, who I had to carry to make the match watchable.” The rest went on as Marauder attempted to use the interview as some form of advertising campaign for himself and his dying brand of soda Conquest, who’s sales are the worst amongst the Pepsi line of beverages. Later I was able to get in contact with his agent, Vanessa Butler, she said the following: “It was a misunderstanding, Marcus has a way of saying what he feels, and some people especially in the ultra macho setting of professional wrestling are apt to take that as a challenge on their manhood. He didn’t start the fight, and he tried his best to avoid it, but with no staff around to break it up, he had to try his best to protect himself. “When wrestling as the Marauder he’s known by many people world wide from his stint in Tornado Wrestling, Marcus is a very smart man, he wouldn’t do something as simple as get into a fight when he just made his return.” When asked how she knew all of this, with reports saying she wasn’t in the arena, “Trust me, I know my client. Marcus was trying to protect himself.” It wouldn’t be wise for Marauder to turn around and get into scuffles backstage, he is said to be vary wary of working with or around XW superstar Quick Death who Marcus still blames for the death of his older cousin Jullian Corteia, who wrestled under the moniker Melee. The death was a result of a ring spot gone awry, XW ran a tribute show for Jullian a month ago. Among the Marauder hype fest, I got to ask him a simple question on whether or not he’d start a conflict with Quick Death, do to the history. “Of course I wouldn’t.. I’m not stupid, I just know what I know, it wasn’t an accident. That’s all. This is a job, my career, I’m not eager to work with him, I just suit up go out wrestle, go home. I don’t need friends anymore, I don’t need anyone to like me, to hell with camaraderie.” Sounds like the words of someone who’s not causing trouble, doesn’t it? -T.H.W. X-Wrestling
