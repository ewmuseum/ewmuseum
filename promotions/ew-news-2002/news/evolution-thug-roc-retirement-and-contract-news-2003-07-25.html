eVolution: Thug roC Retirement And Contract News

25 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It has been stated before that Thug roC was ready to retire sometime at the end of this month. http://evolution.theewe.net/images/evo-ewnews.jpg It has been stated before that Thug roC was ready to retire sometime at the end of this month. It has been publicly said that the wrestling work schedule for him has been picking up and getting to be too much for the former High Impact Champion. As some of you may know, Thug roC has decided to not only stay in the eVo federation, but to also go back and further his education, so that when he must leave eVo, he'll have something more to fall back on. Thug roC has been excluded from many house shows in the past to further his education, which seemed to be a gift from eVo officials. Officials decided that since Thug roC has been such a great performer, they wouldn't mind giving him some dates off for such a good cause. But with college added to his 'thing to do list,' Thug roC finds himself studying college work rather then studying matches. Seems as if the workload is starting to pick up. A heavy rumor is for Thug roC to retire after his ladder match with Sean Mason this week. It has also been said that Thug roC and Morpheus Omega have been talking about a possible program with each other that would please both the fans, and the two superstars. But these rumors have all ended last night. Justin Fritz, Daniel S., and Tyler Davidson sat down with Thug roC last night in what seemed to be a very long meeting. Witnesses state that the meeting was from 4pm to 8pm, which included an one hour dinner break. The original plan was for the meeting was to schedule Thug roC's house show appearances. Possibly not to wrestle, but to just appear to keep house show attendance up. As only a few main event/mid-card wrestlers make regular appearances, the officials wanted roC to start making more appearances. But the meeting was more then just house show appearances. Justin Fritz brought up Thug roC's retirement and confronted him to make an official statement. Well, the statement was made around 7:30 that made all 4 happy. Thug roC has reconstructed his current contract that was scheduled to end this December. The contract didn't change the amount of money that he'd make, but rather a promise not to retire until at least March of 2004. Although Thug roC can still retire due to injury, or be released, it is certain that Thug roC has made it official that he wanted to stay with the company. Theewe.net caught up with Thug roC and asked why he extended his contract. 'It's pretty simple really,' Thug roC stated, 'Throughout my career I have yet to obtain a significant World Title. Although I've won one in a small indy fed, it doesn't seem fit to go on my resume. But an eVo Universal Title is. I will continue on until I become champion, only then will I be happy enough to leave this great sport.' eVolution
