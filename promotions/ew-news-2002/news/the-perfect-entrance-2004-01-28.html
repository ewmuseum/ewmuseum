The Perfect Entrance

28 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The MWF was greeted by the presence of well known wrestling legend James Wright on Sunday evening when "The Perfect One" revealed himself as FEAR's attacker. Wright and adversary FEAR's hatred for one another goes back years to their days in the Ultimate Wrestling Federation, but now, in the MWF, "The Perfect One" wishes to re-establish the fued in order to gain himself a reputation in the company. FEAR was attacked weeks ago, and his ankle was broken by a mystery attacker, who was revealed to be Wright, who said about FEAR, "I'm going to wait for him to recover and then I'm going to destroy his very existence right in the ring where he claims to be so good. Maybe he is good, but he's not…perfect." JW has already stated that within months he WILL gain an MWF Championship - either the TV (held by Doomsday) or World Title (held by Ace Spade). No doubt, James Wright's MWF career will be decided in a matter of weeks. MWF
