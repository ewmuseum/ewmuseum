DWA: EZ Target in Gun Attack Horror

27 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DWA superstar, EZ Target, was involved in an attempted "assassination" Monday night after his Showdown match. After his match on DWA's Monday Showdown, it seemed EZ's main battle was beating down on his two main rivals, Cemetery Gates and the 7' behemoth Starr List. But it seemed EZ's personal life was spewing up something much worse. A heinous attack was dealt to EZ in Chicago. It is rumoured that notorious Chicago Gang 'The Boyz' were behind the attack, which left EZ needing twenty stitches to the back of his head. After it was found that no long term effects were down to the attack, EZ decided not to press any charges, and will definetly be able to compete in the DWA's biggest pay-per-view of the year, Testament. DWA: Dragon Wrestling Association
