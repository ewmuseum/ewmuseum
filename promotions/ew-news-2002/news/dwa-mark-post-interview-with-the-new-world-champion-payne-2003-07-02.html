DWA: Mark Post Interview with the New World Champion, Payne

2 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just hours after Dead Men Walking came to an end, Mark Post got an interview with the new DWA World Champion, Payne. Allow me to start off by saying this... I've conducted many an interview in my day and few have disturbed me such as my interview with Payne aka Alex Grey. I discovered the DWA's newest Champion wandering down the hall dragging the belt after him like a tired little child would drag his stuffed bear by the arm, that was the first warning sign that should have sent me calling for a doctor, instead the reporter in me won over and i decided that if drew him into an interview he'd merely shake the shock off and show me the Payne persona that so many reporters prior to me have dreaded speaking with. I invited him into my office, it wasn't hard, another warning sign that something wasn't right, he followed me in very quietly and complacently "How does it feel to be the World Champ?" I asked him with a bright smile His reply was quiet, staring at the floor in front of him he squeezed the title tightly "It's mine!" he said quietly but possesively "I earned it..." Now at this point i figured if i'd gotten this close to getting a reaction out of him i'd go the rest of the way and try to piss him off, not many would willingly inflict Payne's wrath but i wanted to try. "There are those that would say you didn't earn the win, or even the shot" i said assured that this would set him off "But how do you feel?" "I feel tired..." he said brushing off the pointed barbs of a question that would have had the fired up Payne down my throat in an instant "They're going to come after me now" he said with what i thought i could detect a faint note of despair in his voice "So you're saying you don't want the belt?" I asked "No!" his voice was a harsh whisper "Everyone else will want it...they'll want to take it from me...want to make it thier own...but it's mine....MINE!" His eyes were wide but they didn't flicker with the self confidence of a man who'd just won the world title...nor did they flicker with the pent up energy and angry aggression of Payne, they flickered with the obsessive fear of losing everything that was held dear, the fear would fester and harden, turning into a psychotic form of resolve that would force some unlucky individual to pry the belt of Payne's cold lifeless fingers. "Ok...let's change the subject" i said not quite so anxious to get him riled up as i had been before "Tonight Lestatt not only beat your friend and Stablemate Jeff Wylde but he also won the 30 man Rumble to win a title shot at Testament, how do you feel about the chance of facing off against him with World Title Gold on the line. "Knight..." he said staring at the floor, his eyes hardened ever so slightly and i thought he might suddenly come roaring out his shock, and i have to admit that deep down a small part of me wanted him to...it was clear this man wasn't well . "He'll want this more then anything...he wants to hurt me...take everything from me...I'm worried about him the most" he confessed "Wont sleep at home tonight...he'll be waiting...can't show my face for awhile either...he'll jump me given half the chance...Knight hides in the shadows...all of them...he could be hiding in this office for all i know" his eyes darted around wildly as he spoke "Knight...Roberto...Lestatt...he wants my belt...my gold...he's jealous...he wanted it...wanted to take it off the waist of his new sweetheart Mary himself...after he fucked her brains out...Knight..." He was beginning to ramble and my worry was transforming into Dread "But Knight...he's always wanted what's mine...he can't have my belt...if he comes after me...if he tries to take it...I'll Kill him..." The last words were spoken in such a dead monotone voice that it frightened me, without a shred of emotion to express the strength of his words i still felt that if Knight had been in my office and Payne had been in possession of a firearm he would have killed Knight where he stood without hesitation, after this realization i decided it was time to wrap things up. "So tell me..." I began "What can the DWA expect from Payne in the coming..." He exploded, the small glass table was thrown into the air as he lunged to his feet, it hit the floor hard and shattered, i was on my feet notepad held up in front of me as if it would ward off his attack had he chosen to hit me...but instead he looked at the broken glass with a sense of awe, or maybe it was surprise, then turning and dragging the belt behind him he walked out of my office, his dead monotone voice trailing after him "My name...is Alex Grey" When my shakes subsided I tried to reach Jordan Brannigan, I wanted to tell him what had just happened and that I thought he'd made a mistake, Something had to be done about his newest World Champs condition, someone could get hurt. Jordan Brannigan wasn't available for the rest of the night, I finally stopped calling around 2am. My fears kept me awake for the rest of the night, not for Payne but for the DWA who suddenly had a raving monster in their midst and was blissfuly unaware. DWA: Dragon Wrestling Association
