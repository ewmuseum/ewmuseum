HSW: Shocker Recap/Preview

3 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Things are definately starting to heat up as the count down to Headstrong Wrestling's Flagship Pay Per View, Christmas Chaos, is set to take place on December 23rd in Denver, Colorado! With Tyler McPherson winning the Breakdown Brawl, he now has himself a shot at Assassin's Headstrong Title at Christmas Chaos. However, through a little convincing, the Owner Joey Matthew did what was right by giving Magenta her shot to be in that match, as she was not properly eliminated. It was then known that she was 'injured' and she nominated her boyfriend Sabre to take her place. Sabre would win the match thanks to the miraculous recovery of Magenta. This was 2 weeks ago. Last week we saw a big Eight Person tag team match in which Tyler teamed up with once-rival and new Evolution Champion(as well as Junior Heavyweight Champion), Man With No Name, and the number one contenders for the Tag Team Titles, Order of Chaos members The Boogeyman and Bloodhound. They faced Headstrong Champ Assassin, his partner and number one contender for the Junior Heavyweight Title El Nutso and the Tag Team Champions, Rio Carmichael and Meleza Draven, better known as Nocturnal Supremacy. Not only that, but Sabre was the guest referee, Magenta the special outside enforcer. Through a big battle of guts and pain, Tyler McPherson would end up making the Champ Assassin tap out in Tyler's very own hometown of Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada. Also last week on Shocker- Athena Halliwell d. Brian Rage *After that match, Athena's sister Heather Halliwell made her presence felt and let Athena know that she isn't playing games when she attacked Athena to build up their match at the Pay Per View. Mjolnir d. Blake Rozdale in a Hardcore Non-Title match. Afterwards, Mjolnir relinquished his FnX Championship out of the blue. Order of Chaos member, Aaron Anubis, was originally planned to face Tai Hashi. He felt Tai wasn't worthy of facing "The Future", so his replacement was none other than new HSW member Devon Jacobs who had already wrestled that night. Devon and Aaron did not seem to be on the same page though. Devon Jacobs d. Tai Hashi Skip Rawlin d. Edward Twiztid in a Hardcore Match The X-Rated Ikons, Lexington and Chris Steele, d. Vandal Hearts, Ghost and Reno Whytechurch Devon Jacobs d. Nathan Masters in the opening bout of the night. Not only that, but to start to show off Joey Matthew came out and announced a BIG main event stipulation for Christmas Chaos, which HAS to be seen to be believed! You can access the latest Shocker results at the HSW Website now. THIS WEEK! Shocker invades Los Angeles, California Nate Newsome debuts in his hometown after much hype last week against also hometown boy Anton Chase Chris Steele takes on Brian Rage Anubis and Jacobs try to cooperate against Vandal Hearts in a Tag Match Athena Halliwell battles the Japanese Rock Star Tai Hashi in a Singles Intergender match. Order of Chaos member Bryan Bryn Bryson takes on Nathan Masters. In a Hardcore Tag Team Match, Tim Greene teams up with Blake Rozdale to face Skip Rawlin and Edward Twiztid. The Tag Team Titles will be on the line as The Boogeyman and Bloodhound take on Nocturnal Supremacy(c) And in the main event, Tyler McPherson, Man With No Name and Sabre take on Assassin and El Nutso in a 3 on 2 Handicap match booked by Commissioner Rey Valiente. Don't miss Shocker live..This Sunday night.. on The WB! Headstrong Wrestling
