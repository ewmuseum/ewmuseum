Cool Rumoured For Wrestling Return

12 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The retired 22-year old powerhouse, DJ Cool, is rumouredly on the verge of a return to the wrestling world, after supposedly retiring from the 'sport' in mid-2003. The highly-regarded wrestler, previously of federations such as the UWF and OWA, was last seen in a PTC Extreme Title #1 Contender elimination match in July of this year - a match won by Tristan Belmont, who would then by awarded the title. He then retired completely from wrestling - the household name and huge face announcing that things were becoming 'too easy'. However, only shortly after the retirement he was linked with federations such as fWo and FAW (the latter link creating a huge public argument involving himself, Hardcore Hoefel and FAW owner Lisa Tien), but never appeared in either federation. And he has denied these latest rumours, which emerged this morning from an anonymous internet source stating he could be about to sign for a number of federations, including possibly his own. However Cool, from one of his many residences around the world (this one in Monte Carlo), denied any link. He said, "these rumours are just plain dumb. Being DJ Cool, I wouldn't even consider a return to active wrestling and put myself down by facing the low-lifes I often get booked against. I'd have nothing to lose, yes, but whats the f**king point?". He also added, "If I was going to return, i'd say so myself. I don't do secrecy". However, he didn't deny taking on a non-wrestling role, which could be interesting. Will Cool be about to become a manager or other non-wrestling person in a major federation? Unlikely, but only time will tell. [name withheld] Legends 2003
