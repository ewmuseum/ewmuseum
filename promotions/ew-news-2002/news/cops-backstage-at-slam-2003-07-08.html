Cops backstage at Slam

8 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Law enforcement officials in Illinois were backstage at Old School Wrestling's Slam show on Sunday. Cook County police officers were seen backstage at OSW Slam on Sunday, reportedly searching for Old School Champion Matt Register. However, upon their arrival, Register had already left the arena. Following his in-ring confrontation involving Rhett Locke and Jeffrey Wayne, Register was seen being rushed out of the OSE Arena by what were assumed to be assistants. Shortly after their departure, the law enforcement arrived and began asking for him. Upon learning of his disappearance, the officers promptly left the building without a word to any OSE officials. There has been no word from Register, nor from Cook County police as to what brought this about. The Old School Champion is currently unbooked for Slam next week, and it is unknown if he will be in attendance to watch Locke vs. Wayne, nor if the county will try to catch up with him again. More news as it develops. OSE
