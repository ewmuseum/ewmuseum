"Impulse" Brian James comments on X Wrestling situation

15 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
IBJ was not shy when speaking with Claude Mitchell about his current situation on how he has been push out of X Wrestling. Well it has been over a month, but there has been some further development in the dispute between “Impulse Brian James and X Wrestling. For those of you that missed it the first time around. On June 17th, 2003 I ran a story that stating the IBJ was being pushed out of a federation he wanted to stay with in X Wrestling. At that time we were not sure as to who and why this was happening, all we knew was that despite being a top draw, and wanting to resign with the company, that upon his contract ending, no new deal was being offered. Well recently I got the transcript form the most recently taped edition of Bitch With Mitch (At this time due to pressure from GLAAD, the show has not be released to the public.) Here is what was said. Mitchell: Good thing you brought up Xw. Did you see the first show when they came back, Blazer had some choice words about your run as Xw World Champion. James: Yes. Blazer just has this complex about people being better than him, I think Inferiority Complex is the word. Then it was followed up with. Mitchell: Back to the contract situation, why not let the world hear it right here at Bitch With Mitch?! Everyone will think it's cool to have important stuff revealed here, right? James: I had originally a year contract, with an option for another 9 months. Not exclusive, but a normal run of the mill contract. It runs out, as people know, on August 11th. Jason Roberts was engaged in talks with me before he sold the company, but as soon as Blazer got control, all talks stopped. I want all the fans of me out there to know, this isn't my doing. This is one of a man who wants all the glory to himself. Mitchell: I know Blazer from experience, and he's even been on this show twice, I don't really like him at all. It's strange to say I liked Jason Roberts better. James: Agreed, I haven't always seen Roberts, eye-to-eye, but he keeps the fans in mind. Which still leaves the question of why? Why would Blazer push IBJ out of X Wrestling, even though it would hurt the companies ratings, and overall money making? I called down to Miami at Xw headquarters, and was told that Blazer will make a statement as it pertains to these comments by IBJ. More on this as it develops. X Wrestling
