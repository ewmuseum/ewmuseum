KroW and LLB at it once again.

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The backstage war between Roland Miles Ermann and Levine was sidelined for a bit, but it seems to have returned. Recently, backstage at Action! Wrestling, a lot of rumors have been circulating that Levine (KroW) has been getting into more backstage altercations with management lately. The rumors have yet to be declared information, but one incident was made clear. Ever since the contest between Christopher Fox (at Juggernaut II), Roland Miles Ermann (LLB) has been taking time off from the business. The reasons have yet to be that clear, some say he has injury, while others say he has been suffering from some sort of depression. Whatever the case may be, it seems KroW did not take to it lightly and wanted to express his feelings directly to the source. It's said that LLB received a call on his cellular phone from KroW, who had his number from back in their PIW days. It's said that KroW actually berated LLB for taking time off and accused the World Champion that: "The only god damned reason you take time off is because you can. Since your family and have that unsackable agreement with Reed, you take advantage of it. I see through you bull&^$" Both men got into a LARGE argument for about thirty minutes, with KroW simply hanging up on LLB. Management immediately got involved and demanded that Levine explain himself and apologize. Levine did explain himself and stated he was sick of "Roland getting special attention, while guys like Avalon, Garvin, and Cylear are wrestling THROUGH injuries". It's known that KroW was not the only one to feel this way, as management was also approached by wrestlers such as: Sean Cylear, Bobby Minio, and Sharc. Even a newer addition, Fiend, talked of the special treatment LLB received backstage. Management has decided NOT to take any action against KroW, seeing as how he was not alone in what he thought and said. One must wonder, with LLB returning in only a short time, how will they react to him backstage? And will we see ANOTHER brawl between LLB and KroW (similar to the one that took place before KroW left the company)? This line of events between these two competitors only grows worse as time goes on. Action! Wrestling
