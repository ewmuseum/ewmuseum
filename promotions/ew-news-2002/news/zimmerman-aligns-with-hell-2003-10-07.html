Zimmerman Aligns With Hell

7 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
One-half of the Dyad Champions signs year-long endorsement deal with website. As a premier heel, Keith Scott Zimmerman gets told a lot of interesting things on his way to the ring. But the voices who've been telling him to go to hell suddenly look very precient--just not in the way they'd like to be. Fresh off his success in capturing a tag belt with the legendary Flying Frenchie at the September A! PPV Out Of Control, website www.tshirthell.com has signed KSZ as a spokesman for a year-long contract. Terms were not disclosed, though rumor has it it is a contract in the low six figures allowing Zimmerman all of their free irreverent t-shirts already for sale and those to come during the contract with an option for a second year if he continues to be successful. Executives with the site said the choice was made a while ago and they were just arguing terms. "Watching him through guys through windows with our Next Governor of California T-shirt on," laughed public relations head Matthew Spaulding, "we just thought that was really [expletive] awesome. Plus, apparently, he's really good and stuff and a big geek so it seems a natural crossover for us." Zimmerman, reached for comment before Pressure Point, was in high spirits. "I'm looking forward to it. I bought their product before, obviously," he tugged at his "Sharing Is Caring/F[**]k the RIAA" shirt. "So I'm more than happy to pimp something I like anyway. They make a funny product and considering the amount of fellow cynical a[**]holes in the world I think we'll both be happy with how things go come October 2004." Action!
