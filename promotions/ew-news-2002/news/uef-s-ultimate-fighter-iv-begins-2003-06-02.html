UEF's Ultimate Fighter IV begins...

2 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
UEF's biggest tournament ramps up this week on Protocol... Ultimate Electronic Federation's biggest contest of the year has begun on the fed's weekly show Protocol. Ultimate Fighter, has had a two-year previous history of being UEF's most exciting tournament and has twice awarded it to one great particpant and Grand Slam Champion who is no longer with us in UEF, Shadowstorm. This year, however, he is not involved and the prize is certainly up for grabs by those talented many who stock UEF's ranks now. The matches awarded advancement in the tournament to the next round to Jonathan Drachen(the Krash Course Champion), Ace, Ice Cube, Prometheus, Zaz, and Jay Moreno(the United Kingdom Champion). The honorable eliminees consisted of UEF James Sunderland, Tama Porter, m1xer, Master, the Legendary Scorpion, and UEF great Galuda. The matches showcased the excellence of all participants as they vied for a right to the richest cache in the land, the Ultimate Title held by ShadowDM aka. Jory Johnson, it's recently-crowned champion. Next week, a remainder of the first round competitors will take each other on in great matches which include: Xavier .vs. "Blinded" Kurt Gooden SSP .vs. Safire (A return for Safire, one of UEF's greatest Hardcore/Krash Course wrestlers in its history.) Yasmin Kerr .vs. John "The Truth" Snow Hiddukel .vs. Greg Stevens (Another great Hardcore/Krash Course wrestler will be looking to advance here) Joey White .vs. Kidd Krash and the main event will pit the great Four Time Former Ultimate Champion and Current International Champion Chris Hardy against newbie prospect Retro for advancement in the brackets. All in all, the race for the pirze of and Ultimate Title match is shaping up to be quite a fierce competition and certainly not one to be missed by any who might see it. See you at Protocol! ULTIMATE electronic federation
