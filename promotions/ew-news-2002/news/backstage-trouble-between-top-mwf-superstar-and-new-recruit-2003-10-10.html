Backstage trouble between top MWF superstar and new recruit...

10 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Maximum Wrestling Federation is being forced to investigate an incident between one of the MWF's more senior roster members and a wrestler on a trial period with the company. Reports have come from backstage at a recent house show in Illinois. The MWF backstage crew were forced to come between the MWF's top star Troy Knight and new recruit Sozzey as the two men exchanged words over each other's styles. Sozzey reportedly said some unfriendly comments to Troy Knight who reacted to them in a manner which caused Sozzey to try and attack the MWF Television Champion. The two men have now been calmed down and it is thought a handshake was made. The MWF President will be alarmed as this is not the first backstage clash. After Monday Night Dynamite went off air MWF World Champion Kidd Kidman let his feelings known to the MWF President himself about the decision to call the match for the MWF Tag Team Championships a draw. The Maximum Wrestling Federation has lost a number of wrestlers in the past two weeks that were crucial to it building its success as a major force in the Illinois region and touring the surrounding states. The backstage morale is said to be very low at the moment and Troy Knight has been credited with keeping this morale up, the recent bad blood between Knight and Sozzey may change Knight's attitude. Troy Knight and Sozzey will have to put their differences aside on Monday Night Dynamite as they are booked in a tag team match. Sozzey is making his MWF debut alongside tag team partner Mak against Troy Knight and Craig Clayborn. MWF management are hoping the two men can get through the match without further incident. Maximum Wrestling Federation
