Tony Vigilante Calls It Quits

15 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tony Vigilante has rocked the foundations of Furiously Addictive Wrestling, his current federation, and shocked the wrestling world by announcing his unexpected retirement. The 28-year-old, former Innovation of Wrestling superstar, announced his retirement at 11:00am today at a press conference through his agent Douglas Freeman. Over the past few weeks, and more importantly since dropping the World Heavyweight Title on November 9th, Tony Vigilante has not been his former self and diabolical performances were becoming a grown concern for FAW officials. It has come as no surprise, though, as Tony Vigilante was on the sidelines from November 9th until December 15th following the shoot incident at FAW's Deceit PPV, which left him wounded as a result of two bullet shots. Since his return in December, he has been defeated three times and has gained little clue as to who the actual assailant was who struck him down and this all but influenced Tony, 28, to consider his future in the wrestling industry. Lisa Tien, the manager of FAW, defended her star the moment he was shot down. But in the recent press, notably before the January 13th episode of Panic, she did hint at a lack of performance from Tony Vigilante and also advocated the concerns of critics who branded him a "shadow of his former self". Tony Vigilante could not handle the pressure anymore and admitted his ambitions lay beyond the wrestling ring following the shooting incident. He felt things in wrestling had gone gone beyond normal control. He refused to comment on his retirement and consequently leaves the wrestling industry with nothing to say. Furiously Addictive Wrestling is expected to make an announcement on their situation with Tony Vigilante in the coming days. Furiously Addictive Wrestling
