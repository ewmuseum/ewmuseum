Television Title Tournament!

21 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A.C.E. Owner Orion Chambers has official signed the contract along with the six members of the tournament. The tournament will be held on July 6th, 2003. This mourning at 10:21 am A.C.E. Owner Orion Chambers accounced the six members of the first ever, teleivision title tournament. The six wrestlers are, JP, Travis Carter, Wednesday, Tyler Van Serio, Duzza and Mark Blaze. They all signed a contract to make the tournament official. The single elimination tournament will be held on the first card of Sunday Night Catastrophe, July 6, 2003. Before signing the contract Orion Chambers had this to say, "I am very exited about this first card, we have a special main event that I will announce at the show and we have these six superstars wrestling in the first round of the television title." The bracket is as written: Travis Carter vs. Wednesday JP vs. Tyler Van Serio Duzza vs. Mark Blaze Orion also said that the winner of the JP and Tyler Van Serio's match will get a bye and advance to the finals. When Tyler Van Serio was asked for a comment about his situation he said this, "Well, the way things are going, I think I will be in a great match. JP is very skilled, and so am I. Advancing is in the name of the fed, and that's what i'll probably be doing. He has skill, and so do I. But I know one thing, I will never give up, if I lose now, if I lose all my matches, I know I will never give up, it's the Italian Passion." The same think was asked to JP, Van Serio's opponant and he simply said, "No doubt about it I am going to win the bye and the title. I'm gonna kick his ass plain and simple." Thats all for now... ACE
