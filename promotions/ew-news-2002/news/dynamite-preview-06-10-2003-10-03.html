Dynamite preview 06/10

3 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dynamite is making its homecoming to Illinois and after last weeks performance four and a half thousand fans will be eager to see what the MWF has in store for them. Dynamite continues to impress the Illinois faithful as four great matches have been booked up to fill the card. Craig Clayborn and The Vine kick off the action in a hardcore match, NomaD has better be watching as two men who are perfectly capable of offering a challenge will be putting their hardcore skills to the test. Seth Caliko follows his up his victory on Dynamite last week with a match against Ace Spade, "The Player" will be wanting to make amends for his defeat at Implosion and Caliko could face the full force of Ace Spade's attack. Sean Harley will be looking to keep his cocky dreams alive as he defends the MWF Television Championship against Flash and Troy Knight, can "Troy Boy" pose any threat to the "Phoenix"? Or can Flash pull a surprise victory out of the bag. The Main Event will be slightly different to as advertised, Insane Wayne and Iniquity will defend their MWF Tag Team Championships against the new team of Kidd Kidman and Hell Fire Kidd...can they beat the champions? Maximum Wrestling Federation
