Jamie Shock Humiliated At SAS

9 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
UWF Television champion Seymour Almasy humiliated World Heavyweight champion Jamie Shock at last night's Sunday Afternoon Supremacy whilst also announcing who will challenge the world champion next for his crown. Television champion Seymour Almasy last night humiliated Jamie Shock at Sunday Afternoon Supremacy whilst also announcing that a member of the Wimp And Chimp Connection will face him for the World championship next Sunday. The RPG-loving character, who has only been beaten once in his time for the Universal Wrestling Federation, announced it after the main event inbetween former stablemates the Local NVA Company and the Wimp And Chimp Connection themselves. The night's events started fairly normally with the opening match inbetween regular opener Chaos and newcomer Sacha Ruzhyo. The match was fairly even until Ruzhyo took his chance and won the fairly even match. We then continued onto another match featuring another newcomer in Blake Ransom. He won his debut match against Ruzhyo and Ernie French but he would face harder opposition against Stable Wars finalist Bruce Gordy. Ransom came out on top, though, extending his unbeaten record and strengthening his place in the federation. After that we cut backstage to see Jamie Shock causing disruption in a dressing room where 3 wrestlers who were in different Stable Wars alliances sit. There is Shadow Blade, who represented the Shadow Klan, Kevin Starr of the Elements Of Destruction - the last person eliminated in the final - and Max Damage of the now split-up Young Bloods. He enters, exchanging insults before the three go to attack him. Just before they do his tuxedo and mask-wearing bodyguards walk in before beating the three down with nightsticks whilst Shock walks off to find Joshua Dunn. Back to the action and Dante faces Craig Wright in a mid-card match-up. Craig Wright's last match was against Stable Wars winner DJ Cool where he lost but Dante fought at Stable Wars. The Dark Angel, however, struggled against Wright but just won it although not 100% cleanly. Next up was the only title match of the evening where Seymour Almasy defended his television title against White Tiger. It was originally supposed to be Shadow Blade but he was injured early by Shock's bodyguards. As expected the luchador White Tiger couldn't beat Almasy although The Final Fantasy did need run-ins to help him achieve his goal. After that, meanwhile, Kenny Mercury made a speech on the ramp. He said that he would face any 4 wrestlers good enough to challenge him. The crowd probably didn't take any notice after the Young Bloods premature elimination from Stable Wars by the Elements Of Destruction but no-one cared anyway. After that followed the evening's penultimate match where Kevin Starr faced Brad Johnson. The Firestarter was to blame for the Prime Alliance's early elimination from the Stable Wars final but Starr was only eliminated at the end by Pete The PG Tips Chimp. The match itself was dull but won by Kevin Starr who continued his good form since returning to the UWF a while back now. Next up we cut backstage to Jamie Shock again who cuddled his World title in his dressing room. Suddenly smoke poured into the room until his visibility was reduced considerably. A woman in a mask emerged into a view and, Shock not knowing who it was, gone to shake her hand when she offered it. When they made contact the woman tightened her grip until a sword appears - hitting him in the back of the head. This causes him to fall when Seymour Almasy appears holding the sword he uses so after. He and Aerith Heartilly, the woman and his manager, walk off with his world title whilst leaving him on the floor. Meanwhile, in the arena, the crowd are going wild whilst the preparations for the main event begin. The main event inbetween the Local NVA Company and the Wimp And Chimp Connection soon got underway, though, and was a fairly even match. The NVA had the upper hand until late on when the WACC started to turn things around. But, before the match can end, Almasy heads down to the ring followed by an injured Jamie Shock and Aerith Heartilly. He steps into the ring with the WACC already well into the crowd avoiding him and goes to shake hands with the NVA's Duc Chu Song. But, as he does, he pulls him toward him and knees him in the gut! The NOGOOD stable clearly splits up before Almasy finishes both NVA members to the cheers of the crowd. He then speaks via a microphone, saying: "You know, I've come to a crossroads. Stable Wars V showed me that. Henceforth, there is but ONE person I can rely on! And that person is Aerith! My one friend all these years, the one person that likes me, that cares about me for who I am, and not for some other purposes". He continues, stating: "From now on, Seymour Almasy will be Seymour Almasy. I came to the realization that good or evil is not based on one's beliefs! It is based on one's actions! I will continue to fight to spread the glories of Yevon! And, if to you people that is a sin, so be it. My holy crusade begins now. For so long, I have held the UWF Television Championship, and a fine belt it is, but I want more! I CRAVE more! And so, taking a quick look at those who populate this strange land, I looked for the unworthiest of them all, and I found our World Champion, Jamie Shock.." The crowds reaction becomes mixed with some boos, some cheers and a lot of whispering inbetween the members of it. Almasy goes on, though, stating: "I am well aware that a member of the Wimp and Chimp Connection Stroke 9 Mach 2346 Fire 2 Black Waltz #3 FFVIII will have an opportunity to defeat Jamie Shock for the World Championship next week. But when he fails, I am serving notice to Mr. Shock that a legitimate challenge has arrived. A challenger with true skills is here to wrest the championship from your undeserving waist". Suddenly a large letter 'Y' comes down from the ceiling. Seymour picks the World champion up and places the unmoving Shock against it. Heartilly, meanwhile, takes some television cable and begins to tie him to it. Almasy asks for a 'guilty' or 'non-guilty' answer on the charge of 'heresy in the eyes of Yevon'. When there is no response he says he is guilty and says his sentencing will now come. Suddenly the letter 'Y' raises back to the roof with Shock attached to it. Turning upside down it lowers again with Shock looking horrified. He lands safely before Almasy takes his world title. Almasy walks off with it, saying he will win the world title at the next pay-per-view. The transmission ends but many questions have been left unanswered after SAS - why did Almasy leave NOGOOD? Who will fight Jamie Shock next SAS? Why will they not win? Who will Kenny Mercury face at WNU? Many questions lie without an answer but one thing is for sure - that was an SAS to remember.
