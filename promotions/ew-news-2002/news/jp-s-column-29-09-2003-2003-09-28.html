JP's Column 29/09/2003

28 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JP gives his analysis of the Implosion Pay-Per-View and looks back on the Dynamite that followed... Hello everyone, JP's back again and well I have to rant about the Pay-Per-View, it was more spectacular than I thought it could ever possibly be. The management really booked some top class matches and well done to all involved because the performances were at the highest level, especially in the Main Event where some new stars really shone through. Dynamite was also a good after-show, it gave a chance for some more action and also a storyline to develop, shall talk about that later. Let's do this then, I am going to go back to Implosion and take a look at everything that went down, there certainly was a lot of everything... We started the show in the right way for me, after all the fireworks we got an interview with the "Untouchables". Ian Gonzales asked some great questions, his technique is really coming along and he got some good answers. It was nice to see a show of unity from a team who before Implosion were certainly at odds with another, especially after Kidman's AWOL a few weeks ago. They showed a united front and talked of leaving Implosion with a few gold straps to boot. The action began with what was for me and many others the match of the night. Troy Knight and Ace Spade went one on one in the ring and the crowd was so into this match, I've never seen a crowd care so much about the outcome of the match and the screams when Ace Spade won the first fall confirmed it was something special. The two men gave absolutly everything and with an incredible storyline built up they couldn't help but let the drama get to them, Ace Spade went down in the last man standing match and so the cage was lowered for the third and deciding fall. They ended up ontop of the cage and I could hardly watch as both men ended up falling off and crashing through our table....it was judged Troy hit the ground first and he accepted the victory. A stunning match. A short segment followed between Dom Stoate and Sean Harley. Harley and Stoate have been at odds for a while now and it really came to a head as Harley demanded to know who the mystery opponent he'd be facing was...Stoate kept quiet as Harley guessed and guessed, he eventually stormed off none the wiser. The MWF Television Championship Fatal Four Way was a good solid match, Harley put some good work in to pick up the victory. The Jeopardizer was revealed as the Mystery Wrestler, I was surprised to see "Jay-Z" back in the ring so soon, I didn't realise his injury had healed faster than previously thought and he surprised the crowd a little by coming back. He put in a good performance but the ring rust showed and he ended up being pinned. Sean West and Flash were in the match as a bit of a side show, their feud is yet to really hit the burner and if they do no doubt they'll have the same popularity as Ace Spade and Troy Knight are currently enjoying. Insane Wayne and Iniquity were backstage next as they looked forward to the main event. Both men seemed confident they would walk out with something, neither man was going to settle for defeat. Wayne seemed very relaxed but Iniquity was a little tense but this is understandable given it was his first ever MWF Pay-Per-View and he was main eventing! Zach Fredricks and NomaD were expected to have the hardcore match to end all hardcore matches and it went well, but not as well as I expected. Both men put one hundred percent into the actual match but the fans weren't as into it as say Troy Knight/Ace Spade and the simple reason was hype. NomaD is yet to become a popular champion and Zach Fredricks can't make up his mind if he wants the fans support or not. Considering this the fans reacted well and NomaD marched to victory in a horrific match, a lot of blood was lost in an epic encounter but there is definitely better to come. We were then treated to Sully doing what he does...quite well and that was an interview. Doomsday was subject to Sully's poking around and he handled it well. "The Deadly One" is a top wrestler and he should find everything he wants in the MWF, he talked about the troubles of not yet achieving a victory in the MWF and also his friendship with Insane Wayne, or should I say mutual respect. The Main Event rolled round and the fans were really up for it. Anarkist and Simon Masters didn't look too strong as the match started and it became clear they were on the back foot, Insane Wayne and Iniquity showed excellent early teamwork and really took it to the two other teams. Kidman and EyCe took a while to find their rhythm but when they did they suddenly looked dangerous. Wayne and Iniquity came off with the first fall and claimed the MWF Tag Team Championships and the crowd became hooked as the battle commenced for the MWF World Championship. Anarkist didn't battle as hard as I would have expected to retain his belt and seemed to be happy to let Simon Masters do most of the work. In the end this was his downfall as Kidd Kidman put in a stunning performance, except Insane Wayne so nearly took home the biggest prize in the MWF, I could of sworn Charles Galliant had counted the three but I was mistaken. Kidman picked up the victory, a surprise victory if you ask me and became the MWF World Champion. The evening was not quite finished, the MWF President came hurtling down the ramp and Anarkist was subjected to quite a beating. Walker let out a lot of anger on Anarkist and the crowd went ballistic as Walker got in the ring and fired Anarkist, there and then, ten months and he was gone. Implosion was a show that for me showed the old guard fading and the new guard coming through. Sean Harley is now the MWF Television Champion, NomaD is still the MWF Xtreme Champion, Insane Wayne and Iniquity made that breakthrough to capture the MWF Tag Team Championships but Kidd Kidman becoming the MWF World Champion was a big shock. The MWF has reached a turning point then and the roster expanded to show signs of this. Doomsday has been given a full contract with the MWF and I applaud the management for this wise decision. He is a top wrestler both in the ring and outside, his dedication is what made him such a hit in the UWF but the fact that he is yet to win a major Championship amazes me. The temporary roster has been the scene of quite a lot of chopping and changing. Mickey Bombastic was offered a full MWF contract but turned the company down, he is currently touring Australia with a promotion and is then hoping to wrestle in Japan. Speaking with Dom Stoate I understand that Bombastic has an open offer with the MWF so that when he returns he is entitled to walk straight onto the roster after an impressive temporary spell. Shawn Hunt was less than impressive and he was given his marching orders, he showed no ambition and if there is one thing that Walker hates more than Anarkist, it has to be slackers. Two new wrestlers have been signed up to take the place of both Bombastic and Hunt. One of these two men is not so new, The Vine has been in the MWF before and returns to try and make amends for a stint in the MWF which did not satisfy either the MWF or himself. He will be bringing his unique style to help the MWF on its continual push for greatness. The second wrestler is Craig Clayborn and I've been watching Craig for quite a while and can tell you he is the next Troy Knight. He has the same personal understanding with the fans and loves to be infront of the camera, he's willing to let you into his life and the demand from the fans is for more wrestlers like Troy, Ace and now Craig. The biggest farewell in MWF history was had backstage at Implosion as Anarkist departed. I shed a tear or two as he walked out of the MWF for the last time, no longer a Champion and a man I feel who will not be totally happy with how Implosion went. He gave ten months of loyal service and to be fired by Walker was very out of the blue. Of course this firing was simply to enhance the storyline but one gets the feeling Anarkist would have prefered to go out in glory. Dynamite then continued the good work that Implosion started, it's beginning to recover its flare and it began with a segment between Insane Wayne and Iniquity. The MWF Tag Team Champions are getting a lot of airtime these days and you can see why, the on screen chemistry is all there and the two men discussed Implosion and Wayne decided Iniquity should get an MWF World Championship shot. The opening match was poor by Dynamite standards. Iniquity and Doomsday couldn't quite click again and Doomsday managed to stumble through and get a victory, Iniquity was physically tired and Doomsday seemed cautious not to make mistakes as he really wanted the first victory. He got it, not in style but it's a W in the win column. Another segment and this time it was Insane Wayne in the office of the MWF President. Fantastic work from these two, the fans know there is a great deal of history between them and Walker was doing his best to make it dramatic and it worked. Wayne asked for Iniquity to get a shot at the MWF World Championship but Walker's not that stupid, he booked Wayne against Kidd Kidman next week with the condition that if there was to be a shot for Iniquity, Wayne would earn it. The Main Event was a good match, Sean Harley sleepwalked through it while Mickey Bombastic gave one hundred percent and I thought he was going to go on to become a huge star if he hadn't decided to move onto Australia. Sheldahl was hardly a factor in the match and if anything he took away from the chemistry between Bombastic and Harley. Harley picked up what will be for him an easy victory. Dynamite was a normal show, nothing really special about it....except the segment between Walker and Wayne, I hope we see more of these two together as it is obvious they work really well together. The management have booked for Dynamite upcoming a card that can only be described as packed! Here's what I think will go down... Craig Clayborn versus Sean West versus Seth Caliko versus The Vine: The first of two fatal four way's and I think Walker needs to sort this out. I don't mind the odd fatal four way but to throw random wrestlers into matches is not helping anybody. Having said that there are some excellent wrestlers in this, Sean West looking for his first MWF victory will not find it easy. Craig Clayborn looks to me as if he can be a big star in the future and Seth Calkio and The Vine will want to prove to their worth. I have to tip Sean West, it's about time he won a match! Iniquity versus Tommy Raines versus Doomsday versus The Jeopardizer: Going to be a shock for The Jeopardizer this, being so far down on a Dynamite card. It's the first time he's been a part of Dynamite for months now and he'll no doubt be looking at the card and feeling a bit shocked. Iniquity is the fastest rising wrestler in the MWF at the moment, Tommy Raines shows an attitude that could explode at any moment and Doomsday is a wrestler who has plenty of experience and a dangerous opponent in the ring. This match could be the match of the night if all perform to expectations, I'd go for The Jeopardizer to get a vital returning victory. NomaD versus Zach Fredricks: The feud continues, eight days after their match at Implosion they're at it again and this time it's non-title and non-hardcore. We're going to see these two men wrestle and that should swing the match in favour of Fredricks, having said that NomaD has EyCe at his side and Fredricks could really do with a partner to even the numbers a little. Insane Wayne versus Kidd Kidman: The Main Event will see Kidd Kidman in the ring for the first time since his Implosion success and Insane Wayne awaits. I have to admit I wouldn't usually give Kidman a prayer in this match but recently he's shown a determination to take the fight to his opponent and the way to beat Insane Wayne is to get him onto the back foot. Wayne is fighting for the chance of Iniquity having a shot at Kidman next week, I don't think Kidd will be wanting to defend his MWF World Championship at next weeks Dynamite. That's all for this week folks, I'd like to wish you the best of health as recently there has been a bit of illness going round the MWF. All the best. JP (John Pilkington) MWF - JP's Column
