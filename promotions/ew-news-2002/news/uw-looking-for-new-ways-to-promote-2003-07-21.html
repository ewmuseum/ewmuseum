uW looking for new ways to promote.

21 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rumors swirled last week that universalWrestling was considering sponsorship of a race team, those rumors were confirmed today. Christian Keaton, in his weekly column, 'From The Top', confirmed today that uW is indeed looking for new ways to promote the established, yet overlooked promotion. "Yes, there will be a uW race team. Dan Reirdon, uW Board member and I have had an unsponsored race team running in the USAR Hooters Pro Cup series this season." Keaton wrote; "We have had a few single race sponsors, but no full time backing. This week Dan and I spoke to the board, and received approval for a full-time sponsorship of the number 29 Chevy Monte Carlo in the USAR series." The new uW Board of Directors brought Christian Keaton back for unique ideas to promote the fed, and this is clearly one of them. "I have no clue how this story was leaked, but there you go, it's public." Keaton commented. The driver is Josh Reirdon, son of a Keaton business partner. The car will debut this week at Hickory, North Carolina Speedway in the Galaxy Foods 250. universalWrestling
