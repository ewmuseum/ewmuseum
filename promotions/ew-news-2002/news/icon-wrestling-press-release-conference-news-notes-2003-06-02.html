ICON Wrestling Press Release, Conference News & Notes

2 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
News and notes from the ICON Wrestling news conference today in Pittsburgh, PA. PITTSBURGH, PA - Local Wrestling fans are practically salivating at recent turn of events here. Earlier today, Avery Black, a local investor, announced plans to open a regional wrestling promotion that would cater to the interests of modern wrestling fans and wrestlers. Of greater interest was the announcement that local legend-- and world wide superstar-- Hades would be involved in the project. In fact, the federation, ICON Wrestling, would hold shows out of Hades' Hell House training school. The school has produced, among others, former EWA Champion Mack Ensassarol, and WEW superstar Texas Jack Danson. "We're thrilled to have an industry veteran like Hades involved with our product," an ICON representative commented. "The Hell House is ideal for the type of shows we want to produce. Not only will our athletes be able to perform in front of modest crowds, but they'll be able to make use of the on-site training facilities as well." Hades involvement also also caused speculation about ICON's involvement with the IWF/WOW, of which Hades is a current member. "Well, let's just say we're working on it," noted Steve Omen, the future play-by-play man for the show. "Certianly, the IWF/WOW is one of the, if not the premiere federation's in the industry today. I'm sure the higher ups of ICON would love to establish working relations with the I Slash. What I can confirm," Omen reveals, "is that we have been in contact with Neo-Pro, perhaps to do some cross promotional material with them. We're also talking with various other feds about doing projects, although I can't say anything at this time." Omen offered some information about the format that ICON would use. "Well, there won't be any flash shows, for one thing. We just don't have the funds for that. Our talent will just have to develop their skills during the show itself, tentatively called 'Straight From Hell.'" SFH will be produced weekly, although shows will be shorter than what wrestling fans might be accustomed to. "It's all about examining the market. We wanted to strike a balance between coming out on a regular basis and the budget that we were working on. ICON will have a steady stream of short, easy to watch shows for today's fan." The fledling promotion is looking to begin broadcasting its shows at the end of June. "I'd say that's a good estimate. Right now we're still looking to complete the roster. We've taken in less established indy wrestlers as well as some fresh graduates from the Hell House, although we're still looking for a few more guys to round out the roster. We're taking in any weight class, although we don't have any women's or tag division as of the moment." ICON Wrestling
