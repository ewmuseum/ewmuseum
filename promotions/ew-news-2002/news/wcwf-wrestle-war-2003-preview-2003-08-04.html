WCWF: Wrestle War 2003 preview

4 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It’s official. This year’s WCWF Wrestle War pay-per-view will be the biggest ever. All of the federation’s titles will be on the line in the legendary Match Beyond, one of the warring factions will disband, and a WCWF legend will leave the federation… forever. WCWF Commissioner Jackson Therapy spoke after releasing the final details of the event. “In past years, the Match Beyond has been a multiple-team test of endurance and trust. This year, with Nathanial Duke and the Fates trying again and again to destroy the federation, we’ve felt it necessary to put together a match that will settle this feud once and for all.” The normal Match Beyond setup consists of two rings, side-by-side, surrounded by a steel cage with a roof on top. Team members (Wrestle War 2002 featured five four-man teams) enter one at a time until all of the wrestlers are inside the structure. Once all wrestlers were in, the eliminations would begin. If a wrestler was knocked out for a ten count, he and his team were out of the match. The winning team’s captain would receive a shot at the WCWF World Title at the next month’s PPV, Capital Combat. “But what we’ve done is make this year’s Match Beyond a two-team affair,” Therapy said. “The four remaining members of Duke’s group will be on one side, and four men representing the WCWF will be on the other team. The Judge, Matt Walker, Thump, and Shawn Stevens are the four men I’ve chosen to represent WCWF. “You’ll also notice that all of the major title holders are in this match. That’s because all of the titles will be on the line. Because of this, we’re doing the eliminations differently this year. This year, if a wrestler fails to answer a 10-count or says “I Quit”, he’s eliminated from the match, but the rest of his team stays in. The winning team will get all of the titles. The losing team must break up.” The other co-main event match on the card is a “Loser Leaves Town” match between WCWF legends and former friends Nathanial Duke and Raymond Chandler. And not only will the loser leave the federation, he will also hand over control of his group (if it is still intact) to the winner. When asked about all of the stipulations for the event, Therapy had a strong response. “It needs to end. This deal between Nate and Ray, between the Fates and the federation… it needs to end,” Therapy said. And at Wrestle War, it will. It’s only three matches, but they’re three matches that will change the face of the WCWF forever. ----- Wrestle War card: Loser Leaves Town Match: Nathanial Duke vs. Raymond Chandler Loser quits the federation, also, he hands the rights of leadership of his group, either the KoA or the Fates, to the other man. Wrestle War: The Match Beyond Wrestle War this year will be a two-team, eight-man event and will have the World, US, International Television title and Tag Team titles on the line. All these belts will go to the winning team, the losing team will also disband their group. Team WCWF: WCWF World Champion The Judge, Shawn Stevens, WCWF U.S. Champion Matt Walker, and Thump Team Duke: Jester, WCWF International Television and Tag Team Champion Dane Rennier, WCWF Tag Team Champion Stan Stone, and Brad Jackson Hardcore Invitational Battle Bowl: Double ring, top rope eliminations, hardcore rules.
