Kahn Announces Coming Soon 19th November 2003 The president and executive director of the PW made a major announcement out to all of the wrestling world. He made a remarkable speech and assured the people that the PW is no joke. He took names of some of the greatest highlights of wrestling and appointed them as his staff members. He said that the Phenomena of Wrestling will make its mark in the wrestling world and today is not just any regular day, it is the day when the idea of PW was told out to the rest of the world. He further continued his speech and said that the weekly show of PW will soon be named, at the moment it is only known as Saturday Night Live! Not very attractive I know but he stated that the staff and faculty is still indecisive about what the name should be. A lot of backtracking had to be done to pick out the members of the staff for the upcoming PW. He also stated that the PW will only have a specific number of superstars who will take part so that individual attention doesn't wear off and that this way each and every wrestler in the PW will be known. Mr. Kahnn also tells the press a little story about his previous experiences with a man known as Joe O' Connor, the man with whom he had begun the amazing reign of the GWO (www.globalwo.com). But unfortunately that man had fired Mr. Kahn because an argument had taken place. He concluded by saying that just a few days after he got fired, the GWO officially closed down. He said that him and his group of professional staff members will never let such a tragedy happen to the PW and that the PW is coming soon. He told the press that the PW is ready to take over with a storm. Phenomena of Wrestling (coming soon)
