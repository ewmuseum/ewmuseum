Thug roC wins match at PRIME PPV!

15 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thug roC steps into a PRIME ring with a lawsuit at stake. PRIME It was agreed on, that if Thug roC were to defeat both Shane Wethers and Mr. Showtime at Fatal Flaw, that he'd have to drop his lawsuit against PRIME. Thug roC, who is currently suing PRIME for wrongful termination, seems to have had the tables stacked against him, facing two great opponents at the same time last Sunday. Thug roC though, proved why he is the so-called "best cruiserweight in the business" Thug roC, with the help with a steel chair, was able to pin Shane Wethers in the ring, and take the win over both men. Thug roC, not exactly the modest of all people, was able to escape with the win, leaving the PRIME fans upset. Thug roC, who is the eVo United Champion, seems to want to go full-steam with his lawsuit against PRIME. He knows, that if he wins the lawsuit, he will have two options. He can either put PRIME down the drain, or take control of PRIME, putting Taun Pham out of a job, much like Pham did him a few months back. It should be interesting to see what Thug roC will do these next few weeks, but I doubt he will be quiet
