Pressure Point 75 Review

12 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, I'm back dunna nunna nunna dunna nunna... ACTION! PRESSURE POINT REPORT By: Keith Scott Zimmerman E-mail: [detail withheld] Date: 10.9.3 (taped 8.9.3.) The Big News: To paraphrase "Vietnow", Jeff is your Jeff is your Jeff is your only god. Title Changes: None. Face/Heel Turns: Daze, maybe? Best Match: 4. Xuxa played Ricky Morton off the bat and we set up the sandwich dropkicks with a double facebuster. Tanya got a heat segment in and things settled down. Tried to fend her off but she got the Scott Hall battering ram in. Pah. Double team dragon suplex but I break the plain, which is really the only way one should get out of such a move. Messed up a spinebuster combo (Fated Hurricane?) and Pierre wasted Xuxa with the Time Bomb before getting back in. Set up Pierre with a DVD but the Guillotina missed! We pressed on with an impressive suplex/triple jump quebrada combo only for X to break it up with a somersault springboard Perro Aguayo. Wow. Finally we do the lucha everyone dies on the floor trainwreck to a huge spot, though the leg lariat suicida damn near made me wet myself. Triple jump off the old guy, but I take the backdrop driver to set up the 9 count. (Me taking a BDD. The IRONY, it stifles.) They counter Deluge but I KEEL X with an STO into the rail, a rising palm strike, and an Exploder on the floor. They hit me with a thing so bad-ass I can't really describe it other than OW and Pierre hits the guillotine legdrop this time for the save. After countering a double brainbuster, they fell pray to the Renaissance (a Stackplex with a super release fucking German) and the Diamond Dust Superkick combo. Wow. Probably the best match I've had to date, in about my fourth tag match. Those girls rock. Worst Match: 1. Pretty much a squash won with the BELLY-TO-BELLY OF DEATH; couple cheap shots for Daze afterwards to get his heat back. (What heat, yes, I know. I am an Internet writer.) Absurdity of the Show: Conrad Ramsey gets a shot at the A title before ME? I mean, what the HELL is that about? I know Jeff's protective of the belt, but c'mon, I earned #1 contender by being in that time limit draw where I really won the match. And you KNOW THIS. Moment of the Show: The ORIGINAL Action Championship. Breakdown: Ellen & Anne gave a half-assed apology...Jeff gave his victory speech, and unveiled a new and better Action Championship belt. Right before he could give me the first show, Danger interrupted. Seeing as he tried to jump in line ahead of me to get the first shot (uh, who came closest to beating Jeff last? Thank you) me & the guys had to check our ring sizes with his ugly mug. Avalon & the Arcades came out, followed by Granpa Simpson. Jeff gave him the shot instead...Daze/Scott tonight, loser goes back to the side of the milk carton...The Survivors made Jeremy's acquaintance...1) See above...Speaking of the side of the milk carton, Ninja K popped in for a cameo...Paulton promo...Hm, TV's after Michelle Branch and he's facing LLB tonight. This is one of those Miami/Florida situations where I hope the scoreboard falls and kills the lot of their stupid asses...Prodigy/Danger for the C Title tonight. Wow, TWO Miami/Floridas. This is undoubtedly punishment for my absence...Sharc plotted revenge; how sad is a feud when I have to root for freaking SHARC...They're gonna pair up Ninja K and Deft because the Hardy Boyz were great but what they were missing was ABSOLUTE SILENCE...Sabotage/LoD3 tonight...2) Above average face v. face. False finish in the new kid's favor, so maybe something'll come of that... Sharc finally becomes a face in my eyes by beating the shit out of Fox & Bill after a trainwreck segment...Leigh & Rook talked shop with the Dangers...IBJ taunted Coral...3) Stupid SE match, Britney Spear wins it, woo hoo hoo it's all been done...Sharc turns heel with a go-go PIW promo. It's DEAD. Ex-federation. It ceases to be. It's gone to meet it's maker. It's bleeding demised. OK Go 3:16 sez GET GET GET GET GET OVER IT. Capische?...Deft whined about getting teamed with Ninja...4) See above...Levine cut his ninth-rate brooding Raven clone promo before Sharc interrupts; PPV match for the actual worthless piece of shit to follow, probably with stipulations. Miami/Florida III. Just rolling tonight, aren't we...Heimdal's taking time off so that Jeff doesn't accidentally hit him with nine inches of limp dick any time soon...5) Russa got a moment in the sun but the newbies won a near-squash via a powerbomb/modified guillotine legdrop combo... Ninja went in for character makeover...6) Danger won with the crossface...Ninja & Deft...still hate each other! Surely they will be D champions soon, because that's the way this sort of thing usually turns out...Ringer talked to A!M...Jeff psyched out Ramsey...7) Ha, Jeff cracked him in the mush with the belt before the bell rang. Genius. Anyway, Conrad chopped Jeff for like five minutes straight to get the momentum back. Absolutely numbing. Ramsey cheated by using the post and using the Britney but Jeff kicked out. Fnyeah. In a great combo, Jeff Ravened him into the corner and followed up with the running Li--Garvin Bomb for a nearfall. Conrad hit an Arn and Original Slam countered the actual Original Slam for another. So THAT'S who Coral's dad is! MetamuciLock but Jeff gets out with Zimmerman CI before the REAL OrigiSlam--damn it, kick out. Pah. Chair beatdown leads to the Jeffshooter. Allelujah. Grade: A-. Good stuff this week, the title matches esp. I'm sorry if sounds like I fucked the Legacy this week but for kids their age to have these intricate sequences and put on that match, I'm just in awe. They could become the Midnight Express of the 21st Century if that big old guy doesn't eat them both in their sleep. *hears tittering* *reads it back* No, people, NO! No! People...
