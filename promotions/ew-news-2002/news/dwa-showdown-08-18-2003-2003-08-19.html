DWA: Showdown 08/18/2003

19 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The final show before Downward Spiral. In it, the DWA saw yet another person eliminated on the road to declare a new World Champion. With Jordan Brannigan gone until the pay per view, Alexandria Simmons has been using her powers to the utmost advantages. She apparantely tried to seduce Gideon Nazareth, but for what reason, it is uknown. It didn't work, as Gideon caught on to her plan, and while it was tempting, if nothing else but to ultimately screw over Brannigan, he walked away. Gideon Nazareth vs. Ninja X Gideon Nazareth made short work of the man who is trying to desperately prove that he can still bring in ratings, because if he can't, he will be fired on Downward Spiral. Gideon forced him to tap out with the Reckless Abandon. After the match, we see Television Champion, Brad Keidis walking the halls. Keidis was the first man eliminated from the World Title Elimination match just a few days ago on Surge. Also, a few weeks ago, he came out and destroyed his former FoW stable mates. Well, one of them came back for revenge, as John Reed beat the hell out of Keidis, and challenged him to a match for the TV Title on Downward Spiral. Keidis accepted, and the match was set. Elsewhere backstage, Ninja X was goaded by Shockwave, another wrestler who has to prove his worth by the pay per view. The two are life long enemies, and it looked like they were about to go at it right there in the hallway, when suddenly a woman stopped them. Nobody had ever seen this woman before. She offered them the opportunity of a life time. Both were so intrigued that they went with her to listen to what she had to say. Alex Grey vs. Typhoid Mary vs. Donnie Darko In what was another awesome match between the three, in the end, Typhoid Mary eliminated Donnie Darko from the match, leaving just her and Alex Grey as the possibilities to take on Gideon Nazareth at Downward Spiral for the undisputed World Heavyweight Championship. Gideon watched from ringside the match go down, and then all participants received a shock. Alexandria Simmons came out and said that the new World Champion must be the best, and the only way to prove they are the best is to see the Main Event of Downward Spiral...Gideon Nazareth vs. the winner of Alex Grey and Typhoid Mary...to be an IRON MAN MATCH!!! The crowd went crazy at this, as this is the first Iron Man Match in the history of the DWA. It will definitely be a huge match, and it's less than a week away. DWA: Dragon Wrestling Association
