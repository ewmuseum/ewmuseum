Anyone who questioned the intensity within the AWA would have thought differently by seeing the scene that took place that lonely afternoon in the small Virginia town. However, the action took place not in the center of the ring but in a conference room. The battles all started over the name of the federation and continued on to the very product they wanted to produce each week. "I wanted to just give up the old name and go on about our business, however the other members of the board had taken it personal." stated the CEO. "Finally calm heads won out and I made them realize that our true success isn't based in a name, it's based in the quality of production." After the board members finally agreed on the new name, AWA, a whole new problem faced the group. "We have a wrestler by the name of Jason Carter, this guy is a loose cannon." The CEO said an obvious look of despair on his face. "He has unlimited talent, but damn it, he will do anything to be a thorn in my side. I know this guy is going to try and ruin our plans to go national." All though the meetings ran into the wee hours of the morning, no solution was found to keep Carter off of Insanity and away from the USA network's scouts who will be in Hollywood for the July 16th edition of the show. "How can you keep one of your biggest draws off the card, and still expect to win over a major network like USA?" asked the CEO. As I, Tony Tsunami, write this column we are already seeing the headaches that can accompy running a business with the potential of the AWA. It seems like darts can come in flying from every angle, and no dart is as sharp and pointed at the heart of the federation like Jason Carter. Everyone realizes that a national televison deal would not only raise the federation's exposure level but could also raise the star power of it's wrestlers, and even Carter realizes this. However Carter fears the worst when it comes to expansion - growth equals more headaches for the superstars and more competition from the outsides. Carter fears growth because of his own gain, it's not what's best for the AWA... it's what's best for Jason Carter. I hope to be getting an exclusive interview with Carter in the coming days, so please check back often for the latest news on this breaking story. This is Tony Tsunami signing off! AWA
