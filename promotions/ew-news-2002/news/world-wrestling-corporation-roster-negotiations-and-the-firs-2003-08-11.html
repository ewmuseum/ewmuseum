World Wrestling Corporation: Roster, Negotiations, and The First Show

11 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The World Wrestling Corporation has been an up and coming federation that has been giving alot of the newer faces of wrestling a chance to shine. With superstars like Osyrus, Trent Hazard and Kory Storm being bound by contract...time can only tell what will turn up. HBO and WWC have been meeting with negotations...but a clause has been brought about... Earlier today, Home Box Office met with World Wrestling Corporation (WWC) executives today about a possible deal which would give the WWC a time slot once a week for a weekly wrestling program. The higher ups of the HBO company have stated they are very interested in the proposal, but require a sample of the work (or possibly a trailer) to review in order to make a final decision. Paul Levine, owner of the WWC, took a few weeks to figure out how they could showcase their wrestling program...but after some long meetings...the board of the World Wrestling Corporation have come up with a way to showcase their talent. On August 20th, 2003; World Wrestling Corporation will be presenting their first ever event in the Nassau Coliseum in Uniondale, Long Island, New York. The location is where the Elite Wrestling Corporation, WWC's Developmental federation, started back in 1996...thus alot of fans will look forward to seeing some EWF characters return. The show will not be televised, but full results will be available at the WWC Webpage (http://wwc.ewlounge.com). Tickets went on sale just three days ago, and the sale has been going quite well with a good amount of tickets currently being sold. Besides the actual roster being in attendace, the board of Home Box Office (HBO), executives from FOX Broadcasting Company, as well as members of the USA Network will be showing up to view the prospect wrestling show live. It's expected that each of these networks will be looking for: crowd reaction, how well the wrestlers perform, if fans enjoy the product, and how well they are treated upon their time away from the office. "This is the first big step for this federation," said Vice President of WWC, Aaron "Tact, "It's a risky move, one that could be the deciding factor as to whether or not this federation is going anywhere. But we are looking forward to the venture...and hope that the network executives see how good of a product we can produce" The official card was given out on the WWC website, and thus far the card goes as thus... Disciplinarian Many New York fans recognized many of the names being from the federation "Xtreme Wrestling (XW)". The East Coast based federation of XW is quite popular in Florida and New York, and since the event was being held in New York (for about $20 tickets), the fans were very interested. Names like Disciplinarian, Gods of Death, Rivan, Mattock, KaGe, Blazer, Devastation and Ryojin Tachikawa...all names from XW (nearly half of the show...not a coincidence). Also the names from EWF (Asian Sensation, Steve Muzio, Sangre and Ken Davison) helped bring in a crowd. But the Main Event announced was what got many fans interested between former PIW World Champion, Chris Mezz, former IOW World champion, Kory Storm, and former Action! host and PIW superstar, Trey Vincent. And we promise you, at WWC ONLINE...we will be giving you full updates on the show and how it went for the WWC. This is the deciding factor...now all must wait and see how it will turn out.
