uW shakes Detroit

7 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The uW road report from Detroit, Michigan is finally one to be proud of. Deals made and deals broken, but the fans got every pennies worth. Bane Loneheart made his uW debut in a big way; by welcoming another newbie with a chairshot! Turk rumbled down uW's entrance ramp and was met with metal from Loneheart. The two met up backstage later on and had to be separated by pseudo-manager Tim Reigns. Rumors from the booking department have these two slated to face-off on next week's show. Bryan McFadden tried to get an update on all of uW's hiring moves and was handed this quote; "Fine you want a story? Here's all I can give you: on July 13th uW will never be the same, or more like it ever was." quoted from Rick Little uW CEO. Bryan was as confused as I was at this one. Match one was Tyrael versus Steven Rawlings for the Hardcore Title. Rawlings won but not until after local police interrupted the bout to question him about the murder of fellow uW roster member Nuissance. Police officials allowed the match to end after the interruption, but wisked Stephen away in cuffs after the '1-2-3'. Ashton Crowley and Tony Field, Dysfunction, made a deal with the devil. Better known as Tyler Bayne, uW owner, to protect uW from the onslaught of the Reckless group. In a show of "teamwork" DiscoBoy Johnny K. and lesbian sisters, Cindy and Mindy put on a display at center ring for the Detroit crowd. The view was well received except for Under the Influence. Wes and Kirbinati stormed the ring and powerbombed both women to the concrete floor as fWo members Leroy and Hector appeared to help. This move by Wes and Kirbinati forced the wavering fWo to choose it's side in the battle for uW, and those chose Universal over Reckless. Match three pitted Wes of UTI versus Hector of fWo, thanks to the booking of Jaysin Styles Reckless leader. Of course poor over-matched Hector was hung out to dry by the two younger men and Wes won after a chairshot to Hector, special thanks to tag partner Kirbinati. Shawn StJames, another new member of the Reckless faction, did meet eye-to-eye with former Universal Champ Tony Field. StJames beat Field at the latest uW pay-per-view, Requiem, with the help of his new stable-mates. StJames threatened Field's life, and it is very clear the fued between these two stars in far from settled. The main event saw a teaser for the aforementioned fued; Matt Andrews and Shawn StJames squared off versus Dysfunction (Tony Field and Universal Champion Ashton Crowley). Andrews still has Crowley's title belt, and Andrews still insists he's the champion despite what played out on national television. Of course this match wound up with no winner because Reckless appeared to save their buddies StJames and Andrews with their ringleader Jaysin Styles in tow. The ref lost control and Crowley still doesn't have his title belt back. Nuissance isn't dead. Well, why in the hell was Stephen Rawlings arrested earlier in the night for his death then? Huge charade, Rawlings in cuffs, locked in a small room, Nuissance appears-- yes, do the math. Show run down- Attendence: 10,349 Tyrael v Stephen Rawlings- Rawlings wins. (13:23) Turk (fed debut) v Brooks Boren- No contest, not only did Turk beat Boren to a pulp backstage, both men never made it to the ring thanks to the interfere from Bane Loneheart.(0:00) Wes (UTI) v Hector (fWo)- Wes wins. (8:36) Parental Advisory (Mustang & Jacobs) v Slash Tannon- Parental Advisory wins (Jacobs pin). (5:11) MAIN EVENT- Matt Andrews & Shawn StJames v Dysfunction (Tony Field & Ash. Crowley) No contest, Reckless interfered, who would have guessed? (17:54) universalWrestling
