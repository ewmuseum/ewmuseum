PCW Friday Fusion Recap For 6/13

15 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The night was characterized by the women who affected nearly every part of the night's show, including the giant main event that would see more than one surprise in the its course. Yeah, we're getting a logo, bare with us. Cal Seaver opens the show talking about his expectations and excitement for the night, until he sees the gas-masked man who has been messing with Abner. He tries to talk to him but gets no response Dave and Jeff do their customary opening spiel about the night After a short contest, Sergio Bullent d. Robertson with a schoolboy roll up after a failed stalling suplex by Robertson Flaymin Aymin comes out to address his sorrow for the death of El Diablo’s wife, Angela, after the tractor incident a few weeks back. He says he’s sorry and hopes that he and El Diablo can let this feud go before something else horrible happens Syn and James Edwards enter their own sleep chambers, preparing for their trip to space Stone Burns confronts a woman he thinks is Lacuna Debris, but finds out it isn’t. As he gets away from her Lacuna comes into the picture and he tries to attack but is seized by a group of security guards courtesy of Lacuna Manuel Labor d. Stone Burns (maintaining the PCW Extreme Championship) after Lacuna comes out to distract Burns, giving Labor the chance to dropkick a chair into Burns’ face to claim the victory Silvio Rochelli is talking on his cell phone to someone he has hired to follow Haley around, saying not to screw up and not to worry about Matt Jackson who is on the ship with him, or anyone else for that matter Syn and Edwards are still in the sleep chambers, Syn is busy singing along to Britney Spears’ “Oops I did It Again” Miss Moonlight d. Lacuna Debris to keep her PCW Cruiserweight Title after an action packed, fast paced, cheat free match, hitting her finisher, Moonlight. Sivasian Krikorian and SOCIETY come out from the back and attack Moonlight. Windsor then comes out to help her allies, but Sivasian and SOCIETY regain control Abner talks to a shrink about all the latest happenings with the masked man and about his favorite topic, alcohol Justice Inc. are discussing the new superheroes, and their own futures. Warrior suggests they watch cartoons to calm themselves and their counterparts appear on the TV saying that Justice Inc. is out and Justice Co. is in Matt Jackson pulls out with a victory in the Four Corners match against Silvio Rochelli, Tyler Preston, and Warrior, nailing Silvio with Fatal Impact. John Gargano comes out and E2 stomp away at Warrior. Blind Rage comes out for the save, but gets attacked by E2 as well The Steel Masked Man is in the boiler room of the airship and talks about how there are 720 hours left until he reveals himself, and how the Triumvirate will come to see their faults as he will bring justice to PCW, ridding them of the Triumvirate Syn and Edwards are still in the sleep chambers. Edwards is sleeping and Syn is still suffering from delirium as he plays with sock puppets What should be a one on one No DQ match, turns into a handicapped match as John Gargano comes out with his partner, Tyler Preston. Brianna instantly falls to the double team, and things get worse as Dorn Tracer comes out making it a triple team on Brianna. He cracks her with a steel chair and it looks like it’s all over until the Steel Masked Man makes an unexpected assistance to Brianna taking out Tracer. In the end Brianna d. Gargano with a hurricarana DDT type maneuver Gaz Green announces that he won’t be fighting Dante Jackson, but that another Green will take his place, Tessa Windsor-Green! Their match goes back and forth and Gaz offers some assistance by shocking Jackson. In the end Dante Jackson d. Tessa hitting the Osaka Street Cutter. Jackson and Gaz attack each other, but Gaz gives him a shock of a lifetime, using it to aid his escape Syn and Edwards are finally out of the sleep chambers The Main Event instantly turns into a four on one against the PCW World Champ, Skylar Green. One by one they pound away on Skylar, until Miss Moonlight comes out from the back showing Bob Charlie a paper saying she is Skylar’s partner. Moonlight helps even the score a bit and everything just turns into chaos resulting in Bob Charlie calling the bell and ruling it a no contest. Primetime Championship Wrestling
