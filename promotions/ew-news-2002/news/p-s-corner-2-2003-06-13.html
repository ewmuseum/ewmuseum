P.'s Corner #2

13 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Why do people think that I'm dumber then I actually am? I'm not Harry! I'm not Lloyd! This one goes out to all you dumb asses! Mick P.'s Corner #2 Well, this weeks installation of my column has come around, and today, you can bet your sweet ass I'll talk about some comments I got about my last column. First off... the tA remark about the champ being Nova and not some person people call Nerva... I'm Mick P. damn it! I don't make mistakes, so the name is Nova, not Nerva! But no! Several people had to email me telling me I was wrong, well bullshit, I'm not dumb like the rest of you, remember, you fans don't know shit about the wrestling world, I know much more then you do for sure! I'll just grace you all with some of the comments I got, here's the first email... "Dear Mick P., I recently read your column in eWNews and I just wanted to say that you suck! First off, how could anyone in the wrestling world not know that the woman you referred to as Nova is actually Nerva!? I mean, come on, she made the number 1 Newsmaker for doing that once and you should know that because you work for the damn magazine. So in short, you're a dumb f*ck and should be shot for being that. Sign, John Q." Well, John Q., I'm not dumb, it is you that is dumb, her name is Nova and I know that for sure. If you want to send bitchy ass comments like this, then do that with the Bitch With Mitch column, not mine, because I won't tolerate it. Ok, so back to wrestling, what's new this week? Well, we have the rebirth of the hWo, yes, Hardcore Wrestling Organization is back with another installment, hopefully this federation will do much better then before, and it's last run under Trev Echo was pretty good if I remember correctly, now this Trevor Norman fellow should do a good job with the hWo, but I just thought it deserved at least a mentioning in my column, you can see it's first show back on the new TNN/Spike TV Sunday night at 8:00 PM EST, 7:00 PM CST. Other news this week, MET II is shaping together nicely, but will it surpass Survivor III in any glory or ratings? I seriously doubt it will, first off OSE is doing MET II at the wrong time, so it can just get the Survivor III rejects at this moment, that's about all, it's like Fame coming on right after American Idol, it's just getting the damn rejects, not anyone who's worth being on the damn thing. But, I wish OSE and the tag teams who join MET II the best of luck, the concept of it is sure to be interesting to see, so I'll keep a lazy eye on it in the next week and will comment more on it as it becomes more clear. Speaking of Survivor III, RAM is hospitalized for some seemingly unknown reason, either that or no one really cares! The only teams in that damn contest that really matter are Tim Walden/Jane Matheson, Brotherly Love, and that Shabazz team, and that's basically it. Tim Walden though, I thought I'd need to mention this to everyone, because not many people know Tim Walden. But Walden has come a long way in Survivor III, by far the most surprising person in the competition. Many fans will remember Walden from his near two year run in Primetime Championship Wrestling, where he won a few titles, including the Cruiserweight title and TV title, but he never did get much respect over there, from fans or other wrestlers, so to see a guy move from a fed where he got no respect to a huge opportunity and a show hosted by the fWo is pretty much amazing and inspiring, just shows what hard work and sacrifice can do for a man, and he's worked his ass off and deserves all that he's recieved from it, so go on Tim Walden, and win that damn thing! All right, I'm sorry, I had to get that out, Walden and I worked together for a short time, and I've worked with his lover... I mean friend, as well, who is now wrestling PWC, Byron Calver being that person. Speaking of PWC, what ever happened to the hype for the locally based federation? It just seems to have disappeared, it's like... Ron Starr has just lost all respect of the fans, the man formally known as The Phantom Booker has pretty much just lost his touch I guess in the wrestling world, and this one of the most shocking set backs in a federations past, I remember it started with such hype, but has just fallen dead short of expectations, but that doesn't mean it can't continue to climb, I'll keep an eye on it as well to bring you further updates next week, but there's not much to tell as far as I'm aware of over at PWC. As for PCW and OSE news, who gives a rats ass directly what happens in those feds... so I've got nothing. fWo hasn't done anything too special this week worth reporting, just continuing it's power drive in the ratings race against WWE. tA, who gives a damn... A!, once again, who gives a damn... Ah, here's an interesting note, rumor has it that ACW executives are calling former ACW wrestlers that are now currently enrolled in hWo verbal contracts "rejects", no, this is a true rumor now, a little birdy told me this one. And if that's right, then that shows to prove that there is no love lost between owner and wrestler in the wrestling world, not everyone can have a great relationship with their boss, and when you leave a fed like ACW for hWo, well, it just looks kind of sad for ACW, especially if you're a highlight of the federation. But, I thought that was worth giving some notion to in this column, nothing confirmed on that however, but it's a rumor. Other rumors, hell, I'm jackshit full of rumors I suppose, but who knows, half of these I could be making up just to fill in for time and space on my column, :-D. Rumor also has it that hWo owner, Trevor Norman has a few huge announcements for the show... well duh! I mean it's a new fed with the first show, that's almost a redundant rumor, but there are rumors that another established star in the wrestling world will be debuting in hWo soon, who that is, is unknown at this time, but I guess we'll just have to watch Sunday night, now won't we? Bah, screw the rumors, I don't like reporting rumors as they tend to all be false anyways, that's why their rumors, and the fact that I tend to start them half the time anyways. I guess that'll be all for this second installation of P.'s Corner... Next week for sure you'll bet I'll bitch about how I don't have my own logo on the website and magazine for my column, yet Mitch and Poison Ivy do, as well as whatever the hell happens in the wrestling world. For now, that's all folks, remember, send those comments, but any trash talk will just get trash talk back. -Mick P.
