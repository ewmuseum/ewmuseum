DWA: Showdown Results 07/03/2003

4 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just days after one of the biggest pay per view's in their history, the DWA broadcasts Showdown on a special night. The night began regularly enough...with a match. In fact, the match featured one of the wrestlers who made their debut in the Royal Rumble, Lupus Feral, along with his amazingly beautiful manager, Sophia Thorn. His opponent, none other than Impact. What's this you say? Wasn't Impact on the list of the wrestlers recently released from the DWA? He was indeed, but apparantely there is a clause in his contract that he cannot be released for at least a month after signing it. He had a mic in his hand and he was ready to address the crowd. Unfortunately for him, Lupus was ready for the match to begin, and he began a vicious attack on Impact which went to the outside of the ring, where Lupus peformed his finishing move, known as Feral Throwback, straight through the announce table. Lupus then climbed into the ring, and Impact was counted out. Sophia, Lupus's manager, got the mic and said they demanded Misfit, another newcomer who premiered in the rumble, who also happened to have thrown Lupus off the top of the ramp while making his debut, come out for a match. Misfit didn't come out, but Commissioner Gideon Nazareth sure did. Gideon, apparantely having his own agenda, said he could have Misfit in a match tonight, but not a one on one match. Instead, all the rookies of the DWA would be in a Battle Royal, and both Lupus and Misfit would be in the match. Lupus and Sophia accepted this for now, and left the ring, leaving Gideon all alone in the ring. Gideon's first order of business was payback to Ronnie Johnson, who is the former Commissioner, who was entered into the rumble in an attempt to win his spot back. Gideon said that the two people who helped Ronnie try to win, Derek Cantina and Donnie Darko, also known as the tag team, Intoxicated Justice), will face each other in a match next week on Showdown, and if they don't wrestle well against each other, they lose the Tag Title shot they earned for Testament. Then Gideon spoke directly to Ronnie Johnson, who since being demoted has been one of the commentators for Showdown. Gideon said seeing as how Ronnie wanted to be a wrestler, he's getting a match tonight. Before Gideon announced who his opponent was, Gideon himself attacked him, throwing him into the ring. Apparantely, Gideon booked himself into a match with Ronnie, and spent the next five minutes destroying him, before finally ending it with his finishing move and picking up the 1, 2, 3. But Gideon wasn't done yet, as he continued his assault, trying to permantely injure him, when Complicated comes out to the ring, making the save and running Gideon out of the ring. Gideon then told Complicated he would be taking on Grovel at next week's Showdown. In the back, President Jordan Brannigan was dealing with someone he considered a problem, in the form of Velocity Champion, Zula Shandor. He said that she would defend her title against Royal Rumble winner, LeStatt Knight, and the referee for the match would be Brad Keidis, who she is currently feuding with. Back in the ring, it was time for the Battle Royal to begin, as the newest members of the DWA made their way to the ring. EZ Target, Alex Jones, Cemetery Gates, Starr List, Lupus Feral, and Misfit. Lupus and Misfit immediately began to trade blows, and while they were against the ropes, Alex Jones dropkicked them both over the top rope, but they didn't notice as they continued to fight on the outside of the ring, and in the end, Misfit was thrown off the top of the ramp by Lupus. Back in the ring, Alex Jones was the next one to go, being eliminated by the largest female in professional wrestling, Starr List, with the combined help of Cemetery and EZ Target. But then, while Cemetery Gates was paying attention to Starr, Target eliminates him. EZ Target was the winner. But it wasn't over yet, as Cemetery climbed back in the ring, showing his anger at Target for the way he was eliminated, and the two began to trade blows, only for EZ Target to get a chair shot to the back of his head followed up by one to Cemetery Gates. Who did the chair swinging? Starr List, who left the ring, with a little smile. DWA: Dragon Wrestling Association
