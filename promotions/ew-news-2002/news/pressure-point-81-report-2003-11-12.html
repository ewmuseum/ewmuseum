Pressure Point 81 report

12 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hope you like segments... Date Aired (Taped): 5.11.03 (3.11.03) Number: 81 Location: Oakland Coliseum [i]Posted by Keith Scott Zimmerman[/i] The show began with Reed cutting a McMahon quality promo. And which McMahon? Stephanie. It's REED. Me/Max for the B next week, him vs. IBJ & TFF for it tonight--assuming, of course I just haven't set it up for him to get weakened before our match. Like I'd do such a thing. [b]1. Hiro Kitagami squashed the Phoenix.[/b] Phoenix looked as good as he could look for not pulling off any offense, and Hiro finished with the axe kick. (DUD) Segments! Brooding Raven Clone FUHFORWID. Another bizarre Nowell segment. Conrad & Julius kept sniping at each other. Jeff, soon to be Time's Man of the Year, looked out for Coral's well being. [b]2. Julius Arcade & Conrad Ramsey fought to a double countout.[/b] For some reason She was wearing a nurse's outfit. I get the feeling that was Reed's idea somehow. Conrad went for the anklelock right away but Julius kicked it away. Ramsey blew a kipup (why even bother?) but hit the AA for a nearfall. Julius hit the Angle overhead for the same, but then their partners came out and eventually they Pier 4ed their way to a DCO. So I guess Sophia Loren's not going to date either of them then. (*½) More segments! The new referee got re-signed despite the screwup last week. And more Nowell weirdness. [b]3. Coral Avalon beat Bradley Duncan.[/b] Gee, I wonder if they're going for a face/face David/Goliath vibe here. Naaaaaah. Jeff came out for commentary goodness. Nice to see Duncan's addiction is now safe enough to be mocked in a spot. Duncan gained advantage with the powerbomb and then hit a couple shoulderbreakers to start his arm work and for a near fall. Single-arm DDT and Fujiwara ARMbar. How the hell do you crib the Daniel Phillips playbook and never, ever win?! Play of the Day from Avalon but Bradley came back with the half-nelson suplex. That really should've ended things. Coral sent him to the floor with the Jericho springboard dropkick and then they did the Rey/Kurt lucha over the ref spot. Emerald Fusion and turnbuckle bomb but since's Bradley's a jobber...eh. Dudley Dog from Avalon and here's Bradley with LARIATOOOOOOOOOO~! But since's Bradley's a...yeah. Full revolution Curveball finished. (***) Son of more segments! IBJ & Rook don't like each other. Sharc feared that Fifth Rate Raven would bore his girlfriend to death. Reed's a moron. [b]4. I beat Rook Black.[/b] I hit the AA for one and I know I'm going to catch hell for that. You know, in retrospect I dominated this match and other than the tope con hilo and the Yakuza kick Rook didn't get in much offense. Nice end sequence where the Communication Breakdown actually got me the win off a simple Psychodrama reversal. Train keep a rollin. (***) Bride of the son of more segments! John Rocker died. Yay. Raven Lite burned Sharc's girlfriend with a branding iron. Where have you gone, Donnie Daze, a nation turns its lonely eyes to you. [b]5. Max Danger beat Brian James and the Flying Frenchie in a triple threat match to retain the Bantam Championship.[/b] Max ran like a pussy but they made him pay with a double suplex on the floor. Rook drew IBJ. Some man Max is, can't win a title match without running interference. Frenchie opted to tap out to Max's weak crossface since he had to go to fWo later. (negative 11 billionty. Or **.) Nephew of the daughter of the bride of the s...ah, fuck it. Me and Max fought, and with fighting there are no real winners, kids. Some new kid in. IBJ/Rook will be no DQ. MATCH OF THE NIGHT: Eh, nothing really jumped up and made me do the happy dance. One of the last three, pick one on your own personal prejudices, as I'd say mine. BEST SEGMENT: *shrug* FUNNIEST SEGMENT: Reed's opening show psychiatry session. WRESTLER OF THE NIGHT: Gotta say me by default. Coral beat Bradley; well, who hasn't? Max won a match I set up because Frenchie saw the opportunity to let me have the belt rather than take it, squash, DCO. I win by default. It's not a big honor this week, it's like winning Prettiest Inbred in the Applachians. WRESTLER WITH THE MOST MOMENTUM: Max for winning the triple threat AND the Wild Bomb Quadruplet. HOTTEST FUED: against Max. UNDERPERFORMER OF THE NIGHT: The following all lick balls for looking good last week and not following it up: Sabotage, the Legacy, Bobby Minio, and Jeff Garvin. FLATTEST SEGMENT OF THE NIGHT: I dunno, I'm not a big thing of whatever it is Nowell's doing with the Comedy Troupe B-Team. At least not yet. And I tried this week, so you apologists can cram it with walnuts. LINE OF THE NIGHT: "...and I'n turning into Jim Johnson! NOOOOO! I don't want to be gay!" A! Pressure Point 81
