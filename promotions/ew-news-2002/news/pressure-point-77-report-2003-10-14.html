Pressure Point 77 Report

14 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
...or Queen Kicks Off, Jeff Goes Down, and Things That Go Boom In the Night. ACTION! PRESSURE POINT REPORT By: Keith Scott Zimmerman E-mail: [detail withheld] Date: 8.10.3 (taped 6.10.3.), [77] The Big News: Avalon solidified his #1 contendership with a pinfall on Jeff in the main event six-man tag, and the announcers pushed it as the first "defeat" Jeff's suffered since winning the belt. Title Changes: None. Face/Heel Turns: None. Best Match: 4. We build heat--ooh, Russian STO, might have to keep that in the rotation--but Coral gets out with the Headliner. OR DOES HE? Rook gets the hot tag, and they steal our move with the Wotsitolla Boot. Finally, I play Ricky Morton (no, really) until the champs (that being us) get off the Best Tandem Brainbuster in the Business. Avalon saves. DEMOLITION DECAPITATION~! OLD SCHOOL THAT SHIT DOWN! Rook saves. I work Max over: dragon backbreaker, spine on the pine, and then the DOUBLE back suplex elbow driver with Pierre. Time Bomb doesn't do it. Danger Original Slams out the...uh...Original Slam and tags in Coral. Pier 6 leads to the 3B and Denoument on Rook, which does absolutely no good but everyone bought into, at least. Two-leg Owen drops me in front of Allison, Curveball takes out Pierre, and sandwich Roaring Elbows followed in concert with the Golden variant. Bah. Worst Match: 2. Squash. Is Brian getting punished for something? How do you go from being competitive against Avalon to getting destroyed with a chair shot by The Man Of 1,000 Faces? Absurdity of the Show: "Those with the pens didn't want to pencil us in?" It's offical: Ramsey's senile. Moment of the Show: I can't believe the WB let the words "donkey punch" make it to air. Breakdown: We came out to celebrate our dominance over everything, Max came out and I'll take the B belt from him at a point TBD in the Bay Area swing. He tried to ask Jeff for another shot and the Hamburglar came out, and ran his mouth about nothing in particular. Six-man main event...FUHFORWID...Sharc was on the lookout for KroW; Bait's changed to her real name...Danger/Landers on W/E this week...FUHFORWID...Ninja & Lucha got the verbal warning...Reed's mad at HardCase and is sending Daze after him... 1) Really good crazy stuff, reminiscent of Amazing Red/Low-Ki. Deft won with his Double Jeopardy...Sharc got some money...FUHFORWID...Arcades v. We Can't Hear You. Ha, Liz looks bummed. Too bad, sister! You had your chance, you pissed it away. Get SARS...FUHFORWID...Donnie Daze, the Corporate Carnage Champion. Those words make sense seperately but when you throw them together like that... 2) See above...Rook's the third man for the faces, who went off into this odd and yet logical Looney Tunes spiel...Donell Arcade, as he tends to do when someone or something gives him a swift kick in the pussy, is slowly turning heel...3) Didn't really go anywhere, established Donell as having heel tendencies, all came out in the wash with the TLD... Carson Nash is coming! Why don't I care?...4) See above... Grade: C. VERY seg heavy. I don't care how good the last two matches are, you gotta have more than four standard matches on a two-hour show. I just don't like that sort of entertainment before wrestling (you, a member of Wrestling 101? Do go on!) to the extent where it's something like 2.5:1 in non-in-ring stuff. I's just saying is all. Show Results: 1) Deft s. Ninja K 2) [CARNAGE] HardCase s. Brian James 3) Sanford & Son tld. the Dangerettes 4) Coral Avalon, Rook Black & Max Danger d. Wrestling 101 (Avalon pins Garvin; Golden Elbow) Pressure Point 77
