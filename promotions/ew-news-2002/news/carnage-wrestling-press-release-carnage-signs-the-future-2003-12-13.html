Carnage Wrestling Press Release: Carnage Signs The Future

13 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Earlier yesterday afternoon, Carnage Wrestling announced the signing of one of the hottest stars in the independent wrestling scene. Read to find out exactly who this major player is. REDMOND, Washington -- December 11th 2003 -- Earlier this afternoon, the Nintendo Chairman, Satoru Iwata's assistant Kii Nasaki made an announcement that left the independent wrestling scene with it's jaws hanging. Carnage Wrestling signed 'The Future' Ashton Lockheart, World Champion to both the International Wrestling Council and American Championship Wrestling. 'I'm very thrilled to have Ashton, here apart of the Carnage Wrestling roster. There's just an undeniable magic to the kid, pure and simple. When I headed to IWC to give him the contract I had drawn up for him, I stood in amazement backstage as the crowd showed their appreciation for his talents with a seven minute standing ovation. He's one of the best students of the game I've ever come across. As his nickname suggests, Lockheart just might be the future of this industry. He has all the tools to really leave his mark on the business. I must say I can't wait to see him get in a Carnage ring and compete. I'm quite thrilled to see what he'll be capable of in a Carnage Wrestling environment.' said Kii Nasaki. Since his debut in July of last year, the twenty three year old Lockheart has evolved into one the most popular and successful superstars on the independent circuit. His in ring performances have made the industry's most jaded cynics stand up take notice and on some occasions, even applaud. In IWC, Ashton captured the promotion's World Championship for an unprecedented three times and it's Television Championship. Meanwhile, as apart of the ACW roster, Lockheart won the World Championship on two occasions. His exciting and innovative in the ring earned him Match of the Year, Rookie of the Year, and Wrestler of the Year in both promotions. About Nintendo of America, Inc.
