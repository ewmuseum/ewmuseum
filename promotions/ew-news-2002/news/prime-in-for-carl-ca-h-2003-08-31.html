PRIME in for Carl Ca$h?

31 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Carl Ca$h... a name that brings out a number of feelings upon being mentioned. Some people cringe at the superstars out of ring antics, while others marvel at his undeniable talent. Could be in line for a return to wrestling on a TV near you? Carl Ca$h has been out of mainstream wrestling for months now, with most big time promotions refusing to touch the former headliner due to his unenvious reputation for walking out and causing heat backstage. At the twilight time of 6:20am this morning, Ca$h was caught on film meeting a PRIME official in his hometown of Flint Michigan. Does this early morning liason mean that PRIME are indeed looking to sign "Mr Platinum"? It certainly wouldn't be the first time that Taun Pham has signed a controversial superstar to the company, but having already turned down Ca$h's services well over a month ago, it seems unlikely that the top brass would have such a change of heart. While Carl Ca$h might not be arriving in PRIME anytime soon, CorX on the other hand has joined the company. The former FSW and IWA all rounder signed earlier today and could well join up with his colleagues in Ontario for ReVolution this coming Monday. PRIME
