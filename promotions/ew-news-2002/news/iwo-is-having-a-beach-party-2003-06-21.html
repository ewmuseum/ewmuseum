IWO Is Having A Beach Party!

21 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
And guess what? You're invited! Granted, of course, that Mother Nature doesn't flood the show out. Somebody call my momma! Today, the IWO proudly presents the seven Beach Party, once a big drawing event on pay-per-view, now a gala of an indy show! But it seems as if, checking the local weather bulletins, that Beach Party could very well become Rain Party. President Ford has told me personally that he has a few ways to make the event happen no matter the weather, but rain could be a definite factor in the chaos that will be today's show. With the original plan of the card being played outside in the sun and on the beach, it'll be hard to have the wrestlers wrestling on wet canvas'. The idea of a rescheduling was brought up by the IWO board, but a lot of the wrestlers in the federation have commitments from other promotions, and a rescheduling might affect their travel. Whatever the case, IWO officials and staff are planning on going on with the show, and we hope the IWO faithful turn out for our Wet and Wild Spectacular. Great, Snoop Dogg's gonna sue us now. The card as it now stands: [World Title Tournament] Coral Avalon(A!) vs. Schitzo Tod Keith Scott Zimmerman(A!) vs. Greg Allocca Jack Breaker(A!) vs. Trey Vincent(BOB) HardCase(A!) vs. Markus King(LIWA) [Grudge Match] Kory Storm vs. "The Hardcore Homo" Jamal Wilson(aWc) [Fall Two out of Seven] Great Dragon vs. Ninja K Team VIAGRA{High Flyer(fWo) & Tony Davis(A!)} vs. Arcade and Son(A!) The Amazing Casorakia(SJPW) vs. Hurley Cambria(A!) Donnie Daze(A!) vs. Deft(A!) Nathan Daniels(aWc), The Amazing Casoraki(SJPW), and Hardcore Isoceles Trapezoid(UWF) card subject to change. IWO - Your life's not the same without it!
