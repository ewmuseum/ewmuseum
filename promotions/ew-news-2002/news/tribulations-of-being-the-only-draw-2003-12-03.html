Tribulations of Being the ONLY Draw

3 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
J. Leslie Voss explains to ew-news about how tough it is to be the only draw, in his opinion, in the Hardcore Wrestling Organisation. You know, it's tough in this business. The busy road schedule with not seeing any friends or flying home to Australia to visit my mother. Meals from restaurants and roadside diners, not having that loving touch of a home cooked meal. But most of all, the toughest thing about being a hWo superstar? Being the ONLY hWo superstar. Being the only draw in that one horse promotion that can fill the arena or make the crowd actually get involved in the show. And you can call me a bigot and an egotistical pig but you'll find, band geek, that I speak the truth. How many of you tune in each week just to see if I fail? I think you'll find the answer is ALL of you. Those Nielsen points shoot through the roof with the mere sight of me on your television screens because I'm so photogenic and look so darn dashing on the screen. But the real reason is I'm just like reality TV. I've got you all hooked. Everybody loves misery and that's exactly what I bring. To all of those curtain jerkers and jobbers on the roster. Do you know how hard it is to carry a show? For the last four shows I have taken the hWo roster upon my back and carried them over the finish line with me. I have made that show entertaining and keep the viewers clicking back. Step aside, Philbin. Pack your chin up and fuck off, Leno. There's a new entertainer in town and he owns the television ratings. I'm on once a week and I still draw a bigger rating than you to 'Net surfing virgins ever could. Children all over the nation are looking at me and starting to realise that what I am saying is true. That their daddies are good for nothing bums who do nothing but masturbate over the underwear section of the latest K-Mart catalogue. That they can find their real mother by reading Penthouse and see a picture of her for the first time. I am a true draw. I am a true commodity to the wrestling business because I make other people look good. And all I ask is for a little respect. Give me a little respect, a title shot and the hWo will be jetting along into the future with their heads up high because they know that I, J. Leslie Voss, will carry them into the future. And for the four thousandth time, Trevor Echo... I won't sign a written contract. Yours in entertainment, J. Leslie Voss the Hardcore Wrestling Organisation
