Matches Announced For Rumble Rage

20 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As mVw draws closer to their annual January pay per view, Rumble Rage, the show itself is beginning to take shape. Three HUGE title matches have been announced for the show as of today. The currently vacant Tag Team Championships will be on the line when the veterans and three time Tag Team Champions, Niji and Fire, lock it up with Zero and Ozz. These two teams have been at each other since Novembers Annihilation, and their battle has spilled over into the new year to crown the new Tag Team Champions. On the Hardcore Championship side of things, it came as a shocker to everyone backstage when Chairman Escobar released that Paulina Hart would be thrown to the wolves, so to speak, and be forced to wrestle Delta 9. The situation between these two is unbelievable bizarre, to say the least, where in fact the champion (Delta 9) has become the hunter. Going so far as to commit rape, which trial is still pending. There is no doubt in anyone's mind that Paulina has gotten on the bad side of Escobar, and is somewhat of a sacrificial lamb. And of course, it was just a matter of time before the highly speculated L.A. Johnny Stylez/Thomas Greade showdown was confirmed. These two have also been at it since Annihilation, and it's going to be one for the ages when they finally lock it up in a one on one contest with Greade's US Title on the line. A United States Title that was stolen by Stylez a few weeks ago, that Greade has yet to regain possession of. December 8ths Malice saw Thomas Greade savagely beaten by Stylez and it's going to be one for the ages to see if Thomas Greade can retain his title and regain possession of the belt itself at Rumble Rage. With these three matches added to that card, on top of the Rumble itself...Rumble Rage is shaping up to be one for that ages. Be sure not to miss it live on pay per view, January 19th, 2004! Maximum Velocity Wrestling
