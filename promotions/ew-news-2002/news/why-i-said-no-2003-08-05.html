Why I said "No!"

5 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I've been getting calls constantly. My phone won't quit ringing all because one man won't give up on signing me. One man really wants me in his fed, the oWo. I've been getting calls constantly. My phone won't quit ringing all because one man won't give up on signing me. One man really wants me in his fed. Jebediah Jackson, the commissioner of the oWo is trying to get me in his fed but it's just not happening. I can't go over to Australia and wrestle. I thought I'd take opportunity to write a column on here to let all of you know why I can't sign the contract for the oWo. Family. My mum and dad live on the Mississippi and there's no way I wanna go that far away from the River I was brought up on. The River that is my life blood, it's muddy water runs through my veins. It's hard enough being in Ohio let alone Melbourne, Australia. Plus, I can't leave my mum and dad. They've been there the whole time for me. None of this would be possible if it weren't for them. Those two are the best friends I've ever had, the only friends I've ever had. To move that far away from them would be pure torture. Sure, I wanna be the best in the World. There's no doubts about that. And the contract that Jebediah showed me would set me up for life. The money was not an issue. I could work for a year and feed a small African nation. I wanna make my parents proud. I wanna be the best in the world but there's no reason why I have to go so far away from them. I'm as far away from them as I wanna be. I've told Sugar I'm nervous about even going to the East Coast, I don't think I even like being that far away from the Mississippi. Truth beknown, if things were different, I might have taken that offer. I might have gone across the other side of the world to wrestle in Australia. But family keeps me here. And thReat. I love being in thReat. Sure, it's tough being the new kid on the block, trying to cut the grades together. But I'm having the time of my life. I can see myself finishing up my career there. Good luck to the oWo, I'm sure you'll do just fine. But please, quit bothering me! YOU RING ME ONE MORE TIME, JACKSON, I DARE YOU! If you ring me one more time I will be making a trip to Australia, to kick your ass. "No!" means "No!" oz Wrestling organisation
