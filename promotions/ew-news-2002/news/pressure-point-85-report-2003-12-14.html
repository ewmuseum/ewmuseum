Pressure Point 85 Report

14 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
4 Crappy Matches 3 Daniel Phillips 2 Title Belts And a report by KSZ Pressure Point Report Date Aired (Live To Tape): 10.12.03 (8.12.03) Number: 85 Location: Spokane Arena, Washington (state) Posted by Keith Scott Zimmerman Keithentary: It worked this week, because two people debuted, wrestled good matches, and got over. But we can't keep this up every week. We can't have a dumping ground first hour and then all of a sudden pull Lazarus and kick out the jams motherfucker in the second hour. It's not good business. Attention spans are short. Hell, I review ALL of the shows and there's stuff I ignore and pass by. The most important part of our show is between 9:15 and 10:00--when the opening segment is over and we haven't quite gotten to the big hook that should be at the top of Hour 2. (It shouldn't be there, but since Vince books like he's still against Nitro...) Instead, what happened this week is what happens most weeks: it's where the crowd pop went to die. Two sneezes, and a squash. That doesn't draw viewers. And all we had to do was switch #5 with #3 and the show looks a little better, building up to two quality matches at the hooks of the hours that get the new kids over. I'm just saying. Food for thought. Jeff began by telling Patricia how to properly referee, but Max Danger had to poke his big loser ass into the discussion. Anyway, this leads to Max vs. IBJ if he wants the shot at Jeff...LLB's one man fan club showed up with a biscuit...We have hired Alias; he cut the usual first mission statement promo. If we're smart we could get a Alias/Jeff match... 1. Carson Nash beat Bret Halloway. Sneeze Nash won (his first) with the Tombstone. Did I hear the D-Maf chant "You killed the Dude!" at Carson when they were taking Bret away? (DUD) Reed & LLB jibber jabber...Nowell FUHFORWID...Anaconda got ready. 2. The Ringer beat Anaconda. This may be the first time in recorded history someone was shown right before their match and didn't win. Sneeze again that Morg won for Ringer for some reason. Is this April 2002 and no one bothered to tell me? (DUD) It's sucking bad: I got Woodchuck Bill, Chris Chambers, and more Nowell weirdness. 3. Bradley Duncan squashed the Phoenix. Phoenix actually got in a couple of flash-rollups but got the Clothesline From Hell. Leigh was out with Bradley just when you thought this matchup couldn't insult your intelligence anymore. (DUD) Sharc/Alias tonight. Well, finally, something that'll keep me awake. And it only took half the fucking show. Excelsior!...Joe, of course, is now goingto have to fight Chiaki to preserve the honor of his testes. Liz is pretty snippy, which means she's not getting any... 4. Donnie Daze beat Brandon Youngblood by forfeit. He chaired the Hawkeye before their match. And so it continues. (DUD) Max was informing LLB Jr. how to have a great career and piss it away...Liz said SCREW YOU, GRAYSON, YOU'RE FAHHHHHHHHHHHHHRED!. WOW. I guess that's the end of that. And why is my iPod playing "Hate To Say I Told You So"? Probably coincidence...Sharc took out Reed with the SKO and promised to kill Raven Lite once and for all in a stip match on the Christmas Point, at which point he'd accept the trophy. Here's how the match would go if I was booking it (lord knows looking at the first hour I couldn't do worse): Sharc enters. Little Raven enters. Sharc shoots him in the face and we all piss on his corpse. Then we rip him open with a chainsaw. Then we stuff him full of candy. Then we pay people to beat it out of him. Then we light him on fire. THERE'S your RATINGS~!, Reed...Nowell, Woodchuck, Fox... 5. Go-Go Yubari beat Joe Black. Joe, change to the "Hurt" cover! C'mon, do it for ol' Keith. School Girl is using the song of the year, "Battle Without Honor Or Humanity". Chris Rock 3:16 says I think Quentin Tarantino's gonna sue somebody. Anyway, Go-Go beat the shit out of Joe backstage, so here you go. Chiaki Kuriyama starts off with the TAKA springboard plancha and a Owenzuigiri for two. Ooh, straight Yakuza kick out of a hiptoss attempt. Good for her, nice to see something new come out of that spot. Joe's selling like the Paris Hilton sex tape. Joe did the Union Bomb! Only two, though. Bah. Double springboard suicide moonsault! To quote the archbishop Lewis Black, "Hoooooooooooooooly FUCK." Liz, cut the damn check. Chiaki with the Iron Claw but Joe counters out into the Blackbreaker and let's all rest a second or eight. Joe dodges the Space Flying Tiger Drop and teases using a chair. Since when don't you use a chair? Anyway, she gives him the Rios DDT, which if nothing else sends a clear message: use the fucking chair! If Joe wasn't the picture of man he wouldn't've kicked out of that OR the Sicilian Slice (the Kyoto Krunch? DAMN I'M GOOD). Lionsault gets her two, Joe gets the Anderson spinebuster. It's a shame we're like giving this away. Fake heart attack cradle for Joe, but it's curtains soon after as she turns from the Iron Claw into a URAKEN~! for the duke. Git all dis, as they say. In the hood. Where I'm from. (****) Chris [hearts] Go-Go...Someone beat LLB Jr. Sorry, if I'd done it you would've seen and heard it was me...Youngblood got back at Donnie...IBJ open challenge next week,which is a shame since I went out there and did the same for the B title for the benefit of all you who don't watch Whatever...LLB vs. Fake LLB. I'm sorry this is the WWF in 1994 and no one told me. I know there was some confusion on that earlier... 6. Alias beat Sharc. Alias is rolling early, which makes me happy as I now no longer have the most signature moves. Half crab, the headbutt, the Hammer Jammer KILL THE FOOL! Sharc took all that but pulled off the SKO for a near fall. Levine provided a distraction and Garner won with the damn Royal Treatment. Note to self: 1) immolate 2) defenestrate 3) watch bear rape corpse. (**½) MATCH OF THE NIGHT: 5. BEST SEGMENT: Sharc/Alias, which was face/face without being cowardly and ball-less. FUNNIEST SEGMENT: Nothing was intentionally funny this week. Unless you count the LLBs. WRESTLER OF THE NIGHT: Got to give it to the winning newbies. WRESTLER WITH THE MOST MOMENTUM: See the last. HOTTEST FUED: None yet. Let's see next week. UNDERPERFORMER OF THE NIGHT: I wish we'd won a title match tonight. FLATTEST SEGMENT OF THE NIGHT: Anything with LLB or Nowell. SPOT OF THE NIGHT: Double springboard suicide moonsault! LINE OF THE NIGHT: "Why don't you grab a noose and say hi to your mom for me?"--Jeff, to Max
