Rock Starting Revealed In PRIME

12 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
That's right, Rock Startling has signed a multi-million dollar deal with PRIME. We've got the scoop... Rock Startling is considered by many, to be one of the most recognised names in the business. After nearly two weeks of negotiations, he and Taun Pham finally ironed out all the details to his PRIME contract and Startling signed on the dotted line around noon today EST. "Of course I'm glad we've got him." Pham beamed, while addressing journalists. "Rock Startling is an extremely talented and decorated individual, I'm certain he will be very successful PRIME. Expect to see him on TV in the near future." No sooner had this deal been struck, but the PRIME marketing machine was already churning out official Rock Startling jerseys and tees. Startling has had a career with several highs and lows, plus he has come out of retirement once, even at his young age. He has also had his share of personal problems to deal with, but Pham was quick to dismiss any talk of his personal life as it stands now. We'll all have to wait to discover what Startlings current situation is and where his intentions lie in PRIME. PRIME
