DWA: Dead Men Walking PPV Results

2 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dead Men Walking set a record setting pay per view viewership for the DWA. In it, the FoW took on Intoxicated Justice and Shockwave...LeStatt Knight takes on Jeff Wylde in a Last Man Standing match...a 30 Person Royal Rumble...and Typhoid Mary defends her title against Payne. June 29th 2003.... The Atlanta Georgia dome bore witness to the greatest PPV in the Dragon Wrestling Associations illustrious history. In what many are calling the year’s greatest matches we saw titles change hands, Champions fall to darkness and men become heroes. In the weeks leading up to this night of rampaging pain and blood the DWA felt a power struggle between Mason Stone and Jordan Brannigan come to an end at Hostile Take over thanks to the help of Gideon Nazareth. Once in power he made many changes, one being to place Gideon in the position of Commissioner and Ronnie Johnson as commentator. Else where a feud developed between The members of the Fow and the newly returned Shockwave. But Shockwave was soon joined in his battle with the hero of the DWA, Donnie Darko. But while others were joining in conflict, another pair were joining in another manner. It was soon determined that the Unified World champion, Typhoid Mary, And Lestatt Knight were each seeking the other in a new kind of camaraderie. But while each looked to the other for support, each had to deal with a faction of the other’s demons. The Revolution, consisting of Jeff Wylde and Payne were gunning for Lestatt Knight and Typhoid Mary respectfully. All of this resulting in numerous attacks and out of the ring battles through out the month. Through out all of this chaos and strife there still awaited the fact of the 30 man Royal Rumble. A battle above all battles who’s winner would decide who would go on to face the World champion at Testament, the next Pay Per view in July. At the Showdown before Dead Men Walking Jordan Brannigan announced his list of the entries for the rumble and their order. Most notably were the two first fighters chosen to enter. First, by Commissioner Gideon’s request, Grovel. And second, a man that would already be competing in a last man standing match, Lestatt Knight. The list continued on from there with a few surprises and a few new faces. One name among the list caught many off guard, that being Ronnie Johnson. Not being a wrestler, many wondered why he would even want to fight in the Royal Rumble.. If he had a choice in the matter that is. But With the promise of his old position being returned to him upon his victory, Johnson was more than game. And so with the stage set and the combatants ready Dead Men Walking 2003 and began. At the start of the night Jordan Brannigan came out and drew the crowds attention to the ceiling above. There, hanging above the ring was a Cell. With a smile he announced that the night’s Main event pitting World champion Typhoid Mary against Payne would now be a Hell in the Cell match. In The first match of the night Intoxicated Justice (Derek Cantina and Donnie Darko) along side Shockwave would take on The F.O.W. (John Reed, Brad Keidis, Drastic) in a 6 man elimination tag match. The match began and in no time it was obvious that this was going to be a hellacious match. However the first man eliminated was Derek Cantina via a well timed roll up pin by John Reed. With a 3 on 2 handicap, things did not look good for Darko and Shockwave. Later on after more heated back and forth battle it looked as if The F.O.W. Would take another victory as Drastic took Shockwave up to the Top turnbuckle, but with a burst of energy Shockwave delivered a few well placed rights and then took Drastic down with a Top rope whiplash in the center of the ring to get a pin. The odds were now once again by all accounts even, but for how much longer? The battle continued in the ring and out as the two teams warred with each other. Soon Darko locked Reed in the “fortress of solitude” and forced the man to tap out. But just as things were looking up for the team of Darko and Shockwave, Drastic and Reed garnered the referee, Karen Young’s, Attention. Thus giving Keidis the chance to nail Shockwave with a hidden chair. After tossing away the evidence Keidis took the pin and got the 3 count. Now the odds were once again relatively even.. Donnie Darko fought on though, as only the hero of the DWA could and managed to fight out of a near pin. But the FOW was not finished yet, Reed and Drastic soon had Karen Young knocked out, thus causing Darko’s tap out victory over Keidis to go unrewarded. Darko soon sees Young's condition and goes to revive her. As he does this Keidis rolls out of the ring to retrieve his velocity title and rolls back in with the intent of nailing Darko, but from the entranceway Zula Shandor comes running down and rolls into the ring, ducking a title blow from Keidis she nails him with a thrust kick knocking the title into Keidis’s face. Keidis staggers back into Darko and is caught by a left handed “Savior” to Keidis. Darko goes for the pin and the now revived Karen Young counts the 1,2,3 thus winning the match for the side of Intoxicated Justice and Shockwave. Else where in the back locker rooms, we witnessed a brief union of two warriors on the side of right in the battle for the DWA. Lestatt Knight and Mary were sharing a few moments alone before Knight’s match with Jeff Wylde. In an uncommon display of affection, Typhoid Mary gave Knight a good luck kiss. This moment that should have been frozen in time was instead shattered by the appearance of Faust, a.k.a. Damien Black. Faust appeared with an arrogance beffiting a man of his attitude. Mary was on the verge of throttling him, but some how held back, heated words were exchanged instead of fists. The end result being Mary telling Faust to go away and slamming the door on his face. Faust only smiled and left a single rose on the door step. A moment later an unknown figure came to the door, picked up the rose, and then ripped it to shreds and walked away. The next match to take place was a Last man standing match between Lestatt Knight and Jeff Wylde. The match began with utter brutality being displayed by both men, and it only got worse from there. Both men were soon on the ends of near ten counts, but both fought on. It was only a matter of time till the battle waged it self out of the ring and past the barriers. They battled amongst the crowd, even using the environment around them to their advantage. Jeff wylde, taking a beer from one fan proceeded to use it to blind Knight as he sprayed it in his eyes. The two men soon found them selves in the back hallways of the stadium and here the battle got even more vicious. Many chair shots and Garbage can smashes later the two men found themselves in a maintenance closet where Knight proceeded to use the equipment there to attempt to mop up Wylde... Literally. By shoving a mop in Wylde’s face Knight picked up the advantage, however briefly. For soon Wylde takes the advantage and hurls Knight into a stack of steel chairs which causes an avalanche that nearly keeps Knight down for the ten count. But Knight carries on as to does the horror. Both men continue to battle on. There fight eventually taking them into a Bar located in the Georgia Dome. People soon scattered as the two combatants took each other and the room apart. Soon enough both men crash through the exit of the bar and to the outside, but this is not an improvement for with a deadly spear Knight smashes Wylde through a large window over looking the parking-lot and both men crash there. By the time the Ref arrived on the scene the two men were once again trading blows. With many dented cars and smashed windows later the two battered men find them selves on top of a bus. After a few more traded blows Knight German suplexes Wylde off of the Bus and into the roof of a car, collapsing the roof with the impact. Both men were down till the 9 count but as Knight stood up completely, Wylde collapsed back down and Knight picked up the hard fought for win. Finally it was time for the 30 man royal rumble. Simple rules, 30 men, after the first two they are let in one by one in two minuet intervals, to loose you have to be thrown over the top rope and have both feet touch the ground. Simple no?... Tell that to the 30 men that fought to hell and back during the match. o start it out we saw Grovel come out, followed by Lestatt Knight’s introduction, but Knight did not show. The crowd was growing restless, Gideon Nazareth came out to declare Knight having only a minuet to come out till he was eliminated from the rumble. The count ticked down, with the crowd going crazy, trying to bring Lestatt out. But as the time got to 30 seconds the fans died down in despair. But then with 10 seconds left on the clock “O Fortuna” began to blare across the arena, and a battered, but determined Knight, stepped out. The match began. An excellent display of skill began as the two squared off. With 2 minuet up EZ target arrived and with two more Clicks of the 2 minuet counter we had two more fighters. The last being NEX, who within a short time was eliminated by Grovel. With another man eliminated and another tick on the two minuet clock Faust came to the ring much to the hatred of the crowd. He represented the first of the Jordan Brannigan’s “surprises”. Perhaps knowing Typhoid Mary’s hatred for Faust, Knight went head on at him and the two battled viciously. They were soon both on the outside. Faust using the tricks that he knew best began to batter Knight with a pair of brass knuckles. But as he was doing this someone came from behind and nailed Faust with a steel chair. Before anyone could catch sight of who did it, they were gone. With four more ticks from the 2 minuet clock we saw the entrance of Zula shandor, who immediately went for Brad Keidis who had already entered the rumble. The two were instantly at each other’s throats. Zula wanting to settle the score between her and Keidis. After 5 ticks of the two minuet clock and many elimination's later we saw the arrival of Impact, who has not been seen in the DWA since he was beaten and told to never come back by the then body guard of Jordan Brannigan, Raven. But within a few seconds he is eliminated by Grovel. Two more ticks on the two minuet timer go by and we then saw the return of Hellraiser, a former American champion. HellRaiser goes straight for Ninja X, who proceeded him into the rumble. With a choke slam over the top rope Hellraiser eliminates Ninja X and then eliminates him self as he continues on after Ninja X. After three more ticks on the 2 minuet clock Ashura arrived on the scene and in a kind of sneak attack eliminated Jeff Wylde, much to his surprise and anger. With smirk on her lips she turned away from the raging Jeff Wylde only to be eliminated my Lestatt Knight who simply threw her over the top rope. As the next combatant, Brad Clinton, arrived; Ashura and Wylde had a brief fight on the outside resulting in a victory for Ashura who sauntered by Clinton as he continued on towards the ring. Two more clicks on the clock and we were greeted with the return of Meishi to the DWA, but it was soon made public that she was only going to be back for Dead Men Walking, but the fan reaction left no doubt that she was sorely missed. After the excitement of Meishi’s return it was noticed that the count down clock was no longer starting at 2 minuets, but instead at one. It seemed that Jordan Brannigan had decided that the speed up would make things more interesting, and so it did. With 2 more clicks on the now 1 minuet clock we saw the entrance of Ronnie Johnson. And it seemed that Ronnie was in this battle not alone.. But with else...although slightly intoxicated, they always sought Justice. Donnie Darko and Derek Cantina soon made it obvious that they had Ronnie’s back and were going to see him as the DWA’s Commissioner once more. With 2 more clicks on the 1 minuet clock we saw the arrival of a new fighter in the DWA, the masked man known as Misfit began to make his way to the ring when he passed by Lupus Feral, another new comer to the DWA who was recently eliminated. Lupus seemed to dismiss Misfit and began to walk by when suddenly Misfit grabbed hold of Lupus and launched him off the edge of the ramp way before heading into the ring. A few more elimination's and then one more tick of the clock went by and we were greeted with the return of Complicated. In a near elimination Drastic decided to release Ronnie instead of eliminating him, possibly in gratitude for Johnson bringing him into the DWA. But it was all for not as Grovel nailed both men with a close line sending them both over. By then Cemetery Gates arrived on the scene. But he, Derek Cantina and Misfit are eliminated prior to the final and 30th entrant.. Gideon Nazareth. Gideon goes directly in and manages to nail both Complicated and Grovel sending both over the top rope. But Grovel manages to land on the ring apron and roll back in, Complicated is not so luck. But he soon has his revenge as he distracts Gideon allowing Grovel to shove him over the top rope, thus eliminating him. The two were soon at it on the out side. Finally it was down to Lestatt Knight and Grovel, two men who had been in it since the beginning of the rumble, now all they had to do was eliminate just one last person. The two battle for advantage, at the last moment Knight battles back and manages to send Grovel over the top rope, winning the 30 man dead men walking rumble and securing his spot for the world title match at Testament It was finally time for the Main Event. World title Hell in the Cell, pitting Unified world champion Typhoid Mary against the man known as Payne. The match began as many of the other matches in this night of hell and blood had.. With sheer brutality. The match is a constant back and forth battle between the two, neither giving an inch and neither willing to give any quarter. The ring, the cage, chairs, tables, ring posts, all are used and all are soon coated with splashes of the combatants blood. There is no describing what kind of hell each fighter is determined to put the other through. After a good 20 minuets into the match, a match filled with horrifying attacks on both sides, Typhoid Mary seems to gain a brief advantage and lands her recent partner and Payne’s long time nemesis’s finisher on him “Knightfall” right through a table. This leaves both fighters out of it. The two are soon both up and the bloody battle continues. With tosses into the ring posts, and the face first smashes into the cell walls, this match continues to live up to its name. The two continue to battle back and forth but soon enough Payne’s size and strength win out and he gets control and sends Mary back into the ring and delivers a Power slam and follows it up with the first pin attempt of the night, it is a no go at a 2 count. This does not deter Payne who delivers a Brain buster and goes for another pin, again just the 2 count. Now it is Mary who comes out with a barrage of attacks that give her brief control, enough of a control to deliver a pulling Pile driver and then a pin attempt of her own, but she two only gets a 2 count as Payne gets his shoulder up. This continues on for two more back and forth pin attempts before the match progresses back into its struggle for dominance as the two battle back and forth once more in the hell that is the cage entrapped ring. Soon both are on the outside again fight for all they are worth. Mary manages to get a moment to reach under the ring and retrieves some kerosene and a lighter, these along with a chair help to form one of the most dangerous weapons in wrestling, a flaming chair, and this is just what Mary does. As payne gets back to his feet Mary delivers a hard blow with the flaming chair which results in a seemingly near explosion as it collides with Payne’s face sending him flying back into the door of the cell. The ref proceeded to put out the fire with an extinguisher from under the ring. Mary soon uses the Extinguisher her self, but not in the way it was intended. She delivers a jarring blow to Payne which results in the door of the cell going off its hinges and both combatants going to the out side. Mary has the advantage on the out side for a while but soon it is a tug of war again between the two gladiators. Payne manages to knock Mary to the ground, giving him time to climb the cell, Mary is soon in pursuit. The two stalk each other and then collide in battle once more, and amidst the chaos Mary slaps on the “Straight Jacket” And Payne, not being able to handle it, taps out, but the tap out does not count, since it is out side of the ring. The two are soon back on their feet, Mary has the advantage briefly but a quick catch by Payne sends the advantage back in his direction and after a few set up moves he delivers “A Lesson in Payne” and goes for the pin, he gets the three count but once again, the match can not be won on top of the cage but in the ring. A close line from Payne sends Mary off the cage and through the announcer’s table. Payne drags her back to the ring and delivers a modified flap jack to Mary and goes for a pin. Only a 2 count, somehow Mary gets her shoulder up. Payne then tosses her face first into the cell wall, he goes for the pin again, still only a two count, Payne is furious. With an abundant amount of frustration he slaps on a Crippler Crossface in the center of the ring. Mary did not even have the energy to cry out. Calling out for The ref to check her, The referee checks on Mary and it is quite obvious that she is unconscious. As the Referee is announcing Payne as the new Unified champion, Payne interrupts him and quickly snatches the title and the mic from the referee and in cold yet triumphant words he cried out. “Not Payne…my name is Alex Grey…and I am your World Heavyweight Champion!”. And with those words the most horrific Pay Per view in the history of the DWA came to a close. DWA: Dragon Wrestling Association
