Weekly Indy Round-Up

3 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Weekly report on the Independant Wrestling scene. Weekly Indy Round-Up by Slippery Pete (courtesy of the Fan Site) [detail withheld] Yeah, I took time off. So sue me! It's not like much was around to report anyhow. Beyond the Booze: As many of you saw on Fox Sports Net's "Beyond the Glory", they devoted the show to some of the dark stories of professional wrestling. One segment included current EMWC Television champion, Tommy Stephens, and his problem with alcohol. Appearantly, it isn't an angle - he really does have a problem and has seeked treatment for it in the past. Many are saying that his match at Redemption could be the final straw for Stephens - if he doesn't start conducting himself in a serious and sober manner, he could get the ax. Shame. Some other stories shown are how some various wrestler's physical health has taken a toll due to the high impact lifestyle they lead in the ring. And of, course, they showed the mandatory backyard idiots smashing lightbulb tubes over each other's heads. Why does the mass media always have to associate that filth with the tro pro's? Radio Highjinks: GLCW & EMWC stars Juan Vasquez and Luke Kinsey made some stops on the LA radio dial, visiting the Sam Rubin show on 97.1 TALKi in the midday on Friday. It was an... odd interview, to say the least from what listeners in the LA area reported. Vasquez seemed uninterested, and Kinsey kept interrupting Rubin and insulting callers. However, they did manage to promote the upcoming EMWC PPV, Redemption. The duo then jumped to KROQ's "Kevin & Bean" show, where they met Tommy Stephens - who actually wasn't boozed up. They kind of settled down, though, so it was basically a plug-fest. More on the Dial: FSW star, Ricard, made an appearance on the syndicated "The Allan Handelman Show", where he plugged the upcoming FSW Day of Reckoning show. Not a bad interview, but Handelman kept acting weird and Ricard... well, seemed to have every other word bleeped. Wreslting TV: I've heard reports that independent investors would like to create a 24-hour wrestling channel. Now considering the number of the feds with TV shows AND the P*WIN, BATTLE*DREAm's, Redshoe Pro, and even Chock Full's own CFF federations that could tape their programs on handheld digital camera, this channel would have lots of programming. Just got to get the thing up. And that's all that I'm reporting. I've got some important sport bike shopping to do!
