The wrestling business

18 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Nothing is what is seems anymore, esse. The world is the world, and the world is a sad, sad place. Me, I'm in a funny mood. Let me tell you about everything hommes... it's all on the table tonight. Your fWo Internet Champion, Spyder Hello Internet readers, For some reason esse, they expect me, Spyder, to write things about how you're all my bitches and my slaves. Maybe I'm getting old, but I don't have the energy for it anymore. I don't care about any of you really, so why would I want you as my bitches? The only bitches I keep around are my two labs back in San Quivera and the multitude of women I sleep with every week, which of course includes your mother and your sister. That's life. I'm in a funny mood, hommes, funny because it's summer and it's hotter now. I'm in the fWo, and it has always been my opinion that the promotion wasn't worth much until Spyder showed up within it. That's true too, you know. Ratings are up, buy rates are up, and a lot of people are walking around esse, walking around richer than they were before. A lot has changed in the business. It isn't the wrestling business like a lot of you know it, it is the wrestling business like it is going to be for everyone in the future. I think about it esse, and a lot of thoughts come into my head. Years ago I was wrestling down in Mexico. Those were good times. Guys like the Spanish Dragon, El Flamito, Jesus Martinez, Exploder, even one guy who is in the fWo today, Temerario. I was the center jewel in a lot of these promotions. This isn't the bad guy talking esse, this is true. I was the man they came to see. Now I'm an aging wrestler, up in America, and trying to once again lead a promotion out of the toilet. But sometimes it is tough. A few shows ago I was scheduled to wrestle Scott Collins. I canceled the match because of all of you. I go onto the internet to check on all of you little boys and girls, and I don't see people talking about one of the hardest workers in the business. Me. I see people complaining about the wrestling industry being in a slump, people talking crap about each other, little fat middle-aged men perving on little girls, but no comments on Spyder. There is a Wrestleonline forum, and it's time to call an ace and ace and a spade a spade... this forum sucks for wrestling. You have a couple of loyalists who kiss each others ass but don't give credit to the hard work other people are doing. I watch some pathetic loser post up show reports for the fWo material you see on TV. Lots of internet sites have this. The fWo show reports are posted on Classics, Meltzer's site and other places. But on this Wrestleonline forum you have people who are so busy kissing each other's ass that they ignore good material elsewhere. I noticed this guy finally gave up on them and stopped posting. Like a plant with no water, this area will eventually shrivel away like the nothing that it is. But things aren't much better elsewhere, esse. A few shows ago, when I canceled my match with Collins, I talked about some of the cancers in the fWo locker room. People like Aimz, Brand Frontier, Eli Flair, Poison Ivy, XXXStasy and Lance Knight. Are these the best the promotion has to offer? No, of course not. And esse, that wasn't a fake shoot or an angle. I really don't like these people. Most of the guys in the locker room don't. Because hommes, in the wrestling business you have people that put themselves on a pedestal above everyone else. They try to dominate the wrestling scene. I laugh when I see Voran Xias pull one over on management... that pale gringo doesn't have any talent in his body but he pulled one over on the fWo management, Brand Frontier and Lance Knight. Who has the belt today hommes? Not the fWo's version of the clique, and that's what matters in the end. I'm going to tell you a story hommes, a story about life, time, and what happens to those who try and control everything. Una vez sobre una época había una serpiente que deseó cruzar un camino ocupado. La serpiente tenía su meta pero sabía que él era demasiado lento conseguir allí. La serpiente llamó tan abajo del águila y pidió que él le ayudara a cruzar el camino. ¿El No. dicho del águila, porque cómo podría él confíe en la serpiente? Pero la serpiente era muy persuasiva y convenció eventual el águila que él fuera un buen individuo que acaba de necesitar ayuda. El águila tomó tan la serpiente y lo llevó al otro lado. La serpiente entonces mordió el águila, que pone morir del veneno como la serpiente preparada al devour él. El águila fue sorprendida, y recordó la serpiente sobre su promesa. La serpiente dijo que él había significado su promesa, pero que él seguía siendo una serpiente, y una serpiente es una serpiente. Those people? The fWo's clique? Those are the snakes. But I'm no eagle... I'm no fool who trusts the snake. I'm the spider, and the Spyder always wins in the end. Until next time, esse, Spyder The Spyder's home
