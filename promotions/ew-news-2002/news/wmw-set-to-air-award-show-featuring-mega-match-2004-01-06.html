WMW Set to Air Award Show Featuring Mega-Match

6 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WMW is planning an award show like no-other. If a 35-man title match isn't your thing, this probably isn't the story for you. Like many other organizations across the world, WMW is set to acknowledge the beginning of a new year, and they plan on kicking it off with a bang. Maybe not just a bang. Maybe an explosion. After a year that had its ups and downs, WMW has come up with a match idea like no other. During the program in which they plan on airing their end of the year awards, they also have planned a match in which every superstar on the WMW roster takes place in a battle for the federation's three major single's titles. In what will be a 35-man match, featuring returns from some of the federation's past greats as well as some debuts from the younger competitors just hired, the federation's Great Lakes, Midwest, and Ace Superior/Shoot Championships will all be on the line for any superstar to win. Pinfalls, submissions, knockouts and more will certainly light up the awards ceremony which is being held this Saturday, January 10th, 2004. Forget "Wrestler of the Year." This match has everyone raving, so it might just be one you don't want to miss. Wrestling Midwest
