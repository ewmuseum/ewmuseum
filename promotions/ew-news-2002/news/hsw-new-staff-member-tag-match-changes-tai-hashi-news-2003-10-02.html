HSW: New Staff member; Tag Match Changes; Tai Hashi news

2 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HSW Signs a new Staff member as well as parts ways with two superstars! Just recently HSW President Joey Matthew was contacted by a man named Garrett Kett about joining up with HSW's creative team. Kett's resumé was impressive enough that Joey signed him on the spot. Joey said in response: "Garrett seems to be a real bright guy and I believe with him added onto the Headstrong Wrestling team, things will definately turn out better. Day by day improvements are being made and this is yet another example of how HSW is improving into something big." In other news, The Tag Team match that pitted Mjolnir and Shane Taylor vs Nocturnal Supremacy made up of Meleza Draven and Rio Carmichael has been changed to a Handicap match because Matthew and Taylor came to an agreement that Taylor could leave the fed. Taylor asked Matthew if he could be released reportedly and Matthew had nothing against that. It will now be Nocturnal Supremacy vs Mjolnir in a first round match up in the Tag Team Title Tournament. Tai Hashi will be wrestling his last match in Headstrong Wrestling this Saturday when he faces newcomer Claymore. Tai and Matthew have also made an agreement to end that contract by Saturday. HSW wishes the best to both men. Headstrong Wrestling
