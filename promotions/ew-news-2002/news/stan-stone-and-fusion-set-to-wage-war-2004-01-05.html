Stan Stone and Fusion Set To Wage War

5 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Animosity Wrestling's Stan Stone is set to defend his Extreme championship against Fusion at Dark Horizons following vicious aasaults from each. With the Extreme Title on the line, Fusion and Stan Stone are set to end their feud once and for all. Will Stan Stone accept Fusion's challenge to a cage match? Will more violence be unleashed by the two as we draw closer to January 25th? Tune in weekly to Tantrum for the latest updates! Animosity Wrestling
