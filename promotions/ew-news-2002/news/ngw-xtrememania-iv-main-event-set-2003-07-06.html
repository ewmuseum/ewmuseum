[NGW] Xtrememania IV Main Event Set.

6 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After weeks of waiting the finals of the NGW World Title Tourny which is the main event of Xtrememania IV has been set. At the Night of Kings PPV on June 13th John Hitchin started a 8 man NGW World Heavyweight Title Tourny to crown a new champion after the title was vacated by NGW legend Time Bomb apron his retirement. Now finally after Storm this week we have the 2 men that will face off in the finals of this Tourny at Xtrememania IV.....Scot King and Matt Douglass. In the first round Scott King defeated NGW veteran Ty Sanders with his finisher ‘Down Under Thunder’. But in his second round match was not as easy. Scott King faced off against NGW rookie Jerome 'Blayze' Jordan. Jordan had knocked King out with a DDT when Rob Zone who was screwed out of his match earlier in the night and was furious came to the ring. He got into the ring and stared at Jordan and then tossed him the chair. The referee got up and saw the chair in Jordan's hands and King out cold and DQ'ed Jordan giving Scott King the win and the spot in the finals at Xtrememania. Matt Douglass's road to the finals was also a interesting one. In his first round match Matt Douglass faced off against the unluckiest man in NGW Frankie the Cameraman. This man is not a qualified wrestler but seems to find himself in some horrible matches. He held the Xtreme Championship for a short time after Dallas Reeves vacated him but was quickly lost after being destroyed by James Jackson. This match was no different as Matt Douglass destroyed Frankie and dominated the entire match except when Scott King came out and distracted Matt and Frankie pulled out a 2 count, but after that Douglass connected with a powerbomb and frog splash to get the win. In Douglass's second round match he faced off against NGW newcomer Rob Zone in a match that would cause great controversy and havoc later in the night. This was a great match but it was all ruined when The Outlaw Nation made their second NGW appearance and attacked Matt Douglass which gave him the win in the match by DQ. Now their is more between these men then just the World Title. These two men truly hate each other and the rage in this match will be off the charts at Xtrememania IV in a main event that should be one to remember. With 2 weeks before Xtrememania IV in Toronto Canada these two men will certainly begin playing the mind games to try and gain an advantage before the match. But rumors from within NGW say that Scottywood is planning on adding another twist to this match. What that is we aren't sure yet but knowing Scottywood it will be something huge. Until then we will just wait and see what happens between these two on Storm next week. They will be facing off against each other in a Tag Team match and will surly be just a sample of chaos we will see on July 20th at Xtrememania IV. Next Generation Wrestling
