EWC's Dark Wolf on Media Tour

7 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With just one today before Monday Night Slaughter and Dark Wolf has been on the media circuit causing a storm of rumours about the show tomorrow. Dark Wolf told an interviewer on Friday night that he returned to England to 'capture the hearts and minds of the public, not just the championship', he also told his opponent for tomorrow, Ice, that he would send him back to America in a body bag. Ice, who is a former English Heavyweight Champion as well, is the smaller of the two and gives up around 100lbs. Wolf, who failed a drugs test last year will always be the more agressive wrestler in the contest, but depending on his mood Ice has a lot of tricks up his sleave. Wolf, who is the lifetime English Trilogy Champion is scheduled to take on Ice in one of the Semi Finals for the English Heavyweight Championship, while Ryan Matthews takes on Aaron Rain.
