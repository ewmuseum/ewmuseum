DWA: Leader of the FoW Speaks Out After Winning Tag Titles

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
At Hostile Takeover, the stable of the Future of Wrestling made a huge impact in the DWA. Brad Keidis captured the TV Championship, while John Reed and Drastic won the Tag Team Championship. After the ppv was over, Luke Dillard sat down with leader of the FoW, John Reed. Luke Dillard stands with newly crowned Tag Team Champion, John Reed. John has changed into his street clothes of jean shorts and an FoW t-shirt. He holds his title belt over his shoulder and has a smile on his face as ole' L.D. turns to begin the interview. Dillard - First off John, let me congratulate you and your buddies for your victories here tonight. John - Thanks, L.D. Dillard - Even though Drastic's small package had a small variation thrown into it. John - Listen man. A win is a win. So what? Dillard - Right. Sorry. So, how does it feel to have de-throned Intoxicated Justice, so to speak, from their near two-month reign as World Tag Team Champions? John - How do you think it feels?! Damn good! It's like the saying goes. "We came. We fought. We won." Dillard - What exactly do you mean by that? John - We did just what we said we were going to do. Brad won the Television Title, and Drastic and myself beat the so-called "hero" and the drunk for the World Tag Team Titles. IT just proves that the Future of Wrestling truely does lay within us. We're tired of all the old guys getting the opportunities. It's time for some youth to step into the light and bask in the glory. Dillard - With all due respect, John, you yourself are approaching thirty, aren't you? This catches Reed of gaurd, but he quickly comes up with a cover. John - Uh....um....there had to be a veteran of the sport there to guide these young talents in their rise to the top. Let's just leave it at that....you're beginning to step across that thin line between being an interviewer and an enemy. Dillard - Okay....sure....whatever you say. Anyway, I must get your comments on the evnets that we witnessed in the main event. What are your thoughts on Jordan Brannigan's victory? John - He won. So what? I could care less who has control. Both Mason and Jordan ignored us down in the FoW when we needed the attention the most. I'm sure he'll do a better job than Mason ever did, but I'm never going to forgive him for what he did to me and my buddies. Dillard laughs a bit before his next comment. Dillard - Jesus...you sound like a little girl..."I need attention!" "I'm such a pu--" John - You're really pushin' it pal! I wouldn't finish that sentence. John has reached up and has his hand set on Dillard's shoulder as he begins smiling. Dillard - Right...So now that the FoW holds two of the four championships, where will you guys head next? John - Oh I don't know, L.D. You'll just have to wait and find out. Dillard - I guess so. Well John thanks. Just one final question before we go... John - Shoot. Dillard - Is it true that this is you're longest winning streak in almost two years, now standing at 2? A look of anger comes across Reed's face as he turns. John - This interview is over. Dillard - Could you please just answer the question? Without warning, John spins around and clocks Dillard in the mouth with a right hand, barely moving the title belt hanging over his shoulder. He watches as Dillard begins holding his chin while on the ground. John - That good enough? John laughs and turns, walking away. DWA: Dragon Wrestling Association
