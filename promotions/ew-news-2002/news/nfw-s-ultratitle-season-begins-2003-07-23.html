NFW's ULTRATITLE Season begins!

23 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In week one of the 'new' NFW professional sports league format, Hornet and Eli Flair were among the winners, while 'XXX' Sean Stevens narrowly lost to Joey Melton. In week one of the professional sports league format, Hornet and Eli Flair were among the winners, while 'XXX' Sean Stevens narrowly loses to Joey Melton. More importantly, because of Stevens' loss to Melton in the "interconfernce" match - New Frontier will take a road trip to North Charleston, South Carolina for the next two weeks as the South Conference has taken homefield advantage. In other matches, Evan Aho narrowly escaped injury at the hands of 'Lord of Darkness' Damien Priest to win, Shane Southern continued in his NFW winning ways in a nailbiter over Lawrence Stanley and also two triple threat matches came down to the wire awarding two wrestlers 5 points extra for the victory. The Ultratitle will be competed for over a 20 week long professional sportslike atmosphere, with the winner being awarded the infamous Ultratitle trophy and a wrestling legacy. The ESEN Sports.com website (http://fwrestling.com/host/nfw/ESENSports/ESENSports.com), while not fully functional can help anyone looking to learn more about the roster and the upcoming week's schedule.
