hWo Desolation Results

23 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A very Dynamic night! hWo’s “Fuck Old School” tour continued on last night, as we witnessed what many are calling a groundbreaking show for the Hardcore Wrestling Organization. Desolation Two had many highlights from Dynamo’s debut as an hWo superstar, to the Branson Blair/Tommy Kane I quit match. Majik Seven Vs Phate - This was the opening match on the night. Not a bad hardcore match and a good way to open the show. Some nice spots, and lots of hardcore action. The winner of this match Majik Seven via pinfall. Heaven and Hell Vs Semore Smoke and Lucas Field - Heaven and Hell’s in-ring debut in hWo also got a lot of hype from around the wrestling world and the former 21W superstars did not disappoint. Heaven and Hell were looking for revenge, however on this night they would not get their revenge. The winners of this match Semore Smoke and Lucas via pinfall. Frost Vs Edo Jakosben - Last week Big Little Italy had gotten the best of Frost, and this week Frost was looking to get the better of Big Little Italy. However Big Little Italy cost Frost his match this week against Edo Jakosben. The winner of this match Edo Jakosben via pinfall Tommy Kane Vs Branson Blair - In this teacher vs. student match, many were giving Tommy Kane a huge advantage. However much like last week, Branson Blair would pull another upset. After a brutal match of back and forth hardcore action, Branson Blair was able to make Tommy Kane say “I QUIT!” Omega Vs Dynamo - The Main Event of the evening did not disappoint. Many of the fans were looking for fWo superstar and now hWo superstar as well Dynamo to win, however Omega picked up the win. There is no doubt that this match is a candidate for match of the year as both Omega and Dynamo put 110 percent into the match. Winner of this match Omega via pinfall. -Highlights from the show- 1) Mystery Wrestler talking with Trevor Norman, Norman promises a Dynamic Night 2) Anima still looking to find Trev Echo as commanded by Norman, Anima also gives Trevor information on why hWo was sold by Echo. 3) Debut segments from “The Fan” Sean Akins 4) Branson Blair/Omega feud continued to develop as Branson wrote Omega a letter to get him to love and forgive. 5) Lucas Fields interrupts Heaven and Hell’s debut, which leads to Heaven and Hell vs. Semore Smoke and Lucas Field 6) Frost announces he will get revenge on Big Little Italy 7) Omega is insulted by Branson Blair’s letter 8) Big Little Italy attacks Frost once again, and costing him a match against Edo Jakosben 9) Omega confronts Branson Blair before the main event and lets him know that “God is going to have to forgive me for what is to come.” 10) Dynamo is revealed as Omega’s opponent for the main event 11) The show ends with Sean Akins stumbling around the backstage bloody and in pain Desolation Two
