DSW Parent Company Changes Name

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Darkside Wrestling's parent company Xtreme Asylum Entertainment has changed it's name after five years. los angeles, ca. thur. june 26, 2003 the parent company of darkside wrestling, xtreme asylum entertainment has announced that effective immedetly their name would be changed to the diesel entertainment group. the reseon for the name change is simply that word 'xtreme' is becoming wildy popular and is springing up if promotions names across the world. asylum is even becoming over used as the nashville based nwa-tna has dubbed the nashville fairgrounds as the tna asylum. as for the names of their properties (the xtreme asylum arena, the xtreme asylum academy, and the pay per veiw xtreme warfare) they've yet to make a decision. it is most likely that the school and the arena will be renamed but the ppv brand will remain the same. when i contacted ben lake this morning he said the name change was vital to the success of his company. 'the diesel entertainment group has always strived to be the best and most innovative sports entertainment production in the world. to keep that claim we can no longer use a name that can be confused with hundreds of smaller promotions and which in the long run discredit us. the diesel entertainment group is going to be distinctive and when a revolutionary idea happens and is put into effect people will look and know who it was.' as quoted from the diesel entertainment group owner ben lake. http://www.darksidewrestling.com
