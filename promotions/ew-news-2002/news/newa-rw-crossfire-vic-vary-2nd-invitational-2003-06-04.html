[NeWA] RW Crossfire - Vic Vary 2nd Invitational

4 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As Trent Shanahan gets ready to face Vic Vary for the NeWA World Heavyweight Championship, his region RW - prepares to hold it's second Crossfire *special* - The Vic Vary Invitational. http://netwrestling.hypermart.net/Images/wnc.jpg The NeWA fans are in frenzy coming towards the end of the month. All three major regions will be holding their spectecular Supercards at the end of this month. The next Supercard in line for the NeWA fans is without a doubt RW's Crossfire: Vic Vary 2nd Invitational. For those who aren't familiar with this event, the RW rewards it's franchise player, Vic Vary, with a special appreciation night. NeWA's World Heavyweight Champion himself, would take part in the gigantic four-ring mayhem. The rules are quite simple - 40 man, 4 rings, 10 man in each ring. Simple battle-royal would decide the winner of each ring - by the end of the night one man would be standing. The President of Ricochet Wrestling has already declared some early entrances to the competition. He decided that Vic Vary himself would participate and be in the competition. To add some spice, the RW Board decided to let Trent Shanahan, the number one contender for the NeWA World Heavyweight Championship a shot at Vic Vary right there at Crossfire. Both man would participate in this event. NeWA's World Cruiserweight Champion, Brayton Pulse also announced his participation. Pulse just bounced back after a glaring win against five of the top Cruiserweights the Alliance has to offer. Special guest appearances: "Paragon of Perfection" Grant Sexton - BWA's undefeated pathological liar has agreed to participate in this event. Sexton is well-known for his "special" tactics in order to win a match. Adam Burke - Former NeWA World Cruiserweight Champion Adam Burke would make a special appearance in this competition. Burke has been hailed as one of the most impressive wrestlers around the alliance. Wrec Hannibal - BWA's Ironman Champion has also declared his participation in the event. Hannibal has to defend his title against Hunter Hill coming into BWA's Supercard: "Night of Champions". All these and a lot more would take part in one of the greatest regional competitions in NeWA history. Stay tuned for more Crossfire updates. National eWrestling Alliance
