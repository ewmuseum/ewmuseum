PRIME's Nik VanCore's Contract Up?

20 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Early today, news was let out on to the public today that current PRIME superstar Nik VanCore's contract runs out on New Year's Day, and with that being said there has been little to no signs of any contract negotiations between PRIME or Nik VanCore, it looks as if nVc could be closing out his contract next week on ReVolution Early today, news was let out on to the public today that current PRIME superstar Nik VanCore's contract runs out on New Year's Day, and with that being said there has been little to no signs of any contract negotiations between PRIME or Nik VanCore, it looks as if nVc could be closing out his contract next week on ReVolution in the Intense title match. Many reports say that Nik VanCore has been very unhappy with his PRIME career as of late, and also being injured right when a big push was coming on also, so now just returning he could be right out the door again. Many reports have also included that nVc has been in major talks with the returning SHOWrestling. Nik VanCore has not said anything about his contract going up and has kept it very secret from the fans until now. PRIME officials have been said to have been in talks with him, but it hasn't been looking very well. This could also be a reason as why nVc worked a program with Shane Wethers at Onslaught, that could be his final PRIME PPV match. Another note would be that nVc goes out fighting for a title, but if he wins the title then what then? Many are hoping that if nVc wins the Intense title he will sign a contract maybe to extend his time, but if he wins the title and leaves, then what? Or what is he loses? One PRIME official was quoted by saying "Nik is having problems like every superstar does, his body is getting older, and he can't take the risk's he once could. He is very worried that he won't be able to be that major player he wants to be, without doing the crazy things he does." Another official was also quoted saying "nVc is one of those guys that if he can't give it 110% he doesn't want that big spot, he likes to let the younger guys get that spot if he can't, after that injury he took at Fatal Flaw, he just hasn't been the same guy in high spirits as he once was." Many people didn't see what happen to Nik VanCore after Fatal Flaw, he wasn't able to move his body, and many said that was scary to him, and that many people have never seen nVc that worried about a stunt like that before. It is very uncertain at this point what Nik VanCore might do, many are saying he is going to stay, and some are saying he is leaving for other things. Many close friends to nVc have said he has been very unhappy with what has been going on with his character, and that he has been wanting out of his contract for a very long time, and now that time has come. Many are saying that this is why he came back now instead of the extended 7 months he was supposed to take off, so he could close out his career here in PRIME on a good note. Lance Jackson a close friend of nVc's was quoted by saying "Nik does what he wants, he is one crazy son of a bitch, and you can quote me on that one. I know after Fatal Flaw, fighting off the injures just where to much, and that Sebastian Starr forced him to take time off to heal. Nik is one of those guys that doesn't like to dissapoint, or leave one fan not happy, I really don't know what he is going to do, he has pretty much kept this all to himself like a true professional does." Officials are saying that nVc is putting this off to the last minute, and they probably won't know what will happen to the Break-Age Superstar until after the Intense title match. nVc has let out that he will give an official statement that will only be found on the official PRIME site, so stay tuned for more details. http://prime.theewe.net
