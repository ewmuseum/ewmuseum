LOW: Interview With The Challengers

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After revealing themselves to be the mystery opponents of Tag Team Champions Phoenix Rose and Derek Vinyard, Jesse Jamester and Scorpion have claimed a victory, earning themselves a shot at the gold this week. After a long hard fought on Realm 36 the tag team of Jesse Jamester and Scorpion had come to what many thought would be a never likely duo. However the two pulled out an incredible victory as Jesse hit his new finisher the, Panther's Pounce, on Phoenix Rose for the win. In the locker room Jeff Leon just had to get some word with the new team that called themselves the 'Revolutionary Outlaws' so he hid himself in there locker room as they returned to the room. The Interview: Jesse Jamester comes walking in the locker room with Scorpion right beside him, the two looking like they were cousins for they are so pumped about the match. Jesse grabs a towel, squinting his eyes; he wipes the blood from his face before too much of it falls to the floor. The battle was gruesome, the war will be sadistic, but that’s not what the team cares about, they care about becoming known. Jesse: Who’s that? Still aching from the 10 chair shots he received from Derek Vinyard he tosses a few painkillers in his mouth, however Scorpion grabs him by the throat and slaps Jesse in the face. Jesse spits them out, and understands what Scorpion is doing. Jesse: Sorry Scorp… It’s just a habit. Scorpion: Yeah, a bad habit too. What were you saying before the painkillers? Jesse: I saw someone; over they’re near the bathroom. Scorpion checks it out as Jesse slips some painkillers in his mouth and swallows them whole. Scorp finds Jeff Leon hiding in the bathroom, and grabs him by the collar of his nice leather jacket. The little runt is like a doll in the hands of Scorpion, as he is pulled out into the locker room. Scorpion: Now what do you want? Leon: I’d just like to get an interview with you two, just a few questions. Scorpion: Sure… Jesse: Yeah okay, but next time, hide yourself on the outside of the room, or this chair… You see this chair; it’ll be upside your head! Leon: Sorry for that. I couldn’t wait outside because there is more than one interviewer trying to get a promotion here. So if we could, lets get this done fast, before that bitch comes looking for you two. Jesse: Oh a female reporter? What she looks like? Leon: (Sarcastically) Fat and ugly, like a grandma with no teeth. Jesse: Ah shut up, that’s why you’re single still; don’t know how to treat a woman. Leon: Lets start. First off, why did you two tag up? Is there any specific reason that the duo of Scorpion and Jesse Jamester teamed up? Scorpion: Yes. To be honest, no one wants to be second best, and that’s what people see us as. Jesse is a world title contender, yet one loss puts him at the bottom of the ranks? I don’t know how it happened but he didn’t deserve that. If anything he deserved a re-shot, however Trigger isn’t here and that won’t happen. Hell I don’t know what’s going on with Belmont right now. Have you seen how stressed he is? I am worried for the benefit of LOW and that’s why I asked Jesse, if he wanted to become something better. I said look at the teams that have passed over LOW, the Shadow Riders, Broken Reality, RIP, and others… All of them were great champions however we, we will become one of thee best! Jesse: Exactly. At first I told Scorpion it wouldn’t work. I didn’t have what it took to be the ruthless son of a bitch I had been in the months before. However Scorpion knows me a little too well. He gave me something… Jesse turns around and looks in his bag, there he pulls out something that brings a smirk to his face. Jesse: The one and only original Jamester mask! Jeff Leon jumps back. The mask is stitched, bloody, and torn. It’s much the same as it would have been, for Jesse had been through hell in that mask. Scorpion: After I handed that to Jesse, he said why not. It was perfect, the two of us, in one ring, facing off against other tag teams, were unstoppable. Leon: Well that clears up a few questions I had… Hmm, what about your name, the Revolutionary Outlaws? Jesse: Jeff, in all of your years of wrestling have you heard the name, Revolutionary Outlaws? Leon: No. Scorpion: That’s exactly why we called ourselves that. Were the new dawn, we crack the whips that others follow. Sure we aren’t WORLD CHAMPIONS, or hold any titles, but we are a threat, and that’s what makes this team a team… Jesse: Defiantly. Leon: What about the Tag Titles? Scorpion: There ours come Saturday. Jesse: Simple as that. A ladder match, a dumpster match, hell even a tables match, any of them wouldn’t have mattered. We’re champion material. You could take Belmont and team him with anyone, and we’d still beat him. Leon: So are you saying you’ll give all challengers a title shot? Jesse: Sure. After we win the belts, we’ll give everyone a title shot. No number one contender matches, title matches. Too many times have I seen Tag Teams just come into LOW and then leave. This is the time to prove us; it’s time to take the tag division… (Cracks his neck and looks up) to new heights! Scorpion: Therefore, we offer open challenge to ANY two men who will take us on in a title match on Realm 38! Stipulation will be added when the time comes… The two toss Jeff out of the room and smirk just before closing the door. Lights Out Wrestling
