EW-News Exclusive: RaGE Announces August's Unleashed Venue!

27 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Nothing is keeping Real and Graphic Entertainment down from making its return and providing fans with the action they have waited for all of June. The company has gone as far as promoting August's summer blockbuster already! http://www.rageproject.com/images/rage_ewnews.jpg During the near one month break that the offices of Real and Graphic Entertainment had to deal with a legal matter, they also had the chance to come up with a number of new things, including the Tour Dates for the months of July and August. As EW-News has now learned, the general consensus at Real and Graphic Entertainment has been to hit its mainstream audience once more as soon as it comes back in July. For many of the dates that have been signed on this new tour, cities that had been scheduled to host events in June have been redeemed with the chance to host events in July. Some of those cities include: Las Vegas, Houston, Washington, DC, New Orleans, and Atlanta. Real and Graphic Entertainment is also offering smaller venues that cost less to rent out, and hold a lot less than the usual 15,000 to 20,000 fans that Real and Graphic Entertainment draws. These venues are located in smaller areas and are expected to hold a couple hundred plus. The largest venues rented out hold 2,000 or so people with standing room available, and should be an easy sell out for RaGE. Those shows will focus lesser and developing talent, but should be an interesting experience for any fan. Though all that news is great, the main focus of those private meetings that Real and Graphic Entertainment executives held on their coffee breaks from the "RaGE Name Trial" were about two major events this summer: Se7en(Seven) Deadly Sins and Unleashed III. In the meetings that were held, TJ Faith concluded that the company needed to step up its level of advertising and promoting in these events. To quote TJ: "[Our] previous attempts to promote and advertise anything we have done have drawn only mild success to what we could be achieving. With more focus as far as commercials, promotional advertisements, and endorsements with sponsors, the success of these two summer events could be nearly double of any of the company's previous, even surpassing Total HaVok III... All we need to do is deliver what the fans will expect from us after all the promoting that is put into these two events. It is time to cut our losses, and double our gains." That motivational speech at one of the many meetings was enough to motivate Real and Graphic Entertainment executives to push the envelope with both events for this summer. Se7en Deadly Sins was voted to be held in Baltimore, Maryland. The theme to the event would of course focus around the number "7" and the company has come up with a number of references to the number including the fact that they are holding the event in the seventh State in the Union, a nice touch. Numerous things from starting the show at 7 p.m. and running it in the seventh month as well add only to this experience. The event that was perhaps focused on more and debated endlessly between the executives came to be Unleashed III. The third of it's kind, yes. There was a lot of "head butting" going on as the officers debated over where the event would be held. Of course one of the most limiting facts to this decision would go to the market that the company has been able to draw the most money out of in the past four months. Knowing well that the event of Unleashed is the highlight to the summer, choosing the location of the event was indeed critical. To deliver the "earth-shattering" show that Real and Graphic Entertainment was aiming for, only one place would work. It was through an unanimous decision that the company of Real and Graphic Entertainment came to the conclusion that the Staples Center in Los Angeles, California, would indeed play home to Unleashed this year. Strangely enough, that is the exact place where Fear: Version Two debuted from on March 1 in front of a full house on the West coast. The ratings of that show were pleasing for both RaGE and the Fox Network, and now the company seems to want to try its like there once more. So, as it appears to be now, Real and Graphic Entertainment will return to California for the first time since March and they will be closing the summer out in Los Angeles. Numerous other dates in August will include stops to Minneapolis, Calgary, Toronto, Montreal, Oklahoma City, Vancouver and San Diego. Those will all be a combination of small and large scale events and tickets go on sale for all those events in August on July 12. Tickets will range anywhere from $15 for tier seating to $35 for ringside. At the small scale events, tickets are an even $20 all around. For Unleashed and other Pay Per View Events, ringside seats will be worth $200 and varied arena seating ranges from $50 to $150. All ringside seats to any Real and Graphic Entertainment event come with a free T-shirt ($20 value). For information on tickets, please call Real and Graphic Entertainment Headquarters in Atlantic City, New Jersey, or your individual box offices. Real and Graphic Entertainment
