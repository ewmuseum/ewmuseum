Major Stars Return to DW @ Destruction #75….

9 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Dynasty Wrestling world was tonight turned on its head as in total five old faces returned to the federation, as well as one debut on the weekly Monday Night Destruction broadcast, the seventy fifth in the series. DW - Getting Back on Track Mike Vitucci promised that it would be a night of many surprises and it certainly was with many DW fans finding it hard to comprehend what they saw with their very own eyes. DW Final Thoughts host SSB returned to active duty and immediately hit up a rivalry with Smooth Criminal, with a First Blood Steel Cage match already been booked for the DW Titans PPV. One half of the DW Tag Team Champions Chooch defeated Zyfloi in a hard fought match up. Legendary DW Tag Team the Adams Brothers turned on each other with Kevin Adams putting his brother Dave on a stretcher before learning that they must now team up at the DW Titans PPV to take on the Tag Team Champions. Xander Epic won his debut match by DQ after Bryce Frishcer resorted to taking out his frustrations on the young rookie with the use of weapons. Sean Manning defeated Psyko Stevo in another hard fought battle to book his place as one of the Six men in the DW Titans PPV main event battle for the World Championship belt. After the match Sanctioned Violence, who has not even signed a DW contract debuted on DW TV, challenging Psyko Stevo to an unsanctioned fight at DW Titans. International Champion Gillesp defeated Suicidal in a match for the Hardcore Championship to add another belt to his waist. By winning the match Gillesp also booked his place as part of the six man main event of DW Titans for the World Championship belt. DW acting C.E.O Mike Vitucci came to the ring and promised a surprise for the Six Man Main event at Titans, and delivered four times over! Vitucci first brought out former World Champion Hellfire X to make his return to DW. If that wasn’t enough he brought out former World and International Champion Chris Boswell to also return. Just to add a little extra excitement he brought out another returnee in the former of Boswell’s half brother, former Two Time World Champion and former International Champion ‘Superstar’ Shawn Storm. Just to cap off what is set to be the most exciting main event in history, Vitucci announced the return of another DW legend, with Hall of Fame member, former Two Time World Champion Havoc being the man to complete the six members of the Titans Main event!
