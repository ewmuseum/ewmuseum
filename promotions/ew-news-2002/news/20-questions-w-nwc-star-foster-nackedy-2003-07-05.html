20 Questions w/ NWC Star Foster Nackedy

5 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's our very first edition of PW Trash's (soon to be) infamous 20 Questions. Our first victim is NWC: South's Foster Nackedy. Without further ado... http://e-magine.revolutiondigital.com/[detail withheld]/foster20questions.htm 1. What are you currently up to? This is your only chance to promote your shit. Right now I am actually thinking about taking a short break from wrestling, but as far as the game itself goes, I am sitting comfortably atop the NWC:South branch with the title in hand. I am currently working on a record deal with my band Heads and Tails, and even going through scripts. I'm an all rounder, bitches. 2. So, level with us: GuppiePhysh? What was the reasoning behind that name? Was SeaSnayl already taken? Honestly? The creative development board in KNF told me they needed a good gimmick with fish. They even offered the name, and me being the young stupid rookie took it. The reason I took it was because I looked around at the other names of the superstars and it all kind of went together. See, what I didn't realize in my young age was that the KNF creative department was on crack. We had names such as Dan-e-O, Homeless Havok, Le Poncho, and Crudley. So there, GuppiePhysh was just another name. Big jump from that to the big leagues, huh? 3. What made you decide to get into professional wrestling? At first I did it because I needed a way to make money. See, in New Orleans I was a martial arts instructor, but I couldn't get much money out of that. Part of that whole GuppiePhysh thing was that I loved the aquarium, and my main drive for wrestling was to keep the aquarium alive. I heard trials were coming to New Orleans, and some of my students told me I would do well. So I went. 4. Being from the "bad good guys" as you are, if the Rogue's Army wasn't in existance, where would you be? The Traditionalists, or Team BVI? With a Rogue faction out of the game, I would most likely be in the traditionalists, or not taking a side at all. Because the point is, I hate what Ben Van Iten is doing to the NWC right now. And in some way I want to be a part of stopping it. And if the more effective way was taken out of the mix, I'd have to still find a way to fight. 5. Rate the following South wrestlers on a scale of 1 to 10. 1 being as talented as Deuce Crimson and 10 being a wrestling god. Jade = Definitely a 3, and not only because she is a worthless bitch. Just because she can't really wrestle. Darian Morrow = As much as we're at odds now, he can't be denied. 8. Luke Denton = He's got flash, but he doesn't have anything to back it up. 5. Wyatt Connors = A mastermind, but no wrestler. His best place is behind a desk. 6. Hiro Saotome = Hiro can wrestle. Nothing else about it. 9. Eve Mercantice = She's impressive. She can get it done. 7. Christopher Packard = I don't know, he isn't really that good. Because he puts me to sleep every time he talks, I give him a 2. Mark Tower = Tower is monstrous, though he seems a bit slow. 7. Vin Alejandro = Now this guy is funny to watch. He can wrestle, but he's too After School Special for me. 7. 6. What is your take on the 'war' in the NWC? Who do you think is winning? It's a stalemate, really. No one is giving ground, and no one is really getting an advantage. This is how I expected it to be. It is going to take a major battle to start the explosions. Maybe even a small war in itself. 7. Do you really expect us to believe you were dating that Morgan chick for that long and never knew she had a dick? Do you ever secretly wish that Magen had a few extra inches under her belt? One word for you: blowjobs. And about Magen? Hell no. She's perfect the way she is. 8. So Foster, does the fact that a woman accomplished what you couldn't, beating Tony Pride for the South Heavyweight Title, still make you toss and turn at night? Well, dick, the girl did the same thing both I and Kaylo Laven did...only thing was, she got away with it. Kudos to her. But she's still a whore. 9. If there's one thing in life you wish you could take back, what would it be? Might we suggest 'french-kissing a shemale' as one of the options. One thing in my life? Inviting Kaylo to be in the ring with me for my wedding. Being married that way would have been so much sweeter. But, hey, you can't take things back. Oh, and fuck you for the shemale comment. If you didn't know she had a penis you would be all over it, you little sexually-deprived fuck. 10. So, you were a bad guy a while back. What triggered that, and what made you decide to be 'good' again? It's all about people. When I came into the NWC, I was the fresh-face youngster who wanted to take on the world. I loved to wrestle, and I loved to make everyone happy. But then Loki assigned Joe as my manager. Joe Frederickson is a shitty guy, and he made me change my mindset and my name. Then, eight months later, Magen came by and I realized what a dick I was being to her and everyone else. Not to mention my mentor and father-figure was dying in the hospital. All of this made me decide to change everything. 11. The last of Foster Nackedy: Last movie you saw = Anger Management. Major disappointment. Last CD you listened to = Eminem "Eminem's Show." Last time you changed a diaper = Yesterday. Magen was out. Last time you got drunk = When was the last time I had to watch a Sean Laughreah promo? Last opponent you seriously got pissed off at = Hm. Really? I always act up angry on screen, but I never really get pissed. I can't say. Last time you thought about strangling Ben Van Iten = When he came to Revival. Last time you considered legally changing your name = Just a few hours ago, when I considered changing it to "Fuck you." 12. Name the three high points of your career and the three low points of your career to date. High points: Winning the South heavyweight championship, getting married to Magen (hey, it happened on television), and going toe to toe with Steve Sire and not quitting. Low points: Being screwed out of every big match I've ever been in. You can find three in there, I'm sure. 13. How did it feel when the NWC 100 came out and Kaylo Laven was above you? Do you think you got robbed? At first I was pissed, I have to admit. I mean, I did beat him before, and I came out on top. But when I thought about it, I started off 2002 sucking. So the right decision was made, I'll give them that. Thing is, does that make Kaylo the better wrestler? Again, Hell no. 14. Why didn't you try to do something after Joe killed your dad in front of you? Not only your dad, but your mentor and friend as well? Are you waiting for him to come back as another primary color? Ha, no, dick. As far as I think, my dad deserved dying after what he did to my mom and to Joe's dad. Maybe not so gruesome, but it was a shitty deal he pulled. As for Joe, I can bet you all the money I have that he will be back. He wants another round of fucking with Foster Nackedy, but this time I'm ready for him. 15. You have to pick one of the following. Which will it be? A. A nuclear reactor explodes near Detroit, killing hundreds and making the surrounding area uninhabitable for years to come? B. You lose a bet and are forced to become Kaylo Laven's personal servant for an entire year. C. You have all of your fingernails and toenails ripped off with a pliers, without anesthetic. Definitely not B. I'd die before I put myself at Kaylo's mercy. Now, I'd like to consider myself a humanitarian and claim that I would rather have my fingernails and toenails ripped off before the whole nuclear reactor thing, but hey. I don't know anybody in Detroit. Hopefully Kid Rock is visiting family up there and gets caught in it too. 16. What crossed your mind when you decided to carry on the family tradition and name your son something completely ridiculous? And were you actually seeking a wife whose name matches the absurdity pattern your lineage has already laid out? Were you thinking at all? Better question, were you thinking when you made these questions? Magen and I have been together since freshman year, and the kid's name is a good name. What is your name, huh? Is it something like John or Clarence? Judge not lest ye be judged, you motherfucking crack addict. 17. What NWC wrestler or personality deserves a kick to the nads, and why? Jesus, there are too many answers on this one. As far as wrestler, we're going to say Bobby Irish, because he's been giving me slack recently. What do you remember about fighting the following people? Hunter Sabuani = damn, he was good. I mean, really good. Everything I did he had a counter for, and he was almost a blur when he moved around the ring. But the thing is, I lost to him on a botched count. I had a lot of that match in my control. It was around then when I realized that no one could really beat me. Lance Marshall = I'm sure it is different facing the Lance Marshall of now, but the Lance Marshall of then was just plain scary. He was mean and angry, and he wouldn't show any remorse at all. Again, I held my own. And again, I lost by a cheating Andy O'Brien. But Lance Marshall is awesome in the ring, no bottom line about it. Kaylo Laven = This boy is good, but he isn't as good as all of his plotting and talking suggests. He was a bit harder to face than Andy O'Brien, but I expected all of his attacks as they were coming. He is pretty predictable. Michael M. Maxwell = He is a shoddy wrestler, I think...I really can't remember. He's pretty forgettable. Steve Sire = he called me Pots before, but I think in the ring that he and I are most alike. Steve and I look alike, have the same build, and when we fought we did the same moves two or three times. It was pretty freaky. That match was hell for me, because Magen threw in the towel when I know and everyone else knows I could have been the champion. But I had him right where I wanted him. Steve and I may be alike, but I know I'm the better wrestler. 19. Does it ever bother you, just a little bit, that you were once hit in the head with a chainsaw by a guy named 'Lester B. Frans'? Eh, not really. Because no matter that he hit me, no matter that he conspired to kill my dad...he lost to Chris Packard. And that just fucks everything up. 20. Time for PW Trash's Word Association. We mention a name and you give us your thoughts. Lance Marshall = Great guy, slightly misguided. Real match, I could take him. Wyatt Connors = Sneaky, underhanded, devilish, and a great guy to have a conversation with. Ben Van Iten = Giving him credit, he is a genius. He's also a monster who needs to be dealt with soon. Billy Page = He's come into his own. My opinion about him has changed drastically over the years. Sigurd Von Glatz = Doesn't know how to run a business. Doesn't know how to make a federation exciting. Doesn't know how to wipe his ass properly. Kaylo Laven = What can I say? I hate him. Hope he dies. Steve Sire = Just like I said, he dropped out of everyone's sights. Sure, he beat me, but he couldn't do it alone. Sound and the Fury = Weird ass mofos. They are pretty good at what they do, though. Hope they win council-wide gold. It'll be good for them, and good for me because they'll be out of my hair for a few months. Michael M. Maxwell = I'm the only guy to beat him when he was in LCW. Now I'm gonna beat him in his other form. Either way he's just a homoerotic prick who's begging to be destroyed. Well, that was interesting. Foster gave us some shit, but overall it was a fun little question and answer. Just between the staffers at PW Trash, though... we still think he did it with the dick girl.
