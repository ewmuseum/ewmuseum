Think Red #1

4 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Join me, Comrades, as Ivan Stanislav shows you the wonders of Communism in the OSE and what you can do to help! Greetings to all wrestlers and fans out there! For those of you that do not know who this is, I am Praporschik Ivan Sergeiovich Stanislav. Wrestler of the OSW, Champion of the Working Class, Hero of Russia, and Supreme Commander of the most influential and positive force in the Old School Wrestling Federation: The Red Army. Understandably so, many people look upon Communism and instantly see it in bad light. Capitalist propaganda perpetrated by United States and her allies have given Communism a very bad name, but allow Ivan to allay your fears, your doubts, and introduce you to new and positive role that communism has been implemented. In the OSW, it is run by a powerful, evil President known as Trashcan Man. This man, as filthy in mind as he is in body, rules the OSW with iron fist and oppresses all that dares to challenge his supremacy. Ivan Stanislav himself has fallen victim to his evil wiles. I carried the OSW World Title for months upon months, and then when he was finished with Ivan, instigated way to have Ivan be cheated out of title, and knocked from his pedestal of Proletariat Protector! Indeed, some people have gone with this change by Trashcan Man, but then again, it was Trashcan Man that murdered former Hardcore Legend Ed in cold blood and brutalized Ivan’s long time companion: Tempest. However, from the ashes of this treachery, the Red Army rose and now has instituted it’s new will to fight and to live, that many people should watch and emulate. The Red Army, headed by Ivan himself, consists of six loyal communists and we are all equal! We fight together, travel together, and share all the money that we make with one another. The forces of Trashcan Man, The Asylum, who rely on cheating and stealing to win, are unable to defeat us due to our communist brotherhood! Other federations should take heed and join us! We all know that eventually, great communist revolution will spring up and we will all have to choose a side. Do you wish to be on the side of the bourgeois capitalists who will ultimately fail? I think not. Men like Eli Flair and women like Poison Ivy have seen the power of Ivan Stanislav and his benevolence. People from fWo who know and love Tempest have seen how happy she has become under the protection of The Red Army. Meanwhile, those like Tyler Nelson are now out of job, and Trashcan Man is losing control of his federation! Within Red Army, we hold World Title and United States titles, as well as control the Security of the federation! As you can see, all have flocked to join our ranks and protect themselves from Trashcan Man’s tyranny. Those in other federations should do the same. A visit to Primetime Championship Wrestling weeks ago merely enhanced this point. The members of PCW greeted their old Comrade in arms with smiles and good wishes! It goes to show how wonderful Communism is! It is true honor to be writing to the masses. The masses are what is truly important, not money hoarding capitalists. And Ivan Stanislav knows that they are all around us. Thus, this is Ivan’s charge for you: Look at the OSW. View the Red Army in all its glory. And then, take what you have learned and use it in your home federation! Together, we can all revolt and create the perfect wrestling community the likes of which have never been seen! And if tutelage is needed, there are ways to contact Ivan Sergeiovich and he would be more than happy to help in any way, shape or form! To view the Red Army in all its wonder, go to www.oseentertainment.com The Red Army can be viewed under the active roster and as always, we constantly police and patrol the OSW during Sunday Slam to make sure nothing is out of hand! Thank you, My Comrades! Ivan sends you the best wishes in world! Think about the status of home federations! Think about what communism can do for you! But always.. ALWAYS think Red! Das vidanya Comrades! Ivan Sergeiovich Stanislav Red Army Commander
