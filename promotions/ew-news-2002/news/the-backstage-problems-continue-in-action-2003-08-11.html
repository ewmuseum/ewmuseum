The Backstage Problems Continue in Action...

11 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The return of LLB, the leaving of Sylo, and the antics of KroW and Sharc...it seems that Action Wrestling's backstage area has become World War III. It seems that with the surprise return of Roland Miles Ermann, LLB, the Action Wrestling backstage area went into an uproar. Rumors have it that the entire backstage area was very displeased that Jeff Garvin was forced to job to the returning LLB, and see it as Roland using his creative control card. One superstar who seemingly was fed up with Roland and how he used his power, was none other than...no, not Jeff Garvin...Max Danger. Danger has always been known as the easy going laid back locker room leader of Action Wrestling, ever since his debut on the very first Action Wrestling show...but it seemed he finally took the leadership role and confrontation Action executives, and more importantly, Roland Miles Ermann. Directly after Pressure Point went off the air...Max Danger and Roland Miles Ermann were said to have met outside the arena and began to have a verbal warfare...that was ready to turn into a physical warfare...until Action road agents broke the two up. Prior to this, it's said alot of the roster members weren't happy about LLB immediately gaining a victory upon his return...some even stating it was "bullshit". Jeff Garvin wasn't as tweaked as Duel, Danger and KroW. KroW, who is already on bad terms with management (it has been reported KroW only has 3 months left on his written contract, and no negotiations have been made to resign the Satanic God), stated bluntly on a recent radio show that, "It's funny how Roland disappears, comes up with some lame excuse for being gone...and suddenly gets a win right off the bat...when I had to earn my way...and I'm still doing it. Like I said, the fucking federation runs around Roland and kisses his ass so much that you can smell the shit on their lips". But Roland's return wasn't the only thing that went wrong on the LIVE broadcast of Pressure Point. The segment between Jeremy Bold and Sylo was completely SHOOT...meaning, it was not scripted and was entirely true. The segment was scheduled to have Sylo become a hired gun of Bold's, and Bold to offer the blue haired monster alot of money in return. But before the segment took place, Jay Edward Sylo believed it would hurt his character...but executives refused to change the idea. So as the cameras roled, Sylo went off on Bold...and actually DID punch him, and actually DID quit. It was a move that startled many, as well as got a few negative reactions from some roster members. The team of Arcade and Son had posted on their official website about the Sylo ordeal, which in turn had Sylo respond to it on Arcade and Son's fan board...but the post was deleted, and not saved in time. And if the controversy with Sylo and LLB wasn't enough, KroW also has been taking apart in the role of "asshole" backstage. The man known as Levine, has been reading over the contract he and Young agreed on a few months ago...and noticed that he also had a creative control card...meaning he could cut his own segments and do his own thing. It's said that KroW has already approached executives about the clause and has stated that he will be using his creative control card TONIGHT on Pressure Point...wishing to cut his own promo on the card. Executives have no power over Young's signature...thus, they have granted KroW the TV Time. With Sylo seemingly gone (although executives are attempting to fish a way out to either: sue Sylo, or bind him to the contract), LLB returning, and KroW using his newly found creative control card clause...Action's backstage area can only get worse. This Pressure Point should be very interesting...on Television, as well as off. Action Wrestling
