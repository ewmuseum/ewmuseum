Dynamite heads to home of the Packers!

5 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Green Bay, Wisconsin will play host to Monday Night Dynamite as we head towards the MWF's September Pay-Per-View, Implosion. We head to Green Bay, home of the Packers for what should be a memorable show. Doomsday a former UWF star kicks off the action against Iniquity. Iniquity is looking all the more like a future star wrestler after his teaming up with Insane Wayne to create a force that the MWF should be worried about. Joe Foale makes his in-ring return against rookie Mickey Bombastic, it should be a fast paced match between two well matched wrestlers. Troy Knight and Ace Spade continue to wage war and Flash and Sean West find themselves caught up in it...Knight wants revenge on Spade after the attacks on his son and 'lover'...Spade won't back down. Flash will be wanting to avenge the beating given to his former pupil Rodney Ferno by Sean West. Insane Wayne returns to MWF action and finds the beast that is Simon Masters standing in his way, Anarkist has brought in Masters as a partner and now the Chaotic Future look to beat down Insane Wayne again. The Main Event will see EyCe attempt to gain retribution for last weeks shame, Sean Harley is the man he will be trying to beat. Harley is without tag team partner Scotty Heart who retired last week...should be violent as the rules are no disqualification. Also the MWF will announce the signing of the new MWF General Manager, who will replace Fozzy? Click here for all the latest Monday Night Dynamite happenings...
