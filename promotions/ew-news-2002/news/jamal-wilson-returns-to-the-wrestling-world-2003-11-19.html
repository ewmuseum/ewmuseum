Jamal Wilson Returns to the Wrestling World

19 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After a long departure since his short stint in the Asylum, and his longer lasted stint in the Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling; a federation has brought Jamal Wilson to their fed... The World Wrestling Corporation, a federation recently brought onto an HBO weekly contract (that debuted last week), had recently went into a deal with Jamal Wilson. Wilson, a man most known for holding the PIW United States Title, as well as being a part the Asylum's roster, has gone missing ever since his release from the fighting league. But it seems that Paul Levine, and his WWC executives, have agreed to take Jamal Wilson in. But the complications of the contract are strange... It seems Levine wasn't very interested in the deal, but promoters felt that giving Wilson a shot would gain publicity. Levine went on to state, "Wilson is a great entertainer...but his character really is odd around the WWC roster. And the guy has a past of being unloyal to his federations..." With that comment said, it seems Wilson was brought into the WWC under a verbal 3 month contract, which means that Wilson is not contractually oblidged to only be in the WWC, and the WWC holds all rights to release Wilson without the standard fees upon release. It's seems to be a very uneasy relationship, but one that saw Jamal Wilson returning to the wrestling world against WWC newcomer Steve Muzio. Check out more at the WWC Online.
