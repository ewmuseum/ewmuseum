WCWF SNI: Tag titles decided, PPV card finalized

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With control of the WCWF hanging in the balance, the final Sunday Night Impact before Asian Assault was supposed to answer some key questions heading into the event. Instead, all the events of June 22 did was show that the Fates appear to be falling apart at the seams. The opener featured three returns and one debut. “Misfit” Jonny Morgan and Spike Alexander were making their in-ring returns to the WCWF after injury, Chris Blair was back after an absence, and former WWRPG wrestler Charlie Owens was making his WCWF debut. Owens and Morgan controlled the early action, but things didn’t get interesting until James Michaels came down the ramp with the old North American Wrestling Federation tag team belts in hand. He KO’d Blair and Alexander with them, then hit Owens, allowing Morgan to get the pinfall. Morgan and Michaels were the last NAWF tag champs as Fight Club — was Michaels feeling nostalgic? There wasn’t time to find out as Michaels faced off with Stan Stone next. The newly psychotic Stone manhandled Michaels, who’s no lightweight himself, early on in the match, but his injured knee gave and Michaels fell on him for a two-count. Enraged, Stone would recover and give Michaels a chokeslam from the top rope all the way to the floor! Needless to say, Stone won the match by countout. Raymond Chandler’s International Television Title defense against Dan Bonez turned into a submission clinic. Bonez never stood a chance against Chandler’s technical attack on this night, and Chandler retained his title after forcing Bonez to tap to The Line. One of the worst-kept secrets in the WCWF is the hatred among members of the Fates, especially between World Champion Jester and the somewhat ironically named Bad Blood. So you can imagine there were few punches pulled when Jester teamed with Hardcore Champion Brad Jackson to face Dane Rennier & Stone for the WCWF tag team titles. The match got off to a strange start when the champions were introduced separately, then Rennier got double-teamed early and Stone did nothing to help. When Stone did enter the match, he took the brunt of the Fates’ assault, but Rennier saved the match at one point with a frog splash from three-quarters of the way across the ring. Rennier and Stone unleashed a devastating Point of Impact/Dane-O-Might combo on Jester, but the world champion managed to get his foot on the bottom rope. A later exchange would lead to Stone whipping Jackson into Rennier, allowing Jester to hit the Punchline on Rennier and win the titles! After the match, Rennier and Stone argued, and Stone gave Rennier the Point Of Impact… THREE times. SNI Results: “Misfit” Jonny Morgan d. Charlie Owens, Spike Alexander, & Chris Blair Stan Stone d. “The King” James Michaels by countout International Television Title Match: Raymond Chandler (c) d. Dan Bonez by submission Tag Team Title Match: Jester & Brad Jackson d. Bad Blood (c) (Dane Rennier & Stan Stone) by pinfall Jester & Jackson win tag team championship ---------- Asian Assault Sunday, June 29 Tokyo, Japan WCWF World Title match Jester(c) vs. The Judge A match some time in the making, and it all rides on this. Judge, the federation's last best hope, up against the seemingly unassailable Jester. Will Shooter break Duke's deadlock and give Chandler a foothold to build on, or will Duke's men simply roll over him and leave the WCWF to fight grimly for another month? Re-match tag match, best of three falls, WCWF U.S. and International Television Titles: Raymond Chandler (c-INTV) and Matt Walker vs. Dane Rennier (c-U.S.) and Stan Stone Broken ankles, injured teammates and electrocution. Chandler and Walker have taken the worst that Stone and Rennier can throw at them, and have kept the fight alive. Can they go at it again? Can they cover each other's backs and can they rely on each other's weakened states? Can they survive not doing so? Rules: Whoever pins a title holder takes that man's title. If one man should end up holding two titles and take a pinfall, the lower-ranked title will be handed over. There will also be another stipulation that won’t be announced until the PPV. WCWF Hardcore Title Invitational: As Brad Jackson's hardcore reign stretches to rival that of the legendary Redemptor, can he survive another Invitational? More to the point, who is capable of challenging him? Open to all active WCWF wrestlers Battle Royal (Winner gets INTV Title shot at the next show): Includes: "Misfit" Jonny Morgan, Dan Bonez, “The King” James Michaels, Chris Blair, Charlie Earl Owens, Spike Alexander, “Rapid” Rick Reno, Garnere World Class Wrestling Federation
