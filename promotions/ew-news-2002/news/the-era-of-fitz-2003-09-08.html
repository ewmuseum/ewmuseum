The Era of Fitz

8 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lanier's Out, FitzPatrick's In A historic Winner Takes All Pay Per View saw the 3 Championships change hands, the first ever Siren Champion, and the Legacy Championship being vacated. The main event saw Fred Fusion versus Daniel Adams for the Core Wrestling Legacy Championship, a match billed as "The Match Of The Century". It saw Fred Fusion hit with a chair by he's own manager Gene Fitzpatrick, and Fred being pulled on a very much knowing Daniel Adams. "Fred was too stupid to realise, you see, Adams will get the gold when he defeats Lanier on Smash, and I get control of Core Wrestling." After firing Fred and stripping him of the title, Aline Fanco then came down to show us all that we have been screwed. Little over 12 hours since he has claimed office, Fitz has fired long time Core Wrestling Announcer Taylor Snyder, and also promoted Patton Warren to Chief Of Staff. New announcers will be recruited before Smash is to take place in Earls Court, North London. Rumours circulating is that the remaining members of The British Empire, are disgusted with the treatment of their friend Fred Fusion, and are reported to speak their feelings this Smash. Also, Tom Mason recieved 10 stitches from a gash in he's forehead he recieved after a chair shot from Fitzpatrick in the Main Event. Mason is reported to have been extremely pissed, and this might not be the last we see of Tom Mason and RaGE. Core Wrestling
