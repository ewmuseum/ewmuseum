Cool: I Feel Sorry Lisa Tien

23 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Former Universal Wrestling Federation superstar DJ Cool has today stated that he feels 'sorry' for Lisa Tien after she launched a heated attack at the wrestler yesterday evening. The English-born wrestler, who has recently been linked with Tien's federation FAW, yesterday laughed at rumours he would be joining the federation. The wrestler had recently been linked with fWo aswell, but yesterday Tien said he is 'not fWo quality, or FAW for that matter'. But DJ Cool stated, outside of an apartment in Monte Carlo, Monaco this morning: "I feel sorry for Lisa Tien. She actually thinks I don't know I never received an offer from FAW? It was rumours, and laughable ones at that". He also gone on to say: "I wouldn't set foot inside of a FAW arena or appear on their shows if I was paid a billion pounds, dollars or anything else to do so. Its a poor federation, and i'm sure that Mrs. Tien certainly knows that. I could buy it out if I really wanted to". He then also verbally attacked Tien, stating: "You really must be a twisted, sick old woman if you honestly think I would lower myself to compete against people called Hardcore Hoefel and 7th Wonder Of Foam. I find it amusing that you even think I received an offer when I never even stated I did so, yet I also pity you for the fact you are so damned deaf that you can't hear what I am saying". He also said: "I could get into any federation should I want to - be that fWo, LOW or even some scum like FAW. You really think I give a sh*t what you think? No. I'm DJ Cool, and if you haven't heard of me you've been isolated from television, radio, news and much more for the last 3 or so years". Primetime Central
