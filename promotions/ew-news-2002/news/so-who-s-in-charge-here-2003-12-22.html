So Who's In Charge Here?

22 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With ReVolution under 24 hours away, a backstage source has revealed that neither Tosiaki Motoki or Hin See will be attending the Thomas & Mack Center for the event. Instead, Motoki and the Asian Cowboy will be gambling the night away in one of Las Vegas premiere casinos like the Golden Nugget (left). In fact, we understand that Motoki is going to meet an old friend from Japan there while Hin See will draw the match stipulations for his Forture Cookie Challenge from the same spot... With PRIME still rocking from the revelation Sebastian made from his hospital bed during the climax of ReVolution Monday night, the question is now... who has Sebastian sold his co-ownership stock to? The rumor mill is already spinning into overdrive with Taun Phams name being the obvious favorite. Pham has the money, the resources and the motive to return to power in PRIME having been conned out of co-ownership by his father and Toshiaki Motoki. Indeed Motoki is sweating that Taun Pham could be in line to return and is baying for the blood of his betrayers. But who else could Sebastian be refering to when he spoke of an "old friend"? Perhaps he meant Tony Rolo, but then it's highly doubtful that "The Specialist" has the capital to bid for Sebastians share in PRIME. What then of former contacts like "The Noble" Wellington Littlebrook or even Victor Blood? Blood was a constant thorn in Sebastians side during his previous promotion $oldout Wrestling, maybe he wanted to inflict the same scourge on Motoki & co? Some sources are even speculating that Hugh Hefner might be the mystery buyer! Hefner and Sebastian became friends upon meeting at a photoshoot back in 1998 when Sarah Deamonte (wife of former JEW World Champion Seph Deamonte) posed for Playboy. Since then Hefner has been a firm fan of Sebastians work and even invested $1 million $W with Sebastian honoring this by having Hefner visit one of the shows and book his own "Hugh Hefner Bonus Match" where Thug roC and Nik VanCore were involved in the National Title 4-Way Buried Alive Match. It's possible, but the bunny man remains an outsider as regards to this mystery transaction. PRIME and its fans will just have to wait until the January PPV where the reign of the new co-owner will officially begin and their identity will be revealed. First though is the end of year show ReVolution XX and it all goes down in Vegas tonight on UPN! PRIME
