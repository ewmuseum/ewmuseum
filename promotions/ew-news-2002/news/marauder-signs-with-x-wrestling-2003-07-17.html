Marauder signs with X Wrestling

17 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Third Times a Charm. X Wrestling has signed Marcus Corteia, better known to the wrestling world as Marauder. On the latest edition of Tuesday eXtreme, it was announced that Marauder will be making his return to X Wrestling for the third time. Xw commissioner Jack Wallace announced that, Marauder will be making his televised return to X Wrestling at their annual Xtreme Violence pay per view, on August 10th. Up until that point, Marauder will be working the house show circuit. His return to the ring full time in X Wrestling, will come almost a year after the last time he performed on consecutive shows. When his career hit a long hiatus, it was due to his premature exit from the Asylum as a member of the group known as Stranglehold. The line between wrestling and fighting is a thick one as Marauder found out, receiving a broken leg at the hands of the now deceased Exxa Decimal. Even up to his injury, Corteia has been well known for his ego and him being a constant instigator in every promotion he’s been in; other places he caused problems include: jOlt, Tornado Wrestling, XWF, and Ring Rage. The only promotions he wasn’t noted as having problems in included Monte Burns’ NEWA, and the fWo, it’s assumed that the latter is because he didn’t have the chance to make an appearance on the promotion’s show before it went on hiatus. Marauder was at the top of the wrestling world, in 2000 as he reigned supreme in Tornado Wrestling. A former three-time International Champion in the federations first run, Marauder claimed the fed’s World Heavyweight Title and held it for 63 days. Due to his popularity, and his believed marketability, Pepsi-Cola signed him to a deal where he got his very own soda, in Conquest Grape Soda. With his fall in popularity, Conquest has been less and less visible in stores, as the cola giant releases more variants of it’s Pepsi brand. Somewhere in the wrestling world there must be a group of fans who still favor Marauder, as he finished in the top three of theNews initial Hall of Fame voting. Many feel this maybe his last possible run as with over times he has burned many bridges, and many people are not sure if his body has ever truly recovered from all the years on the road including the aforementioned injury at the hands of Exxa Decimal. Marauder couldn’t be reached for comment, but word coming from a respectable source, is noted as saying that Marauder respects Xw for putting together a memorial event for his late cousin Jullian Corteia, known throughout his career as Melee. Whether or not that respect will be translated into Marauder to be on his best behavior is yet to be seen. One thing is known though, if he stays in control and manages to find himself wrestling at the level he did in 2000, bringing in the 24 year old will pay huge dividends for X Wrestling. X Wrestling
