Shane Wethers On The Offensive?

6 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Eternal Punk seems to be on the warpath just when PRIME and eVolution seem to be working to bury the hatchet. Wethers has had enough and is now threatening to raise hell! MORE.. It's been nearly a month since relations between PRIME and eVolution Wrestling broke down in such a spectacular way, with Shane Wethers becoming embroiled in the center of it all. It seems that the Eternal Punk is getting tired of this front office tug-of-war and has decided to target PRIME and Taun Pham himself in order to put this issue to rest. Whethers has been caught several times, both on and off camera boasting about how he will take on Pham and beat him to pulp if he feels like it. Whethers has also spoke of a plan to bring down PRIME although has mentioned no specific details. Whethers of course is reknowned for his ability to get under the skin of anyone he chooses, afterall, they don't call him the Eternal Punk for nothing! While Whethers seems to be pouring oil on the situation, sources reveal that representatives from both PRIME and eVolution have met behind closed doors in order to resolve this dispute before it damages either one or both of the companies. Understandably, neither Pham or Tyler Davidson were present as it's doubtful as to whether the two men could stand in the same room as one another after Davidson quit/was fired from PRIME. The aim is for both promotions to make some sort of mutually acceptable out of court settlement and put this feud to rest in the near future. With that being said, it might be hard to resolve this painlessly if Shane Whethers does indeed go through with his threats towards Pham and the whole of PRIME in general. PRIME
