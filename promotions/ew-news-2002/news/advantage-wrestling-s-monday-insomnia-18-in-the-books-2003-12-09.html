Advantage Wrestling's Monday Insomnia 18 In The Books...

9 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With all that happened on Monday Insomnia people just cant wait to see how things transgress on Tuesday Rage. Monday Insomnia kicked off with a bang as Razor Blade announced that all former champs who had signed on the dotted line would get to keep the belts they had worked so hard on obtaining. With that being said the North American Champion Teemu McCorde came out and picked up his belt and Tag Team Champions A Tendency 2 Start Fires picked up their gold. It was later in the show that Steve Ewing took his X belt and made idle threats at Razor Blade. Razor Blade then booked an X Championship match for the very next week where he must defend his belt against whom many consider to be the franchise of the federtaion, Big Poppa. It now looked as if all former champs had joined up except for the World Champion. Joy Boxster and Gwen battled to a count out as they both picked up a draw in a grueling contest. Blake Cassidy picked up a win over Sean Casey and Cody from T2SF picked up a win over Skylar Clay. Later on in the show it was announced that the main event would turn into an AW first ever Bra N Panties match which gained much appreciation from the crowd as Angel Dawson took on the lovely Sapphire. Angel had Saph teetering on elimination and it was there that we saw she was going "commando" if you will. But a drop to the face and a pair of shorts coming off revealing a thong made Sapphire the winner. It wasn't long after that Supernova did infact show up to take back what was rightfully his... the AW World Championship. Razor brought out the gold and then on the Advantage View a bus rolled up... but not just any bus... a bus that was strikingly familiar to AW past... low and behold Creature and Jack Duncan had made their way to AW again and came inside the arena with a huge entourage' of people. Creach came out to a huge ovation up until the point he challenged Supernova and made statements that he wasnt worthy of being the champ. Supernova didnt take too kindly to these threats and after Creature threw out a challenge for next week on Insomnia, Nova retaliated with asnwering why not just do it tomorrow night on Tuesday Rage. So the match has been made and on Tuesday Rage Nova defends the World Championship against the man from the abyss Creature. All in all I felt this was a great show and great way to kick off Advantage Wrestling but dont take my word for it, go see for yourself as there is much more that I just cant get into at this very moment. Monday Insomnia Announcer & Head Promoter -Sean Levy- Advantage Wrestling
