Official ReV XIX Preview (PRIME)

11 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After the highly charged fifth PPV that was Onslaught, PRIME returns hom from Osaka, Japan bringing another red hot edition of ReVolution programming... PRIME heads back to home territory this Monday after tearing through Osaka for ReVolution in Seattle. With the fall-out from Onslaught expected, the Key Arena is in for a rough ride as the company seems to be becoming more unstable by the week. Headlining the show is newly crowned Global Champion Rock Startling as he faces "The Enigma" Joey Troy in a non-title bout. Despite the gold not being on the line, this is Troys chance to step up to the plate and attempt to defeat the superstar who did what Troy couldn't Sunday night and defeated Ian English. Speaking of Ian English, how will the former Global Noble react after the controversy of the State Penn Match? Britains favorite Anarchist teams up with fellow UK exports and Tag Team Champions, The English Luchadore and Adam as they take on the motley team of Lance Jackson, Shane Wethers and P2 Records Tony Zatarok. The rest of the card offers up the usual dose of PRIME quality with Kincade, Terry Rain in action plus an in ring debut for Ignatius Lisieux. Having proved his Intense Title credentials at Onslaught by defeating Rogue, Black Angel is put up against Triumph Frost who is 2 for 2 since arriving with a brand new, if not damaged, Mercedes Benz to boot. With The First in his sights, Black Angel will be put to the test in this one. If that wasn't enough, Shane Wethers will continue his investigation in an attempt to find out who had him put behind bars in a Chicago set-up. We saw his cousin Huey Lucio attempt to extract some answers from Toshiaki Motoki directly on Sunday Night, but both men discovered they have a lot in common... hired goons. Meanwhile Motoki has his own mini-mystery to solve in trying to discover who sent him that "prank" envelope filled with jellified meat pieces in an attempt to unsettle the co-owner. Also, Hin See promises that he has some "motivation" for Joey Troy, while Alex Caine is tipped to make his first ReVolution appearance in months! Plus Justin "Stormm" Michaels, Mike Wade, Tony Rolo, Steve Starr, Johnny Capone are all expected to attend the event. It all goes down Monday night in Seattle! PRIME
