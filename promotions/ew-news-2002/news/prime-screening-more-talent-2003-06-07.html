PRIME Screening More Talent?

7 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Despite being a relative new kid on the western wrestling block, PRIME owner Taun Pham has been consulting with numerous sources inorder to identify who should be next to join the PRIME family. Reporter Scott "Scoops" Mckannon reviews this weeks key names and events. Just days after Hin See signed up §adi§†ic, PRIME has continued to bolster its ranks with further fresh recruits. So far only Gabriel Ranae has been officially confirmed and unvieled, but several names have been linked with the company. Ranae is billed by many wrestling insiders as the latest cruiserweight prodigy to emerge, although controversy surrounds his origins. "Yes, he swears that he is from the future." Said Pham, half smiling. "He's a very complex individual, but his potential talent is vast and if he believes he is on a quest to save wrestling, then that's good enough for me." Reports from PRIME HQ also suggest that Erin Christoph is currently negotiating a contract with the company with talks being in the advanced stages. Apparently, Tyler Davidson has been very influencial in reccomending Pham recruit Christoph, meeting with the PRIME owner 3 times this week. Davidson and Christoph have a very colored past with each other and it will be interesting to see how they interact if Christoph does indeed sign to PRIME. Meanwhile photographs of Ian English leaving the PRIME offices are circulating around the internet. Rumored to be taken by a fan waiting for autographs, the photos show English hobbling away on crutches, seemingly carrying an injury. English was 8-0 back in $oldout Wrestling, but he has not worked since and many people expect he may never carry out ring work again. Perhaps this weeks ReVolution will shed more light on there rumours. PRIME
