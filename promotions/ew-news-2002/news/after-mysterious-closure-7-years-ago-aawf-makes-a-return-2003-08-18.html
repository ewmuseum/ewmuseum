After Mysterious Closure 7 years ago, AAWF Makes a Return?

18 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The AAWF is returning and rumor has it former CWL World Champion, SVJ is involved, along with the likes of Dane Rivers, Tony Vigilante, Miyagawa, and possibly the Extreme Brothers...although nothing is guaranteed, or higher then a rumor! AAWF Headline #1 The AAWF, Across America Wrestling Federation is set to make a return, after its mysterious closure 7 years ago. The promotion was off to one of the hottest starts that any promotion has ever seen. 30+ Active stars on the roster as of their first event. It seemed like nothing can stop the promotion as it steamrolled to it's first Pay-Per-View, in which had one of the MOST talked about endings. If I could go into more depth then I would, although the rights to the footage, and even the power to speak about the AAWF past is still confidential to all press. Although, We can speak about the old owners, The Danger Family. Jordan Danger, and his son Don Danger did most of the work, while the first Danger Lady sat in the back. So fast forward back to today. AAWF owner family, The Danger's , have "sold" the promotion to 4 former independant wrestling promotion owners. As of right now, that is all the information that the new owners wanted to be released, but that doesn't mean that there isn't any rumors. There are 2 of the next 3 rumors, that I can stand strongly behind. First rumor is as goes... *A Paper is pulled from his own chest pocket, and he begins to read* Roster - AAWF was a promotion of pride for the month that it stood strong as an ox, and from what a reporter heard while being pushed away from a limo, owned by one of the new owners. I can aquire the following information to not be a mislead... He was angry at the site of all the reporters being on his limo (it was next to impossible for him to get out.) and he clearly answered something that a reporter screamed, under his breath, which is the exact wording the reporter told me he heard, and he clearly said "Three Letters, SV....G!"...And yes folks it is ok to say that it was in fact a J he said, although with all the noise, it sounded like a G. And if it was a J, then we can 100% say "Superstar" Vince Jacobs has signed with the AAWF. And then the new owner went on to mumble, "D-something....Ers... and after long hours of thought only one person came to mind... Former ACW Contender, Dane Rivers! So take it or leave it, the new owner was 2 syllables away from saying two main stars have signed with the AAWF! The second rumor is that Tony Vigilante, Miyagawa, and the Extreme Brothers, will also be joining the roster. Although rumors are only rumors, and their is no proof. Although the news site is rated well. And the third, which I can not stand behind all that well , is the Danger Family will have something to do with AAWF. That brings this news story to an end. See you for the next headline. Thank You. AAWF Preview Site
