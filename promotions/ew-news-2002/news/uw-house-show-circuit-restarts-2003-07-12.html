uW House Show Circuit Restarts

12 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Universal Wrestling has started up their infamous house show tours again. uW has always put on a great weekly television show, but the small promotion has become infamous for working awesome "house-show-only" storylines for crowds committed to following the federation. Road Manager Tony Dwight; "We try to offer something to the fan as an alternative to the weekly show. There's entire alternate storylines playing on our road circuit. These fans read transcripts and anticipate our division of uW returning so they can keep up." uW just released it's line-up for the next few weeks before taking their show to Europe for a month. Dwight commented; "We're really excited about going overseas. We have two storylines that the dedicated American House Show devotees will probably be upset we didn't work here so they could have a chance to see it." 07-16 - Ottawa, Ontario, Canada 07-23 - Bangor, Maine European Tour- 08.06 - Toulouse, France 08.06 - Barcelona, Spain 08.20 - Amsterdam, Netherlands 08.27 - Liverpool, England All Tickets available through Ticketmaster or the individual venue. Tickets for each show go on sale 45 days prior to the event. Dwight did comment; "We'll have to make sure the talent and the crew behaves while we're in Amsterdam." he said chuckling. This House Show tour of Europe coincides with a television tour as well. This is the first time uW has ever held a pay-per-view outside the U.S. and the first time in 2 years they have went overseas. Look for Culmination 2 live from London on August 31st. universal Wrestling
