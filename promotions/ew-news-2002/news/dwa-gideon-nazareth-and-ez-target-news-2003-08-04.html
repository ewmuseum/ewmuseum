DWA: Gideon Nazareth and EZ Target News

4 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Gideon Nazareth sees a reunion with an old friend, while EZ Target sees justice served on the men who attacked him. After an attack on EZ Target that sent shockwaves through the DWA, fans and superstars alike were surprised to hear EZ would be pressing no charges. But it seemed that EZ was getting it right-It seemed the attack was a random thing, that EZ was in the wrong place at the wrong time. Well just two days ago, Mr. Dubose, EZ Target’s Business Manager, was the victim of an attack even more heinous and brutal than EZ’s. He was punched several times, thrown into a wall, and had his had smashed into his desk. And the perpetrators of this disgusting crime? The Boyz, the most feared gang in Chicago, and the group of thugs rumoured to be under the attack on EZ. What seemed like a random thing, has turned into something much worse, with fears EZ may be the target of a hired hit man. But now, after brilliant CCTV footage captured the Boyz on tape, it seems that The Boyz won’t be playing around Chicago for some time yet-As they’ll be spending some time in detention. In other news... The first alliance in DWA history has been reinvented, a year after it split up. With this weeks return of Lazlo Ferngelder, all eyes were on him and Gideon Nazareth to see fi their alliance would be reformed. A year and a half ago, coinciding almost with the birth of the DWA, master manipulator Gideon Nazareth and paranoid conspiracy theorist Lazlo Ferngelder began a relationship that carried through the federations first eight months, until injury and paranoia abrubtly ended the partnership. Experts say that had the DWA had Tag Team Titles during this period, hte team of Nazareth and Ferngelder would have been practically unbeatable. Last week, after a nearly year long absence, Lazlo has made his return to DWA television, and has subsequently rejoined his former ally. Notably, on his return, it has been revealed that during his absence, Lazlo has lost an arm, and in it's place has a prosthetic limb with a hook, preventing him from wrestling in the DWA. Never the less, Lazlo's presence with Gideon Nazareth either in or out of the ring could give Gideon the edge over the rest of the DWA roster to regain the wordl title that is so precious to him. Additionally, Lazlo has stated that it is his goal to rekindle his relationship with his now ex-wfie, former DWA world champion Typhoid Mary, who, in his absence, has had two long term relationships with other members of the DWA roster. What will the return of former Television champion Lazlo Ferngelder and his allianc with Gideon Nazareth mean to the DWA? The world will have to wait to see. DWA: Dragon Wrestling Association
