eVolution: More News As The Hiatus Nears An End

23 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
More news out of the eVolution offices as the hiatus nears its end. With Max not on tour this year, this is Shawn Brown with the news and blues of eVo. I will try to break down each part of eVo, breaking down the in ring ability, promo ability, and fan support for many of your eVo superstars. If you were asked 6 months ago who would be in the Faith main event, and what kind of match it would be, I doubt anyone would’ve guessed it. But Shocka and Kimson pulled out all the stops and put on a great match. Keep in mind that their match had to follow the likes of Jay Jameson, Xander, Asis, Thug roC, and a whole cast of great athletes. At first glance, I thought that it was possible that the main event wouldn’t be the best match on the card, but obviously I was wrong. The mixture of Kimson and Shocka, might not be appealing to the eye at first, but great work by both men. The Faith card was filled with unbelievable matches, but maybe most notable was the cage match between Matt Hunt and Aaron Justice. These two guys are at the top of their class by far. In a federation filled with guys who don’t even earn their keep, these two might’ve put on the 2nd best match of the night, behind the main event, and ahead of the ladder match. Without this match, I think Faith would be near disappointing, that’s how good this match was. It was Hunt and Justice, both putting on their best effort in this match, making sure that the eVolved title is no longer looked over. Hunt showing that he has a lot of talent in him, just might be the future of this federation. Coming up, we’re looking at the Road to Glory Tournament. Now last year, it was Shocka becoming the first ever winner, and he went on to win the Universal Title. Even though some say Shocka isn’t as good as he was a year ago, it truly propelled his career. Now this year, there are a large number of guys who could very well take the tournament and the #1 contendership that comes with it. At the eVolution website along with the arenas that eVo has been touring, we conducted a poll of who just might win, here’s what the fans thought. Xander – 32% Asis – 30% Thug roC – 17% Scott Frost – 9% Other – 5% No official winner – 7% Pretty close between Xander, a former champion, and Asis who, many say, would make the perfect buyrate for the next PPV. As many know, Somers’ formula for PPVs are the top-rated heel against the top-rated face, we might see Asis as the overall winner. BUT, what if Somers thinks roC is a better heel, or if Xander is a better athelete? The tournament is up in the air, as there’s no telling what can happen. James Kimson has held on to that Universal Title since the end of August. Many think that he just might hold on to it until next August, him being looked at as the hardest worker in the company. Lets take a look at what he can do… “The Hype” James Kimson… Legends XXX Kimson vs. Christoph- Kimson’s first singles match Kimson was shocked that Christoph had gotten his shoulder up but he quickly made his way back up to his feet and stood back waiting as Christoph slowly got up to his feet. As Christoph finally made it back up again Kimson drove a hard kick right into his midsection and quickly set him up before he delivered the History In The Making and again made the cover. Legends L Kimson vs. Scott Frost – 50 weeks in the making The fans are stunned as Kimson and Frost both try to get to their feet. They both arrive and trade right hands but Kimson gets sent to the ropes and bounces off with a Flying Forearm connecting! Frost is bleeding from the chair shot as his brains were scrambled by that insanely hard shot that took the rest of what he had to kick out of. Kimson grabs Frost up in History in the Making and plants Frost, he covers. Legends LVI Shocka and Thug roC vs. James Kimson and Scott Frost – Tag team hell Shocka is surprised and Kimson has gotten up, being Shocka who doesn’t see him. Shocka turns and SKimson plants a well aimed kick to the gut and then he plants Shocka with History in the Making! The fans burst into cheers. Kimson covers. “The Hype” James Kimson…. As the eVo family tours the world, the fans back in the states filled out polls upon polls, one asking exactly when would Kimson lose his title. Before January – 53% Between January and March – 39% He’ll have it for a year – 5% He’ll retire with it – 3% One title, a lot of guys in their career has at least one title, but is it the title that counts? Tag team titles, mid-card titles, hardcore titles, they all mean different things to different people. But the most sacred title out there, would have to be that Universal Title. A title that means that you are the best that is currently out there. And not many know this, but backstage, where the cameras are not rolling, there are bets and polls on two guys who have never gotten that far in any fed. Asis and Thug roC. Both are very talented, and both are very skilled in what they do. Asis, a veteran with companies built by Daniel S., and Thug roC, who is no stranger to Somers, competing against him in $W. Now, Thug roC is a veteran to federations by Starr Inc, a company lead by Sebastian Starr. In those federations, Thug roC won the JeW Cruiserweight and Bronx titles. He also won the $W Tag team, Cruiserweight, and National Titles. Asis’ history looks something like this, Rising Star champion, 2 time tag team champion, continental champion, all under eVo. So we all know what that means. Asis is one title away from an eVo Grand Slam title, while Thug roC is one title away from a career Grand Slam title. Who will get to their goal first? It all starts in a few weeks when the Road To Glory Tournament officially starts. A tale of big men has been set after this statement: “When you step into a cell and fight your way through the other side, that’s when you’re bad. But a little rotator cup injury put the punk on the shelf. I’m the baddest this company has ever seen, no doubts. Look at Shane Whethers, his punk ass left the company after I handed his ass to him. Sovereign, heh, nothing but a punk.” That was the big man Machine who has been acting as Thug roC’s bodyguard until the writing staff can find something for him. Meanwhile, Sovereign isn’t too far from return, as he has finally been cleared for minor in ring bumps and workouts. Justin Fritz has been Sov’s trainer thus far, although that can change. There is no set date nor time frame for his return, it could be tomorrow or 3 months from now. There’s no telling on how damaged his ring rust and timing is, so we all can only sit back and wait. Scott Frost, under went surgery on his neck. As informed previously, Scott was diagnosed with a severe pinched nerve and critical fracture to his neck. Dr. Jho, who has preformed many styles of neck surgery on many superstars from World Wrestling Entertainment, such as: Kurt Angle, Lita, Edge, and Chris Benoit. Jho went on record stating that 'the surgery was very successful, and the nerve is now relaxed. The fracture was quickly replaced with a new more flexible plate and his recovery will not be long. Scott is a very healthy, and strong competitor. I am glad that I can help the life of an superstar such as himself.' Scott Frost, will be resting in bed for at the minimum of a week and a half to get adjusted to life after the surgery. He will be released and sent back home, and within two to three weeks time will be ready for the long road of rehabilitation. On behalf of the staff of eVolution, we wish him the best of luck and cannot wait until his return back into eVolution. eVolution
