ACW Championship Up For Grabs?

19 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ICU gone... ??? in! As of ACW's last show, ACW World Champion ICU had left the federation, dropping the belt on his way out. Two title have been left to the wide open, one being the Television title, dropped by then-champion Alias on an earlier date, and the World title dropped recently. The federation is now leaving to speculation as to who will be considered for the honor of fighting for the vacant championships. Some say that Osyrus, a former two-time World Champion himself, would be chosen. Others have claimed the former TV Champ, Alias, as the best man for the spot. Many have been claiming that new-comer "Superstar" Vince Jacobs will be the man to walk up to the plate... along with other popular choices being the manic-depressive Jason Kain, the strangely unstable Quinton May, and even quite possibly the stunning Jade Greene. Still others are thinking a new approach is going to be taken... But with the board of directors behind closed doors our sources are not even sure that we'll come away from ACW's Glory with a champion. Further news will be brought up if and when it arrives. http://acw.ewmania.com
