WE'RE BAAAAACK...

9 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Turmoil came back to its regularly scheduled time with a blast, and ended with a shock. Turmoil came back to its regularly scheduled time with a blast, and ended with a shock. Right off the bat we were introduced to two new friends of now-incarcerated Ron Starr in Richard James and Alan Zoober, two cronies who seem to stop at nothing to achieve but the complete downfall of PCW, and put a little money in their pockets in the process. They made it clear that yes, indeed, the one and only Meanstreak would be in attendence, no questions ask. Did they follow through on their promise? You’ll see… Terlayne Sterling started off on the wrong foot by getting under the nerves of “The Buzz” Cal Roberts, who in return attempted to put the rookie in his place, and when things got heated up, security was there to immediately break things up. A shame… As far as the damage to Robert’s vehicle goes, his insurance will probably pay for the damage. Arson then gave Shaun Styles a congrats on his victory last week, and an earful, too. In their PCW debut, PWA newcomers Avery Vance and the Irishman Terlayne Sterling were both in a treat, coming in the form of fellow PWAer Donavan Emerson, who also seems to have been signed to PCW. Terlayne picked up the victory, but “The Buzz” made an appearance too, with neither one really getting the last laugh. But guess what? They’ll be settling this in the ring, come next Turmoil… After RAH tried to cheer up the boss, unsuccessfully, I might add, PCW’s acting president and favorite redneck “The Malice Man” Duke Williams had a few choice words for Dick and Zoober, while Bungle carried on…well, “bungling” out back. Sandy Wong, last known to be dead at the hands of a violent Violence Jack made her return to the side of her ex, and PCW’s reigning World Champion, Skylar Green, and the two seemed to patch up the past. Next, Zoober and Dick were back at their troublemaking again, making their plans clear to destroy Skylar Green later on in the night, where he was to have a brutal handicap match, compliments of Ron Starr and Co. And on the way, did they turn The One Who Rocks against our president? Only time will tell… Shaun Styles took on Cyrus Raynes in a singles match, with both looking great, the rookie Raynes taking it to the veteran, but the fun was cut short due to the backstabbing tendencies of Brian Arson who was looking for revenge on Styles. But it’s not over by a long shot. Ask Styles, he’ll tell ya… Meet Dick Weed, another PCW businessman, and another dickhead. Bent on ending the reign of our champ, he was overheard speaking treason on a cell phone, compliments of a thrifty cameraman, who, in return was fired for his bravery. But would Sky, with his connections with the Malice Man, fix things up? Alexander Pryce made his in-ring debut. Well, kind of… Bringing his mother…yes, that’s right, his mother to the ring, he “treated” the audience to his luscious body, padded his flamboyant ego, and yes, strutted his prickish attitude with a promise to bring his lovely singing talents to PCW, and indeed, improve the hygiene of both PCW wrestlers and fans alike. Ah yes, next was the much awaited, much commented on brawl between Jay Phoenix, a veteran of the wrestling world, looking to finally raise his stock in Primetime, who took on the unlikely team of Awfa and Bungles, PCW’s fatman and faggot, respectively. (Excuse the language, for purposes of entertainment, of course.) It started off bad for the Native of Arizona with Jay being pummeled by the Somoan monster. But could he come back? Well, we’ll never really know as problems between the team of Awfa and Bungle lead to the climatic point where PCW’s favorite gay teddy bear, Bungle, turned on his partner, costing them the match. But was it worth the hug he was later to receive from Phoenix? And did he get the man’s rainstick going? Who knows, who knows… Anyway, after the match Skitzo, not usually so even tempered as at least somewhat seem, went over the edge, threatening and badmouthing his tag partner for later on in the night, Rent-A-Hero, who in return…well, probably forgot about it, saved a few lives, and played a bit of Playstation 2. Next up, meet Bobby Agg, Skitzo’s new shadow. Yes, that’s right. New shadow. Via orders from the man himself, our president Duke Williams, ordered a man to follow and monitor all actions of Skitzo for the next few weeks in PCW, at least until things calm down. With things riding on the edger lately, Duke feels that he can’t take the chance of…oddly enough…a Sktizo going over the edge. Go figure? A confusing scene came next with Bungle being beaten down in the ring by a new figure, Pastory Troy, while attempting to reveal nude photos of Judgement, which, for your information, will be selling on e-Bay… (Which I know you want to know.) Ah, the tag match came next with the freak team of the black Brit, Michael Jacques, and homemade redneck Abner taking on the even more unlikely team of Skitzo and Rent-A-Hero. Things started off in a flash, with the schizophrenic horrendously beating down the redneck, But Abner came back. But after Skitzo tagged in RAH (after he also tagged in himself…) Abner was down for the count again, and again, PCW’s resident redneck, came back from the dead… But it wasn’t so… Upon suggestion from Jacques that Abner’s an alleged “manwitch,” things got a little out of hand, with RAH seemingly turning on his fellow face, granting it was a wrestling match. But in the end Jacques the last laugh, rolling up the superhero from behind and getting the count. After the match, at a bar across the street, exhausted and angered Abner managed to get himself into even more trouble via the kidnapping hands of two homosexuals and a bouncer in a plan pressed by his archrival, Michael Jacques. The PFIK made his triumphant return with a somewhat awkward interview with Skylar Green, followed by the ending drama of PCW’s redneck…who now has a tattoo on his ass reading “I Love Mark,” and yes, it’s a long, long story… And finally, the exhilarating night ended with an engaging, exciting main event, by the end of which the fans were all on their feet, some cheering, some booing… Skylar green tried to get the upper-hand on the threesome of Sebastain Toomes, OSW’s first World Champion, and the tag team of Entertainment Extreme, consisting of Gargano and Preston. But soon, he received the short end of the stick, just as Ron Starr arranged it, but not before getting in a few shots. But wait! Hold the phone! INPHINO BLITZ! Yes, OSF legend and longtime master of mayhem Inphino Blitz made his PCW debut, taking out the threesome with one massive strike. Bodies were flying everywhere, and soon, the Malice Man was forced to put the perspective parties in their proper places… But not before the arrival of legendary OSW/PWCer, Propaganda! He made his way to the ring, soon wreaking havoc on the unsuspecting victims, including PCW’s very own president! And PCW went off the air, ending one of its bets shows in months, with its shocking announcement by Dick and Zoober, that indeed, Duke Williams may in fact be forced legally resign his reign as PCW’s president… PCW!
