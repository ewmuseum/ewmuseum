DWA's New Owner Speaks Out!

5 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just moments after screwing the owner of the DWA, Mason Stone, out of it, new owner, Jordan Brannigan had some things on his mind. The DWA has gone through a lot in the year and a half it's been open, but on Sunday night, it went through the biggest change the DWA has ever seen, because at Hostile Takeover, Jordan Brannigan defeated Gideon Nazareth to steal the ownership of the DWA from Mason Stone. However, if he had beaten him fair and square, it would be big news, but this was not the case. Jordan had apparantely orchestrated one of the biggest screw jobs in the history of wrestling, by pulling in Gideon Nazareth and commentator Norville Matthews, in a conspiracy that goes back months. In a press conference held after the pay per view, Jordan had some strong words for his critics. "Fuck you," Jordan said, with a sly smile across his face. The smile has been permanently on his face ever since he saw Mason Stone taken away in the back of an ambulance after the severe attack he received at the hands of Gideon Nazareth. "The DWA is mine to do with as I please. If anyone pisses me off, they won't be fired, because I wouldn't dream of letting them run-off to another fed, but no, they'll be forced to sit-out the remainder of their contract, until they disappear from the lime light. "If you don't believe me, get in the unemployment line behind Lee Tanaka. He didn't like the way things went down tonight, and he said so. So he's packing to head back to Japan, seeing being fired automatically revokes his work visa." Jordan went on with his comments, drawing even some die hard wrestling reporters to get pissed off at his comments, but then his tone changes rather suddenly. "But Mason Stone was wrong about one thing. My goal is not to destroy the DWA. I have acheived my goal. The DWA is mine, Mason is gone, and he will never return, but with the DWA being mine, I plan to make it bigger than even Mason could have imagined it. If the wrestlers want to whine about being dupped, they need to get over it, because I already have a list of wrestlers that need to shape-up or they're going to be shipped out permanently. "This business is about success. Those that have it are called winners, those without it are unemployeed. At the moment, I am all about success, and the DWA will be to. The DWA is about to be taken for a ride to the top, and one way or another, everyone that is coming on board is going to help...whether it by them succeeding, or by them plummetting back to whatever hole they came from." And the ride has already started. With a week off, the DWA returns to it's original Monday night home on a brand new network, FX. And, in their second hour, they will be competing directly with pro-wrestling big wigs, WWE and FWO. Jordan has promised that the DWA will become edgier, and many people point to the latest pay per view as a good sign of where the DWA will be heading. Whether that's a good or bad thing is something that only time will be able to tell. DWA: Dragon Wrestling Association
