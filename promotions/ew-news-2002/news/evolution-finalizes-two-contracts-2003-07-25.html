eVolution Finalizes Two Contracts

25 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just before Legends XLIV aired earlier today, eVolution finalized the contracts of two new stars to the company. http://evolution.theewe.net/images/evo-ewnews.jpg After days of negotiations eVolution came to terms with a superstar that they have long sought after since their days as part of the Fedwars organization. Today on the day of Legends XLIV it had been confirmed that eVolution had finally come to an agreement with Shane Wethers, the former Eternal Wrestling and $oldout Wrestling superstar and one of many talents that was left homeless following the closure of PCW a few weeks back. Wethers had entered negotiations with eVolution in the past as one of the biggest free agents in Fedwars but he had instead chose to sign with FdW, where he had quite a run-in with another current eVolution star Morpheus Omega, but FdW closed shortly afterwards and while Omega headed straight to eVolution, Shane found his way slowly over to Primetime Championship Wrestling. Shane was by many accounts a rapidly rising star in PCW and was on the verge of big things when he was sidetracked with an injury and then was on his way back to the ring when PCW came to an untimely end. Shane remained a free agent and searched the market for several weeks where he came close to signing a deal with PRIME but instead at the last minute it seems signed a deal with eVolution. Shane does have a history with several people in eVolution including the owner Tyler Davidson, as the two men had a very heated war while in $oldout Wrestling together. Whether the history there played any part in the decision is unknown but it should also be noted that it was only a couple of weeks ago when the story broke about Tyler Davidson having walked out on PRIME and didn't have very many kind words for the company afterwards. Wethers did make an appearance of sorts on Legends this week but he is definitely scheduled to appear this Sunday night at eVolution’s ReVelations pay per view event in Manchester, England. What role he will play there is still unknown at this time. As though that signing wasn’t enough for the day, eVolution also finalized the contract of another newcomer. Robert Frost, the brother of two time eVolution Universal Champion Scott Frost, has also signed his contract with eVolution and is expected to begin work for the company immediately. Scott Frost’s time on camera has dwindled over the past several months reportedly due to injuries but he is expected soon to make a full time return to action along with his fiancée Seara who has already returned. There is also no word yet on just what role Robert Frost will be playing when he debuts though judging by a promo aired on Legends there will certainly be some immediate connection made between the two brothers. eVolution Legends XLIV
