RaGE: Finale and Yet, The Beginning

30 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The final press release has been posted by Real and Graphic Entertainment. Now, the company is looking towards January 9 and the start of the 2004 Season. The full card is available now and things kick off this weekend on the website. Never before has Real and Graphic Entertainment created this much of a stir before a show. Perhaps, the new functioning way of the company and structure of just fifteen shows for the year has people buzzing, thinking it has to be good. Or, it may just be the time taken to construct the way the company will function this year. Whatever the reason is, Real and Graphic Entertainment has been working hard to come clean on its promise to deliver great wrestling each month to fans. Of course, it is hit or miss for the company in 2004 with New Dawn on January 9, and everyone knows just that, which is why they have been working extremely hard in preparing for it. For the past two months, the company has been releasing press conferences on the site and informing fans about the upcoming season, trying to get them exciting for what has been on the brink of returning. With a new roster, new format, new style and presentation, many are already confident that Real and Graphic Entertainment will excede the 2003 year, its predecessor. But no matter what has you looking at Real and Graphic Entertainment, the New Dawn main event involving two heated rivals, who just a few months ago were embracing in the middle of the ring, is another intriguing reason to be watching. Shawn Kain's jealousy continues and he will unleashed his team on Pat Dwyer in what is being called 'The Crucible' at New Dawn on January 9. After the long awaited announcement, the teams for the two men have been announced and some shocking past rivalries will be reunited inside the Crucible in less than two weeks. For more on New Dawn, the Crucible and its teams, and Real and Graphic Entertainment visit: SE-Live.com Real and Graphic Entertainment
