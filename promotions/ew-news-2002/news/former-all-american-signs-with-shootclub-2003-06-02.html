Former All-American Signs With Shootclub

2 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Former New York City football standout Sean Moss now known as Jae'Hood signed a developmental contract with the well-known ShootClub at a press-confrence in New York City. Jae' was a well known linebacker for Saint John's Private School and a highly touted NCAA recruit. After legal issuses forced Jae' to forfeit his NCAA eligibility the former All-American vanished from the face of the earth. Rumor has it that Jae' was once a a underground fighting legend called by followers of the sport "Gods Hands". A statement was released by Jae' today in which he had this to say "I will not reject nor accept the rumors circulating about me being some God of an underground fighter but I do have this to say. I'm the man once known as Sean Moss. I'm the man now known as Jae'Hood. I have always excelled in everything I have went in pursuit of in my life. That's why I signed with ShootClub. Im not concerned about my well publicized legal woes, nor the various rumors. the only thing I am concerned with is climbing to the top of the Pro-Wrestling ladder. To all the promoters and CEO's " I'm Money In The Bank". To all my wrestling oppoents " I'm a problem ! And you can take that to the bank and cash it." ShootClub
