Starblazer to Return Soon

23 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With the return of the once successful wrestling company EYE Phoenix Rose has decided to return to in ring action to defend his Gold championship against the likes of other returning superstars such as Makai, "Urban Warfare" Carl Dee and current Platinum champion, Shirata Rinjiro. Phoenix Rose, who has been out of action for around six months now, announced his return to the ring in a press conference in his hometown of Tampa Bay, Florida earlier this morning. Rumors are circulating that Phoenix Rose’s contract may extend past in-ring action. An article in the latest edition of Pro Wrestling Illustrated hinted at a staff position for the current EYE Gold Champion with a substantial wage involved. Whether these rumors are true or not have yet to be confirmed by either the company or Phoenix Rose himself. During the press conference early this morning Phoenix Rose stated: “I am looking forward to working off my ring rust and getting back in to action against some of my old colleagues along with some fresh new talent too. It should be a great experience for all involved.” For updates on the specifics of Phoenix Rose’s contract be sure to check on the official EYE website or return here to EW-News.com for the most up to date news as and when it becomes available.
