NWC:A...Atlantic Action Replay

14 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Atlantic Action Replay 6/14/03 Atlantic Action Replay FIIIIIINALLY.... This column has had some life put into it and is about to roll into action. Who am I? What is this all about? Well let me answer both of those right now. I’m PJ Smith and this column is going to be dealing with various bits and pieces that are NWC: Atlantic related, thoughts and opinions from a fan’s eye view. Some things people will agree with, some they won’t, some may get an assassin hired to kill me, others might want to give me the freedom of their city! It’s all about opinions and kick starting chatter and talk about the good, bad, ugly and fun things that have been going on through the week. Seeing as the June 15th Maelstrom is about to air, let’s go back in time some to the last edition of the show. You couldn’t go far away from your TV set without seeing some razor sharp verbal battling from the various stars of the Atlantic crew. Being a wrestling addict, yep you guessed it, I saw them all! So I thought it was time to give myself the hardest job imaginable and pick out my “Video Of The Week”. What got me hooked to the screen and finally stopped me snacking on my Cheese Puffs, no folks, NOT Cheesy Poofs! Video Of The Week: Crash And Burn – "Friend And Brother" Every single big gun was in action last week, including the names like Morgan, Jake Summers, Leonard Aarons III and more. However, there was something about the Crash And Burn video that really grabbed me. A very deep and poignant ending reflecting on Memorial Day and it’s significance which even for someone who may not be familiar with what Memorial Day is about, cannot help but make you think. This team in themselves have been a strong and consistent force in the world of the NWC from as far back as I can remember. This weeks video cannot fail but to demonstrate why. It served up your usual slice of wrestling trash talking but also went for a serious slant on a real life event, which for me made it stand out. It’s important to add that there were some strong efforts all the way through the roster last week, but in my mind, “Friend And Brother” stood out as something just that little bit different. Anyone that can stop me snacking for five minutes, has to be good! One of a couple of fine wins last week came when Nathan Boswell retained his New England Title against Vinny Azzaro. For me, Boswell is heading up to become the Next Big Thing, a cliché that is used on a regular basis, but in this case I think it’s true. Azzaro is one very tough customer but Boswell is a fighting champion and took the challenge head on. Whilst Nate may not be the fastest of guys in the race, his awesome size and power really make it hard for an opponent to get the advantage. Personally, I’d love to see him take on Morgan for the East Coast Championship. Mind you, Bobby Irish may have something to say about me putting his main squeeze in that fantasy match up! Lockdown. What was that all about? It certainly offered a new complexion on wrestling. I couldn’t help but think as I was watching, where is that steady and reliable form of violence we’re all used to? There was something just really odd about all those guards wandering around, making sure everything was above board. I really would suggest that people keep Joey Long away from that whacky stuff, because I’m kinda worried about what he’s going to come up with next! Maybe next week he’ll move Maelstrom to Alcatraz and put everyone in their own cells?! The only Lockdown I approve of is one where I’m locked in a room with Vicki Vixen and she’s.....Ahem. Women drivers. I know there is that sexist theory that men are supposed to be better drivers than women. Although from looking at the way The Amazing Rando’s ex girlfriend was driving, is it any wonder that men think that? In fact, I’m beginning to agree! Sheryl was randomly charging at people trying to kill them because she thought they were The Amazing Rando himself. I’ve heard of being driven mad by your ex, but that’s just ridiculous! Put her driving license and keys in the same locked box as Joey Long’s whacky backy please. A fond farewell then to Jay Draven, gone but not forgotten. How about Leonard Aarons III though? We’ve got him on our screens for the foreseeable future with his Ratings Express. I know, I’ve had an idea! Maybe we should appoint Sheryl as the driver of the Express, she could be Aaron’s chauffeur! Killing two birds with one stone. Leonard Aarons is a mean, angry bastard with the cockiness required to make him the very pinnacle of Atlantic. The NWC:A seems to be breeding some very dangerous people these days, what with the rising emergence of Damien Kahn and other young entrants. Maybe they’re taking tips off Aarons? All I can say is, that is one focussed Express and I can’t help but think that certain people really need to be concerned now that he’s focussed again after disposing of Draven. Heads up, train with an insane driver coming through! And finally in this edition, congratulations to Morgan who won the East Coast Title in the big main event. I have to confess to needing a damn score card to try and keep track of all the title changes that went on in that one. I think it was a no brainer that Ian Paul Freely wasn’t going to come out of it with the belt. As soon as you see Jake Summers as your first opponent, you know you’ve had it. A man that probably made a bit of a mistake was Marc Smith. Sure he did beat Jake Summers, taking the title off him and kicking him out of the game, but a blindside attack followed by an arrogant cover, is that really wise? I’m so not convinced! Smith’s reign lasted some while (relatively speaking) after a ‘successful defence’ against Ethan Arcane, no relation to the South’s Rictor I might add. With two wins in one night, you probably couldn’t blame Arcane for feeling that little bit cocky. However, when you’re met by a former North American Champion in the form of the unpredictable/offbeat Amazing Rando, you can probably forget about it. Which exactly what happened. Smith’s reign was ended by the Atlantic’s resident hero to the masses! There is however, no hero strong enough to deal with the package that is Morgan. She’s a psyche out for most people and her ability to boot is a lethal combination. A deserved champion who got her chance to see how things panned out, then slip in the back door (pardoning the expression) to get the duke and the gold. I would rather not contemplate the night-long celebrations that went on with her and Irish though! No, no and no! Now Vicki Vixen in Lockdown...that’s more like it! Well, fellow Altantic watchers, I think the time is nigh where I say goodnight from me. I shall be back next time where we find out whether Jake Summers can get revenge on Marc Smith for that rather ‘low’ elimination from the East Coast scene and keep his Heavyweight gold, whether the high flying Polar Opposites can pull one more out of the bag and wrest the Tag Titles from Dominion, can Heatwave upset Morgan and take away her newly won prized possesion – the East Coast Championship, plus what will happen when the two volatile forces that are Damien Kahn and Nathan Boswell, clash for the New England Championship? Another big week looming folks, keep it locked on NWC:Atlantic, I know I am! God bless, be lucky. P.J NWC: Atlantic
