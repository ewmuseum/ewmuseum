LMS Warmup next on RTV

12 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With less than two weeks away before GWO's first pay per view of 2004, Last Man Standing, owner ukBill has set up a huge card for this weeks Rampage TV. In less than two weeks away, GWO's first pay per view of the new year will hit the airwaves on January 25,2004. Coming off last weeks RTV, the final three members have been set for the main event at LMS to go up against GWO World Champion DOA in a four way elimination match. Three of the four men will be participating in a LMS Warmup match, as the boss has dubbed it. Also, the GWO TV title and Xtreme titles will be on the line. A triple threat street fight and tables match also on the bill. Here is the rundown of this weeks Rampage TV. (RTV) ------------ Non-Title Triple Threat Match DOA VS. Tony Mastermind VS. Trainer Jim ------------ TV Title Match Mike Mitchell © VS. Layne Walker ------------ Table Match Mike Andrews (w/ Ron Jacobs) VS. Twisted ------------ Xtreme Title Match (No DQ) J.C. Sharp © VS. Blaze Bomber ------------ Singles Match Dirty Dawg VS. Antwon Young ------------ Triple-Threat Street Fight Ghetto Munky VS. John Malvarack VS. Zach Rossmiller ------------ Those are the matches for this weeks edition of RTV. RTV airs every Friday only on GNN. G-dubbyah-O
