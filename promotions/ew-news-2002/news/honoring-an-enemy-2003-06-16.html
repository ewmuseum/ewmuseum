Honoring An Enemy

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After their clashes took them over the edge, Mike Meyhu and Trigger may be forming a new relationship after both of their careers have ended. In The End. In the End of a brutal battle, not only was blood spilt, not only were bones broken, not only was a winner proclaimed, but in the end, only one man stood on his two feet, and left the other one with no choice: Never to be seen in LOW again. The original chairman of LOW drove the tyrant that was Mike Meyhu out of Lights Out Wrestling, with contribution of his apprentice, Tristan Belmont, in the greatest battle of all times, a Warrior Match. A distraught Trigger left the ring after, saying he had to leave, and would never return, under pretext that HE, had not ended the war alone. Right before he left the arena though, it has recently been revealed that Trigger did one last bid of business, one last sign of respect to a man that had haunted him for so long. Trigger inducted the name of his grand nemesis, Mike Meyhu, into the Hall of Fame, making the Number One Brawler in the Business, the second inductee into the prestige that is the LOW Hall of Fame. Along with Lotus, Mike Meyhu’s name will be immortalized for ages to come. Lotus, and now Mike Meyhu, both names that will never return to a LOW ring, but both names will never be forgotten by old and new fans alike, are now immortalized. Reasoning behind Trigger’s actions were unclear, though we think it is safe to say that Mike Meyhu, by hook or by crook has been a legend here in LOW, and has done every thing that must’ve been done to be a Hall of Famer even more. Lotus? Mike Meyhu? They will be forever remembered as gods before men Lights Out Wrestling
