Fighting Feds Threatened by Congress

5 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Today, bill H-1136 was proposed on the floor of the House of Representatives. H-1136, better known as the anti-fighting bill, has been met with stiff legislation from various groups such as the UFC, PRIDE, King of the Cage and OSF. The bill would require all fighters to wear 8 oz. gloves, headgear, mouthpieces and footpads. OSF is Ready to Lead the Fight Against Congress 'Fighting is barbaric and we don't need that sort of garbage where our children can see it,' said L. Brent Bozell III at a press conference after the bill was introduced. The leader of the Parents' Television Council went on to say that all fighters were 'mongoloids' and 'almost as bad as those stupid wrestlers.' Shawn Stewart, a prominent member of Old School Entertainment's fighting division was prepared for these outbursts. 'First, by eliminating a forum in which hand to hand combat can be tested and improved upon. Without such a forum, law enforcement agencies will suffer, as will the armed forces.' Stewart also lashed out against Bozell's personal attacks. 'For him to refer to me as a barbarian, mongoloid or idiot shows how close-minded and truly ignorant Mr. Bozell is. In fact, I am going to take great pleasure in making sure that this bill is defeated. I will not leave Washington until this bill is defeated. It's not anyone's place to do parenting for bad parents and by creating this job, my very livelihood is in danger. Heaven forbid that my dreams survive.' The bill is set for a vote in the House on the morning of September 1st, and in the Senate that same afternoon. OSF news will bring you more information as it develops.
