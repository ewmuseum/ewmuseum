Survivor III: Former Gold Team Shares Same Feelings.

21 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As the remaining teams in the Survivor contest head into the final leg of the contest, the former members of the Gold Team open up about their feeling over the merger and the possibilities that they may be the next target in the contest. fWo Survivor III As the clock ticks away to the first challenge facing the newly merged Survivor teams, those that remain are fending off nerves as they await word of what to expect next. For the four teams that make up the former Gold Team, nerves are just the tip of the iceberg. One team was heard saying, "Oh yeah, I'm afraid of what happens if we don't win the first immunity. I know the [former Green Team] are licking their chops as they eye the bullseye on our chest. It's almost sick." Another team agreed, "I was hoping that we'd be back to even by now. Instead of still being down five green team members to four gold. It may be every man for himself, but that really won't be the case until they've cast us off." The Gold Team amassed an impressive late run, closing the record gap of four-to-one to four-to-three. If nothing else, this could be what saves them from falling victim to overwhelming numbers. "We hope it doesn't come down to that." Survivor III Official, Twitch, stated upon leaning of the Gold Team's sudden, vocal paranoia. "That's the whole idea behind 'every team for themselves'. We can't control how these guys vote, but we hope that it doesn't come to that." "Look at what they did after the shuffle." Another team added, "Look at who they singled out with their votes: the two Gold Team groups they picked up. And we still have one of their own left, so really, it's six on three." "You can't trust anyone now. That's the general purpose of the merger, they don't know what's coming next." said Thumper, a Survivor III representatives. "We could come at them with a challenge an alliance would totally hinder." And the teams concluded with, "It makes you wonder if they're patting you on the back because they think you're a great guy and want to be your friend; or if it's because they're marking their spot for the knife?" Come voting time, will the former Green Team prove this wild theory correct, or is all in the Gold Team's head? Only time with tell. fWo Survivor III Online Site
