Jessica Takes fWo Leave of Absense

12 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“It was supposed to run through Meltdown, or at least right before it.” Those were the words of Chris Shepherd in response to Jessica/ Kali Saturos/ .Three departing early from the program that she’d been running with Deacon, Areumna’derin Rahl, & Crucifix. “We were initially asked to work the program by Jessica, and then we wanted to throw in 2 more people to allow for a tag team battle for the big show before Meltdown. The initial plan was to run a ‘LegionWars’ concept with Deacon being the foil to troubled individuals with Multiple Personality Disorder. That was an obvious play on the biblical story of Jesus and Legion. Crucifix fit in perfectly since he was freshly returning and looking for a way to get re-involved with the fWo. They had a lot of plans, but then Jessica’s real life problems began to take over.” This, of course, is in reference to Jessica no-showing at a few smaller appearances. In another case of real life invading art, Jessica began having emotional problems backstage, possibly due to the overly negative response from the fans. Those situations escalated backstage until fWo management had to step in and give her some time off to “find her smile”. This happened a few short weeks after Tim Veneno (Rahl) received orders from his doctor to limit his ringwork, further hampering the program. No word on why this order was given. “It’s definitely not what we initially planned, but with David (Crucifix) still eager to work this program to some sort of conclusion, and Tim (Rahl) playing a limited role, I think we can put together a solid story that will keep the fans entertained. So, the Legion Wars never transpired, but I believe the Cross that Deacon must bear will likely keep people just as fascinated.” fWo Wrestling
