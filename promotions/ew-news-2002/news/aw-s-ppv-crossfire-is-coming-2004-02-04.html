AW's PPV Crossfire is coming!

4 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Will new champions be crowned... and who will win THE RUMBLE!? Advantage Wrestling's PPV event Crossfire will be coming up on Sunday February 8th. The event will include all three title belts being put up on the line for grabs. Will the newly formed tag team of Brad Daddy and Sean Page be able to top T2SF and take home the tag gold? Viper has a huge task ahead of him as he takes on Big Poppa for the X-Title championship. Most people say that Viper is in way over his head for this one, however he is determined and you cannot question his heart. This will be a true battle of guts and courage. Perhaps the closest match of the night could occur when Teemu Stauch McCorde puts his North American title on the line against a tenacious Jake Keeton, and the man who speaks to the devil himself... Chazz Irvine. Can McCorde hold off these two men who are going to be giving all they have for the gold, or will one of his opponents catch him offguard? The question of the night may be "Just how good is Supernova?". Nova has set the record for holding the World Title longer than any previous champion in AW history, will that streak be continued or will he lose it to a girl? Sapphire says that she has Nova's number and we don't mean telephone number. She believes she has the formula to bring down the top man in AW right now. If Creature has any say in this however, he will be the one walking out of the arena wearing the most prestigious title in AW around his waist. This is certain to be a match of epic proportions. Could it be a dawning of a new era for a new World Champion??? Last but not least AW will feature "The Rumble". Rules are simple... be the last one left in the ring, when all the other opponents have been eliminated over the top rope. The superstars entered into the Rumble will be Slippy, The Vine, Viper, Mike Thump, Tomorrow Man, Chris Rickter, Brian Richman, Alexander Realms, Sean Page, Teemu McCorde, Jake Keeton, Chazz Irvine, Ed Gesumaria, Josh Gesumaria, Julianna Guiliano, Steve Ewing, Brad Daddy, Jack Duncan, Jack Diamond, Stone Devane, Angel Dawson, Mark Adkins, Skylar Clay, Caution, Cody, Complicated, and Big Poppa as well as up to 3 mystery superstars. Who could they be, and better yet who will walk out victorious!? Tune in Sunday to find out, ONLY on PPV!!! Advantage Wrestling
