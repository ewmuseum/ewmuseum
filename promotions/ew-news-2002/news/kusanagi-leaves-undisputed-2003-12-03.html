Kusanagi Leaves Undisputed

3 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kusanagi departs with a good promotion hopes in working with Prime Wrestling in the near future which is looking unclear as for right now Kusanagi draws the end of the line with Undisputed after it's down time. He has said it not to be there fualt but the down time of the promotion couldn't keep him happy as he had alot of time awaiting the change in UNDISPUTED hoping in some way to preform for them as a company more then just those he worked with, and in other events some would only see this as him trying to become more of a " Man of Wrestling " un told future this young superstar may have he indeed is going to wrestle with or with out a promotion. " I wish i could do more with undisupted, but i have nothing i can do far as just wrestle and i don't know how far Undisputed will go with the slim roster and slim staff roster. " Said a half upset kusanagi but he is drawing his skills onto the federation Prime Wrestling. Another qoute taken form Kusanagi while being interview was " I wish to some way contact those at the Prime Wrestling promotion and get into there family of wrestlers and earn my way thru to a title shot of some time, even if i have to wrestle bottom cards and opening matches, long as i know i will be covered promotion wise i shall work ". The 19 year old will be hoping to work for a diffrent promotion for a long period of time once signed and is very egar to work.
