Fox to Re-Enter the Wrestling Business?

13 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Following strong showings by the fWo on ABC, former host of Wednesday Warfare rumored to be looking for a wrestling replacement Since Fox kicked off the fWo's flagship Wednesday Warfare program many months ago, there has been much confusion at the offices of the TV giant. They've watched the fWo move to ABC and make a steady climb in the ratings with Monday Night Ruahh~!!, to the point where their ratings are MUCH higher than they were for Warfare. "Some of us TV executives are pretty worried." an inside source told us. "The higher-ups have been complaining that the fWo and wrestling wasn't the problem, as was originally thought, but maybe Fox as a network mis-handled it. The very strong ratings the fWo has had since moving to ABC certainly make an arguement for that theory." Fox has grown increasingly worried about their Monday night programming- as if enough fans weren't getting stolen away to cable's WWE RAW, which, despite falling ratings, is still a cable powerhouse- ABC has been rapidly increasing its ratings on Monday nights since they added the fWo to their line-up. And while Fox has done okay with summer hit OC and now the baseball playoffs, word is that no one wants to discuss what happens when the playoffs are over, now that the OC is moving to a new night. "We can barely fend off Ruahh~!! now, with baseball playoffs!" the same source said. "The fact is, no one wants to think of what will happen once we're left to regular series- ABC will be crushing us in the Monday ratings. And historically, other forms of counter-programming just don't work against wrestling- wrestling fans are generally loyal to wrestling. This is a lesson we've learned well over the years when other networks have tried to counter-program Warfare when we still aired it. It just doesn't work." So, word is out of Fox that they are looking to take the "fight fire with fire" approach- they are currently looking to add wrestling back to their schedule, more than likely in time for the mid-season switches. The thought is that if they can find a wrestling product hot enough to compete with the fWo (and, indirectly, the WWE) on Monday nights, they can quickly take away ABC's ratings power on Mondays. The question is, what wrestling promotions are they looking at? The two most popular wrestling promotions other than the fWo and the WWE right now are Action! and OSW, but A! already has a Monday night contract with the WB and OSW hasn't expressed any interest in moving their flagship Slam program to a new night and time. Another possibility is the TNA promotion, rapidly gaining in popularity lately, but word is that Fox wants a federation that will put the majority of its effort into the Monday night show (rumor even has it that they would be against a promotion running monthly PPVs, wanting more emphasis put on the weekly monday show). TNA, of course, has the weekly PPV business plan, and thus it just wouldn't work with Fox's plans. So what will happen next? Is wrestling really returning to Fox to make a run at ABC and the fWo, and if so, what promotion will it be that ends up with this lucrative TV deal? Unfortunately, inside sources have their limits, and mine has reached his. However, I will keep you updated to the best of my ability on this potentially blockbuster story. Check back here soon!
