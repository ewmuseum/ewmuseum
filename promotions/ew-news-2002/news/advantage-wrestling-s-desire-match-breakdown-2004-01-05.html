Advantage Wrestling's Desire Match Breakdown!

5 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, well, well... this is looking to be a great event but lets just breakdown these matches a little further shall we? Battle Royal! (Ron Von Jobber, Swaggie The Kidd, JJ Skittles, Eric Tione, Angel Dawson, Blake Cassidy, Gwenivere, Mark Adkins) Already the self declaring Mark Adkins has said this should be called the Mark Adkins Battle Royale. He said he will throw these men one after another. Ron Von Jobber is a mainstay in Advantage. He lacks wins in big matches though. JJ Skittles has made an AW name for himself while at the same time Angel Dawson once had as well, this one looks to be a nice fun opener for a great card that already is. #1 Contender To North American Title Chazz Irvine vs. Symon Vaughn w/The Mind Bender Symon Vaughn has launched onto the AW Scene with massive attacks on anyone who stands before him. His size makes him a matchup problem for anyone. Chazz Irvine is another newcomer though he has some great in ring experience appearing on Tuesday Rage the past few weeks. The man is pretty much a nutcase and believes the devil lives inside him.... if anyone is going to take down the monster than I'd say someone as crazy as him has a pretty damn good shot! #1 Contender To X Title (Triple Threat/NO DQ) Viper vs. Acrophobia vs. Skylar Clay This was one has been brewing for several weeks.Viper and Acrophobia have been chomping at the bit to get this one on. They have clashed in and out of the ring and even Nathan Slade was battling out in this fued but when left it was looking to be a one on one grudge match until a win from CEO Adkins over Skylar Clay ended up releasing the order that if he wanted a rematch he'd have to beat both Viper and Acro making this match once again a triple threat, throw in the #1 Contendership to the X Title and NO DQ and we got ourselves a match up! (Grudge Match/Anything Goes) Big Poppa (c) vs. Steve Ewing Rematch of a very brutal first match. These two could really put up the match of the show. Ewing is a helter skelter nut case, while Poppa is just the man of AW. The winner will be the one who controls the tempo. This one should be very intresting to see. #1 Contender To World Title (Supreem Cream Ladder Match) *First To Grab The Supreem Cream Pie hanging in the bucket and then Pie their opponent wins.* Sapphire vs. Brad Daddy If you don't have nothing nice to say don't say it. Brad Daddy didn't heed this advice and spouted some stuff on Sapphire. Now he is in over his head with a hell cat in Sapphire to deal with. Two long term AW Icons should equal one hell of a good match. Did we mention that this is for the #1 Contendership to the World Title? Yeah! Also, after Brad Daddy's antics on past Insomnia he will get a chance at finding his Supreem Cream pie in Sapphire and this time the pie will be hanging from the arena in a bucket where only a Ladder can reach... first to pie the opponent, wins. Tag Team Championship Match Skarrphace vs. T2SF Does GFWA blood still exist in the new age AW? This feud seems to have carried over and now Skarrphace stands alone. Could turn into a 2 on 1 non stop beat down if Skarr is not careful. Sean Casey was involved in this earlier and shouldn't be standing too far behind, we'll just have to watch and see if that infact does come true.. North American Title Match (Carribean Strap Match) Teemu McCorde (c) vs. Jack Duncan The Force meets SR round 1. Jack is one tough son of a gun while Teemu is the dweeb. Makes for one interesting match, but what makes it even better is that they will be strapped together. These two are a pair of AW Veterans, and will be fighting for the coveted AW North American Championship, one that's prestige equals that of the World Title. This match should tear the roof off! Oh that's right, there is no roof at Qualcomm...oh wells. Main Event World Title Match (Cage match) Supernova (c) vs. Creature And now we are here. From night one of the AW Return this one has been set. The reigning world champ Nova. The man with the bus full of maniacs. What makes it more interesting? In a cage. Supernova hopes to take his title reign past that of longest holder Jake Keeton whilst Creature is looking to take out members of Supreem Regime and utterly destroy them, throw in the fact that the World Title is on the line and this heated rivalry is boiling over and this is going to be crazy, this will be out of control even if they are inside a cage. Get ready for blood, get ready for wild, get ready for pure intensity, this is the one everyone will be checking out, get ready for DESIRE, because it's not too far away where we will find out which one of these two men really HAS IT! The actions seems to be there but with the hiatus that is with the missing north east camera angle shot we'll just have to wait and see what will come of this ppv. AW
