NFW ULTRATITLE Roster Changes

28 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NFW North signs former GXW World Champion and current CSWA top contender; also in negotiations with former CSWA EN World Champion. New Frontier Wrestling announced this week that two wrestlers have been released from their NFW North Conference roster slots and their contracts. An inside source reports that the two wrestlers considered at best to be "wild-card hopefuls" are now being replaced by possible favorites to walk away with the season crown. President Quentin Sullivan confirmed that one of these replacements is a former GXW World Heavyweight Champion and current top contender to the CSWA World Championship and has already signed his contract to compete in the 2003 Ultratitle. Sullivan on the recent signing said, "We're certainly excited for his next week. He's got two World Championship title matches before he arrives in NFW for his first match in Week 3 - it certainly could give credence to the fact that NFW certainly has the cream of the crop out there." Sullivan went on to say that an offer sheet has been sent out to a former CSWA EN World Heavyweight Champion that reportedly came to NFW seeking a chance to compete for wrestling's top prize. With these two roster additions, New Frontier Wrestling would have to be considered as a potential contender for nationwide syndication if the 2003 season goes off without a hitch. Within the next week, if Eli Flair wins the fWo World Championship - New Frontier would also have the fWo World Champion along with fWo Internet Champion, 'XXX' Sean Stevens. Ironically, both competitors compete in NFW's North Conference. Slowly, but surely NFW's underground reputation is starting to spread across the nation. With the surprise additions of Eli Flair, Sean Stevens along with CSWA legends Joey Melton and Hornet - NFW certainly had started in the right direction of turning the Ultratitle into professional wrestling's Vince Lombardi Trophy. In Week 3, it looks like they might just take two steps closer. Index of New Frontier - website still not 100% completed, but surfable.
