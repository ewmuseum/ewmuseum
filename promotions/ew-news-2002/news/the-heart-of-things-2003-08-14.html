The heart of things

14 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The hat is down, will you pick it up or piss your pants? So here I am again and once again I find myself tangled in this web with Spyder (no pun intended). Why should I care who you are? What you’ve done? Or anything like that when you’ve never done one thing; Beat me. I’ll congratulate you though, you run around like Rey Mysterio Jr. with a stick up his ass. I wanted to give you a chance though Spyder so I watched some of your matches…and brother geriatric wrestling went out years ago. King of the cruiserweights? More like dead weight. You talk about me taking steroids yet I’m in the gym while you’re somewhere probably stuffing your face full of burritos. I suppose it’s great being a champion in the fWo though…seeing how easy it is. A third grader could be world champion there because you have so many people running around worried about who’s fucking who and not putting out a good product but you wouldn’t know what that was would you? Are you really Spanish? Because I’ve met quite a few and not all of them say esse after everything. You sound like something out of a bad Ice Cube movie. So really Spyder how are you and I different? Well for one I don’t hide behind a mask and call it Mexican tradition. Seems to me you have something to hide and probably for good reason. Secondly it seems you’re jealous that I was in PIW and you weren’t because you keep referring to it. Maybe it was because they only let people with…what’s that word I’m thinking of…oh yeah TALENT in. Oh and don’t give me that “I’m in fWo esse” bullshit because fWo is like a revolving fuckin’ door. Finally, on the streets there aren’t people for you to jump off of to do those fancy little moves that you seem to like to do. Can you even fight? Seriously, I bet there’s not a single person in tA that you could beat, hell I bet there’s a lot of people you couldn’t beat, including a one armed old man. You talk of how PIW was a stupid name…fans wrestling organization. Is it ran by fans? Do fans wrestle in it? I don’t remember seeing any unless you beat up some poor kid in the first row because you needed to take out some of the obvious sexual frustration you’re having. Blue balls are a bitch “hommes” I do find myself still with this throbbing headache though and I think you’re the source. You keep saying the same things over and over “I could talk about my title reigns blah blah blah” well why don’t you? Could it be because they don’t mean shit in the real world? If you’re such a big man then lets do this…I see it like this, some promotion would LOVE to have Spyder vs. Sylo one time only. If we can’t sanction it then I’ll put out some of my own money for us to do this…what do you say are you in or are you out? This is your chance to shut me up, it’s laying in your lap…what will you do? Take it or run like the little bitch you seem to be? Oh and remember…there not insults when it’s the truth. -Sylo P.S: What the fuck is an Alistair Gordon?
