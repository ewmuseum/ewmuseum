Advantage Wrestling's Cody rips into creative team

1 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cody, of T2SF, says in an interview that he is not happy with Advantage Wrestling's creative team. San Francisco, CA - Today turning an interview for San Fran Wrestling Mag Cody Carmichael, one half of the AW Tag Team Champions A Tendency 2 Start Fires, let it be known his displeasure with the Advantage Wrestling creative team. When asked how he felt about working for AW, Cody replied: "I'm not real pleased with the way they've been handing Tomorrow Man and my character as of late. We were signed so they could base a tag team division around us and they haven't done that. So what have they done with T2SF? They put me in a match with some new Skylar Clay guy, who I beat cleanly. Now Clay doesn't seem as motivated. Then they put Tomorrow Man in a dark match against Sapphire. A dark match! That match right there could have been a main event easily! Then we get slaughtered by some new strong man called Symon Vaughn. For whatever reason, AW is high on this guy. And I shouldn't even talk about my disgust with our current angle with Skarrphace. The creative team has built that up to be nothing but a filler pay-per-view match as far as I'm concerned. Does that answer your question?" This Saturday, Cody and Tomorrow Man are set to defend their AW Tag Team Titles in a handicap match against their former GFWA employer Skarrphace, the man many believe made T2SF what they are today. An unnamed Advantage Wrestling official commented that: "Cody may be placed in the dog house and/or pay a fine for speaking out against the creative team, only days away from AW's Desire pay-per-view. Even jobbing the Tag Team Titles to Skarrphace in a handicap match isn't that far off seeing as Tomorrow Man will be wrestling injured for awhile." Ew-news.com was the first website to break the news that Tomorrow Man had in fact been injured during an indy match last week. So obviously, this has been a hard month for both members of A Tendency 2 Start Fires and going into the new year, it doesn't look like things will get much better. Advantage Wrestling
