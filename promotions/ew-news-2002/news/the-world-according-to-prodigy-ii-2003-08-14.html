The World According To Prodigy II

14 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After a stressful week that included one botched match, one scathing commentary and countless rumours regarding my release from Action wrestling I would honestly just like to clear a few things up. Contrary to popular belief, there is no legitimate heat between Sweet Aroma and myself. Veronica and myself are actually very good friends and I am enjoying working my current angle with her, and to be honest I can’t imagine life when I’m not feuding with Sweet Aroma. So don’t count the whole thing out just yet, we will bounce back next week and there’s a hell of more to come. The shambles of a match last week was more my fault than hers, I failed to inform A! of a small injury I picked up at an Indy show, and the match suffered subsequently because of it. Rest assured that Veronica and myself are working to somehow incorporate this shambles of a match up into a potential storyline. Secondly my opinion on Spyder is very much the same. He hasn’t had the decency to respond to my allegations probably because he has nothing to say. The truth is, Spyder is playing political games because he thinks he can muscle his way into the spotlight, manoeuvre himself into the position where the fWo officials meet his every need. Truth is, I wouldn’t be surprised if I saw Spyder wrestling for the Indy feds in a few months because I don’t think his little stay in the fWo will last much longer. I believe that Jay Edward Sylo, (A man who I’ve never really came into contact with in Action) called out Spyder and even offered to put up his own money to do so. I can honestly say that this is one of the best ideas yet, the animosity between these two is the kind of thing that deserves to be in a ring, and I’m sure the wrestling community would love to see these two get it on the ring. Heck I was going to propose the same thing myself, (only with me wrestling Spyder instead of Sylo) I was going to suggest we hold the match in the IWO, an independent federation I know would love to hold the match and a company I am an avid fan of. But personally, I wouldn’t hold your breath for any of this happening. Big ‘stars’ like Spyder tend to have a habit of dropping off the face of the planet when someone tells them to put up or shut up. On a very much-unrelated note, Max Danger and Jeff Garvin put on another potential classic on this week’s ‘Pressure Point’, which everyone should check out given half a chance. I haven’t managed to pop my tape of RUAHHH~! into the VCR yet but when I do expect a brief opinion of the show popping up in one of my updates. I will also attempt to broaden my horizon, and start following a lot more companies programming the subsequent weeks. I received word that the fWo’s Poison Ivy was involved in a serious incident earlier this week, my thoughts and prayers are with both her and her family as they try and make it through this difficult time. He’s hoping that she pulls through okay.
