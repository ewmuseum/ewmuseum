OSF Assault Recap June 4

5 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wednesday June 4 - Chicago, IL One night delayed thanks to the Cubs game, OSF Assault took to the air and the night started off with OSW President Trashcan Man and a surprise visit from the UPS Man. Once again the UPS Man brought a package from an annoyomous tipster. This time the package contained a video tape. Trashy started to play the tape which contained information on Red Army leader Ivan Stanislav. OSF: Assault After a brief sample of the tape the Opening Credits rolled and then brought us inside the building where Paulo and Duke argued over the Sammy Sosa incident before cutting to the first commercial break of the evening. -Commercial- Returning from the break we see Shawn Stewart and Kenyatta Jackson walking and talking about winning. They leave on friendly terms as one of the Clan members surprises Jackson as he comes out of his locker room and straight passed him. Inside the room Kenyatta notices a digital timer about to hit zero. He quickly shuts the door and takes cover. A small explosion is heard and Jackson opens the door to see that the locker room is now filled with a foam bubble solution. We then head into the first fight of the night as John Crichton takes on Kraig Carter. Crichton comes out first to the cheers of the crowd and pulls out a mic and starts to talk about his attack on Profusion last week. He starts to rub it in some more when Profusion interupts him to the shock of Crichton and the fans. Profusion states that he is here and ready to compete in tonight's match against Corbin, before announcing Kraig Carter. Carter comes out from the back and goes down to the Octagon with Profusion following him to ring side. Crichton takes the early advantage in the match, but Carter turns the tables, before getting caught with a shot from Crichton and both men are down. while the ref checkson John, Profusion sneaks into the cage and revives Carter before getting caught by Crichton. John nails Profusion and turns right around into "Case Closed" and he is down for the count. Winner: Kraig Carter via Knock Out. Cutting backstage we see Shawn Stewart once again cutting an interview with Junky Turner when he is attacked by a stage worker that turns out to be Brock Newbludd. -Commercial- After the break we go back to Shawn Stewart receiving medical attention in his locker room when he has a surprise visitor. Tempest is there to check out how Shawn is doing and the two talk about their fight last week and then wish each other luck before Tempest takes off. Our second match of the night is Round 3 of the Best of 7 Series between Kenyatta Jackson and the Clan, in which Jackson leads 2-0. Jackson comes out first followed by a small Ninja representing the Clan. Jackson tries to defend himself against a vicious opening assault from the Ninja but the little guy is too quick for him in the beginning. After a few more attacks the Ninja knocks Kenyatta down but only for an 8 count before Jackson pulls himself back up to his feet. Jackson tries to land a hard right but catches only the unforgiving steel cage on an already injured hand. The Ninja capitalizes nailing him with a kick that drops him for a 9 count this time. The Ninja goes on the attack again but Jackson manages to shake it off and connects with the "Knock Out Blow". The Ninja is out but Jackson is in obvious pain as he clutches his right hand. The lights go out and the defeated Ninja is gone when they turn back on. Winner: Kenyatta Jackson via Knock Out to go up 3-0 in the Best of 7 Series. After the match a video promo featuring rising superstar Miguel Carranza III plays delving into his past as a boxer and his goal to bring honour back to his name. -Commercial- Returning from the break we head right to ringside for the next contest. Miguel Carranza III comes out to cheers first followed by the entrance of Jake Hancock which draws nothing but jeers. Hancock jumps the gun sucker punching Miguel during his warm ups to start of the fight, and Jake stays on the attack until he knocks Carranza down and then stomps on his face drawing blood. Hancock continues the punishment and hits "Straight to Death" on the latino causing the ref to stop the fight and check if Carranza can continue. He's about to call for the bell when Miguel shoots back to life and stops him grabbing his arm and tries to use the ref as leverage to stand. The ref shakes him off and Carranza falls to the ground for 9 count before finally reaching his feet once again. Jake goes back on the attack knocking Miguel to the ground a couple more times before the crowd starts to rally around the fallen latino. Jake silences the crowd with a vicious elbow but Miguel manages to fight back with a series of punches and eventually manages to knock Hancock to the ground for the first time in the fight. Jake gets back up but Miguel has his finisher move in store and as he's got him up in the air a desperation shot catches Miguel inbetween the eyes and both men go down awkwardly. The ref checks on both men and calls the fight as neither is fit to continue. Result: Draw -Commercial- Coming back from the break we are see Miguel exiting the trainer's room as he bumps into Julius Corbin on his way to the ring area for his fight. Corbin makes fun of Miguel's recent performance and his inability to win the fight, but Carranza reminds him that he's already won his Top Tier Qualifier Match and perhaps Corbin should focus on his. -Commercial- We head down to the ring area where the next match between Julius Corbin and Profusion will determine who will advance into the Top Tier Qualifier Match. A banged up Profusion comes out first followed by Corbin as the crowd jeers both men. Profusion attacks first but Corbin spins the tables as he manages to get Profusion down for an early 6 count. Profusion tries to fight back but thanks to a couple of low blows Corbin manages to lock in "Animositisomia" to get the submission victory. Winner: Julius Corbin via Submission. We cut backstage after the match to the snack table as Akage Kitagami is bitching about what's left at the table, when Tempest comes up behind him. Tempest asks Akage if he's found anything out about his missing brother Hiro, which almost brings Akage to tears. Akage asks to change the subject so Tempest obliges turning the conversation to their match later tonight and which nut he wants to have in her grip. The two joke about it and wish each other luck as well as reasurance that neither will let Inphino get in the way when Akage nails Tempest with a shin to the head knocking her out cold. He pulls a lead pipe out of his boot and walks away as we cut to a commercial break. -Commercial- We return inside the woman's locker room as Jada Hunter is returning to the room. She thinks she is alone at first but Lillith is there to confront the woman she came to OSF to beat. The two talk about how Jada did or did not earn the Queen of Extreme Title and the segment ends with Lillith sliting her own arm causing a disgusted Jada to head for the showers. Back to the ring area as Ray Chavez makes his entrance followed by his tag partner for the match Wilhelm Von Krauser. The two don't say anything as they wait for their opponents Anthony DaVatta and Frank Scarpo, DaVatta Inc. that come out to relentless booing. Krauser and Anthony start off the fight and Anthony takes the advantage from the get go, quickly showing good team work with Frankie. Krauser finally manages to tag in Chavez fairs no better as he is quickly knocked down to the ground for a 9 count. As Chavez gets up, Anthony is beating on Krauser and the ref is distracted with them allowing Frankie to slide on the Brass Knuckles and Knock Ray Chavez out for the ten count. Winnesr: DaVatta Inc. Cut backstage to a worried Loyal Communist Party Member Wang looking frantically for Tempest. Not being able to find her he decides to go get the Detective Kraig Carter to help him find her. They decide the best place to look is the most obvious and go to the buffet table where they find Tempest lying out cold ont he floor. They revive her and Carter adds Akage as a list of suspects for his case on who murdered Tempest's father and they end of stating that they should go to Hong Kong to investigate the murder. -Commercial- Returning from the break Jack O'Hara and Jinx make their way down to the ringarea for thier tag team bout to the boos of the crowd. As they ready themselves in the cage, Tomas and Taylor come out to a large pop and enter the Octagon. Tomas and Jinx start off the intergender tag match, Teresa gives Jinx a free shot before attacking her and knocking her down for a four count. After some more punishement O'Hara tags in but is caught with a drop kick from Tomas before she tags in Scott Taylor. Scott sees an opertunity and actually starts to climb the inside of the Octagon and drops an elbow on O'Hara from about half way up, keeping Jack down for a 6 count before Jinx breaks the count by attacking Taylor from behind. He knocks her off but O'Hara is back up and manages to beat Taylor around a bit before putting him down for an 8 count. Scott jumps up to his feet and catches a suprised O'Hara with "Off to Never Never Land" and scores the knock out victory. Winners: Teresa Tomas and Scott Taylor via Knock Out. After the match Scott and Teresa both do a little mic work bitching out the DaVatta's and telling them it will be a tag match between Anthony and Frank vs Tomas and Taylor next week on Assault. -Commercial- Backstage Shawn Stewart is talking with Tony Scarpo Sr, Shawn at first thinks he is going to be punished for his loss last week but Pops assures him that is not the case and that Shawn actually did well considering. And tells him if there is anything he needs to come to him. -Commercial- The Main Event is for the OSF World Heavyweight Championship where Tempest defends against Akaga Kitagami with Inphino Blitz as referee. Miyabi comes out first to introduce the competitors and takes the oppertunity to play the cheap shots off on her exboyfriend as Akage makes his way to the ring area. As Akage is in the Octagon she then announces Tempest as the soon to be Former OSF World Champion, which definately annoyes the champion enters the eight sided circle. Finally the Special Ref is introduced as Inphino Blitz makes an oversized entrance and the fight finally begins. It's alot of back and forth action with Inphino messing up counts for both fighters before Tempest knocks Akage down and Blitz quickly counts the Knock Out. Winner: Tempest via Knock Out. Post match Inphino nails Tempest with the "Inphinite Reverb" on the Title belt. -End of Show- Check out the Full Results
