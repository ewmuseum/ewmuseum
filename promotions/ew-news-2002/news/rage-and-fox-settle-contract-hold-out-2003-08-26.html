RaGE and Fox Settle Contract Hold-Out

26 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Everything was fine months ago, but then on the first of August, Fox decided to cancel Real and Graphic Entertainment's television contract. For the first time since then, the truth is exposed as to what happened and why... It was back in February that Real and Graphic Entertainment and the Fox Broadcasting Company managed to agree on a contract for the first half of the year in television production. That contract of course gave Real and Graphic Entertainment broadcasting time for Fear and Relentless each week. At the conclusion of those contracts in June, Fox was eager to re-sign the company with a new deal. The new deal was signed for two shows, Uncensored on Sunday nights for one hour and Fearless on Wednesday nights for two hours. It was a contract that would last until the beginning of September for Uncensored, and hopefully until the end of the year for Fearless. When August started things suddenly came to a halt as Fox decided to pull the plug on Real and Graphic Entertainment television. It was due to the content of the Jamal Kage and Robert Maxwell story that began at Se7en Deadly Sins. The strange thing was that Fox appeared fine when it first happened. Though, many now believe Fox was only fine with it because it happened on Pay Per View and not their station. But when Real and Graphic Entertainment Fearless aired on July 30 after Se7en Deadly Sins, Fox was reportedly nervous and after that show it decided to cancel the contract for Fearless and Uncensored. Now, that arises the question of whether or not it was possible. The Fox Network had a contract to uphold and they certainly were not honoring it by stopping the production of Real and Graphic Entertainment shows. But while all this happened, Real and Graphic Entertainment refused to publicly comment on what was happening. Much was the case as back in June, Tom Mason avoided the media. This time RaGE did one thing differently, they continued to tape their shows in front of live audiences in arenas throughout the country. Reportedly, it would have cost RaGE more money to have canceled or even rescheduled shows. The venues which had been rented out would have been wanted more money to pay for the rescheduling, and refunds to ticket holders would have been unavoidable. So, by continuing to perform and tape their shows, Real and Graphic Entertainment was backing up their productions and preparing for what ever would have to happen. At the worst, the company would have to forget about Fox and turn to another Television station, perhaps Spike TV or even something like the USA-Network. The company was hoping to still reach an agreement with Fox on the contracts and content. Tom Mason was reported to be extremely irritated, yet calm, when he held a meeting with News Corporation Executive Rupert Murdoch. Many may know of the relationship between both business men and after their meeting, which lasted just under an hour, both sides came out to an agreement. This new agreement was announced publicly today and it will allow Real and Graphic Entertainment to run its shows from August on Wednesday and Sunday nights through September. In order to limit the quantity of televised RaGE shows in September, there will be no new shows airing until October. However, all scheduled RaGE shows for September will take place and fans will be treated to a worthwhile show, says Tom Mason. This deal will also enable Real and Graphic Entertainment Fearless to run until January of 2004; while Uncensored has been given time until November 2003. Sources say Fox is still a bit nervous with the quality of the production on Uncensored. There seems to be a lot of promise in this September Television lineup for Real and Graphic Entertainment. There are five televised shows that were taped and never aired in August and they will be showing on September 3, 7, 10, 14, 21. That enables Real and Graphic Entertainment to take a little time off before throwing together their Summer Highlight show taped on August 31, to air on September 28. Fans attending that event will all be obligated to sign agreements not to leak information about Unleashed prior to the show airing on September 28. Interesting enough, Real and Graphic Entertainment house shows throughout September will feature the champions prior to Unleashed. So, even if a title did change hands, the crowd will have no indication to the change having occurred, though the records will state that August 31 was the official date of the title change. From all this information, it appears that Real and Graphic Entertainment more than has their production covered and is making it their priority to protect the secrecy and reality of Unleashed from the public right now. This only makes RaGE a must see product for the month of September! Real and Graphic Entertainment
