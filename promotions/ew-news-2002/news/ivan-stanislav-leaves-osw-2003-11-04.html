Ivan Stanislav Leaves OSW

4 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After years of service, The Red Scare has finally left the wrestling world. In a shocking and startling turn of events, Ivan Stanislav announced that he will no longer be part of the OSW roster. Stanislav, who was booked on Sunday Slam! in a World Title Match against World Champion Scott Taylor, instead no showed the match and instead talked to the wrestling world via satellite from Siberia. With a watchtower over his shoulder and snow falling around him, Stanislav made it clear that he was formally leaving his wrestling home of over a year and a half and now would be manning a watchtower in secluded Siberia. “Earlier this week, Praporschik Stanislav acquired his change in orders from Russian Government. He has been reassigned. No longer are his duties to ‘create revenue and bring glory to Russia through American Wrestling Federation.’ Now, it is merely to patrol this area, in the middle of Siberia, from watchtower over there.” Stanislav had several reasons for his leaving, but his major reasons were for failing to uphold his duty to his country. “It is not easy to give up, like I have. But indeed, I have. My dream is dead. No longer do I find it possible to communize federation, it is impossibility. Now, I am faced with rebuilding whatever life Ivan once had… or did he ever have life, since he has been blindly dedicated to a façade?” Ivan Stanislav entered into American wrestling in May of 2001 in the now defunct Primetime Wrestling Federation. There, he became a Tag Team Champion, Continental Champion, World Champion, and was wrestler of the year for 2001. Stanislav was also later inducted into the PCW Hall of Fame. Stanislav’s career took off even further in the OSW. Stanislav held the OSW American title, and became the longest reigning OSW World Champion, holding the title for 182 days. He was also Wrestler of the Year in 2002, Angle of the Year 2002, Promo of the Year 2002, and was part of the “First Couple” of the OSW with Tempest. Ivan Stanislav has been in the OSW since it opened in February 2002 and was the only original member of the roster not to take a break, until now. “Ivan Stanislav’s leaving will be a blow to the OSW, as many people, whether they liked it or not, saw him as a keystone in the federation. He may have been overbearing and violent, but he worked hard night in and night out and protected the OSW whenever the call was sounded.” Said OSW Commentator Nate Dumas. Stanislav was active in the wrestling community as well. He invaded the PCW over a year after his exit in an attempt to take over the company, and also invaded the Asylum, but was thrown out of the federation by Token Weed. With Stanislav’s exit, there is a hole in the roster of the OSW. With him, he takes Head of Security Alexei Ruslan, Loyal Communist Party Member Wang, The Two Round Faced Women, and his stable, The Red Army, has been disbanded. But the OSW roster is very healthy and after coming off of a successful pay per view, appears poised to surge forward. “Something tells me, and I don’t know why, that Ivan would not have left had the OSW not been ready.” Said Nate Dumas, “He always said that he hated the OSW and what it stood for… yet there appeared to be a lot of love in his heart for the OSW. He even called it his ‘home.’ Which is a bold statement for one such as The Russian Bear.” While Stanislav leaves nothing tangible in the OSW, he does leave a blue-collar work ethic that other roster members can look up to. He leaves behind a sense of pride that he accidentally instilled within the federation. He leaves behind an era, yet with his exit starts another. The Bear has not retired, but he has left. We at the OSW wish him the best of luck in his future… whatever it is that Siberia holds for him. Das vidanya Ivan Stanislav. Thank you.
