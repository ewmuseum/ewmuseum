Tyler Davidson and eVolution Respond To Taun Pham Claims

30 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Was Shane Wethers signed to PRIME or not? Tyler Davidson responds to the claims of Taun Pham. Despite all the other rumors that are circulating concerning him stepping down from the position of President within eVolution, Tyler Davidson is still the owner of the company and had a few things to say regarding his own former employer in PRIME, Taun Pham. Needless to say the hostilities between PRIME and Tyler Davidson have not totally subsided following Tyler’s walk out on the company about a month ago and now with the latest hostilities between PRIME and eVolution forming in relations to the signing of Shane Wethers things are going to get even worse it seems. PRIME has threatened a lawsuit against eVolution for their signing of Shane Wethers as PRIME representatives insist that Wethers had already agreed to a contract with PRIME a few weeks before his signing with eVolution. Tyler Davidson was approached for comment on the situation and unlike Taun Pham Tyler was a bit more loosely lipped on the subject and many others as well. “It’s apparent to me and many others that Pham is still quite a bit bitter with me for taking my leave of PRIME after he decided to try and screw me around. Hell even after I left he tried to damage my character as a performer but that didn’t work out for him so now he’s going to try a different route to take care of his issues with me. It just goes to show what kind of business man Taun Pham really is doesn’t it?” “Shane and I have a long history together in this business dating back to Sebastian’s $oldout Wrestling. I’ve had long talks with him and Thug roC both over the past several weeks and know beyond any doubt that Shane never signed any contract with PRIME despite being in close negotiations with them, eVolution, and several other companies including OSE and Action. However when PRIME were going through all their trouble with UPN it showed him exactly where the best place to go would be and that was needless to say eVolution. Regardless of whatever lies and threats Taun wants to throw our way I’m quite confident that Shane and eVolution will have a long history together as Shane re-establishes himself as one of the top stars in this business.” “Personally it just seems to be that poor little Taun is a bit jealous of the success eVolution has had compared to his PRIME company. If he does indeed go through with this foolish lawsuit than Taun can be certain that I will have the top lawyers in this country representing eVolution and they will squash his petty lawsuit.” With that Tyler ended the conversation and walked off to continue his own work for the day no doubt also make any possible preparations that are needed for the potential lawsuit. Obviously there aren’t very friendly relations between these two companies thanks in part at least to the volatile relationship between Taun Pham and Tyler Davidson. Whether or not things continue to heat up remains to be seen but this is one battle that just might be settled in the court house. eVolution
