Cage/Gillesp Rivalry Heats Up...

8 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With DW World Champion Gillesp set to meet former 2x World Champion 'The Enigma' Chris Cage in the ring on Monday Night at Destruction, the war of words has already started between the heated rivals. With Chris Cage only returning to Dynasty Wrestling after a year’s absence two weeks ago, the former 2x World Champion is already creating a stir in the locker room. Cage set his targets firmly on Rick Lacey when he returned, forcing the Hall of Fame member back into the ring for a match at Global Takeover 2. Despite putting up a valiant effort, it was too soon for Lacey to return to the ring after the injuries that he sustained and with a gunman taking shots at Lacey, Cage picked up the win. With Cage already beating Lacey, it seems that ‘The Enigma’ has turned his sights on Lacey’s friend and the current DW World Champion, Gillesp. Branding Gillesp as repetitive, ‘ignorant’ and a ‘disgraceful World Champion’, Cage has certainly caught the Champions attention. When asked about ‘The Enigma’ Chris Cage at a recent press conference, Gillesp had this to say, “(Cage is) Nothing but a jealous man spewing lies. Chris Cage is a sad, sad excuse for a man. He is just like that guy who lives down the street from you with the mullet who thinks it's still 1990. Cage keeps talking about all the things he has done in the past. He brags about beating up Malcolm King?? Who the f**k is Malcolm King?? When was he a star?? 1987?? And he beat Chris Boswell!! HAHA like that is an accomplishment!! I've beaten Chris Boswell so many times, he probably thinks of me as an abusive step-father since I've slapped him around so many times. Look Cage you need to realize this just like all those guys who still have mullets need to realize it ain't 1990 anymore!! It's 2003 Cage and your time has long come and gone. It's a new era in Dynasty Wrestling.....the era of the Straight Man!! I thought Cage learned his lesson last time when I made him submit to his own finisher, but I guess he is too stupid! You know the old saying it's better to have guts than brains?? Not in this case!! Cage will be joining Boswell in the retirement home after Monday night…..” Chris Cage and Gillesp are set to meet for the first time since Cage’s return on Monday Night Destruction this week as they square off in the centre of the ring in a non-title match.
