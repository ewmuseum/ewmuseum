[CEW] First Show of Tenth Year

21 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cyber Extreme Wrestling kicks off its tenth year of history with Icestorm 2004 ... http://ppv.cewentertainment.com 10 years ... The large majority of federations in the United States are lucky to last a year, let alone two, three ... or ten. But one fed has reached that ten year test of time in 2004. That fed, is Cyber Extreme Wrestling. Now let's set the record straight: Cyber Extreme Wrestling, in and of itself as a brand, is only five years old, beginning on a regular basis in 1998. But the history of the federation, traced through NWF, CIWA, HCW, and UEW, goes back to 1994. CEW itself is on the verge of its 150th episode of [CEW] Thrill, a weekly mainstain since August 12, 1998. The 150th episode is projected to be February 12, 2004. But tonight, is not Thrill 150. Tonight, Cyber Extreme Wrestling presents the first show of its 2004 year, the pay-per-view extravaganza: ICESTORM! For the World Title: Shooting star Cassandra Avery takes her high-flying style against 5-time CEW World Champion AC. For Managing Control of CEW: 'Showtime' Nick Alexander, AC's greatest nemesis and Cassandra Avery's boyfriend, takes on James Everett III, the managing owner of CEW, and member of AC's stable, the 3-pAC. In the annual King of Death Matches tournament will take place: Match 1 - Bull Rope Match Buzzsaw vs. Ezra Mayhem Match 2 - Cage Match Da Link Went vs. Allen Watts Match 3 - Barbed Wire boards in the corners Match Johnny Psychotic vs. Alex Coleman Match 4 - Kendo Stick Match FTS Champion Shayne Gowers vs. Vincent Francis Also on the card: For the North American Title: Weezle © vs Jeremy Hunter Television Title Tournament Final: Deimos vs Lord Skywolf Muzzolini vs Jakob Mayhem [CEW] Icestorm 2004
