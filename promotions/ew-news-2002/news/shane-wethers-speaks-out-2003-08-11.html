Shane Wethers Speaks Out

11 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With all of the controversery surrounding him concerning PRIME and eVolution, Shane Wethers finally decided to speak out on the situation. eVolution Has Been The Center of Controversy Lately Shane Wethers continued his egotistical assault on Prime and some of his fellow evolutionists today whilst quizzed by a reporter on the speculation surrounding his move to Evolution. Wethers has been at the heart of rowdy arguments and accusations saying he moved to Evolution whilst under contract at Prime. Following Shane’s attack on Taun Pham at Legends, where he used a look-alike to insult and make crude remarks about Taun and his ethnic nature, he went on to warn off both Prime members and Evo. Wethers, who turns 28 next month, has caused nothing but trouble since he returned and when asked by reporters, what his actual aim was in Evo, he replied; ‘To piss the world off’. So far he has done just and continued to do so this morning when stopped outside of the Hard Rock café in central California. Wethers had questions thrown at him about remarks made by Retired Evo member Terlayne Sterling. Check out his interview below. First things first, there has been a whirlwind of controversy over you in recent weeks, claiming you screwed Prime into the ground by signing with Evolution despite having signed contracts with them. What are we to think of all these accusations? “Think what you want, you only will anyway. I’m a gifted, talented and very smart athlete, why would I want to stay around in a company like Prime and compete with losers like that?” But at Legends you confessed that you had only joined Evo so you could make Tyler Davidson’s life a living hell. Why would you say this about a guy who is trying to defend you? (laughing) “Tyler is keeping the odds straight, keeping me in Evo. He doesn’t like me, why should he? I made him look a fool in $oldout Wrestling. I sure as hell don’t like him and I’ll prove that to him when the time is right. He’s just doing the right thing. There have also been a lot of feelings revealed from members of Evo including Terlayne Sterling. Do these critics inspire you to do even better in Evo? “Sterling just some delusional pickledick who quite clearly feels outclassed by me, some people accept it and commend my talent but some people, such as Sterling seem to whine and cry about it. They made the guy retire because he was absolutely boring. As for me being even better? How can you be better when you’re excellent? It’s hard to be humble when you’re me. The same goes with Carl Ca$h, he says that I’m stealing the spotlight? Of course I am, don’t tell me he expects any of the spotlight. After all, I beat his ass in $oldout Wrestling, he’s not in my league. Critics have also said that some of your remarks at Legends were rather on the racist side, what are you feelings towards this? “Racist? I think not, he’s a typical Japanese Muppet and I don’t care what anybody thinks. I didn’t pay for there remarks nor did I ask for them, the people loved my little interview with Taun because they know the Eternal Punk is capable of delivering and entertaining, yet truthful, presentation.” Finally, What can we expect from Shane Wethers in Evo? “One of the most gifted competitors in the game, I will climb the ladder and turn evolution into one of the biggest companies in the game. No one will be ever able to match it’s potential, as for all the cocky Prime losers, they can watch and learn. Maybe someday they’ll be an icon like me. (Breaks out laugh) ..Yeah right! And pigs might fly!” The interview was cut short as Shane barged his way through reporters and climbed into his waiting limo. It was believed that Shane was off to meet with a member of Evo Staff, but whom the staff member is remains anonymous. As things heat up in the Prime/Evo war camp, one question still remains. Who has liability over Shane Wethers? eVolution
