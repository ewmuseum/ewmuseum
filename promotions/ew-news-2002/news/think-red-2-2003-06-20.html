Think Red #2

20 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Russian Bear of OSW talks about the upcoming OSE Ascension Pay Per View, their new development community, a 'revolution' in the works for eW News, and crushes a certain Spyder that bugs him! Comrades of wrestling world, it is Ivan Stanislav here once again with another edition of “Think Red,” the most unbiased piece of print in circulation today! As many of you know, and some of you may be too ignorant to recognize, OSE is holding Ascension Pay Per View next Sunday… this is tremendous event. The OSE will introduce its major interfederation title, the Old School Title, in which the winner will be determined through the Ascension Main Event. The Main Event is a three tiered king of the mountain type of match where the winner, he or she who manages to remain atop the spire itself, it crowned the Old School Champion. Not only this, but Ivan himself has match against that dastardly Trashcan Man, foul President of the OSW, and the winner will attain not only the presidency, but also to start on the top tier of the Ascension Mountain! It is refreshing to know that after all this time, finally a just Premier, in the form of Ivan Stanislav, will finally be achieved. Trashcan Man has no chance, for Ivan Stanislav, the crowd favorite, has the support of all people in OSW in finally toppling this tyrant and restoring order and stability to the OSW itself! Also, it would appear that Timo Bolamba, treacherous Asylum member as well, is having match up with our wonderful World Champion, Comrade S., in which the winner will gain the OSW World Title as well! I have no doubt in my mind that S. will make quick work of the Samoan and continue to bring the title the honor that it deserves. However, this article is not merely to talk about pay per view… for there are other things occurring throughout OSW as well. First and foremost, Ivan Sergeiovich would like to announce that the Chicago Development Community (CDC) is going to be forming within the OSE very shortly, and this training academy will help train those that long to join our ranks as well as give them the necessary tools to actually survive in a competitive environment like OSE. Because of this, Ivan Stanislav urges all communists to join CDC and you will be granted amnesty by the then Premier Stanislav, and will automatically be accepted under the banner of communism! Also, there is a bug that has been nagging Ivan Stanislav quite much. A Spyder, if you will… Little Spyder from fWo, do not presume that your jargon filled language and uneducated comments towards Ivan Sergeiovich was left unnoticed, for the Russian Bear of the OSW never, ever forgets. You may feel safe, hidden behind the walls of your precious fWo… but nothing can stop the retribution that Ivan Stanislav promises to levy against you when the time comes. Also, Ivan Stanislav is patient man, so you can just wait and stew over this, insect. Furthermore, you only show your ignorance so much more when you claim that ‘this is what wrestling has come to? Russians?’ Perhaps you live deep within that web of yours beneath the ground and do not get out much, dog, but I do not believe that warriors from Mother Russia are very uncommon within the ranks of wrestling circles. Perhaps if you were not too busy thinking up interesting slang and unintelligent babble regarding individual’s mothers, you would focus your multifaceted eyes towards the truth: You are a Spyder… one that can be crushed quite easily. But, I digress, for Stanislav certainly has better things to do than waste his time berating tiny little insect. Also, to the news people of this fine collection of articles… …Ivan has not witnessed one Russian flag, one hammer and sickle, or one representation of proletariat anywhere! This is very unsettling and must be addressed quickly! We demand it! Lest we revolt and handle this in our own fashion. Finally, the business of these little shakeups around here, notably in the OSE’s area of concern: There are many of you out there that Praporschik Ivan Sergeiovich Stanislav does not know, and this could be quite unfortunate. We of the OSE, especially once Ivan Stanislav is Premier of the OSW, are very excited about the opportunities that this provides while we move into the direction of many foreign, yet quite productive federations. While men like Spyder, dumb to the world and blind to all those around him, may continue their ignorance, it is Ivan’s great hope that you will all embrace the OSE and give her credit that she so deserves. And finally… in closing… Watch the OSW/OSF and prepare for OSE Ascension which will occur on June 29th! Fighters and Wrestlers alike will battle for the Old School Title while many other titles are also on the line! CDC is rapidly approaching and will welcome those that are willing to learn! Ivan Stanislav thinks this is great opportunity! He thinks that the OSE is headed towards bright future! He thinks that Spyder is curled up in ball like felled insect… …and he always, ALWAYS Think’s Red! Das vidanya, Comrades!! Praporschik Ivan Stanislav Red Army Commander OSW
