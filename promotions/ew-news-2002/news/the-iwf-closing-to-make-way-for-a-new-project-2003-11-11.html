The IWF closing to make way for a new project?

11 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As of late, hints have been dropped for the possible closing of the Icon Wrestling Federation to make way for a new project! A handful of IWF officials have been dropping hints as of late about the Icon Wrestling Federation possibly entering a merger with another big professional wrestling promotion. The IWF opened it's doors officially on March 18th, 2000 with it's very first installment of it's flagship show, IWF Sunday Night Lightning. Three years and eight months later, the Icon Wrestling Federation may be seeing it's demise. Over the years, the Icon Wrestling Federation has served as the home for such talent as Chazz Mendel, John Daniels, Blaise Martelle, Chase Martelle, Miss Phoenix, RPG, Jason Drake, Scott Monroe, Freak Daddy, Matty Midget, Ace and more. We have questioned several fans of the possible idea of a merger and though they are saddened by the possibility of the IWF closing it's doors, they are anxious to see what may be in store for the future if the rumors about this new promotion are true. We attempted to catch up with the IWF's Chairman, John "The Iceman" Lugo for a comment but he refused to comment on the matter. The only thing that Mr. Lugo would say to our reporters is that "the sports entertainment business is unpredictable and anything could happen." For further information about the Icon Wrestling Federation, visit their website at http://www.efeduniverse.com/iwficon/ Also, be sure to tune into IWF Sunday Night Lightning this sunday night at 8:00 PM Eastern Time on the TNT network as we air LIVE from the Aloha Stadium in Honolulu, Hawaii!
