Former PURE-ite in negotiations with indy promotion

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Former PURE standout has been linked with a Californian independant promotion. Indy promotion, The X, are reportedly in negotiations with former PURE standout, Raizo Tenzan. The owner of The X, Jason Marah, was a member of PURE while Tenzan was there and became a fan of his in-ring work. "Tenzan was probably the best thing PURE had going for them. He was their best wrestler and his ability on the mic was above most of the other lads there." said Marah. Tenzan, who was the leader of PURE's "Saikyo System", has since seemingly fallen off the face of the earth after the promotion's demise. Tenzan has been linked with other indy promotions such as Ron Starr's PWC. Ryan Miller of The X confirmed the promotion was interested in the competitor, but a deal was far from being done. "Although Raizo is amazing in the ring, his backstage attitude has gotten alot of bad reviews and right now the last thing we need are self-centred egotists. Hopefully the rumours aren't true though. There are still alot of grey areas in the negotiations, such as the wage factor. He can't expect the same amount of money each week as he was getting with PURE, we just wouldn't be able to afford him. Hopefully we can seal a deal with him, but things still seem a far way off. He's been linked with places like PWC and PCW and they're more equipped than us to offer him a larger wage packet, a packet we wouldn't be able to compete with. But I'm still hopeful we can come to an agreement." If Tenzan does sign, he will join another former PURE star on the roster, with Cordozer Stevenson who headlined The X's first show, After Dark 1 and he certainly won't be out of his element with just over the third of the roster having an Asian heritage.
