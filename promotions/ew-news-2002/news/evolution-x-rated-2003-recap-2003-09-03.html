eVolution: X-Rated 2003 Recap

3 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The second annual X-Rated pay per view event for eVolution aired Sunday night from Twickenham in London, England. The show featured a great deal of action and some twists that are certain to alter the way eVolution operates in the future. Highlights: A brief meeting was shown between Tyler Davidson and Daniel S. concerning Tyler’s intentions for eVolution. Who are the three members of The Committee and what are their plans for eVolution? Aaron Justice made his debut and battled against The British Bomber Morpheus Omega and Sky Night both made their return to eVolution after a somewhat lengthy absence. Brianna Washington tried to impress people once again in a battle against the massive Deacon. The eVolved Champion Scott Evans was introduced to his mystery opponent for the night as chosen by The Committee, Matt Hunt. Aaron Frost and Marcus Escobar battled against Machine and El Agave Diablo in a tag team cell match. Question Beyond X and Malcolm King finally faced off one on one to settle their issues. Scott Frost and Seara, The HeartBreakers, and The BodySnatchers battled to determine the number one contenders to the Tag Team Titles. Terlayne was obviously not in the best shape of his life despite having a shot at the Cell Championship later in the night. The Syndicate defended the Tag Team Titles against Jason Relms and Luther Wisdom whose identity issues continued. Damon O’Halloran defended his Cell Championship against Terlayne and Sean Sterling in a triple threat cell match. Shane Wethers and Tyler Davidson continued their rivalry from $oldout Wrestling with their second ever one on one battle. Carl Cash and Shocka fought inside the Tiers of eVolution cage. Cash was then found beaten and bloody backstage with a message from the attacker written in the blood of Cash. Thug roC and Ryan Mason battled to unify the ULWN and Continental Titles. Jay Jay Jameson and Morpheus Omega came head to head again as two of the more violent stars in eVolution collided. Xander defended his Universal Title against James Kimson and Asis in a triple threat match. eVolution X-Rated 2003
