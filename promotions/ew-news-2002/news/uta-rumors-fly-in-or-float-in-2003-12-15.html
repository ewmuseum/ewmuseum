UTA Rumors Fly In, Or Float In?

15 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Captin Roomer of the UTA does a little rumor spreading, is any of it true? RRR MIGHTY!!! Welcome to my new UTA column. They call my Captain Roomer, maybe because I am the Captain Roomer...and there's nothing I like to do more than ship rumors to you UTA folks'! Let's get started with what's been goin around latley... First things first. How about the return of Devon Lynch eh? It was great to see someone with talent such as his to come back to the UTA. Welcome back Devon. With the new year coming around, look for some end of the year ROSTER CUTS made by the UTA Office. Some names heading that list are possibly DMT, Aaron Marty, Stone Cold Thug, and possibly even the current UTA Champion Dark. With much feeling that Dark's win at The Return not a respectful win, his image has been lowered. As could his status with the UTA itself. What in the 'Hell'?! What in the world are the 'Hell Razors' doing back here in the United Toughness Alliance? Just ask one man, and that is G-Man. From current research on this group, look for nothing but...'hell' from these 3 people. I guess in EWF (not sure what stands for) this was thier 'nWo' of the promotion. I'm not quiete sure on how Kevin Hawk could fit in such a highly promoted stable...but I'm sure soon we will find out. What's with all the injuries?! Has anyone noticed besides me that the UTA has been having a bad string of injuries. Not all from injuries, but four men are already out of UTA action as it is...Crimson Lord, Sebastion Bush, Goliath, and Nathaniel Draven have been on the bad side of these. Word has it the the Red Cross will be spectating UTA events for now on until they feel not too. This because they feel that with current work that the UTA is not taking as much care as their workers as they should. Look for UTA officials to 'calm' down their matches in order to make a safe atomosphere for its workers. G-Man about to sign an official UTA contract? I sure hope so. As the leader of the Hell Razors, G-Man has a target on his back. After viewing The Return (which got a 10 on my scale), the guy that sees the biggest target on G-Man's back is Klash. Or I guess they call him Jace Hardy too, I don't know. I've viewed Klash's latest interview and if I'm the only one that can see it then you must be all blind...he for surley just called out G-Man. I think that with that kind of action, and with G-Man having as much starism as he does, he'll accept this call-out and we could officially see the return of G-Man at the next pay per view against Klash. Well, looks like my shipment is all but completed in this edition. I'll have a whole ship load next time. See yah then mates! United Toughness Alliance
