Predictions: EMWC's Redemption PPV

3 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Let's start this preview with a look up & down the show, which could be huge... or a huge disappointment. Predictions: EMWC's Redemption Just The Facts! PPV Predictions Webster P. Just The Facts! staffer ([detail withheld]) Let's start this preview with a look up & down the show, which could be huge... or a huge disappointment. Intro Match: Randall Crane vs. Derek Irvin This feud started in the twilight of this past PPV cycle, so the heat's not as up-to-snuff as some of the other matches on this card. However, this could turn out to be a pretty good match. Not so much for Crane, let's face it, isn't dynamic in the ring but rather for Irvin who's a real star in the making. Eric Wayne Grayson may or may not make an appearance, but in my opinion, it won't matter like always. My prediction? Irvin gets the win and gets the push to the new sub-division of the Triple Crown. Television Championship Match: Tommy Stephens [c] vs. The Scrayper I don't know, this could very well be the big, black hole of the card. You got Tommy Stephens playing a drunk and devaluing the TV Title every moment he steps into the ring against a guy who's got good in-ring skills in the Scrayper, but not much fan heat. In fact, this heel/heel match-up should DUD the crowd out. If it was Robert Kellan or even Jonas Olrikke (or even all four), it would be a better match. My prediction? Scrayper gets the win since Stephens hasn't done anything with the title and EMWC would want a non-Ego MAX Triple Crown final. Hell On Earth: World Tag Team Championship Match: The Prophets of Rage vs The Second Dynasty vs Billy Classon/Jeremy Rhodes vs The Towel Boys One cell, four team, title and division on the line... I kind of think that no matter what these teams do, there's no chance that the tags will remain. Just looking at the teams involved will tell you that. None of them have much of a widespread fan following or recognition (besides the Towel Boys, but that's more of the Jim Towel-antics of the past). There's talent there, minus the Prophets, but not enough to warrent the division staying. That said, this should be a fun match if it doesn't get too cluttered in the cage. My prediction? The Second Dynasty takes the gold. Extreme J Semifinals November vs Vespasian Reed Shane Destiny vs Michael Keening Shame that these will be the last Junior matches in the EMWC (at least until Blue gets pressured into bringing it back by the hot-cruiser-of-the-moment). However, all four of these men deserve to be in the semifinals of the lackluster Extreme J tournament, but they (minus November) really don't show off what the division should be about: high flying, death-defying, non-stop thrilling action. Shame they couldn't push Infierno in there or revive Harisoto Mashima or Macht Kraftwerk's careers. However, have to make with what's due. My predictions? November over Reed; Shane Destiny over Keening; and finally November over Destiny in a very good finals. Final GIW Match: Levitcus Nelson vs. ??? Ok, this could be a real downer and pretty much just depends on whoever it was that fought Nelson (some of the GIW fans in attendence that night know and said the match was a good one). The question here is that is it worthy of taking pay-per-view time? Personally, I don't think so. Seeing Nelson lumbering around the ring doesn't make for pay-per-view material and considering the most talented man in GIW, "Bad Eye" McBaine, was in an other match (and certainly would've been promoted for this PPV line-up) I can't see it living up to the hype that EMWC puts on it. But hell, of the GIW matches I've seen on tape, they did do good shows. My Prediction? Can't say much, but I'll go with whoever the surprise is than Nelson. Cage Match: North American Championship Match: Luke Kinsey [c] vs Michelle Bailey Here's where the Show really turns it up into the high profile and heat-fueled feuds. This one's been brewing ever since Luke Kinsey's stablemates, Juan Vasquez and Tommy Stephens, tormented Bailey under the Ribera Kid masks. A well booked feud thusfar, this match should shine and surprise many folks out there. Kinsey's a world class performer and Bailey's a highly underrated wrestler that can compete with Kinsey. The cage, though, doesn't seem needed to add anything to the match, but works within the story to keep Ego MAX out of the match (though, I'm sure they'll be present - maybe even a new member? If so, I'd say it would be new tag champs, the Second Dynasty). My Prediction? Bailey finally gets her revenge and gets the NA Title and be the Queen of the new Triple Crown division while Kinsey moves on to the World. #1 Contender Match: Juan Vasquez vs Jeff Matthews Another Ego MAX-influenced bought and yet another good match. Vasquez is godly in the ring and his style's quite the opposite of Matthews'. The stip, #1 contender, is flimsy as it really didn't warrent a match for this PPV and kind of takes away from the fact the two have been feuding ever since Vasquez, Kinsey, and Stephens unleashed Ego MAX on the world and Matthews. However, the match should be dynamic, with a good up & down tempo between the two offenses. My prediction? Tough since both have history with Chris Courtade, but I'll pick the safe bet in Jeff Matthews. 2 Out Of 3 Falls: Steve Spector vs Devon Case What was supposed to the Pay-Per-View's main event is still the show's best match on paper. On one hand you've got Devon Case, who can seemingly do everything, against Steve Spector, who makes everyone he's wrestled with ten times better. While their feud hasn't really been all that hot (and at times, confusing as to who you should root for) and the allegations made on the last An Evening to the Extreme seem to not add anything (Case's back has been just fine the past two years and the Spector-Redemption thing doesn't seem to hold), just the fact that these two are in the ring together for not one, not two, but (most likely) three matches makes this the focus of the night. My Prediction? This is a tough call, but I will go with Case in a down-to-the-last minute gem and most likely have some shocking post-match stuff (what, I don't know). Unsanctioned - Non-Title Match: Chris Courtade [c] vs Mark Langseth I'd say it's not a good sign that your main title isn't being defended on one of your major pay-per-views, but this is a different situation. From all accounts of what I've heard, Mark Langseth's career is done for real so having the chance to put the title on a basically dead-man isn't good for the company. But I also hear that it still could be a work and that this match could just turn into another heel-game by Langseth, maybe forming a stable with Courtade (I highly doubt that scenario, though). In any event, this match should be enjoyable just for the bloodshed and brutality factor that's kind of missed on for an EMWC Pay-Per-View. Langseth isn't much a brawler, but Courtade's ability in the area can make others look like they've been training with Simon Ezra. And if this is indeed Langseth's last match, expect him to pull out everything he can to make the match his best (though one has to wonder if his body will give out in the match). My Prediction? I can't see any ending that doesn't involve Courtade being the victor seeing as he's not only staying in the company, but is also the champ. Overall A thumbs-up on the pay-per-view as pretty much all the matches all good and there's something for every type of wrestling fan on the card. Worth ordering. EMWC Wrestling
