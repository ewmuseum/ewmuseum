Former cW Star Signs With mVw

14 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
mVw Chairman, TAJ Escobar, held a press conference early Tuesday morning to prove the rumors true. Escobar announced that Maximum Velocity Wrestling has indeed signed former Core Wrestling superstar, Jack Diamond. This came as a sock to many, due to the questionable reasons for which Diamond was released from cW. Escobar took the mic and stated, "I have spoken to Matthew Lanier (cW Chairman) and I am fully aware of Diamond's past, and the grounds he was released from Core Wrestling on." Referring to Jack Diamond stealing the Legacy Championship belt after defeating Skill Willie in a non-title match, less than two minutes after the bell rang. "I believe we have proved time and time again that controversy is something we welcome here at mVw, adds a little something fresh to the pot, stirs things up. I'm very excited about the signing of Jack Diamond, and I look forward to watching him work with us here at mVw," Escobar said. After TAJ stepped down from the podium, Jack Diamond himself stepped up to a suprisingly large ovation. When asked for comments on the cW situation, and why he decided to sign with Maximum Velocity Wrestling, Diamond simply stated that; "I finally found a place that will treat me with all the respect I deserve. The difference between mVw and cW is that the owner of mVw actually knows that the hell he's doing. This company's first good decision was to leave the sinking ship known as SE-Live. This company's second great decision was to hire on Jack Diamond." After which Diamond walked away from the area, not answering anymore questions. It's safe to say that things in mVw are going to be somewhat different with Diamond walking around, and we wish him the best of luck. Jack Diamond debuts at Rumble Rage in the over the top rope battle royal, January 19th. Maximum Velocity Wrestling
