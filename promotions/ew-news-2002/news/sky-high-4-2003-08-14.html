Sky High #4

14 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's been a few weeks, but Skylar Green is back with a few words for his loyal readers (all five of you). Okay, I'm back. Hope you didn't miss me too much. Busy with getting the kids' registration for school, then had to buy uniforms, actually furnish that place that we live in... general parent stuff. At the moment I'm on an indefinite leave from eW, Inc. until I can get all my duckies in a row, but I don't think that I'm going to go back the way things are. Nothing to do about the fed itself, but they hired Skylar Green, the blind man... and that's not a role I want to play anymore. For the past 22 months, I've played the role of a blind wrestler. I was good at it. I blindfolded myself, had on special contacts to impair my sight... I did the act well enough that it carried me through four different federations, all of which had some sort of network deal. It was a unique thing, sure, and I was paid well to keep it up, but look what it cost me. I couldn't talk about movies or shows I'd seen, I wasn't able to look at my kids out in public... not completely unbearable but it was a hassle, to be sure. But I give it up now. The gimmick is dead; I'm just Skylar Green now. Not the amazing blind wrestler, hopefully just an amazing wrestler now. I don't want to be hired for the gimmick, but for my abilities. I don't rock the boat, and I do what I'm asked; this, however, I will not budge on. It's over. I'm just a guy who wrestles now. With that overwith, I feel like just generally ranting about some stuff. Nothing in particular, and probably not having to do with wrestling at all. I won't get to do this very often, and I haven't been able to for about two years now, so I think I'm entitled. 1) Stop worrying about eating healthy. Everyday we're getting new terrorist warnings about people trying to hijack planes or trains or alien spaceships to go kamikaze on our asses. There's still the threat of biochemical terrorism and WMD's that are apparently being passed around like party favors over the black market. I could die today, if so I'd rather have a good meal in my stomach when I go. So forgive me if I just laugh at you when you say "Oh, don't eat that, it's too high in calories." Which one of us has our priorities messed up? 2) It's official: EVERYONE is running for governor of California. Politicians Actors Washed-up Actors Gallagher A Professional Wrestler A Sumo Wrestler Gus the Janitor... All it takes is residency, $5300, some forms, and 65 signatures from people saying that they'd vote for you to get on the ballot. Frankly, I'm surprised that Gary Coleman is running, because even he doesn't think that he has a chance in Hell of winning. Of course, we know what this election is about: giving the people what they want. Letting their voices be heard. A radical idea, one that they tried two years ago WHEN THEY ELECTED GRAY DAVIS! The economy is down because California's economy was and still is based on the stock market itself. If the market goes down, the economy goes down. It's not Gray Davis's fault, that problem was there before him and you can't expect him to fix the budget problem right away without raising tax rates through the roof. The energy problem? The people themselves caused the problem through bad habits in their usage of energy (and what happened to blaming Dick Cheney?) so you can't blame Gray Davis. And even if you do want him out of office; Arnold? What the hell is the Terminator going to do, give the budget some weights to lift? Stupid, stupid people... 3) File-sharing rocks Just thought I'd mention that. I got all my music before the punishment for it was death, or even worse, castration. Now I just download music from non-greedy artists who don't mind sharing their music with the world without a need to get a huge paycheck out of it. But they get paid off of tours and public appearances anyway so the argument against file-sharing was, is, and always will be largely a movement by record execs not to have that extra $5k taken out of their multi-million dollar salary. I think we need to amend the Law of Marginal Utility, rich people seem to work in the exact opposite way. Well, that's it out of me for now. I'm heading out to a show now. That's right, your friendly neighborhood Skylar Green is still making appearances, but in indy federations. Which ones? I'll let you look that up yourselves. Later. Sky
