Donnovan Vega Wins WOTW

7 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Washington D.C. - After spending the last month entrenched in the grueling War Of The Worlds tournament, one man is left standing above the rest of the Maximum Velocity Wrestling roster. Donnovan Vega, a name that has been a staple of mVw since it's beginnings has once again risen above the odds to become the sole survivor out of 32 combatants, battling it out for a shot at Chris Michaels and the World Heavyweight Championship at January 19ths Rumble Rage. These two former tag team partners have been ripped apart over the gold and will now meet one on one. It seems as if every time Vega has come close to the World Heavyweight Championship, it has slipped away from his grasp. As was the case in November at Annihilation when Michaels and Vega where the last men standing in the horrific Annihilation Chamber, which pitted six men all fighting it out for the most prestigious prize in this business. At the end however, it was Chris Michaels pulling it out, hitting Vega with a roll up out of no where, for the win and the gold. Donnovan Vega has been very vocal as of late stating that this time around, the gold won't pass him by. Vega was quoted as saying "A change in mVw is far over do, these people need a real champion, not Vanilla Ice." Vega, a former United States, Hardcore, and Tag Team champion, has certainly earned the right to be the World Heavyweight Champion. Defeating such mVw sensations as Luther Wisdom, Ozz and The Demented One. His showdown with "The Empire" Chris Michaels promises to be on which we will not soon forget. Maximum Velocity Wrestling
