Various News Concerning eVolution Stars

5 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A quick look at some news floating around eVolution headquarters concerning Thug roC, Sean Mason, and Scott Frost. To all of those who might've forgotten, Thug roC tonight will be live at the eVolution website for the live webcast at 6 p.m. Be sure to be there to hear what he says on backstage politics, wrestling in other forms of entertainment and more! In speaking of Thug roC, a lot of heat is being issued towards him as of late when he personally asked off of house shows. He feels that his body is no longer like it use to be, therefore he wanted a few shows to rest it. It is already known that Thug roC is on a limited contract where he only works a few shows a month not counting house shows. Since his return in March, Thug roC has had a mere 5 matches, the fewest of any active member in that 3 month span. It is said that his 'cruiser' days when he was younger took a much larger toll on him then other wrestlers. Some may think Thug roC is reaching a premadona stage, one that he's reached in the past, and some may think that he has reasons for his request. Either way, expect conflict between officials and Thug roC in the future. From the Ashes had a good buyrate this past month although the numbers aren't in yet. Backstage officials seem to be very confident in the show as many high spots and memorable moments were made. Numbers should be in later in the week. It's obvious that Sean Mason's fractured jaw will keep him out of action this week, but don't expect him to miss next week as he should be able to return. It has been released to the public that Scott Frost was NOT released from eVolution at all. The 'I Quit' thing was infact Scott Frost and eVolution's way of giving Scott Frost some time off as life with his fianceé Katherine Jackson also known as Seara of eVolution has been there lack of since Scott Frost won the Universal championship. Scott Frost did have something to say to the public. 'The match between, myself and Xander, was definately a well performed match up. Xander is a very good friend of mine backstage, and he does deserve the championship. But you better bet your sweet ass that Scott Frost will be coming back for that there strap of gold.' Frost said jokingly. 'Also, my engagment with Katherine Jackson or as most people know her as: Seara. Has started to go down hill, due to her personal life and my succeeding career. So, after a talk with Tyler Davidson owner of the eWentertainment and the eVolution franchise. He allowed me some time off to help patch up our personal life.' Frost said. In addition to this statment, it was also said that Scott Frost will be in attendence in the backstage of Legends one week from tomorrow night. He will be backstage with his friends, and cheering them on. eVolution
