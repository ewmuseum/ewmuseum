NeWA seals ESPN Taping Deal!

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After tremendous effort from both sides, ESPN and NeWA finally signed a Television agreement deal. How does that affect you? RW, BWA and OWC... Coming your way! NeWA?! Soon on your home screens. Both ESPN Networks and the National Wrestling Alliance are proud to showbout their newly signed agreement. It's been known that the National Wrestling Alliance has been aching for a deal to bump them up the federation's ranks. ESPN answered the call and agreed to take the NeWA under it's own worm care. NeWA's Head of Media Operations Chris McQ stated: "We're very satisfied with this brand new deal. We'd like to congratulate ESPN on their new signing and state that we are proud to be affiliates of the same networks as fWo and eVolution. " Tom Bracket who's been monitoring the deal commented on this matter: "The NeWA are another symbol of our growing dominance in the wrestling business. We hope that with our help and care the NeWA would be able to strive for the best and aim for the stars. We wish our best of luck to the traditional and commited alliance." Finally, viewers across the world would be able to watch their favorite NeWA stars on National Television. Until next time... National eWrestling Alliance
