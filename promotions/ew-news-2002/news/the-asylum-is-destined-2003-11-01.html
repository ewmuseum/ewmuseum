the Asylum is Destined?

1 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
tA signs much-hyped "miracle fighter", winning multi-promotional bidding war. tA looks to be getting some good publicity for once. If you've watched any news or talk shows in the past few months, you must know of the 22-year-old girl who goes by the name of Alyssa Destiny. But in case you've been living under a rock for the past few months, we'll sum it up for you anyway. Born Alyssa Harrison but legally changing her last name to Destiny, Alyssa is the daughter of famous kickboxer Steven Harrison. While training in Africa two years ago, she came down with a very rare illness, and was all but pronounced dead. However, in a miracle she recovered from her disease completely, and began training once again to relive her dream. Since completing her eight-month training schedule, she began appearing on various talk and news shows with her uplifting story, winning the hearts of millions of Americans everywhere as she negotiated with various fighting organizations for her arrival. Every promotion from those that have females in their ranks already (tA and OSF) to those that don't (PRIDE, UFC, and K1 were all reportedly planning to build a new female division around her had they signed her) were involved in the bidding war. In the end, however, the most controversial of these promotions, the Asylum, was the one who signed her to a contract. Perhaps because of the promotion's desperate need for some good publicity for once, Joe Campbell spent a lot of green on her, but he's reportedly hoping all of her mainstream hype can help find the Asylum more fans than ever before. Her debut could happen at any moment, and word coming from inside tA is that the promotion will officially announce her signing on the next edition of The Show, and begin further hyping her debut. This is definitely an interesting situation, and we'll keep you updated on it. the Asylum
