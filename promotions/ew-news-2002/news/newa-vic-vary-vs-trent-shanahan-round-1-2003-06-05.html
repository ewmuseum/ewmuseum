[NeWA] Vic Vary vs. Trent Shanahan - Round 1

5 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wednesday Night Crossfire featured the announcement of Round One in the on-going feud between "Simply Superior" Trent Shanahan and NeWA's World Heavyweight Champion Vic Vary. Also... Namada Kamazaki or Nick Hades to return! Vic Vary and Trent Shanahan: One of them won't survive! The first round of the Vic Vary vs. Trent Shanahan Sega has been announced by the President of RW. Last night's also featured the ring-order revealing for the Vic Vary Invitational. The surprise: Both Trent Shanahan and Vic Vary are in the same ring! Shanahan who hasn't gotten the chance to lay his hands on the arrogant Champion... He'll get his first and only shot before his title shot at The Vic Vary Invitational. "Simply Superior" can't wait to receive his first ever title shot. Another surprise in the ring-draw is the return of either Namada Kamazaki, former NeWA World Cruiserweight Champion or local maniac Nick Hades. One of the two would be part of Ring 1 in the Double-V Invitational. The former NCW fans are aching to see their local hero return to the scenes, even if it's in England, Ohio or Louisiana. Nick Hades would forever be remembered for the amazing battle with Kai Lewis in Halloween Hell. During that match, a girl was set on fire and Hades never returned after that fiasco. Many predict this event to be the Regional competition of the year. 40 man... 4 rings... 1 survivor... THE VIC VARY INVITATIONAL! Until next time. National Wrestling Alliance
