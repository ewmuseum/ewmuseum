CSWA Celebrates 15 Years with Giant PPV

2 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The CSWA has turned 15 years old, and its annual celebration is coming up! CSWA ANNIVERSARY 2003 has been christened CSWA15 for short, and is scheduled as the next summer pay-per-view for the venerable league. The CSWA has undergone major problems recently, between an out-of-control "invasion" by a rival league, to sagging ratings, and widespread problems with morale. CSWA owner Chad Merritt may be in the same situation he was in back in 1998 when the CSWA was trying to recover from major scandal and a court-ordered work stoppage. CSWA ANNIVERSARY 1998 was titled "Titanic: Sink or Swim." The overwhelming response to that storied pay-per-view was that the CSWA could continue to make it on an international stage despite significant obstacles. But what about now? The wrestling business is in a new political climate. The WWE has consolidated two other major wrestling promotions, the fWo has returned to the United States with a major television deal, and the fight for pay-per-view dollars is as important as ever. With the CSWA's lagging schedule and more and more taped shows playing on television, can CSWA owner turn things around with the roster that he has? CSWA15 is a watershed moment. Will the CSWA go out with a whimper, or will it recover some of its former glory days? CSWA Wrestling
