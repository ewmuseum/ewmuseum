[Empire]Keeton returns to gold and Wrestler runs for Governor

14 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jake Keeton made a return to the squared circle in Empire and came out with a win and a championship. Plus, Big Poppa plans on joining the political circus in California. Broken was live from Seattle, Washington on Tuesday night and it was explosive! In a night that named who would compete for the North American Championship at Start Game, a champion was named!! Jake Keeton, signed with Empire earlier last week and on his first official night in the Empire, won the all roster battle royal and became Empire’s first Television champion! Other high profile names made their debut in Empire at Broken also, namely Jim Rooster and Brad Daddy! Both competed against each other in a chairs match! But one of the biggest surprises of the night came from Big Poppa! On a night where Big Poppa was not only entered into the all roster battle royal and he had a falls count anywhere match with Mark Adkins, Big Poppa made a huge announcement! He plans on running for governor of California, when the recall elections take place!! Big Poppa, is a native of California, born in the capital of the state, Sacramento. To aid Big Poppa in his efforts, Empire Wrestling is providing him with complete financial backing. With all this news, the owner of Empire Wrestling being at the next show and the long awaited return to the squared circle from, what some call a wrestling legend, Razor Blade, Broken from Miami is sure to be great!! Don’t miss it!!! Empire Wrestling
