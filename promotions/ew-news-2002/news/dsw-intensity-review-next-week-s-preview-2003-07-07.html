DSW Intensity Review, Next Week's Preview

7 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DSW Hype! 'Darkside Wrestling is Cool' - Canadian Glen The DSW Hype logo appears on the screen as the lights in the studio go up and we’re introduced to Hunter Bishop behind the desk and Jonathon McCloud sat beside him. Hunter Bishop: Hello everybody and welcome to this jam-packed July 7th edition of DSW Hype. In the guest presenter seat today is the play-by-play commentator for Intensity, Jonathan McCloud. We’ve also got a few words from Big J himself as Jack Savage dropped by earlier for a quick interview, and we have some very exclusive backstage footage from last night’s show. Jonathan, great to have you on the show, let’s dive right in with the biggest story of this huge week for DSW, Nomad appearing on Intensity last night. Jonathan McCloud: And what a shock it was, not only to all the fans in the arena but to most of the talent working as well, and certainly to Ben Lake. Hunter Bishop: Well you were sat that at ringside last night, just what was the reaction from the crowd like? Jonathan McCloud: It was amazing, I haven’t heard a crowd like that since the first Beyond Help. The fans were on the edge of their seats for pretty much the entire match, but it was a whole new world once Nomad’s entrance music played. Hunter Bishop: Now the last time we saw Nomad it was on the very first episode of Intensity where he dropped the title to Andy Hartley and left to pursue his career in Japan. I’ve not heard anything about any DSW contracts being signed, and so far as we know neither has anyone else, so just what is he doing back on DSW TV? Jonathan McCloud: Well from the looks of things he was teaching Ben Lake a lesson and saving Jack Savage’s dreams of headlining Beyond Help. Hunter Bishop: Well nobody on the DSW staff would give us any kind of comment on this situation, but we do know that Nomad will be conducting an interview on next week’s Intensity. Nomad isn’t the only old face showing again, as last night’s broadcast saw the return of Dead Pool masquerading as Jynxx. Hype cuts to a clip from Intensity. Jynxx pulls his opponent up off the floor, hooks his head and plants him with a devastating Evenflow DDT! Jonathan McCloud: Hey…wait a minute… Jynxx hooks the leg and the referee counts a groggy 1…2…3! The referee raises Jynxx’s arm in victory but Jynxx pushes him away, grabs his mask and then yanks it off! Jonathan McCloud: That’s Dead Pool! Alan Anderson: What the hell?! Jonathan McCloud: That wasn’t Jynxx at all it was Dead Pool! The Atheist Anarchist is back in DSW and Scott Myers just became the first damn victim! Hype returns to the studio. Hunter Bishop: DSW’s resident anti-theologist making a shocking return there and handing the team of Blackheart and Myers their second loss for the night. Jonathan McCloud: But of course that wasn’t the last we saw of Scott Myers for the evening, as later on he got involved in yet another altercation with the North American Champion, Chaotica. Hunter Bishop: The booked match was Chaotica taking on newcomer MST, but that match didn’t even get underway as Scott Myers jumped Chaotica, leading MST to come to her aid. After a brutal backstage brawl between the three competitors, the stage is now set for next week when the tandem of Chaotica and MST will challenge Blackheart and Myers for the World Tag Team Titles. We’ll be right back. Hype goes to a commercial for the all new Darkside Wrestling action figures, including Lotus, Hawkeye, Ricky Valentine and Jack Savage. Hunter Bishop: Welcome back to Hype with me Hunter Bishop and my guest for today, Jonathan McCloud. Jon, before the break we announced the Tag Team Title match that will go down on next week’s show, who’s gonna have the upper-hand there? Jonathan McCloud: Well the tension between ‘Misogynist’ Myers and Chaotica has been building for weeks now, so it’s really going to be an intense affair when they get into the same ring. On one side you’ve got the drive and determination of a hungry team like MST and Chaotica, plus the North American Title in their ranks doesn’t hurt. But they are very much a green team, they’ve never worked together before and MST has only wrestled one match so far in DSW. As much as most of us behind the scenes are appalled by Myers’ attitude towards women, he and Blackheart are the established, experienced team, and I think Sunday night we’re gonna see why they are the champions. Hunter Bishop: Well Blackheart and Myers’ new running buddy Adrian Temple will also be in action this coming week. After losing the Contract Ladder Match last night ‘The Future’ will not be headlining Beyond Help, but he will be getting a shot at one of the most controversial wrestlers of the last fifteen years when he steps into the ring with Dead Pool. We all saw how Dead Pool made his return to DSW, and we know from past experience just how capable and brutal this man can be. So if I’ve gotta make a prediction for that match then I’m going with The New Enigma, Dead Pool. Jonathan McCloud: Wow, I couldn’t disagree with you more Hunter. Dead Pool certainly is capable and brutal, and he may well be The New Enigma, but where’s he been? I’m sure lots of wild theories about his anti-religious antics will pop up on wrestling forums, but the bottom line is he hasn’t been inside a wrestling ring. Ring rust is definitely going to be a factor in this contest, you could see some last night when he took on Scott Myers. And you can’t afford to be even the slightest bit rusty when you’re taking on someone as gifted, talented and just plain hungry as Adrian Temple. He has ended two career here so far, one of which being the near legendary Andy Hartley, so I say the future is now and The Future is going to bury The New Enigma. Hunter Bishop: When we come back we’ll have that exclusive backstage footage from last night, don’t go away. Hype goes to a commercial for Darkside Music, the latest CD containing entrance music from Tech N9ne, Audioslave, P.O.D. and 50 Cent. Hunter Bishop: Welcome back to Hype, I’m Hunter Bishop alongside Intensity play-by-play commentator Jonathan McCloud as we run through the top stories and run down the card. Now we’ve already covered the story of Nomad showing up at the TV tapings last night, and Dead Pool making his shocking return to the ring, but one former impact player from the past that no one expected to see back in Darkside Wrestling is the former World Heavyweight Champion EtcH. Jonathan McCloud: I was truly floored when Rob Halden told me that EtcH had signed a contract. For those viewers who don’t know who he is, EtcH was the Champion of DSW’s main rival VPW, at the time when competition between the two was most bitter, right up until the point that Ben Lake bought the company. What followed was a hate-filled war between the two promotions that saw EtcH do the unthinkable and beat Nomad for the World Heavyweight Title. Hunter Bishop: EtcH was backstage last night, hanging out with the boys in the locker room and talking with various staff members. From what we’ve heard Mr. Lake wanted EtcH to clock up some more time in The Studio before debuting him on TV, so that newer fans and viewers would get a feel for who he is. We now know that EtcH’s return match in DSW will be on next week’s show as he takes on Ricky Valentine. Jonathan McCloud: And what an interesting situation we’ve got next week, Hunter. Last night Ricky Valentine became the #1 Contender in the World Television Division, and Rob Halden announced that he would get his shot at Hawkeye at Beyond Help. Hunter Bishop: Indeed he did, so whilst a pissed-off Hawkeye did not become World Heavyweight Champion, he is still the World Television Title holder going in to Beyond Help. Yet this week he is denied any kind of physical match up with his #1 Contender, and instead will take on Joshua Cason in a non-title bout. But knowing the Man-Monster as we all do, I have a feeling it’s only a matter of time before he and El Chupacabra have a confrontation. And now for what we promised you at the top of the show, backstage footage from Intensity after the show ended and cameras in the ring stopped filming. This is exclusive to Hype. Hype plays the never-before-seen footage. Backstage at Intensity last night Jacob Steele is in the parking lot putting his bags into the trunk of his car, when Jack Savage limps across to him, his ribs taped up heavily. Jacob Steele: Oh next week is gonna be fun with you in this kinda shape. Jack Savage: Guess we’ll see, because so far all I seen you do is run your mouth and catch a beating. Jacob Steele: Yeah? Well I had a few private words with Adrian Temple and gave him all the goods on you, I told him everything he needed to know to put you in this condition. Jack Savage: Well I’m still standing, in fact in victorious. So if that’s all you got then I can’t wait till next week! Jacob Steele: Maybe you won’t have to! Steele lunges at Savage and the two men grapple with each other to the floor. Ricky Valentine, MST, Chaotica and a group of officials and road agents are quick to the scene and start pulling the two men apart as the recording ends. A video package hyping the debut of Jayde plays briefly. Hunter Bishop: Intensity opened last night with the return of the Masked Mercenary himself, Jacob Steele. Steele was an icon during his hey-day, drawing some of the biggest houses the company has ever seen in his main event clashes with Nomad. But it wasn’t exactly a warm reception he got upon his return. Hype cuts to a clip from Intensity. Jonathan McCloud - Jacob Steele is getting a three on one attack here, there is no way he can fight off these men. As Steele fell flat on his face, Blackheart dropped down and applied the Blacklock (crossface) and then Adrian Temple went over to his legs and hooked in the St. Paul Shooter (sharpshooter). Meanwhile Scott Myers got up and started to kick away at his ribs as Steele yelled and tap out furiously. Referees came down to the ring and started to pull off the three men but the officials all got knocked on the mat for their troubles. Alan Anderson - What do Blackheart, Myers, and Adrian Temple even have to do with each other? Why are they attacking Jacob Steele!! As the three men looked back down at Jacob Steele, 'Cochise' then hit the speakers and Jack Savage ran out and slid into the ring. Adrian Temple quickly ducked outside as Jack Savage started to go to work on both Myers and Blackheart, Savage quickly disposed of Blackheart over the top rope and then he turned around and hit the 187 on Scott Myers. The crowd going nuts as Savage cleared the ring. Savage then grabbed Steele from the back and helped him to his feet, however Steele quickly turned around and grabbed Savage .. not even looking to see how it was, applied a bear hug and threw Savage back over his head for a Steele-Plex! Hype returns to the studio. Hunter Bishop: That could well be an extremely important moment in the history of Darkside Wrestling, as it is the very first appearance of The Syndicate. Johnny Blackheart, Scott Myers and Adrian Temple have formed some kind of twisted alliance within DSW and not even an icon like Jacob Steele could stand against them. Jonathan McCloud: But also from that one moment we have got the main event of next week’s show, as that one Steele-Plex would lead toward the signing of a match between Jack Savage and Jacob Steele. Hunter Bishop: And earlier today Jack Savage dropped into the studio to give us this interview on his success last night and his upcoming match. Hype goes to footage recorded earlier in the day. Hunter Bishop: How are you feeling after that ladder match? Jack Savage: Pretty beat up I can tell you. Temple and me took it to the limit, really pushed the boundaries of what’s been seen on Intensity so far, and that has consequences. My neck and my back are killing me, and my left ankle has swollen up quite badly. Hunter Bishop: So tell me Jack, how does it feel to know you’re going to be headlining Beyond Help XXIII? Jack Savage: Big J’s excited, reporter-man. It’s not just main eventing a Pay Per View, it’s Beyond Help, the ultimate showcase, the greatest spectacle in pro-wrestling. And this year’s is shaping up to be the best of all time. Plus it’ll be all the better for Jack Savage walking out the new World Heavyweight Champion. Hunter Bishop: Now after your altercation with Jacob Steele last night you’re going to be taking him on in the main event of next week’s show. What are your thoughts on the situation? Jack Savage: The situation is just fine with me. I’m sat backstage at Intensity watching the show, watching this old has-been from DSW’s dark past get his ass handed to him about five seconds after walking through the doors by three other guys. I think to myself “Jack, is it Senior Citizen Beat Down Day?” and when the answer comes back negative, I decide to go lend a hand. And what do I get for my troubles? Nothing but attitude, bad language and Steele-Plex. Well swearing just ain’t cool kids! A lot’s changed since Jacob Steele was running around this place, and Sunday night I’m gonna show him the biggest change of all…Jack freakin’ Savage! Hype returns to the live feed from the studio. Hunter Bishop: Jack Savage there from a few hours ago. Well the main event for next week is set, it will be Big J and Jacob Steele going mano-a-mano on Intensity, be sure to check it out. That’s all we got time for this week, my thanks to Jonathan McCloud for joining me today. Don’t forget that Intensity is live and exclusive only to the Box Office Network this Sunday night, and be sure to check out darksidewrestling.com for all the latest news. The light dim, the sound stops and the feed cuts as Hype goes off the air. DarksideWrestling.com ..... Bring it on.
