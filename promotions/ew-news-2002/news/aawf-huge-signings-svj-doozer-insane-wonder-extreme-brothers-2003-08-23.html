AAWF: HUGE Signings : SVJ, Doozer, Insane Wonder, Extreme Brothers, Machine and Dane Rivers!

23 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It has been confirmed that the AAWF has signed former CWL World Champion, "Superstar" Vince Jacobs, Insane Wonder, Doozer, Dane Rivers, The Extreme Brothers, and Machine! Launch date also set! Plus More! Welcome back everyone for the 4th installment of headlines leading up to the re-launch of the AAWF after a 7 year silence. So lets get right down to business. In the third headline I promised you all I would have a transcript of exactly what happened at the Official AAWF Press Conference, and indeed I do have a transcript, although instead of reading through the entire thing, I thought I would just drop the bomb on the world by stating the main parts of the conference. I'm so excited to announce to you the first 10 members of the roster it's almost making me tingle all over... so without any further adue, I would like to tell you the following names have signed with the AAWF and will be competing from the first show! Signee number one - Former CWL World Champion, "Superstar" Vince Jacobs! An incredible wrestler, and a man that gets things done! And for the rest of the signees... The Extreme Brothers! Mike and Rich Extreme are making a return to the spotlight, apart of the AAWF, and should serve as a powerhouse team to enter the tag division...and who can count them out of the singles division. Both men are amazing superstars! Da Da Da Dane River! Mr. Rivers has also signed with the AAWF! His first fed back since he was main eventing apart of the ACW. Good Luck To Him! And, to conclude the signings, I can guarantee former fWo Cruiserweight Champion, Insane Wonder and former DWF Dream Champion, Doozer to the AAWF Roster! Two HUGE Signings here! And to add to that, rumor has it that Doozer is going for one man, and one man only, Mike Extreme. If you can remember when both guys were apart of the DWF, they had a huge feud going, and Doozer seemingly feels the hatchet is not buried yet, and he expects to bury it! And, for the last signing, it was a cold feeling that shot through my body when I heard his name. MACHINE! I have no idea who the guy is, but it was released that he hails from Alaska. I can't wait to see this guy, probally one of biggest talents going! Thats all folks...and also a premature date for the launch of the AAWF was set for September 21, 2003. See you next time with more information. Jack Metro AAWF Preview Site
