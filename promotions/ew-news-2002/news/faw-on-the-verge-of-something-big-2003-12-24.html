FAW on the verge of something "Big"

24 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
New Furiously Addictive Wrestling Talent Head, Bonita Lake, expresses her opinion that Furiously Addictive Wrestling is on the verge of something big in 2004. FAW is on the verge of something big, according to the new Head of Talent, Bonita Lake. Officials and superstars have developed a cast iron spirit in recent weeks and there has been an unprecedented energy within the federation as FAW heads into the New Year, but Bonita Lake, talking from a press conference, could not hide her delight when asked about the current situation within Furiously Addictive Wrestling. Head of Talent, Bonita Lake said: "The atmosphere is fantastic at the moment. Everbody is knuckling down and working hard." "From what I can see there is a lot of focus within the camp. The superstars are determined to make a name for themselves in 2004." Bonita Lake was asked about the roster situation within Furiously Addictive Wrestling, and whether or not we would see any new faces before the New Year or any faces after the New Year. "With everybody maintaining a high standard it is hard for me to bring in fresh talent. It is common knowledge we have a packed roster, probably too packed in all honesty." But Head of Talent, Bonita Lake admitted that new faces would arrive soon, but are unlikely to make any appearance before the New Year. "I have searched high and low for talent I feel will mesh with the current group. It's hard to integrate fresh talent with loyal, committed talent as you don't know what to expect. I'm sure the new faces will receive a warm reaction, though, and I fully expect people to be surprised by the choices we have made." Bonita Lake refused to mention any intended targets and quickly dismissed claims Apocalypse was set to walk out on Furiously Addictive Wrestling after When Worlds Collide, which takes place on January 4th, 2004 from the SBC Center in San Antonio, Texas.
