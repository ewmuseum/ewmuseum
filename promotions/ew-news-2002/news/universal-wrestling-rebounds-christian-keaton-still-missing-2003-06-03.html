Universal Wrestling rebounds, Christian Keaton still missing

3 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
When Christian Keaton disappeared, handing over ownership of his beloved Universal Wrestling Promotion to Greek Entertainment, uW seemed down and out ... but we were wrong. Universal Wrestling shut down New Mexico this past Sunday, when it's first Edition of Sunday Night Suspense aired on it's new Network, HBO. After several contract violations, the USA newtork that was home to uW's weekly show for nearly a year, canceled their television contract, and seemingly put the promotion back underground. New owner, and former Superstar, Tyler Bayne reached and agreement with the HBO network officials early Saturday morning, and Sunday kicked off a new era in the uW history. "USA couldn't handle our brand ... we've got loads of talent, and some of them can be a bit rowdy, so now we've got a license to kill, so to speak", said Bayne, commenting on the new home of Suspense. uW had been on the edge for weeks after owner Christian Keaton once again disappeared, leaving the promotion to wither away. But with the return 'BIG' Little Italy, Discoboy Johnny K, and Sadistic Scott Styles - plus the addition of stars such as Japanese Lotus, Under the Influence, and Big F'n Cat (Superfan Winner) the promotion looks to be on the rebound. And with the new weekly time slot, replacing HBO's award winning series Six Feet Under at it's nine o'clock time slot, there is alot of pressure to put out top notch programming. Bayne commented. "HBO has giving us a money slot, and were expected to produce the kind of programming that can fill that slot .... so that's what we'll do." Tune in for Suspense this Sunday, airing LIVE from Phoenix, Arizona. And don't forget to call ticket master and reserve your tickets for Requiem, live June 29th from San Fransisco California - ONLY ON PPV! Universal Wrestling
