Pressure Point 66 Report

15 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We've got a new champion! Who IS it? ACTION! PRESSURE POINT REPORT By: Keith Scott Zimmerman E-mail: [detail withheld] Date: 8.7.3 (taped 7.7.3.) The Big News: We have a NEW Action Champion--or will, by the end of the next PPV. Four-man round-robin tournament between Heimdal, Garvin, Danger, and, of course, the NEW Ratings Draw. Title Changes: I guess the stripping of LLB counts. Avalon still hasn't defended since the beatdown I layed on him. Face/Heel Turns: -- Best Match: 3. Rook bumped great for me, considering the size difference. I think the playing possum leaping uppercut's a great add to the arsenal. We polished off the old tandem Owenzuigiri off the latest WoC (see, Duncan, we didn't forget about your Silent Bob ass) and did all the classics: fake tag off a clap, jumping in the ring when the hot tag should be made. Sadly the bastards won when Rook rolled Daniel up with the tights. Worst Match: 6. Wasn't really a match, just a crazy brawl. Looks like we're segueing to KroW/Sylo. PIW LIVES! Absurdity of the Show: -- (first time for everything) Moment of the Show: Getting announced as a tournament participant. Breakdown: 1) Max continued to take a Super Big Gulp piss on his evil legacy, some tool from thReat came out and Max made him tap to the cross-armbreaker...Went up to Joe and told him if he didn't strip LLB of the title he might want to find out where UPS is hiring since a bunch of us were going to walk...Payne threw Russa around for singing karaoake, as well he should...The Dream Team of me & the Archbishop take on the non-Duel LCW refugees later...Prodigy played Kobe Bryant...2) Nice spot where they did Hogan but Prodigy countered with a cobra clutch. He bumped like a fiend and rolled up Russa on the Lion Tamer attempt. Nothing bad to say... Frenchie debuts next week. Be happy...Daniel got in Schmidt's ugly mug and backed me up...Frenchie debuts next week against Deft...KroW said Minio was dead. Minio said nuh-uh so KroW beat the UCKFAY out of him--they get tagged up later...the Arcades reiterated the earlier comments of me and Phillips, showing the importance by having the tag champs who are FACES say something...3) See above...DeNucci registered a complaint with Joe as well...The champs got ready...4) Chambers seems to be learning a lot more from Joe than from us. Maybe because we browbeat the hell out of him. They did a fallaway slam/backbreaker combo that should debut at #5 on the Things That Are Awesome countdown for the week. Chris has inner awesomness--who the hell knew? They kicked out the jams for a first time team but Chris ate the inverted brainbuster before the double Savage elbow. Still a good showing, even if we went 0-2 today... Garvin got in Joe's face...Minio talked to his assumed partner...Danger & Ramsey went to Joe--can you tell I am serious?...5) Match was odd and a sneeze, Daze hit a simple punch and GOD sold it like the Hand of God for a KO. Probably storyline related, just don't know how yet...Daze got suspended as a result...Next week will be the 101-hosted LLB DEpreciation Night, thus answering "What do you do after going 0-2 today?"...Rook & Heimdal talked to Joe and did mental fucking LAPS around him. Kudos to them, esp. Rook, for his eloquence on the subject...Jeff talked up Prodigy...Scott made his monthly appearance to be KroW's partner...Ramsey was on the TV Show and Duncan came to interrupt. They will be Benoit/Booker Ting that shit down...Avalon also went to Joe...6) See above... Edward Olmstead came out, and you can scroll up to The Big News. Grade: B-. Mostly an outside-the-ring show, setting up the stripping of the A title and what the fallout from that will be. Nothing horribly bad and since the A title's an overhanging arc from the company, I wouldn't particularly FUHFORWID anything. Next week, the tournament begins. And soon, I will be your champion. Get on the bandwagon while there's still space. Show Results: 1) [CARNAGE] Max Danger s. J. Leslie Voss 2) Clayton Prodigy d. Russa 3) Rook Black & Frank Heimdal d. ME & Daniel Phillips (Rook pins Phillips) 4) [DYAD] Arcades d. Wrestling 101 (Donell pins Chambers) 5) Donnie Daze ko. Giovanni Ollivander DeNucci 6) Bobby Minio & Sylo NC. KroW & Johnathon Scott Pressure Point 66
