Free Wrestling Tickets!

8 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
More controversy coming out of Empire. This time involving claims that Empire is stealing wrestling fans by giving them free tickets. The Staples Center is the premier spot for Empire Wrestling's first 'Broken'. On the 15th, Broken will come from L.A.'s Staples Center live on pay per view. The staples center has a seating capactiy of over 20,000. So far only 3,000 tickets have been sold for Empire's inaugural show. So Empire Wrestling has worked out a deal with Ticketmaster and the Burger King. Two for Two. If you buy two tickets for Broken thru TicketMaster, Burger King will provide you with an additional two tickes. So it's four tickets for the price of two! "Again critics are jumping all over this." Owner of Empire Wrestling, D.jack, said in a statement. "Empire Wrestling will be putting on a great show and we've already signed a guest to appear on the show!! We are just trying to give fans a quality wrestling show they can sit down and enjoy. But critics think I'm giving out free tickets to try and steal fans. I'm not. That's why we've signed deals with companies like Ticket Master, Sony and Burger King. So everyone can benefit." Deals are still being worked out between TicketMaster and Empire Wrestling, still trying to get ticket prices for all show, including pay per views, lower for fans. For now, the deal between Burger King and TicketMaster will allow more fans to watch Broken Live! Empire Wrestling
