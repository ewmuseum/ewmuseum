FAW's Scott Costella Hospitalized!

10 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Former FAW Tag Team Champion, Scott Costella, has recently been admitted to hospital. For the past Four days, Scott Costella has been in hospital recovering from his fall, down a flight of 18 concrete steps, this past Sunday at Panic, in his hometown of Detroit. It seemed that Scott was angry with himself after loosing to Chris Thunder and getting knocked out of the Diabolical Day tournament and therefore wasn't watching where he was walking. While only suffering a fairly mild concussion and mild bruising of the ribs, Doctors wanted to keep him in until they could be sure he was okay. Scot remained unconscious for roughly ten hours after the fall and was taken to a local hospital in his hometown of Detroit. His parents were called and were soon by his side. Doctors say he will be able to take part at Diabolical Day and there is no risk of further injuring his ribs, as they should be healed up by the weekend. Scott will be released from hospital on Friday the 11th. Furiously Addictive Wrestling
