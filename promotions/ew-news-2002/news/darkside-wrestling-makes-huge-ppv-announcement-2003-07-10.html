Darkside Wrestling Makes Huge PPV Announcement

10 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DSW Announces World Television Championship Match For It's biggest Annual PPV los angeles, ca. thur. july 10, 2003 The stakes have just been raised yet again. On Sunday night at Darkside Wrestling’s television broadcast Intensity it was announced that Ricky Valentine would challenge Hawkeye at Beyond Help MMIII for the World Television Championship. Valentine won his #1 Contendership in a hard-fought battle against DSW mainstay Johnny Blackheart, and the “Man-Monster” Hawkeye has been champion ever since winning the first-ever No Escape Match at Xtreme Warfare. These two superstars are scheduled to clash at DSW’s biggest Pay Per View event of the year in a match that already has fans and insiders alike talking. The story broke in the early hours of this morning when a press release was issued from the offices of Rob Halden, Executive Producer of Darkside Wrestling. The release stated that Mr. Halden was now making said confrontation an Iron Man Match with a set time-limit of thirty minutes. This means that Hawkeye and Ricky Valentine will wrestle for thirty minutes straight, non-stop, with the winner having the most decisions in his favour at the end of the time limit. This marks the first Iron Man Match in the history of Darkside Wrestling, and just the latest of many bold moves made by Mr. Halden since taking over control of the World Television Division. A dynamic strategy of ruthless talent signings to his division, the booking of high-drawing matches and even the creation of the No Escape Match has ignited fan interest in the title and division again. Darkside Wrestling Owner Ben Lake said: ”Rob has really turned things around in the last month, the World Television Title is quickly becoming the most sought after championship around. I’m positive the Iron Man Match at Beyond Help will only escalate the competition for the belt.” Executive Producer Rob Halden said: ”It’s a gruelling, gruelling match, but one that I know both [Ricky] Valentine and Hawkeye are more than up to. We’re going to see something very special at Beyond Help, because simply put, I only allow the very best to compete for the World Television Title.” Beyond Help MMIII takes place on July 27th in the Sapporo Dome, Japan.
