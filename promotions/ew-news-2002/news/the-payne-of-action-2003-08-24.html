The Payne of Action!

24 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An insight in to recent backstage issues at Action! Wrestling After a night of hiding in the towel basket of the Action Wrestling locker room at a recent event I realised that the backstage atmosphere at Action Wrestling is in a dire state which is in desperate need of immediate attention. I over heard conversations between Reed Young and roster members, board directors, and other staff members. All I heard was pissing and moaning. There were roster members bitching about the authority figures, the authority figures bitching about other authority figures, the works. Around two weeks ago the backstage crew, the roster and the staff members were said to have ”Buried the hatchet”, but from what I can tell that just isn’t the case. I mean, most guys seem happy enough, but two men seem to be stirring things up, one man more than the other. The main offender would be Payne Fosters, the other offender, surprisingly, was Bobby Minio. Payne was the man that brought up the topic, Bobby seemed to be simply defending himself and attempting to make Payne drop the topic. Of course, Payne did no such thing and continued with his assault on the board of directors and other staff members, dropping a few names that I shall not mention. Many people will be thinking as they read this article… “Why am I being told this?” Well, I have to be honest, I don’t know. I just felt people should know, I felt that people should understand that large-scale federations have problems too. I guess I am writing this to inspire people who are at the helm of smaller scale federations, to let them know that no matter what problems come their way, they can overcome them. Action have had problems like this before, as have other large scale productions such as fWo, thReat, The Asylum and many, many more. And all of those promotions have overcome their problems and become stronger because of it. Allow me to go back to the topic of Payne Fosters and his un-burying of the hatchet. It makes me sad to know that one man's immaturity is seemingly dragging the moral of an entire federation down. Action and its employee’s had buried the hatchet on the recent disagreements and laid them to rest little over a week ago and now Payne Fosters, with his silly, immature remarks behind peoples backs is going to be dragging back the past. Payne Fosters is a weak man, weak in the sense that, no matter what happens, he cannot let go of the past… Payne Fosters is an immature guy, a selfish bastard and an out-and-out asshole… For now, this is ‘Mister. E’, signing off. Action!
