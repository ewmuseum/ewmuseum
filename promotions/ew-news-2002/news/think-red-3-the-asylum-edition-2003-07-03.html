Think Red #3 : the Asylum Edition

3 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With all that is going on in wrestling world, one certainly needs to find solace in the fact that the revolution WILL continue! Comrades, Praporschik's attention is now levelled towards the most lawless of all places: the Asylum. Greetings Comrades! It is I, Ivan Stanislav here once again with another edition of most unbiased piece of print in existence: Think Red! While one may think that this column would be devoted towards the debacle that could only be known as Ascension, or perhaps the implosion of the WO community, it is not. We have better things to pay attention to. Like revolution! And when one thinks of revolution, certainly one federation in particular sticks out in minds of those in wrestling community: the Asylum. As Ivan thinks of the Asylum, he cannot help but be confused. Of course, those federations that are outside of scope of Old School Entertainment sphere of influence generally have no bearing on anything Ivan Stanislav cares about. It is not ignorance, mind you, but merely fact that the flies that buzz around are not nearly as important. But the Asylum has indeed gotten Ivan Sergeiovich’s attention. Consider for yourselves if this is good or bad thing. The research that Ivan has done on this ‘federation’ has brought together some startling and unnerving results. I use ‘federation’ like so because, quite frankly, it appears that anarchy rules in this establishment. It appears that the participants are protected by some form of amnesty that prevents them from criminal action by the corrupt United States government if terrible things happen to their roster, and more often than not, this is the case. They must be paying those capitalist pigs much money for this protection, but nevertheless they have achieved it. But the Asylum, for all of its brutality and barbarism and childishness, does have much potential. This is the tenor of my column today, Comrades. Imagine, for once, if the Asylum were to finally gain some semblance of order? With communist banners strewn about and the workers all being treated equal! Imagine, if you will, if rules were enforced and the weapons that normally are illegal were no longer seen on television! Imagine, just for moment, if four letter words were taboo and higher thinking could be achieved! That, indeed, would be beautiful thing. As Ivan looks at the OSE and how lawless it is, he cannot help but eye the other federations of the area. Fortunately for the Asylum, they have garnered The Red Army’s attention now. Therefore, this is what Ivan envisions: The Asylum will shed the chains of its anarchy and embrace communism! It will become an extension of the already mighty Red Army and become controlled by the sovereign authority of Mother Russia! Those that do not conform to the wonderful ways of communism will be handled personally by The Red Army. I believe our track record for ‘reeducation’ speaks for itself! Asylum! Ivan Stanislav is watching you carefully! Those of you that wish to continue your anarchist ways and show your inability to be effective leaders will be liquidated. Those that are true communists will become mighty leaders of wrestling world! You never know when The Russian Bear will appear… As for the OSE… watch yourself, Stanislav is watching you! Comrades, I have little time left to continue with this column. However, let it be known that the Asylum is now set to be a core example of communism at work. The OSE WILL change to my ways eventually, but the Asylum could very well be next in line! Comrades of the Asylum! Think about your future! Think about the amazing benefits! But always.. ALWAYS Think Red!! …and if you deny that… prepare to me purged… DYAAHAAHAA!! Praporschik Ivan Sergeiovich Stanislav Red Army Commander Old School Entertainment
