Important IWO Information.

21 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The IWO has been off to a decent start since it's reopening. The IWO released it's first set of DVDs today with some interesting extra features. Fans at the July 5th show flocked to our DVD section and picked up our first show, Hostile Takeover. The DVD included in the set for a total of twenty five dollars was a second disc with the greatest matches from the 2003 Hall of Famers. The DVD for the Beach Party VII will be available for purchase sometime this week on the website or at the August 9th show. Here are the recent statistics from our past three shows: Hostile Takeover: Battle of the Belts, May 31st, 2003 @ The Murphy Rec Center in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania Paid attendance: [standing room only] 523 ( $20 for general admission and $30 for ringside seats) Sales from Merch (DVDs, VHS, shirts, hats, posters, autographed 8 by 10‘s): $1,344 Total gate: $12,804 Despite Beach Party being a big show for the IWO, [name withheld] decided that it was for the fans and they should get a bargain. He lowered the price of admission for the show. Beach Party VII, June 21st, 2003 @ The New Jersey Boardwalk Paid attendance: 323 ( $5 for general admission and $10 for ringside seats) Sales from Merch (DVDs, VHS, shirts, hats, posters, autographed 8 by 10‘s): $1,032 Total gate: $3,147 Blood, White and Blue, July 5th @ The Trocadero in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania Paid Attendance: 563 ($15 for general admission, $30 for ringside seats) Sales from Merch (DVDs, VHS, shirts, hats, posters, autographed 8 by 10‘s): $2,492 Total gate: $12,437 [name withheld] was very excited about the gates, "We're doing really good business. A lot of the guys are working hard and the fans are plunking their money down to see a good show, and that's what they are getting," he said to me today. "We've got a few stiffs, but we're not asking them to return and we've hired some new wrestlers instead." This is Greg Parker signing off. Click here to purchase the Hostile Takeover DVD!
