Wrestling Television Station Debuts Tonight

7 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wrestling Television, better known as WTV, officially kicks off tonight, and they fully realize that in the world of wrestling, tonight is "thrust or bust" time. ^That's not the WTV logo...just pointing that out. After weeks of buildup and overall hard work, Wrestling Television, or simply WTV, is ready to debut it's Monday Evening Lineup tonight to the immense amount of wrestling fans across the country. WTV is and was the brainchild of it's President and Owner, Joseph Shocker, who had been working on the idea and everything to get it rolling for years. After finally getting enough done, Mr. Shocker announced to the wrestling world his plans, and to mixed results. "Unfortunately," Mr. Shocker remarked, "WTV was not as popular of an idea as I had hoped. Some really enjoyed the idea, and sent me many kind words of praise. Others were skeptical as to whether or not a television station just for wrestling could ever work. Either way, the good outweighed the bad so we went with it. And less than one month later, we're already ready to roll." Ready they are. In his first ever official press conference, Mr. Shocker let the world know he was looking for six good strong up and coming, or already established wrestling federations. Instead he got ten. "We actually got more than we expected out of the wrestling world, and because of that we knew to just keep pushing forward regardless of the skeptics. Besides," Mr. Shocker joked, "it's not like I have been getting bomb threats yet." The ten federations that have signed up consist of the American Wrestling Alliance, Below Zero Wrestling, Central States Wrestling Alliance, Championship Wrestling Federation, Extreme Wrestling Entertainment, Firk'n Hardcore Wrestling, Global Wrestling Organization, International Championship Wrestling, Radically Awesome Wrestling, and National Pro Wrestling, which signed up mere hours before the opening festivities. "We know there's a long road or a short road ahead of us." Mr Shocker said. "Both will require hard work, but only one has a future, and that's the long road. It's the short one that we do not want to end up on. Therefore we know, it's 'thrust or bust' time. We're either going to shoot to the top, or we're going to hit rock bottom." WTV Programming starts tonight at 6:00 PM Eastern Time, with newcomers to the wrestling scene, Global Wrestling Organization, and their show eXodus, which has already debuted, just not on WTV. That's followed by American Wrestling Alliance's Defiance at 8:00, and CWF's Monday Night Massacre at 10:00. BZW's Monday Rising was scheduled for 4:00, but the show is not as of yet ready to air. Tonight starts what could be a future staple in wrestling television. One can only wonder what is in store for all of us tonight, and for the future down the road. Visit Wrestling TV Here
