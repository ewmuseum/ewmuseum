Dynamite preview 29/09/2003

28 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The MWF heads home to Illinois, it's home territory to kick off a whole two months of shows... Monday Night Dynamite heads to Illinois to stamp its mark back on the home terrority and a card of delights awaits. Craig Clayborn will open his account with the MWF as he takes on Sean West, "Desperado" is currently on one hell of a losing streak and will view this as an opportunity to finally get a win in the MWF, The Vine makes his in-ring return to the MWF and he'll be all fired up, Seth Caliko makes his MWF in-ring debut to and will no doubt he'll be just as competitive. Iniquity takes on Tommy Raines next in another newcomer versus roster match, Raines has made an impressive start in the MWF but not as spectacular as Iniquity who has risen swiftly up the ranks to become one half of the MWF Tag Team Champions. Two extra men have been added to the match, The Jeopardizer a former MWF Xtreme and Tag Team Champion as well as Doomsday who will be looking for his second victory in his MWF career. NomaD and Zach Fredricks will meet face to face again, they can't surely go any better than their contest at Implosion which saw NomaD retain his MWF Xtreme Championship but no doubt they tension will be unbearable as they meet in a non-title match. Another non-title match will be the main event as Insane Wayne takes on MWF World Champion Kidd Kidman. Kidman was somewhat of a surprise winner at Implosion and Wayne takes him on with the full hope of winning. The MWF President agreed to the match and the stipulation that if Wayne wins Iniquity will receieve an MWF World Championship shot next week on Dynamite. The stakes are high, but who will come out on top? Maximum Wrestling Federation
