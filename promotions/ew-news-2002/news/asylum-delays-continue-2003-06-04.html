Asylum delays continue?

4 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chris Richards gives us a special report on the recent delays in scheduled events for fighting promotion the Asylum and offers theories on why they haven't aired yet. Chris Richards here, bringing you a special report on the current state of events with Asylum Fighting. As many of you that follow the promotion will know. There have been massive delays with regard to many of the recently scheduled events for the promotion; we are still yet to see broadcasts for shows set to air on the 18th, 25th and 31st of May. The most glaring of these absentees is perhaps the event on the 31st that was set to be Asylum's monthly PPV, Turmoil. The promotion and indeed the promoter Joe Campbell are notorious for delaying their events at the drop of a hat but never before have we seen such a large void in broadcasts, this huge delay has spurned many theories thus far but I believe that given my position with the company I can narrow it down to the more likely possibilities. The first and perhaps most likely scenario is that in the past three weeks something, or several things have occurred which Joe Campbell does not want you to see. He has been known to delay events where things have not gone to plan in order to come up with a means of rectifying the issues before the media can get involved and essentially ruin his element of surprise. If he's kept things off the air for this long the chances are that when we see the Asylum again, whatever did transpire will have already been dealt with. Another possibility is complete removal of broadcast rights with TNN, who have recently come under heavy fire from many authority figures for broadcasting such explicit material. If such is the case it may be that we don't see the Asylum for an extender period of time, if in fact we see it return to television at all. Many have suggested that this is likely not the case given that up until present day the Asylum and those involved have almost been immune to action by the authorities. There is feeling however that regardless of this, it is only a matter of time before steps are put in place to bring an end to Joe Campbell's violent and supposedly untouchable empire. We should certainly know more in coming weeks, be it the return or indeed final exit of the Asylum... I will bring you the latest news as it occurs. the Asylum
