FFWA Making Waves

24 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The FFWA's first televised event a blast, next week's booked, and a huge PPV Announcement! Last night, or well early this morning at around midnight, the Utopia Network aired the triumph television return of the FFWA! Black Tuesday was set to be a massive return for the FFWA and sure enough it was. The opening match saw two of FFWA's development tag teams battle it out in what was said to be 'one hell of a match' by Edmonton reporter Sebastian Montesque. Another reporter said, 'There was so much action that I never wanted to get up..' and later said '...it brought a tear to my eye to think that the world of wrestling may actually have a future again.' Black Tuesday was filled with important announcements, major ocurring and the debuting of future stars in this profession. One major announcement mentioned on the website and other top sites on the internet was that the FFWA has an open invitation to all wrestlers the world over to come and take part in their first Pay-per View Resurrection. The invitation is for the Main Event world title match. Details on the match can be found at the Resurrection website, there is a link available on the FFWA website to it. With one major week in the FFWA just passed, and even bigger ones to come, FFWA Commisioner 'The Josh' Joshua Kosidlo only had this to say, 'It just gets better and better.' Their next card is another Black Tuesday, coming to you live one minute prior to midnight Tuesday late night on the Utopia Network, check your local listings, and you don't want to miss this week, where the FFWA will decide what four wrestlers will be placed into a mini round robin tournament for the Old-School Championship, and in the MAIN EVENT the Extreme Championship will be defended, and after a controversial title win just last night/ this morning this should prove to be very, very interesting.
