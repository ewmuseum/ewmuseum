Chris Michaels On His Way Back?

15 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
mVw Superstar and former quad crown champion, Chris Michaels, could be back in the ring as soon as October 27ths Malice. Michaels was thought to have severe damage to his neck and spine after falling head first from the top of a ladder to the arena floor, but after further examination, it was found the injuries where much less than originally though. Michaels rejoined the mVw crew on the road, when they returned from a tour of western Europe October 15th in Sacramento. Michaels was spotted in the ring, with mVw road agent Chris Paine, before doors opened working on basic maneuvers and getting his timing back. Michaels looked to have not lost a step, and we at mVw anxiously anticipate his return to action. As he will most likely have his sights set on L.A. Johnny Stylez and former manager/friend Chairman TAJ Escobar, for putting him out of action. Maximum Velocity Wrestling
