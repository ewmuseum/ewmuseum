The X issues statement concerning Rent-A-Hero

15 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greg weaver has released a statement revealing The X's tie with the former PCW star On The X’s recent “After Dark” show, a superstar of wrestling surprised the capacity stripclub crowd with his presence. During a gang assault on Nathan O’Neill after his impromptu match with Saikyo System member, Lotus, the masked hero, Rent-A-Hero dashed out to the ring to the aid of the young Irishman. RAH, as he’s popularly known, drove all members of the Saikyo System out of the ring with their tales between their legs as the former PCW star stood tall. The appearance sparked rumours the Rent-A-Hero had walked out of his contract with thReat and signed with the Californian based promotion, but executive Greg Weaver has categorically denied any written contract had been agreed between the federation and Rent-A-Hero. “We’d love to secure RAH’s signature, but the matter of the fact is that we couldn’t afford to pay him week in, week out at his rate and pay thReat compensation on top of that. It’s just not feasible for a company in our position,” Weaver said, “but we have come to a verbal agreement with him. Rent-A-Hero will appear on After Dark and any other events The X decides to hold whenever he wishes to appear for a small cheque that would please our bank managers.” So, it seems Rent-A-Hero will be appearing on shows for both threat and The X for the foreseeable future, which is sure to please his legions of fans.
