Column review, what a waste

16 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ésta es la historia de un hombre y de su tentativa de permanecer despierta mientras que lee con todos estos perdedores pathetic que me cuentan sus historias de la vida. Dios, usted que todo aspira tanto compaired a mí. As the fWo Internet Champion it is my duty to come onto the internet and talk to all of the idiots. Which means, yourself. Now please esse, after reading the other articles I see how some people try and come onto the web and talk trash to point out what an evil person they are. Please. Anyone can write smack hommes, but deep down most of you other writers are just pathetic, fat losers behind a keyboard. A chimp can write trash, a God like myself can do so much more. So this is my review of these columnists, such as they are, and I'll save the best for last. First you have Sean Studd. He was boring months ago and is boring now. This is a pathetic loser still clinging onto the tattered remains of that shit organization PIW. Hey esse, it's nothing to brag about when your promotion opens and closes more often than Desire's dressing room door. Even though some of you whining, pathetic babies still talk about the great days, the rest of us all see you brainwashed idiots for what you are. PIW was never a top promotion. The only people bragging about it were the stupid wrestlers themselves who had drank so much Kool-Aid and been bent over the owner's desk with a jar of KY that they believed that their trailer park of a promotion was something. This "legendary" promo you cut with Vincent wasn't funny, wasn't memorable, and had the entertainment value of rubbing a cheese grater against that little gringo twig you have between your legs. Here's a promo for you: you and your promotion was filled with little sixteen year old boys who found entertainment in pathetic gay jokes because you were all taking it up the backside from your pathetic leader. You hommes, you're too stupid to understand how much you got sodimized... not even Ender would stick it to you as often. Mi dios. I don't know who Keith Scott Zimmerman is or why I should care about Action. The whole name of the fed is the exact opposite of what you get there. Same about [name withheld]. I like the little picture on the web of one of the wrestlers getting ready to bury the belt. I agree hommes, get that stinking thing below dirt. Then you have Vincent Pembridge waving pom-poms for some other promotion... oh, who cares esse. Using the word 'cunt' doesn't make you a man. No comments lately from the big dumb Russian bear. Perhaps a Vodka truck crashed near his little communist shack. Aimz, a wrestler in my promotion who is currently holding a title for me, came on to talk smack about something. I was trying to understand what she was writing esse, but the little girlie-street language just made my head hurt. After I get done beating the hell out of Poison Ivy perhaps I'll smack around the little red head with the big flapping mouth. You know what they say esse, get in the kitchen and make me pie. Leave the fighting to the men... such as most of them are. As for Ivy... love you, like you, or hate you, I will never be able to ignore you? I'm sorry, I didn't hear the rest of what you were saying because I was wondering how the red flush will look on your backside after I bent you over my knee and paddle you in the middle of the ring. Good job, esse, on the mock outrage of pointing out your little political games. If Silver Fox wasn't such a flaming dandy then I'd suspect that it was you who took care of getting Eli Flair's little title shot in a couple weeks. But knowing our great "leader" and his little preferences perhaps it was the Original Nobody becoming the Original Know Body on his knees, esse. Kisses to your little toy... I hope he enjoys watching what I'm going to do to you. I'd love to be the little smarky internet heel. Sadly I must just stick to being the legendary God that I am. Mi dios, hommes... it's my burden to bear.
