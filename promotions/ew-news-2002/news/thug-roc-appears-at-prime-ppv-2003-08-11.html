Thug roC Appears at PRIME PPV

11 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thug roC makes an appearence at PRIME's Free Fall Sunday Night! Free Fall, PRIME's latest pay-per-view was thought to be a dud. Many believed that the card was heavily booked wrong, and was going to be a waste of everyone's time. But PRIME showed all doubters wrong by producing a great show. One of the many surprises on the show, was the appearence of Thug roC! Thug roC, who has been in the spotlight for the whole Shane Whethers debacle, made an appearnce backstage on PRIME cameras. He and PRIME owner, Taun Pham had a verbal fight over Thug roC's release from PRIME. Thug roC surely made his presence known to the public that night, as he accidently shoved Pham out of the way from a bullet at the end of the night. We have yet to find out what Thug roC's intentions that night were, as the gunfire put a halt to the verbal argument he had with Pham. Is Thug roC still employed by PRIME Wrestling? Was he at Free Fall to cause trouble? Will he show up at the next ReVolution? Only time will tell, as nobody is quite sure what Thug roC has up his sleeve
