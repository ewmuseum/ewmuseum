UWC Signs Ohio-Based Wrestling Promotion...the MWF!

4 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Recently formed UWC has signed up a new exciting product to represent it's Mid-East section. The MWF, Maximum Wrestling Federation has been in operation for six months and has impressed many with it's well produced style. UWC approached the MWF about a deal and the MWF put pen to paper to claim the Mid-East section, running states Ohio, Illinois and Iowa. Recently formed UWC has signed up a new exciting product to represent it's Mid-East section. The MWF, Maximum Wrestling Federation has been in operation for just six months and has impressed many with it's well produced style. UWC approached the MWF about a deal and the MWF put pen to paper to claim the Mid-East section, running states Ohio, Illinois and Iowa. The Maximum Wrestling Federation, owned by Edward Walker and Joe Forrest (AKA Fozzy), has worked it's way steadily to become a well known Ohio promotion which has now toured through Illinois and Iowa producing strong ratings on local networks with is leading Monday Night Dynamite show. The MWF's General Manager Fozzy was very enthusiastic about bagging the deal with UWC and he commented "UWC is the MWF's chance to shine in a brighter light. Viewings figures should double due to the publicity, and I wager that several of our stars will benefit hugely from the Inter-Fed competition". The MWF's President was unavailable for comment at the current time. The UWC looks to be starting strongly and the first Breakdown card is expected soon...the MWF will be producing Monday Night Dynamite on June 9th as they tour Minesota. After the chaos that was MWF: MAYhem then new MWF World Champion Anarkist looks to bring the 'Chaotic Future' he promised to the MWF...but Sam King will have other ideas. Maximum Wrestling Federation
