Return of eVolution's Cell Division

2 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After nearly one full year of inactivity the once feared Cell Division has returned to the airwaves as part of eVolution and their attempts to bring in some new audience members and boost the ratings as they rapidly approach their one year anniversary on July 3rd. After nearly one full year of inactivity the once feared Cell Division has returned to the airwaves as part of eVolution and their attempts to bring in some new audience members and boost the ratings as they rapidly approach their one year anniversary on July 3rd. The Cell was a casualty of financial trouble in the early days of the eVolution promotion and also a deal that the company was forced to make with it’s original network sponsors in Splink l Parp who constantly badgered the eVolution offices to tone down the violence on their products before they pulled the plug on them altogether. As though that wasn’t enough bad publicity for the division the subsequent death of LeRoy Mohammed as a result of injuries suffered in one of the early Cell fights helped to add to the decision by Daniel S. to shut the division down for what he thought would be good. With the recent shake-ups in eVolution and Tyler Davidson once again taking over full control of the company from Daniel S. however the Cell Division which Tyler had started has once again made its return to television at the recent From the Ashes pay per view. Tyler has quickly appointed former eVolution Commissioner, Cameron Griffin as the man that will be in charge of re-establishing the Cell Division and rumors have already started concerning what the mind of Griffin has in store for it and the rumors that of a second individual that is assisting him in the duties. Who this man is remains a complete mystery to everyone including Tyler Davidson but the idea of a Chamber of Survival match for the Cell Championship has already surfaced and is heavily rumored to go down at a New Dawn on June 29th at Madison Square Garden. eVolution
