[CEW] I Say 'Jump' ... Do You Ask 'How High?'

5 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CEW World Champion laid down the law this past Tuesday night to his chosen challenger: AC. It was two weeks ago that Muzzolini was faced with a choice ... who to grant the Rage In The Cage title shot. The choices? North American champion Kris eGo; Da*Link*Went, Muzzolini's rival over the last few months, who had taken him to a time-limit draw two months before; perennial contender and holder of the longest title reign in history, "Showtime" Nick Alexander; and five-time CEW World Champion "The Big" AC. Each CEW superstar made his case to be granted the title shot, both at the press conference held days after High Impact, and at Thrill two weeks ago. But in the end, it was still Muzzolini's decision ... and no one could possibly sway that man one way or the other ... his mind is too sharp to be swayed. The stage was set for a tension filled night. "Showtime" Nick Alexander kicked off the show, all but ordering Muzzolini to give the title shot to the Demon's Head. In typical fashion, by the end of the segment, all four potential challengers and the World Champion had made their way out to the arena floor. Muzzolini announced that his challenger had already been determined, but the challengers, and fans alike, would have to wait until that evening to find out who would faced Muzz at Rage In The Cage. As the night wore on, the anticipation grew. Finally, Muzzolini entered the ring, calling out his potential challengers, saying that he wanted to announce his choice to their faces. Muzzolini ran down each of the four men, telling them why they deserved it, but, for those that he did not choose, why they didn't deserve it. First, it was Kris eGo that was escorted to the back by security. Then, Da*Link*Went made his way out of the ring. It was down to SNA and AC. And then there was one. Muzzolini ordered security to escort Alexander from the ring, telling the Demon's Head that he shouldn't have questioned Muzzolini's authority. Alexander and the champ had agreed to a title match months before, and Alexander had been granted that shot just the week before at High Impact, in a fatal four-way. Alexander wasn't happy, he wanted a one-on-one ... but he's not going to get it. Muzzolini vs. AC. The greatest number of title reigns versus, perhaps, the greatest CEW champion. Two masters of the mind game. To win at Rage In The Cage ... neither man can allow himself to be broken in this mind game fight. Both men will bend, but, in all likelihood, neither will break. AC got his first test the very next week. Monday night, his training was interrupted by a call from CEW officials, saying that he needed to be at Thrill the next day. Muzzolini had put the challenger into a match against Lord Skywolf. But it didn't end there. Saying that he wanted AC to "jump" for him, Muzzolini ordered the five-time champion to lay-down for Lord Skywolf at the five-minute mark. The list of CEW stars to defeat AC is slim, the list of clean victories slimmer still. Kris eGo, "Showtime" Nick Alexander, Top Gun, and Aaron Gaspar are four of them, all former champions. But now AC was being told to do the job. Not asked to, not requested, ordered to. And at stake? His title shot. Muzzolini threatened to fake an injury if the number one contender didn't lie down. AC tried to fight the order. He tried to put Skywolf away before the five minute mark. But he was unable to, the color draining from his face as the clock hit five minutes. Muzzolini appeared on the 'Tron, ordering AC to lie down and for Skywolf to pin him. Both men did so reluctantly, Skywolf not appearing to want any more part of this than AC. But then a possible savior appeared, in the form of the television champion, and Skywolf's former tag team partner Cassandra Avery. Avery hit the ring, delivering repeated chair shots to Skywolf to prevent the pin. She told AC that he didn't have to lie down ... but she didn't understand. AC knew he didn't have a choice. He knew the shot at the title belt was on the line ... and he knew that if he blew this, he may not get another shot. "The Big" AC blasted Avery with an ACT, much to Muzzolini's delight. The champion then allowed the frustrated challenger to pin Skywolf and seal the victory. Will AC be made to "jump" again this week? Who knows ... well, who, other than Muzzolini. The reasons for Muzzolini's actions are unclear. Does he want to demonstrate control over AC? Prove to the world that the gold is more important to the big man than his dignity or honor? Or does he just want to show that while you may not be able to teach an old dog new tricks, you can certainly still lead them around on a leash? While the purpose may be unclear, the results are crystal. AC began to lose his focus last week. Muzzolini's order, along with his threat to pull himself from the match, took AC's attention away from his training, or his attempts to get the upper hand in this mind-game matchup, and forced him onto his heels. AC's back is against the proverbial wall ... and he may not be able to find his way out in time for Rage In The Cage. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Also last week on Thrill 130 ( http://thrill.cewentertainment.com/archive/130.htm ) Ryan Anderson defeated Shank and Driver in a debut handicap match, then faced off in-ring with fellow rookies Weezle and Michael Moseley. Weezle and Moseley ended up coming with blows, with Weezle being shoved out of the way before coming back with a spinning heel kick to Moseley. Brian Blazer successfully defended his FTS championship against Weezle due to interference by Moseley. Responding to Weezle's spinning heel kick, Moseley hit the ring as it appeared Weezle may win the FTS belt, hitting Weezle with a spear and leaving him at the mercy of Blazer. This week Blazer will defend his title against Moseley with Weezle set for guest commentary. Kris eGo defended his North American title against Mark Knight in a largely technical bout. eGo used the ropes to defeat Knight, with both men countering each other's technical moves. Longtime CEW followers have said that eGo may be the second, only after Jeremy Hunter, to be able to hang with Knight inside the ring ropes in a technical match-up. Look for these two to possibly continue in the future. Speaking of Jeremy Hunter, a large majority of the wrestling world collapsed in SHOCK as SJH returned to the CEW ring to face off against Nick Alexander. The week before, an Alexander interview had been interrupted by a flash of "FEAR" on the 'Tron. This week, Alexander called out the man responsible for the interruption. After repeated call-outs, no one had shown, until a young woman and her son appeared on stage. These two were recognized as Jeremy Hunter's estranged wife and adopted son. Alexander, however, had ended Hunter's career months before, interrupting Hunter's retirement with a blatant attack. Everyone feared that Hunter would never return to the CEW, but even with crutches, SJH did return to the ring. Alexander, however, was not influenced by Hunter's return. Seeing the crutches and believing Hunter to not be a threat, the Demon's Head preceded to run down his former friend and the former CEW superstar. However, once SNA had turned his back ... the crutches dropped. Alexander turned around and was promptly met by a Primal Fear in the middle of the ring. Hunter left the semi-conscious SNA in the ring, his message of "FEAR" properly delivered. However, on the same night that saw the return of a formerly injured star, the CEW might have lost a member of their roster, perhaps for good. In the main event, Da*Link*Went faced former FTS champion Phoenix Murdoch, hoping to get revenge for an Upstarts (of which Murdoch is a member) spoof on the retirement of Descendent and re-formation of D'z Nutz (which Link leads). The two rapidly rising stars put on a Match of the Year candidate, well deserving of ****. In the last moments of the bout, Murdoch had gained the upper hand, hitting Link with his finisher, the Suplexcide, and was setting Link for a Suplexcide off the top-rope. Link countered, instead hitting his finisher, the Corkscrew Piledriver, off the top-rope. Link easily won after that huge move, though it may have come at a greater cost than a loss for Murdoch. The referee quickly called for medics, who found Murdoch in an injured state. Kris eGo, the Upstarts leader, announced that Link may have injured Murdoch enough to put him out for the rest of his career ... though, again, Murdoch's injuries have yet to be specified. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- This week on CEW Thrill: Murdoch's health status will be announced, as his Upstarts partner and North American champion, Kris eGo, looks for revenge against Da*Link*Went. Has Muzzolini set up another match for AC? Another test that AC must pass before their match at Rage In The Cage? Last week Cassandra Avery nearly thwarted Muzzolini's plan, though the message was still loud and clear. This week, AC faces Upstart member Dru Jericho. It's been nearly 30 days since Cassandra Avery last defended the TV title. She must defend the belt, or it will be stripped. Her opponent? Her former tag partner, and the man that cost her the North American title, LORD SKYWOLF! Brian Blazer takes on another CEW rookie for the FTS title: Michael Moseley is in the ring. Last week Moseley cost Weezle a possible FTS victory, this week Weezle sits in as special guest commentator! Shayne Gowers returns to CEW television. The man wants to turn his sights to hardcore fighting ... he'll get a challenge ... what kind of challenge, who knows, but he'll get one. The self-proclaimed future king of Hardcore will face the king of Old School (and we do mean old ... like, Hogan-old) EDDIE ACE! And to open the show, two future CEW hall of famers, and former CEW superstars collide. For weeks Allen Watts has been trying to get a contract to re-join CEW and re-join his friends in D'z Nutz. Last week Jeremy Hunter attacked SNA, even without a contract. THIS WEEK, the two men will FIGHT, with the winner, and ONLY the winner, to receive a contract into CEW!
