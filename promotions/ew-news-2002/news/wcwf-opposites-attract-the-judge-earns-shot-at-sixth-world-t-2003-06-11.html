WCWF Opposites Attract: The Judge earns shot at sixth World Title

11 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Judge won a brutal three-way Last Man Standing match at WCWF’s Opposites Attract pay-per-view to earn a shot at his sixth WCWF World Title. The night kicked off with the start of an unusual Hardcore Title match. Challenger Invisus had negotiated a stipulation that the hardcore title would return to 24/7 rules for the pay-per-view. Brad Jackson’s Hardcore title would be on the line, as well as the number one contendership of Steve Murdock. The match would not end until the champion had either pinned or been pinned by the number one contender — whoever that ended up being. It began with Jackson chasing Murdock down the ramp to the ring, but Murdock was able to escape Jackson’s assault and head backstage. This allowed the first of the Lethal Lottery tag team matches to take place. The Judge and Thump were teamed at random against Dane Rennier and “The King” James Michaels, who were never on the same wavelength during the match. Rennier refused to tag Michaels in until the match’s end, and Michaels, in response, jumped down from the apron to the floor, effectively walking out on his partner. One Hung Jury and End Of The Road powerbomb later, Judge and Thump had advanced. Moments later, Invisus and Murdock fell from an upper-level guardrail into the ring. Murdock took the brunt of the fall, and Invisus was able to pin him and become the new #1 contender for the Hardcore title. He left to look for Jackson, then Jackson came to the ring and also pinned Murdock, not knowing that Invisus had beaten him to the punch. After Jackson returned to the back, the second Lethal Lottery match began. This one featured recent rivals Stan Stone, one-half of the WCWF Tag Team Champions, and United States Champion Matt Walker, who had lost the tag team belts to Stone and Rennier on the previous show. They were facing WCWF legend and backbone of the Fates Nathanial Duke, and Stone’s fellow Fate Shawn Stevens. Duke (in his first in-ring action in over a year) and Stevens worked Walker over before a mistake by Stevens allowed Stone to enter the match. Stone hit the Point Of Impact on Stevens, and Walker came in to get the pinfall and win the match for his team. After the match, Stone attacked Walker with a steel chair, then looked to shatter his knee with an aerial attack, but Thump rolled the U.S. Champion out of the way and took the shot himself. Stevens then apparently turned on the Fates, attacking Stone, Rennier, and Jester with the same chair before walking out of the arena. Jackson and Invisus, who had been brawling backstage the whole time, turned up on a walkway over the arena. Invisus rammed Jackson’s head into a support, a move that proved to be his undoing as both men fell from the walkway into the ring. Jackson ended up on top, got the three count, and kept his title. With Thump on his way to the hospital, what was to be a four-man Last Man Standing match became a three-man affair. Walker attacked Stone on the ramp during his entry, looking for payback for his attack on Thump. They fought on the ramp until Stone took the chair to Walker’s knee in the same manner, then went after Judge in the ring. As they traded submissions, Walker got to his feet. Stone was forced out of the ring, and Walker surprised him with a Tornado DDT off the ring steps onto the concrete floor. Revealing a steel brace wrapped around the knee that Stone attacked, Walker put Stone down for a seven count before he stood and pulled Walker out of the ring. Walker avoided another attempt to injure his knee and gave Stone a Diamond Cutter on a chair on the floor, then rolled him in to Judge, and they used a Hung Jury/Jersey Driver combo to finish off the Hardcore Legend. Judge then planted a tired Walker with the Hung Jury, and Walker couldn’t get to his feet in time to beat the 10-count, giving Judge a WCWF World Title shot at the next PPV. And he will face Jester, the man who blames him for the destruction of the LKO, for the World title at the next PPV, tentatively titled Asian Assault. Jester defeated WCWF Hall of Famer Dan Bonez to successfully defend his championship for the second straight month. Opposites Attract Results: Lethal Lottery Tag Match The Judge & Thump d.
