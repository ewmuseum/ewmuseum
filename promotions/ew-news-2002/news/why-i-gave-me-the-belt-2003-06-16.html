Why I gave me the belt

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm responding to the fans of SbW as to why I, J. Leslie Voss, the owner of SbW, awarded myself the title at Medic Alert two and a half months ago. Not a day goes by where I don't get fan mail. It surprises me that some of this is hand written, because basically the only people who really get to experience SbW are people from Baton Rouge, and their education system isn't A grade, lemme assure you of that. Majority of the mail is referring to our first event, when I awarded myself the Spinebuster Wrestling World title. They all want to know why I thought as myself as championship material. Again, the Baton Rouge thing comes to mind, they're all a couple of fingers short of a glove. So I'm gonna explain it to them. R E A L S L O W ! Well, let's take a glimpse at the roster. Not for then, how about for now. We'll take a look at the person I first defended the title against, Alex Wood. The Lumberjack. The man so proudly homosexual he constantly asks opponent, and I quote, "Got Wood?" I was a little unsure at first about getting into the ring with him, let alone giving him a shot. I wouldn't have even given him a shot at the World title had he not threatened to pummel my head with a 4x4. Yes folks, not even your standard wrestling 2x4, this man has the audacity to go another inch thicker. I was a little unsure about wrestling because he might have checked if indeed I had "Wood". That makes me a little uneasy. It'd be like dropping the soap in the aWc showers while Jamal Wilson washes your back. But the reason why I didn't award the title to him was for the mere fact that at the age of 27, he's already over the hill. Two shakey knees and practicing for most of his career with the illustrious August Monday, he's plain to brittle to go carrying a title like this. If he let it lay over his shoulder it'd probably crumble beneath it's weight and get dented on the ground. The verdict on Alex Wood? Not championship material. Some people have been mentioning cHEESE to me. I told them to see a doctor and they'd clear up the VD for you right away. But then they reitterated their point and suggestively remarked they were talking about one half of the ONLY tag team dynasty in wrestling's history. I scoffed and informed them that that was the reason right there. He's a tag specialist and names himself after a seminal discharge. Hello, cHEESE! There's no way I'm going to put the World title on someone who names themselves after the four day old trapping of some thirteen year-old's foreskin. The verdict on cHEESE? Not championship material. Now, a little less than a month ago I defended my title against Bradley Duncan. The star of many feds, including Action! where he performs currently. But here's another tag specialist trying to make it in the singular world. Unfortunately for Bradley, he has no mike skills and little to no charisma. Have you heard this guy talk on a mike? Neither have I. He's a friggin' mute! I've never heard of anything quite so pathetic and/or stupid as a mute holding a world title. He can't hear you talking about him. He can't accept a challenge. He can't even have a quirky catch phrase that'll make me plenty of money when I decide to do some copyrighting and merchandising. He's an absolute dud. And he hangs onto the coattails of someone obviously five times more talented than he in Action! And I'm talking about Max Danger. The verdict on Duncan? D E F I N I T E L Y not championship material. So it really only leaves me. Because the rest of the roster have a bright future in curtain jerking. Take a look at what I did for thReat in the mere month they had me aboard. Their PPV buy-rate went through the roof. Well, it wasn't great, but I certainly got them that extra 20,000 viewers, didn't I? I'm the Main Ratings Draw folks. That's the reason why there's a title around my waist and probably not around yours. And more to the point, in only 5 days time, at Crimson Fist of Glory, I will prove my point yet again. Nobody is bigger or better than the Big, Bad, VossMan! Yours in entertaining, J. Leslie Voss Spinebuster Wrestling
