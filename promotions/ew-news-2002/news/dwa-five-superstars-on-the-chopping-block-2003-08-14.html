DWA: Five superstars on the chopping block?

14 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Earlier this week, DWA owner, Jordan Brannigan, hired his 'girlfriend' former DWA announcer, Alexandria Simmons, as a ratings consultant, giving her the power to fire and hire superstars at will. She started with a fury, letting five superstars know exactly where they stood in the DWA. Two superstars, long time members of the DWA, Ninja X and Shockwave, have been put on notice, saying that if the two aging superstars do not impress her by the next pay per view, which is only two weeks away, then their contracts will be terminated. Three other superstars were not so lucky. Monster female wrestler, Starr List, had her contract terminated after a small dispute over her direction. Some are saying that she is being made an example out of, for people not to question their own direction. Also on the chopping block were Hellraiser, who just made his return last month at Dead Men Walking, and Cemetery Gates, who has only been in the DWA for a few months. Both were terminated due to not meeting contractual obligations. There seems to be no worry from the DWA front office about the loss of these three superstars, even though the DWA has a poor track record of keeping new stars, as many of their seasoned superstars have been with them for close to a year or longer. "It's a competitive business. The DWA is competitive.Some people can't hack it here and go some place easier," Jordan Brannigan said. "The DWA is one of the best feds on television, plain and simple. Some people can't handle that kind of pressure, and they eventually crack under the pressure." The DWA has had higher profile leavings that they were worried about. Just in the past few months, they have lost World and Velocity Champion, LeStatt Knight, in what was a last minute decision to take over his family business after his uncle's untimely death. The DWA also loss brother and sister Evo and Zula Shandor in seperate months, due to them losing passion for wrestling and wanting to pursue other options. Besides Knight, the biggest shock of losing a superstar came late last month, when Grovel was arrested and charged with the vicious assault of a young woman. He was held without bail and his trial is scheduled to start in September. He faces first degree assault and battery charges, as well as, stalking chargest. If convicted, he can face up to ten years in prison. "It was a shock to everyone," former Commissioner and current Announcer, Ronnie Johnson said. "Grovel was always a little nuts, but nobody expected something like this. But even though it wasn't expected, nobody is really surprised either by it. I just hope he pays for what he did." However, the DWA did sign someone who is expected to make big waves in the future. His name is Descent, and he appears to be a great athlete. "I see him doing a lot of stuff here in the DWA," interviewer, Destiny Mandrake, said when asked about Descent. "He has a lot of talent, a bit of an ego, but so do half the people in the lockerroom. If he doesn't go the way of some of the other rookies, the future could be very bright for him." DWA: Dragon Wrestling Association
