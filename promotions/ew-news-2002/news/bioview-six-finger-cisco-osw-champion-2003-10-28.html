Bioview: Six Finger Cisco OSW Champion

28 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OSW World Heavyweight Champion, Six Finger Cisco, the youngest wrestler to win the heavyweight title at 23yrs, 9days -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Division: Wrestling Status: Active Real Name: Cisco Milloy Birthdate: September 12th, 1980 (He's 23 for those that can't add) Hometown: Austin, Texas Height: 5'11 Weight: 230 lbs. Nicknames: The Main Attraction, Six Finger, Six Finger Cisco Allignment: Face Entrance Music: 'Wherever I May Roam' by Metallica -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Information Combat Style: Technical with a mix of hardcore and speed (Think Benoit/Jericho with a dash of Foley) Set Up Move: Sixth Finger Eye Poke (Cisco pokes the eye of his opponent using his 'Thindex' finger ) Finisher: Six Finger Discount (Submission move where Cisco grabs his opponent's Adam's apple with all six fingers and chokes them out ) Title History: OSW World Award History: Youngest Wrestler to Win Heavyweight Title at 23yrs, 9days Other Federations: Primetime Wrestling Academy (PWA), Eternal Wrestling (EW), Primetime Championship Wrestling (PCW), Five Star Wrestling (FSW) Other Federation Awards: PWA Graduate, EW Cruiserweight Champion -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Quotes "Chicks dig the sixth finger." "20% more grip....yes it makes a difference." "Don't fuck with the freak because he'll freaking fuck you up!" History: The Beginning Born in Austin, TX to Joe and Jenn Milloy(who happen to be cousins), Cisco Milloy was not your average child. Cisco, from the day he came into this world up until the day that he received his diploma from Austin High was constantly ridiculed for his extra finger. Buying gloves, shaking hands, or even playing a friendly game of thumb wrestling, you name it and the six fingered wonder had a problem with it. The kids did not let things like this slip by. The freak, now graduated and with nothing really planned in his future, decided to attend the Republican National convention and show his support for George W. Bush in his presidential campaign. Bush gave his speech and began going around and shaking hands with the crowd. The deformed one got his chance and it was ugly. I guess Bush didn’t like being grabbed by a Six Finger handshake because he pulled away and seemed disgusted by the whole ordeal. The crowd started laughing at Cisco and 20 years of rage built up was released in the form of a Six Finger Discount on the future president. When Bush came to he banned Cisco from the state of Texas and all Texas Ranger baseball games. The main attraction fled the state from that scene and eventually got a job as a freak in some traveling circus that had stopped in New Mexico. The freak traveled around the country, experiencing many different places and unique individuals. During an altercation with a freak named Grendel, a she/male dragqueen, the six fingered one ended up choking out his co-worker. He was fired from that circus. For the next half a year or so Six Finger Cisco joined different freakshows and circuses and even tried starting his own circus. The money was bad. The road life was harsh. And the freak made many enemies and very few friends along the way. Cisco knew that he had a gift for choking people out so he figured wrestling could be a way to make it big. He soon joined the Primetime Wrestling Academey where he quickly learned the ins and outs of wrestling and graduated to Eternal Wrestling. When that company went bust Cisco was promoted to Primetime Championship Wrestling. Here the freak could finally prove that he could be someone. After winning his first few matches the six fingered one went on a terrible losing streak and quit the PCW. After nearly a year of inactivity Cisco returned to the ring by means of Five Star Wrestling. . Unfortunately, that company changed ownership and all employees were released from the roster. It didn’t take long for SFC to sign with another federation. Nearly after a month from his FSW debut, Cisco made his Old School Wrestling debut. Now the deformed one is in the spotlight. He is in the top federation. Can he prove that he can accomplish something in life? Time will tell….. The Asylum Our six fingered hero had been battling it out with some of the top competitors in Old School Entertainment. With only one win to show on his record the freak became enraged every time he lost. He could not cope with a loss. It was the end of the world if he lost a match. Soon the losses got ugly. Cisco would have the win and someone would interfere in the match and cost him the victory. The President of the OSE, Trashcan Man, sought out Cisco to give him advice and soon became fond of the young talent. Dave Gibson, Mike Marvelous, Ming, and Exceptional Dave Hurst formed the stable Power Play and made it theirduty to attack Cisco during matches and beat him down in 4 on 1 attacks. During a match, the members of Power Play came out to beat Cisco down once again. To the surprise of many, Azala Zameer and Timo Bolamba came down to the ring followed shortly by the President Trashcan Man himself. The foursome handled Power Play and at this moment officially announced the formation of another version of the historic stable, Asylum. The Birth of a Champion September 21st, 2003. THE biggest night in Six Finger Cisco's career. Receiving a world title match basically on the same night, the Six Fingered One took the bull by the horns and rose to the occasion, beating then OSW champion Boda to win the strap. He finally proved to himself and the world that he's more than just a freak with a sixth finger. Now, he wrestles to become a legend. He wrestles to become the best that ever lived. The Birth of a Rivalry Leading up to Road to Ruins, Scott Taylor came back as a part of Tempest's Devil's Own stable. The group attacked Cisco week in and week out, setting up a world title match pitting Cisco vs. Scott Taylor. During the match, Cisco fiance Natasha came down and turned on Cisco, nearly costing him the match and revealing that she was in cahoots with Tempest. Scott Taylor then turned on Tempest, claiming he didn't need her to win the title. With all of the distractions, Cisco was pinned and lost his title on his first defense setting up what will certainly be only the first chapter in Taylor and Cisco's rivalry. OSE: Wrestling
