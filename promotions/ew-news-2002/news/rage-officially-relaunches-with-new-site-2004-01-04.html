RaGE Officially Relaunches With New Site!

4 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After six months on the shelf, Real and Graphic Entertainment makes good on its promise to relaunch. Complete with a renovated site, new features, and 400 + venues in a directory, all to shock the entertainment world. After the long wait, Real and Graphic Entertainment is finally back in full swing. The past six months have been filled with tiresome talks, boring traveling, and nothing but hype. With all that coming to a close, it is now time for the company to put forth the effort which it has talked about for the past few months. All the press conferences and promoting to the beginning of a new year stop here, as Real and Graphic Entertainment calls the bluff and starts the 2004 season. As many are skeptical as to what the 2004 season will hold, so are many of the new wrestlers to have signed with the company for this year. Never before have people talked about hype as in this manner. But those who know the business know that hype is nothing until someone steps up to land a company or a character on the map. The executive board and the wrestlers are making their attempt to land Real and Graphic Entertainment on the map of sports entertainment on January 9, 2004. The start of the new year brings across the first event, New Dawn. Like the name says, it is the start of the company's new year and new direction. With only fifteen televised Pay Per View shows throughout the year, many question Real and Graphic Entertainment's ability to hold the interest of fans and wrestling critics. The Owner of the company shakes his head at nay-sayers and says "The company will surpass the expectations of its predecessors." There is only one way to tell if Real and Graphic Entertainment is ready to finally step up to the plate and be taken seriously by the rest of the sports entertainment world. On January 9, 2004, the company has to come ready to play with New Dawn and leave the past behind them. If a solid three-hour event can be delivered on that night, many will tell you that the direction of the future is based on the response to New Dawn. Real and Graphic Entertainment will make their own future this Friday night and certainly, a great deal of the entertainment world will be watching. For a look into the features of the new renovated Real and Graphic Entertainment site, including the 400 + Venues Directory, visit SE-Live.com! Real and Graphic Entertainment
