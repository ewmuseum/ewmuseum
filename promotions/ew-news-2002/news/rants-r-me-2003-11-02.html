I'm tired of constantly being overlooked for someone who's not doing [explicative]. I'm tired of busting my ass and getting nothing for it. Not even a "Hey, nice job. Great stuff out there." So you know what? [explicative] you. [explicative] you with a wooden spoon. I'm tired of being your saving grace. I'm tired of being the load bearer for a division that's full of [explicative] smoking, whiny mother[explicative]. Who was a favorite to win Survivor? Not them. Does anyone remember that? No. Does anyone remember how the G-O-Dub and I CONSTANTLY put a Tag Division back on the map? No. Does anyone care? I'd think not. They only care about everyone else, "accidentally" casting a blind eye our way. So on that note: Joey and Frankie, go [explicative] yourselves. Elliot and Thomas, go [explicative] yourselves and show them how you do it right. Chris and Jim, you can go [explicative] yourselves while you cry about it. Tyler and Conor, go [explicative] yourselves two times. Shabazz and RAM, [explicative] you for being so worthless. You're a disgrace to anyone that's every labeled themselves "Tag Team". Not now nor did you EVER deserve to be the Survivor champions. I hope you enjoy having your thumbs shoved up your [explicative], I'm making sure everyone forgets who the [explicative] you are. You feel me, "dawg"? Get over yourselves, you were the runner-ups or you "won" Survivor. Awesome, great, you're all doing nothing while you ride out your fifteen minutes and wait for the next batch of worthless nothings to get shipped in from OCW. Good for you. Really. World Peace Organization need to get off my property. Or "yard" as it so sadly called in Connecticut. Ric and Pierre might be legends of this company, but that's for what they did on their lonesome. Hogging MY Gold isn't helping anyones cause; least of all mine. I'm in the show for a reason, to bring in a different brand of fans. The ones that understand and respect tag team wrestling. What it takes, how it's done and what it takes to be on top. I'm no Busch-league rookie. I'm not someone that was plucked off the street and told to dance in the ring for ten minutes before laying down for the j-o-b. I cruise those Internet communities while I'm on the road. I read what you write, how you "overlook" what I do to blabber about how awesome Brand Frontier is. That's fine, it's nothing new for me. I've built a whole career around it, living off the spite and the anger. Trying to understand how someone like The Tallywhackers can get over with blinding stupidity and when I do something of a HIGHER, MORE ENTERTAINING quality, it's only garners a "meh". I've given up. Eron's helped me understand that I have to go out there for me; not you. I have to do what I want, not what you want me to do. I suddenly know how the blind man felt when Jesus gave him sight. I can see what I was going, how much it was driving me down. But it's ok, I'm better now. And you're better for knowing this. This so-called "Tag Division" is one of the worst I've ever encountered. The majority of the talent is lazy and uninspired, most of them are lost sheep looking for guidance. However instead of coming to oh, say, ME about what they should do; they ask someone like Silver Fox. "Hey Fox, what should I do? I feel like the fans aren't connecting with my character. It's bringing me down." And Fox would tell them "Take a step back, get your head clear and work this out. You have that ability in you." What the [explicative]?! That's advice?! Here's the best advice I can give. Get the belts off WPO. Get them off them now. Save us all from this horror and right so many wrongs. The lose to Phoenix disgusts me, there's no logic to the move and it only cheapens the value of the title. Now when I win the belt, it'll be like "Pfft, that Title's been around more times that a French [explicative]. Ain't worth jack." And what's that say about the people who are going out and giving 400% each night just to wear the Title? Is it going to be worth it? No, it's not. It's not fair to have these people (the LoD) pouring themselves into this division to get short changed. I don't expect this to stay up for long, Disney will probably flip when they read it. Something about "negative attitudes toward the product" and you're [explicative]damn right it is. This is the wake-up call. I am your host for the revolution. The time is now, change is immanent. I am the future, I am the present, I am the best. I am the voice of progress. Bow before my might. -NOG Dairytown.net
