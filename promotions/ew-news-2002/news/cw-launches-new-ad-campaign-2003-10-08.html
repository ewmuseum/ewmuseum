cW Launches New Ad Campaign

8 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In attempts to generate intrest and ratings, cW introduces a new ad slogan and campaign. EAST LONDON – October 7, 2003 – Straight from the office of Eliza FitzPatrick in the new fabulously redesigned Boleyn Public House, Ms. FitzPatrick announced a new ad campaign to drive Core Wrestling into the 2004 year. Advertising and publicity has been at an unusual low since Eugene FitzPatrick took office in Core Wrestling. The ratings for Smash have dropped and many complaints are even coming from backstage. “I hate Smash on Sundays. Fitz did it just despite of us. He knew it was our day off. I miss Lanier running this show,” cW Superstar, Sidarian commented, “I even had to bring on this new attitude to get mine in cW.” Eliza FitzPatrick held a live press and web conference to launch the new ad campaign to help re-stimulate the interests of the fans and the wrestlers in Core Wrestling. Promotion of the new ads and possibly new sponsorship deal are currently in the works. Eliza has been on the phone for the past week before Smash planning and getting together her new campaign. “I always have to pick up after Daddy,” Eliza said. No comments from the office or administration of Eugene FitzPatrick about this new campaign being implemented starting last night. But one can bet he was not happy about the comments and changes his daughter has been making. “It’s Core Wrestling,” Eliza concluded with, “You got to Feel It.” coreWrestling
