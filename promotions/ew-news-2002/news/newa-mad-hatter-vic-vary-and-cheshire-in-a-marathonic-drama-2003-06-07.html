[NeWA] Mad Hatter, Vic Vary and Cheshire In a Marathonic Drama on "No Survivors"

7 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OWC's Pay-Per-View "No Survivors" ended with a bang as the Triple-Threat match for the Great Lakes Championship ended in a dramatic manner. By the end of the night, OWC crowned a brand new Great Lakes Champion. Cheshire, Mad Hatter and Vary... What a drama! Almost all of OWC's fans who attended Columbos last night, left with a rightly-earned smile on their faces. Ohio Wrestling Coalition's fans gathered to witness the first Pay-Per-View held by the OWC in almost three months. The result? An epic drama in the Main-Event. OWC fans were in lost of superlatives to describe the amazing three-way title match for the Great Lakes Championship. The Main-Event hosted current NeWA World Heavyweight Champion, Vic Vary. Vic Vary also serves as OWC Great Lakes Champion - he needed to defend his title against two OWC greats: Former NeWA World Heavyweight Champion, Cheshire, and OWC mainstay Mad Hatter. The marathonic drama ended in a spectecular victory for Cheshire. Both competitors were thanked by the crowd with cheers for their enormous effort. "No Survivors" also featured the match between "The Heartbeat of Canada" Ken Jansen and The Curator. Curator coming off a big match with Vic Vary at "Dying Degree" tried to upseat the Former NeWA World Heavyweight Champion. Following the match, Ken Jansen thanked the fans with an emotional speech. Many claim that the OWC Pay-Per-View set the standard for the rest of the Regional Pay-Per-Views this year. ESPN were satisfied with the event and claimed to be impressed with the Ohio region. We can only wish for more Regional PPV's like the one OWC showbouted last night. Until next time... OWC's
