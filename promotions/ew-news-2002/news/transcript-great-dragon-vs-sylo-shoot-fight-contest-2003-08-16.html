Transcript: Great Dragon vs. Sylo - shoot fight contest

16 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The match everyone wanted to see. The two men stood in the center of the octagon-shaped cage, reminiscent of the Ultimate Fighting Championships' cage. Both men wore shoot fighting gloves, as well as not wearing any shoes due to the nature of the contest. The referee gave them final orders regarding the contest. The rules included: There was only one fifteen minute round, No Low Blows, No Fishhooks, Knockdowns, which meant hits due to the opponent knocking the victim down, or the victim seemingly losing consciousness, rendered a ten count on the downed opponent unless the referee noticed he was out of it completely, Submissions and stoppage due to severe bleeding were allowed and finally, there was a three knockout rule in place. The referee explained the rules to the two gentlemen, as Great Dragon nodded, his mask still tightly in place. Sylo sneered at his opponent for a moment, before the referee motioned for them to shake hands. Dragon extended his arm out, and Sylo stared at it for a moment, before slapping it. Dragon stared at Sylo, a sneer of his own appearing on his face. This sign of disrespect would not go unanswered. The referee motioned for the bell, and the two opponents stared at one another. Almost immediately, however, Dragon shot out his leg, and low kicked it into the back of Sylos' knee. Sylo, caught off guard by the kick, quickly responded with a strong right hook towards Dragon. Dragon, however, noticed it, and moved his head out of the way. Sylos' hand, however, managed to still graze the side of Dragons' head, causing Dragon to stumble back a few inches. Dragon shook his head, and cleared the cobwebs. Just as he did, Sylo swing another hard right hook, attempting to take Dragon out of the contest quickly. However, Dragon, realizing he couldn't dodge out of the way quickly enough, used his pro wrestling and shoot training experience in taking falls to the ground, and fell backwards, ducking the hard right by Sylo. Sylo, unable to halt his own momentum, turned himself around by missing Dragon, exposing the back of his leg. Dragon quickly took advantage of the opening, and proceeded to kick his foot into the back of Sylos' knee, hoping to cause enough damage to Sylos' leg to force him to collapse to the ground. Sylo, however, turned around, and moved out of the way, using his gut instincts from back in his bouncer days to avoid anymore kicks from Dragon on the ground. Dragon slowly got up, and Sylo decided to use his opening to his own advantage. Sylo used all of his available momentum to swing his arm upwards with a powerful uppercut. Dragon attempted to dodge out of the way, but it was too late. The uppercut connected with Dragons' chin, and sent him reeling into the cage walls. Sylo smiled as Dragon collapsed on the ground, and began making taunting motions, trying to prove to the audience who watched this contest take place that he was now dominant in this contest. At the six count, however, Dragon got up to his feet, and shook his head, putting himself back into a fighting stance. Sylo smiled, and motioned for Dragon, and Dragon complied. Sylo began swinging his left and right hooks, but Dragon, seeing them coming, smartly dodged out of the way of the attacks. Dragon quickly spun himself around, and as Sylo went towards him, Dragon connected with a stiff roundhouse kick to Sylos' shoulder. Sylo stumbled around, grasping his arm, and rubbing it to get some of the pain away. However, Dragon was using his opportune moment to take advantage on the legs that he was trying to take Sylo out with once more. Dragon began kicking Sylo in his weakened back knee area once again, trying to suck the energy keeping Sylo up dry. Sylo, immediately sensing danger, spun around quickly, throwing a powerful left hook towards Dragon. Dragon, meanwhile continued kicking at Sylos' knee area, and as Sylo turned around, connected with his kneecap. Dragon gritted his teeth, as his may have damaged or broken a toe with the kick, and Sylo used this own moment to his advantage, completing his left hook, and knocking Dragon back into the cage wall, taking him down. As the referee began counting Dragon for a second time, Sylo limped slightly towards his downed foe, and smiled. Sylo sat down on top of his opponent, ramming his weak knee into Dragons' chest area, and prepared to begin a mounted strike on Dragon. Dragon cried out in pain, which increased Sylos' confidence. However, Dragon was playing possum all the while, and Dragon hooked Sylos' weak leg with his hands. Sylo stared in shock as a smile pursed Dragons' lips. Sylo attempted to get up, which was Dragon wanted to do. Dragon hooked the leg with his own legs, and in almost an instant, twisted the leg, which took the big man down to the mat. Dragon quickly applied on a heel hook, and Sylo was crying out in pain now. Dragon smiled as he continued to apply pressure to the hold, and it seemed as though Sylo would have no choice but to submit to the skilled opponent. However, Sylo extended his arms, and grabbed onto the cage wall. Sylo used all of his strength to move himself up, getting up slowly onto his feet. Dragon noticed this, and released the hook, rolling himself backwards, and onto his feet. The announcer mentioned that Dragon had only one knockdown left, and Dragon stared at Sylo. Sylo grabbed his weakened leg, and sneered at Dragon. Sylo limped around the ring, and Dragon smiled, knowing if he could lay in another good shot at the leg, Sylo would be done for. Dragon circled around Sylo, as Sylo motioned for him to come closer, then began throwing punches at Dragon, hoping to take Dragon down for a final time. However, Dragon quickly swung out his leg, executing a standard high kick, and connected with the shoulder that he hit early, causing Sylo to pause for a moment. Immediately, Dragon swung out his leg, going for a low kick to the weakened knee. Sylo, enraged that Dragon managed to hit him in the shoulder again, attempted another powerful uppercut to his opponent. As the two swung their powerful hits, Dragon realized he had to take another dive, and pushed himself back down to the ground, as Sylo swung his uppercut at him. Dragon managed to miss this final, deadly uppercut, and connected with his low kick to Sylos' knee. Sylo grasped his knee, and promptly collapsed to the ground, as the referee began a knockdown count on him. Dragon, however, did not want to give Sylo the chance to get up, and promptly grabbed at Sylos' weakened arm, attempting to lock on a Cross Arm Breaker. Sylo, however, realized Dragon was going for the devistating arm submission, and locked his arms in a ring shape, hooking his hands together. Dragon, at the same time, thought one step ahead, and quickly grabbed at Sylos' leg, locking on a hiza jujigatame submission hold. Sylo cried out in pain, and attempted to roll through, but that made things only worse. Dragon turned the hiza jujigatame into a reverse hiza jujigatame, preventing Sylo from moving anywhere. The referee checked on Sylo, and Sylo attempted to resist the painful submission lock for nearly a minute. However, Dragon had put him in a situation that he could not defend himself, as he was now on his stomach, facing back to him, and he had no choice. He tapped. The bell rang, as Dragon got off Sylos' leg, and the ring announcer announced him as the winner of the brutal contest. Dragon wiped off some blood that was seeping down his chin due to one of Sylos' powerful punches, and then looked down at Sylo. The referee lifted up Dragons' hand in victory, and Dragon quickly extended his arm down to Sylo. Sylo took it. Dragon helped Sylo up, and though he was limping heavily, Sylo managed to stay on his feet as Dragon lifted his arm in the air. Tonight, both men proved their worth in terms of fighting to one another.
