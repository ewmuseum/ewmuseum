HSW: Board Meeting- Recruitment, Division Discussions, TV Buyrates, more

30 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Headstrong Wrestling's Chairman, Joey Matthew, held a conference Monday afternoon with the Board of Directors to speak on the current state of Headstrong Wrestling. Many topics were touched on including the different divisions, recruitment that needs to be done, Shocker And PPV buyrates and what is in store for 2004, plus more. His comments were as follows: Christmas Chaos turnout- He said that he was happy with the show, but there were mistakes by a few that took the show down. Ratings were definately good for this show and were the best to date in HSW. Overall, Joey was happy with the big Pay Per View. Junior Heavyweight Division- Mr.Matthew said that The Man With No Name is doing a good job as champion. Joey says he wants this division to get back into the spotlight more as he feels things have been stale as of late in it. Joey noted the signing of one knew Jr.heavyweight in Paul Methric and mentioned he would be a good addition. Mention of El Nutso and Tai Hashi being good competition right now, if not th only.He said he'd do everything in his power to recruit new talent for this division as it is a necessity. FnX Division- Joey commented on Mjolnir's blatant blasts on Headstrong Wrestling, but mostly Joey, and said that Mjolnir had negative feelings for the wrong reasons. "If he was unhappy where his character was going, he should had said something much sooner before he did." Joey noted that HSW will be better without Mjolnir. He also said that the FnX division is going to be pushed high in the coming weeks leading up to the next Pay Per View. He said that new talent has been signed specifically for the division and other current talent are making the transition to it. Expect big matches in the FnX division at and leading up to the next PPV. Tag Team Division- Joey is unhappy with this division. He said that Vandal Hearts had been doing no good since their debut, though he thought he had something big with their signing. Joey said that he has spoken to them about leaving and they have agreed to pack their bags soon. Joey noted that the Tag Team Division definately needs some new faces, so hopefully current HSW stars will attempt to team up and go for the gold, or new talent will be recruited. Joey says the Tag Team Titles will fit the Dark Alliance, Bloodhound and The Boogeyman, very nice and they should help that division as it attempts to grow. He said the X-Rated Ikons could do better, but they have been doing pretty good since their debut and could be in line for another title shot soon. Joey will be on the look out for Free Agent Tag Teams. Evolution Division- He said The Man With No Name is a perfect champion for this division right now. He noted he does a great job holding both titles because he deserves them both for the hard work he gives each week. Mention of new competition in this division, a possible feud with Sabre and MWNN, or other talent in HSW may be ready to challenge for this belt. Headstrong Championship Division- Joey said Assassin has done well as the Champion and put on a great show at Christmas Chaos. He says the fans are really into him each time he wrestles but he could put forth more of an effort backstage at times. He said that Tyler McPherson is definately being considered to challenge for the title soon, and other names were brought up for the very same thing, one of those being Heather Halliwell. Joey feels that Heather could be a good champion as could Tyler. He feels Assassin represents the company well. On Shocker in the past 4 months of broadcast- He said that there has definately been an improvement in the shows produced since the debut on September 6th. November sweeps was especially a good time for Shocker. The WB is sticking with HSW and will follow up after the 9th show in the contract and decide whether or not they will keep HSW on that network. Joey said that in 2004, Shocker will be one of the most watched shows on Television if he has any say in that. On Promotional Appearances- Joey says he has been making many promotional stops through the past four months trying to get the word out on Headstrong Wrestling. He said that starting in February HSW Talent will be booked for talk shows and other shows to promote HSW, but mostly the weeks bef Headstrong Wrestling
