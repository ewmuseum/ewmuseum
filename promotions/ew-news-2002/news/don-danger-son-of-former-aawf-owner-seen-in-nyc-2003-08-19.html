Don Danger, Son of Former AAWF Owner Seen in NYC

19 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The son of former AAWF Owner Jordan Danger, was seen in NYC entering a apartment building of some sort... AAWF News Story #2 Danger Family Sightings in New York City. Welcome back. My name is Jack Metro(same reporter as the first headline) and this is the second headline that is being written on the AAWF. And in this article you will know that the information are not rumors, they are facts. Take my word for it. 2 days ago, I was walking down 46th street on my way to a local store(I live in NYC) And I couldn't help but notice this man, dressed in a business suit, with jet black hair slicked back..Those were my first thoughts on the man in front of me..as seeing him was unavoidable with his gleaming hair, and height over the other pedestrians. Then the next thing that hit me was I think that's Don Danger(Remember who Don Danger is...The son of Jordan Danger, apart of the Danger Family, which were the people who owned Across America Wrestling Federation) I thought it because Don Danger was known for his jet black hair, and wearing it exactly like it was. So I thought to myself on what I should do.. Should I go up to him? I quickly decided to walk quick past him, and peer over my shoulder to actually prove it to myself that, that was him.. So I began to walk, and as I did, Don Danger looked over his shoulder like something tapped him on his shoulder. With that split second look, I can guarantee it was indeed Don. I saw almost his whole face. And after he looked over his shoulder he turned to his right and entered a building which looked like a apartment building and that also gave me a look at the other side of his face, which also looked exactly like the Don I remember...Just more mature looking, or how some would say, a colder looking face. So then I thought, why would Don Danger be in New York City? I could not pinpoint anything, other then he possibly may be meeting someone in the building that is linked to the AAWF, or, he indeed is linked to the return of AAWF and he has office space in that building.. Or something like that. Those were the only thoughts I could think of on why Don Danger would be in NYC, and linking him to the AAWF, which was a rumor in my last headline, and still today. So there you have it...Facts that Don Danger was is in NYC.. And oh yeah, the new AAWF Headquarters is in NYC! I saved this bit of information for last just to get your heart pumping... Hope you enjoyed the second headline. Thanks and See you when I have more information. -Jack Metro AAWF Preview Site
