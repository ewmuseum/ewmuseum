DWA: Showdown Short Results

17 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After receiving some of the strongest ratings that the DWA has ever seen last week on their new home, FX, Showdown returns, as the days are becoming less and less as Dead Men Walking looms just over two weeks away. In it, Ashura takes on Jimmy Changa, Donnie Darko takes on John Reed and Brad Keidis, and in the main event, World Champion, Typhoid Mary, takes on Jeff Wylde, in a non-title match. The show began as World Champion Typhoid Mary and LeStatt Knight talk about the problem their having with The Revolution, and this sets up two different matches for Dead Men Walking. LeStatt Knight will be facing Jeff Wylde in a hardcore match, where as Typhoid Mary will put her World Title on the line against Payne. Ronnie Johnson, who last week was demoted from being Commissioner by new owner Jordan Brannigan, had his first day as commentator, and things didn't seem to start off too smoothly with broadcast partner, Jeremy Taylor. Ashura vs. "Jammin'" Jimmy Changa --These two superstars have been scarce from action lately in the DWA, but they came back with a vengeance. Ashura picked up the victory on the heaviest man in the DWA. Jordan Brannigan met with the DWA's resident hero, Donnie Darko, to discuss the problems that Donnie has with Brannigan's reign as owner. The two didn't exactly see eye to eye on opinions, but in the end, things may have turned out better for both parties. Darko, who was teaming with Evo Shandor against the FoW next, was informed about Evo's sudden release from the DWA, and told that he would be getting a new partner. Donnie Darko and ??? vs. FoW --John Reed and Brad Keidis came out for the FoW. Then Darko came out, wondering who his partner was, then Shockwave came out, making his second appearance after his return on Surge. Shockwave went straight after the FoW, but Reed got the better of him, but then Darko hits the ring, nails Reed with the Savior and picks up the immediate 1, 2, 3! The shortest match in DWA history. After the match, Keidis yelled at Reed for losing the match before they left. Typhoid Mary vs. Jeff Wylde Non-Title Match --This match was one of the highlights on Showdown for the year, as the two superstars went toe to toe with each other. Payne came out with Wylde and attempted to interfere in the match, but LeStatt Knight, who came out with Mary, chased him away. However, Payne did get involved, but Mary got her revenge by nailing Payne with a baseball slide sending him over the guardrail into the crowd. Payne then went after Mary, but Knight stopped him and the two began to brawl outside the ring. Mary then nailed Wylde with a huge implant double arm DDT and picked up the 1, 2, 3! However, after the match, Payne drove Mary through the announce table to end the night. DWA: Dragon Wrestling Association
