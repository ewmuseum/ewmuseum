OSE Shows Take Place

19 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Both Assault and Slam! have taken place only one day apart from each other due to circumstances beyond the control of OSE. But Slam! proved to be worth the wait, and Assault has proven anyone who said it couldn't work wrong. OSF Assault Detectives, and orgies, and alliances, oh my! This week on Assault, we were once again graced with the presence of Myabi. What evil did Inphino Blitz have in store, and what will come of this next week? Case open or closed? Kraig Carter is still on the trail, but will he find the answers he seeks, or is there someone that doesn't want him to know anything? Akage Kitagami faced off with John Crichton. But by the end of the match, one of them wasn't moving. Only one way to find out who it was, and why. Miguel Carranza III and Julius Corbin took on Six Finger Cisco and "Exceptional" Dave Hurst. Could Cisco and Hurst put aside their differences and come out on top? The Best of Seven continued between Jackson and The Clan. Who won this one? The Clan needs a win, and Jackson can't afford to lose. For the World Title, Lillith challenged Tempest, and Inphino made a special appearance. Would his presence cause Tempest to slip, or would the Cyberpunk Goddess show why she is the champ? Doll and Shawn Stewart went one-on-one in the main event. And you'll never guess what happened in the end. Kenyatta Ahmed Jackson and The Clan had their share of encounters, Boda bashed OSF, and many more exciting and shocking happenings occured during the course of the show. So what are you waiting for? Go check it out! OSW Slam! No catchy slogan, just the best wrestling show coming at ya once again. Ivan and the Red Army made an appearance yet again, but what could they possibly do to bring Trachcan Man to tears? Whatever it was, they did it. But what TCM did would shock the world. The Maximators took on Dougie & Freddy. Each team needed a win, and one came out on top in the end...with a little help. Is The Asylum still intact, or has the thoughts of defection brought an end to the group? Darryl Fountain faced off with Matt Register in a Disco Death Match. Both men are part of two of the top teams in OSW, and a win for Darryl would propel The Funkadelics higher up the ladder. Could he pull one off, or would Register show why he is one half of the Tag Team Champs? PI faced off against Cassius Deamont and Shocker in a triple threat match for the U.S. Title. Could two enemies hold on long enough to dethrone the champs, or would Politically Incorrect squeak out with another win? A match for the middle tier spot took place. Whose side will be getting the advantage at Ascension? Jeffrey Wayne took on Scott Taylor, Alan Kriegman, and Azala Zameer in a TLC match for the top tier spot. Who came out on top, and who will be going into the PPV on top? A non-title match saw Boda pitted against an unexpected foe. Who was it, and could Boda win with the inclusion of a special referee in the match? And in the main event, Timo took on Gibbo to see who is the number one contendor. Who walked out with a chance to face Boda? We saw Taylor make an appearance, DarkStar searching for Talena, and the return of a beloved OSE employee to action. And no, it wasn't in-ring.
