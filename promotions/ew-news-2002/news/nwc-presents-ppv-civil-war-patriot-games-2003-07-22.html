NWC Presents PPV - Civil War: Patriot Games

22 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The National Wrestling Council Executive Committee is proud to release their upcoming PPV Line-Up for Civil War: Patriot Games. On a night that the future of the National Wrestling Council will be changed in ways nobody could have ever imagined, the NWC brings to you one of it’s most historic matches ever, WARGAMES! Not only will regional pride be on the line, not only will bragging rights be on the line, but the winning team will give their region control of all NWC title shots at the next PPV! 16 men, 2 rings, 1 steel cage. Anything can happen, and it probably will. One NWC EC member had this to say, "If you thought Across Enemy Lines 5 was the pay per view to end all pay per views, then don't miss Civil War: Patriot Games!" Another had this to say, "With savvy veterans like Alexander, Tony Pride, Billy Page, Edward Lambert and future stars like Teron Hain, Sylis Walker and Lazarus all on one card, the NWC fans will certainly get their money's worth." The NWC for the first time ever opens their doors to talent outside the NWC in an effort to allow wrestling superstars from all over the world to showcase their talents in a one night only battle royal. This event features such names as Rent-A-Hero, Duke Williams, Kory Storm, Magnum Wolf and many others. The NWC talent advisor also mentioned that one of the participants, Donny Diamond, is in talks with NWC:Atlantic about a possible contract, but we were unable to get any details at press time. For a complete Civil War: Patriot Game Line-Up go here: http://www.nwconline.net/civilwarlineup.htm There you will find links to the promo boards for each and every match. DO NOT MISS THIS EVENT!!!
