SilverHAWK's ACW Glory Preview

5 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This Sunday, the next ACW extravaganza will take Indianapolis, Indiana by storm, here is the current card but be warned, there are going to be a few surprises popping up as some matches still have to be finalised by the ACW staff. All-Star Championship Wrestling SilverHAWK here...it's not often that I go on this World Wide Web but I thought I should give you guys over at eWNEWS the card to this Sunday's Glory Event and a small Preview. ---------------------------------------------- [No DQ Grudge Match] Hillary Small Vs. Jade After Jade's last success in a PPV match against ToK, whom she retired from ACW, she has been forced to live with Hillary Small constantly following her as it seems that Small is envious of Jade's success, who will come out on top? [Homicidal Tendencies Match] El Janitors Vs. .vindication Vs. The Kole Brothers Homicidal Tendencies Match? The first rule of the HTM is that ANYTHING GOES. The second rule of the HTM is that it will be fought under Tornado Tag Team rules. The third rule of the HTM is that it will be fought under Iron Man rules; team with the most number of decisions win. Who Will Survive? [Last Man Standing] Chad Stalin vs. Jason Reaves After both men originally debuted at Pain or Pleasure, their distaste for one another was present since that day, and now they look to end their rivalry with a Last Man Standing match. With Stalin injured last Sunday, it will be a miracle if he can even make it to the match, but will he be able to stand over Reaves as the referee makes the ten count? Only time will tell. [Scorpion Title Match] Vincent Pembridge(c) Vs. Gabriel So'ialu Gabriel So'ialu has made a quick impact in ACW as his debut a number of weeks ago has fueled one of the most compelling match up of the night. Pembridge is a man with no mercy, of which he has shown on a number of occasions including last Sunday's show were he put down both Quinton May and Gabriel So'ialu in the same night. Will Pembridge get what is coming to him? [ACW Battle Royal for the Vacant Television Title] Competitors Unknown Alias had done all that he could with the TV Title, and a few weeks prior to Glory, he gave it up to SilverHAWK. Now, HAWK has announced that a brand new Champion will be decided, and the title will go to someone whom has never had Gold before in ACW. So who will come out on top, in a match whom nobody knows the competitors? Will they be present ACW stars? Or will someone else come in and claim the Glory...at Glory? [Steel Cage Match For the ACW United States Championship] A*Dubbs Vs. Dante Inferno It was a war which had been boiling up for the last seven weeks, and now it seemed that the first time these two men would square up, could quite possibly be the very last, as they step inside a custom build ACW steel cage. Will Inferno finally do something worthwhile with his talents? or will the current Champion sneak a victory in a clash which is promised to be nothing more than gruesome. ["I Quit" Match] Jason Kain Vs. Osyrus The last meeting between these two men lasted a full night as they fought a best of three special match at Pain or Pleasure, but now they deem to finish this feud once and for all, as the loser will finally end the feud by saying those special words..."I Quit." [Pounded and Fused Match] Alias Vs. 'Superstar' Vince Jacobs As Vince Jacobs strolled into ACW only but a few months ago, nobody could have predicited the heated feud that he would have with long time fan favourite Alias. With the World Title now vacant, it is thought that one of these two men will finally end up with it, but before that is even decided, they will go through a war in a Pounded and Fused Match. With some fans not knowing what the match really consists, it is sure to be something special, as a wrestling legend takes on a wrestling legend in the making. ---------------------------------------------- Enjoy The Show. SilverHAWK All-Star Championship Wrestling Glory 2003
