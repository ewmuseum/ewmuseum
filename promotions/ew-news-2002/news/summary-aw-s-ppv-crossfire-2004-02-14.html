Summary: AW's PPV Crossfire

14 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You won't believe what went down at Crossfire! Advantage Wrestling held their monthly PPV and just as expected, it was a truly memorable night for AW superstars, staff, and fans alike. Crossfire sold out the Alamo Dome in San Antonio, Texas, and the capacity crowd got exactly what they hoped for… The first match of the night was for the X-Title, between Big Poppa(c) and Viper. Shortly before the match, Viper was backstage being interviewed and said some things, which the champion took very offensively. In a sneak attack, Big Poppa speared his challenger, sending a message and giving him an immediate advantage for the match. Poppa’s attack certainly paid off in the long run, as he was able to retain his title, by holding off his determined challenger. Viper truly gave his heart and soul for that match but it just wasn’t his night, and the title stayed in the hands of the true owner. Our next match of the night was highly anticipated, as the newly formed tag-team consisting of Brad Daddy and Sean Page went into the title match as the favorites against the long time titleholders of T2SF. The match was decided when Sean Page knocked Tomorrow Man of T2SF out cold with an illegal shot behind the refs back and Brad Daddy finished Tomorrow Man off. The ref had to end the match because of a KO and Tomorrow Man was taken to the hospital. Who knows what the future hold for T2SF, but we all know that Cody has been on a role in the singles matches as of late, and if Tomorrow Man is deemed unfit to wrestle again, Cody will surely do him proud. The North American Title went up for grabs in a three-man match between Teemu Stauch McCorde(c), Jake Keeton, and Chazz Irvine. Each man gave it their best effort but only one could win. The man that won pinned both opponents, and that man was Jake Keeton. Yes, that is correct, the new North American champion is Jake Keeton, ending Teemu’s wonderful reign. Only time will tell if McCorde is now ready to take on the big boys in challenging for the World Title. The owner of Advantage Wrestling came out after the North American Match and promptly teased our interests for the next PPV entitled Warfare, which will showcase 16 AW Superstars in a “Slap of the Mat” tournament. The rules were not covered, and the participating superstars were not revealed. More information is expected within the next couple weeks; pretty much all we know is that it will be a submissionist’s tournament. Now onto the Rumble. What a showcase of talent this was. Our first two superstars down to the ring were Nathan Slade and Brian Richman. Our next man in, at the three position, was Cody, who hammered away at Richman as soon as he entered the ring. Cody was aiming high as he promised his battered tag partner that he would win the whole damn thing. Slade was the first one out of the Rumble, as a joint effort between Cody and Richman sent him to the outside mats. The next man in was a new talent in AW, Slippy. Just as quick as Slippy came in the ring, he made his exit and Cody and Richman remained in the ring. We waited for the next superstar as Cody was not going to attempt to throw out the much larger Brian Richman. Announcer John Fulmer announced that he would be the next entrant in the Rumble and slid into the ring. Richman and Fulmer have a history, however the history is not enough for an upset as Richman is able to throw out Fulmer without too much of a fight. Jack Duncan came in as the sixth and after he failed to capitalize on an opportunity to eliminate Richman, Richman instead eliminated him. Unfortunately for Richman though, his success got to his head and as he began gloating, Cody was able to kick him over the top rope and eliminate him. Cody stood in the ring alone until The Vine and Viper came out. Announcer John Fulmer made his way back to the booth. Shortly after, Mike Thump joined in the ring as well, as the ninth entrant. Thump and Vine made an alliance to try to get rid of people, however it was disrupted as Mark Adkins eliminated Vine as soon as he entered. Thump then made the mistake of going after Adkins and was eliminated by him as well. Skylar Clay enters next, and when Sean Page is announced to enter, he gets pre-emptively decked by the Gusemarias who we not supposed to have been at ringside yet. Viper, Adkins, Clay, Page Josh and Ed Gesumaria and still, Cody were all in the ring at once. This doesn’t last as Viper knocks out Adkins and Cody eliminates Clay. More superstars kept pooring in and getting eliminated. Most notably, Rickter got eliminated by Viper, then Viper got eliminated by Stone Devane. Cody made an unbelievable effort, however he could not quite fulfill his promise and was eliminated towards the end by Stone Devane. Devane eliminated stars left and right and finally it came down to him and Big Poppa. The two men battled and in the end Stone Devane was victorious, earning his opportunity for a World Title shot at the next PPV. Hard to believe the Rumble wasn’t even the main event. The main event was for the World Title, between Supernova(c), Creature, and Sapphire. One would think that with Sapphire being a girl, she would have a huge disadvantage, but she has proven to be deserving of her spot as a contender for the World Title. Co-owner Rock Startling joined in to announce the match claiming that he “wanted to get to know the superstars better”. The match went down and although it was believed that the friendship between Nova and Sapphire would turn this into a handicap match for Creature, he was able to pin both Nova and Sapphire. It was obvious that the real reason Startling had come down to ringside was to get under the skin of Sapphire. A new World Champion was crowned and Creature proved that he is the best thing in AW right now by defeating the leaders of Supreme Regime. The aftermath of the World Title match is almost too much to describe. Creature and his buddy Jack Duncan lock the rest of The Regime in a room so they cannot get down to ringside. Creature used his special weapon to seriously injure Nova when he came down to save Sapphire from getting a beat down. The only way to truly understand what took place is to read the provided transcript from the official AW web page… http://www.advantagewrestling.com/fans/posts/759.php What will happen now in AW as we have new champions, and our legend and AW hero in the hospital… but for how long? Tune in and find out! Advantage Wrestling
