IHW Re-Opens

26 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We're back from the grave... to piss you off bitch! We're back IHW Headquarters finally open applications to the public after a total of nearly 6 months of hiatus. Sources lead us to believe that the re-launch PPV – Resurrection will take place a month from now and should be one of the most spectacular shows to ever be aired by IHW; period. At the moment, no talent has been signed by IHW but in the immediate future hope to see new stars aboard the roster, both Singles and Tag Team spaces are available. We hope to become a well established fed within a matter of months and we at IHW are against all forms of superstars cheating their way up the rankings – this time around IHW hopes to become the playground of legends and are fairly sure that they are capable of handling the most seasoned veterans. http://ihw.fedhead.com from there enter the site and read through the information provided and you should be aquainted with the IHW lifestyle. Again, enter the IHW website from http://ihw.fedhead.com and fill out an application if you are at all interested. On the site we have a range of CGI scripts to help it function smoothly, the most important to you as a roleplayer being the Backstage Script. I don't know if you are familiar with the script, but basically it allows you to Update your own bio, record and post roleplays which will be stored in your own area. We've got a news script installed, which may also be of use to you if you wish to post any rumours and/or columns on the site... we also have our very own private message boards which are for IHW members eyes only, which will be the basis of all the Backstage interaction and working between our members - in the past we have always promoted a strong friendly OOC atmosphere and this time around it's going to be no different what so ever. http://ihw.fedhead.com/koi2.html is a sample of our results, written before some server errors nearly caused the death of IHW. I'm quite confident in myself to say that our results will have improved considerbly as the majority of our staff's writing ability has increased throughout the summer. IHW is also considered to be 'the missing link' between Wrestling and Fighting; below is an extract from our 'About' section which will explain to you exactly what IHW is like: The idea of IHW is somewhat new, we focus sherely upon the shear violence and entertainment value of our shows as apposed to the actual face value of our shows - sure we book some of the most outrageous matches known to man, but it is up to you; the wrestlers to make the matches as great and they are supposed to be. IHW is like every other wrestling promotion you come across, minus the pure senseless violence and adult entertainment which happens throughout the promotion on a regular basis. All matches within IHW are hardcore rules; which means no disqualification. This means there are no rules; pin falls count anywhere, there is no time limit what so ever and beating the living shit out of your opponent with a steel chair is perfectly legal. Although the rules of combat within IHW may seem relatively easy to understand, there are a few aspects of competition you may wish to read through before heading any further within your relationship within IHW. Firstly, if the referee decides that one competitor us in a situation which may be life threatening (or at risk of serious injury) they have the authority to end the match and award the victory to his or her opponent. The manager of any given wrestler also has the ability to end the match, in the same way the referee does. If the chosen manager believes their wrestler is in any great danger they then have the ability to "throw in the towel" immediately awarding the victory to the other participant. However, these rules are not ironclad and may change on a case-by-case basis, depending on the situation. Wrestlers and IHW authority have the right to assign stipulations on behalf of themselves and/or their opponents, if all parties within the given situation agree to them. As you can see, our promotion is 'the missing link' between Professional Wrestling and Fighting, a quality that we at IHW are damn proud of! The offer is still on the table if you are interested. Thanks for your time, I hope you consider my offer. Criminal - http://ihw.fedhead.com Insane Hardcore Wrestling
