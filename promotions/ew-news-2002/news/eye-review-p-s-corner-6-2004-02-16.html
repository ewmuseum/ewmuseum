EYE Review: P.'s Corner # 6!

16 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Upon the heels of my last column where I interviewed Semore (Simon) Smoke, I've had quite a bit happen in my life, wanna know about it? Hi folks, I’ve been having a few problems in my professional career that I thought I would update you all on. I interviewed Simon Smoke in my last column. Well, Simon has now reached out to me and changed my life forever, yep, he’s done the impossible and he’s made the once hardcore heart of Mick P. a heart of gold with Jesus lying in it, and with that, I hope I bring you the best that I can about the wrestling world, and a monthly update on my Christian friend, Simon Seymour Smoke. This month I’m writing my column on the heels of EYE wrestling’s first show since it’s close. EYE wrestling brought you it’s very best in the show tonight and didn’t disappoint any wrestling fan. Simon Smoke is beginning his great message on the wrestling program and the other wrestlers are entertaining the non-saved fans as well. Folks, here are the results to the first five matches of the first round platinum bracket. Chris Bagwell defeated Tomb Phate defeated River Burke Simon Smoke defeated Razor Blade R.A.T defeated Marcus Walker Rinjiro defeated Quinton May Next week’s five matches should be: Areyya Vs. Riddle Carl Dee Vs. Fenix G.O.D Vs. Kanye Adam King Vs. Ron Williams Makai Vs. Phoenix Rose You never know what else may come from the wrestlers of EYE wrestling, so I’d recommend you watch the show! I will make a special note on the Simon Smoke match, of course, I hate to shill my best friend, but an important fact should be mentioned about Simon Smoke’s win over Razor Blade. Those of you who know the wrestler Razor Blade know that defeating him is no easy feat. Simon Smoke defeated him almost easily thanks to the help of the Lord himself. God helped Smoke defeat the almost unbeatable Razor Blade. Razor Blade before his loss to Simon Smoke was 78-3-1, which means that he’s only lost 3 times before in the past and now he’s got his fourth ever career loss to Simon Smoke. That shows that God helps whoever believes in him and whoever is on his side, and you better believe in him for real. Other wrestling news out there today, PCW has signed former PCW, PCW2, and OSW World Champion, Boda to another contract, this coming as a shock as many believed that Boda would probably head off to some big time federation like fWo, but he’s chosen to go to his roots of PCW. Also in PCW, Cal Roberts has defeated Skylar Green to become the new PCW World Champion. Well, that was some small PCW news, fWo, blah, there’s no reason to go into a spill about fWo, fWo is talked about enough on this website and in this magazine that it really doesn’t need or deserve another column to shill it’s wrestlers. I focus on the wrestlers who aren’t talked about that often, the ones that deserve to have at least a few words mentioned about them in some form or fashion. Well, with that, I leave you, this month really hasn’t been that eventful, or at least to the point that I think I have to speak about it, but most everything has already been said. So I’ll sign off with all the praise to God I can give. Thank you for reading. -Mick P.
