Face to Face with the WWC Owner

3 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Head News Reporter of the WWC, Robbie Ravnos, went face to face with the new fed head of the year 2003, Paul Levine. In this interview, Ravnos asked all the questions that the people wanted to know and than some. http://wwc.ewlounge.com/images/wwc_logo.jpg Robbie Ravnos - The year of 2003 has been marked as the year of new and up-and-coming federations for the wrestling business. Dozens of wrestling promotions have begun in his year of 2K3, and one of the dozens have been the World Wrestling Corporation. This federation was built by a man named Paul Levine. Paul Levine is someone with a large wrestling business history, working for Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling, jOlt Wrestling, and a short tenure in Old School Wrestling. The federation began slowly, running small shows, but Levine pulled a fast one on the world and suddenly showed how rich and powerful he truely was. Earlier this week, Paul Levine (and the WWC) bought out their "ally", EWF, receiving all rights to the Elite Wrestling Federation, and all contracts. Today, I sit down with Paul Levine, to find out what's in store for WWC, and how long he had planned this out. Paul Levine - It is great to be here Robbie, I thank you for your time. RR - Well Mr. Levine, we have heard the reports on the EWF buying, and I want to get straight to the point. How long did you, Paul Levine, set up the buyout for EWF? Because according to close sources, they have said you've ALWAYS had the money...what made you wait those 3 weeks? Why not just buy out the EWF off the bat? Levine - A great question, and I like the 'straight to the point'-like way you run things. The EWF buyout wasn't really planned, I didn't just want to rush into things without looking over the company. I already took a risk in building the office complex so early, I didn't want to rush into a federation and not know I am ready for what I'm about to do. I've seen WAY too many federations close on me and others, and I refuse to be one of them. So I made sure I had my staff, and I made sure everything was in check. And when I saw that I really wanted to do this, I took a very quick plan and bought out my "ally" in EWF. Now, rest assured, if those we released from EWF were good workers, we referred them to another federation with the same pay. RR - Well, with the EWF bought out, what are your plans for the EWF? Levine - If you have seen how other large companies run things, they own other smaller federations called Developmental Communities. These federations are around for new superstars to work out their kinks, or for old superstars to work off their ring rust. I'll be keeping the EWF alive, and most of the roster I currently have will be placed in EWF to work out some kinks and to also gain more overness with the crowds. RR - So with the majority of the current contracts you hold, going to the EWF, what is in store for the main federation called WWC? With most of the current roster going to EWF, it will leave the WWC roster bare...what do you have in store for the future of the WWC? Levine - Well, for the past few weeks I have sent my best talent scouts around America to search for some talent. I have sent Lauren Host, Brandon Ramos and Danny Vicious, my top three talent scouts; to look for some Grade A talent that could work for the WWC roster. I have recently gotten calls from Lauren Host and Danny Vicious on a few names that they believe should have a meeting with me. RR - Can you give us a hint as to who those names are? Levine - Actually, I'm not at liberty to say. I don't want to get people's hopes up, if things do not work out. But the names I've heard, HAVE been around the wrestling circuit a few times...enough for people to know who they are. RR - Well, one question I can ask is this: I've seen ALOT of federations that just grab the excess talent from 'big time federations' and place them on their roster. For instance, NWATNA (www.nwatna.com) has signed guys like Raven and D'Lo Brown...rejects from the WWE Inc. (www.wwe.com) I'm wondering if the WWC is going to do the same...or are we going to see some brand new talent entering the ring? Levine - I'd say about half and half. I'm a very open man when it comes to rookies, because in my opinion EVERYONE deserves a chance at the spotlight. Now I won't deny the fact that some names that have been given to me by some of my talent scouts have been remotely big names, but others have been basically new to the mainstream...most Danny Vicious's recommendations have been fairly new and straight out of training camp. RR - When will we expect to hear any news on the signings of this new roster for the WWC? Levine - You really can't say with these kind of things. We can have very easy negotiations and get done in a day or two, or we can have alot of problems with the contracts and be waiting a good three months for the superstar to sign. But, if I was a betting man...and I am, I'd put my money on within the month. I believe you will be hearing reports within the month about the roster. RR - We all look forward to it, can't wait to see what you have in store. But, changing the subject, I have some concerns on the WWC. Mainly, when is the corporation set to begin taping...and also, when will we be hearing of a WWC weekly televised event? Levine - As it pretains to the start of the World Wrestling Corporation, I am looking for somewhere in the Fall...most likely November. The moment we have a set date on the start of the WWC, I will talk with Television Networks about a weekly event. In fact, I have a meeting with ABC, as well as HBO, about a weekly event in July. RR - I hope to hear some reports about that. Now Mr. Levine, I have to ask one strong question. I have heard ALOT of reports about you and your rivalry with certain wrestlers of todays world. I've heard things about you and Eron the Relentless going at it a few times, I've heard you and Action! Wrestling have gotten into verbal attacks, and I've also heard you have held a grudge with the now defunct IOW...any comments on that? Levine - Well Robbie, I'm actually going to address all of that within due time. I'm actually thinking of expressing my thoughts on the first event of WWC, to let everyone know who I truely am and what I'm about. RR - Well Mr. Levine, that is all the questions I have for you. I thank you for your time, and look forward to continuing my work here in the World Wrestling Corporation. Levine - Anytime Robbie, anytime. WWC Online
