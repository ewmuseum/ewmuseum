Empire Wrestling gets BROKEN!

21 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EW's, First edition of "The BIG Picture" talks about This past Tuesday's Broken show, etc... By: VP Mr.BIG Welcome to the first ever edition of "The BIG Picture" I am the VP of Empire Wrestling, Mr.BIG. This past Tuesday, Broken was live from Atlanta, Georgia. Tuesday night, Broken named a number one contender for the Empire World Championship. But by night's end, a new champion was named!!! And that name was Nathan Slade. Nathan Slade defeated Jake Keeton for Keeton's T.V. title in a controversial match. Keeton, open about his rage towards the match, felt that Slade and West side Raph were in together from the begining. Slade was quoted saying this "Are you insinuating that I had this planned all along, huh? Not at all! Wether Raph was the ref or not the outcome would still be the f**king same, me defeating that redneck Keeton! I was a problem, 'The Solution' couldn't solve whether his toothless ass likes it or not!!" In that same night, the Empire Wrestling Number one contender was named as Razor Blade gets the victory over Big Poppa in an amazing match up. RFB will now go one on one against the Champion at the up coming PPV, Mutiny. That's about it for this episode, just a reminder that this Tuesday's Broke will be live from Civic Center - Charleston, West Va. Live on Showtime!
