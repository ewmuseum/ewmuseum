Action! Wrestling Bantam champion Coral Avalon responds to Voran Xias' challenge

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With just four days away from a major pay-per-view, and with multiple injuries he's been struggling with, what does Coral Avalon have to say about Voran Xias? A statement issued by Coral Avalon via his website dealt with the recent challenge laid out by Voran Xias. "I don't know what Mr. Xias has against me or the other champions, really. Normally, I'd look at comments like he made on his show and just shrug them off. But, there's one thing I don't do, and that's turn down challenges. I will accept his challenge." If the challenge goes down, Coral Avalon will be wrestling Voran Xias on June 27th, just two days before his match with Keith Scott Zimmerman on the 29th, in Action's upcoming "Till Death Do Us Part" pay-per-view. He has been working with several injuries, including a sore neck from his matches with Duel and Daniel Phillips, constantly injured ribs first injured at Make It Or Break It back in March, and a low-grade concussion from a botched moonsault by Jeff Garvin at Juggernaut II.
