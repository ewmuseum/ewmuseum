PCW Tuesday Turmoil Recap

4 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This past Tuesday Turmoil, Primetime Championship Wrestling saw a strange unity between three roster members who share the same last name, amongst other things. PCW Tuesday Turmoil Recap For 6/3/03 -Tessa Windsor-Green and Dante Jackson considered a mutual alliance, until Gaz Green showed up and threw everything out of whack. -Certain someones decided to impersonate Justice Inc. and substituted for them in saving a nearby victim of an accident -HANK~! d. Dan Osbourne via pinfall after hitting the Off-Beat to claim his first victory since returning -Gaz Green tried to prank the surname-sharing World Champion, Skylar Green, but the results were not exactly what he would’ve wanted -Silvio Rochelli brought in some friends, who were assured by Rochelli that a plan was in the works -Matt Jackson & Sergio Bullent d. Shane Robertson & Menolly Xenos via Jackson’s pinfall on Robertson after hitting Nuclear Fission -A mysterious someone decided that they needed to finally fulfill James Edwards’ dream, but seemed to have bad intentions in mind -The famed tractor that once brought the reign of Media on PCW made a fatal return for someone else and was part of finally having a decision in the vendetta of Flaymin Aymin and El Diablo -SOCIETY d. Lacuna Debris via submission after a quick annihilation and then locking in Conformity -Post-match, SOCIETY and Michael Chang protested to PCW and the public that the fines placed on the Order, until Miss Moonlight stopped the little press conference and, with the help of Lacuna Debris, cleared the Order from the ring -Silvio Rochelli found a most devious way to get his message across…but then was cut off abruptly -Tessa continued to dream and Team Green solved at least one of the night’s problems -Kael Law d.Flaymin Aymin via pinfall in Law’s debut after hitting the Crimson Dynamo -Gaz Green decided to tell Dante how it is only to get a platinum dissing in the middle of the ring -Flaymin Aymin seemed very reluctant to talk about Angela and took off -Team Green (Skylar Green, Gaz Green, Tessa Windsor-Green) d. Messiahs Of The Dark Prophesy (Pharaoh, Dante Jackson, Dorn Tracer) via a Gaz pinfall on Dorn after a sit-down powerbomb and as per the pre-match stipulation, Tessa Windsor immediately got her TV title shot -Tessa Windsor d. Dorn Tracer for the TV Title via pinfall after the Final Vision and Team Green stood tall at the end of the night, surprisingly united Primetime Championship Wrestling
