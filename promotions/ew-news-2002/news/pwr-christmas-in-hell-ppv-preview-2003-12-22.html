[PWR] Christmas in Hell PPV Preview

22 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This weekend, PWR Christmas in Hell airs live from the Palace of Auburn Hills in Detroit, Michigan! This year, Platinum Wrestling Revolution plans to give wrestling fans everywhere a holiday season that they will not forget with PWR Christmas in Hell! For the past three weeks on PWR's flagship show, "Glory", Title Tournaments have taken place to shape the card for this sunday's pay per view event and the way things have turned out, it will be a great one. The PWR Suicidal Championship will be on the line as Zachalous Dangerous will take on MacBeth. MacBeth has always been known to be an evil man and his extreme background lead people to believe that MacBeth would be the favorite going into this match. But over the past few weeks, Zachalous Dangerous has shown the PWR crowd that he is capable of competeing with the PWR's best. It should be interesting to see how it turns out... The PWR Primetime Title will also be on the line as the seven foot monster, Gargantuflem will go one on one with "The Legend", Mr. Rich. Both men are no stranger to gold....but neither have been fortunate enough yet to touch PWR gold. That is more of a reason why these two men are expected to bring their "A" game this sunday night as they try to win the Primetime Championship in this match. Both of them being veterans in the sport, they both know that their best effort will be what it will take to win this one. The Palace of Auburn Hills will also be the host of the two Semi-final matches of the PWR Territorial Tournament to determine which two men will compete in the PWR Territorial Title match on the January 4th edition of Glory. In one match, we will see Drone, Joe Johnson and Jose de Vaca. In the other, we will see Erik Murdock, Blood Thirst and John Daniels. It should be interesting to see which two will be able to rebound from their losses last week on Glory to win their semi-final matches! I can't wait! And all of that action will lead to our huge main event. It will be a six man hell in a cell match for the vacant PWR World Heavyweight Championship. The six participants will be Scott Monroe, Nick Dangerous, Chazz Mendel, Stephen Jackson, Logan Darkholme and Marco. In this match, all issues will be put to the side. There will be no allies. All six will be competeing for the same thing, to become the PWR World Champion. They will tear the house down but after all of the debris is left, there will only be one winner. But who will it be? Expect all of that and so much more as you see PWR Christmas in Hell on Sunday December 28th LIVE from the Palace of Auburn Hills in Detroit, Michigan on Pay Per View! Still looking for a reason to become a PWR fan? Check out this week's Pay Per View and you'll find that reason! PWR Website
