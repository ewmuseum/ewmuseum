Emo Kid going insane?

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As seen earlier today, Chris Carter destroyed a wall in a hotel room after having a known spree of crying on or at fWo Survivor III events. Is their more problem to him than we know? With the strenuous schedule and the torture of eliminating a team from the game, it is understood that a team in this Fans Wrestling Organization's Survivor would be stressed or even frazzled, but would they reach the point of destruction? As today, when Green Team members Chris and Jim Carter, better known as Emo Kids, checked out of their room, an attendant noticed a small, rectangular hole on a wall inside the room. This is just one in a series of events that Emo Kids, mainly Chris "Carraba", have participated in that are knowingly disturbing. On the first edition of fWo Uprising, May 30, after a mishap during a match with teammates Villani Connection and banished teammates 2 Sents, Carraba broke out into tears on screen. The reaction was quite disheartening around the team as people were worried that the 19-year-old Florida youth would not be able to take the events in the day of an everyday fWo Superstar. The events continued on the next Monday, when Carraba and his brother bumped into a management official while Carraba was in tears and contemplating leaving the contest. Today’s incident was brought upon by the fact of the well publicized shake up of the Green and Gold Team. When asked his brother and team mate, Chris threw the brothers’ beeper machine into the wall after receiving the news of the shake up and the new team rosters. The beeper was never recovered; however it is believed that the beeper was in fact inside the wall where the whole occurred. Jim Atkins offered to pay for any damages occurred by his disturbed brother. After these three events, the rest of the Green Team, the fWo organization and his brother look on wearily as they fear for the emotional state of the youth. fWo Survivor III
