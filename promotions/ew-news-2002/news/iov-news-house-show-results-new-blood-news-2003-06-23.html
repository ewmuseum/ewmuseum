IOV News: House Show Results, New Blood News

23 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What a night it was at a house show for the begining of Mr Self Destruct's first week as intern Commissioner! At a house show, we witnessed the crowning of new IOV Tag Team Champions! Hapy Bob and MIKEY beat out the teams of SHOCK/SINN, TYRANT/DRADE, SANDOWN/MARTZ/X-FACTOR! A bloody battle it was! We saw the rise of a new fiend, TYRANT and the heart of Champions in SANDOWN and X-FACTOR! SHOCK and SINN were at throats end and I'm sure that the stable of "New Blood" is at an unrest with SHOCK and SINN going at it last light after the match and Happy being one half of the Tag Team Titles! We also witnessed the crowning of the new XTV Title Champion, TYRANT! Although his reign is questionable, there is no doubt that this man will be a terror to deal with! MSD is now into his second and final week before "The EVE of DESTRUCTION" ends and SHOCK takes the helm. At DisOrder 6/30/03, we'll see: The IOV WORLD CHAMPION, "The Franchise" Tony SINN, take on the #1 Contender and IC Champion, SHOCK BLESNAR, for the WORLD TITLE, in a cage! The "Vet's v Rookies", WAR GAMES match will pit the "Rookie Team" led by SANDOWN, TYRANT, X-FACTOR, THE KROW and DRADE, take on TEAM VET, LED by Mr Self Destruct, Happy Bob, MIKEY, The GAME! All the members of team Vet were once in the old EWE/NWE! So get ready and hang on as the final week of "The EVE of DESTRUCTION" closes! (Card Subject to change) IOV Wrestling
