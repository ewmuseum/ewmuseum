It's me... MAR-I-O!

9 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Which Wog beat Seth Anson around the head and is currently moving his life, and family, to Baton Rouge for a new start in wrestling at SbW? The fans of Spinebuster Wrestling have a little more excitement to add to their day when Crimson Fist of Glory rolls around, and it's in the form of a little Wog from Australia. Yes folks, J. Leslie Voss has managed to sign someone else with a little bit of skill, someone else who has been over with the fans back from IOW versions 3 and 4. And I'm talking about Mariopoulos Pontarakos Jnr. Still not ringing any bells? OK, I won't leave you guessing any longer. Voss managed to snare Mario the Wog. The second generation superstar who currently resides in Hollywood, after travelling a fair way around the world studying his craft. You may remember Mario from his feud with Seth Anson in IOW, helping put over the major talent there. Well, Mario wants to step up into the limelight, and having seen what J. Leslie Voss has already done with his promotion, Mario thought he was just the man for the job... "Voss is, like, so cool. He's made himself into, like, one of the biggest underground stars in this business. He's totally great and has, like, so much in-ring ability. He's put his name on the map and he's, like, only been over here for a little while. "I'm definitely, like, totally stoked to be a part of the SbW roster. There's, like, soooo much talent there and it's got to be one the, like, strongest Indy promotions EVERRRR!" Although talking to Mario is like the incarnation of a male Valley girl, Mario seems to have his feet firmly in the ground. His father, "The Greek Stallion" Mariopoulos Pontarakos Snr., had a very illustrious career, on the verge of making it into the big time when he fell privy to the high life. "I contribute my whole, like, life to my father. He made what I am today and made me, like, grow stronger as a person and performer. I take this business, like, totally seriously, coz it's in my blood. This is my family's way and this is how it remains." The 24 year-old father and husband says he is happy to relocate down to Baton Rouge to get closer to what could be a fresh new start to his career and his family. "Charlie got a transfer from the Hollywood PD and they're, like, totally willing to take her on in Lafayette. In fact, she'll probably get promoted or something. And Spike, my son, is like, two years old now. He's growing up so fast he'll be, like, driving next. Baton Rouge is a nice town and we want to, like, start over and get out of Hollywood. So many tragic memories there." So the Wog has arrived in Spinebuster and the fans are already growing excited over his arrival. Can Spinebuster be the new start that he hopes for? Could this be his opportunity to finally get noticed and make it into the big time? In my opinion, I think Mario will take SbW by the scruff of the neck, shake it until it drops the title in his lap. But only time will tell... Spinebuster Wrestling
