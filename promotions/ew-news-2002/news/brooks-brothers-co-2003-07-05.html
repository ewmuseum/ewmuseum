Brooks Brothers Co.

5 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After a flimsy few days, the cWf is back on its feet with the guidance of AJ Brooks and Levi Brooks. Or is it misguidance? cWf doesn't look like this. After a flimsy few days, the cWf is back on its feet with the guidance of AJ Brooks and Levi Brooks. Or is it misguidance? Only a few days ago, AJ Brooks left his brother Levi in search of bigger glory and most importantly for him money, by opening up his own business (which was rumored to be a hotel) out in the Los Angeles area; but after a few days it seems AJ Styles has decided to come to cWf... all for the wrong reasons. "You see," he told me yesterday evening while on Jet Blue, the new surprising sponsor for Championship Wrestling Federation, "I'm not coming back for money this time... I'm coming back to win a bet! You see, I had a special talk with him yesterday where he beliggerantly insulted me... he said I couldn't make it... he said I couldn't make anymore money... bullshit! So I said, FUCK YOU bastard and said that I'd be coming back to the cWf to prove him wrong!" After hearing this surprising turn of events, I asked him what he was exactly going to do. "You know that Michael Scopes guy that we just signed right? There is no WE. That was me... that was my farewell gift for cWf... a big name to top off the list... I wanted to go off in good intentions, so I signed him for them! But now? BUT NOW THEY ARE SCREWED because Michael Scopes and I are going to RUN OVER the cWf... NO ONE will stop us!" I then asked him about MMA and IOW sensation, Christian Embers but didn't receive anything but a, "FUCK YOU" and a "GOOD BYE." I then talked to Levi Brooks who, like always, didn't have much to say. What he did say was perplexing... "My brother is an idiot... I DON'T NEED HIM! I got Jet Blue to cover me baby! He's coming over here to see if he can piss me off by making a little money... bah, idiot... me and Christian Embers will take care of him." I then asked him about AJ Brooks' comments about Michael Scopes. "Michael Scopes? He's a washed up wrestler... he's not even that 324 pound behemoth he was in IOW or FATE... he's 271 pounds! Bah, he's an idiot! CHRISTIAN EMBERS WILL KILL THEM BOTH." AJ Brooks and Levi Brooks feud? Michael Scopes and Christian Embers feud? WHAT ABOUT "SUPERSTAR" VINCE JACOBS! Stay tuned for more... cWf - Championship Wrestling Federation, the madness never stops~!
