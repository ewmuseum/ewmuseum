NGW: Back in Action

5 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NGW is back after a short vacation. They held a press conference in NYC. Read more for details... With the return of NGW there is much excitement floating around. The staff members, ring crew and announcers are getting pumped up for the return of Tuesday Night Storm on October 27th. Alot has been put in place for the return, and NGW's high paced weekly show will be back with a new look and a new attitude. A press conference was held at NGW Headquarters in the heart of downtown New York City. Scottywood, KrazE, Ricky Blaze and NGW World Champion Scott King made appearences at the conference. Not only was the announcement made that the first Storm will be held in late October, but the name and location of the reborn NGW's first PPV. "High Stakes" will be held at Caesars Palace in Las Vegas on November 23rd. I caught up with former NGW XC champion Ricky Blaze. He seems to be excited about the return of NGW. "So NGW is comin' back, huh? Well you sure as hell know I'll be back. My home is NGW, and my playground is the ring with the letter NGW stamped on it. If anyone gets in my face this time around, they're going down." He said while I caught up with him at the gym after the press conference. I have also seen many NGW superstars training for NGW's premier episode of Storm. The roster seems to be excited for what NGW's owner Scottywood has in store for them. Reporting, Chris Smith NGW
