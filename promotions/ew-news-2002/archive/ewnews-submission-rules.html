Submit a News Story or Opinion Piece Last Updated: March 18, 2003

Using the form below you can submit a news story or opinion piece for possible inclusion onto the eWNews website. To submit a story, use the form below to enter in the required information. Your submission will be reviewed by a member of the eWNews staff and posted in a timely manner. Thank you for contributing to eWNews!
Please note: Some articles may be selected for publishing in the eWNews monthy magazine and by submitting to this website you are granting eWNews and partners to republish your submission provided proper credit is made.

Posting Rules:
1: Do not post blatant shills for your fed or character. 2: Do not post news for feds that have not yet posted at least one (1) card. No news for "Coming Soon" feds.
3: Do not post personal, OOC attacks on anyone or any remarks that may be perceived as racist.
4: And finally, this site is intended to operate as a real in-character wrestling news site. Please do not use OOC terms like "efed", "ewrestling" or "applications" in your articles.
5: Have fun!

The submission form's own fields: Article Type (News Article or Opinion Piece); Your Name; Your eMail; Headline; Leader; Story/Opinion Piece; Included Artwork; Art Caption (optional); Link (optional); Link Name (optional).

The page ended in a working submission form, which is not served. Its ten field labels are listed in the last paragraph, as the form printed them; the five posting rules and the republication clause are the hub's own words, unchanged.
