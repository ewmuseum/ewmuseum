The Staff

Damon Trask
Editor and Newsroom Manager
Damon began his broadcasting career on the ring-announcing team of the now-defunct UWA. Damon Trask always seems to have a story to tell, and although these stories may be absolutely full of either half-truths or bold faced lies, they are always published on time. Trask has been heard to say "I don't care if it's not accurate, is it printable?"


Wolfgang Spatz
General Wrestling Reporter
"The Everyman" was a German legend in the 70's and 80's, wrestling all over Europe and Asia, and is considered one of the father's of modern-day hardcore wrestling. He officially hung up his wrestling boots in 1993 after 35 years, staying with the business as referee and part-time announcer for several federations over the last decade.

Chris Richards
Asylum Fighting Reporter
Chris Richards began as a wrestler in many small indy wrestling feds and worked himself into one of the top regional wrestling promotions ever. From there Chris seemed to be one of the best wrestlers the promotion had to offer, until a freak accident in the ring left him with a surgically replaced knee, and to never wrestle again. Chris fell in love with the Asylum, and stars like Borst and A.D. This is his first reporting job, so this should be a doozy.


Jade Jackson
General Wrestling Reporter
Growing up in a male-infested family, the love for Professional Wrestling was always in the house. Jade, though never was fond of it in her early life, soon found herself watching and going to events with her Brothers’ more often than not. Doing so, she started a love for Wrestling like no other finding it very interesting to follow. Being a small-build girl though Jade didn’t make it too far when it came down to being able to wrestle, but still followed it and grew a talent for sharing the Federation stories with others.


Sheik Yerbouti
General Wrestling Reporter
Working as a freelance/field reporter for The News, Yerbouti gained the respect and admiration of the wrestling industry for his concise news reports and strong opinions. Sheik Yerbouti is a definite advantage for any News agency.
