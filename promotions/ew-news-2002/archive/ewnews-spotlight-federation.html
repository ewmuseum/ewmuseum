CEW - Cyber Extreme Wrestling Spotlight for November, 2003

Stats Call Letters: CEW Website: www.cewentertainment.com Weekly Show: Thrill Recent PPV: Oct 28, 2003 "Out For Blood" Dallas, TX First Aired: JULY 30, 1998 WORLD CHAMPION: "SHOWTIME" NICK ALEXANDER NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION: KRIS eGo FTS CHAMPION: SHAYNE GOWERS TV CHAMPION: JOHNNY PSYCHOTIC TAG CHAMPIONS: THE 3-pAC

Reviewer's Ratings ROSTER TALENT: 8/10 Roster talent is based on promo ability and wrestling skill of the entire federation roster, from the main event talent right down to the curtain jerkers.
> SHOW QUALITY: 7/10 Show quality rating is based on the production value of the weekly show(s), the quality of scriptwriting, acting and most importantly, wrestling. STAR POWER: 5/10 Star power is based on the name value of the federation's main event stars, not only in the eyes of that federation's fans, but in the eyes of the entire wrestling industry.

LONGEVITY: 9/10 Longevity is based on the federation's history, it's ability to persevere, and the length of time the federation has been in the public eye.

*Reviewer's Ratings are the opinion of the Editor, and may differ from others on Staff at eWNews.

Federation History Not Available at presstime.

Reviewer's Comments Few federations show the longevity and commitment of Cyber Extreme Wrestling. Although they can fly under the radar and miss out on the limelight that other federations enjoy, CEW is one of the last undiscovered diamonds in the rough. Veterans like Shayne Gowers, Buzzsaw and Nick Alexander lead the lockerroom with strong main events and credible title-holders.

But the real strength of the CEW lies in it's up-and-comers such as Vincent Francis, Jakob Mayhem and Ryan Anderson. These rookies, should they remain healthy, will be stars in the years to come.

Even though it's been somewhat of a slow buildup, CEW is poised on the edge of national recognition. eWNews is proud to call CEW a Spotlight Fed.
