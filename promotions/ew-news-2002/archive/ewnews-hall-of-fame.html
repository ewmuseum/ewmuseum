Machina Inducted to EW-News Hall of Fame in October, 2002

Stats Federations: fWo, CWL Real Name: Ed Theme Song: Come With Me, by Puff Daddy Born: August 19, 1975 From: South Korea Height: 5' 11 Weight: 180lbs Wrestling Style: High-Flying Signature Moves: Leg Drop, Plancha, Huricarana, Tope Finisher Move: 450 Splash Finisher Name: Hope He Doesn't Move Current Team: Lone Gunmen Title History fWo World Tag Team Championship 10/14/98 , Wednesday Warfare fWo World Tag Team Championship 12/30/99 , Countdown '99 2 History The man known only as Machina first debuted with the start of the fWo league, hailing from South Korea. Machina was at first a silent do-gooder, who let his high-flying performance in the ring speak for him. However, after a near-career ending injury at the hands of War, Machina began turning to cheating tactics in an attempt to gain the Crusierweight title. Somewhere along the road, his mind completely snapped into tiny little pieces.

Recruiting Rudo Insano, and then later Sid Snow and Eclipse, they formed the team known as the Lone Gunmen, and are often considered completely insane.

Machina's fWo career came to a halt in March of 2000, when he was lost at sea and feared dead. It was later found that he had survived that mishap, washing up on the deserted island used for the filming of the TV show "Survivor". Machina joined up with the cast of the show, trying to get kicked off the island so he could come back to civilization.

Machina later joined the legendary CWL promotion, and retired from wrestling in 2001. His wonderful mixture of comedy and sincerity entertained us all, and Machina footage from both the fWo and CWL continues to make wrestling fans around the world, even after his unfortunate early retirement from the game.

The News is proud to induct Machina as our first Hall of Fame entry.
