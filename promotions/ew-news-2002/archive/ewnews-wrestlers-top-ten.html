The Wrestler's Top Ten

With this feature, we will ask a different wrestler each week to comment on his or her top picks of the best wrestling talent in the industry today. The talent listed below are the opinions and choices of this week's "Hotchair Wrestler", and not those of The News staff. This feature is not to be confused with the "Newsmakers" posted elsewhere on this site, which are chosen by the staff at The News.

This week's "Hotchair Wrestler" is fWo Wrestling's Great Dragon. Read below to find his picks for the top of the wrestling industry, listed down to number one, in his opinion the top star today.

10 - Mark Windham The man is the current CSWA World Heavyweight Champion. He is a veteran of the Professional Wrestling industry, and has long ago earned the right to wear the title in CSWA, and subsequently in any other promotion he was involved in. I hope to see more of this great worker in the future, as the main reason he wasn't higher was my unfamiliarity with his work.

9 - Eli Flair
"Total Elimination" Eli Flair. A man who has spent the better part of his career in the CSWA and HEW. Flair long ago earned my respect, but has over the last few years suffered some pretty nasty injuries that have debilitated his in ring skill a bit. He's still a great worker and a nice guy backstage from my few encounters with him, but he's not on the top of the list as from a working standpoint, he's not exactly in peak condition.

8 - Mr Haunt
"The Devil Machine" has garnered much respect recently, where in 21wrestling he earned the right to carry the promotion's World championship. Haunt is a talented wrestler, but he doesn't appear to have quite as much experience or greater potential as some of the other superstars I will mention further down, though he is indeed a great worker.

7 - Sylo
Sylo is a very talented individual who has had several years of in-ring experience. He has had some great matches that I've seen, and from people I know, I've heard he has a pretty good demeanor outside of the ring. I see great things in the future from him in PIW.

6 - "Golden" Glen Miller
 Glen Miller is a man I had the priviledge of working with, albeit for a short while, in fWo Survivor II. He is a veteran of the ring, being in it for around three decades. He still works very well for a man who recently turned fifty. He is sort of like the Terry Funk of 21wrestling, except that instead of trying to destroy his body with insane maneuvers on weapons and so forth, he utilizes ring psychology very well for a man of his age.

5 - "The Cult Icon" Jon Crisp
Jon Crisp is an extremely talented man, who has proved his worth at being a champion back in the IML, TEW, TCW and RW. He is very technically sound, and though problems backstage in fWo caused his push to be delayed, he was on the verge of becoming a superstar prior to its shutdown, from what I was told. I'm hoping he reaches World level status in 21wrestling very soon, just as Mr. Haunt did.

4 - Mufanga Kagos
What can I say? Ben is a good friend of mine from back in the fWo Survivor II, and I even knew him from some Indy promotions prior to that. Ben may not be the most fundamentally sound technician known in Wrestling history, but he can put on a good match, given the opportunity. I see great things for him in the future.

3 - Ric Chronos
Ric Chronos is an EXTREMELY gifted technician, as well as a gifted mic worker, which is very rarely seen in this industry. Ric knows how to get under the crowds skin, and to get the response that he wants to. In addition, he can carry most people to a ***+ match, most of the time. Yes, he did have Alcohol problems, but he overcame them, and I doubt they will be plaguing him anytime soon, if ever. I hope to see him continuing World title feuds in whatever promotion he will be in.

2 - Zero
Zero is a talented Hardcore brawler that I got the chance to meet a few times backstage in the fWo during Body Count 2002. The man knows how to put on a great Hardcore match, and though things apparently fell through between him and fWo, I hope he regains his popularity in his alma mata, HEW, where his feud with Havoc grew.

And the Number 1 Pick...

1 - Ruben Ross
My number one is 'The man who pops the twenty share', the 'Black Plague' Ruben Ross. Ruben is a man whom I've gotten to know from Body Count 2002, as well as backstage in WWRPG. Ruben is one of the most skilled High Flyers I've seen, who can utilize other styles of wrestling to put on classic matches. I'm pretty sure he could put on a ****+ match with a piece of paper if he wanted to, even. This man is a former WWRPG champion, FOUR TIME WWRPG "I Quit" Champion, as well as holding every fWo title, except for the Hardcore and Tag Team titles. He also won Junior Heavyweight titles in two other promotions, and because he's only 23, he has many years ahead of him, unlike what many internet reporters claim.
