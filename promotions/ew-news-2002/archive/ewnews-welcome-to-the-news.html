Welcome to The News

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Well, it's finally here.

This site is to cater to the minds of you, the wrestlers and fans of Professional Wrestling, with news and stories about your favorite federations and wrestling superstars.

The staff of The News hopes to bring you a well-rounded plethora of information, centered on most, if not all of today's most popular wrestling federations.

Please visit the "Letter to the Editor" section of The News to see your chance to contribute to the stories we provide. Simply fill out the form and send it in with any article you would like to see appear on the site, and it will be posted shortly. We hope this feature will help curb complaints about the federations we choose to cover. We want to cover them all, of course, but there simply isn't enough hours in a day to do so. Therefore, if any federartion owner or staffer would like news about his/her federation to appear on this site, just send it in and we'll post it for you. There are two simple rules you MUST follow when sumbitting news, and this is the same for the reporters on staff, as well as those of you who would like to contribute articles.

1. Everything must be in-character. If anything has the letter "E" before "wrestling", we won't run the story.

2. There will be no blatant shilling. If there is a statement that sound even close to "Come join my fed", we won't run it.

Thats all the serious stuff... if you would like to contribute, please read the existing stories, to get a feel for the way we want the posts to read. And have fun.

Thanks,

The Editor of the News,
Damon Trask
