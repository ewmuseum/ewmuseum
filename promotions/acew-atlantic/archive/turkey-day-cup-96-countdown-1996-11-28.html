Turkey Day Cup '96 Countdown

28 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Live on RSPWF - if you don't believe me that ACEW is not a lame e-fed, then check out this Countdown before our PPV comes out later!! Here goes, and thanks for reading!!! ------------------------------------------------------------------ (Camera opens to about 10,000 screaming and cheering fans LIVE! from Madison Square Garden in New York City!! Chris Taft and Volcano Jackson are there at the broadcast booth!!) Chris Taft (CT): Hello ACEW fans!! Welcome to the Turkey Day Cup `96 Countdown!! We're just hours away from the start of TDC `96..and it's going to be an exciting card!! Volcano Jackson (VJ): That's right, Taft!! I can't wait till I kick Quicksilver's sorry ass all over this piece of crap arena!! CT: And a special hello to all of our fans watching on RSPWF!! VJ: Stupid ungrateful marks!! CT: I think you're confusing RSPWF with RSPW, Volcano!! VJ: Oh yeah..heheh.. RSPW. Where else could you find out news from someone's best friend's father's brother's best friend's cousin's mom's father's cousin's brother's son's classmate's bodyguard's dad's best friend's security guard who works for WCW!! CT: Well, let's get to our only match here on the Countdown real quick - it's Billy Hollywood of the Superstars squaring off against Mark "Flash" Purcell of Intensity Inc. VJ: This should be a great match, with Billy Hollywood destroying Purcell!!! CT: Don't doubt the athletic ability of these two atheltes!! They may not be big names in e-wrestling, but that sure can take the fight right to you!! VJ: Let's get down to the aisle-side camera, shall we?? (Arn Anderson's WCW Music plays as Billy Hollywood comes down the aisle with his manager, Vic Victory!! He receives a huge chorus of boos as he comes down the aisle, flipping off some fans!!) CT: That was very rude!! VJ: These New York pukes deserve that kind of treatment!! (Threshold by Stavescare plays as Mark "Flash" Purcell runs down the aisle headed towards ringside to a HUGE crowd pop!! He is highfiving the fans as well!!) CT: What an ovation the fans here in Madison Square Garden give Mark "Flash" Purcell!! VJ: What have these fans been smoking?? They think Purcell has a CHANCE!! HAHAHAHA!!! CT: I think Purcell has a chance... VJ: Ha. (Bell rings) CT: Well, here we go! VJ: This is going to be a massacre!! CT: Billy Hollywood and Mark Purcell lock up! Billy gains the advantage as he takes down Purcell in an armdrag takedown!! Quickly changes it to a standing wristlock!!! VJ: A great maneuver! CT: Purcell quickly reaches the ropes and Billy Hollywood breaks the hold...BILLY HOLLYWOOD KICKS PURCELL IN THE SIDE OF THE RIB AREA!! That has got to hurt!! VJ: Like I said earlier, Billy Hollywood doesn't play around!! He will take it to you!! CT: Hollywood sends Purcell into the ropes..Hollywood ducks down..Purcell with a Sunset Flip!! Count!! (1...2...KICKOUT!!!) VJ: Billy Hollywood kicks out at two!! You're not going to get him that easily, Purcell!! CT: And now Hollywood sends Purcell towards the ropes again..Purcell reverses and TOSSES HOLLYWOOD OUT ONTO THE CONCRETE!!! Wow!! Billy Hollywood is HURT!!! VJ: I can see that, Taft!! Look how he hit the concrete!! CT: Purcell is climbing the turnbuckles..Billy Hollywood doesn't see him!!! Hollywood turns around and MARK "FLASH" PURCELL HITS HIM WITH A FLYING DROPKICK!!!!! VJ: Both men are down now..that dropkick hurt Billy Hollywood more, though!! CT: And now Purcell gets a chair from a fan..he turns around and BILLY HOLLYWOOD SPIN-KICKS THE CHAIR IN PURCELL'S FACE!!!! VJ: YES!! A GREAT MOVE BY BILLY HOLLYWOOD!!!! CT: Hollywood rolls Purcell back into the ring. Hollywood grabs onto the top rope and COMES DOWN WITH A GUILLOTINE DROP on Purcell!! Nicely executed!!! VJ: Hollywood takes a little too much time to make the cover!! There he goes!! Lateral press!! (1...2...KICKOUT!!!!) CT: And the busted-open Mark "Flash" Purcell kicks out at two!!! VJ: And now Billy Hollywood hits Purcell with a couple of kneedrops in the small of the back!! Trying to keep Purcell on the ground!! CT: Well, it's working!! Billy Hollywood is climbing the turnbuckles!! He's at the top turnbuckle!! Billy Hollywood with a SHOOTING STAR PRESS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Count!!! (1...2...KICKOUT!!!!) CT: PURCELL JUST KICKED OUT OF THAT!! Wow!!!!! VJ: The ref was taking so much time!! It's not fair!!! CT: Purcell now sends Hollywood into the ropes...Purcell ducks down and BILLY HOLLYWOOD CATCHES PURCELL.....BILLY HOLLYWOOD EXECUTES A DOCTOR BOMB!!!! VJ: Hollywood is quick to cover!! Lateral press!! (1....2....3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!) Announcer: Heeeeeeeere is your winner...........BILLLLY HOLLLLLLLLYWOOOOOOOOOOOODDDDDDDDDDDDDDD!!!!!!!!!!!!! CT: Well fans, we'll see you here on the ACEW Network and on RSPWF for Turkey Day Cup `96 later today!! VJ: Happy Thanksgiving, pukes!! (Camera fades to black)
