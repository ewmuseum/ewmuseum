I am your host The Boss along with my cohost Mike Anderson. We got a great program for you tonight but first let me tell you that Chris The Scoop Adams is doing fine and will be conducting interviews tonight.
MA: Yeah and I understand that he has a few comments that he would like to address to Hardcore Jack.
TB: You mean Hardcore Jackass don't you? MA: Now TB what did Hardcore Jack ever do to you? He was just sending out a message to General Lee.
TB: Maybe so but Chris Adams is not a trained wrestler. He is a interviewer. MA: That is the risk he takes getting in the ring with them. TB: He wasn't in the ring with him. He was in a conference room. MA: Well I am glad that he got his ass kicked. He deserved it. Did you see how many wrestlers he ran off last week?

I suggest that you keep that mouth of yours shut. TB: I was just stating the facts......You are a jackass for doing what you did to Chris. HJ: You know I am in a good mood so I am going to ignore the fact that you just insulted me.
TB: Why are you here? HJ: I am here to say a few words if you don't mind. MA: No we don't mind at all! TB: Whatever just get it over with. HJ: Listen up General Lee I know that you saw me kick Chris Adams' ass last week and I know that you heard what I said about your dead dad. So on that note I am putting out a challenge to you.
TB: Your challenging General Lee? HJ: Yes that is what I am doing. I am challenging General Lee but not just any kind of match. No!!!!! I am challenging him to a bobwire bat on a pole match.
TB: A bobwire bat on a pole match.What is that? HJ: It is simply like it sounds. A bat with bobwire wrapped around it will be hanging from a pole in the corner and the first person to climb the pole and grab the bat and use it on the other wins the match.
TB: OK when is this match going to take place? And what do you have against General Lee anyways? He never did anything to you. HJ: Did you hear a word I said last week? His father #%$#$ me over when I was a little kid.
TB: Could you please watch your mouth. HJ: Oh you can call me a jackass but I can't come out here and talk my shit. Hell no that is not how it goes buddy.You see I am going to do what the hell I want,when I want and to whom I want and there is not a damn thing that you or anyone else in this damn fed can do about it.
TB: Is there anything else that you want to add? HJ: You are running on thin ice TB. One of these days I will show you why they call me hardcore but tonight I am going to let you slide. And as for you General Lee you name the time and place and I will bring the bat wrapped in bobwire and I will show you just how hardcore I can get.
[Hardcore Jack throws his headset off and leaves the set.] TB: What a jackass! MA: You better watch it TB. You saw what he did to Chris Adams last week. TB: Well let's forget about him for awhile.Let's go over the matches that have been signed in the debut of Tuesday Night Terror.
MA: What a night that is going to be. TB: Yes it is going to be one hell of a night. In our first match we are going to see All-American Boy face Bowa The Snake Man. I really don't know who to pick with this one Mike. Bowa is a coming off an injured knee from an organization up in Canada and All-American Boy has his mind on other outside interests.
MA: Are you blind? Bowa is going to rip All-American Boy in two. He is going to take that dumbass and show him what the snake man is all about. This match won't last too long. Bowa will most likely win this one in less then a minute or two.
TB: I doubt that.Let's go to the UICW gym where Bowa is standing by with Chris Adams. [scene takes you to the UICW gym where Chris Adams is about to interview Bowa The Snake Man.] CA: Bowa next week you will be facing All-American Boy. How do you plan on beating this veteran in the ring?
Bowa: Well first of all I am not a rookie anymore. Those days are over. You see I am here to win the gold and that is just what I plan on doing. You ask me how I am going to beat All-American Boy. Well I don't give my game plan away to noone. You see when I step in that ring All-American Boy will know.
CA: Know what? Bowa: The answer to your question you moran.No wonder Hardcore Jack beat your ass last week on the Weekly Wrapup show.
CA: I would rather not get into to that. Bowa: Fine by me. All-American Boy that is good that you are trying to help the kids but you better forget about all those brats when you step in the ring with me. You see when you step in that ring with the snake man you better be prepared or you will get bitten.
CA: Bowa last week I asked you if you were turning your back on the fans and you really didn't give me an answer. I was wondering if you could answer tonight.
Bowa: Look Chris I am only going to say this once and that is it. Up in Canada I did everything for the fans. I signed autographs. I went to charity events and everything else you can think of for the fans. Hell even when I was a Perfect Role Model I did things off and on for the fans. My question to you Chris. Is what did the fans ever do for me? Yeah they bought my merchandise but that didn't pay the bills. Did they win the TV Title for me?
NO!!!!!!! I won that title. Did they successfully defend that title for several months? HELL NO THEY DIDN'T.
CA: How can you say that about the fans Bowa. When you injured your knee you received several hundred letters from your fans wishing you well.
Bowa: Yeah and I guess that was good. I needed some toilet paper in the hospital. CA: That was uncalled for. Bowa: Look I don't need the fans. I don't need you. I don't need anyone. I just need to go out there and do what I am paid to do.
CA: And that is? Bowa: I get paid to wrestle and that is just what I am going to do. Come next Tuesday I am going to kick All-American Boy's ass.
CA: There you have it TB. Bowa looks to be confident about his match with All-American Boy next week. Back to you TB. [scene takes you back to TB and Mike Anderson] TB: What is wrong with Bowa? He had all the fans backing him now right off the bat he is turning his back on them.
MA: Look TB!!!!!!! Bowa is just doing what every wrestler wants to do. TB: And what might that be? MA: Tell all those snotnosed kids to stuff it. TB: Not every wrestler is like that. MA: Name one that isn't. TB: As a matter of a fact I can almost bet that All-American Boy doesn't feel that way and speaking of him let's take a look at some video footage that was taken this past weekend.

THE AMERICAN FLAG. [All-American Boy pauses for one moment] AAB: Men everyday put on the uniform and go to work. They work hard to ensure our safety. No one says thank you. [All-American Boy pauses for one moment again] AAB: You see Bowa this coming Tuesday on the debut of Tuesday Night Terror I am going to take you apart. Come next week you have the chance to prove it to me. See ya in the ring and fans remember to support your local charities.

MA: Bowa is going to kick his all-American teeth down his throat. TB: We will just have to wait and see next week on TNT. In our next match we are going to see Perfect Weapon take on General Lee. Champion. CA: It has been a pleasure interviewing you Chris and good luck with your match next week against Perfect Weapon. And I look forward to seeing you kick Hardcore Jack's ass in that ring.
