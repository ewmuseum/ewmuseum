UICW Weekly Wrapup Show

13 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ In the background you see several shots of the UICW Headquarters being built. The camera takes you through the front door. You go through a long hallway where you see several offices. Then you come to a door and the door flies open. Then the screen goes blank and all of a sudden the UICW logo comes charging right at you through the screen in 3D.]
**************************************************************************** ********* ULTIMATE INTERNET WRESTLING ORGANIZATION PRESENTS.......................... UICW WEEKLY WRAPUP SHOW LIVE FROM UICW STUDIOS ONLY ON THE WEIRD WIDE SPORTS NETWORK **************************************************************************** ********* [The scene opens up and you see two men sitting at a broadcasting table.] TB: Welcome everyone to the first ever UICW Weekly Wrapup Show. I am your host The Boss and along side me is my assistant Mike Anderson.
MA: Your assistant????? TB: Ok cohost. Is that better? MA: Yes!!!!!!!! TB: I can see right now that is going to be fun working with you. MA: What is that supposed to mean TB? TB: Just what I said.. Now on with the show...Ladies and Gentlemen we are only 2 weeks away from the debut of Tuesday Night Terror. And for the next hour we are going to take a look at several superstars that have already signed with the UICW...
MA: Can't wait until Terror....... TB: Me either Mike...Fans Mike and I will be hosting Tuesday Night Terror each and every week right here on the Weird Wide Sports Network......
MA:Also cant wait to get my first paycheck from here. TB: That is always nice..For those of you that don't know what the UICW is well let me explain it to you. The UICW stands for Ultimate Internet
Championship Wrestling which is based out of Indianapolis,IN. The UICW will host a weekly show known as Tuesday Night Terror each and every week right here on the Weird Wide Network....
MA: Yeah!!!!!!!!Yeah!!!!!!!!!!Yeah!!!!!!!!!! Let's get to the interviews already!!!!!! TB: Will you stop? Fans our very own Chris The Scoop Adams had a chance to sit down with some of the UICW superstar in the UICW this past Tuesday and here is what each had to say.
[scene takes you to a conference room where Chris The Scoop Adams is sitting down with Bowa The Snake Man.]
CA: Welcome everyone to my first interview in the UICW I am Chris The Scoop Adams and sitting next to me is Bowa The Snake Man. Now Bowa how do you feel about being in the UICW?
Bowa: How in the hell do you think I feel? I feel great!!!!!!!! My knee is at 100 percent and I am ready to get back in the ring and kick some american ass.
CA: Should be a change in lifestyle now that you are competing here in the states again. Bowa: It doesn't really matter whether I am in the United States, Canada or anywhere else my main goal is to be the best and kick some ass.
CA: You seem a little bit in a bad mood. Why is that? Bowa: Look I am sick of this stupid ass interview! I came here to the UICW to wrestler not to chitchat with a moran like you. So unless you have some intellegent questions to ask me then this interview is over.
CA: Bowa what is with this attitude of yours? Bowa: Look Chris for the first couple of years of my wrestling career up in Canada I was a joke. Yeah I won one TV Title but noone took me serious there. Everything I did in or out of the ring was for the fans. Even when I was a Perfect Role Model I didn't get the respect that I deserved. Well here in the UICW things are going to be different.
CA: How different? Bowa: Well for starters I could care less about the fans think about me. I could care less what you are anyone in the UICW cares about me. I am here for one person and one person only and that is the snake man. I don't care who the UICW front office wants to put me in the ring. It doesn't matter who it is. You see I am going to kick all their asses and win the gold here in the UICW.
CA: Which gold are you referring to? Bowa: Each and everyone of them. So if you don't mind I have to go to the gym and work out.
[Bowa gets up and walks out of the room.] CA: Well fans it looks as though there is a big change in Bowa. What does he have instore for the UICW? I guess we will just have to wait and see back to you TB.
[scene takes you back to The Boss and Mike Anderson] TB: What is Bowa's problem? MA: I didn't think that he had a problem.He is just stating the facts. He is tired of not getting the respect that he deserves in this sport and instead of crying about it like so many people do he is going to do something about it..
TB: I still think that he has a bad attitude.. MA: Whatever TB let's go back to Chris with his next interview. [Chris The Scoop Adams is sitting in a conference room next to a man decked out in black.] CA: Ladies and Gentlemen I am sitting here next to the son of the late General Buddy Lee Thompson ...... General Lee how are you doing? GL:Chris I am doing fine....I just want to thank everyone that showed up to the 1st annual General Buddy Lee Thompson Memorial Tournament.......
CA: General Lee I have to ask this but are you upset that All-American Boy won that tournament last week?
GL: What?????? Why in the hell would I be upset about that. All-American Boy and I have had our problems in the past but he won that tournament fair and square and that is that.
CA: Ok if you say so.General Lee what are your goals here in the UICW? GL: My goal is to be at least half the wrestler that my father was. CA: That is honorable. GL: Of course it is. I can't wait to step in that ring and win some matches. CA: What do you think will happen between you and All-American Boy now that you two are friends again.
GL: Wait a second there Chris! I never said that we were friends. I just said that we don't have a problem with each other anymore but if we were to go up against each other in the ring I would kick his ass like I have every other time that we have faced each other.
CA: OK WELL!!!!!! What do you think of the UICW so far? GL: Let's see here.....Does it really matter how I feel about this promotion? As long as I get a paycheck I will be fine..
CA: Don't you want to win some titles? GL: Yes but I am not going to kiss noone ass to get them. CA:So you are going to rely on your talent alone? GL: YOUR DAMN STRAIGHT I AM!!!!!!!!! CA: Ok if you say so. GL: What the hell did you mean by that? CA: I just meant that I don't think that you are going to get many title shots with that attitude.
GL: Just watch me in the ring and you will see. CA: Well General Lee it has been a pleasure speaking to you. I think! GL: I am out of here!!!!!!!! [General Lee gets up and storms out of the room.] CA: WHAT THE!!!!!!!!!! [scene takes you back to TB and Mike Anderson] MA: Great going Chris 2 for 2 on making the wrestlers mad. TB: What is with these wrestlers? MA: What do you mean? TB: Their attitudes! MA: Chris is asking them stupid questions. TB: Maybe so but they need to show a little respect. MA: So does Chris! TB: Fans a few days ago UICW office was sent a video from UICW superstar Trailor Park Trash Chris Jackson. Let's take a look at what he had to say. [Scene takes you to a porch on a broken down trailor where a man is sitting on the front step.]
Trailor: Well well well I see that someone is smart enough to give Trailor Park Trash a chance...You see I am going to kick everyone's ass in the UICW....And I am going to be the first ever World Heavyweight Champion. [Trailor Park Trash spits on the ground] Trailor: I mean look at the roster so far. You got a 500 lb fatass,a man that has something wrong with his lip and brags about it, a regect car from the Dukes of Hazard , a man that thinks that he is an all-american,a guy that calls himself a snake man, and a no talent wannabe karate kid regect..
I mean what do I have to worry about? [Trailor Park Trash spits on the ground again.] Trailor: When I do step in that ring I am going to show the world that this no good Trailor Park Trash is the best damn thing that has ever stepped in the squared circle. So get ready for a good ol Kentucky ass whippin. And I will see ya in the ring......
[scene fades out and then you go back to The Boss and Mike Anderson] TB: Well he had some nice things to say about the UICW superstars. MA: Yes he did but why would he want to live in a broken down trailor like that and show it on national tv?
TB: Maybe that is all he can afford. Did you ever think about that? MA: Maybe so but I wouldn't go around bragging about it. TB: You beat everything you know that! MA: Why thanks! TB: I didn't mean.....Oh nevermind! Let's go back to Chris and see who he is interviewing now.
[The scene shifts back to the locker room where we see Chris Adams standing with the Terminator.]
CA: Terminator what do you think about the competiton here in the UICW? Terminator: You see Chris its like this there is no one here who can touch the Terminator. You have 6 wannabes and 1 inbreeder from Kentucky who has never known what its like to live in a house without wheels.
CA: You dont think highly of Trailer Park Trash do you? Teminator:How can you respect somebody who cant even get a date outside of there own family.
CA: Just because he is from Kentucky doesn't mean that he dates his relatives. Terminator: Yes it does.And I got to go. Late for an interview with Entertainment Tonight. [Terminator gets up and walks out] Chris: Well there you have it folks the Terminators thought on the UICW competition. [scene takes you back to The Boss and Mike Anderson] MA: Can he keep anyone for the whole interview? TB: It is beginning to look like he can't. MA: What a moran! TB: Better watch what you say about him.He is dating the prez's step daughter. MA: So that is how he got this job. TB: He got it by his qualifications. MA: Yeah and I look like Hulk Hogan. TB: Well you are starting to go a little bald. MA: REAL FUNNY!!!!!!!! TB: I thought it was. Well fans this past weekend Chris The Scoop Adams had a chance to catch up with Big Blob at an all you can it restuarant. Let's check this footage out.
[scene takes you to a restuarant where Big Blob is sitting down at a table with tons of food in front of him. Chris Adams sits next to him and begins talking.]
CA: Big Blob could I get a few words in with you? BB: Make it quick! I am trying to eat here. CA: I looks like you have enough food here to feed a family of 4. BB: Oh this! It is just something to get me by until my steak dinner gets here. CA: WHAT!!!!!!!!! Big Blob don't you think that you are eating just a little too much! BB: NO! I think that I am eating just right. I am doing the one thing that I love to do and that is eat food.
CA: Well Big Blob I would like to talk to you a little bit about the UICW if you don't mind.
BB: Sure go right ahead but remember make it quick because me still hungry. CA: Big Blob you have signed with the UICW to wrestle. What are your thoughts on your new career here in the UICW?
BB: My thoughts? What do you mean? CA: I mean what do you think about wrestling in the UICW? BB: I am going to love it. I get paid and with that money I can buy more food. CA:Big Blob with all do respect don't you think that you should think about slowing done a little and get in shape.
BB: For what? CA: To wrestle. That is what you are getting paid to do you know? BB: I can wrestle just fine Chris. I use my size to my advantage. CA: Yeah but the rate you are going you are going to die at an early age. BB: Well in that case I hope that I die eating. CA: Are we going to talk about wrestling or you just care about eating. BB: I care about eating and wrestling. Say do they have any good restuarants in Indiana. I sure would like to visit them sometime.
CA: Oh brother!!!!! Big Blob I wish you the best of luck in the UICW. Thank you for your time.
[With a mouth full Big Blob answers] BB: DANT MENTION IT!!!!! [scene takes you back to the UICW studios] MA: MAN THAT GUY IS HUGE!!!!!!!! TB: Yes he is Mike. MA: Hey at least he didn't walk out on Chris like the others have so far. TB: Yeah but the question is can he walk! MA: Yeah he can walk.I saw him walk to the salad bar. TB: You crack me up! MA:WHAT! TB: Nothing. Let's just go to the video footage that we received last night shall we? MA: Why not! [scene takes you to an alley where All-American Boy is standing with a group of kids] AAB: The UICW has ask me to speak about myself for promotional reason. Well I have a better ideal.You see these kids around me need our helps. They are into gangs,drugs and whatever else illegal. These kids don't need to be doing this.They need our love and understanding.They need us to show them that what they are doing is wrong.
[All-American Boy points to one particular kid.] AAB: You see this kid.His parents died last year in a horrible accident and now he is running with gangs. He has no family. Of course he thinks the gangs are his family but he is wrong. He needs our help. He needs us to help him get off the streets............WHAT THE????????
[All of a sudden two thugs hit All-American Boy from behind and the screen fades out] [scene takes you back to The Boss and Mike Anderson] TB: Did you just see what I saw Mike? MA: I sure did! Serves him right for sticking his nose in business that doesn't involve him.
TB: He is just trying to help out the kids. MA: Yeah and there is nothing wrong with that but this is wrestling and he should do that kind of stuff on his own time not ours.
TB: You are just cold hearted. MA: Whatever I am just telling the truth. TB: So am I. Fan's lets go to the conference room once again where Chris The Scoop Adams is standing by with Perfect Weapon. MS: I just got word that Triple L and his wife are in the locker room and are wanting to speak their mind.
TB: Ok let's go to them. [scene takes you to the lockerroom area where Triple L Libber Lipped Lenny and Jenny Anderson are standing.]
LLL: WELL IT IS ABOUT DAMN TIME YOU MORANS!!!!!!!!!!!I have been waiting all night to be heard. I want each and everyone to listen up and listen good, whether it be the people in the front office, the broadcast staff or the wrestlers I am going to kick each and everyone of your asses if you get in my way of making my dream come true. And that dream is to become the World
Heavyweight Champion. Yeah I have held gold in the past but never have I held the World Heavyweight Title and I am going to do whatever it takes to achieve that goal.
JA: Yeah and don't just think that I am a woman and want do much damage. I can hold my own and if any managers or wrestlers get out of line during matches with my Lenny I will make that China in that other promotion look like Marge Simpson. So UICW get ready for a war.
LLL: A war that is never going to end until we reach the top of the UICW and are crowned the World Heavyweight Champion. You see I have had it up to here with everyone getting the glory. It is my turn to shine and that is just what I plan on doing. You see I have had to put up with morans in other feds thinking that they are all bad and not showing me any respect well I got news for you if you don't respect me then you will get your ass kicked by me when you face me in that square circle.
JA: Triple L why don't we go lock the doors and do what we do best..... LLL: Sure thing babe. Hold on one second. Come Tuesday January 25th ,2000 I am going to show the UICW and the whole world for that matter that Triple L
Libber Lipped Lenny is the best thing that has ever happened to wrestling and there isn't much that you can do about it. Now Jenny let's get our groove on if ya know what I mean.
JA: I sure do stud! [the door closes and the scene goes back to The Boss and Mike Anderson] MA: WOW what a woman! TB: Control yourself Mike! She is taken. MA: I know Triple L is one lucky dude. TB: That he is. I have seen Triple L wrestle before in another promotion and he means business.
MA: I am sure that he does. TB: I understand that Chris The Scoop Adams is in the conference room ready to do an interview with Perfect Weapon. Let's go there now......
[scene takes you to the conference room where Chris The Scoop Adams is sitting with Perfect Weapon]
CA: Perfect Weapon how are you doing? It is a pleasure to have you on the show here tonight.
[Chris shakes Perfect Weapon's hand] PW: I am doing fine. Thanks for asking. CA: You know Perfect Weapon that in less then 2 weeks you will be debuting in the UICW. PW: Yes and I can't wait. Who am I facing anyways? CA: I am not sure who you will be facing. From what I am hearing the card will be made out next week on the Countdown to Tuesday Night Terror Show.
PW: Like it matters. I am going to win anyways. CA: You sound very sure that you can win. Why is that? PW: You see I have been in the martial arts for over 20 years now and I have only been showed up a few times.
CA: That is very impressive but this is wrestling not karate. This is a whole different ballgame.
PW: Why do you say that? I mean the object is to beat up your opponont. Isn't it? CA: Yes but there is more to it then that. PW: I know I was just messing with ya. I have all the respect in the world for these guys. They are great athletes.
CA: Hey I wish you all the luck in the world Perfect Weapon. PW: Thanks. CA: Do you have any thoughts on the UICW ? PW: Yes I am really looking forward to competing in the UICW. I can't wait to get in that ring and perform for the fans.
CA: Well that time is almost here. I look forward to seeing ya in the ring Perfect Weapon. [Both men shake hands and Perfect Weapon walk out] TB: Perfect Weapon seems like a pretty nice guy to me. MA: Looks like a wimp to me. TB: Why is that? MA: He thinks that he can win just because he has 20 years experience in karate. Big deal. He doesn't look all that tough.
TB: I beg to differ. I think that he will go far here in the UICW. MA: Yeah right!!!!!!! TB: Well we will just have to wait and see. Fans it looks like we have just one more interview for you tonight. I understand that Chris is ready for us to go to him for his last interview.
[scene takes you to the conference room where Chris The Scoop Adams is sitting next to Hardcore Jack]
CA: Welcome to the show Hardcore Jack. HJ: Listen you pathetic piece of shit. The only reason why I am here doing your stupid interview is because the front office is paying me to do so. So
I suggest you sit there and let me speak. CA: But........ HJ: Look here is what I want you to do. I want you to ask me what I feel about each wrestler that has signed a contract with the UICW and I will speak my mind. You think that you can handle that?
CA: SURE!!!!!! HJ: Well start you moran! CA: Ok what do you think about All-American Boy? HJ: That piece of shit couldn't lace my boots. He is a no talent bum. All he cares about is saving the world. Buddy Lee should of left him in the streets where he belonged.
CA: What do you think about Bowa The Snake Man? HJ: Bowa! What a joke.This guy is nothing but a ripoff charactor of the 80 version of WWF's own Jake The Snake Roberts. This guy needs to go back to the store and by him a real gimmick.
CA: What do you think about Perfect Weapon? HJ: I am going to kill two birds with one stone. You see Perfect Weapon and Terminator are nothing but karate rejects. Yeah they are good at what they do but I hate to break the news to ya fellows but this is wrestling and karate just aint going to cut it around here. And Terminator why don't you get a ride with Bowa and go buy you a original gimmick as well.
CA: What do you think about Big Blob? HJ: That fatass. He is a slow piece of shit and if he ever steps in the ring with me I will knock a few hundred pounds off of his fatass. Big Blob you are pathetic. You need to go see Jenny Criag.
CA: What do you think about Trailor Park Trash Chris Jackson? HJ: First of all if you live in a trailor you don't go around bragging about it. Dude you need to get out and make some real cash you loser! Take your inbreeding ass out of that couch and get a job.....
CA: What do you think about General Lee? HJ General Lee! I will tell ya what I think about General Lee.......You see when I was just a little boy I loved to watch General Buddy Lee Thompson wrestle. He came to my home town of Indianapolis one time and I saved up my lunch money for weeks to buy a ticket. When I got their I saw him signing autographs. There was a big line and I had to wait a long time. Just when it was my turn to get an autograph that bastard said he had to go and I never got my autograph. Well I will tell you that really crushed me. I went home that night and threw away everything I had of him.
CA: I am sorry to hear that! But I don't think that Buddy meant to hurt you. HJ: Well General Lee I told myself that one day I would become a wrestler and kick his ass.I am sorry to say that the old bastard died before I had a chance to. So General Lee I am going to make it my goal here in the UICW to make you pay for what your dad did to me and just to give you a little example of what I am going to do.
[Hardcore Jack gets up and picks up his chair and nails Chris Adams over the head with it. He starts stomping away on the fallen Adams. He picks Chris up throws him up on the table and nails him through the table with the
Rulebreaker!!!!!!!!!!! [screen fades out] TB: OH MY GOD!!!!!!!!SOMEONE CALL 911!!!!!!!!!! MA: WELL IT"S ABOUT TIME SOMEONE DID SOMETHING TO THAT DUMBASS!!!!!!! TB: How can you say that Mike. Chris is not a wrestler. It wasn't his fault that Hardcore Jack never got an autograph from General Lee's father.
MA: No but Hardcore Jack was just sending out a message to General Lee. Thats all! TB: THAT'S ALL!!!!!!! He could of killed him. MA: I am sure that Chris will be fine. He is a big boy. He can handle it. TB: I hope Chris is going to be alright! We will be praying for him. MA: You can but I am not going to. TB: You can be down right mean sometimes. You know that? MA: I try to be. TB: Well fans I hope you enjoyed the show tonight. We will be back next week with the Countdown to Tuesday Night Terror Show right here on the Weird Wide
Sports Network. We will have more interviews and a preview of what we will see on the debut to TNT. I am TB and along with my cohost Mike Anderson we wish you a good night!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
