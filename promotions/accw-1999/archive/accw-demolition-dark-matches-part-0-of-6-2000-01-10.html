ACCW Demolition, Dark Matches (Part 0 of 6)

10 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@ @ DARK MATCHES @ @@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@ **************************************************** "Sensational" Christian Hardcastle -vs- EdgeCrusher **************************************************** The evening's matches opened with this battle of light heavies, both of whom put on a lightning quick display in the early minutes. Both men exchanged a series of moves filled with high-flying and eye-popping manuevers. Hardcastle grabbed an early edge after a devastating springboard DDT. He made good of the opportunity, delivering some punishment to EdgeCrusher, while coming within inches of winning the match on several occasions. However, a miscalculated moonsault left an opening for EdgeCrusher, who got a second wind and capatilized with a spinebuster/legdrop combination. Hardcastle, though, reversed a powerbomb attempt into a hurricarana and followed with a dropkick that sent EdgeCrusher sprawling. However, Hardcastle spent too much time jawing with fans, allowing EdgeCrusher to regain his composure and drop
Hardcastle with a devastating T-Bone suplex. Much to the surprise of everyone,
EdgeCrusher quickly applied the Kata Hajime, prompting Hardcastle to finally submit after hanging on as long as he could.
********************* WINNER: EdgeCrusher VIA: Submission TIME OF MATCH: 5:38 ********************* *************************************************** The Football Hooligans -vs- The Masked Meanies *************************************************** The Hooligans "returned" to ACCW with a vengeance, wasting no time in coming to the ring and attacking the Meanies before the bell. After depositing Meanie #2 over the top rope, Dazza and Smithy performed some gorgeous double-team maneuevers on Meanie #1. Dazza eventually stayed alone with Meanie #1, and tore into him with a barrage of martial arts moves. Dazza wnt to the top and connected with a diving headbutt, getting a two count. Smithy took the tag and overwhelmed Meanie #1 with power moves. At one point, Smithy had Meanie #1 up in a gorilla press, but Meanie #2 ran in and dropkicked Smithy, causing him to drop Meanie #1 and prompting Dazza to hit the ring. A four way brawl ensued, with the Hooligans gaining the upper hand. After a Smithy powerbomb on Meanie
#2 and a Dazza DDT on #1, Dazza went to the top and hit the London Legdrop on #1, while Smithy tossed #2 out of the ring. Dazza made the cover and got the win for the Hooligans, who continue to impress as they move up the tag ranks. ********************* WINNER: The Football Hooligans VIA: Pinfall TIME OF MATCH: 4:29 ********************* *************************************************** Shane Larson -vs- "The Icehouse" Lance Ozburn *************************************************** Ozburn was rumored to be saying in the back that he would get his first win over Larson, but Larson had other ideas. After a furious exchange of moves to open the match, Larson gained the upper hand with a beautifully executed tornado DDT. Larson went right to work on Ozburn's neck, hitting him with a vicious brainbuster from the second rope and getting a near fall there.
Ozburn, though, soon came back after a low blow and a piledriver. Ozburn mounted some offense of punches, but missed a splash from the top, giving
Larson the opening he needed. After a Samoan Drop, Larson connected with a swinging neckbreaker followed by a Spike DDT, his finisher, to get the win.
********************* WINNER: Shane Larson VIA: Pinfall TIME OF MATCH: 5:03 ********************* *************************************************** HellFire -vs- The Highway Patrol *************************************************** After losing at Inaugural Brawl, Hellfire had something to prove, and they came out on fire (pardon the pun), against the ever-hapless Highway Patrol. Negas started off against Anderson and took a quick advantage after a series of quick high-flying moves, the most impressive of which was a hard missle dropkick that resulted in a near fall. Anderson got in a rake to the eyes, and began pounding on Negas with hard fists. Anderson hit a spinebuster and got a two count, but went to the well one too many times, as Negas countered his next attempt with a swinging DDT. Negas then tagged out to Brown, who immediately floored Anderson with a clothesline. Willis decided to get involved, and got floored as well. Brown cleared the ring of Willis by tossing him over the top and through the ringside table, but got caught with a kick and a swinging neckbreaker when he turned back to Anderson. Brown quickly reversed an irish whip attempt and leveled him with a belly-to-belly suplex. A tag to Negas set up the finisher, as Brown dropped Anderson with a powerbomb, quickly followed by a Negas guillotine legdrop, The Incineration, for the win.
********************* WINNERS: Hellfire VIA: Pinfall TIME OF MATCH: 5:48 ********************* *************************************************** "Double V" Vic Valiant -vs- Mr. White *************************************************** Two newcomers making their ACCW debuts appeared in this match, and did not disappoint. Valiant took an early advantage, dazzling the crowd with a barrage of high-flying moves coupled with mat-wrestling. Valiant scored a near fall after a double-underhook suplex, but Mr. White's manager, Mr. Black, tripped
Valiant up on the follow-up, allowing Mr. White to gain an edge. White took the fight to Valiant, landing a number of impressive moves himself, including a tornado DDT which almost won him the match. A missed moonsault gave Valiant another chance, and he didn't let it go, following it up with a released German suplex and a superkick that about took White's head off. Valiant quickly went for the pin, but Mr. Black pulled the official out of the ring, prompting Shane
Potter to call for the DQ. After the match, White tried to attack Valiant, but
Valiant saw it coming and tossed White to the floor.
********************* WINNER: Vic Valiant VIA: DQ TIME OF MATCH: 4:56 ********************* *************************************************** James Holloway -vs- "Showtime" Cyrus Bennett *************************************************** Two more newcomers did battle in the final match before the airing of Demolition. With both men wanting to make a good impression, this match could have easily been on the air quality. Bennett and Holloway had a furious exchange in the first two minutes of the match, with both men getting near falls. Holloway took a quick advantage after a spinning back suplex, and followed with some pretty vicious kicks to Bennett's ribs. Bennett came roaring back after reversing a hurricarana into a sitting power bomb, getting a near three count. Bennett went on the offensive, again getting near falls after a snap DDT and a running neck breaker. Holloway's intensity showed, though, as he mounted a comeback. Holloway countered a crucifix attempt with a
Samoan Drop followed by a snap suplex and flying fistdrop, which got another near fall. Holloway then began stomping away on Bennett, but got a little too pushy when referee John Barnett called for the break. This gave Bennett an opening, and he capatilized with a running kneelift followed by a bulldog and near fall. Bennett brought Holloway back up, but Holloway caught him with a thumb to the eye. With bennett looking for his bearings, Holloway planted him with a DDT. Holloway lifted him back up, but Bennett from out of nowhere executed a small package, getting the 3 count and the win. After the match, the fans gave both men a rosuing ovation for their work.
********************* WINNER: Cyrus Bennett VIA: Pinfall TIME OF MATCH: 10:26 *********************
