ACCW Demolition (Part 4 of 6)

10 January 2000

[Screen opnes on the annoucners] BS: Well that was a crappy ending to the Lightweight title match before the break. RA: I thought you hated the whole match. BS: I did. But I bet I like this next match. RA: You probably will. Next up we've got a battle that took shape back at
Inaugural Brawl. After Rob Trainor claimed to be the "mystery attacker" and lost to Jay Moore, three men showed up and laid waste to both Trainor and
Moore, and it turns out that Mexx along with Darius and Apollo were responsible for the attacks all along.
BS: Darn straight. Hey, you'd better not get on their bad side, Robert. They may lay you out too.
[The camera cuts to Dave Stewart, in the ring ready to make the intros.] DS: This contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, from the depths of the Brazilian jungles, he is accompanied by his manager Darius and bodyguard
Apollo, weighing in at 287 pounds, here is.....
M E X X !!!!!! [Jungle noises fill the air as the entire arena turns light green. As soon as the jungle drums start to play and the green flares shoot out from the aisle, As Mexx explodes out of the entranceway wearing his traditional light green Capoeira battle attire, green flares shoot upward at each Mexx continues to dance to the rhythm of the jungle drums and his dreadlocks sway back and forth. The crowds chant of the words MEXX OUT echoes througout the stadium. Mexx stops and stares at the ring quarterway from the aisle barking at the fans and out comes Darius wearing his usual armani suit looking rather casual, yet the look on his face is very defined with determination.
Following Darius is a man standing 7'0 tall and a massive build who answers to the name Apollo, Darius' bodyguard. In Apollo's hands is the very chair that provided the explosion to "Tiger" Jay Moore's head. Mexx sprints to the ring, climbs in and throws the outer part of his ring attire out of the ring now wearing his dark green pants. Green strobe lights flicker throught the arena while begins a Capoeira style routine showing his agility and experience. The lights flicker back on and Mexx climbs onto the corner and screams while beating his chest.]
[As Darius climbs through the ropes, he quickly asks for the mic. Once recieved he walks to the middle of the ring and begins to speak while Apollo stands holding the symbolic chair beside him. Mexx leaps onto the nearest cornerpost to sit and watch the entranceway.]
Darius: .....SUNDAY....BLOODY SUNDAY.... WHAT WORDS to describe such a Brutal outcome of what took place during the much Anticipated, yet ever Sooo
Surprising DEBUT of THE TREACHEROUS THREE. I Do hope Our message has become crystal Clear to all of you, if not....lets role the incredible footage shall we?? .....
(On the screen above, a scene from Inaugral Brawl display the end "Tiger" Jay
Moore and Rob "Shootmaster" Trainor match.)
RA: Jay Moore has defeated Rob Trainor! What in the Hell?!?!? [A large seven foot man enters the ring an takes Jay Moore out with a chair shot to the head. The Blood just seems to explode from Moore's head on impact. He then tosses the referee out of the ring and continues to hit both Trainor and Moore with chair shots as he waves someone in from the back.]
(With each chair shot, the fans cringe to the sight of the each impact. As the video ends, Darius chuckles with delight and begins to speak) Darius: As you can as all see here....WE SHOW NO MERCY....We Fight When WE
MUST...We don't waste OUR TIME...WE want what is ours and that is RESPECT!!!!
WE THRIVE on RESPECT and when it is not given, WE will do anything IN OUR POWER
TO ACHIEVE IT...EVEN If WE NEED TO ATTACK EVERY WRESTLER in this
FEDERATION....Then ATTACK WE WILL DO.... (The crowd expresses mixed reactions with both boos and cheers) AS FOR the FORMER PRESIDENT...MR. William Wright... (The crowd pops slightly) AS you sit at home, considering your retirement, You will remember that very day that putyou where you are...And you will remember us...TOTAL DOMINATION is
OURS for the taking...we shall STORM through All COMPETITORS...And together, as a COHESIVE UNIT...WE ARE AN INSEPARABLE FORCE....WE WORK AS ONE!!!
[A new voice comes over the PA.] Voice: Are you though yet? [The crowd stirs in anticipation of who that might be, and seconds later are given the answer, as returning ACCW Commissioner WILLIAM WRIGHT emerges from the back and walks onto the ramp.]
RA: There he is! He's back! [The crowd is cheering at his reappearance. He looks quite the same as he did before, except maybe a little lighter in weight. He wears a dark navy jacket and olive green shirt, supplemented by dark navy pants. Holding a microphone in hand, he walks toward the area where the ramp descends to the ring.]
WW: First of all, I don't know where you THOUGHT I'd be tonight, but unfortunately for you three, I'm right where I belong...at ACCW Demolition!
[Crowd pops] WW: You see, two months ago, when you three jumped me and left me a bloody mess, on the night that I should have been CELEBRATING...that night, you three wrote a check that you're NOT going to be able to cash.
[Wright starts walking down toward the ring, much to the surprise of most in the Richmond Coliseum.]
WW: I don't know what you three expected. Did you expect me to just quit, to just pack my bags and go home? If you did, then you three are dumber than you look. You see, now I am BACK, and it's YOUR time to pay the piper.
[Wright reaches the ring, climbing the steps and stepping between the ropes. As he enters the ring, Apollo makes a move toward hi, but Wright holds up his hand in a "stop" motion, prompting Apollo to do just that.]
WW: Whoa, hold up there, big man. I wouldn't be smacking me around if I were you. See, I'M back in the driver's seat now, which means that you three get the priviledge of answering to ME. And seeing as how you treated me so bad the first time we met, I think I owe it to myself, ACCW, and all these fans to return the favor!
[Big crowd pop. Darius is getting noticeably upset, perhaps even worried. Mexx is trying to get at Wright, but Apollo holds him back.] WW: Tell you what, since Mexx there is so anxious to fight, why don't we see how well he does on his own. Darius, Apollo...for this match, both of you are
BANNED from ringside!
[Big crowd pop.] WW: And to make it more interesting, if either of you get within 50 feet of this ring, Mexx will be disqualified on the spot. So why don't you two go back to the back, and let's see how good your guy really is.
[The crowd gives a huge pop at this, while Darius is yelling something at
Wright. Wright tosses the microphone down and steps out of the ring, heading toward the commentators booth. Reluctantly, Apollo and Darius climb out of the ring and head to the back, jawing with jeering fans the whole way out.]
DS: And his opponent... R O B "T H E S H O O T M A S T E R" T R A I N O R!!! [The Shootmaster enters to smoke and a large amount of pyro. His music "So
What" by Metallica starts as he is coming down the ramp. He is walking to the ring and breaks a kendo stick over his head. He reaches the ring, flips up the apron and grabs a roll of barbed wire from underneath and begin to wrap his fists in it. He drops the roll at ringside and gives himself a couple jabs to the head with his fists.]
BS: I'm not sure that guy's all there. RA: Maybe not, but he sure seems to be one tough customer, and he's got a score to settle with Mexx tonight.
BS: Ah, Mexx will wipe the mat with this rookie. [DING! DING! DING!] RA: There's the bell, and these guys waste no time at all, having a good old fashioned slugout in the middle of the ring! And Sam Espizito is letting it go! Trainor is getting the better of this exchange, and well he should as he has a height and weight advantage. Trainor now shoves Mexx back into the ropes, there's a big knife-edge chop!
[CROWD: WHOOOOO!] BS: Sends him for the ride, goes for a clothesline but Mexx ducks underneath. Trainor turns around and gets a knee in the gut for his troubles. Mexx hooks him, and plants him with a nicely executed gourdbuster.
RA: Nice move there, but instead of following up, Mexx is looking for Darius at ringside. But Commissioner Wright already took care of that.
BS: Yeah, the cheater. RA: How is that cheating? BS: It just is. RA: Mexx finally goes back over to Trainor, who is on one knee, and greets Mexx with a palm blow to the stomach. Now a stiff kneeling uppercut sends Mexx reeling, Trainor now to a vertical base going right on the attack. Shoves Mexx into a corner and just unleashed a series of boots to the mid-section.
BS: BANG! And follows it up with a HARD roundhouse kick that caught Mexx right upside the temple! Mexx is stunned by that one.
RA: Trainor with an armwringer and an irish whip, no, he holds onto the arm and whips Mexx back into the near corner. And Mexx hit the turnbuckles with some impact there. Trainor now going to work with some STIFF kicks to Mexx's ribs!
BS: There's that shootfighting background of Trainor's coming into play. RA: Good point. Trainor now with a pair of forearms to the side of Mexx's head. Whips him out of the corner to the far side, and Mexx hits hard!
Trainor waits and locks Mexx in a front waistlock, and plants him with a nice spinebuster! BS: Rob had a lot of power behind that move. Brings Mexx back to his feet, hooks him, and delivers a snap suplex! He's really giving Mexx a shi....
RA: Hey, don't get censored. Trainor still on the attack, brings Mexx to one knee, and OUCH! A vicious knee right to the bridge of Mexx's nose, he very well may have broken it with that blow. Shoves him into a corner, and here we go again with the barrage of kicks!
BS: Well, Trainor's a big boy, and he ain't exactly Mr. Nice Guy there. I like that attitude.
RA: You would. Trainor now locks on a side headlock and pulls Mexx out of the corner to the center of the ring. Trainor really ringing that hold in tight,
Mexx looks for an escape. Mexx sideteps and grabs Trainor in a waistlock, lifts him up and drops him with a modified backdrop driver!
BS: But Trainor still held onto the headlock. RA: That he did. Mexx quickly back to one knee, Trainor still wrenching on that headlock. Mexx with a pair of elbows to the midsection forces Trainor to break the hold. Mexx hits the far ropes and charges at Trainor...
BS: WHOA! Mexx just dropped Trainor with a shoulder block! RA: Trainor has almost 50 pounds on Mexx, but Mexx really packed some impact in that blow. Trainor back on his feet, and is met with a kick to the ribs. Big right hand from Mexx to follow up! That stuns Trainor somewhat, allowing Mexx to move in and send him for the ride. Trainor off the ropes, Mexx catches
him...tilt-a-whirl backbreaker! BS: Did you see how much speed Mexx took him around with? Oh wait, you're too old. You can't see that fast.
RA: What does age have to do with seeing fast? BS: I don't have time to teach you. RA: Trainor is down, that had to hurt. Mexx straight back on the attack, now stomps away at the head of Trainor. Helps him back up, there's a scoop and a slam. Mexx off the ropes, here comes an elbowdrop...
BS: NO! Trainor rolls out of the way and Mexx hits nothing but canvas! Trainor waiting for him to get back up and WHOA! Rob just about took Mexx's head off with that clothesline! Now THAT was stiff!
RA: Vicious move by the Shootmaster! There's our first cover of the match! ESPIZITO: ONE.................TWO.... RA: And Mexx kicks out at two. I think that was a rather quick count there. BS: See, I told you you couldn't see fast. RA: Trainor brings Mexx to his feet, and welcomes his back up with a poke to the eyes. Steps to the side and hooks him, side Russian legsweep! And look at this, Trainor is going for a figure-four!
BS: He's got the leg up and wraps it... RA: NO! Inside cradle counter by Mexx! ESPIZITO: ONE.................TWO............... BS: Trainor kicks out at the last second! I think that caught him off guard, and I think it may have pissed him off.
RA: Why do you say that? BS: Well, the fact that he's punching Mexx's lights out has something to do with it. RA: Good point. Trainor has Mexx back into a corner and is just slugging away with stiff right hands. Mexx is stunned, and Trainor goes to the far side.
Now comes running in, but Mexx gets the boot up! That caught Trainor right in the mush! Trainor turns his back and puts his hand to his mouth, I guess checking to make sure all his teeth are there.
BS: Mexx has hopped to the top rope, waiting...Trainor turns around and gets
FLOORED by a missle dropkick! Cover by Mexx!
ESPIZITO: ONE.................TWO.................THR RA: NO! Trainor just barely escaped there! But a great move by Mexx has the big guy on the ropes. Mexx goes right back to the attack, brings Trainor to his feet. Trainor still stunned, Mexx sizing him up, ENZUGIRI! Trainor is out! Cover!
ESPIZITO: ONE..................TWO.................THRE BS: I don't believe it! Trainor kicked out again! This guy is double-tough! RA: I'm duly impressed. Mexx waiting on Trainor to get to his feet, Rob's back up, here comes Mexx...
BS: OH MY LORD! RA: Mexx with a cross body block that just took BOTH men tumbling over the top rope to the floor! Trainor's head got cracked on the floor, and Mexx is favoring his leg. That was flat-out suicide.
BS: Mexx also is up, and Trainor's not. Mexx grabs a chair, time for a little heavy metal. Trainor slowly to his feet, and Mexx sizing him up and takes his cut...
RA: BUT TRAINOR COUNTERS! He just landed a thrust kick that hit the chair and snapped it right back into Mexx's head! Mexx dropped like he was shot, and I think he's busted open.
BS: Yes! Blood! Who-hoo! RA: Will you stop that? Trainor getting his second wind, now brings Mexx up and slams him head into the guardrail! Mexx is on wobbly legs, and Trainor grabs the chair, what's he doing? Hops back onto the apron and climbs the buckles. Trainor leaps off with chair in hand...
BS: BUT HE MISSED! Mexx moves and the chair hits the guardrail! That chair is almost broken in half. Trainor turns around right into a right cross from
Mexx! And another! Trainor is stunned, and now Mexx gets the chair again...
RA: And smashes it over Trainor's head! Trainor's legs are wobbling, Mexx goes back and gets a running start, and smashes the chair on his melon again! And now Trainor's bleeding!
BS: Aw, man, this is great! If you want blood, you got it! RA: Mexx grabs Trainor by the neck, OH MY! He just hopped the railing into the crowd while holding on, and dropped Trainor throat-first over the railing! He could have busted Trainor's larnyx!
BS: What are you, a doctor of some kind? RA: Actually... BS: You're a doctor of stupidity. RA: Mexx grabs Trainor and rolls him back into the ring, and Trainor is in trouble, too, as Mexx has taken firm control in this matchup. Mexx ascends to the top rope, this could be all she wrote for Trainor.
BS: You mean that show with that old chick? RA: No. Mexx leaps off, here comes a diving headbutt... BS: NO! Trainor rolls out of the way! Mexx's head just got cracked on the mat! Both men are down, and Espizito has no choice but to start the ten count!
ESPIZITO: ONE......TWO.......THREE......FOUR.......FIVE.....SIX RA: Mexx is stirring! ESPIZITO: SEVEN......EIGHT..... BS: Mexx is on his feet! Goes after Trainor, but Trainor was ready and took
Mexx down with a double-leg, well, takedown.
RA: And Trainor is going for the Snapshooter! But Mexx is fighting it! Trainor almost has the legs in position... BS: NO! Mexx leaned forward and gouged Trainor right in the eyes! Trainor drops the legs, he's been blinded. Mexx is back up, there's a stiff kick to the guts of Trainor! Mexx hooks on a inverted front facelock...
RA: THE MEXXECUTOR! There's that impaler DDT! This could be all! ESPIZITO: ONE..................TWO...................THREE! BS: Foot on the rope! Trainor's foot was on the rope! Espizito is waving it off! The match is going to continue.
RA: But Mexx is in total control now. Brings Trainor back to his feet and pushes him into the corner, locks on a 3/4 rear facelock and gets a running start...DIAMOND CUTTER!
BS: That's his set-up for Mexx Out! RA: Indeed it is. Mexx is going up top, Trainor is out cold. Here it is... BS: And he connects! Somersault legdrop from the top! MEXX OUT! RA: This match is over, wait! He's not going for the pin. Instead, Mexx drags him to the middle of the ring, that's why! He just slapped on the Mexx Out
Dreadlock!
BS: Geez, how many 'finishers' does this guy have? RA: He's got it on, and I think Trainor is losing consciousness. One thing about Trainor, you know he won't give up. Espizito is checking Trainor, he's signalling for the bell!
[DING! DING! DING!] DS: The winner of the match.....MEXX! RA: Mexx gets a big win here tonight, and I think he's making a point here, because he refuses to let go of the Dreadlock! Espizito is, well, kind of trying to get Mexx off. And here come a slew of officials to try the same thing, although Sam has given up and gone to the back. Mexx still won't let go, and Trainor has been out for some time now.
BS: Wait, here come Darius and Apollo. RA: I guess it's OK for them to be out here, since the match is over. Darius is yelling something to Mexx, and Mexx finally lets go. Thank goodness they came out here!
BS: Or not. Apollo just got in the ring and cracked Trainor with a chair. RA: That's just ridiculous, three-on-one like that. They're finally leaving the ring, and all three men holds their arms up in victory. This really ended up backfiring on Wright.
BS: Yeah, he's a regular Einstein. Mexx is a top athlete in ACCW, just because
Darius and Apollo aren't at ringside doesn't mean this guy can't win.
RA: We've got to take a break, when we come back, Fuse defends the ACCW Television Title against "Tiger" Jay Moore! BS: Oh that's just great! [Commercial break]
