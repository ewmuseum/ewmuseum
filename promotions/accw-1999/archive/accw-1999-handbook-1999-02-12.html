What is ACCW? 1.1 Kayfabe ACCW stands for Atlantic Coast Championship Wrestling and is based on the Atlantic Coast of the United States of America. It's headquarters are Dunn Towers located in Atlantic City, New Jersey.
The organication is owned by Dunn Enterprises which is owned by Calvin Dunn. It provides hot wrestling action two nights a week on the Dunn Broadcast Network.
1.2 Non-kayfabe version ACCW is a fantasy wrestling league. Those who wish to participate in ACCW create wrestlers and roleplay with these wrestlers. They follow their wrestles story in ACCW by reading the "live" card which is sent out each week. The ACCW is based on depth of angles, creativity of roleplays, and strats from the ACCW handlers.
2. Who runs ACCW? [name withheld] is the man behind ACCW. He created it and he has the final say and he has run many feds before. He has a lot of experience and is quite creative, so ACCW should be a fun experience.
However, the real people who run ACCW are the handlers. They're the ones who help write matches, strat, create storylines, and make
ACCW a fun experience. 3. What shows does ACCW run? 3.1 Pure Excitement When: Saturday nights Hosted by: Max Money and Jason Keller Where: Dunn Sports Complex located on the boardwalk in Atlantic City, New Jersey What: Pure Excitement is ACCW's premier card everyweek. As ACCW grows, so will the action of Pure Excitement. It will start off as a one hour 4-5 match show then possibly grow to a two hour 7-8 match card.
3.2 ACCW Update When: Thursday nights Hosted by: Max Money and Jason Keller Where: ACCW Studios in Dunn Towers What: A preview of the upcoming Pure Excitement along with the flashes from the ACCW superstars.
3.3 Pay Per View When: Various times in the year Hosted by: Max Money and Jason Keller Where: Various locations What: Pay Per Views are ACCW's biggest shows. All the feuds die down, new feuds light up, surprises show up, everything happens here in an action packed time. Currently, there are four per year:
Crowning Glory (March): 16 Man Tournament for the Glory Cup Coastal Clash (June): ACCW's Biggest Extravaganza Summer Madness (August): ACCW's premier summer event Hell on Earth (November): ACCW's most extreme event headed by the dangerous Hell on Earth match.
4. Who are those guys in the shows? 4.1 Calvin Dunn He is the owner of the ACCW, he rarely makes an appearance on ACCW TV, but is usually present during the ACCW pay per views. He oversees all operations, but does not participate in making ACCW decisions. 4.2 Bryan Sharp He is the president of ACCW. He signs matches, suspends wrestlers, and is always present on ACCW TV.
4.3 Drake Savage The ACCW Head of Security. He is a huge man that can man handle any ACCW superstar, so when they get out of line Drake steps in.
4.4 Max Money & Jason Keller These are the commentators for all ACCW TV shows. Money is an aging family man while Keller is an obnoxious, twenty something man, with a craving for sex.
4.5 Damon Cutter The ring announcer whose booming voice introduces the ACCW superstars. The ladies at ringside also seem to like this handsome man.
4.6 Marty Dell & Whisper Hayes These two are the backstage interviewers who dig deep in the dirt of the ACCW locker rooms. Marty is the fresh out of broadcast school man who screws up a lot. Whisper is the sexy woman of the ACCW who everyone in the ACCW has the hots for.
4.7 Referees Headed by Chief Official: Lindon Mills. They control the action in the ring. The rest of the staff consists of Henry
James, Chris Sampson, and Lars Davis. 5. What titles can I shoot for? 5.1 Heavyweight Basically, the big one. Whoever holds this title basically owns the Atlantic Coast, well, that is in the wrestling ring. Everyone in ACCW shoots for this belt. Defended at every supercard and once every three to four weeks.
5.2 Television The secondary title and the iron man title. It is defended every other edition of Pure Excitement and on every supercards.
6. How are matches decided? ACCW is a booked league, meaning that all matches are decided by a human brain. Basically, what you do each week in ACCW results in the booking decision for that week. The more you do, the better chance you have to win.
Good flashes are an obvious must. This is the only visible thing to the entire league, and probably bears the most weight of the decision for that week. However, although you may have the better roleplay, you won't win every match. You are only allowed one a week, however, and they appear on ACCW Update.
Strategies also come into play. Although one of the least important of the four factors, they could be a tie breaker of some sort. And, you can probably get your character over, or do spots you want to see by sending these in.
Participation is a factor. Angles could be the most important, and may override all other booking decisions. If the bookers have something in mind down the road, or there's an angle which can be furthered through the result of one match, it may go that way.
Always remember that losing doesn't kill you. Also to note, upon entering the league, everybody loses a match or two. We need to find out if you're in it for the long haul, not splitting after one bad thing happens. This way, every wrestler goes through an initiation period, learning the ropes in the league. Slowly, they'll challenge the "old guard" and become one with the league.
7. What can I do to win? 7.1 Flashes Flashes are where you get to speak for your wrestler. They're what the league sees, and how people will react. This pretty much where things happen in how the character grows.
When flashing, use common sense. Don't use excessive profanity, or if you do, [bleep] it out. Don't use other wrestlers without permission. Don't use racial remarks, or anything else which may offend. Use your best judgement when sending things in.
There are only one spot for flashes, one spot for backstage comments, and one in-ring interview on request. ACCW Update is the main show, where everybody talks about their upcoming match on Pure
Excitement. The backstage comments are limited to just a few words with a backstage interview. The in-ring interviews are limited two about 2-4 a card, they are given on a first come first serve basis.
Feel free to use the ACCW fictional staff in your interviews, provided you keep them in character.
ACCW formats all it's shows the same way, trying to keep the flashes in a recognizable format. This is:
[Setting, descriptions, actions here.] MM: Talk Here. Any descriptions or actions go into square brackets, while any spoken word go in quotation marks. To start a wrestler's speech, use the initials of their name, followed by two spaces, a dash, and two more spaces.
Although we'd like you to help us out and use this format, it's not compulsory. As long as we can read it, it's fine.
7.2 Strats Strats help you guide your wrestler in the actual wrestling ring. You can submit specific moves, sequences, targets, or anything else he might do during the match.
We try and follow all strategies, but it may not be possible all the time based on somebody else's strategy. Keep that in mind.
Again, use your best judgement when strategizing. Obviously you can't strat to beat the other wrestler up with a baseball bat.
Non-match strategies are covered in the next section on angles. 7.3 Angles Possibly the most important aspect of ACCW, creating angles allows you to truly guide your wrestler to greatness.
ACCW always tries to accomodate its handlers by using their angles, keeping it as a handler participation league.
Angles may include post match attacks on another wrestler, turning on another wrestler, doing something during the show, or things like that. Anything which you'd like your wrestler to do on the
Scraps, but doesn't involve wrestling is classified as an angle. But, angles also includes the long term planning between two wrestlers. More often then not, these involve two or more handlers working on a storyline between their two wrestlers. And, more often then not, these are the most interesting angles, as it has double the brain power working on it. We encourage these types of angles, with proper notice.
Angles allow you to control your wrestler. Make use of them. 7.4 Miscellaneous We've always got things behind the scenes for people to do. Matches, proof reading, keeping me from killing myself, whatever. If you're really bored and want something to do, we'll find you something. 8. What are the deadlines? Sometimes, you can get away with sending things after the deadline. We're pretty flexible. But, when we can't wait any longer, or have to work on something with the deadline in mind, they are followed.
This is the warning, so don't complain if you send something post-deadline and it's not used. 8.1 Pure Excitement Strats: Thursday In-rings (on request): Wednesday night Angle requests: Sunday - Tuesdays 8.2 ACCW Update Flashes: Wednesday night 9.
