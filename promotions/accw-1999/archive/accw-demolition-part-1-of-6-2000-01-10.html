ACCW Demolition (Part 1 of 6)

10 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The screen opens to black, with the exception of a small ACCW logo on the bottom right. After a two second delay, the screen comes to life with footage from the PPV...]
[As ACCW comes back on to National Television we are taken back to the Hell in a Cell match that took place during Inagural Brawl]
Voiceover: In one of the most anticipated and talked about matches. [The screen fades to Kossuth and Fuse in business suits signing the contract in President Hoppers oval office]
Voiceover: Each side had a goal.....To destroy the others! [The camera fades into a picture of Kossuth's and Fuse's faces side by side.] Voiceover: All for this. [A Picture of the ACCW Televison Title fades into view upon a black screen] Voiceover: Each man with words of glory [A few video shots are shown of Kossuth in New Mexico, his Dark Carnival as it fades to Fuse in his house each man speaking but with no words coming out]
Voiceover: The final stipulation Kossuth: Fuse, you are facing me "The Child of Fire" and that means.......You've already lost. I'm calling the final stipulation now. If I don't defeat you this Sunday I WILL LEAVE ATLANTIC COAST CHAMPIONSHIP
WRESTLING. Voiceover: The talk was finally over and Armageddon ensued [Clips are shown of the ladder match. Fuse's thundering Belly to Belly Suplex, the sickening thud of Fuse enzugiring Kossuth out of the ring then followed by Kossuth's mid-air drop kick on the flying Fuse and ending with
Kossuth's murderous chair shot that crumples Fuse]
Voiceover: The finish! [Both men continue climbing the cage until on top. Kossuth is the first up to the top.] RA: Kossuth meets Fuse as Fuse is reaching the top. Boot from Kossuth, but Fuse hangs on. Another kick from Kossuth, but HIS FOOT IS CAUGHT!
Fuse catches the ankle of Kossuth and now is able to get to the top of the cage. Both men on top, and both men back to their feet.
BS: You know, I have seen these type of matches before, and those tops aren't made for guys to be on top....they are made to hold the guys IN the cage.
RA: Very true, Brent, but no matter, they are on top now! Boot to the gut of Fuse....NOW DDT!!!!
BS: ALRIGHT! Kossuth with the advantage again. I just wish they were on the mat, so that referee Wilson could make the count!
RA: I have a feeling that he could've got a three after that move.! Fuse is still not moving following that DDT!
[Kossuth steps back a few steps and motions....] BS: Watch this! Kossuth going to do a splash on Fuse! RA: It appears that way! He takes a few steps.......NO!!!! FUSE MOVED AGAIN! Oh my! Did you see that cage about give way? BS: I sure did! Kossuth shouldn't have to be fighting in these conditions! RA: Fuse back to his feet, and he looks around at the crowd. I think he is going to try and finish off Kossuth here....could it be time for the
ELETRIC SHOCK? BS: Lord, I hope not! RA: Fuse now hooks Kossuth for a suplex....NO! This is not Electric Shock. But he does have him up for a suplex....BRAIN HAEMORRHAGE!!! BS and RA: NOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [As the announcers scream, Kossuth and Fuse both fall through the roof of the cage and land on the mat. Fuse bounces and lands on top of Kossuth with a draped arm. Both men appear to be out cold.]
RA: OH MY GOODNESS! BOTH MEN JUST FELL FROM THROUGH THE CAGE AND ARE ON THE MAT! REFEREE MIKE WILSON HAS NO CHOICE BUT TO COUNT.... 1..... 2........... 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [DING DING DING!!!!!] BS: NO!!!!!! KOSSUTH GOT ROBBED! Voiceover: Was this the way it was all supposed to end? Did Lady Luck toss her coin into the air and it landed....Fuse side up? So what did it all mean.
[Suddenly nothing but a black screen.] Voiceover: The aftermath [During the break right before the main event we see former ACCW President
Chris Hopper power walking through the back of the arena as the on-looker and passers by turn their head. Before you ask No, this was never shown on the
PPV because it was happening during the introductions of the main event.
Hoppers seems to be confused by his facial expressions and the small camera crew rushes behind him to the end of this particular hall way. Hopper arrives at the door and walks in without knocking and speaks with a shock tone in his voice]
Hopper: About what just happened out there ...... [Suddenly Kossuth whips his head around and meets the camera crew which promps Hopper to quit speaking]
Hopper: I'm sure something can be worked out. I mean...it......... [Suddenly Kossuth just slams his blue locker door cutting him off once agan. He isnt even dressed he's still in his ring attire covered in sweat and blood and he has his Adidas black and white back strapped over his neck and shoulder and suddenly he approaches Hopper. As Hopper beings to speak out a few more words Kossuth just shoves him against the door of the locker room and walks out into the hallways. The camera crew rushes to follow as Kossuth walks out the back door of the arena never looking back walking into the night.]
Hopper: GREAT...THAT'S JUST [BLEEP] ING GREAT. TURN THAT DAMN THING OFF. [The screen changes to a dark, pitch black.] [The screen remains black for a few seconds. Slowly, flames begin to build up on the bottom of the screen and climb about half-way up. The crackling of the flames is heard, until a Jimi Hendrix-style guitar riff begins playing in the background, quietly at first but steadily increasing in volume. As the riff hits its peak, a blue and red "ACCW" logo appears in the center of the flames. The flames then extend the length of the screen, eventually engulfing the whole picture.]
[The flames slowly break up, but the ACCW logo stays put it in the center of the screen. The flames finally disappear completely, leaving the logo against a black background. A rough, raspy semi-screaming voiceover is heard.]
Voiceover: THIS IS ATLANTIC COAST CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING!!! [The scene abruptly switches to a quick sequence of wrestling action as a
Metallica-style guitar riff now plays. A heavy drum sequence kicks in and a few brief scenes of buildings being destroyed is mixed in with the wrestling scenes. The pace of the changing scenes picks up, continuing to get faster until they are changing in less than a second. Suddenly, the background goes blackish-gray and an "ACCW Demolition" logo appears in the middle of the screen, turned at a 30 degree angle to the left. The logo stays for a few seconds, then crumbles down as the screen is filled in by a panoramic shot of the outdoor stadium.]
[The camera shifts to a front view of two men standing in front of an ACCW banner. The man on the right appears to be in his early 50's, wearing a gray suit and tie. His hair is mostly gray and he has a serious but excited look on his face. The man on the left is considerably younger and shorter than his counterpart. He wears a dark navy suit and tie, and has neatly trimmed dark hair. The man on the right speaks up.]
Robert Allen: Hello everyone, and welcome to the Richmond Coliseum in Richmond,
Virginia! This is Demolition! I am Robert Allen, alongside Brent Scott, and
Brent, we're coming off a huge pay per view!
Brent Scott: That's the first thing you've gotten right since we opened our doors, Roberto. It was indeed huge, but the best part was seeing Bryce Jordan crowned our first Heavyweight Champion.
RA: But that match was not without controversy, we'll see what new commissioner
William Wright has to say about that. We also crowned our first Light
Heavyweight champion... BS: Good ol' Dragonchump. RA: "DragonFIST" Alex Carbajal, who gets no rest as he has to defend the title
TONIGHT against both Deuce and "Box Office" Tripp Shade. But the biggest story tonight HAS to be what we saw to lead off the show. Kossuth apparently is gone from ACCW.
BS: For the second time. RA: Former President Chris Hopper tried to stop him from leaving, and you must admit some rather unusual circumstances surrounded the end of that match. Both he and Fuse are fortunate they weren't killed there.
BS: Yeah, lucky us. Now we just get to listen to Fuse ramble on and kiss the fans' butts. And keep in mind, Fuse didn't REALLY win that match. He just happened to have his arm fall on top of Kossuth at the end. So he's lucky he's still the TV Champion.
RA: Also in a MAJOR upset, the team of Snowed Under became the first Tag Team
Champions, upsetting both the Deadly Duo AND the Delta Destroyers on their way to victory.
BS: Yeah, and you can bet the farm that the Duo won't take this laying down. RA: Fans, we have so much to talk about, and SIX big matches on tap for tonight, plus an in-ring appearance by returning Commissioner William Wright!
BS: [very sarcastically] Yay, rah. RA: Um, remember he signs your paychecks now. BS: Oh yeah. Welcome back, sir! RA: Before we go to our first match of the new year, we've got one half of the
ACCW Tag Team Champions scheduled to speak his mind about a few things.
BS: Which half? RA: Snow Storm. BS: CRAP! [Snow Storm appears in the tunnel, and just stands there as he basks in his glory, the crowd gives a huge pop when they see Snow Storm slowly but surely walking out to the ring, he stops and points to the screen.]
SS: Hit it boys. [The screen shows Snow Storms, great flying Bulldog off the top rope, and the crowd gives a bigger pop.]
[The camera then focuses on Snow Storm he is in a better view of the camera now and you can see he is wearing a pair of runners, baggy cargos, and a shirt and his ACCW TAG TEAM CHAMPION belt. Which is blocking the view of the slogan on the shirt. He strides confidentially to the ring as "You aint seen nothin yet" plays on the loud speaker. Snow Storm, walks to the ring, pulls a chair from beneath the ring and throws it into the middle of the ring. Snow Storm slides under the bottom rope and grabs the mic, which was already lying there. He circles the ring before sitting down to have his chat.]
SS: Hey guys whats doin. Well I m here before you allto do a couple of things, but I would just like to start off by thanking two great friends of mine, one being Down Under Derek Davies. This guy is a great mate, and will always be one, I owe him this. [Taking off his Tag Team Champion Starp, and holding it high above his head] [Huge crowd pop]. This guy is known as the Babe Ruth of
Wrestling in Australia, this guy completely dominates the sport where he calls home. And the other Beerbarron Ben Reeves, you all know him, and you probably are thinkin why would u thank him, well Mr Reeves took me under his wing before
I had my own Michael, I could have called him Dad because he was more of a father to me than my real Dad. Well guys I think we owe these guys a big 3 cheers how bout it.
[Crowd Chants Down-Under-Down Under] SS: Nah I mean as in three cheers, ok [crowd goes wild] ok hip hip [hooray] hip hip [Hooray] hip hip [loudest hooray yet]. But I'm sorry to ruin our litlle party but I have to have a litlle chat about these so called Smart guys, actually I think there name Is pretty stupid if you ask me, do you agree
[Holding the mic up to the crowd] [YEAH!!!! The crowd returns]. These so called scholars attacked two men that are bigger, better stronger and smarter than them..do u think that is smart [once again holding the mic up to the crowd]
[NO!!!!! the crowd returns even louder this time]. No I didn't think so, you see by doing that you have now ruined any chance of stardom in the ACCW, cause when we pay you back we will either do one of two things. One we could hurt you that bad that you will have to retire from wrestling altogether, or two we could just kill you, ruining your whole life. But you could make your stay here simpler, stay out of our way, we have better things to do than teach your wrestler WANNABES how to wrestle. Well Sod, may I call u that, I will anwyay, its up to you, you either die, be seriously injured or you could just stay out of it. And if you are as smart as you think or tell us, u will leave this FED right now..And now to you Cuban, who the hell do u think you are seriously who. You come here and tell us all how good you are and how you are undefeated and how you are all that. TELL ME WHO IS A CHAMPION, and who is some wanka that got his ass kicked by Crusader, man u deserve to be a LOSER that fights the likes of " The hill" and the florist. And that is giving you a wrap, maybe you should just piss off and go join the Cuban militant, and piss off poor people cause you are just degrading your country even more, and that's pretty hard to do. So maybe one day we will take you up on the offer of you and ya mystery boyfriend but for now, I think we can leave at this. Snowed Under cant fight NO HOPERS like CUBAN. Now onto happier notes,
I would like to congratulate Mr. "trendy Bryce Jordan" [crowd gives a mild pop], This man defied the odds and won, good on ya mate. And as for Fuse, same to you congrats you deserved that victory, cause Kossuth is a SUCKER, maybe him and Cuban can have a three some with the Florist [the crowd roars with laughter]. And for Fuse one again congrats you thoroughly deserve that win, again. And Alex, that was a good show, I wouldn't mind seeing that again. But on this subject who hear thinks that SNOWED UNDERS TITLE REIGN WILL LAST THIS
WHOLE CENTURY nah just kidding [laughter fills the stadium], well I think I have to have a serious note to a William Wright, I would just like a singles match soon, cause
Tag is Fun, but it wont last for ever.
SS: And now for my other reason, I am here to offer any single wrestler in this whole federation a chance to wrestle the one and only ME!!, I m sick and tired of watching the likes of Cuban getting a go. I have had two matches, 1 against a pathetic excuse for an athlete like Davies, and one a survivor series where it was me and Tiger Jay versus four people cause those stupid drongos Hellfire had no idea. So who wants it, u just call my name tell me the time or place
and I will be there. But make sure it's a fair one, cause remember I never forget. And I would also like to thank all those people that turned up to our shout after the PPV, it was a great time and fun was had by all. And thank you
[pointing to the crowd and circling] you have been a great host and a great audience, just remember always stick by me, and u will be right.
[He slides under the bottom rope and walks towards the tunnel as "you aint seen nothin yet" plays over the loud speaker again, and snow storm slaps the crowds hands as the crowd chant Snow-Storm-Snow-Storm.]
BS: Thank GOD that's over. I didn't think he'd ever shut up. He's worse than
Fuse. RA: Well, the fans seem to like him. BS: Yeah, and what do THEY know? [CUT TO COMMERCIAL]
