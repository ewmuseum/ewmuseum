ACCW Coastal Stampede

13 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ATLANTIC COAST CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING PRESENTS..... COASTAL STAMPEDE JANUARY 13, 2000 GREENVILLE, NORTH CAROLINA =========================================================== [The screen is black. Suddenly, the sounds of a drill turning can be heard as "Poundcake" by Van Halen kicks into effect. As the drums and then Eddie
Van Halen guitar kick in, the screen goes from all black to an overhead shot of an arena, with an ACCW wrestling ring positioned in the center of the picture. The ring is empty, but every seat you can see from the view we have is full, with the fans anticipating the coming event. The arena lights dim and a light show begins, seemingly to signify the beginning of the show in the arena. Sammy Hagar jumps into the song with his unique vocals, and the music slowly begins to fade out. The picture cuts to a studio set, with a large desk in the middle, and a large screen behind it.
ACCW reporter/interviewer "Emerald" Eddie Eaton sits behind the desk, a stack of papers in front of him, and a coffee mug in hand. He sets the mug down on the desk as the music cuts and the camera focuses on his face.]
EE: Good evening, ladies and gentlemen. Welcome to ACCW corporate headquarters in Jacksonville, Florida, for the first ever showing of ACCW
Coastal Stampede. We'll be reviewing the matches that took place earlier in Greenville, North Carolina, this week. We've got some rather important matches to cover tonight, including out main event, with Fuse defending the
ACCW TV Title against one-half of the ACCW Tag Team Champions, "Down Under"
Derek Davies. But first, we have a youngster making his ACCW debut... ******************************************************* "Trailblazer" Tim Joseph vs. "The Missile" Scott Cruise ******************************************************* EE: "Trailblazer" Tim Joseph wasted no time in wowing the Greenville crowd with his high-risk approach. He immediately opened up on Cruise with a pair of spinning headscissor takedowns, then grounded the Missile with a superb dropkick. Cruise rolled out of the ring to catch his breath, but
Joseph gave him no rest, diving onto Cruise with a running springboard plancha, which got a big response from the crowd. Joseph immediately hopped onto the apron, jumped to the second rope, and executed an Asai moonsault onto Cruise. Joseph tossed Cruise back in, and went to the top again, this time attempting a senton bomb, but Cruise scouted it and Joseph drew nothing but mat. Cruise caught his wind, then caught Joseph in a spinning DDT as he stood up. A quick cover got a two count, and Cruise stayed on the assault. After a few high-risk moves of his own, Cruise went to the well one too many times, when Joseph turned a diving shoulder tackle into a quick DDT, which turned out to be the one highlight move of the night. With Cruise laid out, Joseph brought him to his feet and executed the Trailblazer, a variation of a single-arm DDT combined with a faceslam, a very impressive move to see. That secured the youngster a three-count and a successful debut in ACCW.
********************** * WINNER: TIM JOSEPH * * VIA: PINFALL * * TIME: 4:27 * ********************** EE: I tell you, the crowd in Greenville really responded well to this kid, and he appears to have a bright future ahead of him in ACCW.
******************************************************* Pash "the Flash" Kavana vs. Mr. White ******************************************************* EE: We had another youngster slated to make his debut, but apparently he had transportation problems and was unable to make it to Greenville. But since ACCW doesn't want to disappoint, a replacement was announced for
Kavana -- Crusader. Crusader made short work of Mr. White, wanting to make a good showing after his loss to The Big Chill on Demolition. Crusader dominated this one from start to finish, and Mr. White really didn't mount any offense whatsoever. Crusader quickly finished it off with a sidewalk slam and the Penance, a torture rack, for the win.
********************** * WINNER: CRUSADER * * VIA: SUBMISSION * * TIME: 3:07 * ********************** EE: Well, I guess Mr. White got more than he bargained for, as fate would have it. Crusader looked very sharp coming off his loss to TBC, and he still has the ability to make an impact in ACCW. Before the next match,
"Showtime" Cyrus Bennett was given a chance to explain his actions on last week's Demolition, here's the footage of that, er, waste of time...
< The lights dim as 'Ecstasy of Gold' thunders on the house speakers. As the music crescendos, 'Show Time' Cyrus Bennett, steps out from behind the curtain and the lights flash back to brightness. He strides to the ring, soaking in the mixed reaction from the crowd, looking over
them with an indifferent stare. He enters the ring, wearing slacks, black tennis shoes, a black ball cap, and his trademark long sleeve t-shirt that reads, "Uh-Oh... Show Time!!" on the front. He picks up the mic and waits for the crowd to grow quiet. > CB: I'm sure a lot of you are wondering about the other night. < He listens to the mixed reaction from the crowd and grins slightly. Then grows serious again as he brings the mic to his mouth. > CB: What, in gawd's name, was a newcomer to the ACCW doing tearing up the main event in his first appearance? What were his motives? What is this guy thinking? Does he wear boxers or briefs? < He looks over the crowd again, maintaining a dry serious face. > CB: Well, I have only one answer for that. ....... Boxer briefs, baby. < The crowd gives Bennett a few sputtering laughs. > CB: Now then, now then. Anyone who doesn't know about 'Show Time' needs to be educated in the worse way. Why? Because you've obviously had your head stuck straight up your ass. Heheh... That's why. < Crickets can be heard chirping throughout the crowd. > CB: Yours truly, is none other than the Pulsepounder, the Immortal and the SHowSto-..... Uhh... No.. ...
< He pauses momentarily to regain his point. > CB: While I'm out here, getting a few things off my chest, let me teach you the 'Show Time' cheer. When I'm about to apply the 'Forever Drop' I scream 'Uh-oh!' and you scream-- < He holds the mic while obscenities are yelled. > CB: No... no... no. You yell 'SHOW TIME!' as I knock the punk out. What's wrong with that? It's not too hard is it? Sweet Delucia, people. So, on to another point, and if you're lucky, my last point. < The world's largest single gasp of relief is uttered throughout the crowd. > CB: I see I have a match tomorrow with a tag teamer named 'Snow Storm', well, my compadre, it's gonna be nothing more than a 'Show Storm'
tomorrow night and until then, I remain Immortal. < Bennett drops the mic and steps out of the ring as the audience throws another mixed reaction towards his back. He strides confidently to the back as his music plays again. >
******************************************************* "Double V" Vic Valiant vs. Cuban ******************************************************* EE: Vic Valiant, a former tag team partner of Tripp Shade, made his in-ring debut against Cuban. Double V took control early on, using a combination of mat wrestling and high-flying to keep Cuban off guard. But Cuban came back after a low blow and took control, keeping Valiant grounded with various blows to his legs, with some high impact slams mixed in. The fans began to rally Vic, and that seemed to get him going as he fought back
Valiantly, pardon the pun, and reversed a Cuban power bomb attempt into a hurricarana. Vic, having his second wind, unleashed an amazing aerial attack, taking Cuban down with a missile dropkick followed by a diving headbutt. Valiant dropped Cuban with his Valiant Skull Driver for the win.
*********************** * WINNER: VIC VALIANT * * VIA: PINFALL * * TIME: 4:41 * *********************** EE: So Vic Valiant is victorious in his first match in an ACCW ring, I know we can expect to see big things from this guy in the future. Next up, we have a match that features another competitor from the Light Heavyweight division, James Holloway, but before that, here's some footage filmed backstage in Greenville.
[Backstage, in Jay Moore's locker room, we see Lauryn sitting on a bench, talking excitedly]
Lauryn: Where are ya gonna take me, Uncle Jay?? What do I get to see? Jay: er... well, there really isn't that much to see, Lauryn.... its a pretty boring place! Lauryn: Well what about the ring? I wanna go out in front of all the people!! Jay: No! You can't go out there, Lauryn! It's too dangerous, and I can't just bring you out!
Lauryn: If I went out, would I get hurt like you do when you go out there.... like when that big man hit you in the head with the chair and the blood came out everywhere?
Jay: What?! How did you know about that?? Lauryn: I saw it on the TV, when the man with the camera was here..... [Jay slaps his forehead, and curses to himself] Jay: Lauryn, just ignore that, it wasn't as bad as it looked! Lauryn: But your face was all weird after it, Uncle Jay. Jay: Look, Lauryn, why don't I show you around the back. Maybe we'll find something.... just try to forget about what you saw on the TV, ok?
Lauryn: Ok!! [Excitedly, Lauryn moves out of the room, Jay reluctantly following her, as the scene returns to the arena]
EE: That may not seem too important now, but it does lead to something that we'll show you later on in the show. For now, let's get to the next match...
******************************************************* Dr. Hate vs. James Holloway ******************************************************* EE: Holloway wanted to make a better showing following his loss to Cyrus
Bennett. He started off on fire, hitting Hate with a barrage of methodical suplexes and slams. Hate made a brief comeback, putting Holloway down with a swinging neckbreaker and rolling somersault splash, but Holloway would regroup and come back with a renewed intensity. Holloway was very impressive in showing his technical prowess, chaining several moves at a time together. A snap suplex followed by rolling neck snap set Dr. Hate up for a quick and stiff DDT. Holloway wasted no time after that, hitting the
Cornwall Spike for the win.
************************** * WINNER: JAMES HOLLOWAY * * VIA: PINFALL * * TIME: 4:15 * ************************** EE: Holloway showed some very impressive wrestling skill in that match, and looks to be ready to make a move up the ladder here in ACCW. And speaking of climbing up the ladder...
******************************************************* The Football Hooligans vs. The Highway Patrol ******************************************************* EE: The Football Hooligans appear to be getting on a roll here, and they continued their streak against the Highway Patrol. The match began as a four-way brawl with Dazza paired off with Willis and Anderson with Smithy.
The Hooligans took immediate control, beating the life of our resident officers. Dazza eventually ended up in the ring with Willis, and took him apart with some dazzling, pardon the pun, mat-wrestling. A tag to Smithy brought in a vicious spinebuster-legdrop combo and a pinfall attempt, which again brought on a four-way brawl as Anderson hit the ring. Dazza disposed of Anderson after a powerbomb, leaving Willis all alone to be on the receiving end of The Final Whistle.
*********************************** * WINNERS: THE FOOTBALL HOOLIGANS * * VIA: PINFALL * * TIME: 2:53 * *********************************** EE: So the Hooligans win an easy one there, but have their work cut out for them as the face the Deadly Duo tomorrow on Demolition -- that is, if the
Duo show up. Before the next match, The Big Chill was given an opportunity to speak his mind. This is what he had to say...
("Calm like a Bomb" by Rage Against the Machine begins to play as the The
Big Chill walks out from the backstage entrance and stands still. TBC surveys the crowd. His blonde hair is cut short into a Caesar cut and sports a neatly trimmed blonde goatee. TBC is wearing a short sleeve grey button-down shirt, black pants, black leather shoes and black Oakley sunglasses. TBC raises one arm as pyrotechnics explode around him. The crowd breaks into boos and jeers, and surprisingly, a small smattering of cheers. He begins to walk to the ring. As TBC walks by a camera, he says
"Everybody sit back and listen carefully. You might just learn something."
TBC enters the ring. TBC walks over to the ring announcer and takes the mic from him. He stands still for a few moments as the crowd settles down.)
TBC: It's become apparent to me that there is a lot of confusion here in the ACCW. And I guess its up to me to explain certain things and set the record straight. "Trendy" Bryce Jordan. From where I'm sitting, chump, it seems as though you're worried about all the wrong things. It seems as though you are overly concerned with what is trendy in the world. I guess that's where you got your handle from. Let me break it down for you. The big problem with that, Jordan, is... things that are trendy tend to quickly lose their importance and substance. In a few months, what is trendy now, well, it's out of style and it's outdated. People don't remember trends,
Jordan. They seem to do better remembering the classics. You know, things that get better with age. So, lets see if you can follow. These clothes, punk...
(TBC runs his hand over the left hand side of his shirt.) TBC: ...these clothes are trendy. What's inside these clothes...well, that my friend, is classic. You see, Bryce Jordan, you've got less than 24 hours until you step into the ring with a classic. You have got a date with destiny, Jordan. A date many in the past have not been ready for. How well have you prepared for it, Jordan? Judging by your own words, I'm guessing that you haven't prepared all that well. You look at me and see a fashion statement. Jordan, you're moving your lips, but nothing is coming out but a bunch of gibberish. What you should see is the man who has ran through everyone and anyone in his way. You should have that little boy Friday of yours run out and grab some video tapes of me. Just send the chump out to the local Blockbuster, have him look in the instructional video section, and look for the topic called "Mass Destruction". It may not be in, so he may have to wait for a little bit. It is a popular video. Watch it a few times and then you'll be ready for the massacre. You and your people act like they forget about TBC.
(TBC walks over to the ropes and leans on them.) TBC: You see, Jordan, you got to get into the ring with the T, the B and
... (Before TBC can finish his catch phrases, a section of the crowd screams out "the C". TBC straightens up. A wicked smile comes across his face. TBC chuckles.)
TBC: Well, I guess someone's figuring out the score. We'll find out tomorrow if Jordan has caught on. Like I was saying, you gotta get into the ring with the man known as... "the Goods". And that's just bad news for you. I've faced chumps like you before. You're all the same. All that posturing. All that posing. You look more like a model or an actor than a wrestler. You remember wrestling, right? It's not all about flying around and looking pretty. It's not about wearing pretty clothes and what not.
(TBC takes off the shirt, revealing a chiselled chest.) TBC: It's not about this. (TBC holds the shirt out.) TBC: It's about being willing to bleed all over yourself. It's all about sacrifice. And that's where we differ, chump. What's the difference between me and you? You talk a good one, but you don't do what you're supposed to.
World Champion? I think not. You're not willing to go to the edge. Me? I live on that edge. You want to prove me wrong? I got an idea. We can scream
"who cares" and sees who means it. So what it comes down to, Jordan, is tomorrow night, the talk stops and the action begins. See, once you're in the ring, you're in my realm. $500 shirts don't count. Fancy watches don't mean a thing. It's just going to be me and you. It's just going to be about who can take more punishment. I'll give you a little hint about who I think has got more tolerance: he ain't worried about CK1. So come the 14th of
January, the ACCW's going to have a new champion on their hands. And you're going to find out what many other chumps have found out the hard way: you don't want to step to the T, the B and the C, cuz you'll end up wanting to
D,I,E! Shine up the belt, it's coming home.
("Calm like a Bomb" by Rage Against the Machine begins to play again. TBC drops the mic and climbs out of the ring. He begins to make his way up the aisle. About half way up, he notices a small group of fans with Pro-TBC signs. He shakes his head, laughs and continues making his way to the back.)
******************************************************* "Sensational" Christian Hardcastle vs. Shane Larson ******************************************************* EE: So the question is, would the "real" Christian Hardcastle show up for this match? I don't know if the "real" one showed up, but the one that did show up looked better than the one we have seen the past couple of weeks.
Hardcastle opened up on Larson with a new intensity, dropping Larson with a combination of an aerial and ground attack. Larson did mount some offense of his own, countering a Hardcastle suplex attempt with a suplex of his own. But Larson's offense would be short-lived, as Hardcastle hit his
CattleBuster DDT, then followed with Simply Sensational, a 450 degree splash, for the win.
******************************** * WINNER: CHRISTIAN HARDCASTLE * * VIA: PINFALL * * TIME: 4:17 * ******************************** EE: So I guess Christian will claim that the "real" him showed up in Greenville. I've heard some lame excuses before, but that one takes the cake. Anyway... ******************************************************* Rob "the Shootmaster" Trainor vs. The Hill ******************************************************* EE: Rob Trainor was rather unhappy following his loss to Mexx on Demolition, and took his displeasure out on The Hill in this match. Trainor attacked before the bell, and Hill had little chance to mount any offense in this one. After a series of stiff martial arts-like kicks with
Hill in the corner, Trainor grabbed a chair and brought Hill out with a vicious DDT right onto it. Hill tried to get in some offense, but his few attempts came up short. Trainor took Hill's head off with a hard clothesline, then locked on the Snapshooter, getting a quick submission win.
*********************** * WINNER: ROB TRAINOR * * VIA: SUBMISSION * * TIME: 3:13 * *********************** EE: Wow, Trainor was pretty hot, and The Hill was unlucky enough to be on the receiving end of that beating. Trainor is still a force to be reckoned with in ACCW. Now, going back to the Jay Moore footage earlier, this is why it became important...
[Jay Moore, along with his little cousin, Lauryn, make their way down the backstage corridors, Lauryn prancing around excitedly. They turn the corner, and
Jay bums into Snow Storm] Jay: Oh, hey man SS: Hey, Jay, whats doin'? Cute little, er, niece ya got there Jay: Second cousin actually.... what you doin' here anyway man, don't you have a match tomorrow??
SS: Yeah, but I came to support Derek tonight. Jay: Oh, right, he's got a TV title shot, right? SS: Yeah.... bad luck, by the way, for the other night.... ya put up a good fight. Jay: Yeah, Fuse was better though.... I don't think luck had nothing to do with it.... Derek'll probably have a hard time tonight if Fuse fights the way he's been fighting. I'm sure he'll do well though.
SS: Yeah... [Short silence, until Jay speaks up] Jay: Where's Lauryn?! SS: Huh? Oh... she was just there a second ago? [Jay's eyes wander around, searching the corridors] Jay: LAURYN?? Where are you?? SS: Oh, man.... [Jay takes off down the corridor, still yelling for Lauryn, as the scene fades out] ******************************************************* Mexx vs. Edgecrusher ******************************************************* EE: Mexx didn't wait for the bell before jumping all over Edgecrusher. After attacking Edgecrusher with a barrage of punches, he dumped him to the outside, where Apollo was waiting to whack him with a chair. Mexx then wowed the crowd with a suicide plancha over the top rope, and considering how big Mexx is, that was a sight to see. However, Mexx got a bit too cocky, and took too long in setting Edgecrusher up for a superplex.
Edgecrusher fought back and knocked Mexx to the mat, then devastated Mexx with a somersault neckbreaker. Edgecrusher kept the momentum, keeping Mexx off his feet by using his speed. But Mexx proved to be a bit too much, reversing a DDT attempt into a northern lights suplex. It didn't take long before Mexx hit the Mexx Out to get the win.
******************* * WINNER: MEXX * * VIA: PINFALL * * TIME: 5:23 * ******************* EE: After the match, as Mexx, Apollo, and Darius made their way to the back, Lauryn wandered out and saw them, then ran screaming into the back.
Moore, who had then caught up with Lauryn, became outraged. After Lauryn was safe with Snow Storm, Moore grabbed a chair and went after the trio.
Maybe Jay will leave his cousin home next week. Moving along... ******************************************************* Steel Cage vs. The Scholars of Destruction ******************************************************* EE: Two of the top contenders in the tag team ranks battled it out in the next to last match of the evening. Cage and Bradley started this one off with a furious exchange of moves. Bradley got the quick advantage after a cheap shot on a break, and rocked Cage with a spinning heel kick. A tag to
Everett allowed the SoD to show their true area of expertise, double-team moves. They quickly cut the ring in half and isolated Cage, coming very close to winning the match on a few occasions. However, a blown double-team move allowed Cage to make the hot tag to Perez, who came in like a house on fire, taking it to both Bradley and Everett. Perez hit a shooting star press on Everett, and probably would have gotten the win had
Bradley not made the save. Cage came over and took out Bradley, and a four-way fracas ensued. In the commotion, Everett took a chain out of his tights and nailed Perez with it, seemingly right in front of official Aaron
Stephens, who then made the three count. The SoD celebrated their win while Keri Cage argued with Stephens. But then, Snowed Under made their way down with lead official Michael Wilson behind them. Wilson declared that the match would continue, and that Stephens would not be allowed to officiate the rest of the match. Stephens and the SoD were all stunned by this, allowing Perez to roll Everett up from behind to get the three count and the win. Snowed Under, meanwhile, just smiled at the SoD and walked to the back. The SoD quickly took off after the tag champs as Steel Cage celebrated their win.
*********************** * WINNERS: STEEL CAGE * * VIA: PINFALL * * TIME: 9:32 * *********************** EE: Interesting finish to say the least. I hope we find out exactly what's going on with official Aaron Stephens, and see if this SoD/Snowed Under thing leads anywhere. Next up...
******************************************************* TV TITLE MATCH: Fuse (c) vs. "Down Under" Derek Davies ******************************************************* EE: The main event of the evening featured a great match to end the first ever Coastal Stampede, with TV Champion Fuse defending against one-half of the Tag Team Champions, "Down Under" Derek Davies. After a handshake to start, the two showcased a nice display of technical wrestling, with Davies coming out on top due to his size advantage. Davies began working on
Fuse's legs, in an attempt to keep him grounded. Davies was inches away from winning the title after hitting Fuse with a devastating patented choke bomb, but Fuse managed to get a foot on the ropes. Davies got a little too comfortable and let Fuse slide out of a press slam attempt. When Davies turned around, Fuse met him with a beautiful dropkick. Davies got back up, but then got dropped by a springboard dropkick from Fuse. Fuse dropped
Davies with a piledriver and got a two count. Fuse stayed on the offense, using his high-flying arsenal to keep Davies off his feet. At one point,
Fuse executed a new move that he calls the Lightning Bolt, a springboard hurricurana into a double leg-hook, which Davies barely managed to escape.
But Davies' strength came into play again, as he turned a Fuse flying body press into a powerslam. Davies made it up first, and from out of nowhere, hit a running power bomb that looked to leave Fuse in a world of hurt. At this point, the Scholars of Destruction all of a sudden appeared on the rampway, dragging Snow Storm behind them -- apparently they had attacked him backstage. The SoD's taunted Davies on the mic and beat some more on
Storm, literally forcing Davies to leave the ring and rush to the back to aid his partner. The SoD's split, and Davies chased them into the back, leaving referee Michael Wilson no choice but to count Davies out.
******************** * WINNER: FUSE * * VIA: COUNTOUT * * TIME: 11:53 * ******************** EE: Incredible finish! Looks like there might be something heating up between the Scholars and Snowed Under. The SoD basically costs Davies the match, Fuse retains, and that wraps up our first ever Coastal Stampede!
Don't forget to check out our flagship show, ACCW Demolition, tomorrow night! Next week we're covering the matches that will take place in
Durham, North Carolina, as we wrap up our NC tour! See you then! ["Poundcake" slowly fades in, as a highlight reel showing various spots from the matches plays. The ACCW logo appears in center screen, while everything else fades away. We are left with just the flaming logo on a black background, as the show ends...]
