The North-American Wrestling Alliance The Beginning After investigating ways to start a new fed and participating in several feds that have either folded or gotten stagnant [name withheld] decided to start his own fed. Looking to capture the magic of good feds like ECW and the glory days of gimmick wrestling he needs wrestlers to back him up.
The Shows The NWA has only one show a week so [name withheld] can write the show by hand. If he decides he can handle it after a few weeks he will start to write two cards a week. The show right now takes place on Monday night and is titled NWA Prime
Time. Super Cards The NWA is a small promotion run by a single man. Add to this the fact that PPVs in real life are purely presented for revenue and PPVs are obviously not needed. However, in place of PPVs the NWA will run monthly SuperCards. In each of these shows all NWA titles will be defended and any outstanding feuds will be settled. All of these shows are simply named NWA Main Event - MM/YY.
Role-plays As many feds say that they want quality over quantity, as the NWA does, this goes without saying. However, all of your RPs should not be from the ring, arena, backstage, etc. Try to make your RPs interesting. Perhaps one of the reporters is interviewing you at your house. Or maybe your leaving for a camping trip when you find out your needed for a match. Be creative. Also, don't be racist or hateful and don't attack other wrestlers unless they allow it.
