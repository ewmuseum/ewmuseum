Deadline nears...

5 January 2010

LQJT86C — 5 January 2010 Just a reminder, RP threads will be CLOSED this Friday at 11:55pm eastern time. If you're booked and haven't done more than 1 RP, I'd like to see you put up another one before deadline. If you're booked and haven't RP'd at all...you're getting left off the next two cards.
Thanks to Josh and Devin for bringing their A game this cycle. That's two of the very best right there; that's why I asked them to join, and why I matched them up the first week. You guys did not disappoint!
As for the rest of you f*ckin' clowns... ;) I'm pleased with the output thus far. The RPs themselves have been fantastic; you guys deserve credit for writing killer RPs using brand new characters, that's not an easy thing. But we can do a little better in terms of quantity; I'd like to see everyone hit 2 RPs for the cycle.
In my case, Mike and I will have the card up EARLY (most likely), since I've already written the show opening, certain segments, etc. Pretty much everything that could be written at this point, has been written. I'm confident we can get the matches done before next Friday, and you'll be able to read the card earlier than planned.
If an NLW card is ever late, feel free to write me an angry "F*CK YOU WHERE IS IT???" message, as it's only fair. You guys work and put in the effort, and it's up to me and my cardwriter(s) to reward you with a quality, on-time card.
...and trust me, I think you're really, really gonna like FNV 01. So get back to writing! :eek: -Billy DWoods — 5 January 2010 <3 There. Take my heart. :o You scallawag. JLevinson — 6 January 2010 Aw, gee, shucks, boss... ya didn't have to say all that...
