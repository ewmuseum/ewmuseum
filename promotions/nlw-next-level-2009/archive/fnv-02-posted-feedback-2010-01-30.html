Did I mention LEAVE FEEDBACK...? -Billy RStrawsma — 30 January 2010 Match of the Night Bobby Jack Windham vs. "Electric" Eddie Patton. Windham's prayer midway through the match was ficking Conan.
Face of the Night Impulse. Pulls off a tremendous main event victory. Definitely the face of the federation at this point.
Heel of the Night Bobby Jack Windham. You can't help but hate him right now. Wrestler of the Night Impulse again. His win was the culmination of a huge main event. Segment of the Night Canyeta and Arcangel IV beat down the random luchadore. I thought the mic spot that followed really established Canyeta as a power player in the tournament to declare the first Open Weight Champ.
Quote of the Night Mylde, talking about the outcome of the Scheinberg/Wanderlust match: "It was like Paperboy in there! Nipple rings, Chocolate milk, Mordy Rosenberg…who could win under such conditions?!"
Other Praise -Wanderlust's mane getting struck by a quart of chocolate milk thrown from the audience made me LMAO.
-Loved Chief Big Bet's debut. Love the character, up and down... from the entrance music to the "Bow to the Chief!" zinger. Everything is hilarious.
-The writing in this show is all very good and consistent. I mean, seriously... it could be the drugs talking, but the matches were all evenly paced, and padded with the right amount of segments to break up the action. That, and all the matches were well-written... whoever contributed to this deserves kudos. Every match seemed to accurately portray a tinge of the off-the-wall personalities involved.
