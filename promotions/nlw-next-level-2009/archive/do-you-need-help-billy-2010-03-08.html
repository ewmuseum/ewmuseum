Also, my computer exploded. No, I'm kidding, none of that stuff happened. Grad school has been killing me with work, and it seems like every time I sit down to try and write, I have something else I need to do. After Wednesday, my schedule gets a little easier in terms of work since I'll be turning a sh*tload of stuff in, and I'm hoping that by then I'll have FNV 03 up. Then, [name withheld] and I will sprint to get FNV 04 finished. When that happens, I'll announce some mild, mild (Mylde?) changes to NLW. In short, I will need more help in the future, but not much. Maybe a third matchwriter to contribute something for each card, ya know? Also, I quickly realized that sticking to such a tight schedule is probably unrealistic, and that while we're still gonna do our best to get everything out bi-weekly, sometimes more time will be needed.
I really do apologize for the lateness; it's usually not like me. Everything will get back on track here, I promise.
