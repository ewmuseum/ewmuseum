LQJT86C — 12 February 2010 I, [name withheld], have f*cked up. (SHOCK AND AWE!) ...so the card's gonna be late. But, not to fear, we're still on schedule, because the FNV 04 thread has gone up, aka the Golden Boy GP. FNV 03 really doesn't impact FNV 04 too much since it's separate segments of the roster competing, so this isn't too big a deal. Unfortunately, the last couple days have been busy school-wise, and the next few are gonna be even busier with visitors coming to the house and the evil known as Valentine's Day (which my GF and I are celebrating a day early), thus rendering your Prez completely incapacitated for the entirety of Saturday.
In all honesty, I could've gotten a rush job up on time, but I feel that a tournament should be of a higher quality. No, it's not going to be 250 pages, not even close; the first round is shortformed. However, there's more "layer and fabric" to the show, and if I rushed it, it wouldn't have had that special touch I'd like to think FNV has had thus far. FNV 03 will go up TUESDAY at the latest, my word. In the meantime, the Golden Boys don't have to wait as the thread is already up. See the lineup post for more info.
When corners have to be cut, they'll be cut, but in this case, I don't mind making you wait a few extra days if the schedule doesn't have to be sacrificed. We're still on schedule, so it's all good, and I apologize for the lateness. If this were just a normal FNV, I would've given you the ****ty version and just moved on to the next card, but in this case, I felt it waiting was a better option, especially since it's only a few days. Mike wrote his matches on time, so this is on me. This will not be a regular occurrence.
-Billy LQJT86C — 17 February 2010 Yeahhh...not so much. Guess my word is sh*t and all. Give me 'til Thursday, possibly Friday. For me, a week late is inexcusable, but things keep happening which are delaying me. No, my family hasn't died, my computer hasn't crashed, my house hasn't burned down, my girlfriend hasn't left me...hell, I'm not even employed at the moment. But school is kicking my ass, and last second things keep coming up. This won't be a continuous problem, as I the bi-weekly FNV TV show isn't so important that I need to delay it for quality purposes, but for the tournament and future PPVs, if there's something keeping me from putting in a decent effort, I don't mind being just a little late. I hope this isn't killing anybody's will to write; it's only a week, and because of the nature of the tournament it isn't setting us off schedule. The RP deadline is also Friday, so I wanna see more RP out of the Golden Boys. Then, Zeus willing, we'll get the other part of the tournament up on schedule next Friday and be back on a torrid pace.
Again, my apologies, just bare with it and please know that this isn't the norm... fugginVOSS — 17 February 2010 Sorry if my Jeebus stuff is a little inconsistent... Finding my feet with the character. PaulNJ21 — 17 February 2010 Life happens, don't worry about it....I'm in NLW for the long haul.
