LQJT86C — 5 December 2010 With the PPV over and finished, my plan was to put the league on hold until our next big step was ready to be announced. Without spoiling too much, we are looking to do a big, long-term collaboration with NFW. At the moment, NFW is lagging, and I'm not sure what's going on. They still have a couple of cards that need to come out.
Until then, I was thinking of running a December card in the vein of a straight to DVD special event. Five matches, featuring our biggest stars, headlined by Magnus vs. Zesty for the Open Weight title. I'd like to get Patton and Windham on the card, Scheinberg, and even Wanderlust if Devin is up for it. RP deadline would be a week, 2-max RP threads, and a commitment to get this thing out on time at all stops. If a match isn't RP'd for, it doesn't get written. My goal would be to have it out before December ends.
So what do you guys think? I won't book it if no one's got the time, or isn't interested. 5KnuckleSammich — 5 December 2010 Frisco Jones clearly be yo biggest star so he gon' be on it, fa'sho. PaulNJ21 — 5 December 2010 I'm in, it has been awhile since I've had a chance to RP. RStrawsma — 5 December 2010 Magnus is getting fat off of the roasted children of Impulse left over from his Thanksgiving celebration. I say give him a reason to shed some pounds and book a show.
Throbbin Wood — 5 December 2010 I would be up for it depending on when the RP deadline is. I would be down for anything after the 20th.
LQJT86C — 7 December 2010 Alright, between the replies here and the ones I received via PM, we have more than enough to run a card. Any other takers? Last chance to get in on this before I post the card tomorrow.
Going once? Going twice? Devin, are you out there? I demand a Wanderlust appearance! batman cool — 7 December 2010 alex austin=in JLevinson — 7 December 2010 Patton is in.
