-Billy JLevinson — 13 January 2010 F*cking awesome. Just awesome. Great, great debut card. Good matches, unbelievable results that could've gone either way. Mad props to Devin for absolutely bringing the heat... I was blown away.
**************SPOILER ALERT****************** The arrival of Impulse and Canyeta was beyond huge. Just great, great stuff. I thought I marked pretty good for Impulse, but when canyeta came out I was wanted to start chanting HOLY SH*T! HOLY SH*T! at my computer screen.
The fake commercials are f*cking hilarious. I don't really have anything bad to say about the card. It was exactly what you want in a debut card: exciting, not too ridiculous, and it sets the stage for what's to come.
Great job to everyone involved and thanks again to Devin for kicking serious ass as per usual.
Throbbin Wood — 13 January 2010 We laughed, we cried, and the golden dreidel was a nice touch to an odd showdown. The rest of the show was good too, but you don't need me to tell you that, do you? Can't wait to get going and see where the next show takes us to.
Maybe Eli and Windham can have a holy war? Christ Lovers vs. Christ Killers. Bring the family for that one!

Seth — 14 January 2010 ---Immediately, I was drawn into the show with the opening. The show flowed very well together and I had a few lawl moments with the announce team. Even the fake commercials were great. Little things like the gritty old-school feel of the place are awesome to see play out.
--As I told Billy over AIM, LOVED the segment with Bobby Jack Windham bashing the crowd. --Impulse as a "big name" coming out to the show was a lot of fun and with Pete on the roster, that gives us more competition. Damn you all!
--To the person who wrote Gravender/Ford/... awesome. --HOT match between Wanderlust/Patton and either one of those could've easily won the match. Devin and Josh are easily two of the front-runners to be inaugural champion and that hurts the rest of us.
Overall, fantastic show. I look forward to seeing how well other characters do in the weeks to come as we march towards the OWGP.
PurpleBlueberry — 14 January 2010 Really great card, particularly cohesive and confident, especially for a debut with so many new rasslers.
Loved the Canyeta appearance, and dig the angle. Commercials are a riot, can't beat mysogeny and racism - it's new yawk! Also liked the feel of the crowd, it's easy to see them and the ballroom itself working out as another character.
The not-so-subtle message sent in the Gravender/Ford match was another highlight for me, "not doing the job on rp means your dude's ass is doing the job on the card" as well as the opposite being true with Wanderlust and Patton really putting on a show and both getting heat from the crowd.
