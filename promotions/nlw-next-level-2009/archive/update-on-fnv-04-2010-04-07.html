Update on FNV 04

7 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LQJT86C — 7 April 2010 What, you thought we had taken so long with FNV 03 that we'd die after FINALLY posting it? Nope, 'cause FNV 04 is very, very close to being done, and we only started it yesterday. Now I'm not gonna give you a strict date on when it comes out, since I already shot my credibility up last time I tried that. But I'd be shocked if it didn't get posted in the next few days.
WHAT'S DONE: -Intro -Prologue -1st Round -Two commercials -2nd Round WHAT'S NEEDED: -Finals This thing is way ahead of where we were a week into the writing process for FNV 03. Like I laid out in a previous post, we can't stick to that crazy schedule we set out to meet, at least not all the time (especially not during busy school months for me). But I'm fairly certain we can be consistent as say, EPW (who have pretty much been the standard for consistency)...maybe even slightly faster.
So don't jump ship, and get ready to be blown away by some of the stuff we have written. If you haven't already done so (you haven't), please read FNV 03/King of all Monsters GP and leave feedback. It's the shortest card we've done, and it won't take long to read I promise. One person ([name withheld]) has left feedback, and well...you know how I feel about feedback. Makes my nut hairs stand at attention and sh*t.
-Prez person LQJT86C — 13 April 2010 Everything's finished, except for the main event which Mike swears on his life will be done today. And damn it, I for one believe him! Expect this thing posted today or tonight. Can't wait either, 'cause you guys are gonna love this one.
