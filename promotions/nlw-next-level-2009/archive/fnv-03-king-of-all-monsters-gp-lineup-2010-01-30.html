FNV 03/KING OF ALL MONSTERS GP LINEUP

LQJT86C — 30 January 2010 Where for ONE NIGHT...mortal men become GODS! (CUTTO: Gray faded shot of MAGNUS DESTRUCTO, VIC GRAVENDER, YUTAKA MAEDA, DUKE MACKEY, and ZESTY MORDANT, standing shoulder to shoulder...some with their arms crossed, others with hands on hips. Below them is a graphic reading "WHO WILL BE THE KING OF ALL MONSTERS?
FRIDAY NIGHT VULGAR! KING OF ALL MONSTERS GRAND PRIX! ONE NIGHT ONLY! Featuring... 'The Watertown Wrecking Ball' Vic Gravender 'Dangerous' Duke Mackey 'The Dreaded Devourer' Magnus Destructo Yutaka Maeda 'The Blue Bastard' Zesty Mordant Dane Lauritsen Rory Henderson 'The Polish War Machine' Hans Nowack FRIDAY, February 5th for the TELEVISION TAPINGS to be aired on February 12th! Hammerstein Ballroom, Manhattan Center- 311 W. 34th St. 11 PM to 12 AM **** 21+ ONLY! **** $25-$75 -------------------------------------------- OORP: RP deadline is Saturday, February 6th at 4:00am PM EASTERN TIME. Normally the deadline's Friday, but since I didn't get a chance to post the lineup around midnight, I won't jip you guys out of the few extra hours. ;-) As you might have noticed, there aren't any set matches. That's because the RP thread will take place battle royal style, and we'll figure out brackets on the actual card. Card will be posted on Friday, February 12th.
SAVE HANS NOWACK! SPECIAL OFFER! Hans is getting jobbed mercilessly unless YOU save him! We're throwing him in the tournament as a stock character, who will job unless someone steps in to RP him. First come, first serve. You jump in and do a Hans RP, he's yours, and you've got as good a chance of winning as anyone else. Hans is a big polish dude, known for completing impossible feats of strength. His manager, "Miss Ann", would do the bulk of his promos for him. But hey...do what you want with him!
Standard 48 hour stack rules apply, though I hope some crazy bastard will be rapid fire RPing to keep everyone on their toes! (That means YOU [name withheld]!)
Throbbin Wood — 31 January 2010 Bastard... Should I get bored before the deadline, you tempt me with "save Hans Nowack." I'll talk to TheDamnGame and see if he has time to burn. Probably not but it would humour him, surely.
LQJT86C — 31 January 2010 Definitely. I don't think any league has done something like this before, and it's a fun opportunity for somebody to jump into the mix. Optimally, I'd like for someone new to try him out, but if your friend or someone else can't do it, feel free to jump in yourself.
TheDamnGame — 31 January 2010 This is definitely interesting. I shall think about this.. this sounds like a guy that wouldn't be a jobber if he had someone RP'ing him. And I personally haven't rp'ed anywhere in months. Saving a character.. also intrigues me. As I've never seen that anywhere. In any league. Anywhere.. So .. it sounds like fun.
LQJT86C — 31 January 2010 Cool. Well, don't think about it too long, cause the deadline's next week. ;-) Jump right in and get those lazy bastards going!
LQJT86C — 2 February 2010 Hey don't all jump in at once!
