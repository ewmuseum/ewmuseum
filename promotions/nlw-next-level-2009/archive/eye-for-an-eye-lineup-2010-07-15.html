LQJT86C — 15 July 2010 Oh, we've spared no pains and we've spared no dough; And we've dug at the secrets of long ago; And we've risen to Heaven and plunged below, For we wanted to make it one hell of a show. -Charles G. Finney, The Circus of Dr. Lao (EVENT POSTER: Black background, featuring the above quote and pictures of IMPULSE and EDDIE PATTON lining the left side; MAGNUS DESTRUCTO and BOBBY JACK WINDHAM lining the right.
