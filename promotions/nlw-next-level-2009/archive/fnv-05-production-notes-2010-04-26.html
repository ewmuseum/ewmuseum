FNV 05 Production Notes

LQJT86C — 26 April 2010 Alright, so the deadline passed and now we're back to card production. I noticed a couple of you RP'd 10-15 minutes past the deadline. This is more my fault for not locking the thread after deadline, but in this happens in the future, I'll give your opponent the extra 24 hour window to reply. I'm not going to complain though, as I'd rather have you RP late than not at all. Great job all around, I was pleased with the amount and quality of RP posted.
As for FNV 05, I'm not going to promise you it'll be here by Friday. I'm hoping me and Mike can make a major dent in it this week, but with the amount of work I have coming up, you might have to wait a little longer. Once FNV 05 goes up, the next show is our first PPV, EYE FOR AN EYE.
-Billy
