Harry Holocaust

John Doe — 22 December 2009 NLW Singles Bio Name: Holocaust Harry Height: 5’7 Weight: 124 Hails From: Parts Unknown Music: In The House, Without A Heartbeat By John Murphy Physical Appearance: http://img.dailymail.co.uk/i/pix/2007/11_01/daysofnightDM_468x555.jpg He wears the rags of a former Nazi concentration camp prisoner, as his body is dirty from no wash or bath, blood all over his face with the looks of a zombie. He is a white man, with short hair.
Ring Attire: Same as above. In-Ring Style: He is a scrapper, no wresting experience this zombie runs aimlessly around the ring and attempts to just mutilate his opponent with rage.
Entrance: CUE UP: In The House, Without A Heartbeat By John Murphy as all the lights dim out and turn to a blood red, the theme picks up as a man walks out from the curtain blood running from his mouth and growling as he charges at the ring at full sprint.
Moveset: Clawing of the face, missile drop kick, charge tackle to the knee, jump on the back and choke out, eye gauge
Finisher: Infected – biting of the neck till opponent taps out. Personality:Harry Holocaust has no personality, his mind is infected with a virus unknown to man.
Background: US Government Document 12084-AX3 Pentagon System File 4418-AWL-3 Threat Level Orange. Subject Name: Unknown. Case Name: Harry Holocaust. Per DR. Gespei US Army Core, unknown male found in Auschwitz in a containment area. During further assessment we find the subject to be infected with some virus unknown at this time. Subject has been taken to US Pentagon for further assessment.
UNKNOWN DATE: Project has been terminated. Throbbin Wood — 22 December 2009 I don't care what anybody says, I love it. I must admit, I expected the guy to be an actual Nazi and laughed at the name. It sounded like something The Sex Pistols would do. Oh well, I like the zombie concentration camp prisoner thing. His grunts and groans are sure to electrify many arenas to come.
