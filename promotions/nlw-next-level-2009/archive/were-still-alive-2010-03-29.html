We're still alive

29 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LQJT86C — 29 March 2010 It's true, we are. Card will be up later, but don't expect anything of mammoth proportions. My lateness with this thing has been a complete disgrace, and I should be shot in the face for it. Fortunately for me, the chances any of you will do it are slim to none, except maybe for [name withheld] who might actually run into me at a Mastodon concert next month in CT.
No excuses, no "my entire family died in a plane crash so it'll be done next week" bullsh*t. Legit real world stuff caused it to be delayed the extra two weeks, and after that it was just good ol' fashion procrastination. In the future, we might not be able to meet the hardcore schedule I laid out for us in the beginning, unless maybe a third writer comes aboard (that's your opening!), but either way you CAN expect that these cards will be up in a timely fashion.
My creative intuition for card writing has returned, thanks in no small part to these two orange kittens my girlfriend gave me. I picked them up last night, and named them both Odin. Together, they are The Odins, and they've given me new inspiration. So, together with the power of The Odins, I will present FNV 03 in it's entirety...later on this evening.
Also...keep an open mind while reading the main event. -Your loving Prezident RStrawsma — 29 March 2010 You missed the awesomeness that is Mastodon by about six years.
