LQJT86C — 9 May 2010 Just checking in to let you guys know FNV 05 is still a work in progress, and it will probably be out in the next week or two. I would say to look for it around the 16th/17th. Reason being, this is my last week of school, and will be my busiest (the last two weeks were crazy as well). Unfortunately I wound-up no-showing PRIME and doing very little for the last EPW and A1E cards, not to mention being late with my match writing for NFW, so my school schedule has spilled over everywhere else as well.
No worries though, I knew this would be an issue as soon as I posted the lineup. It's an "expected lateness" so to speak. If there's any way I can get this up earlier, I will. Once FNV 05 is up, it'll be my priority to have a quick turnaround for the PPV.
In the meantime, post some storyline promos if you wish, and come up with ideas for PPV matches/feuds you'd like to see.
-Billy LQJT86C — 5 June 2010 All segments for the show have been submitted, and it's basically on me to finish the show at this point. Look for it in the next week.
