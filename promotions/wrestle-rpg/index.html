Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

WRESTLE_RPG entered FidoNet's own EchoList by May 1996, a wrestling role-play echo with no traffic of its own recorded anywhere in this project's search. The richest surviving account of what it would have played like comes from a different board entirely: on 236 FANTASY SPORTS, an ExecPC BBS echo for general fantasy-sports talk, a would-be participant pitched the game on 10 June 1996, describing wrestlers created up to four at a time, tag teams built from them, a manager, chosen moves, in-character “Trash Talk,” and a champion's belt — and asked sysops directly to carry the echo he was recruiting for.

The pitch drew one taker within two weeks, and drew the board's own moderator's attention just as fast. Wrestling and role-play were both ruled off-topic for FANTASY_SPORTS within days, a boundary the moderator enforced twice more that summer against a second poster who pushed back at length — that wrestling was as much a sport as any other fantasy simulation, and deserved the same discussion room. The moderator's own answer named the policy as the echo's founder's, not his, and pointed the argument at exactly the room built for it: “If you want to run a Wrestling RPG, try the Wrestling echo.”

WRESTLE_RPG reached FidoNet's backbone five weeks after the pitch, added 15 July 1996 under a listing all in exclamation points: “WRESTLE_RPG !!!!! WRESTLING ROLE PLAYING GAME !!!!!” Its own directory entry billed it plainly — “Wrestling RPGs & Fantasy Echo… to play Wrestling Role Playing Games and for fantasy discussion. Open access to all.” — carried under FidoNet's real-names flag, at a modest twenty messages a week, out of two small single-sysop boards, one in Dallas and one in a small Kentucky town.

Traffic went quiet that April, and the backbone dropped the echo on 6 July 1997 for low volume since the first — not quite a year after it was added. Its own EchoList listing outlived the carriage by two years, standing unremoved through the July 1999 edition and gone by August's. A search across every rescued FidoNet archive this project could reach — the largest surviving mirror of the network's own message base, the Usenet side of its gateway, every local index in hand — turned up not one message the echo itself carried. What survives of WRESTLE_RPG is entirely its own paperwork, and the argument, on somebody else's board, over whether it should exist at all.

No wrestling role-play survives to recount Written by AI, 2026

Every document WRESTLE_RPG's own record holds is paperwork about the echo itself — its own backbone add and drop, its own directory entry — or an argument, on a different board entirely, over whether it should carry the game at all. No match was ever staged in what survives, no wrestler named beyond the abstract count a recruiting pitch promised (“up to 4 wrestlers… 2 tag teams… 1 manager”), and no story to recount.
