The SHOW / The Sandy Hook Outlaws of Wrestling — Founding FAQ

The SHOW The Sandy Hook Outlaws of Wrestling -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 1. What is this fed? -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= It's an e-wrestling federation, duh! Actually, to be more specific, it's an on-card e-wrestling federation with a little twist. It's a tiny, tiny fed. No TV deal. No huge audiences. No big salaries. The shows will be either on tape or an internet report. Which means, shows will be outdated by the time they're viewed. The touring schedule isn't firm at all. The shows are booked as interest and the financials dictate. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 2. Who runs this gig? -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Whit McCoy is the owner and operator of The SHOW. He's a semi-wealthy business man who made his small fortune from the coal mines of Eastern Kentucky. Wrestling is a business he's always been fascinated with... but he's always been too cheap with his cash. He wants a global federation... but he'll only foot a big enough bill for a tiny little indy fed. So, he formed a wrestling company in his hometown of Sandy Hook, Kentucky. Population: 991. It has a strong following from the locals, since it's something to do in the middle of nowhere... but outside of it's home base, the following is very, very weak. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 3. What are the specs? -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= First off, SHOW is a summary fed with fully written 'supercards'. Basically, we're going to run this thing as if you were a casual follower of The SHOW. 85-90% of the shows will be internet recaps: This means, it will be a show report, detailing what all went down at the card. It will be sprinkled with excerpts from the promos, that will be weaved into the results. 10-15% of the shows will be 'supercard' caliber' shows. We'll have one every 7-8 shows or so. Just depends. Basically, they'll be fully written shows that will be presented as if they were on a tape you just popped into your VCR. They won't be 'live' and they won't be broadcast on TV. We might get into the internet broadcasting side of things on down the line... but for now? The SHOW is ran through tapes and internet reports. So, now you might be wondering... on these summary cards, all you use are small bits and pieces from the rp's? What will happen, is there will be an on-card packet that will come out after the show. It will essentially be a roleplay packet. From those rp's, I will take the meat of each roleplay and weave it into the results as if it were being reported. You can still do in-rings and/or strats and run-in attacks. You can still do vignettes and all of that stuff. They just won't be on The SHOW itself, in full. They'll be spliced and weaved into the summary card and then later, in the packet will be released in full. Now, the tricky part, is that these roleplays are being done _live_ and on the event itself. So, they will all be backstage pieces. The deal is, they won't be broadcast live [OOC, they will be sent via a packet, though] and thus, they're summarized just like the matches on the card itself. On Supercards? The oncards go right on the card itself. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 4. Staff -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= President : Whit McCoy Announcers : Henry Voss Tony Miracle Interviewers: Terry 'Gramps' Witzel Steve Kendrick -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 5. The Shows -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The SHOW: It will be released on Monday nights and is our internet recap show. It will generally consist of 3-5 fights. It will be entirely summary based and might weigh in at about 20k total. SHOW Offs: The post-card roleplay show for the fed. This is where you build angles, roleplay and run your trap. You can also do post-show roleplays for this as well, if you want. And ofcourse, we'll have a sprinkling of supercards here and there. They'll all be named at a later date, but they will be fully written. However, don't look forward to seeing monster shows. 50k will be the norm here. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 6. Title(s) -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Right now we just have one title in SHOW. it will probably stay that way, because we won't be a huge fed. The title is simply called the Showcase Strap. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 7. The Low down -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Ok, if you've made it this far and you're OK with the following: - SHOW is a summary/commentary based fed. - SHOW is an oncard only fed. - SHOW is a tiny fed and we don't welcome established characters. - SHOW is pyro, big entrance & millionaire free. - SHOW isn't on any kind of firm schedule. - SHOW is for the angle minded. : then you're ready to take a look at the app, which is below. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 8.
