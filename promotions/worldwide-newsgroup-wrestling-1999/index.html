Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Worldwide Newsgroup Wrestling opened with a single sign-up post on 28 August 1999, inviting readers to reply with a user name and a wrestling alias: “WORLDWIDE NEWSGROUP WRESTLING needs you to join in and kick some butt!!!!!! Sign up here with you user name and wrestling alias,then in 1 week i'll set up a card and get the matches going!”

Its own format ran entirely in thread: each match unfolded over a week of back-and-forth replies between the two entrants, opened with “some mic action,” with interference open to “just about anybody who is listed and involved,” and its self-declared referee judging the winner on the words and actions posted rather than any dice or stat system. It planned tag matches, triple threats and battle royals as needed, men and women competing at the same level, and four championships still to be decided — an Internet Heavy Weight Champion, a Newsgroups Choice Champion, a Net Tag Team Championship and a Hardcore Net Champion.

No card, result, or further post survives beyond that founding call; whether it drew entrants or ran a single match isn't in the surviving record.
