Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Revolutionary Wrestling Federation opened on 27 September 1998 from Madison Square Garden, New York, its own announcers declaring on air, “It's time for the Revolution to begin… LET THE RWF BEGIN!!!!!” The card doubled as the first round of a sixteen-man World Championship tournament, run over consecutive weekly broadcasts it called Saturday Supreme.

Jack Vars and D.B Bugg both advanced through two rounds unbeaten; “Da Greatest Ever” Shawn Walsh was granted a bye straight into the semifinal after his own opening win. The tournament's own semifinal and final are not attested anywhere in the surviving record, nor is the announced 25 October pay-per-view, Dead…or Alive!!!, that the promotion was building toward from a graveyard-set video teaser.

The same card's undercard turned on “Superstar” Tyrone Parker, who no-showed two scheduled matches in a row only to jump his opponent from behind after each one, and was fired on air by the promotion's own president for it. Advertised anyway as the ten-man battle royal's own “mystery man” for the promotion's first National Championship, still under termination, Parker outlasted nine other wrestlers — including two who beat each other by mistake, his own two replacements, and a late pairing of Ravage and Pirate Jean Jacque Lamaire — to become champion at eighteen years old.

The surviving record runs to 3 October 1998, the promotion's own second Saturday Supreme, still counting down to its announced pay-per-view. Nothing records whether that event happened or how the promotion's run continued past it.
