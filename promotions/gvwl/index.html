Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The GVWL ran its entire card from the real, contemporaneous WWF roster and let its readers decide every match by mail-in ballot. Its earliest surviving document, 10 July 1997, opens its own “Tournament Festival” series: a ballot for single-elimination World, Tag Team and Cruiserweight title tournaments, votes mailed to its president and results published back to voters and to the rec.sport.pro-wrestling and rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy newsgroups alike.

Three straight rounds narrowed those brackets — first-round results on 16 July, a second round on 24 July that also opened a fourth, Intercontinental tournament, and a third round on 4 August that crowned Tag Team champions (the Outsiders) and Cruiserweight champion (Sabu) while opening a fifth, Television Championship tournament.

Its 19 August “Hot Summer Battle” card crowned the rest: the closest vote of its run, a 4-4 World Title Tournament final, went to the Undertaker over Stone Cold Steve Austin by Tombstone; the Outsiders held the tag belts against the Headbangers; and Chris Benoit took the Cruiserweight title from Sabu in a cage rematch. The same card carried a straight elimination Death Match and a “Loser Leaves the Fed Forever” stipulation match that voted Savio Vega out of the promotion for good.

Two further cards kept turning its title matches into voted gimmick stipulations — a Casket Match, a Chicago Street Fight, a Ladder Match and an “I Quit” Match on “No Rules in Late Summer,” 9 September, and a sprawling multi-way Television Title vote among sixteen names on “For Whom the Bell Toes,” 23 September.

No results ballot for either of those two later cards survives, and the promotion's own record ends on that open 23 September vote; whether either card was ever decided isn't in the surviving record.
