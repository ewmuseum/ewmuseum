Results: GVWL Tournament Festival Part I

16 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GVWL Tournament Festival Part 1: World Title Tournament: Shawn Michaels bt. Raven (4-3) A very close contest developed into a massive brawl, but finnaly Michaels was able to connect with the Superkick and pinned Raven The Giant bt. Arn Anderson (5-2) Arn Anderson fought the Match of his life, but the Giant was too strong and performed his chokeslam Sting bt. Jeff Jarret (7-0) Sting dominated the Match all the way and pinned Jarret after only 5 Minutes Stone Cold S.A. bt. Ahmed Johnson (7-0) Another one-sided Match, which came to an end when Stone Cold performed the Stone Cold Stunner Hulk Hogan bt. Randy Savage (6-1) Hogan used all the help he could get from outside and pinned Savage after hitting him with brass knuckles The Undertaker bt. Ric Flair (6-1) Not even his cheapshots could help Ric Flair here, he got Tombstoned and pinned Lex Luger bt. Golddust (4-3) A very close matchup, Golddust went for the Curtain Call, but Luger blocked it and made Golddust submit with the Torture Rack Bret Hart bt. Vader (4-3) One of the big surprises, after Bret Hart was not able to connect with the Sharpshooter Vader went for the Moonsault but missed, which enabled Bret to get the pin. Tag Team Title Tournament: The Outsiders bt. Public Enemy (6-1) After dominating PE completely, Hall went for the Outsiders Edge, and pinned Rocko Rock Steiner Brothers bt. Harlem Heat (5-2) The Steiners got a lucky win here, after Sherri tried to interfere, the Steiners used her shoe and pinned Booker T L.O.D. bt. Owen Hart/B. Bulldog (6-1) Hawk and Animal controlled the Match all the way, and went for the Doomsday Device to pin Owen New Blackjacks bt. Nasty Boys (5-2) Another surprise, Blackjack Windham pinned Saggs after Blackjack Bradshaw illigely interfered to send Knobbs out of the ring Cruiserweight Title Tournament: Rey Misterio jr bt. Syxx (4-3) A great technical Match ended after the ref DQ Syxx when other NWO Members tried to interfere Sabu bt. Rob van Dam (5-2) Sabu used his technichal strenght to pin Rob van Dam Dean Malenko bt. Flash Funk (4-3) Dean Malenko made Flash Funk submit, after a great Match, which saw both Wrestlers have a lot of pin chances Chris Benoit bt. Eddie Guerrero(7-0) What a one sided encounter, Guerrero never got in the Match and was finnaly pinned after 7minutes Thanks everybody for voting, the new card will be out on Friday, hope you all participate again.
