Results: GVWL Hot Summer Battle

19 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GVWL Hot Summer Battle: World Title Tournament FINAL: The Undertaker bt. Stone Cold Steve Austin (4-4) Well, it was the closest outcome possible, but via the Tombstone The Undertaker won, but for all Austin Fans, a Rematch in this situation is sure. Tag Team Title Match: The Outsiders(CH) bt. The Headbangers (8-0) Upps, that was almost no-contest, the Outsiders all the way. Cruiserweigth Title Rematch(Cage Match): Chris Benoit bt. Sabu(CH) (5-3) The Cage was Benoit's Terretory, he won the GOLD held by Sabu, is a third Match necessary to settle this. IC Title Tournament Semifinals: Mr.Perfect bt. Vader (5-3) Perfect took the advantage early on, but Vader had his chance missing with the Moonsault and being pinned afterwards. DDP bt. Ric Flair (5-3) That sets up the DDP vs. Perfect IC Title Match next week. TV Title Tournament Semifinals: Sting bt. Bret Hart (4-4) That was a close matchup, but the late charge by Sting, and the Interference of the Hart Foundation made Sting the winner, but be sure for Bret Hart to get a Title Shot soon. Psycho Sid bt. Owen Hart (6-2) The height of Psycho Sid decided the Match, and gives him a Shgot at the TV Title vs. Sting Death Match: Eliminators bt. The Gangstas (5-3) The Eliminators won the Death Match and might be on the way to a Title Shot. Losser leaves Fed forever Match: Jeff Jarret bt. Savio Vega (8-0) Sorry, Savio, but your way is out, you wanted it you got it, Savio Vega will never Wrestle in the GVWL again Main Event: Special Beach Match(takes place in a Ring filled with Sand): Hollywood Hogan bt. Lex Luger (6-2) What a strange Match, Luger was just to heavy to make his moves in the Sand, will Hogan be aiming at a Title now? Thanks for voting, and one quick reminder, suggestions for Matchups and participating Wrestlers are always welcome. Your President, [name withheld]
