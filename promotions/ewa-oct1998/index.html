Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Extreme Wrestling Alliance introduced itself on 21 October 1998 as “established and running” already, its own president describing a closed-circuit e-mail federation limited to created wrestlers, up to two per roleplayer as either two singles slots or a tag team.

It ran two shows, Wednesday Night War and Saturday Night Showdown, with matches written by its own president rather than resolved through any simulator, and named five championships — World, Tag Team, US, Intercontinental, and Extreme — with no champion yet credited to any of them.

No document survives before or after this recruitment post; nothing establishes when Extreme Wrestling Alliance was actually founded, who its own president was, or whether either show or any of its five titles was ever contested on the page.
