Wanting to join a good E-fed? Tired of those boring feds that waste yoru time? Well, look no longer! Your dreams have come true! The XCWF is the place for every wrestler to get a chance to become a star! If you are experienced then this is the place to go! Everything you need to know is right here! So what are you waiting for?
Rules 1. Cursing is allowed, If people can cuss in the WWF why can't we? 2. NO TOSING! Please don't be someone that is a jerk about the language in the roleplays. I have warned you already. *NOTE* IF anyone is afraid of being tossed put a disclaimer on it.
3. *AND MOST IMPORTANT* ROLEPLAY!!!!! This is the most important part to a fed. Without roleplays, feds just suck. So please do you best and roleplay at least once for each match. I am also a nice person, so if you can't find the time then I understand, but if the problem persists of several weeks than I will have to terminate you. *Several weeks being 4-6 weeks*
4. Don't whin about loosing. If there is one thing I hate it is soar losers. If you lose and want to quit e-mail me only, but I hope that no one stoops that low. 5. Roleplay on time. This one is easy. I give you deadline your roleplay before that, got it. You can still roleplay after wards though. Sometimes I count thos roleplays if they are not too late. It all depends on what mood I am in.
