A fantasy league on the rec.sport.pro-wrestling newsgroup in which readers voted by e-mail on matches between real wrestlers, living and dead, and its host wrote each result up as a narrated bout: a singles and a tag-team tournament from November 1990 to March 1991, then belts of its own defended on numbered cards that spring. Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The RSPW Federation was a fantasy league run on the rec.sport.pro-wrestling newsgroup by a poster who signed himself Phoenix. Its wrestlers were real ones, "a complete mix of ALL(yes, even dead ones such as Brody) wrestlers", in a fantasy world where, as he put it, the WWF, the NWA, the AWA and the USWA all existed at once. He set the matches, readers sent him their votes by e-mail, and he wrote up how each match went: "The matches are real and the fans decide who wins."

It opened on 27 November 1990 with a singles tournament of sixteen wrestlers, and Ric Flair beat Hulk Hogan with the figure four in the final, posted on 28 January 1991. A tag-team tournament of sixteen teams followed from 3 February; on 10 March the Road Warriors beat the Hart Foundation in its final, 22 votes to 18. The next day, agreeing with readers who wanted titles of the league's own, he made the four finalists its first champions: Flair the RSPW World Heavyweight Champion, Hogan the North American Champion, the Road Warriors the World Tag Team Champions and the Hart Foundation the North American Tag Team Champions.

Numbered cards followed, voted the same way. Card #1, at Madison Square Garden in March 1991, put all four belts on the line, Flair defending against Sting, and by 23 April a third card was promised. Other posters ran events of their own under the league and called it the RSPWF: an All-Jobber Battle Royal announced on 18 March 1991, with "a push for some as-yet-unnamed RSPWF title" among its prizes, and in June a regional Maine State Championship, a belt its author had proposed in March, ranked below "the RSPW World or North American Titles".

How it ended is not known. By August 1991 a reader was asking what had happened to "the RSPW federation we had a little while ago". In February 1992 Phoenix wrote that school work had been "getting a bit hectic" and asked whether to get "the RSPW Federation back in action"; that March its World, North American and World Tag Team belts were booked on Dreamcard V, "courtesy of" their host. In 1994 a parody production on the same newsgroup revived the RSPWF name.
