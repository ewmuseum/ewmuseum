RSPW Tag Team Tournament Final Round!!!!!!!

3 March 1991
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is it!! We are down to are last two teams for the final round of the tournament. So, here is the match up: Final Round: ----------- 1) The Hart Foundation vs. The Road Warriors Due to the beating of Doom on Ellering, he will not be at ringside for this match. So, all you need to do is send me your votes and tell me who you think should win the tournament and maybe why they should. Remember, the Harts defeated the Freebirds in Round 1, the Skyscrapers in round 2, and the Midnight Express in round 3. The Road Warriors beat the Rock n' Roll Express in round 1, the Steiners in round 2, and Doom in round 3. Remember this when you are thinking about your votes. So, mail in the votes before Sunday, March 10th and on that day I will post the full match results of the Harts vs. The Roadies. With no more to say, get in those votes!!!!
