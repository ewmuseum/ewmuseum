RSPW Tag Team Tournament Round 2 results!!(LONG!!)

24 February 1991
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yes, I know it's about time that these things got posted, but this was the only chance I had. So with no further interruptions, on with the matches!
Round 2: -------- 1) The Midnight Express vs. The Midnight Rockers(Whichever team loses must drop the Midnight in their name) Jimmy Cornette is announced and takes the mike from the announcer. The music for the Express comes on and Jimmy says, "Now coming down the ring aisle, the greatest tag team alive; the Sultans of Suede, the Experts of Executions, and the TRUE Midnights around; Sweet Stan and Beautiful Bobby, THE MIDNIGHT
EXPRESS!" Bobby and Stan come down to ringside with the fans cheering. AS soon as they reach the ring, their music stops and the Midnight Rockers music come on. Marty and Shawn come down the ring aisle with the fans cheering as well. The bell rings and Bobby, Stan, and Cornette do their typical hug. As soon as Jimmy leaves the ring, Stan and Bobby catch two crescent kicks to the face! Marty and Shawn then slam them. When they get up Bobby gets hit with a double clothesline and is knocked outside the ring. Stan gets whipped to the rope and catches a double elbow. The ref finally gets Marty out and Shawn places Stan in an armbar. He tags to Marty and he comes off the top with an elbow to the arm. Marty gives a twist and takes Stan down in a fireman's carry. He drops a knee onto the arm and the ref asks Stan if he wants to give up. Stan says no and Marty gets him on his feet. Stan throws a well placed kick into the stomach and comes bouncing off the ropes. Marty catches him in a well placed powerslam and covers for one. Bobby has finally made his way onto the apron. Marty tags in Shawn. Shawn readys Stan for a suplex. Stan blocks it and suplexs Shawn instead. Stan tags to Bobby. Stan whips Shawn to the ropes, leg trips him, and Bobby comes down with an elbow to the back. Bobby then places Shawn in a "camel clutch/chinlock". The ref asks if he wants to give, but Shawn says no. Bobby holds this for a while, but Shawn starts to get up. He makes his way to his knees and grabs Bobby's head. Shawn then quickly snaps down driving Bobby's chin into Shawn's head. Both men are down on the mat. The ref starts counting and each man tries making it to their corner.
Stan gets tagged in first and pounds a fist into Marty's head. Marty tries to come in, but the ref stops him. At this time, Shawn is strung out on the top rope with Bobby holding his legs at the end. Stan comes off the ropes and leap frogs over Bobby onto Shawn's back, while also clotheslining him on the top rope. Cornette also comes over and smashes the racket into Shawn's face. Stan whips Shawn to the ropes and backdrops him. He tags to Bobby. Bobby climbs to the top ready to fly. He hits Shawn with a bodypress from the top. But the ref counts two and a half as Shawn gets out! Bobby infuriated tags to Stan.
They go to double suplex Shawn. But as Shawn is up in the air, Marty comes in and brings him down safely! The Rockers then hit Bobby and Stan with a dropkick. They Rockers get on each man and start pounding fists into their heads. They whip the Express into each other followed by an elbow to the face.
Stan gets pounded into the turnbuckle with such force that he is thrown clear. Bobby is now in the ring with Marty who delivers a beautiful flying elbow. A high knee drop is put into Bobby's head and the tag is made. Both Rockers go to the ropes to fly. Stan comes up behind Marty and pushes him far making him completely miss. Though, Shawn does hit and go for the cover. Cornette distracts the ref and Stan comes in nailing Shawn with the racket to the back to the head. He places Bobby over Shawn and quickly gets out. The ref turns around and counts the pin. Winners: The Midnight Express. Due to the stipulation, the Midnight Rockers change their name to the Rockers.
2) The Skyscrapers vs. The Hart Foundation: The Skyscrapers come down first to ringside with no music, but a serious look in their eyes. The Hart's come down with music and the WWF Tag belts. Brett gets his glasses taken off and gives them to a woman at ringside.
The woman gives Brett a kiss and he goes back into the ring. Before the bell even rings, Sid and Dan charge in. But, the Harts are ready and dodge both men. Brett hits Sid with a dropkick and then a few shots to the head.
Neidhart slams Dan to the mat followed by kicks to the head. The Scrapers retreat and regroup as the Anvil cackles. Anvil starts off against Sid as the bell rings. The lock up and Sid pushes Anvil to the mat with authority. Anvil locks back up and this time slams Sid to the mat! Anvil quickly drops a knee to Sid's head and tags Brett. Brett places on a headlock, but Sid easily gets up and whips him to the ropes. Brett comes back and slides under Sid, tags the
Anvil and they both clothesline Sid. Anvil stomps on Sid and then chokes him while laughing. The ref makes him break and he tags back to Brett. Anvil holds Sid while Brett comes off the top with a beautiful elbow to the head.
Sid drops and Brett covers for one. He holds Sid in a front face lock to wear him down. Sid powers up and reverse atomic drops Brett. He calls for Dan's boot and slams Brett into it. Sid tags to Dan. Dan holds Brett in a front face lock standing and pounds forearm shots into his back until Brett hits the mat. He then rubs Brett's face into the mat and tags back to Sid. They both whip Brett to the ropes and double clothesline him. Sid laughs and picks Brett up. Brett tries to fight back, but to no use. Sid punches Brett in the head and he goes down. The Anvil looks worried knowing that Brett is overpowered easily. Sid bodyslams Brett and then drops a leg. He tags to Dan. Dan takes
Brett in his arms and side suplexs him. He covers, but Brett gets out right before the three count! Dan is steaming and kicks Brett a few times. He tags back to Sid and holds Brett. Sid gets ready for a foot to the face, but Brett slides out of Dan's arms! Sid nails Dan right in the head and Brett flies for the corner making the tag. Anvil comes in and clotheslines Sid and then Dan.
He knocks their heads to together which sends Dan to the outside. Brett goes along the outside and throws Dan into the post which makes him bleed. Anvil whips Sid to the ropes and shoulder tackles him. He covers, but only for two.
Dan and Brett are going at it on the outside and Dan nails Brett with a chair, which send him into next week. Dan comes in and nails Anvil with a forearm.
The ref makes Dan get out and the match under control. Sid tags Dan and they both suplex Anvil. Dan covers, but only for two. Dan drops a few elbows on
Anvil and tags back to Sid. Sid pounds Anvil in the corner and starts lifting him up on the ropes. He gets him sitting on the top turnbuckle for a possible superplex. Dan holds Anvil's tights being sure he won't move. Sid sets it up and hits the superplex on Anvil. Sid being confident doesn't cover! Instead, he goes for the power bomb. But, Anvil rakes Sid's eyes and then low punches him in the groin! Anvil makes his way to the corner, but Brett isn't there!
Dan knowing he clocked Brett laughs. But, what Dan doesn't see is Brett with a chair behind him who clockes Dan into next year! The ref never sees this being too busy watching Sid and Anvil. Right before Sid gets a hold of Anvil, Brett runs to the other side and tags Anvil's hand! Ref calls it a tag and Brett is a house of fire with punches and kicks. He lays a beautiful backbreaker on Sid with an elbow to the head to follow. He slams the big man and tags to Anvil.
Brett slingshots Anvil over the top rope and gets the three count for the win! Winners: The Hart Foundation 3) The Road Warriors vs. The Steiner Brothers: The Steiners come down first with their music and the U.S. Tag belts on their waists. Then, Ozzy's "Iron Man" starts to play and the Road Warriors come flying down ringside. They slip under the ropes to get the attack, but the Steiners expected this and start pounding on the Roadies before they get ever get up! Rick drops a series of headbutts on Animal and Scott kicks and pounds on Hawk. Scott gets Hawk to his feet, hits him with a series of armdrags, and then dropkicks him outside. Animal gets hit with headbutts, forearms, and then a Steinerline. He covers, but only gets one. The bell has rung by now and Scott goes outside and Hawk and him go at it. Hawk throws
Scott into the rinside barrier and then continues to hit him. Rick has Animal in an armbar, but he gets up and whips Rick to the ropes. Animal delivers a perfect dropkick that floors Rick. Animal kicks Rick down and goes to tag
Hawk. But, Hawk isn't their since he is fighting Scott on the outside. Rick takes this advantage of a pause and headbutts Animal in the stomach. He whips him to the ropes and backdrops him followed by and elbow. Rick covers for one.
Scott is hurting and Hawk goes to throw him into the ringpost, Scott reverse this and throws Hawk into the ringside table! Scott slams Hawk and it and it breaks in two. Rick is kicking at Animal by this time and whips him to the ropes. Rick slaps on the bearhug and then belly to belly suplexs Animal! Hawk now gets up and goes to clothesline Scott, but misses. Scott comes back and
Frankenstein's Hawk on the outside floor!! Rick only got two on the count.
Rick belly to back suplexs Animal and hold him for the pin, but on two Animal gets his shoulder up and Rick ends up pinning himself! Though, Animal quickly exits the ring seeing that they both took a bad beating. The Steiners stand proud inside the ring and the Warriors come back inside. Both teams stand there eye to eye and the Warriors extend their hands to the Steiners. The
Steiners accept and the crowd goes completely wild knowing both teams fought hard and well. Winners: The Road Warriors
4) Tully Blanchard and Arn Anderson vs. Doom(Stipulation, J.J. Dillon nor Teddy Long are allowed down at ringside) Tully and Arn come down first with no music. Doom follows with the NWA Tag belts. The bell rings and Tully starts off against Simmons. They lock up and Tully quickly takes him down with a leg sweep and puts him in a chinlock.
Tully tells the ref to check for a submission, but Simmons gets up with Tully on his back and smashes him into the corner. Simmons then takes Tully and hiptosses him to the mat. Tully seems surprised and is reluctant to lock up again. Simmons grabs Tully by the hair and throws him face first into the mat.
Ron tags to Reed. Butch places Tully in a headlock. Tully tries to break out of it, but can not. He finally back suplexs Reed, but he still holds on. They both get up and Tully whips him to the ropes. Reed comes back and Tully drops.
Reed jumps and stomps on Tully's back on purpose. He then comes down and places the headlock back on. Reed tags to Simmons and they whip Tully to the ropes, Tully slides underneath a double clothesline and struts around.
Furious, they grab Tully and slam him into the turnbuckle about 10 times. Finally, Tully finds his way to his corner and tags to Arn. Arn and Simmons go to lock up, but Arn places a boot to his stomach. He gives him a snap mare and holds on a choking chin lock. When the ref can't see, he places his thumb into
Simmons throat. Otherwise, he holds on the chinlock. This is held for a few minutes and then Arn whips him to the ropes. He hits him with an arm buster.
Arn drops a few knees onto Simmons arm and tags Tully. Tully drops an elbow onto the arm from the second rope. Tully twists the arm and tags back to Arn.
Arn goes over and nails Reed. Reed goes to come in, giving Tully and Arn a chance to double team. Arn gets Simmons' legs on his shoulder with Tully holding his head. Arn drops out and Tully slams Simmons face into the mat for a lateral guillotine. Tully covers but only gets two. Tully whips him to the ropes and clotheslines Simmons. Tully follows with a bodyslam and goes for the top rope. Tully goes to fly with a flying elbow and misses as Simmons rolls out. He tags to Reed and Butch comes in a house of fire. Reed starts pounding on Tully and then on Arn. He slams their heads together and Arn hits the canvas. Simmons goes after him on the outside. Tully rakes his eyes and gets the upper hand on Reed. Tully gives him a neckbreaker and then covers for two.
Arn and Simmons are fighting on the outside. Ron picks him Arn and press slams him back through the ropes inside the ring! It quickly becomes a four man brawl. Reed and Arn are now fighting and Tully and Simmons are going at it.
Arn whips Reed to the ropes and hits him with the gourdbuster. He then helps
Tully pound on Simmons. They whips Simmons to the ropes and clothesline him, followed by two elbow drops. Arn goes back to Reed and whips him to the ropes again. Arn drops his head for a backdrop, but Reed DDT's him! Arn is then kicked outside the ring. The ref gets control and Reed is back outside.
Simmons places Tully in a bearhug. Tully tries to fist his way out, but the more he hits Simmons, the tighter Simmons makes it! Simmons tags Reed and gets
Tully higher in the air. Reed mounts the top rope and hits Tully with a flying clothesline while Simmons comes down with all his weight on Tully making him easy prey for the three count. Winners: Doom.
