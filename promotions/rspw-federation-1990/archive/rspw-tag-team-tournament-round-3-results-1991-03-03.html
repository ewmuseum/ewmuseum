RSPW Tag Team Tournament Round 3 results(LONG!)

3 March 1991
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The votes are complied and in so let's go to ringside: Round 3 -------- 1) The Midnight Express vs. The Hart Foundation: Cornette is introduced and then takes the mike. He says, "Now coming down the ring aisle, the greatest tag team alive, and soon to be the NEW WWF
Tag team Champions, Beautiful Bobby and Sweet Stan, The Midnight Express!"
Bobby and Stan come down the ring to cheers from the crowd. As soon as they hit ringside, the Hart Foundation music plays and they come down with the belts. They enter the ring with a huge look of determination. Neidhart takes off Bret's glasses and Bret gives them to a kid at ringside. The bell rings and Neidhart starts off against Stan. They lock up and Anvil tosses him to the mat and cackles at him. Stan complains about a hair pull and they lock up again. Anvil again pushes him to the mat. Stan is getting furious. They both go to lock up, but Stan does a leg sweep and takes Anvil down. He holds on to the leg and twists it a few times. He drops an elbow down and gets up to repeat this about two more times. Stan tags to Bobby. They each grab a leg and eagle split Anvil. Bobby gets the big man up and hits him with a few forearm shots. He whips him to the ropes going for a backdrop, but the Anvil drops an elbow behind his neck. He then gives Bobby a swinging neckbreaker and tags to Brett. The Hitman goes to work starting with a chinlock. He holds this for a few and Bobby gets up and whips him to the ropes. Both men crisscross and the Brett goes down. Bobby comes back and Brett places on an abdominal stretch. Bobby won't give up though. Brett reaches out and tags
Anvil. Anvil comes running and headbutts Bobby in the stomach. Anvil smashes
Bobby's head into a turnbuckle. He whips Bobby to the other corner, but Bobby reverses the whip. Anvil goes hard into the corner and comes out running into a clothesline. Bobby tags Stan. Bobby whips Anvil to the ropes, leg trips him and Stan comes down with a leg drop to the back of Anvil's head. Stan covers, but only gets one. Stan bodyslams him and then drops a high knee into
Neidhart's head. Stan throws him through the ropes to the outside. Stan goes over and slaps Brett as a taunt. Brett comes in, but the ref stops him. Bobby at this time, takes the racket, goes to the top rope and comes down hard on
Anvil's head with the racket. Bobby then slams him into the railing. Stan goes back and pushes Anvil back in the ring. He tags to Bobby. They whip
Anvil to the rope setting up for the "Flapjack". Anvil though leaps on their arms and makes them smash heads together! He goes over and makes the tag to
Brett. Brett comes in as a house of fire smashing both their heads together.
He headbutts Bobby outside of the ring. Stan gets whipped to the ropes and powerslammed perfectly. Anvil gets tagged and he picks up Brett backwards and slams him onto Stan! The ref counts two, but Stan somehow manages to get up.
Anvil tags Brett and places Stan in a backbreaker over his knee. Brett comes off the second rope with an elbow to the throat. Bobby is back on the apron by now. Brett goes over and smashes Bobby in the head. Eaton tries to come in, but the ref stops him. At this time, Anvil is choking Stan while Brett is kicking him viciously. Brett whips Stan to the ropes, but Stan reverses it.
Brett bounces on the ropes, but ends up getting Bobby's knee in his back. Stan comes down dropping an elbow to the head. Stan belly to back suplexs Brett and covers for two. Stan tags in Bobby. Bobby delivers a dropkick to Brett and then chokes him. The ref makes Bobby break. Bobby whips Brett to the ropes and delivers an Eddie Gilbert "Hotshot" clothesline on the top rope. Brett goes down holding his throat. Bobby slams Brett into a neutral corner. He whips him to the otherside, but Brett reverses. Brett whips Bobby into the corner and goes charging in. Bobby ducks and Brett slams himself into the turnbuckle. Bobby tags back to Stan again. Stan goes over and powerslams
Brett into the middle of thr ring. Stan sensing victory tags back to Bobby.
Bobby goes and mounts the top rope for the "Rocket Launcher". Stan goes to help him fly. Bobby flies through the air, but Anvil makes a run in and nails
Bobby in mid air! He then shouldertackles Stan through the ropes onto
Cornette! Anvil goes back and Brett, who was playing oppossum, tags Anvil. Anvil places him in the bearhug, Brett flies off the ropes, and delivers the devastating Hart Attack. Brett covers Bobby for the three count. Winners: The
Hart Foundation.
2) The Road Warriors vs. Doom: "Iron Man" comes blaring over the P.A. and the Road Warriors and Ellering come down to the ring. Then, Doom and Teddy long also come down to ring side. The bell rings and the Road Warriors attack Doom right away. Hawk pounds on Simmons while Animal takes on Reed. Animal devastates Reed with punches and then dropkicks him outside of the ring. Hawk takes Simmons and gives him a neckbreaker. This follows with a fist to the face and a cover of one. Hawk tags to Animal and they both deliver a suplex to Simmons. Animal whips Simmons to the ropes going for a backdrop. Simmons seeing this leapfrogs over Animal and comes back with a bulldog! Simmons kicks Animal in the head and tags to Reed. Reed and Simmons whip Animal to the rope and give him a double elbow. Reed gets Animal up and headbutts him. Animal goes and headbutts Reed back! Neither seem to have any effect on each other. Animal challenges to a test of strength. Reed accepts and they lock up. Animal easily gets the upper hand and drives Reed to his knees. The ref asks if he wants to submit, but Reed says no. Reed then headbutts Animal in the stomach until he lets go. Reed comes bouncing off the ropes for a bodypress. But,
Animal catches him and press slams him into the Road Warriors corner. Animal tags Hawk. They whip Reed to the ropes and go for a double dropkick, but Reed holds on to the ropes and they both miss! Reed calls Simmons in as they double team Hawk and Animal. Reed and Simmons both deliver clotheslines and then belly to back suplexs. They each toss Hawk and Animal outside and follow them out. Each Warrior gets banged into the post and then into the railing.
Simmons and Reed pound on the Warrior's heads and bust both of them open! Reed and Simmons get back in to stop the count and then go out again. Reed and
Simmons go to whip Hawk and Animal into each other, but The Warriors reverse this and whip Reed and Simmons into each other! Then they follow with a double clothesline to the back of their heads. Hawk pushes in Simmons while Animal and Reed fight it on the outside. Hawk suplexs Simmons and the leg drops him.
He whips Simmons to the ropes, but Simmons flies with a beautiful shoulder tackle. He then headbutts Hawk and goes for a cover. The ref counts two, and
Hawk gets out. Animal gets Reed and slams his head into the timekeepers table, knocking him silly. Animal gets back on the apron. Simmons places Hawk in a camel clutch and tries to make him submit. Hawk refuses and starts to power out of it. Simmons holds on tighter and Hawk starts to get winded from working his way out. Animal seeing his partner in trouble goes in and clotheslines
Simmons. On the outside, Ellering and Long are getting into a heated argument. Ellering nails Long and starts to beat on him. But, Reed comes over and pounds on Ellering. Reed holds him so Long can beat on him. Long takes one of the NWA Tag belts and busts Ellering open badly. Inside, Simmons goes to tag Reed, but he's busy fighting Ellering. Hawk makes the tag to
Animal and he comes in pounding on Simmons. He smashes Simmons head into the corner and then whips him to another following in with an elbow. Animal takes
Simmons and powerslams him to the mat. He tags to Hawk who goes for the top rope. Animal gets Simmons on his shoulders and Hawk flies with a clothesline from the top rope. Hawk covers Simmons and gets the three count. But, Reed and Long come in with the belts and pound on Hawk and Animal. Simmons gets up to help as the Road Warriors are taking a beating. Both Warriors are busted open bad and down come the Steiners for the save! Rick "Steinerlines" Reed outside the ring and Scott does the same to Simmons. Long seeing he is in trouble dives outside the ring. Doom hearing they lost the match by pin, walk away arguing the whole way back to the dressing room. The Warriors finally get up and the crowd goes nuts. Ellering is stretchered away as the Steiners help the Warriors back to the dressing room. Winners: The Road Warriors.
