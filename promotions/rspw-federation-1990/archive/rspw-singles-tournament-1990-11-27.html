RSPW Singles Tournament

27 November 1990
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I had come up with this idea over the Thanksgiving break and want to share it with the rest of the readers and posters on this board. I will be posting here a Singles Computer Tournament in which the posters will get to decided who wins the matches! I will give the match ups between wrestlers and ask for people to e-mail(please, DO NOT POST who you think would win) the matches. Each match will be determined by number of votes for each wrestler. I will then post how the match would have taken place and who won. The voting will probably last for about a week at a time or more. Here is Round 1: Round 1: 1) "Mr. Perfect" Curt Henning vs. Sid Vicious 2) Lex Lugar vs. Larry Zybszko(sp?) 3) Ric Flair vs. Randy "Macho Man" Savage 4) Nikita Koloff vs. Jerry "The King" Lawler 5) Hulk Hogan vs. Arn Anderson 6) Andre the Giant vs. Barry Windham 7) The Ultimate Warrior vs. Stan "the Lariat" Hansen 8) Sting vs. Kerry Von Erich There was alot of other wrestlers I knew I could have placed in the tournament, but I thought these were the best ones to use. Now, all you need to do is e-mail who you think will win each match. If you like, describe the match to me or tell me why you think a certain person would win over the other. Respective things should be remembered such as Henning has Heneen(sp) at ringside, Savage has Sherri Martel, and certain wrestlers(i.e. The Four Horsemen) are apt to cheat more than others. The voting period for Round 1 will end this Sunday, December 1st. I will at that time post winners, how they won, and the matches for Round 2. I will hopefully have a RSPW Tag Team Tournament in the making. Have fun and send in those votes!(E-mail only!)
