RSPW Tag Team Tournament

3 February 1991
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After the humungous success the RSPW Singles Tournament had, I am now *VERY* proud and pleased to bring you the RSPW Tag Team Tournament! This is a battle between the best 16 tag teams in wrestling to see who is number one. The best part is you get to vote who wins each match! I took the teams from popularity and success of the teams to see who should be in this tournament. Also, last semester I had asked people to send me a list of which 16 teams they would like to see in this tournament, and took those votes into consideration. Here are the 16 teams participating in round 1 of this tournament: 1) Doom: Ron Simmons & Butch Reed. NWA Tag Team Champs. 2) The Hart Foundation: Bret Hart & Jim "the Anvil" Neidhart. WWF Tag Team Champs. 3) D.J. Peterson and The Trooper. AWA Tag Team Champs. 4) The Steiner Brothers: Rick & Scott Steiner. U.S. Tag Team Champs. NWA 5) The Road Warriors: Hawk and Animal. WWF. 6) Tully Blanchard & Arn Anderson. Last affiliation: WWF 7) The Midnight Express: "Beautiful" Bobby Eaton & "Sweet" Stan Lane. Manager: Jimmy Cornette. Last affiliation: NWA 8) Demolition: Ax & Smash. WWF 9) The Bladerunners: Sting & The Ultimate Warrior. Last affiliation: UWF 10) The British Bulldogs: Davey Boy Smith & The Dynamite Kid. Last affiliation CWA. 11) The Midnight Rockers: Marty Jannetty & Shawn Michaels. WWF. 12) The Rock n' Roll Express: Ricky Morton & Robert Gibson. NWA. 13) The Skyscrapers: Sid Vicious & "Dangerous" Dan Spivey. Last affiliation: NWA. 14) The Superpowers: Dusty Rhodes & Nikita Koloff. Last affiliation: NWA. 15) Power & Glory: Hercules & Paul Roma. Manager: Slick. WWF. 16) The Freebirds: Michael "P.S." Hayes & Jimmy "Jam" Garvin. NWA. These are the 16 teams and there current or last affiliation known. This tournament seems like it will be a battle of the NWA vs. the WWF. Now, these are the matches for Round 1 of the RSPW Tag Team Tournament! Round 1: -------- 1) The British Bulldogs vs. The Midnight Express 2) The Midnight Rockers vs. D.J. Peterson & The Trooper 3) Demolition vs. The Skyscrapers 4) The Freebirds vs. The Hart Foundation 5) The Road Warriors vs. The Rock n' Roll Express 6) The Superpowers vs. The Steiner Brothers 7) Tully Blanchard & Arn Anderson vs. The Bladerunners 8) Power & Glory vs. Doom Now, all you need to do is mail me who *YOU* think will win each match in this round and maybe why or how they win. Then, next Sunday, Feb. 10th, I will post a *FULL* match coverage for each one and their respective winners. I appreciate all the people who voted the singles tournament and hope they vote here again. Please take into account all managers at ringside, and previous records, and success of each team. Thank you and see you at the matches!
