RSPW Singles Tournament Winner!

28 January 1991
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, here it is everyone! After a month of waiting, this is the match for the RSPW Tournament Winner! Enjoy!
1) Ric Flair vs. Hulk Hogan Ric comes down to ringside with Arn and to his typical music. He struts around for a while and then "Real American" starts playing. The crowd goes nuts and Ric gets furious. Hogan comes down flexing to the crowd. When he finally reaches ringside, Ric attacks immediately with a few chops and fists. The bell rings and Ric whips Hogan into the corner. He follows with awesome chops to the chest that even Hogan staggers from. Ric goes for a hiptoss, but Hogan blocks it and hiptosses Ric. Hogan starts going to work on
Flair with fists to the head. Ric goes down and goes outside for a breather.
Hogan follows and headbutts both Arn and Ric together! Hogan then bodyslams
Ric and places Ric and himself back in the ring. Ric pleads, but Hogan kicks him a few times. He suplexs Ric and covers for two. Hogan whips Ric to the ropes, but Ric hits him with a devastating dropkick to the face. Flair starts to work on Hogan's leg. He hits Hogan with a few kneedrops and a cover for two. He gets Hogan up and Hogan with tremendous strength whips Flair into a corner. Ric does a "Flair flip" runs the apron, climbs the ropes, and flies at
Hogan. Hogan catches him and powerslams him. He covers and gets two. Hulk throws Ric to the ropes and Ric ducks a clothesline only to place a sleeper on
Hogan. HOgan finally reaches the canvas. The ref checks the arm and on the third drop, HOgan gives the finger wave. Hulk makes it to his feet and backslams Ric onto the mat. Ric gets up first and hits Hogan with a neckbreaker. He then tosses Hulk outside the ring. Arn takes a chair and slams it into Hogan's back when the ref isn't looking. Hogan gets up with the eye and nails Arn into next Tuesday with the chair. Hogan gets back in and throws fists to the head at Flair. He whips Flair off the rope and gives him the foot to the face. Hogan goes for the leg drop, but Ric rolls out. Hogan is holding his knee and Ric quickly gives a woo to the crowd. The crowd woos back and Flair gives HOgan the knee buster. Hulk is now in agony. Ric gives the sign and slaps on the figure four. Hogan tries to fight and almost seem that he would get out of it. Ric with all determination holds on the figure four. Hogan passes out from the pain and the ref counts to three! Ric gets up and is declared the winner!
RSPW Singles Tournament Champion ------------------------------------- "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair I hope everyone enjoyed the tournament. Watch soon for the RSPW Tag
Team TOurnament. Later dudes!
