RSPW Singles Tournament Final Round!

15 December 1990
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The time has been long and the matches have come. But, it's here. This is the final round for the RSPW Singles Tournament. This will decide who is THE BEST of the best around. It started with 16 and now it's down to two wrestlers. For those who haven't seen it, this is what the breakdown looks like from Round 1 through Round 3. Round 1: 1) Sid Vicious vs. "Mr Perfect" Curt Henning Winner: Sid by pinfall 2) Lex Lugar vs. Larry Zybszko Winner: Lugar by submission 3) Ric Flair vs. Randy "Macho Man" Savage Winner: Flair by pinfall 4) Nikita Koloff vs. Jerry "the King" Lawler Winner: Lawler by pinfall 5) Hulk Hogan vs. Arn Anderson Winner: Hogan by pinfall 6) Andre the Giant vs. Barry Windham Winner: Barry by DQ 7) The Ultimate Warrior vs. Stan "the Lariat" Hansen Winner: Stan by pinfall 8) Sting vs. Kerry Von Erich Winner: Sting by pinfall Round 2: 1) Sid Vicious vs. Lex Lugar Winner: Lugar by pinfall 2) Ric Flair vs. Jerry "the King" Lawler Winner: Ric by pinfall 3) Hulk Hogan vs. Barry Windham Winner: Hogan by pinfall 4) Sting vs. Stan "the Lariat" Hansen Winner: Sting by pinfall Round 3: 1) Lex Lugar vs. Ric Flair Winner: Flair by pinfall 2) Hulk Hogan vs. Sting Winner: Hogan by DQ And here it is everyone...the Final match up for the Tournament. It is:! Round 4: 1) Ric Flair vs. Hulk Hogan So, what do you need to do, you ask. Send to me your votes on who will win this final round. Also, please tell me why you think that person will win. Votes can be sent up until December 21st. Then, the voting will be over until next semester. When school starts again, I will repost this last round so everyone can vote and it will be fair for all to see. Thanks to everyone who has helped with these commenting and voting. I hope you all enjoyed this. Look forward to the RSPW Tag Team Tournament next semester! Happy Holidays!!!!!
