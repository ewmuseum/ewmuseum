RSPW Tag Team Tournament's Final Round results!(LONG!)

10 March 1991
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The votes are in. The match has been set. And we are ready to go! Final Round: ----------- 1) The Hart Foundation vs. The Road Warriors: You can tell that the crowd is very anxious to see this one go on it's way. "Iron Man" starts blaring over the P.A. and the Road Warriors come down to ringside alone. They have a death look in their eye and are bandaged in some places. Then, the Harts music begins to play and they start walking down the aisle with the WWF Tag belts. They reach ringside and the two teams face each other in a stare down. Obviously, Brett is the smallest, but he doesn't look scared. Each team then turns their backs and walks to their corners.
Neidhart takes off Brett's glasses. Brett goes outside and gives them to a little girl at ringside. The little girl gives him a big hug and Brett goes back in. The bell rings and Neidhart starts off against Animal. Both the power men of their teams and they lock up. Animal easily throws Neidhart to the mat. Neidhart gets up and they lock up again. This time, Neidhart leg trips Animal and holds on to the arm. He places his boot in Animal's face and really pulls on his arm, almost as if trying to rip it out of it's socket.
Animal makes his way to his feet and smashes Neidhart with his free arm. Neidhart smashes back and it becomes a brawl between the two. Animal wins and whips Neidhart to the ropes catching him with a powerslam. Animal tags to
Hawk. Hawk goes to drop an elbow, but Neidhart moves. Neidhart drops a headbutt and then tags to Brett. Brett comes in and holds a headlock on the big man. Hawk gets up easily and belly to back suplexs Brett. But, Brett holds on to the headlock. Hawk gets up again and whips him to the ropes. Hawk goes for a clothesline, but Brett slides under him. Brett waits for Hawk to turn around and hits him with a dropkick. Hawk doesn't fall, so he hits him with another one. Hawk finally hits the mat. Brett follows with a leg drop and covers for one. Brett tags back to Neidhart. Brett holds Hawk and
Neidhart plants a forearm to the stomach. Neidhart then whips Hawk to the ropes, but Hawk comes back flying through the air with a shouldertackle. Hawk tags back to Animal. Hawk and Animal whip Neidhart to the ropes and hit him with a double HIGH backdrop. Neidhart comes down hard as the crowd gasps.
Animal then suplexs Neidhart and covers only getting two. Animal gets him back to his feet and rams his head into the turnbuckle. Then, Neidhart flips Animal into the corner and begins to throw his shoulder into his stomach. Neidhart goes to whip Animal to the other corner, Animal reverses the whip. Brett goes running to that corner and places his body on the turnbuckle so Neidhart hits that instead of the corner. Animal comes charging in, but Neidhart and Brett move so he runs into the turnbuckle. Neidhart tags Brett. They both take
Animal and double atomic drop him. After, they double dropkick him to the back so that he goes through the ropes to the outside. Hawk comes in for a double clothesline, but the Harts duck it. They retaliate with their own double clothesline taking Hawk down. Brett goes after Animal on the outside while
Neidhart stays in with Hawk. Brett takes Animal and throws him into the post and then the ringside barrier. Brett then pushes him back inside the ring.
Neidhart has Hawk in the opposite corner smashing and pummeling him. Brett starts giving kicks and chops to Animal in his corner. Then, the Harts whip the Roadies and each other, but the Roadies reverse the whip slamming Brett and
Neidhart into each other. The Roadies go to clothesline both men to the back of the heads, but the each Hart sees the other Road Warrior coming and yells
"Duck!" The Harts duck and the Road Warriors end up clotheslining each other.
Neidhart takes Hawk and powerslams him to the mat and then throws him outside. Animal, who was still the legal man, gets elbowed by Brett to the head. Brett tags to Neidhart. They whip Animal into a corner and then whip each other around until Neidhart slams his body into Animal's. Neidhart tags back to
Brett. Neidhart places Animal on his knee in a backbreaker. Brett comes off the second rope and drops an elbow to the throat. Finally, Neidhart takes
Animal into the bearhug high. Brett comes flying off the ropes and gives
Animal the "Hart Attack". Brett covers, but Animal kicks out on two and a half. Brett drops a knee, but Animal just gets up. Hawk is back on the apron by now. Animal starts to pummel Brett like a rag doll. Neidhart tries to come in, but runs into a Hawk dropkick which knocks him outside the ring. Brett attempts to fight back, but to no use. Hawk and Animal just start to pound on him and then nail Brett with a double dropkick. Animal then tags to Hawk and
Hawk mounts the top rope. Animal places Brett in a military press with his head facing away from Hawk. Hawk comes off the top, grabbing Brett's head on the way down, and delivers an AWESOME bulldog! Hawk flips Brett over and gets the three count. But, while the Road Warriors are celebrating in victory, Doom flies down to ringside and starts beating on them! Simmons takes Hawk and starts kicking him and fisting him. Reed starts to kick Animal with his cowboy boots and then starts choking him. The ref tries to stop it, but gets nailed and thrown outside the ring. Simmons takes the NWA tag belt and bashes it into
Hawk's head relentlessly. Reed is choking Animal with his belt and then starts to gouge his bad eye out. Reed continues to hit Animal above his eye, looking as if to re-injure it. But, Neidhart and Brett come back in the ring with chairs and slams them into Simmons and Reed. The Harts continue to beat the chairs on Doom until they go down. Brett then nails Simmons with the WWF tag belt and he goes flying to the outside. Neidhart is pummeling Reed and the holds him in a bearhug. Brett comes flying off the ropes and nails Reed with the "Hart Attack" instead of with a clothesline, with the WWF tag belt! Reed and Simmons finally retreat. The Roadies bruised and bloody, have made it back to their feet. Both teams shake hands and hug as the Harts raise the Road
Warriors hands in a hard fought victory.
RSPW Tag Team Tournament Champs --------------------------------- The Road Warriors! Well, there you have it folks. Just so you know, the tally of votes were VERY close and this is what it looked like in the end.
