RSPWF: Maine State Championship

25 June 1991
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Seeing as precious little has come out of the RSPWF lately, I'm officially announcing an E-Mail Tournament. This is an RSPWF-Sponsered tournement for the Maine State Regional Championship, to be held alternately in
Portland, Me and Bangor, Me. The Maine State Championship, of course, is not as prestidigious as the RSPW World or North American Titles, and so many of the top names are not competing. However, it also isn't a Jobber title (as that would be too boring for me to write).
The rules are simple. The wrestlers are listed below, in alphabetical order. Participants in the mailing should list them, from 1 to 8, in the order *you* feel the group would come in. Do not list any as ties. So, if you feel Joe Shmoe could beat anyone else in the group, list him first. Or, if you feel Joe Shmoe couldn't win against a hand puppet, list him last. I will then add up the votes by assigning 8 points for any #1 vote, 7 points for a #2 vote, and so on, down to 1 point for each time a Wrestler is voted last.
This makes for some amusing possibilities -- a wrestler could be rated second by every participant (gaining 7 points for each vote) and win, for example.
Then, I'll set up the cards to reflect the choices. There will be three rounds -- one with four matches, one with two matches, and the
Championship Match. However, participants only need to vote once. I might decide to hold an undercard for the second and third matches -- in which case I will allow voting for the other matches similarly to other RSPWF events.
The participants are (in Alphabetical Order): Arn Anderson Ted DiBiase (w/Sensational Sherri) Bret Hart Owen Hart Paul Roma Jacques Rougeau (w/Jimmy Hart) Roddy Piper (w/Virgil) Davey Boy Smith (w/Winston) Remember, rate these wrestlers from 1 to 8 and send the results to me. The deadline is midnight, July 12, 1991. Entries recieved after that will not be considered. *Please* E-Mail your choices.
Eric, Lord Sabre |-}--------
