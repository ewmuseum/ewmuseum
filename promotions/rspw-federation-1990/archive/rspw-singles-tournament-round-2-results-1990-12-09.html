RSPW Singles Tournament Round 2 results(long)

9 December 1990
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Everyone has been waiting I assume for these matches to arrive. I compiled all the votes again and I felt found some surprising results. So, with no further a-do, here are the results of Round 2!
1) Sid Vicious vs. Lex Lugar Sid comes down to ringside with Arn Anderson. Lex comes down alone. They both look each other over and the bell rings. Both men lock up and Lex shoves Sid into a corner. Sid falls in shock and Lex gives him a pose down.
The crowd cheers wildly. They lock up again and Lex hits Sid with an armdrag. Sid charges again and gets hit with another armdrag followed nicely by an armbar. Arn complains to the ref about a hair pull. Lex holds the armbar on
Sid's right arm. Lex gives it an elbow or two. Sid is obviously in pain. Sid makes it to his feet and gives Lex a few kicks to the stomach. Lex lets go,
Sid bounces off the ropes and hits Lex with a shoulderblock. Lex doesn't budge. Lex throws punches at Sid and Sid starts throwing some back. It becomes a wild melee and Lex gets the upper hand knocking Sid out of the ring.
Sid goes to regroup, but Lex follows. Arn distracts the ref as Sid hits Lugar with a chair to the stomach. Lugar goes down hard. Sid then picks him up and drops his stomach on the barrier. Sid follows this with a few good kicks to his abdomen as well. Sid gets Lugar back inside and he follows. Sid slams
Lugar to the mat and drops an elbowsmash on his weakened stomach. Lugar howls in pain as Sid covers him. But Lugar gets out at two. Sid gets Lugar to his feet and whips him into the ropes. Lugar comes back and receives a knee to his abdomen. Arn cheers as Lugar hits in canvas in excruciating pain.(big word ;)
Sid cradles up Lex, but only gets a two and a half count. Sid gets frustrated with the ref's counting and yells at him. Arn tells Sid don't worry about the ref, just win. Sid gets Lex up and places him into a abdominal stretch in the middle of the ring. Lugar is hurting, but won't submit. Sid hits Lugar in the stomach a few times as well hoping he'll give up, but he doesn't. Lugar summons all his strength and hip tosses Sid to the mat. Though, Lugar falls down as well. Sid gets up and quickly power bombs Lugar. Sid goes for a lame cover, but Lugar kicks out with authority! Lugar gets him and Sid kicks to the stomach. Lugar doesn't feel a thing and nails Sid with a forearm to the face.
Sid goes down and Lugar powerslams him. Lugar follows with an elbowsmash. Lex gets Sid up and whips him to the ropes. Sid comes back and receives a clothesline that puts him into a 360 slam on the canvas. Lex small packages him and makes Sid easy prey for the three count. Winner: Lex by pin.
2) Ric Flair vs. Jerry "the King" Lawler Surprisingly enough, Flair comes down to ringside alone as does Lawler. The two men lock up with Lawler placing Ric in a arm-bar twist. Flair gets out and reverses this. Jerry throws Ric into a corner in where Flair does a
"Flair-flip" over the top and walks the apron. Ric flies back in a gives
Lawler a few chops to the chest. Lawler goes down hard. Ric gives Lawler a knee buster and then loosens up his legs. Ric calls for the figure four and locks it on. Lawler is in pain and tries making it to the ropes, with no success. Ric grabs the ropes for extra leverage, but the ref almost catches him. Lawler goes to use the chain on Flair. Flair knocks the chain out of his hand and holds the figure four even tighter than before. There is a popping sound and Lawler submits. Lawler's leg is busted and needs a stretcher. Flair taunts him and walks away giving a woo to the crowd. Winner: Flair by a submission.
3) Hulk Hogan vs. Barry Windham Hulk comes down alone and to his regular music. Windham comes down with Arn at his side. The two men lock up as the bell rings. Hogan displays his power by tossing Windham across the ring. Windham goes outside to confer with Arn. Hogan goes and grabs Windham by his hair and drags him back inside.
Hogan then hits Windham with a few punches followed by a turnbuckle smash. Hogan whips Windham into the ropes and Barry ducks a clothesline. Barry comes back and delivers an awesome lariat that knocks Hogan silly. Barry goes to work on Hogan with an elbow and a cover for two. Barry holds Hogan in a chinlock. Arn distracts the ref a few times and Barry chokes Hogan. Hogan starts to go down and the ref checks Hogan's arm. It falls twice and on the third drop, Hogan gives the wave of the finger. Windham realizing the "hulk up" coming on drops an elbow on Hogan's head. He follows this with a few knees to the back. Windham stands up Hogan and hits him with a dropkick. Hogan falls into a corner and Windham follows him in with a few kicks to the head.
Hogan is bleeding. Windham gets Hogan up on the ropes in an attempt to set him up for the superplex. Hogan now on the top rope starts to stir. Arn comes up from behind and nails Hogan in the head with a fist. Windham stands Hogan and himself on the top rope and hits the superplex with accuracy. Windham goes over and covers Hogan, but Hogan kicks out at a two and 3/4 count. Windham slaps on the Claw and Hogan starts shaking. Hogan elbows his way out and starts heading towards the ropes. Arn goes to grab Hogan, but only gets a boot in the face. Hogan hits Barry with three punches, whip to the rope, and boot to Windham's face. Hulk hits Barry with the legdrop and a three count.
Winner: Hulk by pinfall. 4) Stan "the Lariat" Hansen vs. Sting Stan comes down with the US belt and immediately gets ready for the match. Sting comes down with music and the NWA World belt in hand. As soon as
Sting gets the the ring, Stan goes to jump him. He hits Sting with a few punches as the bell rings. He goes for a punch to the stomach, but only finds his hand hurting as Stan hits the NWA World belt! Sting laughs and retaliates with kicks and punches and a whip into the corner. He follows Stan in with a
"Stinger-splash" while still having the NWA Wold belt on! Sting places the belt in the corner and goes to work on Stan. He places him in a side headlock and Stan whips him to the ropes. Stan catches Sting with an elbow and goes for another one. Sting moves out of the way and Stan crashes on the mat. Sting places that arm in a chicken wing behind Stan's back. Sting gives the arm a few knees and Stan is hurting. Stan gets to his feet and makes it to the ropes. The ref calls for a break and Sting lets go and then gets sucker punched. Stan throws Sting through the ropes and takes it outside. He delivers a double ax handle to Sting's back. Stan follows this with a knee to the back and then a chair. The ref calls for both men to get back in the ring.
Stan goes to throw Sting into the corner, but Sting reverses and throws Stan into the post. Sting gets back in and rolls back out again. Sting gets Stan and throws him into the barrier. Sting rolls Stan back inside the ring and he follows. Stan plays possum and hits Sting with the lariat. Sting goes down and he is now bleeding. Stan throws punches at Sting's head making the wound worse. Stan covers and gets two. Stan continues with a kneedrop and then a splash on Sting's back. Stan gets Sting into the boston crab in a hopeful submission. Sting does not give up and makes his way to the ropes. Stan breaks the hold and picks up Sting to bodyslam him. On the way down, Sting grabs Stan's leg, and wraps him up in a small package and a three count! Sting
's hand is held up in victory and Stan hits Sting with the cowbell. The ref gets tossed outside the ring as Stan chokes and beats down on Sting. The Four
Horsemen, realizing the opportunity, come down and help beat on Sting at ringside. Lugar and the Steiner Brothers come down and help Sting. It's four on five until the Ultimate Warrior comes down! The Warrior comes and nails
Stan with his own cowbell and beats up on his wound. The Horsemen and Stan retreat from ringside as the Steiners, Sting, and the Warrior raise their hands in victory. The Warrior and Sting both proudly hold up their titles and hug in a joyful reunion. In an interview after the match, Sting and UW announce their return as a tag team for the tournament as The Bladerunners! Winner: Sting by pinfall.
The set up for the Round 3 semifinals will follow this post. I hope everyone enjoyed the matches.
