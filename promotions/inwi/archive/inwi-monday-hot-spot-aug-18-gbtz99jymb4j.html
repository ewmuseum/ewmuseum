INWI Monday Night Hot Spot!! ======================== Monday, August 18, 1997 Bull and Finch Tavern, Boston ========================================================== Brian Newark- Welcome fans to Monday Night Hot Spot.... [Newark's voice is drowned out by the opening guitar riff of "Under My Voodoo" by Sublime. The sounds of this tune cause the fans to explode in cheers as "The Technician" Zack Sharp steps through the curtain and out into the isle. Sharp is wearing his usual ring attire consisting of his "Technician" pants trunks, shades, and a white "Technician" T-Shirt. His long black hair is tied back and the Northeast Championship belt is around his waiste. Also in his right hand is a straight jacket and the other is a microphone. He steps in the ring.] Zack Sharp: Hello fans! [Huge Pop!] Well, I have a lot on my mind, so let's get to it. You see this thing with Mr. Ed Rogers is really getting on my nerves. I mean this thing has been going on for a couple months now and nothing has been resolved. Each time we face each other it ends in a double DQ and each time we wrestle another opponent the other interferes. So, here is what I am going to do! [Sharp removes his glasses and hangs them on his collar.] I am here tonight for one reason and that is to put Ed Rogers in this straight jacket and to send him to the looney bin forever! [Sharp holds up the jacket as the fans pop once again.] So I want a match with Ed Rogers tonight for this belt, but of course I think someone is going to disagree with this. That is right, cause the Commish says. [Sharp begins to talk kind of nerdy to impersonate the Commish.] This match should be saved for the Pay Per View. [Sharp stops this voice and returns to business.] However, I do not really care because I want Ed Rogers tonight. Before I go, Commish, I would l like to quote the great Rage Against the Machine and say this "[Beep] you I won't do what you tell me!" [The fans pop once again as "Under My Voodoo" plays over the P.A. once again as Sharp exits.] Brian Newark - Well that was interesting to say the least.
