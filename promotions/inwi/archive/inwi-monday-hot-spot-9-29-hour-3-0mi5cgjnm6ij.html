[INWI] Monday Hot Spot, 9-29, Hour 3

30 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Hot Spot, Hour Number THREE!] [ A laser light show brings us into the beginning of Hour number three of the BEST Monday Night Show on TV. The originators... not the imitators. We are the INWI... the revolutionary force in eWrestling today. The camera goes to Brian Newark who is at ringside ready to start off Hour number three...] Newark - We are back. And let me tell you. We are here to stay. We won't leave for any amount of time. We are here every Monday and don't take vacations! Speaking of that, the NEW decided to take a vacation for a week after their Pay Per View. Maybe that's because they got embarassed! We beat the hell out of them! Let's go up to the ring for our first match! Match 7 Cloud Eagle (with Ultimate Perversion) vs "Bulldozer" Tony Riley Eagle backs Riley into the corner and a knee to the bread basket. Sets him up on the top rope. Riley pushes him away and gets up on the turnbuckles, double axehandle. Eagle back up to a vertical base, boot to the gut, DDT. Cover, 1........2.,and a kickout in 2 by Riley. Eagle brings him back up, picks him up, Reverse shoulder breaker. Then a backbreaker. He is up on the second rope and is waiting for Riley, he jumps, oh my, Riley caught him, spinebuster!!! Cover, 1.........2.......and a kickout by Eagle. Eagle slithers outside and Riley is out there after him. Eagle suckered him in, and he throws Riley into the post. Riley stays on his feet however. Eagle charges, bulldog on the floor!!! The crowd is chanting Eagle and he is really pumped up. Eagle throws him, back into the ring. He grabbed a chair and he is on the apron. Riley sees the chair and charges, Eagle nails him between the eyes and Riley goes down. Eagle catapults himself, ARABIAN FACE BUSTER!!!! Cover 1.........2.........and Riley just kicks out in 2 and a half. Eagle is going to the top rope and, jumps with a splash, but Riley caught him and turned it into a powerslam. He picks up Eagle, sets him up, piledriver. he turns Eagle onto his stomach and now Riley is on the second rope, Legdrop to the back of the head, 1.........2.......and a kickout in 2 by Eagle. This has been an exhausting match for these two combatants. Riley throws Eagle to the buckles, charges, Avalanche in the corner by Riley, and a backbody drop nicely done. Eagle is back into the corner, Riley charges again, he missed and his shoulder went into the post. He backs out and Eagle with a low blow, dropkick, and a leg sweep and Riley goes down. Eagle goes upstairs, Frog Splash, cover, 1........2.......and a kickout by Riley. How the hell did he kick out of that one? This time Eagle charges, Riley with a press slam, and a stun gun into the rope. Eagle is hurt and Riley is going to the top rope?? Eagle shakes the ropes, and Riley goes groin first into the buckles, Eagle goes up with him, BELLY TO BELLY SUPERPLEX!!!! Cover 1.........2.........3. It's over. Eagle wins with a beautiful belly to belly superplex, and a big win for him against Riley. Again your winner in 12:13, Cloud Eagle. Match 8 - Light Heavyweight Championship David McCarter vs D.D. (Doctor of Disaster) This one is for the Lightweight Championship and it should be a dousy. DD is circling around the ring and now goes for a leg but Mccarter waves it off. DD into the ropes, and a shoulderblock and McCarter goes down. DD is on the top rope already. He tried for a standing centon but he missed and now McCarter has the upper hand in this matchup. He picks DD up by the hair and whips him in, backbody drop but DD lands on his feet, goes into the ropes, spinning heel kick, nicely done. McCarter slides outside, grabs the railing and takes a breather. Here comes DD with a suicide dive. What a move but McCarter remained on his feet. DD goesback into the ring and is going to try another suicide dive, he missed because McCarter saw it and he pulled the guard railing over and DD rammed into it and has busted his forehead wide open. McCarter throws him back into the ring and assends to the top turnbuckle, missle dropkick, cover, hooks the leg, 1.........2.......and a kickout by DD. DD is sent in again, he ducks underneath, leapfrog by McCarter, high cross body by DD, but McCarter's weight rolled him over onto DD for the cover, 1..........2.......and barely a kickout in 2 and a half. McCarter knocks him to his knees with a forearm to the kidneys, and a swinging neckbreaker and DD isd really shaken up on that one. McCarter is climbing to the top turnbuckle, elbowdrop from the top rope and he really got some elevation on that move. Another cover on DD, 1...........2...........and another kickout by DD. This guy wants that title bad and he refuses to be pinned here. Kick to the gut by McCarter and he sets him up, piledriver coming up, no, DD cartwheels out of the piledriver and delivers a beautiful Samoan drop on McCarter. Now DD is on the outside, he has a table and he throws it into the ring. The ref seems to be letting this slide for now. He sets up the table in the corner and McCarter seems to be a little groggy. DD is putting McCarter across that table and is going up stairs. OH MY GOD!!! DD tried for a 450 splash but he missed and went right through the table. The ref is trying to get the fragments of the table outside the ring because both men are down and out. The ref got the ring clean and is now starting to administer the 10 count. 5...........6.........7......and McCarter is back up, he picks up DD, irish whip, DD ducks underneath, McCarter grabs him, SLEEPER HOLD ON DD!!!! DD is too weak to fight this move off by McCarter. He is slowly fading here. DD drops to one knee. The ref is saying to the crowd that he is beginning to count. 1..........the hand drops, 2...........the hand drops..3...............THE HAND DROPS, IT'S OVER!!! McCarter has beaten DD with the sleeper hold. Again your winner in 10:10, and still INWI Lightweight Champion, David McCarter. Match 9 - The Gauntlet, Week 1 Jason "Crusher" Chandler vs "The Knuckle" Jimmy Chooley Chandler started this match extremely quickly attacking Chooley with a vicious belly to back suplex, dropping Chooley hard on the back of his head. Chandler then scoops up Chooley and sends him hard into the corner and follows him in with a deadly elbow to the side of Chooley's face. OUCH!! It's apparent now that Chandler may have the intensity to go through this gauntlet...but 10 wins in a row, that is surely a near impossible task. Chandler crotches Chooley on the top turnbuckle and goes up after him...Both men are standing on the top rope....Snap Superplex from the Top Turnbuckle!!! Unbelievable, and Chandler gets right back up..amazing..Chandler goes for the cover..One..Two..thr--No!!! Chandler picked up Chooley..He didn't want the pin, that could be a huge mistake in Chandler's attempt to capture some INWI gold. Chandler pulls up Chooley..He's setting him up for a Piledriver..Jumping Piledriver and Chooley's head nearly crunched from the impact..Hey NEW, Hey Bloom you see that?!? This is the REAL Crusher, not some almost but no really wanna be. Chandler pulls up the almost liveless body of Chooley....Chandler locks on a body hug...Belly to Belly Suplex...He just nailed Chooley with the Crush..It's over....Chandler cradles both legs...one..two...three..!!!!!!! Damn, an impressive first match of the gauntlet by Jason Chandler. Here is your winner in 4:32, via pinfall. Jason "the Crusher" Chandler!! Match 10 Ultimate Perversion(with Cloud Eagle) vs "The Real Deal" Michael Bledsoe This match has been started and neither man is in the ring. The action has already started on the floor!!! Perversion with a right...Bledsoe fires back..these two INWI superstars are battling outside the ring...Perversion sends Bledsoe hard into the security rail and charges....BACK DROP BY Bledsoe sends Perversion Over the security rail and crashes hard to the concrete floor. Bledsoe now on top of Perversion and pummeling him. Bledsoe pulls up Perversion and rolls him back into the ring...Finally. Bledsoe's setting up for a Double Arm DDT..<BAM> nails it...Quick cover..One..Two..No!!! Perversion shows he still has plenty of life left. Bledsoe pulls up Perversion and whips him into the ropes...Back drop by Bled-No!!!! Swinging DDT by Perversion. Perversion just turned the tide of this match. He pulls up The Real Deal and nails a standing Spinebuster...Quick cover..One..Two...No!!! Real Deal rolls his shoulder... Perversion pulls up Bledsoe and is setting up for a tiger bomb...wait!!!! Here comes Harvey Thunder and Michael Slater charging the ring..and they're attacking both Perversion and Bledsoe. Thunder tosses Bledsoe over the top rope and down hard to the floor. Thunder and Slater double teamming Pervesion...Wait who's that?!? It's Cloud Eagle!!!! Eagle hits the ring and runs right through Harvey Thunder....Perversion turns the tides and is now punishing Slater.....It's a pier six brawl [ok Gorilla] Wait now who's coming?!? It's Michael Bane and Jonathan Hammer and they're brawling now with Michael Bledsoe on the outside...It's these three men that will be involved in a vicious 3 Way Dance inside of a steel cage. All three men are battling outside the ring, and Slater, Thunder, Perversion, and Eagle are battling inside the ring...What action, only brought to you by the INWI. You don't see this type of action in the N.E.W., well not until we take it over anyway...INWI officials and ARMED security forces are returning to the ringside area to begin to break up this melee. Hmmm...awful lot of armed security around these days..hahha wait 'til we get back at the N.E.W. At 9:22 Both men were disqualified for outside interference. Official's decision a DRAW!!! ===========================================================Brian Newark - ARGHHH!! Hour Three is over, but we have been told we can keep going until we are done because we are the Best Monday Night Show on TV... we'll be right back with our Main Event!! =========================================================== [Hot Spot Continues...]
