[INWI] Hot Spot, 10-27, Hour 3

27 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Hour Three begins with a light show and the camera again starts to fan over the crowd. This time it catches signs held up by the full house: "Springer Rules" "Bill Bob is No peoples Hick" "Apples is way to Rotten" "Hammer that Nail!" "INWI 4 Life" "Pillow is NOT Fluffy" and many others all over the place. Camera switches back to Newark, who is now in a blue denim jacket with INWI Hot Spot on the back and he is set to go as we shift into hour number three...] Brian Newark - WELCOME back ladies and gentlemen to the ORIGINAL Monday Night Show!! We have a solid THIRD hour set for you and sit back an let's kick it off with the former Champion.... Springer as he takes on Michael Bledsoe. How will Bledsoe be? Let's get to the ring! Match 9 Springer (with Dr. Bedpan) vs "The Real Deal" Michael Bledsoe This is a match that shouldn't happen. You saw what happened earlier tonight. Those spineless jellyfish the Brotherhood, attacked Bledsoe as he entered the building. You know Bledsoe is not 100% for this match and can he be focused on the Springer? I mean, you all know Bledsoe... he is planning on how to get back at the Brotherhood. This is crazy times. The Brotherhood just doesn't give a damn. They go after everyone! Springer is in the ring and here comes Bledsoe. There's the bell and we are underway. Collar and elbow tie up in the middle of the ring and Springer takes control and slaps on a headlock. You know after that Spike piledriver, Bledsoe's neck is his weak point. Bledsoe sends Springer into the ropes and they collide but neither man moves. Bledsoe off the ropes and Springer sends him down with a clothesline and Bledsoe is grabbing his neck. I think he is really hurt and I don't know if the INWI should let this match go on. Springer picks up Bledsoe, but Bledsoe finds something and slams Springer. Bledsoe off the ropes and nails an elbow. Bledsoe picks up Springer, sends him into the ropes, back body drop...NO! Springer stops and nails a spinning neck breaker. I think Bledsoe is really hurt as he is groping his neck as Springer scoops him up and grabs him into a bear hug and nails a Stun Gun on the top rope! Springer with a cover, 1....2....NO! Bledsoe showing some heart as he kicks out of it. Springer shakes his head, scoops up Bledsoe and nails a reverse neck breaker and then bounces off the ropes and drops a leg. Springer signals to the crowd and gets ready as he picks up Bledsoe and there's the Springer Cutter!!!! A cover by Springer, 1.....2......3!!! It's over. Here is your winner in 8:57, The Springer!! Man, the Springer gets the win and INWI officials are helping Bledsoe out of the ring. You know he'll have words for Bill Bob Woijicowski and Vinny Vitrone next week. Wow... [After Springer has beaten Michael Bledsoe, the INWI-Tron flashes and up pops everyones favorite INWI Personality, Scoop Ropes!] SCOOP: Hey Springer, you punk. How we doing today? Oh, I bet you're doing fine after beating up on Michael Bledsoe. Well you realize that if the Brotherhood hadn't giving Bledsoe a Spike Piledriver in the back earlier, you would of lost. You know why? Because you are a loser man... a true loser. And you challenge me to a match. Ooooh, you are a big man. I have been retired for five years you stupid ass. Why in the hell would I want to come out of retirement, just to face your stupid ass? Exactly. There is no point because we all know I would beat the living hell out of you. Now then. You better learn to keep your mouth shut and then it will all be good. [Springer grabs a mic from ringside and goes to talk.] SPRINGER - Scoop, you're a piece of trash. You talk a mean game, but you couldn't carry my jock strap. The truth of the whole matter is, I am a former INWI Heavyweight Champion. You NEVER in your life held a Heavyweight Championship and are jealous of me. Well, Jealousy gets you no where punk, so you may as well put up or shut up because this crowd knows I would kick your ASS!!!! [Crowd erupts in a "Kick Scoops Ass" chant... "Kick Scoops Ass.... Kick Scoops Ass...''] SCOOP: Springer, you and all of these peon fans can lick me. Because the truth of the whole matter is, I'm not going to waste my time on a punk like you. You are not worth it. Never have been and never will be. I'm outta here... [With that, the INWI-Tron goes blank and the crowd continues their chant... "Kick Scoops Ass..." ass Springer goes to each turnbuckle and raises his hands in the air and the crowd explodes each time...] Match 10 - Gauntlet Week 5 Jason "The Crusher" Chandler (with Oz) vs "The Icon" Pimp Daddy Brown Chandler looks ready to go here and remember folks, this is week number five of the Gauntlet for Jason Chandler. Brown looks focused however as he hits a left hand to the mouth and he backs Jason into the corner and hits a dropkick and Jason's head just snapped back. Jason is on the mat and Brown is yelling at him to get up. Jason looks pissed and he looks up and hits Brown with a right hand. He sends him into the ropes and hits a shoulder tackle. He sends him in again and he meets the same fate. Jason is playing to the crowd. He is sending the message that he hates Brown. He looks back and Brown with a thumb to the eye. Chandler staggers back and Brown with a dropkick and Chandler goes over the top rope and hits the floor hard. He is holding his right knee on the ground here and he looks concerned. Meanwhile, Brown is on the apron and he just hit Jason in the head with an elbow. Brown and a fan are having a discussion and Chandler grabs a chair and he waffles Brown over the top of the head with it. Brown slumps to the floor. Chandler is setting him up and he hits a vertical suplex on the floor. Jason is smelling victory early here which could be a mistake. Brown is back up and a scoop slam on the floor by Chandler. He picks him up again and he is holding him up and he rams him into the post. He steps back and he does it again. He drops Brown and he is clutching his back. He has the chair again and he sets up the chair in the aisle. He slaps him in the face which gets Brown mad and he charges and Chandler hits a drop toe hold and Brown's face just smashed right into the chair. Chandler takes his head and rams it into the chair. Brown is back up and he dropkicks Chandler into the railing. He wraps Chandler's knee around his leg, lifts and sends his knee right through the chair. That isn't good for his aggravated knee as you may know. Chandler is limping over to the ring and Brown kicks him in the ass and he goes back into the ring. Brown is ascending the turnbuckles and he is perched up there, he leaps and hits a high-cross body and he covers the man, 1......2....and a kick out by Chandler. The gauntlet was almost over right there. Brown has the leg of Chandler and he is giving him the spinning toe hold. He is wrenching it on and Chandler reaches up and grabs the head of Brown and brings him over with a small package, 1.....2.....and a kick out by Brown. Chandler is back up and he hits a back suplex and he covers, 1.....2.....and a kick out by Brown just barely. Brown with a leg sweep and Chandler falls on his face. Brown on top now and he has the STF applied. He is pulling back on the head and I think Chandler is going to tap out right here. He rolls over and they both hit the ropes and the ref made Brown break the hold. Brown is back up and he yells "I got him now" Chandler is up and Brown grabs a leg, but Chandler hits an ensugeri. The crowd is really behind this guy as he gets up and hits a dropkick. His leg is really hurt because after he hit the move,he fell to the mat and grabbed his leg. Chandler fighting off the pain. But Brown with a dropkick to the knee. Brown backs him up into the corner and wraps Chandler's arm around his head, he lifts and hits a northern lights suplex with a bridge, that could do it, 1.......2.....and a kick out by Chandler. So close, so very close. Chandler back up and he swats away a dropkick and hits one of his own. He whips Brown into the corner, leaps, and hits the buckles and falls to the canvas. Brown is ascending the buckles and he is smiling to the crowd. Chandler crawls over and shakes the ropes. Brown hits crotch first into the top of the buckles. Chandler is up and he is going for a suplex from the mat. No wait, he grabs the leg of Brown and goes over, HE HITS THE PIMP A PLEX, 1.........2.......3!!! He beat Brown with his own move. What a resourceful move by Chandler and the Gauntlet continues. Your winner in 14:11, Jason "The Crusher" Chandler. Match 11 - Television Championship Pig Pen (with Oyster Boy) vs Ultimate Perversion (with Cloud Eagle) Well, here we go. In another wild match for the Television Championship. The new champion Pig Pen will defend his title in a re-match against Ultimate Perversion. Perversion is in the ring and there's the champion, Pig Pen. He enters the ring and hands the belt to the ref and Perversion attacks!! Perversion sends Pig Pen into the ropes and nails a diving clothesline and the ref finally rings the bell! Perversion scoops up Pig Pen and sends him into the turnbuckle. Perversion starts to pound away on Pig Pens head and the crowd counts along, 8.....9......10!! Pig Pen stumbles out of the corner and falls to the mat and Perversion goes to the top rope and nails a flying elbow!! A quick cover, 1......2......3!! He got him!!! Oh my god... let's get the official word!! Here is your winner in 54 seconds and NEW INWI Television Champion, Ultimate Perversion!! Wait, look out! Here comes Harvey Thunder and Michael Slater from the crowd and they attack Perversion!! Cloud Eagle is in the ring and we have a four way brawl. Perversion sends Thunder over the ropes and Eagle and Perversion are double teaming Slater but he rolls out of the ring to safety. Thunder and Slater leave laughing and Perversion and Eagle stare down the aisle. Wow...let's get ready for the next match. [As Ed Rogers starts to get ready for his match the INWI-Tron starts to flicker and the house lights drop. All you see on the screen is a sock puppet... a clean version of Smelly. The crowd gasps and a quick close up of Ed from the reflection shows the look of confusion...] VOICE - Hey you.... dumb ass.... yeah you Ed... it's me... the REAL SMELLY!! That's right you stupid moron, I am with a real Wrestler now and he is a King on top of that! King Apples!! HAHAHA!! And you Ed, oh you will pay. I can't believe you think that you have the real Smelly. After all of the years we spent together Ed...you can't tell the difference? I am shocked and upset. But Ed... your time is ticking and guess what, I have a real wrestler now!! Tell 'em King! [King Apples walks into view and smiles at the camera. Another quick shot to Ed's face shows the look of disbelief. Apples smile gets bigger as he begins to speak...] APPLES - Hey Ed you stupid idiot. It;s me, King Apples! Your best pal in the WHOLE world. Well, this just shows I am always one step ahead of you. You see, I have the real Smelly. I gave him a bath in the washer and now he is forever grateful. That IMPOSTER you have in your hand is a FAKE SMELLY!! That is not the Smelly that "fired" you and that is not the Smelly that you know... this is the real Smelly Ed. Come and get him Ed.. [With that, the screen goes blank and Ed's face turns to anger. He kicks the bottom rope and stares at his "Smelly." He then charges Michael Bane and this match is under way...] Match 12 - Non Title Match Mr. Ed Rogers (with Smelly) vs "The Master of Pain" Michael Bane Here we go with Rogers and Bane. Bane is circling the man and he looks insane. Pretty much the same way he looks every week. Bane goes for the leg and Rogers with a break and a forearm to the back of Bane. Bane goes into the corner. He calls for Rogers to come. Rogers charges, Bane jumps up split-legged on the ropes, Rogers hits the buckles, and Bane rolls over the man with a sunset flip, 1.....2..and Rogers kicks out. Remember this is a non-title match. Bane runs into the ropes, springboards off and hits a cross body, 1....2....and Rogers kicks out. Bane is looking impressive early on here. Rogers with a boot to the gut, double under hook release power bomb by Rogers and the cover, 1.....2.....and Bane kicks out, getting the right shoulder up. Rogers with a dropkick but Bane moved, Bane hits a leg sweep and nails an elbow drop. Picks up Rogers and puts him down with a scoop slam. Bane is going up top, he nails a leg drop from the top, 1.......2....and Rogers kicks out. Rogers goes to the outside and is trying to clear the cobwebs. Bane is into the ropes, leaps, goes for the suicide dive but missed and went neck first into the guard railing. That must of hurt. Bane is on his feet but Rogers has a chair and he plants it in the back of Bane. Bane rolls back into the ring and Rogers goes to the second turnbuckle. Clothesline from the second turnbuckle. He covers the man, 1.......2....and a kick out by Bane. Bane is on one knee and here comes Rogers with a clothesline to the back and Bane goes over the top rope and to the floor. Rogers goes on the apron and is measuring Bane and he hits an elbow. Rogers picks up Bane and drops him throat first onto the guard railing again. Bane is back up somehow and he plants an elbow to the stomach of Rogers. He jumps over the guard railing, jumps, springboard splash off the guard railing and to the concrete floor. What a move by Bane and he throws Rogers back into the ring. Rogers is up and he dropkicks Bane and he goes down. He picks up Bane from behind, goes for the back suplex, Bane rolls out of it and hits a dropkick to the knee. What awareness by Bane. Rogers grabs him around the waist this time and brings him over with a belly to belly. He picks up Bane again and he hits a DDT. He covers Bane, 1........2.....and Bane just kicked out getting the shoulder up in time. Rogers is looking to put away Bane here. He sets Bane up on the ropes and goes up with him. He is going for a superplex but Bane with a shot to the stomach and he has him in a power bomb position. He rolls over into a sunset flip power bomb from thetop, 1........2.......no!!! Rogers just kicked out. Bane looks frustrated, I think he thought he had him. Bane stands and thinks for a moment. He grabs Rogers and pulls him over to the corner. He Irish whips him back into the neutral corner and runs, leaps for the Hurakanrana out of the corner, no. Rogers reversed it into a Tigerbomb, 1.......2.....no!!! Bane escapes once again. How do they keep kicking out? Bane is back up first and he is going upstairs again. He jumps and Rogers catches him and puts him down with a spine buster. He is signalling for his finisher and someone is running down the aisle. It's Apples? It's Apples. He is pounding away on Rogers and Bane doesn't know what the hell is going on. Bane is standing in the corner and watching Apples absolutely manhandle the exhausted Ed Rogers. There is the bell and this one is over. Obviously Rogers will win the match by Disqualification but he is paying the price right now. Apples with the Applesauce drop. Rogers is out in the ring. Apples and Bane leave the ring and Rogers is motionless. Again your winner by DQ in 16:42, Mr. Ed Rogers. MAIN EVENT Match 13 - INWI Heavyweight Championship "The Ringmaster" Tony Houston (with Mr. Flamboyant) vs Harvey Thunder (with Michael Slater) This should be an interesting match to say the least, following the attack that The Brotherhood inflicted upon Harvey Thunder, as well as Cloud Eagle, Michael Slater, and Ultimate Perversion last week. Thunder charges the ring and jump starts the match a right by Thunder..another..Houston fires back but Thunder ducks underneath a clothesline attempt..Boot to the stomach by Thunder Swinging Neckbreaker ...Thunder quickly to the second turnbuckle...leaps..Frog Splash...on Houston...And Harvey Thunder has an early advantage on the INWI Heavyweight Champion. Thunder pulls up Houston and sends him into the ropes...Houston ducks under a Thunder clothesline and rebounds off the ropes and drops Thunder with a decapitating Spinning Leg Lariat. Houston back to his feet and inserts both middle fingers into the face of the fallen Harvey Thunder. Houston pulls up Thunder...winds up the arm..Short Arm Clothesline staggers Thunder into the corner...Houston charges and Thunder raises his left knee and Houston charges hard into it..Thunder hops onto the second turnbuckle and leaps..Thunder just nailed the Heavyweight Champion with a Flying Reverse Bulldog...Thunder hooks the leg..One..two..No!!! Thunder stays on the offensive, he drops a fist on Houston and then pulls up the Champ. Thunder with a right and fires Houston into the ropes..Thunder drops his head and Houston greets that mistake with a vicious double arm DDT. Houston back to his feet and he looks bitter...Houston now stomping a mud hole in the fallen Harvey Thunder...Houston pulls up Thunder ..Full Nelson by Houston..and he trips Thunder and drives Thunder face first into the mat...Wow that was an excellent and new move by Tony Houston.....Thunder is now belly down...Houston grabs Thunder by the chin..OUCH!!! Reverse rolling neck snap on Thunder..Houston back up..and does it again...Houston stomps Thunder in the neck and pulls him to his feet...Harvey Thunder is sent into the ropes...Fireman's Carry by Houston...Too Cold Driver..Houston just hit the T.C.D. He covers...One...two...WAIT!!! that's Adam Kuhl coming out of the crowd..and he's got a chair...<SMACK> across the back of Tony Houston..<SMACK> and again....Adam Kuhl has just attacked the INWI Heavyweight Champion...Here comes the rest of the Brotherhood....and Kuhl flees the ring and exits out through the crowd to a thunderous ovation from the crowd... Unbelievable, however, I do believe that The Brotherhood and more importantly in this case, will seek and likely get revenge, and knowing the Brotherhood's M.O., at a time when it is least expected...let's get the official word. Here is your winner in 13:32 and still INWI Heavyweight Champion, via Disqualification, "The Ringmaster" Tony Houston!! =========================================================== Newark - Ladies and gentlemen, we are out of time for this week!
