We have title matches and all sorts of great things happening tonight by the Revolutionary Force in eWrestling .... let's head up to the ring for our opening bout of the night!!!!! Graves attacks right away and he just choke slammed Pig Pen!! WOW! Graves scoops up Pig Pen, sends him into the ropes and nails a big boot. Graves leaves the ring, gets on his chopper and leaves the ring… =========================================================== Newark - WOW, Graves made his debut quick and successful! Pickle has his back turned and Bledsoe catches him with a clothesline as he turns to face him. Bledsoe goes up to the top rope...A elbow smash...Pickle is hurt....Bledsoe goes up again....Pickle moves and now both are down on the mat. Bledsoe is the first to his feet he has pickle... he signals the crowd...back breaker. Bledsoe grabs Pickle by the hair and brings him to his feet... he sends Pickle to the rope... a drop kick to the head of Pickles. Bledsoe takes Pickle and applies a figure four...Pickles is about to submit...NO!!... Bledsoe breaks the hold. Bledsoe lifts Pickle...A belly to belly suplex.... Pickles is hurt. Bledsoe to the ropes again...elbow smash...how much punishment can one man take. Bledsoe whips Pickle into the corner again...a cloths line. Bledsoe scoops Pickle up and slams him to the mat. Here is your winner in 9:48, Michael Bledsoe!! =========================================================== Newark - After his rough outing at Invasion '97, Michael Bledsoe rebounded with a pretty good showing tonight against Dell Pickle. Next up is "The Master of Pain" Michael Bane as he takes on "Kodiac" Dan Harris. =========================================================== Match 3 "Kodiac" Dan Harris vs "The Master of Pain" Michael Bane Bane is a nutcase. He is really working on the arm and now he turns it and delivers a armbar. He rolls with it and it's a pin attempt, 1........2....and Harris kicks out but Bane holds onto the leg. Harris breaks the hold and gives Bane a Japanese armdrag and then a hiptoss, and then a dropkick. Very nicely done by Dan Harris. Bane is on the ropes and he leaps and nails Harris with a shoulderblock.. Harris rolls to the outside and that is going to be a mistake as here comes Bane over the top and to the floor on top of Harris wirh a summersalt plancha. That was simply unbelivable. Bane is on the top rope again and he leaps but this time he misses and he goes right into the guard rail and he really hits his ribs hard. Harris notices this and he rolls him back into the ring and is starting to put the boots to Bain's ribs. Irish whip now, and a backbody drop but Bain lands on his feet and he just dropkicked Harris in the shoulder and he yelps out in pain. Bane is on the attack once again and he has him back in that armbar. Bane with an inseguri and a cover, 1......2....and Harris kicks out and grabs Bain around the waist and takes him over with abelly to belly suplex. Bane gets up and delivers a boot to the gut, lifts him up and goes down with a piledriver. Boy this guy Bane doesn't feel pain. Bane is on the top rope again and he comes down with a missle dropkick. This guy is all over Dan Harris. He throws Harris to the outside again and he irish whips Harris right into the guard rail and he hits that bad shoulder really badly and he yells out in pain again. Harris back into the ring now and another thumb to the eye and he gets Bane in an Octopus Stretch. Harris is going to try to take advantage of Bane's ribs. He is really wrenching it on here but Bane tosses him over his back and hits an elbow o nthe top of the head and puts on an armlock front faced. Harris is not giving up though however. Bane is back up and he hits a boot to the gut, he lifts and hits a northern lights suplex with a bridge, 1......2.....and Harris kicks out. But Harris's arm looks really bad and the ref may have to stop this one. Harris is fighting with everything he has and he knocks Bane down with a flurry of rights and lefts, now a trifecta to the gut. Bane seems to be coughing up blood a little bit. Bane is into the ropes again and he dropkicks the shoulder again. Harris is definitely in trouble with his arm here. Harris with a knife-edge chop and Bane goes down. Harris is blocking out the pain. He is throwing Bane into the corner now and he is shoulder tackling Bane in the stomach. He runs to the opposite corner, runs back, dives, oh my. He went for a missle shoulder block to the stomach but Bane moved and Harris hit his shoulder on the post. Bane smells victory. Harris is still clutching his amr in the corner and he foes out blindly and Bane slaps on a Fujiwala armbar. Harris is screaming and he just tapped out. The ref calls for the bell and your winner of the match in a time of 8:18, "Master of Pain" Michael Bane. =========================================================== Newark - Michael Bane with a great win tonight over Kodiac Dan Harris.
