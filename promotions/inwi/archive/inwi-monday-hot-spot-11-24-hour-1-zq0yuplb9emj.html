[The scene then fades away and goes to the beginning of INWI Hot Spot. This is a series of shots of all of the INWI Superstars. It shows Tony Houston holding onto the INWI Heavyweight Championship as Springer gives him a Springer Cutter off of a ladder, it shows Mr. Ed Rogers and King Apples going at it, it shows James "Simply The Best" Pillow with the INWI Light Heavyweight title and Angel at his side, it shows Sam Adams draped in a U.S. Flag with Militia at his side and the New York State Title over his shoulder, it shows Solid Gold in a four way brawl with the Comforters in their infamous battle for the Unified Titles, it shows Bill Bob Woijicowski and Mr. Cherry in their wild battle for the Light Heavyweight Title and then it shows an action packed seconds of ALL INWI superstars and it ends with the screen exploding and you see INWI..... Hot Spot with the Hot Spot in flames...] INWI Presents Monday Hot Spot!! November 24, 1997 Broome County Arena, Binghamton, NY Brian Newark - Welcome ladies and gentlemen to the ON TIME MONDAY HOT SPOT!! Tonight we have four title matches on the card as well. We are the INWI, the revolutionary force in eWrestling entertainment. We also have some superstars here tonight still trying to make names for themselves!!! So ladies and gentlemen, enough of the jabbing... let's get to the ring!! And we'll have no Scoop Ropes tonight. Match 1 Pete Robinson vs Banana Robinson in the ring now and I'm sure he wants to get back on track since he lost to the "General" at the PPV. Banana with a drop toe hold and an elbow to the back. Robinson grabs the leg and trips up Banana and he goes down. Banana grabs the ropes and pulls himself up and Robinson with a forearm to the head and he throws him to the outside. Robinson with a headbutt on Banana and then he throws him into the railing hard and he goes down. Banana back up with a right hand to the mouth and then he irish whips him shoulder first into the post. He throws Robinson back into the ring and sends him into the ropes and hits a huge dropkick and Robinson goes down. Robinson back up to a vertical base and he picks up Banana and delivers a spine buster. He goes to the outside and jumps up to the top and leaps and hits a centon. He covers the man and hooks the leg, 1.........2......and a kickout by Banana. Robinson picks up Banana and delivers a DDT. He covers him and hooks the leg again, 1........ 2....and a kickout again. He throws Banana into the ropes and hits a powerslam. He picks him up and a double underhook and a suplex. He stomps on the head of Banana and he hits a legdrop. Robinson hits a short arm clothesline and he drags Banana over to the corner. He goes up to the top rope and hits a moonsalt, he covers, 1........2 ......and Banana puts his foot on the bottom rope and the ref says no. Robinson back up and Banana with a low blow and a pickup and a fallaway slam. Banana jumps and splits and he hits the split-legged moonsalt and he hooks the legs, 1.......2.......and Robinson kicks out. Banana is up on the top rope now and he is waiting for Robinson to get up and he goes for a dropkick and he misses and Robinson puts him in a Scorpion Death lock. He has it on and he is waiting for the submission. He has it on and Banana reaches and grabs the ropes and the ref is making him release the hold. Robinson back up and he is stomping on the head of Banana. Banana hits a headbutt and hits a dropkick. Robinson rolls over and he is back up and he charges Banana and Banana with a drop toe hold. He grabs the arm and he has the Fujiwala armbar on and now Banana is in the position for the submission win. Banana lets the hold go for some reason and he hits a vertical suplex. He irish whips Robinson into the ropes and Robinson climbs the ropes and turns around and hits a high-cross body and hooks the legs, 1........2.......and Banana kicks out. Banana with a scoop slam and an elbow drop. Runs into the ropes and hits a..no, he rolls him up and grabs the tights, 1...... 2.......no!!! Banana with a belly to back suplex, and a rollup, 1........2......and a kickout. Banana goes for a right hand and he misses, and Robinson turns the arm around his head and hits a northern lights suplex with a bridge, 1.........2.........3!!!! He won it with the Northern Lights suplex. Again the winner of the match in the time of 11:12, Pete Robinson. Match 2 Thugs 4 Life (with Southside Balla) vs The All Americans This should be an interesting match pitting two teams in the INWI that are on the rise. Both teams are taking their respective time just getting to the ring. The team that wins this match could possibly be in line for a title shot in the near future, and the loser will likely drop on the contender's ladder. It's going to be Gahan and Westside Playa startign it off. Both men lock up and, WP pushes Gahan into the corner...<SMACK> WOOOO Big chop by the Westside Playa..and again....WP sends Gahan across the ring into the opposite turnbuckle and charges...and runs jaw first into Gahan's jaw. Gahan scoops Wp and plants him hard on the ma, Gahan pulls up WP and sends him into the rope, High Knee to the face of WP, staggering him. Gahan scoops Westside Playa...running powerslam by Gahan...and tags in Shaffer, Shaffer is going to the top rope and leaps...Leg drop across the chest of the fallen Playa. Shaffer pulls up WP and stuns him with a big knee lift...and takes down WP with a hooking clothesline...cover.. One..two..No!! Westside Playa rolls his shoulder. Shaffer climbs to the second turnbuckle on the inside and waits for Westside Playa to get up....WP rises and Shaffer leaps...POWERSLAM BY WESTSIDE PLAYA!!! Excellent show of strength by WP... Westside playa crawling over and tags in Eastside playa...Eastside in quickly and mauls over the staggered Shaffer with a Thez Press. EP pulls up Shaffer and sends him into the ropes..Spinebuster and EP dropped down and turned the Spinebuster into a Tiger bomb..One..two..Gahan in and breaks the count. Eastside Playa pulls up Shaffer and front face lock..DDT by EP ... EP goes to the outside and climbs to the top turnbuckle and leaps..Frog Legdrop!!! Eastside Playa covers...one..two..th--No!!! Shaffer was just barely able to escape that pin attempt. Eastside Playa bounces off the ropes and Springboards of the second rope and nails a Springboard Headbutt...another cover..One..two..No!!! OUCH!!! Gahan just flew off the top rope and dropped a leg on the back of Eastside Playa head...and EP is hurting...Shaffer crawling towards his corner and tags in Gahan. Gahan pulls pu EP and nails a Fisherman's Suplex...a Gahanplex? One..two..th--No!!! EP rolls his shoulder. Gahan pulls up EP and sends him into the ropes with a forearm uppercut..Gahan bounces off the ropes and BOTH Gahan and Eastside Playa falls to the floor and are now brawling on the outside..here comes Westside Playa but Shaffer meets him and all four men are brawling outside the ring!!! The ref's count is at 5 {6..7..8..9..10..!!!} We got a countout but these two teams don't seem to care as they are still brawling...And finally some order is being retained as INWI officials make their way down to ringside and break up the melee..let's get the official word At 9:32, Both teams have been counted out. The official's decision...a DRAW!!! =========================================================== [scene opens on the locker room] Chandler: Lookie! I got my belt...but you know what...this doesn't mean **** now...I want bigger and better things...I want that world title... [drops the belt and steps away..] Chandler: But I'll use this piece of tin to catapult me to the top...Cloud Eagle, you stupid friggin injun...i already kicked your ass once to earn this belt, why in the hell do i gotta do it again? Oh well, it doesn't matter cuz i kicked your ass before and i'll do it again...I guess you get to face me cuz i whooped your little boyfriend's ass to get this belt...did he send you to come get me since he obviously can't?? Get outta my face! [slams the door on the cameraman..] =========================================================== Match 3 Ultimate Perversion vs Bradley Gold We are under way with this matchup and it will be very interesting to see how Brad does in singles action. UP with a right hand to the jaw and pushes Brad back into the corner. Knife-edge chops and Brad's chest is all red. Irish whip out of the corner followed in by a clothesline. Irish whipped him again but missed with the clothesline on the other end. Brad out of the corner and hits a dropkick. UP shakes it off and he is back to a vertical base but a dropkick to the legs by Brad and he goes down. Brad picks him up and sends him into the ropes and an elbow to the face. He holds the head down and then stomps on the face of UP. He runs into the ropes, jumps over UP, into the ropes and misses a leg drop. He is very inexperienced in singles compition. Brad trying to clear the cobwebs and Up is up on the second rope. He leaps and hits a double axehandle to the head. UP with an irish whip and a powerslam. A cover by UP, 1.......2......and Brad kicks out. UP with an armbar and takes him down with a Fujiwala armbar and he is going for the submission here, the ref is checking him and he says he is OK. UP lets him up and a kick to the ribs and a piledriver, 1.........2.......and a kickout by Brad. Brad with a thumb to the eye and he irish whips him in, leapfrog by Brad, UP goes for a clothesline, Brads duck underneath, UP goes for a tilt-a-whirl but Brad reverses it into a tombstone piledriver. Both men are on the mat now and the fans are on their feet. I think Brad is expecting to roll over and make a tag but he can't. He has to fare for himself. He rolls over and he goes for the ropes. He waits on UP and he hits a clothesline. Brad climbs the ropes and hits a high-cross body. He doesn't go for the cover, he jumps up and climbs the ropes again and he leaps and he misses a huge elbowdrop. UP on the offensive. He grabs Brad and hits a belly to belly suplex and the cover, 1.......2.....and Brad kicks out again. Some key mistakes by Brad and they might have cost him the match. Brad is back up and went for a rake to the face but he caught him and hits a knee to the stomach and a back kick to the face and Brad hits the canvas. UP clotheslines Brad and he goes flying to the outside. Brad is back up and UP is on the apron. UP jumps and hits a big forearm to the back and Brad goes flying into the railing. UP with a boot to the gut and he is going for something but Brad flips him over the back and he hits the mat. Brad picks him up and hits a powerbomb on the outside. UP's head just bounced off the mat and he could be out here. Brad rolls him into the ring and covers the man. 1......and Brad jumps off of him and he is climbing the ropes? Why is he doing that? He jumps and he misses a legdrop. UP is shaking it off and he is back up and he hits a tiger suplex and Brad's head hit the canvas hard. UP with a powerbomb and he climbs the ropes and hits a huge guillatine legdrop. He is signalling to the crowd that this match is over and someone is flying down the aisle. It's Blake Gold. He is in and he is pounding away on UP and the ref has called for the bell. Someone else is coming down the aisle, it's Cloud Eagle. He has a chair and he is slamming down on Solid Gold. The fans arte going nuts and we have a 4 way brawl in the ring. UP with a clothesline that sends Blake over the top rope and to the floor. Cloud Eagle with a chairshot on Brad and he goes to the outside. Solid Gold is still yelling at them in the ring and the winner of the match by DQ in 9:10, the Ultimate Perversion. Match 4 - Television Championship Jason "The Crusher" Chandler (with Oz) vs Cloud Eagle (with Ultimate Perversion) This match will test the new INWI Television Champion. Jason Chandler, who beat Ultimate Perversion at the Final Countdown for the title, now faces his partner in this interesting match. Eagle is already in the ring with Perversion and here comes Chandler and his manager, Oz. And man is Oz big. Chandler gets in the ring and holds the belt up and throws it at the feet of the referee. Chandler then attacks Cloud Eagle and takes him down to the mat. Look out as Chandler looks reall focused here. This match could be one of the best of the night. Chandler with a kick to the head of Eagle and then an elbow drop. He grabs Eagle, sends him into the ropes anddrops the big man with a clothesline. You can see the fire in Chandler's eyes and you know that he's been on one hell of a strak. He hasn't lost in the INWI in at least three months and I don't think he plans on losing to soon. I know he wants to keep that belt and hopefully move up to one of the bigger belts. Chandler with a knee lift to Eagle and he sets up, OH MAN.... Chandler just suplexed Eagle. I guess that's why he is billed as the master of the suplex. Chandler with a cover, 1....2....NO! Eagle kicks out and rakes the eye of Chandler. The Crusher stumbles backwards and Eagle clotheslines Chandler into a corner. Eagle climbs onto a turnbuckle and starts to pound on Chandler as the crowd counts along. (7....8....9....10!!!) Eagle hops off, Chandler staggers out of the corner and Eagle flattens him with a HUGE clothesline. Eagle with a cover and we have a new champ, 1......2.......NO!! OH MY!!! Chandler kicked out. Eagle scoops up Chandler, sends him into the ropes and grabs him in a bear hug. Chandler is trying to fight it and plants an elbow in Cloud's face. Chandler gets free and stumbles around the ring. Eagle has regained his senses and charges Chandler and tackles him to the mat. Eagle is pounding on the Television Champion!! Eagle wants that gold that has gotten away from him before. Eagle scoops up Chandler and sets him up for a big kick, but Chandler ducks!! He grabs Clous Eagle and sets up for an interesting belly to belly suplex and nails it!! Cloud railed his head there and he looks hurt. A cover by Chandler, 1.....2......3!!!! He got him!! Perversion is in the ring and Chandler rolls out of the ring!! Here is your winner in 13:29 and still INWI Television Champion, Jason "Crusher" Chandler!!!
