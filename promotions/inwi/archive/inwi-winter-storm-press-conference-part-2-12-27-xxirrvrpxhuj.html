[INWI] Winter Storm Press Conference, Part 2, 12-27

30 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= James Pillow/Vinny Vitrone =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= ["We are the Champions" by Queen begins to play as The INWI Tag Team Champion James Pillow and Vinny Vitrone make there way up to the podium. James has on tight black jeans, a white t-shirt that says "I DO WHAT I WANT" on the front, his INWI Tag Belt is around his waist. Vitrone than follows Pillow. Vitrone's sporting a shortly shaven haircut close to the scalp. He has on a pair of Oakley sunglasses. Vitrone has on a pair of bagging "Old Navy" carpenters. He's also sporting a pair of shoes with "christmas lights" all around the laces. James steps up to the mic first as the reporters start screaming for there questions to be answered. . .] James "Simply The Best" Pillow: Okay, you first. Reporter#1: James why did you turn you back on the FELLAS and. . James "Simply The Best" Pillow: Shut your damn mouth. I'm sick and tired of that bull crap, Now if you media hogs can't think of any intelligent questions then this interview will be ended right now. Reporter#2: Is there any animosity between yourself and Vitrone after you had a match against each other? James "Simply The Best" Pillow: Well it was tough. It's never easy to lose a match. . but Vinny took me to my limit and after a long grueling match he got the win. I'm just glad that if I had to lose that it was against someone as great as Vinny here. "Vindicator" Vinny Vitrone: Yea, I never got so worked up. Pillow showed me why he's the reigning N.E.W. champion. Of the N.E.W. matches all through the year, that had to be the toughest. Pillow showed me some stuff. But in the end I nailed an incredible rare maneuver to get the win. You plebeians have any questions? Bill Apter: Hi! I have a question for Mr. Vitrone. You have turned into alot!. Before, a while ago you were merely a mid carder now it looks like you've really have risen into an incredible singles competitor and obviously a great TAG TEAM eWrestler "Vindicator" Vinny Vitrone: Well, finally here in INWI they show what a star I am. Here, the lil' bro is able to perform around a roster of high talent for any ordianry man. but than again I'm a BROTHER. I've recently been screwed out of alot. As for me being a star, now? Hell, they don't call me the "hippest dog in the land for nothing." Hell, I'm even starting to put up a race with Brother Houston in the "who ended more careers" award. Bill Apter: Okay and now one for you James. How does it feel to be the first man in the INWI to hold a singles title and now a tag team title? James "Simply The Best" Pillow: FINNALLY! Apter asks something that doesn't tick me off. Well Billy Boy how do you think it feels? It feels great. I already made history in the INWI once when I became the first two time Light Heavyweight Champion, and now I'm the first to be a singles champ and a tag champ. Only members of the Brotherhood have what it takes to excell as high as myself, and Vitrone who has improved more in this past year then anyone in the history of this sport, that's why he's a Brother. Reporter#2: This is for the both of you. You will have your first title defense at Winter Storm against the Comforters. What are your plans going into this match? James "Simply The Best" Pillow: Comforters? They're getting back in the ring with us? Geez they're dumber than I thought. We kicked there worthless asses once and took the tag team gold from them. Afterwards we even gave them a little reminder of why they shouldn't bother facing us again. But it seems they didn't catch on so we have to kick another hole into their heads and knock the rest of their brains out. And when they feel the SPIKE! They will know why the Brotherhood is and always will be the most elite group in the sport of wrestling. "Vindicator" Vinny Vitrone: Preach it, Brother Pillow. Hell yea. Goin' into this match, the Comforters are gonna be the first in the INWI to feel a new surprise of the li'l bro. We proved we're the greatest team in the world on many occasions. This is I feel we're gonna see a little more of the little brutta'. Damn! This's been long enough. Brother Pillow, you ready? James "Simply The Best" Pillow: Yea, I believe you've been graced by our presence for long enough. Let's go Lil' Bro. ["WE ARE THE CHAMPIONS" comes back on as James and Vitrone make there way out.] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Tony Houston =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= DeLuise: Next on the agenda, is the INWI's Heavyweight champion, and the man who will do battle with Tommy Graves tomorrow night, in the second half of the main event, "The Ringmaster" Tony Houston. [Out from behind the curtain steps Tony Houston, who today is dressed down, wearing a pair of black sagging, Mecca jeans, a black and white Brotherhood T-Shirt, the INWI Championship is drapes over his left shoulder.] Houston: Ok ya'll let's make this quick like, I got plans..[as he turns his gaze towards an extremely attractive female in the back of the room.] [The Press room stirs] Reporter: Mr. Houston, Phil Musnick TV Guide and the New Yo-- Houston: [cutting off this dickfer short] Whoa!!! You do NOT represent the Rotten Apple, you just happen to reside there, as for you asking me any questions...well you see the door there, dun? Musnick: Yes. Houston: Well, I'm going to give you a five second head start to the door before I prove just how scripted I can be.... Musnick: Mr. Houston, please.. Houston: One... [Without further hestitation, Musnick flees the Press room to the surprising cheers from the other reporters. However, DeLuise steps to the podium.] DeLuise: Mr. Houston, you cannot come in here and start threatening the press, they do have a right to be here... Houston: Shut yer trap DeLuise...In fact I do have the right to be here, and to do anything I damn well please. You know why? DeLuise: why? Houston: Because this strap..[Houston extends the INWI Championship over his head with one hand] says I can..it says I"m the best in the INWI, not one man can hold a candle to me. The Brotherhood support me and I support The Brotherhood, one man, Sam Adams tried to break us, and well, we broke him. DeLuise: Yes well, let's please continue with the Press Conferenc-- Houston: [cutting off DeLuise] and another thing I have a right to...to end this damn press conference, I feel no need to sit here and jabber a full twenty four hours before my match tomorrow....I'm gone.. DeLuise: [grabbing Houston by the shoulder] Mr. Houston please you must contin-- [Houston turns around and with an open palm pushes the face of DeLuise away from, not a violent push, however, with meaning as DeLuise turns slightly embarrassed towards Press Row.] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Ultimate Perversion =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= <Ultimate walks up to the mic. He looks around the room. Where there used to be fans at the back, now there are none. Ultimate is alone.> Reporter 1: Ultimate, what has caused this sudden rift between you and Cloud? Ultimate: Cherry is my friend and partner. There is no rift. Reporter 2: Er... Okay... What are your thoughts on the PPV match between you and Cloud? It's obvious that Cherry is going to interfere. Ultimate: Cherry is my friend and partner. He only does what's best for me. Reporter 3: (Obviously a tabloid reporter.) Is it true you are the spawn of aliens? Ultimate: Cherry is my friend and partner. I am not the spawn of aliens. Reporter 4: (With a quizzical look) How many fingers am I holding up? Ultimate: Cherry is my friend and partner. Four. Reporter 5: (Starting to get whats going on.) Ultimate. Dance like a chicken. Ultimate: Cherry is my friend and partner. [Ultimate then proceeds to walk around stage clucking. This is soon brought to an end when from the back of the stage, Cloud Eagle rushes in carrying a chair. He smacks Ultimate firmly in the back of the head. Ultimate goes down. (Yet continues to cluck.) Cloud lifts Ultimate up and holds him from behind as a figure walks onto the stage wearing a long overcoat. OH MY GOD! It`s Trent VanDenBerg! He walks to the mic.] Trent: I`m back! [He picks up the mike stand, and swings it, hitting Ultimate square in the stomach. Ultimate tries to double up, but Cloud holds him so Trent can hit him again, and again, and again. MY GOD! They're beating him to pulp. Cherry tries to run onto the stage, but from out of nowhere, 2 huge bodyguards appear, and start to set about Cherry. Trent wraps the mike stand around Ultimate, drags him toward him, then delivers a swift headbutt, the sound of bone on bone sickening. He let's go, and Ultimate slumps to the floor. Cloud picks up the mike and looks at the reporters.] Cloud: You want something to print, I'll give you something to print. Since I first came to the INWI, I have fought with honor. Sometimes that honor wasn't reciprocated, yet still I fought with it. No more. I have been betrayed. The time for honor is gone. This is WAR. [Cloud throws the mike onto Ultimate and walks off with Trent. As they walk past the bodyguards beating up Cherry, Trent waves them off. All 4 leave the stage, leaving both Cherry and Ultimate badly beaten up.] Brian Newark - WHOA.....what a way to start off this weekend. We are out of time with the press conference today. See yoy tomorrow night. Make sure you order Winter Storm '97.....
