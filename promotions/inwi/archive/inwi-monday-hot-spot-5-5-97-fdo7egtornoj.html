[INWI] Monday Hot Spot!! 5/5/97

6 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
INWI Monday Night Hot Spot!! ============================================================ Monday, May 5, 1997 All Night Dance, Syracuse, NY ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well, this will complete our swing of upstate, New York and after our Studio show on Thursday, we will start a different state swing next weekend. Those will be announced at a later date. Welcome one and all to Monday Hot Spot!! This is the real Monday Night Show and we have three solid matches on the card for you tonight. We also have an update on Mattress for you after he was beat in a vicious attack by Mike "The Gavel" Garvey at yesterdays card. The Main Event is for the United States Championship and we have two other non-title matches on the card. Match 1 - Debut Match "The Technician" Zack Sharp vs "The Knuckle" Jimmy Chooley Well, we don't know why, but it seems like Dell Pickle didn't show up tonight, but Jimmy Chooley has stepped in for him and will take on Zack Sharp in his debut match. Sharp enter s the ring and goes after Chooley right off the bat and nails him with some quick punches and sends the Knuckle to the mat. You know, up in the stands there are like 10 or more people sitting in bright pink outfits and we have no clue who they are. Maybe we can find out what that deal is later. Sharp hits a swinging neckbreaker and Chooley looks hurt already. Sharp drives Chooley's face into the mat with a bulldog and hits a second neckbreaker. Sharp places Chooley on the top rope and goes up as well. Looks like a superplex, no a Brainbuster!! I think he calls that The Ultimate Demise! 1...2...3!! It's over that quick. Your winner in 3:42 in his Debut, "The Technician" Zack Sharp! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well, we have some comotion up in the stands and all of the people in pink have left. Michael Pontiff is in the ring getting ready for an interview, but we are not sure with who. Hey wait, look here come all of the guys in pink. What the HELL is this? They are all getting into the ring and they have shirts that say jWo, what the heck is that another ripoff of the nWo?? Let's go up to Michael Pontiff... ============================================================ Michael Pontiff - Well, I was called into the ring to conduct an interview with the men in pink, otherwise known as the jWo, whatever that is suppossed to be. The leader is Oyster Boy, the manager of John Q. Denver. Oyster Boy, what is this blatant ripoff you are coordinating? Oyster Boy - Well Pontiff, it's like this. All of these guys here are sick and tired of the life of losing. For that, we have formed the jWo, the JOBBER WORLD ORDER. And I have three words for you and the rest of the INWI, "We're Taking Over!!!" Pontiff - Oh comeon, you can't be serious. You and your guys are a bunch of jokes. Hell, I COULD take a couple of you. These guys are helpless and you are at the helm of them. What do you expect to do with these guys? Oyster Boy - If you don't watch it Pontiff, I will personally kick your ***. You talk big, but you have never been in the ring. Let me introduce the jWo, the group of the future. From the top there is John Q. Denver, Malvolio, Dell Pickle, Jerrid Hoss, Daniel Smith, Brian Johnson, Mike Jones, Bonk and Bink and PigPen. These guys are the future of the INWI and you will ALL stop laughing at them. Pontiff - Whatever Oyster Boy, it's your dream world, I'm outta here... [ With that, Oyster Boy grabs Pontiff and Pontiff floors him with a short armed clothesline. Pontiff leaves the ring laughing at them. ] ============================================================ Brian Newark - What a joke these guys are and they are standing in the ring like they own it. Oyster Boy just got flattened by Michael Pontiff and they look like they want to attempt to do something. Hey look at this, look who's coming to ringside... it's Michael Hollywood and Harvey Thunder and they are laughing like never before! ============================================================ Match ??? Or is it? Michael Hollywood/Harvey Thunder attack the jWo Hollywood and Thunder storm the ring and they are kicking some major tail! Out goes a couple of the men in pink and they look lost. Daniel Smith goes to clothesline Thunder, but Thunder ducks and Smith clotheslines Malvolio out of the ring. Hollywood just sent Pig Pen over the top and there is only a couple from the jWo left. Thunder sends Dell Pickle out and Hollywood tosses John Q. Denver and the two of them just single handedly cleared the ring. Look in the runway, there are like 10 INWI superstars just laughing at these guys and the jWo is scared to run back that way, so they exit through the stands. What a joke these guys are, I hope they are not for real... but I guess only time will tell. Hollywood and Thunder are in the ring laughing and they head back to the dressing room. Heaven help us.... ============================================================ Brian Newark - That had to be one of the dumbest things I have ever seen. I really don't think that these boys really believe they can take over. They should be thankful enough to be in the INWI... let's go back to the ring and our next match... ============================================================ Match 2 - The Former Champ Is Back ''The Icon" Pimp Daddy Brown vs "The Big Fan" Devin Terry Well, The Icon is in the ring for the first time since losing his title to Brody Thunder and it looks like he is ready for action. I am sure it's not to far down the road before Thunder and Brown meet up again for the INWI Heavweight Championship. There's the bell and we are underway. Terry strikes and takes Brown down to the mat and applys an armbar. Brown is back on his feet gets out of the armbar and nails a short armed clothesline on Terry and sends him to the mat. Brown scoops up Terry and connects with a neckbreaker and then slams him to the mat. Brown bounces off the ropes and drops a leg and another. It looks to me as though Brown has the fire in his eye again and he is looking to get his gold back. Brown picks up Terry and sends him into the ropes. Brown floows with a knee to the midsection and then drops him to the mat with a DDT! Brown points to the crowd and picks up Terry. Here comes a suplex, no it's the Pimp-a-Plex! 1...2...3!! It's over. Your winner in 8:21, "The Icon" Pimp Daddy Brown!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Before our next match, which involves Bodyguard Jones, Tiffany Love is in the back with Jones. Let's get some quick comments. ============================================================ Tiffany Love enters the hallway and sees Bodyguard Jones on the way to his next match. TL: mr.Jones, can I get a word with you? BJ: Hey lady, I don't think this is a good time. I'm on my way to put away another wimp who dares call himself a wrestler. TL: It'll only take one sec... BJ: Geez, you're annoying the hell outta me! Alright, but just for a sec. TL: mr.Jones, it seems that your feud with Psycho Sal and Mean Eugene is unending. How will you be able to concentrate on your career, and winning titles, if there's always a chance of someone looking to stab you in the back. BJ: Well, I tried to forget about them and move on, I've tried defeating them, but it seems like nothing works. It's like a huge splinter in your foot, hurting and annoying the hell outta you and not letting you go on. TL: How are you going to deal with the problem at hand? BJ: Well, we all know I'm wrestling next and chances are - there'll be an interference by the KOD. I think I'll have to wipe them out from the face of the earth before they leave me alone. So now I've decided to fight fire with fire! Psycho Sal, I'm the hunter now, and you are my prey!!! Bodyguard walks off through the curtain, receiving a loud cheer from the crowd. ============================================================ Match 3 Bodyguard Jones vs Apocalypse Well, Bodyguard Jones finally gets a match away from his former stablemates, but I am sure he will have to watch his back in this encounter. Apocalypse attacks before the bell and nails Jones in the midsection. Jones doubles over and Apocalypse pounds him to the mat with a series of forearms to the back. Apocalypse grabs Jones and puts him on his shoulder and hits a powerslam. Two count, but Jones kicks out. Apocalypse again scoops up Jones, sends him into the ropes and connects with a diving lariat. Jones rolls to the corner and Apocalypse climbs to the top ropes.... double ax handle and Jones falls to the mat again. Apocalypse hits a couple of elbows and drops a standing leg drop on the back of Jones' head. Apocalypse grabs Jones, but the Bodyguard pokes him in the eye, then puts the big man down with a clothesline. Jones is starting to kick on Apocalypse and is really doing a number on his knee. Jones grabs Apocalypse, sends him into the ropes and puts him on the mat with a dropkick. Jones climbs to the top and delivers a flying elbow! 1...2..NO! Apocalypse kicks out. Jones walks away and Apocalypse uses the ropes to help himself up. Jones runs at him and Apocalypse pulls down the top rope and Jones flys to the floor. Now Apocalypse is jabbing with the ref in charge and, like this is a surprise, here comes Psycho Sal. OW! He just crushed a chair over Jones twice and now rolls him back in the ring. Sal leaves and Apocalypse drops a leg. 1...2...3!! It's over and in a controversial way. Your winner in 11:23, Apocalypse!! MAIN EVENT United States Championship P.O. Box vs "Marvelous" Mike Quick Mike Quick wanted title shots and he is getting them and he faces a tough challenge in the new United States Champion, P.O. Box. P.O. Box finally won this title after months of trying and I don't think he plans on giving it up to soon! Quick, who is one of the, if not the, fastest men in the INWI has his sights set on the gold around P.O. Box's waist. Quick attacks Box before he even gets the belt off and drops the champ with a dropkick. Quick then climbs to the top rope and drop kicks Box from there as he stands up. Quick bounces off the ropes and drops an elbow then another. Quick then dives and headbutts Box and P.O. Box rolls out of the ring. Quick goes to the apron and dives, but Box catches him and slams him to the floor. Box then follows with a series of boots and then rolls Quick back in the ring. Box follows and climbs on top of Quick and starts to beat him into the ground. The ref pulls Box off of Quick and it looked like Bow wanted to deck the ref. Box grabs Quick by the hair and pulls him to his feet and hits him with an elbow to the bridge of his nose then chokeslams him! 1...2...NO! Quick rolls his shoulder up! Box gets back to his feet and Quick kicks him between the wickets and Box doubles over. A rollup by Quick, 1...2...NO! Quick is back to his feet and dropkicks Box as he gets to his feet. He looks to the crowd, flips them off and looks like he is setting up for his Quick Ending. Wait a minute, there is a wacko in the front row that just jumped over the rail. He has a chair, gets in the ring and nails Quick. There's the bell. This madman is all over Quick and we have no clue who he is. He has a shirt on that say's BBW, but that's all we can tell from here. He gets one more chair shot in on Quick and exits the ring and heads back through the crowd. We'll try and find out about that one by this weeks Studio Show. Your winner in 14:32 as a result of a disqualification, "Marvelous" Mike Quick!! Well, Box keeps the belt, but I don't even think he is happy about what happened here. ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well folks, we are out of time! We will try and find out who that guy was and we will hopefully be able to tell you this week at our Studio Show. Until then, I am Brian Newark for the rest of the INWI saying so long! ============================================================ This was a presentation of the INWIŽ, May 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= INWI Wrestling.....
