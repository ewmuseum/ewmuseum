[INWI] Inside The Ropes, 11-9, Part 2

11 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Newark: Welcome back to INWI's Inside The Ropes, here with me, still are Scoop Ropes and the newest member of the INWI announce team, and to say the least the man who has a unmoral political platform, Riley Michaels. Michaels: What?!? I'm no damn politician Newark I state the truth it may be the harsh truth, but it's the truth non the less...Am I right Scoop? Scoop: Without a doubt Riley. And you and I are of the same opinion of that good for jobbing, no talent, EX paper champion, The Springer. Newark: Scoop I do believe you'll eat those words... Scoop: Springer don't have the balls to do anything about it...Next up we have a member of The Brotherhood, the newest member, "The Vindicator" Vinny Vitrone, and he's got a guest. ============================================================ [The camera once again slowly begins to focus on none other than the flashing lights of a fancy restaurant in Manhattan, NY. A sleek, black, stretch-limo pulls up at the front door. You can't see who's inside because of the tinted windows. The driver gets out walks over to the back of the limo and opens the door. Outwalks Vinny Vitrone in a suit w/ a shiny, sparkling blue sportscoat. Behind him walks out a beautiful blonde who looks stunning in a beautiful long high-split dress. Vitrone grabs her arm and they walk in to the restaurant. . . . .] Restaurant Attendant- Welcome to ToTo's, how many people will be sitting at your table tonight? Vitrone- Two........... Restaurant Attendant- Well, ok. It'll be a twenty minute wait. . . Vitrone- <grins> Step aside, yo. I goin' to get somethin' to eat. R.A.-Well, ok but you must wait until we have an open table. Vitrone- Listen, beeyatch this is f'n royalty. I'm a member of the one n' only "Brotherhood". Unless ya' want to get spiked, step aside. R.A.- oh, well you didn't say that before. . . . Vitrone- jus' get me a table. . . . . . R.A.- Right this way. [The man clears the way as Vitrone and his accompanice follow. He brings them over to a private little booth in the corner. Vitrone tells him to get along & w/o an argument he does so. . . .] Vitrone's date- Well, Vinny. . . . . . I see a camera over there. . . Vitrone- Good, I have ta' say some shit. 'Tis 'bout "Pig Pen". I guess 'dis fool didn't realize when I took out his lil' buddies. . . that I'm for real. I'm not gonna lose ma' undefeated streak to some second rate loser who escaped from a damn farm. 'Tis punk's da fourth pig in Brother Bill Bob's story. He's gonna get lifted n' dropped then his ass is gonna be dragged 'round the ring by da' HHHIIIIIIPPPPPPESSSTTT DDOOOOGGGGGG in the land. Like always I dedicate my matches to a Brother, but this time. . . I'm dedicatin' it to Mr.Flamboyant. I'm gonna dismantle da' Barry Horowitz wannabe n' that's all I gotz' to say 'bout that. Next up is my first INWI Pay Per View debut. N' boy am I ready. I'm steppin' in da' ring with Mr. "Spike Me Please" himself, Michael Bledsoe. Mikey boy don't know what the "Brotherhood" means. It means "we retire people". Jus' ask the wannabe gangsta' Vito Rizzotto. Came here talked his shit, n' now where is he? Dumped on his head. Bledsoe goes out n' calls himself "The Real Deal" If you want The Real Deal than look at this biznitch beside me. . . . . . Now I'z gonna 'git somethin' to eat. . . So until the next time you have the pleasure of talking with me.............This has been: LiL' Bro "Da Master Of Revelation" " The man who put fear in the eyes of Brian Newark" "The Vindicator" Vinny Vitrone "Don't Tell Me. . . I already Know I'm Great" ============================================================= Michaels: Hahahaha Ain't The Brotherhood great!! And with this man, though he is there "Lil' Bro'" they have only gotten better. Scoop: Yeah he's good, but speaking of good, let's NOT talk about The Springer because as everybody in the eWrestling world knows, he's a yellow bellied chicken.. Newark: Everybody does not know that Mr. Ropes...and ...[From out of seemingly nowhere Springer emerges behind Scoops, who does not seem him, however Newark and Michaels does. Newark cracks a smile from ear to ear.] Scoop: And let me tell you another thing about this EX champion, his parents...Newark you know why his parents never got divorced? Well? Newark answer me? What the hell are you smiling at? You know this one?? Well the reason they never got divorced is because nobody wanted custody of him...See Newark it's about time you appreciate my sense of humor.. [Springer is still standing motionless behind Scoops, as Newark continues to smile, and Michaels taps Scoops on the shoulder and points behind him.] Scoop: What Riley?? AHHHHHHHHH!!!!! [Springer grabs Ropes and nails a right hand followed up by a SPRINGER CUTTER!!!!] Newark: AHHAAHAHAHA Springer just Springer cutterized Scoop..I love it... [Springer grabs the head set off of Scoop head..] Springer: Scoop, you piece of trash, that was only a SAMPLE of what I"m going to do to you once we get into the cage...Come next Saturday, your ass is mine.. [As Springer tosses the headset onto the prone body of Scoop, the medical unit rushes to the aid of Scoop and has to physically carry him back to the back area.] Michaels: That was uncalled for....Scoop didn't do or say anything wrong....Wait..what.YES!!!! We have some comments set from Bill Bob Woijicowski..let's not waste any time...let's hear it.. ============================================================= [The camera fades into what seems to be a maze. As the cameraman tries to find his way out he stumbles across none other than Bill Bob Woijicowski and Tiffany Love] TL: Well Bill Bob you are the eigth man in Chandlers run of the Gauntlet. So far he has looked rather impressive. BBW: Impressive my ass...he hasn't faught anyone of the Brotherhoods caliber yet...jWo...impressive...ha...they are a bunch of no talent losers like the others he has faced... TL: Most people feel he has proven himself no matter what the outcome of his final matches... BBW: Proven himself...what a joke...let me tell you about proving oneself...I have proved myself...I have held belts...I have won matches and fought against the best the INWI has to offer...I have fought against great men like Tony Houston...I have beaten Mr. Cherry not once but twice...I beat McCarter... and the list goes on...now that is proving oneself. But the most impressive proof is that I am in the Brotherhood not Chandler. Chandler the only thing you are a "Crusher" of is that bunch of jWo losers. In fact why don't you start your very own Brotherhood....the Jobberhood since that is what you are and always will be. The only reason we are fighting this week on Hot Spot (the Only ontime Monday Night Show) is because of that sniveling commissioner. I don't see how you are going to be able to handle a wrestler of my caliber. You might as well go back to your first job as a Port-o-John maintenance man. All this talk of a Gauntlet...well beat someone better than the jWo...the only Gauntlet I see around here is this very one we are standing in. TL: Is that what you believe to be true... BBW: Yes it is and if it weren't for my contract I would not ever step in the ring with someone so beneath me. I would rather wrestle his fat stankin' mother. [The camera fades out as BBW turns to leave through the maze.] ============================================================= Newark: Some interesting comments made by another member of The Brotherhood, Mr. Michaels, he apparently feels that Jason Chandler has wrestled talentless athletes here in the INWI during his respective gauntlet run...And come Hot Spot Wojicowski will do battle with that same man, Jason "The Crusher" Chandler... Michaels: Yeah you are right Newark, he'll face Chandler on Hot Spot but let's not forget Chandler's match with Slater at the PPV could mean absolutely nothing if he loses to Bill Bob. That 10th leg of the Gauntlet has been scheduled, however, he Chandler loses tomorrow, then it's just a "regular" match on the PPV, and Perversion will not have to defend his title, IF or should I say WHEN Bill Bob beats Chandler....And speaking of Brotherhood business, we have a man up next who has been dropped on his neck severly at courtesy of The Brotherhood, and come the PPV will try to make amends against Vinny Vitrone in a Dog Collar Match.. Newark: Here is the "Real Deal" Michael Bledsoe.... ============================================================= [camera fades in on Real Deal Michael Bledsoe in his locker room and he is sitting at a desk with some paper in front of him. He looks at the camera and he appears shocked but he smiles] {RDMB}- Boy, you guys never quit do you? [the camera shakes no] {RDMB}- Actually you are just in time. I have been writing up this "List" that I was working on when you came in. This is a list of both people that I have beaten and people that don't have long before they are added to the "List". Let's see here. Why doesn't the camera get a closeup of this, zoom in. [camera zooms in and there are a bunch of names] {RDMB}- Let's start from the beginning. Ah yes, here we go. Dell Pickle. Yes, he was a fine wrestler but he couldn't subdue the master of the Final Solution. Let me stress on that if I could. The Final Solution is a Crucifix Shoulder Slam from the top and some people imitate it (badly). But I am the originator. The next man up was "Kodiac" Dan Harris. Boy, I beat him in 3 minutes. Tells you what kind of stooge he is. My next match was against Jon Hammer at my first PPV. I beat him by DQ. Next up was a big four corners match against "Master of Pain" Michael Bane. I beat him fair and square in that match. Then I guess Hammer and Bane just couldn't wait to be humiliated again so I went into a match at my second PPV and I beat both of them in a Triangle Steel Cage Match. Another point I want to stress on, I beat Michael Bane one on one, then I beat him and Hammer again, and then he gets a World Title Shot? What the hell is that about. Where is my title shot? Speaking of title shots, after the bloody steel cage match with both men beating on me for an hour, a little later on in the night the commish says, "Why don't you come back from the hospital, crawl your way down the aisle with an IV unit on, and fight the fresh US Champ, Sam Adams. So I did, and just the fact that I went down there is major and I lost. Name number 2 on the hit list, Sam Adams. So then after a week off to heal, I come back and I meet the Springer. But, before the thing starts, I get jumped in the hall and get spike piledrived on my head, broke a vertibre, and then still wrestled and pulled off good moves, but I lost. So then the next week, I am picked to be in the Guantlet against Chandler. Fine, I got nothing against him. So before that in the aisle going to the ring, I get jumped again. By the same two lovely, classy people as before. I get in the ring, have Chandler dead in his place ready to end his little dream of his and the two asses jump me again. Big Bob, number 3 on the hit list. Now, this week I have to face Mr. Harvey Thunder. Number 4 on my hit list. Now you may be asking who is # 1. It's you Vinnie Vitrone. The dog collar match at the PPV, I am going to turn The Brotherhood into the biggest joke in wrestling and the foundation will crumble, and the first brick is Vinnie Vitrone. Sam Adams, I haven't forgotten you though. Your'e time will come, I guaruntee it. Now, if you will excuse me, I have some work to do. [Camera fades out as Bledsoe picks up the pen and starts to write again. He thinks to himself for a moment and then starts to laugh. Camera fades] ============================================================= Newark: WHOA!!! Some real bold statements from The Real Deal and apparently he's got a list and .. Michaels: [cutting off Newark] Schindler had a list that meant something, who the hell is this guy? Michael Bledsoe...the only thing that he proved, well actually two...First, he proved that he takes beatings well, and secondly he proved he's a humanoid..The Brotherhood will crumble?, The Brotherhood is a joke, well That JOKE Broke your vertabrae, and come the PPV your next well be stretched out all in part to "The Vindicator" Vinny Vitrone..So hang on, 'cause Bledsoe your neck will be hanged.. Newark: Next up we have a "protector" or sorts, a body guard if you will, of the commissioner, Peter DeLuise, the man is Pete Robinson, and come the PPV will attempt to keep the commish-hood, within DeLuise's hands....let's hear from him... ============================================================= (The scene is in front of a large INWI banner, which is hung as a backdrop on the wall. Pete Robinson is standing in front of the banner. He is wearing a sweatshirt with the American flag taking up the entire front, a black INWI baseball cap, and blue jeans.) Pete Robinson: The match has been signed. I am set to take on Sam Adams for that title and, more importantly, to keep you from becoming comissioner and screwing up the INWI. But, first I have to face Bodyguard Jones on Monday Hot Spot. Well, Mr. Jones, I wish you the best of luck, but I'll be coming forward at full steam. I'm not going to look past you, because that could cause me to lose. I can't let that happen, and I won't let that happen. This victory is important to give me that needed momentum into Final Countdown. Prepare to see, Mr. Jones, what one man can do when he is backed by an entire nation. And that's not just any nation, but the greatest nation on earth! And at Final Countdown, Sam Adams you will find out even more so. I will be ready for you. It will be time for you to pay for your crimes against DeLuise, against the INWI, and against the American people. ============================================================= Newark: Damn, the guy is really full of spirit, and really makes you happy to be an American and a INWI member doesn't it Mr. Michaels.. Michaels: YACK YACK, damnit, what the hell is wrong with people, they lack originalty so they make believe they're patriotic? This guy probably was a hot dog fender at the local street corner at the INWI Arena...Now, 'cause he's kind of big, he's a threat, well, Mr. Robinson, [in a singing tone] Here's to you Mr. Robinson, come the PPV will find if you know, how to throw..here's to you Mr. Robinson, Sam Adams is gonna shove his fist down your throat...oh oh oh...HAHAHAHAHAHAHA Newark: WILL YOU STOP!!! And speaking of Sam Adams we have some pre-recorded comments from the New York Champion... Michaels: Indeed, a true patriot, not this Johnny_come_lately..... ============================================================= [Fade from black. Sam Adams is sitting on a chair beside his demands, piled on the floor. Sam is wearing a red, white, and blue jacket.] [Sam Adams] Comments? You commies want comments and you think that I am going to give you comments? Tough luck. Thats the problem with you commies, you think that the nice 'ol federal government is going to make sure that you get through life okay. You lazy bums... Oh... and by the way: McCarter, once again we are going to meet mano-e-mano in the ring... The last time, me and Militia gave you problems... This time we are going to end your problems, PERMINATLEY. [Fade to black.] ============================================================= Newark: As you were saying...'A true patriot...'? Mr. Michaels? Michaels: Yeah Newark...I--I---I da_ da_ don't stutt___stutter...Adams will do battle this Monday Night...Bare in mind, Monday Night, not several days later, but THIS MONDAY Night, Sam Adams and one of his long time foes, in former Light heavyweight Champion, David McCarter will do battle, perhaps for another time, or who knows, maybe for the last time. Newark: And with both of these men being involved in huge matches at the PPV, runs ins could and should be expected...Next up we have the Most Special Personality in the INWI, and he just so happens to be our Northeast Champion as well, fresh out the Asylum, Mr. Ed Rogers along with Tiffany Love.. Michaels: YES!! This guy may be nuts but he is hilarious.... ============================================================= [Fade from black.] [SCENE: A park. Ed Rogers is wearing a sweater and slacks. Tiffany Love runs up from behind, holding a microphone to his mouth.] [Tiffany Love] Mr. Rogers, a few words about your upcoming Tommy Graves. [Ed Rogers] What kind of words do you want? [Tiffany Love looks confused.] [Ed Rogers] Do you want nouns? If so, "loser", "moron", "idiot", and "inbred" come to mind. Do you want verbs? "will lose", "will bleed", "will submit". All of them work and when used together, they make a sentence: "That loser will lose." See how wonderfully they work together? [Tiffany Love] Uh, I was hoping for something more substantiall than that, Mr. Rogers. "That loser will lose." doesn't really add much to the pre-match hype. [Ed Rogers] And this match really shouldn't have much hype. I mean he is a loser, I am a moron, and if we fight then the loser loses and the moron wins while he gets beat up by King Apples and his fake manager. [Tiffany Love] Wait a second... You are predicting that you are going to be attacked by King Apples? [Ed Rogers] Not only do I predict it, but I am going to promise it. [Tiffany Love] How can you make such a statement? [Ed Rogers] You'd think the same thing if you replaced his toothpaste with superglue. [Rogers shows Tiffany a half-full tube of Colgate. Fade to black.] ============================================================= Newark: HAHHAHAHAHAAHA You said it Riley, he is a funny guy..he replaced the King's toothpaste with Superglue, maybe we won't be hearing from the #1 Contender to Rogers' strap.. Michaels: I never said this guy was funny, he's sick and demented, but it's all good, so what Apples won't be able to open his mouth this week, but rest assured he'll open some whoop ass, to steal a cliche, on Rogers.... Newark: Mr. Michaels I am receiving word that you may like.. Michaels: What that? YES!!! Carmen Electra is here for me.... Newark: Uh..haha. No....the INWI Heavyweight champion and co-founder of The Brotherhood, "The Ringmaster" Tony Houston is set to give us some comments...and come Monday Night he'll defend his Heavyweight title against the light heavyweight chamion, James "Simply The Best" Pillow. Michaels: Uh..well that of course was going to be what I said next....take it away Brother Houston.... ============================================================= [The scene slowly fades from the crowded and congested streets of downtown Brooklyn...Brooklyn's Finest by Jay_Z and Biggie Smalls can be detected from a seemingly unknown source..] [The camera slowly begins to pull up from the street and up...and up..and then focuses on the top of one of the many studio style apartment buildings. The camera crew slowly make their way down what appears to be a familar corridor..Several extragent pictures of some of life's warriors are covering the walls of the private corridor....Rocky Marciano, Muhammad Ali, "Smokin' " Joe Frazier, Ghandi?? No not Ghandi....amongst others..Th camera crew approaches the only door in the corridor, other than the stair case, and the elevator, and knock, and is immediately greated by a large, bellowing bark. The camera crew immediately begins to realize where they are...] [A voice is heard through the door.] Voice: Cujo, come 'ere...bring 'yer ass.... [The door slowly opens and a man who is wearing a black "BROTHERHOOD" T-Shirt with the sleeves cut off, revealing toned, yet not muscle bound arms, the man is wearing a navy blue, Ralph Loren Polotm hat, and is wearing a pair of black workout pants.....and no more then three feet behind him is that dreaded 130lbs German Rottweiler....The man is the INWI Heavyweight Champion, "The Ringmaster" Tony Houston.] Houston: Aight ya''ll bring yer asses... [The camera crew enter the dwelling and the Rottweiler quickly takes a seat on one of several leather love seats...Houston however quickly sits in his thronelike" black leather recliner, the wall behind him reveals dozens of accomplishments, several championship belts, including the ones that were "bought" and including the cherished INWI Heavyweight Championship.] Houston: Have a seat ya'..Well, ya'this Monday night the entire wrestling world get a treat...they's gets ta' see me, THE one and only Ringmaster, not a title self applied like so many of 'deez egotistical punks, but a title that has been earned, by beatin' 32 of the best the INWI has ta' offer...ya'll know it's been a while since I've looked forward for a title defense as much as 'dis one comin' up...Pillow boy, you's wanna come out here in The Brotherhood's stompin' ground 'n challenge me, call me scared...well, son...let's take a look at just who was scared.... [Houston picks up his universal remote and turns the television and VCR with a simple push of a button... The screen shows last week's Hot Spot and has Pillow in the ring, stating many thing, the main being that Tony Houston is scared....Houston replays it...and again...and again...then let's it play and the reveals the ultimate, Homer Simpson DOOOHH look on Pillow's face as Houston makes his way from the back, Houston then freezes the frame.] Houston: Now Jimmy boy...look at that face..[points to the screen] Tell me, if that isn't a look of somebody realizing they just ****** up, then I dont' know what iz...Son, I ain't even tryin' to take up any time, 'cause well, son, ya' ain't even worth it, 'n I got mo' shiznit to do...but son, Pillow, come Monday..you's gonna be Zero and two 'gainst me...'n yer just lucky I don't drop some pounds 'n take that title of yers..but hey..you've already tainted that strap...'n the longer ya' hold it, 'da longer ya' discredit it..only when a Brother takes hold of that strap will any juice be put back inta'...and by the way, Jimmy boy, looks as if ya' gonna be zero and two 'dis week 'gainst the Brotherhood, as at Final Countdown, Brother Bill Bob will take that strap 'n kick yer teeth down yer throat...now ya'll git...Watch 'em Cuj' watch 'em... [With that, the large Rotty begins to rise off of the sofa, and begins to growl loudly which quicly prompts the camera crew to flee the home of the Heavyweight champion..] ============================================================= Newark: Well our heavyweight champion sure does have a way with words wouldn't you agree, Mr. Michaels? [Riley Michaels is on the phone talking with somebody, but he doesn't realize that they are back on the air..] Michaels: [still on the phone] That's right, tickets for Final Countdown go on sale soon, well don't get smart with me or I'll slap you in the mouth....Do you want me to knock you out? Newark: We'll we're out of time....for Riley Michaels...and the knocked out Scoop Ropes..I"m Brian Newark........ Michaels: [still on the phone and looks up to see he's back on the air] Ok mom, I'll talk to you later...Whoa Newark I got this...See ya' on Monday Night Hot Spot. =========================================================== =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The INWI™ "The revolutionary force in E-Wrestling" Visit us on the Internet: http://www.catskill.net/harmer For info, E-mail: [detail withheld] INWI®™, November 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
