[INWI] Invasion '97 PPV, 10-5, Free 4 All

5 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Invasion '97 Free 4 All Syracuse, NY October 5, 1997 ===========================================================Joseph Collins - Welcome everyone to the Free 4 All for Invasion '97. I am Joseph Collins, stepping in for Brina Newark because he will be manning the booth for the pay per view. With me is Scoop Ropes. Scoop, what a card we have tonight. Scoop - It is a great card and what matches we have tonight. I can't wait for that bell to ring in the first match. Hopefully the NEW will just stay away and we won't have to embarass them. Well, enough of this chatter, let's get to the comments Collins. Don't tell me you're just as big of a moron as Newark is. Collins - Let's hear from Harvey Thunder and Michael Slater earlier this week. (Tiffany Love is standing in the Lockerroom with the Current INWI Television Champion, Harvey Thunder and Michael Slater.) Love: Mr. Thunder, How does it feel to be the champion again? Thunder: It feels good!! I got my TV back!!! Slater: Thats right, This title is now back where it belongs!!!! Love: I know that Ultimate Perversion disagrees with that statement!!! Slater: Perversion is crying that he was robbed of the title. Well, Ms. Love, Let me ask you a question. If you owned something and I took it from you without your permisssion then that would be stealing, right? Love: Yes. It most definately would. Slater: Now lets say you knew I took it and while I was in the ring, you snuck into my locker and took it back. Would this mean that you stole as well? Love: No, Not if it was mine to begin with. Slater: Exactly. Perversion keeps forgetting that HE stole this title from Harvey, all I did was help Harvey get back what was rightfully his to begin with. Do you see my point? Love: Well, Yes But... Slater: It doesn't really matter what you, Ultimate Perversion, Cloud Eagle, or anyone else thinks. Harvey Thunder is the rightful Television champion, The title was stolen from him and I helped get it back. A few weeks ago I told everyone that they would see us do some things that they may not like. Well, I warned you. Love: Yes, But it just doesn't seem right to do something that you said was wrong when somebody else did it!!! Slater: You have a point. From now on you won't see anymore complaining from us. Instead you will just see results. Hit us and we will hit back harder!!!! This interview is over!!! (Slater and Thunder storm out of the room) Scoop - These guys actually look like they have a fighting out look now. These guys are determined. Collins - That is true Scoop. And what matches we will have tonight with those two and Perversion and Eagle. This next interview was taped after Hot Spot went off the air this past week. Tony Houston responds to Adam Kuhl's little attack... [The scene fades into the lockeroom following the attack on "Ringmaster"Tony Houston after his match again, umm, tough John Q. Denver.] [Houston is scene making his way from the lockeroom with a rather unaffected appearance on his face. Houston is dressed in a long, black, leather, trench coat which is hanging loosely off Houston. Showing his black, slightly sagging, Nautica pants, and black steel toed Timberlain boots.] [Houston exits the building and makes his way towards his 1996 black HummVeey, and opens the door and sits in it, and then turns his attention to the camera. And a following Tiffany Love.] Houston: What's up baby boo..With yer ol' fine ass anyways, what can I, Tony Houston, do for you. Love: Would you care to make any comments regarding Adam Kuhl's attack and commetns? Houston: Pssh! Ya' think, Yeah let me say a few words 'bout this lil' bastard. First, son, you wasn't in da' Ringmaster tournament, for a reason, you don't have the ability...second, dun, I did lose to yer boy Cougar, but check it out, I done whooped his sorry ass in the Ringmaster tournament...And not to sound like I don't care...But...Look what happens when you beat Tony Houston. You wind up six feet under..They ever find the guy who crashed into ol' Cougar boy..? Love: Mr. HOUSTON That is not right!! You have no class!! Houston: [almost insulted at Love's comments] What?!? No class? Love hop your ass up in here and grab yer ankles and I'll show ya class. [Love drops the microphone and storms back into the building. Houston then instructs the camera man to hand him the mic, which the camera man quickly do.] Houston: Bye Bye Biznitch. Now anyways, Kuhl...ya wanna challenge whom I fight in the INWI, that's some shiznit, considering who you've been in the ring with. Son, you ain't even a quick shine of sunlight...They say even the sun shines on a dogs ass, but Kuhl...you ain't even a dog..So when does the sun shine on you? It's simple, it don't you talk all this 'bout how on Cougar's death bed, you made this promise to finsih Cougar's feuds...well, first stop sounding like good ol' JR, dumb ass, and second, talk all you want...But maybe as Cougar knew, you have to DO it, not talk 'bout it..So boy, you wanna piece of me..Bring it done..or we can make it a tag match..Me and King Apples vs. Kuhl and Cougar...In a no holds barred match..Ya'll might even have a slight advantage...'cause ya' knwo Cougar can't feel no pain now....oh yeah [sarcastically] R.I.P. Cougar. hahaha [Houston continues laughing as he starts his Hummer and takes off into the distance..] Scoop - Houston is a dangerous person. He will have a great shot tonight to win the title against the Springer. And Kuhl has picked on a serious group in the Brotherhood. Collins - True... very true. Let's hear from another member of the Brotherhood... Bil Bob Woijicowski... ( the lights fade into an orchard...An empty twelve pack of Genny Red-Eye...most of which are crushed...liews under a Cherry tree.) ***Tiffany Love is seen in the orchard with Bill Bob Woijicowski and his rottweiler Jake. Bill Bob is wearing blue jeans, dark brown cowboy boots, a black NY Yankee Hat, and a black "Brotherhood eats the N.E.W." shirt...he also has a patch on his ass that reads..."N.E.W. SUCKS"*** TL: Bill are you ready for your match in the upcoming PPV? It is going to be tough facing a brother of a member of the Brotherhood. BBW: That is a load of **** Tiff... He is no longer a brother of da almighty King himself...BURPPPPP...the only brothers the brotherhood have are eachother and the brothers or something... you know what I mean....Hiccccup...don't cha Tiff? TL: I guess... What about your brother Jim? BBW: OH Yeah....BURRRRRPPP.... he is my brother ....that's because he's blood.....get it? TL: Whatever...So tell me about the match... BBW: Well I am gonna be in that there ladder match...wait what is a ladder match....BURRRPPP...oh no that is gonna be the Ringmaster himself....damn who am I wrastlin agin again? TL: You are wrestling against Cherry...Apples brother. You really are a drunk. BBW: Yes I am!!! NO wait I am in that three way cage thing a majigger arent I? TL: Never mind. BBW: I am gonna kick Kodiak Chew Dan' Hairy's ass....Yes I AM!!! TL: Some one get me outta here. BBW:Well no one 's a stoppin yer ass so get witch yer stankin self and interview someone a little more yer type like that little ***** Pillow Biter....BURRRPPP...HA HA HA.!!! TL: You need to get some manners. BBW: I have one wanna see it....HiCCCCUP!! TL: I am gone. ***The lights fade out onto Bill Bob 's passed out body under a tree...his dog Jake is licking his face in a vain attempt to wake him*** Scoop - HAHAHAHAHAHA.... man, he was blasted! Collins - Well, that he was. Anyways Scoop... we made this shorter for a reason... because that's allw e have on this Free 4 All. The people don't want to see the Free 4 All, they want to see the Pay Per View itself. Let's get it on! =============================================================-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The INWI™ "The revolutionary force in E-Wrestling" Visit us on the Internet: http://www.catskill.net/harmer For info, E-mail: [detail withheld] INWI®™, October 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
