Winter Storm '97 No Holds Barred Ultimate Perversion vs Cloud Eagle (with Trent VenDenBerg) Tren VenDenBerg is back to haunt Perversion and wow, this one should be an interesting match. Here comes Trent VenDenBerg and he has a chair and NAILS Perversion!! Perversion falls to the mat and he is hurting if not out. VenDenBerg throws water on Cloud and wakes him up.
