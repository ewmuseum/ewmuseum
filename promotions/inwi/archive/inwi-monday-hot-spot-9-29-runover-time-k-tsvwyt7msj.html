[INWI] Monday Hot Spot, 9-29, runover time

30 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Hot Spot runover...] Brian Newark - Welcome back ladies and gentlemen and we have been given the ok to go on and continue our show and take as long as we need to finish. That's why we get the ratings and why we are the best promotion in the world. We get the support. Let's go to our Main Event. Match 12 - INWI United States Championship Sam "The Minuteman" Adams vs Bodyguard Jones As Adams was giving his US championship belt to the ref, B.G. Jones bounces off the ropes and sent Adams staggering into the corner with a huge shoulder block. Jones with a monster knee lift on the champion in the corner..Another...and a third...Jones fires Adams back across the ring....Adams sprinboards off the ropes and nails Jones with a high crossed body press...One..-No strong kick out by Jones. Adams back up first and nails a dropkick to the face of Jones, who was only on his knees. Adams bounces off the ropes and crashes on Jones with a Senton Splash. Adams pulls up Jones and nails a jab...another...and a devastating uppercut, stuns Jones....Adams off the ropes...Sprinboard Hurricarana into a roll up by Adams..One..Two..Th--No!!! Kickout out by The Bodyguard. Adams pulls up Jones and fires him into the corner...Adams charges Handspring Elbow Dive crashing across Jones. Jones staggers out of the corner and Adams connects with a Bulldog. Cover by Adams..One.Two..Th-No!!! Another kickout by Jones. Adams up..Low Blow by Jones drops Adams. Jones uses the ropes to pulls himself back up, and a stern stomp to the ribs of Adams...Jones pulls up Adams and fires him hard into the ropes..Adams ducks a clothesline..but is nearly beheaded by a Big Boot by Jones. Jones pulls up Adams..Belly to Belly Suplex...One..Two..Th--No!!!! Kickout by Adams..we almost had a new US champion. Jones pulls Adams to his feet and scoops him....Leaping Side Walk Slam by Jones..Jones off the ropes and drops a HUGE legdrop across the chest of Adams...Hooks the leg..one..Two..Th--No!!! Adams again kicks out. Jones pulls up Adams and fires him into the ropes...Adams again ducks a clothesline..and ducks under the big boot..and nails a Sprinboard Clothesline that drops Jones...Adams slowly making his way to his feet, and Jones is starting to rise now...Superkick by Adams drops Jones...Adams climbs to the top turnbuckle...Moonsault by Adams!!! Hooks the leg...one..Two..Thr--No!!!! Jones draped his leg over the bottom rope. Adams pulls Jones to his feet and nails a forearm uppercut that staggers Jones..Jones fires back with a right..Adams with a right...Jones with a headbutt stuns Adams....Jones is setting up for the Powerbomb!!! He lifts..Adams flipped through and landed on his feet, UNBELIEVABLE!!!! No!! but Jones drops him with a lethal clothesline. Jones pulls up Adams and sets him on the top turnbuckle...Adams fires back with a right hand....another....a poke to the eye...well um...it works....Adams grabs Jones...Spinning DDT on Jones...Jones' head was just spiked into the mat..Adams covers...One..Two...Three!!!! He got him..Great match displayed by the United States champion and Patriot. Sam Adams. Here is your winner in 14:30, via pinfall, Sam "Minuteman" Adams retaining the INWI United States Championship. ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well folks we are now out of time... but as we leave.... we want to bring you one more thing from the NEW Pay Per View.... this is what "The Ringmaster" Tony Houston thinks of the NEW.... watch and enjoy. So, until this weekend.... keep it in the ring.... ============================================================ [The screen fades into Billy Lane standing in front of a lockeroom door that on the front above a large star, reads "Cold & Calculating"/"Ringmaster" Tony Houston on a gold template.] [Lane knocks on the door...] Lane: Hello? Mr. Houston? Are you there? <KNOCK> <KNOCK> Lane: Hello?!? Well Shorty I guess we won't have a word from the North American Champion... Shorty: Lane open the door and see if he's in there, possibly he's passed out..he's turned into such a damn drunk since he sold out.... Lane: [almost fearing his safety] uhh.....no Shorty.. Shorty: Do IT OR YOU'RE FIRED!!! Lane: damnit.. [Lane opens the door cautiously and reveals a television turned on, a several chair turned over and unoccupied laying on the floor, with a empty case of Killians Red and a half empty bottle of Jose Cuervo resting on the center of the 'round table'. But no North American Champion, Tony Houston is visible.] Lane: Well, it would appear that we will not get the interview with North American Champion Tony Houston. Perhaps we'll try again later this evening as his time for his title defense draws closer... [Lane exits Houston's lockeroom and enters the bathroom, and the camera crew for some odd reason follow him in, but begins to fade...] Voice: Who da hell is 'dat? [Camera stops it's fading out, and focuses on a stall, where two black steel toed Timberlain's can be seen with a pair of dark blue Tommy Hilfiger pants can be seen around a man's ankles, as he is "relieving" himself.] Lane: Uh...ummm..It's Billy Lane, I was hoping to conduct an interview with Tony Ho-- [Lane is cut off] Voice: It's the Cold & Calculating Ringmaster...'n ya' found 'em...So start yer interview..biznitch... Lane: [befuddled] Uh...ok...I can wait... Houston: Damnit...don't make me git up 'n shit on this floor and beat yer ass. Lane: Ok.. [Lane almost talking to the stall door.] Lane: Well my first question is an obvious one...What do you think of your opponent tonight, "Tough" Tommy Chaos...? Houston: Wel, first, 'da only thing Tough 'bout tonight is' my tryin' ta' pass 'dat nasty ass mexican food I had. But as far as my feelin's on Chaos..well....[grunts, and the sounds of something dropping into the toilet water] Answer yer question? Lane: Umm..that's disgusting.. Houston: Damnit Lane, if ya' wanna see disgusting...I'll chuck one of 'deez terds yer way...I was just tryin' to be honest..'n ya' know that's my best qualitity..ya' asked a question, 'n I answered it.. Lane: Umm...Ok...well, next I must ask, ewrestling fans, have seen you in your apparently new home of the INWI, you have been wrestling obviously under the influence of... Houston: Mine yer bidness. Ya' don't even need ta' [grunts and water splashes again] AHHHHHHH!!!! As I wuz sayin' ya' don't even need ta' worry 'bout what Tony Houston iz doin', all ya'll need to worry 'bout is if I can defend my strap. [Houston then lowers his hands and shows the North American Championship belt under the stall door towards the camera.] Houston: And Lane, ya' can love me, hate me, cheer fer me, boo fer me, but one thin' ya'll can deny is I am, along with King Apples are yer top two draws, in the N.E.W. And ain't no punk, who kisses ass to da' bum assed Fellas is gonna take my strap...Tommy Chaos? Who da' hell has he beatin'? Anybody..probably not, if Shorty and OldFella think by jus' trowin' somebody up against me in an' attempt to get MY belt, we'll ya'll, ya'll can kills that friggin' plan. Not only is Chaos not in the same league as me as wrestler, but he ain't even in my same class as a fighter...'n dat's what's it's all 'bout. Lane: Well, that can be debated..my next quest-...<SNIFF> <SNIFF> What's that smell? Houston: Christ Lane yer stupid..I just finished tellin' ya I had Mexican food last night...Do the math... Lane: King Apples was to say the least avoiding the question if he knows who Jim Whitten's INWI opponent would be..would you care to reveal anything... Houston: Uh sure....[grunts yet again, and a transparent cloud fills the restroom] He's gonna whoop Whitten's ass. Perhaps more so then WE already did. Next question... Lane: Well, I guess, we will have to see what happens... Houston: Yes we will...biznitch...And for ol' Crusher Whitten and his confused personality, boy, those who get dropped on their necks shall be wiser then dare talk smack to da' Brotherhood....'Bunch of pussies' huh? [sounds of laugher is heard from behind the stall door] Boy, ya' keep askin' for 'nother ass beatin'. damn, not e'ven Bloom is THAT stupid...Son, if ya' thought what that cornflake received at the hands of the Brotherhood was bad..well, son, prepare to join a late, great INWI-ite, "Jammin'" Jimmy Cougar in da' funeral parlor..'cause whitten you'd best have God on yer side WHEN we get 'hold of ya'. And boy, it's not a matta' of if we get ya', it's only WHEN!!! Lane: Well, Mr. Houston I guess that concl-- [Lane is cut off, as the pants are slowly pulled up and the man in the stall rises and apparently does his business, flushes the toilet and exits the stall with the North American Championship over his left shoulder, and the INWI Ringmaster strap draped over his right shoulder..] [Houston's hair as always is flawless and his intimidating cold, blue eyes, simply stare into the camera as he walks towards the sink to wash his hands..he then look into the mirror to see Lane and the camera crew standing behind him.] Houston: And as for ol' Tough Tommy Chaos, well, son. ya' see 'deez belts...[pats both of them] This was for the loot [pats NEW North American belt] and this was to prove what everybody knows [pats INWI Ringmaster belt] that I am in fact the true master of the ring..not only in the INWI but all over the world. And to earn this strap, I defeated several wrestlers in a 32 man tournament, and each and anyone of 'em could have easily dismembered ya', so what do ya' think the winner of such a tourney is gonna do to ya'? Chaos you had best lock your door, put all your furniture near the door, 'cause you have crossed into the sights of the Cold & Calculating Ringmaster, and ain't nothin' you can do to avoid an asswhoopin'.. Hidin' will help ya' not..Yer a marked man... [Houston finishes drying his hands and exits the restroom leaving Lane standing wondering if what he just experienced really happened....a live interview taken place in the Men's room...] [screen fades] =========================================================== =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The INWI™ "The revolutionary force in E-Wrestling" Visit us on the Internet: http://www.catskill.net/harmer For info, E-mail: [detail withheld] INWI®™, September 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
