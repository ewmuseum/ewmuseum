[INWI] Inside The Ropes, 10-19

20 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Inside The Ropes INWI Studios, October 19, 1997 =========================================================== Brian Newark - Welcome fans to this weeks Inside The Ropes. What excitement we had last week on Hot Spot as a new Light Heavyweight Champion was born in Mr. Cherry. This week there is some excitement as Four people will vye for that championship in three exciting matches this week. We could essentially have two new champs crowned in one night. But before we get to our show tonight, let's got to Scoop Ropes.... our co-host! Scoop - Hello world, it's your favorite person in the whole world here. I am the greatest announcer and hell... I just thought of something.. if the Springer was Champion when I still wrestled and he was a champion... I would of no doubt been a champion. Newark - If you say so Scoop. Well, let's hear from James Pillow right after he lost his title last week. <SCENE-Inside the dressing room of James Pillow after Hot Spot. It is empty but then the dorr flies open and James comes in and isnt very happy. Angel follows trying to calm him down. He is still in his ring attire, he superkicks his locker and sits down on a bench, Angel sits next to him still trying to calm him down. James gets back up and gets right up in the camara. . .> James "Simply The Best" Pillow:A Travesty. . A Mother ******* TRAVESTY OF JUSTICE HAS TAKEN PLACE HERE IN THE INWI! The INWI is out to destroy me now, first they allow those two pieces of low-life garbage at ringside during my match and then I lose my title because Mr. Cherry held onto my tighs and everything else he could get his hands on. The ref saw it CLEAR AS DAY, But did he stop the count? NO! He wanted me to lose. <kicks locker again> Angel: <gets up and walks over to James, starts kissing him> James honey, calm down. Look at it this way next Monday you will be able to become a two time INWI Light Heavyweigh Champion. You did that in N.E.W. and after you won it from Cherry for the second time you never lost it. Remember? James "Simply The Best" Pillow: <starts to compose himself> Yeah you're right. They may have taken it from me, but I'm only going to win it back. And no one can stop me. <laughs> So they made a four way dance for the title. Quite frankly once I kicked McCarters ass for the title he should've been moved way down the list of contenders. . .but it doesnt matter, I beat him once I can do it again. As for you Woijicowski, you piece of Brotherhood trash. Beating you is going to be the most enjoyable of them all. Once Houston and Apples see you get your ass kicked by me do you think that they're going to say "Oh that's ok you had a bad night" I dont think so, you'll be planted right on your head faster then you can blink. And Mr. Cherry, well I guess this makes us even again,Eh? 3 wins apiece. Well this Monday I'm going to break the tie once again as I take back what is rightfully mine and that is the INWI Light Heavyweight Champion. Take a good look at me I am going to be a Two Time champ. . because I am. . . . SIMPLY THE BEST! Scoop - Awww.... Pillow is mad. What a shame. What's he think he is, some sort of a god? Hey, I personally thought Cherry pinned him cleanly. Newark - You were obviously not watching the same match. Let's hear from the new champion, Mr. Cherry. [Mr Cherry sits back in the lockerroom at the Scoreboard in Albany, NY after winning his INWI Light Heavyweight Championship. He has the belt around his waist and has a big smile on his face...] CHERRY: You know Pillow, we have met times before. And it always seems like the titles are on the line. Well, I just proved how much better I am than you by getting another win over your sorry ass and winning yet another title from you. It feels good to get some gold again and look... you are bare again. No gold for you. That really sucks doesn't it? I almost hated beating you..... well, no... actually I loved it. Because I hate you. [Cherry reaches into his bag and grabs a bottle of spring water. He takes a couple of slugs of it and then puts it down.] CHERRY: You know though Pillow, it was almost as easy as it used to be... beating you that is. And I have to say, the INWI belts are nice and it will look good with the others I have beaten you for. As for this weeks Hot Spot, after I beat the hell out of that hick Bill Bob whatever his name is and hopefully you beat McCarter, I'll get to whip your ass again. But, if McCarter beats you.... I'll just beat the hell out of him. As for you Bill Bob... you claim you're so good. You screw around with a former friend of mine. And MORON, Apples is not my BROTHER he was a FRIEND. I realize where you're from there's no difference becaue you are all related, but I assure you we don't go that way up here. So you stupid red neck, I'll see you on Hot Spot. [Cherry takes the INWI Light Heavyweight Championship and puts it on the bench as he prepares to shower and take off after his match. The screen fades to an INWI logo...] Scoop - See, he seems to know what he wants to do. He wants to whip some ass this week and walk away with his title still intact. Newark - But he could lose that strap in the first match. It seems to me, he's looking right past Bill Bob Woijicowski. Speaking of Bill Bob, let's hear from him now. ***The camera fades into a scene of "Little House on the Praire" where Laura Ingalls is seen in great danger...she has fallen ill and it is not known wether she will survive the illness...suddenly she has recovered....The camera then fades back away from the television to a scene of a man petting his dog with tears in his eyes....It is Bill Bob Woijicowski and Jake...*** BBW: That really chaps my ass. TL: You like this ****?? BBW: No. TL: Then way are you watching this and crying? BBW: Well this was on and I was too drunk to change the channel. TL: But why are you crying? BBW: Cause the bitch didn't die...I had my hopes up and everything! ***BBW takes a swig from his can of Genny-Red eye...a six pack still lies at his feet.*** BBW:Jake...Beer! ***With this BBW throws the empty into a small bile of about 65 empties across the room and Jake picks up a full beer from his feet giving the beer to BBW...BBW rewards him with a bone.*** BBW: Tiffany I have a comment.... TL: What is.. BBW: I am not done speakin' woman! Now Pillow... you looked past Cherry and took a loss for that ther' belt a' yers and now it's Cherry's...I wont make that same mistake and Cherry....Well guess what...BBW is a comin' fer yer sorry ass...don't make the same mistake as that "pillowbiter" and look past me cause I might just have Jake jump up an bite ya in yer ass. Don't forget that I get the first piece of you and the winner will have the belt and be facin' either McCarter or the Pillowcase himself less than an hour after we tangle for the belt...I will be that man. I will be all business in the ring...I am not looking past Cherry but at him, then and only after I have won will I worry about my first defense. Don't underestimate the power of "The Redneck's Champ" or the Brotherhood!! Now Miss Love you can speak. TL: Nevermind. BBW: Good. Pillow biter I'll see you on Next weeks Hot Spot...McCarter I will see you tonight. ***The camera fades out as Tiffany Love exits the house of BBW....or should I say shack...*** Scoop - Damn... did you see that? Bill Bob is emotional and sensitive. Who said the Brotherhood didn't have any feelings? Newark - Whatever you say Scoop. You fall for it all... no wonder you got out of wrestling...Let's hear from YOUR favorite wrestler, the Springer... Tiffany Love in the dressing room with The Springer TL: Well Springer it has been a bad time for the triplets. You loose the Heavyweight belt and the Comfortors lose the tag belts (Springer cuts in) Springer: Just hold your tongue there missy. I know that that mouth Ropes is putting these words in your pretty little mouth. However I don't have to take any of the crap from you or that big mouth has been washed uped loser you hang with. TL: Well I have to ask these....... Springer: I'll do the talking here. First of all, I didn't lose the belt. The new so called champ didn't pin me, in fact I had him in the cutter ready to give, he just happen to hold on the belt. TL: Correct, however you still........ Springer: I know I lost the belt. I can live with that, what I can't live with is that big mouth has been taken pop shots at me. Scoop you want a piece of me? Ask anyone, I'm not that hard to find. You want to be the man you got to beat the man. And mouth, I'm the man. TL: You do realize that Scoop doesn't wrestle anymore? Springer: I think I know that sweetie. Put up or shut up Scoopy. This interview is over! Scoop - Oooooooh.... what a tough guy. He's lucky I still don't wrestle or I'd whip his ass... Newark - So, come out of retirement for one match... Scoop - Uh... um.... I told you Newark, I'm retired! I don't wrestle anymore and that Springer is lucky! Newark - Whatever Scoop...let's hear from his stablemates the Comforters. (After the match Tiffany is in the dressing again with the Comforters and Dr. Bedpan) Boxspring: Solid Gold we beat you and we can do it again. We may have lost our belts but we have not lost our will to win, tell them Mattress. Mattress: We are still one of the best tag teams in this fed and we will stay on top. Tell them what is in line Doctor! DrB: We had a bad night at the pay per view. We lost all our gold. All that did was make us more hungry to keep going and win back all the gold we lost. No need to say anything Tiff, We have said all that needs to be said. Scoop - Oh yeah, those guys are real solid as well. The whole stable... with their leader the Springer are losers. Newark - Better watch you mouth Scoop... never know who can be around. Let's hear from the All Americans, who will take on the Comforters this week. (The camera opens up on the All Americans in the INWI gym in New York. They are doing their normal routine in the gym. They pause to talk to the camera.) Gahan - Hello fans of the INWI! It all comes to a halt this Monday Night. Well face one of the best tag teams in this federation, the former INWI tag team champions...The Comforters. Shaffer - In our minds this match will determine the number one contendership for the INWI Tag team titles. Well as everybody knows we want those titles so Comforters we are going to give it 110%. Gahan - Comforters we look forward to this great match. We'll see you in the ring. (The camera fades out as the All Americans go back to the gym.) Scoop - Those guys are just as big of patsy's as the Comforters. That ought to be a ''cute'' match. Newark - Actually, that will be a great match between two excellent tag teams. Let's go to comments from Jason "Crusher" Chandler, who is in week four of his "Gauntlet." [scene opens on a large room filled with stuffed buffaloes and indian paintings on the wall..a man walks in the room wearing a loincloth only...] Chandler: See, Cloud Eagle? I can be an Indian too! [dances around chanting and singing..] Chandler: Damnit I better quit, I just washed my Prowler...I guess I gotta face Mr. Injun himself in this match I have in the stupid little gauntlet they made for me...Hell if Cloud Eagle is like every other injun I know, he'll be too busy drinking and gambling to concentrate on this match..well it won't matter cuz I am gonna kick your @ss anyways! [picks up hatchet and throws it at a picture of Jerry Deluise on the wall..] Chandler: Bull's eye! Deluise you keep throwing all these joke opponents that are all image and no substance...unlike me, the real deal! I am all substance! I tell you what Deluise...why don't you be the 10th man in this gauntlet...then I would have something to look forward to...probably too chicken! Well I guess I'll go rest up for my big match... [pours a double shot of vodka..] Chandler: Ah screw it..I don't need rest...Cloud Eagle's going down like all the rest...all I have to say is...NEXT? [scene fades away..] Scoop - I'm telling ya Newark, he's gonna do it and then win a title and then the INWI is going to get scared. Damn. Newark - Well, he has the skills... no doubt about that. But anyways fans, we are out of time on Inide the Ropes. So, we'll see you tomorrow night for Monday Night Hot Spot!! =========================================================== =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The INWI™ "The revolutionary force in E-Wrestling" Visit us on the Internet: http://www.catskill.net/harmer For info, E-mail: [detail withheld] INWI®™, October 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
