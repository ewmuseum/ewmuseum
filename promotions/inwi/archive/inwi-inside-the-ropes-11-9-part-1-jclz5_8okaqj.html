[INWI] Inside The Ropes, 11-9, Part 1

11 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Inside The Ropes November 9, 1997 [The screen slowly begins to fade into the several letters appears on the screen....I....N....W....I.. The letters are quickly accompanied by a crowd like chant.."INWI" "INWI" "INWI" The screen then opens to the tri-hosts, seated behind their now infamous desk. Brian Newark, Scoop Ropes, and the new member of this announce team..Riley Michaels, who like last week is wearing a black and orange Tiger mask.] Brian Newark: Welcome again, ladies and gentlemen, yet again, to INWI presents...Inside The Ropes with me this week, as always, the man is deathly afraid of the former Heavyweight Champion The Springer..here is Scoop Ropes.. Ropes: Well first Newark, it's the EX heavyweight champion, sounds much more permanent, and in his case, it was a fluke, and he will never again, have a chance to taint the most cherished prize in the INWI. And second, why do I have to be scared of a fluke? That's all he is pure and simple, a fluke. And third, this nonsense of me being FORCED out of retirement to face this chump in a cage, we'll Springer you asked for it..you got it..And I'd like to take tis time to introduce our newest member of the INWI broadcast team...Riley Michaels... Michaels: Thank you Scoop, and to say the least I'd have to agree with your statements, how is the INWI going to force you to come out of a retirement? An established, yet RETIRED superstar..it's a perfect example of the politics practiced by DeLuise. Scoop: Oh no...I've realized it...Newark I know who Riley Michaels is.. Newark: Who Scoop? Tell us.. Scoop: He's the guy sitting right next to me wearing the tiger mask. Next up we have a newcomer who goes by the name of Brass Knuckles? Riley: Isn't that what Newark's mother in law's name is? ============================================================= [ Camera shows a street in Philidelphia. On one side of the street are homeless people putting there hands over a trash can with fire in it. On the other side of the street are 2 gangs ready to start a fight. Then the camera focuses on Brass Knuckles. He is wearing a long coat and leaning up against a building. He looks at the camera as it gets closer to him. He then looks straight ahead. Brass Knuckles: I know what I am getting into. INWI, that's a BIG step. You know I have seen and heard a lot about INWI through NEW. And being in both major feds will be hard work but that's the way it goes. I made a name for myself in NEW and still working up there but it is also time to go for the gold right here in INWI. I want to see what this BIG and GREAT league has to offer. That is why I came here. For the challenge. Give me everyone you got. Mr. Cherry, Pig Pen, Tony Houston, Mr. Apples, Mr. Banana, Pimp Daddy Brown, the jWo. You all have your days numbered. I ain't gonna crap around. I'm going straight ahead. And no is gonna stop me. So I am making an open challenge to anyone he wants to try me out. Anyone who has guts to step in the ring with me. Anyone. I'm gonna instigate a war in INWI. And in that war I will be the victor. So don't underestimate me because if you do you won't like the results. Cuase when I snap that Brass Cutter on you it's lights out and you won't even know what hit you. But it will hurt. [ Brass Knuckles walks away from the camera. He walks between the gangs who were gonna brawl and the gangs scatter in fear, Camera fades out. ] ============================================================= Michaels: [looking into the camera and then starts laughing] What is it with these biznitch from this "supposed" World league, have to keep kicking that leagues name around..it's quite sickening and to be honest sickening.. Newark: I would have to agree with you on that point Riley Michaels. Next up we ha- Scoop: [cutting off Newark] Damnit Newark, what did this guy say, his finisher is the Brass Cutter? Well, [in a Church lady voice] Isn't that special... Michaels: However, next on tap we have a Bud draft..No.. We have comments from last week from Playmaker and Asswhupper and their stank footed Mama yet again.. ============================================================= ( In the locker room of the Asswhupper after his match) Mama: First of all to those two lil boys who host Inside the ropes. Lets clear something up right now. I am NOT! Asswhupper's nor Playmaker's mother. I am 21 years old. Asswhupper is 24 you shit *****! And whoever called my stank footed if you make one more comment about me Im gonna beat you like YOUR momma should have!!! Playmaker: Calm down girlie calm down. Damn the woman of the outift is pissed just imagine whats gonna happen when the Asswhupper gets pissed. Its not gonna be a pretty sight I can gaurentee that. Right now we are issuing an open contract to anyone who has the balls to accept. We wanta match next week on Hotspot. It doest matter who! Asswhupper: All that matters is that someone gets thier ass whupped! ============================================================= Michaels: [laughing] What?!? What did that house cleaner say to us, Scoops? Scoop: [also laughing] .... Michaels: You believe this? What is the world coming to when Stank assed, foul mouths biznitches through childhood insults at people...Dumb Stank footed skeezer. Newark: Please Mr. Michaels, and Scoops you are no better, if you don't watch yourselves you may be payed a visit from that threesome. Michaels: Well, I have no problems with threesomes, but the two "dicks" can keep themselves home. Come on Mama, I love it when you call me big Poppa. Throw your legs in the air and... Newark: Ok Mr. Michaels that's quite enough. Let's hear from a man, who could possibly be the next Northeast Champion, and make the Apples vs. Rogers match a titless Falls Count Anywhere match..Tommy Graves.. ============================================================= (The lockerroom is quiet, the lights are off, Tommy Graves is sitting on one of the bench's, towel over his head, sweat dripping from forehead) Tommy Graves: Can you hear the silence, eating away at the walls. Can you hear the legends, as they fall. The time has come and shall pass. Its time to fetch this demon cast.......... (yelling) ED ROGERS, SON, YOUR ON THE WRONG ROAD, THE WRONG STREET, RIGHT STRAIGHT TO HELLSVILLE. (getting back into controll) I've worked to hard, to long, to get where i'm at, just for you to take it away from me. My body will not fail me, my soul, will carry me to the top. Jonathan Hammer is my First in command of this war, and wont let me down either. .......can you hear the rain drops falling or the broken, calling. screaming your name with no one to blame...... (Yelling again) DONT BLAME ME FOR THE PAIN. DONT BLAME ME FOR THE HURT. I GAVE FAIR WARINING, ED ROGERS, YOU WONT BE THE FIRST TO FEEL THE WRATH, AND YOU WONT BE THE LAST. (under control again) Monday night, i take the Northeast Title. You have been warned. ......enter the darkness at your will its only your soul, that i must kill. ============================================================= Newark: Interesting comments from Tommy Graves who can be the next Northeast Champion. Michaels: Newark is this INWI WRESTLING or Poetry class? Everybody here knowing that he was simply reading the cue cards that boy may be a good fighter but he is not a mental giant...Who's he trying to fool, sounding like Benny Hinn. Scoop: Benny Hinn? Hahaha Isn't that Springer's dad? And come the time when the power_that_be force me out of retirement against my will and put me in a cage with a no talent jabrony Springer and come Final Countdown in the Molson Centre he'll find out just how great I am. Newark: Well, I highly doubt that Scoop..but next up we have some comments from a man who will challenge "The Ringmaster" Tony Houston for the INWI Heavyweight Championship, on a Scaffold....the "Master of Pain" Michael Bane. ============================================================= (camera opens on Mike Bane in the dark ecesses of the back.....) [MB] "The Master of Pain...has been quiet recently. But I have let my work in the ring speak for me. You all now see how the Master of Pain...is indeed...one of the best wrestlers in the world today. King Apples. You are what? A king among complete asses? That is something to be proud about. King Apples...you don't know the meaning of pride...you don't know the meaning of fear, of pain....Apples...I will soon teach all of this to you. And to the rest of your friends. The Final Countdown is alomst upon us....and I have been told I am the challenger at the INWI World's title. Not only that...but it is in a match that I so adore....a scaffold match. Houston, I show no mercy...and you will find that out soon enough." (camera fades out) ============================================================= Michaels: Michael Bane maybe a master of pain, and a master of submission holds, however, he is stepping out of himself, when he insults King Apples, or any other member of The Brotherhood, and if you don't believe me, ask anybody in the INWI or eWrestling world that has felt the power of The Brotherhood...two more words..Spike Piledriver. Newark: This is true that The Brotherhood is a powerful group, however, Tony Houston and his heavyweight strap could be in for a severely Extreme match when he and Bane take to the scaffold. Scoop: And speaking of extreme, that perfectly defines what the Springer IS NOT!!! But what define Springer is a lackey. Let's get some comments from a new comer to the INWI who will undoubtedly add some fires and flash to the Light heavyweight Division...El Nervioso. ============================================================= [The scene slowly fades from yet another meaningless, Susan Powter commercial and then reveals several Latin American wrestler engaging in a truly remarkable Lucha Libre six man affair.] [As per the usual when these "luchadores", participate in wrestling matches, it's almost a lock to be the best or one of the best on ANY wrestling card...And despite some other, southern based promotion thinking their fans are stupit, those at the INWI do not believe that to be the case...In that southern based promotion they would have you believe that the word "luchadore" is a special style of wrestling...well it's not...It's only Espanol for Wrestler...] [As this video clip comes to and end, a ,man begins to walk closer and closer and closer towards the INWI television camera..The man is wearing a black with silver lightening bolts mask and is dressed in a black with similar silver lightening bolts tights...] Masked Man: Hola..que pasa? me IIamo es El Nervioso, y yo es en la INWI...[the man then attempts to speak some English, obviously it's a struggle] Uhh..how you Americans say...I am on yo'..my way to the INWI to push for uh a...light heavyweight division and to uhh compete for the INWI Light heavyweight championship, which that punta James Pillow currently disgraces...all I am talkin about is bringing a Lucha Libre style to the INWI in which THAT title should signify..For those of you's who don't know much about the Lucha Libre style of wrestling..it's an all out, NO REST HOLDS and each y every match is filled with excitement..my name is El Nervioso...The Shocker...and prepared to feel The Shock.... [Scene fades as El Nervioso returns to the ring as he prepares for his debut in the exciting world of Light Heavyweight action.....World Wide Style...] ============================================================= Newark: This guy appears to be, to say the least, a mysterious man of sorts. According to our scouting reports he is and was one of the top wrestlers ever to come out of Mexico. Michaels: Yeah well, his english seems to be a little broken...ya' know what I say about that....it appears the Harder you hit El Nervioso [with a mocking spanish accent] the more english you get out of him. Scoop: HAHHAHAHAHA I sure am glad that we brought Riley Michaels here, he certainly makes my job that much more enjoyable, shoot, after months of working with boring Newark, thank you...But I"m not thanking that bald headed fool, DeLuise from forcing me out of retirment, and he could be hearing from my lawyers. Next we have the umm...tag champs.. Newark: In their minds maybe.... ============================================================= <SHOCK HORROR! Not a hotel room, but backstage at the INWI. Ultimate is wearing one of the tag team belts they "borrowed" on Hot-Spot. They both wear their "54 Seconds to Glory" t-shirts, avaiable at any INWI merchandise stand.> Tiffany: Ultimate, Cloud, I guess you think these are rightfully yours? Ultimate: <Grinning like a naughty 12 year old> Yep. We decided that they really didn't belong to the owners, so we decided to give them a new home. <Grins again.> Tifffany: I only see one, where's the other one? Ultimate: <Ultimate and Cloud look at each other and giggle like schoolgirls> It`s in a safe place. Tiffany: Why did you do it? Ultimate: My Television title was lonely. <Grins yet again.> Tiffany: Turning to you now Cloud, you seem to take second place to Ultimate these days, does that bother you? Cloud: Not at all. We are great friends, and I`m glad to help. Now we are taking on the tag team championship together. I am very happy. Tiffany: In regards to the tag team you two have formed, how come there's no catchy name? I mean you have "Solid Gold", you have "The Comforters", why not choose a name for yourself? Ultimate: What? And be lumped into the same category as those idiots? Tiffany my dear, why would we want to associate with those lemmings? We are both individuals. Divided we are powerful, together we are unstoppable. We do not need a cover all name, but if the INWI insists on one, why not this. <Points at the text "54 Seconds to Glory"> Tiffany: Ultimate, Cloud, you're both insane, thank you. ============================================================= Michaels: Nice shirt, but Newark I believe that you can sue them for copyright violations... Newark: What do you mean Mr. Michaels...?? Michaels: Well maybe not you but your wife anyway, 'cause she was telling me that you coined that phrase on your honeymoon night.... Scoop: HAHAHAHHAAHA Damn, not only does he add Color but he speaks the truth. And speaking of truth, I believe that "54 seconds to glory" is the story of Springer's career. But we now have to take this quick break. =========================================================== =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The INWI™ "The revolutionary force in E-Wrestling" Visit us on the Internet: http://www.catskill.net/harmer For info, E-mail: [detail withheld] INWI®™, November 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
