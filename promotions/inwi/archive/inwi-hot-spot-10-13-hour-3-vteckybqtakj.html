[INWI] Hot Spot, 10-13, Hour 3

[Hour Number three of INWI Hot Spot opens with Firewors and flashing lights all over. The crowd chants INWI...INWI....INWI....INWI. The camera shifts to Brian Newark, who is at ringside.] =========================================================== Newark - Welcome fans to Hour Number three of Hot Spot! We are going to go LIVE to Commissioner Jerry DeLuise, who has an announcement on the situation with the Light Heavyweight Championship.... =========================================================== DeLuise - In watching the Light Heavyweight Title change hands earlier this evening, you could clearly see that Mr. Cherry held the tights of James Pillow. However, a referee's decision can not be over ruled. Therefore, Mr. Cherry is the Champion. But, next week on Hot Spot, Pillow will get a shot to regain his title. Not forgetting the fact that McCarter and Woijicowski are suppossed to be involved, so next week... on HOT SPOT... we will have a FOUR WAY DANCE for the Light Heavyweight Championship!!! =========================================================== Newark - WHAT news! Wow, that was unexpected!! Let's head back to the ring! =========================================================== Match 9 - Non Title Match. Texas Tornado Match The Comforters (with Dr. Bedpan) vs Solid Gold (with Jimmy Stardom) This match is a return match from Invasion '97 in which Solid Gold captured and unified the Tag Team championship following a 450 Splash. This week it's a Texas Tornado style match and a non title match...So anything can happen. Blake and Box start inside the ring and Mattress and Bradley are on the outside brawling..Blake nails a standing thrust kick that sends Box staggering into the corner...Blake charges and nails a stingerlike splash in the corner...Mattress on the outside has Bradley on his shoulder and drops him jawfirst on the ring apron...Blake off the ropes and nails a Piscada onto Mattress on the floor..Great high risk move by Gold brother Blake. Blake is now punching away at the head of Mattress. Blake pulls up Mattress and rolls him back into the ring and quickly scales to the top turnbuckle and leaps..Flying fistdrop..cover..one..two..No!! Box Spring bounced off the ropes and dropped a thunderous legdrop across the back of Blake's head breaking up the count. Box pulls up Blake and fires him into the ropes..Spinebust-No!! Spinebuster into a Tiger bomb by Box Spring great move!!! One..two..No!!! Bradley back into the ring and breaks up the count with a dropkick to the back of Box's head. Mattress grabs Bradley and now they're brawling...Mattress reverses an irish whip and nails a powerslam..No!! Bradley held onto Matress's leg..One..two...th--No!!! Mattress rolls his shoulder. Bradley back up and nails a Spinning Heel Kick.but is mauled over by Box Spring from the blindside....Box hooks the leg...One..two..NO!!! Bradley rolled his shoulder. Box Spring up and Mattress joins him..They fire Bradley into the ropes and send Bradley flying high into the air with an impressive double back drop. Box Spring quickly bounces off the ropes and drops a Splash onto Bradley..One..Two..T--No!! Blake comes over and breaks up the count. Blake pulls up Box and scoops him...Side Walk Slam..Blake quickly off the ropes and sends Mattress sprawling to the corner with a Springboard Dropkick. Bradley back up and now Solid Gold is double teaming Box Spring...Blake scoops Box in a side walk slam position while Bradley bounces off the ropes..Side Winder double team SideWalk Slam/guillotine legdrop combo..Blake hooks the leg..One..two..No!!! Again Box springed out of the cover..[catch it?] Blake drags Box Spring towards the corner...he's going for the 450 Splash...He leaps..NO!! Mattress back in and nailed a Forward Jawbreaker and Blake is hurtin'..!!! Mattress kicks Bradley in the stomach...<BOOM> POWERBOMB on Bradley....Both of the tag team champions are down and in trouble...Mattress pulls up Bradley...Box is on the top rope...Mattress elevated Bradley...SEALY POSTURPEDIC!!!!! Box hooks both legs...One..two...three....It's over I don't believe!!! Let's get the official word, as The Comforters extract some form of revenge against the Tag team champions, Solid Gold.. Here are your winners in 15:23 via pinfall, The Comforters... =========================================================== Newark - Well, the Comforters get revenge, but not the belts. These two will meet again, that is for sure. Let's go back to the ring... =========================================================== Match 10 Television Championship Ultimate Perversion vs Jonathan Hammer Perversion is in the ring now and the two lock up. This one is for the TV title and look if you will at Jonathan Hammer. We all saw that brutal cage match he had at the PPV with Bane and Bledsoe and he is all taped up and scarred, he is a mess. But he is back here tonight on the Hot Spot fighting for a title. Hammer with a boot to the gut and a big forearm to the back. Perversion drops to one knee and delivers a low blow. He stands up and gives a dropkick and Hammer goes down. Irish whip on Hammer and he grabs Perversion by the throat. UP breaks the arms and dropkicks the knee. Repeated stomps to the head of Hammer and UP is in control. Irish whip by Perversion now, Hammer ducks underneath and hits a clothesline. Hammer sets up Perversion on the top rope and climbs up with him. UP shoves him off and Hammer hits his head hard on the canvas. UP is up and hits a shoulderblock and a cover, 1......2.....and no. Hammer just kicked out. Irish whip and another dropkick by the Television champion. Hammer rolls outside the ring and grabs a chair and throws it back into the ring. Up goes for the chair but Hammer throws it up and it smacks UP right in the face and he goes down. He is on his knees and Hammer is over himwith the chair. He swings but UP ctahes the chair. They are fighting back and forth for the chair and finally Hammer lets go of the chair and UP flies down to the canvas. Hammer with repeated boots to the head and UP out of desperation swings the chair and hits Hammer square i nthe knee. He screams out in pain and he goes down. UP is busted open from those boots and the xhair shot. Hammer isclutching his knee and UP is going up top. He clips Hammer's knee from the top rope. Unbelivable. Hammer is really in pain now, you can see it on his face. UP runs into the ropes and delivers a bulldog and Hammers head just bounced off the canvas. UP is in complete control and is going to put away this match right now. Wait, the crowd is standing up and two men arte coming down the aisle. It's Slater and Thunder. They climb into the ring and are pounding away on UP. Hammer is just standing there and laughing as Thunder and Slater are just tearing apart UP. Wait, the crowd is standing again. It's Cloud Eagle. He is rushing down the aisle as well. He nails Thunder and here comes all 4 men in the middle of the ring. They are in a huge brawl and here comes security and other wrestlers from the back. This feud has boiled over the edge and this one is going to be settled real soon, you can count on that. Again your winner by DQ and still INWI TV Champion in 13:35, The Ultimate Perversion. =========================================================== Newark - Perversion keeps his title, but this hatred with Slater and Thunder is getting worse. Let's go back into the ring for our first half of our double main event. ============================================================ Match 11 - Title vs Title Mr. Ed Rogers (with Smelly) vs Sam "The Minuteman" Adams This match to say the least should be interesting..Not only is this a title for title match, but these two men are "special" to say the least. Both men have their respective problems, however, are excellent competitors in the ring. Ed makes his way to the ring second and to say the least looks horrible as he is being seconded to the ring by the world famous wrestling genious *AHEM* Smelly. Ed storms into the ring but is met with a running knee lift by Adams...Adams taken quick advantage of this match, Adams sends Rogers into the ropes and nails a Thez press..great move and now he's punching away at the face of Rogers..Rogers rolls over and is now on top of Adams...and now he's punching away at the face of the United States champion...Adams reverses it and is now on top..Seems like a night with Marv Albert..rolling biting, and punching... Rogers to the floor..Adams up and bounces off the ropes...He missed a baseball slide dropkick as Smelly pushed Rogers out of the way..Smelly? What did I just say? Anyways Rogers drops Adams with a hooking clothesline and then drops a headbutt on Adams on the floor. Rogers rolls Adams back into the ropes...What the hell is that man doing..? Rogers just put his hand to his ear and then climbs to the top rope..He leaps..Frog Spla--No!! Adams rolled out of the way and Rogers splashed the mat. Adams slowly making his way back towards his feet and bounces off the ropes...Rolling cradle..One..two..thr--NO!!! Rogers just barely rolled his shoulder. Adams quickly stays on the attack and pulls Rogers to his feet...Fisherman's Suplex...One..two..th--NO!! again Rogers fights free of another pin attempt. Adams is now arguing with the ref? The ref is a commie? Um...well ol' Adams sure is special...Adams turn his attention back to Ed and is dropped with a DDT, good move by Rogers, bad mistake by Adams...Rogers is still slow to get up..however he's stirring and pulls up Adams. Belly to Belly Suplex..executed to perfection....Roges up and bounces off the ropes and drops an armpit? to the face of Adams...One..two..th--No!!! Now's it's Adams who must fight to keep his title. Rogers up and brings Adams to the corner and crotches him on the top turnbuckle..he's going up after 'em...Superplex with both men on the top rope..Rogers rolls his arm across the chest of Adams..one..two..thr--NO!!!! Adams again rolls his shoulder...Rogers is actually getting a lil' frustrated..He pulls up Adams again and fires him into the ropes..Adams ducks under a clothesline and drops Rogers with a Springboard Hurricarana and is now punching at the head of the Northeast Champion. Adams quickly up and bounces off the ropes...Spingboard Moonsault..Adams hooks the leg...one..two..thre--NO!!!! Rogers just barely rolled his shoulder unbelievable match between these two great fighting champions. You don't get this action anywhere else except in the INWI. Adams back to his feet and on the attack...he pulls up Rogers and sends him into the ropes with a Forearm Uppercut..Adams whips Rogers into the ropes..drops his head..Swinging DDT..Rogers just spiked Adams head hard into the mat..Rogers hooks the leg..One..two..th--NO!!! Adams again proving why he is the INWI U.S. champion, and not only because of his paranoid patriotism. Rogers pulls up Adams and he's setting up for the Puppet Master....<BOOM> He hit it..Rogers hit the Puppet Master!!!! Rogers is going to leave the ring with both the New York and the Northeast Championship..WHOA!! what the hell is that man doing? Rogers is in the corner and he looks scared....Rogers rolls under the bottom rope and streakin' down the aisle way looking over his shoulder and over his head....6..7...8...9...10!!!!!!! And he just lost the match...and look in the ring, there's that damn sock he calls Smelly....and Adams is still motionless in the center of the ring...Damn this Rogers has some deep seeded problems....he had the match won...well I guess it's over let's get the offcial word.. Winner: At 18:49, via countout...Sam "The Minuteman" Adams..And Adams retains his New York championship as does Rogers because no INWI title can change hands on a countout... =========================================================== Newark - Ed NEVER ceases to amaze me. Let's get up to the ring for our final match of the night as Tony Houston defends his newly won INWI Heavyweight Title. =========================================================== Match 12 - INWI Heavyweight Championship "Ringmaster" Tony Houston (with Mr. Flamboyant) vs Mr. Banana Houston took his time down the aisleway, which is understandable following the spectacular Ladder match at the PPV in which he defeated The Springer for the INWI championship. Houston has a bandage covering his 15 stitched cut above his left eye, again damage done to him at the pay per view, where he rammed his head into the ladder. Houston enters the ring and gives the belt to the ref, and Banana immediately attack with a boot to the midsection. Side walk slam follow up by Banana. Banana with a cover..One.two..No!!! Houston rolled his shoulder..Banana back up and fires Housto into the ropes..Houston ducks under a clothesline attempt and drops Banana with a Spinning Leg Lariat. Houston stomps Banana in the head and pulls him to his feet...Swinging Neckbreaker and Banana's head snapped off the mat...cover..One ..two..No!! Banana rolled his shoulder...Houston quickly back to his feet and stomps the throat of Banana. Houston pulls up Banana, and works on the neck again with a Side Russian legsweep neckbreaker. Banana stomach down on the mat, Houston grabs his chin..OUCH!!! Houston just fell forward and cracked Banana's neck with a Reverse rolling neck snap..And again..Houston is vicious and is working on the neck of the bigger Banana. Houston with a cover..One..two..thre--No!!! Banana rolled his shoulder..Houston back up and pulls Banana to his feet, Banana counters a right hand with a belly to bell suplex and covers..One..two..th-No!!! Houston rolled his shoulder. Banana staggers to his feet..and drops a falling headbutt right on the bandage above Houston's left eye, and the Champion is in trouble..Banana pulls up Houston...He's setting up for a Powerbomb!!!! <BOOM> Tiger bomb by Banana...Covers..One ..two...thre--No!!! Houston again just barely rolled his shoulder...Banana is now arguing with the ref..and giving Houston a chance to catch his breath..Houston up quickly..Boot the stomach of Banana..Cold Dro--NO!!!! Banana pushed Houston off the move, what strength...Banana with a boot to the back of Houston's head..Unbelievable..that's the first time we've seen anybody counter Houston's Cold Drop...And I believe now that Houston and his belt are in serious trouble...Banana pulls up Houston and fires him into the ropes...Stun Gun on Houston...Banana doesn't go for a cover, but this hatred is deeper than appears on the surface..Houston AND Apples are leaders of The Brotherhood, and Banana HATES Apples...so in a sense HATES Houston, so this match has more heat that ya' think...Banana pulls up Houston and sends him hard into the corner..Banana charges....Houston ducks and Banana is backdropped over the top rope and to the floor..HE LANDED ON HIS FEET!!! I don't believe...Banana rolls back into the ring and drops the champion with a running clothesline..Banana...signals for the Banana split...No Houston counter with an elbow to the back of Banana's head...Double Arm DDT by Houston....Cover..One..two..th--NO!!! Banana drapes his leg over the bottom rope..Houston back up and nails a boot to the back of Banana, and pulls him up...Housto with a knee lift and bounces off the ropes..Single Arm DDT...Houston is really starting to work over the neck of Banana...Houston covers...One..two..thr--No!!! Unbelievable Banana is the man now showing toughness..Great match thus far...Houston pulls up Banana..arm wringer..into a <SMACK> Standing Thrust backick...Houston pulls up Banana and is going to do it again...Banana catches Houston legs....German Suplex..No bridge all impact!!! Houston just landed hard on his face..and his cut has open and the champion is bleeding again!!! Banana like an animal smells the blood and goes on the attack..Banana pulls up Houston and sends him into the ropes..Big Boot nearly takes Houston's head off...Banana off the ropes and drops an elbow...Banana pulls up Houston...He's going for the Banana Split...No Houston slips off the back...Houston scoops Banana in a Fireman's Carry...<CRUNCH> Houston just dropped Banana head first into the mat with a Fireman's Piledriver....He's called it the Too Cold Driver...or as he likes to call it the T.C.D. Houston covers...One..Two ...three...!!!!! It's over...Unbelievable match and Houston retains the championship of the INWI Heavyweight ranks... Winner: At 14:43 via pinfall, "Ringmaster" Tony Houston retains the INWI Heavyweight Championship. =========================================================== Newark - And we are out of ti--- WAIT! We have Tiffany Love who caught up with Sam Adams as he is leaving the building... let's hear this as we leave.
