[INWI] Monday Hot Spot, 12-8, Hour 3

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Mr. Flamboyant grabs the mic at ringside and flips it to King Apples, who is standing in the ring with Vinny Vitrone, James Pillow and Tony Houston after attacking their former Brother and adding James Pillow. Bill Bob Woijicowski is out in the ring and the crowd is booing and are stunned at this whole situation...] King Apples - You see, when you think you have our fast ball figured out, we throw a curve ball. All of you smart marks think you have the Brotherhood figured out. Well, none of you had a clue. NONE OF YOU. And man, this is great. James Pillow. Me and James, we go back a long way. We've beaten each other and hell, he's had a go or two with Houston as well. But look at this, he's wearing the colors man. He's a Brother. No more of that Fellas crap here baby. This is our house and Pillow is now a part of it. You see, the Brotherhood is full of elite members. And now we just got better. We got rid of our dead weight and brought in a real man. Now, I know certain people are watching and shaking their heads. They are saying, why, James, why? You want the answer? I'll let him answer it for you!!! HA HA!!! James "Simply The Best" Pillow:Why,huh? WHY YOU ASK?!?!? Well isn't the answer obvious? Take a look at the men standing in this ring. We have the INWI Heavyweight Champion, "The Ringmaster" Tony Houston, we have the INWI Northeast Champion "King" Apples, and we have the next INWI Light Heavyweight Champion "Vindicator" Vinny Vitrone. How could I pass up the chance to join with the biggest, baddest and best group in all of the wrestling world? Well don't hurt yourselves trying to think of the answer, but it is quite simple. .I COULDN'T! Now I also suppose you are wondering why I'd turn my back on the FELLAS? Well I didn't, they turned their backs on me. I've been competing in the INWI holding the banner of the FELLAS and getting attacked constantly and did either Harris of Bloom come out and help me when I was being beat down by four other men? HELL NO THEY DIDN'T. I didn't turn my back on them, they did the ol' 180 turn around on me. But now where from here? Houston has the Heavyweight gold, Apples with the Northeast title, will I be taking back the Light Heavyweight Title? Hell no, I had it twice and quite frankly I don't really care about it anymore so I'm going to let ol' Vitrone have the opportunity to destroy that piece of trash Michael Bane. As for me. . .well there's only one thing left for me to take and that's the INWI New York Title. I don't care who has it, Adams . . .Robinson it doesn't matter but whoever s holding it after tonight is going to become a marked man by a member of the Brotherhood. And when you're marked by one member of the Brotherhood <points around the ring> You're marked by the ENTIRE Brotherhood. Now all you peasants out there bow down and kiss the feet of The most elite group in wrestling, THE BROTHERHOOD! [Houston walks up from behind, the newest Brother, James Pillow and pats him on the back and takes the mic. from him.] Houston: Praise the lord!!! He has seen the light. It has taken some time, but the true colors of a certain fella group has finally emerged...but more on that later in the show. And as fer ol' Billy Bob...well, he informed us of his plans on moving up...well, first of all, that sorry piece of trash's head got a lil' too big..once you are part of The Brotherhood.. there is no moving up..now we are not being conceited...but like always..I speak the truth..The Brotherhood is atop the eWrestling mountain. However, ol' Billy bob felt the need to leave...fine...however, with all this talk about time honored traditions goin' around, well we wouldn't be us, if we let that slip by..so Woijicowski.....don't feel bad.. anybody who attempts to leave The Brotherhood will feel the time honored tradition..HA HA!! Spike...Time Honored Tradition..same damn thing...you and yer Rottpoodle Jake, get the hell out of our house..bitch. Let not the question to Brother Pillow be WHY? but instead an exclamation of FINALLY!!!! King Apples: You know, as I was saying this is a great day in the day of the Brotherhood. We have the Little brah behind us. We have our new Brother in James Pillow. And of course you got the Founders. The bottom line of the whole thing is the fact that we are the most elite group in all of Wrestling. James Pillow has seen the light. And Bill Bob has followed time honored tradition. It touches me in the heart. But, I promised myself I wasn't gonna cry. See, the greatest thing going in the world today is us. And we are not stopping. When you think you got us ALL figured out, just remember, I used to be a pitcher in baseball. And wooooooo, does my curve ball have a break on it. We the Brotherhood, are the greatest thing going today. So deal with it. [The Brotherhood all starts to laugh as Apples drops the mic and they all leave the ring. The camera shows a quick shot of Bill Bob Woijicowski on the mat, out. The camera then fades to the start of Hot Spot Hour number three...] [The letters I---N---W---I pop on the screen one by one. Then they explode as the logo of Monday Night Hot Spot comes onto the screen. The music "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne starts to crank and the arena goes black. The INWI-Tron pops on and the beginning of Hot Spot starts.... [It starts by showing a close up of INWI Champion Tony Houston with the INWI belt over one shoulder and the Ringmaster Belt over the other. He just smiles at the camera. Jason Chandler holding up his Television Championship and then the screen starts to show all sorts of pieces from the INWI..... James Pillow nailing a super kick on Rick Brown, Vinny Vitrone in his dog collar match with the "jWo" Icon Pig Pen, Ultimate Perversion and Cloud Eagle going at it with Harvey Thunder and Michael Slater, Bill Bob Woijicowski reading one of his ''stories'' to his nephew Bobby Bob...Cherry nailing a Cherry Bomb...Mr. Ed Rogers staring at Smelly...Tommy Graves nailing a choke slam...David McCarter on the mic...The Asswhupper and Thugs 4 Life standing with their arms crossed.... The Camera switches to Brian Newark at ringside.] [Newark is wearing an INWI rugby shirt, has his head set on, wears wire rimmed glasses and is ready to go. He flashes the camera a smile and we are ready to go...] HOUR NUMBER THREE!!! Rochester War Memorial, Rochester, NY LIVE!!! Brian Newark - Can you believe what just happened there? The Brotherhood just turned on one of their own. Bill Bob Woijicowski was jumped and spiked three times. But beyond that, James Pillow has joined the Brotherhood. That's right, you heard me correctly, James Pillow. What in the world is Pillow thinking? His former Fellas must be in shock if they are watching this. But look at the other side, Michael Bane has captured the Light Heavyweight Championship, his first INWI Title. And we are not even done yet. We still have Hour Number three. Pete Robinson will put his career up tonight and also, Michael Slater said if he loses, he will retire. Such a stip for any wrestler to say. Man oh man, what a night, let's get up to the ring. But, Perversion, who seems to have been involved in a personality change, is starting to turn into an extremely vicious man. Perversion into the ring and is simply staring across the ring at Michael Slater who is attempting mildly at getting the crowd excited however they have no reaction for him. Slater kicks out...Perversion starts to methodically drop elbow to the side of Slater's face. geez...Perversion has just snapped..he's going wild with elbows to the face of Slater..Perversion is on top of Slater and he's punching away at the open face of Slater....Perversion up and pulls up Slater and whips him into the ropes and nails a Thrust kick to the mouth and nose of Slater... Slater's bleeding from the nose and mouth...Perversion on to Slater quickly..he grabs Slater and yanks his head backwards..and delivers a nasty forearm across Slater's face..and again...and again.. Perversion has definitely changed!! He's a vicious vicious person in this match. Perversion pulls up Slater and whips him into the ropes... Perversion bounces off the ropes..and nearly takes Slater's head off with a diving Lariat... Perversion yanks the injured Slater to his feet and he's going for a Tiger Bomb.. BAM Perversion just nailed a Tiger Bomb on Slater..into a cover..One.. two..th-No!!! Slater rolled his shoulder. Perversion pulls up Slater and scoops Slater... Perversion is going for a Tombstone.. JUMPING TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER by Perversion ...Slater's neck just crumpled from that blow...Perversion covers...and hooks both legs, needlessly...One..two..three!!!! I don't believe it...The Ultimate Perversion just destroyed Michael Slater....This New Perversion could actually be a better persona, definitely more vicious. Let's get the official word..as Perversion appears to have calmed down now that the match is over. Here is your winner in 8:44....Ultimate Perversion!!! Whoa!!! Cherry is making his way towards the ring...He's just standing in the aisle and staring with an unimpressed and disgusted look. Perversion sees him and the two engage in a staring contest....Cherry then turns and returns to the lockeroom.. Perversion appears to be going off.... Perversion pulls Slater to his feet..he's going for another Tombstone....He leaps... JUMPING Tombstone again!! MAIN EVENT - TITLE vs CAREER Match 12 - New York Championship Sam "The Minuteman" Adams (with Militia) vs Pete Robinson Can you believe this match? I mean, from what I understand, this was all Pete Robinson's idea. He he fails to win the title here. Yeah, fails to WIN THE TITLE. Not the match, but the title, then he is out of the INWI. Sam Adams and his career are not on the line. Just the New York Championship. This has the makings of being a great match. We all know the first match between the two was a good one and that caused Sam Adams to become Commissioner for a week. And obviously, this turns out to be Robinson's last shot at the title. Both men are in the ring and the ref holds the INWI New York Championship up in the air for all to see. Militia leaves the ring and we are set to go. There's the bell and we are underway. Tie up in the center of the ring and Robinson with an arm drag take down. Robinson working on the arm and Adams slowly moving back to his feet and he nails Robinson in the midsection...and again...and one more time to break the hold. Adams off the ropes and he drops Robinson to the mat. Adams scoops up Robinson, sends him into the ropes and nails a drop kick!! Robinson is on the mat and Adams with a cover, 1.....2.....NO! That was close. Robinson's career almost ended right there. Adams grabs Robinson and sends him through the middle ropes and to the floor and then follows him out. Robinson to the floor and grabs Adams and sends him face first into the ring post. Robinson is really taking it to the Minuteman. Robinson grabs Adams and sends him into the ---NO!
