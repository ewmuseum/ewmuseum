[INWI] Monday Hot Spot, 11-3, Hour 1

4 November 1997

*David McCarter is making his way down to ringside, through the crowd. He steals the ring mic and climbs in* [McCarter] Right, now this has been enough of the INWI I can stand. I've been your greatest INWI Light Heavyweight champion, and what do I get in return. A bloody pat on the back and job well done. What does Sam Adams get?? He freakin' United States Title shot. Where's the blood fairness in that. *The crow obivously begins to get unpleasent at these words* [McCarter] Right, I pin James Pillow to go on to the finals, but nooooooo! That blind referee bloody well lies right to my freakin' face and says I pinned myself. Well, to hell with him! I beat James Pillow not once, but twice. And what the hell does the INWI prez give me. A match against Mr.Cherry!!! Of course I beat the snot out of that chap, but then the INWI committee has the bloomin' balls to come along and leave me off tonights card?? Well bugger off INWI. McCarter isn't going to take this crap anymore!!! Sam Adams was right, this place is corrupt with communism, and facsim and all they freakin lot of ya's are nothing but american trash that Britain and the rest of the world has to put up with. *The crowd now begins to throw garbage into the ring* [McCarter] Right, I'm a huge star now.....thanks to me. No one else but me. And you bloody yanks are trying to take credit for my success. Well INWI, this new McCarter is taking back his Light Heavyweight title....and when I'm done...I'm eyin that Heavyweight title as well.....CIAO!! [The scene then fades away and goes to the beginning of INWI Hot Spot. This is a series of shots of all of the INWI Superstars. It shows Tony Houston holding onto the INWI Heavyweight Championship as Springer gives him a Springer Cutter off of a ladder, it shows Mr. Ed Rogers and King Apples going at it, it shows James "Simply The Best" Pillow with the INWI Light Heavyweight title and Angel at his side, it shows Sam Adams draped in a U.S. Flag with Militia at his side and the New York State Title over his shoulder, it shows Solid Gold in a four way brawl with the Comforters in their infamous battle for the Unified Titles, it shows Bill Bob Woijicowski and Mr. Cherry in their wild battle for the Light Heavyweight Title and then it shows an action packed seconds of ALL INWI superstars and it ends with the screen exploding and you see INWI..... Hot Spot with the Hot Spot in flames...] INWI Presents Monday Hot Spot!! November 3, 1997 O'Reilly's Tavern, Portland, Maine Brian Newark - Welcome ladies and gentlemen to the BEST MONDAY NIGHT SHOW ON TV! We are out on MONDAY'S and we have the best action going! We have great action for you tonight and we are THREE SOLID HOURS! Three hours on Monday Nights... every Monday....On Monday's!! Scoop Ropes will join us for Scoop Raps this week again and I had the pleasure of sitting down with Brody Thunder in an interesting interview. In future weeks we will bring you some big sit down interviews with others as well! Stay tuned to the INWI for that. Enough of me jabbing my mouth, let's get up to the ring!!! Match 1 "Master of Pain" Michael Bane vs Awesome X Bane is in the ring now and look at the guy he is a nut. He is scarred all over the place and Awesome X looks at him like he has three heads. X charges and Bane with a drop toe hold and an ankle lock applied and he is going for the submission. He lets go of the hold and runs to the corner. He jumps to the second rope and comes off with a dropkick. X rolls to the outside and slams the mat in frustration. He grabs a chair and throws it into the ring and Bane smiles. X picks up the chair and Bane right on him with a dropkick to the head. He grabs the chair and he goes up on the ropes. He puts the chair under his legs and comes down on the face of X with an Arabian Face Buster. A cover by Bane, 1.......2......and a kickout by X. X with a slap in the face and he sends Bane into the ropes and puts him down with a spinebuster and he is pounding away on the forehead of Bane. Bane with a reversal and an armbar. He is pulling the arm back and X gets out of it and kips up and hits a beautiful side kick and Bane goes down and to the outside. Bane grabs another chair and he goes under the ring and he has a table. He folds the arms of the table in and he throws it into the ring along with the chair. X is there and he catapults himself over the top rope and onto Bane and he drops the chair and table he had in his hands. X throws Bane into the guard rail, picks him up and rams him into the post and them picks him up and chucks him into the steps and Bane is hurt. He gets up and hits a low blow and throws X back into the ring. Bane is up on the ropes now and he hits a centon onto X. He covers the man, 1........2.....and X kicks out. X with a poke to the eyes and he takes Bane over with a belly to back suplex. He is on the ropes and he misses and takes a header into the mat. Bane whips X into the corner and grabs a chair. He sets the chair up in the middle of the ring. Bane is in the opposite corner and he runs, jumps onto the chair, hits X and takes him over with a hurricanrana out of the corner, what a move. Bane is up now and he goes into the ropes, jumps up to the top, leaps of and he just hit the triple jump moonsault. He covers X, 1........2......and X kicks out. X with a knife-edge chop and Bane goes down. X sends him into the ropes and hits a northern lights suplex with a bridge, 1.......... 2........and Bane kicks out. Bane looked shoked by that move. He spins him over and has a pinning prediciment. 1.........2.......and a kickout by X. X sits in the ropes and Bane charges and X backbody drops Bane over the top rope and to the floor but Bane landed on his feet. He grabs the chair and the table and he jumps into the ring with them because X is almost out of it. He puts the table out in the ring and it's set up there. The chair is on the mat and Bane picks it up and sets it up in the middle of the ring like before. He sets up X on that table and he is out. Bane is in the corner and he runs, jumps up on the chair, leaps off and does a split-legged moonsault off the ropes and through the table with X. WHAT A MOVE!! The crowd is going insane and they are chanting Bane, Bane, Bane, Bane. Bane is hurt so he can't make a cover and the ref is starting the 10 count. He has reached 7 and Bane is up and he grabs X and X grabs his neck. Bane knocks down X on his stomach and he applies the Camel Clutch. Bane is the master of submissions and this is one of his specialties. Bane is wrenching it on and X just tapped out. Here is the winner of the match in 13:21, "Master of Pain" Michael Bane. Match 2 - Debut Match Malice vs Fuego Another quickly declining athlete, the wrestler formerly known as Fuego, and could be on his way out after this match with this new comerFuego goes for the leg and Malice picks him up and sends him into the ropes, scoops him up and plants him down with a sidewalk slam. Fuego is grabbing his head and Malice slaps him in the head, picks him up to his feet and clotheslines him in the back of the head and Fuego is very hurt. Malice yells to the crowd and grabs Fuego around the throat and throws him into the corner. He is pounding away on Fuego in the corner and Fuego seems to be busted open in the back of the head. He is bleeding and Malice loves it. He picks up Fuego and he press slams him over his head and he throws him into the 3rd row. Fuego looks hurt but he comes back over the railing and he is on the apron and Malice grabs him and suplexes him over the top rope and back into the ring. He grbas Fuego around the neck again and he picks him up and drops his throat over the top rope. Fuego bounces back off and he is on the mat. Malice is surrounding Fuego and he is holding back Malice. He is calling for the bell here and I don't really know what's going on here. The announcement is that Fuego is unable to continue but I don't know, he could be paralyzed on the mat here. Here comes Damage Control out to get Fuego. They are loading hi monto the stretcher here and Malice is just standing there laughing. What a sicko. The winner by forefit in the time of 4:10, Malice. Match 3 Tommy Graves vs Mr. Cherry This should be an exciting match for the newcomer to the INWI facing a former lightheavyweight champion, and INWI veteran, Mr. Cherry. Cherry is waiting in the center of the ring and awaiting the entrance of the newcomer, out steps Graves from behind the lockeroom curtain and makes a deliberate way towards the ring and then enters the ring and walks right in front of Cherry, and they're standing nose to nose.. Graves winds back and staggers Cherry into the ropes with a vicious and thunderous right hook. Graves charges and sends Cherry to the mat with a nasty short armed clothesline. Graves off the ropes and drops a fist right between the eyes of Cherry... Graves viciously yanks Cherry to his feet and fires him into the ropes...Flap jack by Graves and Cherry landed face first in the center of th ring. Graves climbs to the second turnbuckle and waits for Cherry to get to his feet. Graves leaps and spins Cherry in a 360 degree spin with a lethal clothesline...Graves covers One..two..No!!! Cherry showing his toughness by rolling his shoulder. Graves pulls up Cherry and fires him into the corner...Cherry springboards off the turnbuckle and nails Graves with a Spinning Heel Kick..But it did not drop this monster...Cherry up again and bounces off the ropes... Hurricarana by Cherry great move..OH MY Graves just popped back to his feet. Cherry off the ropes...ducks underneath a Graves boot and springboards off the ropes on the far side...Springboard Missle Dropkic--NO!!! Graves just side stepped Cherry's attempt at a Missle dropkick and Cherry crashed hard to the mat. Graves grabs Cherry by the throat and pulls him to his feet..Unbelievable strength and brutality..Chokeslam on Cherry.... Graves snatches Cherry to his feet and doubles him over with a low blow...Graves bounces off the ropes and Spikes Cherry with a devastating Spinning DDT...Graves covers...One..two..three!!! I don't believe it, this newcomer made it look easy against possibly the #1 Lightheavyweight contender...let's get the official word...Here is your winner in 8:32....Tommy Graves!!!! (Just as Tommy Graves is about to leave the ring the P.A. starts to blare "Good God" by KORN. Jonathan Hammer walks out of the entrance way as the crowd goes nuts. He is wearing black combat boots, a black KORN shirt, and black jeans. He runs down the aisle and slides under the ring ropes.) Mr. Graves...It seems you want yourself a tag team partner. Well I've been a singles wrestler all my life. But they say change is good. Well I've been watching some old tapes of you and I must admit you are impressive. I look at the tag scene in the INWI and I must admit it's crumbling. Solid Gold, All Americans, Comforters they all suck. You want a tag team you got it! Let's kick everyone's ass in this fed. I'm in! (Hammer reaches out to shake Graves hand and Graves shakes it. The two of them then walk into the back as the crowd cheers...) Match 4 The Asswhupper (with Mama and Playmaker) vs "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset This should be an interesting match as Asswhupper, the Playmaker, and Mama make their way towards the ring..Sunset tries to jump start the match with a Piscada to the outside but Asswhupper and Playmaker caught him and both dropped him throat first on the steel security rail...and NOW there's the bell..so Sunset just got his well..."ass whupped" before the bell...Asswhupper into the ring and sends Sunset hard into the corner face first...Sunset staggers out backwards..AW bounces off the ropes and drives the back of Sunset's head into the mat with an Inverted Bulldog...Cover..One..two..No!!! Sunset barely was able to kick out...AW back on the offensive..he pulls up Sunset... he's setting up for a Piledriver..and SNAP he hit a lethal jumping piledriver....AW yanks the hurtin' Sunset to his feet and whips him hard into the ropes and nails a Stun Gun on Sunset...AW is climbing to the second turnbuckle and drops a Leg across the throat of the fallen Sunset...Asswhupper is settin' up for the Money Maker...No Sunset pushes him off into the ropes...Backdrop by Sun--NO!!!! Asswhupper put on the brakes and just drove Sunset's face into the mat with a modified face first piledriver....that's a great move there ladies and gentlemen....AW yanks Sunset to his feet and crotches him on the top turnbuckle..AW is going up after him...Both men are on the top rope...TOP ROPE BELLY TO BELLY SUPERPLEX....Sunset is out cold....AW back to his feet and pulls up the prone Sunset to his feet.....MONEY MAKER!!!!! It's over....One..two..three!!!!! YES the Asswhupper showing again just why he has earned that title....let's get the official word.....Here is your winner in 9:53...The ASSWHUPPER!!!!! Brian Newark - What a solid first hour here on MONDAY Night Hot Spot!! This is a great night so far and so much more to come.
