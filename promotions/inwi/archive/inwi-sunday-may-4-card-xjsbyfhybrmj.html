[INWI] Sunday May 4 Card

4 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
INWI - Sunday, May 4 Ithaca College, Ithaca, NY ========================================= Brian Newark - Good evening ladis and Gentlemen and welcome to this INWI show on a lovely Sunday Night. We have a great action packed card for you so sit back and relax and let's get it going with the Northeast TV Championship! But, before we go to that match, let's get taped words from the NEW United States Champion, P.O. Box!! ============================================================ [Tiffany Love stands in the locker room of the new United States Champion, PO Box.] Tiffany Love: Congratulation PO Box on becoming the new United State Champion. PO Box: Thank you, Ms. Love. This really means a lot to me and my family. This shows everybody what hard work and determination can take you. I've been through a lot of things the pass couple of years. I had to deal with the unfortunate death of family members. I had to give up wrestling for three years to take care of my family. And when I returned back to wrestling everybody kept doubting me. Everybody doubted my but my best bud, Ernie the Caddie. *Ernie pops up behind PO Box gives PO Box a brief hug* Ernie: You deserve it man! I told you, you could do it. I told you! I told you! Tiffany Love: Ernie, you must be proud of this young man. Ernie: You better believe it. [In the background appears two BIG shadow figures. The shadows keep getting closer and closer to interviewers and interviewees. The shadows finally stop and the camera zooms in on who's shadow it is. It's the other members of the New Coalition Order, the Tag Team Champions The Big Brothers, with Senator Bradley.] *The Big Brothers breakout a couple bottles of champaign and start spraying in on everybody in the locker room* Steven Big: Enough of this serious crap. Let's get down and partttty! PO Box: YEAH!!! *Everybody in the locker room finally loosens up and starts dancing.* Tiffany: Well I better get out of here before this party gets out of control. Back to you guys. ============================================================ Match 1 - Northeast TV Championship Mike "The Gavel" Garvey vs Bill Bob Woijicowski This match has some wild things already. The Gavel has turned on the fans and he is up against the Springer and Woijicowski. Now, Woijicowski is looking to get his hands on some INWI gold and that would be Garvey's Northeast TV Title. Garvey and Woijicowski come to the center of the ring and the Gavel slaps Woijicowski. Bill Bob comes back with a short armed clothesline and drops the champ to the floor. Bill Bob follows it up with an elbow. Bill Bob looks in control of this match so far and you know he and The Gavel aren't best of friends. You also have to wonder if the Springer is going to have any part in this match. Bill Bob scoops up the Gavel and drives him to the mat with a powerful body slam. You know, Bill Bob is pretty strong for weighing under 200 pounds. Bill Bob picks up Garvey and sends him into the ropes. The Gavel grabs the ropes and Woijicowski misses a dropkick. Gavel drops a knee and is trying to gain his stamina back. Garvey grabs Woijicowski and drives him to the mat with a bulldog and gets a two count. Garvey heads to the ropes and climbs them and nails Bill Bob as he stands up with a double ax handle. Hey, who is this coming out to ringside? It's "Marvelous" Mike Quick and he has a crowbar!! Oh man, we know he and Bill Bob haven't been getting along. Quick comes to the ring and starts pounding on the ropes with that crowbar. What is his problem? Woijicowski sees him and they are exchanging words. Here comes the Gavel and he attacks Bill Bob. He is really taking it to him and is setting up for it, yes! It's the final verdict! 1..2...3!! Your winner in 12:22 and still INWI Northeast TV Champion, Mike "The Gavel" Garvey! Quick is in the ring and is nailing Woijicowski in the ribs with that crowbar! Here comes Springer!! Springer flys into the ring and nails Quick and the crowbar goes flying! Springer then goes after Gavel!! Here comes Springers stablemates the Comforters! Quick dashes out of the ring and leaves the area to the boos of the crowd! Quick has the crowbar though and the others are tending to Woijicowski! OH MY GOD!! Garvey just cracked Mattress with that crowbar and he flops to the mat! Springer turns and nails Garvey with a drop kick and he falls out of the ring and runs off with his belt. Mattress is out and the stretcher is coming. This is not a good thing. ============================================================ Brian Newark - That whole situation just got worse, thats for sure. Up next is the Tag Team Title Match. We have comments from the Champion Big Brothers before the bell. ============================================================ *The Big Brother are out on the golf course getting some R&R, when Michael Pontiff enters to a couple words from the Big Brothers* Pontiff: Hunter and Steven, you guys are going to be facing the Rizzotto Brothers this Sunday night. Do you guys have anything to say to them? Hunter: Rizzotto Brothers, you guys better watch what you say. You think we don't have the guts to show up?! Are you crazy?!! We will show up and we are looking forward to showing up. Because we love what we do, and that is to give something BIG to the other tag team. Steven: That's right brother, when we get through with the Rizzotto Brothers the only thing they are going to have is each other because the Big Brothers are going to show them what family is all about. Pontiff: Thank you. I can already smell the family feud going on between the Rizzotto and the Big's. ============================================================ Match 2 - Tag Team Championship The Big Brothers vs The Rizzotto Brothers This will be the first time we get to see the Rizzotto Brothers in action together and they somehow got themselves a title match. This will be Gino Rizzotto's debut here in the INWI and the two of them look ready to take on the reigning champion Big Brothers. The Big Brothers have been nearly unstoppable since winning those straps back in late March. And when the Big Brothers come out they get a wild ovation from the crowd, they are really loved by these crowds! It will be Hunter and Vito starting things off tonight and there's the bell. Vito gets a couple quick punches in as he ducks away from the bigger Hunter. Hunter finally gets his hands on Vito and picks him up in a chokeslam and sends him to the mat. Gino heads into the ring and Hunter greets him with a clothesline. Both Rizzotto's roll out of the ring and Vito looks upset. Back in they go and Vito clips Hunter's knee and starts to stomp on him. Vito tags in Gino and Gino picks up where Vito left off and stomps on Hunters knee. Stven is on the apron trying to get the crowd behind Hunter and Hunter jabs Gino in the jaw and staggers him backwards. Hunter rolls over and tags in Steven. Steven comes in and scoops Gino up and slams him to the mat. Vito climbs the ropes and nails Steven from behind and oh my Gino just nailed a belly to belly suplex! Wow, that was really impressive. Gino tags in Vito and Vito drops a knee to the back of Stvens head and then grabs a hold of him. It looks like Gino his going to superkick Steven. Steven ducked and Gino just nailed Vito square in the jaw. Hunter comes in and sends Gino out of the ring and there it is! The Big Splash! 1...2...3!! Your winners in 12:32 and still INWI Tag Team Champions, the Big Brothers! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well, next up is one of the most interesting people in the fed, Mr. Ed Rogers. Michael Pontiff is in the ring getting ready to interview the NEW Northeast Champion before his title defense against the Springer. ============================================================ (Michael Pontiff, wearing a suit that probably cost $11.50 at a flea market, and holding a microphone stands in the middle of the ring. In the crowd you see a several obscene signs that make this look like a hardcore card.) Pontiff: Today, I have the...pleasure of interviewing one of the most talked about wrestlers in the INWI. He is just off a stunning, and quite frankly surprising victory in the Battle Royal for the Northeast title, he's the one, the only, the insane, Mr. Ed Rogers! ("Won't You Be My Neighbor?" is played over the loud speaker however nothing else happens. Pontiff is signaled over to the side of the ring by a ring attendant, and the attendant tells him something. Pontiff nods and returns to the middle of the ring.) Pontiff: I have just been informed that Mr. Ed Rogers is nowhere to be found, we will continue with the next mat... (Just then, Mr. Ed Rogers emerges from beneath the time-keepers table where he was hiding with, of course, Smelly. He comes into the ring, after glancing back and forth with a sense of paranoia about it.) Pontiff: Mr. Rogers? Well I'll- Smelly: Drop the rigmarole, bub. My boy here has been hiding from the evil demons that Springer conjured to allow him to win the Northeast belt. Is this how this fed treats all it's wrestlers??? Not even a decant place to hide from the unholy? (The cameraman gets a close-up of a very confused Mike Pontiff.) Pontiff: Well, I...er that is to say this federation...I mean...er...Huh? Rogers: Oh no! There back! Springer will pay! (And with that Rogers planchas out of the ring and stumbles the whole way back to the dressing room area.) ============================================================ Match 3 - Northeast Championship Mr. Ed Rogers vs The Springer Man, Mr. Ed Rogers gets more and more insane as the days go on. Rogers, who recently won the Northeast Championship with a win in the Battle Royal on Monday Hot Spot, is set to defend against the Springer. The Springer was already out here once tonight and the former Northeast TV Champion is looking for some more gold. Springer rushes into the ring and attacks Rogers right off the bat and sends him into the turnbuckle. The Springer hits a couple of chest chops and lets out a cry for the crowd. Rogers bails out of the ring and Springer follows and catches him. The Springer slams Rogers on the outside and follows with a giant leg drop. Back in the ring go the two and Springer sends Rogers into the turnbuckle and is going for a splash. Rogers moves and thengrabs him and drops him on the top rope. Rogers sets up for a slingshot and Springer lands on the ref and he is out. Rogers gets up and covers Springer but there is no ref to make the count. Rogers gets up and sits in the corner talking to Smelly. Man this guy really is a kook! What in the hell is this? Here comes Mike Garvey. Can't this guy ever keep his nose in his own business. He used to be a hell of a guy, but now he's a punk. He comes into the ring and nails Springer with a Final Verdict. Garvey leaves the ring and he flips off the crowd and leaves laughing. The ref is coming to and Rogers gets up and connects with the Puppet Master. 1...2...3!! It's over... your winner in 9:57 and still INWI Northeast Champion, Mr. Ed Rogers. Match 4 - Televison Championship Mr. Apples vs Jason "The Crusher" Chandler Apples, who is currently the longest reigning champion in the INWI, is in for a stiff challenge from Jason "The Crusher" Chandler. The Crusher is really climbing up the ranks of the INWI and is looking to get ahold of his first title. Chandler attacks Apples before he can even get the belt from around his waist and suplex's him! That was impressive! Chandler gets on top of Apples and is driving fist after fist into Apples skull. Chandler picks up Apples and hits a short armed clothesline. Chandler again picks up Apples and connects a running Bulldog and goes for the cover. 1...2..NO!! Apples gets his shoulder up! Chandler again picks up Apples and looks like he is going to punch him, but Apples blocks it and hits with a right hand of his own. Chandler crashes to the mat and Apples goes off the ropes and drops a big leg. Apples grabs Chandler and sets him up and has him, but he drops a suplex instead of the Applesauce Drop. Apples scoops up Chandler, bounces off the ropes and hits a diving clothesline. Apples is really taken over in this match. Apples drops an elbow then looks for another leg drop, but Chandler moves. Chandler gets up and drives a knee into the forehead of Apples and picks him up. Chandler sends Apples into the ropes and goes for a backbody drop, but Apples stops and hits the Applesauce Drop!! 1...2...3!! What a match and Apples barely escaped with his title. Your winner in 14:21 andstill INWI Television Champion, Mr. Apples! ============================================================ Brian Newark - What a match and you know that's not the last that we have seen of Jason Chandler. Next up is the main event of the night, but before that, we have a taped interview with an INWI newcomer, "The Technician" Zack Sharp. Then we will go up to the ring where Brody Thunder will make his first title defense as INWI Heavyweight Champion against Ultimate Perversion. We will also catch some quick comments with Ultimate Perversion before the match. ============================================================ [ The scene opens to a fast paced limo riding down a highway. The limo pulls in front of INWI headquarters. The limo driver leaps out of the driver's side door and scurries over to the curb and opens to the door. Out pops a man standing 6' 5" tall dressed in a tan Armoni suit. He also wears black shades and has his long black hair tied back. This man is Zack Sharp. He stares and looks up at the massive building and then sees the camera and begins to speak ] Zack Sharp: So this is it the INWI, the place where many greats seem to wrestle. As I know already my friend Brody Thunder is the Heavyweight Champion. Well, congratulations Brody on another title, but Brody is this going to be like the NWA, buddy, when we are going to trade that belt back forth. Well, I guess it looks that way. Then I see Jason Starr and Jimmy Cougar in the ranks of the INWI, they were quite good wrestlers in the USIW. I wonder if they are still good. The thing that I am here for is to prove that I am the best. I am going to prove why they call me the Technician. Whether I have to beat every single person in the INWI, I will just to prove that I am the best. The Technician has spoken! [ Sharp walks into the building ] ============================================================ Tiffany Love is standing in the interview area backstage. Love: First I would like to state I am doing this interview under protest. I'll give this guy one more chance, but if he blows it... Shhh... Here he comes now... <Ultimate walks on camera.> Well, after Ultimate Perversion's dramatic press conference a couple of weeks ago, and his wholesale destruction of Cloud Eagle at Supercard, just where is he headed now. What is in the future for Ultimate Perversion? He's here with me tonight to give a brief interview before he goes up against "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder for the INWI Heavyweight Championship. Ultimate, thanks for joining us. <He nods.> So, what's in the future for Ultimate Perversion now after leaving Trent's stable? Ultimate: One word answer. Gold. Lone Wolf has dominated the INWI for too long. I am here to take him down. A lot has happened in the last couple of weeks, and I'm here to say WATCH OUT. The new and improved Ultimate Perversion is in town, and I ain't taking **** from anyone. I'd also like to apologize to you Tiffany. <Stunned look on Tiffany's face.> I have treated you unfairly in the past, and I'm sorry. Love: Thank you. Onto other things, what do you think of your chances tonight? This isn't the first title match you've had, but, and no offence, you always seem to lose the title matches. You're a great wrestler, but what causes you to fall at the final hurdle? Ultimate: No offence taken Miss Love. I can tell you the exact reason I failed so often before. That loser Trent was holding me back. Not anymore. I'm here to take this title by whatever means necessary. Brody, if you're listening, it's all over for you. Your time as number one is over. By the end of this evening, I will be the Heavyweight Champion of the INWI. <From off camera, a group of fans shout out "We love you Ultimate Perversion." Ultimate flashes them the peace sign.> Love: I guess I can't let this interview go by without mentioning the last couple of weeks in detail. First, you took over the House of Denial press conference, and quit the stable in the most public way possible, leaving Trent VanDenBerg needing hospital treatment, then you tore apart Cloud Eagle in the ring. A tough 2 weeks for you or not? Ultimate: On the contrary, quitting that den of losers was the best thing I've ever done. There's nothing holding me back now, and Brody's going to discover what my REAL potential is tonight, in the most painful way possible. Love: Well, it's nearly time for you to be in the ring, so I'll say thank you, and good luck. Ultimate: Thank you. <He walks off camera. Love waits a few seconds until he's out of earshot.> Love: I have to admit to being shocked. I'm guessing he's pumped about the match, but I was not expecting him to be so pleasant. Will this new Ultimate Perversion last? I guess only time will tell. This is Tiffany Love reporting for the INWI. Now, back to ringside. ============================================================ MAIN EVENT Match 5 - INWI Heavyweight Championship "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder vs Ultimate Perversion Well first off, I have to say WOW to the sudden turnabout of Ultimate Perversion. He actually apologized to Tiffany Love. But, the road isn't easy for Perversion, who comes up against Brody Thunder for the INWI Heavyweight Title. It took a while for Thunder to get his mitts on this belt and I am sure he doesn't plan on giving it up to soon. Thunder comes into the ring and he doesn't even acknowledge the fact that there are fans in the crowd. Thunder enters the ring and hands the belt to the ref and walks right up to Perversion and starts jabbing his trap. Perversion cocks back and pops Thunder sending him to the mat. There's the bell and we are underway. Perversion goes right after Thunder and he crawls to the corner and Perversion uses the ropes to gain leverage as he kicks away at Thunder. Thunder already looks battered in the corner, but he is a sly wrestler. Perversion scoops up the champ and slams him to the mat and then kicks him again. Perversion goes outside and to the top and as Thunder gets up, Perversion dives with a clothesline and puts Thunder on his back again. 1...2...NO!! Man was that close!! Thunder barely got his arm up and Perversion is arguing with the ref on that one. Perversion goes back to Thunder and gets ready to scoop him up, but gets a thumb to the eye. Thunder rolls out of the ring to gain his strength back and Perversion is in the ring trying to get his eyesight back. Thunder comes back in and hits a running lariat that sends Perversion on his back. Thunder follows it up with a series of elbows, bicks him up and hits a neck breaker. Thunder again picks up Perversion and powerslams him to the mat. Thunder hits a backbreaker and then a DDT. Thunder has taken over total control of this match and looks like he is setting up Perversion for a torture rack... NO!! It's the Thunderbolt! 1...2...3!! Your winner in 13:17 and still INWI Champion, "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder! Well, Thunder may of defended the belt, but he knew he had a hard one there. ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well ladies and Gentlemen, that was another great card put on by the INWI. We are out of time, so for the rest of the INWI, I am Brian Newark saying so long from Ithaca and see you tomorrow night at Monday Hot Spot!! ============================================================ This was a presentation of the INWIŽ, May 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= INWI Wrestling.....
