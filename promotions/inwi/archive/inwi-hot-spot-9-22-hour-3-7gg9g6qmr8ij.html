[INWI] Hot Spot, 9-22, Hour 3

26 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Hour Three of Hot Spot begins with fireworks again and then the camera swings to Brian Newark at ringside who is in his INWI sweater. Newark smiles as the camera swings over the crowd and catches some of the signs. "Pillow will be fluffed" "NEW SUCKS" "INWI is THE REAL DEAL" "Brotherhood4Life" "NEW4NEVER" The camera then zones in on the entrence ramp as the theme from "High Plains Drifter" echoes through the crowd...] {Brian Newark makes his way into the ring for a scheduled interview.He stands in the center of the ring and raises the mic...] Brian Newark: Okay fans right now we're about to hear from someone who's got a big decision to make. He said he'd make it _right_ here _tonight_. And now it's time to hear his decision. So right now let's bring him out... [Over the PA system comes the eerie theme from "High Plains Drifter".] Brian Newark: He _is_ the former INWI heavyweight champion hailing from Tombstone,Arizona.He says he's 'the toughest hombre in INWI'... ...the Lone Wolf himself.......Brooooody Thunnderrr! [The crowd swells with a collective heel pop as the spotlight hits the curtain at the top of the aisle.After a few lingering seconds the curtains swing open and out steps Brody Thunder. He's dressed in blue jeans,black hat and a black t-shirt which simply reads:"TRY ME". He pauses briefly,with his hands on his hips,looking over the crowd. An evil grin slowly appears on his face and he then begins walking to the ring. He steps through the ropes and takes his place beside Brian Newark.] Brian Newark: Mr Thunder...Mr Thunder..you are a man living under the microscope it appears lately. Obviously you're in the hunt to regain the INWI Heavyweight title but tonight you've got a much bigger challenge to think about. As many of the fans may or may not know... the N.E.W., a rival wrestling promotion, has issued a challenge. A challenge to the INWI. A challenge to you,Mr Thunder. A challenge to face one of their top stars in the ring at their PPV show on Sept. 26. They said they wanted an answer as soon as possible. You said they'd have it tonight. The INWI...the entire _world_ is anxious to hear your decision. So let me get straight to the point. What is your answer? [The crowd seems to fall deafeningly silent as if hanging on Thunder's every word. Thunder simply smiles, looks around the crowd and leans towards the mic being held by Brian.] Thunder: Brian Newark...how long have you known me? [Brian is a little puzzled by the question.] Brian Newark: Well...I..I believe about seven months. But... Thunder: An' in alla that time have I done everything I've said I was goin' ta do? Brian Newark: I'd have to say..yes. Yes,you have have. But I don't.. Thunder: An' Brian Newark...in alla those seven months..be it right or wrong..have you _ever_ known me not to be a man o' my word? Brian Newark: Quite frankly...no. Thunder: So Brian Newark...ya gotta believe me when I say...I could care less 'bout some other wrestlin' promotion wantin' ta prove itself. My checks get signed by the INWI. I wrestle fer the INWI. It's jus' that simple. Brian Newark: So...you're not going to... Thunder: Y'know I saw that whole deal with all o' the INWI so-called 'superstars' showin' up over there an' spread some hate an' discontent. I thought ta myself..'hey,them jabronies wanna stomp each other's noggins..hell that's their business'. Me? I got one focus. That INWI title belt. Brian Newark: Then you aren't accepting... Thunder: Y'see I got nuthin' ta prove. I've been ta the top o' the mountain. I'll be there again fore too long. So the question becomes jus' what the hell do _I_ get from kickin' some NEW runt's hide? So I thought about it long an' hard.Why should I kick the NEW in the tail?An'then the answer came ta me, Brian Newark. An' ya know what that answer was? Brian Newark: Uh..no..I don't.. Thunder: Lemme tellya. The answer ta why I _should_ kick the NEW's ass...heh,heh... [The camera gets a close-up of Thunder who agains sports that devilish grin.] ...is because I _can_. [Thunder shifts his position and appears to be directing his comments now strictly to the NEW.] Thunder: So NEW...lissen up folks. There's a storm headin' yer way. Y'see I ain't the kinda man ya go pokin' yer finger in my chest. I'll break the damn thing off. You fellas seem ta think yer pickin' a fight with someone who'll jus' roll over fer ya. Ask anyone in this business an'whether they like me or hate me they'll all tell ya the same thing. If ya get in _my_ face I'll put ya on a gurney. See I checked..an' it jus' so happens my social calendar is free on Sept 26th,boys. [The crowd begins to swell with a face pop. A shocked smile comes over the face of Brian Newark.] Brian Newark: So..you're saying that...you'll accept the challenge? [Again the crowd swells with a face pop anticipating the obvious now.] Thunder: I'll not only accept the challenge I go ya one better. If I lose ta whatever NEW pantywaist they put in that ring...I don't care if it's that pencil-neck Eddie Jacobs...or that lardass Bobby Davis or even that no-talent clown Vinny Rizzo ...that's right boys,Brody Thunder does his homework...an' if I lose that match... [Thunder's smile disappears.] ...then I'll hang up my boots an' go home fer good. Brian Newark: Are you saying you'll....retire if you lose? Thunder: You hard o' hearin',Newark. If I lose ta whatever jabrony they send ta that ring I'll leave this sport. Plain an' simple. An' jus' so ya don't go gettin' any ideas bout doin' a gang job on me lemme tell ya up front... ...I ain't comin alone. See Mama Thunder didn't raise no fool in her lil boy Brody. I prefer ta travel alone but even I know when it's safer ta have the numbers with ya..not against ya. [Thunder winks at the camera.] So NEW....ya better be ready fer a war like you ain't never had up there in yer precious lil federation. There's a storm a-comin' yer way, an' where the lightnin' strikes ...folks'll tell ya... ...Thunder ain't far behind. See ya on the 26th boys. [Thunder exits the ring to a huge face pop and storms up the aisle not acknowledging the fans accolades as he does so.] Brian Newark: Well there you have it wrestling fans. The Lone Wolf has accepted the challenge of the N.E.W. organization and will be facing an N.E.W. representative on Sept. 26th. _And_ should he lose the match he's stated that he will in fact retire from wrestling!Well fans we'll have that match footage after the PPV and will air it right here on the INWI wrestling network. You won't want to miss it! Match 9 - Four Corners Match. Bill Bob was led to the ring by his brother, Jim Bob Woijicowski. Don't take this guys redneck attitudes lightly though, he can go at it. He is also involved with attacking the NEW and has been hanging with Apples and Houston, so that makes him even worse. There's the bell and we are underway. He looks possesed. He looks psycho. Bill Bob scoops up Brown, sends him into the ropes and nails a spinning heel kick and then climbs to the top rope and nails a leg drop from the top. That doesn't mean that they are bad, weak, or wrong. It just means that they choose a different path. Others keep going like they've always gone, like the runaway trains that they always were. [Pause.] Wug: Now, more than ever, I've shown that I'm a carriage. A carriage on the runaway train called the "Brotherhood". When you're rolling along that track with guys like C&C and the King, you can guarantee that you've found yourself a pretty damn unstoppable vehicle. But I'm also an individual, and one hell of an individual one at that. [Glenn cracks a wide grin.] Wug: Sure, I get a kick out of the NEW thing. And sure, I get a kick out of ruffling the feathers of the other "collectives"... but I'm a singles wrestler first and foremost. I have a career to think about. Maybe some people think that I came to the INWI for the money, like I'm some kind of mercenary or shock-trooper for the Brothers. Hey, if that's how I seem, you can believe what you want.. But know this - I don't need the money. Some people are mercenary 'cause they get a little pleasure out of it. [Another pause.. He rubs his hands together as he gathers his thoughts, then cracking his knuckles one by one..] Wug: So I'm stepping into the ring again. That's what I'm here for, and I'm hoping that is what Thane MacDuff is here for. We've both done pretty well for ourselves so far in the INWI. I've taken a look at what you have to offer. Not too bad. But is it enough to stop.. (he points a thumb to his chest, then over his shoulder).. the runaway train?
