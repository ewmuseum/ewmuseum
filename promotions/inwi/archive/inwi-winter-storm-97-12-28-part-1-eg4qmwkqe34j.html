Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
VOICE OVER: The INWI has gone through some wild times. A battle with another fed. Battles in their own fed. Many great names have been here. "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder. "Bullywug" Glenn Hudson. "The Technician" Zack Sharp. King Apples. Tony Houston. Springer. Mr. Ed Rogers. Sam Adams. [The screen then shows clips of matches between Thunder and Sharp...matches of Hudson...and clips of the famous ladder match between Tony Houston and Springer. It shows Apples in a match with Ed Rogers. And Sam Adams laughing at the crowd. It then shows all of them next to each other with their respective belts that they have won.] VOICE OVER: Champion. Such a word. A word that epitomizes this sport. A champion is "the man." A Champion is one that everyone strives to be like. A Champion is one that all envy. A Champion is what everyone wants to be. [A video clip shows a picture of all of the INWI Championship belts. After each belt it shows a picture of all the men who have held the titles.] VOICE OVER: Champions and the INWI go hand and hand. INWI Champions are some of the best in the world. Many men have started out in the INWI and gone on to great things. Some of the best wrestlers in the INWI are those that are not takes seriously. Some of the best are those you would never expect to win. [Video clip shows many INWI superstars that don't get the respect they deserve from their peers. It also shows a couple of people who have left the INWI. It shows Michael Bane winning the INWI Light Heavyweight Title from Bill Bob Woijicowski. It shows Michael Bledsoe.....and then Jason Chandler. David McCarter winning the Light Heavyweight title from Sam Adams.... Jonathan Hammer and Tommy Graves teaming up.] Voice Over: Youth is a great thing in this sport. But can it beat some of the greatest talent to walk in a ring. Can youth stun the world and beat experience. Can the young keep the titles they already own? What can happen when these two collide? Does anyone know? We will all find out tonight. Only on pay per view. In Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.....] The INWI Presents... LIVE FROM THE CORESTATES SPECTRUM Philadelphia, Pa WINTER STORM '97 December 28, 1997 [The crowd inside is going nuts as the crowd is packed with nearly 20,000 screaming fans.
