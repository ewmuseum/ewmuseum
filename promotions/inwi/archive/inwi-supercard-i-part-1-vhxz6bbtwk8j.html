[INWI SuperCard I - Part 1

28 April 1997

INWI SuperCard I ============================================================ Sunday, April 27,1997 LIVE ON FOX The Spectrum, Philadelphia, PA ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well, well, well, We are finally in the BIG TIME!! It may of taken us over a month of hard work to get here, but we are here!! This is INWI Supercard I and we are coming to you LIVE on the FOX Network. This is going to be a great show as FOX has opened up enough time for us to do our show. What about the Main Event?? "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder gets his crack finally for the INWI Heavyweight Championship. On the other side, all the fans are now embracing "The Icon" Pimp Daddy Brown as he tries to hold onto the title that only he has held. The INWI... we are now becoming mainstream. Before we head to the ring, let's get comments from one of the wrestlers oppening up SuperCard I!!!! ============================================================ Tiffany Love tries to catch Bill Bob Woijicowski as he enters the dressing room. TL: Bill Bob tell us about your match? BBW: I'm going to make a cucumber out of that pickle. Dell is in a serious pickele if you ask me! TL: Bill Bob wait.... BBW: Not now I don't have anything more to say so good bye! TL: Well you heard it, Bill Bob enters this match with complete confidence. ============================================================ SuperCard Bill Bob Woijicowski vs Dell Pickle Well, Supercard is underway and this has the makings of being a tremendous card! We are stating things up With Bill Bob Woijicowski and Dell Pickle. Woijicowski sends Pickle into the ropes and follows with a clothesline. Woijicowski stomps at Pickle then uses the ropes to help put force behind a couple of knees. Woijicowski scoops up Pickle and slams him to the mat and heads to the top rope. Woijicowski nails a leg drop from the top and Pickle is in alot of pain. It looks like his first match on LIVE national TV won't be a long one! Woijicowski again heads to the top and nails the Spur!! There's the cover...1...2...3!!! Your winner in 3:42 in our first match, Bill Bob Woijicowski! SuperCard The Terminators vs The Fantastic Surfers Hi and Tide come out to their pumping music as the Fantastic Surfers get this crowd pumping here in Philly! And the Terminators, Blade and Gunner charge the ring and attack before the bell. There's the bell! Blade and Gunner are already pounding on on both of the Fantastic Surfers and throw both Hi and Tide over the top and the crowd goes into a chorus of boos. Well, we finally have one on one and it's Gunner and Tide getting ready for action. Gunner takes control and works Tide to the mat. The Terminators know not to let these Surfers start flying! Gunner slaps an armbar on Tide then works their way to their feet and Tide does a back flip to get out of the hold. Tide hits Gunner with a drop kick and he tags in Hi. Gunner gets back up and tags Blade, so we have all new compeditors in the ring. Hi bounces off the ropes and gets caught, scooped up and powerslammer. I just think these Terminators are to strong for The Fantastic surfers. Blade scoops up Hi and sets him up for a vertical suplex and drops him to the mat. Blade again picks up Hi and sends him into his corner and follows with a clothesline. These guys look like they want to end this match now. Blade goes across the ring and clotheslines Tide off the apron and goes back to the work at hand. Gunner is in the ring now as Blade climbs to the top rope. There's a powerbomb from Gunner and Blade flys from the top with a leag drop, it's Termination!! 1...2...3!! Here are your winners in 9:32, Blade and Gunner, the Terminators!!! SuperCard Apocalypse vs Mr. Cherry Apocalypse has been on the upswing lately and he goes up against a member of the Brotherhood of Sour Grapes. Cherry, a high flying risk taker, may have some troubles against the bigger Apocalypse. Apocalypse lunges at Cherry as the bell sounds and hits the turnbuckle. Cherry waits and nails Apocalypse with a drop kick as he turns around. Off the ropes he goes and nails a flying elbow that puts Apocalypse on the mat and he rolls out of the ring. Cherry goes to the top and flys, but Apocalypse moves and Cherry looks really hurt outside the ring. Apocalypse picks him up and chokeslams him outside the ring then gets him up and back in the ring. Apocalypse drops a leg and gets a two count, but Cherry barely got his arm up. Apocalyps lifts Cherry up by his hair and puts a neck bender on. You can hear Cherry screaming in pain. Cherry finally figures a way out as he stands up and delivers a back drop on Apocalypse as both men are on the mat. Cherry stumbles to his feet as does Apocalypse and Cherry hits a drop kick. Cherry then nails a leg and he's climbing to the top rope.... oh my god!! He nails the Cherry Bomb!!! 1...2...3!! It's over!! What a great match, but your winner in 13:42 is Mr. Cherry!!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well, we are now at our first title match, the INWI Northeast TV Title. Lets get some comments backstage from the challenger, Mike "The Gavel" Garvey. ============================================================ *Tiffany Love is standing in the interview area backstage; standing to her left is Mike "The Gavel" Garvey* Love: Fans, it looks ike Supercard 1 is off to an exciting and great start; up next is a match for the Northeast TV title which features the man who is standing next to me..."The Gavel" Mike Garvey. Garvey: Miss Love, tonight is the night! Tonight, The Gavel will get a taste of the gold. I've worked long and hard in the INWI and I feel that tonight is the night! Springer, it's been nice knowing you as the Northeast TV champ, but tonight I'm gonna take that title away and put it around my waist where it rightfully belongs. Love: You certainly have had your share of shots at the gold and you've come up short each time...Springer is one hell of a challenge ya know. Garvey: Springer, you've been carrying that title with an injustice, and the Gavel is just the man to bring a little justice to it. After tonight, you're gonna feel a little bit lighter because after I hit the Final Verdict, the N.E. TV title will be lifted off of you...and justice WILL be finally served. Love: Ladies and gentlemen, could we see a new N.E. TV champ tonight? I'll be watching...let's get back down to the ring. ============================================================ SuperCard Northeast Television Title Match The Springer vs Mike "The Gavel" Garvey Well, we are at our first title match and this promises to be a great one. You heard Bill Bob Woijicowski when he said he may have to watch this match, so who knows what could happen. Hey, there he is! Woijicowski has come about halfway down the runway and set up a chair. Looks like he wants to get a close up look at this match. Mike "The Gavel'' Garvey notices Woijicowski and points at him with an evil look and we are almost set to go in this match. There's the bell and Springer and Garvey lock up. Springer is sent into the ropes and comes back to knock Garvey down. Springer scoops him up and bodyslams Garvey to the mat. Springer grabs Garvey again and nails a standing dropkick that knocks the Gavel out of the ring. The Gavel shot right back up and got on the ring apron and he looks like he is ready to flip out. He quickly enters the ring and charges Springer but is met with a clothesline. Garvey again is on the mat and Springer drops a leg. The Springer gets up and shouts at the crowd and they go nuts! The Springer bounces off the ropes and goes after Garvey but is met with a shot down low. Garvey then nails a couple knees to the head and runs out of the ring. Oh my, he just nailed Bill Bob Woijicowski with a a clothesline and he just hit a version on the Final Verdict outside the ring. I think Woijicowski is out. Springer is running towards Garvey but he grabs Woijicowski's chair and nails Springer in the head with it and the Springer falls to the ground. Garvey scoops up Springer and puts him over his shoulder and then gets to the ring where he throws him in. Garvey climbs in and hits the final verdict. 1...2...3!! We have a new champ. The winner and NEW INWI Northeast TV Champ, Mike "The Gavel" Garvey!! The crowd is now booing Garvey and he used to be cheered by the fans. He just flipped off the crowd as he walked wack to the lockerroom with his new belt. Woijicowski and the Springer look like they are just coming through. This guy makes me sick with what he has done and I am sure the Springer and Woijicowski both want at Garvey now. ============================================================ Brian Newark - Before our next match we are going backstage to get some quick comments from both Harvey Thunder and Michael Hollywood, two opposites who I guess are now strong friends. Lets get the comments. ============================================================ Michael Pontiff: I am here in the lockerroom talking with two guys who have apparently become friends. Michael Hollywood and Harvey Thunder. I have to ask Michael, Why the sudden change of heart? Hollywood: I haven't really changed. Maybe I've grown up a little. When I was kid, I never had a role model. I just got things done and didn't look up to anybody. Now a few weeks ago, I hear news that there is some guy here who looks up to me. My first instinct was to find this guy and beat him up. But when I saw Harvey, I saw a kid who never had the chance to have a role model and something just happened. That is why I stopped Solid Gold from attacking him. Pontiff: It appears that they have kicked you out of the House of Style for it. Hollywood: Nobody kicked me out. I quit the House of Style. Bradley and Blake are two more guys who need to grow up and smell the coffee. Pontiff: Well tonight you face Senator Bradley. Do you have any comments about the match? Hollywood: All I have to say is that even though you might try to tell people that I have "changed", I still believe that I am the best wrestler alive and I will show that to Mr. Bradley tonight. Pontiff: O.K. Harvey, How do you feel your odds are in your match tonight against Jonathan Sunset? Thunder: Michael Hollywood is my hero!!!!! Pontiff: I know but can you talk about your match a little. Thunder: With him by my side I can't lose!!!! Ha!!!Ha!!!Ha!!! (With that Thunder storms towards the ring and starts rolling around. Hollywood runs after him to try and calm him down) Pontiff: O.K. Well it appears that Harvey is ready for his match as he has already entered the ring. Let's get back to ringside!!! ============================================================ SuperCard Harvey Thunder vs "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset Here is an interesting matchup as the two men lock it up. Sunset, the former Northeast Champion, is on the biggest losing streak of his career and can't seem to jump out of it. Thunder on the other hand is on the uprise and is being supported somewhat by Michael Hollywood of all people. I mean, Hollywood left The House of Style because of this guy. Absolutley amazing. Sunset has taken the upper hand here and just hit a running DDT. Sunset gets a two count but Thunder got his arm up. Sunset scoops up Thunder and nails a snap suplex and follows it up with a series of elbow drops. Thunder is reeling right now and can't seem to get into a good rhythm. Sunset goes outside and he's climbing the ropes. Sunset leaps and it's a splash, but Thunder moves!! Thunder is trying to get to his feet as Sunset is rolling around on the mat. Thunder drops a leg and goes for a quick cover. 1...2...3!! Oh my, I think that splash absolutley devastated Sunset and he still looks out cold. Here is your winner in 8:33, Harvey Thunder. Uh oh, look out here comes Solid Gold and they are storming the ring. They have Harvey Thunder backed up in a corner and they are starting to pound on him. HEY LOOK!! Here comes Michael Hollywood and he has a chair! Solid Gold bails from the ring as Hollywood comes in to check on his new friend. Man oh man, this is getting exciting. Solid Gold is looking up at Hollywood and I can't read lips, but they don't look happy. They just stare at Hollywood as they leave the ring area. Hollywood helps Thunder back to the dressing room and his match is up next! SuperCard Michael Hollywood vs Senator Bradley Hollywood heads back to the ring by himself as Senator Bradley is already in the ring. Hollywood storms in and locks up with Bradley and we are underway. Hollywood slaps on a headlock and is sent into the ropes. Leap frog and Bradley catches Hollywood in a sleeper, but Hollywood gets out with a elbow to the midsection. Hollywood grabs Bradley while he is bent over and hits a piledriver and Bradley is grabbing his neck. Hollywood scoops him up and hits a dropkick that sends Bradley to the floor. Hollywood follows him out and slams him on the floor. Hollywood wants to get this match over as quick as possible because he knows Solid Gold is still around somewhere. Hollywood sends Bradley back into the ring and sends him into the ropes. What a collision!! The ref goes down as does Bradley and Hollywood. Oh god, this is bad...here comes Solid Gold. Blake comes in and nails Hollywood with a powerbomb and Bradley flys with a splash. Now they go after Bradley and Blake piledrives him to the mat. Bradley picks up Bradley and slams him on top of Hollywood and they are leaving the ring. Here comes Harvey Thunder and he still looks hurt from the last match. Solid Gold meet him in the aisle and double clothesline and they are kicking him!! The ref is coming to...1...2...3!!! Ahhh, what a bad call! Your winner in 7:37, Senator Bradley!! Thunder is hurt as are both Bradley and Hollywood. This is not a good situation. ============================================================ Brian Newark - So far, what a great card!! The Booger Brothers are looking for gold and hope to earn a shot if they win tonight. They are backstage before their match against the former champions, the Mac Daddies. ============================================================ Tiffany love enters the dressing room to meet the Booger Brothers. There they are waiting with their manager Q-Tip dresses in their bright green wrestling tights. They sit together on a bench forming a seemingly endless of green blob. TL: Well what are your expectations of the match tonight? Flem: Well if you don't mind Snot I'd like to take this one. The Mac Daddies may think they're all that but I promise you by the end of the night the only ones that will be maccing will be us. TL: Those are big words for an inexperienced pair of wrestlers. Snot: You know what Ms. Love, we may be just the new kids on the block but when we knock off the block of our opponents there will be no telling what damage will be done. The Mac Daddies think they are the ones who will win this match but with Q-Tip in our corner we can't lose. Q: That's right Tiffany my boys can't lose. They'll fling and I mean fling the Daddies back to the getto where they belong! TL: Those are strong words. I hope you two can back it up! Q: Don't you worry your pretty little head about my boys they'll be doin' all the damage tonight. Flem: Are you ready for the most fun since the last time you pulled a big chunk of wax out of your ear Snot? Snot: Sure am bro. Q: Are you ready for all you can handle Macs because my boys are as slippery as fresh boogers. TL: That's a beautiful image!? Q: Aint it Tiffany?! TL: Well thank you boys for this time and good luck! Q: Thank you Tiffany the pleasure has been all mine! ============================================================ SuperCard The MacDaddies vs The Booger Brothers The Booger Brothers, Flem and Snot, unsuccessful in taking the Tag Team Titles from The Big Brothers. Now they go up against the former tag team champions, Da Pimp and Da Playa, The Mac Daddies. It'll be Booger and Da Playa starting things off and here we go. Collar and elbow tie up as Da Playa takes early control. Da Playa slaps on a headlock and nails a standing bulldog. Da Playa tags in Da Pimp and Pimp takes over where Playa left off. Pimp is stomping at Booger and drops a knee to the head. Scoops him up and sends him out of the ring. Pimp follows him out and nails Booger with a chair and he crumples to the floor. They are setting up a table and roll Booger back in the ring. Pimp grabs him, but he is on the apron. Is he going to suplex him over? NO!! Booger blocked it and just tossed Pimp through the table. Booger makes his way over to Flem and makes the tag. I don't know if Pimp is going to get up. Playa is out there and helps Pimp back in the ring. Flem dropkicks Playa off the apron and out into the crashed in table. Flem drops a leg on Pimp and scoops him up, sends him into the ropes and there's a dropkick. Pimp falls out of the other side of the ring and crumples to the floor. It looks like Pimp is really hurt and he's crawling around the ring, but the ref is occupied with The Booger Brothers. One of the Mac Daddies rolls back into the ring and there's a rollup!! 1...2...3!! Oh my god!!! It's over!! Was that the legal Mac Daddy? Your winners in 11:27, The Mac Daddies!
