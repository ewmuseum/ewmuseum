[INWI] Hot Spot, 12-22, Runover time

30 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MONDAY HOT SPOT!!! RUN OVER TIME!! HERSHEY PARK ARENA, HERSHEY, PA LIVE!!! Brian Newark - Ladies and gentlemen, thanks for staying with the number one wrestling show on Monday Nights. We have two minutes left and have been allowed run over time and we are set to go, so let's get back to the ring. Match 11 - Special Rematch The Springer (with Dr. Bedpan) vs Jonathan Hammer Well, this was a great matcgh last week and should be a great match this week. Both men have huge matches at the pay per view and this is a very important match. The bell has sounded and we are underway. Hammer and Springer lock up in the middle of the ring and it's a stand off. Both men back off and then they tie up again. Hammer with a headlock....Springer sends him into the ropes....leap frog..... and they crash shoulder to shoulder and neither of them budge. Hammer with a right hand and Springer fires back. Springer with three shots, off the ropes and sends Hammer to the mat with a clothesline. Springer with an elbow drop and then a cover, 1....NO! Hammer kicks out at one. Springer now puts on a chin lock and Hammer trying to work to his fee and now gets there. An elbow to the midsection of Springer and another. Hammer breaks the hold, off the ropes, shoulder block that sends Springer to the mat. Hammer off the ropes again and nails a kick to the midsection.....double arm DDT! What a move and Springer is down! A cover by Hammer, 1........2.......NO! Springer rolls a shoulder and I am not sure how. Hammer now goes off the rope and nails a leg drop and then goes for another cover. 1......2.......NO! Springer again kicks out and the crowd is starting to go nuts. This is wild. Springer is back to his feet as Hammer brought him up. Hammer swings and Springer blocks the left. Springer fires back....again.....and again! A headbutt by Springer and Hammer falls to the floor. The Springer is feeling the crowd and off the ropes he goes and splashes Hammer. Springer grabs Hammer and there's a suplex. Springer is looking like he's on a mission in here and he is ready. Springer to the second rope and nails an elbow. A cover, 1.......2......NO! Hammer kicks out. Springer scoops up Hammer, swinging neckbreaker. He's setting Hammer up for the Springer Cutter and we all know when he hits that, it's over. No one hits that move like the former Champ. Springer picks up Hammer and is setting up......Hammer blocks it, twirls Springer around and there's a belly to belly suplex! Hammer is hurt and can't go for a cover as both men start to get to their feet. Hammer is upfirst and kicks Springer in the midsection....picks him up, inverted atomic drop. Hammer takes Springer and places him on the top rope. Is he setting up for a superplex?? NO!! DDT OFF THE TOP ROPE!! Hammer with a cover, 1........2..........3!!!!! It's over! Oh my!! Hammer just pinned the former Heavyweight Champ!! Here is your winner in 13:24, Jonathan Hammer!!! MAIN EVENT Match 12 - Tag Team Championship James Pillow/Vinny Vitrone (with Mr. Flamboyant) vs The Comforters (with Dr. Bedpan) This should be an interesting tag team affair featuring two of The Brotherhood, Pillow and Vitrone whom have been successful in recent weeks tagging together. And of course we have the experienced tag team champions..Box Spring and Mattress...The Comforters. This should to say the least be truly..a main even caliber match. It's going to be Vitrone and Box Spring starting this match. Box locks up with Vitrone and pushes the Brother into and unleashes a chop..Wooo!!! Box headbutt Vitrone and sends him across the ring and follows in with a Handspring Elbow dive. Box Spring pulls up Vitrone and scoops Vitrone and tags his partner, Running powerslam by Box and Mattress leaps off the top rope with a flying elbow across the chest of Vitrone. Cover..One..two..No!!! Vitrone kicks out with authority. Mattress pulls up Vitrone.. thumb to the eye by Vitrone..not scientific but effective. Vitrone grabs Mattress and takes him over with a short, quick, Snap suplex..Vitrone tags in Pillow and Pillow leaps to the top turnbuckle and waits for Mattress to get to his feet...Pillow leaps, Flying Hurricarana..Pillow is now pounding away at the face of Mattress...Pillow pulls Mattress to his feet..side waist lock...Side Suplex and Mattress crashed hard on his head..Pillow with a kip up and bounces off the ropes and drops a legdrop across the head of Mattress and covers..One..two..th--No!! Mattress rolls his shoulder. Pillow pulls up Mattress and sends him into the ropes and tags Vitrone in...Pillow drops down in front and Vitrone nails a Thez Press and is pounding away at Mattress' face. Vitrone scoops Mattress..Box Spring off the top rope and drops kicks his partner in the back and Mattress is covering Vitrone..One..two..t-No!!! Vinny Vitrone kicked out....Both men back up...boot to the midsection by Mattress..blocked by Vitrone..Insiguri kick by Mattress and Vitrone crumples to that mat. Mattress is crawling towards his corner... and he NEEDS to make a tag soon..Mattress tags in Box Spring!! Box Spring in and drops an elbow on the back of Vitrone...Pillow comes in..Box Spring catches him with an Inverted Atomic Drop and sends Pillow to the outside with a dropkick. Box Spring pulls up Vitrone and sends him into the ropes...Box catches Vitrone and drops him down with a Samoan Drop...Box Spring covers..One..two..th--No!!! Box pulls up Vitrone and whips him into the corner...Vitrone did a flip and is now seated, stunned on the top turnbuckle..Box Spring climbs up...Belly to Back Superplex!!! Box Spring covers..One.. two ..thr--No!! Pillow in and drops a leg across the back of the head of Box Spring. Mattress back in and is now brawing in the corner with Pillow. Pillow nails Mattress with a low blow and tosses Mattress over the top rope. OH MY G--!!! Mattress just crashed hard through a table!!! Box Spring up and pulls up Vitrone...He's going for a DDT.. <SMACK> SUPERKICK ON BOX SPRING!!! Pillow pulls up Vitrone and slaps some sense into him....they both look at the injured Mattress on the outside..he's grabbing at his knee...Pillow and Vitrone are on the top rope.Pillow leaps..legdrop...Vitrone leaps... Splash...Pillow coves...one..two...three!!!!!! I don't believe it!! The Brotherhood has another championship....They now have the INWI Heavyweight, Northeast, and NOW Tag Team Championship. Pillow goes to the outside and rolls in Mattress and rolls him into the ring..Mattress who apparently injured his knee...Pillow pulls up Mattress.. Simply The Bestplex!! Vitrone goes to the outside and grabs a chair and drapes it over Mattress' left knee..Vitrone is on the second rope..and drops leg across the knee...The Brotherhood are trying to mame this guy...however, after several stomps to the knee, Pillow and Vitrone leave the ring with their new belts..Let's get the official word. Here are your winners in 13:39.......and NEW INWI Tag Team Champions....James "Simply The Best" Pillow and "The Vindicator" Vinny Vitrone!!!!! Brian Newark - Oh man, the Brotherhood has just claimed the Tag Team Championship. But why did they attack the former champs after the match. That was junk and I can't believe it. They will get theirs for that one, I am sure of that. But ladies and gentlemen, we are out of time. Stay tuned and the FULL PPV CARD will be announced!!!!
