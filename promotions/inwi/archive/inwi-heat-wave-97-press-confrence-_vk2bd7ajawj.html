Heat Wave '97 Press Conference The Manhattan Center, New York, NY Saturday, June 28, 1997 ============================================================ Brian Newark - Welcome ladies and gentlemen to the Press Conference for the INWI's first Pay-Per-View, Heat Wave 97. We have a bunch of superstars on hand tonight and also from what I understand a big introduction of a new superstar by Commissioner Jerry DeLuise. Commissioner DeLuise is on hand to introduce all of the wrestlers as they come to the podium to do their speeches. Most wrestlers will have the opportunity to speak and then they will usually allow members of the press a couple of questions before they leave. We know that Brody Thunder and Zack Sharp are both here so that could get interesting. We don't know how many wrestlers have showed up to talk with the media, but we do know that the champions and challengers of the Championship Matches were told by Commissioner Jerry DeLuise to show up. As of this time it is my understanding that we only have a couple of champions and a couple of challengers here. I know this will not make DeLuise happy if others don't show up. Well, nonetheless, we do have some of the participants here tonight and some of them have things to say. Let's first go to Mr. Ed Rogers, who this weekend will take on 10 members of the jWo. Let's now give the podium to Commissioner Jerry DeLuise, who will be the MC for tonights activities. Jerry DeLuise - Welcome members of the press to the Press Conference for the INWI's first ever Pay-per-view.
