INWI Monday Hot Spot!!

23 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
INWI Monday Night Hot Spot!! ============================================================ Monday, April 21, 1997 Nighty Night Club, Buffalo, NY ============================================================ Scoop Ropes - Welcome ladies and gentlemen to Monday Hot Spott!! from the Nighty Night Club in Buffalo, NY! We are rocking and have three great matches for you tonight. Without further delay, let's head up to the ring. Match 1 - Non Title Match The Glamour Boys vs Solid Gold The Glamour Boys have decided to take the INWI by storm and go into the tag team ranks. Because this is their debut match as a tag team, the INWI refused to give them a title match, but decided that they would let them take on the TV Champs in a non-title match. It'll be Jimmy Cougar and Blake Gold starting things off. Cougar moves around the ring slowly watching his counterpart then attacks and hits Blake with a running lariat. Cougar is on him like a rabid animal and he is pounding Blake. Bradley wants the tag, but he can't get to him. Cougar stands up and spits on Bradley and quickly tags in Jason Starr. The Hollywood Kid picks up where Cougar left off and he is whipping Blake. Bradley dashes in the ring and clobbers Starr. Here comes Cougar and we have an all out brawl. Blake is still on the ground, I don't know what the Glamour Boys did to him, but he is out cold. Bradley is thrown out of the ring, but comes right back in and he has a chair! Bradley nails Starr and then Cougar! There's the bell! Your winners in 5:27 as a result of a disqualification, Jimmy Cougar and Jason Starr, the Glamour Boys!! But look at Blake Gold, he is still out col. What the hell did the Glamour Boys do to him? here comes the stretcher and we'll have to look into this one. I am sure Commissioner Jerry DeLuise will issue a statement onthis one. Match 2 - INWI Northeast Television Title Match The Springer vs Bill Bob Woijicowski The Springer has a big title defense coming up against Mike "The Gavel" garvey at this weekends Supercard I, but first has to get by a stubborn Bill Bob Woijicowski. Here we go and Woijicowski starts out right away and nails the Springer. Springer goes back into the ropes and Woijicowski connects with a knee to the mid section. Woijicowski scoops up Springer and slams him and follows with an elbow to the forehead. Bill Bob climbs to the second rope and hits another elbow then smacks Springer around on the mat a little bit. Springer looks really hurt as Bill Bob hits a leg drop and then another. Bill Bob looks to the crowd and signals for it! Yes he nailed the Spur!! We are going to have a new Champion tonight! WAIT!! Here comes Mike Garvey and he nails Woijicowski and there's the bell. Man oh man... you knew Garvey wanted to make sure the Springer had the belt this weekend. Wow, Garvey nails Springer with a knee to the back of the head then starts to pound Woijicowski. Oh wait, Springer is up and clips Garvey's knee!! Wow!! Tere it is, he slaps the Coil on him and Garvey is in pain! INWI officials are out here and get the Springer to let go as Garvey hobbles to the back. But, the winner in 8:32 as a result of a DQ and still INWI Northeast TV Champ, The Springer!! Scoop Ropes - Before our main event, let's grab a word with the challenger, "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset. ============================================================ (The camera is in a room that is quite nice and on the wall is the INWI Northeastern Title in a frame. In walks "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset) Tiffany Love - Mr. Sunset, it seems you have been on a bad streak lately. Are you still ready to wrestle in the INWI or are the rumors that you are leaving true? Sunset - Yeah, I am still here world. I may not of regained that Northeastern Title like I have wanted to, but I am still here. I just so happen to be on a bad streak lately. Well, That's going to end! The Chuckle Heads are still here and we will gain some gold soon! We are ready to get it on! I will not leave this league until I am ready to and that is not so yet. I still have some scores to settle and I want to get more gold. I have won one title, now I am ready for another. Michael Pontiff - So what are you shooting for? Anything in particular? Sunset - No, nothing in my mind, I just want gold. I had the Northeast Championship belt once before, so that may be a way to go. I really just want a title and I will do whatever I have to do to get it. Now, I have to go train to get some gold, I'll talk to you later Michael. (Sunset leaves the room and touches the frame with his old INWI Northeastern Title...) ============================================================ MAIN EVENT Match 3 - Television Championship Mr. Apples vs "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset Well, Sunset finally gets his title match again. He has had a bad streak lately, but he is looking to turn it around and wants to get started with Mr. Apples and the Television Championship. We are set to go and there's the bell. Apples attacks right off the bat and knocks Sunset down. The crowd is into this, or should I say the girls, who go crazy for sunset. Apples scoops up Sunset and slams him to the mat and follows with a vicious leg drop. Apples again picks up the Sunset and delivers a short arm lariat that knocks Sunset down again. Apples picks up Sunset and looks for a punch, but it's blocked! Sunset is battling back! A standing dropkick and down goes Apples. Sunset climbs the ropes and goes for a body block. 1...2...NO!! What a close one. Sunset picks up Apples, but Mr. Apples reaches out and grabs Sunset by the neck! Chokeslam!! Picks him up... and a kick to the midsection. There it is, the Applesauce Drop!! 1...2...3!! Your winner in 8:47 and still INWI TV Champion, Mr. Apples!! Hey look out, here comes Mr. Ed Rogers and he jumps Apples!! Lookout, there's Firestar and we are having SuperCard right here!! These three are pounding on eachother!!! Scoop Ropes - We are out of time fans!! Apples, Firestar and Rogers are still going at it! We'll see you later this week! For the rest of the INWI, I am Scoop Ropes saying so long!!! This was a presentation of the INWI, April 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= INWI Wrestling.....
