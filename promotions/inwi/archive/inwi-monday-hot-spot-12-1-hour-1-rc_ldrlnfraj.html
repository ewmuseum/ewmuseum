[INWI] Monday Hot Spot, 12-1, Hour 1

9 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera immediately cuts on as a rumored appearance by the famed INWI Heayvweight Champion is proving to be true, as a black, HumVee pulls in at high speed into the backstage area of where Hot Spot will be taking about an hour later...] [The HumVee comes to a screeching halt, then quietness....The driver side door slowly opens and steps the INWI Heavyweight Champion, "The Ringmaster" Tony Houston. Houston has on a long, black, leather, trench coat which hangs open, Houston is carrying a black duffel bag, and is wearing a white New York Yankee hat ducked to cover his eyes. However, Houston does not appear to have the Heavyweight title belt with him. Perhaps it's in the duffel bag.] [Houston walks to the 'INWI Personnel Only' area of the arena, where he is quickly met by Tiffany Love] Love: Mr. Houston, Houston: Aight Love, just calm down....So how was the Commish's office? Love: I have no idea what you are talking about. Houston: We's all know ya' and Adams '*ahem** discussed yer contract... Love: Mr. Houston I never.. [Love drops the microphone and storms off...] Houston: Biznitch, now go run ta' yer man Adams' office 'n try 'n git me fired...Bitch lying knowing she swallowed da' clam...But anyways...Looks as if me 'n Chandler's gonna tangle yet again...Well boy, ya' claim to be some type bad ass 'er somethin' well dun, I's gonna show ya' what bein' a bad ass is all 'bout..ya' think yer somethin' 'cause ya' got that T.V. strap...That belt ain't makin' ya' great...the Heavyweight Championship..Which I have..THAT makes ya' great...'n I am great...The Brotherhood is great...ya' all know by now..that goes hand in hand.. [Houston turns and begins walking towards his dressing room and the camera follows] Houston: Chandler ...me 'n ya' have a lil' history...Ya' were the first person ta' defeat me...ya' pinned my shoulder to 'da mat..cool...ya' had a lil' help from a cupcake tulip, who The Brotherhood ran out of the INWI mind you. But ya' still beat me...but dun I did catch my revenge...showin' just how great I am..I whooped ya' in The Ringmaster tournament finals...'n it was my second match of the night..ya' had one match..so that just prove my point...I's great...and Chandler I'm the champion, 'n come the end of the night..I'm gonna be $25,000 richer... [Camera fades as Houston enters his locker room and slams the door shut, showing some unusual emotion, though not revealing too much of what he has on his mind.] [The letters I---N---W---I pop on the screen one by one. Then they explode as the logo of Monday Night Hot Spot comes onto the screen. The music "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne starts to crank and the arena goes black. The INWI-Tron pops on and the beginning of Hot Spot starts.... [It starts by showing a close up of INWI Champion Tony Houston with the INWI belt over one shoulder and the Ringmaster Belt over the other. He just smiles at the camera. It then shows King Apples nailing a tomb swirly on Ed Rogers. It shows Springer nailing a Springer Cutter on Scoop ropes from the top of the cage. It then shows Springer draped in an INWI flag flexing....] [It shows Sam Adams draped in American flag with Militia behind him and him laughing with the New York Title around his waist. It shows Michael Bane, Michael Bledsoe and Jonathan Hammer in one of the most grueling cage matches in INWI history. It then shows a close up of every championship belt in the INWI.... at different camera angles...] [It then shows Commissioner Jerry DeLuise in an INWI Arena looking over the INWI. Jason Chandler holding up his Television Championship and then the screen starts to show all sorts of pieces from the INWI..... James Pillow nailing a super kick on Rick Brown, Vinny Vitrone in his dog collar match with the "jWo" Icon Pig Pen, Ultimate Perversion and Cloud Eagle going at it with Harvey Thunder and Michael Slater, Bill Bob Woijicowski reading one of his ''stories'' to his nephew Bobby Bob...Cherry nailing a Cherry Bomb...Mr. Ed Rogers staring at Smelly...Tommy Graves nailing a choke slam...David McCarter on the mic...The Asswhupper and Thugs 4 Life standing with their arms crossed.... Solid Gold turning on manager Jimmy Stardom... The Comforters holding up the Tag Team titles, Malice in a match....The All Americans waving American flags...and a final shot of the jWo huddling together....] Then you see Pete Robinson walk past the camera with an American Flag jacket on...the camera follows him and on the back of the jacket you can see "INWI" as Robinson walks into the flames ahead.... then the camera fades and fireworks go off inside the arena and it's time for Hot Spot. The Camera switches to Brian Newark at ringside.] [Newark is wearing an INWI rugby shirt, has his head set on, wears wire rimmed glasses and is ready to go. He flashes the camera a smile and we are ready to go...] INWI Presents Monday Hot Spot!! December 1, 1997 Utica Aud, Utica, NY Brian Newark - WELCOME ladies and gentlemen to Monday Hot Spot. What a card we have for you tonight as each of the Champions in the INWI will go at each other for $25,000!! Also involved in this special seven man tournament are top contenders Springer and Mr. Ed Rogers. There are so many possibilities tonight as to who could face who. This will be an exciting night, so hold onto your seats. Not only that, the tag team titles are on the line and Solid Gold will take on the All Americans in a big tag team match. Tommy Graves will take on David McCarter, Michael Bledsoe will square off against Cherry, Vinny Vitrone will face the Asswhupper and Jim Whitten will make his INWI debut tonight against Jarrid Hoss. Let's get up to the ring for our opening bout of the evening! Match 1 - $25,000 Tournament 1st Round Bill Bob Woijicowski (with Mr. Flamboyant) vs Mr. Ed Rogers (with Smelly) The Light Heavyweight Champion, Bill Bob Woijicowski will now take on the former two time champion, Mr. Ed Rogers in this first match of the tournament that Sam Adams set up when he was Commissioner. This tournament has the makings of being great and this match will start everything off. And there could be some interesting matches in this tournament as THREE members of the Brotherhood are in this and who knows what could happen. We could essentially see the end of the Brotherhood tonight, but I don't think we could be that lucky. Both men are in the ring and Ed is talking with Smelly. This guy is really whacked and what about the accusations that King Apples has the real Smelly? Are they true. Is Ed a doubter? Ed with an obvious size advantage in this match over the reigning Light Heavyweight Champion and that could prove to be a deciding factor. But remember, no titles are on the line in this tournament, just $25,000!!! There's the bell and we are underway! Bill Bob attacks right away takes down Ed with a tackle and then punches at him. This guy shows why he is the rednecks champ, he just loves to brawl! Bill Bob scoops up Rogers and nails a Hurracanrana! Amazing! A quick cover, 1....NO! Ed is out and quickly and Ed rolls over to a corner and cowers and then gets back up. Bill Bob charges and is met with a foot to the gut and Ed with a double arm DDT! Ed bounces off the ropes and nails an elbow and then rubs Bill Bob's face into the mat. Ed is looking like he really wants to win this money. Of course, that could also lead to making Smelly happy if he wins this check. Ed grabs Bill Bob and sends him face first into a turnbuckle and then rubs Smelly into the face of Bill Bob. Ed- "Tell your Brother Apples, I have the REAL SMELLY!" Man, I guess Ed wants a message to be sent there!! Ed throws Bill Bob out of the corner and Bill Bob rolls out of the ring. Ed follows out and Bill Bob kicks him in the mid section. Bill Bob with a DDT on the outside and then he hops to the apron and nails a splash. Bill Bob scoops up Ed and nails a couple of punches and then rolls him into the ring. Both men back into the ring and Bill Bob to the top rope and nails a leg drop, 1.......2......NO! Ed kicks out, remarkable!! Ed and Bill Bob back up and are trading punches. Ed with a short clothesline that puts Bill Bob to the mat. He grabs Bill Bob and sets him on the top rope and is going for it...YES! Ed just connected with the puppet master!! 1.....2......3!!! It's over!! A Brotherhood member has been beaten, but they will yack about the weight difference here. Here is your winner in 13:56, Mr. Ed Rogers!!! Match 2 - $25,000 Tournament First Round "The Ringmaster" Tony Houston (with Mr. Flamboyant) vs Jason "Crusher" Chandler (with Oz) Imagine this...In the very first round of a tournament two men who are main eventers here in the INWI. And both of whom are champions...Tony Houston The Ringmaster and the INWI Heavyweight Champion. Chandler the INWI Television Champion. In fact Chandler has defeated Houston before..He handed the Heavyweight Champion his first defeat here in the INWI, though it had interference of a INWI reject, "Lackluster" Bloom... but a defeat nonetheless...however,Tony Houston defeated Chandler in the finals of The Ringmaster tournament, so it's one win apiece, tonight we are going to see the tie breaker. Chandler is awaiting in the ring for Houston to arrive and as Houston sets on the apron, Chandler rushes and is met with a shoulder block to the midsection... Houston slingshots back in..Blockbuster Neck breaker...Unbelievable move!!! Houston covers..it could be over early..One..two..NO!!! Chandler kicks out...Houston as we've been seeing more of lately, is working over the neck of his opponents, namely tonight the Television champion. Houston pulls up Chandler and fires him into the ropes...and catches Chandler and takes him over with an Overhead the head belly to belly Suplex... Houston covers..One..two..No!! Chandler again with a kick out. Houston pulls up Chandler and locks him in a front face lock..Chandler quickly counters with a Northern Lights Suplex into a pin..One..two..No!! Houston gets his shoulder up..Both men up and Houston charges...Chandler ducks underneath a clothesline and takes Houston down with a clothesline of his own. Chandler pulls up Houston and locks on a Belly to Belly Suplex...That's one of Chandler's favorites...Chandler covers..One..two..t-No!!! Houston rolls his shoulder...Chandler pulls up Houston and drags him to the corner...Chop WOOO!!! <SMACK> another...Chandler whips Houston across the ring and connects with splash onto Houston. Houston comes staggering out of the corner...Chandler bounces off the ropes...Bull-No!!! Houston ducked and Chandler falls to the mat... Houston is still a little stunned but bounces off the ropes and nails a Dropkick to the seated Chandler's face. Houston covers..One..two..t-No!!! This time it's Chandler just barely getting his shoulder up...Houston grabs Chandler by the chin...SNAP!! Reverse Rolling Neck snap by Houston...and he does it again..AH that just sends shivers up the spine...well more pain in Chandler's case...Houston pulls up Chandler and is setting up for a Piledriver...BANG..Houston nailed the Piledriver....and covers...One..two..thr-No!!! Chandler with his foot across the bottom rope...Houston is up and is arguing..."**** You"__Houston to the ref followed by two middle fingers to the ref's face. Well Mr. Houston, certainly deals with his frustration in a different way...Anger Management? Ultimate Perversion? Anyways, Houston pulls up Chandler..he's going for the Too Cold Driver...He scoops Chandler..Chandler slipped off the back...and nails a boot to Houston midsection...doubling him over...Chandler going for the Double Underhook Backbreaker ... Chandler...No!!! Houston breaks his arms free...Houston with a right hand..COLD DROP!!! Houston hit the Cold Drop...Wow we haven't seen that move in a few weeks.... Houston covers...One..two..three!!! He got him....the INWI Heavyweight Champion prevailed over the Television Champion however no titles are on the line....Let's get the official word... Here is your winner in 12:29, advancing to the 2nd Round, the INWI Heavyweight Champion, "The Ringmaster" Tony Houston Match 3 - $25,000 Tournament First Round King Apples (with Mr. Flamboyant) vs Springer (with Dr. Bedpan) Here's a great match in the making. The Springer, a former INWI Heavyweight Champion against King Apples. What is so interesting about this match is the fact that if King Apples happens to win this, he will face his fellow Brotherhood member Tony Houston in the second round. Houston earlier beat Television Champion Jason Chandler. So this match has some exciting possibilities. What would happen if Apples and Houston squared off in the center of the ring. Both men are arguably two of the biggest names in the sport and both hold INWI Titles. I think if by chance these two hook it up, every one in the back will be watching on. But don't look past the Springer. Apples has to first beat the former Champion in this match and I am sure Springer would lover to have $25,000 extra bucks. Springer is already in the ring talking things over with his manager Dr. Bedpan and here comes Apples. He is actually walking talking with Mr. Flamboyant, It is rare for him to walk to the ring as his over sized ego usually likes to be carried. Apples is in the ring and hands his Northeast Title to Flamboyant and there's the bell. These two have only faced once before on a house show earlier this year and Springer won by DQ. So I'm sure Apples wants revenge. Tie up in the middle of the ring and Apples takes control with a headlock. Springer sends Apples into the ropes and both men collide and neither moves an inch. Springer off the ropes, leap frog by Apples and sends Springer flying with a hip toss. Springer gets up shaking his head and they lock up again. Apples again with a headlock, but Springer with an atomic drop and then a quick clothesline and Apples is down!! Apples slowly to his feet shaking his jaw a little. Apples with a wise guy clap of the hands to Springer. And they just stare at each other. Apples attacks with a boot to the midsection, then he grabs Springer and slams him to the mat. An elbow drop by Apples and he has gained control of this match. Apples grabs Springer and nails a sidewalk slam and goes for a quick cover.... not even a one count as Springer was quick to kick out. Apples brings Springer to his feet and goes for a punch and Springer blocks it and nails Apples and staggers him. Springer with a couple more and then nails a drop kick!! Apples bounces into the ropes and Springer sends him over the top with a clothesline and the crowd is going nuts. Man, they love the Springer!! Springer sends his hands in the air and then climbs the top rope and dives on Apples!! OH MAN!!! Springer is now pounding on the Northeast Champion and the crowd is sensing a win for the Springer as he rolls Apples back into the ring. Springer back in and there's a cover, 1.......2.....NO! Apples with a foot on the ropes and I think he saved himself there! Springer brings Apples back up and slaps on a headlock and connects with a bulldog! Springer is getting this house to rock!! Springer off the ropes and nails a leg drop and he is back up and lets out a howl [Whooooo!!]. Springer pulls Apples back up to his feet and Apples with a low blow doubles over Springer. The ref didn't get a good view on that or Apples would of found himself DQ'd for that one. Apples back to his feet and stumbles into a corner trying to get his breath back. Apples shakes it off and Springer is starting to get up and Apples delivers a big boot to Springers head. Apples now grabs Springer and puts him on his shoulder and nails a running powerslam. Apples with a cover, 1.....2....NO! Springer kicks out and Apples starts to punch away at Springer. The former Heavyweight Champ is in trouble now as Apples has total control in this match. Apples with an elbow drop and then scoops up Springer and drops him again with a suplex. Apples flexes to the crowd and then smiles and does a mocking howl to the crowd [Whooooo!]. Apples now kicks at Springer and picks him up again and nails a spinning neck breaker. That is usually the prelude to his finisher and he grabs Springer and sets up for the drop, but Springer blocks it! Apples low blows Springer and the ref missed it again and now Apples gets Springer up..... APPLESAUCE DROP!!! 1.......2........3!!! He got him!! Wow, what a match!! Here is your winner in 15:29, King Apples!!! Match 4 - Debut Match Jim Whitten vs Jarrid Hoss (with Oyster Boy) Well, here's an INWI newcomer, Jim Whitten. And he is coming in with revenge on his mind. Whitten, who we understand goes under the moniker "Crusher" will take on Jarrid Hoss. We know we already have someone under the name of Crusher in the INWI, our Television Champion, Jason Chandler. That could prove to be interesting. Whitten is in the ring now and he looks ready for his debut and he awaits the arrival of Jarrid Hoss. Here comes Hoss and he storms the ring and Whitten jumps him right away. Whitten with a series of kicks to the head and he looks at the crowd and just yelled he was going to end this one quickly. I guess he wants to prove a point here. Whitten scoops up Hoss, sends him into the ropes and nails a clothesline and that was a nasty one. Hoss is one the ground grabbing his neck. Whitten grabs Hoss and quickly executes a snap suplex. Whitten is in the 270 pound range and is quite a big man, but looks like he moves quite well. Whitten with an elbow drop and then a quick cover, 1.....2.....NO! Hoss rolls a shoulder and stays in this match. Whitten with a smirk on is face now as he picks up Hoss and punches him in the face a couple of times. Whitten connects with a spinning neck breaker and then drops a knee to the head of Hoss. Poor Hoss hasn't even had a chance to be on the offensive yet tonight and I have a feeling he may not get onto the offensive. Whitten slowly brings Hoss to his feet and sets up for a piledriver ...NO! Powerbomb by the newcomer. Nicely executed and Whitten is now smiling. He points at Hoss and slashes the throat. I think he's gonna end it now. He brings Hoss up again and is setting up for a...pedigree? OW WOW!! A different version of the pedigree as Whitten landed on his backside and Hoss's face crashed hard into the mat. Whitten calls that the Facelift!! A cover, 1....2.....3!! It's over and Whitten is successful in his debut. Here is your winner in 7:47, Jim Whitten!!! Brian Newark - What a great first hour we just had. And look out in the second hour.... Brotherhood members will collide as Tony Houston will square off against King Apples. Now that one should be one for the ages. And no pay per view here, this match will happen on HOT SPOT... in hour number two!!
