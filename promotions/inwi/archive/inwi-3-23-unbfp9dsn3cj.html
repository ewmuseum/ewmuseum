INWI - Day TWO of the War to Crown our NEW CHAMPIONS!! Brian Newark - HELLO LADIES AND GENTLEMEN!!! We are here at a SOLD OUT Broome County Arena in Binghamton, NY. This is the second of three nights that we will bring you INWI action and we are going to crown all of our champions tomorrow night in Albany!! BUT, if these guys want to make it to Albany tomorrow night then they have to win their matches tonight in Binghamton. Two of our Tag Teams will have to wrestle twice, once in the quarters and then their Semi-Final match up. Remember this as well, both teams that make the finals will walk away with gold. The winner will get the Heavyweight Tag Team Straps and the loser will walk away with the TV Titles. Well, enough of me running my trap, let's go to the ring!! Match 1 - Tag Team Title Tournament The Comforters vs The Freaks Obviously these two teams are ready as they made it to this point. The Freaks are two absolute bruisers and could put a damper on anyone's day. The Comforters, Mattress and Box Spring are two of the three triplets that we have in the INWI. It will be Freak O' Nature and Mattress starting things off. Mattress looks like he is shying away from the bigger man, but the Freak reaches out and grabs him by the throat and choke slams him!! Here comes Box Spring and he too is chokeslammed!! I think the Freaks are having a field day in there as Weiland comes in and it looks like they are moshing it up as they stomp both Comforters. One of them rolls out of the ring and the other nails both of the Freaks with Drop Kicks!! There goes Weiland out of the ring and now the Comforters hit the Sealy Posturepedic... oh MY GOD!!! 1 - 2 - 3!!! WHAT AN UPSET!! Your winners in 9:27.... The Comforters!! Match 2 - Tag Team Title Tournament The Peeler Brothers vs Solid Gold These two are fighting for the chance to meet the Comforters in the Semifinals later this night with the winner going to the finals!! It will be Pear vs Blake Gold starting things off. Gold starts it out with a headlock and executes a perfect running bulldog... and that may leave a mark!! Pear is groggly but Blake Gold is posing to the crowd and Pear rolls him up but only for a two count. Blake tags Bradley and they double team Pear... but here comes Mr. Orange and dives on all three of them!! He drags Pear to their corner and the tag is made!! Here comes Orange vs Bradley and Orange is meeing up the Gold brother!! Blake comes back in but Orange hits a double clothesline!! Blake flies to the floor and Orange puts Bradley on his shoulders. The tag is made... Pear climbs to the top and does a cross body block... WOW!! There's the cover and you could count to 100!! Your winners in 10:32... The Peeler Brothers! Match 7 - U.S. Title Tournament "Hollywood Kid" Jason Star vs Jason "The Crusher" Chandler It's a battle of the Jasons!! Starr, the arrogant vetrean who entered the INWI with hopes of gold, comes up against a stingy Chandler. Starr started out the match with a quick dropkick and drops an elbow to keep Chandler down. Starr, figuring the match is already in hand, starts mouthing off at the huge crowd here in Binghamton. Chandler works his way to his feet and bounces off the opposit ropes and plows Starr out of the ring. Chandler climbs to the top rope and nails Starr with a double ax handle stunning the superstar. Back in the ring they go and Chandler keeps the upper edge and gets a couple of near falls. When Chandler started to argue with the man in charge, Starr pops him with a low blow. Starr then re-gains the upper edge and slams Chandler. A drop kick and a neck breaker later, Starr is in total command of the match. Starr fires Chandler into the ropes and slaps on the sleeper in the middle of the ring. Chandler is having a hard time fighting it off even though Starr doesn't have it locked in that bad. The ref checks the arm... 1...2...3... that's the match. I want to know why this hold seems so effective so fast... Your winner in 13:56, "The Hollywood Kid" Jason Starr.
