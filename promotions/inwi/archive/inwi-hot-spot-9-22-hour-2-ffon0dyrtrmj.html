Match 5 King Apples and "The Ringmaster" Tony Houston (with Mr. Flamboyant) vs Dell Pickle, Jarrid Hoss, Malvolio and John Q. Denver This match... I have no clue why it was signed, but it was none the less. The jWo are in the ring, in their famous fluorescent outfits and this time it's green with a yellow jWo emblem on the front. Apples and Houston are on their way to the ring and they each have a pitcher of beer and are stammering a little. Come on, this is crazy. All four of you. <hiccup> And if we don't beat each of you in under five minutes each, you can be the NEW World Champ and the NEW North American Champ. Joseph Collins - Hello and I am Joseph Collins with an INWI report on the funeral of Jammin' Jimmy Cougar. This place is jam packed to the rafters for his services... just the way Cougar would of liked it. He loved a crowd and gets one final crowd here. Many big names in EWrestling are here, including a bunch of Federation Owners that Jimmy wrestled for. We will show you some highlights of the funeral of one of the greatest EWrestlers this world has seen. [The camera pans the crowds of people lining the streets. [Jason Starr approaches the podium and clears his throat.] Jason Starr: I haven't really talked to anybody since the accident so please bear with me if I get a frog in my throat. A few days ago the sport of professional wrestling lost a great competitor. But more importantly we all lost a friend. I'm not gonna go on and on about all of Jimmy's accomplishments in the ring because frankly he reminded us of them constantly. [The congregation chuckles.] But seriously, I would like to talk about the real Jimmy Cougar or more fittingly Jimmy Stevenson. I became friends with Jim many years ago in the WBL where we decided to try something new, the Glamour Boys. Jim introduced me to my first wife, his cousin Darlene. Darlene and I were married a short time later but it wasn't meant to be. I wanna clear this up because me and Jim used my divorce as an angle in the USIW. Jim never caused or had anything to do with our break up it was just a mutual decision between myself and Darlene. After the break up Jim stood by my side and didn't let it affect our friendship which is something that I thought was very big of him. Now that that's been said, I'd like to talk about the man behind the persona. [Jason clears his throat] Jim wasn't the type of guy who would just live high off the hog at others expense. Jim used to watch rookies in the BWF and take them under his wing to train them into superstars much like he did with me. Myself, Zero Gravity, Hector Reynolds and many others can attest to this. Jim turned a lot of guys careers around and never expected anything in return. Jim used to also spend alot of time visiting sick children in the hospital. I remember right before Jim won the USIW World title we filmed a spot in a Memphis hospital with a wonderful boy named Tim. Jim made that boy's last days with us very special. Sadly Tim passed away a short time later and Jim was heartbroken. Jimmy was the kind of guy who wanted to change the world. I think in his own special way he did just that. One little known fact about Jim was that he was a great practical joker. Many of us fell victim to his pranks on many occasions. But they were always in good taste and received a good laugh. I remember one time we were in Los Angeles and Jim put a little something in Lawrence Buffy's tights. Let's just say he had a hard time in his match with all the itching he was experiencing. [Congregation chuckles as the camera focuses on Lawrence Buffy who smiles as a tear rolls down his cheek.] Jim was also a great family man. Many people don't know this but Jimmy's son James jr is also a fantastic wrestler. He was taught by one of the best and I'm sure someday he will make his father proud. Although I said I wasn't going to talk about Jimmy's career I think this has to be said. I've always referred to myself as the REAL Worlds Champion but I think that title should be retired tonight by the man who earned it. I think twenty four World titles and over twenty secondary titles has got to be a record. That's a record that I will let stand and even though my goal has always been one hundred World titles. I think Jim deserves the honor more than myself. This is something that has to do with me and not Jim but I feel I have to say it. Since Jimmy's death I have really taken a long hard look at myself and my career. I came to the conclusion last night that without Jammin Jimmy, there is no Hollywood Kid. The Glamour Boys together were something special but I don't want to do it without Jim. So after the memorial card I will be retiring and looking after Jim jr. Both of us have lost the most important people in our lives and I think it's best that we work through it together. [Jason pauses as a tear starts to roll down his cheek.] I'm sorry...... I had alot more planned that I wanted to say but..... I just can't do this...... Jimmy, Donna, and..... Rachelle. I loved you all with all my heart and may you all live on..... in our hearts.... forever. [Jason starts to cry as Matt Meyers comes up and escorts him back to his seat.] PRIEST: This concludes our services. Will everyone please rise as the family processions out. We will be leaving directly to the burial site. God Bless you all for coming out to share with this family in their time of bereavement. [Everyone stands as the pall bearers carry the casket behind attendants carrying the dozens of flowers before it. The family follows behind, with James, Jr. and Jason Starr immediately behind the casket. When they get outside, James, Jr. salutes his father's casket as it is being placed inside the hearse.] Joseph Collins - Indeed a touching ceremony and I don't think there wasn't a person in the crowd that didn't have a tear in their eyes. A great careeer shattered down in his prime, Jimmy Cougar will surely be missed by all. Cougars actions in and out of the ring were flamboyant and high priced, he lived it in the fast lane. But he did have his secret life. His personal life that we didn't know much about. Which is good, because Jimmy lived both lives with class. Jimmy was a top notch athelete and even more a top notch person. He will be missed by all but the memories of him will last forever. So, Jimmy, we will say this, just one last time for you. And we know you would say it if you were here, "It's Good To Be Great..."
