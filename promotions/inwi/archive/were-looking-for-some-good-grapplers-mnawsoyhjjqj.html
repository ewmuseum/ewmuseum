We're looking for some good grapplers

5 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Join the INWI.... are you one of those people that don't have the time to do role-plays or read real long results. Well then this is the league for you!! I myself am in a couple leagues and can keep up, but I know many people just want to quickly look at the results and everything else. JOIN THE INWI TODAY!!! The INWI is a revolutionary concept in Email Wrestling. Here is what the INWI will be about an do... 1. No Role-plays are needed. If you want to Role-play then don't send it to all wrestlers, send it to [detail withheld] . These will then be put on the WEB site, which is currently being made. Role-plays will help you, but they can count against you if they are childish stupid ones. As I said, they are not mandatory, but if you do them, do them well and you will be looked at better by the league. 2. CARDS - The match descriptions on the cards will be very short... no more than 20 lines a match. This will tell the winner, how he won and any important details. 2A - Cards2 - There will be 2 to 5 cards a week. Most are house shows with one definitely being a TV Taping. There will be one MAJOR card a month, equal to that of a "PPV." This league is like the "Independent" Circuit of Email Leagues.. we don't usually hit the major arenas, just smaller towns. 3. Titles - There will be six titles available to all wrestlers. They are: Singles: Heavyweight, United States, Television and the Northeast Belt. TAG - Heavyweight and TV tag titles. These belts will be defended at almost every show. The TV titles are mandatory to defend at TV tapings and the Major Card and the Northeast Title is mandatory to defend at all cards in the Northeast US. Tournaments to determine all of these champions are slated to start soon. 4. Wrestlers - You are allowed to have three single wrestlers and one tag team. These wrestlers must all be ORIGINAL... in other words, I don't want any REAL wrestlers. Show some imagination in these wrestlers... heck have them be you if you want!! Any real wrestlers either won't be accepted or will be turned into instant jobbers (which is what I will use real wrestlers for at cards anyways!) And make these wrestlers believable please! 5. What I need from you: If you are going to have a stable and a manager that runs all the wrestler... fill this out: Stable Name: Managers Name: Hell/face? - For Each Single Wrestler, fill this out: Name: Height: Weight: Hometown: Heel/Face: Top 10 moves: Finisher (describe if you use an original name): Describe your wrestler: For your Tag Team, fill this form out: (Each wrestler) Name: Height: Weight: Hometown: heel/face: 5 moves: AS A TEAM: Top 5 moves: Finisher (describe): Description of the team: When you are finished, please email your wrestlers to [detail withheld] As soon as I receive your wrestler, I will email you back confirming it. Thanks!!
