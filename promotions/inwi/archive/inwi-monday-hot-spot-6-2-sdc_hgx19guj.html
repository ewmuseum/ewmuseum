INWI Monday Night Hot Spot!! ======================== Monday, June 2, 1997 The Belvedere, Jefferson, NY =========================================================== [Bill Bob Woijicowski sits on the lockerroom floor with a pondering look upon his face. His rottweilier Jake sits beside him.] BBW - Well Vito, before our little 4 way dance you made alot of comments about me and the Springer. You said you were going to ''Drop" me. Well, I guess that never happened since I am wearing this. [BBW points to his newly won INWI Northeast TV Title.] Well, I guess you know how it feels to be dropped since you are the big loser at the show in Utica. You are just a small time punk trying to become big time. Well, good luck! Ha ha!! [Tiffany Love enters the room.] TL - Hello Bill Bob. BBW - Hello Ms. Love, you liik lovely this evening. TL - Thank you, but now onto some questions. Tell us about your match last night. BBW - Well Ms. Love, I won the match and am the new Champion now. What else can I say. TL - Well, you did only win because of the Springer, didn't you? BBW - I won the match and yes, Springer did play a large part in the match, BUT, I still won and his help was not needed. TL - What about the masked man? BBW - This man better watch himself. He's making to many enamies and it may just jump up and bite him in the ass. Garvey, you seem quite content to lay your insults, well you liked to call me a pup, huh? Well this little pup was more lke my rottweilier and took a nice chunk out of your ass! I always wanted to start my own business and I've decided to start now, her in my hometown. I am now going to start. Garvey, you can now call me Pimp Daddy Woijicowski and I'll call you my bitch. [Woijicowski laughs at what he just said knowing that there is already ONE Pimp Daddy in the INWI....] TL - WOW!!! BBW - Oh, I almost forgot I was in the presence of a lady. I'm sorry Ms. Love, forgive me. TL - No problem, did you have anything else to say? BBW - Yeah, I want to send out a message to Mike "The Ripoff" Quick. You are nothing but a sell out copycat and ****, why don't I just add you to my brothel and make you my second bitch? I may even get medieval on your ass and jam a crowbar where the good lord split ya! Crow my ass, the only crowin' you are gonna be doin' is Crowbarrin'. TL - Well thatnks for that colorful commentary. BBW - ****, there I go again, forgettin' I'm with a beautiful lady once again. I better go before I embarass myself further. Goodnight Tiffany. TL - Thanks Bill Bob, but I still have more to ask of you, if you don't mind. BBW - Well, I guess I can stay for a little while longer. Jake will have to burn a steamer in a few, so we have a couple of minutes. TL - Well, what do you think about the new lot of wrestlers coming into this federation? BBW - Well, all these guys like Animosity, D.D., Sharp, and Tony Houston think they are going to come in here and clean house because they like to refer to the INWI as a federation of bitches. Well, what do have to judge this by? A couple of wannabes and jWo jabronies - who are they? Come and talk to me when you find someone real who will spank your asses all over the ring like the champs Brody Thunder, Mr. Apples or PO Box. The Lone Wolf is the man around here and I have yet to see anyone consisitently beat him or deserve to. The Springer is the only man to beat him and he has my utmost respect. I hope one of you finally have the guts to shut up and get into the ring with the "Lone Wolf" and he'll bite yer ass off like little Jake here "The little pup ghe is," right Garvey? Animosity, you need to quit your cryin,' you are the real bitch of this federation and the only win you have is against one of those jWo bitches, HA, what a friggin' joke! Brody Thunder will chew you up and spit you out like the chaw in my mouth. [BBW spits into the spitoon by his feet on cue...] TL - One last thing, tell us about your match tonight with Gino Rizzotto. BBW - Gino can join my brothel as well as his brother who already has. They can both be my bitches and call me the Grand Daddy Pimp. I'm gonna knock him the **** out and he's gonna get a taste of the "Spur." TL - Thank You...... BBW - Sorry Ms. Love, I still have more to say. TL - OK, go ahead Bill Bob. BBW - Garvey and Quick you better watch yourselves and I want to tell you that Jake is a bit hungary and after the next time I beat your sorry asses I'm going to let him chew on your bloody carcases. [BBW stares into the camera and a tear starts to fall down his cheek....] BBW - I just want to say to my wife who was home last night watching my win with my five year old son, YO ADRIAN, I DID IT!!!! [The camera fades out] ============================================================ Brian Newark - Welcome Ladies and Gentlemen, we have some exciting matches for you tonight and we are LIVE on Sportschannel USA from Jefferson NY for Monday Hot Spot!! What a card we had last night. Bill Bob Woijicowski withheld all the odds against him and won the INWI Northeast Television Title in a 4- way dance! All of the other INWI belts were defended without change and now we have Monday Hot Spot!! Tonight, Brody Thunder is in a non title match and Bill Bob Woijicowski defends his title for the first time! Lets go up to the ring for our opening match! ============================================================ Match 1 Michael Hollywood vs Malvolio of the jWo Michael Hollywood is facing one of the tougher members of the jWo in the Shakspearean Malvolio. Hollywood enters the ring with Harvey Thunder, who last night lost to Mr. Apples in his bid to be the INWI Television Champion. Both Hollywood and Thunder were on the recieving ends of monster like chokeslams from Apples and Apples scored the pinfall win. Theres the bell and this match is underway. Hollywood charges at Malvolio and takes him down with a football tackle and starts to pound on him. Hollywood up with Malvolio and nails a side suplex then comes crashing down with a headbutt. Malvolio is not looking sharp in this match and Hollywood is taking advantage of it. Hollywood scoops up Malvolio sends him into the ropes and clotheslines him to the mat. Hollywood looks like he is going to piledrive Malvolio to the mat...no it's a powerbomb, 1...2...NO!! Malvolio barely kicks out! Hollywood looks irritated and Thunder is on the outside cheering on his best pal. Hollywood off the ropes and gets caught when Malvolio hits hium in the midsection. Oh no, Thunder LOOK OUT!! There's Solid Gold and they are beating on Harvey Thunder!! Hollyood doesn't see it yet, now he does!! He's charging the ropes, but Malvolio catches him in a rollup, and Blake Gold runs and grabs Hollywood's leg and the ref doesn't see him, 1...2...3!! It's over!! The jWo has a win, but it was tainted and you know Hollywood is going to be mad. Hollywood is up and he is charging after Solid Gold. Hollywood catches Blake Gold as Bradley heads into the lockerroom area. A knee to the midsection and Hollywood just DDT'd Blake on the floor! Hollywood drops a leg then scoops him up. Another DDT on Blake oin the floor! Hollywood and Thunder head to the back and Blake is out! Your winner of this match in 10:23, Malvolio of the jWo!!! Match 2 - Northeast TV Championship Bill Bob Woijicowski (Champion) vs Gino Rizzotto Bill Bob Woijicowski is defending the title he won last night against the Brother of the former champion. Woijicowski enters the ring with the new gold around his waist. Woijicowski takes the belt off looks at it and hands it to the ref. This is for that title and Gino Rizzotto attacks as soon as he hands the belt to the ref. Rizzotto lands a clothesline that puts Woijicowski on the mat. Bill Bob is trying to get uo, but Gino kicks him in the ribs to put him on the mat again. Gino scoops up Bill Bob and nails a suplex. Theres a cover, 1...2..NO! Bill Bob kicks out. Gino back up with Woijicowski and sends him into the ropes and nails a diving lariat that sends Bill Bob out of the ring. Gino follows him out and he and his brother Vito, who is on the outside, are beating on Bill Bob. Finally the two go back in the ring and Gino keeps control with a front face lock. Bill Bob reaches up and rakes the eyes of Gino and he lets go of the hold. Bill Bob off the ropes and he dropkicks Rizzotto to the mat. Woijicowski drops a knee then a leg on the fallen Gino and the momentum has changed in this match. Bill Bob sends Gino into the ropes and delivers a spinnin heel kick that drops the bigger man. Bill Bob is starting to gain the adrenilen back and he looks pumped. He walks over to Gino and Gino goes low, doubling up Bill Bob in the process. He sends Bill Bob into the ropes and the ref gets clipped as well. Gino picks up Bill Bob and the ref isn't that bad as he's trying to get up. Wait, Vito is in the ring and Gino is holding Bill Bob. Gino charges with a clothesline, Bill Bob ducks and Vito nails Gino!! Bill Bob is up and hits a superkick that sends Vito over the top rope! Bill Bovb to the tope rope and he leaps on Gino, yes, it's the Spur!! The ref is there, 1...2...3!!! It's over, your winner in 15:23 and still INWI Northeast Television Champion, Bill Bob Woijicowski!! Bill Bob leaves with the belt and Vito and Gino start pushing eachother, but then all of a sudden stop as if someone said something. That's wierd.... MAIN EVENT Match 3 - Non-Title Match "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder vs Animosity Well, the only reason Thunder took this match from what I understand was because he didn't like the way this Animosity was talking about him. The championship committee said no to a title match for the fact that Animosity has yet to prove himself and they could not give him a title shot. Animosity is in the ring waiting for the INWI Heavyweight Champion. Thunder comes through the curtain and he looks ready for this one, but of course when doesn't he look ready? Thunder enters the ring and takes off his belt and lays his cigar next to it on the edge of the match. There's the bell and Thunder attacks the youngster and bodyslams him. Thunder then drops a series of elbows and then a leg. Thunder scoops up Animosity and hits a suplex and then picks him up and slams him. Animosity is on the edge and Thunder scores on a neckbreaker and then a side suplex! Thunder is really focused in this match! Thunder is getting ready...are you kidding, already? Yes!! Thunder just nailed the Thunderbolt! But he's not done, he picks Animosity up again and hits a secon Thunderbolt!! 1...2...3!!! It's over, your winner in 3:10, "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder!! Wait a minute, Thunder is asking for the mic and he gets it. He walks over to Animosity, looks over top of him and he raises the mic... "So tell me Animosity, who's the bitch now?" Thunder drops the mic on Animosity, grabs his belt and cigar and heads to the back. Wow, that was something! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well ladies and gentlemen, that wraps it up from Jefferson and Monday Hot Spot!! Remember to tune in this Thursday for INWI In The Studio!
