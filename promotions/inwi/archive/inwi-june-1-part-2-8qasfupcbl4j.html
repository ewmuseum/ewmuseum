[INWI] June 1, Part 2

2 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
============================================================ Brian Newark - Welcome to the second half of our show tonight and it is great so far! Before we gwt into our tag team championship, lets hear from INWI star, Steven Casper! ============================================================ (Michael Pontiff is in the ring ready for an interview) Pontiff: Ladies and Gentlemen, please welcome once again, a youngster trying to make a name for himself here in the INWI, from New Orleans, Steven Casper!!! (As Casper makes his way down to ringside he is met with a lukewarm response. The crowd just can't seem to give any type of response to this guy. All of a sudden there is a loud reaction from the crowd. From out of nowhere come Solid Gold and they nail Casper from behind. They are just pummelling him with the TV tag team belts. Casper is out cold and Solid Gold walk towards and enter the ring as the medical crew run to help Casper.) Pontiff: Bradley, Blake!!! What was that all about? What did Casper ever do to you guys!?!?!?! Blake: He just attempted to take our interview time!!! Pontiff: This interview time was given to him by the INWI board. Bradley: (Looking at Pontiff with an angry face) We can do this interview without you!!!! (With that Pontiff drops the mic and takes off down the Isle. He helps the medical crew get Casper onto a stretcher and take him back to the locker room) Blake: (Picking up the mic) Thats right!! Get that piece of garbage out of here. You know, Not many people have been taking us seriously as of late. These tag titles around our wastes are not flukes. We earned these titles and we will hold on to them for as long as we want to!!! Bradley: First of all, Lets talk about the Friendship Express. You guys, and I use that term loosely, seem to have a problem with defeat. Why don't you just take it like "men", which I'm not sure you can do, and live with it. If you can't, then try and do something about it. We DARE you!!! Blake: Now lets talk about Jason Starr and Jimmy Couger, The Glamour Boys!!!! You guys thing beating us for these titles will be a walk in the park. If your talking about New York's Central Park in the middle of the night then maybe your right. Bradley: All you guys talk about is how successfull you've been in other federations. We are in the INWI now and any past accomplishments mean nothing here!!!! We are the Television Tag Team Champions!!! You two haven't been able to win any titles yet, so we will let that speak for itself. Blake: You know Brad. We try to bring a little class into this Federation and what does it get us? No respect from anybody!! Well, maybe we will forget about class for a while and start beating respect out of people. Bradley: Yes so watch out INWI because what we have done before will not prepare you for what we are about to do next!!!! (They throw down the mic and exit the ring to the loud boos of the crowd) ============================================================ Match 9 - INWI Tag Team Championship The Terminators (Champions) vs The Freaks The Terminators, who upset then champions the Big Brothers for the belts, face former TV Champions The Freaks tonight for the INWI Tag Team Titles. It looks as though it will be Freak O' Nature and Gunner starting things off tonight and they meet in the center of the ring and tie up. Gunner takes control quickly and sends Freak into the ropes and nails him with a running clothesline. Gunner drops an elbow and tags in Blade. Blade takes over on Frek where his partner left off. Blade up with Freak and drops him hith a snap front facelock. Weiland comes in and breaks that up for the Freaks and then is pushed back by the ref. While that is going on, the Terminators double suplex Freak and then nails a spike piledriver!! There's a cover, the ref jumps over 1...2...NO, Weiland breaks it up again! The ref is now warning Weiland and the Terminators nail another spike piledriver on Freak and he is out cold! Weiland now picks up the ref and throws him through the ropes and goes to save his partner. Clothesline on Blade and one on Gunner! Weiland scoops Gunner and slams him out of the ring and then clotheslines Blade over the top rope as well. Freak is still down, but I think he'll be ok after they get him to the back, at least we hope. Your winners, in 7:46, as a result of a disqualification, and still INWI Tag Team Champions, The Terminators!! Match 10 The Big Brothers vs The Glamour Boys Well, this is an interesting match to say the least. The Big Brothers, who were the INWI Tag Champions before being upset by the Terminators, are looking for their rematch. In their way tonight are "The Hollywood Kid" Jason Starr and Jimmy Cougar - The Glamour Boys. The Glamour Boys are undefeated so far in the INWI as a tag team and are looking to get their hands on some gold. What better than to start your quest for a championship with a win over the former champions? It is going to be Jason Starr and Hunter Big starting things off tonight. Hunter grabs Starr right away and hits a chokeslam that puts Starr on the mat and rolling around in pain. Hunter drops a big leg on the neck of Starr and nearly gets a three count by Cougar came in and broke it up. Hunter tags out to Steven and Steven picks it up where his Brother left off. Starr pokes Steven in the eye and rolls over and tags in Jimmy Cougar. Cougar bounces off the ropes and drops the Big Brother with a drop kick then goes to the top rope. Steven back up and Cougar flys! High cross body block...NO! Steven catches Cougar and goes with the momentum into a powerslam!! He stays on for the cover!! Hunter interceptsd Starr with a clothesline..1...2...3!! It's over! Your winners in a great match in 13:32, the Big Brothers! I think the Terminators better watch out because the Big Brothers want their belts back! At the same time, Cougar and Starr are looking at Solid Gold. This could get exciting! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Next up is the INWI Television Championship. Mr. Apples will be defending against a most unlikely challenger, Harvey Thunder. Thunder has found new life since being under the tutelage of Hollywood. Lets get comments from them. ============================================================ (Tiffany Love is standing in the isle right outside the backstage curtain about to about to conduct an interview) Love: Ladies and gentlemen please welcome tonight's challenger for the INWI Television title, along with Michael Hollywood, Harvey Thunder!!! (The crowd erupts at the two enter the ring) Love: First I'd like to get some comments from Harvey about his match tonight. Hollywood: Please Ms. Love, Allow me to speak first. First of all, I would like to apologize to you for my actions towards you when I first entered the INWI. There are a lot of wrestlers in this federation with big mouths. I know because I used to be one of them. This man standing beside me helped me to see the light. Now I will return the favor by watching his back during his matches. Love: Thank you Mr. Hollywood. You really do seem to have changed. Now Harvey what chance do you think you have of defeating Mr. Apples for the title tonight? Thunder: Mikey says I can do it. He is my hero. I believe him. (Thunder sticks his thumb in his mouth) Love: OK. Do you have any other comments? (Thunder just stares at Love and continues to suck his thumb) Love: Michael, Since I have you out here maybe I can get your feedback on the recent actions of your former friends, Solid Gold. Hollywood: First of all, Solid Gold were never my friends. We had the same manager and the same philosophy but I could never totally trust them and now everybody can see why. I guess they proved that they didn't need me to win matches for them. As long as they have Jimmy Stardom wielding a chair instead, they could go on winning. Love: What do you think of their attack on Steven Casper? Hollywood: I think that was really stupid. With all the enemies they have in this federation, why would they want one more. I've been watching this kid in the ring. He may not have a great record right now but he has heart. I don't think he will let the Gold's live this one down if and when he returns. Love: And how do you feel about the challenge that Solid Gold has put out to you two?? Hollywood: Right now Harvey and I are concentrating on our singles careers. He has a big title match tonight. But if sometime down the line, The INWI championship committee thinks we deserve a shot at Solid Gold, We will gladly take it. (Thunder takes his thumb out of his mouth and starts to speak) Thunder: I like Television. Mikey says I can have a belt with a TV on it!!!! (Jumping up and down) Love: Yes, Well you will get your chance very soon. Well, It looks like we are ready for our next match. That you very much for talking with me. Hollywood: No problem Ms. Love. Love: Let's get back to the ring!!! ============================================================ Match 11 - INWI Television Championship Mr. Apples (Champion) vs Harvey Thunder Harvey Thunder, who has had a series of impressive winss ever since getting together with Michael Hollywood, faces his toughest match to date, a shot at the INWI TV Championship and Mr. Apples. Thunder enters the ring and Apples is waiting for him. The two men come to the center of the ring and Thunder extends his hand, but looks scared. Apples shakes it and theres the bell. The two lock up in the center of the ring and Apples overpowers Thunder and scoops him up for a powerslam. Apples picks up Thunder and sends him into the ropes and nails him with a dropkick. Thunder flys out of the ring and into the barrier. Apples follows out and goes after Thunder, but Hollywood gets in his way. Apples and Hollywood are now having a staredown and Hollywood pushes Apples back. Apples stumbles back then grabs Hollywood and chokeslams him on the outside! Thunder sees this and screams and sticks his thumb in his mouth. Apples grabs Thunder and chokeslams him next to Hollywood and then drops a leg on the two of them. Apples grabs Thunder and sends him into the ring and sets him up and drops the Applesauce Drop! 1...2...3!!! It's over! Your winner in 7:42 and still INWI TV Champion, Mr. Apples! Apples leaves the ring, but look at this crap. Solid Gold is out here and they send Hollywood into the ring and prop him up in a corner. Thunder is still out cold and Solid Gold start to pound on him. Hollywood dives on top of Thunder to protect him and Solid Gold just start kicking at him. The crowd is up.... here come the Friendship Express and they chase after Solid Gold saving Thunder and Hollywood. I am sure Hollywood will have something to say about this whole evening! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well ladies and gentlemen, what a card so far! Next up is the big four way dance for the INWI Northeast TV Title! Lets get comments from the Champion, Vito Rizzotto! And then we'll here from the former champion, Mike "The Gavel" Garvey!! ============================================================ Vito: I said I'd take on all comers, and that's what I'm gonna do ! Firestar, I'll admit it: I was lucky on Monday, but the fact still remains: I won ! Woijicowski, I've never faced you before but get ready to be DROPped. And Garvey, one thing I learned early in my old career is that you never attack from behind. Sunday, you're gonna pay ! ============================================================ *Mike Garvey sits in his office in street clothes with a crowbar in his hand* Pontiff: Fans, I'm sitting here with none other than "The Gavel" Mike Garvey...a man who has caused a lot of havoc as of late here in the INWI. Gavel, what comments can you give us regarding your upcoming chance this Sunday at becoming the Northeast TV champ for a second time? Garvey: Pontiff, let me tell you something....I came into this place looking for success and a place to practice justice. And lately, I've had to extract more justice than I'd like to be dishing out. Firestar, you got lucky and beat me for the Northeast TV title....and what did that lead me to do? Extract some justice, that's what I had to do to teach you a lesson. Pontiff: Some HARD justice is more like it. What about Springer and Bob Woijikowski? Garvey: Springer, I'm fed up with your ***. I beat you once, and I'll beat your sorry *** again if you get in my way. As for that Bob punk, I'm fed up with you too. You've suffered the consequences of getting in my way once before, and only a fool like you would dare do it again. I don't know what you two guys think you're gonna do with me...you both show up everywhere you go....you follow each other around like two lost puppy dogs trying to find their momma. What do you two do on the weekends....wash each other's cars? I'm tired of you two. You guys are just going to be pushovers in the four way dance match. After I beat the living snot out of your two sorry *****, I'm gonna kick some whoop*** out of that Vito punk and get my belt back. I'm done with this interview! Pontiff: Wait, just a few more questions... Garvey: Didn't you hear what I said?!?!? I said, THIS INTERVIEW IS OVER! *Garvey smashes the camera with the crowbar* ============================================================ Match 12 - 4 Way Dance for the INWI Northeast TV Title Vito Rizzotto (Champion) vs Bill Bob Woijicowski vs The Springer vs Mike Garvey This should be a fantastic match! Four of the most exciting wrestlers in the INWI and they are matching up here for the Northeast TV Title. Three of the people involved have tasted gold and the fourth has come close on numerous occassions. The rules are simple, once your beat, your out of the match. All four men are in the ring and they brawl until one is the winner. The winner is the Northeast TV Champion. There's the bell and we are underway. Springer attacks Garvey right away and Woijicowski and Rizzotto are going at it. Springer scoops up The Gavel and slams him to the mat then dives on him with a headbutt. Woijicowski up with Rizzotto and drops him with a suplex! Rizzotto looks hurt early in the match and Woijicowski slams him again. Bill Bob walks over and gets a cheap shot in on Garvey as he and Springer are now double teaming on Garvey. Rizzotto stumbles to his feet and leaps at Woijicowski, but Bill Bob turns around and clotheslines him to the mat. Back to the double team they go and Garvey looks rather hurt. Springer up with Garvey in a suplex and Woijicowski climbs to the top and does a bodyblock on him. 1..2..NO! Garvey gets an arm up! Woijicowski looks mad as hell and he walsk over to Rizzotto and takes his aggressions out on him. Springer looks to help Bill Bob, but Rizzotto kicks him in the chest and stumbles him back and small packages Bill Bob. 1..2..NO! Hey, look out! Springer stumbles back as well and Garvey rolls him up and grabs tights! 1...2...3!! Oh my god! Springer has been eliminated in 9:37. And he is hot, but officials take him out of the ring. Bill Bob is over there kicking at Garvey and not letting up. Rizzotto is up and is heading over to the other two, but there's the masked man again!! He has a chair!! WHACK! He nails Rizzotto and takes off in the crowd. Bill Bob sees what happened and goes for the cover, 1...2...3!!! The Champion is eliminated because of the Masked Man at 13:47. Now it's Garvey and Bill Bob. Woijicowski walks over to Garvey and The Gavel goes low and Bill Bob doubles up in pain. Garvey up and he suplex's Woijicowski and then slams him. Garvey is taking over and looks like he is going to regain the title he once held. Woijicowski is looking to mount a comeback, but Garvey is all over him. Garvey sends Bill Bob into the ropes and he collides with the ref. Garvey slaps the ref to try and revive him and then sets up Bill Bob. There's the Final Verdict!! And a cover...no ref!! Garvey is now trying to revive the ref and the Springer sneaks out of the crowd and he has a crowbar!! He just nailed Garvey and then draped Woijicowski over top of him. Springer leaves by the crowd.... there's the ref and the count, 1...2...3!!! It's over!! Your winner and NEW INWI Northeast TV Champion in 20:31, Bill Bob Woijicowski!! MAIN EVENT Match 13 - INWI Heavyweight Championship "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder (Champion) vs Apocalypse "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder faces a tough challenge tonight in Apocalypse, who recently has been on a pretty hot streak. Apocalypse enters the ring ready and you can tell, just by the way he is moving about the ring staring at the Champion, who suprisedly enough came down to the ring first. Theres the bell and Thunder attacks just as it sounds. Thunder is working on the legs of Apocalypse and wants to keep him on the mat. Thunder comes off the ropes and dives at Apocalypse's legs and takes out his knee. Apocalypse crumbles to the mat and Thunder is on him quick as a cat. Thunder applys a spinning toe hold and Apocalypse is screaming in pain and Thunder wants him to give up. Apocalypse won't do it and Thunder gives up the hold. Apocalypse limps to his feet and Thunder attacks again and hits the big man with a clothesline that staggers him. Apocalypse won't go down to easy and Thunder realizes that. Thunder bounces off the ropes again and flys with a knee to the mid section of Apocalypse and again the big man staggers. Thunder once again takes off the ropes and dives with a clothesline and Apocalypse falls hard and I think he is really hurt! Thunder sees the real weakness now and scoops him up and nails a swinging neckbreaker. He then sets up for it, but Apocalypse is so big, YES!! He just nailed the Thunderbolt!!! 1...2...3!!! Your winner in 9:27 and still INWI Heavyweight Champion, "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well ladies and Gentlemen, that's it from Utica! We had a great card here tonight and we have Monday Night Hot Spot!! Tomorrow night! So, for the rest of the INWI, I am Brian Newark saying goodnight!! ============================================================ =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The INWI "The revolutionary force in E-Wrestling" Visit us on the Internet: http://www.catskill.net/harmer For info, E-mail: [detail withheld] INWIŽ, May 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
