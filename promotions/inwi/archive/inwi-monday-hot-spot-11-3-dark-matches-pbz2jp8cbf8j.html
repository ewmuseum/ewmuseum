[INWI] Monday Hot Spot, 11-3, Dark Matches

4 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DARK MATCHES.... Joseph Collins: Hello everyone, I am Joseph Collins and I have Dark Matches to report to you. Before we went on the air tonight, we had nine matches take place and we would like to report them to you. Here are the results. The New York State Championship Sam "The Minuteman" Adams (with Militia) vs Thane MacDuff Sam Adams retained his belt as he, in his own words..."Beat a Scottish Communist". Adams finished off MacDuff at 7:37 with his finisher the "Liberty Bell". This one was all Adams. Adam Kuhl vs "The Big Fan" Devin Terry (with Prince Chuckle Wuckle) Kuhl won this one at the 6:30 mark by DQ as Vinny Vitrone came in and hit Terry over the head with a chair on behalf of Tony Houston. Pete Robinson (with Commissioner DeLuise) vs Pig Pen (of the jWo) This one was no contest as Robinson tried to break Ultimate Perversions record by finishing off Pig Pen at the 59 second mark with his "American Neckbreaker". Jonathan Hammer vs Jarrid Hoss (of the jWo) Most of this match took place outside the ring as Hammer put Hoss through a table and rolled him back in the ring for the cover at the 9:58 mark. "The Knuckle" Jimmy Chooley (with Prince Chuckle Wuckle) vs John Q. Denver (of the jWo): Chooley won this one, despite the antics of Oyster Boy,with his new finisher "The Cannon Ball", which is a cannon ball off the top rope onto his hallen opponent. Chooley won at the 8:27 mark in the match. David McCarter vs Malvolio (of the jWo): The jWo's losing streak continued as McCarter beat malvolio with a figure four 2:52 into the match. Oyster Boy tried to break the hold but McCarter held on and eventually put Oyster Boy into one of his own. Fantastic Surfers (with Prince Chuckle Wuckle) vs Thugs for Life (with Southside Balla) The Thugs took this one to prove their debut win against the Surfers was no fluke. They finished off the Surfers at 18:21 with "Da Jig". The Booger Brothers vs Bonk and Bink (of the jWo) The Booger Brothers Flem and Snot made light of their jWo opponents giving them the "Snot Rocket" at the 11:54 mark. The Freaks vs Dell Pickle and Michael Jones (both of the jWo) The Freaks one this one by DQ as the whole jWo stormed the ring at the 15:97 mark.
