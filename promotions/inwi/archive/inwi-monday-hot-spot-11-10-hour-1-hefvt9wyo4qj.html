You can't even understand a damn word that piece of trash attempts to utter out of his mouth. Tiffiny Love: Well it's getting a bit to deep in here for me. So give whatever last comments you have so I can get out of here. James "Simply The Best" Pillow: You leave when I say you can leave. . .but I wont keep you much longer, quite frankly looking at you is making me almost as sick as I'm going to be when I have to get near Houston. But back on the subject at hand, Houston you assume that you will beat me, you assume that I wont give you any trouble, well Houston ASSUMPTION IS THE MOTHER OF ALL **** UPS! And it's going to be what costs you the INWI Heavyweight Title. .well that and the fact that I'm just a whole lot better then you will ever be. INWI Take a look at your new leader. . . and say goodbye to Tony Houston. Now get the hell out of my locker room you've taken up far to much of my time. Both men are in the center of the ring and staring eye to eye and bumping chest to chest...Pillow says something to Houston, and Houston retaliates with a Internation Sign of Discontent...the middle finger, to the face of Pillow... Pillow locks up with Houston and takes him down with a hip toss...Houston back up quickly and arm drag by Pil--No!!! Houston pulls up Pillow and fires him into the ropes...Pillow ducks under a clothesline and rebounds off the ropes and sends Houston to the outside with a flying forearm... Pillow bounces off the ropes...And misses a baseball slide kick to the outside and is dropped by a hooking clothesline by Houston. Well, Houston is nobody's friend.. unless you are Brother....Houston pulls up Pillow and scoops him and drops him throat first on the security rail..Houston rolls Pillow back into the ring...cover..One..two..No!! Superkick by Pil--No!!
