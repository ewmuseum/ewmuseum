[INWI] Fireworks '97, July 5

5 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The INWI presents... A special tribute to America on her birthday... FIREWORKS '97 LIVE ON THE FOX Network. From the US Air Arena in Landover, MD Saturday, July 5, 1997 ============================================================ [The scene fades from a Maryland State Police officer issuing warnings to those who have ideas of using fireworks at any upcoming social gatherings, and returns to the parking lot area of the US Air Arena, in Landover, Maryland. As a car enters the parking structure an uniformed officer jumps high into the air as somebody has tossed a "jumping jack" at his feet. Vince Coleman?? No it is a harmless practical joke that burns the officers shoe laces.] [Thousands of people begin to file their way into the US Air Arena, home of the Washington Bullets/Wizards and Georgetown Hoyas and tonights is the "vacation" spot of the INWI as they present FIREWORKS '97, surprisingly the security guards have been warned NOT to allow Vince Coleman into the arena, fearing he may take the name of the card a little TOO serious] [Inside the sold out arena people again begin to take full advantage of their first Amendment rights as they display various signs. "Houston Sux" , "Apples is the fruit on MY tree" is a sign held up by a well chested female. Even the area of the arena that has been more and more noticeable over the past weeks..A Sign held up reads Mr. Rogers' Neighborhood..and the crowd begins chanting ROG-ERS ROG-ERS. for the truly "special" athlete of the INWI, Mr. Ed Rogers.] [Brian Newark enters the ring with a microphone and prepares to kick off the show with a bang, but is beat to the punch as somebody throws several firecrackers into the ring and Newark scurries to the sanctuary of his announce table.] Brian Newark - Welcome ladies and gentlemen to Fireworks '97. This will be an annual show LIVE on Fox to celebrate the fourth of July as only the INWI knows how! This will be a tremendous card as we have seven exciting matches for you including FOUR title matches! In the Main Event, "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder will take on "The Most Extreme Man in the INWI" Jonathan Hammer. Also, in a tributing fashion, Mike "The Gavel" Garvey will defend his United States Championship against the man he beat, P.O. Box. The Northeast Championship and the Light Heavyweight Championship will also be up for grabs! Let's go down to ringside for our opening bout... ============================================================ Brian Newark does his regular introduction, but is interrupted by this. ["Under My Voodoo" by Sublime blasts over the P.A. system in the United States Airlines Arena in Landover, Maryland as every fan in the building turns towards the wrestlers' entrance to see who appears, the man who appears is Zack Sharp dressed in a white sleeveless INWI T-shirt and his usual long black and white wrestling trunks. The INWI Northeast Title is slung over his shoulder. He gets a lot of cheers from the fans, but surprisingly a lot of boos.] Zack Sharp: The reason I have come out here is to apologize to you the fans. [Pop!] The reason being, ever since I got word that I would face Brody Thunder for the INWI Heavyweight Title at Heat Wave 1997, I became a changed man. I was a better wrestler, but I had a bad attitude. I was overcome by my ego and my surge to be the best. I totally disrespected everybody from Brody Thunder all the way to the INWI officials. I was basically a pain in the ass. [Pop! A person screaming "Your Damn Right" can be heard.] However, after Heat Wave 1997, I got a phone call from one of my many teachers in the past. He told me how he saw a change in me, he told me he did not see the same man who earned the name "Technician." He gave me a wake up call, he told me to stop threatening other wrestlers, he told me to stop disrespecting everybody especially the fans, and he told me just to stick to one thing and that is being the best I could be. So tonight, I step in the ring with Mr. Ed Rogers, a man who I have threatened and down right put down in the past couple of days. I have been looking over Mr. Ed Rogers as if he were an easy opponent, but he is not, of course he isn't. If he was a pushover then he would have gotten his ass handed to him by the jWo, but he didn't. He beat 10 members of the jWo in under 18 minutes. That is a feat I respect, I respect Mr. Ed Rogers as a wrestler, but I do not show any respect to him when he clocks me in the back of the head with a chair. Mr. Ed Rogers, you want the Northeast Title, but I must prevent you from doing it. I must do it for the fans and I must do it for my self. Ed Rogers, I must beat you, I must beat you in order to pay you back for what you cost me on Sunday night. Payback is what I am aiming for, if it means droping you on your head, stretching your arm, or knocking you out, I will do it because I want payback. Ed Rogers it is just too bad payback........ ...........is a bitch! [Pop! As "Under My Voodoo" plays again and Sharp climbs to all four turnbuckles and salutes the fans before walking back to the locker room.] ============================================================ Match 1 - Light Heavyweight Championship Mr. Cherry (With Mr. Flamboyant) vs Sam "The Minuteman" Adams This match could be awesome! Mr. Cherry, one of the high flyers in the INWI since the beginning, will take on newcomer Sam Adams and his newly won Light Heavyweight Championship. There's the bell and Firework's '97 is underway! Mr. Cherry bounces off the ropes and takes Adams down with a dropkick. Cherry again off the ropes, over Adams, back across, Adams with a leap frog and then he hip tosses Cherry. Adams turns to the crowd and puts his arms in the air as they boo him. Cherry back up, a perfect drop kick to the back of the head and Adams crashes to the outside. Cherry mounts the top rope and he leaps and nails Adams with a body block and both men are down. Cherry staggers to his feet and rolls Adams back into the ring. Cherry is setting up for his Cherry Bomb Moonsault! He leaps, but Adams gets his knees up and Cherry crashes into the knees! Cherry rolls around on the mat in pain and Adams uses the ropes to help himself up. Adams starts to kick at Cherry's midsection now and you know that will take it's toll on Cherry. Adams scoops up Cherry and slams him to the mat and then drops a series of elbows in the mid section. Cherry is trying to figure a way out of the ring, but Adams won't let him out. Adams scoops up Cherry and slams him into the turnbuckle and ties his feet on top. Cherry is wide open. Adams starts to kick in that rib area... takes off to the opposite corner... runs and drop kicks Cherry in the ribs. Cherry is in severe pain! Cherry falls to the mat and Adams drops a knee from the second rope. Cherry needs to either turn this around real soon or quit the match or he may have some serious injuries. Adams grabs Cherry and nails a snap suplex... climbs to the top rope and nails an elbow drop! Adams sets Cherry on the top and he's up there... looks like a reverse DDT... there's the Liberty Bell!! The cover, 1...2...3!! Your winner in 15:35 and still INWI Light Heavyweight Champion, Sam "The Minuteman" Adams!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - We hear that Jammin' Jimmy Cougar want's to speak in the ring... well let's go up to the ring and see what he has to say... ============================================================ Beautiful People by Marilyn Manson blares over the loudspeaker as Jimmy Cougar comes out to the jeering mob of fans. Adam Kuhl is at his side, arguing with a few of the ringside patrons, as Cougar is eyeing a couple of the lovelier ladies at ringside. Cougar already has a mic in his hand, and instructs the ring announcer to vacate the ring. Jimmy Cougar: Kill the music! (music stops) Now don't ever let it be said that Jimmy Cougar doesn't listen to his fans! I've heard that millions of you have written the INWI offices, wondering why my fantastic talents haven't been showcased on this card! Well, I've taken matters into my own hands! Isn't that right, Adam? Adam Kuhl: You said it, Double J! A fan starts berating Kuhl and Kuhl turns to him. Adam Kuhl: Shut your yap before I shut it for you, you four-eyed little freak! Jimmy Cougar: Hey, save it for my opponents, Spree! Now, it seems that no one in the INWI is man enough to take my open contract that I've got set up with the league offices, so I'm out here to make a formal challenge to anyone in the INWI! If any of you so-called superstars have the balls, come out here now, and let me put you in the Cougar Clutch! If you escape within 20 seconds, I will leave the INWI forever!!! Adam Kuhl: Don't Jimmy! Don't do it! Jimmy Cougar whispers something in Kuhl's ear, and Kuhl laughs maniacally. Jimmy Cougar: Come on now! Who will it be? John Q Denver comes down to ringside, and he looks ready for a fight. Kuhl is still laughing, as Cougar starts tapping his elbow. Denver comes to Cougar and Cougar slaps the Cougar Clutch on him. Adam Kuhl checks the arm. Within seconds, Denver is out like a light. Cougar drops him and Kuhl starts beating on him with a baseball bat handed to him by one of the hardcores at ringside. Jimmy Cougar: Come on, INWI! Is this the best you can do? I want some real competition here! Jerrid Hoss comes out next, and reluctantly steps up to Cougar. Double J once again slaps on the Cougar Clutch, and Hoss fights it off for a couple of seconds before finally falling asleep. Cougar drops him as well, and Kuhl starts beating on Hoss with the baseball bat. Jimmy Cougar: This is almost boring! Why doesn't the commish just hand me the title and save Brody Thunder the challenge of losing it to me! Oh wait, what's this? Cougar starts laughing as Dell Pickle makes his way to ringside. He looks ready as Cougar slaps the Cougar Clutch on him. Pickle starts fighting it off. He looks like he's almost ready to break it, when Kuhl nails him with the bat. Cougar then intensifies his grip with the hold and within seconds, Pickle is out. The crowd erupts in a huge ovation, and Cougar raises his hands, thinking it's for him. He turns to acknowledge the cheers and stares right into the face of P.O. Box. Cougar turns as pale as a ghost, and he and Kuhl dart out of the ring. Kuhl isn't quite fast enough, as P.O. grabs him and starts wailing away at him with lefts and rights, but Cougar grabs his legs and pulls him out of the ring before any serious damage could be done. Cougar and Kuhl exit to the jeers of the fans. ============================================================ Match 2 "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston (With Tony Riley) vs Cloud Eagle (With Ultimate Perversion) This match is interesting. Tony Houston and Ultimate Perversion have been going at it recently and Cloud Eagle is teaming up with Perversion lately. Houston enters the ring as normal, all cocky, and he attacks Cloud Eagle. He doesn't even wait for the bell! Houston tackles Eagle and beats him with a series of left and rights! What's this? "Bulldozer" Tony Riley just clotheslined Ultimate Perversion on the outside! Wait, here come INWI officials and they are breaking those two up and sending them to the lockeroom. This is great, it will be a one on one match! Houston is screaming at the INWI officials and turns around and is flattened by a couple of chops by Eagle. Houston on the mat and Cloud Eagle scoops him up and slams him to the mat. Eagle with an elbow and another. Eagle again up with Houston sends him into the ropes and drops him with a leaping elbow. Eagle to the top rope... a jumping knee...yes! It found it's mark and I think Houston is out! Maybe someone will finally beat him and shut him up! Eagle with a cover, 1....2..and...NO! Houston barely gets that shoulder rolled! That was a close one and I even think Eagle is annoyed about that one. Eagle up with Houston and sends him into the ropes and follows with an elbow. Eagle is now landing a series of chops and they are lifting Houston off of his feet! WOW! That is some serious force, Houston is 265 pounds! Houston just poked Eagle into the eyes and he rolls out of the ring to catch his breath! Houston takes Eagle by the legs and slams him down and then drags him into the ring post feet first and Eagle will be singing high soprano for a while! Why is the crowd going nuts? It's "Desire" Brandon Bloom... we saw him last week!! He just nailed Tony Houston and Houston head first into the ring post. Desire kicking at Houston and Houston scurries off to the other side and will you look at that look on his face? He is stunned! But first the decision, your winner in 12:31 as a result of a disqualification, "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston. Houston over the barricades and is in the middle of the fans. Desire in the ring and he has the mic... let's see what he has to say... [Desire is wearing Structure khaki pants, a long sleeve white collared shirt that is unbuttoned exposing his muscular hairy chest. His James Dean facial features and hair are driving the chicks wild. Jen is standing next to her men... she is a tall, slender brunette with a true hour glass shape.... she is wearing bell bottomed hip hugger pants, and a mid drift exposing her pierced navel. Desire takes the mic, and speaks in his low, whispery voice......] DESIRE: For those of you who know me, you know who I am. For those of you that don't, I am "Desire" Brandon Bloom and this is the love of my life, Jen. "Cold & Calculating" Tony Houston, you know me very well. You know the pleasures I seek. Every day, I get pleasure from something new. I love to give it, and I love to receive it. Isn't that right baby? [.... Desire kisses Jen....]...... Tony Houston, I told you that I would track you down, anywhere you go. This feud between us goes way back. This feud between us is far from over, and you cannot escape from it. To be honest, I do not care about the INWI.... my pleasure, the pleasure that I will receive is the knowledge that I have ended your career, your entire existence Tony Houston. Ohhhh yeah, I'm in ecstasy just knowing that I will kick your ass. Now it's time for a line you've heard a thousand times Houston...... JEN: [whispers] Sweet dreams, BITCHES.... Houston is stunned and he flips off Bloom from the crowd. A group of Houston fans have gathered around him and are chanting "C and C, C and C..." Bloom just looks at him and Houston starts to laugh. Tony Riley is in the ring and he just nailed Bloom... Jen scurries out of the ring to safety. Houston back in and they kick on Bloom... VOICE - HEY! Enough of this crap you little wimps. I got history with both of y'all, but hell I'm moving on with my career. (Out of the back steps Mr. Apples, the INWI Television Champion. He has his belt securely around his waist and he holds a mic. The action in the ring stops as Houston and Riley go to one corner and Bloom rolls out of the ring to stand with Jen. All look up the runway.) Apples - You guys hate each other. There's no doubt about it. Hell Bloom, I hate you and you know it. There's nothing stopping me and Houston from kicking your ass like we did in the old days. (Crowd gasps to know that Apples and Houston have a history together.) Now Bloom, I think it's great you have come to the INWI. It's a great federation, BUT.... you gotta watch yourself. You don't have the Fellas here and you're in new territory. You may have the fans behind you, but you know as well as I do.. sometimes you need more than that. (Apples looks down at the ring as if he is going to walk down there and then heads to the back. Bloom and Jen are next to leave and they stare at Houston as they walk up the runway to the back. Houston and Riley look at the crowd and get a chorus of boo's. They flip off the crowd and leave the ring.) Match 3 David McCarter vs James "Simply The Best" Pillow (With Juggernaut) This match is going to be fast and furious. This one is a big match in the hunt for the Light Heavyweight Championship. McCarter was a finalist in the Tournament and Pillow has looked really good so far in his early going of the INWI. Therefore, this match has the expectations of being a great match. McCarter and Pillow to the middle of the ring and they lock up. McCarter takes the early advantage by sending Pillow into the ropes. Pillow off the ropes and McCarter clotheslines him to the mat. McCarter off the ropes himself now and he drops a leg. McCarter goes to the outside and up to the top, but Pillow is up and he just grabbed him and slammed him off the top rope. That is definitely a change in momentum! Pillow on the second rope and drops a knee. Pillow scoops up McCarter, sends him into the ropes and nails a high and powerful drop kick! These two aren't afraid to take this match to the air, that's for sure! Pillow rolls out of the ring and is talking to the Juggernaut. McCarter off the opposite ropes and there's a suicide dive on both of them! Oh my god! That was an amazing move! Pillow is out on the outside and McCarter scoops him up and sends him into the ring railing. McCarter grabs Pillow and rolls him into the ring and he climbs to the top rope! He wants to end this soon! Juggernaut shakes the top rope and McCarter can't keep his balance and it's a nutcracker! Pillow on the top....superplex! Pillow with a cover...handful of tights... 1...2...3! Oh my god, what an upset! How didn't the ref see the Juggernaut. McCarter has a lot of room to complain about this one and I think these two may face each other down the road again sometime... Your winner in 13:42, James "Simply The Best" Pillow. ============================================================ Non Televised Matches * The Springer got "The Big Fan" Devin Terry to submit to the Coil in 6:33 * Jason "The Crusher" Chandler defeated Dell Pickle in 4:21 * "Too Slick" Rick Brown beat Psycho Sal in 8:21 * Ultimate Perversion and Mr. Banana fought to a double DQ when both Mr. Apples and Mike Quick interfered at 10:01 * The Terminators successfully defended their titles against Contrast in 12:44 * The Friendship Express defeated The Comforters by DQ when Solid Gold interfered at 9:27 * Michael Hollywood defeated "The Knuckle" Jimmy Chooley in 5:32 * "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset pinned Harvey Thunder after Solid Gold interfered at 7:33 ============================================================ Match 4 - Northeast Championship "The Technician" Zack Sharp vs Mr. Ed Rogers The champion and the challenger have no love lost for each other, that's for sure. Sharp won the Northeast Title just before Heat Wave '97. Rogers was supposed to get a shot at his title again. Rogers won his match, but it was no longer for the Northeast Championship. Sharp ending up losing his Heavyweight Championship match to Brody Thunder, thanks in part to Mr. Ed Rogers. This match should be really exciting! Rogers is already in the ring and he is ready to go. Sharp enters the ring and Rogers stares down at him. The crowd is going nuts, but that may be because Sharp now has come back to the fans and at the same time Mr. Ed Rogers has a big following now! There's the bell and Rogers attacks right off the bat. Rogers tackles Sharp to the mat and starts to pound on Sharp. Sharp still has his belt on and Rogers grabs it from him and stands up and stares at it. He really wants that gold back. Sharp attacks and he lands a knee to the back of Rogers. The belt falls to the ground and Sharp just nailed a reverse neckbreaker. Sharp is going to take Rogers apart piece by piece if he can, I know he's not happy with him. But hey nobody ever said Rogers was stable and Sharp has something that he was supposed to get a chance at. Sharp sends Rogers into the ropes and nails him with an elbow. Rogers crumbles out of the ring and Sharp follows him out and does an ax handle from the apron. Rogers is reeling and Sharp is possessed. Sharp scoops him up and drops him on the railing. Sharp is sick and I don't think he cares about anything at this moment other than hurting Rogers. The ref is counting still... Sharp just nailed Rogers with a chair. 8...9....10! That's the match. Sharp gets ready to slam Rogers... through a table! he is irate! Officials are out here and they separate this melee and Sharp heads to the back. Here's the official word, At 9:11, both men were counted out of the ring and the referee has declared this match a Draw!!! Match 5 - United States Championship Mike "The Gavel" Garvey vs P.O. Box This should be an interesting re-match. Last weekend Garvey beat P.O. Box for his coveted United States Championship. The INWI immediately set up this re-match for Fireworks '97. Garvey has his second INWI Championship and P.O. Box is looking to get it back. There's the bell and these two come to the center of the ring and just start slugging it out! I think we're in for a good old street fight in this match! P.O. Box just flattened Garvey, picks him up and slams him hard to the mat. P.O. Box grabs Garvey again, sends him to the rope and nails a big boot that sends Garvey over the top rope. Box follows out, but is met by Garvey who whacks him with a chair! Garvey then DDT's Box on the ground and he starts to take over this match. Garvey rolls Box back into the ring and he climbs to the top rope. There's a flying elbow and a cover! 1...2...NO! Box kicks out with plenty of room! Garvey scoops up Box, sends him into the ropes and sends him to the mat with a dropkick. Garvey then grabs Box, picks him up and sends him into the turnbuckle. Garvey hops to the second turnbuckle and starts to pound on Box, but Box drops an inverted atomic drop and Garvey looks like he's in some pain! Box then bounces off the ropes and nails Garvey with a vicious clothesline! Box off the ropes again and he drops a powerful legdrop! Box with a cover, 1..2..NO!! Garvey gets a shoulder up and then rolls out of the ring. Box follows and gets a knee down low. Garvey then rolls back into the ring as does PO Box. Oh my god!! Garvey just nailed the Final Verdict! This match is over! There's a cover, 1...2...WAIT! The ref called for the bell, someone has attacked Mike Garvey! Who is that? OH MY GOD!! IT'S FIRESTAR!! He's back!! Firestar is back!! Firestar is back!! And he's beating on Mike Garvey! Lookout, here comes the Springer and now Firestar and Springer are beating on Garvey. There's the Springer Splash! Springer has Garvey in the Coil! Firestar off the ropes and he drops a leg on the back of Garvey's head. Firestar and Springer than leave the ring as Garvey is just barely moving. The official word is, your winner in 14:33 as a result of a disqualification, Mike "The Gavel" Garvey!! MAIN EVENT Match 6 - Heavyweight Championship "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder vs Jonathan Hammer (With Johnny Danger) Brody Thunder may have his hands full as Jonathan Hammer is the Most Extreme Man in the INWI. He finally gets his shot at a title and it is the BIG on, Brody Thunder and the INWI Heavyweight Championship! Thunder is in the ring as is Hammer. Hammer just grabbed a chair and broke it over his own head. What the hell was that for? Is this guy crazy? Thunder just smirks at Hammer and shakes his head in disbelief. Well, I guess there are more and more Extreme Wrestlers now a days! Whatever floats their boats I guess! Thunder walks around the ring and the two lock up in the middle of the ring. Thunder takes early control with a headlock. Hammer sends Thunder into the ropes and puts his head down but Thunder stops and nails a DDT on Hammer. Hammer is down and Thunder drops and elbow, a knee and then a standing legdrop. Thunder scoops up Hammer and sends him into the turnbuckle. He smacks Hammer's head into the turnbuckle 10 times and stops. Hammer keeps doing it to himself and turns towards Thunder. Thunder takes a step back and again shakes his head in disbelief. Hammer bounces off the ropes and nails Thunder with a diving lariat. Thunder falls to the mat and Hammer starts to pound on him with lefts and rights. Hammer scoops up Thunder and sends him out of the ring. Hammer outside with Thunder and he just sent Thunder into the ring railing. Hammer gets a running start and dives at Thunder but Thunder moves! Hammer crashes head first into that ring railing. I don't care how hard your head is, you're not getting up from that metal ring railing. Thunder takes Hammer and sends him into the ring...sets up...there's the Thunderbolt! 1...2....3!! Your winner in 13:22 and still INWI Heavyweight Champion, "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well, I would like to thank everyone for tuning in tonight to the INWI's Fireworks '97 LIVE on FOX! This was a great way to celebrate America's Birthday and we hope you enjoyed yourselves. So, for the rest of the INWI, I am Brian Newark saying see you at Monday Hot Spot!! ============================================================ =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The INWI "The revolutionary force in E-Wrestling" Visit us on the Internet: http://www.catskill.net/harmer For info, E-mail: [detail withheld] INWIŽ, July 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
