[INWI] Ringmaster House Show, July 29

30 July 1997

INWI House Show - July 29 Scranton, Pa ================================================= Brian Newark - Hello ladies and gentlemen, the INWI held a special House Show tonight in Scranton, Pa. On this card they held the final seven second round matches of the Ringmaster Tournament. The finals are slated to be Sunday, August 24, 1997 at the NEXT INWI PPV!!!! This PPV, The Ringmaster '97 will come to you from the Fleet Center in Boston!! The INWI also had three other matches during the night so let's get the total recap right now!! Ringmaster 2nd Round Mr. Apples (With Mr. Flamboyant) vs P.O. Box For the first time in four months, Apples came to ringside without a belt. P.O. Box, the former U.S. Champion has been off for a bit. Apples took advantage of that and controled most of the match. He stunned the crowd and with the use of the turnbuckle nailed the Applesauce Drop on Box and got the win in 13:48. Ringmaster 2nd Round Bill Bob Woijicowski vs Ultimate Perversion Bill Bob advanced to the third round unopposed when Perversion couldn't show up because he was arrested at Monday Hot Spot!! Ringmaster 2nd Round Springer (With Dr. Bedpan) vs Harvey Thunder (With Michael Slater) It almost seemed as though Harvey Thunder was distracted throughout this match and never mounted a full offense against the Springer. The Springer went to the air at the end of the match and really took Thunder off his feet and got him to give up to the Coil at 12:01. Note that Thunder didn't come out with the TV title because he refused to keep it. Ringmaster 2nd Round "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston vs "The Technician" Zack Sharp This match was all that it was suppossed to be. Both men had numerous chances to win the match, but you knew a match like this wouldn't end clean and it didn't. In what was argueably the best match of the night, Houston advanced to the third round via a countout win when Ed Rogers interfered and Sharp went after him. Time of match: 25:10. Ringmaster 2nd Round Jason "The Crusher" Chandler vs Vito Rizzotto This match was also excellent and the crowd kept pumping throughout the match. Vito Rizzotto, the crowd favorite in this one, looked like he was on his way to a win. However, Apocalypse came out and nailed him with a chair and allowed Chandler to get the pinfall at 13:44. Ringmaster 2nd Round "Marvelous" Mike Quick vs Michael Slater (With Harvey Thunder) This, hands down, was the crowds favorite match of the night. Sportsmanship was shown by both men throughout the match and it was great wrestling. Both men had their chances, but it was over at 17:34 when Quick applied a perfect victory roll for the 1, 2, 3. After the match both men shaked hands and left the ring together. Ringmaster 2nd Round "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder vs "The Icon" Pimp Daddy Brown In one of the most anticipated matches of the night, the INWI champion showed his dominance of the former champion. There were times when it looked like Brown was on his way to victory. Amazing enough, Thunder kicked out of the Pimp-a-plex at the 13 minute mark and Brown was stunned. From there, Thunder took control and won with the Thunderbolt at 16:22. Television Tag Team Championship Solid Gold (With Jimmy Stardom) vs The Terminators (With Johnny Danger) The Terminators, the former tag champions, were trying to get some gold with a win over Solid Gold. The TV Champions, Solid Gold wanted to hear nothing of it. Solid Gold took advantage of many things throughout the match and scored a major victory in the tag team scene when they knocked off the Terminators at 14:57. Tag Team Championship The Comforters (With Dr. Bedpan) vs The Friendship Express The Friendship Express, who have held the TV Tag titles numerous times, were looking to get their hands on the big belts. The Comforters, who just recently won them, had different ideas. These two battled back and forth, but neither team had a serious upper hand. This match ended at the 20 minute mark, a time limit draw. MAIN EVENT Light Heavyweight Championship Sam "The Minuteman" Adams vs David McCarter This was the other match that could of been argueably the best match of the night. These two, who seem to have a serious dislike for one another, went at it tooth and nail in a bloody match. McCarter thought he had won the belt when he thought he was announced as the champ and he had the belt in his hands when the ref signaled that he did not win. Adams struck and rolled him up for the pin at 37:47.
