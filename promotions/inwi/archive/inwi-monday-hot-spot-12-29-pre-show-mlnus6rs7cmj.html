[INWI] Monday Hot Spot, 12-29, Pre-show

30 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
INWI Hot Spot Pre Show Hosted by Joseph Collins December 29, 1997 =========================================================== Joseph Collins - What a response from last nights Pay per view....and we have been given 30 minutes earlier this week so that some of the wrestlers can talk about last night before Hot Spot! Let's hear from these wrestlers NOW! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Jonathan Hammer =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= (The camera opens up on Jonathan Hammer in the locker room after Winter Storm "97". He is sitting on a bench with ice all over his knee!) What we saw tonight was travesty ladies and gentlemen! Bill Bob Whatever, I truly had respect for you. But now you had to go and take away my spotlight. For once it was my time to shine. I've been waiting for my chance to shine in the INWI! My chance to stand up and take my place among the elite in the INWI. Damn it boy tonight was my night. You will pay. The Northeast title will be mine. YES IT WILL. "King" Apples don't worry. I still owe you an ass whooping. If we ever fight you will be my personal bitch in the match. Nobody drops me on my head and gets away with it. Now it seems after getting attacked last night, some stupid ass in the INWI decides to put me and Graves up against The Freaks. What the {bleep}. If I was injured enough not to fight "King" Apples then how in the hell can I fight The Freaks tonight. Did the INWI forget I'm the KING OF EXTREME! I fight anytime. Freaks I'm gonna kick both of your asses because it seems my partner Tommy Graves ain't talking. INWI you've created a monster and only time will tell what I will do! (The camera fades to black.) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Armand Robicheaux =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [Through the INWI curtain come the one and only “The One Bad Stud” Armand Robicheaux, with is mass of long blond hair tied back in a pony-tail as an ice cold stare radiants from his piercing blue eyes to go along with a malevolent whiplash smile on his face, stripped to the waist in a black leather trench coat Crocodile skin pants with Alligator skinned wrestling boots. Cradled in his arm as usual, the simply ravishing femme fatal, Akasha, with her flaming red hair pulled back in a french braid with a pair of Ray-ban sunglasses on her angelic face while dressed for success in a black sequin number with a French cut the side of her leg to her thigh. Armand & Akasha pose like fashion models then head down the aisle and into the ring. Akasha take the house mic from the ring announcer an holds it under Armand’s face. Armand with a low, dusky Cajun accent.] Armand Robicheaux: Well, well, well, so this is the INWI. So this is what its like to wrestle on Monday nights?. . . Not bad, not bad at all. For you fan here tonight and watching this at home your in for a treat. You get to see the Man that will be running rough shot in this organization for the first time. And for all you Idiot Savants in the back, watch whats in store for you when I make a cucumber out of this Dell Pickle! [Akasha slowly strips the black leather jacket off of Armand, showing off the body of a Greek God with a Black Mamba Tattoo on his shoulder blade, and twirls it in the air as “The One Bad Stud” Armand Robicheaux shoots both fists in the air and bows his head.] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Vinny Vitrone =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [The camera shows a shot of Vinny Vitrone after losing the INWI Tag Title at the PPV] "The Vindicator" Vinny Vitrone- Look, I'm in a rush and I bet you people think it's great huh? INWI has just screwed the Brotherhood not once, but TWICE. Ha, I promise revenge, you're all gonna meet a friend of mine, soon. A REAL important friend, he's gonna show you all when you least expect it. We never signed contracts to fight either BBW or The Friendship EXPRESS!! Y'all think it's funny? That's alright. Tomorrow on Hot Spot, I promise you I'll reclaim another title for The Hood, The lightweight title. 'Til then, though. I'm out. . . [CAMERA goes to fade] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Mike Hickden =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= {The Franchise Mike Hickden's theme comes on, then the fireworks, silver fireworks blast off. The Franchise Player walks to the ring. He makes his way to the ring then starts posing to the crowd buffing his muscles up. The Franchise Player then goes to the second turnbuckle making already the raucous crowd a little louder. He jumps off and goes to Tiffany Love} Tiffany Love: Ladies and Gentleman The Franchise Player Mike Hickden. {The fans had mixed reaction on The Franchise Player} Franchise so glad you are wasting your time from training your match against Jarrid Hoss, to take 15 minutes. The Franchise Player: HAHAHAHAHA.. Do you expect that I need to train for a opponent like him. I've done my homework you know, I've seen him wrestle, which isn't "Great wrestling" if you know what I mean. I know I am being a little arrogant but I won't let this nobody beat me. Training might be a good idea for an mediocre wrestler, but am I an "Average" wrestler, I don't think so. Tiffany Love: I see Mike, next question this is what I've been waiting to here, from you, people has been talking about you. In the locker rooms from a "Wrestler" who I can't name right now, said That talking about Brotherhood like that isn't going to get you nowhere, not just cause your a rookie but because you have began INWI in just 2 weeks. What you have to say. The Franchise Player: I disagree. First off, I'm not those wimps that let this Brotherhood stable push me around, I just want to be known and heard of what I think about The Brotherhood. Not only have the feared wrestlers warned me about the Brotherhood but so have the wrestling editors for magazines and broadcast journalist for INWI too. They have called me a premadonna, a pretty boy, and all the other crap. Frankly I just don't care what they think, it does not matter. The "Yellow journalism", is a joke, case close. I mean I can take a few pointers from the media point of view but what they said about me that I won't say since kids are here, just sadden me, of how this sport is going [Hickden throws down the mic and walks to the back] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Malice =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [As Malice is celebrating his victory in the ring with Sapphire one of the INWI's announcer's makes their way to the ring.] Announcer: Malice, I'd like to get a few comments from you if I may Malice: Make it quick I have parties to hit. Announcer: First off congratulations on the victory tonight. Do you have any comments on what took place with Riley and Scoop in the 6 man match tonight? Malice: What 6 man match? Two of those so called "men" were nothing but punks. The way I see it they can't win if the stakes are even, they have more then proven that. Rick Brown, my hat's off to you, you put up one hell of a fight. Fate brought our meeting to this point. You have become another stepping stone on my path of destruction, but don't look down upon yourself for losing, for many have lost to me and many more will. Now we have this N.E.W organization talking trash about INWI. Step on down boys and back up the talk, cause this isn't where the "big boys" play. This is where the MEN play. And talk is cheap, so either put up or shut up. Now giving it is the holiday season I have a couple packages in back for Scoop and Riley. Announcer: Did I hear right?!? You have gifts for Scoop and Riley? Malice: Yeah thats right, cue up the big screen. [The large video screen comes on showing Riley and Scoop's locker room. Someone knocks on the door as Scoop answers he receives to wrapped packages. Upon opening the packages Scoop and Riley look inside to see lifelike dummy heads of themselves!] [Malice into the mic] Merry Christmas boys, enjoy it while you can because it just may be your last. =========================================================== =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The INWI™ "The revolutionary force in E-Wrestling" Visit us on the Internet: http://www.catskill.net/harmer For info, E-mail: [detail withheld] INWI®™, December 1997 All material is copyrighted to the INWI and is NOT to be reproduced without the expressed written Consent of the INWI. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
