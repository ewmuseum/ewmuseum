INWI INSIDE THE ROPES Sunday, July 6, 1997 ================================================== Brian Newark - Welcome ladies and gentlemen to Inside The Ropes. As always, I am Brian Newark along with Scoop Ropes coming to you LIVE!! We are in Washington DC, after celebrating the Fourth of July weekend with Fireworks '97! And what a card that was...
