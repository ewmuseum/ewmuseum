Match 5 Michael Slater (with Harvey Thunder) vs Cloud Eagle (with Ultimate Perversion) Well, to say the least, these two hate each other. And they have gone back and forth beating the living hell out of each other. And their two partners... Ultimate Perversion and Harvey Thunder. Well, let's just say that makes an interesting four man fight. There's the bell and we are underway. Cloud Eagle tackles Slater and starts to pound on the face. I guess that just through wrestling out the window. Eagle off the ropes and drops a leg and a quick cover, 1....2....NO! Slater kicks out. Cloud picks up Slater and slaps on a headlock. Slater sends Eagle into the ropes and nails an fore arm that stumbles Eagle. Slater sends Eagle to the mat with a clothesline and then drops a knee. These two just want to pound the hell out of each other. I don't think they really care who wins or loses, rather they would rather seriously injure the other wrestler. Slater scoops up Eagle and sets up for a DDT... NO! Eagle sends Slater over the top rope with a back body drop. Eagle goes to the outside and grabs Slater and sends him into the barrier and follows that up with a knee. Eagle grabs Slater and sends him head first into the ring post. Wait...here comes Harvey Thunder and he is cut off by Ultimate Perversion and they are brawling on the outside. Thunder slams Perversion's head into the ring barrier and then starts to kick at him. Wait, the referee is calling for a bell and what has happened? This could be interesting. The referee has counted both men out of the ring at 5:47 and this match is a draw!! But the barawl is still going on at ringside! Slater and Cloud Eagle are just beating on each other and Thunder and Perversion are making their way to ringside. They are all brawling now and INWI Officials are out there. They are trying to break it up, but both sides are not willing to let go. Thunder has Perversion and sends him into the ring post. Eagle charges at Thunder and sends him into the barriers with a shoulder block. Now the officials are between these two groups and they have finally seperated them. This is crazy stuff. These four really hate each other and there is no telling if they will ever get away from these beatings. Match 6 Bodyguard Jones vs Pete Robinson (with Commissioner DeLuise) Pete Robinson is getting ready for his match against General Samuel Adams at the pay per view. Robinson has the weight of the INWI on his shoulders as if he loses and Sam Adams keeps the New York Title, then Adams becomes the INWI Commissioner for a week. Robinson faces Bodyguard Jones here and this match is underway. Robinson and Jones tie up and Jones grabs a headlock and Robinson lifts him up and nails a belly to back suplex. Robinson scoops up Jones, sends him into the ropes and grabs Jones in a bearhug. Jones is waving but he won't give it up. Jones reaching back and he nails an elbow in the face of Robinson. Jones bounces off the ropes and floors Robinson with a clothesline. Jones with an elbow drop and he is starting to take over the match. Jones grabs Robinson and sets up for a suplex. Robinson blocks it...and blocks it again. Robinson reverses it and gets Jones over. Robinson back to his feet and grabs Jones and sends him into the ropes and there's a knee lift that sends Jones to the mat. Robinson grabs Jones again and nails a swinging neckbreaker. Robinson is setting up that neck for his finisher. Robinson grabs Jones, brings him up and sets up for a piledriver and connects with it. A cover by Robinson, 1......2.....NO! Jones somehow rolled a shoulder and Robinson looks amazed. Robinson signals to the crowd and sets up....YES!! There's the American Neckbreaker!! 1.......2.......3!!!! Robinson got him and this is getting interesting for this weekend. Wait a minute.... from the crowd. IT'S MILITIA!!! He slides in the ring and attacks Robinson and here comes Sam Adams from the back and he clotheslines COmmissioner DeLuise!! Whoa, there's a definite fine!! Adams in the ring and they are double teaming Robinson. This isn't right. Wait till this weekend already!
