[INWI] House Show, 6-21

21 June 1997

INWI House Show - June 21 Montgomery C.C. Germantown, MD ================================================= Joseph Collins - Hello ladies and gentlemen and we have another house show to report to you, which includes the final four matches in the opening round of the Ringmaster Tournament! We had a great card at the Montgomery County College Campus in Germantown and let's get the results! Match 1 - Ringmaster Tournament - First Round "Marvelous" Mike Quick vs "Hollywood Kid" Jason Starr This has to definitley be one of the best matches of this tournament. Both wrestlers took some risks in this match and in the end it worked against one of them. Jason Starr went to the top and went for a double ax handle, but Quick drop kicked him to the mat. Quick followed with a surprise Quick Ending and an upset win over Starr in 19:35. Match 2 - Ringmaster Tournament - First Round Awesome X vs Vito Rizzotto This match featured some high flying moves from Awesome X and some power moves from Vito Rizzotto. In the end, Gino Rizzotto stood in the walkway trying to distract Vito, but Vito finished off Awesome X with a Mob Drop at 11:21 for the pin. Vito then flipped off Gino and the crowd started chanting "Vito...Vito..." Gino exited through the curtain. Match 3 - Ringmaster Tournament - First Round Mr. Ed Rogers vs Jimmy Cougar Ed Rogers, still caught up in what has been happening with himself, Smelly and the jWo didn't seem to focused on this match. Rogers did scare Cougar early by getting several near falls, but Cougar got the upper hand and won the match with a cradle roll up at 11:57. Match 4 - Ringmaster Tournament - First Round Bill Bob Woijicowski vs Steven Casper Steven Casper showed the Northeast TV Champion why he should be a contender, but Woijicowski showed him why he is a champion. Woijicowski beat on Casper from midway through the match until the end when he got the pin at 13:21 following the Spur. Match 5 Bodyguard Jones vs Raver Bodyguard Jones is getting ready for his match against Psycho Sal at this upcoming pay-per-view with a big win over Raver. Jones won the match at 9:32 by pinfall. Match 6 - TV Tag Titles Friendship Express vs Solid Gold This match almost crowned new champions, but late interference caused a total war and a no contest. Solid Gold looked like they were ready to win the belts, or at least set up to win when The Glamour Boys stormed the ring and attacked both teams. Soon after the Freaks entered the brawl and the ref called the match a no contest. Match 7 - Northeast Championship Dell Pickle vs Mr. Cherry This was another normal title defense by Dell Pickle. Pickle was getting creamed by Mr. Cherry when he high tailed it out of the ring. Cherry followed him out, sent him in and nailed the Cherry Bomb moonsault and was seconds from the championship. Then the rest of the jWo attacked and Pickle was DQ'd. Cherry won the match in 8:31, but Pickle retains the belt. Match 8 D.D. (Doctor of Disaster) vs Senator Bradley D.D. came in rather mad about getting eliminated from the Light Heavyweight Tournament and took all his aggressions out on Senator Bradley. D.D. won the match via submission with the Defibulator at 5:47. Match 9 - United States Championship P.O. Box vs Springer The Springer was going for his second title here in the INWI when Mike "The Gavel" garvey interfered. Garvey rapped Springer with a crowbar and P.O. Box hit the final delivery for the pin and the win at 12:29. Garvey is slated to meet Box at the PPV. MAIN EVENT Match 10 - Tag Team Championship The Terminators vs The Booger Brothers The Booger Brothers gave it all they had and several times came within a slap of the hand to winning the INWI Tag Team Championship. The Terminators, gearing up for their big defense with the Big Brothers, took over and claimed the win at 11:01. ============================================================ Joseph Collins - Well that was a great card! We will be back tomorrow night with Inside The Ropes so join us then!
