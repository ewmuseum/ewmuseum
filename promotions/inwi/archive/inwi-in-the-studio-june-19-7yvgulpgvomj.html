[INWI] In The Studio, June 19

19 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
INWI - IN THE STUDIO June 12,1997 INWI Studios, Delhi, NY ============================================================ [The camera opens with a wide shot of blue skies, billowy clouds roaming across them. The picturesque view in interrupted by a loud thud. Then another thud. And another. The camera pans down as we see Brody Thunder driving a fencepost into the ground. He's wearing blue jeans and black hat.Clenched in his teeth is his trademark cigar. Sweat covers his body as he brings down his sledgehammer again. Thud. He lowers the hammer. smiles at the post,then looks into the camera, still smiling.] Y'know sometime's things jus' don't add up in this business. Now..lemme see if I got this straight. I do an interview sayin' I like Tony Houston's style. I say he reminds me o' myself when I was a pup. I also say that what he did ta DeLuise was wrong,but that's life. An' I also told him that if he thought that Brody Thunder was as easy ta beat as DeLuise,he'd be makin' the mistake o' his career. So what does ol' "Cold an' Calculatin'" do? [Thunder swings the hammer onto the fencepost. Thud.] He volunteers his bagman fer dental work. "Cold an' Calculatin'",Houston? Hmph..more like "Scared an' Cowardly". What's the matter, Houston? Didja figger out that ya can't back up yer mouth? Ya come inta my backyard an' wanna put the badmouth on me? Didja think I'd jus' ignore yer insults and yer challenge? Well I guess we know it weren't rocket science ya studied, now don't we. [Again the hammer falls. Thud.] Ya flap yer gums bout bein' in feds that I was never accepted to. [Thunder sets the hammer on it's head and rests his arms on it.] Yer right. They didn't accept me. But tell the whole truth, m'man. The plain truth is.....they were scared o' the Wolf. They all knew I weren't gonna be no "company champion". They knew they wouldn't be able ta control an' manipulate me like they do all o' their jabronies...like you an' 'Dozer. So don't try an' build yerself up ta be some all-fire "superstar". Ya weren't nuthin' but a big fish in a small pond before. But now....yer in with the big dogs. Ya came knockin' on _my_ door,friend. An' I'm the one who'll put yer ass in a sling an' not break a sweat doin' it. Me versus Riley? [He swings the hammer once again onto the post,driving it down a couple o' inches. Thud.] Sure. I'll be there. But I want this stipulation. When I pinned his tail ta that mat fer three...I want a match with _you_,Houston. Plain an' simple. Ya can talk the talk,my friend... ...but can ya walk the walk? [Thud.] Y'see it's gonna take more'n some punk with a limited vocabulary o' cuss words an' an IQ lower than his waistline ta beat me,son. I've said it before an' I'll say it again. The only man I ever feared is buried next ta my momma an' my equal ain't been born yet. Don't believe me,Houston? [Thud. Thunder readies the hammer again over his head but pauses to glare into the camera.] Try me. [Thud.] I said there ain't a man in the INWI that can beat me. An' someday...someone might prove me wrong...but I do know this much, Houston... [Thunder stops pounding the post.] ...it won't be_you_. Not now. [Thunder brings the hammer down again. Thud. He stops and looks at the camera.] Not _ever_. Monday night I'm puttin' yer lackey on the shelf, amigo,an' then I'm comin' gunnin' fer you. Run if ya want. Hide if ya must. But sooner or later... [Thunder now stares directly into the camera and clenches his teeth.] ...I'm gonna mail yer sorry ass back ta whatever backwater federation ya came from. An' that's a fact. I'll see _you_ Monday night, Houston. Bring yer flunkie. Bring yer schoolyard attitude an' bring whatever cuss words ya can think o'. Yer gonna need 'em. An' me? I'll be bringin' what yer stooge really needs... [The hammer falls once more onto the rugged fencepost. Thud.] ...the asskickin' o' his life. See ya Monday night,boys. Don't be late now,y'hear? [Thunder picks the hammer up over his head. As he begins to swing it downward,the camera pans up to the skies. Thud. The screen goes abruptly black. Thud........thud.........thud....] ============================================================ Brian Newark - Brody Thunder has accepted the challenge from Tony Houston and will take on "Bulldozer" Tony Riley this Monday Night on Monday Hot Spot!! But, what about Thunder's challenge. Is this anything you can do, I can do better? Thunder could be in for some fun times over the next couple of weeks. Well onto business today, it's IN The Studio!! The Light Heavyweight Championship Tournament is underway and we have had some great action so far! We have two more matches in that tournament, A match in the Ringmaster Tournament and Mr. Apples defends his title in the Main Event against "The Icon" Pimp Daddy Brown!!!!! Let's get up to the ring for our opening match! Match 1 - Light Heavyweight Championship Tournament Sam "The Minuteman" Adams vs "The Knuckle" Jimmy Chooley Sam Adams, who is trying to rid the USA of all the ''commies'' as he likes to call them, is looking to advance in the Light Heavyweight Championship Tournament. Adams rushes the ring and attacks Chooley right from the get go and flattens him with a diving lariet. Adams scoops up Chooley and hits a snap suplex! Chooley is already looking hurt in the middle of the ring. Adams goes off the ropes and hits a leg drop and then picks Chooley up just to nails a belly to belly suplex! Adams is dominating this match! Adams again picks up Chooley and sends him into the ropes and executes a perfect drop kick that sends Chooley out of the ring. Adams follows and grabs Chooley and sends him into the ring post. Adams rolls Chooley back into the rinf and goes to the second rope....and hits an elbow! Adams looks like he is getting ready, yes!! There's the Liberty Bell! 1...2....3!!! It's over! Your winner in 5:21, Sam "The Minuteman" Adams! Adams has advanced to the semifinals of the tournament! Match 2 - Ringmaster First Round Ultimate Perversion vs "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset Boy this match brings back memories! This is the match that was the finals of the Northeast Championship back in March, of course won by Sunset in a stunning upset. What a different way these two have gone since then. Sunset has gone down, while Perversion, in many peoples eyes, is a champion without a belt. Perversion has had his chances, but with the interference of his former stabelmates and now Tony Houston, Perversion just hasn't been able to win any gold. Now Perversion has a chance to show the critics and he opens up the Ringmaster with Sunset. Perversion and Sunset have been going at it and Sunset has the upper hand. Sunset scoops up Perversion, sends him into the ropes and goes for the back body drop...no! Perversion puts on the brakes and hits a swinging necbreaker! Perversion, who is now beeing actually cheered by the fans, is trying to swing the momentum. Perversion sends Sunset into the ropes...belly to belly suplex! Perversion gets back up and grabs Sunset and powerslams him! There's a cover but no leg hook, 1...2....3!! Oh my, Sunset couldn't kick out! Your winner in 11:59, Ultimate Perversion! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Next up is more quarterfinal action of the INWI Light Heavyweight Championship Tournament. Let's get comments from one of the hot newcomers, David McCarter! ============================================================ *The camera opens up on a dusty old wrestling ring. David McCarter is stretching himself near the ropes when his sparing partner steps into the ring. He jumps up and down a bit , some dust rises off the canvas. They lock up and McCarter turns into a headlock, the partner pushes him into the ropes but McCarter reverses and nails the sparing partner with a bely to belly on the fly. The dust flies off the ring again with the impact. McCarter then scoops the partner into his arms, and leaps up and down into a side suplex. He quickly pulls himself to the 2nd turnbuckle and nails him with an elbow and ocvers for three. He then turns to the camera* [McCarter] Well it seems you INWI chaps seem to sneak up on me at any moment. Welcome to my gym it's not much but it's a nice little place to train. *McCarter steps out of the ring, shakes hands with the sparing partner and towels himself down.* [McCarter] Well now, it seems I've been entered into the Light Heavyweight title tournament. And what a way to kick off my career in wrestleing by locking up a piece of gold. And there is probably no finer piece of gold than that brand spaking new Light Heavyweight title. Well no secrets here chaps, I'm going for the win and my Sleeper Hold is probably one of the very best. Simple and effective but you D.D..... You will be the second in my line to that title. I hope you come prepared for your match.....cause I'm coming at you non stop until I win that belt. *McCarter stpes back into the ring, and wrestles some more with his sparing partner* ============================================================ Match 3 - Light Heavyweight Championship Tournament David McCarter vs D.D. (Doctor of Disaster) McCarter is an excellent compeditor thus far and he definitley has the talent to win this belt! McCarter enters the ring and there's the bell and we are underway. D.D. attacks right away and takes McCarter down with a tackle. D.D. hops on top and starts to rub McCarters face into the mat. D.D. picks up McCarter, who, just got his ring coat off, and slams him to the mat. D.D. outside and up to the top....he leaps, a flying leg drop! 1...2...NO! McCarter gets his shoulder up! D.D. grabs McCarter and nails a side suplex. He takes McCarter and sets him up on the top and he is looking for a superplex. Wait a minute! McCarter has blocked it! He just tossed D.D. off the top rope face first! McCarter stand up and splaches D.D. on the mat! The crowd is jumping now as both wrestlers are taking chances! McCarter with D.D., sends him into the ropes, drop kick and D.D. falls over the top rope! McCarter is climbing to the top rope! He leaps, D.D. moves and McCarter goes face first into the ring rails and he looks out cold! D.D. moves in and drops a couple of knees to the back and McCarter is busted open from landing on the rail. D.D. tosses McCarter back in the ring and goes to the second rope where he nails a knee. D.D. thinks he has this match won and he is jawing with the crowd. McCarter is moving a little, but I think he is done. D.D. comes back over and goes to scoop McCarter. Wait, there's a rollu up! Small package! 1...2...3!! Yes!! McCarter rolls out of the ring and D.D. is hot! Your winner in 14:35, David McCarter!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - What a great match that was! A bloodied David McCarter advances in the Light Heavyweight Tournament! Up next is a great main event with Mr. Apples and Pimp Daddy Brown facing off for Apples TV Championship. Before that though we have a special announcement from Commissioner Jerry DeLuise. Lets go to the commissioner... ============================================================ [Commissioner Jerry DeLuise is sitting at his desk as the camera moves in on him. He looks serious and is still wearing a neck brace from the attack that Tony Houston put on him last week.] DeLuise - Due to the recent activities and everything surrounding all of this outside interference and myself as the commissioner feeling unsafe, I have contacted a long time friend of mine about possibly coming into the federation on a par time basis as a bodyguard of sorts for me and the federation. You fans will know him once you see him and he, along with his manager will help the INWI in it's quest to become the elite in E-Feds today. This person will not only enter to help myself, but as a wrestler. He is a world class compeditor and I am sure everyone will enjoy him when he shows up. Back to you Brian. ============================================================ MAIN EVENT INWI Television Championship Mr. Apples vs "The Icon" Pimp Daddy Brown What a great match this is going to be. The Icon, the first ever INWI Heavyweight Champion, is taking on the longest reigning champion in the INWI, TV Champion, Mr. Apples. How this match was signed is beyond me, but you can see the mutal respect that these two have for eachother. Both men come to the middle of the ring and lock up. Apples shows his superior power by slapping a headlock on Brown. Brown sends Apples into the ropes, but Apples puts Brown on his back with a shoulder block. Apples picks up Brown and nails a bodyslam and then a leg drop! What power that had behind it! Apples again goes to grab Brown but Brown pokes Apples in the eyes and the big man stumbles backwards. Brown up and DDT's Apples to the mat! Brown grabs Apples and sends him into the ropes and puts him to the mat with a flying knee. Apples is on his back and Brown bounces off the ropes and drops a big knee. There's a cover, 1...2...NO! Apples kicks out! I thought we had a new champion! Brown slaps on the mat showing his disgust and then grabs Apples again and hits a swinging neckbreaker. Brown picks up Apples and sends him into the ropes and drop kicks him and Apples flys backwards into the ref! Apples is out! The Ref is out! This is crazy! Pimp Daddy Brown back to his feet and he signals to the crowd, he scoops Apples and sets up! Yes there's the Pimp-a-plex! He's got the pin but the ref is out! He's way past a three count! Wait, here comes the Masked Man! This is getting worse for Apples!! The Masked Man to the top rope.... OH MY GOD! He just crushed Pimp Daddy Brown from the top while he still had the Pimp-a-plex on! Why is he saving Apples, he has been screwing him the last couple of weeks! He bolts through the crowd and Apples staggers to his feet and sees the situation and just collapses on top of Brown. The ref sees this and makes the count, 1.....2......3!!! Yes!! It's over! What an absolute classic match! Your winner in 23:46 and still INWI TV Champion, Mr. Apples!!!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - What a match!! Why did that Masked Man save Apples though? And what is this surprise Jerry DeLuise has in store for the INWI? What about the Light Heavyweight Championship Tournament? So far it's excellent! We are out of time though, so we'll see you this weekend for Inside The Ropes! ============================================================ =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The INWI "The revolutionary force in E-Wrestling" Visit us on the Internet: http://www.catskill.net/harmer For info, E-mail: [detail withheld] INWI®, June 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
