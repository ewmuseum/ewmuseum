INWI Utica 3/18

22 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
INWI - "We are here and we're not taking any prisoners!!" LIVE from the Utica Aud in Utica, NY!! Ben Lamson - Hello everyone, I am Ben Lamson and I will be bringing you all of the other Tournaments that are not in the Onondoga War Memorial in Syracuse. Tonight we will open up The Television Title Tournament, The U.S. Title Tournament and half of the Tag Team Tournament and the Northeast TV title matches.... We are the INWI and we are taking the Email Wrestling world by storm. Before we get going in tonights matches we have a interview to bring you. Unfortunatley for Tiffany Love, she got stuck with interviewing "The Hollywood Kid" Jason Starr again, and I guess he's not to happy about his spot in the INWI Tournaments. Let's go to that interview with Tiffany... -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------------ {Scene opens back stage where Tiffany Love is trying to get an interview with the very irate Jason Starr} Tiffany Love: Hello rasslin fans! I'm standing outside the dressing room of the self proclaimed REAL Worlds Champion Jason Starr. Judging by the noise he appeers to be trashing his dressing room! {Tiffany knocks on the door} Jason Starr: Who the #$@% is it? Tiffany Love: It's Tiffany Love, Mr Starr. I was hoping to get a statment from you. {The door swings open} Jason Starr: Oh, I'll give you a statment sweetheart! {Jason makes a guesture with his hand that is censored out by the camera} Tiffany Love: No offence sir, but who tinkled in your corn flakes? Jason Starr: I'll tell ya who! The %^$#@!& INWI, that's who! What the hell am I doing in the US Title tourny? I'm the REAL Worlds Champion, I've won twelve world titles! Tiffany Love: I really don't know. Jason Starr: I'll tell you why! It's because they know damn well that if the Hollywood Kid was in the tournament for the heavyweight title, he'd win it! Not only that but If I won the heavyweight title, they know damn well that they'd never find anybody good enough to take it from me! This type of conspiracy is nothing new to me, I deal with it where ever I go. Except this time they made a little boo boo. Tiffany Love: What boo boo? Jason Starr: Unknowingly they entered my best friend and thirteen time world champion in the tournament! That's right me and my partner Jammin Jimmy Cougar are the Glamour boys and together we've won over sixty titles. I will win that US title, and with the Hollywood Kid watching his back, Jimmy Cougar can't lose! Tiffany Love: If your so sure that you and your friend Jimmy outclass everybody in the INWI, then why did you even bother joining? Jason Starr: That's real simple toots! There ain't nothing in the world that me and Jimmy love more than gold belts! Soon we'll have them all and there ain't a damn thing anybody can do to stop us! Tiffany Love: How can you be so sure? Jason Starr: Ha ha, it's cause I'm a STARR and they're not! Deal with it!!! {Jason slams the door and Tiffany turns to the camera} Tiffany Love: Well there you have it fans, straight from the horse's mouth! I only hope that this guy isn't as good as he claims, cause if he is........the INWI is in trouble! -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------------ Ben Lamson - WOW!! Jason Starr is not a happy camper to say the least. I think the people in the U.S. Title tournament may be in trouble after that interview. Starr tonight takes on "The Knuckle" Jimmy Chooley. I can't wait to see him perform in the ring and see how well he backs it up!!! Lets go up to the ring with our first match of the night!! Match 1 - Northeast TV Title Tournament Baker vs Daniel Smith The Baker went right to work on the less experianced Daniel Smith and used his knowledge to advance. Although Smith tried and tried to mount some offense, The Baker would drop him as soon as Smith started. You could tell that the Baker was having fun throughout the match as he kept laughing at Smith and smacking him and laughing at the crowd. The match ended at 6:02 when The Baker did the Baker Bomb and covered Smith for the easy three. Match 2 - Northeast TV Title Tournament The Springer vs Brian Johnson This match was quicker than the opener. The Springer wanted to waste no time getting into the second round. The Springer, the third triplet of the "Comforters," was in Utica while his Brothers were on the card in Syracuse. The Springer worked on the legs and back through the first three minutes, then slapped the "Coil" (scorpion) on Johnson. Johnson wasted no time giving up and the Springer advanced at 3:43. Match 3 - Tag Team Title Tournament The Peeler Brothers vs The Voyeurs of Utter Destruction The Peeler Brothers, more members of the Brotherhood of Sour Grapes, came to the ring wearing straps from what I think said the INF... but I'm not sure... so these guys are champs else where and want to enter the INWI.... well boys, this league ain't no pushover!! The Peeler Brothers started out quick when Mr. Pear went after Eric "The Mystrey Man" McBird. The two locked up and this great match was under way. Pear took over the beginning of the match and had the near fall until The Alien came in and broke it up. The Alien and McBird then double teamed Pear as the Ref tried to keep Mr. Orange out of the ring. The Peeler Brothers though had their sneaky manager, Mr. Flamboyant at their side. And when the match went outside, Flamboyant got a couple shots in on the Voyeurs and the crowd went nuts. The Peeler Brothers then took over. Their quick moving style and quick tags kept the Voyeurs off their feet. McBird tried to mount a comeback, but Pear rolled him up in a small package for a surprise win. Your Winners in 10:17 - The Peeler Brothers!! Match 4 - TV Title Tournament Vito Rizzotto vs Harvey Thunder The man surrounded by mob like people intimitated Thunder right off the bat. I don't know if Thunder even knew what to expect from this guy. I know if I was in the ring, I may even back down from him. I am in the box and getting chills. Oh well, we're underway. Rizzotto takes control right away and is in the middle of the ring trading blows with Thunder. With the match under way, Thunder has lost the nrervousness that he was showing in the beginning of the match. Thunder just slammed Rizzotto and dropped a massive elbow and goes for a cover... 1, 2 , NO!! Rizzotto runs from the ring then slips back in and jumps Thunder from behind working on the neck and back. He scoops him up and looks like he's setting him up for a reverse DDT... YES... It's the MOB DROP!! 1 - 2 - 3!!! Here is your winner, in 9:35... Vito Rizzotto!! Match 5 - TV Title Tournament "Hollywood" Dan Sausville vs "The Big Fan" Devin Terry "The Big Fan" is all that he says that he is. He was out chilling with the crowd when Sausville entered the ring. The crowd really seems to get into Terry and his clan, the Chuckle Heads. Sausville leaves the ring and jumps Terry ouside the ring, using everything but our TV monitors here at ringside... and oh my, there goes one of them!! Sausville fires Terry back into the ring, just beating a double countout. Terry decides to fight fire with fire and slows down Sausville and I don't think "Hollywood" will be singing to many low parts to soon. Terry fires Sausville into the ropes and executes a perfect drop kick. But this run of Terry's would only last for so long. Sausville used his superior strength to wear down Terry. A couple of body slams and an elbow drop later, Terry was out in the middle of the ring. Sausville drops the Hollywood Drop and covers Terry for the pin at the 17:21 mark. Match 6 - U.S. Title Tournament "The Hollywood Kid" Jason Starr vs "The Knuckle" Jimmy Chooley Well, we finally get to see if this arrogant Starr can back up his words. His opponent is the second straight match featuring a member of the Chuckle Heads, this time being "The Knuckle" Jimmy Chooley. Starr starts out right off the bat show boating to the crowd and if I am not mistaking he is claiming he will walk through this tornament and this Federation? Give me a break. We are finally under way..... And Chooley fires Starr into the ropes... clothesline!! Chooley I think is trying to make a statement in this match. Here's a try at a suplex... no it's reversed! Starr starts to take over this match and is controlling it by outsmarting the younger Chooley. I guess by winning all the gold that this Starr has, you have that experiance. Starr fires him towards the ropes and Chooley collides with the ref.... this could be trouble!! Hey... who's this coming down to ringside?? HEY, THAT'S JIMMY COUGAR??? I thought he was supposed to be in Syracuse? Man, he must of flown here... he's handing something to Starr and he runs off... what was that? Starr is rubbing it on his wristband and there's Starr's version of the sleeper... and the ref is coming to. Oh my god, it can't end this way.... he checks the arm, 1, 2, 3.... it's over. Your winner at 8:27, "The Hollywood Kid" Jason Starr. Match 7 - U.S. Title Tournament Jason "The Crusher" Chandler vs Firestar This could prove to be an interesting match. Firestar attacks right before the bell and is mauling Chandler. Chandler, being wise rolls out of the ring. Firestar follows and OUCH!! Takes a chair to the head!! Chandler scoops up Firestar and DDT's him on the floor. The Crusher breaks the count and comes back outside the ring. Chandler places a chair on Firestar and gets a running start from the ring apron and dives.... WOW!! Firestar just planted Chandler with a chair as he dove towards him. I don't know if Chandler even knows where he is now. This match hasn't even really been in the ring yet today. Firestar breaks the count and rams Chandler into the ring post 10 times as the crowd chants with it. They don't know who to cheer for in this match, but that's the problem with two heels!! Back into the ring we go and Firestar is still beating on Chandler. Firestar goes to scoop him up and Chandler rolls him up and grabs some tights at the same time... 1.....2.....3!!!!! Your winner, in 10:10, Jason "The Crusher" Chandler!! Match 8 - TV Title Tournament Mr. Apples vs Raver Apples, a member of the Brotherhood of Sour Grapes, wasted no time in going after Raver. The Big man from Orchard Park, NY was taking no prisoners. He came out donning another belt, so that must mean he is some kind of champion in another league. Apples drives Raver to the mat with a solid standing clothesline!! He then scoops him up by the hair?? OUCH!! Apples throws Raver into the turnbuckle and follows him in with a clothesline...he then drags him out to the center of the ring and nails Raver with a chokeslam. Apples picks up the lifeless Raver and sets him up for a brainbuster... YES!! He nails him with the "Applesauce Drop!!" There's the easy cover and the win for Mr. Apples at 6:27. Match 9 - Tag Team Title Tournament The Fantastic Surfers vs The Unholy Alliance You know, these guys from the Unholy alliance actually scare me. We have some frightful figures here in this federation. The Surfers, Hi and Tide are definitley overmatched here. The attack is on as both members of the Unholy Alliance attacks and are mauling the two beach bums from California... more members of the Chuckle Heads. Now it settles down and it's Blade up against Tide.... Blade is absolutley mashing Hi and then goes over and clotheslines Tide... boy that was called for. I think the Alliance is just going to have a good time tonight. Here's a dropkick from Tide... but it barely takes any effect on Blade. Hey, who's this coming to ringside? That's the Voyeurs of Utter Destruction... what are they doing out here? They already lost tonight....Hey they are taunting the Alliance and the Alliance is chasing after them.... what is this all about?? The ref begins the count and I think the Alliance has just realized whats going on and they are heading back to the ring. .....8.....9....10... That's the match. What the heck happened?? Your winners, via countout... The Fantastic Surfers. And here come the Alliance and they are beating on the Surfers... this could be doom for them in the second round..... Match 10 - U.S. Title Tournament The Bodyguard vs Reignman The Bodyguard, part of the Kings of Destruction looked ready for this one as Reignman entered the ring. Reignman saw the quick attack and dodged it as he delivered a couple blows of his own chasing The Bodyguard from the ring. This one could get exciting folks... Reignman climbs to the top rope and dives on The Bodyguard who was outside the ring!! That could spell broken ribs!! The Bodyguard crawls back into the ring and nails Reignman with a real low blow then gives him a swift uppercut. Rignman looks to be grasping for air and The Bodyguard trips him up. He looks like he is setting him up for something and nails a face first powerbomb!! Ow, that may leave a mark in the morning... the cover and the three count. Your winner in 6:21, The Bodyguard. Ben Lamson - Well, before we head into the home stretch.... we have an interview for you in the back. Our next match features someone who left his job as a postman to join the INWI. He;s a very interesting fellow.... let's goto the interview... -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------------ Michael Pontiff: *standing in the locker room* "I am here in the locker room standing next to the man who could possibly be the next U.S. champ, PO Box....." PO Box: *interupts him* "Whoa whoa whoa, POSSIBLY the next champ? Let me tell you something Pontiff, I garantee that I will win the US title! I was the most reliable postman in my town, and I delivered on schedule. So when I say I'll win the U.S. title, I WILL deliever on schedule!" Michael Pontiff: "Well, before you win that title, there seems to be a huge obstacle in your way...by the name of Zues!" PO Box: "Ya know, I was good at being a postman for one reason....I didn't let anything get in my way! So Zues, neither rain, nor sleet, nor snow or ice, I promise you that I will drive right through you to make my final delivery...and that delivery is the US title being put around my waist! So when I get done with you, you'll be nothing more than a Greek myth!" *exits* Michael Pontiff: "Well, PO Box certainly sounds pumped and ready to go as he faces Zues for the US title and if I were Zues, I'd be getting ready for the fight of the century." -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------------ Ben Lamson - I guess PO Box is ready for action and with that, let's go up to the ring for that match!!! Match 11 - U.S. Title Tournament PO Box vs Zeus The match between the powerhouse and the ex-postal carrier. This should prover to be commical. Hey wait, this post man is for real as he just attacked Zeus and got the big man down to his knees. A series of brawling techniquies really seems to work!! Zues grabs the Postal worker and picks him up in a giant chokeslam and nails it!! Zeus goes for the pin, but PO Box kicks out at two.... The PO Box man is coming back though and nails Zeus with a series of rights and lefts! They are not even effecting the big man... but that poke to the eye will work. PO scoops him up.. bodyslam!! Herse comes a suplex.... amazing!!! This PO Box is a strong man... must be from all those years of delivering the mail in the bad weather!! He puts Zeus up on his shoulders for a back drop and is climbing the ropes??? What is he crazy?? He's on the top and nails it... "The Final Delivery!!" There's the cover... and the pin!! Your winner in 8:17, PO Box!! Match 12 - Tag Team Title Tournament MacDaddies vs The Big Brothers The MacDaddies came dancing out to the ring like they were all that and a bag of chips and the two brothers by the name of Big followed. Hunter and Steven Big... part of the New Coalition Order with PO Box and Senator Bradley are both very large people. Da Pimp and Da Playa... comprising the MacDaddies both hail from the Ghetto and are both rather large themselves. Let's hit the action. Da Playa and Steven Big start it off and Da Playa used a ear smack to get the advantage. Here's a bear hug.... with arms like that, that move has to hurt! Hunter Big comes in and breaks it up, but as the ref is sending him back to his corner, Da Pimp and Da Playa douple team Steven. Steven comes up with a double clothesline and tags in his brother and the crowd is going crazy!!! Hunter just nailed them both with a double noggin knocker and then a DOUBLE DDT!! Here's the cover 1....2.... NO!! Da Pimp and Da Playa roll outside the ring to discuss the situation. Back into the ring... Da Playa drives his fist into Hunter's throat and runs over and smacks Steven. He comes Steven but the ref is holding him back. Uh oh... the MacDaddies are setting up.... DaPlaya just choke slammed Hunter and Da Pimp is coming off the top with an elbow... it's DA SMACK!! Here's the cover.... and the pin. Your winners in 18:19.... the MacDaddies!! Match 13 - TV Title Tournament Mr. Ed Rogers vs Michael Hollywood Rogers came out to the ring with a very poor singer singing "Won't you be my neighbor.." and this other guy... man do we have alot of "Hollywood's" in this federation!! Well, the final first round match is under way!! Hollywood zips across the ring and clotheslines Rogers as he was taking off his "Manager," a sock puppet named "Smelly." Hollywood is all over the crowd pleasing Rogers and the crowd is not happy about this sneak attack. Here's a leg drop, but Rogers moved!! Rogers, still slow to getting up is looking to get an advantage. He is starting to skip around the ring... what is this? I think Hollywood is stunned and theres a punch to the head and another and down goes Hollywood. Rogers throws him into the ropes and it's a back drop. He's setting him up for something on the ropes. It's a German Suplex from the top rope.... the Puppet Master!!! There's the count and the match. Your winner... in 11:37... Mr. Ed Rogers!! Wow...I think the word for today is WOW.... can you all say WOW?? Ben Lamson - Well people and fans from all over.... that's it from Utica. We have had a wild card here and I am sure the same can be said about the card in Syracuse!! Tomorrow in Binghamton.... all of the matches leading up to the finals!! Then on Sunday in Albany.... EVERY Championship in the INWI will be determined!!! Until tomorrow night in Binghamton... KEEP IT IN THE RING!!! INWI Wrestling..... The Revolutionary force in E-Feds
