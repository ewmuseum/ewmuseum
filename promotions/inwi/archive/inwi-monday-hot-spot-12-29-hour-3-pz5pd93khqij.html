[INWI] Monday Hot Spot, 12-29, Hour 3

30 December 1997

Brian Newark - We have tried and tried to find out if King Apples is in the building and it has come to our attention that he did not show up tonight. I guess we won't get to he-- Voice - Shaddup punk before I drop you. [Newark stops and looks up and King Apples has a mic and is walking to the ring. He is in blue jeans and a Brotherhood t-shirt. He has a mic in his hand and walks to the ring. As he gets to the apron, the crowd starts chanting BBW..BBW..BBW!! Apples eyes get as big as silver dollars and he is staring out at the crowd. Apples then gets in the ring.] King Apples - SHUT UP! All of you morons have no friggin clue do you? Bill Bob wins this damn match last night after two things. One, he had to wear a mask. Two, he had to use a chair. [Crowd POP] SHUT UP!!! Do you not see someone cheating here? Bill Bob CHEATED! [Crowd POP!] [Apples walks around and kicks a turnbuckle and flips off part of the crowd. He looks down at Newark, who has a smile on his face, and growls.] King Apples - This is bull crap. I got cheated out of my title belt. The Brotherhood NEVER cheats. I don't like this. And Bill Bob, you are gonna get yours. And what's with the mask? You weren't a man to take me on without the mask? Had to be sneaky? Well Bill Bob...Lonestar....whatever your name is now. I'm gonna open a 55 gallon drum of whoop ass when I get you. Because your little frame won't take the 'Drop to well. [Crowd starts to chant Apples Sucks....Apples Sucks...Apples Sucks.] King Apples - SHUT UP! SHUT UP! You damn freaks in the crowd have no clue. NONE. You are all uneducated pieces of crap. I was cheated out of the Northeast Title. Can't you see that? I was CHEATED! [Crowd starts chanting BBW....BBW....BBW] King Apples - SHUT UP! Bill Bob, Yer ass is mine. I don't care how long it takes... but I am gonna whip your ass. I am going to beat you from corner to corner. You have felt the spike before and you will feel it again. As for everyone else in this fed, your all done. You will ALL feel the wrath of the Brotherhood. And to you other masks, you panty wearing wimps, Friendship Express....you're on the list as well. [Apples throws down the mic and gets on the apron. The crowd starts chanting Apples Sucks.... Apples Sucks.... Apples Sucks. Apples covers his ears and then flips off the crowd and walks to the back flipping off people on the way.] [The letters I---N---W---I pop on the screen one by one. Then they explode as the logo of Monday Night Hot Spot comes onto the screen. The music "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne starts to crank and the arena goes black. The INWI-Tron pops on and the beginning of Hot Spot starts.... [It starts by showing a close up of INWI Champion Tony Houston with the INWI belt over one shoulder and the Ringmaster Belt over the other. He just smiles at the camera. It then shows King Apples nailing a tomb swirly on Ed Rogers. It shows Springer nailing a Springer Cutter on Scoop ropes from the top of the cage. It then shows Springer draped in an INWI flag flexing....] [It shows Sam Adams draped in American flag with Militia behind him and him laughing with the New York Title around his waist. It shows Michael Bane, Michael Bledsoe and Jonathan Hammer in one of the most grueling cage matches in INWI history. It then shows a close up of every championship belt in the INWI.... at different camera angles...] [It then shows Commissioner Jerry DeLuise in an INWI Arena looking over the INWI. Jason Chandler holding up his Television Championship and then the screen starts to show all sorts of pieces from the INWI..... James Pillow nailing a super kick on Rick Brown, Vinny Vitrone in his dog collar match with the "jWo" Icon Pig Pen, Ultimate Perversion and Cloud Eagle going at it ...Cherry nailing a Cherry Bomb...Mr. Ed Rogers staring at Smelly...Tommy Graves nailing a choke slam...David McCarter on the mic...The Asswhupper and Thugs 4 Life standing with their arms crossed.... Solid Gold turning on manager Jimmy Stardom... The Comforters holding up the Tag Team titles, Malice in a match....The All Americans waving American flags...Michael Bane holding up his newly won Light Heavyweight title...Jim Whitten pounding on Jonathan Sunset....It shows Mike "The Gavel" Garvey in his return to the INWI...and a final shot of the jWo huddling together....] Then you see Springer walk past the camera with an American Flag jacket on...the camera follows him and on the back of the jacket you can see "INWI" as Springer walks into the flames ahead.... then the camera fades and fireworks go off inside the arena and it's time for Hot Spot. The Camera switches to Brian Newark at ringside.] [Newark is wearing an INWI rugby shirt, has his head set on, wears wire rimmed glasses and is ready to go. He flashes the camera a smile and we are ready to go...] Monday Hot Spot HOUR THREE!!! December 29, 1997 The Igloo, Pittsburgh, Pa **The 35th Edition of Monday Hot Spot** Brian Newark - Well, I guess I was wrong. Apples was in the building and he seems to be a little upset. You can bet your bottom dollar that he is looking for Bill Bob Woijicowski. These two will no doubt meet down the road again. And whoo....what a third hour we have planned for you. The tag titles are on the line as is the TV Title. And this hour will be wild. What are we waiting for? let's get to the ring! Match 8 Ultimate Perversion vs Sam "The Minuteman" Adams (with Militia) Yet another interesting match following last nights Pay Per View, as Adams attempts to actually wrestle a clean match in his New York title defense Cherry last night, and though he nearly took Cherry out several times, he was unable to put him away for the three count, and Cherry eventually hit the Cherry Bomb and defeated Adams to win the New York Championship. And as Perversion makes his way towards the ring, Cherry leads almost dragging the belt behind him as if it's a caveman bringing home "fresh" dinner. Adams is in the ring with Militia at ringside, well at least tonight no damn gorilla suit...and speaking of that..what the hell is the deal with Mr. Ed Rogers?? Siding with Adams?? But with Ed, the thing is, even HE doesn't know..and then David McCarter siding with Adams?? Who'd a thunk it? Both men are in the ring and Adams jump starts the match and attacks Perversion is punching and kicking at Perversion...Perversion swings and Adams ducks...Right, right, left, dropkick only staggers Perversion...Adams with a right..ducks a left by Perversion..right by Adams...Perversion caught Adams punch!! And just spun Adams around in the air with a monster clothesline...Perversion pulls up Adams and sends him into the ropes..backdro--No!!! Adams flips over Perversion but is too proud of himself and is dropped with a thrust kick to the face by Perversion. Perversion pulls up Adams and sends him into the ropes..Powerslam by Perversion...One..two..NO!!! The former New York champion kicks out. Perversion climbs to the second rope and leaps..Legdro--No!!!! Adams rolled out of his way and Perversion just crashed hard onto his hip. Adams is starting to rise and bounces off the ropes and drops a fist to the head of the fallen Perversion...Adams pulls Perversion and stomps Perversion in the stomach and leaps...Standing FrankenAdam takes Perversion over and Adams is pounding away at the head of Perversion...Adams pulls up Perversion...Adams is setting up for a Vertical Suplex.No!! Instead Adams stepped his left leg in and drives Perversion head first into the mat. Adams goes to the outside and ascends to the top rope...and leaps..Flying Elbow crashing across the chest of Perversion..One..two..No!!! Perversion kicks out...Adams pulls Perversion up and bounces off the rope...Whoa!! Cherry just tripped Adams from the outside..Adams up and is arguing with Cherry..Wait!!! Militia is in the ring and choking out Perversion.. SMACK!!! Cherry just waffled Adams with the New York Belt. There's the bell...Cherry into the ring and he's whipping Adams with belt..Militia sees Cherry working over Adams and lets go of Perversion...Militia grabs Cherry and press him over his head...Military Press by Militia...Perversion on the other side of the ring is starting to stir...Militia is working over Cherry...Militia whips Cherry into the ropes...Cherry grabs the rope and stops..and then points behind Militia and Perversion mauls over Militia. Perversion scoops up Adams and lifts and holds him on his shoulders..Cherry goes to the outside and leaps...Legdrop/Powerbomb combination....Cherry's Orchard is working over Adams and Militia...Somebody's heading down the aisle way.!!! Who is that?? It's Ed Rogers !?!?!?! What the hell...Ed hits the ring with "Smelly" on his hand and a chair in the other...Cherry sees Ed and flips himself over the top rope and heads to the back, as Perversion soon follows before Ed can unload with that chair. Let's get the official word. At 8:32, the referee's official decision, a Double Disqualification!!! =========================================================== *Tiffany Love is in the ring standing by...* Tiffany: Ladies and gentlemen....the man who made his return to the ring last night at Winter Storm, none other than "The Gavel" Mike Garvey!!! *the crowd begins to boo loudly as everyone turns their head towards the aisle* *Mike Garvey has a more arrogant look than ever...he comes down the aisle in his black judge robe and he climbs up onto the apron....giving the fans a disgusted look, he slowly steps through the ropes and towards Tiffany* Tiffany: Now Gavel, there was some question as to whether or not you could handle a challenge like Springer last night at Winter Storm...but you proved everyone wrong with a victory...one which was aided by a hand full of tights! Gavel: WHAT?!?! HAND FULL OF TIGHTS?!?!? What are you talking about?! You and everyone else knows that I don't need to resort to tactics like that to win my matches...I was just on the verge of making him submit to me, but I thought I'd go easy on him and just pin him instead. Springer, you felt hard justice once before, how'd it feel this time around? *chuckles* Ya know, you're just as easy to beat as you were the first time around. Now, lets talk about people who actually matter...people like ME. Now, later on tonight, I'm gonna take that nobody, Jason Chandler, and dish out a little hard justice. Tiffany: Well, that may be the case, but he is the TV champ....I think you're going to have to dish out a little more than hard justice Mr. Garvey. Gavel: Listen, he's nothing....a nobody....it won't take me long to put him away. He calls himself "The Crusher"...well, son, if you're back there hiding in the locker room and listening to this, you don't know the definition of "crusher" until you've met the Gavel. Get ready son, 'cause in a matter of time, I'm gonna take that TV championship belt and put it around a waist that fits it a little better. And after I do that, you can just put your head down low and walk back to the locker room, and don't even bother stepping back out into the ring, 'cause you're finished after I get done with you. Nobody's gonna know who you are after tonight...as a matter of fact, I forgot what your name was already! Now what was it again...I seem to have forgotten.... Tiffany: Jason "The Crusher" Chandler....his name that is... Gavel: I DIDN'T ASK FOR YOU TO ANSWER...what the hell's wrong with you? Tiffany: Nothing, I was just.... Gavel: Well, whatever it is you were doing, don't do it anymore, understand? Tiffany: Yes, but if I could, I'd like to touch on another subject...The Franchise Mike Hickden...he's offering his assistance to you, and you have yet to respond to that request. Gavel: *chuckles* The Franchise? The Franchise?!?!?! Who the hell is this guy? Listen son, the Gavel's never heard of you, never will hear of you, and frankly, I could care less about who you are. I'm a former champ in the INWI, and you, you're a nobody, and you expect ME to want YOUR assistance? Look, get your head straight, get a name for yourself, and ask again later....'cause I got other things to worry about. Chandler, I'll see ya later.... *Gavel storms out of the ring as the crowd boos* =========================================================== Match 9 - Tag Team Championship Solid Gold vs The Friendship Express The New tag team champions, returning are The Friendship Express, as they were the surprise replacement for The Comforters last night against James Pillow and Vinny Vitrone, originally as two masked men, but following the match was revealed that in fact The Friendship Express, Sanchez and Geller, have returned to the INWI in a big way. As well as The Friendship Express having a successful pay per view last night, as did the former INWI Tag team champions, Solid Gold, defeating The All American's in a wild Texas Tornado match. Now tonight both teams will go at it and the Tag Team championship is on the line. Blake and Sanchez are going to be starting this tag match...They lock up and Sanchez pushes Blake into the corner...big knee lift, lifts Blake off the mat....Sanchez scoops Blake into a Fireman's Carry and takes him down..Sanchez quickly with a dropkick to the back of Blake's head. Sanchez tags in his partner Geller and the champs whip Blake into the ropes and Double Flapjack by the champs..Geller pulls up Blake and scoop him onto his shoulder and falls straight back standing backdrop into a pinning combination..One..two...No!!! Whoa!! Blake just bridged his way back to his feet and takes Geller over with a backslide..one..two..No!! Geller kicks out and is up quickly...but is met with a vicious forearm uppercut by Blake...and Blake takes down Geller with a Dragon sweep...Blake tags in his brother and partner Bradley and they whip Geller into the ropes and drop 'em with a double dropkick. Bradley pulls up Geller and again sends him into the ropes and plans Geller with a Spinebuster into a Tigerbomb..great move....One..two..No!! Geller again with a kick out. Bradley tags in his brother again. Solid Gold whips Geller into the ropes...but Geller ducks under the double clothesline and rebounds off the ropes and nails a dropkick on Solid Gold, one foot for each..Geller up and tags in Sanchez..Sanchez with Blake in the corner and sends him across the ring and charges....Blake raises the foot, Sanchez slams on the breaks, grabs Blake's leg and takes him over with legdrag. Sanchez quickly goes to the outside and to the top rope...Flying Bodypress...One.. two..th--No!! Bradley in to break the count..Geller is on the top rope and sends Bradley to the outside with a Missile Dropkick. Geller off the ropes and plancha's to the outside on top of Bradley...Blake and Sanchez up..Blake with a headlock,. Sanchez pushes Blake into the ropes...Sanchez with a leapfrog....Blake ducks under a clothesline but is taken down and rolled up by Sanchez with a hurracanrana into a reverse victory roll... One..two..three!!! He got him...The Friendship Express have defended their newly won straps...to say the least, that was surprising, but not really an upset...Let's get the official word, as this brawl continues, in and out of the ring. Here are your winners in 12:32......and still INWI Tag team Champions, The Friendship Express Match 10 - Television Championship Jason "Crusher" Chandler (with Oz) vs Mike "The Gavel" Garvey In only his second match back, Mike Garvey gets a title match. No doubt one of those "options" he had put in his contract. Garvey could walk out of this match with the Television championship. But I don't think that after he fought so hard to keep that title from Michael Bledsoe last night, he plans on giving it up tonight. And to be honest, I have a feeling Bledsoe plans on having another shot at that belt. Bledsoe is on the card tonight and he takes on Malice. Tie up in the center of the ring and Garvey with a punch to the side of Chandler's head. Chandler backs up and sakes his head and attacks Garvey. He tackles Garvey and starts to pound his melon into the ground. Chandler off the ropes and he drops an elbow and another. Garvey rolls out of the ring and starts to head to the back. He is saying he doesn't want this match. Chandler slides out of the ring and chases down Garvey and clotheslines him to the floor. Chandler scoops up Garvey and slams him on the outside! Chandler again grabs Garvey and puts him on his shoulder, charges the ring and he is gonna slam Garvey into a ring post! NO! Garvey slips off the back and sends Chandler face first into the ring post. Chandler slams hard and crumples to the mat. Garvey laughs and then grabs Chandler and sends him into the ring post again. Chandler is cut. Garvey kicks at Chandler's head and then grabs him and sends him into the ring. Garvey back into the ring and he sends Chandler into the ropes.... clothesline. Garvey with an elbow and then a standing leg drop. Garvey grabs Chandler....snap suplex! Garvey doesn't look like he has any ring rust. Garvey grabs Chandlers leg and drops on his knee. Chandler grabs his knee and is rolling around on the mat. Garvey off the ropes and drops an elbow on the knee. And Garvey is now kicking at Chandler's knee. Garvey is setting up for something....what is this? Garvey just slapped the figure four on Chandler and Chandler is screaming in pain. Garvey has shown something new in his arsenal. Chandler is not giving up and is looking to turn it over. He's turning......HE REVERSED it!! Garvey crawls to the ropes and it is broken. Oz pulls Chandler out of the ring and is trying to get feeling back in his leg. Chandler rolls back in the ring and Garvey walks over and gets a thumb in the eye. Chandler then nails a quick DDT and Garvey is face down on the mat and Chandler is still trying to get feeling in his knee. Chandler off the rope and an elbow drop to the head of Garvey. The TV Champ is starting to turn this match around. Chandler grabs Garvey and sets up and nails a vertical suplex. Chandler is the king of the suplex. No doubt about that. T Bone suplex by Chandler on Garvey and he is setting up for this one. Chandler with Garvey and there's another suplex! A cover by Chandler, 1......2.......NO! Garvey kicks out and Chandler brings him to his feet, eye rake by Garvey and Chandler stumbles back. Garvey off the ropes and clotheslines Chandler to the mat. This one is going back and forth. Spinning DDT by Garvey and Chandler hit hard. Cover by Garvey, 1.......2........NO! Chandler kicks out. Garvey grabs Chandler and is setting up for something.... another DDT. Chandler can only take so many of those. Another cover by Garvey, 1........2..........There's the bell! Do we have a new champ? Garvey to the outside and grabs the Television Championship....but wait...the ref is talking to the announcer. Let's get the official word. Ladies and gentlemen, the 20 minute time limit expired in this bout before the three count could be made, making this match a DRAW! Jason Chandler retains the TV Title! Garvey looks hot and just throws the belt into the ring and takes off. Oz helps Chandler up and gives him the belt as they leave the ring. Brian Newark - What a wild match and Mike Garvey got a draw in only his second match back with the INWI and against the TV Champ. What a match. And what a show we had tonight ladies and gentlemen. But we are out of time.
