[INWI] Monday Hot Spot, 12-8, Hour 2

9 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Brian Newark is standing in the middle of the ring getting ready to do another interview. Out of nowhere, the crowd begins to stir loudly and then a commotion breaks loose. A figure walks out from behind the curtain and the camera man rushes to get a shot of who it is. A man slowly walks down the aisle with a determined look in his eyes.] Newark: Hey buddy, whoever you are, this time is reserved for... [The man coming down the aisle is pushing cameramen out of his way] Newark: Hey, somebody get security...this guy is....wait a minute, oh my god, it's MIKE GARVEY!!! THE GAVEL!!!! I CAN'T BELIEVE IT! [Mike Garvey steps up onto the apron and looks out into the crowd as some of them are cheering and some are booing. He steps through the ropes...] Newark: Mike Garvey, what are you... [Garvey shoves Newark to the mat and takes his mic and some of the fans scream in approval] Garvey: Well, well, well...if it isn't the INWI. I would say things have changed, but it seems to me that the competition is as sorry as it was the last time I was here. I've been gone for almost 3 months....takin' care of other business. But that's outta the way now and there's some stuff I gotta get off my chest. Bein' in the midst of all this sorry competition is gettin' to be a problem...but it sure ain't the only problem I got! I got a problem with the so called "commissioner" of this stupid league. He's got nothin' goin' for him, nobody likes him, so if I were him, I'd kick my own sorry ass outta the league and go somewhere else! Another thing I got a problem with is this sorry bunch of punks who call themselves the Brotherhood. Now, I dunno who the hell you think you are, but not a single one of you could ever measure up to what I am. I was the best damn wrestler in this league, and you think you can just prance on in here and steal my spotlight? Not if I have anything to say 'bout it. Just 'cause you hold 3 titles doesn't mean anything...'cause you and I both know I could beat all your sorry asses for those titles if I wanted. [The mixed sound of boos and cheers gets louder] Garvey: The jWo is another thing that pisses me off...but I'm not even gonna say nothin' about them, 'cause their too sorry to even mention! Something else that gets to me is the way they took away the United States championship and turned it into the New York title. I'm a former US title champ...the greatest one there ever will be, and the brainiacs in the INWI committee decide to get rid of that title! That's what's wrong with this damn league...too many people thinkin' that they can run this place. Well, ALL your sorry asses are gonna have to bow down to me, the greatest wrestler that ever stepped foot into an INWI arena. But what's RIGHT about this league, is that The Gavel is back! And I know a lot of you still remember me, and I know you're happy to see me back... Springer, Bill Bob Woijikowski..you know I ain't done with you punks yet from when I was last here in the league. And Tony Houston, that sure is a shiny belt around your waist, but you and I both know it would look much better around MY waist. And for any of you punks back there in the locker room, you think you got what it takes to beat the Gavel? Well then just march your sorry ass out here and I'll show you what HARD JUSTICE is all about. And as long as I'm here, justice WILL BE SERVED!!! COURT'S ADJOURNED!!! [Garvey throws the mic into the crowd as they're mainly booing him now...some fan catches the mic and starts screaming into it as Garvey walks back to the locker room area] [The letters I---N---W---I pop on the screen one by one. Then they explode as the logo of Monday Night Hot Spot comes onto the screen. The music "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne starts to crank and the arena goes black. The INWI-Tron pops on and the beginning of Hot Spot starts.... [It starts by showing a close up of INWI Champion Tony Houston with the INWI belt over one shoulder and the Ringmaster Belt over the other. He just smiles at the camera. It then shows King Apples nailing a tomb swirly on Ed Rogers. It shows Springer nailing a Springer Cutter on Scoop ropes from the top of the cage. It then shows Springer draped in an INWI flag flexing....] [It shows Sam Adams draped in American flag with Militia behind him and him laughing with the New York Title around his waist. It shows Michael Bane, Michael Bledsoe and Jonathan Hammer in one of the most grueling cage matches in INWI history. It then shows a close up of every championship belt in the INWI.... at different camera angles...] [It then shows Commissioner Jerry DeLuise in an INWI Arena looking over the INWI. Jason Chandler holding up his Television Championship and then the screen starts to show all sorts of pieces from the INWI..... James Pillow nailing a super kick on Rick Brown, Vinny Vitrone in his dog collar match with the "jWo" Icon Pig Pen, Ultimate Perversion and Cloud Eagle going at it with Harvey Thunder and Michael Slater, Bill Bob Woijicowski reading one of his ''stories'' to his nephew Bobby Bob...Cherry nailing a Cherry Bomb...Mr. Ed Rogers staring at Smelly...Tommy Graves nailing a choke slam...David McCarter on the mic...The Asswhupper and Thugs 4 Life standing with their arms crossed.... Solid Gold turning on manager Jimmy Stardom... The Comforters holding up the Tag Team titles, Malice in a match....The All Americans waving American flags...and a final shot of the jWo huddling together....] Then you see Pete Robinson walk past the camera with an American Flag jacket on...the camera follows him and on the back of the jacket you can see "INWI" as Robinson walks into the flames ahead.... then the camera fades and fireworks go off inside the arena and it's time for Hot Spot. The Camera switches to Brian Newark at ringside.] [Newark is wearing an INWI rugby shirt, has his head set on, wears wire rimmed glasses and is ready to go. He flashes the camera a smile and we are ready to go...] HOUR NUMBER TWO!!! Rochester War Memorial, Rochester, NY LIVE!!! Brian Newark - Now that was a surprise. Mike "The Gavel" Garvey is back in the INWI after three months off. What a way to start off the second Hour of Hot Spot. That was unexpected to say the least and I am sure Garvey has some unfinished business to settle here in the INWI. He has held a pair of titles in the INWI. Garvey held the United States Title for a little over two months and also was the Northeast TV Champ for about a month. No doubt Garvey wants INWI gold again. We'll have to see when he gets in the ring what his plans are. We have a jam packed second hour of Hot Spot, including the No disqualification match for the Light Heavyweight Championship. Let's head to the ring! Match 5 Cherry vs "Too Slick" Rick Brown Cherry, the man with a new attitude is next. He has turned into one strange bird. Rick Brown is in the ring ready to go. And here comes Cherry. Cherry is in the ring and Brown is all over him as he gets into the ring. A right hand by Brown and Cherry goes down. Another right hand and Cherry goes down and rolls out of the ring. Brown into the ropes and a suicide dive and it almost took Cherry's head off. Who lit under a fire under Brown's butt? Plus, Cherry needs to bounce back after that loss to Bledsoe a week ago. Cherry back into the ring and Brown is up top. What is he doing? He goes for a high cross body but Cherry with a leaping dropkick right into the stomach as Brown came down. Cherry on the offensive. He has Brown up pushes him back into the ropes and then an Irish whip and a flying forearm by Cherry. He gets up and hits a corkscrew leg drop and goes for the cover, 1.......2......and a kick out by Brown. Cherry pulls him up and hits a back breaker and he is holding him on his knee for extra leverage to the back. Cherry lets him go and hits a well placed elbow to the back. Brown clutches his back as he gets up and Cherry with a dropkick to the back and Brown falls through the second and third ropes and tumbles to the floor. Cherry is on the apron and he hits a flying clothesline from the apron and both men go flying into the steps at ringside. Cherry is the first man up and he is moving towards Brown and oh my, Brown hits him with a low blow and a clothesline and Cherry is down. The lip of Brown you can see has been busted open when both men hit the steps just a second ago. Brown picks up Cherry by the hair and tosses him into the apron. Brown steps back and charges and Cherry picks up his legs and Stun Gun's him on the apron. What a move!!. Cherry is back into the ring and he runs into the ropes and a summersault plancha. The fans are going wild here. Brown didn't get a lot of that plancha and now he is back into the ring but still on the mat. Cherry onto the apron. He springboards off the apron, onto the ropes and goes for a hurricanrana roll up and Brown drops him with a Tiger Bomb, nice reversal. Brown goes for the cover, 1......2....and Cherry kicks out. Cherry back up to a vertical base and he ducks under a clothesline by Brown and goes for a go behind and hits a power hoist. Brown landed right on his head. Cherry is going to the top and he hits a beautiful swan dive head butt and that should be all for Brown, 1......2......and Brown's foot was on the ropes. What a lucky break. Cherry has him and a belly to back suplex and a cover, 1.......2......and no!! Cherry is getting frustrated. Brown with a stunning right hand and Cherry hits the canvas. A scoop slam by Brown and he is going up top. He misses with a guillotine leg drop but he is up and a boot to the gut and he has him in a powerbomb, he has him up and Cherry flips out of it and hits a belly to belly suplex. He gives the signal, he goes up top and hits the Cherry Bomb Moonsault. That should be it, 1.......2.......3 and we have a winner. Cherry earned every bit of that match and the winner of the match in the time of 14:21, Cherry!! Match 6 Jim Whitten vs "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset (with Prince Chuckle Wuckle) This should be a decent match, with both Whitten and Sunset showing that they could be INWI superstars. The match is about to begin, lets go to ringside. Coming to the ring, right now, that's Jim Whitten.!! And Here comes Sunset. Whitten is the favored guy here, he's really shown that he's got the skills, its just a matter of time to see if he's got the heart. The bell has sounded, and we're off in this match. Whitten and Sunset tie up, Sunset throws Whitten into the ropes, Whitten bounces off, ducks under the arm of "Hubba Hubba", bounces off the other rope, and hits Sunset with a flying clothesline. What a move! Whitten grabs Jonathan by the head of his hair, and slaps with him with a pair of chops to the chest, Sunsets chest is pure red right now, those were some loud chops! Whitten flings Sunset into the opposite corner, following him with a clothesline. Does it again, to the opposite corner. He attempts it again, but Sunset reverses it, and lunges Whitten into the corner, Sunset attempts to clothesline Whitten in the corner, but Whitten lifts his leg, and gets a surprised Sunset in the stomach. Sunset is hunched over, and Whitten kicks the stunned Sunset. Sunset is down on the canvas, and Whitten is about to attempt a splash off the top rope. He gets to the top turnbuckle, but as he gets to the top, Sunset jumps up, and kicks the ropes. Whitten loses his balance, and falls to the ringside floor! Sunset runs to the opposite ropes, bounces off the ropes and catapults himself over the ropes, onto a stunned Jim Whitten! What a move. Sunset picks up Whitten, and slams his body to the floor. What's he doing now? He's exposing the concrete floor, he's taking the ringside mat, and pulling it off the floor! He picks up Whitten, and Slams him onto the exposed ring floor again! Whitten is in a great deal of pain right now. Sunset jumps into the ring to break the 10 count. Sunset is back outside of the ring. He's leaning over Whitten, and Whitten gets him with a punch to the jaw. And another. Sunset decides to take this back inside the ring. He tosses Whitten into the ring. "Hubba Hubba" Is back in the ring now, and attempts to throw Whitten into the ropes, but Whitten reverses it, and throws Sunset into the ropes. Sunset bounces off the ropes, is bouncing towards Whitten, Whitten bends over, and flips Sunset out of the ring, on to the exposed concrete!!! That back fired on Sunset, that's for sure.! Whitten is on the top turnbuckle, and hits Sunset with a leg drop from the top! Hogan, eat your heart out pal, that's why the INWI is the place where the superstars wrestle! Whitten throws Sunset back into the ring. Whitten starts stomping on Sunset, kicking him over and over, and over. Picks him up, and attempts a suplex, but is to close the ropes, Sunset bounces off the ropes, and onto a stunned Whitten, Sunset covers him...1....2... And a kick out by Whitten. Almost an upset of sorts their by Sunset. Both men look quite tired, but Whitten is the first to get back to his feet, but is met with a punch to the chin. Whitten returns the favor, then the two begin trading closed fist's! Back and Forth, over and over, both men are on one knee, finally, Whitten takes one hard on the chin, and falls to his face. Sunset jumps on the fallen Whitten, and straps on a Boston crab. He's got it on tight, but the ref doesn't see Whitten's leg on the rope...The ref asks if Whitten gives... DO YOU GIVE?... NO....DO YOU GIVE?.... NO.... GIVE UP (SUNSET).... GIVE UP (SUNSET).... DO YOU GIVE... NO NO NO DAMMIT... Sunset starts to bounce on Whitten's back, again, and again. Sunset breaks the hold. Whitten is in great pain. Sunset hits Whitten with a couple of leg drops, and goes for the cover......1.......2.......Kick out. this match ain't over yet. Sunset is getting frustrated. He kicks Whitten in the stomach, and drops another leg drop on him, goes for the cover... 1...... ....kick out. Not even a 2 count this time. Sunset is yelling at the ref now. "COUNT FASTER YOU MORON" " FASTER COUNTS"...as Sunset is yelling at the ref, Whitten gets back up. He twists Sunset around, kicks him in the stomach, and hits him with a DDT. Whitten signals for his patented maneuver the Facelift, its an inverted Pedigree, and hits Sunset with it. Did you see that maneuver! Instead of falling on knees, he fell on his rear end, causing Sunset to hit his WHOLE HEAD, not just the side of it! An amazing maneuver...Whitten covers the dazed Sunset, 1.........2 .........3!!! We have a winner! Jim Whitten is your winner with the Facelift at 14:43. I guess you could say, The Sun has Set, on Jonathan Sunset. Lets go to our next match!!! =========================================================== (The Asswhupper pulls up to the Arena. He Playmaker and Mam are rushed by reporters.)
