[INWI] Monday Hot Spot, 11-3, Runover Time

4 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Match 12 - Special Six Man Tag Match King Apples, Tony Houston, Bill Bob Woijicowski (with Mr. Flamboyant) vs Michael Slater, Harvey Thunder and "Kodiac" Dan Harris This extremely exciting match has several ramifications...First, it was Thunder, and Slater that who were attacked during a tag team match involving the Brotherhood and Thunder and Slater's arch-enemies..Eagle and Perversion, and as everybody knows by now, The Brotherhood and The Fellas are to say the least not on friendly terms....and Dan Harris is a member of the Fellas however quiet he may be...It looks as if we're going to have a slow, deliberate pace, well at least to start this match...It's going to be Bill Bob Woijicowski starting against the former Television Champion Harvey Thunder... Thunder locks up with Bill Bob and pushes him into the corner but it is quickly reversed and Bill Bob sends Thunder into the ropes...Springboard Clothesline by Bill Bob staggers Thunder into the corner and Bill Bob quickly charges and sandwiches Thunder with a Handspring, Sommersault Elbow Dive...Thunder staggres out of the corner and Bill Bob takes him down with a Bulldog headlock..One..two..No!!!! Thunder rolls his shoulder...Bill Bob pulls up Thunder and tags in King Apples and sends Thunder into the ropes again..and Apples drops him with an impressive Powerslam..One..two..t-No!!!! Apples pulls up Thunder and sends him into the cor--No!! Thunder reverses and follows Apples in with a BIG CHOP WOOOOO Thunder near his corner and tags in Michael Slater..Slater pulls Apples to the center of the ring...and scoops Apples and drops him across the knee with a backbreaker...Slater off the ropes and drops a leg on Apples and covers..One.two..No!!! Apples just launched Slater off that pin attempt. Both men back up and Apples with a right hand...Slater with a right...And Apples jabs Slater in the eye with a thumb..classic amateur wrestling...Apples drags Slater to his corner and tags in Houston and elevates Slater with a Vertical Suplex...and drops him...Houston off the second rope with an elbow to the throat of Slater...Houston sees Harris standing on the apron looking stupid, so he whips Slater into his home corner and he tags in Harris... Houston locks up with Harris and takes him over with a snapmare and follows up quickly with a dropkick to the back of his head...Houston grabs Harris' chin...Reverse Rolling Neck snap..OUCH THAT is a vicious move by Houston...and another one...and he knows how to use it...Houston pulls up Harris and drops him with a Side Walk Slam... Houston up and whips Harris into the rope tags in Bill Bob, Houston drops down and Bill Bob nails Harris with a Missle Dropkick from the top rope...Bill Bob yanks up Harris by his hair and bounces off the ropes...Tirt-A-Whirl Headscissors by Bill Bob. Bill Bob with a cover..One.two..No!! Bill Bob pulled him off the mat...And he tags in Apples who looks like a man possessed and presses Harris over his head and drops him over the top rope and Harris crashes hard to the floor. Slater and Thunder into the ring and are brawling with Bill Bob and King Apples, meanwhile on the outside Houston sends Harris face first into the post...and then rams his head hard into the steps...Houston rolls the near unconscious Harris back into the ring....Apples pulls up Harris..He's setting up for the Applesauce Drop...BANG!!! He hit it...He covers..One..two..three!!! He got 'em....Wow The Brotherhood made it look relatively easy once Harris stepped into the ring....The Brotherhood are on the outside celebrating...Wait a minute look in the irng...Slater and Thunder are screaming at the fallen Harris...They're blaming him for the loss...Thunder pulls up Harris, Slater to the top rope..and leaps..Side Walk Slam/Guillotine legdrop... that's a side winder....Slater and Thunder leave the ring and they are heated...Wait ..The Brotherhood are going back into the ring..What are they doing?? Apparently this weekend, Harris interfered in a match with Apples...OH NO!!!! Apples has a chair and Houston and Bill Bob are holding Harris up...Apples off the ropes and winds back <CRACK> OH MY GOD!!! That's the hardest chair shot I've EVER SEEN IN MY LIFE... Harris looks like he's out...Apples drops the chair over the fallen Harris and Bill Bob goes to the top rope..and leaps...Spur onto the chair which crushes Harris' face.... Houston pulls up Harris and scoops him in a Fireman's Carry...Too Cold Driver on the chair!!!! They're trying to permanently injure Harris....Apples pulls up Harris and has him in a piledriver position..Bill Bob and Houston are on the second turnbuckle....TRIPLE SPIKE PILEDRIVER ..Harris' neck just crunched underneath him....Harris is out and in serious trouble.....Apples has him up again...Houston and Bill Bob leap off the top turnbuckle...another Triple Team Piledriver..This is sickening....Wait who is that??? It's Vinny Vitrone who enters the ring...Apples pulls up Harris yet again...Houston, and Bill Bob are in corner on the second turnbuckle..and Vitrone is on the adjacent turnbuckle ..they all leaps..QUADRUPLE TEAM SPIKE PILEDRIVER!!!!! I think they just killed the Kodiac....The Brotherhood exit the ring laughing almost hysterically as the medical unit make their way to check on the prone body of Harris... Here are your winners in 18:33, via Pinfall, Bill Bob Woijicowski, "The Ringmaster" Tony Houston, and "King" Apples.----The BROTHERHOOD!!!!! Brian Newark - Well ladies and gentlemen, we are out of time!!! What a great night of ACTION we had for you on MONDAY NIGHT HOT SPOT!!
