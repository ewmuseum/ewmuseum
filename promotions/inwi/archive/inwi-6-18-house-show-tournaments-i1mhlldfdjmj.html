INWI House Show - June 18, 1997 Killington, VT Tournament Special Show ================================================= Joseph Collins - Hello ladies and gentlemen and welcome back to another House Show Report! We had more matches from the Light Heavtweight Championship Tournament and more of the opening round of the Ringmaster Tournament! Let's get to those results! Also fans, check out the web site, it has been recently updated! The address is http://www.catskill.net/harmer . Check it out NOW!! ============================================================ Match 1 - Light Heavyweight Tournament Jimmy Cougar vs "Marvelous" Mike Quick In a stunning display of wild risk taking moves, Jimmy Cougar advanced. Quick looked like he was well on the way to victory when the ref was knocked out. Out of the stands came Bill Bob Woijicowski who DDT'd Quick on the floor outside the ring and then rolled him back into the ring. Cougar then covered him for the easy win in 14:23. Match 2 - Light Heavyweight Tournament "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset vs Awesome X Jonathan Sunset, who was the former Northeast Champion, was looking to get another gold belt. It will not happen in this tournament. Sunset was winning this match, but got clumsy as he scooped up Awesome X and was rolled up with a cradle for the three count at 9:57. Match 3 - Light Heavyweight Tournament David McCarter vs Steven Casper David McCarter, one of the hot newcomers into the INWI, went back and forth with ring vet Steven Casper throughout the match. Late in the match, McCarter sent Casper into the ropes and caught him with the sleeper. McCarter finished off the match in 16:31. Match 4 - Light Heavyweight Tournament Bill Bob Woijicowski vs "The Hollywood Kid" Jason Starr Woijicowski made his way to the ring with his Northeast TV title and he wants more gold. In a see saw matchup, both men went to the air and Woijicowski was setting up for the Spur. Jimmy Cougar, Starr's partner, got the attention of the ref and when that happened, Mike Quick appeared and cracked Woijicowski from the top rope with a crowbar. Starr coverd Bill Bob for the 1-2-3 and the win at 13:49. Match 5 - Ringmaster First Round The Springer vs Animosity The Springer kept up his hot streak and fought off Mike Garvey by having the Comforters at ringside and got the submission win over Animosity by using the coil. Time of the match was 11:28. Match 6 - Ringmaster First Round Mr. Cherry vs Cloud Eagle Mr. Cherry, who was trying to advance in two tournaments in two nights, felt defeat in the opening round of the Ringmaster. Cherry advanced in the Light Heavyweight Tourney the night before. Cloud Eagle advanced in the Ringmaster by pinning Mr. Cherry after he missed a Cherry Bomb moonsault at 13:53. Match 7 - Ringmaster First Round "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston vs Psycho Sal Commissioner Jerry DeLuise let Houston out of his suspension for one night to fulfill this commitment. Houston had a short time back as Sal was attacked by Bodyguard Jones as he came to the ring and then Jones tossed him into the ring. Houston played with Sal for a little while then pinned him after a Cold Drop at 4:23. Match 8 - Ringmaster First Round "The Icon" Pimp Daddy Brown vs Gino Rizzotto Mr. Banana didn't show up for the match, so Commissioner Jerry DeLuise threw him out of the tournament. He replaced Banana with Gino Rizzotto. Gino and Pimp Daddy were going at it pretty good when Vito Rizzotto showed up and distracted Gino. Gino was then put in the Pimp-a-plex and Brown scored the pinfall at 7:48. Match 9 - Ringmaster First Round "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder vs Dell Pickle Northeast Champion Dell Pickle suffered his second defeat in two nights. Thunder beat Pickle from the opening bell and scored the win following the Thunderbolt at 3:29. MAIN EVENT Match 10 - Tag Team Championships The Terminators vs The Fantastic Surfers The Terminators defended their titles successfully in front of the capasity crowd in the main event. The Terminators scored a pinfall victory over The Surfers at the 11:34 mark. ============================================================ Joseph Collins - Well, the first round of the Light Heavyweight Championship Tournament is in the bag and here are the matchups for the second round! Sam "The Minuteman" Adams vs Jimmy Chooley Jimmy Cougar vs "Hollywood Kid" Jason Starr Mr. Cherry vs Awesome X D.D. (Doctor of Disaster) vs David McCarter Well, for the INWI, I am Joseph Collins saying so long! ============================================================ =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The INWI "The revolutionary force in E-Wrestling" Visit us on the Internet: http://www.catskill.net/harmer For info, E-mail: [detail withheld] INWIŽ, June 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
