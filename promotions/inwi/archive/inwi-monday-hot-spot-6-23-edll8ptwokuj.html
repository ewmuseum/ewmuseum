[INWI] Monday Hot Spot!! 6-23

23 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
INWI Monday Night Hot Spot!! ======================== Monday, June 23, 1997 The Ugly Mug, Cape May, NJ ========================================================== Brian Newark - Welcome ladies and gentlemen to Monday Hot Spot!! What a card we have planned for you tonight. "The Technician" Zack Sharp receives his first ever title shot in the INWI as he faces Dell Pickle for the Northeast Championship. "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder takes on one of his toughest opponents to date in "Bulldozer" Tony Riley. We also have the two final quarterfinal matchups in the Light Heavyweight Championship Tournament. Let's go up to ringside for the opening match of the evening. Match 1 - Light Heavyweight Championship Tournament Mr. Cherry vs Awesome X These two enterend the ring and are exchanging blows right away. Cherry bounces off the ropes, leaps and nails a hurakanrana! Awesome X is dazed but is stumbling to his feet and Cherry nails a drop kick that sends Awesome X flying to the floor. Cherry climbs to the top rope and dives!! OH MY GOD! He just nailed a splash from the top to the floor and I think he may even be hurt after that fall! Cherry stumbles to his feet, grabs Awesome X and suplex's him on the outside. Cherry rolls in the ring, breaks the count and comes back out. Cherry over top of Awesome X, but X goes low and Cherry leans over in the pain. Awesome X just DDT'd Cherry on the outside!! Wow! Awesome X scoops up Cherry and rolls him back into the ring and climbs to the top rope. Cherry works his way to his feet and Awesome X dives.... yes! He nailed a diving lariat! Cherry just flipped over and he looks out. A cover by Awesome X, 1...2...NO! Cherry gets a shoulder up. Awesome X scoops up Cherry and sends him into the ropes and drop kicks him to the mat. Awesome X with a standing leg drop and another cover, 1...2..NO! Cherry again kicks out. Awesome X again up with Cherry, bodyslam and then he grabs him again! Cherry into the ropes, no a reversal! Awesome X bounces off and gets a spinning heel kick right in the face. Cherry and X are both up and tie it up. Cherry takes control and gets sent into the ropes but ducks on a clothesline and hits another spinning heel kick! Cherry is going up to the top rope...he's setting up for it...the Cherry Bomb Moonsault! Yes! He nailed it! The cover, 1....2....3!! It's over! Your winner in 23:24, Mr. Cherry! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Before our next match, which is the Northeast Championship match, I want to introduce to you one of the newest wrestlers to enter the INWI, "Too Slick" Rick Brown. Let's get comments from him now! ============================================================ (The scene is inside of the D.C. Athletic Gym, where the camera pans down threw various older rooms, including a boxing mat where two younger kids are sparring and into a few other side rooms where fans are running, since there's not any A/C in this building...As the camera moves from room to room you see "Too Slick" Rick Brown watching a stack of tapes from various INWI shows) Brown: *looking away from the camera* Man, some of these guys are impressive in this fed, a lot of good wrestlers.. (Brown stops the tape and ejects it as he turns to the cameras) Brown: INWI...Introduce yourself to "Too Slick" and very soon, you will see why you should be excited I'm here.. (Brown sits back in his chair and puts his feet up on the desk) Brown: The D.C. Gym, some of the greatest athletes have walked through these doors and I'm no exception to that as you will see, because "Too Slick" is coming to the world of INWI very soon and I GUARANTEE when I get there, you will see a wrestling style that will make you wanna say: "Oh ****"... (Brown puts his feet down and scuffles through a few more tapes) Brown: I know you guys wanna here more about "Too Slick" but I gotta study these tapes so I can be at tip top form when I get to your world and I know the fans of INWI will be glad a came....PEACE!.. (camera fades out as Brown sticks another tape in) ============================================================ Match 2 - Northeast Championship "The Technician" Zack Sharp vs Dell Pickle of the jWo This has the makings of being a great match...well sort of. Pickle, who hasn't won since winning this title, is a fast comp editor who gets away and keeps the title. He has a huge match this weekend for this Northeast Title against former champion Mr. Ed Rogers. Meanwhile, Zack Sharp, still undefeated, has a Heavyweight Title match against Brody Thunder this weekend at Heat Wave '97. There's the bell and we are underway. Sharp takes control, sends Pickle into the ropes and nails a hard clothesline. He then grabs Pickle and slams him to the mat, following that up with a couple of elbows and then a big knee. Sharp up with Pickle, snap suplex. He has him up again, sends him into the ropes and Sharp connects with a belly to belly suplex! Pickle looks hurt and some of the jWo has come down to ringside. Malvolio is up on the ring apron and Sharp dropkicks him off! Sharp back to Pickle and lays him on the top rope, he looks like he's setting up for a superplex.... NO! He just nailed the Ultimate Demise! There's the cover, 1...2...3!!!! YES!!! Can you believe it? Your winner in 5:24 and NEW INWI Northeast Champion, "The Technician" Zack Sharp!!! LOOK OUT!! Ed Rogers is in the ring and he has a chair!! He just nailed Zack Sharp on the back of the head and he is beating on the Technician. Rogers is going nuts and I guess I can see why, now his match at the PPV is not for a title! Rogers scatters from the ring as INWI officials come down..... wow.... ============================================================ Brian Newark - Next up will be another Quarterfinal of the INWI Light Heavyweight Tournament. But before that, let's see what the Springer had to say in an interview with Scoop Ropes. ============================================================ Scoop Ropes tries to get an interview with Springer. SR: Well Springer another opportunity for gold gone right down the toilet, any comments? Springer: Who gave you permission to come into my dressing room? You know that I only give interviews with Tiffany!!! Tell me how I got stuck with you?? Any way what do you want? SP: I asked you if you can afford to keep blowing these championship chances? Springer: EXCUSE ME, BLOW THESE CHANCES!!!!! ARE YOU OUT OF YOUR MIND??????? In all my matches I am in command until that coward comes in and hits me from behind!!!! The only blown chance was when I had the chance to end this fued once and for all!!!! This punk is beginning to get under my skin. SP: You must admit though that you have helped in keeping this fued alive haven't you?? Springer: Who the hell do you think your talking to???? Sorry to all my fans for the bad tounge. I have done nothing that any other normal human would have done. I fought back. I tried to be nice and just warn this punk, but no he continued to push all my buttons, SP: Willl we see this fued end any time soon? Springer: I think your asking the wrong person that question. This interview is over, and next time send Tiff, I don't like talking to you. ============================================================ Match 3 - Light Heavyweight Championship Tournament Jimmy Cougar vs "Hollywood Kid" Jason Starr I never thought I would see this happen. This has the makings of being a phenomenal match. Tag Team partners, still friends and ranked in the tag team ratings. The Hollywood Kid and Jimmy Cougar getting ready to lock 'em up. This should be a solid match. The two come to the center of the ring, shake hands and there's the bell. Starr and Cougar lock it up in the middle of the ring and it's a stale mate. Cougar bounces off the ropes, Starr over top of him and back he comes, Starr with a hip toss. Starr follows up with an elbow and then an arm bar. This is already a great one! Cougar works his way to his feet and sends Starr into the ropes and nails a clothesline. Cougar grabs Starr and slams him to the mat then drops a leg. Cougar to the middle turnbuckle and hits an elbow and then gets a two count. Cougar back up with Starr and sends him flying into the opposite corner and Charges. Starr moves and Cougar crashes into the ring post and he looks hurt. Starr picks up Cougar and hits a snap suplex and then bounces off the ropes and hits a high elbow drop. Starr for the cover, 1...2...NO! Cougar gets his shoulder up and Starr looks surprised! Starr picks up cougar and sends him into the ropes and flattens him with a diving lariat. Starr again sends Cougar into the ropes and theres a spinning heel kick that puts Cougar down. The cover, 1...2..NO!! Oh my god, how did Cougar kick out?? Starr now looks frustrated and kicks Cougar a couple of times. Starr bounces off the ropes and goes for a leg, but Cougar moves and Starr crashes to the mat! Cougar grabs Starr and sends him into the ropes and nails a knee to the midsection that flattens Starr. Again Starr is sent into the ropes..... double clothesline and both men are down! The ref starts the count! ....6.....7....8....Wait ....both men are up! Cougar locks in, belly to belly suplex!!! Both men are down again. They are exhausted! Cougar rolls a little and his arm is on Starr! The ref goes for the count, 1....2....3!! It's over and Jimmy Cougar has just won a HUGE match! Your winner in 34:46, Jimmy Cougar! ============================================================ Brian Newark - That match turned out to be something spectacular! Those two really went at it! Well, next up is our main event and before the match, let's get comments from the challenger, "Bulldozer" Tony Riley. ============================================================ [Screen cuts from coverage of the previous match, and only with a short chance for a brief explanation, and into the lockeroom area.] [The camera crew begin walking past various lockeroom's and then reaches a door which on the last door at the corridor a sign is visible. As the camera crew grows closer the message is revealed in Crimson Red lettering almost oozing down the wall...FATAL ALLIANCE.] [Several knocks at the door are unanswered but the fourth knock arises a voice.] Voice: Who is it?!? [After the camera crew announces their selves are invited into the lockeroom.] [Immediately upon entering two men are visible. One is "Cold & Calculating" Tony Houston. Houston is dressed in black Polo pants that are slightly sagging, a white INWI T-Shirt covered by a black leather vest. Houston is whispering something into "Bulldozer" Tony Riley's ear. Riley is set for his match and is dressed with black wrestling singlet, on the back in crimson red lettering reads BULLDOZER. Bulldozer is wearing black boots that are covered by leather shin guards, and two black gloves with crimson red on the knuckles and inside of the fingers, however two finger covers are missing on the middle and ring finger of Riley's right hand, are covering both of Riley's hands.] [Houston stops whispering and lifts his head] Houston: So ol ***** assed Brody Thunder wants to challenge the athleticism and intelligence of Cold & Calculating. First, of all I don't remind anybody of anybody, but when others are compared, they are compared to ME, son. First, you attack where I've been, I've told ya' son, Objectively, you would be raped in any of the leagues I've dominated. I give you your due, you are an outstanding wrestler, not quite as good as me or the "Dozer, but you are good. Then you go and attempt to challenge me, son, you've best concentrate your rage on this man. [Points to Bulldozer]. He's 6'11" 345 pounds of an keg of whoop ass, BEO-tch. As for that, you'd first have to defeat 'Dozer to get any credit as far as being in MY class of professionalism. And that ain't happenin'. And as far as your Heavyweight Title, ****..that ain't ****. I'm not even worried about that NOW. You see, son, I have plans before I strike, I unlike you judging by your words, stick to a plan, if you aren't in my Calculations..then you are safe from Tony Houston, however, son you keep pushing the wrong buttons on me, it'll be your ass. [Houston takes a step back and the huge Bulldozer rises and takes the mic from Houston.] Riley: [Pulling his hair into a pony tail his prematch ritual] BRODY You claim to be the best, son, but who the hell have you beat, son? A jWo member, and so other ****, you are yet to face the intensity and pure skill that is involved with the Fatal Alliance. *****, I unlike you speak little, and use my actions as the ring as words, and boy, just where do you think I earned the name Bulldozer *****. Yo' Cold let's be out son. [Camera fades] ============================================================ MAIN EVENT INWI HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder vs "Bulldozer" Tony Riley Hell of a debut, don't you think. Tony Riley could win the INWI Heavyweight Title in his debut match. Of course, that is only because Brody Thunder had an open challenge and Tony Houston accepted on behalf of Riley. This could be an interesting one and maybe Thunder won't go into Heat Wave '97 as the champion.... you never know. There's the bell and Thunder just glares at the big man on the other side of the ring. Thunder charges and tackles the big man and starts wailing on him. Tony Houston is at ringside so someone ought to watch him. Thunder scoops up Riley, sends him into the ropes and misses the clothesline, but Riley turns around and flattens the champ with a clothesline. Thunder is scooped by Riley and slammed hard to the mat. Riley looks at Houston and Houston gives the thumbs up. Riley goes back to work and grabs Thunder and there's a choke slam! Thunder could be done! Riley with the cover....1....2..NO! Thunder kicks out! Houston is upset on the outside and Riley is jabbing with the ref. Wait a minute.... Houston is in the ring and he is getting ready to Cold Drop Thunder....there's Ultimate Perversion! He nails Houston with a chair and they both fall out of the ring! Perversion just nailed Houston with the chair again! Riley sees this and wanders to the ropes. Thunder is up! He's up and ready. He grabs Riley....sets him up... OH MY GOD!! Thunder just nailed the Thunderbolt on Riley!! That was amazing! 1...2...3!! It's over! Your winner in 11:39 and still INWI Heavyweight Champion, "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - How amazing was that? What a great card and what a great main event! This was one of the best INWI Monday Hot Spot's in a long time and it's a shame we have to end it....but we do! Thursday in the Studio will be a shortened Version of our Normal show. There will only be TWO matches on that card and they will be both of the Semi-Final matchups for the Light Heavyweight Tournament! It's time to go, so for the rest of the INWI, I am Brian Newark saying so long from Cape May, NJ and see you this weekend at Madison Square Garden for Heat Wave'97!! ============================================================ HEAT WAVE '97 ONLY ON PAY-PER-VIEW JUNE 29,1997 ONLY ON PAY-PER-VIEW THE WAR BEGINS ANEW SHARP VS THUNDER AND THIS TIME.. ..IT'S NOT JUST FOR TITLES... ..IT'S FOR REAL. ============================================================ =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The INWI "The revolutionary force in E-Wrestling" Visit us on the Internet: http://www.catskill.net/harmer For info, E-mail: [detail withheld] INWIŽ, June 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
