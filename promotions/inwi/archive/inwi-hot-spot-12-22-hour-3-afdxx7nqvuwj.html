[INWI] Hot Spot, 12-22, Hour 3

30 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene fades from varying infomercials and returns to a suburban park that has been hit heavy, in southern New York. On one of the snow covered benches rests a man who has an extremely look about him. This guy is relatively short, however, is dressed for a night in the Rotten Apple.] [This man lifts his head, and is revealed to be founder, and former N.E.W. C.E.O. before leaving for reasons of his own, Shorty Rok. He notices the camera and it's crew drawing closer and slowly reaches into his left coat pocket and produces a small, yet purpose serving tape recorder.] Shorty: Well..even before I get started let me just say that these are opinions expressed from ME, from MY mind and not the opinions of anybody else in the INWI, the NEW or anybody..but me .In the recent hours of my retirement from running any leagues, I have heard some statements by people, who to say the least have had ego problems...I've been attacked by a former friend, Tony Houston, as well as the unenviable task of having to listen to an extremely 'Old Fella. Now I've taped these little comments and find it best served to conduct this interview here..in the cold..Because that's exactly how I intend to respond. Bloom you want to bring me into the fold with your problems with The Brotherhood by insulting my way of running things...well let's just let the people hear some of your interview..and I'll have responses of my own. [Shorty pushes the play button his tape recorder] DESIRE: N.E.W. improves everyday and in every way. I think the front office didn't have their minds together at first. But once Shorty Rok went splitsville and went to that other promotion the N.E.W. has improved tremendously. The cards are better, the quality is better. People have their **** together, and the membership number increases daily. Ever since you became in power President GoodFella, which seemed like a lot longer before it was "official," the N.E.W. has improved daily. GOODFELLA: Thank you for the compliments. It sounds like you have a problem with Shorty Rok? DESIRE: No. But I think some measure of respect was lost when he quit because he was "too tired," then I found out he's working for the INWI. He was a passive President, and I think his direction and your direction President GoodFella conflicted at times. Politics were sometimes played. However, under your control, I think things are running smoothly and more efficiently. [Shorty in almost a fit of anger hits pause.] Shorty: Whoa!!! Right there ol' Bloomy gus. Who the hell are you to comment on the who's, the what's or even the why's of me departure. As far as me working for the INWI? First, not that it's any of your business, but where did you get that impression? Am I at any events? Damnit, you aren't even at any of the events.. I was a passive president, you might say...well at times perhaps, perhaps I was, but as the CEO of the N.E.W. And SOLE Founder, first I should not need to baby sit the athletes here, and second that is what a vice president IS for. Isn't it? Let's hear what other ignorant comments this fool spewed from his power hungry, delusional mind. [Shorty "undoes" the pause button] GOODFELLA: So do you think N.E.W. is better off with me in command? DESIRE: God yes. You see, the thing is, you work with the members. You help them improve their interviewing skills, making them become better wrestlers. Look at James Pillow, Dog with Rabies, Tommy Chaos, Vinny Vitrone, Brass Knuckles, ARMAGEDDON, and the list goes on, they've all improved their "interviewing" skills. Why? Because you got involved with them GoodFella. You get in their face and let them now how to become a better wrestler, and then give them the time to get better. [Shorty again hits pause] Shorty: ERRRRRRR Hold the frig up...Is this ego maniac saying that GoodFella built this league? I guess the members have nothing say about. Those know I can care less of what people think about me, but I was never...ever...ever one to say that I am responsible for building careers..But since you want to bring it up...Let's see..who signed James Pillow..who signed Vinny Vitrone, who signed Tommy Chaos...I do believe it was me...GoodFella helped Vitrone...so that is why he's part of The Brotherhood now eh?? Imagine that. [Shorty hits play again] GOODFELLA: The INWI is a big issue recently. You had a very short stint in the INWI. Recently, "King" Apples and Tony Houston have likened your quitting the INWI to their quitting of the N.E.W.. Would you like to explain the whole story? DESIRE: Yes, because it's about time their propaganda ended. Apparently, my campaign of Houston and Apples quitting because their worried too much about their character credibility and because they don't have balls has concerned them to the point of bringing up my INWI stint. Let me explain this. We'll call the President of the INWI "Jerry DeLuise." DeLuise and I discussed a working deal. It would be part-time, and it would inter-act with the N.E.W.'s story line between myself and the Brotherhood. I would go in there, build up some heat, and wrestle only the Brotherhood which would advance the feud. Apparently, DeLuise produced a hemmoroid out of this, saying I had to work my way up the ladder. Not the original deal, but I live and let live and I worked my way up the ladder. I didn't mind, although I have seen rookies get instant feuds with the Brotherhood, but we won't go there. Secondly, and most importantly, is the movie. As you know, my movie "In the Ring" comes out to theaters on Christmas Eve. Well, apparently, I wasn't allowed to do "interviews" from the set of the movie. When I asked why I could not do any interviews from the set of the movie, he basically gave "the committee doesn't like it" as his "great excuse" and told me that if I did an interview, I would "be attacked." Literally send thugs just because I would do an interview from the set of the movie. Well, because I felt this was censorship, I left the INWI. Censorship sucks, and this is America, not Communism, and I think that's why Sam Adams is over in the INWI..... end the Communism over there. As far as I know, this is America, and I can do anything I want. [Pauses] Shorty: Now Bloomy waste, I cannot speak for what the commish told you, however, aren't you the same man who claims he will never wrestle outside the N.E.W., well like an adulter, if it's done once, he more than likely will happen again..and Bloom how's the Infected Kiss?? That rash go away? However, as for Houston, I can't stand the man, and frankly, Apples is no better but check this, though the attack I received I have continued to follow the career of Tony Houston...you say a major feud with rookies? You don't want to go there?? Well, the only time I have seen Houston in the ring with a rookie was for a match? But I guess in your and GoodFella's warped mind...Whoa Whoa!! Wait a minute....GoodFella...Bloom's a Fella..The Fella even when they are not around seem to be pushed to superstar status...a Good Fella..GoodFella NEW President..Hmmm...could it be? And speaking of OldFella, and yourself making references to some politics? Well let's take a look at that? IF there was politics involved would you have been a champion and NOT Tony Houston whom I was close with at the time? Just how in the hell, with you being in only two leagues for short periods of times, did you become a NEW "Legend" you claim to be screwed by politics..but before you do, try looking in the mirror and checking what got you there. Other than Jen's services..you want to know the real reason she left you? It had nothing to do with you and Infected Kiss...IT's probably not even your damn kid. {Unpauses} GOODFELLA: Do you feel you're quitting over censorship and Apples' and Houston's quitting the N.E.W. are different? DESIRE: Drastically different. One's freedom is way different than worrying about your credibility because of a body slam, a loss, and just being plain out right scared. Shorty: [speaking over tape] Yeah that's why a 243 guy BLOOM can do a Spinning leg drop off the top rope and then slam a 650lbs man right? And try to justify it. GOODFELLA: So, what do you think are the differences between INWI and N.E.W.? DESIRE: My buddy Kodiac Dan Harris said it right.... quality. That's the name of the game folks. INWI changes angles every week, and thinks moves like the "tombswirly" is a work of art. Meanwhile, the N.E.W. gives you the best every week, each week, and it's an in your face attitude. Number two, we don't bury people. I don't care if they aren't consistent "interviewers" or not. When you take bread off the table, it's wrong. How can they sleep at night? I saw what they did to Kodiac, and it made my stomach turn. Number three, the quality of the members. I think, if you compare list to list, N.E.W. has a better roster depth than the INWI. Especially with the many recent additions to the N.E.W.. [pause] Shorty: Best every week or whatever...hell yeah, but since I left how come all the television channels never seem to be able to put out Global Warfare out in a timely manner.??? But hey, it's a better league..sure you can break your word..but look at the two..First, Bloom comes to me and OldFella BEGGING for a retirement...I naturally found it to be absurd..BUT you lost and should have retired..But you went back on your word and are now wrestling? Wrestling fans don't forget That easy....you seem to think fans are stupid...now they may be..but their memories do not forget THAT quickly. And GoodFella seemingly goes back on his word damn near every week, by claiming Global Wafare will be out on Monday, and then some how it gets tape delayed and pushed back to Tuesday at the earliest. See Bloom I can 'shoot' without breaking kayfabe..something you really need to work on. [unpause] GOODFELLA: Yeah, but they got Houston, Apples, Vitrone, and Pillow! DESIRE: So? They're all N.E.W. products. Look at it this way..... N.E.W. made them all what they are. They all had their "big shot" in the N.E.W.. So, when I compare the lists, I don't worry about them. The only product of the INWI's that I really, really like is Bill Bob Woijicowski. So, who are the INWI products? Brody Thunder, Ed Rogers, Sam Adams, Michael Bane. The rest are good, but not overwhelmingly great. Thunder is on hiatus, and Ed Rogers and Adams are the two best regulars that are INWI products. N.E.W. products go deeper. I might give the nod to INWI for about the first top five guys on the list, but as you go down the list, our line-up is deeper. We can go with anybody as the leader. We have more of a future too..... Polo Stetson, Assaf Levavy, Patty Babes Carter, Terence Ali, Balial, Matt Flash, "Stunning" Steve Miller, Adam Beckman are all the future stars of tomorrow. The INWI is getting away with an angle that N.E.W. started, the Brotherhood, with wrestlers who are original N.E.W. products. INWI claims to be the originators? Don't think so. And while they're worrying about the angle that N.E.W. started, they're not concerned about bringing up their prospects. [pause] Shorty: Again, I'm not in a position to speak for the INWI, except, don't play with fire Bloom. As for Stetson.....let's just say he looks REAL familiar....give him an accent and a different name, but he's the same person. You know what I mean, you know you've tried to deny it..but I know..I KNOW. As for the INWI using a NEW angle? Well, who said it was? If I believe correctly it was Tony Houston, Mr. Flamboyant and King Apples who put this idea of a Brotherhood to you..and secondly, it then was MY N.E.W. so you have no say in it..but then you are only a wrestler and who are you to say a damn thing about anything? Which brings me to another point..you wanna make comments about stealing ideas...well what about the entire NEW...it Was MY CREATION and not one person in the N.E.W. can deny that..and no matter what is said..I am still a FOUNDER!!!!! and making money of YOU..so Bloomy boy go make me some more money. GOODFELLA: I'm going to ask a question, which will be the last question on the INWI, Apples, and Houston. "King" Apples is making great claims about beating you and retiring you. What are your feelings? DESIRE: The thing about myself is that no matter how many times I get knocked down, no matter how many times I get beaten.... I get right back up. First off, let me just say this.... on that fateful night in August, Apples beat me. Nothing else to it. I'm not going to cry or quit like Apples anytime he's faced with a loss. See, because Apples has it wrong. He thinks that when he beat me, I lost everything. I didn't. He thinks by beating me, I got weaker, and my life was lost.... I only got stronger. You see, I don't mind getting knocked down. Because I get right back up. I've suffered only three losses in my entire career...... Ricky "Dragon" Steamboat, "Bullywug" Glenn Hudson, and "King" Apples. Did I cry, or head for the hills? Nope. I'll admit, Apples has caught on faster than anyone I know. Early in my title reign, I would have killed him in the ring. I think when I lost though, it was actually better for me in the long run... it gave me a chance to start a new career as an actor, and it also gave me time to spend trying to make a family. It was actually a blessing in disguise. GOODFELLA: Do you think if you wrestled against "King" Apples again, do you think you would win? DESIRE: Yes I do. I think a lot of internal politics and outside interests and hobbies prevented me from winning the last time. However, I don't think the match will ever take place. GOODFELLA: Why not? DESIRE: Because of politics. I wouldn't dare face the politics within the INWI and the political influence of "King" Apples in the INWI.. I also doubt he'll come to N.E.W. to wrestle me. And I do not want to wrestle for anyone or anywhere else than the N.E.W.. GOODFELLA: Do you respect Tony Houston and "King" Apples? Do you think they are high quality wrestlers? DESIRE: Yes, I respect them and I think they are some of the best names in the sport. However, our personalities conflict. And in addition to that, their actions have also disturbed me at times, which is why I'm brutally honest here. GOODFELLA: Let's switch gears for a second. Who do you see as the next "Legends of the N.E.W.?" DESIRE: One last comment on Apples, I didn't think he would be the next "Legend of the N.E.W.." I knew he wouldn't, and never will as long as I'm here. However, there's a ton of prospects, which makes it interesting. I think Bill Bob Woijicowski has a great chance. Assaf Levavy, who interestingly enough comes in here claiming he's a Legend, which remains to be seen. I think "Stunning" Steve Miller is another great prospect. "Kodiac" Dan Harris, who has never been Heavyweight Champion. [Shorty hits stop] Shorty: Well you are right..you did lose to Apples..Cleanly I might add....no politics...no arguments..you were pinned..it was your idea..and it blew up in your face. you claim it's made you stronger..well how so?? Since your retirement what have you done? Made a movie that was only out for 2 1/2 hours and made only 13.50 because your parents went to see it...then you break your word about not wrestling anymore...and in doing so, broken your ho's heart. Steve Miller? Well, we've booted him before being inactive..he's no legend, icon, and realistically not even a threat. Harris...well....apparently nothing is important to him..if he cares not about the IC strap how concerned do you really think he is with you? Despite what he says. As for a legend of the N.E.W.? Well you are in fact looking at him..I made you Bloom, sounds irritating..now you know how everybody else in the league feels when you claim the NEW built them..but one think I DID build...THE NEW. What he said about the INWI was a bunch of crap. We have more than five talented wrestlers and I am not going to list because I personally think every wrestler here is a good wrestler. Our roster is solid and we keep it small so each person in the federation can shine. We don't allow 45 or more wrestlers to come in just because they want in. Wrestlers are screened so that we can give the highest quality product that we can. As far as the Brotherhoods comments on the NEW and what ever else they speak on, it is their opinion. There is no need for a man who left the INWI because that we did not want to "advertise" a movie and that he lost a match to bad mouth us. As for this GoodFella, he has never been and never will be part of the INWI. The INWI and NEW are two different federations and go in different directions. The INWI is a more upbeat federation and we all have a good time and get laughs. Everyone in this federation is very talented and deserve the pushes they get. As for the NEW I don't know them well enough. But they are more serious in their angles and everything else. They are more of a soap opera. Both federations are unique and that is good as each leagues wrestlers enjoy where they are. The slander and the "shoots" are getting out of hand and it has become evident that the NEW is just really out to bad mouth the INWI. We have got word from very reliable sources that the heads of NEW have tried to get wrestlers to bad mouth and beriddle the INWI. It is a childish act and the INWI has decided to cut all ties with the NEW. The inter fed angle that was tried was the WORST angle ever tried and to this day the INWI regrets ever trying it. The INWI will survive with or without the beriddling of the NEW. As far as we are concerned, the NEW no longer exists. We are sure that some people mat want to comment of the situation that is going on, but other than a rare comment from disgruntled people or employees, the INWI will no longer stoop to this level. We are the INWI and we are our own federation. The NEW will keep trying, but they know the truth. We are quality on time and we will always be. This matter is closed now and let's hope that the acts of crap on both sides will end and each federation can move on and prosper. The inner fed angle is over and will never be back and now it can rest. Let's each move on and end it. Again, these were my thoughts and not those of the INWI or anyone else.
