[INWI] SuperCard - Part 3

28 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
============================================================ Brian Newark - Before our next match, we have comments from Jason "The Crusher" Chandler, who is up next against Vito Rizzotto! I believe this was taped as he entered the Arena tonight. ============================================================ [standing right outside the entrance to the arena...Chandler walks up and grabs a mic and stares into the camera.] Chandler: Why in the hell do I gotta face this ***** here tonight? I should be facing a champion! Vito Rizzotto..His mom used to be my maid, until I caught her stealing my Spaghettios! Weren't you a character on Grease? One of the T-Birds or something? Well, I know this much. I ain't heard of you, but by the end of you, you won't be able to forget my name. You are just a stepping stone on my path to gaining belts for the NBK! And to anyone who might try to get in my way of total dominance, just remember...The rest of the NBK are watching my back, so don't forget it! Now get out of my face and watch me kick his ***! [walks out..] Chandler...Vito...Vito...Tito....Vito...where are you? Come get your *** kicked... ============================================================ SuperCard Vito Rizzotto vs Jason "The Crusher" Chandler Here's a match that both need to win. Rizzotto, the former INWI Television Champion, is trying to regain some gold. Meanwhile, Jason Chandler is trying to get some respect and at the same time get some gold. There's the bell and we are underway. Rizzotto circles Chandler and strikes taking out the knees. Rizzotto puts on a spinning toe hold then drops an elbow down at the top of the legs. Ouch!! Rizzotto gets up and laughs at the crowd and goes back to work, scooping up Chandler and slamming him to the mat. Rizzotto drops an elbow then scoops up Chandler and sends him out of the ring. Rizzotto climbs out to the apron and leaps but Chandler catches him!! Bodyslam on the concrete!! Chandler, still staggering a little, grabs Rizzotto and sends him into the ring rail. Rizzotto is hurt as he is limp on the railing. Chandler charges but Vito's brother, Gino steps in and nails Chandler with a chair. The ref calls for the bell and Gino is beating on Chandler with the chair. Vito finally gets up and he joins in this beating. They are beating on Chandler real bad and here comes members of Chandler's group it's Oz... and the Rizzotto's clear out. Chandler is broken open at ringside and the Rizzottos are laughing as they walk back to the lockerroom. Your winner in 10:32 as a result of a disqualification, Jason "The Crusher" Chandler!! SuperCard Bodyguard Jones vs Psycho Sal This fued is getting bad and these two just want to beat the hell out of eachother. Bodyguard Jones, formerly in the Stable with Sal and manager Mean Eugene didn't get any respect from them and they had a falling out. Sal charges Jones, but he slips out of the way and is pounding Sal in the corner. Wow, Jones is really taking it to Sal and then he flips off his former manager Mean Eugene. Jones heads to the opposite turnbuckle and charges, but Sal puts up his big boot and smacks Jones right in the mush. Jones falls to the mat and Sal comes out with a crushing leg drop that really hit the spot. Sal scoops up Jones and fires him out of the ring then ties up the ref. Oh cut this out, Eugene is giving some boots of his own to his former bodyguard and this is disgusting! Sal finally comes out and picks up Jones and slams him to the concrete then rolls him back into the ring. Sal follows back in but he is doing it with attitude. Sal again picks up Jones and slams him to the mat and then heads for the ropes. Jones is trying to get to his feet and has. Sal is just getting to the top rope and Jones shakes the top rope and Sal lands in an uncomfortable way. Jones is gaining the momentum and gets up top. He starts pounding away at Sal as the crowd counts along all the way to 10. Sal is signaling for something.... and he sets up for a suplex! There's a superplex from the top rope and Jones for a cover. 1...2...NO!! Jones goes to the top himself and hits an elbow drop then he is going for something...it's a boston crab?? That's not his normal finisher but it could work! It does!!! Sal gives up!!! This one is history as your winner in 12:34, Bodyguard Jones!!! Watch out!! Here comes Mean Eugene, and Raver... and Jones rolls out of the ring flipping them off. The crowd is going nuts! SuperCard Three Way Dance INWI Television Championship Mr. Apples vs Mr. Ed Rogers vs Firestar This should be one of the best matches of the night!! Three men in the ring at the same time looking to walk away with the Television Title! There's the bell and we are underway and all three are just looking at eachother to see who will make the first move. There it is! Apples attacks Rogers and this match is now raring up! Apples pops Rogers and puts him on his back and clotheslines Firestar who was attacking. Now it's Apples and Firestar, but Rogers gets up and attacks Apples and these two are going at it again. You know any chance these two get to go at it, they will. Rogers nails Apples in the throat and DDT's him to the mat. Firestar then nails Rogers with a big boot and down goes Rogers. Firestar gets both Apples and Rogers up and hits a double noggin knocker. Apples tumbles out of the ring and Firestar scoops up Rogers and sends him into the turnbucke with a snake eyes! Rogers crumbles to the mat and Firestar goes for the cover, 1..2...NO!! Apples comes back in the ring and kicks Firestar as he's stands up and that sends the masked man out of the ring. Apples climbs on top of Rogers and starts to pound on him. What a match. Firestar climbs to the top and goes for a leg on Apples, but he moves and Firestar nails Rogers, but he didn't land right and he looks hurt. Apples grabs Firestar, but Firestar goes low and puts Apples on the mat. Firestar goes over to Apples and sets him up, yes!! He nailed the Burned star. Firestar falls ontop of Apples but Rogers comes over and nails Firestar with an elbow to the back of the head. Why the heck would that madman break it up? Apples would of probably been done! Rogers grabs Firestar and hits the Puppet Master!! 1...2...3!!!! At 11:32, Firestar has been eliminated!!! Man, Firestar is going to be some mad about this one. Rogers goes over to Apples and picks him up and hits a body slam then drops a knee to the head. Apples looks like he's cut open and Rogers climbs on top and starts to pound on him. I don't think Rogers cares about the Title, he wants to seriously hurt Apples. Rogers scoops up Apples and sends him into the ropes and goes for a backdrop, but Apples goes to his knees and nails Rogers with a punch to the face. Apples then picks up Rogers and hits him with a neck breaker then a short armed clothesline. He signals to the crowd and sends Rogers into the ropes and kicks him in the midsection. Here it comes, the Applesauce Drop! NO!! Rogers blocks it and hits the Applesauce Drop on Apples!! There's the cover 1...2...NO!! Apples got his foot on the rope!!! WOW!! What a match!! Rogers scoops up Apples and sends him into the ropes and down he goes again! Apples puts on the brakes and there's the Applesauce Drop!! 1...2...3!!! It's over!! Your winner in 21:32 and still INWI Television Champion, Mr. Apples!!! What a great match!! This one will be hard to top! SuperCard SUPER Grudge Match PO Box vs Jimmy Cougar I think these two may hate eachother more than Apples and Rogers and this should be another wild match. Both men go to the middle of the ring and start to slug it out! PO Box lands a solid right that floors Cougar and he lifts him up and connects with a short armed clothesline. Box grabs Cougar and takes him to the corner where he rams his head into the turnbuckle 10 times as the crowd chants along. Cougar stumbles out of the corner and falls flat on his face and Box nails an elbow to the back of the head. Cougar looks out, but you know these Glamour Boys always have something up their sleeves. Cougar starts to work to all fours, but Box nails a leg drop to the back of the neck to floor Cougar again. PO Box scoops up the lifeless Cougar and sends him out to the floor and follows out. Box picks up Cogar and looks like he is going to bodyslam him but instead slams him into the ring post then drops him on the steel steps. PO Box is rubbing Cougar's head into the ring post and I think Cougar is cut open! Yes he is and it is a gusher! Box tosses Cougar back in the ring before the 10 count and heads to the top rope! Wow, this is different! Box leaps but Cougar gets his leg up and nails PO Box right in the face. Cougar staggers to his feet and hits a knee in PO Box's face to floorhim again. Now Cougar tosses Box out of the ring and he follows him out. Cougar lands a dropkick on the outside to floor Box, then picks him up and suplex's him on top of a folded chair. Cougar sends Box back in the ring and heads to the top rope. Box is up and sees it and catches Cougar and slams him!! He signals to the crowd and sets up...yes, it's the final delivery!!!! 1...2....3!!!!!! PO BOX has just won!! Your winner in 15:47, PO Box!! Lookout, here comes Jason Starr and he has a chair and nails PO Box! Oh my, I guess this fued isn't ending with this match! Starr helps Cougar out of the ring as Box trys to figure out what just happened. SuperCard United States Championship Match "Hardcore" John Stilwater vs "The Hollywood Kid" Jason Starr This is a rematch between the new champ and the former champion and this could be a wild match. I think the reason Starr nailed PO Box was to hope he was down and out so he couldn't have a part in this match. There's the bell and this match is underway. Stilwater attacks and Starr sidesteps him and Stilwater goes crashing into the turnbuckle. Stilwater follows with a series of lefts and rights and floors Starr. Starr pops Stilwater low and sends him to the mat with a kick to the face. Starr follows with a series of elbows and knees and then nails a legdrop and goes for a cover. Starr got a two count as Stilwater got his shoulder up and Starr continues to pummel the champion as he gets on top of him and is using Stilwater for a punching bag. The ref pulls Starr off of Stilwater, but Starr gives the ref a shove and starts pounding on Stilwater again. Once again the ref pulls Starr off and now they are bickering back and forth with eachother. Obviously the referee has the upper hand because he could disqualify Starr at any time because he keeps touching him. Starr turns around and Stilwater is up! He kicks Starr in the gut then nails the DDT!!! 1...2...3!! Your winner in 9:34 and still INWI United States Champion, "Hardcore" John Stilwater!! Wait, here comes Jimmy Cougar and he is still bloody from the last match. He nails Stilwater with a chair and keeps on doing it. Starr gets up and applys the Starlock. Stilwater is out cold. Starr grabs the U.S. Belt and puts it around his waist and leaves the ring. Starr has just taken the U.S. Title belt and I don't think he plans on giving it back to soon. ============================================================ Brian Newark - Man have we had some wild matches so far and we have two left. Next up is the Tag Team Championship match and we'll get words from the champions, The Big Brothers. ============================================================ *Micheal Pontiff is standing in the back with the current Tag Team Champions. The interview is being shown on the big jumbotron hanging from the ceiling* Micheal: Thank you very much. I'm standing back here with two REALLY big men, the Big Brothers! *crowd cheers* Micheal: Now you guys have a really BIG challenge tonight, no pun intended, as you face the Comforters who I would say are looking extremely sharp these days. Hunter: Comforters, listen up....me and Steven have worked too hard and too long to lose the belts this soon. We plan on staying the Tag Team Champions for a long time and if you think you're gonna take these belts away from us, then you've got another thing coming! Steven: That's right Hunter, Comforters, you're nothing but two oversized pitiful excuses for mattresses....and there's no way we're gonna lose the belts tonight to the likes of you! Me and my brother didn't grow up to be losers, and the only thing I see coming out of tonight is me and Hunter keeping the Tag Team titles. Micheal: Now guys, what's gonna happen if all of a sudden, you get caught in the Seely Posturpedic?!?!? Steven: First of all, the name of that move is too silly to even affect us! *chuckles* You two just don't size up to the size and mass of the Big Brothers, and when you get caught in the Big Splash, back to the end of the line you go....because the Big Brothers are staying at the top for a long time coming! Micheal: Thank you very much, and good luck tonight! ============================================================ SuperCard Tag Team Championship Match The Big Brothers vs The Comforters The Comforters have earned this title shot, but not many teams have been able to keep with Hunter and Steven, the Big Brothers. These two have been unreal since claiming the INWI Tag Team straps and they don't plan on giving them up to soon. It will be Hunter and Boxspring starting things out and we are set to go. There's the bell and we are going at it. Hunter slugs Boxspring and Boxspring returns the favor. These two big men are going at it in the center of the ring and Hunter floors Boxspring with a clothesline and then nails an elbow. Hunter tags in Steven and Steven picks up where Hunter left off. Steven works on the back of Boxspring and then applys a bearhug. Mattress hops in and dropkicks the back of Boxspring and he falls on top of Steven. The Comforters got a two count there and we almost had new champs. Steven grabs both Comforters and nails a double noggin knocker and both men fall down to the mat. Mattress staggers back up and drop kicks Steven and staggers him. Mattress and Boxspring execute a double clothesline and floor Steven. The Comforters are going crazy and theres a double elbow drop. Here comes Hunter and he chokeslams Mattress. Hunter then presses Mattress and sends him over the top rope and on top of a table. Steven then sends Boxspring into the turnbuckle and Hunter follows for the Big Splash!! Here's the cover, 1...2...3!!! It's over, your winners in 10:21 and still INWI Tag Team Champions, The Big Brothers!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Are you all ready? This is the match we have been waiting for! The match for the INWI Heavyweight Title! Brody Thunder has some comments in the back, so lets get them and then lets rumble!! ============================================================ [Brody Thunder paces in the hallway awaiting his match with Pimp Daddy Brown. He appears very focused and anxious as he wrings his hands. He looks up to see that the camera is on and walks directly towards it,stopping about five feet away.] Hey! C'mere with that thing! I got me a message fer that paper champion o' the INWI. Brown... In jus' a few short minutes yer gonna step in that squared circle with me. No more talkin'...no more posturin'...no more speculatin'. We're gonna hook'em up in the biggest match the INWI has ever seen. But it won't jus' another wrestlin' match... ...it's gonna be a war. You know it...I know it..the INWI knows it. An' when the dust settles two things are gonna be true that ain't true now.One is that them folks out there will have seen one helluva scrap an' two... ...I WILL be the new INWI Champion. That's my destiny,Brown.....an' there ain't anything on this godforsaken planet that's gonna stop me from reachin' it. Ya ain't the first champion I'll beat...yer jus' the next. Get ready ta sweat an'bleed "Icon"...that's what's waitin' fer ya in that ring tonight courtesy o' the one man who can deliver it... ..."Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder. Now get outta my way.... [Thunder bruses past the camera man almost knocking down in the process of getting to the ring.] ============================================================ SUPERCARD MAIN EVENT Steel Cage Match INWI Heavyweight Championship "The Icon" Pimp Daddy Brown vs "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder Well ladies and Gentlemen, this is what we have been waiting for! This is the Main Event of SuperCard and it is for the INWI Heavyweight Title. "The Icon" pimp Daddy Brown is facing his toughest challenge as champion in "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder. The cage is up, the two are in the ring and we are set to go. There's the bell and we don't have to wait any longer!! Brown and Thunder come to the middle and they are jawwing at eachother. God I would love to know what they are saying! Thunder starts this match off as he slaps Brown and Thunder returns the gesture. Now they are exchanging punches and "The Icon" floors Thunder! Brown gets on top of him and punches him then picks him up and slams him into the cage. Thunder looks like he has a little crimson flowing already as Brown keeps slamming his head into the cage. Brown stops and Thunder falls to the mat and Brown slaps on a scorpion!! Thunder is screaming in pain, but he won't give up! Brown slams on his back and Thunder goes back down to the mat and Brown drops an elbow. Thunder is crawling to the ropes to try and get up, but Brown comes with a dropkick to the back of the knee of Thunder. He goes to grab Thunder, but the "Lone Wolf" pokes him in the eye and Brown backs up trying to gain his sight back. Thunder is trying to get feeling back into his leg and is now kicking Brown. Thunder hits a DDT and gets a two count. Now it's Brown that is on the bad end of this fight. Thunder sends Brown into the cage again and again! Brown falls to the mat and Thunder picks him up and sends him in the turnbuckle and follows with a flying elbow. Thunder looks over the situation and drops a knee on Brown's leg. It looks as though Brody wants to take out the leg of Brown. Thunder has just put the figure four on Brown. Brown won't give up and he sits up and thumbs Thunder's eye and that breaks the hold. Thunder gets up as Brown does and there's a double clothesline!! Both men are down in the middle of the ring and the two are trying to get up. Brown is up first and he heads for the door and now Thunder is getting up!! Brown is almost at the door, but Thunder grabs him from behind and hoists him up, what is this? It's the Thunderbolt!!! 1...2...3!!! Oh my god!! We have a new champ!! Your winner in 14:37 and NEW INWI Heavyweight Champion, the "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder!!! Thunder leaves the ring with his new belt and Brown is just sitting in the ring looking stunned. I'm sure this isn't the last we will see of these two!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - WOW!!! SuperCard I is now history and what a card it was! We have a couple of new champions and Commissioner DeLuise will release a statement later about Brody Thunder's Northeast Title. I don't think Thunder cares about that one now, he has the Heavyweight strap! Well, we are now out of time!! We want to thank FOX for putting us on live and for the rest of the INWI, I am Brian Newark saying good night!!! ============================================================ This was a presentation of the INWI, April 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= INWI Wrestling.....
