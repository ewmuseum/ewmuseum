[In the background of the locker room sound bites from the previous nights Inside the ropes can be heard first is the voice of Mr.Cherry whom Bill Bob Woijicowski has just defeated for the belt] [CHERRY: As for you Bill Bob... you claim you're so good. You screw around with a former friend of mine. And MORON, Apples is not my BROTHER he was a FRIEND. I realize where you're from there's no difference because you are all related, but I assure you we don't go that way up here. So you stupid red neck, I'll see you on Hot Spot.] Voice:Spoke a little too soon didn't we Cherry? [Next a clip from James Pillow can be heard] [PILLOW:As for you Woijicowski, you piece of Brotherhood trash. Beating you is going to be the most enjoyable of them all. Once Houston and Apples see you get your ass kicked by me do you think that they're going to say "Oh that's ok you had a bad night" I don't think so, you'll be planted right on your head faster then you can blink.] Voice: You are next Pillow...and as for Brotherhood trash...you are about to become litter...as I throw your sorry ass all over the ring with my fellow brothers looking on. What you don't seem to understand is that I am Brotherhood 4 Life and you are nothing to me... [The camera focuses in on the location of the voice...It is Bill Bob Woijicowski fresh off his win against Mr.Cherry for the Light Heavyweight belt...he stands amid a crowd with the belt draped across his shoulder...He is the new champ and he will not have to wait long to defend it as he will face James Pillow shortly.] BBW: Pillowbiter why don't you just shut yer' damn mouth and come and try to get what you want...I promise I wont bite...but Jake might... [With that Jake attacks the camera furiously and then it fades into black.] Brian Newark - Bill Bob Woijicowski is the NEW INWI Light Heavyweight Champion and he just made his thoughts clear about what he plans on doing to James "Simply The Best" Pillow later tonight in Hour Number Three. Pillow will be going for his second reign as the INWI Light Heavyweight Champion. That will prove to be one of the best Light Heavyweight matches in the INWI. Let's head back to the ring as Jerry DeLuise has a mic and is in the ring! [Commissioner Jerry DeLuise stands in the ring and he has a mic. DeLuise has an INWI suit on with the INWI logo over his left chest pocket. He has a tie with red white and blue letters on it that says "U.S.A." ] DeLuise - So this supposed General thinks he is going to take my job as Commissioner? And he thinks he is going to take over the INWI? I think not! [Crowd pops!!] Well, I have some BIG news for Mr. Adams. I have personally gone out and found someone who is a true American. This person backs America and ALL of her people ONE HUNDRED PERCENT!! [Another crowd pop and a chant of USA USA!!] Ladies and gentlemen.... let me introduce to you.... PETE ROBINSON!!!! ("America the Beautiful" begins to play as Pete Robinson comes out into the aisle. He is wearing blue wrestling trunks with an American flag on both the front and back, and he has a white baseball cap with the letters "U S A" in red with a blue outline. He high-fives some of the fans on his way to the ring, walks up the stairs and steps through the ropes. The microphone is handed to him.) Pete Robinson: Well, folks, I was sitting back at my home watching the INWI on television when I saw Commissioner DeLuise attacked by Sam Adams. And, let me tell you something, I didn't like it. You know, Sam Adams comes out here and says that he is a true American? He thinks that he is the perfect American, and that the rest of us are a disgrace? Well, let me tell you something, Mr. Adams. You're the disgrace around here. The United States of America would be nothing without all the people living here. The USA would be nothing without all the hard-working people sitting here in this arena and sitting at home watching on TV. But, you don't respect them. Well, Adams, I'm fighting back. I'm fighting for the greatest nation on earth, America. I'm fighting for the greatest wrestling organization on earth, the INWI. And I will make sure that you pay for your cowardly attack on Commissioner DeLuise. U! S! A! U! S! A! (The crowd continues the USA chant.) DeLuise - And now, we will get to see Pete Robinson first hand as he wrestles in the next match!!!! Match 5 - Debut match Pete Robinson (with Commissioner Jerry DeLuise) vs Awesome X This match could prove to be exciting because Robinson is looking to get his first win and Awesome X is trying to get back on track. There’s the bell and we are underway. Awesome X dives at Robinson’s legs right off the bat and takes him down to the mat. Awesome X punching at Robinson and scoops him up and nails a snap suplex! Awesome X grabs Robinson, sends him into the ropes and nails a high drop kick that flattens Robinson. Awesome X to the second rope and he nails an elbow and there’s a cover, 1…NO! Robinson kicks out at one. Awesome X picks up Robinson and nails another snap suplex. Awesome X picks up Robinson, sends him into the ropes and hits a strong armed clothesline. Robinson flies over the top rope and to the floor. Awesome X goes out after him and waits on the apron. Robinson stands up and Awesome X dives, wait! Robinson caught Awesome X in mid air and just nailed a belly to belly suplex! Awesome X is hurt on the floor! Robinson takes advantage of this situation as he picks up Awesome X and sends him face first into the metal ring post. Robinson then scoops up Awesome X and drops him on the steel steps. Robinson grabs Awesome X and grinds his face into the steps and Awesome X is busted open. [USA! USA! USA! USA!] Robinson grabs Awesome X and rolls him back into the ring and heads to the top rope. Robinson flies on Awesome X with a frog splash and a cover, 1…2…NO! Awesome X kicks out. Robinson back up, grabs Awesome X and drops him on his arm. Robinson takes Awesome X and sends him into the ropes and connects with a big clothesline! Robinson picks up Awesome X and there’s a swinging neck breaker. Robinson again with X and he sets up for a reverse neck breaker… YES! There’s the American Neck Breaker!! A cover, 1…..2…..3!! Here is your winner in 8:35…. Pete Robinson!! [USA! USA! USA! USA!] Brian Newark - Pete Robinson is successful in his debut match here in the INWI and Jerry DeLuise is backing him all the way here. Next up....before our next match.... we have a rather interesting interview with Mr. Ed Rogers. [Scene: A park on beautiful autumn day. The leaves are changing all sorts of different weird and wonderful colors, The joggers are wearing those neat hooded sweater things, and Tiffany Love is sitting on a park bench eyeing little girls with bad intent....er....no...sorry, I have Aqualung stuck in my head. Tiff is in actuality waiting for someone on that park bench. Smelly the Sockpuppet, manager of Ed Rogers and all around bad guy comes up from behind Tiffany's shoulder. He snickers into the camera once or twice before giving Tiffany a great shock when he blows in her ear. Tiffany jumps away before turning around to see who did it.] TL: What in Sam's heck are you doing? SY: Hey, baby, don't worry, your dream boat has arrived! TL: I wouldn't exactly call you my dream boat. SY: Sure you do. TL: I do not! SY: Yes you do. TL: No I don't! SY: Yes you do. TL: No I don't! SY: Yes you do. TL: No I don't! SY: Yes you do. TL: No I don't! SY: Yes you do. TL: No I don't! SY: Yes you do. TL: Okay, already! I do! Just shut up about it, okay? [Tiffany sighs.] SY: Alright, honey bunch. [Tiffany ignores the last remark.] TL: I am here to get an interview from the man you manage, Ed Rogers. May I please talk to him? SY: Only if you marry me. TL: I am _not_ going to marry you! SY: Then I guess you will just have to talk to me, instead. Who cares about that good for nothing slime ball of a evolutional throwback anyway? TL: I think that most of INWI wants to hear from the man who has single handily dominated the ranks since the beginning. SY: Yeah, but Brody Thunder is a queer. TL: Brody Thunder is most definitely not a queer, and what does that have to with anything? I was talking about Ed Rogers! SY: How do you know that Thunder isn't a queer? [He pauses for a moment, and Tiffany begins to blush.] So that's how you got this job, eh? Secondly, Ed Rogers has done anything but dominated the INWI ranks. TL: How dare you insinuate that me and Brody... ugh. [Tiffany gets herself under control.] Why do you continue to insult your own wrestler? SY: What do you mean? I am not insulting Ed, I am just telling the truth. And the truth is he's stunk up the joint! He lost to Pickles, he lost to Sharp, he lost to Apples! And Apples is crazy! TL: WAAAIIITTTT a second. We've been through all this once before. Let me personally set the record straight.... Apples is not insane, he is not out of his mind, he is fine. Period. SY: You know this just like you know that Thunder is straight, huh? TL: THE RELATIONSHIP THAT BRODY AND I HAVE IS PURELY BUSINESS! SY: I don't care what you and Brody are doing. Why did you bring Brody up again, anyhow? I thought we were agreeing that Apples is insane. TL: Wha? Me? When did I agree that King Apples was insane? SY: You mean you doubt that a person who calls himself King is out of what little mind he had? TL: No, I mean yes, I mean... Apples is all right! SY: Is not. TL: Is too. SY: Is not. TL: I am not going to get into that again. If you don't have anymore proof to back up your outlandish claim I _will_ be leaving. SY: You want more proof? He treats a sock like a real person for Pete sake. I have been in the looney farm enough times visiting Ed to know that when someone starts talking to a sock and calls himself a king... you might as well invest in large amounts of Prosaic. TL: [Tiff laughs.] Your saying that someone who talks to a sock is out of it? SY: No. Not if the sock happens to be the single greatest mind in the history of all things like I just happen to be. But with Apples it is clearly a case of being sexual abused as a child. He needs a special friend to confide in, and if happens to be delusional enough to think that I would be caught dead with a schmuck like him there is nothing I can do about it. It is his fantasy world he lives in. [Tiffany looks at her watch.] TL: Well, Smelly, it's been interesting, but I should really be getting to.... uh.... bed now. SY: Really? TL: Yes. It's almost... six o'clock. I don't usually go to sleep this early but I am feeling a little under the weather. SY: If you come back to my house I could check you out... You know I am a certified gynecologist. [Tiff gets up and starts to back away.] TL: Uh... That's all right.... I just need a good nights rest... [Tiff turns and leaves at full sprint. Smelly looks down.] SY: It's your fault Ed. [Fade to black.] Match 6 - Chandler's Gauntlet - Week 4 Jason "The Crusher" Chandler vs Cloud Eagle Chandler goes for win four in a row. This match should be a little tougher to Chandler. Cloud Eagle came down to ringside without Ultimate Perversion, but you know Perversion is in the back watching on the monitors. Any trouble to Cloud Eagle and Perversion will be out here. There’s the bell and we are underway. Chandler and Eagle lock up in the middle of the ring and Chandler with a fist that rocks the big man. Chandler off the ropes and comes back with another fish. Chandler can’t believe that Eagle has not gone down. Chandler off the ropes again and Eagle nails a belly to belly suplex on Chandler and Chandler looks hurt as he grasps his ribs. Eagle scoops up Chandler, sends him into the ropes and there’s a big chop that sends the Crusher to the mat. Eagle drops an elbow and then a knee and there’s a cover, 1….2….NO! Chandler kicks out. Eagle grabs Chandler and sends him into the turnbuckle and starts to pound his head into the turnbuckle. The crowd counts along with it, 5….6….7…..8….9….10!!!! Chandler staggers out of the corner and he falls face first to the mat. Eagle covers again, 1…2….NO! Again Chandler surprises everyone and kicks out. Eagle grabs Chandler sends him into the ropes, clothesline, NO! Chandler ducks, off the other ropes and there’s a diving elbow into the mush of Eagle. He staggers backwards and Chandler goes to the top rope and connects with a diving clothesline that sends Eagle to the mat. Chandler scoops up Eagle and OH MY!! Chandler just nailed a belly to belly suplex on the big man!! Chandler with a cover, 1…2…NO! Eagle kicks out and Chandler with a chin lock. They work their way back to their feet and Eagle sends Chandler into the ropes, duck under and then a spinning heel kick from Chandler puts Eagle back into the corner. Chandler climbing the ropes… he’s gonna pound Eagle again. No, wait! There’s a spinning DDT from Chandler!! A cover, 1…..2…..3!!! It’s over!! Here is your winner in 12:46, Jason "Crusher" Chandler. WOW, what a match and Chandler will move into week five next week. Match 7 - New York Championship General Samuel Adams (with Militia) vs "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset (with Prince Chuckle Wuckle) The two light heavy weights are in the ring and there is the bell. Sunset is up and charging...Adams jumps over him...runs to the rope as Sunset follows...he hits the bottom rope and flips backward into Sunset...Sunset is out cold he never saw that coming. What a maneuver! Adams climbs the top rope and drops the elbow...a cover...1...2...no he breaks it off. Adams helps sunset to his feet...he sends him across the ring into the ropes and lands a drop kick. Sunset is out...what is Adams doing...he is sitting on the top turnbuckle waiting...is he waiting for Sunset to get up? Sunset begins to move but Adams stays atop the rope...he is paying Sunset no attention. Sunset looks like he has no clue where he is...he staggers to his feet...there goes Adams ...Adams leaps from the ropes and ...ouch...a bulldog. Sunset is down again...what punishment. Adams has returned to the ropes but this time he is sitting with his back to the ring...that could prove to be a bad decision on his part. Sunset begins to regain his senses...He sees that Adams is paying no attention and charges the corner. Adams hurls himself over Sunset at the last minute and Sunset flies into the turnbuckle and out of the ring. Adams follows Sunset outside the ring...he lifts Sunset above his head...what strength. Adams then drops him throat first onto the rail...Adams helps Sunset to his feet...he sends Sunset into the stairs...Sunset is out cold yet again. Adams helps Sunset back into the ring...he climbs the ropes...a leg drop...and a cover...1...2...no he breaks the cover off again...Adams is just toying with him now. General Samuel Adams!!! [Pachelbell's Canon starts to blare on the sound system and out from the back comes a throne being carried by jWo members. On top is King Apples and "The Ringmaster" Tony Houston. They are standing and laughing at the crowd. Bill Bob Woijicowski and Mr. Flamboyant walk ahead of the throne heckling the crowd. All four men climb into the ring and Flamboyant chases the announcer out of the ring. Flamboyant produces a mic and begins....] Flamboyant - Oh yeah... we love all of you! And we know you love all of us. There is a reason why we are the greatest group in wrestling today and that's because we are great! But really, we need someone to conduct this interview and there is only one man capable of doing this. ME! HAHAHAHA!! King, it is talked about that you have a MAJOR announcement for us? King Apples - You see, the truth of the whole matter is we have three in the Brotherhood. It's a giving that our International Brother Glenn Hudson is traveling all over the world for the next month. But we need a full time fourth member. We have gone out and we have recruited one of the hippest guys around. And we are gonna bring him out tonight. This is our little brother. In other words, if you mess with our little brother, you have to deal with us. Flamboyant - <jumps back and acts surprised> YOU are ADDING a new person to the Brotherhood? Wow, this is an announcement that everyone must be sitting on their streets just waiting to find out who. So Apples, WHO are you adding to the Brotherhood? Apples - Well, you see... we have such a close knit group. We had to find someone that we could all trust and hold as a Brother. A Brother is a hard thing to come by. That's why we only have a select few of us right now. I mean, look... there's myself, Brother Houston and Brother Bill Bob. Heaven knows that we are already the dominant force in eWrestling and after this latest addition we will only be better. Flamboyant - <jumping around like a little kid> So come on King, are you gonna tell us WHO this new member is? It's killing ALL of us..... [The camera pans over Houston and Bill Bob, who have smirks on their faces. All of a sudden Pachelbell's Canon starts to play over the loud speakers again and here comes the throne again. On the top is the new member of the Brotherhood. No one knows what to expect as they come out and he starts to flip off the crowd. He wears a leather vest and black tights. On the back of his vest it says "Vindicator." This new member gets into the ring and struts around and Bill Bob swats him upside the back of his head and he stops...] Apples - Ladies and gentlemen, let me introduce to you the newest member of the Brotherhood, "The Vindicator" Vinny Vitrone!! This is the newest member of the Brotherhood and watch out world!! Vitrone - Yeah... Welcome me all of you stinking fans. I am the newest member of the Brotherhood and now all hell will break loose. For a long time I have looked up to the King and now I will be along side of him. It's time for everyone to see what the Vindicator is all about! And the Brotherhood is the most dominant force in all of eWrestling. See, the thing of the who--- [Vitrone is cut off when Bill Bob swats him in the back of the head again. Vitrone stands up and hands the mic back to Flamboyant...] Flamboyant - Now then Apples, is it MY understanding that you are gonna show off Vitrone TONIGHT on Hot Spot??? Apples - You have heard correctly Flamboyant. You know that Jason Chandler is running this Gauntlet thing? Well, That's what Vitrone is going to do. But, he is gonna do it all tonight!! He is going to RUN the GAUNTLET tonight! That's right, we the Brotherhood, have selected some of the finest wrestlers in the world to face our new member. He will prove himself to US tonight and to all of you moronic fans! Let's get this action started!! [With that, Apples, Bill Bob, Houston and Flamboyant exit the ring. Leg one of the gauntlet over....Here comes Brian Johnson charging the ring and is quickly met with a thumb to the eye by Vitrone...Vitrone bounces off the ropes and nails a bulldog. Vitrone pulls up Johnson and sends him into the ropes and scoops him and drops 'em with a Spinning Side Walk Slam..Vitrone going to the outside and to the top rope...Frog Splash...Vitrone by passes the cover and pulls up Johnson...he lifts... Applesauce Drop? Well, I guess that's a tribute to stablemate King Apples...One.. two.. three.!!! Vitrone again victorious...Who's coming down next..Vitrone is making this look relatively easy. Michael Jones is next up..and he charges the ring..and connects with an overhand right that staggers Vitrone...Jones whips Vitrone into the ropes and sends Vitrone high into the air with a back drop...Cover..One..NO!! kick out on one by Vitrone. Jones pulls up Vitrone..He's going for a Piledriver..NO!!! Vitrone with a low blow..He MUST have learned that one from The Brotherhood..Vitrone grabs Jones..WHAT!! Cold Drop!!! Well, there's a how ya' do for Tony Houston's move....one..two..three!! And that's three down....5 more to go before he meets jWo champ, Dell Pickle.. Here comes Jarrid Hoss and again he is met before he enters the ring with a baseball slide dropkick by Vitrone. Vitrone bounces off the far sides ropes and nails a Piscada that sandwiches Hoss between the floor and Vitrone. Vitrone stomps the head of Hoss and rolls him back into the ring...Vitrone quickly to the top turnbuckle and leaps...FLYING SPUR!!! There a tribute to fellow Brotherhood member Bill Bob. Cradles both legs..One.. two.. three!!! Vitrone pulls up Malvolio.. Hangman's Neck breaker..that almost snapped Malvolio's neck great move..Vitrone pulls up Malvolio and sets up for a Piledriver..NO!! he kicked his legs out and turned it into a Tiger Bomb..great move..One.. two..three...!!
