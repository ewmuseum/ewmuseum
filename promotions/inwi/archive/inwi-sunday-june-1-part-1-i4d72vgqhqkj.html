[INWI] Sunday, June 1 - Part 1

2 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
INWI - Sunday, June 1 Utica Aud, Utica, NY ========================================= [Micheal Pontiff stands in the ring. Suddenly the haunting theme from "High Plains Drifter begins seeping thoughout the arena's P.A. system. Pontiff looks confused at first but then realizes what's about to happen and shakes his head. A spotlight shines on the curtain at the top of the aisle. The curtains are flung open and out steps Brody Thunder. Big heel pop! He's dressed in wrestling gear with the INWI title around his waist. Wearing his trademark black hat and a cigar clenched in his mouth, he makes his way down to ringside and enters the ring. He doesn't smile or acknowledge the fans at all. Once in the ring he walks to the center and stands beside Pontiff.] Pontiff: Mr Thunder...you are not scheduled to be out here at this ti... [Thunder abruptly grabs the mic from Pontiff.] Thunder: Pontiff...Ya see what I'm wearin' son. [Thunder points to the title belt. Pontiff nods in nervous agreement.] THUNDER: Thus here gives me the right ta come out here anytime I damn well please...got it? Now get lost. [Pontiff shakes his head in disgust. Thunder turns back to the crowd.] THUNDER: I'm out here fer a few reasons. Seems I gotta straighten a few folks out as to jus' how things are in the INWI. Alotta new blood is comin' inta this federation an' more'n a few o' 'em think they're the next INWI champion. I'm here ta tell ya,fellas... [Thunder stares into the camera.] ...yer wrong. Dead wrong. [Thunder begins slowly walking about the ring as he speaks.] Plain an' simple. I hear alla ya buympin' yer gums 'bout yer gonna do this an' yer all gonna do that...but the fact o' the matter is...when ya step in the ring with the Wolf.. ..ya can talk all ya want... [Thunder pauses ta agin stare into the camera.] ...but ya ain't gonna do_squat_. [Thunder stops and stares directly into the crowd,while smirking.] First off "D.D."... ...I heard YOU say there ain't anyone who poses a threat ta ya as ya climp ta the top. Well friend...yer lookin' at more'n a threat ta yer climb... ...I'm a threat ta yer flamin' career. When ya knock on my door ya best be ready fer retirement cuz that'll be yer next stop. End o' story. [Thunder begins walking again.] Now then..."Animosity"... ..yer another pretender lookin' fer yer fifteen minutes o' fame. Well ya better look elsewhere,bub,cuz it ain't comin' at the expense o' the man yer starin' right now. Don't believe me? I don't really care. Wanna try me? I'll put enough stitches on yer squash ta make it look like a road map,son. This ain't no Sunday school, this ain't no daycare center... This here's the INWI. The bigtime,amigo. An' where yer headin' is on a one way trip ta the ER o' yer choice... [Thunder hitches a thumb to his chest and smiles.] ..courtesy o' Brody Thunder. Ya said "It takes bitch to beat a bitch." [Thunder shakes his head as if amused at the thought.] Spoken like the true adolescent punk that ya are. Well Animosity... [Thunder stops and point directly into the camera with a look of contempt and confidence.] ...I seen ya here earlier. I know yer in the back watchin' this. I'll make it real easy fer ya. Why doncha ya come on down here ta the ring right now... [Thunder takes off the title belt and lays it on the ring mat as if laying down the gauntlet.] ...an' I'll show ya jus' how much of an ass-kickin' this here "bitch" can give! [Big pop! Thunder lowers the mic and stands in the middle of the ring with his hands on his hips. He stares at thetop of the aisle as if waiting for Animosity to appear. After several seconds Thunder raises the mic again.] I'll tell ya why he ain't comin' out here. [Thunder picks up the belt and lays it over his left shoulder.] Fear an' common sense. [He begins walking around again.] That brings me ta my opponent fer tonight. Apocalypse. [Thunder smirks sarcastically.] Catchy name. But if all ya needed was a catchy name ta win this strap I wouldn't be wearin' it. It takes blood, sweat an' agony ta hold this strap, my friend. An' that's somethin' yer about ta experience when that bell rings tonight.I worked too damned hard ta get this an' I'll be damned if some snot-nosed little boogeyman wannabe is gonna take it away. Big man...I'm comin' atcha with with both guns loaded. If ya think I'm foolin' around... ...then yer already finished. Jus' like that. This ain't no hobby fer me it's how I put food on my family's table. Way I see it..yer tryin' ta take that food away. Well I got a "revelation" fer ya Apocalypse... [Thunder once more stops and faces the camera.] ...it jus' ain't happenin'. Not tonight. Not by you. Hmph..."Apocalypse". "The war ta end all wars"... ...that's what the Good Book says. Well I guess that monicker's appropriate cuz after tonight...it ain't jus' gonna be yer title hopes that're over... [Thunder gets in very tight to the camera and smiles a devilish grin.] ..it's gonna be yer career. See ya soon,amigo. [Micheal Pontiff enters the ring and Thunder walks over to him with the mic. Thunder holds it out for Pontiff to take but as Pontiff reaches for it Thunder drops it to the mat and laughs. He leaves the ring to a chorus of catcalls and boos,none of which have any effect on him as he disappears behind the curtain. Pontiff stands ready in the ring to announce the next match.] ============================================================ Brian Newark - Welcome ladies and gentlemen to another great card presented to you by the INWI. We have returned to our roots as we invade Utica, NY. This is where one our first cards were held and we have a great card in store for you tonight. I'm also sure that fans here in Utica will be impressed by how much we have grown as an oranization. Tonight we have for you 13 matches, including SEVEN Title matches! The main event is a one fall match for the INWI crown as Brody Thunder faces Apocalypse. Also we have a four way dance for the INWI Northeast TV Title! We have an "extreme" match for you of sorts and also we have another debut match. Without further delay, let's go up to the ring for our opening match here LIVE on Sportschannel USA! ============================================================ Match 1 - INWI TV Tag Titles Solid Gold (Champions) vs Contrast We are starting this one out with a title match as Solid Gold gets set to defend their TV Tag Titles against Black and White - Contrast. Solid Gold has been on a roll lately ever since regaining these straps and I am sure they don't plan on losing them tonight. Blake starts off with Black and takes early control. Black sent into the ropes and Blake bounces off the opposite and nails a flying knee. Black is groggy after that knee and Blake drops another knee and then a leg! Blake tags in Bradley and Bradley scoops up Black and hits a belly to belly suplx! They are absolutley dominating this match! Bradley locks in his legs, wait, that's the Sharpshooter! Black is in serious pain! Wait a minute, look...it's the Friendship Express!! Ricky and Alfonso dash into the ring and Ricky nails Bradley from behind. Ricky throws Blake over the top rope and Contrast went out under the ropes. The Friendship Express really wants Solid Gold and those TV Titles. Your winners as a result of a disqualification and still INWI TV Tag Team Champions in 5:32, Solid Gold! I am sure these two team will be meeting again down the road. Match 2 Animosity vs Ultimate Perversion Ultimate Perversion, who stepped in for the Springer when he was bumped to the 4 way dance. Perversion feels he has a lot to prove and here he gets his chance against and up and coming superstar. Animosity could have a long stay here in the INWI, but having to face Ultimate Perversion in only his second match is not a good thing. Perversion enters the ring and immediatley starts to pound on Animosity. He gets him on the mat and he slaps him in a camel clutch! Man, Animosity is already in a bad situation. Wait a minute, here comes Trent VenDenBerg and he's screaming from outside the ring. Perversion lets go and walks over to the ropes and Animosity gets back up and he jumps Perversion nailing him with lefts and rights! Animosity sends Perversion into the ropes and goes for a back body drop. NO! Perversion nails a DDT!! Perversion picks up Animosity and slams him to the mat. Off the ropes goes Perversion and drops a leg! Perversion picks up Animosity and sends him into the ropes again...spinning heel kick! Whoa, Perversion is really working this match! Wait...look, there's the Climatic Surprise! 1..2...3!! It's over, your winner in 6:57, Ultimate Perversion! And Perversion exits the ring looking for his former manager! Match 3 - United States Championship P.O. Box (Champion) vs Bodyguard Jones With all the success that Bodyguard Jones has had with his ex stablemates, he finally has earned a title shot! Jones comes to the center of the ring and he and P.O. Box shake hands and we are underway. This should be an excellent match. Jones with a quick takedown on the bigger Box and then to an ankle lock. Box powers out af it and gets back to his feet. Jones and Box tie up and Box slaps on a headlock. Jones sends Box into the ropes, but gets flattended on a body block. Box drops an elbow, but misses. Jones back up and dropkicks Box out of the ring. Box is shaken up on the outside and Jones dives outside on top of Box and puts the champ on his back! Jones is pumped and he hops back up on the apron then dives at Box again, but Box moves. Box rolls back into the ring and Jones slowly follows. Box drags Jones to the middle of the ring and goes for a leg drop, but Jones moves again! Maybe lady luck is riding on the back of Jones tonight! Jones up with Box and he bodyslams the big man! Wow, what a show of power! Jones is working it right now, and something is going wierd here. Jones drops an elbow....wait, someone is under the ring. It's that masked guy again and no one seems to know who he is. He is interfering all over the place! Ouch! He just cracked Jones on the back with a chair and Jones crumples to the mat. Box rolls over on top of Jones. 1....2...3!!! It's over! Your winner in 8:37 andstill INWI United States Champion, P.O. Box!! My question is this though....who is this masked guy? ============================================================ Brian Newark - Next up is newcome and possible sensation, "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston. He will be up against former INWI Northeast Champion, "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset! Let's get comments from Houston. ============================================================ [The scene is a local INWI non televised house show. For the event their is a good attendance and the fans are involved with the matches taking place. As "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset enters the ring and begins to dominate a name-less fill in for the nigtht's card, one man simple remains sitting in his seat and staring with an angered look on his face. This man's icey blue eyes seem to burn a hole through the wrestlers, the ring, and the entire arena] [The camera zooms in and reveals that "Cold & Calculating" Tony Houston sitting in total distain for his next scheduled opponent, "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset. Houston turns and looks towards the camera and waves them closer.] Houston: Look at this bitch out there. [points to the ring] How is the president of this league going to pit ME against this ham and egger. First of all, how you gonna call yourself Hubba Hubba. What is that? Sounds like a chewing gum. [Calmly] But maybe he's got something. He goes into the match solid, but really knows it's just a matter of time before he's broken down. Then he is spit in the trash. Son, when you enter my domain you step into an entirely different level of comprehension that is far superior to your psyche. [Houston turns and sees Sunset have a relatively easy time with his match against the name-less fill in.] Houston: Damn! This match is STILL going it's been at least five minutes and this guy doesn't even look like he's left his house in a week. Mr. Sunset, for some bizarre reason you think you could hang and bang with the likes of "Cold & Calculating", [shrugs shoulders] Psss Well, son, bring your best...I've got me a nice sized 40 ounce of Whoop Ass just fixin' to be force fed to your dumb self. But Sunset come Sunday night you will be lixin' your wounds and wondering just why "did I F$%K with "Cold & Calculating" Tony Houston, you will prove my point that this league has been overmatched by the skills of Tony Houston. [Houston looks at the ring and then the camera follows and sees "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset winning his match, but when the camera turns back expecting to see Houston all that is scene is a blue seat numbered 129. The camera then focuses on Houston as he makes his way towards the door, almost dulldozing through anybody in his way with a stiff shoulder.] [Camera returns to the ring and awaits the next match of the evening.] ============================================================ Match 4 "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston vs "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset Here could be another interesting match up. Tony Houston, probably the most arrogant S.O.B in the INWI and the former INWI Northeast Champion, Jonathan Sunset. There's the bell and we are underway! Houston attacks right away and nails a belly to belly suplex that really hit the spot on Sunset. Houston drops a standing leg then scoops up Sunset. Houston sends Sunset into the ropes and hits him with a back body drop. Houston and Sunset both back up to their feet and Houston suplex's Sunset to the mat. Houston tosses Sunset out of the ring and follows him out. Houston grabs Sunset before he can get back into the ring and body slams him outside the ring. Houston then rolls Sunset back into the ring and looks like he wants to end it...yes!! It's the Cold Drop. 1...2...3!!! Your winner in 4:39, "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston!! Match 5 - Debut Match D.D. (Doctor of Disaster) vs Dell Pickle Well, D.D. is finally making his debut here in the INWI and he faces, from the jWo, Dell Pickle. D.D. rushes the ring and attacks Pickle right off the bat. D.D. takes Pickle down with a dive to the legs and then starts to punch on him. He wants to end this match quickly! Pickle up, only to be sent crashing to the mat by a nasty clothesline! D.D. scoops up Pickle and hits a backbreaker and looks into the crowd....he looks like he is signaling for something! Yes! D.D. is setting him up....there it is! The Difibulator! He lays Pickle on his stomach and he sits on his lower back and bores his knuckles into his Pickle's ribcage. Pickle gives up and this match is over in 3:01. Your winner, D.D.!! Match 6 "Marvelous" Mike Quick vs "The Technician" Zack Sharp This should be an absolutley excellent match. Both men are looking to get a title shot and Sharp is really on the rise. Quick is in a fued with the Springer and Bill Bob Woijicowski, but got paired up in this match. Sharp and Quick are both in the ring and there's the bell. This one is underway! Sharp leaps at Quicks legs and grabs one and takes him to the mat. The Technician realizes that Quick is one of the fastest in the INWI and he has to keep him off his feet. Sharp slaps on a side headlock and is trying to take some stamina out of Quick early in this match. Sharp is being moved to his feet, sent into the ropes by Quick, but clotheslines Quick on the way back through. Sharp then drops an elbow on Quick to try and keep him down. Sharp starts to work on Quick's legs and starts a spiining toe hold. Man, this could get Quick to give it up. Wait, Quick has the rope and Sharp is forced to break the hold. Quick rolls out of the ring and Sharp follows. Sharp on the apron and dives on Quick and the crowd is going crazy! The Technician is really showing us what he can do tonight. Quick still on the outside and Sharp up to the top rope. What the hell is he doing.... OH MY GOD!! A MOONSAULT from the top rope all the way to the floor and both he and Quick look out cold! Sharp staggers to his feet andraises his arm in the air and thwe crowd is going nuts! Sharp rolls back into the ring and is going up again....HOLY COW! He just Moonsaulted Quick from the top rope for a second time and now they are both hurt! This is crazy! Sharp again is up and rolls Quick into the ring. He covers him...1...2...3!! It's finally over! Your winner in 10:21, "The Technician" Zack Sharp!!! Match 7 - Northeast Championship Mr. Ed Rogers (Champion) vs Jason "The Crusher" Chandler Well, Ed Rogers is putting his strap up against one of the people many say is a champion without a title in the INWI. Jason "The Crusher" Chandler has had a long road to get where he is now and is hoping to finally get his hands on some gold here in the INWI. Chandler and Rogers are in the ring and Rogers is just staring into space. Theres the bell and Rogers just is looking at the roof. Chandler is confused and I don't think he knew what to expect from Rogers. Both men are just standing there and Chandler finally gets fed up. Chandler rushes Rogers and nails him with a clothesline that sends him into the turnbuckle. Chandler then begins to nail Rogers with a series of punches! Rogers is screaming in the corner as though he was scared. This guy really is not playing with a full deck of cards! Chandler backs off then grabs him out of the corner and nails a suplex and then gets a two count. Rogers kicks out and tries to crawl away, but Chandler catches him and won't let him go. Rogers pokes Chandler in the eye and rolls out of the ring. Chandler is up and he is looking for Rogers, but he has vanished. Where did he go? Wait, he's on the other side of the ring...he comes up behind Chandler and goes for it...YES! The Puppet Master! 1...2...3!! Oh my, your winner in 8:47, Mr. Ed Rogers!! Chandler is going to be upset, but should get another title match out of this match alone. Wow... ============================================================ Brian Newark - Our next match is between our two, well, extreme wrestlers, Jonathan Hammer and John Stilwater. Let's get comments from Hammer! ============================================================ The camera fades in on a alley somewhere outside. Jonathan Hammer is standing there with a cane in his hand. Without further ado Hammer smashes it over his head. Blood is pouring down his face as he speaks.) INWI you got your first glimpse of what Jonathan Hammer can do when I destroyed Sen. Bradley. "Hardcore" John Stilwater ha his name even says he's "Hardcore". Look at me I don't need to put Hardcore or Extreme next to my name the fans know it. Stilwater your time is up. (Hammer headbutts the camera) Fade to fuzz! ============================================================ Match 8 - Let's get to the Extreme! "Hardcore" John Stilwater vs Jonathan Hammer We all know the INWI isn't that extreme, but we have a couple of wrestlers here that are. Jonathan Hammer, who entered the ring already bleeding and threw a chair into the ring...and "Hardcore" John Stilwater. Stilwater is a former United States Champion and wants gold again. Stilwater charges at Hammer and takes him down and starts to wail at that open cut on Hammers head. Stilwater gets off Hammer and starts to kick at his head as well. It looks as though Stilwater wants to seriously hurt Hammer, maybe so they never have to face again. Bt, I doubt it... I guess it's because they are extreme. Stilwater up with Hammer and sends him out of the ring over the top rope and then follows him out. Stilwater out and he comes after Hammer, but Hammer grabs a chair and nails Stilwater with it. Stilwater falls back and Hammer nails him with the chair two more times. Hammer is busted wide open, but it looks like he loves it! He grabs Stilwater and sets him on his shoulder like a powerslam, but instead drives him headfirst five times into the ring post. Now Stilwater is Busted open and he gets dropped. Hammer breaks the count and rolls back outside where Stilwater nails him with a chair and they start to brawl. They are heading back to the lockeroom area and Stilwater nails his infamous DDT!! Stilwater scoops up Hammer and carries him to the ring and rolls him in. There's the cover...1...2...3!! Wow, that one is over! Your winner in 11:59, "Hardcore" John Stilwater! I'm sure this is the last we have seen of these two! ============================================================ Brian Newark - What a great first half ladies and gentlemen!
