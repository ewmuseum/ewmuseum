[INWI] Hot Spot, Sept 22, Hour 1

26 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene opens up in downtown Delhi, NY. It's a small town, only about 2500 people. But when the State College is in, they double to about 5000 people. Outside of a quaint little bar, the Shire Pub, is King Apples and "The Ringmaster" Tony Houston. They have a bunch of people around them and they are tossing back a couple of cold pints. In walks "Bullywug" Glenn Hudson and Mr. Flamboyant and they join Apples and Houston in a couple of cold ones...] The camera angle switches to out back of the Shire Pub. It's a parking lot, but it is well lit with INWI equipment. A ring is set up in the middle of the parking lot and there is seating for a couple hundred people. There are bleachers set up all around and a capacity crowd is expected. You can still tell it's early as no one is there but the ring crew. All of a sudden, talking is heard. The camera switches angles and seen coming from the Shire Pub is King Apples, Tony Houston, Mr. Flamboyant, Glenn Hudson and now Bill Bob Woijicowski is with them as is Commissioner DeLuise. They each, other than the Commissioner, carry a pitcher of beer with them.] All of them step into the ring and the camera follows them in. Mr. Flamboyant tosses a couple of chairs into the ring and they all sit down. Apples stays standing as all of the rest of them look on and drink their beer.... Apples then begins to speak..] King Apples - You know, Commissioner DeLuise and all of the powers at INWI central have said that we the Brotherhood along with our INWI counterparts should address the N.E.W. about our little invasion. Somehow, I got elected to talk and I have no clue why. The Commish over here says it's because I am the N.E.W. World Champion. Which I am and I love it. This is a great time in my wrestling career. I have the Brotherhood in Tony Houston and Glenn Hudson, I have good friends like Bill Bob and his bro. I have the INWI behind me and the whole fed. And I hold the World Championship of a federation that can't stand me. [The ''crew'' behind Apples begins to laugh, then Bill Bob Woijicowski stands up and holds his pint in the air...] Bill Bob - I'd like to propose a toast. A toast to the INWI and the Brotherhood. And here's to our collective people, kick N.E.W.'s asses! Whoo! [All of the men stand up and clink their glasses together and drink some of their beer. Apples then begins to speak again...] Apples - So tonight, when Brother Houston and myself team up, to take on a dominant foursome, we will show the world what we are going to do to the NEW. And if any of those punks like James Pillow want to step up to the plate, we'll kick their asses. So, if you'll excuse us, we need to have a couple more cocktails before we have to wrestle tonight. You will hear from us all again tonight.... [With that, the entourage of INWI wrestlers gets up and exits the ring. The makeshift arena outside of the Shire Pub in Delhi then begins to fill up. The next shot you see is a shot from over top, with the lights on and the crowd ready. You know it's time for.....] INWI Monday Night Hot Spot!! ======================== Monday, September 22, 1997 The Shire Pub, Delhi, NY ========================================================== Brian Newark - Welcome to Monday Night Hot Spot!!! And what a show we have planned for you tonight. THREE solid hours of INWI action coming to you from our hometown, Delhi, NY!! 12 Matches, including FOUR championship matches. Not only that, the United States Champion, Sam "The Minuteman" Adams has a shot to win the INWI Heavyweight Championship!! We have a great card planned for you tonight, so sit back and enjoy! Also tonight, Commissioner Jerry DeLuise will comment on the loss of Jimmy Cougar, we have footage from his funeral as well. Also, we will bring you the latest from NEW. Without further delay, let's kick off Hour number one with the Television Championship!!! Match 1 - Television Championship Ultimate Perversion vs Harvey Thunder (with Michael Slater) The rematch for the Television Championship. If this is as good as the first one, this belt could jump back into the hands of Harvey Thunder. Michael Slater, as usual is on the outside with Harvey Thunder. Cloud Eagle is absent in this match and Perversion is alone. There's the bell and we are underway. Perversion and Thunder tie up in the middle of the ring and Perversion shoves Thunder back. Thunder comes back and slaps Perversion. Perversion with a clothesline that sends Thunder to the mat! Perversion scoops up Thunder, sends him into the ropes and drops him with a diving clothesline. Thunder rolls out of the ring and goes over to Slater. Perversion comes out behind them and nails a double noggin knocker on them! Perversion rolls back into the ring and the crowd cheers. Thunder comes back in and nails Perversion with a low blow! Thunder on his feet and kicks Perversion to the mat and then starts to kick at him. Thunder climbs on top of Perversion and starts hitting him with left and rights! Thunder scoops up Perversion and nails a vertical suplex!! A cover by Thunder, 1....2......NO! Perversion kicks out and we almost had a new champion. Perversion picked up by Thunder and sent into the ropes. Thunder up and he starts to punch at Perversion. Perversion grabs him, reverse atomic drop and then a clothesline!! Perversion is pumped now and he grabs Thunder and slams him to the mat!!! Perversion off the ropes and nails a leg drop!! Perversion is rocking the house now!! Perversion with a DDT on Thunder and then he goes to the top rope!! Wait, Slater is up there and the ref is occupied with Harvey Thunder. Both men are on the top rope!! Slater with a DDT off the top rope!! OH MY GOD!! Slater out of the ring!! Harvey Thunder walks over and falls on Perversion, 1....2.....3!!! Oh My god!! We have a NEW TV Champ!! Here is your winner in 14:21 and NEW Television Champion, Harvey Thunder!!! Slater caused that one and Thunder is the new champ. Cloud Eagle will be upset that he wasn't hear for this one. ============================================================ Brian Newark - WOW!! One match down and we already have a new champion. That was amazing and crazy. Harvey Thunder is a two time TV Champ here in the INWI. Wow. Before our next match, let's go to Commissioner Jerry DeLuise, who has a statement regarding the death of Jimmy Cougar... ============================================================ [The video wall goes to the back where Commissioner Jerry DeLuise is sitting behind his desk at INWI Headquarters. DeLuise is sitting waiting for the cue to begin. He then begins to talk...] As Commissioner of the INWI, I would like to take the time to say that we would like to send out condolences to Jimmy Cougar's family on their loss. When we got the news of Cougar's death, were were shocked and upset here in the INWI. I think I can speak for the whole INWI when I say we will miss you Jimmy. Jimmy Cougar was an original member of the INWI and always made things exciting. He, at the time of his death, was the Northeast Television Champion here in the INWI. His athleticism and antics are something that we will all remember here in the INWI. He and his partner, Jason Starr, were always at the top of their game and always were professional in what they did here in the INWI. Jimmy Cougar will be missed by all who ever got a chance to watch him or work with him. I can say from the bottom of my heart, that he will be missed by all of us in the INWI. Our thoughts and prayers are with the family and friends of Jimmy Cougar. Our thoughts and prayers are also with Jason Starr. [The camera fades away from Jerry DeLuise and pops up a picture of Jimmy Cougar holding the INWI Northeast Television Title and then fades away to a commercial...] Match 2 Firestar vs Bodyguard Jones Firestar made his appearance to the ring first, and while he is waiting in the ring for BG Jones, the crowd begins to rise and start chanting..INWI...INWI...INWI, apparently out of nowhere the INWI fans have been hit with a sudden surge of league patriotism. Jones makes his way towards the ring and immediately Firestar and Jones begin to exchange haymakers...Right by Firestar..Left hook by Jones..stuns Firestar..Big Boot to the face of Firestar by Jones...Jones scoops the staggered Firestar and drops him across his knee with a deadly back breaker. Jones off the ropes and drops a fist to the throat of Firestar and goes for a quick cover..One...two..No!! Firestar with a relatively strong kick out. Jones pulls up Firestar and "fires" him into the ropes...Spinning Side Walk Slam...Firestar just bounced off the mat as a result of the impact. Jones however, doesn't go for a cover, that could be a mistake. He's going to the second rope? That's not normal territory for him...He leaps..Guillotine Leg--No!! Firestar rolled out of the way and Jones just crashed hip first hard onto the mat. Firestar bounces off the ropes and nails a Dropkick to the face of Jones, who is sitting on his hind end. Good move by Firestar...He pulls up Jones and attempts to whip him into the ropes...Hooking clothesline drops, the big bodyguard...Firestar off the ropes and drops a standing splash onto Jones.One...Two...NO!!! Jones rolled his shoulder. Firestar back up quickly and pulls up Jones...Forearm Uppercut send Jones' saliva flying through the crowd..that's nasty. Another uppercut by Firestar..and a third forearm uppercut sends Jones staggering into the corner...Firestar charges from across the ring Big Spla--No!!! Jones caught Firestar in a body hug and drops him face first on the top turnbuckle....Jones pulls up Firestar..he's going for the powerbomb..he lifts..<BOOM> he hit it..he hit the powerbomb!!!! Jones covers..One...Two....three!!!! he got 'em with the powerbomb... Here is your winner in 8:32, Bodyguard Jones!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Tonight, the Brotherhood team of King Apples and Tony Houston will take on the INWI. I guess, in an interesting state that they are in, Houston and Apples are in the back. ============================================================ [The scene fades to backstage of INWI Monday Night Hot Spot and several slurred sounds can be heard emerging from behind a closed door at the end of a long, winding corridor.] [As the camera and it's crew slowly grow closer, and closer towards the door the sounds are more and more recognizable. The camera crew are startled as a glass apparently is dropped and shatters on the concrete floor of the lockeroom.] [The camera crew knocks on the door and all the noises and carrying on ceases.] Voice: Did you hear that? [Silence is only detected as the camera crew knocks again] Voice: There it goes again...Who is it?!? Love: It's Tiffany Love reporting for INWI Backstage. Voice: Oh snap bring your fine self up in here.. [Tiffany Love enters the lockeroom and immediately the cloud of smoke engulfs her. She notices several men, including Bill Bob, King Apples, Mr. Flamboyant, and "Ringmaster" Tony Houston all sitting around a large circular table which is covered by several half empty pitchers of beer and a box full of Cuban cigars is the center of piece of this special group of athletes.] [Tony Houston finishes off yet another beer and places his cigar in the ashtray, and leans forward and reveals a pair of bloodshot, yet still icey and intimidating cold blue eyes.] Houston: Hey baby, Damn Ms. Love ya always come up in here lookin' all good. Well, Ms. Love why don't ya' sit yerself right here..[Houston pats his lap and reveals his N.E.W. North American Championship belt wrapped around his waist] Love: Why uh no Mr. Houston I am a professional and should be treated like an eq- [Love is interrupted as the entire group of drunken Brotherhood all begin laughing at Tiffany's "femine" attitude.] Houston: Well, baby whas yer problem...Why are you here ruining our drinkin'? Love: [almost angered] Mr. Houston you are the INWI's Ringmaster and you have a match tonight along with King Apples...against the majority of the jWo. Houston: [mocking surprising] What? When was this announced? Me and my fellow Brother must risk major injury at the hands of that excellent organization. However, as great as they is, they ain't nothing when matched up wit' the great combination of King Apples and the Ringmaster Tony Houston. Love: [appearing to suspect Houston and Apples among other are intoxicated] Are the two of you going to be able to make it to the ring. Houston: What the hell do you mean ya' stank ass biznitch? Love: Well, the two of you look a little...tired. Houston: Tired my ass. I'm drunk then a mutha .. Apples: Watch your mouth.. Houston: I was talkin' 'bout The Brotherhood. Anyways, Ms. Love if you ain't her to give us some...well...good interviewing..you can go... Love: Well I never..[and starts to storm out of the lockeroom] Houston: Well, Love we ain't gonna discuss why you never...'cause we don't even have 'nuff time to do all that...ya'll [to the camera crew] keep yer asses put. Now as we are preparing....in fact going through some very serious trainin' [finishes another beer] for our match against the jWo. [King Apples pour Houston another beer and almost falls out of his chair, as he drunkenly attempts to sit back down but regains his balance.] Houston: 'N as fer the cha--cha--champ of the INWI, Springer...Let's not forget son, that you and me are gonna be doin' it up in MY ring, for the belt that you accidently "earned". Well, son, when we git ta' that damn 'Dome up in the 'Cuse that belt will be mine...as the ring already is... [Houston turns his attention from the camera crew and waves them out as "Bulldozer" Tony Riley emerges from seemingly nowhere and physically escorts the camera crew from the Brotherhood lockeroom] [Screen fades as several new beers can be heard being popped open.] Match 3 - Television Tag Championship Solid Gold (with Jimmy Stardom) vs The Friendship Express This match should be outstanding. These two teams are major rivals and the Friendship Express definitely has their eye on those TV Tag Title belts. Blake and Geller will start this one off. Both men meet in the middle of the ring and they are bad mouthing each other. Slap in the face by Blake and he backs into the corner. Here comes Geller and a drop toe hold by Blake. Picks him up and a knife-edge chop and Geller goes down. Picks him up again and whips him into the buckles, Blake charges and delivers a dropkick to the knee. He tags in Bradley and Bradley is going upstairs. Blake holds Geller's legs and Bradley comes down with a legdrp to the back of the head. Irish whip on Geller, dropkick by Brad. Tag back into Blake and Solid Gold looks impressive early on here. Blake is setting up Geller on the ropes now. Blake climbs up but Geller pushes him down to the canvas. Geller hits a missile dropkick, cover 1........2..and a kickout in 2. Geller makes the tag to partner Ricky Sanchez and he is a house of fire. Double clothesline on both members of Solid Gold. Irish whip on Blake, backbody drop!!! He is on the top, FROG SPLASH!! 1..........2......no, a kickout by Blake. Geller tagged back in but he still looks fatigued. Thumb to the eye by Blake and a tag into Brad. Brad holds his head up, standing dropkick!! Brad has a hold of his legs, STF!!! He has it sinched in real good and Geller looks out of it. Wait, Sanchez is on the top rope. He just dropkicked Brad right in the mouth and he lets go of the STF. What an amazing maneuver. Geller rolls over and tags in Sanchez and he picks up Bradley, belly to belly suplex, cover 1.......2......and a kickout by Bradley. Back suplex by Sanchez and he is going upstairs once more, CENTON SPLASH!! 1........... 2........... Blake broke up the count and Sanchez is pissed. Sanchez nails Blake in the corner, and here comes Blake, but Geller is right there after him. All 4 men are going at it and the ref is just letting this one go. Blake and Brad irish whip Geller, duck underneath, high cross body onto both men, cover 1........2.......and a kickout by both men. Here is Sanchez and Solid Gold look to be in a bit of trouble. Scoop slam on Blake and Brad is thrown out of the ring. Geller has Blake on his shoulders, Sanchez comes off with a high cross body but, OH MY GOD, Blake, while on Geller's shoulders, caught Sanchez and delivered a fallaway slam off Geller's shoulders. Geller can't belive it. He throws Blake to the outside, Sanchez is still down in the ring. Bradley is back and he is on the top, Sanchez is helpless pray for Brad, 450 SPLASH!!!! Cover 1.........2..........3. What ring sense by Brad and your winners in 13:11, and still INWI TV Tag Team Champions, Solid Gold!!! Match 4 Jonathan Hammer vs Awesome X Jonathan Hammer and Awesome X... this match could be real interesting. Hammer... still having to watch his back could be a little to cautious in this match. There's the bell and we are underway. Awesome X charges and nails a drop kick. Hammer flys back into a turnbuckle and Awesome X mounts the turnbuckles and punches away. Hammer with a headbut to the midsection and then he throws Awesome X off of him. Hammer follows and starts to stomp away at Awesome X and then drops an elbow. Hammer picks Awesome X up by the hair and drops him to the mat with a DDT! Hammer looks like he really wants to beat on Awesome X in this one and is doing a pretty good job of it. Hammer scoops up Awesome X and nails a short armed clothesline and then hits another DDT! Hammer then sends Awesome X over the top rope and the crowd goes nuts. Hammer to the apron and he dives at Awesome X and nails him! Hammer is going nuts! Hammer sets up for a suplex and drops Awesome X onto a table. What is he thinking? Hammer to the apron and he dives on Awesome X and through the table. Oh my god!! Hammer gets up and he is laughing! He grabs Awesome X and rolls him into the ring. Hammer to the top rope and nails a leg drop on the lifeless body of Awesome X. A cover, 1....2.....3!!! It's over! Your winner in 9:47, Jonathan Hammer! [HOUR NUMBER ONE DONE!!!!]
