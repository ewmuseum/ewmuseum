Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
INWI INSIDE THE ROPES Sunday, June 15, 1997 ================================================== Brian Newark - Welcome ladies and gentlemen to the INWI's all new show, Inside The Ropes. We have an action packed show set for you today and joining me unfortunatley is Scoop Ropes. Scoop Ropes - Ahhhh shatup Newark. Yer nothing but a snivling little baby anyways and I'm getting sick of the fact that you are such a little kiss up. Kiss kiss kiss, you get peoples asses so wet from your tounge that they need to shower after you get don... Newark - That's enough Scoop! We have a good sized audience here and we don't need to hear that filth out of your mouth tonight! Welcome folks to todays show where we have some interviews and some in studio guests! Not ONLY that, but we will announce some of the matches for our first INWI Pay Per View on June 29!! Scoop - Yeah that's right and we got all sorts of great matches scheduled for that PPV. Let's see if the jWo can take over the world! Ha ha ha!! Can you believe the jokes that we have as champions in this federation? They are lucky as hell I don't go and kick all of their as... Newark - Scoop, I'm warning you..... Scoop - Shut up Newark. Yer really starting to get on my nerves dammit! I may have to kick your ass right now if you don't shut your hole. Now anyways, as the schmuck was saying, we have a great show for today and I am your superior host. From what I understand we have a couple sets of comments from Ed Rogers today....so let's catch the first of two. NOW, I like this guy and I'm sure he'd kick yer ass too Newark! ============================================================ (Mr. Ed Rogers looks depressed after his loss. He sits alone, in a dark corner of his dressing room, with his right hand supporting his head, while his left hand remains hidden.) Ed: Smelly? Can you hear me Smelly? I'm sorry Smelly. Please talk to me! (A voice. A sinister voice, yet a happy voice comes from the shadowy depths of the room. A voice that is almost too happy...) Smelly: Ed, I told you not to lose to Dell Pickle. Ed: I know you did, Smelly, but... Smelly: No buts! What you did was bad. You've been a bad boy, Ed. And what did I say that I would do if you lost? (Ed begins to cry.) Ed: Smelly, please... Smelly: WHAT DID I SAY THAT I WOULD DO IF YOU LOST!? Ed: You said that you where going to fire me. Smelly: Good. Now receive your punishment like a man. (Out of the darkness Ed pulls his other sock covered hand that he thinks of as a manager. But Smelly is holding something. Slowly the blackness lets go of the figure to reveal what the sock possesses. To your utter horror, it becomes clear that the object in question is a flame-thrower. A single flash of light, a single scream, and then darkness. Ed has been fired.) ============================================================ Newark - Holy cow!!! Did you see that? He flamed himself! This guy is really nuts and I can't seem to understand why he puts himself through this. Scoop - What are you, an idiot Newark? Smelly flamed him and I think Rogers deserved it after losing to the jWo. Man Rogers is a loser and I can't stand him... Newark - Right before that interview Scoop, you said you LIKED him.... Scoop - I didn't say shit Newark and if you don't watch your mouth, I'll kick your ass cause I really don't like you. Newark - Well alrighty then.... Lets hear from Ultimate Perversion, who currently is up to his ears in people who want a piece of him! Both his former manager and Tony Houston want him... Scoop - Well, maybe if he didn't dress like such a.... Newark - Keep it to yourself Scoop.... ============================================================ <Backstage as usual> Tiffany: Ultimate. It seems I'm always here talking to you. I have a few questions for you. Ultimate: OK Tiffany: First and foremost, what about your feud with Houston? Ultimate: What about it? The guys a jerk. I laughed when I heard he'd been suspended. He should be kicked out of the INWI. He's just a jobber. I'll take him on anytime. Houston, if you're listening, just you and me, cagematch. Then we'll see who the real wrestler is, and it sure as hell ain't you punk. Tiffany: Things seem to be quiet now between you and Trent. Are you talking? Ultimate: (Snorts) No chance. I want nothing more to do with that idiot. I have better things to do with my time than talk to that loser. I won't waste my breath. Tiffany: Does that include Cloud Eagle? Ultimate: Yep. Until he realises that he's working for a loser, there will be no contact between us. Tiffany: Finally, with Houston suspended, are you looking forward to getting in the ring with no interference. Ultimate: Interference? Don`t you mean distraction? Houston scares me about as much as my grandmother does. Wether he's there or not, nothing can stop me. Tiffany: Ultimate, thank you. ============================================================ Newark - Interesting enough.... but can he handle his own? I mean, Houston is amazing and Cloud Eagle is no slouch! Scoop - Well I hope he gets his ass kicked, he bugs me, if nothing more than the way he dresses. It's disgusting. Newark - Well I don't think he really wanted your opinion, now did he? Scoop - Shut up or I'll kick YOUR ass, get on with this damn show.... Brian Newark: And what about the recent situation with.... Voice: Jus' a minnit! Wait jus' a flamin' minnit,Newark! [A figure walks onto the studio set from behind the camera. The figure walks up to Brian Newark's position. As he does so the figure is revealed to be none other than Brody Thunder.] Brian Newark: Ah..Mr Thunder...you are not scheduled to be out here tonight and if you could please just leave... Scoop Ropes: Hey Brian, where's your manners? When the heavyweight champion makes an appearance he deserves some respect. Let him speak and keep your mouth shut while he does so. [Brian shoots a look of contempt at Ropes. Ropes simply smiles a very sarcastic smile back at him.] Brian Newark: Look,Mr Thunder..if you'd like we can give you some air time at the end of the show and then you can.. Thunder: Yer gonna make the INWI heavyweight champion...'wait'.. until the _end_ o' this show? What ails yer head,son? I'm the flamin' champion. I can do exactly what I want when I want. Yer jus' like the whole INWI bureaucratic suits who wanna try an' keep me quiet an' not expose the truth. An' that truth.. ...is the fact that they're scared. They know there ain't a man in this here organization who can put my shoulders ta the mat fer a three count. _You_know it. [Thunder points at Brian Newark.] _You_know it. [Thunder points at Scoop Ropes.] Scoops Ropes: Yeah..I know it! It's all of these other fools like Newark... [Thunder shoots a hard look at Scoops,who shakes his head in agreement.] Thunder: The whole dad-blamed world knows it.But most importantly... Commissioner Deluise knows it. An' like I said... ...he's scared. How else can ya explain why he hasn't announced an opponent fer me yet? I'll tell ya why. Cuz he can't find anyone with guts enough ta step through them ropes an' face me man-to-man,_that's_ why, Newark. Brian Newark: Well I don't think that's it at all. I think that he's just waiting for the _appropriate_ time to announce your opponent but as you know he was injured recently at the hands of one "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston and he's been hosp... Scoops Ropes: Oh stop making excuses for that lame duck Deluise, Newark. I think the champ's onto something here. Thunder: "Appropriate time"? An' jus' when would that be? When I'm standin' in that ring waitin' fer the bell? Naw.. he's delayin' cuz they got no one ta face me. Plain an' simple. Hell,I might as well retire this belt right now. Mebbe if he didn't try an' stick his nose in where it don't belong he'd be able ta eat solid food and tell me jus' who I'm facin' at this big fancy pay-per-view deal. Brian Newark: I'm sure that Commissioner Deluise will have an opponent for you in time for the big pay-per-view special coming later on this summer. After all look at the talent we've got here in the INWI. We've got such stars as the TV champion,Mr Apples... Thunder: Yeah an' what's that finishin' hold o' his...the Apple- sauce drop? Well if he ever stepped inta the ring with me the only applesauce he'd be concerned with was the fact that it would be the only food he could eat. Brian Newark: How about former Northeast champion..Ed Rogers? Thunder: Heh..he don't wanna come ta _my_ neighborhood,son. I'd beat him like a rug. He stole that strap from the Gavel an' he knows it. Now I ain't no Gavel fan but where's the flamin' commissioner when that happened.I know what it's like ta face two men an'lose yer belt because o'it. It ain't fun but more'n that... ..it jus' ain't right. I seen ol'Dell Pickle now has that title. Now there's a champion ta be proud o',eh? Well if ya remember right...I squashed him like a bug when he got in my face. Him an' that whole jWo pack o' dogs gave Rogers a dose o' his own medicine. Again without any consequences. But that's the kinda operation Deluise runs here. His favorites jus' run roughshod over everybody without concern fer the rules or consequences. An' that's why he's scared o' me. I ain't one o' his little puppets. He can't BS a BSer...an' he knows it. Who else ya got? Brian Newark: How about "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston? [Thunder pauses,rubbing his chin.] Thunder: Lemme tell ya bout Tony Houston. I like him. I really do. He kinda reminds me o' myself when I was younger. He's a tough wrestler but I got one bone ta pick there. If ya wanna prove yer tough ya don't go around beatin' up on old men. I hate Deluise jus' as much as the next guy, an' mebbe he did deserve ta get dropped on his squash _but_... ...he ain't a wrestler. Now Houston's a big boy an' I'm sure he can handle himself with whatever becomes o' that whole mess...it really don't concern me an' I could care less...but I will say this. If Tony Houston thinks that Brody Thunder would drop as quickly as DeLuise.....well, he'd be makin' the biggest mistake o' his short career. It's jus' that simple. So is that all ya got? Pickle, Rogers,Apples an',Houston? That's a pretty sorry buncha talent there Newark. I mean all o' the others like Springer,Cloud Eagle,Animosity,Apocalypse,Vito Rizzotto... [Thunder suddenly shows a look of real anger as he looks into the camera.] ...an' by the way "Vito"..if ya open yer trap again 'bout me I'll make it my personal mission in this life ta not jus' put ya on a stretcher...I'll put ya right outta the damn sport,son. I done beat ya like a rented mule _twice_ now so ya might wanna quit bumpin' yer gums before yer career is decided fer ya. Got it,amigo? Now then... Scoops Ropes: Sharp. [Thunder stops dead and glares at Ropes.] Thunder: What? Scoops Ropes: Zach Sharp. "The Technician". What about him? [Thunder nows appears to be very agitated at the mention of Zach Sharp.] Thunder: Hey....lemme jus' tell ya bout ol' Zach. Yeah...he's tough. He's smart. He's beaten me once. ONCE. An' that took two guys ta get the job done. But I've since rectified that losse an' put his shoulders ta that mat fer a second time. Ask him. He's man enough ta tell. I beat 'im. Fair an' square..in the middle o' the ring. Zach Sharp holds no mystery fer me. I know him as well as he knows me. An' when we hook'em up everybody knows there's gonna be one helluva fight.But the outcome will be the same. I'll get my hand raised an' he'll have another number in the loss column. It ain't about respect with me an' Sharp anymore... It's about bein' the best. An' that's what I am,Newark. The best there is in this sport today. Whether you, DeLuise, Sharp or anybody else likes it or not... [Thunder looks directly at Brian Newark.] ...it's the flamin'truth. Learn ta deal with it. [At this point, Thunder abruptly leaves the set. Brian Newark shakes his head as Scoops Ropes just looks at him and grin.] Brian Newark: Now why'd you have to get him so riled up at me for? Scoops Ropes: You should have seen your face when he looked at you. Hahaha..I thought you might have to go change your shorts...hahaha! {Brian Newark shoots a look of anger at Ropes, gathers up his papers and begins..] Brian Newark: *ahem* As I was saying before...what about the recent situation with the Rizzotto Brothers? These two have costed each other many matches and... Scoop - HELL with the Rizzotto's, I think you should go check your undies Newark! I think you are still shaking over the fact that Thunder was going to kick YOUR ass!! NOW THAT would of been entertainment! Newark - You know Scoop, you are really going to get on my nerve and I know there are people out there that frighten the hell out of you and god knows I want to see that day. Scoop - NOBODY and I mean NOBODY scares me and I won't back away from anyone! I will kick EVERYONES asses if I have to! [Suddenly, Wild For Da' Night by Rampage and Busta Rhymes erupts throughout the PA system and all the fans turn their gaze towards the aisle way, expecting a visitor but only air emerges from behind the lockeroom curtain.] [The crowd begins to boo as THEY realize who's music is playing....] [Two men begin to make their way through the crowd, and eventually, undetected directly behind ring announcers, Brian Newark and Scoops.] [One man is dressed in a navy blue Tommy Hilfiger hoodie, and has black Polo pants, his Timberline boots are black and with large treads on them. And has jet black hair with a similar goatee, and dark black Armani glasses.] [The other man follows behind, and is wearing a black INWI T-Shirt with a black vest that reads "FATAL ALLIANCE" in Crimson red scripture. This man is wearing dark black Nike windbreaker pants and his red hair is pulled back into a ponytail and rests on his shoulder blades.] [These two men are immediately recognized as "Cold & Calculating" Tony Houston and "Bulldozer" Tony Riley.] [Tony Houston, who was recently suspended for assaulting INWI Commissioner, has a microphone in his hand, and continues to walk towards the announcer's platform, still unbeknownst to Newark and Scoops, who unsuspectingly continues to chatter about various situations.] Houston: Hey Ya'll Shut the hell up.. [Newark and Scoops turn around and reveal a surprised look on their face at Houston being in the arena, following his suspension.] Houston: Why don't ya'll wipe those stupid ass looks off your faces, 'cause, frankly, ya'll look REAL foolish. To be truthful, I don't know why ya'll look so surprised. This little political attempt at preventing Tony Houston from achieving greatness, said nothing about me being in the arena. [Houston and Riley, who is remaining on point, searching for any attempted visitors that would attempt to come near Houston. And Scoops you'd best whipe that "I'm better than everybody" look off your face, or it'll be knocked off. Scoops: Go right ahead Mr. Houston, I do believe that we will no longer see you around the INWI. Houston: [reveals a slight grin] True, the little suspension handed down, said if I hit anybody blah blah...BUT it said nothing about this man [points to Riley] hitting anybody, now did it. [Scoops face changes from cockiness to humbleness.] Houston: Thank you, you fake assed thug. Anyways, I have a few things to get off my chest. First, Mr. DeLuise, I see no reason for my suspension. What did I do that was so wrong? I've been warning the INWI that if anybody, ANYBODY steps into the ring while I'm conducting my business, they'd be instantly smacked the **** up. Did you think that I'd make an idle threat? Son, the INWI is no place to make idle threats, and like I've been promising, I've delivered. [Houston turns and slaps a pound with Bulldozer.] Houston: [With a wise ass tone] Now...Onto more important matters. Now, Pervision, you saw close, REAL close..how the calculations can seriously affect your life. Ya'll may not take heed to the early warnings of Tony Houston, but ya'll feel to realize, I must talk all this shit for a reason. And I must have SOME kind of posse, and INWI I do believe you have met him. "Dozer...A former 2 Time World Tag Team Champion, but now as chose to remain....inactive..and will serve as my body guard, boys before you try bitching...especially you Perversion...he's got a manager's license, so he's got the right to be at ringside. Scoops: So you feel that you are superior to the I-- [Cuts Scoops over with a slight, cold stare that Scoops instantly responds with silence, as Bulldozer, as well, took a step in Scoops direction] Houston: Perversion, I have observed you are in the top ten, well as you have recently experienced, as the INWI has that was WAY before "Cold & Calculating" was around, and before, you so stupidly decided to interject your way into one of my matches. Ok, as far as Perversion goes..all I have to say is this..Once I entered the action last week, and IMMEDIATELY took advantage, you had no ability or will to counter...Well, in fact it took the help of a bum assed Commisher to save you, and Perversion, he felt the same...hhahahaa He asks for a mere $10,000, now that may affect ya'll broke mother*******...but not Tony Houston, shittttt...That ain't no thang. But bitch assed Perversion, take this to heart..Protect Ya' Neck. [Houston throws the head set at Scoops and he and Bulldozer Tony Riley methodically make their way to the exit of the arena, amist a showering of booes. Houston occassionally stop and instigates a battle of snaps with a ringsider....] Newark - Now THAT was funny. Scoop, I think you should go check your shorts! When Houston said Riley would kick your.... Scoop - Shut up you putz. Now what was all this crap about the Rizzotto's? ============================================================ [HE (the Don) is sitting in a dark room. Whoever loses will leave your organization, never to return. All I know is that DeLuise better set this shit up or the DON will kick his ass! Hahahaha....DeLuise gets ripped again....now THAT would be funny! Newark - Well I'm sure Commissioner DeLuise won't dissapoint us in this case. I'm sure the Don's wishes will be respected.... How about Zack Sharp though. He wants respect and he says he wants it now. He says his record is no fluke and it IS flawless... Scoop - Ahhh, he's just another punk who thinks he's Brody Thunder. No one and I REPEAT, NO ONE IS BRODY THUNDER!!! I wonder what he has to say now... ============================================================= [ SCENE: The stench of sweat fills the air as a kind of mist that just makes you feel dirty fills the dimmly lit gym. The faint sounds of kicks slamming into a bag echo through out the room. A shadow of a 6'5" tall man strikes the bag with quick, fierce intensity, the kind of intensity that makes you shiver everytime you are witness to it. The man pushes back his long black hair as some sunlight pushes its way into the room revealing the face of Zack Sharp. He then stops a begins to speak. ] You know what they like to call me. They like to call me the hot newcomer. They call me the man who burst into the INWI with a 7 match undefeated streak. Well, I am more than just a hot newcomer.... [ Sharp cracks a small smile ] .....I am the Technician. [ The smile disappears from his face as he pushes his hair back ] What does that mean you ask? It means I am one of the best. It means I am a veteran of the sport. I worked my tail off for some 8 years to earn that name and in know way I am going to be passed by as just a hot newcomer. [ Sharp delivers a thrust kick to the bag and then grabs a towel ] I am main event material and the rest of the INWI knows it. The Commish knows it, Thunder knows it, and every other wrestler in the INWI knows it. You don't rock the foundation of the INWI the way I have in the INWI without earning respect. [ Sharp throws the towel over his head as a shot of his waist is shown ] You see something missing. Well, I do. I see a strap missing from around my waist.
