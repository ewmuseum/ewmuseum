[INWI] Hot Spot, 10-20, Hour 1

20 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene slowly opens into a wide open shot of the parking lot at the Ugly Mug in Cape May, New Jersey.] [Hundreds, possibly thousands are swarmed around the ticket booths in a desperate attempt to purchase a ticket for this...... INWI Presents MONDAY HOT SPOT!! The Ugly Mug, Cape May, NJ [As several hundred disappointed fans slowly begin to make their way back towards their means of transportation, several jokesters speed by and throw several fire crackers sending a majority of them running for their vehicles..Why would that be Vince Coleman? The scene slowly fades out...] [Scene fades into the interior of The Ugly Mug and the arena area is full and it appears that everybody and their brother has a sign..HOUSTON 4 PREZ BROTHERHOOD SUX KING 4 GOD!!! LONEWOLF IZ DEE BOMB DIGGY!!! amongst hundreds amongst thousands more...] [The camera slowly pans around the arena, then focuses on the ring, and then finally to the INWI announce table where Brian Newark is waiting almost impatiently..] Brian Newark - WELCOME ladies and gentlemen to the NUMBER ONE Monday Night Wrestling Show on Television, INWI's Monday Hot Spot!! And We come to you LIVE from Cape May, NJ this week and what a WILD card we have for you tonight. Three solid hours of wrestling excitement. The Light Heavyweight Title could change hands TWICE tonight and tonight should be excellent. We have Scoop Raps, a new member of the Brotherhood will be introduced and Commissioner Jerry DeLuise has someone here who will be his answer to SAM ADAMS!!! Enough of the talk, let's go to the ring for our opening match!!! Match 1 - Light Heavyweight's James "Simply The Best" Pillow (with Angel) vs David McCarter This is a re-match of Invasion '97 without the INWI Light Heavyweight Championship on the line. Although the winner here will face the winner of Cherry and Bill Bob Woijicowski. And then the winner of that match will be the INWI Light Heavyweight Champion when they leave here tonight. In all reality, we could see a new Light Heavyweight Champion twice tonight. Both men are in the ring and there's the bell and we are set to go with two of the best Light Heavyweights in the business today. Both men come to the middle of the ring and lock up. Pillow sends McCarter backwards and then follows it up with a clothesline. Pillow with an elbow and then a standing leg drop. Pillow bounces off the ropes and there's a head butt! I have never seen Pillow do that before! He scoops up McCarter, sends him into the ropes, back body drop... NO! McCarter drops to one knee and nails Pillow in the face with a punch! Pillow staggers back and McCarter clotheslines Pillow over the top rope! McCarter grabs the top rope and slingshots himself over the top rope and on top of Pillow. McCarter scoops up Pillow and sends him into the metal ring post and then nails a super kick!!! Wow!! Pillow falls to the floor and he looks seriously hurt! McCarter grabs a chair and he is pounding on Pillow and the ref is on the outside and McCarter drops the chair and rolls back into the ring. McCarter just stands there and waits and heads back to the outside. McCarter grabs Pillow and sends him into the ring railing. McCarter then grabs Pillow and sends him back into the ring. McCarter back in the ring and scoops up Pillow, sends him into the ropes and theres a drop kick! McCarter scoops up Pillow again and connects with a tilt a whirl back breaker! McCarter really looks like he's on a mission tonight! McCarter sets up Pillow for a suplex and there's a German Suplex, that's Pillow's finisher! McCarter just hit the Simply The Best Plex!!! The ref counts, 1.......2.......Pillow rolls a shoulder,.....3!!!! What in the hell? Pillow got his shoulder up...what's going on??Let's hear the official word... Here is your winner in 14:23.... James "Simply The Best" Pillow!!! What in the HELL? Let's check this on replay... oh my god.... McCarter pinned himself on his bridge. Pillow has rolled out of the ring but McCarter is still in there and he looks hot! McCarter has left the ring and he attacks Pillow!! DDT! McCarter just DDT's Pillow on the outside and he goes to the back. Oh my god... Hey wait a minute... before our next match, Bill Bob Woijicowski is in the back.... =========================================================== [The scene is the INWI locker room...Scoop Ropes is with Bill Bob Woijicowski moments before his bout with Mr. Cherry...] SR: BBW can we get some words from you about your fight with Mr. Cherry that is about set to begin. BBW: I whipped his ass once and I am about to do it again. He may be a brother to the King but I am about to whip his princely ass. I am gonna take Cherry and gore the pit right out of him. I want that belt and he's got it...I wont rest until it is mine... and I am not a pleasant person in the morning to begin with...believe me Cherry you don't want me to lose any sleep...and I'm not planning on it and neither should you. SR: How about facing the winner... BBW: Not now we'll talk later I have a job to do. =========================================================== Match 2 - Light Heavyweight Title Match Mr. Cherry vs Bill Bob Woijicowski (with Mr. Flamboyant) This is the second match in this interesting Light Heavyweight title situation we have tonight. Not only is this match for the Light Heavyweight Championship, but the winner will face James Pillow tonight, who barely beat David McCarter in the first match. Woijicowski is already waiting in the ring and Mr. Cherry, the reigning Light Heavyweight Champion, comes to the ring. Cherry in the ring and he hands his title to the referee. The ref holds the belt up for everyone to see and we are ready to go. There's the bell and we are underway. Woijicowski and Cherry lock up in the middle of the ring and Woijicowski slaps a headlock on Cherry. The champ sends Bill Bob into the ropes, leap frog and then they collide and both back up a little. They both go at the ropes and Bill Bob kicks Cherry in the midsection and nails a swinging neck breaker. Bill Bob stomps on Cherry, lifts him up, sends him into the ropes and nails a drop kick. Cherry off the ropes and falls to the mat. Bill Bob goes to the top rope and dives on top of Cherry, a cover, 1...2....NO! Cherry kicks out. Bill Bob scoops up Cherry, sends him into the ropes and there's a spinning heel kick that sends Cherry through the middle ropes and to the floor. Bill Bob to the top rope...man, he is nuts. Cherry stands up and Bill Bob dives!! OH MY GOD!! Cherry moved and Bill Bob crashed into a chair and he looks seriously hurt. Cherry is up and kicks Bill Bob and stumbles back into the ring. Cherry rolls out of the ring and scoops up Bill Bob and slams his head into the chair. Cherry then picks up Bill Bob and WOW!! Cherry just hit a hurakanrana on the outside!! Cherry on top of Bill Bob and he is pounding away at him! Cherry picks up Bill Bob and sends him into the ring and then grabs him again. Cherry with an Irish whip into the ropes and nails a spinning heel kick. Down to the mat goes Bill Bob. A cover, 1...2...NO! Bill Bob kicks out. Cherry scoops up Bill Bob and nails a snap suplex and gives the signal. He's going up to the top!! He leaps for the Cherry Bomb Moonsault!!! Bill Bob rolled out of the way and both men are down! Bill Bob getting to his feet and he's up... and Cherry is getting up..Bill Bob with a DDT! Bill Bob is going to the top rope and there's the SPUR!!! A cover, 1....2....3!!! It's over!! Here is your winner in 17:23 and NEW INWI Light Heavyweight Champion, Bill Bob Woijicowski!!!! Match 3 "Too Slick" Rick Brown vs "Kodiac" Dan Harris This match could be exciting because the two of these guys are high flyers and like to take risks. "Kodiac" Dan Harris has been in a rut lately and "Too Slick" is looking to get himself back into that Light Heavyweight Title scene. There’s the bell and these two are underway. Kodiac bounces off the ropes and catches Brown with a drop kick to the face. Brown falls to the mat and Kodiac jumps on top and starts to pound away at him. Brown flips it over and starts to pound away on Kodiac. Brown picks up Harris, sends him into the ropes and hits a spinning heel kick. Harris falls hard to the mat and Brown with a quick cover, 1….NO! Harris kicks out at 1. Brown scoops up Harris and slams him to the mat and goes to the outside. Brown to the top rope and he dives, but Harris moves and Brown crashes to the mat. Harris takes Brown and sends him over the top rope. Brown slowly gets up, Harris bounces off the opposite side and there’s a drop kick through the bottom rope and Brown crashes hard into the ring railing. Harris bounces again and there’s a suicide dive as he lands on top of Brown. Harris picks up Brown and sets him up against a ring post. Harris gets a chair and he charges… LOOK OUT!! Brown just drop kicked the chair and Kodiac falls to the floor. Brown to the ring apron and drops a foot on the chair that is still on Kodiac’s face. Brown takes the chair away and Harris is busted wide open. Brown slams the chair on Kodiac’s face again and he is getting worse. Brown scoops up Harris and sends him face first into the ring post and then rolls him back into the ring. Brown to the top rope and nails a frog splash on Kodiac and doesn’t go for the pin. What is he doing? Brown is trying to signal for something… look out, here comes a suplex. What the…. that’s the Simply The Best-Plex!! Brown is using James Pillow’s MOVE!! 1…..2……3!! It’s over!! Here is your winner in 10:34, "Too Slick" Rick Brown!!! Next up is a match for the Tag Team Titles... wait... what the hell is this.... [The building goes black and the INWI Jumbo Tron goes into some footage from INWI's Invasion '97...] [The screen then fades into a video clip of the now historic ladder match between then INWI Champion The Springer and "The Ringmaster" Tony Houston. Houston's eye is in bad shape...and Springer sees that...Springer pulls up Houston and drops him eye first on the top turnbuckle with a Snake Eyes type move. Springer sees the fallen and almost destroyed ladder and begins to set it up...He's climbing up....Houston is now starting to move, the crimson mess that he's in, it's a wonder he can still see...but Houston is climbing up the opposite side of the ladder...both men are at the top..and are now exchanging right hands...Springer falls and loses his balance...Houston wipes the blood out of his eye...NO The ladder just collapsed...IT was able to support the weight of both the champion and challenger but when the balance of the ladder was changed by Springer being knocked off, it somehow collapsed..and both Springer and Houston are down again. Springer however, seemed to take the less of it, as Houston fell the ladder crashed on top of him. Springer slowly and painfully to his feet pulls up the ladder...and then Houston....Springer fires Houston into the ropes..Fireman's Springer Cutter coming u--No!!!! Houston fought his way loose..boot to the stomach..and a double arm DDT driving Springer's face into the fallen ladder..Unbelievable...Springer is now a bloody mess...It's a vampire's delight here in the Carrier Dome in Syracuse New York. Houston staggering around the ring...and he's now setting up the bloodied ladder....and he's climbing it...Houston is at the top of the ladder..He's got the belt and is on his way down...He sees Springer slowly getting to his feet..Houston leaps....off the ladder trying to get both feet safely on the mat to win the title...Springer with the SPRINGER CUTTER!!!!! on Houston!! Unbelievable..he hit it...Springer hit the Springer Cutter..Wait!!! Look at the Ringmaster Tony Houston...He's out cold, but still has a death grip on the INWI championship belt....That means we have a new champion!!!! Springer looking down on the only now nearly unconscious Houston. And with a shocked, to say the least, expression on his face simply shakes his head and exits the ring, as the INWI fans applaud both athletes for this possibly the best match of the year....I may hate the man, in Houston, but after tonight, he still is the biggest S.O.B. in the sport, but quite possibly the most intense..as for our former champion Springer, I'll guarantee He'll be back, Houston is still face down but has his arms still wrapped around the championship belt...I've never seen such determination..such instincts..he felt the Springer Cutter but refused to let go of the championship belt, and now we have a new INWI Champion.. Here is your winner in 25:32, after holding onto belt and both feet being on the mat..And NEW INWI Heavyweight Champion...."The Ringmaster" Tony Houston!!!! [As the video ends, the screen slowly fades to black....] [Several letters then emerge over the screen....] The Word on the street is that the rampage returns.. [With that, the rest of Wild For Da' Night by Busta Rhymes and Rampage is heard in the background, and that is the entrance for only one...the INWI Heavyweight Champion..."The Ringmaster" Tony Houston.] [The video fades then quickly returns with a close up of the INWI Championship strap...and the camera pulls back.. and again.. and again.. and finally, the man known as Ringmaster is seen sitting in a black leather throne like chair at his home in the self proclaimed Rotten Apple.] [Houston is sitting with the recently earned INWI Championship belt wrapped proudly around his waist and he has a bandage above his left eye from the affects of the grueling ladder match at the pay per view, and recently in a vicious match against Mr. Banana in which the eye was damaged, forcing the stitches to open and "juice" again.] Houston: [in his traditional relaxed and for lack of a better adjective calculating voice] Well, well, well INWI...and more importantly Springer..I's hate ta' be the man who told ya'll so, but I's told ya' so....I told ya' I'd enter the ring as challenger at Invasion '97 but I'd leave it victorious, and not only victorious, but the new champion [pats strap] 'N me bein' a man of ma' word, I did so..and The Brotherhood now has two major...actually three major championships...but we's don't really need to git inta that, 'cause the ummm... N.E.W.er fed or whateva' has tried ta' place an injunction of sorts against the INWI from whoopin' ass..well, boyz, guess what? Me and The Brotherhood ARE eWrestling...it's not a N.E.W. thing..not even really a INWI thing..but a Brotherhood ''n our quest for excellence thing.. [Houston stands..and makes his way towards the kitchen where he quickly returns with a tall cold one... and returns to his seated position after taking a long, healthy swill of his brewski.] Houston: See 'dis Brian Pil{beep} All week we's hear 'bout how this wrestler fought through pain..blah blah blah...whateva' that's why he OD'ed on pain killers...Well, for ya'll who saw that ladder match then my match 'gainst Banana last week knows that I can deal with pain..and deal with it victoriously...and Loose Cannon...this iz 'da only pain killer The Brotherhood needs..[holds beer glass over his head] and we ain't goin' anywhere...The Brotherhood is here foreva'...'N if any of ya'll are doubting this..check the tape...homeboy nailed HIS move that even defeated the great Brother Brody Thunder, on me and he STILL couldn't kill my efforts fer gettin' this strap..Come 'ere Cuj'. [With that Houston's large 130lbs Rottweiler emerges from seemingly nowhere and quickly begins growling at the interruption--the camera crew] Houston: Well ya' this man [pets Rotty on his head] says this interview clip is 'bout over..But to close ya'll segment let me say 'dis. INWI if ya'll thought what I did to GET the strap ....[video fades in Houston leaping off the ladder and being Springer Cutt but holding onto the belt to capture the INWI's most cherished trophy] was Bad...Just wait 'til ya'll see what I'll be willin' ta' do to keep 'da strap.... [With that the camera crew quickly make their way from Houston's home and quickly out the door with the Rott quickly on their heels..] Brian Newark - Well, it's obvious that video was taped....because Tony Houston is here tonight. Just what he and the Brotherhood are capable of is yet to be determined. I have a feeling a lot of people are going to have to answer to these people. Let's get to the next match... Match 4 - Tag Team Championship Solid Gold (with Jimmy Stardom) vs The Fantastic Surfers (with Prince Chuckle Wuckle) This is an interesting title match. Solid Gold puts the gold on the line against the Fantastic Surfers. Solid Gold stayed away from a couple of the other marquee names and signed to defend here. Of course, with these cocky idiots, the Fantastic Surfers could walk out of here with gold. It will be Bradley Gold and Hi starting things off. Collar and elbow tie up and Bradley slaps a headlock on Hi… Hi sends Bradley into the ropes, leap frog and then Bradley comes back with a clothesline that sends Hi to the mat. Bradley with a well executed elbow drop and then a boot to the face. Bradley takes Hi and throws him into his own corner and Hi tags in Tide. Bradley kicks Tide in the midsection and then drops a DDT on him. Bradley tags in Blake who takes over right where Bradley left off. Blake kicks at Tide and then scoops him up and nails a suplex. Blake with a cover, 1…2…NO… he picks up Tide’s head. I think he had him at that point. Blake takes Tide, sends him into the ropes and there’s a drop kick. Tide falls to the mat and Blake goes to the top rope and there’s a splash! Blake scoops up Tide and throws him into his corner and he tags Hi back in. Hi comes charging and Blake nails a belly to belly suplex on Hi. Solid Gold is just toying with the Fantastic Surfers. Blake tags in Bradley, double Irish whip and then a double clothesline. Bradley grabs Hi and tosses him out of the ring. Blake on the outside after Hi as Bradley has the referee occupied in the ring. Blake slams Hi’s head into the ring post. Jimmy Stardom starts to kick away at Hi and then Blake rolls him back into the ring. Bradley comes over, grabs Hi and nails a snap suplex. Blake enters the ring and Bradley sets up for a belly to belly and nails it as Blake super kicks Hi…. That’s the Solid Gold Eliminator!!!! A cover by Bradley, 1…..2…..3!! Here are your winners in 10:01 and still INWI Tag Team Champions, Solid Gold!!! =========================================================== Newark - What a great first hour of Hot Spot!!
