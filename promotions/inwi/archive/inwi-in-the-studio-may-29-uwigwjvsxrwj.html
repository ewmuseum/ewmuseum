[INWI] In The Studio, May 29

29 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
INWI - IN THE STUDIO May 29,1997 INWI Studios, Delhi, NY ============================================================ Brian Newark - Welcome Ladies and Gentlemen to INWI In The Studio. As usual, we are LIVE from Delhi, NY on Sportschannel USA and we have a great card planned for you tonight. INWI Heavyweight Champion, "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder defends his gold against the only man to beat him in the INWI, the Springer. Solid Gold will put their TV Belts on the line and we have two Debut matches for you as well! Let's head up to the ring for our opening bout of the evening. Before our opening match, lets get comments from Vito Rizzotto on his recent win to become the INWI Northeast TV Champion. ============================================================ [As Vito Rizzotto enters the locker room after his win, his brother Gino is waiting for him] Gino: Vito, I have just a couple of things I have to tell you. The only reason you won tonight is because you got help. Be thatas it may, you promised me first shot, so I want it soon. Another thing, what's going on with you, you used to be the toughest guy in the world, butnow, you're getting soft ! Vito: The title shot isn't up to me man, it's up to the championship comittee. And as far as becoming soft goes, I spent over 10 years with complete disregard for the rules, let's just say I read my rulebook recently and I like what I saw. Gino: You heard what HE told us Monday, we have to shape up or we're in deep you know what . . . Vito: To be honest, I don't care about HIM anymore and if he has a problem with that he knows where to find me. Gino: Just one thing pal, if HE tells me to come after well . . . Let's just say that "blood is thicker than water" crap is out the window. Vito: You just do what you gotta do. ============================================================ Match 1 - Debut Match Animosity vs The Baker Animosity, the newcomer here in the INWI will open up against an INWI vet, The Baker. This has the makings of being a good one as The Baker looks like he's ready and Animosity looks like he is no stranger to the ring. Baker attacks Animosity, but he steps out of the way and crashes into the turnbuckles. Animosity grabs Baker and slams him to the mat. Off the ropes, knee to the head. Animosity up with Baker, in the vertical position, suplex! Animosity looks strong and quite solid in this match so far. He could be a force here in the INWI. Animosity scoops Baker up in a powerslam and nails it! I think the Baker is hurt. Animosity scoops up Baker and nails a backbreaker. Animosity lays on his back, takes Baker's head and puts it between his legs and squeezes his neck while he has the Bakers two legs in each armpit and bends the Bakers back and Baker can't take the pain anymore....it's over! Baker has submitted to the Spine Bender and your winner in his debut match in 5:31, Animosity. ============================================================ Brian Newark - Before our next match, let's go up into the ring, where Michael Pontiff is with the former Northeast TV Champion, Firestar. FIrestar held the title for a day until Mike Garvey cost him the title. Let's see what he has to say. ============================================================ Michael Pontiff - He rose from obscurity and won the Northeast Television Title in an uset victory over Mike "The Gavel" Garvey. He then was defeated the next night by Vito Rizzotto for that title, with the help of Mike Garvey. Firestar, how do you plan on responding to these attacks? Firestar - Well Michael, let me say this. That was my first title in the INWI, but it won't be my last. And Mike Garvey, I have your number. (With that, Mike "The Gavel" Garvey emerges with a mike in his hands. And says something quickly...) Garvey - Firestar, you're nothing. As soon as I get done beating on Woijicowski and Springer, I'll whip you. As far as you being ready, you still don't know how to watch your back.... (With that, "Marvelous" Mike Quick jumps in the ring and nails Firestar with the infamous crowbar. He is just pummeling Firestar and he is broken wde open. Garvey storms the ring and together they are letting Firestar have it. Springer and Bill Bob Woijicowski come running out and chase Quick and Garvey off. Here comes a stretcher and they need to get Firestar to a hospital quickly...) ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well, I guess Mike Garvey is keeping himself busy lately. Our next match is for the INWI Television Tag Titles, let's get comments from the champions. ============================================================ "The Following announcement has been paid for by The House of Style" (The scene opens in an undisclosed hotel room. Sitting comfortably on a couch with the TV tag belts on there laps are Solid Gold) Blake: Look at us, The only three time Television tag team champions in the history of the INWI. Bradley: Thats right. We told all of you that this is the Golden Age of Wrestling and we are the leaders. We survived without Michael Hollywood. We beat the Friendship Express twice, proving our dominance. Blake: And tonight we face The Freaks. You two losers think you will have an easy time because you beat us once for these titles? Well, It will not happen again. Tonight we will prove our dominance over you and then we will work our way down the line. Bradley: Any tag team that wants to get in the ring with us is welcome. Especially Michael Hollywood and Harvey Thunder. We saw your match against those two jobbers. You guys were horrible. I think you should quit wrestling and stop torturing yourselves. Blake: You know something? The week hiatus that we had did not cool us down one bit, Glamour Boys? We haven't forgot about you two. Sometime down the line you will feel the pain of some Golden revenge. Bradley: Bet on Solid Gold. You can't go wrong with the best. (The scene fades into darkness) ============================================================ Match 2 - INWI Television Tag Team Titles Solid Gold vs The Freaks Here we go. Champs, three time that is, against the former champs. The Freaks have finally got their rematch and they are ready. It's Freak O' Nature and Bradley Gold starting things off. Gold off the ropes, catches Freak with a dropkick. Bradley again off the ropes, but Freak grabs him and sends him out of the ring. The Freak follows and Wieland is down there as well. They are double teaming Bradley out of the ring and Freak just nailed Bradley with a chair. They roll the hurt Gold brother back into the ring and Freak goes for a cover. 1...2...NO! Bradley barely escapes. Freak tags in Weiland and Weiland drops a leg. The Freaks are really going nuts on Solid Gold and you can tell they want those Title belts back. Weiland up with Bradley, who has yet to make a tag, and drops him with a hard suplex. Weiland drops another leg on the chin of Bradley and tags out. Freak in again and Bradley goes low and drops Freak to the mat. Both men are down on the mat. Freak rolls to Wieland and makes the tag. Wieland grabs Bradley before he can tag and hits a piledriver. He looks like he's setting him up...theis match could be over!!! Wait a minute, here come the Glamour Boys...Jimmy Cougar and Jason Starr dash into the ring and they are all over Wieland. Bradley rolls to the ropes and he and Blake walk to the back with their TV Title belts. The Glamour Boys continue their assault on the Freaks and toss them out of the ring. Your winners as a result of a disqulification in 6:37, The Freaks!!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well, the Glamour Boys are back to their old ways I guess you could say. Up next, in his debut match, is "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston. We have comments from him now. ============================================================ [The sun finally shows his self. Hiding like a coward behind the protection of the pillow like clows. The patrons of the largest city in the world are going about and minding their own business. Businessmen and women are going about their business. Millions of people begin shrinking from human size to ant size, as the camera zooms up.] [As the camera swings around it immediately picks up a man with black faded hair, and a black goatee and icey blue eyes staring over the ledge of the Empire State Building. He slowly lifts his head and faces the camera. This man is wearing a black T-Shirt with Fatal Alliance scripted in red lettering. He's wearing black Tommy Hilfiger pants, and tan Timerlain boots. The scent of Drakka Noir is emenating from his person.] [It's "Cold & Calculating" Tony Houston] Houston: [in a disgusted tone] I here that the INWI is the best of the best. And for an opening match you pit me against, I man named after a Kosher product. But that's all fine and good ya'll, 'cause he's fixin' to get a whoopin' just the same. Pickle you'll be exeriencing first hand the ass beatin' that coming to you for even THINKING to sign on the dotted line for this match. [Houston reaches in his pocket and pulls out a INWI wrestling magazine, and quickly scans and article that appears to have grabbed his attention] [Houston lifts his head from the article and flashes a devilish Jokerlike smirk] Houston: JWO? LOL Is that supposed to be a crew? Well, son you tell them to come on down, and they'll get an ass beatin' just for being with you. Boy, if by now you haven't done your homework on "Cold & Calculating" Tony Houston, you'll wish your had after a give you an ass stompin' Rotten Apple style. As far, that punk ass almost mafioso Rossit-Whateva'...That belt has gained the attention of Tony Houston, boy, you'd better watch your back, son..Cause that title will soon be mine. And son, even sooner than you think. But Vito boy, you have no clue what I'll do and when I'll do it. Watch your back son. [Houston turns his head and begins walking towards the elevator and enters it and begins his descend to the streets of the Big Apple] [Screen fades] ============================================================ Match 3 - Debut Match "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston vs Dell Pickle Well, I guess it's to return a favor, but Mr. Apples, the TV Champion, is sitting in the front row for this match. If you remember, Houston, the INWI newcomer, sat in the front row of Apples match on Monday Hot Spot!! It would seem that these two have a history outside of the INWI. Houston, who shows his cociness as he enters the ring, attacks jWo member Dell Pickle before the bell and is already issuing a severe beating. Belly to belly suplex from Houston and then he hits a swinging neckbreaker. He looks like he wants to end this one quick. Houston ready for a suplex....wait he points to Apples. Houston just nailed Pickle with an Applesauce Drop!!! 1...2...3!! It's over in 2:01, your winner, "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston. ============================================================ Brian Newark - Next up is a most interesting matchup. The INWI Heavyweight Championship. Springer vs Thunder. This is definitley going to be a wild one. Lets get recorded comments from the champion. ============================================================ [Brody Thunder is seen getting out of his red Jeep Ranch Cherokee in the INWI parking lot. A camera crew rushes up to him as he grabs his wrestling gearbag. He appears very impatient and very focused. The camera shakes as the crew walk with thunder into the garage area.] Cameraman: Any comments on your match tonight,Mr Thunder? [Thunder nevers stops walking as he answers.] Thunder: Yeah...I got me a comment. Ya tell that ham-n-egger Springer that tonight he's gonna be dealin' with retribution. I owe that tinhorn fer our last scrap...an' tonight I'm aimin' ta repay that debt...in spades. Cameraman: Any special strategy for the match? [Thunder smirks in disgust at the question.] Thunder: Special strategy? Hell yeah. I'm plannin' ta kick his over- rated ass around that ring an' outta the INWI fer good. This ain't jus' a title defense tonight. It's payback time. An' ya know what they say 'bout payback,doncha 'Mr Cameraman'? Now get that contraption outta my face...! [The camear crew stops as Thunder brushes passed them.] Cameraman: What about the comments made by Zach Sharp? [Thunder abruptly stops as if running into a brick wall. He wheels around with a look of utter contempt at the cameraman's blasphemous question.] Thunder: Sharp?! Ya wanna know what I think o' Zach flamin' Sharp an' his worthless dribble?! I heard him spoutin' off the other day. Tellin' 'his side' o' the match where I got robbed. Biggest damn pack o' lies I ever heard! Hell he didn't have the guts ta even mention the name o' it. It was the NWA world title! Bleep that out if ya wanna but that's it. Sharp...this ain't the flamin' NWA. This ain't no fed where bias officials are gonna protect yer thievin' hide! This is the INWI,ace. This ain't some rinky-dink organization who favors the pretty-boys. Yer in the bigtime now amigo... An' NEXT time when we meet I ain't jus' gonna end yer title hopes... [Thunder points his finger directly into the camera lense.] ...I'm gonna end yer career! [Thunder quickly enters a backstage door leaving the camera crew outside. The cameramna turns to his young assistant...] Cameraman: Hmph...I think that went well don't you? [The camera cuts to static abruptly.] ============================================================ MAIN EVENT Match 4 - INWI Heavyweight Championship "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder vs The Springer The Springer...the only man to beat Brody Thunder in the INWI. Finally, he gets his shot at glory and he looks ready. Brody Thunder, the INWI champ, is ALWAYS ready. To the middle of the ring these two giants go and they are jabbing with eachother. Springer swings and nails a right and drops the champ to the floor. Off the ropes and Springer with an elbow! Springer looks up and ready for this one. Springer again up with Thunder and slams him to the mat with good force and then goes to the top rope. Springer is going for a splash....he misses! Thunder rolled out of the way! Thunder is now up on his feet and grabs Springer and hits a short armed lariat. Springer down and Thunder drops a couple of knees then has his foot on Springers throat. The ref breaks it up and Thunder starts jabbing at him. He has the ref backed up in a corner and the Springer is up on his feet. Opposite corners, he flys and nails the Springer Splash!! Oh, wait, when he hit Thunder the ref got pinched and he is out cold. Thunder falls to the mat and Springer just locked Thunder in the coil!! We have a new champ, no man can survive this!!! Comeon ref, GET UP!!! Thunder is in pain. Whats this, look who's in the crowd...it's Mike Garvey. Here he comes, and nails SPringer from behind with a spinning heel kick. Springer crumples to the mat....Final Verdict delivered!! Garvey runs from the ring as Woijicowski emerges from the back. Thunder is up and sees that Springer is out, scoops him up, Thunderbolt!! The ref is coming to, 1...2...3!! Your winner and still INWI Heavyweight Champion, "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder!! Man, the Springer is not going to be to happy about this one! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well ladies and gentlemen, that's it from Delhi and our Studio Show. We'll see you again this Sunday for a LIVE card on Sportschannel. That card will be announced later tonight. So, for the INWI, I am Brian Newark saying so long from Delhi!! ============================================================ This was a presentation of the INWIŽ, May 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The INWI "The revolutionary force in E-Wrestling" Visit us on the Internet: http://www.catskill.net/harmer For info, E-mail: [detail withheld] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
