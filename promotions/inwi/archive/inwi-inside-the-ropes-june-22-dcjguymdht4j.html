[INWI] Inside The Ropes, June 22

22 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
INWI INSIDE THE ROPES Sunday, June 22, 1997 ================================================== Brian Newark - Well, I guess it's my pleasure to welcome you all to INWI Inside The Ropes. I'm Brian Newark along with my co-host Scoop Rop.... Scoop Ropes - CO-HOST? Why you horse's ass, I am the HOST! You are just some little peeon that the INWI decided to pair with me. You are nothing buta mic hog and before this years over, I know I'm gonna put you through a table. Newark - Well...I guess Scoop has a little aggression this week and maybe because the INWI has not decided to start up Scoop Raps again yet? Or is it because you got caught with that bimbo in the lockerroom? Either way Scoop, we know your in a pleasent mood today. Scoop - Newark, I'll change what I said before. It's not going to be the end of the year, it'll be by the end of this SHOW!!! Newark - whatever you say Scoop. But anyways, the action is getting hot here in the INWI and we are ready to go! As I said before welcome ladies and gentlemen to Inside The Ropes. We have some comments from some of the best wrestlers in the INWI tonight and we'll get to that right after this messa.... Voice - You just wait your sorry little ass Newark. You ain't going to no commercial yet. I'm gonna talk and if either you or Scoop try and cut me off I'll kick both of your asses. [All of a sudden "All Mixed Up" by 311 starts to blare on the loud speakers. Out comes INWI Television Champion Mr. Apples set in jeans and a red T-shirt with BSOG on the front. He has the NEW TV Title clasped around his waist.] Apples - You know, I have some things that I am finally gonna say, and you'll stay with me until I'm done or it's your asses. And don't run your mouth at me Scoop or I'll hit the Applesauce Drop so quick on you they won't have time to put you into a jar. See, I'm sick and tired of this friggin masked man. He comes out and jumps me and other wrestlers whenever he feels like it. After jumping me for so long, he all of a sudden saves my ass when Pimp Daddy Brown has me pinned. Now don't get me wrong, I didn't want to lose....but why all of a sudden is this guy protecting me? Is he the one that wants my belt? I want him to show himself now, where I can look him into the eye and know who this man is. I am sick and tired of being jumped....I want to see this man face to face. [All of a sudden it goes black and an erie voice comes on the sound system. You can tell that it is mechanically enhanced and he speaks for just a moment.] Voice - Apples. You are correct. I am the one that wants that belt. That is why I saved your sorry ass from Pimp Daddy Brown. I know you and all your weaknesses. I am the one man who can take you down and you will get to see me very soon. [The lights pop back on and Apples is just standing there with a dead look on his face. Newark walks up to him...] Newark - Apples... are you ok? Apples - It can't be...it just can't be..... Newark - What can't it be Mr. Apples? Apples - It just can't be.... [Apples then drops the mic and walks back to the back stage area....] Scoop - Newark... WHAT the hell was that all about? Newark - I have no idea Scoop, but let's go to that message now... ============================================================ Two men that know eachother very well. This time they are meeting for more than just a title...this time it's a war. "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder vs "The Technician" Zack Sharp. All at Heat Wave '97 brought to you by the INWI! And only on PPV....call your cable company today! ============================================================ Newark - And Heat Wave '97 will be hot Scoop! What a great card the INWI has planned for that one. Scoop - No doubt Newark. What a main event that is and with 18 exciting matches on the card, who knows what could happen! Newark - Well, Trent VenDenBerg has been having trouble with a man he used to manage and he is with Cloud Eagle in previously taped comments... ============================================================ <Camera focuses in on Trent VanDenBerg, with Cloud Eagle standing behind him in the locker room. There is no interviewer. Trent talks directly to the camera.> Trent: So I've lost my tagteam from the INWI, but that was because they SUCKED! They couldn`t cut it here, so they left for some other lesser federation. My man here (gestures to Cloud Eagle) is my sole representative here in the INWI now, and all my efforts are focused on him. He won his first round Ringmaster match against Mr. Cherry. I guess you could say Cloud really popped his cherry. (Trent laughs. Cloud Eagle is NOT laughing.) And, on a more sombre note, that former stablemate, and now renegade loser, Ultimate Perversion advances as well. Every silver lining has a cloud I suppose. I see that little wuss hasn't won any titles since leaving me. (Behind Trent and Cloud, Ultimate enters the room, and slowly strolls up behind Cloud. He taps him on the shoulder. Cloud just steps back, letting Ultimate through. Trent is blissfully unaware.) Well, I guess that just proves that the little pansy ass punk really isn't all that, and he'd beter watch his back because my man Cloud here... (Ultimate taps Trent on the shoulder. Trent turns around, and the color visibly drains from his face. Ultimate smiles.) Ultimate: Payback time boy. (Ultimate grabs Trent by the neck and pins him to a locker. Trent is struggling, but his feet aren't touching the ground.) Well Mister VanDenBerg, looks like your time on camera is over, and now I'm in charge. (Trent continues to struggle.) Now, who did you say was a pansy ass punk? (Trent would answer, only he's choking.) I can't hear you boy? I seem to recall you said *I* was the pansy ass. (Trent manages to say a few words.) Trent: (Seriously choked voice.) Cloud. Help me. (Cloud Eagle just stands back, head bowed.) Ultimate: There nobody here who's going to help you now Trent. It's just you and me, and I think we, and all the viewers out there, can see who the real man is, and who the "pansy ass" is. You've criticised me for so long my friend. You have dragged me down to your level. You couldn't stand that I quit your pathetic little stable, and since then you've been hounding me, putting me down... Well NO MORE! This is it. The final payoff, and it ain't gonna be pretty my friend, no siree. You're going down my man. (With that, Ultimate pulls Trent's head back, and smacks it hard against the locker, knocking him out cold. Ultimate then opens the locker, stuffs Trent inside, closes it, and locks it.) Now that little distraction is out of the way, I have a few words to say about me and MY man here, Cloud Eagle. We may be from opposite ends of the table, but we want the same thing. Justice. To Mister "Cold and talentless" Tony Houston, you say I don`t have a posse. Now I do. One man. Cloud Eagle. That`s all I need. And don't send your little puppy Riley after me, because I'll swat him like the bug that he is. No doubt there's some out there who are surprised by this move today. Well life is just FULL of surprises ain't it. I managed to get a few words with Eagle a few days ago, and I believe he has something to say. Cloud, the stage is all yours my friend. (Cloud steps up.) Cloud: Ultimate and I have talked, and though we differ in both styles and attitude, I can no longer sit back and let Houston cause my former stablemate, and, dare I say friend, so much trouble. With that in mind, and after a great deal of thought, I have decided to team up with Ultimate for the foreseeable future. For the record, I still consider myself under Trent's management (Ultimate just shakes his head), but contrary to what he may think, he does not own me. I am my own man, and I choose, for now anyway, to side with Ultimate and help him. (He turns to a locker and opens it. He takes out a peace pipe, lights it, takes a puff, then offers it to Ultimate. Ultimate graciously bows his head, and accepts the offer. He takes a puff, and returns the pipe to Cloud who steps back so Ultimate can take the camera again.) Ultimate: So INWI, there you go. Ultimate and Cloud. The ULTIMATE partnership of good and bad. (Wicked grin.) Watch your back Houston. You know Cloud won't attack you unprovoked, and that I gotta respect, but you also know that if you, or your little loser friend Riley for that matter, lay one finger on me, you're going to experience some REAL pain at the hands of my partner here. (Cloud Eagle nods.) One more thing. If the INWI can handle it, Cloud and I want a shot at the tag title. Just one shot, that's all we ask. Wether we get that shot or not could shape our future together. (A groan comes from the locker Ultimate stuffed Trent into. Ultimate punches the locker.) Shut up, I`m trying to talk here. Where was I?.. So, how about it INWI? Think you can handle the most awesome combination to hit the INWI? You know where to find us. (Ultimate grabs Cloud, and puts his arm around him. Cloud smiles, and puts his arm around Ultimate, and they both smile at the camera. They both lift up their shirts to reveal t-shirts, both of which say "Things ain't ever gonna be the same.") <As the screen fades> Voice: Trent VanDenBerg was eventually found in the locker by a janitor. After being taken to hospital, he was kept in overnight for observation, then released with an order to rest for 2 weeks. ============================================================ Newark - Now that could be an impressive tag team! And Perversion finally got his hands on VenDenBerg. Maybe that'll be the last we see of good 'ol Trent. Scoop - Are you crazy Newark? Perversion should be arrested for that assult on Trent. What a great guy Trent is. Trent, I hope you get better soon! Newark - You would hope that.... Let's get some comments from a man who will have a real busy week here in the INWI, "The Technician" Zack Sharp. ============================================================ [SCENE: Zack Sharp sits dressed in one of his many Armoni suits, lounging on is couch, reading one of the INWI's latest press releases. He has his shades on and his long black hair is tied back as usual. He browses through the article looking for his name and then a wide grin stretches across his face. He sets down the paper and looks up.] Zack Sharp: Yeah, I finally got respect. This Monday night I have the Dell Pickle for the INWI Northeastern Championship and of course I can not forget the big dance live on pay per view against Brody Thunder for the INWI Heavyweight Championship! [Sharp pushes a piece of hair out of his face] First thing is first, Dell Pickle, if I recall I was supposed to have faced you on my debut, but I think you were injured or something. This Monday night, I will face you for your INWI Northeastern Championship belt. I am the best, in the business and no Pickle is going stop me. Get ready for the ride of your life because Pickle, the Technician is on a roll. I am a frieght chain heading straight for my goal the INWI Heavyweight Title, I will run through things on my way and if it means you, then god dammit I will. The Northeastern gold is coming to the lair of the Technician. Like many other titles before, I will defend it with honor, but you all know there is one thing I want by the INWI Heavyweight Championship get ready INWI because June is the month of the Technician! And Thunder, don't think I forgot about you because you will get yours, oh, yes you will get yours. Bank on it! [Fade] ============================================================ Newark - Well, let's see what Sharp can do this week. Of course we know if he should win both belts, he has to vacate on of them. But, nonetheless, he has to face Dell Pickle and probably the entire jWo for that Northeast Championship and then he has to face the Heavyweight Champion, Brody Thunder...that's a rough week. Scoop - I agree Newark. I think Sharp may get by Pickle, but Thunder gets up for these main events... I don't see him getting beat. Newark - You wouldn't. Well, next up....let's visit with our favorite...er, nutcase, Mr. ed Rogers... (Uhm, are the INWI editors drunk again? A scenic yet seemingly non-wrestling related shoot of a duck pond can be seen through the eye of the mighty camera. On the grassy shores, a few children are frolicking like children do. A pair of lovers whisper nothings into each others ears over a mid-day picnic. And Ed Rogers is bathing amid the water foul. A black square censors his vital areas.) Ed Rogers: Hello television neighbors! It's me, Ed Rogers! I'm cleansing myself of the evil aura that Smelly infested me with. So when I fight those no good jWo demons, I'll be nice and lemon fresh! (A policeman walks up to Ed.) Policeman: Sir? It's been reported to me that you have been causing a small disturbance. Ed Rogers: Well...I.... Policeman: Don't you know that Indecent Exposure is illegal in this town? I'm afraid that I'm going to have to take you to the station. Ed Rogers: The station! Policeman: That's correct, sir. Ed Rogers: Oh, boy! Did you hear that television neighbors? We get to go to a train station! (He looks around, confused.) Policeman: Neighbors? Ed Rogers: That's right! Let's go now! (Ed hooks the Policeman's arm with his and skips off, waving to a group of nuns as he leaves, naked.) ============================================================ Scoop - HA HA HA HA HA....That had to be the FUNNIEST thing I have seen in a long time. You were right when you called him a NUTCASE!!! BWAHAHAHA!!! Newark - (Trying to be professional and not laugh...) well, uh, yeah...let's go quickly to another commercial... ============================================================ Heat Wave '97. Thunder and Sharp re-new and old friendship. Now the INWI Heavyweight Strap is on the line. Also, The Finals of the Light Heavyweight Tournament. Will it finally end between Bill Bob Woijicowski and Mike Quick. Or between Bodyguard Jones and Psycho Sal? Each of those matches will be in a cage....so we'll find out! ONLY on Pay-per-view, INWI present's Heat Wave '97, LIVE Sunday June 29! ============================================================ Newark - Well I for one can't wait for Heat Wave '97. That is going to be a fantastic card. Up next Michael Pontiff has caught up with Jason "The Crusher" Chandler about the Ringmaster.... ============================================================ Michael Pontiff: Hey Chandler, can we get a word with you about the Ringmaster tournament? Chandler: Not now. All I have to say is...one easy win in the books, more to come! Until this tournament is over, all my attention is going towards the tournament....Get out of my face! ============================================================ Newark - I guess short and sweet and to the point. Scoop - That's why he has to be one of the favorites Newark. He could care less about interviews, he just likes to kick ass! Newark - Well, let's see what happens to him in the next round, but he's definitley capable of winning that tournament. This next interview was taped after D.D. won his opening round match in the INWI Light Heavyweight Tournament, but as we all know...he's been eliminated already.... ============================================================ [Tiffany Love is at ringside with D.D. after the match of Stephen Casper and David McCarter.] Tiffany- Well how do you feel now that you know you will be facing David McCarter in the second round of the Lightheavyweight tournament? D.D.- First of all I thank Jerry for creating this tournament. I feel it is a great idea. Now as for wrestling David in the second round I am glad because experiance helps in matches and me and David are matched up pretty good in ring experiance. So all that leaves is ability and I feel I got the better on him in that catagory. I actually thought Casper would come out victorious because there are many ways to get out of a little sleeper hold. Tiffany-Thank you for your time and enjoy the rest of the night. D.D.-Your welcome and I cerainly will. [ Tiffany leaves the side of D.D.] ============================================================ Newark - Well, he has the right attitude and I'm sure he'll make an impact in the Light Heavyweight Division. Scoop - Nah, he needs to get meaner. With a finisher like he has, he needs to get real mean. Right now he's just a little pussy... Newark - Scoop, how many times must I remind you to act professional and watch your mouth on TV? Scoop - (under breath) Bite me.... Newark - (Gives a cold stare at Scoop...) Well....Newark: And speaking of match ups, we've got "The Bulldozer" Riley going up against the INWI heavyweight champion Brody Thunder in tommorrow night's main event. And if Thunder defeats Riley, he wants "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston in the ring. Now Brody Thunder's got to have alot on his mine these days. Besides challenges from Riley and Houston his opponent for our big PPV show was announced this weekend. The Lone Wolf will be squaring off against "The Technician" himself...Zack Sharp! IN WI cameras caught up to Brody Thunder yesterday at the arena. Let's roll that videotape right now. [The tape opens with a shot of the mpty arena. The wresling ring sits silently in the center of the floor. The arena is fully lit and entering the shot from the right is Brody Thunder. He's dressed in street clothes and smoking his trademark cigar. He walks to ringside, grabs the bottom rope with a hand as the camera makes it's way to a waist-up shot.Thunder, staring into the ring and through his clenched teeth,begins to speak...] THUNDER: Things sure are startin' ta really heat up 'round here. Good. I was gettin' bored. [Thunder pulls on the bottom rope as if testing it's tightness.] But tomorrow night I'm gonna be hookin' 'em up with the the man they call the " 'Dozer"....Tony Riley A.K.A.the henchman fer ol' Tony Houston,the man too scared ta face me himself. [Thunder turns his gaze towards the camera and smiles that evil grin.] Hey..it's okay,Houston. Fear and common sense has stopped many a fool from gettin' his head handed to 'im in this business. So yer in good company there. But ya can't hide fer long cuz I'm aimin' ta knot up that Riley's skull but good tomorrow night. It's real simple. He may be bigger an' me... an' he may be stronger than me... but he one thing he _ain't_... [The grin leaves Thunder's face and is replaced by stoic confidence.] ...is _better'n me. An' that's a natural fact,son. Tomorrow night I'm puttin' yer man down fer three an' leavin' laid out in the middle o' this ring him. An' then... [Thunder removes the cigar from his mouth and spits on the ground.] ..I'm comin' fer _you_,ace. [The cigar is returned to it's place between his teeth.] I'll show ya jus' what 'cold an' calculatin'' _really means. I've seen yer kind come an' go an' believe me when I tell ya ...they didn't get the job done. An' niether will Riley. So I'm tellin' ya straight up an' I'm as serious as a rattler's bite when I say this.. I'm fixin'ta kick yer ass right outta this sport,friend.Y'see winnin' a match with ya don't hold no thrill. Naw...I don't wanna jus' beat ya, m'man... [Thunder's gaze turns cold as he stares into the camera.] ...I wanna beat you _up_. It's jus' that simple. [He returns his gaze to the ring and again pulls on the bottom rope, snapping it as he releases it.] So ya bring yer boy Riley ta the ring tomorrow night. Bring yer mouth an' bring yer whole tired young-punk-with-an-attitude act. It ain't gonna help ya cuz first I'll tan his hide an' then if ya got any ..._guts_..at all...I'll put the numb on you too. I'm an' equal opportunity asskicker,amigo. Makes no difference who's across that ring. When the smoke clears only _one_ man will be standin'. An' that man... [Thunder hitches a thumb to his chest.] ...is Brody Thunder. [He turns to leave.] An' I wanna say one other thing. I'm talkin' ta the man who'll be facin'me at the PPV.The man who calls himself'the Technician'.. [Thunder wheels around,looking directly into the camera.] ...Zach Sharp. [Again a smile crosses his face.] Seems like ol' times don't it,Zack? I got somethin' you want.. ..you got nuthin' I need. Yet strangely enough,our paths keep crossin'. Well like the sayin' goes... "All good things must come to an' end." An' at the PPV I'm not jus' gonna end them title hopes an' dreams yer havin'...I'm endin' yer career.I don't cling ta remorse or regret,Zach...y'know that by now. So whatever I gotta do ta knock yer sorry carcass outta my way...ya know damn well,my friend... [Thunder drops the cigar to the ground and rubs it out. He then lifts his head and stares into the camera.] ...I'm gonna do it. Chairs..tables....whatever it takes. You know the drill. Ya might be the Technician now but yer gonna be in need o' a MOR-tician after the PPV. We know each other well. An' I _do_ respect yer ability. This ain't about respect. We both know that. This is about one thing an' one thing only... [Thunder holds up an index finger.] ...being the best. Plain an' simple. [Thunder again turns to leave.] So Zach..ya best be in the shape o' yer life. Ya best be in the top o' yer game an' ya best be ready ta fight...cuz the man ya best be preparin' fer is... [He turns his head back slightly around and looks into the camera...] ...the _best_. See ya at the PPV, my friend. [Thunder walks away as the camera focuses on the almost neon-like INWI logo on the ring apron. The screen fades to black. A single drumbeat sounding like a heartbeat can be heard getting louder and louder. Words fade in and out on the screen in succession: [thump thump..] JUNE 29,1997 [thump thump..] ONLY ON PAY-PER-VIEW [thump thump..] THE WAR BEGINS ANEW [thump thump..] SHARP VS THUNDER [thump thump..] AND THIS TIME.. [thump thump..] ..IT'S NOT JUST FOR TITLES... [The heartbeat stops abruptly. The words fade out followed by a clap of thunder which shoots these words forcefully onto the screen in a crimson red...] ..IT'S FOR REAL. [The words linger but then also slowly fade. Cut back to Brian Newark and Scoops Ropes at ringside.] ============================================================ Newark - That will be a war. I can't wait to see these two go at it. People have been waiting a while to see Sharp and Thunder go at it in the INWI. Scoop - That is, of course, if Thunder can get past Bulldozer on Monday Night Hot Spot!! I mean, Dozer is no small boy Newark. Newark - You are correct and that will be a battle. I can't believe that Thunder would put his title on the line less than a week before Heat Wave '97. That's two huge challenges within a week. Scoop - That's why he's called a champ. He wrestles and he defends that title. He's not like SOME champions I see who go three months without a defense of their title. Newark - I know what you're saying Scoop. Well ladies and Gentlemen, we are out of time for today, so for Scoop Ropes and the rest of the INWI, I am Brian Newark saying so long and see you at Monday Hot Spot!!. ============================================================ MONDAY HOT SPOT!!
