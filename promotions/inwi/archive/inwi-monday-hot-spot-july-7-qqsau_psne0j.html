INWI Monday Night Hot Spot!! ======================== Monday, July 7, 1997 Mick's Tavern, Washington, D.C. ========================================================== [The camera cuts to a shot of the lockerroom. Brody Thunder stands leaning on the open door of his locker. He is dressed in his wrestling gear. The INWI title is slung over his shoulder. The stub of a smolder- ing cigar is clenched between his teeth. He appears to be in deep thought when Brian Newark begins...] Brian Newark: Mr Thunder...Mr Thunder..tonight you face the man you defeated to win the INWI heavyweight title...The Icon" Pimp Daddy Brown. Your thoughts on tonight's match up if you would...? [Thunder,without looking up,slams the door. He spits out the cigar stub and stares straight into the camera.] Thunder: My thoughts?! I'll tell ya what I'm thinking! I'm thinkin' it ain't a good day ta be Pimp Daddy Brown! Cuz tonight he becomes the first wrestler that the Wolf sends ta the emergency room on account o' that pompous jerkwater o' a commissioner. Well then...DeLuise...buddy... [Thunder swings the belt down into his hands,looks at it then looks back into the camera with a look of sarcastic determination.] ..this one's fer you,m'man! Ya wanna play tough...ya wanna be a biiiig man an' pull rank an'threaten my livlihood? My family's way o' life? Fine. Yer responsible fer what happens out there,son an' I hope ya can learn ta live with it.......cuz it ain't gonna be pretty.I'll give ya fair warnin' right now. Ya better put yella tape around that ring tonight, "Commissioner"... [Thunder alternately rubs his taped wrists.] ...cuz yer about ta see a crime scene. [Thunder slings the belt back over his shoulder to leave but pauses. He points a finger directly into the camera.] An' Brown... ..don't think yer gonna sneak in the backdoor with the help ol'DeLuise an' steal this strap...cuz it ain't gonna happen. I'm fixin' ta come ta that ring an'kick the livin'tar outta yer two-bit jabrony ass. Yer gonna help me send a message.A message ta the Commissioner an' the entire INWI. A message that says... The rules may have changed,but the beatin's still the same. An from here on out...one thing's fer sure... [Thunder put his black hat on and cinches it down firmly.] ...ain't no one safe from the Wolf. End o' story. [Thunder slams his fist into his hand. He then storms off camera. Cut to Brian Newark.] Brian Newark - Fans that was the INWI Heavyweight Champion, Body Thunder! We all know that Commissioner Jerry DeLuise laid down the law to him that DeLuise was the boss, not Thunder. Thunder is hot and now tonight on Monday Hot Spott!! must face the man he beat to become the Champion, Pimp Daddy Brown. This match should be a wild one along with the other three we have for you tonight! This should be a great card, let's go up to the ring for our opening bout of the evening. Match 1 Ringmaster Tournament - Second Round Matchup Jammin' Jimmy Cougar (With Adam Kuhl) vs Cloud Eagle (With Ultimate Perversion) Adam Kuhl is at ringside with "Jammin' " Jimmy Cougar talking some strategy with his man and the Ultimate Perversion goes over some last minute ideas with Cloud Eagle. The bell rings and Jimmy Cougar looking across the ring at the massive Eagle who is dwarfing him by about 8 inches and outweighs Cougar by around 60 pounds! They lock up and Jimmy goes for Eagle's leg, he takes him down with a dragon screw takedown, nice technical move. He seems to be working over that leg now with a lever leg lock. Cloud grabs the ropes though to cause a break by the ref. Both men up again, another lock up and Eagle smashes his massive forearms into Jimmy's back. Cougar is down, but Eagle won't let him ake a breather, he lfts him right up into a chokeslam. Picks him up again, running powerslam. If Cougar takes more of these punishing power moves, he might not win. Eagle sends Cougar to the ropes, Eagle bends over for the backbody drop, but Cougar stops and gives a swift kick to Cloud's head. Cougar takes Eagle's arm and locks it in a FugiWarra arm bar....Cougar lets go and Eagle clutching his shoulder. Cougar goes up top. Eagle is up...Cougar off with a HUGE CROSSBODY, NO...Eagle dodged and Cougar hit the ref! Eagle up and gives a powerbomb to Cougar and now he hits two massive leg drops. Meanwhile Ultimate Perversion is trying to wake the ref....Eagle covers....WAIT, "Marvelous" Mike Quick comming down with a chair, HE HITS PERVERSION! The Ref is out, a manager is out and there is a pinning predicament...but that is quickly broken by Mike Quick with a shot to Eagle's Bakc and then to his head. Mike Quick is out of there as his namesake suggests and Cougar is comming to. He sees Eagle, and Eagle is a little woozy looking...COUGAR LOCKS ON THE COUGAR CLUTCH!! The ref is up, he sees Eagle tapping out...Your winner is Jimmy Cougar at 11:46. ============================================================ Brian Newark - Wow! What a surprise ending but nonetheless, Jimmy Cougar moves on into the next round with a big win over Cloud Eagle! Next up is a big match.... Vito Rizzotto and Mr. Ed Rogers! Let's hear comments from Vito Rizzotto! ============================================================ Vito: Mr. Ed Rogers, I already took one worthless piece of crap out of the INWI and tonight, I'm gonna do the same to you. Now that I have my family's entire support, nothing can stop me. But Rogers isn't the only one on my mind. Like I said before Heat Wave, Thunder, you know damn well the only reason you beat is because Gino nailed me outside the ring. Now you say you're great and all, so why don't you prove it. Let's do it one more time, pal. And while we're on the subject of beefs with different people, I can recall someone else costing me a match not too long ago. A certain masked man came in and cost me my TV title. Well, Mr. Banana, I'll be watching you very closely the next few weeks, so after I take the title away from Thunder, I'm coming after you. INWI look out, 'cause no one is safe from the Mob Drop anymore . . . ============================================================ Match 2 Vito Rizzotto vs Mr. Ed Rogers Mr. Ed Rogers in the ring now up against Vito Rizzotto. Vito is quite a lot bigger than Rogers in the gut area, but who knows what the outcome will be. Collar elbow tieup, Ed swings around for a waistlock, Vito with a back lbow to Ed's face to break that quickly though. Vito clotheslines Ed down. Picks him up again, then a stiff looking Short arm clothesline. He pickas him up on his shoulder for a powerslam of some sorts, Ed shifts his weight though, INVERSE DDT! Nice counter! Ed with a few Knee drops to Vito's arm now, and a few stomps. Ed picks him up, throws Vito into the ropes, and catches him on the rebound wiht an armbar DDT. Nice move. Ed goes up top and connects with a splash. Cover...1...2. and a kick out by Vito. Ed picks him up. Low blow by Vito.(Males in audience groan...) Vito with a drop kick to ed that sends him down. Vito picks him up and a Piledriver by Vito. Vito picking Ed back up...ED WITH A SMALL PACKAGE! 1...Kick out by Vito. Ed bounces off the ropes and dives down taking out Vito's legs. Oh no, Zack Sharp comming down to ringside now, you know that means trouble....Ed sees him! He's pointing it out to the ref and the ringside officials are keeping Sharp at bay...Ed picks Vito up who is clutching at his knee. Ed setting him up on the second rope..PUPPET MASTER!!! Pin by Rogers...1...2...3! An important victory for Ed Rogers! Your winner in 12:33, Mr. Ed Rogers! ============================================================ Brian Newark - That's a huge win for Ed Rogers. Thats a set back for Vito Rizzotto though. I'm sure Rizzotto will be back, he always has in the past. After all, he's held two titles in the INWI and I'm sure he plans on winning a third. Next up will be Jason "The Crusher" Chandler against "Marvelous" Mike Quick, but let's first hear from a man that is the first and only Light Heavyweight Champion here in the INWI... Sam "The Minuteman" Adams!!! ============================================================ (Sam Adams is found at a Fruit Market, walking up and down the aisles checking every piece of fruit he picks up. Just then, a mean look gets on his face as he picks up a can of frozen strawberries. Sam Adams marches striaght to the counter, shoves the can in the manager's mouth, and starts to yell out : ) [Sam Adams] You are a ****'* COMMIE (Sam Adams picks up the manager and throws him through the window. This draws a number of eyes toward Sam Adams. After he got that off his chest Sam seems to cool off. ) [Sam Adams] Well, what are you looking at? (Sam Adams looks kind of embarassed so he storms out of the market. He didn't go long before noticed that the "communist" camera man was following him.) [Sam Adams] Do you want me to freak'n talk, well I'll freak'n talk then. I challenge any sorry commie that will step in the stink'n ring with me. And for that commie, Mr.Rogers I also challenge you since you are only 4 stink'n pounds away. But I don't care who I wrestle Because it doesn'really matter which commie I wrestle becauseI know that all of them are going to end up like the commie manager of the Tooty-Fruity Commie Fruit Store. And for those freak'n fruit people, Mr.Apples, Mr.Cherry, Mr.Banana, Mr.Lemon, Mr.Peach, and Mr.Communist Strawberries I am going to squeeze you into a communist drink.And that is all I have to say to you, commie! ============================================================ Match 3 Jason "The Crusher" Chandler vs "Marvelous" Mike Quick (With Thomas Gold) And we have a lock up and Jason "The Crusher" Chandler with a quick knee to the gut, and a quick snap suplex on Mike Quick. Jason using his size advantage to manhandle Quick now, Jason with a boot to the chin, and he locks on a Camel Clutch. All his weight being set into the back of the smaller and lighter Quick. No submission by Mike and Jason lets him go. Jason setting up Quick for a Northern Lights suplex, but Quick blocks and hits a DDT. Nice Counter there by Mike. Mike climbing the ropes and hits and elbow drop on Jason. Mike with a body scissors now on Jason but he is able to break it soon after it is applied. Both men up now Mike runs at Jason with a Lou Thez press, Pin...1...kick out. Mike's up now and Jason gets to his feet...leaping Huricarana by Quick! Quick getting a burst of energy now and bounces off the ropes, somersaults, jumps and splashes Chandler. Pin...1...2. kick out. Jason to his feet, Mike running across the ring, he jumps on the middle rope, Asai moonsault connects onto Chandler but Chandler doens't go down! He gives him a sitdown tombstone. Ouch...Jason picks up Mike and gives him a full nelson, he's swinging Mike like a rag doll now. He turns it into a full nelson suplex with a bridge! 1.....2....no, a kick out by Mike Quick. Mike slides to the outside now for a breather, Jason wants to go out after him...the ref keeping Jason back...THE ULTIMATE PERVERSION RUNNING DOWN WITH A CHAIR! HE HITS MIKE IN THE BACK AND ROLLS HIM BACK IN! Jason saw it and doesn't look the gifthorse in the mouth. He rolls up Mike Quick and we have a 3 count. Great match! Your winner in 13:56, "Marvelous" Mike Quick!!!! MAIN EVENT Match 4 - INWI Heavyweight Championship "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder vs "The Icon" Pimp Daddy Brown This is the third time these two have met. What's the old saying, "Three strikes and your out?" Well, if Brown doesn't win it here, I have a feeling his chances of winning it are slim and none. This is the first person that Commissioner Jerry DeLuise threw at Brody Thunder to make a point. The two lock it up in the center of the ring, Thunder with a headlock. Brown sends Thunder to the ropes, leapfrog and then Brown sends Thunder to the mat with a big clothesline. That was huge! Brown scoops up Thunder, sends him back into the ropes and nails a drop kick! Brown is in control of this match and really wants to get that title back! Brown scoops up Thunder and slams him to the mat. Brown to the outside and to the top rope. He looks at the fans and they roar their approval. Brown leaps... Thunder gets a foot up and Brown lands square on the boot and he falls to the mat. Thunder rolls to the ropes and helps himself up and tomps at Brown. He won't let up and he scoops up Brown, short armed clothesline! That was a vicious move! Thunder scoops up Brown and nails a suplex and then goes to the second rope. Thunder just nailed a leg drop off the second rope, the cover, 1...2...NO! Brown gets a shoulder up! Thunder stares at the ref and continues to work, he scoops up Brown and connects on a couple of punches. Thunder off the ropes and misses a clothesline! Brown sends Thunder to the mat with a clothesline and then gets him up... a knee to the midsection! He's setting up for the Pimp-a-plex! NO! Thunder blocks it... knee to the chest... there's the cattebuster DDT!! A cover, 1....2....3!! It's Over!! Your winner in 14:24 and still INWI Heavyweight Champion, "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder!!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - That was a great match and that's the end of another Monday Hot Spot!! We had a super card tonight and we hope you all enjoyed it! So, for the rest of the INWI, I'm Brian Newar saying see you Thursday, In The Studio!! ============================================================ =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The INWI "The revolutionary force in E-Wrestling" Visit us on the Internet: http://www.catskill.net/harmer For info, E-mail: [detail withheld] INWIŽ, July 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
