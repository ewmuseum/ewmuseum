[The scene then fades away and goes to the beginning of INWI Hot Spot. This is a series of shots of all of the INWI Superstars. It shows Tony Houston holding onto the INWI Heavyweight Championship as Springer gives him a Springer Cutter off of a ladder, it shows Mr. Ed Rogers and King Apples going at it, it shows James "Simply The Best" Pillow with the INWI Light Heavyweight title and Angel at his side, it shows Sam Adams draped in a U.S. Flag with Militia at his side and the New York State Title over his shoulder, it shows Solid Gold in a four way brawl with the Comforters in their infamous battle for the Unified Titles, it shows Bill Bob Woijicowski and Mr. Cherry in their wild battle for the Light Heavyweight Title and then it shows an action packed seconds of ALL INWI superstars and it ends with the screen exploding and you see INWI..... Hot Spot with the Hot Spot in flames...] Brian Newark - Welcome back ladies and gentlemen to hour number two of Monday Hot Spot!! We are coming to you LIVE from the Broome County Arena in Binghamton, NY!!! Let's get back up to the ring!! Match 5 Tommy Graves (with Jonathan Hammer) vs Harvey Thunder Graves is in the ring and he looks scary. Thunder doesn't look scared however. Thunder charges and he hits a clothesline and Graves to the outside but he landed on his feet!! Thunder is on the apron and he jumps and Graves catches him and runs him into the post. He has the TV cable and he is choking Thunder with it. He irish whips him into the guard rail, Thunder reverses and Graves hits the steel. Thunder charges and Graves caught him again and he hits a tombstone piledriver on the outside. He rolls Thunder into the ring and Thunder is holding his head and trying to think of a strategy. Thunder with a boot to the groin and a DDT. He covers Graves for the pin 1.......2.....and a kickout and Graves sits up and gets up and a big kick to the face. Thunder goes to the outside again. Thunder is on one knee and Graves doesn't see him. Graves to the outside and he just got walloped with the chair by Thunder. Graves is still on his feet. Thunder with a little momentum and he just smashed the chair into the forehead of Graves and he falls to one knee!! Graves tries for a clothesline but Thunder with another chairshot. Graves finally goes down and Thunder throws him back into the ring and goes up top. He is wobbling on top but he jumps and hits a picture perfect frog splash. He covers Graves, 1........2.......no!! Graves kicks out and he is back up and Graves appears to be busted wide open from the chairshots. Graves with a chop to the throat and then an open hand punch. He sends him into the ropes and hits a powerslam. Graves back up and he lifts Thunder up and drops him over the top rope. Thunder's head snapped back and he hits the canvas. He is not moving. Graves is smiling to the crowd and he is just sick. He picks up Thunder again and he hits a neckbreaker. Picks him up and hits a swinging neckbreaker. Picks him up by the hair and hits a sidewalk slam and then he covers Thunder, 1........2......3no!! Thunder is up and that was only 2. Thunder back up and a poke to the eye and Graves back into the ropes and Thunder with an irish whip and he hits a jumping knee to the face. Graves goes down but he is right back up again. Graves with a right hand but it missed and Thunder with a vertical suplex. Graves back up and Thunder with a right hand but Graves caught the hand and now he is crushing the hand with everything he has. Thunder is on his knees in pain and Graves let's the hand go. Graves with a kick to the head and Thunder goes down. Graves with an irish whip and a mophia kick. He runs into the ropes and hits a leg drop. He picks up Thunder and Thunder with a low blow and he irish whips Graves into the corner and followes him in with a clothesline. Thunder is on the second ropes and Graves is up and he shakes the ropes and Thunder hits the buckles. Graves grabs him around the throat and hits a chokeslam from thje ropes. He covers Thunder, 1........2.......3!! Graves puts Thunder away with the chokeslam from the second rope and a big win for him. Again the winner of the match in the time of 8:14, Tommy Graves. Match 6 "Master of Pain" Michael Bane vs David McCarter This one is gonna be different as David McCarter as of late has had a change of heart. He just seems like he doesn't care anymore. Once he was one of the biggest fan loved wrestlers in the INWI. Now, he is confused. The crowds don't know what to expect from him this week or any week for that matter. Well, Michael Bane is ste to enter the ring and he starts the walk down the runway as the crowd boos. He flips off a couple people and laughs at them. What the hell is going on? Some guy just jumped out of the crowd and flattened Bane with a chair. Oh man, it's McCarter. Look at him, he looks horrible. He has a cut off t-shirt with the Union Jack on it and he is just wearing jeans. Doesn't he know he has a match today? McCarter picks up Bane and smacks him again with the chair and then he is kicking at him. McCarter just doesn't care anymore. What is wrong with him? I mean. he gave up an awful lot. But he is pounding on Bane. This isn't right, Bane didn't even see it coming. McCarter just slapped the figure four on Bane!! What is wrong with him??? Wait a minute, here comes MICHAEL BLEDSOE!!! I didn't even know he was here this week!! He nails McCarter with a chair and McCarter releases the hold. McCarter turns like a wimp and heads into the crowd. Bledsoe holds the chair up and the crowd goes nuts. Why did he save Bane? I mean, Bane and him hated eachother. What the heck. I guess this is a no contest as we never got it started. Let's get back to the ring... Match 7 The Asswhupper (with Mama and The Playmaker ) vs Michael Slater This should be a good match, definately has the caliber to be the best match of the night, only time will tell. Slater is in the ring, and is waiting in a neutral corner for Asswhupper....who finally arrives following a long wait following his introduction. Asswhupper and Slater in the center of the ring and are engaging in a staring contest.. which AW breaks with a right hand to Slater's ear. Slater fires back with an European Uppercut that staggers AW..Slater grabs Slater's right arm and drops AW with a Short Arm Clothesline. Slater off the ropes and drops down with a big Splash...Cover by Slater..One..two..No!!! AW rolls his shoulder. Slater pulls up Asswhupper and fires him into the ropes and catchs 'Whupper with a Powerslam..into a pinning predicament.. One.. two ..t-No!! Asswhupper again with a kickout. Slater pulls up AW and pushes him into the corner and is now working him over with body shots...left to the head..right to the body...and DEVASTATING left hook by Slater drops Asswhupper!!! Slater climbs to the second rope..he's setting up for a Push Off Splash...Bouncing on the ropes..and leaps..NO!! Asswhupper rolled out of the way and Slater crashes hard onto the mat. Asswhupper up quickly with..well..two middle fingers into the face of Slater... Asswhupper pulls up Slater and takes him right back down with a snapmare and AW bounces off the ropes and nails a dropkick directly in the face of Slater's mouth... OUCH!! Asswhupper pulls up Slater and kicks him in the stomach.right into a DDT by Asswhupper good combination wrestilng by Asswhupper, who covers..One..two..th-No!! Slater rolls his shoulder..Asswhupper pulls up Slater and scoops him and takes him down with a Side Walk Slam....AW goes to the outside and is on the top turnbuckle, Asswhupper leaps..and connects on the Flying Legdrop....AW covers...One.two..thre-No!!! Slater again just barely rolling his shoulder..Asswhupper pulls up Slater and pushes him into the corner..and rams Slater into the top turnbuckle...and again.... Asswhupper mounts onto the second rope and is pounding away at the face....OHHHH Slater just grabbed Asswhupper and walked out and dropped into a modified TigerBomb .. One..two..three!! No!!!! The ref is holding up only two fingers...Both men are down and hurting...However, Slater is starting to stirr....Slater pulls up to his feet and walks over towards Asswhupper...And drops a BIG leg across AW's back...Slater rolls AW over and covers...One..two..thr--No!!!! Asswhupper kicks out again. Slater pulls up Asswhupper and sends him into the ropes...Asswhupper ducks a clothesline and puts on the breaks....MONEY MAKER!!!!!! Asswhupper hit the Money Maker....he covers... One...two...Three!!!!!!! He got 'em..And Asswhupper earned this victory..proving he belongs in the INWI.....Let's get the official word... Here is your winner in 13:43, via pinfall....The Asswhupper!!!!!!! Match 8 - Northeast Championship King Apples (with Mr. Flamboyant) vs James "Simply The Best" Pillow (with Angel) This match pits two great athletes against each other, and to be perfectly honest, I really can't stand either one. However, these two have an extensive history with each other, and that's all that needs to be said, we don't need to linger on there wheres and whens, but Apples does "owe" Pillow one. Pillow is waiting in the ring and appears to be anxious at the chance of earning another title here in the INWI. Apples is carried towards the ring by several less talented wrestlers. Apples steps into the ring.And Pillow jump starts the match...Apples still has his Northeast Championship belt on..Pillow sends Apples into the ropes and staggers the champ with a Flying Forearm. Pillow up quickly and charges and takes down Apples..One..No!!! Apples kicks out quickly, both men up and Pillow bounces off the ropes and Springboards off the second rope and takes over Apples with a Springboard Hurricarana, Pillow is punching away at Apples' head..Apples kicks out and turns and levels Pillow with a monsterous right hand. Apples grabs Pillow by the throat and with unbelievable strenght pulls Pillow to his feet and takes him back down with a perfectly executed Belly to Belly Suplex. Apples covers...One..two..No!! Pillow kicks out. Apples up and yanks Pillow up by his hair..Apples sends Pillow into the ropes...Pillow ducks under a clothesline and rebounds off the ropes and is nearly decapitated by a HUGE Boot to the face by Apples. Apples pulls up Pillow and scoops him and lifts Pillow high over his head and steps out..OUCH The Northeast Champion just dropped Pillow with a Gorilla Press Slam..Apples off the ropes and drops a big leg across the back of Pillow's head...Apples covers again..One.. two..and t--no!!!! Pillow again, showing why he is a former two time holder of the INWI Light Heavyweight Championship. Apples pulls up Pillown and is choking him...the ref's counting..{one..two..three..four..fi..} Apples JUST broke the choke on time...Apples sends Pillow hard into the corner...Apples charges...Pillow just dropped down and Apples ran full throttle into the turnbuckle. Apples is stunned..Pillow rolls him up..One.. two..thr--No!!! Apples rolled out of the school boy roll up. Pillow pulls up Apples...Arm wringer into a Belly to Back Suplex..Good move by Pillow..using more leverage then strength...Pillow bounces off the ropes..Double Jump Moonsault onto Apples...NO cover by Pillow, instead Pillow goes to the outside, and climbs to the top turnbuckle... leaps... Impressive height on that Frog Splash..Pillow even had to adjust in mid air...Pillow hooks the leg...One..two..thre-NO!!!! Apples again rolls his shoulder..Pillow is readying for the Superkick..Apples is slowly making his way towards his feet..here comes Pillow ... SMACK Pillow just nailed Apples with the Superkick, however, Apples was able to get his hands up and the kick hit more shoulder then face...Pillow bounces off the ropes and nails a Spinning Legdrop and covers..One..two..three--No!! Apples with his foot on the bottom rope...Pillow up and slowly climbs to the top turnbuckle and waits for Apples to rise...Apples on his feet and Pillow leaps...High Cross body Pr--No!!! WHAT STRENGTH by the champion..Apples just caught Pillow, lifts him to his shoulder and drops Pillow hard with an inverted Michinoku Driver..Apples drapes his arm over the fallen Pillow..One..two..three!!!! He got 'em!!!!! Unbelievable...Let's get the official word. Winner: At 20:01, via pinfall King Apples retains his Northeast Championship. Wait what's going on..?!? Jonathan Hammer is running towards the ring and he's got a chair, and Apples is still down following an outstanding match...WAIT Houston just caught up to Hammer from behind and nailed him with a right hand..Hammer drops the chair and both men are brawling in the aisle way...Apples has his belt and is closing in on Hammer...HEre comes Tommy Graves!!! Graves and Houston, Hammer and Apples are brawling in the aislewauy let's get somebody to break this up..here comes security and INWI officials..and finally this melee is broken up...well folks we certainly got our money's worth on that match, but stay tuned as two of those four men will be out later on for the main event, pitting Tony Houston vs. Jonathan Hammer...
