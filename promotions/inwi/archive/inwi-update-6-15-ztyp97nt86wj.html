INWI UPDATE June 15, 1997 ================================================ Joseph Collins Reporting ================================================ Joseph Collins - Hello everone out there in INWI Land, I am Joseph Collins and I have some reports for you! First, I have 3 house shows to reprt to you and then I have some of the matches for the INWI's first Pay-per-view to announce! ============================================================ House Show, Buffalo, NY, Friday June 13 D.D. (Doctor of Disaster) pins Pig Pen in 6:32 The Big Brothers pins The Booger Brothers in 9:10 Mr. Ed Rogers pins John Q. Denver of the jWo in 13:20 The Glamour Boys pin The Fantastic Surfers in 12:01 Jason "The Crusher" Chandler pins The Baker in 8:19 TV TAG TITLES Friendship Express pin The Peeler Brothers in 13:57 TV TITLE Mr. Apples pins "Marvelous" Mike Quick after Bill Bob Woijicowski interfered at 12:13 NORTHEAST TITLE Jonathan Hammer beat Dell Pickle by DQ. Pickle retains title at 7:22 Sam Adams pinned Jonathan Sunset in 12:46 MAIN EVENT INWI HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder def Mr. Cherry by DQ when Zack Sharp interferred at 8:34. House Show, Rochester NY, June 14 Springer def Harvey Thunder by submission with the Coil at 12:42 Zack Sharp def Jarrid Hoss by pin in 5:23 NORTHEAST TITLE Michael Hollywood def Dell Pickle by CO at 9:35. Pickle retains title. Jonathan Sunset pins Vito Rizzotto at 8:32 after Gino Rizzotto interfered. TAG TITLES Terminators pinned Booger Brothers in 11:31 TV TITLE Mr. Apples beat Senator Bradley by DQ at 14:01 when the Masked Man interferred. UNITED STATES TITLE Ultimate Perversion def P.O. Box by DQ when Tony Riley interferred at 11:59 Solid Gold and The Glamour Boys battled to a 20 minute draw David McCarter pinned Jimmy Chooley in 9:31 MAIN EVENT INWI HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder pinned Animosity following the Thunderbolt at 9:32 House Show, Syracuse NY, June 15 Pimp Daddy Brown pins Malvolio in 8:31 Cloud Eagle pins Oz in 7:46 Ultimate Peversion pins Raver in 6:01 Springer and Mike "The Gavel" Garvey battle to a 20 minute draw NORTHEAST TV TITLE Bill Bob Woijicowski pins Devin Terry in 8:21 Bodyguard Jones defeats Psycho Sal in 8:32 by DQ when Raver interferes Apocalypse pins Stephen Casper at 11:21 Awesome X pins Animosity in 8:11 "The Technician" Zack Sharp pins D.D. in 12:41 MAIN EVENT INWI HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder pins Harvey Thunder in 13:54 ============================================================ Joseph Collins - Now how about some matches for the INWI Pay-per-View on June 29? Here are the first matches announced for the show! NORTHEAST TV TITLE CAGE MATCH Bill Bob Woijicowski (Champion) vs "Marvelous" Mike Quick UNITED STATES CHAMPIONSHIP P.O. Box (Champion) vs Mike "The Gavel" Garvey TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP Terminators (Champions) vs The Big Brothers SPECIAL STIPULATIONS Bodyguard Jones vs Psycho Sal (Mean Eugene in cage above ring for match) - if Sal wins, Jones returns to management of Mean Eugene - if BG Jones wins, he gets 5 minutes in the ring with Eugene GRUDGE MATCH "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston vs Ultimate Perversion TAG ATTRACTION The Comforters vs Contrast CAGE MATCH - LOSER TO LEAVE INWI Vito Rizzotto vs Gino Rizzotto NORTHEAST CHAMPIONSHIP Mr. Ed Rogers (Challenger) vs jWo - A member of the jWo comes out in 1 minute intervals. If Rogers beats all the members that come before Dell Pickle, Rogers will face Pickle last for the Northeast championship. The jWo will send 9 members with Pickle being the 10th. ============================================================ Joseph Collins - WOW! What a card so far for the PPV! And the MAIN EVENT has yet to be announced! Some of these matches will be wild so call your local cable operator and get the PPV ordered NOW!! We are out of time, so until next time I am Joseph Collins for the INWI saying so long! ============================================================ =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The INWI "The revolutionary force in E-Wrestling" Visit us on the Internet: http://www.catskill.net/harmer For info, E-mail: [detail withheld] INWIŽ, June 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
