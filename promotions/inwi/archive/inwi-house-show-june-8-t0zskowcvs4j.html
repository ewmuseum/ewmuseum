INWI House Show - June 8, 1997 Burlington HS Burlington, VT ================================================= Joseph Collins - Welcome back fans of the INWI and here is the report from Sunday afternoon's house show! Another great card was put on as the INWI went into Vermont for the first time and were treated to a packed house! Here are the results! ============================================================ Match 1 - "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston vs D.D. (Doctor of Disaster) This match featured two newcomers and both were undefeated entering the match. This was a great match to open up to as both men had chances to win. Tony Houston picked up the most impressive victory with the Cold Drop at 13:42. Match 2 - The Springer vs Dell Pickle (of the jWo) The Springer, former Northeast TV Champion, had a rather easy match as the fans of Vermont saw what the jWo was all about. Springer got Dell Pickle to submit in 3:24 to the Coil. Match 3 - Jason "The Crusher" Chandler vs Senator Bradley Jason Chandler kept up his climb to the top when he defeated the former Senator Bradley with the Crush in 5:58. Match 4 - Harvey Thunder vs Pig Pen (of the jWo) Harvey Thunder, with Michael Hollywood at his side looking for Solid Gold, picked up another win in his impressive line of victories when he pinned Pig Pen in 7:39. Match 5 - "The Technician" Zack Sharp vs "Maniac" Michael Manson Zack Sharp kept up his undefeated ways and his rising in the rankings when he beat one of Mike Quicks stablemates, Michael Manson, with the Ultimate Demise in 9:22. Match 6 - Television Championship Mr. Apples vs Gino Rizzotto Gino Rizzotto was looking for gold that his brother once held/ Gino was in this match and took control late when Vito came in the ring and went for a superkick, but Apples dropped and Vito nailed Gino. Apples tossed Vito out of the ring and then gave Gino the Applesauce Drop for the pin at 11:21. Match 7 - The Friendship Express vs Bonk and Bink (of the jWo) The Friendship Express, still looking to get their titles back from Solid Gold, picked up a relative easy win over Bonk and Bink. The Friendship Express scored the pin at 6:34. Match 8 - Tag Team Championship The Terminators vs The Fantastic Surfers The Terminators kept up their impressive streak of title defenses when they beat Hi and Tide, the Fantastic Surfers. With this win, they are looking at a rematch with the Big Brothers soon. The Terminators got the pin at 8:01. Match 9 - The Glamour Boys vs The Booger Brothers The Glamour Boys, Jimmy Cougar and Jason Starr, are still looking to get their hands on some INWI Tag Team gold. They picked up yet another win in the tag team ranks when they got the pin over the Booger Brothers at 7:17. MAIN EVENT Match 10 - INWI Heavyweight Championship "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder vs Vito Rizzotto Well, you can probably guess what happened here. Lone Wolf took control early, but Vito looked as though he may be able to pull off the stunning upset. Vito was aginst the ropes and had Thunder in a front face lock. Gino Rizzotto slammed a chair to the back of Vito, and I don't think this one was an accident anymore. Vito let go of the hold, then fell victim to the Thunderbolt and Thunder pinned Vito at 12:36. ============================================================ Joseph Collins - Well everyone, that was Sundays House Show report and we'll be back LIVE tomorrow night with Monday Hot Spot!!
