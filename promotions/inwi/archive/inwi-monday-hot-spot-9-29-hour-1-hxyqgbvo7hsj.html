The truth of the matter is, we are out to show that our federation produces better wrestlers and better matches. We are a superior federation to a supposed World power. Well, we don't want to go World. We are good, just the way we are. And we are not done yet. We know you are at our pay per view. Eddie Jacobs. I know you. You are one hell of a wrestler. But you are coming into our house. But, throughout this show, you will see highlights of what happened at the NEW Show... and no doubt, you will love every one.... [With that Hip Hop Hooray by Naughty by Nature cranks on the loudspeakers and the Brotherhood exits to the roar of the crowd. At ringside is Brian Newark, who is up on his feet clapping. Newark has the head set on and wears jeans and an INWI Rugby shirt. He sits down and we are ready to start....] INWI Monday Night Hot Spot!! ======================== Monday, September 28, 1997 Chester's Sports Pub, Oswego, NY ========================================================== Brian Newark - WELCOME ladies and gentlemen to Monday Night Hot Spot!! We are the great Monday Night program! And what a card we have planned for you tonight. 11 great matches and we will also bring you the highlights of this past weekends N.E.W. Pay Per View. That is where the INWi went 3-0 and kept two of the N.E.W.'s championships. Not only that, there is some trouble on the NEW side as "Kodiac" Dan Harris turned on James Pillow. Both men wrestle in the INWI as well as the NEW and what could that mean? We have Championship matches and also, Jason "Crusher" Chandler will be in week one of his "Gauntlet." All this and much more, only on INWI Monday Hot Spot!!! So, Houston, beware, cause you never know when the One Man Crime Spree will appear next... While this last portion of Kuhl's speech was going on, the crowd got noticeably excited, as Adam Kuhl made his way through the crowd. Obviously the speech was taped, but Houston didn't realize it and kept watching him on the video wall. Kuhl grabbed a chair and rolled into the ring and the fans were deafening. Houston turned around to see what the commotion was about and was met with a chair to the face. Kuhl then smashed Houston in the knees with the chair, which knocked Houston down. Then Kuhl scaled the ropes and came down on Houston with an Arabian Face Buster. Here comes King Apples and the rest of the Brotherhood came dashing to the ring. Kuhl left through the crowd to a mixture of boos and cheers. Apples stood up and stared at Kuhl who just had a sick smirk on his face.... [Hot Spot Continues....]
