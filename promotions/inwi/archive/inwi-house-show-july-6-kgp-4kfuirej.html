[INWI] House Show, July 6

7 July 1997

INWI House Show - July 6 Baltimore Public High School, 129 Baltimore, MD ================================================= Joseph Collins - Welcome ladies and gentlemen to another INWI House Show report. We had an exciting show at the Baltimore Public High School, 129 this Sunday. Let's get the results! Match 1 - Debut Match Thane MacDuff vs "The Knuckle" Jimmy Chooley (With Prince Chuckle Wuckle) MacDuff looked excellent in his debut against the veteran Jimmy Chooley. Chooley had little offense as MacDuff dominated from the opening bell. MacDuff won at 7:33 with "Lochness Lock" - a Texas Cloverleaf. Match 2 - Debut Match "Master of Pain" Michael Bane vs Pig Pen (With Oyster Boy) The lighter Michael Bane ran circles around the pudge ball Pig Pen. Bane took it to the air and on the ground and kept Pig Pen off guard. Pig Pen submitted at 8:21 to the "Strappado" - an Indian Deathlock. Match 3 - Debut Match "Stylin" Tommy Blayze vs "The Big Fan" Devin Terry (With Prince Chuckle Wuckle) The Big Fan also stood no chance at the quick Tommy Blayze. A mid siz man, Blayze overpowered and out wrestled Terry and won by pin with "Stylin' to Perfection" (A diamond cutter) at 6:33. Match 4 - Return Match Firestar vs Dell Pickle (With Oyster Boy) Firestar looked sharp in his return to the INWi, but only beat Dell Pickle by disqualification when Mike "The Gavel" Garvey interfered at 8:11. Match 5 "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston (With Tony Riley) vs "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset (With Prince Chuckle Wuckle) Houston looked awesome in this match and Tony Riley watched the aisle the whole time. No one tried to come out and Houston won after the Cold Drop at 6:22. Match 6 Michael Hollywood (With Harvey Thunder) vs Ultimate Perversion (With Cloud Eagle) This match turned to total pandimonium and was declared a double disqualification at 9:31. Mike Quick attacked Ultimate Perversion and Solid Gold attacked Hollywood. Match 7 The Springer (With Dr. Bedpan) vs Jason "The Crusher" Chandler The Springer picked up a big win in this match. This match was one of the best of the night and both men had chances to win. Springer won with a roll up at 11:32. Match 8 - Tag Team Championship The Terminators (With Johnny Danger) vs The Comforters (With Dr. Bedpan) These two teams really went at it for INWI gold. Both teams had their chances and the Comforters were setting up for the Sealy Posturepedic when the bell rang. The match was declared a 15 minute draw. Match 9 - Television Championship Mr. Apples (With Mr. Flamboyant) vs Oz Mr. Apples successfully defended his championship with a pinfall victory over Oz despite interference from Mr. Banana. Banana was about to cost Apples the title when Apples elbowed him off the apron and into the railing. Apples pinned oz in 12:46 following a choke slam. MAIN EVENT Match 10 - INWI Heavyweight Championship "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder vs Apocalypse Thunder wrestled well in this match despite the threats and the laws laid down by Commissioner Jerry DeLuise.
