[INWI] 5-22 In The Studio

22 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
INWI - IN THE STUDIO May 21,1997 INWI Studios, Delhi, NY ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well, after an unexpected vacation, the INWI has returned and I am sure that we will pick it up right where we left off! We have four exciting matches for you tonight and we hope you enjoy them! Let's go up to ringside for our opening bout of the night! Before that match, let's get recorded comments from an exciting newcomer, "The Technician" Zack Sharp! ============================================================ [ Zack Sharp stands in front of an INWI logo wearing his long black and white pants tights with white boots along with his leather jacket. He has his shades resting firmly on his face and his long black hair is tied back. He has his arms on his hips so that the jacket is opened just enough so that you can see his ripped torso. He then begins to speak ] Zack Sharp: Well, INWI, the Technician made his debut and it was successful. First, the Dill Pickle chickens out and then that other guy felt the Ultimate Demise. I proved what I am all about. Now, on In the Studio I take on Apocalypse. Apocalypse, I bet you must think you are immortal. Well, you can be immortal, but one thing is for sure you will be beat by the Technician. When the time comes Apocalypse you will go down. Reason because I am a cocky S.O.B, with the skills to back up my lip. You don't like it well deal with it. The Technician has spoken! [ Fade ] ============================================================ Match 1 "The Technician" Zack Sharp vs Apocalypse Well, Zack Sharp is trying to gather a match with Brody Thunder and he is coming up against his second challenge here in the INWI, this time Apocalypse. The Technician enters the ring and Apocalypse charges right away. Sharp takes the big man down to the mat and the bell rings. Sharp is back up, standing leg drop! Sharp is back up, picks up Apocalypse and DDT's him to the mat! Sharp has been on a roll ever since coming here to the INWI and he is looking strong against the much bigger Apocalypse. Sharp again goes off the ropes looking for an elbow drop, but Apocalypse moves. Apocalypse back up now, grabs Sharp and sends him into the ropes. Big Boot to the head and Sharp crumbles out of the ring and onto the floor. Apocalypse follows out and grabs Sharp and slams him to the floor. Hey here comes Apocalypse over to our table and he is crying about respect. Well maybe he should be more attentitive and then he will get the respect. Here comes Sharp and dropkicks Apocalypse into the barricade and breaks the count. Sharp back into the ring and goes to the top rope. He dives and splashes Apocalypse out of the ring!! Holy Cow!!! Sharp rolls Apocalypse back into the ring and he looks like he is ready.... yes, there it is, the Ultimate Demise!! 1...2....3!!! It's over. Your winner in 9:37, "The Technician" Zack Sharp!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well people, it looks like Zack Sharp will be a force to be reconed with here in the INWI. Next up is the Northeast title match and lets get comments with both of the participants! ============================================================ <Cloud Eagle is relaxed on a couch in a large hotel room. Tiffany is opposite him. She is also relaxed in a chair.> Love: I'm here today with Cloud Eagle. It's been some two weeks since Ultimate Perversion quit The House Of Denial in that memorable press conference. I guess first of all I have to ask has it affected you in any way? Eagle: Yes, in some ways. I'm upset that it came to this, but I could see it coming for a long time. Ultimate Perversion is his own man. He cannot be managed. Love: He seems to have improved somewhat. Do you think it will last? Eagle: I know he's getting frustrated about not winning a title yet. I have a feeling this improvement will not last long. Time will tell. Love: Onto other things. Your upcoming title match against Mr. Ed Rogers, how do you rate your chances? Eagle: I will do the best I can. I can ask no more than that of myself. The glory is not as important to me as it is to the others in this league. Yes, I would like a title, but I am not about to lose any sleep over it. Honour is more important than winning. It is better to lose honourably, than to win dishonourably. Love: Cloud Eagle, thank you. Eagle: My pleasure. ============================================================ (Mr. Rogers' music starts, and he makes he way to the ring. The fans, who haven't taken much liking towards Mr. Rogers boo him loudly. One teenager of about 17 wearing a Austin 3:16 shirt throws an empty coke cup at him. Rogers doesn't react, and someothers along the gaurdrail join along throwing thier trash at the NE champ. Instead of running down the isle to escape the cascade of waste, he stops in mid-step, making the all in throwing range begin to pelt him. He soon sits down and apparently starts to cry.) Mr. Rogers: Why don't you like me? I just want to be your neighbor. That's all. Please, don't hurt me. (whimper, whimper) (Ed Rogers goes over to the guardrail to hug (?) the fan who started the riot. The fan spits in Ed's face. Mr. Rogers starts down to the ring, half heartedly.) ============================================================ Match 2 - Northeast Championship Mr. Ed Rogers vs Cloud Eagle Well, this is an interesting matchup to say the least. Rogers, the rather zany nut case that he is and the honorable Native American, Cloud Eagle. Eagle has been in a bitter fued with his former stablemate, Ultimate Perversion. Rogers comes into the ring and hands the belt to the ref and attacks Cloud Eagle. He is just punching and kicking at Eagle, who didn't even have time to take his ringware off. Rogers climbs on top of Eagle and bites him and then rubs his head into the mat. Rogers is back up and goes over to the other turnbuckle and charges at Eagle. Eagle drops to the mat and rolls out of the ring and Rogers crashes into the opposite turnbuckle. Eagle back into the ring and he sends Rogers into the turnbuckle and nails some chops to the chest. Rogers crumbles to the mat and looks like he is crying. Eagle looks concerned and bends down to see what is wrong. Rogers reaches and goes for a punch but Eagle blocked it! Eagle scoops him out of the corner and hits a beautiful suplex! Eagle bounces off the ropes and drops an elbow and gets a two count. Eagle picks up Rogers and puts him up for a powerslam but Rogers is moving to much and down they both go and into the referee. Both men get up and start to slug it out and no one is gaining the advantage. Wait a sec, here comes Ultimate Perversion. He attacks Eagle from behind, sends him into the ropes and executes a diving clothesline. He then runs out of the ring and Rogers sees his chance. Rogers just nailed the Puppet Master!! The ref is coming to....1...2...3!!! It's over. Your winner in 12:04 and still INWI Northeast Champion, Mr. Ed Rogers! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well, Rogers kept the belt, but I am sure Cloud Eagle will not be the happiest person in the world after this match! Before our next match we have the INWI Heavyweight Champion, "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder. Let's see what he has to say. ============================================================ [Micheal Pontiff stands in the ring awaiting the next match. Suddenly the haunting strains of "High Plains Drifter" theme begins echoing the PA system. The lights dim and a bright crimson red thunderbolt symbol begins slowly spinning on the mat. The rolling sound of thunder can be heard and felt rumbling throughout the arena. A spotlight shines on the entrance curtain at the end of the aisle. The curtains bursts open as Brody Thunder strides through and heads to the ring. He is dressed in his wrestling gear with hat and ever-present black "EVIL, MEAN & NASTY" t-shirt. Around his waist is the INWI Heavywieght title belt. When he reaches the ring Micheal Pontiff gladly gives up the mic to avoid any problems. The house lights return, illuminating the ring. Thunder walks slowly around the ring,looking at the mat. He raises the mic and begins to speak as the crowd lets loose with a big heel pop.] Hey... ...ya can either shut up,sit there an' listen...an' I'll get this over with real quicklike... [Heel pop!] ...or ya can keep flappin' yer pieholes an' I'll be out here all night if I hafta... [Louder heel pop!] ...but either way...I got me somethin' ta say an' I ain't leavin' this ring 'til I say it. Yer choice. [BIG heel pop! Thunder leans against the ropes in mock waiting as the fans'noise eventually dies down and Thunder again raises the mic.] When I was a kid I was always fascinated by my shadow. It followed me everywhere. It never left my sight. It was always there whether I went indoors or outdoors. That damn shadow was always there...followin' me. I began ta hate my shadow. [A quizzical buzz filters through the crowd as Thunder continues walking around the ring.] An' then... ...as I grew older... ...I found out what that shadow was all about. Seems it was nuthin' more than a one dimensional result of light. It jus' clung ta me becuase it was..."livin"..as it were...off o' me. It wasn't anything on it's own. It wasn't anything at all.It couldn't talk to me.It couldn't frighten me.An'most o' all... [Thunder stops and stares into the camera.] It couldn't hurt me. [Thunder begins walking again.] But still it was always there. Followin'...followin'... Then I found out how ta get rid o' my shadow. It was really very simple. Shadows live in the light.So all I had ta do... [The camera gets a tight close-up of Thunder's face which flashes an evil smile.] ...was live in the dark. [Cut back tp a full ring shot as Thunder again begins to slowly walk around the ring.] An' y'know sumpthin' else? It came naturally. It was as if I belonged there. Now don't get me wrong. I ain't no jabrony out here thinkin' I'm sumpthin'...supernatural. No..I'm jus' an' ordinary man in a very dangerous business. I've bleed an' sweat ta get where i am in this sport today an' ain't a man here in the INWI who can say otherwise.But for all I've done in my career...guess what? [Thunder stops dead in the middle of the ring.] I STILL hate shadows. [Again he begins walking around.] An' now a new shadow has turned up here in the INWI. Another shadow that has followed me from place ta place. Relentless an' annoyin'. This new..."shadow"....wants to prove himself at the expense o' the Lone Wolf, Brody Thunder. An' I got me a little message fer this new "shadow"......the man called "The Technician". The man called... ...Zach Sharp. Sharp...we got some history me an' you. An' that history includes the fact that, yes... ...you DID beat me fer that NWA World title yer wearin' round yer waist. That's the truth. But why doncha tell the rest o' the story,champ? Why doncha tell'em how ya got yer hand raised over the Lone Wolf? Why doncha tell 'em it took two men... ..TWO MEN,Sharp... ...ta put my shoulders ta that mat when ya stole my belt in that triangle match. I was beatin' the tar outta that man when you sneak up from behind an' steal the win. Yeah...some champion. Why doncha tell 'em that one-on-one I put ya down like a red-headed stepchild,son. One-on-one ya couldn't get the job done. So ya get yerself inta my match with that other clown an' take advantage o' a man with his back turned. Guess our little one-on-one musta scared ya more'n I thought. I had respect fer ya,Sharp. Ya had the rep as a straight-up guy. Ya fooled me once. [Thunder stares into the camera again.] It ain't gonna happen again. Now ya wanna come inta the INWI an' bump yer gums 'bout "tradin' the title back an' forth"... ...well Sharp... ...the only thing you'll be tradin' is any notion that ye gonna take this strap fer a long ride ta the ER o' yer choice,amigo. Respect only goes so far in this business an' when it comes ta puttin' food on my family's table...well son....yer gonna find out why I am the INWI heavyweight champion....if I gotta put you outta this sport fer good. An' don't make the mistake o' thinkin' I'm jus' out here talkin' ta hear myself speak. Zach...you know me...an' ya know I'm as serious as a heart attack when I tell ya this. I am the best there is in this sport today...bar none. Yer robbery o' my NWA belt ain't gonna happen here. I-won't-let-it. So ya pick yer path an' tread it lightly,my friend... ..cuz ya never know when a 'thunderbolt's might strike. An' now I wanna address another sitruation which has appeared here in the INWI. The so-called jWo. [Big heel pop!] Seems a few folks have a problem acceptin' their lot in this profession. Seems one Mr Oyster Boy is shootin' off his mouth 'bout takin' over the INWI with a buncha jabronies who couldn't wrestle with their conscience an' win. Well Oyster Boy... ...clam up! [Big pop!] Yer little clique o' losers...ain't gonna get anywheres an' if ya think yer feelin' froggy... ..then by all means jump. I guarantee ya..ya won't like what ya find. This ain't no playground fer two-bit no- talent bums an' that pretty much covers you guys to a 't'. So you boys go play with the rest an' stay away from the best. You'll all longer careers that way.Comprende? An' finally...one quick word o' advice ta the latest tenderfoot who thought I was foolin' when I said I'd put his shoulders ta the mat. Ultimate Perversion... ...be extremely thankful yer still able ta eat solid food, son. Ya got beat by the best man ta lace up a pair o' boots in this sport. That ain't braggin'...this here belt proves it. So don't come out here an' put the badmouth on the Wolf. It ain't very neighborly an' more importantly... ...it ain't healthy. The next time I see you in that ring,amigo... ...will be yer last. The INWI ain't jus' another organization with it's own paper champion. Yer lookin' at the who can back up his claims in this ring. That's jus' a natural fact,people. It's like my pappy always said... "I ain't a hard man ta find... ...I'm jus' a hard man ta beat." [Thunder throws down the mic and exits the ring. The music begins to play again as the thunder clap rolls and reverberates throughout the arena. Micheal Pontiff resumes his position and awaits the next matches participants.] ============================================================ Match 3 - Grudge Match "Marvelous" Mike Quick vs The Springer Here is an interesting situation to say the least. Mike Quick is involved in a huge fued that involves himself, the Springer, Bill Bob Woijikowski, some guy that has jumped out of the stands with a camo shirt that says BBW, Mike Garvey and who knows who else. These guys just can't see to stop beating on eachother. Well, no sooner than Quick entered the ring, he and the Springer are pounding away on eachother. Springer gets the upper hand and has Quick down on one knee. Springer gives a couple of boots to the head and bounces off the ropes and nails a spinning heel kick and Quick looks hurt already. One can't help but to wonder who is here tonight and if they will take part in this match. Springer scoops up Quick and sends him to the outside. Springer bounces off the opposite ropes and is going to fly outside but Quick grabs a chair and the Springer crashes into it. Quick scoops up the former Northeast TV Champion and rolls him back into the ring. Quick climbs to the top and nails a leg drop. Off the ropes goes Quick and nails an elbow drop. Quick grabs Springer, Suplex!! Springer is now reeling and out of the ring he is sent. Quick has the ref tied up in the corner and what the hell is this? Two goons have just came out of the stands and they are pounding on the Springer. Wait a minute!! Look who it is and he now has the crow bar.... it's the Army guy again!! He chases those goons back into the stands and the ref has just seen what is going on. He calls for the bell. Let's get the official word. In 9:47, the refereee has declared this match a draw! Wow, this is gtting really bad between these guys. I don't know if they will end this one until they are all injured. ============================================================ Brian Newark - Up next is our main event and it will be one heck of a match. We have some uick comments from the challenger, Oz, before the match gets underway. ============================================================ Oz: Mr. Apples..you hold what I deserve...I deserve gold, and you will be the first to give it up to me. You know what I do with apples? I MAKE APPLESAUCE!! Once you feel the Emerald City Crusher, you will wish that you would have stayed in the fruit salad, where you belong. It is time for the NBK domination of the world of wrestling and you get the unlucky distinction of being one of the first to fall!! ============================================================ MAIN EVENT Match 4 - INWI Television Championship Mr. Apples vs Oz Well, Oz finally gets the title match he thinks he deserves and Mr. Apples is set to defend his INWI TV Title. There's the bell and we are underway. The two tie up in the center of the ring and Oz goes low and takes the advantage early. Oz picks up Apples and slams him to the mat and then drops a fist to the head. Oz locks on a chin lock and brings Apples up at the same time. Oz looks like he is going for a DDT but then just starts to nail Apples in the back with a series of forearms. Apples goes down to his knees and he looks like he may be hurt. Oz kicks Apples in the midsection and goes for a cover. Not even a two count as Apples got his shoulder up quick. Oz is up and goes off the ropes, leg drop... no, Apples moved! Apples is back up to his feet and now he and Oz are exchanging punches. Oz is staggering and Apples nails a short armed lariat and Oz goes down. Standing leg drop and then a knee drop. The tables have definitley turned here! Apples is now rolling and he drops Oz with a big right to the head. Apples follows with an elbow and now he's going to the top rope. Oz is up, Apples leaps, double ax handle! 1..2..NO! Oz kicks out. Apples scoops up Oz and slaps on a headlock. Running Bulldog! Oz is looking hurt.... and apples nails a neck breaker. That sets up for it... yes there it is! The Applesauce Drop! 1...2...3!!! Your winner and still INWI Television Champion, Mr. Apples!!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well folks, that's it from INWI Studio in Delhi. We have a great card coming up this Sunday night to be shown on Sportschannel U.S.A. That card will be announced later Today! Until then, Keep it in the ring! ============================================================ This was a presentation of the INWIŽ, May 1997
