The Joseph Collins Report July 29, 1997 ................................... INWI Studios, Delhi NY ============================================================ In this Issue we have: 1. House Show reports from last week 2. The Ultimate Perversion Situation 3. Harvey Thunder situation 3a. Commissioner DeLuise rules on Thunder situation ============================================================ Note From Joseph Collins: Hello everyone, Joseph Collins here bringing you the first edition of the Joseph Collins Report. Every week we will bring you house show reports and any late breaking news after Monday Night Hot Spot!! This week we have five house shows to report to you that happened last week. Also, the latest in the Ultimate Perversion saga as well as the Harvey Thunder situation and the ruling from Commissioner DeLuise. Also, the latest rankings will be out on the Web Site late tonight or tomorrow so check them out at < http://www.catskill.net/harmer > But, I'm sure you don't want to hear me babble, so here's all the news. ============================================================ HOUSE SHOW REPORT July 22, 1997 Newport, Rhode Island Main Event - INWI Championship Brody Thunder def Cloud Eagle in 14:09 Northeast Champ Zack Sharp def Ultimate Perversion by DQ after Ed Rogers interfered at 12:01 TV Championship Mr. Apples def Awesome X in 11:29 Tag Championships The Comforters def The Friendship Express in 18:01 TV Tag Titles Solid Gold def The Booger Bros. in 5:21 "Lightning" Steven Casper def Dell Pickle in 4:28 Vito Rizzotto def Jammin' Jimmy Cougar by countout in 9:36 The Springer def Animosity by submission at 8:52 Thane MacDuff def Pig Pen in 6:33 Jonathan Hammer def D.D. (Doctor of Disaster) in 11:39 July 23, 1997 Providence, Rhode Island Main Event - INWI Championship "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder def "Bulldozer" Tony Riley in 13:00 Northeast TV Bill Bob Woijicowski def "Maniac" Michael Manson in 11:01 Tag Championship The Comforters def The Fantastic Surfers in 12:09 United States Championship Mike "The Gavel" Garvey def Mr. Ed Rogers by countout after Zack Sharp interfered at 13:47 Jason "The Crusher" Chandler def "The Knuckle" Jimmy Chooley at 8:32 Bodyguard Jones def Mr. Banana by countout at 11:30 "Kodiac" Dan Harris def Baker following the double arm DDT at 5:39 The Friendship Express def The MacDaddies in 15:00 Cloud Eagle/Ultimate Perversion def John Q. Denver/Malvolio in 8:55 July 24, 1997 Worcester, MA MAIN EVENT - Tag Titles The Comforters def Solid Gold at 17:37 TV Title Mr. Apples def Vito Rizzotto in 15:58 Northeast Title "The Technician" Zack Sharp def Preadator in 9:45 The Terminators def The Booger Bros. in 8:01 "Too Slick" Rick Brown def Jerrid Hoss in 3:36 David McCarter and "Master of Pain" Michael Bane battled to a double CO at 11:33 Animosity def Pig Pen in 5:47 Firestar def Senator Bradley in 8:21 The Freaks def The Fantastic Surfers in 11:23 July 25, 1997 Boston, MA MAIN EVENT - United States Title Mike "The Gavel" Garvey def Bill Bob Woijicowski in 18:47 TV Tag Titles Solid Gold def The Friendship Express in 20:57 Oz/Apocalypse def Bonk and Bink in 7:33 Mr. Ed Rogers def "The Big Fan" Devin Terry in 8:11 Bodyguard Jones def The Baker in 4:33 Michael Slater def Vito Rizzotto in 15:22 James "Simply The Best" Pillow def Malvolio in 6:33 Ultimate Perversion/Cloud Eagle def The Fantastic Surfers in 12:01 "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston def Awesome X in 6:42 "Master of Pain" Michael Bane and Jonathan Hammer battled to a double CO in 7:39 July 26, 1997 Reading, PA MAIN EVENT - INWI Championship "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder def Jonathan Hammer in 15:39 Light Heavyweight Championship Sam "The Minuteman" Adams def D.D. (Doctor of Disaster) in 23:47 Tag Titles The Comforters def Bonk and Bink in 8:29 Northeast TV Title Bill Bob Woijicowski def "Too Slick" Rick Brown in 17:33 Jason "The Crusher" Chandler def "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset in 7:21 Mr. Banana def Mr. Cherry in 12:44 Mr. Ed Rogers def Michael Slater in 14:01 Harvey Thunder def Jonathan Hammer in 11:31 Ultimate Perversion def Preadator at 6:12 The Springer def Dell Pickle at 4:23 by submission ============================================================ The Ultimate Perversion Situation Captured by INWI Cameras following Monday Hot Spot!! <Backstage after Monday Night Hotspot. Ultimate and Cloud are walking back after Cloud's match.> Voice: That`s him. <Ultimate turns around to see Trent VanDenBerg pointing at him and gesturing to 3 burly police officers. The officers run toward him, and before he has a chance to move, have him down on the ground and cuffed.> Ultimate: What the hell is this? Officer 1: This gentleman... Ultimate: Gentleman? HAH! <Officer 1 knees him sharply in the back> Officer 1: This GENTLEMAN has reported an assault, and we know it's not the first time you've been involved in something like this, so you're under arrest. Ultimate: But the... Officer 1: Tell it to the Judge. Ultimate: Cloud, I think you'd better find me a lawyer. Officer 1: <As Ultimate is led away> You have the right to remain silent, you have the right... <As Ultimate walks past Trent, he mouths "You're dead!" at him. Cloud stares on impassively, then heads off to find a phone.> ============================================================ Harvey Thunder Situation INWI Report following Monday Hot Spot!! (Tiffany Love is outside the Locker Room with Harvey Thunder and Michael Slater.) Love: Ladies and Gentlemen, I want to be the first to congradulate the new Television Champion!!!! Thunder: I'm not a thief!!!!! Tell 'em Mikey, I'm not a thief!!!! (He drops the T.V. belt from his shoulder and it hits the floor.) Slater: (Picking up the belt) What Harvey is trying to say is he does not accept the way he won that match. Mr. Banana stole the belt from Apples' waste and Harvey was just in the right place at the right time. He is very upset at the way that match ended and he wants to give the title back to Apples because he is still the rightful champion!!!! Thunder: I want my own TV, but I'm not a thief. (Sticking his thumb in his mouth as He follows Slater back to the locker room) Love: Wow!!! This is an INWI first, A champion who doesn't want his title. I'm sure the INWI Championship committee will have to look into this situation!!! ============================================================ Note From Commissioner Jerry DeLuise: To The INWI: As you have seen by now, Harvey Thunder has given up his Television Championship because he feels he did not earn it. The INWI had recognized him as the Television Champion, but he feels he should return it to Mr. Apples. The match is official and Harvey Thunder is declared the winner and the champion. However, if Mr. Thunder feels that he should not be the champion and has given up the title, we at the INWI are in an interesting situation. We have made our decision regarding the INWI Television Championship. The Championship will be awarded on this weeks Monday Hot Spot!! to the winner of a 20 man, over the top rope battle royal. Contestants for this battle royal will be named later this week when the whole Monday card is announced. The winner of that Battle Royal will be the Champion. Mr. Thunder is the only contestant that we will announce right now. ============================================================ [Back to INWI Studios in Delhi, where Collins prepares to wrap up the show] Joseph Collins - What a great week of House Shows and two interesting situations! What is going to happen to Ultimate Perversion? And that Battle Royal should be awesome! Well, we are out of time, so for the INWI, I am Joseph Collins saying so long! ============================================================ =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The INWI™ "The revolutionary force in E-Wrestling" Visit us on the Internet: http://www.catskill.net/harmer For info, E-mail: [detail withheld] INWI®™, July 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
