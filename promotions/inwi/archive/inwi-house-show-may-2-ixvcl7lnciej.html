[INWI] House Show, May 2

INWI House Show - May 2, 1997 Elmira YMCA, Elmira, NY ================================================== Scoop Ropes - Welcome ladies and gentlemen we have a small house show to report to you from Elmira, NY. This was a six match card held in the Elmira YMCA and we had a good crowd on hand for it! Let's check out all of the matches! ============================================================ Match 1 "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset vs Dell Pickle Hubba Hubba, the first ever INWI Northeast Champion, finally got out of his losing streak when he defeated Dell Pickle at 9:23 following a high cross body block. Match 2 Michael Hollywood vs Senator Bradley In a rematch from SuperCard I, Michael Hollywood got a big win when he scored the pinfall over the Senator at 8:32. Hollywood was led to the ring by his new found friend Harvey Thunder. Match 3 Bodyguard Jones vs Raver This fued gets more intense as the days go on. Bodyguard Jone kept up his dominance of his former stablemates when he pinned Raver at 10:21. Match 4 The Terminators vs The MacDaddies The MacDaddies, looking to get back into the title picture, suffered a major defeat when they lost to the Terminators at 11:01 via a pinfall. On the other hand, the Terminators kept up their winning ways and inched closer to a title match. Match 5 Firestar vs Oz Firestar, after suffering a defeat during the three way dance, wants to get back into the championship scene. Firestar started here with a big victory over Oz, scoring a pin at 13:21, following the Burned Star. MAIN EVENT Match 6 - TV Title Mr. Apples vs Malvolio Mr. Apples successfully defended his belt in fromt of the wild crowd here in Elmira. Although the younger Malvolio gave Apples a run, Apples got the pin at 9:21, following the Applesauce Drop. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= INWI Wrestling.....
