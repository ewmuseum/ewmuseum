[INWI] Monday Hot Spot, 12-1, Hour 2

9 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Backstage at Monday Hot Spot. It's dark in a locker room, all but one light. The light shines on King Apples, who sits on a bench with a towel over his head. Being victorious earlier in the night, Apples has to face fellow Brotherhood member Tony Houston in the $25,000 Tournament set up by Sam Adams. No one is in the locker room other than Apples. A quick look around the room shows a couple of chairs thrown around and a couple tables tipped over.] KING APPLES - Tonight you see a miscarriage of justice. You see two men who should not face each other, have to face each other. Forced to square off by a stupid little plan done up by Sam Adams. Well Sam, your idea is going to back fire, I will promise you that. If by chance you beat Mr. Ed Rogers, you will find out pain in the finals. You see Sam, no matter if it is me or Brother Houston that wins this next match, you will have your ass beat in the finals. The Brotherhood will be $25,000 richer and at the same time, you will be spiked. Oh yeah Sam, you will be spiked for this stupid little plan of yours. This will bring back memories of spiking Bloom. This will be ALMOST as good as that. And as for your little girlfriend Militia, get in our way and you will suffer a spike as well. You see, this little stunt you pulled will be one of the worse things you ever have done Sam. If you make it out of the ring alive tonight, I will be surprised. Not to turn my attention away from you Brother Houston. You know what this match means. This is the first time in a long time that you and I will stare across at each other. I realize that this match should not be happening, but none the less, it is. We have to go out there as professionals and do our jobs. Both of us want to win this, no doubt about that. One of us will win and it will change nothing. It will still prove that we are both the best wrestlers in the world today. Them pitting us together will not damage the Brotherhood, it will only make us stronger. And then Sam, get your neck ready because it is gonna get snapped. Now get the hell out of my dressing room... [The cameraman gets chased out of the room by Apples and then fades to black.] [The letters I---N---W---I pop on the screen one by one. Then they explode as the logo of Monday Night Hot Spot comes onto the screen. The music "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne starts to crank and the arena goes black. The INWI-Tron pops on and the beginning of Hot Spot starts.... [It starts by showing a close up of INWI Champion Tony Houston with the INWI belt over one shoulder and the Ringmaster Belt over the other. He just smiles at the camera. It then shows King Apples nailing a tomb swirly on Ed Rogers. It shows Springer nailing a Springer Cutter on Scoop ropes from the top of the cage. It then shows Springer draped in an INWI flag flexing....] [It shows Sam Adams draped in American flag with Militia behind him and him laughing with the New York Title around his waist. It shows Michael Bane, Michael Bledsoe and Jonathan Hammer in one of the most grueling cage matches in INWI history. It then shows a close up of every championship belt in the INWI.... at different camera angles...] [It then shows Commissioner Jerry DeLuise in an INWI Arena looking over the INWI. Jason Chandler holding up his Television Championship and then the screen starts to show all sorts of pieces from the INWI..... James Pillow nailing a super kick on Rick Brown, Vinny Vitrone in his dog collar match with the "jWo" Icon Pig Pen, Ultimate Perversion and Cloud Eagle going at it with Harvey Thunder and Michael Slater, Bill Bob Woijicowski reading one of his ''stories'' to his nephew Bobby Bob...Cherry nailing a Cherry Bomb...Mr. Ed Rogers staring at Smelly...Tommy Graves nailing a choke slam...David McCarter on the mic...The Asswhupper and Thugs 4 Life standing with their arms crossed.... Solid Gold turning on manager Jimmy Stardom... The Comforters holding up the Tag Team titles, Malice in a match....The All Americans waving American flags...and a final shot of the jWo huddling together....] Then you see Pete Robinson walk past the camera with an American Flag jacket on...the camera follows him and on the back of the jacket you can see "INWI" as Robinson walks into the flames ahead.... then the camera fades and fireworks go off inside the arena and it's time for Hot Spot. The Camera switches to Brian Newark at ringside.] [Newark is wearing an INWI rugby shirt, has his head set on, wears wire rimmed glasses and is ready to go. He flashes the camera a smile and we are ready to go...] Brian Newark - Oh boy, we will explode tonight! Houston and Apples. It can't be seen anywhere else... only here LIVE on Monday Hot Spot. How will these two react when they get into the ring together? And what about the other first round match? Ed Rogers vs Sam Adams. This could be a real interesting one. All this and much, much more on Monday Hot Spot, hour number two!! Three hours of great wrestling, EVERY MONDAY NIGHT!! Let's go to the ring!! Match 5 Tommy Graves (with Jonathan Hammer) vs David McCarter Now this match could be rather interesting. These two have some things in common and that's the fact that they are both focused on certain things. What McCarter is focused on at this point is beyond me. I mean, he decided that the fans of the INWI were holding him down and has suddenly done a 360 degree turn. I mean, this is a former Light Heavyweight Champion, who had morals and they have suddenly vanished. Amazing. Graves on the other hand, he is looking high. He and Jonathan Hammer want to take down the Brotherhood, a chore that is hard by any means. None the less, this match is set to go, so let's get it started. The two circle each other and they tie up...bad move for McCarter as Graves takes easy control here. Graves grabs an arm and is working on it and gets McCarter to the mat and then stomps at McCarter. He keeps a hold of his hand and still is stomping at McCarter. I think Graves is here to make a statement. He wants a big win here over a former champion in the INWI. And who can blame him? What a way to catapult a career then with a win over a former champion. McCarter works his way to his feet and gives Graves the old thumb to the eye and Graves releases the hold. McCarter bounces off the ropes and nails Graves with a drop kick and staggers the big man. He does it a second time and Graves is teetering... McCarter to the second rope and there's a clothesline and Graves falls to the mat. McCarter with a cover, 1.....NO! Graves kicks out with some authority. McCarter brings the big man to his feet and starts to wail away at him. He is nailing Graves and is trying to keep him his size and who can blame McCarter there. Wait a second...Graves just reached out and grabs McCarter by the throat and choke slams him to the mat. What strength shown by the big man there and Hammer just tossed Graves something... it's an APPLES!!! Graves crushes the apple over his head and then bites a piece of it. Whoa, I think he is saying something there! Graves grabs McCarter and sets up for a piledr---Graves with a BIG POWERBOMB!!! Graves bounces off the ropes and drops a big elbow and then scoops up McCarter again...sends him into the ropes and the ref gets nailed. This is open game and Graves is setting McCarter on the top rope. Wait...what the hell? Here comes King Apples and Tony Houston and they have chairs!! Apples waffles Hammer at ringside and Houston is in the ring and drills Graves. Apples just choke slammed Hammer on the outside....what a cheap shot artist. They both are cheap!! Apples in the ring and he grabs Graves and sets him up for a piledriver and Houston is on the ropes.... SPIKE PILEDRIVER!! They take McCarter and toss him on Graves. Wait... Apples to the outside and he grabs Hammer and has him up....NO.... don't do it....OH MY GOD! Houston leaped from the apron and they spiked Hammer on the outside. Look out, here comes Bane and Bledsoe as they also hate the Brotherhood and the Brotherhood shows their true colors by dashing through the crowd. The ref is up and he sees the cover, no ref...no... 1.......2.......3!!! It's over and in a crap way. Here is your winner in 11:23, David McCarter. This crap is getting old. DeLuise has to do something about it. Match 6 Cherry vs "The Real Deal" Michael Bledsoe Well, here we go with what should be an interesting match. Bledsoe has stated that he and Michael Bane have thrown aside all of their differences and now will be watching each others backs, so this one could get quite wild if Bane figures his help is needed. Bane isn't on the card tonight, but word is that he is here, so what ever could happen. Cherry on the other hand has shown his new side and has been on a tear as of late. He is on the upside and is looking to regain the INWI Light Heavyweight Title. Cherry is in the ring now and here comes Bledsoe. "The Real Deal" has entered the ring and Cherry is slouched in the corner ready to go. There's the bell and we are underway. Collar and elbow tie up in the middle of the ring and Bledsoe with a knee to the gut and then nails a tilt a whirl slam and that was impressive. Bledsoe with a couple of kicks to Cherry and scoops him up to his feet and staggers Cherry with a couple of European uppercuts. That move has all of a sudden caught on in the world of wrestling. Bledsoe sends Cherry into the ropes, clothesl--NO! Cherry ducks, stops and nails Bledsoe with a dropkick. Bledsoe back to his feet and Cherry with another dropkick that sends Bledsoe out of the ring. Cherry quickly to the apron, Bledsoe up and Cherry nails an Asai Moonsault! That was amazing and now Cherry is pounding away on Bledsoe! What a match! Cherry grabs Bledsoe and sends him into the barriers---NO! Bledsoe with a reversal and Cherry slams hard into the cage. Bledsoe charges in and catches Cherry with a knee. Bledsoe then grabs Cherry and nailed a powerbomb on the outside!! Bledsoe breaks the count and then grabs Cherry and rolls him into the ring. Bledsoe back on the attack now and is on the second rope and nails an elbow. A quick cover, 1.....2....NO! Cherry kicks out and Bledsoe looks upset with the ref on that count. Bledsoe now pounding at the head of Cherry. Bledsoe bounces off the ropes, leg drop...NO! Cherry rolls out of the way and Bledsoe lands on his backside. Cherry over to the ropes and is up and is stomping his foot? Is he going for some sweet chin music?? NO!! Cherry just nailed some SWEET SHIN MUSIC!!! Cherry now kicks at Bledsoe and scoops him up and there's a belly to belly suplex. Cherry to the top rope and nails a FROG SPLASH!!! 1....2.....NO!! Bledsoe kicked out and I am not sure how he did. Cherry got some air with that one! Cherry picks up Bledsoe sends him into the ropes and grabs him...STUN GUN!! Oh man, when Bledsoe bounced off the ropes, the ref got knocked. Look out here. Cherry is going to the top, he is looking to nail a Cherry Bomb and YES!! He nailed it and there's a cover. The ref is out!! Cherry is up and looks frustrated and is going to the top again. He is looking for another Cherry Bomb. Wait, it's Michael Bane!! He grabs Cherry's foot and Cherry is crotched!! Bane runs to the back and the ref is coming to and Bledsoe is up. Bledsoe with a powerful belly to back suplex from the top rope and he covers Cherry, 1......2.......3!!! It's over! Oh man, what a match. It looks like Bane and Bledsoe are really in it together. Here is your winner in 14:35, "The Real Deal" Michael Bledsoe!!!! Rogers is in the ring and awaiting, almost impatiently, Adams makes his way towards the ring, with Militia and we know what kind of chaos he can cause at ringside...Adams into the ring..and Rogers attacks Adams before the bell...Rogers sends Adams into the ropes..and nails a Flapjack on Adams..and drops a quick leg..and another...two leg drops across the head of the New York Champion...Rogers covers..One.two..Adams with a strong kick out. Rogers pulls Adams up and scoops him onto his shoulder and nails a Running Shoulder breaker....Rogers bounces off the ropes and drops a fist into the former INWI Commish's throat. Rogers is on top of Adams and is pounding away.. Rogers looks like a man possessed...Wouldn't it figure though? Rogers pulls up Adams and sends him into the ropes again..Powerslam by Rogers covers..One..two..No!! Adams again kicks out. Rogers picks up Adams and plants him into the mat with a bodyslam..and Rogers is going to the outside and climbs to the second turnbuckle... He's waiting for Adams to get to his feet...Adams is up...Rogers leaps..Flying Clotheslin--No!!! Adams countered the Clothesline with a Standing Dropkick that connected on the jaw of Rogers...Adams slowly gets up and boots Rogers in the back..and again..several stern boots to the back and ribs of Ed Rogers...Adams..figures fours Rogers' arm..into a pinning combination..One..two..No!!! This time it's Rogers who kicks out. Rogers staggers out of the corner and Adams quickly goes to the top turnbuckle and leaps..Missle Dropkick to the back Rogers' head. Adams hooks the leg in deep...one..two..th--no!!!! Rogers again kicks out...The toll of his first round match could be taking a toll on him, as Adams did NOT have a first round opponent. Rogers nailed the Inverted Piledriver.. that's a lethal move..ask Steve Williams...and not Dr. Williams either. However, Rogers is still too hurt to follow up with a cover...but he is now starting to crawl over..he covers..One..two..thr--No!! Militia draped Adams foot over the bottom rope. Rogers up to his feet and pulls up Adams...Rogers is setting up for a powerbomb..he lifts...Adams spins through and lands on his feet.but is dropped by a vicious clothesline by Rogers... That move just spun Adams around in the air. Rogers pulls up Adams and crotched him on the top turnbuckle..he's going for the Puppet Master...Rogers on the ropes..WAIT!!!! What's going on?!?! The lights are out...What's going on??? WHAT THE HELL!! The lights just popped back on and their is a sock resting on the top of the turnbuckle...IT"S SMELLY IT"S SMELLY!!!!! Ed sees Smelly resting on the turnbuckle and drops Adams.. Where the hell is Rogers going?? Mr. Ed Rogers just lept over the top rope and took off down the aisle and appears to be fleeing..a Sock? I don't understand this man, not one bit. The ref's counting..{5..6..7..8..9..10!!!!} Rogers has been counted out....and Adams is still down in the ring...Damn Ed is strange to say the least...Let's get the official word... Here is your winner in 11:39, via count out, advancing to the $25,000 Tournament Finals....the New York Champion, "General" Sam Adams. Match 8 - $25,000 Tournament Second Round King Apples (with Mr. Flamboyant) vs "The Ringmaster" Tony Houston (with Mr. Flamboyant) Whoa!!! What an unbelievable second round for this tournament order by Sam Adams as his last act as INWI Commish. But needless to say both of these men are tough athletes.. both are champions here in the INWI, And probably most interesting that anything is..That not only are these two wrestlers both members of The Brotherhood.. but the two co-founding members. Both men are in the ring, and simply staring at each other with their respective belts in hand. The crowd is going insane as possibly two of the most hated men in the eWrestling world will do battle. There's the bell and this match is official. Houston and Apples lock up and Apples pushes Houston into the ropes...will we see a clean break? Yes....Apples backs away, and both simply smile. Apples and Houston again lock up and Houston takes Apples down with a deep arm drag...Apples back up quickly..and again both men exchange smirks....They appear to be talking to each other..let's see if we can get a mic. down there....."We ain't here ta' please deez biznitches...let's fight...it's all about 'da loot."__Houston...."Let's do it Tone!!"__Apples. Interesting....Apples charges and is taken down with a drop toe hold by the Heavyweight champ..Houston back up quickly and stomps Apples in the back...and bounces off the ropes and drops an elbow across the back of Apples head. Houston pulls up Apples, and Apples fires back with a punch to the stomach of Houston and drops the champ with a vicious uppercut....Apples is on his feet now and pulls Houston to his feet and fires him hard into the turnbuckle, Apples charges and follows in with a monstrous clothesline..Houston staggers out of corner..Apples bounces off the near ropes and drives Houston face first into the mat with a Bulldog... Apples covers...One..t-No!!! Houston kicked out before two....Apples stays on the attack and pulls Houston to his feet and scoops him..NO!!
