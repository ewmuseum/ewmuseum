[INWI] Hot Spot, 12-22, Hour 2

30 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Brian Newark: Ladies and Gentleman, I'd like to introduce to you a man who has returned with a vengeance here in the INWI, "The Gavel" Mike Garvey! *huge chant of boos explode from the crowd as the sound of a gavel striking a desk is heard throughout the arena. Slowly walking out of the dressing room is the Gavel with a disgusted look on his face and wearing street clothes. He makes his way into the ring and gets right up close to Newark* Newark: Now Gavel, your return surprised just about everyone here in the INWI as well as the INWI fans. You made a lot of comments two weeks ago and you were pointing out several stars here in the INWI, but who exactly do you have your eyes set on first? Gavel: Ya know, there's a lot of people here I'd like to dish out some hard justice to, and the way I see it, everyone is equally deserving of some. Springer,the whole Brotherhood, or Sisterhood rather....it don't matter one bit to the Gavel, I say bring on anyone and everyone, 'cause you, all the wrestlers in the back, and every single one of these fans know that the Gavel has what it takes to beat ANYONE here in the INWI. And.... *the boos get louder and louder...Gavel looks out into the crowd* Gavel: Shut up! Shut up! Newark, tell 'em to shut the hell up!!! Now, as I was sayin', bring on anyone you got. Newark: So what you're saying is if we got someone out of the back to come out here and agree to wrestle you, you'll do it right now? Gavel: What the hell are you talkin' about? I didn't say nothin' about wrestling tonight! The Gavel is too good to just wrestle anytime you fed-heads want me too...I wrestle when I want to, where I want to, and who I want...do you understand me? Now, being the one and only Gavel here in the INWI, everyone knows I can beat pretty much every wrestler here, but I don't waste my time with sorry pieces of trash...I only wrestle with people who almost measure up to the Gavel. I want some competition, and if you think you can dig up someone out of that trash dump you call a roster, then bring him on! And whoever the hell you bring out, I want him at the next pay per view, you got that Newark? Newark: Yeah, loud and clear. *The Gavel shoves down Newark to the mat as the boos are continuing to grow...he flicks off some fan with a Brotherhood sign in the front row and makes his way back to the locker room* [The letters I---N---W---I pop on the screen one by one. Then they explode as the logo of Monday Night Hot Spot comes onto the screen. The music "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne starts to crank and the arena goes black. The INWI-Tron pops on and the beginning of Hot Spot starts.... [It starts by showing a close up of INWI Champion Tony Houston with the INWI belt over one shoulder and the Ringmaster Belt over the other. He just smiles at the camera. It then shows King Apples nailing a tomb swirly on Ed Rogers. It shows Springer nailing a Springer Cutter on Scoop ropes from the top of the cage. It then shows Springer draped in an INWI flag flexing....] [It shows Sam Adams draped in American flag with Militia behind him and him laughing with the New York Title around his waist. It shows Michael Bane, Michael Bledsoe and Jonathan Hammer in one of the most grueling cage matches in INWI history. It then shows a close up of every championship belt in the INWI.... at different camera angles...] [It then shows Commissioner Jerry DeLuise in an INWI Arena looking over the INWI. Jason Chandler holding up his Television Championship and then the screen starts to show all sorts of pieces from the INWI..... James Pillow nailing a super kick on Rick Brown, Vinny Vitrone in his dog collar match with the "jWo" Icon Pig Pen, Ultimate Perversion and Cloud Eagle going at it ...Cherry nailing a Cherry Bomb...Mr. Ed Rogers staring at Smelly...Tommy Graves nailing a choke slam...David McCarter on the mic...The Asswhupper and Thugs 4 Life standing with their arms crossed.... Solid Gold turning on manager Jimmy Stardom... The Comforters holding up the Tag Team titles, Malice in a match....The All Americans waving American flags...Michael Bane holding up his newly won Light Heavyweight title...Jim Whitten pounding on Jonathan Sunset....It shows Mike "The Gavel" Garvey in his return to the INWI...and a final shot of the jWo huddling together....] Then you see Springer walk past the camera with an American Flag jacket on...the camera follows him and on the back of the jacket you can see "INWI" as Springer walks into the flames ahead.... then the camera fades and fireworks go off inside the arena and it's time for Hot Spot. The Camera switches to Brian Newark at ringside.] [Newark is wearing an INWI rugby shirt, has his head set on, wears wire rimmed glasses and is ready to go. He flashes the camera a smile and we are ready to go...] MONDAY HOT SPOT!!! HOUR NUMBER TWO!! HERSHEY PARK ARENA, HERSHEY, PA LIVE!!! Brian Newark - Welcome back ladies and gentlemen to Hour Number two of the number one wrestling program on TV. What a night so far and Mike Garvey has laid an open challenge to anyone in the back for the pay per view this weekend. And word just came from the back that Commissioner DeLuise will announce that The Gavel will have an opponent this weekend and he will announce it tonight!!!! Let's get to the ring as my co-hosts of Inside The Ropes get ready for their special six man match. Match 5 Scoop Ropes/Riley Michaels (accompanied by Mr. Flamboyant) vs Jarrid Hoss and Dell Pickle Now this in my opinion is sick. I mean, my two co-hosts of Inside The Ropes are actually going to step into the ring. You would think that Scoop Ropes would of learned that he is not a wrestler any more. I mean, The Springer whipped him and now they are going to take on the Asswhupper and the Thugs 4 Life in a special six man tag team match at the upcoming pay per view. So now they claim that they need a warm up match. But who will they have as their partner? It's a surprise and they are refusing to reveal it. And these two flops came to the ring with Mr. Flamboyant. Yippee, they are the poster boy spokesmen for the Brotherhood. There's something to be proud of. But this match is underway and it will be Scoop and Dell Pickle starting it off. Tie up in the middle of the ring and Scoop with a kick to the gut and then an uppercut. Scoop off the ropes and he clotheslines Pickle to the mat. Scoop grabs Pickle and then tags in Riley Michaels. Riley now in, off the ropes and nails a spinning heel kick to drop Pickle. That was an impressive move from Michaels. Riley off the ropes and nails a leg on Pickle and there's a cover, 1.......2......NO! Pickle kicks out and then Michaels kicks away at Pickle. Michaels tosses Pickle into his own corner and he tags in Jarrid Hoss. Collar and elbow tie up in the ring and Michaels with control and sends Hoss into the ropes, drop kick! Michaels drags Hoss to the corner and tags in Scoop Ropes. Scoop to the top rope and nails an elbow drop. A cover by Scoop, 1.......2......NO! Hoss rolls a shoulder and Scoop looks stunned. Scoop is up and is yacking at the ref and Hoss with a rollup! 1........2.........NO! Scoop barely kicks out and now he is pounding away on Hoss. Scoop signals this is the end and then goes and nails Pickle and he flies off the apron and nails the railing around the ring. Scoop takes Hoss and tags in Michaels and Michaels heads to the top rope. Scoop setting up for a piledriver, Riley leaps....SPIKE PILEDRIVER!!! A cover, 1.......2........3!!! I can't believe they won. Oh man.... Here are your winners in 5:24, Scoop Ropes and Riley Michaels. The crowd boos as they leave the ring. These two are to much. Match 6 Blake Gold (with Bradley Gold) vs Brian Shaffer (with William Gahan) Shaffer taking his shirt off now and here comes Blake with an axe handle to the head and Shaffer falls to the outside. Blake with repeated boots to the head of Shaffer and he is really doing a number on him. Shaffer with a trip and Blake hits the floor and Shaffer now on the offensive. Shaffer with stomps to the face and he throws Blake back into the ring and he sends him into the ropes with an Irish whip and he comes off with a big flying knee. Blake hits the canvas and pops right back up again and gets put down with a clothesline. Shaffer into the ropes and he gets back body dropped by Blake and that was a very good heads up move by Gold. Blake sends him into the corner and he comes charging in with a clothesline. Blake sends him into the opposite corner, no, puts on the brakes and sends Shaffer crashing into the same corner and charges with a clothesline but Shaffer a little too quick. Blake hits the buckles and a schoolboy rollup by Shaffer, 1......2....didn't get the 3 count. Shaffer getting a burst of life here and he is pounding away on Blake. He is going to town with lefts and rights to the jaw and he sends him into the ropes and hits a powerslam and the cover for the win, 1.......2.....and a kick out by Blake. Blake rolls to the outside to clear his head but Shaffer has other ideas. He comes out onto the apron and he is backwards to Blake. Blake with a low blow and then he powerbombs Shaffer off the apron and to the floor. Heads up thinking by Blake Gold. Blake rolls back in and Shaffer slowly crawls back in and Blake is putting the boots to him. Blake picks him up and hits a belly to belly suplex, 1..... 2.....and a kick out by Shaffer. Shaffer with a thumb to the eye and then a boot to the gut and he lifts him up and he is going for a powerbomb of his own but Blake reverses it into a hurracanrana. He doesn't hook the legs because he is running into the ropes and both men meet in a double clothesline. Both men are down and they are out. The ref is going to start the standing 10 count and he is up to 5 as Shaffer rolls around. Blake is rolling to the outside once again and he has a chair and he slides it into the ring. The ref is there as Blake comes back into the ring, the ref kicks the chair out of the ring and Blake is steamed. He is yelling at the ref and Shaffer from behind with a rollup, 1......2 ......no!! That was close and Shaffer with a big splash and the cover, 1.......2..... no!! Close once again. Blake with a thumb to the eye and a dropkick and Shaffer goes into the corner. Blake is in the other corner and he comes out and hits a splash in the corner, no!! Shaffer caught him in the corner and runs out and nails Blake with a spine buster. The crowd is on their feet as two men are rushing down the aisle. It's Gahan and Brad. They are both in the ring and now all 4 men are fighting with each other. Solid Gold and the All Americans to fight at the PPV but they are getting started right now. The ref has called for the bell and the official word is that these two have been DQ'd and they can settle this next Sunday. Again the official decision is a double DQ in the time of 12:31. =========================================================== [The camera slowly begins to pent up and fade in on the hallway of the INWI backstage arena. It goes past many other star's locker rooms including Solid Gold & the jWo. The camera ( or whoever is carrying it ) begins to slow down as it comes around the corner. Then, the camera comes to a complete stop in front of a peculiar locker room which has a bunch of Christmas lights that read: "VINDICAToR" Vinny Vitrone [After a brief pause the cameraman than knocks on the door. He continues knocking as no one answered the first series. Finally, you hear footsteps. Vinny Vitrone comes to the doorway. He seems to get gotten out of the shower as his hair is still dripping on his grey carpet. The cameraman explains something to Vitrone and Vinny lets him in.] [Cameraman:] Hello, Mr. Vitrone. I'm here to get a brief interview for this Monday's match-up against The Comforters. This match is your first opportunity for a strap of gold here in INWI, what do you think about this match. [Vitrone:] Well. To tell you the truth, I haven't thought about this match once. For me, it's just another trip to "Sleepy's". What the hell? I've never been beat up while I was sleeping on a bed, why would I get beat by one while awake? Come on, son. I'm a SUPERSTAR, why don't ya' ask a li'l bit of a better question than that? [Cameraman:] Well, I'm here to do my job, and these are the questions I was assigned to ask. I'm a professional journalist and I graduated college, I feel that these ques----- ["Vindicator" Vinny Vitrone:] Wow, big fraggin' deal. I'm a member of the BROTHERHOOD! I've put 25 people on the shelf, permanently. I've been to jail n' I'm only 25! I was screwed over by the Mafia! I've been setup to be killed! I'm the former OWeCW Champion! I'm the former HWA I-C Champion. Current HWA Tag Champion. I'm the 1997 EHA King o' The Mat! I was the NEW tag champion by myself. I'm not in a good mood right now, yesterday, my neighbor told me a story. The bitch told me I'm not all ITALIAN. She told me I'm 20% Irish. Ha, what does she know? No wonder she works as a head nurse at a major hospital, what kind of job is that? Also, if you want my share of comments, I'll tell you that either I'm gonna have some gold quickly in INWI or I'm gonna hurt someone. Beware of "Family Dinner". Beware of "Vindication Drop". Beware of "Lil' Brutta' Cutta' ". Beware of the HOOOOOOOOOD. Woooooooooooooo!! The hippest dog in the land! [Cameraman:] It's been, uh. Well rather interesting. I think I'll be going. ["Vindicator" Vinny Vitrone:] Don't let the door hit ya in da' ass. [The cameraman walks out the door as the cameras comes to a fade.] =========================================================== Match 7 - New York Championship Sam "The Minuteman" Adams (with Militia) vs Banana Banana is towering over Adams and look out...he looks like he wants that gold that's around Adams waist. Banana nails Adams with a right hand and then grabs the New York title and holds it up in the air. Militia steps up and nails a choke slam on Banana!! Oh MAN! WHAT POWER!! Militia just choke slammed Banana. What in the hell. Militia is one powerful man. I am utterly stunned after that. I really am. Militia leaves the ring and Adams is back up and slaps a figure four on Banana in the middle of the ring. What is this? Banana is fighting it but he can't get to the ropes. Banana is falling fast. He is fighting this and has been in this figure four for over a minute. How is the big man fighting it and Adams is sitting back laughing. This is crazy is what this is. Militia on the ring apron watching and Banana falls back. The ref looks and he has to count it out. 1......2........3!!! Here is your winner in 3:32, Sam Adams!! Militia back in the ring and Militia bounces off the ropes and nails a huge leg drop on Banana. The two of them get up and laugh. Adams grabs the New York title bout and he and Militia leave the ring laughing. What a bunch of crap. Match 8 David McCarter vs Ultimate Perversion Yet another match that on paper appears to be a main event caliber match, pitting two former champions here in the INWI. David McCarter a former Light Heavyweight Champion, and Ultimate Perversion a former two time Television champion. McCarter into the ring and awaits Perversion who initially makes his way towards the ring with Cherry, but half way down the aisle Cherry turns back and casually walks back towards the locker room. Damn that guy has some mental problems...Perversion steps over the top rope..and McCarter takes advantage quickly and dropkicks Perversion sending him right back over the top rope, however, Perversion landed on his feet...McCarter bounces off the ropes and sends Perversion crashing into the security rail with a baseball slide kick...McCarter up quickly and he climbs to the top rope on the inside...leaps to the outside with a double ax handle across the back of Perversion's head. McCarter has taken early advantage of this match and he rolls Perversion back into the ring.... McCarter on the apron..Slingshot Leg drop. One..two..No!!! Perversion just launched McCarter off him...Definitely no pinfall there...McCarter pulls up Perversion and throws him into the ropes...McCarter Springboards off the middle rope...Flying Bodypre--No!!! Perversion countered and dropped McCarter with a punishing powerslam. Perversion pulls up McCarter and pushes him into the corner and follows up with a short elbow... another...and a third crushing elbow by Perversion on McCarter in the corner. Perversion lifts McCarter over his head...and presses him...Perversion is holding McCarter over his head and he just dropped him face first to the floor..Did you hear the smack of McCarter's body hit the concrete.?? Unbelievable power by Perversion...this man who seems to have snapped over the last few weeks as it's apparent he is part of a group led by Cherry..simply known as Cherry's Orchard. Perversion goes to the outside and to the floor and pulls up the injured McCarter...Perversion sends McCarter into the steel steps..not hollow, but our steps are made of steel. Perversion lifts McCarter over his head again and drops him face first on the ring steps...Ouch..Perversion with a kick to the back of McCarter and another..Perversion pulls up McCarter and scoops him onto Perv's shoulder...Jumping Inverted Piledriver on the floor!!!!!!! I don't believe it..McCarter is done...but Perversion isn't..He picks up McCarter again..he's going to give him another one...WAIT!!! It's Cloud Eagle...he's comes up from behind Perversion..he's got a chair..SMACK !!! A wicked chair shot to the back of Perversion..who drops McCarter and only falls to one knee!!!! Eagle again with a chair shot...dropping Perversion..Eagle drops the chair and is now pounding away at Perversion....These two men were tag team partners and suddenly, Perversion almost lunatic turn has forced them apart. Eagle rolls Perversion into the ring..there's the disqualification...Eagle whips Perversion into the ropes...Modified Thez Press....Eagle punching away at the face of Perversion.. Wait..whoa..it's Cherry...he's walking slowly towards the ring...he's at ringside..he picks up the chair that Eagle left on the outside and slides into the ring...he's in the ring with the chair, and Eagle doesn't see him. Cherry off the ropes..SMACK!! OH MY Ga__ That was the hardest chair shot I've seen in my life...Eagle is laying in the ring face down motionless..and he's got a match still...Cherry turns looks at Perversion and heads towards the back...let's get the official word. Here is your winner in 11:32, as a result of a disqualification....Ultimate Perversion. Perversion is still in the ring..he looks down at the fallen Cloud Eagle smiles and leaves...What the hell was the smile for?? Brian Newark - What in the world is going on between Cherry and Ultimate Perversion? This is absolutely crap between these two and what they are doing to Cloud Eagle. Man oh man. Anyways, we are out of time here in hour number two. And what a way we will start off Hour Number Three. The former Owner and CEO of N.E.W., Shorty Rok will speak to the Television crowd.
