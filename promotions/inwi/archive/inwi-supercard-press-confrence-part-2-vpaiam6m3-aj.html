[INWI] SuperCard - Press Confrence, Part 2

27 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
============================================================= Jerry DeLuise - Well, it won't be a walk in the park for the INWI Champions as they face a tough challenge in the Comforters. Next up on the mic is Mike Garvey, who will go for the INWI Northeast TV Championship. ============================================================= *Mike Garvey is up at the microphone* Garvey: I just have one thing to say....tonight will be the night that the INWI will be brought the justice it rightfully deserves. It's gone too long with all this havoc and chaos going around. Fans, press, let me tell you that I'm not treating my match with Springer as just an everyday match....in that match, I'm putting in my heart, my soul, and all the fight that's left in me. Thank you ============================================================= Jerry DeLuise - We have a taped comment from The Northeast TV Champ, the Springer. Tiffany Love caught up to him after his last title defense and he had this to say: ============================================================= Tiffiny Love walks up to the Springer: TL: Springer that was a close one Springer: Tiff I would have got up from that, all that did was make Bill mad at the Gavel. TL: Speaking of the Gavel, Are you ready for your title defense a SuperCard 1? Springer: Tiff what do you think, of course I'm ready. This is my belt, I will keep it till something better comes along. TL: Those are some strong words Springer I hope you can back them up! Springer: As an old friend of mine use to say "To be the man you beat the man" and Tiff, I am the man WHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!! See all of my fans at SuperCard WHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!! ============================================================= Jerry DeLuise - One of the most exciting matches on the card tomorrow will be PO Box against Jimmy Cougar. These two and Cougar's partner Jason Starr have been going at it since the beginning of the INWI. Here is PO Box!! ============================================================= *P.O. Box enters the press conference room of the Hilton. As PO Box takes a seat, he acknowledges his fans by waving to them.* P.O. Box: Hello everybody. I'm delighted to be part of this ultimate extravaganza here in the city of brotherly love. Michael Pontiff: P.O. Box, on Sunday you will facing your long time enemy, Jimmy Cougar. What are your thoughts on the match? P.O Box: I'm finally going to give Cougar what he deserve. He has cost me matches and the U.S. Title. But this Sunday night I'm going to get back at with him. And boy is he going to pay! Michael Pontiff: Are you afraid that Jason Starr will interfere? P.O. Box: Not at all. If Jason Starr interferes I'm going to have someone keep an eye on him. Michael Pontiff: Do you have anything to say to Jason Starr? P.O. Box: Starr, if you want a piece of me. Then you'll get a piece of me. Right after I destroy your partner. You will get a piece. And once I destroy you, then we will have to say good-bye to the Glamor Boys forever. Michael Pontiff: Thank you P.O. Box. ============================================================= Jery DeLuise - Well, it looks like PO Box is ready and confident. The Main Event tomorrow is going to be a great one. Inside a 15 foot steel cage. Pinfall, submission or get out first. This is going to be wild. "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder. He is the current INWI Northeast Champ and he wants it bad. Let's go up to Thunder. ============================================================= [Thunder enters the room from the left walking slowly to the table and taking his place at the podium. He looks at the Commissioner, smiles and shakes his head.] THUNDER: An' they said you didn't come out in the daylight. [Mild laughter filters throughout the room.] THUNDER: Well I got a few things ta say an' then if ya have any questions...I might answer 'em. First let me address the man you people call "the ICON". That's right... I'm talkin' 'bout none other than Pimp Daddy Brown. The man with MY gold belt. That's right Deluise...you jus' sit there and smile...like you always do. You know you've been draggin' yer feet in gettin' me my match with that hand- picked paper champion o'yers. An' ya also know that when I step inta that ring with Brown that I'm gonna walk outta there wearin' that 10 lbs o' gold 'round my waist. A Sunday fulla miracles ain't gonna change that fact,son. Brown... ...we're in fer the biggest game o' chicken in yer life. Don't blink son... ..or you'll miss it. The INWI brass kept ya protected as long as they could.Now yer gonna stare across that ring at a man that not only can defeat ya fer that strap... ...he can end yer career while doin' it. So ya better get ready fer the fight o' yer life an' plan on nuthin' less.Cuz the next INWI World champion's knockin' on yer door,amigo an' on Sunday night yer gonna see jus' why... ...I am the best there is in this sport today. Think I'm wrong? Think I'm bluffin'? Then jus' show up,my friend... ..I'll make a believer outta ya. [Thunder takes drink of water provided on the podium. He looks down the dias at Commissioner Deluise.] But then I suppose I'll be stripped of the belt fer some trumped up reason,right 'Mr Commissioner'? [The Commissioner shakes his head in disdain.] Okay...whaddaya beatwriters want ta ask me? Shoot. REPORTER ONE: Mr Thunder...I'd like to get your impressions of some of the other superstars in the INWI. THUNDER: Ya mean there's another one? Who is it? You tell me who in that pea-brained head o' yers do ya think is a superstar? Go on... REP.ONE: Well...how about Mike Garvey? [Thunder smirks.] THUNDER: The "Gavel"? Who'd he beat ta become a "superstar"? If my memory serves me right I don't recall any jewelry 'round HIS waist since he's been here. Am I right? REP ONE: Well many... THUNDER: AM I RIGHT?! REP.ONE: Yes..he doesn't have a title yet. But many feel he will be the next NE TV champion. THUNDER: Mebbe so...mebbe no. That's why they wrestle the matches. Hell they ain't even laced 'em up an' you got the Gavel takin' the belt. Next? REP.TWO: How about John Stillwater? THUNDER: "Hardcore". That's what he calls himself,right? Well he may be a tough hombre but let's take a look at the list o' stars HE's beaten lately. One...Mr Banana...oh he's a real superstar now isn't he? Two...Jimmy Cougar. He gets his hand raised against that punk Cougar an' ya think he's a superstar? Cougar's jus' another pretender...he ain't no contender. Do ya want me ta go back further. Who'd he beat fer that strap o' his anyways,remember? Yeah...that green- horn tenderfoot Starr. The other Glamour Boy. There's somethin' ta put on his next resume. REP:THREE: Well those same Glamour Boys did quite the number on you the last time you were in the ring together. THUNDER: Alright smartguy...yer right. I got a few knots on my squash that night but it took TWO guys ta do it an' even then they couldn't finish the job. I owe them an' this is the perfect time fer me ta tell YOU,Mr Commissioner that I want a match with them. Not one-on-one cuz everyone knows that wouldn't happen. Naw..I want 'em both in that ring where I can keep an eye on 'em at all times. Now you put that together son or the INWI is gonna become mighty unhealthy fer a few folks if ya get my meanin'. [The Commissioner mouths the words 'I'll look into it' as Thunder smiles grimly.] THUNDER: Ya got anythin' else fellas? Is that the best ya can do? Look... the competition in the INWI is some of the toughest in the world. I wouldn't be here if it wasn't. an' I ain't disputin' that. What I am sayin' is that I'm the best there is in this profession today and on Sunday night...I'm gonna prove that fact by winning the INWI World championship. That's all I got ta say. Next time you see the Lone Wolf... ...I'll have me a little gold ta hold. Bank on it boys... ...cuz it's gonna happen. [Thunder exits the podium and leaves through the side door.] ============================================================= Jerry DeLuise - Well ladies and gentlemen, that concludes the Press Confrence for SuperCard I. Some superstars we not yet in town when we started this press confrence. Word is that Pimp Daddy Brown's flight was held up and that is why he couldn't be with us tonight. Tomorrow night is one of the biggest nights that the INWI will ever have and we hope you all enjoy yourselves. So for myself and the rest of the INWI, we bid you farewell until tomorrow night! ============================================================= This has been a presentation of the INWI, April 1997 ============================================================= [Voice of Scoop Ropes, the INWI Public Relations man come on the screen as a series of match shots are shown of Pimp Daddy Brown and Brody Thunder.] The INWI Heavyweight Championship. It's a sought after title. One man holds it and the other wants it. The two will lock it up in a 15 foot high steel cage. Winner take the gold. You can only win by pin, submission or beating your opponent out of the cage. [A video collage of Brody Thunder starts to flash up on the screen] "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder.... He's from the town to tough to die, Tombstone, Arizona. He is the current INWI Northeast Champion but will gladly give up that belt in exchange for the INWI Heavyweight Champion. He claims it's his destiny to wear that title belt and he will get it. He has strung together one of the most impressive streaks of wins and at one point held the INWI Northeast and Northeast TV Titles. Works alone and could care less about the fans, he just wants the gold. [A video collage of Pimp Daddy Brown starts to play] He is the "Icon" Pimp Daddy Brown. He earned that name from his success in many other E-feds, but decided he wanted the gold here in the INWI. He won the INWI Heavyweight Title on March 23 in Albany, NY. He is the only champion that the INWI has had and he plans on keeping it that way after this weekend. After a turn as of late, the crowd loves "The Icon" and will be 100% behind him at bell time. [A big INWI Logo flashes on the screen and then then a picture of Thunder and Brown staring eachother down] Philadelphia. April 27. Look out because when these two meet, they will give everyone the meaning of SuperCard. ============================================================ =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= INWI Wrestling.....
