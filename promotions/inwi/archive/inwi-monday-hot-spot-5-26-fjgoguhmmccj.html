[INWI] Monday Hot Spot!! 5-26

28 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
INWI Monday Night Hot Spot!! ======================== Monday, May 26, 1997 Jockos Bar and Grill Club, Stamford, CT =========================================================== Brian Newark - Welcome everyone to Monday Hot Spot!! We come to you from Stamford, CT! We have four exciting matches on the card today for you, including a pair of title matches!! Both the INWI Television and the Northeast Television Title's will be on the line. In the Northeast TV Title match, NEW Champion Firestar attempts to hold onto the belt he won just last night. In the Main event, Mr. Apples will defend his Television Title against Ultimate Perversion. The newcomer Zack Sharp is also on the card tonight! Without further delay, lets go up to the ring. Before we go to that match, we are going to get comments from INWI newcomer, "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston. ============================================================ [As the traffic flows below the luxurious studio style home of a legend, a man looks down and sees the notoriously busy streets of the Big Apple. This man turns and re-enters his lavious dwelling. Once entering the building is perfectly clear that this man is successful. In the living room their is a 52 Inch Sony television, with several VCR's resting below, to the side, and on top of the television and the entertainment center. A Sony Playstation rests quietly on it's designated area. On the oak coffee table a Playstation game case rests...NBA Shootout '97. This man slowly reclines in his all black leather chair, the living room has two black leather couches, and two recliner chairs. As the camera begins making it's way towards the other rooms the man methodically rises to his feet, and reveals a look as if to say "Where the hell are you going?" This man has short, professionally faded, jet black hair, stubble appears on his chin, signifying that a goatee could be in the near future. His eyes are a cold icy blue and almost stare right through the camera.] Man: Chill' son. Where the hell do you think you're going. This ain't your crib..it's mind. So sit your sorry asses down...[whistles] Come here Cujo...[Almost on cue, a 123lbs black German Rotteweiler emerges from the bedroom, like the sun cautiously emerging from behind a plethora of dark clouds.] [The Rott. immediately spots the "intruders" and locks his eyes with theirs and engages in a staring contest.] Man: I wouldn't try to staring him down if I were you[talking to camera crew]. He'll maul you, both and quickly.. [The camera crew instantly return their attention back to this man who's wearing a black t-shirt with Crimson red scripture reading Fatal Alliance.] Man: Well, first of all, for all you bitches...My name is "Cold & Calculating" Tony Houston, and I've been whoopin' ass all over the world, from The Rotten Apple to the mystique filled city of Rome, Italy. Now, ya'll don't need to know where I've busted ass, but let's just say in several different feds, I've been the #1 Contender for EVERY singles title that I've even thought of winning. My record is impeccable...I've lost 3 matches in a career with 70+ matches. And this league now appears to be on the verge of yet another "Cold & Calculating" conquest. I've heard that the INWI is the best, well, I know for a FACT that ain't the truth, because I'm the best, and when any of your so-called athletes dare to step into the ring with me, ya'll will all realize just why I'm one of the most feared wrestlers on God's green Earth. [Houston turns on his enlarged television and at the same time hits play on his VCR and a tape begins of a recent match in the INWI. Houston then reveals his notorious devilish Jack Nicholson type grin, and rises.] Houston: This is all ya'll got, we'll, I'm not one to reveal my plans in this world, but I'll tell ya'll this. When the time is right, my calculating plan will be revealed, and the INWI will suffer the consequences. Ya'll wanted "Cold & Calculating" well ya'll got it. Peace. [Houston then informs the INWI camera crew that it's time for them to leave, but the camera crew attempts to ask another question] Houston: Listen, boy, When Tony Houston says it's time for you to go [grabs one of the camera crew] BOUNCE or get bounced..Cujo..[the intimadating Rott. rises from his resting position and makes his way towards the crew who immediately scurry for the door] [Through the door, sounds of laughter can be heard from this new addition to the INWI.] [Screen fades to black] ============================================================ Match 1 - Winner gets title shot The Freaks vs The MacDaddies This one is definitely going to be an interesting match. The winner gets a shot at the INWI Tag Team Championship. The MacDaddies, who were the first INWI Tag champs and the Freaks, the former TV Champs, both know what it is like to have gold and I can guarantee they want more. That makes this match all the more exciting! It looks like it will be Freak O' Nature and Da Playa starting things out here. Da Playa goes with an eye poke right away and staggers the Freak. Da Playa knees Freak several times in the midsection and nails a piledriver. This one could be over already. 1...2...NO! Freak O' Nature barely got his arm up. Da Playa tags in Da Pimp and they double team Freak and then double slam him. Playa to the top and drops a knee. Freak really needs a tag, but he is what could seem like miles from his corner. Da Pimp tosses Freak out of the ring, runs over and pops Wieland. Wieland heads into the ring and the ref cuts him off. Ow!! Da Playa just nailed Freak with a chair several times and then whipped him into the ringpost. Da Pimp comes out and DDT's Freak on the floor. Wieland is going nuts because he can't do a thing. The Mac Daddies toss Freak back in the ring and Da Pimp goes to scoop him up...wait a small package!! 1...2....3!!! Freak rolls out of the ring, what a major upset!! The crowd is going nuts and the Mac Daddies can't believe it! Your winners in 8:34, the Freaks!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Before our next match, let's get comments from one of the INWI's hottest newcomers, "The Technician" Zack Sharp!! ============================================================ [ A plane takes off from an airport in San Jose, California. It soars through the air and we fade to the inside of it. Zack Sharp sits in first class reading the latest fax he got from the INWI. He sets it down and takes a sip of his drink and then turns towards the camera to speak ] Zack Sharp: Well tonight, I have a match in Cape May, New Jersey with "the Knuckle" Jimmy Chooley. I don't know much about you Chooley, so I guess I can't say I am better than you. You might be a 7 foot monster for all I know. Let's see you could be a seven foot monster, a 500 pound sumo wrestler, a high flying jumping bean, a skilled mat technician, or a hardcore to the floor brawler. Let's see I know how to beat all those kinds of men. It really helps to fight in Japan for as long as I did because you come across all kinds of characters. Chooley, prepare for the match all you can because you are stepping into the ring with the Technician. I have beat the best and yes I have even beaten your champion, so get ready Chooley because there is one way for you to go down and that is via the Ultimate Demise. The Technician has spoken! [ Fade ] ============================================================ Match 2 "The Technician" Zack Sharp vs "The Knuckle" Jimmy Chooley Well, the Technician has been making quite a name for himself here in the INWI. He comes up against the stubborn "Knuckle" Jimmy Chooley. Sharp comes forward and accepts Chooley's look for a handshake. There's the bell and this match is underway. Chooley sneaks around back of Sharp and is looking for a takedown and hits a belly to back suplex and then drops a leg on Sharp. Chooley scoops up Sharp and nails a DDT. Theres a cover, 1...2...NO! Sharp kicks out. Chooley is looking better than I have ever seen him look before. Chooley with an elbow, then he bounces off the ropes and another elbow, no Sharp moved! Sharp quickly goes to work, grabs him and nails a clothesline. Sharp to the midsection, a piledriver! It looks like Sharp wants to get this match over with! Sharp connects with a belly to back suplex then hits a swinging neckbreaker. Sharp then places Chooley on the top rope and sets up, yes, it's the Ultimate Demise!! 1...2...3!! Your winner in 6:37, "The Technician" Zack Sharp!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Next up is the match for the Northeast TV Championship. Lets get comments from the challenger, Vito Rizzotto. ============================================================ Vito: Firestar, tonight you meet the biggest challenge of your career. Yeah you've got talent, but you ain't got enough. Vito Rizzotto is going to walk all over you. Gino: And I'm gonna make sure he does. Vito: No you won't ! The last time you "helped" I lost the match. I don't want you anywhere near the ring tonight. Gino: You don't really mean that. Vito: Gino, I have to prove to myself that I can do this. Gino: I'll let you do it on one condition. If you win, I get first crack. Vito: What are you talking about ? (Knock at the door) Vito & Gino: Oh my God it's HIM ! (camera runs out of tape but shows silhouette on the floor) ============================================================ Match 3 - Northeast TV Championship Firestar vs Vito Rizzotto Firestar, just crowned the INWI Northeast TV Champion, is already defending as he goes up against the former TV Champion Vito Rizzotto. Wait a minute, there's a stir in the crowd, look....it's Mike "The Gavel" Garvey! He jumps in the ring and attacks Firestar. As you know, Firestar, with the help of Bill Bob Woijicowski, won the title from Garvey. Woijicowski is pounding on Firestar and Rizzotto is sitting there stunned. The ref doesn't know what to do in this situation because the match hasn't officially started. Garvey just hit the Final Verdict and knocked Firestar out cold! Wait, look...here comes Bill Bob Woijicowski and he Springer!! Garvey heads into the crowd and Woijicowski and Springer follow. The ref is forced and he starts the match. Rizzotto drops the leg and then nails a DDT. Firestar is hurt and I don't think he stands a chance. Rizzotto brings Firestar up....the MOB DROP!! Oh no, 1...2...3!! Your winner in 1:27 and NEW INWI Northeast TV Champion, Vito Rizzotto. This is a disgrace and I think commissioner Jerry DeLuise will have to step in on this one. I can't believe Rizzotto just won the belt. ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well, it's main event time and we have comments from the Television Champion, Mr. Apples. ============================================================ [Mr. Apples is sitting in the middle of his orchard in Orchard Park, NY. He sits by himself, chewing on a cigar. He looks a little upset and you can see the bandage on his head.] You know, I don't know who the frig was in that mask, but I'll get 'em. As for you Perversion, you have stepped into the ring on the wrong day. When I face you for my TV title, you'll be walking out the same way as you came in, with a bare waist. And as for this damn masked dude. I haven't a clue who you are, but if I see you interfering in any matches, I'll find you and beat you down. And you Ed Rogers, you got a lucky win over me. You carcass was beat and you know it. Oh well, we'll meet again, I am sure of that and next time, I'll finish the job. As for the rest of this federation, take note... I'm on the prowl and I want more gold. Thunder, I know you're still out there and I will get ya, just not yet. I'll let you deal with these little kids, then I'll come and get ya. If you think this fed is made up of all jabronies, wait till you step into the ring with me pal. Now, get outta here, I got a match to get ready for. [Camera fades to the INWI Emblem and then a quick shot of the Brotherhood of Sour Grapes logo...] ============================================================ MAIN EVENT Match 4 - Television Championship Mr. Apples vs Ultimate Perversion Well, Perversion won his match against John Stilwater and now he has a title shot against the TV Champion Mr. Apples. Perversion is in the ring and here come Apples. Wait a minute, he has stopped at ringside and is saying something to a fan, at least I think it's a fan. Wait a minute, I see who that it... it's INWI newcomer, "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston. These two have a history with eachother and I am sure it may follow here in the INWI. Apples enters the ring, hands his belt to the ref and this match is underway. Apples grabs Perversion, slaps him into a headlock and is sent into the ropes. Apples goes down on a shoulder block by Perversion. Perversion drops an elbow and then scoops up Apples and drops him with a fist to the face. I wonder if this guy at ringside is affecting Apples. Apples is back up into his feet and is sent into the ropes again. Perversion down for a back body drop, but Apples hits the brakes, drops to his knees and drops Perversion with a right uppercut. Apples now goes to work and scoops up Perversion and suplex's him. Apples off the ropes and drops a big leg and gets a two count! Apples again up with Perversion and nails him with a swinging neckbreaker. Apples goes to the top rope and as Perversion gets up, he gets caught with a diving lariat. Apples again up with Perversion and he nails him with a DDT. Perversion is out on his feet! Apples looks down at Houston and says something to him. He then grabs Ultimate Perversion and hits a front jawbreaker. Hey, that's Houston's finisher, the "Cold Drop"!!! Apples covers, 1...2...3!!! Your winner in 11:31 and still INWI TV Champion, Mr. Apples. Houston gets up and applauds Apples, but is that a good sign or a bad sign? Apples grabs his belt and heads to the back.... ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well ladies and gentlemen, that's all from Stamford! What a great show. We have one new champion, but I don't think Firestar is going to be to happy about this one! A newcomer made his presence known and we only wonder what that will lead up to. So, until this Thursday's Studio show, I am Brian Newark for the INWI saying see you Thursday! ============================================================ This was a presentation of the INWIŽ, May 1997 Visit the INWI Web Site http://www.catskill.net/harmer "The revolutionary Force in E-Mail Wrestling"
