["Canon" by Pachelbel starts to play over the loudspeakers and the crowd breaks out in boo's. Out from the back pops Mr. Flamboyant's head and he has a grin from ear to ear. FLAMBOYANT: Whoooooo!! Well baby, it's one week away. King Apples and Ed Rogers get to go at it again and I know you crowd dwellers are gonna cheer for the man...the myth and the legend, King Apples. That's because we love you and you love us! The King will grab the Northeast Title this weekend, giving us the two highest belts in the INWI. Pit that with the fact that Brother Bill Bob will win the INWI Light Heavyweight CHampionship and you will see why The Brotherhood is the most dominant force in all of eWrestling. We are the greatest thing going today and we will not be stopped. Tell 'em King! KING APPLES - Yer damn right we're the best in the business. We have beaten federations and we have carried federations. We have taken over federations and we have smashed federations. The bottom line to this is, where ever we go, we kick ass. That is not going to change this week or next week. Brother Houston already whipped the **** out of that punk James Pillow tonight. That's right Pillow, open your mouth and get one of our feet shoved down your throat. All your cronies from that other Federation are not hear to help you and allow you to win. The fact of the matter is, you and that cheap floozie you run around with are nothing. And if you try to come around here, I'll whip you myself. Now then, you are in this Light Heavyweight Championship at the Pay Per View. Prepare to lose that title of yours because Bill Bob Woijicowski is going to take it. We will prove our dominance here in the INWI. Now then, tonight I get Michael Bane. Well Bane, I don't care about the outcome of this match. The fact is, you will not pin me no matter what. I am better and that is plain and simple. However, you will suffer pain tonight. I am going to kick your ass from pillar to post and then back to pillar again. You'll be lucky if you make it to the Final Countdown next weekend. Anyways, I'm gone so I can get changed to beat the hell out of Bane. Match 9 - Special Dog Collar Match "The Vindicator" Vinny Vitrone (with Mr. Flamboyant) vs Pig Pen This match is.....well....rather interesting. This upcoming weekend, Vinny Vitrone faces "The Real Deal" Michael Bledsoe in a dog collar match. I guess this is Vitrone's way of getting prepared for the match. Pig Pen is already in the ring and Vitrone enters behind Mr. Flamboyant and he has a dog collar on. He looks ready for this match, or that could just be his arrogance of being in this group. They enter the ring and Vitrone is bouncing around like a little brat. Oh....dang.... Flamboyant just swatted him in the back of the head and Vitrone stops. And we have to take a break....oh wait.... wrong Monday Night Show. We actually show the whole match. Not only do we show the whole match, but we show it on Monday! Anyways, Vitrone hands the one end of the dog collar to Pig Pen and he gets ready to put it on. OW!!! Vitrone just kicked Pig Pen in the midsection and nailed a DDT! Flamboyant comes over and attatches the dog collar to Pig Pen. Vitrone scoops up Pig Pen and sends him over the top rope. He's hanging the little porker. Vitrone pulls him back in and nails a short armed clothesline. What the hell is Vitrone doing? He's pointing to all four corners as if he is going to end this match right now. He tags one....moves to the second and has it....to the third and has it and wait, Pig Pen is up! Vitrone is going for the fourth, NO! Pig Pen stopes Vitrone and just nailed a Slop Drop!! Vitrone is rolling around in the ring and Pig Pen off the ropes and there's a splash!! Pig Pen scoops up Vitrone, sends him into the ropes and clotheslines him with the chain. He grabs him and again sends him into the ropes and nails a belly to belly suplex! Pig Pen is signaling for the four corners and he is going for the win. Pig Pen is a former TV Champion remember, so he's no slouch. Well, ok, so the title was a fluke and he is a slouch, but he's doing a hell of a job here. Pig Pen throws Vitrone over his shoulder and tags the first turnbuckle...and so does Vitrone. Pig Pen has the second turnbuckle...and Vitrone tags it. They each tag the third as well and Pig Pen moves towards the fourth. OH MAN! Vitrone just slipped off Pig Pens shoulder and nailed a DDT! Vitrone tags the fourth turnbuckle and the referee signals for the bell. Vitrone has won this match. Here is your winner in 8:34, "The Vindicator" Vinny Vitrone!!! Match 10 - Non Title Match The Springer (Dr. Bedpan) vs Ultimate Perversion (with Cloud Eagle) The Springer finally gets what he wants next weekend as he gets Scoop Ropes in a cage. What a match that will be. And you saw it all start yesterday on Inside The Ropes as Springer came out and hit a Springer Cutter on Scoop. I haven't seen Scoop today, so I wonder if he skipped town to stay away from Springer. Guess he wants no more Springer Cutters until they get into the cage. Well, there's the bell and this match is underway. It's good to see that Ultimate Perversion only came out with his Television Title and not the Tag Team titles that they stole. Solid Gold is furious about this and that is giving them some real good reasons to fight at the PPV. They are withougt their Gold. So, is their name just Solid now? Hmmmm... there's food for thought. Springer and Perversion lock up and Springer with a headlock. Perversion sends Springer into the ropes and drops him with a clothesline. Perversion scoops up Springer, sends him into the turnbuckles and nails a diving elbow. Perversion with a quick cover, 1.....2....NO! Springer kicks out right away and Perversion slaps on a chin lock and both men work their way to their feet. Springer with an elbow to the gut and another. Springer off the ropes and drops Perversion with a shoulder. Springer off the ropes again, over Perversion, Perversion with a leap frog and then both men collide. Springer rolls out of the ring to catch his breath... and what the hell is this? [The INWI-Tron flickers and up on the screen pops Scoop Ropes...] Scoop - Hey Springer, you wannabe. You finally get your little wish girly girl. This weekend, you and me in a cage. See, the thing is, I am not worried. Your little Springer Cutter was a cheap shot yesterday and that is not gonna happen this weekend. However, it is going to be me that lays your ass out this weekend. See, you think I am scared of you, but I am not. I'll see you later wimp... [Screen goes blank and Springer just stares.] OH MAN! Perversion is taking advantage of the fact that Springer isn't watching as he just nailed a sliding dropkick that sent Springer into the cage on the outside. Perversion to the outside and sends Springer into the ringpost. Perversion grabs Springer and sends him back into the ring. Both men on their feet, Perversion with an Irish whip and the referee gets squashed!! Man oh man! Perversion walks towards Springer and there's the Springer Cutter!! Springer got him and there's a cover. No one is there to count it out. Wait....what the hell? Here comes Scoop Ropes!! He slides in the ring and nails Springer. Scoopp with Springer...kick to the midsection and he NAILS A SPRINGER CUTTER!!! Or is that a Scoop Cut? Man! He grabs Perversion and rolls him on top of the Springer. The ref is coming to as Scoop runs out of the ring and the crowd is booing. THere's the ref, 1.........2...........3!!!! It's over!! I can't believe what just happened. This cage match is going to be wild this weekend. Here is your winner in 13:21, Ultimate Perversion!!!! =========================================================== Special Interview - Part 2..... "The Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder With Brian Newark Newark:*ahem*..Well regardless of your accusations,you are no longer the INWI Heavyweight champion. Now what about your association with the Brotherhood? Thunder: Whadda 'bout it? Can't a guy have friends? Newark: Well it's just that they were unlikely allies,considering your past history. Why _did_ you align yourself with the Brotherhood? Thunder: Simple. Strength in numbers. We had a commmon purpose.We didn't like DeLuise an' didn't care who knew. I ain't never gonna out drinkin' with 'em but I watch their back an' they watch mine. It works out well fer both o' us. Newark: Which leads me right into your match at the NEW PPV. You carried the INWI banner into that organization and came away with a very important victory. Thunder: I didn't carry nuthin' inta that match but my own determination. I didn't ask ta be no INWI flunky boastin' some 'allegiance ta the INWI against the NEW. Truth is...it was a paycheck. A big paycheck. That's all. Nuthin' more an' nuthin' less. Hell I owe the flamin' INWI nuthin'. Certainly not loyalty after the way I got screwed. That match was strictly fer the money an' personal satisfaction. Newark: You really wanted to win the match didn't you? Thunder: Brian...if ya don't wanna win _every_ match...ya got no business bein' in this sport. But anytime ya get ta show another organ- ization who the best is...yeah..I suppose there's a lil added incentive there. Newark: Well we've covered where you've been, now I guess we'd like ta know what you're doing right now. Thunder: Well I'm busy with my ranch right now and I've been wrestlin' a few independant organizations matches ta keep the edge. I did a lil spot on "Walker,Texas Ranger" this past fall. Newark: You looking to become a television star now? Thunder: Nah...they can keep that business. Too much wasted time. I ain't usedta sittin' still fer hours at a time waitin' ta get some action. Nah...I'm a wrestler. That's what I do. My stage is that ring up there.I don't see that changin' anytime soon. Newark: Speaking of your chosen profession, what are your thoughts on the state of the business and where do you see the future of the sport heading? Thunder: Y'know...I'm from the ol' school. Ya laced 'em up an' went ta ring an' got the job done. Plain an' simple. Nowadays ya got guys with multi-colored hair, "dead" guys or guys with their faces painted up like they was goin' treat or treatin' or somethin'. They come down ta the ring with snakes, dogs, birds, an' every other kinda animal jus' ta get some heat. I got no patience fer that [BLEEP]. Jus' get yer ass in the ring an' wrestle. Don't stand out there on television an' preen an' primp yer hair or push some piece o' plastic trash that merchandisin' geniuses have decided ta sell. It ain't about lil stuffed Dell Pickles teddy bears or Mr Ed action figures. It's professional wrestlin',dammit. The toughest [BLEEP]in' sport in the world. It burns my ass evertime I see some juiced up punk with a attitude an' some stupid gimmick get out there an' embarrass this sport. Every night when I climb thru them ropes I'm in there ta give all I got an' take home that paycheck. I got no time fer posin' with fans an' kissin' babies. If I wanted ta do that I'd become a politician. Newark: Since you mentioned it...what _about_ the fans? Thunder: Look...they pay ta see a fight. That's all. They ain't payin' me ta pose with 'em or ta shake hands with 'em. They pay ta see me wrestle. Now I've been blessed in this business ta have the talent an' I made myself inta the best. Fans recognize that. Well _most_ fans recognize that. That's why they put down their money an' put their ass in the seat when I come ta town. That's jus' the way it is. Newark: Okay...now then,where do you see the sport going? Thunder: Well the talent is getting better as far as new blood goes. Ya got a few guys like Vitrone, Harvey Thunder an' Billy-Bob What- everhisnameis here in the INWI.They're the next wave.Then there's guys like Bishop,Demos Canyon an' Derek Mota wrestlin' elsewhere who'll also be on the next crest of champions too.So maybe we're not as bad off as folks say. Newark: Fair enough. Well the most asked question from fans, both in letters and at the arenas,is probably the one you like the least. There's been much speculation that you're going to return at the next PPV and regain the title you lost at Ringmaster '97. Some so-called "hotlines" have you leaving the INWI for another rival promotion over money concerns. Still other rumors have you retiring from the sport altogether. So I gotta ask you...point blank...what _are_ your plans? Thunder: Heh,heh,heh...yeah..I heard alla them stories too. I heard one even had me as dyin' in a plane crash. My wife got a real kick outta that one. Some folks have too much free time I think. My plans? Hmmm...the way I see things right now... I got alotta unresolved issues in the INWI. The least o' which is how the title was taken off me. Springer an' I have a few things ta settle too. Newark: So you're going to return to fulltime wrestling in the INWI? Thunder: Well...I do have a part in "The X-Files" if I want it. I think I may.. Newark: Then the rumors are true? You're retiring?! Thunder: 'Course I got a chance ta prove I'm the best in a federation out west right now too an' I think I might... Newark: You're jumping to a rival promotion?! I can't believe it! Thunder: But I really want ta settle my accounts here. An' I'm gonna... Newark: You're staying! Well I'm sure the fans will be extremely excited to hear the news that... Thunder: But not right now. Newark: Wha..? You _are_ leaving? Thunder: I'm leavin' fer _now_. I got me alot on my plate right now an' my wife an' I are gonna take the time right now an' decide where's the best opportunity for us. I've made me a boatload o' dinero here an' that's allowin' us ta take a lil vacation. After all...there's gonna be another lil Thunder runnin' around pretty soon an' life on the road gets a bit hard on the family. I got plenty o' time ta take more titles an' cause hate an' discontent. Make no mistake, I _will_ be back. But fer now...no. The Lone Wolf is on hiatus. Newark: Well that's big news an' did I hear you say you're going to be a father again? Thunder: Yeah well...that's the truth. An' lemme tell ya...bein' a father is jus' as tough as hookin'em up in the ring...jus' a smaller opponent,that's all. [Thunder smiles slightly as Brian Newark laughs.] Newark: Well Mr Thunder,thank you again for taking the time to be here.We wish you well with your latest edition to the Thunder Family nad we look forward to when you return to the INWI. [Newark extends his hand. Thunder looks at it for a moment,then leans for- ward and shakes it.] Thunder: Right. [Fade back to Brian Newark live...] =========================================================== Match 11 - The Gauntlet Number 9 Jason "The Crusher" Chandler (with Oz) vs Mr. Cherry Well, Chandler demanded two matches and he got them. He luckily got by Bill Bob Woijicowski earlier tonight and now he has to face another solid Ligh Heavyweight, in fact a former Light Heavyweight Champion, in Mr. Cherry. I am looking forward to this match and if Chandler wins it, his match has already been set up for the Final Countdown pay-per-view. If he loses, Michael Slater will automatically get the Title match with Ultimate Perversion. Chandler is in the ring awaiting Cherry and here he comes. Cherry in the ring and Chandler doesn't waste any time as he attacks Cherry. Chandler grabs Cherry, sends him into the ropes and hits a belly to belly suplex. A quick cover by Chandler, 1.....2.....NO! Cherry kicks his way out of it and Chandler locks on a chin lock. Chandler grabs Cherry by the hair and brings him to his feet and nails a knee to the gut and then hits a swinging neck breaker. Chandler now gets on top of Cherry and starts to pound him and then brings him to his feet again. Chandler is possesed. He knows he only needs two more wins and he is in. He will have that title shot he has demanded. But he has to get past Cherry here and then Slater at the Pay Per View! Chandler sends Cherry into the ropes.... reversal by Cherry and Cherry with a spinning heel kick. Cherry grabs Chandler, brings him to his feet and nails a hurrakanrana!! Chandler rolls out of the ring and is trying to shake some sense back into himself and Cherry heads towards the back ropes. Cherry off the ropes and dives over the top rope and lands on Chandler! What a move by Cherry and he is up and the crowd loves it! He stomps on Chandler and scoops him up and rolls him into the ring. Cherry heads to the ropes and he is signaling for the top rope. Wait....what is this? IT'S MICHAEL SLATER!! He's on the apron and trips Cherry. He gets in the ring and starts to stomp at Cherry and the ref signals for the bell. What the hell is going on?? Let's hear... The Winner as a Result of a disqualification, MR. CHERRY!! Wait a minute... that's messed up? Slater did that just so he gets the easy Title shot. That's not right. Something has to be done! Chandler DID NOT lose this match. Wait... here comes Commissioner DeLuise and he has the referee. They are talking this over. Chandler has Slater and just nailed a belly to back suplex and Slater rolls out of the ring clutching his neck. Wait... DeLuise has the mic... [DeLuise] Michael Slater is trying to get Chandler DQ'd so he get's a FREE TITLE SHOT. NO WAY... NOY HERE IN THE INWI!!! I order this match to conitinue and Slater... if you or any of your cronies interfere, you will be SUSPENDED!!! This match WILL CONTINUE!!!! Oh man. Commissioner DeLuise has ordered this match to continue and the crowd loves that decision. Let these two settle it out! Cherry looks stunned and Chandler walks over...set's up...it's a Northern Lights Suplex!! The ref is there, 1......2......3!!! This match is over!! Here's the official word! Here is your winner in 13:37, Jason "The Crusher" Chandler!!! Match 12 - CO MAIN EVENT King Apples (with Mr. Flamboyant) vs "The Master Of Pain" Michael Bane Well, to say the least, this match is very interesting. And I for one am actually surprised that they signed this match. You got one of the leaders of the Brotherhood on one side. And then you have Michael Bane on the other side. Bane battles anothe member of the Brotherhood, Tony Houston, for the INWI Heavyweight Championship this weekend. That should be a battle to say the least. But this is the INWI, no one does any ducking here and all of our wrestlers are top notch. They back down from nobody. Michael Bane is the same way. He didn't have to take this match, but you know how he is thinking... why only take out one member of the Brotherhood, when he has a chance to take out two. Bane just batter watch himself, because the Brotherhood goes as a pack. There's the bell and this match is underway. Collar and elbow tie up in the middle of the ring and Apples grabs the arm right away and tears at it. Apples has the definite power advantage here and is going to utilize that all that he can. Apples sends Bane into the ropes, clothesline, NO! Bane ducks, off the other ropes and dives at Apples. Apples catches Bane and then slams him to the mat and follows it up with a couple of kicks. Apples outweighs Bane by almost 100 pounds and has a big advantage there. Apples scoops up Bane and preses him over his head and slams him to the mat. This may of been a bad mistake for Bane to get into the ring with Apples a week before his Title match with Houston. Apples grabs Bane and sends him over the top rope and goes out after him. Bane is on the floor, Apples goes to grab him...and Bane just nailed Apples with a chair that makes him stumble backwards. Bane up annd nails Apples with a drop kick and Apples falls to to the floor. Bane kicking away at Apples and rolls in the ring to break the count. Bane to the top rope and Apples is still down....Bane flies and nails an elbow drop on Apples. Bane is really on top of things now. He is doing the right thing by making the big man fight on the ground. Bane scoops up Apples and rolls him into the ring and Bane goes to the top again. Apples stumbes to his feet and Bane Dives...high cross body block!!! 1......2.....NO! Apples kicks out with authority! Bane back up and starts to kick at Apples, but Apples grabs his leg and flips him to the mat and he is back up now. Apples with his big foot on Bane's throat. Apples turns around and flips off the crowd, which gets them to their feet. They really hate the Brotherhood. Bane is back up and Apples doesn't see him....
