[INWI] Monday Hot Spot, 11-24, Hour 3

26 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene then fades away and goes to the beginning of INWI Hot Spot. This is a series of shots of all of the INWI Superstars. It shows Tony Houston holding onto the INWI Heavyweight Championship as Springer gives him a Springer Cutter off of a ladder, it shows Mr. Ed Rogers and King Apples going at it, it shows James "Simply The Best" Pillow with the INWI Light Heavyweight title and Angel at his side, it shows Sam Adams draped in a U.S. Flag with Militia at his side and the New York State Title over his shoulder, it shows Solid Gold in a four way brawl with the Comforters in their infamous battle for the Unified Titles, it shows Bill Bob Woijicowski and Mr. Cherry in their wild battle for the Light Heavyweight Title and then it shows an action packed seconds of ALL INWI superstars and it ends with the screen exploding and you see INWI..... Hot Spot with the Hot Spot in flames...] Brian Newark - Welcome back ladies and gentlemen to MONDAY HOT SPOT!!! This is hour number three and we have the INWI Title on the line in this hour! Let's go to the ring!!! Match 9 The Springer (with Dr. Bedpan) vs Ed Rogers (with Smelly) This match could be a great one. The former Heavyweight Champion and the former two time Northeast Champion are gonna hook it up in this match. This is one that I think that everyone has anticipated. And with the news that Springer will face King Apples next week, this one could be real good. Springer is in the ring and he is awaiting Ed Rogers. Where is Ed? He is not coming out from the back. This is crazy right now, let's go. We have a show to do. [The arena goes black and the INWI-Tron flashes...] [Scene: Backstage. Boxes, marked "Fragile" and "This Side Up" line the dirtied bare wooden walls. Discarded papers are strewn across the floor almost hidding the fact that, among the shadows where no one will find him lies Ed Rogers, tied and gagged. The strange man mumbles something and with pleading eyes looks into the camera as the picture fades.] WHAT did he just say? Someone get security back there now... what the hell happened to Ed? This is bizarre. Wait a minute, the Springer has the mic. SPRINGER: You know, I am ready to fight this match. I have been ready for this freak for a long time! [crowd POP!] And now this idiot has somehow tied himself up. Well, next week I have to fight the King Fruit himself and I want a match this week. So if any of you punks have a nut sack, you'll get out here and take a Springer beating! The crowd explodes as Springer jumps in the ring awaiting a challenger and I don't know who is going to come out. Wait, who's this? It's Bodyguard Jones! He has the nerve and the crowd anticipates this one as they will get to see the Springer in action. But I don't believe this official, but we'll call it that way. Jones enters the ring and the ref rings the bell. I guess this one is official!! Tie up in the center of the ring and Springer with a couple of knee lifts to Jones gut, off the rope and nails a European uppercut that sends Jones to the floor. Springer off the ropes and gets air on an elbow drop. Springer up, grabs Jones and nails a SPRINGER CUTTER!!! Springer has that fire in his eyes as he grabs Jones again and nails a second SPRINGER CUTTER!! And listen to this crowd explode!! They love it!! Springer is now setting up.... he applies the COIL!! Jones is tapping out!! This match is over! Here is your winner in 2:25, The Springer!!!! Springer slingshots himself out of the ring and the crowd loves it as he heads to the back. As for Jones, well, his INWI days are now officially over and what a way to go out. Getting smacked around. Let's go to the next match! Match 10 Malice (with Sapphire) vs Cherry Well, this should be quite an interesting match. Malice, a newcomer and a favorite among the crowds, will take on the no nonsense new attitude, Cherry. That's right, no more Mr. Cherry. Just Cherry. And he is interesting now. He wrestles in jeans now and I wonder if that will hamper his high flying. We'll soon see. Malice is already in the ring and listen to this crowd. And here comes Cherry and he sprints to the ring and slides under the bottom rope. Cherry stares across the ring at Malice and then takes off his sweatshirt. Cherry bounces off the ropes and drops Malice with a drop kick. Cherry follows with an elbow drop, sends Malice into the ropes and connects with a spinning heel kick. He can still move, even with this new found attitude. Cherry grabs Malice and gets ready for a suplex and YES! That was impressive as Cherry got Malice over. Cherry to the second rope and drops a leg and there's a cover, 1......2......NO!! Malice rolls a shoulder and the crowd sighs in relief. Cherry back up and drops a fist. Cherry outside the ring and climbs to the top and he is looking to nail a splash... MALICE MOVES!!! Cherry is on the mat rolling around in pain. Malice is up and he grabs Cherry, sends him into the ropes and hits a big foot and Cherry crumples to the mat. Malice grabs Cherry and slams him to the mat and bounces off the ropes.... delayed elbow drop. Malice is getting the crowd pumped and he nails a couple of rights to the head of Cherry. Cherry staggers back and Malice clotheslines Cherry over the top rope and to the floor!! Malice to the outside and he grabs Cherry, sets him up for a slam and drops him over the ring barrier. Malice then stomps Cherry and throws his hands in the air and the crowd explodes!! Malice grabs Cherry and there's a snap suplex on the floor. Malice has Cherry again and rolls him into the ring. Malice to the top and he leaps and Cherry gets a foot up into the face. Malice staggers and Cherry with a roll up, 1.....2....NO! Malice with a kick out. These two are really going at it. Cherry rolls to the ropes and helps himself up and Malice gets up as well. Cherry with a superkick!!! Malice falls out of the ring and Cherry to the apron. ASAI MOONSAULT by Cherry and he leveled Malice. The crowd can't believe it! Cherry back in the ring and he bounces off the other ropes, Malice is up... suicide dive!!! Cherry rolls Malice into the ring and heads to the top rope. CHERRY BOMB!!! Malice is out!! Cherry with the cover, 1.....2......3!!! It's over!! The crowd can't believe it and Malice is no longer undefeated in the INWI!!! Let's get the official word. Here is your winner, in 14:23..... Cherry!!!! Match 11 - New York Championship Sam "Minuteman" Adams (with Militia) vs Dell Pickle (with Oyster Boy) Adams is in the ring and the commisioner looks ready to go. Pickle is now in the ring and it's kind of interesting that Adams picked a jWo member to wrestle. Well anyway Adams with a smile on his face and I don't think he is taking this match very seriously. Pickle with a go behind and Adams smiles. He reverses it and hits a German suplex. Pickle is back up and he hits a dropkick to the back and Adams goes down and he looks shocked. He gets up and he hits a huge right hand to the jaw of Pickle and he goes down. Adams flexes his muscles and Pickle is back up again. Adams turns around and hits a superkick to the jaw of Pickle and he goes flying over the top rope and to the floor. Pickle is hurt on the outside and Adams goes over to the buckles and sits on the top rope. Pickle has a chair and he slides under the ring and Adams is playing to the crowd. Pickle is on the other end and he walks up the steps and smacks Adams in the back of the head with the chair. Adams goes hurdiling off the top rope and hits the mat and Pickle is on the top rope and he jumps and comes down on Adams with the chair and he goes down, 1......2.......and a kickout by Adams and the look on his face is one of shock. he can't belive that Pickle is putting up such a fight. Adams is having a hard time getting up and Pickle is on the offensive now. Pickle with a dropkick that sends Adams to the outside. Adams slams the mat in frustration and he jumps onto the apron. He calls Pickle over and Adams grabs his head and jumps down and Pickle's head comes down hard on the top rope and he bounces back and into the ring. Adams back into the ring and he is smiling again, boy he will never learn. He sends Pickle into the ropes and he nails a Lou Thesz press and he is pounding away on the forehead of Pickle. He picks up Pickle and hits a belly to belly suplex. He climbs the ropes and hits an elbow from the second ropes. Adams looks a little more focused now and it shows in his performance. He climbs the ropes once again and he hits a frog splash. He covers Pickle, 1........2.....no!!! Pickle kicks out and Adams is on the attack once again. He sends Pickle into the ropes, Pickle ducks underneath a boot and hits one of his own and then hits a piledriver and covers Adams, 1......2......and a kickout but barely by Adams. He rolls to the outside and is thinking of what to do. Pickle is into the ropes and he leaps and goes over the top rope and....misses. He went crashing into the aisle when Adams moved and this is what Adams needed. He throws Pickle back into ring and into the ropes and hits a boot to the gut, double underhhok and a powerbomb and he holds the legs of Pickle, 1........2.......3 and a victory for the New York Champion. Again the winner of the match in the time of 6:19, "General" Sam Adams. MAIN EVENT Match 12 - INWI Heavyweight Championship "The Ringmaster" Tony Houston (with Mr. Flamboyant) vs Jonathan Hammer (with Tommy Graves) This should be an extremely interesting match and has more meaning than initially meets the eye. It has been The Brotherhood, more importantly King Apples and Tony Houston that have caused a one Tommy Graves the Northeast Championship, not once but on two occassions. And as of late we have noticed a partnership if you will between the monster man Graves, and the most extreme man in the INWI, Hammer. Hammer is in the ring without Tommy Graves, however, you know that he had best be watching his partner's back. And as the INWI Heavyweight Champion makes his way towards the ring, he arrives alone as well. And people should know and/or expect that The Brotherhood is IN the arena and watching this match closely as they want to protect the INWI's cherished prize. Houston into the ring and he and Hammer start this match out in brawling fashion..Hammer with a right, Houston fires back with an uppercut..Hammer with a knee lift to Houston's midsection and scoops Houston and drops him across the knee with a Backbreaker. Hammer pulls up Houston and Houston fires back with a poke to Hammer's eyes...Houston taks over Hammer with a snap mare and follows up with a dropkick to the back of Hammer's head. Houston drops a leg across the back of Hammer's head. And pulls Hammer to his feet..Houston scoops Hammer and drops him throat first across the top rope with a modified stun gun...Houston covers..One.. two..No!!!! Houston back up and stomps Hammer hard into the back of his head..and again...Houston pulls up Hammer and fires him into the ropes...Houston drops his head...And Hammer counters with a Swinging Neckbreaker on the champ. Hammer is slowly beginning to regain his composure and is on his feet. Hammer pulls up Houston and sends him into the ropes and drops Houston with a powerful Powerslam. Hammer up quickly and pulls up Houston and scoops him into a Fireman's Carry..Somoan Drop... One.two..t-No!!! Hammer bounces off the ropes and connects on a standing Senton Splash...Hammer pulls up Houston and sends him hard into the corner and charges..BIG Clothesline in the corner by Hammer..Houston comes staggering out..a boot to the midsection of Houston doubles him over, and Hammer drives the champion's face first into the mat with a Rocker drop...Hammer covers and hooks the leg..One..two..th--No!!! We were inches away from having a new champion. Hammer is on a roll and he pulls up Houston and sets him on the top turnbuckle and climbs up after Houston...He's going for a top rope superplex..BAM!!! Hammer hit it...Hammer just connected with a Top Rope Superplex on the INWI Heavyweight champion..but both men are down in the center of the ring. Hammer rolls over and drapes his arm across Houston..One..two..thre-NO!!!! Whoa Houston just barely rolled his left shoulder, damn unbelievable fight in the champ, though I can't stand him he is definately a talented man when it comes to knowing his ins and outs of the ring. Hammer is going to the outside and climbs to the top turnbuckle..he leaps...Spla-No!!!!Houston raised his knees and Hammer crashed right on Houston's knees...Hammer is staggering around trying to catch his breath..Houston up..Double Underhook..into a Double Underhook DDT...great move by Houston...Houston now is crawling over and covers Hammer..one..two..thr--No!!! Hammer draped his leg over the bottom rope...Houston pulls up Hammer..TA DOW..Houston just nailed The Cold Drop..we haven't seen that in a while out of Houston.. he covers..Wait..who's charging the ring...it's TOMMY GRAVES...ONE TWO Graves has a chair, and Houston sees him and rolls off of Hammer...and slides to the outside....Graves is challenging Houston to return to the ring, while Hammer is still out after suffering the effects of the Cold Drop...Wait..Apples is coming in from behind the ring..he slides in and bounces off the ropes and drops Graves with a Clothesline to the back of the head. Houston slides back into the ring and pulls up Hammer and scoops him into a Fireman's Carry...The Too Cold Driver..Houston just nailed T.C.D. on Hammer ..he's done....Apples pulls up Graves and scoops him and slams him on the mat. Houston picks up the chair and drapes it across Graves' face and drops a leg on the chair...Apples pulls up Graves and sets him up in the piledriver position. Houston is on the second turnbuckle and leaps..SPIKE PILEDRIVER on GRAVES!!!!! Houston and Apples roll out of the ring with their respective championship belts in tact. At 13:23, the referee has counted Tony Houston out of the ring. Winner by Countout, Jonathan Hammer!!!! Brian Newark - We are out of time!!
