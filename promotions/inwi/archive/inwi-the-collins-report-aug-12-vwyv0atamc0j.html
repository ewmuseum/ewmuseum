[INWI] The Collins Report, Aug 12

12 August 1997

The Joseph Collins Report August 12, 1997 ................................... INWI Studios, Delhi NY ============================================================ Joseph Collins - Welcome ladies and gentlemen to INWI Studios and the Collins report. I am the host, Joseph Collins and today we have an interesting show. We have Arena reports from last week and we also have footage of Tony Houston's celebration after winning the INWI Northeast Television Championship from Bill Bob Woijicowski last night. We also have an interesting ''show'' sent to us from Jammin' Jimmy Cougar. So sit back and enjoy as we journey into INWI Studio's and do all of the things that need to be done. I am Joseph Collins... In This Edition of the Collins Report... 1. Houston celebrates his first INWI Title 2. Cougar rents people and space for his own ''show...'' 3. House and arena show reports from last week... 4. Any other news from the INWI 5. Closing comments from Collins... ============================================================ 1. Houston Celebrates his first INWI Title [The scene fades from the ring where Heavyweight Champion of the INWI, Brody Thunder has successfully defended his title. And fades into the well lit, and loud lockeroom area of Boomers in downtown Oneonta, New York.] [The camera and it's crew slowly make their way towards a particular lockeroom where the sounds of Wild For Da' Night by Busta Rhymes and Rampage are heard escaping from the privacy of the room. Several voices can be heard creeping underneath the sliver of room between the door and the floor. As the camera crew slowly makes their way closer to the door along with Tiffany Love, a liquid substance begins to ooze from underneath the door.] [Tiffany Love approaches the door and knocks several times, and a voice answers the curious knocks..] Voice: WHO IS IT...<HICCUP>? Love: Tiffany Love, reporting for INWI news.. Voice: Tiffany Love??? Love: Yes, I"ve been covering INWI news for quite some time.. Voice: You look g-g-ood? Love: Umm...quite frankly I've earned my position through years of hard work, and studying at the finest schools across the Unit- Voice: [Cutting Love off]: Yeah Yeah Come on in, boo. [Tiffany Love and the camera crew enters the room to observe "Bulldozer" Tony Riley, and the NEW INWI Northeast Television Champion, "Cold & Calculating" Tony Houston with the championship belt still resting on his left shoulder. In the middle of the room rests a keg which has already been drenched in it's contents and the source of the pool that leaked under the door is quickly determined. Love: Tony Houston...Tony Houston.....Congradulations on your amazing victory with the Cold Drop and that very move has helped you earn your first INWI championship and it was in quite impressive fashion... Houston: [Takes tap from keg and inserts it into his mouth and takes a long...healthy swig of the blue collar champagne..] Well, Miss. Love I have got just one thing to say...WOOOOOO!!!! Love: Surely you have to be surprised about how the fans here at the INWI have welcomed you since your "history" with Mr. Apples has been revealed. Houston: [whiping dribbled beer from his clean shaven] Well..Mis--Miss Love with your old phat ass....Pure hot and tempting shortie, but honestly, I can care less whether the fans boo me, cheer me, or both. Shiznit, I came to this league several months ago, and I was booed quite heavily, and I still won my matches...my "history" as you so eloquently put it, with Mr. Apples was revealed the fans begin to look at me in another perspective. And to be honest, it's a entirely new reception..it's unlike that Tony Houston has received at any other time or place. So Ms. Love I honestly b'lieve it would be too early to speak on my emotions and feelings of that. Love: Well...that's is understandable as it is one of the INWI's very own announcers that entitled you with the name of the "cockiest S.O.B. in INWI". Houston: Well, Ms. Love let it be known, and let it be known now the INWI Northeast Television Champion is STILL the "cockiest S.O.B. in the INWI." However, Ms. Love as you can see we still have a LOT of celebrating so unless you would like to be hit on constantly I mus-- [Before Houston can finish his statement, Mr. Apples burst into the room and simply stares at Tony Houston before cracking a smile and embracing the newly crowned champion..] Houston: Brother...this [points to the keg] is not only for us...Be my guest Brother-man.. [Apples then steps to the keg and takes a healthy portion himself, then steps up and spits it into a mist high into the air, and the celebration continues...but Houston steps back to Tiffany Love.] Houston: And ol' Bill Bob Redneck, son, I remember a time when "Cold & Calculating" first entered the INWI you made a reference on how I was talking out my ass...Well son, it does now appear that this "..overly confident rookie.." has whooped your ass....And son, if you think you are going to regain this title [holds title in front of camera.] You need to snap out of that friggin' dream dun. This belt is STAYING with me...Come hell or high wata'... [Screen fades as Houston and Apples persuade to Tiffany Love to do a keg stand...] ============================================================ 2. Cougar rents people and space for his own ''show...'' LIVE FROM MEMPHIS TENESSEE, DIAMOND ACE PRODUCTIONS PROUDLY PRESENTS, JAMMIN WITH JIMMY!!!!!!!!! Jimmy Cougar: Since the INWI can't hire any decent announcers, I've decided that it's in my best interests to conduct my own interview segments! (Huge POP from the PAID studio audience.) Jimmy Cougar: My first guest on the INWI's edition of this show is none other than the One Man Crime Spree, Adam Kuhl. (Symphony of Destruction by Megadeath blares, as Kuhl comes out, swinging his baseball bat. He almost nails a couple of fans, and they start booing, but Cougar points above at the applause sign, and rubs his fingers together, as if to say that they won't get any money, and the jeers turn to cheers. Kuhl makes his way to the interview desk.) Jimmy Cougar: Well, Adam, what a great reaction from the crowd! Adam Kuhl: Well, you have to give these idiots some credit! They know true talent when they see it, and that's what I am! Jimmy Cougar: You sure are, Spree! You're almost as talented as me, for chrissakes! Anyhow, Monday Night Hot Spot has come and gone, and once again, the INWI has screwed over Double J! What do you intend to do about it? Adam Kuhl: Well, Jimmy, what I'm going to do is put an end to one Mr Banana! He's been getting in our faces for a couple weeks now, and enough is enough! Banana, Double J and I have a big surprise in store for you, next time you decide to show your face, and I guarantee that it will be the last time! Jimmy Cougar: Well, Adam, now that we've dealt with this issue, there's your own career you've got to consider. What goals do you have in the INWI? Adam Kuhl: Well, as you know Jimmy, I was in that big battle royal last week, and I managed to pretty well. I think I'm setting my sights on the Northeast TV title. Jimmy Cougar: Well, that's a good starting ground. Anything I can do to help, you just let me know! Now, I've got a few things to get off my chest as well, and since it's MY show.... Adam Kuhl: You just go right ahead, Double J, I'm gonna smack out some fans! Jimmy Cougar: HAHAHA! What a fun guy he is! Now, Mr Banana, you've already got your warning! I will secure another TV title match, and when I do, I want you barred from the building! Your actions were simply deplorable! And as for Newark's comments that I bring this crap on myself, well that just proves what kind of ******** I have to put up with each and every day in this rinky dink promotion! (Cougar grabs a sip of coffee and then continues.) Jimmy Cougar: Now, I've got some words for a man that has repeatedly ignored my challenges. Brody Thunder...the great and powerful Lone Wolf Barry Windham wannabee! After I punk your brother, or uncle, or whatever the hell Harvey Thunder is to you, I want you....BIGTIME! You are the one thorn in my side! Every federation I've ever been in, I've dominated, but for some reason, it hasn't happened yet in the INWI! Well, I've finally figured out the reason. Despite this apparent friction between you and Comissioner DeLuise, I think he's in your backpocket, and that's why you keep defending your belt against idiots like Rizzotto and Zack the Technician Sharp! You don't have the guts to get in the ring with the one and only original Double J, do you? My 6 ft 2, 235 lb frame sends shivers down your spine, doesn't it? Don't answer that, bigman! I know you're bigger than me, and I know you're stronger than me, but what I also know and what big goons like you will never figure out, is that I'm a hell of alot better than you! I've met and beaten EVERYONE that has ever been put in front of me, given time. Oh, I may lose the first time, and I may lose the second time, and I may even lose the third time, but each loss gives me the experience I need to get better, and prevail against some of the longest odds that face anyone in this business. Do you think I won my fifteen World titles by accident? Do you think you are any better than the other men I beat? The answer to both those questions is NO! I am the single most talented athlete to ever grace a wrestling ring, and when the promoters are finally forced into signing a match between us, you and the whole damn INWI are going to find that out! Adam Kuhl: Are you done yet, Jimmy? We're pretty much out of time here! Jimmy Cougar: Yeah, I'm done for now, but this whole business is really getting me worked out! Get Donna and Rochelle on the phone! Tell them they can check into Heartbreak Hotel! Adam Kuhl: You got it, Double J. Ah....a couple of the fans may be needing a little more money than you promised them.....HAHAHHAHAHA! Jimmy Cougar: Oh, Spree, you're too much! For Adam Kuhl, I'm Double J, Jammin Jimmy Cougar, and this has been Jammin with Jimmy! ============================================================ 3. House and arena show reports from last week...
