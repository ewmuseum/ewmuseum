[Winter Storm '97 Continues...] Winter Storm '97 Springer (with Dr. Bedpan) vs Mike "The Gavel" Garvey Springer in the ring now and we are under way. Collar and elbow tie up and Springer with an armbar and an armdrag. Garvey back up and a hip toss and Springer lands on his feet. Springer runs into the ropes and a dropkick and Garvey sidesteps it and drops an elbow drop. Springer is back up and he scoops up Garvey and he puts him down with a sidewalk slam. Garvey's ribs almost shattered completely on that one. Springer Irish whips him into the corner and comes running in with a clothesline. Springer Irish whips him back out of the corner and now Springer charges and Garvey with a drop toe hold and a slap to the back of the head of Springer. Springer is back up to a vertical base and he is going for a Springer Cutter but Garvey pushes him face first into the ropes and he hits a kidney punch and that was well executed. He picks up Springer and hits a belly to back suplex. He runs into the ropes and hits a legdro..no. Springer moved and Garvey hit hard that time. Springer with a running clothesline and another, and another. Springer scoops him up and hits a back breaker and holds him on the knee and that is a submission move right there. Springer with a vertical suplex nicely done and a cover on Garvey, 1......2.....and a kick out by Garvey. Springer is going to the outside and he is climbing the ropes up top and he comes down with a knee drop that misses and now he is limping. Garvey dropkicks the back of the knee and Springer hits the canvas. Garvey has the leg and he lifts it up and slams it down to the mat. He brings the leg over to the corner and he places it on the bottom rope and jumps up and comes down right on top of the leg. Springer is in pain you can see it on the look on his face. Garvey picks him up and sets up and hits an inseguri to the back of the head and he goes for the cover and the win, 1.....2....and Springer kicks out in 2 and a half. Garvey has him now and he is going for the Figure 4 but Springer takes the foot and pushes Garvey off of him and he goes crashing into the buckles. Springer lifts up Garvey on the top rope backwards and he climbs up there with him and Springer with a power hoist all the way over and down and Garvey got planted. The cover, 1...... 2..... and Garvey got the right shoulder up. Springer is back on the offensive and he is trying to nurse that injured leg. A chop by Springer and he hits a boot to the gut and he lifts up Garvey and he hits a shoulder breaker. He picks him and sends him into the ropes and scoops him up and twirls him around but Garvey and Springer do a cartwheel and they are on opposite angles and Garvey spikes Springer with a Tombstone piledriver. Garvey has the opportunity. He has a leg and he spins it around and there is the Figure 4 applied in the middle of the ring. The ref is checking on Springer and he is in real pain here. Garvey is wrenching it on and he is holding onto the bottom rope for leverage but he lets it go when the ref comes over and checks. Springer is blocking out the pain and he is rocking back and forth. He turns it over and now Garvey is in pain. He breaks the hold and Springer is mad. Springer picks him up but Garvey to much to hold on that leg and he puts him down and a SPRINGER CUTTER!! Right there but he was right near the ropes and Garvey flew under the bottom rope and he is down and out on the outside. Springer fell to the mat and he is having a hard time moving because of the bad wheel. Springer to the outside and he grabs Garvey and throws him back into the ring. Springer rolls him over and goes for the cover, 1.......2.....3no!!! No, No, No.!! Springer is very upset and he is over to the referee and he is yelling at the ref that it was a slow count. He grabs the referee by the collar and he is still yelling and Garvey is back up and he has a rollup and he has a hand full of tights and the ref doesn't care, 1..........2.......3!!! It's over. Springer made a mistake and it cost him the match. I can't belive it. Again the winner of the match in the time of 13:21, Mike Garvey. Winter Storm '97 Tag Team Championship Vinny Vitrone/James Pillow (with Mr. Flamboyant) vs The Masks This is odd. This is a team that is supposedly in the INWI. And the INWI front office allowed the Comforters to pick their replacements in this tag title match. The Comforters were of course hurt last week after dropping the tag titles to Pillow and Vitrone. However, they were unable to compete tonight and the INWI allowed them to pick their own replacements. All we know is that these guys are known as the "Masks." Obviously a team trying to regain some glory or trying to finally make a name for themselves. I have a bad feeling that Pillow and Vitrone are going to roll in this match. Both teams are in the ring. The Masks have one guy with a blue outfit and the other is red….so we’ll call them Blue and Red. Pillow is going to start it off for the Brotherhood and Blue is in there for the Masks. Tie up in the middle of the ring and Pillow takes control early with a kick to the groin area and grabs Blue and stuns him off the top rope. Blue is grabbing his throat and Pillow laughs and tags in Vinny Vitrone. The Vindicator goes over and grabs Blue and nails a suplex. Vitrone off the ropes and drops an elbow. Vitrone with another elbow and a cover, 1…….2…….NO! Kick out from Blue and Blue scrambles to his corner and tags in Red. Vitrone laughs and goes for a tie up and Red slaps Vitrone. Vitrone is stunned and look at his eyes. Vitrone attacks and Red with a leap frog, Vitrone off the ropes and Red with a drop kick. These two Masks are here to wrestle, that’s for sure. Red off the ropes again and nails a spinning DDT! Cover by Red, 1………2………NO! Pillow in the ring to break it up. All four men are in the ring and this is a pier six brawl!! I guess you could call this an old fashioned "Slobberknocker!" Red grabs Vitrone and nails a chop [Whooooo!] and then hits a drop kick that sends Vitrone to the mat. Vitrone with a low blow!! Red falls to the floor and Vitrone crawls to his corner and tags Pillow. "Simply The Best" struts into the ring and goes for Red, who headbutts Pillow in the midsection and then drops Pillow on his face. This Masks team can handle their own, there’s no doubt about that! Pillow back up and the two tie up in the ring and Pillow takes control of Red. Pillow with a coupe of knees to the midsection and then fires Red into the ropes. Red bounces back and there’s a spinning heel kick from Pillow to flatten Red. Pillow with a little more strutting and I am not sure he should be doing that yet. This match is not over. Pillow scoops up Red and sends him into the corner and follows that in with a drop kick and Red’s neck just snapped backwards. Red falls face first to the mat and Pillow climbs to the second rope and nails a leg drop to the back of Red’s head! Pillow is a great talent no doubt, but his cockiness is starting to come out again as he struts around. Pillow grabs Red and goes for a roundhouse right….RED BLOCKS IT! Red with a right of his own and a left….drop kick! Pillow stumbles back and another drop kick from Red sends Pillow to the mat. Pillow back up and Red sends Pillow over the top rope with a clothesline!! Red to the top rope and splashes Pillow on the outside!! Red rolls back in the ring and the crowd is going nuts!! Red goes over and tags Blue and Pillow rolls back in the ring and tags Vitrone. Now Vitrone and Blue tie up and Vitrone with an eye rake and then a clothesline. Vitrone with a double arm DDT!! A cover, 1……2……..NO! Blue kicks out. Amazing. That was a great move by Vitrone. Blue and Vitrone both on their feet and Vitrone with a kick to the gut… and then another DDT! Vitrone grabs Blue, sends him into the ropes and the ref can’t move and he gets clobbered. Blue stumbles back…..OH MAN! LIL’ BRUTHA CUTTA’!!!! Vitrone hit his new move and he has a cover and the ref is out! HAHA! The Brotherhood is paying for it now. Pillow in the ring and here comes Red. Four way brawl and this one is getting out of hand. Vitrone up and over trying to get the ref up. Pillow and Red over the top rope and to the floor. They are brawling on the outside. The ref is coming to and what the….. BLUE WITH A ROLL UP!! 1……2……..3!!!!!!! OH MY!!! WE HAVE NEW CHAMPS!! Ladies and gentlemen, the winners in 16:34 and NEW INWI Tag Team Champions….. The Masks?? Wait the Masks are taking the masks off. What’s going on? OH MY! IT’S THE FRIENDSHIP EXPRESS!! THEY’RE BACK AND THEY WON THE TITLES!!! The Friendship Express run from the ring as the rest of the Brotherhood comes to the ring. The walls…. The walls….. the walls are crumbling down…. On the Brotherhood that is!!! Winter Storm '97 MAIN EVENT INWI HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP "The Ringmaster" Tony Houston (with Mr. Flamboyant) vs Tommy Graves This should say the least be an outstanding match. Graves who several months ago, first arrived on the scene and has made an immediate impact, and the majority of that rage has been towards The Brotherhood, more specifically, the champion, Tony Houston. As Houston has seemed to show a total lack of disrespect for Graves ability. Well, enough of the talk, enough of the apparent head games played by the INWI Champion. Let's get ready to rock. Graves makes his way towards the ring with Jonathan Hammer who's right knee is wrapped heavy in ice and is trying to use crutches for support. Graves into the ring and simply leans in the corner staring down the aisleway, with an intense look, and he'd better be intense if you want to win the INWI's most cherished prize. Houston is on his way towards the ring with no Mr. Flamboyant, No Brotherhood. But it's a guarantee that they are looking on and quite bitter following Apples loss of the Northeast Championship to Lonestar..HAHA And I love it. Houston into the ring and hands the diamond studded INWI Heavyweight Championship belt over to the ref. and then quickly begins bumping head with Tommy Graves in the center of the ring. The ref tries to break them but is sent into the ropes with a one armed push by both men. Unnecessary abuse to our officials, but hey, let them go out it. Houston with a boot the midsection of Graves and bounces off the ropes..Graves ducks a clothesline, Houston catches Graves with a Mafia Side Kick only staggering Graves!!! Graves is a monster..Houston charges and clips the knee of Graves dropping the big man. Houston grabs Graves' right knee and drops an elbow on it, and then stomps the knee several stern times. Houston pulls up Graves...and lifts...Houston just dropped the folded knee of Graves across his own thigh...a punishing Knee Breaker. Houston though scum, is a smart athlete in that ring, he realizes that Graves is bigger, so he'll work on his base which will weaken his balance, and that's when Houston will attack. But don't get it wrong, he's still a jerk. Houston pulls up Graves and pushes Graves into the corner and wrapped Graves' right knee in the ropes..and wrenches forward...the knee is bending in a way that knees aren't meant to bend. Houston stomping the knee now...Graves' is in early trouble, if Graves cannot use his monstrous size his offense could be severely wounded. Houston snap mares Graves out of the corner and Houston dropkicks Graves in the back of the head. Houston pulls up Graves and whips him into the ro--Reversal by Graves...Houston got caught..POWERSLAM by Graves. Cover, One...two..No!!! Houston slips his shoulder up. Graves is slowly making his way towards his feet and is favoring his right knee, but who wouldn't after the damage Houston inflicted early on. Graves pulls up Houston and sends the champ reeling into the corner with a vicious forearm uppercut. Graves charges and raises Houston off the ground with an awesome knee lift. Graves grabs Houston in a body hug..Stun Gun!! Cover One..two..t-No!!!
