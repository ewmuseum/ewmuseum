[INWI] 1997 Ringmaster PPV Free 4 All

25 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The 1997 Ringmaster Free 4 All The Fleet Center Boston, Mass. Sunday, August 24, 1997 =============================================================== Brian Newark - Ladies and gentlemen, we are only 30 minutes away from the 1997 Ringmaster pay-per-view. We have all sorts of comments and one match for you on this Free 4 All. "The Icon" Pimp Daddy Brown will take on Predator during the next 30 minutes. But, before that, let me introduce my co host of this Free 4 All, Scoop Ropes... Scoop - Whoooooo! We are here baby and am I ready! I can't wait! Tonight is gonna be a great day and I am so excited! Yeah Newark, this will be a great night! Newark - Well, all of your buddies are wrestling, so I figured you would be exciting. We will also hear from a lot of people, including comments from Mr. Banana! This will be the first time anyone has heard from Mr. Banana since he turned on Mr. Apples!! Let's get started right now and hear from Mr. Banana, who is in the locker room awaiting his match, which is just hours away.... [Mr. Banana sits alone in a locker room. He has a towel in his hand and is only wearing a pair of shorts. He is shaking his head and then looks up and begins to start...] Well Apples, the time has come. Me and you. In a cage. I guess the INWI wants this to be a one time deal. Never have you and I together again. That's fine with me. One time and the winner is the winner. Then we can toss this behind us. I know no matter what, you have new found respect for me. I am no longer in your shadow. I am my own man. I am going to win this match at the Ringmaster. I am ready for you. I have been ready for this for 20 years. Now I get the chance. Well Apples, this is going to be a long match for you because I am gonna beat you from one side of the cage to the other. Apples. I was in your shadow. Not any more. We had it all together, but you never let me get out of your shadow. Now it is time I show you that you should of held me on an equal level the whole time. It is my turn to shine Apples. You and all of your high priced crap. No, it's time to get back to basics Apples. You and me and one brawl. May the best man win. And I do plan to... [Banana turns his back and slowly starts to get ready for the match at hand...] Scoop - That is going to be a nasty cage match. Nasty. Newark - I couldn't agree with you any more Scoop. This is a match I don't know if the INWI was ready for. Let's hear from Jammin' Jimmy Cougar, who goes for the Northeast TV Title tonight... Tiffany Love: This is Tiffany Love for the INWI. I'm here in the dressing room of Jimmy Cougar and Adam Kuhl, as Cougar prepares for his TV title match with Cold and Calculating Tony Houston. Jimmy, I've got a couple of... Jimmy Cougar: Save your breath, Tiff! I know what you're going to ask me. It's the same questions all the time! Adam Kuhl: Do you want me to kick the bitch out, Double J? Jimmy Cougar: Nah, she's amusing! Anyhow, toots, you probably want to know how I plan to take away the belt from a guy that's beaten me before, don't you? Tiffany Love: Well, actually... Jimmy Cougar: HUSH! Not another word! Yeah, Houston's beaten me before, so what! Like I've told you all before, anyone can beat me once! I get lazy just like the next guy....but I learn from my mistakes, and I promise you, I will get the better of him this time! Now, your next question is probably "What do you intend to do if Mr Banana gets involved?", isn't it? Tiffany Love: I wasn't really going to... Jimmy Cougar: ENOUGH! Banana is a non-factor! We have a plan A, a plan B, all the way to Plan ZZZ to deal with that idiot! He will not ruin this chance for me! You see, today is going to be the beginning of a new era. Now, I suppose you will say "But you've said this before....". Well, yeah, I have. You can believe me if you want, or you can keep putting your head in the sand! I am getting this TV title shot for a reason! The last PPV, Jimmy Cougar was stuck in the undercard, with that anchor of a partner Jason Starr, and the buy rates suffered extremely. This time, I've heard some advance word on them, and they're a lot better, simply because Double J is getting a title shot. Tiffany Love: Why am I even here? You obviously aren't going to let me ask any questions. Jimmy Cougar: I don't know. I didn't ask you in here! Adam, take the girl for a drink. I'll finish this one myself. (Cougar grabs the mic, as Kuhl and Love exit the room.) Jimmy Cougar: I GUARANTEE I will win the TV title tonight. You can put that in stone. The next time a PPV rolls around for the INWI, I will be defending said title, or fighting for a better one. This is also a promise. And before the end of the year, much to the chagrin of Brody Thunder and Commissioner DeLuise, who's been holding me back since Day One in this fed, I will be fighting for and DEFENDING the INWI Heavyweight title! Thunder, you can't duck me forever. Tonight is just going to be a sampling of what I'm capable of and I suggest you pay VERY close attention, paper champion. (Cougar slams the mic down, then shouts at the camera....) Jimmy Cougar: ONE MORE THING....IT'S GOOD TO BE GREAT!!! Scoop - He is ready and I think Double J is gonna get that belt tonight! What a match that will be! Newark - I agree, it will be a good match. Let's go up to the ring for our Free 4 All match... Ringmaster Free 4 All "The Icon" Pimp Daddy Brown vs Predator "The Icon" Pimp Daddy Brown returns to the ring at this PPV extravaganza! We haven't heard from the Pimp Daddy in a while, but I am sure he is ready to go and is looking to climb the ranks again and go for the Heavyweight Title. After all, he was the first ever INWI Heavyweight Champion! He is taking on Predator and looks to be ready to go. Both men lock up and Predator goes low to knock the wind out of Pimp Daddy. He now goes to work on Brown's back driving his forearms into his spine. Pimp Daddy now goes low and Predator buckles over in pain, and Pimp Daddy backdrops him down. Daddy drops a leg and now locks on an arm bar. Predator can't reach the ropes because Pimp won't let go. Predator finally gets to the ropes and Brown breaks the hold. Predator now hunching over and holding his shoulder. Pimp back to the ropes and comes off with a scissors kick onto Predator's shoulder. Brown now picks up Predator and delivers a shoulder breaker. Predator in pain as "the Icon" mounts the top. He comes off with a shoulder block but Predator dodges and Pimp Daddy hits hard. Predator trying for a powerbomb and he hits it but his shoulder gives and he falls to the ground. Pimp Daddy is up before Predator and he delivers a few kicks to Predator's shoulder. Pimp Daddy setting up for the Pimp-A-Plex now, and he hits it. It's all over....1...2....3. You winner of this match in 8:01, "The Icon" Pimp Daddy Brown!! Scoop - Well, the "Icon" is back I guess. Let's see if he can stick around long enough to get another match. Newark - He looked sharp there Scoop. We have more comments to go to.... let's hear from "Master of Pain" Michael Bane... (camera opens on Michael Bane in a close up on his eyes, intensely powerful and slowly fades back to reveal his whole person....) ["MoP" MB] "I have been a professional wrestler for 9 years now. All of these 9 long years, through, barbed wire matches, through scaffold matches, through 9 planes of hell matches, have I not come across people, who I despise more than pansy-@$$ butt pirates and their rainbow pride. I hail from New York City. My friends, you have not endured until you have spent a year in the city. Homeless people crawling in the gutters. Ghetto shoot outs. Innocent children being maimed. Murders. Prostitution. Arsen, and these are just a few. Jonathan Hammer, you have not seen hardcore yet. You have not seen what I can do to your body. You do not know the type of hurt I can put you in...your comments at the press conference were uncalled for. Do you think it's funny to laugh at people who have been beat by their own parents? Now you have made this personal and now you are going to pay for your sins in was that you will find out soon. Michael Bledsoe. You come from the most boring town in the United States of America, Yorktown NY. That town is filled with freaks and pot heads. That is where the only place for people to hang out is at the local Texaco station. You couldn't recognize suffering if it started to talk to you on the street. But you will realize pain soon enough when I take you on you piece of crap. But until then you and that putz-off Johny boy can have fun together. Meanwhile, I have to contend with this feather weight...Mike Quick. I will cut through you like a hot knife through butter. You will wish you hadn't been born when I am done with you. I will see you in the ring pansy boy." (camera fades out on Mike Bane) Scoop - Bane is crazy! God he is fun to watch!! Newark - It will be something... hey, we have some problems out in the parking lot, let's go to our cameras out there to see what is going on.... [Mr. Banana and Adam Kuhl are going at it. Banana has Kuhl up and drops him ith a choke slam. INWI officials and Boston Police are swarming all over the place. Banana adds a couple of kicks to Kuhl who is already down. A camera close up shows that Banana is all banged up and is busted open. How Kuhl got Banana outside is what we can't understand. Boston Police scoop up Kuhl and handcuff him. Kuhl starts to shout at Banana who gets escorted inside the Fleet Center by INWI officials. ] Newark - What the hell was that all about? Scoop - I don't know, but Kuhl got some good shots in, did you see Banana's face? Newark - That was uncalled for, but nothing we wouldn't expect from Kuhl and Cougar. Let's hear from "Simply The Best" James Pillow... <SCENE-Inside The SimplyBest Mansion we see James Pillow and Angel. James has on a Black Armani Suit and his hair slicked back. Angel is wearing a white silk gown, low cut around the chest and a slit up her right leg. James looks at the Ringmaster card and begins to laugh> James "Simply The Best" Pillow:Well Mr. Brown it appears that our long awaited match is finally going to take place at Ringmaster.... Well you've already felt my wrath and the wrath of all the Fellas. Now at Ringmaster it's just me and you, last time you walked off the luckiest Son of a ***** on the face of the planet. This time you wont be so lucky...I am ready for all of your little tricks and I have The boys watching my back <shows Fellas handsign> My friend "Oil Slick" you are in for the toughest match of your life, and it's one you aren't going to win. When I'm finished with you they're going to have to mop you off the mat. Another thing I have to address,The Championship Committee is making a big mistake. The Fellas only work on the top of the card and you stuck us on the under card? Well you're going to lose ratings and money. Next time you'll know better and we'll be right at the top, where we belong. Brandon, Kodiac I would say good luck my Fellas, but we all know you dont need luck to beat those two pieces of garbage. I'll see you guys at the hotel after for the celebration. Now back to you Mr. Brown when we step in the ring the better man will win, and I am the better man. After Ringmaster you will know that... Angel:When you mess with Simply The Best <laughs> You DIE like ALL the rest! <James pulls Angel close to him and kisses her long and passionately as the camera fades> Scoop - He's ready for that little punk Brown. "Oil Slick'' is gonna go down and Pillow is gonna trash him... Newark - Either way, that should be a great match. That has some big bearings on the Light Heavyweight rankings... Next, let's hear from The Springer and The Comforters... (Tiffany Love walks into the gym called the bedroom, she hears yelling and screaming coming from across the gym) Dr Bedpan: HELLO TIFF COME ON OVER!!!!! TL: Hello Dr. Bedpan, looks like everyone is getting ready for the big weekend. Any time for a little interview with the guys? DBP: For you anytime, anyone else, like that Ropes punk and they would have to wait. My boys have a great chance this weekend to bring home all the major gold in this federation. This is the shot we have all been waiting for. TL: We know that The Comforters are the Tag champs but do you really think that Springer can beat Thunder? DBP: Who is the only wrestler to pin Thunder ? Springer, why has it taken so long for this to happen? I can only think of one thing, However we have are shot and will take advantage of this shot. Springer: HEY TIFF!!!!!!!! WHOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!! Will guess who has a shot at winning the Heavyweight title? ME!!!!!!!!! Let me tell you this, I believe in my heart that I will win. And just in case you Bill Bob have any ideas of doing something to ruin this chance, I would leave me alone OR YOU WILL PAY DEARLY!!!!!!! TL: He has been a bit of a pain for you lately hasn't he? Springer: Enough about that, Thunder, your a great wrestler, however it's my turn. Boxspring: WHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! This will be the best night in the history of our family, all the major gold and all for us. We can't be denied!!!!!!!!!! Freedom Express look out. We have beat you before and we will do it again. Tell'em Mattress!!!! Mattress: I'm just going to say one thing and one thing only. We are on a mission, we have set our minds to winning both belts and that is what we are going to do. Express, hehehe, you know what happens to your freedom when your in the ring with us, BAM, gone. That is all (all 4 at once) SEE YOU IN THE RING Scoop - Oh, yippeee!!! The little girls are gonna try and win! Yey! I am so happy for them, cause they are all my favorites! God.... those guys make me sick. Why can't someone just beat the hell out of them and get them out of the INWI... Newark - Well, I don't think they like you either Scoop... let's go to Ed Rogers, who is in a strange place from what I understand... ( Ed Rogers sits on the Fleet Center's roof, his hands around his knees. His shaggy brown hair moves with the breeze, it's movement in drastic contrast with the still stare Ed uses to examine the numerous stars. He begins...) Ed Rogers: "I saw the best minds of my generation destroyed by madness, starving hysterical naked, dragging themselves through the negro streets at dawn looking for an angry fix"... Those are the words of Allen Ginsberg, in his most famous work, Howl. I have always felt near to Mr. Ginsberg, and now that I understand his feelings about his friends falling into the abyss of insanity I can say without a single doubt in my mind that we are truly akin. I too know what it is like to see the best men of my generation wasted. Such is the case with the man I face, Zack Sharpe. I tried to save him from Brody Thunder, but I failed. I tried to convince Zack to turn himself into the psychiatric community when he first showed signs of madness, but I failed. If I fail again the INWI is doomed. In a game in which after three strikes one is out, I am in the whole "O" and two. (Ed takes a deep breath and then looks into the camera.) I love the INWI, I am trying to save the INWI, and yet the INWI paints me as the villain and Zack as the crusader against the forces of all that is evil. Zack is a good person, but Zack is not the same man who defeated Dell Pickles so long ago. If you don't believe me, go to Blockbusters and rent the INWI's first paid per view, then fast forward to his match with Brody Thunder, and then look into the eyes of our friend Sharpe. If you can honestly say that the look in Zack's eyes are not that of animal, that of an insane man THEN and ONLY then will you have the right to say that I am wrong. Only after doing this will you have the right to think of me as insane. ( Ed removes his eyes from the camera and looks into the starry night sky, so brilliant it is over the Boston skyline.) It is beautiful out here. And after tonight, only one of us will be able to enjoy it. I will not bother you with worthless promises of my victory. I don't know if I can win. I don't know if I will be alive after tonight. All I know is that after this match, the INWI will NEVER be the same. Scoop - The INWI won't be the same... I bet not. It hasn't been the same since this nut came into the INWI... Newark - That will be a most interesting match, that is for sure. Also, last night, after the Press Conference went off the air, "Too Slick" Rick Brown showed up late. After he apologized for getting caught up, here is what he had to say... Brown: Before we get to questions, I want to say something to all the "Too Slick" Rick Brown fans...I will be done with fluff boy in less than 10 minutes on the Pay Per View.. Reporter: Are you worried about Mr. Cherry sticking his nose in?? Brown: Am I worried?? No, should Mr. Cherry be worried?? Definitely..I mean I've been stuck in this little tussle between Cherry and Pillow boy..I've beaten them both already, so to be honest, I'm a little upset I have to beat them again..But after I get done with fluff boy, if Cherry wants to come in, I'll take him out in less than 5 minutes... Reporter: What about the interference on Mr. Pillow's part in your match last week?? Brown: That was the biggest mistake of his life..If I wasn't anymore upset to begin with, he really pissed me off...The fact of the matter is: "Too Slick" Rick Brown is one of the most exciting young superstars in the industry and if punks like James "Fluffy" Pillow decide they want to test me than bring it on...But after the Pay Per View and after I pin Pillow one more time...I'm moving on to bigger and better things.. Reporter: Does that mean titles, are you going after the TV title?? Brown: Not necessarily, it means there are bigger fish to fry and I'd like to make more progress..If a title shot arouses tomorrow that great, if it takes three months, that's fine too..."Too Slick" Rick Brown has never had a problem with proving himself over and over again...But this is Pillow's last chance...10 minutes or less and you're out D.C. style...Punk...No more questions.. Scoop - Ol' "Oil Slick" will be done with Pillow in 10 minutes? I hope they both are done in 10 minutes... Newark - Either way, that should be a real good match. Well, we are almost set to go to the pay per view Scoop... Scoop - Yes, Thunder will be ripping Springer apart! Newark - As we get ready for the start of the pay per view, let's hear from Mr. Apples... [Mr. Apples sitting alone in his locker room. He has a black shirt that says Brotherhood in red letters. His hair is wet and he is in his wrestling singlet. His boots are below him. He sits back and you can tell that he is focused. He sits back and cracks his neck and talks in a low voice....] Well Banana, the time has come. You and I. Just a couple of hours away. They will erect that steel cage and we will take on each other. You and I. One on one with the best man getting the win. Of course, that best man will be me. Jealousy.... Jealousy Banana. Flat outright jealousy. That's why we are in this situation. No other reason other than that. If you weren't so jealous, we would still be doing things the way we used to. Like best friends. Which I thought we were. I would of done anything for you Banana. Anything. But you had to go and do this. Now you and I have to go at it....inside a steel cage. Unfortunately for what you may think, this will be one of the worst days in your life. Not only have you lost a good friend, but you will also take one of the worst beatings in your life. No longer am I there to back you up. You Banana are going to have a long day in that cage. Jealousy... The INWI is gonna have a hard one with this one. Good thing this match is on pay-per-view, because they could never put this on regular TV. This will be one of the most brutal matches I have ever been in and unfortunately, it is against Banana. Banana, you let that Jealousy get to you. It is horrible, but I will end all of your doubts on who is the better of us tonight. Jealousy... [With that Apples signals for the cameraman to leave and he sits back.
