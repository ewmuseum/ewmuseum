[INWI] Inside The Ropes, Part 2

26 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Newark: Welcome back to Inside the Ropes .. Riley: [cutting off Newark] Enough!!! I'll take this one, Newark, next up we have the MAN. Hey Graves N..E champ...that means Northeast Champion..and the King of the eWrestling territory...."King" Apples... =========================================================== The room is black except for one light at one end. The camera works it's way closer and you see King Apples staring at a TV. On the television is a N.E.W. card. The match is King Apples against James Pillow. The result, Pillow wins and claims the N.E.W. World Championship.] KING APPLES: Pillow, you know you can't claim to be the real world champion... [Apples rewinds the VCR and shows the camera that it took half of the N.E.W. to take Apples down and then "Desire" Brandon Bloom making the infamous three count...] KING APPLES - Pillow, you're nothing but a paper champion in a second rate league. It is a league full of has beens and never will be's. But then again, you and your bed buddy Bloom would know about that. [Apples then fast forwards a little and shows the match with Pillow and Brother Tony Houston on a recent Hot Spot for the INWI Heavyweight Championship. It shows Houston gaining a clean 1...2....3.] KING APPLES: Well Pillow, see what happens when it's fair. One on one. Mano e mano? But you wouldn't know that, because in the N.E.W. that funzanool GoogFella lets everything go if his panty wearing Fella's are involved. And now Pillow, you get to run in the N.E.W. with no competition. Maybe you'll get to wrestle your boyfriend Kodiac, if he ever stays around long enough. [Apples then flicks on the lights and sits back in his recliner. He is wearing a red jump suit with a Phillies hat on. He wears a nice new pair of Nike's and he smiles at the camera. He grabs his beer and takes a swallow.] KING APPLES: But Pillow, we are not in the N.E.W. anymore. It's like Dorothy said to Toto in the Wizard of Oz, we're not in Kansas anymore. Well, you're all alone here James. No punk Desire. No Houdini Kodiac. It's just you. The Fellas are not around to slip it to you after your match is complete. Although, maybe they ought to be back stage to watch me whip the living daylights out of you. Because NOW, it's gonna be fair James. And now, it's my Northeast Championship on the line. [Apples reaches down and grabs the INWI NortheastChampionship. He shows it to the camera and then looks at it closely.] KING APPLES: I have worked very hard to get this title James and you're not going to take it away from me. Not a chance in the world. You know what they say, even a blind chipmunk finds a nut now and then. Well, it just so happens you got lucky in late October. You should of never had that second shot at that title. You had it the month before. But originality moves away, doesn't it? And you know where I am getting at. [Apples gets upand starts to walk out of the room. He takes off his hat and runs his hand through his hair and then puts his hands to his side.] KING APPLES: Pillow, your days are numbered here. We ran off Bloom from the INWI. We ran off, well actually, we sent Kodiac to the Hospital and now he's gone. The Brotherhood has chased each of them out of the INWI, but you still remain. I have some respect for you there, because you stayed to fight. Kodiac had no choice, we about killed him. And Bloom just ran. But you're next Pillow. You're next. And this Monday starts your nightmare ending to this horror movie you call a career. [With that, Apples leaves the room and the camera fades.] ============================================================ Newark: Again, I'll say it that Pillow vs. Apples match will be an OUTSTANDING athletic display. Riley: You really are annoying Newark. And I'LL say it again, that Apples will squash that pansy assed Pillow.. Scoops: He's not a former light heavyweight champion...but he's an EX light heavyweight champion...sounds a lot more permanent. Newark: Yeah well it's sure to be an outstanding match...and on deck we have a special individual... Riley: Special?? what is that your cover up for stupid? Newark: No!!!! IT's the Ultimate Perversion.... =========================================================== <Sitting down in a room somewhere. Ultimate Perversion, wearing the now obligatory t-shirt, wearing very sombre expression.> Tiffany: Ultimate, you lost the TV belt AND the tag belt at the PPV, what are your thoughts. Ultimate: <Composing himself for a few seconds.> What I want to say is this. The title was taken from me, and what is taken from me I WILL regain, by fair means or foul. Tiffany: You`ve lost the title before, but you've always been positive. What's different? Ultimate: I'm getting sick of this **** to be honest. I mean the schedule was set at the PPV so I would definitely lose. Tiffany: Are you implying that the PPV, in regards to the TV title match, was fixed? Ultimate: How many more times do I have to have that title taken away from me? It's like a game of pass the ******* parcel. Tiffany: You seem to be quite upset by the turn of events. Ultimate: Upset? UPSET!? What the **** do you expect. I work my ass off in the ring. The crowd loves Cloud and I, and for what? So I can lose the goddam title again. Tiffany: This week, Cloud has a shot at the title. Would you be upset if Cloud took the title? Ultimate: Not at all. He's the only one who I don't mind winning. I'll be there with him, to help if necessary. Tiffany: Do you mean interfere? Ultimate: Let me put it this way, there are some desperate people in the INWI. If they want to get into the ring and get a piece of Cloud, they have to get through ME first. If I can't win it, then Cloud is a good second choice. Tiffany: Back to your thoughts on the PPV, did you think it was unfairly laid out? Ultimate: Damn right. I mean I had to train hard. Tiffany: But Cloud fought in the tag title match. Ultimate: That maybe, but he may have been injured, and i'd have to take his place. Wrestling in a four way is a hell of a lot different than one on one. I had to train for both just in case, and I paid the price, a price some people are glad I paid. Tiffany: Are you mentioning any names? Ultimate: No. Tiffany: But your opponent wrestled 2 matches as well. Ultimate: True, but he was focused on the TV title. I had that AND the tag team to focus on. There's only so much I can do. That weakness was exploited, and the title was taken from me. Tiffany: You said you don't mind if Cloud wins the TV title, where is he? Ultimate: He's off preparing for the match. It`s very important to him. In his eyes, I was dishonored, and he is fighting for MY honor, to regain the title that was unfairly taken. To right the wrong. He is going through great mental and physical preparation for the match. I believe Cloud will come back a MUCH stronger opponent. Tiffany: Strong enough to beat you? Ultimate: Anything is possible. Tiffany: Finally, given how angry you are, are we going to see a change in the way you fight? Ultimate: Yes you are. I`ve been focused, but restrained. From now on I'll just be focused. If I injure anybody, so be it. Anyone who stands in my way WILL face the consequences. I'm not taking **** fron ANYONE anymore. They can leave the ring voluntarily, on a stretcher, or in a bodybag, the choice is theirs. <Fade to black> =========================================================== Newark: WOW!!! It looks like Ulitmate Perversion is going amist a chance of character as well, as he feels he was robbed of his Television championship. Scoops: It sure does seem a lot of people are going through character changes as of late. Newark: Well, it could be an attitude change that brings him right back into some possession of some gold, here in the INWI, and Cloud Eagle could leave the arena Monday as the NEW Television champion as he does battle with Jason "The Crusher" Chandler. Riley: [yawing] yeah well we have some thugs....thugs 4 life to be in fact with some comments regarding their match against those wuss faces, The All Americans... Scoops: General Sam Adams is the ONLY All American.... ========================================================== Southside: Finally Finally Finally! Why? becasue they are negatively influencing our youth! And the Thugs for Life we love the kids. thats why All Americans you are NEXT! Look what you did to little Mikey.
