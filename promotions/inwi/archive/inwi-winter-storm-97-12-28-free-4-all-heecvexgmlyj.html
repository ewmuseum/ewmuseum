[INWI] Winter Storm '97, 12-28, Free 4 All

30 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The screen slowly fades from the "you have just ordered INWI's Winter Storm" screen to a totally black screen...And Method Man's "I Come to Bring The Pain" begins to lightly make it's way onto the audio, slowly increasing it's volume...A picture of Tommy Graves pinning Cherry months ago, following a vicious DDT. Then a shot of Jonathan Hammer "earning" the moniker as "INWI's Most Extreme Man" with several swift chair shots to various opponent's heads. Tony Houston and King Apples are seen in the ring Facing each other...then again, fighting together, as they execute a dreaded Spike Piledriver on Tommy Graves and Jonathan Hammer.] [Method Man fades and the screen focuses on Brian Newark, Scoop Ropes, and Riley Michaels who, despite having a match later is still dressed in a killer ensemble.] INWI Winter Storm Free 4 All!!! Brian Newark: Welcome ladies and gentlemen, as we at the INWI take on pay per view yet again. However, at this time let me introduce the gentlemen who will be joining me for the free for all, and will have a match later on tonight, Scoop Ropes and Riley Michaels. Riley Michaels: Ahh The pleasure is all yours Newark, and as for Asswhiper and the Bugs4Life they will receive a genuine ass kicking later on today.. Scoop: Ahh How right you are, and shoot, we may not even need our third partner.. Newark: And who would that be? Riley: Damnit Newark, if we didn't tell the reporters we sure the hell ain't gonna tell you. Newark: ahh You two are going to get it...But first, we have yet another of your fans...Malice. Riley: Yet another fool, who needed some snaps.. ============================================================= <As the show is about to begin Malice storms to the ring> Malice: Hey Scoop and Riley, I know your in the building... Get your asses down here and lets finish this now! <The crowd cheers, it becomes obvious that neither Scoop or Riley are coming down to the ring> Malice: Just as I figured, scared. But you see thats ok, You two wanna play the double team shit on me in my matches. Sign your name to the dotted line for a tag team match! There has to be someone in the locker room that hates you two as much as I do. And if there is, I welcome you to be my tag partner. BUT I want a Texas tornado/lumber jack match. Thats right everyone in the ring at once and people to surround the ring so no one gets out. Malice: Now I have here in my hands a front row ticket, I am going to have a seat and watch. If either one of you two even consider interfering in matches I will break you in half. <Malice drops the mic and climbs out of the ring. He hops the guard rail as he high fives some fans. He proceeds to sit down to watch the action.> ============================================================= Riley: MINE!!!! The AmerAcan Dre-eem!!!! What the ....? Newark: See another one of your "fans" and he will be in the front row to keep an eye on you. Scoop: Well when you are as popular as Riley and Myself, we have all the fans to watch our backs. But still in all with the gerthy polka wearing hog out there he'll have no problem with us leaving him along... Riley: Damn fool. So don't we have a match up next or something? Newark? Newark!! Do your job. Put down the damn tequila. Newark: Will you stop!!! You are right up next we have our free for all match, pitting newcomer Jim Whitten and the jWo ICON, Pig Pen. Riley: And we all now the legend status of the man they call Pig Pen, he is truly an ICON, a legend...he's great. ============================================================= FREE 4 ALL MATCH Jim Whitten vs Pig Pen (with Clam Chowder) Whitten in the ring and Pig Pen is just massive. Pig Pen charges and he is so slow that Whitten shuffles over and Pig Pen hits the buckles hard. Whitten with repeated blows to the back of the head and Pen falls to the mat. Whitten with a leg drop and he is waiting for the Pig Pen to get up and that could be a mistake. Pen is up and Whitten over and Pen with a low blow and a short arm clothesline and Whitten goes down. Pen into the ropes and he goes for a splash and Whitten moves and the Pig Pen hits the mat. Whitten is up and he is jabbing away. Rights and lefts to the jaw and a kidney shot and a kick to the stomach and a clothesline and another clothesline. Pen won't go down. A big dropkick by Whitten and Pen is sent into the ropes and Whitten with a high cross body and even the big man Whitten can't bring the Pen down and Whitten falls and flattens Whitten with a power drop. Pen sends Whitten into the ropes and puts him down with a tilt-a-whirl slam. Whitten is holding his back and that must be hurting. Whitten with a rollup to the Pen and Pen just sits down and he flattens Whitten. Whitten is out of it and the Pen ncovers Whitten, 1......2....and Whitten gets the right shoudler up. He picks Whitten up and he has him in a bear hug. He is wrenching it on and Whitten is losing both oxygen and consciousness. The ref is over and Whitten has blacked out. The ref picks up the arm of Whitten and drops it. He picks up the arm again and it drops again. He holds up the 2 sign. He picks up the arm one more time and it drop...no. Whitten has it up and he is shaking it and he spits in the eyes of Pen and he drops Whitten. Pen is trying to clear his eyes and Whitten with a right hand, and another one, and another one and Pen is rocked back and Whitten goes up top and he comes off the top rope and nails a flying clothesline and the Pen goes down. Whitten is over there next to Pen and there is the Facelift. There it is and the cover, 1.....2.....3.and Pen is done. Whitten comes back from an early attack by Pen to come back and win the match. Again the winner of the match in the time of 7:21, Jim Whitten. ============================================================ Newark: What a way to start off the latest installment of INWI on Pay Per View, with Jim Whitten taking out the jWo's ICON Pig Pen, a former Television champion. Riley: No kidding, this Whitten has conquered the jWo, but let's see how he can deal with real competition. I'm surprised we didn't see a Pensault. Or a FrankenPen. Scoop: Hahahahaha Whoa what's this...Hey Riley look!! Look.. Riley: YES!! It's the champ..Tony Houston is making his way towards the ring with a microphone in hand. Well then let's hear what he has to say. ============================================================= [Down the aisleway wearing a black Mecca jeans that are sagging slightly, a white Ringmaster championship T-Shirt and a black New York Yankees hat that is ducked to only barely reveal his chilling, blue eyes. Houston has the INWI Heavyweight Championship belt wrapped over his left shoulder.] [Tony Houston climbs into the ring and sits on top of the top turnbuckle as the crowd in the Corstates Spectrum immediately begin to drown the building with a steady encore of boos.] Houston: Shut the hell up. I gotta say some shiznit..and ya' fools are going to be lucky enough to listen. Graves, big boy...it's only a few short hours away..and no doubt the fear is beginning to set in. You now possess the fear that makes big man, such as yourself, to mere worthless challengers. You have drawn' the shortest draw there is..and punished with right to step into the ring with the man...The Ringmaster..THE Ringmaster...not a title I self proclaimed dun, something I went out an sweated for whooped everybody who dared stepped across the ring from me. Each week I revealed who'd rise the victor, and me being the honorable man that I am, I held true. [Crowd begins to boo and Houston simply turns and reveals that now infamous internation sign of discontent.] Houston: Anyways, Gravesy I'm going to give you the privelege of knowing...knowing the result of the match before it even happens, hell before the actual pay per view even happens...Graves yer sorry ass is going to get dropped on your neck. However if ya wanna dare and step to the plate anyway, so be it, but these marks are gonna be front row for a juicing worse than any Gallagher show could give you....I'm ouuttt. [Houston drops microphone and methodically makes his way back towards the lockeroom stopping periodically to instigate an arugment with a booing ringsider.] ============================================================= Newark: Some interesting comments from Tony Houston, claiming Tommy Graves fears him? Riley: Well Brother Houston does preach the truth on a regular basis Newark, so it MUST be true. Scoop: I hear you there Riley. When was the last time you ever heard The Brotherhood lie? There motto is, "Do unto others before they do unto you?" Newark: Exactly... Scoop: What? It's the golden rule... Newark: Well, in fact I don't believe that Graves fears Houston, I do believe it is the complete opposite. Riley: Who cares Newark, nobody pays $29.95 to hear what you think or believe, just takes us into the next interview. Newark: Next up we have Cloud Eagle, who will do battle against a former partner, Ultimate Perversion or Perversion... Riley: Hell yeah, Perversion has been revealing of late, an extremely vicious side, and even you have to admit Newark has been successful.. ============================================================= <Eagle is standing backstage. With him are the newly returned Trent VanDenBerg, and 3 bodyguards, all dressed in black. (Gee, can we say MIB?)> Tiffany: Cloud. This last couple of weeks has been, for want of a better word, WILD! Cloud: A little too wild if you ask me Tiffany. I do not know what I have done to Ultimate to deserve being stabbedin the back like this, but if that is the way he wishes to play it, then so be it. Tiffany: You, a lot of people say very unfairly, beat both Ultimate and Cherry to a pulp at the Press Conference. Any comments? Cloud: Given how Ultimate has treated me, he is lucky to be ALIVE. You see this. (Cloud rolls up his sleeve, pointing to a still healing scar.) Ultimate gave me that last week when he came to the ring. Do you think I should let that go unanswered? Tiffany: I guess not. Everyone was surprised to see you return Mister VanDenBerg. Why are you back? Trent: Well Tiff, I`ve had time off to consider my options. When Cloud left, I became introspective and tried to examine what it was I did wrong. I am trying to be a nicer person. When I saw Ultimate turning on Cloud, I called Cloud and said "Look, you're gonna need protection from that fat slob. What say we start afresh?" and took it from there. When we decided to start afresh, wipe the slate clean as it were, I hired some bodyguards for us, (gestures to the MIB behind him) since I have a feeling at least one person in the INWI will be rather upset to see me return. A certain person who has put me in hospital before. A certain person who doesn't know that the restraining order has been changed. He is still not allowed near me. I however... (He grins.) Can do whatever I please. It pays to have a brother who is a lawyer. Tiffany: Will you be going to ringside with Cloud tonight? Trent: Indeed, unless the INWI decide to ban everyone from ringside, and even then I have a few surprises. Tiffany: Trent, Cloud, thank you. (As the camera swings back to a view of the crowd, the camera picks out, in the front row, 2 men dressed exactly the same as the bodyguards. Could it be?...) ============================================================ Newark: Wow the return of Mr. VanDenBerg as well as the men in black..uh...damnit you know what I meant..And it appears as if Cloud Eagle is having a vicious swing in his attitude. Scoop: Well if he keeps it up, he could be on the verge of some success..but we'll first see how Perversion dismantles him later tonight. Riley: I'm thinking Perversion may drop Eagle on his head again..Newark, put the beer down and who's next? Newark: Well, Perversion is standing by .. Scoop: Let's got there then. ============================================================= <Ultimate is backstage before his match with Cloud Eagle. He has a cut above his VERY black eye, and small cut on his face, and appears to be walking with some stiffness.> Tiffany: Ultimate. How are you shaping up for tonight. Ultimate: (Speaking as if in pain, probably because he is.) I've been better. My friend and partner Cherry isn't as badly injured, but aside from the black eye, I have a couple of broken ribs. Tiffany: Do you think you should wrestle? Ultimate: Well, the doctors said no, but oddly, when I said I'd have to come back with Cherry, they changed their mind. It's nothing this little baby can't handle. (He whips out a syringe.) Tiffany: What's that? Ultimate: A very strong painkiller. I could get hit by a bus and still wrestle. (From off camera you can hear someone say "I wish you WOULD get hit by a bus, ********." Ultimates popularity is on the slide for sure.) Tiffany: Any thoughts on tonights match? Ultimate: Well, I've been told that Cloud has declared war on me, or even possibly the entire INWI. All I can say is war is war. If he wants a war, he'll get one. Now if you don't mind Tiffany, I'd like to go and sit down before my match, and say hello to Mister Needle here. Tiffany: Sure. Ultimate, thank you very much. ============================================================ Newark: [baffled] Wow, Perversion is having some real problems...I apologize for that display, we here at the INWI are strongly against drugs or any kind... Riley: So Newark, you wanna go drinking again? Is it me or does Tiffany Love get around more often than a ho' on a carosel. Love come interview me, step up to the mike. Scoop: Hahahahahahaha Well apparently next up we have a INWI newcomer, who sent in some pre-recorded comments following last weeks Hot Spot. What's his name? Newark: The Franchise Player Mike Hickdens. Riley: <HICK-CUP> ============================================================ {The Franchise Player Mike Hickdens theme comes on. HE comes out of the ring.. He hesitates a while, seeing almost no fans are there in the arena, after Monday Night Hotspot. Some still there, when they heard The Franchise Players theme. After all that he stops hesitating, then walks to the ring arrogantly. Fans starts to throws coins at him, some people threw soft drinks. Hickden makes his way to the ring, going to the ropes. Hickden starts to take his silver robe off near Miss Love. Love looks a little blushed. Hundreds of people that are still in the arena, starts to yell out "WOOOOOO"..} Tiffany Love: Ladies and Gentleman "The Franchise Player'' Mike Hickden.. {The crowd may some mix reactions.} Well anyway lets start with the question. Well i know how you feel about the "Brotherhood'', how do you feel about them grabbing more gold, are they dominant as ever, now?? The Franchise Player Mike Hickden: HAHAHAHAHAHA.. You know i'll give credit to them, this is probably the first title they won, that they never cheated it for. But on my side they did face "The Comforters", need i say more. Just telling you there Tag Team name, give an imagination of what there about. Now about them being dominant, i don't know, i know there a very extremely stable, but i mean, all the titles they won was not fair, we can all say that.. I would say there a very strong stable, but thats really it. Miss Love you might considered them dominant, i think otherwise. Tiffany Love: I see.. You have asked Mike Garvey, to be a "Allie", he has still to reply.. I guess there is your answer.. The Franchise Player Mike Hickden: I don't think so, maybe "The Gavel" did not here about my request.. Really how can he turn down an offer like this.. I know he wants my services, and vice versa.. He probably miss the "Inside the ropes'' segment last Monday. That is it i guess. Tiffany Love: From inside sources, i have heard you might have an impact on the Pay Per View that is this weekend.. Is any portion of this true at all?? The Franchise Player Mike Hickden: Whoever told you this has a big mouth, but whoever told you this, knows what there talking about too.. You can bet something will happen, in a "Very Bad Way" for the BrotherHood, and wrestlers, who get in my way, or wrestlers who i want to destroy here, just because i feel like it. Love i'll be there you can count on it. Tiffany Love: Ladies and Gentleman The Franchise Player Mike Hickden, {A few fans that was still there, cheered.. Hickden goes out of the ring, then he comes back to the ring, and took something out from his robe.. He gives a gift to Tyfanny Love, she opens it, it was a Rolex GOld Watch.. Franchise Player yells out Happy Christmas Tiffany, this is something for you, thanks for promoting me here in INWI.. They both hug..} [Screen Fades to White] ============================================================= [Scoop and Riley have shocked looks on their faces] Newark: Whoa!! Some bold comments from a new comer to the INWI, claiming that The Brotherhood are good, but not elite. Riley: Well, somebody best inform this little boy that you do not come in immediately and to smack to The Brotherhood, for you will discover quickly how awful the sound of your own neck snapping following a Spike. Scoop: Who's he to worry about how The Brotherhood won their belts, but since he wants to make note of it, Tony Houston beat Springer cleanly in a Ladder Match to capture the INWI Heayvweight Champion, "King" Apples cleanly defeated Mr. Ed Rogers in the bathroom in a Falls Count Anywhere match, and James Pillow and Vinny Vitrone have won the tag belts by cleanly beating The Comfortes. Riley: So you are saying this guy is ignorant. Scoop: No question about it. YES!! Next up we have a Brother, The Vindicator Vinny Vitrone, Shut up Newark, take it away Vinny. ============================================================= [The camera once again begins to fade up into Vinny Vitrone's well, are you not surprised? His lockerroom! Does this guy live in there? Anyways. The INWI officials decided to put an INWI camera inside the arena the night before the PPV. this should be interesting -=oP as the most enthuisastic member of the one and only Brotherhood is about to get snagged. "The Vindicator" is jamming back and forth in his recliner. Listening to the beat of "FLY" by Sugar Ray. Vitrone tries although, unsuccessfully to sing along. In the background you see a couple of drunk up "Jack Daniels" bottles on the floor as well as a spilt bucket of KFC chicken. This isn't the N.E.W, and there is no Playboy Bobby Davis, so yes there is only ONE bucket of chicken, not ten. Vitrone smiles and begins to get out of his chair. He's now dancing along with the song. "Iiii'm gonna kick Bledsoe's ass. Gonna kick his ass, really kick ass worse than his mother's , yea worse than his mother's" "I'm gonna whip him, whip him like Houston does to Graves, whip him like Apples does to Hammer, " "Than it's time for our gold, yea, than it's time for our gold". [Vitrone continues signing to the beat] "Me n' Pillowwwww are guh-guh- gonna whip some ass, Guh- guh-guh gonna whip some ass". [Vitrone than stops and notices the camera under his table. He's puzzled but than he rips it from under his sockets. He apparently has something else to say:] [One Half of the TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS. "The Real Total Package". "The STUFF" "The Master O' Revelation" "The LiL Brutta'" "The Man of 1,000 Names" He is ... ... ... "THE VINDICATOR" Vinny Vitrone] Ahhh. so you thought you can defeat me, huh? INWI, nice try. I'm the MA- err, wait a minute, one of bros might be around here n' my head don't feel good enough to get smacked, I'M THE L'IL BRO!! Come the PPV, Comforters, you're gonna find it out again!!
