[INWI] In The Studio, 6-12

12 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
INWI - IN THE STUDIO June 12,1997 INWI Studios, Delhi, NY ============================================================ <Standard news anchor in a studio setting> Anchor: This is Channel 5 Sports Action News for Tuesday the Tenth of June. The INWI were in town last night, and things didn't end to well for one of the wrestlers involved. <Anchor replaced by silent footage of Ultimate Perversion being interviewed> The wrestler Ultimate Perversion was arrested early this morning by police. The charge? Alleged destruction of his hotel room after failing to win his title match. <Footage changes to footage of very trashed hotel room.> Perversion is considered one of the few wrestlers in the INWI who deserves a title but doesn't have one. A few insiders who didn't want to be named think he is the only person capable of beating Brody Thunder. Maybe one day we'll get to see if that's true. Perversion recently had a very public breakup with his manager, Trent VanDenBerg, and the hostility between them flared again recently when Trent attacked Perversion at a press conference. <Footage changes to show Trent attacking Perversion at said conference.> Perversion was unavailable for comment today, but sources close to the wrestler say that he regrets the incident. The hotel decided not to prosecute when Perversion said he would pay for all damages out of his own pocket. We approached Cloud Eagle, who still wrestles for Mister VanDenBerg, but he refused to comment, saying only that he wasn't surprised... ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well, it seems as though Ultimate Perversion has a little bit of a temper and he is really starting to get angry about losing these title matches. I don't think that his ex-manager helps and definitley not the fact that Tony Houston is now in the picture. Well people, it is time for IN THE STUDIO and I am your host Brian Newark! What a card we have for you tonight here in Delhi! Two title matches as once again Ultimate Perversion gets a shot at gold as he will take on Mr. Apples for the TV Championship! Mr. Ed Rogers will defend his Northeast Title against Dell Pickle of the jWo and we also will have the debut of Sam "The Minuteman" Adams! The Rizzotto Brothers are going to try and push their differences aside tonight as they take on The Comforters, who make their INWI Return tonight! All this and much more, so let's head up to the ring for our opening match of the evening! Before we enter the ring though, let's get comments from one of the newest to enter the INWI, Sam "The Minuteman" Adams. ============================================================ (Tiffany Love stands outside a dressing room, which has a door painted red, white, and blue. Right away you think that this will be another "I love America" loser dork wrestler. But, alas, you are wrong. She knocks.) Tiffany: Hello? Mr. Adams? This is Tiffany Love of the INWI. I need to interview you. Voice: Wait one freak'n second, toots. (The door opens abruptly and a muscular figure steps out. He is dressed in red, white, and blue from his bandana to his wrestling boots. Don't worry, though, he is also wearing red, white and blue boxer shorts as well. His long, greased black hair reminds you of either Bret Hart or Howard Stern, you can't decide and neither can this author.) Sam "The Minuteman" Adams: What the heck do you want, sugar? Tiffany: I will ignore that sexist remark, thank you. Now, what brings you into the INWI? Sam Adams: My wallet brings me into the INWI! Frankly, babe, my collection of dead presidents is getting kind of thin. And I live to love the long gone heros of this nation. (He salutes into the sky.) Tiffany: Uh, yes. And what are your plans in the INWI. Sam Adams: To deport all the dang communists that interfere with the future of the USA. They are all over the place, just like my hero, Joe McCarthy, said. This means you, Lone Wolf! And you Mr. Tutti-Fruiti Apples! Just wait until the All American Dream shoves your Socialist Manifestos up your red behinds! And when an All-American puts something up your behind, you really feel it! Ain't that right, doll? Tiffany: Mr. Adams! Sam Adams: Why don't you call me what you called me last night? Or have you sold out to the Commies? Tiffany: This is an outrage! (Tiffany Love storms out of site. Sam Adams looks directly into the camera.) Sam Adams: You commies better start running, right this second. Cause, I'm gonna take your Russain Vadka, your Cuban Cigars, your Chinese Take-Out and I'm gonna shove it down your red throats'. Nuff said. ============================================================ Match 1 - Debut Match Sam "The Minuteman" Adams vs "The Knuckle" Jimmy Chooley Well the new talent keeps rolling in for the INWi and the champions have to watch their backs with this. You never know as these rookies enter the INWI who could be a future champion here! Sam "The Minuteman" Adams is the latest entry in the INWI and he is an interesting one to say the least. The Minuteman doesn't have an easy one here, but let's see how he performs in the ring. Jimmy Chooley attacks right away and takes Adams down with a slide tackle on the legs. Adams quickly back up to his feet and he short arm lariats Chooley to the mat and then he is on him like a rabid dog! Adams is pounding on Chooley, lifts him up and sends him into the ropes and grabs him and nails a side suplex! He picks up Chooley and nails a running powerslam on him, then sets him up on the top turnbuckle facing the fans. Adams on the top rope...is this a reverse DDT from the top?? Adams leaps and does a flip....he lands on his backside and Chooley lands squarely on his melon! OUCH!! Adams with the cover, 1...2...3!! It's over! Your winner in his debut match in 8:21, Sam "The Minuteman" Adams!!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Next up is the Northeast Championship match. We have both comments from the champion and the challenger! ============================================================ (Dell Pickle in the back before his match for the Northeast Championship. He is dresswed in his pink jWo outfit and ready to go.) Pickle - Man people, this is it. This is the night the jWo shines. We will finally get gold in the jWo after I get done with old nut boy. I am ready for whatever they can send me and I have taken my lumps. I have done all the INWI has asked, now it is my turn to win. The jWo is a great organization and we will soon have ALL the gold in the INWI. Tonight it starts with Rogers. Then we will get Thunder, Apples, P.O. Box, Bill Bob Whatever his name is, The Friendship Express and The Terminators. We will WIN all the gold and it all starts with me tonight. NOW, get out of my face so I can get ready. ============================================================ (Before his match, the camera cuts to pre-recorded footage. It's Mr. Ed Rogers, and Smelly in their locker room. Ed has less of a vacant look on his face then usual. In fact he looks happy.) Mr. Ed Rogers: Hello televison neighbors! It's your friendly friend and king and master: Ed Rogers. It seems lately that I've been winning matches that maybe I shouldn't. Smelly: Got that right, moron! Infact, I'm po'ed at how much you've sucked lately! Your fired! Mr. Ed Rogers: What? Smelly: Your fired! Mr. Ed Rogers: (crying) Please don't fire me! I promise I'll win, and without help! Smelly: Well, I'll give you one more chance! If you don't beat Dell Pickle and beat him good, I'm leaveing you! (The camera fades out.) ============================================================ Match 2 - Northeast Championship Mr. Ed Rogers vs Dell Pickle of the jWo This one should be real interesting...or maybe not. Dell Pickle, of the jWo, has never won in the INWI. My question is this, how the hell did he land a title match? I guess Ed Rogers just wanted an easy match and he needs a win to keep his manager Smelly. It is beyond me how Rogers could get fired by his own sock puppet, but hey....this guy is many fries short of a happy meal! Well, the two are in the ring and there's the bell and we are underway. Rogers jumps Pickle and sticks Smelly into the face of Pickle and laughs while doing it. That right there could knock Pickle out! Pickle scooped up by Rogers, Suplex! Rogers just kicked at Pickles manager, Oyster Boy. Rogers up with Pickle, sends him into the ropes and goes for the back body drop....NO! Pickle puts on the brakes and kicks Rogers in the face! I think that is the first offensive move we have ever seen Dell Pickle do! Pickle scoops up Rogers, sends him into the ropes and nails a drop kick. Pickle is on the offense! Pickle drops an elbow and goes for a cover, 1...2...NO!! Dell Pickle almost got a win and a title at the same time. Man, that would of been sick! Pickle again up with Rogers and he slams him to the mat. Rogers back onhis feet and grabs Pickle. Pickle is stunned!! Rogers just chokeslammed Pickle. 1...2..NO! Pickle gets a shoulder up! Rogers back up with Pickle and sends him into the ropes and Pickle runs over the ref. Pickle is down, Rogers scoops him up...the Puppet Master!!! Oh man, he's got Pickle pinner and the ref is out. uh oh, here comes a bunch of the jWo and Rogers sees them. There's Jerrid Hoss and Rogers sends him into the first row! Pig Pen is sent out of the ring and there goes Brian Johnson!! Lookout! Malvolio just nailed Rogers with a chair and John Q. Denver hit him with one as well! There's like six members of the jWo in the ring and they are beating on Rogers! They all scatter as the ref starts to move and Malvolio tosses Pickle on top of Rogers. 1....2....3!! OH MY GOD!! Are you kidding me??? This is the biggest bunch of crap I have ever seen. I may not like Ed Rogers, but what happened here is bull. Your winner in 14:31 and NEW INWI Northeast Champion, Dell Pickle!!!!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Before our next match, we have comments from The Rizzotto Brothers. These two have had trouble with eachother lately and I hope they can get along for this one as they are facing one of the toughest teams in the INWI, The Comforters in their return match! ============================================================ Gino: Well, it seems the Comforters are back. You guys have chickened out before, and after I'm through with you, you'll wish you'd stayed at home. No one can deliver a Mob Drop like I can, and once you feel it, you'll be in severe disCOMFORT. Vito: What are you talking about man. These guys had contractual obligations elsewhere and now that they're back, they're gonna want to prove themselves. If anything, we should respect these guys. Gino: I only respect 2 people in this world and you're not one of them. Vito: What, are you saying I'm not good enough to be your partner. Gino: No, I'm just saying I'm better then you. Vito: Maybe I wouldn't be so bad if you stopped making me lose all my matches all the time. I'll be taking notes from the apron Thursday night. Gino: Well you'd better bring a BIG pad. ============================================================ Match 3 The Rizzotto Brothers vs The Comforters The Comforters have returned to the INWI! After Mattress has healed all his wounds, they are ready for action and are looking for gold here in the INWI! The tag championships have bounced around a little lately, so you never know! The Rizzotto Brothers, who just don't seem to get along, are trying to work out their differences in thismatch. I guess only time will tell what happens between these two. It is going to be Vito starting out, no Gino...no, what in the world...these two are pushing at eachother already. This match should be interesting. Well, Vito finally sends Gino out of the ring and it'll be Vito and Box Spring starting things off in this match. The two tie up in the center of the ring and Box Spring takes control with a headlock. Vito sends him into the ropes but Box Spring clotheslines Vito on the way by! Box Spring grabs Vito and slams him to the mat. Gino charges in and Box Spring catches him with a clothesline and both Rizzotto's are on the outside and back at arguing! Vito rolls back in the ring and tags Gino. Box Spring tags Mattress and we'll get to see how he looks and if there's any ring rust. Matress charges at Gino and sends him to the mat. Mattress off the ropes, over Gino and Gino for a back body drop....NO! Mattress stops and nails a DDT on Gino and Vito is going nuts on the outside. Mattress picks up Gino and sends him into the ropes, big boot!! Gino over the top rope to the floor! Vito out and slaps Gino trying to get his attention. There's more pushing and Gino rolls into the ring. He tags Vito and the two are yelling at eachother. Gino pushes Vito and Vito points at the Comforters as if he is saying take care of business and we'll take care of eachother later. Vito turns his back to Gino and heads towards Mattress. WAIT!! Gino grabs Vito and nails a Mob Drop!!! He scoops him up and hits a second Mob Drop!! OH MY GOD!! He flips off Vito and then flips off the Comforters and leaves the ring and heads to the back. Mattress drags Vito to the middle of the ring and they set him up...there's the Sealy Posturepedic! Mattress with the cover, 1...2...3!!! Your winners in 12:45 in their return match, The Comforters!!!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Before our main event between Mr. Apples and Ultimate Perversion, we have comments to play from you from "Marvelous" Mike Quick. From what I understand it's a challenge. Let's see what he has to say. We also have some quick comments from Ultimate Perversion, who is backstage. ============================================================ (Fade in to a training facility where Tiffany Love is standing waiting to interview Mike Quick) Tiffany Love: Well I am here with one of the INWI's superstars Mike Quick and he looks angry. Mike Quick: Your damn right. I am angry! I am sick and tired of this shit. Tiffany Love: What are you talking about Mike? Mike Quick: Tiffany you know damn well what I am talking about. I'm talking about this little weasle name Bill Bob. This is some kind of wannabe COWBOY. He also has his little friend running around trying to be me with the crowbar. I got one thing to say you Bill Bob. I want you in a steel cage match where noone can interfere for your title. Just me and you at the upcoming Pay-Per-View! I'm gonna laugh in your face after I kick your ass. And what if you don't accept? Well that means you are a coward and you are afraid to get into the same ring with me. And after the match is over I am gonna be wearing your title and laughing in your face. You cannot defeat the QUICKEST man in the world Bill Bob! Tiffany Love: Now this is an interesting situation. We'll just have to see whether Bill Bob accepts this match. ============================================================ <Backstage area. Ultimate looks VERY angry.> Tiffany: Ultimate, tell us about your arrest. Ultimate: I`d rather not talk about it. Tiffany: OK, what about Tony Houston. Ultimate: What about him? Tiffany: You and him seem to be feuding at the moment. Ultimate: Yeah, so what? Tiffany: He appears to be making veiled threats toward you, and the fact that you are, as he so eloquently puts it, posse-less. Ultimate: I don't need a posse to deal with losers like them. I have friends in the INWI. Houston is the one who should watch out. He talks the talk, but does he walk the walk. I'll meet him anytime he wants, in any match. Just me and him, and none of those idiots he hangs with. A fair fight, him and me. We'll see who the real man is, and who is the child. Tiffany: One more thing... <Ultimate pushes her mike out of the way.> Ultimate: I`ve had enough, I`m outa here. Tiffany: Er... Well, there you have it. ============================================================ MAIN EVENT Match 4 - Televison Championship Mr. Apples vs Ultimate Perversion This is the match we have been waiting for! This could be a great one and we will soon see what is going to happen! Ultimate Perversion is really craving gold and can't seem to get it done. Some say it's because of Trent VenDenberg. Others say it is because he has horrible luck. Nonetheless, he gets his chance at the TV Strap tonight and he is currently in the ring. The crowd is going crazy as Mr. Apples comes through the entrance and heads towards the ring. Wait a minute....there's that masked man coming up behind Apples. Mr. Apples doesn't see him! Turn around Apples!! Ow!! Apples just got nailed with a chair and this masked man is doing a number on Apples. He grabs Apples and nails a DDT on the concrete floor! Apples has to be hurting bad and he has a TV Title match. Perversion in the ring leaning on the ropes like he is going to have an easy match tonight. Maybe Perversion will finally get his title but this isn't the way to get it! The Masked guy up with Apples and slams his back into a guard rail. LOOK OUT!! Tony Houston is behind Perversion and he nails him with a chair!! Perversion crumples to the mat and Houston is all over him! Houston up with Perversion, Cold Drop!! He scoops up Preversion and slams him as "Bulldozer" Tony Riley comes in. Must be Houston and Riley came through the crowd because I didn't see them. Riley sends Perversion into the ropes and Riley gives him the big boot. Then Riley drops a leg and Perversion looks out cold!! Here comes Commissioner Jerry DeLuise and The Masked Man takes off through the crowd and he heads to the ring where Houston and Riley are still pounding on Perversion. DeLuis gets the mic and let's see what he has to say: "Because of the crap surrounding this match, this match is canceled. Furthermore Tony Houston, you and your partner better head to the back before I fine both of you." DeLuise drops the mic and heads over to the ring announcer to tell him what's going on. Looks like this is it for tonight and we are just about out of time! Wait a minute Houston just grabbed DeLuise....Cold Drop!! Oh my god, Houston just Cold Dropped the Commissioner! There will be some big fines on that one!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - We are out of time ladies and Gentlemen....we'll keep you posted on everything. For The INWI, I am Brian Newark saying so long!! ============================================================ =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The INWI "The revolutionary force in E-Wrestling" Visit us on the Internet: http://www.catskill.net/harmer For info, E-mail: [detail withheld] INWIŽ, May 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
