INWI Card Archive

INWI Syracuse 3/18 Card

22 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
INWI - "We are here and we're not taking any prisoners!!" Syracuse, NY... The Onondaga War Memorial. Brian Newark - Welcome ladies and gentlemen to our first ever card as the INWI. We will be bringing you some great matches tonight, so hold onto your seat and get ready for WAR!!! Match 1 - Northeast TV Title Tourn. Bill Bob Wojicowski vs Jerrid Hoss Wojicowski dominated this match from beginning to end and never had to worry about the younger Hoss. In the end Bill Bob delivered his "Spur," A heel stomp to the head from the top rope and claimed the victory at the 3:23 mark. Match 2 - Northeast Title Tourn. "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder vs Dell Pickle Dell Pickle, an up and coming star went right after the experianced Thunder and looked like he was going to maybe pull up an upset. Thunder then started to whip on Pickle and was just having a fun time. Pickle surprised Thunder with a series of near falls... but that just got the man from Tombstone Arizona mad. Thunder used a series of clotheslines to set up his "Thunderbolt," which is a torture rack into a shoulderbreaker... I don't think Pickle will be getting up to soon. Your winner in 8:41... "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder. Match 3 - Northeast Title Tournament "Hubba Hubba Jonathan Sunset vs Psycho Sal Sal went and attacked Sunset before the bell and really worked him over. Sal dominated all but one minute of this match. Unfortunatley for Sal, that one minute cast him the match. Sal was beating on the high flyer and had him pinned on several occassions. Sal decided he could play the crowd but little did he know, Sunset was back up. A couple of near falls frustrated Sal and Sunset scored the pin at 8:09 with a high cross body block. Match 4 - Northeast Title Tournament Lance Draven vs Ice In one of the most exciting matches on the card, Lance Draven came away with a victory over the powerhouse Ice. Ice, managed by Death, held the upper hand throughout the first part of the match. However, Draven started working on the upperbody and head area of Ice. Death tried to interfere, but Draven executed a double noggin knocker on the two. As Ice stumbled backwords, Draven nailed him with the Silencer (a reverse DDT) to claim the win at 11:31. Match 5 - Northeast Title Tournament Ultimate Perversion vs Cloud Eagle Cloud Eagle, the obvious fan favorite, was on his way to a victory, but with the help of manager Trent VanDenBerg, Ultimate Perversion figured a way to win. Eagle, who was using the crowd to pump him up may of let them consider himself invinceable. Eagle went out of the ring to get Perversion, but VanDenBerg cracked him with a chair. The Ref didn't see it as Perversion had him tied up in a corner. After getting rolled back into the ring, Perversion put the Climatic Surprise on Eagle. This most unusal started when Perversion threw Eagle into the ropes then clotheslined him. It ended when he did a pelvic thrust into Eagles face. Definitley unorthodox. Winner... Ultimate Perversion in 9:54. Match 6 - Northeast Title Tournament Mr. Cherry vs Fallen Angel A battle between two fan favorites showed alot of sportsmanship and great action. Both wrestlers controlled parts of this match and showed some high flying as well. Mr. Cherry, who showed to be a major risk taker, used the top rope and alot of chance takers all through out the match. Fallen Angel, who has turned on his sinful ways, took a big advantage late in the match and scored a series of near falls. In the end, Mr. Cherry superplexed Angel, then delivered his Cherry Bomb (a Moonsault) for the pin and the victory at 15:25. Match 7 - Heavyweight Title Tournament Mr. Banana vs Oz The second member of the Brotherhood of Sour Grapes came out and pulled out a victory in a tournament match, but by no means was it easy. OZ, hailing from the Emerald City, and having a slight size advantage, strted out by overpowering the man clad in all yellow. Banana kicked out just before three after a power slam just minutes into the match. The experiance of Oz showed in this match, but the resistence of Banana was also there. At about the 12 minute mark, Banana, after working on Oz's back for several minutes, hoisted the big man up into the Banana Split (a torture rack.) At 12:23, Oz gave the word and Banana advanced to the second round. Brian Newark - Well, it seems like we got ourselves a promo of sorts. We just recieved this tape from the camp of "The Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder, so we figured we'd play irt up and see what he's all about. Thunder was victorius earlier this evening in the Northeast TV Title Tournament, first round.... role the tape boys... -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------------ The camera opens on a lone figure standing atop a butte looking out over the land. The wind wipes his black duster like a flag in the breeze. The sound of rolling thunder can be heard as black clouds quickly roll across the sky.Lightning flashes and a low raspy voice can be heard over the noise.] "Well, well, well. I'll bet the Devil must be in shock about now. Ya see folks....Hell just froze over. That's what your precious INWI Commissioner said would happen before they'd let the me wrestle here.Well guess what,boys... I'm here. [The camera whirls around to the front of the man revealing the visage of "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder. The camera tightens in on his face.] I'm here to tell you people and all those so-called superstars of the INWI that life as you know it just got a whole lot tougher. M'name is Brody Thunder. I'm 6' 2'...267 lbs of all fury and no worries. I was born and raised in the "Town Too Tough to Die"... ...Tombstone, Arizona. You don't grow up in Tombstone to become no corporate pup- pet or bleedin'heart liberal pantywaist.No sir,you grow up fast to realize that life ain't fair and you gotta take what you want cuz God doesn't give a man a mark in life... you gotta make your own. [He lights up a cigar.] Now I ain't here to talk.I ain't here to dance and I damn sure didn't come here 'cause I like the weather.I came here for two reasons and two reasons only.... MONEY and TITLES. And how do I get them? By winning matches...plain and simple. All I ever hear,no matter where I go is "the INWI is gonna be great." or "the INWI is where the best talent's goin' to".But when I hear that the INWI is gonna be the TOUGHEST... [Camera gets a tight facial closeup.A sly grin comes across his face.) ...well,that's when ol'Lone Wolf takes notice. I look at the Contenders,pretenders an'deadenders ya got here in the INWI and I figure my waist won't be bare for too long. Let's see......ya got some guy who calls himself, what was it again... ..."P O Box" . What the hell kinda handle's that?! Well son, if ya hook 'em up with me yer gonna give "Return To Sender'a whole new meanin'. Plain an' simple. [Thunder lights up his trademark cigar.] Then ya got some jabrony who calls himself "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset. Another fancy-dan pretty-boy who needs ta get his jaw jacked just fer thinkin' he's a wrestler. Now don't get me wrong. I understand that I can't just come into the INWI and demand a title be given ta me. I realize I've gotta stomp a few mudholes in some folks to get some attention. I don't have a problem with proving myself...hell,it's a hobby of mine. [Thunder watches the smoke swirl around almost admiring it.] What I AM saying is that when my shot comes, I'll drag my opponent through Hell and back to get that 10lbs of gold, make no mistake about it. No one, man nor beast, ever got up from the Thunderbolt and I'm bettin' Superman don't wrestle here. Too bad....cuz he's about the only one who'd have a chance. An' that goes for the rest of you so-called "superstars" of INWI. You've all been given notice. The Thunder's comin' and there's not a thing anyone can do about it. Line 'em up Mr Commissioner. [An evil grin returns to Thunder's face.] Hell's comin' to the INWI...an' it's name is Brody Thunder. "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder. The "Terror from Tombstone"."The Arizona Assassin". Call me what- ever you want,but before too long y'all will have to call me by my rightful title..... ...CHAMPION. It's like my pappy always said..."I ain't a hard man ta find... [The grin leaves Thunder's face suddenly.] ...I'm just a hard man ta beat!" [Videotape fades to black as a loud clap of thunder roars and slowly fades.] -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------------ Brian Newark - Well I guess this guy think he has the INWI figured out and from what he looked like earlier, he just may. Lets go back up to the ring for more action!!! Match 8 - Tag Team Title Tournament The Comforters vs Contrast This should be an interesting one. The Comforters, Mattress and Box Spring are two of three triplets here in the INWI. Contrast, who consist of Black and White are some mean guys managed be Mean Eugene and part of the Kings of Destruction. The match started out with some quick tags and each team gaining a little momentum. Then Corntrast started to take over, with the help of Mean Eugene, the team started rolling all over The Comforters. The Comforters found a way to get in the match though. Being part of triplets, they did the old switch and Contrast didn't catch it. From there, the Comforters double teamed White and got him down in the middle of the ring and performed their "Seely Posturepedic," whick is a double somersault off the top rope onto White. They got the pin just before Black could come in andf Break it up. Your winners at 17:23, The Comforters. Match 9 - Tag Team Title Tournament The Freaks vs Penny and Hill This team definitley fits their name. The Freaks look like something out of a bad movie, but they can perform in the ring. Penny and Hill, who got a rousing cheer from the crowd when they came out stayed with the Freaks, but could never get the total upper hand. Freak O'Nature took Penny towards the end of the match and threw him outside the ring and followed him out there. From there he pummelled him into the ring posts and knocked him out. Back in the ring, Weiland set up Hill with a powerslam. Freak scooped up Hill and Powerbombed him. Weiland then came off the top rope with a leg drop to complete the "Freakbomb." Your winners in 13:02, the FREAKS!! Match 10 - Heavyweight Title Tournament "Hardcore" John Stillwater vs Stephen Casper Casper started out this match quick and started with some basic moves to throw Stillwater off. Hardcore then showed what he was really like. He ripped off his INW F'N I shirt (which this reporter had no clue they had!!) And opened a can off whoop ass. HHardcore just sarted to pummel Casper with a chair and was on the verge of being DQ'd when Casper rolled him up for a two count and almost a huge upset. After a chokeslam, Stillwater picked up Casper and slapped the DDT on him for the 1-2-3 and the win. Your winner in 12:01... "Hardcore" John Stillwater. Brian Newark - Before our next match, lets go back to the locker room where we have an upset Jimmy Cougar. Cougar, who has held his share of titles, is not happy with the way these tornamnets were set up. Not only that, with two cards at two different places tonight, Cougar and his partner "Hollywood Kid" Jason Starr are in two seperate locations!! Let's go to the interview... -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------------ Jimmy Cougar is pacing back and forth, obviously very upset about something. Craig Kelly enters the room with a microphone. Craig Kelly: Double J, what's the matter? You're obviously very upset about something! What is it? Jimmy Cougar: Get out of my face, Kelly! I'm not happy right now! Craig Kelly: That's obvious, Jimmy, but your fans want to know what's going on! Jimmy Cougar: You want to know what's going on? I'll TELL you what's going on! When I signed with this fed, I was promised that the Glamour Boys were going to be pushed and pushed as the biggest stars in this rinky dink promotion! Craig Kelly: But Jimmy, you ARE in the Heavyweight title tournament! Jimmy Cougar: Big freaking deal! I shouldn't even have to fight in the tourney! They should just GIVE the belt to me or the Hollywood Kid! Craig Kelly: But, the fans would be cheated out of a tournament and some very exciting matches then, Jimmy! Jimmy Cougar: Cheated, my ass! Now, they get to watch me wipe the floor with some jobber for 5 minutes! Lucky them! And why the hell is Jason Starr in the US title tourney? He should be right alongside me in the Heavyweight title tourney? Craig Kelly: I don't know, Double J! I'm not the commissioner! Jimmy Cougar: Well, you find that decrepid old fossil and when you do, also ask him why the Glamour Boys aren't fighting for the tag team titles!!! If he's so damn concerned about giving the fans their money's worth, then maybe he should have thought of that! This league had better shape up, or I may just ship out! Craig Kelly: Any words for Apocolypse? Jimmy Cougar: Who? Craig Kelly: Your opponent this weekend! Jimmy Cougar: I don't have time to worry about some worthless jobber! I got a title to be fitted for! I'll talk to you later! It's good to be great!!! -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------------ Brian Newark - Well, I for one think this guy better put up or shut up!! He has the arrogance to be a champion, that's for sure.... but does he have what it takes to make it in the INWI?? We'll see as we go back up to the ring... Match 11 - Heavyweight Title Tournament Jimmy Cougar vs Apocalypse This was barely a tune up for Jimmy Cougar as he showed his vetrean style and ring savvy. Apocalypse had a brief stint where he had a couple near falls on Cougar, but that didn't seem to faze the arrogant Cougar.... he actually laughed it off!! I can't believe this guys arrogance! Apocalypse fires cougar into the ropes and goes for a back body drop.... NO!! and inverted neck breaker.... uh oh, here it is... It's the "Cougar Clutch"... and Apocalypse is going to sleep... there the raising of the arm... 1-2-3. It's over... How the hell did he put him to sleep with that basic move? I think INWI President Jerry DeLuise will have to look into this one. Your winner at 9:27 - Jimmy Cougar!! Match 12 - Tag Team Title Tournament Solid Gold vs The Hellraisers An all out brawl started right off the bat with these two heel teams as Bradley and Blake gold took on Damian and Cobra. The managers on the outside even got into a shoving match with eachother, but being smart, Jimmy Stardom ran away from the Hellraisers manager Death. Back in the ring Blake and Bradley were using quick tags to wear down Cobra. I feel for this ref... he has his hands full! The corners emptied many times throughout this match... and that spelled doom for the Hellraisers. Death climbed on the apron looking to help, but Jimmy Stardom nailed him with the ring bell!! Stardom then got chased to the back by Damian... but while that was happening, Bradley delivered a belly to back suplex and Blake superkicked Cobra on the way up for the "Solid Gold Eliminator!!" By the time Damian reached the ring it was to late as Solid Gold advanced with a victory at 14:39. Brian Newark - Before our final bout... we have one last interview. This was taped earlier this afternoon as Tiffany Love visited with Senator Bradley. I guess he was ''voted'' into his position as the so called leader of his group, but let's roll the tape and see what's on the Senator's mind.... -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------------ Tiffany Love: "Hello, this is Tiffany Love reporting to you from the State of the Bradley Address. As most of you know, he is gunning for the heavyweight title and he is....oh wait, here comes the senator." Senator: "Hello my fellow wrestling fans, I am here not to waste your tax dollars, not to pull some kind of government scam, and I'm not even here to brag about my supermodel good looks...even though that's what I usually do. I am here to win your support as I enter this heavyweight tournament! Are there any questions from the press?" Tiffany Love: "Yes, I have a question. What do you plan to do if you win the heavyweight title?" Senator: "I'm glad you asked me that question. Oh by the way, you look absolutely beautiful tonight Miss Love. *wink* As I was saying, I'm glad you asked me that. I have a solid championship plan. First of all, after I win the title, I will reign as a fair and honest champion. My fellow stable members will look to me as an example, and they will win their titles also. The second part of my plan is that if anyone dares to challenge me to a title shot, then I will give it to them if I see that they're worthy enough. Now, are there any more questions before I close this conference?" Tiffany Love: "Yes, I have one more question. What do you have to say to your opponents as you enter this tournament?" Senator: "What do I have to say to my opponents??? Well, I'll tell you what I'll say! Oh by the way, those earings look absolutely stunning on you! *wink* All of you listening out there, I'll say this one time only....I WILL win the tournament and any of you who thinks you can stop me from reaching the top will just go down and I promise you that when I get done with you all, you'll wish you had stayed at home to study your politics! Thank you for your time." Tiffany love: "Well, there you have it, the Senator has made it loud and clear that he will win the heavyweight title and reign as a fair champion. And as we enter this upcoming tournament, only one thing remains unclear in my mind.....do these earings really look that stunning on me tonight???" -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------------ Match 13 - Heavyweight Title Tournament "The Icon" Pimp Daddy Brown vs Senator Bradley This was a great way to end the first ever INWI card in Syracuse. The final first round match up of the INWI Heavyweight title tournament. "The Icon," who dubs himself that name do to the fact that he has won many titles, took the early advantage in this match. Senator Bradley, of the New Coalition Order, was cheered by the fans and used that to his advantage. With Ernie the Caddie at ringside, Bradley came within a slap of the mat of advancing on several occassions. The Pimp Daddy held his composure though and took a couple of breathers outside the ring. Brown then put on a little clinic in wrestling as he nails Bradley with a fall away slam then drops a leg..... He looks like he is setting him up for a suplex... or a perfect-plex... NO IT'S A PIMP-A-PLEX!! 1-2-3... thats the match.... your winner, in 15:27, "The Icon" Pimp Daddy Brown!!!! Brian Newark - Well that's it from Syracuse, NY and the Onondaga County War Memorial. Be sure to keep your sets on as The Show from Utica is up next!!!! All of the other first round matches will be held in Utica... then Tomorrow night we are in Binghamton for the rounds leading up to the finals!! Finally, it's a stop in Albany, NY for the Tournament Finals....where SEVEN champions will be crowned. There are also talks of adding a couple more matches to that card!!! This is the INWI and I am Brian Newark.... see you tomorrow night in BINGHAMTON!!!! INWI Wrestling..... The Revolutionary force in E-Feds
