[INWI] House Show, June 6

6 June 1997

INWI House Show - June 6, 1997 Warwick HS, Warwick, Rhode Island ================================================== Joseph Collins - Hello everyone, I am Joseph Collins for the INWI and I am here to report on a house show for you. House shows will be all weekend for the INWI, Friday, Saturday and Sunday. Here is the first of three and this was held at the Warwick High School in Warwick Rhode Island. Here are the results: Match 1 - Jason "Crusher" Chandler vs Malvolio Although Malvolio scored a big win over Michael Hollywood, he couldn't keep up his streak against Jason Chandler. Chandler ended the match in 9:32 with his "Crush." Match 2 - Vito Rizzotto vs Michael Hollywood This match went back and forth throughout and it looked as though Rizzotto was going to get the win, but his Brother Gino decided to get involved and ended up knocking Vito out so that Hollywood could claim the pinfall victory at 8:57. Match 3 - Mr. Cherry vs John Q. Denver Mr. Cherry returned from a long haitus to pick up an impressive win over John Q. Denver, following a Cherry Bomb in 9:10. Match 4 - Tony Houston vs Pig Pen In an interesting matchup, Tony Houston abused Pig Pen in the ring never letting him catch any offense and picked up a big win following a Cold Drop at 5:49. Match 5 - Awesome X vs Dell Pickle Awesome X remained undefeated in his stay in the INWI when he claimed a win over Dell Pickle in 10:37. Awesome X got Pickle to submit to the figure four. Match 6 - Northeast Championship Mr. Ed Rogers vs Psycho Sal In a great match, Ed Rogers retained his title. Sal looked as though he was going to claim a title, but Bodyguard Jones came out and nailed him with a chair allowing Rogers to nail the Puppet Master and get the win in 11:31. Match 7 - "The Technician" Zack Sharp vs "The Knuckle" Jimmy Chooley The Technician, who recently moved up in the polls, is looking for his first title shot. Sharp took it to Chooley from the beginning, never letting up, and picked up the win after the Ultimate Demise at 7:29. Match 8 - United States Championship P.O. Box vs Predator P.O. Box put his belt on the line against the youngster, the Preadator. P.O. Box showed why he is the United States Champion by dominating almost all of this match and picking up the win after the Final Delivery at 8:01. Match 9 - Tag Team Championship The Terminators vs Contrast The Terminators again put their belts on the line and avoid the Big Brothers. Contrast came in and looked solid and a possible title change was happening. But, the Terminators stuck in there and retained their titles with a pinfall win at 12:31. Match 10 - Main Event INWI Heavyweight Championship "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder vs Bodyguard Jones This was a great match all through out and Jones had chances to become the INWI Heavyweight Champion. Near the end, Jones was tossed outside the ring and Psycho Sal came out and powerslammed him on the concrete runway and threw him back in the ring. Thunder then hit the Thunderbolt and got the pin in 13:46. ============================================================ Joseph Collins - Well, that was it from Warwick!
