[INWI] In The Studio, May 1, 1997

1 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
INWI - IN THE STUDIO May 1,1997 INWI Studios, Delhi, NY ============================================================ Brian Newark - After a brief haitus of In The Studio, we have returned and what a card we have for you tonight! Two title matches are planned, including the TV Tag Team titles when the Freaks defend the titles against ex champs, the Friendship Express. Also, in a special tag team match, the Glamour Boys, Jimmy Cougar and "The Hollywood Kid" Jason Starr take on Contrast. In the main event, P.O. Box will attemp to grab gold when he goes up against United States Champion "Hardcore" John Stilwater. We are also hoping to get an interview from the NEW INWI Champion, "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder. Let's go up to ringside for our first match of the night. ============================================================ Match 1 - Special Tag Team Attraction The Glamour Boys vs Contrast Jimmy Cougar and Jason Starr have decided to enter the ranks of the tag team division here in the INWI and are searching for gold. The two are having their first tag team match here in the INWI as they take on Contrast. It looks like Jimmy Cougar and White will start things off tonight and there's the bell. Cougar and White tie up in the center of the ring and Cougar nails white with an elbow to the mush. White staggers back and Cougar creams him with a short armed clothesline. Cougar tags in Jason Starr and he picks up where Cougar left off. Starr has White in an arm bar and drops the leg on his shoulder. White screams out in pain as he pokes Starr in the eye. White rolls over and tags Black. Black comes in, sends Starr into the ropes and nails him with an elbow and then drops a knee. Black stars to scoop up Starr and the Hollywood Kid goes low and Black falls to the ground. Cougar comes in and they are double teaming Black. White comes in and Starr clotheslines him over the top rope. Into the ropes goes Black and Starr cinches the Starrlock!! The ref checks the arm, 1...2...3! That's the match! Your winners in 7:21, the Glamour Boys!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well, I guess the Tag Team ranks of the INWI better look out for the fact that the Glamour Boys are now looking to grab that gold! Before our next match, which is for the INWI TV Tag Team Titles, we have an interview with the NEW INWI Northeast Champion, Mr. Ed Rogers. ============================================================ (Tiffany Love is walking toward Mr. Ed Rogers on the sidewalk of some street.) Tiffany Love: Could I have a moment of your time Mr- Smelly: The NEW Northeast Champion of the INWI has no time for interviews, however if you come by my hotel room later maybe you and me... Ed Rogers: Smelly, behave yourself this instant! I apologize Miss. Love, my manager can quite mischievous at times... Surely you can interview me, Miss. Love, I always have time for my television neighbors! And let me start off by saying that I won this belt for...Gilligan! No that's not right...wait, I know I won this belt for someone...oh yes I won this belt for my television neighbors! Tiffany Love: Mr. Rogers, now that you have fought it out with Mr. Apples, and lost cleanly, do you feel that your differences are behind you? Ed Rogers: Who did I fight it out with? Tiffany Love: Mr. Apples. Ed Rogers: Who is he? Tiffany Love: (annoyed) You know, the guy that stomped on Smelly... the Television Champion of the INWI!!! Ed Rogers: The INWI??? Tiffany Love: DON'T tell me you don't know what that is. Ed Rogers: (laughing politely) Of course I know what the INWI is, I hold their Northeast Title. However I think that this "Mr. Apples" character may just be in your imagination, Miss Love, but that's okay! We ALL need imaginary friends. Tiffany Love: (to the point of pulling out her own hair) MR. APPLES IS REAL!!! HE IS THE T-V CHAMP! Ed Rogers: (in a reassuring tone) Miss. Lane, how can that be, when everyone knows that I'm the Television Champion??? ============================================================ Brian Newark - Is it just me or is this guy more wacked out than I thought? I have a feeling that Apples and Rogers is not over and these two will continue to go with it. Next up is our TV Tag Team Title match and this should be a great one. The new Champions, the Freaks, take on one time champs, the Friendship Express! Lets go up to the ring! ============================================================ Match 2 - TV Tag Team Championship The Freaks vs The Friendship Express This should be a great match as these two championship caliber teams are set and there's the bell. It looks like it is going to be Alfonzo Geller and Weiland going at it first. Geller comes off the ropes and dropkicks Weiland and he just staggers backwards. Geller trys three standing dropkicks and just moves the big man. Weiland reaches out and grabs Geller and hits a chokeslam and tags in Freak O' Nature. Freak comes in and drops an elbow on Geller and runs over and hits Ricky Sanchez. Ricky tries to come into the ring but the ref stops him and the Freaks are double teaming Alfonzo. Finally Ricky gets past the ref and gets the Freaks off of Alfonzo and drags him to his own corner. There's the official tag and Ricky comes in and he looks quite irrate. Ricky dropkicks weiland, goes back off the rope and sends the bigger man crashing to the mat with a flying elbow. Ricky then goes after Freak and slams him to the mat and goes to the top rope. He's going for a splash, NO!! He misses and he is really hurt! Freak goes for the roll up covver, 1...2...NO!! Ricky kicks out at the last minute! Freak tags in Weiland and Weiland scoops up Ricky and powerslams him to the mat. He then tags in Freak who looks like he's goiing to the top rope and he is taking his time. Here comes Alfonzo and he dropkicks Weiland, who flys over the top rope and lands on a chair...he looks really hurt from that fall. Alfonzo then shakes the top rope and Freak is going to be singing higher than usual tonight! Alfonzo sets up for a superplex and nails it! Ricky is on another rope and the there's a splash! 1...2...3!! Your winners and NEW INWI TV Tag Team Champions in 13:32, Alfonzo Geller and Ricky Sanchez, the Friendship Express!!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Wow! New Champs and we still have one more match to go! BUT, before we go up to the ring for the main event, Michael Pontiff is going to conduct an in ring interview with the NEW INWI Heavyweight Champion, "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder. ============================================================ [Micheal Pontiff is standing in the ring. He nods and begins to speak.] PONTIFF:Ladies and gentlemen...at this time I'd like to bring out a man who recently acheived his.."destiny"..as he calls it, here in the INWI. He's the first three time champion in INWI history and he is currently the INWI World champion. He is..."Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder!! [Thunder appears at the top of the aisle. He's dressed in street clothes with black jeans,hat and t-shirt which reads:"EVIL,MEAN & NASTY". Draped across his left shoulder is the INWI World title belt.With his trademark cigar clenched in his teeth and a sarcastic grin on his face Thunder makes his way down the aisle and into the ring. He grabs the mic from Pontiff almost knocking him over in the process. Thunder begins to speak...] You can leave now,son. I don't need ya fer what I'm gonna say. [Thunder waves Pontiff out of the ring. He then slowly walks around the ring looking out at the crowd with a look of disdain.] WELL...I hate ta say I told ya so but... [Thunder flashes an evil smile.] I TOLD YA SO. [Big heel pop!] Oh I know y'all didn't believe me when I said I was gonna be the next world champion...but here's the proof. [Thunder pats the title belt.] I said I would defeat that tenderfoot pretender...the ICON ...Pimp Daddy Brown. An' here's the proof. [Thunder again pats the title belt.] An' I said I would fulfill my destiny by any means necessary. [Thunder once again pats the title belt. He puffs hard on the stogie sending a cloud of grey smoke swirling around his head.] An' now... ...whether ya like it or ya hate it,the simple fact remains... [Thunder stops dead in his tracks and stares into the camera.] I am the WORLD heavywieght champion. Not the TV champion. Not the Northeast champion. [Thunder smiles eerily again.] The flamin' WORLD champion. [He lowers the mic as he continues to walk around the ring.] An' now all o' these so-called superstars here in the INWI gotta come ta me if they wanna see if they measure up in the INWI. Well step right up boys. I'm an equal opportunity asskicker an'I ain't goin'anywheres. The rules o' the game jus' changed amigos... [Thunder drops the cigar to the mat and rubs it out with his foot.] ..an' yer lookin' at the man who made this all possible. His name is Brody Thunder. Learn ta spell it boys... [Thunder gets in close to the camera for a tight close up of his face sporting that evil grin.] ...cuz it's gonna be yer epitaph. [Thunder throws the mic down and leaves the ring heading back up the aisle to the lockerroom area.] ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well the man means business and I hate to correct a man like to him, but the INWI Heavyweight belt is not yet recognized as a World Title, but maybe Thunder will bring us to that. I guess you never can tell. Let's go up to the main event of the night, for the INWI United States Championship, P.O. Box goes for the gold against "Hardcore" John Stilwater. ============================================================ MAIN EVENT Match 3 - United States Championship "Hardcore" John Stilwater vs P.O. Box This should be one of the most interesting matches in a while. Two contrasting styles and P.O. Box is going for a title that his biggest enemy once wore. And we know that the Glamour Boys are in the arena, so PO Box is going to have to watch his back. There's the bell and we are underway! PO Box and Stilwater just start pounding on eachother in the middle of the ring and the bigger P.O. Box takes control. Box slaps a headlock on Stilwater and connects on a running bulldog. Box is now rubbing Stilwaters face in the mat. Stilwater looks like he has some red on his face. Box rolls him over and starts pounding on him and trying to open that wound up even more! Stilwater is busted wide open and this is a crazy situation. Box scoops up Stilwater and sends him to the mat with a hard slam. Box is whooping it up with the crowd and sets Stilwater up for the boston crab? No he slingshots him into the turnbuckle and "Hardcore" keeps going and over the top rope. Box follows him out and Stilwater is up against the ring post. Box charges and Stilwater moves. Hardcore moves in for the kill and starts to pound Box's head into the ringpost and I think he is cut open as well now! This match is turning into the crimson river! Box falls to the floor and Stilwater picks him up and sends him into the ring. Now Stilwater is going to send Box into the turnbuckle with a slingshot. Oh no, the ref can't move and he goes down! Stilwater signals for it and there's the DDT! The ref is out though and Hardcore is trying to wake him up. Oh my, this is a surprise, here comes Jason Starr and he has a chair. Starr comes in and Stilwater holds Box up so Starr can nail the postman. BOX MOVES!! Starr nails Stilwater and Hardcore crumbles to the mat. Box gets the chair and nails Starr with it and he falls from the ring! He looks at Stilwater, picks him up and gets ready. There's the final delivery!!! 1...2...3!!! WOW!! Your winner in 14:32 and NEW INWI United States Champion, P.O. Box!!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Wow, what a great show we had tonight! We heard from Brody Thunder and Ed Rogers as well as crowning two new champions! This will be a hard one to match! Well, we are out of time In The Studio and we have to go. So, for the rest of the INWI, I am Brian Newark saying so long from Delhi and see you this weekend!!! ============================================================ This has been a presentation of the INWI, May 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= INWI Wrestling.....
