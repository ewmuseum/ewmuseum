Here is your winner in 13:20...and NEW INWI TELEVISION CHAMPION PIG PEN!!!!!!?!!?!?! =========================================================== Scoop Raps... with INWI's bad boy Scoop Ropes These are only my opinions, but look out… they may be yours as well. Can you believe it, I actually don’t have much on my mind this week, but I do have one thing in particular that I would like to talk about. The Springer and his pansy little group. Along with their manager, Dr. Bedpan… they are the biggest woosies ever to step foot in the ring. When I was still wrestling I had some tremendous matches. I held a couple of titles throughout the world and I thought I had a pretty damn good career. Now you got this big mouth Springer, who insists on shooting off his mouth and throwing idle threats at me. Wah wah wah Springer, so you lost your title. You should feel proud that you held a Heavyweight Championship. Now you feel compelled to tell me I should look out. Let me tell you something Springer, back in my day, I wish you had wrestled. I would have loved to got in the ring with you and whipped you from pillar to post. You are just the biggest whiner I have ever laid my eyes on. I mean you cry at my insults over and over, but remember Springer, that’s what I am paid to do. I am paid to give my opinion and my opinion of you is that you need to get a better killer instinct. I’ll give you some credit. You have talent, that’s there. Sure, winning the belt may of been a fluke, but you defended it for a month. That’s takes courage and ability. But your little baby attitude gets you in trouble. If you put all that aggression that you show towards me into the ring, I may give you credit. Until then, you are just a whining little baby. I am Scoop Ropes and that’s my opinion. =========================================================== Match 10 The Comforters (with Dr. Bedpan) vs The All Americans The Comforters fresh off their non title victory last week against the INWI Champions, Solid Gold have yet another challenge ahead of them this week as The All American are definitely rising in the tag team ranks here in the INWI. CO MAIN EVENT Match 11 - Light Heavyweight Championship Bill Bob Woijicowski (with Mr. Flamboyant) vs James "Simply The Best" Pillow (with Angel) The second Light Heavyweight Title match of the night here on Hot Spot. James Pillow looks to become the first ever two time Light Heavyweight Champion in the INWI when he takes on Bill Bob Woijicowski. Woijicowski won the title earlier this evening from the man that beat Pillow last week, Mr. Cherry. To get to this match, Pillow slipped by David McCarter after McCarter pinned himself. Woijicowski comes out and dives into the ring and goes right after Pillow. Woijicowski on top of Pillow and he is pounding away with lefts and rights. Woijicowski scoops up Pillow, sends him into the ropes and there's a diving knee. Pillow falls to the mat and Bill Bob scoops him up and clotheslines him out of the ring. Bill Bob follows him out and slams Pillow's head into the ring. Bill Bob with some kicks to the mid section and then sends him to the arena floor with a clothesline. Bill Bob starts to jaw with the crowd and he doesn't see Pillow get up. Pillow with a clothesline on the outside and that sends Woijicowski to the floor. Pillow picks up Bill Bob and rolls him into the ring, hits the ropes and there's a leg drop. Cover by Pillow, 1.....2....NO! Woijicowski kicks out and this match will continue. Bill Bob just won that title and he doesn't want to lose it right away. Pillow scoops up Woijicowski and there's a snap suplex. Pillow to the outside and to the top and he nails an elbow and there's another cover,1......2.....NO! Pillow looks confused now as he can't believe Bill Bob is kicking out. Pillow grabs Woijicowski, but Bill Bob pokes him in the eye and Pillow staggers backwards and Bill Bob charges with a clothesline. Pillow slams hard to the mat and Bill Bob starts to stomp away on Pillow. Bill Bob scoops Pillow and suplex's him to the mat. Bill Bob back in control, grabs Pillow and hits a short armed clothesline. Wait, Pillow fights back and these two are exchanging punches in the middle of the ring. Pillow with a standing drop kick and Bill Bob falls to the mat. Pillow sends Bill Bob into the ropes and connects with a spinning heel kick. Pillow with a quick cover, 1...NO! Pillow kicks out at one. Pillow grabs Bill Bob and he's setting up for a piledriver!! Look out, Bill Bob blocked it and there's a back body drop! Both men are down and Bill Bob is to his feet and then Pillow and Bill Bob sends Pillow to the mat with a drop kick. Bill Bob again begins to stomp away at Pillow and he has the total up swing in this match. Bill Bob is dancing in the ring and he thinks he has this match won and I almost have to agree with him. Bill Bob to scoop up Pillow...OH MAN!!! Pillow was playing possum and he has rolled up Bill Bob and he has a handful of tights!!! 1...... 2......3!!!!! OH MY!!! Let's get the official word! Here is your winner in 17:46 and NEW INWI Light Heavyweight Champion, James "Simply The Best" Pillow!!
