[INWI] Hot Spot, 10-27, Hour 1

27 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene switches to the back where "The Real Deal" Michael Bledsoe is entering the arena. He has a not so pleasant look on his face and he has his duffel bag on his shoulder. Tonight, Bledsoe has a big chance to move up the ranks as he faces the former Heavyweight Champion in the Springer. He comes through the door and the lights go out...] BLEDSOE - What the HELL? [All of a sudden you hear someone get nailed by a chair. INWI officials are staring to scream and get the lights back on. At the same time you can hear someone taking a beating and the lights finally come back on. When they come on you see Vinny Vitrone and Bill Bob Woijicowski beating on Bledsoe. Woijicowski scoops up Bledsoe and sets him up for a piledriver. Vitrone gets on a table and completes the spike piledriver on the floor. INWI officials are trying to break it up and finally get Woijicowski and Vitrone away from Bledsoe. You then see the two walk off but not until Woijicowski throws a Brotherhood T-Shirt on Bledsoe. The two then leave laughing and INWI Officials tend to Bledsoe....] [The scene then fades away and goes to the beginning of INWI Hot Spot. This is a series of shots of all of the INWI Superstars. It shows Tony Houston holding onto the INWI Heavyweight Championship as Springer gives him a Springer Cutter off of a ladder, it shows Mr. Ed Rogers and King Apples going at it, it shows James "Simply The Best" Pillow with the INWI Light Heavyweight title and Angel at his side, it shows Sam Adams draped in a U.S. Flag with Militia at his side and the New York State Title over his shoulder, it shows Solid Gold in a four way brawl with the Comforters in their infamous battle for the Unified Titles, it shows Bill Bob Woijicowski and Mr. Cherry in their wild battle for the Light Heavyweight Title and then it shows an action packed seconds of ALL INWI superstars and it ends with the screen exploding and you see INWI..... Hot Spot with the Hot Spot in flames...] INWI Presents Monday Hot Spot!! October 27, 1997 The Bullseye, Pittsburgh, Pa. Brian Newark - Welcome ladies and gentlemen to this weeks edition of Monday Hot Spot!!!! What a card we have for you and what a way to open the show. Michael Bledsoe, all by himself, attacked by two members of the Brotherhood. The more of these guys I watch, the sicker I get of them. What do they have against Michael Bledsoe? This is unreal... are they just making a statement? Also on the card tonight, Ultimate Perversion tries to regain his title and his self respect when he takes on Pig Pen. A new edition of Scoop Raps as well and much, much more all on Hot Spot!! With that, let's go up to the ring with an INWI newcomer, Asswhupper! Match 1 - Debut Match The Asswhupper (with Playmaker and Mama) vs Dell Pickle (with Oyster Boy) The Asswhupper, an INWI newcomer has entered the ring with his two managers. No doubt does this guy look like inner city problems and I have a feeling he can make some problems in the INWI. Dell Pickle, who's been on a slide ever since his loss of the Northeast Title, is looking to take advantage of a newcomer. There's the bell and we are underway. Asswhupper charges and nails a diving clothesline that sends Pickle to the mat. Asswhupper picks up Pickle and puts him on his shoulder and nails a running power slam. Asswhupper scoops up Pickle and sends him into the ropes and connects with a belly to belly suplex. Asswhupper to the outside and he's on the top rope and there's a moonsault! The Asswhupper is really taking it to Pickle in his debut match. Off the ropes bounces Asswhupper and nails a leg drop and then he goes to the outside again...to the top and connects with a frog splash!! Asswhupper grabs Pickle and floors him with a hard clothesline. Asswhupper looks to the outside and looks like he has an ok and sets up Pickle for a diamond cutter... YES! There's the Playmaker!!! 1....2......3!!! Here is your winner in 5:01, Asswhupper!!!! Match 2 - New York Championship General Samuel Adams (with Militia) vs Michael Slater (with Harvey Thunder) This should be a great match for the New York Championship...Slater starts the match quick and attacks Adams as he was making his way into the ring...Slater pulls up Adams and sends him hard into the corner..Slater charges and elevates Adams with a Monster Knee lift..Adams staggers out of the corner...Slater bounces off the ropes..Bulldog on Adams..Slater covers..One..two..No!!! Slater pulls Adams to his feet and sends him into the ropes...Slater leap frogs..Adams stops and nails a perfectly executed Standing Dropkick on Slater...Adams up quickly...Fisherman's Suplex on Slater..One..two..No!!! Slater rolls his shoulder..Adams up and pulls Slater to his feet and sends him into the corner with a vicious European Uppercut ....European uppercut? Don't say that around paranoid patriot Adams...Adams lifts Slater and seats him on the top turnbuckle..Adams climbs to the top turnbuckle.... Top Rope Hurakanrana by Adams ...Adams up quickly and bounces off the ropes ...Springboard Legdrop..Adams with a cover..One..two...No!!!! Slater showing his toughness...Adams pulls up Slater and sends him into the corner..WOOOO and follows it up with a stinging chop...and another...and a third chop stinging the chest of Michael Slater...Adams sends Slater across the ring..Slater springboards and drops Adams with a Springboard Flying Forearm...Both men are down in the center of the ring...Slater is starting to stir, Slater to his feet and pulls up Sam Adams... Side Walk Power bomb by Slater..he hooks both legs..One..two..Th--NO!!! Adams kicks out...ya' know he may be a bit different but Adams is definitely a fighting champion...Slater pulls up Adams...Slater bounces off the ropes..Single Arm DDT by Slater...Another cover..One..two..th--NO!!!!! Adams again narrowly escapes pinfall. Slater yanks Adams up by his hair and fires him into the ropes...Backdrop by Sla--No!! Adams countered with a Sunset Neckbreaker ... Blockbuster?? Great move there by the New York Champion...That move should give 'em a breather..but he's not taking it..Adams back up and sends Slater into the corner and Adams methodically follows him in....He's setting up for the Liberty Bell..BANG he hit it..Adams hit the Liberty Bell....Adams covers One..two..three!!!! Adams retains his belt in an outstanding match..WHOA!!! WAIT..Here comes Perversion and Eagle.... Perversion and Eagle are working over Slater and here comes Harvey Thunder with a steel chair...Thunder in the ring CRACK and sends Perversion to the outside....Eagle sees Thunder wielding the chair and quickly scurries to the outside....This feud is FAR from over folks..but let's get the official word...Here is your winner in 15:32 and still New York Champion, Sam "The Minuteman" Adams!! =========================================================== Newark - What a great couple of matches to start off Hot Spot!! Let's hear from Tommy Graves before his match... =========================================================== Setting: The room is dark..in the background, you can here .....in justice for all is being played. Tommy Graves: The beginning is the end is the beginning.....and so goes the show. Tommy Graves is still hear, and he ain't going ANYWHERE...but the TOP! Some will ask..."Who are you", and I'd answer that the only way I know how....Grab the ******* moron...slap him OR her in the face...(Ya know Tommy Graves is an equal opportunity ass kicking machine) AND TELL HIM OR HER....I'M THE DEATH MACHINE IN THE INWI...AND THERE AIN'T A DAMN THING YOU CAN DO ABOUT IT. Can I do this by myself....can I rule the INWI by myself? Hell ya I can...but I choose not too, ya see, I'm looking, I'm looking for a partner...a man ta watch my back, as I destroy this damn league, tear it limb, from bloody limb. And I already have a person in mind......... Silence (scene changes) (a photo appears on the tv screen) The Photo is of Jonathan Hammer =========================================================== Match 3 Tommy Graves vs "Kodiac" Dan Harris You know, this Tommy Graves is a monster. He entered the INWI, got people all curious as to who he is and is now whipping some serious tail. I must say that he will be a force to be dealt with here in the INWI. Today he takes on "Kodiac" Dan Harris in a big match for both people. It seems as though Harris has been on a streak lately and from what we understand from the INWI front office, Harris has been giving his notice of some sorts. We'll see over the next couple of weeks what they mean I suppose. Well, Harris is in the ring and Graves slowly enters the ring and stares across the ring at Harris. Harris charges Graves and Graves grabs him by the throat and nails a choke slam! Harris is wiggling on the mat in pain as Graves scoops up Harris and press slams him over the top rope and to the floor and the crowd is going nuts!! Graves to the floor and he grabs Harris and sends him into the ring post. He grabs Harris again and sets up for a power slam, NO! He rams Harris' head into the ring post and then again! Harris is broken open and Graves just rolls him back into the ring. Graves back into the ring and nails a leg drop on Harris. A cover by Graves, 1.....2....NO! Graves picks up Harris and laughs. Graves sets up Harris and presses him again and drops him face first on the turnbuckle. Harris just falls to the mat with a thud and he looks like he is out. You can't even hear him moan from the pain, but he has been silent for a long time! Graves scoops Harris and places him on the top rope and he goes up as well. He looks like he is going for a super plex.... NO! A DDT from the top rope!!! Graves is back up and he covers Harris with one finger!!!! 1......2.......3!!!! Its over! Here is your winner in 7:36, Tommy Graves!!!! Match 4 Adam Kuhl vs Jonathan Hammer This match pits two men who have been going in slightly different directions of late..Hammer probably still hasn't recovered from a BRUTAL 3 Way Dance Cage match at INWI's Invasion '97 and Adam Kuhl who is scheduled to meet the INWI Heavyweight Champion, Tony Houston in an obviously to be brutal Scaffold Match at the next PPV. Kuhl enters the ring and takes quick advantage as Hammer was allowing the ref to check his boots..Kuhl drops Hammer with a devastating roundhouse kick that nailed Hammer on his ear. Kuhl scoops up Hammer and drops him head first with a DDT..Kuhl covers..One..two..NO!!!! Hammer rolls his shoulder ..Kuhl back up quickly and pulls up Hammer and fires him into the ropes..Spinning Side Walk slam by Kuhl good move....Kuhl goes to the second turnbuckle on the inside and leaps..Guillotine Legdrop...Hooks the leg..One..two..NO!! Hammer again rolls his shoulder...Kuhl is looking a little frustrated...Kuhl goes to the outside and ascends to the top turnbuckle and waits for Hammer to get to his feet....Kuhl leaps...No!!! Hammer counters Kuhl's Double Ax Handle attempt with a Super kick that drops Kuhl!!! Unbelievable move by Jonathan Hammer. Hammer is now on the offensive..Hammer nails a standing leg drop on the fallen Kuhl...and another...and a third..and goes for a quick cover..One..two..No!!! Kuhl kicks out. Hammer pulls up Kuhl..Irish whip by Hammer...Reversed by Kuhl..Back drop by Kuhl--No!! Hammer bouncing off the ropes countered with a lethal Spinning DDT..but doesn't follow up with a cover..Could be a mistake....Hammer pulls up Kuhl and sends him into the ropes....Pancake by Hammer and Kuhl just landed face first on the mat..That's an ol' Midnight Express and great move there...Wait!!! Who's that?!? It's the Heavyweight Champion Tony Houston and King Apples..they storm the ring.. Hammer sees them coming and wisely exits the ring...Houston pulls up Kuhl...and holds him..Apples off the ropes and plants a size 16 in the mouth of Kuhl..Apples pulls up Kuhl and fires him into the ropes...drops down...Fireman's Carry by Houston... <BANG> TOO COLD DRIVER...Houston just hit the T.C.D. on Kuhl and he's motionless laying in the middle of the ring...Just who will The Brotherhood NOT attack.. Let's get the official word.. Here is your winner in 8:34, Via Disqualification....Adam Kuhl!! =========================================================== Newark - Ladies and gentlemen... that's the end of Hour ONE!!
