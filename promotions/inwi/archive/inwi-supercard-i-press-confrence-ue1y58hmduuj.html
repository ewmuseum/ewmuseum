[INWI] SuperCard I, Press Confrence

27 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Press Confrence for SuperCard I Philadelphia Hilton Saturday, April 26, 1997 ================================================== Brian Newark - Hello one and all to the INWI SuperCard I Press Confrence. We are coming to you live on Sportschannel USA and of course Tomorrow Night we will be coming to you LIVE on Fox for our first ever SuperCard! The INWI has 20 great matches planned and all but the Northeast Title is on the line! It will prove to be a great night of wrestling. But, without any further delay, let's go up to the podium where many wrestlers will speak about their matches tomorrow night! First, we have INWI Commissioner Jerry DeLuise, who will announce each of the speakers for tonight. =============================================================== (Commissioner Jerry DeLuise walks to the podium to a decent ovation. He is clad in a very nice tuxedo, with the letters "INWI" embroidered on the pocket. He get's to the podium and speaks.) Jerry DeLuise - Welcome ladies and gentlemen to SuperCard I. I guess you could say that SuperCard will be the Wrestlemania of the INWI, our "super show" so to speak. For the fact that we are a young and upcoming federation, we didn't want to risk doing a pay-per-view. Step in Fox, who offered to play it live on Fox this Sunday April 27. Before I introduce our first speaker, do any of you have questions for me? Reporter 1 - Yes Commissioner, what about the latest stunts that the Glamour Boys have done? They are now working as a team and really dampering people's shots at titles because of their actions. DeLuise - We are currently looking into this and we realize that they are trying to take any title they can. We will be watching them very closely this weekend. Reporter 2 - Mr. DeLuise, what about the Main Event. We have heard it will be in a cage and we have heard it won't be in a cage. Can you tell us what that match will be? DeLuise - The Main Event, which will be for the INWI Heavyweight Championship, will be inside the confines of a Steel Cage. In this match you can win by pinning or making your opponent submit, or leaving the cage through the door or over the top. But, enough of me, let's get some of the wrestlers up here. First up with be one of the men going for the TV Title, Mr. Ed Rogers! =============================================================== [Mr. Ed Rogers walks to the podium looking around like he is scared of all the bright lights and the flashes that are going off. With him as always is his "manager" Smelly. At SuperCard I, Rogers will attempt to finally gain the INWI TV Title from Mr. Apples and Firestar in a three way dance.] Reporter: Mr. Ed Rogers, do you think that the you are skilled enough to pull out a victory over such stiff compition as Firestar and Mr. Apples. Mr. Ed Rogers: When I was I child I had a toy car...vroom, vroom. I liked my toy car. I got it from Santa. But the bully next door took it away from me. He broke it. Mr. Ed Rogers: Mr. Apples! Yes, I'm talking to you! You killed my wife and almost killed Smelly! I wanted to make-up, but you ruined the cake I gave you! Then you sprayed me with your evil disinfectant. I couldn't see, but I could hear you laughing at me. Mr. Ed Rogers: Do you know what happened to the bully? That night I snuck out with my dad's hacksaw, and cut off his hand. He didn't brake any more of my toys anymore. Mr. Apples, Mr. Firestar welcome to my hell, I hope you enjoy your stay! =============================================================== Jerry DeLuise - What in the hell was that all about? I truely hope that story he just told was a lie because if it is true, maybe some law will be brought to this maniac. I don't know what to think about him. Every time I hear him talk, I get the chills. Our second member to come out will be another person involved in the three way dance, Firestar. =============================================================== [Firestar walks into the INWI press conference room as photographers take snap shots and some commotion is heard in the background. Firestar walks in with a pair of blue jeans, a plain black T-shirt, and black boots on. Firestar has not removed his mask. He approaches his seat and looks around the room for a couple of seconds. Firestar finally takes his seat and gives the signal that he will now take questions. Tons of reporters raise their hands in hopes of getting a few comments from Firestar. Firestar points at a male reporter who has a notepad and a pen in his hands. As the reporter is about to speak a few more reporters continue to keep their hands up in the air and some press the record button on their tape recorders] Reporter #1: Mr. Firestar, your title match that you've requested will be a three way dance. How do you think you'll fair in this type of brutal match? [Firestar pauses for a few seconds then glances at the reporter who asked the question and prepares to give his answer] Firestar: Three way dance, eh? Listen up, I don't care what type of match it is! The bottom line is that Firestar will get the title shot that he so rightfully deserves. To answer your question, I have really NO strategy for this one. I'm in this match with only ONE thing on my mind and that is to capture the INWI Television Title. If I have to go through TWO pieces of garbage then so be it. The only statement I will give you as an answer is that I guarantee a victory for Firestar as well as the INWI Television Title. Next question... [Five reporters raise their hands at the same time, Firestar looks at all of them and points at a female reporter all the way in the back area of the INWI press conference room] Reporter #2: Firestar, there have been talks that you won't be able to get the job done. What are your thoughts about these critics? [Firestar quickly responds] Firestar: Listen lady, I really don't care what the critics say. Firestar is out here busting his behind for myself, not for some incorrect critic. When I win the INWI Television Title, its going to be MINE, all MINE! So, like I said, I don't care what the critics think. Time and time again I have proven the critics wrong and now it's time to do it again. One more question and I'm outta' here...Next... [A man with eyeglasses in the front row raises his hand, Firestar points at the man and says] Firestar: You there in the front with the eyeglasses. Reporter #3: Firestar, I and many of us in this room at this time have followed you for most of your career. Now, my question...You have always worn that mask you have on your head right now. Firestar, why do you where the mask...Do you have something to hide? [Firestar gives the reporter an angry look and stares at him hard enough to burn a hole through him. Firestar glances around the room for a second and then returns his attention back to the reporter who asked him the question] Firestar: What? Now, what kind of question is that, eh? Huh? What kind of question is that?!?! Answer me, boy! Reporter #3: I was just asking bec... Firestar: NO! You were being ignorant that's what you were doing. I have no time for ignorant questions, I'm outta' here! [Firestar quickly gets up and makes his way towards the exit. Some members of the media try to call him back, but he pays NO attention to them whatsoever] Reporter #3: Mr. Firestar, wait! [Firestar exits the room and leaves everyone irate because of his early departure] ===============================================================Jerry DeLuise - Well I guess that we know that Firestar does not like to be questioned about his mask! Our next speaker is the current INWI Television Champion. Please welcome, Mr. Apples! =============================================================== [Mr. Apples, in his normal Tuxedo and big Cuban cigar, walks up to the podium, acknowledging the press and fans in the back. As he gets to the podium and adjusts the mike so he doesn't have to bend over that much.] Mr. Apples - Before I take any questions, I have a statement I would like to make. Tomorrow night is a big night in the INWI, for all of the wrestlers and for all of the federation. Tomorrow is when we get to show the country what we are like in the INWI. I am a part of this and I feel honored. I hope all wrestlers, no matter if they like the fans or not, will feel honored to be involved. As for myself, I have one of the biggest challenges in my career. I have to take on two rather tough individuals in Mr. Ed Rogers and Firestar. Both men have given me tough challenges by themselves and I bet that they will be even tougher when I have to fend them both off. I know they want to get rid of me first, then worry about eachother, so I will be on my toes for this one. Now, if anyone has any questions, I will gladley answer them. Reporter 1 - Mr. Apples, you have had a nice reign in the INWI as the TV Champion, but it seems like Mr. Ed Rogers is always a thorn in your side. Your thoughts on him? Apples - Mr. Ed Rogers has been somewhat of a thorn. He really seems like he enjoys interfering in my matches and being a tough guy. Let me tell you this though, once we are in that three way, Rogers will see the real deal. I want to get rid of Firestar first, then I will deal with Rogers. He is someone who will be wimpering in the back before the night is through. But, I have an autograph session to get to for the INWI, so I have to call an end to this. I will promise you this, I am walking in with my TV belt and walking out with it. Firestar and Rogers, your days are numbered and there is only one day left. ============================================================= Jerry DeLuise - Well the three way dance between Firestar, Rogers and Apples is bound to be one of the matches on the card. Next up is Vito Rizzotto, who says he will only answer one question. He will face Jason "The Crusher" Chandler at SuperCard! ============================================================= Reporter - Mr. Rizzotto, you are the former INWI TV Champion and you are having a tough time getting back to that status. Your thoughtsd on your counterpart for tomorrow night? V. Rizzotto: CRUSHER ! I'm ready for you. I don't care what measures I have to take, but I will whip you from pillar to post at SuperCard I. After I'm done with you I'm gonna go after everybody else who stands in my way,in my quest to become #1 ! And just to make sure you don't pull any funny stuff, I'm bringing in my twin brother Gino, just so it's a clean fight ! ============================================================= Jerry DeLuise - Bringing a twin brother is making it a clean fight? Oh my, this could turn out to be a good match as well. Next up is a recorded statement from the House of Denial. It seems they are in a little disaray and they want to attempt to clear this up with the rest of the INWI. Please watch the screen in back of me as we roll the tape. ============================================================= [A sign appears on screen saying "Recorded Earlier Today for the INWI"] Trent: The House Of Denial have called this press conference not just on the INWI's insistence, but also to clear a few things up. As you may notice, one of our stable is not here. Ultimate Perversion refused to appear due to both Tiffany and Michael refusing to interview him anymore. I believe this flagrant abuse of power should not be tolerated any longer. We at the House Of Denial will no longer conduct interviews with ANYONE in the INWI. From now on, all communication with the INWI will be through press conferences like this one, until such time as we receive an apology from both Tiffany and Michael for the appaling way they treated Ultimate Perversion. Yes, I see you at the back Tiffany, and you Michael. Why not make things easier for all of us, and apologise here and now for the whole world to see? <This statement is greeted with a shake of the head from Michael, and a derisive snort from Tiffany. Trent waits a few seconds, then sighs.> See what happens to some people when they get a sniff of fame. Very sad. Where was I... Ah, yes... We will... from you lot anymore. I should have had gold by now, but due to the referees, and my appaling manager, I'm still not champion. If these <BLEEP> had even half a brain in their heads, they'd realise not to mess with me. <He leans forward and drags a camera in up close to his face until all that can be seen is his face leering into the camera.> Feel my pain. <He spits on the lense. The shot cuts to another camera. Trent gets up and tries to push Perversion out of the way, but Perversion pushes him down again.> No more Mister Nice Guy. <Tiffany snorts derisively again. Perversion glares at her.> Is that you Tiffany? You talentless bimbo. Shut the <BLEEP> up. And is that that pussy Michael next to you? Come here Michael. I'll show you what real pain is you <BLEEP>. Come here. <Perversion goes to jump over the table, but Trent gets up and holds him back just long enough for Tiffany and Michael to escape. Perversion roars in anger, and picks Trent up with his left hand, lifting him off the ground.> Trent: What the <BLEEP> do you think you're doing. I own you. I'm trying to stand by you. <The cameras pull back, and the Voyeurs quickly get up and nervously back away. Cloud Eagle continues to sit serenely as if nothing unusual is going on.> Ultimate: I've had it with your stable. It's a waste of time you <BLEEP>. You're a waste of space. You're not a manager, you're just a publicity seeking little runt. I quit. <Ultimate pulls back his right hand and punches Trent squarely on the jaw, then throws him to the floor. Trent is obviously unconcious. Ultimate turns back to the Camera.> Now hear this. I am no longer with, nor do I want to be associated with the House Of Denial, and you pussys <He points at the Voyeurs who are standing near a fire exit.> I'll take you both on anytime, and as for you lot <He gestures wildly to the cameras, then kicks the table over at them. The cameras pull back further. Ultimate turns back to finish what he started on Trent as all but one of the cameras are rapidly removed from the room. As he advances on him, Cloud Eagle moves with the speed of a cheetah to put himself between Trent and Ultimate.> Out of my way Eagle. <Cloud Eagle just stands there, facing Ultimate down. Ultimate looks confused, and attempts to punch Cloud Eagle, but he catches his fist.> Cloud: In the ring is the place for this. Not here. Leave. <Ultimate takes another swing, but again Cloud stops it.> NOW! <Ultimate glares at him, then storms out. Cloud looks into the one remaining camera.> This will be settled in the ring shortly. Peace to you all. <He bends down, and tends to Trent. As the camera fades to black, you see Mystery Man faint in the corner of the room. The Alien just looks at him.> ============================================================= Jerry DeLuise - Well I don't think they cleared things up, I think they made it worse. This is the second stable in the last week or so to have troubles and it is getting interesting. This was recorded a couple days ago and this is why Ultimate Perversion and Cloud Eagle are slated to go at it tomorrow at SuperCard. Next up is Bodyguard Jones, who will face former stablemate Psycho Sal at SuperCard. Jones requests no questions as he just wants to issue a statement and leave. ============================================================= *Bodyguard adjusts the mike to comfort his tall stature* BJ: I know alot of people want to know what I have to say about the kings of destruction. All I want to say is this, whatever happened between me and Mean Eugene I've already forgotten. You see, I hold no grudge against him for one reason, when he attacked me after my match that night -he really did me a favor. You see Eugene, the day you ripped up my contract, was the day my career took off. I became a star, and left you in the dust! The only grudge I hold, is one for Psycho Sal. Bro, at Supercard, I'm gonna make you wish you'd never stuck your nose in my business. What I want to promise to the fans is that not only will I put on my best performance so far, but I will emerge victorious and will eliminate the scum that calls himself Psycho Sal!!! Thank you everyone, I'm out!!! ============================================================= Jerry DeLuise - Well Bodyguard Jones has been on a major roll lately and he seems focused for his big match. Next up is a team hoping to get the INWI TV Tag Titles, the Freaks! ============================================================= [crowd cheers as Weiland and Freak O'Nature walk up to the podium.] Weiland: Well, the big day for us is Sunday. We plan on going up there into the ring and winning those belts. Freak O'Nature: HEEEEEEEYYYYYYY!! heckler: You FREAK! Freak O'Nature: That's right! We're the Freaks! And soon to be TV Tag Champs. It's gonna be a piece of cake whipping Solid Gold...they ain't nothing but some refugees from the 70's. Stayin Alive, Stayin Alive...Stayin Aliiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiive! Weiland: (stares blankly at Freak O'Nature): OK! Well, we are looking forward to the big day, when we take our first step toward total dominance of the INWI, and bringing home a belt for the Natural Born Killers. (Oz, Bishop, and Jason "the Crusher" Chandler join them at the podium.) Oz: The NBK's taking over starting at the Super Card. First we'll get the TV Tag belts, then soon, the rest of us will be champs! (Freak O'Nature starts running around in circles) Freak: I can't wait! I can't wait! Wait till mommy sees my new gold belt! Wait...mommy's dead! Waaaaaaaaaaaahhhhhhhhhhh! (starts crying...Bishop leads him away) Weiland: Look what you people did! You made him cry! Well the only people crying Sunday will be Solid Gold, when we take their belts!!!!!! NBK For Life! ============================================================= Jerry DeLuise - Well, I must say that was interesting, but they do seem like they are ready to go! And now, lets go up to Their opponents and the current champions, Bradley and Blake - Solid Gold!! ============================================================= Rex Hufferman (The Ring Report): I have a question for Solid Gold. I was wondering what your chances of holding on to the TV tag team titles tomorrow will be? Bradley Gold: What kind of a question is that? You know what, I will answer it. First of all since this is a question about our match tomorrow night, I will hold off on commententing about The Glamour Boys. My brother is not here for this press conference because he is still recovering from Monday night but he will be there tomorrow and he will be 100%. Our challenge comes from The Freaks. Freak O' Nature and Weiland... You guys don't stand a chance. Tomorrow will be just another title defense like all others and will only act as a stepping stone for us on our way to the big gold, The INWI tag team titles. Hufferman: Come on. How could you say the Frreaks don't even have a chance? That is really arrogant. Bradley: Arrogance has nothing to do with it. What we have is confidence. If you look at the rankings, We are the number two team right now in the INWI, The Freaks are much lower and they have fallen quite a bit. I see no problem. The whole country will get to see the greatest tag team totally dominate their competition. Hufferman: You guys have needed the help of Michael Hollywood in the past. What will you do without his help tomorrow night? Bradley: There you go again! Nobody can seem to understand that we don't need Hollywood's help. He seems to have found a new friend and he can quite frankly go to hell. We won these titles the first time without his help and we would have won them the second time even if he didn't help. The Friendship Express was no match for us. No team is a match for us and that is why I see no problem in defeating the Freaks tomorrow night!!! Hufferman: Why do you guys want to beat up Harvey Thunder? Bradley: That has nothing to do with tomorrow night. I have said all I have to say about Supercard. Any other topics will have to wait!!!! ============================================================= Jerry DeLuise - I am sure that title match will be very interesting to watch as Solid Gold will have to worry about the match and at the same time, watch their backs! Next up on the podium are the INWI Tag Team Champions, Hunter and Steven... The Big Brothers!! ============================================================= *As the Big Brothers are announced, they walk up to the podium in their outrageously priced suits* Hunter Big: Ladies and gentlemen, me and my brother Steven are honored to be here. Our time in the INWI has been the best of our lives. Steven Big: That's right, and as the Tag Team Champions, all we have to say is that we're going into Supercard 1 with everything we've got and we're gonna keep these belts around our waists. Comforters, I know you're out there listening....you guys are in for the fight of your lives...because the Big Brothers are going to Splash our way to retaining these belts. Thank you (Continued...) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= INWI Wrestling.....
