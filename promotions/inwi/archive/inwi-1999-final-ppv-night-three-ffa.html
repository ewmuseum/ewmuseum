[INWI] Final PPV — Night Three, “Thanks For The Memories” Free 4 All

21 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
INWI Presents... "Thanks For The Memories..." The FINAL INWI Pay Per View March 21, 1999 FREE 4 ALL ======================================================== BN: Well fans. This is it. The last INWI show. And it has been one great weekend. Tonight will be no different. Final champions are going to be crowned left and right. We already have a final Trans-Atlantic champion in Kris Klepan. He knocked off Jeff Drake last night for that strap. More will be decided tonight. But sit back for the next hour and enjoy the Free 4 All. And then, in one hour, Scoop and I will join you LIVE from the INWI Arena in Delhi, New York... to bring you one of the most fantastic wrestling events ever. See you then. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= INWI World Television Champion "Brass Knux" Jason Williams =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [The camera now opens to the inside of the arena. How the the camera got passed the locked doors remains a mystery. Inside the arena is clean. The bath rooms are empty, the floor has a rather pleasant twinkle to it, and all is silent. The sound of the camera man's foot steps is all that can be heard. A red curtain that separates the food and bath room is moved. A wide view of the entire arena is now seen. The empty seats that are nicely folded up give the arena a good look. The already built wrestling ring stands proudly in the middle of everything with the official INWI logo embroiled in it.] [Up above, the sky boxes hover over all the seats. Inside Sky Box C, someone's feet are kicked up on a table and a cold drink is in hand. As this person notices the camera, he hops out of the seat and exits the room he is currently in. In a minute of so, the red curtain on the other side of the camera parts. None other than "Brass Knux" Jason Williams emerges. Knux jogs down the arena steps.] [Williams is not wearing what he originally was in the parking lot. The black hat has been removed, as well as the black jacket. Williams hair is slightly messed up from the hat and his large biceps are now seen as his black "Bro' Ho" T-shirt exposes them. His baggy blue jeans are held up by a black leather belt.] [Knux reaches the bottom of the arena and leaps over the guardrail. He slides quickly into the setup ring and bounces a few times against the ropes. Williams then leaps to the second turnbuckles and stares in amazement at the quiet arena. I must have left that back open. Anyway, here I am, "Governor" Knux... oops! Sorry. I've got to get off that damn subject. [Knux chuckles.] BRASS KNUX: All right, let's get down to some business. I didn't break into this place to fool around, I came to talk about an old friend of mine that I'd like to. . . well, I'd like to kill him! This man is that smelly redneck, BILL BOB WOIJICOWSKI! [A look of enmity sweeps across the face of Brass Knux.] BRASS KNUX: That piece of crap has been nothing but a thorn in my side. The crap that went on with him goes way back. All the way back to the N.E.W. days. That little jackass used to taunt me backstage. I hated his guts. And I still do. [Knux begins to think of something.] BRASS KNUX: I've said this before and I'll say it again. . . As you all know Woijicowski and I never, ever liked each other. It all started in the N.E.W.. A tournament was held for the vacant Intercontinental Championship. Bill Bob and I against each other in a second round match. During the match, I cleaned his clock was a nice beer bottle shock over the head. I left him in the ring a bloody mess. His redneck ass made it to the next round. I'll never forget it. From then on, he provoked me. He bragged about his win... a defeat he got over me by disqualification. Bill Bob Woijicowski is the reason I got into this whole "war" with the INWI in my younger days. One time we fought in the INWI, and after that one match he proclaimed to be the man to run Brass Knux out of the INWI. But, I'm going to prove him wrong by kicking his sorry redneck ass all over the damn ring! Man, that little speech sounds very familiar, where have I heard it before? [Knux shrugs.] BRASS KNUX: That's not important. What's important is that I am going to kick some redneck ass! And why you ask? [Knux pulls a T-shirt out of a bag and holds it up to the screen. Knux reads the words that are on the screen aloud.] BRASS KNUX: "Cause I Hate Rednecks!" Yeah! The merchandise store was opened early this time!
