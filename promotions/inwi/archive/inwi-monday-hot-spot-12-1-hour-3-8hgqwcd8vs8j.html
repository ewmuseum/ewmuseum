[INWI] Monday Hot Spot, 12-1, Hour 3

9 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The letters I---N---W---I pop on the screen one by one. Then they explode as the logo of Monday Night Hot Spot comes onto the screen. The music "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne starts to crank and the arena goes black. The INWI-Tron pops on and the beginning of Hot Spot starts.... [It starts by showing a close up of INWI Champion Tony Houston with the INWI belt over one shoulder and the Ringmaster Belt over the other. He just smiles at the camera. It then shows King Apples nailing a tomb swirly on Ed Rogers. It shows Springer nailing a Springer Cutter on Scoop ropes from the top of the cage. It then shows Springer draped in an INWI flag flexing....] [It shows Sam Adams draped in American flag with Militia behind him and him laughing with the New York Title around his waist. It shows Michael Bane, Michael Bledsoe and Jonathan Hammer in one of the most grueling cage matches in INWI history. It then shows a close up of every championship belt in the INWI.... at different camera angles...] [It then shows Commissioner Jerry DeLuise in an INWI Arena looking over the INWI. Jason Chandler holding up his Television Championship and then the screen starts to show all sorts of pieces from the INWI..... James Pillow nailing a super kick on Rick Brown, Vinny Vitrone in his dog collar match with the "jWo" Icon Pig Pen, Ultimate Perversion and Cloud Eagle going at it with Harvey Thunder and Michael Slater, Bill Bob Woijicowski reading one of his ''stories'' to his nephew Bobby Bob...Cherry nailing a Cherry Bomb...Mr. Ed Rogers staring at Smelly...Tommy Graves nailing a choke slam...David McCarter on the mic...The Asswhupper and Thugs 4 Life standing with their arms crossed.... Solid Gold turning on manager Jimmy Stardom... The Comforters holding up the Tag Team titles, Malice in a match....The All Americans waving American flags...and a final shot of the jWo huddling together....] Then you see Pete Robinson walk past the camera with an American Flag jacket on...the camera follows him and on the back of the jacket you can see "INWI" as Robinson walks into the flames ahead.... then the camera fades and fireworks go off inside the arena and it's time for Hot Spot. The Camera switches to Brian Newark at ringside.] [Newark is wearing an INWI rugby shirt, has his head set on, wears wire rimmed glasses and is ready to go. He flashes the camera a smile and we are ready to go...] Brian Newark - Welcome back to hour number three of the best Monday Night Television Wrestling show! That's right, INWI Hot Spot. The show to watch on Monday nights. And if you missed Hour Number two, you missed a classic. King Apples and Tony Houston is a battle for a shot in the finals of the special $25,000 tournament as ordered by then commissioner Sam Adams. And now Adams gets to face King Apples in the finals! The tag titles will also be on the line in Hour number three, so let's get to the ring!! Match 9 All Americans vs Solid Gold This match could be one of the best tag matches of the year. Two of the top contenders for the INWI Tag Team Championship are in this match and Solid Gold, who has worn titles on five separate occasions in the INWI are looking to gain a little revenge for losing their straps to the Comforters. This could be awesome. There's the bell and we are set to go. Blake Gold and William Gahan will start things off in this match and they tie it up in the center of the ring. Blake with a slap to the face of Gahan and Gahan swats back. These two are exchanging fists and this one is going to be wild. Blake with a short clothesline and Gahan is down on the mat. Blake with an elbow drop and then quickly tags in Bradley. Bradley grabs Gahan by the hair and slams him head first into the turnbuckles. The Gold Brothers look ready to get back into the championship swing of things and I really think they want those titles back. I guess that goes with the name. Bradley sends Gahan back to the mat and then drops a knee, but Gahan moves and scoots over and tags in Brian Schaffer. Schaffer quickly mounts the top rope and nails a cross body block on Bradley, 1.....2......NO! Gold kicks out and Schaffer is back on him with a series of rights and lefts. Schaffer scoops up Bradley, places him up on the top turnbuckle and nails a superplex!! A cover by Schaffer, 1......2.....NO! Blake breaks it up and now all four men are in the ring going at it. These two teams are really starting to hate each other! The ref gets smacked by Bradley Gold and then they send Gahan over the top rope and to the floor. Schaffer in the ring and gets double clotheslines and Solid Gold is setting up. They are going for the Solid Fold Eliminator!!! Wait..... it's Thugs 4 Life!! OH MY GOD! And they just attacked Solid Gold and this is a pier six brawl!! DDT on Bradley and Blake is tossed out of the ring. Schaffer with a cover and the ref is coming to, 1.....2.....3!!! WHAT AN UPSET!! Here are your winners in 10:01, The All Americans!!! There is a brawl outside the ring and all three teams are going at it. Wow, what the coming months will hold for the tag division! Match 10 "The Vindicator" Vinny Vitrone (with Mr. Flamboyant) vs The Asswhupper (with Mama and the Playmaker) Well, here is another interesting one. The Asswhupper and Vinny Vitrone. "The Vindicator" is another member of the Brotherhood and it is no secret that these guys are wanted men. And look out for the fact that two of the Brotherhood supporters are my two co hosts of Inside The Ropes, Scoop Ropes and Riley Michaels. It's no secret that the Asswhupper is not to happy with them so I am sure he is looking to make an impression on them with a big win over Vitrone here. Tie up in the center of the ring and the Asswhupper smacks Vitrone and then backs up and laughs. The Asswhupper then turns to the crowd and sticks his hands in the air and laughs. Vitrone grabs his jaw and then attacks from behind and he is all over Asswhupper. Vitrone sends Asswhupper to the ropes and there's a spinning heel kick and that is followed up by a standing leg drop and a quick cover, 1.....2....NO! Asswhupper kicks out! Vitrone scoops up Asswhupper, sends him into the ropes again and misses a clothesline, Asswhupper catches Vitrone with a Hurracanrana and hooks the legs, 1.....2.....NO! Vitrone kicks out, but Asswhupper catches him and sends him into the ropes, diving forearm and Vitrone is down. Asswhupper out on the apron and springboards in to nail Vitrone with a splash. This is an exciting match! Asswhupper off the ropes and nails a leg drop and then a standing leg drop. Asswhupper picks up Vitrone and connects with a short armed clothesline. Asswhupper in charge of this match so far and as Vitrone gets up, Asswhupper nails a DDT!! A cover by the Asswhupper, 1......2.....NO!! Vitrone rolls a shoulder and Asswhupper picks up Vitrone. Vitrone sent into the corner, Asswhupper charges and Vitrone moves!! Both men are on the mat and they are both trying to get to their feet. Both are now up, Vitrone sends Asswhupper into the ropes, reversal, leap frog by Asswhupper and both men collide. Asswhupper staggers back off the ropes and Flamboyant grabs his foot! Asswhupper trying to get free and finally does but staggers into the arms of Vitrone.... Vindication Drop!!! A cover, 1.....2.....3!!! Wow, what a match. Here is your winner in 11:23, "The Vindicator" Vinny Vitrone!! Match 11 - Tag Team Championship The Comforters (with Dr. Bedpan) vs The Freaks (with Oz) The Comforters, the new INWI Tag Team Champions from the Final Countdown will defend the titles against the former Television tag team Champions. The Freaks, part of NBK with Jason Chandler, are looking to add the INWI Tag Titles to their group. And boy would that be interesting. We are all set as all four men are in the ring and we are ready to go. It's going to be Mattress and Freak O'Nature starting things off and they are going right at each other. Lefts and rights are flying and we all know the Comforters are always up for a good brawl. Mattress sends Freak to the mat and look out....big leg! What a two move combo there as he wasted no time in doing it. Mattress grabs Freak and sends him into his corner and he tags Wieland. Wieland charges Mattress and takes him down with a tackle and them starts to pound away on him. The ref is warning Wieland but he doesn't care. I guess as long as they make a statement they are happy. Boxspring in the ring and he rips Wieland off of Mattress and now all four men are in the ring. What is it with tag team matches tonight? Can't these people just wrestle? What ever happened to the days of good old fashioned wrestling? Good tag matches? They are out the window now a days. The ref separates the two teams and Mattress tags in Boxspring. Wieland and Boxspring tie up and Wieland takes control. We haven't heard much from the Freaks as of late, but they look like they don't have much by way of ring rust. Bouncing off the ropes is Boxspring and he charges at Wieland, but the big man keeps his ground. Once again...what the? Mattress in the ring and is on all fours behind Wieland. Boxspring charges and.... OH MAN!!! He just did a reverse bulldog on Wieland over Mattress. Wieland looks out and there's a cover, 1.......2.......Mattress nails Freak.....3!!!! It's over! Oh my, what a finish! Here are your winners in 8:24 and still INWI Tag Team Champions, The Comforters!!!! Match 12 - $25,000 Tournament Finals King Apples (with Mr. Flamboyant) vs General Sam "The Minuteman" Adams (with Militia) What a finals to this exciting tournament, in which the winner will win $25,000. But so far in this tournament, that money is not what has been highlighted..but instead the action.. First, we saw Chandler and Houston battle and Houston advanced to the second round to face his co-Brotherhood member, King Apples who advanced to the second round by defeating The Springer, then met Tony Houston in the second round, and which could be argued was the match of the year, in which "King" Apples actually pinned the INWI Heavyweight Champion, Tony Houston cleanly in a small package. So Apples, the Northeast Champion has earned his right to be in the finals. Against, Sam Adams, who practically gave himself a First Round Bye, then in the second round he defeated Ed Roger via count out when the lights went out and a sock possibly Smelly appeared when the lights returned and Ed took off towards the back and was counted out by the referee. Now we come to the finals..and when questions should be answered, it would appear if more questions have been asked...Will Tony Houston hold any animosity towards Apples, and will Houston break up The Brotherhood tonight? Will Pete Robinson make an appearance to attack Adams..Will Graves and Hammer come to attack as they appear to be heading into a feud with The Brotherhood..If the Brotherhood is still around after tonight's occurrences. Adams is in he ring waiting for Apples to make his appearance...Apples comes out from the back with Mr. Flamboyant at his side and is unusually walking towards the ring. He has his eyes centered on the chest of Adams in the ring and has a look like he wants to punch a hole straight through Adams...Apples climbs to the apron and steps over the top rope..Adams charges...Flying Bodypre--No!!! Unbelievable Apples just caught the former Commish...Apples presses Adams over his head and drops him throat first onto the top rope...Apples bounces off the near rope and drops a leg across the chest of the New York Champion. King Apples pulls Adams to his feet and sends him into the ropes and mauls over Adams with a vicious clothesline.. Adams staggers backwards and falls out of the ring. Militia is there, however, to roll Adams back into the ring..Apples over and pulls up Adams and scoops him...Tirt-A-Whirl Backbreaker on the New York Champion, the INWI Northeast Champion looks like a man possessed..Apples bounces off the ropes and splash Adams...Apples covers.. One..two..No!! The King is unable to hold Adams down. Apples back up quickly though and pulls Adams to his feet...Apples pushes Adams into the corner.. WOOOO Big Chop by Apples...and again..Apples sends Adams across the ring and charges..Right into a boot by Adams..Apples is staggering..Adams pulls himself to the second turnbuckle and leaps..Missle Dropkick by Adams dropping King Apples..well I guess you would say it's a Patriot Missle huh? Adams pulls up Apples...and connects with a Spinning Neckbreaker...Adams covers..One..two..No!!! This time it's the Northeast champion kicking out..Adams pulls up Apples and bounces off the ropes.. Single Arm DDT by the General...Adams is not going for a cover..he's going to the outside and climbs to the top turnbuckle..and leaps..Flying Elbow across the chest of Apples..Adams hooks both legs..One..two..t-No!!! Again Apples fights out of a pinning attempt. Adams with a boot to the ribs of Apples..and another..Adams bounces off the ropes..Double Jump Moonsault onto Apples that was a Great move by Sam Adams!!! Adams covers again..One..two..thre-No!!! Apples just barely rolled his shoulder. Adams pulls Apples to his feet and sends Apples into the ropes..Apples side steps a thrust kick and crashes into the ref...Uh oh..Anything can happen now....Adams turns and bounces off the ropes and crashes hard across the back of Apples...Militia is in the ring!!! This monster is in the ring, and he's going to help Adams win the $25,000 tournament...Militia pulls up Apples holds him..Adams bounces off the ropes and nails Apples on the jaw with a right hand...Wait!! Who's that now?!? It's "The Ringmaster" Tony Houston. And he's making his way down the aisle with an angered look on his face to say the least..Who's he here to attack? Apples? Apples did pin Houston's shoulders to the mat in the last round..Houston rolls into the ring..Militia is still holding Apples up...Houston says something to Adams..Has he just turned on The Brotherhood? Over money...I think he's telling him to hit Apples again...Adams bounces off the ropes with a right h-NO!!!! Houston scoops up Adams and spikes him headfirst with the Too Cold Driver!!!!!!! Apples just lifted a boot to the groin of Militia and he's doubled over..Apples falls to the mat..Houston grabs Militia..Cold Drop on Militia!!!! Houston is a wrecking crew in there..But is he going to attack Apples?? Houston looks down at Adams, at Militia and smiles...and then turns to Apples and that sinister smirk just disappeared off the Heavyweight Champion's face...Houston pulls up Apples...Wait he's talking to him..Apples is talking right back at Houston....Apples turns and scoops up Adams..and puts him into Spike Piledriver position..Houston climbs the second turnbuckle and leaps..SPIKE PILEDRIVER on Sam Adams!!!! Houston rolls Militia out of the ring, and exits the ring quickly himself..The ref's starting to move...Apples covers Adams and hooks both legs, needlessly...One...two..Three!!!!!! I don't believe it, Houston had most of the people in the building thinking he was going to attack Apples...Houston pulls up Apples and hands him his Northeast Championship belt, and the $25,000 check. This man has earned his name....Cold and especially calculating...I thought he was going to waffle Apples...Let's get the official word. Here is your winner in 15:22 and winning the $25,000 tournament...the Northeast Champion...."KING" APPLES!!! Brian Newark - Wow, what a final match to a great night on Hot Spot. No doubt Apples will be looking to rest tonight after that display and winning three matches for $25,000!
