Inside The Ropes Sunday, November 2, 1997 Brian Newark: Hello INWI fans again we present an ON TIME INSIDE THE ROPES report...This is a special show here tonight, as in we have as always, the man who is scared of Springer, Scoop Ropes. Scoop Ropes: Why should I be scared of Springer, he's a never will be, with a fluke title reign, I'm a retired superstar...and Springer has not even reached that ledgendary status such as myself..but enough about me...I'll bring out our new guest commentary, well, he seems familar but who knows...here is....I don't know what's his name, Secret Short Guy? [Out walks a man that stands about 5'5" and appears has a respectable build, he's sporting a reddish, black goatee, apparently dark blue eyes. However, as daper as this man apears, he's wearing a black and orange tiger mask. He walks in and sits right next to Scoop, and places a headset on.] Guy: Yo' Scoops first of all, it's Riley Michaels. Damn idiot. The only thing that seperates me from the rest of you INWI idiots..is that i'm the one doing the ass kicking, while you punks, get the ass kicking......INWI, ya better give your soul to the lord, cuz your asses are mine! ============================================================ Setting: Its night time, you can see the moon, and a few stars..it's peaceful, but then you hear the words of Tommy Graves. The lives that will be ruined in the path of Graves that i must dig, are not mine, i will feel no remorse. I cannot feel pity for stupidity, and that is exactly what you've all been, stupid. And anything can happen. Newark: True, in a way, it's true that when you face one member of The Brotherhood, you face them all. But in my opinion this man with some more experience could soon challenge for an INWI gold.. He shows him to the living room where they sit down on the couch. Below them is Vitrone's rug which is a nice dark green. . . but it even has a visible beer-stain. Rizzotto thinks who he is. . . well he ain't nothing. I've been in more gangs n' wipefd out more people than 'da immigrant can count. All I hafta say is "Bring Da' Pain" .now this interview is over [Vitrone gets up & pushes Newark out towards the door. Newark keeps muttering out how he "didn't get his answers". Newark exits. Newark than rings the bell..] Vitrone- Go away. . . or I'll tell "Brother" Bill Bob ta' bring Jake. ============================================================ Michaels: Hahahahha The newest member of The Brotherhood, Vinny Vitrone has put a scare into my co-host...To say the least, this guy has his own ideas on what a "hip cat" should be, however, I have seen this man before, and seen him almost transform from a raw and unexperienced rookie, to quite a capable grappler, and his membership in The Brotherhood only proves my point further...OH I see that Scoops is back.. Ropes: That damn Springer, who the hell does he think he is, damn fluke, now why should I fight with him, I"ve said it before and I'll say it again, I'm R-E-T-I-R-E-D , Retired Springer, can't you get that through yer thick, fluke title reign head.? Together we'll set the tag team world on FIRE! The Comforters are just the start.
