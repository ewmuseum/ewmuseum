[INWI] Winter Storm '97 Press Conference, Part 1, 12-27

30 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Winter Storm '97 Press Conference Press Room, Corestates Spectrum December 27, 1997 =========================================================== Brian Newark - Welcome ladies and gentlemen to the final pay per view of the year 1997 with the INWI. We have ahad a tremendous year here and we hope that it will continue in 1998. We of course are looking forward to March, which is the month that we will celebrate our one year anniversary. We will have a month long celebration and it will be a great time. Now then, today we have many of the INWI superstars who have come to face the media before their upcoming bouts tomorrow night. Let's go to our emcee for the days events. Commissioner Jerry DeLuise. DeLuise - Thank you Brian. Welcome members of the media and to everyone watching on FOX Sports. This is a great day for the INWI and what a pay per view this is shaping up to be. So many superstars will be here today and of course, when they go at it tomorrow. Now, let's get to the wrestlers! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Pig Pen =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [Pig Pen waddles to the podium and his eyes are wide open. He wears a belly shirt that says "jWo Icon" on it. On the back of the shirt it says "The Real INWI TV Champ." He comes to the podium and grabs the mic and sits down.] Pig Pen - Uh….hello. Reporter 1: Pig Pen, what do you think of being billed as the jWo Icon? Is it added pressure? Pig Pen - Sure. Why not? Reporter 2 - Pig Pen, the week you had as TV Champ had to be the best in your career. Do you think you’ll ever win gold here in the INWI again? Pig Pen - I think I have the talent to. If I get the chance again, I have to do it. That title meant the world to me. I let all of my fans down when I lost it. I also let down the jWo and for that, I am sorry. Reporter 3 - Pig Pen, this weekend, in front of millions of people, you face Jim Whitten on the Free 4 All. Any game plan? Pig Pen? Game plan? Reporter 3 - Yeah, you know, what are you going to do in the match? Pig Pen - Oh, I see. Well, most likely lose. I’m not that talented. I am a chunky, slightly over weight individual with a solid belly to belly suplex. I wish I was a little bit taller. But, if I can hit Whitten with my patented Belly to Belly…..look out, I may win. I hope I can win, my ma and pa will be here watching and cheering. This will be a great time for the whole Pig Pen family. Now, if you can please excuse me, I have to meet my mom and dad at the airport so we can go find an all you can eat buffet. [Pig Pen waddles off stage.] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Jim Whitten =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [Jim Whitten walks into the Press Conference room, flashes from camera's goes off as some people take pictures of the newest member of the INWI. He is wearing a Blue Wu-tang hoodie with the hood up over his Black Fubu hat, you can hear his Lugz boots hitting the ground as he walks. He has a bottle of Poland Spring water in his hand and takes a drink from it, he reaches the Podium, sets the water on it and says...] Jim Witten: First question. Reporter #1: Hi, Greg Apter from eWrestling monthly. Mr.Whitten we all know you are in N.E.W., and you have wanted to join INWI for a while now can you tell us what the difference between the two is? Jim Whitten: First off Apter this is a INWI Press Conference not a N.E.W., let me make this clear to all of you I don't want any questions about N.E.W.. But since I didn't make that clear when I came in here I will answer that but not in full so we can get on with the conference. Ok, there are differences between the two and they don't like eachother that's been proven, but the biggest difference would have to be the way each of the two feds have differen't stars and one of the two I won't say which one milks there so called "Star" every week. Next question. Reporter #2: Mr.Whitten, this will be your first PPV here in the INWI and you will face an "ICON". How will you prepare for this match? Jim Whitten: Well Pig Pen is a fat boy, although in my entire eWrestling career I have never faced a man as fat is Pig Pen, but I have been watching some tapes of men who have beat him and all of them see to have worked on one body part so that's what I will do. Also I have a new move to show all of you and when I hook it on Pig Pen he will tap out right away if he is smart otherwise I will break his neck and back. In other words come Sunday I will kick Pig Pen's ass! Next question. Reporter #3: Mr.Whitten do you feel you are ready to step up to the plate and handle the real big shot's of the INWI? Jim Whitten: Hell ya' I'm ready for em', I've been ready. But as you all know you have to make a name for yourself before you can even think about titles. I mean haven't you people seen me wrestle I am great and everyone know's it, I am 5-0 here so far, I know I can't win every match I wrestle but I can win alot of them and I want to win alot of Gold here. Seeing the people who I have beat so far dosen't mean nothing to me there jobbers, this match with Pig Pen will hopefully put me on the map here and then I will get the shot's that I want but untill then I'm just gonna work hard and beat the hell out of whoever get's in my way. Couple of more question's then I am done. Reporter #4: Hi, Kim Harris of eWrestler mag. There has been some rumors of after you reach your goal's in the INWI you will leave the fed are those rumors true? Jim Whitten: Honey, why do you think they are called rumors? No I would never leave the INWI unless I had a retirement match and lost or things just weren't working out then maybe I would leave. But things are going great here and I have been treated better here than any other fed I have ever been in. If you want rumors Kim why don't you come to my lockeroom after this conference is over and I'll give you all the rumors you need if you know what I'm talking about. Two more questions. Reporter #5: If you win you match against the "ICON" Pig Pen what are your plan's for the future in the INWI? Jim Whitten: What do you mean IF, of course I will win against that fat man. My plan's for the future in the INWI you ask, well I'll give them to you. After I beat Pig Pen hopefully I will get some big time matches here and win them so that it can put me into stardom here, but stardom doesn't come easy and I know that better than anyone so I'll just take it one match at a time. Last question. Greg Apter: Everyone know's that you have problem's with the Brotherhood and some wretlers in the INWI, when do you plan to take care of your problems? Jim Whitten: Figures Apter would be the last question and you got the first question also are'nt you lucky. But good question, first the INWI members that dumped me on my head. They will come but not in order I will be booked for a match with one of them and kick there ass and that's how I will get my revenge, I won't say give me all ten guys in a row because I would never beat all of them one after the other. Now about the Brotherhood well in my eyes they are the best I don't like to admit it but I speak the truth. Getting my revenge on Houston and Apples will be a task itself but I can do it but that won't come for a long time. Well I'm done, I'm gonna go have a smoke. The pleasure has been all yours. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Scoop Ropes/Riley Michaels =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [Scoop Ropes and Riley Michaels walk out to the podium. They have black shirts with yellow polk a dots on them.] Riley: Say hellow to the AmmmERICAN DREEEEEEEEEEAM! But, this ain’t the dubbea see dubbea. This is the INWI and this weekend Scoop, myself and an old friend are gonna do some fun fun…. Scoop - That’s right all of you jerkoffs, this six man match will go down in history as a great match as I will be 3-0 in the INWI. Reporter 1 - Who is your "mystery" partner? Riley - MORON. Scoop - If we told you, now it wouldn’t be a mystery, would it? Reporter 2 - Scoop, how can you claim to be undefeated in the INWI? You lost to Springer. Scoop - I did no such thing, I beat him to a bloody pulp. You were there Riley, tell ‘em. Riley - That’s what I saw. I saw you beat on that no good punk right away. That was beautiful man. Reporter 3 - Do you guys actually think you have a chance? I mean, come on….these guys will whip you. Riley - Uh…whatever. You forget, we have a third member. No one knows who it is. And it will be a surprise. Although, you have all seen him before. Scoop - Yep, he’s even been IN the INWI. That’s your only hint though. Anyways, we got things to do. See ya. [Scoop and Riley walk off stage laughing.] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Banana =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [Banana walks out to the stage in a pair of blue jeans and a white T-shirt with a Mobile home on it. He slugs a beer and gets to the podium.] Banana - Yo. Reporter 1 - Banana, you have a huge match this weekend. You face none other than Mr. Ed Rogers. A win here could get you a big title match. Your thoughts? Banana: Win or lose, in the booze. And I’m gonna kick some ass. Even if ol’ Ed wins, WHAMO… he’ll be a hurtin’ unit. Reporter 2 - You have also recently under gone a change in attitude. What’s the deal? Banana - Yo. Suck my ****. I am who I am and blow me if you don’t like it. Yer nothin’. Just remember, I like to kick ass. Win or lose, I’m kicking some ass and drinking some beers. Reporter 3 - So, you have no concern for titles? Banana - Nope. Reporter 4 - So, what’s the game plan for tomorrow? Banana - Kick ass. I’m gone. [Banana walks off the stage…] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= "Too Slick" Rick Brown =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Reporter: "Too Slick" Rick Brown, we haven't heard from you lately, why have you been so quiet?? Brown: I've been training a lot...The war with James Pillow took a lot out of me mentally, which is why I haven't been performing well lately...But Rick Brown is a refocused man and I'm ready to perform up to my capabilities, next question?? Reporter: Is there anyone in particular on "Too Slick" Rick Brown's list.. Brown: I'm itching to step in the ring with Pillow again..I beat him like his momma used to early in the series and I tapered off late...In order to do that, I have to climb the ladder again...I'll do it whatever it takes to become the best Light-Heavyweight in the INWI, next question.. Reporter: Brown what do you think of your opponent, Malice?? Brown: I really don't know much of him..I'll view some tapes and check out what he's made of...Life always I want a good clean match with me being the victor of course <small laugh from all parties>, next question.. Reporter: Mr. Brown we've recently seen you in other organizations, has that been the cause of your slump?? Brown: I don't think so, I like wrestling different wrestlers and being active...I don't enjoy the travel all the time but I'm currently just an East Coast wrestler...I faced Pillow on another organizations card the other night and he lucked out yet again, last question... Reporter: Rick, you always like to predict when you will take your opponent out..Any predictions.. Brown: Well like the saying say, a win is a win and it feel like heaven but Too Slick will be disappointed if he doesn't win at 7...minutes that is... (Brown leaves the table).. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Malice =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= <The door swings open, in walks Malice along with Sapphire. Malice is wearing a black shirt & pants and a long black leather jacket. Sapphire is in a skin tight shiny black leather outfit.> Reporter 1: Malice, what do you have to say with the recent interference in your matches from Riley and Scoop ? Malice: Well, it is more the obvious those two punks can't come at me face to face. First you cost just me a match, then you get me and Asswhupper counted out. The **** ends here, Asswhupper if you need a hand against these punks you know how to find me. Reporter 1: What about your match at the PPV against Too Slick Rick Brown? Malice: Well, seen as how I can promise Ccoop trying to pull some **** so I get a DQ or a countout. I plan to make quick and painful work of Rick Brown, It isn't personal it is pleasure. And Scoop I want you and your peon monkeyboy Riley to watch the match carefully. Picture yourself in the ring with me. And sign your names on the dotted line to get it done. Reporter 2: Who do you respect the most in the INWI? Malice: Good question, I'd have to say Cherry and Springer are the ones I respect the most. There are many fine wrestlers here and there are even more punks. The time has come to seperate them and get rid of the trash. Reporter 1: Sapphire if I may ask a question, what are your feelings on Scoop and Riley? Sapphire: Well, Since they are so close to each other, they do make a cute couple. But in all honesty Scoop couldn't beat me on his best day and Riley isn't worth the air he breathes. Malice: In closing, win if you can, survive if I let you. Fate has brought you to this point Rick Brown, tempt it and see what happens. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Cherry =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [Cherry walks to the podium with his mask on and his red hooded sweatshirt. He has ripped blue jean shorts on and a pair of black work boots on.] Cherry: Make this quick. Reporter 1 - Cherry, what is your involvement with Ultimate Perversion? Cherry: No comment. Reporter 2 - Cherry, there are rumors circulating on the fact Perversion is only the first to join "Cherry’s Orchard." How true are these rumors? Cherry: No comment. Reporter 3 - Cherry, why the sudden change? You used to strive on the fans and now you seem like you are a very troubled individual. Cherry: No comment. Reporter 4 - Cherry, you’re a former INWI Light Heavyweight Champion. You have a chance this weekend to claim the INWI New York Championship. How do you think you will fare against Sam "The Minuteman" Adams? Cherry: I, of course, will win the title. I want that title and will get it. Next question. Reporter 5 - What seems to be your problem Cherry? How come you are avoiding questions? Cherry: I avoid no questions. I have answered all of the questions asked of me. Just because my answers are not what you want to hear does not make them not answered. I am now leaving. Quote the Cherry every day… [Cherry exits through a curtain on the side.] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Sam "The Minuteman" Adams =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [Reporter:] Mr. Adams... [Sam "The Minuteman" Adams] Make it snappy, I have to be out of here by nine to catch my plane. [Reporter:] Plane, Mr. Adams? [Adams:] Yeah, I have a little business down Texas way to handle. It seems that a few old commie friends of the INWI, that I most selflessly deported, have resurfaced in a wrestling organization down there. This time, I'm gonna get rid of them for good. [Reporter:] I would like to hear what you think about Cherry, your opponent at Winter Storm. [Adams:] Well, first I thought he was a fruit, but then I reconsidered. You see, I believe that this country will only ever complete it's manifest destiny of controlling the world if we stop jumping to conclusions about people. I mean, sure, he hung around commies, he talks like a commie, he looks like a commie, but can we be sure that he is a commie? Of course not. That's why, Sam Adams, master of teaching by example is going to fight Cherry fair. [Reporter:] You are going to fight... fair? [Adams:] I promise. . . . [Adams:] Really. . . . [Adams:] Scouts honor. . . . [Adams:] Don't believe me, huh? Just wait, and you'll see. You'll see. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Cloud Eagle =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= <Cloud Eagle walks to the mike. He has a grim expression on his face. He is wearing the black t-shirt with him and Ultimate on it. Before he speaks, he rips the t-shirt off. With his free hand, he pulls out a container of lighter fluid and soaks the shirt. He drops it to the floor just a bit away from the mike. He pulls out a box of matches, lights one, and drops it onto the shirt, which instantly catches fire. As the shirt burns, Cloud moves to the mike.> Cloud: There will be no questions. Every answer is there. <He gestures to the fire.> What once was, is gone. You are DEAD Perversion. <Cloud Eagle turns and walks off stage as a collection of fans applaud at the back of the room.> =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The All Americans =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= A taped version of the All Americans. Shaffer - Solid Gold your days are numbered and the final stop is tomorrow. (The camera fades to black.) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Michael Bane =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= (The Master of Pain walks to the podium with the Light Heavyweight belt on his shoulder, and uproar of reporters suddenly rises as they yearn to question him.....) ['MoP'MB] "OK, shut the hell up right now you goddam leeches! I am not answering one single question from you moronic pissants...I have had enough of you all. Now down to business. I won this light heavyweight title on my own. Yeah...you can say that the Brotherhood of Butt-Pirates helped out, but the truth is they didn't. I was whipping Bill Bob Woijicowski's ass all over that ring...MY ring, to win MY belt! I had him screaming in agony from the minute that bell rang. I almost broke his goddam leg...and I would have too if the Brotherhood never came down. I tell you this right now..and all of you remember it. The entire Brotherhood....Apples, Vitrone, Houston and any other piece of crap they want to bring in....I will be there to inflict massive pain unto you.....WE will be there to scar you. The Brotherhood falls soon. The Revolution begins! The Final Solution will be brought upon all of you. I swear this to you all, that this belt will stay around MY waist at Winter Storm when I face David McCarter....that if the Brotherhood tries...even tries to interfere, I will castrate them personally......and I say now....the INWI will not be the same after Winter Storm '97." (Michael Bane leaves the podium as the reporter's jaws flap in the breeze...) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Jonathan Hammer =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= (The P.A. system erupts with "Good God" by KORN as Jonathan Hammer, "The King of the Extreme", enters the INWI media center. He is wearing a black Jonathan Hammer, INWI t-shirt (only 14.99!), black cut-off jeans, an Insane Klown Posse baseball cap, and black Grinders boots.) Hammer - Ask away all ya reporters. Reporter #1 - Excuse me Mr. Hammer, You face King Apples tomorrow, what are your comments? Hammer - First off don't call me Mr. Hammer. It makes me feel old. For christ sake I'm only 24. Now onto King Apples. What the hell is up with the "King" thing anyways? Maybe the fairy king. Apples, it all comes down to me and you tomorrow at Winter Storm "97". The Northeast title is on the line. But for once in my life it's more then the title. It's more then the money I make for wrestling. For once it's about hatred. Apples you've crossed my line. This match will come down to who hates each other the most. This match will go until one man can't fight anymore. Reporter #2 - Do you think the Brotherhood will try to save King Apples if he looks like he's losing? Hammer - I hope they try. Lately we've been seeing the INWI fight back. Hopefully if the Brotherhood show's their face then Graves, Bane, and Bledsoe will show up. Reporter #3 - What a minute didn't your feud with Bane and Bledsoe a while back? Hammer - Hey who says you can't forgive and forget? Bane and Bledsoe beat me fair and square and I beat them fair and square a few times. Bledsoe beat myself and Bane in the triangle cage match and I give him all the luck he needs against Jason Chandler. Reporter #4 - What did you think of your match up with the Springer two weeks in a row? Hammer - Springer is one of the best wrestlers in the INWI. He beat me fair and square two weeks ago and I beat fair and square this week. He's a former champion. I feel like my career in the INWI is on the up and up. With the momentum from that victory I feel I can take everything Apples has to offer. Now if you fine ladies and gentlemen will excuse me I've got some business to take care of. I'll see ya'll tomorrow. ("Good God" by KORN plays once again as Jonathan Hammer leaves the media center.) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= King Apples =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [Mr. Flamboyant and King Apples walk out on the stage for their Press Conference. King Apples defends his Northeast Championship his weekend against Jonathan Hammer.] Mr. Flamboyant: Hare ye, hear ye…. All rise and honor the most royal man in the INWI, King Apples. This weekend, King Apples will face a punk who thinks that it is his time to become the INWI Northeast Champion. Jonathan Hammer, that is not so. It is not your time. It will never be your time to beat King Apples. It is not time that Apples wants to get rid of the INWI Northeast Championship. You see you little pleb, you are nothing more than a cockroach. You are annoying and won’t go away. Well Hammer, this weekend, you will see why the Brotherhood is the most dominant force in eWrestling. King Apples: Mr. Flamboyant, as always, beautiful. This weekend, hopefully I will be able to get rid of that little cockroach Hammer. And hopefully at the same time, Tony Houston will rid us all of Tommy Graves. And hopefully these two will get the picture that they are punks and we will always come out on top. The deal is, we will walk in with three championships and we will walk out with three championships. I am going to beat Hammer and keep the Northeast Title. Houston will beat Graves and keep the Heavyweight Title. And Vitrone and Pillow will beat the Comforters and walk out still the tag team champions. It’s good to be great. Now then, make these questions quick, I got things to do. Reporter 1: King Apples, you are awful arrogant about this match. Shouldn’t you be a little worried? Hammer is a great wrestler and he has the talent to knock you off and win your title. King Apples: Talent? You call beating someone up with a chair talent? When is the last time you saw Hammer do a takedown? He is billed as the "INWI’s Most Extreme Wrestler." Does that tell you anything. He is far from talented. He is a no good bum. Reporter 2: King Apples, that may be so, but aren’t you a little afraid of that style? An extreme wrestler could surprise you and toss all sorts of things into the ring. If that happens, you may be in for the fight of your life. King Apples: Maybe so. But who cares? Hammer can toss all the crap into the ring he wants. I have swung chairs before. I’m a bigger man than he. I would squash his melon if I swung a chair at him. He is a little puss and I can’t wait to pound on him and drop him to the mat with the Drop. Reporter 3: King Apples, what about the rumors that there are troubles in the Brotherhood. We all know that in another fed, they are trying to get Pillow and Vitrone at each other. Add that in with the fact that you beat Tony Houston several weeks back and people are talking. King Apples: It’s all talk. Nothing more than talk. People like to yack their mouths and not know what’s going on. The Brotherhood is tight. We got rid of that no good hick. If he ever shows his face in the INWI, He’ll get more. The Brotherhood is an elite group. We have each others backs. If one person holds a title, we are all champions. We are close. We don’t let just anybody in. The Brotherhood is the best it has ever been and we don’t plan on coming down. Now, if you’ll excuse me, I have better things to o. Hammer, tomorrow yer done.
