[INWI] SPECIAL In The Studio, Aug 14

15 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
INWI - IN THE STUDIO August 14,1997 INWI Studios, Delhi, NY =========================================================== [Mr. Apples in the dressing room area of INWI Studios in Delhi, NY. He is lacing up one of his boots and looks ready to go. The camera comes in and Apples looks up and gets ready to talk...] Well, here we go. The semi-finals of the summer long Ringmaster. So far, this has been one hell of a tournament. Had some huge matches throughout the tourney. People that fans have been dying to see face eachother have. God knows I've had some hard matches. Not that I'm complaining, it just get's me ready for Jason Chandler. I've knocked off some big time guys in this Tourney. Current U.S. Champ Mike Garvey. Beat him. Former U.S. Champ P.O. Box. Beat him. The INWI Heavyweight Champion Brody Thunder, beat him. So as you can see, I've knocked off three solid champions and I don't plan on stopping. The Ringmaster belt and trophy, along with $50,000 are waiting for me. Two more wins and I get that. Now then, If I win and Tony Houston beats the Springer.... that leaves an interesting situation. Myself and Tony Houston going at it for the glory. Well Tony, friend or not, if we face eachother, I'm going for it all. But, that's in the future. We have to each win and that's the key. We both have to win. And it's not like we each have easy matches. We are in the final four of the Ringmaster, so there are four top notch wrestlers here. This won't be easy, but I have the confidence to make it to the finals and win. We'll see. Well, if you'll excuse me, I have a huge match to get ready for tonight. I'll be seeing ya! [With that, Apples walks away from the camera and starts to stretch out and warm up for his match tonight. The screen then fades to an INWI logo and then to an INWI In The Studio logo. The camera then opens up to Brian Newark at the announcers table.] Brian Newark - Welcome everyone to a special edition of In The Studio. Tonight we only have two matches for you and we think these will be some solid matches! I think that we are in for a solid night and by the end of the night, we will have the two finalists for the INWI Ringmaster. That pay per view is just 10 days away! Sunday, August 24 LIVE from the Fleet Center in Boston, MA! That card will be announced tonight along with the card for this weeks Monday Hot Spot!! We will also hear from all four people wrestling tonight before their matches, so without waisting anymore time, let's go to comments. [camera fades in on a large man and a beautiful woman walking around a fruit orchard. The woman is wearing a black form fitting dress with high heels. Her blonde hair is hanging long. The man is wearing an Armani Suit...He looks down at his Rolex, whispers something to the lady and turns around..] Chandler: Yeah I figured you guys would be following about now...I'm surprised you didn't come try to catch us at supper... Miss Sara: Remember hon? We told the bouncers not to let them in. Chandler: Oh yeah...heh heh...so what do you want out of me now? Do you want me to stand in front of your cameras and scream and yell and threaten my next opponent like is normal for a wrestling interview?? [cameraman stares at him dumbfounded...] Chandler: I don't think you know what you are doing, do you son? I tell you what, I'll show you... [Chandler grabs the camera and hands it to Miss Sara...] Chandler: Ok, Sara, you be the cameraman and I'll be the interviewer...this little camera boy here...you can be Mr. Apples... [camera man starts to run off, Chandler grabs him by the neck...] Chandler: I wouldn't recommend that. Ok. So Mr. Apples, what are you gonna do to Chandler when yall face off in the semifinals? cameraman: ...umm...(gulp)..i don't know.. Chandler: (slaps the cameraman) Damnit! You can do better then that...What are you gonna do when you meet Chandler in the ring,. Mr. Apples? cameraman: Try to win?? Chandler: God you're pathetic...even more so then Apples...This is your last chance...MR. APPLES...WHAT ARE YOU GONNA DO WHEN YOU MEET CHANDLER IN THE RINGMASTER SEMIFINALS? cameraman: (very weakly)...I'm gonna kick your butt... Chandler: You are? [Chandler grabs the cameraman and choke slams him down to the ground then throws him up against the tree..] Chandler: I'LL TELL YOU WHAT YOU ARE GONNA DO WHEN YOU MEET ME IN THE SEMIFINALS, MR. APPLES...YOU'RE GONNA GET YOUR ASS KICKED AND LOSE!!! [Miss Sara places the camera on the cameraman...Chandler grabs an apple, puts his arm around Sara and they stare down into the camera...] Chandler: Some people like to eat apples.... [Chandler squeezes apple till it explodes..] Chandler: I like to CRUSH them!! [Chandler kicks the camera and it breaks..picture fades...] Match 1 - Ringmaster Semi-Final Mr. Apples (With Mr. Flamboyant) vs Jason "The Crusher" Chandler What a match this will be. We will be down to the final three after this match and we will have our first finalist! This match could be crazy, that's for sure. Jason Chandler recently has been riding an unbelievable string of wins and I think that he is really ready for this match. Apples is still hot from his loss of the Television Championship, so this match should really be good. There’s the bell and we are underway. Apples takes control early and slams Chandler to the mat. Apples scoops up Chandler and sends him crashing to the mat again with a short armed clothesline. Chandler is trying to crawl out of the ring but Apples grabs him. Apples sends Chandler into the ropes and nails him with a big boot sending Chandler to the outside! Apples to the apron and clotheslines Chandler as he gets up! Apples with Chandler now, sends him into the ring barrier and then slams his head into the steel steps. Chandler is taking a beating so far in this match. Apples rolls Chandler back into the ring and follows him in. Apples slams Chandler in the maiddle of the ring and what the hell is he doing? Apples is going to the top rope! We don’t see this often! Apples leaps, leg drop from the top rope...NO! Chandler rolls out of the way and Apples crashes to the mat hard! Chandler back to his feet now and scoops up Apples and nails him with a couple of chops. Chandler starting to get the momentum now and climbs to the second rope and dives with a forearm that sends Apples to the mat. Chandler drops a standing leg drop then hits a couple of elbow drops. Chandler up and laughs at the fact that he is in control and turns around, Apples is back up!! Apples reaches out and grabs Chander by the neck and choke slams him over the top rope!! That was amazing! Apples follows out and Chandler goes low and Apples buckles over in pain. Chandler crawls away and slowly rolls into the ring. The ref is over looking at Chandler and see how he is doing. Apples walking along now and someone has his leg. Who the hell is under the ring? It’s Mr. Banana!! He slides out and he and Apples are face to face. Banana with a kick to the mid section that doubles over Apples. Banana grabs a wodden chair and smashes it over the back and head of Apples and then runs to the back. The ref starts his count and Chandler is getting up. 7...8...9...10!!! It’s over in controversial fashion! Your winner in 19:21 as a result of a countout, Jason “The Crusher” Chandler!! ========================================================== Brian Newark - Well the first match was just as we expected. There’s no doubt that this next match will be a war as well. Springer and Tony Houston. Both men are ready to go and one will advance to face Chandler at the 1997 Ringmaster. Let’s hear from each of them and go up to the ring. ========================================================== [The screen fades from a local tavern in which several drunken men began to slowly stagger from the entrance and make their way towards the car and enter it and attempt to begin their journey on a drunken voyage to only god knows where.] [The sounds of two men arguing are heard from the inside of the tavern so without hesitation the camera crew makes their way towards the rear of the pub where the disturbance is coming from. As the camera reaches the source of the argument is is determined that it is only one man talking loud with a shorter man sitting next to him.] [The loud man appears to have something wrapped around his waist...The man is dressed with slightly sagging black Tommy Hilfiger pants and the newest INWI T-Shirt that reads..."GOLD & CALCULATING" TONY HOUSTON" and on the back of the shirt it shows a close up picture of the Northeast Television Championship Belt.] Houston: [with a slight sound of drunkness which becomes more evident as Houston picks up a long necked Killian's Red] Well ya'll I see as I sit here preparing for my semi-final against Spring-ger. Son, I noticed that you felt the desire to make your way towards 'da ring last Monday and make en' appearance as I was punishing ol' Billy Bob. Well son, I told you...I told the entire INWI that if anybody...ANYBODY interfere or even show up during a "Cold & Calculating" match that they will feel the wrath of Tony Houston. And son, don't think for a minute that by me winning the title from that redneck will change things...Don't even thing that 'cause the fans are now cheerin' me instead of jeerin' me that my standard policy has changed son. I understand that you, Springer and the redneck have beef, but that beef is not to involve me....but you took it upon yourself to think it did. Well, son, now the Ringmaster Final slot is on the line and to say the least your ass is mine son.. [At this time Tiffany Love begins to make her way out of the ladies room and shocks the camera crew at her "new" appearance. Love is dressed with a short skirt that barely covers her thighs a tight black Daisy Duke type rolled up shirt and calf high black boots.] Love: Mr. Houston how do you fe- <HICCUP> feel about the possiblility about facing your apparently new friend Mr. Apples if both of you are victorious in tonight's matchups.. Houston: [Takes long swig of his longneck] Well, boo...To say the least it would be an interesting finals should both of us take tonight's matches..However, either way and regardless what happens one of the top men in the entire ewrestling world will take the title of the Ringmaster..Apples...Houston it's as good as edged in st-stone that one of the greatest combination in the world will take that title. As for the event that we face each other....Apples....it'll be me and you...All business nothing personal we'll let the best man win..then kick back afterwards and get TWISTED....But for the time being...Springer....may the best man win...... Love: And what are you prediction for the match? Houston: Damn..Ms. Love you may look GREAT but you aren't all that bright are you....Anyway, a prediction..hmm..well that's simple...May the best man win....and Springer..I have every intention of doing so..... [Screen fades as Houston gets slapped for trying to hit on and grab at Tiffany. He smile and shakes his head as he stumbles out the door looking for another tavern...] ========================================================== Tiffiny Love with Springer TL: It's the semi-finals of the Ringmaster tourney and your still around. Any predictions? Springer: Only one, Bill Bob if you come near my match, or cost me a match, what happened in the past is in the past, I will break you and make you pay with pain. TL: The two of you where close friends what happened? Springer: I guess that the help I gave that little runt was not enough for him. I can understand how he feels. It's tough to be in the shadows of the Springer, but I never asked for his help. And now he wants to get into it with me. You listen to me little man, LEAVE ME ALONE OR YOU WILL PAY!!!!!! TL: Do you think he may cost you the Ringmaster? Springer: I'm done talking about him, as for you Tony Houston, remember that no one has ever escaped from the coil. When I slap that on you BAM your done. Hey Tiff, you tell that Scoop Ropes punk if he wants some of me I'll break him to. If he has something to say to me say it to my face. The only wuss in this fed is that little punk. See Ya in the ring. WHOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!! =========================================================== Match 2 - Ringmaster Semifinal “Cold and Calculating” Tony Houston (with Mr. Flamboyant) vs The Springer (With Dr. Bedpan) As we said before, this one has the makings of being a great match. Either of these two men could win it as well as Chandler, so the Ringmaster is still up for grabs! Springer was the first man to beat Brody Thunder and if we’re not mistaken the only person with a true clean win over him. Springer is already guarenteed a title shot at Thunder soon, but winning the Ringmaster would give him a second if the first should fail! There’s the bell and we are underway! The two men tie up in the middle of the ring and we have a stalemate. The two warriors back off and tie up again. Houston with a knee to the mid section of Springer and he takes control. DDT! A quick cover, 1...2...NO! Wow, amazing that he even got a two count there already. Houston picks up Springer, sends him into the ropes and puts him to the mat hard with a diving lariat. Houston follows that up with some elbows and knees and then scoops up Springer. He just slammed the Springer to the mat and goes to the second rope. Houston just conneceted on a knee from the second rope. Houston helps Springer back up and sends him into the ropes and clotheslines the Spriner over the top rope! Houston goes to the apron and dives at Springer, NO! Springer moved and Houston crashes to the arena floor! Springer slowly back to his feet, grabs Houston and sends him into a ring post. Spriner after him again and sends him into the ring barriers and then rolls Houston back into the ring. Springer right away to the top rope and nails Houston with a diving clothesline! This is a great match so far. Springer grabs Houston, oh my, Houston with an eye gouge and SPringer stumbles back. Houston with a clothesline and Springer goes down. Houston in a corner trying to catch his breath. Both men are whipped so far. Houston grabs Springer, sends him into the ropes... double clothesline and the ref got hit as well! All three men in the ring are down! Springer looks like he took the least of it and is slowly getting to his feet. Wait, why all the stirring in the crowd... Bill Bob Woijicowski is out here! He has a chair and smashes it over the head of Springer as he stands up. Springer crumbles to the mat and Bill Bob runs off. Houston to his feet and the ref is moving as well. Houston up and has no clue what just happened. Look out, he just tripped and fell on the Springer. The ref is there. 1....2....3! Oh my god, now that was crazy. Your winner in 19:04, “Cold and Calculating” Tony Houston. I don’t think Houston even realizes it yet.. =========================================================== Brian Newark - Well ladies and gentlemen, we have our two finalists! This should be a great final and we will have our first ever RIngmaste crowned! Also, we saw Tiffany Love packs some pop in her slap... just ask Houston! But remember wrestlers, Tiffany may look great, but she is married! The pay per view line up will be out a little later today. So, until next time, I am Brian Newark for the INWI saying so long! =========================================================== =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The INWI™ "The revolutionary force in E-Wrestling" Visit us on the Internet: http://www.catskill.net/harmer For info, E-mail: [detail withheld] INWI®™, August 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
