[INWI] Hot Spot, 12-22, Hour 3 continued

30 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[After the commercial comes back, Cherry is sitting in a dark corner. Ultimate Perversion is no where to be found. Cherry sits with his head pointed down. He has on his red hooded sweat shirt and a pair of ripped jean shorts as well as his boots on.] Cherry: The waiting game is over. Cloud Eagle, you went against a beloved trust and hurt Perversion. You maimed him. You injured him. You destroyed him. But you did not kill him. You did not bury him. There is no ashes to ashes or dust to dust. Perversion is still alive. He is kicking. He is revived. He is ready Cloud Eagle. One week away you will see, he is ready. The words of wisdom that you should heed are that of me. You should fear us and if you don't already, you will. Cloud Eagle, you have done very wrong. And in the long run, you will pay. You will hurt and you will feel the pain. You will see what you made Perversion go through. You will see what The Orchard is all about Eagle. Heed my advise. Quote the Cherry.....everyday.
