[INWI] Monday Night Hot Spot!! 4-28

29 April 1997

INWI Monday Night Hot Spot!! ========================== Monday, April 28, 1997 Club Boomers, Oneonta, NY ============================================================ Brian Newark - Welcome ladies and Gentlemen to Monday Hot Spot!! We have but one match on the card for you tonight, but the wqinner will recieve the INWI Northeast Title, which was recently stripped of "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder when he won the Heavyweight Title. Here are the participants for this 20 man, over the top rope, battle royal. Commissioner Jerry DeLuise would not allow champions or bonafide Tag Teams into this match. In the Rumble - "The Icon" Pimp Daddy Brown, "The Hollywood Kid" Jason Starr, PO Box, Jimmy Cougar, Mr. Ed Rogers, Mr. Banana, "Marvelous" Mike Quick, Apocalypse, Michael Hollywood, The Springer, Bill Bob Woijicowski, Firestar, Jason "The Crusher" Chandler, "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset, Bodyguard Jones, Psycho Sal, Harvey Thunder, Steven Casper, Ultimate Perversion, Cloud Eagle. ============================================================ MAIN EVENT 20 MAN BATTLE ROYAL INWI NORTHEAST TITLE Here we go and this is going to be wild!! All 20 men are in the ring and there's the bell. 20 men just pounding on eachother looking to claim the gold that was stripped of Brody Thunder. You know certain people are going to go to eachother in this match and that is evident because PO Box and the Glamour Boys are going at it and Ultimate Perversion and Cloud Eagle are locking them up! People are just pretty much feeling eachother out right now but you can't help but to wonder who is going out first! Jason "The Crusher" Chandler goes right after Jonathan Sunset, who is a former Northeast Champion! Apocalypse just chokeslammed Steven Casper and he is being stomped on the mat! Whoa!! Someone just got tossed out of the match and let's see who it is... Oh that's to bad, Bodyguard Jones was tossed out by Psycho Sal at 3:21 and they are still mouthing eachother. Here comes Mr. Banana and he clotheslines Psycho Sal over the top from behind! Sal is out at 3:34!! Sal and Jones are going at it on the floor and the officials are seperating them. Those two have not so nice feelings for one another! Back in the ring, The Glamour Boys are pounding PO Box and I don't know how long he will last with this beating. Bill Bob Woijicowski comes over and whacks Jimmy Cougar and stumbles him backwards. Woijicowski got one of the Glamour Boys off of Box, but Box is still on the mat. The Springer has Sunset backed into a corner and trying to get him over the top. Mr. Ed Rogers comes over and nails Springer then tosses Sunset at 5:22. Rogers and Springer then start trading blows and the Springer has Rogers backed into a corner! Apocalypse and Mr. Banana are going at it in the center of the ring and these two big men can really punch! Jason Chandler and Michael Hollywood are also going toe to toe, but Harvey Thunder just jumped Chandler and Thunder and Hollywood are doing a number on Chandler. Oh my, Steven Casper just got whipped out of the ring hard by Apocalypse at 6:47. The former heavyweight Champion, Pimp Daddy Brown, is getting jumped by everyone. I guess they figure the person that tosses him out will make a name for themselves. Now "Marvelous" Mike Quick and Bill Bob Woijicowski, the two smallest wrestlers in the INWI, are going at it. Firestar comes over and grabs them both by the neck and hits a double chokeslam!!! Firestar sends Woijicowski over the top rope at 7:59 and Quick goes over the top courtesy of Pimp Daddy Brown at 8:01. This is really turning out to be a great match as everyone still in there can smell the gold. Man, Ultimate Perversion and Cloud Eagle are still going at it. These two have been beating eachother since the opening bell. Perversion has Cloud Eagle up against the ropes and is trying to put him over, but Eagle's balance is amazing. Wait a minute, Rogers is charging and clotheslines both men and they both crash to the floor at 9:32. We now have 12 men left in the ring and they are all getting more and more nasty with eachother. Both Glamour Boys are ontop of P.O. Box again and I have not figured out how PO Box has lasted this long in the ring. Harvey Thunder and Michael Hollywood are still working on a team as they have Apocalypse backed into a corner and are going at him. They each grab a leg and send him over the top and Apocalypse is out at 12:45. Look out though as they turn around there stands Firestar and Mr. Banana!! These four start exchanging blows in the corner and the two big men are taking control. Firestar has Thunder ready and he presses him and throws him to the floor!!! Thunder is out at 13:21. Banana and Hollywood are going at it and Firestar comes over and hits a double nogin knocker and sends Banana over the top at 14:01. Hollywood pokes Firestar in the eye and slows him down. Pimp Daddy Brown and Rogers are going at it and the Springer jumps in. We only have nine people left in the ring and we have just passed 18 minutes in the match! It has been over four minutes since our last man went out of the ring. PO Box is finally on his feet and he is going at it with Jimmy Cougar and Jason Starr still!! Jason Chandler and Michael Hollywood are exchanging blows and Chandler is taking control of the situation. Chandler has the Crush set!!! Hollywood is screaming in pain!! Chandler picks him up and tosses him over then stars to wipe his hands like it was nothing! Hollywood is out ay 19:56! Chandler turns around and there is Firestar!! It's the Burned Star!! Firestar then sends Chandler over the top at 20:19. Look out below!! Jimmy Cougar just landed in front of me as PO Box got rid of him at 22:34 and now he is all over Jason Starr! Ed Rogers is looking at Pimp Daddy Brown and Firestar brawling on the ropes and he attacks and sends the former Heavyweight Champion over the top at 23:47. We are now down to five wrestlers left! Firestar, PO Box, Ed Rogers, Jason Starr and The Springer. Firestar has the Springer and Chokeslams him to the mat and drops a leg. Ed Rogers then clotheslines Firestar over the top at 25:11. Rogers then picks up Springer and nails the Puppet Master!! He tosses the Springer out at 25:46. Jason Starr has PO Box in the Stralock and he is getting woozy!! Starr just throws Box over the top at 26:34. Box looks up and trys to get back in the ring, but the officials are holding him back. Starr is gabbing at hi and flips him off! He comes Rogers and nails Starr from behind and sends him to the floor!!! Box is now laughing as he heads back to the lockerrom! Your winner in 27:03 and NEW INWI Northeast Champion, Mr. Ed Rogers!!!! Rogers is sitting down in the ring with Smelly and his new belt and looks like he is crying! What a great Battle Royal!! Below are the quick results of the battle royal! Eliminated Eliminated by who Time Bodyguard Jones Psycho Sal 3:21 Psycho Sal Mr. Banana 3:41 Jonathan Sunset Mr. Ed Rogers 5:22 Steven Casper Apocalypse 6:47 Bill Bob Woijicowski Firestar 7:59 Mike Quick Pimp Daddy Brown 8:01 Ultimate Perversion Ed Rogers 9:32 Cloud Eagle Ed Rogers 9:32 Apocalypse M.Hollywood/H.Thunder 12:45 Harvey Thunder Firestar 13:21 Mr. Banana Firestar 14:01 Michael Hollywood Jason Chandler 19:56 Jason Chandler Firestar 20:19 Jimmy Cougar P.O. Box 22:34 Pimp Daddy Brown Ed Rogers 23:47 Firestar Ed Rogers 25:11 Springer Ed Rogers 25:46 P.O. Box Jason Starr 26:34 Jason Starr Ed Rogers 27:03 ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well, that about wraps it up from Oneonta at Club Boomers! Mr. Ed Rogers is our NEW INWI Northeast Champion and here is the tentative line up for the Studio Show on Thursday! MAIN EVENT U.S. Title P.O. Box vs "Hardcore" John Stilwater TV Tag Titles The Freaks vs Friendship Express Special Tag Team Match The Glamour Boys vs Contrast And the INWI is trying to hopefully set up a special interview with the NEW INWI HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION, "LONE WOLF" BRODY THUNDER!!! We'll see you Thursday!!! ============================================================ This was a Presentation of the INWI, April, 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= INWI Wrestling.....
