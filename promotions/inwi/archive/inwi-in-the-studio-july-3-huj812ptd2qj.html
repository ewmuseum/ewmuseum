INWI - IN THE STUDIO July 3,1997 INWI Studios, Delhi, NY ============================================================ [The scene fades from a Fry the Insanity and returns with a pulled back shot of the INWI Studios in local Delhi, New York. As of later tickets for INWI events have been extrememly hard to come by and this time is no exception. The ticket window is shut and a sign on the "door" reads "SOLD OUT". Many irrate fans are standing in front of the window, pondering their various options of how to enter the "revolutionary force"'s card.] [As some of the ticketless INWI fans begin making their way back towards the parking lot a man wearing a black shirt that reads Why Yes. I am a Mark approaches them.] Guy: Ya'll need tickets? I have front row for $400. Watcha need? [Howerver, this man approached the wrong person, as one of the INWI fans is a policemen and the scalper is now being read his Miranda rights.] [The scene returns to the inside of the INWI Studio, and yet again, it's a standing room only crowd, the INWIites are going insane and taking advantage of the 1st Amendment and holding a large amount of various signs..."Apples will Peal Banana" "Houston is a Bum" "Thunder Bulldozed Riley" and almost tons of others.] [The crowd begins chanting IN-WI IN-WI IN-WI] [The positive chants for the INWI quickly turn into booes as a man begins making his way towards the ringside area. This man is dressed in a long, black, leather trench coat, with, in Crimson Red, scripture reads FATAL ALLIANCE. This man is wearing a black sunglasses, and black Polo pants, with Steel toed boots. He climbs the ring stairs and grabs the microphone from the ring announcer.] Man: Ladies and gentlemen, and I use BOTH those terms loosely. I've decided to treat all you Delhi-ians with a glimpse of "Cold & Calculating" before I drop ol' Mikey boy on his throat. [Crowd booes and some begin a Houston sucks chant.] Houston: [in a calm tone] #$%$ Ya'll. Ya'll act as if I like being in this poor excuse of a city. Christ..this shit ain't no city...ain't even a village....it's barely a town. But that's another matter, and ya'll who live here know that already [Crowd booes even LOUDER] Now, tonight, I've been scheduled to take on "Marvelous" Mike Quick..Well, some of that name does have some validity. Quick, you claim to be the fastest man in all of wrestling, shiznit, I ain't gonna dispute that, but how Quick will you be to change your name to "Marvelous" Mike Ass Whooped, when I Quickly drop you on your friggin' throat with the Cold Drop. But Quick, I have had no beef wit you...That is until you put your name on the dotted line, tonight I ain't in no mood for talking..I'm in the mood to do a lil' ass whoopin' and since I can't get no damn champion...You'll have to do..So boy, while these other insignificant matches are taking place, you sit in the back and try to keep convincing yourself that you are marvelous 'cause boy, when you step into MY ring, you will be left counting lights on your back, clutching at your throat, right after I punish you with the Cold Drop. [Houston begins to leave the ring, but stops in the corner] Oh snap, if you were thinking that the 'Dozer wasn't in the arena just 'cause he ain't out here, now...HAHAHA Don't be so damn ignorant, ya'll. If I'm in an arena you can be restassured that the Fatal Alliance is in the Hiiizzzouse...So Quick I'll be pounding on yourself, in a short time, ya'll could say...QUICKLY. [Houston throws the microphone at the ring announcer and leaves the ring and welcoming all the booes from the sold out INWI Studio. As Houston is about to enter the lockeroom he stops grabs a "Houston Sucks" sign and rips it then inserts both middle fingers into the fan who was bearing the sign.] Brian Newark - Welcome ladies and Gentlemen to In The Studio! We are still hot off of our FIRST ever pay-per-view Heat Wave '97! And what a PPV is was.... "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder survived the biggest match of his INWI championship reign when he beat "The Technician" Zack Sharp, thanks in part to Mr. Ed Rogers entering the ring and nailing Sharp with a chair. I guess Ed really wants that Northeast Title back. We also saw the crowning of our first ever Light Heavyweight Champion, Sam "The Minuteman" Adams. Tonight we have a great card for you planned including the Main Event... for the INWI Tag Team Championship... Solid Gold looks for the belts as they take on the defending Tag Team Champions, The Terminators. We also have three other great matches so let's go up to the ring!! Match 1 Mr. Ed Rogers vs Harvey Thunder Mr. Ed Rogers, who essentially cost Zack Sharp the Heavyweight Championship this past weekend, has a tough matchup with Harvey Thunder. Thunder came to ringside with Michael Hollywood and he is looking for a big push lately. He has a good record and really wants a title. BUT, he and Hollywood have been all tied up by Solid Gold. The two tie up in the center of the ring and Rogers takes Thunder down to the mat and has him in a chin lock. Thunder works his way to his feet and nails Rogers in the midsection with an elbow. Thunder off the ropes and sends Rogers to the mat with a shoulder block! Thunder again off the ropes and he drops an elbow on Rogers, 1...2...NO! Rogers kicks out, is on his feet and he clotheslines Thunder to the mat. Rogers scoops up Thunder and sets him up for a suplex.... he's got him up.. and yes! Drops the suplex! Rogers off the ropes and dives with a headbutt! Rogers to the outside and to the top??? He leaps and just nailed Thunder with a leg drop off the top rope!! 1...2...NO! Thunder kicks out and as Rogers looks down at Thunder, Thunder pokes Rogers in the eye and rolls away to safety. Thunder helps himself up and as soon as Rogers gets up he charges with a clothesline! Thunder with a standing leg drop and the cover, 1...2...NO! Rogers kicks out! Wait a minute, here comes Solid Gold and they are screaming at Hollywood and Thunder. Thunder turns to them and Rogers garabs Thunder and hits a reverse DDT! 1...2...3! Oh my god! Solid Gold has caused Thunder a chance at winning this match. Your winner in 13:47, Mr. Ed Rogers!!! Match 2 Bodyguard Jones vs Mr. Banana Mr. Banana, who recently turned his back on the fans and on his longtime best friend Mr. Apples, is up against a tough Bodyguard Jones, who finally rid himself of Psycho Sal and his former stable at the pay-per-view. There is no word if Mr. Apples is here tonight and I personally have not seen him, so we'll see. These two have not seen eachother since the pay-per-view when Banana was unmasked. Banana and Jones lock them up in the middle of the ring and Jones takes the power control early and sends Banana to the mat. Jones scoops up Banana and slams Banana to the mat and drops an elbow. Banana looks rusty from not wrestling ina while and as we said about Jones, he is on an upswing. He could be looking at a title shot of some sorts really soon. Jones up with Banana, sends him into the ropes, down for a back body drop, but Banana kicks Jones in the face and sends him backwards. Banana goes right after Jones, scoops him up and slams him to the mat. Banana picks up Jones and delivers a big right hand that again sends Jones to the mat! Banana to the outside and he climbs to the top... Jones is up... and Banana comes down with a double ax handle that sends Jones back and he runs into the ref and the ref is down! Banana nails a swinging neckbreaker on Jonesand he is setting up for an Applesauce Drop? Wait a minute, here comes Mr. Apples!! Apples is in the ring and he just clotheslined Banana!!! He sets up, the Applesauce Drop!! Apples places Jones on top of Banana and he runs off.... the ref is up, 1...2...3!! It's over!! Your winner in 11:58, Bodyguard Jones!! ============================================================ DARK MATCHES Jonathan Hammer pinned "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset in 5:23 David McCarter pinned Dell Pickle in 3:21 James "Simply The Best" Pillow pinned Pig Pen in 5:23 Jason "Crusher" Chandler defeated Mr. Cherry by Countout The Comforters beat Contrast in 11:21 "Too Slick" Rick Brown pinned Senator Bradley in 6:31 "Bulldozer" Tony Riley pinned Jerrid Hoss in 1:46 Cloud Eagle pinned Oz in 5:42 Ultimate Perversion beat Steven Casper in 6:21 'Jammin Jimmy Cougar pinned "The Big Fan" Devin Terry in 6:21 Vito Rizzotto and The Springer fought to a 10 minute draw ============================================================ Match 3 "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston vs "Marvelous" Mike Quick This match is big for both people involved. A win will propell the other farther up in the ranks. Quick lost a big shot at the Northeast TV Title this past weekend when he lost to champion Bill Bob Woijicowski in a vicious cage match. Houston enters the ring and he attacks the smaller Quick right off the bat. Rememeer, Quick is under 200 pounds and Houston is up around the 265 mark. Houston needs to keep Quick grounded in this match. Houston attacks right off the bat and he grabs Quick and slams him to the mat. Quick pops right back up and bounces off the ropes and drop kicks Houston to the mat. Quick again bounces off the ropes and hits a spinning heel kick on Houston. Quick with a cover and barely a one count. Quick back off the ropes and once again drop kicks Houston and Houston rolls out of the ring to gain his composure. Houston is talking to Riley outside the ring and Quick is to the top rope, he leaps...RILEY CAUGHT QUICK!! And Riley slams Quick on the outside. Houston starts to kick at Quick and he grabs him and rolls him back into the ring. Houston back intothe ring as well and he starts to kick at Quick. Houston up with Quick and he presses Quick!! There's the slam! Wow, Houston just press slammed Quick... that was impressive! Houston with a cover, 1...2..NO! Quick rolls a shoulder and Houston is up in the refs face! Quick up, bounces off the ropes and drop kicks Houston into the ref. Look out...here comes Ultimate Perversion!! Perversion just waffled Riley with a chair and the big man crumbles to the mat! Quick has Houston and Perversion is going to nail Houston! Perversion swings, Houston moves and Perversion nails Quick! Houston then smacks Perversion, takes the chair and nails him! Houston looks at Quick, scoops him up, there's a cold drop! The ref is up... the cover, 1...2...3!! MAIN EVENT Match 4 INWI Tag Team Championship The Terminators vs Solid Gold This match could be a great one folks! Solid Gold, the three time Television Tag Team Champions, take on the reigning Champions, The Terminators. The Terminators this past weekend retained their titles by beating the Big Brothers in a huge rematch. Now Solid Gold is looking to get their hands on the bigger of the prizes. It's going to be Blade and Blake Gold starting this match off and there's the bell. The two come to the center of the ring and tie up. Blade takes early control, sends Blake into the ropes...back body drop..NO! Blake stops and nails a swinging neckbreaker. Blake scoops up Blade, sends him into the ropes and hits a dropkick! Blake off the ropes and drops a leg then scoops up Blade and hits a suplex. Blake tags in Bradley. Bradley with Blade, into the turnbuckle, Blade moves, rolls over and tags in Gunner. Gunner charges Bradley and Bradley goes with the momentum and powerslams Gunner. Bradley with a cover, 1...2...NO! Almost new champions! Solid Gold has dominated this match so far! Bradley scoops up Gunner, sends him into the rope and hits a back body drop...to the top goes Bradley...he leaps and nails a splash! 1...2...NO! Blade comes in and makes the save! Bradley back up and he just DDT'd Blade! Bradley tosses Blade over the top rope! Now Solid Gold has Gunner and they are setting up for the Solid Gold Eliminator! YES! They just hit it and we are going to have new champions! Bradley goes for the cover, 1....2....NO! Michael Hollywood just got into the ring and he broke it up! There's the bell! Harvey Thunder just came down to ringside and there's a brawl between Solid Gold and Hollywood/Thunder going on! These guys have no love lost for eachother!! The Terminators grab their belts and head to the back and Solid Gold bails from the ring as well. They are just staring at eachother... then Solid Gold leaves to the back. Your winners in 12:01 as a result of a disqualification, Solid Gold. The Terminators retain their titles! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well, that's it from Delhi, NY! We'll see ya Saturday for our special card in Landover, Maryland! That will be a big bang card at The US Air Arena! For the rest of the INWI, I am Brian Newark saying so long! Don't forget to fill out your surveys and Role Call and SEND THEM IN!! See ya this weekend! ============================================================ SPECIAL CARD July 5, 1997 US Air Arena, Landover, MD MAIN EVENT INWI Heavyweight Championship "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder vs Jonathan Hammer Light Heavyweight Championship Sam "The Minuteman" Adams vs Mr. Cherry Northeast Championship "The Technician" Zack Sharp vs Mr. Ed Rogers United States Championship Mike "The Gavel" Garvey vs P.O.
