[INWI] Hot Spot, 10-13, Hour 2

16 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Hour two opens at the Home of INWI Commissioner Jerry DeLuise. He is sitting in his chair watching Monday Hopt Spot as the camera zooms in on him. DeLuise is in an INWI sweatshirt and a pair of sweats. ] Brian Newark - Commissioner DeLuise, first off, let me ask you how are you feeling after the attack of Sam Adams? DeLuise - Brian, don't worry, I am fine. Nothing can keep me down and I will be on hand at Hot Spot next week. My doctor just recommended that I stay home this week. Newark - Ok, then let's get to the news. What is this blockbuster announcement you are making? DeLuise - Well Brian, it is like this. The INWI is a regional federation, based in the Northeast. Therefore, I have some things to chat about regarding Samuel Adams. From this day forward, he is no longer the INWI United States Champion. <crowd POPS> We at the INWI have decided to take the INWI title and make it the New York State Title. Sam, of course, will be the first New York Champion. I know this will displease him, but he will have to live with it. Furthermore, for his blatant disregard of the rules and respect, he is being fined $5,000 dollars. More action is pending. But I have taken enough time, please Brian... return to the matches!!! [The camera fades away from DeLuise to a surprised Newark, who is stunned!] =========================================================== Newark - Welcome fans to hour number two of Hot Spot and what an announcement. That was something. Let's head back up to the ring for our next match. =========================================================== Match 5 King Apples (with Mr. Flamboyant) vs Fuego Apples is looking just massive tonight. He is looking at Fuego and pointing. Fuego doesn't look a bit scared. Apples charges and Fuego moves and he hits the buckles hard. Fuego with a dropkick inthe corner. Then a dropkick to the right knee, a dropkick to the left knee, a dropkick to the head, and finally a dropkick to the chest and Apples slumps down to the canvas. Fuego is just lightning quick. Fuego back up to a vertical base. But unfortunately so is Apples. Fuego goes for a dive down low on Apples but he is just caught. Apples with a press slam and oh my god, he just threw Fuego into the crowd. Where have I seen that before? Oh well, Fuego looks dazed but he is going under the ring and Apples didn't see it. He is looking for Fuego on the outside and here Fuego comes on the other side with a chair. He is climbing the ropes, and he nails Apples in the back of the head with a chair. Apples stumbles into the ropes and Fuego throws the chair at him and Apples catches, throws it right back, and it nails Fuego right in the head. Apples is over Fuego now and he hits a thunderous legdrop, cover 1........ 2.......and Fuego kicks out. Apples runs into the ropes and goes for an elbow but misses and Fuego is up and running. He goes into the ropes and springboards off with a triple jump moonsalt, 1..........2...and Apples takes Fuego and just throws him off of him. Apples is back up and he is angry. But Fuego has a chair again. Apples catches Fuego in a bearhug and is wrenching it on. Fuego hung onto the chair however. Fuego is hitting Apples in the head with the chair while he is in the bearhug. This is unbelivable. Apples is just absorbing the blows and refuses to let go of the hold. Fuego now has the chair and is putting it between him and Apples and is trying to squeeze his way out. He is free but Apples with a monsterous boot to the face and Fuego is just a pile of mush on the mat. Apples picks him up and puts him in the bearhug again and this time Fuego doesn't have a chair. He just might black out here. Apples is shaking him while he is in the hold and Fuego's eyes are rolling in the back of his head. He appears to be out and Apples is loving every minute of this you can tell. The ref picks up Fuego's limp arm, 1!!!!!, he picks it up again, 2!!!, he picks it up one more time, if it drops this match is over, 3no!!!!! Fuego got the hand up and Apples breaks the hold and Fuego drops to the ground. Fuego with a low blow and Apples screams in pain. Fuego is getting some life with some support from this crowd. Fuego with right hands to the face, another, another, and another. Apples looks out on his feet but he refuses to go down. Fuego into the ropes and hits a flying clothesline and it staggers the big man. Fuego goes up top, Missle Dropkick and Apples goes down!!! Fuego is going to the top and I think he is going for the Raging Inferno, Apples is up, he leaps, and Apples caught him, APPLESAUCE DROP!!!! Apples hit it but he can't give a cover, he rolls on top, 1.........2........3!!! Fuego is out and Apples wins this one, but it wasn't easy as Fuego gave one hell of a fight. Again your winner in 10:12, King Apples. Wait a second, Tony Houston has come out to ringside as has Bill Bob Woijicowski. What are they doing? Apples grabs Fuego and sets him up for a piledriver… wait a minute, Houston and Bill Bob are on the ropes. Double Spike piledriver! OH MY GOD!! The Brotherhood leaves the ring after that unnecessary attack. =========================================================== Newark - That was uncalled for and I think something should be done about the Brotherhood. The fprmer Heavyweight Champion, The Springer is next! ============================================================ Match 6 "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset (with Prince Chuckle Wuckle) vs The Springer (with Dr. Bedpan) Sunset is in the ring and here comes Springer darting from the back. He has been on a rampage since losing that World Tilte belt from one Tony Houston at the PPV. He jumps into the ring and nails Sunset with a clothesline. Irish whip on Sunset and a backbody drop nicely done. Repeated boots to the head by Springer and he throws Sunset to the outside. He has a chair and he gives it to Sunset, leaps and delivers a spin kick to the chair and Sunset goes down. Irish whip into the guard rail and then a clothesline and Sunset goes down. Springer is on the apron and he jumps and hits Sunset on the floor with an elbow drop. Springer brings him back into the ring along with the chair. He sets up the chair in the middle of the ring and he picks up Sunset by the hair and sends him into the ropes and gives a drop toe hold and Sunset's head just bounced off the steel chair. He picks up Sunset again and sets hi mon the top rope backwards. He climbs up there and hits a back suplex from the top rope. Cover, 1........2.....and a kickout by Sunset. How the hell did he kick out of that one? Irish whip again and Sunset ducks this time, grabs Springer's head and bounces it off the chair. He has him in a side headlock now and he sends him into the ropes, Sunset with momentum and he hits a shoulderblock and Springer goes rolling to the floor. Sunset is on the top rope and he hits a plancha. Springer looks mighty dazed at the moment. Springer rolls back into the ring and Sunset is on top again, high cross body but Springer's weight rolled him over, 1...........2......no!! Sunset just barely kicked out. Springer goes into the ropes and hits a clothesline. He puts Sunset's arm around his head, lifts, northern lights suplex with a bridge, 1......2.......and no!! Sunset just kicked out again. Springer is back up and he gives Sunset a boot to the gut. Lifts him up, spins, corkscrew piledriver. Wow, we have never seen that before. Sunset is out of it. Springer is on the top rope again, he leaps, and Sunset meets him with a dropkick to the stomach. Both men are down and the ref is giving the standing 10 count. Springer back up first anmd he hits a leg drop and covers the man, 1........2......and Sunset kicks out. Sunset with a thumb to the eye and he gets Springer in an Abdominal Stretch. He is really wrenching it on and Springer looks hurt. His ribs took a beating when he got hit with that plancha on the floor. Sunset is holding on to the top rope for leverage and the ref caught him. The ref is now telling him to break the hold. He won't, the ref counts, 1......2....3......4..and Sunset lets the hold go and Springer goes into the corner. Sunset goes up and over and is trying for a Hurricanrana. He goes for it but Springer doesn't go over with him. He runs back and is going for a jawbreaker. Springer grabs Sunset's head, SPRINGER CUTTER!! He hit it and the fans are going nuts. Cover on Sunset, 1......2......3.!! This one is over and Springer is back on his winning ways here in the INWI. Again your winner in 8:21, Springer. =========================================================== Newark - The Springer back on the winning ways and it won't be long before he wants his title back! Watch out Tony Houston! Let's head back to the ring! BUT before that, we have the FIRST edition of "Scoop Raps," which will be a weekly show on INWI TV! ============================================================ Scoop Raps….. with Scoop Ropes You know, I have a lot of things brewing on my mind and it’s time that I spit them out. These views are mine and are not those of the INWI, although they may be. What’s going on recently is a bunch of crock and I think everyone in the INWI, NEW and every other federation around knows it. Let’s first look at this recent situation between the INWI and the NEW. You have a small time federation that invades a ‘’World Power" and creates some serious heat along the way. This had to be one of the hottest angles going in the sport today. But it’s over. Wanna know why? Because the NEW got scared. You heard me right. They got scared. They saw the talent of this up and coming promotion and said "DAMN, we could get our asses kicked." Well, they did get their asses kicked, but they showed a lot of heart and determination and were fighting back. They had recently won a couple matches on an INWI pay per view and then it crumbled. President Bruno Sammartino, who thinks he’s the greatest wrestler that ever lived, decided that he would ban the INWI. Or wait a second, maybe it was that Vice President, GoodFella. Whoever the hell it was, they decided that they would ban the INWI wrestlers who were not contracted with the NEW. And if an INWI wrestler steps into an arena, they will sue the INWI. Well, tell me this NEW. What if our wrestlers have a night off, buy a ticket and go to watch a NEW show? You are going to sue the INWI? I think you better get your words straight when you say something like this. Furthermore, when the INWI Owner makes his statements regarding this situation, you can be sure that all hell will break loose. Any way you look at it, this angle, idea, invasion… whatever you want to call it… hell it’s a damn story line… is over. In the long run, this ending is probably the best things for both companies. But again, this is me speaking and I have no outlook in the decision making. Well, they are telling me I am out of time. So until next week, this is Scoop Ropes and these are MY opinions. =========================================================== Match 7 - Light Heavyweight Title Match James "Simply The Best" Pillow vs Mr. Cherry This match is going to prove to be really exciting. The two are in the ring and Pillow hands the belt over to the ref and Cherry attacks right away. Cherry dives at Pillow and takes him down and starts to punch away at him. Cherry scoops up Pillow, sends him into the ropes and nails a high drop kick that crashes Pillow to the floor! Cherry grabs Pillow, hits another standing drop kick then goes to the top rope. Cherry up top… OH MY GOD! He just flipped and nailed a LEG DROP! I have never seen that move done with such perfection! A cover by Cherry and we have a new champ…1…2…NO! OH MY GOD! Pillow kicked out of that in an amazing show of courage! Cherry looks up and can’t believe that he didn’t get the win. Cherry grabs Pillow sends him into the ropes, Pillow ducks a clothesline then comes back with a drop kick and both men are down! Both men have made their way to the ropes and are getting to their feet! Both are up and Pillow just nailed Cherry with a superkick and Cherry flips out over the top rope and OW! Lands on a table! Pillow looks over the situation and climbs to the top rope…. PILLOW DIVES! Oh my… Pillow just landed on Cherry and they both went through the table. This is crazy! Pillow slowly gets to his feet and stumbles around… he then grabs Cherry and sends him into the ring. Pillow falls on top of Cherry, 1…2…..WAIT! Cherry has his foot on the bottom rope. That’s amazing… how did he know to do that? I thought he was out cold! Pillow can’t believe it and he rolls Cherry over into the middle of the ring and there’s another cover. 1…..2….NO! Cherry gets a shoulder up. What’s going on here? Cherry is relying on total instincts. Pillow grabs Cherry, sends him into the ropes and nails a spinning heel kick! Pillow is really dominating this match now. Wait a minute… what the hell is this? That’s BILL BOB WOIJICOWSKI! What is he doing out here? He’s just watching the match.. he’s in a three way with David McCarter and the winner of the Pillow Cherry match next week for the Light Heavyweight Title. Pillow turns around as Cherry gets up and there’s a Hurakanrana! Wait… there’s more. Here comes McCarter and he attacks Bill Bob and they are brawling. Pillow is watching in amazement as they brawl on the outside. Wait a minute, Cherry is up… he just hooked Pillow in a roll up and he has tights! 1….2…..3!! OH MY GOD!! What an upset!! Let’s get the official word…. Here is your winner in 18:36 and NEW INWI Light Heavyweight Champion, Mr. Cherry!! Cherry grabs the belt and exits through the crowd. Meanwhile, Pillow is hot. He grabs the ref and nails a powerbomb!! He has gone nuts! Pillow leaves the ring and I think he is going to look for Bill Bob and McCarter. Wait, we just got word from the back that there will be an INWI Official statement regarding this situation before the show ends!!! =========================================================== Newark - A NEW Champ is crowned in controversial fashion. I wonder what the statement will be regarding this situation? Let's head back to the ring... =========================================================== Match 8 - Gauntlet, Week 3 Jason "Crusher" Chandler vs Adam Kuhl Adam Kuhl, the long time friend of the late Jimmy Cougar, makes his return to the INWI rings. He has a tough challenge tonight though as he takes on Jason Chandler in week three of the Gauntlet. Now Chandler is getting more and more cocky as this whole escapade gets farther along and as his opponents have now become top notch opponents…it is going to get harder. Chandler storms the ring and Kuhl starts to kick at Chandler as soon as he enters the ring. Kuhl stomping away at Chandler and now he picks him up and sends Chandler to the ropes and nails a spinning heel kick. Kuhl with a quick cover, 1…NO! Chandler kicks out right away. Kuhl is being smart here as he scoops up Chandler and slaps a headlock on him… Chandler sends Kuhl into the ropes, leap frog and Chandler just nailed Kuhl with a clothesline! Chandler now stomping away on Kuhl and he picks him up and nails a suplex. Here’s where we know Chandler is hot. When he gets a chance to show off his suplex’s, he’s on a roll at that point. Chandler scoops up Kuhl and there’s a belly to belly suplex! A cover, 1…2…NO! Kuhl kicks out! Chandler up with Kuhl again and nails him in the midsection with a kick. Chandler with a DDT!! No cover, Chandler picks up Kuhl again and throws him over the top rope! Chandler to the outside and stays on the apron. Kuhl gets up…Chandler leaps, but Kuhl kicks Chandler right in the bread basket! Kuhl takes Chandler and slams his head into the mat and then sends Chandler face first into the ring post. Chandler looks like… yes, he’s busted open. Kuhl now pounding on the bloody face of Chandler and sends him into the cage around ringside. Kuhl takes Chandler and sends him back into the ring and goes to the top rope! Kuhl sets up for a moonsault… YES! Kuhl scrambles and hooks a leg, 1….2….NO! OH MAN! Chandler has kicked out of a moonsault! Kuhl looks hot and starts to pound away at Chandler’s bloody head. Wait a minute…. The crowd is stirring a little…There’s TONY HOUSTON! What the hell is he doing here? No one in the ring sees him and Kuhl grabs Chandler, sends him into the ropes, ref is hit! Chandler is down again and Kuhl is laughing. Wait, Houston enters the ring and grabs Kuhl…. TA DOW! COLD DROP! Kuhl is out! Houston grabs Chandler and pulls him over on top of Kuhl and leaves the ring. The ref is up….1……..2……..3!!!! It’s over….here is your winner in 14:53, Jason "Crusher" Chandler!! =========================================================== Newark - Chandler is 3-0 in the Gauntlet and NOW, we have words from the INWI Owner, Pete! I hear this is regarding the ending of the INWI/NEW situation... let's go to it now... =========================================================== [INWI Owner Pete sits at a big desk at INWI Headquarters. The camera is rolling and you can see Pete messing with some things on the desk. Pete had a shirt, tie and suspenders on. He has short spiked hair and wire rimmed glasses. He begins to speak] The recent actions between the INWI and the NEW was one of the hottest in all of eWrestling. Unfortunately, this agreement has come to an abrupt end. It was under an agreement that the INWI and NEW would break all ties with each other and move our separate ways. Obviously, the NEW decided to go beyond that. Besides the fact that they claimed to have won this little feud, which is one of the biggest bunch of bull I have ever heard, they have said they will sue us if we enter a NEW arena. Well these words go out to the NEW executives. The decisions you have made and the comments you have made are all on your own. The agreement that was reached between the INWI and the NEW was that of a clean break due to administrative difficulties and the fact that agreements were hard to make with each other. At no time was it ever mentioned that one side had won or lost and nothing about a banment of wrestlers. However, if that is the deal, then that is fine. In all reality, there were no ‘’true’’ winners in this feud. If anyone won, then it would be the INWI. We had two NEW championships and a big win on the pay per view. We also invaded more and had more damage done. But I am not one to come in front of INWI fans and claim victory. But I guess you would need to do that as you switch to Monday Nights. We all know who the ‘’original’’ Monday Night show was. Hot Spot. As for you suing the INWI, I have contacted our lawyers. If an INWI wrestler makes his way into a NEW Arena, the NEW cannot sue the INWI. There is no grounds. We are not sending our wrestlers into the NEW anymore and if they decide to go, it is on their own free will. We are not in charge of what they do as long as they meet their contractual obligations with the INWI. Therefore, you can take your idle threats and stick them. In conclusion fans of the INWI, there was no winner in this feud. I will not take any credit for a win because NO ONE won. This was a great idea on paper, but when done, it was a pain in everyone’s asses. I hope you all understand that what is being said in the NEW is junk and is a total false hood. Please accept my apologies on behalf of the INWI for the fact that we could not keep this idea a float. Good evening to all and thank you for your time. [The camera fades out to an INWI logo and then shifts to Brian Newark.] =========================================================== Newark - WOW! We'll be back for hour number three!!!!! ===========================================================
