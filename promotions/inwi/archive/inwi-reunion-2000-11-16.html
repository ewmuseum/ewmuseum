Jonathan Hammer O=============================================================O “Lone Wolf” Brody Thunder O============================================================O [Scene: Fade into a clear blue Arizona sky. Not a cloud in sight. A light breeze whistles thru the midday air. The camera slowly pans down to show some of the rugged Arizona landscape the silent but deadly desert stretching out beneath the dry warmth of the sun. As the camera continues to pan downwards, a raspy, familiar voice breaks the serenity.] VOICE: So there I was...mindin' my own business...enjoyin' my...'semi-retirement'..when my phone rang. I picked it up an' realized right then...that Hell had just frozen over. Y'see..it was INWI Commissioner DeLuise...an' he was calling Brody Thunder. [Thunder steps into view from the left side of the shot. He's dressed in faded jeans, a turquoise blue shirt with black bolo tie, a black Durango hat and black Lucasi cowboy boots. A pair of mirrored sunglasses shield his eyes from the harsh glare of the Arizona sunshine.] BT: I have to admit...my first reaction was to hang up. He said there was going to be a 'reunion' o' the INWI. My next thought? Who cares. I did my time. I fulfilled my obligations. An' jus' cause the sonuvabitch gave me a piece o' tree bark that reads "Hall of Fame" doesn't mean I owed him a _damn_ thing. [He tips back the brim of his hat.] BT: So he offers me a spot on the show. [He smirks.] BT: I tell him to use his hat fer a bedpan... not in so many words , o'course. Then he through out a few names. King Apples. Tony Houston. Andrew Tucker. Three o' the toughest hombres I'd ever stood across from in the ring. Three men who knew what it meant to pay the price in this business. Three men who earned everythin' they ever got in this sport. An' they were three men who Brody Thunder respected. I ain't the sentimental type. I don't cry at movies an' I damn sure don't have a lick o' regret or regret fer anythin' I did inside that squared circle. So when that donut-eatin' pantload said I'd be in the ring with those three men...men I'd fought against...men I'd bled against... men fer which I'd have no compunction fer puttin' a burden on their insurance companies.... well...I did exactly what you'd expect I'd do. [He removes his sunglasses and places them on his hat. His eyes squint in the glare of the sun, but his stare is unmistakable.] BT: I agreed. [That devilish grin widens just a bit.] BT: An' what that means, boys... is things jus' got a lil tougher than ya bargained fer. Fer months now all I've heard is that 'Thunder's gone soft' or 'Thunder's lost the fire'. The armchair wrestling experts all wrote me off cuz o'the cancer in my neck. Everybody seems to think the cowboy is past his flamin' prime an' ready to be put ouit to pasture. Everyone thinks Brody Thunder doesn't have 'it' anymore. Bulls[BLEEP]t. What I've done is come back from the toughest fight o'my gawdamned life. I didn't do it to be some flamin' posterboy for medical science. I didn't do it to be some rolemodel for others. I didn't do it to lobby the flamin' government for more research money. I did it... ...fer me. Brody Thunder has never quit at anything in his gawdamn life an' I'd be damned if I was gonna quit then. An' the whole time I was rehabbin', I was waitin'. Waitin' fer that chance to prove ta alla them so-called 'experts' that I wasn't the washed up hasbeen they all said I was. Two months ago my waitin' ended with that phonecall from DeLuise. I'd gotten my chance to stick it in the craw o'every snot-nosed lil dirtsheet who thought they'd thrown dirt on my grave. An' to them an' every other pantload who wrote off Brody Thunder... here's a newsflash. The reports o' my death are greatly flamin' exaggerated. [He stares intensely into the camera for a few moments after he stops speaking, accentuating his point.] BT: Apples... Tucker...Houston...this is gonna be a helluva night for the INWI. The fans'll pack the joint. The wrestlers will all have goosebumps in the lockerrom. DeLuise will probablu blow a gasket at the amount of the gate. Oh this will be a real big dance fer the INWI... but make no mistake about it. I ain't comin' there to lose, amigos. WE got ourselves one helluva match. A man's gotta be pinned three times 'fore he heads to the shower. After each fall they spin a wheel to create a whole new match. Ain't no amount o'trainin' an' preparation gonna help ya in this match. It's surviival o'the fittest once that bell rings an' boys.. if there's one thing Brody Thunder knows about... ...it's survival. BT: An' at the Reunion..that's jus' what I plan on doin' cuz y'see it's not jus' a comeback for me, oh no. It's a gawdamned ressurection, my friends. A flamin' miracle in the squared circle. Lazarus in wrestling boots. This ain't jus' a match fer Brody Thunder. It's a crossroads. It's the chance to prove one more time that I am the single greatest wrestler lacin' 'em up today. I've lived by that credo an' i damn near died by it but one thing i never did... ..was abandon it. At Reunion I'm not jus' comin' ta win a match. I'm not jus' comin' to relive old times. I'm not jus' comin' to take money outta DeLuise's pockets.. [He winks.] BT: I'm comin' ta win. Brody Thunder's back from the dead...an' fellas? [Thunder places the sunglasses back on his face as the smile disappears.] BT: I'm bringin' Hell with me. [He lowers the brim of his hat and merely walks off camera. the camera then slowly pans back up to the clear...cloudless...sky. Fade out....] O============================================================O King Apples O============================================================O [The scene opens inside of a dark building. As the camera pans around, you see that it is in the old INWI Arena. The arena is all set up again in it's wrestling form, with a ring set in the middle. We've exchanged wins with one another. We've had some great times. But this match Drew, I am going to the top. Nothing will stop me here. Not you, not anyone. No doubt one of the most respected and feared people in wrestling. And we have one hell of a history. Thunder and myself have had some of the greatest matches in this sport. He knows that this match will most likely be the last ever under and INWI banner. And he wants his hand raised. [Apples chuckles.] KA: But again Brody, I don't plan on losing. This is my match. I have been training for this match for weeks. I am in the best shape of my life. The game here is survival. You never know what will be spun on that damn wheel. Never. Am I worried? Hell no. There hasn't been a match I haven't been involved in. And I don't plan on letting any off these three get in my way. Especially not Thunder. [Apples again stands and walks to the ropes and stops.] KA: It's amazing knowing who has come through these ropes and wrestled in this ring. So many names, so many stars. There are so many things that make this federation special. It was a great idea to have this reunion card, just one time. To see what the fans remember, what they want. And what they want, we will deliver. So Drew, Brody and Tony.... I'll be seeing you soon. In the middle of this bad boy. Eddie has gone vintage for this promo: dark jeans and boots, per norm, but now an old INWI-endorsed shirt bearing the "W.W.E.D." logo is stretched across his chest. The Southerner now sports a gruff beard, grown only recently, and is busy pulling tape from his gnarled left hand.] Ej: "Ah don't mean ta degrade INWI when Ah say that. But Ah learned something when we squared off in Tampa... Ah may not have had my title, Ah may not have had my big win.
