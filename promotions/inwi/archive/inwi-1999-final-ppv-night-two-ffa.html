INWI Presents... "Two Years in the Making" Night 2 - INWI Final PPV FREE 4 ALL March 20, 1999 ======================================================== BN: Welcome fans to the second night. And this is the Free 4 All. Sit back and enjoy as for the next hour, you'll get to see the participants as they get ready for tonight and the solid card. Also, we'll have a match for you here in this hour. So sit back and enjoy... and in one hour Scoop and I will join you for night two of the one of the best weekends in e-wrestling history. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= INWI Trans-Atlantic Champion "Cornerstone" Jeffrey Drake =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [The camera fades into a water fountain in the backyard of INWI Trans-Atlantic Champion "The Cornerstone" Jeffrey Drake's mansion...Drake is sitting on the edge, and his precious title belt is tossed over his shoulder. He also notices something else....coming around the path on the left side of his house...the vivacious Tiffany Love...wearing a very skimpy outfit as opposed to Drake's faded jeans and ripped t-shirt. They begin a very quick conversation about night one of the INWI 3-Day Pay-Per-View.] TIFFANY LOVE: Jeff, we saw it on Hot Spot...we're going to see it on Night two...The Cornerstone and The Kamikaze going at it for the third time. The first time you scored the victory and the belt, and just a few nights ago, we saw it end in a double-count out. What will you be doing differently to ensure a victory at the Pay-Per-View? JEFFREY DRAKE: Well, Tiff, I played into his game...I became the hunted instead of the hunter..at the Pay-Per-View..Night two...you're going to be seeing me just go straight in for the kill...nothing else. I am going to make sure that Kris Klepan doesn't come anywhere near this title belt. TIFFANY LOVE: You got wacked pretty hard with a chair back on Hot Spot..how are you feeling after that? JEFFREY DRAKE: Injuries heal..but hate stays for a long time, Tiffany, and this little "rivalry" isn't going to be going away overnight. Someone's going to end up getting seriously hurt at the PPV. I'm not making any bold predictions..but someone's _DEFAINITLY_ not coming out in one piece! TIFFANY LOVE: You probably garnered the biggest win of your career when you defeated the now former INWI World Heavyweight Champion Setzer Van Strife...it was a diqualification victory however... JEFFREY DRAKE: So what? A win is a win...Van Strife made the mistake and he screwed himself more or less. In the books, I have a win over him. TIFFANY LOVE: True..and between you and Kris Klepan..I am sure that you would want the books to read 2-0-1 after the first night. JEFFREY DRAKE: You can say that again. There is no chance in hell of Klepan leaving town with _MY_ title. It's staying with me...besides...he only keeps getting these title shots because he _BUYS_ them...but hey, my mother didn't raise a fool...$10,000 is a HELLUVA lot of money, but he is gonna be getting played for a fool...because he's blown ten thousand cold bills...and they're resting comfortably in my pocket..Heh heh.
