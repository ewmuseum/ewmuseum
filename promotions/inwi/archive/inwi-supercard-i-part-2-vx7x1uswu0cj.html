[INWI] SuperCard I - Part 2

28 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SuperCard Cloud Eagle vs Ultimate Perversion Here is an interesting one. Perversion and Eagle. An American Indian and whatever you want to call Ultimate Perversion. Whatever you do call him, he wins quite often. I just can't believe what is going on with Cloud Eagle. I guess I never knew he was involved with this group. Ultimate Perversion left the stable of Trent VanDenBerg and immidetley Cloud Eagle stepped up to take the challenge. I highly doubt it will end here, but this is most definitley the start of it. Here we go and Perversion dashes across the ring and gets nailed by Cloud Eagle. Cloud Eagle has a dead stare on his face and you know he means all business. Cloud Eagle scoops up Perversion and nails a vicious tomahawk chor then a quick thrust kick. Perversion rolls out of the ring and crawls to a chair trying to use that as leverage to get up. Cloud Eagle follows him out and Perversion folds the chair and nails him with it! What a chair shot as Cloud Eagle crumbles to the floor. Perversion picks up Cloud Eagle, slams him tot he floor, then rolls him back into the ring. Perversion climbs the apron then goes to the top rope. Perversion hits a perfect flying clothesline!! Cloud Eagle looks stunned as he slowly works his way to his feet. Perversion goes for a roll up and has the tights!! 1..2...3!!!! What a win for Perversion! He struts around the ring and stares dead straight at VanDenBerg.... He raises his hands into the air and leaves the ring. Cloud Eagle is up now and he does not look happy. I have a feeling this won't be the last time these two meet up. SuperCard INWI Television Tag Team Titles Solid Gold vs The Freaks Here we go with our second title match of the night! Solid Gold, who recently grabbed the TV Tag titles back from the Friendship Express, have a tough challenge here in the Freaks - Freak O'Nature and Weiland. The Freaks haven't had the best luck lately, but they are hoping to turn it around tonight! There's the bell and this match is underway. It will be Weiland and Bradley gold starting things off tonight and they are just staring eachother down as they circle the ring. Weiland makes the first move as he dives at Bradleys legs and takes them out from underneath them. Weiland then climbs on top of Bradley and starts to pound on him and Blake comes running in, but the ref stops him and Freak O' Nature comes in and they are double teaming Bradley. Freak leaves the ring and as the ref comes back, they tag out. Freak comes in and keeps up on Bradley where Weiland left off. OUCH!! Bradley just went downtown on Freak and he is crawling to his corner. He gets there and tags Blake in. Blake comes in and nails Freak with a diving clothesline. Freak is rolling around on the mat and Blake is just stomping at him. Here comes Weiland and nails Blake with a clothesline from behind and sends him into the ref!! Bradley comes an and DDT's Weiland and he is down! Oh god no... here come the Glamour Boys and they nail Solid Gold from behind. Ahhh, Starr has Bradley in the Starrlock as Cougar nails blake with a chair and sends him out of the ring. Bradley is out cold in the center of the ring and The Glamour Boys leave the ring. Freak O' Nature comes to and sees Bradley and lays on him. No ref, don't come to, this shouldn't happen. He is coming to, 1...2...3!! We have new champions. Your winners and new TV Tag Champions, The Freaks in 15:32. Bradley is still out as they bring out the stretcher. SuperCard Mr. Banana vs "The Big Fan" Devin Terry Wow, I still can't believe last match. They had to take Bradley out on a stretcher and Solid Gold will definitley seek revenge. Here we have a very angry member of the Brotherhood of Sour Grapes. Banana storms the ring and goes right after the Big Fan and nails him with a knee to the back. Banana scoops him up and powerslams him and follows with an elbow drop. Banana scoops him up again and looks like a bodyslam but no, he nails a back breaker. He is going for a quick win here. There's a neck breaker and he is signaling to the crowd that it is time. There it is!! Banana puts Terry in the Banana Split and Terry has to give it up!!! Your winner in 2:37, Mr. Banana! SuperCard The Peeler Brothers vs Contrast Here is a big tag team match with the winner looking at a title shot down the line. The Peeler Brothers, the former TV Champions, are looking to get some gold back. One of the other members of the Brotherhood, Mr. Apples already has gold, but has a huge title defense tonight! It's Mr. Orange and White starting things off in this match. White attacks and takes Pear down and starts punching on him and gets up and gives a good kick to the forehead area. It looks like Pear is already busted wide open and he is! White picks up Pear and takes him to a corner and sends him head first into a ring post. Pear falls backward and is out cold. I don't think he is even moving. This looks bad and White sends Pear out of the ring. Black scoops him up out there and sends him headfirst via a slingshot into the ringpost. Black then rolls him back into the ring. Mr. Flamboyant is going crazy on the outside and waving at the dressing room. Here comes Mr. banana, Mr. Cherry and Mr. Apples and they huddle up. White is continuing his assult on Mr. Pear. Orange heads into the ring and clotheslines White out of the ring and Orange is overtop of Pear. Mr. Pear is not responding. Orange signals for the ref to come over and he signals for the bell. The rest of the Brotherhood rushes the ring and the stretcher is on it's way out. Your winners in 6:56, Contrast. The Medics are in the ring tending to Mr. Pear and this is not a good situation. We will keep you posted on how he is doing and again Monday. All concerned fans on him and Bradley Gold can check out our Web Site at http://www.catskill.net/harmer to get the latest on their injuries. We wish both of them the best of luck. ============================================================= Brian Newark - Wow, this is a great card and we want to thank Fox for going live with this one! Before our next match, lets get comments from Steven Casper. ============================================================= <-Stephen Casper is on his way to the ring when Tiffany Love stops him for an interview-> Tiffany Love: Hello Stephen. I wanted to ask you about your opponent tonight, "Marvelous" Mike Quick. Steven Casper: Well, Mike believes that he is the fastest guy in the federation. I think that I can match just about anyone in speed. We will find out just who the better man is. Love: (Surprised) It seems that you have gained some confidence since your last inerview. Casper: Well, At that time I had wrestled one match and I wasn't really sure if I was cut out for this fed. I even considered calling it quits, but then I got a few wins under my belt and I feel a little more confident in my abilities. Love: Thank you very much for talking. (Casper proceeds to the ring) That is one man determined to prove himself in the INWI, I wish him the best of luck. ============================================================= SuperCard "Marvelous" Mike Quick vs Steven Casper "Marvelous" Mike Quick, the fastest man in the INWI is looking to claim a big win here at SuperCard over the up and coming youngster, Steven Casper. Quick hops in the ring, bounces off the ropes and delivers a dropkick right of the bat that knocks Casper out of the ring. Casper stands up and looks rather annoyed at what just occured. Casper hops on the apron and points and yells at Quick but Quick bounces off the ropes and dropkicks Casper off the apron and into the first row. WOW! Quick climbs to the top ropes and dives on top of Casper as he comes back into the ringside area. Casper, I don't think, knows what to do with a guy with this kind of speed. Quick gets up and gets a running start and dives at Casper, but Casper moves and Quick goes head first into the ring railing. Casper picks up Quick and slams him into the floor then sends hium back into the ring. Casper hops back into the ring and drops an elbow from the second rope on top of Quick and Quick is still rolling around in pain. Man oh man are people getting hurt tonight! Casper heads off the ropes and drops a leg across the neck of "Marvelous" Mike Quick. Casper looks at the crowd and laughs at them and then turns his attention back to Quick. He leans over to pick up Quick and gets a right to the jaw and stumbles back. Quick is back on his feet and dropkicks Casper! Where in the heck does Quick get all of this energy? Quick nails a running clothesline then goes to the top rope and nails a splash. I don't think Casper knows what is hitting him! Oh my!! He is going for it and nails the Quick Ending!! 1...2...3!!! That's the match!!! Your winner in 10:23, "Marvelous" Mike Quick!! ============================================================= Brian Newark - Michael Pontiff is in the back with his favorite Tag Team, The Friendship Express. Lets see what they think about their match tonight. ============================================================= *Micheal Pontiff is dragged backstage by his camera man into the backstage interview area where the Friendship Express is already waiting; he has an uncomfortable look on his face* Pontiff: Do I HAVE to do this *whispering to his camera man* Camera man: *whispers back* YES, you have to do this...I had direct orders from Miss Love to have you do this interview..she seemed rather amused about it when she told me. Pontiff: Oh, just great...ahem...I'm standing here with none other then the Friendship Express...who, against my will, have gotten to know very well over the last few cards. Ricky: *puts his hand around Micheal Pontiff's waist and smiles big for the camera* Micheal, me and my shower buddy Alfonzo have had one heck of a time getting to know you too! Pontiff: Uh...yeah, sure. Anyways, let's talk about your upcoming match tonight against the Voyeurs of Utter Destruction. Alfonzo: *puts his arm around Micheal's shoulders* Well, the Voyeurs of Utter Destruction have an awfully nasty name...but ya know, me and my little massage buddy Ricky can get a little on the nasty side too. *smiles* Pontiff: *sweating bullets* Um...you don't mean what I think you mean do you? Alfonzo: Yup, it sure is.....*sticks his tongue out at the camera...then starts making motorcycle noises and puts his hands in the air as if starting it up* Vrooom, vrooom!!! Look at me! I'm on my big bad Harley and I'm about to take a nasty drive down to the ring! Is that what you were thinking of Micheal? Pontiff: Um..not exactly, but never mind. Alfonzo: C'mon Ricky, climb on back, we'll take a ride together on my big bad nasty Harley! *Ricky comes up behind Alfonzo and wraps his arms tightly around Alfonzo's waist and hugs firmly* Alfonzo: Vroom vroom!!!!! Pontiff: Uh, before you go, what do you have to say about your recent loss to Solid Gold after outside interference by Michael Hollywood? Ricky: Oh, hehehehehe, we have a little suprise in store for both Solid Gold and Michael Hollywood tonight. Hehehehe. I'm sure they'll love it...I know me and Alfonzo will. *winks to the camera* Pontiff: Well, it looks like the Friendship Express haven't forgotten what Michael Hollywood and Solid Gold did to them on Monday Hot Spot a couple weeks ago...and if I were Solid Gold or Michael Hollywood, I'd be feeling really uncomfortable right now...as if I'm not already uncomfortable here. *Ricky and Alfonzo are running around the interview area pretending to ride a Harley* Pontiff: Uh...let's get back to ringside before things get out of hand back here..quick!!! Ricky: C'mon Micheal, let's all take a ride! *Alfonzo runs into Micheal and is forced to take an imaginary ride on their Harley* Pontiff: Tiffany Love, I'll get you for this!!!!! ============================================================= SuperCard Friendship Express vs Voyeurs of Utter Destruction Well here are the Friendship Express as they are in the ring and dancing it up for the fans and we are waiting for the Voyeurs of Utter Destruction. And they are not coming to the ring yet, what the hell is going on? We are on live TV!! Wait there's the music and Here comes the Alien, but he is by himself...where is Eric "The Mystrey Man" mcBird? You know, when they had that press confrence a week or so ago where they all broke up, I was wondering what the hell was going on with them. Well, the Alien doesn't look like he wants to wrestle, but he is going to. There's the bell and we are underway. The Friendship Express see an opening and take it as they attack. The Alien isn't even into this match and is getting beat up pretty good. Uh oh, Alfonzo goes for the Diamond Cutter and here is a splash from Ricky... it's the "Ambiguous Squash." 1...2...3!! Your winners in 3:27, the Friendship Express!! Where the heck was the Alien's partner? What is going on here? I really hope that Commissioner Jerry DeLuise looks into this one....
