[INWI] May 25 Show

26 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
INWI - Sunday, May 25 Hartford Civic Center, Hartford, CT ========================================= Brian Newark - Welcome ladies and gentlemen live on Sportschannel USA and we have a solid card for you tonight, including three debut matches and maybe the match that will finally end the fued between Mr. Apples and Mr. Ed Rogers. These two have been going at it since the beginning of the INWI and tonight they meet in a 15 foot high steel cage. Also on the card is the long awaited rematch between "The Icon" Pimp Daddy Brown and "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder!! Without any further delay, let's go up to the ring for our opening bout of the evening!!! Match 1 - Debut Match Awesome X vs Dell Pickle (of the jWo) Dell Pickle, sporting his new jWo pink outfit is looking to get a win over the INWI newcomer, Awesome X. Pickle comes to the center of the ring and nails X with a right fist and staggers him backwards. Awesome X shakes it off and charges, nailing a diving lariat! X scoops up Pickle, slams him and works on the knee. X scoops up Pickle and drops him on that knee and signles to the crowd. Is he going to try and end this match already?? YES! There's the 4X4 - a figure four leglock and it's in the center of the ring! Pickle gives up and this one is history! Your winner in 2:47, Awesome X!!!! Match 2 - Super Grudge Match Bodyguard Jones vs Psycho Sal We know the bad blood these two have for eachother and I don't think it's ending to soon as they are meeting yet again in the INWI ring. Jones is in the ring taking off his jacket and here comes Sal and nails him from behind. Sal is working on the Bodyguard and drops him headfirst to the floor. Sal scoops him up and tosses him over the top rope and follows him out. Sal grabs a chair and nails Jones with it and again! Sal takes Jones and sends him into the ringpost headfirst and then heads back into the ring. Oh look at this, Mean Eugene is getting a couple cheap shots in on Jones outside the ring. What a worm.... Sal is back outside the ring and charges at Jones and the Bodyguard moves and Sal crashes into the ringpost. Jones rolls back into the ring looking to catch his breath and revive himself. Sal is staggering to his feet and is trying to get back into the ring. He is on the apron, Jones bounces off the ropes and dropkicks Sal into the ring railing!! Jones is going to the top and splashes Sal outside the ring!! Oh my god!! They both look hurt after that one!! Hey... what the heck is this?? A masked man has just made his way to ringside!!! He clotheslines Jones as he tries to get up and then DDT's him to the floor! Jones is tossed back into the ring as is Sal. Sal falls across Jones....1....2....3!!! It's over!! Your winner in 10:31, Psycho Sal!! The Masked Man is gone, he disappeared through the crowd... who the hell was that??? ============================================================ Brian Newark - Before our next match, lets get some comments from a rather, well, extreme individual, Jonathan Hammer. ============================================================ (The scene opens on a well built man standing in front of an INWI flag. Suddenly he pulls out a beer bottle and cracks it over his head. Blood is pouring over his face as he looks at the camera.) Hello INWI allow me to introduce myself. I am Jonathan Hammer. I have come here to kick some ***. I don't care who the hell you are the Hammer's Throw will take you all down. Step up to the plate and have some balls. It don't matter if you're big or small, fat or skinny I am here to crush. I'm the extremest f*cker in town. Step up. (Jonathan Hammer suddenly headbutts the camera.) Fade to fuzz. ============================================================ Match 3 - Debut Match Jonathan Hammer vs Senator Bradley Hammer has a hard debut match here in the INWI with Senator Bradley. Hammer is a big guy and charges the ring laying out Bradley with an elbow to the head. Hammer immeditley tosses Bradley out of the ring and sends him into the crowd. Wow, what a chair shot and Bradley goes down! Hammer brings him back over and scoops him up and slams him on the timekeepers table! Hammer sends the Senator back into the ring. Hammer follows him in and hits a suplex. It looks as though he is working on the back and he nails a belly to back suplex that rocked Bradley. Hammer is looking to end this match....it looks like a powerbomb is coming up, no...it's a pearl river plunge... I think he calls it a Hammer Throw! 1...2...3!!! This match is history.... your winner, in 5:11, Jonathan Hammer!!! Match 4 - Television Tag Team Titles The Friendship Express vs Solid Gold Well, we know these two teams have a history with eachother and they are ready to go. It will be Blake Gold and Ricky Sanchez are starting things out in this match. The champions, The Friendship Express, are looking to hold onto these titles tonight. These two teams have exchanged them back and forth and really work on eachother. Blake and Ricky go at it early and Blake gets Ricky on the ground in a chin lock. Blake tags in Bradley and they start to double team Ricky. Alfonso Geller charges in, but the ref stops him and the Gold Brothers are really working Ricky over. Finally Alfonso goes out of the ring and Bradley slams Ricky to the mat and nails a standing leg drop. Bradley again picks him up and hits a snap suplex and gets a two count. Back into a chin lock goes Ricky and he really needs to tag. Bradley just went into his tights and I think he has a rope choking Ricky out!! Finally the ref breaks it up, but can't find the rope. Bradley brings Ricky to his feet and Ricky grabs something of Bradley's....and it's a private part if you know what I mean. Bradly falls to the ground in pain and Ricky dives tagging in Alfonso Geller!! Geller is going to town as he flattens Blake who charged in and then DDT's Bradley. 1...2...NO! Blake broke it up. Alfonso DDT's Blake to the floor and goes back to work on Bradley. Ricky is back and they send Bradley into the ropes and double dropkick to the floor! Ricky comes out and he looks like he's going to send Bradley back into the ring and does, but Solid Gold's manager, Jimmy Stardom, just flattened Ricky with a chair. Bradley is on the mat though and Alfonso bounces off the ropes and gets tripped up....he stumbles and Blake rolls him up...1....2...h a handful of tights...3!!! It's over!! Your winners in 14:37 and NEW INWI Television Tag Team Champions, Solid Gold!!! Match 5 - Debut Match NWOD (Carleton County Kid and "The Natural" [name withheld]) vs The Fantastic Surfers I tell you what, Hi and Tide, the Fantastic Surfers are in trouble for this match. The two from the NWOD each stand at 6'8'' or better and the Surfers are not that big! It looks like it will be Brymer and Hi starting this match and Brymer just grabbed Hi and slammed him with ease. I tell you what, the champion Big Brothers better watch out for these guys! Brymer tags in the Carleton County Kid and the Kid keeps up where he left off and he sends Hi into his own corner and he tags in Tide. Tide comes in coutiously and ties up with the Kid and gets a short arm clothesline for doing it and is on the mat. The Kid drops an elbow and tags Brymer back in. Brymer enters and chokeslams Tide to the mat and goes for a cover. 1...2...3!! Oh my lord, he got them with that choke slam and even Brymer looks surprised. Your winners in 5:56, the NWOD!!! Match 6 Michael Hollywood and Harvey Thunder vs Malvolio and John Q. Denver (jWo) Hollywood and Thunder are tagging up for the first time and maybe they are looking to make a run at Solid Gold's championship....we know Hollywood and Gold are not buds anymore, so anything is possible. The jWo, which is some sort of a joke in my eyes, but not in their eyes, is supposedly some sort of revolt by the wrestlers who rarely, if ever win here in the INWI. Hollywood and Thunder recently cleared the ring of the whole jWo when they announced who they were. Hollywood and Malvolio start out this match and Hollywood takes the early control and hits a running bullog that knocks the Shakespearean rip-off silly. Hollywood tags in Thunder and Thunder connects a reverse DDT and then he and Hollywood double team Malvolio in the corner. Hollywood flies to the opposite corner and nails John Q. Denver, who falls off the apron on top of manager Oyster Boy. Hollywood rushes back over, scoops up Malvolio with a front face suplex and Thunder nails a leg drop from the top rope!! 1...2...3!! It's over! Your winners in 6:27, Michael Hollywood and Harvey Thunder! Match 7 - Northeast TV Title Mike "The Gavel" Garvey vs Firestar The Gavel is taking a night off from his big fued to grant Firestar a title shot. Firestar, who came close to winning the INWI TV Title, is still looking to get his hands on his first title. Theres the bell and we are underway. Firestar takes control after a tie up and slams Garvey to the mat and follows with a knee. Firestar nails a suplex and climbs to the top, hitting a diving clothesline and getting a two count. Garvey was inches to losing his title in record time. Firestar scoops up Garvey, sends him into the rope and theres a spinning heel kick! Garvey rolls out of the ring and Firestar follows him out. Firestar grabs Garvey, but Garvey goes low and Firestar falls over in a little ball. Garvey comes back up to his feet and starts nailing Firestar with a series of kicks and then climbs on him and pummels him with lefts and rights. Garvey scoops up Firestar, suplex's him, then breaks the count right before a 10 count. Garvey again grabs Firestar, slams him to the floor, picks him up and throws him into the ring. Garvey back in, climbs the ropes and nails a leg from the top and gets a two count!! Garvey scoops Firestar, sends him into the ropes and he collides with the ref!! Garvey is laughing as he knows he can get away with anything here. Garvey rolls out of the ring and grabs the ring bell and slowly heads back into the ring. Hey, theres a russle in the crowd, it looks like someone is coming through the crowd and Garvey is setting up Firestar with that bell. Oh my god... it's BILL BOB WOIJICOWSKI!!!! He's back!!! He OK!!! Wow, he climbs into the ring and he has a crowbar!! He taps Garvey on the shoulder and Garvey spins around.... Woijicowski just nailed Garvey and Garvey crumbles to the mat. Woijicowski kicks the bell out of the ring and puts Firestar on top of Garvey. Come on Ref, get up!! He's up, there's the count....1....2....3!!! Wow!! It's a major upset!! Your winner and NEW INWI Northeast TV Champion in 13:42, Firestar!!! Woijicowski has disappeared and Garvey is out cold.... Match 8 - Winner Recieves TV Title Shot "Hardcore" John Stilwater vs Ultimate Perversion This match should be a wild one. The winner will recieve a title shot against TV Champion Mr. Apples. Stilwater, a former United States Champion, is trying to get gold again. Perversion has yet to win gold here in the INWI, but he is ranked quite high and has scored a bunch of major wins. Perversion enters the ring and is immediatley attacked by Stilwater and he won't let up. Stilwater kicks Perversion in the midsection and nails a piledriver. 1...2...NO! Perversion barely got his arm up in time! Stilwater scoops up Perversion, sends him into the rope and there's a big clothesline!! Hardcore hops on top of Perversion and starts pounding away on him and the ref tries to get in and gets shoved. Stilwater better look out or he'll get DQ'd! Oh my, Perversion just rolled up Stilwater, 1...2...NO! Hardcore kicks out then starts kicking at Perversion. Stilwater soops up Perversion and sets up for a suplex. Perversion blocks it, then blocks it again! Perversion reverses it and suplex's Stilwater! Both men are down and are working to their feet...both are up and Stilwater clotheslines Perversion out of the ring. Stilwater on the apron and nails a double ax handle from the top. He sets up Perversion on the ring post and goes to get a running start. Stilwater charges and Perversion falls out of the way and Stilwater collides with the ring post. Both men are down and Perversion is working his way to his feet. He rolls into the ring...8...9...10!!! Perversion beat the count! Your winner as a result of a countout, Ultimate Perversion at 11:24. Match 9 - United States Championship P.O. Box vs Jimmy Cougar P.O. Box is still in his bitter rivalry with Jimmy Cougar and you have to wonder if Jason Starr is around anywhere. P.O. Box enters the ring and hands the belt to the ref and points over at Cougar. He knows that Cougar is capeable of beating him and with the possibility of Starr being around, those odds get better. Theres the bell and these two rivals lock up in the center of the ring. Cougar chops Box in the throat and connects a quick DDT to take early control. He didn't hit it fully, but he got enough to gain the control of the match. Couga with an armbar and he puts in a couple of cheap shots and P.O. Box looks a little frustrated. Back up to their feet, Cougar hits a short armed lariat to put Box on his back. Cougar off the ropes and nails an elbow drop and another and a third. Cougar picks up Box, body slam and a one count. Cougar again with Box, belly to belly suplex! Box rolls out of the ring and Cougar is dancing in the ring to the boo's of the crowd. Cougar strolls to the ropes and sets himself to slingshot over the top on top of Box. Cougar flies and Box sticks a boot up and Cougar lands on top of the boot and falls back to the floor. Box works his way to his feet and drops a leg on top of Cougar and then rolls into the ring to catch his breath. He breaks the count and Cougar gets up and stumbles into the ring. He backs into P.O. Box and Box takes him and nails a reverse DDT! he goes for a cover, 1...2...3!! Oh my god, I thought Cougar got his arm up, but the ref says it was a three count. Your winner in 11:11 and still INWI U.S. Champion, P.O. Box. ============================================================ Brian Newark - Next up is the match for the INWI Tag Team Championship and we have words from the challengers, The Terminators. ============================================================ Blade: Sunday, May 25th the new reign og the Terminators will Begin! Gunner: That's right. Big Brother's! you the next contestent on How bad are we gonna kick your *****! Bring those titles all nice and shined up for the new champions. Blade: We are gonna beat you like a couple of read headed step children, showing the world that the Terminators are the tag team of the future. Gunner: We are gonna set you up and take you out...... Blade: Terminator Style! ============================================================ Match 10 - Tag Team Championship The Big Brothers vs The Terminators Well, Blade and Gunner, the Terminators are on a roll and they have earned a shot against the INWI Champions, The Big Brothers, Hunter and Steven. It looks like it will be Hunter and Gunner starting things off and these two start slugging out in the middle of the ring. Hunter takes the upper hand and sends Gunner into the corner and he slips out of the ring. Blade charges Hunter and he gets scooped up and pressed!!!! Hunter tosses him over the top and sends Blade on top of Gunner. The crowd is going crazy and the Big Brothers look as solid as they have. Blade and Gunner regroup outside and Gunner enters again and charges Hunter and clips him at the knee and the big man crumbles to the mat. Gunner starts stomping on that knee and he pulls him over to his corner and tags in Blade. Blade climbs to the top rope and nails Hunter on the knee from the top rope. Blade takes over and slaps on a figure four and Hunter is screaming in pain. Steven charges into the ring and breaks it up, but alot of damage is done. Blade walks over and tags Gunner and Gunner goes to attack Hunter, but Hunter nails Gunner and then rolls over and tags Steven. Steven in the ring now, clotheslines Gunner and knocks Blade off the ring apron. Steven slams Gunner and then scoops him up again and looks like he is setting him up for a powerslam. Blade gets up, on the top rope and dropkicks the back of Gunner. Steven falls backward and Blade nails Hunter. Gunner has tights, 1...2....3!! Oh my, what an Upset!!! Your winners in 15:32 and NEW INWI Tag Team Champions, The Terminators. They run out of the ring with the belts quickly and head back to the lockerrooms. I don't think the Big Brothers are going to rest on this one. Match 11 - Steel Cage Match (Non-Title) Mr. Apples vs Mr. Ed Rogers Well, I guess this is what thesae two needed. Just the two of them, alone in a 15 foot high steel cage. We know all about these two and their hatred towards eachother. Rogers enters the cage first and crawls into a corner with his Northeast Championship and Smelly. Here comes Apples, with Mr, Flamboyant, and he strolls into the ring with the INWI TV Title draped around his waist. He hands the title outside the cage and attacks Rogers and he is on him and going nuts. Apples takes Rogers out of the corner and sends him face first into the cage. Rogers still has Smelly and his title, but Apples grabs them both and sends them out of the ring. Apples up with Rogers and body slams him and follows with an elbow. Apples climbs to the top rope and drives a knee into the midsection of Rogers and Ed curls up in a ball of pain. Apples scoops up Rogers and nails a short armed clothesline, picks him up and sends him into the cage again. Apples is now rubbing Rogers face into the cage and he is broken wide open! Apples pulls him back and hits a reverse DDT and Rogers looks severly hurt. Apples has just dominated this match from the beginning and I don't know if Rogers can survive this type of match. Apples again grabs Rogers and there's a swinging neckbreaker, then a rude awakining! He's setting him up for it! There's the Applesauce drop!! Apples has this match won and he heads for the door! The ref opens it, but wait, theres that masked guy again! He slams the door in Apples face and the big man goes down! The masked man hops into the ring and nails Apples with a piledriver and then pulls Rogers to the door and then he heads back through the stands. Rogers sees where he is and tumbles out of the cage...oh my god, what a screwed up thing. Who is this masked guy? This is the second match he interfeared in. Your winner in 13:47, Mr. Ed Rogers!!! Match 12 - The Re-match for the INWI Heavyweight Championship "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder vs "The Icon" Pimp Daddy Brown This is the one many have been waiting for. The re-match between the two goliaths. They are in the ring and Thunder just stares over at Brown as he chews on a cigar. Thunder has the INWI Heavyweight Title Belt around his waist and he looks focused. Brown is oplaying his fans and he looks ready as well to take what was once his. Theres the bell and the two come to the center of the ring and a staredown ensues. Thunder then slaps Brown and Brown returns with a right to the face and drops the champion. Brown scoops up Thunder, sends him into the corner and follows with a flying elbow and Thunder falls to the mat. Thunder rolls out of the ring and Brown follows. Brown is on the apron and Thunder staggers to his feet and Brown nails a diving clothesline!! Brown up with Thunder and hits a side suples on the runway and then he rolls into the ring to break the count. Thunder starts to wander off like he's leaving, but Brown tracks him down and lands a belly to back suplex and then carries him back and rolls him into the ring. Thunder crawls into a corner and Brown follows him and ignores his pleas for a timeout. Brown delivers a couple boots and then Thunder dives out of the corner and tackles brown and starts pounding away with rights and lefts. Thunder has gained the momentum now and scoops up Brown and nails a suplex and then follows with a leg. Thunder again up with Brown and theres a DDT! Thunder for the cover, 1...2...NO! Brown gets his shoulder up! Thunder looks amazed and he is up, bounces off the ropes and goes for an elbow, Brown moves! Thunder is hurt and he starts to get up, small package, 1...2...NO! Thunder kicks out and both men are up. Brown with a boot to the midsection and he catches him for a suplex, no, it's the Pimp-a-plex!!! We could have a new champion!! There it is!! 1....2....NO!! Oh my god, Thunder kicked out of the Pimp-a-Plex and Brown looks amazed as both men are on their feet again. Brown charges Thunder and Lone Wolf catches him, he has him up...yes, it's the Thunderbolt! 1...2...3!! Oh my god, this match is over! Your winner in an amazing match, in 21:05 and still INWI Heavyweight Champion, "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well ladies and gentlemen, what a great night of wrestling we had here in Hartford! We have three new champions and what action we had in the main event and the cage match. And who is this masked guy? I guess we will try and find out soon. So, for the rest of the INWI, I am Brian Newark saying good night from Hartford and see you tomorrow night at Monday Hot Spot!! ============================================================ This was a presentation of the INWIŽ, May 1997
