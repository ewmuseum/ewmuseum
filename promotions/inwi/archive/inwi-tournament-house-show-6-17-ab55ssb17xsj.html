[INWI] Tournament House Show, 6-17

INWI House Show - June 17, 1997 Watertown, NY Tournament Special Show ================================================= Joseph Collins - Well the Light Heavyweight Championship Tournament is underway and some matches have happened here tonight at this special House Show. Also, some first round matches of the Ringmaster Tournament were held. The same will go on tomorrow night at another Tournament Special house show. Let's get you the results! Match 1 - Light Heavyweight Tournament, First Round Sam "The Minuteman" Adams vs Dell Pickle Sam Adams, the hot newcomer, took on the current Northeast champion in this first round match. Adams dominated from the opening bell and took a pinfall win with the Liberty Bell at 7:24. Match 2 - Light Heavyweight Tournament, First Round Raver vs "The Knuckle" Jimmy Chooley In our first upset of the tournament, "The Knuckle" Jimmy Chooley advanced to the second round when Bodyguard Jones interfered and helped Chooley score the pinfall over Raver at 9:21. Match 3 - Light Heavyweight Tournament, First Round Mr. Cherry vs John Q. Denver Mr. Cherry, who almost had his grasps on the Heavyweight Title, trys his luck in a division suited for his weight. Cherry advanced to the second round with a Cherry Bomb Moonsault and then the pin in 11:31. Match 4 - Light Heavyweight Tournament, First Round "The Big Fan" Devin Terry vs D.D. (Doctor of Disaster) D.D. advanced to the second round with his methodical ways. D.D. took control late in the match and then won by submission when he applied his "difibulator" Time of the match was 6:56. Match 5 - Ringmaster Tournament, First Round Match P.O. Box vs Mike "The Gavel" Garvey In a preview of their Pay-Per-View match for the INWI U.S. Championship, P.O. box scored the pinfall after the Springer came in and nailed Garvey with a crowbar. As Box got the pin at 11:21, Springer left laughing... Match 6 - Ringmaster Tournament, First Round Match Jonathan Hammer vs Mr. Apples Mr. Apples, the TV Champion, had a rough challenge in the INWI's most extreme wrestler Jonathan Hammer. Apples got the win, after both men were broken wide open, following an Applesauce Drop at 13:45. Match 7 - Ringmaster Tournament, First Round Match Michael Hollywood vs Apocalypse With Harvey Thunder outside the ring watching his back, Michael Hollywood advanced to the second Round of the Ringmaster with a big win over Apocalypse. Hollywood pinned the big man in 11:32. Match 8 - Ringmaster Tournament, First Round Match Preadator vs Jason "The Crusher" Chandler Chandler, who has been making a rise to the top lately, picked up a big win and moved into the second round with a win over Preadator, winning with the "Crush" in 9:21. Match 9 - Ringmaster Tournament, First Round Match "The Technician" Zack Sharp vs Malvolio Sharp, who recently demanded that he stop being treated like a newcomer, took his aggressions out on Malvolio. Sharp won the match with the Ultimate Demise in 8:37. Match 10 - Ringmaster Tournament, First Round Match Harvey Thunder vs Bodyguard Jones In another interesting first round matchup two interferances caused the winner in this match. First Solid Gold came out, clubbed Michael Hollywood, then beat on Harvey Thunder. While that was going on, Psycho Sal came out and nailed Bodyguard Jones witha chair four times. When the ref, who was knocked out, came to, Thunder draped his arm across Jones and got the controversial pinfall at 15:49. ============================================================ Joseph Collins - Well there's day 1 of our Special Tournament House Shows.
