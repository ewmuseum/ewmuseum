[INWI] House Show, May 3

3 May 1997

INWI House Show - May 3, 1997 Jamestown High School, Jamestown, NY ================================================== Scoop Ropes - welcome ladies and gentlemen to our Saturday House Show. We had 10 solid matches on this card in front of nearly 1,000 people!! It was a wild time here in Jamestown, so let's bring you all of the results! Heavyweight Champion Brody Thunder even made an appearance here and jabbed at the crowd for a couple minutes before leaving. ============================================================ Match 1 Dell Pickle vs Harvey Thunder Harvey Thunder, with Michael Hollywood at his side, had little trouble with Dell Pickle. Thunder, who is no relation to Brody Thunder, picked up the pinfall win at 5:33. Match 2 Bodyguard Jones vs Raver For the second consecutive night Bodyguard Jones made a fool out of his former stablemate. After this match though, Jones got a hold of Mean Eugene and beat on him for a few minutes. Psycho Sal came in and save Eugene. Jones pinned Raver in 8:12. Match 3 The Glamour Boys vs The Fantastic Surfers The Glamour Boys, Jimmy Cougar and Jason Starr won their second straight match as a team and are still in search of gold. The Glamour Boys won this match in 10:01 via pinfall. Match 4 "Marvelous" Mike Quick vs "The Big Fan" Devin Terry Mike Quick got back on track in this match. The fastest man in the INWI scored the victory here with a Quick Ending at 8:56. Match 5 Cloud Eagle vs Steven Casper Cloud Eagle, who is also in a fued with former stablemate Ultimate Perversion, picked up a victory tonight with a pinfall over Steven Casper at 6:43. Match 6 The Comforters vs Contrast The Comforters, who lost in their title bid at SuperCard, picked up a major win over Contrast following a Sealy Posturepedic at 9:37. Match 7 Ultimate Perversion vs Psycho Sal Perversion won this match with relative ease following the first two minutes. Sal was out of the ring and Bodyguard Jones came down and nailed him five times with a chair. Perversion took over from there and scored the pinfall at 6:29. Match 8 Northeast Championship Mr. Ed Rogers vs Senator Bradley In his first defense as champion, Mr. Ed Rogers defended his belt successfully against Senator Bradley. Rogers pinned the Senator at 10:47 following the Puppet Master. Match 9 INWI Tag Team Championship The Big Brothers vs The Peeler Brothers The Peeler Brothers, former INWI TV Tag Team Champions, were looking to get their hands on the bigger gold. Hunter and Steven, the Big Brothers, would have no part of that. The Peeler's became another victim as the Big's scored the pin at 11:19 following the Big Splash. MAIN EVENT Match 10 - United States Championship P.O. Box vs Michael Hollywood Michael Hollywood, with of course Harvey Thunder, went up against the NEW INWI U.S. Champion, P.O. Box. Now we all know how hard P.O. Box fought for this belt! P.O. Box defended his title successfully in this match and pinned Hollywood after the Final Delivery at 14:49. ============================================================ Scoop Ropes - That was a great card as usual! TOMORROW NIGHT!! Don't forget we have a great card set for Live TV On Sportschannel USA and it will be wild! Once again, here is the card: MAIN EVENT INWI Heavyweight Championship "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder vs Ultimate Perversion TV Championship Mr. Apples vs Jason "The Crusher" Chandler Tag Team Championship The Big Brothers vs The Rizzotto Brothers Northeast Championship Mr. Ed Rogers vs The Springer Northeast TV Championship Mike "The Gavel" Garvey vs Bill Bob Woijicowski PLUS UPDATES ON RECENT HAPPENINGS!! JERRY DeLUISE WILL BE ON HAND WITH A STATEMENT!! ============================================================ This was a presentation of the INWI, May 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= INWI Wrestling.....
