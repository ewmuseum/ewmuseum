[INWI] Heat Wave '97 PPV - Free 4 All

29 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Heat Wave '97 Free 4 All Madison Square Garden, New York, NY June 29, 1997 =============================================== Joseph Collins - Welcome ladies and gentlemen to the INWI Heat Wave '97 Free 4 All. We are only a half an hour away from the first ever INWI Pay-per-view, Heat Wave '97. This is what the people have been waiting for all along. I am here with Scoop Ropes as we will hear from wrestlers about their upcoming matches tonight and also we have a debut match here on the Free 4 All. "Too Slick" Rick Brown will take on "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset. Remember, Call your local cable company and order Heat Wave '97 NOW! Scoop Ropes - Yeah you stupid schmucks, do one thing smart in your life. Order this pay-per-view. This will be one of the best pay-per-views you have ever watched. The only bad thing is you don't get to listen to me for the whole show.... Collins - I don't know Scoop, that may be good... now, let's get comments from the newcomer, "Too Slick." (Scene: Camera cuts back to the gym where "Too Slick" Rick Brown has done all of his training thus far..He's sitting in a small office with a fan blaring, he's watching more tapes) Brown: So someone wimped out and "Hubba Hubba" has stepped in...*laughs* Hubba Hubba?? Hmmm...anyway, getting past the stupid name I must say I've looked at tapes and checked some things out...You haven't been very successful have you.. (Brown fast forward and rewinds footage) Brown: You seem a bit sloppy, but forget all of that, you Jonathan Sunset are in the same boat as "Too Slick", you're trying to make a name for yourself...Well I hate the inform you Hubba Hubba...But "Too Slick" Rick Brown is no one's stepping stone.. (Brown ejects a tape and grabs another to push in) Brown: So when we get in the ring tonight and I climb up to the top and hit The Landing Post...Hubba Hubba...The Sun will Set on YOU!! (camera fades as Brown puts his feet back up on the desk, watching more tape)... Collins - Well, it looks like he is ready for this match. He better be, Sunset is a former Northeast Champion. Scoop - Sunset is a putz. He can kiss my ass. But this Slick guy seems like he's alright, but we'll have to see. After all he is new. Collins - True enough Scoop, only time will tell. Now then, let's hear from Ultimate Perversion, who will tonight face Tony Houston. Scoop - Here's a treat... <Backstage area. Ultimate and Cloud are whispering to each other while Tiffany introduces them.> Tiffany: I'm Tiffany Love, and I'm here to interview Ultimate Perversion and Cloud Eagle about Ultimate's grudge match tonight against "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston. Ultimate, what are you thinking right now? Ultimate: Tiffany, I'm just thinking one thing. I'm thinking of how I'm going to take that little punks ass and use him to clean the ring up. Let there be no mistake. Houston is going DOWN! Tiffany: This feud has been going on a while now, but not as long as your feud with your ex manager. He must be pretty angry that Cloud Eagle has decided to be your partner. Ultimate: Trent couldn't organize an orgy in a brothel. Cloud still wishes to remain under Trent's management, and even though I believe that's a mistake, I support his decision. For to long I have tried to be a one man force in the INWI. I now realize that given some of the scum in this federation, you need someone to watch your back. I, in turn, will watch Cloud's back when he fights. As for Trent himself, yeah, he's angry. He's also pretty quiet after I stuffed him in that locker. (Smiles.) And while I remember, that punk Scoop Ropes has been badmouthing me. Anytime you want to step in the ring with me Scoop, I'm waiting for you. Just don't make any plans for afterwards, because there won't BE an afterwards for you, and you'd better believe it. Tiffany: Back to your match tonight, what do you think of your chances? Ultimate: Chances? Chances!? Let me tell you something Tiffany. Tony Houston is just a minor irritant. Something like a cockroach. And we all know what we do to cockroaches don't we. (Stamps on floor, then smiles.) Make no mistake, Mister Houston, you will be squashed. Cloud and me are going to be doing a little pest control in the ring tonight. Tiffany: Turning to you now Cloud, any thoughts on tonights match. Cloud: As you know, I am a man of honor, and I will not start anything tonight, but mark my words, if something DOES happen, I WILL finish it. I'm here to watch my partners back, and if anyone tries to interfere with his match against Houston, they will experience the true meaning of pain. Tiffany: Is Trent angry with you? Cloud: Yes, but anger is counterproductive. He may be angry, but one day he will realize the reason I did this. Until then, I will wait. Tiffany: Ultimate and Cloud, good luck, and thank you. Ultimate: (As they both walk of.) Luck doesn't enter into it. Tiffany: Well, there you have it. Back to the ring. Collins - *laughing* Ha ha ha - Scoop - seems like you have made an enemy or two. You ought to watch your trap a little more often. That was quite fun--- Scoop - Shut your trap Collins, cause I'll kick your ass no problem.... [As Scoops and Collins continue to comment on the previous comments a stirring begins in the crowd behind them] [There's two men, a larger man who is dressed in a black "doorag" covering his fiery red hair, he's wearing a black Busta Rhymes shirt with a black leather vest covering it, also wearing black Polo pants, with Timberland steel toed boots. And another man who seems to be receiving the larger amount of "boos" falling behind. He's wearing a Grey Pittsburgh Pirate hat ducked down to cover his icy blue eyes. He's wearing a navy blue INWI hoodie, and is wearing white Tommy Hilfiger pants sagging slightly.] [It's "Cold & Calculating" Tony Houston and "Bulldozer" Tony Riley] [Houston and Riley continue to make their way towards Scoops and Newark's announce both, still unnoticed by the announce crew. Houston and Riley climb onto the platform.] [Riley smacks Scoops in the back of the head and Scoops is cut off in mid sentence. Scoops: [Standing up] What the fu-- [Scoops is immediately cut off and sits back down when he sees the huge size of one of the visitors. Houston steps up and grabs the head set off of Scoops head, and pushes his chair down towards the end of the table, and Scoops falls over and out of the chair. Houston puts on Scoops headset and leans on the announce table.] Houston: Welcome ol' Newark, now I assume you are going to be quiet and let me say my piece right? [Houston leans right into the face of Newark.] Listen before you answer, let me tell you in my short time in the INWI I have held some sort of respect for you so realize what you are about to say. [Newark simply shakes his head in the affirmative.] Houston: Smart man. Now, as this almighty Pay Per View takes place, I figured and make a brief statement now. When "Cold & Calculating" wants something said, he doesn't WAIT for a time allotted to me, I Take the time I want. [Crowd begins to boo heavily, however, the buy rate for the PPV slowly starts to increase.] Now, tonight ya'll get to the pleasure of seeing me whip this Perversion character. I heard some recent comments by you son, talking about how now you have a small posse and now you can beat me. Acting like I can't beat your ass anyway. Now son, why are you going simply repeat the exact SAME THING I said. Son, it makes NO Sense. No thought...simply any lack of thought, which I may add Perversion is a master of, retake of a simple mention of a minor part of my Calculations. But now Perversion while you were out worrying about back up my ass has been remaining active, in various outside adventures, you been running around and pleading for you man to return to your side. But son, how reliable is your man, didn't he leave your side once before? Oh snap, he did, didn't he. You had to beg him to watch your back, now what's stoppin' him from doing that shiznit again? Now, Perversion you may feel you are ready for this match, but boy, compared to Tony Houston, you are going to be embarrassed biznitch. Ya'll get the free sight of me dropping that waste of space and television time, Perversion right on his head, then Perversion..You are going to FEEL getting dropped on your throat. COLD DROP TA DOW!!!! [Houston drops the head set to the table and gets up and leaves, as Riley physically pulls up Scoops and forcibly sits him in his announce seat. As Riley and Houston are leaving Scoops slowly whispers out of ear sight..touch me again and that's your ass.. Screen fades to INWI commercial.] Collins - Once again, you should check your shorts Scoop. I think you just let something go! Man that was funny. Scoop - Collins, I'm gonna kick your ass, go to the friggin ring already... Heat Wave '97 Free 4 All Match "Too Slick" Rick Brown vs "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset Well, here's our actual first match of the night. The newcomer, Rick Brown making his debut match against the former Northeast Champion Jonathan Sunset. Both men enter the ring and they are ready. There's the bell and we are underway. Brown and Sunset lock up and Sunset takes control. Sunset sends Brown into the ropes and dropkicks him to the mat. Sunset drops a knee then goes to the second rope and drops an elbow. Sunset is looking to get back into the title scene again and has had a rough time lately. Sunset scoops up Brown and slams him to the mat and heads outside to the top rope. Sunset flies looking for a leg drop but Brown moved! Brown gets up as does Sunset and Brown just nailed a hurakanrana!!! Brown adds in a couple of punches and he has taking control of this match! Both men up and Brown nails an inverted atomic drop, then sends Sunset to the mat with a dropkick. Sunset rolls out of the ring and tries to gain his composure back.... oh my god! Suicide Dive by Brown and he nailed Sunset! Brown is up and sends Sunset back into the ring. Brown to the top rope and nails a leg drop. Brown then looks to the top again. I think he is going to try and finish this off. Brown leaps, it's a tumbleweed plus an extra flip....I think he calls it the Landing Post!! 1...2...3!! Your winner in 8:35, "Too Slick" Rick Brown! Collins - Well, another newcomer has made his debut successful here in the INWI. Scoop - Whoopy. How about we get some more comments Collins? Collins - We'll do that Scoop. Let's go in the back an hear from Mr. Ed Rogers, who takes on the whole INWI tonight. (Ed is alone, in his dressing room, looking in his vanity mirror. An autographed photo of Fred Rogers is wedged into one corner and a picture of Ed with Cactus Jack doing the "bang bang" thing is in the other. Ed is drawing a large, black tear drop with a make-up pen under his left eye.) Ed Rogers: Mirror, Mirror, on the wall who is the most sain of them all? (Ed waits for a response. He keeps on waiting. And waiting. He starts to look annoyed when the mirror doesn't give him an answer.) Ed Rogers: No! Who is the most sain of them all??? WHO??? (Ed collapses on the vanity, crying. His head bangs into the mirror, cracking the surface.) Ed Rogers: Why? Why? Why won't the mirror talk??? I'M NOT CRAZY! I'M NOT CRAZY! THEY ARE CRAZY!! Mr. Apples is crazy... Zack Sharp is crazy... Dell Pickles is crazy... BUT I'M NOT CRAZY!!!! (Ed raises his bleeding face from his arms to see, for the first time, that he broke the mirror.) Ed Rogers: No! Please, no! My God No! Seven years...bad luck!!! (Ed stumbles backwards, with the agility of a drunk, out of his dressing room.) Scoop - The hell he isn't crazy... Collins - I actually have to agree with you there. That's the scary part of the whole thing. How about us hearing from Mike "The Gavel" Garvey. *Micheal Pontiff is backstage with the Gavel Mike Garvey* Pontiff: Ladies and gentlemen, next to me is a man who tonight could become US champion, Mike Garvey. Mike, if I could get a word from you for a second.... Garvey: Listen here Pontiff! You or anyone else in this damn league don't get words from me unless I say so! Lucky for you, I've got some things on my mind! PO Box, you've had your time with the US title, but it's about time that you finally felt what hard justice is all about. A lot of people in the INWI know what it feels like, just ask that punk Springer and he'll tell ya. Pontiff: Well, you sure have had your problems with Springer as of late. Garvey: Don't you mention that name without my permission ever again Pontiff! Pontiff: I'm sorry, I'm really sorry, I didn't meant to... Garvey: You know you meant it...don't make me teach you what hard justice is all about too! Pontiff: Now, we wouldn't want that...there's thousands of fans watching right now.... *Garvey grabs a crowbar and knocks out the camera lens* Collins - I think Garvey is ready for this match and he want's gold again. Scoop - He's my type of wrestler Collins. He said hell with the fans and now he's doing it for himself. Now let's go hear from the almighty guru himself, Commissioner Jerry DeLuise... Jerry DeLuise - Welcome fans to Heat Wave '97! As I have said before, this will be the biggest night in INWI history thus far and this will be so exciting. Also tonight, you will get to see the friend that I have brought in to help watch the backs of INWI execs from bruisers in the fed. It is time that wrestlers laying their hands on myself or other employees of the INWI to stop. Scoop - 'Bout time you did something... Collins - Commissioner, who is this fella that you are bringing into the federation? Is he big, small? Does he have a background in wrestling? DeLuise - I can't say who it is, but nonetheless, he is here tonight. He has wrestled before and he is decent sized. He can handle his own if that's what you wanted to know. But, I just wanted to warn you that he is here. Also on hand tonight is The Bullywug, Glenn Hudson. he was, as you all know, at the press conference last night. He is backstage getting to know some of the wrestlers and officials and watch our first ever pay-per-view. Hopefully, he'll be on hand to wrestle at our next pay-per-view. But, I must go help get ready, so I'll see you all later. Collins - Well fans tonight should be interesting. Will this man show his face or not...we'll have to wait and see! Next we will hear from the Springer... Scoop - Now here's my favorite wrestler...not. This guy is such a moron... he's a geek. Maybe this guy that DeLuise is bringing in should beat the hell out of the Springer and his Bed Buddies... Tiffany Love at the Stingers power plant the Bedroom: TL: Well Springer no gold for you this time around but Michael Hollywood is a tough opponent, what are your plans for him. Springer: Well first of all, I'm glad your the one doing this interview, I didn't like that other dude. Second, this Michael Hollywood will be my next victory, he is tough but I feel I'm in the best shape of my life. Feel these biceps!!! These AB's, my god I can barely contain myself. TL: So this is a stepping stone for you? Springer: I believe it will be. Who is the only one in the INWI to beat the current champion, ME, Who should still be the Northeast TV champ, ME. This current path I'm on I deserve some shot at one of the major belts. TL: A major belt? Springer: YES A MAJOR BELT!!! I've put in the time and the matches, the only ones that I've lost have come at the hands of someone interfering. (In the back ground yelling and screaming) Springer: WHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Hey boys come on over here it's Tiff. Mattress: Wow Tiff you look great, but enough about you, The Comforters are ready to beat some heads in. Where in shape and ready to pound. BoxSpring: You tell them Mattress, WHOOOOOOOOOOOO Where back and ready for some gold of our own, So look out world because here we come. Springer: WHOOOOOOOOOOOO SEE YA AT THE PAY PER VIEW Scoop - Yippee....The Springer is ready and so are the Comforters. Oh happy happy joy joy. I wish I was just like them... those punks. Yeah right... they should be fired... Collins - You just never learn Scoop...you just never learn. Let's go up to ringside where Michael Hollywood and Harvey Thunder are there for comments. (Michael Pontiff is standing in the ring waiting for Michael Hollywood who will face The Springer next.) Pontiff: Ladies and Gentlemen, Please Welcome Michael Hollywood!!!! (Hollywood makes his way down to ringside. It is no surprise that Harvey Thunder is by his side. These two have been inseparable lately. The two enter the ring and stand beside Pontiff) Pontiff: Well Gentlemen, How does it feel to be on a Pay-Per View? Thunder: (Looking at the camera and waving) Hi Mommy!!!!! Hollywood: You can see how excited Harvey is to be here and so am I. This is a very big step for the INWI and I am confident it will bring nothing but good things in the future. Pontiff: Mr. Hollywood, How do you feel your chances are in your match against The Springer? Hollywood: I think my chances are good. I respect The Springer for his ring ability and his past accomplishments. My Career would be catapulted if I could get a win over him. I will go into the match as I do all my matches, With my best effort. If its not good enough then that just proves how good a wrestler The Springer is. Pontiff: I still can't get over how you attitude has changes since you first debuted. I commend you for your honesty. Now since we have Harvey out here, I'd like to get his comments on his opponent: Awesome X. Thunder: Awesome X... X, Y, Z!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!(Jumping up and down) If I beat Awesome X, Then I can be Awesome Harvey!!!!! Can't I Mikey?????? Hollywood: Harvey, You can be anyone you want to be!!!! Thunder: I want to be champion!!!! Hollywood: Well, Maybe someday!!!! Thunder: Is that tomorrow or next week!!!! Hollywood: Only time will tell!!!! Pontiff: Thank you for you time gentlemen but this interview has to end so you can get ready for tonight. (Hollywood and Thunder walk back to the locker room as Pontiff signs off.) Collins - Well, Harvey Thunder is all the same! And he and Hollywood seem focused for tonight. Scoop - There's the match of the year, the Springer against Michael Hollywood. I can't wait for that one, it's where I'll go get another beer. Collins - You have to much aggression Scoop. Speaking of aggression, let's hear from Jonathan Hammer, the Most Extreme Man in the INWI. (Jonathan Hammer is sitting in his dressing room when Micheal Pontiff walks in. Hammer is wearing a black INWI shirt. On the back it says "Only the Strong Survive." He also has on his black wrestling trunks.) Pontiff-Excuse me Mr. Hammer can I get a word with you? Hammer-Well what the hell do you want? Pontiff-What are your thoughts on Jarrid Hoss? Hammer-Who the hell is this Hoss character? I've never heard of him! But I'll tell you one thing boy...he's in for the @$$ whopping of his life. Now get the hell out of here you pansy little girl. (Pontiff leaves and the camera stops taping.) Scoop - See, that guy is cool. He'd kick your ass Collins! I like him! Collins - Well, I would bet he could kick your rear as well. Let's hear from Solid Gold before we have to send it up to Brian Newark in the booth! (The Scene opens outside an undisclosed Gold's Gym. Jimmy Stardom meets the cameraman at the door.) Stardom: Good, You finally made it!!! We are on a tight schedule and Solid Gold wants to speak. (Stardom opens the door and walks in with the camera following. He approaches Bradley who is admisistering a piledriver to a sparring partner. Bradley sees that its time to talk and he walks up) Bradley: Now you can see why Solid Gold could not attend that stupid press conference. We are busy training for our match with The Glamour Boys. They are a tough team but we can and will beat them because we are a tougher team. (Blake approaches wiping his face with a towel) At Heat Wave '97, the INWI's first pay-per view, We will prove to The Glamour Boys and every other tag team that we are not only the uncrowned television tag team Champions, but we are also the future INWI tag team champions. We were robbed of those TV titles on three separate occasions. The last because of some masked man. Blake has something to say about him. Blake: There are a lot of wrestlers who want to get there hands on that masked man. We want First dips on his head. He cost us those TV tag team titles against The Friendship Express, a team we have clearly dominated. We want a rematch to get those belts back for a fourth time. And after that the only way we will part with them again is by forfeit when we win the big gold. Stardom: OK, That's all the little peons need to hear for now. You boys get back to work. The rest of your talking will be done in the ring. (Stardom leads the cameraman outside the gym and slams the door in his face.) Collins - It looks like Solid Gold is ready for tonight! This whole card is going to be great! Scoop - That it will be and this is why the INWI is becoming one of the major players in Wrestling today. Call your cable company and order it NOW! Collins - Well, we have comments from one more tag team. The TV Champs, the Friendship Express are standing by with Michael Pontiff. They defend tonight against the Freaks. Scoop - Oh joy, the peter puffers get to talk. We all know that these guys like to play pirates, butt pirates that is. Argh Matey... let Capt'n Ricky into the canyon.... Collins - ENOUGH SCOOP! Jeepers, we have kids watching at home.... *Micheal Pontiff is unfortunantly standing backstage with the Friendship Express* Pontiff: Ladies and gentlemen, Heat Wave '97 is here and under way! And I'm standing here next to the current TV Tag champions the Friendship Express. Now guys, this is pay per view, and just about anything goes...now, I've noticed a few slight "differences" in you and the other stars here in the INWI. Now, I was scared to mention anything about it earlier, but now that we've gone big time, well..I thought you could comment on what I'm talking about. Ricky: *looks confused* I don't get it, what are you talking about? Pontiff: Well, ya know...um....YA KNOW! Alfonzo: um...no, we DON'T know. Pontiff: um...*getting nervous*...never mind! Let's talk about your title defense against the Freaks tonight! Ricky: Freaks, me and my shower buddy Alfonzo know all about getting freaky don't we?!?!? Pontiff: * ? * Alfonzo: That's right! So tonight, we won't be stepping into any odd situation, because you boys seem to be right up our league! Ricky: Freaks, you can tell that me and my playtime buddy Alfonzo are excited.... *Pontiff's eyes are wondering around and just happened to catch a glance of Ricky's and Alfonzo's tights and notices the strange shape it forms* Pontiff: um...*gulp*....what do you mean when you say "excited" ???? Ricky: I'm saying that we can't wait to get out there in front of all our fans and give them the chance to see us defend our titles again! Pontiff: *sigh of relief* oh, okay, good to hear that! Now let's get back down to ringside! Scoop - Those guys are the real fruit loops if you know what I mean... Collins - Easy Scoop. That's all the time we have for the Free 4 All, so if you haven't already, order Heat Wave '97!
