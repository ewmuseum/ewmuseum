HEAT WAVE '97 June 29, 1997 Madison Square Garden New York, NY =============================================== [The screen opens up with an aerial shot of downtown Manhattan, revealing the famous shot of the "Fuji" sign in Times Square. That shot then fades and then re opens above Madison Square Garden and the outside Event board lit up and moving...INWI presents......Heat Wave '97.. Main Event....INWI Heavyweight Championship Match...Heavyweight Champion....."Lone Wolf".....Brody Thunder.....vs...."The Technician".......Zack Sharp....This time..It's for more..than..just titles...] [The line to enter the history filled arena is around the corner, as eager INWI fans await their chance to enter this historic televised Pay Per View event.] [The camera shots switches to the inside of Madison Square Garden, and already the capacity crowd is going INSANE. Many of the fans have brought with them their various signs. Signs reading....LONE WOLF 4 Life.....ZACK IS TECHNICALLY SHARP...."COLD & CALCULATING" SUCKS....and a strange sign that reads...ROGERS NEIGHBORHOOD..] [As a ring announcer counts down the start of Heat Wave '97, the crowd begins to go insane and count down with him...5...4...3...2...1..And fireworks blow up around the ring and the lockeroom ramp, instigating the sold out standing room only, and arena crowd to go CRAZY. Newark is dressed in a white, collared shirt with a navy blue INWI blazer and a pair of pressed, dark blue, pants, with wing tipped black shoes. Newark has an appreciative smile on his face for the fans support thus far of the INWI most recent Pay Per View. Newark turns to the fans closest to him and his announce position and begins to pump up the crowd even more, as if they needed it.] Brian Newark - The TIME has come!! This is probably one of the most unbelievable things I have ever been a part of and the INWI has definitely gone big time. But it will be hard to stay in your seats tonight ladies and gentlemen because this will be one of the most unbelievable cards ever put on by the INWI. What about the main event? "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder will take on one of his toughest challenges for his INWI Heavyweight Title, the newly crowned Northeast Champion, "The Technician" Zack Sharp. All but one title in the INWI will be up for grabs tonight and even the non title matches will be awesome. We also have the finals of the new Light Heavyweight Championship Tournament. We have some BIG grudge matches with some interesting stipulations involved with them. This whole card is going to be fantastic, so hold on to your seats and lets get it on!
