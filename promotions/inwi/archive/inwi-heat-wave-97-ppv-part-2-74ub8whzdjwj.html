[INWI] Heat Wave '97 PPV - Part 2

29 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
============================================================ Heat Wave '97 Continued LIVE from Madison Square Garden in New York City... ============================================================ Heat Wave '97 Special Stipulations Match Bodyguard Jones vs Psycho Sal This is an interesting match. If Sal wins, Bodyguard Jones returns to Mean Eugene. If Jones wins, he gets five minutes alone in the ring with Eugene. For the whole match, Eugene will be secured in a cage above the ring. There goes Eugene now, up above the ring and he doesn't look happy. There's the bell and we are underway. Sal attacks Jones and they start to exchange blows in the middle of the ring. Jones hits a short armed clothesline and puts Sal on his back. Jones looks up to Eugene and points at him as he scoops up Psycho Sal and slams him to the mat. Jones takes Sal and nails a vertical suplex and then drops an elbow. Jones is possessed in this match and you know he really want Mean Eugene alone for 5 minutes. Jones just Piledrove Sal and that really hit the spot. A cover, 1...2...NO! Sal barely gets the arm up and on our split screen here, you can see Eugene got scared there. He saw his career flashing before his eyes, even though it is a shabby career. Jones scoops up Sal and has him on his shoulder he gets a running start, powerslam!! Jones is dominating this match and he really wants Eugene. He's on the second turnbuckle pointing at Eugene and Sal attacks from behind! He pulls Jones off the turnbuckle and to the mat. Sal hits an elbow and starts to mount an offense. Sal scoops Jones but Jones hits a diamond cutter! He goes for the cover, 1...2...3! It's over in 11:09, your winner Bodyguard Jones. Jones goes outside the ring and then under the ring...he has some handcuffs...what's that for? He has just handcuffed Sal to the ringpost! They are lowering Mean Eugene and Jones is waiting. It's gown to the ground and Jones grabs Eugene. The five minutes starts now! Jones slams Eugene into the ring post and Eugene falls to the ground. Jones throws Eugene into the ring and he climbs to the top. Jones flies from the top rope and nails Eugene with a leg drop! Jones is loving this and you can see him laughing. Jones has Eugene up for a piledriver and nails it! That had to hurt. Sal is still trying to escape and he has the key of the handcuffs! Where did he find that? Jones just nailed a diamond cutter on Eugene and he is out. Sal rushes into the ring and Jones clotheslines him! Jones leaves the ring as an all new man now! The crowd is going crazy as he walks to the back and Eugene is out! Heat Wave '97 Television Championship Mr. Apples vs Preadator Mr. Apples is still watching his back for this masked man after being attacked by him at the press conference yesterday. That took Apples off guard and he is now watching himself. He better not worry to much because he has a big opponent tonight in the Preadator. Apples and Preadator come to the center of the ring and stare each other in the eye and Preadator takes the first swing. Apples steps back and fires back and Preadator stumbles back. Preadator dives with a clothesline and takes Apples down. Preadator grabs Apples and slams him to the mat. Preadator drops a leg off the ropes and Apples is in trouble. Preadator grabs Apples and goes for a suplex. Apples bocks the suplex and reverses it. Apples is back on his feet and has the Preadator on his feet. Short armed clothesline and Apples just flattened Preadator. Apples grabs Preadator and he puts him up on the shoulder and there's a nasty powerslam. Apples is starting to gain the full momentum. Apples up with Preadator, sends him into the ropes and catches him... there's a choke slam!! Apples looks to the crowd and says he is going to finish it off! Apples grabs Preadator... sets up for a suplex, NO! It's the Applesauce Drop! This match is all but history! 1...2....3! Your winner in 10:56 and still INWI Television Champion, Mr. Apples!! Look out! There's the Masked Man and he has attacked Apples again! The two are changing blows in the ring and Apples just flattened him! The crowd is going bonkers! Flamboyant has lowered the cage that held Mean Eugene Last match. The Masked Man has ran out of the ring and Apples is chasing him! The Masked Man has just ran into the cage and they now have him cornered. Apples grabs him and slams his head into the cage a couple of times, then drives him face first into the bottom of the cage. Apples takes him out and sends him into the ring where he sets up and nails the Applesauce Drop! Now he's getting ready to take off the mask... the crowd is going nuts, they want the mask to come off! Apples starts to take it off and oh my god... it can't be... but WHY? The crowd is in shock... It's Mr. Banana!!! Why did he turn on his best friend of so many years? Oh my god.... why oh why? Apples is in shock as is Flamboyant and they are just staring... Banana rolls out of the ring and heads to the back as the crowd boo's him... Apples and Flamboyant can't understand it. Heat Wave '97 Steel Cage Match - Loser Leave INWI Vito Rizzotto vs Gino Rizzotto Well, as they set up the cage for the next match... I can't help but to think of this Apples thing... WOW! Anyways, this is a brother vs brother match. The loser will leave the INWI forever, as requested by Don Rizzotto... this will be an interesting match. Gino is already in the ring and as Vito enters the ring to the crowd going nuts, Gino just stares at Vito. The cage door is shut, it is secured and we are ready to go. This is one of the matches they paid to see! Gino attacks Vito right off the bat and this one is underway. Remember fans, in the INWI you can win cage matches two different ways. One, by pinning your opponent, or two, by leaving the cage through the door or over the top and getting to the floor before your opponent does. Gino just sent Vito into the cage and Vito is busted open already! This match is going to get vicious! Gino has Vito and slams him in the middle of the ring. Gino goes for the door, but Vito grabs his ankle and brings him back. Gino scoops up Vito and sends him back first into the cage. Gino is really using the cage in this match. Gino is rubbing the already bloody head of Vito along the cage and Vito is going to be busted open even worse. Wait, Vito just used a reverse kick and Gino slumps over backwards. Vito grabs Gino and slams him to the mat. To the second rope goes Vito and hits an elbow. Vito up with Gino and sends him headfirst into the cage and again! Now he's trying to wash the cage and Gino is his sponge! Now Gino is broken open and we have a true bloodbath in this match. Vito sends Gino into the ropes and floors him with a big clothesline. Vito heads for the door!! I think he's going to get out! NO! There's Gino and he clobbers Vito. Vito crumbles to the mat and Gino grabs him and sends him into the cage again! There's a powerslam by Gino and he's calling for the door to be opened! He's gotta make it to the other side of the ring! He's gonna eliminate Vito Rizzotto from the INWI!!! Wait, Vito is up! Can he get to Gino? He runs...grabs Gino, OH MY GOD!! MOB DROP!!! 1....2....3!! It's over! Your winner in 15:34, Vito Rizzotto!!!!!!!!!!!!! Heat Wave '97 Steel Cage Match Northeast Television Championship Bill Bob Woijicowski vs "Marvelous" Mike Quick This match should be just as heated as the last one. These two truly hate each other. There's no friendship in this match. As we said in the last match, there are only two ways to win a cage match in the INWI. Leave the cage or get pinned. No Disqualifications, no count outs.... "Marvelous" Mike Quick is in the ring....and he awaits the Northeast Television Champion. Here comes Woijicowski and he storms the cage and then runs to attack Quick. Woijicowski grabs Quick, inverted atomic drop! Then Woijicowski floors him with a short clothesline and the crowd is going nuts! Woijicowski scoops up Quick and slams him, picks him up again and sends him into the cage. That's the thing about cage matches, it's a legal object to use! Woijicowski again sends Quick into the cage and Quick staggers back and to the mat. Woijicowski with a cover, 1..2..NO! Quick kicks out. Woijicowski up with Quick, but Quick pokes Bill Bob in the eyes and then sends him to the mat with a standing drop kick. Quick sends Bill Bob into the ropes, spinning heel kick! Quick has changed the momentum of this match to his favor. Quick again picks up Bill Bob and nails a snap suplex. Quick goes for the door, but Woijicowski grabs him by the tights and keeps him in the cage. Quick starts to kick at Bill Bob then picks him up and powerslams him! What a move, unexpected, by Quick! Quick with a cover, 1...2...NO! Bill Bob slips out. This is definitely for a title, you can tell that by the way these two are going at it! Bill Bob rakes the eyes of Quick and then slams his head into the cage! Quick staggers back and Bill Bob clotheslines Quick from behind as he is falling backwards... that was vicious! Bill Bob really wants this one you can see it! Bill Bob to the top, oh my! He just nailed the spur!!! There's a cover, 1...2...NO!! Quick rolled his arm! Bill Bob to the top again and he nailed a second spur and Quick is down and out! This time Bill Bob goes for a third spur.... No he's climbing the cage! He's over the top...and he drops to the floor! Your winner in 21:31 and still INWI Northeast TV Champion, Bill Bob Woijicowski!!! Quick is still out in the cage, but he did get his shoulder up on a Spur.... amazing. Let's get ready for the Main Event... ============================================================ Brian Newark - Guess what time it is ladies and gentlemen? It's Main Event time here at Heat Wave '97. "The Technician" Zack Sharp, the current Northeast Champion, will attempt to gain the Heavyweight title with a victory over "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder tonight. Let's hear from both wrestlers.... with the champion first.... ============================================================ [A split screen appears with Brian Newark on the left and Brody thunder on the right. Thunder is pacing intensely back and forth with his hands on his hips.] Brian Newark: Mr Thunder...Mr Thunder you're just moments away from what could be the toughest challenge you've had to face here in the INWI the man who's called "The Technician" by his peers and the man who believes tonight will be his night. Are you indeed ready for Zack Sharp? [Thunder stops,cocks his head as if in astonishment at Brian's comments. He rubs his hands together.] Thunder: Y'know Newark...yer startin' ta really tick me off,boy. Ya ask me if I'm "ready fer Zack Sharp". [Shakes his head] Hell yeah,I'm ready fer 'im! If weren't I wouldn't have this strap in the first place. I'm ready ta hand ol' Zack the wrestlin' lesson o' his life an' there ain't no better place ta do it than right here in the INWI. I've beaten the best. Brown,Springer,Apocalypse,Cloud Eagle,Riley, Rizzotto... ..every flamin' guy that's stood across that ring from me. An' tonight...I don't see things changin'. Zack..like I said before,amigo...yer good.._real_good. But.. ...ya ain't the best. That's my domain,my friend..an' ta get there ya gotta put my shoulders to the mat fer three. I don't think ya can. An' in a few minnits we're fixin' ta find out. So Zack...better grow some eyes in the back o' that ego-filled head o' yers..yer gonna need 'em. Now get that camera outta here.... [Thunder puts his hand over the lens as he speaks. The left side of the screen goes to static.] ============================================================ [Continued]
