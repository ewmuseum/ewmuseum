[INWI] Fireworks '97, Pre Gamne Show, July 5

5 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FIREWORKS '97 PRE GAME SHOW.... LIVE ON THE FOX NETWORK =============================================== Joseph Collins - Welcome ladies and gentlemen to the Fireworks '97 Pre-game show live on the FOX Network. We have a great show planned for you tonight, including four title matches. BUT, before we go to the ring in just 30 minutes, we have comments from some of the wrestlers. But, joining me for this Pre-game show is the most outspoken person in the INWI, Scoop Ropes. Scoop - You know it Collins! I am the man and everyone strives to be like Scoop! Collins - Well, I doubt that... but anyways...let's get comments from James "Simply The Best" Pillow, who tonight takes on David McCarter. The Future of The INWI The True Franchise in wrestling HE IS SIMPLY THE BEST <The camara opens to the inside of The Best Of The Best Mansion....James is sitting at a desk with Angel at his side he has on a black armoni suit and Angel has on a white dress.....James is watching a tape of his debut match...he turns it off and laughs> James "Simply The Best" Pillow:Well my presence is known and it will be felt in a big way in my tenure here.....Cause I am The Best....and as you can see I only surround myself with The Best...The best paintings <points behind him> The Best house....and the best <pulls Angel down and kisses her> well you get the picture......now on to my next match with David McCarter...I hope you can at least make me break a sweet...cause the last guy sure didnt....when I finish you off with the best move in wrestling The SimplyBest-Plex you wont get up and I will move one step closer to The World Title...which will be around my waist before the year is over.....cause.... Angel:<whispers in a sensual voice> When you mess with Simply The Best...You Die Like All The Rest..... Scoop - See, now that guy is cool. He knows where he wants to go, and he'll probably end up kicking McCarter's ass. Collins - Well, I guess we will find out later tonight. That match could get one of them a shot at the Light-Heavyweight Championship. Let's hear from Pillow's opponent, David McCarter. *The camera opens up on McCarter working on the heavey bag in his Manchester gym. He looks a bit angry but is still keeping his cool. Every impact throws more dust off the bag in his little gym. Finally he stops and towels himself down, then he motions to the camera* [McCarter] Well, it seems you INWI chaps are fast about getting your scoops ASAP. *McCarter wipes his brow a bit* [McCarter] Well Mr.Sam Adams put up a helluva a fight. I put up a helluva fight....but you mum always said the cookie doesn't crumble onto your plate. Although the ending was a bit quesntionable, I plan on meeting Mr. Adams in the future. And I expect that you my good chap put that Light Heavyweight title on the line. Next time Mr. Adams I won't be such a push over. *David moves over to his locker and begins upacking* [McCarter] Am I upset that I lost?? No. I went undefeated in the tournament until the very end. I still haven't lost a match outside the tourney and I don'y plan on losing any in the future. Mr. Adams you better watch out cause I'm climbing the ladder, and those rungs are getting closer and closer to you. No matter what your thoughts on currnet politics are. Just like China taking back Hong Kong from us, I'm going to take back that title that I deserve. *McCarter makes his way to the showers and then stops for a minute* [McCarter] Sorry chaps, this is were this interview stops. Ciao. Fade Scoop - G'daye mate... you want some tea and crumpets? Hey McCarter, how's Di doing? I had her... Collins - Scoop... WHAT are you trying to do? You can't be that much of an idiot...Let's now hear from Zack Sharp, who will defend his Northeast Championship against Mr. Ed Rogers. He is also annoyed at the loss he suffered to Brody Thunder at Heat Wave '97. [SCENE: A pissed off Zack Sharp storms into the INWI Interview Area wearing jeans and and is bare chested, he tosses the INWI Northeast Title belt aside and pulls on a white "Technician" T-Shirt. He takes a seat in the director's chair in the center of the room and takes a large sip of the beverage provided by the INWI, but instead of setting it back down he throws it against the wall. The glass shatters into bits a pieces as Sharp glares at the camera and speaks.] You know what I hate, I hate pieces of crap who stick there nose where it doesn't belong. The piece of crap I am talking about is....... [Sharp pounds his fist in his hand and his face looks more pissed off] .........Ed Rogers! Mr. Ed Rogers, you had the nerve to walk your sorry caracass down to the ring and cost me the INWI Heavyweight Title. I had the Sharplock on Thunder, I had the champ screaming for mercy, I knew Thunder would not give up, but I was just waiting for his arm to give and his body to collapse. Of course it did not happen this way, no, Mr. Ed Rogers had to hit me with a damn chair. However, Ed Rogers, you have signed yourself up for a dislocated shoulder. The Sharplock the most painful move on this planet, so Mr. Ed Rogers get ready to have a full cast on your arm. Now........ [Sharp swings his head back and he looks to be even more pissed off.] ......Brody Thunder. Thunder, knows he was beat, he knows he should not have that belt around his waist. Yet, what does he do he denies the whole thing, he acts like Ed Rogers' char shot had nothing to do with it. He acts like he broke the damn hold, he acts like he did it all by himself, but Brody guess what there was a third man! Oh my goodness where have I heard that before? Oh my gosh, that is Brody Thunder's excuse for me beating him. Hey Thunder, you can't beat me without a third man now can you Thunder? Atleast, the third man in the match I beat you was legal now huh, Thunder. Deny it all you want pal, but next time I get you in the ring, the contract will be full proof there will be no third man next time. Cage, Outside Interference Clauses, or whatever, but I will get that belt. Thunder, you can say I did not get the job done this time all you want, but the job was done Thunder oh it was done, you can not deny that. Thunder, the next time your third man will be no more. Get ready Thunder cause I am going to take you out......... ...............................One on One! [Fade] Scoop - *pretending to wipe away a tear* That was beautiful Collins. He is going back to the fans. The fans is definitley what got Thunder to where he is, right? Give me a break Sharp... Collins - Well, it just shows that you have no clue what the fans can do to some people. Let's hear from "Marvelous" Mike Quick and we hear he is irate... (Fade in back to the locker area where Mike Quick is irrate. He's going nuts.) Mike Quick: AHHHHHHH! You ******* idiot. Ultimate Perversion you are the dumbest person in the world. You just make me so mad (throws trash can at the wall) How the hell could you hit me. Why did you even try to intefere in the match. I could have won the match if it wasn't for your dumb ass. You know what I owe you a ass kicking Ultimate Perversion. I can't believe this crap man. I'm gonna kick your ass boy. I'm going on a RAMPAGE NOW!! I'm starting all over again and I'm gonna start by kicking your ass Ultimate Perversion. I'm gonna teach you not to **** with me. (Grabs camera and smashes it against the wall. Picture fades out.) Scoop - Ha ha ha!! He is pissed and I think he may be looking for Perversion. Any word if Quick is here tonight? Collins - Word from the back is no INWI officials have seen Quick tonight, so he may not be around to attack Perversion. But Perversion isn't on our live card either. I guess we'll have to see! Finally let's hear from the New INWI United States Champion, Mike "The Gavel" Garvey. *Mike Garvey is waiting backstage with Tiffany Love* Love: Fans, we're about to go up to ringside for this upcoming US title match, but I'm standing here with The Gavel Mike Garvey who just this past weekend at Heat Wave '97, defeated PO Box to become the US champ. Gavel, coming into Heat Wave, you were gunning for the title as the challenger...how are you going to approach this match being on the other side as the champion. Gavel: Miss Love, it ain't no different....PO Box, at Heat Wave I delivered you in the wrong route and beat you like the dog that you are. And tonight is gonna be no different....you might as well stuff yourself in a box and ship yourself off to the hospital, because tonight, you're gonna learn a little more about hard justice. Love: Well, some would say your win at Heat Wave was a little tainted because of Springer. Gavel: Springer, I've told you time and time again, you choose to mess with hard justice, you're gonna pay the consequences. Don't you ever learn boy? Hard justice is not a game, it's a way of life! You chose to play with it, and you found out the hard way. Next time I see you, I'm gonna stuff you and that no good disgrace of a postman PO Box in a shipping crate and drop you off the top of the Washington Monument! Miss Love, if you'll pardon me, I've got a postman to beat. Scoop - Now there's one of my favorites. Ever since he turned his back on the fans a couple of months back, he has been awesome! Wow.... he rules that division! Collins - Well - that's your opinion Scoop. Let's get comments from Jonathan Hammer, who tonight has a shot at Brody Thunder and the Heavyweight Championship! (Micheal Pontiff is standing in the ring in his normal getup!) Ladies and gentlemen please help me welcome to the ring Jonathan Hammer! (Good God by KORN blares over the loud speakers as Jonathan Hammer walks out from behind the curtain with a chair in his hand. The crowd goes nuts. As if on command Hammer hits himself in the head three times with the chair. Blood is everywhere and the crowd is losing it.) Pontiff-Welcome Jonathan Hammer! Hammer-Stop your blabbering you piece of crap! Don't suck up to me, so say what you want to before I get pissed. Pontiff-Can I get your comments on Heat Wave 97? Hammer-My comments don't matter a damn bit it's these peolpe in the audience that matter! (The crowd is on it's feet probably hoping for more blood!) This Hoss character sucked though. The jWo? What's this crap? I could take them all. Pontiff-What do you think of your up coming match with INWI Heavy Weight Champion Brody Thunder? Hammer-To tell you the truth I'm surprised I've got a shot already. Brody Thunder is my kind of wrestler though...he don't take crap from anybody. We could be real good friends if it wasn't for the fact that I'm going to take his title. (The crowd erupts in a chorus of cheers.) Don't get cockey though Thunder I've been training real hard for that title shot. I'm taking the title and there ain't a godamn thing you can do about it! (Hammer heads back to the back to the cheers of the crowd as Good God comes back on!) Pontiff-Well folks that's Jonathan Hammer Scoop - Well, he is extreme... let's see what happens when he faces the Champ tonight. Collins - Well that's all the time we have. let's go into the US Air Arena and to Brian Newark for the call of tonights matches! ============================================================ =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The INWI "The revolutionary force in E-Wrestling" Visit us on the Internet: http://www.catskill.net/harmer For info, E-mail: [detail withheld] INWIŽ, July 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
