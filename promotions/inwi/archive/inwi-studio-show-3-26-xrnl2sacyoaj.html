INWI - IN THE STUDIO March 26,1997 ===============================================================- Scoop Ropes - Welcome ladies and gentlemen to the first ever INWI Studio Show here in Delhi, NY. On this show we will have some interviews, four matches and announce some future events. How's this for news though, The Glamour Boys are in some trouble today after the sentence passed down by the INWI Board Of Directors. After their brutal attack on Commissioner Jerry DeLuise, something had to be done. These two think they can run this federation, but they have another thing coming. As for Commissioner DeLuise, he is still under observation in the hospital, but is ok. We have taped comments from him later in the show. Let me introduce to you my partner in crime for tonight's show, Michael Pontiff, one of the INWI's best interviewers. Michael Pontiff - Thank you Scoop and we do have a great show planned for you tonight and we hope you enjoy it! We have FOUR great matches planned for you tonight and three of them are title matches, while the other is an interesting handicap match, with the loser leaving the INWI. Thsi could spell doom one half of the Glamour Boys. Can you imagine what these two would do if Jimmy Cougar was out of the INWI?? WOW!! How about we go up to ringside for our first match of the night... but before we go there, we have Tiffany Love in the back with Cloud Eagle earlier this evening. We also have an tape given to us by Brody Thunder. From what I understand, this was taped a day after his title winning performance. We'll check that out after Cloud Eagle's interview. ===============================================================- <The camera focuses on a Native American sitting cross legged on the floor, smoking a pipe.
