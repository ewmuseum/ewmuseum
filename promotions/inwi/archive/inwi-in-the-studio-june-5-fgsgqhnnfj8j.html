[INWI] In The Studio, June 5

6 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
INWI - IN THE STUDIO June 5,1997 INWI Studios, Delhi, NY =========================================================== [Once again we return to the highly lighted recreation room of Zack Sharp's house. In one corner sits the familiar pool table completely cleared with balls. On the walls are posters and photographs of Mr. Sharp's illustrious career. To another corner of the room sits Sharp's trophy case filled to the brim with Sharp's many titles. Zack Sharp thought sits in a comfortable black leather chair facing a large bay window that looks out onto the ocean. He is decked out in his usual Armoni suits on this particular day it is a dark green one. His long black hair is tied back and he tries to hide the visible stitched wound on his face. He then turns to face the camera and speak ] Zack Sharp: Well, my busy schedule has finally caught up with me. Days after suffering this gaping wound on my face in another place that the INWI officials told me I can not mention, I will step into the ring with Cloud Eagle. I am not saying I can't handle it, but who knows what this wound could do to me. [ Sharp scratches himself as if to be thinking ] Ah, I have had worse. Now Cloud Eagle, let's see what he is all about I guess I will have to check the great INWI homepage. [ Sharp sits up and walks over to his already booted up computer. He double clicks on American Online, types in his password, and does the rest of the pointing and clicking until he reaches the roster page of the INWI homepage ] Ah, let's see he is one of the good guys so I don't need to expect and dirty tricks. He has a manager I hope he doesn't cause any trouble. Look he is about the same size as me. I also see he is one of those great Native American wrestlers. [ Sharp points and clicks his way back to his desktop and then leaves the station and goes back to his seat by the window ] So Cloud Eagle you should be another tough competitor here in the INWI. I will come to the ring and I hope you will too. The two of us will go nose to nose in the center of the ring and may the best man win. Well, I guess since I am such a cocky S.O.B I guess I should say I am going to win, but I won't. That's right the great Zack Sharp has finally found himself mortal! So Cloud Eagle, I will say this may the best man win, I just hope you come prepare because if you don't you will be victim to the Ultimate Demise. See ya boys later! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Welcome ladies and gentlemen to another edition of INWI In The Studio! And what a main event we have for you tonight! Loser leaves the INWI in a no holds bar street brawl! The two most extreme wrestlers in the INWI, Jonathan Hammer and "Hardcore" John Stilwater will try to put the other out of the INWI in this most interesting matchup! Newcomers Zack Sharp and Tony Houston look to keep their winning streaks on the line tonight! Without further delay, let's head up to the ring for our opening match of the evening. ============================================================ Match 1 "Marvelous" Mike Quick vs "The Big Fan" Devin Terry Mike Quick, after a wild match loss to Zack Sharp, is looking to get back into the wiining ways as he takes on Devin Terry tonight. Quick enters the ring and attacks Terry right off the bat and he looks like he's ready for this one. Quick nails Terry with a quick lariat and puts him on the mat. Quick drops an elbow and then scoops up Terry. Quick with a slam and then a standing leg drop. Once again he scoops up Terry and nails a belly to belly suplex then heads to the top rope. Terry working his way to his feet and Quick nails a dropkick from the top rope!! Quick looks like he wants to end this one and there's the Quick Ending!! 1...2...3!!! It's over! Your winner in 3:01, "Marvelous" Mike Quick!! Wait a sec, look out!! Quick just got jumped from behind by the Springer and Bill Bob Woijicowski!! The crowd is going nuts and Springer nails Quick with a crowbar! Here comes Mike Garvey and we know he and the Springer and Woijicowski aren't the best of friends! Springer and Woijicowski head out of the ring laughing and exit through the crowd. Man, I guess this stuff with these four is never going to end! Match 2 "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston vs Steven Casper Tony Houston is trying to keep his impressive streak of quick wins tonight against Steven Casper, who is also on the upswing of things. Houston, who as I have said before in my opinion, is the cockiest SOB in the INWI and so far has backed things up. Houston enters the ring with a monster next to him. WHO the hell is this? I'm going to try and find out who this guy is as soon as we can. There's the bell and we are underway. Houston grabs Casper and sends him into the ropes....clothesline!! Casper hit the mat hard on that one! Houston drops a thunderous elbow and then laughs at Casper. He's over by the ropes talking to this monster and Casper is up. Roll up!! 1...2...NO! Casper almost had an upset victory! Houston is now pounding on Casper and almost swings at the ref! That would of been a definite DQ! Houston regains his composure and nails Casper with a leg drop and gets a two count. Houston scoops up Casper and hits a snap suplex! Houston again up with Casper and hits a belly to belly suplex. Hey, I just got something here....this guy at ringside is Houston's bodyguard, "Bulldozer" Tony Riley and he is huge. Houston throws Casper out of the ring and then gets the ref tied up. Hey, Riley grabbed Casper and powerslams him outside the ring. Riley then fires Casper in the ring and Houston is laughing. Houston takes ahold of Casper and nails the Cold Drop!!! 1...2...3!!! Your winner in 7:27, "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston! Houston and Riley leave the ring with another great victory. ============================================================ Brian Newark - Before our next match, let's go up to the ring, where Michael Pontiff is standing by to interview Solid Gold! ============================================================ (Michael Pontiff is standing in the ring ready to conduct an interview.) Pontiff: Ladies and Gentlemen, Please welcome the current INWI Television Tag Team Champions, Bradley & Blake Gold, Solid Gold!!!! (Solid Gold has ripped off WCW's Ric Flair as the theme to 2001 blares over the loudspeaker. Bradley and Blake emerge from the locker room and make their way to the ring proudly wearing their championship belts. When they make it to the ring, Pontiff is ready to ask questions) Pontiff: It seems you two have been very busy as of late with Michael Hollywood, Harvey Thunder, and The Friendship Express. Not to mention The Glamour Boys. Blake: These are all people who are jealous of Solid Gold and they have every right to be. Just look at us!!! Bradley: Yes. We have proven time and time again that The Friendship Express is no match for us. They claim that they want to have their way with us in the ring? (Looks down at the TV title) These belts prove that you can't get the job done. You guys have had all the chances in the world and we are still the champions. Pontiff: Yes but it seems that you always have your manager Jimmy Stardom at ringside to help you out of trouble. Blake: Listen up. Jimmy is our manager. He handles our contracts and gives us spiritual support at ringside. We don't need him to win matches, We just need him to sign them. Pontiff: Some people like The Friendship Express may disagree with that statement. Bradley: Well, "Some people" just can't accept the fact that we are the best in the INWI. Look at Michael Hollywood for example. He quit the House Of Style and look what has happened to him. He lost to a member of the jWO. Those guys can't beat anybody. I told you he was a loser...(The Golds look stunned as the crowd erupts into cheers. They see Michael Hollywood and Harvey Thunder Storm the ring and stand with them face to face) Pontiff: Michael Hollywood, You look like you have something to say!!!! Hollywood: I couldn't just stand in the locker room and listen to these guys spew out there garbage anymore. You keep interfering in our matches night after night and I am getting sick of it!!! That is why we want to put an end to it once and for all!!!! (Bradley and Blake get worried looks on their faces) Pontiff: Michael, What are you getting at here!?!? Hollywood: If you two have the guts to get in the ring, We challenge you to a tag team no holds barred match that won't end until both men on one team are unable to continue!!! We don't care if the titles are on the line or not!!!! Pontiff: Oh My, That is a huge stipulation!!! Do you have a response? Blake: (Looking dumbfounded.) Uh... We have to consult our manager for this one!!! Bradley: ...Yeah... We won't comment on that now. Hollywood: I knew it, You guys are chicken!!!! Come on Harvey!!! (Hollywood and Thunder turn their backs to leave the ring but Solid Gold jump them from behind. The four start a brawl that spills out into the isle and leads back to the locker room.) Pontiff: Oh my!!! What a brawl we have going here!!!! We will try to send a camera to the back to see how this develops. The challenge has been made!!! Will Solid Gold accept!?!? These four really hate each other. I am out of here. (Pontiff drops his microphone and walks to the back of the arena.) ============================================================ Match 3 "The Technician" Zack Sharp vs Cloud Eagle Here's another hot newcomer and he is also undefeated in the INWI. The Technician is still looking for his first title shot. Sharp enters the ring and attacks Cloud Eagle at the bell and this match is started up. Sharp nails Eagle with a belly to belly and he takes control early. Sharp up on his feet and sizes up Eagle and drops a knee, but Cloud Eagle rolls out of the way and Sharp crashes to the mat. Eagle grabs Sharp and slams him to the mat and then bounces off the ropes and splashes Sharp. Sharp rolls towards the rope, but Eagle grabs him and scoops him up. Eagle has Sharp up and drops a powerful suplex that knocks the wind out of Sharp. Now Sharp rolls out of the ring and Eagle follows. Eagle catches up to Sharp and attempts a suplex, but Sharp blocks it. Sharp then nails a suplex of his own and that looks like it hurt both people. Back in the ring they go and Sharp grabs Eagle and connects with a DDT!!! Eagle looks hurt and Sharp picks him up and sends him into the turnbuckle and Eagle goes in face first. Eagle bounces off and Sharp rolls him up, 1...2....3!!!! Oh my lord, I can't believe Sharp just got Eagle on that rollup and I don't think he believes it either! Wow what a surprising finish to that match! Your winner in 8:47, "The Technician" Zack Sharp!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Before we head back to the ring for our main event, we have comments from "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston, who won his match earlier this evening. ============================================================ [After yet another rather easy match, easy as far as "Cold & Calculating" Tony Houston is concerned, Houston makes his way back towards the lockeroom amist a crowd full of booes. As Houston walks through the lockeroom curtain several other wrestlers great him with nothing more than a "screw him" look on their face. Houston paying no significant attention to the other wrestlers and heads straight for his luxurious lockeroom.] [Houston sits in his chair that reads "Tony Houston" on the back of it and relaxes. Though Houston just successfully defended his claim as being "..The toughest and most methodical warrior ever to bless the INWI" his demeanor is remarkably calm and calculating. Not a single bead of sweat is apparent on the conditioned body of this World wide contender] [Houston lifts his head and flashes the now notorious Jack Nicholson smirk] Houston: Damnit, if I've said it once I've said it a million times, I WANT SOME COMPETITION. I came here to the INWI expecting some of the toughest fighters in the world, and so far, I've whooped a bum, a has been, and a never will be. [With a cocky tone] I do realize that I AM GREAT but ya'll surely can provide some kind of challenge. It's not hard now to realize how this league has some punk ass champions. As far as Billy Bob the Redneck he wants to know if I'm the real deal..CAll me a wannabe, son, I've checked your record son, to be quite honest, Your record is not all that impressive, what are you 1 match about .500? [Sarcastically] That does make you an icon doesn't. Son, When you can remain a high winning percentage you can question the ability of "Cold & Calculating" Tony Houston, but until then, just stop frontin' and admit that you are the honory president of the jWo. But I guess that's a damn redneck for you. But now onto more important matters. [Houston walks to his leather briefcase, enters the appropriate combination then snaps it open. Houston pulls out a black Pentium labtop and inserts the power plug into the nearest outlet and then turns it on and hits enter] Houston:[to the camera crew] Watch this. [The labtop reveals a downloaded file of one of the more successful wrestlers in the INWI. Showing several of his matches on way to a top ten ranking. However, for some odd reason the face of this man is obscured with some sort of a hazy covering.] Houston: Now ya'll are probably wondering what top INWI athlete is that. Well, son, if ya'll can't figure out, Why the hell would I reveal my plan...my calculating plan. Boys, of the INWI let's just say only one person will ever know what I am doing. And it's me, son. Not even the almighty upstairs now what the hell I'm doing. Just one of many reasons that I have EARNED the name "Cold & Calculating". [Houston whistles and his 123lbs Rotteweiler emerges from almost thin air.] Houston: [with a grin] Guess which one of us has to leave..ya'll best bounce....[talking to the camera crew] Watch 'em Cujo [the rotty begins to reveal his carinvorous teeth and slowly walks towards the camera crew. [The camera crew quickly exits Houston's lockeroom as they wonder just who the hell he was talking about....] [Screen begins to fade, but a loud voice emerges from behind Houston's door.] Voice: INWI BE AWARE........ ============================================================ MAIN EVENT Match 4 - Loser Leaves Town, No Holds Barred Jonathan Hammer vs "Hardcore" John Stilwater Can you believe this one? Newcomer Jonathan Hammer, who already lost a match to Stilwater, signed for this match, a loser leaves the INWI match, No holds barred. Stilwater, a former United States Champion, is on an upswing, looking for gold again. Hammer is in the ring and here comes Stilwater and he has a chair! WOW! Stilwater just cracked Hammer with a chair and Hammer falls to the mat. Stilwater goes for the quick cover, 1...2...NO!! Hammer gets a shoulder up! Stilwater up with Hammer and nails a suplex and then sets up for a piledriver and he nails it with Hammers head on the chair!! Stilwater scoops up Hammer and tosses him over the top rope and goes out after him. Hammer is looking hurt already and Stilwater scoops him up and sends him in the ringpost head first and it looks like Hammer is broken open! Stilwater is laughing and it must be he thinks he has this match won already. Stilwater again up with Hammer and drops him throat first on the ring side railing and then sends him into the ring post again! Hammer against the post and Stilwater charges with a chair, but Hammer falls out of the way and Stilwater crashes into the post with the chair. Hammer rolls back into the ring and Stilwater is trying to get to his feet. Finally he is up and rolls into the ring and Hammer leaps on him and starts nailing him with left and rights. Hammer staggers to his feet and grabs the chair that was in the ring. Stilwater works his way to his feet and Hammer nails him with the chair...and again and it looks like Stilwater is broken open as well! Hammer scoops up Stilwater and Stilwater goes low and Hammer falls to the mat. Stilwater scoops up Hammer and nails the DDT! That's his move, this match is over!!! 1...2...NO!!! Oh my god, Hammer got his shoulder up and Stilwater can't believe it. He is up in the refs face trying to change that call, but this match must go on! He's got the ref and it is not looking for the man in the pinstripes!! WAIT!! Hammer is up and he has the chair!! He just nailed Stilwater and drops the chair! Stilwater stumbles back and Hammer nails a reverse DDT on the chair! There's the cover, 1...2...3!!!! OH MY GOD!!! Stilwater has been beat and he is out of the INWI!! What a major win for the youngster, Jonathan Hammer and he is going nuts in the ring! Let's get the official word.... Your winner in 13:27, Jonathan Hammer!!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Wow, what a great match and a great card! John Stilwater is out of the INWI and Jonathan Hammer has made a quick name for himself! Tony Houston and Zack Sharp also scored some major wins tonight and this is getting exciting here in the INWI!! Mike Quick also got back into the winning ways, but he was attacked by Springer and Bill Bob Woijicowski after the match. Well, for the INWI, I am Brian Newark saying so long from Delhi!!! ============================================================ =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The INWI "The revolutionary force in E-Wrestling" Visit us on the Internet: http://www.catskill.net/harmer For info, E-mail: [detail withheld] INWIŽ, May 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
