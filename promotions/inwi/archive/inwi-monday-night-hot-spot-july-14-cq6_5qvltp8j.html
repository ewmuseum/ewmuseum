[INWI] Monday Night Hot Spot!! July 14

15 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
INWI Monday Night Hot Spot!! ======================== Monday, July 14, 1997 Ugly Mug, Cape May, NJ ========================================================== (Symphony of Destruction by Megadeath blares over the soundsystem, and Adam Kuhl makes his way to ringside, with his baseball bat in tow, and makes his way over to the commentators table. He threatens the ring announcer and takes the mic out of his hand, and then approaches Brian Newark.) Adam Kuhl: Well, Newark, I hope you're happy! After your little tirade against Double J yesterday, he's been barred from the arena tonight! Huge POP from the crowd. Kuhl taps his bat on the table. Adam Kuhl: I oughta take your head off with this bat right now! Brian Newark: You touch me and I'll sue, Kuhl! What are you even doing here? You're not an INWI wrestler...you're not even a licensed manager, and your buddy is barred from the arena! I'm surprised the commissioner hasn't been out here already to take you away! Kuhl starts laughing hysterically. Adam Kuhl: You had best check your facts, Newark! I just filed papers with the INWI this morning! Newark looks stunned. Brian Newark: What???!!! What will you be...are you a wrestler? A manager...? Adam Kuhl: Wouldn't you like to know? Wouldn't the whole world like to know? All the wrestlers in that locker room are shaking in their boots right now that the One Man Crime Spree could be coming after them! Well, I'm not ready to tell anyone right now! When the time is right Newark, you'll know and the whole world will know, but for now, I'd just warn you to all watch your steps! Brian Newark - Welcome ladies and gentlemen to Monday Hot Spot!! We have returned to the Ugly Mug in Cape May, NJ for this great card. TWO title matches plus.... Mr. Cherry and Mr. Banana will do battle. The odds are Mr. Apples is lurking around in the back. Also on the card, Michael Hollywood will take on Oz!! All this and more, so hang on to your seats! Let's go up to the ring for our opening match. Before we go to the match itself, let's hear from Oz!! ============================================================ [scene opens...Pontiff is getting ready for his interview with Oz..he stands outside a majestic green door...] Voice: Come in. [Pontiff swallows the lump in his throat and turns the knob...inside the smell of incense overtakes him...there are burning candles, and in the back ground some mellow music from Pink Floyd is playing....all he can see of the big man is a pair of bright green eyes...] Oz: I've been too quiet here in this federation. (takes a breath and sighs)...my partner, Chandler has been proving that the NBK are the team to beat...now I must take an active stance here in the INWI... Pontiff: What are your goals, Oz? Oz: Silence, you puny fool. I only have one goal..and that is total and complete dominance of the INWI. Soon the INWI will be another part of my Emerald Kingdom.... Pontiff: What do you mean? Are you gonna take over the INWI? Oz: I need not explain myself to you. Only hear this. Someday, the INWI will recognize me as the savior and will flock to me and beg for my leadership, as they did in the Emerald City so long ago... Pontiff: Any thoughts on your opponent tonight? Michael Hollywood is quite a talent! Oz: Hollywood..the place where stars are born. It only pales in comparison to the glamour of the Emerald City....soon you will all see this. Michael Hollywood, I am sorry that you are the one who will be in the ring with me for my emergence as a major player.... Pontiff: Are you predicting victory for yourself tonight Oz? Oz: I predict nothing. I make no estimations or guesses. The fact is, I will be the one with the win after tonight. Not him. [in the background, "Another Brick in the Wall" begins playing...Oz reaches up and hits a button..."No Excuses" by Alice in Chains begins...] Oz: As the great Layne Staley of Alice in Chains puts it...I will be champ, and there are No Excuses that I know of... Pontiff: When do you expect to get a title shot? Oz: you've wore out your welcome... [ out of the darkness walks a large white man wearing all white and carrying a bible..] Bishop: Sir, it's time for you to leave... Pontiff: But I ain't ready! Bishop: (staring directly in his eyes..) The Lord is ready for you to leave...and what the Lord wants....(grabs Pontiff and throws him threw the door, breaking it in two)...he gets... Oz: Thank you.... [camera fades out...] ============================================================ Match 1 Michael Hollywood (With Harvey Thunder) vs Oz (With Jason Chandler) This should be interesting. Oz is one of the biggest wrestlers in the INWI. And would you look at that lady with the Oz sign? Oz will you marry me? Whoa... that's a little to much for me! Oz sees it and goes to hug the lady...wait, the lady just came over the railing! She has...oh my god...the wig....it's Jammin' Jimmy Cougar!! He's got the Cougar Clutch on Oz!! Adam Kuhl is out and they are double teaming Oz! Michael Hollywood is just watching from the ring. Here comes Jason Chandler!! Chandler is all over Cougar and Kuhl and chases them away! Oz is woozy and still has to wrestle Hollywood! Wow, what a beginning! Chandler goes to the ring with Oz and I think we are finally underway! Michael Hollywood about to lock up with the man who is definitely the biggest thing in this federation...OZ!!!The big Oz just throws Mike into the turnbuckle hard. He catches him off the rebound with a big powerslam. Oz off the ropes now...He misses the leg drop. Hollywood up and delivers kicks to the bigger man's mid section. He tries the bodyslam....he does it! Hollywood up top now, a big elbow drop across the collar bone. He picks Oz up, Oz looks dazed, Hollywood off the ropes and drop kicks the big man, but it doesn't faze him. Another one...and still no effect. Hollywood again with another drop kick and the great and powerful Oz just swats him down. Oz picks up Mike now and powerslams him. He lifts him up for a chokeslam but Mike is flailing his legs and kicks Oz where it hurts! Oz is down,Mike picks him up and hits a suplex, what strength! He mounts the top, he's signalling.....Oz is on his feet....CLOTHESLINE OFF THE TOP! Pin....1...2....3!! Your winner in 12:01, Michael Hollywood! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well....next up will be the first of our two title matches tonight. Solid Gold will look to regain the INWI TV Tag Titles. This match should be inter--- Bradley Gold: Hold on Newark, We have something we wanna say. Newark: Well, It appears Solid Gold have invited themselves in. What do you guys want? Blake: Just listen up and we will do the talking. Right here tonight we have a rematch with The Friendship Express to get our TV tag team titles back for a fourth time. Bradley: Nothing is going to get in our way this time. Hollywood & Thunder better keep their noses out of it, so we can show the world just who the best tag team in the INWI is. Blake: And the whole world now knows that the mysterious masked man is Mr. Banana. Well You have to be Bananas to think you can cost Solid Gold a championship and get away with it. Banana, We will get our revenge very soon!!!! Bradley: Solid Gold is madder than ever and that is bad news for everybody else in the INWI!!! ============================================================ Match 2 - Television Tag Titles Solid Gold (With Jimmy Stardom) vs The Friendship Express This is a match we have seen many times before. Solid Gold looks ready for this, but better watch their backs because they are marked men here in the INWI! It will be Blake Gold and Ricky Sanchez starting things off. Sanchez off the ropes and drops Blake with a drop kick. The champs also look focused! Blake back up and a low blow on Ricky and then Blake sends Ricky outside of the ring. Blake has the ref tied up and Bradley is beating on Ricky outside of the ring. Alfonso Geller tries to come in to help Ricky but the ref stops him and Solid Gold double teams Sanchez! Geller finally back outside the ring and Blake tags in Bradley. Bradley to the top rope and nails a leg drop! Ricky needs a tag really bad in this situation. Bradley sends Ricky into the ropes, back body drop...NO! Ricky kicks Bradley in the face. Bradley crawls over and tags Blake. Ricky leaps and tags in Alfonso! Blake puts on the brakes, but Alfonso grabs him, into the ropes, drop kick! Alfonso back up with Blake and slams him to the mat. Alfonso to the second rope and nails an elbow. Alfonso sends Blake into the corner, hops on the second rope and starts to pound away on Blake. The crowd counts along and they get to 10. Wait... Blake grabs Alfonso...inverted Atomic drop! Both men go down. Alfonso barely up, tags in Ricky. Ricky over top of Blake and goes to scoop him up. Wait!! There's a roll up!! He has the tights..... 1....2....3!! Holy Cow!! I can't believe it! We have new champions! Your winners and NEW INWI TV Tag Team Champions, Solid Gold in 13:29!!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Before our next match, which should be an interesting one, we have Mr. Ed Rogers. I guess he has an interesting offer.... let's hear from Rogers now... ============================================================ ( The camera impolitely cuts to darkness, right in the middle of one of Scoop's sentences. With a sudden burst of brilliance that only is found in dark coat closets when someone pulls the light switch, the least sane man in wrestling stands before you. In his hands he holds a strait jacket, open and ready for it's next customer. ) Ed Rogers: Hello Neighbors. Hello INWI. ( Ed releases one hand's grip on the jacket so that he may wave into the camera. ) Hello Zack. ( Ed lets the sleeveless piece of apparel drop to the floor. The look on his face changes from that of dumb found euphoria to one of... regret?) Ed Rogers: Zack, why are you acting so difficult? We all know that you have lost it. I gave you a chance to come and meet my friends at the sanatorium. But your still as stubborn as a bull. ( The camera zooms in, a little closer to Ed.) Ed Rogers: Zack, I hoped that it wouldn't come to this but it has. I had hoped that you would see that your illness was hurting those you love, but..... oh it's to late to explain myself now. What I am doing, needs no explanation. ( The camera takes a super tight close up of Mr. Rogers. The entire screen is consumed by his worried face.) Ed Rogers: Zack, I gave you fair warning. I told you to go to the mental health hospital. You didn't. But when a child doesn't take the medicine it's mother gives it, what does the mother do? SHE SHOVES THE PILLS DOWN THE BRAT'S THROAT. That, Zack, is what I must do for you. If you do not go to the sanatorium by yourself, I am going to force you to go. I challenge you to a Strait Jacket Match. The winner gets the Northeast Title. The loser gets a two week paid vacation to El Wacko Sanatorium. Isn't that the best consolation prize you ever heard of? ============================================================ Match 3 Mr. Cherry (With Mr. Flamboyant) vs Mr. Banana The Masked Man turned out to be Mr. Banana. Now we all know that many people are after his head and he is facing former friend and stablemate Mr. Cherry. What made Banana turn his back on all those years of friendship? I don't understand and NO one has been able to get anything out of him. This should be an interesting match to say the least. There's the bell! And now former friends turned enemies are staring off in the ring. Mr.Banana taking on Mr. Cherry. The larger Banana now just choking Cherry...he picks him up in a choke lift! Banana now throws him across the ring. What power! Mr. B picks Cherry up and throws him into the ropes....big mafia kick to the skull! Deadly maneuver. Mr. B now with the limp Cherry in hand has him in a full nelson. I don't think Cherry can counter that sort of move...wait, he just went low on Banana! that's a way to counter it. Cherry now with a headbutt down low as well. Off the ropes...and drops an elbow. He mounts the top, and sentons onto Mr. B still in agony! Cherry taking advantage of the downed big man now and he frog splashes off the top rope again. He mounts up one more time....another splash...Banana had a boot up and Cherry goes down. Banana back in control with a piledriver...he sends Cherry to the ropes, Cherry with a drop toehold out of NO WHERE! Mr. Apples runs out from the back and this could get interesting! Banana doesn't see Apples! Cherry complaining to the ref about something now. Apples is in the ring!! Apples turns Banana around and kicks him in the mid section! He scoops up Banana...setting up....he just nailed the Applesauce Drop!!! He quickly leaves the ringside area and Cherry now mounts the top. Cherry Bomb Moonsault onto Banana!!! 1....2....3!!! Big Win! Your winner in 11:01, Mr. Cherry!!!! MAIN EVENT Match 4 - Tag Team Championship The Terminators (With Johnny Danger) vs The Comforters (With Dr. Bedpan) It's main event time here in Cape May, NJ! And we are ready. The Comforters, one of the most popular tag teams in the INWI today will try to win their first titles tonight! They face strong opposition in The Terminators, who have held the INWI straps for over a month! These guys are good. It's going to be Mattress and Blade starting things off in this match. Blade rushes Mattress and tackles him to the mat and nails a bunch of lefts and rights. Blade up with Mattress, sends him into the ropes and sends him to the mat with a clothesline! The champions are already starting to take control of this match. Blade tags in Gunner and Gunner grabs Mattress. Gunner with a DDT and a cover! 1...2...NO! Mattress gets his shoulder up and he needs to get a tag. Gunner sends Mattress into a turnbuckle and follows with a vicious knee to the midsection! They have Mattress in their corner and the Terminators are double teaming him! Box Spring coming across but the ref sees him and sends him back to the corner. Mattress just slumps to the mat as Gunner tags in Blade. Blade grabs Mattress, suplex...yes! Right in the middle of the ring! A cover, 1...2...NO! Once again, Mattress gets his shoulder up! Blade looks mad now. Blade gets into the refs face, I think he thought he had him for the three count. Mattress is crawling...he makes the tag to Box Spring!!! Box Spring in and Blade is shocked! Box Spring with a kick to the midsection...sends Blade into the ropes...big clothesline! The crowd is starting to get pumped here and The Comforters can smell the win! Box Spring scoops up Blade...blade with a thumb to the eye and he tags in Gunner. Gunner attacks Box Spring and these two are duking it out in the middle of the ring! Box Spring drops Gunner and Blade charges! Box Spring drops Blade with a clothesline and Blade rolls out of the ring! Mattress on the apron ....he dives on Blade outside! Box Spring with a big suplex on Gunner. Johnny Danger on the apron...wait, Dr. Bedpan just grabbed Dangers foot and Danger went chin first on the apron... he's out! Mattress has Gunner...powerbomb! Mattress on the top and he dives with the splash and the Sealy Posturepedic!! The cover, 1...2....3!!! It's over! Your winners and NEW INWI Tag Team Champions in 16:46, The Comforters!!!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Two new champions crowned here in Cape May! And what a card it was. Jimmy Cougar is trying to make his mark and look at the Apples/Banana thing... that is just heating up and could get interesting. The Comforters won their first ever titles and Solid Gold won their fourth TV Tag Titles. What a card. For the rest of the INWI, I am Brian Newark saying so long!! ============================================================ =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The INWI™ "The revolutionary force in E-Wrestling" Visit us on the Internet: http://www.catskill.net/harmer For info, E-mail: [detail withheld] INWI®™, July 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
