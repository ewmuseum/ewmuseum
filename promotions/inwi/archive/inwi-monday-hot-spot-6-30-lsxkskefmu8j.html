[INWI] Monday Hot Spot!! 6-30

INWI Monday Night Hot Spot!! ======================== Monday, June 30, 1997 O'Flannery's Pub, New York, NY ========================================================== [The scene fades from the insanity restarting as Susan Powter, with her annoying self, being strapped to the electric chair, as many state residents have grown tired with her.] [The screen opens with several hunderd people surrounding a small tavern in Little Ireland in New York City. O'Flannery's Pub is the scene of the expanding venue of the INWI. As a large amount of people wait on line to either enter or in their attempts to find a ticket from local scalpers, a man slowly walks past the crowd and steps over several drunken men laying on the crowded asphalt and walks right towards the entrance.] [The large bouncers attempt to stop this man who simply returns their questions with a head butt followed by sereral punches and even a 3 Stooges like eye gauges, and continues on his way into the building and then stops at the bar.] [The man is dressed in a long, black leather trench coat, black Starter work out pants and white Fila sneakers. The man is wearing black Armani sunglasses but soon, removes them to reveal his icey blue eyes. The man calls over the bartender] Bartender: Wus yer poison laddy? [Man lifts his head and the bartender takes a step back in shock] Bartender: Oh sorry, Mr. Houston..the usual.. [The bartender returns with a Killians Red longneck, and awaits for instruction from Houston.] Houston: [taking a healthy swig of his brewsky] What time does all this start, boy? [Bartender retorts that the event will be starting shortyly] Houston: Ok, well I'll be back. [Houston finishes his barley and hops and leaves the bottle on the bar then seemingly disappears into the crowd.] [The crowd on the outside has finally reached their seats in the small, yet comfortable surroundings around the squared circle in the middle of the modest pub.] [The Bartender enters the ring and begins to attempt to pump up the crowd for the night's following action, and successfully the drunken Irishmen begin to start thumping their feet in anticipation of the first match.] [As the Bartender prepares to introduce the first match's combantants but is interrupted as a boom box begins playing Wild For Da' Night and "Cold & Calculating" Tony Houston enters the ring and the bartender wisely passes the microphone to Houston.] Houston: Wuz' UP My PEOPLE...[Crowd pops] Shut the hell up, ya'll can just sit there and thump your feet but now..Shut the hell up..'cause "Cold & Calculating" has something to say...[Crowd booes] It would appear that the powers that be, have scheduled me in the near future with some character who calls himself "Marvelous" Mike Quick, the quickest man in wrestling. Have ya'll ever seen this man, he's Anorexic..he's like 33lbs, ain't no wonder he's quick, but son, if you really believe that you can hang with Tony Houston, you are as sick as the disease that has surrounded your pathetic life. You talk about a ******* Quick Ending..Boy, the only Quick Ending you'll be seeing is when you are dropped on your throat with the Cold Drop..TA DOW!!!! So if you are man enough, step up to the plate, otherwise just get to steppin' bitch ass. [Houston throws down the mic. and exits the ring amist a chorus of booes, the bartender, in the ring, picks up the mic and begins to restart his introduction.] Brian Newark - Welcome Ladies and gentlemen to Monday Hot Spot!! And that was an interesting opening! We are coming straight off of one of the Greatest shows ever put on by the INWI, our first pay-per-view, Heat Wave '97. Heat Wave '97 saw "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder retain his INWI heavyweight Championship is a controversial, but classic match with "The Technician" Zack Sharp. And what about the Masked Man? Mr. Banana turns on long time best friend Mr. Apples? Why? Mike "The Gavel" Garvey also won in controversial fashion, but is the new INWI United States Champion with his win over P.O. Box. Sam "The Minuteman" Adams won the first ever Light Heavyweight championship and all of the other champions retained their titles. Tonight we have a couple of matches for you and we will have words from some of the wrestlers last night.... including Brody Thunder!! I bet Thunder has a lot to say from la---- (Brian Newark's welcome to everyone is interrupted by "The Beautiful People" by Marilyn Manson.) Newark: We all know what this means, fans! The Glamour Boys aren't even scheduled to be here tonight. What's the meaning of this. (The camera pans to the entrance to the dressing room, and for about 20 seconds, no one comes out, but the music still plays. Then, Double J, Jammin Jimmy Cougar comes out, accomponied by a women on either shoulder. He slowly makes his way to the ring, basking in the pure disdain that the fans are throwing his way. He steps into the ring, but not before instructing the women to stay at ringside.) Brian Newark: Well, this is certainly an uncalled for visit. You and Starr aren't even scehduled for interview time right now! Should I get Tiffany Love out here? Jimmy Cougar: What's the matter? You don't have the balls to interview me or what? (Strong reaction from the crowd. A fan in the front row shouts out Double J is Double gay and Cougar screams back at him.) Brian Newark: No, I'll interview you. I just thought... Jimmy Cougar: See, that's your problem Newark! You think too damn much! Now, I've got some things to get off my chest! Last night, the INWI had it's big PPV debut, and from all accounts, it was a success! (Huge POP from the crowd) Brian Newark: Yes, in fact, we are certainly making strides to getting into the "big-time". Jimmy Cougar: No, you're not! That's where you're wrong my friend. Last night should have been the crowning glory for the INWI, and instead it was a complete joke! Your esteemed commissioner buried the Glamour Boys way down at the bottom of the card wrestling in some stupid grudge match against Solid Gold for a SHOT at the tag team titles. Brian Newark: Speaking of the Glamour Boys....where is Jason Starr tonight anyhow? Jimmy Cougar: I'm getting to that, Newark, so just shut the hell up! (Big reaction from the crowd, some negative, but a fairly vocal heel pop) Jimmy Cougar: Now, why the hell are the two biggest stars in this sport wrestling a couple of no-namers in a meaningless match in the first hour of a PPV? It makes no sense to me! Brian Newark: It was hardly a meaningless match, Jimmy! Jimmy Cougar: Yes it was! Since when did I give a damn about tag team titles? I'm World title material baby! I realize that this rinky dink little promotion doesn't have a World title, but that's gonna change! Remember when I said that this promotion isn't on the right track to the big time? Well, when I win the Heavyweight title, it will have done it! I will be the first INWI World Heavyweight champion! Brian Newark: Are you nuts, Cougar? You have a mediocre singles record at best here in the INWI, and Brody Thunder is practically undefeated! How do you plan to accomplish even getting a shot, let alone winning the belt? Jimmy Cougar: I won't fill you in on all my secrets, but step one was getting rid of Jason Starr! (A hush of disbelief falls over the audience.) Brian Newark: WHAT??? The Glamour Boys are no more? Jimmy Cougar: That's right, baby! He was holding me back! I proved to the world last week who the better wrestler was when I kicked his ass in the lightheavyweight tournament, and I'm sick of tag teaming anyhow! I was demanding singles matches from Day one in this fed, and the commissioner just wanted to push the Glamour Boys! Well, now he doesn't have a choice! Now, for part two! I've got someone watching my back that will strike fear into the hearts of anyone who gets in my way! Even though he's only 5 ft 11 and 225 lbs, he's the meanest SOB you'll ever meet in your life, and I'm proud to introduce to you all, the One Man Crime Spree Adam Kuhl!!!!! (Cougar turns toward the entrance to the dressing room, and Newark and all the fans look that way as well. "Symphony of Destruction" by Megadeath plays and everyone awaits the appearance of Kuhl. Thru the crowd a scruffy man carrying a baseball bat makes his way to the ring, but hardly anyone notices. "Beautiful People" begins again, and Cougar and Kuhl walk out of the ring.) Brian Newark - Well that was interesting... Jimmy Cougar dumps "The Hollywood Kid" Jason Starr? Now that's interesting. Why on earth would he dump the former U.S. Champion and a partner that was helping him to the top of the tag team ranks. That is interesting to say the least. Let's go up to the ring for our opening bout. Match 1 Cloud Eagle vs Raver Cloud Eagle, accomponied to the ring by Ultimate Perversion, is ready for a match with Raver. Eagle awaits the bell and then he attacks. Eagle chops at Raver and then sends him into a corner and chops some more. Cloud Eagle with a body slam on Raver and then an elbow drop. Cloud Eagle scoops up Raver sends him to the ropes and puts him down with a big chop! The crowd here at O'Flannery's is going crazy! Cloud Eagle up with Raver and powerslams him to the mat! Eagle to the second rope and and drops a knee on the already out Raver. Cloud Eagle then sends Raver out of the ring to the cheers of the crowd. There's Ultimate Perversion... he has Raver and sends him face first into the ring railing. I think Raver might be flowing with a little crimson as we speak. Perversion scoops Raver and slams him outside and then picks him up again and rolls him into the ring. Eagle picks up Raver and executes a nice belly to belly suplex! I just think he ought to finish this off because Raver is already hurt badly. Eagle scoops up Raver and places him on the top rope. Cloud Eagle with a belly to belly suplex from the top rope! Oh my god! He then stands Raver up, there's the Spine Tingler! A cover, 1...2...3!! It's over. Your winner in 9:47, Cloud Eagle! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Michael Pontiff caught up with the new INWI Light Heavyweight Champion Sam "The Minuteman" Adams a little while after he won the title. Let's check out what he had to say. ============================================================ (Michael Pontiff is infront of the Statue of Liberty in New York. It is only an hour after Sam Adams won the INWI Light Heavyweight Title and he is already drunk out of his skull. He has taken a soap box out and is standing on it, makeing a fool out of himself. Mike attempts to get an interview from the champion.) [Michael Pontiff] Mr. Adams, if I could have a momment of your time? [Sam Adams] Hey! Everyone wants a momment of my time now, buddy! I'm a champ! Wheaties, and Nike, and Gatorade are calling me all at this very second! I know how God feels! The question is, how much do you want to get an interview with the REAL American? [Michael Pontiff] Well, I really would like to talk to you. The fans of the INWI- (Sam Adams holds out his hand.) [Michael Pontiff] What? Oh.... (Mike pulls out his wallet and hands over a fifty.) [Sam Adams] That's better! You know, I never talk to anybody before they show the proper respect to our fore fathers. Now, what the heck do you want? [Michael Pontiff] Well, some of the fans of the INWI think that your victory may have been...uh...tainted. [Sam Adams] Fans-Smans! Commies, the lot of 'em! Why, was the victory at Gettysburg "tainted" 'cause a few no-good rebels got their heads handed to 'em? Was the victory at Normandy "tainted" 'cause we had to put a few nazies in shallow graves? I think not! [Michael Pontiff] So, your saying that you didn't pull McCarter's tights? [Sam Adams] What do you think I'm say'n? I maybe did pull at tights, but I already had the match won when I did. I had heard that he was from some thrid world European Country like England or somethings so I was just checking the label on his boxers to see if they were made in Canada, or Texas, or some other communist place, AND THEY WERE! (Sam starts to scream over the crowd.) HEAR THAT EVERYBODY? DAVID MCCARTER IS A LOW DOWN DIRTY COMMUNIST PUNK! HE HATES AMERICA! HE SUPPORTS THE NEW WORLD ORDER! HE RIDES KAWISAKI BIKES INSTEAD OF HARLEY'S! HE IS FROM MANCHESTER ENGLAND! HE IS A SOCIALIST, A MARXIST, A COMMUNIST, A MAOIST, AN ANTI-CAPITOLIST, AND A CHICKEN! [Michael Pontiff] Ahhh...yes.....I have a feeling Scoops is going to like this guy. Back to the H-Q. ============================================================ Brian Newark - Pontiff had to hand over a fifty...that was actually funny. I wonder if that goes on his expense account? Later tonight we will also have comments from Brody Thunder. Michael Pontiff caught up with him at the airport and they chatted about the main event at the Pay-per-view. First let's go to this special announcement. ============================================================ Mr. Ed Rogers. One of the craziest individuals to ever grace the ring. His acts are crazy, zany and sometimes out right nuts. See the real Mr. Ed Rogers when we show you all of his greatest hits and interview the man himself. This will be something you DON'T want to miss. Stay tuned for more details... ============================================================ Brian Newark - I bet that will be insane. That man is not all there, but that could be one of the most prodacative inteviews of all time. I actually can't wait for that one. Mr. Ed Rogers' Greatest Hits. That show will be out soon fans, so stay tuned for details! Let's go up to the ring for our next match. ============================================================ Match 2 - Debut Match "Simply The Best" James Pillow vs Jerrid Hoss Here is one of the hot newcomers to the INWI. James "Simply The Best" Pillow. Can't tell that he's a little cocky, can ya? I wonder if he's related to Tony Houston with that kind of arrogance. Pillow enters the ring and he has his valet Angel with him. Pillow looks ready for this debut and there's the bell and we are underway. Hoss, of the jWo, had to wrestle twice yesterday! I'm sure he is still sore from that. Pillow sends Hoss into the ropes and nails him with a belly to belly off the ropes and could probably pin Hoss right now if he wanted to. Pillow up with Hoss and slams him to the mat and he heads to the top rope. Pillow with a leg drop from the top and he gets up and laughs at Hoss. Of course we all laugh at Hoss, he's a member of the jWo, that is if they still exist after Mr. Ed Rogers beat them all by himself last night! Pillow sends hoss into the ropes and drop kicks him out of the ring. OH MY GOD! Pillow follows it up with a marvelous suicide dive! This guy could have a great stay here in the INWI! Pillow on his feet grabs Hoss and sends him into the ring. Into the ropes goes Hoss...a super kick. It looks as though Pillow is thinking about a cover or not, there it is. 1...2....3!! Your winner, in his debut match in 7:31, James "Simply The Best" Pillow! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well, James Pillow looks tough and he could have a good stay here in the INWI. Let's see what he does down the road! With his weight, he can also compete for the Light Heavyweight Championship! Okay wrestling fans, just like we promised we've got an exclusive interview with the man who retained the INWI heavyweight title at Heatwave '97 in very controversial fashion...Brody Thunder.The INWI's own Micheal Pontiff caught up with the "Lone Wolf"earlier this morning at the airport. Let's roll that videotape now... ============================================================ [Cut to video footage of Brody Thunder sitting in a lounge seat looking just off camera. Then a quick cut to Micheal Pontiff who is seated opposite Thunder and who looks just off camera.] Pontiff: I guess I should start with the obvious question that's on everyone's mind...how's the arm? [Thunder smiles and extends his arm gingerly while rubbing his elbow. The smile twinges with pain as he bends the arm back into a resting postion.] Thunder: I'd be lyin' if I said it didn't smart a little. I don't think any permanent damage was done but jus' the same... I'm takin' 'er easy with it. Pontiff: When he put that armbar on...the Sharplock...when he put that on you everyone thought he'd won the match. What was going through _your_ mind as you laid there? [Thunder face contorts to an expression of concern.] Thunder: I was lookin' fer the ropes ta break the damn hold..what the hell d'ya think I was doin'? Ain't nothin' wrong with goin' fer the ropes ta break a hold...it's in the rules. It's a defensive manuever o' escape...that's all. Cuz I'll tell ya this much...I sure as hell wasn't gonna give up. That ain't in my nature. I'd jus as soon have my arm snapped that quit in a match. plain an' simple. I-don't-quit. If anyone tells ya differently then point'im out an' I'll..'readjust'..their thinkin'. Pontiff: It was at this point when the controversy started. The referee was inadvertantly injured and was down on the mat,unable to see what was about to happen. And what was about to happen was shocking to say the least... [Cut to footage from Heatwave '97. Zach Sharp has Thunder in the Sharplock. The voice of Brian Newark can be heard in the background as the video continues:] > "Whats all the commotion outside the ring? It's Mr. Ed Rogers and he has > a chair! He just nailed Zack Sharp and again! Sharp is forced to release > the hold and Rogers grabs Sharp and nails the Puppet Master! Rogers is > still mad over Sharp winning that Northeast Title..." [Cut back to Micheal Pontiff.] Pontiff: Ed Rogers comes in... [Cut to a quick shot of Thunder smiling.] Pontiff: ...and hits Zach Sharp with a metal chair. Not once but twice. Of course Sharp lets go of the hold and is all but out cold. Ed Rogers then stands over the fallen Zach Sharp almost taunting him and could've inflicted more damage...but you...you just watched it happen. [Cut back to the grinning Thunder.] Thunder: Now what the hell was I _supposed_ ta do? Rogers comes in an' waffles ol' Zach with a Texas flyswatter,as we used ta call 'em, an' I'm the bad guy? How? How does that make me the bad guy? I was checkin' my arm fer damage not babysittin' the ref. It's his job ta keep the law in the ring...not me.You call it 'controversial'...I call it good fortune. That's the way I see it. [Cut back to Pontiff.] Pontiff: I recall that you had warned Ed Rogers once before about inter- fering as I recall... [Cut back to Thunder who leans back in his chair a bit.] Thunder: Get yer facts straight. What I said was if he came ta my neighborhood lookin' fer _me_ I'd dust 'im like an old rug. But his beef weren't with _me_...Pontiff...it was with Sharp. If that chair come down on my squash would ya be sittin' here askin' Sharp why didn't he stop it? Hell no an' you know it! But fer some flamin' reason ya wanna hold me up to a different set o' standards all because the 'good guy' gets his noggin knotted up,right? Tell me again how things ain't bias 'round here,Pontiff. You know it an' I know it. Don't try an' cover it up. [Cut back to Pontiff,who sports a look of contempt.] Pontiff: Well...be that as it may...you then proceeded to add insult to injury... [Cut to more video from Heatwave '97. This time Brian Newark's voice is loud and clear: > "I don't think Sharp knows where he is after the brutal attack Ed Rogers > just put on him. Thunder signals....he looks for it.... there's a cattle > buster DDT!" [Cut back to Pontiff.] Pontiff: ...you execute a cattlebuster DDT on him. A nearly unconcious man and you execute a manuever that few concious men would be able to withstand. And yet it wasn't over yet... [Back to yet more video from Heatwave '97. Brian Newark's voice now in a high-pitched almost scream: > "The cover, 1...2...NO! Sharp rolled a shoulder! Holy cow!!" [Micheal's voice now speaks over the video which continues showing Thunder hammering a semi-concious Sharp with punches.] Pontiff: You set the already injured Zach up for your signature manuever... [The video continues as Brian Newark's voice again is heard loud and clear: > "...there's the Thunderbolt. No one gets up from that! 1....2....3!! It's > over, but it wasn't easy! Your winner in 26:57 and...." [Newark's words fade out as Micheal Pontiff's voice is heard.] Pontiff: ...you hit him with the Thunderbolt. [Quick cut to Thunder who's got a cheshire cat grin going.] Thunder: Didn't kick outta that now,did he? He,he... [Back to Pontiff.] Pontiff: No..no he didn't. It was feared that he might've sustained serious damage as a result but we've not received any word on his condition. Not that you'd care but... [Cut to Thunder.] Thunder: Hah! Listen...Zach's a big boy. He knew the risk when he decided to become a professional wrestler. Now I may not agree with the man but I do know this about Zach Sharp. He's tough. Tougher than most. But last night he met the man who is the toughest. He lost. Plain an' simple. Not my fault he's got as many enemies in this sport as I do. That's all part o' the game. Zach knows it. That's why he's in this business. If he couldn't handle the work he'd be flippin' burgers right now. . [Back To Pontiff.] Pontiff: I sense an almost underlyin' respect between you and Zach Sharp. Is that the case? [Cut to Thunder. He sits up and rubs his jaw.] Thunder: Like I said..I may not like the guy. He's a tad bit too high on his moral horse fer my taste......but I do respect him. Sure. In this business, when ya see folks who've paid their dues an' make it to the top despite the hardships... yeah sure ya gotta respect that. I think Zach would feel the same way. He's a pro. A pro goes out there night after night an' gets the job done. Do I respect him.....yeah. Am I gonna be downin' a few longnecks with him? Don't hold yer breath. [Back to Pontiff.] Pontiff: The big question. Will there be a rematch? [Thunder sits back in the chair. He looks back at Pontiff in a cold stare.] Thunder: Why? So I could beat him up again? How many times do I gotta beat him up? How many stitches do I have ta give 'im? How many injuries do I have ta inflict....before everybody sees the truth. The cold hard fact is... ...I _am_ the best there is. Bar none. I've beaten everyone the INWI has put in front o' me. That's why I'm the champ. That's what a champion does...he beats the competition. [Cut back to Pontiff.] Pontiff: There are those who feel a true champion would not have taken a tainted victory such as what we witnessed last night. Thunder: So what? Whaddaya think I should do? Just say 'Oh I'm sorry ya got yer head tattooed by Rogers,Zach. Why don't _you_ take the belt.'. That ain't the way the game's played,amigo. If that had been me face down on the mat don't you think fer a minnit ol' Zach wouldn't smell gold an' take a three count. Ya win some. Ya lose some. That's life. A rematch? I've said all along that my equal ain't been born yet. Whoever steps through them ropes with is gonna have one helluva fight on their hands. I don't care if it's Sharp..Rogers..Houston.. Garvey...jWo...Brown...Hollywood...Chandler... even that name- stealin' punk Harvey Thunder. Makes no difference. I'll lace 'em up an' put 'em down. Jus' that easy. [Back to Pontiff.] Pontiff: Well it's been very... INTERCOM VOICE: Flight 343 is now boarding at Gate 15A. Flight 343 is now boarding at Gate 15A. Thank you. Pontiff: Looks like we've got perfect timing. Well thank you Mr Thunder for taking the time to talk with us. Thunder:[sarcastically] Yeah right. Had a ball. Adios. [Cut back to Micheal Pontiff who looks directly into the camera this time.] Pontiff: There you have it. Straight from the...Wolf's mouth, if you will. Back to you guys in the studio. Brian? [Cut back to a shot of Brian Newark.] ============================================================ Brian Newark: Well Thanks Mike for that very revealing interview. Brody Thunder is quite a perplexing man. One minute he'll speak with respect for Zach Sharp and then in the next sentence he'll rip him apart. Very interesting. I'm sure the war between these two greats is far from over. Now wrestling fans it's time for the main event. Tonights Main Event will pit Jason "The Crusher" Chandler against Michael Hollywood. ============================================================ Match 3 MAIN EVENT Michael Hollywood vs Jason "The Crusher" Chandler This could get either of these two a title shot in the near future if they win this match. Michael Hollywood attacks at the bell and we are underway. Hollywood was accompinied by Harvey Thunder and Chandler was by himself. But Chandler is a scrapper and he would fight 2 on 1 if he had to. Hollywood scoops up Chandler and slams him to the mat. Hollywood off the ropes and drops an elbow. Hollywood again up with Chandler and there's a suplex and a cover. 1...2...NO! Chandler kicks out. Hollywood again lifts Chandler and Chandler pokes Hollywood in the eyes. Hollywood stumbles back and Chandler scoots around him and theres a belly to back suplex by Chandler! Chandler scoops up Hollywood, vertical suplex, yes!! Chandler with a weak cover and Hollywood kicks out at the one count. Chandler slams Hollywood, then kicks him out of the ring. Chandler follows out and slams Hollywood into the railing. The two go up the runway a little and Chandler just DDT'd Hollywood on the runway and then just dropped Harvey Thunder with a clothesline! Chandler breaks the count and comes back out. Chandler delivers a double nogging knocker to Hollywood and Thunder! Wow! Chandler runs back into the ring and Hollywood stumbles to the apron. Chandler grabs Hollywood and suplex's him over the top rope! Chandler with a cover, 1...2...NO! Hollywood up and Chandler is yelling at the ref. Hollywood attacks Chandler and Chandler falls to the mat. Hollywood just powerslammed Chandler. Hollywood to the second rope and nails an elbow. Hollywood has the momentum shifting back his way...wait a second...LOOK OUT!! Solid Gold just rushed the ring and attacked Hollywood! There's the bell! Thunder in the ring and Solid Gold get rid of him right away! Solid Gold are taking it to Hollywood and Chandler just left the ring. Here come officials and Solid Gold scatters.... Hollywood is out in the ring. Your winner in 21:21 as a result of a disqualification, Michael Hollywood! ============================================================ Brian Newark - That was an interesting ending and I think that Solid Gold and Hollywood/Thunder will meet up again sometime. Well, we are out of time, so for the rest of the INWI, I am Brian Newark saying see you this Thursday for In The Studio!!
