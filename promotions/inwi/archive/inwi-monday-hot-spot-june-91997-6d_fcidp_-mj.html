[INWI] Monday Hot Spot!! June 9,1997

9 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
INWI Monday Night Hot Spot!! ======================== Monday, June 9, 1997 The SPIN Nightclub, Portsmouth, NH =========================================================== Brian Newark - Welcome Ladies and Gentlemen,to INWI's Monday Hot Spot. We have some very exciting matches for you tonight and we are LIVE on.... VOICE: Newark....shaddup. Brian Newark - Oh no...not again... VOICE: These folks in this waterin' hole don't wanna hear yer dribble. What they wanna hear is who's sorry ass is next in line ta be kicked by me! [The theme from "High Plains Drifter" echoes throughout the room. A spotlight shines on the curtain at the top of the aisle. A few seconds later Brody Thunder emerges carrying a mic. He's dressed in street clothes and black hat. Over his left shoulder is the INWI heavyweight title. Firmly clenched in his teeth.He makes his way to the ring.] THUNDER: Y'see all o' these so-called "superstars" here in the INWI seem ta be fallin' short in their quest ta become champion. Last night it was Bodyguard Jones. Good fighter...great loser. An' how 'bout the rest? Ol' Pimp Daddy Brown.... ...Animosity.....Apocalypse....the Springer...Billy-Joe-Bob Whajamacallim..Cloud Eagle..the list goes on an' on.The way I see it there ain't a man in this organization that can put my shoulders ta this mat fer three seconds. [Heel pop! Thunder slowly walks around the ring looking into the crowd.] THUNDER: But I still wanna know if there's a man left in this god- forsaken hellhole that has the...._guts_..ta come down here right now an' sign this contract ta face me in two weeks on Monday Hotspot. Now I know them monitors back there are on so all o' you "superstars" can hear me. All I want is a man. ONE man...who thinks he's got what it takes ta run with the Wolf. [Thunder lowers the mic and watches the curtain. A few seconds go by and nothing happens. Thunder raises the mic again.] THUNDER: Ain't this sumthin'? Not one wrestler back there is man enough ta come out here an' step in the same ring with me ta sign a piece o' paper..let alone face me in a match. It jus' goes ta prove what I been sayin' all along. There ain't a man in this organization....period. Well Newark...ya go tell that two-bit commissioner ya got here..that the next time I come out here I want a name on a contract. I'm as serious as a snakebite,my friend.An'if I don't get a name on that contract soon... [Thunder smiles that familiar evil grin.] ...then I'll start findin' my own victims. It's that simple. [Thunder throws down the mic and exits the ring. As he disappears behind the curtain, Brian Newark begins again...] ============================================================ Brian Newark - Um, ok....wow, interesting to say the least. Brody Thunder is a man on a mission I guess and we'll just have to see what happens in that situation! What a card we have for you tonight ladies and gentlemen! TWO Title matches, the debut of David McCarter, Tony Houston and Jason Chandler go at it and the first Scoop Raps with the INWI PR man, Scoop Ropes. Let's get comments from one of the INWI's newest superstars, then check out his debut match!! ============================================================ *The camera opens up on a misty morning in Manchester. The fog somehow has made it into the streets and the only source of light is buzzing from the lamp posts. A figure can be seen jogging, and pumping his fists in the air. The familiar "clap, clap" of running shoes hitting the slick concrete is getting bigger and the shadow finally has some features to it. He is a medium built man, 6 foot maybe 6'1". He looks bulky but the sweater hides it well. He makes his way to a nearby bench and puts his foot up so he can tie his shoelace* [McCarter] Cheers, I see those boys from the INWI have finally made their way to Manchest. Well it's good to see something new around here. *McCarter finishes up with his shoe and takes in a deep breath* [McCarter] AHHHHHHHHHH!!! I jsut love the way that fog rolls into here on the rare occasions that it does. You're lucky to be here, but I'm babbling right now so onto matters. *McCarter goes into some stretchs* [McCarter] It seems to be, that the powers what ever they are guided me to the INWI. And I am scheduled to wrestle here very soon. Who?? Well that all depends on who the brass see fit. *McCarter finishes his stretchs* [McCarter] Well cheerio, I've got tranining to finish. Why don't you stay around a while and see what's happen around here. Great town. *McCarter continues off into the morning, as the "clap, clap" of his tennis shoes fade off, the fog soon swallows the shadow again.* Fade Out. ============================================================ Match 1 - Debut Match David McCarter vs Pig Pen (of the jWo) New blood has hit the INWI and David McCarter gets to open his career here against a member of the jWo, Pig Pen. And this guy lives up to his name, he is a slob in every aspect. McCarter doesn't seem to like the fact of who he has to fight here and his look of disgust shows that. McCarter slowly enters the ring and bounces off the ropes and dropkicks Pig Pen, but he doesn't fall. McCarter attacks with some punches and nails a belly to belly suplex. How did he get his arms around Pig Pen though??? McCarter jumps to the second rope and nails an elbow drop. McCarter up with Pig Pen and sends him into the rope and nails him with a flying knee to drop the big man once again. McCarter again hits a second rope elbow and scoops up Pig Pen. He just slapped on the sleeper!!! Pig Pen is looking groggy.....the ref is going for the arm. 1....2....3!!! Pig Pen is out and his arm dropped three times!! Your winner in 5:31, David McCarter!!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well, Davis McCarter was successful in his debut here in the INWI. Next up is another newcomer, Tony Houston going up against Jason Chandler. Let's get comments from Jason Chandler now! ============================================================ Chandler: Lately, I've been on the wrong end of the 1-2-3...well that's about to change! Cold and Calculating, huh? That's no match for the CRUSHER! I am the best wrestler alive today! Soon, the INWI will bow down to me, and realize that I deserve to wear gold more then any one else around here. I am the master of the suplex, and there is not a move I can not do. I am sorry to have to put you through hell, rookie, but it's the luck of the draw! ============================================================ Match 2 "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston vs Jason "The Crusher" Chandler This match is an interesting one. Chandler, who is on an upswing is one of the men the INWI fans think is an uncrowned champion. He's got all the tools, but has had bad luck in his title matches. With a win here, he may be able to grab ahold of another title shot. Houston, in my opinion, is one of the cockiest SOB's in the INWI and he'll have his hands full tonight! Houston enters the ring and Chandler attacks him right away! Chandler scoops him up and nails a suplex! Chandler does call himself the master of the suplex and shows another with a belly to back as Houston gets up! Houston rolls from the ring and can't believe what has happened. The Bulldozer hops on the apron and starts screaming at the ref, who comes over trying to get the big man off the apron. Chandler comes over as well and nails Bulldozer and Riley falls from the apron! Lookout, Tony Houston just clipped Chandler from behind and he is looking to end this match like right now....he is slapping on a scorpion.....oh my, but Chandler reaches the ropes. Houston up with Chandler and nails a snap suplex. Houston with a leg drop, then picks up Chandler and slaps on a reverse DDT and he is looking for the win again. There's another scorpion in the center of the ring but the ref is over with Bulldozer Riley and Chandler is in serious pain. There's some serious comotion outside the ring, it's ULTIMATE PERVERSION!!! He just hopped in the ring and the ref doesn't see him and Riley is trying to get in to show him! Oh my god, Perversion just nailed Houston with a chair right across the back of ther head!! Perversion rolls out of the ring and goes into the crowd and Riley is trying to follow. Meanwhile in the ring, Chandler is up and nails a german suplex!! There's the bridge, 1...2...3!! Oh my lord, Houston has just been pinned and that's all in part to Ultimate Perversion. I wonder if Perversion was just sick of Houston's cockiness....wow! Your winner in 12:56, Jason "The Crusher" Chandler. ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well folks,it's time for a new segment on Monday Hot Spot!! Scoop Raps is going to be the view of the INWI from the eyes of Scoop Ropes with guests and everything you can imagine! Let's go to the set for this weeks installment! ============================================================ SCOOP RAPS with Scoop Ropes Well people, now they want me to do my own segment here on Monday Hot Spot!! It's not enough that I already do ALL the PR work around this five and dime promotion? Man oh man, now, I have to do my own segment. Well this week's gues is......me! I am my own guest and you know why that is? Because I love myself and I am the greatest thing to ever happen to the INWI! Ok, enough about me, I have to talk about stuff. My first thing is what about these Rizzotto Brothers? Is this a riot or what. They both look like they are from the mafia, they are causing eachother matches and they keep talking about "HIM." Well, who the HELL is HIM??? I want to know dammit! If I was involved with that messed up situation, which thank god I'm not, I would club Gino if I was Vito. Look at what Vito has done. TV Champion and Northeast TV Champion. Now if he gets rid of that oaf Gino, maybe he'll get gold again. Gino is a walking advertisement for clueless people! I can't get over that moron Gino though.... What about this masked man running around the INWI getting involved with everyones matches and not caring who it is? I have heard what people will do to this guy when they get their hands on him, but no one has been able to yet! I like this guy, he is a rebel and he is stirring up the INWI. Everyone is pissed at him and that's to cool. I think he's getting done what he wants to and there's nothing anyone can do about it! And is anyone ever going to beat Brody Thunder for that INWI Heavyweight Strap? NO WAY!! Ha ha ha!! I tell you what the INWI should do, call it the Brody Title and leave it at that. This guy is just awesome and I don't think anyone, other than myself that is, can beat him! Well, they are telling me I'm out of time, and I knew they would once I got started. So I'm Scoop Ropes and until next week, stay out of my face! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Next up is the TV tag team championship between Solid Gold and The Friendship Express. Wait a minute....look who's in the crowd, it's Jimmy Cougar...and Tiffany Love is going to get comments from him. Let's get those before the next match. ============================================================ (Jimmy Cougar is spotted at ringside, watching the matches. Tiffany Love approaches with a microphone.) Tiffany Love: Mr. Cougar, what are you doing in the sta... Jimmy Cougar: Cut the Mr Cougar crap, toots! You can call me Double J, or just Jimmybaby like you did last night! Tiffany Love: Cougar, I'm warning you, I'm not putting up with any of that today! I just want to know why you're here! Jimmy Cougar: I'm just scouting the comp...HAHAHAHAHA! Just a sec, I'll get this out..the competi....HAHAHAHAHAHA! Tiffany Love: What's so funny? Jimmy Cougar: Well, just the thought that there is ANY competition for me in the INWI is enough to put me in stitches! These are untalented loafers who don't belong in the same ring with greatness such as myself! The only person in this upcoming tournament we're having with anything close to the talent that I have is my good friend and tag team partner Jason Starr! Tiffany Love: Speaking of the Hollywood Kid, where is HE right now? You two are usually inseperable. Jimmy Cougar: He's training for his big match with Mike Quick. Tiffany Love: Which begs the question...why aren't you training for your match with Ed Rogers? Jimmy Cougar: MR ROGERS???!!! HA! This is about as close to training as I could possibly get for that oaf! Listen, Tiff...just book your tickets for the finals of this Ringmaster tournament, cause you're going to see something that you've never seen and never will see again, and that's the Hollywood Kid against Double J in the finals! And don't think we won't go all out when it happens, cause this Ringmaster crown could be just the steppingstone either one of us need to finally ascend to the heavyweight championship of this worthless promotion! Tiffany Love: Just one more question, Jimmy... Jimmy Cougar: No more questions! I'm trying to watch the matches here, babe! Make yourself useful and get me a hot dog! Tiffany Love: For the INWI, I'm Tiffany Love! (Camera fades out and we hear Tiffany) Tiffany Love: What the hell do I look like, a slave! Get your own damn hot dog, you egomaniacial little..... ============================================================ Match 3 - TV Tag Team Championship Solid Gold vs The Friendship Express These two won't be happy until they kill eachother. This is one hatred I am glad I am not involved in. This one is for the straps and there's the bell to start things off. It'll be Ricky Sanchez and Bradley Gold starting things off. Ricky shouts to the crowd to get them going and it works as the crowd here starts to get into the swing of the match. The two lock up in the center of the ring and Ricky slaps on a headlock and Bradley sends Ricky into the ropes, to the mat, leap frog and Ricky nails a drop kick and down goes Bradley. Ricky looks to scoop Bradley and gets a thumb in the eye and then a stun gun! Ricky is hurt and Bradley tags in Blake. Blake scoops Ricky and nails him with a standing suplex and Ricky crashes to the mat real hard. Blake drops a leg and tags in Bradley again. Bradley picks up Ricky sends him into the rope and Ricky reaches out and tags Alfonso Geller! Bradley doesn't see it and clotheslines Ricky and goes for the cover. Alfonso leg drops Bradley and picks him up and slams him to the mat. Geller to the top rope, he flys, Bradley moves and Geller crashes to the mat. Bradley scoops Geller, sends him into the ropes and he crshes into the ref and now the corners empty! All four men are going at it in the middle of the ring and Blake sends Ricky over the top rope and into the ring barrier. Hey, look who it is! It's the masked man!! He jumps in the ring and he has a crowbar?? What in the world?? He nails Blake and sends him over the top rope! He then nails Bradley and then nails Alfonso? He's clearing house!! He just laid Alfonso on top of Bradley and leaves the ring. The ref comes to, 1...2...3!! WE HAVE NEW CHAMPS!! Your winners in 14:21 and NEW INWI TV Tag Team Champions, the Friendship Express!!!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well next up is the Main Event for the INWI United States Championship. Ultimate Perversion and P.O. Box for the Title. Next up! First, let's catch some comments from Ultimate Perversion! ============================================================ Tiffany: I`m in the dressing room with Ultimate Perversion, who has a title shot against P.O. Box. Ultimate, how do you see the match going? Ultimate: I'm gonna win. That's all there is to it. Tiffany: A lot of people think you should have a title by now, but you still haven't won a title yet. How does that make you feel? Ultimate: I'll restrain myself and just say it makes me feel VERY angry. It's starting to make me wonder just what I have to do to get a title around here. A lot of the earlier failures were down to my loser of a manager, and since I left him, I think I`ve improved. Regardless of the current situation, it's only a matter of time before I win a title. Tiffany: Talking of your former manager, have you had any contact with him recently? Ultimate: (Snorts) I haven`t seen or heard anything from the little buttwipe since my last press conference. Mark my words. He will get whats coming to him. I have no doubt. Tiffany: From you? Ultimate: (Smiles) Tiffany: Moving on, you been drawn against Jonathan Sunset in the 1997 Ringmaster. Any thoughts? Ultimate: Yes. I hope he has medical insurance. (Smiles again.) Tiffany: OK, well you're due in the ring, good luck against P.O. Box. Ultimate: Luck doesn`t enter into it. Before I go, I have something to say. (Ultimate stares menacingly at the camera.) VanDenBerg, I know you are out there watching this. Tonight you will see me do what you would never have let me. I am going to be crowned champion. Something which your stable of inbreds will never get to be. As for yourself. If you interfere in any way tonight, you will pay. Keep a look out over your shoulders, because when you least expect it, I'll be there. (Ultimate walks off toward the entrance to the arena.) Tiffany: Well, there we have it. Ultimate Perversion before his title match. He definitely holds a grudge. I have a feeling that the animosity between Ultimate and his former manager will come to head sometime soon. I just hope I'm not around when it does... ============================================================ MAIN EVENT Match 4 - United States Champions P.O. Box vs Ultimate Perversion Here's another wrestler that many consider an uncrowned champion. Ultimate Perversion hasn't had the luck that many people thought he would. His recent split with his manager and stablemates hasn't helped at all either. Perversion enters the ring and there's the bell and the Champion P.O. Box just looks over at him and stares. The two come to the middle and theres the tie up. Perversion with the shove off and then the knee to the midsection, DDT!! Perversion with the cover and gets a two count. P.O. Box barely kicks out of that one and this match continues! Perversion picks up Box and lifts him and lams him to the mat. Off the ropes and Perversion with a splash!! Wow, Perversion is really dominating this match. Maybe this will be his time after all. Perversion scoops PO Box and locks in a headlock and Box looks like he is in pain. Box sends Perversion into the ropes and puts Perversion to the mat with a shoulderblock! PO Box off the ropes, legdrop, NO, Perversion moves! Perversion goes to pick up Box, but Box rolls him up, 1...2...NO!! Perversion kicks out! This is an excellent match!! Perversion stomps at PO Box and scoops him up, but Box nails Perversion with a right and Perversion falls to the mat. Box looks like he is getting ready for this match and he scoops up Perversion and powerslams him to the mat and heads to the ropes. Up to the top goes Box??? Perversion working to get up and Box dives with a clothesline!! Oh my god, Perversion just caught Box and nailed a DDT in midair! That was amazing!! Go for the cover and we have a new champion!! Wait, the crowd is stirring and there's Bulldozer Tony Riley and he comes to ringside and he is screaming at Perversion. The ref is trying to stop this and Perversion is trying to reach Riley. Riley on the apron and the ref is having a hard time keeping them apart, wait look it's Tony Houston!!! Perversion gets shoved by Riley and the ref is shoving Riley away. Perversion turns around and there's Houston!!! Cold Drop!! Houston exits through the crowd and Riley heads to the back. P.O. Box is on his feet and grabs Perversion, Final Delivery! 1...2...3!! It's over! Your winner in 14:27 and still INWI United States Champion, P.O. Box!! ============================================================ Brian Newark - What a great card we had for you today! New TV Tag Champions and an interesting challenge from Brody Thunder. He has that open contract fro two weeks from today and who will accept it? This could get interesting! Also, what a situation we have brewing between Tony Houston and Ultimate Perversion! Well, we're out of time, so until this Thursday, I'm Brian Newark for the INWI saying good night!! ============================================================ =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The INWI "The revolutionary force in E-Wrestling" Visit us on the Internet: http://www.catskill.net/harmer For info, E-mail: [detail withheld] INWIŽ, May 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
