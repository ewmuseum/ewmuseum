King Apples is NOW ON THE NET!!!

24 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
King Apples (formerly known as Mr. Apples) who is the N.E.W. World Heavyweight Champion and a former TV Champ in the INWI now has his own home on the Net! The Leader of the Brotherhood is currently competing in the INWI and the N.E.W. but is willing to take offers! =) The site is still under construction as there are things still being done, but they will be up soon. Check it now and check back later for the updated pages! http://members.aol.com/KingApples/index.htm Thanks! The King
