[INWI] Inside The Ropes, 10-12

13 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
INWI Inside The Ropes Sunday, October 12, 1997 =========================================================== Brian Newark - Hello fans, hot off the heels of Invasion '97, we bring you Inside The Ropes. It's a small show this week, but tomorrow night should be a good one for the INWI. We will bring you highlights of this past weekends "N.E.W. Global Warfare" where the N.E.W. Banned all Non NEW contracted wrestlers from an INWI event, or they will SUE! That in my mind shows the victory here as we have scared the living daylights out of them SO bad that they had to ban us. That is a great day in the INWI. Let me introduce to you my co host, Scoop Ropes. Scoop - I tell you Newark, I knew we were better then the NEW and this just proves my point. Tomorrow, I hear that the INWI OWNER will release a statement regarding this situation. That is highly unusual as he usualy likes to stay away from these items, but this is a great day. Whata ya say we head to this weeks comments from REAL wrestlers in a REAL organization! Whoooooooooooooooo! Newark - That's what we'll do Scoop. From the Revolutionary Force in eWrestling, let's hear from our Wrestlers....We'll first hear from the new INWI Light Heavyweight Champion, "Simply The Best" James Pillow.... <SCENE-An expensive Italian Resteraunt in New York City. James and Angel are sitting at a table sipping on wine. James has on a three piece black armani suit, his Light Heavyweight Title is wrapped around his waist. He pats it gently. Angel has on a gold dress low cut around the chest and very short. James puts his menu down leans over and kisses Angel. He then looks over and spots the camara. . .> James "Simply The Best" Pillow:Geez can't I get a moment of privacy from you guys? Oh well I suppose you just wanna hear from your new Champ,Eh? Well I hate to say I told ya so....actually I dont mind telling you so with that said. . .I TOLD YOU SO! I beat Brown in that steel cage ane he is now merely a stain on the canvas. I am now and forever THROUGH with Rick Brown. He'll never get a shot at this title because he doesn't deserve it. As for McCarter <laughs> Well I showed how good you are and just how much BETTER I am. I went through a cage match with Brown and almost passed out cold afterwards and you still couldn't finish me off. I came into that ring half dead and you couldn't finish the job. I beat you 1-2-3 in the center of the ring and took the INWI's Lightheavyweight Title. And I plan on doing what I did to The NEW Cruiserweight Title. . .you know what that is? I RETIRED IT!! I beat evetyone that got in my way and The execs said that there was no competition for me so they dropped the cruiser division and created the North American Title which I won. . . hell if it wasn't for me there wouldn't have even been a North American Title for him to win. Now My opponent at Hot Spot knows about this well, for it was Mr. Cherry that I defeated to become NEW Cruiserweight Champion on two ocassions. Now he's challengeing me for The INWI Light Heavyweight Title next Monday on Hot Spot. Well Cherry I take it you're out of The Fellas? Well that's you choice my friend but on Hot Spot I am going to squash you like I did to Brown and McCarter. Now if you don' t mine I'm starving so get out of my face,CAPESCHE? <The waiter comes over as the camara fades. . .> Scoop - His ass will be handed to him soon enough. That match between David McCarter and Bill Bob Woijicowski will be the next champ in my honest opinion. Newark - Well, Pillow defied alot of odds to get to where he is and I am sure he won't want to give up that title soon. Let's hear from Jonathan Hammer... (The camera opens up on Jonathan Hammer inside the locker room after the INWI's Invasion "97". He is sitting on the locker room bench with his head in his hands. As he looks up we can see his now famous NEW sucks shirt (only 19.95!) Well first off I'd like to congradulate Micheal Bledsoe on his victory tonight. I will make no excusses on my loss. I gave it 100%. Bledsoe gave it 110%. I look for a rematch sometime soon. I would also like to apologize to the fans. I'm sorry. I told you all I would win and I didn't live up to my expectations. Now it seems I face Ultimate Perversion for the Television title. You just won this at the Invasion "97". Congrats on the victory. But don't forget this. I will come at you full tilt. Both guns ready. I want a title and I will not be denied. I lost my chance for the U.S. Championship. I will not be happy until I win myself and the fans a title. Ultimate Perversion I respect you as a champion and a wrestler but this Monday that respect goes out the window when we step in the ring. (Camera fades to black.) Scoop - He is one determined person that's for sure. But the fans bring him down and that's why he'll never succeed. Newark - Well, I don't know about that. I mean, Hammer has a superb record in the INWi and it just so happens that he had an off night. But that three way dance cage match was exciting. Let's hear from The All Americans... (The camera opens up on the All Americans in the locker room after Invasion "97".) Gahan-Whoo! Our second win in the INWI. This time over the Freaks. What a match. You guys gave it your all but we came out on top. Now it seems we have to keep climbing the mountain in the INWI tag scene. We don't care who's next because we aren't happy until we win the tag team titles and bring them back to the fans of the INWI. Shaffer- Now we are off this Monday but the Comforters and Solid Gold face off in a rematch from the INWI's pay per view Invasion "97". We will be watching this match closely as we respect both teams. Someday our time will come and the All Americans will reign supreme. Scoop - Those pretty boys will NEVER beat Solid Gold. NEVER. Man, all these guys think they can beat the greatest tag team in the history of the INWI? I think not. Newark - Speaking of your favorites, why don't we hear from Solid Gold right now? Actually, I think it is just their manager, Jimmy Stardom... (The Scene opens in The Office of House of Style manager, Jimmy Stardom, Jimmy is sitting at his desk with the INWI tag team titles in front of him) Stardom: Well, You all tried but in the end, The talent of Solid Gold could not be cheated... ...I knew for a long time about the INWI's conspiracy against Solid Gold. It was so apparent from the very beginning. They were cheated out of the initial tournament for these titles. They had to climb the ranks from the bottom. After six months of INWI red tape, they were finally granted the title match they deserved. The INWI finally saw the talent in Solid Gold and granted them a title shot... .....That's Bull!!!!!! The only reason Solid Gold was awarded this title shot was because the INWI didn't want two tag team championships floating around anymore. They thought about just stripping Sold Gold of the TV tag team titles, but they knew that I would sue this fed so fast, they wouldn't know what hit them. So they had to hide their disregard for the best, by signing a Unification match... .....Well, Gentlemen, Your plan backfired!!!! Solid Gold once again proved what they have been saying all along... ...They are the best team in the INWI, bar none, and it will stay that way for a long, long time!!!!! Just look at what they did for the Television tag titles. Here was a title that symbolized failure. The title was awarded to the LOSERS of the Tag Team Championship finals. The only reason the INWI granted Solid Gold a shot at these titles were because they had such a low value. Not many teams wanted to be associated with failure!!!! It took a team the caliber of Solid Gold, The first team to actually WIN those titles to give them the value that they had at the time of the unification match last week. They held those belts on four separate occasions. Some might say in the end, that the TV titles were even more sought after then the Big gold. The TV titles then became the INWI's way of trying to keep Solid Gold happy without giving them the title shots they deserved!!! Well it didn't work. Week after week, Bradley and Blake demanded that they deserve title shots and then they proved it in the ring!!!! So the INWI tried to appease them by granting them a few on some low key House Shows. Due to outside interference, Solid Gold, the team to ever grace an INWI ring, came up short. Now we come to last Sunday and the point of this whole monologue. Solid Gold received the title shot they deserved but not for the reason they should have!!!! The INWI was surprised that a team could put the TV titles in the same value category as the INWI tag team titles. Solid Gold's Dominance of the TV tag team ranks raised doubts as to just who the best tag team in the INWI was. We knew it was Solid Gold all along, and now they have proved it. Comforters, You are a very good team. But Solid Gold is a GREAT team. They proved that last Sunday and are now celebrating as the INWI tag team champions!!!! Tomorrow night on Hot Spot, They will prove it again. Now with the TV titles no longer in existence, There is no easy way to decipher who is second best, but who really remembers second best anyway. When people look back on the Tag Team history of the INWI, they will remember two words... SOLID GOLD!!!!!! We will be here for a long long time. (The scene fades back to the INWI studios) Scoop - See, now there is a fine manager. He is great and his tag team is great. God they are great. Newark - So is your opinion, but I am sure The Comforters will be after their gold again real soon. I guess we have the return of Smelly... and he is with Ed Rogers, let's hear from them... [Video from the INWI's latest pay per view extravaganza, "Invasion '97" is played in slow motion. "King" Apples has Ed Rogers unconscious in the middle of the ring as the referee is declaring the match a time limit draw. If Apples had done something, _anything_ just a second faster than he did, he would of won and became the Northeast Champion. However, he did not and so his arch rival, the man who has made his life in the INWI unbearable remains the Northeast Champion, ignoring the fact that he would of been unable to kick out of any pin attempt had there been time.] [The video ends with "King" Apples yelling at the referee. In it's place, Smelly the Sockpuppet, who was revealed to be the "man" responsible for the many attacks on Ed Rogers seconds before the match began, is shown in front of an American Flag, waving in the wind. One would guess that Ed Rogers is below the view of the camera, but that is only speculation.] Smelly: Guess what, television morons... I'm _BA-A-ACK_. That's right, _the_ Smelly the Sockpuppet, greatest managerial talent of all time, has returned to the INWI. Why have I returned? What am I going to do here? What are your plans for Ed's match with Sam Adams? Those are the questions on all your dense and yet empty minds, and I _will_ answer them in a very short amount of time, but first I want all the wrestlers in the INWI to pay close attention to what I am about to show you. Cameraman, give them an eye full of what has happened to there [mock pity] dear, dear friend Ed Rogers... [The camera follows Smelly's body/Ed's arm down to where it joins up with Ed's body. And Ed Roger's body is a mess. His face is bleeding from the mouth and nose. His eyes stare desperately at the camera. His right hand holds up a piece of typing paper which has written on it in a wild script "CALL 991". After about five seconds of looking at this pitiful site, the camera pans back up to Smelly.] Smelly: That was a warning, to any man who plans on crossing the path of the greatest manager of all time. You may win the first battle, but you will never, _ever_ win the war. Now, on to more important things. I know that you all must be wondering, quite incoherently I might add, why I have returned. Well being the nice guy I am, I will explain my motives slow enough that even your feeble minds will comprehend. You see, I am a very nice person, heck I will go as far as to say that I am the nicest man in the world, but even my good temper has a breaking point. And when Ed Rogers killed me, that line was crossed. Sure, by that time I was dead so I couldn't really do much about it, but I sure was ticked. So there I was, in hell, having demons shove pitch forks up my rear, POed at Ed Rogers, and then it hit me: I had a twix bar in my pocket! So I tipped toed over to Satin's house and I made a deal... He could have my twix, if I could have earth! And guess what he said... OKAY! So, I have returned to earth, and to the INWI with two missions in mind... 1) punish Ed Rogers and 2) take control of the world. And I _will_ do both! I don't care if Ed wins any more! I don't care if Sam Adams and Militia turn him into blood pudding! I don't care about him! [Smelly laughs as the screen fades out.] Scoop - Oh joy, the return of the "sock manager." Just what we have been waiting for.... Newark - I agree there Scoop. Let's hear from Jason Chandler, who will be in week three of the "Gauntlet" this week against Adam Kuhl... [scene opens on a large, beautifully decorated, but empty room...around the room there are championship belts hanging on the walls and on one wall we can see a picture of Stone Cold Steve Austin and Chandler standing together. Suddenly we hear the lock click and the door knob turns slowly...Chandler walks in...he puts down his bags and walks upstairs to check on his dogs...he comes back down and pushes the button to listen to his messages..] machine: BEEP! Hello Mr. Chandler this is AT&T calling and asking if you would..CLICK! Chandler: Shut up! machine: BEEP! Mr. Chandler I have been watching you for some time and I feel we need to talk. Contact Vince McMahon at..CLICK Chandler: You ain't got the money it takes Vinnie! machine: BEEP! Chandler this is Deluise...you better get back home and get ready...Monday Night you're gonna face Adam Kuhl in the third leg of the gauntlet...and be careful, this one is no pushover! Hahahaha..CLICK! Chandler: So Deluise thinks he is gonna find someone here in the INWI that can stop the Crusher from his ultimate goal of being champion? He might as well just join the long list of fools who have tried to do so....Adam Kuhl, huh? Never heard of him....Damn Deluise keeps sticking me in there with jobbers! There isn't anyone I can't beat! I made it to the finals of the ringmaster tourney and I got beat by a man that I have beaten before...I want some real competition or this whole gauntlet thing is just a joke... [Chandler slouchs down in his couch...] Chandler: Don't worry, Deluise...I'll be there to take care of Mr. Kuhl...But for the next 7, let's get me some top notch opponents?? Now get out of here and let me go to sleep! [camera fades on Chandler lighting up a cigarette and gets up to make himself a kamikaze..] Scoop - You realize he is gonna win all 10 matches and become a champ, don't you Newark. That's what the bigs at INWI headquarters don't want, but it is gonna happen! HAHAHA!!! Newark - He could do it, that's for sure. But we'll see, he has a long way to go. That is all the time we have this week Scoop... so until tomorrow night, keep it Inside The Ropes!!! =========================================================== =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The INWI™ "The revolutionary force in E-Wrestling" Visit us on the Internet: http://www.catskill.net/harmer For info, E-mail: [detail withheld] INWI®™, October 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
