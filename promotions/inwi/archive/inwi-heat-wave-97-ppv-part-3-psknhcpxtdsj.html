[INWI] Heat Wave '97 PPV - Part 3

29 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
============================================================ Heat Wave '97 Continued LIVE from Madison Square Garden in New York City... ============================================================ ============================================================ Brian Newark: Well fans, the champion appears to be as focused as we've ever seen. It should be a real war and you've got yourself the best seat in the house right here on pay-per-view...the way pay-per-view should be...the INWI way! Now let's hear from Zack Sharp. ============================================================ [The screen opens to Tiffany Love standing with Zack Sharp. Sharp wears long pants trunks that are all black except for two gold stripes running down the legs with the words "The Technician" written in black on the stripes. He also wears gold boots, shades, a leather jacket, the INWI Northeast Title around his waist, and his long black hair is tied back.] Tiffany Love: I am here with the INWI Northeast Champion and challenger for the INWI Heavyweight Championship here tonight, you know who I am talking about, I am talking about "the Technician" Zack Sharp! Well Zack, tonight is the big night and it looks like you have a pair of tights why the black and gold instead of your usual black and white? Zack Sharp: Well, tonight is my night of gold and what better than to replace the white with gold because tonight I will rise to the top of the INWI. Tiffany Love: Well, at the press conference yesterday you said some pretty harsh things about Brody Thunder. You also mentioned something about beating Brody Thunder by submission. What did you mean by that? Zack Sharp: It is about time that I have said some harsh things about Thunder. Ever since I came to the INWI he has been talking about ending my career. Well, there is no way in hell he is going to end my career tonight. If anybody's career is coming to an end tonight it is his. As far as submission goes that is what I would love to do, let's just say Thunder's arm will never be the same after tonight. Tiffany Love: Well, what do you mean by that do you have a new move or something? Zack Sharp: Just wait till the main event because it will be full of surprises for Thunder. You and everybody else will have to wait to find out. Tiffany Love: Well, I guess that is it any last comments? Zack Sharp: Thunder, tonight I will hand you the second loss you receive from me. When I am through with you, you will not be the same. I might just end your career tonight you never know, but one thing is for sure I will walk in with one title and I will exit with two! Tiffany Love: Back to you Brian! ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well both men are sounding ready for this one. This is a great Main Event for the INWI's first ever Pay-per-view. I hope all of you fans have enjoyed it, because I know everyone at the INWI has! Now, let's go up to Dan Mouth for the formal introductions of this Main Event.... ============================================================ Dan Mouth - Ladies and gentlemen, This is the MAIN EVENT!!! This match is brought to you by the INWI - The revolutionary force in E-Wrestling today! This match is scheduled for one fall with NO time limit and is for the INWI Heavyweight Championship!!!! First the Challenger! From San Jose, California, weighing in at 245 pounds, here is the current INWI Northeast Champion, "THE TECHNICIAN'' ZACK SHARP!!! [Big Crowd Pop!] And his opponent, hailing from the "Town to tough to die," Tombstone, Arizona.... weighing in at 267 pounds, here is the current INWI Heavyweight Champion.... ''LONE WOLF'' BRODY THUNDER!!!! ============================================================ Thunder:[voice over] Hey Sharp! I'm talkin' ta ya,son! Fer months now you've been nuthin' but a thorn in my side. We've both spilt blood an' teeth over which one o' us is the best. Well tonight...in front o' all o' yer fans...Brody Thunder ends the speculation. Tonight..heh,heh.. ..tonight son yer in fer a big surprise. ============================================================ Heat Wave '97 MAIN EVENT INWI Heavyweight Championship "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder vs "The Technician Zack Sharp Sharp is looking for Thunder but he hasn't come from the back. Where is Thunder? This is for his title and we just heard him. This is really odd. Now Sharp is calling out to him, but Thunder is no where to be found. Wait...there's a stir in the crowd...Thunder is coming to the ring through the crowd and Sharp does not see it! He slips into the ring and he just waffled Sharp with the belt and Sharp falls to the mat. Now Thunder is kicking away at Sharp and this crowd is in awe at this planned sneak attack. Thunder scoops up Sharp and slams him to the mat with some force behind it. Thunder off the ropes with an elbow and he finally takes off his ring vest... Thunder again scoops Sharp and hits a suplex and he has total control of this match! Thunder goes for a cover, 1...2..NO! Sharp with a shoulder up. Thunder stares at the referee and continues to work on Sharp. Thunder with a knee on the Northeast Champion and then he bounces off the ropes, there's a leg and Sharp moves! Thunder crashes to the mat and grabs his...well we're on PPV....ass! Sharp staggers to his feet and Thunder gets up. Sharp with a belly to belly suplex! Thunder is down on the mat and he rolls out of the ring and Sharp climbs to the top rope and.... OH MY GOD! He just drop kicked Thunder from the top rope and Thunder crashed into the cage and Sharp crashed to the ground. Both men are out! The referee is counting. 8....9...10! Oh no... NOT on Pay-per-View... the match can't end this way. Wait, here comes Jerry DeLuise and he is telling the announcer something.... [Dan Mouth: Commissioner DeLuise said this match MUST continue! We are on Pay-per-view and people all over and people here in the arena WHAT a winner! This MATCH WILL HAVE A WINNER!!!] [HUGE crowd POP!] You heard the mouth, we must have a winner! Sharp is slowly getting to his feet, but Thunder is still out. This match has been going on for over 15 minutes! Sharp staggers to Thunder and rolls him into the ring. Sharp looks to the crowd and screams.... the crowd reacts and this could get exciting. Sharp sends Thunder into the ropes and they collide with the ref! The ref is out! Sharp said he would make Thunder give it up, but Thunder won't ever give up. Sharp sets up thunder....what is next? Sharp in the center of the ring and he latches on... YES! He has the Sharplock on, which is a Fujinama Armbar one of the most painful submission holds in the business. Thunder screams at the pain that is shooting through his body and they are in the center of the ring, or pretty close to it! Thunder is trying to figure a way to escape and I don't know if he can. Whats all the commotion outside the ring? It's Mr. Ed Rogers and he has a chair! He just nailed Zack Sharp and again! Sharp is forced to release the hold and Rogers grabs Sharp and nails the Puppet Master! Rogers is still mad over Sharp winning that Northeast Title... Rogers leaves the ring and both men are out in the center of the ring. The ref is staggering up to his feet and he starts to count them both down, but both men are making it to their feet. Both up and Thunder clotheslines Sharp back to the mat. I don't think Sharp knows where he is after the brutal attack Ed Rogers just put on him. Thunder signals....he looks for it.... there's a cattle buster DDT! Thunder just hit one of his finishers. The cover, 1...2...NO! Sharp rolled a shoulder. Holy cow!! Thunder starts to pound on Sharp again and then sets up...there's the Thunderbolt. No one gets up from that! 1....2....3!! It's over, but it wasn't easy! Your winner in 26:57 and still INWI Heavyweight Champion, "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder!! What a classic match... ============================================================ Brian Newark - What a great Main Event! Hell, what a great first Pay-per-view for the INWI. This will prove to people that we are part of the big time. I would like to thank everyone for watching tonight here at Madison Square Garden and also at home on Pay-per-view. This was one of the greatest cards I have ever been associated with and we hope you all enjoyed it. So, for the rest of the INWI, I am Brian Newark saying see you tomorrow night on Monday Hot Spot!! ============================================================ =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The INWI "The revolutionary force in E-Wrestling" Visit us on the Internet: http://www.catskill.net/harmer For info, E-mail: [detail withheld] INWIŽ, June 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
