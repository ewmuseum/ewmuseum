INWI Monday Night Hot Spot!! ======================== Monday, June 16, 1997 Jackie's Night Spot, Oswego, NY ========================================================== Brian Newark - Hello ladies and gentlemen and after last nights rather interesting show, I am still around and we are bringing you Monday Hot Spot!! From Oswego, NY! We have 4 matches scheduled for you tonight, including "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder defending his INWI Heavyweight Championship. Also on the card we have newcomers David McCarter and Sam "The Minuteman" Adams! Also... Voice - Wait a minute Newark, it's time for me to tell the world just what the hell is going on.... Newark - What in the world? [Just then the entrance curtains open up and out walks the INWI Television Champion, Mr. Apples. Apples is in street clothes with the TV Title securely around his waist. Apples has a mic in his hands and he makes his way to the ring and gets in the ring...] Apples - Newark just shut that peep hole of yours and let me talk for a sec. I got some things on my mind. First of all this damn masked man who's been running around making an ass of himself. Yeah, he's cost people some matches and even affected some of my matches. I'm sick and tired of this piss head running around and not showing himself except in a mask. I personally want to know who this guy is. If I get my hands on this little punk, I'll kick his ass!! (BIG POP) Second of all. Brody Thunder. You are the INWI Heavyweight Champion and have been a fighting champion all along and I'll give you that. But, now yer talking about me and I didn't like the tone of your voice. I think if you know what's good for you son, you'll just kep your mouth shut when it comes to Mr. Apples or you as well may land on your ass! You mind yer business and I'll mind mine...(Big POP!) Then ya got all these other pretenders here in the INWI. Well, line up people. Yer in for a beating. I am the Television Champion and I am a PEOPLE'S CHAMPION (HUGE Pop!!) Now then, all you newbies, get yor feet wet and then I'll take care of you! I know your still there Tony Houston and your still up to your same old games (laughs). You always have been a bad ass Houston and I kinda like that, but yer in the INWI now and it ain't that easy.... Well, I said what I had to say. I took up enough of your time Newark. Now you can have your little show back and get on with the card.... Brian Newark - Well, I guess Apples just had to get some things off his shoulders. But as I was saying, a great card tonight and without further delay, let's get down to the ring!!! ============================================================ Match 1 - Grudge Match Mr. Ed Rogers vs Malvolio of the jWo Well, Rogers is going to find away to get back his Northeast Championship and he is starting with any member of the jWo he can get his hands on! Malvolio, who other than Dell Pickle, is the only jWo member to score a victory in the ring, is up for this challenge. Ed Rogers, all burnt up and no Smelly is just trying to get his belt back and maybe get Smelly back. He is really unorthadox and I don't know if he's even stable enough to be in the ring. Rogers gets in the ring and Malvolio attacks and there's the bell. Malvolio up with Rogers, suplex! Rogers rolls out of the ring and is looking for escape. Here comes Malvolio and Rogers kicks him, grabs a chair and nails him with it! Rogers with a DDT and Malvolio is hurt. Wait, the jWo head manager Oyster Boy has a chair, but Rogers sees it and kicks the chair in Oyster Boys face and he goes down. Rogers grabs Malvolio and rolls him in the ring and then does the same with Oyster Boy? Rogers up with Oyster Boy....Puppet Master. Wait, now he has Malvolio....Puppet Master!! He covers them both, 1...2....3!!! Your winner in 4:47, Mr. Ed Rogers. He looks possesed and I think he really means business.... Match 2 Sam "The Minuteman" Adams vs "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset Well, the Minuteman, who claims he will rid the INWI of all the Commies out there, is up against former Northeast Champion, "Hubba Hubba" Jonathan Sunset. The Minuteman charges the ring and attacks Sunset before the bell rings and we are underway. Minuteman on top of Sunset and he is pounding away at him with lefts and rights! Adams scoops up Sunset, sends him into the ropes and nails a clothesline! Sunset down on the mat and Adams climbs the ropes and leaps with a legdrop but Sunset moves! Sunset is up, bounces off the ropes and dropkicks Adams as he stands up. Adams stumbles out of the ring! Sunset bounces off the ropes again and OH MY GOD! Nails a suicide dive on Adams outside the ring! The crowd is pumping now as Adams is getting pummeled and the crowd loves to see that! Adams sent back in the ring andSunset climbs to the top rope....he dives, but Adams catches him and powerslams him to the mat! Both men are down but Adams is now on his feet....he sets up....there it is...The Liberty Bell! 1...2...3!! Your winner in 9:49, Sam "The Minuteman" Adams! ============================================================ [As the ring announcer enters the ring and prepares to announce the up coming match he is cut off, by a steady turn of heads of those in attendance at Jackie's Night Spot.] [Wild For Da' Night by Busta Rhymes and Rampage erupts driving the crowd in Oswego, upstate, New York to almost part like the Red Sea. Half of them are loyal and devoted fans who boo, and then a small group of younger fans who mildly cheer as THEY know who is about to make an unscheduled appearance.] ["Cold & Calculating" Tony Houston and "Bulldozer" Tony Riley make their way from behind the lockeroom curtain. Houston is wearing a black Fatal Alliance T-Shirt, a white world champion New York Yankee hat slightly ducked to cover his icey blue eyes, around his neck is a 5 inch thick gold chain. Houston is wearing black Tommy Hilfiger pants, slightly saggin'. Bulldozer is methodically making his way behind Tony Houston. Riley is wearing a white INWI T-Shirt with a black Fatal Alliance leather trench coat, a "doo rag" cover his shoulder blade long, red hair and he's wearing dark black Armani glasses.] [Houston and Riley continue to make their way towards the ring and ascend the steel stairs, as Houston climbs into the ring, Riley steps OVER the top rope and stands behind Houston, as Houston stares into the face of the ring announcer, until the announcer realizes the non verbal threat and hands over the microphone and frustrated leaves the ring.] [Wild For Da' Night fades and Houston flashes a Jack Nicholson smirk] Houston: Ok, ya'll. I've have blessed you with a Cold & Calculating presence, ya'll can shut the hell up. I've got a few things to let off my chest, and then I'll let ya'll go back to your wack assed show. Wack Assed, 'cause that little weasel DeLuise seemed fit to suspend me. First, I have one thing about this little suspension..**** it and DeLuis **** you. You have no other choice but to attempt to keep me from knocking down your paper champions. But DeLuise what will your scheme be when this meaningless suspension is up? It oughta be interesting. And speaking of paper champions, that name does not fit any champion in the INWI more so then, YOUR United State Champion..P.O. Box. Son, I need not sit here and tell ya'll what I think of you because, frankly, everybody knows, you ain't no friggin' fighting champion. You were made by the fed to hopefully represent that belt with pride, and whether they admit it or not, you have failed and failed miserably.. Where have you been P.O. Box, we ain't heard shiznit from you in quite a while. Well, you might as well consider yourself a marked man son, 'cause no damn paper champion is gonna stop me from whoopin' ass all over this federation. [Crowd begins chanting for the U.S. Champion to make an appearance.] Houston: Ya'll are wasting your breath, that biznitch ain't comin' out, while I'm in MY ring. And as for his next opponent Mike Garvey..another lil' bitch in this organization who the INWI attempts to create as a championship contender. But come on ya'll we all know that he ain't shit. He ain't the real deal. Ya'll wonder who the real deal is. Well you are lookin' at THEM!!!. [Crowd begins to Boo at the mention of Garvey...] Houston: [calmly and cooly reveals his cold smirk again] Ya'll keep trying to force some people out here, to force me from this ring. But you see this large man behind me, he's here to prevent DeLuise from using his little suspension against me. The suspension read that I can't attack anybody blah blah blah, well the 'Dozer is here to prevent that. And once this little political attempt of riding the INWI of Houston has passed, ya'll see me whoopin' ass yet again. [The two prepare to exit the ring but Houston stops.] Houston: And as for your heavyweight champion Brody Thunder, son, you believe you're all that, but son, we have been and ruled federations that you could only dream of being ACCEPTED to. And you come out here, and have the balls to say there ain't no man in this league that can hold a match to you. Well, son, this man right here, "Bulldozer" Tony Riley is more than capable and more than willing to beat you down, and TAKE that damn strap from around your waist. Son, there ain't no man that can hold a match to you, you are right there, but The Fatal Alliance is going to torch you. Now the only thing you need to worry about is if YOU have the balls to accept our challenge. [Wild For Da' Night starts again, as Riley and Houston methodically make their way back towards the lockeroom occassionally Houston stops and instigates an argument with one of the upstate fans, and then extends both hands into the face of one fan, who flinches then as the fan turns his face back around, extends both middle fingers....and continues to leave the arena area.] ============================================================ Match 3 David McCarter vs The Baker David McCarter, another hot newcomer here in the INWI trys to extend his winning streak with a win over longtime INWI grappler, The Baker. McCarter, who came to the ring to the roar of the crowd, is exciting and wants to make his stay a good one here in the INWI. McCarter enters, there's the bell and the two tie up in the ring. McCarter reaches around the waist and nails a belly tobelly suplex on Baker. McCarter up and scoops Baker and sets up for a suplex, yes! Baker crashes hard to the mat. McCarter up again, but Baker goes low and McCarter keels over in pain. Baker grabs McCarter and slams him to the mat and follows with an elbow. Baker agin off the ropes and drops a leg on McCarter and then the cover, 1...2..NO! McCarter gets his shoulder up! Baker scoops McCarter sends him into the ropes, misses a clothesline and McCarter with a dropkick that flattens Baker! McCarter sends Baker into the ropes and kicks him in the midsection...looks like a piledriver, YES!! Baker hit the mat hard. McCarter up with Baker and nails a reverse neckbreaker then signals to the crowd....yes, there's the sleeper!!! Baker is going to be out...the ref checks the arm, 1...2...3!! It's over! Your winner in 9:19, David McCarter!!! MAIN EVENT INWI HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder vs Mr. Cherry Mr. Cherry, a stablemate of TV Chmapion Mr. Apples, gets his shot at the big title today! Cherry enters to the cheers of the crowd and then a little of a heel pop as the Champion Brody Thunder enters the ring. As usual, he looks ready for business. Thunder enters the ring, takes off his ring ware and hands the belt to the ref who holds it up for all to see. This match is set and there's the bell. Cherry off the ropes and catches Thunder with a dropkick. Cherry is a high flyer and may be able to take over Thunder unless Thunder can ground Cherry. Cherry outside the ring and to the top rope and he dives, but Thunder moves and nails him with some kicks. Thunder up with Cherry and sends him ito the ropes...leaping lariat and that snapped Cherry back. Thunder follows with a leg and he picks him up just to powerslam him to the mat. Thunder again up woth Cherry and nails a running bulldog, 1...2...NO! Cherry gets his shoulder up! Thunder picks up Cherry and scoops him up for a slam, but wait, Cherry just rolled up Thunder 1...2...NO! Thunder kicks out and Cherry rolls out of the ring to gain his breath. Cherry heads back into the ring and ties up with Thunder. Cherry sends Thunder into the ropes and nails a dropkick. Cherry again up with Thunder, belly to belly suplex!! Cherry nails a standing legdrop and leaps to the top rope, he's setting up for his moonsault, OH MY GOD! Cherry just nailed his "Chery Bomb!!!" Cherry with the cover, 1....2....3!! WE HAVE A NEW CHAMP???? Cherry has the belt and he is celebrating his big win....wait, the ref is WAVING OFF THE PIN!!! Thunders foot is STILL on the ropes, but Cherry doesn't realize it. His manager, Mr. Flamboyant is trying to tell him but Cherry is trying to get the INWI Belt on....Thunder is getting up.....he attacks Cherry from behind and sets it up perfectly, there's the THUNDERBOLT!!! 1....2....3!!! Thunder keeps the belt and this crowd is stunned....we had a new champ if Cherry would of just stuck with it.... Thunder takes the belt and leaves the ring area..... ============================================================ Brian Newark - Well ladies and Gentlemen, we are out of time! We almost had a new champion tonight, but Brody Thunder held onto the belt. And what about the challenge? "Bulldozer" Tony Riley accapted Brody Thunder's challenge, NOW, will Brody Thunder accept back. That may be the MAIN EVENT next Monday Night. So, for the rest of the INWI, I am Brian Newark saying so long!!! ============================================================ =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The INWI "The revolutionary force in E-Wrestling" Visit us on the Internet: http://www.catskill.net/harmer For info, E-mail: [detail withheld] INWIŽ, June 1997 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
