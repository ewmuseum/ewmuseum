LCW Newsletter

entries dated 20 October – 16 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
December 16, 1999: As of next week, any roleplays posted on the message board are to count towards your required character roleplays. Any interviews for the actual shows are to be emailed to me. Also, any specific strategies you wish to use would be appreciated, and more than welcome! December 14, 1999: The roster cuts have been re-defined as creative changes in the roster, as the Front Office has decided to increase the roster to 45 wrestlers. However, this does not change some of the decisions about the current roster. The changes are as follows: The following people are being cut from the roster: "The Executioner" Steve Williams The following people are receiving a warning (for various reasons): "The Extreme Fanchise" Tyrone Walker "Brooklyn's Finest" Kai A. Lewis "No Gimmick Needed" Jason Jousma "The Gunblade Man" Seifer Almasy "The Hardcore Hero" Snake Eyes "The Texas ICON" Texas Lightning Those people are asked to email me as soon as possible. The following people have retired as of Lethal Lottery: The Wannabes The Outlaw Enforcers The roster is being expanded in order to increase the ranks of the tag team roster. No singles wrestlers beyond the few that have already contacted us will be accepted. Any existing handlers wishing to send in a tag team are reminded of the 3 wrestler limit. November 20, 1999: The roster cuts have been decided on, relatively speaking, and will be enforced as of the December Pay Per View. Speaking of the Lethal Lottery, the award for that tournament has been changed to a title shot at Valentine's Heartbreak in February, and a trophy. Brackets for the tournament will be up a week before the pay per view, the teams have been decided by random draw, and the matches will be decided by coin toss, up to and including the Winners Face Off for the trophy and title shot. November 16, 1999: The link to the wrestler application has been removed due to the closing of the roster in preparation for the upcoming cuts which are planned for the end of November. Any entries that have been received to date will be considered when the roster re-opens, but as it stands, there is one spot open, which has been promised to a recruit from the NeWA. November 14, 1999: A new World Cruiserweight Champion and new World Extreme Champion will be crowned at You Must Remember This as the Cruiserweight tournament comes to a close and three men are competing in a frying pan three way dance for the two titles, respectively. Also, Reaper, Beast, The Titans and The Equalizers have all announced their retirement, effective immediately following the pay per view, as are World Tag Team Champions The Renegade Outlaws, who will either lose the titles to The Outlaw Enforcers, or vacate them. More on this as it develops. November 6, 1999: Limestone City Wrestling is no longer a part of the National e-Wrestling Alliance, after having been cut from the membership. October 20, 1999: This page has been installed so that visitors and LCW members alike may find out what's going on and what's been updated! Email any news items to the LCW Media Supervisor.
