Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1.0000 Purpose: To give people the opportunity to have fun living the life of a professional wrestler in an e-federation game. 1.0010 Ownership: Limestone City Wrestling is owned solely by [name withheld], also known as Adam Douglas. 1.0020 Front Office: There are five (5) positions in the Limestone City Wrestling Front Office. These offices are President, Vice President/Commissioner, Vice President/Assistant Booker, Vice President/Relations, and Media Supervisor. In the event that one of the officers is absent, the officer above will assume responsibility of the absent office. 1.0021 President: Destination organizer, Head of Recruiting and Head Booker for the promotion. 1.0022 Vice President/Commissioner: Presidential Representative on "television," Presidential Advisor, Primary Disciplinarian, and Main Troubleshooter. 1.0023 Vice President/Assistant Booker: Assists in the booking of matches and nominating contenders to the titles. 1.0024: Vice President/Relations: Promotion and Recruiting within the e-wrestling community. 1.0025: Media Supervisor: Assists with the presentation of the "televised" events, and coordinates the availability of information for members and guests to the promotion. 1.0030 Contracts 1.0031 Full Time Contracts: A full time wrestler will commit to compete in the LCW for one (1) year, and is required to post a minimum of one (1) character or angle building roleplay every ten (10) days to receive one (1) match per roleplay. 1.0032 Part Time Contracts: A part time wrestler will commit to compete in the LCW for a six (6) months, and is required to post one (1) character or angle building roleplay per month to receive one (1) match per roleplay. 1.0040 Championships 1.0041 Championship Committee: The Championship Committee consists of the President, Commissioner and Assistant Booker. 1.0042 World Heavyweight Title: Any wrestler is eligible to compete for this title. World Heavyweight Champions will be required to defend the title every thirty (30) days. 1.0043 World Television Title: Any wrestler is eligible to compete for this title. World Television Champions will be required to defend their title at every "televised" event. The holder of this title will be instantly named the #1 Contender to the World Heavyweight Title. 1.0044 World Extreme Title: Any wrestler is eligible to compete for this title. It will be defended once (1) every two (2) weeks, and at every Pay Per View. On weekly "broadcasts" the stipulations will be drawn at random. Pay Per View match stipulations will be submitted to the Commissioner by the competitors in the match, and he will decide which ones will be enforced. 1.0045 World Cruiserweight Title: Any wrestler weighing 230 pounds or less is eligible to compete for this title. It will be defended once (1) every two (2) weeks, opposite to the World Extreme Title, and every Pay Per View. No special stipulations will be levied on the matches, except for the occasional Falls Count Anywhere match. 1.0046 World Tag Team Titles: Any pair of wrestlers is eligible to compete for these titles. World Tag Team Champions will be required to defend the titles every thirty (30) days. 1.0050 Rankings and Promotion Status: The promotion's rankings will be updated at the beginning of every month, following a meeting of the President, Commissioner and Assistant Booker. Rankings will be based on performance in the previous month, as well as existing storylines. The promotion's status will be updated following each title change. 1.0060 Scheduled Cards: The tenative schedule for a period of two weeks will be posted on the events page. The same holds true for each month's pay per view. 1.0061 Matches: Match lengths will not exceed twenty minutes, unless specific stipulations dicate a change. Each match will follow the standard rules of a match, save for special matches, as per the standard in every wrestling promotion, real or fantasy. However, our referee are also BLIND AS BATS! 1.0062 Roleplays: Matches will be determined on the basis of roleplays. In Limestone City Wrestling, quality will overrule quantity. Offensive language is to be kept at a tasteful level, remembering that some roleplays will be put into the weekly "broadcast," and are therefore subject to the same censorship rules as apply to television programs. 1.0070 Roster: Limestone City Wrestling's roster shall not exceed the maximum of forty-five (45) full and part time wrestlers. 1.0080 Monthly Chats: Limestone City Wrestling will be holding a structured chat once (1) per month. All handlers are encouraged to attend. Dates and times for these chats will be sent to the roster well in advance of the event. Any questions about these rules may be forwarded to LCW President Adam Douglas.
