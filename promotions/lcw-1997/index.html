A GeoCities roleplaying e-fed whose forty-three-wrestler roster and four surviving title ledgers ran back to a 14 February 1997 opening night, briefly hubbed under two alliances before being cut loose from one of them, and staged a "WAR GAMES!!!" main event pitting HICAL, The Wraith, and Angel of Death against a trio billed the Nation of Angels. Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Limestone City Wrestling opened on 14 February 1997, the date its own Heavyweight and Tag Team title ledgers both record their first champions crowned by tournament. By its own May 2000 roster snapshot it carried thirty-four full-time and nine part-time wrestlers, plus ten named tag teams, playing a hybrid of judged roleplay and TNM 7.0 match-writing under a five-officer Front Office and a rulebook capping the roster at forty-five. Four of its titles — the World Heavyweight, World Hardcore (billed the World Extreme Title in the rulebook), Television, and Tag Team championships — survive as complete, dated reign ledgers in the fed's own hand; only the World Cruiserweight Title, held by Angel of Death as of its own last dated mention, has no such ledger of its own.

The fed's own homepage billed it as affiliated with the National e-Wrestling Alliance (N.e.W.A.) and a member of the All World e-Wrestling Council (A.W.e.W.C.); its own newsletter records the N.e.W.A. relationship ending on 6 November 1999, "after having been cut from the membership." Its own announced 19 March 2000 pay-per-view card, St. Pat's Slaughter, closes on a "WAR GAMES!!!" six-man main event: the Triple Threat (HICAL, The Wraith, and Angel of Death) against the Nation of Angels (John "the Gator" Cole, "The Showstopper" Chris Spencer, and "The Wolverine" Micheal Lennox) — the last event the fed's own surviving record announces.

Affiliations

British Championship Wrestling (BCW, 1999)

Sister promotion of LCW (1997) (1999)
BCW’s own home page carries a graphic link captioned “Our Sister Promotion,” pointing at the address independently identified as Limestone City Wrestling. BCW’s own News page (18 June 1999) confirms it in the operator’s own words: “we have formed a relationship with long time LCW Promoter Adam Wemp... he runs the NeWA Ontario promotion in Canada,” and a 24 June 1999 post has the same operator ordering a title match “as both the presidne of the BCW and V.P of the LCW.” BCW’s own Gaslaw Gala broadcast (16 May 1999) bills LCW’s own Beast outright as “the LCW Superpower” on his BCW debut, and BCW’s own 4 April 1999 card credits LCW’s own John Cole with his established LCW finisher, the Gator Bite.
