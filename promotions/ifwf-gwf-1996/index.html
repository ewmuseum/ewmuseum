A March 1996 sub-federation of the Global Wrestling Federation, running a full Heavyweight Championship tournament across two servable rounds. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This promotion opened on 16 March 1996 as, in its own words, “a sub-federation of the absolute superb GWF,” hosted alongside the Global Wrestling Federation's own geocities site and running its first card, Weekend Warfare, as the field for its own inaugural Heavyweight Championship tournament.

The surviving rounds pit an eight-man bracket down to two, called by broadcasters Daniel McAfee and “Wildfire” Matt Adams with Stefan Stasheff on the microphone — among the bouts, the Undertaker outlasting Cronus of the stable the Gods of War. The final, 24 March 1996, locked Vader against Phoenix, a straightjacketed “day release from Arkham Asylum,” inside a fifteen-foot steel cage; Vader ended it with a top-rope Vaderbomb to become the first IFWF Heavyweight Champion.

The celebration lasted seconds. A locked-out entrant calling himself Sammy the S&M Man stormed the cage with a chair, flattened both finalists and the referee, and grabbed the microphone to explain himself: he had won GWF's own Mortal Combat match, was promised a title shot, and instead found the promotion broken up and its wrestlers scattered into “tiny little federations” — dumped into this one, he said, without the tournament he felt he'd earned a place in.

A closing note promised an IFWF International Championship tournament the following weekend; no further document survives to show it was run.
