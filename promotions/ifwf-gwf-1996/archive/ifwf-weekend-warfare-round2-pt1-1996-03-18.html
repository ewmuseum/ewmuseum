Hello again. This is the first part of the second round of the IFWF
Heavyweight Champion Tournament. The first part has also been posted, but unfortunately was labelled as
<IFW> Weekend Warfare - plain text format - LONG Sorry about that. In case you are wondering, yes I did post the first half as a uuencoded binary, yes I now know better, and yes, I won't do it again. Please don't flame me, I'm begging ya!
As mentioned before, the IFWF is a sub-federation of the absolute superb GWF. The GWF website can be found at
http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/3311/ If you have any thoughts, constructive criticisms, reviews or suggestions on this card, please feel free to e-mail me at:
[detail withheld] I'm always on the lookout for ways to improve me fed, and your thoughts are welcome. Thank you, and enjoy the card. There is more to come... PS: This message looks best in a word processor with a fixed font. But it's still readable in just about anything.

Adams (MA) clutch microphones. Both men are smartly dressed with blazers with the IFWF logo. Matt Adams is wearing sunglasses and an
IFWF baseball cap.
DM: Welcome To WEEKEND WARFARE, live from the IFWF arena in sunny Los
Angeles! Hi, I'm Daniel McAfee and this is my broadcast colleague,
'Wildfire' Matt Adams, with the second Weekend Warfare this weekend!
This is a special programme to handle the second, third and final round of the IFWF Heavyweight Championship Tournament!
MA: Absolutely! I'm really looking foreward to this, McAfee! I've waited too long already, so let's go down to Ringside for that jerk,
Stefan Stasheff!
Stefan Stasheff (SS): Wwwrrreessstttlliinnngg Fffaaaannnsss, Let's Get
Ready To RUMBLE! This match is the first in the second round of the
IFWF Heavyweight Championship, and is scheduled for one fall, with NO timelimit, NO countout, NO disqualification and NO Managers! Already in the ring, weighing in at 450lbs and representing the Gods Of War, here is CRONUS!
<Crowds boos. They didn't like what Cronus did to the Deacon in the first round. Cronus just smiles>

DM: You're right, Matt. But remember, the Undertaker has experience with Giants, and Cronus certainly qualifies as one of those!
MA: And the lights come back on at a gesture from the Undertaker! How does he do that?! DM: There's the bell! Undertaker and Cronus in the centre of the ring! They're trying to stare each other down, Matt! Undertaker is saying something, but I can't make it out from here! Wow - he's drawing a finger across his neck! We all know what that means!
MA: Yeah - doesn't that mean it's a song? Or is it a play? Anyway - who cares as the two lock up! Undertaker with a thrust to the neck!
Cronus reels back, and Undertaker with another thrust! And another! And another! Cronus is reeling, but he's still on his feet! DM: The Undertaker with a DDT! Cronus is down! Cronus is down! MA: Well, that may have left Cronus with a headache, but he's back up! Now the Undertaker whips Cronus into the ropes! DM: No! The Undertaker lowers his head too soon! Matt!

Tiger Driver! Cronus drops over the Undertaker and Referee Bob North starts the count ... 1... 2... Kickout with authority by the
Undertaker! He's sitting up! Cronus cannot believe what he sees! Cronus is backing away from the Undertaker, but the Undertaker slowly stalks toward him, McAfee!
DM: Undertaker grabs hold of Cronus, and whips him to the turnbuckle! No, Cronus has reversed it! Undertaker SLAMS into the turnbuckle! You can feel the impact up here! That has to have taken it's toll on the
Undertaker's back! Undertaker is face down on the mat!
MA: Incredible! The Undertaker is not moving! He's hurt bad, McAfee! This could be it for the Undertaker! DM: And as Cronus slowly moves toward the Undertaker, we have to take a break! I cannot believe the bad timing...

monster! MA: Unbelievable! DM: Cronus is back up to his feet! What incredible stamina this monster of a man has! MA: Undertaker whips Cronus into the ropes! No - Cronus reverses! The

Undertaker is out cold!
DM: Cronus has just ignominiously thrown the Undertaker back into the ring, and here's the three count! I can't believe it! What an evil thing to do!
MA: Cronus, I love you! I want to bear your children! Cronus has eliminated The Undertaker from the tournament!
SS: Here is your winner, representing The Gods Of War, Cronus! <Crowd booes wildly. They don't like what they have just seen!> DM: And as our ringside doctor checks out the Undertaker and Referee
Bob North, let's take a commercial break....
