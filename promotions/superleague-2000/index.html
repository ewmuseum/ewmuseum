Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The SuperLeague survives in one announcement to rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy, dated 29 August 2000, describing its own return rather than its founding: no document says when it first began. It described itself as a circuit of real-wrestler-portrayal federations, each run by its own booker as an independent region, that shared results and drew on one common talent pool — wrestlers billed under their real-world names from the WWF, WCW, ECW, XPW and elsewhere.

Its own comparison was to the NWA: a World Championship and World Tag Team Championships traveled from one region to the next roughly monthly, while each region kept its own local titles alongside them — a Southern region's own Southern Heritage Title and Texas Heavyweight Title, offered as the pattern.

No further document survives to name a fellow region, a champion, or a result. Whether the SuperLeague's return actually took place, and how long the circuit ran once it did, isn't in the surviving record.
