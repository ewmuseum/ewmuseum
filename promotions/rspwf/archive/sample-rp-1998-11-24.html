Sample RP

24 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera slowly...opens up on a darkened locker room area, where the UWF
Hardcore/Extreme Champion, Dave "too Extreme" Bean, and the UWF Lightweight
Champion Sean Fischer slumly stand, next to their locker, with a wooden bench in front of them, and some other lockeroom essentials in the background.
The camera then zooms in on Dave Bean, who glares straight into the camera] --{Dave Bean}-- "For one year...one long, tough, and painful year, I have awaited this very moment. The moment that will not only change my life and career, but change the history of wrestling...forever. You see, a group of guys, that like to be known around the world as Damage Incorporated....have been walking around, talking off more then what they may be able to handle, and now, all that talk will come down, to this one match, a match that has been labeled as the most extreme, the most hardcore match of it's time. A match, where not only will there be barbed wire, a match where not only will there be a steel cage...but a match where there will be nearly ten-thousand volts, of electricity going through this steel cage...a little "too Extreme"?
Nah...not for me, and not for my man right here, the UWF Lightweight Champion, Sean Fischer...." [Dave Bean moves out of camera veiw, as the camera backs out, then zooms in on Sean Fischer, who stands on another side of the lockeroom, with his title gleaming to the screen]
--{Sean Fischer}-- "No cute introductions today....because you see, the UWF Lightweight Champion, the best damn LightWeight Champ for that, is not in anything near of a good mood...and you all should know why.....last week, Damage Incorporated decided to jump me from behind, as they, and everyone knew they couldn't handle this, not in their current or future lifetimes.
But anyway...the cost me two grand, cause of the stitches I got...once again, a thank you to them....but the "your welcome" comes this Sunday, when you step into the ring with the future....GWF Tag Team Champions....of the entire, freaking world. Prepare to be Neutralized.."
--{Dave Bean}-- "...Cleveland Style...." [Camera fades]
