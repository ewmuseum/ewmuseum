[RP] Heidi Leick-Where are your heros 4/? Critique/Enjoy

4 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ ** Rock Superstar by Cypress Hill starts to play in the background ** ] [ The scene opens up into the darkend fall nighttime skies of NYC... the Empire
State building is in the background, and you can tell, since the Word Trade
Centers Twin Towers and Statue Of Liberty is on your left, that you're on the
Brooklyn Side of the City...
The camera pans down and back... some closer buildings appear, and they look like projects.. the ghetto in Red Hook maybe... and before you,... the woman you know as Heidi Leick appears in your view.. she's wearing a leather MC jacket, a pair of torn & faded skintight stonewashed jeans, combat boots, and a
T Shirt that fittingly reads...
Guns N' Roses "Welcome to the Jungle" on... Heidi smiles evilly as the camera zooms into a closeup of her scarred, yet beautiful face... ]
Heidi Leick: Oh my,... it seems that GDWA has seen fit to send me thier ~trash~ for disposal once again.. or.. should I say.. thier *fake cough* Heroes...
One Radhi Ananda... [ Heidi laughs evilly, looking up at the sky, shaking her head... ] When will they ever learn? [ She looks back down after talking to her "higher power" ] It Seems Never... [ She tilts her head to the left, coyly ]... Oh yes... and lest I forget to mention... UnLEICK the past.. I did not ~start~ it this time.... No Radhi.. I had BIGGER.. better things than you on my mind... LEICK the GDWA World Title... But.. and there always seems to be a but here.. ~You~, like some spinoff of a bad TV Series that you never want to see again... YOU decide to come down to ~MY~ match and stick your old, ugly, stinky, non soap using, no shower nasty ass , no deodorant , non underarm or leg shaving , barefoot banana eating , tree climbing , face painting , bone in your nose , coconut picking, no green card having , welfare check cashing, 7-11 owning, mighta--bin, never-was nose in MY BUISNESS when I'm in the middle of kicking the HELL out of Marissa Monet and Bloody Mary's asses... with the Help of
Andrea Chandler may I add...
Oh Andi.. great job at picking up the slack there, I'm glad to see our little
~motivational~ ~talk~ backstage paid off...
Anyways... getting pack to CASE in POINT.. Radhi Ananda... [ Heidi smiles, almost demonically as the camera pans back and she walks down the darkened allyways.. the camera leading her on ]
Radhi... Maybe my statements about me dismantling and killing the GDWA "Heros" and
"Legends" offended you...
Sorry old gal.. but I've got news for ya...It's the truth... Ask Zaranna or Double O... [ she pauses, looking mockingly sympathetic] Oh wait.. I forgot.. you un-edu-ma-cated just can't grasp the details of the telephone [ Heidi keeps walking.. camera focusing on her as she paces towards it... ] Maybe you think you have something to prove... And you "think" that I'm the best here to prove it against... Hey.. *laughs and smiles smugly* You're Right... [ Heidi stops in the alley and leans against the wall ] Maybe you think that everyone's happy to see you back here cause your a SELF PROCLAIMED GDWA LEGEND... How cute... No Radhi... no one cares about you.. just like no one would care about ~Daisy
Butterfly~ if she still were here... but the difference between you and her is...
With her... I'd feel there was a legitmate challenge to me at least. [ Heidi smiles, nods, shrugs and continues walking until she comes to a courtyard...] You see.. Radhi... you may ~think~ you've got the advantage here... being the
"Black Goddess" from the ~wild~ jungles and all *yawn*...
And that's Ok.. we can all ~DREAM~.. can't we? But Radhi.. Be honest with yourself... I mean, hell... I've seen you on tape... and I have to tell you... I couldn't see what all the hoopla about you returning was about...
See... even though you were nothing special then, from what I see... You were soo~oo much better then, than you are today... To Quote Bon Jerkoff.. you're "Living on a prayer" [ Heidi shakes her head in disbelief ] I've seen you in the locker room.. and quite frankly.. you're NOTHING to look at.. I've seen you work out.. and you're quite pathetic... In fact, I feel bad when an old ~"legend"~ like yourself , beaten, bruised, and looking for one last big payday,... tries to recapture her "Glory Days"...
[ Heidi smiles, stunningly ... knowing in definite fact what reference she's just refferred to ]
Radhi. Ananda.. Nothing special, nothing exciting.. in fact,... compared to anyone I've already beaten, you're probably the least of the "hardcore" women
I'll ever have to worry about old timer...
A Terry Funk you're ~definitly~ not. [ The camera pulls back a bit ] And you're probably the least of the "technical" girls I'll ever have to contemplate... A Chris Benoit, you'll never be near... [ The Camera pans back in ] You want to know something Randhi? You may come from the Jungle... but I come from the CONCRETE Jungle.
And take it from me.. someone who's been in both places... It's just a ~li~i~i~ttle~ tougher here... [ Heidi stares at the camera psychotically as she shows her thumb and forefinger barely an inch apart ]
Radhi... I've been to your Jungle... and beat it.. Got my JOTC certs from the USMC to prove it... [ She nods, in a definite manner ] Radhi.. I've got Four words for ya... WELCOME TO MY JUNGLE... [ Heidi points, and the camera sirls towards some gang members beating down a few street thugs and maybe a security guard, before coming back to Heidi ]
You see Radhi, .. My JUNGLE.. extends from the streets and dead ends, to the airports and highways, to the trees and forests ... from the deserts and glaciers .. to the wresting ring... from where you say you live , to where I know I do ....
And While you had to ~LIVE~ dealing with ~Tigers and Lions and Bears.. Oh My..~
[She yells.. " Hey MORONS.... I'm TRYING TO CUT A PROMO HERE"
The Gang Bangers stop, turn and look at Heidi.. one says.. " OH SHIT.. DAT CRAZY BIOTCH IS BACK..... RUN!!...... She makes a funny face as she laughs amd the gang bangers take off...] The Lesser animals in my jungle.... [ smile ] They ~love me.. they really do.. I've had to ~SURVIVE~ dealing with the fiends, criminals, rapists, gang bangers, assassins and murderers of the streets of NYC...
Radhi... you've lived in the jungle.. yes .. it's true. But do you truly know what it means to be a SURVIVOR? [ The camera zooms into Heidi's face.. looking definite....determined ] I'm a "Ghetto Superstar" Radhi.. I've reached for the stars, fell to earth, did it again and again till I was battered, bruied and bloody.... and still was never beaten into submission.. and I never will be.
I came from a poor family.. made the military my career, until they determined
I was too much for them to handle...
I've made 5 star motion pictures.. and beat the crap out of every director who tried to lay me in Hollywood
I've made my wrestling career a success... and I'm not about to let a pathetic loser who's trying to hang on to their past stand in my way.. No Radhi..
To Quote the song.. So you wanna be a Rock Superstar, and live large, a big house , fine cars and rate charge, comin up in the world don't trust nobody gotta look over your shoulder constantly...
Thats my life..., looking over my shoulder, constantly.. because Heidi Leick , always RISES to the TOP, and whether it's FRIENDS.. or FOES...
Everyone... Whether I LEICK it or NOT.. Everyone's an assassin.. and.. Everyone... Whether I LEICK it or NOT.. Everyone wants what HEIDI's GOT. [ Heidi walks out further, towards the origin of some noise in the background ] See.. it doesnt say , you wanna live in a grass hut, with a cloth skirt and no shoes.. singin da blues, going to the stream to take a bath cause my parents can't afford a map to the nearest town...
[ She laughs.. and you hear the cameraman start to laugh but stifle himself ] No Radhi... [ Heidi looks down] I may have not had to worry about the poison dart having to fly at my head... but I had to worry about the bullet aimed at my brain...
And I may have not had to worry about the same "animals" that you do, [ She looks back up, more determnined than ever]
But sure enough, there ~were~, and ~are~ as many , if not ~more~, "predators" and "assassins" in my jungle as there were in yours... And they all hunt ... they all hunt in packs with much, much more deadlier weapons... [ She looks at the camera and points towards the downed security men, thugz and citizens ] Yes... everyone here in this City ~dreams~ of achieving of what I did... The older people that have survived this mess tell me I changed the way people grow up here...
And , What they aspire to be... some call it a Changing of the guard,... From the old to the new Others say it's Transfusing the blood.. From the bad to the good [ she shakes her head in disbelief ] I laugh at them.. and say.. " How do I, Heidi Leick, influence anyone to do anything but beat the hell out of everyone and everything they see?"
The answers they give me make no sense.... maybe, one day, someone out there can help me with that.. but back, back to the thought at hand.... back to
Radhi.. back to the "Black Goddess"
[ She waits a moment to collect her thoughts ] Radhi, you may think you're safe, with me and you doing battle in a common place, but this battlefield is all mine.. from the recesses of the sub basements, to the concession stands, to the ring, to the ceilings where the banners and scoreboards hang...
Ask Anyone who'll give you the time of day what kind of Competitor Heidi Leick is... and they'll tell you.. I'm vicious.. I never give up.. and they'll tell ya you have to put forth maximum effort to get me to exert the blood, sweat, and tears it'll take for you to get close to beating even a part of me that's been lost over the years of trying to make it to the top.
Radhi, I hear your thoughts.. you're telling yourself you have to fulfill the big dreams you have to come back to GDWA and prove your a legend here,...
And who better to try your hand against, so early in your comeback, so you know whether to hang up the boots or not.. although in your case, it's probably athletes foot and callouses,.... instead of boots....
Than me... The Queen of Extreme, Hardcore Bytch, Suicidal Icon and Innovator of Violence.. The SAVIOR of the SYNDICATE, and the LEGEND or HERO KILLER of the GDWA... Heidi Leick.. [ She walks up to a securty guard, who's knocked out and bleeding profusly.. she kneels downand cradles his head in her arms, then looks in the camera, deleriously delighted in demenita and pschosis ,.. and licks his bloody forehead and talkes his blood and wipes it across her face like camo paint...]
Yes Radhi.. you may say you ~LIVED~ in ~your~ JUNGLE... But I ~SURVIVED~ in ~my~ JUNGLE.... You go and and keep playing your gimmick... Refugee... And I'll... And I'll live mine.. For You see... you've prove it true.. Just LEICK it says on the new T Shirts out there.. "99 out of 100 people agree,.. Everyone wants to be JUST LEICK ME..." [ The camera fades out with Heidi Leick's face, hauntingly shadowed and covered in the security guards blood, and a police siren wailing in the background ]
