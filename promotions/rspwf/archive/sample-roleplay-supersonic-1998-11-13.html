[Sample Roleplay] Supersonic

13 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Take a look, critique, comment, flame, give me fed offers, whatever. This RP I used for the N.E.W.
--------- [The scene cuts to the control center of the building which "On The Spot" is taped. Several monitors are along the wall, with all sorts of controls right in front. The room has about three people in it, with two carefully monitoring the screens, making sure all is going well. The third man sits on a wheeled chair in the center of the ring, facing the camera.]
[This man is none other than Supersonic, who had revealed himself to the world only minutes ago.]
[APPEARANCE-His facial expression is relaxed, almost satisfied. He is wearing a sleeveless, black Nike t-shirt and a pair of jeans. A small gym bag sits on the table next to him. A bottle of water is also on the table.]
Supersonic: It appears that everyone in the N.E.W. wants to get a good glimpse at their future. At their future franchise. At their next legend. At their future champion. All those things. From the day I signed with the N.E.W., I knew that I would be doing big things, in fact, the pres said that he would be expecting them from me. And I am _not_ on to dissapoint! What you have in
Supersonic is 6 foot 2, 234 pounds of pure wrestling talent. Enough talent to carry this fed one-armed into the next milennium, and with some of the guys here in N.E.W., that is _NOT_ and easy thing to do.
Yes, I have seen what this fed has to offer. I am impressed. Very impressed. A few friends in here. Jack Vars namely. Some acquaintences, like James Pillow, who have have seen before, and have even fought, but that's about it. Plus, there are tons of future rivals, tons of wrestlers who's asses will belong to
_me_!
And what better place to start than with Nick Demola and Shawn Walsh? [Supersonic takes a drink from his water bottle.] First off, let me comment a little bit on Nick Demola. I've noticed he's been
_very_ cocky going into this match. Well, Nick, cockiness gets you _nowhere_ unless you have the _talent_ to back it up. Do you have the talent _to_ back it up? That will be determined. I have stepped in the ring with you before, well, it really wasn't a ring, it was a parking lot, but the same thing. Unfortunatly
I lost in that match, but keep in mind you needed a parking lot, a car, and so many other things to get me down. And that was in Tag Team Hell. This is the
N.E.W. Thins have changed there, Nicky.
But you've been talking so tough about Shawn Walsh, but you know what? Allow me to take a quote from Raven. What about me, Nick? What about me? I am far more of an opponent than Shawn Walsh will ever be. With a name like "Da Greatest
Ever" he obviously is _not that_ good. But the only person you've concentrated on at _all_ is him. You didn't even scratch the surface of your "mystery person". And you know what Nick? You will pay for it in the long run. And it will hurt you. Very badly.
You're only looking at half your problem, half your challenge. But in my case, Shawn Walsh is maybe about a tenth of your challenge. The other nine tenths you will have to deal with none other than me, Nick. You need to focus on the whole problem. You can't be so naive. It will get you nowhere.
Don't expect to have much luck at Born Again if you continue focusing on only a small part of the match, one that will not be hard to deal with.
Sure, you can hit the film room. We all do. But Nick, you wasted so much time worrying about Walsh that you missed valuable time to learn about me. The consequences can't be good. Not good at all. You don't want to fall to the basement of the federation, do you? Well, if you're not careful, you will. And with me around, the chances are even greater of that happening.
[Supersonic takes another drink of his water.] Now Shawn, let me ask you a question. What _truly_ makes you "Da Greatest
Ever"? The fact that you can't win a match at all? The fact that you sweep the floors of the basement of the wrestling rankings to keep a decent job? You may be Da Greatest Ever, but da greatest _what_? Loser? Yeah, that sounds right. In fact, I bet you that it's true. But in only a few weeks I will find out for sure.
Yeah, I have seen you around the wrestling world, yet I have never actually seen you fight. You could be the best thing to hit wrestling for all I know.
Or, you can be the exact opposite. But you know what? I really don't care. Because at Born Again, you will go down to the one, the only, Supersonic. But until then, I hope you're in the gym, training your ass off. I hope that you are in the film room from dusk until dawn, staring at the screen until your eyes buldge right out of your pathetic skull. But it probably won't be good enough for you. Because you and Demola really do not stand a chance this match.
This match is just step one. Step one in my N.E.W. dominance. It starts with _you_ Walsh and Demola. I hope you feel special. Because it won't last. [Supersonic, does, well, guess...He drinks some more water.] Hey Nick, remember Tag Team Hell? Yeah, I lost. Not much I can do about that one match. But there is something I can do about _you_. That is beat you.
Humiliate you. Avenge the loss. Not many people can claim that they took two straight matches from me. And I assure you that you will not be added to the list. N.E.W. might be a wee bit more familiar to you, but that doesn't mean
_jack_ to me, because the importance of it is, well, none.
Not one damn bit. You call yourself the Bulldog. Well, a Bulldog may not be the nicest of all dogs, but it is not one of the biggest. Well, here is a little comparison to you. You are the Bulldog. I am the wolf. I bloodthirsty, powerful, deadly wolf.
If a bulldog wound up in the middle of the woods with a wolf, the wolf would tear it to shreds. And being like a wolf, I will tear you to shreds. Sure bulldogs may have big teeth, but a wolf is another story.
Why am I talking about wolves, anyway? [Supersonic shrugs.] But Nick, your time is running out. Soon, a new person will reign supreme in the N.E.W. Can you guess who? I'll give you a hint. It is _NOT_ you. Still haven't figured it out? Well, if you can't after this hint, you need help.
You're looking right at him now. Yes, me. I am the future of this organization right now. Sure, I may be just another rookie to most of you, but those who have seen what I can do know that I am speaking fact.
Because I don't lie. People like you and Shawn Walsh, well, they do. They go around claiming they are the best thing since sliced bread, but they can't back up a single word they say. And people like you, Nick Demola make me sick. And people like you need one thing. One, simple thing. One word.
Therapy. And I supply it. Free of charge. All you have to do is step into a wrestling ring. And I do the rest. Nick, you and Shawn Walsh are signed up for an appointment at Born Again. I'll see you there.
See you in the ring, punks. [Scene fades.] Mike Handler of Supersonic Handler of no-name crap such as Billie Joe, Big Earl, Eraser, Peabody A. Frink,
Rulebreaker, and Speed Demon
Marking out for space on the Mad Goat Show Vice president/head recruiter for NWWA "Will work for RSPWF 200 votes"
