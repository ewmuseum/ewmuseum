RSPWF Card Archive

Chris Douglas/Rocko Johnson flash war

21 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-Chris Douglas vs. Rocko Johnson flash war for match at Nucleaur Fallout 2-- ([name withheld])vs. ([name withheld]) [Chris] ( Chris Douglas and Titanicus are in the NEFW TV studios. ) Chris Douglas: Some might say I'm nuts, trying to get back the heat I once had. An impossibility some might say, but do I look like I care? Hell no.
The fact of the matter is, that on my best day I'm more than capable of getting to the top of any federation, and on my worst day I can beat most of the wannabe so-called superstars that seem to think they've made it in this god-forsaken business. Well, it looks like the Executive committee realise that me, even at this somewhat unstable mentality, is more than capable of working a decent pay-per-view match, since they've changed Douglas-Johnson to pay-per-view television. Rocko, either way, on a Heart Attack, a house show, a pay-per-view, wherever, whenever, I can beat you. I know it, the fans know it, and what makes it so amusing, is that you know it too. You wanted me in the ring when you thought I was out of this business. When you thought a
Chris Douglas flash was as rare as a Creed world title. But the bottom line is that I'm giving it my damndest. When I play, I play for keeps. Rocko, you might be talented enough to get close to the top, but you'll never be good enough to reach the top. Perhaps that's something I haven't been able to do... in this fed anyway... but I have more potential in my pinky finger than you have in your entire body. Plus I have the talent to match. Anyway, there's still a few days until the pay-per-view, let's see what kind of flashes you can churn out, and we'll see if I eat my words.
Anyway, with Friday Night Fusion almost upon us, I'm preparing for a match against somewhat of a gimmick wrestler, or rather a walking gimmick. The
Fisherman? That's just a plain embarassment to my gradually re-increasing status here in the NEFW. Go back to being a fisherman, cause I know for one the chances of you being able to pull off something decent against me is about zero. And here now is someone who sure as hell isn't going to miss a match in
Germany, Titanicus. Titanicus: Fisherman, you can bet your life Chris vill have me in his corner in my homeland on Friday. But enough about your destruction, now ve vill talk about an upcoming tag team match. Ve are still in Europe, the real home of wrestling, vhere myself, all eight foot of me, and Chris Douglas vill be taking on the Jackals in a tag team title match. Some might say our tag team wrestling has gone a little stale, but the only thing that'll be going stale is the Jackals' rotting bodies as I chokeslam both of them through the canvas.
Jackals, prepare to vave goodbye to those titles. They're ours. And Magma, it looks like ve'll be taking each other on at the pay-per-view. Could be an interesting match, or it could be a typical Titanicus match... you know...
vhere you go eight foot up in the air and vind up making a huge dent in the floor. Only against you, it's going to be Extreme. Sounds like fun for me, how about you?
( Camera fades. ) [Rocko responds] (Camera slowly zooms into to reveal Rocko Johnson holding his hold Television Title in his hands. He paces around the room while his manager Tracy Moore sits down behind him reading a magazine. A reporter enters the room but is told to sit in the corner and just listen not speak. Tracy looks up at Rocko and sets down her magazine, she gets up and speaks to Rocko)
Tracy: Rocko, what's wrong? Rocko: Nothing, I'm fine. Tracy: Rocko, I know you better than that, what's the matter? Rocko: I said nothing, just let me be. Tracy: It's the title isn't it, your still thinking about your loss in the Unification Match aren't you? Rocko: I don't know what your talking about... Tracy: Rocko, listen to me, there will be other shots, you will hold more gold here in the NEFW, don't worry.
Rocko: I know, it's just hard to forget your first taste of gold. You work so hard and it took me so long, but I just let it slip through my fingers, it won't happen again.
Tracy: You've got to forget the past and look to the future, you've got some big matches coming up if your not focused you could get seriously hurt, your not facing the little guys any more, you've got the top guns, the big men, the main eventers.
Rocko: I know, I know, and it all starts with Badboy. He may not be a big dog here but he's no rookie in the ring or in the NEFW. I've just got to stay focused and utilize each and every oppurtunity I earn. If he makes one mistake I've got to encounter.
Tracy: And then two nights later you have Backdraft, if you don't maintain yourself he'll set you on fire, literaly.
Rocko: There's a match where my strength will really be felt. But it doesn't stop there, at Nuclear Fallout 2 I face my biggest task that I've faced here in the NEFW, stronger than Old Brown Heineken, sneakier than Brandon Charm, and quicker than Rude Dude, I've wrestled some of the greats in my time here, but honestly, none of them compare to one man, yes, Chris Douglas...
Tracy: I'm all ready working on your gameplan, I've all ready started working out things that could and are likely to happen in that match, Chris Douglas said you only wanted the match because he was slipping for a week, he thinks that you wanted him when he's weak.
Rocko: Chris, I thought you knew I was better than that, I want you at your best, at your strongest, quickest, most technical and sneakier than ever. I want the best Chris Douglas has to offer because after I pin your shoulders to the ground for the 1-2-3 I want to say I beat him at his best!!!
Tracy: And you will Rocko, you will. Now, we've got to keep you on schedule, hit the weights, I don't care if your the strongest man in the NEFW, you've got to get even stronger if you want to beat him.
(camera slowly fades to black) ********************************** [Chris comes back with more] ( Chris Douglas is seen once again in the NEFW TV studios. ) Chris Douglas: Rocko, what the hell are we going to do with you? You wanted me at my strongest, my best, when I was on a roll, when I was unstoppable, when I stopped the show on different TV shows, when my name was known in every household around. Well, if you wanted that, pal, you honestly should have asked for the match a month or two ago. That's when I was at my best, being ranked higher in the RSPWF awards than anyone else around here at that time.
Then a couple of flashes I put out in the last few weeks. You saw that, and you capitalised on it. Kudos. Well done. Congrats and all that crap. You did what I would have done... you took advantage of the situation. That's highly commendable in my opinion, but unfortunately for you, Chris Douglas decided to get things back into gear and with all cliches aside, decided to make a comeback. I'm not saying I'm ever going to be able to reach the status
I reached when I was holding the Anthracite and Pay-Per-View titles for longer and more times than anyone else. I'm not saying I'll be as good as when I held Megacorp's APW belt for so long nobody remembers anyone else having held it. But that's the past. I wouldn't be flashing right now if I didn't think there was at least a chance of me making it as big, or possibly bigger than back then. That was then and this is now, Rocko. You've got to understand something, Rocko, anyone you've beat around here, I've probably beaten them too. And probably multiple times too. Devastation, I've beat the lot of them. Creed, he's been beat a couple of times by me. Galliano, ditto.
Smasher, OBH, Melancholy, Charm, Dixon and even Myers a couple of times too... the list goes on and on. I've beat them all up, and I've kicked them all around. Possible exception is Rude Dude, but I see that changing in the not too distant future. I am without a doubt the past of this business, and although I might not currently be the present, I'm damn sure as hell going to be the future, mask or not. The bottom line, Rocko, is that as good as you are, and with all your ability, you still can't compare to me at my best, and
I'm going to show it to a world-wide paying audience. Everyone knows when it comes to the crunch, I can get the job done, whatever you want the job to be referred as, but I'm going to quote someone that stole my Misfits idea I was using ages ago, and repackaged it as DX in a different fed... Rocko... when
I'm at my best, I lay down for nobody. I'm the bad guy remember Rocko, I'm supposed to be sneaky, I'm supposed to be confident, I'm supposed to kick up a fuss if things don't go my way and I'm forced to fall. I might not be at my best, but I'm making no excuses. Should you beat me, I'm not going to come out here and whine about it, saying you got me at my weakest. I'm not at my weakest Rocko, but I'm sure not at my strongest either. I've been given my chance to show what I can do... a week or so advance notice of this match, so if I fail, it's my own fault. That's fine. I fell one hell of a lot of times on my way to the top. I can only do a certain amount to help you look good in the ring Rocko, to work a decent match. You have to add fuel to the fire too, and sure, you're doing that. But nobody adds more fuel than Chris Douglas when Chris Douglas has his concentration level at one hundred percent. And there's no other organisations I need to worry about anymore Rocko, I can give it my all here. I don't need to defend other people's belts elsewhere, I just play here now. I lost my title shot here in the NEFW. The title shot that I worked for several months to get. I lost it cause I didn't have my full concentration on NEFW situations. I've never been given a chance at a singles title here, and perhaps due to my actions in the last few weeks I might never.
But I know I'm not laying down and calling it a day. I know I'm going to work my way back up there, even if it takes several more months to do it. And how am I going to do that? I'm going to beat the people around here that are considered top
contenders. That's where you come in, Rocko. You've the stepping stone I need to get back up there. Quite simply, I can live with you being here. My ego is no longer so big that I feel the need to be the only talented person in a federation. I'm learning to live with the fact that I'm somewhere where I might not get as much control as I've had in past federations. I've called the shots in the past Rocko, and you and your buddy Cyclone know that. I've always had the pull in the past, the ability to dictate who lays down for me and who I help to the top. Right here that's changed, but I intend on swaying the balance back into my favour with pure talent alone. Rocko, a word or two of advice... forget about your past losses and throwing away the Unification match and so on. And you don't need a side-kick or valet to tell you that
Rocko. It's common knowledge. Yes Rocko, you're capable of beating me, but it just ain't going to happen on pay-per-view. Perhaps on a minor show somewhere in outer-guatamala or somewhere, but not when the whole world is watching. Cause when the whole world is watching, that's when I'm at my best, and that's something you're going to have to realise and live with. So churn out another flash, Rocko, hit the gym, hang out with your woman, do whatever you have to do to make this a match to remember, like all my pay-per-view matches in the past. Ladies and Gentlemen... Chris Douglas is back, and that, as Mr. Ass would have said, is the "Stone Cold" truth.
( Camera fades. ) [Rocko keep’s on ticking] (A voice is heard speaking but blocking the site of the man is a locked door. The reporters edge closer to the door to try and listen in and capture the man behind the voice. The voice is very familiar to the NEFW reporters and one of them finally knocks on the door. The door swings open and out walks
Rocko Johnson with Tracy Moore nowhere in site.) Reporter: Rocko, you've got some serious talking to do, Chris Douglas had hundreds of things to say to you, now, the question is what do you have to say to him?
Rocko: Well, pull up a few chairs and grab a few drinks because I'm going to be here awhile. Get comfortable, stay relaxed, and keep that camera rolling.
(The reporters pull up chairs and clean out the soda and candy vending machine and flick the camera on, they sit back and let Rocko got to work.)
Rocko: You are finally starting to get the point Chris Douglas, I said it before, and I'll say it again, I want you at your best. Now, you've have a total of two weeks to train for this match as do I, and I was hoping the challenge by myself to you would bring you back into the picture of the NEFW, obviously phase one is complete. You may be telling yourself right now that
I'm just talking bull sh*t because you accepted the challenge and thought that I could capture you at your week point, well, it crossed my mind but if I wanted you at your worst then I would have asked Lord Arthur Deysbrooks personally for a match and not call you out in front of the world a week ago.
You see Chris, you think you know me, you really do, you keep telling yourself your better than me, well that may be, but soon enough we will all find out for sure. I'm not wrestling you for a DQ win, I'm not wrestling you to win by a countout, I'm wrestling you to pin your shoulders to the mat and do something I don't think anyone in the NEFW or APW has ever successfully done, and that is shut you up! I've been around, I've seen you wrestle, I was there when you ran Creed out of the NEFW, I was there when you closed down the
AWE, I was there when the Misfits unquestionably ruled the APW and I was there when Chris Douglas first joined the NEFW, and trust me Chris, I'm going to still be here when you leave! I've earned everything I've ever had in the
NEFW, I put together a team called Devolution that won the stable titles. I pinned Rude Dude to become the first team to ever successfully defend the stable titles. I once was the North American Champion who Brandon Charm not once, but twice to take the belt away from him, and I pinned him again at the
Unification Match only to then be attacked by Five Fingers of Death who didn't have the balls to get involved in the match when Charm and I were exchanging blows.
You see, I've been around as well, my titles may not compare to the titles or recognition you've received in your career but one thing I've never done is demand to receive "push" in a fed I participate in like you did in the APW. I earn my "push" and I do a damn good job of earning it. I'm not asking for you to lay down in this match, no Chris, I'm putting you down and your going to stay down whether you like it or not. I've faced the likes of people like you before, and I've faced the teams you represent before, it's no secret that I knew the man Cyclone because the fact of the matter is he's like a brother to me, but I am not him personally. You can call it what you want Chris, but when we meet at the pay-per-view this match is going to beyond a "main event", their may not be any titles involved but this match is going to show the world that the good name of wrestling is still alive. We both now you've paid your dues here and well deserve a world title shot but your going to have to go through me first and that my friend is know easy task. Ask Old Brown
Heineken, ask Brandon Charm, ask the NEFW wrestlers who've faced me and have had there asses tossed around the ring like yesterday's garbage because nobody but nobody compares to the strength that I posses. Not you, not Aries,
Apocalypse, Titanicus, Slayer, or any other big man that thinks he's tough. Wrestlers go on and on about how they grew up on the streets of Los Angeles, or Chicago, or the back alley behind 7-11, not me Chris. I grew up in your average home in the Rocky Mountains of Colorado. But one thing is for sure nothing was handed to me on a silver platter. Most wrestlers who grow up in poor areas get their one lucky break to go big time, not me Chris, it took me a hell of a long time by beating the nobodies of feds and working my way up the ladder of life to where I am today. I don't walk into feds and demand title shots, I earn those shots. Now I'm not saying you don't but let the record show you had Pete's Wicked Title strapped around your waist in less than two months of being in the NEFW, but who did you beat, the Wrangler, I think he was about as mute for two months as you've been in the last two weeks. Chris Douglas, when you decided to make a return after I challenged you, you kept telling yourself, this is my chance, some punk thinks he can take advantage of me and roll right past me to the top, so you decided to come back thinking another easy win for Chris Douglas, well I've said it about five times and I'll say it about fifty more before we meet, this match is not going to be a walk in the park for you Chris Douglas, whether you like it or not this is going to be the battle of your life one way or another. And come
MARCH, 1, 1998 in BERWICK, PENNSYLVANIA at "NUCLEAR FALLOUT 2", our paths are going to meet for the first time in the history of the NEFW, once you cross that line Chris, there's no going back, and you better be prepared to expect anything, and everything!!! Chris Douglas, mark your calendar because in eight days I will see you in the ring!!!
(camera fades to black) [Chris really starts to heat it up] ( Chris Douglas and Titanicus are in the NEFW TV studios watching the latest flashes. ) Chris Douglas: What do we have here? Titanicus: I think Rocko Johnson is trying to be you, Chris. Chris Douglas: It damn sure as hell looks that way big man. Titanicus: Everyone knows that the painfully long, yet heat generating flash belongs to you, Chris. Everyone knows you invented it.
Chris Douglas: I know, I know, and yet Rocko thinks he can pull one off all by himself. Titanicus: Vell, not quite, he did have a reporter there to protect him. Chris Douglas: So true, and Rocko, it's good to see you're trying to get some additional heat behind our match and attempt to pull up the slack, but trust me, where Chris Douglas is concerned, there is no slack whatsoever. It's nice to see that you think so much of me Rocko, but trust me, it does nothing to my ego. My ego is already ten times the size of Texas, pal, and everyone who's been around for a while knows it. You see Rocko, there's a reason for that.
It's because my talent matches it. That's right Rocko, I talk the talk because I walk the walk. I back up what I say. Go on, ask anyone. I don't need to give examples like you, cause there isn't anyone around here who would even try to deny it. Rocko, you've had a hell of a lot more time to train for this match than I've had. You've had your entire career to train for this match, pal. Everyone spends their entire career hoping for the break that you've just got, Rocko. Everyone hopes for the big time match that I'm giving you, Rock. This match to me, is just another match, that's it. Sure, it's an important one, but no more so than
Douglas-Charm, or Douglas-Heineken, or Douglas-Myers, or indeed any of the other matches I've worked my ass off to put a good show on in. To you, Rocko, this match is your make or break. It's the all important chance at the real big time for you. Rocko, despite that, I ain't taking this match lightly.
This is my chance to make the big comeback to the NEFW. To release some of that untapped potential that I possess. And trust me, Rocko, I intend on doing so. If phase one of your little plan was to bring me back into the picture, sure, you can count that as being achieved. If your plan was to bring me back into the picture, then Rocko, you've brought back a monster. A man with the rage and the fury to get back what I once had... and I once had everything. That's right, the whole world. But Rocko, it wasn't just you.
It was just everything. Seeing people around here, seeing the way the titles are going. I know I'm good enough to make it to the top here. You see Rocko, it wasn't that I didn't WANT to come out and talk about the situation, it was that I COULDN'T. Time was something I had very little of, and my concentration was at an all-time low. I'm a man who doesn't spread himself thinly, and when I do something, I make damn sure that I do it right. So, in my attempts to get other things in my life back in order, my wrestling career was forced to take a back-seat. I'm not saying that this isn't important to me Rocko, cause everyone knows that I obsess over all of this. But if I'm not able to do this right, then as far as I'm concerned, I shouldn't be doing this at all. The point is that I know in my mind that I CAN do this right. Back to the old days when Chris Douglas called the shots. When the execs were eagre to please me. Happy to make me happy, cause when I'm happy I work a better match. Everyone knows that, Rocko. So, in time that's the way things will be once again. I'm not saying it's going to happen over night, cause it didn't in the first place. It's going to take time, and it's going to take people to fall to me. Take a look at yourself, Rocko, you're damn good, but you are by no means main event material. That's me, Rocko, the original main eventer. Rocko, everyone knows that the reason for your challenge was to attempt to catch me off guard. Sure, you're capable of beating most of the people around here, but not me. There is no shame in trying to beat the best when the best isn't paying attention. But just about the time you issued the challenge, I was just about ready to sit up and take notice. You were a week or so too late Rocko. Had you come out the week before and yelled for the match, I probably wouldn't have even noticed, and I surely would have paid for that, in much the same way I did against Rude Dude. But now I'm back. I'm ready, willing and definitely able to beat you Rocko, and anyone else that stands in my path too. Rocko, have I ever made a claim that wasn't true? I don't think so, in fact I know I haven't. That's why I've never said in so many words that I would for sure get an easy one two three over you. I know it's not going to be a walk in the park or a picnic for me, but I also know that I'm capable of beating anyone, as I've proved before. And Rocko, you're no different to all the others that have fallen to me in the past. Sure, this is now and that was then, but in all honesty it's one of the same thing. But there's one thing that you say that surprises the crap out of me, Rocko. You say that you're going to shut me up? My God, how many times have I heard that in the past? Too many, that's how many. Galliano's said it, everyone in
Devastation Inc have said it, Talonis said it, Dixon said it, Wrangler said it... pretty much everyone who is anyone has said it at one point in their miserable careers. And take a look, Rocko. Does it look like I've been shut up? I didn't think so. You see Rocko, you seem to want to compare past achievements. Sure you've been around, but so have I. If we were to compare what happened in the past, I'd come out in a higher league altogether, Rocko.
I mean, I've ended careers. Creed shouldn't even be here flashing right now... I put him out for good. But despite the fact that we've been partners and enemies, myself and him have a different schedule altogether right now.
Maybe if Megacorp decide to hire again around here, maybe then our paths will cross, but for now, I'm putting all my attention into my match against Rocko.
And Creed wasn't the only one either. Dixon is another, Wrangler is another, and Talonis, even though Creed seems to be taking credit for that. Rocko, I haven't just ended careers, I've ended entire federations. You've said it yourself Rocko, you commented on that. It is also in your own admission that
I, along with the rest of the Misfits ruled the APW. For those of you who don't know, the Misfits were the original Degenerates... the original top heels... the original heat generators. For all the Starmen, LeBarrons and other newcomers who might give a damn, what you see Shawn Michaels and Hunter et al doing in the WWF, and the antics they get up to... that's what the
Misfits were doing quite a while ago. Before them, anyway. You see, despite the fact that the chances of me getting that much power in this fed are somewhat low, the chance is still there. But I'm man enough to be able to co-exist in an organisation with other people. I'm also man enough to be able to exist in an organisation that I don't have total creative control over my matches in. Not that I ever have in the past, but it's sure seemed like that way to some people. Rocko, if you intend on being here until I leave, then you'll be here for one hell of a long time, cause I have no plans on going anywhere. Rocko, when I go, I'll be taking everything with me, pal. I don't walk out on things if they get a little tough. People know that, Rocko. YOU should know that, Rocko. Rocko, there is no need to play the martyr act, saying that you're a self-made man. Talking about how you've earned everything you've had. That's an old routine, Rocko. Pretty much everyone around here made it on their own. I know I sure did. Rocko, so you talk about the people you've beaten in the past? You've beaten Brandon Charm twice? Rocko, I hold at least twelve falls over Brandon. And in my, as well as pretty much everyone else's opinion, Brandon is one of the best in the business. And Rocko, you're talking of my <makes quotations with his fingers>
"push"? Rocko, the APW push incident was all worked, pal. I've never demanded a push, I said it to get myself over as the big egotist, and Megacorp complied with it, cause it looked so damn real it just wasn't true. I've earned my push by playing my role, Rocko. And for the record, I'm no gimmick wrestler. Rocko, I honestly could not care less whether you want me to lay down or not, the bottom line is one of us will be, and unless you can prove to me that you deserve to be the one who isn't laying down, there's no way in hell I will be. You can talk all you want about having faced the likes of me before. Rocko, you've never faced anyone with my god-given ability and raw unadulterated talent. I'm one in a billion, pal. You don't see many people like me in one life-time. Rocko, I came this close <holds fingers millimetres apart> to getting my world title run, but you've got to understand that it wasn't just the politics of the federation that kept me away from it, it was the fact that back then I had too many other things going on. Right now, this is the only place I compete. This place gets my full attention. Rocko, let the record show what you want, but if you're in doubt of my methods of getting the pay-per-view title in such a short space of time, it's because I deserved it. In those first few weeks of being in this place, I put more effort into what I was doing than anyone else. I flashed more times in those few weeks than some people had in the whole year. I deserve what I get, Rocko. This ain't going to be the battle of my life, Rocko. It's going to be just another battle on the way back to the top for Chris Douglas. Rocko, try as hard as you want to pull something out of the bag, cause no matter how hard you work,
I'll work that one little bit harder, just like I always have done. That is how it should be, Rocko Johnson, and that my friend, is how it is.
