RP Critique - Sex Pistol

5 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm kinda new to the e-wrestling business and would appreciate (constructive) feed back on my latest RP. (The Pistol is listening to some of the recent interviews given by POW wrestlers and becoming increasingly angry. Finally, he stops the tape and stares at a camera. As he does so, he begins to speak.)
Sex Pistol: Black Ninja, I have listened to your childish rantings for long enough. You ask where I get off saying that I'm a better wrestler than you. I repeat, yet again, compare the win-loss records. But, if you still can't see what's blindingly obvious, you and me, one on one, at next week's
House Show. By the way, leave off Sheo. Who cares if he's gay? I mean, when I first saw Brandy, I thought you had a male escort! Anyway, the only thing wrong with Philly is he's wearing my belt.
Blazer, listen up, you tell me to stay away from your boy's match with
Philly. You're mistaking me for someone who cares. It's just a matter of time before the belt's round my waist, so I don't mind who I have to beat to get it. Also, you ask why I don't want revenge on Manson. Think about it.
Not only did he get himself DQ'ed and miss out on a title shot, he's condemning himself to an eternity of teaming with Ninjy! That's revenge enough, thank you very much.
Finally, Copy Cat, hope you haven't forgotten about our match tomorrow, because I can't wait to pick up another win to move me even further ahead of
Ninja and Kagiri.
(The Pistol reaches forward and turns the camera off.) Thanks a lot, Pistol
