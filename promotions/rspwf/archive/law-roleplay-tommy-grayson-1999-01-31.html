[LAW Roleplay] Tommy Grayson

31 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens at a garage sale in upstate New York. Various items are being sold. Toasters, picture frames, tupperware, old clothes, etc. It is afternoon around 2:32 PM. The garage sale seems to be doing bad. Suddenly, a
Ford Taurus drives up to the garage sale and a familiar-looking man gets out of the driver's side. It is "The Loose Cannon" Tommy Grayson.]
[Tommy is wearing a black leather jacket over a black t-shirt that reads in white lettering "I said no to drugs, but they didn't listen." He is also wearing Levis blue jeans with a black, leather belt, white Nike sneakers with black trim, and gold-rimmed mirror-shaded sunglasses. His black, spikey, crewcut hair has a grungy, messed-up look as usual.]
[Tommy steps up to one of the tables and begins looking at what looks like a dilapidated trash can lid. The salesman walks up to him.]
SALESMAN - Interested in that? TOMMY GRAYSON - This damn thing? Nah. It'd break as soon as it touches somebody's forehead.
SALESMAN - Uh...say 'gain? TOMMY GRAYSON - I said it'll break as soon as it touches somebody's forehead! [Tommy shoots the rusted trash can lid onto the table. He looks at the salesman and takes off his sunglasses.]
TOMMY GRAYSON - Hey, you got anything else here that's _NOT_ old? I'm looking for something that can be used as a weapon. You know...for professional wrestling.
SALESMAN - Ah, I see...yer a pro-rassler, eh? TOMMY GRAYSON - Yeah...got a problem with that? SALESMAN - Um, try searchin' Yahoo.com for weapons n' stuff. TOMMY GRAYSON - I already did that...all I could find was freakin' porno sites! SALESMAN - Hey, um...mind givin' me some addresses? TOMMY GRAYSON - Alright, alright...www.bondage.com...is that good enough for you? Now, find something for me! You know, like a tazer or somethin'!
SALESMAN - Sorry...but some slick-haired guy already bought a tazer...his name was Scott something...
TOMMY GRAYSON - Well, whatever. Just find me something that kids can't touch. I've got a match coming up against Branden Travers this coming Sunday, and I can't afford to lose a match against a pathetic twit like that. Go find something, and I'll wait here. I've gotta cut another damn good promo.
SALESMAN - Right! I'll go find sumthin'... [The salesman exits view of the camera. Tommy looks back at him, shakes his head, and chuckles slightly. He turns to the camera and begins to speak.]
TOMMY GRAYSON - Well, what a night High Anxiety was. A night full of shockers, indeed. I pissed on Higor Rosa's picture...or poured a cup of piss on it. Like I said, there's no way in _HELL_ that I'm gonna show Da Big
Tommybowski to the whole damn arena! Besides...it was kinda cold in there and
I didn't want him to shrivel up. Anyways, after that, I dominated that battle royale, only to be eliminated by several guys. Particularly Branden Travers.
Y'see, Branden, you made a big-ass mistake in helping to eliminate me. Why? Because I've got a short fuse and I will hurt _ANYBODY_ that tries to bring me down...and you tried to bring me down, Travers. _BIG_ mistake.
I'm a master at pain, Travers. A word that you will _TRUELY_ know the meaning of come Sunday, February eighth. Seeing you suffer, Travers, just gives me near- orgasmic pleasure. Hey, maybe I'll prepare a cup of urine just for you, Travers...I'll buy one of those tupperware storage cups over there, piss into one, label it "URINE FOR TRAVERS" and then, after I beat you, I'll pour it all over your damn face! Then we'll find out if my urine has the same acidic effect as that dog from the movie "Man's Best Friend."
[The salesman walks over to Tommy carrying a roll of razor wire.] SALESMAN - Is this more like it, sir? TOMMY GRAYSON - Damn, man...I COULD KISS YOU! This is perfect, man! [Tommy snatches the roll of razor wire and looksat it in admiration.] TOMMY GRAYSON - How much is it, exactly? SALESMAN - Um, fer you it's free! TOMMY GRAYSON - [chuckles] That's awesome! Perfect! Hey, got anything else? SALESMAN - Um...hold on... [The salesman goes off-screen for a few seconds...suddenly, he drags in a
_HUGE_ oil drum. Tommy pretends to get an amazed look on his face.]
TOMMY GRAYSON - Okaaay...that's a little too big to fit into my Taurus. Hell, I'm not even gonna bother dragging that damn thing around! Get that thing outta my face, man! Take your ass along with it! You're taking up my interview time!
[Tommy obnoxiously pushes the salesman off camera. Tommy turns back to the camera, and a sadistic smile cracks on his face.]
TOMMY GRAYSON - [chuckling] As I was sayin', Travers...I'm gonna make your blood flow out of your body like cheap wine from a keg. Travers, I'm gonna make you live...
...LIKE... ...A... ..._SUICIDE_!! And you better heed this warning, Travers... ...LIVE IF YOU _DARE_... ..._DIE_ BECAUSE YOU _MUST_!! [Tommy gets into his Ford Taurus, taking the roll of razor wire with him. Fade to black.] *Handler of: "The Loose Cannon" Tommy Grayson [EFW, EWA, ICW, JPW, LAW] Current EFW Extreme North American Champion Winner of ICW's Biggest Lunatic and Most Underrated Award "You can't have my cornbread...cause I'm from New
York City! There are gonna be consequences and reprocussions!"
