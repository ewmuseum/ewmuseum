[Please Critique] Dark Wolf Flash

14 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings to all. It's been quite a while since I've posted a flash here but I'm looking to improve so here's one of my best. All critiques are welcome. Plus, if you have any tips for improvement, they are welcome as well.
Background of the Flash Dark Wolf was taged up with a Navajo Indian descentent called Crazy Horse. The team went by the name Deadly Force. Their manager, Wild Animal, was
Dark Wolf's girlfriend. This drove Crazy Horse nuts with jealousy. He eventually attacked the two (several months before this flash) and put Wild
Animal in a coma. The team patched things up when Wild Animal recovered.
Then the jealousy returned. Crazy Horse was taking it to the next level: breaking into her locker room at the arena, sneaking into the women's dressing room at a Chicago-based YMCA, and (after this flash) kidnapping
Wild Animal, forcing a confrontation between himself and Dark Wolf.
With this flash, Dark Wolf was realizing what was happening and these were his thoughts. Following the flash, Dark Wolf turned on Crazy Horse, causing the team to lose a match to unify the NAWC and NWWA Tag Team Titles. (It was a week later that the kidnapping happened.) Anyway, enough of the background. Let's hear your thoughts on this.
Thanks. Neal (handler of Dante Krieg, aka Dark Wolf) http://dantekrieg.cjb.net ****** FLASH ****** [The scene fades in to a television screen. The volume is turned down considerably. Zoom in to what it shows...]
START VIDEO FOOTAGE Wild Animal [off screen]: AAAAAHHHH!!!!! [Cut to inside the dressing room as we see Crazy Horse sitting on a bench in a pair of swimming trunks. He jumps with the scream and grabs a nearby pair of jeans and begins putting them on.]
Wild Animal: Crazy Horse!! This is the second time you've done this!! Crazy Horse [buttoning his jeans]: I'm so sorry Wild Animal!! The clerk at the desk must've gave me bad directions or something. The door was open so I didn't realize this was the women's dressing room. I'm so sorry.
Wild Animal: You will be when Dark Wolf finds out!! Crazy Horse: No, please don't tell him. Please. We need to focus on our opponents for Tuesday. He doesn't need any distractions. Please don't tell him. This was just a mistake.
Wild Animal [hands on her hips]: Just get out Crazy Horse. I can't believe you could do this again!! What if other women were in here?! Then what would you do?!
Crazy Horse [raising the palm of his hand to her as he backs out]: I'm sorry. But please don't tell him. I promise it won't happen again.
Wild Animal: It better not!! END VIDEO FOOTAGE [The television clicks off, leaving the sounds of static. Cut to a shot of
Dark Wolf lying in his bed, his pillow proped up against the headboard, the sheets pulled to just above his waist. He is sweating heavily. A lamp glows dimly on an end table to his right. Dark Wolf picks up the alarm clock that sits next to it. He almost whispers the words...]
Dark Wolf: Two thirty in the morning. [Sigh] God I need some sleep. [He puts the clock back and turns to his right side. We then see Wild Animal wrapped snuggly beneath the sheets. She is sound asleep. Dark Wolf looks at her with eyes full of tears. Quickly, and so as not to drench her with them, he slips from the sheets into a pair of jeans. He turns off the lamp and heads for the door when we hear Wild Animal's voice, soft, almost a wimper.]
Wild Animal: Where are you going? Dark Wolf [quietly]: I can't sleep. So I'm gonna go for a walk. I'll be back in ten minutes though.
Wild Animal: I'll go with you. Dark Wolf: No, it's okay. I need to think things through. You go back to sleep. I promise I'll be back here in no more than ten minutes.
Wild Animal: Alright. [Dark Wolf opens a closet door and pulls a fleece from it. He puts it on and exits the room. Cut to outside the house. It is nearly pitch black in areas but a street light provides enough illumination to see. Dark Wolf walks down the sidewalk leading from the house and turns left onto another sidewalk.
His walk is brisk, yet we can keep up with him. He begins to talk, not looking at the camera but staring off into the distance, into the future.]
Dark Wolf: That's twice now in a week. [Sigh] It's started again. His jealousy determines his actions. He can't keep his mind on what's important at this stage in his career. All he wants is to get up close with my girl.
[He stops at a crossing, looking quickly in either direction, then continuing...] Dark Wolf: But she is my girl. She always has been, and always will be. I won't let anything get in the way of that. He put her in a coma last time because of his jealousy. When she came out of it, I promised her I'd never let it happen again. She pleaded with me not to befriend him again. Pleaded for God's sake. And I ignored her. I ignored the only thing that truly means the world to me. I ignored her opinion and her intellect. I thought I would make the best decision possible, to let him back into our lives.
[We reach what looks to be a busy street, though now it is almost deserted. Dark Wolf turns left and continues...] Dark Wolf: Her instincts were right all along. She knew what he was like, what he was capable of. She knew that if he got away with it, he would do it again. And by the looks of things, that's where he's headed once again.
[He wipes the cold sweat from his forehead.] Dark Wolf: Nobody knows Crazy Horse's true nature. Nobody has known him as long as I have. And frankly, nobody cares but me. I have made a critical error in my life. I let her manage the two of us when she could just have managed me. But then again, that would have made him more jealous.
[He sniffles lightly with the cold. Yet he is still sweating heavily.] Dark Wolf: All these feelings I've suppressed in the hopes that my efforts would be rewarded. Indeed we are the N.A.W.C. Tag Team Champions. Soon, we shall face Jason Blake and Jimmy Hinh to unify the titles. But what is it worth? Why should I endanger my loved one's life once more, just for the sake of glory? My God, what have I done? How can I right the wrongs that he has created? He was once my friend. But now he is nothing more than a monster driven by the urge for sex. Many consider me a monster, but at least
I am loyal. At least I won't sink as low as him. At least...
[He trails off, his many thoughts obviously overwhelming him. He falls to his knees and begins to cry, overcome by his feelings, his anger. Finally he rises once more to his feet, the determination evident in his eyes, in his voice.]
Dark Wolf: We will face Blake and Hinh on Tuesday. Together, and as a unit. We will conquer these petty worms. But when the day is done, Deadly Force will be no more. I will personally vacate the title for worthier champions.
Indeed, I am a worthy champion, but Crazy Horse is not. We need to be a worthy team in order to hold those belts. That, we are not. So Crazy Horse, if you're watching this, realize what destruction you've caused. You've ruined a perfectly good friendship and the best of tag teams.
[Dark Wolf turns around and starts back the way he came. The scene fades out.]
