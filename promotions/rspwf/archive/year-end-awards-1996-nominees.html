Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fans of e-wrestling: These are the rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy awards for 1996. The voting deadline is December 5th at 3PM PST. You can vote using the ballot in the other news message. Do not post to the newsgroup, sent your ballot to [detail withheld]@direct.ca A first place vote is worth 5 points, a second place vote is worth 3 points, and a third place vote is worth 1 point. You do not need a 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place vote. Please only vote once per e-mail address. Fed heads do e-mail this post and the other post to other e-wrestling fans and players. If you know any further information about specifics of the league(s) a wrestler, match, or card is in e-mail me also. If you wish include comments on the nominees or spoof awards (Best Aegis, Most Improved, Best Loudmouth on RSPWF, etc.) Include your name and e-mail address in the ballot. And... maybe your wreslter or fed might win.
