-*Personal Statistics*- Name: James Storm Height: 6'0" Weight: 255 lbs From: Calgary, Alberta, Canada Age: 25 Finisher: The Storm Warning (Death Valley Driver, turned into a Stiff Michinoku
Driver) Theme Song: "When Worlds Collide" by Powerman 5000 Wrestling Style: Ring Technician, with Power and High Flying Abilities as well. Relatives: Lance Storm (Brother) Trained By: Lance Storm Professional Debut: 1996 Former Aliases: Kid America -*Biography*- James Storm is the relativly unknown brother of ECW and WCW star, "The Canadian
Icon" Lance Storm, and with all the attention Lance was getting, James never quite got his chance to shine in the world of professional wrestling. He first wrestled under the name "Kid America" in a now defunct independant fedaration, in Austin Texas, but his love for his home of Canada soon caused him to tear off the Red, White, and Blue crested Kid Amercia costume, and he burned it in the middle of the ring, wearing nothing but a pair of Canadian Maple Leaf
Boxers, and he showed the world his true feelings by badmouthing America, and he even went as far to attack, and cripple the top guy, just simply because, he was everything the Americans were, just like Bret Hart hated Steve Austin for being a foul mouthed Drunk, James Storm hated America because American's are what's wrong with society today, but when people come to James, telling him to love it or leave it...He just simply tells them, its not his fault, that
America is a human waste land...and he tells them to Not Blame Canada...but to
Blame Themselves...ever since that unfortunate riot, he has donned a pair of white Pants style tights, with the Red Canadian Maple Leaf Design on each leg, to show his love for his country...and he is now a free agent...awaiting his hiring, to show the world...Just why the best wrestlers come from
Calgary...Alberta, Canada -*Contact Information*- If you feel that "The Canadian Hero" James Storm is the person your fed needs to keep things at the top then send an E-Mail or an America Online Instant

Canada....Blame Yourselves.. ::He pulled the microphone from his lips, and looked over at the booing croud, a smirk on his face at their obvious displeasure with his words::
