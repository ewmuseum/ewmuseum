3 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings.... Below is a sample RP for a character that I've been handling for over a year now. His gimmick is pretty simple, he was my first character, but he's still my deepest and best. I'm hoping to hear from some of the larger feds, WWO,
EMWC, AWMC, UEW, SCRA...but will listen to offers from any organization.
Thanks. (An all consuming darkness overcomes the scene. Your eyes are forced towards the screen...there's almost something hypnotic about it. Out of the darkness, beginning with a dull hum deep within the recess and growing gradually to the forefront, static distortion plays to the dark background.)
(The confusion of the static is broken up by the voice of sounds to be a news report of some kind.)
Reporter: Tonight, GSW has perhaps glimpsed the future...and his name is Logan
Gunz. The virtual unknown came from nowhere to wow the California crowd with his overwhelming intensity and explosive ability.....
(The voice fades back into static. The distortion seems to gain a rhythm of sorts as the mind attempts to make sense of the confusion. Once again, the static fades, and is replaced by another voice.)
Reporter: Last night, in action from the SWF, Logan Gunz and Shooting Star battled to a double disqualification. Gunz becoming the first man to put any blemish on the meteoric rise of Star...watch for these two to continue their feud for years to come.
(Once again, the beloved static returns...and fades into yet another voice.) Reporter: The Star-crossed path of "The Lone Enforcer" Logan Gunz took yet another turn last night. In SWF action, Gunz seemingly had defeated Shooting
Star for the Emerald City title until The Outlanders interfered....
The question is beginning to swirl around the NWC, "Can Logan Gunz win the big one?" He's already being called the "Best Wrestler in NWC Without a Title".
(Static returns, then fades out...repetitive, isn't it?) Reporter: Tonight in CCW, Logan Gunz debuted. Gunz, a member of SWF until it was bought out by Colossus Industries, has had his contract purchased by Ray
Pierce and The Elite.
It appears as though they intend on humiliating Gunz to no end, forcing him to drop out of the CCW title tournament. Once again, the question bears asking,
"Will Logan Gunz ever break his curse?".
(20th verse, same as the first...static in...static out) Reporter: Dateline, Motor City Wrestling. In a unexpected turn of events, "The
Lone Enforcer" Logan Gunz, a man thought to have hung up the boots for good...has signed a minimum salary contract with MCW.
Gunz, a one-time Seattle cult icon, brings his unbridled intensity to MCW with one mission...a chance at NWC gold. He has entered himself into the battle royal at Coboslam XIII, hoping to garner a shot at the Motor City Title. Fans across NWC are asking, "Is it finally Logan's turn?".
(Finally the blackness comes alive with images from Coboslam XIII....) ML: Manhattan just opened up a barrage on Gunz.....Lefts and rights clubbing
Gunz....Manhattan sends Gunz into the ropes, Gunz rebounds....Manhattan scoops him up....Gunz glides over and....
[The crowd pops....] Sammy: OH MY GOD! ML: Gunz just picked Manhattan up....Military Press position.....Rogers is just now getting to his feet and....
[The crowd explodes again...] Sammy: IMPOSSIBLE! ML: Logan Gunz in an amazing feat of strength....BENCH PRESSED MANHATTAN and sent him flying onto Mike Rogers as he's single handidly eliminated both former
NWC World Tag Team Champions!
(Another scene flashes across the screen...) ML: No, look at Gunz fight it.....But Tobe is punching away at his head....something has to give here....Gunz staggering around with Tobe firing away at his head.....Gunz turns....
[The loud sounds of the crowd exploding and a crashing can be heard....] ML: OH Sammy: MY ML: GOOOOOODDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDD!!!!!!!! Sammy: DID YOU SEE THAT!?!! [DING, DING, DING....] ML: LOGAN GUNZ JUST RUNNING POWERBOMBED TOBE HIGH OVER THE TOP ROPE AND DOWN THROUGH THE BACKUP ANNOUNCE TABLE! MY LORD!!! [The crowd explodes...] Ober: LADIES & GENTLEMEN, TOBE HIGH HAS BEEN ELIMINATED.....AND SO YOUR WINNER IS...... "THE LONE ENFORCER" LOGAN GUNZ!! (Silence once again overtakes the screen as still photos of the ladder match between Jimmy Floyd and Meltdown are shown. They follow from Gunz entering the ring and tackling the ladder out from under Meltdown, to Logan scaling the ladder and retrieving the title belt. The last still shot is of Logan standing over Jimmy Floyd after placing the Motor City title on his waist....the screen once again goes to black)
(The camera fades up on a pair of steel green eyes glaring through the faint light. The shot draws back to reveal the full, unshaven face of Logan Gunz.
Little emotion is perceivable in his sharp features, his blood seems as if it might run ice cold.)
(The camera draws back even further, revealing his surroundings...or better yet, lack of them. There are no discernable shapes, objects, figures, or even walls surrounding Gunz as he sits with his elbows propped on the table before him.)
(His t-shirt, cut off at the shoulder, reveals the tatoos that identify him as
"the panther". One on each arm...the glare in their eyes seems to emulate that of Logan. Underneath the panther upon his left arm are the words, "DOOM ON
YOU", inked into his flesh. A blue bandanna is tied securely, as always, around his freshly shaven head..."GUNZ" lettered across the front.)
(The scene is quite intimidating as the light from a candle in front of him dances within his eyes. Logan takes a deep breath...and begins to speak.)
Logan: Once in a millenium, Mother Nature makes genetic mistakes...when that occurs, she must move as quickly as possible to correct it.
Jimmy "Pretty Boy" Floyd has proven to be quite that mistake...and come this
Saturday...he will be corrected.
(The orange light flickers as an unseen wind passes through the room.) Logan: Floyd feels it necessary to question my record within the NWC. He's used the words "dark-match", "mid-carder", and "bit-player" to describe me. My record speaks for itself, Jimmy. One loss in close to a year within the NWC...I challenge you to be able to say the same.
So...why no titles? The answer is simple, much like you would like to do, champions do their best to avoid the forest. They realize that once they enter, all hope is abandoned. The overwhelming presence stalking their every move becomes too much...it begins to squeeze their conciousness. They feel the footsteps behind them...they begin to break out in cold sweats...and they run in terror.
(Gunz rubs the stubble under his chin with his left hand.) Logan: Floyd also questions why the people of the Motor City would chant the name of Logan Gunz, even in his first match. Unlike you, these people are educated, decent people. They have been watching the NWC for quite some time, and they appreciate a rarity in this sport...a man that doesn't back down.
These are working class people, Jimmy. When they pay their hard-earned money to come to a DCW show, they want to see MEN. Not two men in touch with their
"feminine side", wearing robes that look more like ball gowns, hair down to their posterior, gingerly stepping to the ring. What they want is a man who contains the animal within, yet is unable to unleash it upon command...a man who they know has uncompromisable morals and values...and a man whose balls arrived three days earlier, on a dump truck.
The story, nay saga, of Logan Gunz has become a journey into the reaches of how much dissapointment one man can take. The underdog, a man that no one respects...yet makes everyone shake where they stand, finally overcoming his inner demons and rising above. That is what has caused several of the fans of
NWC to follow my career, and it's why they chant my name in the arenas...not yours.
(Logan leans back in the chair and casts the glare of his eyes to the surrounding darkness.)
Logan: Each man has his own destiny. Some men's destiny leads them down the golden path to greatness. Some, on the other hand, have to walk each path until they find the correct one. Regardless which destiny is yours, however, they all appear the same at the beginning. Much like this room, no matter where you turn, all you can see is the darkness.
What seperates the Gunz from people such as Floyd?? I accept my destiny and move forward without fear. For the last year, I've accepted that I may never be destined to attain NWC gold. I resigned myself to the fact that, to my glory-hounding colleagues, I would never be more than just another cog in their publicity machine. Men like Jimmy Floyd, who measure their success in title belts and groupies at the hotel, can never understand what motivates me to continue on my path.
The thrill of the kill is unknown to them, they know only the fear of being killed. (The camera draws close in on the legendary eyes of "The Lone Enforcer". The candlelight continues to dance in their green iris, seeming to mimic the obvious fires that burn deep within his soul.)
Logan: That's why I've surrounded myself in the darkness here, Floyd. To give you just the slightest feeling of what it will be like, psychologically, when you step between those ropes Saturday night. What it will be like when the lights in the arena go down, you've taken that girlish robe off your yellow back, and all the talking is over. You will know then, that you have entered the lair of the one true predator within the NWC...and you will soon begin to realize that your chances of survival dwindle with each second that passes.
You are now beginning to get a hint of what it's going to be like to stare into the eyes of the panther. When that "tunnel-vision" begins to overtake your mind, and at every turn you see nothing but "The Annihilator" baring down on you. You'll try to run...and realize that your legs, paralyzed by the fear, will fail you.
Your delusions of stardom, your feeling of invincibility, and your egocentric state of mind will begin to deteriorate as you come to terms with the fact that you must now back up your hollow words.
(Leaning back forward onto the table, Gunz dips his head slightly. His eyes now peer out through his eyebrows in a menacing display. His right hand begins to rub the tattoo upon his left arm, as if preparing the panther for hunt.)
Logan: You've attempted to map my "failed" NWC career, like so many before you. Like each one of them, you will feel the impact of the Shotgun Press and become nothing more than another in a long line of those who wished to venture into my territory.
Say what you will, and ramble how you must...come Saturday night, we will meet at the third crossing of our careers. Round one was mine, round two went your way...it's time to bring this to a definitive conclusion.
This Saturday night, Jimmy Floyd, fate will be watching...and she is merciless. She will watch as I call her out, and you tempt her. This Saturday, I challenge my fate and attempt to alter the course of my destiny forever...you, Jimmy
Floyd, should just hope to survive.
(Logan looks down and blows out the candle, sending the room into complete blackness) Logan: Doom on you, Jimmy Floyd...for this Saturday, the weak light of your career will be snuffed out eternally, while the panther's new destiny will finally come to light.
(The blackness holds on the screen for a few seconds, despite the dark shadow of the room...the intensity radiating from Gunz is still tangible. That holds for a few seconds and then fades as the scene goes to static.)
(END)
