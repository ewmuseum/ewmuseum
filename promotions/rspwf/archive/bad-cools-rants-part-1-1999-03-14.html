Bad-Cool's Rants, part 1 -- Niggers

14 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[NOTES] Here's a roleplay I did earlier... Against dumb niggers. Enjoy. ______________________ (As the camera comes on, we see Bad-Cool walking in the mean streets of
Halifax, Novia Scotia, Canada with his new girl, Jenny Talia. Jenny has on a silk red top that cuts off just above her belly button as well as silk pants that hang down to briefly show her panties but have golden chains that make them hang up to only show the top of her black panties -- the chains connect to her belly button ring on her nice, soft, and firm chest.
Bad-Cool has on a black leather jacket, black finger gloves, blue denim jeans, and a black AWFL T-Shirt. As they pass a few stores, they stop at a ghetto and look down at the niggers in the alleyway. Jenny starts to point and laugh at the homeless niggers on the street as Bad-Cool laughs too)
Bad-Cool: There's supposed to be an anti-rascism thing in the AWFL, but NO
ONE in the AWFL has, is, or probably ever will be black and in this league, so BC is going to make fun of those thin-skinned shitskins as long as he wants to! And if you have a problem with it, suck my dick.
(Bad-Cool and Jenny Talia move to a nigger who has on a torn pirates short and torn khakis on. He has holes in his shoes and his Monkey face is looking for some possum to chew on, maybe even some yams)
Bad-Cool: Hey, nigger! (Suddenly, the nigger stands up and notions for 3 of his ghetto buddies to chase after Bad-Cool. As the 4 men -- who basically have the same attire -- move forward, Bad-Cool takes something out of his pocket. A cross. He then takes his lighter out and burns it and throws it at the niggers)
Bad-Cool: Look familiar, boys? Remember when your grandfather got burned at the cross a few years back for acting so much like a nigger?
(The niggers cower away as Bad-Cool laughs at them. As Bad-Cool continues for a few minutes, Jenny soon joins in but Bad-Cool stops her and the niggers have a jagged smile since BC is finally stopping. But to make the niggers mad, they laugh for a few more minutes)
Bad-Cool: So, BC has a plan for those nigger scum... Step I: It was necessary to keep the niggers from forming their own "free state". Thus spread out and diluted amongst the majority white population they lost the ability to organise into a credible fighting force. Now they can only form small gangs that at best might burn down their own neighborhoods rather than wage war on neighboring states. Step II: Herd them into concentration camps called the "inner city" were their destructive urges can be turned against themselves. Step III: Start cutting off the social "safety net"
they have become dependent upon. Step IV: Watch them turn on each other like starving rats in a barrel. That way, the niggers will get what they deserve. Maybe they won't act so much like monkeys then...
(Bad-Cool points Jenny Talia who is acting like a monkey. She then does a
Monkey-like dance around the niggers. This pisses them off big time)
Bad-Cool: Martin Luther King Jr. had a dream, but the white race has a PLAN... (Bad-Cool reaches into his pocket and pulls out a black crown. The camera zooms in on it. Instead of saying "Black", it says "Nigga")
Bad-Cool: Did you hear the news? Crayola officially changed the name of the color black to "Nigga" color. So, now, your kids can color with the niggers! Isn't that great, educating these dumb nigger kids while they're young?
(Jenny laughs for no reason as Bad-Cool and her move out of the ghetto and
Bad-Cool throws his crayon down and steps on it)
Bad-Cool: Final word for now: All you need to know of black "culture" you can find out on the Fox TV show, COPS. The niggas are lazy, shiftless thieves!! So, remember world, BC hates jigaboos, and their possum-loving
yam-eating asses are going to be BC's. And that's that. (Bad-Cool and Jenny move along and flip off the niggers who pass by in their red corvettes and their white broncos and insult the niggers every change they get -- it's just part of Bad-Cool's new anti-nigger campaign)
-"Mr Ratings", Former KKK Member ISH 4 LIFE
