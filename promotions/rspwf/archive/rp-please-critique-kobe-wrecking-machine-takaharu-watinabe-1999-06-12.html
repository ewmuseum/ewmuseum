(RP, please critique) "Kobe Wrecking Machine" Takaharu Watinabe.

12 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here's a flash I just did for G-pro. Hopefully, it holds up. -- (Fade in on the interior of a nondescript room. Looking at the screen this scene could be taking place in any number of buildings. A high-school gymnasium, a rented warehouse, or a set built specifically for this purpose.
In the center of the scene stands a podium, with folding tables on either side. At the tables sit a number of official looking types. On top of the podium a number of microphones. Each has the insignia of a television station. All is still for a moment as a crowd gathered before this podium remains silent, waiting.
Finally, a horrible sound like that of metal tearing is heard. The crowd reacts negatively, every man and woman holding their ears, and looking almost panicked. From stage right comes a young Japanese man dressed in a metallic tuxedo. A harsh spotlight shines down on him from above, causing his body to shine like the sun. He stops in front of the podium, facing the crowd. Finally the spotlight dies, and he removes a pair of dark, black sunglasses from his face. Finally the horrible sound stops and he begins to speak.)
"Machine": *Ahem*, are these things on? The man begins to tap each microphone in turn. The crowd finally realizes that the horrible sound has ceased, and everyone takes their hands away from their ears.
"Machine": Hello? Can I have a confirmation on the microphones please? Reporter: Yeah, they work! Can't you hear? "Machine": Huh? Oh, oh, wait! The man before the podium then removes a pair of earplugs from his ears, a huge grin on his face.
"Machine": My fault, heh, that noise can make you deaf, you know. Well, folks, I bet you're all wondering why I called you here today. Please allow me to introduce myself: I am the "Kobe Wrecking Machine" Takaharu
Watinabe!!! On the upcoming sunday I will step into a G-pro ring not once, but twice to do battle and DESTROY many a man! First, I shall prove myself against the Zokugan Sangai, aiding my unworthy luck of the draw opponents to victory, then on to the battle royal. Like a whirling Typhoon I shall spin about the ring, buffetting all that stand in my way! In the end, I shall stand alone. . .
(Suddenly a simply gorgeous japanese lady reporter pipes up. . .) Lady: Excuse me? You say that your name is Watinabe? "Machine": Why, yes I am beautiful. Heard of me, have you? Lady: The same Watinabe that lost to Stripe this past Sunday? "Machine": LOST!? Whoo-hah, I think you've got it wrong, sistah! I pinned that guy at least a hundred times!
Lady: No, I saw that match, Stripe hit you with an Ocean Cyclone Suplex, and pinned you. "Machine": It. . .it's not time for questions yet! I. . .let me think! (Watinabe continues to sputter as the lady reporter presses her point.) Lady: Now, what about Ozeki Shumasita? "Machine": Who? Lady: The *ahem* (smiling big.) "Mad_Pooper"? "Machine": HIM! THAT BASTARD! HE RUINED MY BOOTS! Ozeki Shumasita is the
FIRST man I shall eliminate from the battle royal!
Lady: Actually, according to my sources, Shumasita has retired from the G-pro. . . "Machine": WHAT? NO! (Eyes wide and mouth gaping, the self-named "Wrecking Machine" falls silent. The lady reporter, however, does not stop.) Lady: Now, you say that you're going to defeat Zokugan Sangai in the Round
Robin tournament for the vacant Trios titles, correct?
"Machine": (Nods.). . . Lady: I see here that you are partnering with one Toshiaki Takada, quite a good man to have as your partner. He did defeat the hated Jarred Jones after all. The only hole in your claim is the mystery partner, who is this man?
"Machine": I. . .I dunno. . .who? (The crowd of reporters begins to laugh. Louder and louder they become, laughing and pointing and losing any semblence of professionalism. The
"Wrecking Machine" is completely out of his element at this point. He stares at the crowd who laughs at him with a look of abject defeat on his face. It is definitely clear at this point that he is not prepared for trios action.
The crowd quiets after a few moments and the woman, the only one even_willing to speak to Takaharu continues her hard press.)
Lady: (Still laughing, fighting to get the words out.) I. . .heh. . .how, how do you expect to win against a regular team, the Zokugan Sangai, if you don't work with your partners? My god, you don't even KNOW your partners. .
. Have you even ever spoken to Toshiaki Takada? "Machine": I. . .maybe! (The laughter grows in intensity as everyone sees that this is not the case.) Lady: Let me get this straight, you, an unknown, who has no experience, who has never laid eyes on his partners, expect to defeat perhaps the most powerful force in the G-pro, and THEN go on to win a battle royal, and you don't even seem to know who is in the battle royal?
"Machine": I know_who is in the battle_royal. . . Lady: Really? Who then? (The woman finds a piece of paper as she responds, seemingly ready to check his answers.)
"Machine": ME! So long as I am in the ring, there can be no other! I am the future of the G-pro! QUIT LAUGHING!
(Looking around at the cackling mass of professional reporters the "Kobe
Wrecking Machine"s anger knows no bounds. He glares for a moment, causing the laughter to escalate in volume. He then tosses the podium to one side, and stalks off. Before he is off camera the horrid sound of destruction that introduced him pipes up again, and he clutches at his ears. The people laugh even harder as it all fades to black. . .)
