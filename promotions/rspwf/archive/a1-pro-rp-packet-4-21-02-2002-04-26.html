[A1 PRO] RP Packet, 4.21.02

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A1 PRO Roleplay Packet for April 21st, 2002 ***** [1] Jimmy Taylor [2] Yoshikazu TENZAN [3] Pak ***** [The camera fades into a room at a Claifornia motel. It's nothing too fancy, but it has all the basics. The room is a mess. Clothes, and various other things are strewn across the floor. As the camera pans, we see the owner of these things. That person is Jimmy
Taylor. For a wrestler, Jimmy is small to say the least. He is only 5'11" and, only tops the scales at 195 LBS.
He's also pretty young, only 19. He's wearing a Pair of Khaki Cargo Shorts, a Navy Blue T-Shirt, and some
Sandals. From the way he looks, you're probably asking yourself, "he's a wrestler?"] JT: This is pretty cool... [He smiles.] I sign my first contract, and the suits decide to put me up in a motel. It's nothing too great, but it'll do for now. When the summer nears it close, they'll be puttin' me up in the best hotel they can find.
First thing first, I gotta prove myself. I need to show that I'm ready. That's fine. I'm lookin' for my big break, and I think A1 PRO is going to be the place. Before the champagne starts poppin', I've gotta take care of a few things. First one up is
Anthony Virtue, the "Pure Athlete." [He pauses.] Shouldn't be too much of a problem. After him, I'll win the next few rounds in the Tourney. And before you know it, I'll be King of the Coast. I can't be too confident. It_is_my debut. I've still got to get by Anthony Virtue. But, let's face it. Virtue is old and slow. I'm ready to skyrocket to the top of this company, and the top of this sport. Virtue is on his way to a crash landing.
It kinda puts things into perspective. Here I am, beginning my career. While Anthony Virtue is just about ready to ride into the sunset. And, I'll be one to give him the extra nudge. Face it Anthony, you're old school. I, on the other hand, am riding in on a new wave. I'm ushering in a new era...
[He runs a hand through his blonde hair and continues.] You're world is going to come crashing down real fast, Anthony. There isn't a thing you can do about it. Just sit back, relax and enjoy the show. It'll be over before you know it. Everything that you've got won't be enough, Anthony. In the end, you're gonna end up falling short.
When you think about it, it's not a difficult thing to understand. I win... you lose... That's pretty clear cut. I'm not losing my A1 PRO debut. Hell, this is my debut, period. I'm not gonna lose that either.
And, Anthony Virtue thinks he can beat me? Not in my lifetime, boys... Not in my lifetime...
[He begins to chuckle.] The fun is just beginning... There is a helluva lot more to come. You can count on that. It all starts with you Anthony. The first rung on my ladder to greatness. A climb that I'm ready to make. I'm ready to tear this place up. Watch out, Virtue, 'cuz I'm ready to light you up. Now, there's only one thing left to do. That's to show up in the A1 PRO Arena, and prove I've got what it takes. I've gotta prove that I can win. All I need to do is put Virtue down for the pin... And, I_can't_wait to do that...
Good luck, Anthony... [He pauses.] You're gonna need it... [With that, we fade out.] ***** [The camera opens to a blank screen.] "The time is almost here." [Even with those words, the black picture still consumes the entire screen.] "A1 Pro. A revival. A new beginning." [The garbled Japanese voice pauses.] "No more mistakes. No more confusion. This time around..." [Slowly the picture begins to brighten.] "...you pray for the best." [A pair of slanted charcoal black eyes. Angry and deep, they gaze into the camera.] "No more bad blood. No more hatred. You swore this to yourself, A1 Pro. You swore that this time it would be different."
[The camera's focus remains solely on the intense set of eyes. Filling up the entire screen, they begin to swell with hatred.]
"You lied, motherfucker's. You lied to the fans, to the wrestlers, to the employees." [The camera slowly begins to pull backward.] "You lied to their face. And you know what? I'm proud of you." [And with that, a complete face is revealed. His black hair is tangled and twisted to form a head of shoulder length dreadlocks. Black lipstick paints his lips and a black liquid eyeliner outlines his right eye. A nasty scar bisects his right eyebrow as well as in the middle of his eyebrows. This face, unknown to the world, glows with animosity.]
"Tenzan Yoshikazu desu yo. A1's most recent acquiring." [Concentrating on the head shot, the camera focus' a bit more. As he slowly nods in agreement, his dreadlocks swing in conjunction with his head.]
YT: "When I signed that application you knew you were asking for trouble. You knew you had brought A1 back to step one. Back to what corrupted it in the beginning."
[The camera begins to pull away even more.] YT: "You just couldn't resist the temptation, could you? Bringing me into A1 would be your proudest moments. The dark horse that would cut through this federation like wind, surely filling the stands with paying customers."
[A full body shot now reveals the entirety of the kid. His lightly tanned skin is marked in tattoo's along both his arms. A black nail polish color each one of his fingernail's. He wears a black and orange "danger to society" t-shirt and a black pair of vinyl wrestling pants. His small frame is slightly muscularly toned, but nothing ridiculous. As he continues to speak, the camera remains steady on his image.]
YT: "Well, you know what? I won't disappoint you. If you want to sacrifice your competition to fill a few empty seats, be my guest. I'd be more than happy to destroy this place..."
[His lips widen with...dare I say...happiness.] YT: "...for a second time." [The rabid Yoshikazu TENZAN continues to shine with glee. His dreadlocks drape over his face and before brushing them away he wags a middle finger at the camera, bidding it a farewell. He pushes back his curtain of hair and begins to quietly laugh to himself. Fade out.]
***** [As the scene opens up, we see a solemn figure sitting in an empty room, all alone. Sitting in a common brown, steel folding chair, he has his head resting in his palms, which, in turn, are resting on his knees.]
[In the background, P.O.D.'s "Thinking About Forever" is playing in the background, and, as it looks, that's exactly what this man is doing.]
#Time Goes By...# [Well, you know the lyrics. Or at least you should.] [He's a pretty average looking guy, brown hair in a crew cut, average body tone, and lightly tanned skin. On his left wrist is a semi-faded tattoo of a dark, black sword. He's wearing a pair of denim shorts and a white,
Jersey Devil's jersey. This is Pak, the Jersey Devil.] [This man's face is curled as if there's something his thinking about very hard, but what it is, is just out of reach. Or maybe, something's wrong.]
[He sits there, this perpetual look of perplexment on his face. Moments pass, and the song cuts out.]
[Pak] Man, I hate that song. [Suddenly, the lights dim a little. There is some sounds in the background, probably talking. Suddenly, a male voice speaks, but it sounds a little muffled.]
[Man] Ah, that isn't your line? [Pak] I don't care. [Pak stands, and knocks the chair over behind him.] [Pak] Why are you people trying to make me look like a freakin' idiot? [Pak begins to walk forward, showing that this is really just a backdrop. That's Hollywood for you...] [Camera Man] Hey, we've got a contract. [Pak] Shove it. [Camera Man] You can't tell me that, we... [Pak] Yeah, yeah contract. You want to film a promo? Follow me then... [Pak leads the camera man outside, to the hallway. Pak takes a look to the left... and them to the right. Pak goes to the right.]
[Camera Man] Where are we going? [Pak] Quiet. Pak turns around and grabs the camera by the lens, and shoves it down, so all we see are a pair of shoes. Everyone give thanks for microphones.]
[Camera Man] What's your deal? [Pak] My deal? That's the thing. My deal was that I'd bring my own brand of entertainment to A1. So, just follow me, and well get this done.
[The camera is brought back up to eye level, and we see the back of Pak's head as he heads down the hallway. After walking a few feet, we come up to a pair of crew men. They seem a little surprised about Pak and the cameraman.]
[Crew Man 1] Hey Bill, who's your buddy? [Camera Man] He's... [Pak] Hey, let me talk for myself... [Pak squeezes himself in-between the two men, putting an arm around each man
's shoulders.]
[Pak] You two are going to be my first two examples... [These two, with their history with working with wrestlers, don't like the sound of this and try to get away. Pak holds tight, and while one gets away, Pak still has one "example."]
[Pak] Ok camera boy, hit it... [Pak takes a moment, and straightens up, still holding on to the crew man. While saying his next two words, he gives one of the cheesiest smiles ever.] [Pak] Hi there. [Pak] I'm standing here with one of my Pak-Men, and I just want to ask him one thing. What does he think of my opponent, "the Experiment" Shannon
Roth? [Crew Man] Well... [Pak] Is he the man? [Crew Man] Maybe... [Pak] What's his name? [Crew Man] Shan... [Pak] Where's he from? [Crew Man] I dun... [Pak] What's he gonna do? [Crew Man] How should... [Pak] Now, what "my" name? [Crew Man] Pak? [Pak] Where'm I from? [Crew Man] Ahh... [Pak whispers something into the crew man's ear.] [Crew Man] Jersey? [Pak] And what am I gonna do? [Crew Man] Why... [Pak] Sorry buddy, you've gotta wait and find out like everyone else... [Pak now gives the crew man a "gentle" shove.] [Pak] We're done. [Now Pak turns his attention to the camera man, and smiles.] [Pak] We done? [The camera nods ups and down.] [Pak] Good. Let's go get a Pepsi.... [Fin.] *****
