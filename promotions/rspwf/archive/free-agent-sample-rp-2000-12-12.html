FREE AGENT SAMPLE RP

12 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys I posted a few days ago saying I was looking for a new fed. A sample of my work is below, I don't claim to be great like everyone else does but I do claim to be ok. I'm just looking for a FUN league to participate in and am stil looking for the right league. I'll list what I am looking for in a league then
I'll insert my roleplay. Thanks! 1. No Hardcore Leagues, again NO HARDCORE LEAGUES. 2. E-Mail based, no rp board sites or leagues. 3. Only orginal wrestlers allowed. 4. Some kind of limit on rp'ing, like one or two rp's per match. (I do have a life outside of the compueter.) 5. At least one card a week that is on time! (Also would like to kind of be guaranteed at least one match a week. If I don't get booked in matches there really isn't much to rp about.)
--------------------------------------- Sample Roleplay ---------------------------------------- CS Christopher Scarlet "The Goldenboy" (The scene opens to a van pulling up in front of a mansion, the van has satelite equipment mounted on it's roof. The van comes to a stop and a reporter gets out along with a camera man carrying a portable camera. The reporter and the camera man make their way up to the huge double doors of the house and are greeted by a butler who shows them into the main sitting room. The reporter takes a seat on the leather couch opposite of a huge red leather chair with an ottoman sitting in front of it while the camera man positions himself for the perfect camera angle. Finally the man they came to see, Christopher Scarlet, makes his way into the room and shakes the hand of the reporter. Scarlet appears to be looking in very good shape and has his long blond hair pulled back into a tightly braided pony tail. "The Goldenboy" takes a seat in the big chair putting his feet up and getting comfortable as the reporter motions to the camera man to start rolling the film.)
[Reporter known as The Wrestling Doctor] Chris let me say it's very good seeing you again and I just want to thank you for granting me this historic interview.
["The Goldenboy" Christopher Scarlet] It's great to see you to again Doc and as far as giving you this interview, I couldn't imagine anyone else doing it.
[Wrestling Doctor] Chris it's been a couple of years since we have seen you compete in active competition and really focus all your efforts on your wrestling career. Why did you leave the sport and what have you been doing?
[Christopher Scarlet] Well, when I was in the XBWL as you know I did capture the World title which was the greatest accomplishment in my whole career. However, I felt I had to lose that title quickly because people felt that I only had that title because of my association with XBWL president Johnny Macaroni. I earned that belt but as long as I was associated with the president no one was going to give me the credit I deserved as champion. So after I gave up the title I left the XBWL and wrestling all together because I wanted to take a break and just get the rest of my private life together before returning to the ring. In my time away from wrestling I have focused on my families race horse farm here in Lexington as we have been breeding some great thoroughbreds that should one day compete in the
Kentucky Derby. It's amazing the millions of dollars that people will spend to own and breed those magnificant animals. As much as I love the horse racing business my first love has always been professional wrestling and now I'm ready to return full time to the ring.
[Wrestling Doctor] Well it will definately be good to see you back in action. What kind of physical shape are you in and do you think you will have a lot of ring rust?
[Christopher Scarlet] Ring rust? Well, I'm sure there will be a little ring rust but I have actually competed a little bit lately in a small league called HGW. It was more of a hardcore league which doesn't suit my wsrestling style or preference. I mean as you know I am a technical wrestler and I do much better in a more competitive situation as far as technical wrestling goes. I have never been known as someone who is a great hardcore wrestler, in fact I'm much too good looking to take a chance on scarring this body. (Chris chuckles a little.) I competed in the HGW for a few months trying to work as much rust off as I could in a small federation such as theirs but now I'm ready to get back to work and as the old saying goes, get off the porch and run with the big dogs. Now as for my physical shape, all I can say is that I am in the best shape of my life. I'm 6 feet 1 inch in height, 223 pounds, and at 27 years of age I am just now reaching the prime of my wrestling career. I literally just about jump out of bed in the morning to begin my raining routine because I am so excited at the opportunities awaiting me out there in various federations.
[Wrestling Doctor] You say various federations. Is it safe to say that as of now you have yet to sign a contract with any one company?
[Christopher Scarlet] I have not signed any contract yet but I am looking over several interesting proposals. I do however think that I will be deciding on something very soon.
[Wrestling Doctor] How many different leagues are in the running and can you give us some names so we will know which leagues have a shot?
[Christopher Scarlet] Well there are really only two leagues that legitimately have a shot at signing me at this time, they are Malacian Championship Wrestling and Epic Wrestling
Alliance which are both newer leagues looking to make a name for themselves. However you know how this sport works and there could be several leagues that make a push to sign me in the next few days and that could change everything.
There are lots of great leagues out there and you don't hear a lot about them because they don't advertise much. Those federations are the types of leagues that I generally enjoy competing in because they are more catered to the sport itself.
[Wrestling Doctor] Chris, I have heard that Malacian Championship Wrestling could be the next big thing. They are restarting their league and they have signed some heavy hitters as far as well known talent. Wouldn't it be in your best interest to go to a league that has such high hopes and higher level talent? I mean after all, it would help you gain some recognition and draw attention to your talent if you were to step into the ring against some more well know talent.
[Christopher Scarlet] Well Doc let me just say this about all that. Recognition is a great thing, it is what makes a wrestler feel as if he has made it in this sport. Every wrestler strives to be the best and to compete against the best, but at the same time there is a lot to be said for happiness. Malacian Championship
Wrestling is indeed a front runner but they also mentioned to me the big names they have signed and it makes me wonder about the league. Is it a league trying to get over by signing well known talent and then getting other guys such as myself to put over those big names? I will use [name withheld] as an example because if I'm not mistaken he is one of their signee's. I do not personally know Ian and that makes it hard for me to judge anything. I do not know what he is capable of in the ring and I personally do not know what kind of person he is as far as his personality goes. [name withheld] could be one of the nicest guys in the world and he also could be an egomaniac. Like I said I don't personally know him so I can't say for sure. When it comes to a league like MCW I worry that they sign those guys because they are well known and promise them the world, then they have to hire lesser known guys like me and then they have me do the j-o-b to them in order to keep the big name wrestler happy so their league gets good exposure putting the league over. I'm not in this sport for that purpose, I'm in this sport to enjoy myself while at the same time making a name for myself. How can I do either one of those if I have no chance going in of getting a fair shot? I mean think about it, let's say for an example you have [name withheld] and Christopher Scarlet in a World title match taking place in front of the entire wrestling world. [name withheld] is well known, I am the dark horse.
Maybe I'm better in the ring and I put in more time and effort than him, but what kind of champion would I be? Would it be in the league's best interest to put the title on me even though I deserve it, or would it be a better idea to put the belt on [name withheld] who is well known and can get the league more exposure?
See what I mean? These are all things you have to take into consideration when making a tough decision and is why I am leaning towards signing with Epic
Wrestling Alliance. I am keeping my options open though, nothing has been decided just yet.
[Wrestling Doctor] You make a good point. Well Chris, that's all I have for now. Make sure you call me when you make a decision, you know it's good for my career to be given the big stories.
[Christopher Scarlet] No problem Doc, you know you are the man when it comes to interviews. I wouldn't trust anyone else to do it. See ya next time.
(The scene fades to black.)
