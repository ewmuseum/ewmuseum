Chavez RP - Criticism welcome. Tell me how to improve

25 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Basically, my character's a nutty Indian with a bit of a drinking problem. This is not the sort of gimmick that takes itself too seriously. It's worked for me though. I'm rather proud of what I've achieved with this character so far.
Here's one after Chavez wins a match but his scheduled opponent laid down for him. [Scene fades in on a tattoo of a drunken, bald eagle, toting a bottle of Jack
Daniels, on somebody's arm. We ponder the significance of this drunken eagle tattoo and wonder who it belongs to. We then slowly zoom out to reveal that the arm and tattoo belongs to none other than Chavez, the insane, drunken
Indian. He is mounted on a Harley Davidson motorcycle, standing on the side of a desert highway, clad in a leather riding vest, leather gloves, torn jeans, cowboy boots, copious amounts of gold chains on his wrists and neck and dark sunglasses. We get a good view of the seemingly endless highway that stretches out in front of us before we switch back to Chavez on his hog. He guns his motor a couple of times before he ever so slowly turns to the camera and speaks in a low, ominous voice]
"Another Monday Madness has come and gone. Yet, there's this empty feeling I have. Something just isn't right. I know I pinned Ravishing Rip right in the middle of the ring. But I have this overwhelming feeling that he just DIDN'T try hard enough. Does anybody else think so?"
[Chavez glances at the camera as if prompting the viewer for a response] "It's the strangest thing. But...But I'm not going to let that ruin my day. It's just me and my hog, the highway, the sky, earth and the breeze. And of course...GOOD OLD JACK DANIELS!!
[The crazed Indian reaches into his vest pocket and pulls out a flask of whiskey. He guzzles it, letting the booze dribble down his chin in an unabashed show of gluttony. Satisfied, he then stuffs the flask back in his coat pocket and breathes deeply]
"So Rip. Pale face. If you have any balls, you will step in the ring with
Chavez again. And try to make an effort this time. I mean, I wouldn't want anybody to think that you...actually...took...a...dive??
[Chavez looks down at his bike in a moment of contemplation. Then he looks back up at the camera, his lips unfurling in rage]
"SON-UM-A-BITCH!! HE TOOK A DIVE!!" [The insane Indian, suddenly realizing the reality of what happened on Monday, shakes his head in frustration. Beads of angry sweat start forming on his forehead as the veins look like they might pop]
"So that's it huh? You think your going to get away with laying down for
Chavez? Well, I don't think so, amigo. But either way, thanks for the booze!
But it's going to take more than a little moonshine to bribe your way out of the beating that's coming your way. Maybe a LOT of booze. No, wait. No, I'm going to kill ya! But...I'm always up for a drink!”
[The maniacal Native American revves his motor some more and looks down the highway. He brushes his hair out of his face with one gloved hand]
"Now on to more important matters. It seems that Robbie Douglas has been incarcerated. My attempts at setting him free where [pauses] less than successful. Not only that but I've been banned from Al Coholic's for exhausting their supply of Sidewinder 69 [TM]. So Brev, buddy. If you want to find me, just send me a smoke signal. Maybe we can bust some heads in the future. Until then... [Chavez starts making engine noises and humming the first few lines of "Born to be Wild"]
"Boooooorn to be Wiiiiiiild!!" [Chavez makes more engine noises before he finally kicks his motor into action] "BOOOORN TO BE...[pauses] WIIIIIIIIILLLLLLLLLYYYYYYY-DDDDDD!! [The crazed Indian then speeds off down the highway, leaving a cloud of dust and engine fumes in his wake. We fade out to Chavez driving off into the New
Mexico sunset, screaming out the chorus of "Born to be Wild" and with his free hand, waving a bottle of Jack Daniels in the air]
Here's another one that's longer and I put some more thought into it. Har Har
[Fade in on Chavez, the insane, drunken Indian, sitting at his favorite watering hole, the notorious Thirsty Injun Bar and Grill in Parts Unknown, watching the ARMAGEDDON Channel and drinking Jack Daniels straight from a bottle. We get a closer look at the mad Native-American and we see that he is wearing his new T-shirt today. It’s a black, sleeveless T-shirt that has reads I’m not crazy… on the front and I’M COMPLETELY! @#$ INSANE!!! on the back. He also is wearing torn and faded jeans, a pair of cowboy boots and a navy-blue bandanna on his head. The crazed Indian slowly turns his head in the general direction of the camera. He looks particularly wasted tonight.
Perhaps he’s celebrating a successful title defense in another fed. Or maybe he's just having a good day. The mad Indian has himself a good swig of whiskey then sets the bottle down. He looks up at the small TV set that’s perched on top of the bar and lo and behold, there’s an interview by the
Atomic Dog. Chavez has a stern look on his face as he watches the entire interview before they que a commercial for Viagra™ featuring Bob Dole]
Chavez: Atomic Dog. Let's see what do we know I know about you. Your good, you might be undefeated and you have quite a squaw over there as well.
However it goes down on Monday, the people are going to see one hell of a match. Looks like the suits finally decided to give the people what they want to see.
But there's two things you should know about the man who is going to be standing across the ring from you...
[Chavez turns to the camera and glares into it with a look of malicious intent] Chavez: I'm not exactly what you would call a...stable individual. So you best not get on my bad side or the consquences could be...Could be rather...
[The maniacal Indian clenches his fists together for a brief moment, then regains his composure]
Chavez: Damn those steel plates in my head! And for your sake, I hope you won't be yelling "I'm in da House" or anything about being "Bowdy, Bowdy" or
"Rowdy, Rowdy" while we are having our match. If you do, I wouldn't want to be you, brother. I seriously wouldn't!
[Chavez shakes his head then looks up at the TV again and there’s a promo for the next ARMAGEDDON show. The main event features Brev Jazed vs. Morrison]
Chavez: Dog, I'll get to the second thing in a minute. Now, let me point out that there's a man with a good work ethic. Superstar? No. Jazed. He might be a puny weakling but he's verging on the brink of insanity. He's a loose cannon just about to be fired. And that's the edge that lights the flame that has the competition running like hyenas from crazed herd of water buffalo's.
Now, back to the second thing Atomic Poochie needs to know about Chavez. I don't like to be told what to do. Your telling me to show up for my match all clean and sober?
Well, we'll just have to....SEE ABOUT THAT!! [The deranged Indian takes a huge swig of Jack Daniels then slams the bottle down on the table]
Chavez: Bartender! Bring me the a shot of SIDEWINDER 69™ [We zoom in on the bartender’s face as Chavez mentions THE SIDEWINDER 69™ He looks shocked]
B: You want… THE SIDEWINDER 69™? [Chavez glares at the bartender impatiently. He slams his fists down on the table] C: YES GODDAMNIT! B: But…But that is like five hundred percent proof! You’ll be drunk for a week! [Somebody off-camera] He isn’t really gonna drink that! IS HE? [The insane, and severely intoxicated Indian reaches over the bar and grabs a bottle of SIDEWINDER 69™ and pours himself a rather generous drink. He takes the potent drink down with one swallow]
C: Wooh! That’s much better. Now what was I talking…About just a minute ago? I’m sh-upposed to be rasslin’ some guy on Monday. What was his !@#$% name? [The bartender tries to silently put the bottle of SIDEWINDER 69™ back on the shelf but the drunken Indian grabs it from his hand. He has himself another dangerously potent sip]
C: Lassie? Bingo? Atomic Bowzer? What was-sh his name?! Atomic Poochie! Yeah, that's it. And after I'm done laying it into this mongrel, I might have some fun with that squaw of his. Hey, Destiny, after I'm done whoopin' your Dog, feel free to drop by my dressing room and play with my tom-tom's!
[The drunken Indian winks at the camera. He seems to be swaying from side to side. He has himself another swig of sidewinder for good measure]
"Cuz I'm not Bowdy, Bowdy and I sure as !@#$% @#$%^ ain't Rowdy, Rowdy! But
I'm de-fin-it-ely...drunk. Now I'm going to go home and watch lotsa video tapes of Atomic Poochie wrestling then I'm going down to the gym to get really pumped up for five hours a day!"
[We zoom out gratuitously and we can see Chavez is now standing on the bar
One of the people sitting up at the bar leans over to his partner and whispers...]
Person: He’s gonna go home and finish off that bottle of sidewinder! [Chavez jumps off the bar and lands on all fours down on the bar floor. He is careful not to spill a drop of SIDEWINDER 69™ as he picks himself up and sprints out the door]
[Voice off-camera] Chavez, where are you going? [Chavez turns around for a brief moment] C: I’M GOING TO @#$%!& DISNEY LAND!! [We fade out to the insane, drunken Chavez running out the door with the bottle of SIDEWINDER 69™ clasped in his hands]
