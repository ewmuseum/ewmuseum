Need: Roleplay Critique *Justin Tyme*

15 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scene - Justin is seen pacing back and forth outside of the CEO's office.. he looks really nervous, and is hesitating. A Camera Crew approaches him, and he stops and looks at the camera, and then the camera zooms in on the CEO's office door.
Justin - Im guessing you would like to know, what I am doing here, well, I think it is pretty obvious.. I want to get out of this match.. there's alot more ridding on this then what Butch thinks, it's not just the possible destruction of the Leap of Faith. What Butch doesn't realize is.. well.. I have a perfect record.. and I want to maintain that record.. I don't want to the title... but I want to keep my record, that is really all that is important to me.. One thing is for sure though, Morbid Wolf will not remain the Television
Champion for long, oh no.. thats a definite.. Morbid Wolf has exactly Eleven
Days, Two-Hundred and Sixty-Four Hours, Fifteen Thousand Eight Hundred Forty
Minutes, Nine Hundred Fifty Thousand Four Hundred Seconds left until you lose that title.. Time is ticking, and your clock is running out fast, you see you have had your time to shine, your fame and glory, now it is time for Leap of
Faith to step into the light and not only take that TV Title away from you, but eventually the stable titles as well.. you see.. you can run to your little crew members back there, we don't care, we will take anyone you can bring forward, Sabbertooth, the MeW Champion, we can take him, hell I can take him..
Scorpion Kid, exactly what he calls himself, a kid, Night Hawk, beat his ass before, beat his ass again.. Nightmare.. when we kick his ass, it will be a nightmare, and you Wolf.. we will hunt you down like the cheap son-of-a-bitch you really are. You see you can bring anyone, and everyone, forward to challenge us, because that is all we crave, thats all we desire...... a challenge, and you my friend.. are not a challenge, no.. in fact, your are just the opposite of that.. you are easy money.. you are like the whores down on
Orange Blossom Trail, back in my hometown of Orlando, Florida.. EASY! Why don't you just back off? I mean.. I know, you know, we all know.. I have kicked your ass before.. and I can and will do it again, and again, and again.. until you reconize that I am what you would like to become... I am the King of Extreme..
remember that.. it means I will take you any day, any time, any where, and I will beat your ass to a bloody pulp.. The next morning you will take up in the local hospital.. laying in bed.. with a bodycast on.. and you will look out of the little slit of cast that was removed right in front of your eyes.. you will sweat with nervousness.. you will call the nurse over.. and since you can't move, you won't be able to see that she is wearing a Justin Tyme, Leap of Faith shirt.. and you will ask her, where the hell am i? and the response will not be like the response in that Disney Commercial.. oh no.. the response will be...
(mimicking a womens voice) sir.. hello sir.. yes.. you are in the Blah Blah
Blah hospital in Blah Blah Blah, you are here because you suffered major bodily injuries last night when you got beat my Justin Tyme.. I mean.. when you fought with Justin Tyme.. (ends mimicking) and that is exactly how it will go. You see
Wolf.. I will go back to my street fighting style.. and I will kick your ass there.. and it will hurt alot more.. because I am a dangerous person.. I fought almost daily in school, I had to fight off gang members, thugs, and everyone of that sort.. so I am not a man you want to mess with.. you don't know me...
you don't know what I know, you haven't seen what I have seen, you don't know what I have experienced.. All I can say right now is.. Game on!
Butch... brother Butch.. well.. I have't known you long... not long at all.. but I have been very pleased with what I have seen from you... you are a good wrestler, and a great friend.. so far.. but a friendship can only go so far..
and at Game Day.. to me.. it isn't about the title, it isn't about kickin' Morbid Wolf's ass... not really... but to me.. it is a test.. a test of our friendship.. can we withstand fighting against eachother? we'll have to see...
who is the strongest person in the Leap of Faith.. another test... you see
Butch.. I don't want to beat you, I don't want to hurt you.. I simply want to win, that's all that matters to me now.. whether or not I hold the title.. I don't know.. but you can bet that I will not hold back against you, whatever
Morbid Wolf is gonna get.. you can expect to get that too.. I will offer you a challenge you cannot and will not refuse.. all I ask for is the same.. I wanted my first Supercard match-up here in the MeW.. to be something special..
something I will always remember.. I didn't want to face that coward Morbid
Wolf again.. he is doing absollutely nothing but bringing me down.. lower and lower into the ranks.. but finally someone comes along.. who has the guts to not only step up and get in Morbid Wolf's face.. but also to be able to step up and get into mine.. not a single man has done that here in the MeW.. and you will be the first, and I am guessing you won't be the last.. but you will be the first example.. of what happens when you mess with me.. what the result is when you mess with me.. I will walk out of the Pepsi Arena.. the television champion.. the only thing I would like to know.. will the Leap of Faith lose a member? or will we be stronger than ever .. that is the decision I leave you with.. it's all up to you.. know that once you are out.. you cannot get back in without proving yourself.. proving yourself more than you had before.. and that will not be easy.. good luck at game day
Well well well........ it seems as if Flying Tyme finally gets to go head on for the tag titles against who? none other than 'Stunning' Steve Anderson, and that pussy-wimp suck-up.. Odin.. Odin.. god I hate that name.. supposively the runner-up in the MeW tournament.. how did you slip by me? you are nothing but a pathetic ass hole to me.. and I will prove just how pathetic you really are..
you think you can take on Flying Tyme single handidly... let alone me? That's is false.. like your mom.. you couldn't take out anyone in this federation single handidly.. you may be an anderson, possibly a relative of the living wrestling legend.. Arn Anderson... but that has nothing to do with you, and what I am going to do to you at Game Day..Yeah.. I knocked your brother out so what? He was getting in my way? you wanna do something about it? you wanna actually go out Sunday and try to kick some fucking sense into me? Well you know.. I could use some of that.. sense.. I need some... you see I look at everyone here in the MeW the same.. pathetic Jocks trying to make a living..
not me though.. I love kicking ass.. I have to kick an ass weekly to keep my sanity.. and you know.. its more than a week until Sunday.. and we have no card until after Game Day.. so my sanity may as well be gone.. All I can say is that you better be prepared Anderson, Odin, Butch, and Morbid Wolf.. because I will let go on all of you on Sunday.. you can bet your asses, that this dog will be off the chain.. and loose to rome over your asses.. all i have now to say..
is.... G A M E D A Y!! [Scene Fades out]
