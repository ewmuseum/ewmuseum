ICE Looking for Talent, 1997

17 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi Guys ICE is looking for quality roleplayers to bolster it's ranks. Below you will find the latest Players Guide, and Character Sheet, and on RSPWF, you will fins some of the recent cards. Please enclose a sample interview, whether it be for the wrestler(S) you are submitting or not. Thanks Later [name withheld] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-= Independent Championship E-Wrestling Players Intro and Guide V 1.5 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-= =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-= Independent Championship E-Wrestling History =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-= ICE was formed in May of 1996 by current President [name withheld]. ICE was to immedietly attract some top singles and tag team wrestlers to bolster the creation of [name withheld]. Such well known e-wrestling stars like Robski and Sabin Rene Figaro were soon to sign contracts with ICE. With the arrival of Bone Lord and The Mighty Jack Ov, ICE was to see an ongoing feud between Robski and the so called Kaos, Karnage and Killing; the KKK. This feud culminated in libel suits being fired at Robski by Bone Lord's manager Missy Voodoo concerning an aleged pregnency. When a local organisation, the MWL folded, ICE's ranks were to be bolstered further by the arrival of the Fraternity of the Stone, and From Canada with Cod. The Cod's joined the Southern Man and Robski in the Commonwealth Companions, and ICE was to see it's biggest feud as the Companions, Fraternity, and KKK feuded between each other. Robski's wrestling in ICE was to be limited during the latter part of July and August, following a bitter feud between himself, a dedicated Birmingham City supporter, and White Yorke, a fan of soccer rivals Aston Villa. Robski was eventually cleared of all charges related to an arson attack at Villa's home, Villa Park. August was to herald the aparant end of ICE. Just prior to Below Freezing, ICE's biggest PPV spectacular, and the venue of a mammoth Tri Stable War Games match involving the Fraternity of the Stone, the KKK, and the Commonwealth Companions, ICE was to go under due to finacial irregularities. President [name withheld] denied any involvement, but ICE was closed down. October 1996. [name withheld] resurfaced along with a new business partner, a lady known only as Shaz. ICE was to come with them. This was a new age in ICE history. Many old stars were to return, but many new ones were to arrive. The Armed Forces, former Tag Team World Champions elsewhere, and a new tag team sensation in Better off Dead. The singles roster blosomed once more. Things look good for ICE. January 1997, and ICE entered it's newest era. With proffits sky rocketing, ICE President [name withheld], cotacted head of Roadkill Productions, [name withheld], with an offer to buy the parent company of Electronic Roadkill Wrestling Inc. The deal went through, and ERWI was merged into ICE. [name withheld] took a place on the Board of Directors, and a new age blossomed. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-= ICE Cards and Shows =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-= ICE currently holds 3 cards a week. Each card is handled by a different person, and all contains live commentary. The cards are: ICE Monday Night Carnage (Handled by [name withheld] [ [detail withheld]@kindred.demon.co.uk ]) Roakill on ICE [Tuesday] (Handled by [name withheld] [ [detail withheld]@aol.com ] and [name withheld] [ [detail withheld]@kindred.demon.co.uk ]) ICE Thursday Night Mayhem (Handled by [name withheld] [ [detail withheld]@kindred.demon.co.uk and [detail withheld]@hotmail.com ]) Strat's, interviews, and any other important things for these cards needs to be sent to the respective person. ICE also produces two weekly magazine shows. These air on Wednesdays, and Sundays, and are known as ICE Cold. These are a mix of News, Rumours and Flashes. If you want to submit a flash for this to the respective writer. Those are: ICE Cold (Wednesday) ([name withheld] [ [detail withheld]@kindred.demon.co.uk and [detail withheld]@hotmail.com ]) ICE Cold (Sunday) ([name withheld] [ [detail withheld]@freenet.mb.ca ]) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-= ICE Role-playing =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-= The most important part of this fed is going to be the role-playing of it's members. ICE works on an Open Flash System. This means basicly that, each handler will mail his flashes to everyone in the fed themselves. Some people will like this, some wont, but this is the way we have decided to go. ICE also runs magazine shows twice weekly. Flashes are welcomed for this, and need to be sent to the respective person responsible for the show. [See above.] Flashes, and Interviews are also welcomed for cards. To make sure that your interview gets in, send it to the Card Writer at least a day prior to the card. As this is a booked fed, many people will think that it is the quantity of flashes that they write that will win them matches. That's not the case. Someone who writes one or two good length flashes, where his character develops or increases the amount of heat in an angle is going to do better than someone who sends in 10 2 line repetitive flashes. Like : I'm going to kick wrestler B's butt and if wrestler C gets in the way I'll get you as well. Swearing is also allowed, but keep this to a minimum. The one big rule on roleplaying is this... You must roleplay at least once per week. If there is a good reason why this won't happen, then just let [name withheld] know, and he'll understand. Everyone wants to keep ICE interesting, but to do that, everyone needs to be involved. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-= ICE Matches and Strategies =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-= To gain 'live' matches, you need to stay involved and at the forefront of what is going on in ICE. These matches are the ones the fans would want to see. Matches for the cards are set up based on what challenges you are making in your interviews/flashes and other matchups which we feel will go down well. Dark matches follow much the same lines, but these are more oftenly booked by the Fed Head and Staff. If you specifically want a dark match on a card, just give [name withheld] a shout. For matches, I highly encourage you to send in any strategies. If you want your guy to beat on his opponent on the outside for most of the match and it doesn't happen because you didn't tell me, don't blame me. If you want to jump a guy on his way to the ring, let me know. Sneak attacks are allowed, but let's try and keep them to a minimum. There are other way's of building heat. Again, I need any of this at least a day before the card, preferably two. If it's not in on time, I'll hold it back, and add it to the next show. Strats need to be mailed to the Shows Writer at least a day prior to the card coming out. E.G. Wednesday for Mayhem. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-= ICE Titles =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-= Belts. We have titles here as well! We currently sanction three singles titles and one tag team title. All ICE titles can only be won by pinfall or Submission unless specified. (See Note below for an exception to this.) The singles titles are:- ICE World Title (Universal Weight. Must be defended only at PPV's. Other matches can be Title Matches however.) ICE Mayhem Title (Universal Weight. Must be defended once a fortnight, and at every PPV. Other matches can also be title matches as well.) ICE Unified Title (Former ICE/ERWI Inter Federational Title. Universal Weight. Must be defended twice per fortnight, and at every PPV. Other matches can also be title matches.) ICE Independent Title (299lbs and under. Must be defended at one card per week. Other matches may also be title matches. May change hands on dq and Count Out decisions as well.) ICE Regional Title (299lbs and under. Must be defended once per week at any card. Other matches may also be title matches.) And for Tag Teams we have: ICE World Tag Team Titles [Note: If a wrestler or team manage to beat the champion(s) on three separate occasions by dq or countout during one title reign, they win the belts.] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-= ICE Stables =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-= Stables and Alliances are welcomed, but again, you've got to tell me otherwise I won't know to write them into a card. We wouldn't want your mystery stable member running into make the save when you jump someone else now would we! ? =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-= ICE Rankings =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-= ICE works on a points system for rankings. When a wrestler first enters ICE he is credited with 100 points. He then gains and loses points depending on how he fares in his fights. The points gains, and losses are as follows:- +50 for a win by Pinfall or Submission +35 for a win by Dq or Count Out +20 for a draw -35 for a loss by dq or Count Out -50 for a loss by Pinfall or Submission +75 for winning the World Title/World Tag Team Titles +60 for winning the Mayhem Title +50 for winning the Unified Title +40 for winning the Indepedent Title +30 for winning the Regional Title =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-= People and Places =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-= The following are the fictional ICE officials and places in the ICE Universe. We are a booked E-Fed, who reward good participation from it's players. Currently we hold two 'Live' cards a week, and one 'Arena' card. To be a success in ICE, you will need to stay involved. Flashes are very important in this, but they are not the only thing that will gain you success here in ICE. Strategies for any of your matches are recommended, as are any ideas for angles. Get in touch with the handler of the wrestler with whom you are feuding, and work out the angle with them. But always keep me informed of what you are doing. You don't want to have a great angle you have been working on ruined before it has started because you didn't tell me about it..... Because participation is so very important in ICE, when working on your wrestler, make sure he is someone that you are going to be able to role- play. There's no point in designing a great character, only to find out that you can't take him anywhere. Bear in mind, that we want your character to develop as much as possible when you play him. We want to expand on their lives outside of the ring. You are alowed a maximum of 2 Singles Wrestlers and 1 Tag Team.
