(The Knick Man) Sample RP - Comments would be appreciated.

1 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here's a roleplay for my main character, The Knick Man. He placed #74 on the
RSPWF Top 200 in my first year of e-wrestling while wrestling in the ASW. He can play both the face or the heel, but this roleplay is a heel one. He currently wrestles for the AICW as a face, and the ICWA and WWO as heels. He's also now the commissioner of his first league, the ASW, and he's playing the heel in that league. This is the roleplay that got me into the finals in the
ICWA World Title tournament. I would appreciate any comments, but if you
e-mail me, please direct all comments to [detail withheld] Thank you. ------------------------------------------------------------ (The scene opens up to a black background with a single image of the flag of the country of Poland. The Polish National Anthem is playing in the background as we gaze upon the image of the flag. Suddenly, without warning a huge basketball bounces on the screen as we watch a large image of a foot wearing a
Knick Man Championship Sneaker come crashing down onto the flag. We hear a scretching sound as the anthem comes to a complete halt. The anthem is then replaced with the sounds of "Go NY Go NY Go" as the camera widens its image starting
from the foot. As the camera continues to expand the image, we can see the foot is attached to a huge video image of The Knick Man dribbling a basketball on a basketball court. His only defender between the basket is a small stick figure with the words "Mike Wick" above his head and an arrow pointing from the name to the stick man. The Knick Man image is about twenty times large than the size of the Mike Wick image. The Wick image lifts his hands up as if he were trying to play defense on The Knick Man, but The Knick Man image just steps on him and crushes him like a bug. The Knick Man image then leaps up into the air and does a thunderous slam dunk as the scene explodes.)
(A new scene opens up, this time in a huge room. Inside we see many shelves of books. There is also a large television with a VCR attached to it and we can see the real Knick Man inside of the room. He is sitting at a table watching footage of the match between "Punisher" Mike Wick and Juicy Fruit. The Knick
Man is
dressed in a black tee shirt which says "His Royal Airness" on the front. The
Knick Man looks up and smiles that classic Knick Man Championship smile at the camera.)
Knick Man: One down! Two more to go! In my ICWA debut, not only do I wrestle in THE MAIN EVENT, but I completely humiliate this so called Preacher and had that boy dropping like a heathen worshipping an idol! You see, you ICWA fans may have thought that his knee was hurt. However, I, The Mighty Knick Man, your Lord and Master, and of course, your first ever ICWA World Heavyweight
Champion, will reveal to you a NEW REVELATION! The reason why The Preacher spent so much time on the mat was becase HE WAS WORSHIPPING THE KNICK
MAN!!!!!!!! Hey Preacher, now that I have CRUCIFIED your chances of ever winning a World Title, will you now worship THE TRUE GOD of this sport?
(The Knick Man turns around to reveal the back of his tee shirt. On the back is an image of The Knick Man flying in the air, while ICWA superstars such as
The Preacher, Adam Mazur, Axon Blade, and Mike Wick watch from the ground. At the top of the tee shirt are the words "Air" Knick Man)
Knick Man: AIR KNICK MAN!!!!!!!!!! I'm flying through this World Title tournament baby!!!!
(The Knick Man laughs, then turns his attention towards the Television.) Knick Man: Now that I have converted The Preacher, it's time that I teach another sinner in this federation the TRUTH of my power and ability. This young lad goes by the name of "Punisher" Mike Wick! You see, Mikey here seems to have a little "problem" with the punctuation of my name! He seems to pronounce my name "Knicks Man", in the plural for all you English majors out there! Which is
perfectly understandable. I mean, when I step into the ring, as I'm sure The
Preacher can TESTIFY to, it's like wrestling against MORE THAN ONE MAN!!!!!
Maybe the ICWA booked me for the wrong tournament. Maybe they meant to put me in the ICWA World Tag Team Title Tournament! I mean, if I really wanted to, I could win the tag team titles by myself! When your best tag team is a freaking pretty boy who can't keep his eyes off his own self and a 7 foot 450 pound
RETARD, how hard could it be for The Knick Man to beat the ICWA tag teams in a tournament to capture the belts! But anyway, "Punisher" being that I'm a nice guy and all, I have decided to bring my good friend, Dr Lawrence Chan, a national leader in the study of phonetics, to teach you how to pronounce my name! Come on out here Dr Chan!
(A short chinesse man in glasses enters the room. He is dressed in a blue button down shirt and a pair of blue slacks.)
Knick Man: Grettings Dr Chan! Dr Chan: (with a heavy chinesse accent) Hello Knick Man! Knick Man: Would you care to share with my opponent the proper phonetical punctuation of my name?
Dr Chan: (looks confused) Huh? Knick Man: Would you care to share with my opponent, the proper phonetical punctuation of my name?
(Dr Chan pauses and has a huge expression of confusion on his face.) Dr Chan: Um...Um...Hello Knick Man! Knick Man: Wait a minute! Dr Chan, do you speak English? Dr Chan: Um....Um....Um....Hello Knick Man! Knick Man: Oops!!!! My bad!!! I forgot. Dr Chan is a professor in phonetics for the CHINESSE language! But the cat can't speak english to save his life!
Anyway, I guess I will have to just recommend my new program for you Mr Wick! It's called...... (Drums blast in the background as Dr Chan holds up a box. On the box we see the words, "Knick Man Phonics")
Knick Man: KNICK MAN PHONICS!!!! Now even and idiot like YOU can learn to pronounce my name!!!
Dr Chan: Knick Man Phonics works for me!!! Knick Man: Thank you Dr Chan! You may go now! Dr Chan: Huh? (The Knick Man pushes Dr Chan off to the side.) Knick Man: Well now that I gave you your phonetics lesson for the day Mr Wick, tune in next Monday where I will give you your WRESTLING LESSON! You say you're "oozing" with talent? Well you're "oozing" with something alright but it sure ain't TALENT! I mean, you beat a FAGGOT in more ways than one, if you know what I mean (grins) and now you expect that you are going to beat The
Knick Man as well? (laughs) Well sorry to burst your bubble JACK, but it don't work like
THAT! You see, you may have got your "victory" over Juicy Fruit, but can you truly count that as a victory? I mean, the guy probably ENJOYED you being on top of him for the 1-2-3. And from what I heard, that wasn't the ONLY time you were on top of him that night! But I ain't the one to gossip so you didn't hear that one from me!
(The Knick Man sits back down at his desk and puts his feet up on the table.) Knick Man: War Machine? (laughs) Well looks like "The Machine" is gonna malfunction this Monday Night! Because you see, Mr Wick here is more concerned about his butt buddy Adam Mazur then with his match with YOURS TRULY! Yeah,
I'm sure you would LOVE to "team up" with Mazur alright! Just like you "teamed up" with Juicy Fruit! Tell you what, how about you, Mazur, and Juicy Fruit get together and do whatever you FAIRYS like to do together while I march victoriously on through this World Title Tournament and claim what was rightfully mine the moment I signed with this federation! The ICWA World
Heavyweight Championship! And you know what's the best part? I get to take out
SCRUBS like you that THINK they are TOP BILLING when reality says your talent ain't even worth a half a PENNY! You see, I'm the master in this game of human chess in the wrestling ring, and you Punisher? You're nothing more than a PAWN that I will manipulate, then SACRIFICE en route to MY ICWA World Title!
(The Knick Man takes his feet off the desk, opens it, and looks inside. He then pulls out a file marked "Punisher" Mike Wick and places it on the table. The
Knick Man closes the desk, opens the file and looks through.)
Knick Man: You see Wick, I eat pathetic wrestlers like you for BREAKFAST! You ain't dealing with one of your boys from the AWA/GWA! You're dealing with an
INTERNATIONAL SUPERSTAR on the basketball court AND in the wrestling ring!
I've beaten TOP superstars, men rated in the RSPWF TOP 200 wrestlers of 1997, which by the way I was rated #74 IN MY ROOKIE YEAR! Were YOU on that list?
(grins) And you think that "maybe" you might meet up with your buddy Adam Mazur in the finals? Wick, your participation in this tournament ENDS this Monday
Night! The only way you're going to the finals is if you buy a ticket to watch ME wrestle Blade or Mazur! You see Wick, beating The Knick Man is a
MAJOR achievement! Hell, only ONE man has ever pinned my shoulders to the mat in my ENTIRE wrestling career! Them odds ain't good pal! So you go and get ready for your match against "Knicks Man" You go take all the preparations that you need! But in the end, it will all be useless! (stands up) Because
Wick...
(stares intently at the camera) in the end, you will just screw it up, just like always pal! You're a screwup
Wick! I don't even need to know you to know that! Always have been! And always will be! I however am a man destined for greatness! And there's nobody in this federation that will be able to stop me from achiving that greatness!
Not Adam Mazur, not Axon Blade, and most certainly not a SCREWUP like you
"Punisher" I am The Greatest Basketball Player, The Greatest Wrestler, and The
Greatest
Man on the Face of The Earth! And when our match is history, I will go on to the finals, while you will lay flat on your back COUNTING THE LIGHTS! See ya soon Mikey! Se ya soon pal!
(The Knick Man exits the room as "Go NY Go NY Go" plays in the background. Scene fades to black.) American Internet Championship Wrestling http://members.tripod.com/~AICW [detail withheld]
