[CRITIQUE] Langston Crete....from OWE.....

30 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, This is one of the first flashes I did for this character, tell me what you think. -- Chris ===================== LANGSTON CRETE ===================== [Fade in.] [The scene fades into a bowling alley somewhere in Pennsylvania. The camera opens up to show numerous men and women enjoying themselves as they compete in one of America's oldest past times. The sound of pins crashing together echo's throughout the alley. Suddenly, the camera begins to focus on one man. This man has a light colored skin and stands about 6'1" tall and he is bowling alone. He is dressed in a pair of red and white Hawaiian shorts with a black sleeveless t-shirt which reads "OWE" in red letters. He has short brown hair which he wears spiked up with a red visor. The man also sports a nice physique...]
[The camera begins the focus on a behind shot of the man, leaving his face and identity unknown to the world. He steps up onto the lane with a shiny blue bowling ball in his hand. All ten pins can be seen standing up. The man approaches the lane and releases the ball down it with force. The ball explodes into the pins knocking all ten of them down. The man spins around quickly and throws his arms up into the air. It can now be seen that this is none other than OWE superstar Langston Crete. Langston walks off the lane and towards the camera as he begins to speak...]
LANGSTON CRETE: As you can see I do a lot of bowling. I got a lot of practice in my days. Friday nights, Saturday nights, I didn't have much else to do, so I went bowling.
[He pauses while he sits down in a chair.] LANGSTON CRETE: Allow me to introduce myself to you. My name is Langston Crete, the newest addition to the OWE roster. Getting here wasn't easy though, the road to the OWE was a struggle for me. I was born the son of Robert and
Jennifer Crete right here in good ole' Pennsylvania. That's where the bond between us ended. Ever since I was a child they tried to talk me out of this wrestling thing. "It's just a phase" my father said, "He'll be a lawyer like me". Yeah right dad! That was your dream, not mine.
[Pause.] LANGSTON CRETE: My parents gave me no money, they gave me no support, and I had to work for everything I wanted. I loved playing with my wrestling action figures as a child. My dad would always buy me G.I Joe's and Tinker Toys.
Who the hell wants to play with stupid A** Tinker Toys? Nobody. But that's enough about my childhood; let me move onto my high school life. High School is somewhat like pro wrestling. Everyone has there little clique which they fit into and if you're not in a clique you're ridiculed for being different. That's how it was then and that's how it is now. I mean really, sometimes I do outrageous thing, who cares? I like to have fun and I'm gonna do what pleases me, not everyone else.
[Langston readjusts his position and now lay's down across two chairs.] LANGSTON CRETE: I always went on double dates with one of my friends, of course he would get the hot girl and good ole' Langston would have to pick up the ugly friend. I didn't mind, I was fine with it. Everyone has to get some lovin'
somewhere so I wasn't too picky about that. [Langston smacks himself in the head.] I'm sorry, I'm getting a little off the subject of what you came here for. I should talk about the OWE.
[Pause.] LANGSTON CRETE: This week I make my OWE debut against Greg "The Great" Daniels. This will be my first match in front of such a big audience. I've wrestled in small time feds but now it's time for the big time.
If I were to come out here and tell you that I'm the greatest wrestler to ever live or that I was going to dominate the OWE, that would be a bold faced lie.
The fact is, I can't tell you that. I can't predict the future. There are some world class athlete's in the OWE and I'm not gonna discredit any of them, they've earned their way here. If I become great, then that's fine with me. If it happens it happens.
[Langston sits back up and looks into the camera.] LANGSTON CRETE: You see this face? I'm a real person. When you see me each and every week you are getting the real Langston Crete, no B.S. I'm not gonna slap a fancy moniker on my name like "The Great" in order to sell tickets. I'm just flat ole' Langston Crete, some may think it's boring, I think it's real.
I can't make outrageous promises to you but I can tell you the truth. Truth is,
I love to entertain. I love seeing the fans smile and sometimes I'm in disbelief that it's over me. There is one promise I can make to you fans of the
OWE though, no matter how badly beaten I get, no matter if I lose a thousand matches in a row. I will _NEVER_ stop coming. I have heart and determination and when I go out there every week I put it all on the line. Risk is not in my vocabulary because I would use it too much.
[Langston stands up and grabs his blue bowling ball.] LANGSTON CRETE: Ladies and gentlemen, I'm Langston Crete, just an average guy living in a world where you have to be above average to succeed. I'm gonna try and prove that wrong. I start this Sunday on Impact. Until next time.
[As that is said Langston approaches the lane and rolls the ball towards the pins one more time. The pins explode as Langston racks up another strike. The camera fades out.]
