[Flash Critique Pretty Please] Opposites Attract

6 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This was done for KCW's battle royal during Summer Heat, so it's _very_ recent, but at the same time... not. Anyway, let me know what you jigga's think.
Comments WANTED, DAMMIT! ----------------- [Open up with a single, easily identifiable sound; the sound of a steel chair smashing into a wall. OK, so it's not so easily identifiable, but it is when you actually _see_ a steel chair go flying seat-first into a wall.
Pan back, and we see the source of the thrown chair; a steaming, angry "True Living Legend" Brian J. Blottie. "Bloody" Buddy Borders stands calmly to the side, shaking his head.]
"B"BB: I thought you said we were involved in this battle royal KCW is holding... BJB: Shut up, shut up, _shut up_! "B"BB: But... [Brian spins on the much taller, tubby-rific Borders and glares up at him.] BJB: No, I don't want to hear a single _word_ out of your trap. According to the KCW, we _are_ involved in the battle royal.
"B"BB: Are we the two men to be announced, then? BJB: No. "B"BB: Did they mistake us for "Silky" Levaughn and Carl Blair? BJB: _God_ no. "B"BB: Then... what? [Blottie starts pacing back and forth, as Borders just stands there and watches.] BJB: I'm telling you, this is a conspiracy to hold me down. First they make me think that the KCW is _the_ place to be, duping me into signing a contract that I want out of, and now they're treating me like lowlife
_trash_. I'm "The True Living Legend" for Christ's sake. The best of the best. DA MAN~! [A snicker from Borders, which is silenced by another Brian J. Blottie glare.] BJB: And _this_ is the treatment they give me. Not even announcing that I'll be involved in the biggest match in KCW history. "B"BB: Uh... biggest match in KCW history? Did you hit your head? BJB: No! This is the debut of a future KCW Tag Team... Knoxville City... and Volunteer Heavyweight _Champion_. This is the beginning of the "Reign of The Legend". "B"BB: Yeah, whatever. Like _you_ could win a title in a major promotion. BJB: I'm a former... "B"BB: A former regional champion. Yeah, yeah, yeah. And let me guess... you're a former WWO superstar too, right? And let's not forget your "amazing" run in the first MotR tournament. You've certainly proven your... [Snicker.] "B"BB: ... "legendary" status to the professional wrestling world. BJB: Screw you, Buddy! "B"BB: No, Brian. I don't lean that way. BJB: _That's not what I meant_! [A huge grin appears on Borders' mug, as he has to stop himself from laughing outloud.] "B"BB: Anyway, who cares if the KCW promotes us or not? We know that we can entertain the fans, we know that we can beat everyone in this fancy, dancy battle royal, and we certainly know that when we win and get our title shot, we'll be the #1 contenders to the KCW Smokey Mountain Tag Team championship. So let's just go in there and do what we promised the fans... _knock some heads_.
BJB: Whoa, whoa, whoa. [Brian puts on the brakes of his pacing, as he comes face to face with Buddy.] BJB: Let's get one thing clear. When I win this battle royal, _we're_ not trying for any titles. _I_ am trying for titles; the Volunteer Heavyweight title, to be exact.
[Buddy's grin is replaced by a stern frown.] "B"BB: What? BJB: You heard me, numbnuts. We'll be a tag team as long as it benefits me. But the first chance I have to send you packing back up to Michigan,
I'm taking it. You're my free ride to superstardom here in KCW. The tag team titles first, then singles gold next. You can just call me Brody freakin' Thunder, and you're my Hardin. Useless baggage easily discarded when you're not needed anymore.
[With that, Borders grabs a hold of Blottie by his shirt, and slams him against the nearest wall.]
"B"BB: Listen here, "Legend". I didn't agree to be your tag team partner because I liked you, and I certainly didn't expect you to carry your own weight in our partnership. But one thing I do expect is that you'll keep your end of the bargain. You pay me, I take your money, and I cover your ass so that you don't get it handed to you. There will be no "dumping" of anyone around here. Understand?
[Total and utter fear is the only way you can describe the look on Brian's face as he is held forcibly against the wall by Buddy.]
BJB: Yuh-yeah. I understand. Cuh-cuh-can you let me go now? "B"BB: Fine. Just remember... you cross me, I cross you _out_. [Borders releases Blottie, who scampers across the room.] BJB: Jay-sus, man. I was only kidding with you. I'd _never_ dump you. You're, like, the best tag team partner a guy could have. "B"BB: Shut up, kiss ass. [Brian is obviously panicked, looking back and forth, but still never taking his eyes off of Buddy.]
BJB: OK, we need to devise a gameplan. So... after I eliminate the last man in the match, and we're the final two men, you walk out, and I'll take the victory.
"B"BB: Do I need to come over there? BJB: AH! OK! OK! You can have the victory, and we'll go on to our first of _many_ tag team title wins.
"B"BB: Hmmm... I don't know. The Volunteer Title _does_ sound kinda nice... BJB: WHAT?! [Buddy's grin and Brian's look of total frustration are the last things we are left with as we cut away to our best friend in the e-wrestling bidness.
You know him, you love him... THE FADE TO BLACK!]
