[Critique] Written Match

20 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is the first match that I've written out in full. I would appreciate it if some of you could offer me a little constructive critisism. Let me know what I should do better, what I should do that I didn't do, what I shouldn't have done that I did do, those kinds of things.
For this match I just made up two wrestlers. ------------------------------ Darren Gillmore vs. John Denn [FWF World Championship] Jeremy Shaffer - This match is a single fall for the Fake Wrestling Federation
World Championship. First on his way to the ring at this time, weighing in at
300 pounds, from the New York City, John Denn!
[John Denn's theme music begins to play. He comes out of the entrance and makes his way to the ring.]
Jeremy Shaffer - And his oppoenent, weighing in at 320 pounds, from Dallas, TX, accompanied to the ring by Willy Rubar, he is the FWF World Champion, Darren
Gilmore!
[Darren Gilmore's theme music begins to play. Darren Gilmore and Willy Rubar come out of the entrance and make their way to the ring.]
Mark Blaney - Alright folks, it's time to get this one started. [The bell rings.] Mark Blaney - Gilmore starts it off with by punching Denn in the head. Mike Matheney - Gilmore follows up with a knee to the midsection, now an elbow strike to the back of Denn's head.
Jerry Richards - Gilmore pulls Denn to his feet. Denn nails Gilmore with a forearm smash. Mike Matheny - Denn takes Gilmore off his feet with a shoulder tackle. Denn starts stomping away on Gilmore.
Jerry Richards - Denn with an elbow drop. Denn climbs to his feet. Oh, a hard punch to the back of Gilmore's head.
Mark Blaney - Gilmore makes it to his feet. He kicks Denn in the stomach. Gilmore executes a DDT on Denn. Mike Matheny - Gilmore back on his feet now. Gilmore with a knee drop onto
Denn. Now an elbow smash from Gilmore.
Robert Everett - These two guys are morons. I can't stand either of them. I hope both the losers lose this match.
Jerry Richards - Shut up Everett. Robert Everett - Make me. Mark Blaney - That's enought guys. Oh, Gilmore takes Denn down with a spear. Mike Matheny - Gilmore's on top of Denn. He's just beating the crap out him. Jerry Richards - Gilmore's up now, and, wow what a kick to the head. That one had to hurt Denn.
Mike Matheny - Oh man! Denn with a low blow on Gilmore. That'll have him singing high in the shower tonight.
Robert Everett - Now that's what I like to see. Mark Blaney - Denn nails Gilmore in the head with a knee attack. Jerry Richards - Gilmore trys to fight back, but that low blow's still got him stunned. Denn takes Gilmore down with a hard clothline.
Mike Matheny - Denn goes for the pin. Referee Brian Dalton - 1...2... Jerry Richards - Gilmore kicks out. Oh, Denn nails him with an elbow smash. Mark Blaney - Denn pulls Gilmore to his feet. Whoa, Gilmore comes out of nowhere with a clothsline. He sent Denn flying with that one.
Mike Matheny - Gilmore picks Denn up. He throws him to the ropes, Denn rebounds and...Oh. Gilmore took him down with a big boot.
Jerry Richards - Gilmore pulls Denn up. Gilmore grabs him by the neck. Gilmore slams Denn with a big chokeslam.
Mike Matheny - Gilmore pulls Denn up again. Gilmore puts Denn's head between his legs. I think he's...Yes! Gilmore slams Denn with a powerbomb! Gilmore goes for the pin.
Referee Brian Dalton - 1...2...3! Mark Blaney - Darren Gilmore keeps his title. Jeremy Shaffer - The winner of this match, and still FWF World Champion, Darren
Gilmore!
