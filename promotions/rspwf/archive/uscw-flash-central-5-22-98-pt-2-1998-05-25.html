USCW Flash Central 5/22/98 Pt. 2

25 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Abyss "Shotgun" Shawn Evans Da Lench Mob (Noumenon) "The Enforcer" Mike Adams Sinister (2) Daniel Stephens -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- <Abyss sitting in in his hotel room. He sits in a black leather chair and has a blanket over him. Sitting on his lap is the World Television Title and beside him is a cup of steaming coffee.>
Abyss: Everyone is wondering why I did not show up for Wednesday's match. Was I scared? Hell no. I was sick unfortunately. But Seagram, he was the scared one.
What a loser that Seagram is. Anyway, I see that Linkywent couldn't handle
Atomic Punk. So does this mean that Punk is actually getting better or is
Delinquent actually as bad as everyone says? I'd take the latter if this was a bet. Hahaha
Abyss: My next victim is Tony Dylan, who is probably an illegal alien from
France or such. Listen here kiddo, I beat SP and you know you can't do it, so just back up before you get smacked up. Now I realize there's some hot new
"talent" here in the USCW, but the only thing these bitches are talented at is kissin my ass. Ladies Lover Jeff Gillis. Ha! There's a loser for ya. The only thing the ladies love about you is the way they can beat the shit outta your puny ass. Listen buddy, there's on one "Hardcore Sensation" around here and ask around. You'll find out that that is me, "The King of Extreme". You show me what you got and if your good enough, I'll give you a shot at gold. I earned the nickname of "King of Extreme" you just come in here calling yourself shit.
And if you don't believe I earned look at my match against Shin Path in the cage with weapons and look at who the victor was. Now get your big head outta my damn federation or you'll get the bashing you deserve. Oh yeah, Atomic punk, just remember when our match comes that I'm a-fuckin-mazing!
<Abyss takes a sip of coffee and the screen fades.> -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- ( The scene is in " Shotgun " Shawn Evans home. The screen shows his basement, where his personal gym is located. As we zoom in closer, we see that Evans is giving hard rights and lefts to his punching bag ;
each hit nailing the bag hard. And Shawn doesn't show any sign of letting up. Shawn has tape wrapped around his wrists, and is wearing the new " Shotgun " Shawn Evans t-shirt and shorts set put out by the
USCW earlier in the morning. Finally, he stops and grabs his towel, as he prepares to talk to the camera. )
Shawn : Well? I've finished my training. You can ask what you wanna ask now. Reporter : Well, I just wanted to get your thoughts on Chris... Shawn : Dammit! I already said no more interviews about Chris already. Get out of my house! ( Shawn points towards the door, as the reporter is about to leave. The reporter walks up the steps and is just about out when... ) Shawn : Wait a minute. I might as well make use of you while you're here. ( The reporter walks back down the stairs. ) Shawn : While you're here, I do have somebody I wanna talk about. And that somebody is Daniel Stephens, the self-proclaimed " Future Legend "
. This Sunday me and you are gonna face off in the ring to see who the real #1 contender is, and who will get the shot at the lightheavyweight title.
( Shawn picks up his water bottle, puts his towel down, and takes a drink before continuing. )
Shawn : ( smirks ) Well, let me give YOU a little warning " Mr. Future Legend " ( in a sarcastic tone ). Since you've last seen " Shotgun " Shawn Evans, I've regained " the edge " and I'm more dangerous than ever. Unluckily for you, there's nobody, and when I mean nobody, I MEAN nobody, to stop me from pummeling you into the ground.
My punches hit as hard as hammers ; my kicks are gonna waste you faster than you'll believe.
( Takes a long breath ) Shawn : Basically, what I saying is that I'm gonna beat you around in the ring. I'm gonna prove to the world that I'm dangerous ; even by myself. Ya know, Shotguns are dangerous. And this Shotgun is the most dangerous of 'em all.
( grins ) Shawn : So you'd better get ready " Future Legend " cause the Shotgun is comin' to town. Now get out.
( Shawn pushes the reporter and the cameraman out as he starts practicing his kicks on the punching bag. )
FADE TO BLACK -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- <you see noumenon and eightball> eightball: ha ha ha! it looks like the new jobbers think they can wrestle with the big boys.
seagram: didnt we already talk about how jobbers always think they can beat everyone? mopreme: ok, then lets talk about something else. seagram: what? eightball says what about this! <you are looking out a security camera at the convenience store one half a block north of the location of the last house show. you see seagram walk in and out of the picture towards the back. when seagram returns to the counter, he has several items and places them on it. outside the window you see someone almost walk in and then duck behind the wall, out of site. seagram hands the man the money and the man bags his stuff and seagram starts out the door. at that moment he comes threw the door backwards shattering the glass and stuff flying out of his bag. he starts to get up and abyss walks in and starts to kick him. the guy at the counter picks up the phone and starts talking hysterically abyss jumps ontop of seagram and starts to punch him. seagram slowly wrestles abyss to the bottom and seagram starts to beat on his head.
the men get to there feet and you see them throwing eachother around and the guy is hiding behind the counter, ducked down. all the sudden you see flashing
blue light and the cops come running in. they jump on abyss and seagram and throw them to the ground and handcuff them and take them away. mopreme: now what was that all about. seagram: that was just abysses way of getting out of a match with me. he had to keep his title somehow. so this is how he did so.
eightball: can you blame him. he know he would lose. seagram: and for his stupid ass actions he will be paying dearly. abyss you will be in the ring this saturday standing alone, like usual and i will come in and make my attack on you so ferce that your ass would rather have your nuts in dares grip. that mistake you made at the store will be your last.
eightball: abyss, and everyone else, always remember, if you fuck with us, you fuck yourself.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- ( Scene opens to the outside of USCW headquarters. It is a clear spring day, as " The Enforcer " Mike Adams strolls around, admiring the scenery. Mike has his sunglasses on, along with the new
" The Enforcer " Mike Adams t-shirt and shorts set. Finally, he stops to say something. ) Mike : So how's it going USCW fans? With Chris gone for a while, and me finally having some more matches, I've decided to talk to you for a while.
( Takes a deep breath ) Mike : Ya know, I haven't been making a major impact ; wait let me rephrase that. I haven't been making ANY impact on the USCW for the longest time. And, if you knew me well enough, I intend to do something about it. Last Midweek Madness, I came out and I wrestled The
Demolitionist one on one in the ring in front of thousands of fans. And I managed to pin The Demolitionist in front of thousands of fans. ( Looks down as if thinking for a moment, then looks back up. ) Mike : But that was no challenge. Facing some low-carder is not what Mike Adams calls a challenge. So what's next? Or who's next? This Weekend Edition, I face the one they call the Shadow Warrior. ( Pauses ) Mike : ( In a disgusted tone ) Shadow Warrior? That's all the USCW can give me? Some low carder named Shadow Warrior who hasn't done anything but sit on his lazy *BLEEP* and remain on the payroll?! Give me a break Spicer! I will beat this Warrior and I will beat him fair and square.
( Pauses for a moment, as if thinking then begins to talk again. ) Mike : Enough about him. So Chris is gone for a while. You know what that makes me? That means I'm the sherriff in charge. They don't call you The Enforcer for nothing, and I'm gonna make sure that justice and tradition stand in the USCW. If it means I have to go through EVERYBODY in the USCW, I will do just that ; Win ; lose ; or draw. So get ready fellow workmen. I'm about to make an impact in the USCW, and it ain't gonna be a small impact.
( Screen fades with Mike once again walking around admiring the scenery. ) FADE TO BLACK -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- (The scene opens up in a down town drug clinic. There is no one inside except for a woman at the desk and the doctors in the back. A man in a thick coat comes walking in through the door and approaches the front desk. He throws his coat off to reveal himself to be Sinister of the
USCW.) Woman: Can I help you? Sinister: I have been contaminated by my boss! I want you to test me for drugs now! Woman: Your name? Sinister: You don't know who the fuck I am? Woman: Your name sir, and please watch the language. Sinister: Sin fucking ister Woman: Ok Mr. Sin Ister, I will need you to give me a urine or blood sample, so please go into the back room and...
Sinister: You need a blood sample? Here you go! (Sinister takes a large knife out of his pocket and slices his arm down the four arm and wipes the blood all over the counter.)
Woman: Oh my god! Sinister: That good enough toots? (The Woman calls for the doctors and two of them come out to see what the commotion is.) Doctor: What is the meaning of this? Sinister: I have been contaminated! Rob Spicer, big business tycoon, Is a fucking drug pusher!
Doctor: Oh my god! Are you sure? Sinister: You got it! I went to the police as well, and his pompous ass is busted. (The doctors go to work making phone calls and the normal business and Sinister uses the moment of commotion to slip out of the door.) Sinister: Well, well, Robby my boy your empire is falling apart. A drug dealer of all things! Drugging your wrestlers! Feeding your favorites steroids while your enemies are drugged to keep them from winning.
Pathetic old man, looks like your ass got busted finally. You sit up in your fucking tight ass little office and lord over us like some kind of fucking king. Well guess what Robby? You messed with the wrong one of your fucking subjects. I am going to ruin you, I am just going to take a it a step further than Viper did and not only ruin your business but ruin your life. Now that I have put that in the air, lets move on to my match this Sunday against your old friend Storm. To this date I have NEVER lost to any member of the SWA, nor will I ever. Try druggin me, it won't matter, I could beat Storm in my sleep, dance on his body, and kick your ass before i woke up. Storm thinks this is the end of our little war?
Not happening Storm. it ain't happening till i have run your ass out of this federation once and for all. I ran your baby brother out of the
UWF, then he comes crawling back after I had finished him off and left the league. Wonder if you will try the same thing.... anyway, your a waste of time Storm, a washed up great trying to ride off of past glories. I have taken out more legends than you have wrestled friend, and if i remember correctly, you were one of them. I guess the only difference is, this time around I don't have anything to take from you, except your career, and maybe that little tight assed woman of yours.
(Sinister rounds the corner to see a raid being done on the USCW building. A swat team rushes in and a narcotics unit follows behind.
Sinister bursts into laughter as he watches with glee.) Sinister: Now that is a sight! It's real sad you know. The only way Spicer could get his other wrestlers to match up to me and Viper was through the use of drugs. Now, this coming Pay-per-view, there is no way you can stop me from breaking your little farm boy lover. Yoder is gonna step in the ring, no rules, against the Sin man. Viper has his go around, now the Sin man gets his. Time to get your ass planted once and for all, in the filed of Sin. See ya soon farm boy, I can't wait.
(Sinister turns away laughing. he disappears into the crowd of onlookers as the raid takes place.)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- (The scene opens up in Rob Spicer's office where the president of this corporation sits going over the next card. The phone begins ringing and the president picks it up.)
Rob Spicer: Yeah? Sinister: Got a present for you Robby boy. It should arrive right about... now! (A swat team busts into the room and pin Rob Spicer on to the ground.) Policeman: This is a drug bust! Put you hands in the air now! (The camera distorts, then 'snows' over for a moment. The view clears up and resumes a normal picture, showing Rob Spicer talking to a narcotics detective, laughing with the man, and shaking hands with him. He reaches into a filing cabinet and pulls out a file photo of Sinister. He makes a copy of it and hands it to the detective. He opens the top drawer of his desk, pulls out a stack of VIP passes for USCW June Jackpot, and hands them to the detective and the S.W.A.T. team. The two men shake hands again, and the detective leaves with his team.)
Spicer: I'd like to take this time to apologize for the actions and false accusation of one of my misguided employees. Sinister seems to have an inferiority complex, and seeks to balance it out by his crude conduct and bogus claims against me. The man is a disturbed individual, and needs help other than what we can provide for him here. That help is being arranged for him now. In the meantime, I ask you all to excuse his behavior and overlook it as the rantings of a disfunctional individual. Thank-you.
(Spicer picks up some papers and a day planner from his desk, and walks out of the office, shutting the door behind him.)

stephens: yea, but only a few seconds. camera man: sharpe beat you in the three way dance, what do you have to say about that? stephens: it was a good match. stephens put on a great performance. nothing else needs to be said.
camera man: what about your match with shawn evens coming up sunday? stephens: as you all know, when stephens steps into the ring he will win the match. by winning this match he will be going against kid sharpe at the pay per view. this should not come as a surprise. stephens will then beat sharpe for the belt and go on and show you all that he is the greatest wrestler ever and let you all know why he is the future legend.
camera man: what about evens being hurt? stephens: it doesnt matter if evens is hurt. he will give 100 percent and so will stephens. the only difference is that stephens will win. after stephens wins the title, he will successfully defend it against shotgun the next weekend.
camera man: what a... stephens: that is all stephens has time for. <stephens gets in his car and leaves> -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -----------------------------------------------
