[Nassir asks a favour!] Critique this RP! Napoleon w/ The Mini Rebels.

20 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi there! OK, I'm asking my first ever RSPWF favour! AAAAH! This is the first time I've ever asked for a critique... no wait, there was that 6000 word rp I posted several months back that no one read, bastards!!!
Well, this is *much* shorter (yeah NPWA! Up yours! ;-)) so you've got no excuses! This is my *latest* character, Napoleon, the 4'4" 115lb mini. His angle is that he has an 'army' of minis and they are out fighting for respect, pissed off as they are by being used as comedic sideshows for years. He's still fairly new-ish (i.e. out of the box but not broken in yet) and he's got his OWF debut coming up against the excellent
Boltz. Since I think I'm going to lose to that Dan can spark some kind of angle off, I have no fear letting Boltzy get a jump on me before the release of the flash packets. YOU HEAR THAT?! NO FEEEEAR!
Muahahahahaha! So anyhoo, enjoy ye and get thee upon thine critique! (or such as...) ********************** [SCENE: The OWF Clamor interview room. The Mini Rebels are there - that is to say, El Pyrato, Petree, Panther Cub, Indigo and their almighty leader, the 4'4" 115pound Napoleon! Napoleon is dressed as if for wrestling, in a camouflaged shellsuit and black boots. He still wears his military-style cap though.
Napoleon has set up a display board, upon which he has placed a black silhouette of a man. Underneath the silhouette the words "The Enemy:
Boltz" are written. The four other minis sit in chairs facing Napoleon and the board. Napoleon waves his stagger stick at the board.]
Napoleon: My loyal Mini Rebels! The time has come to take the battle directly to the enemy! Our first skirmish with a representative of those oppressive despots who have kept us down for so many years! His name is Boltz! He was raised on the streets! His father was abusive!
We laugh in his face! Ha Ha *HA*! His petty little issues matter little to those with a noble cause, such as us! This victory shall be an easy one, my companions in arms! We have more skill, we have more determination, and we have greater numbers. This Boltz is outclassed on all sides!
[Napoleon points to Panther Cub with his swagger stick.] Napoleon: I now invite our primary reconnaissance... person, Panther Cub... Panther Cub: Maurice. Napoleon: Maurice, to come up and present his scouting report on this Boltz. Panth... Maurice, if you will? [Panther Cub stands and fishes around inside his leather jacket, pulling out several knives and brass knuckles before locating the item he was searching for: a pointer. Napoleon sits as Panther clears his throat.]
Panther Cub: Right. Now this Boltz is one badass mutha<BLEEP>. He stands at 6 foot 4 and weighs 237 pounds, and most a' dat is muscle. I need not tell you that that's a lot bigger than any of us are. He's really gonna kick our asses unless we cut him up real bad real fast.
[The minis murmur worriedly. Panther Cub smashes his pointer against the display, silencing them.]
Panther Cub: SHUT IT! I ain't through yet. The rest of my report is somewhat more encouraging. Boltz' wrestlin' style is primarily aerial.
Being his size and weight, I don't think 'e's quite as good at that as Napoleon, with automatically gives him the edge in a straight high-flyin' fight. In addition, he thinks 'imself something of an
'ardcore wrestler. I'll dispel those illusions, personally. [Panther pats his laden leather jacket and grins wickedly. The minis laughs and clap.] Panther Cub: Finally, there's five of us and only one of him. He won't know what hit him until he sees us jumpin' up an' down on his big ass!
So the bottom line is this. He's bigger and stronger than we are alone, but together we're going t' beat him good! Petree, get up here man and tell 'em how to hurt him!
[Petree slumps up and takes the pointer. Panther Cub takes his seat. Petree runs a hand through his greasy hair and points in the general vicinity of the diagram's groin. He pulls a red marker pen from his pocket and draws a big red 'X' over the groin.]
Petree: Yeah, everyone hit this place a lot, 'cause it hurts when you do, but make sure you leave some nice juicy balls for me. Also, go for his eyes. He can't see if you gouge his eyes out and leave his sockets empty, gaping, bloody holes. Hit him in the stomach. We'll feast upon the half-digested nourishment that spews forth. Take his knees out.
He'll crawl around like the pathetic worm he is until we can slit his throat. He'll bleed to death like a pig,
__LIKE__ __A__ __PIG_ __DAMMIT!__ [Petree scrawls red 'X's all over the diagram in a mad frenzy, breathing heavily and muttering something inaudible. Napoleon takes him by the shoulders and leads him back to his seat. He flops down and puts his head between his legs.]
Napoleon: Petree you freak! Calm down before I give you a demerit! *cough* Thank you for that briefing, soldiers! At long last, the months upon months of arduous training and detailed planning are about to pay off! On the March 28, OWF Sunday Inferno, the Mini Rebellion will fire the first shots in the war to end all wars! We're going to take _OVER_ people, and we're going to _GET OUR RESPECT_! Even if it means spilling our blood, and even if it means breaking our bones, by
God people! We're going to fight until the last ounce of strength is sapped from our muscles, and until the last breathe escapes our lips!
Onwards, brave Mini Rebels! Onwards towards our __DESTINY__!!! [The minis leap out of their chairs and raise a mighty cheer! Napoleon puts his hands on his hips, sticks his chin out, and nods, satisfied.
He raises a clenched fist into the air.] Napoleon: VICTORY! [The minis pump their fists in the air and chant, "Victory! Victory! VICTORY!" Napoleon smiles portentously, and nods firmly - as the scene fades to black.] -- [name withheld] AKA: [detail withheld] (IRC) [detail withheld] (ICQ) Hamlet (RL) Handler of: Robie123 [Hated by people with a sense of humour] Napoleon [Just so no one else is confused, Napoleon is a midget] James Warnham [The true Vanilla Midget!] The Toxic Marauders [Damn you Scampini!
