-[FLASH]- "DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE

8 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens up to a grassy field in the middle of the country. A forest surrounds the open field, which the grass is knee high. It's a wonderful day as the sky is a beautiful blue and the clouds are white and fluffy. The camera looks over the scenery until a sound is heard. Sounds like footsteps in the grass. The camera scans around until we see a man walking in the field. This man is wearing a black T-shirt that reads "The Love Man Is Coming... To Love
You To Death", faded blue jeans, a pair of circular lense sunglasses, a Miami
Dolphins hat, and a pair of old, worn-out, Nike's. This man continues to walk until he finally see's the camera man standing there. He begins to walk towards the camera man and as he gets closer, we notice that this man is none other than "Dangerous" Dusty Love. He stops in front of the view and looks out to enjoy "mother nature" as he begins to speak.]
"DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE: Nice day to be outside, eh? I like bein' out here. It's so relaxing. But when ya think about it, my whole life is relaxing. I've got the nicest cars, the finest whine, the most beautiful women, and a very, very, nice, house. As you've seen before. Man, life is grand when you have the luxury that I do.
[Dusty, then, looks into the camera and lets out a cocky smile and continues on.] "DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE: And it only gets better. The team is back together. Justin Miller, James Miller, Tony Paseno, and me are all back together to do what we do best... And that's to _CONQUER_ the ICW. If you remember the last time we were all on the same team, you'd remember that we completely took over the UHW. Back then, I had the World Heavyweight Championship around my waist.
And today... "The Italian Stallion" is the one holdin' it. But as long as it's in the family... It's fine with me. There's nobody that we can't handle... And I do mean _NOBODY!_ And that includes _YOU_, Shane Diamond.
You thought that you'd be able to just walk into Chicago and leave with the
International Heavyweight Title?
[Dusty laughs a little and then continues on.] "DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE: Well, if you actually believed that... Then you're even stupider than I thought. Diamond... You brought all that you had... And it still wasn't enough to beat "The Big Man." Sure... Some might say that I interfered... And some might say that Justin and James Miller interfered. But the way I see it... Is like I said before... All is fair in love and war.
You should've saw it comin'. You had your back-up, and Paseno had his. But the fact of the matter is just this - His back-up was _BETTER!_ Just take a look at us. "The Picture of Perfection" Justin Miller... One of the greatest wrestlers to step foot inside a ring. Former World Heavyweight Champion _MANY_ times over. And in many different federations. Then, you have "Simply
Perfect" James Miller... And he is what he says he is... _SIMPLY PERFECT!_
But next, you have the ICW International Heavyweight Champion, "The Italian
Stallion" Tony Paseno. He's the ICW International Champion... Enough said!
Some might call us a "Dream Team"... And I can't do anything about it, but
_AGREE_ with 'em!
[Dusty flashes his pearly whites with another conceited smile. He, then, begins to walk and continue talking.]
"DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE: You've got the four of us... Up against Shane Diamond, Westside Walavan, D.B. Bugg, and "Pretty Boy" Andrew. Looks like a miss-match, if ya ask me. But if they wanna risk their lives to step in the ring with us... Fine by me. I just wanna send out a warning... Diamond,
Bugg, Walavan, Andrew... This match _WILL_ be hazardous to _YOUR_ health!
We're coming to teach you guys a lesson. We're comin' to show all four of ya, that we're the _NEW_ leaders of the ICW! We're the young blood that's pumping through the ICW's veins! And you guys are nothin' but _SECOND CLASS_ to guys like us. We're just that much better... And on Friday night, you'll see exactly what I mean.
[Dusty Love stops walking and looks into the camera. He continues...] "DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE: Westside Walavan... You might be the ICW Television
Champion, but if you ever stepped in the ring with any of us one on one...
You'd have as much a chance of winning as a fart in a whirl-wind. Walavan... If I were you... I'd leave the country. Because my good pal, Tony Paseno, ain't too happy with you right now. You came out and ya took his belt after he won it... And that don't set too well with him. And believe me you... He won't leave the Hartford Civic Center without it. Next up, ya got D.B. Bugg.
When I say that name or think about it... One question comes to mind - Who the hell is this guy? I've never heard of ya... I've never seen ya in the ring...
But yet, you wanna come out and attack _ME_, of all people, from behind? I don't know much about ya... And I don't really care. But I do know this -
You're _NOT_ a brain surgeon. You attack me, and I strike right back _HARDER!_
Bugg... I'll see you on Friday night... And you ain't gonna like how I introduce myself. But the last member of the team, is "Pretty Boy" Andrew.
Again... I've never seen or heard of ya before, but all I know is that you stuck your nose into _OUR_ business... And now it's time to pay the consequences. But I can tell ya this... You might be "Pretty"... But don't you wish that you were _THIS_ pretty?
[Dusty smiles and starts walking again. He walks a few feet before speaking again.] "DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE: If you ask me... There's four men that are gettin' ready to be not only out wrestled... But out classed... And certainly, out done. "The Miller Brothers", "The Stallion", and me... We're ready for this match... And most of all, we're ready to prove to the world that we are, what we say we are... And that's a _"PERFECTION CONNECTION!_ Get ready boys...
'Cause "The Love Man" is coming to... [Dusty Love pulls his glasses down to the tip of his nose and looks deep into the camera with his sexy brown eyes.]
..."LOVE"... ...YOU... ...TO... ...DEATH!!!" [The camera zooms in on the dead serious face of Dusty Love. He sits his glasses back up-right on his face and then lets out his patented cocky grin.
He turns and walks off through the grass and into the woods. The camera fades out as Dusty Love disappears in the trees and bushes.]
