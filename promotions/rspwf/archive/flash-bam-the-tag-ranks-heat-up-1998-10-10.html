[FLASH] BaM!!! The Tag Ranks Heat Up

10 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ The scene opens up to inside a smoke filled bar. The bar is full of alcoholics and drunkards, all trying to get every sip of beer possible into their disgusting mouths. To the left, is the bar. Behind the counter is probably the only respectable looking person in there- the bartender. He's serving the customers their alcohol as two burst out in laughter watching the pool game. To the right is a series of three wooden tables, only one occupied, and a pool table. Three men are shooting some pool. A Mad looking one, wearing a white undershirt and a pair black jeans is staring over at the men sitting at the table in a spiteful manner. Another man, with a cigar jutting out of his mouth is shooting, decorated with a green Nike shirt and a pair of cargo pants. And finally, the last one is unshaven but has confidence oozing out of his body as he sits there with a black t-shirt and a pair of black khakis. In his hand as he sits, is a shiny pool stick. Looking at this man you can tell he is an obvious pool Shark. He also seems to be looking over at the two men at the table. The angle now shifts. Showing the two men at the table. One seems to be a mix of white and italian.
He's wearing a black bubble vest and a pair of black jeans. His dark blonde hair is wet and slicked back. He's also wearing a pair of shades and by his facial
expressions you can see that he is intensely watching the man across from him at the pool table. The other man is black. He is glaring at the men as well with the same intensity. This man is bald and is wearing a black muscle shirt and loose blue jeans. On his head is a black hat that says BaM in twisted, rugged red letters. He slowly takes his hat off, still staring, and sets it on the table. The man the two men are staring at is visually upset and seems to be of Mexican descent.]
[The intimidating man walks around the pool table with stick in hand and
"accidently" hits the italian man with the end of the stick. The italian man does nothing however and remains calm. With a smirk on his face, he walks to the other side and juggles his pool stick a little. Now he says, "Aye, homos. Watch 'dis." He sets up to break and hits the cueball with tremendous force sending it slamming into the set of balls. The seven and eight ball are sent flying off and into the two mens table, crashing into drinks and the like. In slow motion, the eight ball knocks off the black mans hat... It slowly falls to the ground and immieadiatly as it lands the two men stand up. The italian knocks the table over and looks pissed as hell the black men rushes over to the mexican. He swings his pool stick but the
black man grabs it, pulls it away and kicks him in the stomach. Keeling over, the black man
sends the edge of stick flying straight towards the back of the mans head knocking him to the ground. The other two players run to his aid but the itlaian man sees it and clotheslines one of them on to the table and begins to pound away on his face. Meanwhile, the black man WHACKS the other guy straight across the face. The other man, standing on the pool table lifts the pool player up and hits a DEVASTATING FISHERMEN
BUSTER. The pool player's head has miracuously gone through the table. The two men walk off, one picks up his hat and they head outside as if nothing happened.] Shiloh Mount: (Italian Guy) TWL... You have no idea what the hell you've gotten yourself into.
"The Blazer" Barry Blades: The dear prez of this gig promised us an opportunity. And like any starving artist, we gladly accepted.
Shiloh Mount: Indeed... Heh heh... We've been roaming the countryside month after month, year after year hoping for a shot like this. And now while thats very fortunate for us, the rest of the fag-teams have a couple of cold days in their forecast. But, Barry. What I find interesting here is this lil ole division that we've been placed in. As
I looked over the list of "talent" I noticed that... Well, it's just missing something... Do you know what it is?
"The Blazer" Barry Blades: I think I might know... [With a smile forming on his face.] Shiloh Mount: It's missing us god damn it. Me and my pal here are known notoriously throughout the indies, and I don't mean the spicy ones, as
BaM.
"The Blazer" Barry Blades: And that stands fo' Bad Ass MuthF*kas. Shiloh Mount: Ya see, TWL is not your regular federation as many of you fans have seen yourself. Here, you have to have some balls. Here you have to prove yourself in a talent pool filled with the elite and hell, we couldn't resist!
"The Blazer" Barry Blades: It ain't only that... We've never held any tag straps to be painfully honest. The past three federations we have been in kicked us out because "We're a threat to their talent"... HA!
Everytime I saw that last pathetic pimply faced president he was pissin' in his pants. Shiloh Mount: I wouldn't worry about that here, man. I was lookin' through the division and I saw some chicas that might be "worth our time". If I remember straight, one team one there was the 504 Boyz... AH
HA HA! "The Blazer" Barry Blades: That reminds me of a Levi commercial for some reason. Shiloh Mount: Oh yeah, and then the NEW AGE OUTSIDERS! [Both burst out in laughter.] "The Blazer" Barry Blades: Thats worse then the Legion of Steiners! This isn't the best they have is it?
Shiloh Mount: Oh, no. They have at least one quality team. From what I've heard these Object of Desire are the champs and could give us a run for our money. "The Blazer" Barry Blades: Yeah, thats somethin' we haven't had in a while. Shiloh Mount: No doubt about that. TWL, BEE-AYE-EM is ready and rearin' to go. And they don't call us Bad Ass MuthaF*ckas for nuthin'... Heh heh. [With that, both Shiloh and "The Blazer" begin walking towards their car in the small parking lot of the trashy tavern. The three men stumble out of the tavern, two of them bleeding profusely as The Bad Ass MuthaF*ckas turn around, and speed directly towards them, barely missing them by a few inches. The camera fades as BaM drives off in the dark night.]
