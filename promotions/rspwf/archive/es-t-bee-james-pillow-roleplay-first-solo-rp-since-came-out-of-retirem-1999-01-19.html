"ES T BEE" JAMES PILLOW Roleplay [First solo RP since came out of retirement]

19 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[SCENE:The camera opens up to the home office building of the IWC. The camera moves around the hallway as we see secretaries, interns, wrestlers and many others walking the halls, doing work, talking, etc. The camera then moves all the way down until we see a pair of doubl doors and a sign that reads
"PRESIDENT JAMES MCDANIEL" on it. The camera moves in as the door opens and a women walks out. She stumbles as she fixes her skirt and her shirt not completely tucked in. She loks nervously around and goes and sits down at a cubical. The camera then moves into the office as we see the desk and a cloud of smoke above it as the back is to the camera. The chair then turns as we expect to see McDaniel but it's not him. It is non other than the leader of The
Gentlmans CLub, James Pillow!]
[APPEARNCE:James has on a black Armani suit with a green tie with the letters
"GC" on it and a pair of shiny Gucci dress shoes. His hair is nicely combed, and he is clean shaven. James also has a cigar in betwen his lips as well as a sly grin. James takes the cigar out of his muth and blows smoke rings into the air as he chuckles and begins to speak. .]
"ES T BEE" JAMES PILLOW - I assure you. .me and Mr. MACDaniels personal secretary were merely going over some notes. . .I swear on Bill and Hillary's marriage. <laughs> But anyway. . .me 'n the top dawg of the IWC have ourselves a little meeting in <looks at watch> Ohh. .20 minutes or so, but I got here a little early to take care of that little business. .now I got some more time left on my hands. .so why not give you people what you want and that's a few words of my magnificent wisdom. .so let's get right to it. . .I guess I'll start with the man who <pauses> Just isn't a gentleman. I guess you could come right out and say he's nothing more than a piece of white bred, trailor trash.
Just another man who is. ._JEALOUS_ of the Gentlemans CLub and wishes he could fit in with the likes of John Carey, the most Hardcore wrestler to ever step foot in the ring. .The virtual future of this industry, Scott Sexton, the man who sizzles so much he can't go near water because it evaporates. .and of course, the biggest legend in the history of this sport. .the greatest g'damn man to ever step foot inside of a ring, The Es T Bee, James Pillow! Eric
Emerson, you just keep on flapping those gums. .but you'll never be like us. .because you don't have the talent, the skill, or the just plain raw ability to even come close in comparison to the likes of anyone in the Gentlemans club.
.hell ole MACDaniel could probably take you out. . [James takes the cigar off of the ash trey and flicks some of the ashes off, h then places it in his mouth and goes on. .]
"ES T BEE" JAMES PILLOW - You talk about Derek DOuglas. .Derek DOuglas beat me? <laughs> Derek Douglas has never had the _PLEASURE_ of stepping in the ring with me one on one. .but if we had, then he wuld've been broken, bloodied and beaten just liek you will be if you ever grow the scrots to get in the ring with me. Eric Emerson <chuckles> You need to get over the jealousy thing. .face it, you just can't compare <holds out arms> Not a damn thing you can do about it. .I was jusst born superior. .get used to it.
[The phone then rings and James picks it up. He moves away from the camera and speaks lightly so we can't hear what he is saying then hangs up. . .]
"ES T BEE" JAMES PILLOW - Trying to listen in on a mans conversation? Well if you must know that was The rpez telling me he was gonna be late. .that's all you need to know. Because each of you people out there are on a need to know basis. .and quite frankly you just don't need to know just yet. <Straightens tie> Moving along. .we come to one Johnny Glamour. Another man who throughtout his career has _IDOLIZED_ me. .has wished he could be half as great as I am. .
.hate to break it to ya Glam, bu you just don't compare. I thank you for the nice welcome back. . .But I know deep down you're just _DREADING_ the day we have to step into the ring against each other. .and seeing as how we both have our sights set on the biggest pussy in the IWC. . .somthing we actually agree on. Magnus Colby. .and his world title. As for you humiliating me? <laughs and shakes head> Uh uh. .not gonna happen. .and you might as well cancel my trip to
Glamourville, 'cause I got you a one way ticket to a very poulated town. .and that's the town of everyone who thought they could beat me. .but just failed
_MISERABLY!_
[James places the cigar down on the ash trey and gets up from the leather seat almost reluctantly. We then notice his zipper is undone. James stretches a bit and looks down, he quickly zips it up and begins to speak again. .]
"ES T BEE" JAMES PILLOW - Moving along to the end of the line for this eve and that is my partner in the Lethal Lottery and my good pal, Candyman. Thnaks for the little trip <grins> Down memory lane. .but I'm still young, and my memory hasn't gone just quite yet. I know our past with each other. .and I know our futurue involves a big win in the Lethal Lottery and more gold for the both of us. . .providing you hold up your side of things, because I know I will be. .
[James walks over and points to a picture that shows James and Candyman standing side by side along with the other members of the now defunct Rough
Justice. . .]
"ES T BEE" JAMES PILLOW - It's amazing how many things can change in a few mere months. You talk about attitude changes. If my attitude has changed. .it has for the better. It has because now I'm finally speaking my mind and telling it how it is, weither you, or any of those fans out there like it or not. .if they don't, then ain't that to bad because I'm not here to get the admiration of anyone. .I'm just here to win matches, as well as titles. <pauses> What happens in Lethal Lottery. .well, I guess time will tell, I'm no fortune teller, so we have to sit back and go for the ride.
[James walks back to the desk and sits down on it. He rest the bottom of his feet agaisnt the front of the desk. ]
"ES T BEE" JAMES PILLOW - I know who you are C-MAN! You damn well better know who I am. .And I damn sure know who you are. And if all goes correctly. .we could be the next tag team champions. So play your cards right buddy ole pal ole chum and you can ride the SimplyBest X-Press all the way to the bank!
[James slides backwards on the desk until we falls into the leather chair. He then puts his feet up on the desk. We then hear the door open as James McDaniel walks in. He then looks at the camera and guves the cut sign. He then walks over and hops onto the desk knocking James' feet off the desk as the camera slowly. .]
[Fades To Black. . .]
