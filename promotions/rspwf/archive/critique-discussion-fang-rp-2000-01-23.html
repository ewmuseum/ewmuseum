[CRITIQUE/DISCUSSION] Fang RP!

23 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys and gals. Just posting a Fang roleplay that I finished writing for the WWO. Was just hoping to get some feedback... things I'm doing wrong, things
I'm doing right (at least I hope there are a couple things).
Enjoy! ---- [The camera opens onto a desolate street, somewhere in American's vast urban landscape. The sky is a dark, overcast grey color, and the scene is dominated by a steady fall of thick drops of rain. The heavy, staccato rhythm of rainfall fills the otherwise silent void... As the clip-clop cadence continues, the camera moves languidly through the rain-swept streets. Slowly, a dark city scene emerges. A skyline, dominated by looming skyscrapers, almost gothic in their design, dirty streets upon which debris sails along guided by a chilling wind, almost in mockery of tumbleweeds in the classic western setting. And from this new frontier scene, this dark, gritty urban wilderness, a man comes to the screen, leaning against a moldy brick wall, and resting directly below a sign that says "Pawn Shop."
The man is none other than WWO superstar and self-proclaimed 'warrior', Fang. Today, the wrestling enigma wears a black leather jacket, zippered up in front, and a pair of Levi's 501 jeans. The collar of his jacket is lifted up, to protect his neck from the wind and rain. The man's arms are crossed in front of his chest, and there is a cynical, condescending smirk plastered across his visage. In his whispery, intense voice, the grappler begins to speak.]
FANG: You try so hard to be identified as the villain, but you fail miserably. Just as you tried to retain what _was_ your Internation People's title, and again... failed. Much in the same way you aspired to take the United States championship this past weekend...
[Fang's smirk grows into a smile, which he restrains immediately. He blows on his hands to keep them warm, then continues.]
... and couldn't do it. Your goal in life was to become a Hollywood film star, and you managed to fuck that up too. Face it Mr. Detson...
[Fang sighs. Then he strikes his trademark devilish grin.] You're a failure. You have high hopes and maintain incredible goals, which, if achieved, would make you a star. They'd give you fame and fortunes far greater than any other in the World Wrestling Organization. You would be labeled a Hollywood Icon, Mr.
Detson. Your merchandise would be on backorder before even going on sale. Johnathan, you would be standing in that blinding white hot spotlight that you've craved since your inception... and you have it all planned out in your mind. You know exactly how it needs to be done, right down to the very last moment. Sad thing is... you don't have the work ethic to do it.
[Fang nods and again blows on his shriveling hands, the wrestling enigma's clothing becoming more and more waterlogged.]
What is even more sad is that you have some talent. You can be a fine grappler... you are one. But you don't have the heart of a warrior or the courage. You don't have the mettle to confront your challenges face to face or to fight your battles on your own.
But as I said to Mr. Kole and Mr. Travers before defeating them both... I am finished bestowing guidance among those who seem lost. So now, Mr. Detson, I'm coming for you. Many people probably think you helped me out at Season's
Beatings. The fans, the critics, the suits... they think you assisted the wrestling enigma.
[Fang shakes his head and then buries his hands in his jacket sleeves for warmth.] Yet you know and I know that your sole intention was to mar the credibility of my victory. I find it ironic... you sought to take the integrity of that three-way battle and throw it out the window in the same manner that you claim to dominate the competition. You conquer your opponents with the assistance of two or three men on your side and take most dear pride in it. You know what I think?
I think that deep down inside Johnathan Detson knows he's a fraud... an imposter. At last, Mr. Detson, you have been unveiled. You are no man of violence and action. You are no warrior infused with blood lust and berserk spirit. All your life, you have never been anything more than a coward who looked up at the mediocre mass of humanity and wished you could become a part of it... but you never could fit in no matter how much you tried. I have never been a part of humanity, but I've never desired to be. The wrestling enigma has always been destined for greater things. You, Johnathan, are merely a misfit, a mental defect, a less than imperfect creation. You had to put on a big charade to impress those idiots in the stands. You made it look like you were a movie star. Somebody to be idolized. Now you lack even the strength to uphold this... _image_.
[Fang spits in disgust. After a brief pause, he continues.] Gaze into my eyes, Mr. Detson. Any others in the World Wrestling Organization who may doubt me, look there also...
[At this, the camera begins to close in on Fang's face. There is a gleam of frenzy in those eyes; an unhinged intensity. It is almost indefinable, but unmistakably there... the ruthlessness, the cold fearlessness, the tension before the explosion of violence, the look that makes you avoid the stranger on the midnight train... the window of madness. The wrestling enigma speaks.]
Gaze into my eyes, and you will witness the difference between _image_ and
_substance_. There is no falsehood in what I project to the world. I have never been anything less than honest. I forge no "angles" to fool the crowds and make myself out to be something that I am not. I am no gimmick.
I relish in every bone I break; in every act of violence and destruction I unleash. Pure evil does exist in this world, and it is standing right before you...
This is real, Mr. Detson. That is where we differ. And that... my unfortunate, naive, foolish friend... shall undoubtedly be your downfall. [Fang's devilish grin again creeps to the surface.] This time, Johnathan, you chose to meddle in the affairs of the wrong man. [After burning holes through the camera lens with his gaze for several moments,
Fang abruptly seems to calm a little, and perhaps, something of the old focused, clear minded Fang resurfaces.]
Now the rumble... the entire roster. This match is to determine the best; the
World Champion. There is not a man out there that doesn't want it. There are plenty who don't deserve it. I am probably near the top of that list, but the wrestling enimga will be damned if he doesn't go out there and put his heart, mind, body, and life on the line. And if you even want a chance at winning you better be able to say the same for yourselves, because when you face Fang...
... everything is at stake. Who _is_ the best? Only time will tell. All I can do now is... [Fang makes a welcoming gesture with his arms...] ... welome you _all_, with open arms, to your own destruction.
