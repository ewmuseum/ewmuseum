Dan Sweeny's appearance on The Price is Right
Bob Barkr: And now for next item up for bids. *F.I.N.E by Aerosmtih plays, as Dan Sweeny comes through the studio audience* Rod Roddy: Mr Dan Sweeny is modeling, and brand new spoortswatch!!! *As Rod goes through the boring explanation of the watch, Dan starts to talk to the contestants* Dan Sweeny: Hey kids. Contestant 1: Hey, yer the IRWF International Champion. And the only person that makes NEW entertaining!!
Dan Sweeny: Yer right Contestant 2: What's it like to be a pro wrestler, isn't it all just fake and yer just pumpin yerself full of steroids?
Dan Sweeny: You, are an idiot, see I'm entering the ICW now, true the other feds, are all great fun, but the ICW is more hardcore, and more real than anything I have ever seen!!!! Hey if you win this bid thing, can I go up and play for you?
Contestant 3: Sure!!! Dan Sweeny: I'll give ya anythng I win, hell I'm with The Ronin now, I'm loaded, but I just wanna play.
(The contestants go through he bidding process, and Contestant 3 wins) Contestant 3: I wanna have Dan Sweeny play for me. Bob Barker: Come on down!!!!! Dan Sweeny: *shakes Bob's hand* This is a childhood dream come true. Bob Barker: Ya gotta do me one favor Dan Sweeny: OK Bob Barker: *grabs a guy in a suit* This is the lawyer who's tryin to get me on a sexual harassment suit.
Dan Sweeny: But I saw you, you were all over those chicks. And, well, you aint the pretty boy that I am.
Bob Barker: Just Sweeny-sizer him!!!!!! Dan Sweeny: OK *picks lawyer up and Sweeny-sizers him* Bob Barker: Thanx, now we play........ Rod Roddy: You have a chance to win $25,000!!!!!!! Bob Barker: Let's go to the punch board. Dan Sweeny: 25,000? That's nothin, now that I'm with The Ronin. (runs over to contestant three, and hands him a wad of cash) Let's play a fun game!!! *snaps his finger*
Rod Roddy: It's time for Plinko!!!!!!! Dan Sweeny: Kick ass!!!!! Bob: Here is our lovely assistant Tina!!!!! Dan Sweeny: *wraps his arm around Tina, and heads up the stairs* Bob Barker: Wait, you haven't won any chips yet! Dan Sweeny: Thats what you think Tina: *reaches into the chest region of her dress, and pulls out 5 plinko chips* Bob Barker: So much for a fair game Dan Sweeny: Bobbo, I'm droppin the first chip. Bob Barker: *flips off Dan Sweeny* Dan Sweeny: *hands chips back to Tina* Now, ya done it!! Sexual harassment, hell everyone does it. But flippin me off means death, you may have kicked Adam Sandlers ass, but I aint The Water Boy, I'm ten times the man he is!!!!!
Bob Barker: Bring it on!!!!!! Dan Sweeny: *takes a Plinko chip, and throws it at Bob Barkers head, and hits* Bob Barker: Don't sing it or fling it, bring it Dan Sweny: *climbs on top of the Plinko board, and moonsaults down on to
Bob knockin him out cold*
*The studio audience goes crazy* Dan Sweeny: Look for me later ICW, I'm here, and I aint leavin quietly!!!! *high pitched vice* You so Right Danny Boy!!!!!! *Please email me with what ya think, whether good or bad, or what I can do better Thanx Bill *
