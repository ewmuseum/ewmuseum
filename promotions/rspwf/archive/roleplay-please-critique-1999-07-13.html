ROLEPLAY - Please Critique!

13 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
((The scene is "The Late Show with David Letterman". It has just come back from break and the audience is going nuts as the camera pans into David Letterman, sitting at his desk. At the far end of the couch, sits Liam Neeson. David begins to talk.....))
LETTERMAN: Now just before we bring out our next guest, I'd like to say something to the US Women's Soccer Team.....you know, if you ever need a place to crash or you know....a little consolement, you come talk to me because I am there for you.
((The audience forces out a laugh at the joke he's made almost a hundred times already during this show. Paul Schaffer also joins in, making the joke seem funnier than it really is......))
SCHAFFER: AH HA HA. Yes.....yes. The Soccer Team....they'll come see you. Ha ha ha...... LETTERMAN: Yes......yes they will. ((Audience laughs again.......)) Anyways, it's time to bring out our next guest, he is a professional wrestler for the Sunflower State Wrestling organization, he calls himself.....
((Checks his card and begins to talk, over-emphasizing the words....)) THE HARD-CORE I-CON. Hey.....maybe this guy isn't a pro wrestler after all eh? ((Audience begins to laugh insanely at the rather stupid joke....)) Here is.......TRACKER!!!!! ((The audience explodes with cheers as the band rips into a rendition of "Evenflow" by Pearl Jam and Tracker comes out from backstage. He is wearing a pair of khaki shorts and a black polo t-shirt. His hair is tied back behind his head and he has a pair of sunglasses on top of his head. He walks over to the desk, Tracker shakes Liam Neeson's hand and Dave gets up and shakes his hand. He whispers something in his ear and Tracker laughs. Tracker sits down and the interview begins.....))
LETTERMAN: So Tracker....uhm.....is that you real name? Tracker? TRACKER: As far as this interview goes it is. ((Audiences "oooh"s. Letterman laughs it off....)) L-MAN: So how are things going with your career? I heard something about you winning the Ultimate Fighting title?
TRACKER: Yeah that's right. I won it at the last Sunday Night Blast show. L-MAN: I heard about how brutal of a match-up that was, but let's be honest here.....wrestlings fake right? C'mon. You can be level with me. I won't tell anyone. The audience won't tell anyone. You might have to kill Paul afterwards but that's ok!
((Audience and Schaffer laugh as Tracker smiles and puts his hand to his chin, trying to think of the right way to put it.....))
TRACKER: Let's just say....the kind of wrestling I do is real. L-MAN: Yes....for the folks that don't know, Tracker here is what they call a....a......a HARD-CORE wrestler. So you know.....you could like fall off of the Empire State Building and survive as long as their was a wooden table on the ground to break your fall, correct?
TRACKER: (Half laughing) I'd say so yeah. L-MAN: Now, I've also heard that you are a former Navy SEAL, is that right? TRACKER: Yep....I spent about 2 years with the Navy, most of it was as part of a SEAL team.
L-MAN: So what's this thing I've heard about.....a "shoot" fight? TRACKER: Well, if you want to know what a shoot fight is, just get the next SSW Pay Per View, "Wicked Games" and watch my match against
Chris Diorio.....we're gonna be fighting in a Vale-Tudo match. What that basically means is you have to make your opponent submit using kicks, punchs, chokes and submission holds. There are a few rules though.....no biting, no hair pulling.....
L-MAN: So basically your're not allowed to fight like a girl? ((Audience laughs.)) TRACKER: Well...exactly. L-MAN: So you have a title defense before that don't you? TRACKER: Yeah....I gotta face this idiot that calls himself Blaidd. Somehow he got the spot of top contender. L-MAN: Blaidd huh? Does he fight vampires on the side? ((Audience forces out a weak laugh.)) TRACKER: Hell, probably. He sucks so bad in the ring he probably needs a second job, know what I mean?
((Liam Neeson for some reason starts to talk....)) NEESON: You know, in my new movie, "The Haunting" we all have to fight demons.... L-MAN: Hey now, you got your time to talk, let the HARD-CORE I-CON have his spot. ((Audience and Liam laugh.....)) TRACKER: I thought your new movie was Star Wars? NEESON: Well, I am just so good I do two movies at a time! TRACKER: Yeah....kinda like me when I fight in a couple different feds at the same time. I'm just so damn good everyone wants a part of me!
L-MAN: I also work another TV Show.....over there on the Playboy network with the US WOMEN'S SOCCER TEAM!
((The entire audience, Paul Schaffer, Liam Neeson, and Tracker look at Letterman, confused, trying to figure out what the hell he is talking about....))
L-MAN: Well....you know.....the.....uhm.......women's Soccer team....uh......WELL! It's time to go to commercial, when we come back we'll be joined by our musical guest......the BARENAKED LADIES!
((The show goes to commerical and an SSW cameraman picks up the conversation between Tracker and Letterman, as they start to whisper.....))
TRACKER: What the hell was that all about? I still had 5 minutes left. L-MAN: I know, that joke bombed though, I had to get out of it. Hey....I'll give you a canned ham to make up for it. ((Letterman pulls out a canned ham from under his desk and hands it to him...)) TRACKER: I don't want no god damned ham! ((Tracker tosses it into the crowd, hitting an overweight "Letterman Rules" t-shirt wearing fan in the head, causing him to be knocked unconscious and fall backwards, crushing an 80 year old woman, she screams "Someone get this fat f**k off of me" as people rush over to help....))
L-MAN: What the hell!? Are you NUTS?! TRACKER: Well......yeah, I thought you knew that! Haha. ((Letterman stands up and screams at Tracker, just as the show comes back on the air....)) L-MAN: GET THE F**K OFF MY SHOW! ((A hush falls over the audience and camera crew. Letterman suddenly realizes they have come back from break. Lettermen quickly introduces the band....))
L-MAN: Yeah......uhm.......playing the new single "If You Fall" from their new album, here are....THE BARENAKED LADIES!
((The camera pans over to the band, who are staring, mouths wide open at Letterman. Letterman re-introduces the band but they still stand there staring blankly at him. Lettermen starts to talk....))
L-MAN: Well, that's all the time we have folks! Good night! ((Tracker slips into the backstage area as the show goes off the air, where the SSW camera crew catchs up to him....))
TRACKER: Well damn, that didn't go to well. I hope I didn't ruin his career......hmmmm......maybe I'll go see what Conan O'Brien is up to.
Blaidd, I am so looking forward to our match, I beat you once and I damn sure will beat you again. Sure you are probably saying to yourself, "But Tracker needs Diorio to help him beat me" Well no way,
AH-AH, you are wrong my man, I had the match well in hand and I was just about to end it when that punk decided to make the win a tainted one, but I guess I'll redeem myself this Sunday on Blast, see you in the ring Blaidd.....
((Tracker runs away as he sees Letterman coming his way with an armful of canned hams. Letterman tosses a ham at Tracker but it hits the camera and the scene abruptly switchs to static as the camera falls to the ground......))
