[Flash] [FBK in ICW] Critique Please...

1 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ SCENE: The United Center in Chicago, Illinois. The camera shows the outside of the arena where fans are packing into the United Center, no crowd like when
Michael Jordan was still around though. The camera then opens up to the inside of the arena where the Charlotte Hornets and home team, Chicago Bulls are warming up. The arena doesn't have the electricity that it did during the championship era of the Bulls but many people have come to see if the Bulls can turn around their losing streak and win a few games. The camera pans around the front row section and then comes to a stop on someone familiar. The camera zooms in a reveals ICW wrestler and newest Devestation Inc. member, Full Blood
Killah...]
[ APEARANCE: FBK has on pair of tan Aeropostale cargo pants along with a pair of reddish brown Timberland boots, his shirt is a brown and red plaided Old
Navy button down. His hair is gelled forward and his side burns are slightly grown out, about down to the end of his ear. He's chewing gum and blows a bubble every few seconds...]
[ The camera begins to approach the wrestling prodigy and kneels down in front of him. FBK turns his head and sees the camera and looks surprised at first but then sees the ICW logo on the man's shirt. FBK begins...]
FULL BLOOD KILLAH: What the hell is this? Da Killah is kinda about to watch a basketball game here in Chicago. Y'know, da Bulls, da Bears...DA KILLAH! We all make this state great. So please, be gone.
[ FBK turns his head and continues to watch warm-ups. The camera doesn't budge though, just staring at FBK's face. FBK seems to get a little pissed off. He turns around a few moments later after the camera refuses to move...]
FULL BLOOD KILLAH: You've got to be the annoying piece of camel crap da Killah has ever met since he's blessed the earth by being born. This isn't the place for an interivew, follow me...
[ FBK gets up and walks from courtside, and walks up a flight of stairs. He walks around the food vendors vicinity and makes a right into a room. The camera walks inside and is surrounded by sinks, stalls, and urinals...a bathroom. Its empty and FBK locks the door so there will be no interruptions.
FBK paces back and forth around the bathroom and then stops and looks at the camera. He takes a breath and is about to speak, but then continues to pace. He then stops and speaks simultaneously...]
FULL BLOOD KILLAH: D.B. Bugg you have got to be the biggest scum walking on the earth today. You think your some friggin Einstein by thinking da Killah would turn on you, well guess what genius...it wasn't going to happen. But now since you gave da Killah that spinning neck breaker, you knocked some sense into him.
Why would da Killah wanna be associated with a _LOSER JAY-BRONE_ like yourself?! Bugg, just because you wear the International Title doesn't make you the best thing since sliced bread...because da Killah occupies that position.
Bugg, if da Killah wanted he could walk the aisle, stand toe to toe with you and smack the stupid right out of you. Bugg, that International Title could be da Killah's but its not in his interest, not just yet. Why would da Killah say that? Reason one, because he don't need some piece of tin around his waist to prove he's the greatest wrestler to ever set foot in a wrestling ring. Reason two, da Killah likes to climb the ladder...he'll start with the lower titles and work his way up, it brings more fun and suspense. But Bugg, da Killah is getting his hands on you this Friday anyways and as the old saying goes, revenge is a bitch and its gonna be the same for you this Friday. Bugg, along with DI, da Killah is gonna beat the living _CRAP_ straight out of you. This ain't no little feud, this has become personal since you decided to act tough last Fallout and deliver that neckbreaker to me...call the hospital Dumb Bitch
Bugg, because when you're fighting FBK...
...._YOU'RE AS GOOD AS CHECKED IN_!!! And that goes for your cronies, Walavan and Lionheart as well, bitches are gonna die... [ FBK then moves across and sits on the sink. He stares at the ground deeply, in a train of thought. He then looks up at the camera...]
FULL BLOOD KILLAH: But da Killah can't look too far ahead, this Tuesday he has a _BIG_ match-up against former TV Champ, Konrad Curze. Curze, da Killah knows you're a former TV Champion and he also knows you have a seven and four record here in ICW. Well, get ready for number five in the loss column. Curze, you see, when you go up against the most exhilarating, breath-taking wrestler in the world...you just don't measure up...in more ways than one biznitch! Curze, there's something goin' around that da Killah just laughed his ass off when he heard about it. Da Killah heard that you are a former TV Champion, but he also heard the _ONLY_ reason you won is because the champion at the time, Cyris, had to leave the federation for personal reasons! [begins to break out into laughter] You won because your opponent went to James Pillow and told him he had family problems so wanted to job and take some time off! He couldn't wrestle anymore because it was affecting his life so he practically _GAVE_ the title to you! Curze, da Killah don't even consider you a former ICW Television
Champion just for that, it makes him sick to his stomach, but pretty damn funny! And Curze, its not gonna be that easy when you face da Killah because he doesn't job...he goes a hundred and ten percent, gives beatings as he sees them and brings chairs and tables into his act. You don't stand a chance in hell against a man with the skill of da Killah because he's just that freakin' good!
[FBK licks his finger and then touches his leg with the finger] OUCH! You ain't stopping da Killah, he's on _FIRE_! Curze, da Killah's gonna rip you a new
_AS[BLEEP]LE_ this Tuesday and there ain't nothing you can do except run!
[ FBK begins to chuckle under his breath and looks into the camera with an arrogant grin. He then suddenly stops laughing as he just thought of something.
He proceeds...] FULL BLOOD KILLAH: Da Killah just remembered something that evolved this past
Fallout. What da Killah is saying is that this past Friday, a Continental Title match was signed for Spring Slamathon and da Killah had his eyes set on that title. The part that even makes da Killah sick to his stomach is how those jay-brones Nyghtstorm, Chuck Williams, and Jason Williams get the title shot.
It boggles da Killah's mind for crying out loud! Da Killah beat each of their asses up and down the ring in a "mini" battle royal on Fallout about two weeks ago. And what does da Killah get? Diddley dick! James Pillow, da Killah sure as hell hopes your listening because he wants a shot at the Continental Title. FBK could beat the living _HELL_ out of any one of those four jay-brones involved in that match. Hell, he already beat three of them in the battle royal! But they are more deserving? I _DEMAND_ to be rewarded for that win! Da Killah is the high priced ticket, he's what the fans come to see...not John Beverly, not
Jason Nyghstorm, not Jason Williams, not Chuck Williams...the one and the only
F-B-K! Hell, throw da Killah in that match at the pay per view, what will be so bad about it? You'll actually get ratings for a Continenal Title match? Thats the bright side! Beverly, da Killah's calling you out...are you too
_PU[BLEEP]Y_! Or do you have some balls to answer a man _DESERVING_ of a title shot, unlike those three other pieces of camel crap. Da Killah can guaran-damn well-tee victory over you, unlike those three other dumpster doo-doo's that couldn't lace your boots. Hell, Pillow, da Killah will beat Nyghtstorm, Knux, and Mr. Wonderful again if you want him to...and da Killah can save you the time later on, just add another check in FBK's win column because thats the way it'll _ALWAYS_ be!
[ FBK pauses for a few moments and then a few knocks at the bathroom door are heard...] VOICE: HEY! Open the god damn door! FULL BLOOD KILLAH: Shut your mouth you pile of dirt, watch the game, da Killah doesn't need to be interupted.
VOICE: Why I outta...once you open the door there's gonna be trouble for you pal! FULL BLOOD KILLAH: We'll see about that...anyway, the guys are getting a little ticked and da Killah shouldn't have locked the door so he's gonna close this one out. Well, its an honor to be part of the _BEST_ stable here in ICW,
Devestation Inc. And the Supreme Dream Team are full of wanna-be's and posers that don't deserve to be in the same ring as da Killah and the rest of DI. This
Friday ain't gonna be nothing different...da Killah's gonna slap dem bitches up! And with Grayson and Case in FBK's corner, the only question going through fans minds is...how long will SDT go before getting their shoulders pinned to the mat for the one, two, three! And Curze, you were lucky to win the TV Title, but your luck has run out once that match has been signed between yourself and the plain and simple shit of ICW, Full Blood Killah. Curze, you're a big boy, six eleven, three hundred twenty pounds...but as the old saying goes...the bigger they are...
..._THE_... ..._HARDER_... ..._THEY_... ..._FALL_!!! See you in the ring and da Killah can guarantee it won't be a pleasant experience for you...
[ FBK then opens the door and in the doorway stands a man about five feet, five inches tall with glasses. FBK just laughs and shoves him to the side. The camera looks at the short guy's pants and they have a stain, indicating he just wet his pants when he saw FBK.... FADE ]
