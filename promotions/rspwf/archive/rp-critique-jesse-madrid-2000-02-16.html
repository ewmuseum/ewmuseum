[RP Critique] Jesse Madrid

16 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is probably my first RP posted to this board, so I am expecting anything. What you are about to read is a RP in which I chalenged the current federations champion. Any comments will be appreciated. Thanks
[Fade In] ::Scene:: A women passes by the camera, a very beautiful women. Her entire body is very delectable. She is holding onto a tray, and on that tray are a few drinks.
She walks to a table near a window, and there, sitting on a stool, is Jesse
Madrid. She hesitantly walks to his table. With a big smile, she sets the tray down on the table, and removes a glass from the tray. She looks at Jesse and she tries to speak, but only breath comes out of her mouth. She smiles, and she then speaks
Waitress: [shy] here is your...I mean this is your...[begins to shake] [Madrid interupts her and speaks] Jesse Madrid: [smiles] Don't be intimidated by me miss. It is I that should be intimidated of you. Your eyes [pauses] they sparkle like the nile. Your smile, it is brighter than a flame. Please, don't let me be a distraction.
[The women takes a deep breath and sighs. She then collects herself and she places the glass on the table]
Waitress: [sentiously] Vodka martini. A strong drink, that defines a stronger man. [Jesse picks up the glass, and slowly drinks the entire content. He looks to the women, and he licks his lips. He speaks]
Jesse Madrid: If I were to send you flowers [hesitates] let me say this again. If I were to let you place your lips apon mine...would you be gratefull? [The waitress bites her lower lip, and looks Jesse into the eyes. She licks her lips and she speaks]
Waitress: [softly] Yes [The waitress leans down as to kiss Madrid and he holds his hand up, and turns his head. The waitress looks at him, with pain in her eyes. Her voice begins to tighten up and her eyes begin to water.]
Jesse Madrid: You can go now [The waitress stumbles and picks up the tray. Her lips begin to shake, and her body begins to weaken. She turns and she walks away. Madrid lights up a cigar, and he takes a single puff. He exhales the smoke, and he smells the potent aroma from the cigar. He speaks]
Jesse Madrid: There is a man in the UwF that I respect to the fullest. A man that has accomplished much in his career. That man [hesitates] "The Franchise"
Tommy Stylz. [sighs] Stylz, as much as I respect you, I know that if I want to gain your respect, we are going to have to step into the ring, and fight.
[puffs cigar] I am ready to fight you. I feel it in my entire body. I want to face you just this once. No stipulation, no title shot, just a match.
[Madrid rolls his neck as to pop it. He then takes the olive from his glass and eats it.] Jesse Madrid: You can accept my chalenge, or you can deny it. But I think that you want to fight me as badly as I want to fight you. I know that in the back of your head, you want to be the first man to beat Jesse Madrid. Well,
[smiles] I am giving you your chance. But a fair warning; Do not think for a single second that you are better than me. Expect to be beaten. For as good as you may be, there is nothing that will prepare you for me. No match that you have ever had will amount to the match that I will give you. There will be no explanation for the pain that I will bring unto you. If you do not heed that warning [pauses] I will beat you. If you want to prove me wrong, then fight me. Show me that you are the better man. [puffs cigar] I am ready...Are you?
[Madrid smells the cigar, and he looks out of the window] [Fade out] -=([Jesse Madrid])=- [1 5 - 0 ] [ GxWp Tag Champ ] [UwF IC Champion ] [Soon to be-UwF World Champion ] (XxBadCompanyxX)-Jesse Madrid
