26 February 1999

I know this is kinda long, its from an AoL fed though so bear with me, please email me and tell me what you think.
[Fade In] [The scene opens up in an old broked down house. It looks as if it hasn't been inhabited in years, things from the family that once lived there still remain.
Pictures, furniture, still remain, though covered by dust and cob webs. The house is in total darkness, with the exception of the moon light coming in through holes in the roof. Footsteps can be heard coming down the hall, a long shadow can be seen approaching the door. Then, The Reaper comes through the doorway, the lack of light casts an eerie glow on him. He begins to speak]
The Reaper: Time has passed quickly hasn't it? It seems like only yesterday that The Reaper was still a mortal as you all know them.
Running through these halls, playing in these yards, this was my place to dream what other little kids never dared. This....this is the former home of The
Reaper incase you haven't guessed that by now. Much different from the dark and secluded place that you all have seen as my present home eh? This is the place that many men have grown to hate, for this is the place that The Reaper was spawned. What events in my life possessed me to become a man of the darkness? What possessed me to seek and destroy all men who oppose me? I guess you all are entitled to know.
[The Reaper walks over to the front door, and kneels down, next to a large red stain on the floor]
The Reaper: This has so much to do with it. Look at that, what does it look like to you? It is human blood. It is the blood of my mother. For it was here, this very spot that my father decided to take it upon himself to end my mother's life, and I stood in that doorway right over there, and watched, in pure horror, not knowing what to do. He stabbed her several times, and ran out the door, left her laying here, I ran to her side, and my mother died in my arms on that night. So where do you all tie into this?
Well, I knew at that very point, if I could not trust my father, I could trust no man. And at that moment, the love I once had for my father, all turned into hatred, and I knew that if I must hate my father, I must hate all men. That was the night that did it, it was then that I become the being I am today, and it was that night that now possess me to destroy everyone! So do you see now
Warrior? WHat you thought was some kind of a religious belief of mine, was really a tragic event in my life, that shaped the way my mind would work forever. You say I serve the devil, I serve no one but myself Warrior! Now do you see why I am this way Warrior? Now can you understand why
I_MUST_destroy you? You shall pay for something you had nothing to do with. I will reveal a secret about myself, when I step into the ring, I picture my coward of a father standing there, facing me man to coward. And when I am attacking you, it is the only thing that I can do until the day I find my father, for he was never captured, but he cannot run forever, and Warrior neither can you.
The Reaper: The Warrior of Justice, to me, there is no justice in this world, for I experienced that first hand. For if there were justice, what happened to my family would never have happened, and thus, you would be safe right now, but you aren't Warrior, you must face The Reaper as I aim to destroy you! When you step into the ring, you shall see darkness there, and nothing more. Deep into that darkness peering, long you shall stand wondering, fearing, doubting, dreamin dreams no mortal should ever have to dream. Not knowing what is going to happen next. Not knowing whether to stand your ground, or to try to get away some how. Then the attack shall begin, and when your movements cease, and your breaths are more shallow, and the life seems to be draining out of your body, then, and only then shall it stop
Warrior. When you can no longer even lift your head, then it will be over, but you still must feel The DeathSpike. Warrior, it never really got personal between us, this is just the way I approach every match, and you now know those reasons why. Now you know what happens to me, when I make that transformation from the man I am now, to the animal I am in the ring. Yet, you do not perceive the futility of all your attempts to stop me, and bring justice, for you cannot stop me Warrior. You talk about how I'm the one following you around, like you are trying to get into my mind. You cannot rattle a mind that darkness lurks in, as if an inherent positive quality, powered forth upon all objects of the moral and physical universe in one unceasing radiation of destruction. I know that this is all a little too much for you to get, The
Reaper talks, you listen, but you don't understand. The words sink in, you just don't get the meaning within them. But in your mind, you have all the answers. The problem is, that you don't know the question. You thought you had it all figured out, but then I dropped that bomb on you about my past, and why I am this way, and that will catch you off guard. The final days are at hand. The hour glass that is your title reign is now grasping to its last few grains of sand. You are now in my hands Warrior, nothing that you can do will stop me from having my way with you. Should I choose to let you keep your life, then that is the way it is going to happen. If I choose to completely destroy you, then it shall happen. Do you not see that you cannot pull this one off? Do you not see that even the man that is famous for beating the odds loses sometimes? All your complaints, all your moaning, and groaning about the way things have gone, it is all pointless. The president did his best to protect you and keep The Reaper away. But THe Reaper would not be denied, I issued a challenge, and the president refused to let it go through. But then you issued a challenge of your own. What was the motive behind that one? Was it because you wanted to face The Reaper on your terms? Or was it because you knew Clark wouldn't allow it? Warrior, very legitimate questions, which I am sure that you shall have an excuse for during the next time you speak. I will be waiting for this one.
[The Reaper stands, and walks over to a chair, and sits down] The Reaper: The showdown that everyone has been waiting for. The one that the CCW has wanted to see for a long time now, is finally happening, and is now one day away. Tomorrow night Warrior, you and I will step into the ring and do battle against one another, in the best match the CCW has ever seen. Unfortunately, their favorite son shall be destroyed in this match. Everyone in the CCW thought no one could knock you off as champion, and that may have been true. But then The Reaper came, and the doubts of your staying power then came up. The doutbs of whether you could weather this storm or not were coming up all over the place. And I know you didn't like that one bit, because for so long you were the top dog in the CCW, and it looked as though you would stay that way, with not many serious challengers in the CCW after Shane Adams left. And now The Reaper is coming onto your turf, and challenging you, and I understand you getting upset about that, who wouldn't have gotten upset? But that still doesn't solve your problem Warrior. Nothing can. Nothing will ever solve what is in my past, i know that, and I know that you are going to come out and that is going to be one of the first things that you say, but atleast I can bring a little better feeling into my soul by doing what I do. You aren't the one who did it, neither is anyone else that I've ever faced, but you all face the same fate, and that is to pay for the crime that happened to my mother. It must be done, there is just no other way around that for me personally Warrior, I have to do what I do, or I couldn't live with myself. Granted what I do may not be the best thing, but it is the only thing
I can do like I said earlier. Warrior, this one will be one that you won't want to tell your grand children about, or maybe you will. Some day down the road, you can pull your little grand children up on your knee, and tell them about the time you stepped into the ring with the man of the darkness, and you would like to be able to say how you came away with a win, but you can't, you won't. You can compare my arrogance to Shane Adams all you want to, you can speak of your victory over Shane Adams all you want, that means nothing to me.
I've beaten The Headliner twice, that has no bearing on this match whatsoever. But I guess you're just trying to relive your glory days, since there won't be too many more of them for you to remember when you retire, for the rest of your career will be The Reaper running the show! Sad but true, I'm your worst nightmares made real, I'm the pain that you will feel. Doubt me if you dare, but you won't win that way, nor any other way, this is just something that I have to do. You chose to test me, and for that there is no excuse, and there is no turning back, for once you challenge me or accept my challenge, your soul is damned until The Reaper takes you out, and then you shall forever live with the pain of the memories of your destruction by my hands. Think the darkness can't hurt you, and you are so very wrong Warrior, what you don't know, can hurt you!
The Reaper: Dreams of war, dreams of liars, dreams of dragon's fires, nothing shall be safe on this night. Say your prayers little one, don't forget to include everyone. These are the things that you shall see, these are the things that you must do, as your final days as champion are at hand. The end is in sight for you now Warrior. Look deep into the words I say, dig into them, but do not look too far, for I feel that you shall miss their true meaning. It seems that for the most part, you already have missed what it was meant to be. You try perhaps too hard to look within me, and fine some sort of hidden meaning. You think you have The Reaper figured out, when really you have barely begun to scratch the surface. you are off somewhere, lost in a sea of confusion, you aren't going to do it Warrior. You think that you are, for you think you know me [chuckles] Perhaps The Reaper's prophecy has already begun to be fulfilled? Perhaps I have dug into the chinks of your armor. You were a foot to even think you could beat me at my own game. For others have wrestled within the cell before, but I take their carnage, and I take their violence, and I raise it a notch. A cage match against me is far from normal. For I am not your normal wrestler. You shall find that out at
CyberFest 99 Warrior, I am the realization of your worst fears in this sport.
you never should have signed on that dotted line, that was when you sealed your fate. The choies we make in life sometimes give us more then we bargain for, but we all must live with our choices. You've chosen to face the most destructive man in wrestling history. You try to make yourself believe that you're on my level, soon you shall find that you aren't. Live with that fact
Warrior! We each walk the path that we've chosen. The fact that this match is happening is not my fault Warrior. But the events that happen in the match, the pain, the destruction that awaits you, that is my falut. Everyting that happens to you, lay the blame on me. For it is I that will allow myself to destroy you. But what choice do I have? You think you can stop me, you think that you will be able to stop the domination of the CCW? So now, now I must fight you. I didn't necessarly want to, but I have no choice. I tried to explain, but you didn't hear me though, and now......*May Darkness Imprison you. Beware and be warned for nothing else matters, and nothing is safe again!!* Welcome to the DarkSide!!!!
[The Reaper stands up, and walks out of the room] [Fade out] * Quote enclosed by astericks was used courtesy of Chuck, aka Mystic*
