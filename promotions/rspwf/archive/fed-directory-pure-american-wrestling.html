Pure American Wrestling (PAW) Website: www.efeduniverse.com/paw/ Owner: [name withheld] Description: PAW is a national wrestling promotion based on the East Coast of America. Their first show was back in mid June, 2002, in which VD Vitrone defeated Alex Martinez to become the first National champion. PAW is ran, onscreen, by co-owners Anthony Virtue & Brian J. Blottie. We have a very talented roster with great characters. We are a bi-weekly fed with two shows - Fireside: the promo/RP show and Unleashed : the actual wrestling show. Both these shows come out on alternate Thursdays. Keynote Members: Some of our top names, character-wise include: 'Vindicator' VD Vitrone; Johnny Axis; 'Black Jesuz' Dontae Wood; Andrew 'Flash' Tucker; 'Breathtaking' Brendan Blade; Simon Bach; Alex Hawkins; The Quiet One; Dave Fresco; Fishboy; Shane Diamond; Federation Champion: 'Vindicator' VD Vitrone Flash Show Descripition: In-between the last and forthcoming Unleashed, PAW presents 'Fireside' - the bi-weekly RP show. Hosted by either Anthony Virtue or Brian J. Blottie, they hype upcoming shows, air the RPs/promo's of the PAW's wrestlers and comment on them afterwards. Regular Show Description: PAW is a bi-weekly promotion which has two shows - Fireside (RP show) and Unleashed (wrestling show). Unleashed has approximately 5-6 matches, most of which short in size, packed with solid action and helping to progress angles. Segments are a main fact PPV Description: PAW has four PPV's per year - one every three months. Our first PPV will happen mid-late September. As of now, it is still unannounced and without a date or name.
