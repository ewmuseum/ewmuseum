<The scene opens up to the desert of Las Vegas, Nevada. It is day time as the heat is unbearable to the point where you can almost see it in the air. The camera continues moving around until we see a sign standing going all the way above the road as it reads "YOU ARE NOW LEAVING LAS VEGAS!" The camera slowly zooms in onto the sign then fades out. . >
<The camera comes back into focus now sitting on the opposite side of the sign, outside the city limits of Las Vegas. The camera moves around going to look back into the city but we see a figure standing next to the sign, his back is to us as the camera pans in on him. He glances over his shoulder as we see a tooth pick jutting out from his mouth. He shoots a crooked grin then slowly turns to the camera. . >
<This man has is wearing a pair of baggy DKNY blue jeans and a gray Everlast sweatshirt with their logo on the front of it in white. He also has on pair of black Converse shoes, a silver cross chain,and a pair of guess sunglasses. The man crosses one leg over the other and flicks the tooth pick around in his mouth before he begins to speak. . >
MAN: Well. . .the AIWA, who would've thought that the likes of me would make it here? Growing up in the city of sin. .the city of lust, Las Vegas only to break away and go make a name for himself. . .speaking of names, let me introduce myself, the names Munroe. .Alexander Munroe, but you will get to know me as the
"SHOW STEALER!" <Munroe shoots an obvious cocky grin to the camera as he pulls the tooth pick from his mouth and tosses it on the ground. . .>
"SHOW STEALER" ALEXANDER MUNROE:AIWA, I've been waiting and watching since the doors opened and now it's time for me to step through those very doors and decimate and destroy every bit of competition you can throw at me. Give me your tired, your poor, your hungry. .your World Champions and I'll give you back a defeated man.
<Munroe takes his sunglasses off, immediately squinting due to the extremely bright sun. He clips the glasses to his shirt collar and leans his head back against the sign pole. . .>
"SHOW STEALER" ALEXANDER MUNROE: I'm going to keep this short and sweet as I do not have much to speak on at this point in time. I'm merely here to let every know that I have arrived. .and hell hath no fury. . .like that of the Show
Stealer. So each and everyone of those here in the AIWA better watch there backs. .because I'm here to *STEAL THE SHOW!*
<Munroe flashes another crooker grin at the camera as he pulls the sunglasses from his collar and places them over his eyes, he then turns and begins to walk down the long winding road leading away from Las Vegas as the camera slowly follows and then. . .Fades To Black. . .>
