The Hottest Free Agent in The Buiness??

26 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello everyone, my name is Michael, and I handle "Fabulous" Freddy Floyd. Freddy currently competes in [name withheld] LVPWA, and is looking for one more federation to compete in. I have posted an In-ring below for your viewing pleasure. So if you think your federation has what it takes, then e-mail me at
[detail withheld] or IM me (same name) and we'll talk. Thanks a lot. - Mike, handler of "Fabulous" Freddy Floyd [ LVPWA 2-0-0 ] ............................................................. V/O Good . . . Evil . . . Good . . . Evil . . . { Lights Inside of the arena fade to darkness, and a blue Spotlight Shines downs onto the ramp } Cue - Jekyll and Hyde's "Good and Evil" # Good and Evil # # And There Merits Too # # Men Has Argued Through History # # As Well They Should, My Philosophy ... { The Curtain is pulled back, and from out behind it steps "Fantastic" Florence Floyd. She wears a black short leather jumper, and a pair of high black leather boots. Her long hair is tied back in a pony tail, and she removes her new designer sunglasses to wink at the camera }
# And Child Can See # # Good Is Evil .... # { Florence steps aside, and following her out is her brother "Fabulous" Freddy Floyd. Floyd wears his blue wrestling tights and a new "Fabulous" One T-shirt. Freddy takes Florences hand and gives her a twirl in front of the camera }
# And Therefore all Evil is . . . . GOOD # { Video Wall Comes To Life } _ -= "F A B U L O U S" =-_ F R E D D Y F L O Y D + _ -= "F A N T A S T I C: =-_ F L O R E N C E F L O Y D { The Two begin to make their way down the isle, and taunt the fans all along the way. Freddy gets right up in their faces, and Florence blows kisses to them }
{ Freddy walks up the steps and holds the ropes open as his sister makes her way up and into the ring. Once both inside Florence walks around the ring and blows kisses out to the fans, as Freddy mounts the second turnbuckle and draws in the huge chorus of boos from the fans.
Freddy gets down and makes his way over to the ring announcer position and rips the microphone out of Rick
Milton's hand as the music fades } "Fabulous" FF: "Well morons, we meet again. And this time its right here on <ahem> beatiful, and a <ahem> charming, and most of all ... <ahem> inviting Las Vegas Strip. You know the thing I like most about Las vegas ... leaving! And thats exactly what I plan to do as soon as I win my match up tonight, hop and plane and head back to a more ... um whats the word ... oh yes, civilized enviornment. As a matter of fact, I would be content if I never had to set foot into the state of Nevada again. But its the price one must pay to be the best. I mean, its not my fault that this federation has decided to make its base in the filthiest city on earth, I just do what I do, where I have to do it to bring home the money and the gold, my two favorite things. Which brings me to an excellent point. I mean, I really don't see why I wasn't granted a world title shot right off the bat. All of the wrestlers here certainly realize I am far superior, and really all I would doing is granting them a favor, by saving them from the trouble of me having to beat them up. And wrestling me is something I wouldn't want to wish apon anyone. Don't you agree morons?"
{Crowd lets out a defening burst of heel heat, as they scream and begin to throw garbage into the ring.
Freddy extends him arms as if to welcome the boos } "Fabulous" FF: "Yes idiots, I know you love me. And that is why right here tonight, live on Heavy Metal Monday, I am going to grace you all with the sight of seeing me perform live here tonight in this very ring. I do however extend a small, make that very small, apology to all of you
Las Vegas Losers, becasue you see I will not be wrestling a high profile match up tonight, oh no. Instead I will have to make do with two of the more over-rated wrestlers I have ever come in contact with in my days.
I mean really, Sean, who the hell do you think your kidding with all of the Superstar business. Your nothing more than a deadbeat loser, who is trying to ride on coatails. And this LVPWA is really stretching it asking me to take care of some punk named Chris Del Whatever the hell is name is. Really, lets quickly analyze this situation.
"The Ultimate Male" as he calls himself. trying winning a a match first buddy, then we'll talk about you being the
Ultimate male. As a matter of fact Chris, I may just have to confront you about that before our match up. Yeah, you and I will have a little pre-match chat, and you can try and explain to me what makes you the "The Ultimate Male"
casue right now, I'm not convinced. As a matter of fact morons ... "Yes all of you idiots sitting in the chairs out here, thats right I am once again requesting the useage of your short and fragile attention spans. I know I left you hanging for a minute while I talked to my opponents, but its back to us again ... ok."
"I am going to go out on a limb and make a prediction that each and everyone of you can take to the bank ...
On second thought, you losers would probably be deffered from the back, so you can all can just take this promice and stick in your back pocket for safe keeping. It goes like this. Within three months times, thats right folks, three months time, I "Fabulous" Freddy Floyd will stand before you as the Las Vegas Pro-Wrestling Association World
Heavyweight Champion, and I start the proof right here tonight. It may sound like a pretty big claim, but when you are as good as I am, keeping a promice like that may in fact come even earlier than predicted. I know there are doubters out there, but I have been doubted my whole career, and that hasn't stopped me from winning title after title, after title."
"Come of Florence, we have some business to take care of." Cue - Jekyll and Hyde's "Good and Evil" { Together Freddy and Florence leave the ring. Freddy whispers something into Florences ear and she runs ahead.
Freddy flashes a manical smile at the camera before he himself walks through the curtain and back into the dressing room area }
