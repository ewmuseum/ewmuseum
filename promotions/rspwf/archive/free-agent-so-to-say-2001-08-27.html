Hey people. I quit e-wrestling about a year to go due to the fact that one..I joined feds that were just opening cause I wanted to be part of something as it got better, but what ended up happening was an owner getting tired of being an owner and closing them down..and two.. I was tired of seeing owners afraid to job thier main superstars,afraid the handler would leave.. So.. Its been a year and I realised I miss this.. And Im wanting back in.. But, id like a decent fed to come back into.. I dont care if its just starting or is struggling... I just want the promise from the owner when he asks me to join that its not gonna shut down soon and he will do everything in his power to make it a good fed. So with that being said.. I'll tell some info about my wrestler
His name is Shane Jenkins.. same as mine... Why? Cause he is me... What ive found to work best with me in e-wrestling is to base the charecter on me..
Whats I would do in those situations, how I would respond to a match or opponent.. I have all the respect in the world for you guys who handle charecters with tricky gimmicks.. I personally cant do it, plus I have more fun this way.. So the way Shane Jenkins acts,dresses,fights, is what I would do if I was there..
