J.B. Destiny: It's beautiful isn't it. You see Arty what your looking at right now is happiness, what your looking at is love, but most importantly what your looking at is freedom. You see here in America we believe in truth, justice, and The American way. But you...you want to put an end to all this don't you??? You wan't the world to become a communism right??? Theese little kids will be running this country and if there going to run America we have to teach them right from wrong and it's people like you with your ignorant, and racist comments that are going to confuse them. And as much as theese kids are the future of this planet J.B.
Destiny is "The Future" of wrestling, that makes me a role model, and that's why I act like I do. Think about it you never hear me using foul language or ignorant comments. I come out and speak the truth and when I step into the ring I do whatever it takes to win which teaches them the killer instinct that they'll need in the world of business but above all else I want them to know that genocide, and for thoose of you who don't know what genocide means it

So for everyone watching out there I want to send out the message that we can't let another hate group rise to power...no matter what they say nothing good can come from killing a certain group of people. Little Boy: Heck yeah your J.B. Destiny...but why don't you wrestle anymore??? J.B. Destiny: I do, but right now I wrestle in Japan, as a matter of face I'm doing an interview right now, and you two are gonna be on T.V. over there.
Little Girl: About what??? J.B. Destiny: Hmmm... Little Boy: I think she asked what your doing an interview about. J.B. Destiny: Oh, well I'm gonna be wrestling this guy in Japan on Sunday for
The World Title.
Little Girl: Who??? J.B. Destiny: A guy named Aryan Arty. Little Boy: I never heard of him. J.B. Destiny: Well it's proably for the better, he's a real bad guy. He likes
The Nazi's.
Little Girl/Little Boy: The Who??? J.B. Destiny: The Nazi's...umm they were a group of guys who didn't like other races, because they thought they were better than them.
Little Boy: Other Races??? J.B. Destiny: You know like a black person is a different race than you. Little Boy: Oh, they don't like Micheal Jordan??? J.B. Destiny: Nope, but don't worry about them there not around anymore. I'll tell you what go back and wait with your dad and when I'm finished with my interview I'll give you guys autographs O.K.???
Little Girl/Little Boy: O.K. (The kids run back to there father and J.B. Destiny looks back at the camera.) J.B. Destiny: You see that Arty how can you teach kids like that to hate other people for no good reason??? I know that I might sound like I'm repeating myself but I have to get my poin across, Arty nothing good has ever and I do mean EVER come from hate and nothing ever will. I have to ask you what do you think is so good about The Nazi mentallity in the first place??? Is it the fact that they wanted to eliminate competition because they were trying to avoid the inevidable which was that they would eventually loose all the power they accumulated. Or maybe you liked the face that Adolf Hitler, the leader of the whole government hated people of Jewish decent when he himself was
Jewish...it's ironic really in the end he killed the one true "evil" Jew...himself. Or maybe you like the whole idea of an Aryan nation. A society where everybody is the same, where everybody thinks, talk, and do what they are told. All I know is if everybody was exactly like each other then this would be a pretty dull world. That's why I'm going to take so much pleasure in beating you for your belt this Sunday at Inferno. It's Pay-Per View, you know
I've never lost a Pay-Per View match and I don't plan on starting anytime soon, you see Arty J.B. Destiny excells at big time events. Everytime I've wrestled on Pay-Per View I've accomplished something amazing, my very first match was on an SWA Pay-Per View and I was victorious. As a matter of fact I beat a seven and a half foot, and five hundred pound man named The Insane § Giant in seventeen seconds and ever since that referees hand came down and struck the mat for the third time J.B. Destiny has been a house hold name. Then on the most recent Pay-Per View I appeared on which was just about a year ago in The
XBWL...it was a big night for "The Destined One" it was "XBWL Summer Sizzler
99" " it was J.B. Destiny fighting my own partner...my own Tag-Team
Championship holding partner Steve Edwards winner took home both XBWL Tag straps and the respect of there opponent, and guess what happened after about
twenty minutes of some of the best technical wrestling ever shown I came out on top and now I have you...I would say this match was a joke but I don't find anything funny about what I plan on doing to you. Arty this goes way beyond
The JPWF World Title, if it didn't I would beable to promise the fans that I would run a clinic out there but I don't plan on putting on a wrestling match.
I'm just gonna go out there and fight you and we both know you can't even hold a candle to my wrestling ability but I also know that you couldn't last with me in a fight either.
(J.B. Destiny, glances over to where the little girl and boy are standing and smiles. He then looks back at the camera.)
J.B. Destiny: Arty you see thoose two little kids??? Well when I step into that ring Sunday night that's who I'm fighting for. Sunday night if you have the guts to show up you can consider yourself DESTINED FOR DESTRUCTION!!!
