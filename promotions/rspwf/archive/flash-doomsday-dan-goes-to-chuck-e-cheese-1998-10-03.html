[FLASH] Doomsday Dan goes to Chuck E. Cheese!

3 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tell me what you think, Give me you opinions. The scene opens up in front of the bar that Dan was in last week. It is about 3:00 PM, on a cold, chilly day, in Vancouver, B.C. There are three guys on the curb, drunk, that were kicked out of the bar. In the parking lot, there are a row of 4x4's and a row of Harleys. The camera zooms in on
The Doorway as a large man goes flying out the swinging doorway. He lands on the curb beside the three others. A few seconds later Doomsday Dan and
Maniacal Mark come through the door.
</p> "I told you, stay off my seat! Hey camera man, you've seen what I do after a hard fought match, and thats come here. Now lets go see what I do
BEFORE a match."
</p> Dan hops into his 4x4 as Mark hops in the passenger seat. The camera man hops into the TV truck and tries desperately to keep up with Dan, who has peeled out of the parking lot.
</p> The camera man drives for a good 20 minutes, following Dan across side streets, through red lights, until finally, they reach a Large Shopping
Plaza. Dan opens the door of his 4x4, hops out, and heads right for.....The
Chuck E. Cheese?!
</p> Dan walks inside as a Large Mouse comes up and hugs him. Dan kicks the costume where the guys stomach should be, throws him over the railing, and heads over to the stage with the mechanical robots. As he walks up to the stage, the drummer, a mechanical dog, says "What's your name? I love you."
</p> "Shut up!" Dan yells, as he kicks the dogs head off with one swift kick. Cries are heard in the crowd. "Time for the games!" Yells Dan.
</p> Dan heads over to the whack-a-mole game. He sees a 5 year old kid playing with both foam paddles. He grabs the paddle, and whacks the kid on the head.
</p> Dan heads over to the pinball section. He goes up to a Machine and plays a game. He immediatly loses all three balls in the span of a minute.
Dan gets so pissed, he punches the top glass, which shatters into pieces. </p> "That game was rigged! Now on to my favourite, The Skee-Ball." </p> Dan walks over to the row of about 12 Skee-Ball Machines. He sees an 8 year old kid, climbing up the ramp, carrying an armfull of balls, and putting them in the holes.
</p> "Hey kid, what are you doing? That's not how you cheat! Watch this! </p> Dan lifts his foot, kicks the metal casing, and it opens. He then takes the whole roll of tickets and sits down on the table.
</p> "Your probably wondering why I came here. Full of snot nosed kids, and stupid mice. Well, this place disgusts me so much, that I'm so pissed, I'm usually still mad by the time I get in the ring. Now on to...."
</p> Just then a 6 year old kid walks up to Dan and says "Doomsday Dan, your my hero, can I have your autograph?"
</p>"No!! Get the hell away from me you snot nosed punk!" The kids starts to cry and runs to his mom. "Anyways, on to business. The
Bowler, you probabaly not watching this, because I booted you @$$ out of the
NIWF. Good luck being a bum. Rampage, I have a tendancy to send my opponents into hiding. Are you one of them? I haven't seen you lately. I just hope you show up on Saturday. Next off, NOKANDU, I was impressed by your showing on
Wednesday with Nomad. I hope you step it up for the tourney. And finally, tomorrow we start The Beach Battles tour, right here in Vancouver, Canada, my home country. Although it is freezing down here, the arenas will be hot as long as I am there. Mark, lets go!
</p> They leave through the doors, leaving a trail of crying kids and broken equipment behind them.
