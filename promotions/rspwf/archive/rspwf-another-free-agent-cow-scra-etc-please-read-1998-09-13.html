____________________________________________________________________ {More 2 Life by Master P plays over the loudspeakers. Announcer: Come to the ring. Hailing from Anchorage, AK, standing 6 "1 and weighing 225 pounds, here is ....
Scott "SKATIN" Richards!!!! Richards walks down the aisle. Wearing his birdhouse T-shirt and his long black tights. Richards enters the ring. His theme music fades. He picks up a mic and begins to speak.} Scott "SKATIN" Richards: Know I most of you guys are thinking who the hell is this guy. You are thinking why the hell is he hell. In just a short time you guys are going to find out. I am not going to say I am the greatest, strongest, coolest. Well, maybe I am the coolest, but that is besides the point. I have joined the ASCW, because well, I guess, nah. That isn't it. I really joined it, because this is the future of wrestling. Right now you got a few low key stars like Vars, Pillow, Parker, but you don't have much. So I am here to just make this fed better. With me here this ant no jobber fed anymore. People like to say about there fed this is where the big boys play.
Here it is were Richards play. I am here now and you guess can't do anything about it. Well, maybe you could. You could try and challenge me, and fight me. Still, I am not here to sit on my ass all day and wait intill someone wants to fight me. As you might be able to tell just by looking at me that most people don't like to get in the ring with me. No prob there. I guess I will have to just pick a fight. I don't want to fight any jobbers. I don't want to start at the bottom. So I am going to start at the very top. I want to start off with maybe the biggest star the ASCW has right now. What is his name you say? It is that guy named James Pillow.
{Richards scans the booing fans. He has a big smile on his face.} Scott "SKATIN" Richard: Pillow don't think I have for gotten you. I know you have a huge rep. People think you are the a good wrestler. You think you are
Simply The Best. What a joke. <Laughs> You might be the best in some feds, but when you step onto the same fed the Richards. You are the furthest thing from it. You are facing Jack Vars later tonight. A man who I also have no respect for. It is the main event. That is why the ASCW really needs me.
Having two jobbers in the main event isn't a great start. Hey, maybe I will have a little surprise for James in that match. I will talk about that later.
{Richards sways his head. Then he thinks to himself. Trying to remember something.} Scott "SKATIN" Richards: Oh Yeah this is what I was going to say. I have heard some of your words that you have said about yourself. <Grins> You say it is to hard to be good. You say it is hard to be great. Just wait intill you get into the ring with me. I will show you what being great is all about. I know that you must be thinking to yourself I must be a joke. You are thinking you might have never heard of me. You are thinking that I am not going to be able to stand 5 minutes with you. <Laughs> When I do get to meet you face to face.
I am going to whip that huge grin off your face. You have also said what you have done here and there. You are a champ in this fed. You are the biggest name in another fed. Do you think I or anyone else in this fed cares about that. That means nothing. You have to prove what you can do in this fed the
ASCW. Pillow, let me tell you before hand. It isn't going to be that easy to prove yourself when you have to go up against me. <Shakes his head> Pillow, just watch your back.
{Richards now has an evil grin on his face.} Scott "SKATIN" Richards: I want to talk about something else besides Pillow for a sec. I want to talk what I want here in the ASCW. I just want to be brief about it. I want the ASCW World Title. I don't really care who I have to go though to get that belt. Vars, Parker, Pillow, or whoever. So if anyone else wants to get that title also you are going to have to go though me. After you have seen my in action you are going to know that is it going to be a tough task. <Grins>
{Richards then puts his hands through his hair. Then he looks up into the rafters. It has a huge sign saying Megabrawl.}
Scott "SKATIN" Richards: Oh yea. I almost forgot the reason I am even here. I have a match. <Laughs> I can't believe someone actually wanted to get into the ring with. What was that guys name again? MANIMAL I think. <Shakes his head>
We are both newcomers here I see. I guess you must be new to the game or something. If your not then you just have some brian damage, or something. I don't think anyone would be that dumb just to sign a match with me. When people fight me they have weeks and weeks of scouting. This don't just go into the match without ever seeing me fight, and if I remember right I don't think I have ever gotten in the ring in the ASCW. When I step though the rope there is going to be just be one thing going though me head. You want to know what that is. To just knock your sorry ass butt all over the ring. <Grin> As I said before this is going to be my first match in the ASCW. With that being in mind
I want to impress people, and there is no better way then to impress then winning. So buddy, heres the deal. You are just in the way of me and what I am trying to show. So once I beat you for the 1........2........3 I hope there is no hard feeling.
{Richards then starts to leave. Then he begins to walk be in the ring. Scott "SKATIN" Richards: Pillow, just remember. I have a little surprise for you soon. I am not going to tell you were or when. That isn't important. You will know when it hits. You will know when you have felt the pain which you are going to receive when I get my hands on you. So, Pillow watch, out because
"SKATIN" is looking for you. Thing is that I. You are the main target on my mission right now. I shouldn't really say mission. Your just the next man in the waiting list who is going to get hurt by me. <Laughs>
{Richards music plays and he leaves with a big smile on his face.} Handler-[name withheld] P-BEAR EuLw, CNWL 142 on Worst 200 96 on Top 300 Scott "SKATIN" Richards ASCW 163 & 156 on the Top 300?
