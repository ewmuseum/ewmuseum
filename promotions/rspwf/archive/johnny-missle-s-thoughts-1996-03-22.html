Missle: Well,well,well,I've got the whole e-wrestlong world watching. What am I here for you may ask,to enlighten you all with my thoughts and to introduce the newest singles wrestler and the newest,most dangerous tag team. But first of all I'd like to talk about the Missourri
Tiger,matt Tiegs. It seems I;ve finally got a match with him for the WIWF
North American Title. When will it happen,I don't know but when it does
Matt you'd better be ready. Second subject,a Sidewinder Imposter! That's right,Ive seacrhed the globe for league to participate in and what did I find. I found Brutus "The Sidewinder" Noir or something in War! Well
Brutus,since the WAR! is filled up I'm issuing a challenge to you. We'll pick a man at random,he writes the match. Me and you,one on one in a steel cage match. I see you'e a brawler,well what kind of name is
Sidewinder for a brawler?!? This is what somebody with the name sidewinder should be able to do....
(Johnny Missle runs off the set being followed by the camera. He runs into a training room,grabsn a wrestler at random and rolls him into the ring. He savate kicks the wrestler donw,jumps to the top and performs a
540 moonsault\splash onto the man then runs back to the original set)
Missle: You see that,perfect ariel ability. Perfect form. Now my opinions on the closing of the CFWF...IT SUCKS! Alex Extreme,you name the fed,I'm there! We were gonna eat in the big steel cage match,I'd have that North
American title around my waist one more time but then,what happens,after
3 and a half months of perfect running it shuts down! AHHHHH! Extreme,you got lucky,if you've got the guts name the fed! Now for my three new entrants into the e-wrestling world!
(A large masked man walks onto the stage. Johnny Missle grabs the mask and rips it off to reveal a man with short,bleached hair,spiked. He stares at the camera with a look of sadism in his eyes. He wears a large blue robe which Misssle rips off to reveal a torn flannel short,blue jeans cut off.)
Missle: I've searched everywhere for a man like this,I give you....ELMO
"The Onslaught" ARMSTRONG!!!
(Elmo Armstrong rips the flannel shirt off of his body to reveal a muscular chest. He flexes at the camera)
Elmo: Anyone,ANYONE who thinks they're stronger then me all ya gotta do is call me to a fed and I'll be there.
Missle: And now the craziest new team in the world of e-wrestling,I give you.... (A man wearing a black mask with eye holes walks out wearing wrestling tighs. He's pretty muscled. A man behind him walks out wearing a blue mask and wrestling tights walks out. He's just as muscled)
Missle: I give you....OMEGA! (The first man nows then jumps up with a reverse roundhouse kick) Missle: And his partner......SPAWN!! (The second man lashes out with a kick) Missle: Together they are....Army of Devestation. All of us are looking for a few good feds,you want the best,we're right here baby and we're not goin' ANYWHERE!!
(fade out)
