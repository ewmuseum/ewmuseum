I'm looking for a federation

9 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
To all you cheif scouts out there, my name is [name withheld], and I am the handler of a great Role-Player, known as
Doomsday, his stats are below, also I have included some sample role-plays of pure quality, so IIWF or IECW or another top federation, go on book me into your roster.
______________________________________________________________________ Name: "The Torturor" Doomsday age:26 Height:6"5 weight:386lbs description: Doomsday is quite an agressive man, who in just 3 months has acomplished the gaining of many titles.
Doomsday started off in WOLF, where he nearly went on to be the Intercontinental Champion, but it closed down, he then went onto a federation called RCW, where he sucessfully went onto to capture and still holds today the
RCW Tag and Stables titles. Whilst in this federation, Doomsday wanted more gold, so he decided to join more federations.
IN January of this year, Doomsday joined UWF, where he captured the East Coast Title, he then drooped it and won the UWF
International Title. Doomsday then went the IWL, where he held the International Title for a month, he then lost it, and retired from this federation. After he had retired from the IWL, Doomsday then went on to the UCW, where he successfully captured the TV Championship, he also was given the nickname the "Torturor", because he always gives a deathsault through a table, and he trys to maim his opponents, this then closed down and now has reopend as the ECW, Doomsday then rejoined the ECW and is going strong.
Once Doomsday had acomplished all of that, he then joined a federation called CREW, which he is still in now, here he gained the Intercontienental
Championship ,and going strong, then finally Doomsday came to the IIWA, where he will hopefully do the same and capture some gold.
At the moment, he is also in negotions with the NAWA. Physical Description: Doomsday is 6"5, and comes from a pro wrestling background, he has black hair, red eyes and he always wears a red and black mask.
Doomsday can sometimes change his apperance, to all black or just red, he never takes off his mask, as he was burned as a child.
Doomsday has a tatoo of a red devil on his right shoulder, and he always wears around his neck a black and grren amulet, this is somehow connected with his family tradition of carrying something that is precious to you.
From: London England Finisher:Demon Driver Move is like a: Pedigree Style of wrestling: Brawler/Technical Doomsday is like: A mixture of Knae,Undertaker and HHH. Manager:GateKeper Description of manager:The GateKeeper, is a former proffesional wresteler, who has won numerous titles, such as XWA TV,UCW TV Title and now the ECW
TV Title. He brought Doomsday into wrestling, because he wanted somewhere he could compete and show off his brutal powerhouse skills.
________________________________________________________________________ Sample Interview **The scene opens up outside the WOLF building, seen entering, is Doomsday and the his manager, D.K Lynch, Doomsday is wearing a pair of red trousers and black top, saying The "Torturer", D.K Lynch is wearing a black suit and a red tie, with black boots.
Doomsday then motions over to a WOLF Official, who then directs him over to the WOLF Presidents office, Doomsday then knocks the door of the
Presidents office, he then enters. Doomsday grabs a chair for his manager, then orders him to sit down, he then grabs another chair and sits down.
The WOLF President then gets ready to conduct an interview with Doomsday.** WOLF President: First of all Doomsday, it gives me great pleasure to be speaking to someone of your talent?
Doomsday: Well thanks Mr President, but you all know that if it wasn’t for WOLF, I wouldn’t have gained anything, so if I may, I wish to offer you a token of my appreciation.
**Doomsday then turns to D.K Lynch, who then passes him his chequebook, Doomsday then pulls out of his shirt pocket, a black Parker pen, which has been engraved with the letters WOLF President, Doomsday then uses the pen to sign a cheque, Doomsday then says.**
Doomsday: Wolf President, it gives me great pleasure to honour you with a cheque for $100,0000, also enclosed is a special pen especially engraved with your name, take it for if it wasn’t for you I wouldn’t be standing here now.
**The Wolf President doesn’t know what to say, he is amazed, he then speaks.** WOLF President: Doomsday, as much as I would like to accept your kind gift, I feel that it would be wrong of me, so therefore I have to decline, as some people may see it as a bribe.
Doomsday: WOLF President, I don’t care what the people will think, I’m giving you a gift and if you don’t take it, then I will be offended.
WOLF President: Very well then, I will accept it after May 15th, that way people will not be able to say that it is a bribe, are you okay with that.
Doomsday: Yes, that is okay, now where were we. **Doomsday then puts the money into an envelope and seals it down, the interview is then restarted.** WOLF President: So Doomsday, how do you feel you are doing at the moment and where do you see yourself progressing?
Doomsday: Mr President, I came back to WOLF with one aim, that is either become WOLF Intercontinental Champion and last week, well Whippo, you sure put up a good fight, but once again the real champion prevailed, don’t be disheartened, for now you can be like all the rest, resting in solemn peace for the rest of eternity.
Or make sure that I did everything I can to rid WOLF of the "Wildcard" Kevin Salo, Kevin Salo, the day you double crossed WITCH was the day you met your maker, for the rest of eternity, you will realise why nobody interferes in my
WITCH business, so Salo, if I can get past DeathRoe, then rest assured Bad Boy you will be next, then Salo, the PPV you and me, I know, if I win the slot, lets make the match an Inferno Casket Match, if not then just a normal Casket match, that way nobody can cost me the chance at what is rightfully mine.
Now Bad Boy Sean Combs, I have to congratulate you, for you are now in round 2 of the tournament, but soon, we shall meet, when we do, yeah the ratings will hit the roof, for I Doomsday will once again drive the demons out of you, The
Corkscrew Death Drop shall drop you down to Hell, from hence you shall never return. **Doomsday then walks over to the water faucet, pours himself a glass of ice cold water, then sits down and carries on with the interview.** WOLF President: Doomsday, how do you feel about the situation here in WOLF concerning the Crow?
Doomsday: Crow, that is someone, who deserves to be shown why he just can’t go around proclaiming, that he shall be the one who is going to destroy WITCH, for as I say, Crow, many have tried, yet none succeed, that is because WITCH is to smart for the likes of you guys, so Crow, I suggest to you, either you be quiet or I personally will start on you, I know, lets have a match, one which we should have had before WOLF closed, do you accept, do so and be crushed.
**Doomsday then motions over towards the window, opens it and the sun shines on his sparkling CREW IC Championship, he then sits back down.** WOLF President: Doomsday, as you know, we have a bit of a problem with CREW, if it came down to it, would you ever leave CREW?
Doomsday: Me, the currant CREW Intercontinental Champion leave, well when the time comes, maybe, but not because of some petty feud of yours, so CREW, I’m still yours and your only truth speaking champion is here to stay until the day of the dead arrives.
WOLF President: My final question, is now you know that you are going to face DeathRoe in the Quarter finals of the Tournament, how do you feel about this? Doomsday: I have had enough of talking, D.K Lynch speak to the President. **Doomsday then turns to D.K Lynch, who then says these final comments** D.K Lynch: DeathRoe, as much as it hurts me to do this, next week, Doomsday and DeathRoe in Round 2 of the WOLF Intercontinental Tournament, well
DeathRoe, I say to you as I have said to Doomsday, all you have got to do is perform to the best of your abilities and make sure that you both have a good fair match, I will personally make sure that nobody interferes, apart from that both of you feel proud, you are representing WITCH and remember all of us will be routing for you both, finally may the best man win.
WOLF President: Well thanks Doomsday for a long but good interview, good luck and I look forward to seeing you in action next week.
**Doomsday and D.K Lynch, then stand up, position their chairs back over in the corner, close the door of the Presidents office, then leave the WOLF building, they then step into an awaiting limousine, which then spurts off into the distance and is no more.**
________________________________________________________________________ Sample Role-Play **The scene opens up in Tokyo City Airport, seen standing in the distance are 3 figures, the camera then spans the area then motions towards one of the figures.
A porter is seen carrying some bags, then beside him is D.K Lynch, the camera then motions over to another figure, this time it is Doomsday, he is in his luxurious red and black suit, his CREW IC Title, is sparkling around his waist, he then motions over towards a window, looks out and then begns to speak.**
Doomsday: So the time has finally arrived,Doomsday is here in the land of the rising sun, Tokyo City, what a nice place this is, I come on a mission to seek out and destroy the one man who dares to cross my territory, that man, Diomand Dog.
Diomand Dog, I respect you for what you are, not for what you have done, you see, you failed to beat Jay Wire, now that is a mistake, one that soon shall bring about your downfall, for no man on this entire planet can withstand the punishment which they recieve from me, Diomand Dog, you maybe oneof us but ever since you joined CREW, I belive that you set out to destroy it, so for that one reason, you shall fall amongst the dead opponents of mine.
**Doomsday then turns from the window, motions towards the porter who then is ordered to leave the area, Doomsday then carries on speaking.** Doomsday: Diomand Dog when we meet, you will just be like a stone waiting to be stepped on, then my next opponent will just have to deal with the punishment that I will inflict on them.
**Doomsday then stands up and raises his hands in the air, as he does so a flash of red light is heard, he then carries on speaking.** Doomsday: When the time comes Dog, I will teach you a valuable lesson, the lesson of Destruction, for when you dare to enter my territory, you are creating a rift between your destiny and I have been angered by your actions, that is why you leave me no choice, but to drive the Demons out of you, once and for all.
Dog, do you know what Hell looks like, because when you are finished with, that is exactly where I am going to send you, there will be no escape, your body will burn in the fires of Hell, oh yeah.
**Doomsday then orders hismanager to pick up his CREW IC Title, D.K Lynch then hands it to Doomsday, he then carries on speaking.** Doomsday: So now that I here in Tokyo, Diomand Dog, be prepared for soon, we shall meet and when we do, oh won’t it be sweet seeing your entire body be consumed and your soul taken, Phantasm, you will also soon feel the wrath of the True Lord of Darkness.
I am the "Torturer" and each and every opponent who stands in my way of retaining the CREW Intercontinental Championship had better watch their backs, because when they least expect It, I’ll be there.
**Doomsday then starts to laugh maniacally and says these final few words.** Doomsday: CREW, a new era has just begun, an era that just totally wipes out all the rest, W.I.T.C.H has been reborn, and boy will it survive, be warned we are more deadly then you think, we can just snap and that is the end of you.
**Doomsday then motions towards the porter and his manager, they then all leave in a limousine, they then are no more as the scene fades to planes landing at Tokyo City Airport**
