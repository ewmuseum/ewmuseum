["Sadistic" Shane Alexander] Roleplay Time.

29 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am just posting this for fun, that is all. =D ================================================ [The scene opens up to blackness. Nothing is seen nor heard. Then a thunderous bang is heard, and the words "SIMPLY SADISTIC" appear in white. Then what looks to be blood starts to drip down over the words. The screen is totally red now.]
[The screen then fades to a shot of a run down bar in East Rutherford, New
Jersey. It is late, like around 10:30 at night. The sky is pitch black and the street lights are either broken or flickering. The bar is called Barney's
Place. The camera is showing a wide angled outside view of the bar. It is a brick type of small house like building. The door is large and wooden. The windows seem to be painted different colors. The sign at the top has a green backdrop and white lettering that spells out "Barney's Place." A man just comes stumbling out of the bar and falls in front.]
[The camera then fades to a wide angled shot of the inside of the bar. The cameraman must be standing towards the doorway because the shot is showing the entire bar. There is a long bar bench to your immediate left. The bartender is standing there wiping shot glasses with a clean white towel. There are two men sitting at the bar bench. One looks to be a business man sitting and having a couple of beers. The other man is taking a shot of Vodka after shot, after shot. He looks very *scrubby* Then there are booths all around the wall. There is a few people at the booths. In the middle there at small tables with metal chairs. The camera picks up your next EMWO TV Champ, Shane Alexander, sitting at the table by himself. The camera walks steadily and slow. They get behind
Shane and then tap him on the shoulder. He turns around. Revealing that he is dead hammered. He starts to talk.]
Why is it that you assholes can never leave me alone? [The camera man replies with a scared little boys voice.] S-S-S-Sorrrrry M-M-M-M-Mister Alllllexandddder. [Shane looks at him then takes another sip of his Heineken.] No problem. I guess you are here to get a word about the comments of that little shit Mike Robinson. Huh?
[The cameraman replies with yes.] No problem. Sit down. Take a load off. Here I got you. [As the cameraman sits down Shane turns toward the bartender in the front.] How about a little service here Barney? [The bartender grunts as he goes to the back.] Now, what were we talking about, oh yeah, Mike Robinson. Before I talk about what he said, I think I will comment on something that I have noticed with him. Have you ever noticed that he is always pointing at himself? I think we might need to look into that. I mean he might go blind from all that jerking off that he is doing. I mean how can someone love themselves that much and not jerk off? I dunno. Anyway.
[Shane stops and laughs at his own joke.] Back to the comments. He once *again* called me a *scrub* I mean I do not care or anything but if you are going to have an interview at least put some facts down that are new. I mean you call me a scrub every damn week. At least I will call you an asshole one day and the a shithead another. See the difference between me and you is that I have variety.
[A tall blonde Caucasian woman walks up to Shane, she asks him what he would like. He at first looks puzzled, then he turns and speaks.]
Oh do you mean drinks or sex? [The waitress smiles and then answers.] I will take your drink first, then we will talk later. [Shane gives a sinister smile.] No problem. Give my friend here a Heineken. Thanks honey. [The waitress walks away, shaking her ass at Shane.] Whooh! Now that is some prime rib I would sink my di..oh teeth in. Oh didn't I see a waitress with you Mike? Oh yeah that is right, you are afraid of woman, afraid that you will not be as *flashy* as you are in the ring? Oh wait. You are not even *flashy* in the ring. Anyway, back to the *scrub* part.
I may be a scrub but you know what. I will scrub the floor with all that blonde hair. And you must really think that I do not know that you know how you won the title. You know I pulled you off.
You know it. But you have to go and say that it was not me. STUPID! 'Cause now I am going to have to kick your ass. [The waitress walks back with the Heineken. She places and asks if that would be it. Shane nods yes.]
Drink up. [The cameraman replies.] I do not drink Mr. Alexander. [Shane then looks puzzled and grabs the bottle. He smashes it over the cameraman's head. The camera drops and the picture gets all distorted. Then
Shane lifts the camera up and places it on his shoulder. He shows the bloodied cameraman. He then turns the camera and shows a close up of his face. He is pissed.]
That is *not* half as bad as what *I* am going to do to you Mike. Be prepared. [He throws the camera and the screen fades to static.]
