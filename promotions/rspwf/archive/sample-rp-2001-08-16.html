((I didn't put this in the RP, but if you do want to know what is painted on the wall scroll down to the end
[[The alley way hangs with the stench of garbage. It hangs in air so thick that
Sara barely is able to stay in the alley. the Cameraman coughs involuntarly as he follows Sara through the alley way. There was another stench that hung in the air as well, and it was just as thick, but it wasn't the garbage or anything in it. It was blood which seemed to be painted on the walls. Saying "I want to play" or "They will claim it"]]
[Sara] Are you getting this? He's definately here. We may have found him. {Cameraman] I hope so, one more blood soaked alley and I'LL be the one going nuts. [Sara] Well, the cops weren't able to get anything out of him about this, maybe he'll lighten up with our charm.
[Voice] Really? I thought that last detective was really charming, don't you think so Cabeza? But she is right, she does have some more beauty than that officer did. Who knows, maybe she'll even play with me, her and her friend.
Would you like to play? [[Sara looks at the cameraman who shakes his head and the camera no but she just waved him off]]
[Sara] We'll be happy to play, but first would you like to talk to us first. [Voice] Sure, I would love to, but the voices would like reassurance that you will play. Please, let me see your driver's liscense.
[[Sara looks at the cameraman who shakes his head and the camera no in a faster pace]] [Sara] Okay, [[She takes out her wallet]] Here you go. [Voice] No, toss it to the shadows. Don't worry, nothing will be stolen. The voices have no use for petty things like cash and credit cards. They just want reassurance.
[[She tosses it to the shadows and a few minutes later it was tosses back with everything still in it]]
[Voice] Sara Michelle. 1st and maine apartment 127. Now the voices know where you live Sara Michelle. I do hope you don't break your word. Because a promise made will be a promise kept.
[Sara] [[Swallows hard and finally realizes she's over her head]] Okay, will you come out now?
[[from the shadows came a figure in raggy clothes and moppy unkempt hair. His frame was well built and he seemed to have intelligence behind the eyes, but something was still uncomfortable for her. His smile, how it never moves or twitches. It remains constant showing those beast like teeth. Apparently they were made like that by himself from the evident scar tissue on his mouth, lips, and gums]]
[Man] Well, here I am Sara Michelle. Interview as much as you pleased, but remember.... you promised you would play. I invited a friend. [[Shows a limped object in his hand and showed it was a dead rat. Sara screamed as the cameraman dropped his camera on some garbage and ran away]]
[Sara] What are you doing with that? [Voice] Oh, don't worry. Cabeza won't interrupt your interview. He's very the silent type. He can go on for weeks not saying any words. I wish some people could do that as well.... you seem to be doing a good job so far being silent, but I thought this was an interview.
[Sara] Um...uh...Okay... first what's your name sir? [Voice] Schiz... the voices named me Schiz. [Sara] Schiz? Short for Schizophrena? [Voices] Maybe, I don't question the voices. [Sara] Okay, can you tell me about these writings? "I want to play" and "They will have it" Have what?
[Schiz] Good question, you're obviously a good reporter. Going straight for the point.... they will have it.... that is all that they want.
[Sara] Want what? [Schiz] That shall be revealed later after the interview. [Sara] Now, rumor has it that you went and joined the [Add fed name here]... why? [Schiz] Because I want to play... they have many playmates... perhaps I can find some who would like to play with me.
[Sara] Well, thank you. I've got to go... [Schiz] Aren't you forgetting? You promised to play. [[ A garbage bag falls on the camera but audio is still going on]] [Sara] No...get back... I don't want to play... I DON'T WANT TO PLAY! [[A loud crash is heard followed by a scream as the garbage bag comes off]] [Schiz] I want to play... will you play with me? (In case you're still interested what was painted on the wall was rats blood. Not human if THAT'S what you're thinking. And what happened to Sara? Use your imagination. Its more fun that way.
