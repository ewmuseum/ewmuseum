[Roleplay Critique] "Devastating" Damon Knight

12 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Usually folks, this isn't my style. But, I want to know what you think of this roleplay and my character. Currently i'm in RCW and NNEW. This roleplay was for
GSWP, but I don't know what happened.
Anyway, give me your honest feedback. Let me know what I can do to make this character better. And, of course, what you think on this roleplay.
[name withheld] [A cold, overcast, rainy day in the capital of the United States, Washington,
D.C., and the camera shows an interior shot of a well-lit study within a very modern-looking house. The lights in the futuristic-furnished room are out except for the spotlights around the fireplace, and a large flat screen television set on the largest wall of the room. The curtains of the window overlooking the landmarks far below bang against the window frame periodically with every gust of wind that blows up the hillside. The figure seated in the center of the room, however, makes no move to get up and secure the cracked window, and as the camera moves around the large easy chair, we see the familiar form of Damon Knight, his hands folded under his chin, staring blankly ahead at the wall in front of him. The young man seems to have recovered well physically from his ordeal in River City, but his silent, demeanor suggest that
Knight has a lot on the mind. The curtain bangs away in the breeze, and Knight takes no notice as his gaze remains locked on the wall in front of him.
The camera shifts to the object of Knight's attention, and we see an entire wall full of trophies, plaques, and title belts from the careers of Damon
Knight. The entire history of the young man in the wrestling ring is here, from the then nineteen-year-old Knight's first title nearly four years ago as the TWL Television Champ, to Knight's acceptance of the 1999 RSPWF Awards, to the TWL tag team titles Damon Knight and "Mr. Main Event" Chris Douglas won, to
Damon's more recent wins as the greatest-ever, in many people's minds, GPW
Television Champion. But the young superstar's attention is focused in the middle of the wall, a blank space. The curtains nor the television on the wall to the left, which is showing the Sporting News show on Window's Sky Sports
One.]
TV: ...now to professional wrestling, and California is still shinning brightly as ever and they await the return of a once great wrestling organization,
Golden State Wrestling Promotion.
[The image changes to show the press conference which was held no long then a week ago. Knight seems not to hear the commentary on the TV, and continues to stare at the wall.]
TV: ...in what was later described as one of the most entertaining federations, ever, is set to make their return on January 31st, on which day a new World champion will be crowned. In the past the federation had such wrestling greats as Fang, Johnny Glamour, and King Snake.
[The TV cuts to a shot of the three wrestlers, holding the titles in their arms, proudly.] TV: ...however, that is all a thing of the past, and now, now it is a time to crown a new champion. The Golden State promotion has already announced the first card, Survival of the Fittest 2001: "The Resurrection." This federation has always had some of the top talent in the world, and this time around, it will be no different with wrestling greats like: Shawn Slykk, James Pillow,
Eric Emerson, Steve Houston, and Dave Pietka. Not to mention rising stars such as: Tommy Lionheart, Kenneth Morlock, Logan Armstrong, Shawn Jamison, and the brightest of the youngsters in many people's mind, "Devastating" Damon Knight.
[After hearing his name, Knight suddenly changes directions, turning left to face the television. As the broadcast journalist continues to talk Knight swings back around to face the wall.]
TV: ...King Snake said the teams have been drawn out, announced and that all the teams will battle it out until two teams are left only to break up and fight it out in a battle royal.
[Knight suddenly glances back at the television, letting that last remark sink in, and the sudden look of understanding on his face gives way to expressions of confusion, disbelief, then finally betrayal and grim resolve. For long moments, Knight continues to watch the broadcast, then finally get up and crosses the room to the television set.]
TV: ...the future of the wrestling world... The legends... The GSWP. Ladies and gentlemen, who will take home the gold and go down in history? Stay tuned to find out and we bring you more in the weeks leading up to the return of GSWP!
[Knight snaps off the television, leaving no sound in the room but the whistling of the wind and the banging of the errant curtain. The wrestling great appears deep in thought, his mind turning faster and faster as, at last, the upcoming GSWP event begin to become clearer and clearer to him.
And then he laughs, a sinister, virulent laugh, and returns to his chair, a strange smile crossing his face as he folds his hands under his chin once more.
And stares at the wall of trophies once again. The camera follows his gaze back to the wall once more, and this time we see exactly what it is that Knight has been obsessed with this whole time.
In the center of the wall is a blank space. A space that is to be filled by one of the biggest prizes in all the land, a World title.
Knight continues to stare at the wall, but his demeanor has now completely changed from his former sullenness, his mouth spreads into a determined, arrogant grin, and his light brown eyes burn once again with their familiar intensity as the young man turns around, leaning against the wall, arms crossed, beginning to speak.]
DK: Y'know, the Golden State Promotion is to once again, be resurrected. But, this time there are no Fangs or Duke Putrids. This time, ladies and gents, we have 32 others who have their name on the paper in hopes of bringing home gold.
And who would've ever thought that Damon Knight would _ever_ sign a contract to be apart of the organization?... It just doesn't bear thinking about. I mean, how could I sign a contract to wrestle for a promotion that overrated, so-called "superstars" like King Snake and Johnny Glamour once ruled? How could
_the_ man force himself to surpass standards set by men, who, put simply, aren't fit to shine his boots? And how could _the_ man force himself to grapple on the "hallowed canvas," once occupied by men, who, on his worst day, he'd beat in ten seconds flat?
[Knight gives the camera a puzzled look before returning to his arrogant grin.] DK: So, maybe the question needs to be asked... What reason could "Devastating" _possibly_ have for accepting a GSWP contract offer? James Pillow? Nah ... he knows I'm "Simply The Best." Shawn Slykk? Doubt it. I'm "Simply The Shit." Eric Emerson? Nope, Chris Douglas taught me the game, I just perfected. But hell, I'm glad the guy has another protege.
So what is it? Respect... Sorry, none of that, I honestly couldn't careless. So here it is... I'm for one very simple reason. The spotlight that the
GSWP title will bring... Ever since I realized it, I've referred to myself as the "greatest talent in this business, bar none." Yet, I've been pushed on the back burner, time and time again. They call Jeff Hawkins and Alex Extreme
"superstars." They called Madison J. Valentine and Jason Blake "greats." And the only thing "they" said about me was "Twice the talent, less then half the spotlight."
[Knight stands up straight, the tone in voice, while remaining cocky, changes to a more frustrated sound.]
DK: Well now, things are different. Now I'm taking the big bucks. Now I'm doing what I gotta do. And when I'm the _Main Event_ of every evening, I'll shove it all down their throat followed by my boot.
[Now, almost visibly radiating in sheer arrogance and hate, Knight proceeds.] DK: But alas, Damon Knight has never been governed by a greed for money. Nah,
I'm here to fulfill the prophecy. You see, many leading religions speak of one true savior, whose arrival has been foretold for centuries. Well, in the
"promised land" of the GSWP, _I'm_ the Messiah. I ain't gonna save anybody -- quite the opposite -- but I'm no false prophet. I don't care if your name's
Vars, Gatlin, O'Neil, Bombastic or whatever... If you're on the list, I'm tearing a hole in your carcass, and sending your soul to hell.
Get it right... There's punishment for each and everyone person on the GSWP roster, friend or foe. And, (chuckles) it looks like half of you can get it on day "one." I don't care what you've been though, get in the ring with Damon
Knight, and it comes to worse.
[Fade.]
