Roleplay in which to critiquify upon.

2 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
If anyone cares, take a look and tell me what you think. It's the first for my new character; my first character in several years, and he's kind of an artistic intelligent (sort of) anti-social type, who doesn't care much for professional wrestling despite his talent. He just wants to save money to finance his poetry =) He's in a debut rumble with a bunch of other scrubs.
-Joe ------------------- Soft sea breezes. The whisper of warm wind. Gently caressing the sunken face of the skeleton. [Signed: "Abdiel."] "These are my words, it is my skeleton, and to paraphrase the words of
Spiderman, 'It is my gift, but yet it is also my curse.' I am Abdiel. And I speak of wrestling."
[Up?] [What's that?] [Over there. In the haze.] [Pan right.] [Focus now.] [A full size male skeleton sits crumpled in a Lay-Z-Boy recliner with a hazy gray background; a dreamy picture, but a reality for our Abdiel, in a metaphoric sort of way. He shall explain.]
"Skin. Quit simply the barrier between life and death. Gone. But was it taken away or did our subject freely give way? Hmm. This is the situation I'
m faced with. Professional wrestling. detestable. Main events, grudge matches, battle royals, hardcore, busted open, feuds, enemies: All such horrible words pertaining to horrible events performed by horrible people, enjoyed by the horrible."
[And now the face of Abdiel. Dark, tanned, handsome. but of course. An alluring mixture of Spanish, French, Native American heritage, etc, to create an appealing magnetic countenance. Countenance, what a ridiculous word. Only in the English language and pretentious depictions of "whatever"
would a word such as 'countenance' appear, and never in the intricate and beautiful poetry of our Abdiel. Me. Which leads me to.]
"Painting, poetry, and prose. my three true loves; my focus taken away by my dreadful ability to perform a quality headlock. And a pretty damn good flying leg lariat, or so I'm told. They say my hurricanrana is second to none."
[A large hand runs through wild red uncontrollable hair sticking an inch in every direction from Abdiel's scalp, propping it up in random places.]
"I really don't want to turn into that skeleton, you know. Not that I'm afraid of death, I'm more afraid of life, really. More specifically, leading a life I can't be proud of in my final seconds, during my final breath. I'm currently three fourths of the way through writing my second novel, but right now, due to monetary reasons, my concentration needs to be elsewhere, away from what I love to do and directed toward what I -have- to do."
[Abdiel sits on a bed in a dark room. Red sweatshirt, khaki slacks, and white socks cover his body; red tinted sunglasses sit on the black comforter to his right. With pen in hand and notebook on lap, our Abdiel writes.]
[And I quote.] . Our Abdiel sits on a bed in a dark room . . Pen in hand, notebook on lap . . Shit in head, thoughts swimming . . Fuck! . "Whatever. I can't concentrate." [Hmm.] "I think I have an idea." . Concentration concentrating on that . . which is undeserved . . THAT! . . which will convert our Abdiel into . . Bones . . Dead skeleton on forest green recliner . "Yeah, that's good." [And now he looks up and to his left, eyebrows raised, another thought storming his brain.]
"What will I do about that fucking battle royal?" [A question I'm sure we're all asking.] "So many names so unfamiliar." "Such a worthless prize on the line." [Writing again.] . Win worthless prize . . Win more worthless prizes . . Win adoration from the fans . . Who you "love so dearly" . . Climb that proverbial ladder . . Win more worthless prizes and adulation . . Get money . . Get MONEY . . The blood that rushes through the capitalist's veins . [Blah.] "There ain't enough of that damned capitalist blood flowing through my bank account to pay for my survival while I concentrate on my real loves. So here
I go. On that road. So many proverbial roads. So many proverbial proverbs in which to spew out of my mouth, through my pen, or to be painted onto my canvas. So for now, -F- the proverbs, and let me just say it how it is."
[Abdiel in the center of the ring.] "FROM WITHIN THE CONFINES OF THESE ROPES, I SHALL TRIUMPH!" [Throws hands up in air with celebration, now children. Smiling children, happy children, not only children, but everyone - an entire crowd of smiling, inaudibly screaming, delighted fans. They silently chant and the words are scribbled onto Abdiel's paper, unknowingly, through his hand, while deep in thought: .]
. Abdiel! . . Abdiel! . . Abdiel! . ... ABDIIIEEEL! . [Hmm. That's great. Now he should answer the phone.] "Hello?" "Yeah, hey, it's Guy here. You know-" "Oh hey, Guy, what's happening?" "Well, you know, I just wanted to say good luck on your upcoming thing, ya know. Your match or whatever. I'll be in-"
"Thanks." "Gotcha. Yeah, I'll be off in Italy with Sherona, just a spur of the moment kinda thing; we're leaving on the fourth."
". But, Guy, spur of the moment is, you know. that very moment. Not decided ahead of time."
"Well, Abdiel, that's right, but we just kinda decided out of nowhere, ya know?" "Hmm. Sure." "Plus I got some business to take care of over there. But yeah, I'll see ya on the flip side, dude. Heh, "dude." "Flip side." I'm so hip. Shit, anyway, good luck. I'm rootin' for ya. Win that damn thing and become a star."
"I'll sure try. 'man.'" "Great. Bye." "B-" [Click. Abdiel follows suit and hangs up, rolling his eyes over his latest encounter with his mysterious, eccentric, and filthy rich best friend,
"Guy."]
. Fuckin' Guy . [He writes.] . Lucky bastard . . Hot girlfriend . . Rich fucker . . Italy . . Jet settin' to the big boot . . Missing an important day . . in his best friend's life . . Always letting me down . . Never helping me up . . or out . "Blah. Oh well, one friend is better than none a friend, I presume. Shit. I' m not an honorable man; I'd be happy to accept a check for a 'measly' ten thousand dollars in order to finance my work, to allow me to concentrate on what I love instead of putting on the (proverbial once again) mask, pretending to be everyone's little high flying hero, pretending to me
"their" Abdiel when in reality they don't deserve 'this' Abdiel. They don't understand 'this' Abdiel. They only know they sure as heck like it when I jump on folks from the top rope and what have ya. I'd pay him back after I made my riches as a true artiste."
[Our Abdiel throws on his red tinted shades. Time to go out.] "They mean well though, in a simpleton kind of way, loving their simpleton
'rasslin and drinking their simpleton beer. Shit, too much thinking and babbling, need to climb."
[To the bright summery hot and shiny 'top' of the hotel we go; yes, we were in a hotel. Our Abdiel lets out stress and clears his mind by partaking in his fourth true love, climbing. He's a regular Spiderman, that Abdiel. Of course he didn't scale the whole damn building, just the first couple levels: the utility 'shed' offshoot and onto the top of the first story, above the lobby.]
"Whew. Great. Fun." [He says somewhat mockingly of himself, but yet it's the truth. Not a drop of sweat covers Abdiel's face, nor is he in any way out of breath. He sits
Indian style near the edge of the building, which looks over a small wooded valley.]
"Now to meditate over these damn bio's and figure out how to eliminate my fellow National Wrestling Council: "South" competitors from the match, over yonder ropes, on my way to superstardom and great riches, so I can
subsequently. -leave- this sport." [Pulls out several stacked and folded papers (bio's of competitors) from red sweatshirt pocket to study.]
"Hmm."
