[Flash] "Snake Eyes" Eddie Jacobs --> Critque, Please

8 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[does a little golf-clap] Bravo... Bravo... Ya've gone and proved why you're no longer the champion. =) Naw, J, ya' know I mark for ya'.... And that was a good flash... But I want both you and [name withheld] to look at this... I'm comin' Setz. =) [ The arena lights slowly begin to dim, casting off an eery mudluscious color. This causes the fans to start chanting ; majority with chants of boos, while the diehard fans give off chants of respect. This all being caused by a
high-impact sound of rythmic fury. ] [ "Blackout" by DMX featuring the LOX & Jay-Z is the cue for this mayhem. ] SR : Yea, baby. Finally, we get to here from someone who matters up in this hellhole. Finally it won't be so boring.
BN : Who would that be? I've never heard this music before. SR: Ahh well. . . I forgot you're one of them _ordinary_ people. Y'see, Newark, I toldja I got the hookup . . . I know everything. # Yo.. I used ta' have bad luck # #Now you might see me in a jag-truck# #Mad stuff. . .evil wit' a dime or a bad duck# #Double Are-T wit' tha' matching bandana# #Thirty-years young, blue steel wit' no hammer# [ The arena lights begin to flash circa the colours of blue and red. Huge spotlights of these colours, blasting the vision of the fans with each lyrical from the song. As the lights continue to lance from the heavens, the sound level of the song continues to rise drastically. ]
#An' I see ya'll niggas tryin' ta' glance at# #the kiss. 'Cuz I walk around wit'chall holdin# #bands on ma' wrist. Phonin' ya' women, tis'all# #drunk offa Corona's an' lemon# [Loud pillars of smoke emit from both sides of the rampway, and continue to pour out, yet still - the music continues to grow in volume. ]
#Y'know I'm still right in the 'mean'# #Lightin' the green... I need the buggy# #Even though I look ; right in the bean# #Jus' find out who my team, boos'in' da' Bills# #Niggas put out sony albums; tryin' ta' boost they sales..puttin' the cream on da' table# #Keepin' their eyes on the table...# [A lone figure stand at the beginning of the aisleway. But it isn't until the smoke clears that it's reveled this figure is
V.D. Vitrone. Clad in his casual gear, being a black Hilfiger jersey, saggy black carpenter jeans, and a pair of "airwalk"
type shoes. ] BN : Oh Geez... Not this idiot. Anyone but this idiot. SR: Show some respect, Newark. Vitrone's a world class athlete, and it seems like he's getting his mental stability back.
[ Vitrone basks in the reaction he has, aggresively talking words of confidence to the fans as he passes by. However, now, Vitrone reaches the ring. . .]
[Modified] #Experiencin' a Blackout..# #Blackout.. Blackout.. # #Bl...a...ck....out# [ Vitrone raises a fist in the air as the music fades, shifting instead to the low-drift voice of V.D. Vitrone ]
VDV - "Tis 'bout time the Eye-En-Dubbya-Eye decided to spend the big bucks fer my entrance, 'cuz I was gettin' sick of watchin' Setzer Van Strife come out here wit' a firework display seen only during the Chinese New Year."
[Heel pop.] SR - (VO) "He's right though, Newark. Van Strife, as much as I like the guy, is still relatively new to this federation compared to Vitrone.. An' well, SVS has already been awarded a world title shot. . . and all it took him was one."
BN - (VO) "Hey...nobody helped Setzer win the title. He earned his title shot." SR: (VO) "So has Vitrone, yet he was never awarded one... But shutup for a while. . . Vitrone's gonna talk again. "
VDV - "But anyways. . . we all know how good of a sportsman, I am, so I'll keep this nice an' sweet, a characterization that is V.D. Vitrone."
[Heel pop again.] "Oh, shut yer' flamin' yaps before I go up an' down each row shovin' my _sweet_ Vienna sausage down each an' every one of your throats."
SR -(VO) "Heh.. Vitrone _is_ great." BN - (VO) "He's controversial, I'll give ya' that." VDV - "Now, back to business.Last Sunday Night. The second annual 'Groundhog was Punted'. It was a great night for many INWI superstar and even was a witness ta' many changes of titles.
An' I'll get back to that in a second, but first lemme talk about a man who showed me somethin' else that night. "
"Jack Vars. Maybe ya' were right all along. You _do_ have what it takes to be a true jobber. I mean, who else would be laid out like a virgin on prom night after just a simple knee-lift? Noone I know. So, Vars, as a act of gratitude for our match last Sunday, I'm goin' ta' give you a gift here tonight. . ."
"I'm gonna give ya' the tightest assembly of talent ta' ever grace the INWI. jWo Vitrone!" [Pop? For mention of the jWo?] " Yes, Vars. Ya've heard me right. Them losers are all yours, do well wit' them, c'uz frankly - I've got my sights set on better things."
[Pause.] "But even before I get to that, I have yet one more man to mention. INWI's new World Television Champion, Nick 'Bulldogg' Demola. Well, Nicky boy, if ya' hadn't notice - I had a change of heart durin' that TV Battle Royale last Sunday. Y'see, I originally went in there, ruthlessly throwin' people's carcasses over the top rope, an' to the floor. But then,
I've come ta' a realization. " "There I was, goin' backwards in my career. Eddie Jacobs & King Apples, who aren't fit ta' lace my boots, were involved in what was arguably the biggest match in INWI history, while I was stuck in there with twenty-other men who couldn't cut the mustard, fightin'
for a petty belt that I've broke records in holding for close ta' five months." "Demola, don't take this the wrong way. Winnin' the Television Title is one helluva accomplishment, but well... There's an old sayin'."
"Been There, Done That." [Mix Pop.] " An' well, I don't see any time left to waste on goin' after the same belt again. Not until I finish the goal I set for myself upon enterin' the INWI, anyway. I promised myself that before I ever throw ma' wrestlin' boots away, I would wear the INWI Title 'round my waist. "
"But, previously, I've always had other things on my mind. Guys ta' take care of. Rookies ta' teach a lesson to. An' well, all the crap to that same effect. Well, second-hand accomplishments stop here."
"I'm goin' after the big one. The belt held by Setzer Van Strife. The sport's most cherished prize. . . InterNet Wrestling Incorporated's
World Heavyweight Title. An' well, there's an extra bonus thrown in there. The bonus of redemption. "
"Setz, I owe ya' an asswhippin'. I've owed ya' one ever since ya've stuck that big-ass nose of yours inta my feud wit' LOCO. An' well, since yer' boy LOCO ran like a bitch, I'm jus' gonna hafta lay _you_ out."
"An' after I completely eliminate the existance of the Lynch Mob in INWI, I'll be the single-star. Holdin' up the World Title fer' just my happiness."
"'Cuz all I care about is myself, now. . ." "Hell, ya' can even call me the 'Lone Wolf' of the 90's. . ." "Now hit ma' music." ['Blackout' starts up again as Vitrone exits. ]
