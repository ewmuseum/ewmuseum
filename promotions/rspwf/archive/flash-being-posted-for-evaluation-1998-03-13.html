Flash being posted for "evaluation" ...

13 March 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi everyone.. I know putting flashes up in this newgroup frequently result in flames, but I'm hoping that can be avoided this time.. :>
Anyway, some quick background on my flash: I actually am in a fed (NECW) and, of the few people who commented on my ability, they all said my flashes were "good" .. which is not very descriptive at all.
Anyway, I'm putting my flash up here in hopes of getting a more detailed analysis of it, just so I can work on improving my weak spots. (Please note that this is a REALLY long flash for me, they're usually much shorter.. this one was planned on being a short one, but somehow just "grew" naturally. Also: I usually have my wrestlers somehow involved, it's not normally just their manager speaking... but
I still felt this would be a good flash to use. And finally, yes, I'm involved in a feud right now with the people I'm addressing in the flash.)
And, without further ado, here is my flash.. post any criticisms, or email me at [detail withheld] .... [Camera opens on a familiar scene -- the Skar Family Training Gym, which is part of the larger Skar Wrestling School in Bay City,
Michigan. The camera pans around the room, showing the walls of the gym. On the walls are pictures of Jack Skar in his prime, and belts on the walls, replicas of the ones he's won and, in a few cases, the actual physical belt. Also on the walls are pictures of other wrestlers who received the training in the gym, including Jacob Skar and Don Ford, as well as Erik Skar, Jacob's deceased brother.
Finally, Jack Skar, Jacob's father and trainer, appears in the view of the camera. The NECW World Tag Team Titles are visible on a table behind him. There is a wrestling ring behind him, and Jacob & Don are sparring with other trainees. Jack is dressed in his standard sweats.. he looks at the camera and begins speaking.]
Jack: I just finished watching the most recent flash from the Crazy Animals.. or is it Crazed Animals? They can't even decide on their own team name.. at any rate, Zookeeper, after seeing how you treat
Gorilla, I can almost understand why you whacked my knee that cane... I can't imagine your "training" methods are any better.. you abuse your men and expect them to _win_? More likely they'll come to hate you for it. I've never once touched any of my wrestlers, and they've gone on to win numerous titles... plus, the Skar Wrestling School is actually a respected establishment.. any wrestler from Detroit you can name has trained here at one point... but this is off subject.
Put simply, Zookeeper, your tactics and general view on life disgust me. I can see you thinking now.. "Does this interview have a point?"
.. and, yes, it does. The point is, with the way you "discipline" your men, there's no way they're performing up to their maximum, and if they aren't at their peak, they won't be beating my boys, I can tell you that much. Your men have a great deal of potential, but until you change your ways, they won't be living up to half of it.
[Jack stops briefly and looks at the walls, then at the tag team belts that his son & his son's friend from childhood hold, and then begins speaking again.]
Jack: I, myself, have won over 10 World titles in 4 different countries... I've lost track of how many titles the graduates from my wrestling school have won.. but one thing is certain.. all this never would have happened if me, or any of my graduates, had been abused in the way you abuse Gorilla. It's inhumane.. but, then, perhaps that's why you call him Gorilla.. to dehumanize him? So you can try not to feel any guilt or remorse for what you do? That's all I can think of.
[Jack stops for a moment as a new wrestler comes over to him and asks for some advice on applying an armlock.. Jack briefly shows him, then turns back to the camera.]
Jack: As I was saying... well, Zookeeper, my time is almost up for this interview, but before I go, let me just tell you what you're up against at the PPV... you may think you know what to expect, but you don't. To put it briefly, Don hates you for much the same reason I do.. and Don is dangerous when he's upset... but Don isn't your biggest problem, oh no.. it's Jacob who you need to be worrying about. You see, Jacob once had a brother named Erik. Erik was trained at this school and quickly rose to the top in another major federation. However, during his first truley big match.. the
Headline event of a PPV where he had a shot at gaining the World title, he was powerbombed on the concrete outside the ring.. his head hit at an unfourtunate angle, and his skull was fractured and he was put into a coma... he died shortly thereafter. This traumatized
Jacob, who was at ringside and only 13 at the time. Jacob has never entirely healed from this, and now.. well, he has only two ambitions in his Wrestling career.. the first, and foremost, is to win the
World Heavyweight Title Erik never could.. he has not yet achieved this goal in the NECW or anywhere else. His second is to seek vengeance upon his brother and anyone else who is injured that badly.
You see, Zookeeper, he blames your men for what happened to my knee by you. He wants you all to pay. Plus, he is in possesion of a
World title.. true, it is not the World Heavyweight title, but he values the Tag Team titles nearly as much and does not want to lose them. So, add some determination to the mix. The final product is someone who will fight until he's quite literally almost dead. It will be an epic battle, Zookeeper. A battle you'll wish you'd never become involved in.
[Jack turns, and walks over to the ring, and the camera fades as he watches his wrestlers practicing in the ring.]
