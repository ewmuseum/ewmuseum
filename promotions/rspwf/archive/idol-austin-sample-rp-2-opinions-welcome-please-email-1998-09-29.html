Idol Austin Sample RP#2, opinions welcome, please email

29 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This one is from the NWC. [The scene opens in "Incredible" Idol Austin's penthouse suite at the MGM Grand
Hotel and Casino in Las Vegas, NV. As the cameramen arrive to the room, there is a huge party going on, obviously some sort of pre-celebration party on
Austin's part. The camera swings around the room, and you see several celebrities. Over in one corner, stand Siegfried and Roy, on a makeshift stage singing is Wayne Newton. In other assorted parts of the room you see Elvis impersonators by the dozen.]
[The camera moves around the room in search of Austin, and find him over by the wet bar talking to one of Las Vegas' many showgirls. Austin is a wearing a white suit with matching tie, and white shoes. His shoulder length sandy blond hair is pulled into a ponytail, and as per usual, he is smiling.]
[Austin sees the cameras and waves them over before continuing his conversation with the showgirl. When the cameramen get in place, Austin gives the showgirl a fond kiss goodbye on the cheek and turns to speak.]
Ah. Good to see you managed to find your way to this little soiree I am having here tonight. It is my official "Welcome Home" party. The people of Las Vegas just love me. And they have reason to. I am a winner, and Vegas loves winners.
So, I see that a couple of the participating teams have decided to try to write me and Arrows off as serious challenges to the World Tag Team Title. The notion is obviously absurd, but then, so are the people who have voiced them.
In one corner we have The Mercenaries. Steve Sands, and Warfare. Every time they open their mouths, I lose a little more respect for them. Let me see here. I believe they said something to the effect that Incredibly Perfect couldn't win the Battle Royal because they lacked the..was it pure strength? To win.
I don't know about the rest of you, but Incredibly Perfect is far from weak. We are also masters of the Battle Royal game and how to win them. On three occasions, I have been in a Battle Royal in the NWC, two of them for championships, and I have won all three. It didn't require strength, or even all that much skill. Just good strategy.
[A serving host walks by with a tray filled with full champagne glasses, Austin reaches over and grabs one before continuing.]
You may have been good soldiers, or whatever it was you were before becoming wrestlers, but I am a ring general. I know my battlefield so to speak, and I know how to claim victory from the clutches of defeat. And even though there are some who say Battle Royals are 99% luck, and 1% strategy, I argue it is the reverse.
Sands, Warfare, face it. You may have beaten the derelict Long Riders for the
MCW Tag Team Title. You may have ALMOST beaten the Syndicate Old Machine for the NWC World Tag Team Title. But Incredibly Perfect is not those other teams.
We are the New Age of Tag Team Wrestling, carrying on the Renaissance started by The Ruthless Villains in March of this year. And we WILL win the World Tag
Team Championship and continue the domination that The Powers of Arrogance has enjoyed for the last six months.
The only way we lose this match is if you kill us, and we all know how impossible that is. You can't kill greatness, and even if you could, superiority lives forever. Go back to Michigan and play champion there.
Because the World belongs to Incredibly Perfect and the PoA. But, I grow tired of talking about The Mercenaries. The very subject of them bores me to tears in a surprisingly short amount of time. [To the cameramen]
Walk with me a bit, I have more to talk about. [The camera follows Austin as he walks away from the bar and begins to make his way through the mass of humanity at the party. He stops from time to time to say hello to some of his guests, and then he makes his way out of the double doors leading to the balcony of his suite.]
[Down below, you can see the lights and activity that make the Las Vegas strip famous. Austin watches the movement of the city and then leans against the railing and downs the rest of his champagne before continuing.]
Another team that seems to think they have a fighting chance at winning MY
Battle Royal is Apocalypse. Apparently, Apocalypse thinks that lightning can strike twice for them. Unfortunately, I beg to differ.
Apocalypse, you had your chance to become World Tag Team Champions and blew it in the worst way imaginable. You WON, and then through your own acts of abject stupidity, had them taken away just as quickly.
I have to thank you though. It is because of the two of you that all of this, meaning the Battle Royal, is possible. It will be you, and you alone, who the rest of the world will blame when Incredibly Perfect walks away with the title.
And I wouldn't have it any other way. Two months ago, I walked into Washington, DC with a plan, and a destiny. The plan was to cement myself in history as one of the greatest ever by beating the other "top contenders" of the NWC in a massive match. The destiny was the NWC World Heavyweight
Championship. And now, I return home to Las Vegas, NV with a similar plan and a similar destiny. With Shawn Arrows by my side, Incredibly Perfect will defeat the top contenders in NWC Tag Team competition, and when it is all said and done, we will walk out of the ring as the World Tag Team Champions. We will bring the titles back to the PoA, and in the same stroke, place my name permanently in the NWC record books as the first man to win NWC World Titles in the singles and tag team divisions.
You speak of your accomplishments, and they mean nothing to me. You speak eloquently about the beauteous wonders of war. And yet, through all that talk, there is that hollow pit in both of you stomachs that remind you every day about how you had your chance, and blew it.
You say "Such is the proclamation of War." Your proclamation of war is meaningless. You are fighting a war you cannot win, against the greatest stable ever to grace an NWC ring. But if that is how you want it, then that is how it will be. Here is MY proclamation of war. You will be fodder for my greatness. Nothing can prevent that now.
[Speaks again to the cameramen.] I have said my piece for the day. In case you have forgotten, this is a party in my honor, and who would I be to spend all my time talking to you when there are people in there who love me?
[Austin turns and walks back into his penthouse as the scene fades to black.] "Incredible" Idol Austin Former NWC World Heavyweight Champion Pacific West Coast Heavyweight Champion 1/2 of Incredibly Perfect w/Shawn Arrows, SWF Tag Team Champions and Next NWC
World Tag Team Champions
