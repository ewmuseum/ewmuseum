[Critique] Villains and Prisons and Low Blows, OH MY!

8 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi all, Just wanted to get a critique on my rp...it's the lastest one I've written and was wondering if any of you have some suggestions. I was a bit out of it when I wrote it (i.e. tired from a blah day at work...heh), but still I'd appreciate some comments.
This is my first post here, so I'll give you a bit of background. I handle a female wrestler that goes by the name of "2Short." She's the valet of Adam Simon (who has posted here), and she fights only men. By no means does she look like Chyna (which some have assumed), because as the name implies, she is very short. The character is based on me, and like myself, 2Short is just slightly under 5 feet tall, 115 lbs. She is known for being ruthless and violent, and her trademark weapons are a .38 Special and a nailgun. She has corporate ventures and has engaged in criminal activities outside of the ring, which I work into my rp's. Oh yes, 2Short is known for attacking her opponents' genitals in creative ways - just to give you an idea, her finisher is the "Testicular Powerbomb". Since I'm Adam's valet, I also am in the stable De-Evolution X. I make stable references by saying that my opponents will "fall down the evolutionary ladder" now and then.
I've been in e-feds for just about a year. The first few months I was just a valet, but for the past 7-8 months, I've been doing competitive rp's. I've been in 6 feds, and I'm presently just active in XHCW as the only female wrestler (and handler) in that fed. In each fed I've been in that had a Women's title, I've won it. In addition, I've also had hardcore, intercontinental, and light heavyweight titles - at one point I was active in 3 feds and held titles in each of them.
Also, out of curiosity...do you guys see many female handlers? I still consider myself somewhat of a "newbie," but I like to write and am always open to suggestions. Anyhoo, please offer some
comments...thanks. :) -Dina ---------- (2Short has one foot up on a low retaining wall. She rests an elbow on her knee and looks out past the water and admires the downtown San
Francisco skyline. A breeze blows by, flapping both her long mane of hair and her black leather trenchcoat. She watches the sailboats go by and sighs as she thinks of Florida, her home state. Her cellular rings, and she picks it up.)
2Short: Talk. Adam Simon: Hey babe, how'd the board meeting go earlier this morning? 2Short: It went fine. I chewed out Ellison's ass since Oracle's stock has been shit for the last year. Oh well, as long as I keep getting options at pennies on the dollar, once it goes up, we'll make a mint babe.
Adam: Heh...sounds like a plan. So tell me, did you have a nice cup of coffee and read the Wall Street Journal as you made your plans today to conquer the world?
(2Short grins.) 2Short: With a toasted bagel, as always. Remember, my flight's coming in around 9 a.m. tomorrow.
Adam: I'll be there. What are you doing for the rest of today? 2Short: Sightseeing. I'll call you later. Adam: Bye. (2Short hangs up the phone, and looks out back into the cold San
Francisco Bay. She then looks over her shoulder and looks at all the buildings behind her. She grins.)
2Short: Alcatraz...ah, my brethren. (2Short makes her way to the main prison facility. She grabs a walkman audio tour and walks along the inside, hearing tales of prison life.
As she reaches the "D Block," otherwise known as "segregation," she pauses before following a small group of people into a solitary confinement cell. As she enters the cell, she closes the door, enveloping her and the small group in darkness. She pulls her headphones off and she hears the people make some noise in alarm, but they fall in silence as 2Short talks.)
2Short: Darkness...confinement...the timeless methods used to punish, to break down one's sanity. Just as those I am about to face in a few days, they too will meet similar methods of torture.
(2Short takes a deep breath, and she can hear the people shuffling in the darkness.) 2Short: Skit, a self-proclaimed hero of sorts, you are to meet a villain like none you have encountered before. As you realize, I am not your everyday criminal. Unlike my brethren such as Al Capone who have taken residence here, I have not been caught for anything I've done. Yet, my face has graced the likes of The Ten Most Wanted list...the bounty on my head more than triple the value of XHCW, all on the assumption that I have committed certain crimes. Ah...such a thrill. That is almost a past life, but the greed, the desire to make you say my name in mercy, to destroy the essence of your soul...thrills me as well.
(2Short sighs.) 2Short: Skit, I am just imagining nailing you down to the mat by your cape, and applying a leg drop right on your super jewels...can you feel it? How about a testicular twist...can you feel it now? Or, what about a good old fashioned low blow...how about now? Does that make you double over just thinking about it? Imagine this: as I climb up on a turnbuckle and you see me fly towards you as you are left incapacitated, you will wonder whom you have crossed to deserve such bodily harm. Our match will reenact the classic tale of the hero versus the criminal, of good versus evil. However, this time, this bad, evil girlie will win.
(2Short emits a low chuckle, and the people behind her make some anxious noises. A man speaks up, somewhat uncertain.)
Man: Um...excuse me Miss? We'd like to get out of here...it;s dark and... (2Short interrupts him and continues.) 2Short: And Cage...does it upset you that in order to get a chance at your former title you have to vie against Skit and myself instead of having a direct shot at Ramsus? Hmph...take a number and stand in line. Just like Skit, you have had the opportunity to face me in the ring once before. With Skit it was a 3-way dance, which I won, by the way. With you it was a 6-person tag, which my team won, by the way.
(2Short addresses the crowd.) 2Short: Hey, does anyone see a pattern here? ANYONE? ANYONE? Crowd: Oh yeah, sure...yep. (2Short ignores the crowd once more and her voice permeates throughout the cell.) 2Short: Cage - it is SO appropriate that I am here, in a famous prison, contemplating my upcoming encounter with you. You will feel like you've been imprisoned in the gates of Hell once you meet me in the ring. Shall I detail out how you will join the "Just Got His Ass
Kicked by 2Short" club? Shall I count the ways? Well, that may not be necessary. All you need to know is that I will redefine "extremeness"
to yet a higher level. The question is, can you keep up with me? I know you want that shot against Ramsus as much as I do, but son, just accept the fact that this is not your time. Oh, you can bring your twig of a girlfriend down to the ring with you - I will snap her in two just for giggles. That would be fun, wouldn't it?
(2Short hears the crowd behind her, restless. She smiles as she opens the door. The people rush out, their claustrophobia relieved. 2Short slowly leaves the cell and leans against a cold, metal wall.)
2Short: Skit and Cage...hope you both enjoy falling down the evolutionary ladder. From what I hear, it's one hell of a ride.
(2Short puts her headphones back on, and continues with the audio tour.)
