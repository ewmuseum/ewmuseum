(- Sample Roleplay: Logan -)

28 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, I was just wondering if someone could give me alittle advice about how to improve my roleplaying. I recently did this one for the NAIWF, but I think it could have been better... Oh, and I was wondering if someone knows of another way to get into Gyeah without applying as a woman wrestler :-)
Thanks... -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=--=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [The camera opens up to a shot of none other than Logan, sitting in a solid black directors chair. The surrounding is totally black, there is only a single bright light coming from above Logan's head... He is dressed in a pair of black wide-leg jeans, heavy-duty black steel-toe combat boots, as well as a solid black shirt and silver "ball" necklace around his neck. Logan's eyes appear to be bloodshot, as though he has not slept in days... He merely sits there for a moment, then finally begins to speak his mind.]
Logan: Rick Hendrix, when I am finished with your rotting carcus inside that squared circle you will be condemned to an eternity in Hell for your sins. What do you think it feels like to be in the underworld Rick? Where all you smell is death around you, and you constantly hear the screaming of tortured souls? You wouldn't be able to bare the agony it will bring to you. Yes Hendrix, this place is Hell... It is by far the worst place anyone could be sentenced to.
You, however, Rick believe that you can defeat me. That wasn't you talking,
Rick. That, my friend, was arrogance. You are too cocky and believe far too highly of yourself. In order for you to override your fear of me, which you say you do not have, you need to bring me down. You will only be allowed one shot at Logan... For you see, you will not live through this confrontation...
[Logan pauses for a moment, and adjusts his position in the chair. He then speaks again.] Logan: Now then, this is what is meant by torture, Hendrix. You are forsaken to become chained to a wall, where a demon shall throw flames at you day and night. Rick Hendrix, your soul shall be brought to this place, and everytime you are struck you shall think of the man that put you in this god forsaken place. I know it is difficult for you to listen to this, because it shall all become a reality as these days progress... There is nothing you can do about it, except run and hide. But remember that one way or another I shall find you and punish your soul far greater then I plan on doing now. As for anyone else in the North American and Intercontinental Wrestling Federation, if you would like to have the same fate as Hendrix, then please feel free to acknowledge me and I will see about sending you to Hell along with him...
[Logan gets out of the directors chair, and turns on another light... There is now a wall of light to his back as he continues to speak.]
Logan: Life is ment to die... Some die at birth, while others die upon the age of one hundred and five. Hendrix... you, my friend, will die when the bell rings. When the first movement is key. Hendrix, the game we play is a very simple one my friend. Two men enter a structure of wood, steel, as well as cable. Only one man comes out the victor, and in this case... only one man comes out alive.
[Logan runs his hands through his black hair. The camera zooms in on a closer shot of his face.. Logan's eyes seem hynotic as he continues.]
Logan: Rick Hendrix, this is not too different from the days of the Roman
Empire. Men walked into a monsterous colliseum made of nothing but stone. Men whom were prisoners of war... even political prisoners. Regardless of their crimes, they were sent into the arenas to battle it out. The stakes, however, were much higher back then. There were no championships in the Coliseum days.
Oh no my friend, they fought for their lives. If their opponent did not murder them in combat, the emperor would order both men killed by either his armored guards, or they were throw to the lions... And on February tenth, Rick Hendrix, you shall be thrown to a very hungry lion...
[Logan walks off camera, and the scene slowly fades to black.]
