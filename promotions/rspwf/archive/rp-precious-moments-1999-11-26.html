<RP> Precious Moments

26 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(It is raining rather hard as the scene opens. A large building, decked out in
Christmas lights already, stands high against the darkened sky. We look down to a giant engraved stone, and as lightning flashes, we can clearly read the stone; "New Haven Municiple Building". Suddenly, we hear a maniacal laugh...
and then another. We look to the right, and then to the left. Yet the laughter doesn't seem to be coming from the sides, but from... RIGHT IN FRONT
OF US! From behind the stone, onto the top of it, leaps two figures, draped in the darkness of the night. Water drips off them, as we try to look closer...
try to see whom these diabolic creatures are. Then, another lightning bolt strikes, illuminating the two. Illuminating and making us realize that all our fears were for naught. For it is the two most lovable men in pro-wrestling today, The Binky Fairy and The Winky Fairy. The Binky Fairy pulls a flashlight out of the back of his tights, and turns it on. Both Binky and Winky's faces light up with joy, as they stare at the flashlight)
The Binky Fairy: LIGHT STICK! WOO HOO! The Winky Fairy: Oh, oh, oh! Shine it on me, Bink! Shine it on me! (The Binky Fairy shines the light onto Winky, who starts prancing around on top of the rock)
TWF: Doo, doo, doo... I dance for you, people! Dance with me! TBF: Get down with your bad self, Winky. (Winky stops dancing suddenly, and stares blankly at Binky) TWF: I'm not bad, Binky. TBF: Oh no, I didn't mean bad as in bad. I meant bad as in good. TWF: How is bad, good? TBF: (Shrugs) It's these weird humans. They come up with the silliest stuff! (With that, Binky and Winky both fall silent. They stare at each other, the rain pouring down all around them, for several seconds. Finally, Winky gets an excited look on his face)
TWF: OH OH OH! Guess what I heard! TBF: What did you hear? TWF: We're going to be in The Squared Circle's tag team tournament! TBF: Ummm... what's that? TWF: I don't know, but we're going to be going against two teams in one night! TBF: How do you know that? TWF: That Zambrowski guy told me. TBF: Really? Then who are we going to be against? TWF: Well... first we have to take on The X-Factor. TBF: I hear that they're pretty good. Are they evil? TWF: I don't know. TBF: Then why are we taking them on if they're not evil? (Winky shrugs, then slumps down a bit) TWF: I don't want to take on a team that's not evil. TBF: Me neither. (Both now slump down, and sigh deeply) TWF: Well... I guess we have no choice. TBF: What do you mean? TWF: We're going to have to wrestle them to get to the evil. TBF: Did you say... evil? TWF: Yep! If we beat The X-Factor, we get to take on either The Wild Cards or
Schwag. TBF: We've beaten The Wild Cards, but have we beaten the evil out of them? TWF: I don't know, but I think we should make sure. TBF: And as for Schwag... TWF: Schwag is beyond evil. They make evil look good! TBF: I wouldn't take it that far, but they ARE pretty evil. I mean... all they can think about is human procreation!
TWF: And without love, procreation doesn't mean anything. TBF: Thusly, they must be taught a lesson in love, in the usage of the human body, and in GOOD.
TWF: And there is nothing like handing out a lesson in good! I'm so excited now! TBF: This is going to be so much fun... (Suddenly, the flashlight goes out, and we are cast in darkness again) TWF: Hey! What happened to the light stick? TBF: I don't know... TWF: Ummm... Binky... TBF: What is it, Wink? TWF: Do you think Beezleweezlebutt, the fairy-eating monster, will come back? TBF: No, Winky. I'm sure he won't... (Binky stops, and looks to the left, and then to the right) TBF: Ah... did you hear that, Winky? TWF: Hear what? (A loud growl is now audibly heard) TBF: That. TWF: Uh oh... (Lightning flashes, illuminating the humongous one-eyed fairy-eating monster known as Beezleweezlebutt standing behind Bink and Wink)
TBF: RUNNNNNNNNNN!!! TWF: AHHHHHHH!!!! NOT AGAIN! (As Binky and Winky run, a deep sigh is heard) Voice: Why doesn't anyone want to be my friend? (With that, setiment, we fade... ummm... we stay in black. Until next time...)
