WrestleVison PPV Exclusive Interview

18 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Toxic Shock (speaking in a more quiet tone of voice): Well, here I am with "The Exorcist" Butch Williams, a day after his defeat at the strong hands of "Magnum" Biff McGriff. A defeat the cost him his International War Title. Butch, can you tell me - and the paying fans - exactly how you feel right now. Butch: Right now I`m totally pissed off. Not because I lost the belt, but because I lost the belt to a moron like Biff McGriff, and because he`s a member of no-hopers Armageddon. I could have lost it to anyone else - anyone who isn`t a memeber of Armageddon that is - and I`d be fine right now. To be honest, I didn`t even want the title anymore. But I sure didn`t want to lose it to Armageddon. It makes the job for the New Dawn Army a hell of a lot more messy. Toxic Shock: But you are going to fight for it, aren`t you? Butch: No. I don`t want the International War Title anymore. Tox: You`re sounding a lot like your brother, the former World War Champion Seth Blitzspear. He gave up his title as well. Butch: Well, perhaps it is genetic. I don`t know. I know I want the World title though. Tox: And there is a lot of tought competition out there. Butch: Damn right. Though I`m not going to name names. I don`t wnat anyone to think they get my praise. Tox: Not even the members of the NDA. Butch: They know what I think of them. Tox: And what about Seth. What do you think of him? Butch: Well, it`s his action that caused Thunder to go bad. And when Thunder went bad, things went worse in War! Again, he joined Armageddon, but just like all the other stooges that have been a part of that outfit, they`re just going to be used to secure Tonnage`s status. And haven`t you noticed how inactive he is recently. Clearly, "The Beast" is at an end, and when he is gone, they all will go as well. Tox: Armageddon look pretty strong to me right now. And they`ve got a title, which _you_ said they wouldn`t do. Butch: Hmph. Tox, just take a look at War!Fare and you`ll see what happened. Sid Starr left them. And I`ll bet you any amount of points that others in Armageddon will soon be having the same feelings as him. Besides, I`ve got one or two "inside agents" in Armageddon anyhow, and they`re telling me that others are feeling like that. when you become a member of Armageddon, you`re no longer an individual. And that, Tox, people don`t like that. Tox: Butch Williams, we`re out of time. Thank you very much. Butch: Tox, it`s been my pleasure.
