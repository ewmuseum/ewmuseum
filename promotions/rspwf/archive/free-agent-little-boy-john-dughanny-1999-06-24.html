[Free Agent] "Little Boy"John Dughanny

24 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I have created yet another new wrestler and he is looking for a federation where he can't kick ass and take names. He is a rowdy texan and he wants only a federation that tolerate his mean bully like behavior. If anyone is interested in him then please email me or leave a message on the newsgroups detailing your federation and what you have to offer.
Wrestler's Name: "Little Boy" John Dughanny Height: 6'2 Weight: 270 lbs Hometown: San Antonio, Texas Entrance Music: The Theme Music from "Gunsmoke" Manager's Name: Jimmy The Greek Finishing Move: "The Black Diamond" (Stone Cold Stunner Off Top Rope) Biography: "Little Boy" John Dughanny is used to being a lone rebel. His younger brother owns a saloon in San Anio and John often bounced for the bar.
He made sure that nobody messed with his family or their business. If anyone dared to interfere in Dughanny business, then John would take care of them.
John has had to fight in some of the baddest back alley brawls ever seen. Whoever dare to challenge John will find out that he is not just fighting to win, but to survive.
