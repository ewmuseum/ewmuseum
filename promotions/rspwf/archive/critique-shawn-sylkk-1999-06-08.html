[CRITIQUE] Shawn Sylkk . . .

8 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Not exactly my best flash . . . kind'a short now that I reread it, but it's the first RP I've done in about two or three weeks and I'd just want some opinions on it, thanks . . .
[The scene is an alley in Long Beach, California. The sky is dead black and a street light is the only shimmer of light seen. From the back of the alley walks a shady figure. As the figure draws closer and closer, the figure becomes more clear. The figure steps directly under the street light and has his head bowed down. The man is wearing a black sleeveless t-shirt, baggy black jeans, and a pair of black hightops. The man raises his head to reveal that it's "Showtime" Shawn Sylkk with an emotionless expression on his face. A slight, momentary smirk crosses his lips as the light glimmers off his bald head.]
"S" SS: A poor man's Johnny Glamour? More than the sneak attack by Brian Bradley. More than Chris Winters savin'
me from the attack, and goin' on a Freezer Burn DDT rampage. More than anythin' else that came out of anyone else's mouth last Sunday night, that sticks in my mind the most. Glamour, I'm gonna be perfectly honest with ya
'cause it seems that you're so disillusioned by your self- started smoke bein' blown up your glitzary, Glamoury ass that you've lost all sense of reality. Johnny, pass the gas and let the smoke out. Get your mind off of your
Tupperware enchanced valet and your Glamour groupies. [Sylkk pauses for a few seconds, his expression not changing. He bows his head once again and shakes it from side to side, almost in a pitiful manner. He stops shaking his head and raises it again, still an expressionless look on his face.]
"S" SS: Got your mind cleared? Good. Glamour, since the day you've entered the GSWP, you've been nothin' but the front office puppet. You're nothin but their freak on a leash. Someone who's had the proverbial chauffered drive down easy street. No stop signs. No speed bumps. Just a smooth ride toward the GSWP World Title match. And since
Fang, coincidentally, is nowhere to be found -- probably because he has his head so far up his own ass that if he tries to talk, he'll eat his rectum away to nothingness -- you probably have an easy street chauffered drive to the
GSWP World Title as well. [Shawn's eyes suddenly fill with anger. His light brown eyes go from emotionless to a look of steel. His nose and mouth twitch as to signify uncomfortableness.] "S" SS: And _THAT'S_ where the problem comes in, Johnny. I worked my ass off for that title shot in the first place, only to be juked out of the title not once, but twice. All you did was be at the right place, at the right time and pin Fang in an eight man tag match. And, all of a sudden, you have a title match? I had Fang beaten twice and just 'cause out of pure luck, you get a shot?
["Showtime" spits on the ground, showing a definate sign of disgust. He reaches into his right pocket and pulls out the infamous pinky ring and puts it on his left pinky finger. He gives the ring a kiss and balls up his left hand, only to move it out of sight.]
"S" SS: Glamour, what happens if you actually have some bumps in the road if you become champ? Don't even answer,
Glamour, 'cause I'm goin' to find out firsthand. I'm not goin' to be your speed bump. However, what I will be is the one who intoxicates the chauffer who drives you down easy street. What I will be is the wall which he crashes the limo into. And what I will be is the man who gives the window of your limo a Sylkkalicious superkick, jumps in, makes you give the ring a kiss, and put you out of action like I did to Shawn Parker and like I will do this
Sunday night to that pushover mute, Brian Bradley. [Sylkk pauses and gives an evil, fiendish smile. His eyes light up and his signature smile creeps upon his face slowly.]
"S" SS: Hold on tight, Glamour, 'cause after this Sunday night, your ride on easy street will get a lot rougher when it's your turn to be on stage with "Showtime."
[Shawn Sylkk turns around and walks back down the alley as the scene slowly fades to black.]
