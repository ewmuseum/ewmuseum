*FLASH* 'KID CHARISMA' CHRIS QUILLER *FLASH*

4 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Let me know what you think of this, my third effort for GPW. ****************************** 'KID CHARISMA' CHRIS QUILLER ****************************** To: The various ranks of Global Pro-Wrestling From: Da Kid <<The scene opens in what appears to be a bar. The bare brickwork walls are dull red which play as an interesting contrast to the dull red and muddy-brown carpet. Various pictures and beer mats hang from crudely attached nails in the wall, most at a precarious angle. Standard wooden tables and chairs stand around in no particular fashion, mostly filled with drinking customers. Some of the other customers are seen propping up the solid-looking pinewood bar, standing behind which, is a young-looking girl with a short skirt and crop top. She smiles to the various men as they order drinks, stopping sometimes for an occasional word or two with one of them.
Over in the far corner is a sturdy pool table, strangely unoccupied. The table is standing on four thick wooden legs and has an unblemished blue canvas top, recently renewed. The cues stand in a rack on the wall opposite the table.>>
<<Two people walk into the bar from the left-side of the screen. The two men are recognisable as Chris Quiller and Jake Bevan, pro-wrestler and manager from Global Pro-Wrestling. Quiller is dressed in the same beige cargo pants from his first appearance on GPW 's Shockwave program but has changed in favour of a tight white shirt with 'Da Kid' in big black text across the back and 'Kid Charisma' across the front. Bevan is dressed in a casual blue business suit and shirt. The two meander over in the direction of the pool table and stop.>>
BEVAN: Fancy a drink Quiller? What'll it be? QUILLER: I'll have a beer Jake. _Not_ in a frickin' glass either. <<With that, Bevan departs for the bar, leaving 'Kid Charisma' to grab the set of pool balls and arrange them in the diamond formation of 9-ball pool.
Quiller is just choosing from the limited selection of cues when Jake Bevan returns with two bottles of beer.>>
BEVAN: Got it Quiller. Just as you asked. Now you ready for an ass-kicking on my behalf? I'm pretty damned fine at 9-ball.
<<Chris Quiller's eyebrows raise slightly as he smirks at the thought of being beaten.>> QUILLER: What?! That _ain't_ gonna happen Jake and you know it. Let's make it interesting shall we? Whaddya say to a $50 dollar bet? Winner takes all?
BEVAN: You're on hotshot. You break. <<Quiller grabs a cue from the rack and places the white ball on the spot. He strikes the ball dead-centre and shatters the 'diamond' of balls. The balls go everywhere including the red '3' ball which rolls into the far corner pocket. Quiller smiles in the direction of Bevan.>>
QUILLER: Whaddya make of that Jake? Looks like I'm gonna be $50 better off and I'll not have to wait too long either.
BEVAN: We'll see Quiller. Don't get your hopes up yet. Go on then, your shot again. <<Bevan reaches behind himself for a cue then stands patiently for 'Kid
Charisma' to take his next shot. Quiller lines up the cue and strikes the cue ball just to left side. It spins off to the right, ricocheting off of the cushion and hitting the '1' ball. The yellow ball hurtles towards the bottom corner and rattles in. Bevan leans up against the wall, still looking confident. Chris Quiller lines up a shot at the '2' ball and shoots. The ball slides over the blue canvas and into the pocket. Bevan is starting to look slightly worried as he chalks the end of his cue in anticipation.
Quiller again lines up his shot, this time at the '4' ball. He strikes the white cueball and sinks his target. The same pattern follows for all the balls upto and including the black '8' ball. During this period, Bevan is getting quite worried.>>
QUILLER: Heh heh heh. Not so confident _now_ are we Jake? No chance to pull out now. Get your wallet ready and prepare to be beaten.
BEVAN: I'm still sure I can beat you. I'm not usually wrong and this time I say you won't win.
QUILLER: Jake Bevan - always the optimist. Pah! This time you _are_ wrong. <<With yet another smug grin, Chris Quiller lines up his final shot. He pulls the cue back ready to strike when he is interrupted by his manager.>>
BEVAN: Oh. I forgot to tell you. I got you that match you wanted.
QUILLER: Really? Took your damn time. Well? Don't keep me waiting. BEVAN: The details came through this morning. QUILLER: ...and? BEVAN: You're fighting Nick Richard on Friday's Global Invasion. <<Quiller looks slightly puzzled as he hears the name of his opponent. He wrinkles his brow and appears to be thinking.>>
QUILLER: Nope. Can't think who the hell he is. BEVAN: He's... QUILLER: Don't bother Jake. I really don't care about Richard-whoever. I just care about the upcoming pay-per view. Have they booked my match yet? Do
I get my shot at Chris Page?
BEVAN: We're still awaiting news but I don't think you'll get the shot
Quiller. Looks good for you to be at Future Fight though.
QUILLER: Okay, I don't see why I don't get the shot but okay. Now back off and let me win my fifty bucks.
<<Chris Quiller, sets up again for his crucial shot, when again, Jake Bevan interrupts.>> BEVAN: By the way - did I talk to you about the Shockwave ratings yet? QUILLER: No? What have you got to say? BEVAN: Well, after your appearance on Shockwave two weeks ago, ratings went up by about 5%. I reckon it's because people are tuning into see 'Kid
Charisma'. That's good news Chris. Popularity could really give your GPW career a boost. QUILLER: You should know by now that I don't give a crap what the fans think or do. They come to see me wrestle and inadvertently pay my bills. That the way things work. A hate:hate relationship and that's the way I prefer it.
Now _shut up_ and let me get on with this. BEVAN: Sorry Chris. Go ahead. <<Quiller turns around, obviously agitated. He sets up for his pot but instead of preparing his aim, fires off a rocket of a shot. The cueball strikes the '9' ball with tremendous power and sends it flying toward the back pocket. As the ball nears the hole, it starts to slightly veer off course, and ultimately, ricochets off the pocket and off into the centre of the table. Chris Quiller closes his eyes for a brief moment of contemplation before looking towards a smiling Jake Bevan. Quiller says nothing but stands aside for his manager to have a crack at downing the final ball. He hits it dead on, sending it hurtling towards one of the middle pockets. The ball rolls into the hole and out of sight. Bevan lets off a small smile before turning around.>>
BEVAN: Luck Quiller. Nothing but... Pay up then. I won fairly [for once]. QUILLER: Hhmph... <<'Kid Charisma' lets off an angry sigh. He fumbles in his back pocket for
$50 and slams in down on the table. He throws his cue down on the table and storms off leaving his empty beer bottle on the table also. Bevan smiles triumphantly as he returns the cues and pool balls to their correct places, picks up his $50 and walks out of view.>>
