[RP CRITIQUE] Jedah

4 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey. I need some input. This flash is from a couple of months ago, I just need some input from you guys. I don't mind brutal comments, but keep it real. Don't want anything that would sound like it's coming from someone with the maturity of a ten-year old.
And for those who don't know him. Jedah is commonly mistaken for an Undertaker clone, which could be understandable. However, he's not. He believes in an event called "Extermination Agenda", where Death will select the next stage of
Mankind, elimination everyone who doesn't make the cut. Very gothic and sometimes enigmatic.
{{SCENE: It's a dark night in a cemetary, and we see a large figure kneel before two gravestones. He seems to be muttering something that can't be heard from this distance, but as he stands to his full height, we know who is paying respects. The massive Jedah slowly walks away from the gravestone, and the cameraman walks to meet him. Jedah watches the cameraman walk up and he stays still as he approaches. He stands in a long black overcoat, which covers a black shirt and pants. His pale face is without his usual make-up, but holding a somber look. He finds a park bench and sits down. The cameraman follows, and sets his camera on his tripod. He sits next to Jedah, who doesn't recognize him.}}
Jedah: A rival of mine had recently suffered a loss, which brought me here to pay my respects to my late wife and son. When I spoke with him, even though I was the last person he wished to speak with, he told me he was relieved that she had passed on. I told him that The Mistress knows when the human soul has suffered enough, and that The Mistress had taken her to her final destination.
I believe, for the first time, that my rival believed my words. I'd rather not speak of it anymore, for there are other events to be spoken about. Such as my debut in a Newcomers Battle Royal. It is somewhat typical, but I suppose it shall be the only way for myself to become introduced. And with thirteen other opponents, it shall be an interesting battle. Let's go over them one by one, given a small analysis of who I believe them to be from what little they have said. Some may think it to be rather hypocritical, but I have been known to tell what a person is like by the words he speaks.
Cameraman: Well, who's first? Jedah: There are only a few I can actually speak of. So few of them have spoken. Have they decided to remain silent? Mayhap, but it is no concern of mine. I shall not know anything of them, save the fact that they do not wish to face their opponents. So, I will speak of those who have had something to say.
Mike Masters, who believes to compare himself with the heroes of Greek Times. Any man can stand as a statue, but a real man admits that there are some out there who can defeat them. He refers to me and the remaining twelve as
Philistines? HA! I am of no such thing, Mike Masters. I don't doubt that you don't know who I am too clearly, as do many of the souls here within the ICW.
However, know this. Many men whom I have humbled were similar to you. Untouchable, and believing that the championship is their birthright. Mike
Masters, when you walk into the ring, you will find Jedah not to be as innocuous as you believe. I am far deadlier than any other man you have competed against. You are an arrogent soul... and as such, will be dealt with appropriately and in the same way as most arrogent souls... with minimal force and effort, for souls such as yours are never worth that effort.
Cameraman: Tough words for someone who just entered the ICW. I've seen better talk out of others...
Jedah: And if you wish to see more you would kindly keep your derogatory remarks to your own mind. I don't wish to hear them, and do it once more... and you will hear no more.
Cameraman: Fine. Who else is there? Jedah: Brian Storm. He believes the same as most rookies, we gain no respect. Well, it all depends on the man. When did your career start, Brian Storm? I started well over two years ago. My record speaks clearly. I may be new to the
ICW, but I am far from anyone's definition of a rookie... and no doubt, you are far from such as well, if you are experienced. If you have the material to back you up, then don't bother considering yourself a rookie, because you are above such a term. However, an attitude such as yours and overzealous self-confidence always leads to downfall. You have a beautiful escort, but she is only as valueable as the distractability of your opponent. Seeing that I am one of your opponents, her value is not too high. I am focused, I am determined, and I am directed. I had a female manage my career, and I do give her credit for enhancing my career as she did... but she was a bother. Her... significant other had a problem with me, and she broke the contract. Now, your ladyfriend may be loyal, but you can never be completely certain. What is to say she will not walk out on you?
Cameraman: Are you going anywhere with this? Or are you just going to talk about Luscious Lightning all night?
Jedah: You fail to see the point, simpleton. Most, and I say MOST, women cannot be completely trusted. That is my opinion, Brian Storm. Beautiful women such as her tend to bounce around, and not the way you would like it. They go from person to person, following the color that they desire... Gold. Do I care if she is not like that? Of course not. I, myself, follow a woman. "She" is my direction, "She" is my determination, and "She" is my focus. The High Dark
Mistress speaks to be, and she shall appear one day, when the Agenda is executed. But, the topic of women aside, Brian Storm. You have a lot of bravery to believe Jedah to be as easy as an opponent you may believe. You gave advice... now allow me to give some. Underestimate the Consort of Death... and soon you shall answer your folly directly to her!
Cameraman: And that Underground Quicksilver guy, he said something. Jedah: So he did... Cameraman: And? Jedah: And what? Cameraman: You had all that to say about your other two opponents who had something to say and you're not even making a comment on Underground
Quicksilver? Jedah: He wasted his breath... I see no reason to waste any of mine on such a small topic. But I shall say this, never will I stay my wrath because I believe one to be a waste of effort. The Fury of Jedah is never calmed and never-ending. So many believe themselves to be superior to everyone... and then they face me. And when my hand raises their bodies to the High Heavens and then plunges their worthless corpses into the Burning Hells, they find themselves humbled... and not as high-up as they believe themselves to be. I have been in numerous battles, and my defeats are few. I do not greatly boast of my ability, for it leaves me to be as vunerable as my foes. I am not self-absorbed, but I am proud of my accomplishments. For they are my victories, and they belong to no other man.
Cameraman: Sounds like you are boasting to me, pal. Jedah: It matters not what you hear, but what you shall soon see. I do not promise the world, as so many others do, that I will topple all thirteen of my opponents... but I will be the victor of this conflict. For many can try...
they can run and hide as much as they desire... but no one escapes the icy grip of the High Dark Mistress... As it is written in the Book of Damnation... "But he that hides a dark soul and foul thoughts... Benighted, walks under the mid-day sun... Himself is his own dungeon..." Do not fear her grip, but embrace it... But run from it... and trap yourself within your own mind...

Jedah: For the time of Final Retribution is at hand... and it shall be the Hand of the Mistress that brings you to your final reward... and your... final...
breath! {{The motorcycle revvs off, making very little sound. He is lost in the midnight horizon. End of Scene.}}
