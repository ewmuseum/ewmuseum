Sample rp: Tell me what you think

17 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
< The jam packed crowd of the RCW bustles about doing their normal business when a darkness befalls them. Silence accompanies it, for they sit quietly in wonder of what's to happen next... With a loud thunder, the theme song of long awaited wrestler Caine comes on. "Cumbersome" plays through out the now screaming arena as their local small town hero makes his way to the arena; his face expressionless, the tail of his overcoat following at his ankles, and his
RCW World Champion belt gleaming in the stage light. As he nears the end of the walk way, he stops... a pause ... and he throws his arms into the air, his head back, and eyes closed. The audience screams at a volume to rival that of a Metallic concert, front row. He sits taking in the admiration, then continues his march into the ring. Once there, he removes the belt, holds it high letting the thunderous crowd get a good look, and then throws it before him. He pulls a mic out of his pocket and starts talking>
Caine: Mr. Hard Core Jack Roberts... You want this belt so bad, why don't you take it! <audience cheers louder. He waits for them to calm down, and then continues> We've been at it for what? 3, 4 feds now. You always chasing me around wanting another beating... Well, I've had enough! < his face grows dark, and his eyes burn cold. The audience still yelling> It's now or never.
I'm ready to face you man to man ... if that's what you call yourself. Come out here and stair death into the eyes, for your time is up. Only the strong survive, so come out here now and speed up the process of elimination! Let me show you what Darwinism's all about, because if you're not out here right now,
I'm coming to your house tonight with a camera crew following. Your days were numbered last week, and that number's ran out... It's time to face up, and pay your dues! <he throws the mic far into the audience, and grabs his belt, holding it high above his head, as if to say "Come and get it!". He jumps the ropes with the belt on his shoulder, then holds it up again as he walks down the isle with the audience now chanting his name>
