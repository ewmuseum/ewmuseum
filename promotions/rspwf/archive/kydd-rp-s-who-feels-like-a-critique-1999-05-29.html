[Kydd RP's] Who feels like a critique?

29 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm sure the spacing is all messed up, but try maximizing your screen. It may help! I did this a few weeks back, it's nothing special. In fact, I am not real sure why I even posted it! I guess I am just so damn'd happy that I finally finished mulching my damn yard! So, tell me what 'cha think!
[Fade in...] [The scene is the skyline of "The City of Lights", Las Vegas, Nevada. We see the flashy lights all around! People from not only all over this country, but all over the world are walking around the busy streets, trying to get from here to there. The camera pans around, and we see the MGM Grand, The LuXor, Ceasars
Palace...]
[The camera focuses on a jet black 1999 Mustang convertible cruising down the street. We can here the faint sounds of "More Human than Human" by White
Zombie playing in the car. As the camera gets closer, we hear the sounds more clearly. In the drivers seat, we see none other than the GLOW's Goldenboy, and one and only _Show Stopper_ Scott Jamison. Jamison has on a pair of Oakly
E-Wire glasses. Along side Jamison as always is the very lovely Miss Tammi
Winters. Miss Winters has on a pair of matching glasses. ]
[The car is stopped at a red light, as a GLOW camera crew quickly approaches to get a look at the MEGAStar. Jamison sees the crew coming, and takes off his glasses. Jamison is always ready for an interview, reguardless of the time and situation! Jamison looks blankly at the camera for a few moments. He then smiles, and begins to speak in that cocky tone of voice that we have grown to know and love...]
SSSJ - You guys just don't quit, do 'ya? Heh.. it's all good, I enjoy all the camera time I can get. And
'ya know, I got a funny feeling that I am gonna' be gettin' a lot more exposure now a days. You all saw my more than impressive showing against the other top cruisers in the game, and I didn't back down from any of them. GLOW Lightweight champ, Tim Beck... I know that you and I are cool, real cool... but you didn't beat me just yet.
'Ya see, Tim... You may have the strap that gives you the claim of a champion, but in my eyes...
the eyes of _the Show Stopper_, you haven't impressed anybody just yet. You didn't pin me, you didn't pin Boltz, you pinned my tag team partner, Tyrone Parker. I think it was a fluke myself, but that is past us. We are in the Now, and Tim Beck, __I__ am the now. I am the future of this company, and I am the man that should be wearin' the gold in the lightweight division.
Speakin' of my tag team partner, "International Excellence" Tyrone Parker, did anyone catch the Wednesday show? Oh Lord... did we do a number on those guys! "The Big Mean Guys from the Streets"
sure sounded like an intimidating team, but my main man Park' and I showed them what was up. Did you see that Super Kick I layed the one dude out with?
_PERFECTION..._ but did you expect less? Nope. I didn't think so. 'Ay King Apples. I suggest you and the lint man watch 'yer backs. There are some new guns in town. The new age of wrestling for the GLOW...
Style Inc. is the best tag team that this federation has to offer, and we are going to prove it in the next few weeks.
Right now, I have some other business to tend to. Damon Knight. The man who claims to be so "Devistating"... [Jamison smirks...] Yeah right... Listen here, punk... its time that you got put in your place. It's time that you learned that you don't just step into a federation like the Great
Lakes Online Wrestling association and get a shot at a big name like me. I am going to hurt you, Damon
Knight. I am going to make you wish you never stepped foot in the GLOW. I am going to make you cry. How can I be so confident?
_Easy..._ Because I am that good. I have proved it in the past few weeks, and I will continue to prove it from now until the end of time. After I piece up Tim
Beck for the Lightweight Strap, I am gunnin' for ole' Es Tee Be. But it starts with you, Damon Knight. My journey starts because yours is going to end. I bet 'yer pretty pissed at President Russel for startin' ya off against me, huh... I know I would be. ___Follow the Super Kick road.... Cause you just met.... THE WIZARD OF AWES____ [The light turns green, and Jamison drives off into the beautiful Las Vegas night... We again hear "More Human Than Human..."]
[Fade Out...] Kydd ~RSPWF's "Wizard of _AWES_!"~ Handles "The Show Stopper" "The Golden Boy" "The Franchise" ~(_Scott Jamison_)~
