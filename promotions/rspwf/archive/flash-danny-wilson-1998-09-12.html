This is a old RP I did in CoW a while back, so I decided to post it. Tell me what ya think!
< Danny Wilson> [Scene] The scene opens up inside a Ice Cream Truck. The Ice Cream Man has a big afro, bell-bottoms, platform shoes, and a tight light green shirt. The kids are all jumping around. But instead of the normal Ice Cream Truck music, It is playing "It's Raining Men". One if the Kids starts to sing along, "It's Raining
Men", But stops when a man on a bike rides bye. The Ice Cream Man changes the music to the Y-M-C-A. All of the kids start doing the Y.M.C.A till a rattling in the back is heard. A man dressed in all black knocks the Ice Cream Man out with a hammer and takes a note out, he tapes it on the man, It read's
"Supersonics Future". The man rips of the ski mask, and reveals himself to be
"High Impact" Danny Wilson. He changes the music to "Do the Hustle" Then he rips the microphone from the buckle of his pants.

[Man] What are you doing? [DW] See SuperSonic!! This is what is gonna happen to you!! You and Paul Clark can pretty much see the future, and that is me being crowend the CoW World
