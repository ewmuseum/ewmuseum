[Sample Roleplay] Jason "the Playa" Geribo

5 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a roleplay I usually send as a sample roleplay to get into feds, I am sending it here because some big time feds might want to look it over and second guess me. Also if someone that regularly critiques roleplays could look this one over and help me out I would greatly appreciate it. Thank you.
== == == == == == == == == == == == == == [the screen is filled with a shot of many women and men inside of a house. The camera seems to be in an arial view or on a set of staircases since the shot is showing the top of everyone's head. There is music in the background. It seems like "Super Thug" by NOREAGA. The camera man looks to be walking upstairs now.
He reaches the top in there is a long hallway with many room doors closed. He walks to the first door and knocks. There is a moan and then a deep voice that yells.]
"Get th' hell outta here." [the camera man then moves to the next door. He knocks and a man answers with...] "Who is it?" [the camera man answers the man with...] "It is a wrestling reporter from Los Angeles, I am here to conduct an interview." [the man pauses with the answer then says...] "Okay, come in." [the door opens and there is a white man with short black hair laying under the sheets with a woman beside him. The man appears to be sweating and has a cigarette pressed between his lips. The woman is a white woman with long blonde hair a little longer then her shoulders. She is lying against the man and seems to be trying to sleep. The man starts to speak in a low, near whispering voice.]
"You are here to interview me? Why is that? Is it because of my looks, my skills, or did I get in the bed with your wife?"
[the camera man replies.] "I am not doubting your looks, and I do not think you have been with my wife. I am here to ask you a few questions about your wrestling life."
[the man nods and replies.] "Ahh yes, my skills. Well, as you probably know, I have been in many federations before. That is where I showed some of my skills, the only problem was that the promotions that had the privilege of having me were, how can I say this? Not up to speed with me."
[the camera man cuts him off short.] "So where did you get the name of the Playa?" [the man answers with a sharp look in his eyes.] "My name is Jason Geribo, I am the playa with the most skills in _and_ out of the ring. The ladies know exactly why I am what I say I am, yet they still coming back for more, why you ask? Well, for the simple reason of, the sex is _incredible_ And they do not care if they are being used because I am giving them something there husbands cannot. That is almost like the way I am in the ring. I am using you to get what I want. I am using you as a guinea pig for the world to see what happens when you mess with the best. That is just the way I operate."
[the camera man shakes Jason's hand as the woman wakes up.] "Honey, who is this?" [Jason answers.] "A man that was just leaving." [the camera shows the door then turns around and the woman is gone from view, the only thing you see as the camera fades is something moving up and down under the sheets in front of Jason.]
