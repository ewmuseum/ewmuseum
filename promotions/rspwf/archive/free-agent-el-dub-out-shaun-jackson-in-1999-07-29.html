RSPWFers, This is my newest character. I created him in about an hour and a half. Regardless of that, I am looking for one fed to get his skills sharpened up in. I will not start seriously handling him until school begins, but I would like to get some kind of experience with him.
I have not typed up a role play for him yet, but I will do so this evening. If you would like him in your federation, please
e-mail me at [detail withheld] . Thank you very much for showing interest in him. Here are in stats in the most complicated application I have ever written -- the OWF's. I know that AOL's newsreader is going to totally screw up the formatting, so you might want to open it up in Notepad with an 800x600 screen resolution.
I hope to hear from you soon. Dan. handler, Shaun Jackson --------- STATS --------- ------------------- HANDLER INFORMATION ------------------- Your Name : [name withheld] Your E-mail : [detail withheld] Your AIM/ICQ : [detail withheld] Your Age : 16 -------------------- WRESTLER INFORMATION -------------------- Wrestler's Name : Shaun Jackson Wrestler's Weight : 262 Wrestler's Height : 6'5" Wrestler's Age : 26 Wrestler's Hometown : Los Angeles, California ---------------------------- WRESTLER GIMMICK INFORMATION ---------------------------- Wrestler's Story : Shaun Jackson was born in Los Angeles, California, in 1973. His mother Danielle Nelson-Jackson and his father Jerome Jackson had worked hard to move into their new surburban home away from the dangerous city.
Danielle and Jerome raised Shaun with as much respect for morality and dignity as possible.
Despite the fact that they did live in the surburbs,
Danielle and Jerome
knew that they couldn't escape the gangs and crime, so they taught
Shaun for as long they could to stay away from the gangs. Unfortunately, after being threatened, Shaun joined a gang. However,
Shaun never
participated in criminal activities of the gang; he chose to further his education, while picking up a tip or two on being tough.
Proud that their son managed to avoid death and jail,
Danielle and Jerome
chose to fulfill their son's one and wish -- to be able to attend the
University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA). While attending UCLA, Shaun befriended an amateur wrestler, who was looked upon as one of the best college wrestlers in the country. The friend told
Shaun to attend
one of his wrestling meets, and he eventually persuaded him to go.
After watching his friend steamroll through the competition, Shaun waited for outside of the locker rooms. However, after hearing a few scuffles and some shouting inside the locker room, Shaun decided to see what was going on. When he rushed in he saw his buddy being jumped by three fairly big guys. Shaun quickly rushed in and
no-selled a couple of punches, before sending a guy through a wooden bench with a military press slam. At this time, another man heard the fighting and also came in the locker room, but this man was none other than legendary pro wrestler Lawrence Williams -- and he saw the whole thing.
It just so happened to be that Shaun was a fan of Lawrence's, and when Lawrence said that he was impressed with Shaun's natural skills and offered him a spot under his agent, Shaun couldn't say no. Of course, his amateur wrestling friend was a bit offended, since amateur wrestlers tend to look down upon professional wrestling, but after being trained by the younger Williams for over a year after college Jackson is ready to stake his claim in the world of professional wrestling.
Wrestler's Attitude : Being raised by militaristic parents has really helped build Shaun Jackson into a no-nonsense type of guy. He has always been big and intimidated, but his disciplined and never-say-die attitude is what makes him such a worthy contender in any federation. Shaun was raised on morals, so you will not see this man letting any sort of fame go to his head. A real class act.
Wrestler's Gimmick : Like previously stated, Shaun Jackson is a no-nonsense type of guy. He accepts nothing less but excellence, and if he has given a raw deal, you can expect him to do something about.
Wrestler's Enemies : Shaun hates laziness. He is disciplined beyond the point of expected discipline. He hates people that have a lack of morals, integrity, or respect. Basically, anyone who supports in any way, shape, fashion, or form the deprecation of society is a sworn enemy of Shaun
Jackson.
------------------------- WRESTLER MOVE INFORMATION ------------------------- Wrestler's Style : He is a top-notch brawler with a hint of technical wrestling skills. What Lawrence saw in that locker room wasn't mat or submission wrestling -- it was raw,
in-your-face brawling, and that is what he helped Shaun specialize. In other words, don't expect to see Shaun winning matches with a sharpshooter any time soon.
Wrestler's Strengths : Power. Obviously, Shaun is a very powerful main. He spent over a year working in intensive work-outs, helping to build his upper and lower body. He has a lot of power, and when he hits you, you'll know it. You will not see a lot of power disadvantages in his matches.
Discipline. He's very disciplined, so you will not find him getting into things with his arrogance or cockiness. He knows where the line is, and he dares not to cross it.
And with that discipline comes focus, the ability to concentrate on your opponent and _only_ your opponent when in the ring. He does not need to worry about anyone on the outside, because his discipline tells him to worry about your most dangerous threat.
Heart. He has a lot of heart, which was gained when he was able to go onto college despite being in a gang. He is a survivor, and anyone who is able to survive adds a little mental strength to himself or herself. This man has survived a lot, thus having a lot of mental strength. He will not give up until he is able to walk no more.
Wrestler's Weaknesses : Inexperience. This is obviously his number one weakness. He has never wrestled a single wrestling match in his life, so this is a learning experience for him. Expect his big heart to lead him into traps in matches, such as chasing an antagonizing opponent around the ring, only to get knocked-out by his manager. Things that veterans know _not_ to do are things that
Shaun will do.
Heart. While a strength, his heart is definitely going to lead him into deadends -- deadends that may cost him a title or his career. He won't stop fighting until his opponent gives up in any way or he beaten into such a bloody pulp that breathing is his biggest challenge. He doesn't know how to stop fighting, whether physically or mentally.
