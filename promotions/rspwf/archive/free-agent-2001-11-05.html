Free Agent

5 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys I'm looking for a new fed to join. I'd like to find a reliable league that will accept my one singles wrestler and tag-team. (total 3 guys) I'm not into hardcore leagues, they have been done to death. A sample roleplay is below, not my best but hey.... I'm a little out of practice. Anyway, if you have any openings in your fed please send an e-mail to me at
[detail withheld].... Thanks! [We fade in to a three bedroom town home where three men are sitting around watching an Indianapolis Colts football game. All three men appear to be in top physical condition, as well they should be after just completing their training to become professional wrestlers.]
Jordan: Hey guys, now that we are done with our training what's our next step? I mean where do we go from here? Adam: All I know is that I don't want to compete in any of those minor league promotions. We need to try our best to get some kind of deal with one of the bigger leagues so we can at least get ourselves on national television.
Prince: There are plenty of opportunities out there fella's, we just have to decide which one we will take. We graduated from our wrestling training faster than anyone else in our class, we are the cream of the crop and the offers will soon begin to come in very soon.
Jordan: Um, are you that sure about those offers coming in? I mean, we haven't wrestled a professional match yet so all the big promotions out there don't know who we are so why would they want us?
Prince: You keep asking dumb questions like that and the only people that will get offers is Adam and I. The major federations out there are like any major league baseball team, like any of the major college programs, like any other team that is considered to be the best in their respective sport. They all have scouts, and when real talent is out there they find it. Scouts know about us you can guarantee that, and now that they know we are ready for action they will find us.
Adam: I agree with Prince, Jordan we are headed for greatness. You and I, we are a great tag-team, and Prince is a great singles wrestler. Just imagine,
Prince wins a World Championship and we win a World Tag-Team Championship. How great would that be?
Jordan: I could handle that. Jordan Valentine and Adam Destiny, World Tag-Team
Champions. Man that has a nice ring to it.
Adam: Yeah, that does have a nice ring to it but you know what? I think it would sound better this way. Adam Destiny and Jordan Valentine, World Tag-Team
Champions.
Jordan: Nah, I like it better my way. Prince: Will you two stop it? It doesn't matter which name goes first, you would be the best either way.
Adam: That's true, we would be the best. Jordan: Okay, let's forget that. My big question is what league would we want to go to? I mean are we just going to accept the best financial offer we get or are we going to go with the offer that represents the best chance for us to get the most TV time.
Adam: I love money, it's what makes the world go round baby. Prince: Yeah money is good but we need to go with what offers us the best chance at stardom and to get stardom you need exposure. You might get a better offer with a lesser promotion, but without exposure the ceiling of opportunity is limited. Now take a lesser offer with more exposure, you can get more well known and that in turns brings a higher financial ceiling. Quick money isn't always the best way to go.
Jordan: So what promotions does that mean we should consider? Prince: Well, there's the EMWC, RAW, KCW, NEW, RCW, LWC, NEO. Adam: Don't forget the UWF and WWO. Prince: See there are plenty of options out there, we just have to find the promotions that want us or are willing to give us a shot ans then try and decide which is the best fit.
Jordan: Well, let's try to all stick together. I'd hate it if Prince went to one promotion and we had to go to another.
Adam: Well, I say if we get the opportunity and the contract will allow us to work multiple promotions we try to do that.
Prince: Yeah, that's the route to go. The more promotions we can work the more exposure we can get. Thing is though, we have to make sure that wherever we compete we give one hundred and ten percent. It wouldn't do any of us any good to work in multiple promotions and be so beat up that we spread ourselves thin.
We only work where we know we can give it our all. I do agree with the trying our best to stick together.
Adam: I'm with you both on that one. We stuck together through thick and thin during our training, I'm not about to split with you two now. Besides, together we would be more effective than being on our own.
Jordan: We are going to be a three man wrecking crew once we get started. Prince: I'm just anxious to get started, I'm getting tired of sitting around. I want the offers to start coming in and we need to figure out a way to get that to happen.
Adam: Well, do you think we can get any of that footage of our training matches? You know the ones I'm talking about, the tapes we watched of ourselves to critique what we were doing right and what we were doing wrong?
Prince: Yeah, we could probably do that. I think I have those tapes somewhere,
I'll try to find them so we can submit them to some of the bigger promotions.
Jordan: Did you see that? Manning just threw a touchdown pass to Marvin Harrison! Man that kid has an arm. [Scene fades to black with all three men turning their attention back to the football game.]
