"Twister RP-Please Critique"

16 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
--[ As the scene fades in, from the "IPWF Legends" promo, we are subjected to the monotoned funeral music which will head off the beginning of Twister's IPWF funeral. The words "God Bless You, Twister" fade in on the big screen, while
President Chuck Buckman, followed by two men carrying a coffin, descend down the rampway. As the camera pans the audience, we see signs of remembrance for the recently deceased, Twister. Once inside the ring, the coffin is placed on the center of the canvas, and the two men backup to opposite corners of the ring. Buckman then pulls a microphone from his coat pocket, and with much trouble he begins to speak ]--
Buckman: First off..I would like to welcome you all to tonight's event, but I cannot say that everyone will be the top of the line. Twister was a big part of what the IPWF stood for, he came into this federation with his head up high, and knew just what to do to make himself famous..and he did it. He rode with the Extreme Revolution and brought them to stardom, but when an opportunity came for even more greatness on his part..he turned his back on them. A genius in the industry..and outside of the industry. During his career here he held numerous titles including the World Title twice as well as the Television twice. Twister fought some of the IPWF's greatest matches to date. Fighting with the likes of Fabu, Crippler, Scorpion, Exhibitionist, and many more..he became "the man". Now we remember him..here tonight...and I am sure...you will agree with me when I say....we'll miss you, Twister.
--[ A dark, gloomy feeling passes over the entire arena, and even Chuck Buckman begins to shed tears. All of a sudden, a man wearing a fisherman's cap, a yellow winter jacket, and baggy jeans leaps over the guardrail and slides into the ring. He crawls over to the coffin and begins to sob tremendously. The two men who carried out the coffin try to pull him away, but they come to a sudden halt and their eyes widen. As the men step back, the mysterious fan stands..his face covered by the shadow of the hat. Removing the hat, he takes a bow, and when he comes up we see the sly grin of....TWISTER!?!?! ]--
Twister: HELLO OUT THERE IN TV LAND!!! HaHaHaHaHaHa... --[ As he unzips his coat, he pulls out the World Title and places it on the ground before him. He then reaches into the netted side pockets and pulls out...A special edition Star Wars Nestle Crunch Bar? He takes a bite then starts begins to preach his good word ]--
Twister: Hmmmmm....Yoda tastes kinda funny. What the..?? This microphone actually works? I thought the good man in the sky jus wanted me to make an appearance an' accept some kinda award...ya know to send down under with me...but I guess not. Thas ok, I see your reasoning for this....I need no award to tell me about my su-per-ior talent compared to all the unoriginal bastards in the back...I need no award to tell me that I am "the man"..hahaha...right Chuck? But let ME tell YOU that I love each..and..every..one of you! Hahaha...I mean, who else would go through the troubles of screwing you all over so many damn times? Certainly not
Scorpion..certainly not Crippler...certainly not..anybody! --[ Twister halts his speech to dodge a few pieces of trash that is hurled at the ring..more specificly..at him ]-- Twister: Ha...you missed me bitch! Hahaha...but to tell you all the straight down diggity truth, the real reason I was resurrected for you all this very evening was NOT to screw you over...oh no, for that is just a given..but the reason I came here tonight is to announce what will happen to the title I so rightfully held for my entire return in the IPWF. Yes, I returned...and I conquered..but the shameless beatings I gave all of you was nothing to me. You people are nothing...the so-called wrestlers are nothing...and this title means something. Deadly combination....the low class losers in the back all wanting this prestigious title...tsk tsk tsk. You know sumtin? I almost got as teary eyed as Chuck here right before I hopped into this ring. After I "died", I was looking through some ol' interview recordings and some of the latest matches performed by the "superstars" of the IPWF...and I dropped to my knees and sulked. What is wrong with you people? The mindless babbling idiots you cheer for are only fueled by you..they will continue to be more and more incompetent and idiotic....all because of you people. The reason this federation is on its' downfall...besides the fact that I left...is because of...YOU PEOPLE!
--[ The echoing sound of boo's intensify...Twister just laughs...]-- Twister: Now now...settle down little ones...I did come here for a reason remember. Show some respect for the estatic twistmatic. Now, where was I? Oh yes..the World Title status. Who is left to even come close to my talent? Who is good enough to hold this prestigous title? Haha...some prestige. We have guys in the category with Syxx Kid and Homewrecker....wanting a title shot?!?
Do you people believe that will ever happen? Now I know youre thinkin that the
World Title is only fit for one man...that being me..but I have to dissapoint you all here today. I know you wanted me to stay the champ forever...but I am gone....and the men I have chosen...well....arent. So I sayeth this...on July
31st...we will hold a special Saturday Night Bloodbath. You got it...a heavyweight title match to end all heavyweight title matches, the biggest event to hit the grandstand...THE HARDCORE HEAVYWEIGHT MATCH!
OoooOOOooooo....AaaaaAaaaaaa..haha! Dont get your hopes up fans...these people cant entertain. But the ones that will be trying ever so hard to make crap...look like a match will be...
--[ Twister points to the Big Screen and the list of names appear. the names being...The Natural, Scorpion, Crippler, Atomic Mass, Exhibitionist, One member from MOS only, and the Unknown Champion ]--
Twister: there you have it folks...the losers who will make losing look worse. Now, this leaves some questions to be answered..problems to be pondered in your peonic minds. Questions like "Why did Twister get the power to do that?".
Well prepare yourelves for the shocker my friends....I am now "the official man". Haha...you got it...the big Vice-President. Good versus Evil....G vs.
E....Shaeffer against me. Two VP's you ask? I answer with deep remorse....yes. So just like that old song says.....I'VE GOT THE POWER!
Hahahahaha....peace...I'm out. --[ Twister laughs as the fans boo and hiss at the once fan favorite. Then, as the camera begins to fade into another commerial, twister walks over and drops a rose on the downed IPWF President. Finally, the camera fades away to a
"Ringmaster" PPV promo ]--
