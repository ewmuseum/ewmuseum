RP - CHECK OUT MY BRILLIANCE ALL YE MORTALS [and then tell me how great you think i am]

18 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene fades in on a glass of Jack Daniels. We ponder the significance of this glass for a minute. Then a massive hand reaches out and grabs the glass from out of camera view. We slowly zoom out. Now we can see the hand belongs to Chavez, the insane drunken Native American. He is wearing a black T-shirt that reads "Too Drunk to Fuck" in bold white letters on the front, a pair of faded bell-bottom jeans and cowboy boots. He also has a blue bandanna, authentic Rolex and lots of gold chains on him. He holds the glass in the air for a minute. Then toasts himself. Then he pours the drink straight down his throat, slams the glass on the table and falls of his seat. While Chavez is trying to get back to his feet, the camera pans around the room. It looks like we are in a bar but there is nobody else there with the exception of the bartender, who is cleaning glasses behind the bar. Then we hear a drunken voice off-camera]
"Hey, over here you fool!" [A hand reaches out, twirls the camera around. Now we can see Chavez again. He's standing, well barely standing, and pointing at the camera] "Alright, there's three of ya! And one of me! But I can still take ya!" [Chavez takes a wild swing in the general direction of the camera and falls flat on his face]
[Voice off-camera] "Chavez, what's gotten into you man? You are worse than usual!" [Chavez picks himself up off the floor, dusts himself off then grabs a bottle of Tequila that is resting on a nearby table. He takes a huge swig then sits down on the floor]
"Chavez has lost all sense of direction. Chavez doesn't know where his career is heading. What about me? What about Chavez?"
[He turns his back to the camera and continues to guzzle the cheap booze] "Ever since....Well, you know. Since THAT federation folded, Chavez has been doing a lot of thinking. What is next for Chavez? Where is Chavez career heading? Is it all worth it?"
[Chavez gets up off the floor and starts pacing, sort of stumbling around the room] "But there is no time for Chavez to think. They are already lining them up. There is much work for Chavez.....Gimme that!" [Chavez reaches for something off-camera. It looks like a piece of paper. We zoom in on the paper and it looks like a IHW lineup sheet]
"Let's see Chavez is scheduled to wrestle....." [He looks closer, sort of not believing what he is seeing] "This is a joke right?" [He reaches for the bottle and takes a HUGE swig then slams it down on a nearby table] "Raeko? RAEKO??" [He starts pacing around the room at a more tedious pace] "They are going to put me in the ring with some guy named Raeko? And not expect somebody to go to the hospital? This must be a joke! Where is the REAL competition? [switching to Native accent] It is said that one who has lots of patience will always thrive over those who seek to slaughter sacred buffalo
[back to normal] Bull fucking shit! I want, I want.....Jenna Jameson! Get me a match with Jenna!"
[voice off-camera] "Well the match is signed with Raeko...." [Chavez has a fit, throwing chairs around the room. Then seemingly, settles down a tad] "But alright let's see what he have here. Raeko, The Spiritual Warrior. Excuse me, I think I'm going to be sick! Who would be cruel enough to put this young buffalo inside the ring with Chavez? But the damage has been, or will be, done. You see this brave has been many, many battles. Chavez might not know a drop-toe-hold from a suplex but he does know one thing. How to make your life very, very miserable. ....."
[Chavez sits down on the floor with his head in his hands] In the meantime, where is my shot at the big time? Where is my glory? What about me? What about Chavez?"
[Chavez gets up and suddenly he overturns a table, knocking glasses to the floor in a loud crash. Then starts to stumble out of the bar. He turns around for a moment]
[Voice off-camera] "Chavez, where are you going?" [Chavez looks into the camera with a steely gaze but with a certain sadness and desperation in his face]
"I don't know....." [Chavez slowly walks out the door while "Where Have All the Cowboy's Gone?" by
Paula Cole sputters out of the juke book. Scene fades to black on this Oscar worthy moment......]
