-[Aidan Madison] RP Review And Update

7 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Update: 'Playboy' Aidan Madison is currently going into a match with The Mad
Goat and Greg "What-Ever-His-Last-Name-Is' in the semi-finals for WAR!'s
Liberty Title. -[ Scene ]- The scene opens up on Aidan Madison's private airplane headed for WAR!'s next card. You can see right away that 'Playboy' Aidan Madison is on the plane. The camera man is sitting right beside him as he sips on a un-indentified drink.
Aidan Madison looks into the camera and begins to talk. -[ 'Playboy' Aidan Madison ]- So, I guess this is the big time. It isn't more than I expected it. Although,
I guess I can say I'm still un-experienced. I did beat Super Heavy Stan...but to tell you the truth, how hard is that? Not too hard. But, I'm still a rookie and I still have alot to learn. And I think I'm about to get a learning experience from Greg Allocca and The Mad Goat. Now, I'm being totally serious here. Greg, dammit, are we really facing a Mad Goat? Or is that just some mentally disturbed guy's name? Either way, I'm afraid to step into the ring with that guy.....or goat. I'm not saying I won't be able to beat him because I will. Then, it makes me think because what happens if it really is a goat. All he would have to do is give Greg and I a swift kick in the nuts and we will both be down for a long three count. Ah, I got that taken care of. That is why they make cups for protection. I have 'The Mad Goat' out of the way for now. So now, I guess I have to think about Greg Allocca.
Greg, tell me this. What do you have againest me? Why did you attack me at
4.0? Is it because you know that a newcomer like me is better than a vetran like you? I best that is it. Don't worry Greg...I won't embrasses you too bad when we meet in the ring. And as for the fact that you think I'm an alien.....Geeze, I'm not even going to go there. Talk to you later.
-[ Scene ]- The camera goes blank.
