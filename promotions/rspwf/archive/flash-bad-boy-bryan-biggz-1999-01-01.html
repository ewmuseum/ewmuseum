{Flash} "Bad Boy" Bryan Biggz

1 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{All across America people are tuning into AIWA , one of the hottest wrestling broadcasts on television today. Todays show begins with Conrad Alexander standing by the back entrance awaiting the next wrestler to enter so that he can cap an interview , but little did he know the Bad Boy was making his way in and he's in a Bad Mood!}
  Conrad Alexander: Excuse me ... Biggz ... Conrad Alexander here , I wonder if I may have a moment of your time. You have just signed an exclusive contract with AIWA , maybe you'd like to say a few words to the fans.   Bryan Biggz: Ok , first of all Conrad {Said in a Smart-Ass manner} ... my friends call me Biggz ... So you can call me
Mr.Biggz ... got that!  As far as the fans of the AIWA , nothing really needs to be said.  I mean take a look around ... you have Bryan Biggz , then you have the rest of these guys.
Most of which wouldn't know an Wristlock from a Wrist watch.  There's only one reason that a fed signs Bryan Biggz
... that's becuase they know Bryan Biggz equals dollarsigns. I've soldout arena's from Nebraska to Nantuchet!  Why? Becuase the fans know a true God when they see one!
  Fact is I'm really here for only one reason ... that reason is Candyman.  I offered this guy a challenge. A challenege to step in the ring and prove himself a true champion , as he likes to be known , but instead of signing the contract ... he tucked his tail and he ran! Well he doesn't have anywhere to run to now!  Candyman ... I'm here now and I will do everything in my power to make your life a living hell , until that day I finally get you in that ring!  You claim to be a legend , a rolemodel and a champion ... I claim you're nothing more then an overweight chump!
  But as we all know certain steps must be taken before an event of such magnitude can take place.  And this Thursday night step one goes by the name of X-treme.  I highly doubt his name represents his abilities and you can bet your a$$ that I will expose him for the fraudulant , no-talent that he really is!  You call yourself X-treme ...
well this Thursday Night , you're going to extremely have your butt kicked!  Any questions !?!
  Conrad Alexander: Well Mr.Biggz , thank you for your time and ....   Bryan Biggz: Did I at any point in time say that I was finished!?!  Conrad just stand there and hold the mic like a good little interviewer and shut your piehole!  Now .... before I go put on the wrestling gear and get a little workout going , I have a few more things to address!
  Devon Case ... seems that you don't know when to stop running your mouth.  I hear that ever since my name came up here in the AIWA ... you've been talking a whole lotta smack.  You've been calling Bryan Biggz a b-rate jobber. Well son , if thats the case why don't you learn to read and write a little better and sign your name on the dotted line and let's me and you find out who the b-rate really is!  From what I hear
... you haven't lost a match yet here in the AIWA , but that doesn't surprise me
... I'd probably be undefeated to if I hid behind the New Breed and challenged all my opponents to a No DQ thumb wrestling contest!  Fact is Case , you couldn't shine my boots ... little lone last in the ring with me.  But anytime you feel man enough ... just sign the line and get ready to feel your shoulders on the mat for a one ... two ... three!
  That's all and Conrad just remember ... Bryan may not like you ... but boy Jesus loves you! HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!   {With that Bryan Biggz walks off still laughing , as Conrad stands there with a look of shock and amazment on his face.}
