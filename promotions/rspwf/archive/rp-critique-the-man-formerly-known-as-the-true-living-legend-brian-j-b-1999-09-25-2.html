RP Critique - "The Man Formerly Known As The True Living Legend" Brian J. Blottie

25 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is the RP that leads into the RP in question. Read and enjoy. FYI - Dan Mazin is the color commentator for the fed, and a friend of Blottie's that likes doing him "favors" like this.
______________________ The arena is packed this night, as over ten thousand rowdy fans are ready for a great night of action. Then, the arena turns from a chorus of excitement to a chorus of boos, as "Head Like A Hole" by Nine Inch Nails starts blaring
over the loud speakers. As the music is played, Brian J. Blottie emerges from behind the curtain on the entrance ramp. He slowly walks down to the ring with purpose, a look of total and utter concentration on his face. As he climbs into the ring, he looks around him in disgust at the blantant disregard the pro-wrestling fans have for him. But at the same time, it thrills him that so many people have followed him enough to know to hate him. He walks over to the side of the ring, asks for a mic, and is given it. He walks back to the middle of the ring, and begins to speak.
"Let's get passed all the formalities. I'm not here to talk about how great
I am, and how I'm going to turn "The Devil's Advocate" into deviled eggs. I'm not here to brag. I'm here to call the man out! Damian, get your ass out here and show me what kind of a man you are!"
At that, the arena goes dark, then springs to life as red beams start to shoot from everywhere overhead. Damian's music begins to play, and you can hear what sounds like an engine being revved loudly in the back. Then, out from behind the curtain, Dan Mazin, dressed up in a long black cloak, with 666 written on his forehead, rides down to ringside in a go-cart that looks like a red corvette. Mazin gets out of the go-cart, then slides into the ring. He holds his hands up above his head, recieving the loudest set of boos yet.
Mazin looks around him, in shock, then grabs the mic from Blottie. "Who do you think you are? I am "The Devil's Advocate"! I am here to show the ICPW that I am, quite simply, the most evil man in the world! Cheer me, heathens! I am your hero!"
Mazin only riles the crowd further with his words, as Blottie grabs the mic from Mazin, a smirk cracking his lips, he looks Dan up and down. He shakes his head, and then tries getting into Mazin's face. Unfortunately, Blottie has three inches on Mazin, so he is forced to kneel down a little in order to go nose to nose with Dan.
"Seriously. I thought you'd be a little taller. A little more intimidating. A little more... stylish", Blottie says, touching Mazin's robe, which starts to shed at Blottie's touch.
"I am the 'Devil's Advocate'. This robe is made out of the fibers of Hell. These fists are made of iron. And this elbow. Well, let's just say my elbow is made out of a substance human's have never come in contact with. Prepare to meet my father!"
After this last quip, Dan starts to spin, then hits Blottie with an elbow to the chest. It's Hell's Elbow! Mazin starts to jump up and down like a giddy school girl, not realizing that Blottie is standing behind him, looking at his chest, and laughing. When Mazin spins around, he's met by an intense glare by
Blottie, which causes Mazin to fly back several feet, and cringe on the mat in fear.
"What... what are you? I hit you with Hell's Elbow, the most devastating move in pro-wrestling. I have you beat. Fall down, damn you!", screams Mazin, as Blottie slowly approaches him.
Blottie bends down in front of Mazin, grabs the mic from his hand, and looks down at him. "So this is the great and mighty "Devil's Advocate". Cringing and screeching like a little school girl after finding out that his weak ass finisher is like bitch-slapping me."
Mazin starts to scream, "It's devastating, DEVASTATING!". Blottie holds back a laugh, as the fans are no longer booing, but actually laughing along with Blottie at Mazin's hilarious impersonation of Damian. Blottie finally regains his composure, and starts to speak again.
"Go ahead and cringe on the mat, Damian. Cry like the little bitch you are. Because I'm not going to take you out today. I'm going to wait and watch you squirm some more until Saturday. That way, when you've wet yourself incessently for the past five days, when you've lost all controls of your bowels and stink like a newborn who hasn't been changed in a week, when you are ripe for the picking, as well as the smelling, I will lift you up off the mat, begging and pleading, and I will drop you down into the Grosse Pointe Driver.
And then I will climb to the top rope, look around me at all the fans, who will boo me because I have taken apart their "hero", and I will drop Simply
Legendary from the sky. And like God passed judgement onto Lucifer for his sins, so I will pass judgement on you. And there will be a fall. And it will be counted 1... 2... 3."
The fans are booing louder than ever, but there are also several that can be heard chanting "legend" over and over again. Mazin looks up at Blottie, a look of fear on his face, and starts to whine and cry.
"DADDY! THE BIG BAD MAN IS BEING MEAN TO ME! COME KICK HIS ASS!" With that, the lights go out again, and this time flames shoot out of the entrance ramp. The fans stare in anticipation, as this time, through the curtain, walks a midget dressed up like Satan, painted red from head to toe, a red tail trailing behind him, two red horns, and a mini-red plastic pitchfork in his hand. He slides into the ring, and starts poking Blottie with the pitchfork. Blottie just looks down, and then can't take it anymore. He breaks down laughing, as do most of the crowd. The little red midget runs from one side of the ring to the next, screaming something about how he was going to steal their souls. Blottie pulls himself off of his knees, trying to stop himself from laughing uncontrollably, but not doing a very good job of it. He shakes his head, leaves the ring, and walks to the back, as in the ring the little devil guy is screaming at "Damian" to get up and fight like a man.
Blottie walks through the curtain, laughing to himself, as his skit had gone better than he could have ever planned. He sees an interviewer waiting for him, but before he can get over there, he is stopped by one of the stagehands.
"Excuse me, Mr. Blottie, but you have a phone call on line 2." says the stagehand, before going off to continue prepping the wrestlers.
Blottie walks over to the phone, holding up his hand to the interviewer and cameraman to signal to wait a minute, and starts to talk. The smile quickly disappears off his face, and is replaced by a look of stunned disbelief. He hangs his head, as he puts the phone back on it's receiver, and walks right passed the interviewer, who was about to start to speak to Blottie. The interviewer looks confused, and tells the cameraman to follow him. Both men follow Blottie, but are cut off by Blottie slamming his door shut in their faces. The interviewer looks over at the cameraman, shrugs, then goes off to try catching another interview before the next match.
