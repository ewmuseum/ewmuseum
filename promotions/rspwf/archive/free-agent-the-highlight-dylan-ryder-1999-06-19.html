[FREE AGENT] "The Highlight" Dylan Ryder

19 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, I don't really want to call myself a "free agent", simply because I currently compete in the GSWP. But, I am looking for 1 more fed to compete in, so look over the flash that's included in this post, and get back to me on it.
Thanks. ----- [The scene opens up to a stage. A fairly large stage, hundreds of people crowd around to witness a unique press conference, one in which is headlined by none other than the GSWP's very own "Highlight", Dylan Ryder. The stage is a modern stage, with a banner hanging above reading "GSWP Press Conference". Chairs line the stage, along with a small podium complete with microphone in the center of the stage.]
[Ryder, sitting on a wooden chair on stage, is dressed in an appropriate manner, wearing a brown-colored Polo shirt, along with black dress slacks and black leather dress shoes. He looks over the crowd for a moment, before getting out of his seat and heading to the center of the stage on the podium, where he begins to greet to the crowd.]
DR - Hello everyone, welcome to the first press conference headlined by myself,
GSWP's Dylan Ryder. I will be taking questions from reporters from the crowd, so if you do have a question, feel free to speak. Let's start with the female reporter in the blue shirt..
[The young-looking female steps out of her chair, raising the microphone to her mouth as she begins to ask her question.]
Rep. #1 - Dylan, with the current success of yourself, you have been atop the
GSWP roster as of late, garnering an amazing 3-0 record. My question is, how did it feel to defeat one of the biggest names in professional wrestling, "Mr.
Charisma" Christian York at Impact a few weeks ago? DR - To be honest, it felt really good. I feel that I fought a long, hard battle with him, and with his willingness to not give up, it gave myself a challenge. I've been looking for that challenge ever since I first stepped into the Garden State Wrestling Promotion, and I'm suprised it came as one Christian
York. That victory will never be forgotten, as it was my biggest to date in my career.
[As he finishes his speech, another reporter, this time a male, steps out of his seat, and starts to ask Ryder his question.]
Rep. #2 - Ryder, I've been wondering, as the rest of the wrestling world has as well, do you plan on hunting after any championship titles while in your stay with the GSWP? The brand new title, named the Garden State Championship title, will be up for grabs in 2 weeks...
["the Highlight" rubs his chin for a moment, pondering the question the reporter has just presented him. After a few brief seconds, Dylan starts to speak.]
DR - Yes, I do intend on going after a major championship title in the GSWP. The Garden State Championship title is going to be awarded to the winner of the
15-man battle royal in 2 weeks, and I will be looking forward to that match. It gives me another opportunity to gain respect from my fellow wrestlers and of course, the fans. Winning the title would mean everything to me, but I don't expect to just walk into the matchup, and walk away with the grand prize. I expect a clean, hard-fought battle against 14 other men who want the prize just as much as I do. Next question.
[Again, another male reporter, fairly large, stands up from his chair, and asks a question to "the Highlight.]
Rep. #3 - You have been in the GSWP only close to a month now, and already you are signed with big matches against top names in this sport. First was
Christian York, and now you are left to face another "big name", King Snake. What are your feelings about the match? As you know, "Midas Touch" Johnny
Glamour hired him as the "higher power" to help him capture the GSWP World
Heavyweight Title at Summer Showdown '99.
[Dylan pulls back his long, jet-black hair as the man finishes his words, steps into the microphone once again. After hearing the man's question, he quickly starts to reply to him into the mic.]
DR - Another challenge. Yes, this one may be a bit more difficult, but nonetheless, a challenge. I've seen King Snake fight his way to the top in numerous wrestling federations around the country, and he's no "easy pickings".
A rough-and-tough cowboy as he likes to be called, has sealed Glamour the GSWP
World Heavyweight title. Does Glamour deserve this award? In a sense, no. His antics have disgusted me, and the rest of the wrestling world, and I'd love to see him lose the title to a fellow wrestler down the road. But, I have to worry about King Snake at this week's "Impact". Snake, I have prepared for this battle all week long. You're no stranger to me, so I know just how you fight.
If you think I'm just an average rookie to step all over, think again. "the
Highlight" is not about to give up a major opportunity to gain respect here in the GSWP. I hope you understand that Snake. Good luck this Sunday. May the best man win.
[After finishing his words, Ryder takes a breather, and soon after, walks over to a table where he grabs a bottle of water. Taking a drink from the water, he puts the bottle down, and walks back up to the podium, and continues to speak to the crowd.]
DR - I have time for one last question. I'd like the lady over there, in a yellow and red dress, to address her question.
[The older lady, presumably in her mid-30's, raises her microphone to her mouth, as he starts to ask the final question for the conference.]
Rep. #4 - As you all know, this past Sunday at the GSWP pay-per-view Summer
Showdown '99, you Dylan Ryder, was attacked by a man in a Yankees hat, later identified to be none other than former GSWP Television Champion Boltz. How do you feel about the attack?
[Dylan once again ponders the question for some few seconds, thinking of quite how to put it in words. Soon, he begins to address the crowd with the final reply.]
DR - Frankly, Boltz, the stunt you pulled at the pay-per-view angered me greatly. To attack Dylan Ryder with a chair as a way to get yourself back into the GSWP was shocking. Shocking, and stupid. Boltz, you thought you were all
"cool" in that baseball hat, hiding your face from the crowd, only to attack two other fellow wrestlers moments later? I hope not. It was a sad way to put yourself over with the fans. If you want to attack me again, try your best.
I'll be there, waiting. But this time, there will be a retaliation. One from myself, "the Highlight". Keep that in mind.
[After those strong comments from Ryder, he walks away from the podium, and proceeds to exit the stage. Fade to black.]
