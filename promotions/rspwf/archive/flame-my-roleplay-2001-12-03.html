Flame my roleplay.

3 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade in] [Camera fades in slowly... as a matter of fact, so slowly that it seems like it is having trouble doing so. The camera fails, but audio is still qued in with the 20 dollars worth of equipment used for this segment.]
"Ah, shit... Figures... Nobody wanted to see me in this business to begin with and even the hint of coming back is such an atrocious thought that even in their minds, it gives them that queezy feeling in the pits of their guts."
[Camera flashes on with a bright light, some distorted color and a wave in the picture as if you were watching an old Zenith on its last legs. Nevertheless, the picture finally comes through and shows Ian Walsh leaning over, looking into the camera, trying to fix it.]
"I think we got it. I never made it big enough to get much money and now that I have been retired for 2 years, I'm in deeper debt than Homeless Joe...
but I still get laid more..." (Holding that thought and looks to the camera's left at a blow-up doll sitting in a chair with a dirty sanchez and a ravaged bride's maid-style dress.) "Well... at least my hand hasn't pressed charges on me yet, but I almost have to rape it for some action. Anyway, the reason why I am talking to you, the people... um... well, I need money."
[Ian pulls his pockets out and nothing but dust and lint comes falling out of the pockets.]
"I need a job. Uncle Escobar is after me and even Reverend Henry E. Miller is after me for stealing from the collection plate. I can't take this poverty anymore. I need a job..."
[Cries... After a few seconds of crying, the camera starts fizzing out. Ian slams his palm against it to stop the flickering.]
"Dammit! See? So somebody hire me. I don't care if it's even Eddie Jacobs or some assface like that... Please, I need money, badly!"
[A man comes running at Ian from the field behind him... looks to be a hick from the backwoods, perhaps the true owner of this house from where Ian is speaking.]
"DERRRRRERRRRRERRRRRR!!!!!!!!!! Git offa mah property!" [Ian runs likes the wind and the camera focuses on Old Man Missing Toof.] "Sumbitch." [Fade] -Ian Walsh "Ethan de Sade"
