[FREE AGENT #2] "The Iron Bull" Jake Demola

23 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NOTE: Yes... this is the FINAL Demola character. I swear. There are no more. I'm looking for an active fed. Preferably closed roleplay, but if it is open roleplay I don't want a board. Weekly cards would rule. Doesn't even have to be an "established" fed. Any one will do. But only one for this character.
Here's the app. I'll take a look at any fed that writes me. Write me at [detail withheld] and if you need to see proof of my work... uh... write me and I'll give you some old roleplays I've done. Thanks to G/M for the app. I really like the format. I've adapted it a bit, but it's an excellent app.
APPLICATION ----------- SECTION ONE - HANDLER INFORMATION Your Name: [name withheld] Your eMail Address: [detail withheld] SECTION TWO - WRESTLER INFORMATION Wrestler Name: "The Iron Bull" Jake Demola Gender: Male Hometown: North Conway, New Hampshire Height: 6'2 Weight: 255 Birthdate (MM-DD-YYYY): 07-10-1979 Alignment (Face/Neutral/Heel): Face Gimmick (detailed): A tough face. No nonsense brawler. Consummate underdog. Always takes abuse in long, brutal fights. Oldest of Nick
Demola's two sons. Inherited his toughness and in-ring charisma, but none of his athleticism and arrogance. Kind of the black sheep of the family.
Theme Music (Song and Artist): "You Know You're Right" by Nirvana
Detailed Ring Entrance: [A thumping bass beat, haunting in nature, sounds out over the loudspeakers as the crowd gets to its feet. "You Know You're Right" by
Nirvana begins...]
# I will never bother you # # I will never promise to # # I will never follow you # # I will never bother you # [From behind the curtain steps a man in long black tights with a white towel around his neck and short hair. He is built athletically, and stares forward, walking to the ring methodically as Kurt Cobain screams, and the crowd joins...]
# PAAAAAAAAAAAIN # # PAAAAAAAAAAAIN # # PAAAAAAAAAAAIN # # PAAAAAAAAAAAIN # [Jake Demola steps up the ringsteps and into the ring. He stretches in the corner and tosses his towel over the near ringpost. The sight of the word "PAIN" tattooed in large red letters on his back, and shaved into the back of his head with his short haircut is apparent as the song fades.]
Physical Appearance: FACE - Ruggedly handsome, with a flattened nose and cauliflowered ears from fights and grappling of all sorts. Short hair with the word "PAIN"
shaved into the back of his hair. BODY - Athletic and thick. A large tattoo on his back reading, in red letters, the word "PAIN"
Ring Attire: Black long tights and a white towel. That's it. Three Strengths: 1. Fighting ability - Can flat out brawl and fight better than any man in the ring 2. Stamina - Will not quit and can go in a fight forever 3. Takes punishment - Will feel pain, but won't let it get to him. Will NEVER quit Three Weaknesses: 1. Not much technical skill - Doesn't have a lot of experience in wrestling 2. Lack of aerial assault - Self-explanatory 3. Balls - Thinks with his balls, not his brain. Will get into a lot of impossible situations SECTION THREE - MOVE INFORMATION Primary Wrestling Style: Brawler Secondary Wrestling Style: Boxer Move List (10-25 Moves): 1. Assorted punches 2. Assorted kicks 3. Sleeperhold 4. Leaping headbutt 5. Armbreaker 6. Neck wrench 7. Backbreaker 8. Choke Out 9. Weapon use 10. One-two punch combo Signature Moves (3-5 Moves): 1. Left hook (a Demola signature. As good a punch as there is in the game) 2. Western Lariat 3. Demola Driver (DVD, one of the few moves he's picked up along the way from watching his dad) Setup Move and Description: None needed, comes from nowhere Finisher Name: The Iron Bull Finisher Description: leaping, spinning DDT SECTION FOUR - MANAGER INFORMATION (Optional) Manager Name: Manager Description: SECTION FIVE - BACKGROUND AND SAMPLE ROLEPLAY Background/History: The forgotten Demola. Nick's first son, a product of a failed relationship with an as-yet-unnamed woman. Jake was provided with money by Nick, but not much else.
Jake grew up fighting because of the similar looks he shared with his dad. He boxed for years, wrestled for a couple in high school. He was a great boxer, a so-so wrestler.
He always hated pro wrestling, but joined up when he found he had a bit of talent for it. And when he found that his last name opened doors and provided him with much-needed money.
