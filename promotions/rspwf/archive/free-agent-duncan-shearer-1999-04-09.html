[FREE AGENT] Duncan Shearer

9 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera opens up inside "Cheers", the bar where everybody knows your name. It is a normal day at the bar, Norm is seated on his faviourite stool, Woody is serving drinks, and there is a fairly peaceful atmosphere in the bar. It is
Wednesday afternoon, around about three o'clock in the afternoon. From the pool room, comes Sam Malone, arm in arm with a beautiful blonde.]
NORM: "Oh, Sammy, not bad, man." [Malone winks at Norm, who then turns to Cliff Clavin.] NORM: "So, Cliffy, what shall it be today then? Anything decent on the tube? CLIFF: "Well, there's Boston wrestling." NORM: "Sounds good, stick it on." [On the Television, there is now taped highlights of the Boston Wrestling
Council's latest card. We see Duncan Shearer, the BWC World Heavyweight champion executing his finishing manouver, a superkick on his opponent, and winning the match.]
NORM: "Boy, that guy's awesome." CLIFF: "As a matter of fact, I'm a bit of an athlete myself. You don't do all these postal rounds without building up a rather impressive physique."
NORM: "Yeah, whatever you say, Cliffy." [Suddenly, the door to the bar opens. A fairly tall and well built man walks into the bar, and down towards the counter. The man has got long, black hair and piercing blue eyes. He takes a seat at the bar.]
WOODY: "What will it be, sir." MAN: "Scotch on the rocks, barkeep." NORM: [from other end of the bar] "Hey, Cliffy, isn't that..." CLIFF: "I'd say it is ... hey, Shearer." [The man, now identified as Duncan Shearer looks up and waves to Cliff in acknowledgement.]
CLIFF: "Are you ... Duncan Shearer, or am I mistaken." DS: "No, your not mistaken." CLIFF: "Stick around, kid, you'll get used to saying that." NORM: "Ignore my 'friend'. This is a great honour Mr. Shearer. But what are you doing here?"
DS: "Please ... call me Duncan. And well, this is the neighbourhood bar, right, where everybody comes to get away from everything. I just thought that I would give it a shot."
NORM: "How are things going in the BWC. Your still dominating, right." DS: "Actually, yesterday I wrestled my last match. I then forfeited the title and quit the federation. I figured that I'd spent far too much time there and it was time for me to move on to a bigger name federation."
WOODY: "Strange. I thought the BWC was a pretty big name." DS: "Compared to the WWO?" WOODY: "Well, Boston Wrestling Council sure is a lot of letters." NORM: "Ignore him. So where are you thinking of heading to?" DS: "That's undecided. All the top presidents have been informed of Duncan
Shearer's availability. I'll listen to offers, then make my choice."
CLIFF: "I say you should join the U.S. Postal service!" DS: "You what ... " [Fade.]
