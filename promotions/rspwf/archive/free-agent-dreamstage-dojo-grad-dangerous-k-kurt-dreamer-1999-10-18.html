One of the prolific mat technicians in the history of modern wrestling as touted by his brothers Shane and Stevie, as well as a master of almost all variations of the suplexs.Perhaps the most athletically gifted wrestler in the business today, Kurt Dreamer wrestles like a mat veteran, performs aerial high-spots like a Luchadore and is well versed in
Martial Arts to boot.With his steady ringwork and unique style, he's sure to make a splash wherever he lands.
A cousin of IFO/IFC (shootfight) Heavyweight #1 Contender Wayne Grayson, older brother of Stevie Dreamer/Starr and younger brother of Shane Dreamer/Starr, Kurt has taken the first steps in making a name for himself in the sport. Combining aerial tactics with a solid technical background, the up-and-comer has yet to make serious impact in the wrestling World, but is adapting to the American style, and has improved markedly since leaving the DOJO 1 year ago.
Recently, Kurt has been teaming with Jinsei ASAKA of Neo Japan Pro and a fellow Dreamstage DOJO Graduate with some success. Although he is still a relative "rookie", Dreamer is quickly becoming one of the more promising stars to come out of the Dreamstage DODJO, and could be a serious player in the title scenes around the world in time.

Broom sweep into a knee bar He breaks your guard and stands up. Keep a grip on his arm or head and place your foot in his hip.(my right foot is in his left hip in this case.)
Slide your hips out to the same side where you just placed your foot on his hip and place your other leg flat on the ground in between his legs as shown.
With your free hand, grab his heel from the inside of his leg. Bring him down by pushing into his hip with your foot, blocking his heel with your hand and chopping his other foot out with your leg.
As he gets to the ground, keep ahold of his heel with your hand. Be sure to keep the momentum going to aid this move.
Get to your knees and bring your leg up and over his leg, while still holding on to his heel.
Now the tricky part..... as you lift his leg up in the air as shown, you want to spin your pelvis around his knee cap so that you end up on your right side.
Then lock in a kneebar..... the grip used to hold his leg in place is a "tombstone grip". To make him submit, squeeze your knees together and while keeping his shin tight to your chest, thrust your hips forward
