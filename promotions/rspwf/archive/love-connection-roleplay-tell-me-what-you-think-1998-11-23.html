Love Connection roleplay: tell me what you think

23 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, Just wanted to share a roleplay with all of you and maybe get some comments and criticisms. Feel free to tell me anything you like. This is
Brad Morris and Lance Love of the Love Connection of the UUW!! Thanks,
Larry
(Camera shows a black limo pulling up to what appears to be a hotel. The camera zooms in on the sign that reads HEARTBREAK HOTEL in a big red heart.
The driver of the limo opens up the back door and out steps Brad Morris followed by "All Natural" Lance Love. Brad is wearing a black armony suit and matching tie. He blonde hair is pulled back in a ponytail and a bandage is clearly seen above his right eye. Lance is wearing blue jeans and a light blue tee shirt with red lettering that reads LOVE CONNECTION.
The camera quickly follows them into the hotel. A fan comes over and begins to talk to Love Connection.)
Man: Hey, that was a nasty smack you took the other night. It looked like it hu......AHHHHHH!!
(Brad all of a sudden picks the man up by his collar and holds him up in the air.) Brad Morris: Listen to me pipsqueek, I'll give you three seconds to get the hell out of my face before I stick your head all the way up your ass.
You understand me? (The man quickly shakes his head yes. In the back Lance is struggling not to burst out laughing. Brad then shoves/throws the man away and the man quickly runs out of the building.)
Lance Love: That was cool. huh huh. Brad Morris: Well, welcome to my own private hotel in which I have owned for two years now. I turned this place from a rat infested dump to the nicest place to stay in all the world. Now, follow us up to our own private sweet and we can do a little chit chatting.
(The camera follows Brad and Lance up an elevator to room number 69. Brad opens the door and the cameras pan the room. In the middle is a gigantic whirlpool with three georgeous ladies already in it. One of which is the beautiful Love Doll. There are two beds, both with full length mirrors above them. Brad goes over and kisses Love Doll as Lance goes and whispers a few things to the other two girls.)
Lance Love: Right On DUDE!!!! Two for the price of one, that's what I'm talkin bout. Yea!!
Brad Morris: Will you shut up you lame brain. Before we get to our own business, maybe we should fulfill the needs of our cameraman. You obviously have a mission, to get a few words from the greatest tag team in not only the UUW, but of all the world. The two greatest singles wrestlers combined forms one hell of a team, which we proved the other night at the pay per view. I have a few words for one, Nick Demola. Nicky, pal, you saw exactly what happened. Smack, or crack, which ever one it was took an illegal chair, and smacked me upside the head. There was no way around it, they cheated. They weren't even in the frieking match anymore. And what do you do? We deserve a title shot next week, not a chance to kick the shit out of but crack and smoke. Demola, watch it, because you may not realize what you're getting yourself into.
Lance Love: That's right dude. Now, but Brad, we've got to quit looking at the past. It's the future baby!! That's what it's all about. And those two monsters Crack and Smack, well you know the old saying, the bigger they are the harder they fall!! Crack, smack, the only think I sense missing is the pop. But believe you me, you'll get a first class introduction to the pop everytime you hear one of your bones breaking!!
Your big, your ugly, your fat, but it doesn't intimidate us. Because no matter what way you look at it, beauty comes before everything. And the only place I can ever see those belts at home is around the two sexiest waists in the UUW, myself's and Brad's.
Brad Morris: Do you boys think a little chair shot is gonna hurt me? You think that's gonna keep me out? Sorry boys, I've been hit with much worse.
I've been run over by cars, thrown down escalators, stabgbbed, beaten,
I've faced it all. But one aspect that goes along with being the Love
Machine, i always, ALWAYS, come back better than ever. Crack, smack, you both must be on drugs. That's seriously the only thing I can see that could possibly convince you to risk your own well beings against the next tag team champions of the world, the Love Connection.
Lance Love: Do you think we need the crowd cheering and screaming our names to become champions? Hell no!! We've gotten this far being the most ruthless SOBs in the business, and there's nowhere to go but up my friends.
Brothers from Hell, we're smarter than you, more clever than you, stronger than you, better wrestlers than you, and oh yea, a hell of a lot sexier than you two overgrown dumb animals. And that's what matters, whether you like it or you don't like it. It's what counts, and it's what we got and
YOU DONT!!!! WE'RE SEXY, AND YOU AREN'T!!!! HA HA HA!!! Hurry up Brad,
I'm ready for my rub down from the beautiful vixons over there.
Brad Morris: Put an ice cube down your shorts Lance. Just one more thing,
Desolation Row, don't think we've over looked you. You've got those straps, so in my book that makes you marked men. And trust me, if you don't have eyes in the back of your heads, your in for a whole lot of trouble. Cause you never know when the Love Connection is gonna break their next heart!!!! Now get out of our faces!! We've got bidness!!!
(Camera fades out as the men walk over to the whirl pool.)
