[Critique] RP From "Sir Psycho Sexy" Brayden Leeper

3 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is the RP I just recently used to win the CWF Intercontinental Title. Tell me what you think
-=The scene cuts to Kevin Richardson in the back=- [Richardson] "What an awesome match that was. Coming up next will be one of the most anticipated matches in CWF history. This feud has been brewing for a long time. The two competitors are willing to put it all on the line for the coveted
CWF Intercontinental Title. A Cell, and on top of that is a ladder. The only way to win is to climb the cell, then the ladder. And on top of that ladder is a belt. The CWF Intercontinental Title belt. The first man to grab it will be the winner. And the two competitors are not exactly the best of friends as
White Knight defends his title against "Sir Psycho Sexy" Brayden Leeper..."
-=Voice from Off Camera=- [Voice] "Did I hear you say my name?" [Richardson] "White Knight?" [Voice] "Of course not you idiot" -=Through an open door to the left comes Brayden Leeper. He has on his black hair tied back into a ponytail. He approaches Richardson, with White Chair in hand=- [Richardson] "Mr. Leeper, I beg you not to do what I think you're going to." [Leeper] "Don't worry you moron. I'm not gonna bash your pathetic little skull in with my White Chair."
[Richardson] "Well then, this CERTAINLY is a shock. Brayden Leeper, you're finally talking!"
[Leeper] "Well, what do you know? I am talking! I haven't cut an interview in front of the camera in a long time. Make sure you get my good side! Oh wait, all views of me are my good side, so it doesn't really matter. I could stand in front of a camera all day, and not say anything, and your ratings would skyrocket. Isn't that right Kevin?"
[Richardson] "Uh, yeah sure. Since you've decided to unexpectantly break the silence, do you plan to purposely lose the match tonight again, to further prove a point that you can do exactly what you want here in CWF?"
[Leeper] "Have you lost your mind? Maybe I DO need to beat you with my chair to knock some since into your ass. OF COURSE NOT. The past few months, I have wreaked havoc on every superstar here in CWF. No one could touch me, and no one can now. EVERY wrestler I've come across in CWF has fallen to me. Yet, to show that I Am the MAN, I purposefully lost. That period is over. And it should have ended when I got into the ring with Rage on that fateful episode of Loaded. But no. White Knight wants to stick his nose into my business and cost me a match I had every intention on winning, costing me the match."
[Richardson] "With all due respect, you kinda stuck your nose into Knight's business first"
[Leeper] "With all due respect, you can kinda kiss my ass. You're lucky you're getting this interview. Everyone in their right mind would DIE to be the first one to get words from Sir Psycho Sexy. And you become the lucky one that gets the interview that will change the face of the CWF forever. My first promo."
[Richardson] "Actually, Mr. Leeper. I had a promo with you after your first match in CWF. Right after your match with Nick Cage at Psycho Circus."
[Leeper] "That's right. Back when I was in my Psycho mode. That was one hell of a match. Another man I could have beaten whenever I wanted. Nick Cage, now CWF
Champion. And I had him beat. Just goes to show you my talent level is THE best in CWF. I could beat the champion any time I wanted. White Knight should be happy that I decided to start with him. He was in a failing program. But now that he's feuding with me, his popularity has skyrocketed. Everyone wants to see the biggest asshole on the face of the planet get his ass handed to him.
Everyone wants White Knight to kick my ass. Everyone wants to see me bleed. Everyone wants to see me take the huge fall off the cage, or through the cage tonight. Everyone wants to see my career ended right here tonight. But let me tell you this. No one will see any of that. Because I am a force to be reckoned with here in CWF. And I will prove that tonight. There is NO one that can beat me cleanly. No one that can beat me fair and square. And I proved that through the course of my stay here in CWF. I am undefeated."
[Richardson] "Mr. Leeper, that's a little bold isn't it? A man who has NEVER won a match here in CWF calling himself undefeated."
[Leeper] "What did I tell you before. Don't cross me Kevin, or I'll cut this interview short. I could have beaten ANY of those wrestlers whenever I wanted.
Right after the Krush Groove, the match could have been over. Yet I decided to continue. Inflict more punishment. Get myself disqualified. And why you ask"
-=Kevin gets a puzzled look on his face=- [Leeper] "Uh-hem. And why you ask!" -=Leeper motions with his hands to Kevin, who finally gets the idea=- [Richardson] "Why, Mr. Leeper" [Leeper] "To be the biggest asshole in CWF. You see, wins and loses aren't what counts the most. It's how the fans react to you. It's who you piss off. If I pissed off the right people, I knew I could get a shot at the CWF
Intercontinental Title, and that's exactly what I did. I Pissed EVERYONE off. From Nick Cage to White Knight, to every single last ass in the seats. However, all the women still find me Highly attractive, and the weekends aren't exactly boring for me if you get my drift. This whole thing with the White Chair, beating everyone in sight over the head with it? I didn't want to do all that.
I'd rather go in and wrestle a good, clean technical match. But I did what I had to do to get what I wanted. And I'll do WHATEVER it takes to get what I want."
[Richardson] "And what is it exactly you want here in the CWF?" [Leeper] "When I was growing up, I wanted to be loved by the fans. I wanted their admiration. And to an extent, I still do. It kind of hurts to be booed out of arenas. Right now, I want the CWF Intercontinental Title. But I'd also love to be loved by the fans. Tonight, I'd like to hear every ass in those seats chanting my name, cheering for me. Wanting to see my kick the crap out of
White Knight. Wanting to see me make him bleed. Wanting to see him take the fall. Wanting to see me become the new CWF Intercontinental Champion. If they choose to boo me, so be it. That's OK, and that's their prerogative. It won't affect my performance tonight. Nothing will affect my strive for the title. And
I will hurt the White Knight tonight."
[Richardson] "Any last comments about tonight's match?" [Leeper] "There sure are." -=Silence=- [Richardson] "Well?" [Leeper] "Thank you for realizing you have a job to do Kevin Richardson. Tonight, marks the new beginning of my career. Like you all saw during the Free
For All. I am ready to take out the White Knight. I will not be thrown off the cell. Knight, I don't know if you will. But I won't. I refuse to lie down anymore. I refuse to lose. I'm going to climb to the top of the cell, then the ladder. And when the bell is rung, and one man is down and out, bleeding and lifeless. You will hear "AND NEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEWWWWWWWWWWWWW CWF INTERCONTINENTAL
CHAMPION........" And my name will follow. Then be screamed by thousands of fans who just went through a Leeper orgasm. They'll have realized you were a passing fad, and I am Too Sweet To be Sour, the Tower of Power, and tonight,
I'll be the man of just not the hour. But also the man of the fans hearts. They won't know why, but they can't keep my name out of their mouths. I have the intangible White Knight, not you. As much as they hate me right now, while I'm beating you down, they'll want to love me that much. And all the anger will find a new direction. Tonight, White Knight, not only will I take your belt out from under you. Or in the case of this match, out from Over you. But I'll also take all of your fans. The belt won't hurt you much, or maybe it will. But what will hurt the most, is when my hand is raised, and all of your former fans are screaming for me. And you can believe that!"
-=Leeper walks off=- [Richardson] "Thank you for your comments Mr. Leeper. Well, that big cell is being lowered as we speak, let's get back to the ring for this historic match."
-=The camera cuts back to the ring=-
