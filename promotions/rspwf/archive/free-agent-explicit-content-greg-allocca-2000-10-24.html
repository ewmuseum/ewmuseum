[Free Agent] "Explicit Content" Greg Allocca

24 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I lost my internet a while ago, just recently got it back and am finally looking for a federation to join. Booked preferably one that's been around for at least a month or two, but I'm willing to join a newer fed if it's promising.
If your in need of wrestlers I have friends who will probably venture into it with me. NE wayz here's two of my rps from a fed called AWA earlier this year.
Promo Spot - 8/22/00 [The sound of a cymbal being hit begins to come over the TV screen. Strands of blue hair fly on and off the screen in synch with those cymbal hits. Finally, some high speed guitar chords begin to follow in as the blue hair starts to wail around faster.]
# OH YEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAH! # [Finally, the scene switches to a picture of a skinny hispanic looking wrestler with shoulder-length blue braids standing on the top rope waving the fist of metal in the air.]
# My brain distorts the pleasure No facts behind my eyes This pain I've come to treasure Being down has become life # [Now, clips of this strange blue haired man in wrestling competition begin to be shown. First he is about to
be powerbombed by a HUMONGOUS man with long black hair, but he somehow regains leverage and manages to hurl over the big man in a hurricanrana. The scene changes to this blue haired man bouncing off the ropes and coming crashing down onto a man outside the ring with a running somersault plancha.]
# Don't know which one is real Been kicked in the groin I drown the way I feel Though I will to go Losing All # [This time we see our blue haired assailant as he tosses a chair at his opponent. His opponent catches it, and a second later he hits a superkick to slam the chair right into the poor man's face. Another switch as this man is shown standing next to Markus King the refereee raises their hands in victory as we look around the ring to see broken tables, bloody chairs, and other weapons of destruction. Now, we see the same man, but this time without his blue hair or any hair for that matter. He exchanges a few right hands to knock his opponent to the ground, and immediately hits a standing moonsault...the referee counts the 3, and then brings him a large title belt.]</B>
# I'm lord of misery I'm king of the hill I'm a broken man of the world In a state of....# [This time another familiar face appears as this blue haired man is struggling on top of a ladder with none other than AWA World Champion Post. We switch ahead to see the referee count 1, 2, and 3 to declare the blue headed man the winner of the brutal contest with Post. Now we switch again to see him as he springs up to the top rope, and comes crashing back down with an unbelievably executed corkscrew moonsault. Another 3 count, and a victory. A few more quick flashes as we watch this same man execute a Rocker Dropper, a swinging neckbreaker, a somersault into a lariat, and even a death valley driver.
Finally, just as the music begins to change we see him as he is chokeslammed through a table. But we stick with it to see him as he pulls himself to his feet.]</B>
# My wrists are slit....I'm losing all Gun at my head...I'm losing all My bones are broken....I'm losing all Last time I had...I'm losing all Losing All # [Now, scenes of this man being whacked upside the head with chairs, baseball bats, and even toilet bowls are shown. Now on top of a hell in the cell he charges at someone, and knocks him off. However, the man was able to hang on and pulls the blue haired assailant off the top off the cage. Just as impact with the floor is made we skip to another scene. Some large man is holding this blue haired man, and someone else almost 25 feet in the air! From the stands of some arena, and he just slams them both down! They go crashing through 2 tables before they finally hit the floor. And some how this blue haired mystery man is able to roll over and cover his opponent! The referee than throws a title belt on top of the man.]</B>
# Losing All Losing All Losing All Losing All # [Finally, as the last switch we get to watch as the blue haired man is battling with another MUCH larger man inside of...A CHURCH! Somehow it must've caught fire because there are flams all around them. The larger man slams him into a wall, and he ends up going right through. But the blue haired guy's arm catches fire in the process. He tries desperately to pull it out, and after he does so he nails the bigger guy with a jumping ddt. AND THE FLOOR BENEATH THEM crashes through. But before the screen fades out we see the name "Greg
Allocca" come across the screen.] Back In Blue - [Just as the opening festivities for Slaughter finish up...the sound of a cymbal being hit repeatedly echoes through the arena, followed by some fast paced southern style heavy metal guitar riffs. And then echoing through the speakers...]
SINGER'S VOICE - "OH YEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAAAH!" [As "Losing All" by Down continues to open up the lights in the arena change to an annoyingly bright shade of blue. Out from behind the curtains steps the new
AWA recruit "Sexual Blueberries" Greg Allocca. His long blue hair isn't braided today, but it's tied back on top while the sides are still left hanging down. Some members of the crowd seem to recognize the man from his independent circuit days, and greet him with cheers while the rest of the fans aren't quite sure what to make of him.]
Dock - "Who the hell is this dork?!" [Greg slowly walks towards the ring taking a few moments to admire a well-endowed young lady on his way. He hits the ring, and motions for a microphone. The officials at ringside seem reluctant, but they go along with it.]
Greg - "AWA, I HAVE ARRIVED!!!!! And I hadn't been signed up with the organization for more than a few days before somebody decided they were gonna start breakin' my balls. And who should it be other than my very own best friend, or at least I thought, Markus King!"
[Crowd gives a respectable pop at the mention of King's name.] Greg - "Marky, Marky, Marky, how could you ever even think...for even just a nanosecond that I would EVER in my life consider teaming up with...Post. [Acts as though a chill went up his spine.] I mean that's just...blahg! I bought some...well, nevermind. Let's just say I found my face hanging over a toilet bowl thanks to some of Post's plants.
So, Mark, I know your out "finding your smile" as someone once put it, but when you get back you need to
realize that you CAN and ALWAYS WILL be able to trust me. Which is a lot more than I can say for the country music bimbo you seem to be hanging around with during your off time...watch your back with her dude...just remember what she did to you last time.
BUT...I've been looking at what's going on around here...it seems as though
Post has got himself a nice little
crew here...and I don't like it. Not one bit. And apparently Post is a little irked because I showed a couple pictures of him from back in the day when I
whipped his sorry alcoholic ass all over the arena. So, I assume that I've got to be somewhere on their hit list. But remember Post ol' buddy....I ALWAYS...deal the first blows in ANY fight. So, you watch your back tonight...and you watch it good. GOOD DAY!" ["Losing All" by Down starts up again as Greg exits the ring.]
