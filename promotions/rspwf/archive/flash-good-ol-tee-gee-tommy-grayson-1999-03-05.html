[Flash] "Good Ol' Tee Gee" Tommy Grayson

5 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here's a roleplay that I did for the IWA (yes, I do mean _IWA_, not ICW)...tell me what you guys think. Here are the ground rules, in case there are first time Roger Eberts out there:
-Constructive Criticism only -If you don't like it, don't respond to it by saying "this shit sucks!" -If you like it, good for you =) Thank you. -------------------------- [SCENE: A fancy Italian restaurant in Manhattan, New York. The restaurant is empty and the lights are moderately dim, as part of it's theme. Fancy, soft instrumental music can be heard playing in the background. We see several paintings on the walls, hanging above some of the tables. The cameras move around, and then we see one waiter cleaning the table and resetting everything, from the plates to the forks to the knives, and that little glass vase with the rose in it. Then, we see a familiar looking man, having a plate of spaghetti and meatballs and a glass of expensive looking red wine. The cameras close in on him, and we see that it is none other than "Good Ol' Tee Gee" Tommy Grayson. The self-proclaimed
"best damn athlete in sports entertainment."]
[APPEARANCE: Tommy is wearing a black Armani blazer over a dress shirt with a design of a jaguar's skin on it. He is also wearing a pair of black dress pants, leather Gucci shoes, and a gold Rolex watch. Tommy is also sporting a pair of shaded Oakley sunglasses. His crewcut black hair is spiked with gel and his face is clean-cut and clean shaven.]
[Tommy takes another sip of red wine. He cleans his mouth with a napkin and appears to be acting like the perfect "gentleman." The cameras focus in on him...he takes another mouthful of spaghetti and sucks in the last noodle, making a kind of bland "slurp" sound. Tommy wipes his mouth with the napkin again and looks up at the camera. He begins to speak.]
TOMMY GRAYSON - I see you gentlemen have finally tracked me down, eh? [chuckles] You camera men are damn resourceful for a bunch of pathetic morons whose IQ matches that of a big dog who spends its days chasing it's tail and ice cream trucks. [brief pause] Whoa, whoa, whoa! Don't you _DARE_ give Good
Ol' Tee Gee that look! EVER! [lowers his voice] Anyway, I'm sure you're not here to watch me eat, so, I might as well begin giving one of the best damn promos in sports entertainment...like I usually do.
[Tommy smiles and takes a sip of wine. He speaks again...] TOMMY GRAYSON - The International Wrestling Association. Yet another federation that has been made for Tee Gee to dominate...and dominate I will.
IWA Television title, IWA United States title, IWA World title...[chuckles] piece of cake for Good Ol' Tee Gee. [Tommy pretends to get a shocked expression on his face...] TOMMY GRAYSON - [in an exagerrated voice] GASP!! Wait! What about Tee Gee's competition? What about all those pieces of _TRASH_ who want a shot at all these belts as well?! [normal tone] Humor me...none of these jabronies are any match or up to the caliber of Good Ol' Tee Gee! Westside Walavan?!
Please! This piece of crap is so damn dumb, he'd make a stegosaurus look like Einstein! This guy's got brawn, but he's got no noodles...[brief pause] excuse the pun. James "Simply The Best" Pillow. One of the most elite wrestlers of our time...but like a wise man said long ago -- "You're nothing but an old piece of _JABRONI TRASH_!!" Well, maybe it wasn't long ago...I, being the wise man, said it a few seconds ago! Now, don't get your panties in a knot...[mocking tone] STB...[normal tone] I'll apologize for the comments that I've made about you. Maybe after you're six-feet under. [smirks]
[Tommy signals for the check...] TOMMY GRAYSON - How about Jack Vars? This piece of travelling trash is everywhere, isn't he? I can't go a damn place without seeing his ugly, little
Truth Or Consequences, New Mexican face everywhere! [laughs] I'll tell you what, Jack, I'll let you let me beat your ass and then I'll prove to the whole world why I am...
..._THE BEST_... ..._DAMN_... ..._ATHLETE_... ..._IN SPORTS ENTERTAINMENT_!! After all, there's no better person you can, uhm, _LIE DOWN_ to. Next, we have "The God Damn Show" Tim Beck. Love the nickname, Timbo...it's a damn shame to hear you've been damned by God, but trust me, bucko...
[Tommy pulls off his sunglasses, leans foward, and stares into the camera with his brown eyes.]
TOMMY GRAYSON - Tee Gee feels your pain. Being "thunderstruck," so to speak isn't the best damn thing in the world! [puts his sunglasses back on] Anyway, folks, you're looking at the _NEXT_ IWA World Heavyweight champion...and there's not a _DAMN_ thing anybody can do 'bout it. Let's just face it...I'm not just the most awesome athlete, I'm not just a good athlete...I'm the _BEST
DAMN_ athlete. [brief pause] In sports entertainment, of course! [smiles] Tee Gee is the man to beat...but unfortunately, "L"s aren't gonna be in my record here in the IWA. How do you pronounce hundreds of "W"s?
[The waiter puts the check on the table...] TOMMY GRAYSON - Now, IWA, I leave you with this message... ..._I'M THE MAN_... ..._AND_... ..._YOU AIN -- [Tommy is cut off by his 19-year old lackey, Scottie, running into the scene...] SCOTTIE - TEE GEE, YOU DA MAN!! LET ME PAY THIS BILL!! TOMMY GRAYSON - Damn it, Scottie! Don't you _EVER_ interrupt Good Ol' Tee Gee when he's saying his damn slogan!
SCOTTIE - Sorry, Tee Gee...BUT MONEY'S A BIG MATTER!! [Scottie pulls out a twenty dollar bill and places it on the check. Tommy stares at the bill. He pulls off his sunglasses and he appears to have a look of annoyance in his eyes.]
TOMMY GRAYSON - Wh -- what the _HELL_ is that?! Do you know how much this damn restaurant costs? It's as sure as hell more than that!
[Tommy pulls out several more twenties out of his pocket and places them on the table. He puts his sunglasses back on and prepares to leave...]
TOMMY GRAYSON - Oh, and as I was saying to those other IWA jabronies... ..._I'M THE MAN_... ..._AND_... ..._YOU AIN'T JACK_!! SCOTTIE - YEAH!! TEE GEE, YOU DA MAN!! [Scottie and Tommy turn their backs on the camera and exit the restaurant. The waiter comes to pick up the cash. . .FADE.]
