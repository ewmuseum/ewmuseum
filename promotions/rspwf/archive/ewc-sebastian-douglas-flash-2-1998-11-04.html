[EWC] Sebastian... Douglas... Flash # 2...

4 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello again, The second of four flashes by "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian is listed below, for his upcoming matchup with Derek Douglas in an EWC Grudge Match on November the 11th. Once again, feedback, flaming and such, is always welcome...
Enjoy... ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- [The current N.E.W. Cruiserweight Champion, "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian, is seen walking out of the lobby of a hotel as the scene fades in. A black stretch limousine pulls up in front, as Sebastian, wearing a pair of Oakley sunglasses and an expensive looking three-piece suit, accompanied by a hotel employee who is carrying his luggage, approach the car. The man places Mike's luggage inside the limo, and turns back to Sebastian, as he is pulling out his wallet, preparing the man's tip.]
MS: Here you are... [Sebastian hands the man his money.] MAN: Thank you Mr. Sebastian, and we hope you have enjoyed your... MS: What the fu[bleep] ever! A man could get better service at a Motel 6, for God's sake. MAN: But, Mr. Sebastian, sir, this is one of the finest faciities in the state, if not the entire country.
MS: Oh yeah? Well, why don't you just shut the hell up and get back to your job, you pathetic cretin? I've already tipped you more than what this miserable place pays you in a month, so just move on.
[As the man walks away, he can be heard muttering something under his breath. Sebastian simply chuckles a bit, before jumping on back of the limo to address the camera.]
MS: Y'know, I feel sorry for people like that. That miserable piece of trash goes through the day, scrapping to get by, realizing he will never amount to a damn thing, yet he still goes on...
[Sebastian removes his glasses, as an arrogant grin overcomes his face.] ...kinda reminds me of you, Derek Douglas. [Pauses.] Glad to see you're enjoyin' yerself 'fore match time, Derek. Kickin' it with Ricki Lake and company, I see... Whatever gets ya through the day, kid. As for me, I been lookin' at the tapes o' you in the ring. And, after doing so, I came to one conclusion. You're average. All Derek Douglas is in this sport is "just another name." Nothing special or significant about you. Face it, kid. You're a nobody! You may be extreme, you may be crazy, but there's one thing in this world that you will _never_ be. And, that's...
Mike Sebastian. [Sebastian actually seems serious now.] And what was it you said? I've never faced someone like you... I don't know what I'm up against, you say? For once, I don't disagree with you. You're actually right on this one. Because, let's face the facts -- inbreds don't find themselves on this side o' the tracks too often...
[Grins.] And, what't this new attitude you've got going for yourself now? What brought on this surge of impowerment?
What... you sleeping without the night light now? As fer you being too dangerous, crazy, and extreme for Mike Sebastian's blood... what do you plan on doing... Runnin' with scissors? [Yet another smirk crosses the man's face.] But, I got people askin' me everyday -- "What makes you think you can defeat Derek Douglas?" Well, I'll admit it...
[Mike looks somewhat saddened after saying this.] I don't think I can defeat Derek Douglas. [Sebastian hangs his head low, seeming beaten already.] ...I _know_ I can. [Mike returns his head to its upright position, showing that he was just portraying a distraught expression. Sebastian chuckles and continues.]
MS: There ain't one thing that's gonna stop the Stock Market from _crashing_ down a week from tomorrow. So, call Wall Street, and check the Dow Jones, because the way I see things, from here on out, Douglas and I are goin' in opposite directions.
As always, I'm headin' tuh the top... [Sebastian points upward.] While Douglas is fallin' tuh the bottom of the barrel, not that the punk wasn't already there tuh begin with. But Douglas, all
I can promise ya is that you'll get the ride you were waitin' for. The problem for you is -- it's a one way ticket. Only two stops along the way, ace...
One -- Piedmont Medical... And, if they can get ya off life support, I thought ya might like to come to my my victory party... at...
The bank. Because, on November the 11th, kid-o, I'm cashin' in. You just be good n' ready to pay up. But, rest assured, I'll see ya on the flip side, Douglas.
_BANK_ on it. [Sebastian climbs into his limousine, and the motorcade speeds away, as the camera watches it depart. Fade.]
