[FLASH] Daniel Stryfe [Net!]

1 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here, this is my first Net RP in over 6 months. So it is not the best, but I am sure it is better than my AOL rp's. Now please give some constructive criticism, don't be to F'N flame with everything you have! I had enough of
that. :) [xXx] ... Who's Guilty? The day has come. The time of reckoning. Who will perish in dreaded hell and who's soul will be content within the pleasures of heaven? Looking past the words spoken with a wicked tongue and the evil deeds done within one's life, looking into the consciousness of man. What is the real evil that seems to plague mankind? Who are the real demons that walk this earth? Is it those whose minds have become devious because of a lifetime spent trapped in a caged hell, or is it those who invented this caged hell years ago and have done nothing to help destroy it?
Who's guilty? Frankenstein, or the doctor who created it? The sword, or the man who has slain with it? Which is the real evil, the man who kills another for food, or the man who does not share his food to prevent the killing? While you sit in judgment of a criminal, you may very well be the one who's guilty of greed, deception, and hatred. Those who are rejected at the gates of heaven shall be dragged off into the pits of hell, viciously torn from this life by the nonliving, the phantoms of the dead. These beasts take the form of a demented carnival, that of a wicked, dark circus, led by one. One who was created by your own evil ways, one who will judge your very fate, one known only as...
"Certain Death" Daniel Stryfe ... [xXx] [...Fades in...] [...It's a cold dark night. Nothing can been seen, except the gray cloud, which is the thick fog at the top of the mountains, on a dark night. Nothing can be heard. Not a word, not a breath, nothing. From out of the darkness you hear footsteps. Far away at first, but getting closer by the second. They sound so close, as if you could reach out and touch whatever they come from, when suddenly they stop, and again the dark night become dead silent. A gray mist suddenly come from the darkness and from beyond that very darkness come a deep, frightening voice...]
Voice: "The darkness. It is the power in which I bring with me every day. It is the source of all that makes me go. Day in and day out, it helps me do what I need to do to survive. And I do not plan it to be stopped now. No. I plan to make the power greater. Therefor making me close to unstoppable. Why such a hunger for power? For it is power you need, it is what you thrive on. What makes you tick, so to speak. And it is what is going to push me all the way to becoming the UCHW world heavy weight champion."
[...You hear a few more footsteps, and from out behind the darkness walk a giant man. A very built man. With arms and legs like tree trunks, and a face with the darkest complexion you have ever seen. As you stare into his face, and he stares right back, you notice that one of his eyes is green, and the other, red. As the steam is wiped off from the camera, you see that the man is
"Certain Death" Daniel Stryfe. With a deep breath and another cloud of smoke, he again begins to speak...]
Daniel Stryfe: "The power in which I speak is the power gathered over the years after collecting useless soul, after useless soul. Each one wandering, wondering, what is right, what is wrong. Is what I did right? Or, what good can I do?
And for what? For they fear evil. Why? Simply because the possibility that they may not make it. Make it to heaven. For God perhaps would be angry with them. And this is what people run there lives on. The fact that they want to be accepted. But does the bible not speak of forgiveness? Does God not forget? Isn't it wrong not to forgive? So what is the worry? Why must everyone try to be perfect, try to be good? Just the chance that they may not be accepted. Myself, I believe God will forgive me for all my sins. Cause God is a forgiving man, women, or whatever it is he may be. But it does not get in the way I live my life. For I live my life the way I wish to live my life. It is the life that God handed me, and said here, do what you will with it. And that is exactly what I am doing. What I want..."
[...Daniel Stryfe pauses for a moment and takes a deep breath. Another gray cloud coming from within his mouth. He takes a step back and slowly turns his head, looking all around him. He then return his face to the camera and starts to speak again...]
"If he does not forgive me for that, then so be it. I shall live my life, as well as my after life in the same place, and the place I speak of is hell. But see, I have tried to change my life. Unlike most common mortals, who do nothing but whine, do nothing but cry, and do nothing but rely on others to help them out with there problems. I do not rely on other people to help me.
For what ever problems you have, you caused. And you have to learn to fight to survive. And come Tuesday that is what I will have to do. Fight to survive.
It is me again nine other men. Each fighting for each other, for the same thing. Fighting for are life, for this is our life. Wrestling is the only thing that hold us men up. It is our job, and our desire. Without it, we would be nothing, on a street to no where. So each of us are going into this with the same mind set, each thinking the same thing. But the truth is only one of us will walk away. Who it will be? Time shall tell. But I do know this, I will be doing the best I possibly can, for if I lose this match, it perhaps may be the end of me. For after years of fighting for what I believe, and fighting the way I want, to come away with nothing would prove to be not worth it anymore. And if I am to win. I would feel time for a change. For I would have done all I have fought for. All the souls, lives and career I have ended and taken, shall all be given back, and I shall pray for forgiveness from
God. Now to those nine other men, I do not know, nor do I care what you will do, but be careful the way you handle such things. For much like me, you may take a wrong turn somewhere, and if that is to happen..."
[...Stryfe gets a strange look on his face, and pauses momentarily, then begins to speak again...]
"You would end up places you don't want to be, and see things you wish not to see. So in closing, I say to you poor, unfortunate souls facing me tomorrow, be prepared, for I am going to give you, but a small example of what my entire life has been like. And trust me, you will not like it, but much like me, you will learn to deal with it. And please, take some advise for your long journey. Never underestimate the power in which you do not understand..."
[...Daniel Stryfe looks dead into the camera for a moment and then turn his head, and the rest of his body follows. He slowly walked back off into the darkness in which he came. You are left with the soft footsteps of Daniel
Stryfe...]
[...Fades out...] [xXx] ... The time once again is upon us. Again evil will take over. The power struggle will change. And it will be nothing more than... Armageddon.
For I now have a chance to single handily become the very best the UCHW has to offer. And if I win this title, I see myself being the holder of that title going into the millennium. And if this is so, my already great power will grow to even larger heights. And in the mitts of all this carnage, all this destruction, the souls of nine other poor men will be up in the air. The lives of nine other men will lie solely in my hands ... [xXx]
