Ragnarok RP..please comment on.

19 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[we come back from a commercial to the CD announcing booth. Seated in the booth are Johhny Gomez, Nick Diamond, and WWF Heavyweight champ Stone Cold
Steve Austin]
Johnny : Welcome back Deathmatch-a-holics..we've already seen one heck of an evening already..with the main event still to come.
Nick : Let's recap the previous matches as they ready the ring for the Main
Event [a still picture comes up on the screen as the team does voice-overs. The first picture is Jon Stewart holding Craig Killbourne's head..with the spine still attached. Craig's body is lying nearby]
Johnny : Our first match saw current "Daily Show" host Jon Stewart against former host Craig Kilborne.
Nick : Craig started out fast....but Jon *really* picked it up a notch here
Austin : I guess 3 failed network talk shows can add up to alot of whoop-ass to get rid of.
[next picture is a frozen Vince Vaughn about to be shattered by a sledgehammer weilding Christopher Walken]
Johnny : This was the *oddest* match we have ever seen here on Celebrity Deathmatch...the battle for the title of..."The Guy who creeps you out the most when standing within five feet of him".
Nick : I have to agree Johnny....as soon as the match sarted.....Vince kept re-enacting lines from "Terminator 2" word for word......
Austin : Kinda hard to lose to a man when you know how it's gonna end... Johnny : Too true Stone Cold. Nick : At this time we would like to introduce our special guest announcer for tonight's Main Event.
Johnny : He makes his home in Las Vegas, Nevada and has been a *huge* force in professional wrestling since his debut just over a year ago.
Austin : I gotta tell ya......I like this kid....I mean <beep> I don't like *anyone*..yet I like this kid alot...the EWO Heavyweight and Hardcore Champion...and leader of the New Apocalypse..Ragnarok! <a bastardized version of "Illusion" by Creed plays as Rags makes his way to the announcing booth>
<Rags takes a seat next to Austin> Rags : Hey..thanks for the compliment Steve...means alot comin from you. Austin : Let's not starts huggin and kissin each other Rag's I'm just givin' credit where credit is due and you happen to be one tough sonofabitch..and that's the bottom line.
Rags : <chuckling> Fair enough. <looking to the camera> and now....the MAIN EVENT. <the Celebrity Deathmatch "MAIN EVENT" graphic appears on the screen> Johnny : In one corner...Captain James T. Kirk and his chief science officer
Mr. Spock. Austin : yeah and in the other good old JC Picard and that Klingon whoop-asser
Warf. <camera cuts to the ring> Mils Lane : Let's GET IT ON! Rags : Well....it appears that the 2 captains will get things started out here..and Picard *quickly* takes the upper hand with a series of stiff jabs.
Nick : Eventhough Kirk is getting it handed to him right here...he doesn't seem to be showing it....
Austin : 's cause he has the range of a cell phone in the Holland tunnel. Johnny : Tag is made to Spock...and Kirk seems to be confering with his teamate about a game plan.
Kirk : <dramatically> Spock...........I..........think...we..................might.........want to..................
<we hear a bloody "SLICE" as we see Spock's head fall from his body> ..............attack..........quickly..................................keep them............<seeing Spock's head>.....oh.......dear.
<Warf becons for Kirk to enter the ring> Rags : so much for the peace treaty huh? <all laugh> <Kirk grabs Warf by the shoulder..squeezing hard> Nick : Hmmmm....looks like a failed attemp at a Vulcan Nerve Pinch. <Warf grabs Kirk by the wrist..and snaps his arm back...breaking it off> Austin : yep...but this is a successfull attempt at a Klingon Ass kickin.. <Picard jumps in and grabs Kirk from behind> Warf : Captain....having 2 members of the same team in the ring at the same time without the proper tag being made is in direct violation of Deathmatch rule number..
Picard : Warf..... Warf : you...just want me to hit him alot huh? Picard : make it so. Rags : Well........unless Bones or Scottie come down soo.....this one is all but wrapped...
<<<<<<<<CCCCCCCCCCCRRRRRRRAAAAAAAASSSSSSSSHHHHHHH>>>>>> < a large White Star ship crashes through the roof and lands on the ring...crushing all 3 individuals as Mils Lane dives to safety>
Austin : What the hell??? Johnny : This is WRONG.....so VERY VERY WRONG... <a hatch on the ship opens and standing up out of the cockpit is Captain/President Sheridan from the Babylon 5 station> Sheridan : ummmm......anybody got a pair of jumper cables? Rags : I always KNEW B5 would be the death of Trek. Austin : damnit....THAT's where I know that S.O.B from.. <Austin stands on the announcers table> Austin : anyone think that little Mnimbari counceor Delen is a hottie....give me a HELL YEAH!
<the audience goes apeshit> HELL YEAH!!!!!!!! Nick : Well....that wraps up another episode of Celebrity Deathmatch..I'm Nick
Diamond.. Johnny : I'm Johnny Gomez Rags : I'm Ragnarok Austin : and I'm Stone Cold Steve Austin tellin ya.....Good fight.. Rags : and Good Night. <camrea fades> Rob handler of..... Ragnarok EWO Heavyweight Champion EWO "unsanctioned" Hardcore Champion leader of the New Apocalypse and Vinnie "Stone Pony" Cepaccelli 1/3 of the Jersey City Street Machine "There's a new kind of justice in town...JUST US!"
