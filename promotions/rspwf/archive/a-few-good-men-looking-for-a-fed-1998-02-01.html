A Few Good Men looking for a fed.

1 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi, I'm the handler of A Few Good Men. We were a stable in the now defunct
BBCW, and are starting in a new fed called HCW. I am looking for a new fed to bring FGM to. If you are a fed for original wrestlers only, full commentary on cards, and will let me have at least three out of my six wrestlers in your fed
(with whoever cant wrestle [due to wrestler limits] as managers) E-mail me.
Below I have included a short sample roleplay. (The camera fades in as "Slick" Mike Dippi is sitting around in a Boss shirt, and Boss jeans, with five people in Boss cloths standing around him.)
"Slick" Mike Dippi: Yo, WHAZZUP peeps? You really thought I'd retire? You really though I'd leave? Naah, and I know you all are so very happy, you all love me. I know you do. But this time, I dropped those losers the DoD, them and their obsession with death. Don't need them. I got a real alliance now, a real family. This is A Few Good Men. FGM are here for one reason, and one reason only.
(Hits a remote control and "It's All About the Bengamins" starts blasting from his stereo system. He hits the remote again and it stops.)
"Slick" Mike Dippi: Yea, it's all about da bengees over hea. But, before I go on, I'm gonna introduce A Few Good Men.
(Points to the smallest) "Slick" Mike Dippi: Over here, Sean G. (Sean G gives the NYC bloods signal) Sean G: Eassssssstsiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiddddeee! Yo, I just over hea representing some Queens flavor. I just hea wit my boy Mike, just chillin'. Over hea to represent Queens!
"Slick" Mike Dippi: Yea, dos ****** down in Queens got dat **** on locks ova dea. Yo... (motions to another one of the guys) "Slick" Mike Dippi: Yea, now dis is cool cat Easy C, representin' Manhatten over there. Easy C: Yea, representing Manhatten island, we got it going on. Me and my gang, we bustin dem up over there. Now we ready to bust some lip over here in BBCW.
Big Balla's, yea right. You dont know balla's untiul you've been in my hood. Mike D: My next boy, is simply know as Da Mack, from all up in Bronx. Da Mack: Yea, BOOGIE DOWN BRONX everyone, BOOGIE DOWN BRONX!! Yea, and 2Poc said "California knows how ta party." Uh uh, da Bronx knows how ta party.
(Da Mack gets A Few Good Men chanting "NYC, knows how to party". "Slick" finally gets them calmed down.) "Slick" Mike D.: Aiight, aight. Calm down. Yo, everyone, my next boy is from
Harlem baby!! Spreading joy and drugs up in dea, Da Great Craig T!!!
Da Great Craig T: Yea, first I wanna say big up to all my homies in Harlem,
Joe, Jerry, Cindy, Doug, and Shaniqua. See, I just here keepin' it real. Just keepin' it real. BBCW betta watch out, couse Da Great Craig T. is coming to a ring near you.
("Slick" Mike Dippi gets a huge smile on his face as he looks at the last man sittin' on the couch. He's huge!!!)
"Slick" Mike Dippi: My last, but definatly not worst hommie, a man all of you saw with the DoD. He just got out of the Dungeon, and he's mad as hell!!!
Everyone welcome, GOLIATH. Goliath: RAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Sean G: Yea, dats my boy. Easy C: Peace out. Everybody: Peace out. (camera abruptly cuts off.)
