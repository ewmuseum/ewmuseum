[ The camera fades out ad BBB Boston Bad Boy goes back to the P.A.L Gym....] =================================== Ian Walsh =================================== {--Begin Transmission--} [Camera fades in.] [The camera drop-focuses on a brisk and foggy spring morning. The sunshine is peaking through various holes in clouds above. The camera pans down to show the hard, black macadam that surfaces what seems to be a road. The camera swings to the left a tad and shows what resembles a cobblestone curb. The cobblestone varies in color. All bricks are the same size and shape and are held together with concrete.]
[A squirrel gallantly hops over the curb and off of the road. The camera pans up and shows some of the trees. It seems as the fog is starting to break up.
That is a sign that this will be a sunny day. In the distance, still caught up in the fog, a figure can be seen. Although it is hard to see the figure in detail, it is prevelant that a figure is there. A bird chirps in the background. After a moment of chirping, the is interrupted by a sharp,
low-toned voice.] "The fog. It makes it hard to see what is standing before me, that's if there is anything standing before me."
[The figure leans his body to his left and against a tree in the distance. He crosses his left leg around his right and relaxes it.]
"Brushing my hands in front of me will not do anything. The clouds are dense and thick and there is nothing that I can do to clear that
_path_ for me to walk along."
[He crosses his arms and sighs. The camera zooms in a little bit.] ". . . It can be tiresome out there; in the fray. After fighting many battles, I may be due for a break."
[He chuckles.] "I really don't think that _I_ am the kind of guy who needs to take some time off so I can _rest_ up."
[The fog eventually starts to break apart at a rapid pace. The figure can be noticed as Ian Walsh.]
"I realize that if I wait around long enough . . ." [Ian pulls himself away from the tree and quickly walks down the road. He stops about thirty feet from the camera.]
". . . I will find the spot to _break on through_ and get to the _other side_." [Ian is wearing black and white striped, Reebok sneakers, black sweat pants with the pant legs rolled up to the knees, white socks, an orange, Ocean
Pacific T-shirt, a black, French cap worn sideways, and black sunglasses.] "I have found that spot. And courtesy of John Anderson, I will stay on the other side . . ."
[Ian waves his right arm horizontally from his left to his right and then points to the ground.]
". . . and take over." [Ian smiles, but that smile turns into a devilish grin.] "I have been in this sport for such a long time and I plan on being here until the day of my death. When will that be? Who cares?
All I know is that I am going to continue to kick a lot of ass and I am taking down names as we speak."
[Ian points into the camera with his right arm as he slides his sunglasses down his nose a tad with his left arm.]
"You may have a _peak_ at greatness . . ." [He slides his sunglasses back up. He then retracts his arm and points to himself with his thumb.]
". . . but I am the only one who will achieve it." [Ian slowly turns around as he bows his head in silence. The camera comes around from the other side and shows Ian with his hads folded and his face pale and expressionless.]
"As time goes on, I see that the challenges are abundant and they keep coming in the numbers. I have not failed to accept my challenges. I don't intend on failing when I challenge a man to a match . . . or a fight."
[Ian points at the ground with his right hand.] "There . . . . . . that is the place where John Anderson will end up. When it is all said and done, I will be declared the winner. And . . ."
[The camera follows Ian's index finger and shows a mist-filled ground.] ". . . when the smoke clears . . ." [The fog breaks and shows the macadam.] ". . . you, Mister Anderson, will not just be declared the loser . . ." [The camera shows Ian's face with the sun shining in the background.] ". . . you will be declared . . . . . . my victim." [Ian walks to his left and into the front lawn of somebody's property. (Ok, it's his.) He walks down the two stairs as he holds onto the rail on his right. The rail is painted white, but the paint looks to be peeling.]
"As I head back down the stairs of greatness, I find the competition that is supposed to be on _my_ level."
[Ian laughs.] "Give me a break . . . You mean to tell me that there are people who are actually on _my_ level of ability? You mean to tell me that there are actually people out there with as much talent and skill as Ian Walsh? Yeah . . ."
[Ian nods his head and smiles as if he was humored in some way.] ". . . right." [Ian continues to walk along the vast front lawn in front of the red brick house with red shingles.]
"I walk down this path . . . with the confidence and the mindset that I am the absolute best in this business. I have the attitude that I cannot be beaten."
[Ian stops and puts his hands on his hips. He turns toward the camera.] "Can you consider me as cocky? . . . No." [Ian takes his hands off of his hips and continues walking.] "Mister Anderson, you cannot consider me as cocky. Why? Well, I have what it takes to back up every single word that comes out of my mouth." [He stops walking and turns his head toward the camera.] "There is nothing that you sorry wretches can do about it!" [Ian points in the direction he is heading in and the camera swings to it's right to see what it is.]
"There she is . . ." [The camera shows a giant, gray boulder in the middle of the lawn. Ian walks toward the boulder and after a moment he gets there.]
"This is the mountain . . . the mountain that everyone wishes that they can be the _king_ of."
[Ian climbs the boulder and stands on top of it. He raises his hands in victory and yells.]
"_I_ am the _king_ of this _mountain_! No one can dethrone me! This is my _rock_! _I_ will not be defeated! I will not be beaten! Mister Anderson, there is nothing you can do, my friend! You, like everyone else, will have to face my wrath; my reign of terror! And no one will survive . . .
. . . and I mean . . . . . . no one . . ." [Ian makes the leap of faith from the boulder and lands on the soft ground with good balance. He raises his hands in victory once again.]
"I take the risks . . ." [Ian smiles.] ". . . and I don't fear the consequences." [Ian starts to walk toward the front porch of the house. He stops and motions for the cameraman to follow along.]
"The ring is like my home. I know it inside and out. John, I hope you know that when our match comes along, it is just like you _breaking into_ my home. The ring is my home and you are just another member of the long list of intruders who have decided to take it upon themselves to _come on in_ on their own free will. Mister Anderson, this _invasion_ will be a failure . . .
. . . and this failure . . . . . . will cost you dearly." [Ian opens the white framed screen door by the black handle and enters his house. The screen door closes behind him.]
"Oh, and Johnny boy . . . . . . prepare to meet . . . . . . your demise." [Ian slowly closes a heavy, wooden, brown-stained door. The birds start to chirp again. The camera contiues to focus on the door until it finally sstarts to lose focus.]
[Camera fades out.] {--End Transmission--} =================================== Ian Accepts the "BBB" Challange =================================== {--Begin Transmission--} [Camera fades in.] [The camera drop-focuses on a empty room with pearl gray, concrete walls. The light is bright enough to see everything, but it isn't too bright. In the corner of the room, there is a puddle. The focus of the camera lens slowly moves up the wall and shows a vertical line of water. The camera lens focuses on the ceiling and shows water slowly trickling down the wall. There is a deep silence that overwhelms the entire room. Suddenly, a familiar, relaxing and calming voice breaks the silence.]
"It's almost as if the room is crying . . . . . . like it is filled with saddened and horrid feelings. Feelings in which cannot be controled or contained . . . Feelings that cause stress and tension in such great amounts that it will just explode and make a hellish scene . . ."
[The voice pauses. The camera zooms out and it shows the whole room. In the center of the room, sitting in a brown, wooden chair, is "Human Torture Rack"
Ian Walsh. Ian continues.] "One day . . . . . . the tension and stress will be too much of a burden. Then . . . . . . SNAP!" [Ian smiles . . . He is wearing blue-jeans, a plain, white t-shirt, and he is wearing black and white, Reebok sneakers. The camera shows his mid length hair parted in the middle. His five-day scruff looks dark as night. His face shows a spaced-out look that makes him look like he is in deep thought or is day dreaming. His arms are crossed as if to prevent them from falling off. The camera slowly zooms in.]
"Everything just gives way. Your life gets turned up-side-down . . . You no longer are in control of your mind. You are no longer in control of your actions . . ."
[The camera stops zooming in and it shows Ian from appoximately ten feet away.] "I beat Punisher Kegonite, in the N.A.W. East Coast Title Tournament Tournament." [Ian pauses and slowly strokes his nose with his left thumb and index finger.] "Now . . . . . . I see that I want an even bigger fight. I want to face B.B.B. Boston Bad Boy. Triple B . . . Well well well . . . You are just the wretch that I need to beat in order to gain even more fame in the wrestling world. And that, my friend, will not be a problem."
[Holding his chin, he looks into the camera.] "Triple B, ol' boy . . . You make me wonder. Where would I be if I didn't want to face you? Who would I want to go and fight? Would it be some low life, some
_nobody_? Then again . . . you don't seem to rank much higher in my book." "You go around and think that you are some bad-ass. You go to the ring with the same mindset as everyone else and you think that you can't be beaten."
[Ian shakes his head.] "Poor man . . . Do you really think that you are unbeatable? Are you actually that stupid to you think that you are going to get passed me, let alone, get passed me without a fight?
We can find out the answer to that burning question, my friend. Oh yes . . . I am challenging you, Triple B, to a match right here, right now, for my title. You wanted the shot . . . and now I am giving it to you."
[Ian shakes his head again in disgust.] "Ya know . . . it's a shame. Triple B, you have what it takes to make it big in this business. Hell, you have made it big. Your name is famous in this federation. Well, ya see, Triple B . . . I want my name to be a _big_ name, too. I will make it happen, Triple B. Oh yes . . . I'll make it happen."
[Ian reaches underneath the chair and pulls out a glass bottle that is filled with iced tea. From the label, you can tell that it is lemon-flavored Nestea.
Ian twists the metal cap off of the bottle and takes a sip of the tea. After a sigh of satisfaction, Ian continues.]
". . . Boston Bad Boy . . . In just a few minutes, you will be in for a _real_ fight. This won't be any stupid corporate type of fight. Ya see . . . I know what you can do. You capabilities are greater than many people's. However, my capabilities . . . my skills . . .
my threshold of pain . . . They all surpass that of yours. So when you and I step into that ring . . . When you and I look at each other in the eyes . . . you will see that I am not any normal man. You will see that the intensity makes my blood boil . . . Triple B, ol' buddy . . . In just a few minutes, you will need to prepare yourself. And as many people have heard me say this before, I will say it again. Triple B, you, my friend, need to prepare yourself. 'What for,' you may ask."
[Ian smiles.] "That's easy . . . . . . Prepare to meet . . . . . . your demise . . ." [Ian looks down at the floor and goes into deep thought.] [Camera fades out.] {--End Transmission--} =================================== NAW East Coast Title Match "BBB" Boston Bad Boy Vs.
