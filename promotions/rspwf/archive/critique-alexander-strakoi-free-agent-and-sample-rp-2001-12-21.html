Critique - Alexander Strakoi Free Agent and Sample RP

21 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello chaps, I don't know if anyone remembers my character or me but I used to handle an e-wrestler going by the name of Alexander Strakoi along with his manager
Pierre de la Bergerac. I'm thinking of getting back into e-wrestling but don't know where to start. If any prospective fed-heads are out there and would like to read one of Alexander's RPs then please read below. If anyone would like to offer any constructive criticism then that also would be very much appreciated, I've been in a few relatively big feds (hasn't everyone)
but one of them was NEO Wrestling which I'm not sure is still ticking over. Anyway, the sample RP is below, please read on: John PS: Also, two of my friends are also looking to get back into wrestling, one, Chris, handle Cracked and Crazy (hardcore singles wrestler) and the other, Will, handles Red Hot (with his manager Handsome Hank Shank)
---- (Scene opens to the arousing sight of Alexander Strakoi -- yes, that shining symbol of loveliness himself -- swaggering confidently into view and sporting a rather smug look on his otherwise chiselled features. He pauses for a moment, as if finding his bearings, before moving towards a large vacant pool table. Yes, that's right, Alexander is currently in a bar, one of many that litter the small town of Medicine Hat, Alberta, Canada. The bar itself is a quaint sort of affair as, like many other small town bars, it has a pool table, a few tables and chairs dotted here and their and of course the usual clientele. This clientele however, instead of fulfilling a perfectly good stereotype by resembling something just short of a bunch of talking chimpanzees, seem to have gathered themselves around the aforementioned pool table with a look of general concern and/or confusion on each of their grubby faces. Alexander, never one to be indecisive, pokes one of them gingerly in the shoulder, presumably hoping to God that the individual's glum disposition isn't infectious....)
Alexander: Er.... are you infectious? Guy: What?! Alexander: Oh nothing... anyway, what seems to be the problem here? (The Guy -- 'for he shall be called thus' -- points dumbly towards the base to the pool table where a well worn boot can be seen protruding out from underneath. It wiggles drunkenly as a loud fart cracks its way free as if in a greeting to Alexander's manly presence. Alexander, well trained in the habits of the drunkard, sniffs reluctantly at the now feted air.)
Alexander: Is that you Pierre? FROM UNDERNEATH: ................................ could be... Alexander: What are you doing laying there? Pierre: Thinking. Alexander: Thinking? Pierre: Oui, thinking. (As the crowd around the table dissipate into the background, Alexander grabs a chair and stands in next to the only part of Pierre visible to the camera, his boot.)
Alexander: Thinking is quite a dangerous habit for you Pierre; it's got you into a lot of trouble in the past.
Pierre: I know that Alexander, but sometimes life presents you with a situation were you have no other option but to think, and I'm afraid I'm in one of those situations now.
Alexander: What do you mean? Pierre: Well, look at it from my point of view; there I was, drinking happily in my hotel room, pondering on whether or not I should order an
"educational video" from the front desk and before I knew it I wake up in a bar underneath this rather smelly pool table!
Alexander: ... well, it wouldn't be the first time something like this has happened to you Pierre. Remember last week?
Pierre: Yes, but why is it happening more regularly? Alexander: You've got me there Pierre... what where you drinking when you where in your hotel room?
Pierre: Bleach. Alexander: Oh well in that case y.......... WHAT!?! Pierre: Ordinary household bleach... its good for the bowels. Alexander: But... but... but... its suppose to be LETHAL if you drink it!!! Pierre: Bah! Blatant lies and fabricated nonsense Alexander, I've been drinking the stuff for years, and it's hasn't done me any harm *woof*.
Alexander: If you say so Pierre. (Alexander smiles wryly for a moment.) Alexander: Anyway, there's no point trying to remember now... chances are that you've killed off those specific parts of your brain with the bleach.
Pierre: (sighs) I suppose your right. (With a rather unfortunate squelchy sound, Pierre removes himself from underneath the table and plucks a small pocket size bottle of scotch out from his noxiously stained raincoat.)
Pierre: Where to now Alexander? Alexander: To the hospital to get your stomach pumped again. Pierre: (happily) Righty-ho then, lead the way! (The two men exit the bar and begin to make there way along the streets.) Pierre: So how do you feel? Alexander: How do I feel about what? Pierre: Your victory over Matthew Reason last Saturday. I thought it went rather well, all things considering...
Alexander: "All things considering"... Pierre I thrashed that little rapscallion all over the ring last Saturday and you know it. Okay, Dumars might have helped out a little, but all in all I was my usual outstanding self and no one can say differently!
Pierre: Okay Alexander, calm down, I was only making conversation. Jesus, your so touchy. (The two men walk in silence for a few moments before Alexander turns once again to face Pierre.)
Alexander: Who am I fighting this week? Pierre: No one really, I think Wallace Storm might have given you the night off for being so blatantly fantastic.
Alexander: (nodding) And I deserve it Pierre. Its not easy being this tremendous all the time, it takes a lot of time and effort to be this way.
Pierre: I'm sure it does Alexander. Alexander: What can I say? I'm a god amongst men. Pierre: Yeah Alexander, I know... Alexander: THE Man for the Moment. Pierre: You said... Alexander: A Shining Example To All... Pierre: OKAY ALEXANDER, I GET THE PICTURE! Alexander:....... Pierre: ....... Alexander: Pierre? Pierre: ...... yes? Alexander: Shout at me like that again and I'll break both your legs. Pierre: (Sniffs the air and shrugs.) Fair enough. (With that both men turn into an open door way with the sign "General Hospital" over the doors and, as if by magic, the scene begins to flicker, and fades to black.)
