This is an RP i did for the WWO (Ran by [detail withheld] ) And i would like some feed back. Dont matter which kind. Im re-sending it, cause last time i posted, i had my e-mail address wrong, so im not even sure if it made it to the RSP-WF
------------------------------------------- [ The Camera Fades in onto a dark room, at an unknown location. Viewers who put two and two together, will remember that this is the location of the man known as Mystic. As the Camera stares into the darkness, a glare of light shines across a table directly infront of the camera. After a few seconds, a
Strike of a match, and a candle is lit. You can smell the fumes of the wax melting to fuel the fire. The Camera Continues to stare into the Flame, as
Mystics Voice Pirces Through the silence ]
Mystic: Given the Propper Time, we all shall pay our dues. Be it in life, during sleep, or after death. You walks the fine line, the boundary between love and war, life and death, the world as we know it, and My World. Call it a lost cause, [chuckles] or live my life, and call it a way to go. Down and out, where do you turn, darkness. Shadows stretch forth outword, grabbing you, not letting go, screaming, yelling, crying for help. Breathing your last breath, crying your last tear. . .
[ Cold Silence hits the room, the Camera Still Focusing on the Candle ] . . . . . . . . . Until you Breath no more. Blood Runs cold, until it doesnt run no more. You see no downside to anything you do. You see no reason to stop inflicting pain. You wake up each and every morning, [Smirks] But then again, do you wake up _At_ All?_ I guess it goes with out saying, that people dont take to fondly of what i say. Call it inhibitions, call it skepticism, [Chuckles] or call it common Sense. Its either Their way, or the
High way. But when you step into my world, you enter _MY_WAY_.
[ More Silence, as the camera still pears into the candle ] Mystic: Hypnotized, Mezmorized, see it dance, flicker, your watching its every mood. and _SMACK_. Right upside the head it hits you, yet you're so gone, you didnt see it come. Subject of a full frontal attack [Chuckles] No, just a witness to the execution of the mind. Games of remorse, never letting your attention be put forth onto the oncoming terror. Eye Candy if you will, let you see what you want to so, and make you feel as if your avoiding the terror. Are you Prepared for the Oncoming threat? [Deep Breath] Don't Lose your Eyes, and ay attention to what you want to see. Expect then
_Unexpected_ [ A Deep Breath, makes the Candle Flicker, Almost going out, a breif light is left ] Mystic: Flickering, your last taste of Light, until darkness sits in, and then _LIKE THAT_ . . .
[ A swift breath blows out the candle, as the room sets to darkness ] . . . . . . . . . It All goes to waste, and all your hard work, your dedication, is sent into the unknown. Never, _EVER_ to be heard from again.
a Challanger you call me, a Champion it shall be. A Name Never vanquished, always in your sight. For to be a champion, you must have done something, but to become a champion, you must be doing something.
[ As the Darkness Sits across the Room, you can here the Candle being lifted, a flash of light, as the candle is relit, only inches from Mystics
Face ]
Mystic: Avalanche, the World Champion of the WWO. As i see it, you have one of two options to choose from. You can either Win this match, and go on with your life, not really accepting much from the win, considering im a new comer. Or, the Fact that in the wings, thier is a thought, that deep in your mind you may know the fate that is fotelling your future. And that this new comer, will do away with your ranking as world champion, and send you back down the latter of success, awating your reclaim to fame. [Chuckles] And as i see it, if i have my way, not only will you fall from this latter, but when you reach, you will only be grabbing air. Your name doesnt scare me, your ranking doesnt phaze me, and your World Title, doesnt turn me away. A chance of a life time, that i shall not let go. And you're the one sitting thier, not realizing that this, this stardome that you have "Earned" If you will, is all coming to an _END_.
[ As the camera Zooms in on the Flame of the candle once more, in the background, Mystics Face is a blur ]
Mystic: Are you willing to prepare for your downfall, undecided i bet. For deep in your mind, i know its their. The Fact that you cant let go. The
Little tid bit of info, thats eating you up on the inside. This New Comer wont go away. Deep in your heart, thumping into nothing, i know you wonder.
I know you watch, and i know you prepare. Deep sleep, about to be _SNOWED_ in upon. Avalanche, your time is very close, your time is very near, and you my freind [Chuckles] Shall be another feather of Destruction, to be added to my cap.
[ The Camera then focuses onto Mystics Face, and Candle is put a blur ] Mystic: The World Champion, is a title like no other. Avalanche, soon to be striped of it, shall be the first on my way _BACK_ to the top. Beware and
Bewarned, nothing is safe again. And, like this Interview may you thrive upon these words, May Darkness . . .
[ Mystic Blows out the candle, as the room goes to black ] . . . . . . . . . [ Chuckles] Imprison you. [ As the Scene Sets into darness, the camera stares ito the nothing, as chuckles are heard echoing into the nothing. The camera Slowly fades away, upon these bone chilling sounds of laughter ]
