[FREE AGENT] Max "The Beast" Cayn

27 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens in a small office. A man sits behind a desk with a telephone held to his right ear. He is clean shaven. He wears a black suit, light blue shirt, and a multicolored tie. A name tag on his left breast pocket reads, "Gerald Stuart, Sports Agent." Across the desk from him is a large man. His long blonde hair is tied back in a ponytail. His goatee is neatly cut. He has dark blue eyes and a small nose. He leans back in the cheap chair in which he sits. We turn back toStuart as he begins to talk.]
Stuart: Yes. Hello. Is this the World Wrestling Federation? Okay. Can you put me through to Mr. McMahon? [pause] Stuart: Mr. McMahon. Hello. My name is Gerald Stuart. I am a sports agent. Yes. I'm calling you regarding my client. Max Cayne. Yes.
Have you received those video tapes I sent to you? You did. Good. And what do you think? Will you sign him up? ... Why not? ...
[The man across from Stuart sighs and puts his foot on the desk. Stuart signals him to put his foot back down. The man complies.]
Stuart: Okay. Thank you Mr. McMahon. Sorry to have troubled you. [Stuart hands up the phone. He takes a pen from the notepad before him and begins scribbling quickly.]
Stuart: Well Max, the WWF won't take you either. Max: Why not? I'm the hottest rookie around. Stuart: I know. But McMahon says his roster is full. Which is a bunch of bull to me. Max: What about the ECW? Stuart: I called them yesterday. They gave me the same answer. No. Max: Alright then. I see we're gonna have to do things the hard way. I noticed that camera up there on the ceiling.
[He points to the ceiling.] Max: That gives me an idea. Call a camera crew in here. We're gonna do a little promo. Stuart: I'm not authorized to take that kind of action. Max: Fine. Tell the camera crew to meet us in downstairs in the lobby in ten minutes. Stuart [sighing]: Alright. [Stuart gets up and leaves the room. He closes the door behind him. Max smiles and puts his foot back on the desk.] Max: Dumb bastard. [A few moments pass and then the camera fades out. It returns to a new scene. We are now in the lobby of Stuart's building. The camera pans around to find several secretaries chatting on phones to their boyfriends. The camera then focuses in on a white couch near the entrance. Max and Stuart sit on the couch. Stuart looks nervous and continually glances down at his notepad. Max sits casually. His right leg crossed over hs left. He wears a cammy shirt with an emblem for the
US Marines patched on the left. He also wears black leather pants. A beret rests on his right leg.]
Max: It's about time you people got here. First of all, I'd like to point out that this is a promotion for me. When this is done, I want copies made and sent to all the top federations around the country.
Okay. First of all, I am Max Cayn. But while in the ring, people often call me The Beast. I'm an ex US Marine and I served on a battleship with my Company. I gotta tell you. Those Navy bastards couldn't keep their own bunk neat if it killed them. But let's not get sidetracked.
I began training as a wrestler five years ago. A couple years later, I graduated and went to a small backyard federation. It was quite degrading so I left. Next I went to a small-time Indy fed by the name of Philadelphia Hardcore Wrestling. I became the World champion there and was undefeated until they filed bankruptcy. So that's where I am now. Without a job and without a respectable salary.
[Stuart hands him a leather pouch. Max unzips it and pulls out two video tapes. He holds them in front of the camera.]
Max: These are videos of my past career. These should be sent to all federations that this promo is sent to. Review them. And give me a call.But don't think that just because you wave a hefty check in front of my eyes that I'll immediately join your fed. I have some conditions. A, I want a title shot within the first six months of my career there. B, I want a lot of airtime. C, if you want my services, you better offer me a good push. D, Your fed must be established. And what I mean by that is, you must have been around for AT LEAST, six months. And E, your roster has to be reasonably sized with original personalities. I won't even waste my breath to even think of joining some fed with a Stone Cold ripoff. So those are my conditions. Do you want Max "The Beast" Cayn in your federation? Then contact my agent,
Gerald Stuart here, at
[detail withheld] [Max stands up and stops for a moment. We can tell that he is at least seven feet tall and very close to three hundred pounds. He walks off and the camera focuses in on Stuart.]
Stuart: You people hire him. Because without him having a job, I don't get paid. [Stuart stands up and walks off in a different direction. The camera fades out and then crackles. The tape stops.]
