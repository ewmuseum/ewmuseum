Classic heels and faces

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
For those of you out there who want to be something other than a tweener, here`s a guide to the traditional characteristics of a good heel, and a good face. Match-writers, take note as well. A GOOD FACE... - Fights heroically back from defeat. - Plays to the crowd before and/or after a match - Fights cleanly - Will abide by the rules, even if the wrong decision goes his way - Will get lots of cheers from the crowd - Will rescue other faces...and even some heels - Will fight for the fans, for the people and defend the pride of others - Fights with courage - Shows mercy - Only gets mad when really provoked A GOOD HEEL... - Will cheat to win - Can be cowardly - Will lie, will resort to calling names - Is booed by the crowd - Insults the crowd - Will sneak attack opponents - Plays mind-games - Has no respect for anyone except, maybe, other heels - Will not come to the rescue. If anyone can add to this list, it`d be appreciated.
