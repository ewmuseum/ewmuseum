rp from Sasuke

8 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fade in...} {Scene:An empty room. The windows are shut and the shudders are pulled down. Only the smallest rays of sunlight penetrate through the cracks and crevices. In the corner, lurks a lone figure, cradling his knees and staring towards the light. In his hands, he holds a bloody and torn mask. He rocks himself slowly, back and forth, out of and back into the shadows. A whispering giggle can be heard emanating from the dark. The face, hidden deep within the self-made darkness cannot be made out. However, the camera can still focus in on a familiar pair of bloodshot eyes. It stares intently at the small rays of light.
Not a blink. Not a twitch. Not a single hint of movement. The eyes stare. And the remain staring as a taped voice picks up...}
"...THE CRAZIEST PERSON IN THE HWA..." {More giggling.} "WHAT THE HELL IS PUDDING?" {The laughter begins to grow.} "Pathetic." {The laughter is at an audible level.} "I can see all...and I know all..." {The laughter continues to grow in pitch...} "Rob Van Dam.....Who?" {The laughter stops. Silence, and nothing more. We hear heavy breathing and then...a familiar voice...}
Sasuke:"They mock me..." {Sasuke pauses.} Sasuke:"Who?" {Sasuke answers himself.} Sasuke:"They do." {Sasuke's eyes remain fixated on the shudders.} Sasuke:"I am what they wish to be. I do what they can never do. I achieve heights they may never achieve. I am what they aspire to be. For that, they mock me."
{Sasuke rocks back into the shadows and remains in the deepest depths of darkness within the corner.}
Sasuke:"Who? {He pauses.} Sasuke:"...They do." {A cough. Nothing more.} Sasuke:"I give them originality. I give them dreams to shoot for. Goals to reach that they cannot and never shall achieve. I push them beyond their own limitations. I give my all to better them. And for what? They mock me."
{A sigh.} Sasuke:"Who?" {Sasuke ignores himself.} Sasuke:"They mock me. They mock me and they laugh. But they...they base themselves on...me. For that, they mock only themselves and their friends. They would be nothing without me. "
{The voice of Stone Cold is heard from a past interview...} WELL SASUKE ALLS I GOT TO SAY TO YOU IS YOU ARE THE BIGGEST PIECE OF TRASH I
HAVE EVER SEEN. BESIDES SANDMAN OF COURSE. WELL YOU HAVE TO DECIDE WHAT YOU ARE
DOING. ONE DAY YOU DONT WANT TO WRESTLE THE NEXT DAY YOU ARE KICKING THE
(CENSORED) OUT OF SOME JOBBER. {The laughing begins again.} Sasuke:"How soon we forget our own words." {Another, older clip of Steve Austin is played.} I RESPECT THE GREAT SASUKE MORE THAN ANYBODY IN THE HWA EVER. {Sasuke lowers his head. However, his gaze on the daylight penetrating the shudders remains.}
Sasuke:"And the jobber he is refering to. It is his friend. His stablemate,
Sean Etter. See? They do not even have respect for each other. Why should I think they should have respect for anything? Much less, for me? And for that, it is not a surprise that they mock me. "
{A deeper sigh...} Sasuke:"Their respect cannot be earned. It cannot. For they do not know what respect is. No...respect must be taken. Taken by who?"
{He pauses.} Sasuke:"Taken by me." {The first smile seen in days appears on Sasuke's face.} Sasuke:"The jealous, the naive, the downtrodden, the...pathetic. They all do not know what was once common knowledge. They have all forgotten what should never have been forgotten. And for that, they shall pay dearly."
{He draws a bit closer to the light.} Sasuke:"People so easily forget. They must be reminded of their mistakes. They must be reminded of the dangers of forgetfulness. And for that, I shall teach them. I shall remind them of what they are. And what they can never be. I shall remind them of where their place is. Of their boundaries. How sad...people who need to be reminded of their very own boundaries...by one, who does not have any boundaries."
{The smile grows wider.} Sasuke:"It is their own failure to remember. It is their fault and their's alone. I cannot be held accountable for their ignorance. And the time for fun and games is over. The time for learning and knowing has begun."
{He moves closer to the light. Close enough for the rays to lighten his body and reveal the tattered and torn clothing. But not enough to reveal his face.}
Sasuke:"And when all is done, they shall know. They shall remember. I shall show them the path from their ignorance. And show them what they can never be."
{He pulls the torn mask closer to himself and puts it back on.} Sasuke:"And tomorrow night. Another shall be taught this lesson. Rob Van Dam shall finally learn what the he cannot battle what ALL aspire to be."
{Sasuke drifts back into the darkness.} Sasuke:"Who?" {Sasuke smiles.} Sasuke:"Me." {Sasuke sits there rocking silently back and forth. Silence once again fills the daylight air. Clouds gather and darkness fills the room as camera fades to black.}
