[RP Critique] "The Only Bitch" Crystal Marquez

10 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey people, I'm E.J. and some of you know me as the handler of Kuba Rico. Rico's wife,
Crystal, has been wrestling for about two months now, and she has a shot at the
ICW women's championship at the next ppv. Here's a roleplay that I did for last week's card. Note: Asrael is Rico and Crystal's "third partner" in the marrige ;-) I welcome all questions, praises, and insults :-) Gracias.
========================================================= [The scene opens at a Park Fast parking lot in New York City. It's daytime, and the lot is quite full. There are cars of many makes and models, but the camera is focusing on a red Dodge Durango. The camera turns to the left a few feet and we see Crystal Marquez walking to the
Durango. She motions for the camera man to walk to the truck. She's wearing a pair of dark blue jeans, a white shirts, and black boots. Over this, she's wearing a three-quarter length leather jacket, and a pair of sun glasses are over her head. She reaches in her purse and takes out a set of keys. She deactivates the car alarm, and opens the driver's side.]
CRYSTAL: Get in...we're going for a lil' ride. [The camera man goes around the truck and enters through the passenger's side. Crystal turns on the ignition and turns on the stereo system. She put the sunglasses over her eyes and pulls out. She pays the man in the booth and heads for the street. While the two are talking, Crystal does not look at the camera, but keeps focused on the road]
CAMERA MAN: Where are we going? CRYSTAL: I know where _I'm_ going. I'm going to La Guardia Airport to catch a flight to Washington D.C. I dunno where you're headed.
CAMERA MAN: Without Rico? CRYSTAL: He's down there already. He met up with Devon Case earlier. They're probably killing each other right now. I had to make sure things were cool at the club before I left.
CAMERA MAN: Are you all set with your Bug Zapper match at Shockfest? CRYSTAL: Oh, no doubt...but Asrael and I have to take care of the Midknight sisters first. They are going to pay _big time_ for what they did to Azzy last week. They are just a pair of playa haters, and they are jeallie that I have a shot at the belt against their buddy.
CAMERA MAN: Tiffani and Arista have wrestled together for quite some time now. You two have yet to do so. Do you ladies have the chemistry?
CRYSTAL: Azzy and I have wrestled with each other before [giggling] but I won't go there right now. You see, when you have the talent of Crystal and Asrael, you don't need to work together for a long time. We just click like that. We're both hardcore, and we can both take it to the air.
The Midknight Sisters can't heck us, Camera Guy. We have a stronger connection than any other two women have in the ICW...even the former
Dichotomy. Ask Azzy....she'll tell ya. CAMERA MAN: Let's talk about the your match at Shockfest. You are still considered a rookie by ICW, and well to be honest, all of wrestling's standards. You only have six matches to date. Do you think that you are ready for the big time as the ICW Women's champ?
CRYSTAL: What makes you think that I am not? You must remember, that I have fought in a few matches in the ICW before I did it "full time." I was in some pretty tough matches with my Kubanito against some of the so called best. This ain't nothin' different, except it's one on one. This has been in the works for quite some time, but like the ICW with Rico, they also kept me down. They just wanted a pretty face and a fly body when I signed a contract to the ICW. Well...they still have that, but this fly body has a pair of fists and feet that can rip some chick a new one.
CAMERA MAN: Shockfest is the most dangerous pay per view that ICW has. Even Rico came up a little short last year in that Crucifix Match. He was gone for a few weeks and he still feels the effects of it sometimes. So why are you coming up with a match that's even more dangerous than that?
Bug zappers aren't the safest things in the world. CRYSTAL: That's easy. I want to make a point. I want to show every woman in the ICW that _hands down_, I am the toughest one around here. I know I'm going to get hurt. So what? It comes with the territory. The ladies around here are so delicate, it makes me sick. Look at the card...there are only four women in the entire pay per view! There's me of course against Tara and she's there cuz she has to be. Once again,
Asrael got screwed out of a Hardcore Title shot, so she took it upon herselef to be the ref, and Chana...we'll...let's just say that Chana is an Asrael-wannabe. A woman who thinks that she can heck it against the men. The only women that belong in this pay per view are myself and Azzy, cuz we are hardcore in _every_ sense of the word. We made the initiatve to get there, and ain't nobody out there is gonna stop us. This Saturday on Fallout, we are going to make a statement, and that statement is that
_nobody_ _is_ _gonna_ <bleep> _with_ us. Plain and simple. Before I take the highway, I'm going to drop you off, so get you're $hit together. CAMERA MAN: How am I going to get back? CRYSTAL: You shouldn't have come with me then. Take a cab or something. CAMERA MAN: I don't have any money! CRYSTAL: Then walk...I don't care. [The car stops and the camera man walks out of the car.] CRYSTAL: I'm not "The Only Bitch" for nothing, ya know. HONK HONK CRYSTAL: BYYYYYYYEEEEEE!!!! CAMERA MAN: You got that right.... [The camera man starst walking and you see his left thumb in front of the camera lense, looking to hitch a ride. All cars keep passing him by.]
CAMERA MAN: Damn.
