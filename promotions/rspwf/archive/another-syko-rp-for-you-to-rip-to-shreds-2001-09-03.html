Another Syko RP for you to rip to shreds. =)

3 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yes, i'm bored. I need something to do. So posting RPs on RSPWF seems like a good idea. A little background, Krumm & Xian Pu are friends of Syko. They weren't booked, but have their own little beef with the Authority (as you probably saw on this week's Mothership, if not, go read it.)
And yes, I know I gave no indication as to the whereabouts of the trio. Besides that, feel free to blast me or love me, whichever you'd prefer. -------------- "Gimme that beat, bitch! *slap* We are at WAR!" Cypress Hill - 'Fuck Westside Connection' [And we are. War with the Authority.... was with Jeff Kane.... War....huh.... what is it good for?] Syko: Title defenses? Andrew Krumm: Revenge? Xian: Proving who's better than who? [Well fine. Forget the song then.] Syko: Dude, i'm gonna make it simple. You asked for war, you're getting your war. if you think you're gonna outlast me, you're fuckin' nuts, okay? So you've got a spiky little bat.... and claim to know a thing or two about hardcore. Fine. Bring it. Bring it all. I am so ready to take on the whole world right now. But that's not what i take issue with right now, after all, a match is a match.
What I take issue with, is the fact that you don't quite grasp the concept of this title, you have no idea WHAT I HAVE PLANNED FOR IT, and the fact that you're NOT READY, as a man, as a competitor, or as a sick mind to hold this title.
Sure, we're going extreme this week. But does that mean that this is all i'm turning the belt into? Another belt for junk matches noone cares about?
You must be out of your god-damn mind. I picked this stip for this match for one reason and one reason only. The people want to see it. They care about seeing Jeff Kane get absolutely fucked up beyond all recognition, they wanna see you bleed and scream, and all that.... I took this title in a 4 way match... and the only reason people cared about that match was because of me. Because Syko was in it, making his return. Noone gave 2 shits about this title before I had it.
Solkoff claimed there was no threats to his reign for the 3 months he had it. Do you know WHY? Solkoff, you're a NOBODY. Noone cared about you. Noone cared about your extreme championship run. People literally turned their tvs to other channels when you came on. Don't believe me? Check the overnight ratings for your entire run. Ratings went from 8.0 with an 11 share for whatever segment was before you, to like 3.2 with a 5 share. The whole thing was bad for business, bad for the SEWF, bad for the viewers, and bad for the credibility of this title. You're retiring from extreme? GOOD. You sucked anyway. Let me give this belt some damn credibility. After all, we can't have some pussy that runs from a true challenge holding what will become the most coveted title in the SEWF. And you know how it's gonna get there? You know what's gonna make people care about this title and the power it has?
The simple fact that I know how to use it. That I know, and I understand the power this title has. I mean, I can make my match anything I want..... but people like you Solkoff... people like you, Kane.... You wouldn't know what to do with the damn thing. And noone would care.
They care when I hold it. People see me with this title, and know that i'm the biggest threat to anyone in this place. People see people like you...
and they say 'So what?'. And that's the difference between a real champion, like me, and a CHUMP ASS TRICK LIKE YOU.
So Jeff... do you get it yet? Do you see what you've gotten yourself into? This title was never meant to be softcore. That doesn't mean I won't do a submission match or some normal type shit every now and then... and it doesn't mean that I won't go all out extreme on a nigga like you, just for kicks....
All it means, is that I'll do what I want. If a certain situation could use a certain stipulation... then I'll make it happen. And if you don't like that..... well, all you gotta do is take this title away. But to do that, you've gotta pry it out of my cold dead hands.... and even then, you'd better have a crowbar or somethin'. Jeff Kane.... this week, you walk in as the first challenge to my reign. And believe me, you won't be the last. I plan on going through anyone that wants a piece of this, or a piece of me.
Unlike SOME PAPER CHAMPIONS, I'll take on all comers, from all nations..... So please.... all of you.... come step up.... so I can knock your asses right back down. I'll show you all how it's done, and why I, Syko, Angel
Martinez am on the minds, and on the lips of every wrestling fan. Yo Drew, tell these cats what it's all about.
Andrew: Authority..... you wanna sit and wonder why noone's taken you out? You issue open challenges to the SEWF locker room? You guys get dumber every god damn week. Believe me, you wait until Sunday. we'll see just how unchallenged you guys are. I think Sunday's gonna be fun.... how about you
Syko?
Syko: It's gonna be a blast.... unless you're Jeff Kane or part of the
Authority. Those bitches don't even realize what they're in for. Now, if you'll do the honors Xian?
Xian: Hai. KYAAAAAAAAAH! [Ouch, sidekick to the camera, and we out.] -- ~~Syko~~ ~Remove a 0 to reply~
