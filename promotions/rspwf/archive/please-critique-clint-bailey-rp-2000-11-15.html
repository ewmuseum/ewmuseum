role-play. However, not efeds. Thanks Clint ___________________________________________________________________ [Tempered]---------------------------------------------------------------- --------------------[Assurance] "...In this game the lesson's in your eyes to see. Though things change, the future's still inside of me. We must remember that tomorrow comes after the dark. So you will always be in my heart, with unconditional love..."
[ The voice over which you just heard comes in clearly. The words once spoken of a man who believed "shit don't stop." Today he rest in peace. The voice over is being repeated numerous times. The scene fades into a first person point of view. Your still, but you've swaying from left to right. Your look over to the right to see water. This explaining the swaying bath and forth, left to right. Out in front of you a small window, and lower a steering wheel.
Further out, a group of seats until they reach the end of the must be boat you are setting in. Beyond the boat you see water upon water. All of it which is very choppy. Further out a small light can be seen. The repeated voice stops.
A lower voice comes in. This man speaks his words slowly. ] "...Straight from the mother fuckin' land of the heartless..." [ Still in first person point of view. You see a hand reach out turn a key, which is in the ignition. The boat gives a crank, the motor turns once ...
twice and than starts up. The hand reaches over further to the right and pushes the stick up. The boat begins to move as you see the hand places on the steering wheel. He does not move the wheel but you head straight forward.
Another voice comes into play. This voice, much quick than the last. This voice much louder than the last.]
"...It's not a game. We move in and move out. Most of you try to follow the route. But they can't see what we see..."
[ A flash of harsh reality flashes before your eyes. As a quick & bright lighting bolt strikes the water. Your startled, but you continue forward. As you come across what just flashed before your eyes, you thank the Lord. You look down only to see a few fish floating atop the water. While steam rises from the water. The only thing on your mind if "forward, forward, forward" as you get closer and closer. The light comes into view better and better. A few minutes pass of nothing mere than traveling on thus water. Eventually you hit land. You look up, to see the light. To see this building towering in front of you. Another voice is heard. This one very familiar to the last one. ]
"... In our way of life, people come, people go, that's the way life is. I don't know what to do, guess I'll just handle it..."
[ You walk a few paces forward, to only see a door in front of your eyes. Your left hand reaches to as & you grab hold of the door knob. A noise of reckoning can be heard as you remove your hand from the door. You turn around and place your back up against the door. Your eyes are wide open, as your ready for all challengers. After a few minutes of looking around, you turn back towards the door. Where once again you place your hand on the door knob. You slowly open the door as it squeaks open. You step into a dark hallway. Your hear a match strike, as you look down after you've stricken the match. You begin to look around. You look down at the water your stepping in, to only think of the lighting which was only one hundred yards from you earlier. You think look at your feet, in which you see rubber souls on your boots. You smile. You begin to walk over to the right. Your match goes out. You strike another one and continue to the right. You see a flight up stairs. You begin to walk up these stairs. Thinking the whole time "this must lead to the light" step after step.
You reach the top to see the light. Another voice comes in. This one a woman's voice. A woman in fear as she screams at the top of her lungs. ]
"...WHY ARE YOU FUCKING WITH ME!..." [ Her voice slowly fades out as the first person view still exist. You walk towards the light. Once you reach the light, your standing side by side of the light. You lean over a small brick wall & look out towards the water. The light shines out, further & further. Your amazed by the length of what is sheds on that water path. You look to the left and see darkness. You close you eyes for a second, you lose control! Your actions are no longer at your fault, as you lift your right elbow up and place it through the glass of the light. The light blows ... you cant see anything. What you was once amazed at now you've felt it's pain. For you feel blood dripping off your elbow hitting the bottom of what once use to be a bright light for men at the water path.
You fall to the ground & black out. Now the scene fades out of a first person point of view, to only fade into a third person point of view. You now see
Clint Bailey, on the ground. A match next to him, which was once used for light to get to the light. Blood is everywhere. From a small cut to his elbow. A voice can be heard. This voice is firm and speaks it's meaning. ]
"...Just what is unconditional love? Let me tell you, come listen to my truest thoughts, my realest feelings You've all wondered from day one. Just what in this life can bring you happiness like nothing ever before. What could shine in on a rainy day. This is why I present unconditional love. I've witness this love far too many times. While other men refuse to see the light.
The only let themselves down. They are the only ones suffering from what they lack. Blame yourself for these actions. Don't false comment on others for your self satisfaction. <pause> Many men learn the hard way. Many just go with the flow. However, if you reach out and touch unconditional love, that is when you might want to call yourself a man. That is when you have done something. Until then, you need to think twice as of where you stand in this business..."
[ Those words repeat, over & over. Each time they start over the voice gets lower & lower until it fades out. Than the scene fades back into, still focussing in on a third person point of view. As Clint Bailey is laying gently on the concrete. No movement can be seen. The light into the room is slim to none. It is more of a dark shadow blazing as Bailey. Suddenly a shake of the head can be seen as Bailey lifts his head up. He looks down to his right elbow and sees the sight of dried blood, while on the ground still blood. Clint takes his left hand and whips the blood on the concrete with his index finger.
He looks closely at it and than whips it on his pants. Bailey than lifts his body up as he places himself up against the wall. Still a dark shadow for a cast. Bailey utters a few words. ]
"...In this life everyone dies. However, in life you must see unconditional love. What am I just speaking about? Unconditional love is brought to you when your born. It's there all through out your life. <pauses>
When you've found the past, at what has happened each night you've stepped into the squared circle. Win or lose, you continue. To die a man is a must for us all. However, to die with pride in my eyes, you must reach out & grab unconditional love once in your life..."
[ The third person point of view flashes back to first person for a total of five-second. You see Bailey focussing on his hands. Which are shaking. The camera switches back to third person and Bailey's head is draped down.
Bailey's figure comes more into play, for the dark shadow of a cast has become just a little enlightened. Bailey begins on again. ]
"...On Saturday <sigh> Shafter you will fall, knocked straight from the horse. But will it leave you weakened? Will it leave you with the strength to get up spread your wings and head out for better things? Ryan before Saturday, will you feel unconditional love? If so, there could be a good outlook in this match for you. I challenge you Ryan to reach out & grab unconditional love for
Saturday's match. Push me out of the way. Give me that challenge. Assure a win in this bout. Can you do this Ryan? Or have you already fallen off your horse? Was your win for the NMW Streetlife title, just a fluke? Or can you actually handle your own? You once said you cant perform an actual move in the ring, but you can bash someone's head in with a chair. Well, Ryan, many men can do that. There is nothing to be proud of with it either. <pauses> so on an ending note. Shafter, what will you do when you catch no practice? When what your dealing with it tight like nuts and bolts? So will you be bringing your chair on Saturday?..."
[ The camera stays on third person, as Bailey gets up. He looks out to the water way once more and begins his journey back. For he has down what is needed. He begins down the stairs. Out the door and into the boat. The motor turns once, twice and starts up again. Smoke rising from the motor. Bailey looks into the camera and lips "Fuck Shafter" as the camera fades out. ]
