[Free Agent] Christopher "Spot" Light

12 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, I won't lie, and say I'm the greatest thing ever...I am good, but I can be beat, but I will definately try, no matter who the opponent is. I have been in several good feds as other characters, but I have decided to make Spot be my main character right now, with the exception of Michael " 5000 " Watts in another promotion. But I would love to join any fed right now, I think I could be a great asset, anyway, here is some background information:
Requirements: - Must Be Open For Atleast 3-4 Months - Must Have A CW Division - Not A Board Fed - Loyal, And RPing Handlers - Minimal Junkmail - Flash Packets Are Okay, But I Am Not Too Used To Them. Name: Justin Age: 16 Years RPing: 3-4 Wrestler Name: Christopher "Spot" Light Age: 24 Hometown: Simi Valley, CA Heel/Face/Neutral: Usually Neutral, Till The Fed Gets Going And I Align Myself
With Somebody.
Theme Music: "Edgecrusher" by Fear Factory Style: Highflying, occasional Brawler. Strengths: Speed- Christopher is a VERY fast, agile man, he ran track in H.S. and College, he has very strong legs.
Agility- A former gymnast as well, Christopher is very good at jumping around and dazzling fans, and opponents with his offensive flurrys.
Intelligence- He is a very smart man, he graduated from the University Of
Florida, getting his degree in psychology.
Weaknesses: Strength- He is not a very strong man, upper body wise, lower body his legs are very useful for high flying maneuvers, but he is not very good at matching upper body strength with anybody.
Ring Awareness- Being a rookie, he has minimal ring experience. Over-estimation - He sometimes over-estimates his own abilities and messes up, which incites a furious temper that can sometimes get him DQ'd.
History: He grew up in Simi Valley, in a very rich family, all through out H.S. he ran track and occasinally he would practice gymnastics. He and his brother Jacen, had always shared a love for wrestling, and his brother went on to fulfill that dream, while Christopher went away to the University of Florida,
Christopher graduated, attaining his degree in psychology, and saw the money, and fame that his brother was receiving and immediately he wanted some to. The two formed a tag team, in one promotion, but shortly afterwards his brothers greed led to their seperation. Now Christopher, still a rookie, having never wrestled a SINGLES match, is looking for a new promotion to call home.
Appearance: He is a very athletic man, with a nice body, his 6Pac and short spiked black hair atract alot of attention, from ladies that is. He has one tattoo, that of the Florida Gators logo, on his right arm, his eyes are a baby blue, usually enhanced with the aide of contacts. He has a scar above his upper right eye, from being hit with a bottle in a bar fight in college. But other than that, he is a very attractive man.
