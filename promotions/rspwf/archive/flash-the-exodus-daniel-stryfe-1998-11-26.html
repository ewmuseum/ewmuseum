[FLASH] "The Exodus" Daniel Stryfe

26 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a new character and the first time I RPed with him. It kinda sux, but isn't a horrible RP. Tell me what ya' think. All except FireofFury, cause he blows.
The Story... [xXx] ... Death. A common every day happening. In which hundreds and thousands of people are hurt by. Some more than other, but in the end we are all saddened by such a terrible happening. For one man, his whole life was filled with death. Every memory, every dream, taken away, or filled with darkness as a form of death. A poor, lonely boy, who was abandoned not by choice, not with love, but by darkness. For the poor boy named Daniel, death was his life. Mother, father, grandparents, in many different ways his entire family had been taken away. Left with nothing at the age of fourteen. He was left on his won for many years. And for many years he had made nothing. He finally got a chance. One day someone picked him up, and gave him a chance.
And since that great day, after losing all his family and all his dreams, there was a slight chance of hope for this young boy. For now, he had something to look forward to. Now, he once again had a dream. With the little boy behind him, the man went on to become many things, but as of late has again been left with nothing. And with a strange turn of fate, the man now, again has a hope, and again has a dream ... [xXx]
Scene: [...The camera slowly begins to fad into a dark, quiet night. A slight breeze blowing gently through the tall trees, and out through the open planes. The stars as well as the moon shine brightly down upon the open forest, hitting the ground like a feather. There is a slight glare coming from a rock, that lay in the middle of a small river. The leaves of the tall trees, fall off slowly falling to the ground, some hitting the lake softly, and lay on the top. You see some ripples in the water as some small fish try to bite the leaves, and then leave after realizing it is not food. A long black snake slithers through the water and up onto the shore, quickly hiding beneath a large rock pile. Off in the distance you hear a wolf howl, and then a hoot of a nearby owl. Then the forest become silent. So low you can hear as the feet of small animals run along the ground. And out of the darkness of the woods, comes a deep, loud voice...]
Voice: "It has been many months for me. And already I feel a tad bit rusty. But it seems like after all the hard years of work, that they may finally pay off.
After all the blood, sweat and tears, I may finally get something out of this horrible, death filled life. I again have a dream, again, a goal. So many years it has been, I can't remember the last time I had something to look forward to. But now, someone has given me a chance. They saw me, and thought that I was worth something. So they picked me up, and brought me home, so to speak. So they throw a series of tasks to at me, and I must say, they will not be easy. But if I where ever to get back on my feet, I would have to start here, and it would have to be now."
[...Someone walks out from behind a large tree, and he stares dead into the camera. As if starring right through you. It is a tall, built man. With arms and legs like tree trunks. He is in all black, which blends in with the darkness of the woods. Nothing on the man shows any bit of life at all, besides a slight sparkle which comes from his left ear, which upon it has a small gold earring. A skull... As the camera focuses in you see it is UCHW superstar, "The Exodus" Daniel Stryfe. In the same voice, with the same tone, he again begins to speak...]
"The Exodus" Daniel Stryfe: "You are correct, I have come out of retirement, and have already been given a goal again. And yes, it has been a long time, but I think I can handle it well enough. Now, it did not take long, and already I am in a title match. Not a regular match, a nine man battle royal match. And as you know, with my past I am not one to do well in those. But then again times have changed. I am the best to know. So I go into it like any other match, this time not knowing anyone I am here to face. We have some men, thinking they are quote, dark.
But no men really understand the meaning of dark. I have been there, and lived it. I understand the power in which comes along with that title. I am dark.
It is who I was made to be. And as all of you know, that is what I am. I play no game, and I speak no lies. I do as I say, and say as I act. Now, there are some men who play games with it. Men such as Jonny 'Gothic' Anderson. He talks about how someone's name is spelt. And I can look at that, with only disgust. With the title of 'Gothic' I would expect much more than a bad ass character. But I can see he was given his gimmick. Unlike myself, who is exactly who I am. For no one tells me how to act. Because I don't act like someone I'm not. But anyway, there are other men in this here battle royal.
One is Kandyman. And the first thing this man did, was start No Limit Soldiers. And as some of you know, I have a very bad past with them. He goes on to say how I am biblical. Because of the Exodus. But I have to ask you, do you have any idea what Exodus means, Kandyman? Any idea at all? I have a strange feeling you do not. But fear not, I shall show you what it means. I will show you all what it means. But before I go any farther, there is one more person I would like to talk a little about, and that is the 'intoxicated
Mexican' Salvo. This man would seem like the one who would know very little, but it turns out that this man is the only one with a head on his shoulders.
He knows where he stands, and what he is capable of. And I see I better expect much to come from him, and to watch out for him."
[...Stryfe pauses for a moment, the begins to speak again...] "Now, you see how calm I may seem. But deep down inside I am dying and ready to tear havoc upon anything that I come in contact with. But this is what drives me. That is the energy that I must save for when the time is right.
Much like this small river here. Doesn't appear to be much, but a few small fish swimming around. Strange, I see far beyond that."
[...Stryfe bends down a picks up a small stick, then walks over to the waters edge. He turns his head back to the camera...]
"You have to think about all the possibilities, and test each plan out before finally coming to a conclusion, for some plans just may be..."
[...As Stryfe says that he throws the stick in the water and quickly backs up. Almost suddenly there is a big splash as the water goes flying, and as it clears a large alligator lie spread out, with its mouth open on the shore. It closes its mouth and looks around before finally going back into the water...]
"A mistake... Be prepared all, the Exodus has now begun!" [...Daniel Stryfe turns his back and begins walking down a small path in the woods, while the camera slowly fades to black, finally cutting to commercial...]
