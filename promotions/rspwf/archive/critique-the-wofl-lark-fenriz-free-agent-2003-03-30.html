[CRITIQUE] "The Wofl Lark" Fenriz (FREE AGENT)

30 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CAUSING CHANGES IN MAN Wow, maybe I am another Moses... PART TWO OF TWO - "Humbling the Fool" [One would expect Lark Fenriz, the KAWF Global Champion, to have quite a creepy kitchen. Maybe black curtains covering the window, boards lay across the panes themselves, just furthering promising the kitchen area to the darkness. Maybe the marble countertops would be black as coal, and the stove burners a bright, red. Perhaps the refrigerator's freezer would be extra large, just to hold all the raw meat Lark enjoys devouring. And, if there's an island in the middle of Hell's Kitchen on Earth, it might contain cookbooks describing out of this world food, food that few people would dare to even try.]
[That image wouldn't be too far off from Lark's actual kitchen.] [There are no boards over the windows, but there are black curtains, though they aren't drawn. Why would they? It's in the middle of the night. Candles burn vigorously on the island, and on the countertops.
Everything is lit up by the dancing flames. Somewhere off the distance a wall of noise is being played, most likely one of Lark's favorite death metal bands.]
[He just can't seem to live without the stuff.] [Lark's standing over the sink, rubbing something down with a towel. In the corner of the kitchen appears to be the handle of a mop, the rest covered up by the island. Lark, shirtless and dressed on in black silk boxers, is quite focused at the task he's performing, paying no attention to the camera behind him.]
["So clean now... So much better..."] [He drops object into the sink. It clutters like it was made of metal, and Lark turns around. His first glance is at the floor, and then finally, with a smile, at the camera.]
Lark Fenriz: As times drift slowly away, a man goes through countless revelations. His attitude and beliefs change. What he cares about most differs from the day before. It is these revelations that are the difference between a good man, and a great one. Why?
Because the great man changes for the better, while the good one... <He looks down at the floor, a devious smile clinging to his pale skin.> He fails to really change at all.
[Lark turns around, once again his back is pointed towards us. His focus is on the floor, which is red and black tiled.]
Lark Fenriz: Eric Cooper, you have undergone one of these such revelations. You have realized the error of your ways, and shed the skin of the material world.
You've grown bored with what is easily attainable, and have chosen a higher goal to aim for--respect!
Sadly, you might already have that from of those silly boys in the back. Unknowing are they that you are nothing to respect. Oh well, boys will be boys... <He turns his attention to the camera once again, a quick smile, and then back to the floor, still staring at something.> Nonetheless, Eric Cooper, you fail to gain my respect. Not because you're wrestling skills are sub-par. Not because you're voice annoys me. Not because of anything you've really done, but rather haven't done.
[And with that, he grabs ahold of the mop, and turns around, leaning back against the counter. There's a devilish gleam in his eye, something is going on inside the madman's head.] ["This place looks good as new... Just a bit more mopping, and the bleach will ruin it all."]
Lark Fenriz: You paint me out to be some horrible creation, an abomination to the wrestling world. All
I ask, Eric Cooper, is why? Why am I so horrible? I have done nothing wrong thus far in my stay here in the KAWF! I have won match after match. I have defeated opponent after opponent. I've spoken the brutal truth so many men wish they could harness. <A smile that rings of pride covers his face, as his hands slide to the mop handle, pulling it towards him.> If anything, I'm one of the few good things KAWF has to offer.
[He pushes the mop across the floor, slowly and carefully, as if this deed is life threatening.]
["Back and forth, nice and calm. Can't miss a spot..."] Lark Fenriz: I could see how things in your mind could be so greatly distorted. You've been so self-absorbed thus far in your stay, how could you have possibly noticed my amazing achievements?
[And with that, we fade away to darkness.]
