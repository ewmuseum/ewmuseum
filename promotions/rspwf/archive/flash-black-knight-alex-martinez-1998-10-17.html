Flash - "Black Knight" Alex Martinez

17 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(fade into a clip. A clip of Alex Martinez being carted away from ringside. A clip of Magnus Colby laying in the middle of the ring on top of a pile of bricks. Colby is being looked at by numerous members of the UEW's medical staff.)
(The scene changes. We are now in a living room area. Magnus Colby... all 6'6 and 300 pounds of him is seated on the couch. He is dressed in black pants, and a white collared shirt is visible underneath a black sweater. His brown hair is cut short and well kept.)
Alex Martinez... the Black Knight. Well, to me there is only one 'black night.' It is that night. The night that I was carted away from ringside. I have fought in dozens and dozens of matches in my life. One time... just one time I have been carted away from ringside.
(Magnus seems lost in thought for a moment. For just a moment it is as if
Colby is reliving the pain of that match.)
Alex, so many things happened in that match. I took countless power bombs and slams... hell, I even took two Fire Bombs... the second on a pile of bricks.
Well... I won't get into the details of the match. You were there... all of our fans saw it. I will not rehash that black night...
(Magnus seems lost in thought again, but a smile begins to form across his face.) Martinez, you talk of a path of broken bodies filled with pain and fear, is left wherever you go. Alex Martinez... I have been there. I have done that.
(Magnus lets out a short laugh.) Martinez, I have taken your best shot and I am still here to tell about it. Sure, I spent a month in a wheel chair afterwards... sure, I have been through two months of rehab to get back into shape to get in the ring again.
(The look on Colby's face changes. He suddenly grows serious.) Martinez, I will trade the pain and the suffering that I experienced that night... that black night. Because in the wake of the fateful evening, I have now grown. I once thought myself invinceable. And now I know. I can take a pounding even from the Master of Destruction himself... and live to tell about it.
Alex Martinez, don't get me wrong... you are the most evil... the most dangerous man I have ever stepped into the ring with. I lived to tell about it though... and that brings me peace of mind that all the money in the world could never bring me.
(The camera starts to fade out.) Oh and one more thing. Alex... one of these days... one of these days our paths will cross once more. On that day, I will repay the damage that you did to me... with interest. You can take that to the bank!!!
(Fade to black.) [name withheld] handler of Magnus Colby Former UEW American champion Former IWC World champion Former IWF/WOW Tag champion Former IWC North Eastern champion Former? DWO Eastern champion "I never held a title I couldn't job"
