USCW Flash Central 5/12/98 Pt. 1

13 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Viper Joe Mills (2) Jacob Yoder The Delinquent VooDoo Spectre The Marauders -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- (Scene opens to the outside of the USCW arena where crowds of people are going in and out... A bannaer strung across the entrance proclaims today
"USCW FREE Wrestle-a-Thon, only $25 per person!!!" The camera walks closer to the arena, and finally makes it through the doors to the inside...
Inside we see vendors selling shirts, the camera stops for a while to watch the merchandise sales... The BIG sellers as we watch are the "GDDC---Itz not just a fan club, itz a way of LIFE" T-Shirt, and the "What the Fuck is
Spicey gonna do now" hats... The camera watches for 5 or 10 more miutes, then heads in to the arena itself, where we see the wrestling ring set up, and what looks like Willy Vanilly in there wrestling some teenager off the street... Amazingly, Vanilly's losing!!! The camera gets closer, and as it does, we see someone come running down to the ring, pick Vanilly up, and powerbomb him over the top rope.. The camera pans in on the man who is revealed as the USCW World HeavyWeight Champion, Viper... Viper grins at the crowd and grabs a mic)
Viper: Alrighty then... So, who wants to be first on the Snake express to sleepyland??? C'mon, ya'll were SCREAMING to get in the ring with broke boy over there... Doesn't anyone wanna do a little "fun" wrestling with me??? C'mon!!! Don't be shy!!!
(The camera pans around, and we see EMT's and security moving closer to the ring slowly, none look like they wanna try to STOP Viper from what he's doing yet, they just wanna protect the crowd...)
Viper: Ahhhhh C'mon fellas, yer not scared are ya?? Hey YOU!! Yea YOU!!! (Viper points to a HUGE man with a bald head and tattoos all over himself) Viper: Do YOU wanna step in this ring and try me on?? C'mon, I KNOW a man yer size can't be a SUPER pussy.. Well I guess ya could, hell, look at ya, ya ain't moved yet.. Pussy boy
(At that the man gets up and charges the ring, he jumps in the ring and rushes Viper... Viper moves out of the way and sticks his leg out, the man trips and falls flat on his face, flattening his nose... A blood starts to run out of his nose, the man gets back up and stalks slowly towards
Viper... Viper just grins and looks at him, Viper and the man lock up, and the man picks Viper up and bodyslams him... Viper jumps to his feet and laughs as he kicks the man in the balls, hooks him up, and hits him with
The SnakePit... As the man is laying senseless, Viper starts going crazy and kicking and punching the man... We see the man's nose explode with blood, and his ears start to bleed... As Viper is working over the man's arm we hear a loud CRACK!!! As his elbow twists backwards and his arm snaps!! Finally Viper picks the man up and gorilla presses him to the outside of the ring, where the man hits the steel rail and bounces off to the concrete)
Viper: Heh, that was invigorating... Anyone else wanna come wrestle The
Snake?? C'mon fellas... I'll be nice, I PROMISE!!!
(As Viper says that the man outside the ring gets up with the EMT's help... The man grabs a mic) Man: DAMMIT you son of a bitch!!! I'm gonna call my lawyers RIGHT NOW!!! Rob Spicer and his USCW are in DEEP shit! He's gonna get slapped with a lawsuit like he wouldn't BELIEVE!!! And he can thank YOU when he does!!!
Viper: I'll make sure he knows that numbnuts, now get the fuck outta here before ya get hurt some more!! Oh, and please have a nice day, and come to
OUR next free event(The Wet T-shirt contest) in a few days!!!
(Viper grins at the man as he limps away, then turns back to the crowd with a smile) Viper: Now C'mon fellas, I was only mean to him cause of what he said to me in the ring... You gus didn't hear him call me a fuckin pussy bitch??
Well if ya didn't, thatz what he called me, and thatz why I did that... S
C'mon... I wanna help Spicey raise all his money, and I want you fans to
LOVE the USCW!!
(Finally a younger kid, about 8 steps in to the ring and Viper just grins as he keeps holding the mic while he's wrestling him)
Viper: So kid, think you can beat the USCW HeavyWeight champ??? Kid: Yea, I'm SUPER-ULTIMATE-HULK-GOLDBERG!!!! Viper: Ummm, what ya little prick??? Kid: HEY, I'll beat you cause I ALWAYS win when I wrestle, I beat my dad,
I beat my uncle!!! Cause I'm the greatest wrestler ever!!!
Viper: Wel, yer dad, and yer uncle must be little pussy bitches then, cause yer just a little fuckin kid who couldn't beat HIMSELF yet!!!
Kid: You can't say that about my DAD!!!! (The kid rushes Viper who puts a hand on the kids forehead and looks around grinning... In the crowd we see two men stand up and walk towards the ring... AS they reach the ring the kid sees them)
Kid: DADDY, UNCLE CHRIS!!!! This mean man is picking on me!!! Dad: Yea we know son, we'll take care of it... Uncle Chris: Yer going down Snake Boy (Viper starts to laugh as the two men get in the ring... Viper picks the kid up and throws him at the dad, both the father and the kid flip over the ropes and land on the concrete floor... The fathers head busts in to the concrete, as his son's head smashes in to his nose... In the ring Uncle
Chris charges Viper who laughs as Uncle Chris smashes in to him and falls over... Viper then picks Uncle Chris up, turns him over, and tombstone piledrives him in to the mat... Uncle Chris is unconscious as Viper picks him up and tombstone's him again and again, until blood runs out of his ears, nose, and mouth... Viper then throws him out on to the father and son outside the ring)
Viper: Sheesh, overprotective family members suck, so what do ya think fellas? Dontcha think the USCW free events are FUN!!!!
(The crowd is muttering and leaving as Viper says this, pretty soon the arena is empty except for Uncle Chris, the kid, and the father who are just now getting up)
Dad: The USCW is gonna pay for this Viper! Rob Spicer will be hearing from my lawyers!!! Uncle Chris: And mine too!!! I KNOW Rob Spicer, and he won't let you get away with this!!! But he'll STILL be hearing from my lawyers!! There will be millions to pay!!! And we'll make SURE he knows it's YOUR fault!!!
Viper: Please do, I'd like it if ya did that, now get the fuck outta here lameoids, before I get MAD!!!
(The man, son, and Uncle Chris leave as Viper turns towards the camera) Viper: Well, Shining Path, yer next boyo... You say yer gonna take THIS title from me?? Please, it'd take yer WHOLE Moral Majority to even come
CLOSE!! So quit all yer posturing and accept the fact that you ARE gonna get hurt, and hurt badly tomorrow night!! You say yer NOT a one hit wonder?? Funny, ya lost the foil TV title over a WEEK ago, and just NOW have ya come outta yer little sulking fit and said sumthin about it... And maybe yer NOT a one-hit wonder, but yer SECOND hit, WON'T be at my expense boyo...
Viper: So keep on hopin and dreamin, hell itz good for ya, but DON'T expect ANY of that to help you, yer MY bitch tomorrow night SP... And yer
NOT gonna win our little match!!!
Viper: Now, Yoder, be as mad as ya feel ya need to... I gives a fuck, like I said, YOU were just in the wrong place at the wrong time... If ya wanna take it personally, go ahead... Me and Sin will be MORE than happy to whip the everlovin SHIT outta you and Danny Dare... Oh what FUN that'll be... So Yoder, I have YET to hear you accept My challnge to YOU for the
World title... Are ya THAT scared to get in the ring with me again Amish
Boy???
Viper: Well, I guess I'll get the fuck outta here fer now... I think I've had enuff fun fer one day!!
'Nuff Said -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- (Sitting in a dark room is Joseph Mills. The only light that is shining is a
"god light" from overhead.)
Joseph Mills: Once again, children search for fame and fortune. Entering a profession in which they are truly not ready for. Pain, torture, and agony are all the byproducts of this existence. Over the past few day there has been silence from the camp of The Commonwealth of Pain. I'll explain to you why. There is no longer any such thing as the Commonwealth of Pain.
Maxwell Chamberlain came to the realization that he is a washed up old fool who can no longer "hang" with the younger current talent that makes the
USCW. He has gone back to England to hide his god awful face from the light of society. Tommy Lowe. Tommy Lowe got fired when he quit making choices that where better for the team and started making choices that filled his Swiss bank account! So I sent him to be with his money and I sent one of his arms to the St. Zappa's Museum as a visible witness of what happens when I get screwed! Me and D.C. are still tight but this is my thing now! I have been on a journey. A journey to strengthen my soul, my body and my mind.
I walked across the valley of darkness never fearing the evil that lives apart of it brushing it aside, destroying it when I needed to, proving that I had control over the course of my own events. And as I walked throughout that land of silence and
truth, my truth was revealed to me. I was a man standing with the WOW North
American Singles Title. I was myself on two different times standing with the
WOW Worlds Championship. Then my life moved to the BWFE and I saw myself stand as the United States Champion. Then Tommy Lowe came along and told me to try out tag team wrestling! I did and did a damn good job holding the world and the intercontinental titles. Then my life became Total Destruction and the singals career that I stood as master was also totally destroyed. As I walked in this land I also
was a man that cared not for the well being of others around him. Taking all of the chances, the extreme chances that need to be taken. Table, chairs, barbed wire,
bats, and branding irons. Any and everything he could get his hands on. And I saw my happiness. The joy that I once held in this profession. Now Joseph Mills makes his walk back down the road that he owned when he start in this sport. Now Joe
Mills starts collecting all of his toys of mass destruction and is ready to show everyone that extreme is in the heart and is not something that is forgotten or suppressed. I have been suppressed in the world of tag team wrestling way too damn long. The singals division must once again feel the fear that it felt when Joseph Mills stepped through
the door for the first time. Prepare yourselves for the extreme realization of your worst nightmares. Joseph Mills has spoken.

Time does not have the same substance as it once did does it? The matter that pulls your existence into one whole being is slowly separating it self as if to tell to you run and hide.
Darkness.... The unending material that exists.... Exists in the souls of every living man.... Some of us ignore
it.... try to push it aside and shun it.... all for not.... Then there are those who except
it and allow it to take and active part in their worthless little lives.... Those are the few that are truly in control... control of their actions and their thoughts... Maxwell Chamberlain denied his true being to the point that it consumed him... and now he is
at home... to hide for ever more.... you each shall experience the same fates.... the fates that can destroy the unsuspecting... unknowing... washed up waist of nerves
that infest this great federation... I shall be your realization... the darkness that makes it self know.... visible to exterminate ... to be the strong that removes the weak.... the hunter that captures it's pray... and extreme dignitary braking down the
wall ... and coming closer and closer to braking you as well.... the world holds a new out look... when the messenger of pain is prepared to deliver... the message that proclaims your own destruction.. the letter that you all refuse to believe... the reality that will be reviled... The damnation that will not be stopped... welcome to the extremes of reality.... the extreme end of each of you all.... Joseph Mills has spoken.
