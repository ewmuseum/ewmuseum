[FLASH] "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian

4 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello everyone, Someone told me they wanted to see a Sebastian flash, which was seconded by another, so I just decided to post one I recently did. Anyhow, here it is.
Flame at will. -- [The camera opens to the horizon. It is dusk, as the sunset gradually falls over the horizon, which seperates ocean from sky. A blend of orange and yellow is seen across the cloudless sky as the shot ascends, revealing the crashing waves at beach's shore. Jellyfish are seen camped out upon the beach's white sand, as well as various seashells and random debris. With a full-scale shot of the oceanfront, various people are seen; some of which whom are still in their swimming trunks or bikinis, others whom are dressed in regular street clothing. One of these men being WWO superstar, Mike Sebastian.]
[Rather than his customary Armani suit or Polo outfit, Sebastian is dressed in a pair of rugged Calvin Klein blue jeans, rolled up at the ankles and a tight purple shirt, which reveals his strong arms and muscular torso. Mike's hair appears uncombed, or at least it was and the light breeze has mussed it up a bit. With a trail of footprints left behind him, Sebastian saunters forward with the waves crashing across his bare feet. As the camera moves closer, Sebastian continues with his head bowed, not acknowledging the camera's presence.]
MS: Yeah, I know. It's not what you expected. I know I'm supposed to be out here ranting and raving, throwing a
'lil hissy fit just to make all you fans at home crack a smile because of all that happened back at War of the Ages... but I'm not going to give you the satisfaction. Not the fans, not Stone, not Hudson, and certainly not that worthless scrub, Tyrone Hayes.
[Sebastian sidesteps a jellyfish, keeping his eyes focused upon the sand, before finally looking up.]
MS: Tyrone, Tracy... I'm glad I could be your guinea pig for a night so you fellas could go and form your 'lil group. Carnage-a-Trois, or whatever is on top of the world and it's all because of me being on the receiving end of a Prodigy Driver. And hey, I'm not gonna lie to you. It hurt... it hurt like hell. But I'm still here,
guys -- walking, talking, and waiting... [Mike stops, placing his hands in his pockets.] MS: ... for this Thursday night. [Sebastian continues his brisk pace, kicking at a seashell, or crab, or something.] MS: It may come as a surprise to you, Tyrone, but revenge is a two-way street. I can come at you saying "Payback's a bitch" all I damn well please, but that's not gonna get me any further until I actually _do_ something. You, on the other hand, you get to just sit there, look pretty, and do your best to stop me. And you can ask your friend Tracy there -- that is no easy task.
If you look back at War of the Ages, it sure as hell looks like one, I know. But take away the mask... take away the entourage...
take away all the distractions, Tyrone... and what have you got left? I'll tell you what you've got, big man.
You've got the two of us meeting one-on-one in the next round of this Road to the Title thingamajig. But this isn't Atlanta, Ty.
I'm not going to come down, hand you the victory, and then get my ass handed to me like last time. Eh-eh.
Now it's me and you, Hayes. Man-to-man, mano y mano. No second-rate excuses for tag team partners. No more weight to be carried on my back. No more Alex Perkins' or David Holmes'.
Just me... and you. Winner takes all. [Sebastian stops once more, allowing the tide to roll in. The bottoms of his jeans are hit from the splashing, but Mike does not seem to have any concern for this. His platinum blonde hair blows in the wind, as he crosses his arms, staring back into the camera.]
MS: And hey, if this is the beginning of Carnage-a-Trois versus Mike Sebastian, then you know... that's fine. I don't mind playing the David versus Goliath game just a bit. Three-on-one? Sounds fair to me. Just know going in who the hell you're dealing with here,
Tyrone. You aren't the only man to have defeaten Roundhouse, you know. [He cocks his head to the side and jets a thumb towards his chest.] MS: As long as your next of kin knows how to say "Mike Sebastian" when addressing your cause of death, then by all means... [Mike immediately grabs the neck of his purple shirt and rips it straight down the front, revealing his chiseled, bronzed physique.]
MS: Let the games begin. [He pauses once more, walking off into the distance. The sides of his shirt blow in the wind now also, along with his hair. The camera fades on one final thing -- footsteps.]
