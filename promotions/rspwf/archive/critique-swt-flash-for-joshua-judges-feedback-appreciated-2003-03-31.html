[Critique] - [ SWT flash for Joshua Judges ] - [Feedback appreciated]

31 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Not one of my better one's with Joshua, but I can't find hardly any of his . .. Bah Oh well Voice : " Thank you once again my Lord." [ The camera pans in, and as we see Joshua Judges on his knee's, holding his hands high into the air praying with his eyes closed tightly.]
Joshua Judges : " I love you my lord, I love that you had me do your will, I love that you let me get out of my match without injury. I love that your giving me a chance to fight someone which has a chance to actually love you.
A man who shows glimpses of being a true follower of your words." [ The camera pans back a touch, and we see the surroundings of Joshua Judges. He is on his knee's on the peak of a mountain somewhere on the outskits of
Verdun, France.]
Joshua Judges : " I hope that I can guide him towards your light. I hope that you give me a sign to help me send him in the direction of your glory. I want to be the best prophet I can be for you."
* Zooommmmmmmm * [ Joshua's eyes open widely as rain begins to fall, with some lightning in the area as well. Oddly enough though the rain falls on the outside of the mountain and not actually on it. Joshua looks on slowly lowering his hands to his side.]
Joshua Judges : " Is this a sign my Lord ? Are you telling me my prayers are not only being listened to, but answered ? "
Voice : " What do you think ? " Joshua Judges : " Yes my Lord . . . I really bele. . " [ Joshua gets cut off as a man comes from nowhere and places his hands on
Joshua's shoulder.]
Man : " I live at a near by cabin, The top of a mountain isn't the safest place to be when lightning is occuring my friend, and you shouldn't be drinking up here."
[ Joshua stands up tall, and faces the older man as a look of sadness comes across his face, realizing once again he is not getting a sign from God, but being spoken to by a local Verdun resident.]
Joshua Judges : " I do not drink, anything but the word of God. His love is the only thing I get drunk off of."
Man : " Sureeeeeee buddy . . . I'm gonna have to ask you to leave the mountain top now if you would. It's supposed to get dangerous up here tonight."
Joshua Judges : " God will protect me." Man : " I am sure he would, but I am asking you to leave the mountain. I tend to watch the radar and warn people of the enviroment. I almost consider it my duty since I live up here."
Joshua Judges : << Joshua looks up to the sky, then back down to the man >> I will do so ,but only cause I feel the Lord wants me to pick my fights, and this is not one of em."
Man : " Sure buddy, You don't got to go home, but you can't stay here." [ Joshua begins to walk away from the man towards the path which leads to the bottom of the mountain.]
Joshua Judges : " That may not be a fight in which he wants me to pick, but the next Battleground is one in which he wants me to. You can tell even by the NAME. Battleground just shoots off aura which means _FIGHT_
Which is what I shall do. After all the words I have said, After all the praying I have done. Finally I get a chance for my words to be heard on a grand scale. Finally I get to teach the words One on One to a soul in which will listen.
"The Truth" Joshua Judges competing against "Midas Touch" Johnny Glamour has all the right tools of being something _GREAT_ in the eyes of the fans of the
S.W.T and most importantly in the eyes of God. This fight could be the closest thing to heaven on earth if done right."
[ Joshua reaches the bottom of the path and looks back up at the mountain. Rain gently falls on top of his head now.] Joshua Judges : " There is know mountain I can't climb, nor any mountain I can't move with the Lord on my side.
Glamour is simply another mountain which must be climbed or moved. That S.W.T is the Truth.
That Mr Johnny Glamor is the Truth." [ Joshua raises his head towards the sky as the rain drops onto his face.] Joshua Judges : " Give me the strength my Lord. Give me the power once again. Give me the heart. Give me the strength to shed my blood for you and this purpose in which you have me competing in. Just continue to do your will threw me week after week. Use me as your vocal cords, and as you canvas to paint a beautiful picture.."
[ Joshua lowers his head, and looks to the camera as a bit of blood falls from the bottom corner of his eyes, as we assume it's from praying hard as
Jesus and many other bibical figures did in the Bible.]
Joshua Judges : " Amen, my Lord." [ Fade Out ]
