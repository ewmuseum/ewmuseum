[CRITIQUE] - Steinman RP

30 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Okay, guys, I've never done this before, so please, just go easy on me. At the moment, I'm running an angle whereby I am something of a 'mystery man' (hence no name references) and I am 'stalking' another character in my league - Southern Championship Wrestling. My character is, admittedly, somewhat unoriginal - very much a Gen-X, gothic-style wrestler in the mould of Edge, or any member of the Brood. He's not very talkative, so I tend to let NWC's do all the talking in the flashes, with short, cryptic lines. Anyway - hit me with your critique'in...
P.S. - any of you SCW'ers who might be reading this, please don't give away my identity. It would really screw up an angle I've been working hard on for the past two weeks, so just be nice, okay? I'd do the same for you. Honestly.
======================================================================== << The scene opens outside a ramshackle old building; cracks gradually split apart the brickwork of the walls, the grimy windows crazed and splintered, or blocked by rotting wooden boards. The sign which has been hung above the entrance bears the scorch-marks of a fiery encounter, but displays the words 'Steele's Gym' in blistered, sun-paled red paint. In the west, above the concrete horizon, the embers of the evening sky glow pink and orange as the fingers of night begin to strangle the life from day. The dilapidated gym, which could be found in any back-street in any city in the United States of America, lies dormant, silent, with only the distant sound of rumbling traffic to be heard. >>
<< Slowly, the front door creaks open and the gym's proprietor, Luke
Steele, steps out into the pleasantly mild air of dusk. At almost forty, the man appears to be excellently conditioned, with muscles bulging from the clinging, crisp white tee-shirt which adorns his upper body, and a black Nike hold-all slung over his powerful shoulders. Reaching into the pocket of his sweat pants, he pulls out a set of keys, and secures his gym for the night with a rusty, yet faithful iron padlock. >>
<< With his back to the street, it is the sound of footsteps on the concrete sidewalk that draws his attention. Knowing the nature of the neighbourhood, he whirls around quickly, poised, ready to defend himself from any wanton attacker. However, the tall, slim man that stands before him seemingly poses no threat, simply stood, the epitome of calmness.
His attire is somewhat unusual, almost as that of a priest - a large, midnight-black vestment worn loosely around an athletic, hardened torso, bearing some sort of insignia, a '†' symbol, upon the chest, looking as if it were painted in blood. Locks of raven hair are streaked down his shadowy, stubble-greyed features - features which do not betray him to emotion. His eyes are cast in a cold, cerulean blue, which probe those of Steele. After a split-second for examination, the gym owner breaks the awkward silence... >>
[STEELE] : "Phew! You gave me a bit of a fright, there, man. You, err... you want something? You'll have to forgive me, I live out in the 'burbs, so I don't really know my way around the city as well as I should..."
<< A pause. The man replies eventually, his voice slow, quiet, but powerful nonetheless. His monotone bears the unmistakable accent of a
European upbringing, with his focus, his gaze, not shifting from the face of Steele. >>
[INTRUDER] : "Where is the ‘Panther’..?" << The newcomer’s breathing is shallow, barely detectable beneath his billowing drape of darkness. There is something unnerving about his manner, even in silence - something in the dark demeanour in those icy blue eyes which commands respect, even fear. Steele seems puzzled by the request, mulling it over, but unable to break through the enigmatic words… >>
[STEELE] : "The ‘Panther’? Well, I’m sorry, buddy, but you’ll have to be a bit more speci…"
<< Realisation, remembrance. >> [STEELE] : ".. oh, yes. I get it now. You want Jared Mathys, right? That’s who you’re looking for - the wrestler with SCW? Well, he was here earlier, but he’s not a regular. He was just looking for a place to shoot his interview. Is it something important?"
[INTRUDER] : "There are wrongs which must be righted. He must be made to realise that with actions… come consequences…"
<< Steele seems slightly puzzled - the words of the man are anything but colloquial. A black choker necklace has been knotted tightly around his throat - so tight that it moves when he speaks, when he breathes. A tiny, silver cross hangs from it. The gym’s owner takes a few moments to consider what was said, before picking up the conversation. It is notable that he almost seems reluctant, as though sensing that he may be becoming involved in something which a man with a wife and a ten-month-old baby may want to stay away from. >>
[STEELE] : "Well, I’m sorry, but he left hours ago. You just got here too late, unfortunately. I’m sure if you called up the SCW offices, they could help you out…"
<< Without a word of thanks, the stranger spins away, leaving Steele somewhat baffled, at the door of his battered, run-down old gym.
Scratching his foppish, sandy brown hair, he shakes his head, with the man prowling down the back-street, as though looking, hunting, for something. In parting, Steele calls out to him… >>
[STEELE] : "Who _are_ you?!" << The stranger turns, the wind toying with his clothes, his hair. Once again, his stare locks with Steele’s and the two stand, as opposites, neither man looking away. Finally, comes the reply, his words chilling, almost ominous. The owner listens intently, curiosity burning… >>
[INTRUDER] : "I am justice. I am hate. I am revenge. I am the bellman, who calls for Mathys’ downfall. Hear it not, my friend, for it is a knell, that summons thee to heaven, or to _hell_…"
<< Once again, he whirls away, pacing purposefully away from the scene, still hunting, still stalking. Steele is almost entranced, baffled, with a thousand thoughts, a thousand questions, still swirling about his mind. As this mysterious ‘predator’ melts away into anonymity, and
Steele trying to make sense of the happenings of the past few minutes, the screen slowly dims, leaving only nightly blackness. >>
