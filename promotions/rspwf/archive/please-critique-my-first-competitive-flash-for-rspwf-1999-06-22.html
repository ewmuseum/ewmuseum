[Please Critique] My first competitive flash for RSPWF...

22 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys, this is my first competitive one for RSPWF, so I am open for your bashing if need be, I know you guys will give it to me! Here it is...
___ [The scene is the all-to-familiar one for "Five Star" Greg Gabbard, for those that do know him. However, some people are seeing the man for the very first time, as the camera peers into what appears to be a training dojo, where a man is dressed in a black trunk and singlet outfit with multiple colors and designs on them. Among them are what look like airbrushed designs of the name 'Five
Star' in various spots on the outfit, in colors of turquoise, red, green, purple, and silver. Those same five colors each make up a side as well in the various five-sided star designs that are decorating the outfit. Also noticable on the man is the medium length blonde hair that he sports, not put up in a ponytail as he sometimes does though, and his sparring gloves, that look as if they'd be regulated by a mixed martial arts fighting association or something.
He has boots that come quite far up on his leg, and at the point that they end is met with his kneebrace, leaving only a small portion of his leg between underneath his trunks and above his kneebraces as the only viewable part of skin on his leg. This man that is preparing for the upcoming tour with DFK by sparring on the mat with a training partner of his, is "Five Star" Greg
Gabbard. Gabbard has a waistlock on the mat on the partner, and both go into a vertical base with the waistlock in place. A full-rotation release German suplex from "Five Star" to the training partner ensues afterward, with no bridge, leaving all impact on the neck of the partner. After following up with a fisherman buster, "Five Star" picks up a towel that is lying on the mat's upper corner, and he wipes his brow, and takes a drink of a bottled water, and he approaches the camera for some words.]
"Five Star" Greg Gabbard: Well, well, it is my first speaking assignment to give to Dream Factory K. The cameraman is all equipped and is ready for my words of wisdom. Now you all got a sneak peek at my action, but that is a very, very, small sampling of my work. Not even enough for an hour d'voures.
For those that aren't aware of it yet, I am "Five Star" Greg Gabbard. I am the man that this promotion will be built on. That's right.... I will carry Dream
Factory K on my shoulders. A gaijin. But the thing is, this gaijin is the best worker in Japan right now. You have different kinds of wrestling here, but one thing remains, I am the best one. After dominating the US indy scene for some time, I have decided to move out here and test the waters. And now I find myself on a full-time swing of DFK.
GG: You know, it isn't easy living up to my nickname. "Five Star". You know what that means? Well you should. In the very great matches that take place in the sport, they get five star ratings. Those are a very rare occurance, yet they still happen every now and then. You have to have two very great workers in the ring to get a five star match going. Well now, that isn't the case.
You see, I am such a worker, I can carry any tub of lard on my back to a five star performance. Each time anyone steps in the ring with me, it is going to be the best match of their respective careers. Every one of the other wrestlers there are going to live at the top of their game while in the ring with me. Because I am the man here. I am the single best worker here. There have been classic names in the past here in Japan. I don't even need to mention them, do I? Oh well, you could start with Rikodozan, and the next generation including Baba and Inoki, and right now the best pure wrestlers going include Samuari, Liger, Otani, Misawa, Kobashi, and Kanemoto, and you could step it up a notch with some guys from Gunryo Pro Wrestling, like TORA and Stripe and Fury and Tenzugame, but one thing remains the same, one thing they all have in common besides just being good workers. They all are surpassed by myself.
GG: Now that I have arrived, the pace of wrestling will be stepped up a bit. I know there are other great guys here, but nobody... _nobody_ is at my level. Nobody is capable of delivering five star matches every single time. Well I am. And I will deliver. They don't get much better than me. This dojo is lucky to have my presence. I am going to participate in this upcoming tour, and the tournament within the tour. The Lions Grand Prix tournament I believe it has been dubbed. And in the opening day of DFK, I will compete in the LGP for the Nippon Dream as I face off with Yoshihiro Fujinami. Now you could be an interesting character, could you not? You have a strong namesake to live up to, especially your last name. Let's just hope for your sake that you aren't
::cough:: washed up ::cough:: as Tatsumi is. Now I am sure you are a decent worker....well maybe... but anyhow, you will look good since I am there to save your butt in the ring. Of course, I will win that one, and move on in the chase for the Nippon Dream.
GG: That will take us to the second night, which like the first, will be in the wrestling famous Tokyo Korakuen Hall, and while I have an off night from tournament action, I will be meeting up in the final non-tourney match on top of the evening, a six man elimination match, with Shinigami, Bandito Suicida, and my now familiar nemesis, Yoshihiro Fujinami on the other side. With me?
Well it doesn't really matter, but for argument's sake, I will have Pierce
Bleed, and Heinosuke Gosho, so they are in good hands, being tag team partners with me and all. The next night will be Champion's Hall, in which the first of the quarterfinal matches take place for the Nippon Dream, and I will be involved in that of course....remember, I will beat Fujinami in the first day at Korakuen. And that will be another step in my advancement for my gold.
GG: Well that is an introduction to what is upcoming in DFK, my friends. Now that I am aboard, things certainly have changed. For the company itself, it's changed for the better with my christening up of the roster. For the other wrestlers within it, it can be ok, if you're my partner, or if you really need someone to give you a credible match on your resume. But for any opponents standing in my way, it is bad that I have arrived. I may sound cocky, but hey.... I have the right to be, don't I? I am the man that will rule the roost here in the Factory. I will be taken seriously, even though I am a junior heavyweight gaijin. That doesn't matter. Everyone will see exactly what I am about, and I can back up my words with my in-ring ability. Which I admit, does mean that I have a lot to back up with, since my words are big! Well, all I can see, is I am sure you'll be hearing from me again soon, as "Five Star" Greg
Gabbard continues on his way to the top of DFK. And always remember, from this point on, Dream Factory K will never be the same.
[As he finishes up his speech, the sparring partner, in recommended fashion, sneaks up behind Gabbard and attempts a backdrop suplex, however, Gabbard flips backward to land on his feet, and delivers a Yakuza kick to the turning around partner. A corkscrew legdrop, and corkscrew senton variety follow up as the camera begins to back out with a cocky pose eminating from Gabbard, one that we may have to see for a while to come....]
Fade out.
