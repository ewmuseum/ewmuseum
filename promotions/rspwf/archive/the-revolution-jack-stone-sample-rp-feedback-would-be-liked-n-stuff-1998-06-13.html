"THE REVOLUTION" JACK STONE Sample RP <Feedback would be liked n stuff :)>

13 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene: Inside the locker room of Jack Stone following IRON MAN! It is empty as the camara zooms around, we then see Jack Stone's wrestling tights, and vest on a nearby chair. The camara then moves over to where there's a chair sitting in front of a mirror, on that chair is resting the GWA World Championship Belt.
The camara zooms in on it as we hear the sound of running water, a moment later we hear the sqeeking of a knob being turned and the water shutting off. After a few moments we hear footsteps and then Jack Stone comes into view. He has on only a towel around his waist as we see the great muscular definition of his arms and chest. He has a black eye, and a small cut above it, Stone walks over to the chair in front of the mirror, picks up the World Title, sits down and palces it over his lap and begins to speak. . .]
"THE REVOLUTION" JACK STONE - <Looks down at the title> Well I did it. . .after one hour of pure hell, one hour of non stop battle I did it. <looks up into the camara> I am the very first Global Wrestling Association World Heavyweight
Champion. This has got to be THE greatest feeling in the world, sure I'm battered, I'm so tired I think I'm gonna pass out, but I'm not feeling any of that because I'm on a pure championship high. I must extend my hand out to
Arthur Essex, the man showed me why he is indeed one of the best in this sport today, as well as one of the toughest. . . But I said that I was coming into this match to win, and that's what I did. I started to doubt it a bit myself when Essex hit the first pin fall over me. I knew right there I had to turn it up to full power. . .but then he got me in the Crossed Swords and thought it was over there. But it wasn't, I made it to the ropes and hit the pinfall to tie things up. I knew right there that it was my time, it was my time to shine in this sport, and take home not just my first piece of championship gold, but my first WORLD Championship. In the end I hit the Stonecutter and the rest was history. Essex I said I wasn't leaving without the gold, and I kept my word.
But anytime you want a rematch, anytime youu want to go at it again with me than just say the word, just say the word and I'll put the gold on the line and we'll go at it again. I'm going to be a fighting champion, I won't back down from anyone, and I'm sure everyone in the GWA is going to be gunning for yours truly. From The Phantom, to Matty Dee, to Mr. Submission they will all want a chance to try and dethrone The Revolution.
[Jack runs his hand through his wet hair, then runs it down his cheek to his chin, he grins and goes on. . ]
"THE REVOLUTION" JACK STONE - But like I said, I'm gonna take on each and every one of them. It already appears as my first challenge has been set. Next
Friday, International Incident I step into the ring in my first title defense against Justin Miller. I'm not going to mince words, I don't like you Miller. I remember when you first came in here talking smack about how you would easily take the gold from me if I happened to have it. . .Well Miller I have it, and you have your chance to take it from me. Can you do it? I don't think so. I know you're willing to do anything, and everything to get ahead. Hell you showed that at Iron Man when you and the rest of your clowns attacked your brother James. That just shows how low you will go. <grins evily> But Miller,
I'm not afraid to go low to make sure I win. You wanna play the dirty games, than I can too and I can play them much better. Sure, you have your Revolution watching your back, making sure you win, and I don't give a damn about them.
I'm not focusing on them, because the more I focuse on them the more of an advantage it gives you. I'm not playing into your hands Miller, sorry pal but it just isn't gonna work.
[Jack picks the World Title up off of his lap and places it on his right shoulder. . .] "THE REVOLUTION" JACK STONE - I know you want this Miller, but you're not gonna get it. I've worked to hard, for to long to get this far and I'm not going to let one of the many walking, talking egos of the wrestling world take it away from me. Bring your so called Revolution, bring a whole god damn army with you,
I don't care. I'll fight off the world if need be to keep this title around my waist. Miller you think you're the greatest thing since sliced bread, well sliced bread was made many years ago and many things greater than it have been made since so that doesn't hold much water for you. Miller I know you can talk the talk, but thus far I haven't seen you back any of your words up. I hope you can do more than talk this coming Friday otherwise it's going to be a very bad, and very painful experiance for you. Now I'm not going to say I'm going to beat you easily, no, not gonna happen. You are a top notch wrestler when you don't let your ego control your every move, if you weren't you wouldn't have this shot. But I'm not going to let you just walk in and take this belt from me without working your ass off for it. No, no, just not gonna happen. You're going to have to put your ego in check and wrestle the greatest match of your life if you even want to think about beating me, but if you don't do that. If you walk into the match thinking you're going to roll riht over me and take this <pats title> from me, well than you got another thing coming, and that other thing is called the StoneCutter. Essex felt it and he went down, just like you will when I hit it. Now I'm not going to sit here and ramble on, I'm going to leave you with this. Miller my friend, International Incident you're going to find out what the rest of the GWA has thus far. The Revolution is here. . and he's taken, NO Priosners. . .
[The camara zooms in on Jack's icy blue eyes as he stares right into the camara as we slowly fade to black. . . .]
