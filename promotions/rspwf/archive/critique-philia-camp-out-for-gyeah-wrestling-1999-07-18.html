18 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Preceeding information : Philia is the Godess of Obsession.. Ian Flaming is her cameraman/boytoy/valet.]
(The scene opens to a clear, starry summernight. You can see the moon, hiding behind the treetops. The viewpoint lowers to a shot of the pine forest. The warm glow of a small campfire can be seen lighting up the trees.
The cameraman steps forward, splish splash. He cusses and the shot changes to so we can see his drenched shoes and pants from having to walk through the shallow creek. As he approaches the glow, campfire songs can be heard.
Peeping from behind the bushes, the film continues in a voyeuristic way. GYeah lady-wrestler Philia is sitting around the campfire, dressed in a cute girlscouts uniform, with a bunch of 15 year olds, as they roast marshmallows over the open fire. Green two-person tents are set up behind them, obviously there sleeping provisions. The chants die down and Philia speaks.)
Philia : Okay girls, it's allready late, time to go to your bunks. Girls (in unison) : Oooooow.. Philia : But that doesn't mean you have to sleep right away. (A sly smile crosses her lips, as the girls run off happily to their tents in pairs, holding hands. Philia gets treated to quick kisses as each pass her by, wishing her goodnight. After a while, all girls are in the tents, and Philia is left alone at the campfire. She munches on the last marshmallow as the fire starts to die down. Looking towards the bushes, she whispers.)
Philia : You can come out now, sweetie.. (The cameraman comes out from the bushes, and takes a seat across the fire from Philia. He speaks with a manly voice.)
Ian Flaming : Damn, baby, why did you want me to take that kind of shot ? Look at my pants.. all wet. Philia : Mine too, sweetie, I guess we'll have to take them off later. (She smiles wickedly, and speaks again.) Philia : It was just to get that dramatic opener for my interview. I like doing things like that.. You know I do.
Ian : Do I ever.. Philia : So let's start this. (Philia gets more comfortable on her fireside log and gets a more serious look. Giggles rising up from the tent to the left behind her, make her giggle too, but she quickly returns to the serious face, and begins her interview.)
Philia : Okay, the reason behind making this interview is, well, is that I need an opponent. It would look as good if I stepped into the squared circle against nobody.. Allthough, I think I can entertain the crowd on my own just fine, but for some reason we gotta wrestle.
(She smiles..) Philia : I picked Me, as an opponent. Why ? Because she's cute. I like cute. I like her gigglish pixie nature. Pixies are fun.. So why would I want to fight her ? Well.. Maybe you didn't know this, but
Pixies are magical creatures.. Just like Leprechauns. And Leprechauns have that big pot o' gold. Well, Pixies have Pixie Stix !! I want my sugarbuzz. I want to beat her and take her magical Pixie Stick. Sugar forever, yeah !
(Philia has become real giddy, and Ian whispers something to get her back on track.) Philia : Me, I wanna play with me. Hey, you don't even have to remember a thing about how I beat you. I'll just slip you the Roofie and have myself a
JoyRide on your pretty face..
So, what do you say, Me, you little pixie princess ? Think you've can take the time off from bouncing all over the planet to step into the ring with me
? Let me know soon, girlie.
(After saying those words, the fire dies down completly and the forest is covered by the blanked of darkness again. Vaguely we see Philia get up from the log and walk over to her tent, moving her hips extra sexily as she walks. Flaming obviously gets up and follows behind her, flicking of the camera. All is dark..er then before.)
