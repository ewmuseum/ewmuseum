Is this guy a jackass or what? [RP Critique - Xanthus Tonaka]

5 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens up on the private balcony of BWFE World Champion and 1999
Pillman Cup Winner, Xanthus Tonaka. Currently, The LEGENDARY Xanthus is looking up at the stars and taking in the cold night air. Xanthus is still in his ring attire which consists of tight, solid black wrestling trunks.
Since the new Champion lives in New York City, he left MSG via limo immediatley after BOTB ended. He didn't even give anyone a chance to interview him even though dozens of media members were chasing him like wild dogs. So here he is, just an hour after the show, alone with the stars and the freezing air.
As the camera shifts positions, it reveals the sight of a small table covered by a delicate velvet cloth which basks in the dancing moon light. On this table, is perhaps the most glorious prize the wrestling world has to offer -
THE PILLMAN CUP......Dedicated to one of the greatest ever. The Pillman Cup shines brilliantly in the full moon's glow. Wonderful silver streaks of energy seem to line the outside of it as Xanthus stares at it with carefully contained emotion. He walks over to it, and then holds it up high. He feels it's solid finish and pulls it close to his heart, realizing that not many get to receive this honor. As Xanthus places the beautiful cup down, he stares at the object next to it.......THE BWFE WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP BELT. The belt is shiny, sparkling and new. It's huge 24k gold face is more impressive in person than when it appears on television. Particularly because you can observe the spectacular diamonds which circle around the front of the belt and around the strap. For the THIRD time, Xanthus is able to call himself the best in the BWFE. THE WORLD CHAMPION......truly, the best of the best.
As Xanthus admires it for awhile longer, he inhales deeply and holds the belt up high. Then, he places it around his waist for a few short minutes before resting it on top of the table next to the Pillman Cup. Xanthus now turns, and speaks.
XANTHUS: This is it. <long pause> -I did it. I AM THE PILLMAN CUP WINNER. And once again....the BWFE recognizes me as the UNDISPUTED Champion of the World. I beat Hectic, I beat Hoff, and I beat Dread. I went to
HELL....I kicked the Devil in his red-ass....and I MARCHED through MSG with
FULL FORCE. When it was all said and done.....the smoke cleared......just like I said it would. And after the smoke cleared....I STOOD before ALL OF
YOU as the VICTOR. The man who PROSPERED in NEW YORK. .....Just like I said.
<Xanthus picks up his water bottle which was resting on the floor. He takes a sip.> XANTHUS: Ya' know.....A lot happened at BOTB. And man, what a way to end the MILLENIUM for the BWFE. Just like a Prophet....I predicted that THE
GREATNESS will lead this company into Y 2 K. BUT, along the way, I got to play boss for a bit, I got to throw Torr into some toilet duty, Wolv and I got to screw Dare once and for all, Kelley messed with us little, and GX was able to go into 2000 on top of the World. Even though it seems like a mess.....even though it was hard to tell what was happening.....the one thing for sure is that the GX MASTERPLAN worked. AND that is what counts. Wolv and I said one thing....no matter what happens, one of us is walking out as
Champ. That was the masterplan and it we MADE it happen. For that, I feel
ULTIMATE satisfaction.
<Xanthus takes a sip of his water.> XANTHUS: Now, for those of you at home who think that GX is going to soften up now that we got the final victory of '99....you're dead wrong. As a matter of fact.....you can look for ALEXANTHUS to come back TEN times as strong in 2000. I will continue to get under everyone's skin like no one else can. No matter what.....you can count on me to DRIVE your head nuts!
That's a promise. (because that's what a heel is supposed to do) <Xanthus looks out to the city lights> XANTHUS: So, as I close this last interview before the brake.....I'd like to address BL 13 and Storm. Boys, sorry if you don't like how I play. I mean,
I must admit, I was taken aback by your recent interview antics....they were,
sort of......peculiar! All I heard was a blur of "Alexanthus" and "Wolveron" (and by the way - those are cool names, maybe we'll change them permanently!) I'm not quite sure what your intentions were, but they were odd....and they were kind of childlike. I felt sorely embarrassed for you two when you felt the need to bring our agents into the wrestling world. It was certainly a poor show of judgement on your behalf. I mean, if that's who you guys truly are, then I'm sorry. There are plenty of Psychiatrists out there who can help you out! I don't know, take an anti-depressant or something. Judging by your creativity though, I don't think that's who you are. I think that was frustration getting the best of you. So, enjoy this brake, regroup, relax, breathe, get yourselves together....and gentlemen,
WE'LL DO IT ALL OVER AGAIN. Next time though.....XANTHUS won't be so nice.
:) <Xanthus now turns around and snaps back at the camera> XANTHUS: Oh, and BL.....by the way, this one is for you. YOU'RE FIRED. Sorry buddy, you asked me to do it. <With this, THE GREATNESS picks up the New BWFE WORLD TITLE BELT, and slings it over his left shoulder. In his right hand....he picks up the Honorable
PILLMAN CUP. Xanthus looks outside once more....flashes an EVIL smile, and enters the comfort of his luxurious Park Ave. apartment. Scene fades to solid black.>
