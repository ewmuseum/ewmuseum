(RP Critique) Killer Christian Holmes

13 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Our scene opens across the inner city streets of Houston, Texas on a hot humid night. In one corner of the eye two young teenagers, one black and the other hispanic, are engaging in some sort of deal. In the other corner a white cop is pushing around a young black kid who might not be thirteen yet. Gunshots can be heard in the distance, probably a couple of streets over, not that the cop cares since he's busy intimidating the kid. The heat of summer is rippling above the concrete even at night as the camera sweeps along the ground, picking up speed, slowing down long enough to watch a young black woman holding a baby pause to sob against the doorframe of her low income apartment as her boyfriend, or maybe just the baby's father, stalks to his mustang and slides in without one backwards glance towards the young mother. He hasn't even put the key in the ignition before some crack-addled young white girl struts up to the car in a too-tight purple dress zipped down below the tropic of capricorn and the hemline on the short skirt significantly above the tropic of cancer. She leans in easy, her blond hair swaying, and speaks easy as the young mother can only watch in horror as the baby starts to cry. She tucks her head away trying to soothe the baby, but he cannot be assuaged. The hooker seems to be succeeding in her salesmanship but the cop, once so busy mugging for a twelve year old now seems intent on putting those intimidation skills on the young white girl, possibly the only white girl in this part of the city. The cop, fat, balding, with a real mean streak painted across his face yanks the girl from the car from the black car with a bone snapping pull, spilling the hooker onto the sidewalk. The mustang pulls away quickly and the cop yanks once again on the girl's arm to get her to her feet. She spits at him, but the cop merely backhands her and then manhandles her towards a dark alley.
As they struggle their way to the alley, the pair pass another odd pair standing in front of an old abandoned church. The cop throws the girl into the alley and forces her crying head into his crotch. The camera leaves the cop and the hooker and focuses on the pair in front of the church.]
[It is a very large man with a diminuative yet driven young woman. The
6'7"man, who appears to weigh nearly three hundred pounds of solid muscle, sports curly blond hair that has obviously been dyed since his black roots are showing. His black eyes seem a bit shifty, but his clean shaven smile is enough to disarm anyone. He's dressed in what appears to be an incredibly cheap suit: a faded blue blazer with matching blue pants. He's wearing a white dress shirt with a red tie.
In his hand he holds a metal plated bible, which has several bookmarks in it. His companion, the young woman, is wearing a sun dress with a pleasant floral print that stops mid thigh. The neckline isn't too deep, but it does display a significant amount of cleavage between her obviously augmented breasts. Her long, dyed red hair hangs straight down her back, and her green eyes shimmer like fresh hundred dollar bills. Her tight athletic thighs and calves ripple with muscles and her feet are covered with black stiletto heels, which she looks extremely comfortable in. Her full red lips are straining to smile, but you can see a bit of rage welling within her. The man however holds her back with a sideways glance and gesture of his hand to stay back. He speaks:]
The man: Well, it is time now to introduce myself to the SEWF. My name is Killer Christian Holmes and I am here to make a difference.
With me always is my mentor, my mediator, and my best friend, Sister
Brittany. She is a nun with the Diocese of Saint Thomas, in Austin,
Texas. Right now we are in my hometown in my old neighborhood here in
Houston. It's changed a bit since I left. There's more white people around. I used to be the only one.
Brittany: What about that girl that the cop... Killer: hold on, Brittany, the Killer has something to say to these
SEWF people. Brittany: But Killer... Killer: Don't "But Killer" me right now. I've got something important to say. Brittany: hurumph. Killer: Anyways, the Killer has arrived here in the SEWF and I can tell you right now that I aim to make a difference. But first, a history lesson for all of you.
Brittany: Oh mother. Killer: You see, the Killer grew up here, in this neighborhood, the only white kid on the block. And these are some mean streets. My father never gave a damn, he just had his bottle. My mother, well, she was worked hard. She didn't deserve what happened. And when my father became abusive, his son became more so. In time I grew to be much larger than him, and he couldn't have prepared for it. The rage, the hatred, the evil that stole its way across my body, it consumed me. I turned to drugs. I turned to prostitutes, and the worst, I turned to violence. Violence against the other gangs. Violence against the teachers in school. Violence against the cops. Violence against my dad. And, worst of all, violence against my mother. I've been stabbed, I've been shot, I've been beaten, but nothing hurt more than looking at my mother's face after I had struck her down. She quickly pressed charges, and soon I was locked up in a jail cell for "my own good." I didn't believe her at the time, since I was locked in a tiny cell with a swindler cellmate that somehow always ended up with all of my cigarettes one way or another. But just as I was dominating in the streets, I dominated the jail. Meal time was fear time if you were an enemy of the Killer. Eventually, my transgressions grew tiresome for some of the guards and I spent an agonizing stretch of time in solitary confinement. I couldn't tell how long I was in there, I was just there, alone. Alone with my conscience, alone with my guilt, alone with no one to beat up. When I was let out, I was filled with a rage that would only be purged with a massacre of an entire cell block. But then a visitor came to see me before I could release all of this. I had never had a visitor in my many years in the pen. I didn't know what to think. So I went to the visitation chambers, and it was there that I met Brittany.
Brittany: yada yada yada, he gets released... can we go Killer? Killer: Hold on. And you know what she said to me? She knew all about my rage, and all about my violent past. She knew that I was a Killer, she knew that I was evil to the core. But she touched me. Right here inside my heart, knowing that I didn't really want to be evil, rather,
I was forced. She knew that there was some redemption for a killer like me. She convinced me there was a God, and convinced me that beating up people for no reason was wrong, but beating people to steer them in the right direction towards Christ is immaculate. On that day, alone in my cell, I accepted the Lord Jesus H. Christ into my heart as my own personal Savior. From that day on, I was a model prisoner, only beating people with no regard for the Good Lord. The Killer united the prison, and through my altruistic deeds, the pen saw fit to release the
Killer who has gone on to be the greatest success story Huntsville
State Pen has ever seen. Brittany has guided me with my only marketing skill, my ability to beat people up. She guided me to the shores of the Dalton Extreme Championship Wrestling Federation, where I honed my skill as a fighter, a brawler, a wrestler. I won't go into details about that. The bottom line is that the Killer has now arrived into the SEWF, and I shall deliver the Wrath of God upon the unbelievers.
Those who do not believe in Jesus H. Christ, and those that dare to not believe in the Killer.
Brittany: [restless, trying to speed Killer up a little] Yeah, Yeah,
"Rejoice with me; for I have found my sheep which was lost. " [Luke
15:6] Killer: "Judge not and ye shall not be judged: condemn not and ye shall not be condemned: forgive and ye shall be forgiven." [Luke 6:37]
"But that ye may know that the Son of man hath power on earth to forgive sins. Arise, take up thy bed, and go into thine house."
[Matthew 9:6] Brittany: [pissed off and impatient] That girl over there in the alley! Killer: One last thing: The Killer abides by the rules set down by
Jesus H. Christ: The three demandments:
1) Thou shall accept the Lord Jesus H. Christ into your heart as your own Personal Savior. 2) Thou shall partake of drugs for their ability to bring one closer to God. 3) Thou shall share sexual relations with others to personally communicate the awesome power of the Lord. [The cop appears from the alley. The blond hooker lies on the ground, her purple dress torn and in disarray, sobbing, oblivious to the trash and assorted bodily fluid surrounding her: blood, semen, piss. The cop looks down at her, zipping his fly while he spits on her. In his hand is apparently all the money she had earned doing a hard night's work.
Brittany has had enough. She brazenly walks up to the cop.] Brittany: you a tough guy, beating up a girl like that and taking her money? Cop: Yeah, I'm a tough guy, what are you gonna do about it, sister? Brittany: [touching the cop's chest and cooing] oooh... I like a tough guy like you... let me see what you used on the girl back there in that alley.
[The hooker in the alley sees this and starts to cry some more. She's finally gotten onto all fours, not noticing that her dress is torn so badly one of her breasts is exposed.]
Cop: You mean my fist? Brittany: no, silly, your "nightstick." Cop: Oh yeah... [the cop unzips his fly again, while Killer leans against the church wall and smiles. He knows what's going to happen.]
Brittany: It's... so... DEAD! [Brittany quickly punts the cop between the legs that drops him to his knees in seconds. Brittany backs up and kicks a field goal with the now ground level crotch of the cop. The cop shrieks in pain as the hooker slowly rises in the alley. Brittany kicks again, this time landing it underneath the cop's chin, knocking his head back to near unconsciousness. Killer walks up, and lifts the cop backfirst onto his shoulder. Killer grips his hands under either of the cop's armpits and lifts up so that the cop is over Killer's head. Killer throws the man forward and catches the cop upside down on the way down, piledriving the cop into the ground. The cop, barely conscious, slumps down, unaware that he is still exposing himself.]
Brittany: [she places one of her heels down upon the cop's, um, member. She doesn't apply any pressure, yet] There's nothing worse than a cop who rapes a hooker. [to the hooker] are you okay?
Hooker: I'm okay. Who are you people? Killer: We are messengers from God. Here's the money he's stolen from you. and here's another hundred bucks. Remember, the Lord works in mysterious ways. This was meant to happen. You must accept the Lord
Jesus H. Christ into your heart now. And buy yourself some quality cocaine with the money that I gave you. The Lord wants to speak with you, and sometimes blow is the best way to go. And next time you need money, don't do it for the money, do it for God. Spread the word as you spread your legs. Can you do this, honey.
Hooker: Yes, yes I can. You've changed my whole outlook on life. I love you! [she rushes forward and hugs Killer. Brittany looks dismayed.] Killer: Don't forget Brittany. [The hooker lets go of Killer and hugs Brittany. Somehow Brittany's hand finds the hooker's exposed breast.]
Killer: Brittany, our work here is done. Let's find our path here in the SEWF. If the Killer needs to make a challenge, then Dean, I burned down your toolshed in the DECWF. Do you want to give in to Wrath? Do you want to give into revenge? Answer it yourself, and challenge the
Killer if you like. Let's go Brittany. And don't stomp on the man's wee-wee on the way out. He may find redemption someday.
[Killer and Brittany walk off down the street, where they are approached by four black youngsters. Killer puts his arm around one and Brittany holds another. They walk down the street while the hooker stands over the fallen cop. She smiles to the camera, and the stamp of her high heel echoes throughout the street long before the smashed cop can scream...]
