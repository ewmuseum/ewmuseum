RP critique

13 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Alright, I would like a critique on this RP. It's probably not the greatest but
I don't think it sucks either... I could be wrong either way...
[[The camera fades in on a deserted gymanasium. It pans around the room showing all the various machines and weights. The room is empty, except for the faint sound of hands and feet meeting a punching bag, and an even fainter sound of someone breathing. The camera begins to zoom forward until a shadow is seen in the distance. The camera continues to move forward until the sound is very loud and oscpicous. The camera turns a corner and in clear view is Preston Holt working out on a punching bag. His hands feet are like lightning striking the bag, and his breathing is rythmic and meticulous. He fails to acknowledge the camera'su presence, and begins to count down his punches and kicks. 5.. 4...
3... 2... 1... He stops just as quick as he was going and rest his hands behind his head. He does a couple of quick stretches to cool down, and then rest himself on the bench.]]
Defiant Preston Holt: [Still breathing very hard] So the wWw is already out to get me... I'm beginning to think this sport is based on more on interviews, your out-of-the ring antics, and your look more than your in-ring ability. Day in and day out we are flooded with footage of wWw wrestlers, do we ever see footage of them wrestling? Of course not..
[[Preston turns his head and stares at the wall for a few seconds.]] The wWw has been open for two days, and my fax machine is flooded with transcripts of interviews and what not... One of the guys that has already started with this is my opponent for Monday Night Jericho.... “Superior” Shawn
Parker. I know I’m young... I just graduated from High School a year ago, and college is not a plan at this point in my life, but when it comes to a wrestling ring, I have plenty of savvy. Being a youngster people classify me as being selfish and cocky... [Preston cracks a smile] and maybe they’re right, but I do have respect for my superiors, no pun intended. But the one thing that
I cannot stand is guys that don’t give younger guys a chance.
[[Preston grabs a towel and wipes the sweat off his brow.]] I remember back when the wWw was open before. Everything Shawn and
Shane say about ruling it is very true. They did rule the wWw, but where they screwed up... they didn’t give anyone else a chance. Shawn was complaining about wanting some compeition this time. I remember a fine young athlete named
Clint Edgar who had the potential and the ability to knock anyone off their throne, but Clint was never given the oppurtunity to do so. In fact, he was never even given a match! Now, I’m not stuck on this Clint Edgar guy or anything, he’s an old has been, but my point is, Shawn Parker was “superior” in the ole’ wWw because he wouldn’t allow others the chance to excel enough to stop him.
[[Preston’s face has become flushed with anger, but suddenly a sly grin forms across his face. His eyes begin to look very decieving as he continues to speak.]]
And here is where I come in... I am defiant... I don’t take shit from anybody, regardless if they are older than me or not. Call me a rebel, call me a [In a mock voice] “damn youngster”, call me whatever the hell ya’ want to, but I am not going to bow down to anybody. I’ll resist athourity and fight for what is rightfully mine. Are ya hearing this Shawn? This Monday I am going to prove to you that there are others in this world that can wrestle as good as you... you and your boy Shane. I’ll show the rest of the wWw not to take me lightly because I’m a “rookie”. I work just as hard as the old guys, and I put up just as much of a fight... maybe more.
[[The sly grin is gone and now Preston’s face is very serious. His dark eyes pierce into the center of the camera as he finishes.]]
The wWw is built around a legacy, it’s history. And so is Shawn Parker. Just because he has better credentials than me doesn’t make him the greater power. What matters is here [[Preston points to his heart]] and here [[Preston points to his head]]. Shawn has never faced a power like me, and I am going to prove to him that the wrestling world has passed him by. The new blood as arrived in wWw, and I am out to make a statement. I won’t be held back, I won’t obey your autocratic rules, I will resist... I am defiant.
[[Preston gets up from the bench and goes back to work as the camera fades to black to the sounds of hands and feet meeting a punching bag.]]
