Here is another sample roleplay from some free agents

21 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The GPW is still down to the presidents computer crashing so I am still looking for a great federation. The GPW is one of the best feds that I have ever seen, and very well could be the best. We have a group of three heels that are looking for a top end federation that has been running for a good length of time, and that has a lot of talent to feud with. Please do not send me info about your super ultra great fed that is going to be teh best ever once you get more then three wrestlers because I simply delete them.
Here is the last rp that one of my allies and I did together right before the GPW closed. It is nothing special, but is not too bad either.
Furious Steele << Scene opens backstage of Meltdown after the show goes off the air. Furious Steele and Sabbath are heading down a hallway after their victory over Team Ego, and The Accolades of Eminence. Steele is drenched in sweat after fighting most of the match and Sabbath does not even have a drop of sweat on him. Steele is wearing his usually black sleeveless "I Hate You"
T-shirt, blue jean cut off shorts, and black army boots. His black straggly hair is soaking wet and is covering his face.
<< The only other people in the hallway right now are a group of five security guards that are in a circle talking about this and that. Sabbath and Steele get about ten feet away from the guys and all of a sudden attack them. The five guys are caught off guard and are quickly beaten into submission by Sabbath and Steele. Violence and Corruption lives up to the first half of their name and continues to beat on the guards until. Finally some more security guards show up and break up the fight before any real serious damage is done. Happy with the damage they have done they continue down towards their dressing room where Robert Sental is waiting for them.>>
[SENTAL]: Gentlemen, let me be the first to congratulate you on a truly dominating performance on Meltdown tonight and I was wondering if I might be able to get a few words with you . . .
[SABBATH]: Sental, I should have known you'd be here already . . . it's a good plan though, get in good with us early before the rest of the parasites realize just how important tonight was . . . before they realize that WE are now the major power in GPW . . .
<<Sental brings the mic back towards himself as if to say something but before he can he has the mic brutally ripped from his grasp by Sabbath.
Sental takes a step back in shocked fear and then is casually bumped to the side as Sabbath steps in front of the camera.>>
[SABBATH]: Sental, you're not really needed here, so stay quiet . . . <<Sabbath looks directly into the camera and points at it as speaks.>>
[SABBATH]: All that anyone's who's watching this needs to know is that
Violence and Corruption are back together . . .and anyone who knows anything knows that when Violence and Corruption set their collective sights on someone . . .or someone's . . .those people's lives are never the same afterward . . . broken families . . .broken lives . . .mental scarring . . .
post traumatic stress disorder . . . the list of what follows in our wake goes on and on . . . but that was just the beginning . . . just our warm up round . . .now things have gotten serious . . . take heed . . .you've been warned . .
<<Sabbath hands the mic over to Steele.>> [Furious] Rogers, tonight was your first lesson on what it takes to be elite.. While you follow around Robinson like a lost dog, he takes your glory.. While you sit under the table like a dog waiting for scraps,
Robinson feasts on your hard work.. Tonight you saw first hand what greatness is.. Violence and Corruption is not shackled by friendship, ethics, or rules.. Both of us are willing to do whatever it takes to win a match, even if it means hurting ourselves or each other.. Furious Steele has been called the heart of evil, which may be very true, but then Sabbath is the mastermind of evil. Sabbath knew that you four fools would waste your energy early and that he would be able to stroll down to the ring and take care of you with no trouble...No trouble at all... As for Team Ego, you boys can head back to midcard level with your tail between your legs, because you simply don't have what it takes to compete at this level...
<<Steele takes a step back and holds the mic out in front him as Sabbath takes it and steps back in front of the camera. He takes a second to pull the hair back out of his face revealing the huge mass of scar tissue which dominates the left side of his face.>>
[SABBATH]: Now, I was hoping that the GPW would give me a big, tough challenge to prove myself upon my return . . . but instead, I get stuck wasting my time against The Phoenix . . . now kid, I'm sure you consider yourself a tough guy because you compete in the Brass Knux division . . .and
I'm sure you consider yourself a badass because you've apparently done fairly well in said division . . . and I'm almost positive that what you think about yourself means less than nothing to me . . .and less than nothing is what will be left of your sorry career once you've suffered through the hell that is Sabbath . . . I don't know who you pissed off in the offices to warrant being sacrificed to my return but I thank them all the same . . .
<< Sabbath hands the mic back over to Steele and then stands next to Sental who has still not spoken. Sabbath is almost daring Sental to try to speak.
Steele whips the sweat off his face before he begins to talk.>> [Furious] Carl Blair, The Furious One does not know or care what you did wrong to get into this match, but somebody set you up.. Somebody put you in a match where your career could be ended.. Somebody has evil intentions for you.. This week while you are lifting weights, and training with your sparing partner, The Furious One wants you to think about the opportunity before you, Blair.. The Furious One wants you to think about what kind of chance you have to make a name for yourself on Sunday.. The Furious One wants you to come into that match with everything you have so you have no excuses.. Blair, you are about to take the beating of your life by the elite of the wrestling world.. You are about to be a punching bag for the Furious
One to take out some frustration on.. You are about to get into a match where you could very well have your pathetic career ended... Sleep well while you can Blair, for after Sunday you will never be the same.. After
Sunday you will be nothing more then a shell of your already pathetic career... After Sunday you will simply be no more.......
<<Steele turns to hand the mic to Sabbath. Sabbath takes it without ever looking away from Robert Sental. Sabbath stares a hole through him before speaking . . .>>
[SABBATH]: Sental, now, don't speak . . .just nod . . do you happen to know off the top of your head what the name of the World Tag Team Champions are?
<<Sental nods nervously.>> [SABBATH]: Good, good . . .I thought you might . . .see, we don't happen to know because it's so hard to keep all of these minor leaguers straight . .
.they all seem to look alike . . . so, since you are familiar with them will you do me a favor . . . actually, would you do US a favor?
<<Sental nods immediately.>> [SABBATH]: I thought you might . . .now, go down to their locker room or their hotel room or wherever you have to go but find them . . .then, once you find them you give them a message . . . you tell them that the dark times are coming . . .the light is fading . . .the predators are closing in and they have been cut from the herd . . . tell them that if they wish, they can surrender the belts and the championship to us, no problem . . .but that's not exactly the way we would like to handle it . . . tell them that if they truly fancy themselves to be champions then they would have no reservations about taking on all comers . . . especially us . . . tell them they have been given an ultimatum . . .if we do not have the title belts in our hands or a guaranteed title shot signed by this Sunday then drastic measures will be taken . . .think you can handle that Robbie?
<<Sental nods vigorously while starting to back out of the door. Sabbath points once at the cameraman and once at the door. Taking the hint the cameraman heads for the door as well. Scene fades with a shot of Sental catching the mic thrown back to him by Sabbath while stumbling out the locker room door.>>
