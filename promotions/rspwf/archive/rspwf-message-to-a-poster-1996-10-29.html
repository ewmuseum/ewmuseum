Irrelevant criticism. You are the only one that claimed spelling was important to role playing or this debate. I have noticed that you have a tendancy to pick on one's spelling, while ignoring the subject of the statement itself (and I am not referring to this statement only, but rather several others that have been refutations of your arguments). >> >>- Booked feds have a simulator, the best one there is. A HUMAN BRAIN! > >Filled with prejudices and favoritism. A programmed simulator will favor certain stats. WLS 5.1 favors speed and conditioning rather heavily. If it does not favor certain stats, all characters created have 33/33/33 odds of winning/losing/drawing. Who says that I agree with what the programmer felt was an important stat? Sims have their own forms of bias. >>- Booked feds are based on REAL wrestling just as much as a sim league. > >No, you talk. Sim feds wrestle. Sims do not wrestle, they compare numbers. The sims that are currently out there cannot acuratly simulate the physical side of wrestling, as it is too complex. I am not saying that booked feds are more realistic than simmed feds, but I am also not saying that simmed feds are more realistic than booked. If you wish to simulate the booking aspect of RL pro wrestling, then you cannot rely only on a sim. The fact that these feds simulate the booking aspect of RL pro wrestling is why they are called "booked" feds and there is no need for any other name. >>- Booked feds are like real wrestling just for that reason. They are >>booked! Vinny Mac and Eric B. don't make their decisions via TNM! > >No, they make them out of jealousies, salaries, who their friends are, >etc. Is that included in your booked fed or is it just a lot of talk? Are a wreslter's eating and excercize habits figured into a simmed fed? How about the injuries he sustained in last week's match? How about the fans throwing trash into the ring and making the surface slippery? What about exposure to common colds and flus? How about the altitude of the city the match takes place in? Your refutation does not stand because a simulator cannot take every single factor of a physical match into account any more than the head of booked fed can cover every single aspect of booking a match. >>- Booked feds offer MUCH better matches. > >Sim feds offer more realistic results. This depends on if you wish to simulate the booking side of wrestling or the physical side. >>- Booked feds have more member activity in most cases > >Not the feds I am in. This is not a good refutation because it specifies only the feds that you are in. You are appling something that occurs in a part to the whole. This is a common logical fallacy. Also, the orriginal statement is ultimately unprovable. >>- Booked feds are more fun (IMHO) because you get rewarded for your hard >>work, > >If you think a roleplay is hard work, please get a job. Work and fun are not mutually exclusive concepts. > > >>- Booked feds add more originality, not the same run of the mill stuff. > >Huh? Looks like you are running out of arguments. >>- Booked feds .... RULE! IMHO of course. > >Yep, fresh out of arguments. One should point out that you have made the following arguments repetedly, with nothing new added since the weekend this argument started: 1. Booked feds are not true ewrestling feds. [You do not have the exclusive right to define ewrestling. The term 'ewrestling', as it has commonly been used in RSPWF, refers to all forms of feds: booked, simmed, and otherwise.] 2. Booked feds are biased and simmed feds are not. [Simmed feds are biased based upon what the programmer(s) feel are the most important stats (or sometimes through simple programing errors).] 3. Booked feds are unfair because they remove inactive members. [Expulsion due to inactivity/not providing support is an accepted standard in many organizations, both recreational and professional. If you do not pay club dues, you may not take part in club activities. In this case, club dues are flashes, strats and the like.] 4. Various people do not know how to spell. [Irrelevant to the debate at hand.] 5. Booked feds do not truly simulate booking because they do not take every aspect of booking into account. [Simmed feds to not truly simulate physical matches because they do not take every aspect of a physical match into account. Therefore simmed feds are not better than booked feds on this count.] 6. People do not have the right to insult you just because you do not agree with their views. [True, and you do not have the right to insult others because they do not agree with you. The diference is that your insults are usually not as blatent or direct.] Can anyone else think of anything I have missed? After all, I am human and fallable. Deric
