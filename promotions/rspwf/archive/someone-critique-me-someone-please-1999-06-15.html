[Someone Critique Me] Someone Please!!!

15 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is from an AOL Federation verse The Reaper ([detail withheld]). Be Nice! -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [Fade In] [ Scene ]: [Lights Go Pitch Black] "Living Dead Girl" By Rob Zombie begins to play throughout the arena. The Crowd Erupts into loud cheer's as Scotty Lee
Xtreme walks out from behind the curtain. Scotty Lee Xtreme comes out wearing
Blue Wrestling Tights, with white wrestling shoe's on. He walks out with a bottle of water and a smile on his face. He walks halfway down to the ring and takes a sip of water then spits it into the air. He runs slides into the ring, stands and poses for the camera and crowd. Blue, and Red Fireworks shoot off around the arena. They become calm as Scotty Lee Xtreme Speaks.
[ Scotty Lee Xtreme ]:Another Day. Another Beginning for Scotty Lee Xtreme. Tonight, I am out to speak on one man. The Reaper. Reaper, you've just done a short little segment from the graveyard. Reaper, you first begin to mention, the way people star at you, or are scared of you. Reaper, I for one, am not scared of you. You can talk about anything you'd like Reaper, and I will never be scared. Reaper, other people may have made the same comments as I have in the past. All I can tell you now Reaper, is they are different from Mr. Xtreme.
They are people who will not take the challenge, and step up to the plate like a man, and fight The Reaper. Reaper, I talk a lot about being a man. Would you like to know why Reaper? Simply Because, your not one. Attacking me from behind, showed me you were nothing more than a small little mouse. Reaper, I know very well, that the words I speak could get me into some deep trouble. But think about it, if I were unable to back up the things I say, do you think I would be reciting them now? Reaper, we've been over this. Yes, I know what you are able, and very capable of doing, but Reaper, don't think so highly of yourself. Just when you think you have everything wrapped up, I will bury you.
Reaper, you try to get people scared of what you can do. Well, Reaper I know very well what you can, and most definitely will_TRY_to do, and Reaper, I'm not scared one bit. Yea Reaper, I know what awaits me, and I know you think I am a complete moron for being a man and stepping right up into the eye's of The
Reaper, and facing him one on one, but Reaper, I will you. I am one hundred percent man, and you, dead man, will not take me down. Running my mouth is the only thing I have against you? Reaper, your the moron not me. I have a lot more than you can think of to help me win Reaper. What are they? You ask. Reaper, I am no fool. For you will have to wait until Saturday for me to show you.
Reaper, you think I've stepped up to the plate to make a name for myself,
Reaper, that was the case. Now, it's for respect, and the UXWA Universal
Championship! Reaper, a few day's ago, I did announcer my retirement. But then,
I thought about wrestling without the Innovator Of Xtreme, and how much wrestling would then suck. Reaper, think twice about your last sentence. "The
Reaper is going to walk away with his ticket to fight for that world title, and you......you shall be carted out on a stretcher." Is what you said Reaper. I find this funny. You think I will be carted out on a Stretcher, well, if that's the case Reaper, you dead soul will be wheeled out in a brand spanken new coffin. And I, shall be the one in line for the UXWA Universal Title.
[ Scotty Lee Xtreme ]:Reaper, I've got what I've wanted for so long now. A
Match with a "great." Reaper, The name I will make once I defeat you, will be known to all. "Scotty Lee Xtreme, the one who dropped The Reaper in the center of the ring, 1,2,3." Will be said across America. Then Reaper, you will vanish in the night, and never return. You will have been beaten by the one everyone thought would be just another walk in the park for you. Reaper, your correct,
I've asked you for some help several times, and you've given it to me. Reaper, that is much appreciated, but now, you, as you've already said, must face a man, you helped create. Reaper, you are correct again, before I went to you, and a man, Chuck Williams, I was no challenge, or a threat in wrestling, but look at me now. Look at what awaits everyone in the UXWA. The man that will conquer the world as we know it. Reaper, it is time, it is time to put a challenge in front of you Reaper. The Extremeness is around the corner.
Saturday as a matter of fact. Reaper, I may be knew, but don't think of me as a rookie. I will take your idea's of me being a so called "rookie" and emerge them deep down your throat. Reaper, I finally get my shot at you. This whole time, I've gotten one good comment from you to me. That comment was so vague, I can't even recite it now. Reaper, you claim to "Own" me? Your a funny guy
Reaper. I just tell you, think again.
[ Scotty Lee Xtreme ]:Reaper, you take a lot away from me. You simply think I am a jobber. You think I stand no chance what so ever in the ring with you.
Reaper, your wrong. Do you actually think, that If I didn't think I'd stand a chance against you, or have some sort of thing up my sleeve that I would challenge you? No Way Reaper. You know me better than that. Reaper, you know I want respect, you know that very well. Yet that seems to be what you speak of most of the time. You want to make me think no one will respect me for jumping so high, and falling into the path of The Reaper. Reaper, you think you will destroy me, as everyone else did in my past. What happened to them? They were beat down, badly. You know Reaper, you try so hard to make me become scared of you. It won't work Reaper. Reaper, you can't ruin my career. This move the
"Death Spike" does not compete with the Xtreme Spike off the top rope. Reaper, you my friend, are in for more than you think. You are in for something no other wrestler has given you before. Trust me. Reaper, don't try to threaten me with your "Death Spike," your not going to make me think twice about facing you. Reaper, there's more talent than you can see that I have. Saturday you will see that, and it shall rest in your soul forever. Reaper, we both know I am_Able_to beat you. That was no guarantee to that I_will_beat you, but I am able. On the eighteenth it will all come down to who is the better man. Reaper,
I suggest you watch what you say, you never know, I may just have something
"Wonderful" up my sleeve.
[ Scotty Lee Xtreme ]:Reaper, you are a bit more of a man than I first thought. Reaper, you may have attacked me, you may have said some things that aren't true. You also might very well be the best wrestler I've ever will wrestle. But
Reaper, Scotty Lee Xtreme is something you've never faced. You may have taught me many things, but I've have matured, and I have grown up, I know things you don't Reaper. Keep that in mind. You want me at one hunderd percent, mentaly and phisicaly. Well, you must not, by attacking me it show's you want to have the upper hand in this match. You must know I can beat you at one hunderd percent. Reaper, I'm no fool, I know what you want. That, is to put me on the shelf's for good. Reaper, if I somehow lose, I am not one to complain. I am sure later that evening you wil get a "Good Match," of some sort from me. I am a good compeditior, and a good sportsmen. You should know this. Reaper, you don't scare me at all, you are not going to make me think I stand no chance.
That is exactly what your trying to do. Reaper, you will get it though your head that Scotty Lee Xtreme is very able to defeat you. Reaper, you no it was wrong to attack me while I wasnt aware you were even in the building. Reaper, soon enough the pay back from the attack will make you sweat. You'll soon enough see what I mean. Reaper, now that we actually have a match signed, you can walk into the ring, and I will see you. Reaper, I almost think your better off sneak attacking, or keeping the lights down to beat me up. Reaper, with the lights on, and my focus on you one hunderd percent, Reaper, I dont think you can handle me. Fear is something I laugh at Reaper. Expecialy when you bring it up. Reaper, fear is something I've never had, and unless I am severly injured, at Sacrifice, well then, maybe I will not want to fight you again. But Reaper,
If I lose this match fairly, and am able to compete afterwards, I'll tell you this, I will want a re-match, and damn it, I will get one. Reaper, you think that I have no idea what to do. Well, Reaper I know a lot more than you think,
I will quickly put an end to your cockiness. Let those words rest in your mind
Reaper. Reaper, you say you have me beat before we step into the ring. Reaper, you make a coment of what I might say about this. Well, your right. It's not possible to even have me beat within the first 10 minutes of our match. Reaper, this will not be a normal walk in the park, or shall I say, graveyard for you.
For you will have to try and win. [ Scotty Lee Xtreme ]:Reaper, you are five days away from steping into the ring with me. You will be worried Reaper, that when you get into the ring with me, I will beat you. Reaper, don't let the fame you have get your head. Reaper, soon enough you will meet defeat. This time, not in IWF. Good Day.
