Sex Pistol ROLEPLAY (please critique)

26 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Guys, I'd really appreciate some constructive criticism on this one. Background to be found on POW's site, homepage www.angelfire.com/ut/POW/index2.html . (It is a crowded room, full of reporters, with a stage at the front. The reporters don’t know who has called this press conference. They speculate that it maybe Io, Nomad, or one of the other POW mid-carders. Then, the lights dim, a spotlight appears on the stage, and the Sex Pistol walks in, with Sarah by his side. There is loud applause from the reporters, who realise that this is a first in POW)Sex Pistol: Thanks, guys. Let’s get on with it.
1st Reporter: Mr Pistol, you’ve been in POW three or four months but this is your fist press conference. Why’s it taken you so long?
Sex Pistol: Personally, I feel that there are other ways for a wrestler to talk to his fans –and opponents- which I’m more comfortable with.
2nd Reporter: So why have one now? Sex Pistol: I wanted to dispel the rumours which have been going round POW about any problems I have. As you can all see, I’m in perfect condition, and for anyone who wants further proof, watch me win on Saturday!
3rd Reporter: Just one thing before we get on to your opponents for Saturday’s match. Will Sarah be at ringside with you? Is she your new manager? Sex Pistol: She will be coming out to ringside with me, but in a personal capacity only. She’s not my manager or anything, but she’s gonna give me some support now K-Man’s gone.
3rd Reporter: Yeah, what’s happened to K-Man? Is he gone for good? Sex Pistol: He’s gone alright. To tell you the truth, I think the thing with Cat at Y2Kade where our so called best friend sold us out affected him real bad. He’s been weird lately, as you know from seeing his last few interviews, and after the injuries he got on Saturday, I guess he decided enough was enough.
4th Reporter: Sorry to see him go? Sex Pistol: Definitely. He and Copy Cat helped me a lot when I first came in, and I’ll always be grateful to them for that. I’d team up with him again with a flash if he came back, but I don’t think that’s likely.
1st Reporter: So are you looking for a new tag partner? Sex Pistol: At the moment, I’m concentrating on Lightweight gold. Besides, there’s very few people in POW I’d trust enough to team with, after what
Manson did to me.
1st Reporter: Talking about Lightweight gold, were you serious when you suggested that Tim Russo could be a future Lightweight champ?
Sex Pistol: Why not? His win-loss record ain’t the greatest, but he’s improving, and he never looks like he’s getting his ass kicked. I got a lotta respect for Russo.
2nd Reporter: Have you got any respect for Doomsday? Sex Pistol: Sure. I think as a group and as individuals, Power and Glory command a huge amount of respect. I personally think that Doomsday’s one of the best three wrestlers in the POW, as is Kaanen, and Slaine Rodrik’s probably not far off either. I don’t like any of them, but you have to give them credit for what they’ve done in the few months they’ve been here.
2nd Reporter: And how about L-Mo? Sex Pistol: Now there’s someone I don’t respect. Like I said yesterday, if he’s saying he’s only interested in the TV title, then he shouldn’t be fighting us on Saturday. At least when Sager quit the Lightweight division, he quit for good, he didn’t come back every time the tile was up for grabs.
3rd Reporter: Well, that’s what you think of the others. Do you honestly think you’ll win?
Sex Pistol (nodding): Like I said yesterday, after Saturday night, you will all remember where you were when I was getting high!
(Pistol motions to Sarah, and they abruptly leave.) Thanks a lot, Sex Pistol
