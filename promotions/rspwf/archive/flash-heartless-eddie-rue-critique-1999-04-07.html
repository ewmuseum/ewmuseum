[Flash] "Heartless" Eddie Rue - Critique?

7 April 1999

[The camera focuses. It appears to be a black sinkhole. THe morsel of darkness spread about. Slowly...the image clarifies..as the sound of a drop of water dropping froma pipe is heard, the camera fixes on a hot water pipe. Rapped in bandages in tape, water slowly dips down. Behind the pipe, is seen a wall, cursed and sweared won using graffitti. Slowly, a rat, sprawls along the floor.
It stops, and the camera peers into it's red eyes. But ever so swiftly, and hand dips down and grabs its neck. The camera moves up along the mans arm, as it shows the face of the man. His face drawn out, and motionless. His eyes remain closed as he lifts the rat towards its face. The man opens up his left eye, peering onto the rat. The mans hands are heavily bandaged in white tape..., as he sways his head form side to side, observing every feature about hte mouse.]
MAN - "Most human scholars beleive that rats are the first survivors of the world. Living on for millions of years, they've adapted to the conditions.
However...,the rats themselves, although we do not listen, speak of difference. The rats beleive the humans sprang forth from large rats who adapted themselves in order to explore their vast frontier."
[The man motions his hand along the spine of the rat. Stroking it's fur gently. He swallow's in some air, getting ready to speak some more.] MAN - "Fear the rats and their followers..., for the world was created as the passenger to mischeif and minor evil. The rats caused the great plangue in europe, the rats wiped out the first inhibitors of america. The rats, I tell you...are far worse than their ugly appearance tell us."
[Bows his head. Hanging it down, away fromt he camera.] MAN - "Over the years...throughout the course of my own little history, I seem to have learned alot. I was stupid, foolish, and rather dumb at times....And 12 years of pain has taught me many things. Hardship, possibly being the largest.
I look at the scavangers, the men with hope, the men with a dream...men like the "True Playa" Mike Wilson, and my heart feels no remorse. Maybe if someone told me ahead of time; that the world really wasn't my oyster, and that the people I worked for didn't care about anything more than the all mighty...presidential face on a green peice of paper."
[Drops the rat down by his lap, as he puts two handson his forehead, wiping the sweat off of his head.]
MAN - "You might mistake me for ludicris Mike Wilson, but rest assured..., the one thing I am not, is crazy. I mearly speak of experience. Wise is the veteran, and tasty is the old wine. You can never call new, fresh wine, any better than the classics. Amontillado...S'aveur De Atillo...some of the finest wines. Such is the comparison between me and you. I breed excellence. And maybe you, too, can claim the same remark;- someday. But for now. Do not speak of me in the same light, share, or crop of goodness between me and you. Infact, if I may ask you do so. Do not ever put Mike Wilson and Eddie Rue in the same sentence,...phrase,...or even paragraph."
[Laughs pleasantly.] Eddie Rue - "Careless, like a child, will you be with me, and you shall speak your last words. Conceed or bleed. That is the name of the game, and don't think of changing the rules."
[Bites on his lower lip, as he calmy folds his hands together. Whispering some words the camera is repressed from picking up.]
Eddie Rue - "Oh...sure,...you can look at me, and think to yourself, 'Time heals all wounds.' And not worry about what I will do to your bones. But. What you should be thinking....rather asking yourself is--'Time may heel all wounds, but what heals time?' For I have a phantasmal amount of time to toy with each vessel of blood, each bone that breaks with a throw of a stone."
[Sinisterly laughs to himself, as he lifts one hand in the air, clenching his hand, and then putting up his pointer finger and shaking it tward the heavens.]
Eddie Rue - "Little dreamt could seem so cruel as waiting for the wings outspread, the jagged teeth, the burning eyes, and the dagger-claws that clench the nerves. Wilson...what maybe dreamt in the shortest dream...can wind up being your biggest nightmare..."
[Rue lets out a laugh of joyous conquer.] Eddie Rue - "...Sweet dreams...rest easy...and die unpleaseantly the next day." [Fade out.]
