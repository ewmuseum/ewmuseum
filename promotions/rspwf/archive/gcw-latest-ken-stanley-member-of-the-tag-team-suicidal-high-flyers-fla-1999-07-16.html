[GCW] Latest Ken Stanley (Member of The Tag Team, Suicidal High Flyers) Flash.

16 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ken Stanley [We are getting shots of a mall area. The camera catches Ken Stanley on camera and follows him into Toys R Us. Ken Stanley has a large garbage bag hoisted over his shoulder, and appears to be on a mission.
Ken Stanley seems to be humming a song ... from the movie "Con Air", I believe the lyrics are "Oh nothing makes me sadder than the ... agent lost his bladder on the Aiirplane." Ken Stanley talks to customers as he walks by them.]
KS: Good evening, bumblebeetuna. [Stanley spots the wrestling section and puts the bag down.] KS: You know, this is disgusting. Just look at this assortment of wrestling action figures. You got Brandon Simpson, hey is his action figure afraid to get in the ring with me also? Then you have The
Canucks, I wonder if their masks come off to reveal a team that is very dangerous?
[He picks up an action figure.] KS: What the hell is this? Kossuth? Didn't this guy die in a car accident? Or ... wait, maybe it was he witnessed a car accident and just had to leave. I don't know, but hey why does he have a figure if he ain't even in the GCW?
[He throws the figure down.] KS: Hrm ... ok. So now Ken Stanley or even Tommy Kain action figures here? They must be sold out again. I'll just give some of the ones I brought with me to the store.
[Stanley throws all of the Kossuth figures in a bag, and starts placing Ken Stanley and Tommy Kain action figures on the rack.]
KS: There you go. Wow, it's a good thing I came here when I did. There are probably tons of SHF fans out there waiting to buy these figures. [Stanley turns to a kid who is holding a Ted Bruce action figure in his hand.] KS: Hey, HEY! Kid: You talking to me? KS: Yes, yes I am. Kid: Well what the hell you want you goddamn piece of [BEEP] KS: Dear christ, you see what wrestling does? This kid is what, 10 and he just taught me a new word.
Kid: You want anything, or are you just here to waste my time? KS: Why don't you put that Ted Bruce action figure down, and buy a real action figure? I know you want the Tommy Kain and Ken Stanley action figures, but they were just sold out --- well good news, I just put more up.
Kid: Tommy who? Ken who? KS: Tommy Kain? Ken Stanley? You know, The Suicidal High Flyers? Kid: Oh yea. They Suicidally suck. KS: You must already have one. Funny kid, well enjoy your Ted Bruce action figure ... I hope it breaks, or you choke on it.
Kid: [beep] me. KS: Mushnick is right. [Ken Stanley walks out of the aisle to try and recruit possible buyers. He finds a gorgeous girl, around the age of 18.]
KS: Hey there! You a wrestling fan? Girl: You kidding? KS: No, no I'm not. Girl: Well you should be, you think I am some lowlife person who sits at home for two hours, and flips back and forth between shows?
KS: Ok then, before your 7 foot, 500 pound boyfriend comes out and asks if I'm hitting on his girl, I'm gonna leave.
[Ken Stanley walks over to a middle aged man with a VK t-shirt on.] KS: Hello, how are you today? Man: Just dandy, and yourself? KS: Alrighty. Hey, can I ask you something? I noticed you're here with a wrestling t-shirt on, well it's a VK shirt so it's sort of a wrestling shirt. But anyway, you not by any chance going to buy an action figure today are you?
Man: Well, as a matter of fact I'm going to buy some VK action figures today. Oh, I gotta go --- I gotta go get my wife before I can buy anything. Nice talking to you.
KS: Yeah, we'll see about that. [Stanley walks over to the VK action figures and pulls out a price gun.] KS: I very much doubt that piece of crap in sneakers will be able to afford these now. [The camera pans on the VK box that shows the price is $59.99. Stanley sees the VK fan coming over so he runs behind the aisle.] Man: 60 bucks for an action figure!? What the [beep]ing [beep] is this [beep]. What does this goddamn action figure give me a [beep]ing
[beep]job!?
Wife: Why hunny, calm down! Look at these two ... [She puts her glasses on.] Wife: Ken Stanley, and Tommy ... Kain. You don't have them yet right? Man: No, no I don't. C'mon hunny, let's get these and hurry home. I have a t-shirt to burn.
[They walk out of the aisle. We then see the camera go back to Stanley who is laughing.] KS: Sorry VK ... nothing personal, just business. [A worker walks to the wrestling toy aisle and sees Stanley lurking behind it, and asks him what he is doing.]
KS: Oh nothing, just looking. Worker: Excuse me ... is that a price gun!? KS: No, it's a uh ... well yes it is a price gun ... you see, um when
I meet somebody I like, and I think they look good I um ... I price them. Yes, that's it.
[Stanley comes out and meets the worker in the aisle.] Worker: You're going to have to give me that. KS: Fine. Hey, can I ask you something? [Stanley opens an action figure box of Destiny and Mama Johnson.] KS: Can you tell me how you can sell these action figures with a straight face? Worker: What do you mean? KS: Look! Destiny is a gorgeous young woman, and Mama Johnson is well ... Mama Johnson! Worker: Your point? KS: My point is ... well it's a question really. Why is Mama Johnson's breasts as big as Destiny's!? I mean, I could understand if maybe you had Mama sag, but no sag either. What's with this?
Worker: We don't make the figures sir. KS: She's not even an employee of GCW anymore I don't think. Why aren't these updated!? Worker: Couldn't tell ya. KS: Ok here's a different question for you. Peter Gyves, an announcer for GCW. Look at this action figure!
[He rips the box.] KS: He's ripped!? RIPPED!? What is that crap!? You think Gyves has ever seen the inside of a gym!?
Worker: I don't know man, I'm going to have to ask you to leave. KS: Fine ... Fine, I'll go! But I'm not gonna do what everybody thinks I'm gonna do and just FLIP OUT! All I wanna know is ... who's coming with me!?
Man: Hell, we're leaving anyway ... we'll go with you. KS: Thank you! [The man and wife walk up to Stanley at the exit of the store.] Man: [whispering] Can we meet any of the wrestlers? KS: Yea, sure. I hear Street is trying to get a couple of fans, I'll hook you up with him.
Man: Who? KS: Exactly. [The camera fades out as they walk the mall.]
