{RSPWF} Roleplay Critique

3 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up to the streets of Detroit. It is 2:47am on the first day of the new millenium and the huge party in the street is beginning to die down, the people still in the street are either just hanging around or too drunk to know where to go. The camera slowly turns around and focusses on a man as he begins to walk along the streets. The man is the "Innovator of Extreme" Tracker. He is wearing a pair of light khaki cargo pants, a black fleece pullover and a black bucket hat. He is walking down the street and he doesn't look happy........)
Tracker: So much for the end of the world......you know I really did think that the Apocalypse was coming. Not necessarily on the dawn of the new millenium but it all seemed to be going in the direction of the earth coming to a crashing halt. It started with
Retribution beating me and taking the Extremist strap.....the next logical step was hell freezing over........
Now Retribution, I thought I made it clear to you, but I'll say it again. As long as you hold onto that belt it means absolutely nothing. It is worth about as much as those 2 dollar cigars you smoke. You aren't a wrestler Retribution, you are nothing but a glorified freak of nature who probably forced somebody to sign him to the AWMC. You see "Retro", it's not that I'm scared to face you, in fact, I'd be glad to face you again, perhaps I'll get the Extremist title shot at the next PPV, but right now Retribution, I've got more important things to do..........
(Tracker walks by a house which obviously has a party going on inside it as the pounding bass of "Falling Away From Me" by Korn can be heard throughout the street.....)
So it's time for the second round of the Matsuda Cruiserweight tournament......Devon Case, you and me go back to the good old days of the NCWA. I've been keeping an eye on you as you went to various promotions, and I have to say, I was quite impressed.
I respected you because of what you did in the ring, but than you waltzed into the Motor City and you seemed to think your sh!t don't stink. That pisses me off Devon, nobody walks into Detroit and acts like they are better than everybody else. Maybe where you come from, where everybody around you are a bunch of pansy asses but here in Detroit it's a different game, here in Detroit you prove yourself before you talk sh!t. You have a chance this Wednesday my friend, a chance to prove yourself against the toughest motherf#&ker on the planet.....(points to himself with both his thumbs)....ME.
If my memory serves me correctly Devon, we've never fought against each other. Never in the NCWA, the SSW or the AIWA. You probably thought you were too good to fight me, hell, you probably still think that. Keep thinking it Devon......keep thinking it..........
(As he is walking, Tracker comes across a group of people circled around something. Tracker walks closer, followed by the camera and it becomes clear that there is a fight going on. Tracker decides to watch it along with the rest of the people. The two men are clearly drunk and having a hard time staying upright, let alone throwing punchs. One of the men punchs at the other but doesn't even come close, the momentum carries the wobbily man to the ground, he seems to have knocked himself out. The other man starts to celebrate like he just won a prize fight as some of the people help the other man off the ground. Tracker leaves and begins to walk down the street again....)
Tracker: Well that's New Year's for you, drunk people getting into fights, sorta like how Higor Rosa got started in the wrestling business......
Now for some reason I've been appointed as guest referee for this Detroit Street Fight at the upcoming Explosion. I'm no stranger to the black and white stripes, but something tells me
"they" don't want me to officiate this match cleanly, which is why I am going to call it RIGHT DOWN THE MIDDLE! The fans want to see me cause some havoc, so I'm gonna ENFORCE THE GOD DAMN RULES, yeah it's a no-rules, Street Fight but there has got to be something I could do to ruin their fun!. I've said it before, I don't give a crap what the fans want, from now on, I'm in this business for myself only.
Devon Case, you better be ready for the fight of your life because this Wednesday is going to be one of the hardest matchs you have ever fought.....
(The scene slowly fades to black as Tracker walks away......) /__ \_ __ __ _ ___| | _____ _ __ / /\/ '__/ _` |/ __| |/ / _ \ '__| / / | | | (_| | (__| < __/ | \/ |_| \__,_|\___|_|\_\___|_| The "Innovator of Extreme" The "Hardcore Icon" The "MAYOR Of Barbed Wire City!" Currently competes in: AWMC: 7-2-1, UEW: 1-0-0, Current UEW TV "titleholder" He doesn't need an RSPWF award to tell him he is the best god damn brawler in the world! "If god built a ladder to heaven, I would climb it and elbow drop the World" - Cactus Jack
