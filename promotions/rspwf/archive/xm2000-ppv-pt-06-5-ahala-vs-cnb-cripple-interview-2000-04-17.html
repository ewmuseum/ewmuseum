XM2000 PPV pt.06.5 Ahala vs. CNB (Cripple Interview)

17 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://xwfxtreme.batcave.net Crippler Calhoun - " Stacey, I came here to make sure the XWF is an equal oppertunity efederation and from what I have seen it is not, for example, there are no handicap ramps leading into the ring, how am I suppose to fight a match if I can't wheel up into the ring ! "
Stacey - " You mean your a wrestler ! " Crippler - " Huh, what did you think I was, another gimmick manager, hell no girl, i'm the real deal ! "
Cripple Calhoun lundges at Stacey while sitting in his wheel chair, Stacey leaps away and then put the microphone back near him again !
Crippler - " The bathroom are not handicap ready, the entry to the god damn arena is not easy to get to, they have a ramp, but it hasn't been maintained in years, and I haven't been able to wash my hands since I got here, and with out the proper bathroom for a person like myself, I made a bit of a mess ! "
Stacey creeps away again from Crippler Calhoun, as the interview fades into the ongoing Ahala versus Crazy Noodle Boy match !
The scene is in the arena hallways that go around the actual arena and rows of chairs, Ahala is thrashing on Crazy Noodle Boy's back as they fight down the hallway, she is ramming him into the walls and concession stands, but CNB gets in a toe hold and trips Ahala into a wall, smashing her forehead and cutting her open.
Crazy Noodle Boy then sprints down the hallway, assumably still after the belts, Ahala is up soon after and chases after him !
