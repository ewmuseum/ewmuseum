Free Agent: "The Cavalier" Tom Knight

3 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Since the recent demise of the Pinnacle Wrestling Promotions, my e-wrestler is in search of a new home. I started e-wrestling in April of this year. Here's my character's description:
Name: Tom Knight Nickname: Cavalier Height: 6'2" Weight: 243 lbs. Description: athletic build, with highly developed upper body. Dark blond short hair, brown eyes, clean-shaven.
Style: High-flyer, technical Finishing move: Excalibur (top rope flying lariat) Ring attire: full-length tights, right side silver with a black sword on the outside of the leg, left side black with a silver sword on the outside of the leg. Black boots, bare chest.
Manager: Guinevere (blonde, green eyes, 5'4", 130 lbs., well-toned body, wears classy gowns to the ring)
Entrance Music: Ride of the Valkyries, by Richard Wagner Background: The Cavalier has only been in the professional e-wrestling ranks a few short months, but made a strong impression during his time in the PWP. He did not win a belt, although he was up for his second TV title shot when the federation closed. He and his manager Guinevere were instrumental in the forming of the Young Lions, a stable of rookies and newcomers to the PWP that was considered a success. His best successes were against Sniper, a member of the Young Lions' biggest rival stable, the Pack. He faced Sniper twice, and defeated him both times, the second at the PWP's Anniversary Bash PPV.
I'm looking to join a fed that is booked, with RPs sent through e-mail rather than on a message board, and one that does not focus on hardcore wrestling.
The occasional gimmick match is okay, but I don't have any interest in a hardcore federation.
So if you're looking for a fresh face for your federation, and you've got a website I can check out, drop me a line. I'm eager to get some gold around the Cavalier's waist. I've held gold in two other federations with different wrestlers, but Tom and Guin are still eager to succeed.
