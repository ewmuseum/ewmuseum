Xtreme Hardcore Championship Wrestling (XHCW) Website: http://www.xhcw.com Owner: [name withheld] Description: The hardcore alternative to e-wrestling. XHCW is a federation born in 1998, which ended in late 2000. It was reborn in November 2001, and is better than ever. We were born out of Shock value. XHCW offers high-impact, technical, as well as the most hardcore wrestling available on RSPWF, and the Internet as a whole. Keynote Members: 'Same Ole Savage' Shawn Borders, Duff Man, Pico Flush, 'The Classic' John Sebastion, Scotty 'The Body' Fitzpatrick, 2Short, Adam Simon, Chris Cage, Frost, Jolted, The Dehibilitator, Chris Jacobson, The Juice, 'Sadistic' Steve Liermann, Moose. Federation Champion: The Juice Flash Show Descripition: XHCW Roleplay Packets are the Daily Digests compiled from XHCW's Yahoogroups system. Each week, 'Hawk Block' with Mike Hawk is aired. This is mainly for dark matches and spotlight-type interviews. Regular Show Description: XHCW runs a weekly card, known as Assault. This card runs on Saturday evenings, except in the event of a Pay Per View. Assault is then pre-empted, or reduced to a pre-show for the Pay Per View. PPV Description: Usually on the last Saturday of each month, XHCW offers a Pay Per View Extravaganza. Anything can happen at these events, and nothing is sacred, especially in the hallowed ground of the TRULY Xtreme warriors. News About XHCW
