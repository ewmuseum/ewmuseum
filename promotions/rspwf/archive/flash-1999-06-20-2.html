FLASH

20 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FROM : The Assassin To : Many Scw Superstars ( The scene fades into the back room of a building...Only one light is in the area, but it covers pretty much the whole room. There is a loude noise heard, then Suddenly it stops and the camera begins to roll around the room once again.A black target is then seen,With one Bullet hole right threw the middle of the target.Right in the heart area. The camera then picks up a large wooden table and a man standing behind the table.As we get closer we see the man is wearing a pair of millitary pants, Eye goggles, A baseball cap turned backwards and No shirt showing off his fairly muscular body. The song "War Stories" By
2pac Shakur is playing in the background.As the man standing behind the table is seen smiling, we now notice it is The
Bayou Assassin _JAMES MONROE_ Monroe has a huge variety of weapons surrounding him on the wooden table.Ranging from throwing knives,all the way to high powered assault weapons.
Monroe re loads his rifle, Then aims it at the black target once again...He shoots and once again hit's in the same spot.He set's the rifle down and with a devilish smirk on his face presses the button which brings the target back towards him. Once the target get's back to him he takes it off the rack and puts a new target up there.He then send's it back about 150 yards before stopping it. As Monroe looks over his choice of weapons,He finally decides on one and picks up a Rifle, Which appears to be a 270 Semi automatic rifle. He loads it, then props it up against the wooden table, before speaking to the
Southern Championship Wrestling camera crew.
"The Bayou Assassin" JM : This here is like a second home to me, and any of you
Southern Championship people with a brain can probally guess what my first home is.
( Smiles And for those without a brain,My first home just happens to be the ring which you see me in week after week....From the first day I got here when I beat the late great, God bless his old soul Sunflower God, Who was put out of his misery of being forced to wrestle here last week, Till last week when I beat Dirty
Dick LeBarron like what he is.An Old Out of shape red headed step child. If God was on earth,He would handle his business just like me. All of you try to make it appear like I am a rookie,Yet my skills in the ring would prove you are wrong.All of you say you are gonna defeat me,Yet I HAVE NO LOSSES on my record...Are we sensing a trend here guys ? You all seem to be wrong quiet often......What makes you think I am gonna be wrong ? Are you guys just still assuming ? I say I walk out at the end of this week in SCW, with two big performances under my belt and alot of momentum going into the brass knux title match with Dan Starr....I haven't been wrong yet,Don't expect me to start now.
( Monroe picks up the rifle which he placed against the table earlier and places it on the table....He then rubs what seems to be a smudge off of it, then Aims it at the target,He shoots and Once again hits in the heart area.
Monroe places the rifle back on the Rack and then turns around towards the camera once again.Except this time you can see him chuckling....
"The Bayou Assassin" JM : See that Grace and Wilson....I don't miss targets.....You two kids are in for a big suprise this week when you step into that ring and end up having to count the lights from cha' backside all night long. I have no clue why the Guys upstairs Made yall fight me, Except for you might of made a mistake somewhere along the path to where you are now.They have seen me work,They know I have the talent.....The question is,What in the hell did you two guys do for this to happen ? I am sure yall will run yalls mouth this week,So I will be looking foward to hearing the answer to my question boys. When I said Guys upstairs,I didn't just mean the SCW Booking commitee.I think you might of made the Other guy up there mad to and I am ya punishment...He meant it when he said "All sins shall go punished,Even those which you have asked for forgivness.".Wilson you got a belt, and a win over you will help me out...Grace you have held gold here before and are a member of the
I hate to say it top stable in the SCW right now....Looks Like I am in for a good week.
( Pauses and then walks out of the room,turning the light off on his way out. The camera follows Monroe out ,and they end up in a room filled with death stuffed animals.Ranging from Deer, to Lions from the fields of Africa.......He once again begins to speak...
"The Bayou Assassin" JM : Over the years this has happened, and the same can happen to yall.Don't push me to far near the edge or I might just go overboard.
Killing aint nothing new to me.....Don't look past me, and Don't even think about looking threw me..Focus on me, Prepare and beware, but boys.....This might be ya last week as a non handicap man on earth.....So cha' might not want to worry to much....If ya get the picture im trying to paint.
[ The screen fades with Monroe rubbing the fur of one of the Bear's he killed back in the early 90's]
