Critique-Delicious Dave RP

22 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
***The scene opens on a crowded Indianapolis street. There are hundreds of people walking down it dressed in light coats due to the mid 50 degree weather. Among the hustle bustle of people is a man we all recognize,
Delicious Dave Davidson. He has his hair tied back and his black Oakley’s on as usual. Along with that he is wearing a shirt that says “Chris Who?” obviously referring to Chris Douglas, and a pair of loose drawstring cargo khaki’s. He has a big grin on his face as he walks for no apparent reason.
He turns on to a less crowded street with an occasional passer by and gets ready to speak.***
Dave: Hello again all of you great TWL fans.......no wait I mean Delicious
Dave fans......wait I really meant to say Group Sex fans. Don’t worry I’ll address that later but I have so much on my mind. First of all with
Atlantic Armageddon coming up I’ve got a little bit of time off so I decided to go to the Bahama’s.......However I then realized how little the
TWL cares about me and my pay check which is much smaller than those of the less talented self-proclaimed main eventer’s so I just had my mom fly me home to Indianapolis instead. Now don’t get me wrong, there’s nothing wrong with Indianapolis I mean it is the home of one of the greatest stars in sports history, and oh yeah, Larry Bird lives pretty close too, but let’s face it it’s not a dream vacation location. However before I began talking about TWL’s lack of appreciation for the rave, I was beginning to discuss my, no not my match, but my matches at Atlantic Armageddon. You see fans it seems the TWL has finally realized who the fan’s wanna see, they’ve finally realized who the greatest star in the world today is, they’ve finally realized that when ya put Dave in the ring the ratings, and buy rates for that matter, soar. So come time for Atlantic Armageddon you great fans will get to witness a great day in TWL history as Delicious Dave not only takes the T.V, belt from the undeserving, no talent, load of garbage Adrian
Blades, but he also gets to take home the “Apocalypse” Crown in my old dirty Nike gym bag, it’s gonna be great.
Dave: This brings me to my first opponent of the night, the aforementioned worthless, no good, washed up, undeserving television title holder, him being Adrian “Where’s my Bengay” Blades. Adrian, I heard Rocky shouting for ya in the eighties but I see you must have broken that relationship off and turned to wrestling huh? Now coming into Armageddon this is my first shot at a TWL title of any sorts. This match is my shot to get a name for myself in the TWL. This is my shot to make my momma, and Group Sex proud to say they’ve got something to do with me. This is my chance to show all the Dave doubters, all five or six of you, that Dave truly is The Rave. You see The
Delicious One is gonna show that big, ugly, behemoth, known as Adrian
Blades what happens when you step into the ring with the greatest competitor in the world today. I’m gonna show Adrian what happens when Dave see’s something he wants, that being the TV title, you see Blades Dave always gets what he wants.......Well always unless it’s something like a good old time box of Cookie Crisp, but now they got that new more chocolatey taste crap that tastes just like Cocoa Puffs. You see Blades I’m pissed that they changed Cookie Crisp and since the manufacturers didn’t seem to care that I was Delicious Dave, and said they wouldn’t change the cereal back to it’s previous, tastier, more delicious form I’m gonna have to take out my aggressions on you.
Dave:Then later in the night for all of you Dave hungry fans I’ll be back in the ring, well this time a cage, to do battle with 19 less Delicious opponent’s in Approach of the Apocalypse. Now let’s see looking down the list of competitor’s I see a lot of names that strike my fancy. Now from what I see there are only four real contender’s, me and of course my fellow
Group Sex companions Empathy, Jeff Jacob, and Matthew Hutchinson with me having a slight edge of course. Now on this card I will have already captured the TV title, and Empathy will have already gotten the
Intercontinental Title, and Sex Sells will have defended the tag titles on what is sure to be a banner night for the newest, strongest, and best looking stable in the TWL, Group Sex. The rest of the competitors are just a step, and some are a couple steps, below the Group Sex tangent. You have guys like Adrian Blades, Billy Bob McAmallister, Raymond Knight, Sandman,
Wood, Chris somebody and Xero. Well let’s see I’ve already explained
Blades’s shortcomings. Billy Bob “Living in the past” McaMcmaster is obviously not up to my level. This guy keeps talking about how he used to dominate the TWL in 1947 before The Delicious One hit the scene and sent
Q-Ball runnin to Miami to live in a nice retirement center where he belongs. Raymond Knight has already been showed up by The Rave a countless number of times, and I’ll do it again at Armageddon. Sandman is dead or something. Wood party’s all night with Metal and the other elements and isn’t made of Oak so he’s a little worried about his durability in the ring against a great competitor like The Rave. Then Chris what’s his name who
I’ll get to later. And Xero is.....well I don’t really know what Xero is.
One thing is for sure however, on April fourth Group Sex will emerge as a group of champions like no other stable in the history of the TWL, or the world for that matter.
Dave: There we go I think I’ve said enough about Atlantic Armageddon, so that brings me to last week’s Massacre. Many of you booed me as I walked to the ring with those trashy humans Raymond Knight and Chris something or other. Now fans I can’t blame you for that, but when I turned on those two pieces of garbage and saw you fans light up with excitement and joy.....It was the greatest moment of my life **Dave wipes a tear from his eye** You see just days before Massacre I received a very special phone call from none other than, one half of the tag team champs Sex Sells, Matthew
Hutchinson. He said to me something along the lines of, “Dave you’re the greatest wrestler alive today and would probably kill me, and we need a fourth member to complete the greatest group of athletes ever assembled and how could we be that without you?” Well that’s pretty much what he said, I don’t remember the exact words. So I said to him that the psychiatrist had told me fans hated me as Delicious Dave, but Hutch talked some sense into me and made me realize that guy was nothing more than a loony toon and that to get the fans to love me once again I should join Group Sex, so that’s exactly what I did.
Dave: What really amazed me about it all was how stupid this Chris guy was, you know the guy that thinks he belongs in the main event. This guy came out and called me the weak link of the chain, the chain being him, me, and
Raymond Knight. He obviously hadn’t been at the previous week’s Massacre when I beat the tar out of Raymond Knight and showed him what happens when you mess with the greatest wrestler alive. I showed his hardcore, strait out of the clean South, what it’s like to truly be Rowdy and Blout It. I mean I must be Blout it if I showed him up, right. So now I guess Raymond’s back with his home slices in the hood, drinking a forty ounce or something listening to some Master L tapes, once again wishing he had half as much talent as me, Delicious Dave Davidson. Ya see Raymond ya don’t and neither does your little buddy that came to save the day.
Dave: Chris, there’s a lot of things you don’t do in this world. One, you never drink milk that expired a while ago. Two, you never feed the bears when there is a sign that says not to. Three you never piss on an electric fence. Four, you never assume you can land on your feet if you jump off the roof just because it didn’t hurt your G.I. Joe. Those four I know from experience, the other two would take a complete idiot to do, and they are.
You never team up with a gangster wannabe named Raymond Knight. And lastly you never, EVER, screw with the greatest athlete alive today, Delicious
Dave Davidson. You see Christopher we know you’ve done two of those things and they’re gonna cost you big time. You see Chris I don’t care about your past, or your present, but I sure as hell can tell your future. And that future involves getting the crap kicked out of you by the most electrifying man on the face of the planet, me, The Delicious One. Chris you can take your past titles and fame and throw em into a river, because quoting a line from Happy Gilmore, You’re In My World Now Grandma.
Dave: Finally, many of you out there have been asking me, Dave why did you join Group Sex. To those people I say Look Around. Look at what Group Sex has. We already have the greatest tag team in the sport today in Sex Sells.
We also have two soon to be champion singles wrestler’s in myself and
Empathy. Look at what the rest of the TWL has compared to us. We are the elite. We are what the fans are paying to see. We are the main event. We are the greatest group of athlete’s to ever join together. When great tandems come to mind you think of, The New York Yankees when they had greats like Mantle, DiMaggio, Ruth, and so many other greats. You look at the Green Bay Packers during their glory days. The Bulls that won 5 or 6 titles. And you think of Group Sex with 4 of, if not the greatest athlete’s in the world today. The TWL is ours for the taking, and take we will. Not one of the TWL cowards has what it takes to step up to this line-up. Q-Ball bailed before we could get to him, but there’s plenty left........
***Suddenly a couple of Japanese descent gets close to Dave. The male is wearing a pair of jeans and a light Pacer’s coat. He has black hair and appears to be in his mid-thirties. The woman is also Japanese looking and also has black hair. She is wearing a pair of jeans and also appears to be in her mid-thirties with a red coat on. They glance at Dave then the man gets ready to speak.***
Man: Hello, are you Delicious Dave Davidson? Dave: Yes I am, you all must be tourists huh? Lady: No, actually we’ve lived in Indianapolis our whole lives..... Dave: So what part of Japan are you all from? Tokyo? Man: No, we’re from right here in Indianapolis. Dave: So do you all speak any English? Lady: We’re talking to you right now, of course we speak English. Dave:So you do understand English. Man: We’re from America of course we understand it. Dave: Wow! So do most people from Japan understand English, or is it just you guys? Lady: We wouldn’t know, we’re from America. Dave: Hey, I got an idea, how about I show you guys around a little, you know show you all my country.
Man: Look, it’s our country too. Just because you’re of European descent doesn’t mean I think you’re a German tourist.
Dave: No, No, me-a from Indiana. Lady: Yes, and so are we. Dave: No you all are from Japan, you must be rusty on your English. So do you want me to start showing you all around or what?
Man: No, we’ve lived here all our lives, we know where we’re going. Dave: Look I was just offering, I mean since you are from Japan I thought you might want some help. I’m sorry jeez jump down my throat a little.
Lady: WE’RE NOT JAPANESE! Dave: No, see you are Japanese, are. You really should practice your English. ***The couple walks away with looks of frustration mixed with a little anger.*** Dave: **To the “tourists” as they walk off** HAVE A NICE STAY IN AMERICA! **Back to the camera** Boy those tourists sure are strange. So cameraman you wanna go get a burger or something I know this great place....... ***The camera fades out as Dave starts to ramble about food.*** David handler of........ Delicious Dave Davidson 5-1 in the TWL The future of E-Wrestling
