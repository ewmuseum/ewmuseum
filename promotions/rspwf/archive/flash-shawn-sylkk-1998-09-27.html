Scene: Shawn Sylkk's Long Beach, CA home Time: Approximately 12:30 AM Pacific Time Appearance: Baggy black Ralph Lauren Polo denim jeans. Grey, white, and black
Guess t-shirt. Black Nikes with white swooshes on all sides. 24K gold chain.
Hollywood Polo gold watch on left wrist. A gold ring on his right index finger with a diamond in the middle.
Conditions: 69 degress indoors. [The camera focuses on Shawn Sylkk as he walks from his kitchen to his living room. He keeps pulling up his pants as they begin to fall every few steps.
Sylkk finally makes it to his living room and grabs his Universal remote. He turns on his Kenmore sound system and pushes a few buttons. Suddenly, "Fuck the World" by 2Pac plays throughout the room. Sylkk sits down on his black leather sofa. His facial expression is ice cold as he seems unlike his cocky self. He snickers as the light bulb that's lighting the room begins to flicker. He brings his hands up over his bald head and drags them down his face. Sylkk begins to speak in a deeper-than-usual voice..]
'STS' SHAWN SYLKK: "I have quite a few things to address. First of all, I'm gonna start off with our President, Ralph Julevic. Ralph, as far as I'm concerned, you can go straight to Hell. You want me to go inside the ring against Triple X and Movie Guy and you're pullin' all your puppet strings backstage to make sure that I lose? Hold the Hell up. I make you the most money pal. If it weren't for my ability to put on a damn good match and my ability to create heat, no asses would be in your seats, and you would be on a curb somewhere in New York holdin' up a 'Will Work For Food' sign. Ralph, quite simply, if it weren't for me, this federation would amount to crap and would have folded within a week, just like all your other feds.
Then, we have the Movie Guy. You pride yourself on bein' a 'big star' in
Hollywood. I'm not Charlie Sheen, but around these parts, I am the Hot Shot.
Okay pal, let me address some of the things you said. Incompetent idiot? Such big words for such a little man. Movie Guy, you have it all wrong. I'm not an incompetant idiot, as you'd like to believe.
'For if knowledge is power, than a God am I.' -Jim Carrey, Batman Forever. Do I know who you are? Of course, I do. You're the bozo that's gonna fall to the STS splash come this Monday night. Quite simply, the Sylkksta has you in check like Busta Rhymes. Movie Guy, I couldn't care less if you are the king of b-rate porn, the fact of the miggity-matter is, you have don't anythin'
worth a damn in this business yet. In the JPW alone, I've won the Television and Tag Titles. The TV Title? I threw it into the crowd and now that corporate buttkiss, Marvel, has it by some miracle of God. The Tag Title? James 'STB' Pillow got good taste of the belt before he lost them for us.
As for wrestlin' entertainment, you haven't entertained anyone, even remotely, here in the JPW. Oh yeah, your marquee analogy was way off. You see, no one's heard of ya pal. It's a well-known fact that even Ralph Julevic admits to me sellin' the merchandise around here. I carry the JPW on my back buddy boy and you'll find out why come Monday night in this little three-way-dance. You requested a match, a much undeserved match, Monday night against Triple X.
'Your request is not unlike your lower intestine. Stinky and full of danger.' -Jim Carrey, Ace Ventura: When Nature Calls
Your request met one minor snag, Movie Guy. That snag is me. You see, I put myself into the match, just because I can. Because I sell the merchandise that gives you the zeros on your paycheck.
Movie Guy, stop foolin' yourself with illusions that you're goin' to win. Stop pretendin' that you have a chance of beatin' me. 'Don't torture yourself, Gomez. That's my job.' -Angelica Houston, Addams
Family. Movie Guy -- what do your chance at bein' World Champion, your career after this Monday night, and your chance at bein' someone worth a damn here in the
JPW have in common?
'They're all dead. They just don't know it yet.' -Brandon Lee, The Crow. As for you not askin' Ralph Julevic for anythin'? Um, you asked him for any kind of match with Triple X retardo. Onto you drawin' a sell-out this Monday night? You couldn't draw a straight dot-to-dot line, much less a sell-out in an arena that seats over 10,000 people. If they're gonna show up, it's gonna be because of _ME_.
And you go onto ramble about how our President is a good buddy of yours. How sweet. Maybe you two can go into the forest together and pick up flowers until you're famished, go home, and have a great night of passionate sex with each other until your mommy comes in and finds Ralph's My Little Pony panties on your head!
As for me askin' him to show me the goods? Heh, the goods. Show me the goods? I have a better quote.
'Show me the money!' -Tom Cruise, Jerry Maguire. That's the only thing I ask Ralph to show me, sorry to disappoint your testostrone-filled fantasy. As for you gettin' a lot of women? I guess one date a year with a rubber woman would be considered a lot for you. Then again...
'That's a matter of opinion, and I don't give a <bleep> about yours!' -Joe
Pesci, Get Shorty.
You see, Movie Guy, there's one thing I'm sure about though. I can never be as good as you. You know why that is? It's because I'm so much better than you, I'm so much quicker, agile, stronger, smarter, and athletically gifted than you that I'd have to go into the biggest slump known to man to fall into the category of just bein' 'good.'
Movie Guy, sorry I must disappoint your little boyish hopes and dreams by havin' the biggest star this side of the equator stop talkin' about you, but I must move on. But, I'll leave you with a little partin' quote:
'The Almighty says he can get me outta this, but he's pretty sure you're <bleep>ed!' -Mel Gibson, Braveheart. Now, onto our Paper Champion, Triple X. How are ya feelin' chump? It seems like we have a mime for our World Champion as of late. Triple X, just remember
-- three times I've had you beat, only once have you gotten lucky and gotten help from Ralph. Triple X, you're always and forever in my shadow. Don't you forget why I'm...
...SMOOTH... ...AS... ...SYLKK!!! Ralph, Movie Guy, Triple X, just... ...LEARN... ...TO... ...LOVE... ...IT!!!" [Scene fades to black...]
