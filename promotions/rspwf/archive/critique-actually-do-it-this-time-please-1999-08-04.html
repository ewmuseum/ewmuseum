[CRITIQUE] Actually do it this time, PLEASE!!

4 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, I've been gone for a month and this is my return flash and I would like someone to take a look at it to see if I got rusty while I was gone. I would really appreciate it and thanks in advance.
[name withheld] Handler of "Personification of Perfection"/"The Hammer" Alex Cote Record: 56-6-5 Feds: ICW, WET, GSWP, NBW ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ [Fade in.....] [SCENE: Greensboro, North Carolina. TIME: 4:30. The scene opens on the sidewalk next to the busy street in downtown Greensboro. The cameraman steps out of the ICW van onto the concrete. He turns to his left to see in the window of a small gym. The camera man walks inside and almost immediatly everyone stops what they are doing and watches him walk into the room. Seconds later they continue with their business. The camera walks forward into the center of the room to a weight bench which is currently occupied by ICW's own
"Personification of Perfection" Alex Cote.]
[APPEARENCE: Alex's attire consist of a pair of black jogging pants with a white tang top and a pair of Nike Air high tops. Hanging around his neck is a gold chain and on his right hand is a gold high school ring with a diamond stud. His hair is soaked with sweat and his face looks rough and rugged possibly do to a lack of shaving.]
[Alexs apparently finishes a set. He sets the bar back and takes a breath. He sits up and grabs a towel and wipes the sweat from his brow. He looks up and rolls his eyes after seeing the camera looking directly at him.]
"PERSONIFICATION OF PERFECTION" ALEX COTE: Ya know, I am getting really sick and frig'n tire of walking around and everytime I open my eyes and seeing you lowlife cameramen following me around like a bunch of blood hounds. Give me some damn peace dammit! I have enough crap without having little bastard's like you leech off me!
[The camera man begins to turn around. Alex grabs him and faces the camera toward him once again.]
"POP" ALEX COTE: Did I say you can leave?! NO! Stand right there and listen to what I have to say! I'm spending all my time preparing for my three way dance at Summer Sizzler. I am the only man on the card who was on last years card. At last years, I had a three way dance for my television title and I got screwed out of it and ever since then this company has been playing me like a flute, BUT NO MORE!! Lighting will not strike twice at the Ice Palace and I will walk away carrying twenty pounds of Continental gold around my waist.
[Alex stands up and begins to wipe himself down with the towel.] "POP" ALEX COTE: Let's start off with "The New Legend" Kenneth Morlock. Nothing but a big mouth rookie who _THINKS_ he is the best, boy, what a tired cliche. All you do is bitch and moan about this, that, and the other thing, well, stop it and don't cry to me Argentina because if you think I give half a shit then you're a lambchop short of a mixed grill. Morlock, you're nothing but a joke, and a bad bar room at that. You've been here for a while and after all your dreaming and running your mouth, you still haven't been able to get yourself any gold and if you keep going for it by going up against me then gold will forever be outta your reach because if you get through that thick skull of yours and think back then you will remember that in our last meeting I am the one that left with one more win on my record!
[Alex drapes the towel around his neck and makes slowly his way to the locker room.] "POP" ALEX COTE: Now, Masters, you have made quite and inpact here in the ICW. You stepped in and have dominated the competition but you have to open your eyes and see reality for what it really is and notice that you haven't taken on a man the same caliber as the man who stands before you. So far you have yet to gain a loss but you better get ready for a rude awaking because the gravy train stops here. I'm here to put the brakes to this joy ride and bring you back from cloud nine back to the land of the living. I'll give you credit for what you have accomplished but I'm no longer going to sit by and watch you indulge the luxeries of being a champion. You better enjoy the Continental belt while you have it but just make sure you keep it nice and shing because I want it to look properly for when I get it! Summer Sizzler is the end of your reign and the beginning of a new era. The sand in the hour glass is falling and soon the night of Summer Sizzler will arrive and that is when your time will expire!
[Alex opens the door to the locker room and walks inside. He sits on the bench next to the lockers and looks down to the floor.]
"POP" ALEX COTE: I've spent all my time rap'n my jaws about Summer Sizzler but that is down the road and I have to stop and look forward to this Friday at
Fallout for my shot at the North American title against Full Blood Killah.
FBK, I have a score to settle with you little man. Last time we steps into the ring, your sorry ass walked away with the disqualification victory. But this time there is more at stake then just pride, theirs some gold. I'm taking all my time know to make sure that this Friday I walk in a man and leave a champion. You're nothing but a mediocre champion and you don't deserve a strap here in the ICW. You don't have what it takes to be a man over me and this
Friday you won't have the endurance, the dexerity, or the determination to leave then winner this time. I'm not the man I was last tme, I'm better.
[Alex opens up his locker and puts his towel on the bench.] "POP" ALEX COTE: FBK, I've been beat up, stitched up, starved, wrestled hurt and that shows you I've paid my due here in the ICW and it is time I get paid for my efforts and coming this Friday the dept will be paid in full. You've also been a premiere wrestler here in the ICW but you will never, _NEVER_ be more than me. You better just spend your time thinking about Hidden Essence and the International title at Summer Sizzler because this match will be nothing more than a cake walk for me. Friday is going to be a night to remember and I will have the distint pleasure of trimming your family tree.
Friday night, FBK, is the night of Alex Cote and I will show you and the entire world why I am the "Personification of Perfection"....
.....AND .....YOU'RE .....NOT!!! "POP" ALEX COTE: Now, get your sorry ass out so I can change! [Alex pushes the cameraman out.] [Fade to black.....]
