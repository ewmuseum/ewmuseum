Advertising an Efed

I was not the original author of this. Can't remember who was either. Hehehe...yes, ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls and children of all ages...(ooh...a borrowed quote! *G*), it's time once again to do an essay (since I'm sorta stuck on my card at the moment, waiting for a strat that I approved a late delay for..and I have no idea why I did it)..... So..... here" That's a number of posts I've seen for new e-feds. Uh, you have a webpage, so you're the best fed on the net? I've got news for you. much like a roster doesn't make a federation, neither does a webpage. A webpage is one part of the formula, and it's not necessarily the biggest part of the formula. Tell the handlers a little something about the federation. Why should we visit your website? Say something as to what we have to look forward to. Are your guys competitive? Are they having fun? Give us a sample of the cards you produce...tell us a _little_ something. If you build it, they will come...but only if they know at least a few bits and pieces about it. ADVERTISEMENT #4: The Handbook... I've come to the conclusion that some people don't know where a middle ground is. not as though that's a bad thing always... but I've seen a number of people put up handbooks as incentives to check out their organization, and what do I see in the handbooks? Sections on strats and RP's that have a strat or two and a RP or two... That tells me one of two things (IMO). You're looking for a specfic type of RP style handlers, or you don't think that people know what they're doing when they sign up. It shouldn't take a 50 line RP to explain what you're looking for in your RP'ers. I can understand a short paragraph or two explaining what RP's are and what Strategies are. But to put in one or two of each is in my opinion overkill. If you do it for the first reason (to find specific style Rp'ers), you're locking out a lot of potential members. However, i don't think that most fed-heads limit the style of RP'ers, so that makes me believe that the majority of the time, the RP and Strat sections are for the second reason. You don't think that the reader knows what they are doing. If you don't think they know what they are doing, why are you asking for them? Now don't take that the wrong way... I'm not saying that you should turn people down because they're coming in pure rookies. If that happened, the first e-fed (whichever one can claim that priviledge) would never have been born. it just appears to me that the producer of the "overkill" handbook (and I know not all Handbooks do this) just has no faith in a player coming in and learning the hobby as he goes, and is trying to A) scare them off, or B) mold them in their own exact image. I know that's a broad statement, but it's my personal opinion. If you want to make a handbook, it should cover the general who's (staff), what's (general info about fed... booked/simmed, length of existance, etc.) where's (website/email/contacts), when's (card dates/opening dates/deadlines) and why's (the plug..why you and not the others)... the "how"s are something that players are all going to differ on, and therefore should not be a part of the information book, simply cause it's subjective to each person's viewpoint. ADVERTISEMENT #5: The Spammer Ladies and gentlemen, just because you print up the same advertisement x number of times and give it a different header on each posting doesn't mean that a reader is going to be x number of times interested in your federation. If you've got a good federation, it shouldn't take 3 plugs an hour to get my attention and interest in your fed. If you're going to take that extra time to do multiple posts, why not do it a better way, and write up a handbook (not an NOVEL) that I can peruse instead? It'lll get my attention much more readily than 15 three line posts about "the best fed on the net". Or if you're going to spam, make your advertisements different. That much I'll give the GNW when they did their spam-ads. Each advertisement was different (in a way), but each one should be made to cater to the crowd you're looking for. And finally... ADVERTISEMENT #6 The Superlative fed.. These are the feds that promise the most excitement, the most interest, the most fun each and everytime. You're the fedhead or staff member usually, so yes, it's your opinion that you've got the most exciting fed out there, and well you should believe that. But don't plug it over and over and over...(note: Ad's #5 and #6 are often squeezed together). Let the viewers/readers decide what is and is not exciting.... personally, the fan report idea that was brought up is a great way to see what others think, and then you can base your future advertisements on that. I know that many of you will disagree with some of the above, and you're more than welcome to..(I expect to see the "overkill" one get the most debate, but we'll see.) Anyone who wants to add a fed type I didn't include "subtle ads" for instance, (you know the ones that plug their own fed in the middle of a debate, saying that in the XXX we do things like this... (then they give their example), and then suggest you take a look at it. Those do not seem to bother me as much, nor do the ones that put their federations in their .sig files. Because in their own way, they're keeping the current discussions up, and still getting a plug in for their federation. And heck, I know they're probably interested in what is going on in other organizations by adding to the debates.
