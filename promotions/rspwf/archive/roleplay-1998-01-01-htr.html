Roleplay...

1 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
("Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh is in the locker room sitting around on New
Year's Eve. He looks around and begins to speak.)
"Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh: "They say that holidays are supposed to be happy. I look around this locker room. You know what I see? I see nothing bad sadness. You know, I am sick and tired of busting my a-- for no reason. I am here. I am ready for action. People here think that they are all great because they are superstars. I'll tell you what I think of you. I think that you all suck. You are probably good on the outside, but you suck on the inside. I have the spirit and the determination to keep me going through the thick and the thin. I am able to withstand enormous amounts of pain. I take that pain, and I come back the next day. I come back and bust my a-- again.
'Oh, HTR, it's the holidays! Be happy!' F--- you! I am not going to be happy! Happiness is no longer inside of me. 'Here comes a new year.' Oh really? Who gives a damn? It is just another day that I kick someone's sorry a--, get my pay check, and go home. Some people say that this is supposed to be fun. This isn't fun for me. I take this seriously. It's all that I have.
It's all that I can do. So I have to be good at this in order to survive. You guys that may face me in the future should have a 'New Years Resolution'. You should pray to God that you don't have to get in the ring with me. You should bow down to the man that stands before you and say, 'Oh master, please don't hurt me.' I couldn't care any less about your pleas and your requests. I am not some figurehead or puppet that goes around and brags about my accomplishments. I just take credit for what I have done. As I said before, I do my job, get paid, and go home. I don't relax. I train. I am a machine. I keep going day after day with the same schedule and work hard for what I accomplish. Now, it is time for the big boys. I am sick and tired of being belittled for things. It is time for me to show my stuff. What will you people do? You may just look at me and laugh, but I will turn that laughing into crying after you suffer the pain that I inflict upon your body! What will you do? It is easy. You get in the ring with me, you lose, you get beat down, and you get humiliated. I don't need fame to get me through this business, kicking your a--es will be good enough. There are people in the wrestling world that really make me mad. One thing that you don't want to do is make me mad. There is some punk that is well-known named the Admiral. What in the hell is this? Is this some kind of armed service? 'I am the Admiral. I am back by popular demand.' No you aren't. You are back by your own demand. Hey
Admiral, if you want some of this, come get some. You can either put up or shut up. That's right! I am calling you out you pathetic piece of trash.
When I speak, people listen. The people that laugh fall in a feeble attempt of dethroning the king from his reign. I am sure that you have some talent, but I don't care anymore. You make me sick, and I will shut you up. You name the time, place, federation, I'll go there and kick your sorry butt and teach you to respect the man that stands before you. Make fun of me all you want. It doesn't get you anywhere. People say that actions speak louder than words. I just took some action. I called you out! Don't be so surprized. I though that I was just some idiot with no talent. I just babble on about everything.
I prove my points. I talk for the sake of people's lives. If people take my warning into consideration, they might just be able to escape with their lives.
The key word there is might. That leaves you and me. This will be settled one way or another, Admiral. Oh yes, we shall see who the better man is. Do you see any of that violence going on here? Do you see me babbling on about something that you are not interested in? Now, I am talking to you. You suddenly have interest. What a fool you are. You have the audacity to come out and criticize everyone that you don't like for pointless reasons.
Remember, this is supposed to be fun. If and when we face off, I will show you that this is not fun. I will show you that I take this seriously. I fight for what I have left. Do you think that I will be a push over? I bet you do.
Taking me lightly is something that you shouldn't do. If you beat me, and that is a big if, I will shut up and respect the better man. If I win, I want the respect that I deserve. Are you willing to actually respect someone, Admiral?
Are you willing to face being defeated by a man that you said had no skills? I say that you wouldn't be able to take it. It would eat away at you for a long time. The famous Admiral getting beat by a virtual 'unknown'. It is just something that you will have to live with. One match, Admiral. I am asking for one match. It doesn't have to be extreme. It doesn't have to be special.
Just you and me in a regular match. What do you say? Are you up for the challenge? I am not like the many others that come out and say that they are the best ever. I am who I am. I do what I do. And what I do to you may not be so nice. You will remember the 'Human Torture Rack' Ian Walsh. Win, lose or draw, you will remember who I am. How long will you remember? Days...
Weeks... Months... Years.... No! You will remember who I am for an eternity! You will look into the mirror and you will know that I am a tough man. You will know about how I back up what I say. It is time to cut the crap and get into the ring. May the best man win. Win if you can. Survive, if I let you."
(Camera fades to black.)
