Gunn catches his fist and squeezes. They guy falls to his knees and hits Gunn in the balls. Gunn lets go of the guys fist and cups his private. The guy uppercuts Gunn right in the jaw sending him into another table. The people at that table get up and start an all out brawl. Gunn stands up but ducks just in time to miss getting hit by a chair. He finds the guy who started everything and starts wailing on his ass. Gunn throws several punches to the guys face.
He stops for a second and notices the guys out cold. He drags the guy to the door but before he gets there a man slams a beer bottle over his head knocking him out. When Gunn gets back conscienceness he notices he's been tied up. He looks to both sides and on his left is the man who started the fight. He's still out cold. Both of his legs have been bound together and his arms are tied to some pole above his head. The man next to him starts to awaken. He picks his head up and panicks after seeing he's tied up.
Guy: Where am I? Who's there? Somebody! Somebody please help! Gunn: Shut your ass man. Guy: Who are you? Gunn: I'm the guy you threw the punch at. Well buddy, we're both tied up so why don't we try and figure out a way to get outta here?

Matt: All I wanted was to talk, but no, your too busy for that. Gunn: Why the hell do you have to go and tie me up, though? Matt: I was sent here to do this. Gunn: To do what? Matt: To break you legs or, frankly, just to put you out of commision for a while. Gunn: Who sent you? Matt: That's just gonna be my little secret, Little Dick Gunn. At this Gunn goes crazy. He rips the rope that had his hands tied together. Matt is astonished and gets frozen stiff. Gunn grabs him buy the thought and lays one good punch on him, knocking him out. He looks at the guy still tied up and let's him go. The guy runs out the door like he's just seen a ghost.
Gunn grabs Matt again and drags him through the door. The door opens up and leads to a long hall. At the end of the hall is an open door with daylight coming out of it. He takes the man through the doors and notices that it led to the top of the Shereton. He takes the man over to the edge of the roof and slaps him. The man kinda comes back to his senses and jumps as he notices he wasn't dreaming.
Gunn: Who are you!!?!! Guy: I'm not telling. Gunn picks him up and dangles him over the edge of the building. Guy: My names John!!! Gunn: Who sent you!!?!!
