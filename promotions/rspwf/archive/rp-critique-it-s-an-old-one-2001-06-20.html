[RP Critique] - It's an old one. :)

20 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys, I've been cleaning out my hard-drive, and I came across an old RP from my Ethan
Van Ryan character. All I'm asking is for some views on it from the group
(don't worry, I won't fire back at you. :)) and some points where I could improve, or you could just rave about how great it is. :)
Anyway, for some backround info on it: Ethan is involved in a fued with the much bigger Terrence Steele, and even though their fued is not for a title, it's taken the main event of many shows just for the fact of how crazy their matchs are. Ethan can't seem to beat
Steele, but Steele can't hold him down.
Anyways... Here is it: ------------------------------- [The scene opens up in your normal everyday apartment building. Everything about it is what you would expect as a "normal" place where someone would live. The only thing that isn't quite normal is the wrestling decor. This man is a fan, wait, a _huge_ fan of the sport of wrestling. The camera then turns towards a man that each and everyone one of you watching know, Ethan Van Ryan.]
EVR: Look at me. The _epitome_ of health, if I may borrow my friends term. Granted, Steele, you put me through hell and took be back kicking and screaming. Yea, I'll admit it, you
_did_ kick my ass. But, Steele, just remember,.. Remember what _I_ put _you_ through. You _know_, that you and I put on some of the greatest matchs in HWA's short history, and as far as
I am conserned, we will continue, until one of us, whether it be me, or [cough] most likely [cough] you.
[He snickers and he rubs his unshaven face, then running his fingers through his hair, which, by the way, is almost back to it's natural brown color.]
EVR: Heh, one look at me, would make you think I let myself go, unshaven, mixed color hair,.. The thing is, I haven't had enough time to worry about trife matter like that.. I have an obsession now, Steele, and that obsession is you.
It sounds strange, but it's true. Steele in this one month off, I have leanred everything I need to know about you, I've seen you train, I know what you are all about. I am ready Steele, ready to go to hell and back one more time just to prove to everyone watching, you _ARE NOT_ the man that everyone gives youi credit for.
[Ethan stands up, and walks towards the camera, so as if it seems that he is right in your face.]
EVR: Steele, as far as I am conserned, I have no friends in this match.. Not even Dillon. This "6 Man" match is only about two people. Me, and you. Stevens, Floyd, Bombay, McCain, they don't matter. Steele, me and you are going to hurt each other and _I_ _can't_ _wait!_
[The camera fades.] -- Josh - Wow, that was a short one.
