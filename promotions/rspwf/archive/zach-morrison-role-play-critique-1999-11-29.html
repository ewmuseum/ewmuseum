Zach Morrison Role Play [Critique]

29 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera cuts to after Zach Morrisons last humiliating loss against Brian King. He has his head in his hands, and he's talking to himself. People are walking by and laughing at him as he ignores them. All of a sudden a little kid comes up to him...]
Little Boy: Hey Mr.....can I have your autograph? Zach: What? You want my autograph? I mean you really want "Sweet" Zach Morrison's autograph? Little Boy: Wait a minute....you're the guy who lost like a little girl tonight huh? Zach: WHAT!?!?! Little Boy: AHAHAHA you're nothing but a loser. [The kid kicks Zach in the leg and runs away. Zach screams and his hopping up and down on his foot. He rubs his shin...]
Zach: Comeback here you little punk....unlike the UWC I'm not gonna job to you. I'll beat you so bad that you're going to wish that this was just another day at Gymboree. Damn it...who does that kid think he is? I mean I lost very nobley tonight, and I shouldn't be ashamed at all. You see unlike Brian King I do not need to get over by winning matches. My true talent is on the microphone. King will go on and on about how he beat me, and how I have no right to say anything. Well the fact is that beat me once shame on me....beat me twice....hahaha it'll never happen. Now I'm not asking for a reamatch. Brian
King....I'm telling you that the next time we face I will be the champ and you'll be the challenger.
There is something in this fed called the food chain. You see Brian King thinks that he's that big Bald Eagle that pearches up on top and eats the little mice that run around. The ones that scurry for the cheese, or the ones that eat those tiny little bugs...also known as jobbers. Well you might be that big Bald Eagle, and you might be proud of that...but you're a rare bird. You know why?
I'll tell ya why. You're a rare bird because you get eaten up all the time by the Lions that rome the woods. You got it, and you know that I am the Lion that romes the woods. I walk around and I'm the king of the forest...not the bear, not the racoon, not even the Owl....yeah he might know how to get to the center of a Tootsie Roll Pop, but he doesn't know squat about being the UWC World
Champion or getting a win over a loser like you.
Hell, right now I don't know what that is like. Although in good time I will, for Zach Morrison will not be held back. My teachers did in for 3 years, my mom did it from seeing women, and you're not about to do it to me. I will be held back by no man, women or child....It is my turn to shine, and this ol' light of mine...I'm gonna let it shine! You hear that _Brian King_...you come out and you talk about how you're the man to get the job done. How you're going to beat me and this and that, and I am sick and tired of this 80's Mic Work that you spew out. If I wanted to watch that kind of trash I'd hear old interviews with
"Iron" Mike Sharpe.
I hope you realize in that thing you call a heart that you can't handle "Sir
Sweetness", you can't handle "Mr. Bologna Pogna", and you sure as hell can't handle the "King Of India"....and no I am not Indian. I believe that everyone should eat cow...hell Brian....that is what your dad calls it during foreplay.
Eating a cow...anyway....I believe that your time is drawing near, and the fact is that you're running out of ink.
Your nickname is "Fantastic", but I have yet to see anything "Fantastic" that you can pull off. Was it that "Fantastic" punch to the head you gave me, or how about that "Fantastic" job you did of carrying the match? I've carried oranges better than you can carry a match. I know for a fact that you're interview skills are less than "Fantastic"....as a matter of fact when I hear you come onto the TV I think it's a "Fantastic" time to take a bathroom break or an even more "Fantastic" time to clip my toenails. You're like the Pokemon' that sings all the time...what's his name? Oh...AH HA! Jiggly Puff....."Fantastic" Brian
Jiggly Puff. I'm going to call you that from now on, because every single time
Jiggly gets on the mic people fall asleep.
What a awesome duo you could make, but I think you would get most of the credit. I mean as soon as you go to introduce him everyone will be out way before the singing starts. Maybe that is what is "Fantastic" about you.
Actually I think that is a crock of fondu.....because you're a nothing. I am sick and tired of talking about you, and you should know that when you deal with a guy like me you always get bit in the butt twice as hard as before. I have dealt with scum like you down in Hunnington Beach, and I always end up riding them right into the pier.
Yes they die, but it was always an accident on my part. So don't fret or have any fear, because sooner or later your time will come and we'll meet again.
Next time you're not going to stand a chance in hell, and you won't even come close to beating me. I will not be ignored anymore and soon I will rule over all.
Brian King remember that if you keep riding those waves your heart will smile. Peace out bra! Now where is that little kid!?!?! [Zach Morrison takes off looking for the kid and the camera fades.]
