Sample Pedro Kozaro roleplay

29 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade into a living room.The room is quite small and it's inhabitants look to be lving in squalor. There's a creeky, wooden table in the middle of the room where some PWI magazines lay along with a half eaten sandwich and some crushed beer cans. Budweiser for the record. There's a small TV, about 13 inches, with a cable box on top of it. On top of the cable box lays the converter. Up against the left wall is a three person couch. The couch is very ugly, and doesn't match with anything in the room. For that matter nothing in the room blends. The couch yellow with a flower pattern of orange, green, red, and white flowers covering the entire thing.There are solid brown post on the side of it.
Sitting in between the table and TV is an iron folding chair. Beside it lays another. The one beside it though is seriously fucked up, all dented, it doesn't look like it can be used again. In the back of the room is a refrigerator that runs with a small humming noise that can just barely be heard.In front of it is a kictehn counter, with a sink where there are many dirty dishes piled high.Hanging from the seiling are three cupboard which are behind the counter and to the left of the refrigerator. A loud belch can be heard. Then a man makes his way into the room from a small hallway to the left of the TV. The man is Pedro
Kozaro.He is carrying a can of Budweiser.He appears to be a little tipsy but still in control of himself. He is wearing a pair of faded blue jeans and a black T-shirt. Written on the front of the t-shirt in red writing "XCW, and TWCW have met their match ... ". The shirt has a small hole one the side of it and appears to be well worn. Pedro walks over to the table and picks up one of the PWI magazines. He sets his
Bud down beside him.The back of his t-shirt is now visible and says "The
Mexican Wonder Kid". The camera moves in front of him slowly as he begins to speak.]
PK: So, it's the big week. Party central here at TSrW. We take a vacation to party hard, as I have done, then return for the PPV. Why do we take a vacation? Because the front office is missing in action.
Missing in action ... but now they're back. Back and ready to roll. Great news ... right? Sure, we all get to return to action. A return to putting our body's on the line every night for some damn regional promotion. The story of my career. That's why I'm stuck in a shit ass apartment like this. Yeah, I onced lived the high life. Had a wife and kids to. I was on top of the world ... a champion in the TWCW and XCW.
They called me the Mexican Wonder Kid. The next big thing in wrestling they said. Well the money stopped flowing and the problems started.
The wife and kids left, took what was left, and the rest, as they say, as history! Hahahaha ... Mexican Wonder Kid.
Now where was I before I got to rambling about my troubles .... [Pedro sets down the PWI issue he had been holding.He picks up his can of Bud and takes a huge gulp. He sets it down. Pedro then scratches his chin while thinking about what he was talking about before.]
Oh yeah! The PPV. First Strike '98, LIVE, only on Pay Per View! Call your local cable operator now so the TSrW can make bucket loads of money! It get's better, I have to wrestle before the PPV, against some guy named Sk8er road or rumbling roadhouse, or some assinine thing like that.
[Pedro leans forward towards the camera] What? What's that? Roadhouse Sk8? Well ladies and gentlemen the camera man has ever so graciously informed me of the fact that for your viewing pleasure Pedro Kozaro will be taking on the man known as
Roadhouse Sk8 in his debut match. WHO THE HELL IS THIS GUY! Well whoever he is I don't feel sorry. See, most guys would say, "Man, I feel sorry for you. Your in the wrong place at the wrong time. I'm just gonna hev to kick your ass!" well, I am not most guys. How can I feel sorry for anyone after the way I ended up? They said my career had so much promise, I had so much ahead of me.I was going to be the star. Now look at me? Look at me? I'm nothing, all I have to fall back onto is my job over at the UIWF as head of security, and wrestling in some regional promotion. So Roadhouse Sk8 I don't feel sorry for you! I'm going to climb my way back to the top, where I used to be, and where I should be! You are just the first man I am going to kick in the face while I climb the mountain!
[Pedro picks up the issue of PWI.He opens the magazine and it almost automatically opens to a section entitled "PWI on the lookout: Pedro
Kozaro"]
See here, right here in this very issue of Pro-Wrestling Illustrated I was a featured article! Right here in the "On the look out" section, where they talk about the rising stars of the sport few people know of,
Pedro Kozaro was listed.Here, I'll read a little excerpt for you.
[Pedro traces his finger along the page until he finds what he's looking for.] Ah, here it is " .... Already holding the Totally WIcked Championship Wrestling Canadian Single, and Tag titles, along with being the XCW TV Champ, being no.1 contender for the XCW World Title, and having posted the best record in XCW history, Pedro Kozaro is well on his way to developing into a big time star. We here at PWI feel he'll be in the big leagues in less then a year. .... "
That was 7 damn months ago! They said go to Japan, hone your skills, become a true star. So I did. Problem was I hit my head on the concrete when I missed one of them damned Asai Moonsaults! They say my attitude has gotten worse, that I tend to snap, and take people out for no reason at all, they that injury did me in. You know what I have to say to them? <bleep> 'em! I don't give two <bleeps> about whether they think I am finished or washed up because I know that I'm not! There's still a lot of ability in this body and if what it takes to get it out is to put myself to the test every single day, the damnit, that's what
I'm gonna do!
[Pedro picks up his can of Bud and finishes it off. He walks over to the refrigerator and grabs another can from the three remaining cans of his twelve pack. He returns to the table and sits down.He opens the can and takes a sip. He then notices the sandwich on the table, he picks it up and looks at it. He uses better judgement and puts the moldy sandwich down.]
Now finally someone has taken notice. The man who calls himself the
Underdog. The man who says he is the man. Of course I'm talking about that idiot Duke Turken. Now I know there's a lot of you out there who like Duke Turken, and I could understand why, and your probably saying "
Hey, don't make fun of the Duke, he's a good guy", well I'm not making fun of him, just stating a simple fact. What is he in now, the semi-finals? The semin-final of a tournament for this promotions
Heavyweight title, and where is he? He's not in the gym. Nope, he's not in the ring sparring either. He's not doing anything to prepare himself. Where is he? He's watching hockey in Dallas. Watching his beloved Oilers get ripped by a bunch of geezers who call themselves "
Stars "! That, my friends, is an idiot! Old Turkey has finally got a shot at a World title and he's eating Nachos and cheese at a hockey game in Dallas. Well good for Turkey, I hope the Pyscho White Boy kicks his ass.
Now the camera man has ever so graciously provided me with a transcript of what old Turkey had to say about me, let's take a look.
[Pedro picks up two peaces of paper from the desk.He brushes some crums off them and straightens them out.]
Here we are " .... A few years ago, I was in a federation called the TWCW, my first federation, back then I was small potatoes, and now I am here in the
TSrW where I am actually something, I am big, I am in the final 4.
Since the days of the TWCW, I have improved drastically, and obviously this Pedro Kozaro punk hasn't. .... "
Let's get one thing straight before we go any further Duke, if there's one thing I accomplished in my career it's this: I owned the damn TWCW!
I remember you walking in there, a wide eyed rookie, came in shooting off his mouth and challenged me. I promptly kicked your ass from here to there. Since then you've gotten better. I see you don't want to get your ass kicked anymore. Well it's an unavoidable fact of YOUR life,
Duke Turken, that I am going to kick your ass again! Now let's read a litte more of what old Turkey had to say.
[PPedro qoutes some more of DT's words of wisdom.] " .... He is stickin' his nose in my buisness and if he doesn't watch it, if he doesn't quit playing with fire he'll get burned. I am not going to direct all of my attention to this guy, why? Because he is not worth it. I have bigger fish to fry in this federation believe you me.
And I don't have the time to play his little tricks. .... " Oh I feel soooooooo special.DT thinks I'm not good enough for him to waste his time on. Puhh-lease Turken. You know that if you step into the ring with me that I'll expose you as the fraud you aree, and you don't like that one bit, do ya Turkey? Bigger fish to fry you say?
Maybe, just maybe, I'll have to get your attention again. Oh by the way, I for got to ask, how did the chair shot feel? I was just wondering.Ok folks were almost done reading what Turken had to say about me, I know it's boring, but bare with me here.
[Pedro picks up his can of Bud and gylps furiously.He then finds another passage of DT's writing.]
" Sure after this tournament is said and done, I'll fight you Pedro
Kozaro, and show you, that I have improved and you haven't. I will make a mockary of your name. "
Pedro and then he goes on to say how he could beat me in an insult contest, or something like that. Maybe you could Turkey, maybe you could, but I know, and you know, I could beat you in a wrestling contest and that's all that really matters.
Just one last thing before I go. AM i suposed to feel honered that you'll fight me after the tournament? Am I? 'cause I don't! Turken I thought you knew me better! I'm not going to sit and wait for you to take the fight to me ... I"M GONNA TAKE THE FIGHT TO YOU!
[cut to static]
