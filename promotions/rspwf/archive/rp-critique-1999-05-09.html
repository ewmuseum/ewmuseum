RP Critique

9 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{DJ Icey's "This is How My Drummer Drums" plays in the background as the camera fades from black to encompass the setting of this scene. Sandra, in a pair of black leather shorts and a blindingly white, sleeveless silk shirt runs a brush through her thick hair and puts the cap on a tube of mascara laying on the table in front of her. She turns and walks out to the main room , dropping an absent kiss onto the top of Eric's head and leaves. He looks up from a book he is reading, smiles at her receding back, then glares at the cameras as they follow her out before dropping his gaze back to the book.
The sounds of DJ Icey fade to nothing as Sandra gets into the elevator and further, out the door of the building. She breaks into a fast walk once on the sidewalk and the cameramen struggle to keep up with her stride while keeping the cameras going. She says nothing in response to any of the few people who recognize her as they go, keeping silent until they arrive at a bench on the far, deserted end of a park.. Sandra sits then, and crosses her legs.}
Sandra: Well? {A small, sickly looking reporter detaches himself from the gourp of cameras and moves forward, making a move to sit alongside of her. Sandra stops him with a flicker of her fingers, and he wavers uncomfortably on his feet.}
Sandra: Stand, please. Now. You wanted an interview, yes?
Reporter: {nods} Sandra: Fine, then. This is, I assume, about that cellulite covered cow
Lindsey and myself.
Reporter: {another nod} Sandra. Yes...of course. And I have quite a few things to say about her, the overused tramp. For instance, I wonder how GW officials would feel if they knew that our "lusty" little clump of plastic, polish and ego is attempting to form herself a little stable?
{Sandra smiles maliciously} Sandra: Lindsey, you degenerated trailertrash buffoon, what do you think to accomplish with this? Gathering - or rather, attempting to - a few of GW's non-stabled wrestlers about yourself, rasping enticements about the downfall of the Brotherhood into their unexperienced little ears... do you really and truly believe yourself intelligent enough, cunning enough to bring about their downfall when you haven't even accomplished what you came to Gyeah
FOR? Quite a little ego bruiser, I'd imagine, finding out that no one but the basest entities employed by this corporation have any interest in you at all; and with that in mind, not even to wrestle you. So you decided to do this.
Silly, ignorant fool. I pity you more than anything else. Tell us, Lindsey, what is to become of your alliance with Psycho Soup? He's the whole reason you got in here, from what's been whispered in the locker rooms and hallways. You offered your dubious "services" to anyone in Gyeah at large and Soup was the only one who accepted.
{She laughs delicately, a delighted smile on her face as she continues to speak, looking out at a lake further down the park}
Sandra: Kind of makes you feel a tad insignificant, wouldn't it? Knowing that no one wants you...But as amusing as I find it to mock you, I digress.
I meant to ask you what Soup thinks of your attempts at arranging yourself a group. He doesn't know, I'd hope. Eric and I had much respect for him at one time, I still do, in a way. And thinking that he would be part of a plan as facile, as JUVENILE as this would really just destroy all of that. So I'm choosing to believe that he doesn't know. That you, like the slimey worm all of Gyeah knows you to be, are going around behind his back in this. For shame, Lindsey.
Hardly...''Trusty'', is it? You know what I'm talking about, yes? That little schtick of yours? "Trusty, busty, etc, etc"... Childish ramblings from one who has the mind of a child.
{Sandra pauses to brush a fallen leaf off of the benchseat beside her and looks at the reporter speculatively}
Sandra: Can you run? Reporter: Yes... Sandra: Then be a dear and go fetch me a drink, please? {The reporter sighs and walks off} Sandra: Such a nice boy. Where was I? Maybe this match that's been set up. I notice from the phone call I recieved concerning it that this will not be a...what was it? Strip match. Now, I know you had that avaricious little mind of yours fixated on a strip match, but it really doesn't matter to me,
Lindsey. Gimmicks like that to fill a match indicate, to me, a lack of skill. If one must rely on catching attention with a flashy display...then another can assume that one really doesn't have the skills to back themselves up in a real competition. Which only makes my job, beating you into a mindless oblivion, that much easier. Do you get what I'm saying? Does your small mind comprehend? I hope so, Lindsey, because I hate repeating myself. Unless it involves what a shameless, cheap whore you are. I could derive enjoyment from that for longer than I can even think to imagine.
So, Lindsey, I suppose that about does it for now. Please, feel free to tell all of us about your little stable plans, and how they aren't coming together, despite your best attempts. And if they ARE... I know that Eric and I would be very interested to learn who was foolish enough to join with you. If just for the entertainment value.
{She stands up and nods to the crew, then strolls off for a walk by the lake} {Interiew end, fade to Kindred ad}
