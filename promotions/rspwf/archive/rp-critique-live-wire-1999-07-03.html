{RP CRITIQUE} Live Wire

3 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a flash that I recently used in the IWA where I am currently competing as Live Wire. This flash won me the IWA Hardcore Title. I was just wondering if you would CRITIQUE it for me and give me some pointers. ThanX
Live WiRe <--{Begin Transmission}--> ::Scene:: <<A strange crackel is heard, then a slow hiss is heard. The camera flashes on where there is a man standing wearing blue jeans, and a black Ecko Unlimited tee shirt. He has on a yellow FuBu hat turned to the side. He is wearing a black mask with barbed wire and a gold wire painted on it. He is sitting in a nice luxury chair, in what seems to be an office. He is on the phone where he is talking very loudly. He hangs up the phone and looks at the camera man. He begins to speak.>>
Live Wire- Well well well, look who it is. Look into the eyes of the Franchise of the IWA. I have been around the world and beat some of the greatest wrestlers in the history of this business. I have won numerous titles in countless federations. But now begins a new era in the career of the man called...Live Wire. I am here in the IWA to bring justice to all. Coming up on Thursday Terror, I have my first match in the IWA. Here is my first chance to show my superb wrestling skills. But this match is like no other. This is a Hardcore Match against Madd Dog. Madd Dog you have no clue what you have gotten yourself into. Because if I have to, I will go to the extreme side.
And believe me Madd Dog, you do not want me to go there. The worst mistake of the president was to sign this match. Lets just look at the rules to this match.
<<Live Wire picks up a piece of paper and shows it to the camera.>> [The Hardcore Title will be distributed onto the man who scores a victory.  There will be three matches on the wheel which will be spun before the match and them chooses are Barbwire for Ropes Match, Baseball
Bat Falls Count Anywhere Match, and Ladder Death Match - Ladder Rungs are wrapped in barbwire/NO DQ/Must obtain belt to win.]
<<Live Wire puts down the paper and stares into the camera. He begins to speak once again.>>
Live Wire- Wow, what an interesting match this could turn out to be. Spin the Wheel Make a Deal. Wow, there are even better stipulations on this wheel compared to the only other Spin the Wheel Make a Deal match. I am talking about the Coal Miners glove match at WCW's Halloween Havoc where Sting took on
Jake Roberts. At least there are more interesting ways for this. I sure hope the wheel lands on Ladder Death Match. That sounds quite interesting. There is no feeling quite like the feeling of Barbed Wire wrapped around your opponent. Or maybe even the match with barbed wire for ropes. Madd Dog, I wouldnt think any worse of you if you decided to just quit right now, and now show up for the match. Because if I were you, I sure wouldnt want to have to go through that type of match. Actually any of those matches on the wheel. So why dont you just quit and let Judge Grudge just hand over that Hardcore Title to me on Thursday Terror. It would save both of us some time and blood. Madd
Dog, once you step into the ring with me, you will never be the same. Just remember what I said. And a warning: Madd Dog, if you decide not to quit, remember that I warned you about what will happen to you. No one should accuse me of anything, Madd Dog, you know what you are getting yourself into right now.
