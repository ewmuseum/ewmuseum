Ian Walsh is looking for another fed.

19 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello all. I am Ian Walsh, handler of "Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh. I am out of school now and I have some extra time on my hands. What I want is simple. I want an established federation with a good couple of months under it's belt. I want the federation to have a closed mailing list and a web page.
I will look over any offers. (Please, no spamming.) **A little about myself** I am 16 years old and I have 14 months of e-wrestling experience. I have held many titles in the small time leagues and a few in the better circuits. My character is basically a heel. I work hard to do well. If you need references, contact:
[detail withheld] [detail withheld] [detail withheld] [detail withheld] [detail withheld] [detail withheld] I am sure that you can find out about how well I do from them. They have seen my work and I have been with their federations for a while. I currently hold the N.E.W. North American Title and the I.e-W.C. World Heavyweight Title.
**To contact me** All you have to do to contact me is e-mail me at [detail withheld] . If you have an instant messager for AOL and you are on an internet server, include the instant messager name with the mail. Thank you.
It wouldn't make sense if I posted this without including a sample roleplay. Below this message is a sample of my work. I hope that you enjoy it.
{--Begin Transmission--} [Camera fades in.]  [The camera drop-focuses on an above ground swimming pool. The blue lining can be seen through the water. There is a glare that is somewhat blinding as the sunlight reflects off of the pool water. The outside of the pool is blue with white trim. The camera zooms out a little as movements in the water can be heard. Ian Walsh is leaning against the left side of the pool from the inside of the pool. His hair is matted down from the pool water. His face is clean shaven. His skin is light. He has a smirk on his face as if he is trying to hide some happy feeling inside.]
 IW: "We are here, at my house. This is my pool."  [Ian elevates himself in the water and perches himself onto the pool ladder. His red swim trunks are soaked from the pool water. His body isn't one that is all that admireable, but he is built. He brushes his hair back with his hands as the camera swings over to the right side of the pool. This shows a more distant, but a front angle of Ian Walsh.]
 IW: "This pool is like the wrestling ring . . . I control _everything_ that goes on inside. However, there are some problems. Yet, I overcome those problems."
[Ian coughs.]  IW: "As I do in the ring, I decide what happens in this pool. I make things happen. I can make people drown.
I can hurt people. I can also have fun."  [The camera zooms in a tad.]  IW: "This Saturday, I will not be controlling what goes on in the pool. I will be, however, controlling what goes on in the ring. And that, my friends, is what counts more.
That is what means more to me." [Ian sighs.]  IW: "American Patriot . . .  . . . you have been chosen as the next victim of the 'Human Torture Rack' Ian Walsh. Congrats, my friend, you must feel like a man on death row. However, you have time to anticipate the abhorrible ass-kicking that you will receive at the hands of the _greatest_ wrestler of all time."
 [Ian smiles and crosses his arms.]  IW: "Years from now, when you are old and shriveled up, you can say to your friends, your family, and even to companions that you faced the man that thrived on the pain of his opponents; a man who many envy and despise at the same time; a man who was known as the 'Human Torture Rack.'"
 [The camera zooms out a tad.]  IW: "American Patriot . . .  . . . I cannot wait to get into the ring with you and set a prime example of why a person should not get into the ring with me. Y'see, my friend, there is no possible way for you to beat me. No . . . it's not momentum that is on my side. I will use my strength;
my skills; my knowledge; and my will to win in order to win this match."  [Ian uncrosses his arms and sets his hands on his thighs.]  IW: "Y'see, Patriot . . ."  [Ian climbs the pool ladder and stands on it.]  IW: "The pool may be shallow, but my impact . . ."  [Ian frog splashes into the pool. After a moment, he emerges from the surface, spits out a mouthful of water, brushes his hair back with his hands and continues.]
 IW: ". . . will be nothing less than _deep_."  [Ian points into the camera.]  IW: "American Patriot . . .  Prepare to meet . . .  . . . your demise."  [Ian submerges underneath the water's surface and the camera starts to lose focus.] [Camera fades out.] {--End Transmission--} Ian Walsh Handler of: "Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh (N.E.W., I.e-W.C., A.I.C.W., V.C.W., I.I.W.A., A.e-W.F.)
