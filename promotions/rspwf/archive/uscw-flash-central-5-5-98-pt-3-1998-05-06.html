USCW Flash Central 5/5/98 Pt. 3

6 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Delinquent VooDoo Spectre Atomic Punk -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------- (The Delinquent is beating on a punching bag) Delinquent: VooDoo Spectre? You mean...voo doo? That is psycho...that guy is nuts, too. I have NOTHING against him though. I will probably end up fighting him some day. DLM got back together, brought a tear to my eye...hahaha. Now that they are back together I need a tag partner to win the tag straps. Any takers? I totally deserve the belts...I basically won them for DLM. I am fighting Willy Vanilly? I got an idea if the president will let me...can I possibly have a match against Mopreme or Seagram? I want to deliberate to prove that I am NOT in their little high school gang and that I can beat better ranked opponents. So what'dya say Spicer? As for Vanilly...I remember a music group...I wouldn't call it a band...a while back and they sang horrible songs.
Wait...I still am not sure how this relates to anything. All I know is that I am gonna kick your ass! I have been fighting on the low card for long enough and I REALLY wanna take a step up. I have plans for some of the un-suspecting wrestlers in the near future. People that have not had much to say lately will be made to talk. I haven't had much time to myself lately and I am going to have to stop lifting weights all the time and start getting out more. I also need to move up in the ranks and get more cash. I am sure I will not have much if I want to get some good stuff...So, I don't care if its Vanilly, Seagram, or
Mopreme...I am moving UP in this fed...so don't ge in my way.
(Delinquent leaves) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------- ::everything turns red and suddenly VooDoo Spectre slowly emerges from under the ring:: VooDoo Spectre: Hahahaha...I cursed Canadian...and I will curse you Rocket...He messed with the wrong man...and at the wrong time...as everyone else has. Anger is my only way to satisfy my needs...you are the next man to satisfy my needs....You will suffer a thousand deaths when we fight...and I will destroy you with my Bad Mojo.....my finishing move. If you think you can beat voodoo....you're wrong....Mwahahahaha....your efforts will be futile....Prepare to fight me....and another thing...I am looking for a title...a gang...and more people to destory. I can not be stopped. I will NOT be defeated. You have seen horrible things before, I am sure...but, you will
see more Chaos...than you ever have...in your life. Another thing Rocket....If you try to jump me....or you try anything funny....Hahaha...It may be a sad day for you...and your family. Also, if you have any brains...you'd forfeit the match....and if you do...I'll fight you no holds barred. Anyone want me? Come and get it...I'll be pleased to....make you more useless than you already are....and if you decide to accept my offer...you have to agree to pay your OWN hospital bill...
::the ring's ropes catch on fire and flares shoot everywhere. Voodoo wraps his black cape around him and disappears under the ring once again::
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------- (camera zooms in on Atomic Punk sitting watching tapes of Storm...you hear laughter comming from AP. He turns towards the camera...)
Hey Storm I don't need to have credentials to wrestle a pathetic little wimp like you. I don't care if you are Pres of the league you will feel the power of the Helix and I will come out on top. The only money that is going to change hands for you this Wednesday is your little piss ant venus paying the doctor bill. I got your JOBBER hanging boys. Come Wednesday I am gonna beat you senseless and then while your laid up in the hospital you can watch all the paint dry and grass grow you want. Since you will be out of action. One more thing..
(Atomic Punk stands up turns around and drops his pants....) Kiss my @$$.....HaHaHaHa..... (camera fades to black....)
