[Sample Flash] Pain & Suffering

7 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[[Sonia is writing up something in her notepad about something. Pain & Suffering are being suspended overhead of the pit of fire trying to untie the other. She looks up and sees the camera being turned on and she continues to write]]
[Sonia] I'm writing up things to do for Heidi's punishment. Oh, it won't be ten lashes. It won't be 10 order of the mistresses. But it will be ten of something. In fact. I have my three finalists of punishment. Let's bring out our contestants!
[[She brings out three pictures of three different forms of punishment]] [Sonia] Number one: I brand you on the back with the ten letters of PUNISHMENT. One letter at a time. That should bring out some pain. Don't worry. They'll go away when your courpse starts to rot. and then....
[[Pain screams out in agony and Sonia comes with a whip]] [Sonia] [[Whipping Pain with each word]] I'm....TALKING....TO....THE.....F&^%'N....CROWD [[Pain's screaming quiets down a little]] [Sonia] That's better. Now number two. Punishment is ten minutes suspended over a pit of fire like my pets.... ONLY you'll be MUCH closer to the fire. I'm a pyromaniac. And finally punishment number three... well, we'll leave that to the imagination. [[Gives a slight chuckle]] Oh, and any of your friends Heidi should happen to interfere with the punishment well then.... your career....
um.....[[scratches her head]] Oh I'm awful with words. I'll use visual aids instead. [[She removes a part of the whip and it reveals a sharp blade and he whips at the ropes of her pets and they fall into the pit of fire and five minutes pass until they finally come out all burned with 1st degree burns.]]
[Sonia] Ah! I know now. Your career goes up in flames. You leave the IWF/WoW! Doesn't that suck! So we both got all to gain and damn ALL to lose! I lose the belts and you lose you dignity. If I don't strip that away before the match.
This match to you maybe personal or professional. But to me it's nothing more than pleasure of seeing you shrival up and beg for me to crush you like the insignificant little SPECK that you are.
[[She starts walking towards the camera]] [Sonia] Now, let's settle this once and for all. Heidi, you're a marines and marines have a conduct unbecoming. Well same thing with mistresses. We have our own Conduct unbecoming. And getting my hands to do physical harm on another person would be that conduct unbecoming a Mistress. And this rank which I earned is more important than these belts of mine. I rather lose these belts than give up my title. Speaking of titles the top 200 tag teams came in and guess what. My pets made it to 20. I guess that shows what good managing skills
I have and what great wrestling skills my pets have. I know that the Killing
Machines are Death & Destruction are higher, but that don't mean &^%$. Because last year they were on the list and had a whole year to improve their fighting skills. My pets weren't on the list last year. My pets only had a few short
MONTHS to prove themselves and a few months was all that they needed. They were the fastest rising team in this federation, and took the belts! I had odds stacked up against me as it is now. And I overrisen them ALL! I now have the MA against me. Hey I wanted new blood and I'm getting it. I am inspired to show that I do rule this federation with an IRON FIST! That these belts DO belong to only me. That your poultry skills are no match for me and my pets. [[Laughs with an evil smile as she looks right to the camera with her icy glare again]]
But, enough with bragging. It's time to get serious again. Now I know this is out of my field but I'll do it anyway. Death & Destruction. You're getting it to your head. You think that just because the MA chose you means you're better.
HELL NO! If you were better than it would be you holding these belts not me! It's not that you're better. It's That MA knew they couldn't get the best. That my power was too great for them to comprehend. So they went to get a force that they could control. They chose the 2nd best in this federation. You. They couldn't get the best.... so they got you. And you can get as many damn
T-shirts as you want. It won't mean a thing. Your total devastation is nothing compared to my order. My order is absolute and final. No one has risen after they have felt my order. Not the Machines, not the Connection, Not the Phlock.
Not even YOU. So damn me if you think you can wave a T-Shirt and make me shiver well then you better think again.
[[She goes back to her throne and cracks her whip]] [Sonia] Now, to the Killing Machines. You seem pretty jumpy. It seems like you're already to go to get you title shot. Well, it ain't gonna happen. Not by a long shot. You are NOT going to take these belts. And for interfering my match against Death & Destruction and for FINALLY shutting their mouths you just signed your death sentence. I would of had them by surprise and defeated..... again. Death & Destruction. But, no. You had to have your piece of the action. Well you're going to get a lot of action. Not to mention, pain, torment, agony, dislocated bones, etc, etc, ditto, ditto, so on and so forth.
For you Machines are a massive strong train on a track which you must obey at all cost. I'm the track and I'm gonna lead you to a cliff. Nothing you can do about it. I beaten you once. I'll beat you again. I'm not going to underestimate you. You are a worthy foe which I must take serious. You are a has-been but still can put up a fight. I was ignorant and stupid first time I lost. I swore to myself I wouldn't be ignorant again. That's how I defeated you the second time. I took all my time and effort of being able to defeat you in our three way dance. Sure I didn't pin you but I got the belts anyway. I got these babies.
[[She takes a deep breath. Then she goes to her pets and gives them some scraps for them to eat]]
[Sonia] Now, lets get to someone else. Badd Attitude. A thorn to my side. You're thinking of taking my belts away from me aren't you. Well that's not going to happen. You're not going to get my belts. And because of your interference I would at LEAST gotten a victory by DQ. I would of settled for that. But no, you had to be a hero and give me a draw. Not very nice. I thought
I could of left you alone but I guess I can't. Oh well. I guess you're another to be crushed. Another to be dominated. Another to become my slave. Pity. You were becoming amusing. But I guess I must end this and put you at the end of your misery. Put your ass in line. It belong to me.
[name withheld] Handler of Pain & Suffering Top 20 and only been around for a few months. Current IWF/WoW Champions Looking for a fed with a GOOD tag team divison.
