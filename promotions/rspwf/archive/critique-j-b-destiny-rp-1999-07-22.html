[Critique] J.B. Destiny RP

22 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
X-tremely Brutal Wrestling Leauge *Failure Is Not An Option* ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ The Dangerous Alliance And The XBWL Proudly Present "The Future" J.B. Destiny ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ (The scene opens up in the sold out XBWL Arena in Louisville, Kentucky. The fans who are jamm packed to the rafters are going absolutley nuts!!! They are all wearing T-shirts wich support there favorite wrestlers and The XBWL in general. Pyro-Technics go off and the crowd gets even louder. As the camera pans around the arena you can see various signs such as: Mike's good again!!!,
I have tickets to Sizzler, Dudley's back jack, and various others.
Pyro-Technics go off again and a small XBWL...XBWL...chant starts as the camera pans over to the announce booth. There are two announcers sitting there one is
Face and the other is Heel. The Face Announcer is a short, overwieght, bald man who is wearing a pair of Docker's stone washed kakies and a white collar shirt that reads XBWL in small blue print in the upper left hand corner. The
Heel Announcer is an average sized man who is wearing a pair of black jeans, a jean jacket which has had the sleeves ripped off and a Harley Davidson Bandana.
Pyro-Technics go off one more time and that is the announcers cue to begin.) Face Announcer: Well we're back here at The XBWL Arena and I just have to say
I was shocked when "Sensational" Sean Dudley re-emerged here in The XBWL. Now it's not so much that Dudley's back but I wanna know what he was doing by helping J.B. Destiny.
Heel Announcer: I wanna know the same thing. I mean why would a man with the credentials of the "Sensational One" help that sorry excuse for a Tag-Team champion???
Face Announcer: How can you say that Destiny is a sad excuse for a Tag-Team partner. Hell the only reason Edwards was even given an XBWL contract was becuase J.B. Destiny asked the front office to give him one.
Heel Announcer: What!?!? How can you say that??? Everyone knows that Edwards is the one who made J.B. Destiny a house hold name. The only reason Edwards left Destiny is becuase Edwards did all the work and Destiny took all the credit.
Face Announcer: Do you just think of theese lies off the top of your head or do you write them down??? Anyway J.B. Destiny is due out here at any moment now, and maybe he'll shine some light on the whole Sean Dudley situation.
(Then all of a sudden Living On A Prayer (Without The Words) by Jon Bon-Jovi begins to play over the arena's P.A. system. The crowd erupts into cheers, although there are still a few scattered boo's. That's when Douglas Wellington
Jr. steps out from behind the curtain. His long brown hair is tied back into a pony-tail and he is wearing a black business suit. He is followed by the lovely Amy who has long brown hair that is about two inches past her shoulders, and she is wearing a sky blue dress. Then following them comes "The Future"
J.B. Destiny. His short black hair has blonde highlights, and is combed forward. His body is in its usual good shape like Brian Christophers. He is wearing a pair of orange wrestling tights (The pant kind) which have a black flame going around one side along with a pair of white wrestling boots which read D.A. in black on each side. As they walk to the ring they slap the hands of the fans who are sitting by ringside. When they finally get to ringside they all walk up the steel steps and Douglas Wellington Jr. holds the ropes open for J.B. Destiny and Amy. When they get into the ring Amy and Douglas
Wellington Jr. walk over to the middle of the ring and wait for J.B. Destiny who has walked over to a ringpost and climbed up to the middle turnbuckle. He then unstraps The XBWL Tag Title from around his waist and raises it above his head. He recieves cheers from the crowd but a few scattered boo's can still be heard. He then climbs back down and goes to join Amy and Douglas Wellington
Jr. in the middle of the ring. When he gets there he hands his half of The
XBWL World Tag-Team titles to Amy. Mean while Douglas Wellington Jr. has taken a microphone out from his jacket pocket which he now hands to J.B. Destiny who waits for his music to stop and begins to speak.)
J.B. Destiny: Well it appears as if a cat has caught Brandon Thortons tounge. Because it's been about a week and a half sincehe's uttered a word about J.B. Destiny. I dunno maybe he thinks if he lays low for a little while, doesn't draw attention to himself, and keeps it quiet that I'll forget about what him and that bitch Jessica did to Amy. But Thorton it's not gonna happen what you did was unforgivable. I still don't know what possesed you to do it. You took what was a commercial fued that was thought up by The APCW front office. But for some reason you decided to make it personal, and that was the mistake of your life. And when I seen what had happened to Amy the only thing that was going through my mind was getting my hands on you. And guess what Brandon.
It's still going through my mind, and it's gonna keep going through my mind until I get my hands on you. Now what kind of man attacks a lady??? Like I said I don't know why you did it but I do know that it takes a pathetic, coward of a man to attack a lady with a baseball bat and when I get you in the ring, and I will get you in the ring. I'm gonna show you what pain is all about and after I beat you I'm gonna let Amy come in and slap you in the face, or whatever else she wants to do to you. I know I come out here every week and talk about you Thorton. And I'm starting to feel like I'm just repeating myself. But that's because there is only so much to say, there is only so much
I can put into words, but when I get you in the ring I will show you what I can't really explain. Because there are only so many words I can say, I can tell you how much hatred for you runs through my viens in only so many words but I'll make sure you understand and feel the pain Amy went through when you attacked her. But I have to move on. And what I have to move onto is the subject of "Sexy Boy" Jack Diamond. Now Diamond how can you come out here and talk about me for ten minutes and after your huge monolauge say I'm not important to you anyways??? It's nothing but a big pile of B.S. becuase if I really didn't pose a threat to you, you wouldn't have said anything about me.
But the truth is you know as well as I do that I am "The Future" of The XBWL. And the reason I called you a second rate mid-carder is becuase I only speak the truth, and that's exactly what I did was tell the truth. Sure you may have held The International Title in the past. But the keyword in that sentence was
"past". And just like in everything else in life you have to move on from the past and to "The Future". Now Diamond you may have been a decent wrestler in the past, God knows your not now but that's besides the point. But now you aren't anything but a joke. Look at yourself the only chance you had of ever holding another title here in The XBWL was if Matt Austin handed you his
International Title. But sadley for you it just wasn't supposed to be and now you will never hold another title in The XBWL. Anyway as for your remarks of me just being another punk kid trying to make a name for himself, well maybe you havn't been watching when I've been coming out here. Becuase in case you didn't notice I've been coming out here the past couple weeks and I've had my half of The XBWL Tag-Titles wrapped around my waits, and correct me if I'm wrong but if I'm half of The Tag-champs haven't I already made a name for myself??? Yeah I think I have. Diamond you've gotta realize I'm the most over man in sports-entertainment and you nothing but a fading memory. And you have to come face reality and realzie you have no "Future left here in The XBWL.
Now as for your two friends Hard Rapp they can come out here and say whatever they want about me, and they can definatley say whatever they want about Steve
Edwards becuase as far as I'm concerned nothing they say holds any water. Look at them Steve Edwards could even beat thoose guys. Now as for you two wanting a Tag-Title match, I just gotta say I don't care I'll give you a title shot.
And if you wanna have a number one contenders match at Summer Sizzler for it be my guest because as far as I'm concerned the winner of that match is just gonna be the first looser. And as far as me calling you Hardcore Rapp all I have to say is opps. What I meant to call you is a bunch of CRAP!!! Which brings me to the rest of the Tag-Team division. It appears that the Tag-Team division has really been getting bigger and heating up ever since J.B. Destiny and Steve
Edwards arrived on the scene about one month ago. And the only way I can explain that is everyone wants to beable to say hey I'm the one who stopped The
Dangerous Alliance but I got news for you guys in the back, it's not gonna happen simply becuase I'm to good to be beaten right now and there is not a team in The XBWL who can beat me and Steve Edwards. Freddie Malone knows that so he's decided to hold a drawing and mix a team up to see if they can stop us well I got news for you Malone nobody's gonna beat me not even Micheal Andrews himself can put an end to my hot streak because I'm the new "Franchise" player here in The XBWL and I'm gonna prove it to each and everyone of my fans, and
I'm gonna do that by doing what I've been doing ever since I got in The XBWL and that's beat people. Now onto Steve Edwards...Actually I'm gonna let you talk about him first. (while he says this he looks at Douglas Wellington Jr.).
(J.B. Desiny hands the microphone back to Douglas Wellington Jr. and he begins to speak.) Douglas Wellington Jr.: First of all Steve Edwards, do you know how to tell the truth??? Ever since I've known you, you have claimed to be things that you're not Mr. Thirty-Six time World Champion. And I guess it's like they say once a liar always a liar. Because Steve Edwards knows just as well as I do that I have not been calling him, or asking him to come back to The Dangerous
Alliance. And guess what Steve I never will be calling you and asking you that. Now Steve you also come out here and ramble on about how "The Destined
One" isn't up to your level of compitition. Well that has gotta be a joke because J.B. Destiny is above the competition level of every XBWL wrestler especially yours. Now I know J.B. has alot to say about you and we're running kind of short on time so I'll give him back the mic.
(Douglas Wellington Jr. then hands the microphone back to J.B. Destiny and he begins to speak.)
J.B. Destiny: Steve Edwards, at Summer Sizzler it's time for your reality check. And I'm the man who's gonna be that reality check for you. Now Edwards
I know your fighting style better than anyone in the business. Hell I know what your thinking before you do and that's why I'm gonna beable to make it look easy when I put you down for the 1..2..3. And Steve this isn't gonna be like one of our Tag-Team matches were you can just fake an injury and leave the arena, becuase I'm gonna keep on beating you regardless, now after the match you'll proably realize the error of your ways and coming crawling back to me and the rest of The Dangerous Alliance, but I'll tell you something right now if you come crawling back you're not gonna be met with hugs, you're gonna be met with right hands. By the way I know you so well like I mentioned before that I know what your plan is. This Saturday at Showdown your gonna fake another injury and try to get out of our match at Sizzler but that's not gonna happen either becuase I'm gonna make sure you stay at 100% from now until the night of our match, not because I like you but because I don't want you to beable to use I wasn't at 100% as an excuse when you loose. So basically Steve your time is up, you can't hide from me anymore and at Sizzler I'm gonna show you exactly why I'm Destined for stardom in this sport. Edwards you are
DESTINED FOR DESTRUCTION!!! Now last Saturday a man made his return to The
XBWL...Acctually a couple men made there returns but there's one inparticular he goes by the name "Sensational" Sean Dudley!!!
(The crowd erupts into boo's. J.B. Destiny stops talking looks up at the crowd and smiles. He slowly raises the microphone back to his mouth and begins to speak.)
J.B. Destiny: Dudley all I have to say is I've heard alot of bad things about you, but you don't judge a person on there reputation you judge them on your expierience with them. And you seem like a good enough guy to me so Dudley all
I have to say is this...I owe you one.
(J.B. Destiny then smiles and looks up at the crowd again. Living On A Prayer
(without the words) by Jon Bon-Jovi begins to play over the arena's P.A. system once again as J.B. Destiny hands the microphone back to Douglas Wellington Jr.
and The Dangerous Alliance exits the ring to cheers and eventually dissapear behind the enter/exit curtain.)
Face Announcer: What did Destiny mean by "I owe you one"??? Heel Announcer: I dunno but does that mean Dudley's gonna be at Showdown, or maybe even Sizzler???
Face Announcer: I guess you'll have to watch and find out, SEAN DUDLEY is back in The XBWL!!!
(As the announcer's keep talking the scene fades to a Pepsi commercial.) ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ This Has Been A J.B.
