[FLASH] Tommy Grayson ... comments, constructive criticism

10 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's not the best RP, but I'd have to say it's pretty damn good. Tell me what you guys think.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------ [SCENE: In the bathroom inside the hotel room of the Marriott hotel somewhere in Tokyo, Japan. The camera closes in on "The Extreme One" Tommy Grayson, who is sitting on the toilet, which has the seat down. Grayson seems to be re-applying the bandages on his cuts and gashes. Grayson's hair isn't neatly combed as usual, and he has a stubble in addition to his goatee, which is usually neatly-trimmed, but isn't. He's shirtless, which reveals the multiple bandages on his back, as a result of the thumbtacks penetrating his skin...he also has a bandage on his side, which looks like it's been replaced; the old bandage is discarded on the floor...blood has completely seeped through it.
Grayson is only wearing half of his ring attire...black trousers, and, simply, a pair of socks. Grayson looks up after throwing a bandage into the wastebasket, and stares into the camera.]
TOMMY GRAYSON: Well...<grunts> good 'ol TG is on his way out of Tokyo tomorrow night. I'm going back to the good 'ol US of A, and I'm gonna be doing some training to kick Tommy Lionheart's ass in that little tournament match I've got coming next week on Friday Fallout. Yeah, yeah...I know...Lionheart's one of those guys that aren't that easy to beat...well, lemme tell you folks something...I have NEVER backed down from ANYBODY...and Tommy Lionheart will be
NO exception. Yeah, TEO is always the underdog, but when it comes to THIS dog,
<chuckles> my bite is as BIG as my BARK...which means, whatever the HELL I say out there, I back up. I said I would kick Kuba Rico's stanky ass throughout the
Tokyo Dome, and guess what? I did just that. Now, I promise that I'm gonna kick
Tommy Lionheart's blimey ass throughout the Fleet Center in good 'ol Boston.
[Grayson gets up from the toilet seat, and walks over to the sink. He washes his face with water, and slicks back his hair with it. He walks into the bedroom, and pulls on a regular grey Hanes t-shirt. He sits down on the corner of the bed, and stares straight into the camera with that cold look of intensity.]
TOMMY GRAYSON: Lionheart, I remember seeing you in action a while ago when you were Lord Humongus...Humongus my ass. I could toss you around the ring like a football. You know...I also remember beating a guy by the name of Lord Humongus back in the ASW...I don't know if it was the same guy as you or NOT...but that doesn't matter now. What matters is the fact that you're NOTHING but a pretender, Lionheart. You think that you're the most extreme and hardcore ICW has to offer? The last time anybody said that here in the ICW, they got hung from above the arena on a damn crucifix...so, Lionheart, don't make claims that you can't back up, or else those claims are gonna get shoved in your big mouth, and then, they're gonna come shooting out like a cannonball after a foot comes and hits you right between the crack. You don't know what you're dealing with,
Lordy-boy. I'm not like your other opponents...the fact is that I'm one of those few guys who can just take a licking and keep on ticking...I have one of the biggest pain thresholds you'll ever see in a while. So, Lionheart, you keep your Lord's Blessing to yourself, because there's no way in HELL that good 'ol
TG is gonna get caught in it.
Lionheart, you called me a "NOBODY." That's a BIG insult to me,
Tommy-boy. <chuckles> Personally, I wouldn't call a guy who's held twelve championship belts in this business a "NOBODY." I'd call them one of the best.
But, if I'm considered a "NOBODY"...then, I would have to say this: "How in the
HELL did it feel to get your face wiped on the mat by a NOBODY?!?" <smirks>
Lionheart, let's talk about YOU ... _AGAIN_. You go out there to the ring, calling yourself "The Lord," and carrying a bible with you...come to think of it, you look just plain PATHETIC. I mean, look at you...you come to the ring looking like a damn choir boy, except with a picture of good 'ol JC on your robe! <laughs> Let me ask you something...what the HELL are you the "Lord" of?
Two letters come to my mind ... S and M! S for Sucking, and M for More-sucking
... it doesn't really sound correct, but it'll do for good 'ol TG. Anyway...the fact is, Lionheart, is that you may be one of the best high-flyers out there, you may be one of the best technical wrestlers out there, and you may CLAIM to be the best brawlers out there, but the fact remains that you WILL get taken to a new level of EXTREME by TEO...
...PLAIN ...AND ...SIMPLE!! [Grayson wipes some water off his face.] TOMMY GRAYSON: "Mr. Main Event" Matty Dee...looks like you're damn tenacious for a piece of me. <chuckles> A simple poem "TG Shakespeare" made up himself.
I'll tell you what, Matty...to prove how Crappy you are, I'm gonna take a baseball bat, and make it go...WHAMMY! <laughs> There's another one! Enough fooling, Matty...I seriously think you need to visit good 'ol Dr. Grayson at the pharmacy...he's got a prescription for an ass-kicking for you. Kuba took his dose, and look how well he's doing. Matty...I have no problems whatsoever about ending your career...as if it really mattered to anybody else. Look at you, Matty...the fans turned on you at a <snaps his fingers> snap of a finger.
You just give the command...<whiny voice> "I don't need you bastards!"...<normal voice> and then they begin the booing. They simply don't care, Matty...but, don't worry, I'm gonna make 'em care...because I'm gonna have you go out with STYLE...everybody's gonna remember Matty Dee as the guy who got BRUTALIZED by Tommy Grayson into oblivion. You shouldn't even consider it an embarassment...you should consider it an HONOR.
Well, before I go...I'd like to tell BOTH Tommy Lionheart and Matty
Dee that when you get in the ring with "The Extreme One"...you're just BOUND to be taken...
...TO ...THE ...EXTREME!! [The screen slowly begins to fade to black as Grayson stares into the camera.]
