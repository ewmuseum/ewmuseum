An open proposal...

7 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
First, this will be a tad different then most posts. Instead of a federation looking for wrestlers, this is a wrestler looking for feds. I was in a fed that recently had to shut down, leaving 3 of my tag teams hanging in limbo. I like running these teams, and am looking for a fed that needs solid teams. I myself am a reliable handler, who flashes at least once a week, and almost always twice. They're solid, readable, and rated PG (most of the time). Any feds that meet the following are the types I'm interested in. One: A fed that has been around a while, maybe 3 months or more. Two: A fed that has roleplaying. Simmed ones are fine, as long as flashes are accepted. Three: Professionalism. I'm looking for a fed that has readable cards and capable leadership. Now, I know it seems like I'm asking a lot, but I like e-wrestling, and hate to see my developed teams hanging in limbo. These teams ARE NOT a package, so you can only accept one if you want. In no particular order: Cutting Edge - Consisting of Laser and Blade, they are two young cocky faces who consider themselves "the best tag team ever to come down the pike". Of course, they have a lot to learn about wrestling! I like these guys because they're the babyface rookies who give it their all every match and aren't afraid to take on the big boys. The Stormreavers - Consisting of Rend and Maim, two HUGE viking badass heels who think the gods themselves gave them permission to raise hell on anyone who gets in their way. This is the classic hardcore team, and I don't mean they put people through tables. They bite, they hit, they choke, they keep hitting, they hurt. Kicked out of two feds for being too rough. They are heels who will NEVER turn face if I can help it. Project Twilight - Consisting of Agent Stone and Agent Jefferson, and managed by Agent Cantebury. They are former FBI agents who decided to give it a go in pro-wrestling. Cantebury does all the talking for these two men, who are a top-notch technical tag team. They come to the ring, win, and leave. For a silent team, they've drawn a LOT of heat in the old fed, and I want to continue to run them. Anyone interested, just drop me an e-mail, and I'll get back to you in a day. Diamondback Chris Myers RSPW's Excellence of Execution RSPWWCW's Television champion NEF'nW Montage champion CWO Television champion #1 Owen Hart mark and all around social deviant
