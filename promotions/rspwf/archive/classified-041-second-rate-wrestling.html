Feds Seeking Wrestlers: Second Rate Wrestling SECOND RATE HISTORY LESSON These are the facts, Jack! In the the year of 2000 President and Owner of Second Rate Wrestling Michael McGovern was just a normal guy who worked in a video store when one day he received word that his Grandmother who he had never visited or even liked had died and left him a HUGE fortune of over 50 Million dollars. Michael was shocked that the Grandmother whom he had never visited would give him all of her fortune that she managed to hide very well. So feeling guilty for having received the money Michael wanted to get rid of it fast and opened up his own wrestling promotion called Second Rate Wrestling. Michaels plan was too make Second Rate Wrestling SO bad that it would lose the 50 million dollars for him and he would not have to feel guilty anymore but to his surprise and everyone elses Second Rate Wrestling became a huge underground cult phenomenom! It was SO bad that it was GOOD! Instead of losing 50 million dollars Michael doubled it to 100 Million. Michaels ambition still is to make Second Rate Wrestling go bankrupt but he needs to make the viewers tune out first. He needs an idea SO bad the viewers will be forced to tune out! Will Michael McGovern find that idea and get rid of his guilt by making Second Rate Wrestling go bankrupt? You will just have to wait and see.
