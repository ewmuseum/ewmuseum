19 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ok, again sorry, man I'm dumb (Image up.) (It's hard to believe that the summation of over a thousand miles of pain, humilation, and jail time would reach its conclusion with something so boring as a shot of an elevator control panel--but lo and behold. It has.)
(Grainy Brakas-brand footage rolls against the chirpy muzak version of
Heart's 'These Dreams', as well as the steady humming of the lift's engine.
This is wholly unspectacular, but in a way, fitting; for a guy committed to capturing a thousand hours in the life of five wrestling nobodies, missing the weekend's events must have been like having a jammed 35mm can while the
Loch Ness monster was tap-dancing ten feet away.)
(And yet, with the dissolution of so much built in the anchronistic empire known as daily Schwahling life... here we are. Sean sighs with malcontent timbre, sounding a lot like a guy who's coming down with a head cold.)
(Then the narration starts.) OSV Brakas: Four floors. It's four fragging floors. Comeoncomeoncomeoncomeon- (The elevator starts to slow.. almost stopping.. and then, miraculously, starts up again like nothing had happened. Sean's voice lapses into frantic anger at nothing in particular.)
OSV Brakas: ForChrist'ssake! Just GO! (Politely, the car suddenly stops. A little bell chimes. And the two ornate doors slide apart, leaving a hazy appraisal of the grandiose' hallway beyond in the melancholy black and white of the film. Sean steps out, and stops. All we hear is breathing. A cough. Then, some patent rhetoric nonsense that'll be dubbed over later by a voice actor with some vocal meat.)
OSV Brakas: Less than an hour ago, we made our landing on the island of
Oahu.. 'Butcher Boy' Nick Papic and myself. This comes after an utter and total disaster on behalf of the aforementioned party, who somehow translated orders to extend communication to Lone Star Wrestling's
'Sheriff' Damon Hayes as free license to publically disgrace himself.. and me. (Sean starts to move, now, with the camera doing that lagging bob courtesy of a poor steadicam switch. He paces slowly down the hallway, focusing mainly on the ornate carpet--maybe for effect, maybe too trashed from jetlag and nerves to care.)
OSV Brakas: Following a turn of events that is simply too embarassing to recount at this point.. consider it a 'fresh wound', if you would.. bail money was made available for Mr. Papic and I, and we were able to barely catch a red-eye flight home.
(A frustrated sigh, heavy with the gurgle of snot, as he stops in front of that familiar door. You know the number well by now--the live-in suite of
Doman Schwahling here at the fabulous Pacifica Grande' Resort, marked
'117'. Brakas appears to be taking a moment for the sake of composure.)
OSV Brakas: After seven or eight transfers on four different airlines, we've negotiated a way here.. only to find out that, in my absence, all hell has broken loose. Exene Schwahling has left, Veronica Kennedy has voluntarily annulled her marriage to Doman, and the new J-Crown champion appears to be on the verge of a complete physical and morale breakdown.
(He straightens himself. The camera focused in blurry attention on the plated gold number.)
OSV Brakas: I stand on the threshold of disaster, but am, as the Schwahsylum has always said.. committed. It's my part to do what I can in trying to glue the pieces back together. If.. I can find them all.
(With such a dramatic pretense, one can almost imagine the swell of John
Williams orchastral as Sean's hand slips up into the frame, and lays a few weak knocks on the wood.)
(No response.) OSV Brakas: You've got to be kidding me. (Thinking better of that depressed groan, Sean hacks up a baseball-sized loogie (Or so it sounds like) before reassuming his narrative brass.)
OSV Brakas: Perhaps broken. Perhaps he's already gone. Perhaps.. dare I even say it.. the road has ended.
(Case as that thought might be, Brakas' hand knows no dignity, and rises to resume a more frantic bang of a closed fist on the door. After about five knocks, the door suddenly swings open.)
OSV Brakas: Ablb.. uh.. (For the rude awakening moment of the day, just add sound. Where there should be a depressed, morose shell of a man who's willingly thrown his entire support system away, caked in the blood from the night earlier, unwashed and unfocused, there is instead..)
OSV Brakas: I'm.. bleah. Sorry.. I meant what was.. ( ..a face worth a thousand adjectives. Something vaguely familiar about this woman--even with her tousseled mess of medusa black hair, swarthy skin of coconut bronze outlining a face with stark light eyes and dark, swollen lips. She raises a manicured eyebrow, elegant fingers curled up around the edge of the door frame, gaze rife with a certain sarcastic amusement at
Sean's blathering.)
OSV Brakas: I got the.. um.. wrong door. (Our vision of Lowrider Magazine coverworthy loveliness just lets those big blues roll, sosh-girl tone firmly fixated in the casual way she plays around Sean's edginess.)
Woman: Don'cha think it's just a lil' early for that, Sean? (Sean's voice has suddenly found a topic worth focusing on.) OSV Brakas: How'd you.. know with the.. my name? (And then, perhaps building on a good start: ) OSV Brakas: It's two in the afternoon, miss.. (The woman's lips just grow into a soft smirk, in no hurry to let that door swing inward--but, on the other hand, no real motivation to let it slam shut in the camera geek's face.)
Woman: Yeah, well, why don'cha come back in a few hours. He's not awake yet. OSV Brakas: Nono.. I'm looking for.. (The chipper tone that comes easing into audial range from what's probably the next room -sounds- familiar.. but, in due logic, there's no way that it could be.. )
Off-Screen Voice: Is that room service? Tell 'em I want more crab cakes. And another round of daqiris.. (The woman's eyes drop sidelong, speaking inward towards the room.) Woman: It's Sean, baby. He's got the camera. (A snicker from within. Nobody sounds that utterly derisive and annoying when they laugh, save one person.)
Off-Screen Voice: He should brought it by -last- light. Let him in. (With a bouncy kind of sarcastic body language, the Latina bombshell slips aside, gesturing grandly into the inner sanctum of the suite. In all actuality, once the light has adjusted, the place looks cleaner than we've seen it in a good month--no more clothes and empty bottles littering the floor, the couch no longer draped with a sheet. Not immaculate by any stretch of the imagination, as Sean nearly takes a header over a pair of boots someone left two steps in front of the door, but.. an improvement.)
(Sean's voice is a helium call for concern.) OSV Brakas: I think I'm in the wrong suite. Off-Screen Voice: What the hell makes you say that, lensboy? (A dramatic, made-for-television blurjerk pan to the right, where the impossible has just entered the living room of the suite. Doman Schwahling, bare-bodied save for a pair of baggy grey sweatpants, hair a tousseled punk-mosh of blue and black, plastic cup of something in hand. Bare torso an art exhibit unto itself, black Maori spirals having their way over lean cut muscle and tanless skin. This apparition of the new J-Crown champion stops over the couchback. Sips from the drink. And watches Sean stand there in a confused stupor, waiting impatiently for him to say something.)
OSV Brakas: B-b-b.. I waaas told that.. nuh.. nah. (Save for a few crosscut scratches on his forehead, Schwahling looks.. fine. That casual expression of cocksure and languid arrogance that only comes after a night of being f*cked. Not -over-, per se', just real well.)
(In shorthand, not the face of a man who's been hyped as losing his entire life in a single night. Not even close. Not even in the same zip code.)
Doman: A stewardess beat you, or something? Calm down. OSV Brakas: I thought that.. it's all over the 'Net that you've been.. that she and her.. and who the hell is..
(Schwahling's eyebrows raise in curious surprise, as if trying to decipher
Sean's uncomfortable stuttering.)
Doman: What.. her? (Sean's head turns, but takes the camera with it. Our friendly door-handling woman is now over by the nearby martinette bar arrangement, pouring herself a bottle of something while she sucks on an ice cube in a manner that would give the pope a heart attack.)
(And it doesn't really take a genius to get with the program from what she's got on, either--white slouch socks levelling off of endless bronze legs, the oversized fit of Doman's 'Schwahsylum 9-8' hockey jersey dangling at mid thigh. And, as it appears, not much else. With the way she grins over at the camera's dopey observation, it makes one wonder if the cliche'
'69' wouldn't be a better number for the wearer.) Doman: That's Jen, man. You don't do much in the way of homework, do you? OSV Brakas: I.. din't.. well.. they told me that.. ('Jen' pops the ice cube from her bee-stung lips, affecting a cynical little bow.) Jen: Jenesis Mercy, Sean. Ms. Oklahoma. Japan Universal Ladies. I thoughcha woulda knew. (Jenesis gives the little glass of crumbled ice cubes a shake, examining it distastefully.)
Jen: You used ALL the ice last night? (Doman gives out a short snort.) Doman: No, -you- used all the ice last night. The maker's down the hallway, and to the left. Sean'll keep me company while you're off exploring.
(The woman's lips toy with a grin that doesn't need any commentary to validate it, as she moves in a mild sashay over to the door.)
Jen: I didn't say it. Doman: You never -have- to. (With a shrug that signifies innocence that just -isn't- there, Mercy slips through the door and eases it shut behind her. The camera has a long way to rise and come back around from its apparent speculation fit on whether or not Jen's got anything on under the literal tail end of the jersey. When it does, Doman's on the couch in a weary slump, one arm high over the back, the other holding his drink at waist level.)
OSV Brakas: Oh, man. Doman: C'mon, lensboy. Calm down, and set the camera over there. You and I need to talk, from the looks of things.
(Sean seems utterly floored by, among other recent developments, the fact that Schwahling's talking to him in a tone other than what one would use in addressing a peon, dog, or paramecia.)
OSV Brakas: What? Put it down where? (Doman gives a short, tired gesture towards something we can't see.) Doman: Over there. Wherever. I want to shoot. (Brakas does his best to act like he's taking all this information and stiumulous in stride, as he quickly stumbles over to what ends up being a chair. After some shucking and jerking, the camera is set: low and wide on the couch. Schwahling idly picks his teeth in a careless waiting, as Sean comes picking his steps back over to the couch.)
Brakas: When -aren't- you shooting.. (Doman lets out an airid snicker. Sean's a little bit Westwood Village and a lot of film school geekboy--bear witness to his ill-fitting black jeans over thin legs, and his 'Army of Darkness' t-shirt. He sits, fiddling around with his wallet chain, alternating his attention between Schwahling and the lens.)
Doman: So. Where ya been? (Sean seems to start with the more family-oriented version of the debacle, but travel fatigue's had its way with him. He just slaps his hands onto his knees, and starts off like a hyperventilating cartoon character.)
Brakas: We got to Dallas.. Papic gets us a cab.. the driver speaks NO goddam english.. so he ends up taking us to Emmett Smith's estate.. where
Papic's idea of getting who he thinks is Damon Hayes' attention is to get dressed up in his goddam ring outfit, and stand out in front of his house, screaming obscenities.. then, of course, the goddam cops show up..
(Doman sips, watching Sean go nuts with an unconcerned patience, sipping from his drink every now and then.)
Brakas: ... they locked us up.. that idiot tried to call his mother, who, from what I've heard, has disowned him about ten times in the last year..
and I try to call you, but the line's busy.. and we end up in the same goddam wing of the jail with the rest of the freaks..
(Sean's out of breath, and he just gives up with a final toss of disgusted hands.) Brakas: .. I just got back about an hour ago. And I found out that I missed the whole deal this weekend. All of it.
(Doman quirks a brow.) Doman: How'd you get out of jail? (Sean goes 'Hunh?' in that way that any good liar does, when you need a second to make some off-the-cuff BS up.)
Doman: How-did-you-get-out-of-jail. Brakas: Oh. They let us off on recognizance. Doman: For what must have been several counts of disturbing the peace. Recognizance. Uh-hunh. (Brakas shrugs quickly. He's a pretty damn awful liar.) Brakas: Hey, I don't know, it's the local bureaucracy, Texas-style. Shoot me. Doman: Yeah. You're lucky they didn't. (He sips. Sean seems to be trying to ante' up the proper succint method of brooching the subject, but just settles for blunt stupidity.)
Brakas: I can't believe you let Exene and Kennedy go. (Sean looks expectant of getting kicked in the head, as do many of the local fans--anyone familiar with the dynamics of Brakas dealings in the
Schwahsylum market knows that this usual behavior recieves a beating of some sort.)
(As it is, Doman just gives a little head-doubt of Sean's comment.) Doman: They already had their minds made up. Brakas: And you didn't bother to try and stop them? Doman: No. Life goes on. (Sean blinks numbly, staring at the coffee table before the two of them. There's a weird kind of brother-to-brother aura around this exchange, and regarding such a phenomenal change in conversational attitue.. it's just weird.)
Brakas: But that was your wife, man. And your sister. I can't believe that anything is worth giving that up.. not for really-real.
Doman: There is no 'really-real', lensboy. Just acquisitions and capital. Brakas: You consider those two capital?! Doman: Personally, I don't care. The blunt expectation.. no, rather, -hope-, was that this fiasco would tear open the exploited rift of emotion that I've been forced to bare over the last few weeks. It gives me unmitigated glee to screw with everyone's head, shed all that domestic nonsense, and still be proud enough to stand up, sneer, and piss in the face of the general public.
Brakas: But you've lost. You've got to see that. For real. Doman: I lose whatever I feel like losing. And gain in similar fashion. I'm not going to be dissected for the glee of the audience, on my sister's whim. You're as naive' as the average idiot if you don't see beneath her facade, and -get- how she's been manipulating this thing all along.
Brakas: You're blaming Exene for this. Doman: I repeat--you'd have to be a naive' idiot not to understand what she stands to gain by getting me out of her way. She sheds a few tears, causes the destruction of my marriage.. it isn't -me- who's the callous prick in this whole deal. If anyone deserves the barbs of the teary-eyed populace, it's her.
Brakas: Yeah, but.. what about -you-? You're just moving on? (Doman sneers off, gesturing flatly towards the surroundings.) Doman: No, Sean, I haven't moved. I'm the same barbed hook in the ribs of this corporation that I was six months ago, except now, I've got something they want.. and can twist that spine to my advantage. I'm -in-. Despite what everyone was hoping.. I've risen to the top, yet again.
(The glass raising high with a tilt, he downs the last of the drink with a noisy slurp. A little toast of the empty cup, setting it on the floor next to his foot.)
Doman: Same as it ever was. I hate to use a word like 'quagmire', but.. it's symbolic in its irony. Because I really do not.. have not.. and will not.. break, for the amusement of those at home, and the meeeel-yuns and
meeeel-yuns watching around the world. (A somber silence. For a long moment. Sean messes around flittishly with the hem of his shirt, before finally getting to the nit and the grit.)
Brakas: And.. -her-? (As if on some magickal cue, the door gives a thump closed off-camera. Doman's attention goes left with Sean's; the chugging of ice loosely washing around in a bucket preceding Jenesis' approach. She bends over real slow and real nice into the side of the frame, setting the container on the table, and rising once more.)
(Sean looks as desperately dazed as Doman does casually cool. Jen gives one of those pouty-lipped and heavy-lidded smiles to the boys, hands braced loosely on the armrest of the couch.)
Jen: Oh, don'cha let me interrupt. I'm just passing through. (Brakas, being the suavecito masculino that he is, deftly changes subjects.) Brakas: So.. what about Sloane? We had a huge problem pinning that guy down last time.. and he's already gearing up for this..
(Doman isn't even listening. The almond-skinned bombastica has used every inch of those long legs to step over his feet, now settled nicely between the two. All eyes up, as the camera is set at a relatively family oriented-angle, where the girl's only visible to us geeks at home from the waist up.)
Jen: I'm gonna go take a shower, baby. Brakas: Erm. Coff. Coff. (Despite the black and white film, it's bluntly apparent that Sean's turned an unhealthy shade of pink. He kinda stares at the floor, as Mercy answers two earlier questions at once while slipping the baggy jersey off; the first for the television audience, that being the flattering piece of shameful g-string glory she's got on underneath the shirt. The second--as to whether or not she's got the matching top on--is answered as Sean pulls a Roger Rabbit doubletake. The legs take their leave from the shot, careful saunter over Sean's shoes. Brakas doesn't blink.)
Brakas: Sloane's.. and.. his guys.. (The jersey makes a return trip, thrown bluntly to land on Sean's head. He acts like he's had an electric eel shoved down his pants, quickly ripping it off his head and trying to relax.)
Brakas: Sloane. What about Sloane?! (Doman's listening no more intently than he was when Mercy first showed up in the shot, but at least he gives Sean a friendly pat on the thigh before rising and moving to follow off wherever Jen's headed.)
Doman: Do me a favor, man. Lock the door when you leave. Brakas: B-b-b.. you can't afford to.. (Schwahling's hand waves him off as a secondplace thought, the last thing we see from the J-Crown champion before he's gone.)
Doman: Later, Sean, later. (Another door slamming. And then.. nothing. But Sean. The film geek sits there for a long moment, in a dully perplexed haze. Just fondling the jersey in his lap in a neurotic motion, eyes still locked on the afterburn of Doman and Mercy.)
(He suddenly comes to, clumsily rising and reaching for the camera. A brief shake of his head, slight disgust there, and the picture flickers out.)
