[OPINION REQUEST] Nathan Young, SCCW Commish

21 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I normally handle the Super Obnoxious "Incredible" Idol Austin. Recently, for
SCCW, a fed I run, I did this interview as part of angle that is being worked in and out of SCCW. I am looking for people to give me their opinions of it.
All are welcome, please keep the flames to a minimum :-) [Rebroadcast from last night's edition of "SCCW Wild Wednesday"] Phil: Fans, I am told that we have some pre-recorded comments from SCCW Co-Commissioner Nathan Young that he would like for all of you to see. Alex: That’s right Phil. As I understand it, Mr. Young was not happy about the proclamation that Donald Vaccaro made on the August 12th Edition of Wild
Wednesday concerning the brewing friction between The Powers of Arrogance and
The Ring Syndicate. It is too bad that Young is not open minded enough to see that Mr. Vaccaro was right.
Will: For those of you who do not remember, Mr. Vaccaro pledged the full support of SCCW behind The PoA in that growing conflict. In his interview, he stated that the Powers of Arrogance were the wave of the future while The Ring
Syndicate were, for all intents and purposes, relics of a better forgotten past.
Phil: We have been anxiously awaiting Nathan Young’s response to this, and before now, he had been very quiet about it. So without further adieu, let’s roll the tape.
[Scene shifts to the corner office of SCCW Headquarters in downtown Las Vegas. The camera pans around the office one time, and the relative plainness of the room shows. There is some sign of the excess one normally associates with people in high places, but not much. The camera focuses in on a large oak desk.
Behind it on the wall, hang 15 championship belts, each one indicating a World
Championship Nathan Young has won. The camera pulls back some and focuses in on
Nathan Young as he prepares to speak.]
Good evening SCCW. I hate to take up time that I know several fans out there believe is better spent on actual wrestling rather than watching a “suit” make some announcement, but I believe that this is important enough that everyone should take the time to listen.
On the August 12, 1998 edition of “SCCW Wild Wednesday”, my counterpart, one
Donald Vaccaro, took it upon himself to come on the air and make some comments regarding the growing friction between the factions, The Powers of Arrogance, and The Ring Syndicate.
I am not sure exactly what possessed him to comment on it, since neither faction has a wrestler under SCCW contract at this moment. Maybe it was for cheap publicity, which Donald Vaccaro excels in, or maybe it was just another reason to get himself on TV.
Regardless of what his motives were, the message he delivered was quite clear. Clear, that is, if you are Donald Vaccaro, or one of his toadies. Donald Vaccaro took it upon himself to speak on behalf of Sin City Championship Wrestling, and then proceeded to announce that SCCW was firmly behind The
Powers of Arrogance in their feud with The Ring Syndicate, calling the
Syndicate “fossils” and saying that they had no place in wrestling. I am aware that there may be several fans, as well as SCCW wrestlers and staffers who may believe that, but I am not one of them. To come out and say that The Powers of Arrogance were the wave of the future and that The Ring
Syndicate was something better left in the past, was nothing short of ignorant.
I for one do not support Donald Vaccaro’s announcement, nor do I endorse the message he delivered in it. I tend to take a more objective view on things, and when I look at what is happening between the Syndicate and PoA, I see what could possibly be a Feud of the Year candidate in the making.
But, looking further into it, it is my belief that it is a feud that will be won by The Ring Syndicate. Being a 15 time former World Heavyweight Wrestling
Champion in my own right, I see the value of experience, and what can be accomplished with it. I also know the hidden advantages that are to be had when your opponent brushes you off as a “relic of a better forgotten past”, and fails to take you seriously.
I look at The Powers of Arrogance, and I see everything that is wrong with wrestling today. The PoA are the total embodiment of the word “excessive”. They are excessive in the way they make their entrances to their matches, excessive in the way they carry themselves outside of the ring, and excessive in the homes they buy. They spend money like it is water, and have no respect for anyone that is not in their “Inner Circle”.
In spite of their excesses, they are what can only be described as wildly successful. You will not see me try to question the success of the Powers of
Arrogance because they are all individually some of the best wrestlers the sport has known. I trained one of their members, “Incredible” Idol Austin, when he was first breaking into this sport. I do not condemn their abilities, only their attitude.
When I first broke into the wrestling scene back in what many of you would probably call “The Stone Age” while snickering behind your hands, it was a much different sport. Sure, you had your wrestlers who thought they were better than everyone around them, but there was none of the excess today’s sport has.
Everyone for the most part respected one another, and had only a love for the sport, and for the fans who supported them week in and week out in arenas around the world.
I was a rising talent in the sport in those days, and I also had a love for the sport of wrestling and its fans. I would often spend hours after a given show signing autographs for the crowd that always gathered outside of the back entrance, some hoping to catch a glimpse of their heroes, others looking for a keepsake of the sport they loved. Wrestlers lived in hotel rooms, and rented out run down apartments. There were no multi million dollar mansions, and expensive condos and penthouses. Only the bare essentials. If anyone DID get one of the penthouses, or the condos, you always knew who was throwing the party after the show.
The most Holy thing back in those days was tradition. Everyone wanted to experience it, and become a part of it, all while writing new chapters in history so others would remember them. And it was an ever-continuing cycle. The tradition thrived, and the sport with it.
When I think of The Ring Syndicate, I think of the days when I was starting out as a wrestler. I think of the tradition they represent. They have their share of excess, but that unfortunately is a part of today’s sport that will never go away.
The Powers of Arrogance do not know what it is like to represent tradition. The
Ring Syndicate does. The Ring Syndicate ruled the roost in the old GWA, and they did it their own way. They broke a few rules from time to time, but then, what wrestler hasn’t? I had a period in my own career where I broke the rules and cheated to win. But the point of this is not who cheats and who doesn’t, it is about what is right and what should be the focus of wrestling.
The Syndicate, through whatever means they accomplished winning what they did, gained their success. But, they never forgot the tradition of the sport they competed in. Men like Classy Freddie Blassy III and Jon Atlas, come from a rich wrestling tradition, both being second or third generation athletes. Ken
Nemesis and Tex Steel are awesome competitors as well. For people to look at these men and say they are fossils when they are merely experienced are as ignorant as those who worship the Powers of Arrogance. Just because something is the past, does not mean it should be forgotten. Think to yourselves how many times you have heard “Those who do not learn from history are doomed to repeat it?” Truer words have never been spoken.
Over the last two decades or so, there have been several wrestlers who have been called “relics”, or “fossils”, or “old-timers” who went into that ring and showed the person calling them that why they were who they were, and why they had the success that they had. That is history, and it is something that should be learned from. But, there are some who refuse to learn from the mistakes of others, and the PoA, like so many others, will be doomed to learn from history.
Without a history, there can never be a future. The Ring Syndicate is a part of history, but they have a place waiting for them in the future. I for one look forward to seeing what they accomplish, and I wish them luck and good fortune in their feud against The Powers of Arrogance.
I do not say that on behalf of SCCW, nor on the behalf of anyone in it save for myself. Because I am not like Donald Vaccaro. I have both more common sense and more consideration for the opinions of others to do something that blatant.
Thank you very much for your time, and please, enjoy the rest of the card.
