[Critique of Roleplay] James Morgan

23 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Figured I'd put this up on RSPWF to get some feedback and critiques. And, don't sweat it guys. I ain't Iceberg or Muraco. I can handle the criticism.
Right now, Morgan is unemployed and I'm looking for a Fed to join with him. Just something stable and somewhere Morgan can fit in and not be "just another." His gimmick, in plain is just a rebel with a cause. I guess you could say a
Rebellious Enigma. Very different and sometimes, different just to be difficult. His desire to go against the grain is sometimes his nature and sometimes, just for the sake of doing so.
So again, any critique is appreciated. Thanks in advance, - Mike E. ======= [The visibility gradually increases upon a man standing in a small bathroom. It appears to be a Public Bathroom as there are three urinals on the far wall and two men stand at the separate sinks, washing their hands. Amidst it all, men entering and leaving, there is one who sticks out. Attired in a torn, tattered, and dirty Leather Jacket with a large Anarchy Symbol on the back with a pair of disgusting and grungy green jean shorts, and black boots. As the man looks up into the mirror, and dries his face off with a towel, he slowly begins to become fixated on the piece of glass. Reaching into his pocket, the man who is now identified as 'Cold Hearted' James Morgan, pulls out a small container of an unknown object.
A Toilet is flushed as the sound carries off screen. The door creaks open and slams shut as a man stands besides James Morgan, hunched over the sink washing his hands. Morgan turns to him, nonchalantly and speaks in a low, raspy tone.]
'CH' JM: Hey . . . [Silence] Hey, . . . [Finally, the man stands upright and turns to James Morgan, drying his face off with a paper towel. Before speaking, he looks around and sees he's all alone.]
MAN: Um, . . . yeah? 'CH' JM: Are you most people? M: Huh? 'CH' JM: Are you most people? M: Um, . . I'm not sure I follow, man. 'CH' JM: Do you consider yourself an 'average' person? An everyday person? M: Oh um, . . . Yeah, I guess so. I'm a typical guy, so uh, . . . yeah, you could call me 'most people.'
'CH' JM: Me? . . I won't call you anything. [An awkward feeling rises as Morgan dips his index finger into his small container and begins to run it across the mirror. The man looks on inquisitively at the mirror, which is now covered in red paint, which reads . .
. Most People & EVR = Fools Morgan places the tin can on the edge of the sink and casts a slow, subtle glare at the stranger. The man side steps to his left to fully see the mirror, and as his head moves across the letters, he steps back and turns to Morgan.]
M: Fool, huh? Well, who the hell are you Mr. [BEEP]ing Perfect? Who are you to label anyone, you god damn freak?
[Obviously angry, the man gets in Morgan's face, pounding his index finger into
James chest.]
Think you're something special, huh? Look at you, you're a filthy freak. Surprised you even come out during the day. [Morgan slouches back against the wall and slowly smirks.] 'CH' JM: Only to meet people like you. I labeled you. You've labeled me. I believe you can go now.
M: Hey, listen up punk-ass, don't you tell me when I can leave and when I can't. No one will tell me what to do. Especially some freak like you.
[The man slams his fist into the Garbage Can and then kicks it over. All the while, James Morgan silently chuckles to himself. The door slams shut as the man leaves, off screen.]
'CH' JM: There must be a point to all of this, Ethan. There must be some rhyme or reason as to why I bothered to come here. But must there? Does there have to be an answer for everything? Or do I just not care to explain to you why I am here? Although, it is quite simple to figure out. You were quite adamant in saying most people cannot hurt you. Such an unintelligent remark.
I say that because that fool I just had this displeasure of meeting could hurt you. Infact, he could destroy you. No one is invincible, Ethan. Perhaps it is time for you learn that. For you see, the simple fact that you believe so strongly
Ethan Van Ryan is untouchable has just awarded me the victory of this match-up.
Mentally, I have you in a corner, back peddling but there's no where to go. So what do you do? [Morgan smirks, as he shakes his head in disgust.] You make things even simpler and you fall victim to self-hype. It is only wise to build one up if their certain they'll never fall. And it is only sensical to know everyone falls. Even James Morgan has fallen. And despite your claims to have never descended, .. Sunday Night I guarentee you that you will Fall.
Yes, .. you shall Fall Sunday. Into a _DEEP DEPRESSION!_ [Morgan turns to the mirror and in faint streaks of red, begins to write something. After a few seconds, he turns away and walks out as the Camera zooms in on the mirror. . .
EVR = EXAMPLE Fade . . to black.]
