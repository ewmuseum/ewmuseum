Devon Case-Free Agent RP

6 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The Golden Nugget, the creme of the crop located on the Vegas Strip, bustling with men in black suits and men with no cash, all pursuing that one vital dream. That dream of holding what is most dearest to the human lifeform, money, the green stuff. The camera swings in through the front doors into the elaborate casino area with the $500 minumum craps tables over to the left and the bar stretching across the right where a few ladies and men are sitting, laughing, and
sipping cocktails, two of them are Devon Case and Precious, wearing a shockingly gorgeous blue one piece while Devon is decked out in a dark blue suit and tie. He is one of those men that signs little kids autographs and gives back to the community. )
(A man, obviously intoxicated, walks over to Devon Case with a pen and a pad of paper.) Man:Oh...my...uh, are you Devon Case. Devon Case: (obviously agitated) Yes, yes it is, what do you want. Man:I was hoping you could possibly sign this for my son Jonathan. Devon Case: (grabs the pen and paper and throws it in a nearby trashcan) Can't do it pretty boy, let me ask you something. Where were you when I needed some help packing my luggage in my mansion yesterday?
Where were you when I had to carry that same luggage up to my penthouse suite this afternoon? Where were the fans quote unquote when I lost $20,000 on the Craps tables earlier tonight? Where were you guys, where was the encouragement?
(Case stands in the man's face) Devon Case:I will answer those for you. You were at home or in your room picking your butt, just hoping to get a glance of a real man in
Las Vegas. And you just so happened to be walking in the casino the same time Devon Case was there and had just enough marbles to give him a try. But it backfired, because I am smart and a lowly common man like yourself is not, I am rich and you are not, I am special and talented and you are not. So I got two things for you. First, tell your kid never, never ever to listen to a band like Hanson or the Backstreet Boys, and second, well, you get 2 seconds to get out of my face before you wind up bloodied and on the floor. One...T--
(The man hightails out of the casino, not stopping once to check back. Case sits back down and orders a drink, Precious turns to Case, looking a little annoyed.) Precious: (sarcastic) Like to see you keep it close knit with your fans DC. Wonder what would happen if that so-called common man flip-flopped with you and I wonder then what he would have done.
Damn, you are so full of yourself you know that, have a few drinks and you become even more of an...well you know. Besides, all the guy asked for was an autograph, he was a good guy trying to please his kid and you almost put a boot in his mouth, sometimes I just don't get you.
(Devon wheels the swivel chair to face Precious eager to speak) Devon Case:Oh come on! That guy wanted the autograph strictly for himself, his name was Jonathan I bet, he deserved a boot to the head is what he deserved. Just cause I am in the WWO and the fame grows from there doesn't mean I have to take it with hugs and kisses and crap like that.
Precious:Well, what about the WWO, Mr.Hotshot, who do you think has enough decency and fame to grace your presence, in the squared circle, and don't give me some two-bit pimp out on the street that you met before you came in here to meet me.
(Devon Case sarcastically goes into deep thought and then smirks. He orders another drink, a Long Island Iced-T by the way, and turns to face Precious)
Devon Case: (very sarcastically) Well, my dear, I think the answer would be, well, nobody. Nobody has the honor to face me so what I figure is that I will tell you who I already dislike and then give you the Devon Case Truth about why I dislike them sound good.
(Precious nods yes) Devon Case:Alrighty, first person will have to be (chuckles) "The Pretty Hate Machine" Travis Mannig. Pretty pathetic is more his style, all hateful and stuff. Damn, makes me look good, and that is horribly bad. He also is what some would call a bonafide cruiserweight, but I think he fits more in the One More Person For
Devon To Runover On His Way To Excellence category. See, Mannig is a man who fears everybody, and when you fear everybody, then in turn everybody becomes your enemy, some may like it that way like say Pee Wee Herman.
Precious: (cuts him off) What the hell does Pee Wee Herman have to do with anything. You are really stretching, really.
Devon Case: (cuts back in) Now wait, I guarantee he made all enemies at that theatre he was caught at right? Yes, I am right so that is very correct, and of course, I am always correct, so don't interrupt me again, thanks. Mannig likes Marilyn Manson, and if that doesn't annoy the hell out of one Devon Case well then, his choice of apparel and abilities does. He considers himself talented, I consider him a nobody, a vagabond, a never-will, and just one name in what will be a longline of Devon Case victims. So, you wanted to know the scoop, there it is, now lets go play some more craps and get DC's money back.
(Devon Case flips a fairly large bill to the bartender and sticks out his arm towards Precious)
Devon Case:Shall we? (Precious wraps her arm around his) Precious:We shall. (The camera fades away as Devon hits the craps table.) -- [name withheld] Wrestler:Devon Case E-Feds He Wrestles:WWO 0-0, CAWF 4-0 (TV Champ) AIM Name:[detail withheld] ICQ#:[detail withheld]
