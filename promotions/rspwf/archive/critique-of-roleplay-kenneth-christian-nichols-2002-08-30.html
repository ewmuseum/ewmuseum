[Critique of Roleplay] Kenneth Christian Nichols

30 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"I'm an idiot." - Ryan Shaffer [Excerpt from "Joke of The Day"] "You bet your ass you are." - KCN [Upon watching "Joke of The Day" air.] [Fade in ... Sun. Bright sun light, as a matter of fact, shining down on Revere Beach in
Revere, Massachusetts. The end of summer is approaching and this is one of the last good beach days left. Hundreds upon hundreds of people; men, women, children, are out on the beach, some in the water, some laying down, others bicycling, and a few rollerblading. It's quite the scene. As the view pans to the left, there is a cliff over looking the beach. The top of the cliff is the greenest grass you've ever seen with random park benches placed over it. It's a park, as a matter of fact. A few random bodies tan in the sun as passerby's take a time to catch a great view of the beach.
As the view widens a bit more, there is a white 2003 Lincoln Navigator, tinted windows, and chrome 22' ADR's. The song "Parabola" by Tool is blaring out of the back of the truck where the hatch window is lifted up. All four doors are open as well as tan leather interior can be seen. Sitting on the roof of the vehicle, back pressed against the windshield is a shirtless Kenneth Christian
Nichols. He is making quite the scene, nearly making every woman who passes by stop dead in her tracks. Nichols has on a pair of faded blue Sean John carpenter shorts, black Air Force One's, and a pair of Polo Sport shades. He sports his new hair cut, a round up, by running his left hand over his extremely short hair. KCN looks amazing, to say the least, but that isn't to say come Most Wanted this perfect body could gain a new imperfections. KCN looks dead into the camera, trying to pull his attention from all of the beautiful women walking by.]
KENNETH ... CHRISTIAN ... NICHOLS: Immortal Wrestling, welcome to Revere Beach. When I first came to the States about three months ago in hopes of finding some of the oh-so-much-talked-about "great" wrestling ... I also asked the travel agent some hot spots for a young man looking to have a great time.
And somehow, someway - Revere Beach was on that list. So, sparing no expenses,
I took a few days off and came up to Boston. And my, oh my, has it been a wonderful week. I've been to every dance club, saw the New York Yankees pound the Red Sox 6 to nothing on Tuesday night, went back to Penn for a required
Drug Test, IW's wonderful policies, came back up here Thursday and played basketball at Cambridge Park, and today I'm at Revere Beach. The weather is expected to get really bad - really fast so I'm taking advantage of it. But you know, despite all the fun I've been having - there is one thing I just haven't been able to get off my mind.
[KCN shakes his head, appearing to be somewhat pissed off at the fact there's been something lingering all week long.]
Ryan Shaffer. I saw his little promo. I took every word and thought it through. Granted, the majority of his whine-fest was directed at Cyrus
Williams. A pretender for the crown, namely IW's Restricted Title. But Williams is another topic for another day. I have to focus on one moron at a time and today's lucky winner is the Human Arsehole, Ryan Shaffer. You see,
Shaft, ... can I call you Shaft? You damn right I can because I said I will.
You see, Shaft ... I know how you're approaching this match. And because I know exactly how you're preparing, how you're training, and what you're telling
yourself - I can't help but do one thing. [KCN abruptly breaks out into laughter, causing a lot of pedestrians to stop and take notice to this random guy with a camera and earsplitting music laughing his ass off. It's all for naught, tho'. KCN was pretending to laugh, much like Cyrus Williams pretends to be good and Ryan Shaffer pretends to be intimidating.]
I can picture you now in the Immortal Wrestling training facility ... preaching to yourself, trying to get yourself motivated ...
[KCN sits up and in a mocking, high pitched voice begins talking ...] "I'm a good wrassla!... I deserve better treatment! I don't have a small pee-pee... I like women, not men... I will one day move out of my parents basement ... I will beat up that big, bad bully KCN ... even if it's only in my dreams ... And last, but SORE-TIN-LEE not least ... I _am_ special! My momma said so!" I hope you believe all of that, Ryan. It's good to use your imagination and convince yourself of such abstract, nonsensical, _bullshit_. It gives you hope. Hope that one day you'll amount to more than a rainbow colored singlet wearing piece of trash no one will _ever_ give two shits and a left nut about.
I'm sorry, I hate to be the bearer of bad news Shaft but that is just how life is. There are certain people who have been divinely selected to be amazing, some to be good, some to be decent, and others to be downright pitiful. It's called variety. If everyone was as good as KCN, would it be as big a deal?
No, but morons and half-wits like you make me look like a billion dollars ... when I can only make myself look like 250 million. So you see, Shaft ... you're not a total waste of space. You do serve some purpose, albeit little and insignificant as it may be because there are plenty like you who can replace you and probably be less bitchier about it, but I digress. Keep your chin up, little buddy! Keep fighting! Jus' like the Little Engine that Could,
Shaft ...
I think I can ... I think I can ... I think I can ... I think I can ... [KCN sits up, his eyes nearly bulging from his head.] I think I can _definitely_ kick you piss out of Ryan Shaffer. [Chuckle.] And come Most Wanted, I think I can do just that ... for all the paying fans in attendance and the twenty or so watching at 1:30 AM on a random Saturday morning. So Shaffer, don't get down on yourself, man. You at least have some longevity.
You're not just the joke of the day, ... but the joke of tomorrow, the joke of the week, the joke of the month, the joke of the year, the decade, the next half-century, the next century, the next millennium ... you are everlasting,
Shaffer ...
As a big _fucking_ joke. [Ear-to-ear smirk ...] Believe that. [Fade to black.]
