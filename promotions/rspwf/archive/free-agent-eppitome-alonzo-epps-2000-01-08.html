Part I: Personal info •Your screen name: [detail withheld] •Your first name (optional): Casey Part II: Basic Wrestler Info •Wrestler Name: "Eppitome" Alonzo Epps (Yes, I know Eppitome is spelled wrong) •Real Name: Alonzo Blackmon-Epps •Hometown: Queens, New York •Date of Birth: 6-17-78 Appearance- •Height: 6'1 •Weight: 220 •Build: Atletic •Heel/Face: Neutral, But Leans Face •Gimmick Be VERY descritptive: Alonzo is an aspiring rapper. Knowing it isn't easy to become a rapper in todays industry, he turns to something else he knows, fighting. While not Technically a "Street Thug" in the sense of the word, Alonzo knows his way around the streetfight. He also grew up wrestling, and thought it may be for him, so he trained with Johnny Rods in New York City since the age of 19. Now, at 21, he has been cleared by Rods to compete in the ranks of Pro-Wrestling, while trying to boost his rap career as "Eppitome" at the same time. He does this by speaking in Rhymed Verse every time he cuts an interview, trying to impress record execs while berating opponents.
•Basic Appearance Description (Run down on how he would look, wrestling gear, coming down to the ring, to the fans): Eppitome is a black male. He's not very dark, yet isn't too light skinned. He has a neatly trimmed thin mustache, and a small goatee on the bottom of his chin (It doesn't connect). His black hair has a close cut haircut. To the ring he wears a quilted blue leather jacket which he leaves open, and takes off before wrestling. He also wears black sunglasses and a blue bandana rolled and tied in the front. He wears blue baggy leather pants, along with a Black Elbow Pad on his right arm, and black sneakers on his feet. He has no tatoos on his body, or physical scars.
