Mark Langseth [EMWC] Putting his past ways behind him, Mark Langseth has been distancing himself from his stablemates in Redemption so far this year as he tries to make a better name for himself with the fans of the E. Teaming with Jake Shaw, he recently won the EMWC Tag Titles against Dynasty in one helluva contest completing the Triple Crown for his EMWC career. A mainstay in the Empire,
Mark Langseth is simply a model of consistency.
24. Tony Cameron [GLCW] Tony Cameron has taken the GLCW by storm since making his debut; he proved himself as a solid main eventer in his feud against Killdozer, and shocked the world when he won the GLCW World Crown at GLCW: Dead by Dawn. Cameron has proven himself a worthy champion, retaining his title in matches against
Juan Vasquez and Jacob Cross, and his current program with Tim Sanders has further elevated Cameron & cemented his status as the #1 guy in the company.
Competing with names like Tommy James, The Gremlin, and Chris Courtade for air time, Tony Cameron has not only proven himself to be a step above the rest, but a champion's champion.
23. Jean Paul Valley [SPW, IGA] This crowd favorite stunned audiences for the first half of 2002, ending a long rivalry with Andy Madison at the recent IGA pay-per-view. Being quite the athlete, he was on his way to hall of fame status at SPW's Shootfire
Icons pay-per-view last month, but an unfortunate accident at the end of the night's opening match cut his dreams short. He was the first man to defeat
Corey Irons in over a year, and is well on his way to becoming one of the biggest names in this business.
22. Tyrone Hayes [WWO] A mainstay and long-time veteran of the rings of the WWO, and a former champion, Tyrone Hayes is a major member of the Upper Crust. Having destroyed a former comrade in Latex Lex by bashing his knee in with a cane,
Hayes continues to run roughshod over the federation. Look for many more good things in the final months of the year 2002 from Tyrone "Purple" Hayes.
21. Rosco Riggins [GWC/MPW] All hail the G/M champion! Rosco Riggins took the G/M by storm the past six months, gaining the belt by defeating "No Mercy" Jeff Flotd and making himself the man in the upstart company. He also was starting to make big strides in the RCW just before it closed, but that hasn't slowed him down one bit. Although his next money feud in the G/M seems to be rather indecisive, expect only good things from this rugged blend of technical and power wrestling.
20. Greg Brians [SPW] The current SPW World champion is on a roll, and a big one at that. Having defeated the Reaper just over a month ago for the big strap, Brians defends his title nearly every show. With his wife Jasolyn standing by his side,
Greg is on his way to possibly becoming the greatest SPW champion ever.
19. JW Oswald [NWC] The Devil Himself. JW Oswald was the leader of the Uppermost Echelon until they threw him out not too long ago. He has held virtually every NWC singles title ever and is always smack dab right in the thick of things. He ruled the SCCW with an iron fist before the reorganization of the NWC, and is truly a modern day NWC legend. Rumor has it that this could very well be his final year in the business, and if so, he will truly be missed by the masses.
18. November [EMWC] The pale aerial showman has really turned heads in the big E as of late with his death-defying offensive repertoire and his drive to be the best junior heavyweight in the World today. Currently in a blood feud with the Messiah,
November is excelling and getting more airtime and continues to prove why many tout him as the next big thing in the junior division, period.
17. Chris Colton [OSW] "The Riverside Thriller" has taken OSW by storm establishing himself as perhaps the OSW's top heel with his brash and arrogant prettyboy style and finesse. [Compiler's Note: "The Riverside Thriller" is simply a kickass moniker.]
16. Juan Vasquez [EMWC, GLCW] The off-beat Mexican superstar shone in the rings of the River City before it closed down, opting to spread out across the globe by entering the Empire and the GLCW shortly thereafter. His match with Devon Case a few weeks ago in the Empire was what many considered his breakout moment as he sets aim on becoming the household name he so rightfully deserves to be. A blend of comedy with a ridiculously fluid workrate, Juan Vasquez could very well be the next big thing in all of professional wrestling.
15. Eric Idol [NIWF] The "Popstar" has made a name for himself in the NIWF, having captured the tag team titles alongside long time ally T-Bone. His humorous gimmick and witty attitude always makes him a target for NIWF stars, and he takes on all comers without much difficulty.
14. AsH [SPW, IGA, NEO] The Cruiserweight Icon, living kickout, and greatest high flyer in SPW history. He achieved SPW Hall of Fame status at the Icons pay-per-view last month, being one of only four men in the history of the game to rise to the occasion. He enjoyed a reign earlier this year as the NIWF North American champion, and is currently a top contender for the IGA Junior Heavyweight title. Good job, AsH!
13. Hannibal Carver [EMWC, NEO] Thus far, 2002 has been the year that made Carver. The year started with
Carver working as a booking agent for BSCW, working the occasional match here and there. Through a friend of a friend of a friend, Carver was given a chance in NEO, and excelled, quickly winning that promotion's
Pacific Championship. He continues to hold it to this day, with the bloody
Street Fight at the Rebirth PPV being a highlight, as well as his current hate-filled fued with Matt Saunders. Carver became a quick cult favorite through his work in NEO, and the calls started flying in. He has since debuted in EMWC, where he's started a program with this Mid-Years'
most underrated wrestler, "El Cholo" Juan Vasquez. In GLCW, Carver came out of nowhere, bloodying Rob Christie with a branding iron and starting a feud with the unstoppable Killdozer. Finally, in GWC/MPW, Carver rushed in during the Battle Royal at Quake of Fire, eliminating a score of G/M superstars and most notably, setting Fang on fire, ending the e-w veteran's comeback and possibly his career.
12. William Craven [WWO] The root of evil is back! Craven is a human nightmare, truly. The recent
"metamorphosis" into a _true_ monster (more so than he even used to be, wich is hard to imagine) is definitely the stuff of nightmares. This guy's return to the WWO has already gonna made a lot of the WWO roster shake in their boots... well... more than he already has done in the past.
11. Dark Soul [WWO] Your current WWO World champion has also, like Tyrone Hayes, been a mainstay in the ever-consistent WWO. Many in the company feel this man is over-talented, underrated, and unrightfully goes unnotcied by the masses in the industry and nobody can tell you why. Well we can tell you this, 2002 may very well be his breakout year among the masses.
10. Corey Irons [NIWF, SPW] The poster boy for the NIWF and SPW, This year marked a triumphant return for Corey Irons. He made a BIG impact as the "mystery man" for the NIWF
Royal Rumble this past Spring, and had a strong showing by finishing second in that event. With his impressive record, he has now earned himself a
World title shot against Sean Steele at the upcoming Civil War supercard event. Having held the NIWF International title earlier this year,
Irons is also enjoying a strong winning streak in the IGA, and is showing promise in SPW and N.E.W. well.
9. Jake Shaw [EMWC] He kicked off 2002 by becoming the longest reigning EMWC Television Champion ever, and then picked up the EMWC North American Title to see if lightning could strike twice, and it just might: Shaw recently won the World Tag Team
Titles with Mark Langseth (Shaw became a Triple Crown "Lite" winner in the process), and he's currently closing in on another record breaking title reign with the North American Title. As the second half of the year begins, there's only one question everyone has on their minds: when the hell is this guy going to lose?
8. Alex Martinez [EMWC] He's back! Yep, the Last American Badass is returning to his top form as of late as he has his sights set on the next EMWC Blood, Sweat, and Tears 2002 and his World Title match against Devon Case and the champ, Steve Spector.
But that's not all, Alex has also appeared on Pure American Wrestling to fight it's champion, and had the highly publicized contest with Brody
Thunder at Wrestlebowl. One of the biggest names in all of wrestling, Alex
Martinez is striving to become "the man" once again.
7. Luke Kinsey [EMWC] The next Eddie Van Gibson? He very well could be as Kinsey has hit the mainstream wrestling scene with a big splash in the EMWC as well as being one of the cornerstones of the old RCW. His match with Alex Extreme at RCWs
Caged Rage 2 turned a lot of heads, and his consistent in-ring and mic work
in the Empire has garnered him quite a following in recent months. A hot feud with Mike Waldrop only heldped solidify his position in the ranks of the Empire and now a wonderful feud with Johnny Pleasance should help propel this man right to the top of the ladder! Only good things are in store for one, Luke Kinsey.
6. "Bad Eye" McBaine [GIW] The one and the only GIW Heavyweight Champion to date is as ruthless and maniacal as they come. McBaine unretired last December to put the small independent on his back as he rode into town and took the Heavyweight title from Leviticus Nelson in a highly publicized contest that took #2 in the
Match of the Mid Year polls. His latest feud with Russ Brady has been nothing short of spectacular, as he awaits Brady in New Orleans at the next supercard with his belt on the line! All in all, McBaine is back and back with a hateful vengeance!
5. Chris Courtade [EMWC, GLCW] The man. Simply put, Chris Courtade is widely regarded as the next legend in our sport and he proves it nightly in both the EMWC and the GLCW, as well as his past exploits in the now defunct RCW. From his hot feud with Jeff
Matthews to his current blockbuster feud with Caleb Temple in the EMWC, to his smokin' feud with Tommy James in the GLCW, to his retiring of Ken Curtis in the RCW, Courtade is at the pinnacle of his career and the sport in general. The household nhame of household names, you don't get much better than one, Chris Courtade.
4. The Gremlin [EMWC, GLCW] A top level player in both GLCW and EMWC, The Gremlin carried the GLCW on his back from October through May, reigning supreme as champion and decimating his competition. Although overlooked, his feud with Adam Donovan leading into GLCW Dead by Dawn was nothing short of stellar, and it was this most recent title reign that secured The Gremlin a spot in the GLCW Hall of
Fame. In the EMWC, he became the latest King of the Death Match, defeating
Alex Martinez at NIA 2002, and then came up just a tad short of winning the
EMWC once again only a few hours later against Steve Spector. One of the main thorns in the side of Redemption, all is good in the camp of perhaps the best pure brawler in the industry today.
3. Steve Spector [EMWC] The longest reigning EMWC/Empire champion _ever_. That's right, Steve
Spector pulled the impossible last year as he finally won the World Title and to further pour salt in the woulds of the corrupt owner Chris Blue,
Spector has held it for the longest reign of any World champion in the company's history! His current feud with Devon Case and Alex Martinez may prove to be his most daunting task to date as the trio fights over not only the title, but the honor of being called the EMWC Ace as well. If anybody is up to the task, the unlikely hero from New Jersey is that man!

1. [Tie] Devon Case [EMWC] The Golden God. Mr. Match of the Year. And now the co #1 man in the world today! Devon Case has been climbing up the ladder in the EMWC at an alarmingly high rate and has been given the title of "hottest rising star in the entire history of the EMWC". His feud and subsequent match with Tiger
Claw at EMWCs NIA 2002 were voted #1 in the Mid-Year polls as Case ended the career of the bonafide legend. On deck? A shot at the greatest prize in the sport, the EMWC World title, at Blood, Sweat, and Tears 2002 against
Alex Martinez and Steve Spector. A monster task for a monster ego, but crazier things have happened.
1. [Tie] Caleb Temple [EMWC] Who better to share the honor than the most evil man in the business? Caleb
Temple epitomizes consistency and downright nastiness as he has found new lows to exploit in his enemies, never passing on an opporutnity to scar an opponent for life. Latest victim? Adam Rogers, who was out for months after NIA 2002 thanks to some friendly fire from Caleb and his lady, Rhoni.
What could possibly make matters worse for the world? Caleb and Rhoni had a child! Truth Marie! And it looks like Caleb may have turned a new leaf, adopting the slogan Real Men Wrestle Clean?! We gotta see it to believe it, and if we here were Chris Courtade, his latest target, we wouldn't believe it for one second. Nevertheless, Temple is perhaps the most well-respected name in the business and a true warrior in the ring.
