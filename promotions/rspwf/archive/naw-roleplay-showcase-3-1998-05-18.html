=================================== NAW Tag Champs "The Bladeliners" =================================== {Fresh off of their stunning defeat of the Towers of Power, The Bladeliners are in a very good mood and as they have their coveted tag team straps around their shoulders, they are making their way back to the dressing room and a camera is in hot pursuit.}
{Once back in their locker room, they begin to start joking and laughing about the match with The Towers of Power. The camera picks up Jade saying that his mother could have done much better, as J.W. laughs. They notice the camera and turn around. Jade and J.W. are both adorned still in their wrestling gear, which consists of matching red trunks with "Bladeliners" in black on the back.
Their black knee pads are very saturated with sweat, as well as the rest of their bodies. Jade speaks.}
Jade: {Taking his hair down from the back.} What you just saw in the INWI was total destruction of two moronic plebians, who think that their muscles will get them tag team gold and we all know that it takes a hell of alot more than that to be the best.
<They both start laughing> Jade: Now, on to bigger and better things here in the NaW. While we do not have a match scheduled for Tuesday Night Tango, we do have something planned for Sunday Showdown and it is a date with you DWR and ya know what Dog? I saw your victory tonight in the INWI, by DQ? Can't you do any better than a DQ?
Guess not. What I want you and all these other low life, 9-5 working sweathogs is this: "Come that cage match, I am going to make your life a living hell."
We have a sorted history you and I, but come Sunday I am going to send you running from the NAW like a dog in heat running from its suitor.
{This brings laughter from the Bladeliners and the camera men in the back.}
Jade: In closing DWR, it has been fun and I have thoroughly enjoyed kicking the dog s---, ooops no pun intended. Anyway DWR, when we enter the cage and I walk out after the match and you are being carried away by the medic on duty there, it will be finished between us and that's the truth. Got anything to say, J.W.?
<J.W. nods and approaches the camera with his NaW tag team gold around his shoulder. He speaks.>
J.W. Storm: You hit the nail right on top of its head, Jade. Just like we have beaten the last four teams that we have faced: Puff and Stuff Stryke
Force, Nirashima and his buddy, Crow food and BBB and let's not forget the
Towers that need showers...
(Jade laughs) J.W. Storm: We have put our plan into motion as one of the dominant tag teams in the NAW and ya know what else?
<Jade nods> J.W. Storm: These tag team title belts are ours. We won them and we shall defend them. I just can't help but to wonder about those Good Ol' Boys that were top contenders for the gold not long ago, where did they run off to?
{Jade replies} Jade: Who cares, probably bumping uglies with their cousins. <J.W. almost falls over with laughter and then replies.> J.W. Storm: More than likely. Well, these belts are here to stay with us and we have open contracts. Any team that wants a title match, just come on and let's get it on. Come on partner, I am going to treat you to a night on the town.
(Before leaving, Jade holds up his half of the tag team titles and replies.) Jade: We are on top, want to knock us off? Come and get it, boys. And DWR, remember your date with destiny? You pathetic plebian, commoner.
{The scene ends} =================================== Video Presentation =================================== {The Bladeliners are truly a fantastic team. At a recent television taping, the fans were treated to a video of the Bladeliners, marking their recent success and accomplishments.}
<Sharp dressed man by ZZ Top begins to play.> <TRANSMISSION> {A limosine pulls into a local NaW arena, the Bladeliners step out with Tuxedo suits on. The picture fades and shows J.W. Storm in the ring, giving Buff
Miller the Storm Front. From there J.W. is leap frogging one of the Towers of
Power and then delivers a devestating power slam again to Buff Miller. The next clip shows Jade smiling in his bath tub in his home in Tampa and then him delivering a bladeliner to the Crow from the top ropes.}
{Another scene shows Jade and J.W. at their private gym, lifting weights on a bench machine, then not more than a few seconds later they are adorned in their finest formal attire and are with two blondes at a local but yet expensive restraunt. From there, Jade and J.W. are watching the servants washing their
BMW's while they are seen giving a high fives to each other and are laughing.}
{As the music continues, it shows Jade taking a chair to the Dog with Rabies and then him holding up one half of the tag team titles. Further on into the clip, J.W. Storm has BBB in a small package and then Jade delivering a drop kick to Rick Taylor. In the final scenes of the video, it is showing J.W.
slapping the fool out of a limp Rob Stryker and the very last clip shows Jade hoisting the North American title high over his head, as he stands on the corner buckle of the ring.}
*Like the stars that are high above in the sky, The Bladeliner's have really come into their own and have arrived at the top of the mountain* =================================== "The Bladeliners" =================================== {Throughout his time in the NAW, for Jade there has been many good times and bad. The good always shines brighter than bad and Jade has thoroughly enjoyed much success. In the span of just two months, the team known as The
Bladeliners have become one of the most dominant tag teams to ever grace the
NAW and they are riding on a wave of stardom.}
OPENING TRANSMISSION <Recently, the Bladeliners defeated The Towers of Power on an INWI card to retain their coveted NAW Tag Team Championships. Following the victory, The
Bladeliners boarded their lear jet and traveled to Hawaii and the scene opens up on a beach in Honolulu.>
(Laying on the beach and staring at the voluptious babes walking throughout the beautiful beach is Jade and J.W. Storm. J.W. is wearing a pair of purple swim trunks and is sitting crosslegged on a beach towel, his sun shades are black
Ray-bans and he has is long hair down to his shoulders. Jade on the other hand is seen getting out of the ocean water and begins his approach to where J.W. is at. He is wearing his long blonde hair tied up and is wearing a pair of royal blue swim trunks, grabbing a towel he sits down and begins to speak with J.W.)
Jade: <wiping the salty water from around his forehead> J.W. we have soundly defeated everyone that we have faced. We simply are the very best in this sport. We comprise the team known as The Bladeliners and with the NAW Tag Team straps around our waist, how can we go wrong?
<J.W. staring at a blonde, turns his attention to Jade and begins to talk.>
J.W. Storm: We simply are the best thing going today. There are many other teams that claim that they are all the best, but we know that there can only be one team that is the NAW Champions and that is truly, madly and deeply, us.
<Jade adds these comments> Jade: Yeah, I have been in some areas that promise great action and then never deliver that action. But, I honestly believe that the NAW and INWI are the very best in competition and always provide the best in matches, for they always and forever shall be the best organizations.
J.W. Storm: You are right, Jade. The best organization going today is the NAW and the INWI. Too many of these pansy ass wrestlers come into a fed and begin to rant and rave about what they will do and what they plan to do. What we have done, is to accomplish just what we have said that we would do and that is to win these coveted titles. We are the champions and now we have a challenge to The Mindset Empire. Sir White Garbage, if you can actually find another partner that can stand to smell your funk, then bring it on and we shall defend these straps against you and any others that want the shot.
Jade: Damn straight. Sir White Trash, you go around for months and claim you are better than the NAW and then all of a sudden, you show up. You are nothing more than a trash talking faggot and if you want us, then bring it on because like I told you when I first started my career in the CHO and I have been telling people for ages here in the NAW, we bow down for NO ONE. If you can't deal with that then you can just suck it.
Several ladies on the beach heard the suck it remarks and they begin to run straight for The Bladeliners, as they gather around Jade says to J.W.
Jade: They know real men, when they see us. <The Bladeliners smile and the scene ends> ===================================
