Jones, good. Now Lets get to the questions, and make it quick. FLOYD JONES: Well Mr Floyd, I will start out by telling you that I am making my rounds to all ...
"F" FF: Alright listen Jones, I'm not here to listen to you tell me anything. Its your job to ask the questions, its my job to anwser, and I absolutly hate it when people get off task, it shows me stupidy really. So whenever you can get yourself together you may begin. And don't make me stop this thing again, becasue really your lucky your here. Go ahead.
FLOYD JONES: Well then, let me start off by asking how you feel about your first round opponent, the man known as Ethan Lo...
"F" FF: {Again Interupting Floyd Jones} Now Listen up
Jones. My feelings on Love are very simple. He is a piece of trash. He is not even quailified to lace up my boots, and if you ask me, he shouldn't even be allowed to be in the same ring with me. And as far as giving me any challenge, that is a joke. I have beaten him once, and I will easily beat him again.

And for that I admire you, but I still think your a piece of trash, and there you have it Jones. Ethan Love is piece of trash. He always has been, he is today, and when I am done with him, he'll still be a piece of trash, who will happen to have a very broken neck courtesy of my "Finale".
FLOYD JONES: Well Mr Floyd, you seem to have the first round of competition already figured out, but what happens when you meet either "Maniac" Mike Massacre or "Dangerous" Kevin Zane in round two?
"F" FF: Okay Jones, another easy one for you. What can you tell me about Zane, or Massacre?
FLOYD JONES: Well they.. "F" FF: {Once again interupting} Exactly, the anwser to my question is a big fat nothing. Becasue both of these morons are nobody's. A Bunch of losers hwo got hired to fill the down time in which I'm not on the air. Thats fine with me. And when I am done humiliating Ethan
Love, I am going to do the same to whichone of these losers makes it to the second round. I can assure either of them one thing, and thats that the train stops in round number two. There isn't going to be any funny buisness with Freddy Floyd. It will be in and out, I'll be done in five minutes, no matter which one of them it is.
FLOYD JONES: So what your saying is that you have the title all but in the bag? "F" FF: EXACTLY!! Your finally picking up, it must be that you have been in the same room with me for a little while, and since I absolutly wreak with Intelligence, some must be just wafting right up your nose. But as far as your correct anwser goes, it was really a trick question, becasue there is no other anwser. There is not one man in this tournament who can beat me.
Now I don't care who any of these morons think they are Tommy Lionheart, Kenneth Morlock. Please, there all scared to death of Freddy Floyd, and I wonder why? Oh no wait, its becasue I have beaten vurtually everyone in this tournament at one time or another, and what in the world can stop me from doing it again.

But if you don't check your attitude, and your scrawny little ass out of my house in about five minutes, your going to have a lot more to worry about than wheather or not you got all of your questions in.
GEEEVES!!!!! { Geeevs runs into the room } "F" FF: Geeves show these men the door instantly. You all have about five seconds to pick up your little equipment before it gets thrown out. Now MOVE!!
