A long shock of red hair protrudes from the back of the mask, and he's apparently in the middle of enjoying a bowl of cereal.]
Masked Man: [in a voice that's remarkably similar to that of Eric Stoltz- not perfect, but damn close] Ah, Roberto, cool, you made it. Who's the bubblegummer?
[Wug glances back over his shoulder, a little unsure of something. Maybe he expected a different scene on this side of the door.]
Blackie: Wug- my partner. He's cool. Masked Man: Any friend of Rob's... So, gentlemen, step into my office? [The masked man walks in, followed by Blackie, Wug, and, obviously, the cameraman. The living room they enter is dimly lit, and poorly kept, a general sense of mess pervading everything. Sitting in the room, though, are two women- one isn't terribly attractive, and is simply sitting there, nodding vapidly as the other woman speaks. The second woman is also wearing a mask, hers a tight white rayon, and the mask has several pieces of piercing-type jewelry stuck in it in various places.]
Masked Woman: See, some people, they go and just buy a mask off the rack- that defeats the entire purpose of HAVING the mask, though, y'know?
Unattractive Woman: Uh-huh. Masked Woman: I mean, if you to a store and buy one, everyone in that store knows who you are, everyone who sees you enter and leave knows... what's the point?
Unattractive Woman: Oh, yah... yah... [The masked man waves Wug, Blackie, and presumably the cameraman upstairs, and they follow. They all end up in a fairly tidy-looking bedroom, and the masked man pulls out a black leatherette briefcase and opens it up. He pulls out three masks: one black, with a red pentagram on it, one white with starbursts over each eye, green and orange, respectively, and one's a sturdy mask of black leather, with a white bar across the eyes and a hole in the back that's obviously for long hair to hang out of. He lays the masks on the bed, and begins speaking.]
Masked Man: This one [indicating the pentagram mask] is from Mexico. That one [pointing at the white mask] is American made. The leather one, that's
Japanese.
[The masked man reaches forward and picks up the Japanese mask before continuing.] Masked Man: Now, those two [indicating Mexican and American] are $135.00 each. That's friend prices. This one here, [holds up the Japanese mask]
this one is $225.00. Now, don't get me wrong- the other two are really, really, really high quality masks- good workmanship, made to last, top quality materials. But this one, [again, holding up the Japanese leather mask] this one's something special. You wear it, and you'll definitely know where the extra money went.
[Wug looks at the masked man, then at Blackie, and then back at the masked man, as if something just went on here that he doesn't entirely understand.]
Blackie: [reaching for his wallet] Gimme the madman. If I like it, I'll come back and pick up another two in a few weeks.
Wug: The madman? Masked Man: Done. [Blackie opens his wallet, and removes a thick sheaf of bills. He tries to count out $225.00... and tries again... and again... and finally Wug reaches over, grabs the money, counts out the right amount, and hands it to our masked man. Blackie smiles vacantly, and is handed the mask.]
Blackie: Mind if I try it on here? Masked Man: Hey... mi casa es su casa... [Fade to black, and then back in on...] Deep Voice: I had a really great time tonight, Rob. Blackie: Well... um... I'm glad to hear that. [Blackie and a very large, VERY muscular woman, Tommy James' bodyguard Koreya, are standing on the portico to a nice looking bungalow. Blackie's dressed in a black silk suit, obviously tailor-made for him, the inside lapels a sparkling, metallic silver, a pair of expensive-looking black boots with silver trim, and a black bandana on his head, Gerardo-style, holding back his braids. The bandana covers most, but not all, of the fresh white bandage on his forehead, and there's little doubt that, underneath the black
Gargoyle shades he's wearing, his eyes are glassy as all hell. The woman is dressed in a red silk cocktail dress, slit along one leg, that almost sorta kinda succeeds in making her look sexy- not quite, but it's obvious someone at least made the effort. Her ensemble is completed with a red haircomb, a pair of red stilleto heels, and a red purse. She has James' black leather duster with the cobra hood pattern on the back thrown over her shoulders, but just barely- there's no way she could wear it, she's just too big. She opens the door, and steps in.]
Koreya: Care to come in for a drink? Blackie: Um... uh... oh, hell, sure, why not? [They step inside... and the camera follows.] Koreya: So, do you like the place? Blackie: Yeah, it's... nice, very minimalist, very theme-oriented... [The bungalow is basically a studio-type apartment, except the bathroom and kitchen are actually separate rooms. The spacious main room is a combination bedroom/living room/den. It's sparsely furnished, the main points of interest being a large black leather couch, a queen-sized waterbed, a big-screen TV, and several dozen weightlifting stations- weight benches, weight machines, weight bars... all displaying a somewhat Cenobyte-esque fixation on personal fitness. Koreya walks daintily- well, as daintily as she can- over to the bed and sits down on the edge, smoothing out the end of the burgundy comforter on it.]
Koreya: Have a seat, Rob. Blackie: [nervously] Um... I think I'll go fix us some drinks... where's the kitchen? Koreya: [Indicating one of the two doors that don't lead outside] Right through there. Protein powder's in the cabinet, raw eggs are in the little fridge.
Blackie: Uh... yummy... Back in a flash... [James walks, with some haste, through the doorway and into the kitchen, where he can be heard rummaging around.]
Blackie: [yelling from the kitchen] Is there a particular blender I'm supposed to use? And do you want the brown or the white eggs?
Koreya: The taupe blender. And the white eggs are hard-boiled, so use the brown. [The sound of a blender, along with a string of invectives from James, some censored, comes from the kitchen shortly thereafter. While the blender's going, Koreya shrugs off the duster, and begins looking through the pockets.
She pulls out a pair of sunglasses, looks at them for a moment, most likely wondering why someone would wear a pair AND keep a pair in their jacket, and then puts them on and looks around the room... The sound of the blender gets louder for a second, and the sound of wet contents whipping out of an open blender and onto the walls resonates through the room.]
Blackie: OH HOLY CHRIST!!! WHAT IS THIS, AN OUTBOARD MOTOR?!? Koreya: No, it's the new WeiderPro 2000 Blender, with high speed functions,
Rob. [Koreya pulls the sunglasses off, and sets them on the table. She rummages around in the other pockets, and pulls out a silver flask, which she also sets on the table, along with a pack of gum. She pulls out a couple of condoms, still in their packages, and "Hmms" at them a second, looks towards the kitchen, and smiles and returns them to the pocket in the jacket. She reaches into the inside pocket, and pulls out the black leather wrestling mask, seen earlier. She lays the duster on the bed next to her, and proceeds to examine the mask, opening it, poking her fingers through the eyes holes, and so forth. She starts trying to pull it back off, but the buckles seemed to be jammed. Just at the moment, the front door opens and admits one Glenn "Bullywug" Hudson, while at the same time,
James steps back out of the kitchen, his upper right half covered with some pale, viscous liquid mess in a spatter pattern. The two men lock eyes, and-]

Palmolive to the masked man, who hands it to Blackie.]

Blackie: [sounding so very, very confused] Um... what am I supposed to do with the dishwashing liquid?
Wug: [sighing] Squirt it under the mask, to make it easier to pull off. Blackie: Oh, ok... um... when? [Wug and the masked man look at each other, each hoping to get to utilize the
"He's your friend" gambit, but the absolute pointlessness of that sinks in.

[Take a guess...] Wug: Thr- Masked Man: [interrupting] Um... hey, did you try unfastening the buckles? Blackie, Wug, and Koreya: [in unison] What?
