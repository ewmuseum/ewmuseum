Roleplay -- Nick Demola and the Church of Demola

30 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NOTE about the characters: Nick Demola is a wrestler that thinks he is the Perfect Creation of God. Big Dick McGee is the wealthy entrepreneur that funds the Church of Demola. Frank Anthony and
David Rheaume are Nick's right hand men.....oh yeah.......and they're cannibals too. :-)
(The camera fades into Big Dick McGee's hospital room in Portsmouth,
New Hampshire. Big Dick suffered a concussion and swears he feels fine, but the doctors wanted him to stay overnight for evaluation.
In the meantime Nick Demola has been preaching in the hospital's chapel while Frank and David visit the morgue.)
Doctor Kenorkian: Richard..... Big Dick: Call me Big Dick please. Doctor: No. Richard. Your condition is not serious at this time. But this is the 25th concussion you have suffered. You had to retire from the ring for fear of post-concussion syndrome. You have to be more careful.
Big Dick: Listen, Doctor Kenorkian.....God, that sounds familiar....I will do what I want, when I want. I am a wealthy man!
If I become a retarded half wit I can still live on life support. So you keep your little opinions to yourself and let me out of bed!
Doctor: I can't do that. Big Dick: Listen guy, I could have your fucking ass fired on the spot. Let me out of this bed or you'll pay dearly.
Doctor: Know what? I'm going to recommend you to a specialist in
Michigan that I know. My cousin. Real nice guy. Likes to help people by ending their suffering.
Big Dick: The only thing I'm suffering from is your dumbass talking to me. Now go away! Doctor: I can't do that. Big Dick: Oh you can't? (Big Dick gets out of the bed and gets in the doctor's face.) Big Dick: Then I'll make you. (The doctor begins sweating.) Doctor: Um.......Er.....Um.......Go ahead Mr. McGee! Big Dick: Thank you. I knew you'd see the light. (Big Dick walks out of his room, still wearing his paper hospital gown. Now, being a man of great height, they couldn't find any robes long enough for him. So this robe is a bit short. And hanging down at the bottom of the robe is......well......the reason that Richard
Douglass McGee was nicknamed "Big Dick". The female nurses just stare and whisper things like "It must be a foot long limp!" and "I'd fuck him, but I'm afraid of heights!" Dick, of course, is oblivious to all of this. He's too focused on finding his three compatriots who are somewhere in the hospital. Finally, Dick gets an idea.
Where would Nick be if he were anywhere in the hospital? Of course! The chapel! Big Dick goes down the elevator, still in his hospital robe, and enters the chapel.)
Big Dick: NICK! Demola: DICK! I see that you have finally taken to agreeing with my idea that everyone should be nude!
Big Dick: What? Oh........ (Big Dick looks down.) Big Dick: Oh well, the ladies got a little fantasy tonight courtesy of Big Dick McGee. No biggie.
Demola: Whatever. Hey Dick! I got some new followers! (Three 90 year old, deaf men are sitting in the front pew staring into space.) Demola: Jeremiah! Mortimer! Abraham! This is Big Dick McGee, the cheif financier of the Church!
(The three men continue to stare into space.) Demola: They are obviously too entrenched in thought and prayer after the epiphanies I have provided for them.
Big Dick: Either that or they're three old, deaf farts sitting in a pew watching a young kid ranting and raving and thinking about how they're so glad they put their Depends on tonight.
Demola: Don't talk of my followers in such foul terms! (Nick directs his comments to his "people".) Demola: MY PEOPLE! I shall return soon to take you to the promised land! (The three old farts just sit there as Nick approaches Dick.) Demola: I thought you were supposed to be in the hospital overnight. Big Dick: Well, someone else overrode that vote. Demola: Who? Big Dick: Me. Demola: Well, if you feel it's best. It's your body. Big Dick: Yeah, it's my body. But what I'm worried about right now is where Frank and David are. They shouldn't be left alone.
Demola: I kicked Frank out of the chapel for drinking the altar wine, and David was busy in the pharmacy last time I heard.
Big Dick: How long ago was that? Demola: Oh, an hour ago or so. Big Dick: So they've probably moved on since then. Demola: Yeah. Where could they be? (Both men contemplate for a moment. Then they look at each other in stunned horror.) Demola and Big Dick: THE MORGUE!!!!!!!!! ==================================================================== (In the morgue Frank Anthony is sitting on a dead guy while David pulls the guy's right leg off.)
Anthony: Ah Dave. This is the life! Free food all the time! And we don't have to kill nobody for it!
Rheaume: Yeah. There's always a downside. Anthony: True. Rheaume: Want a piece of this? The guy was a crack addict so he has some real cool stuff in his blood!
Anthony: Naw. I'll pass. I got my Phil earlier. (NOTE: That was a tasteless comment related to a recent death you all should know about. To anyone who was offended by it, fuck off. :-))
Rheaume: Alright. Suit yourself. (Just then Nick Demola and Big Dick McGee burst through the door. Big Dick still has his hospital gown on.) Demola: STOP IT! Big Dick: Drop that drumstick! Anthony: Speaking of sticks......Dicky ole buddy...... Big Dick: I KNOW! I KNOW! Shut up! Demola: This is NOT the way to prepare for a tag team title match! Rheaume: Aw Nick, you should try this. Here! (David rips out one of the guy's eyes and tries to hand it to Nick. Nick swats it down to the ground.) Demola: Have some RESPECT for the dead! Anthony: But they taste much better when you just rip them limb from limb! Rheaume: Yeah, would you rather we get forks and knives? Demola: ARRRRRRRRRRRGH!!!!!!!!!!!!! Big Dick: Gentlemen, fun time is over. It's time to train for your tag team title match. Nick also has to train for his match against
SubZero.
Demola: Train for SubZero? Don't make me laugh. Anthony: Yeah, Nytro and Glyce can't handle what we got! We're gonna take home the tag titles and use them as plates for when we eat out!
Rheaume: The gold is ours Dick! Just chill out and take a bite! (Big Dick lifts Frank and David up and throws them out of the morgue by their ears.) Big Dick: The limo is waiting. Let's go. (The four men walk out of the hospital and to the limo. Big Dick looks in the limo and sees a young lady.)
Big Dick: Kristin! I didn't know you'd be here! (Dick grabs Nick's coat and covers up. He sits next to Kristin. Nick then sits across from Kristin with Frank and David sorta hanging around on the floor of the limo. Nick could swear that this woman sitting in front of him was the most gorgeous thing he's ever seen. Everything about her radiated. He was awestruck immediately.) Big Dick: Kristin, this is Frank Anthony and David Rheaume..... (Frank and David wave hi with their bloody hands. Kristin just laughs.) Big Dick: And this is The Man, The Myth, The Legend.....The GOD of
Wrestling.....Nick "The Bulldogg" Demola.
(Nick is immediately infatuated with Kristin. Anyone can tell. He extends his hand.) Demola: I'm absolutely charmed to be in your presence...... (Nick grabs Kristin's hand and kisses it.) Big Dick: Kristin is my niece Nick. She's the daughter of my older sister Jacklyn. (Big Dick then whispers, "Lay off. Cut the shit.". Nick replies, "I do what I want, when I want to do it.")
Demola: So Kristin, what are you in town for? (As she opens her mouth Nick becomes absolutely entranced. He has never seen anything have the beauty or purity she does. He hears her words, but is too focused on her beauty to listen to them.)
Demola: Fascinating. Absolutely fascinating. Big Dick: Alright Kristin. This is your stop. Your mom is waiting to welcome you back from college.
(Kristin gives Dick a big hug and says goodbye......then passes Nick a piece of paper with her number on it. She leaves the limo and Nick is smiling like an idiot. Meanwhile Frank and David are arguing over who gets to eat the tongue that David kept in his pocket.)
Big Dick: NICK! NICK! (Nick awakens from his trance.) Demola: Oh........um.........what? Big Dick: Help me here! They're fighting over a tongue! Demola: Oh. Okay. (Big Dick grabs the tongue and flings it out the window. Frank and
David just sit and mope.)
Big Dick: Nick, don't even think about it. Demola: Think about what? Big Dick: She's way out of your league. Demola: I know. The way her lips move when she speaks. The way her eyes shine in the moonlight. The way her hair bounces off of her beautiful face. Way too good for me.
Big Dick: HAHAHA!!!!!! You got it BAD KID! Demola: I sure do. (While Frank and David continue to sulk and Big Dick continues to laugh, Nick looks out the window and to the stars.....dreaming of the woman that he has all of a sudden fallen in (dare I say) fallen in love with.)
