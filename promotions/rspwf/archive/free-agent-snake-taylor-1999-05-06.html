[Free Agent] Snake Taylor

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
My newest character, Snake Taylor, is 6'4", 260 lbs and a face from Brooklyn, NY. His finisher is the Snake Driver which is a Reverse Piledriver, aka: Tombstone Piledriver. I am currently looking for a booked e-federation with a website, open flashes on a message board such as
InsideTheWeb. I am looking for either an established fed or a new fed just about to begin the title tournament or whatever. I have enclosed a sample roleplay that isn't that long but I used for an application so send all offers to
[detail withheld] SAMPLE ROLEPLAY (The scene starts up in a big abandoned warehouse. The floor is made of creaky wooden planks and the walls are also made of wood. The place is deserted apart from a few old crates. Suddenly, a loud hissing sound echoes around the structure. Two large black snakes slither towards the cameraman who is looking very edgy. The two snakes curl around his ankles and while he is kicking and screaming like a baby. While he is doing this, a man steps from the shadows of the warehouse. He is wearing a white tanktop, jeans and
Oakley Wires sunglasses. He crouches to the ground and makes a hissing sound. The snakes suddenly jerk their heads up and make their way over to the man and he picks them up and drapes them over his shoulders.)
Snake Taylor: I see you made it, now I have to show the prez in this fed some of my microphone skills so why don't you do your job and ask me the questions.
Cameraman: Sure thing Snake, tell me, what are your vital stats, height, weight, hometown and so on?
Snake Taylor: I am a 6'4", 260 lbs high flyer who grew up in the alleyways of Brooklyn, New York. My parents deserted me when I was 10 and I have been living in this warehouse for quite a bit of time.
Cameraman: Can you tell the people at home what you look like and what you wear to the ring?
Snake Taylor: Alright, I have no hair and a brown goatee, so if you mess with me, you are in big strife, anyway, I love tattoos. I have barbed wire tattooed around my left bicep and "INSIDE" tattooed onto my right bicep. I wear my lucky ripped white tanktop and army cargo pants to the ring. I top that off with white lace up wrestling boots, fingerless gloves, and I am never seen without my Oakley Wires unless I am wrestling.
(The scene closes when Snake Taylor hises again and releases the snakes off his shoulder. The snakes start chasing after the cameraman who is almost wetting himself while he is running from the snakes carrying his camera.
Snake Taylor laughs hysterically before the scene fades to black and Snake's logo comes up onto the screen, it is a snake curled around a cross)
