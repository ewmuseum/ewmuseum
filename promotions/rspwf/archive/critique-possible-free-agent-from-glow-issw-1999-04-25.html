[CRITIQUE] Possible Free Agent from GLOW, ISSW...

25 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[FADE IN TO A SEMI-DARK SCENE IN A DARKENED ALLEY. IT IS LATE ONE night, and there are few sounds to be heard. The camera is focusing on the ground, and all of a sudden, we see a pair of black boots stride up to the camera. The camera now pans upwards. Startled by what he saw, the camera man takes a step backwards.
We now see what he has just seen. The figure of ISSW superstar John Davies. John Davies is of monster build. He has got long, greasy black hair, although his original colour was blond. Davies has got a gruesome face, with a large scar across his forehead, the cause of which is unknown. Davies has got clear, green eyes, which now stare menacingly into the camera. With his arms crossed by his chest, he now begins to speak.]
JD: "So this is it, huh? *This* is International Superstars of Wrestling, the federation supposedly attacting the biggest names in the wrestling world. Well, I've seen the federation's roster, and I now know I've been tricked. There isn't one name on the entire ISSW roster with the same prestige as mine."
"Guys like Al. E. Gator, Chris Johnson and Richard Gracie don't belong in the same ring as myself. In time, I shall tear them all apart, and prove
that to the wrestling world." "I came to this federation with two aims. The first was to succeed in the federation, take home a good pay-check and a few titles, but my other aim was to hurt, to maim and to kill as many superstars as were possible."
"I've had a long time dream to do both, but first of all, I was told when I came to the federation that I would be competing, given the chance to beat and injure the *biggest* names in wrestling. It's now apparant that
I have been lied to, and for that reason, the president of the ISSW can consider himself a marked man."
"You better watch your back at all times, because you have angered me, and I can't wait to squash you like a bug. I don't care what title you've got, I don't care if you pay my wages, pain is what's important to me, and I'm damn sure bringing that to you."
[Pause] JD: "My debut's been assigned, and it is on the twenty-seventh against this month. I watched the ISSW report, and I saw that Ray Masters was been congractulated for earning a contract with the ISSW. Well, after I've finished with him on the twenty-seventh, there will be even more coverage about this pathetic loser, but it will be about his very own funeral."
"I didn't come to this federation to mess around, to play nicely with losers like that chump, and for that reason, I am going to destroy, to maim him, and quickly when we meet on the twenty-seventh."
"Following on from that night though, my next line of buisness is on the fourth of May, when I take on Jamie Kidd. I've heard of this guy before, it evades my mind where from, but I do know that he is not the standard that I came to this federation to compete against, and so he will be demolished."
"Indeed, Kidd, your punishment, your fate shall be greater than that of Ray Masters, because he is not trying to imitate a legend, such as myself. You claim to be good, and I shall ensure that you are destroyed when we meet."
"Yet I will not let up on Masters, because that isn't my way. By the fifth of May, both Masters and Kidd will have been. . ."
"Hurt." "Maimed." "Killed." [Fade]
