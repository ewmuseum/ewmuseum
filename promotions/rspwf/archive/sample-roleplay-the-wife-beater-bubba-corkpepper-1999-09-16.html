[Sample Roleplay] "The Wife-Beater" Bubba Corkpepper

16 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a sample roleplay, I just did, I needed something to send to leagues. I don't care what you do to it. Critique it, tell me I suck, I don't care. This is just an introductory roleplay, I did with Bubba. Enjoy.
======= [SCENE: It opens down inside a broken down apartment, as it's noticeably nighttime, as the apartment, is completely blacked out and the windows leading out shine with darkness. As suddenly the sound of someone opening the door is heard. The sound of keys, dangling off the key-chain.
The light pops on, and "The Wife-Beater" Bubba Corkpepper is seen walking in his apartment, he drops his gym bag on the ground by the door, as a large dog comes running to Bubba's side. Bubba walks into the kitchen, feeds his dog, and gets some food for himself. Bubba, eats and slowly rubs his giant gut, almost hanging out of his wife-beater. He's wearing a pair of Jean shorts, that are mostly ripped with a couple of holes that are really noticeable. Bubba sits down on the beat-down couch, and begins to speak]
"You know, ever since I've made my graceful return to the wrestling world, nothing has been the same. All the people, that I remember from the beginning, when I first did this crap for fun. They are all gone. Not one of the people, the pioneers of this sport are still around. "Lonewolf" Brody
Thunder. The man that basically put wrestling on the map, has disappeared into a small hole, next to a giant rock. And you've got people, that only the legends would know. Dean Simon, the man who took wrestling to the next level, what happened to him? Everyday, you people ponder the thought of what
/wrestling/ used to be about. God forbid, that anybody wrestles on Monday nights anymore. What happened, to the /good/ days? They've all disappeared.
When every Saturday night, we would go to an arena, and we would fight. It wouldn't matter, if we had fifty people or two-hundred and fifty people. It's making me sick, that people these days have forgot all about the wrestling business. It's all about how people can cuss, and how they can go around telling people, they are going to kick eachothers.. well, you get the picture.
I've taken all my joking-around, and I've thrown it down the toilet. All you see on Bubba Corkpepper, is a gosh-darn gimmick.
[Bubba, stands up, as the dog stands up as well. Bubba stretches, and walks outside on his back patio. The patio is huge, almost as big as the apartment itself, as it's overlooking the endless woods. Tree's sway in the wind, as the swoosh of the leaves rustling around, startles some of the squirrels and other animals. Bubba leans over the rail, and begins to speak]
"For years, I've been wrestling as someone that I am not. I've been wrestling as the "Wife-Beater" and all It's been is some darn gimmick. I've transformed.
I've become a completely new person, with the same moniker, that I will never get over. People say, that they can easily put Bubba on the mat and pin him one-two-three. Now, I've never seen anyone even get lucky enough to get Bubba on the mat. You see, Bubba doesn't' mess around anymore. People have come and go, but Bubba? Bubba is here to stay. The name "Wife-Beater" is going to wreck mayhem in everywhere he goes from now on. When people hear the name "Wife-Beater" there eyes are going to flinch, there ears are going to curl and the hair on the back of their necks is going to stand up straighter than
a gay man. From now on, Bubba is here, so get used to it! [Bubba turns off the camera, that he has been taping this with, and walks away from his apartment. The "Wife-Beater" logo pops up as the scene fades out to a fuzzy/snowy screen]
