Good RPer looking for fed.

15 November 1998

Here is a sample Rp, if you want me to join E-mail me at
[detail withheld] your fed must be around for longer than one month. This Rp is from my old fed where I was a Horsemen. The crowd is still in the arena as they see Doc Hendrix walk out to the ring. He has a mic in his hand and tells the fans not to go anywhere. He then introduces the Television Champion.)
Doc: Ladies and gentlemen I know you have liked what you have seen so far, but now lets bring him out, the WRF Television Champion, Stevie "The Crippler"
Franklin!! (The Horsemen music hits the PA, the crowd jumps to their feet and being to cheer, then out walks Stevie and Arn Anderson. Arn has on black jeans and a white Horsemen t-shirt, while Stevie has on blue jeans and a black Horsemen t-shirt, and the T.V. title around his waist. They make their way to the ring and hold up four fingers to the crowd. The crowd gives them a louder ovation.
They step in the ring and Arn takes the mic from Doc.) Arn: Doc if you wouldn't mind getting the h*ll out of the ring, we would apprecitate it. Now did you like what you saw? (Crowd cheers even louder.)
Well they way you have treated us, it looks like this should be called Horsemen
Mayhem!!! You know Wolfpac we told you not to take us on, we told you that you didn't want to go to war with us, and you didn't listen. Austin you got your a$$ kicked by the real Toughest SOB The Sandman. You boys might want to go on back home and tell everyone that you couldn't cut it against the Horsemen. Now
Stevie you wanted to talk to Z-Pac or someone..(He tosses the mic to Stevie and the crowd erupts giving him a well deserved ovation.)
Stevie: Arn, his name is X-pac I think!?!?! But you know what X-pac, I don't care what the h*ll you wanna call yourself, but you know you can't beat me and you will never be able to lace my boots. You want a shot a the Television title well you got it, but you better make the most of it, because you know that if you don't win, you might not walk out alive. I want you to watch every match I have been in and study the moves I have done really well, because you never know what I might hit you with. I am the most unpredictable wrestler in the WRF, and you my friend are nothing more than a mid-card jobber that can't hang with the big boys. You told me once that I was a good wrestler, well to be good you got to be able to hang with the Horsemen and you can't hang. My friend you wanted a match with the man who beat Kevin Nash, with a man who beat
Val Venis, with the man who destroyed Joober T, well son you must have no d*mn sense, because you will never, be able to beat me. So come next Wenesday I want you to come prepared for the a$$ whipping of your life, and you gonna get
Horsemen style!!! (He holds up four fingers to the crowd and they erupt. He tosses the mic back to Arn.)
Arn: The fact of the matter is this, we aren't nice guys and we do things the hard way. And Sting I still don't know where your coming from but where ever it is, I will find out.
(The Horsemen music hits the PA and the crowd jumps to their feet and begin to cheer Stevie and Arn as they leave the ring.)
