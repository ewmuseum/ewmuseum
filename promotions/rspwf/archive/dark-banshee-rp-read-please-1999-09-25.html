DARK BANSHEE! RP, read, please.

25 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Another flash for a puroresu fed that folded. I had this character, the Dark Banshee, who was parodying the real Banshee, a G-pro legend. He was looking for attention, and got it almost immediately from the Jet Jaguar, a friend of the Banshee.
Admittedly, this baby is hard to understand unless you go back through the G-pro archives (all posted to RSPWF, just go look) to see how it all came about. It was supposed to be on the final G-pro card, but was overlooked somehow.
Feh, just read. -- (The scene suddenly cuts from ringside to a hallway in the back of the Kourankuen Hall. Center-screen stands the pretender to the title of the Banshee, the man who calls himself the "Dark Banshee". He wears his ring attire, a red and black version of the Banshee's green and white singlet, pads, boots and mask, presumably in preparation for his match later on in the night. As he looks into the camera, the world is reminded once again that this is not the original Banshee by the Asiatic eyefolds that peek out from the eyeholes of the mask. He seems quite animated, looking back and forth impatiently...)
DB: C'mon, c'mon, aren't we connected yet!? (A pause of silence, and a few clicks. Dark Banshee begins to pace.) CM (Cameraman): Yeah, yeah we got it... DB: Yeah! WHOooo...finally... Follow me, my manservant! CM: (Under his breath) Manservant? (With a spin and a hop, Dark Banshee begins to walk down the hallway at a brisk pace. The cameraman lingers for a moment before following, giving the masked moron some room...)
JY: What's going on here? BJ: Dark Banshee in the back...what is he doing? Where is he going? JY: I really don't...oh no... (What Jinsei is "oh no"ing about is the fact that Dark Banshee has just shoved his way into a public restroom in the back of the venue. The camera, of course, follows him through, bumping into and shuffling past the door along the way.)
JY: This has all the earmarks of a bad television experience just waiting to happen, Benjiru. I wish they'd cut off that camera feed before this nitwit dishonors the Gunryo Pro...
BJ: With Frazer Fury in charge, I don't think we could sink any lower, Jinsei. Still, it _could_ be entertaining. (In the restroom now Dark Banshee is pulling a member of the ring crew, a short, balding japanese man out of a restroom stall.)
BJ: You, you, away from Dark Banshee's domain! Your presence dishonors this place! (Without a word, still pulling his pants up, the little man scampers from the restroom, bumping the camera as he goes.)
CM: *grunt* JY: I can't even fathom what this man might be doing... (Dark Banshee takes a look outside of the restroom, making sure that the man has gone. Then, from the crotch of his tights, Dark Banshee produces a very familiar mask.)
BJ: HEY! That's the mask of Jet Jaguar II! What is he... (With a grunt, the annoying masked man reaches into his trunks again, withdrawing a flip-top, refillable lighter. He holds both items up for the camera to see, lighter and mask. When he speaks, it is with a poorly done
California accent that he seems to have forgotten to use today up till this point.) DB: Hey, people at home, you know what this is, right? (A soft booing is heard as the crowd begins to react in advance to whatever blashpemy this man plans.)
JY: This can't be! How did he get that!? DB: Ah, I knew that you did! You all know that this dude has dishonored my mask and name by stating that I am not the true Banshee... His offense has not gone unnoticed, no, no, no Jetty. I am the Dark Banshee, I see all and
I know all! BJ: Dude? JY: Hasn't anyone told this guy that Micheal Keening did _not_ have a Californian accent? DB: I have noticed this man's gnarly accusations, and now I react in kind... BJ: *snicker* JY: Gnarly!? DB: Now...I have taken his mask, his pride, and now...I destroy it... (With a flick of the wrist, This odd little man flicks open the lighter, and lighting it in one motion. From there, the mask is applied to the flame...it lights quickly, and burns rapidly. The crowd gives off a violent heel pop, attempting to hammer the offender with their voices, but he just chuckles, and drops the mask to the tile floor, letting it burn...)
JY: Oh...no... BJ: That mask represents so much to Jet! It was passed to him by Shoji Suzuki. I'm sure that he has more than one, but they symbolism, such disrespect! (Quaking with laughter, Dark Banshee looks down at the burning mask.) DB: Jet Jaguar is the past, Suzuki is gone, I do not accept this poser as Jay-Jay. You are fake, you are nothing! JY: Poser!? Fake!? Such hypocrisy! DB: Hah, while I'm at it... (Again this man reaches into his trunks, withdrawing another familiar mask, this one green and white. On seeing it, the crowd gives an enormous heel pop.)
DB: This too is a part of the past, my past. JY: The mask of the Banshee! DB: This too must burn, to make way for the future. (The mask falls into the flame and begins to burn. Again the volume of the crowd increases.)
BJ: This is just too much! Someone needs to put this pretender in his place! DB (Laughing ever harder): This...heh, this, Fake Jet, should be taken as a message. I do this to make a point. Look at it, think about it, then watch me tonight as I tear Mashin Itazuki into pieces, turn him into Mashin cat chow. Maybe when I'm done, I'll shove the chunks of Mashin chow down your throat. How's that for dishonor, fake!?
(With a hop, Dark Banshee hurls himself feet first upon the ashen pile of mask, quickly snuffing out the flame with a few stomps. From there, he picks up the stiff, brittle chunks of charoal in his hands and enters the bathroom stall. The camera follows to the door of the stall, and watches as
Banshee tosses the mask into the pot, and proceeds to flush it down. More boos, ever louder from the arena.)
DB: Get it yet, Jet? When I get my hands on you, all your hopes of fame and fortune will go right down the drain... Now, if you're so bad, just come and get me... You know where I'll be...
(The scene cuts away back to ringside with Jinsei and Benjiru.) -- Every last damn thing I've typed in this post is Š and Ž [name withheld], by god! Whoo-hah!!!
