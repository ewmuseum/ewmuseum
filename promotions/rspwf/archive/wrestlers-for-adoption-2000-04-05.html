wrestlers for adoption

5 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am not looking for a fed to put these wrestlers in, i'm looking for somebody to take control of them and do their roleplays, make their angles etc. I have been trying to do it all myself while i wait for somebody to take over but it is too hard as i have a wrestler of my own in another fed and i have the ReFC to run.
 I have been asked to by the following wrestler's handler to advertise them for adoption. They are all in the ReFC (formerly the
UWA). He has had to give up e-wrestling due to his job taking him abroad. They are all good wrestlers and one is the reigning ReFC world champion
  The Power - He is the leader of The Real Ministry. He used to be the EPWA world champion until he was defeated by Marvellous Merrick. He also held the EPWA youth championship belt. He is currently feuding with an ex-member of the ministry called The Prince of
Darkness. The power blames the prince for losing his chance of winning the UWA world championship belt and that cause the entire real ministry to turn on the prince. They are set to fight in Flaming Casket match in the ReFC (formerly the UWA).
Finisher - Full Power Ahead - Same as the Death Valley Driver.   Zulu - He is a member of The Real Ministry and one half of the tag team called the Dark Side. He is a hardcore wrestler who once held the EPWA hardcore championship belt but was relieved of it after losing to Marvellous Merrick who is now part of another fed called
EW2K. Finisher - Zulu Splash - Big splash of the top rope.   Shadow - Another Ministry member and the other half of the Dark Side. He was the first King of the Cage in the UWA but the King of the Cage title was scrapped by the ReFC.
Finisher - Moonsault - Moonsault of the top rope.   The Prince of Darkness - He is an ex-ministry member and was once Shadows partner in the Dark Side but was replaced by Zulu after he left the ministry. He used to have a manager called The Princess of Darkness but he attacked her using his finisher after she turned her back on him and stayed with the ministry.
Finisher - The Dark Driver - Similar to the Rikishi Driver.   Marvellous Musselle - He is a cocky wrestler. He used to be part of the EPWA. He once held the EPWA youth championship belt. He also held the EPWA tag team title belts with his partner
Marvellous Merrick but they split up when the EPWA closed down. He is currently the ReFC world champion. He is currently being accused of being aligned with the ReFC president by Tsunami who has challenged him for a title shot.
Finisher - Showing some Musselle - Same as the Crippler Crossface done by Chris Benoit of the WWF.
  If you think you may know anybody looking for a tag partner or an extra member of their stable then please tell them about me and to contact me and then i will give them the full infomation on their chosen wrestler(s).
