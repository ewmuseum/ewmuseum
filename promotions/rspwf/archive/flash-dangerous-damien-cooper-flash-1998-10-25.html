[Flash] DANGEROUS DAMIEN COOPER [Flash]

25 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here's a sample of my work...its not my best with this character but its damn good! Dont for get Vote 4 Damien Cooper for RSPWF Top 200!
[The scene quickly fades into the dark demented streets of Harlem, New York. The camera captures the fog that lumes around in the air and steam arising from the street sewers, as you see a man walk along the pavement in a hooded Tommy
Hilfiger sweat shirt and his hands confirmed in sweat shirt's pocket. You can not make out a good picture of this figure that walks the street as the camera fades in even closer capturing the movement of his eyes, you can now make out a clear face as he steps into the light. Next to him is a huge building. He raises his head and then reads the sign above him. The camera slowly zooms in as you see a sign dangling above as a shingle hanging from a ceiling. He walks into the creepy burned warehouse and looks around. He opens his arms and begins to speak.]
["DANGEROUS" DAMIEN COOPER]:Ahh, the fresh breeze of burnt wood in morning. How good does it feel. As I walk along these streets and around society it only gets one thing pumping through my head, and thats, what we all should know by now...a little something called HATRED!! Yes, the one and only thing that I feel deeply inside of me. And nothing more would satisfy me, then taking it out on any and all that crosses my path and whoever treads
on my territory. And luckily for me, someone has...actually if you think about it's more like a battle field to dispose of on my hands. First Beginning with one, "Sadistic"
Shane Alexander. A great hand to hand combat fighter and yet one of the greatest International Wrestlers to be found roming around the globe. And me, myself, is as great as they come! But let me tell you one thing Shane...as you approach me you better damn well be able to take as much pain as you can dish out, because I know for damn sure that i can. I'm going to dish out all of what
I've recieved from these streets of what you live upon. These streets are the cause of all this and now your fate is now lying in my sweaty palms. You walk around like you're big and bad, and you may be, but life is a gamble but when it comes with dealing with me, the devil, thats of big of gamble you'll need to take because we're talking about your life and future and damn well possibly your career. it could all well come to an end. You know, I still have the bashing thoughts and memories of back when I was a kid. All the pain I went through and everything else I've suffered. All that does is just pumps me with more and more of the same, hatred. It will and shall be brought among you
Alexander along with all the other participates in this Battle Royal. Not one day goes past when I think of stepping into the ring, you know as well as i do that when you step through them ropes _EVERYTHING_ is on the line. And there's really no turning back my friend.
[Cooper slowly twists around eyeballing tyhe interior with a smirk openning and cracking on his face.]
["DANGEROUS" DAMIEN COOPER]: You see this building here..this is a prime example of what happens with society. This once was a magnificent foundation and look at what its become. Nothing but another abandoned burned down building lefted by the society we have today. Look at its foundation, the bricks and wood all burnt to shimbles. But all things get a chance, a chance to redeem and take upon revenge among the distributors or what we know as society. Shane, look around you my friend and tell me what you see. Walk along the streets and see what I've been seeing for years. Take a look through my eyes and you and everyone else will experience something you'll wished you havent. Saturday night, an experience for all as you "Sadistic" fall to the feet of me. I know you may think I'm what would you say, pretty fond of myself, and i guess I am.
But nevertheless shall I overabuse my confidence. You see, Slam Dance is just around the corner and nothing shall defy my vengence against you but many others to come. One night will determine it all, as I said before, I hold your fate in my hands...at any second I could smash down on it with ease! You will be a prime example of what vengence is all about. I'm not taking anything from your talent, just implying that your talent is not as remarkable as the talent
I produce here in N.E.W.
["DANGEROUS" DAMIEN COOPER]: As far a championships are concerned and me capturing or holding one doesn't concern me at the least. Yes it'll be an great accomplishment on my behalf and therefore bringing me more and better talents of the N.E.W. And Montgomery and the rest, dont think I forgot about you. It'll be a pleasure nontheless to step in the ring with you all. And'll bring more pleasure when you fall in defeat. Like i said, it's not a matter of titles, but respect and how much you can achieve.This here will be nothing more then an all out brawl, a fight to the finish! Alex, I am the least of your worries my friend. You also have your heart set on getting title shots and thats what distract you from being at your best. But you see a guy like me, a rookie maybe, a newcomer and you think to yourself, "what could he possibly do".
Believe me i know what you're thinking. Why? Because I thought the same thing at one point. Thinking of it going to be an easy task...well let me tell you, this isnt going to be some walk in the park, more like a Rumble in the
Bronx...or even a run through a jungle. I know how good you are, but its up to you my friend to bind by it which is hard to do with more then one thing operating on your mind. I think this is a great tisk for me and now you'll evolved around _MY_ world!!! [smirks] The time has come, and once the moment hits you, you'll be with the many others, another statistic, one of minne at that! There's not alot that runs through this thick skull but veins that pumps like no other. Many want to know, what makes Damien's mind tick...my mind click at the sight, sight of blood. One thing that pumps me is knowing just one of these's blows are going finally set it off...one of the blows is going to strike blood. and once blood is strucken, thats when I'm at my best, I'm like a vulture hovering over his dead prey. And when I do strike upon you...you all will feel my depth wrath of a vengence...my vengence! My vengence against the nation's society! I know I aint great, but I'm...
..."SIMPLY"... ..."THE"... ..."_BEST_"... And I intend on proving it to the world by winning that 15 man Battle Royal. See, as I look upon my opponents, I see nothing but flashbacks of beatings in the back of my head and as I acquire them and look upon you as I look upon them. That means, you all are screwed in the worst way! As i look upon you, I see the guy(s) that made my life horrible, miserable, and I just snap and there's really no stopping me. For a change, this is going to be my day, my night of honor!
[For just a second, Damien breaks a smile. But soonly vanishes, he walks around the burnt room and see bricks dangling off each other and then theres a crackle. He looks down and see's a crack picture frame with a picture of an man and his wife and 2 kids. Right then and there, a smirk redundantly bounces on his face. He rubs his fingertips down the cracked glass and then slowly drops the frame]
["DANGEROUS" DAMIEN COOPER]: As they say, a picture is worth a 1000 words. And once Warfare start, you'll see nothing more then a battle field of blood. The sheded blood of my worthy opponents. Right now my back is pretty much up, against the wall. With the image of 14 other superstars approaching, and i have to take out them one by one. But the deeper one's passion's burn, the more courage that builds up and shields him much harm. You know, a man's heart beat to its own band...and right now its beating, it just feels like I want to explode. You know the feeling, when you're all angered up inside and its all about to explode. Wellthis Warfare, you'll see it all explode!
[He pauses for a few seconds...] ["DANGEROUS" DAMIEN COOPER]: Hatfield, you are one of the many that most definately, will be ranned over to clear the path of Damien's wrath. So far there isnt nothing to stop me. And all the anger and hatred, sall be gathered upon you like ants dead worms! You all live in fear, you fear what you dont understand and that what drives you out. But let me tell you all, when you look across that ring, be better be damn sure when you come over to attack, you better believe that I have every intention of killing you.
[Cooper then begins to kick some rubble and walk out the building.. He starts to walk and then the camera quickly fades to black.]
--Handler of: Mystikal [IWF,CNWL,GWF,JPW][CAREER: 76-12-9] "Dangerous" Damien Cooper [INWI,NEW,ICW,IEWC][CAREER: 3-2-0]
