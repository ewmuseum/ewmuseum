{Free Agent} Irish Devil

21 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm looking for a e-mail fed with fake wrestlers and uses reply to all to send roleplays. I dont really care for those BCC feds. I also dont want to be in a fed where you have to count how many lines you did because there is a silly line minumum. Thats really about it. I'm not that picky just looking for a fun league that you win matches by who did the more entertaining roleplay, not who wrote the biggest essay. Here is my last roleplay right before this other league I was in closed down.
Irish Devil ( Irish Devil is in a bedroom just waking up. He rolls over and see's some
Peurto Rican chick lying next to him. )
Irish Devil: Damn, I must of been drunk. I better go to the doctor to make sure
I dont have any diseases.
( Irish Devil walks out of the apartment building and see's a couple of Peurto
Rican's smoking crack in the halls. )
Crackhead: Yo meda meda, el es de buy coloro tv. Only diez y sinco dinero. Irish Devil: I didn't understand one God damn thing you just said to me. Quite frankly, I could care less what you said to me. Your a crackhead spic and thats all you'll ever mount up to be.
Crackhead #2: Meda Meda, el caro es la stealio. Irish Devil: What? Speak any english immigrant! ( Irish Devil turns around and see's a bunch of Peurto Ricans breaking into his car and starting his car up with a screwdriver. Irish Devil runs after them but they speed away screaming yeeba yeeba angerey angerey. )
Irish Devil: God damn it, just what I needed. I'm stuck in the middle of spicville. ( Irish Devil goes to use the payphone but the phone is broke off. He looks around and see's a bunch of Peurto Rican's with a crowbar opening up the fire heidrant to take a shower . He see's a Peurto Rican with a cell phone acrossed the street and crosses over. )
Irish Devil: Umm...el telefono....me borrow. Phone Guy: I speak english man but you aint using my phone unless you pay me 5 bucks. Irish Devil: 5 bucks? Here take it even though this damn phone is stolen. ( Irish Devil calls for a cab. 10 Minutes later the cab comes and Irish Devil hops in. He reaches into his pocket and opens up a bottle of thunderbird. )
Dothead: Hello my friend. Irish Devil: Bring me to the EWF arena. I'll just see there medical doctors over there. Dothead: Is that near 7/11? Irish Devil: Damn it! All you guys know are 7/11's and gas stations. I'll give you directions. Just go.
( An hour later Irish Devil gets to the arena. He gives the cab driver a tip. ) Irish Devil: Here, go buy yourself a slurpee. Dothead: Oh thank you my friend. ( The cab driver speeds off towards 7/11 as Irish Devil heads into the arena. He gets stopped by some reporters. ) Reporter #1: Irish Devil, what are your comments towards Brian "Yap" Rowe. He challenged you to a loser must walk out match.
Irish Devil: Brian, do you really want to throw your wrestling career down the tubes? You just show me the papers and I'll put my John Hancock on it. Loser must walk out match? I'm digging it. Get ready to pack your bags kid cause
Irish Devil never lost a retirement loser must walk out match and I sure as hell dont plan on it either! I heard him come out here running his mouth calling me a jobber. Well he wont be running his mouth anymore once he has to stick his foot in it.
Reporter #2: You have said some harsh racial slurs towards Pablo Perez. Pablo is still calling you ignorant.
Irish Devil: The dumb spic learns a new english word and cant stop saying it. Listen Pablo, the time for talk is over. Bring your dirty brown ass to the ring so I can kick it. Now get out of my way, I need to see a doctor. I may have
Spic crabs or one of those foreign dirty diseases!
This is where the power lies! (Irish Devil points to his bottle of thunderbird and guzzles it down. Irish
Devil walks into the arena to the medical doctors room. )
Love, Irish Devil [detail withheld] The Legend Destroyer
