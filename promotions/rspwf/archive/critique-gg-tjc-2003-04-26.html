[Critique] "GG" TJC

26 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I went a little bit of a differant direction with TJ on this flash for the SWT tourny, tell me what you guys think.
============================================ "Once more unto the breach, dear friends, once more, Or close the wall up with our English dead! In peace there's nothing so becomes a man As modest stillness and humility;
But when the blast of war blows in our ears, Then imitate the action of the tiger: Stiffen the sinews, summon up the blood."
- Sir William Shakespeare King Henry V , Act 3 [ The scene fades into a very eerie, somewhat rusticly dark scene. A glimpse of an aged bookshelf lined with seasoned dusty novels becomes apparent. A few glimpses of the titles along the spines of them become slightly visible yet the layers of dust do not allow for a modest view. A battle torn hand quickly swipes across the novels and the titles become clearer. The hand reaches for one novel in particular and the scene moves with the book as it is placed on an aged wooden table. The book is then set aside to the right as the now somewhat familiar hand reaches for something off the screen and then brings it to the direct left of the book. A flame is ignited as it is forced to come in contact with the whick of the candle.
The room now somewhat illuminated gives off a historic setting as you see the hand rest on the table. ]
VOICE OVER: It's somewhat humerous to me when a man believes in himself so much that he underestimates the massive force that stands right in front of his face. Don't get me wrong now, I've been known to underestimate what lies in front of me, but never have I been known to not follow through with my words and goals.
At least when I go out to speak upon something, or someone, I know what I am talking about, and do research on whom I speak of. The man this allegory is directed towards is definently going to be found dumbfounded for ever pondering that the force he meets on the 14th of May is a trivial set aside.
[ The angle slowly changes and moves around the wooden table. The chest and arms that belong to the voice being projected become visible. The right hand points upwards and the angle slowly tilts upward and the very familiar face is clearly apparent. ]
"GOD'S GIFT" TJ CORRUPTION: The face is obvious, the skills are obvious, the talent is definently obvious, but why can't a man that goes by the name Jason Keening take heed to the obvious warnings laid right in front of his face? To tell the truth, I heard a little bit of what
Keening had to say, I was pretty disappointed. I took a seat in my humble abode, attempted to soak everything in and better understand the man they call the "Screaming Drillbit."
It didn't take very long for me to come to a conclusion about the kid's sense of being. Don't throw this out of porportion now Keening, because honestly this is just my point of view. I _THINK_ your starved for intelligence Keening. Honestly, I think you care a lot about how people interpret your brainpower. You can come back and say the exact same thing to me if you want Keening, but obviously I don't care to much about what people in this industry think of me. That should be pretty obvious by now, and if it's not then I suggest you watch me better.
[ Corruption reaches into his shirt pocket and pulls out a fresh pack of
Turkish Gold cigarettes. Corruption begins packing the cigarettes on the wooden table as a small amount of dust accumulates around the table. Corruption sets the cigarettes down on the table and sets the lighter down alongside them. ]
"GG" TJC: I've never cared about what other people think about me, all throughout my career people have underestimated me and shunned me to the side. That's all fine and dandy, but one can only be shunned for so long. It all becomes apparent when the 3 counts eventually pile up, the dust clears, and you have an undefeated competitor on top of the game.
Keening, you along others still continue to shun me as being a trivial opponent, good sir, your head must be in cloud nine right about now if you believe the contest on the 14th is going to be an idle cakewalk.
Just like the fallen foes of TJ Corruption before you, the verdict will come clear after your removal from this tournament your so favored to conquer.
Then will you shun me Keening? Then will you continue to live up in cloud nine with your self proclaimed godliness? Will you continue to beat your drum behind forest fires attempting to prove your the Native American's personification of glory? Please Keening, come down off your cloud and back to the real world. Your just another entertainer with paint on his face trying to put on a show for the kids who view our little sports show week after week. Not some Native American hero who will prevail over evil for his culture, and beliefs.
[ Corruption opens the recently packed Turkish Gold cigarettes and pulls one out, picking up the lighter and lighting it in the musky old room.
The smoke bellows and hangs above in the placid library, but Corruption seems to care not for the well being of the room or his lungs. ]
"GG" TJC: Let me just throw something out there Keening, I take it you put at least half of your life into novels like these.
[ Corruption flips the book in front of him on the table to the title page. Corruption's finger points to the title, it reads "A Cultured Individual - The
Know How." ]
"GG" TJC: Primers on becoming a culturally enveloped being are probably where you got your little personality about being a savior for the good of the world and your little Native Americans. Then again, who knows? Maybe you really are just one of those incompetent people who lives by guidelines set by people in their lineage.
Honestly Keening, do you think your ancient relatives really set their morals and standards for the little "Screaming Drillbit" back hundreds of years ago? Keening, even if your lineage tells you to be and act a certain way, all it will ever be is fake. Why continue to try and live with morals of your ancient ancestors when your out in a squared circle night after night performing for drunkard idiots and attempting to beat another man down for that next dollar in your wallet? We don't live a very moral filled life Keening, and if your two ignorant to notice that then I'm truely sorry.
[ Corruption grabs the book off the table and tosses it behind him into the aged shelf. Corruption then stands on his two feet and looks into the camera, picking up the candle off the table and holding it by his side. ]
"GG" TJC: I'm really sorry if this was too personal for you Keening, but someone had to tell you eventually. Forget the past Keening, live to be yourself, do what _YOU_ want to do. Who knows though? Maybe you enjoy acting how you do, and in that case, I think your nothing more then a joke.
Your not a bad guy Keening, and honestly I believe you already have enough things in your life that you feel down trodden about but I've got a job to do. I wouldn't have minded peace between us if you weren't to get in my way, and hell, peace sells. But honestly, who is buying?
[ Corruption begins to walk out the door as he tosses the candle behind him and the books slowly begin to engulf in flames as the camera fades out to black. ]
