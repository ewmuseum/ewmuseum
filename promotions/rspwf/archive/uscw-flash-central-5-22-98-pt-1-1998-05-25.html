USCW Flash Central 5/22/98 Pt. 1

25 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Storm w/Venus The Horsemen Alliance Jacob Yoder The Question All American Male Viper The Marauders -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- Storm: Now wasn't that one hell of a tag-team match? I mean lets face it,
Syske and Dylan got exactly what was coming to them. See boys, all week I have been warning you as to what the SWA had planned. But despite all the warnings, you went off in your own little world and pretended, and preached and blabbed like you could take down the SWA. I honestly began to think that you were believing your own crap!
Venus: Typical little jobbers, always trying to act bigger then they really are. As far as I'm concerned, Syske and Dylan are over. The graves have been dug and their lying in crap that they have dished out. So long loser and good riddance!!
Storm: And speaking of crap, Apocalypse, Genesis and Exodus, just what the hell were you three little shits thinking of? If you think the SWA is going to sit by and forget about the whole incident, then you three asswipes are in for one hell of a rude awakening.
Venus: Where the hell do these jobbers get off thinking they can take on the
SWA? It just baffles the mind.
Storm: Hmmmmm, lets examine this situation. As of now, there are three of them and only two SWA members in the USCW. Pretty good odds if you ask me.
But I think a phone call is needed just to make sure that this little incident doesn't repeat itself. Trust me you three bastards, you haven't heard the last of the SWA. As for the so called All American Male, please, you make my stomach turn. All this American flag waving. You know, the SWA has milked this country for everything thats its worth. And considering what this country has to offer, we have pretty much starved. I mean, only in America can a worthless jobber...
Venus: Oxi-moron isn't it? Storm: Such as this All American Male be aloud to speak. What the hell can this moron possibly have to say thats worth listening to? AAM, you seem to have this hang up with Spicer and the SWA. Now, I really don't know where you get your crap from but listen up because I sure the hell am not going to repeat this, Spicer and the SWA have no relationship what so ever.
Venus: If you say so. HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!! Storm: I mean so what if Spicer and the SWA dine out once and awhile. And perhapes we catch a few ball games in old Fenway Park, but that doesn't mean we're buddy buddy. You know, if I didn't know any better, I would think you were a little jealous of the SWA's success. After all, your career has failed miserably and your going to aim your hatered to anyone with more talent, success, ability, money, blah, blah, blah. But AAM, you have jumped down the throat of the wrong group. You are one patheitc little jobber.....we are the most powerful, successful and biggest stable out there. Geez, lets just see whos going to win this one. Oh and Sinister, your burnign bed awaits your arrival. See you around bud!! And on that note, to everyone, grab a big one and SUCK IT!!
Venus: SWA 4 LIFE!! -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- (Scene: "Detroit" Diesel's Garage.) "Big Man" Mountain: Well Mr. Kain, Mr. Stanley, it looks like your in the same position we are, having to deal with those brain dead morons that call themselves Da Lench Mob. Well they run their mouths at us too,... inbred hillbillies indead!...
"Little" Joe Mountain: ...And we've beaten them two out of the three times we've gone against them.
"Detroit" Diesel Jones: So we propose that you and us "temporarily" pool our resources against them. It's up to you, either way we're going to rob them of their "golden" titles. And that's all we've got to say!
(The screen fades to black.) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- **You see two guards standing in front of the USCW building. The walkie talkie that guard #1 is wearing squaks and he speaks. ** Guard #1: Post #3 here. Go ahead. From the walkie talkie: There is an unknown man coming to the front doors. Be advised. We do not know who it is. Try to hold him at the front door.
**Guard #1 and Guard #2 look at each other and are looking scared. ** Guard #2: If it is Viper, I swear. I will quit right now! There is no way I am gonna end up in the hospital like all these other poor schmucks.
Guard #1: I think I am with you buddy. **Jacob Yoder walks around the corner and stops in front of the guards. ** Jacob: I want to see thine boss, NOW! **The two guards look a little releived.** Guard #1: Get out of here you Amish twit before we beat you up and send you packing to your old Amish lady.
**Both guards chuckle at this just ignore Jacob as he stands there getting redder and redder. ** Guard #2: So anyway, Viper came up to Hutchinson's and just hit him over the head with a baton, almost killed the .....
Jacob: I wilt say this one more time...... Guard #1: Look, I saw Witness. I will kick your *Bleep* if you do not get on your horse and buggy.
Jacob: ARRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRGGGGG **Jacob grabs Guard #2 by the throat and throws him through the glass doors. He then kicks Guard #1 in the mid section and smashes him on his head. The guard falls to the ground. Jacob turns him over, gets on top of him and gets right in his face. **
Jacob: Tell your boss Rob Spicer that I want that match Vs. Viper at the PPV. If I don't, then I will make what Viper did around here look like Sharing Week on Sesame Street.
Guard #1: But.....But....You have a match Vs. Sinister! Jacob: The Delinquent will take my place. I WANT VIPER! I want to show everyone once and for all that I am the real champ. He has been ducking me ever since I used him last weekend. You tell him. YOU GOT IT??????
Guard: Yes sir. *gulp* I will tell him. **Jacob gets off of the guard and leaves. The screen turns blank. ** -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- (A man in a mask is standing in a dark room. A light shines on him from above. No eye slits are shown on the mask. Just a solid black mask with a question mark on it.) The Question\ USCW. Be aware that The Question walks among you. Who am I? That is the question. I have walked among you all for all this time. With this mask,
I will take vengence. I am sick of doing things the "Right" way. Are you scared? You better be. Stephens, Abyss, Sharpe. You are all on my list. What will I do? You come up with the anwser.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- All American Male is seen working out at the gym. He lifts the dumb bell three more times and then stops. He gets off of his weight bench and looks towards the camera.
AMM: Eight out of ten Americans are overweight. That is why it is important to take care of your body. Please start today with a proper diet and steady exercise. Exercise can prevent heart attacks later in life. Now that I have that off my chest. Lets talk about a different kind of gluttony. Storm, you and your Spicer Sponsored SWA are taking more from the USCW than what you are giving. You are a disgrace and an embarrassment to decent wrestlers everywhere.
Do not make the mistake of over looking me. I am coming at you at full force my friend. Male towels off his body and continues. AMM: I am stepping up my training to start a winning streak that has never been seen in the USCW before. I have the kids, the parents and the God fearing church goers of this country behind me, and I will not let them down. Now, if you will excuse me, I have to get back to lifting.
Male sits on the bench and starts his lifting again as the camera fades into black. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- (Scene opens to Viper sitting on the bank of a river fishing... Viper has on a shirt that has pictures of Danny Dare, Delinquent, and Yoder on the front, and it says "Rob Spicer's STOOGE squad" on the back... Viper also has a "Rob Spicer fan club" baseball cap on, blue overalls with the straps undone, and a pair of Nike Air sneakers... On the ground next to Viper is a "Rob Spicer is a dumb fuck" mug, right next to the USCW World Title...
Viper is using a stick with some string tied to it to try to fish... He has a weed hanging out of his mouth, and a 6 pack of beer stuffed in the river to "keep it cold"... Viper looks to the camera grinning)
Viper: So, THIS is how the Amish relax... Wow, what fun it is... See I'm trying to get a "feel" for poor old Yoder and his Amish clan... Seems I upset him when I did my little Wet T-Shirt contest... Well Yoder, I will apologize, I wasn't talking about your WIFE Yoder, I really wasn't... I'm sorry ya thought that, I would NEVER mess with the SANCTITY of AMISH
MARRIAGE!!! Nope, I was talkin about YO MAMA!!!! HAHA, thatz right Yoda,
THAT was the woman who bore you!!!
Viper: Anyhow Yoder, yer right, I AM jealous of you... I'm SO jealous that YOU won a tainted match over ME, as YOU claim I did over YOU...
There's ONE big difference though Amish Boy... MY "Tainted" victory, was a
WORLD TITLE match... Yours, was just another match... HAHAHAHAHA, life sux for you Amish doesn't it???
Viper: But ya DID catch me Yoda, I'm SO scared of you, I just HAVE to attack yer "Booty Boy" to get back at ya... But funny, I seem to remember laying YOUR ass out on my way to him also... Musta been my imagination, or maybe it was a DIFFERENT ugly Amish boy... Either way, don't oversell yerself... You think yer the "Raging Bull"... Heh, I think of ya as more like "The Bull in Heat" 'cause thatz all all yer yellin sounds like...
Viper: Seems The Marauder doesn't want his World Title match anymore... Well, here ya go Yoder, I'M challenging YOU to a World Title match at the
PPV... With ONE stipulation... IF there is ANY outside interference, any at all... THAT person will be fired from the USCW... Itz that easy Yoder, we've had 2 matches, both inundated with interference... So if ya want another fuckin shot at The Snake... YOU agree to keep yer butt boy away, and I'll agree to keep Sinman and Bullet away... Itz that easy, and then you get one last chance to "avenge" yerself...
Viper: Now, Delinquent... I toldja you'd come out and talk about how ya
"got up" and that it "didn't hurt" I knew ya would... THAT is how predictable YOU are... But like I said, I wasn't trying to take you out
Yoda wanna-be... I was just tryin to make ya lose... Itz that simple... And I did what I set out to do... So enuff about you Viper: Lessee, oooohhhh yea, Ladies Lover Jeff Gillis... Been a LONG fuckin time boyo, see you STILL havent; fuckin changed... You say I'm a target? Well hell, THAT will be nuthin fuckin new... Seems like I'm
ALWAYS a target... SO if you feel you need to add yerself to the "I wish I could beat Viper, but I can't so I'll just call him a target" list...
Please, go ahead... I'll get ta you in time... Viper: Oh yea... Seems there's this new "Force" for the hWo... OHHHHH NOOO!!! Itz Jaw???? As in like, LockJaw? IronJaw? Maybe SUPERJaw??? So now yer gonna come get me too eh? Wow, I am SO upset over that... I'd tell you to join the same list that Gillis is gonna be joinin, but hell, I don't feel like sayin it again... But I think you get the point... Oh and look, he's SUCH a bad ass, he's challenging... GASP... THE
DEMOLITIONIST!!!! Boy, you sure do take chances for yer premier dontcha??? WOW!!! I guess I SHOULD be scared of ya huh?? NOT!!!! Viper: And it seems that Danny Dare, ran for it... He came here all ready to go, he attacked me from behind and made a BIG showing pf himself...
Then, when the time came for him to step in the ring with me and Sin-man... HE goes "inactive", which basically means he runs to save his fuckin life... I'm not surprised Dare, That makes TWICE I've made ya run like the bitch ya are... So have fun wherever yer hiding at... I'll be waitin fer yer sorry ass to come back and prove sumthin BESIDES that you can attack people from behind...
Viper: Spicey, yer Stooge Squad just got smaller, Dare ran for his fuckin life... And Yoder and Delinquent are nuthin to me... Hell, I laid them
BOTH out in the span of 30 seconds the other day... Who ya gonna bring along next Spicey? Who's yer NEXT Stooge??? No matter WHO it is Spicey, it won't be enuff... You've been a thorn in my side for WAY too long now, and like all thorns, ya need to be plucked, and broken!!!
'Nuff Said -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- ( Scene opens to the outside of the USCW gym, where who knows how many reporters are waiting outside. " Shotgun " Shawn Evans walks out of the gym, with " The Enforcer " Mike Adams. Evans is wearing his Marauders baseball cap, and his " Shotgun " Shawn Evans shorts and t-shirt ( and still has his knee brace ). Behind Shawn, Mike has his sunglasses on, with the Marauders shirt and shorts. Both have gym bags slung around their shoulders. A large amount of noise floods the street, as both
Shawn and Mike look on in a sad way. ) Shawn : ( Yells ) If you'll all be quiet, I'll answer the question. ( The noise level drops dramatically. ) Shawn : OK. Good. To say the least, YES, I know what Chris did this afternoon. I mean, it was the BIG thing in the USCW today. Am I upset at him? No, I'm not. Chris is my brother, and I understand him more than anybody else, except for maybe Mike over here. Chris is going through a stage in his life that he must go through alone. If I go after him, what's that gonna solve? I'll just prolong this stage, and sooner or later, it'll come out bigger than ever.
Reporter : ( Yells out ) Did you know what he meant when he said that he was going to go " back to his roots " to train?
Shawn : Of course I did. ( At this point, the reporters explode into a flurry of questions asking what Chris mean when he said what he said. )
Shawn : ( YELLS ) QUIET Shawn : That's better. I'm not going to tell you all what Chris meant, cause obviously he wants to go through this stage by himself, with none of this media following him around. Now if you'll excuse me, I've got a match to prepare for.
( Shawn pushes through the crowd of reporters, and limps off the screen. Mike then comes up and starts talking. ) Mike : To me, I'm not upset at Chris. But I will miss him for the period he'll be gone. Me and Shawn can handle everything independantly, and will continue our winning ways. But Chris ; wherever you are, if you're watching this, we'll miss you, and good luck on your journey.
( Mike pushes through the large group and leaves as the screen fades. ) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -----------------------------------------------
