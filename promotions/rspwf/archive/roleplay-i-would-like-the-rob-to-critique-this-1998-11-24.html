[Roleplay] I would like The Rob to critique this.....

24 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is very short but nonetheless, I would like it critiqued by the man known only to us as The Rob.
-- [Fade in.] [A small room with plaques and other degrees hanging on the wall plays host to the scene. The man taking the shot of the scene is standing in front of a large round black table in front of a large window overlooking the city of Boston,
Massachusetts. There is a large black chair in back of the desk. There is someone sitting at the desk as you can see some hair standing on edge from the top of the chair. A voice from afar begins to be heard.]
VA - The city of Chicago? The windy city as they call it. Maybe that nickname is more fitting than you know. You see I feel a wind, a wind of change. The wind is the wind of change and the weatherman is.....
.....Doctor David Diveristy !!! [The chair suddenly swings around revealing Dr. David Diversity wearing a white doctor's jacket, a pair of broken spectacles, and a wild hair dew. He sits in front of the desk with his hands in a folded position. He starts to speak.]
DD - Well, [name withheld] and me both sat down, I thought he needed a brain tumour when he told me he left the
EWC. I would have done it for five dollars and a jaw- breaker, but that is a different story all together. But anyways, he said he was going to form a new promotion, he said it was going to be called CWC. The Chicago
Wrestling Corporation, sounded like a great idea. He said all I had to do was sign the dotted line. I jumped at the idea. So now under the CWC's membership I must fight a man that says, "It Ain't Personal." Well I think that when you get in the ring with me.....
.....IT ....._IS_ .....PERSONAL !!! Why is it personal? Well because you are my first stepping stone to the Cook County Title. You see Antawn, I am not in the CWC to just run around and become so kind of mascot.
No. I am here to win the belts, the first one on my list is the
Cook County Championship Belt. So Antawn. Watch out because when.....
.....WHEN .....YOU .....PAGE .....THE .....DOCTOR..... [His face gets very intense.] .....YOU .....BETTER .....CALL .....A .....REAL .....DOCTOR!!!!! [The scene fades to black.] ------ -Chris ------ Handler of - Dr. David Diversity [CWC, MWA, NEW?, HHWO?] and also "Cutthroat" Craig Cypress [TOP] ------ President of - NOWL - National Online Wrestling League ------ Vice President of - LAW - Los Angeles Wrestling ------
