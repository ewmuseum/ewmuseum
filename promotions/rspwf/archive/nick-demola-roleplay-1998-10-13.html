Nick Demola roleplay

13 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NOTE: I figured that some of you hadn't ever seen my stuff, so I'm posting a roleplay just for the Hell of it. Just so I can get some attention. :-)
NOTE 2: This is dedicated to my cousin Michelle... I only wish I could be there for her. (The camera fades into a hospital lobby in Seattle, Washington where all is peaceful and quiet... until a car blaring the Black Album from Metallica is heard coming up to the entrance... the tires squeal and the 1988 White Dodge
Shadow plows into a parking spot. The man in the Submit or Die Tour '98 t-shirt and black jeans runs out of the car. The darkness of the early morning overwhelms all of the man's surroundings. You can't even see his wavy, dark brown hair as he rushes into the hospital. He crashes through the sliding glass doors and runs up to the receptionist desk...)
Demola: Where is the room for [name withheld]? (The receptionist is still in shock at this turn of events. This doesn't usually happen at three in the morning.)
Receptionist: Room 312. But visiting hours are over. (Nick looks perturbed.) Demola: Listen. She was just brought in here and I need to see here. Room 312? (The receptionist presses a button under her desk.) Receptionist: I can't let you through at these hours. (Nick slams his fist on the desk, breaking it in half.) Demola: DAMN IT! I'M GOING IN!!!!!!! (Three men in blue t-shirts and black jeans come out from a small room. They are all about 6'8", 300 pounds. They stop Nick in his tracks.) Demola: Get out of my DAMN WAY! (Nick realizes that he is swearing for the first time in nine months or so and immediately blesses himself. The three men look at him in a weird way.)
Demola: Are you gonna let me through? Or do I need to break through? (The first man speaks in a deep bass voice.) Man #1: Do I know you? (Nick looks impatient.) Demola: Of course you do! You've probably seen me on TV! (The second man snaps his fingers...) Man #2: Ah yeah! You's dat crazy mutha on TV talkin' bout Tha Myth, Tha
Man.... Demola: THE MAN, THE MYTH, THE LEGEND... THE GOD OF WRESTLING!!!!! YES!!!! NOW GET OUT OF MY WAY!!!!!!!! (The third man speaks.) Man #3: Man, I love your stuff! You's one crazy mutha fucka! (Nick calms down and speaks.) Demola: Guys, this has been great fun, but I have a cousin in room 312 that is dying of bone cancer and I need to see her now before it's too late.
This idiot receptionist won't let me through, but you guys understand, right? (The three men nod.) Man #1: Just be real quiet... Man #2: And give us all autographs! Demola: Fine. As soon as I leave I'll hook you all up. Just let me through. (The men let him through and Nick runs straight up the stairs to the third floor. He finds room 312 and sees his Uncle Bob and Aunt Marsha sitting on the chairs outside of Michelle's room. He goes to hug them and they embrace him.)
Demola: How's she doing? (Aunt Marsha begins to cry.) Bob: Not too well. She's slipped a bit in the past couple minutes. Demola: I'm so sorry. Bob: At least you're here. She wanted to talk to you. Demola: Is she awake? Bob: You can wake her up. She won't mind it. Demola: Thank you Uncle Bob. (Nick hugs them both again and enters the room. It is a completely white room. The television set is still turned on... some spanish game show or something. Nick walks to the side of his cousin's bed. Only 17 years old. Her face is still as sweet as ever. Her eyes, still innocent. Her long blonde hair still the same as it was when they were growing up in Dallas and Detroit together. Nick begins to weep and holds Michelle's hand. She squeezes his hand.)
Michelle (in a weak voice): Nick? (Nick immediately wipes his tears and tries to act strong.) Demola: Michelle... how are you? Michelle: Well... a bit weak. Demola: I'm sorry for asking such a stupid question. Michelle: It's okay. What else could you ask? (Michelle takes a deep breath before speaking again.) Michelle: What have you been doing with yourself? Demola: Well, I have my wrestling thing. I have a title match and a big pay per view on the weekend, not to mention the re-opening of my federation.
But that's all just stupid crap compared to you. I want to know what I can do to help. Michelle: Just be here... (Nick squeezes her hand.) Demola: You need your rest. Michelle: No... (She yawns.) Demola: Yes you do. I'll be back later to say hi some more. I just wanted you to know that I got here ASAP as soon as I heard. I couldn't find Frank,
Dave, or Al, but I'm sure their prayers are with you too.
Michelle: Thank you. Demola: Alright hon, I'll see you in the morning. Sweet dreams. Michelle: Thanks... (She drifts into sleep as Nick walks out of the room. He sees Bob and Marsha and gives them one more hug. He then walks out of the hospital, signs autographs, and gets into his car with the XWF cameraman. He speaks into the camera.)
Demola: It makes you realize how important it is to breathe, doesn't it? It makes you realize how lucky we are. God, I have an amazing girlfriend, amazing friends all around me, an amazing career... and just like that...
(Nick snaps his fingers.) Demola: It could all be taken from me. (Nick wipes his eyes.) Demola: That girl and I grew up together. Forget my character right now. Damn it, we don't need that crap right now. That girl and I grew up together. We went on Easter egg hunts together. We spent Christmases and
Thanksgivings together. She was my buddy growing up. Then she had to move to Seattle and I moved to New Hampshire. I never expected this.
(Nick wipes his eyes.) Demola: You'd think this would make me lose focus, wouldn't you? Hardly. Rather, this has made me gain my focus even clearer. C Murder, I'm not going for a win this weekend. I'm going to give the greatest performance of my life. I dedicate my performance to Michelle. If I win, the belt goes straight to Seattle. She can keep it. I'll pay for a new belt. If I lose,
I'll go straight back to Seattle to be with her whenever she needs me.
(Nick pops in the Metallica CD and puts track number five on pause.) Demola: TRU, XDT, C Murder, Nytro... this isn't about me vs. you. This is about all of us coming together to put a performance on for the XWF fans and for each other. Once again, forget my character, I speak to you now as my real life person. Back in the locker room there is lots of dissention.
There are people who are saying this about each other, or that about each other. Frankly, it's all meaningless.
For once guys, let's do it together. Let's get out there, put on a tremendous show, and show the world that the XWF is THE premier group of talent in wrestling.
(Nick pulls over and looks into the camera.) Demola: And Michelle. This one's for you. I may win, I may lose, I may tie... who cares. But you will know that my heart and soul are with you every step of the way. I may go out there with all the glitz and glamour of a rock star, but inside I am just a man with a huge heart.
(Nick continues driving.) Demola: So don't leave me for dead. And Michelle, hang on. I'll be there for you! STRIKE UP THE BAND!!!!!! Nick Demola is OUTTA HERE!!!!!!!!
(The camera fades out to "Wherever I May Roam" blaring in the car.)
