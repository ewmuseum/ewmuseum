[Flash] Tommy Grayson: Constructive Criticism, please

17 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here's an RP I did for EFW. If anybody wants to volunteer and give me some constructive criticism on this, please feel free to do so. Thanks :)
============================================== [SCENE: EFW locker rooms in the EFW Arena. Time is 8:44 PM during a house show. Cameras first shows a close-up of the EFW Extreme North
American title. The light is reflecting off the well-polished gold. The camera zooms back, and we see the man carrying it is the reigning champion, "Loose Cannon" Tommy Grayson. Tommy has the belt draped over his right shoulder.]
[APPEARANCE: Tommy is wearing a wifebeater (tank top) that shows off his moderately cut and muscular physique, dark blue Adidas pants with three white stripes down each side, and white Adidas sneakers. He has a Tommy Lee-ish look to him as he has his crewcut black hair spiked with gel. He appears to have a slight stubble.]
[Tommy looks up from the belt and then glares at the camera with his cold, brown eyes, which shows no mercy. He does his trademark smirk and then begins to speak...]
TOMMY GRAYSON - So far, Despair, I've been in the lead. At almost every frickin' corner, I have _DESTROYED_ you. Annihilated you. Sure, you fought back...but I fought back _HARDER_, Despair! Ya hear me?!? I don't think you're not giving the effort ya did at first. Ya know why?
Cuz when ya look into these eyes, you _KNOW_ that you can't stand a _FRICKIN'_ chance against Good Ol' Tee-F'N-Gee. You know that you'll _NEVER_ be able to get this title from around my shoulder...and most of all, ya know that you're nuthin' but a piece of trash who couldn't beat _YOURSELF_ if ya frickin' tried.
You're nuthin' but another gothic goon, Despair. Every single _JABRONI_ in the EFW locker rooms know that...and this Sunday, Extreme Heartbreak, I promise -- no, I frickin' _GUARANTEE_ that I will take you... ..._TO_... ..._HELL_... ..._AND_... ..._BACK_!! You'll done nuthin' against me except feel _EXTREME PAIN_, and at Extreme Heartbreak...it will be no different. I am the most dangerous, most wicked, most bitchin' mother -- [high-pitched voice] SHUT YO'
MOUTH! [normal voice] In Extreme Fantasy Wrestling right now...and there's not a damn thing that you or anybody else for that matter can do about it.
[Tommy points at his belt and looks at it. He begins to speak.] TOMMY GRAYSON - Ya see this belt? Ya know what it means? It means that I'm the best son of a _BI-ATCH_ in North-frickin'-America. It means I represent every single McDonald's in North America, every single
Denny's in North America, every single _SOUL_ in North America...and hell, every single frickin' _CRACKWHORE_ in North-frickin'-America!
[Tommy looks up at the camera with that vicious stare again...] TOMMY GRAYSON - I'm representing you, ya fat bozo! You, the fat bitch eating the frickin' popcorn watching me cut this damn promo! So...ya know what I want? No, I don't want a damn thing from your pathetic fat, cellulite ass....nah...I am challenging _EVERY SINGLE_ EFW competitor to come after my frickin' belt! I don't give a [BLEEP] who the hell it is! Whether it's Hellspawn, Impaler, Glass Joe, Mick Mann,
Cold Hearted Killah, War Child, Unleaded...hell, I'll even take on one of those gothic geeks who call themselves the C.o.D.! If ya think I'm issuing these challenges cuz I'm the best damn thing since whipped cream plus sex...
..._YOU'RE DAAAAAMN RIGHT_!! Anyway, enough about all the pieces of trash. Despair, they say that this is the _LAST_ frickin' time I take you on...and ya know what? I'm not gonna waste this one. I'm gonna hit ya with everything I've got.
[chuckles] Fireballs, barbed wire, baseball bats wrapped with barbed wire, brass knuckles, pencils, spikes...whatever ya want, I've got.
And since it's a Cemetary Match, I just might have a special treat for ya, Despair...and I hardly _EVER_ have special treats for anybody. See ya on Sunday, Despair...
...LIVE IF YOU _DARE_!! ..._DIE_ BECAUSE YOU _MUST_!! Cuz you'll _NEVER_ beat... ..._GOOD_... ..._OL'_... ..._TEE_... ..._GEE_!! [Tommy puts a long, cold stare on the camera...FADE.] Handler of: "The Loose Cannon" Tommy Grayson [EFW, EWA, ICW, LAW] Current EFW Extreme North American Champion Current ICW North American Champion Winner of ICW's 1998 Biggest Lunatic and 1998 Most Underrated Award "...I'm gonna pimp 'em around the ring like they were whacked-up, knocked-up, stuck-up two-cent crackwhores on forty-frickin' second street!" - Yours truely
