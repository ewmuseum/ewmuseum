[Flash] Johnny Sledge

14 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[When the scene begins, the camera pans around this dark, smoke filled room and you realize that you are in a sports bar. The place is packed with screaming drunken men at the two big screens that are set up.
Playing is the Undercard for the De la Hoya vs. Quartey fight. The show has just begun and the women’s match is on. The camera goes over to the door and just as the cameraman does this the door opens and it is… yup you guessed it, Johnny Sledge. Sledge is in a very calm mood, since he is only a few days away from his match with his quarterfinal opponent,
Myke Fury. He is wearing a pair of loose fitting jeans, Nike hiking boot, and a black leather jacket. Sledge also is wearing a navy blue
USA Wrestling hat. Sledge taps one of the guys on the shoulder that is sitting at the bar.]
Sledge: Hey Ray, did you see where my buddies are sitting? Ray: Yeah Johnny, the crew of them are at the table over there. Sledge: Thanks man, I’ll talk to you later. [With that Sledge begins to walk over to the far side of the room. By now the Lady’s fight has ended and the second fight is about to begin.
Sledge gets over to a table and there are two guys sitting at the table waiting for Sledge.]
Tony: Wow Johnny, it’s about time you decided to show up. Sledge: Oh shut the hell up. [The bunch of them laugh as Sledge takes his jacket off and puts on the back of the last empty chair at the table. You now see he is wearing an
EWA Hockey Jersey. Sledge sits down and begins to talk with his friends.]
Sledge: Yeah so what’s up with you two slobs. Joe: Nothing much. You know, same old shit just a different day. Tony: Same here man. Not much going on with us two. You seem to be having a jolly old time over there in the EWA?
Sledge: As you can see I have the pleasure of doing an interview now, so I’ll have to get some comments in here sooner or later. I have a match against that kid Myke Fury.
Tony: You mean… Sledge: Yup, him. Joe: hehehehehehe… That’s classic, so what do you have planned for the kid? Sledge: Oh, the usual… I’m going to treat him like any other piece of crap that I have to face in the ring…
[Tony jumps up pointing at the television.] Tony: Did you see that damn punch, he just put him through the ropes! [Joe and Sledge turn around and watch the guy falling through the ropes. Sledge begins to laugh, as the entire group of guys in the bar jump to their feet and begin yelling at the television. This gives the
North American Champion a chance to turn to the camera.]
Sledge: Hey Mykey, how are you doing on this fine evening? I bet you’re sitting there in your home, with all the lights on, just wondering what you are going to do when the two of us step into the ring. Do you fully understand what you are getting yourself into on
Monday? I honestly do not believe you realize it just yet. I am not the number one seed for the fun of it. I don’t have this belt because of my good looks…. Even though…. Uh oh forget it, I’m not going to
become the next “Sextreme.” Sorry about that Tommy, but I just had to get that shot in there… Joe: You say something Johnny? Sledge: Oh no Joe, I was just doing the “interview thing.” Joe: I here ya. [Sledge turns over to the television and begins to laugh out loud.] Sledge: Ahahahahahahahha…. Butterbean is fighting in the next match! Talk about a slob. Joe: Damn, I think that guy still has some mustard on his face from his pre-fight warm ups. Tony: Is that a bottle of Hershey’s Chocolate Syrup that one of the ring men for Butterbean is carrying?
Sledge: This guy is just a tub of crap. [The three of them stop the insults so they can watch the first round of the fight. Whenever a hard punch is landed a few yells can be heard from the guys in the bar. A waitress comes up to the table Sledge is at and begins to take their orders.]
Waitress: Sorry about the wait guys, can I take your order? Sledge: Don’t worry about it. I’ll have a Coke… Waitress: We only have Pepsi. Sledge: That’s fine. Joe: I’ll have a Miller Genuine Draft. Tony: Same here. Sledge: [to Joe and Tony] You guys want to get any appetizers? Tony & Joe: Whatever. Sledge: Alright then, We’ll get the Sampler and the Nacho Plate. Waitress: Is that all? Sledge: Yup that’s it. Waitress: I’ll be right back with the drinks. [The waitress walks away just as the second round of the Butterbean fight being…. What great timing huh? The round doesn’t last that long before Butterbean knocks his opponent through the ropes, and in turn knocking the man out. The crowd of guys in the place begin to laugh at the beating Butterbean just gave that guy, and the waitress brings the drinks over to Sledge’s table.]
Sledge: Thanks. Waitress: The appetizers should be ready in a few more minutes. Joe: No problem. [Sledge passes the two MGD’s over to Joe and Tony. He takes a sip of
Coke before turning to the camera.]
Sledge: So let me get in a few more comments before the next match begins… Myke, you wanted to be one of the best. You said that you want to be at the top of the mountain, and you chance is now. Well you are looking at a person who has been waiting longer, and fighting a lot longer, than you to reach the summit. I am about long reaches away from getting up to the top of the EWA Mountain. I have faced off against snot-nosed punks like you for my entire career, and I expect nothing less than a war with you… well, because you know why. That is my belt being held up right now, and in a matter of a few short weeks I will have the North American Title on my right shoulder and the Global
Hardcore Title over my left one. I said it doesn’t matter who I face, I will take the person out just the same.
[The waitress comes over with the appetizers and puts them down on the table. Sledge then gives her the money for it all and a fairly generous tip.]
Waitress: Thank you very much. Sledge: You’re welcome. [The waitress walks away, and Sledge moves the appetizers into the middle of the table.] Tony: So what do we owe you? Sledge: Don’t worry about this stuff. You get the next round. Tony: Alright Johnny. [The second fight has already begun and seems to be pretty boring. Sledge gets up and grabs his leather jacket.] Sledge: Guys I’m going to go outside and finish up this interview, so we can watch the main event in piece.
Joe: That’s cool. Tony: I here ya. [With that Sledge begins to leave the sports bar. He crosses somebody he knows and the guy starts talking to Sledge.]
Guy: Hey Johnny how you doing? Sledge: Pretty good, and you? Guy: Ok, you know my son is a big fan, can I get your autograph for him? Sledge: Sure, no problem. [Sledge takes out a pen and signs a piece of paper for the guy. Sledge then begins to leave again, but the guy stops him.]
Guy: Johnny, why you leaving so soon, the big fight hasn’t even happened yet? Sledge: I’m going to go finish this interview, so I can watch the main event in piece. Guy: That’s cool. Sledge: I’ll talk to ya later. [Sledge finally gets away from that guy and leaves the sports bar. The cold February evening can be seen through the condensed breath of Johnny
Sledge. Sledge lets out a half laugh before talking.]
Sledge: Damn it’s cold… Well let me get the rest of this interview done, so I can get back in there and watch two guys kick the hell out of each other.
[Sledge paces a little bit, seemingly to get his thoughts together. Sledge stops, and adjusts his hat on his head. He then looks up to the camera.] Sledge: Now at Extreme Measures, Myke, you are hoping that by some miracle of some sort, you defeat me and go on in the tournament. Well that’s exactly what you need a miracle. Because I will be there and at
110%. I’m going to go into that match and destroy you. You see, it’s as easy as that, I’m going to beat the utter piss out of you and there is nothing you can do about it.
You say I am the “Goldberg” of the EWA, well I’m not bald and know more than four moves, but I see what you’re trying to say. I have paid my dues already so for me to be at the point I am at is deservedly mine.
Unbeatable… yeah I guess I seem to be that right now, but maybe you can beat me if… uh… you… oh hell you’re not going to beat me. I’d still beat you if I had both arms and legs cut off and blindfolded. And as you can see… [Waves his arms and then legs] I have all my limbs and it’s a normal match, so the odds of you even coming close to winning are virtually nil. And as for me being the best, I’m not the best… I just win a lot.
So kid, just say your prayers and eat your… oh damn I hope I don’t get sued for using that line… anyway, Myke you know what I’m getting at, you better just start getting over the lose now, because it won’t be that bad trying to get over it later on. I’ve shown many, many a person why, and come Monday you will also find out why I am... The One... The
Only... The Original… Johnny Sledge!!! [Sledge turns and walks back to the door o the sports bar. He opens the door and walks back in, and the picture fades into an ad for Monday night’s Extreme Measures, before finally fading to black.]
