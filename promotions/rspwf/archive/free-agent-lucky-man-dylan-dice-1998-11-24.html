[FREE AGENT] "Lucky Man" Dylan Dice

24 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a recent roleplay I had with the MVW. It's not the best, but one of the only ones I have saved. -------
[Scene opens up to a smokey, dark type of room. Hanging from the ceiling in the room, is one light bulb which has a chain attached to it. In the room, we see various types of items. We see coat hangers, a hamper, wrinkled up clothes on the floor and other things which can't seem to be made out. There is also some sort of door, and from the other side of the door a light illuminates. We then see a man on the floor sitting Indian
Style. The man is wearing nothing but his yellow and brown stained boxer shorts. The man also has a traditional cigar in one hand and a can of
"Lucky 7" Beer is lying on the floor next to him. The man is none other than "Lucky Man" Dylan Dice. Dice takes a puff on his cigar and begins to speak.]
"Lucky Man" Dylan Dice: The decision has been made and the decision stands. In the past week, I have done nothing but plug myself all over the world after my tremendous interview here in the MVW. I have had numerous offers come in, some of which were very tempting. I even had offers from 3 of the top 10 feds. I had someone tell me that they liked my style, but they saw room for improvement. However, in order for me to join with these people, they all requested one thing of me. They requested that I quit the
MVW. I thought about it day in and day out. I was starting to lose sleep over it, just wondering what it would be like to be one of the top stars once again. Hell, I didn't even have any time to wash my laundry for crying out loud. But then I saw one of the coolest things happen in the
MVW. I saw a man by the name of "the Loaded Wepon" Tommy Rifles get crucified. No, I'm not saying I encourage someone to do that to Tommy at all. Tommy is a great guy and a good wrestler. Tommy, I've been there before and I know what it feels like. However, what about this Fang guy?
Is that all you have? I can outdo that any day! I already know by stating these comments, that I am up for some trouble and you know what? I enjoy it! But, there is one other thing that had me pondering this whole weekend and all through the MVW event.
[Dice takes a puff on his cigar.] "Lucky Man" Dylan Dice: Tommy Rifles, I see you took notice to me. Very nice of you to do so. However, I wasn't too thrilled about a comment you made towards me the other day. You called me a *rookie* in this sport. No no, Tommy. You see, I am a *rookie* to the *MVW.* Get your facts straight.
I've been in this sport since 1993, probably longer than you have been here. The way I see it, the MVW is a *rookie* to this sport. When I considered you a legend, I meant in the MVW. But then, you have enough nerve.. you have the *balls* to call me a rookie? I've punked sorry asses like "Bad Boy" Mark Bagwell and sent him home crying to his mommy. I've beaten idiots like Survival, the 1997 RSPWF Wrestler of the Half Year! I know my way around the sport and I've been in fights you can't even dream of. Tommy, I wasn't happy to see what happened to you this past week but I had to say that I got a little chuckle out of it. A man of your caliber should be able to take care of two men by yourself. But let's face it, it's not that you can't do it... it's just that you don't know how to do it. In the 5 years I've beenin this sport, I've had my opportunities against 9 other men, 7 other men and 29 other men. 2 out of 3 times, I'll come out on top. You know why? It's because I know how to handle myself and because I don't *SUCK* like you do!
[Dice ones again gets up and takes a puff on his cigar. He then grabs a a pile of clothes on the floor and places them on top of the closed hamper. Dice then turns around and takes off his boxer shorts to reveal to the camera his bare, hairy ass. Dice then reaches back and grabs the pile of clothes and starts to get changed in front of the camera. He puts on a pair of boxer shorts, a pair of jeans and his infamous "Lucky 7" Club
T-shirt. Dice then turns around and looks at the camera again and sits on the floor.]
"Lucky Man" Dylan Dice: Tommy, enough about you. This is supposed to be all about me. You see, slowly and slowly the MVW is going to realize that
I am going to be their new franchise player here. I just proved it by showing that I care for the MVW by not leaving them for other organizations. I'm supposed to be the "good-guy" here but it seems like that won't be pulled off very easily. I'm gonna have to earn the respect from a lot of people here in the MVW. First and foremost, I'm gonna have to earn the respect of the whole front office. Everybody knows that's where the real brains are. Then, I'm gonna have to earn the respect of the other wrestlers. Why? Frankly, why would the MVW want their new franchise player and their new *star* to be hurt? I go down the tubes, then there goes the MVW ratings! Then, lastly I will have to earn the respect of the fans. If the fans like me and the fans adore me, then what more could I ask for? I'll be selling t-shirts and posters like there is no tommorrow,
I'll be doing TV commercials and getting paid some huge amounts in endorsements. I know I can make the fans love me and I'll just prove it next time I see them!
[Dice pulls on the chain on the lightbulb and the lights go out. He then reaches in his pocket and pulls out a zippo and lights it to illuminate some of the room. Dice then lights up yet another cigar and takes a puff on it, creating circles of smoke.]
"Lucky Man" Dylan Dice: I know some of you may be wondering just where I am. If you haven't figured it out, I'm in my closet. Yes, it is a strange place to do an interview for the MVW, right? Well, I see that already I have an open challenge coming my way. I feel sorry for the person who wants to step in the ring with me, I'll tell you that. I'm not the type of person who is going to worry about who I wrestling because I already know that I can beat anybody on any given day. It's nice to know in advance who it is, but if the MVW wants to keep it a secret, then so be it. As of this taping, I don't know who I am wrestling. Yes, I did say taping? Why is this interview taped? Let's just say the MVW refused to get some live footage because they were afraid of what might happen or what I might do on live television. But, that's not gonna stop me against my open challenge. I'm gonna have a huge surprise for all of you next week. It's all gonna be a part of my master plan! I bet you're all gonna try to sit and figure out what exactly it is. Well, here's a hint.. don't try yo figure out anything cause it's sitting right in front of your face! I have to wonder and think out loud just what I am doing in this closet. Is there a certain meaning to it? I can already picture in my head all the wrestlers and all the fans calling me a "closet case." It just goes to show you what sick and twisted minds we have in the world today. But, let me make this clear. The only ways I will "come out of the closet" is if I am either scheduled to wrestle or when I feel the time is right. Right now, this is my homw away from home and if you don't like it, well.. you know what you can do. Now, GET THE FUCK OUT OF MY FACE!!!!!!
[Dice takes the camera and throws it against the wall of the closet smashing it into pieces.]
