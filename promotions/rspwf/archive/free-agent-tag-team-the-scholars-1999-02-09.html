[Free Agent Tag Team] The Scholars

9 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fed Heds: With the closure of OWF and SWC, and the ending of the KNI tournament, I now have time to compete in another federation.
I am in 2 currently, and I am looking for another one for my resurrected tag team - The Scholars. I wanted to create a tag team because the shortage of tag teams around the e-wrestling scene has gotten out of hand, and I felt as if I had to do something.
I chose this gimmick because well... I guess I could play it pretty well. :) It would allow me to be me and still have fun with it at the same time. I think these 2 guys are pretty cool, but I'm still pretty unexperienced with handling tag teams, so if any of you experienced tag teams have any comments, criticism, or anything of that nature, please let me know immediately.
And of course, if you have any propositions for The Scholars, here are the requirements: = must be a booked federation = must have a decent tag team division = must have a dedicated president = must give me the tag team title upon entrance. Ok, the fourth one is optional, but I won't mind if you give me that. Well, I'm coming up with the statistics later on into the evening, so here is their character development role play. I'd love any and all comments, gentlemen.
Kind regards, [name withheld] ex-president, OWF (email to - [detail withheld] ) --------------- [ Scene - Inside the office of 1/2 of the brand-new tag team, The
Scholars. Carefully and busily writing some sort of research paper at his desk is none other than "Philosophical" Henry Dexter. Clad in a light blue dress shirt, a brown pipe from the corner of his mouth, and golden wire-framed glasses, Dexter continues to frantically write down on his paper.
And from the right side of the camera enters "Sharp" Stephen Douglas, donned in a blue sweater-vest, with a white dress shirt underneath that. Stephen comes up to Henry and he taps on his desk. Annoyed,
Henry slams his pen down on the desk, and simply asks, 'What?' One point to the camera by Douglas, and Dexter knows what he's talking about. Dexter puts the cap on the pen, and he sticks it in his breast pocket, and leans back in his chair, looking to get a bit relaxed. ]
HD: Look at what we have here, Stephen. We have minimum-wage-earning cameramen to get words of wisdom from The Scholars.
SD: [ laughing ] Yes. This is quite amusing. How exactly could you deprecate yourself by becoming a cameraman? You have to take orders from the people on camera, and you have to hold up that heavy lump of metal up on your shoulder.
HD: [ laughing ] He'd probably end up getting shoulder-hand syndrome. And we do not need any shoulder inflammation on the set. [Henry takes a pull of his pipe.] HD: So . . . I am gathering that you ignoramuses were sent here to gather comments from The Scholars on their voyage into the realm of professional wrestling. [ smiling ] I seriously doubt that this odyssey known to the world as professional wrestling is anything to brag about. Stephen and I have only one goal to accomplish in the world of professional wrestling:
SD: To utterly eradicate the scum that resides in it. Take one look around the world of professional wrestling, and look what you get - advertisement and the promoting of sex, land mines and various types of explosive devices being deployed around rings during matches, the advertisement and the promoting of drugs, whether legal or illegal . . . And the most damaging of all . . .
HD: [ shivering ] This is the worst all right. SD: The advertisement and the promotion of stupidity. Basically, professional wrestling has become the breeding ground of stupidity, and stupidity only breeds ignorance; and ignorance only breeds unintelligent things - such as bad grammar, violence outside of the ring, and other threatening things to society.
HD: And it's our job to extirpate all of these negative influences. And how exactly will we go about extirpating? SD: Easy: by methodically, systematically, meticulously - HD: Don't forget "punctiliously." SD: . . . and punctiliously destroying our opponents. While Douglas and I might seem like whimps, you should not underestimate your
opponents - especially us. HD: Before deciding to enter the world of professional wrestling,
Douglas and I decided to look up on the technical side of wrestling - so we studied the voluminous number of various wrestling holds that exist out there that no one uses -
SD: And boy did we find quite a few of them. HD: Exactly. Many people scrutinize the usage of mat wrestling because of the boring factor. While the fans /do/ put the money in the pockets, it /does/ show great ignorance on their part.
SD: Mat wrestling is fascinating. No matter how big or small you are, you aren't immune to mat wrestling. If you're a luche- libre-styled wrestler, you can't wrestle well against the bigger opponents.
HD: If you're a brawler, you can't wrestle well against the bigger wrestlers. SD: But if you're a mat wrestler, you can apply the move anywhere, any time, and on anyone.
HD: And the world of professional wrestling will soon find that out, Stephen. SD: They sure will. Because when you deal with The Scholars... HD: School's year-round. [ Fade. ]
