Dread Saxon Sample RP [NWC/MCW] Please Read and Critique

23 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[:::The shot fades slowly in on a black and white replay of the first Coboslam. Dread Saxon, then just another of the many wrestlers who renounced their GWA membership, stands alone in the ring, after just tossing Dark Angel out. Saxon is in the throws of victory, but doesn't see the underhanded Vic Valari behind him. Vic charges, and Saxon goes flying.:::]
[:::The shot changes, showing Dread facing off with Dark Angel. Dread, going for the win, has Angel locked up in an early version of the Tortured Soul. Out of the back, Valari runs, and in slow motion, throws a handful of itching powder in Dread's face. Dread falls, and Angel gets the one-two-three:::]
[:::The voice of Mr. Big, Lawrence Monroe, cuts in as the shot slowly fades to black:::] "What happens to the strong when our words and deeds are insufficient to keep failure at bay?"
[:::Again, silence. Numerous images quickly flash by, showing Dread's numerous
NWC wins. His initial win over Vic Valari, to claim the Motor City title. His violent and underhanded loss to Mike Rogers. His victory over Steven Sczar to win his first Michigan National title. Steve Sire, as devious as ever, squeaking out a win to take the title from Dread. Images of Dread making Sire submit to the Saxon Justice, and then another slow motion view hitting Vic
Valari with the Eternal Damnation to win his second Michigan Nation title, slowly fading to black.:::]
[:::More shots flash by. Possibly the dirtiest of the dirty, Scorpatoa, enters
MCW, and begins his quest to ruin Saxon. Shots of Scorp stealing a win, and the
Michigan-National title, from Dread. The following battle over the title, ending in a draw just as Saxon hits Scorp with the Eternal Damnation and goes for the pin.:::]
[:::Again, the voice of the leader of the Board, self-appointed leaders of GCW, cuts through the darkness:::]
"It marks us ... sometimes indelibly." [:::Instead of fading, the shot switches, focused on the image of Dread Saxon using a lightning small package to pin Eric Rage and win the NWC World
Heavyweight title. Dread, standing in the middle of the ring, holding the title up as cameras flash throughout the arena. The shot fades, and more quick shots pass by. Saxon defending his title against Victor "Big Hurt" Manson, succeeding even after interference from Eddie Zam and Michael Lewd. Crimson Prophet, after beating Dread, tossing a fireball in his face, getting him disqualified.
Shooting Star and Lee "The Snake" Sabo being almost easily pinned in another title defense. Dread, standing in Cobo Arena, defeating longtime nemesis Dark
Angel. Then a tough-fought victory over Zamza to again retain the title. Then, the shot fades.:::]
"Witness the great Dread Saxon, now living in embers of his former power." [:::The shot fades in, this time in color, showing "The Moronic" Josh Lee struggling to overcome Dread. Then, a low shoulderblock takes out Dread's knee.
Moments later, Josh Lee did the unthinkable. He defeated Dread Saxon. Dread lays in the ring, slapping the canvas as the trainers check out his knee. The shot again fades out.:::]
"The entire Council can smell the carrion stench of failure on the man." [:::New shots flash by, shots of Dread beating Steve Sire for his fourth Michigan National title. Facing Lee Clark and Toronoff, both decisions coming down as controversial. Dread and Toronoff at Renaissance 98, with Toronoff going wild after Cygnus X-1, later revealed to be Zamza, torpedoed Dread's now bad knee, taking him out of the match.:::]
"It draws jackals eager to tear away the remnants of his glory...." [:::Another switch, to the press conference prior to Coboslam XI. The audio cuts in, as James Hill stands to speak:::]
"Therefore, Dread Saxon is hereby stripped of the Michigan National Title {the crowd gasps} and he is suspended!"
[:::Chaos ensues, as Dread leaps up to grab Hill's throat. Held back, Dread collapses, only to get up, and then collapse again.:::]
" ... Men who would push themselves to the breaking point for the honor of saying, "I beat Dread Saxon."
[:::Static filled images replace the past shots, grainy scenes showing a new, changed, much darker Dread Saxon, in the company of James Naismith, assaulting
Tony Pride and other members of the Upper Echelon at Cobo XI. Other shots follow, showing Dread beating Dark Angel and Josh Lee during a match in the
MWA. Scenes of Dread and Naismith laying out an already beaten team in LSW. And then, nothing.:::]
[:::Sounds of Dread's music echoes through the video darkness. The wind blowing, growing stronger, and then the driving baseline of Golgotha Tenement
Blues. A single, still image of Dread after his victory over Dark Angel stands in the middle of the screen, basking in the glory of the NWC title. The goal that, from day one, he had set his sights on. The thing that made him whole.
And, with the sound of a record being scratched, the sound of squealing brakes, the image disappears.:::]
[:::Replacing this is a view of the recent Dread Saxon. A shaved and slightly stubbled head where long black hair once was. A shoddy and unkempt beard where once a well manicured goatee and mustache once stood proudly. Grey-white face paint, with black outlining bone structure, paint the picture of a man who once fought for something, who wore his spirit in crafty and careful archaic designs on his face. Strong, muscular arms rest across a broad chest, both larger than the sleek limbs and defined chest that once identified Saxon. The only unchanged feature is the family crest on his right forearm. No music plays, and only the faint sound of wind can be heard over the hum of the screen.:::]
[:::As the rest, this image too fades, leaving only a silhouette where Dread's figure once was. A lone voice cuts out of the darkness, and echoes eerily in the nothingness.:::]
M. L. Curly: "What happened to Dread Saxon? Where is our Franchise?"
