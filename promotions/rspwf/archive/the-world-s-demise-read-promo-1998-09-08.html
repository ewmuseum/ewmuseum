Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera opens up to blackness, then a voice over starts to speak.] Some people say that they would sell their souls to the devil... This man meant what he said... For what you may ask... The sad thing about it is the answer... He traided it....._FOR THE HELL OF IT_... [The camera fades to a shot of a white man with his face painted white. His eyes are hidden behind a pair of black tinted sunglasses. His hair is died red.
He has a goatie which is painted black. He has a black shirt with a hood. The camera angle stops at the neck of the shirt. The man starts to speak...]
You do not know who I am... But my intentions will be known... I will show all of you what the meaning of pain is... Along with the men who worship Lucifer... WE WILL TAKE OVER... [The man takes his sunglasses off revealing his eyes. The eyes are red and black. Almost like snake eyes. The man looks fierce into the camera. The camera fades to black...]
---------------------------------------------------------- -Chris- Handler of "Sadistic" Shane Alexander [SWP, LAW, SAW] I may be a man of words, but my actions will shut you up. Handler of "The Demon" [None Yet.] It may sound gay, but hey...It works.
