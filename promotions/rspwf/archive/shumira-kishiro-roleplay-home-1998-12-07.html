Shumira Kishiro Roleplay; Home?

7 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The white lights go down and the sparkley curtains come apart. The ring is dismantled and the crew is packed up an on their way. "See ya, Huck. Good luck." Getting laid-off is never easy.
And so she sits on the curb outside the GDWA arena. Sorry..the former
GDWA arena, for you see, the glorious all-women's federation has gone on
"hiatus" and our little hero Shumira is forced to go without a job. Not even a screwjob. She just sits on the curb with her ring-gear on, her street clothes still stashed inside her suitcase. The black suitcase with stickers of her favorite bands; "Souxie and the Banshees" "Placebo" "Nick
Cave and the Bad Seeds" amoung others. Such a sorry sight she is; her green hair still all mussed up, her black mascara running just a little.
Absently, she tugs at her black and purple stripey-socks and sighs. "So where do we go now? I've spent the last two years living from hotel room to hotel room. The ring was my home. The GDWA was my home. And now
I've been kicked out." Shumira looks down at the severed head of a small plush stuffed-animal cat. "Any ideas Muffins? You always have good ideas."
"Well, we could always stay with Mishakal." the little cat-head says. "Hmm..nah. She's a bitch. Plus her cooking is terrible." Shumira says. "Okay..how about Neko?" Muffins offers. "HELL no. She dumped me like a bad habit. The last thing I wanna do is go crawing to that one-eyed little slut for help." Shumira spits bitterly.
"Well, I don't know. Why are you asking ME? I'm just a cat-head!" Muffins shouts. "Sowwwy." Shumira pouts. "I guess we could always go find some other federation to work in..."
"WWF??" Muffins asks enthusiastically. "And job to Sable's breasts? Nah. That's no fun." Shumira says, scratching her chin. "What about ECW? WCW? New Japan?" Muffins offers. "No, no, no. I mean a real fed. A good Fed. Not some cartooney fly-by-night thang." Shumira sighs and stand up, gathering her suitcase and Muffins' head. "I guess we should start looking around. We can't stay here. They'll tow my suitcase away."
Muffins floats alongside Shumira as she walks down the lonely street. "If worse comes to worst we can always talk to the Godfather..."
"You really think I'd make a good Ho? Even with my green funky hair? Wow..just imagine..Shumira the Ho....waitaminit." Shumira baffs Muffins on the head (it's not like Muffins has any other body parts). "You fink!"
