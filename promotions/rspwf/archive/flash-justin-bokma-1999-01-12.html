[Flash]Justin Bokma

12 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Blast it...flame it...do whatever...but I'm _NOT_ a free agent so don't give me any offers. On with the roleplay...
[The scene focuses in on some freshly made skid marks, a loud screeching noise is heard through the deserted parking lot, a red 1969 Mustang is seen speeding around carelessly inside the lot. It stops and peels off heading straight for the camera, it begins to go straight for the camera as it comes within three feet of the camera as it comes to an abrupt halt in front of the camera.]
JB - "Some think they can take on the world as if they were the best, some think they are the best. But I know for a fact, come Tuesday, things are changing for the better."
[The car is reved up a bit and then immediatly speeds off, the angle of the camera switches inside the car as Justin Bokma is driving the speedster.]
JB - "It seems a fella by the name of Scotty Richards thinks he can handle the likes of me, but for poor ol' Richards, I'm lookin' to turn some heads.
You see Scotty, you ain't up to the challenge to face me, and you sure as hell better know this ain't gonna be a fly by match, I am somebody.
I'm fully prepared and ready to go toe to toe with ya, and I fully expect to take you down...no questions asked.
Maybe you don't get it, or you don't know, but one or the other, your lesson be taught, cause I ain't got time for every lil' pis ant out there, so be glad
I'm
givin' you the time of the day. I mean, I could be out gettin' drunk with a couple of buddies...but I'm here, preparing mentally for the match. I hope you're doin'
the same Scotty, cause God knows you need ta." [Justin Bokma gets an evil grin on his face as he begins to speed toward a nearby building, he pushes the pedal down all the way as the car heads for the building, about 25 yards from the building, a cat begins to walk in front of the car making
Jusin slam on the brakes.] JB - "Lucky us, huh? No, lucky cat I put in those new brake pads the other day or else it would just be road kill ta me. But you know what? When I'm facin' Scotty,
I'm pulling out all stops...and I win again. End of the line for him.
Ya see, many think I ain't got what it takes, but for them few that do, and have confindence in me, I thank em'...but for the ones that don't, all I can say is ingnorace is bliss, and there are quite a few mighty happy people out there in this world. I'm guessin' Scotty Richiards is one of em'. But I know for sure, he ain't gonna be too happy when I plaster him to the mat and get the one two three that's for damn sure."
[The scene switches on the outside of the car as Justin Bokma revs the engine again and pulls somehting out of his lap, a cold Shiner Bock beer. He takes a sip and looks at the camera and smirks. The engine is revved about two more times before the camer is left behind in a cloud of smoggering smoke.]
