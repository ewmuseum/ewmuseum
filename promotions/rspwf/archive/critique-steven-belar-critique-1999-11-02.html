Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a flash I did for the UWC a few minutes ago, I want in honest critique,
I'm still new, and young (13 to be more specific) so don't go all out in bashing it.
========================================================================== [The scene fades in on a small room, the wall paper is fading, the furniture; a rocking chair and a small dingy couch seems to be slashed, the interior scattered across it.
The camera pans in on a barred up window, looking over the area outside. The sun shines down on shattered bottles, creating a rainbow effect. The area outside is not at all pleasant, people cursing to and fro, gun shots are heard in the background, while in the foreground people fall to there knee's in a pool of their own blood.]
[The camera quickly changes it setting into the small, shabby room, where a man slumped over his own thoughts can be seen. Rocking back and forth, in discontent. He slowly raises his head, a shadow cloaking his distruat appearance. Oddly enough the man chuckles to himself, beginning to speak;]
Belar: "A childhood memory, one involving drugs, and suffering. You see when I was a child I because of my incapable Mother, was forced to raise to children on my _OWN_, and miraculously I survived, and received a decent education."
[Steven Belar licks his lips, as he cracks his neck] Belar: "But while all of this was going down; a man, no not a man, a childish boy, was basking in his money. This man I loathe, I despise. He is somebody who is unworthy of the title, he has slung around his shoulder. Yes,
Kerry Powell, you will soon enough be kicked off your high pedestal, by a man inferior as some would say to you. Soon enough, Powell, you will reluctantly hand over your World Title to _ME_"
[Steven Belar chuckles lightly to himself] Belar: "And to prove how worthy I am, I will show off my newly found hard-core abilities, brought out by the people who dubbed me insane, I will dismantle Ian Aloxson. Beating him within in inch of his life, and then I'll beat him an inch further! I will beat this bag of bones with the name Ian
Aloxson, from pillar to post, humiliating him in front of 20,000 people."
[Belar sighs to himself shaking his head] Belar: "Ian, this match is nothing personal you see, it's just your in the wrong place at the wrong time; unfortunately I must do this, to prove a point to a man who believes I'm not ready for the big titles, but to make it easier on yourself Ian, don't fight back, that way the kill will be swift, and less painful."
[Belar gets up from his chair, walking onward, opens the door, and sets down a small corridor.]
