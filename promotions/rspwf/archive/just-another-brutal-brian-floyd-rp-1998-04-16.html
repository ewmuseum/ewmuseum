Just another "Brutal" Brian Floyd" RP

16 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene opens at the front door of "Brutal" Brian Floyd's apartment in
Queens, New York. The camera man opens the door ( which is rather rude of him due to not knocking ) and films the front room. Here you see the television left on and trash all over the floor. The camera man then walks to the kichenette, where you see Brian talking on the phone. He has on a plain white t-shirt, cut-off shorts, and brown Doc Marten's sandals. His hair is curly, and as usual, hardly combed. The camera man walks over to the table Brian is sitting at and takes a seat opposite of him. Brian seems to be busy talking about something, and the closeness of the camera allows you to listen in. )
BF: You know I only accept people who I think are worthy, right? ( Brian pauses as the guy on the other end of the phone talks. ) BF: Well, ok. An ASW camera man is here so call me back in a few minutes. Talk to ya later. ( Brian pushes the 'off ' on his cordless phone and sets it down on the table. He looks at the camera, smiles, and begins to speak. ) BF: Well, don't I have alot to talk about? There's the new reformation of The
Cult, my busy wrestling schedule, and the two men who have the honor of wrestling me. Where should I begin?
( Brian gets up and walks over to the fridge. He reaches in and pulls out a
Coors Light. He grabs another one and holds it out to the camera man. )
BF: You want one? ( Brian waits for an answer...and then shrugs his as with a "suit yourself" gesture. He closes the fridge and sits back down. He then opens up the can and takes a big drink. He swallows, lets out a satisfied breath, and continues speaking. )
BF: I guess I'll start from square one, which happens to be my upcoming matches. Since I complained it seems that I got my wish plus one. I only asked for one match, and I wind up getting two, which doesn't bother me at the least. I grew quite sick of sitting in my recliner watching a bunch of mediocre wrestlers steal my spotlight on ASW Fever. Each week I would look at the schedule and find out my name wasn't on the list. The first couple of weeks weren't that bad, because I figured that Samantha was just giving me a well deserved break. But then the next two schedules come in and I'm STILL not on the list. Now THAT got me mad. Here I am, one of the best wrestlers in the
ASW, sitting at home on a Monday night when I should be traveling across
America competing on Fever. It just didn't make sense. Soooo, as all of you know, I had to confront Sam personally on last Monday's Fever. I drove my '98
Corvette to the arena, walked in the back door, and marched my butt to the ring. And you know...it's kinda weird how they timed my music just perfectly as I made my appearance. ( Brian smiles ) But anyways, now that I'm back in action, be prepared for a whole new "Brutal" Brian Floyd. Not only am I going to beat my opponents senseless, but I'm going to show off most...if not
ALL...of my wrestling skill. That's right. You're going to see power moves, high flying moves, technical moves, the whole nine yards. I'm going to prove to the ASW committee, to Sam, and to all of you viewers out there why BRIAN
FLOYD is an up and coming star in the ASW.
( Brian pauses as he takes another drink. He then offers a drink to the camera man, who once again declines. Brian shrugs his shoulders like he did before. )
BF: And my two opponents this week happen to be The Legend and Pancho, a couple of new guys here in the ASW. Now, I don't know either personally, nor do I care to. I'm not on speaking terms with much of the other wrestlers, if you know what I mean. This Legend guy seems to have some sort of chip on his shoulder, and you know "Brutal" Brian Floyd is gonna knock it off. He might
_think_ he's a legend, but in reality he's nothing more than a stepping stone for "The Brutal One" in his quest to get the TV Title. Legend, you might be good, but I can assure you that you're not NEAR as talented as ME.
( Brian pauses again as he puts his beer up to his lips and takes a long drink. He finishes it off and crushes it in his hand. ) BF: And as for Pancho, all I know about you is that you're 7 foot tall and rather large. ( Brian gives the camera a "so what?" look ) To tell you the truth, I don't care how big you are. Size doesn't matter, hell, just ask your wife. ( Smiles ) Sorry, I shouldn't have said that. The size of your genitalia doesn't really matter in wrestling. But the fact of the matter is that I'm gonna show you what kind of a damage a 6'5, 245 pounder can do. You might tower over me in the ring, but I WILL chop you down bit by bit. I'm pretty damn sure that I'll be able to get you up in "The Brutalizer" and drop you down flat on your skull. After that, Pancho, you'll be waking up to the aroma of smelling salts and a room full of doctors that were luckily on hand. And after you regain enough consciousness to know what happened, you'll be kicking yourself in the ass for losing to a guy smaller than you. ( Smiles ) And I'm
NOT talking about genitalias this time!
( Brian's cordless phone suddenly rings and he picks it up. ) BF: Hello? ( Brian stares at the camera as he listens to the person on the other end. After the person has spoken he smiles and speaks to the man. ) BF: Hold on for a second, will ya? ( Brian puts his hand over the receiver and speaks to the camera man. ) BF: Well, your work here is done. I have an important call and frankly I don't have much more to say. So go bug somebody else like Sterling Mitchell or
Darren Shaw.
( Brian then turns his chair to where his back faces the camera. He puts the phone up to his ear and begins to talk to the man on the other end. The camera man stands up and films Brian for a few seconds more, and then turns the camera off, making the screen go to black. )
