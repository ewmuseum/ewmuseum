{Critique} Johnny Blaze...1st RSPWF RP

23 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens to total black. Slowly, pictures of four EHW wrestlers materialize. It's the four men competing for the Extreme Title this Monday! The
Nazi National Anthem begins to play in the background, as the picture of
Heinrich Himmler grows in size and the other three fade off the screen. The scene cuts to a clip of Heinrich Himmler's interview; the part that addresses
Johnny Blaze. We return to the picture of Himmler, the Nazi Nation Anthem still playing in the background, as a voice begins to speak…)
Voice: Heinrich Himmler…Heinrich Himmler, you sound like a very scared man. You resorted to insulting my name in an attempt to cover up the fear that you are experiencing, and then later refereed to me as, "Whatever your name is." It sounds like you are a confused individual, maybe going through an identity crisis after being turned on by your best friend. But your mental state is not my problem, you alone are my problem. You, Heinrich Himmler, the EHW Number One of All-time, the German, the Nazi, you are my problem. And it is you that I will destroy.
You also asked me many, many questions…More actions of a scared man. You asked very basic questions…Where am I from? What is my motivation? Who do I think I am? Well Heinrich, I am going to answer your questions. I am from Queens, New
York, and it is from my background that my motivation lies. Most people in my neighborhood were just statistics, and I grew up thinking that if I didn't rap or sell crack, then I too would become a statistic. But I found a way out, a way to make, in wrestling. That's why I started wrestling, but very soon, I realized that I actually liked it… That wrestling was more to me than just a way out. It wasn't about riding in a limousine, or getting a big fat paycheck at the end of the month, it was about wrestling. It was about winning…About wanting to be the best. And believe me Himmler, I will be the best.
You then asked, "Who do I think I am?" obviously looking for some explanation as to why I chose to attack you. Well here it is: The EHW proclaimed you the best wrestler they've ever had, and I wanted to see what you were all about.
And as far as I'm concerned, you're not shit. I know a dozen brothers back in
Queens who could wipe the floor with you. So maybe you think that just because some corporate brass proclaimed you EHW Number One of All-time, you are untouchable? Nobody is untouchable when I'm around.
So Himmler, you need to stop worrying about your women, stop hailing Hitler, and get your fat ass into the gym. Because Chu-Dog is making this out to be a spectacular match, and I'd hate to disappoint him by kicking your ass in five minutes.
(The camera cuts to a shot of a hooded man jogging in a park. His identity is unable to be determined from this shot, but as he speaks, we know it is the voice that spoke of Heinrich Himmler…)
Voice: I expect you all want to know who I am; For me to reveal my identity. Didn't I drop enough clues when I spoke of Himmler? (The figure drops his hood and…It's Johnny Blaze! Of course, Queens, NY, attacking Himmler…but the voice?!)
Johnny Blaze: What?! Y'all didn't think I could talk white? (The camera cuts back to the picture of Heinrich Himmler, which fades out with the Nazi National Anthem, as the picture of Brian Goldstein spirals into view and Johnny begins speaking again…)
Johnny Blaze: Aiight, I'm done talkin white. Now Brian, first and foremost, what the fuck are you talkin about? Former champion? Tons of respect?
Reputation in other feds? Man, all that's bullshit! I ain't been a former champion, I never wrestled a match in my life! That's why all these punks like
Himmler be dissin me. And respect? Man, you tryin to get off easy. You think if you say you got tons of respect for me that I'ma lay off you?
(Johnny laughs.) Johnny Blaze: Keep your fingers crossed, that's all I got to say! Now, you talkin about how my first match is a title match, and you askin why. Well, I'ma tell you why! Cause I'm that damn good! Man, I ain't like the rest of these cracker ass newcomers who piss away their first few weeks in the EHW; No! First thing I did when I got here was to attack the EHW Number One of All-time, and make a name for myself. But apparently, I didn't do a good enough job, cause
I'm still, "What's his name?" to some of y'all. So I'ma just have to win the
Extreme Title, THEN all y'all know who Johnny Blaze is! And bro, just wonderin, why do you care if my first match is for your title? I mean, if you're half the twinkie you make yourself up to be, a newcomer like myself should be no problem, right? I mean, I'm not a threat to your title? Am I? Why don't you answer me on that one. Yo, and all this shit about, "My reputation in past feds." Man, you trippin! Like I said, I ain't never wrestled a match before in my life! So Brian, you gotta stop actin like my fuckin babysitter, man! Talkin about how I'm gonna wish I didn't challenge the big shots; Man, stop tellin me what to do! I grew up in the ghet-to, I know how to handle myself! Just wait til I plant yo ass with a Ghetto Fabulous, then you'll know who yo daddy is!
Man, the bottom line with you Goldstein, is you need to get outta my business, and worry about protecting your strap. Cause I don't give a fuck if all y'all are in the EHW Top Fifty of All-time…That'll just make y'all look bad when I beat you. Because I'm Johnny Blaze, and I'm blazin!
(The picture of Goldstein fades to nothing, as a picture of Crash materializes and "This Means War!" by Busta Rhymes begins playing faintly in the background, as Johnny speaks over the music…)
Johnny Blaze: Ya know, Crash seems to think that I'm his friend. Maybe he didn't catch me the last time, so I'ma tell him again: You're gonna be my bitch, man! I ain't down with you're little Future of Wrestling shit…Hells no!
Man, fuck y'all! "Seeing if I'm the perfect fit…." Man, that's bullshit! I attacked Himmler not to save yo ass, but to make a name for myself! So stop thinkin we got a little friendship thing going on, cause we don't! Man, you need to check yo self, cause it seems like you countin me out. "We don't expect
Blaze to win." Yo Caz, are you fighting in this match?! I don't think so bitch!
So why don't you get you pimply ass outta of my business! I'ma kick Crash's ass, and if you decide to get involved, if there's anything left of you after
Mike Madison, then I'ma kick yo ass too! Cause I really don't give a damn if you expect me to win or not. I'ma win, and I don't care what y'all say. C'mon, count me out! It's only gonna hurt ya. Cause the more you count me out, the more you say, "Blaze can't win it," the easier it gets for me. So all y'all get ready, cause I'ma educate y'all to the ways of the street!
(The scene cuts back to the park, when Johnny Blaze is still jogging, this time the hood of his black hooded sweatshirt down. He is also wearing black Adidas windpants. He turns his face to the camera, and begins to speak…)
Johnny Blaze: So that's it from all of them crackers, or is it? Nope, Crash and
Caz decided to further their punishment by talkin more smack! Man, you see this park? This is Central Park and right now the sun's just rising, cause it's five a.m., and all y'all wouldn't stand a chance here! There niggas standing behind every tree waiting to jump out at a brotha, except they don't jump out at me.
Why's that? Cause they know I'ma kick their ass! All y'all should take a lesson from these punks and back off, cause the more shit you talk, the angrier Johnny
Blaze gets. And as a man who's already blazin, that makes me extra dangerous.
Anyway, back to those two crackers, Caz and Crash… (The scene cuts back to the photo of Crash, and Johnny Blaze speaks again…) Johnny Blaze: Aiight, so now you're the EHW Number One of All-time, huh Crash? Man, fuck this ranking shit! Do y'all get a piece of gum if you're ranked or sumptin? Well Johnny Blaze don't give a damn 'bout this rankin shit. I mean, all y'all are supposedly the best in the federation, but y'all are scared of me, the new guy, the unranked guy, THE GUY THAT'S NEVER WRESTLED! Huh? Sure, y'all be acting all tough and be like, "We ain't scared of Blaze ," but y'all are scared…I know it. And I'm kinda scared too…Scared of what I'ma do to y'all!
So ya know what Crash and Caz, I don't care if you don't think I got talent! I don't care if you don't want me in the Future of Wrestling, cause I don't want it…I walk alone. So why don't y'all stop actin like I want to be your friend, and accept that I'ma make you my bitch! Oh yo, and then check this shit out:
You say you don't got a problem with me! (Johnny starts cracking up.) Johnny Blaze: Well that's great, cracker, cause I got a problem with you! You're just another fudgepacker standing between me and the Extreme Title. We ain't cool! Man, stop trying to get down with Johnny Blaze, cause Johnny Blaze ain't down wit no one, 'cept his boys. And you AIN'T one of my boys! So man, do the smart thing and stay outta my way…I'ma tell you now: Don't bother extending a hand, because all you're gonna get back is a foot up the ass…Ghetto style, punk!
(The scene cuts back to the park, where Johnny is seated on a park bench drinking bottled water. He speaks…)
Johnny Blaze: Yo, and then Goldstein came out and ran his fat mouth again! Man, y'all white boys don't know when to shut up, do ya? Ya think the outcome's gonna change by how much you talk, huh? Man, Blaze is gonna kick ya ass and there ain't nuttin you can do to stop me. I want that Extreme Title, and I'ma have it. No, I ain't no spoilt brat…How many spoilt kids you know from Queens, huh? Man, I ain't ever had shit! So y'all better watch your back and protect ya neck, cause I'ma whoop y'all.
(The scene cuts back to the shot of all four competitors. The picture of Johnny
Blaze, who is giving his million-dollar smile, knocks the other three photos off the screen, as "Hate Me Now," by Nas blasts. Johnny Blaze yells over it…)
Johnny Blaze: Man, who is that good looking bastard?! Oh yeah, that's me! Do y'all here that…
("Hate Me Now," has reached it's chorus, which has Puff Daddy rapping, "You can hate me now…But I won't stop…Cause I can't stop now…You can hate me now.")
Johnny Blaze: Y'all hear that? You wanna hate me, then hate me! C'mon bitch, I dare ya! Hate me! Hate me now! C'mon! I don't care if you're the Champion! Hate me! I don't give a fuck if you wear them faggot Swastikas! Hate me! You really think I care if you're the Future of Wrestling? Hate me! Hate me!
("Hate Me Now," by Nas fades out, as the picture of Johnny Blaze resumes it's normal size, and the picutres of Heinrich Himmler, Brian Goldstein and Crash all reappear on the screen. Suddenly, red materializes over the black, and in a matter of seconds, it has taken all the black, and the pictures of Himmler,
Goldstein and Crash, under it. All that is left is the picture of Johnny Blaze, giving his million-dollar smile, in the center of the blood red screen. Letter by letter, the words, "Johnny Blaze, EHW Extreme Champion," appear under the picture of Johnny. We get one last shot of Johnny and the writing, before the scene fades to black.)
