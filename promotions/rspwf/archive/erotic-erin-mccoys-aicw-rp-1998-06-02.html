[Scene: Cameras show the crowded waiting room area of an office building. We see about 20 reporters and photographers screaming at a young secertary. She is wearing a black business suit with white stripes and a phone head sets. She stands up and tries to calm everyone down]
Secertary: [yelling over the crowd] WILL EVERYONE PLEASE CALM DOWN! Reporter: You are her secertary! Get her out here! She hasn't spoken to us in weeks and then she comes out with The Stone at the PPV? She can't ignore us!
Reporter: Yeah! Call her out here! We've got to work too ya know! Secertary: Miss McCoys is VERY busy! When she wants to give a statement, believe me she will! [talking into headset] Yes? Yes they are. ALL of them. Ummm.. I can check, hold please. Is [name withheld] from the Wrestling
Clothesline here?
[A dark haired man wearing a pair of jeans and a New York Yankees Jersey steps up from the crowd] [name withheld]: Yo! Right here. What's up? Secertary: Miss McCoys will speak to you.. and ONLY you! Come with me. [The rest of the reporters and photographers are going crazy as the Secertary leads Michael into the office. The camera pans into the office where we see a beautifully decorated office over looking the city of Atlanta. We see
Erin McCoys standing by the huge window looking down. She is talking on a cell phone. Erin is wearing a khaki cargo skirt with two black stripes down each side, a white tank top with a black dragon on it and a black velour jacket. Erin, still talking on the phone, motions for him to take a seat]
Erin McCoys: [talking into phone] Mr Turner.. [smiles] Fine.. Ted. Ted, you can't expect everyone to want to sell their companies to you. I know, I know that you want to own everything but it's just not going to happen. You know I'm here if you want advice anytime you need it. [smirks]. Yes.. Yes. I love you too. Give Jane my love. Bye. [clicks phone off] Jenny, you can leave me and Mr [name withheld] alone. I'm sure he won't bite.
[The secertary leaves as Erin takes a seat in front of Michael on her desk she crosses her legs in front of him and you can see Michael gulp and pull at his collar]
Erin McCoys: So.. you just won the lottery ticket of a life time. All those people out there would give their first born to be where you are right now. So.. this is your interview.. The ball is in your court.
[name withheld]: Well I'll ask the obvious question.. What is the alliance between you and The STONE? The whole world saw you in his corner against
Johnny Smith at the AICW PPV, what's the deal? Erin McCoys: You wanna know the alliance? Well there is no alliance. I saw that Chandler was wrestling Smith and I saw a prime opportunity to once again scew with the Self Proclaimed Golden Boy.
[name withheld]: So I take it that there is still some ill will between you two? Erin McCoys: [smirking] That right there is an understatement. Worst thing in the world is an intellgent woman scorned because not only does she have a reason to be evil but also the means to spin her webs. Ever wonder why
Johnny talks about me in his interviews even when I don't address him? Simple. He NEEDS me. He needs to hold onto what he was when he was with me. He needs to hold onto his once greatness. The fact of the matter is, Johnny Smith was at his best when he was with me. No other manager or alliance he's ever been with did for him what I did. You know, if he would quit talking about it for a while, maybe everyone would forget but no, he couldn't have it. He has to remind the world that he was once with greatness. He has to remind himself that he once had the greatest thing and he let it slip through his fingers. Maybe I'm spiteful but what woman isn't? All I want is to see people get what they deserve. Like it or not, Johnny Smith will always be one jealous and bitter man. He is jealous of what I've become. He's jealous that I was able to move on and become one of the best managers of all time. He's bitter because I've taken so many men to world title status, a status he will never accomplish.
[name withheld]: At the next AICW PPV, it will be Johnny Smith against the Stone once again. Will you be in Chandler's corner?
Erin McCoys: Well let's see if there will even be a match. I'm not going to give you a definte yes or a definte no. Whatever happens, happens.
[name withheld]: What do you mean "If there will even be a match?" Erin McCoys: I mean just what I say. I know Johnny Smith better then anyone else in this sport and I'm saying right now, don't hold your breath on that match.
[name withheld]: Okay.. Moving on. What about the recent comments from Kyle Bane that all you do is sit there and be pretty and are making multi million dollar paychecks?
Erin McCoys: Bane has a lot in common with Johnny Smith in terms of bitterness. Bane is a great athlete and maybe he does deserve to be paid more then he's being paid but that's no reasons to throw sour grapes towards me. Maybe if Kyle gets someone alittle bit better then that Christina O girl to handle his business afraids, he could be making the money I make. But again, we are dealing with amatuers in a world of tough competitors. Only the strong survive here. If you don't have a strong business mind, everyone else out there will give you the run around. I don't like being screwed by big tough business men who think they are Gods of the Unvierse. That's what seperates me from the likes of Christina O and Sensuous Samantha. They are so used to having men do everything for them that they can't stand on their own two feet. If you want to survive in this world, you have to be different, you have to be special, you have to a knack. In wrestling, people can only take so much of the air head bottle blondes we see. If you want someone who's going to stand their, clap and look pretty, your barking up the wrong tree.
[name withheld]: You have called yourself a free agent, are you stating that only the serious need reply?
Erin McCoys: Damn Straight. I change myself for no one.. What you see is what you get. If you like what you see? Make me an offer and if it's good, I promise I won't lead you long.
[name withheld]: Any replys we should know about? Erin McCoys: I think that's for me to know and for the whole world to find out later don't you think? Your getting awfully nosy and I hate nosy reporters. You got your interview now why don't you show yourself to the door.
You've taken up enough of my time. [Erin uncrosses her leg and taps the chair with her foot to make it spin in the direction of the door. [name withheld] shurgs as he leaves the room. Cameras pan back to Erin who smirks as the camera fade out]
