[Critique] RVD Flash

15 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We open up a local gym in, where else, Battlecreek, Michigan. Now, if that isn't a dead giveaway for who's here well, then, just scroll down to the next flash ...
Alright, for those who are still present ... we see muscular, body building men and women lifting, squatting, pushing, pulling, and doing all sorts of things with various weight machines and free weights. There's a hint of Billy
Gunn/Chuck Palumbo vibe in the air, but hey, there's men in tight pants around, so it's unavoidable. Anyway, we mosey on over to the Vertical Leg Press
Machine ... racked up is over 1,000 lb. of 45 lb. weights. 11 or so on each side. And the first thing we hear is ...
"7 . . 8 . . 9 . . 10" The man, the myth, the legend ... more like the Television Champion - Rob Van
Dam. With legs that seem to stretch on for miles, Rob pushes the weight up for a final time before locking the safety on the machine and rolling out of the inverted seat. He grabs a small towel that is resting next to the machine and wipes a bit of sweat off his brow. He takes a swig out of his Poland Spring bottle and twists the cap back on, before dropping it to his side. Van Dam's clad in a pair of loosely fitting black sweats and a purple tank top sporting the Yin Yang symbol on his sternum. Swallowing his mouthful of water hard, he regains his normal breathing, and looks coldly into the camera.]
RVD: 'Stone Cold' Steve Austin ... It's been a long time comin', let me tell you. But Sunday night, January 20th, live on Pay-Per-View, it's finally going to happen. Your bald head just hasn't been able to stay out of my business ... I mean the whole point over who knocked out Vader in the first place doesn't even matter anymore.
Because Steve ... New Years Brawl is gonna mark a turning point. A turning point for Rob Van Dam, and a turning point for the XCW as a whole.
Last Man Standing ... a match that could easily end a man's career. And if I were you, which thank God I'm not, with that little ol' pencil neck of yours, and I had to face Rob Van Dam in just 5 days ... I wouldn't be the most comfortable of people right now.
You're probably too drunk or stupid to even realize anyway. What with you taking orders and playing gopher for the sorry bastard who signs our paychecks now ... you've degenerated yourself. You're a shadow of the Austin there once was. The Austin of 1996 who could captivate an audience with his sheer presence, who could have them hit the floor or the ceiling at his beckoning call ...
Now ... now you're lucky if your little [Rob mockingly "quotes".] RVD: "What" can carry you through a promo, nevermind a match. And you honestly, and I mean look in the mirror when you wake up honestly, think you can step into the squared circle and mix it up, with the best damn wrestler under contract in this business _today_? Maybe six years ago, hell maybe even
3 years ago ... but now? In the XCW? Steve I live, eat, and breathe this, this business, this lifestyle. It's not a joke, it's not a game, it's not fun.
It's _life_. It's _livelihood_. It's what makes Rob Van Dam tick inside. And that fire inside burns deep, it burns a hell of a lot brighter than any flame that could be overcast so quickly as to start taking orders from
_Chad_Costello_'s mangy ass. [Robby cracks his neck to the left and the right with a quick push from his right hand. He rocks it back and forth, loosening up a bit, before he continues.]
RVD: 'Stone Cold', this Sunday, the fun for you ends. The masquerading, the beer swilling, the sneak attacks. Because once you hit the ring with me, one-on-one, and there's no costume to hide behind, my back isn't turned, and
I'm ready ...
And believe me, I _will_ be ready. Once that happens, what exactly do you plan on doing? Kicking me? Stunning me? And having _that_ keep me down for a count of ten? Well, if that happens ... I'd rather not have the XCW
Television Title.
But that's not gonna happen. Redemption Steve. Redemption is the word, and redemption is the act that will happen this Sunday in front of a gargantuan amount of people. And they're going to love it ... of my name isn't
R ... V ... D. See you in a few days ... I've got work to do. [Van Dam turns, walking towards a pull up bar, as we ... Fade to black.] Just lemme know how bad I am : ) Peace suckas, - Hort
