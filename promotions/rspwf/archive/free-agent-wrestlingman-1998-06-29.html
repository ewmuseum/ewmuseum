[Free Agent] Wrestlingman

29 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello all you e-feders. My name is Wrestlingman. I am a free agent looking for a new fed. I have been Severel types of champion. I have a
Cruserweight title, world title, X-treme title, T.V. Title,
Intercontinental title, and European title. Here is what i am looking for in a new fed. 1. CLOSED FLASHING THROUGH E-mail- Where all flashes are sent out at once by a flash guy. No guestbook feds will be considered. 2. WRITTEN CARDS- Cards that are written out and well presented with play by play. No cards that are just results. I need full detail. 3. A WEB PAGE- This fed must have a webpage. That is so i can look back on cards for future refrences in Rps. It also must have an application on it that is On the page. Not an e-mail application. On e that has the text boxes on the page.
4. MUST BE ESTABLISHED- U must have been around at LEAST 10 months. I do not want to join a fed that is going to be closing down in a week. No just starting feds will be considered.
5. Last but Not LEast this is optional. I prefer that the prez's are not wrestlers. I will consider feds with the Pres having a wrestler, but not a good chance of me joining.
If you don't meet all of the requirements DON'T SEND ME A REPLY ASKING ME TO JOIN.... I can wrestle as either a Cruserweight or Heavyweight wrestler. It doesn't matter to me. Below isn't an rp. Right now the fed I am in isn't letting me wrestle. So i write a weekly show called LIVE IN NEW YORK.
This will give you the type of character i am...If you want me mail me at [detail withheld] .... Serious people only....PLEASE REPLY THROUGH E_MAIL!!!!!!! [name withheld]....aNd DoN't YoU fOrGeT iT... __________________BEGIN LIVE IN NEW YORK WITH WRESTLINGMAN_________ ("Shoot to Thrill" By Ac/Dc begins to play. The sceen fades into a New
York Yankees baseball game, then the statue of liberty, MSG. Then it shows a photo of Gordon Adair, and Flaming bob when they were on LINY.
The sceen goes to the front of a building. Up stairs, and into the Studio...) Voiceover: Ladies and Gentlemen, We found that on tuesday the station that caries UWA wrestling will be showing a dog show on tuesday night.
So we are comming at you a couple days EARLY....Without furthur ado, here is your host, Ken WAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAALLLLLLLLLLLLLACE......
(Ken comes out of the back. Wearing a UWA Issued blazer, and black pants.) Ken Wallace: Welcome to live in new york.....Here is everybodys favorite
Wrestler, WRRRRRRRRRRRRESTLINGMAN.
(Wrestlingman walks out of the back. The Wman 4:21 shirt is on. He has on black jeans and tan boots.)
Wrestlingman: It is good to be here. Now to find out who the sisy guest is.... Man from off stage; Your mystery guest is non other than.... "HOLLYWOOD" JOHNNY TAAAAAAMBO. Wman and Ken: Ohhh Sh!t. (Tambo comes out of the back. He just glares at Wrestlingman. He sits down in the chair...)
Ken: Here let me do most of the talking Wman...What is your opinion of
Christopher Maxwell as champion?
Tambo: Maxwell holding the gold is a prime example of how bad Natural Currency was. Not one member of NC held gold. Well, I don't know if you would have considered Nick Wright a member of NC. He won the belt before he joined them, then lost the Cruiserweight Title when he DID join up.
It must have been a curse. anyway, I digress. Maxwell is welcome to parade himself around as champion as long as he doesn't make the mistake of calling MY name. His next stop would be the Irish Republic.
Ken: What do you think of Mr. Adair letting Wman back into the UWA? Tambo: Double edged sword, pally. On the bad side...its letting others know that they can do what they want WHEN they want, and they'll only get a slap on the wrist. On the brighter side, if he comes back that is,
I've been waiting since January to get a little dose of revenge on him.
(looks over at WM) Welcome back, buddy. Wman: Tambo just shut up. We allll know that i can kick your a$$. We all know that I AM THE GREATEST...
Ken: Do you think that you will ever be in a match with the best person in the UWA? Tambo: Everytime I fight, I'm in a match with the best person in the UWA. ME! Ken: Not the asnwer i was look for but okay. Your still not the best like da MAN. Now will you give us some more thoughts on VVV?
Tambo: He's the Steve Carlton of wrestling. He was good once. REALLY good. Bu then he decided to stick around long past his expiration date and has gone sooooo stale. Retire, Viper. Quit making a fool out of yourself. Even the dinosaurs had the decency to die out.
Ken: Do you think we will ever see you in a title match with VVV? Tambo: I could have swore I heard something about no more title shots for him, but anything is possible. Once I have reclaimed my title, I'm open to any and all challenges.
Wman: All challenges? Well, if gordon lets me wrestle again which i am sure he will. I will be the first guy to challenge you. But this time it would be more than a blown off face...
Ken: Could you give us your thoughts on the actions of Billy Joe McCallister? Tambo: BJM is a prime example of whats wrong with wrestling today. I'll site the competitors for this one. WCW. Almost all the big names there have exclusive contracts which say things like, No Working on Wednesday.
Weekends off, and all the other stuff which I won't go into right now. Thats the kind of deal McCallister had, and he let it get to his head. He thought he was much better than he was. Too bad. I was looking forward to running him out of the UWA myself.
Wman: I will take it from here... Why Does your face looks so Ugly? Tambo: Get off the LSD and look again. You'll see a whole new world. Wman: Yeah i saw jens world lastnight... IS it true you have an explosive personality? Tambo: Is it true that I am the real reason you got yourself kicked out of the UWA? Wman: Hardly the reason... What is your favorite kind of firework? Tambo: The kind that malfunction and crash to the ground, burning acres of land. Wman: Have you ever been in an explosive situation? Tambo: Have you ever heard of tact? Or even beyond that, ever heard of getting to the point instead of beating around the bush like some little school kid? Hey, if you wanna find out what an explosion is...(stands up and looks down on WM) why don't you just get in my face and make the move, pally!
(Wrestlingman gets up and takes of the Wman 4:21 shirt. He walks over to tambo and gets in his face....)
Wman: Tambo, you don't want a piece of this. Your trash, you could never beat me. If i remember the only thing you can count as a win over me is when VVV cuffed me to the cage....
Tambo: Hey Pally, a win is a win, and i took your title...The face healed. To bad yours won't after i am threw with you...
Ken Wallace: Guys calm down. For Wrestlingman i am Ken Wallace, saying
If you wanna be on the show mail
[detail withheld] . Good Night everybody.... (Sceen fades into a UWA logo where you can still hear the voices of Wman and Tambo yelling at eachother...)
