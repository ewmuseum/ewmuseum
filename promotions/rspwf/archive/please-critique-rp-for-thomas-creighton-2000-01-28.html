[Please Critique] RP for Thomas Creighton

28 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi all, I've been lurking here for a while, and have decided to post a RP for my character Thomas Creighton for critique. I would appreciate some feedback. I hope you enjoy it, if not, tear it to pieces so I can get better.
[Scene: At a DWO Midwest house show, a match between two locals, trying to get a long term contract in the DWO, has just finished, as the local boys head back to the locker room, "Dirty Deeds" starts blaring over the speakers, and Thomas Creighton walks out from backstage, completely ignoring the two wannabes and forcing them to dodge him, as he heads to the ring, followed by a chorus of boos. Creighton rolls under the bottom rope, stands up, catches a microphone thrown at him with one hand, and begins to speak.]
Thomas Creighton: "Thanks a lot for your support, Davenport, Iowa! Tomorrow night, just a few scant hours away in Peoria, I will be getting my dirty hands once again on Virus, and his bitch Trevor Zumwalt!"
There is a mixture of cheers and boos from the crowd. Creighton: "What was that? Are there actually some people out there with some taste? Who know, completely and utterly, that Virus, and his
Influences, are not long for this world?" The same mixed reaction from the crowd. Creighton: "Well, fans, neighbors, countrymen, I have a guarantee for you. After tomorrow night, there will be NO SUCH THING as "Outside Influences!" Zumwalt will fold as quickly as Blair did, and all that will be left will be
Virus. You see, those three punks were around for one reason, to try to take over the Midwest as quickly as possible. And when they failed, they decided to move on to greener pastures. I don't really blame them, with the competition that is developing here. Sheik, X-Iled, my partner J-Dog, even that punk Cassidy. They can't hold a match to the elite of the Midwest."
The crowd cheers with the mention of each and every one of the wrestlers that Creighton mentions.
Creighton: "Sheik and X-Iled, I know that you are going to be in the finals of the tournament. Hell, I don't even know if you are going to have anyone in the ring with you in the first round! Maybe your opposition has decided that you two are too tough, and have withdrawn! Doomsday and Destruction?
For them maybe, but definately not for the Sheik and X-iled." There is soft laughter from the crowd. Creighton: "You liked that one, did you? How many here want to see Sheik and X-Iled against J-Dog and me tomorrow night?" The crowd explodes in cheers. Creighton: "And who do you think is going to win the finals?" The crowd starts chanting "Sheik, Sheik, Sheik!" Creighton: "What the *#$% are you thinking? Stop chanting that!!!!" The "Sheik" chant continues. Creighton: "Shut up! Shut up! Shut up! If you don't knock the $&#^ off right now, I'm going to cripple that camel $&%&ing @$$*&$# tomorrow night, so he can't defend against Karnage? Got it, you $&#*@ retards?"
The crowd's chant grows louder, and louder, and louder. Creighton: "You asked for it, #&$^%*$&%^#$#! Tomorrow night, I'm not going to go for the win, I'm going to take the Sheik out! The win will come, but only after the Arabic Sheik lays motionless, in the center of the ring.
bleeding profusely, unable to compete! No more Arabic Splashs, no more lottery wins! No more anything out of him, except pained moans from a hospital bed! You got that?"
Creighton rolls out of the ring, and is followed all the way with the "Sheik" chant.
