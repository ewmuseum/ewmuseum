{Free Agent} "FABULOUS" FREDDY FLOYD {Free Agent}

9 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
All Serious Federations, send applications/handbooks to this adress: [detail withheld] Thanks a lot ... Mike, handler of "Fabulous" Freddy Floyd. A Sample In-ring is below... [ . . . . Sequence Initialized . . . . ] [ Cue - "Good and Evil" ] [ The Lights inside of the arena fade completly to black, and a spotlight shines down onto the entrance ramp. ]
Announcer: Ladies and Gentlemen, about to make his way down the aisle at this time ... [ A red glow comes over the arena, and the fans all at once rise to there feet ] Announcer: He hails from Albany New York, and weighs in at 243lbs, I give you ... "Fabulous" Freddy Floyd!! [ The Video Wall Comes To Life ] " F A B U L O U S " F R E D D Y F L O Y D [ From behind the curtain, wearing navy blue bikini wrestling tights, with the word "Fabulous" scripted across the back, steps "Fabulous" Freddy Floyd ]
[ Floyd opens up his arms to take in a huge chorus of boos from the fans. He steps aside gestures toward the curitain. ]
[ Then out steps the beautiful, and vivatious, "Fantastic" Florence Floyd. Floyd wears a navy blue buisness suit, and a very low cut white blouse. She does a turn for fans, and then she and Freddy proceed to make there way down the aisle and into the ring ]
[ Once inside Freddy rips the microphone out of the ring announcers hand and begins to adress the audiance ]
"Fabulous" FF: "Well well well, for all of the idiots in attendance, the morons sitting in the back, and the millions of white trash sitting on there rented sofas watching world wide, get ready becasue the face of the Continental Wrestling Alliance is about to change forever, becasue you see, there has never been an athelete quite like me before in this buisness. There has never been one man who has been able to dominate like I have. Thats why I thought coming here would be a good idea, that maybe I would be competing against some of the top guys world wide.
Boy oh boy was I wrong. When I look down the roster of trash competing here, I cannot help but laugh out loud. There is not one star on the whole damn list. The best this place can do is offer a washed up, worn out, no good hick, like Mitch
"Sure Fire" Gatlin. Now I know Mitch well so its okay for me to say that. He is well aware that he is not even in my leauge, and the one time we stepped into the ring together, not only did he get pinned, but he almost got his neck broke. So If I was you Mitch, I would stay the hell out of my way, if you know whats good for you."
"Okay, now that thats out of the way, here we stand on Friday
Tradition. A show that from now on, I own, thats right, I own it.
Each week I will come out here and amaze and entertain you losers like no one ever before me. And soon enough, oh yes, soon enough, the higher ups of this dump will have no choice but to put me into a match up with the man who wears the gold around here. The guy who gets the duty of headlining this circus show, the man known as Kevin Candido. Now Kevin,
I don't want to make you feel bad, or nervous, or upset, but you see, its only a matter of time before that belt is mine, and I can take this hell hole to a new level, a place that is actually respected and admired. And you will all have no choice but to thank me, the greatest athelete ever to set foot into a ring.
[ Freddy hands the microphone over to Florence ] "Fantastic" Florence Floyd: "Thats right Freddy, and the whole way I will be standing right by your side, leading the way strright to the gold. Then maybe this place will be known for its beatiful, and smart women, not a whore house for washed up, over fourty losers. Its about time some class was brought to this place.
Its sad though, even the fans are disgusting. Your just a bunch of fact, beer guzzling losers, who have nothing better to do with yoursleevs then attend wrestling shows all day. Sad sad sad, wait what am I saying? I don't care, as a matter of fact just being out here makes me cringe, come on Freddy, this place is dirty, lets get the hell out of here."
[ Cue - "Good and Evil" ] [ Together, Freddy and Florence make there way back to the locker room area ]
