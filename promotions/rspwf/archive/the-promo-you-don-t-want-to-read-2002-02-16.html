However, I talk about these things enough, and since the idiots who run this company feel it's almost necessary for me to have my face on television every single f'n day.....
I'll talk about Jim, and about Despair and about Jason, and Justice, and LaGrange, and Masters, and Creighton, and Quint, and everyone else in the free f'n world who is beneath, below, and under me in some shape or form...
in the future. [Mitchell smirks.] Mitchell: Instead, I'm going to do something that is beneficial to the entire world, and not just to the wrestling community.
I'm going to give you, the mensas, simpletons, peions, idiots, ignorant, self-indulging, egotistical, pieces of shit people out in the world, some advice.
It's sound advice. It's coming from a man who has been successful in life. It's advice you should not only listen to, but heed.
If you do, you'll still be failures, but at least you would have tried. And it's more than you could say yesterday.
My sound advice is.... Wait, I want you people to be failures in everything you do. I do not need more people walking around trying to be me .
I have enough of that with Jason Midnight. [evil laughter] Mitchell: Midnight, you troll. Shut up already. Can't you hear the clamoring of the fans for you to stop with your lame, boring, reptitive interviews?
Can't you hear them chanting your name followed that stupid clap, and then the words, in unison..."Shut...the hell up" and more of that stupid clapping?
I'm sure you hear the rousing ovation the fans give you when you walk out to the ring... What? You dont' hear anything? Oh yeah, that's because they don't care. It's not like they hate you, and they are not cheering you. That's my case. Or they like you, and they are cheering you. That's not the case either. It really boils down to you are the biggest interest killing wrestler on the active roster, and perhaps...
just perhaps... the biggest interest killing wrestler of the modern era. [Mitchell smiles] MItchell: I'm going to tell you a story Jason. And I want to pull your chair up to the television real close, turn the volume up, because I think you half blind, and hard of hearing, on top of being the stupidest sonofabitch I've ever met in my entire life...
and I want you to listen carefully, attentively, to my story. This is a story about a man I knew once... His name was Ray Trailer. Ray Trailer was the best interest killing wrestler of all time, in my humble opinion.
Everytime he came out to the ring, it was the company's signal to the fans that it was okay to go to the bathroom. Or get some more beer, some nachos, a pizza, a hotdog, something. Smoke a cigarette, whatever they wanted to do, because you were not going to miss much.
So everytime his music hit, there was a mass exodus of people to the concession stands, the bathrooms. The lines were always the longest when he wrestled. Hell, the boys in the back, the ones that if they had nothing better to do, would sit and watch matches, would automatically find something to do.
I can honestly say that up until now, Ray Trailer was the absolute best at getting the crowd to not care about him.
Until now.. Jason Midnight, I can honestly say that you have now have the mantle. You have the trophy. The prize, if you weeeeeeellllllll... You, due to your lack of creativity, lack of imagination, lack of talent, charisma, and intelligence, have over-taken, and by a large margin, Ray Trailer...
And at Ring Dreams, the 23rd ranked wrestler in the world is going to crown you as the new King of Boredom....royally.

There will be a war come Ring Dreams... You'll be General Custer.. I'll be the Native Americans... and I will kill you. You have a track record of crippling opponents? Seriously? I doubt it. Crippling the intelligence of opponents, maybe even getting a lucky shot in. While you sit there with your little buddies and relive the infamous
"Syrup or Jelly" scene from the HBO prison documentary about tossing salad...

As the greatest single, individual force in the annals of RAW's history... I do better than anyone.. [Mitchell smirks, yet again] Mitchell: Ranking in the PWI......23rd... Ranking in relation to RAW talent......2nd... Getting to shut up Jason Midnight...Priceless... Only Adam Mitchell has the power to make this happen. [Fade out]
