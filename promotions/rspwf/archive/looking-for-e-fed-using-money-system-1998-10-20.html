LOOKING FOR E-FED USING MONEY SYSTEM!!!

20 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am looking for a fed that uses a money system and is fake wrestler only rp fed. Good rpers...Please e-mail me if you have a fed using a money system or whatever...here is one of my sample rps...
[The Camera Fades down to show huge Iron gates. Its complete darkness, the wind picks up, and blows a few leaves across the path. The Gates slowly open, as loud creeks of iron rubbing against iron echoes through out the area. The
Camera Steps through the gates, and into the unknown. As the camera walks down the path farther, he notices a huge stone monument directly in front of him.
Its a huge statue of a gargoyle. It pans up the huge carved stone, and sitting onto of the pirched up arm, is Poe, with Shiver sitting silently next to him.
He looks down, smiling at the camera man, and picks up here.] Poe: CrossCut, it seems you are mad at Shiver, huh? It must be that time of month, now, it is time for you to listen to me...
Gather your thoughts, get them clear, cause as of right now, your guess is as good as mine. No doubts can be seen, felt, or heard. You need to be 100 percent sure of your actions. At the same time, you need to be 100 percent sure that this how you want it. Is this the way you want your life to end? Did you work this all, the days, the nights in the gym, training to come out on top. Did you work this hard for a reason? I'm sure your mother would object to you putting it all on the line. But this is where you wanted to go, Eh? This is what you wanted to do? [Chuckles] If this is what you really WANT to do, then maybe you are that much more insane, than I am. let me whisper in your ear, the things you will feel. The pain setting forth down upon your body. Let me tell you the trials and tribulation of fear, l want to see you squirm. I want to see all of you back away, realizing that this isn't how you wanted to go out. Life is only something worth while, if you make it a while worth living. To live on this earth, without doing anything, seeing anything, saying anything at all, is a life not worth living. I have a long, but short realization of everything. If its yours, no worth fighting for it, if it isn't yours, do what ever it takes, blood, sweat, and tears. Be them yours, or theirs, do what ever you can, to make what's not mine, MINE. And when you wake up where you are, each morning, nothing will change. Every dream will be incomplete, due to the fact that it is nothing more than that. Nothing more than a dream, that will never come true.
Its just a matter of being in the wrong place, at the wrong time. But, the lack of intimidation, the lack of defense by your past problems, hasn't trained you well. You have yet to see a man of my magnitude. A Man who doesn't care about his own life, his own body. I only care for a few things in life, but the main one clear. TO make what's not mine, Mine, and to make the person who has it's life, a Living breathing hell. Around every corner I will stand, around every tree, I will be, lurking in every shadow, I will be standing, watching over you, waiting to pounce. My state of mind is as unstable as the next person. But yet, I like it. I don't hide it. Psychiatrists don't have nothing on me, they cant tell what's wrong. [Smirks] I alone am the you need to watch. I alone am the one you need to keep your eyes on. my next action, my next choice of position, could be where you are, and will you be ready? Will be prepared for what I have to do? I really would love to find out, I would really love to see how you can act. But, would I even give you the time to act? Breaking you limb from limb, would be a game for me. Watching you run, would have the same effect on me, as watching you bleed. And, I hate to say it, but soon, it will be over... Soon, it will all be over. Very soon...
[Poe steps down, and jumps off the statue. He walks around it, doing a circle, the camera stays in front. he comes around from the back of the statue, and looks up. He runs his hands through his hair, and then turns to the camera. He picks up here as Shiver still, waits, and sits...]
Poe: Now, the EWWF puts up the spectacular, Rage in the cage. A House of Steel, twisting tormenting, ripping apart flesh. Chain links, grinding away. The
Metal, where your skull collides, making a thickening Thump. Wouldn't you hate the way it feels to have your head rub up against it, each slide taking away a layer of skin. The Ring, is not longer the battle, the battle is to say alive.
But then you walk in, and on the other side, staring at you with the eyes of a
Wolf, will be the most vile man you have ever witnessed. That man, is Shiver I take pride in how I conquer, and I shall take pride with each and every man in that ring. Not a pride of feeling good, nor a pride of a sickening manner. The pride of knowing that I have ended a mans career, possibly ending a mans life.
All for the simple fact that I wanted to. I just wanted to see how far I could bend a mans body, before it goes snap. The fans at ringside, they will be as disgusted as the people watching at home. I don't care, I don't need to know how you feel. I created the best thing in wrestling today, Shiver, and now, his gain is my gain, his loss...my loss. But whatever he does, I will take. For I am his god, I am his leader, Shiver is my communicator with the lives that will fight him in that ring Sunday, and now, he will take them, just like I did, and he will bring them to me. And I...will end them. The fact remains clear. I will do what I want, to make sure you don't walk away. The cards are in my hands, and I'm about to call your bluff. You better hope your hand, is at least playable, or I will eat you alive. Carry you onto the later rounds, either way, you will need some help to get out. But its hard to find what you need. And its hard to find what it takes, to put me DOWN. [Chuckles] But, its better for you to think. Get your mind in gear. But, prepare for the best, cause the worse, is yet to follow.
[He walks off down the path, the camera follows. He comes to a large fountain, around 50 feet from the statue. In the middle, a carved stone statue of a beautiful woman, with water pouring down from a vase she is carrying, feeds the fountain. He turns, sits on the edge, and picks up here as Shiver follows closely behind him looking at the stars and the full, white moon in the background.]
Poe: But where did I leave off yesterday, ah yes. I warned a few, I foretold the future of the rest. But I failed to really get the feeling of the situation. I failed to really let it go.
[He picks up, and places some water in his hands, and slowly pours it out] Poe: Just like the water, all your hopes, all your dreams, they shall flow away, into the emptiness, or in this case, the fullness, of nothing. The water is a symbol of excellence, and the water is slowly going away. And with the final drop..
[He pours out all the water, and shakes off each and every drip] Poe: ...All your final hopes, a drop of prayers, will slowly... Wash Away. [He stands up, and walks away from the statue, the camera follows, he walks down the path, into a large thicket of trees. The moon light sky slowly shines through the maze of tree branches above. Shiver takes a pail of water, and follows him as he walks through the branches. He picks up here.]
Poe: Now its time to rejoin where I left off. Each stable has their person, their soul choice to lead them into greatness, I lead myself down, to the stable known as me and Shiver, that's all we need right now, soon, after he wins this Extreme Title, maybe we will gather a faction, but right now, all we need is each other, and all we care about...is each other. Shiver is my boy, I am his god. I will rule, Shiver will conquer.
[He continues to walk down the path. The path seems to go on for ever, the camera looks ahead, and it cannot find the end, and it looks back, and its almost as if you cannot find the beginning. The camera turns back, and follows
Poe and Shiver as they continue to walk through the tall, everlasting forest.]
Poe: Next, never to late, and never to far. The man known as...well, what's his name, oh yes, I believe he is the Al Koholic. [Chuckles] If you can keep a straight face, and you can honestly say you have control of this [Points to
Shiver], and you know what you are doing, well then, maybe you are a better man that I have you staked out to be. But, in all reality, I highly doubt it. You can try to get on my good side, or be friends with Shiver, because that is all the hopes you have if you wish to survive the beating you will receive at Rage
In The Cage on Sunday. Try to make ends meet, try try try...
[He walks, and he comes out of the woods, at the end, there is a fork, he stops before he gets there, and turns to the camera. He rubs his goatee in anticipation, and picks up here.]
Poe: Shiver, what do you think? [Shiver shakes his head and raises his shoulders. Poe then chooses a direction and he walks up, towards the fork in the path, and stops. the camera follows a bit. he looks around, and then looks in both directions, he turns back to the camera]
Poe: Just like the way life has a way of playing games with you, life has a way of putting the fork. Which way to go? Will one way lead to death? Will one way lead to Gold? Both ways will have bumps, ways to prohibit you, some for the better, some for the worse. but, hold your head up high, and pick a direction.
Hold your head up high, and choose a path. but, will those bumps turn into pot holes, as the task gets harder? Will those bumps turn into road blocks?
Stopping you in your tracks. A decision you need to make. Is it worth it? Rage in the cage, this cage is going to hold the carnage in, and its going to hold the outside, out. Make the tough choice, all the hate burning inside of you, will fuel something. But, its your choice. Will it fuel your fire? Or will it fuel your end? Its near, its closer than you want it to be. And, by the end of time, you will know. I have spoken, and may you heath my warning. Because, by the time night sets it, Nothing will be safe again. And, you will have NOTHING to look forward it.
[He turns, and starts to walk down the path to the right, the camera stays with him and Shiver, until they fade out of the scene. The camera slowly focuses up, and shows the stars. The camera then fades to black.]
