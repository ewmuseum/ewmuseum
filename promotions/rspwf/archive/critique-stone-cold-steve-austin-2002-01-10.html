[CRITIQUE] "Stone Cold" Steve Austin.

10 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
No, this isn't a joke. I just did an RP with Austin for XCW and I figured: "Why not post this to
RSPWF?" Read it, mark for it, critique it, join XCW immediately.
Well... y'know, something like that. It may be a bit different from standard Austin, but... it's my take on the character. Critique it like you would an ordinary e-W flash...
Here it goes... ------------------------------------------------------------------- [A beer. Budweiser, to be exact.] "WHAT?!" [The camera uniformly drops back, allowing us to see a hand wrapped around the can of beer. It continues to drop back, allowing us visions of an arm...
Eventually, we see "Stone Cold" Steve Austin, clad in a black "Austin 3:16" t-shirt, sitting on a bar-stool. In front of him, are six empty beer cans, each of them identical to the one we've been before.]
[Seated next to the Rattlesnake is a middle-aged balding man, clad in a stained blue t-shirt. The man looks depressed. Ever the friendly neighbor, Austin turns to him and opens his mouth.]
SCSA: "Ah... Ah'm thirsty." Man: "...what?" SCSA: "Ah said Ah'm thirsty!" Man: "But umm... you just drank six beers." SCSA: "Shaddap. Ah'm thirsty." Man: "Ok, man. So drink." SCSA: "Not that kinda thirsty, ya yella-bellied sumbitch. Christ, dontcha know who I am?" Man: "Of course. Your name is..." SCSA: "WHAT?!" Man: "Your name is St.." SCSA: "WHAT?!" Man: "Your name is Stone Cold Steve Austin." SCSA: "Wrong." [Pause.] SCSA: "Mah name is Stone Cold Steve Austin and..." Man: "And you don't deserve to be treated like that?" SCSA: "Wrong." [Pause.] SCSA: "Mah name is Stone Cold Steve Austin and I don't deserve to be treated like that." "Anyways... like ah said: Ah'm thirsty." [Austin turns to face the man seated next to him.] "Not for beer... you piece of trash." [Austin faces the camera again.] SCSA: "Ah'm thirsty for blood. I'm hungry for an asswhippin', and this hunger was unleashed 'bout two weeks ago. This hunger was unleashed back when Ah took that big ole' sumbitch that the other sumbitches call Vader and dropped 'im flat on that double chin of his."
"Since that day, Stone Cold was hungry. Since that day, Steve Austin had the itch to whip somebody's ass." "And back in Texas... when one o' dem pesky litttle mosquitos bites ya... and the sumbitch starts ta itch, ya scratch it."
"So ah scratched it." [Austin pops open beer number seven, takes a healthy swig, and continues.] SCSA: "Monday Night Malice... ah got mah hands around Scott Hall's greasy little neck... an' ah choked the life outta the sumbitch." "Kicked him in his flabby little gut... dropped him on his crooked little chin... and that's all ah got ta say about that!"
[Swig. Austin continues.] SCSA: "That's when ah figgered that sumptin' was wrong with ole' Stone Cold. Y'see... that itch o' mine... it didn't go away." "Instead, the sumbitch got red and started to swell. It went from an annoyin' little itch to one helluva rash..."
"It started ta burn. An' burn. An' burn..." "So, ah had to put it out." [Austin chuckles.] SCSA: "Ah saw that sumbitch Rob Van Dam... an' ah had mah first target. Ah got mahself... a pretty little shirt."
"What?" "A set of dreadlocks." "What?" "A bag of oregano..." "What?" "An' a fake accent, mon!" [Pause.] SCSA: "Ah set the Austin Sumbitch Shooter 3:16 from stun ta kill... and ah zoned in on Are Vee Dee." "Kick. Stunner. Victim numba three." "But it wasn't enough. Ah was itchin'... and ah was burnin'..." "And ah saw Are Vee Dee again. Kick. Stunner. Exclamation point." [Austin smiles sadisticly.] SCSA: "Burn, burn, burn..." "It went from hot ta' flamin... but finally, ah found the fire extinguisher." "This week... Stone Cold's in a battle royal. Nine asses ta whip, stomp a mudhole in, and throw ta the canvas."
"Kick. Stunner. Kick. Stunner. Kick. Stunner... ahhh, ya get the point!" "Bottom line is... ah'm walking out the winner. Ah'm gettin' the title shot at the pay per view... and after ah whip Van Dam's ass fer the
Television title, ah'mma win the world title too!" "Hop along the bandwagon now, fellas..." [Austin smiles one last time.] "Cuz pretty soon, yer not gonna have the chance." [Fade out.] --------------------------------- There ya go. ~Maz
