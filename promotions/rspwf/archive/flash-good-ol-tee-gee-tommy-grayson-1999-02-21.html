[Flash] "Good Ol' Tee Gee" Tommy Grayson

21 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ya know the rules: Constructive criticism or compliments :) This is a flash I did for DSWA. ------------------------------------------ [SCENE: Inside a limosine driving around Tucina, Mississippi, the site of DSWA's Presence of Persecution. Time is around 12:45 AM Central time. Cameras show "Good Ol' Tee Gee" Tommy Grayson sitting in the back seat, holding a glass of wine, along with his valet, Dirty White
Girl. Tommy appears to look quite satisfied by the near-riot he caused in the Lady Luck Casino Arena. Dirty White Girl looks damn sexy as usual.]
[APPEARANCE: Tommy is wearing a black DAMN YANKEES t-shirt, Tommy Hilfiger blue jeans, and black Adidas sneakers. His black crewcut hair is spiked with gel as usual. Dirty White Girl is wearing a pink foa, along with her white short dress and her white high heels. Her bleach blonde hair is let go and laying on her shoulders.]
[A chuckle comes from Tommy as he takes a sip of wine. He turns to
Dirty White Girl and kisses her on the cheek. Tommy turns to the camera with a sinister grin on his face. He begins to speak...]
"GOOD OL' TEE GEE" TOMMY GRAYSON - [smiling] So Good Ol' Tee Gee has made his debut in the Dirty South Wrestling Association...and he made it with a _BANG_!! First, Da Girl comes out and gets all those frickin' Southern morons all horny n' all that smack...
[Dirty White Girl giggles.] "GOOD OL' TEE GEE" TOMMY GRAYSON - [rubs his chin] Then, ya know what happens? The best damn thing the DSWA has _EVER_ experienced in it's short run -- the debut of "Good Ol' Tee Gee" Tommy Grayson! That's right! This is the first time Good Ol' Tee Gee has ever set foot into da deep South. So far, I've gotten a tour of the place...and ya know what? It frickin' disgusts me! All these crackwhores, those red-nosed drunks walking the streets, and God damn it, Good Ol' Tee Gee is a man of _HIGH-MORALS_...I don't walk around with my pants hanging down showing my underwear...I don't drink like a maniac. I _DO NOT_ by any means use strong and filthy language! I am f[BLEEP]kin' _ELITE_!! If ya think that I'm a damn hypocrite and that I'm politically incorrect...
..._YOU'RE DAAAAAAAAMN RIGHT_!! But ya know, all jokes aside...let's talk about the most beautiful chick in the DSWA right now. She is Da Girl. The Dirty White Girl!
Look at her, damn it! Perfect face... [Dirty White Girl smiles at the camera as Tommy motions to her face...] "GOOD OL' TEE GEE" TOMMY GRAYSON - Perfect t[BLEEP]s... [Tommy motions at Dirty White Girl's breasts...] "GOOD OL' TEE GEE" TOMMY GRAYSON - And from what I've seen from certain pictures, the perfect -- [singing like Tom Jones] WHAT'S UP
PUSSYCAT?!? WHOA WHOA WHOA WHOA WHOAAAA!! [normal voice] I'm sure ya get my drift. [grins] I'm sure [points at the camera] that your little
_HICK-ASS_ wants ta lick her up n' down her beautiful body and eat grits off'a her...but ya know what?
..._THAT_... ..._AIN'T_... ..._HAPPENNING_!! DIRTY WHITE GIRL - Nope...cuz they're UUUUUGLLLYYYY!! [imitates regurgitation] "GOOD OL' TEE GEE" TOMMY GRAYSON - That's _EXACTLY_ right! Dee Dubbya
Gee don't go for nasty-lookin' Southern morons! Why d'ya think she's with me? [points at himself] Anyway, I see that I'm gonna be in this big pay-per-view coming up for the DSWA. Lover's Brawl '99. [chuckles]
Ya know what? That name just don't sound right! In fact, it's downright _WRONG_!! I mean, I'm not gonna be in there beating on my lovers or anything, right?!? Anyway, Good Ol' Tee Gee is gonna do what he does best in battle royales: WIN 'EM.
DIRTY WHITE GIRL - That's gonna be a little tough, Tommy...I mean, you've got a lotta competition.
"GOOD OL' TEE GEE" TOMMY GRAYSON - You've got a point there, Girl...I see guys like FBK, Jack Vars, Higor "The Rebel" Rosa, Devon Case, and
Westside Walavan in there. Ya know, it's quite tough, but when it comes down to it, Good Ol' Tee Gee is the man wit' da plan, cuz I've got the brains [points at his head], I've got the brawn, and damn it,
I've got the endurance! So, if ya think Good Ol' Tee Gee is gonna be just another pushover...
..._YOU'RE WRONG_!! I'm gonna show each and every one of those rednecks what a _REAL_ man looks like. It ain't some damn KKK member, and it ain't some slackjawed yokel! It's...
..._GOOD_... ..._OL'_... ..._TEE_... ..._GEE_!! And ya know what? I'm _POLITICALLY INCORRECT_... ..._AND_... ..._DAMN_... ..._PROUD_... ..._OF_... ..._IT_!! And as Ice Cube would proudly say: BIIIIIIIITTTTTCCCCCHHHHHH!!!! [Tommy drinks his glass of wine and leans back on the seat...FADE.] ----------------------------------------------- Handler of: "Good Ol' Tee Gee" Tommy Grayson [EFW, DSWA, ICW, LAW] Current EFW Extreme North American Champion Current ICW North American Champion Winner of ICW's 1998 Biggest Lunatic and 1998 Most Underrated Award
Slowly and methodically turning into a heelish face :-)
