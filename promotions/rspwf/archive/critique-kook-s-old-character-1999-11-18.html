[CRITIQUE] KooK's Old Character

18 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The guy is Dark Blood. He is very much like UT, only insane in his ring style. This is my first RP with him in two years. I'm also looking for a fed to join with him. Anywho...
Here's the flash. [The scene opens up inside a church somewhere in Detroit, Michigan. The church was a victim of an arson attack judging by the burn marks that are on the wall.
The lifeless scene turns insane, as a wall of the church bursts into flames. The fire explodes the near wall. In through the door walks the ragged figure that is "Lord" Dark Blood. Dark Blood is wearing a full black attire. Scars run down his arms, and face. He motions with his arms for the camera man to come closer. He lets off a sly smile, then begins to speak... Slowly.]
"L" DB: The life of men all around are flirting with death. They think they're unstoppable, immortal even. Yet the thought of someone living past eternity is mythical. I have been transformed to kill, and though the thought may seem crude to most, It's set me free to the extreme. I sold my soul to the devil, and in return he gave me the uncanny ability to decimate. I've lived a thousand life's, but only this life, which I live now, can truly be called, good.
[Dark Blood utters a moan.] "L" DB: The simplicity too most, is the aggravation of others. I don't wrestle, I just set out my frustration on a mortal to dominate. Anything goes, and when I wrestle, that has been pointed out numerous times. People may beg to differ, but until you look into my eyes, you will see I'm emotionless.
[Dark Blood looks blankly into the camera.] "L" DB: Neo-Pro, I was beckoned by the devil to enter, destroy, and then carry on elsewhere. My disciples are few, but my hopes are high. I will find the men who I can truly call _COLD_, and then mold them into a life form into witch takes over God's distinction."
[Dark Blood pauses.] "L" DB: Todd Van Rooyen; You've been beckoned first. Why? A power has told me so. You mustn't fight it, because the power within will take over your lifeless being, and replace it with a callousness so disheartening, it'll make you flirt with suicide. And with that, I tell you..
Leave now, or forever hold your peace. [Dark Blood turns his back to the camera. He turns his back and walks into the cold air, leaving a tension behind.]
