Another [name withheld] creation: A free agent, BPA - Barry Protection Agency

18 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I thought it would be funny. Maybe you will, too. -[name withheld] (Ethan de Sade) www.ecpwwrestling1.com www.northamericanwrestling.com [Fade in . . .] [Camera shows The Barry Protection Agency . . . Barry, walking around in a mall carrying a card table, a chair, a small poster board sign, a can of cola, and smoking a cigar that is dangling out of his mouth. Barry is 5'3"/135 and has really short black hair, but most of it has receeded. He also has a thick black mustache, facial scruff, and a belly. He has dark eyes and looks like a hobbit. He is wearing a pair of black wrestling shoes, baggy, black and white sweat pants with black knee pads over them. He is also wearing a baggy,
Everlast sweatshirt with the sleeves cut off at the elbow and black elbow pads. Barry stops walking when he finds an open area between two stands in the mall. He sets up his card table, places his can of cola on the table, grabs a deck of cards out of his pocket and throws them on the table. He also grabs a small sign that says, "BPA - Barry Protection Agency" and he places it on the table.
He takes the chair and sits down. He looks around and then remembers something. He gets up and he walks away. Fade . . . Fade back in and Barry is lugging a door, with frame and all, to his little "office." He puts the door up and fixes it to his liking. He tacks a little BPA sign onto the door and sits back down.
Barry sips his soda and sees that he is done with it so without looking, he chucks it over his head to his left and a clanging noise is heard as well as an
"Oww!" A silence follows for a few seconds. Barry takes his cards and sets himself up for a game of solitaire. He starts to play as somebody, looks like a worker from one of the stands, comes to Barry's table without walking through the door.]
BPA: "Boy! Can't you see the door, boy! Use it, boy!" [The worker leaves in disgust. Fade . . . Fade back in. The worker comes back with two large men who look like security. The workers points BPA out. They walk up to him.]
BPA: "See the door? Use it!" S1: "Sir, what are you doing?" BPA: "Give it up, BOY!" S2: "You are going to have to come with us, sir." BPA: "NEVER!" S1: "Sir, you are under arrest. You are coming with us!" [They grab BPA and BPA puts up a mediocre little fight. The one officer hits him in the face and knocks him out. Barry falls into the other officer's arms and they carry him away. After the officers pass the one stand and are out of the fixed camera's view, the worker walks over the BPA's table and flips it over. The worker runs away as an officer chases him. The camera does not follow.]
[Fade.]
