R.W.W.F. World Champion, Jade "The Blade's" sample rp.

27 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{The scene unfolds at Jade's palacial home in Tampa, Florida. The spacious lawn are neatly kept, the gardener's are trimming the hedge around the home being careful to do it just right and Jade's Merces Benz is being washed and waxed by Sir White Collar, former wanna be wrestler. Inside the home, specifically in the den is a beautiful assortment or art. The plush carpets in the den leave the feeling of walking on clouds. The cameras spot Jade on a plush sofa, imported from Italy and on the solid oak table in front of him is the coveted NAW World tag team title and the R.W.W.F. World's heavyweight championship.}
{Jade is wearing a red, silk robe with the initials J.T.B. in black on the lapel. His long, blonde hair is tied in a pony tail in the back and he is holding a glass of Chardonay, seeing the camera crew he begins to speak.}
Jade: What we have here on this table is the pinnacle of my career. Here is one half on the N.A.W. Tag team World Championship belt and then next to it is the coveted R.W.W.F. World Heavyweight Championship that I recently won at
Summer Blast '98. I have truly done what I have said that I would do in this sport, granted that I have not been to all of the different feds that inhabit the wrestling world, but if I did that then I would never get anywhere due to there being to many of them.
{Taking a sip of his wine and speaking.} Jade: There are many who claim and boast to being the best. One such individual was a man calling himself Deathwish. (AKA [detail withheld]) Many come into the wrestling world wanting to do this and claiming to be that, I at one time was just like this. But, I tell you that it gets you nowhere to expect so much when you have done so little. I have worked my way up the high echelon here in the wrestling world and now the fruits of my labor lay before you.
{The camera zooms in on both title belts and the gleam of the light reflects off the gold of the titles.}
Jade: This RP is just but a sample of who I am and what I can do. True, I shoot my mouth off all the time but I can also back it up and you want to know why? Because I am a hard worker and I am a dedicated wrestler, who is very dedicated to his sport and I want to make this announcement. Any wrestler from any fed that wants a piece of me and what I have to offer, just contact me and we shall make the appropriate arrangements for I am truly the man, the myth and the legend of this sport. Now, be gone before I have my illustrious car washer
Sir White Collar attack your fists with his face. {Jade laughs and the scene ends.}
