Icicle Sample Roleplay

1 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens in a dark room. Then the sound of a window breaking is heard. Then the blowing of wind is heard, and footsteps are heard coming downstairs. The footsteps get louder, and louder. 5 to 1 by The Doors plays in the background. The lights turn on, and change to a dark blue. A huge man steps into the light. Then the voice of an old man is heard.)
Voice: ASk the foold that have challenged this man made from ICE. Inside of his ring it is anything, but NICE. 7' 4" 404 lbs of raw power. The monster of
Iceland is built like a TOWER. HE IS ICICLE.
Icicle: Now I've arrived here to wreak havoc on the little dismemberments of this FSW. The Federation of Supreme Wrestling. IT only fits that name now that I'm here. I heard someone named Brandon Stewart come out here, and say he got ripped off. You see what that makes him is a complainer, and I don't like complainers. If you lose you walkaway humble, and you try harder the next time. Well I don't usually try to teach people any tough lessons on my first few weeks with a company, but it seems to me that this punk really needs an
"BLEEP" kicking. So guess waht Brandon Stewart I challenge you to a match on the next card. Yeah Brandon I'm callin' you out, but the question at hand is whether or not your going to have the guts to accept it. If you do everyone that has anything to do with this fed is gonna know you read The Little Engine
That Could a few times to many. If you don't you will be a smart man, and still have all your bones in tact. Doesn't matter what your decision is now, but sooner or later you'll have to wrestle me. You say you got ripped off.
You'll get your arms ripped off if you step in the ring with me. Stewart what's the matter. Do you feel a chill coming on?
(the camera fades to black, and then Icicle's freeze from the top of the screen.) I'm looking for a booked fed so if you want me in either mail me your URL or your application, and some info on your fed including how you Rolepay.
