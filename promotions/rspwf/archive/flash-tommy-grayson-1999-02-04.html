[FLASH] Tommy Grayson

4 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here's a recent RP I did for the EWA. Tell me what ya think. Oh, BTW, I leave you with these words:
I want _CONSTRUCTIVE_ criticism. Thank y'all very much :) ================================================================ [SCENE: The arrival gate at LaGuardia Airport in Queens, New York. The time is 6:34 PM. Cameras show a large amount of people walking out of the gate from a flight that came from Anchorage, Alaska...the site of the EWA pay-per-view, Arctic Fury. After a few moments, a man begins to walk slowly towards the cameras. As he gets closer, we can see it is "The Loose Cannon" Tommy Grayson. He looks like he's still reeling from his loss to Johnny Sledge. As a matter of fact, he appears to be without Honey, who is usually at his side. All he has is a navy blue
Adidas duffel bag slung over his right shoulder.] [Tommy is wearing a dark brown leather jacket over a black t-shirt that reads on the front "I said no to drugs but they wouldn't listen"
in white lettering. He is also wearing JNCO blue jeans, aubern suede Timberland boots, and mirror-shaded Oakley sunglasses. He appears to have a slight goatee-mustache, as he hasn't shaved for several days.
His spikey, crewcut black hair is styled in a grungy manner as usual.] [He steps in front of the camera and pulls off his sunglasses. He takes them off, revealing his cold, brown eyes, and then hangs them on the collar of his t-shirt. He grins a sarcastic grin and the chuckles lightly as the grin is slowly erased from his lips.]
TOMMY GRAYSON - Well, Sledge, you gave one _HELL_ of a show the other night at Arctic Fury in cold, cold Anchorage, Alaska. I'd have to admit, you really gave it your all...but ya know what? I think you beat me with pure luck. Simple as that. Well, I'll have some closing words on this feud, if ya don't mind. I _DO_ have respect for you. You beat it outta me...but then again, I have as much respect for you as I do for an ugly, toothless two-cent whore walkin' along the streets of
Los Angeles. [smiles] No, I'm not talkin' about the deceased Crystal Laine, if you were wondering. Anyway, Sledge, I'm glad our war is over. Nah, it's not because I'm afraid of you. That's just not the case. It's because I'm afraid that I would've ended your career. Hell,
I coulda done it _ANYTIME_ I wanted to. How d'ya like them apples? Well, speakin' of feuds...[pauses] namely the ones about to begin, I have a little "heat" starting between me and "The Legacy" Shane
Andrews. [chuckles] C'mon, Andrews...who the heck are ya kidding? Do you seriously think you can beat me in a match? Jesus Chri -- I have no idea where you got that kinda thinkin', Shane. Maybe that earring being jacked outta your ear really whacked your weak-ass medulla.
Anyway, Shane, I see you've got a good win-loss record goin' on for ya. You won that battle royale, right? Let's put it this way: I think you're _DAMN_ good...'cept you're not good enough to match up to the likes of an "elite" [southern accent] rassla' [normal voice] like myself. Y'see, Shane, the reason why I'm so frickin' dangerous is
'cause I'm psycho-somatic addict-insane. Um, I hope I got that right...[smirks] [Tommy walks towards the luggage pickup area. He stands in front of the conveyor belt and sets his duffel bag down between his feet. He looks around for his bags, and hasn't found them yet. He raises his eyebrows in an arrogant manner and then turns back to the camera and speaks again.]
TOMMY GRAYSON - Well, um, back to the subject of "The Legacy" Shane Andrews. [smirks] "The Legacy," eh? What exactly are you a legacy of, anyway? [pauses; then blows raspberry] God, that's too easy...I have a
_HUNDRED_ disses for that one. Shane, you want Honey, eh? You can have her, because I'm not, uh, "using" her anymore. So, Shane, take her to the _EXTREME_!!
[Tommy clears his throat and then picks up a briefcase from the conveyor belt. He looks at it to make sure it's his. He looks up at the camera again.]
TOMMY GRAYSON - Anyway, Shane, I'll deal with your _PATHETIC ASS_ later. I've gotta deal with Eli Flair in that Global Hardcore tournament. Flair, I've seen ya around in the EWA for a while...you've been doing well, if you ask me. [sighs] I'm saying that about everybody these days. Anyway, Flair, you're dealing with a man on a mission here. I'm fighting to get _MY_ Global Hardcore belt back. I'm fighting for a belt that is _MINE_!! Flair, you're going down...and when you find yourself in the hospital with a body cast on and eating through a tube, you can blame it all on that damn gimp, Wayne
Rothschild! I'm gettin' my Global Hardcore belt _BACK_...no matter what anybody says about it. The fact remains that I am...
..._A THREAT_... ..._TO_... ..._SOCIETY_!! [Tommy gives a peace sign with his fingers and walks away from the camera...FADE.] *Handler of: "The Loose Cannon" Tommy Grayson [EFW, EWA, ICW, JPW, LAW] Current EFW Extreme North American Champion Winner of ICW's Biggest Lunatic and Most Underrated Award "Haunt my house of pain, and feel my psycho love!" - Skid Row, "Psycho Love" -----------== Posted via Deja News, The Discussion Network ==---------- http://www.dejanews.com/ Search, Read, Discuss, or Start Your Own
