Another Brad Morris roleplay: Tell me what you think!!

14 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens up on Brad Morris. He is still in his black wrestling tights after his victory on Thursday against Bob Thurmand. He is wiping his bald head off with a towel as the cameraman walks in. Brad gets up and snaps the towell at the cameraman who almost drops the camera. Then Morris begins to laugh as he sits back down on the bench. He puts a tee shirt on that has the words LOVE MACHINE crossed out. He smiles.)
Brad Morris: You know what, Thurmand, I hate to say this but: I TOLD YOU
SO!!! I told you I would kick your little ass all over that ring, and that's exactly what I did. Thurmand, you and your little buddies the Dark
Riders can all just go straight to hell. Maybe I'll see you there someday.
But as of right now, my career is headed up up up high. And there aint a person in the WHW that can understand what I'm talking about. Because compared to me, you are all a bunch of low life loosers that don't even have what it takes to shine my shoes.
(Brad gets up and goes to his locker. He opens it and the camera zooms in to a picture that is on the locker. It's a picture of Henry Glockenstein.
Underneath it reads: RIP BREEDER. Brad begins to laugh histarically.) Brad Morris: Thurmand, look at your friend. It's time to move on Thurmand. You know, you can blame anyone you want for the death of Henry. You can blame Roger Lambert. It wasn't his fault. He's too stupid to plan the death of anything. You can go ahead and blame me. To tell you the truth, I like thinking I caused Henry's death. I seriously wish I could take the credit for sending Henry straight to hell. But you know, the only one to blame is you Thurmand. You killed Henry. It was you that convinced him to step into that death match with me, and you know it. Thurmand, it is your name that should be on that head stone, not Henry's. For all I care, I wish you both were sent six feet under man. But your time will come Thurmand. Your time will come.
(Brad shuts his locker as he sits back down.) Brad Morris: Runyan, look at me for a second. I sat down here and watched the rest of the nights matches. And yes, I saw Thurmand make it past that battle royal to face Mentor for that Hardcore Title. Runyan, what gave you the right to put Thurmand in that match AFTER I beat him. It should be me going for that Hardcore Title. It was me that smeared Glockenstein's blood all over it. It was me that became the greatest Hardcore champion in the history of WHW. And it should be me to take ole Thurmands place in that match. Think about it Runyan. Everyone knows that I deserve everything.
But what do I get. Absolutely nothing, and like I've said time and time before, I'm sick of it.
(At that moment Steve Morris walks into the room.) Brad Morris: What the hell are you doing in here? What do you want? Steve Morris: SHUT UP!! Brad, listen to me. I'm sick of your shit!! All
I came in here to tell you was that a match was just signed. You and Ken
Wilson for the US title next Sunday. And you can just shove that US title up your ass big brother. Oh yea, one more thing. It's a no holds barred match which means your gonna get your ass kicked all over that auditorium!
(Steve then slams the door behind him as Brad begins to laugh.) Brad Morris; He only wishes he could be as great as I someday. But now this is a whole new situation I've been put in. The US title huh? One of the titles I've never heald here in the WHW. Ken, you wanted it my friend and now you've got it. You're right. I haven't had the best of luck in other federations. Your right, I haven't heald very many titles in the sport of wrestling. But you know, that was the Love Machine. Now, you're looking at the biggest meanest man you'll ever step into the ring with Ken.
And whether it be right inside the ring, or it be in the bathroom, or out in the parking lot, I'm fully prepared to shove your ass in the smallest corner I can find and just pound the living shit right out of you. Now
Ken, I really do have to give you credit. You defeated Kurt Castle.
Something I couldn't do last month. No, something the Love Machine couldn't do. But now, I can do it. Down deep, I can beat Kastle and I know it. And I CAN beat you Ken. No matter what you say or do, I will be walking out with the US title around my waist. And there aint a damn thing you can do to stop me. You see Ken, I'm smarter than you, better than you, more clever than you, stronger than you, a better wrestler than you, and even though I'm not the Love Machine anymore, I'm still a hell of a lot sexier than you!! So stick that pal!! And trust me, I have many more words for you. You'll just have to wait to hear them. Now get out of my locker room, I got business to take care of!!
(Camera fades out.)
