My First Flash for ICW!

31 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The ICW cameras begin to roll. The scene is a city park in Memphis Tennessee. There are birds chirping, the sun is shining bright on the nice sixty degree day. There are old men and women slowly jogging. They are wearing orange clothing so they either don't get shot by hunters or hit by cars. There are children playing on swings, and sliding down slides, and hanging around on monkey bars. In the distance you can see a tennis court, and a handball court.
There are many young females on both courts playing. There is one small sidewalk in the center of the park equipment, and trees. There are nice green wooden benches along the sidewalk. All of the sudden Kenneth Morlock is seen walking down the sidewalk towards where the camera man is standing. Kenneth
Morlock is wearing a pair of blue Tommy Hilfiger jeans, a red, white and blue stripped Tommy Hilfiger button up shirt, and a pair of white Tommy Hilfiger gym shoes. Kenneth Morlock also has on a pair of rayban sunglasses. Kenneth Morlock has his short brown hair in a perfect part to the right side of his head.
Kenneth Morlock continues to walk down the side walk. He walks past the kids in the park. All of the kids watch him in "AWW" The little kids start to follow him down the sidewalk. Kenneth Morlock walks a little further to the courts, he sits on one of the green benches. The women continue to play tennis and handball as the kids stop and stare at Kenneth Morlock.]
[Kenneth Morlock] Ah. Now this is the life, chilling in the park with no damn interruptions. [All of the sudden the kids start giggling rather loudly. Kenneth Morlock gives a mean look over at them and hollers.]
[Kenneth Morlock] GET THE HELL OUTTA HERE YOU LITTLE BRATS. CANT YOU SEE I, AM TRYING TO DO SOMETHING. [The kids are startled and run off.] [Kenneth Morlock] AND IF ANY OF YOU GOT ANY CANDY GIVE IT UP! [Kenneth Morlock laughs and goes on talking.] [Kenneth Morlock] Like I was saying, a perfect day, chilling in the park with no interruptions...anymore. [Just then the women on the tennis court come over to the fence and begin talking to Kenneth Morlock.]
[Lady #1] Hey Mister. Why are you sitting there watching us? And why is that camera man there? Are y'all taping us or something?
[Lady #2] Yeah, are you some kind of stalker or something? Because if you are we will scream at the top of our lungs.
[Kenneth Morlock] First of all. I damn sure wasn't looking at you two. Second of all, do not flatter yourself, if I was gonna stalk someone it would be someone with some looks, and third of all, SHUT THE HELL UP AND LEAVE OR GET BACK TO PLAYING YOUR
DAMN GAME! {The women are startled and get there stuff and run away very fast.] [Kenneth Morlock] I swear a little piece and quiet to give my interview is all I ask, and I get a bunch of stupid people that wanna come out of the house for the first time in there life today. Why do I deserve this what did I do.
[Kenneth Morlock looks around and now realizes that there is a dead silence and that everyone is gone.]
[Kenneth Morlock] Finally, perfect, perfect enough to give this for ICW. That's right ICW,
Kenneth Morlock has finally arrived for all of your viewing pleasure. It is officially time for all members of ICW, to fold there tray's upright, and grab the mask that is hanging in front of them, because everyone that comes in my path is going DOWN! See ICW New Year's is coming up right around the corner and it is a new year and a new dawn for me. No longer am I coming out to please the fan, and beat who the fans want me to beat. Now I am coming out to beat down who I want, when I want, and definitely how I want. it could be in the back, it might be in the ring, it might be with a bat, or even while I sing. Damn, that rhymed didn't it. I am a poet and didn't know it!
[Kenneth laughs and shakes his head at the camera and then continues on.] [Kenneth Morlock] See ICW, I see the names of the people on the roster, and I just have to laugh my ass off. I mean come on. I am like the only real talent you guys got around here. I see the formation of DI. Cyris and Grayson heading it up. Now
Cyris, you know me and you have a past, we may have never met face to face in the ring, but you definitely know I took you out. Your boys were there, my boys were there, and my faction dominated your faction while running. So it all died. You won the battle, that is right, but the war between me and you will definitely never be over. See things between me and you have really just heated up. We will definitely be meeting down the line here in ICW. Now
Grayson, we just started a little stuff when you hooked up with Cyris. That possibly was the worst mistake of your life. You got the talent, not the true talent like I gots, but you got some talent. Now you are just wasting it all away. So I guess I gotta give you the same warning I give to Cyris, you both better stay out of my way, if you wanna walk out of the arena and not be carted out.
[Kenneth Morlock gets up from the bench and walks onto the tennis court and then continues to talk.]
[Kenneth Morlock] Now onto a lighter subject. A subject that just touches my heart. The death of a close personal friend of mine, Higor "The Rebel" Rosa. I mean if I had a forty I would pour a little out. Oh wait, I don't even like Higor, I mean the guy never did anything but carry worthless gold. To hell with him. I wanna talk about my first opponent here in ICW, Devon Case. See Devon you may not know me, because I definitely don't know you. But I can tell you, you are in one hell of a predicament. I mean both of our debut matches and you get little ole' me to contend with. I gotta wonder, did you piss off the bookers or something. I mean your first match in ICW and you will be embarrassed that bad, and totally dominated. Someone must have vendetta out for you. But it's OK. I promise not to kill you. Only because it isn't in my contract, but I wont kill you, just hurt you. All you are is a stone in my way. And I will take you out boy.
[All of the sudden a squad car pulls up to the tennis court. Two heavy officers get out. One approaches Kenneth while one sits back.]
[Cop #1] Sir, we have a report that someone fitting your description has been coming around here scaring little kids and stalking women. Is that true?
[Kenneth Morlock] Even if it is, which it isn't, what are you gonna do? Take me downtown so
I can get questioned?
[Cop #2] I don't like your attitude that's exactly what we are gonna do, take you downtown. [Kenneth Morlock] And I don't like your weight. So damn what. I will be out in an hour. [The first cop puts the cuffs on Kenneth Morlock, and walks him to the squad car and puts him in. Kenneth Morlock hollers from inside the squad car.]
[Kenneth Morlock] REMEMBER ICW...PRETTY PLUS POWER EQUALS ME! [The ICW camera fades to black as Kenneth Morlock is being taken away in the squad car.] Kenneth Morlock ICW~~ [0-0-0] WWO~~[0-0-0] SSW~~[0-0-0] EWA~~[1-0-0] -1 RSPWF Top 200 1998
