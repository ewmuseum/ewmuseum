USCW Flash Central 5/18/98

19 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Suicidal Tendencies Apocalypse Atomic Punk The Horsemen Moral Majority The Bishop The Marauders -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- (Tommy Kain and Ken Stanley are standing in front of a USCW backdrop, wearing Suicidal Tendencies t-shirts.)
Kain: June 13th....June Jackpot, a four way dance for 100,000 dollars You got a team that is To Ben Announced...then you have Tony Dylan and Chip Syske part of Moral Majority...The Horsemen who are the number one contenders, and then you have the USCW tag team champions, Da Lence Mob.
Stanley: DLM..we know what it is like to be champions. We know what it takes to hold on to those belts week after week, pay-per view, after
pay-per view... Kain: You are going into a huge pay-per view...you want to make it huger USCW officials? Put Suicidal Tendencies in that matchup, and make it a 5 way dance.
Stanley: Allow us to show Da Lence Mob what real champions are, allow us to show the USCW and all the fans a real tag team. Allow us to raise the fan fair in the USCW tag team division.
Kain: So yeah, we are challenging not only those 4 other teams to allow us into the match, but we are challenging the USCW officials to allow us in the match to show them how a real tag team performs.
Stanley: We have experience fellas. We won our titles in another promotion inside a steel cage in a three way dance. We know what it takes to survive inside that ring, and we will do whatever it takes to be in that match, and to win that match.
Kain: When we walk out of that arena raising our arms high, after beating the tag champs and 3 other teams, you fans better cheer....you fans better cheer when you hear this announcement...
Stanley: Your winners... Totally Suicidal Tommy Kain.... Kain: Kompletly Krazy Ken Stanley, Suicidal Tendencies! Stanley: We'll be in that match whether or not we are signed to be. So USCW officials do yourselves a favor and add us to that match. Kain: Why do we say all this...because we can! (Camera fades out slowly) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- (The Time Has Come, flashes across your TV screens as you see Apocalypse standing inside an empty USCW arena. The house lights are off and the arena is completely dark except for the candles put up in the four corners of the ring.
Apocalypse is dressed up in a pair of Blue trunks with a pair of black wrestling tights underneath. There is a big A on the front of his trunks. He is also wearing a mask with what looks like a grey skull on it.)
Apocalypse-You know, in some ways change is good. The USCW has changed since the last time I truly stepped through these ropes. I mean I could go back, talk about the day I first won the title.
(Spicer:the Saint is Climbing to the ring and now so is the Apocalypse the
Saint is going to suplex him in!
Davis:and the ref is not paying attention and is knocked out by the legs of
Apocalypse as the Saint brings him down!
Spicer:the Ref is out and here Comes Genesis, he nails the Saint from behind with a chair!! The Saint is out! The ref is out! And Genesis is pulling the
Saint over and he goes outside the ring and is putting on that Bret Hart figure four he calls Genocide.
Davis:Apocalypse is dragging over and covers the Saint! The ref is still out of it but he is coming over to count! But does not see what Genesis is doing outside the ring!
The Ref:1.......2...........3!!!!!!!!!! Spicer:No instant replay rule and the DOA has taken advantage of it! Genocide has pushed the Saint back into the ring before the Ref sees his legs outside the ring!
Pinelli:The winner and first ever USCW World Champion! The DOA's Apocalypse.) Apocalypse-Or I could tell you that after that match the Saint's knee was messed up big time. Same thing happened to the former ICON of the USCW, The
Spyder. I put two of the biggest names in the wrestling business out. Yet, somehow I was left out of the invitations to come back to the USCW. Now I know why. Mr. Robert Spicer didn't want me to come in here,didn't want me to hurt any of his so-called superstars, Daniel Dare, Jacob Yoder, and Viper. All big names in the USCW now. Yet they can't even begin to compare to the might and skill of Apocalypse. The time has come for me to go back to the top of the the
USCW. My first step to the top is going to be one, Ron Matthews. You and your
SWA pals seem to be big names. Just becasue you span three federations and have a boat load of members is nothing. Ron Matthews I am calling you out. If you got the guts you will accept. The time has come. The end game begins here and only the strong will survive.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- Atomic Punk: Storm if this is all it takes to get under your skin then just wait until I really get started. First of all I don't need to gloat because I am annoying you. Second if you and your little piss ants want to play then I am game. Now you keep referring to me as a jobber, well if this is what a jobber can do to a declining "star" then what is a up and comming Star like me gonna do. I will tell you I am gonna take you apart piece by piece and if your little friends want some then bring it on cause the Atomic Punk aint scared of nobody.
SWA beware.............. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- (Scene: "Detroit" Diesel's Garage) "Detroit" Diesel Jones: (As Da Lench Mob's recent flash plays.) My we have a wicked sense of humor, don't we? Well 8-ball or cueball or what ever you call yourself, you and your cronies had better watch how far you push this "make fun of the Horsemen" gag. Who knows, you might just find out how easy it is for an 8-ball to be crushed under the pressure of a full fledged assault.
"Little" Joe Mountain: And this will never be the end, cause we'll see you at the June PPV!...
(The screen fades to black.) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- <<Camera comes up to show Shining Path"the Terrorist", Tony Dylan, and Chip Syske in thier office at the Moral Majority Health and Fittness Center in Texarkana, TX>> SP: Sinister, you should be afraid of me, because I can kick your scrawny little ass just like I can Viper's or the still silent coward
Bullet. Viper I'm coming to the ring Wednesday and I will take the World Title home with me. WTF, can talk a lot of trash but when it comes to getting in the ring and baking up that trash talk they just can't get it done. When the Moral Majority talks trash about somebody we go to the ring and we back up what ever we say. I will defeat Viper, I will be the next USCW World Champion, and this Wednesday is when it's going to happen. And if Sinister thinks that he could take on the entire Moral
Majority at once I invite him to get in the ring and try it. TD: Now as for Storm and Ron Matthews we will beat them. Storm you didn't beat me in the Tournament. The only reason you won was because of
Sinister's interference. You can't beat me and niether can Ron Matthews. CS: MAtthews a big cog is about to get thrown into your "Machine". And that cog has Moral Majority stamped all over it. I just got called a wannabe buy you guys. I guess you have so much room to talk the way you say the SWA is 4life.
TD: That is just sooooo original. CS: I know I wonder where they ever got that cool slogan. TD: Gee Chip I have no idea. CS: Seriously, you guys need to do something about that. It's getting just a little stale. TD: As stale as your wrestling carrers will be when we get threw with you. <<Camera fades out>> -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- From the Church of the Holy Alliance: Bishop: Sinister, you are wrong in your thinking. You think you can mock God and all that is Holy. In our last match your friend Viper interfered and weakened me that is the only reason you won our last match, and as far as I am concerned you did not win. You cheated
Sinister, that makes you the loser in my book. I am 2 and 0 against you Sinister. Both victories are tainted. That is why I challenged you to the steel cage grudge match. No way to escape and no way for anyone to interfere. You and me all alone Sinister. This time I will show the world why I am the greatest wrestler of all time. I will stomp you into the ground and then when the right moment comes I will crusify you. You should live in fear of me Sinister. I am after all the only man who can save your soul. One word from me is the difference bettween you spending all eternity in the fiery pits of hell or the posibility that I could redeem you and your soul may have eternal salvation. This may be your last chance Sinister. Leave the WTF and Join me in the Holy Alliance.
Let me save you Sinister. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- ( Scene opens to a wrestling school. " The Marauder " Chris Evans, " Shotgun " Shawn Evans, and " The Enforcer " Mike Adams are walking around the school. They are all wearing the Marauders t-shirt
( It Ain't Over Yet ) and Nike shorts. Shawn is wearing his Marauders baseball cap while Chris is has his sunglasses on. In the background, many teenagers are being taught how to wrestle by a number of professional wrestlers. )
Chris : Brings back old memories. Shawn : Yeah. ( Chris points to a picture on the wall. In the picture, Chris, Shawn, and Mike are with some other young wrestlers, with a ring in the background. On closer inspection, those other wrestlers are guys like
Bret " The Hitman " Hart, " The Blackheart " Owen Hart, " The Canadian Crippler " Chris Benoit, " Lionheart " Chris Jericho, " The British Bulldog " Davey Boy Smith, and the late Brian Pillman. ) Chris : Remember this guys? Mike : ( LOL ) How could I forget it? Ya know, I haven't heard from the Harts in a while. I heard they've ALL been doing VERY well now.
Shawn : I my opinion, I think they should leave the WWF and the WCW. ( LOL ) USCW is where it's at. Mike : I miss the good old fashion Stampede Wrestling days. Chris : ( Looks at the camera ) Hello USCW fans. You might be wondering what the Marauders are doing in this old wrestling school.
But before I say anything further, this isn't ANY wrestling school. This is the Dungeon, the starting point for many of the world's greatest superstars. ( Takes a deep breath ) Chris : Well, I've gotten many letters asking about my past ; and what better place to talk about my past than in our wrestling school. I still remember when me and the other Chris's would step into that ring.
( Points to a ring behind him ) Mike : Yeah, it was really something. Before we say anything more, let's have a moment of silence for the late, great, Brian Pillman. We miss you brother.
( Silence ) Shawn : It's been a lot of years since we've come here. The first " federation " we were in was good ol' fashioned Stampede Wrestling. ( LOL ) I remember my first match. It was me and Chris against a couple of guys who shall remain nameless. Boy did we get our butts kicked. We were nearly crying. Look at us now. Successful wrestlers in the biggest fed there is. The moral of that story is to NEVER, EVER give up.
Chris : Me and Shawn were in this school for 6 years. Our parents were always out, and I guess they figured that it was better that we were
SOMEWHERE than on the streets ( LOL ) So I guess they picked here. That really changed our lives forever. Our careers started here, AND we met Mike here. Mike : Hey. What about this Chris? Remember this? ( Mike points to a picture showing a young Chris Evans ( and scrawny ) with a belt around his waist. )
Chris : The Stampede Wrestling middle-weight belt. My first ever. Shawn : I still remember going out that night with the Chris's, the Harts, and the others. That was one helluva celebration ( LOL ). Chris : Pretty wild night. So fans, you wanted to know where my origins came from. Well this is it. I don't have much more to say. I can always talk about my opponents later. But my starting point?
That's another story. Until then... ( Scene ends with Chris, Mike, and Shawn walking around and looking at various pictures, almost as if they are being taken back to the past. )
