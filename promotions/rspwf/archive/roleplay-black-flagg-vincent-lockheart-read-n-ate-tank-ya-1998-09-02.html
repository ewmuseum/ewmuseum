Roleplay - "Black Flagg" Vincent Lockheart - Read n ate, tank ya :)

2 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[SCENE:The very place WWO's TITLE WAVE will take place. Coast Guard Beach,
Massachusetts. TIME:MIDNIGHT <If ya didn't see that coming, you haven't been paying much attention> The waves crash against the shore of the beach as the moon is full, and the stars shining down on the water along with it. The wind gusts strong as if a storm is coming. The camera moves around until we see a figure sitting in the sands. The camera moves closer until we see that it is
Vincent Lockheart. Lockheart just sits there, his long jet black hair blowing in the wind. Lockheart has on no shoes, a pair of ripped black jean shorts, a
"WWO:TITLE WAVE" T-Shirt. Lockheart just sits with his knees up and his arms wraped around them before he begins to speak. . ]
"BLACK FLAGG" VINCENT LOCKHEART- Well Wildarm. . you've managed to stay safer for a little bit longer. .lucky for you because I wasn't feeling to mericful that night. Lucky for you DOnovan came out. .not unexpected at all. Donovan it looks like you've evened up the playing field once again. .but like I said before, I don't like being even. I only like to be in the lead. . .but there's still plenty of time to regain that lead, and you can trust me when I say that
I am going to do so. I may not have much. .but my word is one thing you can count on, and it's one thing you're not going to like at all. You can trust me on that much.
[Lockheart picks up a handful of sand and watches it as it slowly drains itself out of his closed fist back to the grund . . .grain by grain as he goes on. .]
"BLACK FLAGG" VINCENT LOCKHEART - Much like the sand that is running out of my hand. .the sands in the hour glass counting down the time until TITLE WAVE and your destruction DOnovan is slowly running out. ..and that is somthing that not you or anyone else can stop no matter what you try to do. You talk about money.
. sure, it has it's privilages as you have stated. .but it means nothing to me. I've never had alot of it, and I somehow doubt I ever will. But I've gotten by just fine with what I was able to obtain by beating guys such as yourself. See.
.you can talk about the tribbeal warriors gathering there powers before going into battle, but going into the Black Forrest is nothing DOnovan. You can talk abbout it all you want, but both you and I know you don't know the true meaning of Battle. You've never been in a real "Battle" the odds were always stacked in your favor, but this time it's different. .this time you have the odds against you and you just can't handle it. You can try to hide that fact, but it isn;t working. I can see the fear in your eyes everytime my name is mentioned. . .
[Lockheart opens his hand disposig of the remaining sand then blows the rest of it off with his breath before looking up into the sky and going on. .]
"BLACK FLAGG" VINCENT LOCKHEART - Donovan. . .I've never clamed to be doing this to gain honor and respect onto me or anyone else. . .I only do what I need to do to survive. Much like in the animal kingdom it's Survival of the fittest.
Only the strong survive Donovan. . .Are you strong enough to survive? Maybe thus far, but come TITLE WAVE I think it will be time that the new species.
.the stronger species once again takes over by disposing of those lesser than. Or in other words when I finally rid myself of a man named Marcus DOnovan. The difference between a figure four and Connect four? <laughs> How about you
Donovan. .can you explain to the people the difference betwen a Tiger Driver and a Tiger Driver '91? How about a michinoku Driver and a Michinoku Driver #1?
<chuckles evily> You can question my wrestling knowledge. .but though it may seem small, I do have quite a bit. I just prefere to use more convential methods of disposing of those who stand in my way. .that is somthing you will find out soon enough.
[The wind picks up once again blowing Lockheart's jet black hair over his face. He raises his right hand and wipes it to the side then pauses for a moment before going on. . ]
"BLACK FLAGG" VINCENT LOCKHEART - I'm not here to bring respect to anyone. .not even myself. I could care less if people resepct me, or wish I was dead. It means nothing to me, so you can take your guilt trip and lay it on someone else
Donovan. .because it doesn't hold any water with me. You talk about Sacrafice,
Donovan. .what do you know of the word? You;ve never sacraficed anything in your life. .I've spent my whole life sacraficing my body and my life just to
Survive. .see once again we come to Suriviving. At TITLE WAVE we'll find out if you, DOnovan are willing to sacrafice yourself in order to defeat me. .If not, then you'll surely find yourself on the opposite side of things, and it won't be somthing you will enjoy. <laughs> That much I can tell you for sure.
[Lockheart lays down in the sand staring up at the night sky, he places his arms underneath his head elevating it slightly before going on. .]
"BLACK FLAGG" VINCENT LOCKHEART - You will humiliate me? I'm beyond humiliatoion DOnovan. Humiliate my family? I have no family. .humiliate my fans
<laughs> I have no fans either. Looks like you need to go back to the drawing board and think up somthingmore relevant because this just won't work. Donovan the time draws nearer and nearer with each passing day. . . So I want you to think about your life, what you've done and want to do. ..Ponder the errors of your ways. .because the night you get in the ring with me could very well be your last night on this planet, because you're stepping into the ring with your worst Nightmare, and from there it's only a matter of seconds till you get
Black Flagged. . .
Lockheart reais lying on the beach, just staring up into the sky as the camera slowly pans back showing a full shot as a wave rashes down on the shore and we slowly. . ]
[Fade To Black. .]
