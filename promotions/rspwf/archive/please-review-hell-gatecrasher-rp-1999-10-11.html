Please review - HELL GATECRASHER RP!

11 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, Basically all the info you need to know is mentioned. I admit the spelling is poor in some parts but I cannot be bothered to correct it.
Go to www.hellgatecrasher.8m.com for the official HELL GATECRASHER site. Thanks people! ___________________________________________________ A curtain is closed. It blocks off more and more sunlight until it meets the other curtain. The man closing the curtain is Insider.
Insider : Hi folks! I need to go to sleep early today as I have to wake very early tomorrow morning on a mission I'll talk about in a second. I have to wake up at 2AM for a very special investigation for Exposure. Here's a little background info on my target.
(Insider pauses and a tape is played. The tape shows footage of Epic in the ring with HELL GATECRASHER, being the host of The Epic Show and the last time we saw him in PWW - witnessing HG's first public interview in 5 years in which he demands freedom from Epic. HG basis his motives on ambition.
Epic hasn't been seen since which is bizzare because he always seemed to hold an agenda to be in the spotlight. His identity isn't known but maybe his previous one had lots of spotlight in PWW and Epic misses that.)
Insider : There we are. We are waking up so early to investigate the whereabouts of Epic. We were told that Epic is not a nightlife person but likes wandering in early morning. With the help of his friend HELL
GATECRASHER we'll hopefully track him down. Now it's time for me to sleep on it so for now it's Insider ... out.
(We are now showed a garden in the evening. Time speeds up through nightime and when we reach early morning the scene moves on.)
(We are in an executive lounge with Insider and HELL GATECRASHER sitting on a leather seatee. They face a glass door attached to the window almost like a travel suit.)
Insider : I needed that sleep! Here I am with HELL GATECRASHER in the south of England. Let's go.
(They leave and jump in a Merceded Benz car coloured pale blue-green.) Insider : Where shall we go? HG : Remember you interviewed Epic in Shuttenworth field? Insider : Yes. HG : There. (The scene fades and returns at an expansive field away from all commercial life. The peacefulness in solitude is combined with an eerie sense. HG and
Insider look around but all they can see is the farmer's bungalow.)
Insider : He doesn't appear to be here. HG : No. Something definately must be wrong. Insider : Where's his house? HG : I'll take you there. (The scene reappears in a normal but very friendly looking village. We are stationed at a very nice, normal house. HG and Insider are near the door and window and look in.)
Insider : This house doesn't look to have been inhabited for weeks - look at the mail! HG : You're right. Epic never gets this much mail in a day. Insider : Where else could he be? HG : We'll check his ex-wife Emmas flat. (The scene once again returns in a village similar to the last one. We are parked outside a block of flats. HG and Insider ring the bell to one of them. It is dark but not pitched black. HG speaks in his slightly medieval
English accent and Insider speaks in his normal accent.)
Insider : No one's answering. HG : We better leave then. Insider : Any other places to look? HG : Uh I'm not su... well maybe we could try Strangeways. Insider : Strangeways?!? HG : Yes the nickname for it. It's a place that is gothic and such. Epic isn't gothic but he has this thing... I don't know! Let's go there.
(We are viewing HG and Insider in the car. Going past many bushes and obscure paths. There is no traffic what so ever and this is Strangeways.
They pull down a turning and get out of the car down the path.) Insider : Old house there. HG : Yes it is. Probably haunted! Insider : What's that? (Insider points at a menacing looking figure.) HG (laughing) : A scarecrow! Calm down. Insider : Sorry, I'm not used to this. HG : I am. (They come across the turning to an old graveyard marked "Everlourne Lane".) HG : The graveyard. Insider : No I doubt he'll be there! He's not that sombre and morbid. Anyway
I think that's it.
HG : Yes, sorry I was no help to you. Insider : Don't worry, been a pleasure. Insider ... out. (As HG and Insider walk back to the car the camera keeps focusing on the graveyard. Suddenly four shadows are seen lurking by the graveyard as the scene fades to black.)
