Critque J.B. Destiny RP--I wanna know if you guys think this is to long

18 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Destinys Site "The Future" J.B. Destiny (The Scene opens up in J.B. Destiny's hotel room. There is an open suitcase on the floor. And J.B. Destiny is sitting directly across from the camera on a chair.)
J.B. Destiny: Shane Martin...hahaha, Micheal Wilkins billed you as an extrodanary athlete. And I have to admit I did not know what to expect this
Wednesday night when I step in the ring with you. But now I realize you are not the "high calibur" athlete Wilkins said you were, you are just another wrestler who will be counted down for the one...two...three and become yet another W in J.B. Destiny's win column. Martin you come out here and cut what in my opinion had to have been the crudest interview in XBWL history. You used words that are not supposed to be used on telivision. Thoose words are supposed to stay behind the scenes. And since they are supposed to stay behind the scenes I will leave them there. But you did ask me a question that I have to respond to. You asked me what I have accomplished in this business. Well let me tell you what I have accomplished, you better get a pencil and paper ready because this may take awhile. I am a former SWA Asian Champion, a former
SWA Tag-Team Champion, a former SWA World Champion, a Former UCW World
Champion, I have beaten the likes of The Beast, Matt Austin, The Belgian Sensation, Brian Baylor, Golden King, and Steve Edwards himself. Together
Steve Edwards and I brought The SWA to its demise and on top of all that I am a former two time XBWL Tag-Team Champion. So now I ask you what have you done???
Well I can answer that for you. You have done nothing. And all that you have done since you arrived in The XBWL is talk about how you were trained by
"Dangerous" Dave Taylor. A legend in your mind and your mind alone. Well
Shane Martin let me tell you who I was trained by. A man who is arguably the most legendary wrestler of all time, "The Chameleon" Micheal Andrews. Andrews and my late Father trained me to become the perfect wrestling machine and that is exactly what I am. But back to "Dangerous" Dave Taylor for one minute...Now
Martin you sure are close to Taylor arent you??? One might even say you idolize him some what right??? Well I have a little story to tell you about your mentor. Dave Taylor was scared to step into the ring with J.B. Destiny.
As soon as J.B. Destiny signed his contract with The Supreme Wrestling Alliance
Dave Taylor felt a little threatened. Why??? Because the company that he was supposed to head, the company he was supposed to lead put there faith in J.B.
Destiny and as soon as the match was set for "Dangerous" Dave Taylor to step into the ring with "The Destined One" Dave Taylor packed his bags and caught the first plane out of The SWA. And after I drove Taylor out Steve Edwards and myself ran things in The SWA. And now I have the pleasure of taking on his prize student. Personally I would be suprised if you show up to our match
Martin...but hell if you do I will give you some credit. I mean atleast you will be showing everybody that you have more guts than your former teacher.
Again you ask me what have I done in this sport, well my friend that question is laughable.
(J.B. Destiny pauses to take a sip of his NAYA.) J.B. Destiny: Another thing that I found funny about your sad escuse for an interivew was that you made reference to the fact that all I have said about our match is that I am going to beat you. Well God what do you want me to say.
I have told everyone week in and week out that J.B. Destiny only speaks the truth, and I told everyone the truth I said I was going to beat you and that is excactly what I am going to do. I am going to beat you and walk into Out Of
Control Seven: The Hunted as the number one contender for The XBWL North
American Title. Now the last thing you made reference to in your, again sorry excuse for an interview was that you were going to walk out with barbed wire and other weapons of violence. All I have to say is this is The XBWL isnt it,
I mean what do you think I expect you to do. J.B. Destiny always prepares for the worst. And just a little hint Shane it is going to take alot more than a little barbed wire to put J.B. Destiny down for a three count. I have beaten men like you before. Overpushed, overused, enhancment talent that doesn't have any wrestlign ability so he decides to thrive on the hardcore aspect of the industry. I have beaten men bigger than you and a hell of alot more "Hardcore"
than you, hell I beat Beast and he is a former six time Hardcore Champion, I beat Edwards he is a former Hardcore champ himself, not to mention the so called "King Of Hardcore" Brian Baylor. So bring whatever you want to our match because it wont help you. I think that is basically all I have to say.
Now if you will excuse me I have to get to bed. I mean I do have a match tommorrow. (The Camera leaves the hotel room. And the scene fades to black.) This Has Been A J.B. Destiny Interiew And As Usual The Pleasure Has Been All
Yours
