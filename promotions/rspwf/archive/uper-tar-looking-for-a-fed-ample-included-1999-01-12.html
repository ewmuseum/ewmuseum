$UPER$TAR looking for a fed!!!! ($ample Included)

12 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thats right, you all have heard about THE IRON MAN STEVE GORECKI. Well is he coming to an arena near you? Better offer him a good deal otherwise you are out of a pure winner...e mail me at
[detail withheld] and I will surely get back to you. Heres my sample rp... -=(SCENE)=- -=(After Sunday Storm went off the air, Iron Man Steve Gorecki picks up the microphone and begins to speak. He looks somewhat furious that him and Tall
Pete was a dark match and was not seen on TV. Da Possinater is nowhere to be found. Iron Man doesn't look fatigued at all and we all assume that the match was a quickie.)=-
-=(SCENE)=- -=(Iron Man Steve Gorecki)=- You are looking at the future champion of the GWF! That world title will look great around my waste when I get through pounding my way through this stupid battle of the belts tournament. How can one tournament be for four belts? I guess the prez will just have to give the winner...ahem...me all of them! I find that to be the only solution to this puzzling problem. As long as I get the World Title, then I will be happy. I will not settle for anything less, or else Mr. Sanders will have a leave of absence during his job. Now this Tall
Pete character, for those who didn't see it. I don't know why it wasn't on TV, but if the fans don't want to take a look at the future of this federation, its up to you. But this Tall Pete character gave no resistance. I rolled my way right through the guy. Da Possinater got a little action in there too....roll the footage on the metrovision up there....
-=(Iron Man Steve Gorecki)=- -=(SCENE)=- -=(On the Metrovision it shows some footage of the match. Tall Pete is taking one heck of a beating. Da Possinater distracts the ref and then the Iron Man nails him with a poolstick that came out from nowhere. It wasn't done yet...,
Iron Man Steve Gorecki just kept stomping on the young rookie...tons of slams lasting ten minutes without Tall Pete getting a punch in. Then all of a sudden
Hardcore walks out with an electric cow prod. The ref was down after a tie up between the Iron Man which sent him packing to the floor. Hardcore came in with the prod and zapped him right in the back. His muscles all tightened up and jumped almost 6 inches off the ground. He zapped him about 6 more times leaving welts the size of quarters on Tall Pete. It then shows Iron Man picking up the crippled body and giving him the Jacknife Backbreaker...then the
1.....2.....3!)=-
-=(SCENE)=- -=(Iron Man Steve Gorecki)=- I guess that match was just too bloody for the viewers at home. Me and Hardcore go way back, not to say that we are allies, but I will be there for him, and he would do the same for me! The rookie couldn't cut it. I guess those small town feds in WIsconsin got me pumped up for an occassion like this.
This is my home town, where I grew up, I have got home court advantage throughout the tournament. From there on it will all be put on the line when I wrestle. I don't care who my second round opponent will be...lets see what shmuck they put the Iron Man with. There is noone great left. All first round wins were flukes. So Michael Sanders, who do I got man? Are you even going to tell me or should I play a guessing game? I will beat them no matter who it is!
-=(Iron Man Steve Gorecki)=- -=(SCENE)=- -=(Michael Sanders appears at the entrance holding a piece of paper. He's got a microphone and looks about ready to talk when he is greated with a warm welcoming of boos.)=-
-=(SCENE)=- -=(Michael Sanders)=- I hold in my hand the second round results...it was held by a fair draft lottery. Lets now see who the Iron Man faces! In the process...the Iron Man
Steve Gorecki will be facing......NIGHT!
-=(Michael Sanders)=- -=(SCENE)=- -=(The crowd goes wild. Steve Gorecki just looks on at a stare down between the prez and him come to an end when the prez walks backstage. Iron Man begins to talk again.)=-
-=(SCENE)=- -=(Iron Man Steve Gorecki)=- As i said, I don't care who I face, it will just be another victom. Night, you wanted to step in my way a while ago...now you got your chance to prove it to the world how great you are...how big of a man Night really is. Well, you have the millions watching around the world so don't screw up. You are very rusty...haven't had a match in who knows how long. I have been training profusely in order to gain the body that I have today. You have a little scrawny rear end which I will toss all over the place. So Night, I am calling you out right now! Come on Night, if you have what it takes to face me then we will get it on right now!
-=(Iron Man Steve Gorecki)=- -=(SCENE)=- -=(An midget comes out dressed like Night. He runs to the ring and gets pummeled by Iron Man the instant he steps on the canvas. A couple of massive body slams and then a side suplex. Da Possinater runs out from under the ring and up to the top. He nails the fake Night in the back with a baseball bat and then picks him up. He lifts him so high above his head that the midget looks like he is getting sick. Da Possinater throws him into the crowd!!!! The crowd grabs him and pummels him too!)=-
-=(SCENE)=- -=(Iron Man Steve Gorecki)=- Thats what will happen to the real Night, but we won't be so nice to him like this little guy, we will give him the harst treatment. He won't know what hit him. We got my boy here with a ball bat. You never know when Hardcore will pop out from under the curtain and pop you upside your head. Or the Silent
Storm, I have been training with them fellas and they are some of the toughest
SOB's in the business. We will see if you come to wrestle or to have a tea party. You have talked the talk, but I know that you can't walk the walk with me on the opposing corner.
-=(Iron Man Steve Gorecki)=- -=(Da Possinater)=- And what is with him in these bars. Everytime I see him on a TV show hes always in a bar. Talking about a raging alcoholic, I think we have one on our
hands here. He will be trying to punch you but seeing three. Night won't even find his way to the ring. But Steve, you are the man, you got the cash, you got the women, and you got the muscles, can you ask for anything else?
-=(Da Possinater)=- -=(Iron Man Steve Gorecki)=- I know, I got 26 grand in cash from the GWF already, not to mention that huge signing bonus they gave me. But, there is one thing I would like to add to the title of Iron Man and that is some gold around my waste. I have felt plain without a belt and now is the time to grab one. If I get past this match, then
I will easily be a title holder. That is why I must get rid of Night fast.
Night, you don't know any moves, you are a one move super star, and on top of that, you don't have the cash. Da Posse 2000 is on its way to the GWF. You all saw how Da Posse ripped through the WWA, well, there is a little more competition here and it will reign again in a new era. Da Posse 2000 isn't the old way, we are more fierce, we have gotten better, and you will be seeing a lot more of us!
-=(Iron Man Steve Gorecki)=- -=(Da Possinater)=- Night was all ticked off at us because he wasn't Da Posse 2000 material! We didn't want him in and he wanted to force his way into the stable. Now it will be the Iron Man who draws the line between these two forces.
-=(Da Possinater)=- -=(Iron Man Steve Gorecki)=- I have already made one person quit in Tall Pete, it looks like Night will be the next! I will win, but you gotta promise me this Night, and that is you won't cry to the president or anyone like that after you lose! There is nothing worse than a sore loser. I can see if the president wants you to job the match, but that is because you can't cut it in the first place!
-=(Iron Man Steve Gorecki)=- -=(Da Possinater)=- Stevie is just making the GWF, you all wonder why millions around the world watched Sunday Storm? Because Steve was going to be on it....but it was a
"dark" match. Everyone would of loved to see the blood bath that happened...but tomorrows main event will bring millions more to the TV set.
Look at all these fans out here with the Iron Man's tee shirt...they all love him! -=(Da Possinater)=- -=(Iron Man Steve Gorecki)=- So Night, if you are ever ready, and you think you got what it takes....then..... -=(Iron Man Steve Gorecki)=- -=(Crowd)=- BRING IT! -=(Crowd)=- -=(SCENE)=- -=(Iron Man walks out of the ring behind Da Possinater. There is a fan in the crowd with a Night shirt on. Iron Man points to the man and the 7'0" beast, Da
Possinater grabs a hold of the mans collar shaking him vigorously to get the shirt ripped. The shirt rips from the back and the man is almost in shock. Da
Possinater takes the shirt and sets it on fire! Scene fades to a Silent Storm commercial!)=-
