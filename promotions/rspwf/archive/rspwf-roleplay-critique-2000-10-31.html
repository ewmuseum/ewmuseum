It seems like I'm offering critiques pretty often, and have never given myself the same chance at improving. This was written for [name withheld] and
[name withheld]'s New Wave Wrestling Alliance... A sneak preview of sorts, because the flash packet hasn't even come out yet, heh. Anyway, any good points for improvement are appreciated.
====================================== THE WASP [FADE IN] [Scene: Little did we know the many talents of the Wasp. From violence to humanitarianism, the Wasp has proven why he is the "man of a thousand personalities". Today, on top of all that, we find the NWWA's own resident
"chameleon" sitting in a dark room about half filled, center stage at a piano plugging away to a nice slow rhythm. He is accompanied by a violinist, playing an extremely slow version of "The Kiss" from the soundtrack of the Last of the Mohicans, which is, ironically enough, his entrance music as well.]
(### ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ##) [In contrast to what you would hear when the Wasp makes his way to the ring to dismember another victim, the pace gradually slows as he and his accompaniment continue on. His audience are well dressed and well groomed... Just about the exact opposite that you would expect the Wasp to be around. But he seems to fit right in, even if his tie -is- hanging about two inches below his collar and is inside out (the fat part of the tie is in the back, and tucked into his pants while the thin side is hanging out). It is eerie, really... That his whole personality is almost brought out in this form of artistic expression.]
(### ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ##) [Finally the sound stops. The pace has slowed to a point that individual notes no longer tie together to form a melody. The Wasp leans back and pounds the keys hard one last time, as the violinist scrapes his bow across his violin, and everything stops until a slightly audible clap is heard from the patrons. The Wasp leans back and raises his hands, prompting more claps, before turning toward the camera and pulling his mustache to the sides, stretching it well beyond his face.]
The Wasp: (Holds one finger up) In -stark- contrast to my predecessors, the Wasp is a man of many traits. I offer no hint of rest to my prey... And I hunt them down beautifully. As is the case with a certain few NWWA employees who have managed to find themselves on the receiving end of a
-great- beating. Kichoumen, the enigmatic little girl who realized that my versions of pain and suffering are deeper than whatever it is that made him the way he is today. And Jeremy Locke suffered an ironic twist, in that I put him down to prove a humiliating point. He found that the danger in me is always right here (Pokes his finger into his chest) and that the enjoyment I draw from that danger is here (Lifts the same finger to the side of his head). But one man has not yet realized what it is about him that he should forget exists.
[He lowers his hand and looks to his side as a man puts some money in a tip jar on the piano. The Wasp gives him an uncharacteristically warm smile and looks back toward the camera having already blown off the little distraction.]
The Wasp: Matthew MacDonald, I extend to you my most heartfelt thanks. Just like what you see here (waves his hand around), there is an art to what we do to each other... A level that only exists in what we do for a living.
When you beat me (pauses) when you erased my hopes of obtaining the only title in all of professional wrestling that has ever meant anything to me, you made me realize that it was in the past... That better things can be achieved. I use to have a saying that no one won anything without proving themselves through me first. And what happened in that tournament was not
-you- beating me.... (His lips clench) I BEAT ME. And that is where we stand today. [He waves off the next patron offering a tip, noting that 'tips are not why he does this'. Before he can continue a second lady must be waved off, and once again we have The Wasp's full attention.]
The Wasp: The inevitable has happened. It did not sink in right away, in fact it took a little shaking of some ring rust to truly comprehend where I was going. I realized against Jeremy Locke that I was still the man who made it to the finals at the Masters of the Ring III. Then I realized that as dangerous of a man as "Nightshade" is, he is not as -violent- as the
Wasp. (His lips twist into a half smile) And -then- (pause) I realized after beating Kichoumen like a little girl that I am once again the most dominant man in all of wrestling, without a (Poking his hand into the piano keys with each word) DOUBT (###) IN (###) MY (###) MIND (#######).
[He holds his finger on the key, prompting a loud, long note to reverberate throughout the jazz club. Heads begin turning toward the Wasp, who pays no attention, going off on his rant...]
The Wasp: I am the (Hits the piano, 3, 4 keys at a time ###) -licentious- trendsetter of pain and suffering! I am the (###) -proprietor- of violence!! I -am- the (###) KING (###) OF -ALL- (###) WRESTLING!!! (His eyes have gotten as big as a cue ball) MOST OF ALL I AM THE HEIR APPARENT OF
THE SECTOR... (Calm) And I am a future world champion. I have finally beaten myself. And like I said, until this point, -nobody- went anywhere without beating the Wasp. And little did you realize, that at -your- expense... I have done that.
[He leans back in his stool, folding his arms behind his head. The violinist who was accompanying him earlier in their duet walks off, fearing a violent outlash from the unpredictable Wasp. We get no outlash, however... The Wasp turns back to his piano and hits a couple of notes that are actually on-key, and he turns back toward the camera with that warm smile on his face once again.]
The Wasp: Your mind is in the hunt... (###) But your heart is not with your prey! [FADE OUT] The Wasp (From blackness): (### ## ### ## ###) SING US A SONG, YOU'RE THE PIANO MAN...
