[Vote for me] Illusive Dumbass Roleplay (2/3)

9 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene opens up to a park. The park is a small park that's got children's play gyms all over. There's the monkey bars, and jungle gyms, all the way up to a spinning wheel type of thing. At the far end of the park there's a lone figure that's just sitting there staring into space. The camera draws closer as the figure seems lifeless. You can now see the figure is wearing warm ups and a fleece pullover with a hood. Both of which are black. The fleece's hood is pulled over the head so you can't see who it is. The camera is coming closer and it approaches the hooded man. The man still lays lifeless...as though he is dead. The camera notices the figure isn't moving so the camera man walks up to the man and taps on his shoulder. Suddenly the body snaps and jumps up.
The camera man and camera going flying back out of fear. The man is none other than Illusive Dumbass)
Dumbass: What the hell? Camera Man: Uhh sir...you were sleeping I think. Uhh it's time for an interview. You told me to meet you here.
Dumbass: Ohh...sorry!! I was listening to a Guru interview on tape. (Dumbass pulls down his hood to reveal headphones on his head. He then takes the headphones off and places the hood back on his head)
Camera Man: Trust me sir...I completely understand. Dumbass: Damn right you better understand. There's nothing better to put you to sleep for an afternoon nap than a good old Guru interview.
Camera Man: Yeah...I once passed out listening to his interview in a car. I almost got in a crash.
Dumbass: Well that just proves to everybody that you shouldn't listen to Guru and Drive...You just might do something that you can't take back. That's why friends don't let friends listen to Guru and Drive.
Camera Man: Alright man...thanks for the tip. Dumbass: Anytime...now onto business. Guru for the few seconds I stayed awake during your interview I managed to hear my name called in your interview. Than
I had to keep myself awake just to hear what you had to say. Now before I go any further I would like to ask you why...WHY!!!?? Do you have to mention my name last...huh? Are you trying to torture me or something...because if you are it's working so please stop. I mean It's not healthy to fight sleep, and I had to do it for an hour or so. Now while I managed to hear my name...I also managed to hear what you had to say. Or what made sense out of what you said.
You said something about how I was definitely not going to win the tournament because I don't have a World Championship belt. You see Guru...that's exactly why I don't ever understand you. It's not because of your higher intelect but it's how a man can boast about having a higher intelligence and then say something that stupid. I mean...are you mentally retarded or something. Are there a few things that you just can't comprehend, because I'm wondering what posessed you to say something that stupid. That idiotic. It's mere moronic as to what you have said. Having a piece of metal that goes around your waist doesn't prove anything. All it is...is a piece of metal for a specific person to showcase. It's not anything to boast about. It's merely metal. If you think that taking a piece of metal and putting it around your waist makes you a candidate to get far into the tournament then you've been smoking too many TVTC blunt's. Guru you shouldn't even have a belt yourself. I don't even want to know how the hell you got it...you must have fought a damn gorilla that was drunk and on crack. Because I feel sorry to the person that had the render the belt to you. Guru your nothing but a pathetic excuse for a wrestler. Your nothing but a spec in the way of your total destruction. I'm gonna put you down. As far as your belt theory goes...Fuck the belts, fuck the Sledge hammer, fuck you damn interviews, fuck the whole damn tournament, fuck you, you god damn piece of shit!!!! Guru I couldn't give a shit about the tournament.
I want to get a hold of you. I'm gonna tear you a new asshold when I get done with you. You should shut your mouth now so you can have time to scream when
I'm making you bleed like the pussy that you truly are. Your no champion
Guru...your not even a challenge. Your just a nuisance. That's all you are.
Your a damn road block to my ever lasting eternity in Alliance heaven. I will not only kick your damn ass...I'll kick it so hard that you'll give anal retentive a whole new name. Guru I'm gonna bitch slap you like the damn little school girl that you are. Your a damn school girl frolicking in the meadows.
Your lost Guru...you freakin lost... (Illusive Dumbass takes a moment to calm down. He's practically hyperventilating from talking about Guru. After taking a few deep breaths
Illusive Dumbass begins to talk again)
Dumbass: Not only do I want a piece of Guru but I also want to get my hands on
JP Blood. Blood your going to be my next victim...or perhaps I should jump the shit out of both of you at the same time...well I know that you two have bad blood also so I might just watch the show first. Either way I'll get my hand on you one way or another. Blood I'm going to make you feel pain. Not any ordinary pain. Not the pain that you feel over Jim...but something much worse.
I'm talking about the pain you must feel before you venture to the realm of
Jim. It's the inbetween realms kind of pain. It's the pain people never return to. It's escruciating pain, and I'm planning on dealing you that pain.
Your going to be begging and pleading...and I'm not going to let up...I'm going to make you bleed like you've never bled before...and I'm not just talking about your everyday chair shot...I'm talking ravage and sadistic torture. I'm going to do everything that I can to make sure that you and Guru go down...and go down by my hands. I will make Blood bleed sort to speak. I'll be damned if
I let myself drift off and become a has been or wanna be. Do you hear me...I'll be damned!!!! When Friday rolls around preperation will begin for me. Not just your ordinary preperation...i'm talking about the expectation to see something happen. I'm planning on dishing out pain and at the same time
I'm expecting to recieve some...but pain will do me no harm. My adrenaline is already pumping...I can only imagine what my adrenaline will be doing on the day of no mercy. I will terrorize all those who oppose me...and it will be a glorious day.
Camera Man: I hope you kick the shit out of Guru... Illusive Dumbass: Don't worry son...I'm planning on it... (Dumbass stands up and walks over to a children's jungle gym. He finds a seat and takes it. He adjusts himself to get comfortable)
Illusive Dumbass: As for everybody else...well...I'm not going to speculate as much. They seriously don't pose me much a threat...however I will not take them lightly. Sledge hammer...Oh man...what is wrong with this guy...I mean first I have to hear his Cinderella story about his life and then he goes into snow fights and hot choclate...is this some kind of joke...because if it is...I'm not finding it very funny. This guys about to walk into a battlezone where there's gonna be bloodshed almost guaranteed and this guy's living and damn fairytale. This guy needs to take a look and see what he's gotten himself into. This guy thinks this ring is gonna habe lollipops and candy canes. He thinks the ring is made of ginger bread and the ropes are licorice. This guy needs to look into a mirror real hard and notice what he's about to head into...because it isn't that nice. As far as the other's such as c4...well man...this guy is a insane german who babble's on and on about how if we don't step down then it's our punishment that's going to be handed out...or something like that...he's really hard to understand. Anyway's this guy seems a little scared to me...it sounds to me like this guy is afraid to actually step in the ring so he's asking people if they can step aside and not kick his german ass.
Well that's not the way it's going to be. Personally I woudn't mind kicking
German ass one bit...It would be my pleasure. Then there's Wolverine who's been drinking a little too much. He's just a crazed lunatic that I can surely do away with. Then there's Firestarter who is nothing more than a student in training. This guy is whipped. He's the type of person that get's down and barks when you say "Down boy down". No matter...he's just another early exit from the clear ranks. Then lastly there's the ever so quiet Crypt Keeper.
This guy's not even worthy of a wildcard. Once again we have another early exit here. So that rounds out the crowd...I'm gonna get have to get back to you on anymore...Peace.
(Illusive Dumbass stands up and begins sprinting into the distance. The camera man focuses in on Illusive Dumbass as he finally goes over a hill. The camera man then pans around to get a final look at the small town park. This time there's three small children playing on the monkey bars. Fade to orange and then green and then black...but's it's not completely black because I detect a touch of purple)
Ish...I did this roleplay
