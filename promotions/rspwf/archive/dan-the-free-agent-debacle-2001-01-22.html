*** Dan "The * * * * * Free Agent" Debacle ***

22 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey everybody. I've been having the greatest luck lately finding a federation. After my hiatus I joined the IWC which closed down shortly after. I then joined the AAWC which was supposed to be huge... Needless to say that never worked out for whatever reasons. So what I'm trying to get at is that I'm looking for a federation again. For only being in e-wrestling last year for 4 months and getting the 180 spot in the RSPWF, I think I didn't too half bad.
So this is what I'm looking for in a fed. First of all I want to gain some recognition for my character, Dan Debacle. I'm also looking for a place to improve my rp skills. And above else, I'm looking for fun. If any of you out there know of any or happen to run one, give me a buzz. Here's a simple app for those interested...
----------------------------------------------------------------------------- Real Name: Mike Email Address: [detail withheld] ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ E-wrestler's Name: Dan "The * * * * * PsYcO" Debacle Height: 6'10" Weight: 355 pounds Home town: Cincinnati, Ohio Ring Music:George Thorogood's "Bad to the Bone" Alignment: Face/Heel/Tweener Tweener Gimmick: Hardcore drunk. Carries a 40 ouncer of DANWEISER to the ring and his
DANWEISER beer keg.
Finishing Move:* * * * * Chokeslam ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ E-wrestling background: Well, I've been in e-wrestling close to 6 years now. I've been using Dan for about 5. Over the years I've came to this gimmick and has done quite well for me. Right now I say that I'm at my best and am looking to improve more. I was only in e-wrestling last year for about 4 months and made 180 on the endyear which I'm proud of... I've been in hundreds of federations and am looking for a federation where I can improve my skills and gain some recognition. Above all else, I'm looking for fun.
Title History:(if you have no previous e-fed involvement, skip this) 2 X PWA Tag Team Champ 2 X PWA Hardcore Champ 2 X PWA Television Champ BDCW World Champ BDCW Tag Team Champ World Champ in a could other AOL feds And workgin on my first RSPWF title... ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Sample Roleplay:(this will determine your acceptance into the fed) [Scene opens inside Dan Debacle's home in Cincinnati, Ohio. It's the same scene Dan used to use back in his golden days of the PWA, except Cinnamon and that bitch, Double D, aren't there anymore. The two of them have retired due to being put through tables and the like. But back to the matter at hand. In the back of the room you see a fireplace. Right above it you see a poster of
Dan standing with his arms folded and Double D and Cinnamon to each side of him wearing matching leather outfits. Cinnamon has the PWA Hardcore Title over her shoulder and Double D has the PWA Tag Team title over hers. To the left you see an entryway leading to Dan's kitchen. Nothing exciting there, except for the empty DANWEISER bottles over the counter. In the middle of the room there's Dan's nasty couch that's had every kind of liquid imaginable spilled on it, coffee, DANWEISER, vodka, Jack Daniel's, Tequila, and Jagermiester are just a few. Dan then walks in wearing his black leather jacket, black sweat pants, and a fanny pack around his waist. (Why in the world do you always see wrestlers with these dang blasted things? I never see Mic Foley backstage without it. Is it some kind of pro wrestling fad now?) He lays his bag with his clothes and what not in it next to the door. He takes off his jacket and sets up on the coat rack. Dan walks into the kitchen to get America's favorite beer, DANWEISER. Dan then walks back into the room and sits down on the couch.
He flips the television on and as everyone knows with cable television, not a thing is on. Dan turns off the television and closes his eyes when he hears a
knock at the door. Dan gets up and peers out the window. Dan then opens it up with a smile on his face.] Dan Debacle: Where the hell have you been hiding?!? [Dan shakes the person's hand. Out steps out a man wearing a Hawaiian shirt, shorts, and sandals. Only one person in this world is stupid enough to wear summer clothes in Cincinnati where it's about 30 degrees and about an inch of snow on the ground. And that crazy man is not only stupid, but he's also a
Debacle. It's Dan's older brother, Rick, otherwise known as the Hawaiian Witch
Doctor or the Big Kahuna. Rick has had a few stints in wrestling, but never had a break. Maybe due to experience or horrible angles and gimmicks.]
Rick Debacle: Where the hell have _I_ been?!? Your drunken ass never freakin' calls me! And what kind of way is that to say hello to the guy who has saved you more times than you can count?
Dan Debacle: Shut up. Get your ass in here. Wanna a beer? Rick Debacle: Is my last name Debacle? [I guess alcoholism runs in the family. Dan walks into the kitchen and grabs a
40 ouncer of DANWEISER. With a closer look into Dan's refrigerator, all that's in there is DANWEISER. Dan walks back the living room and hands Rick the beer.]
Dan Debacle: So what's up? Rick Debacle: Who cares about me. What the hell is up with you? I thought I taught you better?
Dan Debacle: What you talking about? Rick Debacle: The IWC. What the hell is up with you? You haven't won one damn match. That ain't the Dan that I know.
Dan Debacle: Hey, I haven't gotten my break yet. Rick Debacle: Break? You're the best damn hardcore wrestler there bar-none. Getting a break has nothing to do with it. Dan Debacle: I just haven't had my heart into it lately. [In the blink of an eye, Rick punches Dan right in the mouth, busting his lip. The punch shakes Dan up pretty good.] Dan Debacle: What the hell was that for? Rick Debacle: You turned weak on me. Now you're a fuckin' wimp. You ain't no
Debacle anymore.
Dan Debacle: What? Rick Debacle: You heard me. You're a puss. Dan Debacle: Hold on pal. I let you into my house and you're going to call me this shit? Rick Debacle: I'm going to call you anything I want to for two reasons. One,
I'm your brother. Two, you ain't going to do a damn thing about it cuz your wimp. The Dan I know would have already beat my ass. Ask Chris, he'd tell ya.
He was the one who called me and asked me to get your head back. And that's exactly what I'm going to do.
Dan Debacle: Hey, Chris can stay out of this. This has nothing to do with him. I'm fine right now. I'm in great shape. I'm feeling good. I don't need any help.
[Rick stands up and puts his hands on hips. He turns his back to Dan. Dan looks up at the ceiling right when Rick turns around and punches him in the mouth again. Blood starts to pour from his lip.]
Dan Debacle: Shit man! What the hell are you doing!?! Rick Debacle: Kicking your ass!! You got a problem with it?!? Dan Debacle: You're starting to piss me off! That's what you're doing! Rick Debacle: That's what I'm trying to do damnit, you big wimp! What you gonna do about it?!?
[Dan sits there with no response. Rick snickers and takes a drink of his beer.] Rick Debacle: That's what I thought... The Hardcore Phenomenon himself backing down... Just like you have the last few weeks in the IWC. You're a fucking wimp. You hear me? A wuss!!!
Dan Debacle: Back off Rick. Rick Debacle: I've seen you bust up guys left and right. Not even think about it. Right now you're a scared monkey. You don't have a damn clue what to do.
Your head is so messed up and full of bologna that the Hardcore Phenomenon we all used to love is dead! He's packed up his bags and got the hell out of dodge. Now you're Dan "The * * * * * I'm a fuckin' wimp" Debacle!!
[Rick starts poking Dan in the chest. You can tell Dan is getting agitated but doing nothing about it.]
Rick Debacle: What's wrong cat got your tongue? Huh? Now the big man ain't gonna talk. What the hell is wrong with you? If I had the chance to wrestle for one of the biggest federations in the world, I'd give my best every damn night. And then you got a guy here who doesn't even care if he shows up.
You're a disgrace Dan! [Rick starts poking Dan in the chest. Dan clinches his fist but turns his head, holding back his anger.]
Rick Debacle: What's wrong tough guy? What am I saying? You ain't no tough guy. You're s sissy boy! I dare you to take a swing at me wimp. I dare you to stand up against me!!
[Dan clinches his fist as hard as he can and grits his teeth. He cocks his arm back and in an instant… He doesn't do anything.]
Dan Debacle: I don't have to prove myself to you. Wrestling is a business that you wouldn't know anything about. So how about you stepping out of my face?
Rick Debacle: Rasslin' isn't a business!! I remember when you used to bust heads like it wasn't nothing. Watching blood splatter against the canvas. It was an art to you. A very sick art. You didn't look at rasslin' as a damn business or a sport. You looked at it as a fun sick game. When you weren't worrying about money or fame, you were busting the hell out of people for the hell of it. That's where you got your fame, Dan. Not because this is a business.
[Dan turns his head again knowing that Rick was right. Dan never cared about wins and losses. He used to worry about how many bones he'd break tonight.
How much blood would be spilled. How many people would leave in bodybags. Just like the good ol' days of the PsYcO CoNnEcTion.] Dan Debacle: Rick, this is the last time I'm going to ask you to leave. It's either you leave peacefully, or I'm going to have to call the police…
Rick Debacle: Call the police? Dan never calls the police. Come on. You're a bouncer. How about you bounce me out of here?
[Dan thinks about it for a second. Back when Dan and Rick were little, They used to beat up on each other for fun. Dan sparred with Rick to help him out with this underground fighting he was doing. They'd go out to the garage, close to the door, and just beat the crap out of each other. They had to quit when their parents found out. Dan got his head split open with a shovel. Rick tried to say he fell on top of the shovel, but Rick is a horrible liar.]
Dan Debacle: Leave Rick. [Without even thinking about it, Rick grabs the 40 ouncer and smashing it over
Dan's head. Millions of pieces of glass fling everywhere. Dan closes his eyes and feels the warmth ooze down his face. He opens his eyes and notices that he's bleeding. In an irate rage Dan jumps up from the couch and elbows Rick to the temple. Rick goes down like a sack of potatoes. Dan then screams and picks up Rick. Rick pokes him in the eyes and then rams Dan's head right through the drywall, a spectacular thing to see on t.v. Dan then kicks Rick in the side.]
Rick Debacle: Come on! Show me the Dan I know!! Get over here you…- [Dan throws a fury of punching at Rick. Rick backs up on top of the table. Dan keeps punching Rick in the head, finally busting him open. I guess bleeding is another thing that runs in the family. Rick sits up on the table.
Dan stands there catching his breath. He looks like a crazed, bloodied lunatic who's ready to destroy. Rick smiles at Dan.]
Rick Debacle: You gonna finish me off or stand there? [They both stand up toe-to-toe trading blows. It's like they taking turns hitting each other, just like another game they used to play. The first one to go down loses. The thud of each punch landing on the other person's face is truly disgusting. It's not like the normal stomping of the foot punches you see on t.v. This is legit. Rick finally starts to fall back. Dan, finally noticing his advantage, grabs Rick by the throat. Rick starts to look a little scared, especially since even though Rick is the older brother, Dan is much bigger. Rick is only 6'7" 285, which is still pretty dog gone big, but compared to Dan's 6'10" 355 pounds he looks small. Dan lifts Rick up by his throat. Rick's toes barely touch the ground.]
Dan Debacle: I've been in wrestling for 5 years. 5 long bloody years. I've put people in the hospital. I've broken arms, legs, noses… I've put people through tables, barbwire, cages, funeral car windshields, and through hell.
I've beaten some of the biggest bad asses with my beer keg… One thing I'm not, Rick… I'm no fucking wimp.
[With that said, Dan lifts Rick high into the air. Rick's head hits the ceiling when he's finally * * * * * Chokeslammed through the table. The table breaks clean in half as the bloody Rick lays motionless.]
Dan Debacle: I'll show ya hardcore. I'll show you why they named me a PsYcO. When Eric Logan first saw me, he knew that I had what it takes to become a
PsYcO. Now I own the PsYcO CoNnEcTiOn. I think it's time that I show the rest of the world why I'm so tough. And I'm going to show it in my next match. I guess that makes it that tag team match. I'm not much into it, but I'll do it.
Then we'll see who's ready… For A DEBACLE!!!!!!!!!!! [Dan then gives the sign for the * * * * * Chokeslam. Rick rolls off the table and looks up at his little brother. Rick once again smiles and wipes the blood from his lip. Camera fades to black.]
