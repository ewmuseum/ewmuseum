[ICW Roleplay] Michael Wolfe Please critique

8 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
< There is a dark room that is only lit by a few white candles. The walls can not be seen. Next to the candles sits a man. It is none other than the ICW's very own, "Psycho" Michael Wolfe. He is sitting idian style with his black trenchcoat on that has a picture of a wolf foaming at the mouth. His face is covered in shadows and all that can really be defined are his bright red eyes and little fangs coming out of his mouth. In his hands he holds nothing. >
"PSYCHO" MICHAEL WOLFE: Winter Warfare 99 is over. It has now been two weeks since I last wrestled in the ICW against "Hotstuff" Darren Henrey. He was a man that I underestimated. I saw the 0-0 record and thought he was a rookie.
Now I hear he has been around for a long time and gets a title shot at Fallout this week. I have learned a lot in my three weeks here. I was not prepared for Darren Henry. I did not train hard enough. I figured he was a nobody that
I should not be concerned with. Now he has given me the first defeat of my brief stay here. Darren Henry you are not that great. If I would have known any better there is no way you could of beat me. I would have, and should have put everything I could into our match. Now you make me look like a chump. I am not cool with that. I am what the ICW will cherish in the future. I will one day be a fixture at Fallout and pay per views. By beating me you have made a big mistake. Your career is now at stake. Nothing that you do will be safe.
I will always be the shadow that lurks behind you, and when I have the chance
I will suficate you.
< Suddenly a woman walks in through a door that you could hear open, but not see. She is dressed in all black and wears a cloak over her head so all you can see is a little bit of her brown hair that comes out the front. With her she has a silver cup that looks like it is from the midevil time period. She sits down next to Michael Wolfe. >
"PSYCHO" MICHAEL WOLFE: Soon "Hotstuff" Darren Henry, we will meet again. Be prepared it could be as soon as friday when you fight Tommy "Lord" Lionheart.
When he is through ripping every single part of you apart you still must worry. Do not sleep at night, do not go out without protection. I am in dark matches now so you might not take me that seriously, but you should. You will see the anger that is building up inside of me. The rage that will be unleashed starting with "Natural Born Loser" Spencer Young. After that who knows.
Soon....soon my friend I will have the chance to fight you on the regular card, and then your fate will be decided
< The woman hands Wolfe the cup and he looks at it and then holds it into the light. A slight smile cracks over his face. >
"PSYCHO" MICHAEL WOLFE: "Natural Born Loser" Spencer Young it all starts with you on Friday night. I see that you have not lost in the ICW yet. My friend that will all be different. You see I am done taking the crap from people. I undeestimated an opponent once, and I will not let it happen against you
Spencer Young. You are a complete nothing compared to me. Unlike me, you are destined for dark matches for all of your career. Myself on the other hand will be more well known that the likes of Shane Diamond, Tommy Grason, Triple
X, etc... They are all nothings. Just a bunch of loud mouthed pricks that can not win a match without interference. I was at Warfare and I saw so much interference it made me sick. These are not true athletes. One on one they are your average men, or in some cases women.
< Wolfe takes a sip of the liquid out of the cup. When he hands it back to the woman it looks like blood coming from his mouth. His fangs are covered in it and he licks around his mouth. >
"PSYCHO" MICHAEL WOLFE: This cup is dedicated to Darren Henry. However Spencer Young, you will be the first to see it used in action. It is not to cheat with as an illegal weapon. It is much more than that. Your blood will be the first shed and entered into the cup. You will in a way be the very first sacrafice from the ICW. I will use just a little bit of blood and continue doing so from my opponents until it is filled almost to the rim. Then there will be one more sample left to obtain. The blood of "Hotstuff" Darren
Henry.
"PSYCHO" MICHAEL WOLFE: "Natural Born Loser" Spencer Young, I hope that you are as ready for this match as I am. The world will find out that you are truly what your nickname says on Friday night. Spencer Young on Friday I would like to be the first to tell you.
WELCOME TO MY WORLD!!!!!!!!!!!! <The scene fades out to black.> Handler of: "Psycho" Michael Wolfe 1-1 (ICW) IWF Television Champion
