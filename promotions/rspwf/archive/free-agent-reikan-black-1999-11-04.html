A few notes before you see the app. Technically, he's not an original Creation as far as E-Wrestling Goes. Someone actually uses this name and gimmick in an Independent Federation in Upstate New
York. Oh wait, I use that gimmick in an Independent Federation in Upstate New
York. So Technically, it is all my own doing, so if you hear the name through the grapevine somehow, just know that it's me doing it.
A Few Requirements: - Please, No "Extreme Death Insane Wrestling" style feds. It's ok if feuds are settled with Hardcore Matches. But not ALL hardcore matches. - Net Feds ONLY. - BCC please. I don't care. I can come into a new fed or one that's Established. Now, For the Info! Part I: Personal info ===================== •Your Name: [name withheld] •E-Mail Address: [detail withheld] Part II: Basic Wrestler Info ============================ •Wrestler Name: Reikan Black •Real Name: Reikan Black •Hometown: San Antonio, Texas •Date of Birth: February 23, 1981 •Height: 6' •Weight: 215 •Build: Athletic •Heel/Face/Neutral: Face •Gimmick: Nephew of Independent star J.P. (Just Plain) Black, Reikan is the all around Pretty Boy Latin Gimmick. Been referred to as a "Ricky Martin wanna-be".
•Basic Appearance Description (I want his Physical apperance, detailed. Any scars, tatoos, etc): He is a spanish male. He has black hair that has bleached blonde streaks. It's usually combed in a "messy" fashion. He has a broken heart tatoo on his upper right bicep. He has a build similar to that of HBK. He has two earrings in his left ear, the upper is always a Stud of some fashion. The lower is always a Gold hoop
•Attire (What does he wear to the ring and inside. Be VERY descriptive. I mean elbowpads, kneepads, wrist tape, EVERYTHING! I want any designs he has on any part of his clothing words written, etc): Reikan wears Blue tights. On the left leg, it has white broken hearts all over them. His right leg he has Reikan in black Red going down. On the rear they say "Reikan" in white cursive. He also
wears black boots with red laces and trim. He wears a white kneepad over his left leg with a Red Broken Heart on it. He also wears black wrist tape. He wears a white cut off shirt as well, that says Reikan Black on the front in
Red, and has a big Red Broken heart on the back.
Part III: Moves ========================== •Style- Primary Wrestling Style: Flier Secondary Wrestling Style: Technician •5 Favorite Moves (Or More, if you have your own Name, Supply the name, and the Real wrestling Move Equivilent): 1) Swinging Suplex (He picks the opponent up to do a Suplex, but spins around, dropping the opponent in normal suplex Fashion) 2) Somersault Legdrop 3) Split Leg Moonsault 4) Handspring Elbow 5) Tear Drop Suplex (He hooks the opponent around his thigh, and lifts him up onto his shoulder, dropping him backwards in Suplex Fasihion) •AT LEAST 10 other moves your wrestler does. More is OK 1) Flying Spinning Leg Lariat 2) Missile Dropkick 3) Tornado DDT 4) Frog Splash 5) Springboard Dropkick 6) Side Russian Legsweep 7) Top Rope Hurracarana 8) SuperKick 9) Figure Four Leglock 10) Spinning Heel Kick •Finishers- •Primary Finisher: Diamond Dust -Description: Over the Shoulder Inverted Brainbuster. In simpler Terms, He lifts the opponent as if to do a bodyslam, but places the opponent on his shoulder as if to do a Running Powerslam. He then hooks the opponent around their head, and flips them, dropping them onto their head in Brainbuster Fashion. •Secondary Finisher: Reikan Havoc -Description: He puts the opponents arms through their legs so they are bent over forwards. He grabs them by their hands and lifts them into the air, and does a sitout Reverse Powerbomb •Tertiary Finisher (For Use on Heavier Wrestlers): Spanish Fly -Description: Stardust Press Part V: Ring Entrance ===================== •Entrance Music: "Diesel Power" by Prodigy I send in a different entrance for every match.
